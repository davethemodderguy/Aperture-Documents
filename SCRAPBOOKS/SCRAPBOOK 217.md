# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **RESEARCH ROUNDTABLE TRANSCRIPT**

## **SUBJECT: COMPLEMENTARY EXISTENCE – SHARED EXPERIENCES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Research Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

During the Merryweather Manor reunion, an informal discussion was held between three known complementary pairs within Aperture Industries.

Participants:

- Corin Lockwood
    
- Corey Lockwood
    
- Neko
    
- Miss Neko
    
- Star
    
- Nightshade
    

Although each pair originated through different anomalous circumstances, all exhibit the same underlying characteristic:

Two individuals existing as complementary halves of a single existence.

Research personnel were permitted to document the conversation with the participants' consent.

---

# **THE DISCUSSION**

Corey was the first to ask.

> "So..."

> "...What's it like?"

Neko laughed.

> "Which part?"

> "Having another you?"

Corey nodded.

---

Neko leaned back in his chair.

> "At first?"

> "It's weird."

> "You keep expecting to think the same thing."

> "...Then one day you realize you stopped being copies a long time ago."

Miss Neko quietly added,

> "You become your own people."

---

Corin looked toward Corey.

> "...We still argue."

Miss Neko replied without hesitation.

> "Good."

Everyone looked at her.

> "That means both of you still have your own opinions."

---

# **STAR & NIGHTSHADE**

Star smiled.

> "We were confusing everyone too."

Nightshade gave a small nod.

> "...People kept asking which one was the original."

Star sighed dramatically.

> "...Eventually we just stopped answering."

Nightshade continued.

> "Because the question has no useful answer."

---

# **CORIN**

Corin hesitated before asking her own question.

> "...Do you ever feel..."

She searched for the right words.

> "...Like you're missing something when the other isn't there?"

For a brief moment...

Everyone at the table became quiet.

---

Miss Neko answered first.

> "...Yes."

Neko nodded.

> "It's not loneliness."

> "...It's absence."

---

Nightshade spoke next.

> "...You still function."

> "...But not as well."

Star smiled gently.

> "...It's like reading only half a book."

---

Corey looked at Corin.

Corin looked back.

Neither needed to say anything.

---

# **LUMI'S QUESTION**

Lumi, who had quietly listened throughout the conversation, finally asked something she had wondered for a long time.

> "Do any of you wish you had never split?"

The room became silent again.

Neko looked toward Miss Neko.

Star looked toward Nightshade.

Corin looked toward Corey.

Each pair exchanged the same quiet glance.

Neko answered first.

> "...No."

Miss Neko simply nodded.

---

Star smiled.

> "I couldn't imagine not having her."

Nightshade's expression softened.

> "...Neither could I."

---

Finally...

Corey reached over and gently held Corin's hand.

Corin squeezed it.

Corey smiled.

> "...I don't wish there was only one of us."

Corin quietly finished the thought.

> "...Because then I'd lose my sister."

---

# **RESEARCH CONCLUSION**

Following the discussion, Research Division amended its understanding of complementary manifestations.

While each phenomenon has a distinct origin, all three pairs independently described the same experience.

They do not regard themselves as incomplete fragments searching for reunion.

Nor do they regard one another as replacements.

Instead, each pair has grown into two distinct individuals whose lives have become inseparable.

The anomaly did not merely divide one existence.

It created a lifelong relationship unlike any other.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

As the meeting concluded, Belle walked into the room carrying a tray of cookies.

She stopped.

Looked around.

Then laughed.

> "...Why are all the twins sitting at the same table?"

Without missing a beat, Neko answered,

> "Support group."

Star burst into laughter.

Even Corin smiled.

For a little while...

No one was thinking about anomalies.

They were simply six people sharing stories that only they could truly understand.
