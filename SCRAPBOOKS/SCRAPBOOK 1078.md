# Executive Summary

Neri's voice has returned.

Following the period in which she was unable to speak, Neri has once again demonstrated normal voluntary vocalization.

The voice itself is unchanged from her previously documented vocal characteristics.

It remains:

- extremely soft;
    
- gentle;
    
- quiet;
    
- comforting;
    
- distinctly "cakey" in tone;
    
- and, according to multiple personnel, exceptionally cute.
    

Neri herself does not appear particularly interested in discussing this development.

More importantly:

**She still does not prefer talking very much.**

The restoration of her voice has restored an ability.

It has not created an obligation to use it.

---

# 1. First Recorded Vocalization

The first confirmed vocalization occurred while Neri was sitting in her current plush form.

Jane Doe was nearby.

Jane asked:

> "You feeling okay?"

Neri looked up.

There was a brief pause.

Then, in the same soft voice documented before her loss of speech:

> **"Mm-hm."**

Jane stopped.

She looked at Neri.

Neri looked back.

Jane quietly said:

> "...Your voice is back."

Neri nodded.

No further explanation was provided.

---

# 2. Vocal Characteristics

Audio comparison against previous recordings confirms that Neri's voice remains consistent with her established vocal profile.

It is notably quiet.

Her words tend to sound soft even when the content itself is mundane.

There is a gentle, almost pastry-soft quality to the voice that has led personnel to independently describe it as:

> **"Cakey."**

The term remains unofficial.

It is nevertheless remarkably consistent across observations.

---

# 3. Neri's Reaction to Having Her Voice Back

Neri does not appear particularly excited about the development.

She can speak.

She knows she can speak.

She occasionally chooses to speak.

But she continues to rely heavily on:

- nodding;
    
- shaking her head;
    
- gestures;
    
- facial expressions;
    
- eye contact;
    
- pointing;
    
- and simply doing the thing she wants to communicate.
    

The return of speech has therefore changed communication **availability**, rather than communication **preference**.

---

# 4. Typical Conversation

A recent exchange:

**JANE:**  
"Do you want coffee?"

**NERI:**  
"...Yes."

**JANE:**  
"Anything with it?"

Neri shakes her head.

**JANE:**  
"Okay."

Neri nods.

Conversation concluded.

Total spoken words from Neri:

**One.**

---

# 5. Why She Still Speaks Infrequently

Neri's preference remains consistent with her established behavior.

Speaking requires effort.

More importantly, Neri simply does not seem to feel the need to verbalize every thought.

If a nod communicates the same information as a sentence, she will usually nod.

If a gesture is sufficient, she gestures.

If nothing needs to be communicated, she says nothing.

Personnel are reminded:

> **The return of Neri's voice does not mean she wants to talk more.**

Do not repeatedly ask her questions simply because she can now answer them.

---

# 6. Plush Form

Neri's current plush form has no apparent negative effect on her vocal function.

She can speak normally while in her miniature chibi configuration.

This produces an unusual combination:

A tiny Neri with:

- long white hair;
    
- an oversized hoodie;
    
- a seal tail;
    
- fabric skin;
    
- round glasses;
    

sitting somewhere quietly and suddenly saying something in an extremely soft voice.

Personnel have found this considerably more emotionally disarming than expected.

---

# 7. Jane Doe's Assessment

Jane has become one of the more frequent recipients of Neri's speech.

Jane's assessment:

> "Same voice."

Pause.

> "Still barely uses it."

Another pause.

> "Honestly, that's probably more Neri than anything else."

Neri was present for this statement.

She nodded.

---

# 8. Alice

Alice also heard Neri speak after the restoration.

Alice was visibly surprised.

> "Your voice is back!"

Neri nodded.

Alice smiled.

> "Can you say something?"

Neri stared at her.

Several seconds passed.

Neri finally said:

> **"...Something."**

Alice burst out laughing.

Neri immediately looked away.

No further speech was recorded.

# Final Assessment

Neri has her voice back.

The voice is still the same:

Soft.

Gentle.

Quiet.

Cakey.

And unmistakably Neri.

She can speak whenever she wants.

She simply doesn't want to very often.

C.O.R.E. considers this completely acceptable.

The goal was never to make Neri talk more.

The goal was to ensure that when Neri **wants** to say something important, she can.

And occasionally, when she doesn't need to say anything important at all, the Blacksite may simply hear a tiny voice from somewhere nearby:

> **"...Coffee."**

Which, according to cafeteria staff, is all the information they need.
