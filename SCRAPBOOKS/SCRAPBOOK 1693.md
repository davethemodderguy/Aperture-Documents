# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### RESIDENT WELFARE & MEDICAL DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-JAKE-273  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — RESIDENT WELFARE INCIDENT  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE REVIEW  
**SUBJECT:** Jake — Unintentional External Separation  
**CARETAKER:** Clover Luminare  
**LOCATION:** BLACKSITE EXTERIOR / SAFEHOUSE ACCESS AREA

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Clover Luminare temporarily lost track of Jake.

The incident occurred while Clover was completing routine administrative work outside the SAFEHOUSE.

Jake had accompanied her.

At some point, Clover returned inside.

Jake did not.

Clover subsequently spent approximately **twenty-three minutes** believing Jake was somewhere inside the SAFEHOUSE before realizing that she had last seen him outside.

She immediately returned to the exterior.

Jake was found sitting near the Blacksite perimeter.

He was physically unharmed.

He was not distressed.

He was not attempting to leave.

He was simply sitting there.

With his teddy bear.

Waiting.

---

# 1. THE INCIDENT

Clover had been working outside while Jake sat nearby.

As usual, Jake was not causing any problems.

He had been sitting quietly with his teddy bear while Clover completed paperwork.

At some point, Clover finished one portion of her work and returned inside to retrieve additional documents.

She assumed Jake had followed her.

He had not.

No malicious action occurred.

No emergency occurred.

No anomaly caused the separation.

Clover simply forgot that Jake was still outside.

---

# 2. TWENTY-THREE MINUTES

Clover initially continued working.

She moved between rooms.

Collected paperwork.

Answered several administrative questions.

Checked a financial record.

Then she stopped.

Something was wrong.

She looked around.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "...Jake?"

No response.

She checked his usual locations.

His room.

The cafeteria.

The common area.

The hallway.

Nothing.

Her concern increased rapidly.

She returned to the location where she had been working.

Then remembered.

She had left Jake outside.

---

# 3. CLOVER'S RESPONSE

Clover immediately ran outside.

She did not stop to finish her paperwork.

She did not retrieve her belongings.

She simply went looking for him.

Her candle was still in her hand.

The exterior access area was quiet.

For several seconds, she could not see him.

Then she spotted him.

Jake was sitting beside a low maintenance barrier.

His teddy bear was resting in his lap.

He was looking at the sky.

He appeared completely calm.

Clover stopped.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Jake."

Jake looked over.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Hi."

Clover walked quickly toward him.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "What are you doing?"

Jake looked around.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Waiting."

---

# 4. THE CONVERSATION

Clover knelt in front of him.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "How long have you been sitting here?"

Jake shrugged.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I don't know."

Clover's expression tightened.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Did you try to come inside?"

Jake shook his head.

> **JAKE:**  
> "You said you'd be back."

Clover froze.

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "So I waited."

There was no accusation in his voice.

That made it worse.

---

# 5. CLOVER APOLOGIZES

Clover sat down beside him.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "I'm sorry."

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "It's okay."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "No."

Jake tilted his head.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "It's not."

She looked toward the SAFEHOUSE entrance.

Then back at him.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "I forgot you."

Jake was quiet.

Then:

> **JAKE:**  
> "You remembered."

Clover's expression changed.

> **JAKE:**  
> "You came back."

Clover looked away.

She was visibly trying not to cry.

---

# 6. THE PREVIOUS INCIDENT

C.O.R.E. notes that this incident carries additional significance due to Jake's previous history of being unintentionally left outside.

During that earlier event, prolonged exposure and isolation resulted in severe consequences.

This time:

- Jake was outside for approximately twenty-three minutes;
    
- environmental conditions were safe;
    
- he remained physically stable;
    
- his teddy bear remained active;
    
- no medical deterioration occurred;
    
- Clover located him before the situation escalated.
    

The outcome was therefore substantially different.

However, the psychological implications remain significant.

---

# 7. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake did not appear angry.

He did not appear frightened.

He did not blame Clover.

He simply held his teddy bear and looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Are we going back?"

Clover nodded.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Yes."

Jake stood.

Then reached for her hand.

Clover took it.

They walked back inside together.

---

# 8. CLOVER'S SELF-ASSESSMENT

Clover immediately reported the incident to C.O.R.E.

She did not attempt to minimize what happened.

Her statement was brief.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "I failed."

C.O.R.E. Welfare personnel disagreed with the characterization.

The incident was a human error.

Clover did not intentionally abandon Jake.

She did not knowingly leave him.

She did not disregard a medical emergency.

She simply lost track of him.

Nevertheless, C.O.R.E. acknowledges that the caretaker arrangement exists specifically to prevent this type of mistake.

Therefore, the incident requires corrective action.

Not punishment.

---

# 9. UPDATED CARETAKER PROCEDURE

Clover has agreed to several simple changes.

When moving between locations, she will now verbally confirm:

> **"Jake?"**

Jake is expected to answer.

Before entering the SAFEHOUSE from an exterior location, Clover will visually confirm that Jake is with her.

If Jake chooses to remain outside, Clover will explicitly acknowledge it.

The procedure is intentionally simple.

C.O.R.E. does not intend to turn Clover into a surveillance system.

The goal is merely:

> **Know where the child is before walking away.**

---

# 10. JAKE'S OPINION

Jake was asked whether he wanted Clover to stop being his caretaker.

He immediately shook his head.

> **JAKE:**  
> "No."

> **C.O.R.E.:**  
> "Why?"

Jake looked at Clover.

> **JAKE:**  
> "She came back."

Clover became quiet.

Jake continued:

> **JAKE:**  
> "She always comes back."

No further explanation was required.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Jake is physically unharmed.

No medical intervention was required.

Clover remains Jake's caretaker.

The assignment has **not** been revoked.

However, the incident demonstrates an important limitation:

Even a caretaker can make mistakes.

The solution is not to demand perfection.

It is to create simple habits that make important mistakes less likely.

Jake does not need someone who will never make a mistake.

He needs someone who notices when they have made one—

and comes back.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Later that evening, Clover was working in Jake's room.

Jake was sitting on her lap.

His teddy bear was tucked against his chest.

Clover was reviewing accounting records.

She suddenly stopped.

She looked down.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Jake?"

Jake looked up.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Hm?"

Clover exhaled.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Nothing."

Jake went back to looking at his teddy bear.

A few seconds later:

> **JAKE:**  
> "I'm still here."

Clover smiled faintly.

Her hand rested gently against his back.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "I know."

This time, she checked.
