# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **SECURITY DIVISION – UNIFORM ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: CAMERA MART & SECURITY MART IDENTIFICATION**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following a unanimous vote by the Security Division, the resident Marts assigned to security-related duties have been issued miniature replicas of the standard KCA Security cap.

The proposal originated as a joke.

The Marts took it very seriously.

---

# **UNIFORM**

The issued cap is a scaled-down replica of the standard Security Division hat.

Features include:

- Black fabric matching the full-size uniform.
    
- Miniature KCA Security insignia.
    
- Reinforced chin strap to prevent the hat from floating away during high-speed movement.
    
- Sized to fit comfortably over a Mart's signature tiny black top hat.
    

The Marts refuse to wear the security cap directly.

Instead, it sits neatly **on top of their own top hat**.

Engineering determined this was "close enough."

---

# **AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL**

The miniature security hats are currently issued only to:

- **Camera Mart**
    
- Security Control Room assistants.
    
- Patrol Marts.
    
- Rapid Response Marts assigned to defensive duties.
    

All other Marts continue wearing their traditional miniature black top hats.

---

# **BEHAVIORAL EFFECT**

No measurable increase in defensive capability has been observed.

However, Security personnel have reported a dramatic increase in morale.

Camera Mart appears especially proud of its uniform.

When complimented, it straightens the tiny cap and replies:

> "Security!"

---

# **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

**Camera Mart**

Every morning, before climbing onto the surveillance console, Camera Mart carefully adjusts its miniature cap.

Only then does it announce:

> "Ready!"

---

**Dialyn**

The first time she saw Camera Mart wearing the tiny uniform, she stopped walking.

She stared for several seconds.

Then quietly took a photograph.

It is now her phone wallpaper.

---

He looked at Camera Mart watching twelve security monitors simultaneously.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Miniature Security caps have become the standard identifier for Marts serving in security roles throughout the KCA Headquarters.

Visitors can now easily distinguish them from the rest of the colony.

Although the hats carry no official authority, Security Division unanimously agrees they look the part.

Camera Mart certainly believes so.

Every morning, without fail, it climbs onto the security console, adjusts its tiny cap, and proudly declares:

> "Security!"

The rest of the Security Marts invariably answer from somewhere in the ventilation system:

> "Ready!"
