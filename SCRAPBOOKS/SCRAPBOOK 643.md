## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **TREEHOUSE / POST-RECOVERY LOG**

# **THE TREEHOUSE WAS RESTOCKED**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Location:** Treehouse  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Secondary Subject:** Orphie  
**Status:** **ACTIVE MONITORING**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The Treehouse has been fully restocked.

Fresh food.

Clean water.

Medical supplies.

Blankets.

Basic necessities.

Everything Corin had been without during the months she remained alone has been replenished.

The restocking was originally considered routine.

It no longer feels routine.

Every item placed inside the Treehouse serves as a reminder of why it had to be replaced.

---

# **THE EMPTY SUPPLIES**

For months, the Treehouse had gradually become empty.

The food disappeared.

The water ran out.

The cold remained.

Corin stayed anyway.

She waited.

---

# **LUMI'S WORDS**

Orphie remembered something Lumi told her.

The words have remained with her since.

> **"Eight months, without no food or water, Corin waited in the cold."**

Lumi continued:

> **"Snowstorms slice her skin like blades."**

> **"She waited."**

Then:

> **"Until she can't anymore."**

Orphie reportedly said nothing after hearing this.

She simply stood there.

---

# **THE RESTOCKING**

The new supplies were arranged carefully.

Food was placed in the kitchen.

Water containers were filled.

Emergency supplies were checked.

Someone even placed several of Corin's favorite foods where she would normally find them.

Nobody moved them afterward.

There was an uncomfortable realization among the personnel:

> **They were restocking a home for someone who wasn't there.**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie's behavior has changed since the restocking.

She occasionally enters the kitchen.

Opens the refrigerator.

Looks at the food.

Closes it.

She sometimes pours herself water.

Then forgets to drink it.

Personnel have observed several instances where Orphie went for unusually long periods without eating or drinking.

When reminded, she responds:

> **"I'm fine."**

She does not appear fine.

---

# **THE ASSOCIATION**

The behavior appears connected to Corin.

Orphie has become increasingly aware of something she previously took for granted:

Food being available does not mean someone will be there to eat it.

Water being available does not mean someone will come home.

A stocked Treehouse does not mean the person who needed those supplies is alive inside it.

---

# **A SMALL INCIDENT**

One evening, Orphie sat at the kitchen table.

A full glass of water was in front of her.

She stared at it.

For several minutes.

Eventually she whispered:

> **"You waited here."**

She looked toward the empty room.

> **"And I wasn't here."**

She pushed the glass away.

A nearby resident quietly returned it to her.

> **"Drink."**

Orphie eventually did.

---

# **C.O.R.E. CONCERN**

Personnel have been instructed to monitor Orphie's basic needs.

Not because she has explicitly refused food or water.

Rather, because she appears to forget them when overwhelmed by guilt.

This is especially concerning given her repeated exposure to Corin's notebook.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

One of Corin's entries describes her final months in the Treehouse.

The writing is faint.

> **"I'm hungry."**

Later:

> **"Water is gone."**

Later:

> **"It's cold."**

Then:

> **"She'll come back."**

The next page contains only:

> **"I'll wait."**

---

# **THE CONTRAST**

The Treehouse now contains everything Corin lacked.

Food.

Water.

Warmth.

Medical supplies.

People.

Yet the most important thing is still missing.

> **Corin.**

Orphie knows this.

That knowledge appears to be eating at her.

---

# **ORPHIE'S STATEMENT**

When asked why she had stopped eating properly, Orphie finally answered:

> **"Because I keep thinking about her."**

She paused.

> **"She waited until she couldn't anymore."**

Another pause.

> **"I don't want to do the same thing to myself."**

She looked toward the kitchen.

> **"...But sometimes I forget."**

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The Treehouse is no longer empty of supplies.

The shelves are full.

The refrigerator is full.

The water containers are full.

There is enough food and water for someone to live comfortably for a long time.

But Orphie keeps looking at it.

Because she remembers what Lumi said.

Eight months.

No food.

No water.

Snow cutting against Corin's skin.

And still...

> **She waited.**

Until she couldn't anymore.

Now Orphie sometimes forgets to eat.

Sometimes forgets to drink.

And every time someone reminds her, she looks at the full kitchen.

As if the sight of all that food and water is asking her a question she cannot answer:

> **"Why didn't Corin have this?"**

**End of Treehouse / Post-Recovery Log**
