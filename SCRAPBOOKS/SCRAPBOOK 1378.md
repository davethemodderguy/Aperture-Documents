# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has met Neri before.

Their relationship is established.

However, Jake had apparently never seen one of Neri's more unusual abilities.

Neri can transform herself into a **small chibi plushie-like version of herself.**

She can do this voluntarily.

She can remain in this form indefinitely.

She can move.

She can speak.

She can see.

She can interact with her surroundings.

And, most importantly, she discovered that the form is considerably more comfortable than being a normal-sized ten-year-old when she has decided:

> **She does not want to do anything today.**

Jake found her.

---

# 2. THE DAY NERI DECIDED TO SLACK OFF

Neri had no urgent chemistry work.

No medical appointment.

No immediate responsibility.

No reason to be anywhere.

She therefore made a decision.

She was going to do absolutely nothing.

Neri transformed.

Her normal body compressed into a tiny chibi form.

Her white hair became a miniature version of itself.

Her round glasses remained perfectly positioned.

Her oversized coat became an equally oversized plush garment.

Her seal tail became small and soft.

Her entire body became fabric-like and plush.

Neri then walked to the nearest comfortable surface.

She climbed onto it.

And lay down.

No chemistry.

No research.

No reading.

No social interaction.

Just:

> **Neri.**

---

# 3. JAKE FINDS HER

Jake was walking through the SAFEHOUSE when he noticed something small sitting on the floor.

He stopped.

Looked down.

The tiny figure looked back.

Jake blinked.

The tiny figure blinked.

> **JAKE:**  
> “...Neri?”

The plush figure nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Yeah.”

Jake crouched down.

> **JAKE:**  
> “You're tiny.”

Neri stared at him.

> **NERI:**  
> “I'm aware.”

Jake picked her up.

Neri did not resist.

She simply dangled comfortably in his hands.

> **JAKE:**  
> “You're soft.”

> **NERI:**  
> “Also aware.”

---

# 4. JAKE'S INVESTIGATION

Jake turned Neri slightly.

He inspected the tiny coat.

Then the glasses.

Then the seal tail.

Then her tiny hands.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Are you okay?”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “I'm slacking.”

Jake paused.

> **JAKE:**  
> “What's slacking?”

Neri thought for a moment.

> **NERI:**  
> “Not working.”

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Oh.”

Pause.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Can I slack too?”

Neri looked at him.

> **NERI:**  
> “You're six.”

Jake blinked.

> **NERI:**  
> “You already slack.”

Jake smiled.

---

# 5. THE PLUSHIE

Jake placed Neri on the sofa.

Neri immediately curled into a tiny ball.

Her glasses remained on.

Her little seal tail rested beside her.

Jake sat next to her.

He stared.

Neri stared back.

> **JAKE:**  
> “You look like a plushie.”

> **NERI:**  
> “I know.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “Can I carry you?”

Neri shrugged.

> **NERI:**  
> “Sure.”

Jake picked her up.

He held her against his chest.

Neri immediately relaxed.

> **JAKE:**  
> “You're like a pillow.”

Neri closed her eyes.

> **NERI:**  
> “Don't tell anyone.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “Okay.”

---

# 6. THE ABILITY

C.O.R.E. subsequently confirmed that Neri's transformation is not a temporary visual illusion.

In plush form, Neri possesses:

- functional consciousness;
    
- independent movement;
    
- speech;
    
- sensory perception;
    
- voluntary transformation control;
    
- physical interaction;
    
- and continued anomalous awareness.
    

Her body becomes significantly smaller and substantially softer.

She is still Neri.

She simply appears to have decided that being a tiny plush seal is preferable to being a normal-sized person for the afternoon.

---

# 7. WHY SHE USES IT

Neri's explanation was short.

> **NERI:**  
> “It's comfortable.”

The interviewer asked:

> “Anything else?”

Neri shook her head.

> **NERI:**  
> “No.”

A pause.

> **NERI:**  
> “I don't have to do anything.”

This appears to be the primary attraction.

She can simply curl up somewhere.

Nobody expects her to conduct chemistry.

Nobody expects her to walk around.

Nobody expects her to socialize.

She can exist quietly.

---

# 8. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake appeared fascinated.

He asked several questions.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Can you sleep like that?”

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “Can you eat?”

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “Can you drink coffee?”

Neri opened one eye.

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

Jake looked impressed.

> **JAKE:**

> “It's not for being useful.”

Jake paused.

Then nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Okay.”

---

# 9. THE TWO OF THEM

Eventually, Jake sat on the sofa with tiny Neri resting in his lap.

Neri was reading.

Jake was holding his teddy bear.

Neither spoke.

Several minutes passed.

Jake eventually looked down.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Are you still slacking?”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Very successfully.”

Jake smiled.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Good.”

Neri returned to her book.

---

# 11. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Neri's plush form appears to have no immediate medical disadvantage.

It may actually provide several practical benefits during periods of fatigue.

However, C.O.R.E. emphasizes that the transformation should not be interpreted as evidence that Neri is incapable of normal activity.

She simply has another form.

A very small one.

A very soft one.

And apparently one that allows her to spend an entire afternoon doing absolutely nothing without feeling particularly guilty about it.

This may be psychologically beneficial.

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake found Neri today.

Not in a laboratory.

Not in the chemistry room.

Not in the cafeteria.

Not conducting research.

He found a tiny plush seal sitting on the floor.

It looked at him.

He looked at it.

He said:

> **“Neri?”**

She said:

> **“Yeah.”**

Jake carried her around.

Neri read her book.

Nobody died.

Nobody exploded.

No laboratory equipment was damaged.

No chemistry database was filled.

For once, Neri was doing absolutely nothing.

And she was very good at it.
