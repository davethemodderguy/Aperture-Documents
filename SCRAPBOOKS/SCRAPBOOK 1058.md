## Executive Summary

Alice forgot Plushie Neri in the cafeteria.

Neri was left on a cafeteria table overnight.

At some point during the night, Neri lost her balance and fell from the table.

She landed on the floor.

No injury occurred.

Unfortunately, the following morning, Neri experienced a second and considerably more avoidable incident:

**Jane Doe stepped on her hand.**

Neri was unharmed.

Jane was horrified.

Neri was apparently more annoyed by the situation than concerned about it.

---

# 01:43 — Cafeteria

Alice left the cafeteria.

Neri remained on the table.

No personnel were present.

Neri appeared comfortable.

For approximately thirty minutes, she remained stationary.

At 02:17, Neri attempted to reposition herself.

She moved toward the edge of the table.

Her balance failed.

She fell.

**THUMP.**

Neri landed flat on the floor.

She did not appear hurt.

She remained there.

---

# 02:18–09:46 — Floor Occupancy

Neri remained on the cafeteria floor.

She was awake for portions of the night.

She moved occasionally.

At approximately 04:02, she rolled onto her side.

At 05:18, she rolled onto her back.

At 06:51, she briefly attempted to crawl beneath a nearby chair.

She apparently reconsidered.

She returned to the same spot.

No personnel noticed her.

---

# 09:47 — Jane Doe Enters Cafeteria

Jane entered the cafeteria carrying a coffee.

She walked toward the counter.

Neri was positioned near the edge of the main walking path.

Jane did not initially notice her.

One step.

Two steps.

Three—

### CONTACT

Jane's foot came down directly onto Neri's plush hand.

There was no significant force due to Jane immediately realizing something was underfoot.

She froze.

Looked down.

And saw Neri.

---

# 09:47:04 — Jane's Reaction

> **JANE:** "...Oh."

Neri looked up at her.

Jane immediately removed her foot.

> **JANE:** "Neri."

Neri stared.

Jane crouched.

> **JANE:** "Did I just step on your hand?"

Neri slowly nodded.

Jane closed her eyes.

> **JANE:** "I'm sorry."

Neri looked at her hand.

Then back at Jane.

Jane carefully picked her up.

> **JANE:** "Are you hurt?"

Neri shook her head.

> **JANE:** "Hand?"

Neri shook her head.

> **JANE:** "Okay."

Jane inspected the hand anyway.

Nothing was wrong.

---

# 09:49 — Jane's Realization

Jane looked toward the cafeteria table.

Then toward the floor.

Then back at Neri.

> **JANE:** "You fell off the table again."

Neri nodded.

> **JANE:** "And nobody found you."

Neri nodded.

Jane sighed.

> **JANE:** "Alice forgot you."

Neri shrugged.

Jane looked at the ceiling.

> **JANE:** "Of course."

---

# 09:52 — Alice Arrives

Alice entered the cafeteria.

She saw Jane holding Neri.

She stopped.

> **ALICE:** "Neri!"

She immediately approached.

> **ALICE:** "I forgot!"

Neri looked at Alice.

Alice looked guilty.

> **ALICE:** "I'm sorry."

Neri raised one plush paw.

Alice understood.

She carefully took Neri from Jane.

Neri settled into her arms.

Alice hugged her.

> **ALICE:** "I won't forget again."

Neri gave a small nod.

---

# SECURITY REVIEW

This incident represents the second recorded instance of Neri falling from cafeteria furniture while in plush form.

It also represents the first documented case of **accidental pedestrian contact with Neri following such a fall.**

The cafeteria floor is now officially considered an unsuitable long-term parking location for Plushie Neri.

# C.O.R.E. DIRECTIVE

When Plushie Neri is left somewhere:

**Do not assume she will remain there.**

She can move.

She can fall.

She can end up on the floor.

And once on the floor, she is approximately the size of an ordinary plush toy.

Personnel entering cafeterias, lounges, offices, and other occupied areas are therefore reminded to watch where they step.
