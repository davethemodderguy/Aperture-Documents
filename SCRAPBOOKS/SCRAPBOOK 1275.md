# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon is six years old.

This continues to be relevant.

It became considerably more relevant when she proceeded to outperform an experienced MSAC operator during a controlled reload drill.

The exercise was not intended to be competitive.

It became competitive anyway.

The original objective was simple:

> Fire a controlled string.  
> Reload.  
> Resume firing.

One MSAC operator was using a service pistol.

Nora was using her personal **Slugger** shotgun.

The operator began their reload first.

Nora finished hers first.

By a considerable margin.

---

### LANE 05 — NORA TACHYON

---

# THE RELOAD

Nora completed her initial firing sequence.

The shotgun required additional ammunition.

She immediately transitioned into what the range staff identified as a **quad-load sequence**.

Four shells were staged in her hand.

For the demonstration, the reload was performed as **two rapid paired insertions** rather than four individual movements.

The sequence was exceptionally compact.

No hesitation.

No searching for shells.

No visible pause between the two pairs.

The first pair went in.

Then the second.

The entire four-shell sequence was completed almost immediately.

Nora then performed the final portion of the demonstration:

A **tactical chamber/top-off action** was completed before she returned the shotgun to its firing state.

The whole sequence looked less like several separate actions and more like one continuous movement.

The range camera later measured the entire reload as:

> **“Too fast for several observers to visually separate the individual stages.”**

---

# THE MSAC OPERATOR

Meanwhile, the MSAC operator was still completing the pistol reload.

He had started before Nora.

He was experienced.

He was not slow.

He was simply dealing with a completely different reload system.

He finished.

Looked toward Nora's lane.

Nora had already finished.

She was standing there.

Waiting.
