## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Sora has spent an extended period unable to communicate verbally due to previously severed vocal cords.

Although he has access to a replacement whiteboard, he continues to refuse it following the destruction of his original communication device. His ability to communicate through gestures remains inconsistent, and his social withdrawal has continued.

An unexpected encounter with Guiding Light presented a possible solution.

Guiding Light offered to restore Sora's voice.

Sora accepted.

The initial intervention appeared unsuccessful. Sora produced no audible speech, and no immediate improvement in his vocal ability was observed. After Guiding Light departed, Sora appeared to conclude that the offer had been false.

He returned to his den.

However, the following morning, an unexpected development occurred.

**Sora spoke.**

His first audible words were brief, hesitant, and unfamiliar to everyone who had only known him as a mute child.

The restoration was not immediate. It appears that the effects of Guiding Light's intervention manifested after a delay.

C.O.R.E. has not yet established the precise mechanism.

---

## 2. INCIDENT A — THE OFFER

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE, Floor 7 corridor  
**Time:** 14:16

Guiding Light was visiting SAFEHOUSE when she encountered Sora near the entrance to his den.

Sora had been sitting quietly beside the white wool scarf knitted by Mia and Jake.

He noticed her approaching.

Guiding Light stopped at a respectful distance.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Hello, little one.

Sora stared at her.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** May I sit with you?

After a moment, Sora shifted slightly, leaving space.

Guiding Light sat down.

She did not immediately attempt to touch him.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** I've heard about you, Sora.

Sora lowered his head.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** I know you've had a difficult time.

He remained silent.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** And I know you miss your voice.

Sora's ears lowered.

He instinctively brought one hand toward his throat.

Guiding Light watched him carefully.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Would you like it back?

Sora looked at her.

For several seconds, he did not move.

Then he nodded.

It was a small movement, but unmistakable.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Are you certain?

Sora nodded again, more firmly.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** All right.

She extended one hand toward him.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** This may feel a little strange. You don't need to be afraid.

Sora hesitated before accepting her hand.

Guiding Light placed her other hand gently near his throat.

There was a brief glow.

It was neither bright nor painful. No visible wound opened, and Sora did not appear to experience physical distress.

The light faded.

Guiding Light withdrew her hands.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** There.

Sora blinked.

He opened his mouth.

Nothing came out.

He tried again.

Still nothing.

He touched his throat, then looked at Guiding Light.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Try when you're ready.

Sora attempted to produce a sound.

No audible speech followed.

His expression gradually changed.

He looked confused, then disappointed.

Guiding Light remained beside him.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** It may take a little time.

Sora looked away.

He did not appear convinced.

## 3. INCIDENT B — THE APPARENT FAILURE

**Location:** Sora's Den  
**Time:** 14:42

After Guiding Light departed, Sora returned to his den.

A welfare worker observed him attempting to vocalize several times.

Each attempt produced no audible sound.

He eventually stopped.

When Jake visited, Sora was sitting with his back against the wall.

**JAKE:** Hey, Sora!

No response.

Jake noticed his expression.

**JAKE:** Something happened?

Sora looked toward him.

He opened his mouth, attempting to speak.

Nothing.

Jake tilted his head.

**JAKE:** You okay?

Sora lowered his head.

Jake sat beside him.

**JAKE:** Did somebody upset you?

Sora shook his head.

**JAKE:** Oh.

Jake noticed that Sora kept touching his throat.

**JAKE:** Your neck hurts?

Sora shook his head again.

He then made a frustrated gesture toward his mouth.

Jake understood that he was trying to communicate something about his voice.

**JAKE:** You tried to talk?

Sora nodded.

**JAKE:** Did it work?

Sora shook his head.

Jake's expression fell.

**JAKE:** Oh.

He sat quietly for a moment.

**JAKE:** I'm sorry.

Sora looked toward the corridor where Guiding Light had disappeared.

His expression was difficult to interpret, but his behavior suggested growing frustration.

He eventually turned away.

A staff member later observed him attempting to vocalize once more.

Again, nothing.

### Subsequent interaction

Mia entered the den that evening.

**MIA:** Sora? Jake told me what happened.

Sora looked at her.

**MIA:** Guiding Light said she could give your voice back?

He nodded.

**MIA:** And it didn't work?

Sora nodded again.

Mia sat beside him.

**MIA:** I'm sorry.

Sora looked down.

Mia gently patted his head.

**MIA:** Maybe she needs to try again.

Sora shook his head.

He appeared to have already reached a conclusion.

In a later staff observation, he repeatedly looked toward the corridor and then away, displaying behavior consistent with distrust or disappointment.

One staff member recorded the following private comment:

**STAFF MEMBER:** I think he believes she lied to him.

**SPECIALIST:** He agreed to something he wanted very badly. Then nothing happened.

**STAFF MEMBER:** Guiding Light wouldn't lie about that.

**SPECIALIST:** You and I know that. Sora doesn't have to know it yet.

## 4. INCIDENT C — THE FOLLOWING MORNING

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE, Floor 7 common room  
**Time:** 08:07

The following morning, Jake and Mia were sitting together in the common room.

Sora was inside his den.

Jake was holding a small breakfast plate.

**JAKE:** Do you think he'll eat today?

**MIA:** We can leave it nearby.

**JAKE:** I wish he would come sit with us.

**MIA:** Me too.

Jake stood up.

**JAKE:** I'll go see him.

He carried the plate toward Sora's den.

Mia followed a little later.

### 08:11 — First audible vocalization

Jake crouched at the entrance.

**JAKE:** Morning, Sora!

Sora was sitting inside.

He looked toward Jake.

Jake placed the plate nearby.

**JAKE:** I brought breakfast.

Sora stared at him.

Then he opened his mouth.

**SORA:** ...Jake?

Jake froze.

The plate nearly slipped out of his hands.

For a moment, he did not seem to understand what he had heard.

**JAKE:** Huh?

Sora looked startled, too.

He touched his throat.

He tried again.

**SORA:** Jake.

The second attempt was clearer.

Jake's eyes widened.

**JAKE:** WAIT.

He turned toward the corridor.

**JAKE:** MIA!

Mia hurried over.

**MIA:** What happened?!

Jake pointed at Sora.

**JAKE:** HE—

He stopped, looked at Sora, and lowered his voice.

**JAKE:** Sora. Say something.

Sora looked between them.

His mouth opened hesitantly.

**SORA:** ...Mia?

Mia stopped moving.

Her expression changed immediately.

**MIA:** Sora?

He looked at her.

**SORA:** Mia.

Mia covered her mouth.

**MIA:** Oh my goodness.

Jake was practically vibrating.

**JAKE:** YOU CAN TALK!

Sora flinched slightly at the sudden volume.

**JAKE:** Sorry! Sorry!

He crouched again.

**JAKE:** You can talk!

Sora touched his throat once more.

**SORA:** ...I can?

His voice was quiet and rough with disuse, but it was unmistakably audible.

Mia's eyes filled with tears.

**MIA:** Yes. You can.

Sora looked confused.

**SORA:** My... voice?

**MIA:** It's back.

Sora attempted another word.

**SORA:** Back.

He paused.

Then he smiled faintly.

It was the first time in a long while that Sora had been able to communicate without writing, gesturing, or asking someone to interpret him.

Jake could barely contain himself.

**JAKE:** I'M GETTING EVERYONE!

**MIA:** Jake, wait—

He was already running.

## 5. INCIDENT D — THE FIRST CONVERSATION

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE, Floor 7  
**Time:** 08:19

Bao, Aries, Tom, and a welfare specialist arrived following Jake's announcement.

Jake was standing beside Sora's den, visibly excited.

**JAKE:** He talked! He actually talked!

**ARIES:** Slow down. What happened?

**JAKE:** Sora spoke!

Tom looked toward the den.

**TOM:** Really?

Sora was sitting at the entrance, visibly overwhelmed by the number of people gathering nearby.

Bao approached carefully.

**BAO:** Hello, sweetheart.

Sora looked up.

Bao crouched.

**BAO:** Can you hear me?

Sora nodded.

**BAO:** Can you say something?

Sora hesitated.

His throat moved as he tried to find the right words.

**SORA:** ...Mom?

Bao went completely still.

Then her expression softened.

**BAO:** Oh, sweetheart.

She gently patted his head.

**BAO:** Yes. I'm here.

Sora looked at her.

**SORA:** Mom.

Bao's eyes became wet.

**BAO:** I'm right here.

Jake was smiling so widely that his cheeks had begun to hurt.

**JAKE:** SAY MY NAME AGAIN!

**TOM:** Jake.

**JAKE:** What?!

**TOM:** Give him a moment.

Jake immediately quieted.

Sora looked toward him.

**SORA:** Jake.

Jake nearly burst into tears.

**JAKE:** YES!

**SORA:** Loud.

Aries laughed softly.

**ARIES:** Well, that's certainly a voice.

Even Sora seemed surprised by his own comment.

He touched his throat again.

**SORA:** ...I can talk.

**MIA:** You can.

**SORA:** I can talk.

This time, he sounded more certain.

Mia gently hugged him.

Sora did not pull away.

## 6. MEDICAL AND ANOMALOUS ASSESSMENT

The medical team conducted a preliminary examination.

The following findings were recorded:

- Sora was capable of producing audible speech.
    
- His vocalizations were initially quiet and hesitant.
    
- He demonstrated an ability to articulate familiar names and short phrases.
    
- He experienced no immediately apparent distress during the observed conversation.
    
- The previously severed vocal structures appeared to have undergone an anomalous restoration.
    

The medical team has not yet determined whether the intervention restored the original structures, reconstructed them, or established an alternative mechanism for vocalization.

Further examination is required.

The delayed manifestation is particularly unusual.

Guiding Light's intervention occurred the previous afternoon. At the time, no functional improvement was apparent.

The first confirmed vocalization occurred the following morning.

C.O.R.E. therefore considers it likely that the intervention required time to take effect.

This remains a preliminary conclusion.

### Important restrictions

Sora must not be pressured into speaking continuously to demonstrate that his voice has returned.

He has spent a considerable period unable to use it. His vocal ability should be assessed gradually, with medical guidance.

His restored voice also does not resolve his other difficulties.

His amnesia remains present. His original whiteboard remains destroyed. His emotional withdrawal and distrust following that loss require continued attention.

**Restoration of one ability must not be mistaken for restoration of the child as a whole.**

## 7. FOLLOW-UP — GUIDING LIGHT WAS NOT A FRAUD

At 10:30, Guiding Light was informed of the development.

She returned to Floor 7.

Sora was sitting with Mia and Jake when she approached.

He noticed her.

For a moment, he simply stared.

Guiding Light smiled.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Good morning, Sora.

Sora looked at her throat, then at her face.

He hesitated.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** How are you feeling?

Sora touched his own throat.

**SORA:** ...It works.

Guiding Light nodded.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** I'm glad.

Sora stared at her for another moment.

Then he spoke.

**SORA:** I thought...

He stopped, struggling to form the sentence.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Take your time.

**SORA:** I thought... you didn't.

Guiding Light tilted her head.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Didn't what?

Sora looked down.

**SORA:** Help.

There was a brief silence.

Then Guiding Light smiled gently.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** I understand.

Jake, who had been listening, immediately intervened.

**JAKE:** HE THOUGHT YOU WERE A FRAUD!

**MIA:** Jake!

Guiding Light laughed.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Well, I suppose I did make a rather poor first impression.

Sora looked at her.

**SORA:** Sorry.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** You don't need to apologize.

She crouched to his level.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** You were hoping for something very important. It didn't happen when you expected it to. I understand why you were disappointed.

Sora nodded.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** But your voice was never gone beyond my ability to reach.

She gently patted his head.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Welcome back, little one.

Sora looked at her.

Then, very quietly:

**SORA:** Thank you.

Guiding Light smiled.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** You're welcome.

## 8. CLOSING NOTE — THE FIRST WORDS

For months, Sora communicated through a small whiteboard.

He carried it everywhere.

When it was destroyed, he lost the means through which he had learned to speak to the people around him.

The replacement was never accepted.

The scarf Mia and Jake knitted for him was welcomed only cautiously.

The head pats helped him receive affection without needing to respond.

But none of those things gave him his voice.

Then Guiding Light offered something he had almost stopped believing was possible.

And when it did not work immediately, he thought she had deceived him.

The following morning, he said Jake's name.

Then Mia's.

Then Bao's.

And finally, he said something that had been absent from his life for a very long time:

"I can talk."

Jake was ecstatic.

Mia cried.

Bao called him sweetheart.

And Sora, for the first time in months, could answer them.

There is still much to address.

His original whiteboard is gone. His memories remain unreliable. His days inside the den have not been erased by a single morning of successful speech.

C.O.R.E. will not pretend otherwise.

But today, Sora does not have to point at his throat when he wants someone to understand.

He can call for Mia.

He can answer Jake.

He can tell Bao that he is cold, hungry, frightened, or simply wants company.

He can say his own name.

**For a child who spent so long being unable to make himself heard, the return of his voice is more than a medical development. It is another way back into the world.**

And this time, when Sora says something, the people around him can hear him.

— **C.O.R.E. Welfare Oversight**
