# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / RECOVERY OBSERVATION LOG**

# **ORPHIE — SECOND REJECTION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Concern  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / Marts / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Luna / Solaria  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **REGRESSION OBSERVED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The items previously returned to Orphie have been discarded again.

The sketchbook.

The broken pencil.

Her mother's cookbook.

Her hats.

Everything.

Orphie did not hesitate this time.

---

# **THE RETURN**

The belongings had remained in the treehouse for several days.

Orphie had not used them.

She had not drawn.

She had not cooked.

She had not worn the hats.

She had barely touched any of them.

Corin eventually found the items gathered together near the doorway.

---

# **THE SECOND REJECTION**

Corin entered the room.

The sketchbook was on the floor.

The two halves of the pencil were beside it.

The cookbook had been placed underneath.

The hats had been thrown into a pile.

Corin stopped.

> **Corin:** "...You threw them away again."

Orphie:

> "...Yes."

> "...Why?"

> "...Because I don't want them."

Corin looked at the objects.

> "...You used to love these."

Orphie:

> "...I know."

---

# **THE COOKBOOK**

Corin picked up the cookbook.

> "...You don't have to cook from it."

Orphie:

> "...Then throw it away."

Corin:

> "...No."

Orphie looked at her.

> "...I said throw it away."

> "...And I said no."

Silence.

---

# **THE PENCIL**

Corin picked up the broken pencil.

She had kept it for a long time.

> "...This one matters."

Orphie:

> "...It doesn't."

> "...It mattered to you."

> "...That was before."

Corin looked at her.

> "...Before what?"

Orphie did not answer.

---

# **THE SKETCHBOOK**

The sketchbook was opened briefly.

The disturbing drawings were still there.

So were the old ones.

Corin turned to one of the earlier pages.

A cheerful drawing.

A Mart.

A sun.

Several people together.

Orphie looked away.

> "...Close it."

Corin did.

She did not ask anything else.

---

# **THE HATS**

The hats were perhaps the easiest to explain.

Orphie had stopped wearing them because she had learned to intentionally shift into Luna and Solaria.

She no longer wanted the reminder of why she used to wear them.

She picked up the beret.

Looked at it.

Then threw it into the discard pile.

The same happened with the others.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin did not try to stop her.

She knew that forcing Orphie to keep the objects would only turn them into another form of control.

Instead, she gathered them afterward.

Orphie noticed.

> "...Why are you picking them up?"

> "...Because they're still yours."

> "...I threw them away."

> "...I know."

> "...Then leave them there."

Corin paused.

> "...Okay."

For once, Corin did not retrieve them.

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts observed the incident from outside.

They did not approach.

They were still frightened of Orphie.

One Mart watched as the cookbook was thrown away.

Another watched the sketchbook hit the floor.

Neither entered.

They had learned not to interfere.

---

# **LUNA**

Luna was visibly saddened by the development.

> "...She rejected them again."

Solaria answered:

> "...She wasn't ready."

Luna:

> "...Maybe she doesn't want to be."

Solaria:

> "...Maybe."

---

# **THE MEANING**

C.O.R.E. personnel initially considered the return of Orphie's belongings a positive development.

This assessment was premature.

Orphie did not interpret their return as an opportunity.

She interpreted them as reminders.

Reminders of:

- Happiness.
    
- Her mother.
    
- Drawing.
    
- Cooking.
    
- The Marts.
    
- Her old identity.
    
- The person she believed she had lost.
    

And she does not want those reminders.

---

# **ORPHIE'S POSITION**

When Corin asked:

> "...Do you really want all of it gone?"

Orphie answered:

> "...Yes."

> "...Everything?"

> "...Everything."

> "...Even the things you loved?"

Orphie looked away.

> "...Especially those."

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Sketchbook:** Discarded again.

**Broken pencil:** Discarded again.

**Mother's cookbook:** Discarded again.

**Hats:** Discarded again.

**Marts:** Maintaining distance.

**Corin:** Respecting Orphie's immediate wishes while remaining nearby.

**Orphie:** Continues rejecting reminders of her former identity.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The first time Orphie threw everything away, there was uncertainty.

The second time, there was intent.

She knows exactly what these objects represent.

She knows they once made her happy.

She knows people care about them because they care about **her**.

And she rejects them anyway.

Not because she forgot.

Because she remembers.

The pencil reminds her that she once loved drawing.

The cookbook reminds her of her mother.

The hats remind her of who she was.

The sketchbook reminds her that she once filled pages with hope.

The Marts remind her that she once cared deeply for others.

She threw all of it away again.

And this time, Corin did not pick it back up.

She simply remained nearby.

**End of Recovery Observation Log**
