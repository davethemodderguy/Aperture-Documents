# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At 04:31, Phoenix Raffedella's location signal disappeared.

Her phone battery reached **0%**.

The location-sharing connection with Belle terminated.

No final emergency transmission was received.

No distress signal was triggered.

No final message was sent.

The last known location was recorded successfully before the device powered down.

After that:

> **Nothing.**

Phoenix's location is now unknown.

The SAFEHOUSE has entered an active missing-person response.

---

# 1. LAST KNOWN SIGNAL

Belle's phone displayed Phoenix's location normally until:

**04:30:51**

The marker stopped moving.

For approximately nine seconds, Belle assumed Phoenix had simply stopped walking.

Then the status changed.

> **DEVICE OFFLINE**

Belle refreshed the location.

Nothing.

She refreshed it again.

Nothing.

She opened the emergency contact interface.

The phone reported:

> **LAST LOCATION AVAILABLE**

No new coordinates were received.

---

# 2. BATTERY STATUS

C.O.R.E. later confirmed the reason.

Phoenix's phone battery had reached:

> **0%**

The device shut down normally.

There was no evidence of:

- remote interference;
    
- signal jamming;
    
- device destruction;
    
- deliberate disabling;
    
- removal of the SIM-equivalent;
    
- or anomalous communication disruption.
    

The phone simply ran out of power.

Under ordinary circumstances, this would not be particularly concerning.

Under the current circumstances:

**It is extremely concerning.**
