# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake remains unable to speak following his Nullwalker recovery.

His vocal cords were severely damaged during the five-week blizzard exposure.

Despite this, his general mood has shown measurable improvement.

A significant factor appears to be **Anby.**

Jake has developed a particular fondness for Anby's voice.

Specifically:

> **Her soft voice.**

And, apparently, her tendency to mumble.

Jake cannot currently respond verbally.

He has instead developed an increasingly expressive system of gestures whenever Anby talks to him.

C.O.R.E. has determined that Jake is not merely tolerating Anby's presence.

He is having fun.

---

# 1. INITIAL OBSERVATION

Anby was sitting beside Jake in the SAFEHOUSE lounge.

Jake was wrapped in a blanket.

His teddy bear was resting against his stomach.

Anby was quietly reading.

She occasionally read passages aloud.

Not because Jake asked.

She simply happened to be reading out loud.

Jake eventually looked up.

He stared at her.

Anby stopped.

> **ANBY:**  
> “What?”

Jake pointed at her.

Then pointed at his own throat.

Then made a small gesture toward her.

Anby appeared confused.

> **ANBY:**  
> “You like my voice?”

Jake immediately nodded.

Anby froze.

---

# 2. ANBY'S REACTION

Anby's ears turned red.

She looked away.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Oh.”

Jake continued looking at her.

Anby quietly resumed reading.

After approximately ten seconds, she noticed Jake smiling.

She lowered the book.

> **ANBY:**  
> “You're listening.”

Jake nodded.

Anby smiled.

Then she continued.

---

# 3. THE MUMBLE EFFECT

Anby naturally speaks softly.

She also mumbles when she is tired, embarrassed, or simply speaking casually.

Jake appears to find this entertaining.

During one interaction, Anby said:

> **ANBY:**  
> “I think... maybe we could... um...”

She stopped.

Jake looked at her.

Anby frowned.

> **ANBY:**  
> “What?”

Jake pointed toward his mouth.

Then mimicked her mumbling.

Anby stared.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Are you making fun of me?”

Jake immediately shook his head.

Then nodded.

Anby narrowed her eyes.

Jake started laughing silently.

---

# 4. ANBY DISCOVERS SOMETHING

Anby eventually realized that Jake's inability to speak did not prevent him from being playful.

She tested this.

She leaned toward him.

> **ANBY:**  
> “You're funny.”

Jake nodded.

> **ANBY:**  
> “And annoying.”

Jake shook his head.

> **ANBY:**  
> “A little annoying.”

Jake shook his head harder.

Anby smiled.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Very annoying.”

Jake pointed accusingly at her.

Anby laughed.

Jake laughed silently.

---

# 5. THE VOICE GAME

The interaction eventually became a game.

Anby would say something.

Jake would respond with gestures.

For example:

> **ANBY:**  
> “Cookie?”

Jake nods.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Two?”

Jake holds up two fingers.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Three?”

Jake immediately holds up three.

Anby stares.

> **ANBY:**  
> “You just wanted three.”

Jake nods.

> **ANBY:**  
> “You could've just asked.”

Jake points at his throat.

Anby pauses.

Then laughs quietly.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Right.”

---

# 6. READING TO JAKE

Anby has begun reading to Jake regularly.

Nothing particularly important.

Sometimes a book.

Sometimes an old document.

Sometimes completely mundane material.

Jake does not seem to care what she reads.

He cares that she is talking.

On one occasion, Anby spent fifteen minutes reading a technical document about Blacksite maintenance.

Jake remained completely attentive.

Twiggy entered the room.

She looked at Jake.

Then at Anby.

Then at the document.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> “He's actually listening to that?”

Anby nodded.

> **ANBY:**  
> “He likes my voice.”

Twiggy smiled.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> “Apparently.”

Jake nodded.

---

# 7. JAKE'S FAVORITE PART

Jake appears particularly amused when Anby speaks quietly.

If she speaks normally:

Jake listens.

If she whispers:

Jake leans closer.

If she mumbles:

Jake smiles.

If she accidentally trails off mid-sentence:

Jake looks at her expectantly until she continues.

Anby has noticed.

She now occasionally does it deliberately.

> **ANBY:**  
> “So then I was thinking maybe we could...”

She stops.

Jake stares.

Anby waits.

Jake points at her.

> **ANBY:**  
> “You want me to finish?”

Jake nods.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Okay.”

She continues.

Jake smiles.

---

# 8. ANBY'S VOICE BECOMES A COMFORT

The Medical Division has also observed that Jake appears to relax more quickly when Anby is speaking.

If he becomes restless:

Anby talks.

If he becomes anxious:

Anby talks.

If he has difficulty falling asleep:

Anby reads.

She does not need to say anything profound.

Sometimes she simply describes what she is doing.

> **ANBY:**  
> “I'm getting water.”

Pause.

> **ANBY:**  
> “I'm putting it here.”

Pause.

> **ANBY:**  
> “I'm sitting down.”

Jake watches her.

His breathing slows.

---

# 9. ANBY DOES NOT NEED TO ENTERTAIN HIM

Twiggy eventually reminded Anby that she did not need to constantly talk to Jake.

Anby understood.

She does not speak continuously.

Sometimes they simply sit together.

Jake may point at something.

Anby responds.

Anby may say something.

Jake reacts.

Silence remains normal.

The important difference is that Jake now has the option of hearing someone he likes whenever he wants.

---

# 10. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake has developed a very specific gesture.

When Anby starts talking and he wants her to continue, he taps the side of his teddy bear twice.

This has become informally known as:

> **“Again.”**

Anby understands it.

The first time she figured it out, she looked at him.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Again?”

Jake nodded.

Anby smiled.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Okay.”

She kept talking.

---

# 11. ONE PARTICULAR EVENING

Jake was lying on the couch.

Anby sat beside him.

His teddy bear was tucked against his chest.

Anby quietly described her day.

She talked about training.

About food.

About how Iris nearly drove into a wall.

About Solider 11 arguing with Phaethon.

About Twiggy stealing someone's snacks.

Jake reacted with small gestures throughout.

Eventually Anby stopped.

> **ANBY:**  
> “I'm running out of things to say.”

Jake looked at her.

He tapped the teddy bear twice.

Anby smiled.

> **ANBY:**  
> “You want me to keep going?”

Jake nodded.

Anby thought for a moment.

Then:

> **ANBY:**  
> “Okay.”

She continued.

Jake closed his eyes.

He fell asleep while listening to her.

---

# 12. C.O.R.E. OBSERVATION

Jake's recovery remains medically concerning.

He is still weak.

He cannot speak.

He requires substantial rest and assistance.

However, his willingness to engage socially has increased.

This is particularly notable because the interaction does not require Jake to overcome his current vocal impairment.

He can simply listen.

Anby does not expect answers.

She does not pressure him to speak.

She does not ask him to explain himself.

She talks.

Jake listens.

And occasionally he laughs.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake cannot talk yet.

But he can still laugh.

He can still smile.

He can still point at Anby when she says something ridiculous.

He can still tease her about mumbling.

And he can still make the unmistakable gesture that means:

> **“Keep talking.”**

Anby has discovered that her voice can make Jake feel safe.

Jake has discovered that Anby apparently has an unlimited supply of very quiet words.

Neither seems particularly unhappy with the arrangement.

For now, Jake doesn't need to speak.

He just needs someone whose voice he likes.

And apparently—

Anby's is his favorite.

**— C.O.R.E. Resident Welfare & Medical Division**
