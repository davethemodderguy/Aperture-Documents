# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately 15:42, Clover returned to the residence she shared with Jake Akana.

She had intended to check on him.

Jake had been sick for several weeks. His condition had left him spending considerably more time in his room, and the SAFEHOUSE residents had been attempting to balance their concern for him against their respective work responsibilities.

That afternoon, everyone was occupied.

Clover was the first to return.

She found bloodstains on the door.

When she opened it, she discovered Jake lying on the floor.

He had sustained **two stab wounds to the abdomen and one to the neck.**

He was losing blood rapidly.

His teddy bear, which contained his actual heartbeat and served as his cardiac vessel, had been thrown violently enough to leave visible damage and displacement.

Numerous handwritten notes were scattered throughout the room.

Several contained the same questions:

> **"Who are you?"**

> **"What are you doing?"**

> **"Why are you hrting me"**

One note had not been completed.

The handwriting deteriorated toward the end of the page. The final line stopped midway through a word.

The evidence indicates that Jake was conscious during at least part of the assault.

He attempted to communicate with the assailant.

He attempted to resist.

He appears to have fought back.

He lost.

By the time Clover found him, his blood was spreading across the floor and being absorbed into the grass beneath the residence's anomalous environmental substrate.

Clover immediately raised the alarm.

Perimeter Security responded.

Bao, Aries, Phoenix, and Director Lucious were summoned.

Aries subsequently provided a blood transfusion under emergency medical supervision after Jake's severe blood loss left him critically unstable.

Jake lost consciousness during the emergency response.

His condition remains critical.

The assailant has not yet been identified.

**The SAFEHOUSE is a residential facility. Jake was attacked inside his own room.**

The fact that the incident occurred while everyone was working is not an explanation that makes the outcome acceptable.

It is a security failure that C.O.R.E. must investigate.

# 5. EVIDENCE OF A STRUGGLE

The physical scene was inconsistent with an entirely passive medical collapse.

Several pieces of furniture had been displaced.

There were marks on the floor that required further forensic examination.

The teddy bear had been thrown away from its usual position.

Multiple notes had appeared at different locations within the room.

The distribution of blood indicated movement during at least part of the incident.

Most significantly, Jake's written messages repeatedly addressed an unidentified person.

He asked who they were.

He asked what they were doing.

He asked why they were hurting him.

He asked them to stop.

These messages suggest that Jake was aware of an individual who was causing him harm.

The physical evidence also indicates that Jake attempted to resist.

The precise sequence remains under investigation.

C.O.R.E. cannot yet establish whether the assailant entered through the door, was already inside the residence, or accessed the room through an anomalous mechanism.

No conclusion has been reached regarding motive.

The incident is being treated as a deliberate assault until evidence establishes otherwise.

# 12. PRELIMINARY SECURITY FINDINGS

The investigation remains active.

The following findings have been established.

### 12.1 Evidence of Violent Entry or Confrontation

Bloodstains on the door and the arrangement of objects inside the room indicate that the incident involved more than a sudden medical collapse.

The precise point of entry remains undetermined.

### 12.2 Multiple Written Messages

The notes indicate that Jake attempted to communicate with an unidentified individual during the incident.

The messages are consistent with confusion, distress, and attempts to understand or stop the assault.

They do not independently establish the assailant's identity.

### 12.3 Displaced Teddy Bear

The teddy bear was found away from its usual position, with visible signs of forceful handling.

Given that the bear is Jake's cardiac vessel, the circumstances are being treated as an additional critical concern.

The investigation will determine whether the bear was deliberately targeted or displaced during the struggle.

### 12.4 Evidence of Resistance

The arrangement of the room, the distribution of blood, and the written notes collectively support the conclusion that Jake attempted to resist the assailant.

The available evidence does not yet establish how long the struggle lasted or whether Jake was able to injure the assailant.

### 12.5 No Confirmed Suspect

No individual has yet been conclusively identified.

All persons with access to the residence are being accounted for.

The possibility of unauthorized access, credential misuse, or anomalous intrusion remains under investigation.

# 13. RESIDENT WELFARE REVIEW

This incident occurred during a period when the SAFEHOUSE residents were occupied with their respective responsibilities.

Jake had been sick for weeks.

His condition had made him more dependent on rest and familiar surroundings.

Although his ability to move had returned, he continued to rely heavily on his established means of communication and his trusted relationships.

He had spent the afternoon alone.

That fact does not establish that solitude caused the assault.

Nor does it establish that another resident would necessarily have prevented it.

Responsibility for the attack belongs to the assailant.

However, the incident exposes a separate question concerning residential safety.

The SAFEHOUSE had permitted a sick resident to remain alone in a private room without an immediate way to summon assistance that did not depend on his own initiative.

Jake could manifest notes, but that ability was not a substitute for an emergency response system.

During the assault, he produced messages asking who the attacker was, what they were doing, and why they were hurting him.

He attempted to make the violence stop.

There is no indication that those notes reached another resident before Clover returned.

C.O.R.E. will therefore review emergency communication options for residents who may become unable to reach a conventional alarm or communicator.

The objective is not to require constant surveillance of every private room.

It is to ensure that residents have practical ways to request assistance and that the security system can respond when a violent intrusion occurs.
