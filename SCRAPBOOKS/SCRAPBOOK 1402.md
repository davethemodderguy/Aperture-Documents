# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Anby's voice continues to produce an unexpectedly strong reaction among SAFEHOUSE residents and C.O.R.E. personnel.

The issue is not volume.

It is not unusual speech.

It is not anomalous vocalization.

It is simply the fact that **Anby sounds extremely young.**

Her voice is soft.

Her words are frequently mumbled.

Her tone is naturally cute.

And, despite being the **eldest member of the twelve Project Silverline sisters**, she sounds younger than all five of her surviving sisters.

This has resulted in repeated incidents where residents stop what they are doing simply because Anby said something.

---

# 1. THE PROBLEM

Anby can enter a room, say approximately three words, and cause everyone present to look at her.

Example:

> **ANBY:**  
> "...I'm hungry."

The room becomes silent.

Anby looks around.

> **ANBY:**  
> "...What?"

Nobody answers.

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Why are you looking at me?"

Several residents immediately look away.

The effect is apparently involuntary.

---

# 2. ANBY'S MUMBLING

Anby frequently lowers her voice while speaking.

This produces sentences such as:

> **ANBY:**  
> "I was just wondering if maybe we could..."

Pause.

> **ANBY:**  
> "...go outside."

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "What?"

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Go outside."

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "Sorry."

> **ANBY:**  
> "...It's okay."

The problem is not that Anby is difficult to understand.

The problem is that personnel instinctively lean closer to hear her.

Anby notices.

She becomes embarrassed.

Her voice gets even quieter.

This creates a feedback loop.

---

# 3. THE AGE CONFUSION

Anby is the eldest of the twelve sisters.

This fact continues to cause confusion.

A newly introduced resident was once asked:

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> "Which one is Anby?"

The resident pointed toward Anby.

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "The youngest?"

Silence.

Anby stared at them.

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Eldest."

The resident immediately apologized.

Anby nodded.

> **ANBY:**  
> "...It's okay."

The resident subsequently apologized again.

---

# 4. TWIGGY'S RESPONSE

Twiggy appears to find Anby's voice endearing.

She has also developed a habit of answering Anby's mumbled questions without asking her to repeat herself.

Example:

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Do you think this is okay?"

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "Yes."

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Okay."

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "You were asking about the cake, weren't you?"

Anby nodded.

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Yeah."

Twiggy smiled.

Anby immediately looked away.

---

# 5. SOLIDER 11'S RESPONSE

Solider 11 appears to have discovered that Anby's voice is remarkably effective against her own usual persuasive demeanor.

During one conversation:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Anby, are you coming with us?"

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Maybe."

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "That's not an answer."

> **ANBY:**  
> "...I don't know."

Solider 11 stared at her for several seconds.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "...You can't say it like that."

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Like what?"

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Never mind."

---

> **SORA:**

# 7. IRIS'S RESPONSE

Iris has taken the opposite approach.

> **ANBY:**

> "...I'm not."

> **IRIS:**  
> "STOP SAYING SORRY LIKE THAT."

---

# 8. PHAETHON'S RESPONSE

Phaethon has remained comparatively composed.

However, C.O.R.E. personnel have documented several instances where Phaethon visibly pauses after Anby speaks.

During one incident:

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Phaethon?"

> **PHAETHON:**  
> "Yes?"

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Can you help me?"

Phaethon paused.

Approximately two seconds.

> **PHAETHON:**  
> "...Yes."

No further explanation was provided.

---

# 9. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri appears to have adapted quickly.

She does not comment on Anby's voice.

She simply listens.

During one conversation:

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Neri?"

Neri looked up.

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Do you have any more coffee?"

Neri stared at her.

Then pointed toward the kitchen.

Anby nodded.

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Thanks."

Neri returned to her work.

No incident occurred.

This may make Neri one of the few residents who is completely unaffected.

---

# 12. THE MOST COMMON RESPONSE

Across multiple observations, the most common reaction remains:

**Pause.**

Someone hears Anby speak.

They stop.

They look at her.

They realize they are staring.

They look away.

Anby notices.

Anby becomes embarrassed.

Anby mumbles something even quieter.

Everyone looks back.

The cycle continues.

---

# 13. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

There is no evidence that Anby intentionally uses her voice to influence people.

Her vocal characteristics appear to be entirely natural.

She is simply:

- soft-spoken;
    
- youthful-sounding;
    
- naturally cute in tone;
    
- frequently quiet;
    
- and prone to mumbling.
    

Her personality does not necessarily correspond to the apparent age of her voice.

She remains the eldest surviving sister.

The voice is merely...

Unexpected.

---

## FINAL OBSERVATION

Project Silverline created Anby as the eldest of twelve.

She survived.

She endured repeated deployments.

She protected her sisters.

She carries more operational history than any of the surviving sisters.

She is the eldest.

She is experienced.

She is highly capable.

She is also apparently capable of walking into a room and quietly saying:

> **"...Hi."**

before half the room turns around like someone just activated a universal cuteness alarm.

Anby usually notices.

She usually mumbles:

> **"...Why is everyone looking at me?"**

Nobody has yet found a satisfactory answer.
