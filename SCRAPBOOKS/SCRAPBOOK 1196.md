# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neri was occupied with an extended chemistry research session.

Alice was away from SAFEHOUSE.

Nora therefore required temporary supervision.

Neri considered the available options.

She ultimately selected Jane Doe.

The reasoning was apparently straightforward:

> **NERI:**  
> "Jane."

> **JANE:**  
> "Yeah?"

> **NERI:**  
> "Can you watch Nora?"

Jane paused.

> **JANE:**  
> "...Today?"

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> "I'm busy."

Jane looked toward Nora.

Nora was currently attempting to balance a spoon on her nose.

Jane looked back at Neri.

> **JANE:**  
> "Sure."

Neri returned to her laboratory.

Jane was now responsible for a six-year-old Shark Thiren.

---

# 2. JANE'S INITIAL ASSESSMENT

Jane has extensive experience with:

- intelligence operations;
    
- field extraction;
    
- interrogation;
    
- psychological manipulation;
    
- hostile environments;
    
- anomalous personnel;
    
- SAFEHOUSE residents;
    
- and children.
    

She nevertheless admitted that Nora represented a somewhat unusual combination.

> **JANE:**  
> "She's six."

Pause.

> **JANE:**  
> "She's also a Tachyon."

Another pause.

> **JANE:**  
> "And she's Nora."

Jane sighed.

> **JANE:**  
> "Okay."

---

# 3. NERI'S INSTRUCTIONS

Neri provided Jane with a remarkably short list of instructions.

> **NERI:**  
> "Make sure she eats."

> **JANE:**  
> "Okay."

> **NERI:**  
> "Don't let her get hurt."

> **JANE:**  
> "Obviously."

> **NERI:**  
> "Don't let her steal anything."

Jane looked at Nora.

Nora immediately looked away.

> **JANE:**  
> "...Noted."

> **NERI:**  
> "And if she bites you, don't panic."

Jane raised an eyebrow.

> **JANE:**  
> "Why would she bite me?"

Nora looked at Jane.

Jane looked at Nora.

Nora smiled.

Jane sighed.

> **JANE:**  
> "Right."

---

# 4. HOUR ONE

Jane attempted to begin with a simple activity.

> **JANE:**  
> "Want to watch something?"

> **NORA:**  
> "No."

> **JANE:**  
> "Draw?"

> **NORA:**  
> "No."

> **JANE:**  
> "Read?"

> **NORA:**  
> "No."

> **JANE:**  
> "Cake?"

Nora immediately turned around.

> **NORA:**  
> "Yes."

Jane nodded.

> **JANE:**  
> "Excellent."

---

# 5. THE CAKE INCIDENT

Jane provided Nora with a slice of cake.

Nora ate quietly.

Jane watched.

> **JANE:**  
> "You really like cake."

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> "Sis likes cake too."

> **JANE:**  
> "Yeah."

Nora took another bite.

> **NORA:**  
> "She makes really good cake."

> **JANE:**  
> "She does."

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> "You're not as good."

Jane stared at her.

Nora continued eating.

> **JANE:**  
> "...I haven't made you anything."

> **NORA:**  
> "I know."

> **JANE:**  
> "Then how do you know?"

Nora shrugged.

> **NORA:**  
> "I just know."

Jane quietly reconsidered whether she had made the correct childcare decision.

---

# 6. THE JOURNAL

Eventually Nora retrieved her family journal.

Jane noticed the journal clipped to Nora's waist.

> **JANE:**  
> "You carry that everywhere?"

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> "It's our family."

Jane's expression softened.

> **JANE:**  
> "Want to show me?"

Nora immediately opened it.

For the next forty minutes, Jane listened to Nora explain the Tachyon family.

Names.

Photographs.

Drawings.

Stories.

Little pieces of history.

Jane did not interrupt.

She simply listened.

---

# 8. THE BITE

Approximately one hour into the assignment, Nora bit Jane's sleeve.

Jane looked down.

Nora released it.

> **JANE:**  
> "...Was that necessary?"

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> "Yes."

> **JANE:**  
> "Why?"

> **NORA:**  
> "Because."

> **JANE:**  
> "You and your sister have the same answer."

Nora grinned.

---

# 9. THE TAIL INCIDENT

Jane eventually sat down on the couch.

Nora sat beside her.

Jane's rat tail was visible.

Nora stared at it.

Jane noticed.

> **JANE:**  
> "Don't."

Nora looked innocent.

> **NORA:**  
> "I'm not doing anything."

Jane narrowed her eyes.

> **JANE:**  
> "I've heard that before."

Nora continued staring.

Jane moved her tail.

Nora followed it with her eyes.

> **JANE:**  
> "No."

Nora laughed.

# 10. THE AFTERNOON

By the afternoon, the two had settled into a surprisingly comfortable routine.

Nora:

- ate cake;
    
- drew in her journal;
    
- talked about Neri;
    
- asked Jane questions;
    
- explored SAFEHOUSE;
    
- sat beside Jane;
    
- occasionally attempted minor mischief;
    
- and repeatedly tried to convince Jane that she needed another slice of cake.
    

Jane:

- supervised;
    
- answered questions;
    
- prevented several questionable decisions;
    
- listened to family stories;
    
- provided food;
    
- and successfully prevented Nora from stealing anything important.
    

The last achievement was considered significant.

---

Neri eventually left her laboratory.

She found Jane sitting on the couch.

Jane was holding a blanket over her.

Neri stopped.

> "She was good."

> **NERI:**

> **JANE:**

Then at Jane.

> "...Yes."

> "Okay."

> **JANE:**  
> "...That's it?"

> **NERI:**

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Neri had a problem.

She was busy.

Alice was away.

Nora needed someone to watch her.

So Neri did what was apparently the most logical thing available.

She handed her six-year-old Shark Thiren sister to Jane Doe.

Jane, former Woodland Coorp field agent, intelligence specialist, psychological warfare expert, and generally terrifying professional operative, spent the afternoon:

**feeding Nora cake,**

**listening to family stories,**

**preventing her from stealing things,**

**getting bitten,**

and **watching cartoons.**

Jane later summarized the experience:

> **"I've interrogated people who were less exhausting."**

She nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> "Jane's nice."

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> "Can I stay with her again?"

Jane looked over.

> **JANE:**  
> "Sure."

Nora smiled.

Then immediately added:

> **NORA:**  
> "Can I bite you again?"

Jane's smile disappeared.

> **JANE:**
