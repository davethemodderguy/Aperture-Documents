---

# 2. THE CHALLENGE

For an extended period, Neri's chemistry challenge remained undefeated.

MSAC personnel attempted it.

Researchers attempted it.

Engineers attempted it.

Personnel with considerable confidence in their chemistry knowledge attempted it.

Nobody succeeded.

Then someone finally solved Neri's question.

Completely.

Correctly.

No hints.

No external assistance.

No correction from Neri.

The final answer was submitted.

Neri reviewed it.

She looked at the contestant.

Then at the answer.

Then back at the contestant.

Silence.

Finally:

> **Neri:**  
> “Correct.”

The room reportedly went completely silent.

---

# 3. THE MOMENT

The contestant initially appeared unsure.

> **Contestant:**  
> “...That's it?”

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:**  
> “That's it.”

> **Contestant:**  
> “I actually got it?”

Neri nodded again.

> **Neri:**  
> “Yes.”

The contestant apparently required several seconds to process this.

Someone behind him reportedly whispered:

> **“HE DID IT.”**

The room immediately erupted.

Neri simply picked up her coffee.
