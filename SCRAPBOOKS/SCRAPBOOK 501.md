### **PERSONNEL / RESIDENTIAL COMPANION RECORD**

# **CORIN'S BLUE JAY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Internal Personnel  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Companion Type:** Avian  
**Species:** Blue Jay  
**Status:** **ACTIVE / HEALTHY / VERY SPOILED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has acquired a pet blue jay.

The bird was not formally assigned to her.

There was no containment request.

No anomalous acquisition procedure.

No C.O.R.E. intervention.

It simply started visiting the treehouse.

Then it kept coming back.

Eventually, Corin began leaving food outside.

The blue jay decided the treehouse was its home.

That was apparently enough.

---

# **THE BIRD**

The blue jay is a completely ordinary bird.

No anomalous properties have been detected.

No Ether exposure has been observed.

No dimensional origin has been identified.

It is simply:

**A blue jay.**

Corin has not given it an elaborate anomalous designation.

She just calls it:

> **"Jay."**

---

# **WHY CORIN LIKES IT**

The timing of the bird's arrival was significant.

Corin had only recently regained her ability to perceive color.

Blue was among the first colors she became fascinated with.

Then a blue bird landed on the treehouse railing.

Corin stared at it.

For perhaps thirty seconds.

> **"...You're blue."**

The bird chirped.

Corin smiled.

That was essentially the beginning of the relationship.

---

# **CORIN AND JAY**

Jay quickly became comfortable around Corin.

It sits on her shoulder.

It lands on her hat.

It occasionally attempts to steal the hairclip from her bangs.

Corin does not appreciate the last one.

> **Corin:**  
> "Give that back."

Jay:

> **"Chirp."**

> **Corin:**  
> "...You know exactly what you did."

---

# **THE BEANIE INCIDENT**

Jay has developed a particular fascination with Corin's red wool beanie.

It frequently lands on top of her head.

This creates a rather amusing sight:

A girl wearing a red wool cat-eared beanie...

...with a blue jay sitting directly on top of it.

Orphie finds this adorable.

Corin pretends she doesn't.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light immediately became fond of Jay.

She considers the bird a useful reminder of why her intervention mattered.

One afternoon, Guiding Light watched Corin feed Jay.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "You really like him."

Corin nodded.

> **"He's blue."**

Guiding Light smiled.

> **"Yes."**

Corin looked at the bird.

> **"...I like blue."**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has accepted Jay as part of the household.

She makes sure the bird has appropriate food and a safe place to stay.

She also occasionally has to retrieve Jay from places he absolutely should not be.

One evening:

> **Orphie:**  
> "Corin."

> **Corin:**  
> "Hm?"

> **Orphie:**  
> "Why is your bird inside the pantry?"

Corin looked over.

Jay stared back.

> **Corin:**  
> "...He likes it."

> **Orphie:**  
> "He is standing on the flour."

Jay chirped.

> **Corin:**  
> "...He likes flour."

> **Orphie:**  
> "No."

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT**

Curious Light initially became interested in Jay's behavior.

He began observing:

- Flight patterns.
    
- Feeding preferences.
    
- Social behavior.
    
- Problem-solving ability.
    
- Interaction with Corin.
    

Corin immediately noticed.

> **"Don't experiment on him."**

Curious Light paused.

> **"...I wasn't going to."**

Corin stared.

> **"...Okay."**

He stopped.

---

# **THE SAFEHOUSE**

The SAFEHOUSE residents have largely accepted Jay.

Neko likes him.

Naturally.

Miss Neko has already warned Neko not to bring the bird random objects.

Belle occasionally leaves treats.

Endmin keeps an eye on him.

Bao finds him adorable.

Frost likes watching him outside.

Taiga ignores him.

Mart tries to follow him.

Jay is terrified of the Mart.

Nobody understands why.

---

# **CORIN'S NOTEBOOK**

One recent entry reads:

> **I have a bird now.**

> **He's blue.**

> **I like blue.**

Then:

> **He keeps stealing my hat.**

A final sentence:

> **I think he likes me.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's recovery has not been dramatic.

It has happened through small things.

Food.

Color.

The forest.

Orphie's presence.

Guiding Light's notes.

And now:

A blue jay.

Corin does not need the bird to survive.

The bird does not need Corin to survive.

But they seem to enjoy being around each other.

Sometimes that is enough.

Jay lands on Corin's shoulder.

Corin gently scratches beneath his feathers.

She looks toward the forest.

The world is colorful again.

And for once, she doesn't need to wonder whether she is alone.

She has a very small, very blue companion sitting beside her.

**End of Personnel / Residential Companion Record**
