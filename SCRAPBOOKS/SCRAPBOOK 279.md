# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **CAFETERIA BULLETIN**

### **SUBJECT: UPDATED KITCHEN POLICY – DIALYN**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **POLICY REVISION**

Following multiple successful meals prepared by Dialyn, Cafeteria Services has officially revised its previous recommendation.

The old policy stated:

> **"Do not let Dialyn cook."**

The new policy now reads:

> **"Let Dialyn cook."**

There is, however...

One important condition.

---

# **COOKING PERFORMANCE**

Extensive observation has demonstrated that Dialyn possesses an exceptional ability to estimate ingredients without measuring.

Repeated evaluations have shown remarkable consistency in:

- Portion sizes.
    
- Cooking times.
    
- Liquid ratios.
    
- Ingredient balance.
    
- Texture.
    

Although she cooks almost entirely by instinct, the resulting meals consistently receive positive evaluations.

---

# **THE EXCEPTION**

Personnel are reminded that Dialyn **cannot taste salt**.

To compensate, she naturally gravitates toward stronger flavors.

Historically, this has resulted in... enthusiastic use of chili peppers.

Recently, another trend has been identified.

If given unrestricted access to black pepper, white pepper, or similar spices, Dialyn has a habit of continuing to season until the food reaches the level of intensity **she** finds satisfying.

Most other people reach that point much earlier.

Accordingly, Cafeteria Services has issued a single recommendation.

> **Let Dialyn cook.**

> **Don't let her take the pepper.**

> "...Why the pepper?"

Belle silently held up a pepper grinder.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Dialyn remains one of the most trusted cooks among SAFEHOUSE residents, particularly when preparing simple comfort meals.

Her ability to judge ingredients by instinct has become something of a mystery to the kitchen staff.

The pepper grinder, however, now lives on the highest shelf in the pantry whenever she cooks.

Not because anyone doubts her skills.

Just because everyone enjoys being able to taste the rest of the meal.
