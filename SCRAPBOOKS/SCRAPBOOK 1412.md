# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has been located.

He was found following an accidental conversation between **Bao** and the female Perimeter Security Guard who previously discovered him inside a Perimeter Security Outpost.

The conversation revealed that Jake had been outside the Blacksite during the five-week blizzard.

Bao was unaware.

More concerningly:

> **Bao did not remember Jake being present at all.**

This prompted an immediate welfare inquiry.

At dinner, Bao asked the assembled SAFEHOUSE residents:

> **“Anyone seeing Jake around?”**

The response was immediate confusion.

When Anby asked:

> **“Who's Jake?”**

Bao panicked.

She immediately brought the residents to Jake's room.

The room was accessible.

However, the elevator did not lead to the expected residential floor.

Instead, the elevator opened onto an apparently infinite grassy landscape.

The residents entered.

After an extended walk, they discovered an anomalous circular area where no grass grew.

At its center was a dark humanoid figure.

The figure was entirely black.

Its eyes were closed.

Its knees were drawn toward its chest.

Beside it sat Jake's teddy bear.

The figure did not respond to any attempts at communication.

The teddy bear's physical core remained intact.

However:

> **It had stopped glowing.**

Guiding Light has been summoned.

Investigation remains ongoing.

---

# 1. THE PERIMETER SECURITY GUARD

The same female Perimeter Security Guard who discovered Jake after the blizzard later encountered Bao.

The interaction initially appeared routine.

Then the Guard recognized her.

> **GUARD:**  
> “Hey Bao.”

Bao stopped.

> **GUARD:**  
> “I found Jake at my outpost.”

Bao's expression changed.

> **BAO:**  
> “...What?”

The Guard continued.

> **GUARD:**  
> “Did you know that he went outside and got locked out there in the blizzard?”

Bao did not answer.

She froze.

The Guard noticed.

> **GUARD:**  
> “Bao?”

Bao stared at her.

> **BAO:**  
> “Jake?”

The Guard nodded.

> **GUARD:**  
> “Yeah. Jake.”

Bao's expression became increasingly distressed.

She looked genuinely confused.

Then frightened.

> **BAO:**  
> “I...”

Pause.

> **BAO:**  
> “I don't remember him being around.”

---

# 2. MEMORY DISCREPANCY

Bao immediately attempted to recall Jake.

She could remember:

- his teddy bear;
    
- his room;
    
- his books;
    
- his blue star;
    
- his wings;
    
- his relationship with Anby;
    
- his conversations with the residents;
    
- his previous medical incidents;
    
- his presence around the SAFEHOUSE.
    

Yet the memory of his **recent existence** appeared fragmented.

The Guard's statement created a contradiction.

The Guard remembered finding Jake.

Bao remembered Jake as a person.

But she could not remember seeing him recently.

The discrepancy was not limited to Bao.

This was confirmed shortly afterward.

# 5. JAKE'S ROOM

Bao led the residents toward Jake's floor.

The group followed.

No one understood why.

The residents stepped inside.

The elevator moved.

Then stopped.

The doors opened.

> **Jake's hallway.**

Instead:

> **Grass.**

---

# 6. THE OTHER SIDE

There was no hallway.

No apartment.

No walls.

No ceiling.

No visible Blacksite infrastructure.

Only an enormous grassy field.

It extended beyond the visible horizon.

The sky above appeared strangely normal.

No structures were visible.

No doors.

No buildings.

No indication of where the SAFEHOUSE ended.

The elevator remained behind them.

The residents stared.

> **NIKO:**  
> “...That's new.”

Neko immediately examined the elevator controls.

They appeared normal.

The destination indicator still displayed Jake's floor.

Bao stepped forward.

> **BAO:**  
> “Jake?”

No response.

She walked into the grass.

The others followed.

---

# 7. THE WALK

They walked.

And walked.

And continued walking.

The grass remained almost completely identical.

There were no paths.

No landmarks.

No structures.

No signs of another resident.

No wildlife.

No wind.

The farther they walked, the quieter the environment became.

Eventually, Bao stopped.

Ahead was something different.

A large circular region.

Grass grew everywhere around it.

But not inside the circle.

---

# 8. THE CIRCLE

The circle was almost perfectly defined.

Inside it was bare ground.

At its center sat a figure.

At first, the residents could not identify it.

The figure appeared almost completely black.

Not merely dark.

Black.

Its surface reflected almost no visible light.

It was sitting with its knees pulled tightly against its body.

Its head was lowered.

Its eyes were closed.

It did not move.

Beside the figure sat a teddy bear.

Bao stopped breathing for a moment.

She knew the bear.

Even if the others did not.

> **BAO:**  
> “Jake...?”

No response.

---

# 10. NO RESPONSE

The residents tried multiple forms of communication.

Calling his name.

Speaking gently.

Approaching from different angles.

Waiting.

Nothing worked.

Jake did not look up.

He did not move.

He did not acknowledge anyone.

His eyes remained closed.

His knees remained against his chest.

His entire body remained black.

No azure glow was visible.

No celestial light.

No shooting-star patterns.

Nothing.

---

# 11. THE TEDDY BEAR

The teddy bear was sitting approximately one meter away from Jake.

Neko carefully examined it from a distance.

Its physical structure remained intact.

No obvious tears.

No broken limbs.

No external destruction.

The cardiac vessel was still present.

The core itself was intact.

Neko checked again.

Then again.

His expression changed.

> **NEKO:**  
> “The core's still there.”

Bao looked at him.

> **BAO:**  
> “Then why isn't it glowing?”

Neko did not answer immediately.

He simply stared at the bear.

The core was intact.

But inactive.

There was no visible stellar radiance.

No heartbeat-like glow.

No familiar azure illumination.

Only the physical object remained.

# 17. THE ABSENCE OF LIGHT

Jake had previously been characterized by light.

His eyes.

His halo.

His stellar energy.

His wings.

His rifts.

His teddy bear's heartbeat.

All had provided some form of visible or measurable radiance.

None was present now.

The figure sitting inside the circle appeared to be the absence of all of it.

A person-shaped silhouette.

A closed pair of eyes.

A motionless body.

And nothing else.
