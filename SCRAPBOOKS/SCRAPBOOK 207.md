### **ANOMALOUS PHENOMENON REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD / "COREY" – DUAL MANIFESTATION EVENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – Executive & Research Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

On ██/██/20██, personnel assigned to Shelter T-01 documented an unprecedented anomalous event.

Corin Lockwood was no longer alone.

Without warning, a second individual appeared alongside her.

The two individuals possess identical facial structure, voice, height, and genetic profile.

Despite this, both identify themselves as separate people.

The original individual continues using the name **Corin**.

The second individual introduced herself as **Corey**.

The mechanism responsible for the event remains unknown.

No dimensional instability, cloning process, or biological duplication has been identified.

---

# **INDIVIDUAL A**

## **Corin**

Corin physically resembles her documented appearance prior to her unexplained revival.

Characteristics include:

- Chalky lime hair.
    
- Pale skin.
    
- Extremely low body temperature.
    
- No detectable heartbeat.
    
- No measurable blood circulation.
    
- Does not require food, water, or respiration to remain alive.
    

Medical examinations remain unable to explain how normal movement and cognition continue despite these findings.

Corin herself describes this body simply as:

> "...The old me."

---

# **INDIVIDUAL B**

## **Corey**

Corey physically resembles Corin during the period immediately following her recovery.

Characteristics include:

- Naturally black hair.
    
- Normal skin pigmentation.
    
- Detectable heartbeat.
    
- Normal blood circulation.
    
- Normal respiratory function.
    
- Requires food, water, and sleep consistent with an ordinary human.
    

Medical examination has found no physiological abnormalities.

Corey describes herself as:

> "...The version that got another chance."

---

# **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATIONS**

Although physically independent, Corin and Corey behave with remarkable familiarity toward one another.

They argue.

Frequently.

Usually over ordinary matters.

Examples include:

- Whether Corin has taken her medication.
    
- Whether Corey worries too much.
    
- Which route to take through the forest.
    
- Who forgot to close the treehouse window.
    

The disagreements rarely remain serious for long.

Within minutes, they are often found sharing tea together as though nothing happened.

Personnel have noted that the two seem to understand one another without lengthy explanation.

Each already knows what the other is thinking.

---

# **SEPARATION TESTS**

Attempts to separate the pair for observation were immediately suspended.

During a controlled trial, Corin and Corey voluntarily remained in different sections of the Blacksite.

Within a short period, both experienced progressive distress.

Symptoms included:

- Increasing anxiety.
    
- Difficulty concentrating.
    
- Headaches.
    
- Fatigue.
    
- Disorientation.
    

Before testing could continue, both independently requested to be reunited.

The symptoms resolved shortly afterward.

Executive Command immediately prohibited further separation experiments.

The official order states:

> **Corin and Corey are not to be intentionally kept apart except in circumstances involving immediate risk to life.**

The underlying cause remains unknown.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE**

Their first joint visit to Project SAFEHOUSE caused widespread confusion.

Star reportedly froze in place for several seconds.

Belle looked between them repeatedly before quietly asking,

> "...Which one do I hug first?"

Neko briefly suggested that reality itself had duplicated Corin.

Miss Neko responded,

> "...No theories until Research finishes theirs."

Research has not finished.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin and Corey continue residing together at Shelter T-01.

They work together, visit SAFEHOUSE together, and are almost never observed apart.

To outside observers, they appear like sisters.

To themselves, the relationship is more difficult to define.

They disagree.

They comfort one another.

They occasionally finish each other's sentences.

Neither claims to be "the real one."

Instead, when asked who they are, they answered together:

> "We're both us."

The statement remains the most accurate explanation currently available.
