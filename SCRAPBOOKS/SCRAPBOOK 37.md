# **KNOWN NICKNAMES**

Like many long-serving KCA personnel, Norma has accumulated several nicknames over the years. Most originated naturally from coworkers and friends rather than official designations.

---

## **Norma**

The most common form of address.

Used by nearly everyone, both professionally and personally.

---

## **Normie**

A friendly, informal nickname used by close friends and colleagues.

Primarily heard around Engineering, Project SAFEHOUSE, and during off-duty gatherings.

Norma has no objections to it.

> **Norma:** "I've been called worse."

---

## **Belle's Little Sister**

An affectionate nickname that emerged after Belle and Norma began working closely together and spending considerable time together at the Blacksite.

Although they are **not biologically related**, personnel noticed similarities in their willingness to help others, their approachable personalities, and the ease with which they support one another.

The nickname reflects their close bond rather than any official relationship.

Belle finds it adorable.

Norma usually laughs and rolls her eyes.

---

## **The Juggernaut**

Norma's official tactical callsign.

Originates from her specialized heavy armor and her role as a frontline support operator capable of enduring prolonged engagements.

Among KCA tactical personnel, this is the name most often heard over the radio.

---

## **The Forger**

A nickname earned through her passion for blacksmithing.

Engineering staff frequently seek Norma's advice on metalworking, toolmaking, and custom fabrication.

The title has become synonymous with her craftsmanship.

---

## **The Chemist**

Originally coined by CCD researchers after Norma repeatedly assisted with chemical and biochemical research projects.

Although chemistry is only one of her many qualifications, the nickname remained due to its simplicity.
