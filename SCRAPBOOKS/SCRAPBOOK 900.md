# SAFEHOUSE WELFARE RECORD

## NERI & ALICE — RETURN TO NORMAL ROUTINE

**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL — WELFARE  
**Subjects:** Neri / Alice  
**Status:** **STABLE**  
**Incident Status:** CLOSED  
**Current Assessment:** Normal SAFEHOUSE interaction restored

---

## 1. Overview

Following Alice's recovery and subsequent return to normal activity, both **Neri and Alice have largely returned to their previous behavior**.

There has been no significant deterioration in their relationship.

There has been no continued hostility.

There has been no prolonged awkwardness.

There has been no dramatic reconciliation ceremony.

They simply started hanging out again.

Within days, the two were once again frequently seen together throughout SAFEHOUSE.

---

## 2. Neri

Neri's behavior has noticeably changed.

She still remembers what happened.

She still remembers believing Alice was gone.

She still remembers the guilt she carried during Alice's disappearance.

However, she no longer appears consumed by it.

The cafeteria has returned to being a cafeteria rather than a place where Neri waits for someone who may never return.

She eats cake normally again.

She complains when someone takes the last piece.

She bothers people.

She laughs.

She spends time with her friends.

Most importantly, she no longer appears to treat every absence as evidence that someone has abandoned her.

Her old diary remains unresolved.

The tallies remain unexplained.

Neri still does not remember what she was counting.

But she has stopped trying to force herself to remember.

---

## 3. Alice

Alice has also returned to her usual behavior.

She rides her scooter around SAFEHOUSE.

She bothers people.

She makes jokes at inappropriate times.

She occasionally disappears for a few minutes simply because she wanted to go somewhere.

She continues wearing her glasses despite having only one functional eye.

She continues acting as though none of this is particularly unusual.

Medical staff remain significantly less amused by this attitude.

Alice's physical recovery continues under observation, but she is no longer being treated as an active missing-person case.

---

# 4. Neri and Alice

Their previous routine has returned almost exactly.

Neri walks.

Alice rides alongside her on the scooter.

Neri carries cake.

Alice occasionally asks for some.

Neri usually refuses.

Alice steals some anyway.

Neri complains.

Neither actually minds.

One afternoon, an Archivist observed the pair travelling down a SAFEHOUSE corridor.

Alice was riding her scooter.

Neri was walking beside her.

Alice looked over.

> **“Hey, Neri.”**

> “What?”

> **“You walk too slow.”**

Neri looked at the scooter.

Then at Alice.

> **“You're cheating.”**

> **“It's called technology.”**

> **“It's called being lazy.”**

> **“Same thing.”**

Neri laughed.

They continued down the hallway.

---

# 5. The Cake Incident

Later that week, Neri was eating cake in the cafeteria.

Alice sat across from her.

Alice stared at the cake.

Neri noticed.

> **“No.”**

Alice had not said anything.

> **“I didn't ask.”**

> **“You were thinking about it.”**

> **“Maybe.”**

Neri pushed the plate slightly away.

Alice leaned across the table.

Neri moved it further away.

Alice followed it.

Neri moved it again.

Alice followed.

Eventually Neri sighed and cut off a small piece.

She handed it over.

Alice smiled.

> **“You're nice.”**

Neri immediately looked annoyed.

> **“Don't make it weird.”**

> **“Okay.”**

Alice ate the cake.

> **“You're still nice.”**

> **“Alice.”**

> **“What?”**

Neri tried not to smile.

She failed.

---

# 6. Medical Observation

The medical team has noted an important distinction.

**Returning to normal does not mean nothing happened.**

Both residents have experienced significant psychological events.

However, neither appears to be permanently defined by them.

Alice does not treat Neri as fragile.

Neri does not treat Alice as something that might disappear if she looks away.

They are simply friends again.

That normality is considered a positive outcome.

---

# 7. Final Note

A staff member asked Neri one evening whether she was still afraid Alice would disappear.

Neri thought for a moment.

> **“Sometimes.”**

“Do you think she's going to?”

Neri looked across the cafeteria.

Alice was attempting to balance a spoon on her nose.

> **“No.”**

Alice dropped the spoon.

> **“Ow.”**

Neri sighed.

> **“She's too stupid to disappear.”**

Alice looked offended.

> **“HEY.”**

Neri laughed.

Alice laughed too.

The cafeteria returned to normal.

And for once, **normal was enough.**
