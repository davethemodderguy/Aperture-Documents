# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

C.O.R.E. has confirmed that Jake does not possess a conventional functioning heart within his manifested body.

His heart is located inside his teddy bear.

This is not metaphorical.

It is not symbolic.

It is an actual anomalous displacement of Jake's cardiac function.

The teddy bear contains a small, continuously beating heart.

The heartbeat previously detected from the bear was therefore not merely an unexplained property of the object.

It is:

> **Jake's heartbeat.**

---

# 1. CARDIAC ABSENCE

Medical observation following the recent halo fracture identified an unusual feature of Jake's manifested anatomy.

The expected location of a heart within Jake's chest produces no corresponding cardiac activity.

No heartbeat can be detected from his torso.

No conventional heart is present.

Despite this, Jake continues to manifest normally.

He can move.

He can speak when he chooses to.

He can experience emotional distress.

He can interact with the environment.

He remains anomalous rather than biologically alive.

The absence of a physical heart therefore does not constitute a new medical emergency.

It is part of Jake's manifested state.

---

# 2. THE TEDDY BEAR

The teddy bear is not simply a comfort object.

It is an extension of Jake's manifestation.

Its internal structure cannot be examined through conventional means.

However, C.O.R.E. has confirmed that the object contains a cardiac structure.

It beats continuously.

> **thump.**

> **thump.**

> **thump.**

The rhythm corresponds directly to Jake's emotional state.

When Jake becomes frightened or distressed, the heartbeat changes.

When Jake is calm, it becomes steadier.

The bear therefore appears to function as the physical location of Jake's heart.

---

# 3. ORIGIN

The teddy bear was given to Jake by his mother when he was alive.

Jake used it for comfort.

He carried it.

He slept with it.

He held it whenever he was frightened.

At some point during the transition from living child to ghost, Jake's anomalous form incorporated the bear.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

The current hypothesis is that the bear became inseparable from Jake's identity and subsequently became the vessel through which his heart continued to exist.

---

# 4. THE DISAPPEARANCE

The significance of the teddy bear's temporary disappearance is therefore considerably greater than initially understood.

When the bear disappeared following Jake's emotional collapse:

> **Jake's heart disappeared with it.**

No cardiac activity could be detected anywhere within Jake's manifested form.

Jake remained conscious.

He continued to move.

However, his emotional and anomalous stability deteriorated rapidly.

The bear subsequently returned.

So did the detectable heartbeat.

---

# 5. RESTORATION EVENT

When the teddy bear reappeared, the first detectable phenomenon was:

> **thump.**

Jake immediately reacted.

He looked down.

The bear was against his chest.

He grabbed it.

The heartbeat continued.

> **thump.**

Jake held the bear tightly.

The cardiac rhythm gradually stabilized.

No other physiological change occurred.

---

Nora was present during the restoration.

Nothing.

> **thump.**

Nora's expression changed.

Jake did not speak.

He simply held it tighter.

---

Aeterna was informed of the cardiac displacement.

Then the teddy bear.

> **Aeterna:**

> **Nora:**

> **Aeterna:**  
> “He carries his own heart.”

---

# 8. WHY JAKE HOLDS IT

The teddy bear's role as a comfort object may therefore have a second function.

Jake does not merely find the bear comforting.

He is physically carrying the organ responsible for his manifested cardiac rhythm.

Holding it against his chest places his heart close to where a living person's heart would normally be.

This may contribute to Jake's sense of security.

It may also explain why Jake instinctively holds the bear tighter when distressed.

He is, in the most literal possible sense:

> **Holding his heart.**

His belief that his mother loved him has not returned.

> **His heart.**
