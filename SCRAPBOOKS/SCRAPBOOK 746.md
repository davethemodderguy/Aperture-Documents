# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE / MEMORIAL ARCHIVE**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — REAPPEARANCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** **PRESENT / INVISIBLE / INTANGIBLE**

---

# **THE INCIDENT**

It happened while Corin was cooking.

Nothing unusual.

The kitchen was quiet.

Corin was preparing chicken potato soup.

She had made it so many times that the movements had become automatic.

Chop.

Stir.

Taste.

Adjust.

She was halfway through preparing the soup when something appeared beside her.

For less than a second.

A small white rabbit.

Alice.

Corin froze.

The figure was faint.

Almost completely transparent.

But unmistakable.

White hair.

Bunny ears.

The familiar silhouette.

Then—

she vanished again.

Corin dropped the spoon.

---

# **NOT GONE**

Corin immediately called Neko.

Then Orphie.

Then everyone else.

The conclusion came quickly.

Alice had not disappeared.

She had not been erased.

She had not ceased to exist.

She was still here.

She was simply no longer interacting normally with reality.

**Invisible.**

**Intangible.**

Still present.

---

# **THE DISCOVERY**

Neko began testing the environment.

Motion sensors occasionally registered unexplained disturbances.

Thermal cameras detected nothing.

Normal cameras could not see her.

Objects passed through her.

Hands passed through her.

Alice could not pick anything up.

She could not open doors.

She could not ride her scooter.

She could not touch anyone.

But she was there.

Somewhere.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin stood in the kitchen.

She looked toward the place where Alice had appeared.

Her hands were shaking.

> "Alice?"

Nothing.

Corin waited.

> "If you're here..."

Silence.

Then—

the smallest movement.

A wooden spoon on the counter shifted by approximately two centimeters.

Corin immediately began crying.

Not from sadness.

From relief.

> "You're here."

---

# **THE SOLUTION**

The answer was surprisingly simple.

Alice did not need to be recovered.

She did not need to be reconstructed.

She did not need to be brought back.

She needed to be made **visible again.**

Neko theorized that Alice's transparency had progressed beyond normal visibility.

Her body was still occupying reality.

Her consciousness was still present.

She had simply fallen outside the range of ordinary interaction.

---

# **THE FIRST ATTEMPT**

Neko created a temporary detection field.

It did nothing.

The field detected an anomaly.

But Alice remained invisible.

Second attempt.

Nothing.

Third attempt.

Nothing.

Then Corin entered the room carrying the soup.

The field reacted.

For approximately three seconds—

Alice became visible.

---

# **ALICE**

She was standing in the kitchen.

Small.

Thin.

Almost completely translucent.

She looked confused.

Her ears were lowered.

She looked at Corin.

Corin couldn't move.

Alice couldn't speak.

She simply stared.

Then Corin whispered:

> "Alice."

Alice smiled.

---

# **THE REACTION**

Corin immediately reached for her.

Her hand passed straight through Alice.

Alice was still intangible.

But she was visible.

That was enough.

Corin cried harder.

> "You're here."

Alice nodded.

---

# **THE SOUP**

Corin looked at the pot.

Then at Alice.

She suddenly remembered why she had been cooking.

Chicken potato soup.

Alice's favorite.

Corin carefully placed a bowl on the table.

Alice couldn't hold it.

She couldn't eat it.

Not yet.

But she sat beside it.

For the first time since her disappearance—

Alice was sitting at the table again.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The news spread immediately.

Everyone came.

Not running.

Not shouting.

Nobody wanted to frighten her.

Belle quietly sat beside Alice.

Star knelt nearby.

Orphie stayed close.

Lumi cried.

Neko stared at the instruments.

Nightshade said nothing.

She simply remained nearby.

Everyone had spent days mourning someone they thought was gone.

Now they were looking at her again.

Even if she could not touch them.

Even if they could not touch her.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin sat beside Alice.

She didn't try to grab her.

She didn't try to force anything.

She simply stayed.

> "You don't have to go anywhere."

Alice looked at her.

Then slowly shook her head.

She didn't want to go.

---

**Tangibility:** None  
**Consciousness:** Confirmed  
**Communication:** Nonverbal  
**Existential status:** **PRESENT**

The previous classification of Alice as "lost" has been suspended.

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

Everyone thought the hat and empty journal were all that remained.

They were wrong.

Alice was still here.

Invisible.

Untouchable.

Unable to speak.

Unable to interact.

But here.

The solution may not be to bring Alice back.

It may simply be to remind reality that she is already here.

And somewhere inside the Treehouse, a little white bunny is waiting for someone to make her visible again.
