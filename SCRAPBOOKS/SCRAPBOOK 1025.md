# Assessment

The distinction between Neri's behavior toward John and her behavior toward other SAFEHOUSE residents is significant.

Neri generally demonstrates a **repulsive social response** when someone attempts to approach her.

John is not approaching her.

Yet Neri approaches him.

This reverses the usual pattern.

Possible explanations include:

- John does not carry the same emotional associations as the people Neri has been avoiding;
    
- Neri may perceive John and Jane as sufficiently different to bypass her existing avoidance response;
    
- John may be easier for Neri to be around because he does not actively attempt to care for, monitor, or "fix" her;
    
- Neri may simply like John's company;
    
- Neri may find the situation amusing;
    
- Neri may not understand it herself.
    

No conclusion is currently justified.
