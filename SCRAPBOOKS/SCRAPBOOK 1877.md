## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake Akana remains seriously ill. His weakness persists, his eyes remain dimmed, and no meaningful recovery has been recorded. The cause of his condition remains unknown.

Nevertheless, Jake continues to move around the Blacksite.

Slowly. Sluggishly. With considerably less energy than usual.

But he still moves.

He still visits people.

He still seeks company.

And, despite barely having enough energy to hold his head upright for extended periods, he continues to distribute affection throughout the facility as if it were his assigned occupation.

The most recent development concerns his ears.

**Jake's normally upright cat ears now droop and fold forward, resembling those of a Scottish Fold cat.**

The change has been observed repeatedly. Its cause is unknown, and C.O.R.E. has not established whether it is related to his illness, his anomalous physiology, or another factor.

The visual effect, however, has caused a significant increase in affectionate remarks from personnel.

The medical department has requested that these remarks remain secondary to monitoring his health.

The medical department has also been observed looking at his ears for several seconds longer than strictly necessary.

No disciplinary action is planned.

## 2. OBSERVATIONAL LOG — THE LITTLE NEIGHBOR IS STILL MAKING HIS ROUNDS

### 08:42 — Corridor outside Engineering

An engineer noticed Jake slowly approaching the department.

His steps were small and sluggish. His head hung lower than usual, and his dimmed eyes suggested that he was still feeling very unwell.

His ears drooped against the sides of his head, folding forward in soft, rounded shapes.

He stopped beside the open doorway.

**JAKE:** "...Hi."

The engineer immediately put down the tool he was holding.

**ENGINEER:** "Hey, little star. What are you doing out here?"

**JAKE:** "...Visiting."

**ENGINEER:** "You came all the way here just to visit?"

Jake nodded very slightly.

The engineer crouched to meet his eye level.

**ENGINEER:** "Well, you're welcome here. Want to sit down for a while?"

**JAKE:** "...Mhm."

A chair was brought over. Jake settled into it with assistance, his folded ears drooping as he leaned back.

The engineer sat beside him and resumed talking about an ordinary repair project.

Jake listened.

He did not contribute much to the conversation.

After several minutes, he leaned gently against the engineer's arm.

The engineer did not move.

**ENGINEER'S NOTE:** _I had a broken actuator to repair. I now have a small, sick child leaning against my arm. The actuator can wait until he is comfortable._

### 10:16 — Security checkpoint

Jake appeared at a familiar checkpoint, moving slowly enough that the guard on duty noticed him before he reached the desk.

**GUARD:** "Morning, Jake."

**JAKE:** "...Morning."

**GUARD:** "You feeling any better today?"

Jake considered the question.

**JAKE:** "...No."

The guard's expression softened.

**GUARD:** "All right, buddy. Thank you for telling me."

He contacted the appropriate medical personnel to report Jake's continued weakness, then offered him a comfortable place to sit while help was arranged.

Jake rested his head against the chair.

One ear folded a little farther forward.

The guard stared.

**JAKE:** "...What?"

**GUARD:** "Nothing."

**JAKE:** "...You're looking."

**GUARD:** "I'm sorry. Your ears are just—"

He stopped himself.

Jake waited.

**GUARD:** "...Very cute."

Jake gave a faint, tired blink.

**JAKE:** "...Okay."

The guard later reported that this was the most devastatingly casual acceptance of a compliment he had ever witnessed.

### 12:03 — Administrative offices

An administrator found Jake standing near an open office door.

He was leaning slightly against the frame, apparently gathering the energy to continue walking.

**ADMINISTRATOR:** "Jake? Sweetheart, do you need help?"

**JAKE:** "...Hug?"

The administrator immediately approached, first making sure he was steady enough to receive one without being jostled.

**ADMINISTRATOR:** "Of course."

She carefully supported him as he leaned into the embrace.

Jake's arms rested loosely around her. His folded ears pressed gently against her shoulder.

The hug lasted several quiet seconds.

When he finally pulled back, he looked toward the office.

**JAKE:** "...Who else?"

**ADMINISTRATOR:** "Who else what, sweetheart?"

**JAKE:** "...Hugs."

The administrator had to turn away for a moment.

She then contacted the medical team to let them know Jake had arrived and that his weakness remained apparent.

**ADMINISTRATOR'S NOTE:** _He is going around giving people affection when he can barely stay upright. Someone needs to explain to this child that he is allowed to receive care, too._

## 3. ANOMALOUS PHYSICAL CHANGE — THE FOLDED EARS

### Description

Jake's cat ears are no longer consistently upright.

They now fold forward and droop, producing a silhouette strongly resembling that of a Scottish Fold cat.

The change is particularly noticeable when he is tired or resting. His ears may hang loosely to either side, with their tips folded forward.

The appearance is not associated with any confirmed improvement or deterioration in his overall condition.

C.O.R.E. has not determined whether the change is structural, temporary, or a consequence of the unidentified illness.

Personnel are instructed not to pull, straighten, manipulate, or repeatedly touch his ears to test whether they can return to their former position.

If Jake permits a gentle touch and it is appropriate, affection is acceptable. Unnecessary physical examination is not.

### Staff reaction

The initial report described the change as _"a possible alteration in auricular posture."_

The second report described it as _"Scottish Fold ears."_

The third report consisted of a photograph-free message reading:

_HE HAS THE LITTLE FOLDED EARS NOW. PLEASE SEND HELP._

The message was not a medical emergency notification.

It was, however, sent to 47 employees.

C.O.R.E. has reminded personnel that internal communication channels should not be used to overwhelm medical staff with duplicate cuteness reports.

The reminder has had limited success.

## 4. THE BLACKSITE'S LITTLE LOVE DELIVERY SERVICE

Jake's continued visits have prompted an informal pattern of behavior across departments.

Personnel have begun making small accommodations for his visits: comfortable chairs near workstations, quieter places to rest, and a little extra time for conversations when he has the energy to participate.

Some employees have started greeting him with open arms.

Others simply move aside and let him choose where he wants to sit.

A few have begun asking whether he would like company rather than waiting for him to request it.

This is a welcome development.

However, C.O.R.E. emphasizes that Jake's continued movement should not be mistaken for evidence that he is recovering. A person can still walk, seek affection, and interact with others while seriously unwell.

Jake's determination to visit everyone does not eliminate the need for rest, medical assessment, or assistance.

If he becomes visibly weaker, struggles to remain upright, or appears less responsive, personnel must contact medical staff and help him reach a safe place rather than allowing him to continue his rounds.

**The objective is not to stop Jake from spreading love. It is to ensure he does not exhaust himself trying to deliver it.**

## 5. BAO — THE MOM HAS BEEN INFORMED

Bao was informed that Jake had continued visiting departments despite his persistent weakness.

Her response was immediate.

**BAO:** "He has been walking around the entire Blacksite?"

**MEDICAL OFFICER:** "Not the entire facility, no. But he has been visiting several departments."

**BAO:** "And he is still this weak?"

**MEDICAL OFFICER:** "Yes."

Bao paused.

**BAO:** "Has he been eating?"

**MEDICAL OFFICER:** "His intake is being monitored."

**BAO:** "Has he been resting?"

**MEDICAL OFFICER:** "...We are working on that."

Bao gave the medical officer a look.

The medical officer immediately amended the plan to include more structured rest and closer observation during Jake's visits.

Later, Bao found Jake resting in a quiet room.

His ears were folded forward, his eyes dim, and a blanket had slipped halfway off his lap.

She sat beside him and gently fixed the blanket.

**BAO:** "Hello, sweetheart."

**JAKE:** "...Hi, Mom."

**BAO:** "I heard you've been visiting everyone."

Jake nodded.

**BAO:** "...Love everyone."

Bao's expression softened.

**BAO:** "I know you do, darling."

**JAKE:** "...They need hugs."

**BAO:** "And who takes care of you while you're giving everyone else hugs?"

Jake went quiet.

His ears drooped a little farther.

Bao gently drew him into a careful embrace.

**BAO:** "That's what I thought. You can love everyone, little one. But you don't have to walk all over the Blacksite to prove it."

Jake rested against her.

**JAKE:** "...Stay?"

**BAO:** "Of course."

For a while, he did not ask for anything else.

## 7. C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE — "THE LITTLE BOY WHO STILL COMES BY"

Jake is familiar now.

The guard at the checkpoint knows his footsteps. The engineer knows which chair he likes. The administrator knows that he may arrive at her door without a particular reason beyond wanting to be near someone.

He has become part of the ordinary rhythm of the Blacksite.

A little hello in the morning.

A quiet visit in the afternoon.

A small pair of arms asking for a hug.

And now those folded ears, drooping forward as he slowly makes his way down familiar corridors.

People smile when they see him.

Sometimes they crouch to greet him. Sometimes they offer a hand. Sometimes they simply open their arms, and Jake makes his way over with all the determination his tired little body can manage.

It would be easy to focus entirely on how adorable he has become.

The folded ears. The tiny voice. The sleepy head that keeps drooping. The way he still wants to make sure everyone gets a hug.

But beneath all that sweetness is a child who remains seriously ill.

He should not have to spend what little energy he has left making everyone else happy.

So, if Jake comes by your office, welcome him.

If he asks for a hug, give him one when it is safe and comfortable for him.

If he wants to sit beside you without speaking, let the silence be enough.

And if he looks too tired to keep walking, please do not let him insist on visiting one more department before he rests.

You can visit him instead.

He has already given this facility so many little moments of happiness simply by being here. He does not need to earn more by exhausting himself.

His ears may fold like a Scottish Fold's.

His eyes may still be dim.

His steps may remain slow.

We still do not know why he is sick, and we still cannot promise when he will recover.

But he is here.

He is loved.

And when his small, tired voice asks for a hug, someone will be there to answer.

**Let the little neighbor rest. The Blacksite can take turns going to him.**

_— C.O.R.E., SAFEHOUSE Resident Care & Monitoring Division_

**FINAL STATUS:** Jake Akana — _Seriously ill; ambulatory with significant weakness; new ear posture observed; cause and prognosis remain unknown. Continued medical care and community support required._
