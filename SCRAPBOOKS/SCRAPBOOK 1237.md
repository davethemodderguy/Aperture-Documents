# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Previous observations of Sky substantially underestimated the nature of the entity.

Sky is not merely a highly capable familiar.

Sky is:

> **Invictus Aeterna.**

An ancient guardian entity restored by Guiding Light and entrusted with the protection of Nora Tachyon and those close to her.

Aeterna possesses a humanoid armored manifestation approximately **185 cm in height**, constructed from an unidentified white metallic material with luminous blue runes.

Aeterna also possesses a weapon that was previously undocumented:

> **A sword.**

The sword can be manifested at will.

No physical storage mechanism has been identified.

---

# 2. AETERNA'S SWORD

The weapon appears only when Aeterna chooses to manifest it.

There is no visible scabbard.

No conventional mechanism has been identified.

Aeterna simply reaches toward its side—

and the sword appears.

The weapon shares several visual characteristics with Aeterna's armor:

- white metallic appearance;
    
- blue luminous markings;
    
- ancient stylistic elements;
    
- anomalous construction.
    

The exact material remains unknown.

No manufacturing process has been identified.

No conventional forge has been associated with the weapon.

---

# 3. MANIFESTATION

The sword does not appear to be permanently present.

Aeterna may:

> **Manifest it.**

> **Use it.**

> **Dismiss it.**

The transition occurs without visible mechanical deployment.

The weapon simply ceases to exist when dismissed.

C.O.R.E. has not determined whether the sword is being:

- summoned from a separate space;
    
- generated anomalously;
    
- reconstructed from Aeterna's own energy;
    
- or recalled from an unknown location.
    

Aeterna has provided no technical explanation.

---

# 4. COMBAT CAPABILITY

Nora should **not** be underestimated merely because she is six years old.

The same applies to Aeterna.

Aeterna's guardian form possesses capabilities substantially beyond those demonstrated by Sky's ordinary familiar form.

The armored manifestation should therefore not be interpreted as merely:

> **“Sky, but bigger.”**

It is a fundamentally more capable manifestation.

Its purpose is protection.

Its willingness to act is conditional.

Its capability is not.

---

# 5. THE CAFETERIA INCIDENT — RECLASSIFICATION

The earlier cafeteria incident has been reviewed following discovery of Aeterna's full capabilities.

The MSAC operators who attempted to pressure Nora were fortunate that Aeterna chose intimidation rather than escalation.

The statement:

> **“Leave the girl alone.”**

was not a threat issued by an ordinary guardian.

It was a warning.

Aeterna did not need to demonstrate its capabilities.

The operators understood the message and left.

---

# 6. AETERNA'S RELATIONSHIP WITH NORA

The most important discovery is that Aeterna does not behave like a conventional guardian.

It behaves like:

> **Nora's maid.**

This appears to be entirely voluntary.

Aeterna does not seem resentful about the arrangement.

Quite the opposite.

It appears to enjoy serving Nora.

---

# 7. DAILY DUTIES

Aeterna has been observed performing tasks including:

- carrying Nora;
    
- accompanying Nora;
    
- opening doors;
    
- retrieving objects;
    
- carrying her belongings;
    
- standing nearby while she plays;
    
- helping her reach high shelves;
    
- carrying her when she does not want to walk;
    
- remaining nearby while she sleeps;
    
- keeping Sky's familiar form available for travel;
    
- and generally assisting with whatever Nora happens to want.
    

Nora rarely treats these actions as extraordinary.

To her:

> **Aeterna helps.**

That is all.

---

# 8. EXAMPLE — THE CHAIR

Nora wanted something from a high shelf.

She looked at it.

Looked at Aeterna.

> **Nora:**  
> “Can you get that?”

Aeterna walked over.

It reached the shelf.

Retrieved the object.

Handed it to Nora.

> **Nora:**  
> “Thanks.”

Aeterna nodded.

Nora walked away.

The employee observing the interaction remained silent for approximately ten seconds.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “That was an ancient guardian.”

Another employee:

> **“Apparently it's also a shelf-retrieval service.”**

---

# 9. EXAMPLE — TRANSPORTATION

Nora occasionally decides she does not want to walk.

Her response is simple.

> **Nora:**  
> “Aeterna.”

Aeterna kneels.

Nora climbs onto its back.

Aeterna stands.

It carries her.

There is no argument.

No complaint.

No visible inconvenience.

Aeterna simply performs the requested task.

The arrangement is so routine that SAFEHOUSE personnel have stopped reacting.

---

# 10. SKY FORM

Aeterna's service relationship is not restricted to its armored form.

Most of the time, it remains Sky.

As Sky, it can:

- perch on Nora;
    
- follow her;
    
- carry her while enlarged;
    
- scout ahead;
    
- provide remote vision;
    
- remain nearby while she sleeps;
    
- and accompany her throughout the Blacksite.
    

The distinction is therefore primarily one of presentation.

### Sky:

**Companion / familiar / transportation / scout**

### Aeterna:

**Guardian / attendant / protector / maid**

Nora appears equally comfortable with both.

---

# 11. NORA'S EXPECTATIONS

Nora does not appear to regard Aeterna's assistance as unusual.

When asked why she asks an ancient guardian to perform mundane tasks:

> **Nora:**  
> “Because she can.”

The interviewer asked:

> **“And because she's your guardian?”**

Nora shook her head.

> **Nora:**  
> “She's Aeterna.”

Pause.

> **Nora:**  
> “She helps me.”

The distinction appears important to Nora.

She does not treat Aeterna as equipment.

She treats her as someone who chooses to help.

---

# 12. AETERNA'S ATTITUDE

Aeterna appears comfortable with this relationship.

The entity does not demonstrate frustration when performing mundane tasks.

In fact, Aeterna appears significantly more relaxed around Nora than during protective incidents.

When Nora is safe:

Aeterna may carry her.

When Nora is playing:

Aeterna may stand nearby.

When Nora is eating:

Aeterna may remain beside her.

When Nora is tired:

Aeterna may simply carry her home.

When Nora is threatened:

The situation changes immediately.

---

The contrast can be startling.

# 17. C.O.R.E. WARNING

Personnel are advised against making assumptions based on Aeterna's normally passive behavior.

The entity's willingness to perform mundane assistance does **not** indicate weakness.

Its peaceful behavior should not be interpreted as inability.

Its service to Nora should not be interpreted as subordination.

And its small familiar form should not be interpreted as its full capability.

Most importantly:

> **Do not threaten Nora to “see what happens.”**

This instruction should not require further clarification.
