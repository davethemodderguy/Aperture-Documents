### **ANOMALOUS PHYSICS OBSERVATION**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – KINETIC ETHER MANIFESTATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – C.O.R.E. Research Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following repeated testing, Research Division has concluded that Corin Lockwood's anomalous abilities are not limited to enhanced physical strength.

Instead, her Ether-afflicted physiology continuously generates and manipulates an abnormally large quantity of **kinetic energy**.

Unlike conventional physical force, this kinetic output can be deliberately transferred into external objects.

The result is an ability informally designated:

> **Kinetic Ether Manifestation (KEM)**

---

# **MECHANISM**

Corin's body constantly accumulates an extraordinary amount of kinetic potential through its Ether-dependent state.

Rather than remaining confined to her muscles, Corin can subconsciously or deliberately distribute this force into whatever she is using.

Known examples include:

- Bare-handed strikes.
    
- Bladed weapons.
    
- Blunt weapons.
    
- Tools.
    
- Mechanical equipment.
    
- Firearms.
    

The amount transferred is under voluntary control.

Research notes that Corin instinctively suppresses the majority of this output during everyday activities.

---

# **WEAPON APPLICATION**

## **KATANA**

When wielding her katana, kinetic Ether is transferred along the blade.

This dramatically amplifies cutting force.

Testing confirms that the Ether travels with the swing itself, producing the crimson shockwave previously documented during combat evaluations.

---

## **FIREARMS**

Corin can also distribute kinetic Ether into firearms.

Unlike conventional ammunition enhancement, the Ether is imparted to the projectile at the instant of firing.

Observed effects include:

- Dramatically increased projectile velocity.
    
- Greatly increased impact energy.
    
- Improved penetration against hardened targets.
    
- Reduced projectile drop over distance.
    

No modifications to the firearm itself are required.

The weapon merely serves as the delivery platform.

---

## **OTHER OBJECTS**

Testing indicates Corin can unintentionally empower virtually any handheld object.

During routine maintenance, a lightly tossed wrench embedded itself several centimeters into a reinforced steel cabinet.

Corin immediately apologized.

Engineering requested the wrench back.

---

# **LIMITATIONS**

Despite its versatility, the phenomenon has practical limits.

Corin cannot create unlimited energy.

Greater kinetic output requires greater Ether expenditure and places increasing strain on her already unstable physiology.

Because of this, she consciously avoids excessive use except during emergencies.

Medical Services have strongly advised against repeated maximum-output demonstrations.

---

# **CORIN'S OPINION**

When researchers enthusiastically described the ability as "remarkable," Corin appeared uncomfortable.

She quietly responded:

> "...It's just another thing they left me with."

She did not view the phenomenon as a gift.

Only another reminder of the experimentation.

---

# **LUMI'S RESPONSE**

Lumi later asked Corin a simple question.

> "If you use that strength to protect someone..."

> "...does it become theirs?"

Corin looked confused.

Lumi smiled.

> "No."

> "It becomes yours."

Corin remained silent.

The answer lingered in her thoughts long after the conversation ended.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Research into Kinetic Ether Manifestation remains ongoing.

Corin continues to employ the ability only when necessary and actively limits its use during routine activities.

Although capable of channeling immense force into nearly any object she holds, Corin herself has shown little interest in exploring its destructive potential.

Her primary concern remains unchanged.

Not how much force she can produce.

But how to live with the consequences of possessing it.
