### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / ANOMALOUS OBSERVATION LOG**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — LIFE TRANSFER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** **ANOMALOUS ABILITY CONFIRMED / HIGH RISK**

---

# **THE FLOWER POT**

Alice found a flower pot in the Blacksite's garbage chute.

The flower inside was dead.

Its leaves had completely wilted.

The soil was dry.

Nobody knew how long it had been there.

Alice picked it up.

She carried it back to the Treehouse.

---

# **THE EVENT**

Corin was nearby when Alice placed the pot on the floor.

Alice looked at the dead flower.

She touched the soil.

Then raised one hand above it.

A green rune appeared beneath her palm.

It was unfamiliar.

It was not a symbol previously documented in Alice's medical or anomalous records.

The rune glowed softly.

Then—

The flower moved.

A wilted leaf straightened.

The stem rose.

New leaves unfolded.

The dead flower regained its color.

Roots spread through the soil.

Within seconds, the plant had returned to extraordinary vitality.

It looked healthier than it had probably been in months.

Alice smiled.

Then nearly fell over.

---

# **THE COST**

Corin caught her.

Alice was visibly weaker.

Her breathing had become shallow.

Her body temperature dropped.

Her ears fell limp.

Corin immediately asked what happened.

Alice couldn't speak.

She pointed at the flower.

Then at herself.

She wrote:

> **"Alice give it."**

Corin stared at the words.

> **"Give it what?"**

Alice placed a hand against her chest.

Then pointed toward the flower again.

She wrote:

> **"Life."**

---

# **ABILITY IDENTIFIED**

Alice appears capable of deliberately transferring portions of her own life force into another living or formerly living system.

The effect can restore vitality.

Observed effects include:

- Revitalization of dead or dying plant matter
    
- Rapid restoration of biological activity
    
- Accelerated growth
    
- Restoration of depleted organic material
    

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

The green rune appears to act as the visible manifestation of the transfer.

---

# **THE DANGER**

The ability is inherently dangerous to Alice.

She does not create life from nothing.

She gives away **hers.**

The weaker Alice becomes, the less reserve she has available.

Alice's body already operates under severe limitations.

Her stamina is extremely poor.

Her nutritional reserves are dangerously limited.

Her immune system remains fragile.

Her regeneration requires resources.

Her body is still recovering from extensive previous damage.

And yet—

Alice can give those resources away.

---

# **A DISTURBING DISCOVERY**

Alice demonstrated no hesitation.

She saw something dying.

She wanted it alive.

So she gave it part of herself.

There was no consideration of the consequences.

When Corin asked:

> **"Why would you do that when you're already so weak?"**

Alice wrote:

> **"Flower hurt."**

Corin:

> **"But you're hurt too."**

Alice looked confused.

She wrote:

> **"Alice okay."**

She wasn't.

---

# **THE PATTERN**

This is consistent with Alice's established behavior.

Alice cares about other people.

Alice cares about animals.

Alice cares about plants.

Alice cares about things that cannot even speak.

But when it comes to herself—

Alice's standards disappear.

A dying flower receives Alice's life without hesitation.

Alice herself receives almost nothing.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin immediately moved the flower away.

Alice reached for it.

Corin stopped her.

Alice became distressed.

She wrote:

> **"Flower need Alice."**

Corin answered:

> **"No."**

Alice stared.

Corin knelt beside her.

> **"You need Alice too."**

Alice didn't understand.

She wrote:

> **"Why?"**

Corin's eyes filled with tears.

> **"Because you're Alice."**

Alice looked at her notebook.

She wrote nothing.

---

# **THE FLOWER**

The plant remains alive.

It is now being kept in the Treehouse.

It has become one of the healthiest plants in the immediate area.

Alice checks it frequently.

She waters it.

She touches its leaves.

She smiles whenever it grows.

But she has been explicitly instructed not to use the ability on it again.

For now, Alice appears to understand.

Whether she will obey if she encounters another dying organism remains uncertain.

---

# **PRELIMINARY SAFETY DIRECTIVE**

Alice is not to use the ability without supervision.

Personnel are specifically prohibited from requesting Alice to use it.

Nobody is to tell her:

> "You can save this."

Because Alice will probably try.

And Alice will probably pay the price herself.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Alice has spent much of her life trying to survive.

Now she has discovered that she can give her own life away.

And she is exactly the kind of child who would do it.

For everyone.

For animals.

For flowers.

For anything she thinks needs her more than she does.

That is what makes the ability frightening.

Not its power.

Not the green rune.

Not the ability to restore life.

It is Alice's willingness to use herself as the cost.

She has always cared for everyone around her.

She has simply never learned to include herself among the people worth saving.
