## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / MISSING PERSONNEL OBSERVATION LOG**

# **ORPHIE — INTO THE SNOW**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Cross-Reality Anomaly  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / Marts / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Luna / Solaria  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **MISSING — UNLOCATED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie left the treehouse.

She did not announce her departure.

She did not leave a message.

She simply walked away.

Snow had begun falling across the Blacksite perimeter forest.

Orphie walked into it.

Eventually, she disappeared from sight.

Nobody followed.

---

# **THE DEPARTURE**

The treehouse was quiet.

The belongings Orphie had discarded remained where she had left them.

The broken pencil.

The sketchbook.

The cookbook.

The hats.

Corin was elsewhere in the building when Orphie quietly opened the door.

She stepped outside.

No anomalous manifestation occurred.

No golden flare.

No lunar light.

No solar wings.

Just Orphie.

She walked into the snow.

---

# **THE LAST SIGHTING**

Corin saw her through the window.

Orphie's figure became smaller between the trees.

Snow accumulated across her shoulders.

She did not turn around.

Corin stood there.

She did not follow.

---

# **WHY**

Nobody knows exactly why Corin stayed behind.

Perhaps she believed Orphie wanted the space.

Perhaps she was exhausted.

Perhaps she had finally accepted that there were limits to how much she could force herself into Orphie's life.

Perhaps, after everything that had happened, she simply did not know what else to do.

The result was the same.

She let Orphie go.

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts noticed Orphie leaving.

They watched from a distance.

None followed.

Their fear remained stronger than their curiosity.

One Mart looked toward the forest.

Another tugged gently on its tiny top hat.

Neither moved.

Eventually, the colony returned to the SAFEHOUSE.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The disappearance was noticed.

But there was no search party.

No emergency deployment.

No shouting through the forest.

No footprints followed.

No one went looking.

The SAFEHOUSE simply became quieter.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin remained at the treehouse.

She eventually closed the door.

The room still contained Orphie's discarded belongings.

She did not touch them.

She sat down.

For a long time, she simply stared at the empty doorway.

---

# **THE FOREST**

Orphie's footprints remained visible in the snow for a short time.

Then the snowfall covered them.

One print.

Then another.

Then another.

Until there was nothing left.

No trail.

No direction.

No indication of where she went.

---

# **C.O.R.E. SEARCH STATUS**

A formal search was considered.

It was not initiated.

Orphie had repeatedly expressed a desire to be left alone.

Her previous behavior had demonstrated that unwanted intervention could cause her distress.

With no immediate evidence of physical injury, C.O.R.E. personnel elected not to pursue her.

---

# **THE EMPTY TREEHOUSE**

Her absence became obvious.

The chair remained empty.

The kitchen remained unused.

The cookbook remained discarded.

The sketchbook remained closed.

The hats remained untouched.

Nothing had been moved.

Nothing had been taken.

It was as though Orphie had simply removed herself from the room.

---

# **LUNA**

Luna was silent for a long time.

> "...She's really gone."

Solaria answered:

> "...Yes."

> "...Shouldn't someone find her?"

A pause.

> "...She wanted to be alone."

Luna looked away.

> "...I know."

---

# **NO ONE FOLLOWED**

For weeks, Orphie had asked for exactly this.

> **"Leave me alone."**

Eventually, everyone did.

The Marts stopped approaching.

Corin stopped following.

The SAFEHOUSE stopped sending people.

No one went into the forest.

No one called her name.

No one searched.

No one bothered her.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie walked away from the treehouse.

She walked into the snow until nobody could see her anymore.

The forest swallowed her footprints.

And for once, nobody came after her.

Perhaps this is what she wanted.

Perhaps this is exactly what she had been asking for.

Or perhaps the cruelest part of her belief—

that she was unimportant—

has finally become indistinguishable from reality.

The treehouse remains.

The discarded belongings remain.

Corin remains.

The Marts remain.

Everyone remembers that Orphie existed.

But nobody went looking.

**End of Missing Personnel Observation Log**
