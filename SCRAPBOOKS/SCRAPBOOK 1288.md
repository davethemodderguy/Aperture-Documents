# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has been identified as a **Weeping Angel**.

The classification was provided by **Star**, a Starladian Guardian Angel and direct descendant of the Starlands Guardian Angel lineage.

According to Star, Weeping Angels are angels whose connection to the Starlands has been severed through prolonged exposure to overwhelming **sadness, grief, or sorrow**.

The resulting condition is not a punishment imposed by another being.

It is a state of spiritual deterioration.

As an angel becomes increasingly consumed by sorrow, their wings may disappear.

Eventually, their ability to return to the Starlands may disappear with them.

Once both are lost, the angel can no longer ascend.

They can only wander.

Jake is one such angel.

# 3. WINGS

According to Star, wings are not merely physical structures among Starlands angels.

They represent a connection to the Starlands itself.

An angel's wings allow them to:

- travel between the mortal world and Starlands;
    
- return to the Starlands after completing their duties;
    
- receive guidance;
    
- maintain a spiritual connection with their homeland.
    

A Weeping Angel gradually loses this connection.

The wings are therefore not simply "destroyed."

They **fade.**

Eventually, nothing remains.

Jake currently has no wings.
