## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### ANOMALOUS RESEARCH & RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-ANM-4C-JAKE-307  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESTRICTED — ANOMALOUS PHENOMENON / WELFARE RISK  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE INVESTIGATION  
**SUBJECT:** J-4C-P — The Remnant  
**SUPPLEMENTARY FINDING:** Physical Vulnerability and Persistent Pain

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

C.O.R.E. has confirmed that **J-4C-P, designated The Remnant, can be physically injured.**

The phenomenon is not immune to physical force.

Its anomalous nature does not prevent physical damage, nor does its apparent origin as a manifestation of Jake Akana's pain and suffering render it invulnerable.

Physical attacks against the Remnant can cause additional injury.

The Remnant does not respond to such injury with gratitude, acceptance, or indifference.

It responds with distress.

This finding is particularly significant because the Remnant already exhibits numerous scars and visible injuries across its body. These marks are present during ordinary manifestations, even when no recent physical interaction has occurred.

C.O.R.E. considers the accumulated damage consistent with its persistent presentation of suffering. However, the exact origin of every scar has not been established.

The most important distinction is as follows:

**The Remnant is a manifestation of suffering. It is not an object that can be struck without consequence.**

Personnel are expressly prohibited from deliberately inflicting physical harm upon it for research, provocation, or testing.

---

# 1. PHYSICAL VULNERABILITY

Previous assessments concentrated on the Remnant's unusual physiology, including its floating movement, absent legs, non-thermal blue flame, and visibly damaged head.

These characteristics initially complicated attempts to determine whether ordinary physical interaction would be possible.

Subsequent observations have established that it is.

Physical force can affect the Remnant.

The phenomenon can be hurt.

Its body can sustain additional damage, and its responses indicate that such damage is not merely cosmetic.

C.O.R.E. has not established whether its injuries follow conventional biological mechanisms. The Remnant is not a living human body, and ordinary medical assumptions cannot be applied without qualification.

Nevertheless, its anomalous composition does not eliminate the observable consequences of physical violence.

Personnel must not interpret its unusual physiology as permission to harm it.

---

# 2. EXISTING SCARS AND VISIBLE DAMAGE

The Remnant's body displays numerous scars.

They are distributed across its visible form, alongside the injuries associated with Jake Akana's death.

The existing damage includes:

- Multiple linear scars across the body.
    
- Areas of visibly damaged tissue.
    
- The severe melting-like damage extending from the top of the head toward the eyes.
    
- Ten stab wounds corresponding to the previously documented fatal injuries.
    
- Gunshot wounds of undetermined number and location.
    
- Additional marks whose origins remain unconfirmed.
    

Not every visible mark can currently be assigned to a specific event.

C.O.R.E. therefore distinguishes between **documented injuries** and **unidentified scars**.

The former may be connected to established incidents. The latter remain under observation.

The Remnant's body is not a clean representation of a single fatal event.

It is a visibly damaged form that continues to manifest with extensive physical markings.

Whether all of these scars reflect Jake's experiences, the Remnant's own subsequent injuries, or the anomalous process that sustains its appearance remains unknown.

No conclusion has been drawn that every scar represents a separate act of violence.

However, the overall presentation is consistent with the phenomenon's association with prolonged suffering.

---

# 3. PERSISTENT PAIN

The Remnant appears to be in pain even when nobody is interacting with it.

Its distress is not limited to moments when it is physically injured.

It is observed during otherwise quiet manifestations.

Personnel have recorded:

- A persistently lowered posture.
    
- The tendency to hold its teddy bear close against its body.
    
- Minimal voluntary movement.
    
- Very limited verbal communication.
    
- Visible distress when attempting to speak.
    
- A tendency to withdraw rather than engage with nearby residents.
    
- Reactions consistent with discomfort during physical interaction.
    

These observations do not establish the precise neurological or anomalous mechanism responsible for its suffering.

They do, however, indicate that the Remnant should not be treated as a phenomenon that experiences pain only when provoked.

**Its baseline condition already appears to involve persistent suffering.**

Physical injury adds to an existing problem.

It does not create the problem from nothing.

---

# 4. SPEECH UNDER DISTRESS

The Remnant's voice remains severely damaged.

Speaking appears to cause substantial pain, particularly when it attempts to raise its volume.

Consequently, its speech is usually limited to short, faint whispers.

During one recorded encounter, a resident asked whether it could explain where it hurt.

The Remnant remained silent.

The resident repeated the question.

The figure tightened its grip around the teddy bear.

**RESIDENT:**  
"Can you tell me where it hurts?"

The Remnant's head lowered.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"...Everywhere."

The resident immediately stopped questioning it.

**RESIDENT:**  
"Okay. You don't have to explain."

The Remnant did not answer.

The resident remained nearby without attempting to touch it.

No further speech was recorded.

C.O.R.E. cannot determine whether the word _everywhere_ was anatomically literal or an expression of generalized suffering. It is nevertheless consistent with the Remnant's overall presentation.

Personnel are instructed not to require verbal confirmation before taking visible distress seriously.

---

# 5. RESPONSE TO PHYSICAL HARM

The Remnant is not grateful when someone hurts it.

This may appear obvious, but earlier welfare observations identified a recurring risk: some residents perceive the phenomenon as a manifestation of suffering and may assume that its reactions are predetermined, symbolic, or incapable of being meaningfully affected.

That assumption is unacceptable.

The Remnant's anomalous origin does not make physical aggression harmless.

When subjected to physical force, it exhibits distress rather than appreciation.

It does not interpret injury as assistance.

It does not become grateful because someone has interacted with it.

It does not appear to regard additional suffering as beneficial.

During a documented interaction, a resident attempted to determine whether the Remnant could be physically affected.

The resident struck it.

The Remnant recoiled sharply.

Its teddy bear was pulled tightly against its chest, and the blue flame atop its head flickered.

**RESIDENT:**  
"...You can feel that?"

The Remnant stared at the resident.

It did not answer immediately.

When it finally spoke, the words were barely audible.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"Why?"

The resident hesitated.

**RESIDENT:**  
"I was testing something."

The Remnant lowered its head.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"Don't."

The resident stepped back.

**RESIDENT:**  
"I won't do it again."

The Remnant did not acknowledge the apology.

It remained visibly distressed until it eventually demanifested.

The encounter established that physical force can affect the phenomenon. It did not establish the limits of its durability, its recovery mechanism, or whether all forms of physical damage produce the same response.

No further testing by deliberate injury is authorized.

---

# 6. WHY GRATITUDE MUST NOT BE EXPECTED

C.O.R.E. has identified a potential behavioral hazard in how personnel interpret the Remnant's nature.

Because it is associated with Jake's suffering, some may attempt to interact with it as though it were an embodiment of grief rather than a phenomenon capable of responding to physical contact.

This can lead to inappropriate expectations.

Personnel must not assume that the Remnant will:

- Accept physical harm as inevitable.
    
- Remain passive when injured.
    
- Thank someone for interacting with it.
    
- Regard pain as a form of companionship.
    
- Interpret aggression as concern.
    
- Become more cooperative after being hurt.
    
- Permit repeated injury because it already appears damaged.
    

The Remnant's suffering is not an invitation.

Its scars are not permission.

Its silence is not consent.

And the fact that it does not always resist does not mean it accepts what is being done to it.

**A phenomenon associated with pain does not owe anyone gratitude for causing more of it.**

---

# 7. RESIDENT WELFARE IMPLICATIONS

The Remnant's physical vulnerability introduces a difficult distinction into its welfare classification.

It is not Jake Akana.

It cannot be assumed to possess Jake's consciousness, memories, preferences, or capacity for personal reconciliation.

However, the Remnant has demonstrated responses consistent with distress when harmed.

C.O.R.E. therefore requires personnel to approach it as a potentially suffering anomalous entity whose subjective experience remains incompletely understood.

This is a precautionary determination, not a declaration that the Remnant possesses the same consciousness as a living person.

Its exact nature remains unknown.

Its vulnerability does not.

Personnel who encounter the Remnant are instructed to:

- Maintain a non-threatening distance.
    
- Avoid sudden physical contact.
    
- Never strike, restrain, or deliberately injure it for experimentation.
    
- Avoid touching its scars or visible wounds.
    
- Never attempt to extinguish the blue flame.
    
- Avoid removing or grabbing the teddy bear.
    
- Stop approaching if the Remnant displays distress.
    
- Document any apparent injury without attempting invasive examination.
    
- Report deliberate or accidental physical harm immediately.
    

If the Remnant is injured, personnel must not assume that its anomalous nature makes treatment unnecessary.

C.O.R.E. medical and anomalous research staff must first determine whether a safe, non-invasive assessment is possible.

No procedure may be performed merely to satisfy curiosity.

---

# 8. THE TEDDY BEAR

The teddy bear remains a consistent feature of the Remnant's manifestations.

The figure holds it close, including during moments of distress.

Personnel must not use the bear to provoke a response or attempt to separate the Remnant from it.

During one encounter, a resident moved closer while the Remnant was already distressed.

The resident glanced at the bear.

**RESIDENT:**  
"Does holding that help?"

The Remnant tightened its arms around the object.

No answer followed.

**RESIDENT:**  
"All right. I won't touch it."

The Remnant remained silent.

The resident stepped back.

No further interaction occurred.

C.O.R.E. cannot establish whether the bear provides comfort, represents an attachment, or serves another anomalous function. Its consistent presence is sufficient to justify leaving it undisturbed unless a clear and immediate safety concern requires otherwise.

---

# 9. INVESTIGATIVE LIMITATIONS

The following questions remain unresolved:

1. Does the Remnant experience pain through a mechanism comparable to biological pain?
    
2. Can its existing scars worsen without external interference?
    
3. Do newly inflicted injuries persist between manifestations?
    
4. Can the Remnant recover from physical damage?
    
5. Does physical injury affect the duration or stability of a manifestation?
    
6. Are its scars direct representations of Jake's suffering, or are some independent injuries sustained by the Remnant?
    
7. Does the Remnant possess an independent consciousness capable of expressing preferences?
    

C.O.R.E. will not answer these questions through deliberate physical harm.

The absence of a complete physiological model does not justify uncontrolled experimentation.

Any future examination must be non-invasive, approved in advance, and designed to avoid unnecessary distress.

---

# 10. FINAL ASSESSMENT

The Remnant is a manifestation of Jake Akana's pain and suffering.

It is not Jake.

It is also not invulnerable.

Its body bears numerous scars and visible injuries. Its behavior indicates persistent distress, and physical force can cause further harm.

It does not welcome such harm.

It does not express gratitude for being hurt.

It is already suffering.

C.O.R.E. cannot currently establish whether that suffering is experienced in the same manner as human pain. Nevertheless, the available evidence does not justify treating its injuries as inconsequential.

The correct response is restraint.

Not because the Remnant is secretly Jake.

Not because injuring it will necessarily damage Jake's surviving consciousness.

But because the Remnant itself has demonstrated distress, and there is no legitimate welfare or research justification for deliberately adding to it.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING OBSERVATION

During a late-night observation, the Remnant appeared in the cafeteria.

It hovered beside an empty chair, its damaged body visible beneath the dim lights.

The blue flame atop its head flickered softly.

Its arms were wrapped around the teddy bear.

A welfare officer entered the room and stopped several meters away.

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"I'm not going to touch you."

The Remnant did not look up.

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"I'm going to sit over here. You can leave whenever you want."

The officer took a seat at the opposite end of the table.

Minutes passed.

The Remnant's posture remained lowered.

Eventually, it spoke.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"...Hurts."

The officer looked toward it.

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"I know you're hurting."

The Remnant shook its head slightly.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"No."

A pause.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"You don't."

The officer did not argue.

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"You're right. I don't know what it feels like."

The Remnant looked down at the bear.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"Then don't."

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"Don't what?"

The flame dimmed.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"Make it worse."

The officer sat very still.

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"I won't."

The Remnant said nothing more.

It did not thank the officer.

It did not become comforted.

It did not suddenly stop hurting.

The officer remained at the opposite end of the table, careful not to mistake the absence of further protest for improvement.

Eventually, the Remnant demanifested.

The chair remained empty.

The room remained quiet.

The officer recorded the encounter and left.

There was no breakthrough.

No miraculous relief.

No indication that the Remnant had forgiven anyone.

Only one clear finding:

**It could be hurt. It had been hurt. And it did not want anyone to make it worse.**

---

**C.O.R.E. DIRECTIVE**

J-4C-P is not Jake Akana.

Its suffering does not restore him.

Its scars do not prove that his consciousness remains.

But the Remnant can be physically injured, and its distress must not be dismissed merely because its nature is anomalous.

**Do not hurt it to prove that it can hurt.**

**Do not demand gratitude for leaving it alone.**

**And do not mistake a being's continued suffering for permission to add to it.**
