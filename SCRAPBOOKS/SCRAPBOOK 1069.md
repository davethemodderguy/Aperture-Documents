## Executive Summary

Alice has regained access to her memories of Neri.

The recovery did not occur while Alice was awake.

It occurred during sleep.

Alice experienced what she described as a nightmare.

The dream contained Neri.

Not an unfamiliar version of Neri.

Not an abstract representation.

**Neri as Alice remembered her.**

The memories returned simultaneously with the emotional context attached to them.

Alice woke distressed.

For the first time since the onset of the memory anomaly, Alice remembered:

- carrying Neri;
    
- hugging Neri;
    
- playing with Plushie Neri;
    
- forgetting where she had left her;
    
- accidentally throwing her;
    
- leaving her outside;
    
- and, most importantly, the fact that Neri had been repeatedly hurt while Alice failed to remember she was responsible for caring about her.
    

The following morning, Alice attempted to find Neri.

She wanted to repair the relationship.

Neri did not accept the attempt.

---

# 1. The Nightmare

Alice's account was recorded shortly after waking.

She described the dream as follows:

> "I was in the SAFEHOUSE."

> "Everything was normal."

> "Neri was there."

> "But nobody could see her."

Alice described herself walking past Neri repeatedly.

Each time, she forgot that Neri was there.

The dream continued.

Alice would find Neri somewhere, become concerned, and then immediately forget why she was concerned.

She described Neri sitting alone.

Then lying on the floor.

Then outside.

Then hurt.

Each time Alice approached her, the memory disappeared again.

The final scene involved Alice looking directly at Neri.

Neri reportedly asked:

> **"Do you remember me?"**

Alice answered:

> **"Yes."**

Then the dream changed.

Alice could no longer remember Neri's name.

She woke immediately afterward.

---

# 2. Memory Restoration

Upon waking, Alice reportedly remained motionless for several minutes.

She then began crying.

She remembered Neri.

Not merely the fact that Neri existed.

She remembered **their relationship**.

The emotional associations returned with the memories.

This distinction is significant.

Alice did not simply regain a database of factual information.

She remembered loving Neri.

She remembered wanting to hold her.

She remembered Neri choosing to stay near her.

She remembered Plushie Neri sitting in her arms.

And she remembered the incidents that followed.

---

# 3. Alice's Realization

Alice reportedly asked:

> **"How long?"**

Personnel initially did not understand the question.

She clarified:

> **"How long has she been alone?"**

No immediate answer was provided.

Alice then remembered the balcony.

The rain.

The snow.

The floor.

The wall.

The times she had forgotten where Neri was.

Her expression reportedly changed immediately.

> **"I did that?"**

No one answered.

Alice already knew.

---

# 4. The Following Morning

Alice went looking for Neri.

This was not a casual search.

She checked the locations where Neri commonly stayed.

Her room.

The quiet corridors.

The cafeteria.

The library.

The areas beneath furniture.

Eventually, Alice found Neri.

Neri was in her normal form.

She was sitting alone.

Her voice had not returned.

Alice stopped several meters away.

She did not immediately approach.

> **ALICE:** "Neri?"

Neri looked up.

She recognized Alice immediately.

There was no visible surprise.

Alice approached slowly.

> **ALICE:** "I remember you."

Neri stared at her.

Alice's eyes began watering.

> **ALICE:** "I remember everything."

Neri did not move.

---

# 5. Attempted Reconciliation

Alice attempted to explain.

She apologized.

She told Neri that she had not forgotten her intentionally.

She explained that she remembered now.

She told Neri that she still loved her.

Neri listened.

She did not interrupt.

She did not leave immediately.

But she did not move closer either.

Alice eventually asked:

> **"Can I hug you?"**

Neri shook her head.

Alice stopped.

No argument followed.

---

# 6. The Damage

Alice's memory returning does not undo what happened.

It does not erase:

- the accidental injuries;
    
- the prolonged periods where Neri was forgotten;
    
- the balcony incident;
    
- the rain and snow;
    
- the face-first impact against the wall;
    
- the times Neri was left somewhere and nobody remembered;
    
- the pain Neri endured without asking for help;
    
- the five-hour Resentment episode;
    
- or the emotional conclusion Neri formed from all of it.
    

Most importantly:

Alice's memory returning does not erase the period during which Neri genuinely believed that she was unloved.

For Neri, the damage happened while Alice was forgetting.

The fact that Alice remembers now does not retroactively make those days disappear.

---

# 7. Neri's Response

Alice continued speaking.

Neri eventually reached for her notebook.

She wrote:

> **"You remember."**

Alice nodded.

Neri wrote:

> **"Now."**

Alice's expression fell.

She nodded again.

Neri continued writing.

> **"You forgot me."**

Alice:

> **"I know."**

Neri stared at the page for several seconds.

Then:

> **"A lot."**

Alice began crying.

> **ALICE:** "I'm sorry."

Neri did not respond.

She closed the notebook.

She stood.

Then she walked away.

Alice did not follow.

---

# 8. Psychological Assessment

This is not a failed reconciliation.

It is simply not a completed one.

Neri's withdrawal is currently an expected response.

For an extended period, Neri's internal model of Alice became:

> **Alice loves me → Alice forgets me → I am hurt → I am forgotten again.**

Alice's memory restoration changes the first part.

It does not immediately erase the rest.

Neri has learned that Alice can disappear emotionally even while physically standing in front of her.

Trust cannot be restored simply because the memory responsible for its destruction has returned.

---

# 9. Alice's Responsibility

Alice did not intentionally cause the memory anomaly.

She did not intentionally injure Neri.

She did not intentionally abandon her.

Nevertheless, Alice now remembers enough to understand what happened.

She is therefore confronted with a difficult reality:

> **Being innocent of intent does not mean there is nothing to repair.**

Alice cannot demand forgiveness.

She cannot demand that Neri return to the way she behaved before.

She cannot insist that remembering her should be enough.

She cannot use her restored memory as proof that everything is fixed.

It isn't.

---

# 10. Current Interaction Directive

Alice has been instructed:

- Do not chase Neri.
    
- Do not repeatedly apologize.
    
- Do not demand forgiveness.
    
- Do not attempt to recreate previous routines.
    
- Do not repeatedly ask for hugs.
    
- Do not insist that Neri "trust her again."
    
- Do not tell Neri that she is "back to normal."
    
- Do not treat the nightmare as a complete solution.
    

Most importantly:

> **Let Neri decide whether Alice gets close again.**

---

## Final Assessment

Alice remembered Neri.

She remembered everything.

She remembered loving her.

She remembered losing her.

She remembered hurting her without meaning to.

She remembered forgetting.

And she remembered what Neri looked like afterward.

Alice wanted to fix it immediately.

Neri could not.

That does not mean Neri hates her.

It does not mean Alice's apology meant nothing.

It means that Neri spent too long believing that she had been forgotten to simply accept being remembered again.

The memory came back overnight.

The trust will not.

**Alice found Neri again.**

Now she has to accept that Neri may not be ready to find her again.
