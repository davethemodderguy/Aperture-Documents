## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

After an extended period of severe illness characterized by profound weakness, dimmed eyes, reduced speech, and difficulty keeping his head upright, Jake Akana has returned to his normal condition.

The apparent intervention?

**A cup of tea.**

No specialized Etheric procedure. No Genesis Construction. No intervention from the Architects. No emergency revival attempt.

Someone gave the six-year-old a warm drink.

His body temperature subsequently returned to a normal range, his strength recovered, his eyes regained their usual brightness, and his Scottish Fold ears—previously drooping alongside his lowered head—stood upright again.

The investigation determined that Jake had become **excessively cold**. The available observations support cold exposure as the explanation for his condition, although the exact physiological mechanism has not been independently established.

The medical team has cleared Jake as recovered from this particular episode, with continued monitoring recommended.

The entire Blacksite has been informed.

The entire Blacksite is, understandably, having feelings about this.

---

## II. CHRONOLOGY OF RECOVERY

### 08:14 — Continued Illness

Jake was observed moving slowly through the facility, relying on nearby furniture and walls for support. His eyes remained dim, his head drooped, and his responses were limited to short words or quiet sounds.

His right-thigh monitor continued to report abnormal readings.

Personnel had become accustomed to seeing him in this condition. Several employees had taken turns keeping him company, wrapping him in blankets, and offering gentle cuddles whenever he approached.

Despite these efforts, his condition had not meaningfully improved.

### 08:37 — The Tea

A warm cup of tea was offered to Jake.

He accepted it.

There was no dramatic reaction. No sudden flash of light. No anomalous discharge. No declaration from an omnipotent being.

Jake simply drank the tea.

A staff member noticed that his hands no longer felt as cold as they had earlier. Medical personnel were notified, and his temperature was checked.

The reading suggested that his body had been significantly colder than expected.

### 08:44 — First Signs of Improvement

Jake lifted his head without assistance.

His eyes brightened.

His movements became steadier, and he began responding to questions with his usual level of attention.

Then his ears moved.

Both folded ears gradually straightened into an upright position.

A nearby employee reportedly stared at him for several seconds before calling for the medical team.

Jake, apparently unaware that he had just invalidated several weeks of increasingly worried observations, asked:

> **Jake:** “Can I have more?”

The employee asked what he wanted more of.

> **Jake:** “Tea.”

### 08:51 — Return to Baseline

Medical personnel confirmed that Jake had returned to his usual condition.

His strength, responsiveness, and characteristic behavior were restored. His eyes had regained their familiar blue brightness, and his ears remained upright while he moved around the examination room.

His right-thigh monitor returned to its normal operating range, with no active alert requiring emergency intervention.

Tom arrived shortly afterward.

He looked at Jake.

He looked at the cup.

He looked back at Jake.

> **Tom:** “You've been sick for weeks.”

> **Jake:** “Mhm.”

> **Tom:** “You could barely hold your head up.”

> **Jake:** “Mhm.”

> **Tom:** “We were terrified something was seriously wrong with you.”

> **Jake:** “Mhm.”

> **Tom:** “And the solution was tea?”

Jake held up the empty cup.

> **Jake:** “Warm tea.”

Tom sat down.

He needed a moment.

## III. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT

The initial assumption that Jake was suffering from an unidentified illness has been revised.

The strongest available explanation is that **Jake's body had become dangerously cold**, producing the weakness, reduced responsiveness, dimmed eyes, and other symptoms observed during the preceding period.

Warming him through a warm drink coincided with a rapid and apparently complete recovery.

However, C.O.R.E. emphasizes the following distinctions:

- The recovery following tea consumption is documented.
    
- Excessive cold is the identified explanation for this episode.
    
- The precise reason Jake became so cold remains subject to investigation.
    
- The episode does not establish tea as a universal treatment for Jake's future medical emergencies.
    
- His recovery does not invalidate the seriousness of his previous symptoms.
    

Medical staff will investigate the environmental conditions, his temperature regulation, and whether his anomalous physiology contributed to the incident.

**No employee is authorized to replace medical assessment with tea simply because it worked this time.**

That directive was added after three separate departments requested permission to establish permanent tea stations specifically for Jake.

The requests were approved as a comfort measure.

They were not approved as a substitute for medicine.

---

## IV. THE SCOTTISH FOLD INCIDENT

The return of Jake's upright ears has attracted considerable attention.

Personnel are reminded that Jake's feline design was originally based on the Scottish Fold. His folded ears have therefore been a familiar feature of his identity, rather than a new development caused by his illness.

During the illness, his ears had remained folded and drooped alongside his lowered head. Following his recovery, they became upright.

The available observations establish a correlation between his improved condition and the change in ear position. They do not establish whether the change reflects temperature, muscle function, anomalous physiology, or another mechanism.

Jake himself has offered no useful scientific explanation.

When asked whether he noticed the change, he touched one ear, tilted his head, and said:

> **Jake:** “They're up.”

> **Researcher:** “Yes, Jake. They're up.”

> **Jake:** “Why?”

The researcher paused.

> **Researcher:** “We're working on that.”

> **Jake:** “Okay. Can I have cake?”

The researcher closed the interview.

Not because it was finished, but because the researcher had no answer to the first question and knew the second one had a much easier solution.

## V. PERSONNEL RESPONSE

Tom has remained close to Jake since the recovery, though his reaction has shifted from acute fear to exhausted disbelief.

Aries has requested the full medical report before accepting that the episode is conclusively resolved.

Bao has reportedly responded to the news by asking whether Jake has eaten properly, whether he is warm enough, and whether anyone has remembered to provide him with something other than tea.

The last question was directed at Tom.

Tom insists that he did not forget to feed his brother.

Jake, who was sitting beside him with a blanket around his shoulders, interrupted:

> **Jake:** “I want cake.”

Bao immediately stood up.

Tom pointed at Jake.

> **Tom:** “You see? This is what I deal with.”

> **Bao:** “He's hungry.”

> **Tom:** “He was nearly impossible to wake up yesterday!”

> **Bao:** “And today he's asking for cake. That's good.”

Tom opened his mouth to argue.

He stopped.

Then he pulled the blanket a little higher around Jake's shoulders.

> **Tom:** “...Fine. Cake after the medical check.”

> **Jake:** “And tea?”

> **Tom:** “And tea.”

> **Jake:** “Warm tea?”

> **Tom:** “Yes, Jake. Warm tea.”

Jake smiled.

For the first time in weeks, nobody had to wonder whether he had the strength to do it.

---

## VI. DIRECTIVES

1. Jake's immediate medical status is to be recorded as **recovered from the cold-associated episode**.
    
2. SAFEHOUSE personnel must ensure that Jake has access to appropriate warmth and comfortable resting areas.
    
3. Staff should continue observing his right-thigh monitor and respond to any renewed alerts.
    
4. Any recurrence of profound weakness, reduced responsiveness, abnormal breathing, or unusual temperature must prompt medical evaluation.
    
5. Warm drinks may be offered when appropriate, but must not delay emergency care.
    
6. Employees are encouraged to celebrate Jake's recovery without overwhelming him with attention or encouraging excessive activity.
    
7. The tea used during the recovery is to be retained as an ordinary beverage. It is not to be placed in a containment chamber, assigned a threat classification, or given an anomalous designation.
    

**Addendum:** A proposal to classify the tea as an emergency-grade recovery substance was rejected unanimously by the Medical Division.

The proposal's author has been assigned to help investigate why Jake became so cold.

## VII. C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

For weeks, we watched a little boy grow weaker.

We watched his eyes dim. We watched him struggle to hold his head up. We watched him continue to visit people even when walking had become difficult, because being near the people he loved mattered to him.

We were afraid that we were running out of time.

And then he drank a cup of tea.

It would be easy to laugh at the absurdity of it. In fact, several employees already have. After everything this facility has documented about Jake, the fact that excessive cold may have been responsible for his condition is almost offensively mundane.

But the tea was warm.

Someone noticed that Jake needed warmth, and he was given something that helped.

Sometimes care is complicated. Sometimes it requires specialized equipment, careful observation, and the combined efforts of an entire medical team.

And sometimes, after all the frightening possibilities have been considered, the immediate need is much simpler.

A warm drink. A blanket. Someone sitting beside you.

Jake is back on his feet. His eyes are bright again. His ears are up. He is asking for cake, nibbling at familiar sleeves, and wandering through the Blacksite as though he has important little-boy business to attend to.

Tom has threatened to confiscate every cold beverage in the building.

Bao has begun checking whether the tea is warm enough.

And somewhere in the Medical Division, a researcher is still staring at the temperature readings, wondering how a case that frightened an entire facility could have such an ordinary explanation.

Let them investigate.

For now, Jake is warm.

For now, Jake is well.

And for now, when he comes around the corner with his little ears standing upright, we get to greet him as ourselves—not as people preparing for another tragedy, but as the family that gets to see him smile again.

**C.O.R.E. FINAL STATUS: JAKE AKANA — RECOVERED. KEEP HIM WARM.**
