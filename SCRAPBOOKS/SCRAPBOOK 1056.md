**SUBJECT:** Neri — Normal-Form Weight vs. Plush-Form Handling  
**INVOLVED PERSONNEL:** Neri, Alice

---

## Executive Summary

Alice has become accustomed to carrying Plushie Neri.

This is entirely reasonable.

Plushie Neri is approximately teddy-bear sized, soft, and significantly easier to carry than Neri's normal body.

The problem occurs when Neri returns to normal form.

Alice is six years old.

Neri is ten.

Neri's normal body is considerably heavier than her plush form.

Alice does not possess anomalous strength.

Therefore:

> **Alice cannot simply pick up normal-form Neri.**

Unfortunately, Alice occasionally forgets this.

---

# INCIDENT 01 — "I CAN CARRY HER"

**TIME:** 09:14  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE COMMON AREA

Neri was standing beside a couch.

Alice approached.

Alice had recently spent several days carrying Plushie Neri.

She opened her arms.

> **ALICE:** "Come here, Neri."

Neri looked at her.

Alice placed both arms around Neri's waist.

She attempted to lift.

Nothing happened.

Neri remained completely stationary.

Alice tried again.

Still nothing.

Neri looked down at her.

Alice looked up.

> **ALICE:** "...You're heavier."

Neri nodded.

> **ALICE:** "A lot heavier."

Neri nodded again.

Alice attempted one final lift.

Neri's feet moved approximately one centimeter.

They immediately returned to the floor.

Alice stopped.

> **ALICE:** "Okay."

She released Neri.

---

# INCIDENT 02 — SECOND ATTEMPT

**TIME:** 13:27

Alice again attempted to pick up Neri.

This time, Neri was sitting on the floor.

Alice approached from behind.

She wrapped her arms around Neri.

> **ALICE:** "I'll carry you."

Neri immediately shook her head.

Alice tried anyway.

She could not lift her.

Neri remained seated.

Alice eventually sat down beside her.

> **ALICE:** "You're too heavy."

Neri gave a small shrug.

Alice looked annoyed.

Not at Neri.

At physics.

---

# INCIDENT 03 — PLUSHIE CONFUSION

**TIME:** 18:02

Alice entered the room and saw Neri standing normally.

Alice approached with the unmistakable confidence of someone expecting a successful pickup.

Neri recognized the situation immediately.

She took one step backward.

Alice took one step forward.

Neri took another step backward.

Alice stopped.

> **ALICE:** "I'm not going to drop you."

Neri shook her head.

> **ALICE:** "I can carry you."

Neri shook her head again.

> **ALICE:** "I carried you yesterday."

Neri pointed at herself.

Then at Alice.

Then made a small gesture indicating **heavier**.

Alice stared at her.

> **ALICE:** "...Oh."

Pause.

> **ALICE:** "You're not plushie."

Neri nodded.

> **ALICE:** "Right."

Neri sat down.

Alice sat beside her instead.

---

# C.O.R.E. PHYSICAL ASSESSMENT

There is no anomalous explanation required.

The difference is straightforward.

### Plush Form

Neri's body undergoes her established voluntary plush transformation.

Her muscles become cotton-like and her exterior becomes fabric-like.

She becomes approximately teddy-bear sized.

She is consequently easy for Alice to pick up.

### Normal Form

Neri returns to her ordinary physical body.

Her normal mass returns with her.

She is a ten-year-old child.

Alice is six.

Alice has ordinary six-year-old strength.

Therefore:

**Alice + normal Neri = unsuccessful lifting operation.**

No anomaly detected.

---

## Important Clarification

Alice's inability to lift Neri is not a failure on Alice's part.

Nor is Neri unusually heavy for her normal form.

The issue is simply that Alice has become accustomed to a version of Neri that is exceptionally easy to carry.

Alice's internal mental model has therefore become:

> **Neri = small, soft, portable.**

Reality occasionally reminds her:

> **Neri = ten-year-old child.**

---

# Jane Doe's Observation

Jane witnessed Alice attempting to lift normal-form Neri.

Alice strained.

Neri stood there patiently.

Jane watched for approximately five seconds.

> **JANE:** "Alice."

Alice looked over.

> **JANE:** "That's not the plushie."

Alice looked at Neri.

Then at Jane.

Then back at Neri.

> **ALICE:** "...I know."

Jane raised an eyebrow.

> **JANE:** "Do you?"

Alice stopped trying.

Neri quietly walked away.

---

# Neri's Response

Neri does not appear offended by Alice's attempts.

She generally recognizes that Alice is accustomed to carrying her plush form.

When Alice attempts to lift her and fails, Neri typically:

- waits;
    
- shakes her head;
    
- gestures that she is too heavy;
    
- or simply sits down beside Alice.
    

She does not mock Alice.

She does not complain.

She seems to understand the mistake.

---

## Final Assessment

Alice is fully authorized to carry Plushie Neri when Neri willingly permits it.

Alice is **not expected to carry normal-form Neri**.

Personnel should not encourage Alice to repeatedly attempt lifting Neri.

If Alice wants to be close to normal-form Neri, acceptable alternatives include:

- sitting beside her;
    
- hugging her while Neri is seated;
    
- holding her hand;
    
- sitting in Neri's lap if Neri permits;
    
- or simply staying nearby.
    

### C.O.R.E. Reminder

> **Plushie Neri:** portable.

> **Normal Neri:** ten-year-old child.

> **Alice:** six-year-old child.

> **Conclusion:** Alice cannot pick up Neri.
