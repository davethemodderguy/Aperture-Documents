# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon has become aware of the existence of **Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise**.

This information was not provided to her by Neri.

It was not provided by C.O.R.E.

It was not provided by Director Lucious.

It was not provided by any member of the Tachyon family.

Nora learned about it by accidentally overhearing a conversation between:

- **Elsher — MSAC**
    
- **Jake — MSAC**
    

during which the two discussed their respective Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise.

This discovery produced an immediate and significant reaction.

Nora had previously understood that Neri was:

> **“Really smart.”**

She now understands that Neri has apparently been producing highly restricted, individually awarded equipment bearing the **Tachyon Industries** name.

Her response was predictable.
