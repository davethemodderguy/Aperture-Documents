# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The day passed normally.

Nothing happened.

No emergency.

No attack.

No breach.

No anomaly.

The SAFEHOUSE residents continued with their respective activities.

Work was completed.

Meals were eaten.

Conversations occurred.

People came and went.

Eventually, everyone went to sleep.

It was, by all conventional measures, an ordinary day.

It was also Neri's birthday.

Nobody knew.

---

# 2. THE FOLLOWING MORNING

At approximately **06:██**, Jane Doe entered the cafeteria.

Her stated objective was uncomplicated:

Retrieve leftover chicken from the previous day's meal.

Jane did not expect to find anyone there.

She found Neri.

Neri was asleep.

She was sitting at one of the cafeteria tables with her head resting against her folded arms.

Jane stopped.

She stared.

For several seconds, she did not move.

Jane had seen Neri unconscious.

Jane had seen Neri exhausted.

Jane had seen Neri sick.

Jane had seen Neri deliberately withdraw from everyone.

But Jane had never seen Neri simply...

sleep.

Jane approached carefully.

She checked that Neri was breathing.

She was.

Her breathing was slow and steady.

Jane remained beside the table for several seconds.

Then:

**JANE:**  
"...You're actually asleep."

Neri did not respond.

Jane smiled faintly.

**JANE:**  
"Well."

Pause.

**JANE:**  
"Thank God."

---

# 3. THE CAKE

Jane noticed something beside Neri.

A plate.

On it was an unfinished blueberry cake.

It was clearly homemade.

One portion had been eaten.

Another portion had been cut but left untouched.

Jane initially assumed Neri had simply stayed awake late into the night.

This was not unusual.

Neri's insomnia was well documented.

Jane reached toward the plate.

She stopped.

There was something beside it.

A card.

---

# 4. THE CARD

The front contained handwritten text.

> **Happy Birthday... To me**

Jane opened it.

She read.

And then she read it again.

The following is an archival transcription.

---

# 5. JANE'S RESPONSE

Jane did not immediately move.

She simply stood there.

The cafeteria was silent.

The refrigerator hummed in the background.

Neri continued sleeping.

Jane looked at the cake.

Then at the card.

Then at Neri.

She slowly sat down in the chair beside her.

For once, Jane did not make a joke.

She did not wake her.

She did not touch the cake.

She simply remained there.

After several minutes, Jane quietly spoke.

**JANE:**  
"Yesterday."

She looked down at the card.

**JANE:**  
"It was yesterday."

Silence.

Jane's expression changed.

The realization was beginning to spread.

Neri had not written:

_I wish someone remembered._

She had written:

_I don't want to bother anyone._

That distinction hurt more.

---

# 6. NOTIFICATION OF RESIDENTIAL STAFF

Jane contacted Bao.

Bao arrived within minutes.

Frost followed.

Then Alice.

Then additional SAFEHOUSE residents began arriving after being informed that something had happened.

Nobody initially understood why Jane had called them.

Then they saw the card.

Nobody spoke.

Bao read it first.

She reached the section concerning her.

> _"Bao always has to take care of me because my blood keeps killing itself."_

Bao's expression fell.

Frost read the next section.

Alice stopped at:

> _"I don't really provide any sort of usefulness towards them."_

Jane did not need to explain the rest.

Everyone understood.

---

# 7. THE REALIZATION

Yesterday had been Neri's birthday.

Nobody had celebrated.

Nobody had forgotten deliberately.

That was almost worse.

Everyone had simply been busy.

Busy with work.

Busy with maintenance.

Busy with research.

Busy with their own lives.

Neri had noticed.

And instead of asking anyone to stop—

she made herself a cake.

She wrote herself a birthday card.

She sat alone in the cafeteria.

And eventually, exhausted beyond her usual insomnia, she fell asleep beside it.

---

# 8. BAO

Bao remained beside Neri for several minutes.

She looked at the unfinished cake.

Then at the card.

Then at Neri.

Her voice was quiet.

**BAO:**  
"She thought we wouldn't want to celebrate her."

Frost lowered her head.

**FROST:**  
"She thought she'd be wasting our time."

Jane looked away.

**JANE:**  
"She thought she wasn't worth the time."

Nobody corrected her immediately.

Because the card had already said it.

---

# 9. ALICE

Alice eventually approached the table.

She looked at Neri sleeping.

Then at the cake.

Then at the card.

Alice's eyes became visibly wet.

**ALICE:**  
"She made it herself."

Jane nodded.

**JANE:**  
"Yeah."

Alice looked at the untouched portion.

**ALICE:**  
"She saved some."

Nobody answered.

Alice carefully sat down.

She did not wake Neri.

---

# 10. JANE DOE

Jane remained seated beside Neri.

Her tail was completely still.

This was unusual.

Jane was normally incapable of remaining still for this long.

She looked at the card again.

Then at Neri.

**JANE:**  
"You're an idiot, you know that?"

Neri continued sleeping.

Jane's voice softened.

**JANE:**  
"You could've just told me."

Nothing.

**JANE:**  
"You could've bothered me."

Nothing.

**JANE:**  
"That's what I'm here for."

Jane wiped her eyes before anyone could comment.

**JANE:**  
"...Damn it."

---

# 11. THE CAKE

The blueberry cake was not discarded.

It was placed in the cafeteria refrigerator.

The unfinished portion remained untouched until Neri woke.

No one ate it.

Not even Jane.

A handwritten note was placed beside the plate.

It contained only:

> **"You don't have to eat it alone."**

No signature was added.

There was no need.

---

# 12. NERI'S SLEEP

Neri continued sleeping.

For the first time in years, nobody attempted to wake her simply because she was asleep at an unusual hour.

Medical monitoring confirmed that she was stable.

Her breathing remained normal.

No acute anemic episode was detected.

She was simply exhausted.

Eventually, several residents quietly left the cafeteria.

Bao remained nearby.

Jane remained beside the table.

Alice remained nearby as well.

They waited.

---

# 13. C.O.R.E. OBSERVATION

The incident has prompted a review of Neri's existing psychological observations.

Her previous drawings repeatedly contained themes of:

- being unnoticed;
    
- being left behind;
    
- people arriving too late;
    
- being separated from others;
    
- being unable to call for help;
    
- and believing herself to be a burden.
    

The birthday card presents the same themes in written form.

However, one distinction is significant.

Neri did not ask to be rescued.

She did not ask anyone to come.

She did not complain that nobody remembered.

She explicitly decided that everyone else was **too busy**.

She then removed herself from the equation.

This behavior is consistent with her established belief that her needs are less important than the needs of others.

---

# 14. CORRECTIVE RESIDENTIAL GUIDANCE

C.O.R.E. recommends that personnel avoid turning Neri's birthday into an event centered around guilt.

Do not tell her:

> "We're sorry we forgot."

Do not repeatedly apologize.

Do not make her responsible for comforting everyone else about forgetting.

Most importantly:

Do not make her believe that she caused distress by having a birthday.

The appropriate response is considerably simpler.

Celebrate her.

Not because she is useful.

Not because she survived.

Not because she requires medical care.

Not because someone feels guilty.

Because it is her birthday.

---

# 15. ADDENDUM — MORNING

At approximately **08:██**, Neri began to stir.

Her head lifted slightly.

She blinked.

She looked around.

She noticed Jane.

Then Bao.

Then Alice.

Then Frost.

More people were standing around the cafeteria than she had expected.

Neri immediately became confused.

She looked toward the cake.

Then toward the card.

Then toward everyone else.

She slowly reached for her glasses.

Jane smiled.

**JANE:**  
"Morning, birthday girl."

Neri froze.

Her eyes widened.

She looked down.

Then back at Jane.

She did not speak.

Jane pointed toward the cake.

**JANE:**  
"You left some."

Neri looked at the cake.

Then at the people around her.

She hesitated.

Bao gently pulled out a chair.

**BAO:**  
"Sit."

Neri remained standing.

Bao smiled.

**BAO:**  
"We're eating cake."

Neri looked at everyone.

Her expression was uncertain.

Almost frightened.

As if she was waiting for somebody to tell her she had interrupted something important.

Nobody did.

Alice sat down first.

**ALICE:**  
"Can I have some?"

Neri stared at her.

Then slowly nodded.

Jane leaned back in her chair.

**JANE:**  
"Happy birthday, My Cute Seal."

Neri immediately gave Jane an irritated look.

Jane smiled.

**JANE:**  
"There she is."

For the first time that morning—

Neri smiled.

Very slightly.

But she did.

---

# 16. FINAL ASSESSMENT

The SAFEHOUSE did not fail Neri because they did not care.

They failed to notice.

There is a difference.

But to someone who has spent most of her life believing that she is disposable, the distinction may not matter.

Neri did not want an extravagant celebration.

She wanted nothing.

That was the problem.

She had already convinced herself that wanting anything would be an inconvenience.

The birthday card was therefore not merely a birthday message.

It was evidence of a belief C.O.R.E. had already identified elsewhere:

> **"I am allowed to exist as long as I do not cost anyone anything."**

That belief remains dangerous.

The SAFEHOUSE's responsibility is not to make Neri useful enough to justify keeping.

It is to make her understand that she never needed to justify it.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Neri spent her sixteenth birthday alone.

She made herself a blueberry cake.

She wrote herself a birthday card.

She wished herself a happy birthday.

And then, exhausted, she fell asleep beside it.

The next morning, someone finally noticed.

Not because she called for them.

Not because she collapsed.

Not because something went wrong.

Someone simply walked into the cafeteria.

And saw her.

This time—

they stayed.
