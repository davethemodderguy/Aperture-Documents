# SAFEHOUSE INCIDENT REPORT

## NERI — UNAUTHORIZED ABSENCE / RECOVERY

**Subject:** Neri  
**Primary Search Coordinator:** Jane Doe  
**Duration Missing:** Approximately 3 hours  
**Status:** FOUND ALIVE  
**Medical Status:** Exhausted; subsequently evaluated  
**Security Status:** Resolved

---

## 1. THE EMPTY SEAT

Jane noticed because Neri wasn't where she usually was.

That alone wasn't unusual.

Neri liked walking around.

She could disappear into the cafeteria, wander through the Blacksite, visit Alice, sit with Bao, explore her subspace, or simply find somewhere quiet to read.

Jane initially assumed she was doing exactly that.

Then thirty minutes passed.

Then an hour.

Jane started asking.

> **Jane:** "Has anyone seen Neri?"

Nobody knew.

Jane's expression changed.

---

# 2. THE FIRST SEARCH

Jane checked the places Neri commonly visited.

Cafeteria.

Empty.

Chemistry area.

Empty.

Her usual walking routes.

Nothing.

Neri's subspace entrance was checked.

Nothing.

Jane contacted people who knew Neri.

Bao hadn't seen her.

Frost hadn't seen her.

Alice hadn't seen her.

Corin hadn't seen her.

Orphie hadn't seen her.

Nobody knew where she had gone.

Jane stopped treating it as a casual absence.

> **Jane:** "Okay."

She took out her communications device.

> **Jane:** "We're looking for her."

---

# 3. JANE KNOWS NERI

Jane understood something the others might not have immediately considered.

Neri didn't always disappear because she was lost.

Sometimes she disappeared because she believed **other people would be better off without her around.**

After the previous incidents, Jane couldn't dismiss that possibility.

She remembered Neri's words:

> **"I'm not useful."**

> **"I don't want to bother people."**

> **"I'm not anyone's."**

And the worst:

> **"If I die, I die."**

Jane's stomach tightened.

> **Jane:** "No."

She refused to let that possibility sit unanswered.

---

# 4. THREE HOURS

The search continued.

The Blacksite became increasingly aware that something was wrong.

People checked corridors.

Rooms.

Common areas.

Safe areas.

Places Neri had previously visited.

Her name circulated through communications.

Jane kept searching.

She didn't stop.

At one point, someone suggested that Neri might simply want to be alone.

Jane immediately rejected the idea.

> **Jane:** "Then we'll let her be alone after we know she's safe."

Silence.

> **Jane:** "I'm not assuming she's okay."

---

# 5. FOUND

Approximately three hours after Neri was first reported missing, Jane finally found her.

Neri was somewhere quiet.

Alone.

Sitting on the ground.

Her chemistry book was beside her.

She looked exhausted.

Not injured.

Not under attack.

Just...

tired.

Jane stopped several meters away.

For a moment, she didn't say anything.

Then:

> **Jane:** "Neri."

Neri looked up.

Her eyes widened.

> **Neri:** "Jane?"

Jane walked toward her.

> **Jane:** "Yes."

Neri looked confused.

> **Neri:** "Why are you here?"

Jane stared at her.

> **Jane:** "Because you disappeared."

> **Neri:** "Oh."

That word again.

**Oh.**

Jane closed her eyes.

---

# 6. "THREE HOURS."

> **Jane:** "Do you know how long you've been gone?"

Neri shook her head.

> **Jane:** "Three hours."

Neri looked surprised.

> **Neri:** "Oh."

Jane sat down beside her.

> **Jane:** "Why?"

Neri was quiet.

> **Jane:** "Why did you leave?"

> **Neri:** "I wanted to be alone."

Jane waited.

> **Jane:** "Why?"

Neri didn't answer.

Jane didn't push immediately.

Eventually Neri whispered:

> **Neri:** "Everyone was tired."

Jane looked at her.

> **Neri:** "Everyone kept worrying."

Her voice became quieter.

> **Neri:** "I thought maybe if I wasn't around..."

She stopped.

Jane understood.

> **Jane:** "Neri."

Neri looked down.

> **Neri:** "They wouldn't have to worry."

Jane felt something inside her drop.

---

# 7. JANE DOESN'T YELL

She wanted to.

After three hours of searching, fear, and imagining every possible outcome, Jane had every reason to be furious.

But she remembered the last conversation.

She remembered what Neri heard when people became angry.

So Jane didn't yell.

She simply said:

> **Jane:** "You were wrong."

Neri looked at her.

> **Neri:** "About what?"

> **Jane:** "Everything you just said."

Neri looked away.

> **Jane:** "People weren't tired of you."

> **Jane:** "They were worried about you."

> **Jane:** "There's a difference."

---

# 8. "WE WERE LOOKING FOR YOU."

Jane pointed toward herself.

> **Jane:** "I spent three hours looking for you."

Neri stared.

> **Jane:** "Bao was worried."

> **Jane:** "Frost was worried."

> **Jane:** "Alice was worried."

> **Jane:** "Corin was worried."

> **Jane:** "Orphie was worried."

Jane paused.

> **Jane:** "Everyone was looking."

Neri's eyes began watering.

> **Neri:** "Why?"

Jane looked genuinely confused.

> **Jane:** "Because you're Neri."

> **Neri:** "But I'm not useful."

> **Jane:** "That's not why."

> **Neri:** "Then why?"

Jane answered without hesitation.

> **Jane:** "Because you're ours."

Neri froze.

Jane realized the wording.

She corrected herself immediately.

> **Jane:** "Not like property."

Neri gave a tiny, confused smile.

> **Jane:** "I mean you're part of us."

A pause.

> **Jane:** "You're part of this place."

> **Jane:** "You're part of our lives."

> **Jane:** "You're someone we expect to come back."

---

# 9. THE PART NERI DIDN'T EXPECT

Neri quietly asked:

> **Neri:** "You really looked for me?"

Jane almost laughed.

> **Jane:** "For three hours."

> **Neri:** "The whole time?"

> **Jane:** "Pretty much."

Neri looked at the ground.

> **Neri:** "Sorry."

Jane shook her head.

> **Jane:** "We'll talk about that later."

Neri nodded.

Jane extended her hand.

> **Jane:** "Come on."

Neri hesitated.

Then took it.

Jane helped her stand.

Neri immediately swayed.

Jane caught her.

> **Jane:** "Whoa."

> **Neri:** "I'm okay."

Jane gave her a look.

> **Jane:** "Don't."

Neri stopped.

Jane looked at her carefully.

> **Jane:** "You're exhausted."

Neri nodded.

> **Jane:** "We're getting you checked."

> **Neri:** "Okay."

---

# 10. THE RETURN

When Neri returned to SAFEHOUSE, nobody celebrated loudly.

Nobody lectured her.

Nobody demanded explanations.

Bao simply hugged her.

Frost stayed beside her.

Alice sat nearby.

Corin and Orphie remained close.

Neri looked overwhelmed.

Jane stood slightly behind them.

She watched Neri slowly realize something.

Nobody was angry that she had returned.

They were relieved.

---

# 11. JANE'S PRIVATE NOTE

Later that evening, Jane wrote a private observation.

> _"I thought the worst part would be not knowing where she was."_

> _"It wasn't."_

> _"The worst part was realizing why she left."_

> _"She didn't run because she hated us."_

> _"She didn't run because she wanted to disappear."_

> _"She ran because she thought disappearing would make things easier for everyone else."_

Jane stopped writing.

Then added:

> _"That's the belief we're fighting."_

Another line followed.

> _"Not the anemia."_

> _"Not the fainting."_

> _"Not even the self-sacrifice."_

> _"The belief that nobody needs her."_

Jane put the pen down.

Then, after a moment, added one final sentence.

> _"I'm glad I found her."_

**END REPORT**
