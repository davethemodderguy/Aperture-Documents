# 1. BACKGROUND

The newly introduced MSAC trainee batch demonstrated immediate interest in the Night Stalker program.

Their initial questions were straightforward:

> **“Where can we get one?”**

> **“How do we earn one?”**

> **“Are there more?”**

> **“Can we qualify for one?”**

Personnel attempted to explain the program.

The trainees remained confused.

The matter was subsequently referred to the person who actually created the Night Stalkers.

**Neri Tachyon.**

Neri agreed to explain.

She did not appear particularly enthusiastic about doing so.

She did it anyway.

---

# 2. THE BRIEFING

**LOCATION:** MSAC Training Room

Seven trainees were seated around a conference table.

Two female.

Five male.

Neri stood at the front.

She was in her normal humanoid form.

Her glasses were slightly crooked.

A Night Stalker authorization record was displayed behind her.

> **Neri:**  
> “Yes.”

# 4. THE PROGRAM WAS NEVER INTENDED TO BE MASS PRODUCTION

Neri explained that the Night Stalker project was never intended to become a standard MSAC weapon platform.

It was a deliberately limited project.

The weapons were individually produced.

Each was personally reviewed.

Each received its own documentation.

Each received a presentation case.

Each received three loaded magazines.

Each received a handwritten letter from Neri.

And each was assigned to a specific person.

Neri's wording was simple:

> **Neri:**  
> “They're not equipment.”

Pause.

> **Neri:**  
> “They're mine.”

She looked at the list.

> **Neri:**  
> “I made them.”

---

# 5. WHY MSAC HAS SEVERAL

One trainee pointed toward the names.

> **Trainee:**  
> “Why does MSAC have so many?”

Neri looked at him.

> **Neri:**  
> “Because they earned them.”

> **Trainee:**  
> “Through combat?”

Neri shook her head.

> **Neri:**  
> “Not only combat.”

She explained that Night Stalker recipients were selected through a combination of demonstrated capability, judgment, reliability, performance, and circumstances that Neri personally considered meaningful.

The exact criteria were never standardized into a formal application system.

There was no:

**NIGHT STALKER APPLICATION FORM**

There was no:

**NIGHT STALKER CERTIFICATION COURSE**

There was no:

**NIGHT STALKER PURCHASE PROGRAM**

There was no waiting list.

Neri had simply decided who received them.

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The trainees originally believed the Night Stalkers were simply rare weapons.

Neri corrected that assumption.

They are not rare because production is difficult.

They are rare because **Neri decided there should only be ten.**

Ten weapons.

Ten recipients.

Ten pieces of a project that will not be repeated.

The Sleight of Hand program is different.

There are still four available.

There is still a path to earning one.
