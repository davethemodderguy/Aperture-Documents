# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has begun speaking again.

The return of verbal communication occurred several hours after the cardiac separation incident.

His speech is currently quiet and infrequent.

No neurological or anomalous instability has been detected during speech.

More notably, Jake has changed how he carries his teddy bear.

Rather than hugging it continuously, Jake now wears the bear on his back.

The bear rests against him in a manner resembling a backpack.

Its arms remain around Jake's shoulders.

Its small cardiac manifestation continues beating.

> **thump.**

Jake appears comfortable with the arrangement.

When he becomes distressed, he may still pull the bear around to his front and hug it.

Otherwise, the bear remains on his back.

---

# 1. RETURN OF SPEECH

At approximately **09:14**, Jake was sitting beside Nora.

Nora had been speaking to him for several minutes without receiving a verbal response.

She eventually asked:

> **Nora:**  
> “Jake?”

Jake looked at her.

A pause.

Then, quietly:

> **Jake:**  
> “Yeah?”

Nora froze.

She stared at him.

Jake looked confused.

> **Jake:**  
> “What?”

Nora's expression immediately changed.

> **Nora:**  
> “You talked.”

Jake shrugged.

> **Jake:**  
> “I know.”

Nora hugged him.

Jake tolerated it.

---

# 2. CURRENT SPEECH PATTERN

Jake has not returned to his previous level of conversation.

He remains quiet.

However, he now responds verbally when he wants to.

Observed responses include:

> **“Yeah.”**

> **“No.”**

> **“Okay.”**

> **“I'm hungry.”**

> **“It's loud.”**

> **“Can we go outside?”**

His voice remains soft.

There is no indication that speaking causes physical distress.

The current assessment is that his previous silence represented emotional withdrawal rather than permanent loss of speech.

---

# 3. THE BACKPACK

The teddy bear has developed a new configuration.

Jake now wears it on his back.

The bear's arms rest around Jake's shoulders while its body remains against his back.

From a distance, the configuration resembles a small backpack.

The resemblance has already resulted in several personnel referring to it as:

> **“The Jake Backpack.”**

C.O.R.E. does not officially recognize this terminology.

Personnel continue using it anyway.

---

# 4. CARDIAC ACTIVITY

The bear's heartbeat remains continuous.

> **thump.**

> **thump.**

The rhythm corresponds to Jake's cardiac manifestation.

Jake's chest continues to produce no conventional heartbeat.

The bear remains the physical location of his heart.

When Jake is calm, the heartbeat is steady.

When Jake becomes frightened or distressed, it becomes faster.

Jake appears to find the sound reassuring.

He occasionally reaches behind himself to touch the bear without turning around.

---

# 5. JAKE EXPLAINS THE CHANGE

Neri was the first person to ask why Jake had stopped hugging the bear constantly.

She pointed toward his back.

> **Neri:**  
> “Why there?”

Jake looked over his shoulder.

> **Jake:**  
> “It's easier.”

Neri waited.

> **Jake:**  
> “I can carry it.”

Neri nodded.

Jake added:

> **Jake:**  
> “And it carries me too.”

Neri looked at him.

Jake smiled very faintly.

# 12. CURRENT EMOTIONAL STATE

The return of speech should not be interpreted as a complete emotional recovery.

Jake remains sad.

Very sad.

He still remembers what he overheard.

He still knows what his mother called him.

He has not recovered his previous belief that she loved him.

His halo remains divided into two floating fragments.

He still has no wings.

He remains a Weeping Angel.

He still cannot return to the Starlands.

The difference is that Jake is once again communicating.

He can now say when he is hungry.

He can say when something hurts.

He can ask for someone to stay.

He can say when he wants to leave.

This is considered a meaningful improvement in communication, even though his underlying sorrow remains.

---

# 13. CURRENT CONFIGURATION

Jake's normal daytime appearance now consists of:

- small childlike ghostly form;
    
- small azure cloak;
    
- blue angel halo, fractured into two floating pieces;
    
- teddy bear worn against his back like a backpack.
    

The bear's heartbeat remains audible at close range.

> **thump.**

Jake occasionally pats the bear over his shoulder.

> **thump.**

He appears reassured by the sound.

---

# FINAL OBSERVATION

At 20:17, Jake walked through the SAFEHOUSE corridor.

His teddy bear was strapped against his back.

His two halo fragments floated above him.

Nora walked beside him.

Jake looked toward the cafeteria.

> **Jake:**  
> “Cake?”

Nora smiled.

> **Nora:**  
> “Cake.”

Jake nodded.

They continued walking.

After a few seconds, Jake reached behind himself and touched the teddy bear.

> **thump.**

He kept walking.

He was still sad.

The sadness had not disappeared.

His mother's words had not been forgotten.

His halo was still broken.

But he was speaking again.

And his heart was still with him.

Just wearing itself like a backpack.
