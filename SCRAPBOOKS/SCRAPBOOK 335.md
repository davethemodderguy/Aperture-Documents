# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **SECURITY DIVISION – INCIDENT REPORT**

### **CASE FILE: KCA-IR-163 – "NOT AGAIN."**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **MEDICAL UPDATE**

Following several days of rest and the attentive care of both medical staff and numerous Z-335 entities, **Corey** has made a full recovery.

Medical examination confirms:

- Fever resolved.
    
- No lasting complications.
    
- Cleared to resume normal activities.
    

When informed that Corey was feeling better, the Marts that had been taking turns looking after her collectively replied:

> **"Happy!"**

The temporary bedside rotation was immediately dissolved.

Most of the participating Marts returned to their usual duties.

One stayed for another hour anyway.

---

# **SEPARATE INCIDENT**

Approximately three hours after Corey's medical clearance...

The KCA was attacked.

Again.

---

# **INITIAL RESPONSE**

At **09:14**, Camera Mart detected an armed group approaching the main entrance.

It calmly pressed the internal alert button.

> "Security."

The KCA Security Office responded.

> "How many?"

Camera Mart looked at the monitor.

> "...Enough."

---

# **RAPID RESPONSE DEPLOYMENT**

Within seconds, ventilation hatches throughout the lobby quietly opened.

Hundreds of Rapid Response Marts entered their designated positions.

The attackers, apparently familiar with previous reports, immediately noticed the vents.

One member sighed.

> "...It's the blue blobs."

Another replied:

> "...Maybe this time."

History would disagree.

---

# **ENGAGEMENT**

The attackers attempted to advance using coordinated shield formations.

Rapid Response Marts responded with their standard doctrine.

Phase One:

> Vent emergence.

Phase Two:

> Boulder Mode.

Phase Three:

> Swarm.

Approximately eight seconds later, the entire assault team was lying on the floor beneath a large pile of blue rocks.

One Mart politely announced:

> "Stop."

Another added:

> "Please."

The attackers complied.

---

# **SURRENDER**

Before any KCA officers reached the lobby, the group's leader raised one hand.

> "...We surrender."

The nearest Mart immediately softened back into its normal form.

> "Okay."

Another Mart offered one of the attackers a cheese cracker.

The attacker looked confused.

> "...Thanks?"

---

# **SECURITY FOOTAGE**

Review of the security recordings revealed an unusual detail.

One attacker appeared to recognize exactly what was about to happen.

Just before the vents opened, he quietly said:

> "...I knew we should've picked a bank."

---

Watching the security footage remotely from the Blacksite:

> "No."

> "...Good."

---

**Former Robber Turned Security Guard**

He watched the footage.

Then quietly nodded.

> "...Yep."

> "...That's exactly how I got hired."

---

# **STATUS OF THE RAPID RESPONSE MARTS**

Following the incident:

- No Mart injuries.
    
- No personnel injuries.
    
- No civilian injuries.
    
- No facility damage.
    
- No property loss.
    
- Approximately three boxes of cheese crackers consumed during the post-incident celebration.
    

The Rapid Response Marts returned to their normal patrol routes within minutes.

Several were later observed happily crawling back into the ventilation system.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The KCA continues to experience occasional robbery attempts despite the now well-documented effectiveness of its Mart security force.

When asked why anyone still tries, one Intelligence analyst answered:

> "...Some people think they'll be the exception."

Security footage suggests otherwise.

As the last attacker was escorted out of the building, one small Mart standing on a ventilation grate waved enthusiastically.

> **"Goodbye!"**

The attacker sighed.

Then waved back.

> "...Yeah."

> "...Goodbye."

The Mart smiled.

> **"We'll meet again!"**

The attacker looked at the KCA building one final time.

> "...Hopefully not."

**End of Incident Report**
