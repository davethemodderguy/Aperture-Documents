## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### RESIDENT WELFARE & MEDICAL DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-JAKE-178

**STATUS:** ACTIVE MONITORING  
**SUBJECT:** Jake — Unintentional Social Deterrence  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has recently developed a recurring social problem.

He wants friends.

He is attempting to meet people.

He is making an effort to approach newer SAFEHOUSE personnel and residents.

Unfortunately, Jake's appearance and circumstances continue to produce the opposite result.

New personnel frequently see:

- a six-year-old boy with glowing azure eyes;
    
- white hair;
    
- faint celestial particles floating around him;
    
- a fractured halo;
    
- an unnaturally calm expression;
    
- a teddy bear with a visibly audible heartbeat;
    
- and, occasionally, Jake standing silently in a dark hallway.
    

They then become frightened.

Jake interprets their retreat as:

> **"They don't want to be my friend."**

This is incorrect.

They are usually simply startled.

Unfortunately, Jake does not know that.

---

# 2. FIRST ATTEMPT

A newly assigned researcher was standing near the cafeteria.

Jake approached.

He stopped several feet away.

The researcher looked down.

Jake looked up.

The bear produced a quiet:

> **THUMP.**

The researcher froze.

Jake waited.

Another:

> **THUMP.**

The researcher slowly stepped backward.

Jake tilted his head.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Hi."

The researcher immediately left.

Jake remained standing there.

He looked down at his teddy bear.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Okay."

The bear continued beating.

---

# 3. SECOND ATTEMPT

Jake later attempted to introduce himself to a new technician.

He approached while carrying his bear.

The technician noticed the glowing eyes first.

Then the halo.

Then the floating particles.

Then the heartbeat.

The technician visibly reconsidered every decision that had led to their current employment.

Jake smiled slightly.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I'm Jake."

The technician took another step backward.

> **TECHNICIAN:**  
> "...Hi."

Jake waited.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Do you want to be friends?"

The technician stared.

> **TECHNICIAN:**  
> "...I have to go."

They left.

Jake watched them disappear around the corner.

His expression fell.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Okay."

---

# 4. THE PROBLEM

Jake's social approach is fundamentally harmless.

He does not threaten people.

He does not demand attention.

He does not invade personal space.

He generally approaches slowly and waits for the other person to acknowledge him.

His problem is entirely visual.

He looks like an extremely small celestial apparition carrying a living teddy bear containing his heart.

To experienced SAFEHOUSE residents, this is simply:

> **Jake.**

To someone who has never met him:

> **What the hell is that?**

---

# 5. THE HALLWAY INCIDENT

The most notable incident occurred late in the evening.

A new researcher was walking through a dim corridor.

Jake was sitting against the wall.

He looked up.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Hi."

The researcher stopped.

Jake stood.

The bear's heartbeat became slightly faster.

> **THUMP.**

The researcher stared.

Jake took one small step closer.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I'm Jake."

The researcher took one step backward.

Jake stopped.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Do you want to be friends?"

The researcher turned around and walked away.

Jake remained still.

After several seconds:

> **JAKE:**  
> "...I was being nice."

---

# 6. NORA FINDS HIM

Nora eventually found Jake sitting alone near the residential elevator.

She sat beside him.

> **NORA:**  
> "What's wrong?"

Jake looked down.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Nobody wants to be my friend."

Nora frowned.

> **NORA:**  
> "Why?"

Jake pointed toward the hallway.

> **JAKE:**  
> "They keep leaving."

Nora thought about this.

Then looked at his glowing eyes.

Then his halo.

Then the floating particles.

Then the teddy bear.

> **NORA:**  
> "...Oh."

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "What?"

> **NORA:**  
> "You're scary."

Jake's face fell.

Nora immediately corrected herself.

> **NORA:**  
> "Not bad scary!"

Jake continued looking at her.

> **NORA:**  
> "Just... mysterious scary."

Jake looked at his bear.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I don't want to be scary."

Nora hugged him.

> **NORA:**  
> "You're Jake."

Jake quietly hugged her back.

---

# 7. NERI'S OBSERVATION

Neri later observed Jake attempting another introduction.

A new employee approached.

Jake waved.

The employee froze.

Jake's bear produced:

> **THUMP.**

The employee left.

Neri walked over.

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "What am I doing wrong?"

Neri considered the question.

She looked at Jake.

Then the bear.

Then the glowing eyes.

She took out a small piece of paper.

She wrote:

> **"Nothing."**

Jake read it.

Neri added:

> **"They don't know you."**

Jake looked at the paper.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Oh."

Neri nodded.

She then wrote:

> **"Give them time."**

Jake held the note carefully.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Okay."

---

# 8. AETERNA'S ADVICE

Aeterna was informed of the situation.

She found Jake sitting outside his room.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "You are frightening people."

Jake looked down.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I don't mean to."

> **AETERNA:**  
> "I know."

Pause.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Neither do they."

Jake looked up.

Aeterna crouched beside him.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "You don't need to become less strange."

Jake blinked.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "You need to give them enough time to learn that strange does not mean dangerous."

Jake considered this.

> **JAKE:**  
> "How long?"

> **AETERNA:**  
> "As long as it takes."

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Okay."

---

# 9. C.O.R.E. INTERVENTION

C.O.R.E. has determined that Jake should not be discouraged from social interaction.

His attempts to make friends represent a significant positive development given his established history of isolation and distrust.

Personnel are therefore encouraged to respond normally when Jake approaches.

Recommended response:

> **"Hi, Jake."**

Followed by ordinary conversation.

Personnel should **not**:

- scream;
    
- flee;
    
- point at the teddy bear;
    
- ask whether he is going to haunt them;
    
- ask whether the bear is going to eat them;
    
- attempt to test the heartbeat;
    
- suddenly become fascinated with another hallway;
    
- or pretend they did not see him.
    

The last behavior is particularly confusing to Jake.

---

# 10. ORIENTATION UPDATE

New personnel orientation now includes:

### IF YOU SEE JAKE:

**Do not panic.**

He is a SAFEHOUSE resident.

He is six years old.

He is not hostile.

The glowing eyes are normal.

The floating lights are normal.

The fractured halo is normal.

The teddy bear's heartbeat is normal.

The fact that the teddy bear contains his actual heart is also normal **for Jake.**

If he approaches you and says:

> **"Hi."**

The correct response is:

> **"Hi, Jake."**

You have now successfully survived your first interaction with a celestial six-year-old carrying his own heart.

Congratulations.

# 11. THE FIRST SUCCESS

Several days after the orientation update, Jake approached a new maintenance worker.

The worker had already received the briefing.

Jake stopped.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Hi."

The worker smiled.

> **WORKER:**  
> "Hi, Jake."

Jake paused.

His bear continued beating normally.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I'm Jake."

> **WORKER:**  
> "I know."

Jake looked surprised.

> **JAKE:**  
> "You do?"

> **WORKER:**  
> "Yeah."

Pause.

> **WORKER:**  
> "Nice to meet you."

Jake's expression softened.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Nice to meet you too."

Neither of them moved.

The worker eventually asked:

> **WORKER:**  
> "Do you like plushies?"

Jake looked toward his room.

> **JAKE:**  
> "A lot."

> **WORKER:**  
> "Me too."

Jake smiled faintly.

It lasted approximately three seconds.

Then:

> **JAKE:**  
> "Do you want to see my room?"

The worker hesitated.

> **WORKER:**  
> "Sure."

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Okay."

He walked toward the elevator.

The worker followed.

The bear went:

> **THUMP.**

The worker did not run.

---

# 12. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Jake's problem is not an inability to socialize.

It is that his appearance creates an initial impression that is difficult for unfamiliar people to overcome.

He does not need to change.

He needs people to become familiar with him.

The distinction is important.

Jake spent decades being alone.

He now lives among people.

He wants friends.

He wants someone to sit beside him.

He wants someone to ask about his plushies.

He wants someone to say hello when they pass him in the hallway.

He wants someone to knock on his door and wait.

He wants someone to stay.

He is not asking for much.

He just happens to look terrifying while asking for it.

Jake keeps trying.

He walks up to someone.

He says:

> **"Hi."**

They see the glowing eyes.

The halo.

The floating stars.

The teddy bear.

They hear:

> **THUMP.**

And they leave.

Jake watches them go.

Then he looks down at his bear.

And quietly says:

> **"...I just wanted a friend."**

So C.O.R.E. has one final recommendation for new personnel:

If the small celestial child carrying his own beating heart approaches you—

**don't run.**

Say hello.

You might be the friend he's been looking for.
