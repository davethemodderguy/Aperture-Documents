# 1. MISSION SUMMARY

Aperture Logistics Warehouse 7 was overrun by an estimated **2,500 Woodland Coorp mercenaries**.

The warehouse occupies an area approximately comparable to a football field.

The hostile force established defensive positions throughout the structure and refused multiple surrender requests.

A five-person Aperture team was subsequently assigned to clear the facility.

The team consisted of:

- **Neri Tachyon**
    
- **Nora Tachyon**
    
- **Josh Newman — “Snake”**
    
- **Elsher**
    
- **MSAC Commander**
    

Mission restriction:

> **TACHYON INDUSTRIES MERCHANDISE ONLY.**

No standard MSAC weaponry was authorized for the operation.

This resulted in an unusually specialized five-person assault force.

It also resulted in an extremely unusual amount of screaming from the opposing side.

# 7. NORA'S TRAJECTORY INTERCEPTION

C.O.R.E. observers recorded Nora intercepting incoming high-velocity rounds using her Deadeye.

Her anomalous perception allows her to visually identify the trajectory produced by moving objects.

She does not simply react to where an object currently is.

She perceives where it is going.

The distinction was sufficient to allow her to repeatedly intercept incoming fire.

The effect on Woodland morale was immediate.

The mercenaries had initially believed they were fighting five personnel.

They began behaving as though they were fighting something considerably less comprehensible.

# C.O.R.E. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Five Aperture personnel entered a warehouse occupied by approximately **2,500 Woodland mercenaries**.

They were restricted to Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise.

They cleared the facility.

The most psychologically damaging element for the opposition was not the FSR Deadbolt.

It was not the Night Stalker.

It was not the Sleight of Hand.

It was not even the fact that only five people had entered.

It was Nora.

The Woodland mercenaries expected a six-year-old carrying a pistol to be the least threatening member of the team.

Instead, they watched her calmly intercept incoming high-velocity fire with that pistol.

The radio transcripts show the transition clearly:

> **“There's a child.”**

became:

> **“The child has a pistol.”**

which became:

> **“The child is intercepting our rounds.”**

and eventually:

> **“I am not shooting that kid.”**

The five-person team won.

Nora returned to being a loud six-year-old almost immediately afterward.

Neri returned to chemistry.

Snake returned to admiring FSR-001.

Elsher returned to her Night Stalker.

The Commander returned to MSAC duties.

And Neri wrote one thing in the Tachyon Industries development catalog:

> **ASSAULT RIFLE**

Underneath it:

> **“Need one.”**

The Tachyon Industries collection is apparently not finished.
