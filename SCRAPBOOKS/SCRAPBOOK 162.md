# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **ARCHIVAL APPEARANCE RECORD**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – PRE-INCIDENT & CURRENT APPEARANCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Medical & Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Archived family photographs recovered from the Lockwood family, along with school records from before Corin Lockwood's abduction, provide a clear record of her appearance prior to the experimentation that permanently altered her body.

The contrast between the two records is substantial.

---

# **ARCHIVAL RECORD**

Prior to the incident, Corin was described as a healthy, energetic child.

Archived photographs consistently show:

- Long, flowing black hair.
    
- Bright blue eyes.
    
- Healthy complexion.
    
- Frequent smiling.
    
- No visible surgical scarring.
    

Lumi identified the photographs immediately.

She quietly remarked,

> "She always wanted her hair to be long."

School records describe Corin as cheerful, curious, academically gifted, and frequently seen with her older siblings.

---

# **CURRENT APPEARANCE**

Following the experimentation and subsequent years of Ether-dependent physiology, Corin's appearance changed permanently.

Current observations include:

- Chalky pale skin.
    
- Chalk-lime hair, with no evidence of natural pigmentation returning.
    
- Extensive surgical scarring.
    
- Eyes permanently altered following the loss and reconstruction of her left eye.
    
- A generally exhausted appearance, consistent with chronic insomnia.
    

Medical Services have found no evidence that her hair or skin pigmentation will naturally return to their original state.

These alterations are considered permanent.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin has been shown several childhood photographs during therapy sessions.

She rarely looks at them for long.

During one session, she quietly said,

> "...She was pretty."

The attending psychologist asked,

> "...Who?"

Corin looked at the photograph.

Then answered,

> "...The girl in the picture."

There was a pause.

She did not say,

_"Me."_

---

# **LUMI**

One afternoon, Lumi was brushing Corin's pale hair.

Corin stared at her reflection.

> "...You used to brush my black hair."

Lumi smiled gently.

> "I remember."

Corin reached up and held a strand between her fingers.

> "...I miss it."

Lumi continued brushing slowly.

> "So do I."

Corin lowered her head.

> "...It's gone."

Lumi rested the brush on the table.

She leaned forward and kissed Corin's forehead.

> "Your black hair was beautiful."

She smiled warmly.

> "And the daughter sitting in front of me is beautiful too."

Corin's eyes became watery.

She wanted to disagree.

But for once...

She remained silent.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE**

The residents know Corin's appearance only as it is now.

To them, her chalk-lime hair and pale skin are simply part of who she is.

When Star once found an old family photograph, she looked back and forth between it and Corin.

She smiled brightly.

> "You looked different."

Corin nodded quietly.

Star then added,

> "Still Corin."

That simple statement lingered with Corin long after the conversation had ended.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Medical Services recognize that Corin continues to grieve the loss of her former appearance.

To her, her black hair and blue eyes symbolize a childhood that was taken away before she had the chance to finish living it.

The physical changes cannot currently be reversed.

The memories remain.

And although Corin still struggles to recognize herself in the mirror, those closest to her have never stopped recognizing the person beneath the scars.

**End of Archival Appearance Record**
