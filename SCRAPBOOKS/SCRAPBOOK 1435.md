# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake remains **bedridden and under strict medical confinement.**

His condition has deteriorated sufficiently that he is currently not permitted to resume normal SAFEHOUSE activity.

The Medical Bay is secured.

Jake is not permitted to leave.

Furthermore, **no resident or personnel may visit Jake without explicit Medical authorization.**

At present, only three individuals are authorized for direct visitation:

- **Twiggy**
    
- **Phoenix**
    
- **Bao**
    

No exceptions are currently approved.

This restriction exists because Jake's physical condition remains extremely fragile.

---

# 1. CURRENT CONDITION

Jake remains conscious.

He remains alive.

He remains extremely weak.

Current observations include:

- profound physical exhaustion;
    
- minimal spontaneous movement;
    
- persistent fever-related weakness;
    
- severely reduced stamina;
    
- damaged vocal cords;
    
- inability to speak;
    
- barely visible stellar glow;
    
- thick darkened eye bags;
    
- heavy, lowered posture;
    
- reduced appetite;
    
- prolonged periods of immobility;
    
- and significantly reduced responsiveness compared with his previous baseline.
    

Jake spends most of his time lying in bed.

He rarely attempts to move.

When he does, the movement is slow.

Very slow.

---

# 2. THE HALO INCIDENT

Jake's halo had previously remained suspended above his head.

During a routine medical observation, its stability suddenly failed.

The halo dimmed.

Then separated.

Several pieces detached from the ring.

They fell.

Not gently.

The pieces struck the floor with impacts that personnel initially mistook for dropped medical equipment.

**THUD.**

**THUD.**

**THUD.**

The floor visibly shook.

The pieces had the effective weight of extremely heavy objects despite their normally weightless appearance.

Jake watched them fall.

He did not react.

Phoenix immediately ordered everyone to remain clear of the area.

Twiggy examined the pieces from a safe distance.

Bao looked at Jake.

Jake simply stared at the floor.

His halo was no longer floating above him.

It was lying in pieces beside the bed.

---

# 3. HALO STATUS

The halo pieces remain physically present.

They are currently classified as **unstable stellar-anomalous material.**

Personnel are prohibited from:

- touching the pieces;
    
- attempting to lift them;
    
- attempting to reconstruct the halo;
    
- attempting to return them to Jake;
    
- moving them without Medical authorization;
    
- or treating them as ordinary debris.
    

The apparent mass of the pieces is considerably greater than their previous observed behavior suggested.

No explanation has yet been established.

---

# 4. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake did not attempt to retrieve the halo.

He did not appear frightened.

He did not appear surprised.

He simply looked toward the pieces.

Then looked away.

Phoenix asked whether he wanted them moved.

Jake slowly shook his head.

That was the entirety of his response.

The pieces therefore remain where they fell until they can be safely secured.

---

# 5. VISITATION RESTRICTION

Effective immediately:

> **NO GENERAL VISITATION.**

Residents are not permitted to enter Jake's Medical Bay room.

This includes friends, family, SAFEHOUSE residents, C.O.R.E. personnel, and other anomalous residents.

The restriction is not based on interpersonal concerns.

It is based entirely upon Jake's current medical condition.

His body cannot presently tolerate unnecessary stimulation, movement, prolonged conversation, or repeated visitors.

Communication may consist of:

- nodding;
    
- shaking his head;
    
- pointing;

# 11. TWIGGY'S OBSERVATION

During one examination, Twiggy remained beside the bed for several minutes.

Jake was awake.

He was staring at the ceiling.

Twiggy checked his vitals.

Then the teddy bear.

Then the halo fragments.

She looked back at Jake.

He remained completely still.

Twiggy quietly documented:

> **"Patient appears profoundly exhausted."**

After a pause, she added:

> **"Recommend continued rest."**

No further comment was necessary.

---

# 12. PHOENIX'S DIRECTIVE

Phoenix has issued the following instruction to all personnel:

> **"He's not available."**

When asked when Jake would be available again, Phoenix answered:

> **"When he's well enough to be."**

When asked whether that would be soon:

> **"I don't know."**

When asked whether residents could at least see him through the doorway:

> **"No."**

The answer remains unchanged.

---

# 13. WHY THE RESTRICTION EXISTS

Jake has experienced repeated periods in which he was expected to continue functioning despite severe physical deterioration.

That will not happen again.

He is currently not required to:

- work;
    
- explore;
    
- engineer;
    
- socialize;
    
- entertain visitors;
    
- help anyone;
    
- prove his recovery;
    
- or demonstrate that he is improving.
    

His only current requirement is:

> **Recover.**
