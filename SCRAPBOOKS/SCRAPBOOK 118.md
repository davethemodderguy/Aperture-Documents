# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **RECOVERY OBSERVATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – ONGOING HEALING PROCESS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin Lockwood's condition has shown measurable improvement.

However, C.O.R.E. emphasizes one important distinction:

> **Corin is healing. She is not healed.**

The difference remains significant.

Her trauma has not disappeared.

The memories have not vanished.

The years she lost cannot be returned.

The goal is not to erase what happened to Corin.

The goal is to help her live with it.

---

# **CURRENT RECOVERY**

Following her return to Heaven's Blacksite, Corin has gradually become more stable.

She now:

- Leaves her office more frequently.
    
- Accepts support from others.
    
- Maintains her medication schedule more consistently.
    
- Participates in small conversations.
    
- Allows herself to rest without feeling like she must always be productive.
    

However, difficult moments remain.

Some days are easier.

Some days are not.

Recovery is not a straight path.

---

# **LUMI'S ROLE**

While the Lockwood siblings and SAFEHOUSE residents care deeply for Corin, many of them have responsibilities that limit how often they can be present.

Arcturus continues his duties as KCA's Security Advisor.

Norma continues managing engineering operations and ammunition authorization.

The other residents of the Blacksite continue their own work.

They are not abandoning Corin.

They are simply unable to stay beside her every hour.

Lumi fills that gap.

---

# **A CONSTANT PRESENCE**

Unlike everyone else, Lumi has nowhere else she needs to be.

She does not have a department to manage.

She does not have a mission schedule.

She does not have meetings.

She simply stays.

Some days, Corin works silently at her desk while Lumi sits nearby.

Some days, they drink tea together.

Some days, they do not speak at all.

And that is enough.

---

# **LUMI'S APPROACH**

After learning the full extent of Corin's experiences, Lumi changed how she supported her daughter.

She no longer tries to force memories back.

She no longer asks Corin to become the person she used to be.

Instead, she focuses on small things.

> "Have you eaten?"

> "Did you take your medicine?"

> "You worked enough today."

> "Come sit down for a while."

Simple reminders.

The kind she would have given when Corin was young.

---

# **CORIN'S OBSERVATION**

During a routine check-in, Corin was asked how she felt about Lumi staying with her.

Her answer was quiet.

> "...She makes it easier."

When asked to explain, Corin continued:

> "Everyone cares."

> "I know they do."

A pause.

> "But everyone has something they need to do."

She looked toward Lumi.

> "Mom doesn't leave."

---

# **LUMI'S RESPONSE**

When informed of Corin's statement, Lumi did not consider it a burden.

She answered:

> "I missed fifteen years."

> "I'm not going to complain about spending time with my daughter now."

---

# **FAMILY BALANCE**

Arcturus, Norma, and the SAFEHOUSE residents remain important parts of Corin's recovery.

Belle remains one of Corin's most effective grounding methods.

Norma continues trying to reconnect with her sister.

Arcturus continues quietly protecting his family.

But Lumi provides something different.

Not a solution.

Not a cure.

A constant.

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the report and added:

> "Healing does not happen because one person fixes everything."

> "It happens because someone stays long enough for another person to believe they are worth staying for."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin Lockwood remains under active recovery support.

She is not the person she was fifteen years ago.

She is not only the person she became afterward.

She is someone still rebuilding herself.

And throughout that process, Lumi remains beside her.

Not pulling her toward the past.

Not forcing her toward the future.

Simply reminding her:

She is here.

She is alive.

And she is loved.

**End of Recovery Observation Report**
