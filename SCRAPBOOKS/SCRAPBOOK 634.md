# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / FEATHER RECOVERY LOG**

# **THE FEATHERS THAT KEEP APPEARING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **MISSING**  
**Phenomenon:** **RECURRING FEATHER DISCOVERY**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Since Corin's disappearance, C.O.R.E. personnel have recovered **Corin's feathers nearby almost every day**.

They are appearing in areas where no confirmed sighting of Corin has occurred.

The feathers are unmistakably hers.

They match her known biological and anomalous characteristics.

No personnel have witnessed them being shed.

No surveillance camera has captured Corin placing them.

Yet they continue to appear.

---

# **RECOVERY LOCATIONS**

Feathers have been discovered in increasingly unusual locations.

Confirmed recovery sites include:

- Beneath the Treehouse.
    
- Around the Treehouse's exterior.
    
- Along paths leading toward the Blacksite.
    
- Near Orphie's usual walking routes.
    
- Inside abandoned rooms.
    
- On windowsills.
    
- On tree branches.
    
- Near search teams.
    
- Beside previously investigated locations.
    

Several were discovered only minutes after a search team had cleared an area.

---

# **FREQUENCY**

Average recovery rate:

> **Approximately one or more feathers per day.**

There have been very few days without a recovery.

The phenomenon has continued despite:

- Increased surveillance.
    
- Expanded search teams.
    
- Environmental scans.
    
- Dimensional scans.
    
- Controlled perimeter sweeps.
    

---

# **FEATHER CONDITION**

Most recovered feathers are dormant.

Several retain the characteristic properties associated with Corin.

However, their condition varies.

Some appear completely normal.

Others are:

- Slightly damaged.
    
- Bent.
    
- Covered in dirt.
    
- Damp.
    
- Extremely cold.
    
- Warm despite ambient conditions.
    
- Contaminated with unknown substances.
    

A small number were found with traces of the same **blackened, toxic material** previously identified as Corin's environmentally reacted blood.

---

# **THE BURNING FEATHERS**

The previously documented mass weakening of Corin's burning feathers remains ongoing.

Recovered feathers that are subsequently activated produce the same unusually weak azure flame.

The flame remains:

- Heatless.
    
- Oxygen-independent.
    
- Persistent.
    
- Light-azure.
    

But significantly dimmer than normal.

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

Orphie has begun recognizing the feathers immediately.

During one recovery, a feather was found beside the Treehouse door.

Orphie picked it up.

She stared at it for several seconds.

Then quietly said:

> **"...You're here."**

She looked around.

Nothing answered.

---

# **THE PATTERN**

A concerning pattern has been identified.

Many feathers appear shortly after Orphie has visited a particular location.

For example:

**Orphie visits an area.**

↓

**No unusual activity detected.**

↓

**Orphie leaves.**

↓

**A Corin feather is discovered nearby.**

This has occurred frequently enough that C.O.R.E. can no longer confidently classify the correlation as coincidence.

However:

> **No causal relationship has been established.**

---

# **SURVEILLANCE INCIDENT**

During one particularly unusual incident, a camera observed an empty hallway for approximately four hours.

No person entered.

No entity appeared.

No dimensional activity occurred.

At the end of the recording, a feather was simply present in the middle of the hallway.

It was not visible in the previous frame.

There was no transition showing it falling.

It was simply:

**there.**

---

# **ORPHIE'S OBSERVER SENSATION**

The feather phenomenon may be related to Orphie's persistent feeling of being watched.

Orphie has reported the sensation immediately before discovering several feathers.

One report:

> **"Someone's here."**

Approximately thirty seconds later:

> **Feather discovered.**

No observer was detected.

---

# **NOTEBOOK CORRELATION**

The recovered notebook contains multiple drawings of eyes.

Some pages contain statements about watching and being watched.

One entry reads:

> **"If I can't be seen, maybe I'll watch instead."**

Another:

> **"You'll know I'm here."**

Neither entry contains a confirmed location.

Both are now considered relevant to the ongoing investigation.

---

**Evidence:**  
Recurring physical evidence.

**Problem:**  
No direct sightings.

---

**Problem:**  
No dimensional signature detected.

---

The feathers may be Corin's way of communicating:

**Evidence:**  
Repeated appearances near Orphie.

**Problem:**  
No confirmed message structure.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin is missing.

Nobody knows where she went.

Nobody has seen her.

Yet almost every day...

A feather appears.

Sometimes beneath the Treehouse.

Sometimes along Orphie's path.

Sometimes somewhere that was searched only minutes before.

No footsteps.

No witnesses.

No explanation.

Just another white feather.

And every time one is found, Orphie looks around.

Because that strange feeling is always there.

Something is watching her.

Something she cannot see.

And perhaps the feathers are the only proof that it is still there.
