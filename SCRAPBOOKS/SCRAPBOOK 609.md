### **MEDICAL RECOVERY REPORT**

# **CORIN — WING REGENERATION COMPLETE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE / Treehouse  
**Status:** **Healed / Mobility Restricted**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's left wing has successfully regenerated.

The physical damage caused by the pressure cooker explosion has been repaired.

However, the wing has not yet returned to its previous flexibility.

The entire structure is currently **stiff and noticeably less mobile** than Corin's right wing.

---

# **CURRENT CONDITION**

The wing is fully intact.

New feathers have grown across the regenerated structure.

The luminous properties have also returned.

However, Corin cannot freely manipulate the wing yet.

When she attempts to spread it, the movement is slow and restricted.

Folding it completely against her back is difficult.

Rapid wingbeats are currently impossible.

---

# **CORIN'S FIRST ATTEMPT**

Corin carefully extended the regenerated wing.

It moved.

Barely.

She stopped.

Then tried again.

The wing slowly opened.

Corin stared at it.

Her expression was a mixture of relief and frustration.

> **"...You're working."**

She gently flexed it.

The wing responded.

It was stiff.

But it responded.

---

# **FLIGHT STATUS**

Corin is **not yet cleared for unrestricted flight**.

Although the wing has structurally healed, the stiffness makes sustained flight unsafe.

Her right wing remains fully functional.

The asymmetry significantly affects her balance and lift generation.

Medical personnel expect mobility to improve gradually as the regenerated tissues mature.

---

# **REHABILITATION**

Corin has been instructed to perform gentle wing exercises.

These include:

- Slow extension.
    
- Controlled folding.
    
- Gentle feather movement.
    
- Small wingbeats.
    
- Gradual stretching.
    
- Short periods of controlled movement.
    

No forced stretching is permitted.

The wing must regain flexibility naturally.

---

# **EMOTIONAL RESPONSE**

Corin is relieved that the wing has healed.

But she is not satisfied.

She misses how it used to move.

Before the injury, her wings were highly flexible and capable of precise aerial control.

Now the left wing feels unfamiliar.

Corin described it simply:

> **"It feels like it's not awake yet."**

---

# **BELLE'S REACTION**

Belle was visibly relieved when she saw the healed wing.

She carefully asked:

> **"Does it hurt?"**

Corin shook her head.

> **"Not like before."**

She moved the wing again.

> **"...Just stiff."**

Belle smiled.

> **"Then we'll get it moving again."**

Corin smiled back.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The wing has returned.

The feathers have returned.

The glow has returned.

The pain that once kept Corin awake has largely disappeared.

But healing is not the same as being **fully recovered**.

Her left wing remains stiff, awkward, and unfamiliar.

Corin continues gently moving it.

A little farther each day.

Eventually, it will remember how to fly.

Until then, she keeps practicing.
