# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PROGRESS OBSERVATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – INCREASED TOLERANCE OF CONTACT WITH LUMI**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Executive & Medical Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Over the following months, Search and Welfare Operations documented a gradual change in Corin Lockwood's behavior during encounters with Lumia ("Lumi") Lockwood.

The improvement was subtle.

It was not linear.

Some days Corin still disappeared the moment she noticed another person.

On other days...

She stayed.

---

# **OBSERVATIONS**

Lumi continued following Psychological Services' recommendations.

She never approached quickly.

She never reached toward Corin without warning.

She never blocked a path.

Instead, she allowed Corin complete control over the distance between them.

Eventually, Corin began stopping instead of immediately running.

Sometimes she watched Lumi from dozens of meters away.

Sometimes only a few.

The distance varied.

The fact that she remained at all was new.

---

# **THE FIRST CONVERSATION**

One snowy afternoon, Lumi noticed Corin sitting beneath a familiar pine tree.

Rather than approaching, she sat on a fallen log several meters away.

For nearly fifteen minutes, neither of them spoke.

Finally...

Corin broke the silence.

> "...You're getting better at this."

Lumi smiled softly.

> "At finding you?"

Corin gave the faintest nod.

> "...At not scaring me."

Neither spoke again for another several minutes.

But Corin stayed.

---

# **SMALL CHANGES**

Over time, several changes became apparent.

Corin no longer fled every encounter with Lumi.

She sometimes accepted a cup of tea left beside her.

On colder days, she occasionally allowed Lumi to sit nearby while they watched the snowfall together.

Conversation remained brief.

Silence remained common.

Neither felt the need to fill it.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services describe the change as a meaningful sign of progress.

The improvement does not indicate that Corin's trauma has resolved.

Rather, it suggests she is beginning to distinguish between past danger and present safety in at least one relationship.

Trust, once broken, is rarely rebuilt through dramatic moments.

It is rebuilt through countless quiet ones.

---

# **LUMI**

During one encounter, Corin quietly asked,

> "...You're not giving up?"

Lumi looked at her daughter as though the question had an obvious answer.

> "No."

Corin looked away.

> "...Even after everything?"

Lumi nodded.

> "Especially after everything."

For the first time in many months...

Corin did not leave.

She simply pulled her blanket a little tighter against the cold and continued watching the snow fall beside her mother.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin continues to avoid most people within the Blacksite.

However, repeated observations indicate that encounters with Lumi now end in flight less frequently than before.

Medical Services consider this a modest but significant step toward rebuilding Corin's sense of safety.

Recovery remains ongoing.

Measured not by how far Corin can walk toward others...

But by the growing number of moments in which she no longer feels she has to run away.

**End of Progress Observation Report**
