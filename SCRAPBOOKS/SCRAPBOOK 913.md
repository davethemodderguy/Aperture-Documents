# SAFEHOUSE WELFARE OBSERVATION

**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE CAFETERIA  
**DATE:** [REDACTED]  
**OBSERVATION TYPE:** Behavioral / Welfare  
**STATUS:** RESOLVED — MONITORING ADVISED

---

## 1. Summary

Neri was noticeably sad today.

There was no argument.

No incident.

Nobody had intentionally ignored her.

There was simply a problem that Neri understood intellectually but could not make feel any less painful.

**Everyone was busy.**

---

## 2. A Quiet Morning

Neri spent much of the morning attempting to find someone to spend time with.

She approached several residents.

The responses were almost identical.

> "I'm busy."

Not cruelly.

Not dismissively.

Just honestly.

The Blacksite was experiencing an unusually heavy workload.

Engineering had projects waiting.

Archives were backed up.

Security had reports.

Administrative personnel had meetings.

The SAFEHOUSE residents were, for once, almost universally occupied.

Neri understood.

She always understood.

That was part of the problem.

She knew they weren't rejecting her.

She knew they weren't angry with her.

She knew they would spend time with her when they had the opportunity.

But knowing that didn't make the cafeteria feel any less empty.

---

# 3. Everyone Had Somewhere To Be

The people Neri normally relied upon were unavailable for perfectly legitimate reasons.

### Alice

Alice was sick.

Neri checked on her, but Alice was in no condition to spend the day walking around with her.

Neri left her alone to rest.

### Bao & Frost

The greenhouse required their attention.

There were plants to maintain, environmental systems to monitor, and enough work that neither could simply walk away from it.

### Star & Nightshade

Both were occupied with matters concerning **Starlands**.

Their responsibilities could not simply be postponed because Neri wanted company.

### Belle & Endmin

Both had returned to **Banlands** for a meeting with the **Krampus Compliance Authority.**

They were unavailable for the foreseeable part of the day.

### Everyone Else

Working.

---

# 4. The Cafeteria

Neri eventually went to the cafeteria.

At first, it was crowded.

People came in.

People ate.

People talked.

Neri sat quietly.

For a brief period, she was surrounded by people.

Then the workday continued.

One person left.

Then another.

Then another.

Eventually the cafeteria became quiet.

And then empty.

Neri remained.

---

# 5. No Cake

There was one particularly unusual detail.

Neri had **no sweets with her.**

No cake.

No cupcake.

No candy.

No pastry.

Nothing.

This was significant enough for the observing staff to note.

Neri's usual solitary cafeteria ritual involves cake or another sweet while she quietly remembers her younger sister.

Today there wasn't even that.

She simply sat there.

---

# 6. Observation

Neri eventually pulled her oversized coat around herself and lowered her head.

Then she hid her face.

Both hands disappeared into the enormous sleeves.

She did not cry loudly.

She did not call anyone.

She did not complain about being ignored.

She did not blame anyone for being busy.

She simply sat there with her face hidden.

For once, there wasn't a knife in sight.

There wasn't a cupcake either.

Just a ten-year-old sitting alone in an empty cafeteria.

---

# 7. Why Nobody Came Immediately

The important distinction is that **nobody had abandoned Neri.**

Everyone was simply occupied.

The SAFEHOUSE was functioning exactly as it normally did during a heavy workload.

The problem was that Neri's history does not make that distinction emotionally easy.

Her childhood taught her that people could disappear.

People could leave.

People could decide she wasn't worth the trouble.

She has spent much of her life expecting absence to eventually become permanent.

So when everyone she wanted to see was unavailable on the same day, her mind had very little experience telling her:

> _They're busy. They'll come back._

Instead, it simply felt like:

> _Everyone is gone._

She knows better now.

But knowing better and **feeling safe** are not always the same thing.

---

# 8. Medical Consideration

Staff were instructed not to crowd Neri simply because she appeared sad.

However, she was quietly monitored.

Her spontaneous anemia means prolonged isolation should never be ignored entirely, particularly when Neri is emotionally distressed.

No intervention was necessary at the time of observation.

She remained conscious.

She was breathing normally.

She had not injured herself.

She simply wanted to be left alone for a while.

And, importantly:

**Being alone for a while was not the same thing as being abandoned.**

---

# 9. Later Observation

Approximately [REDACTED] hours later, the cafeteria remained quiet.

Neri had barely moved.

Eventually, footsteps could be heard approaching from the corridor.

Neri did not immediately look up.

She remained curled into her coat.

The approaching resident stopped beside her.

There was no:

> "What's wrong?"

No interrogation.

No attempt to force her to talk.

Just someone sitting down nearby.

After several seconds, Neri quietly moved one sleeve away from her face.

She looked over.

And stayed.

That was enough.

---

# 10. Staff Note

Neri does not always need cake.

She does not always need someone to solve her problems.

She does not always need reassurance.

Sometimes she simply needs someone to **sit there.**

Her sadness today was not caused by anyone doing something wrong.

Everyone really was busy.

Alice really was sick.

Bao and Frost really did have the greenhouse.

Star and Nightshade really did have Starlands affairs.

Belle and Endmin really did have their KCA meeting.

And the Blacksite really was overflowing with work.

Neri knew all of that.

She just wanted a friend.

Today, there wasn't one available.

Eventually, there was.

And for Neri, that was enough.

---

### FINAL OBSERVATION

> **Neri:** _"...It's quiet."_

**Archivist:** "Yeah."

> **Neri:** _"...I don't like it."_

**Archivist:** "I know."

A pause.

> **Neri:** _"...Can you stay?"_

**Archivist:** "Yeah."

Neri pulled her sleeve back over her face.

She didn't say anything else.

She didn't need to.
