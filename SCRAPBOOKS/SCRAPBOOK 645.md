# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / YEAR ONE — PERSONNEL STATUS REVIEW**

# **THE TWO WHO DIDN'T MOVE ON**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **MISSING — ONE YEAR**  
**Review Subjects:** **Lumi / Orphie**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

One year after Corin's disappearance, most personnel have gradually returned to their normal lives.

The SAFEHOUSE operates again.

The Blacksite continues its work.

Residents laugh.

People eat together.

People go to work.

People sleep.

Life continued.

For most.

Two people have not managed to move forward in the same way.

**Lumi.**

**Orphie.**

---

# **THE OTHERS**

Nobody forgot Corin.

They still remember her.

Her photographs remain.

Her belongings remain.

Her feathers are preserved.

Her sketchbook is secured.

Her name still comes up in conversation.

But grief eventually became something they could carry.

They learned how to live around the empty space.

They began moving forward.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi never truly stopped looking.

She continues checking reports.

She asks search teams for updates.

She occasionally visits the Treehouse.

She still leaves food there.

Not because she expects Corin to return immediately.

Because she cannot bring herself to stop preparing for her.

One researcher once asked why.

Lumi answered:

> **"Because if she comes back tomorrow, I want her to have something to eat."**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie is different.

She continues to struggle with the knowledge that Corin spent eight months waiting for her.

She knows the circumstances.

She knows she did not intentionally abandon Corin.

She knows she was trapped.

But none of those facts change what Corin experienced.

Orphie returned.

Corin was already gone.

---

# **THE IMPOSSIBLE REQUEST**

There is one thing both Lumi and Orphie understand now.

Corin wanted to be abandoned.

Not because she truly wanted nobody to care about her.

But because she had convinced herself that abandonment was inevitable.

She wanted the pain to stop.

She wanted someone to finally prove her worst belief correct.

> **"You can just say you hate me."**

> **"You can just kick me out."**

> **"Just say you hate me."**

She believed abandonment would hurt less than waiting for it.

---

# **THEY COULDN'T GIVE HER THAT**

Lumi couldn't abandon her.

Orphie couldn't abandon her.

Even after everything.

Even after Corin disappeared.

Even after the flames went out.

Neither could bring themselves to say:

> **"We don't want you."**

Because it wasn't true.

---

# **LUMI'S STATEMENT**

During a private conversation, Lumi reportedly said:

> **"She wanted us to leave her alone."**

A pause.

> **"I couldn't."**

Another pause.

> **"I still can't."**

---

# **ORPHIE'S STATEMENT**

Orphie was asked whether she would continue searching.

She answered immediately:

> **"Yes."**

When asked how long:

> **"Until she comes home."**

When reminded that one year had already passed:

> **"Then I'll search for another year."**

---

# **THE CONTRADICTION**

Corin's deepest fear was abandonment.

Her deepest desire, according to her notebook, was to know that someone would stay.

These two things became intertwined.

She began believing that she was too difficult to love.

Too fragile.

Too dependent.

Too heavy.

And eventually:

> **"Better off alone."**

Yet the two people who understood her best refused to validate that belief.

They stayed.

Even when she wasn't there to see it.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

Lumi and Orphie continue visiting.

Sometimes together.

Sometimes separately.

The Treehouse remains stocked.

The old waiting location remains undisturbed.

The burnt-out Feather Lantern remains where it was found.

The single feather remains preserved.

Neither has allowed the location to be dismantled.

---

# **THE QUESTION**

A C.O.R.E. analyst eventually asked Lumi:

> **"What if Corin doesn't want to come back?"**

Lumi remained silent.

Then:

> **"Then I'll still leave the door open."**

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

Orphie gave a similar answer.

> **"She doesn't have to forgive me."**

> **"She doesn't have to talk to me."**

> **"She doesn't even have to stay with me."**

Then:

> **"She just has to know she can come home."**

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin spent eight months waiting for someone to leave.

She convinced herself that abandonment was what she deserved.

Eventually, she disappeared.

One year later, almost everyone has learned to live with her absence.

Everyone except two people.

Lumi still leaves food.

Orphie still searches.

Neither can give Corin what she once believed she wanted.

Because they know the truth.

Corin didn't really want to be abandoned.

She wanted the pain of waiting to end.

And if she ever returns...

Lumi and Orphie intend to give her the one thing she stopped believing existed.

Not an apology.

Not an explanation.

Not a promise that everything will be fixed.

Just a place where she can finally stop waiting.

> **The door is still open.**

**End of Year One Personnel Status Review**
