## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### ANOMALOUS RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-ANM-4C-452  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESTRICTED — ANOMALOUS / PSYCHOLOGICAL

**SUBJECT:** Jake  
**EVENT:** Temporary disappearance and subsequent restoration of manifested teddy bear

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following the fracture of Jake's halo and the collapse of his belief that his mother loved him, another significant change was observed.

Jake's teddy bear disappeared.

The teddy bear is not a separate physical object.

It is considered part of Jake's manifested form.

Under normal circumstances, it appears either:

- secured against Jake's back; or
    
- held tightly in his arms.
    

The bear was given to Jake by his mother during his childhood.

For Jake, it had historically functioned as a comfort object.

It was something he could hold when he was frightened.

Something he could sleep beside.

Something he associated with being cared for.

When Jake's belief in his mother's love collapsed, the teddy bear disappeared with it.

It remained absent for a short period.

Then, unexpectedly, it returned.

Jake immediately recognized it.

He took it into his arms.

He has not let go of it since.

---

# 1. THE TEDDY BEAR

The teddy bear has been present in Jake's manifested form since his earliest recorded appearances.

It is small enough for Jake to carry comfortably.

Its exact material composition cannot be determined because it does not behave like an independent physical object.

It appears to be part of Jake's anomalous manifestation.

When Jake moves, the bear moves with him.

When Jake becomes intangible, the bear becomes intangible.

When Jake disappears, the bear disappears.

It has never previously been observed separated from him.

---

# 2. ANOMALOUS CHARACTERISTIC

The teddy bear possesses one unusual characteristic.

It has a heartbeat.

Not a mechanical sound.

Not an artificial recording.

A genuine, rhythmic:

> **thump.**

The heartbeat is faint.

It can usually only be detected when someone is close to Jake.

Jake appears to be aware of it.

When frightened or distressed, he has historically held the bear against his chest.

The heartbeat appears to calm him.

No physiological source for the heartbeat has been identified.

C.O.R.E. has not attempted invasive examination.

The bear is part of Jake.

That distinction is considered sufficient.

---

# 3. BEFORE THE INCIDENT

Prior to Jake's emotional collapse, the bear appeared regularly.

Jake would carry it on his back while walking.

When tired, he would take it into his arms.

When sitting, he would often rest his chin against its head.

At night, he would hold it while waiting for his manifestation window to end.

During particularly difficult periods, he would simply sit with it.

The bear's heartbeat was sometimes audible in recordings.

Jake appeared to find the sound reassuring.

---

# 4. DISAPPEARANCE

The teddy bear disappeared approximately twelve minutes after Jake overheard the Archivists.

There was no visible transition.

One moment it was present.

The next:

Nothing.

Jake's back was empty.

His arms were empty.

He noticed immediately.

He looked behind himself.

Then down at his hands.

Then back toward the floor.

He did not speak.

He had already stopped speaking following the incident.

Nora noticed him searching.

> **Nora:**  
> “Jake?”

Jake did not respond.

He continued looking.

---

# 5. SEARCH

Nora checked the surrounding area.

Nothing was found.

Aeterna examined the manifestation field.

No separate anomalous object was detected.

C.O.R.E. searched the immediate environment.

Nothing.

There was no indication that the bear had been removed.

There was no indication that it had been destroyed.

It simply was not there.

---

# 6. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake's reaction was unusually severe.

He sat down.

His head lowered.

His hands remained empty.

The two fragments of his halo continued floating above him.

Jake stared at the floor.

Nora sat beside him.

> **Nora:**  
> “We'll find it.”

Jake did not look at her.

> **Nora:**  
> “I promise.”

Jake remained silent.

After several minutes, he slowly curled his arms against his chest.

As if he were still holding something.

But there was nothing there.

---

# 7. INTERPRETATION

The timing initially suggested that the teddy bear's disappearance might be connected to Jake's emotional collapse.

This remains unconfirmed.

However, the bear's symbolic significance is clear.

The teddy bear was one of the few remaining objects Jake associated with his mother.

For decades, Jake had believed:

> **Mom gave me this.**

Therefore:

> **Mom cared about me.**

When Jake's belief collapsed, the emotional connection between himself and the object may have become unstable.

Unlike ordinary objects, the teddy bear exists as part of Jake's anomalous form.

It may therefore respond to changes in his internal state.

---

# 8. TEMPORARY ABSENCE

The bear remained absent for a relatively short period.

C.O.R.E. estimates the duration at approximately **one hour and nineteen minutes**.

During this period, Jake became increasingly withdrawn.

He did not speak.

He did not approach the cafeteria.

He did not react to the smell of cake.

He did not respond when Alice attempted to show him her scooter.

He simply sat with his arms folded against his chest.

At approximately 17:04, Jake's manifestation changed.

---

# 9. RESTORATION

The first indication was auditory.

A faint sound was detected.

> **thump.**

Nora looked toward Jake.

> **thump.**

The sound became slightly louder.

Jake looked down.

The teddy bear was there.

It had reappeared against his chest.

Jake froze.

For several seconds, he did nothing.

Then he grabbed it.

He held it tightly.

The bear's small heart continued beating.

> **thump.**

Jake pressed his face against it.

He remained there.

---

# 10. NORA

Nora sat beside him.

She looked at the bear.

Then at Jake.

> **Nora:**  
> “You found it.”

Jake did not answer.

He tightened his arms around the bear.

Nora understood.

She did not ask anything else.

---

# 11. AETERNA

Aeterna examined the bear from a short distance.

The heartbeat was clearly audible.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “It has returned.”

Nora nodded.

> **Nora:**  
> “Why?”

Aeterna looked at Jake.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “I do not know.”

She paused.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “Perhaps it was never truly gone.”

Jake continued holding the bear.

Aeterna did not attempt to take it.

---

# 12. NERI

Neri sat nearby.

She watched Jake holding the bear.

She understood something the others did not immediately recognize.

The bear had not returned because Jake suddenly loved his mother again.

It had returned because it was **Jake's.**

Whatever emotional meaning his mother had once given it, the object had become part of Jake himself.

Neri wrote a short message and placed it beside him.

> **“You can keep it.”**

Jake looked at the paper.

Then at Neri.

He nodded once.

---

# 13. HEARTBEAT OBSERVATION

The teddy bear's heartbeat has continued uninterrupted since restoration.

Current measured rhythm is consistent.

No external power source exists.

The sound appears to originate from within the manifested bear.

When Jake holds it against his chest, the two rhythms occasionally synchronize.

This has been observed repeatedly.

C.O.R.E. has designated the heartbeat:

> **NON-THREATENING ANOMALOUS PHENOMENON**

No further testing is authorized without Jake's consent.

---

# 14. CURRENT BEHAVIOR

Jake has returned to carrying the bear.

During movement:

The bear rests against his back.

During rest:

He hugs it.

When sitting:

He keeps one arm around it.

When sleeping:

He holds it against his chest.

When distressed:

He holds it tighter.

The behavior resembles his previous behavior before the halo fracture.

However, his emotional state has not returned to baseline.

Jake remains profoundly sad.

He rarely interacts.

He does not speak.

He still does not show his previous curiosity.

He continues to miss his mother.

The return of the teddy bear did not restore the belief that she loved him.

---

# 15. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

The bear is no longer being treated as:

> **“Proof that Jake's mother loved him.”**

It is being treated as:

> **“Something Jake was given, and something that became part of Jake.”**

The distinction matters.

Jake's relationship with the bear can continue without requiring him to recover his previous belief.

The bear belongs to Jake now.

Whatever his mother intended when she gave it to him cannot change that.

---

# C.O.R.E. WELFARE ASSESSMENT

Jake remains in a significantly worsened emotional state.

His halo remains fractured.

His speech remains absent.

His baseline sadness has intensified.

He continues to experience persistent hunger.

He remains unable to return to the Starlands.

He continues to exist as a Weeping Angel.

None of these conditions were reversed by the teddy bear's return.

But something small returned with it.

A heartbeat.

A familiar weight against his chest.

Something Jake remembers holding long before he became a ghost.

---

# FINAL OBSERVATION

At 21:36, Jake was sitting beside Nora.

The cafeteria was quiet.

His teddy bear was against his chest.

Its heartbeat was audible.

> **thump.**

Jake's two broken halo fragments floated above him.

He did not speak.

He did not smile.

He did not appear happy.

He simply held the bear.

> **thump.**

Nora looked at him.

She did not ask whether he was okay.

She already knew.

Jake lowered his head against the bear.

For a moment, his eyes closed.

The bear's heart continued beating.

> **thump.**

It had disappeared.

Only for a little while.

Then it came back.

And Jake held onto it.

Not because it could restore the mother he had lost.

Not because it could make the truth disappear.

But because it was still his.

And for now, that was enough to keep his arms from being completely empty.
