## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / ANOMALOUS IDENTITY OBSERVATION**

# **COREY — LOSS OF INTEREST / FORCED MANIFESTATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Concern  
**Subject:** Corey  
**Associated Forms:** Luna / Solaria  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse / Blacksite  
**Status:** **ACTIVE CONCERN**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corey's condition has deteriorated further.

One of the clearest changes is her complete loss of interest in **drawing**.

Drawing was previously one of Corey's primary sources of happiness.

Her sketchbook was almost always nearby.

She drew when she was:

- Happy.
    
- Bored.
    
- Relaxed.
    
- Inspired.
    
- Unable to sleep.
    
- Thinking about her friends.
    
- Remembering her mother.
    
- Spending time with Marts.
    

That has now stopped.

---

# **THE SKETCHBOOK**

Corey's sketchbook remains where she left it.

The earlier pages still contain her old drawings:

- Flowers.
    
- Landscapes.
    
- Friends.
    
- Marts.
    
- SAFEHOUSE.
    
- Food.
    
- Stars.
    
- Hopeful scenes.
    

The later pages contain the disturbing drawings produced during her nightmares.

But there are now no new drawings.

The pencil remains broken.

The replacement pencils remain unused.

Corey has stopped reaching for them.

---

# **LOSS OF INTEREST**

When asked whether she intended to draw again, Corey responded:

> "...Maybe."

She did not sound interested.

When asked what she wanted to draw:

> "...Nothing."

This represents a significant departure from her previous behavior.

C.O.R.E. considers the loss of interest in an activity that previously brought Corey happiness a serious welfare indicator.

---

# **FORCED MANIFESTATION**

Corey has also begun deliberately forcing herself to remain as **Luna** or **Solaria** whenever she visits certain locations or interacts with others.

This is not accidental manifestation.

It is intentional.

Corey now actively chooses:

> **"Not Corey."**

---

# **MANIFESTATION CONTROL**

An important clarification has been added to Corey's anomalous profile.

**Luna and Solaria cannot independently disappear.**

Neither form can decide to return Corey to her normal state.

The transformation is entirely controlled by Corey.

Therefore:

### Corey → Luna

Corey initiates the transformation.

### Luna → Corey

Only Corey can initiate the reversal.

### Corey → Solaria

Corey initiates the transformation.

### Solaria → Corey

Only Corey can initiate the reversal.

---

# **CONSEQUENCE**

Once Corey forces herself into Luna or Solaria, the manifested form is effectively required to remain present until Corey decides otherwise.

Luna and Solaria cannot simply say:

> "I'm going back."

and transform themselves.

They have no independent control over the transition.

---

# **LUNA'S EXPERIENCE**

Luna has expressed concern about this arrangement.

She explained:

> "...Sometimes she doesn't want to come back."

When asked whether Luna could return Corey herself:

> "...No."

> "...Only Corey can do that."

---

# **SOLARIA'S EXPERIENCE**

Solaria gave a similar statement.

> "...She keeps us out because she doesn't want to be herself."

The researcher asked whether Solaria could force Corey back.

Solaria immediately answered:

> "...No."

Then:

> "...I wouldn't anyway."

---

# **THE VISITS**

Corey's behavior has become particularly noticeable when visiting the SAFEHOUSE.

Before entering, Corey may intentionally transform.

She then arrives as:

> **Luna.**

Or:

> **Solaria.**

The residents recognize her immediately.

The Marts approach.

People talk to her.

She participates in conversations.

She may even smile.

But everyone knows:

> **Corey is still inside.**

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts are noticeably happier when Luna or Solaria is present.

They can safely approach.

They float around Luna.

They follow Solaria.

They sit on their shoulders.

They sometimes form piles around them.

This has created an uncomfortable contrast.

Corey can see the Marts happily interacting with her.

But she cannot safely interact with them as herself.

---

# **LUNA'S OBSERVATION**

Luna told Corin:

> "...She misses them."

Corin:

> "...I know."

Luna:

> "...That's why she keeps bringing us."

Corin:

> "...So the Marts can be near her."

Luna nodded.

> "...Even if they don't know it's her."

---

# **SOLARIA'S OBSERVATION**

Solaria expressed the same concern differently.

> "...She's still trying to be around everyone."

> "...She's just doing it from behind us."

---

# **IDENTITY CONFLICT**

Corey's behavior increasingly suggests that she sees Luna and Solaria as safer versions of herself.

As Luna:

She can be calm.

As Solaria:

She can be confident.

As Corey:

She has to remember.

The distinction has become increasingly painful.

---

# **A RECENT CONVERSATION**

Corin asked Luna:

> "...What happens if Corey never comes back?"

Luna was silent.

She eventually answered:

> "...Then we stay."

Corin:

> "...Forever?"

Luna:

> "...If that's what she chooses."

She looked toward the floor.

> "...But I don't think that's what she really wants."

---

# **THE SKETCHBOOK**

The sketchbook remains untouched.

Luna occasionally looks at it.

Solaria occasionally does too.

Neither opens it without permission.

They know what it meant to Corey.

And they know that Corey currently cannot bring herself to use it.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Corey:**  
Severe withdrawal. Loss of interest in drawing. Increasing reliance on alternate manifestations. Continued sleep disruption.

**Luna:**  
Concerned. Unable to independently terminate manifestation.

**Solaria:**  
Concerned. Unable to independently terminate manifestation.

**Marts:**  
Comfortable around Luna/Solaria. Continue avoiding Corey directly.

**Corin:**  
Increasingly concerned and attempting to provide support despite work-related absence.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corey once drew because it made her happy.

Now the sketchbook sits untouched.

The pencil is still broken.

The pages remain exactly where she left them.

Instead of picking up a pencil, Corey changes form.

Instead of confronting herself, she becomes Luna.

Or Solaria.

And once she does, neither of them can bring her back.

They can only wait.

They can speak for her.

They can comfort the people around her.

They can protect her.

But they cannot make the choice for her.

Because the most important transformation is one that only Corey can perform:

> **Coming back to herself.**
