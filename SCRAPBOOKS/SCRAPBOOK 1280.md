# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

News has reached MSAC personnel that **Tachyon Industries is preparing another Limited Merchandise release.**

No official announcement has been made.

The product has not been publicly identified.

Tachyon Industries has not released specifications, production numbers, pricing, or a release date.

This has not stopped MSAC personnel from immediately beginning to speculate.

---

# INITIAL REPORT

The information reportedly originated from personnel associated with the Tachyon Merch Store.

The first message received by MSAC was extremely short:

> **“Tachyon is making another limited.”**

Within approximately twelve minutes, the message had spread throughout multiple MSAC communication channels.

Within twenty minutes, personnel had begun attempting to determine what the product was.

Within thirty minutes, someone had already guessed:

> **“Another Night Stalker?”**

This was incorrect.

---

# THE RUMOR

The most persistent information currently circulating is that the upcoming product is:

> **A lever-action rifle.**

The information has not been formally confirmed by Tachyon Industries.

However, several independent observations have increased confidence in the rumor.

Nora Tachyon has recently been observed with a Marlin Model 1894.

She has repeatedly demonstrated an unusual preference for lever-action platforms.

She has also reportedly approached Neri with a proposal for a Tachyon-branded lever-action design.

Neri is believed to be considering the concept.

# WHY MSAC IS INTERESTED

Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise has developed an unusual reputation among MSAC personnel.

The products are:

- extremely limited;
    
- highly specialized;
    
- visually distinctive;
    
- difficult to obtain;
    
- and often associated with unusual performance characteristics.
    

Existing Tachyon merchandise includes several unique platforms.

A new lever-action would therefore represent a significant departure from the more modern designs currently associated with the company.

Several MSAC operators have reportedly expressed interest purely because:

> **“It's a Tachyon lever gun.”**

---

# THE NORA FACTOR

The rumor has been further amplified by Nora's recent range activity.

Nora has already demonstrated:

- strong familiarity with lever-action handling;
    
- unusually high tactical awareness;
    
- a preference for the platform;
    
- and an apparent belief that lever-actions should be made significantly more tacticool than necessary.
    

One MSAC operator summarized the situation:

> **“If Nora designed it, it's going to have a lever and way too much tactical equipment.”**

Another replied:

> **“So basically, yes.”**
