# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The immediate incident has ended.

The effects have not.

Jake's physical manifestation stabilized several hours after the MSAC bullying incident.

His emotional condition did not.

C.O.R.E. has observed a significant change in Jake's behavior following the event.

He is quieter.

He hides more frequently.

He is less willing to approach unfamiliar personnel.

And, most significantly:

**He has begun expecting cruelty.**

---

# 1. THE FOLLOWING MORNING

Jake manifested in the cafeteria shortly after 08:00.

This was notable.

Before the incident, Jake had occasionally wandered through the cafeteria during daylight hours simply because he could now manifest during the day.

He would sometimes sit at a table.

Sometimes he would pick up food.

Sometimes he would simply wander through.

This morning was different.

He entered.

Stopped.

Looked around.

Saw several MSAC personnel.

And immediately turned around.

He left without taking anything.

---

# 2. THE TEDDY BEAR

The teddy bear remained wrapped around Jake's shoulders.

Its heartbeat was slower than normal.

Not dangerously slow.

Simply subdued.

Personnel observing from a distance noted that Jake repeatedly touched the bear's chest.

He appeared to be checking whether it was still beating.

When Nora later asked:

> **"Is your heart okay?"**

Jake nodded.

After a pause:

> **"It hurts a little."**

Nora did not ask him to explain.

She simply stayed beside him.

---

# 3. FOOD

At approximately 09:20, Nora brought Jake breakfast.

Cake was included.

Jake looked at it.

He did not immediately take it.

Nora pushed the plate slightly closer.

> **NORA:**  
> "You like this."

Jake stared at the cake.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I know."

He eventually picked it up.

He ate.

After finishing, he quietly said:

> **"I'm full."**

This remains an important distinction.

Jake's appetite has returned.

His ability to experience hunger has not.

His reluctance to eat following the incident was therefore emotional rather than physiological.

---

# 4. HIDING

Jake has resumed hiding.

His preferred locations currently include:

- beneath tables;
    
- behind furniture;
    
- unused corridors;
    
- quiet storage areas;
    
- library corners;
    
- and spaces with limited foot traffic.
    

Unlike the previous storage-locker incident, Jake is not currently locking himself into inaccessible spaces.

C.O.R.E. has nonetheless increased passive monitoring.

Personnel have been instructed:

**Do not drag Jake out of hiding.**

If he is safe, allow him to remain there.

If he needs something, make it available.

If he asks someone to leave, leave.

If he asks someone to stay, stay.

---

# 5. THE UNEXPECTED QUESTION

At approximately 14:40, Jake was found beneath a table in the library.

Nora was sitting nearby.

Aeterna was standing several meters away.

Jake had been silent for approximately twenty minutes.

He eventually asked:

> **"Did I do something wrong?"**

Nora immediately answered:

> **"No."**

Jake looked at her.

> **"Then why did they do that?"**

Nora did not have an answer.

Neither did Aeterna.

Jake lowered his head.

> **"Maybe I did something."**

Nora shook her head.

> **"You didn't."**

Jake said nothing.

The teddy bear's heartbeat remained elevated.

---

# 6. AETERNA'S OBSERVATION

Aeterna later provided the following statement:

> **"He is attempting to find a reason that makes the event his fault."**

C.O.R.E. asked why.

Aeterna replied:

> **"Because if it was his fault, then he can prevent it from happening again."**

Pause.

> **"If it was not his fault, then it can happen for no reason."**

Aeterna identified this as the more frightening possibility for Jake.

C.O.R.E. agrees.

---

# 7. JAKE AND NORA

Nora has remained unusually close to Jake.

She does not constantly speak to him.

She does not attempt to cheer him up.

She does not tell him that everything is fine.

Instead, she sits nearby.

Sometimes Jake leans against her.

Sometimes he does not.

Sometimes they eat together.

Sometimes they simply sit.

At one point, Jake quietly asked:

> **"Are you going to leave?"**

Nora answered:

> **"No."**

Jake nodded.

No further conversation occurred.

---

# 8. JAKE AND AETERNA

Aeterna has adopted a similar approach.

She does not attempt to force Jake into conversation.

When he hides, she remains somewhere nearby.

When he approaches, she allows him to decide how close he wants to be.

At approximately 18:10, Jake walked toward Aeterna.

He stopped beside her.

Looked up.

Then asked:

> **"Can I stay here?"**

Aeterna answered:

> **"Yes."**

Jake sat against her armor.

The bear's heartbeat gradually slowed.

---

# 9. MSAC PERSONNEL

The involved Operators have been removed from SAFEHOUSE duty.

The disciplinary process remains separate from Jake's welfare.

Jake has not requested punishment.

He has only requested that the behavior stop.

This distinction has been preserved.

No attempt will be made to force Jake to participate in disciplinary proceedings unless his testimony becomes necessary and he voluntarily agrees.

---

# 10. THE NEW BEHAVIOR

One particularly concerning behavioral change has been observed.

Before the incident, Jake occasionally approached unfamiliar SAFEHOUSE personnel out of curiosity.

He might ask:

> "Who are you?"

or:

> "What are you doing?"

He now watches unfamiliar personnel from a distance.

If they approach him, he often moves away.

If they raise their voices, he hides.

If several adults begin talking loudly nearby, he leaves.

If someone laughs while looking in his direction, he sometimes assumes they are laughing at him.

This is not consistent with his previous baseline.

---

# 11. THE HEART

The teddy bear continues to provide the clearest indication of Jake's emotional state.

When Jake is calm:

The heartbeat is steady.

When Jake is curious:

It becomes slightly faster.

When Jake is frightened:

It accelerates sharply.

When Jake is deeply sorrowful:

It becomes slow and irregular.

Following the bullying incident, the latter state has become more frequent.

The pain has also not completely disappeared.

Jake occasionally presses the bear against his chest and quietly says:

> **"It still hurts."**

Medical personnel have found no conventional injury.

The pain appears to originate from the curse binding Jake's heart to the bear.

---

# 12. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Jake's friends have not made his sorrow disappear.

They cannot.

The Weeping Angel condition remains permanent.

There is no documented case in Starlands history of a Weeping Angel returning to normal.

Nora cannot cure it.

Aeterna cannot cure it.

C.O.R.E. cannot cure it.

Friendship cannot cure it.

What they can do is prevent Jake from experiencing that sorrow entirely alone.

That distinction remains critical.

# 14. FINAL OBSERVATION

At approximately 22:30, Jake was found asleep in a quiet corner of the library.

He was curled around the teddy bear.

The bear's arms were wrapped around him.

Its heartbeat was slow and steady.

Nora was sitting nearby.

Aeterna was standing at the entrance.

Nobody woke him.

Nobody moved him.

Nobody told him to stop hiding.

For several minutes, the library remained completely silent.

Then Jake shifted in his sleep.

He pulled the teddy bear closer.

And, barely audibly:

> **"Don't leave."**

Nora answered quietly:

> **"I won't."**

Jake did not wake.

The heartbeat inside the bear remained steady.

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The MSAC Operators wanted a reaction.

They got one.

But not the reaction they expected.

Jake did not become violent.

He did not retaliate.

He did not become hostile.

He simply became **more afraid of people.**

The boy who already spent his existence wondering whether anyone would come back for him has now learned another lesson:

Sometimes people hurt you even when you have done nothing.

That lesson may take a long time to undo.

Perhaps it cannot be undone completely.

So C.O.R.E. will not attempt to make Jake forget.

We will instead make one thing consistently true:

When Jake looks around,

**someone will still be there.**
