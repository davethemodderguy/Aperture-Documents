# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake remains alive.

His teddy bear does not.

At approximately 03:17, personnel monitoring Jake's condition noticed that the heartbeat previously detectable within his teddy bear had stopped.

No cardiac event was observed in Jake himself.

No physical collapse occurred.

No loss of consciousness occurred.

Jake simply remained sitting where he had been.

The teddy bear's core is no longer producing a detectable heartbeat.

Jake's stellar core, however, remains active.

Barely.

His glow has diminished to an extremely faint level.

His behavior has also changed significantly.

Jake is now almost completely emotionless.

He rarely reacts to his surroundings.

He rarely initiates interaction.

He spends most of his time sitting somewhere and doing nothing.

His head remains slightly tilted to one side.

His halo rests heavily against his head rather than floating in its usual energetic posture.

He appears profoundly exhausted.
