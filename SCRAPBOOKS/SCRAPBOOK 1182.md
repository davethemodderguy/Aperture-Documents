# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon has now witnessed one of Neri's anemic episodes.

Due to Nora's age, Neri elected not to explain the underlying medical condition in technical terms.

Instead, Neri attempted to explain it:

> **“Like you're six.”**

The explanation was medically incomplete.

It was also considerably more understandable to Nora than a conventional medical briefing would have been.

The incident also resulted in an unexpected development.

Nora has begun referring to Bao as:

> **“Mom.”**

She appears to have adopted the title directly from Neri.

Bao has not objected.

---

# 1. THE INCIDENT

Nora was playing on one of the floating islands in Neri's subspace.

Neri had been sitting nearby with a book.

She had been quiet for several minutes.

Then she stopped reading.

Nora noticed.

> **NORA:**  
> “Big sis?”

Neri did not immediately answer.

Her vision had begun to blur.

She placed the book down.

Nora approached.

> **NORA:**  
> “Are you okay?”

Neri nodded.

Then shook her head.

Nora's expression changed.

> **NORA:**  
> “You're doing the weird thing again.”

Neri looked at her.

> **NERI:**  
> “A little.”

Nora immediately became concerned.

> **NORA:**  
> “Is it the blood thing?”

Neri paused.

She nodded.

---

# 2. THE CHILD-FRIENDLY EXPLANATION

Nora sat beside her.

> **NORA:**  
> “What's wrong with your blood?”

Neri considered how to answer.

She could have explained:

- erythrocyte depletion;
    
- abnormal immune targeting;
    
- altered blood physiology;
    
- cardiovascular consequences;
    
- and the established role of Bao's blood support.
    

She did not.

Instead:

> **NERI:**  
> “My blood gets confused.”

Nora blinked.

> **NORA:**  
> “Confused?”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “The little blood cells are supposed to carry oxygen.”

She pointed vaguely toward herself.

> **NERI:**  
> “But my other little blood cells sometimes think they're bad.”

Nora frowned.

> **NORA:**  
> “Why?”

> **NERI:**  
> “I don't know.”

> **NORA:**  
> “So they fight?”

> **NERI:**  
> “Yeah.”

Nora looked horrified.

> **NORA:**  
> “Your blood cells fight each other?”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Sometimes.”

Nora looked at Neri's chest.

> **NORA:**  
> “That's stupid.”

Neri quietly agreed.

> **NERI:**  
> “Very.”

---

# 3. NORA'S UNDERSTANDING

Nora appeared to process the explanation.

> **NORA:**  
> “So your blood has bad guys?”

Neri thought about it.

> **NERI:**  
> “Not really.”

> **NORA:**  
> “Confused guys?”

> **NERI:**  
> “Closer.”

Nora nodded seriously.

> **NORA:**  
> “Okay.”

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> “And then you get sleepy?”

> **NERI:**  
> “Sometimes.”

> **NORA:**  
> “And dizzy?”

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

> **NORA:**  
> “And your heart hurts?”

Neri paused.

Then nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Sometimes.”

Nora's expression became worried.

> **NORA:**  
> “That's not good.”

> **NERI:**  
> “No.”

---

# 4. THE MOST IMPORTANT PART

Nora immediately asked:

> **NORA:**  
> “What do I do?”

Neri looked at her.

She thought carefully.

Then gave the simplest answer possible.

> **NERI:**  
> “Get Mom.”

Nora blinked.

> **NORA:**  
> “Mom?”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Bao.”

Nora's eyes widened.

> **NORA:**  
> “Bao is Mom?”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “She's my mom.”

Nora pointed toward herself.

> **NORA:**  
> “Then she's my mom too?”

Neri stared at her.

A long pause followed.

> **NERI:**  
> “...I guess.”

Nora smiled.

> **NORA:**  
> “Okay.”

She immediately ran toward the exit.

> **NORA:**  
> “MOM!”

Neri blinked.

---

# 5. BAO'S ARRIVAL

Bao arrived less than a minute later.

She expected an emergency.

Instead, she found Nora standing in the doorway.

> **BAO:**  
> “Nora?”

Nora pointed behind herself.

> **NORA:**  
> “Big sis's blood is confused!”

Bao froze.

Neri quietly covered her face.

> **BAO:**  
> “...What?”

Nora explained enthusiastically.

> **NORA:**  
> “The little blood cells are fighting the other little blood cells because they don't know who's bad!”

Bao looked toward Neri.

Neri shrugged.

> **NERI:**  
> “I explained it simply.”

Bao smiled.

> **BAO:**  
> “You certainly did.”

Nora grabbed Bao's hand.

> **NORA:**  
> “Mom, fix her.”

Bao stopped.

She looked at Neri.

Neri looked back.

Neither corrected Nora.

Bao gently held Nora's hand.

> **BAO:**  
> “I'll take care of her.”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “Okay, Mom.”

---

Nora has continued using it.

Examples include:

> **“Mom, where's Big Sis?”**

> **“Mom, can I have cake?”**

> **“Mom, Big Sis is doing chemistry again.”**

> **“Mom, Neri bit me.”**

> **NERI:**  
> “You bit my tail.”

Nora:

> **“Same thing.”**

Bao:

> **“No, it isn't.”**

Neri has established one very simple rule for Nora.

> **NERI:**  
> “You can still get Mom if you think something is wrong.”

Nora nodded firmly.

> **NORA:**  
> “Okay.”

Nora now describes Neri's condition as:

> **NERI:**

Nora, from across the room:

> **NORA:**

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Nora now understands the most important practical aspect of Neri's condition.

She does not understand the hematology.

She does not need to.

At six years old, her understanding is:

> **Neri's blood sometimes gets confused.**

> **Neri can become sick.**

> **Neri sometimes needs help.**

> **If Neri is in trouble, get Mom.**

This is sufficient for her current role as a younger sibling.

More importantly, Nora does not interpret Neri's condition as making her fragile, broken, or less capable.

She still sees her sister as:

> **Big Sis.**

The genius chemist.

The owner of the floating-island room.

The person who somehow made all those Tachyon Industries items.

The person whose tail is apparently extremely fun to bite.

And the person who needs someone to get Mom when her blood starts being stupid.
