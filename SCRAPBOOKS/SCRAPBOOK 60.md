# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL INVESTIGATION LOG**

### **CASE FILE: NORMA LOCKWOOD – RECURRING NIGHTMARES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Medical, Psychological & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the recovery of long-suppressed childhood memories, **Senior Manager Norma Lockwood** began experiencing recurring nightmares.

Symptoms included:

- Frequent awakenings during the night.
    
- Difficulty returning to sleep.
    
- Elevated stress responses upon waking.
    
- Daytime fatigue.
    
- Avoidance of discussing the dreams.
    

After Norma consented to psychological assistance, **Director Belle**, whose Ether abilities allow her to enter and observe dreams for investigative purposes, conducted a supervised dream examination.

Medical personnel remained on standby throughout the procedure.

---

## **DREAM OBSERVATION**

The dream did not present as a normal memory.

It was fragmented.

Dark hallways.

Cold lights.

Echoing voices.

The environment repeatedly shifted between a laboratory and indistinct corridors, as traumatic memories often do.

Belle observed a much younger Norma restrained during what appeared to be an unauthorized experiment.

Records within the dream suggested the objective was to expose her to extremely unstable Ether through invasive procedures in an attempt to produce an artificial anomaly.

The experiment rapidly became uncontrollable.

Personnel within the memory could be heard arguing.

> "The Ether isn't stabilizing."

> "She's still alive."

> "Increase the dosage."

> "No—"

The scene fractured before Belle could identify any individuals.

---

## **AFTERMATH WITHIN THE MEMORY**

The dream shifted again.

The laboratory was gone.

Instead, Belle saw the young Norma abandoned outside the facility.

Weak.

Disoriented.

Unable to understand why she had been left there.

The dream suggested she wandered for days with little food or water before eventually collapsing.

No identifying landmarks remained.

Only the overwhelming feeling of abandonment.

Belle determined that the dream consistently ended at this point, preventing any further recollection.

---

## **ARCTURUS' ACCOUNT**

After reviewing Belle's observations, Arcturus quietly filled in what he remembered.

> "When she disappeared..."

He paused.

> "...I searched everywhere I could."

He lowered his head.

> "Weeks later..."

> "...I found her."

According to Arcturus, Norma was severely weakened and dehydrated when he finally reached her.

She was frightened, barely spoke, and could not explain where she had been.

At the time, he believed she had simply become lost.

Looking back, he no longer believed that to be true.

> "...Someone took her."

---

## **ASSESSMENT**

Belle emphasized that dreams—especially those involving severe trauma—may blend genuine memories with symbolic imagery.

However, the consistency of Norma's recurring nightmares, together with her physical surgical scars and Arcturus' recollections, strongly suggests that she was **abducted as a child and subjected to unauthorized Ether experimentation**.

The identities of those responsible remain unknown.

No surviving records identifying the perpetrators have been recovered.

---

## **POST-INVESTIGATION**

Belle ended the dream session immediately after confirming the source of the recurring nightmares.

When Norma awoke, Belle sat beside her bed.

She did not describe every detail she had witnessed.

Instead, she simply said:

> "I know enough."

Norma looked away.

> "...Was it really that bad?"

Belle gently took her hand.

> "...No child should have gone through what you did."

For several moments, neither of them spoke.

Then Belle added quietly:

> "What happened to you is part of your past."

> "It doesn't define who you are today."

---

## **FOLLOW-UP**

The Blacksite Medical and Psychological Departments recommended:

- Continued trauma-informed counseling.
    
- Monitoring of sleep quality.
    
- Temporary workload adjustments during periods of severe sleep disruption.
    
- Immediate support from trusted personnel if nightmares become overwhelming.
    

Arcturus also requested to be informed if his sister experienced another severe episode.

His request was approved.

---

## **STATUS**

The investigation was concluded with no additional dream examinations planned unless requested by Norma herself.

The focus of her care has shifted from uncovering further details of the past to helping ensure that, when she wakes from another nightmare, she no longer has to face it alone.

**End of Psychological Investigation Log**
