**SUBJECT:** Jake — Accidental Cheek Kiss During Sleepy Transportation  
**INVOLVED RESIDENTS:** Jake, Phoenix, Clover  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — CAFETERIA / RESIDENTIAL CORRIDOR

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately 21:18, Jake fell asleep in the SAFEHOUSE cafeteria.

He was in his ordinary human form at the time, with his white hair, azure shooting-star eyes, and teddy bear beside him.

Phoenix discovered him asleep at the table.

Rather than waking him unnecessarily, she decided to carry him back to his room.

The transportation itself was uneventful.

The incident occurred when Jake stirred during the journey, lifted his head, and accidentally kissed Phoenix on the cheek.

The contact was brief.

Jake immediately fell asleep again.

Phoenix stopped walking.

She remained motionless for approximately seven seconds.

She then looked down at the sleeping child in her arms.

And, according to the nearest witness, her brain appeared to stop functioning.

**C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT:** The kiss was accidental. Jake was half-asleep, made an uncoordinated movement, and showed no apparent awareness of what had happened.

**PHOENIX'S RESPONSE:** Classified as an event of overwhelming personal significance.

---

# 1. INITIAL CONDITIONS

**TIME:** 21:18  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE CAFETERIA

Jake had spent the evening reading.

At some point, he had put his book down beside his teddy bear.

His head gradually lowered toward his folded arms.

Within several minutes, he was asleep.

His white hair covered part of his forehead.

His ears had relaxed sideways.

His breathing was quiet and regular.

One hand rested against his teddy bear.

The other was tucked beneath his cheek.

Phoenix noticed him while passing through the cafeteria.

She stopped beside his table.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Jake?"

No response.

She leaned slightly closer.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Hey, little star."

Jake shifted his head.

His eyes remained closed.

Phoenix waited.

Nothing else happened.

She glanced at the clock, then at the sleeping child.

**PHOENIX:**  
"...You fell asleep out here."

Jake made a small, sleepy noise.

Phoenix's expression softened.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Okay. Let's get you to bed."

She carefully collected his book and ensured his teddy bear remained with him.

Then she picked him up.

Jake, still asleep, instinctively settled against her.

Phoenix adjusted her hold to support his head and back.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Got you."

Jake did not answer.

His tail curled loosely against his own leg.

Phoenix began walking.

At this point, everything was normal.

That would not last.

---

# 2. THE INCIDENT

**TIME:** 21:21  
**LOCATION:** RESIDENTIAL CORRIDOR

Phoenix was approximately halfway to Jake's room when he stirred.

His head shifted against her shoulder.

She slowed down.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Easy. You're okay."

Jake's eyes opened halfway.

His azure irises glowed faintly beneath the corridor lights.

The pupils were narrow vertical slits.

He appeared profoundly confused.

He looked at Phoenix.

Then at the corridor.

Then back at Phoenix.

**JAKE:**  
"...Mm?"

**PHOENIX:**  
"I'm taking you back to your room. You fell asleep in the cafeteria."

Jake blinked slowly.

He seemed to process approximately half of that sentence.

Then he leaned forward.

Phoenix instinctively adjusted her grip to keep him from slipping.

Jake's face brushed her cheek.

His lips briefly touched it.

A small, sleepy kiss.

Entirely accidental.

Entirely unexpected.

Jake immediately settled back against her shoulder.

His eyes closed.

He was asleep again.

Phoenix stopped walking.

Completely.

Her arms remained securely around Jake.

Her expression went blank.

For seven seconds, she did not move.

Then—

**PHOENIX:**  
"..."

A passing resident glanced at her.

**RESIDENT:**  
"Phoenix?"

No response.

**RESIDENT:**  
"Are you okay?"

Phoenix slowly turned her head.

Her face had become noticeably red.

**PHOENIX:**  
"He—"

She stopped.

Looked down at Jake.

Jake was sleeping peacefully.

His cheek rested against her shoulder.

One ear had folded against his white hair.

His teddy bear was tucked safely between them.

Phoenix swallowed.

**PHOENIX:**  
"He kissed me."

The resident looked at Jake.

Then at Phoenix.

Then back at Jake.

**RESIDENT:**  
"Was he awake?"

Phoenix stared at the sleeping child.

**PHOENIX:**  
"...No."

**RESIDENT:**  
"Then it was probably accidental."

**PHOENIX:**  
"I KNOW."

Her voice echoed down the corridor.

Jake's ear twitched.

Phoenix immediately lowered her voice.

**PHOENIX:**  
"...Sorry."

Jake continued sleeping.

Phoenix resumed walking.

She was now red enough that the medical team would probably have mistaken her for someone experiencing a fever.

---

# 3. PHOENIX ATTEMPTS TO RECOVER

Phoenix made a serious effort to behave normally.

She really did.

She reminded herself of the relevant facts.

Jake had been asleep.

He had been disoriented when he briefly woke.

He had leaned forward without understanding his position.

His face had contacted hers.

He had immediately fallen asleep again.

There was no indication that he had consciously intended to kiss her.

These facts were not difficult to understand.

Unfortunately, understanding them did not prevent Phoenix's face from remaining bright red.

She adjusted Jake's position slightly.

**PHOENIX:**  
"You're going to give me a heart attack one day."

Jake mumbled something.

**JAKE:**  
"...Teddy."

Phoenix looked down.

His hand had loosened around the bear.

She carefully repositioned it so that he could hold it comfortably.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Here. I've got him."

Jake's fingers curled around the teddy bear.

He relaxed.

Phoenix's expression softened again.

Then she remembered the kiss.

Her face turned red all over again.

**PHOENIX:**  
"...Unbelievable."

Jake purred faintly against her shoulder.

Phoenix nearly walked into a door.

---

# 4. CLOVER'S OBSERVATION

**TIME:** 21:24  
**LOCATION:** RESIDENTIAL CORRIDOR

Clover happened to be leaving the shared residence when she encountered Phoenix.

She immediately noticed three things.

First, Phoenix was carrying Jake.

Second, Jake was completely asleep.

Third, Phoenix looked as though she had just witnessed the collapse of civilization.

Clover stopped.

**CLOVER:**  
"What's wrong with you?"

**PHOENIX:**  
"Nothing."

Clover looked at Jake.

**CLOVER:**  
"Is he okay?"

**PHOENIX:**  
"He's asleep."

**CLOVER:**  
"Then why do you look like that?"

Phoenix tightened her lips.

**PHOENIX:**  
"He kissed me."

Clover blinked.

A pause.

**CLOVER:**  
"...What?"

**PHOENIX:**  
"ON THE CHEEK."

**CLOVER:**  
"I didn't ask where!"

Phoenix looked horrified at having said anything at all.

Clover covered her mouth.

Her shoulders began shaking.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Don't."

Clover made a strangled noise.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Clover."

Clover turned away.

She was laughing silently now.

**PHOENIX:**  
"He's asleep! It was an accident!"

Clover nodded rapidly.

**CLOVER:**  
"I KNOW!"

She took a breath.

Looked at Jake.

Looked at Phoenix.

And lost the battle entirely.

**CLOVER:**  
"YOU'RE BLUSHING SO HARD!"

**PHOENIX:**  
"PLEASE BE QUIET!"

Jake shifted in Phoenix's arms.

Both residents immediately fell silent.

His eyes remained closed.

He pressed his face a little closer against Phoenix's shoulder.

**JAKE:**  
"...Warm."

Phoenix froze again.

Clover's expression became dangerously delighted.

Phoenix pointed at her with the hand that was not supporting Jake.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Not. One. Word."

Clover held up both hands.

**CLOVER:**  
"I didn't say anything."

She waited until Phoenix had passed.

Then whispered to herself:

**CLOVER:**  
"Oh, this is going in my personal memory forever."

---

# 5. ARRIVAL AT JAKE'S ROOM

Phoenix eventually reached Jake's room.

She opened the door carefully and entered.

The room was quiet.

His bed was ready.

She lowered him gently onto the mattress, supporting his head until it rested comfortably on the pillow.

Jake immediately rolled onto his stomach.

His tail curled around his left leg.

His ears drooped under gravity.

Phoenix stared at him.

The sleeping posture was familiar.

The teddy bear, which she had placed beside him, was drawn close against his upper body.

Jake made a small sound and tucked his face toward the pillow.

Phoenix adjusted the blanket.

**PHOENIX:**  
"There. Good night, little star."

Jake's eyes opened a fraction.

He looked vaguely toward her.

**JAKE:**  
"...Night."

Phoenix smiled.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Sleep well."

His eyes closed again.

Phoenix remained beside the bed for a moment, ensuring that he was comfortable and that his breathing was unobstructed.

Then she stood.

She was almost at the door when she stopped.

Slowly, she turned back toward him.

Jake was still asleep.

Completely unaware.

Phoenix touched her cheek.

The exact spot.

She stared at the wall.

**PHOENIX:**  
"...I'm never surviving this."

From the bed came a tiny, contented purr.

Phoenix covered her face with both hands.

---

# 6. THE FOLLOWING MORNING

**TIME:** 08:06  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE KITCHEN

Phoenix had made one mistake.

She had assumed she could act normally the next morning.

She was wrong.

Jake entered the kitchen carrying his teddy bear.

His white hair was messy.

His ears were slightly uneven.

His eyes were half-open, with his pupils narrowing briefly as he adjusted to the brighter room.

He looked tired.

Phoenix saw him.

Immediately sat straighter.

**JAKE:**  
"Morning."

**PHOENIX:**  
"GOOD MORNING!"

Jake stopped.

He looked at her.

**JAKE:**  
"...Why are you loud?"

Phoenix's expression collapsed.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Sorry."

Jake approached the table and sat down.

He placed his teddy bear beside him.

Then looked at Phoenix again.

**JAKE:**  
"You okay?"

Phoenix nearly dropped her cup.

**PHOENIX:**  
"YES."

Jake stared at her.

**JAKE:**  
"...Okay."

He reached for his breakfast.

A few seconds passed.

Then he spoke again.

**JAKE:**  
"Did you carry me back last night?"

Phoenix slowly lowered her cup.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Yes."

**JAKE:**  
"Thanks."

Phoenix's face softened.

**PHOENIX:**  
"You're welcome."

Jake ate quietly.

He seemed to consider something.

Then—

**JAKE:**  
"Did I do something?"

Phoenix stopped breathing for approximately one second.

**PHOENIX:**  
"...Why?"

Jake shrugged.

**JAKE:**  
"You look weird."

From the other side of the kitchen, Clover made a noise that sounded suspiciously like a cough.

Phoenix gave her a warning look.

Clover became fascinated by her breakfast.

**PHOENIX:**  
"No. You didn't do anything wrong."

Jake nodded.

Satisfied, he returned to eating.

After a moment, he pushed a small piece of food toward his teddy bear before remembering that the bear did not eat.

He pulled it back.

Phoenix watched him.

The embarrassment was still there.

But so was the affection.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Jake?"

He looked up.

**JAKE:**  
"Hm?"

Phoenix hesitated.

She decided against telling him about the kiss.

There was no need to embarrass him over something he had done while half-asleep.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Next time you fall asleep in the cafeteria, I'll take you back again."

Jake blinked.

**JAKE:**  
"...Okay."

He returned to his breakfast.

Then, quietly:

**JAKE:**  
"You're warm."

Phoenix went completely still.

Jake continued eating as if he had said nothing unusual.

Clover slowly put down her spoon.

Phoenix looked toward the ceiling.

**PHOENIX:**  
"I need coffee."

**CLOVER:**  
"You already have coffee."

**PHOENIX:**  
"I NEED MORE."

# 7. C.O.R.E. REVIEW

The incident has been reviewed.

No evidence suggests that Jake deliberately initiated romantic or affectionate contact.

He was asleep, briefly stirred while being carried, and made an uncoordinated movement that resulted in a brief kiss on Phoenix's cheek.

Phoenix's reaction, while unusually intense, has not affected her ability to care for Jake or respect his boundaries.

Clover's eyewitness account has been retained for completeness.

Her subsequent laughter has been excluded from the formal medical findings.

Mostly.

### Welfare guidance

1. Jake may continue to be carried when necessary, provided he is comfortable with the arrangement and is handled safely.
    
2. If Jake wakes during transportation, the person carrying him should support him securely and allow him time to become oriented.
    
3. Accidental contact during sleep must not be treated as proof of conscious intent.
    
4. Jake should not be teased, pressured, or interrogated about the incident.
    
5. Phoenix is advised to maintain ordinary, appropriate boundaries and not interpret an accidental gesture as an obligation or promise.
    
6. Clover is advised that repeatedly reenacting the incident for entertainment would be unhelpful.
    

Clover requested clarification regarding item six.

C.O.R.E. declined to provide it.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING OBSERVATION

Jake had fallen asleep in the cafeteria.

Phoenix had carried him home.

He had briefly awakened, accidentally kissed her cheek, and fallen asleep again.

For Jake, the incident was effectively nonexistent.

He woke the next morning, thanked Phoenix for carrying him, ate breakfast, and asked why she was acting strangely.

Phoenix, meanwhile, had spent the night attempting to recover from seven seconds of unexpected affection.

Clover had witnessed the aftermath and was unlikely to forget it.

Nobody was harmed.

No anomalous event occurred.

No further investigation was necessary.

And Jake?

Jake returned to his room after breakfast, curled up beside his teddy bear, and went back to sleep.

His tail wrapped around his left leg.

His ears drooped.

His eyes closed.

He purred softly.

Completely unaware that, somewhere in the SAFEHOUSE, Phoenix was still trying to convince herself that a sleepy, accidental cheek kiss was a perfectly ordinary thing to survive.
