# Executive Summary

Phoenix continues to experience a severely inverted sleep cycle.

She is generally unable to sleep voluntarily at night.

During the day, however, her narcolepsy frequently causes her to fall asleep involuntarily while working.

This has resulted in a predictable pattern:

> **NIGHT:** Awake.  
> **DAY:** Works.  
> **DAY:** Falls asleep involuntarily.  
> **AFTER WAKING:** Works again.

The following report was written by Phoenix herself.

It was submitted after another fatal accident.

---

# PHOENIX — PERSONAL REPORT

**TIME WRITTEN:** 03:41  
**LOCATION:** KCA WORK AREA / SAFEHOUSE

---

I died again today.

I tripped.

That part was my fault.

I was carrying some medical supplies and caught my foot on something I apparently didn't see.

I fell toward the security door.

The door was opening.

My neck ended up in the wrong place at the wrong time.

The door crushed my neck.

I died.

That is probably the most embarrassing way I've died so far.

I don't think anyone needs to worry about it now.

I'm fine.

Mostly.

The injuries are negligible by now.

Transcendense already handled the important part.

The pain is gone.

My neck doesn't hurt anymore.

There is still some soreness, but compared with everything else I've dealt with, it's basically nothing.

So, medically speaking:

I'm okay.

Again.

# What Actually Happened

I was working alone.

I had been awake most of the night.

I was tired.

I probably shouldn't have been carrying things while that tired.

I tripped.

The security door caught me.

I died.

Then I woke up.

Then I finished putting the supplies away.

Yes.

I know how that sounds.

No, I don't recommend it.

---

# The Part I Don't Like

I didn't really panic when I woke up.

That bothers me more than the accident.

Not because I wanted to die.

I didn't.

I don't.

I just...

I already knew I would wake up.

That's becoming normal.

I don't want that to become normal.

# C.O.R.E. ARCHIVE ADDENDUM

Phoenix submitted the above report without medical prompting.

She did not request emergency assistance.

She did not request an investigation into the security door.

She did not describe the incident as traumatic.

She did, however, explicitly acknowledge that her exhaustion contributed to the accident.

This distinction is significant.

Phoenix continues to demonstrate extremely poor self-preservation while off duty or severely fatigued.

Her ability to recover from fatal injuries appears to have reduced the urgency with which she responds to her own physical safety.

C.O.R.E. does **not** consider this evidence that Phoenix wishes to die.

Quite the opposite.

She repeatedly states that she does not.

The concern is that death has become sufficiently familiar to her that avoiding it no longer feels immediately necessary.
