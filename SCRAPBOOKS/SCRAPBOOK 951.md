# SAFEHOUSE INCIDENT REPORT

## CASE: THE MISSING CUPCAKE

**Incident Classification:** Minor Internal Investigation  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE Cafeteria  
**Investigators:** Neri / Jane Doe  
**Status:** Resolved  
**Primary Item:** One (1) cupcake  
**Estimated Value:** Negligible  
**Emotional Value:** Considerably higher than negligible

---

## 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

At approximately 14:37, Neri returned to her usual cafeteria table and discovered that a cupcake she had placed beside her chemistry book was missing.

The cupcake had been present approximately three minutes earlier.

Neri immediately checked beneath the table.

Nothing.

She checked her coat pockets.

Nothing.

She checked the floor.

Nothing.

She then looked at Jane Doe.

> **Neri:** "Someone took my cupcake."

Jane paused.

She looked at the empty plate.

Then at Neri.

Then back at the empty plate.

> **Jane:** "Do you want me to investigate?"

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:** "Yes."

Jane stood.

> **Jane:** "Then we're investigating."

Neri picked up her chemistry book.

> **Neri:** "I'll help."

Jane smiled.

> **Jane:** "Of course you will, My Cute Seal."

Neri's expression immediately flattened.

> **Neri:** "Don't call me that."

> **Jane:** "Noted."

Jane continued smiling.

---

# 2. INVESTIGATION OBJECTIVES

The investigation was conducted under the following assumptions:

1. The cupcake existed.
    
2. Neri did not eat it.
    
3. Neri did not misplace it.
    
4. The cupcake did not spontaneously disappear through anomalous means.
    
5. Someone physically moved the cupcake.
    
6. The responsible party could be identified through observation, questioning, or inconsistencies in testimony.
    

Neko was specifically excluded from the investigation after stating:

> **Neko:** "I'm not involved."

Jane immediately replied:

> **Jane:** "You answered suspiciously fast."

> **Neko:** "I know how this ends."

---

# 3. JANE DOE'S APPROACH

Jane did not begin by accusing anyone.

She began by determining who had access to the cafeteria during the relevant time window.

She spoke casually.

She asked who had been eating.

Who had passed through.

Who had noticed Neri's table.

Who had seen the cupcake.

Who had entered the kitchen.

Who had left the cafeteria.

She never asked the same question in exactly the same way.

She also paid attention to what people volunteered without being asked.

Neri followed behind her, taking notes.

> **Neri:** "You're not asking them if they stole it."

> **Jane:** "Because then they only have to say no."

> **Neri:** "So what are you doing?"

> **Jane:** "Finding out what they think happened."

Neri stared at her.

> **Neri:** "That's clever."

> **Jane:** "Thank you."

---

# 4. INTERVIEW — CAFETERIA WORKER

**Subject:** Cafeteria Worker  
**Method:** Casual questioning

Jane approached the worker while Neri remained beside her.

> **Jane:** "Busy today?"

> **Worker:** "A little."

> **Jane:** "Anyone come through here recently?"

> **Worker:** "A few people."

> **Jane:** "Anyone unusual?"

> **Worker:** "No."

Jane nodded.

> **Jane:** "What about the cupcake?"

The worker paused.

Only briefly.

But Jane noticed.

> **Worker:** "What cupcake?"

Neri immediately looked at Jane.

Jane did not react.

> **Jane:** "The one that was on Neri's table."

> **Worker:** "I didn't touch it."

> **Jane:** "I didn't ask whether you touched it."

Silence.

Neri slowly lowered her notebook.

The worker realized what had happened.

> **Worker:** "...Oh."

Jane smiled.

> **Jane:** "Exactly."

The worker eventually explained that someone had asked whether the cupcake was reserved.

They had told the person it belonged to Neri.

The person then walked away.

No theft had been witnessed.

---

# 5. SECOND INTERVIEW

Jane changed direction.

She began asking residents whether they had seen someone carrying a cupcake.

Several answers were unhelpful.

One resident remembered someone walking toward the residential wing.

Another remembered someone heading toward the lounge.

A third remembered seeing a plate.

Jane stopped.

> **Jane:** "A plate?"

> **Resident:** "Yeah."

> **Jane:** "What color?"

> **Resident:** "White."

Neri looked at Jane.

> **Neri:** "Our cafeteria plates are white."

> **Jane:** "Correct."

Jane considered this.

Then she asked:

> **Jane:** "Was the cupcake on it?"

> **Resident:** "I think so."

> **Jane:** "You think?"

> **Resident:** "I wasn't really paying attention."

Jane nodded.

She did not press further.

Instead, she asked a completely unrelated question.

> **Jane:** "Did the person look like they were hiding anything?"

> **Resident:** "Actually... yeah."

> **Jane:** "What?"

> **Resident:** "They kept looking around."

Jane smiled.

Neri wrote this down.

---

# 6. THE FIRST BREAK

Jane returned to the cafeteria.

She looked at the table.

Then the empty plate.

Then the floor.

Then Neri.

> **Jane:** "How long was your cupcake unattended?"

> **Neri:** "Three minutes."

> **Jane:** "And you were reading?"

> **Neri:** "Yes."

> **Jane:** "So whoever took it knew you weren't watching."

Neri nodded.

Jane crouched.

She examined the underside of the table.

Nothing.

Then she looked toward the cafeteria exit.

> **Jane:** "They didn't steal it for the cupcake."

Neri blinked.

> **Neri:** "Then why?"

> **Jane:** "Because they wanted something else."

> **Neri:** "What?"

Jane stood.

> **Jane:** "Attention."

---

# 7. THE TRAP

Jane returned to the cafeteria carrying another cupcake.

She placed it on the table.

Neri stared.

> **Neri:** "That's mine?"

> **Jane:** "No."

Neri looked offended.

> **Neri:** "Then why is it here?"

> **Jane:** "Because we're baiting them."

Neri's eyes widened.

> **Neri:** "We're doing a trap?"

> **Jane:** "A very boring trap."

Jane and Neri left the cafeteria.

They did not actually leave.

They waited around the corner.

Five minutes passed.

Nothing.

Ten minutes.

Nothing.

Neri whispered:

> **Neri:** "This is boring."

> **Jane:** "Good investigations usually are."

Twenty minutes.

Footsteps.

Neri immediately reached toward one of her hairclips.

Jane gently stopped her hand.

> **Jane:** "We're not stabbing the suspect."

> **Neri:** "...Okay."

A figure entered the cafeteria.

They looked around.

They approached the table.

They picked up the cupcake.

Jane stepped around the corner.

> **Jane:** "Got you."

The suspect froze.

Neri appeared beside Jane.

> **Neri:** "WHERE'S MY FIRST CUPCAKE?"

The suspect immediately surrendered.

---

# 8. INTERROGATION

The culprit was identified as a junior resident.

The cupcake had not been eaten.

It had been moved.

The culprit had taken it because they thought Neri would become upset and they wanted to see what she would do.

This explanation produced approximately three seconds of silence.

Then:

> **Neri:** "...You stole my cupcake to see me get angry?"

> **Culprit:** "I was curious."

Jane looked at Neri.

Neri looked at Jane.

Jane sighed.

> **Jane:** "You picked the wrong person to experiment on."

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:** "Very wrong."

The culprit immediately returned the original cupcake.

It had been hidden in the lounge.

Miraculously, it remained intact.

---

# 9. RECOVERY

Neri accepted the cupcake.

She inspected it carefully.

Then took a bite.

Silence.

> **Jane:** "Well?"

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:** "It's okay."

Jane smiled.

> **Jane:** "Case closed."

Neri continued eating.

After several seconds:

> **Neri:** "Jane?"

> **Jane:** "Yes?"

> **Neri:** "You're really good at this."

> **Jane:** "Extraction, intimidation, mind games."

She counted them on her fingers.

> **Jane:** "It's what I do."

Neri thought about this.

> **Neri:** "Can you investigate something else?"

> **Jane:** "Sure."

> **Neri:** "Who keeps taking my candy?"

Jane immediately stood.

> **Jane:** "Now _that's_ a serious investigation."

---

# 10. FINAL ASSESSMENT

**Cupcake:** Recovered.  
**Perpetrator:** Identified.  
**Injuries:** None.  
**Property Damage:** None.  
**Neri's Emotional State:** Stable.  
**Jane's Professional Assessment:** Successful.  
**Future Cupcake Security:** Recommended.

### Administrative Note

Following the incident, cafeteria staff placed a small sign beside Neri's usual table:

> **NERI'S FOOD**  
> **DO NOT TOUCH**

A second sign appeared underneath it the following morning:

> **SERIOUSLY.**

The second sign is believed to have been added by Jane.

Neri approved of both.

She then ate another cupcake.
