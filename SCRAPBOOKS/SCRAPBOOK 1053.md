## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### SAFEHOUSE WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-NERI-149  
**CLASSIFICATION:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL  
**STATUS:** COMPLETED  
**SUBJECT:** Neri — Temporary Full-Day Care Assignment  
**ASSIGNED CARETAKER:** Jane Doe  
**DURATION:** One Day

---

## Executive Summary

With the majority of SAFEHOUSE personnel occupied for the day, Jane Doe was assigned temporary responsibility for Plushie Neri.

Jane has previously demonstrated that Neri is comfortable around her.

This assignment was therefore considered low-risk.

The assignment was also expected to be relatively uneventful.

It was not.

Not because Neri caused problems.

Because Jane spent most of the day carrying a plush seal around while attempting to complete her own work.

---

# 08:31 — Assignment Begins

Jane found Neri sitting on a couch.

Neri looked at her.

Jane held out her arms.

Neri immediately crawled toward her.

Jane picked her up.

> **JANE:** "Morning, My Cute Seal."

Neri stared at her.

Jane paused.

> **JANE:** "...You're still mad about that?"

Neri looked away.

> **JANE:** "Right."

Jane carried her with her anyway.

---

# 09:04 — Intelligence Work

Jane began reviewing intelligence material.

Neri sat in her lap.

Jane worked with one hand while the other rested loosely around Neri.

Neri remained still.

After approximately fifteen minutes, Neri repositioned herself.

Jane adjusted her arm accordingly.

No conversation occurred.

This arrangement continued for approximately an hour.

---

# 10:17 — Neri Wanders

Neri climbed out of Jane's lap.

She walked across the room.

Jane continued working.

Neri disappeared behind a chair.

Jane looked over.

> **JANE:** "Where are you going?"

Neri continued walking.

Jane waited.

Neri eventually returned carrying nothing.

She climbed back onto Jane.

> **JANE:** "Good expedition?"

Neri nodded.

Jane resumed working.

---

# 11:42 — Coffee

Jane prepared coffee.

Neri sat on the counter.

Jane placed her own cup down.

Neri stared at it.

Jane immediately moved it away.

> **JANE:** "No."

Neri continued staring.

> **JANE:** "You've got your own."

Neri looked at her coffee.

Then at Jane.

Then back at her own coffee.

She drank it.

Jane smiled.

> **JANE:** "See? Problem solved."

---

# 12:36 — Lunch

Jane ate lunch while holding Neri against her side.

Neri ate as well.

No unusual behavior was observed.

At one point, Jane attempted to put Neri on the empty chair beside her.

Neri immediately climbed back onto Jane.

Jane looked down.

> **JANE:** "...You could've just stayed there."

Neri settled against her.

> **JANE:** "Okay."

Jane continued eating.

---

# 14:02 — Afternoon

Neri fell asleep.

Jane was sitting at a desk at the time.

She carefully repositioned Neri against her chest.

Neri remained asleep.

Jane continued working.

Approximately forty minutes later, another SAFEHOUSE resident entered the room.

They looked at Jane.

Then at Neri.

Then at Jane again.

> **RESIDENT:** "You're working like that?"

Jane glanced down.

> **JANE:** "Apparently."

> **RESIDENT:** "Doesn't that make it harder?"

Jane looked at the sleeping plush seal.

> **JANE:** "A little."

Pause.

> **JANE:** "Worth it."

---

# 15:21 — The Carrying Problem

Jane attempted to move between rooms.

Neri remained asleep.

Jane decided not to wake her.

This resulted in Jane spending the next several hours carrying a sleeping plush seal while performing routine tasks.

A security officer later described the sight as:

> "The most intimidating intelligence operative in the building carrying a teddy-bear-sized seal like a baby."

Jane's response:

> "Accurate."

---

# 17:48 — SAFEHOUSE Common Area

Neri woke up.

She looked around.

Then looked at Jane.

Jane held out her arms.

Neri climbed back into them.

No prompting was required.

Jane gave her a small squeeze.

Neri remained there.

---

# 19:03 — Evening

Jane and Neri spent the evening sitting together.

Jane did not attempt to make conversation.

Neri did not speak.

The arrangement was quiet.

Comfortable.

Nothing needed to happen.

---

# 21:37 — Alice Returns

Alice entered the room.

She immediately noticed Neri.

> **ALICE:** "Neri!"

Neri looked toward her.

Alice approached.

> **ALICE:** "Did you have a good day?"

Neri nodded.

Jane looked at Alice.

> **JANE:** "She was easy."

Alice looked at the way Jane was holding Neri.

> **ALICE:** "You didn't put her down much, did you?"

Jane paused.

> **JANE:** "...Not really."

Alice smiled.

Neri remained completely comfortable.

---

# Final Assessment

Jane's handling assignment was successful.

No medical incidents occurred.

No falls occurred.

No accidental cafeteria-floor incidents occurred.

No one nearly stepped on Neri.

No washing machines were involved.

Neri spent much of the day either:

- sitting beside Jane;
    
- sitting in Jane's lap;
    
- being carried by Jane;
    
- sleeping against Jane;
    
- or wandering away briefly before returning.
    

Jane did not attempt to turn Neri's willingness to stay close into a behavioral breakthrough.

She simply let Neri stay.

### Addendum

At approximately 22:04, Jane attempted to place Neri on the couch.

Neri immediately crawled back into her arms.

Jane sighed.

> **JANE:** "Yeah, yeah. I got you."

Neri settled down.

Jane sat back down.

Neither moved for the next thirty-seven minutes.
