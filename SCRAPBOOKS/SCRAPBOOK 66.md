# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **PERSONNEL DOSSIER**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD**

**Codename:** _The Accountant_  
**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Internal Personnel Archive

---

## **BASIC INFORMATION**

**Name:** Corin Lockwood

**Position:** Head of Accounting

**Department:** Financial Management & Resource Allocation

**Siblings:**

- Arcturus Lockwood (Eldest)
    
- Norma Lockwood (Middle)
    
- Corin Lockwood (Youngest)
    

While Arcturus safeguards the KCA and Norma oversees engineering, research, and countless technical projects, Corin is entrusted with the organization's finances.

Among KCA personnel, it is often joked that she is one of the most feared people in the entire authority.

Not because she carries a weapon.

But because she signs the budget approvals.

---

## **APPEARANCE**

Corin's appearance is immediately recognizable.

- Pale white skin.
    
- Hair of a very light, chalky lime color.
    
- Soft, subdued eyes.
    
- Calm expression.
    
- Professional attire, typically in muted colors.
    

Medical scans reveal almost no conventional signs of life despite her remaining fully conscious and functional.

Her body is sustained by **stabilized Ether**, rather than ordinary biological processes alone.

---

## **MEDICAL HISTORY**

Historical records indicate that Corin was also abducted as a child and subjected to unauthorized experimentation involving unstable Ether.

Unlike Norma...

Corin did not survive the experiments.

Her clinical death was recorded during the procedure.

At some point afterward, exposure to stabilized Ether unexpectedly restored bodily function.

Medical specialists are careful with their wording.

Corin is **not** considered undead in the supernatural sense.

Rather, she is regarded as an individual whose life was restored and is now continuously sustained by stabilized Ether.

This has left permanent physiological changes but no evidence of cognitive impairment.

---

## **PERSONALITY**

Despite everything she endured, Corin is remarkably gentle.

She speaks quietly.

Rarely raises her voice.

Prefers solving problems through discussion.

Her greatest source of stress is balancing the KCA budget.

She has become particularly well known for one phrase.

Whenever Norma submits an ammunition expenditure report, Corin can often be heard saying:

> "...Norma."

A pause.

> "...Please explain why Accounting received an invoice for another several hundred thousand Dennies."

Norma usually answers with an awkward smile.

---

## **RELATIONSHIP WITH HER SIBLINGS**

### **Norma**

The two sisters share an especially close bond.

They understand one another without needing many words.

Neither likes discussing the past.

Neither pressures the other to do so.

Instead, they focus on the present.

Cooking together.

Sharing tea.

Working late into the evening.

Sometimes simply enjoying each other's company.

---

### **Arcturus**

Arcturus remains as protective of Corin as he is of Norma.

He still refers to both of them as "the kids," despite all three now being adults.

Corin has never objected.

---

## **BLACKSITE REACTIONS**

### **Belle**

Belle immediately welcomed Corin with one of her trademark hugs.

Corin froze for a moment before quietly returning it.

> "...Thank you."

---

### **Endmin**

Endmin recognized the familiar look of someone carrying difficult memories.

She chose not to ask questions.

Instead, she simply made sure Corin knew she was welcome.

---

### **Neko**

After learning Corin worked in Accounting, Neko's first question was:

> "...Hypothetically..."

> "...How expensive is one experimental antimatter reactor?"

Corin looked over her glasses.

> "...Hypothetically?"

Neko slowly backed out of the room.

---

### **Miss Neko**

> "...She's going to fit in just fine."

---

### **Director Lucious**

After reviewing Corin's history, Director Lucious authorized unrestricted medical support.

His closing remark was brief.

> "No one here will ever be treated as a failed experiment."

> "Only as a person."

---

## **STATUS**

Corin continues to serve as Head of Accounting with distinction.

Her condition remains stable under regular medical observation.

Although stabilized Ether is responsible for sustaining her life, it has become an inseparable part of her physiology rather than an active illness.

She, like her siblings, bears scars from events that should never have happened.

Yet those who work with her know her not for her past, but for her patience, quiet kindness, and unwavering ability to keep the KCA's finances in order.

As one accountant jokingly wrote on an internal noticeboard:

> **"Norma spends the budget."**

> **"Corin somehow makes the numbers work."**

> **"Arcturus wonders why either of them are arguing."**
