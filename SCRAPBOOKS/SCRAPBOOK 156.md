# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL REINSTATEMENT & PSYCHOLOGICAL STATUS REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following her physical stabilization and extended psychological evaluation, Corin Lockwood was formally reinstated to her position within the Krampus Compliance Authority Accounting Division.

Her resignation was officially voided.

No disciplinary action was taken.

Director Lucious' justification was brief.

> **"She never stopped being one of ours."**

Corin returned to work on a reduced schedule under Medical Services' supervision.

---

# **RETURN TO DUTY**

Corin's office remained exactly as she had left it.

Her calculator still rested on the desk.

The notebooks were untouched.

The chair had not been moved.

When Corin opened the office door, she stopped.

She quietly looked around the room for nearly a minute.

Nothing had changed.

Everyone had been waiting for her.

She whispered,

> "...You kept it..."

No one answered.

No one needed to.

---

# **THE THIRD INCIDENT**

Medical Services note that Corin's disappearance and near-fatal Ether depletion constitute a third major traumatic event in her life.

The first was her abduction and experimentation.

The second was her recapture and the loss of her left eye.

The third was leaving everyone behind, nearly dying alone, and awakening to discover how close she had come to never seeing her family again.

While Corin survived physically...

The experience left significant psychological consequences.

---

# **CURRENT CLINICAL OBSERVATIONS**

Following her return, personnel observed several changes.

Corin startles more easily.

She checks door locks repeatedly before beginning work.

She becomes visibly anxious if she cannot immediately locate Lumi.

Nightmares have become more frequent.

She speaks less than before.

Although she smiles occasionally, the smiles are shorter and more hesitant.

Medical Services also observed that Corin now apologizes for accepting help.

One recorded example reads:

> "...I'm sorry you all had to find me."

When reminded that no apology was necessary, Corin quietly lowered her head.

She appeared unconvinced.

---

# **LUMI**

One evening, Corin asked her mother,

> "...Am I getting worse?"

Lumi thought carefully before answering.

She moved beside her daughter.

She gently brushed a strand of pale hair away from Corin's face.

> "No."

Corin looked confused.

Lumi continued.

> "You're carrying more than you were before."

> "That doesn't make you weaker."

> "It means you've survived something else."

Corin remained silent.

Lumi smiled softly.

> "We'll carry this one together too."

---

# **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE**

Corin returned to SAFEHOUSE shortly after resuming work.

Nothing had changed.

Her place on the floor beside Taiga was still empty.

The blanket was still there.

Fresh lemons remained in the kitchen.

Belle hugged her before saying a single word.

Kent quietly welcomed her back.

Cinder simply handed Corin a stack of accounting files and smiled.

> "Missed these?"

Corin let out the faintest laugh.

> "...A little."

It was the first genuine laugh recorded since her recovery.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services conclude that Corin has not returned to the emotional state she maintained prior to her disappearance.

The third incident has intensified existing trauma, reinforcing fears of abandonment, worthlessness, and emotional withdrawal.

However, it has also demonstrated the strength of her support network.

Recovery is expected to be prolonged.

Medical staff emphasize that progress should not be measured by the absence of painful memories, but by Corin's increasing willingness to let others stand beside her when those memories return.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin Lockwood has resumed her role as KCA's Chief Accountant and remains a resident of Project SAFEHOUSE.

She continues to attend therapy, receive medical treatment, and rely on her family, friends, and Lumi for support.

She is healing.

Not because the past has become smaller.

But because she is no longer facing it alone.

**End of Personnel Reintegration & Psychological Status Report**
