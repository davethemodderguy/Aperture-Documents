# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following his unexpected arrival at Heaven's Blacksite, Aries Akana has begun adapting to residential life alongside his younger brother, Jake, and their roommate, Clover.

Initial integration has been largely successful.

Aries has met several residents, including Jane Doe and Phoenix, and has demonstrated a willingness to cooperate with SAFEHOUSE personnel.

However, three notable discoveries have complicated the process.

1. Aries's spear is a manifestable anomalous weapon composed of a combination of **Star material and Hard Light**.
    
2. Aries possesses horns that Jake does not, despite their otherwise striking resemblance.
    
3. Aries nearly killed Jake after mistaking his teddy bear for an ordinary plush toy.
    

The third incident has been classified as a **critical near-fatal resident safety event**.

No fatalities occurred.

C.O.R.E. would like to emphasize that this is a relatively low bar for a successful family reunion.

# 1. WEAPON ASSESSMENT — ARIES'S SPEAR

## 1.1 Description

Aries's weapon is a spear constructed from a combination of two anomalous materials:

- **Star material**
    
- **Hard Light**
    

The weapon does not appear to be a conventional physical object carried continuously by Aries.

Instead, Aries can manifest and dismiss it at will.

When present, the spear possesses a luminous structure consistent with its anomalous composition. Its physical form appears stable enough to function as a conventional spear, but its underlying material properties remain insufficiently understood.

The weapon was observed during Aries's initial arrival at the Blacksite entrance.

At that time, Aries was under the mistaken impression that Aperture personnel had taken his younger brother against his will.

He subsequently raised the spear toward Director Lucious.

This prompted an armed response from Perimeter Security, M.S.A.C., and the Nine Tailed Fox operator on duty.

The confrontation ended when Jake arrived and identified Aries as his older brother.

Aries immediately dismissed the weapon.

No further hostile action was recorded.

## 1.2 Manifestation Capability

During a subsequent controlled observation, Aries demonstrated that he could summon the spear into his hand and dismiss it again without physically storing it anywhere.

The transition was effectively instantaneous.

No sheath, storage device, or external mechanism was identified.

The spear appears to be connected directly to Aries's anomalous nature.

Whether this represents matter creation, dimensional retrieval, or a distinct property of Star and Hard Light remains unknown.

C.O.R.E. has not authorized invasive experimentation.

Aries has been asked to demonstrate the weapon's basic behavior under controlled conditions, without aiming it at personnel.

He agreed.

**C.O.R.E. CLOSING REMARK:**

## 1.3 Security Restrictions

Until further assessment is completed:

- Aries may not manifest the spear in crowded residential areas.
    
- Weapon demonstrations must take place in an authorized testing area.
    
- The spear must not be manifested during arguments, medical emergencies, or surprise interactions.
    
- Personnel must not attempt to seize the weapon without a documented safety justification.
    
- Aries is not to be treated as hostile solely because he possesses an anomalous weapon.
    

The spear's ability to disappear does not make it harmless.

Equally, the spear's existence does not make Aries a threat by default.

**C.O.R.E. Assessment:** Aries has demonstrated both the capability to manifest a potentially dangerous weapon and the ability to dismiss it voluntarily. His initial confrontation with Director Lucious warrants continued security awareness, but his conduct following reunion with Jake supports provisional residential acceptance.

Aries was informed that Jake's current appearance was not a costume, disguise, or temporary recreational transformation.

It is Jake's actual physical form.

# 4. THE TEDDY BEAR INCIDENT

**INCIDENT CLASSIFICATION:** CRITICAL NEAR-FATAL RESIDENT SAFETY EVENT  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — RESIDENTIAL COMMON AREA  
**INVOLVED PERSONNEL:** Aries Akana, Jake Akana  
**INJURY STATUS:** No fatality; immediate medical assessment completed

## 4.1 Background

Aries was not initially aware of the full nature of Jake's teddy bear.

To an unfamiliar observer, the object resembles a conventional plush toy.

It is small, soft, and worn from repeated handling.

It is also Jake's cardiac vessel.

The teddy bear's heartbeat is Jake's actual heartbeat.

Its continued function is directly associated with Jake's physical stability.

The bear is not merely a comfort object.

It is not a decorative possession.

It is not interchangeable with another plush toy.

**It is a critical component of Jake's continued existence.**

Jake had not yet explained this distinction to Aries.

This omission would have been considerably less consequential if Aries had not attempted to remove what he believed was an ordinary stuffed animal from Jake's hands.

---

## 4.2 Incident Reconstruction

The incident occurred during the brothers' first evening together.

Jake was sitting on the couch with his teddy bear.

Aries approached and sat beside him.

For several minutes, they discussed the Blacksite, the SAFEHOUSE residents, and the circumstances surrounding their reunion.

Jake eventually became distracted by a book.

The teddy bear rested against his side.

Aries picked it up.

Jake immediately turned toward him.

**JAKE:**  
"Give him back."

Aries looked at the toy.

**ARIES:**  
"This?"

**JAKE:**  
"Yes."

Aries examined the worn fabric.

**ARIES:**  
"It's just a plush toy."

Jake's expression changed.

**JAKE:**  
"No."

Aries did not recognize the seriousness of the response.

He assumed Jake was attached to the toy and intended to tease him lightly.

**ARIES:**  
"You're six. You can survive five minutes without—"

He began to pull the bear away.

Jake reached for it.

Aries instinctively lifted it out of reach.

The moment the bear was pulled away from Jake's body, the situation changed.

Jake's physical response was immediate.

His movement faltered.

The azure light in his eyes flickered.

His breathing became irregular.

His hand went to his chest.

Aries froze.

**ARIES:**  
"Jake?"

Jake attempted to reach for the bear again.

His fingers missed.

He fell sideways against the couch.

The teddy bear's heartbeat had become audibly irregular.

Aries looked from the bear to Jake.

Then back to the bear.

**ARIES:**  
"Jake?"

There was no answer.

Jake's body began to lose stability.

Aries dropped to his knees beside him.

**ARIES:**  
"Jake! Hey—look at me!"

Jake did not respond.

Aries clutched the bear, suddenly understanding that something was catastrophically wrong.

He tried to return it to Jake's chest.

The movement was hurried and imprecise.

**ARIES:**  
"Wake up. Jake, wake up!"

Clover entered the room after hearing the disturbance.

She took one look at Jake and immediately called for medical assistance.

**CLOVER:**  
"Put the bear back against him. Now!"

Aries did so.

Clover knelt beside Jake, checking his responsiveness while calling Bao and the medical team.

**CLOVER:**  
"Aries, move back just enough to give us room."

Aries did not move.

He was staring at his younger brother.

**ARIES:**  
"What happened to him?"

Clover looked up at him.

Her voice was sharp, but not cruel.

**CLOVER:**  
"That bear is his heartbeat."

Aries went completely still.

**ARIES:**  
"What?"

**CLOVER:**  
"His actual heartbeat. It's not a normal toy."

Aries looked down at the bear in his hands.

The small rhythmic movement beneath the fabric was unmistakable.

**ARIES:**  
"I didn't know."

**CLOVER:**  
"I know. Just keep it with him."

The medical team arrived shortly afterward.

Bao took over the immediate medical response, with additional personnel assisting as needed.

Aries was moved away from the treatment area only when the responders required space.

He did not resist.

He did not attempt to leave.

He stood in the hallway, holding his own hands together and staring at the floor.

For the first time since his arrival, Aries appeared genuinely frightened of the place he had entered.

Not because its personnel were armed.

Not because its security systems were unfamiliar.

Because he had nearly lost his little brother within hours of finding him.

---

## 4.3 Medical Outcome

Jake's condition stabilized following the restoration of the teddy bear's proper position and the medical team's intervention.

The exact physiological sequence remains under review.

C.O.R.E. cannot conclude that the brief separation alone explains every aspect of the episode, and the incident should not be treated as evidence that any casual movement of the bear will inevitably cause a crisis.

However, the event established beyond reasonable doubt that **the teddy bear must be treated as a medically critical component of Jake's condition**.

The following restrictions are now in effect:

- The teddy bear must not be removed from Jake's possession without an established medical reason.
    
- Personnel must not use the bear to tease, distract, discipline, or surprise Jake.
    
- No resident may test the consequences of separating Jake from the bear.
    
- Anyone who accidentally displaces the bear must immediately alert medical personnel if Jake shows signs of distress.
    
- New residents must receive a clear explanation of the bear's function during their welfare orientation.
    

Aries was given the same explanation after the incident.

He listened without interruption.

When Bao finished, he quietly asked:

**ARIES:**  
"Is he going to be okay?"

Bao looked at him.

**BAO:**  
"He's stable right now. We're going to keep monitoring him."

Aries swallowed.

**ARIES:**  
"I did that."

Bao did not dismiss his responsibility.

Nor did she condemn him for something he had done without understanding the danger.

**BAO:**  
"You made a mistake because you didn't know. Now you know. What matters is what you do with that knowledge."

Aries looked toward Jake's room.

**ARIES:**  
"I almost killed him."

Bao remained beside him.

**BAO:**  
"And you stopped when you realized he was in danger. The medical team is taking care of him now. You can be sorry without deciding that you don't deserve to be his brother."

Aries did not answer.

Bao gave him time.

Eventually, he nodded.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING OBSERVATION

Aries arrived at the Blacksite expecting to fight an organization he believed had taken his little brother.

Instead, he found a residential facility full of people who had already made room for Jake.

Jane offered him a conversation.

Phoenix offered him a place beside the others.

Clover offered him a room, albeit one that now contained twice as many celestial problems.

Bao offered him an explanation when she could have offered only blame.

And Jake?

Jake offered him another chance.

Aries had forgotten how much his little brother had changed.

He had expected to find the same small star he remembered.

Instead, Jake had horns-free celestial features, feline ears, a tail, claws, a teddy bear that served as his cardiac vessel, and a collection of people he called family.

Aries still had much to learn.

He needed to learn the Blacksite's rules.

He needed to learn the residents' names.

He needed to learn that Jake's teddy bear was not an ordinary plush toy.

And he needed to learn that protecting his brother did not mean assuming he understood everything about him.

That evening, Aries sat beside Jake while his younger brother read.

The spear remained dismissed.

The teddy bear remained safely against Jake's chest.

Aries kept his hands to himself.

After several minutes, Jake glanced up.

**JAKE:**  
"You're quiet."

**ARIES:**  
"I'm thinking."

**JAKE:**  
"About what?"

Aries looked at him.

**ARIES:**  
"How much I missed."

Jake considered this.

Then he leaned against his brother's side.

**JAKE:**  
"You can learn."

Aries's expression softened.

**ARIES:**  
"Yeah?"

Jake nodded.

**JAKE:**  
"You're my big brother."

As though that explained everything.

Perhaps, to Jake, it did.

Aries gently rested his hand on Jake's shoulder.

He did not touch the bear.

He did not summon his spear.

He simply stayed.

For now, that was enough.
