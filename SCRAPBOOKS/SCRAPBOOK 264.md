# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **CAFETERIA SAFETY MEMO**

### **SUBJECT: WHY DIALYN IS NOT ALLOWED TO SEASON THE SOUP**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following multiple culinary incidents, the Cafeteria Committee has reaffirmed a long-standing recommendation:

> **Do not allow Dialyn to be solely responsible for seasoning communal meals.**

This recommendation exists for one simple reason.

Dialyn cannot taste salt.

To compensate...

She uses chili.

A lot of chili.

---

# **THE PROBLEM**

Since salt contributes essentially no flavor to Dialyn's perception, she unconsciously replaces that missing intensity with increasing amounts of chili flakes and peppers.

To her, the result tastes balanced.

To everyone else...

It is not.

---

# **DOCUMENTED INCIDENT**

During one community dinner, Dialyn volunteered to finish seasoning a large pot of vegetable soup.

When asked if it needed more salt, she answered confidently:

> "...I think it needs a little more flavor."

She proceeded to add a generous handful of chili flakes.

She tasted it.

Smiled.

> "...Perfect."

Medical staff later confirmed the soup contained a perfectly ordinary amount of sodium.

It also contained enough chili to make several experienced security officers tear up.

No injuries occurred.

Several egos did.

# **DIALYN'S RESPONSE**

After learning everyone found the soup overwhelmingly spicy, Dialyn looked genuinely surprised.

> "...Really?"

She tasted another spoonful.

> "...I thought it was kind of mild."

There was a long silence around the table.

Belle quietly took the ladle away.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Dialyn still enjoys helping in the kitchen.

She washes vegetables.

Sets the table.

Stirs the pot when asked.

However, the final seasoning is now handled by someone else.

Usually Bao.

Sometimes Belle.

Everyone agrees this arrangement is in the best interests of public health.

As the Cafeteria Committee's unofficial motto now states:

> **If Dialyn says it "just needs a little more flavor"...**

> **Hide the chili flakes.**
