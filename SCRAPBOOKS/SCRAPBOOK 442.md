# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CELESTIAL COMMUNICATION PROTOCOL**

# **GUIDING LIGHT — LETTER CORRESPONDENCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – CELESTIAL AUTHORITY  
**Designation:** **GUIDING LIGHT**  
**Role:** Celestial Advisor / Architect  
**Communication Status:** **OPEN — CORRESPONDENCE ACCEPTED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following Guiding Light's public introduction, personnel discovered that direct communication with her is possible.

No ritual is required.

No dimensional equipment is necessary.

No special clearance is needed.

Simply write her a letter.

---

# **HOW TO CONTACT GUIDING LIGHT**

Personnel wishing to communicate with Guiding Light are instructed to:

1. Write a letter addressed to **Guiding Light**.
    
2. Place the letter inside an envelope.
    
3. Draw or stamp a **moon symbol** on the envelope.
    
4. Leave the sealed envelope somewhere safe outside.
    
5. Wait.
    

The location does not appear to matter.

The envelope may be left:

- Outside a residence.
    
- On a windowsill.
    
- Beneath a tree.
    
- On a desk outside.
    
- In a designated correspondence box.
    

As long as the moon symbol is present, the letter can be received.

---

# **RESPONSE**

The original letter will eventually disappear.

No one has observed it being collected.

There are no footprints.

No visible manifestation.

No opening of doors.

The letter simply ceases to be where it was left.

At some later point, a reply will appear in its place.

The response is written in **blue ink**.

The handwriting is consistently attributed to Guiding Light.

---

# **EXAMPLE**

A personnel member once left a letter reading:

> **"Dear Guiding Light,**
> 
> **Are you actually watching everyone?**
> 
> **— A curious employee"**

The following morning, the envelope was gone.

A blue-ink response had appeared beneath the tree.

> **"Yes."**
> 
> **"But I don't stare."**
> 
> **"That would be rude."**
> 
> **— Guiding Light**
> 
> **☼ 🌙**

---

# **QUESTIONS**

The response:

> **"Guiding Light is sufficient."**

Another:

> **"Can we meet you?"**

> **"Not yet."**

Another:

> **"Why did you bring Orphie here?"**

The response was considerably longer.

> **"Because she deserved another chance."**
> 
> **"The rest is hers to decide."**

---

# **CORRESPONDENCE RULES**

Guiding Light has informally established several expectations.

### **1. Be Respectful**

She will answer sincere questions.

She is considerably less enthusiastic about deliberately provocative ones.

### **2. Do Not Expect Immediate Replies**

Responses may take seconds, hours, or days.

There is no established schedule.

### **3. Do Not Send Threats**

One attempted threat received a very short response:

> **"No."**

The letter never reappeared.

### **4. Do Not Ask Her to Solve Everything**

Guiding Light is willing to guide.

She is not interested in becoming everyone's personal problem solver.

### **5. Do Not Send Her Food**

This has happened multiple times.

Guiding Light has politely clarified:

> **"I appreciate the thought. I cannot eat."**

She eventually asked:

A blue note appeared.

> **"You were never special because you could read my notes."**

> **"You were special because you needed them."**

Orphie kept that message.

> 
> **But I'm glad she's here."**
> 
> **— Corin**

> **"So am I."**
> 
> **"Take care of each other."**
> 
> **— Guiding Light**
> 
> **☼ 🌙**

Corin kept the letter.

---

# **SECURITY NOTE**

Attempts to intercept Guiding Light's correspondence have failed.

If an envelope is addressed to Guiding Light and bears the moon symbol, its disappearance cannot be observed.

Personnel have attempted:

- Cameras.
    
- Motion sensors.
    
- Thermal imaging.
    
- Continuous observation.
    
- Physical traps.
    

Nothing has worked.

The letter simply disappears.

Then...

a reply appears.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Guiding Light may be one of the Architects responsible for forging reality itself.

She may possess authority beyond anything the Blacksite can properly measure.

Yet her preferred method of communication is remarkably simple.

A piece of paper.

An envelope.

A moon.

And a quiet place outside.

Write your question.

Leave it there.

She'll answer when she's ready.

**End of Celestial Communication Protocol**
