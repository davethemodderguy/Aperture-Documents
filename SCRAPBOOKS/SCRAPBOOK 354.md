## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELLNESS / DREAM EVENT**

# **COREY — WHITE VOID NIGHTMARE**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corey  
**Location:** Treehouse Residence  
**Associated Entities:** Z-335 (Marts), Corin

---

# **OVERVIEW**

During the early hours of the morning, Corey experienced an intense nightmare while sleeping inside the treehouse.

The event produced no detectable anomalous activity.

However, the emotional response was significant enough to wake her.

---

# **THE DREAM**

Corey found herself standing alone.

There was no sky.

No ground beyond a small island beneath her feet.

No trees.

No buildings.

No sound.

Everything beyond the island was an endless **white void**.

Corey looked around.

Then she saw them.

Her parents.

They stood several meters away.

Waiting.

Corey froze.

> "...Mom?"

No response.

> "...Dad?"

Nothing.

She began walking toward them.

Then running.

---

# **THE FALL**

Corey ran as fast as she could.

Her parents remained ahead of her.

She reached out.

> "...Wait!"

Her foot caught on something.

She tripped.

She fell.

The ground disappeared beneath her.

And—

---

# **WAKE**

Corey suddenly woke.

Her eyes opened.

The white void was gone.

The treehouse was still there.

The night was still dark.

She was still lying on the floor.

---

# **IMMEDIATE RESPONSE**

Corey remained completely still.

She looked around.

Corin was still asleep nearby.

Several Marts were scattered around the floor.

Others were still resting across Corey like a blanket.

No one appeared to have noticed.

Corey slowly wiped her eyes.

She took several deep breaths.

She did not want to wake anyone.

Especially Corin.

Especially the Marts.

---

# **THE MARTS NOTICE**

Despite Corey's efforts, one Mart eventually woke.

It lifted its head.

> "...Corey?"

Corey froze.

> "...Shh."

The Mart looked at her.

> "...Bad dream?"

She nodded.

The Mart quietly moved closer.

It did not ask anything else.

It simply rested against her arm.

---

# **THE SILENCE**

Corey stared into the darkness.

She tried to steady her breathing.

Another Mart woke.

Then another.

None spoke.

They slowly gathered around her.

Not enough to wake Corin.

Just enough that Corey could feel them there.

One gently adjusted its temperature.

Another settled across her shoulder.

A third quietly held onto her sleeve.

---

# **COREY'S WHISPER**

> "...Don't wake Corin."

A Mart nodded.

> "...Okay."

Corey looked down.

> "...Don't wake the others either."

Another Mart whispered:

> "...Okay."

The entire group became almost completely motionless.

---

# **THE AMBASSADOR**

Eventually, the Ambassador quietly arrived from another part of the treehouse.

It looked at Corey.

Then at the sleeping Corin.

Then back at Corey.

> "...Nightmare?"

Corey nodded.

The Ambassador sat beside her.

> "...Here."

Corey whispered:

> "...What?"

The Ambassador spread itself into Blanket Form.

The others followed.

Within seconds, Corey was once again surrounded by a warm layer of Marts.

---

# **CORIN**

Despite everyone's efforts, Corin eventually stirred.

Her eyes opened slightly.

She saw Corey sitting awake.

> "...Corey?"

Corey immediately whispered:

> "...Sorry."

Corin sat up.

> "...Why are you sorry?"

Corey hesitated.

> "...Bad dream."

Corin's expression softened.

She moved closer.

> "...You don't have to hide that from me."

Corey looked down.

> "...Didn't want to wake you."

Corin gently hugged her.

> "...I'd rather wake up."

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts quietly gathered around both of them.

One whispered:

> "...Safe."

Another:

> "...Here."

A third simply said:

> "...Home."

Corey closed her eyes.

She took another breath.

This time, it was steadier.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

There was no white void.

No distant island.

No parents waiting beyond reach.

Only the treehouse.

Corin beside her.

And dozens of tiny blue Marts quietly keeping watch.

Corey eventually lay back down.

The Marts returned to their places.

One settled on top of her head.

She smiled faintly.

> "...You know you're impossible to move when you're asleep, right?"

The Mart was already asleep.

Corin quietly laughed.

> "...Good night, Corey."

Corey closed her eyes.

> "...Good night."

This time, she slept.

**End of Personnel Wellness / Dream Event**
