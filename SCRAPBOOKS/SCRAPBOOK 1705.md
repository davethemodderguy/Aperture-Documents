# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A previously undocumented interaction between **Jake's tears** and **Clover Luminare's anomalous four-leaf clovers** has produced a permanent alteration.

When a tear from Jake comes into contact with one of Clover's anomalous four-leaf clovers, the clover undergoes immediate crystallization.

Unlike Clover's ordinary anomalous clovers, which remain effective for approximately five hours before wilting, the crystallized clover becomes:

> **PERMANENT.**

It does not wilt.

It does not lose its anomalous properties.

It does not require further maintenance from Clover.

The resulting object retains the appearance and structure of a four-leaf clover while becoming partially crystalline.

---

# 1. INITIAL OBSERVATION

The phenomenon was discovered unintentionally.

Clover was sitting with Jake in their shared room.

Jake had been crying.

One tear fell onto a four-leaf clover growing beside his blue manifested flowers.

Clover noticed the change almost immediately.

The clover stopped moving.

Its surface began to shimmer.

Within seconds, the entire plant had transformed into a translucent crystalline structure.

Clover stared.

Jake stared.

Neither spoke.

Clover eventually picked it up.

The clover did not wilt.

---

# 2. PERMANENCE

C.O.R.E. monitored the specimen.

After:

**1 hour:** unchanged.

**5 hours:** unchanged.

**24 hours:** unchanged.

**72 hours:** unchanged.

**1 week:** unchanged.

The anomalous luck effect also remained active.

This represents a significant departure from Clover's ordinary four-leaf clovers.

Standard Clover-grown clovers:

> **~5 hours → wilt → anomalous effect ends.**

Jake-crystallized clovers:

> **Permanent → no wilting → anomalous effect persists.**

---

# 3. ANOMALOUS INTERACTION

C.O.R.E. currently considers the phenomenon to be the result of two independent anomalous properties interacting.

### CLOVER

Provides:

**Localized beneficial luck.**

### JAKE

Provides:

**Crystalline transformation through tears.**

The resulting object combines characteristics of both.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

No attempt has been made to artificially reproduce the process.

---

# 4. CLOVER'S RESPONSE

Clover immediately recognized the significance.

She held the crystallized clover between two fingers.

She looked at Jake.

Then at the clover.

Then back at Jake.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "This one won't disappear."

Jake looked at it.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Good."

Clover smiled.

She placed it carefully inside her notebook.

She did not keep it there for long.

---

# 5. THE LUCY MERRYWEATHER VISIT

Clover was scheduled to visit **Lucy Merryweather** shortly afterward.

She brought the crystallized clover with her.

No formal presentation had been planned.

No KCA ceremony was involved.

Clover simply decided that she wanted Lucy to have it.

Upon meeting Lucy, Clover removed the clover from her notebook.

She held it out.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "I made this."

Lucy examined the crystalline four-leaf clover.

Clover continued:

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Well... Jake helped."

Lucy looked at the object carefully.

> **LUCY:**  
> "Is this one of your lucky clovers?"

Clover nodded.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "It's permanent."

Lucy paused.

She understood the significance immediately.

> **LUCY:**  
> "You're giving this to me?"

Clover nodded again.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Yes."

Lucy accepted it.

For several seconds, she simply looked at it.

---

# 6. LUCY'S RESPONSE

Lucy eventually smiled.

> **LUCY:**  
> "I'll treasure it."

Clover's expression softened.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Good."

She then pointed toward the clover.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Don't lose it."

Lucy laughed quietly.

> **LUCY:**  
> "I won't."

The crystalline clover was subsequently placed among Lucy's personal belongings.

It was not classified as a governmental resource.

It was not entered into KCA inventory.

It was treated as what Clover intended it to be:

> **A gift.**

---

# 7. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The permanent clover is currently considered unique.

C.O.R.E. has no evidence that Jake can intentionally produce the crystallization effect.

Likewise, Clover has not demonstrated an ability to force Jake's tears to interact with her clovers.

The process appears dependent upon direct contact.

Whether the resulting permanent clover can be produced repeatedly remains unknown.

No production program is authorized.

No commercial application is authorized.

No extraction or harvesting program is authorized.
