# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### RESIDENT WELFARE & ANOMALOUS RESEARCH DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-JAKE-259  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — CRITICAL RESIDENT WELFARE  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE — UNRESOLVED  
**SUBJECT:** Jake — Cessation of Stellar Visibility

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake is no longer visible in the sky.

The star previously identified as Jake's stellar manifestation has disappeared.

No transition was observed.

No atmospheric event occurred.

No spatial distortion was detected.

No blue rift opened.

The star simply ceased to be visible.

At present, C.O.R.E. cannot determine whether Jake:

- ceased existing;
    
- moved beyond observable space;
    
- concealed his stellar manifestation;
    
- entered another dimension;
    
- returned to the Grassland;
    
- returned to another location;
    
- or remains somewhere beyond current detection capability.
    

There is currently **no evidence sufficient to conclude that Jake is dead.**

There is also no evidence that he is safe.

---

# 1. INITIAL OBSERVATION

The absence was first noticed at approximately 23:41.

The sky was clear.

Astronomical observation systems were functioning normally.

The region where Jake's blue star had consistently appeared contained:

**Nothing.**

No blue light.

No stellar anomaly.

No unusual radiation signature.

No celestial displacement.

Just darkness.

---

# 2. CONFIRMATION

Neko initially assumed an observation-system failure.

Multiple instruments were checked.

All returned normal results.

Independent visual observation confirmed the same result.

The star was gone.

C.O.R.E. subsequently checked:

- astronomical tracking systems;
    
- anomalous energy sensors;
    
- Starlands observation channels;
    
- known rift signatures;
    
- Grassland access;
    
- SAFEHOUSE spatial sensors;
    
- Blacksite dimensional monitors.
    

Nothing identified Jake's current location.

---

# 3. SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE

The first residents to notice were those who had become accustomed to looking upward.

Bao went outside.

She looked at the sky.

Then looked again.

She remained there for several minutes.

No one asked her what she was looking for.

They already knew.

Nora eventually joined her.

She looked upward.

There was no blue star.

Nora said nothing.

Clover arrived carrying her candle.

She looked toward the same empty section of sky.

Her expression changed.

She lowered the candle.

---

# 4. BAO

Bao continued looking upward.

Eventually:

> **BAO:**  
> "He's not there."

No response.

Bao waited.

> **BAO:**  
> "He's really not there."

She did not cry.

She simply kept looking.

After several minutes:

> **BAO:**  
> "Jake?"

Nothing answered.

---

# 5. NORA

Nora searched the sky repeatedly.

She checked different windows.

Then the roof.

Then the courtyard.

Then the cafeteria windows.

As though changing her position might somehow reveal him.

It did not.

Eventually she asked:

> **NORA:**  
> "Can stars hide?"

Neko did not answer immediately.

> **NEKO:**  
> "Some anomalous entities can conceal their signatures."

Nora looked at him.

> **NORA:**  
> "But Jake isn't supposed to disappear."

Neko paused.

> **NEKO:**  
> "No."

Nora looked upward again.

> **NORA:**  
> "...Oh."

---

# 6. CLOVER

Clover did not ask whether Jake was dead.

She already knew that nobody could answer.

Instead, she took out her notebook.

She opened to the page containing the unfinished drawing of Jake.

The drawing still had no completed face.

Clover stared at it.

Then she looked at the sky.

Then back at the drawing.

She quietly added a small blue star above him.

She stopped.

After several seconds, she crossed it out.

---

# 7. JANE DOE

Jane arrived later.

She looked toward the sky.

Nothing.

She stood there for several seconds.

> **JANE:**  
> "So that's it?"

No one responded.

Jane folded her arms.

> **JANE:**  
> "We don't even know where he went."

Neko shook his head.

Jane looked upward again.

> **JANE:**  
> "Of course."

She smiled faintly.

It was not a happy smile.

> **JANE:**  
> "He finally got tired of waiting for us."

No one corrected her.

---

# 8. NERI

Neri was informed privately.

She did not immediately react.

She walked to the nearest window.

Looked upward.

Nothing.

She stood there for a long time.

Then she took out a small piece of paper.

She drew a tiny blue star.

She stared at it.

Then folded the paper in half.

Neri quietly said:

> **NERI:**  
> "...Jake."

That was all.

---

# 9. AETERNA

Aeterna was asked whether she could sense Jake.

She could not.

She stood outside for several minutes.

Her celestial eyes remained fixed on the empty sky.

Eventually:

> **AETERNA:**  
> "No."

Pause.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "I cannot find him."

This was considered significant.

Aeterna had previously been able to identify Jake's stellar presence.

She could no longer do so.

---

# 10. THE TEDDY BEAR

The teddy bear remains in SAFEHOUSE custody.

Its cardiac mechanism continues to function.

This has become the single strongest remaining physical indicator associated with Jake.

However, C.O.R.E. cannot establish whether the teddy's heartbeat represents:

- a continued connection to Jake;
    
- residual anomalous function;
    
- an independent property;
    
- or something else entirely.
    

The bear continues to beat.

Slowly.

Regularly.

Nobody has removed it from the common room.

---

# 11. THE GRASSLAND

L.S.I.N. conducted a non-invasive inspection of **LSIN-001 — THE GRASSLAND**.

Jake was not present.

The grassland remained stable.

The flowers remained physical.

The sky remained visible.

The anomalous space produced no indication of Jake's presence.

The elevator access point **U-01** remains operational.

No evidence currently suggests Jake entered the Grassland.

---

C.O.R.E. cannot currently answer the following:

**Is Jake alive?**

Unknown.

**Does Jake still exist?**

Unknown.

**Is Jake still a star?**

Unknown.

**Did Jake leave this reality?**

Unknown.

**Did Jake enter another dimension?**

Unknown.

**Can Jake return?**

Unknown.

**Does Jake know everyone is looking for him?**

Unknown.

**Does Jake want to be found?**

Unknown.

The absence of evidence is not being treated as evidence of death.

---

# 13. SEARCH POLICY

C.O.R.E. has authorized **no active dimensional pursuit.**

No forced rifts will be opened.

No uncontrolled searches will be conducted.

No attempts will be made to extract Jake from an unknown location.

This decision is based on one principle:

> **Jake has spent months believing that people only notice him when something is wrong.**

Turning his disappearance into a large-scale operation without knowing whether he wants to be found may repeat the exact pattern C.O.R.E. is attempting to prevent.

Passive observation remains authorized.

Active pursuit does not.

---

# 14. THE EMPTY CHAIR

The common room still contains the chair reserved during the full-moon period.

It remains empty.

Bao still leaves food there occasionally.

Nora still checks the cafeteria at night.

Clover still keeps her candle nearby.

Neri still leaves small drawings.

Nobody has formally ordered them to stop.

Nobody has formally ordered them to continue.

The routine simply remains.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING OBSERVATION

For months, there was at least one certainty.

Jake was somewhere above them.

Even when he stopped returning.

Even when he stopped speaking.

Even when nobody knew whether he was watching.

The blue star remained.

A small confirmation.

A quiet statement:

> **He is still here.**

Now even that is gone.

The sky contains no blue light.

No shooting stars.

No familiar glow.

Nothing marking the place where everyone had learned to look.

For the first time since Jake left the SAFEHOUSE, nobody can point upward and say:

> **"He's there."**

They can only look.

And wonder.

Perhaps he is somewhere.

Perhaps he is far away.

Perhaps he is hiding.

Perhaps he has gone somewhere that C.O.R.E. cannot reach.

Perhaps he simply decided that the sky was no longer somewhere he needed to be.

Nobody knows.

And for once, C.O.R.E. has no useful answer.

Only an empty sky.

And a teddy bear that still has a heartbeat.
