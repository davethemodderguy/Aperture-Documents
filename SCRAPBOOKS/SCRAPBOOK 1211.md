# 1. ESTABLISHMENT

An previously unused room located near the Aperture Armory has been repurposed into a permanent retail and display space for **Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise**.

The room is now informally known as:

> **THE TACHYON MERCH STORE**

The establishment was not originally included in the Blacksite architectural plan.

It appeared after Neri determined that repeatedly handling individual requests for Tachyon-compatible equipment was becoming inconvenient.

Rather than continuing to process requests through the Armory, Neri established a dedicated location.

C.O.R.E. has subsequently categorized the room as a **Limited Merchandise Retail & Display Area**.

---

# 2. OPERATING HOURS

The Tachyon Merch Store is open:

> **09:00 — 18:00**

Outside these hours, the room is closed to general personnel.

The store is not intended to operate as an unrestricted weapons-access room.

All purchases, transfers, and access to functioning merchandise remain subject to existing Aperture authorization requirements.

---

# 3. SHOPKEEPER

The shopkeeper is not permanently assigned.

Personnel operating the counter may vary according to availability.

However, the individual most frequently observed behind the counter is:

> **NORA TACHYON**

Nora has apparently decided that operating a merchandise store is an acceptable use of her free time.

C.O.R.E. has not determined whether this is because she enjoys retail work or because she enjoys telling people about her sister.

Evidence strongly supports the latter.

---

# 4. NORA'S COUNTER

When Nora is operating the store, the counter typically contains:

- a small register;
    
- order documentation;
    
- Tachyon Industries catalogues;
    
- accessory inventory records;
    
- ammunition inventory records;
    
- a stack of Nora's family journals;
    
- cake crumbs;
    
- occasionally a cup belonging to someone who was apparently supposed to be watching her.
    

The final item is not officially part of the store equipment.

---

# 5. AVAILABLE MERCHANDISE

The store specializes in accessories and compatible equipment for existing Tachyon Industries merchandise.

Available categories may include:

### HOLSTERS

Platform-compatible carrying equipment for applicable Tachyon Industries pistols.

---

### SUPPRESSORS

Compatible suppression equipment where supported by the individual platform.

Availability varies.

Not every Tachyon platform accepts every suppressor.

---

### ALTERNATE BARRELS

Platform-compatible barrel options and replacement components.

Certain configurations require Neri's approval before distribution.

---

### REPLACEMENT PARTS

Selected compatible components and replacement parts for Tachyon Industries merchandise.

These are intended for authorized owners and approved maintenance purposes.

---

### MAGAZINES

Compatible magazines for applicable Tachyon Industries platforms.

Availability varies by model.

---

### AMMUNITION

Approved Tachyon Industries ammunition is available where authorized.

Specialized ammunition remains subject to the same restrictions applied by the Aperture Armory.

---

# 6. ACCESSORY COMPATIBILITY

The store does **not** operate on the assumption that every Tachyon Industries product uses a universal attachment system.

Compatibility is platform-specific.

For this reason, customers are expected to identify the merchandise they own before requesting accessories.

Neri retains final authority over unusual compatibility requests.

A previous customer reportedly asked:

> **“Will this fit my Night Stalker?”**

Nora answered:

> **“Which one?”**

The customer pointed at the pistol.

Nora replied:

> **“No, which version?”**

The customer then discovered that Tachyon Industries merchandise has significantly more configuration complexity than initially expected.

---

# 7. DISPLAY WALL

The entire rear wall of the store has been converted into a display area.

Unlike ordinary showroom replicas, the displayed merchandise consists of **actual functioning Tachyon Industries equipment.**

Current display inventory includes:

### NIGHT STALKER

First-generation Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise.

Displayed as a representative example of the original Tachyon pistol program.

---

### CEBERUS

Second-generation Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise.

Displayed as the representative Tachyon shotgun platform.

---

### SLEIGHT OF HAND

The compact Tachyon Industries PDW platform.

Displayed as part of the first-generation Limited Merchandise collection.

---

### FSR DEADBOLT

The latest precision platform.

Only two Deadbolts exist.

The displayed unit is not considered general store inventory.

---

### DEADEYE

The Tachyon Industries pistol developed for the MSAC-oriented merchandise program.

Nora's personal Deadeye and the additional authorized units remain subject to their existing ownership restrictions.

---

# 8. DISPLAY SECURITY

The display wall is not a self-service weapons rack.

The firearms displayed behind the counter remain secured.

Personnel may request to inspect a displayed item where authorization permits.

Unauthorized removal remains prohibited.

The store therefore differs from the Armory in appearance but not in fundamental security requirements.

Nora has been specifically instructed:

> **Do not hand functioning merchandise to random people.**

Her response:

> **“I know.”**

Follow-up question:

> **“Do you?”**

Nora:

> **“Yes.”**

Pause.

> **“Probably.”**

Additional supervision was subsequently recommended.

# 10. CUSTOMER INTERACTION

Typical interaction with Nora follows an unusual pattern.

### CUSTOMER:

> “Do you have a suppressor for the Deadeye?”

### NORA:

> “Maybe.”

### CUSTOMER:

> “Can I buy one?”

### NORA:

> “Are you allowed to?”

### CUSTOMER:

> “Yes.”

### NORA:

> “Prove it.”

The customer presents authorization.

Nora examines it.

> **NORA:**  
> “Okay.”

She then retrieves the appropriate item.

The procedure is remarkably effective.

It is also significantly more informal than most Aperture procurement systems.

---

# 11. NORA AS SHOPKEEPER

Nora appears to take the role seriously.

When operating the store, she:

- checks customer authorization;
    
- identifies requested platforms;
    
- checks compatibility;
    
- retrieves merchandise;
    
- records transactions;
    
- asks Neri when uncertain;
    
- refuses requests that exceed her authority;
    
- explains the differences between Tachyon products with considerable enthusiasm.
    

The final behavior is the most common.

Customers have occasionally entered intending to purchase one accessory and left after receiving a fifteen-minute explanation about why Tachyon Industries has five different ammunition families.

---

# 12. NERI'S INVOLVEMENT

Neri does not permanently operate the counter.

She remains the primary designer and technical authority behind Tachyon Industries merchandise.

Unusual requests may therefore be referred to her.

Neri's standard response to complicated accessory requests is generally:

> **“Let me see.”**

She then examines the platform, accessory, and compatibility requirements.

If approved:

> **“Okay.”**

If rejected:

> **“No.”**

If particularly complicated:

> **“I'll make one.”**

This final response has caused several inventory-management concerns.

---

# 13. MERCHANDISE PHILOSOPHY

The Tachyon Merch Store is not intended to turn Tachyon Industries merchandise into mass-produced commercial equipment.

The limited-production philosophy remains unchanged.

The store exists primarily to provide:

- compatible accessories;
    
- replacement equipment;
    
- approved ammunition;
    
- authorized configuration options;
    
- maintenance-related parts;
    
- and a physical location where personnel can view existing Tachyon merchandise.
    

The actual limited-edition platforms remain scarce.

---

# 14. OBSERVED SAFEHOUSE REACTION

The store has quickly become a minor attraction.

Employees who previously knew Tachyon Industries only from documents can now walk into a room and see the actual merchandise displayed together.

The response has generally been:

> “Oh.”

followed by:

> “Wait, that's real?”

followed by:

> “How much?”

The final question is generally answered by:

> **“You can't just buy that one.”**

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The room was empty.

Now it has shelves.

The shelves contain accessories.

The back wall contains some of the rarest functioning equipment ever produced under the Tachyon Industries name.

And behind the counter, most days, sits a six-year-old Shark Thiren with a family journal clipped to her waist.

Nora has apparently taken to greeting customers with:

> **“Welcome to the Tachyon Merch Store!”**

Followed immediately by:

> **“Do you know what you want?”**

If the answer is yes, she helps.

If the answer is no, she begins explaining.

Personnel should budget accordingly.
