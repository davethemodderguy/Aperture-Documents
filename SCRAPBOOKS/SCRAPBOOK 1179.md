# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately 14:17, an unidentified rift manifested inside Neri Tachyon's room.

The rift was not detected by standard Blacksite perimeter systems.

It opened without warning.

A small humanoid figure fell through.

The figure was approximately six years old.

She possessed:

- a shark-like tail;
    
- clothing appropriate for a cold environment;
    
- neatly arranged hair;
    
- no immediately apparent injuries;
    
- and a familiar fish-skeleton hair clip.
    

Neri was present.

She did not initially understand what she was seeing.

The unidentified child opened her eyes.

She looked directly at Neri.

And said:

> **“Big sis!”**

---

# 1. INITIAL CONTACT

Neri was sitting on the floor of her room when the rift opened.

There was no warning.

No alarm.

No visible buildup.

Just a sudden distortion in the air.

A small figure fell through.

Neri stared.

The child landed awkwardly, recovered, and looked around.

Neri had not yet processed the situation.

The child opened her eyes.

Her expression immediately changed.

Recognition.

Joy.

Certainty.

She jumped toward Neri.

> **NORA:**  
> “Big sis!”

Neri instinctively caught her.

For approximately two seconds, she simply held the child.

Then she carefully lowered her onto the floor.

Neri stared at her.

Her expression was blank.

Overwhelmed.

She finally spoke.

> **NERI:**  
> “Who are you?”

The child blinked.

Then chuckled.

> **NORA:**  
> “Seriously, sis?”

She pointed at herself.

> **NORA:**  
> “You forgot.”

Neri remained completely still.

> **NORA:**  
> “Nora Tachyon.”

Pause.

The girl smiled.

> **NORA:**  
> “I'm your little sister, silly.”

Neri's breathing changed.

> **NERI:**  
> “Nora?”

> **NORA:**  
> “Yeah.”

> **NORA:**  
> “It's me.”

Neri stared at her.

She could not reconcile what she was seeing with what she remembered.

Nora had died.

Neri had watched it happen.

Nora was supposed to be dead.

Then Neri noticed something.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

For a long time, Neri was referred to as:

> **The Last Tachyon.**

She carried that title like an obligation.

Every medical emergency became another reminder.

Every warning became:

> _Don't die. You're the last one._

Every concern became another reason to wonder whether people loved **Neri**, or merely what Neri represented.

That designation is now obsolete.

Neri is not the last Tachyon.

She is not alone.

She is the eldest.

And somewhere in the middle of all the impossible things that happen at SAFEHOUSE, a six-year-old shark-tailed girl has apparently returned from the dead, fallen through a blue rift, met a talking blue moon, and walked directly back into her sister's life.

Neri spent years believing she had forgotten her sister's face.

She hadn't forgotten everything.

She remembered the hair clip.

And Nora remembered her.

For the first time since the Tachyon family was thought to have ended, the family tree has grown another branch.

Not an archive entry.

Not a recovered artifact.

Not a genetic sample.

A little sister.

Alive.

And calling her:

> **“Big sis.”**
