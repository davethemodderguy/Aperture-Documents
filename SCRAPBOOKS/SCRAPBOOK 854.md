# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. — SAFEHOUSE MEDICAL ARCHIVE

### COMPREHENSIVE PHYSIOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT

**File Designation:** SAFE-NERI-MED-005  
**Subject:** Neri  
**Species:** Seal Thiren — Artificially Created  
**Age:** 10  
**Classification:** Permanent SAFEHOUSE Resident  
**Medical Status:** CHRONIC / MANAGEABLE  
**Origin of Condition:** Childhood mistreatment + experimental Thiren transformation

---

# 1. OVERVIEW

Neri's current physiology is not considered representative of a naturally occurring Seal Thiren.

Her body has been extensively altered.

Some of these alterations appear to originate from the experimentation that transformed her into a Thiren.

Others appear to have developed from years of childhood neglect, deprivation, physical and emotional stress, inadequate care, and prolonged exposure to conditions unsuitable for a developing child.

The result is a body that is simultaneously:

- Highly capable in several areas.
    
- Abnormally adapted to certain forms of stress.
    
- Extremely vulnerable in others.
    
- Poorly understood by conventional medical science.
    

In short:

> **Neri survived things her body was never supposed to survive.**

---

# 2. CHILDHOOD DAMAGE

Neri's early life provided essentially none of the conditions required for healthy development.

She experienced prolonged neglect and mistreatment.

Basic needs were frequently disregarded.

Medical care was not reliably provided.

Her family treated her as though her continued existence was conditional on whether she was considered useful.

This appears to have had lasting physiological consequences.

The body of a developing child is particularly vulnerable to prolonged deprivation and stress.

Neri experienced both.

---

# 3. EXPERIMENTAL TRANSFORMATION

The later experimentation that transformed Neri into a Seal Thiren introduced an additional layer of damage.

Neri was not born a Thiren.

She was **made into one**.

The transformation appears to have been conducted under circumstances where her safety was not a priority.

The process altered multiple physiological systems simultaneously.

C.O.R.E. Medical has not been able to determine which abnormalities are:

- Original human physiology.
    
- Consequences of childhood neglect.
    
- Direct consequences of the transformation.
    
- Secondary adaptations.
    
- Anomalous effects introduced by the experiment.
    

Several systems appear to have been affected by more than one factor.

---

# 4. CARDIOVASCULAR SYSTEM

The most immediately concerning abnormality is Neri's cardiovascular stability.

Her heart can function normally under ordinary circumstances.

Under sufficiently high stress, however, its performance can deteriorate rapidly.

Triggers include:

- Severe emotional distress.
    
- Extreme physical exertion.
    
- Prolonged combat.
    
- Excessive stamina expenditure.
    
- Potential combinations of physical and psychological stress.
    

In severe cases, Neri can lose consciousness.

Her heart may enter a state described in medical records as a **systemic cardiovascular crash**.

The exact mechanism remains unclear.

It is suspected to be the combined consequence of developmental damage and the artificial transformation.

---

# 5. WHY NERI CAN STILL FIGHT

This has caused considerable confusion among personnel.

Neri can be extraordinarily effective in short bursts.

She possesses:

- Exceptional mobility.
    
- High reflexive response.
    
- Excellent close-combat ability.
    
- Significant pain tolerance.
    
- Air-jumping capability.
    
- Strong adaptation to physical danger.
    

None of these indicate that her cardiovascular system is healthy.

Her body appears to have developed an unusual strategy:

> **It can perform far beyond what it can safely sustain.**

This makes Neri particularly dangerous to herself.

She can continue functioning long after she should have stopped.

Then, once her reserves are exhausted, her body may abruptly collapse.

---

# 6. BLOOD DEPENDENCY

Neri's requirement for supplemental blood is believed to be another consequence of her altered physiology.

Her body periodically appears unable to maintain optimal blood-related physiological function without external supplementation.

The precise mechanism is unknown.

The requirement is unusual enough that no natural Seal Thiren equivalent has yet been identified.

---

# 7. BAO COMPATIBILITY

Bao's blood is uniquely compatible with Neri.

No satisfactory explanation has been established.

Neri's body accepts it without significant complications, and Bao can safely provide small quantities without meaningful physiological consequences.

The current working theory is that some aspect of their Thiren physiology produces an unusually compatible biological profile.

The possibility that the compatibility is specifically related to Neri's artificial transformation remains under investigation.

For practical purposes:

> **Bao's blood works.**

That is currently the most important fact.

---

# 8. ENERGY METABOLISM

Neri also demonstrates unusual responses to exhaustion.

Rapidly available energy, particularly sugar, can produce noticeable improvement when she is approaching an energy-related collapse.

This is one reason SAFEHOUSE personnel are encouraged to keep suitable high-energy foods available.

Neri already enjoys sweets considerably.

This creates an unusual overlap between medical necessity and personal preference.

> **Medical staff:** "She needs sugar."

> **Neri:** "I need cake?"

> **Medical staff:** "Essentially."

> **Neri:** "Okay."

---

# 9. THE ARTIFICIAL THIREN PROBLEM

Neri is currently the first confirmed artificial Thiren documented by Blacksite records.

This makes her physiology historically significant.

Unfortunately, it also means there is no reliable baseline.

There is no large population of naturally occurring artificial Seal Thirens against which her physiology can be compared.

Consequently, when an abnormality is discovered, medical personnel cannot simply ask:

> "Is this normal for her species?"

There is no established answer.

Instead, the question becomes:

> "Is this normal for **Neri**?"

---

# 10. SURVIVAL ADAPTATION

Some of Neri's unusual capabilities may themselves be adaptations.

Her body spent years operating under conditions where failure had consequences.

Her nervous system appears exceptionally responsive to immediate threats.

Her physical behavior is optimized around rapid movement and close-range engagement.

Her pain and exhaustion responses are similarly unusual.

However, adaptation should not be mistaken for health.

Neri's body is not "strong because it survived."

It is better described as:

> **A damaged system that learned how to keep functioning.**

---

# 11. MEDICAL PHILOSOPHY

C.O.R.E. Medical has established a standing principle regarding Neri:

### Do not attempt to force her body to become something it cannot safely be.

The objective is not to make Neri capable of unlimited exertion.

The objective is to allow her to live normally while minimizing the circumstances that cause her systems to fail.

This includes:

- Regular meals.
    
- Adequate rest.
    
- Monitoring after strenuous activity.
    
- Avoiding unnecessary stress.
    
- Supplemental blood when required.
    
- Rapid access to Bao.
    
- Immediate response to fainting.
    
- Ensuring Neri does not interpret medical limitations as evidence that she is "expendable."
    

The last point is considered medically relevant.

---

# 12. PSYCHOLOGICAL AND PHYSIOLOGICAL INTERACTION

Neri's physical and psychological conditions cannot be considered completely separate.

Her history has made her extremely sensitive to the possibility of losing people she cares about.

When someone she considers a friend is threatened, Neri may disregard her own physical limitations.

This can create a dangerous cycle:

**Perceived threat**

↓

**Extreme emotional stress**

↓

**Protective behavior**

↓

**Excessive physical exertion**

↓

**Cardiovascular strain**

↓

**Collapse**

The SAFEHOUSE therefore considers protecting Neri's friends **and** preventing Neri from destroying herself while protecting them to be equally important.

---

# 13. WHAT NERI IS NOT

Neri is not:

- Naturally fragile in every respect.
    
- Permanently bedridden.
    
- Incapable of physical activity.
    
- A defective Thiren.
    
- A failed experiment.
    
- An expendable asset.
    

The final classification is particularly important.

Her family may have treated her that way.

The SPAWN extremist who experimented on her may have treated her that way.

**Aperture does not.**

---

She is a child who survived it.
