# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CRITICAL INCIDENT / ETHER MEDICAL RECORD**

# **CORIN — THE BODY THAT REFUSED**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Architect-Sealed  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Status:** **SURVIVED / PSYCHOLOGICAL CRISIS**

---

# **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

Corin attempted to end her own existence.

The attempt failed.

Not because she was discovered in time.

Not because conventional medical intervention saved her.

Her body simply **did not respond to the attempt in the way a living body would.**

Corin was already dead.

Her reanimated physiology left the substance incapable of producing the ordinary fatal biological outcome she had expected.

The Ether simply continued maintaining her.

---

# **DISCOVERY**

Orphie found Corin shortly afterward.

Corin was conscious.

Weak.

Confused.

And visibly devastated by the fact that she was still there.

She looked at Orphie.

> **Corin:**  
> "...It didn't work."

Orphie immediately understood what she meant.

She sat beside her.

> **Orphie:**  
> "Corin..."

> **Corin:**  
> "I'm already dead."

She looked down at her hands.

> **"Why won't it let me stay dead?"**

---

# **THE ETHER RESPONSE**

Corin's body showed no conventional signs of poisoning-related biological failure.

Her Ether had effectively bypassed the problem.

Because her body no longer depends upon ordinary:

- Circulation.
    
- Organ function.
    
- Metabolism.
    
- Blood chemistry.
    

the substance had little meaningful biological system to destroy.

The Ether continued preserving the existing structure.

In the most disturbing sense possible:

> **There was almost nothing left for the poison to kill.**

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

The failure did not reassure her.

It made her cry.

She had hoped that, for once, her body would stop fighting her.

Instead, it demonstrated exactly how little control she had over it.

> **Corin:**  
> "I can't even do that."

> **"My body won't listen to me."**

She pulled her scarf tightly around herself.

> **"I don't want to be trapped in this thing forever."**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie remained with her.

She did not scold Corin.

She did not call her selfish.

She did not tell her that she had made everyone worried.

She simply held her.

> **Orphie:**  
> "You're not a thing."

Corin shook her head.

> **"Look at me."**

Orphie did.

> **"You're Corin."**

Corin's expression broke.

She leaned against Orphie and cried.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light arrived shortly afterward.

For once, she had no grand solution.

She could not simply erase Corin's condition.

She could not restore the body she once had.

And she would not force Corin into another miraculous transformation.

She knelt beside her.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "I'm not going to pretend I understand how much this hurts."

Corin looked at her.

> **"Then why do you keep making me stay?"**

Guiding Light was silent for several seconds.

> **"Because I care about you."**

> **"But I know now that caring about you cannot mean deciding what you must do with your life."**

Corin stared at the floor.

---

# **NEW SAFETY MEASURES**

Following the incident, the treehouse received additional quiet safeguards.

These measures were designed around **protection rather than confinement**.

Corin is not considered a hostile entity.

She is not to be restrained unless absolutely necessary.

Personnel are instructed to prioritize:

- Continuous companionship during severe episodes.
    
- Removal of hazardous materials from unsupervised areas.
    
- Emotional support.
    
- Corin's personal autonomy.
    
- Immediate Architect intervention when her Ether becomes dangerously unstable.
    

Curious Light has also formally suspended all research that could worsen Corin's psychological state.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin had spent so long believing that her body was a prison.

Today, it proved her right in the cruelest possible way.

She attempted to escape.

Her body refused.

Not because it loved her.

Not because it understood her.

Not because it had made a conscious choice.

It simply continued doing what it had been reconstructed to do:

**remain.**

And Corin is left with the question she has been asking for a long time:

> **If her body is going to keep existing, can she eventually find a reason to exist with it?**

No one can answer that for her.

For now, Orphie stays beside her.

Guiding Light watches over her.

And Corin remains alive in the only sense her strange existence still permits:

**not because she knows why yet, but because she is still here.**

**End of Critical Incident / Ether Medical Record**
