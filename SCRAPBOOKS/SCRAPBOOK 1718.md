# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately 02:17, an armed group of **fifteen specialist contractors** breached the Blacksite perimeter.

The group was not affiliated with SPAWN.

They were not independent mercenaries.

They were **government contractors**, privately hired through a deniable intermediary and subsequently bribed to conduct an unauthorized operation against Aperture property.

Their objective was specific:

> **Locate and secure Jake.**

The contractors possessed sufficient intelligence to identify Jake as a high-value anomalous resident.

They did not possess sufficient intelligence to understand what Jake actually was.

This distinction proved decisive.

The raid failed.

The contractors were incapacitated without fatalities.

The operation was terminated primarily through resident defensive action.

Jake himself played a significant role.

His contribution consisted of approximately **one improvised device** and one sentence:

> **"Shut your eyes and cover your ears."**

---

# 1. BREACH

The contractors entered through a compromised perimeter access route.

Security personnel detected the intrusion almost immediately.

The group moved with professional coordination.

Their equipment suggested extensive preparation.

C.O.R.E. analysis later confirmed that the contractors had been provided with:

- Blacksite floor information;
    
- resident identification data;
    
- approximate SAFEHOUSE access information;
    
- photographs of Jake;
    
- information regarding his anomalous capabilities;
    
- and instructions to prioritize his capture over all other objectives.
    

The intelligence package was incomplete.

Critically, it omitted several facts.

Among them:

**Jake is a literal star.**

**Jake can manipulate anomalous blue dust.**

**Jake is extremely difficult to surprise.**

**Jake can parry.**

**Jake is very, very good at improvisation.**

---

# 2. INITIAL CONTACT

The contractors reached an interior corridor.

Several Blacksite guards were already moving to intercept them.

Jake happened to be nearby.

He was carrying his teddy bear.

He stopped.

Looked at the armed group.

Then looked at the guards.

Then looked at the object he was holding.

Jake had apparently remembered something.

He approached one of the Blacksite equipment areas and retrieved an **empty flashbang shell**.

No conventional explosive charge was added.

Instead, Jake used a small quantity of his anomalous blue dust.

He shook the improvised device once.

Then looked toward the nearby guards.

> **JAKE:**  
> **"Shut your eyes and cover your ears."**

The guards immediately obeyed.

One guard later described the moment as:

> "The most casually delivered warning I've ever received in my career."

---

# 3. ACTIVATION

Jake activated the improvised device.

The resulting anomalous discharge was substantially different from a conventional flashbang.

There was:

- no conventional explosive injury;
    
- no penetrating fragmentation;
    
- no lethal blast;
    
- and no sustained thermal effect.
    

Instead, the blue dust dispersed into a rapidly expanding anomalous sensory field.

The fifteen contractors were caught within it.

The effect was immediate.

---

# 4. CONTRACTOR RESPONSE

The first visible symptom was ocular distortion.

The contractors' vision reportedly fractured into multiple overlapping color patterns.

Witnesses described the effect as resembling a **kaleidoscope**.

The phenomenon did not appear to permanently damage their eyes.

It did, however, make normal visual processing extremely difficult.

Depth perception became unreliable.

Movement appeared duplicated.

Hallways appeared to shift.

Straight lines appeared curved.

Several contractors attempted to orient themselves using walls.

This did not work.

---

# 5. DIRECTIONAL DISORIENTATION

The second effect was considerably more problematic.

The contractors appeared to lose reliable internal directional reference.

Several attempted to move toward what they believed was the exit.

They instead walked toward the wrong corridor.

One turned around repeatedly.

Another attempted to follow a teammate and walked directly into a wall.

A third confidently announced:

> **"The exit is that way."**

He pointed toward a storage closet.

It was not the exit.

It was not even near the exit.

---

# 6. THE HALOS

The most visually absurd symptom appeared approximately thirty seconds later.

Each affected contractor developed a small, glowing **star-shaped halo** above their head.

The halos rotated slowly.

They resembled the visual effect traditionally associated with cartoon characters receiving a concussion.

C.O.R.E. medical personnel initially believed this was metaphorical.

It was not.

The halos were physically visible.

They also appeared to rotate at different speeds depending on the severity of each individual's disorientation.

One contractor reportedly attempted to swat his halo away.

His hand passed through it.

He tried again.

It remained.

---

# 7. SPECIALIST TEAM FAILURE

The group had entered the Blacksite as fifteen trained specialists.

Within minutes:

**15 specialists → 15 severely disoriented individuals.**

Their training remained intact.

Their equipment remained functional.

Their physical strength remained largely unaffected.

Their ability to understand the situation did not.

Their ability to determine **where they were** had become catastrophically unreliable.

This created an unusual tactical failure.

The contractors knew exactly what they were supposed to do.

They simply could not agree on:

**where anything was.**

---

# 8. JAKE

Jake watched from behind the security personnel.

One guard looked at him.

> **GUARD:**  
> "Did you do that?"

Jake nodded.

> **GUARD:**  
> "Why?"

Jake pointed toward the contractors.

> **JAKE:**  
> "They were coming for me."

The guard looked at the fifteen specialists stumbling around the corridor.

Several were attempting to determine which direction was "forward."

> **GUARD:**  
> "...Fair."

Jake picked up his teddy bear.

---

# 9. ATTEMPTED RECOVERY

The contractors attempted to regroup.

This proved unsuccessful.

One specialist attempted to issue commands.

> **SPECIALIST:**  
> "Everyone form up!"

Three people moved left.

Four moved right.

Two remained stationary.

One walked backward.

Another sat down because the floor appeared to be moving.

The team leader eventually abandoned verbal coordination.

This was not an effective improvement.

---

# 10. RESIDENT SECURITY RESPONSE

Once the anomalous field had subsided sufficiently, Blacksite security personnel moved in.

The contractors were disarmed.

Restrained.

Medically evaluated.

And transferred to secure holding.

No contractor was killed.

Several sustained minor injuries from collisions with walls, floors, furniture, and one another.

The primary medical concern was the severity of the sensory disorientation.

All fifteen eventually stabilized.

The star halos gradually disappeared.

The kaleidoscopic vision resolved.

Their sense of direction returned.

Their dignity did not.

---

# 11. JAKE'S POST-INCIDENT BEHAVIOR

After the contractors had been secured, Jake walked over to the nearest guard.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Are they okay?"

The guard confirmed that they were alive and receiving medical attention.

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Good."

He then asked:

> **JAKE:**  
> "Can I have breakfast?"

The guard stared at him.

> **GUARD:**  
> "...Yes."

Jake left.

---

# 12. CONTRACTOR INTELLIGENCE REVIEW

C.O.R.E. recovered partial mission documentation from the contractors.

The objective was explicitly stated:

> **PRIMARY OBJECTIVE:**  
> Locate anomalous child designated JAKE.

> **SECONDARY OBJECTIVE:**  
> Secure subject alive.

> **TERTIARY OBJECTIVE:**  
> Withdraw before Aperture response escalation.

All three objectives failed.

The contractors did locate Jake.

They did not secure Jake.

They did not withdraw on their own terms.

They were instead carried out of the Blacksite under guard supervision.

---

# 13. THE BRIBE

Financial investigation confirmed that the contractors had been offered substantial compensation to participate.

The sponsoring party had deliberately concealed the full nature of the target.

The contractors were told that Jake was a valuable anomalous individual.

They were not adequately informed that the target was:

- a literal star;
    
- capable of anomalous energy manipulation;
    
- protected by an armed anomalous community;
    
- capable of defensive combat;
    
- and apparently capable of turning a discarded piece of equipment into a highly effective sensory-disruption device.
    

C.O.R.E. considers this a significant intelligence failure.

---

# 14. MEDICAL OBSERVATION

The contractors were not permanently blinded.

No permanent neurological damage was identified.

However, the sensory effects were severe enough to warrant continued observation.

The affected personnel reported:

- fragmented vision;
    
- incorrect perception of distance;
    
- temporary loss of directional confidence;
    
- dizziness;
    
- nausea;
    
- difficulty determining orientation;
    
- persistent visual afterimages;
    
- and, in several cases, the sensation that the room was rotating.
    

One contractor described the experience as:

> "Being trapped inside a kaleidoscope while gravity forgot which way was down."

Another simply stated:

> "I hate stars now."

---

# 15. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The incident demonstrates several important points.

### 1. Jake is not helpless.

Despite his appearance and age, Jake is capable of defending himself and others.

### 2. Jake prefers non-lethal solutions.

He did not attempt to kill the contractors.

His improvised response incapacitated them through temporary sensory disruption.

### 3. Jake remains extremely unpredictable.

His use of anomalous blue dust in an improvised device was not previously documented.

### 4. Jake responds rapidly to threats against the Blacksite.

He did not wait for someone else to solve the problem.

### 5. Jake's carefree personality should not be mistaken for incompetence.

He can spend an afternoon drawing flowers.

He can ask for cake.

He can sit on Clover's lap while she works.

And when armed personnel come looking for him—

He can apparently turn fifteen specialists into confused cartoon characters.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The contractors came to the Blacksite expecting to capture a child.

They encountered:

**A child.**

A child who was carrying a teddy bear.

A child who was apparently more interested in avoiding breakfast being interrupted than in the invasion itself.

A child who looked at fifteen armed specialists and decided that killing them would be unnecessary.

So he found something useful.

Filled it with blue dust.

Warned the guards.

And turned the entire specialist team into a walking collection of spinning halos and confused directions.

The contractors were eventually secured without fatalities.

Jake suffered no injuries.

The Blacksite suffered no significant structural damage.

The only confirmed permanent consequence of the encounter appears to be psychological.

One contractor has reportedly refused to look at a kaleidoscope since.

Jake, meanwhile, finished breakfast.
