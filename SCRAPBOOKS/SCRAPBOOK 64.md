# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **MEDICAL HISTORY ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: NORMA LOCKWOOD – WING DEVELOPMENT HISTORY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **OVERVIEW**

Following a comprehensive medical review, Blacksite physicians concluded that **Norma Lockwood's wings were not present at birth**.

Instead, they developed gradually over many years after the childhood experimentation that resulted in her former Ether Regression Syndrome.

Medical analysis indicates the growth was slow, continuous, and accompanied by recurring pain during development.

---

## **MEDICAL FINDINGS**

Evidence suggests the wing growth began shortly after Norma was rescued by her older brother, **Arcturus Lockwood**.

Initially, the changes were subtle.

- Persistent soreness across the upper back.
    
- Unexplained stiffness around the shoulder blades.
    
- Occasional low-grade fevers during periods of rapid growth.
    
- Gradual changes to muscle and skeletal structure.
    

As the years passed, the wings continued to develop beneath the skin and surrounding tissue before eventually emerging.

Medical staff believe the process took many years to complete.

---

## **A SILENT BURDEN**

What surprised everyone was not the medical findings.

It was how successfully Norma had hidden them.

Arcturus stared at the report in disbelief.

> "...All this time?"

Norma gave a small nod.

> "...I didn't want anyone worrying."

---

She explained that whenever the pain became particularly severe, she would simply excuse herself.

She attributed it to muscle strain from work.

Long nights.

Stress.

Anything except the truth.

She continued attending school.

She continued working.

She continued helping others.

All while quietly enduring the discomfort of a body that was slowly changing.

---

## **ARCTURUS' RECOLLECTION**

After reading the report, Arcturus leaned back in his chair.

For several minutes, he said nothing.

Then, almost to himself:

> "...You always complained that your back hurt."

Norma looked down.

> "...Yeah."

He frowned.

> "...I thought it was from carrying too many books."

Norma managed a faint smile.

> "...I let you think that."

Arcturus removed his glasses.

His voice softened.

> "...You hid it for years."

Norma nodded once.

> "...I didn't want my big brother to have another thing to worry about."

The room fell quiet.

---

## **BELLE'S OBSERVATION**

Belle had always noticed that Norma instinctively protected her back.

She had assumed it was a habit left over from the lingering effects of ERS.

Now it made sense.

> "You weren't protecting an injury."

Belle said quietly.

> "...You were protecting wings that hadn't fully grown yet."

Norma simply nodded.

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Although the growth process has concluded, physicians believe the surrounding muscles and joints occasionally retain residual discomfort, particularly after prolonged flight or strenuous activity.

Norma has adapted remarkably well.

Her flight is now exceptionally refined, suggesting years of unconscious adjustment to the gradual changes in her body.

## **STATUS**

Norma's wings are now fully developed and function normally.

She no longer attempts to hide their existence from those around her, though she still habitually keeps them folded against her back unless they are needed.

Looking back, many of her friends realized they had mistaken years of quiet discomfort for ordinary fatigue.

None of them had imagined she had been living through the slow, painful growth of a pair of wings.

When Arcturus was asked afterward if he felt guilty for never realizing what was happening, he looked toward his sister before answering.

> "...No."

He smiled warmly.

> "...I'm just glad she's finally reached the point where she doesn't have to hide anymore."

Norma returned the smile.

For the first time in many years...

She believed that, too.
