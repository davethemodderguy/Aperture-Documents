# SAFEHOUSE CRITICAL INCIDENT REPORT

## SUBJECT: NERI

### INCIDENT: SEALED ROOM / PERSONAL FAREWELL NOTICE

**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL — CRITICAL  
**Subject:** Neri  
**Location:** Neri's personal subspace  
**Status:** Room recovered; subject absent  
**Concern Level:** SEVERE

---

# 1. THE NOTICE

A handwritten sign was discovered taped directly outside Neri's door.

The handwriting was unmistakably hers.

It read:

> **DON'T NEED YOU TO OPEN THIS DOOR, I'M FINE WHERE I AM.**
> 
> **TAKE CARE OF SOMEONE ELSE**
> 
> **Bao, I'm not your daughter. Go take care of Frost**
> 
> **Jane, those days at Woodland is rough, just focus on yourself, I'm fine. That conversation will never leave between us**
> 
> **Alice, I'm sorry I can't hang out with you, go somewhere else, everyone here is friendly, just have fun with them**
> 
> **Star, no need to worry, I'm fine, just go annoy Nightshade or give fun to other people**

There was no signature.

There was no date.

There was no request for assistance.

There was no explanation.

---

# 2. IMMEDIATE REACTION

Bao was the first person to read the entire notice.

She read it once.

Then again.

Then a third time.

She stopped at:

> **"Bao, I'm not your daughter."**

Bao did not respond immediately.

She simply stared at the paper.

Jane subsequently arrived and read her section.

Her expression changed immediately upon reaching:

> **"That conversation will never leave between us."**

Jane understood what Neri was referring to.

The private conversation between them.

The trust Neri had placed in her.

Jane had never intended for Neri to interpret that conversation as a reason to leave.

Alice's section caused a different reaction.

She read it twice.

Then quietly asked:

> **"Why is she telling me to go have fun with everyone else?"**

Star said nothing for several seconds.

Then:

> **"She's telling us goodbye."**

Nobody corrected her.

---

# 3. THE DOOR IS OPENED

The decision was made to open the room.

The expectation was that Neri would be inside.

Her subspace had previously consisted of floating islands, rope bridges, artificial stars, localized gravity, and numerous personal areas.

That space was no longer present.

The door opened normally.

Beyond it was an ordinary room.

Four walls.

A floor.

A ceiling.

A bed.

A desk.

A chair.

Nothing else.

No floating islands.

No rope bridges.

No artificial stars.

No fractured architecture.

No connected subspaces.

No unusual gravitational zones.

No visible Light manifestations.

Nothing anomalous.

It was simply a room.

---

# 4. THE EMPTY ROOM

Neri was not there.

Neither was there evidence that she had recently left through a conventional exit.

Her belongings were mostly present.

Her chemistry books remained.

Her coat was not on the bed.

Her cake was absent.

No note was found inside.

The room had been returned to an ordinary state so completely that it was difficult to believe it had ever contained a subspace.

This was the most concerning part.

Neri had not merely locked herself away.

She had **closed the world she had built for herself.**

---

# 5. THE SIGN WAS NOT A REQUEST

Personnel initially considered whether the sign was intended to prevent people from disturbing Neri.

That interpretation was rejected.

The language was too specific.

Neri addressed individual people.

She gave each of them somewhere else to direct their attention.

Bao → Frost.

Jane → herself.

Alice → other friends.

Star → Nightshade.

Every person Neri considered important was being told:

**You don't need me.**

And more importantly:

**You shouldn't spend your time looking for me.**

---

# 6. THE OLD MEMORY

The recovered childhood memory is now considered directly relevant.

Neri previously recalled:

> **"If you attract too much attention to myself, I'll be too much of a trouble for people to keep around, and I'll get thrown."**

Her recent behavior followed that belief.

She stopped eating sweets.

She stopped reading chemistry.

She stopped socializing.

She stopped asking for help.

She stopped being seen.

Now she had taken the final step.

She had explicitly instructed everyone who cared about her to care about somebody else instead.

---

# 7. THE MOST DISTURBING SENTENCE

The sentence addressed to Bao is receiving particular attention:

> **"I'm not your daughter."**

This does not appear to be a factual correction.

It appears to be an attempt to **reject the relationship before Bao can reject her.**

If Neri believes she has become a burden, then removing herself from the role of "daughter" may seem like an act of kindness.

She may believe:

> _If I'm not your daughter, you don't have to worry about me._

The same pattern appears throughout the notice.

> **Jane:** You have your own problems.

> **Alice:** Go have fun with everyone else.

> **Star:** Go spend time with Nightshade.

Neri is systematically removing herself from other people's priorities.

---

# 8. JANE'S INTERPRETATION

Jane remained outside the room after everyone else had left.

She looked at the section addressed to her.

Then she said quietly:

> **JANE:**  
> "She thinks she's doing me a favor."

Nobody answered.

Jane continued:

> **JANE:**  
> "She thinks I'm tired."

A pause.

> **JANE:**  
> "She thinks caring about her is something I have to recover from."

She folded the paper carefully.

> **JANE:**  
> "And she thinks that conversation is something I want to forget."

Jane looked into the empty room.

> **JANE:**  
> "It isn't."

---

# 9. BAO

Bao remained focused on one thing.

The sentence:

> **"I'm not your daughter."**

Bao refused to accept it.

Not because Neri was biologically related to her.

Because that was never the basis of the relationship.

Bao had cared for Neri because Neri was Neri.

The distinction appeared to be completely lost on Neri.

Bao reportedly said:

> **BAO:**  
> "You don't get to decide that I stopped being your mother just because you decided you're inconvenient."

She received no response.

---

# 10. ALICE

Alice was unusually quiet.

She had read:

> **"I'm sorry I can't hang out with you."**

She understood the apology.

She did not understand the instruction that followed.

> **"Go somewhere else."**

Alice reportedly remained outside the room for several minutes.

Then:

> **ALICE:**  
> "...I don't want somewhere else."

---

# 11. THE EMPTY SUBSPACE

Neko later examined the room.

The subspace itself had been completely collapsed.

There was no residual floating architecture.

No broken boundary.

No obvious indication of where the islands had gone.

The room had returned to its original physical dimensions.

Neko described the result as:

> **"Not destroyed."**

A pause.

> **"Withdrawn."**

This distinction was entered into the report.

Neri had not destroyed her little world.

She had simply stopped maintaining it.

---

# 12. CURRENT ASSESSMENT

The SAFEHOUSE has entered a critical search posture.

Neri's previous pattern was:

**Hide the pain.**

Then:

**Hide the illness.**

Then:

**Hide herself.**

Now:

**Hide the place where she hid.**

Her sign should not be interpreted as reassurance.

The repeated phrase **"I'm fine"** is inconsistent with her documented medical condition and recent behavior.

More importantly, the sign does not say:

> _"I'll be back."_

It does not say:

> _"Give me some time."_

It does not say:

> _"Please don't worry."_

It says:

> **"Take care of someone else."**

That is not an ordinary request for privacy.

It is Neri attempting to convince the people who love her that they have permission to stop looking for her.

---

# 13. FINAL OBSERVATION

The Blacksite spent weeks failing to notice Neri.

Now Neri has made herself impossible to casually notice.

She stopped eating.

Stopped reading.

Stopped visiting.

Stopped talking.

Stopped keeping up.

Then she sealed herself away.

And when she finally left the room, she left instructions behind telling everyone else to continue without her.

The frightening part is not that Neri's room is empty.

It is that **she made sure everyone would have a reason not to follow.**
