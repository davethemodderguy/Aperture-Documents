# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT REPORT**

### **SUBJECT: COREY LOCKWOOD – SELF-BLAME**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – Executive, Medical & Psychological Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Over the past several weeks, Psychological Services have documented a significant increase in Corey's tendency toward excessive self-blame.

Despite repeated reassurance from family, friends, and medical staff, Corey has become convinced that Corin's continued depression is entirely her own fault.

Medical Division considers this belief to be unsupported by available evidence.

Corey does not.

---

# **CLINICAL OBSERVATION**

Corey has repeatedly expressed the belief that Corin would have recovered had Corey never appeared.

In multiple counseling sessions, she stated that her own existence has only prolonged Corin's suffering.

When reminded that Corin herself consistently rejects this idea, Corey acknowledged hearing those reassurances.

She simply no longer believes them.

---

# **TRANSCRIPT EXCERPT**

**Psychologist**

> "Why do you think Corin is still hurting?"

Corey answered immediately.

> "...Because of me."

---

**Psychologist**

> "Corin has never blamed you."

Corey nodded.

> "...I know."

A long silence followed.

> "...She's too kind to."

---

**Psychologist**

> "What makes you believe you're responsible?"

Corey looked down at her hands.

> "...Every time I get worse..."

> "...She gets worse."

She closed her eyes.

> "...If I disappeared..."

Another silence.

> "...Maybe she'd finally be happy."

The session was paused immediately.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin was later informed that Corey believed herself to be responsible for Corin's condition.

She appeared genuinely shocked.

Her response was immediate.

> "...No."

She shook her head repeatedly.

> "...No."

> "...That's backwards."

Corin struggled to continue.

> "...I'm sad because..."

She looked toward the floor.

> "...Because I know she's hurting."

Corin later requested that the following statement be permanently attached to Corey's psychological file.

> "If Corey disappeared..."

> "...I wouldn't recover."

> "...I'd lose my sister."

---

# **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

Belle closed the report.

> "...She doesn't realize they're carrying each other."

---

**Endmin Merryweather**

---

**Bao**

Bao quietly continued making meals for both sisters.

She refused to prepare only one tray.

---

**Star**

After hearing what Corey had said, Star left another paper star outside the treehouse.

This time, the note attached simply read:

> "For both of you."

---

**Nightshade**

> "...She's wrong."

---

**Lumi**

Lumi spent the evening sitting beside both sisters.

She did not try to convince Corey she was mistaken.

Instead, she wrapped an arm around each of them.

Neither moved away.

---

**Dialyn**

Later that night, Dialyn found Corey sitting outside in the snow.

Corey quietly asked:

> "...Do you ever think everyone would be happier without you?"

Dialyn answered without hesitation.

> "...No."

A pause.

> "...I used to think everyone would be happier if I had never survived."

She looked toward the SAFEHOUSE lights.

> "...Then I met people who proved me wrong."

She smiled gently.

> "...Maybe one day you'll believe them too."

Corey lowered her head.

She didn't argue.

She didn't agree.

She simply cried.

---

**Neko**

Neko reviewed both Corin's and Corey's psychological evaluations side by side.

His concluding note contained only one sentence.

> "...Each believes the other deserves a happier life."

Below it, he added:

> "...Neither includes themselves."

---

**Miss Neko**

> "...That's the tragedy."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Medical Division emphasizes that Corin's depression did not begin with Corey's arrival, nor can it be reduced to a single cause.

Likewise, Corey's existence is not the source of Corin's suffering.

The paradox linking them has created a painful cycle:

Corin grieves because Corey is hurting.

Corey blames herself because Corin is grieving.

Each interprets the other's pain as evidence of their own failure.

Breaking that cycle remains one of Psychological Services' highest priorities.

Until then, the sisters continue to do what they have always done.

They stay together.

Even when both believe they are the one hurting the other.

**End of Psychological Incident Report**
