**SUBJECT:** Jake — Tear Crystallization

---

## OBSERVATION

Jake's tears possess an unusual crystalline property.

When a tear leaves Jake's eye and remains exposed to the environment, it may crystallize into a **diamond**.

The resulting diamonds are genuine physical crystals.

No additional process has been identified.

No external material is required.

No known equipment is involved.

---

## CRYSTALLINE MORPHOLOGY

The diamonds do not form according to a single standardized shape.

Observed specimens have displayed numerous natural-looking forms, including:

- faceted crystals;
    
- rounded formations;
    
- star-like structures;
    
- elongated crystals;
    
- geometric clusters;
    
- and other unusually intricate shapes.
    

Each specimen may differ considerably from the previous one.

Some are remarkably small.

Others are large enough to be clearly visible from several meters away.

Their appearance is consistently described as unusually beautiful.

---

## RESIDENT WELFARE NOTE

Personnel are reminded that the diamonds are **a byproduct of Jake crying.**

They are not to be treated as collectible resources.

Jake is a child.

If he is crying, the appropriate response is to determine whether he needs comfort—not to examine the resulting crystal.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake's tears become diamonds.

They can take many shapes.

Some are simple.

Some are elaborate.

Some look almost like tiny pieces of the night sky.

They are beautiful.

But C.O.R.E. considers the tear that produced them considerably more important than the diamond itself.
