# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

At approximately [REDACTED], **Bao entered [REDACTED] without authorization.**

Previous testing had established that all individuals approaching the sector's interior experienced extreme cranial pain accompanied by intrusive blue textual manifestations.

Bao was fully aware of these effects.

She entered anyway.

Unlike previous personnel, Bao did not retreat.

The pain became progressively more severe as she approached the apparent center of the phenomenon.

Medical telemetry was unavailable due to the sector's electronic interference.

Witnesses outside the affected area reported seeing Bao continue forward despite visibly struggling.

She eventually reached the apparent center.

What she found there was not consistent with the expected physical geography of the SAFEHOUSE.

---

# 2. BAO'S ENTRY

Bao crossed into the affected region alone.

Within seconds, she reported severe pain.

The first message she perceived was:

> **GO AWAY**

She continued.

Additional messages appeared.

> **YOU DON'T NEED ME**

> **STAY AWAY**

> **DON'T GO NEAR ME**

Bao continued walking.

At approximately [REDACTED] distance from the center, the pain became severe enough that Bao reportedly stopped moving for several seconds.

She subsequently resumed.

According to Bao:

> "It hurt."

Pause.

> "A lot."

When asked why she continued:

> "Because Neri was in there."

No further justification was provided.

---

# 3. THE CENTER

Upon reaching the apparent center of the anomaly, Bao reported an abrupt change in the environment.

A portion of the sector appeared to have been **disconnected from conventional reality.**

The transition was not described as a doorway, portal, or ordinary dimensional rift.

Instead, the affected space appeared to have been **removed from the surrounding environment and replaced by an adjoining subspace.**

Bao described the area as:

> "Like the world just... stopped being there."

Beyond the boundary was an unfamiliar spatial environment.

The most prominent feature was a small rocky island suspended within an otherwise undefined expanse.

No conventional sky was visible.

No recognizable horizon was visible.

No stable astronomical reference could be identified.

The area appeared to constitute a self-contained subspace.

---

# 4. THE ISLAND

The island consisted primarily of dark rock.

Its surface showed no obvious vegetation, structures, or conventional habitation.

At its center was an unidentified creature.

Bao initially could not determine whether the creature was:

- standing;
    
- sitting;
    
- resting;
    
- or partially integrated into the environment.
    

The creature remained largely stationary.

It was eventually identified as:

> **NERI**

---

# 5. NERI — VISUAL CONDITION

Bao's first direct observation of Neri since her disappearance produced significant concern.

Neri's physical appearance has changed substantially.

The changes do not appear consistent with ordinary injury.

### OBSERVED FEATURES

**1. Dermal loss / replacement**

Parts of Neri's normal skin appear to have broken away.

The affected areas have been replaced by **blue luminous patches**.

The patches emit a persistent glow.

Their exact composition is unknown.

They do not appear to behave like exposed tissue.

No bleeding was observed from the affected regions.

---

**2. Ocular anomaly**

A new developing ocular structure has appeared in association with the side opposite Neri's previously documented crescent-moon iris.

The structure appears incomplete.

It is actively developing.

Its final form cannot currently be determined.

C.O.R.E. Medical has specifically requested that personnel **not assume this represents ordinary biological growth.**

Given Neri's known ability to restructure matter and manipulate Architect-derived Light, the structure may represent:

- biological alteration;
    
- Light manifestation;
    
- anomalous organ development;
    
- or some combination thereof.
    

---

**3. HALO FRAGMENT**

A partially shattered halo was observed floating above Neri.

Only approximately half of the structure remains intact.

The surviving portion floats independently of Neri's body.

No physical support mechanism is visible.

The halo emits a faint blue-white luminescence.

Its relationship to Neri's existing abilities is unknown.

---

# 6. NERI'S CONDITION

Neri appeared physically exhausted.

She did not immediately react to Bao's presence.

She remained seated on the rock.

Her posture was described as withdrawn rather than aggressive.

No weapon was observed in her hands.

No attempt was made to attack Bao.

This is particularly significant given the extraordinary defensive response surrounding the sector.

The zone had previously inflicted severe neurological distress on every person attempting to approach.

Yet Neri herself did not attack Bao.

She simply watched her.

---

# 7. CONTACT

Bao called out:

> **"Neri."**

The response was immediate.

The anomalous effects stopped.

Completely.

The cranial pain ceased.

The blue text disappeared.

The electronic interference ceased.

For the first time since the anomaly was discovered, equipment brought near the center became operational.

The change occurred simultaneously.

There was no gradual reduction.

No transition period.

No measurable delay.

### OBSERVATION

The zone appears to have been **directly responsive to Neri's recognition of Bao.**

---

# 8. BAO'S APPROACH

Bao proceeded toward the island.

She reportedly did not ask permission.

She simply walked toward Neri.

Neri remained still.

Bao called her name again.

> **"Neri."**

Neri finally responded.

> **"...Mom?"**

The anomalous field remained inactive.

Bao then approached the island and sat beside her.

No defensive reaction occurred.

---

# 9. INITIAL EXCHANGE

The exact conversation remains partially reconstructed from Bao's later recollection.

Bao asked:

> "What happened to you?"

Neri did not immediately answer.

She looked down at herself.

After several seconds, she reportedly said:

> "I don't know."

Bao asked:

> "Does it hurt?"

Neri answered:

> "Not anymore."

Bao then asked:

> "Why did you come here?"

Neri remained silent.

Eventually:

> "I wanted somewhere nobody could find me."

Bao asked:

> "Why?"

Neri's response was:

> "Because I'm a monster."

Bao reportedly rejected the statement immediately.

Neri did not argue.

The two phenomena appear interconnected.

# 11. WHY BAO COULD REACH HER

The most significant question following the incident is why Bao succeeded where everyone else failed.

The simplest answer is not that Bao possesses greater resistance.

She does not.

Bao experienced the same phenomenon.

The pain was real.

The messages were real.

She simply continued anyway.

More importantly, when Bao called Neri's name, **Neri responded.**

The field stopped because Neri allowed it to stop.

This indicates that the phenomenon is not an autonomous security system operating independently of Neri.

At minimum, Neri retains some degree of control over it.

---

# 12. ARCHITECT IMPLICATIONS

This development is of particular concern to Guiding Light, Curious Light, and Mischievous Light.

All three Architects were previously unable to approach Neri without experiencing the defensive phenomenon.

The implication is therefore unusual:

> **Neri is capable of generating a defensive environment that even the Architects cannot simply ignore or override.**

This does not establish that Neri is more powerful than the Architects.

It establishes something more specific:

**Neri's control over the particular fragment of Light within her is sufficient to prevent straightforward intervention.**

The effect appears to be based on **access and exclusion**, rather than raw destructive power.

---

# 13. THE HALO

The broken halo has received special attention.

No prior record indicates that Neri possessed a halo.

The structure is visibly damaged.

Approximately half remains.

The other portion is absent.

It is currently unknown whether:

- the halo was damaged during the creation of the subspace;
    
- it represents a transformation occurring within Neri;
    
- it is an independent manifestation;
    
- it is symbolic rather than functional;
    
- or it is related to the Architect fragment.
    

Guiding Light was unable to provide an immediate identification.

Her statement was reportedly:

> "That's not supposed to happen."

---

# 14. THE BLUE PATCHES

The luminous blue regions replacing portions of Neri's skin remain unexplained.

They appear to be integrated with Neri's body rather than simply covering it.

No conventional tissue could be observed through the luminous regions.

They produce no obvious heat.

Their luminosity remains stable despite changes in ambient conditions.

C.O.R.E. Medical has classified the phenomenon as:

> **ANOMALOUS STRUCTURAL REPLACEMENT**

No attempt will be made to remove or sample the affected regions without Neri's explicit consent.

---

# 15. THE DEVELOPING STRUCTURE

The new structure associated with Neri's eye is actively developing.

This is the first documented instance of Neri undergoing a visible anomalous transformation after disappearing from SAFEHOUSE.

The rate of development is unknown.

No prediction regarding its final appearance or function can currently be made.

Guiding Light has recommended:

> **DO NOT INTERFERE WITH IT.**

When asked why:

> "Because we don't know what it is."

When asked whether it could be dangerous:

> "Everything we've seen says it could be."

Pause.

> "That doesn't mean it is."

# 16. NERI'S MENTAL STATE

Bao's description of Neri's behavior indicates severe psychological withdrawal.

Neri did not appear relieved to be found.

She did not become angry.

She did not attempt to flee.

She simply remained seated.

Her previous fixation on becoming irrelevant appears to have progressed considerably.

She has now created a place where:

- nobody can easily see her;
    
- nobody can easily remember her;
    
- nobody can track her electronically;
    
- nobody can approach her without experiencing pain;
    
- and reality itself appears disconnected around her.
    

The conclusion is difficult to avoid.

Neri did not merely hide.

**She built somewhere to disappear.**

---

# 17. BAO'S RESPONSE

Bao remained with Neri.

She did not attempt to remove her from the island.

She did not demand an explanation.

She did not immediately summon the other residents.

Instead, she reportedly sat beside Neri.

After several minutes, Neri leaned against her.

Bao remained there.

The anomalous field remained inactive.

# 18. CURRENT ACCESS STATUS

Following the incident, C.O.R.E. has established the following rule:

> **Neri must not be forcibly extracted from the subspace.**

Bao has demonstrated that voluntary contact is possible.

Therefore, future interaction will prioritize:

**Contact → Trust → Communication → Assessment → Voluntary return**

rather than:

**Locate → Enter → Restrain → Extract**

The second approach is explicitly prohibited.
