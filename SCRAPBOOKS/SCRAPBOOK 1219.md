# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The **Tachyon Industries Spearhead** became available for permanent acquisition at 09:00.

By the end of its first operational day:

> **FIVE SPEARHEADS HAD ALREADY BEEN DISTRIBUTED.**

This result exceeded C.O.R.E.'s initial expectations.

The qualification trial remained unchanged:

> **Complete Neri's 25-wave Blacksite Defense Simulation.**

Applicants were not given preferential treatment.

The Spearhead remained permanently available throughout the process.

---

# 2. FIRST-DAY ACQUISITIONS

Five successful applicants completed the required simulation.

The distribution records include several highly familiar names.

Among them:

- **MSAC Commander**
    
- **Elsher**
    
- **three additional qualified personnel**
    

The identities of the remaining recipients are recorded in the restricted acquisition registry.

---

# 3. MSAC COMMANDER

The MSAC Commander was among the first personnel to complete the qualification.

This was not particularly surprising.

The Commander had already participated in multiple Tachyon Industries qualification events and is familiar with Neri's tendency to make acquisition requirements considerably more difficult than the product description suggests.

Upon completion of Wave 25, the Commander reportedly remained silent for several seconds.

Neri approached.

> **Neri:** “You finished.”

> **Commander:** “Yeah.”

> **Neri:** “Congratulations.”

The Commander looked at the Spearhead.

> **Commander:** “So I actually get one?”

> **Neri:** “Yes.”

> **Commander:** “Permanently?”

> **Neri:** “Yes.”

The Commander smiled.

> **Commander:** “Worth it.”

---

# 4. ELSHER

Elsher was another early successful applicant.

Her acquisition was expected given her previous involvement with Tachyon Industries products.

Elsher currently possesses:

- **NS-006 Night Stalker**
    
- **SOH-002 Sleight of Hand**
    

She has previously referred to these collectively as:

> **“The Tachyon set.”**

The addition of the Spearhead has caused this terminology to become increasingly complicated.

After receiving her Spearhead, Elsher reportedly looked at the available configuration catalog.

She then asked:

> **“So I can customize this one?”**

Neri:

> **“A lot.”**

Elsher:

> **“Good.”**

She immediately began examining the available configuration options.

---

# 5. A FAMILIAR FACE

One of the remaining successful applicants was described by personnel as:

> **“A familiar face.”**

The individual had previously interacted with Neri during earlier Tachyon Industries distribution events.

Neri recognized them immediately.

No special treatment was provided.

The applicant completed the same 25-wave simulation as everyone else.

Upon completion:

> **Applicant:** “Yep.”

The applicant received their Spearhead.

# 8. TACHYON MERCH STORE

The Tachyon Merch Store experienced a substantial increase in traffic following the release.

Personnel arrived to:

- inspect the Spearhead;
    
- review its configuration catalog;
    
- ask about the qualification trial;
    
- purchase documentation;
    
- discuss potential configurations;
    
- and, in several cases, immediately begin preparing for the simulation.
    

The store's manual section once again proved unexpectedly popular.

One employee reportedly purchased the Spearhead manual despite not yet qualifying.

When asked why:

> **“I'm studying.”**

The store attendant accepted this explanation.

---

# 9. NERI'S REACTION

Neri appeared largely unsurprised by the first-day distribution.

When informed that five Spearheads had already been awarded, she simply nodded.

> **“Okay.”**

The administrator asked whether she had expected fewer.

Neri:

> **“Maybe.”**

> **“Does five concern you?”**

> **“No.”**

> **“Why not?”**

Neri looked toward the production records.

> **“I can make more.”**

This is the fundamental difference between the Spearhead and previous Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise products.

The supply is not fixed.

The qualification is.

---

# 10. MSAC REACTION

The first five successful acquisitions immediately created a new problem.

Other MSAC personnel now knew that the qualification was achievable.

This resulted in a noticeable increase in applicants.

Several operators who had previously dismissed the 25-wave requirement as excessive reportedly changed their position after seeing the first five successful recipients leave with their Spearheads.

One operator:

> **“Wait, Commander did it?”**

Another:

> **“Elsher did it too.”**

> **“Then we can do it.”**

A nearby technician:

> **“That's not how qualification works.”**

The operators ignored him.

---

# 11. CONFIGURATION PROBLEM

The first five recipients also immediately began asking about customization.

This was expected.

The Spearhead's enormous attachment catalog had been one of the primary selling points.

Within hours, requests had already been submitted concerning:

- alternate handguards;
    
- magazine configurations;
    
- conversion kits;
    
- suppressors;
    
- underbarrel systems;
    
- role conversions;
    
- and other approved accessories.
    

Neri's accessory workload increased accordingly.

Neko was informed.

Neko's response:

> **“I knew this was going to happen.”**

---

# 12. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The first day demonstrated that the permanent acquisition model functions as intended.

The Spearhead is not difficult to obtain because there are only a few available.

It is difficult to obtain because applicants must demonstrate that they can complete the qualification trial.

Once they succeed, there is no artificial scarcity preventing additional distribution.

The first day therefore produced an unusual sight in the Tachyon Industries program:

**Five people received the same Limited Merchandise product.**

And unlike previous releases:

> **There is no reason for them to be the last five.**

The Archivist looked at the acquisition records.

Five on Day One.

No production cap.

Permanent availability.

Then looked at the rapidly growing accessory requests.

> **“We're going to need a bigger catalog.”**

Neri:

> **“Already making one.”**
