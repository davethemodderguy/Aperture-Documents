## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / ANOMALOUS PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

# **COREY — WITHDRAWAL EVENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Personnel Welfare / Anomalous Risk  
**Subject:** Corey / Solaria / Luna  
**Status:** **CONCERN — ACTIVE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has observed a significant deterioration in Corey's emotional state.

Over an extended period, Corey has become increasingly:

- Withdrawn.
    
- Quiet.
    
- Sad.
    
- Exhausted.
    
- Emotionally devastated.
    
- Socially isolated.
    

The change appears to have originated from a memory Corey recently recovered.

The exact contents of the memory remain undisclosed.

Corey has not been willing to discuss it directly.

However, both **Luna** and **Solaria** have acknowledged its existence when manifested.

---

# **THE MEMORY**

Something from Corey's past has resurfaced.

The memory appears to be deeply distressing.

Corey has begun spending increasing amounts of time alone, often refusing conversation or companionship.

She has also experienced significant sleep loss.

Personnel have repeatedly found her awake at unusual hours.

When asked whether she slept, Corey commonly responds:

> "...A little."

She usually did not.

---

# **THE MART PROBLEM**

The most serious anomalous consequence has involved the Marts.

Marts naturally gravitate toward people who are:

- Happy.
    
- Warm.
    
- Emotionally positive.
    
- Safe to approach.
    

They previously spent considerable amounts of time around Corey.

That has changed.

Corey's current emotional state has become so overwhelmingly negative that prolonged proximity can cause Marts to become distressed.

This presents a severe danger to them.

---

# **MART SELF-PRESERVATION RESPONSE**

The entire Mart colony has independently changed its behavior.

They now avoid Corey.

Not because they dislike her.

Not because they are angry.

Not because they have abandoned her.

They are protecting themselves.

The colony has effectively recognized:

> **Being around Corey right now may cause a Mart to burst.**

As a result, Marts no longer routinely approach her.

---

# **OBSERVATION**

A Mart was once seen floating toward Corey.

It stopped several meters away.

Corey looked toward it.

The Mart hesitated.

> "...Corey."

Corey quietly replied:

> "...Hi."

The Mart remained still.

Then slowly backed away.

> "...Sorry."

Corey lowered her head.

> "...It's okay."

The Mart left.

---

# **COLONY RESPONSE**

This behavior has become widespread.

Marts now maintain distance from Corey even when she is alone.

If Corey enters a room, nearby Marts may quietly leave.

The response is not coordinated through conventional communication.

It appears to be an instinctive colony-wide preservation behavior.

---

# **COREY'S REACTION**

Corey has noticed.

This has made her condition worse.

She understands why the Marts are avoiding her.

She also understands that they are afraid of dying simply by trying to comfort her.

This has produced additional guilt.

Corey's internal reasoning appears to have become increasingly circular:

> "They're avoiding me."

> "I made them afraid."

> "They can't even come near me."

> "I'm hurting them."

This reinforces her withdrawal.

---

# **IMPORTANT DISTINCTION**

The emotional instability **does not transfer to Luna or Solaria**.

When Corey manifests as either form, the dangerous emotional influence disappears.

The Marts immediately become comfortable approaching them again.

This indicates that the phenomenon is specifically associated with **Corey's current psychological state**, rather than being an inherent property of the shared body.

---

# **LUNA'S RESPONSE**

Luna has become increasingly concerned.

During one manifestation, she quietly stated:

> "...She's getting worse."

When asked who she meant:

> "...Corey."

She remained silent for several seconds.

> "...She's trying to carry something that is breaking her."

Luna's usual smile was absent.

---

# **SOLARIA'S RESPONSE**

Solaria expressed similar concern, though in a more direct manner.

> "...She isn't sleeping."

A researcher asked whether Corey had told her this.

Solaria replied:

> "...I know."

She looked toward the floor.

> "...I remember everything she remembers."

Then:

> "...She's exhausted."

---

# **TWO OUT OF THREE**

This has created an unusual psychological situation.

Three expressions.

One body.

One shared memory.

Two of them are increasingly worried about the third.

Luna and Solaria can observe Corey's decline.

They understand why she is suffering.

They remember what she remembers.

But they cannot simply remove the memory.

They cannot make her forget.

They cannot force her to heal.

They can only wait for Corey to let them help.

---

# **LUNA AND SOLARIA**

The two manifestations have independently begun discussing ways to support Corey.

Their approaches differ.

### **Solaria**

Attempts to encourage Corey to become active again.

She emphasizes:

- Eating.
    
- Sleeping.
    
- Leaving her room.
    
- Talking to someone.
    

### **Luna**

Takes a quieter approach.

She stays nearby when manifested.

She does not force conversation.

She simply makes sure Corey knows she is not alone.

---

# **A PRIVATE CONVERSATION**

During one manifestation cycle, Luna and Solaria were both heard discussing Corey.

**Solaria:**

> "...We need to do something."

**Luna:**

> "...I know."

**Solaria:**

> "...She's disappearing."

Silence.

**Luna:**

> "...She's still here."

**Solaria:**

> "...Barely."

Luna looked toward Corey.

> "...Then we stay."

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts have not abandoned Corey permanently.

They remain aware of her.

They simply refuse to approach while doing so could endanger themselves.

Several Marts have been observed watching Corey from a distance.

They do not approach.

They wait.

A colony representative was observed near the doorway of Corey's room.

It remained there for several minutes.

Then quietly left.

---

# **SLEEP DEPRIVATION**

Corey's lack of sleep is becoming an increasing concern.

She has been observed:

- Falling asleep at irregular times.
    
- Waking repeatedly.
    
- Remaining awake through entire nights.
    
- Sitting silently for extended periods.
    
- Showing signs of exhaustion during routine activities.
    

The SAFEHOUSE staff have begun monitoring her sleep without forcing her into bed.

---

# **LUMI'S OBSERVATION**

Lumi has expressed particular concern.

She understands that the Marts' avoidance is not rejection.

They are protecting themselves.

She also understands that Corey may interpret it as rejection anyway.

Her assessment:

> "...She needs to understand that the Marts aren't leaving her."

> "...They're waiting for her."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

**Corey:**  
Emotionally unstable. Sleep-deprived. Increasingly withdrawn.

**Luna:**  
Concerned. Stable. Actively monitoring Corey.

**Solaria:**  
Concerned. Stable. Attempting to motivate Corey.

**Marts:**  
Colony-wide avoidance of Corey until proximity becomes safe.

**SAFEHOUSE:**  
Monitoring situation.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corey once had Marts everywhere.

On her head.

Beside her.

Following her.

Sleeping near her.

Cooking with her.

Keeping her warm.

Now, the halls become quiet when she enters.

Not because the Marts stopped loving her.

Because they love themselves enough to survive.

And somewhere inside the same body, two other versions of Corey are watching the person they share a life with slowly fall apart.

Luna and Solaria cannot simply take her pain away.

They can only remain beside her.

Two voices.

One body.

One broken part of themselves.

And both quietly hoping Corey will eventually let them help.
