# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neri has produced another Tachyon Industries weapon.

This time, she made it specifically for Nora.

The weapon has been designated:

> **THE DEADEYE**

It is a heavily customized Glock-platform combat pistol configured around a compact **5.7×28 mm** cartridge system.

Unlike Neri's previous Tachyon Industries projects, this item was not created as a general limited-production experiment.

It was made because Nora wanted her own pistol.

Neri apparently decided that was sufficient justification.

Nora's reaction was immediate.

> **NORA:**  
> “THIS IS MINE?!”

Neri nodded.

Nora screamed.

---

# 2. CONFIGURATION

The Deadeye incorporates the following documented features:

- Glock-platform configuration;
    
- integrated targeting system;
    
- 5.7×28 mm ammunition compatibility;
    
- armor-piercing ammunition designation within Aperture's fictional armament system;
    
- manual safety switch;
    
- weapon-mounted flashlight;
    
- 25-round magazine;
    
- suppressed configuration;
    
- Tachyon Industries identification;
    
- personalized Shark-themed decoration.
    

The weapon is visually distinct from Neri's earlier Night Stalker series.

Where the Night Stalker emphasizes understated elegance, the Deadeye is considerably more personal.

It was made for a six-year-old Shark Thiren who apparently believes subtlety is optional.

---

# 3. THE RELOAD DISCOVERY

Nora's first major reaction was not to the appearance.

It was to the controls.

During supervised familiarization, Nora realized that the pistol's slide could be returned to battery through the slide release after inserting a fresh magazine.

She stared at the pistol.

Then at Neri.

Then back at the pistol.

> **NORA:**  
> “Wait.”

Neri looked at her.

> **NORA:**  
> “I don't have to rack it every time?”

Neri shook her head.

Nora's eyes widened.

> **NORA:**  
> “I CAN JUST PRESS THIS?”

Neri nodded.

Nora immediately became extremely excited.

> **NORA:**  
> “THAT'S SO MUCH BETTER!”

Neri calmly added that manually cycling the slide remained an option.

Nora considered this.

> **NORA:**  
> “So I can do either?”

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

> **NORA:**  
> “Best gun ever.”

Neri adjusted her glasses.

> **NERI:**  
> “It's not the best gun ever.”

> **NORA:**  
> “It's the best one I have.”

Neri paused.

> **NERI:**  
> “...Fair.”

---

# 4. SHARK CUSTOMIZATION

The original Deadeye was relatively plain.

This did not survive long.

Nora requested personalization.

Neri agreed.

The resulting decoration package is unmistakably Shark-themed.

Documented visual elements include:

- shark motifs;
    
- stylized fins;
    
- small aquatic markings;
    
- blue-and-white decorative elements;
    
- a shark emblem incorporated into the presentation design;
    
- personalized Tachyon Industries markings.
    

Nora immediately approved.

> **NORA:**  
> “NOW IT LOOKS LIKE ME!”

Neri looked at the finished pistol.

> **NERI:**  
> “That was the idea.”

Nora hugged Neri.

Neri did not complain.

---

# 5. NORA'S REACTION

Nora has demonstrated unusually strong enthusiasm toward the Deadeye.

She has repeatedly referred to it as:

> **“My Shark Gun.”**

Neri has repeatedly corrected her.

> **NERI:**  
> “It's called Deadeye.”

> **NORA:**  
> “My Shark Deadeye.”

> **NERI:**  
> “That's not the name.”

> **NORA:**  
> “It is now.”

No further argument was recorded.

---

# 6. WHY NERI MADE IT

When asked why she created another weapon after already producing the Night Stalker, Sleight of Hand, and Ceberus series, Neri provided a surprisingly simple answer.

> **NERI:**  
> “She wanted one.”

C.O.R.E. requested clarification.

Neri provided none.

This appears to have been the entire design philosophy.

Nora wanted something of her own.

Neri made it.

The resulting object was subsequently decorated with sharks.

---

# 7. ARMAMENTS DEPARTMENT RESPONSE

The Armory initially assumed the Deadeye would be another one-off Tachyon Industries project.

They were correct.

Then Nora started showing it to people.

This created a secondary problem.

MSAC personnel immediately began asking whether additional units could be produced.

Neri's answer was:

> **“No.”**

Nora's answer was:

> **“Maybe.”**

This distinction is currently causing problems.

---

# 8. MSAC AVAILABILITY

The Deadeye is **not currently an MSAC standard-issue weapon.**

Neri has not authorized mass production.

However, a previously established Tachyon Industries precedent remains applicable.

If an MSAC member wants a Deadeye, they may attempt to convince:

> **Nora**

to convince:

> **Neri**

to make another one.

This is not an official procurement procedure.

It is, however, apparently the procedure that now exists.

# 9. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Convincing Nora does **not** guarantee production.

Nora cannot authorize additional Deadeyes herself.

She can only ask Neri.

Neri retains complete control over:

- whether another unit is produced;
    
- who receives it;
    
- how it is configured;
    
- whether customization is permitted;
    
- whether the request is rejected;
    
- whether the Deadeye remains a one-off.
    

Personnel are therefore reminded that:

> **Nora is not the manufacturer.**

She is simply the person most likely to make the manufacturer reconsider.

# 11. C.O.R.E. OBSERVATION

The Deadeye is significant for reasons beyond its technical configuration.

Neri has previously created weapons because of research.

Because of curiosity.

Because of experimentation.

Because she wanted to prove something.

The Deadeye is different.

She made it because her little sister wanted one.

The shark decorations were added because Nora wanted it to look like hers.

The result is therefore less like another Limited Merchandise project and more like a very unusual family gift.

Nora appears to understand the distinction.

She does not describe it as:

> “A Tachyon Industries product.”

She describes it as:

> **“The one Sis made for me.”**

That distinction appears to matter considerably more to her.
