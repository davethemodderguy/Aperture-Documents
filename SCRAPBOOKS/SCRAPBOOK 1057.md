## Current Status

Neri has voluntarily transformed into her plush form again.

No medical incident preceded the transformation.

No emergency was declared.

No one requested that she transform.

She simply decided she wanted to be a plushie.

And so she is.

---

## Immediate Behavioral Observation

Neri was last observed in normal form standing in a hallway.

Approximately several minutes later, the same location contained a small seal/teddy-bear hybrid.

Neri looked up at the nearest person.

Then she sat down.

No explanation was provided.

---

## SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE

Alice noticed first.

> **ALICE:** "Neri!"

Neri looked at her.

Alice immediately opened her arms.

Neri crawled over.

Alice picked her up.

The difference from yesterday's normal-form interaction was immediately apparent.

Alice lifted Neri effortlessly.

> **ALICE:** "See? I can carry you!"

Neri remained in her arms.

She gave a tiny nod.

Alice hugged her.

---

## Bao

Bao later encountered them.

She looked at Neri.

Then at Alice.

Then at Neri again.

> **BAO:** "Back to plush?"

Neri nodded.

Bao held out her arms.

Alice carefully handed Neri over.

Bao hugged her.

Neri settled comfortably against her.

---
