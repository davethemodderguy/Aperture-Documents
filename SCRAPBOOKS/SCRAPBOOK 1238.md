# 1. INTERVIEW PURPOSE

Following the confirmation that Sky and **Invictus Aeterna** are manifestations of the same entity, C.O.R.E. conducted a formal interview with Aeterna.

The interview was specifically intended to clarify:

1. Aeterna's origins;
    
2. its relationship with Guiding Light;
    
3. its relationship with Nora Tachyon;
    
4. the reason for its protective behavior;
    
5. the reason it voluntarily performs servant-like duties for Nora;
    
6. whether its behavior is the result of magical binding, compulsion, or an imposed command structure.
    

The final question was considered particularly important.

C.O.R.E. wanted to determine whether Aeterna was **serving Nora by choice.**

The answer was unequivocal.

---

# 2. INTERVIEW CONDITIONS

Aeterna entered the interview room voluntarily.

No restraints were used.

No containment measures were active.

Aeterna stood approximately 185 cm tall throughout most of the interview.

Its armor remained fully manifested.

Blue runes remained illuminated.

Its sword was not present.

Nora was not in the room.

Aeterna was informed that she was free to leave at any time.

Aeterna remained.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The interview conclusively establishes that Aeterna's relationship with Nora is **voluntary.**

There is:

- no known binding;
    
- no magical compulsion;
    
- no enforced obedience;
    
- no ownership mechanism;
    
- no restriction preventing Aeterna from leaving;
    
- and no evidence that Guiding Light controls Aeterna's decisions.
    

Aeterna explicitly understands that it is free to refuse Nora.

It has refused her before.

Nora accepts those refusals.

Despite possessing that freedom, Aeterna repeatedly chooses to remain with Nora.

It chooses to protect her.

It chooses to carry her.

It chooses to serve her.

It chooses to act as her attendant.

It chooses to call her:

> **Master.**

Not because it must.

Because it wants to.

The distinction is fundamental.

Aeterna's service is not captivity.

It is devotion expressed through choice.
