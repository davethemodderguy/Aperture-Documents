## Executive Summary

Neri's Dell laptop has officially become the **first laptop in Heaven's Blacksite equipped with a 12 TB solid-state drive.**

Prior to this deployment, no Blacksite laptop had been provisioned with 12 TB of internal storage.

The upgrade was approved following sustained research activity and rapidly increasing storage requirements associated with Neri's chemistry work.

The timing was, according to Neko:

> **"Unfortunate."**

---

## I. STORAGE REQUIREMENT

Neri's research activity over the preceding two weeks resulted in substantial accumulation of:

- research papers;
    
- experimental records;
    
- simulation data;
    
- chemical models;
    
- mathematical models;
    
- diagrams;
    
- datasets;
    
- locally generated results;
    
- failed experiments;
    
- revised experiments;
    
- reference material;
    
- laboratory photographs;
    
- notes;
    
- and files whose purpose Neko has yet to determine.
    

Her previous storage configuration remained functional.

It was simply becoming increasingly inconvenient.

Neri's response to being informed that storage space was becoming limited was reportedly:

> **"Oh."**

She then continued working.

---

## II. THE UPGRADE

Neko eventually performed a storage assessment.

The result was sufficient to justify an unusually large capacity increase.

A **12 TB SSD** was installed.

Following initialization and Blacksite environment deployment, the laptop became the first recorded Blacksite laptop with twelve terabytes of internal solid-state storage.

## III. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri was informed of the upgrade.

She looked at the storage information.

Then at Neko.

> **NERI:** "Why?"

> **NEKO:** "Because you were running out of space."

Neri looked back at the screen.

> **NERI:** "Oh."

She returned to her research.

No celebration occurred.

No specifications were discussed.

Neri appeared primarily interested in whether her existing files had survived the migration.

They had.

She immediately resumed work.

---

## IV. THE PROBLEM

The upgrade solved the immediate storage problem.

It also created a new one.

Neri now had an enormous amount of available storage.

Within a relatively short period, she began using it.

Neko's monitoring showed that Neri was generating research material faster than expected.

A temporary internal note was added to the device registry:

> **"DO NOT ASK WHY SHE NEEDS 12 TB."**

This note was subsequently followed by:

> **"Actually, ask."**

Which was followed approximately six hours later by:

> **"Never mind."**

---

## V. ARCHIVAL OBSERVATION

The 12 TB SSD is not considered excessive for general Blacksite computing.

It is, however, exceptionally large for a personal SAFEHOUSE laptop.

Most residents use their devices for ordinary activities:

- messaging;
    
- browsing;
    
- documents;
    
- entertainment;
    
- work;
    
- games;
    
- photographs;
    
- and general personal use.
    

Neri's laptop is different.

It has effectively become a **portable research archive.**

A significant portion of its storage consists of material generated by Neri herself.

---

## VI. NEKO'S COMMENT

After examining the newly upgraded system, Neko submitted the following statement:

> **"I gave the ten-year-old a 12 TB SSD."**
> 
> **"She immediately started filling it."**
> 
> **"I don't know whether to be impressed or concerned."**
> 
> **"Probably both."**

---

## VII. C.O.R.E. STATUS

The device remains authorized for Neri's personal research.

No storage restrictions are currently planned.

Neri has been reminded that Blacksite network storage exists if she requires archival capacity.

She declined.

Her reasoning was recorded as:

> **"I like having it here."**

C.O.R.E. has no objection.

### ARCHIVIST'S NOTE

**First Blacksite laptop with a 12 TB SSD: Neri's.**

Not because she requested the largest laptop available.

Not because somebody wanted to give her an absurd computer.

Simply because she kept doing research until the existing storage became inconvenient.

The most concerning part is that **12 TB may eventually prove insufficient.**
