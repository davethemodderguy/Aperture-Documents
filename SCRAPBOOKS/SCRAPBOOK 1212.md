# 9. AVAILABILITY

The Tachyon Merch Store does not guarantee that every item listed in this catalog will be physically present.

Inventory is deliberately limited.

Some items are produced only after an approved request.

Others require Neri's direct review.

The store therefore operates under the following principle:

> **LISTED ≠ IN STOCK**

and:

> **IN STOCK ≠ AVAILABLE TO EVERYONE**
