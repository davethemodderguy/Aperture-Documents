# 2. PARTICIPANT ADAPTATION

The difference between Week 1 and Week 2 was substantial.

The participants are no longer attempting to simply endure the environment.

They have adapted to it.

The temporary camps established during Week 1 have effectively become permanent outdoor installations.

Participants have:

- improved shelters;
    
- established sleeping areas;
    
- organized food storage;
    
- created designated cooking areas;
    
- established shared supply areas;
    
- developed schedules;
    
- marked frequently used paths;
    
- organized waste disposal;
    
- established informal camp rules.
    

One MSAC operator described the situation as:

> **"We've basically moved out here."**

Another responded:

> **"I think that's the point."**

---

# 3. SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS

Participants have developed surprisingly sophisticated sleeping rotations.

Several operators sleep simultaneously while others remain awake.

The arrangement is not required by the rules.

It developed organically.

One participant explained:

> "We're not guarding against enemies."

Pause.

> "We're guarding against ourselves becoming idiots from lack of sleep."

C.O.R.E. considers this a reasonable assessment.

---

# 4. COOPERATION

The cooperative behavior observed during Week 1 has intensified.

Participants now routinely:

- share food;
    
- repair damaged shelters;
    
- assist exhausted operators;
    
- exchange equipment;
    
- help maintain common areas;
    
- cook together;
    
- wake one another for scheduled activities;
    
- share information about weather conditions.
    

There have been disagreements.

None have escalated into serious conflict.

The competitive nature of the challenge remains intact.

However, participants increasingly appear to view one another as a temporary community rather than exclusively as competitors.

---

# 5. THE COMPETITION PROBLEM

This has created an unusual psychological situation.

The challenge is:

> **LAST MAN STANDING.**

Yet the participants are increasingly invested in keeping one another functional.

One operator summarized the contradiction:

> "I want to win."

Pause.

> "I also don't want any of these idiots to quit."

Another operator responded:

> "Same."

The first operator then added:

> "Don't tell Neri."

Unfortunately, C.O.R.E. is telling Neri.

---

# 6. NERI'S INVOLVEMENT

Neri continues to observe the competition from a distance.

She has not altered the rules.

She has not introduced a new challenge.

She has not announced a deadline.

She has not provided additional instructions.

She has occasionally left supplies near the competition area.

These supplies have included:

- food;
    
- bottled water;
    
- blankets;
    
- basic outdoor equipment;
    
- coffee.
    

The coffee was particularly appreciated.

Neri did not stay to watch the reaction.

---

# 7. THE COFFEE INCIDENT

At approximately 0715 hours, participants discovered a large container of coffee beside the communal supply area.

A handwritten note was attached.

It read:

> **"You looked tired."**

No signature was necessary.

Everyone immediately knew who had left it.

One operator reportedly stated:

> "She's terrifying."

Another replied:

> "She brought us coffee."

The first operator:

> "Exactly."
