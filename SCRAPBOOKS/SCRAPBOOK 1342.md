# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Phoenix Raffedella regained consciousness following the NOST office door incident.

Her recovery was uneventful.

Once fully alert, Phoenix introduced herself to the surrounding SAFEHOUSE residents and C.O.R.E. personnel.

The introduction was considerably more normal than the circumstances that preceded it.

Approximately later, **Dialyn** arrived at the NOST office.

She was immediately informed that Phoenix had been rendered unconscious before she arrived.

Dialyn was not pleased.

Not because Phoenix had been attacked.

Not because Aperture had violated any agreement.

Not because of a security incident.

Because:

> **Phoenix had apparently been killed by a door.**

Dialyn's reaction was described by witnesses as:

> **“Deeply disappointed.”**

---

# 1. PHOENIX AWAKENS

Phoenix's eyes opened fully.

She looked around the medical bay.

Several people were present.

Bao was nearby.

Belle was standing beside the bed.

Jake was sitting at a safe distance.

Neri was quietly observing from a chair.

Phoenix slowly sat up.

> **Phoenix:** “...Hello.”

Nobody immediately answered.

Phoenix looked around.

> **Phoenix:** “Did I miss something?”

Belle:

> **“A door.”**

Phoenix paused.

> **Phoenix:** “Right.”

She looked toward Jake.

Jake immediately lowered his head.

> **Jake:** “Sorry.”

Phoenix smiled faintly.

> **Phoenix:** “It's okay.”

Jake looked up.

> **Jake:** “Really?”

> **Phoenix:** “Really.”

---

# 2. INTRODUCTION

Phoenix carefully stood.

She remained slightly unsteady.

Bao immediately offered assistance.

Phoenix accepted.

> **Phoenix:** “Thank you.”

She looked around the room.

> **Phoenix:** “I suppose introductions are appropriate.”

She straightened her dress.

> **Phoenix:** “My name is Phoenix Raffedella.”

> **Phoenix:** “I'm a Medical Assistant with the Krampus Compliance Authority.”

> **Phoenix:** “I was asked to report to the NOST office while Dialyn was busy.”

She paused.

> **Phoenix:** “Apparently I should have stood farther away from the door.”

Jake quietly said:

> **Jake:** “Yes.”

Phoenix looked at him.

> **Phoenix:** “Apparently.”

---

Phoenix was gradually introduced to the SAFEHOUSE residents present.

### BAO

Bao introduced herself as one of the SAFEHOUSE residents.

Phoenix immediately recognized her medical reputation.

> **Phoenix:** “You're Bao.”

Bao nodded.

> **Bao:** “Yes.”

> **Phoenix:** “I've heard about you.”

> **Bao:** “Hopefully good things.”

Phoenix smiled.

> **Phoenix:** “Mostly.”

---

### NERI

Neri looked at Phoenix.

Phoenix looked at Neri.

A brief silence followed.

Neri quietly said:

> **Neri:** “Hi.”

Phoenix blinked.

> **Phoenix:** “Hi.”

Neri returned to her coffee.

Phoenix watched her for a moment.

> **Phoenix:** “She's quiet.”

Bao:

> **“Very.”**

Neri took another sip.

---

### BELLE

Phoenix already knew Belle.

Belle was the Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority.

Phoenix simply nodded toward her.

> **Phoenix:** “Director.”

> **Belle:** “Phoenix.”

Phoenix:

> **“Thank you for the adrenaline.”**

Belle:

> **“You needed it.”**

Phoenix:

> **“I did.”**

---

### JAKE

Phoenix looked at Jake.

Jake looked nervous.

> **Phoenix:** “And you're Jake.”

Jake nodded.

> **Jake:** “Yes.”

> **Phoenix:** “The one who opened the door.”

Jake's face fell.

> **Jake:** “I'm sorry.”

Phoenix immediately softened.

> **Phoenix:** “Jake.”

> **Phoenix:** “You didn't know I was there.”

> **Jake:** “Okay.”

> **Phoenix:** “Good.”

> **Dialyn is busy so she told me to come in here first**

Phoenix stared at it.

Then at the door.

> **Phoenix:** “I suppose that went well.”

> **“I nearly died.”**

> **“Technically, you did.”**

Belle:

> **“Technically.”**

Several hours later, the NOST office door opened.

Dialyn entered.

She matched the description previously provided to Jake:

- hair divided into two halves;
    
- left side black and white;
    
- right side white and black;
    
- left eye white;

> **Dialyn:** “Hello?”

> **Jake:** “You're Dialyn.”

> **Dialyn:** “Yes.”

Phoenix waved.

> **Phoenix:** “Hi.”

Then looked at Belle.

Then at Jake.

Then back at Phoenix.

> **Dialyn:** “What happened?”

> **Dialyn:** “What happened to Phoenix?”

Belle:

> **“Door.”**

Dialyn:

> **“...What?”**

# 6. THE EXPLANATION

Belle explained.

Phoenix had entered the NOST office.

Jake had opened the door.

Phoenix had been standing directly behind it.

The door struck her.

She lost consciousness.

Her body entered its emergency Ether response.

Adrenaline accelerated the process.

She recovered.

Dialyn listened.

Her expression gradually became more incredulous.

> **Dialyn:** “So let me get this straight.”

Belle nodded.

> **Dialyn:** “I told Phoenix to come here.”

> **Belle:** “Yes.”

> **Dialyn:** “She came here.”

> **Belle:** “Yes.”

> **Dialyn:** “And before I arrived…”

Belle nodded.

> **Belle:** “She died.”

Dialyn looked at Phoenix.

> **Dialyn:** “How?”

Phoenix pointed toward the door.

> **Phoenix:** “That.”

Dialyn stared at the door.

Long silence.

> **Dialyn:** “A door.”

Phoenix nodded.

> **Phoenix:** “A door.”

Dialyn closed her eyes.

Not at Aperture.

> **“It makes it technically accurate.”**

> **Dialyn:** “Phoenix.”

> **Phoenix:** “Yes?”

Phoenix:

> **“Sorry.”**

A new informal procedure was immediately established.

> **CHECK BEHIND IT.**

> **DO NOT STAND DIRECTLY BEHIND DOORS.**

Phoenix accepted this recommendation.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Phoenix Raffedella has successfully completed her introduction to the SAFEHOUSE.

The introduction has established:

- Phoenix is alive;
    
- Phoenix is medically knowledgeable;
    
- Phoenix possesses an anomalous post-mortem recovery mechanism;
    
- Phoenix is employed by the Krampus Compliance Authority;
    
- Phoenix knows Belle professionally;
    
- Phoenix is apparently comfortable around the SAFEHOUSE residents;
    
- and Phoenix has already developed an unfortunate association with the NOST office door.
    

Dialyn's arrival clarified the original misunderstanding.

Jake was expecting Dialyn.

Phoenix was the person who arrived first.

Jake opened the door.

Phoenix was too close.

The door won.

No hostility occurred.

No one is being blamed.

However, Dialyn has made her opinion regarding the door extremely clear.

For now.
