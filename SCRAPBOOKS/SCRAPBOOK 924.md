# SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL DEFENSE TEST

## PROJECTILE EVASION TRIAL — RESIDENT RESPONSE PROFILES

**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL  
**Test System:** Internal Defense System Turret  
**Ammunition:** Non-Lethal Training Rounds  
**Test Type:** Live Reaction / Projectile Avoidance  
**Participants:** SAFEHOUSE Residents  
**Purpose:** Determine individual responses to incoming projectile fire

---

# 1. TEST OVERVIEW

SAFEHOUSE personnel conducted a live projectile-evasion exercise using an **Internal Defense System Turret** configured to fire non-lethal training rounds.

The purpose was not to determine who could simply survive being hit.

The purpose was to determine:

> **How does each resident avoid being hit in the first place?**

The results demonstrated that SAFEHOUSE residents possess remarkably different approaches to incoming fire.

Some rely on speed.

Some rely on armor.

Some rely on anomalous abilities.

Some simply refuse to interact with the concept of being hit.

---

# 3. TAIGA

### Method:

**Rapid zigzag movement**

Taiga does not attempt to block the rounds.

She moves laterally and changes direction repeatedly while crossing the turret's firing line.

The turret's rotational tracking cannot compensate quickly enough while continuously firing.

The result is effectively:

> **The turret knows where Taiga is.**

> **It just can't turn fast enough to shoot there.**

Taiga completed the test without requiring cover.

---

# 4. MART

### Method:

**Riot shield**

Mart carries a riot shield while simultaneously retaining a compact semi-automatic shotgun.

His approach is considerably more direct than Taiga's.

Rather than attempting to evade every round, Mart simply places the shield between himself and the turret.

The shield allows him to:

- advance,
    
- reposition,
    
- absorb incoming training rounds,
    
- and maintain a defensive posture.
    

The shotgun is secondary to the test.

The shield is the important part.

---

# 5. ASTRAL

### Method:

**Being a ghost**

Astral's solution is technically the simplest.

She is a ghost.

Incoming projectiles therefore pass through her rather than striking a conventional physical target.

The turret continued firing.

Astral continued standing there.

The test supervisor eventually asked whether she intended to move.

Astral reportedly replied:

> **“Why?”**

The test supervisor had no answer.

---

# 6. STAR

### Method:

**Mass unanchoring**

Star can temporarily **unanchor her mass**, causing physical projectiles to become incapable of meaningfully interacting with her.

Unlike Astral, Star does not rely on being permanently intangible.

She deliberately changes her physical state.

Incoming rounds pass through her without producing a conventional impact.

Star described the process as:

> **“I just make myself not-touchable.”**

The technical explanation is considerably longer.

Star's explanation is more useful.

---

# 7. NIGHTSHADE

### Method:

**Rapid katana deflection**

Nightshade does not evade the rounds.

She intercepts them.

Her katana movements are sufficiently fast and precise to deflect incoming training rounds before they reach her.

The result is one of the most visually impressive responses recorded during the exercise.

It is also one of the methods that requires the greatest precision.

A single mistake would result in an impact.

Nightshade did not make one.

---

# 8. BELLE MERRYWETHER

### Method:

**Oversized sword as cover**

Belle uses her oversized sword almost like a portable shield.

Rather than attempting to dodge every incoming round, she positions the sword between herself and the turret.

The weapon's size provides sufficient coverage for her to move while remaining protected.

The testing team noted that Belle's solution was considerably more practical than initially expected.

---

# 9. ENDMIN MERRYWEATHER

### Method:

**Shielded wing**

Endmin uses one of her shielded wings as defensive cover.

The wing provides sufficient protection to intercept the incoming training rounds.

She does not need to completely conceal herself.

Instead, she angles the wing to protect the most vulnerable areas while maintaining mobility.

This gives Endmin a defensive posture uniquely suited to her anatomy.

---

# 10. CORIN

### Method:

**Mirage projection**

Corin generates a mirage of herself.

The turret's targeting system attempts to track the false image, producing an immediate targeting problem.

The turret can detect a target.

It simply cannot determine which target is real.

Corin consequently avoids the majority of incoming fire without needing to physically outrun the turret.

The test demonstrated an important advantage of the technique:

> **You don't have to dodge the projectile if the projectile isn't aimed at you.**

---

# 11. ORPHIE

### Method:

**Riot shield**

Orphie uses a riot shield as her primary defensive method.

Her approach is straightforward:

**Put shield between self and projectile.**

Unlike some of the more anomalous solutions tested, Orphie's defense requires no unusual interaction with the projectile itself.

It is simply effective cover carried with her.

---

# 12. ALICE

### Method:

**Don't be alone**

Alice's result required an additional note.

Alice generally does not operate alone.

She is normally accompanied by either:

- **Corin**, or
    
- **Orphie**.
    

This means Alice's preferred defense against incoming fire is not necessarily a personal evasive maneuver.

She has people with her.

If projectile fire occurs, Corin or Orphie can provide protection while Alice relocates.

The testing team therefore marked Alice's individual evasion profile as:

> **Not independently evaluated under normal operational conditions.**

This is intentional.

SAFEHOUSE does not consider Alice's tendency to stay accompanied a weakness.

---

# 13. BAO

### Method:

**Solid ice barrier**

Bao generates a solid ice block between herself and the turret.

The ice acts as immediate physical cover.

Unlike ordinary environmental cover, Bao can create the barrier wherever necessary.

This gives her a major advantage in environments where conventional cover is unavailable.

Her response was summarized by one technician as:

> **“She brought her own wall.”**

---

# 14. FROST

### Method:

**Freezing aura**

Frost does not need to create conventional cover.

She generates an aura around herself that freezes incoming projectiles before they reach her.

The rounds lose their effective trajectory once frozen.

Frost therefore effectively creates a defensive zone around herself.

The test team recorded this as one of the safest methods tested, as Frost does not need to physically intercept the projectiles.

---

# 15. NERI

### Method:

**Agility**

Neri's response was remarkably simple.

> **“Her agility is enough.”**

She rapidly sprinted and zigzagged through the firing area.

Her movements were sufficiently fast and unpredictable that the turret struggled to track her.

Unlike Taiga, Neri's movement pattern appeared less deliberate.

She simply moved **very quickly**.

When asked whether she wanted cover, Neri indicated that she was fine.

However, in normal circumstances, Neri is usually positioned **under Bao's protection** when projectile fire is expected.

This is considered the preferred approach.

Neri is capable of evading incoming projectiles.

That does not mean she should be required to.

---

# 16. COMPARATIVE OBSERVATION

The test demonstrated an interesting distinction between the residents.

Some avoid being hit by making themselves impossible to hit.

**Astral** — intangible.

**Star** — unanchored.

Some prevent the projectile from reaching them.

**Bao** — ice.

**Endmin** — shielded wing.

**Frost** — freezing aura.

Some make the projectile miss.

**Taiga** — movement.

**Neri** — movement.

**Corin** — deception.

Some simply destroy or intercept the projectile.

**Nightshade** — katana.

Others use conventional physical protection.

**Mart** — riot shield.

**Orphie** — riot shield.

**Belle** — oversized sword.

And Alice's solution is essentially:

> **Don't go anywhere alone.**

---

# 17. IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTE

This test used **non-lethal training rounds**.

The results should not be interpreted as proof that every resident can safely evade or withstand real ammunition.

In particular:

- projectile velocity may differ,
    
- tracking systems may differ,
    
- environmental conditions may differ,
    
- residents may become fatigued,
    
- and unexpected attacks may occur from multiple directions.
    

The exercise was designed to measure reactions under controlled conditions.

It was not a declaration of invulnerability.

---

# 18. NERI-SPECIFIC NOTE

Neri's performance generated particular discussion.

Her ability to rapidly sprint and zigzag is impressive.

However, personnel are reminded that Neri's medical condition makes prolonged high-intensity movement dangerous.

Her spontaneous anemia can occur regardless of physical exertion.

Therefore:

> **Neri being physically capable of dodging the turret does not mean Neri should be left to do so unnecessarily.**

When Bao is available, Neri commonly remains under Bao's protection.

Neri is fast enough to dodge.

Bao is safer.

C.O.R.E. recommends the second option.

---

# 19. FINAL CONCLUSION

The SAFEHOUSE residents demonstrated thirteen distinct approaches to the same problem.

There was no universal answer.

There was only:

> **“What does _this_ person do when something comes at them?”**

The answers ranged from highly technical anomalous abilities to simply carrying a very large sword.

The test supervisor's final observation was:

> **“I expected people to dodge bullets.”**

> **“Instead, everyone apparently brought their own interpretation of physics.”**

The Internal Defense System Turret has been returned to standby.

It has also been recommended that future testing involve **less emotionally traumatizing terminology** for the turret's firing profile.

The turret has no comment.

**END TEST REPORT**
