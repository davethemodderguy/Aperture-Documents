# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neri Tachyon's anemia is gone.

Not temporarily suppressed.

Not masked.

Not compensated for by Bao's blood.

Not merely improved.

**Gone.**

The medical team has confirmed the finding **five separate times.**

The fifth confirmation was performed specifically because nobody trusted the first four.

---

The first complete blood assessment produced an unexpected result.

Neri's blood parameters were within normal ranges.

The physician reviewing the results stared at the screen.

Then ran the test again.

The second result was the same.

A second physician reviewed the results.

They requested another assessment.

Same result.

At this point, the medical team became suspicious.

Not of Neri.

Of the equipment.

# 8. MEDICAL CONCLUSION

After five independent confirmations, C.O.R.E. Medical has formally updated Neri's medical status.

### PREVIOUS STATUS

> Chronic / recurrent severe anemia associated with abnormal red-cell destruction.

### CURRENT STATUS

> **No current evidence of anemia.**

The medical team has determined that Neri's previous condition has resolved.

The exact mechanism responsible for the permanent resolution remains under investigation.

The improvement cannot be attributed solely to a single recent intervention based on current evidence.

Further monitoring will continue.

However, monitoring is now precautionary rather than based on an active diagnosis.

> **The anemia is no longer an active condition.**

## C.O.R.E. FINAL ASSESSMENT

For years, Neri's blood was one of the most persistent medical concerns in Sector 4-C.

It failed her.

It depleted itself.

It left her exhausted.

It caused episodes in which she could barely breathe.

It caused her to collapse.

It caused her to hide symptoms.

It caused Bao to repeatedly intervene.

It caused Jane to fear finding her too late.

And now:

It is gone.

Five separate medical confirmations have produced the same result.

**Neri is no longer anemic.**

The medical team will continue monitoring her because she is still Neri, and because nobody at C.O.R.E. has forgotten what happened before.

But for the first time in her medical record, the phrase:

> **"Severe anemia — ongoing"**

has been replaced with:

> **"Severe anemia — resolved."**
