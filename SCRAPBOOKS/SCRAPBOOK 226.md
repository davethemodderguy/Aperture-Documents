### **EXTRADIMENSIONAL INVESTIGATION FILE**

## **SUBJECT: UNIVERSE C-02 – THE LOCKWOOD INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – EXECUTIVE EYES ONLY

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the confirmation that Corey Lockwood originated from Universe C-02, Research Division has reconstructed portions of her native timeline using recovered memories, neurological mapping, and corroborating evidence from the Corin Paradox.

The following information has been assigned a **High Confidence** rating.

---

# **THE LOCKWOOD FAMILY**

Evidence indicates that the Lockwood family of Universe C-02 consisted of:

- Arcturus Lockwood
    
- Norma Lockwood
    
- Corey Lockwood
    

No evidence has been found suggesting the existence of Lumi within Universe C-02.

Further investigation is ongoing.

At the time of the incident, Corey was the same age Corin had been during the childhood events that altered her own life.

---

# **THE RESEARCH**

Arcturus Lockwood had been conducting research of unknown scientific significance.

Recovered fragments suggest the work was of exceptional value.

The exact subject remains unknown.

What is known is that another organization wanted it badly enough to kill for it.

The research was stolen during the assault.

Its current location remains unknown.

---

# **THE INCIDENT**

Recovered memories consistently describe the same sequence.

An unidentified armed organization entered the Lockwood residence.

The attack was deliberate.

The objective appears to have been the acquisition of Arcturus' research.

Corey was hidden by her parents while they attempted to delay the attackers.

She witnessed the confrontation from concealment.

Arcturus and Norma were killed.

Neither survived the attack.

Corey remained hidden until the assailants began searching the residence.

She was eventually discovered.

Witness memory reconstruction indicates she was forcibly dragged from her hiding place.

One individual restrained her.

Another fired a single shot into her neck.

Neurological reconstruction ends shortly afterward.

The final confirmed memory is the sound of footsteps leaving the residence.

---

# **THE IMPOSSIBILITY**

Everything after Corey's death remains unexplained.

Medical science cannot account for the continuation of her consciousness.

Research has found no evidence of resurrection within Universe C-02.

Instead, all available evidence indicates that, following her death, Corey's existence became linked to Corin Lockwood of this universe.

How a deceased child from one universe came to exist as a living adult in another remains entirely unknown.

No known anomalous mechanism can adequately explain the transition.

Research has designated the event:

> **Inter-Universal Convergence Event Ω**

The cause remains unidentified.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL IMPLICATIONS**

Medical Services believe Corey's persistent fear of unexpected loud noises likely originates from the final moments of her life.

Unexpected bangs, gunfire, slamming doors, and explosive sounds consistently trigger a disproportionate startle response.

Not because Corey consciously remembers dying.

But because her nervous system appears to remember what her conscious mind cannot fully articulate.

Unlike Corin, whose trauma centered on prolonged experimentation and dehumanization, Corey's defining trauma was sudden, violent, and irrevocable.

The two histories are different.

The resulting scars are different.

# **CASE STATUS**

The organization responsible for the Lockwood Incident in Universe C-02 remains unidentified.

There is currently no evidence that it exists within this universe.

However, the theft of Arcturus Lockwood's research remains unresolved.

Whether the stolen research bears any connection to the Corin Paradox or the Inter-Universal Convergence Event Ω is unknown.

The investigation remains active.

Until further evidence is recovered, the Lockwood Incident of Universe C-02 stands as both a historical reconstruction...

And a reminder that tragedy can echo across realities.
