# 1. IDENTIFICATION UPDATE

Nora Tachyon's white beret has been formally added to her identifying appearance profile.

The beret is a consistent part of Nora's usual presentation and is frequently worn while she is:

- playing;
    
- visiting the Tachyon Merch Store;
    
- accompanying Neri;
    
- interacting with SAFEHOUSE residents;
    
- traveling with Sky;
    
- or simply wandering around Sector 4-C.
    

The headwear is particularly useful for distinguishing Nora from other young residents at a distance.

---

# 2. BERET DESCRIPTION

Nora's beret is:

**Primary color:** White

**Strap:** Navy blue

**Front insignia:** Golden moon medal

The navy-blue strap runs across the lower portion of the beret.

Centered on the front is a small **golden moon-shaped medal**.

The medal is decorative and carries no known anomalous function.

It is simply part of Nora's personal appearance.

---

# 3. SKY & THE BERET

Sky has demonstrated no difficulty landing on Nora's beret.

The familiar's claws do not damage the fabric.

The golden moon medal does not interfere with Sky's grip.

This has become one of Sky's preferred perching positions.

Typical configuration:

> **Nora:** walking  
> **Sky:** perched on beret  
> **Golden moon medal:** visible  
> **Nora's family journal:** clipped at waist  
> **Nora's shark tail:** moving normally

C.O.R.E. considers this a highly recognizable visual profile.

---
