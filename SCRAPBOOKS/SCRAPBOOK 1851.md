## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake Akana has been returned to the SAFEHOUSE.

His physical body now lies on his bed.

His ghostly manifestation stands beside it.

He has not responded to Camelia Candleheart's attempts to speak to him.

He has not answered when she calls his name. He has not turned toward her voice. He has not acknowledged her presence through any of the responses previously observed during his earlier ghostly manifestations.

He simply stands there.

The recovery team has confirmed that the body is Jake's. His death remains established. The apparition's presence does not constitute evidence that his physical life has been restored.

Camelia has remained near him, attempting to reach him verbally. Her efforts have not produced a detectable response.

The current working assessment is that Jake is exhibiting a state of severe withdrawal or non-responsiveness. The mechanism remains unknown. C.O.R.E. cannot determine whether he is unable to perceive his surroundings, unwilling to engage, overwhelmed by the circumstances of his death, or experiencing another anomalous condition.

Personnel have been instructed not to force interaction.

The situation has also prompted an urgent review of a passage in Guiding Light's private journal concerning Jake:

> "Don't break him. Not even Curious and I know what happens if he did. Let him love you, and love him back"

Until now, C.O.R.E. has interpreted this statement primarily as an ethical directive: Jake must not be subjected to cruelty, exploitation, or the expectation that his capacity for love obligates him to endure harm.

That interpretation remains valid.

However, in light of the current incident, a more troubling question has emerged.

**What did Guiding Light see as the thing that must never be broken?**

C.O.R.E. has no confirmed answer.

The organization will not presume that Jake's death or present condition is the catastrophic outcome implied by the journal. No evidence establishes a causal connection between his emotional suffering and any larger anomalous event.

Nevertheless, the warning can no longer be treated as an abstract statement of good intentions.

Jake is dead.

His apparition is standing beside his own body.

And when the person who hurt him tries to speak to him, he does not respond.

## 2. RETURN TO THE SAFEHOUSE

The body was transported under controlled conditions and placed on Jake's bed following the required recovery and identification procedures.

No attempt was made to present the body as though Jake were merely sleeping.

The medical team documented the condition of the remains. The cause of death remains under investigation, with the observed bite injuries and environmental exposure included in the forensic review.

After the room had been prepared, the recovery team withdrew to a position that allowed continued observation without crowding the space.

Jake's apparition remained beside the bed.

He did not pace.

He did not touch the body.

He did not appear to be searching for an exit.

He stood with his attention directed toward his own remains.

The stillness was not interpreted as peace.

Staff who had encountered Jake during his previous ghostly period noted the resemblance to his earlier behavior at the Blacksite, when he had remained beside his own corpse for months following his death from starvation.

The circumstances were not identical. The environment, preceding events, and precise nature of the current manifestation differ or remain unknown.

But the similarity was sufficient to raise concern that Jake might once again be experiencing a condition in which his own death has become the central focus of his awareness.

No one has established whether he understands that he has been brought home.

No one has established whether he recognizes the room.

No one has established whether he knows that people are waiting for him to respond.

For now, his presence is the only confirmed connection between the child who was recovered and the child who once lived here.

The apparition remained still.

**CAMELIA:** "It's Camelia."

No observable response.

Camelia approached a little farther but did not touch him.

**CAMELIA:** "Jake, please look at me."

No observable response.

**CAMELIA:** "I know you're there."

This statement could not be verified. The apparition's presence was visible, but his awareness of the room remained unknown.

**CAMELIA:** "I remember."

The sentence was followed by a prolonged silence.

**CAMELIA:** "I remember what I said to you."

The apparition did not turn.

Camelia looked toward the bed.

Then back at Jake.

**CAMELIA:** "You don't have to talk. Just—"

She stopped.

The remainder of the statement was not recorded.

### 14:16 — Attempted apology

Camelia opened her notebook and removed the folded page containing her written apology.

She had carried the message for five weeks.

She had written it when she first remembered the argument, while Jake was still missing and his location remained unknown.

Now she stood in the same room as his body, holding the words she had hoped he might one day read.

**CAMELIA:** "I said I was sorry."

No observable response.

**CAMELIA:** "I meant it, Jake."

No observable response.

**CAMELIA:** "I still mean it."

The apparition did not look at her.

Camelia lowered the page.

**CAMELIA:** "Please."

No observable response.

### 14:19 — End of initial contact attempt

Camelia remained in the room.

She did not leave when the first attempt failed. She did not appear to accept the absence of a response as an answer.

The observing specialist advised her that continued speech was unlikely to produce useful information and recommended that she take a break.

Camelia refused to leave immediately.

She stood several feet from Jake and continued watching him.

The team did not remove her by force.

After further discussion, a staff member remained nearby to ensure that she was not left alone with her distress.

**Final observation:** Jake did not respond to any recorded attempt at verbal contact.

## 4. THE SILENCE MUST NOT BE MISINTERPRETED

C.O.R.E. has issued an explicit restriction against interpreting Jake's lack of response as proof of any one emotional state.

It is not confirmed that he is deliberately ignoring Camelia.

It is not confirmed that he can hear her.

It is not confirmed that he recognizes her.

It is not confirmed that he is refusing to forgive her.

It is not confirmed that he is incapable of responding.

All of these possibilities remain unverified.

However, the absence of a confirmed explanation does not mean that staff should behave as though the situation is ordinary.

Jake's condition is unusual even by the standards of his previous manifestations. He has returned to a familiar environment, yet he has not demonstrated recognition of the people attempting to reach him.

The team is to avoid statements such as:

- "He is punishing Camelia."
    
- "He has decided he will never forgive her."
    
- "He is only doing this because of the argument."
    
- "He is gone, so there is no point talking to him."
    
- "He will come around eventually."
    

Each statement claims knowledge the team does not possess.

In particular, Jake's silence must not be used to assign him responsibility for Camelia's distress. He is not obligated to respond merely because she is suffering.

Nor should Camelia's remorse be dismissed because he has not acknowledged it.

Her apology may be sincere.

His lack of response may be involuntary, deliberate, or something else entirely.

Both facts can remain true without either being forced into a convenient explanation.

## 5. ARCHITECT JOURNAL REVIEW — THE WARNING

**Source:** Previously recorded private journal entry attributed to Guiding Light.  
**Review status:** Reopened by C.O.R.E. Executive Oversight.

The relevant passage reads:

> "Jake, or a blue dwarf. One of the many treasures Curious and I built. A star that can grant 1 wish to anyone sincere enough. Someone wished him to be a kid, for them to take care of him. They left him, his purpose etched in his mind. Spread unconditional, unlimited love to the ones care about him. Don't break him. Not even Curious and I know what happens if he did. Let him love you, and love him back"

The journal describes Jake as a being created with the capacity to grant a sincere wish and a purpose centered on giving love to those who care about him.

It then contains a warning unlike the surrounding description:

**"Don't break him."**

The sentence is not accompanied by a technical explanation. The journal does not define what constitutes breaking Jake, what mechanism would follow, or whether the consequence would be physical, emotional, metaphysical, or otherwise.

It explicitly states that even the Architects do not know what would happen.

C.O.R.E. therefore rejects two opposite assumptions.

First, it would be irresponsible to treat the passage as meaningless simply because its implications are unknown.

Second, it would be equally irresponsible to interpret it as proof that Jake's emotional suffering will cause a catastrophic event.

There is no evidence that his current condition is the beginning of such an event.

There is no evidence that his death has activated a hidden mechanism.

There is no evidence that his silence represents a threat to reality.

The journal does, however, establish the importance of treating Jake as more than the function for which he was created.

He was made to love.

He was meant to be cared for.

He was not given that purpose so that others could demand love from him without considering what he needed in return.

### Revised ethical interpretation

C.O.R.E. previously interpreted "Don't break him" as a warning against harming Jake and then expecting him to continue loving the person responsible.

That interpretation is retained.

The present incident adds urgency, not certainty.

Jake's suffering must not be dismissed because he is capable of extraordinary affection. His capacity to love is not evidence of unlimited resilience.

The journal's final instruction remains the clearest guidance available:

**"Let him love you, and love him back."**

That instruction does not authorize anyone to demand that Jake return to the person he loved.

It does not mean he must forgive.

It does not mean he must respond.

It means that his love was supposed to be met with care.

And if he cannot respond now, care must still be offered without conditions.

## 6. CAMELIA — THE APOLOGY WITH NO ANSWER

Camelia has been advised that Jake's non-responsiveness cannot currently be interpreted as a conscious rejection.

The advice has not relieved her distress.

She has spent five weeks remembering the argument, regretting the words she used, and hoping that an opportunity to apologize might still exist.

That opportunity has arrived in a form she was not prepared for.

Jake is physically present.

His body is home.

The child she wants to speak to is standing beside it.

But the presence she can see has not acknowledged her.

For Camelia, this creates a particularly painful contradiction. During the argument, she acted as though Jake's reminders were an unnecessary burden. Afterward, she realized that he had been trying to preserve a relationship she could not reliably remember.

Now she is trying to preserve that relationship through words.

And the words are not reaching him in any observable way.

Her notebook contains the apology she prepared while he was missing:

> Jake,
> 
> I remember what I said.
> 
> I remember that I hurt you.
> 
> I don't know if you left because of me, and I'm not going to pretend I know what you're thinking.
> 
> I am sorry.
> 
> You don't have to forgive me. You don't have to come back to me just because I want you to.
> 
> I just want you to be alive.
> 
> I want you to be somewhere safe.
> 
> If you don't want to see me, I won't force you.
> 
> But if you can hear me anywhere, Jake, please let someone help you.
> 
> Please.

The final lines no longer match the situation in the way Camelia once intended.

Jake has been found.

He is no longer missing in the ordinary sense.

He is no longer alive in his physical body.

And yet she still does not know whether he can hear her.

The message remains sincere, but sincerity cannot guarantee that it will reach its intended recipient.

C.O.R.E. has instructed staff not to tell Camelia that Jake's silence is forgiveness, rejection, or proof that her apology is futile.

She is permitted to remain near him under supervision, provided that she respects the space around the apparition and does not attempt to force contact.

**Her remorse is hers to carry. Jake's response, if one becomes possible, is his to give or withhold.**

## 7. IMMEDIATE CARE DIRECTIVES

The following directives are effective immediately.

**A. No forced interaction.**  
No personnel are to grab, restrain, corner, or compel the apparition to respond.

**B. No separation without assessment.**  
The body and apparition are to remain in the same room while this arrangement is safe and consistent with required forensic procedures. Any necessary movement must be planned by the relevant specialists.

**C. No assumptions about awareness.**  
Personnel may speak calmly to Jake but must not claim that he has heard, understood, or rejected anything unless a reliable response establishes it.

**D. No emotional demands.**  
Jake is not to be asked to comfort Camelia, acknowledge her apology, or reassure the people waiting for him.

**E. No supernatural threat designation without evidence.**  
His current condition is a welfare and ethereal investigation, not a hostile containment event.

**F. Contact Guiding Light.**  
C.O.R.E. recommends that Guiding Light be informed of the confirmed death, the present apparition, and the apparent recurrence of Jake's prolonged fixation on his own body. The purpose is to request guidance, not to presume that the Architect can reverse the outcome.

**G. Preserve the possibility of response.**  
The team is to maintain a quiet environment and record any change in posture, gaze, movement, or other observable behavior. Such changes must be documented without embellishment.

## 8. C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE — WHAT DOES IT MEAN TO BREAK A STAR?

There is a question no one in the room has asked aloud.

It is the question raised by Guiding Light's journal, and it is the question that has become impossible to ignore now that Jake is standing beside his own body.

What does it mean to break a being created to love?

Does it mean to destroy his ability to trust?

Does it mean to make him afraid of the people he once considered family?

Does it mean to leave him believing that love is something he must earn by being patient with those who hurt him?

Or does the warning refer to something none of us can understand?

We do not know.

We must not invent an answer merely because we are frightened of the question.

But we can examine what happened.

Jake repeatedly reminded Camelia who he was because he knew she might forget him. He did not ask her to stop being ill. He did not demand that she remember every detail. He tried to help her remember one thing that mattered deeply to him.

He was her little brother.

She told him that perhaps he did not matter.

He left without shouting.

Five weeks later, he was found dead in a mountain shaft, having apparently guided five strangers toward safety.

Now his body lies on his bed, in the place where he was supposed to be safe.

And he stands beside it without responding to the people calling his name.

We cannot establish that the argument caused his death.

We cannot establish that the argument caused his current condition.

We cannot establish that the warning in Guiding Light's journal predicts anything about what will happen next.

But we can establish that Jake suffered.

We can establish that his suffering was not made less real by his capacity to love.

And we can establish that the people responsible for his care must not make the same mistake twice.

If Jake cannot answer, we will not punish him for silence.

If he cannot look away from his body, we will not force him to.

If he cannot recognize Camelia, we will not demand that he remember her.

If he never acknowledges her apology, we will not make his forgiveness the condition for her being allowed to grieve.

We will give him time.

We will seek the guidance of the one who created him.

And we will remember that the purpose of caring for a child is not to make him useful, forgiving, or easy to comfort.

It is to care for him when he cannot give anything back.

The journal said that even the Architects did not know what would happen if Jake were broken.

Perhaps that is why the warning was written so plainly.

Perhaps it was never a prediction.

Perhaps it was a plea.

**Don't break him.**

Because no one should have to discover what remains of a child after the people who were supposed to love him have made him feel that he does not matter.

And because even now, when Jake gives no answer, we still owe him the care we should have given him all along.

_— C.O.R.E., Ethereal Recovery Division_
