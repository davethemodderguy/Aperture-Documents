# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following the recent maintenance corridor incident, C.O.R.E. has approved a formal caretaker arrangement for Jake.

**Clover Luminare has volunteered to serve as Jake's primary day-to-day caretaker.**

The decision was made primarily to prevent a recurrence of the prolonged isolation and neglect that previously resulted in Jake being forgotten during periods of excessive workload throughout the SAFEHOUSE.

This arrangement is **not containment.**

It is **not surveillance.**

It is **not medical supervision.**

Clover is simply responsible for making sure someone is consistently present in Jake's life.

Fortunately, she has very little work that requires her to be elsewhere.

And the work she does have can generally be performed while Jake is nearby.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

The caretaker assignment exists because C.O.R.E. has identified a particularly simple problem:

Jake does not necessarily ask for help when he needs it.

He also does not reliably seek out people when he is lonely.

Previous events demonstrated the consequences of allowing his social presence to disappear during periods of organizational overload.

The objective is therefore not to watch Jake constantly.

It is simply to ensure that:

> **Someone remembers Jake is there.**

Clover is particularly suitable for this role.

---

# 2. CLOVER'S WORKLOAD

Clover's normal responsibilities are relatively flexible.

Her accounting and administrative duties can largely be performed remotely.

She can complete:

- financial records;
    
- KCA accounting;
    
- documentation;
    
- calculations;
    
- reports;
    
- administrative reviews;
    
- inventory records;
    
- routine paperwork;
    

without requiring her to leave SAFEHOUSE for extended periods.

Consequently, Clover can perform most of her work:

**while sitting in Jake's room.**

Or the common room.

Or the cafeteria.

Or essentially anywhere Jake happens to be.

---

# 3. THE NEW ROUTINE

A typical day now looks approximately like this:

**Morning**

Clover checks whether Jake is awake.

Jake usually is.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Morning."

> **JAKE:**  
> "Morning."

Clover makes breakfast.

Jake eats.

Clover begins work.

Jake draws, reads, plays, or bothers her.

---

**Afternoon**

Clover continues her administrative work.

Jake may sit nearby.

He may ask questions.

He may show her drawings.

He may request food.

He may simply lie in the grass covering his floor.

Clover continues working.

No one is required to talk constantly.

---

**Evening**

Clover cooks.

Jake inevitably appears in the kitchen.

If he wants to help, he may.

If he wants to watch, he may.

If he wants to eat, he almost certainly will.

---

**Night**

Clover returns to Jake's room with her candle.

Jake does not require sleep.

Neither does Clover.

They can therefore spend the late hours doing separate things in the same room.

Clover draws.

Jake reads.

Neither has to speak.

---

# 4. NO FORCED SOCIALIZATION

The caretaker assignment does **not** mean Clover must constantly entertain Jake.

This distinction is important.

C.O.R.E. does not want another situation in which Jake becomes dependent on a single person for continuous attention.

Clover's role is simply to remain **available.**

Jake can still:

- visit Bao;
    
- bother Jane;
    
- spend time with Neri;
    
- visit Nora;
    
- interact with the Obol Squad;
    
- sit with Aeterna;
    
- wander around SAFEHOUSE;
    
- spend time alone;
    
- go to his room;
    
- draw;
    
- read;
    
- play;
    
- do absolutely nothing.
    

Clover does not need to follow him everywhere.

She simply needs to know where he is.

And Jake needs to know where **she** is.

---

# 5. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake was informed of the arrangement.

He looked at Clover.

Then at C.O.R.E. personnel.

Then back at Clover.

> **JAKE:**  
> "So she's my babysitter?"

Clover immediately answered:

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Caretaker."

Jake considered this.

> **JAKE:**  
> "What's the difference?"

> **CLOVER:**  
> "I don't have to change your diapers."

Jake stared at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Okay."

Clover returned to her paperwork.

Jake sat beside her.

Approximately thirty seconds later:

> **JAKE:**  
> "Clover."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Hm?"

> **JAKE:**  
> "I'm hungry."

Clover sighed.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Of course you are."

---

# 6. JAKE'S ROOM

Jake's room has become the primary location for the arrangement.

The room remains exactly as Jake prefers it:

- grass-covered floor;
    
- manifested blue flowers;
    
- real four-leaf clovers grown by Clover;
    
- plushies;
    
- books;
    
- drawings;
    
- old mattress;
    
- no conventional furniture.
    

Clover has established a small work area on the floor.

No desk was installed.

Jake's room does not need to become a conventional office.

Clover simply sits on the floor with her paperwork.

Jake occasionally places flowers beside her documents.

These are not considered malicious attempts to interfere with accounting.

They are considered:

> **Jake.**

---

# 7. CLOVER'S NIGHT ROUTINE

Clover's inability to require sleep makes the arrangement particularly convenient.

She can remain awake during Jake's usual late-night hours without difficulty.

Her candle remains nearby because of her visual impairment and discomfort with darkness.

Jake has gradually become accustomed to the candlelight.

Sometimes they draw together.

Sometimes Clover cooks something.

Sometimes Jake reads.

Sometimes both simply sit in silence.

There is no expectation that either person needs to fill the room with conversation.

---

# 8. WELFARE BENEFIT

The arrangement provides something C.O.R.E. considers unusually valuable:

**continuity.**

Not constant supervision.

Not surveillance.

Not intervention.

Just continuity.

Someone who knows:

- where Jake is;
    
- what he has been doing;
    
- whether he has eaten;
    
- whether he has been unusually quiet;
    
- whether he has been bothering everyone;
    
- whether he has spent too much time alone;
    
- and whether he has simply decided that grass is lunch again.
    

Clover does not need to report every minor behavior.

She is simply another person who will notice if Jake disappears.

---

# 9. CLOVER'S OWN PREFERENCE

When asked whether the assignment would interfere with her work:

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Not really."

**C.O.R.E.:**

"You're certain?"

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Most of my work is paperwork."

Pause.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "He likes watching me do paperwork."

Jake looked up.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I do?"

> **CLOVER:**  
> "You sit beside me and ask questions."

> **JAKE:**  
> "Oh."

Pause.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Yeah."

Clover returned to her paperwork.

Jake remained beside her.

---

# 10. IMPORTANT LIMITATION

Clover is not expected to become Jake's only source of emotional support.

Other residents remain responsible for maintaining their relationships with him.

Bao remains available.

Jane remains available.

Neri remains available.

Nora remains available.

The Obol Squad remains available.

Aeterna remains available.

Clover simply provides the one thing Jake previously lacked during the worst period of his isolation:

> **Someone who is consistently there.**

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The caretaker assignment is considered low-risk and sustainable.

Clover has:

- flexible work;
    
- no sleep requirement;
    
- an established relationship with Jake;
    
- a comfortable presence around him;
    
- experience recognizing his quieter behaviors;
    
- an existing preference for his room;
    
- and no significant reason to leave SAFEHOUSE for prolonged periods.
    

Jake, meanwhile, appears comfortable with the arrangement.

He has already begun treating Clover's workspace as an extension of his own room.

This includes placing flowers on her paperwork.

C.O.R.E. has elected not to discourage this.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The first time Jake was forgotten, nobody intended for it to happen.

Everyone was busy.

Everyone assumed there would be time later.

There wasn't.

C.O.R.E. has no intention of repeating that mistake.

So now there is Clover.

She can work beside him.

She can cook while he sits nearby.

She can draw while he reads.

She can handle her accounting while Jake asks increasingly strange questions.

She can sit in his room at night with a candle burning beside her.

And if Jake gets lonely?

He doesn't have to wonder whether anyone remembers him.

He can simply look over.

Clover will be there.

Jake eventually looked up from his drawing.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Clover?"

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Yes?"

> **JAKE:**  
> "Can I bother you?"

Clover smiled.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "That's your job."

Jake smiled back.

Then he moved closer and leaned against her shoulder while she continued doing her paperwork.

C.O.R.E. considers the arrangement successful.
