### **SAFEHOUSE PERSONNEL / EMOTIONAL RESPONSE REPORT**

# **THE SAFEHOUSE RESPONDS — CORIN AND ORPHIE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Family Crisis  
**Subjects:** Corin / Orphie  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE — Central Living Area  
**Status:** **CRITICAL**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The SAFEHOUSE residents were informed that Corin had deteriorated significantly following Orphie's disappearance.

The Marts had already stopped approaching Orphie.

Now they had stopped approaching Corin.

For the residents, this confirmed something far worse than simple isolation.

Both sisters were disappearing from the people around them.

The SAFEHOUSE reaction was immediate.

---

# **STAR**

Star broke down.

She cried loudly.

Not quietly.

Not gracefully.

She simply could not hold it in.

Star had always been one of the most relentlessly positive people in the SAFEHOUSE.

She was the person who could walk into a room and make it feel brighter.

She was the person who believed that almost anyone could be helped.

The idea that both Orphie and Corin had reached a point where even the Marts were afraid to approach them devastated her.

> **Star:** "...Why?"

She covered her face.

> "...Why can't we help them?"

Her voice broke.

> "...They're our family."

Star continued crying.

> "...I can't fix this."

---

# **NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade immediately stayed beside Star.

She did not attempt to give her a speech.

She simply held her.

> **Nightshade:** "...It's okay."

Star continued crying.

> "...No, it isn't."

Nightshade remained calm.

> "...I know."

> "...They're disappearing."

> "...I know."

> "...And we're just standing here."

Nightshade tightened her arms around Star.

> "...We're not."

She paused.

> "...We're still here."

---

# **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus reacted differently.

He became extremely quiet.

As Orphie and Corin's older brother, he had spent much of his life viewing himself as someone who should be able to protect his younger sisters.

Now one had disappeared into the forest.

The other was psychologically deteriorating inside the same home.

He sat alone for several minutes.

Eventually:

> "...I should've noticed."

Nobody answered.

> "...I should've known."

Norma immediately interrupted.

> "...Don't."

Arcturus looked at her.

> "...What?"

> "...Don't blame yourself."

He looked toward the floor.

> "...I'm their big brother."

Norma:

> "...And you're still their brother."

---

# **NORMA**

Norma's response was much more controlled.

She did not cry.

She did not shout.

She began asking questions.

Where was Corin?

What was her current condition?

How long had she been alone?

What exactly happened with the Marts?

Was Orphie confirmed alive?

Was there any evidence of where she had gone?

She was trying to build a solution.

But eventually, even Norma's composure cracked.

She looked at Arcturus.

> "...We're losing both of them."

Arcturus remained silent.

Norma's voice softened.

> "...I don't want to lose my sisters."

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi said almost nothing.

She simply listened.

She heard everything.

Orphie disappearing.

Corin becoming unstable.

The Marts fleeing.

Star crying.

Her children struggling.

For a long time, she remained completely still.

Then she asked:

> "...Where is Corin?"

Someone answered:

> "...At the treehouse."

> "...And Orphie?"

A pause.

> "...Unknown."

Lumi closed her eyes.

---

# **A MOTHER'S RESPONSE**

Lumi had already been dealing with her own illness.

Yet when she heard what had happened, her condition became secondary in her mind.

She stood.

Slowly.

> "...I need to see my daughter."

Arcturus immediately approached.

> "...Mom, you shouldn't."

Lumi looked at him.

> "...She's alone."

> "...You're sick."

> "...She's alone."

There was no argument after that.

---

# **THE SAFEHOUSE**

The entire central living area became unusually quiet.

Nobody knew what to say.

The SAFEHOUSE had always been loud.

Marts bounced through hallways.

Star laughed.

Bao cooked.

Taiga wandered around happily.

People talked.

People argued.

People watched movies.

People existed together.

Now everyone was thinking about the same place.

The remote treehouse.

---

# **THE MISSING SISTERS**

Orphie had spent months believing:

> **"I'm unimportant."**

Corin had spent months proving that she would stay.

Eventually, Orphie disappeared.

Then Corin began falling apart.

Now both sisters were isolated.

One by choice.

The other because the person she depended on was gone.

---

# **STAR**

Star eventually stopped crying loudly.

She wiped her face.

> "...I'm going to help them."

Nightshade looked at her.

> "...You don't have to do it alone."

Star nodded.

> "...I know."

---

# **NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade remained beside Star.

Her role was no longer simply calming her.

She understood that Star's hope was one of the things holding the SAFEHOUSE together.

> "...You can't carry everyone."

Star:

> "...Then we'll carry each other."

Nightshade smiled faintly.

> "...That's better."

---

# **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus finally stood.

> "...I'm going."

Norma:

> "...I'm coming."

Arcturus:

> "...No."

> "...I'm her sister."

He paused.

> "...I'm going."

---

# **NORMA**

Norma followed anyway.

> "...You're not going alone."

Arcturus sighed.

> "...Fine."

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi watched her children prepare.

She quietly picked up her coat.

> "...I'm coming too."

Arcturus turned.

> "...Mom."

Lumi:

> "...She's my daughter."

Nobody argued.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The SAFEHOUSE had always been built around one idea:

> **"Make it feel like a home, not a containment chamber."**

Tonight, everyone was reminded why.

A home is not defined by walls.

It is defined by the people who refuse to stop caring when someone disappears.

Star cried because she could not accept that hope had failed two people she loved.

Nightshade stayed beside her.

Arcturus blamed himself because he believed he had failed as an older brother.

Norma refused to let him carry that blame alone.

And Lumi, despite everything her own body was going through, only had one thought:

> **"My daughters are alone."**

For the first time in a long while, the SAFEHOUSE did not feel like a collection of residents.

It felt like a family preparing to bring two of its own home.

**End of SAFEHOUSE Personnel / Emotional Response Report**
