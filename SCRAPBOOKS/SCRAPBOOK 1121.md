# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

Neri's recent Armory visits have officially progressed from:

> **“Looking for inspiration.”**

to:

> **“Taking inspiration home.”**

At approximately 16:12, an inventory discrepancy was identified involving the following assets:

**MP5SD platforms:** multiple units

**4.6×30mm ammunition:** approximately **150 rounds**

The equipment was subsequently confirmed to have been removed from the Armory by:

> **Neri.**

No evidence indicates hostile intent.

No evidence indicates an attempt to conceal the removal.

No evidence indicates that Neri understands why everyone is currently concerned.

---

# 2. SECURITY FOOTAGE

Armory footage was reviewed.

Neri entered normally.

She walked to the relevant storage area.

She examined the MP5SDs.

She examined the ammunition.

She paused.

She apparently made a decision.

She placed the equipment inside her oversized coat.

She walked out.

The Armory door closed behind her.

Approximately twelve seconds later, the Armory technician monitoring the feed said:

> **“...Did she just take those?”**

Another technician:

> **“Yes.”**

Pause.

> **“Should we stop her?”**

The first technician:

> **“Do you want to tell Neri no?”**

Neither technician pursued the matter.

---

# 3. NEKO'S RESPONSE

Neko was notified.

He immediately requested the inventory report.

After reading it:

> **Neko:**  
> “MP5SDs?”

Pause.

> **Neko:**  
> “And 4.6?”

Another pause.

> **Neko:**  
> “How much?”

> **ARMORY TECHNICIAN:**  
> “One hundred and fifty rounds.”

Neko closed his eyes.

> **Neko:**  
> “She's making something.”

Neri has not returned the equipment.

She has not explained what she intends to do with it.

She has not requested authorization for a new project.

She has not even admitted that she is working on anything.

When asked:

> **“What are you doing with the MP5SDs?”**

Neri replied:

> **“Research.”**

When asked:

> **“What kind of research?”**

Neri:

> **“...Research.”**

No further clarification was provided.

---

# 5. WHY THIS IS CONCERNING

The issue is not the MP5SDs themselves.

The issue is the sequence.

Neri has recently:

1. spent increasing amounts of time in the Armory;
    
2. examined numerous weapon platforms;
    
3. taken extensive notes;
    
4. asked increasingly specific questions regarding materials and design;
    
5. repeatedly used the phrase **“inspiration”**;
    
6. and now removed multiple MP5SDs and ammunition.
    

This strongly suggests that the observation phase has ended.

Neri may now be entering the:

> **“Let's see if I can make this better.”**

phase.

C.O.R.E. has considerable historical evidence suggesting that this phase should be monitored carefully.

---

# 6. THE NIGHT STALKER PRECEDENT

There is an obvious reason Armory personnel are nervous.

The last time Neri became sufficiently interested in an existing weapon platform, the result was:

> **NIGHT STALKER**

What began as discarded M1911s eventually became one of the most restricted and historically significant weapons in the Blacksite.

Ten were produced.

Only ten.

The final unit is now permanently displayed in the Blacksite Museum.

Neri subsequently announced:

> **“I won't make anymore Night Stalkers.”**

This statement remains valid.

However, nothing in that statement prevents her from making:

> **something else.**

---

There is currently insufficient evidence to determine what Neri intends to develop.

Possible explanations include:

- material research;
    
- ammunition research;
    
- ergonomic experimentation;
    
- anomalous-material compatibility testing;
    
- a completely unrelated chemistry project;
    
- modification of an existing platform;
    
- or something C.O.R.E. has not yet considered.
    

The last possibility is regarded as the most likely to cause Neko a headache.

---

# 8. NERI'S RESEARCH BEHAVIOR

Personnel should remember that Neri does not necessarily begin projects with a complete objective.

Her established research pattern is:

> **See something.**

> **Become curious.**

> **Ask why.**

> **Take notes.**

> **Experiment.**

> **Discover something unexpected.**

> **Ask another question.**

This process can continue indefinitely.

Her 12 TB research incident demonstrated the scale of this tendency.

Therefore, it is entirely possible that Neri herself does not yet know what she is making.

She may simply have found something interesting.

---

# 9. ARMORY RESPONSE

The Armory has been instructed to:

- document missing inventory;
    
- preserve all relevant security footage;
    
- notify Neko of further removals;
    
- avoid confrontation unless immediate safety requires intervention;
    
- maintain accurate records;
    
- and, most importantly,
    

**stop leaving interesting things where Neri can reach them.**

The final recommendation was added after a lengthy argument.

Neko supported it.

Neri did not.
