Two unrelated observations were recorded within the same day.

The first concerns **Jake**.

C.O.R.E. has confirmed that Jake possesses direct control over how his manifested body interacts with gravity.

This capability is consistent with his established nature as the **human manifestation of a Blue Dwarf Star**.

Jake does not merely resist gravity.

He can determine how his manifested form responds to it.

The second concerns **Phoenix Raffedella**.

Phoenix fell asleep on her laptop during the middle of the day.

This behavior is consistent with her established combination of:

> **Narcolepsy + insomnia.**

The problem is that her body does not reliably allow her to decide **when** she sleeps.

# 1. JAKE — GRAVITATIONAL INTERACTION

Jake's relationship with gravity has been reassessed following several observations inside the SAFEHOUSE.

C.O.R.E. previously documented that Jake possesses extraordinary stellar properties.

This observation clarifies one specific aspect:

> **Jake can control his gravitational interaction.**

His manifested body does not have to respond to gravity in a conventional manner.

Jake can allow himself to:

- remain normally grounded;
    
- become extremely light;
    
- effectively become weightless;
    
- remain suspended;
    
- alter how strongly gravity affects his movement;
    
- or move through an environment without behaving as though ordinary gravitational forces are acting upon him.
    

This appears to be an instinctive property of his stellar manifestation rather than a learned technique.

# 3. WEIGHT

Jake's apparent physical weight is therefore not necessarily constant.

This does not mean that his body lacks measurable mass.

It means that his interaction with the local gravitational field can be modified.

When Jake wants to be carried:

He can be light.

When Jake wants to sit normally:

When Jake flies:

When he wants to remain completely still in midair:

He can.

C.O.R.E. therefore advises personnel not to use Jake's apparent weight as a reliable indicator of his physical mass.
