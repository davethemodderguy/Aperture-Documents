# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Phoenix Raffedella is missing.

At approximately 06:18, SAFEHOUSE residents discovered that Phoenix was no longer present within the apartment complex.

Her room was empty.

Her medical equipment remained.

Her personal effects remained.

Her emergency supplies remained.

Her phone remained.

Her KCA keycard remained.

There was no evidence of a struggle.

No evidence of forced entry.

No anomalous breach was detected.

No rift was recorded.

No portal activity was recorded.

No security alarm was triggered.

Phoenix simply—

**left.**

The only item discovered was an envelope addressed to:

> **BELLE MERRYWEATHER**  
> **DIRECTOR — KCA**

Inside was a resignation letter.

Nothing else.

Belle was the first person to receive the notification.

She was contacted after SAFEHOUSE residents realized Phoenix had not appeared for her scheduled morning routine.

At first, this was not considered unusual.

Phoenix had recently stopped sleeping.

She frequently remained awake throughout the night.

She occasionally isolated herself.

However, by 07:00, she had not been seen anywhere within the SAFEHOUSE.

A search of the apartment complex began.

# 2. HER ROOM

Phoenix's room was unusually orderly.

Her bed was untouched.

Her emergency medical supplies were arranged exactly as they had been previously.

Her spare clothing remained.

Her personal notes remained.

Her phone was sitting on the desk.

It was powered off.

Her medical assistant identification remained attached to the keycard holder.

This was not consistent with someone preparing to perform medical work elsewhere.

It was consistent with someone who had decided:

> **They were no longer coming back.**

Belle read the letter twice.

# 10. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Phoenix's disappearance appears deliberate.

However, her current location is unknown.

The resignation letter does not contain:

- a destination;
    
- a planned route;
    
- an explanation of where she intends to go;
    
- a timetable for return;
    
- instructions for contacting her;
    
- or any indication that she intends to return.
    

Her decision appears connected to her recent deterioration in sleep and emotional stability.

Of particular concern is the repeated pattern visible in both Phoenix and Jake:

> **Conceal distress.**

> **Avoid asking for help.**

> **Decide that other people would be better off without the inconvenience.**

> **Leave before anyone can object.**

Phoenix spent weeks trying to convince Jake that he was worth bringing home.

She may now have decided that the same reasoning does not apply to herself.
