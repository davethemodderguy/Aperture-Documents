# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **TREEHOUSE / RECOVERED PERSONAL PROPERTY**

# **CORIN'S NOTEBOOK — VISUAL ANOMALY ADDENDUM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Item:** Corin's Personal Notebook  
**Previous Status:** Recovered  
**New Finding:** **REPEATED EYE IMAGERY**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Further examination of Corin's notebook has revealed an extensive quantity of drawings that were not initially documented.

The drawings consist primarily of **eyes**.

Hundreds of them.

Possibly more.

They appear throughout the notebook with little regard for page order or subject matter.

---

# **EYE MOTIF**

The drawings generally fall into two categories.

### **TYPE A — BLACK EYES**

A completely black, irregularly circular shape.

The interior resembles:

> **Thick black gel.**

The edges are rarely perfectly circular.

Some appear smeared.

Some appear to have been repeatedly traced over.

Some are little more than black masses.

There is generally no iris or pupil.

Just black.

---

### **TYPE B — NORMAL EYES**

The second type is considerably more conventional.

A normally drawn eye.

Visible iris.

Pupil.

Eyelid.

Occasionally eyelashes.

The drawing quality varies significantly.

Some are carefully rendered.

Others appear rushed.

---

# **THE DISTURBING PATTERN**

The two forms frequently appear together.

A normal eye.

Beside it:

A black eye.

Sometimes dozens surround a single normal eye.

Sometimes an entire page is filled with them.

Several pages contain nothing except eyes.

---

# **ORIENTATION**

A particularly disturbing characteristic was discovered during examination.

Regardless of how the notebook is held, many of the drawn eyes appear to be oriented toward the reader.

This is likely the result of Corin deliberately drawing them that way.

However, the effect is extremely unsettling.

One researcher described it as:

> **"Feeling like the page is watching you."**

Another refused to continue examining the notebook alone.

---

# **PAGE 87**

Page 87 contains approximately thirty-six eyes.

Most are black.

One normal eye appears in the center.

The black eyes surround it.

The central eye is looking directly outward.

Written underneath:

> **"They're still here."**

No additional context is provided.

---

# **PAGE 103**

A normal eye occupies the upper half of the page.

Beneath it is a black circular eye.

The black shape has been heavily overwritten.

The surrounding paper is damaged from repeated pencil pressure.

The only text reads:

> **"Don't look."**

---

# **PAGE 119**

The entire page is filled with black eyes.

There are no words.

No flowers.

No other drawings.

Just eyes.

Some are tiny.

Some cover nearly half the page.

One occupies almost the entire sheet.

All appear to face the reader.

---

# **PAGE 142**

A single normal eye appears in the center.

Around it are numerous black eyes.

The black eyes are drawn progressively closer to the center.

The final black eye almost touches the normal eye.

Written underneath:

> **"I can feel them looking."**

---

# **POSSIBLE INTERPRETATIONS**

C.O.R.E. psychological analysts have proposed several possibilities.

### **1. Hypervigilance**

The eyes may represent Corin's increasing fear that she was being watched or judged.

### **2. Self-Observation**

The normal eyes may represent Corin herself, while the black eyes represent intrusive thoughts or negative self-perception.

### **3. Fear of Abandonment**

The eyes may symbolically represent Corin constantly checking for someone returning.

### **4. Unknown Anomalous Influence**

This possibility has not been ruled out.

No confirmed anomalous properties have been detected within the drawings themselves.

---

# **IMPORTANT NOTE**

The drawings **do not move.**

No eye has changed position during observation.

No eye has blinked.

No eye has been observed leaving the paper.

The unsettling effect appears to be purely visual.

At present.

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

Orphie was shown several pages.

She immediately recognized Corin's drawing style.

She stared at the black eyes for several seconds.

Then she quietly said:

> **"...She was scared."**

A researcher asked how she knew.

Orphie answered:

> **"She draws things when she can't say them."**

She continued looking through the notebook.

Then she found a page containing a single normal eye.

Beside it was a black one.

Orphie stopped.

---

# **THE LAST PAGE**

The final page containing eye imagery is almost completely blank.

There is one normal eye.

Beside it is one black, irregular circle.

Both stare directly toward the reader.

Beneath them:

> **"Which one am I?"**

No answer follows.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

The eye drawings are now considered a significant component of Corin's psychological record.

They appear to increase substantially during the period of Orphie's absence.

The black-eye motif is particularly concentrated in the later entries.

No definitive meaning has been established.

The drawings are therefore being preserved exactly as found.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The notebook was originally thought to contain Corin's thoughts.

It now appears to contain something else as well.

Her fears.

Her waiting.

Her self-doubt.

And hundreds upon hundreds of eyes.

Normal eyes.

Black eyes.

Eyes drawn over and over until the paper nearly tore.

Every one of them staring outward.

At the person holding the notebook.

At the person reading her thoughts.

At whoever happens to turn the page.

One researcher summarized the experience simply:

> **"I know they're drawings."**

> **"I know they can't see me."**

> **"...I still feel like they are."**

**End of Visual Anomaly Addendum**
