# 1. SURVEY RESULT

The Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise survey has concluded.

The winning category was:

> **SNIPER RIFLE**

Neri acknowledged the result with a single response:

> **“Okay.”**

The following morning, she went to the Armory.

The Armory subsequently became considerably less certain about what was happening.

---

# 2. MATERIAL WITHDRAWAL

Neri accessed the Armory using her authorized credentials and requested a substantial collection of sniper platforms and associated ammunition.

### Platforms removed for research:

- **7× Remington 700 rifles with Cadex chassis**
    
- **2× TAC-50 AMR**
    
- **4× ORSIS T-3000**
    
- **2× SCAR 20 DMR**
    
- **1× M82A1 AMR**
    

Additionally:

> **Multiple quantities of sniper-caliber ammunition**

were withdrawn for controlled research and testing.

No unauthorized personnel received access to the material.

The Armory initially assumed Neri intended to compare the platforms.

They were correct.

They were also incorrect.

She intended to compare them **and then apparently design something else.**

---

# 3. NERI'S OBJECTIVE

When asked what she was doing with fifteen different rifle platforms, Neri responded:

> **“Research.”**

The Armory asked whether this meant she intended to reproduce one of the existing designs.

> **NERI:**  
> “No.”

> **ARMORY:**  
> “Then what are you making?”

Neri paused.

> **NERI:**  
> “A sniper rifle.”

The Armory accepted this answer.

They did not receive any additional information.

---

# 4. DEVELOPMENT PROCESS

Unlike several previous Tachyon Industries projects, Neri has allowed Nora to observe the development process directly.

Nora has therefore been present for portions of the project involving:

### CONCEPTUALIZATION

Neri begins with handwritten sketches.

Pages contain:

- rough silhouettes;
    
- dimensional concepts;
    
- component arrangements;
    
- notes;
    
- crossed-out ideas;
    
- alternative configurations;
    
- arrows connecting different concepts;
    
- and repeated revisions.
    

Nora frequently sits nearby and watches.

Sometimes she asks questions.

Sometimes she simply stares at the page.

Sometimes she says:

> **“That's cool.”**

Neri usually responds:

> **“Mm.”**

---

# 5. DESIGN

Following the initial sketches, Neri develops more detailed designs.

This stage involves repeated revisions.

A design may exist for several hours before being discarded.

One page was reportedly covered entirely in corrections.

Nora asked:

> **“Is that the final one?”**

Neri shook her head.

> **“Why?”**

> **NERI:**  
> “It's wrong.”

Nora looked at the page.

> **NORA:**  
> “How?”

Neri pointed at three separate sections.

> **NERI:**  
> “Those.”

Nora nodded as though this explanation was perfectly adequate.

---

# 6. MOLDING AND FORM DEVELOPMENT

Once Neri is satisfied with a conceptual design, development progresses into physical prototyping.

This includes controlled:

- molding;
    
- forming;
    
- material preparation;
    
- structural experimentation;
    
- and fit evaluation.
    

Neri performs the work herself.

Nora is permitted to watch from the designated safe area.

She has repeatedly asked whether she can help.

Neri's answer remains:

> **“Watch.”**

Nora accepts this.

For approximately five minutes.

Then she asks another question.

---

# 7. FORGING

The project subsequently progressed into controlled fabrication.

Neri's workshop activity has included forging and forming components for the prototype.

The exact manufacturing methods remain restricted to authorized personnel.

C.O.R.E. documentation intentionally does not reproduce procedural fabrication information.

The important observation is that Neri is not simply assembling an existing rifle.

She is developing a new Tachyon Industries platform.

---

# 8. ADJUSTMENT

Prototype components are repeatedly evaluated and adjusted.

Neri's normal workflow has become:

> **DESIGN → PROTOTYPE → TEST → IDENTIFY PROBLEM → ADJUST → TEST AGAIN**

Occasionally:

> **DESIGN → PROTOTYPE → TEST → “NO.” → START OVER**

Nora has witnessed several complete redesigns.

She appears fascinated by the process.

---

# 9. TESTING

Testing is being conducted under controlled Blacksite conditions.

Neri evaluates prototype behavior before deciding whether a design element should remain.

Nora has been permitted to observe selected tests from an appropriate distance.

She has developed the habit of asking:

> **“Did it work?”**

Neri's responses have included:

> **“Yes.”**

> **“No.”**

> **“Almost.”**

> **“Again.”**

The last response is apparently the most common.

---

# 10. NORA'S INVOLVEMENT

Nora is not an engineer on the project.

She is an observer.

However, Neri has deliberately allowed her to see the development process.

This appears to be significant.

Neri has previously kept her work private.

With Nora, she appears comfortable leaving notebooks open and allowing her younger sister to watch the process unfold.

Nora has consequently learned that the creation of Tachyon Industries merchandise is considerably less glamorous than the finished products suggest.

It mostly involves:

> **Paper.**

> **More paper.**

> **A lot of thinking.**

> **Something that doesn't work.**

> **More thinking.**

> **Something that works slightly better.**

> **More testing.**

And eventually:

> **“Okay.”**

---

# 11. THE CAKE PROBLEM

MSAC quickly discovered that Nora had become the project's unofficial information bottleneck.

Nora knows:

- that Neri is working on the sniper rifle;
    
- that several prototypes exist;
    
- that Neri is testing them;
    
- that the design is changing;
    
- that the project is progressing.
    

Nora also knows that MSAC desperately wants updates.

MSAC therefore discovered an extremely simple strategy.

Cake.

---

# 12. FIRST ATTEMPT

An MSAC operator approached Nora.

> **MSAC:**  
> “Hey, Nora.”

> **NORA:**  
> “Hi.”

The operator produced a slice of cake.

Nora stared at it.

> **MSAC:**  
> “Want this?”

Nora took it.

> **NORA:**  
> “Yes.”

> **MSAC:**  
> “So... how's the sniper rifle?”

Nora immediately understood.

She took another bite.

> **NORA:**  
> “Good.”

> **MSAC:**  
> “Anything else?”

Nora shook her head.

The operator sighed.

> **MSAC:**  
> “Worth a try.”

Nora continued eating.

---

# 13. THE SECOND ATTEMPT

Another operator brought better cake.

Nora looked at it.

> **NORA:**  
> “That's good cake.”

> **MSAC:**  
> “It's for you.”

Nora accepted it.

> **MSAC:**  
> “So...”

Nora stared.

> **MSAC:**  
> “How far along is Sis?”

Nora considered the question.

> **NORA:**  
> “She's making the rifle.”

> **MSAC:**  
> “We know that.”

> **NORA:**  
> “Then why ask?”

The operator had no answer.

Nora continued eating.

---

# 14. THE THIRD ATTEMPT

MSAC eventually became more sophisticated.

They provided:

- cake;
    
- additional cake;
    
- and a small plate specifically for Nora.
    

Nora looked at the arrangement.

> **NORA:**  
> “You want something.”

> **MSAC:**  
> “Maybe.”

> **NORA:**  
> “It's about Sis.”

> **MSAC:**  
> “Maybe.”

Nora took the cake.

> **NORA:**  
> “She's testing it.”

Silence.

> **MSAC:**  
> “That's it?”

> **NORA:**  
> “That's what I know.”

The operator nodded.

> **MSAC:**  
> “Fair.”

Nora continued eating.

---

# 15. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri is aware that MSAC personnel have been bribing Nora with cake for project updates.

She has not reprimanded them.

When informed directly, Neri simply said:

> **“If Nora wants to tell them, she can.”**

The officer asked whether Neri was concerned about confidential information.

Neri responded:

> **“She doesn't know everything.”**

Pause.

> **“And if she does, I don't mind.”**

C.O.R.E. considers this an explicit authorization for Nora to discuss non-sensitive project progress.

Technical designs remain restricted.
