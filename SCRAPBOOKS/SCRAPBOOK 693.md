# **OVERVIEW**

Alice has been scheduled for a comprehensive medical examination following the discovery of her unusually poor physical condition.

The initial examination produced a considerably longer list of abnormalities than expected.

Alice's body has adapted to surviving conditions that should have been fatal.

The adaptations themselves are now causing additional complications.

The most significant discoveries are:

> **Severe systemic toxicity.**

and

> **Extreme Ether concentration within the bloodstream.**

Alice remains conscious, mobile, and capable of ordinary activity.

This should **not** be interpreted as evidence that she is healthy.

She is not.

---

# **PRIMARY FINDING — BLOOD TOXICITY**

Alice's blood contains unusually high concentrations of toxic compounds.

The exact composition is difficult to determine due to the number of different substances present.

A significant portion appears to originate from prolonged consumption of wild flora.

Alice spent more than eight months surviving on:

- Grass
    
- Flowers
    
- Wild plants
    
- Snow
    
- Other available vegetation
    

Many of those plants contain naturally occurring compounds that would normally be harmful when consumed repeatedly.

Alice's body accumulated them.

Rather than immediately collapsing, her physiology adapted.

---

# **POISON RESISTANCE**

Paradoxically, Alice demonstrates an extremely high resistance to known poisons.

Testing indicates resistance to essentially every currently catalogued biological and chemical toxin accessible to C.O.R.E. testing protocols.

This includes substances that would normally be lethal at very low concentrations.

The resistance is believed to be an acquired adaptation.

Months of continuous exposure to unknown wild flora appear to have forced Alice's body into an unusual state of chemical tolerance.

However:

> **Resistance does not mean absence of poisoning.**

Alice's bloodstream remains heavily contaminated.

Her body is effectively functioning while carrying a toxic burden that would incapacitate most organisms.

---

# **EXTREME ETHER CONCENTRATION**

An additional abnormality was discovered during blood analysis.

Alice possesses an **extremely high Ether concentration**.

The concentration exceeds ordinary biological tolerance thresholds by a significant margin.

Despite this, Alice shows no immediate signs of Ether rejection.

C.O.R.E. researchers currently suspect that her prolonged exposure to anomalous environmental conditions may have caused her body to adapt alongside the toxins.

The relationship between the Ether concentration and her poison resistance remains under investigation.

---

# **IMPORTANT DISTINCTION**

Alice is **not immune to harm.**

She is resistant to specific forms of harm.

This distinction is critical.

Her body can tolerate toxins that should kill her.

It cannot tolerate:

- Prolonged starvation
    
- Severe dehydration
    
- Extended cold exposure
    
- Major physical trauma
    
- Excessive exertion
    
- Serious infection
    
- Prolonged sleep deprivation
    
- Continued physiological stress
    

Alice's body has survived extraordinary circumstances.

It has not become invincible.

# **WHY ALICE IS STILL ALIVE**

The medical department has no single explanation.

The current hypothesis involves several simultaneous adaptations.

Alice's body appears to have compensated for:

**Malnutrition.**

**Cold exposure.**

**Poison exposure.**

**Ether exposure.**

**Physical weakness.**

**Immune deficiency.**

Rather than one adaptation protecting her, multiple abnormal adaptations appear to have collectively kept her alive.

The result is a body that is extraordinarily resistant in some ways...

and extraordinarily fragile in others.
