# SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL INCIDENT REPORT

**CASE:** NERI DISAPPEARANCE  
**INCIDENT:** RECOVERY OF PERSONAL EFFECT  
**LOCATION:** DISCARDED BACKUP GENERATOR ROOM  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE SEARCH  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — C.O.R.E. / SAFEHOUSE

---

## 1. SUMMARY

A search team conducting a secondary sweep of unused SAFEHOUSE infrastructure discovered **Neri's fish-bone hairclip** inside a discarded backup generator room.

The hairclip was found on the floor approximately [REDACTED] meters from the room's primary entrance.

No blood was found.

No signs of struggle were found.

No damaged equipment was found.

No footprints, drag marks, discarded clothing, or other identifiable trail were found.

No additional personal effects belonging to Neri were recovered.

The discovery therefore provides **confirmation that Neri was physically present in the room at some point**, but provides no reliable indication of where she went afterward.

---

## 2. RECOVERED ITEM

**Item:** Fish-bone hairclip  
**Owner:** Neri  
**Condition:** Intact  
**Damage:** None observed  
**Anomalous activity:** None detected

The hairclip has been positively identified as the same decorative fish-bone hairclip Neri routinely wore alongside her crossed blade hairclips.

Unlike the other hair accessories recovered from Neri's room, this item has no known weapon function.

It was a sentimental possession.

### SIGNIFICANCE

The hairclip was reportedly a gift from Neri's younger sister.

Neri consistently kept it separate from her actual weapons and treated it as a personal keepsake.

It was one of the few objects investigators believed Neri would be unlikely to abandon intentionally.

For this reason, the recovery has been classified as **high significance despite containing no direct evidence of injury or hostile contact.**

---

## 3. LOCATION

The item was discovered in:

> **OLD BACKUP GENERATOR ROOM — SUBLEVEL [REDACTED]**

The room has been out of normal service for an extended period.

The primary generator equipment was removed years ago. Remaining infrastructure consists mostly of disconnected electrical conduits, obsolete maintenance equipment, dust accumulation, and several abandoned storage fixtures.

There is no regular reason for a SAFEHOUSE resident to enter the room.

The search team initially entered after expanding the search perimeter to include locations with:

- minimal foot traffic;
    
- limited surveillance coverage;
    
- low ambient noise;
    
- poor visibility;
    
- and little chance of casual interruption.
    

The room matched several behavioral patterns previously associated with Neri's withdrawal.

---

---

## 5. THE PROBLEM

The discovery initially produced a simple theory:

> **Neri was here.**

The problem is everything after that.

There is no evidence establishing:

- when she entered;
    
- how long she remained;
    
- whether she was alone;
    
- why she entered;
    
- whether she left normally;
    
- whether she used another exit;
    
- whether her Light-derived abilities were involved;
    
- or whether the hairclip was deliberately left behind.
    

The room contains no useful visual record of her presence.

This is particularly problematic given the previously confirmed phenomenon affecting recordings containing Neri's face.

---

## 6. MEMORY CROSS-CHECK

Personnel involved in the recovery were interviewed immediately afterward.

Results were inconsistent.

Several personnel could remember that **someone** had been searching the abandoned generator areas.

They could not identify who.

One worker reportedly stated:

> "I remember seeing a little coat."

When asked whose coat:

> "I don't know."

Another stated:

> "There was a reason we were looking down here."

When asked what the reason was:

> "I don't remember."

The search supervisor then reviewed the written search order.

The document clearly stated:

> **SEARCH SUBJECT: NERI**

The supervisor reportedly stared at the name for several seconds before saying:

> "...Right."

This confirms that the memory suppression phenomenon remains active.

---

## 7. PERSONAL SIGNIFICANCE

The recovered hairclip was transferred to SAFEHOUSE custody.

Upon seeing the item, several residents reacted differently.

### FROST

Frost immediately recognized it.

She reportedly held the item for several seconds before quietly saying:

> "That's hers."

No further statement was made.

### ALICE

Alice initially failed to identify the object.

After being shown a photograph of it, she reportedly said:

> "The fish one."

When asked whose it was:

> "...Neri's."

She did not explain how she knew.

### NEKO

Neko identified the item immediately.

He subsequently checked his personal notes regarding Neri's disappearance.

A handwritten reminder remains attached to the first page:

> **NERI WAS HERE.**  
> **DO NOT LET YOURSELF FORGET.**

Neko added beneath it:

> **She was here.**

---

## 8. BAO

Bao was informed after the recovery.

She did not initially remember seeing the hairclip recently.

She did remember that it belonged to Neri.

After being shown the recovered item, Bao reportedly remained silent for approximately [REDACTED] seconds.

She then said:

> "She wouldn't leave that."

When asked whether she believed Neri was injured, Bao answered:

> "I don't know."

When asked what she believed happened:

> "I don't know."

She subsequently requested that the hairclip not be placed into general evidence storage.

The request was approved.

The item is currently being kept in a secure personal-effects container accessible to Bao and designated C.O.R.E. personnel.

---

## 9. CURRENT SEARCH THEORY

Investigators are currently considering several possibilities.

### POSSIBILITY A — ACCIDENTAL LOSS

Neri may have removed the hairclip temporarily and accidentally dropped it.

**Assessment:** Plausible.

However, the sentimental importance of the item makes prolonged abandonment unusual.

---

### POSSIBILITY B — DELIBERATE ABANDONMENT

Neri may have intentionally left it behind.

**Assessment:** Plausible.

If true, this could indicate that Neri wanted investigators to know she had been in the area.

Alternatively, it could simply indicate that the item had become associated with memories she no longer wished to carry.

There is currently no evidence supporting either interpretation.

---

### POSSIBILITY C — MEMORY/RECORD SUPPRESSION

Neri may have deliberately manipulated the available information surrounding her movement.

Given her demonstrated ability to interfere with visual records and suppress identity-associated memories, this cannot be dismissed.

However:

**No evidence currently demonstrates that she erased physical traces.**

---

### POSSIBILITY D — THIRD-PARTY INVOLVEMENT

Another individual may have encountered Neri and subsequently removed her from the area.

**Assessment:** No supporting evidence.

No signs of struggle or forced movement have been discovered.

---

## 10. IMPORTANT NEGATIVE FINDING

The search team found **nothing else**.

No trail.

No message.

No blood.

No damaged equipment.

No discarded weapon.

No clothing.

No food.

No footprints that can confidently be attributed to her.

Just the hairclip.

This distinction has been emphasized repeatedly during briefing.

> **The hairclip is evidence of presence, not evidence of what happened afterward.**

---

## 11. DIRECTIVE

The generator room is to remain undisturbed pending further investigation.

Personnel are prohibited from:

- altering the room;
    
- removing unidentified objects;
    
- attempting to restore suppressed recordings;
    
- attempting forced memory recovery;
    
- using Neri's known abilities against her;
    
- or publicly speculating about the meaning of the hairclip.
    

Search teams are reminded:

> **DO NOT RELY ON MEMORY.**

All observations are to be written down immediately.

If Neri is located, personnel are instructed to prioritize **communication and safety over apprehension.**

She is a missing resident.

She is not a hostile entity.

She is not an escaped experiment.

She is not an asset.

She is **Neri**.
