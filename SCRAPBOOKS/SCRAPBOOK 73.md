# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **MEDICAL ANOMALY REPORT**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – LEFT OCULAR ORGAN**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **SUMMARY**

During post-operative recovery, medical staff made an unexpected discovery regarding Corin Lockwood's removed left eye.

Contrary to all conventional biological expectations, the eye did not undergo normal tissue degradation.

Instead, residual stabilized Ether continued to preserve its cellular integrity.

The organ was placed in a sealed medical preservation jar and entrusted to Corin's custody at her request.

---

## **MEDICAL OBSERVATIONS**

Repeated examinations over several weeks documented the following:

- No observable tissue decay.
    
- Stable Ether activity within expected limits.
    
- Cellular structures remain intact.
    
- No evidence of infection or uncontrolled anomalous growth.
    

Medical personnel concluded that the eye remains biologically viable due to the same stabilized Ether that sustains Corin's body.

Researchers emphasized that the preserved eye is **not independently alive or conscious**. It exhibits no awareness, perception, or neurological activity on its own.

---

## **REATTACHMENT POTENTIAL**

Because the ocular tissue remains viable, physicians believe surgical reattachment may be possible.

The procedure would require:

- Specialized microsurgery.
    
- Ether synchronization to Corin's existing physiology.
    
- An extended rehabilitation period.
    

Success cannot be guaranteed, and Corin has not yet decided whether she wishes to undergo the operation.

For now, the eye remains securely preserved.

---

## **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

When a researcher asked why she kept the preservation jar instead of requesting an artificial eye, Corin looked at it quietly.

> "...It's still a part of me."

She smiled faintly.

> "...If one day it can go back where it belongs..."

> "...I'd like to try."

She carefully returned the jar to its padded case.

> "...Until then, it stays with me."

---

---

## **STATUS**

Corin's preserved left eye remains stable in secure storage under her care, with periodic examinations by Blacksite medical staff.

At present, no emergency intervention is required.

The decision to pursue surgical reattachment will remain entirely Corin's.

Until that day comes, the small preservation case is kept among her personal belongings—not as a curiosity, but as the hope that something stolen from her may one day be returned.
