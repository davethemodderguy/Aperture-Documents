## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL PROFILE ADDENDUM**

# **COREY — ALTERNATE DESIGNATION "SOLARIA"**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corey

---

# **OVERVIEW**

On multiple occasions, Corey has been addressed by the name:

> **"Solaria."**

The designation originates from **Corey's native universe**.

At present, no verified explanation exists regarding:

- Why the name is used.
    
- Who first gave her the designation.
    
- Whether it is a title, nickname, or legal name within that universe.
    

Despite numerous interviews, the reason remains unknown.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corey has confirmed that she is comfortable responding to either name.

Personnel may address her as:

- Corey
    
- Solaria
    

She responds to both without hesitation.

---

# **INTERVIEW**

During a routine interview, Corey was asked:

> "...Why does your universe call you Solaria?"

She remained quiet for several moments.

Finally, she answered:

> "...I don't know."

The interviewer asked if she had ever tried to find out.

Corey smiled faintly.

> "...A few times."

> "...Nobody could really explain it."

---

# **PERSONNEL OBSERVATIONS**

Staff have noted no behavioral differences when Corey is addressed as "Solaria."

Whether called:

> "Corey?"

or

> "Solaria?"

Her response is identical.

> "...Yeah?"

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

The Marts appear completely unconcerned by the alternate designation.

Several have naturally incorporated both names into their vocabulary.

Examples include:

> "Corey."

> "Solaria."

> "Teacher."

> "Friend."

The Ambassador later summarized the colony's opinion.

> "...Same."

---

# **CORIN'S COMMENT**

Corin was asked whether she preferred one name over the other.

She shrugged.

> "...She's still my sister."

> "...The name doesn't change that."

---

# **NEKO'S COMMENT**

Neko occasionally switches between the two names without explanation.

When asked why, he simply replied:

> "...Both work."

---

# **STAR'S COMMENT**

Star once jokingly greeted Corey by saying:

> "...Good morning, Solaria."

Corey looked up from her paperwork.

> "...Morning."

Star blinked.

> "...You didn't even question it."

Corey smiled.

> "...People call me that sometimes."

---

# **RESEARCH NOTE**

No anomalous activity has ever been associated with the name "Solaria."

It does not trigger any measurable Etheric response, memetic effect, or dimensional anomaly.

At present, it is simply considered an alternate designation whose origin remains unexplained.

Further investigation continues.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

One afternoon, a Mart walked into the Engineering Division looking for Corey.

It paused.

Looked around the room.

Then called out confidently:

> "...Solaria?"

Corey looked up from beneath the oversized sleeve of her lab coat.

> "...I'm over here."

The Mart happily floated over.

Without another word, it climbed onto her shoulder and settled there.

Whether someone called her Corey...

Or Solaria...

The Marts always found the right person.

**End of Personnel Profile Addendum**
