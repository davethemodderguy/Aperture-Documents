# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **ARCHITECT / MEDICAL INCIDENT LOG**

# **THE CURIOSITY OF CURIOUS LIGHT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Architect-Sealed  
**Subjects:** Corin / Curious Light / Guiding Light  
**Location:** Corin's Treehouse  
**Status:** **RESOLVED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Curious Light's curiosity eventually became a problem.

As one of the Architects, Curious Light is naturally fascinated by unusual systems.

Corin is perhaps one of the most unusual systems he has ever encountered.

A deceased biological body.

No normally functioning organs.

No blood circulation.

No conventional metabolism.

A shattered will sustaining itself through Ether.

From a purely scientific perspective, Corin is extraordinary.

To Curious Light, she was an unanswered question.

Unfortunately, he temporarily forgot something important.

**The unanswered question was a person.**

---

# **THE INVESTIGATION**

Curious Light began observing Corin more closely.

He asked questions.

He examined the Ether surrounding her.

He watched how her body responded to changes in her emotional state.

He became particularly interested in the relationship between Corin's consciousness and the Ether maintaining her body.

At first, Corin tolerated it.

She answered what she could.

Then Curious Light began asking questions about her death.

---

# **THE QUESTIONS**

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Do you remember the exact moment your biological functions stopped?"

Corin became quiet.

> **Corin:**  
> "...Yes."

Curious Light continued.

> **"What did you experience immediately afterward?"**

Corin looked away.

> **"...I don't want to talk about it."**

Curious Light nodded.

Then asked another question.

> **"What happened to your consciousness while your body was being disassembled?"**

Corin froze.

The room became silent.

---

# **THE CHANGE**

Curious Light noticed something unusual in the Ether.

It had become unstable.

He leaned closer.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Interesting."

Corin's hands began shaking.

> **Corin:**  
> "...Please stop."

Curious Light did not immediately realize what was happening.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Your Ether appears to react strongly to recollection. If I can understand the mechanism—"

> **Corin:**  
> "Stop."

Her voice cracked.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT INTERVENES**

Guiding Light appeared immediately.

Her expression changed the moment she saw Corin.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Curious."

Curious Light looked over.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "I think I discovered something."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "You did."

A pause.

> **"And you are done."**

Curious Light stopped.

---

# **THE REALIZATION**

Guiding Light moved between them.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "She's not a specimen."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "I know."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Then stop treating her like one."

Curious Light looked toward Corin.

He finally noticed the state she was in.

Her eyes had gone distant.

Her Ether was fluctuating.

Her breathing was unnecessary, yet she was doing it anyway.

Her hand was gripping the edge of her scarf.

She was remembering.

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT'S RESPONSE**

Curious Light immediately stopped the examination.

His expression changed.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "...I didn't mean to."

Guiding Light answered:

> **"I know."**

> **"But intention doesn't make the damage disappear."**

Curious Light looked down.

> **"...You're right."**

For once, he did not defend his research.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin eventually spoke.

> **"You were just curious."**

Curious Light nodded.

> **"Yes."**

> **Corin:**  
> "I know."

She looked at him.

> **"But I don't want to remember all of it just because you're curious."**

Curious Light was silent.

Then:

> **"Understood."**

---

# **NEW RESTRICTION**

Guiding Light established an immediate rule.

### **CORIN IS NOT TO BE EXPERIMENTALLY EXAMINED WITHOUT HER EXPLICIT CONSENT.**

No probing.

No Ether measurements.

No attempts to reproduce her condition.

No memory extraction.

No testing the limits of her body.

No questions regarding her death or experimentation unless **Corin herself chooses to discuss them.**

Curious Light accepted the restriction without argument.

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT'S PRIVATE NOTE**

> **"I wanted to understand how she works."**
> 
> **"I forgot that 'how she works' isn't the same question as 'who she is.'"**
> 
> **"I won't make that mistake again."**

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S OBSERVATION**

Guiding Light remained beside Corin until she had calmed down.

She did not explain what had happened.

She simply stayed.

Later, she told Curious Light:

> **"Your curiosity isn't wrong."**

> **"But there are some things you learn by being trusted, not by taking them apart."**

Curious Light nodded.

> **"...I'll remember that."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Curious Light's curiosity was never malicious.

He genuinely wanted to understand Corin's impossible existence.

But Corin is not an equation.

Her scars are not data.

Her Ether is not merely a phenomenon.

And her death is not an experiment waiting to be reconstructed.

She is a person who remembers.

Curious Light eventually understood that.

And Guiding Light made sure he stopped before curiosity became another source of pain for Corin.

**End of Architect / Medical Incident Log**
