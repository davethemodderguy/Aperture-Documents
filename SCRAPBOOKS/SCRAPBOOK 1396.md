# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

An unexpected social connection has developed between **Solider 11** and **Jane Doe**.

The relationship appears to be based on a particularly specific shared experience:

Both are **Rat Thiren** who have operated as insiders within organizations hostile to Aperture interests.

Jane was previously embedded within **Woodland Coorp** as an Aperture informant.

Solider 11 previously operated within the environment created by **Project: Silverline** before eventually establishing herself as an independent contractor.

Both therefore possess experience with:

- maintaining a cover identity;
    
- observing hostile organizations from within;
    
- withholding information;
    
- recognizing institutional patterns;
    
- surviving while surrounded by people they could not fully trust;
    
- and knowing when to keep their mouths shut.
    

The two have apparently decided that this gives them enough common ground to become friends.

C.O.R.E. has provisionally classified this phenomenon as:

> **RAT SOLIDARITY.**
