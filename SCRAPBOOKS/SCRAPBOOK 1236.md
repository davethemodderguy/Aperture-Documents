# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon was walking through the Blacksite as usual.

Sky was following her.

The two eventually entered the cafeteria.

Several MSAC operators approached Nora.

The interaction initially appeared casual.

It was not.

The operators began pressuring Nora to provide **Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise** without payment or authorization.

Nora refused.

The operators continued bothering her.

Nora became visibly uncomfortable.

Sky was perched nearby.

Then Sky disappeared.

---

# 2. THE WHITE LIGHT

At approximately 14:17, the cafeteria was suddenly engulfed by intense white light.

Personnel reported temporarily losing visual contact with Nora.

The light did not appear to originate from any conventional Blacksite lighting system.

It surrounded the cafeteria.

Then:

**Everything became silent.**

The light condensed.

A figure appeared.

Approximately:

> **185 cm tall.**

It was humanoid.

It was wearing a complete suit of unfamiliar white metallic armor.

No recognizable Aperture, MSAC, KCA, or Tachyon manufacturing characteristics were identified.

The armor contained glowing blue runes.

No exposed skin was visible.

The figure stood directly between Nora and the MSAC operators.

Then it spoke.

---

# 3. FIRST STATEMENT

The voice was described by witnesses as:

> **“A warlock preparing for war.”**

It was deep.

Controlled.

Cold.

Completely serious.

No shouting was necessary.

The figure simply said:

> **“Leave the girl alone.”**

The cafeteria became silent.

One operator stared at the armor.

Another slowly stepped backward.

A third reportedly whispered:

> “What the hell is that?”

The armored figure did not move.

It simply looked at them.

The operators left.

Quickly.

---

# 4. WITHDRAWAL

Once the operators had exited the cafeteria, the armored figure turned toward Nora.

The blue runes dimmed.

White light surrounded the armor again.

The entire figure disappeared.

A moment later:

**Sky was sitting beside Nora.**

The familiar looked completely ordinary.

White feathers.

Blue eyes.

Moon collar.

No armor.

No weapons.

No visible indication that anything unusual had happened.

Nora looked at Sky.

> **Nora:**  
> “Thanks.”

Sky chirped.

Nora continued eating.

---

# 5. IDENTIFICATION

Subsequent investigation established that Sky is not merely an anomalous falcon.

The familiar possesses another identity.

Its name is:

# **INVICTUS AETERNA**

The designation is associated with an ancient guardian entity originating from a historical Latin context.

The entity existed long before its current association with Nora.

Its original purpose was protection.

Its original form was substantially different from Sky's current appearance.

---

# 6. GUIDING LIGHT

Guiding Light confirmed that she encountered Aeterna in a severely degraded state.

The guardian had effectively been:

> **Lost.**

> **Broken.**

> **Abandoned.**

Guiding Light restored and salvated the entity.

The exact process remains outside current C.O.R.E. understanding.

Afterward, Guiding offered Aeterna a new purpose.

Not servitude.

Not ownership.

A promise.

Protect:

> **Nora Tachyon.**

And, by extension:

> **Nora's friends.**

Aeterna accepted.

---

# 7. THE FAMILIAR FORM

Aeterna's normal form is Sky.

A white-feathered falcon with:

- blue eyes;
    
- a moon collar;
    
- blue luminescence;
    
- anomalous flight capability;
    
- variable physical size;
    
- extreme strength;
    
- cognitive connection with Nora;
    
- and the ability to perceive and communicate with her.
    

This form is deliberately non-threatening.

Nora can carry Sky.

Sky can perch on Nora's:

- finger;
    
- shoulder;
    
- head;
    
- beret;
    
- nearby furniture.
    

Sky can also enlarge sufficiently to carry Nora through the air.

This is how Aeterna normally spends its time.

---

**INVICTUS AETERNA**

The transformation from Sky to Aeterna is remarkably simple.

### SKY

**White light**

↓

### INVICTUS AETERNA

### INVICTUS AETERNA

↓

# 10. COMMAND RESPONSE

Nora can intentionally command the transformation.

The command does not require a specific verbal phrase.

A direct intention appears sufficient.

In one observation:

Nora looked at Sky.

> **Nora:**  
> “Aeterna.”

White light appeared.

Aeterna stood where Sky had been.

Nora then pointed toward the cafeteria exit.

> **Nora:**  
> “Come on.”

Aeterna immediately followed.

Nora eventually climbed onto its back.

Aeterna carried her.

---

# 11. AUTOMATIC PROTECTION

Nora does not always need to issue a command.

Aeterna may transform automatically when Nora is threatened.

The cafeteria incident is currently the clearest example.

Nora did not tell Sky to transform.

Sky simply disappeared.

Aeterna appeared.

The transformation appears to have been triggered by the familiar's own assessment of the situation.

This suggests:

> **Sky does not merely obey Nora.**

It actively protects her.

---

### SECONDARY

Nora's friends and close SAFEHOUSE residents.

It has a specific purpose.

Protect Nora.
