# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Aries Akana is no longer coping with Jake's death in a sustainable manner.

Following the fatal attack on his younger brother, Aries has developed a pattern of prolonged nighttime wakefulness accompanied by repeated consumption of Nitro Fuel.

He frequently remains awake late into the night.

He drinks Nitro Fuel while working, reviewing information, sitting alone, or attempting to occupy himself with tasks that do not appear to require his immediate attention.

The behavior has become sufficiently recurrent to concern both the Medical and Resident Welfare Divisions.

Nitro Fuel is not an ordinary energy drink.

Its effects include:

- temporarily increased focus;
    
- gradual depletion of the consumer's stamina;
    
- and light intoxication once the consumer's stamina has been exhausted.
    

This creates a particularly concerning cycle.

Aries drinks Nitro Fuel to remain focused.

The resulting focus allows him to continue whatever activity is keeping him awake.

His stamina gradually declines.

If he continues consuming it after exhaustion, intoxication follows.

His sleep remains disrupted, and his physical condition may deteriorate further.

The cycle then repeats.

C.O.R.E. cannot establish from these observations alone whether Aries meets the clinical criteria for a depressive disorder. However, his persistent grief, disrupted sleep, withdrawal, and repeated use of a stamina-draining substance warrant immediate assessment.

**Nitro Fuel is not the cause of Aries's bereavement. It may, however, be becoming one of the ways he avoids experiencing it.**

# 1. OBSERVED BEHAVIOR

## 1.1 — Nighttime Activity

Aries's sleep schedule has become increasingly irregular.

Personnel have repeatedly observed him awake during hours when the residential sector is otherwise quiet.

He may be found:

- sitting alone in the SAFEHOUSE lounge;
    
- reviewing security reports that have already been reviewed;
    
- reading incident documentation concerning Jake;
    
- staring at his computer without making progress;
    
- walking through the residential corridors;
    
- or sitting at a table with a container of Nitro Fuel.
    

He does not always appear to be working toward a particular objective.

Sometimes he opens a document, reads the same section repeatedly, and closes it without making any changes.

Sometimes he sits in front of an inactive screen.

Sometimes he simply drinks.

When asked why he is still awake, his answers are usually brief.

> **ARIES:** "Not tired."

On another occasion:

> **ARIES:** "Still have things to do."

When asked what those things were, he looked at the documents in front of him.

After a prolonged pause, he answered:

> **ARIES:** "...I don't know."

He then returned to the screen.

No further questions were asked.

## 1.2 — Social Withdrawal

Aries has also become less willing to spend time in shared residential areas.

He still responds when directly addressed, but he rarely initiates conversation.

He may sit with other residents without participating in the conversation.

If someone asks whether he wants company, he often says that it is unnecessary.

If someone offers to accompany him to bed, he may insist that he is not tired.

These statements do not establish that Aries wishes to be alone permanently.

They establish that he is increasingly reluctant to accept assistance.

Personnel should not interpret his withdrawal as rejection or assume that he no longer values the people around him.

His behavior is consistent with someone whose emotional resources are severely depleted.

# 2. NITRO FUEL CONSUMPTION

## 2.1 — Relevant Properties

Nitro Fuel is a stimulant-like anomalous beverage composed of sugar, fermented Banlands Roses, Artiso, and lemon.

Its effects are unusual.

The initial effect improves focus.

However, that focus comes at the expense of stamina.

Continued consumption or continued activity while depleted may leave the consumer exhausted, after which light intoxication can occur.

The beverage therefore does not provide unlimited energy.

It allows a person to remain focused while gradually consuming the physical resources required to continue functioning.

For an individual already experiencing disrupted sleep and significant emotional distress, this is a poor combination.

Aries appears to be using the initial focusing effect to remain awake rather than allowing himself to rest.

The subsequent stamina depletion does not necessarily persuade him to stop.

Instead, he may continue sitting at his desk, drink more, or attempt to finish whatever task he has assigned himself.

Eventually, he becomes visibly exhausted.

## 2.2 — Why This Is Concerning

Aries is not simply staying up late with a drink.

He is repeatedly exposing himself to a substance that can worsen the very physical state he is already neglecting.

Potential concerns include:

- accumulated sleep deprivation;
    
- increasing physical exhaustion;
    
- impaired judgment after stamina depletion;
    
- reduced coordination if intoxication develops;
    
- inadequate food and fluid intake if he remains absorbed in his activities;
    
- and further deterioration in his ability to manage his grief.
    

These are risks requiring assessment, not proof that every listed consequence has already occurred.

The immediate concern is the pattern itself.

Aries appears to be prioritizing continued wakefulness over recovery.

C.O.R.E. has not established that he is deliberately attempting to harm himself through Nitro Fuel consumption.

Personnel must not make that assumption.

However, the absence of confirmed self-harm intent does not make the behavior safe.

# 5. THE GRIEF BENEATH THE BEVERAGE

Aries's behavior cannot be understood exclusively as a substance-use concern.

The Nitro Fuel is the visible behavior.

The bereavement is the context.

Aries traveled a considerable distance to find Jake after their separation.

When he finally found his younger brother, Jake was frightened, vulnerable, and in need of care.

Their relationship was not immediately easy.

Jake had reasons to fear abandonment.

Aries had to learn that caring for Jake involved more than loving him; it required dependable presence, clear communication, and the willingness to arrange help when he could not stay.

Despite the difficulties, they began to establish a relationship that was genuinely theirs.

Jake reached for him.

Jake accepted rides on his back.

Jake fell asleep against him.

Jake told him that he loved him.

Then someone impersonated Aries and attacked Jake.

Jake survived that attack, but his sense of safety was severely damaged.

Later, Jake was left alone again.

This time, the attack was fatal.

Aries is now grieving not only the child he lost, but also the relationship he was still trying to repair.

He cannot make another attempt.

He cannot tell Jake that the person who resembled him was not him.

He cannot explain that he never wanted Jake to be frightened of him.

He cannot promise that he will return.

And he cannot do any of the small things that had begun to make their relationship feel ordinary.

The grief has no immediate task through which it can be resolved.

There is no next visit.

There is no next meal together.

There is no next opportunity to do better.

There is only the absence.

Nitro Fuel may offer Aries a way to occupy his attention without requiring him to confront that absence directly.

But it cannot change it.

# 6. WHY HE KEEPS STAYING AWAKE

C.O.R.E. has identified several plausible explanations for Aries's nighttime behavior.

These remain hypotheses and must not be treated as established psychological findings.

### Hypothesis A — Avoidance of Rumination

Aries may be attempting to prevent intrusive thoughts by maintaining a continuous activity.

### Hypothesis B — Guilt-Driven Overwork

He may believe that remaining productive, reviewing security information, or attempting to identify the attacker is a way to compensate for what happened.

### Hypothesis C — Difficulty Tolerating Rest

When activity stops, Aries may become more aware of Jake's absence.

Sleep may therefore have become associated with an unwanted confrontation with grief rather than relief from exhaustion.

### Hypothesis D — Loss of Routine

Jake's presence may previously have influenced Aries's daily schedule, including when he stopped working, where he spent his time, and when he returned to his room.

With Jake gone, the structure that once organized parts of his day has disappeared.

### Hypothesis E — Combined Factors

More than one explanation may be operating simultaneously.

The most plausible working interpretation is that Aries is using Nitro Fuel to remain occupied during hours when his grief becomes difficult to tolerate.

However, personnel must ask rather than assume.

Aries should be given the opportunity to explain his own experience when he is ready.

# 7. MEDICAL AND WELFARE RESPONSE

C.O.R.E. Medical Division has issued the following recommendations.

## 7.1 — Nitro Fuel Monitoring

Aries's consumption should be documented in a non-punitive manner.

Monitoring should establish:

- approximate frequency and quantity;
    
- time of consumption;
    
- whether he has eaten and hydrated;
    
- whether he is already physically exhausted;
    
- and whether signs of intoxication appear.
    

Personnel must not assume that the beverage is safe simply because it is familiar to SAFEHOUSE residents.

They must also avoid treating every instance of consumption as deliberate self-harm.

If Aries is visibly exhausted or intoxicated, he should not be permitted to continue hazardous work or operate equipment that requires unimpaired coordination.

If severe symptoms occur, Medical Division must assess him.

## 7.2 — Sleep Restoration

Aries should be offered a gradual return to a sustainable sleep schedule.

The objective is not to force him to sleep on command.

Nor is it to leave him awake indefinitely because he refuses assistance.

Personnel should reduce unnecessary nighttime work, help him establish a predictable wind-down routine, and offer quiet company when requested.

Nitro Fuel should not be used as a substitute for rest.

## 7.3 — Nutrition and Hydration

Food and water should be offered regularly.

Aries may be too absorbed in his activities to notice that he has neglected basic needs.

Personnel should avoid making meals into confrontations.

If his intake becomes persistently inadequate, a medical assessment is required.

## 7.4 — Psychological Assessment

A qualified clinician should assess Aries for depressive symptoms, complicated bereavement, sleep disturbance, and possible substance-related harm.

This assessment must be conducted without presuming a diagnosis.

Aries should also be asked directly, in a calm and nonjudgmental manner, whether he has thoughts of harming himself or feels that he cannot continue living.

The question should not be avoided merely because it is uncomfortable.

If he indicates immediate danger, personnel must respond according to the established emergency protocol.

# 8. RESIDENT RESPONSE

Personnel have been instructed not to respond to Aries's behavior with ridicule, anger, or moral judgment.

Do not tell him:

> "You're drinking yourself stupid."

Do not tell him:

> "Jake wouldn't want this."

Do not tell him:

> "You need to move on."

Do not tell him:

> "You're making everyone worry."

These statements are unlikely to help.

They may instead reinforce the impression that his grief has become an inconvenience to other people.

More appropriate responses include:

> "You've been awake a long time. Want me to sit with you?"

> "You don't have to finish that report tonight."

> "I'll get you some water."

> "You don't have to explain anything."

> "We can stay here quietly."

> "You don't have to be productive to deserve company."

Personnel should continue offering support even when Aries declines conversation.

However, they must also respect his reasonable requests for privacy.

**Presence should be offered consistently, not imposed indiscriminately.**

During one late-night observation, Aries was found in the cafeteria.

He had selected a table with two chairs.

The other was empty.

**ARIES:**

**ARIES:**

His hands tightened around the container.

"I'd give anything to complain about that again."

**ARIES:**

The officer remained seated.

For that night, at least, he did not have to occupy every second with something else.

# 10. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Aries is showing a concerning pattern of depressive symptoms and self-neglect following the death of his younger brother.

His recurrent nighttime Nitro Fuel consumption appears to serve a functional purpose: it allows him to remain focused and occupied when rest would leave him alone with his grief.

However, the beverage's stamina-draining effects create a risk of worsening exhaustion and possible intoxication.

The pattern is not sustainable.

C.O.R.E. recommends continued observation, medical assessment, gradual sleep restoration, adequate nutrition and hydration, and accessible psychological support.

No confirmed evidence currently establishes that Aries is using Nitro Fuel with the intention of dying or injuring himself.

That question must nevertheless be assessed directly rather than answered through assumption.

The primary welfare objective is to help Aries regain the ability to rest without requiring him to suppress his grief.

He does not need to stop missing Jake.

He needs to be able to miss Jake without destroying his own health in the process.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Aries has discovered that Nitro Fuel can keep him focused.

For a while, it gives him something to do.

Something to read.

Something to examine.

Something to finish.

As long as he is occupied, he does not have to sit in a quiet room and think about the brother who will never come back.

So he drinks.

He keeps working.

His stamina fades.

He becomes exhausted.

And if he pushes too far, the drink that helped him concentrate can leave him intoxicated when he is already depleted.

It is a cruel arrangement.

He is trying to escape his grief by consuming the very resources his body needs to recover from it.

But the grief is still there when the container is empty.

Jake is still gone when the screen goes dark.

The sun still rises on a SAFEHOUSE in which one small resident will never wake up again.

And Aries must eventually face the fact that staying awake cannot bring his brother back.

C.O.R.E. will not demand that he face that fact alone.

He has already lost Jake.

He should not have to lose his sleep, his health, and his remaining sense of safety simply because he cannot bear the silence.

Tonight, if Aries is found awake with another container of Nitro Fuel, personnel are instructed to approach him gently.

Do not begin with an accusation.

Do not begin by taking the drink away.

Begin by asking whether he wants company.

If he says no, remain available.

If he says yes, sit with him.

If he cannot explain what is wrong, do not make him find the words.

And if he eventually admits that he is staying awake because he does not want to wake up to another day without Jake, do not attempt to correct the feeling.

Acknowledge it.

Stay with him.

Help him get through the night safely.

Because Jake's death has already taken something irreplaceable from Aries.

**His brother should not become the reason Aries stops taking care of himself.**
