# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **CAFETERIA OPERATIONS LOG**

### **EMERGENCY KITCHEN STAFF REASSIGNMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal

---

## **SUMMARY**

Due to a temporary shortage of cafeteria personnel following a non-life-threatening workplace incident involving an Engineering experiment (see Incident Report ENG-███), the Blacksite cafeteria was unable to prepare lunch for the day's personnel.

Until the injured cafeteria staff recovered, volunteers were requested.

Before anyone else could respond...

Belle Merryweather raised her hand.

Norma Lockwood raised hers immediately afterward.

---

## **EMERGENCY HEAD CHEFS**

**Director Belle Merryweather**

**Senior Manager Norma Lockwood**

Temporary Assignment:

**Blacksite Cafeteria**

Duration:

**Until Cafeteria Staff Recovery**

---

## **KITCHEN OPERATIONS**

The two quickly divided responsibilities.

**Belle** handled:

- Ingredient preparation.
    
- Vegetables.
    
- Seasoning.
    
- Desserts.
    
- Taste testing.
    

("Quality assurance," according to Belle.)

---

**Norma** handled:

- Grilling.
    
- Baking.
    
- Timing multiple dishes simultaneously.
    
- Organizing the kitchen.
    
- Coordinating volunteers.
    

Despite neither being employed as cafeteria staff, lunch service began precisely on schedule.

Several kitchen employees later remarked that the operation was run with surprising efficiency.

## **ENGINEERING INCIDENT**

Neko quietly entered the cafeteria.

He looked around.

Then slowly approached Belle and Norma.

> "...About the cafeteria staff..."

Belle smiled.

> "...They're recovering."

Norma folded her arms.

> "...Would you like to explain how a harmless prototype resulted in half the kitchen being treated for minor injuries?"

Neko looked at the ceiling.

> "...In hindsight..."

Miss Neko appeared behind him.

> "...It involved compressed steam."

Norma sighed.

Belle giggled.
