# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake's celestial greatsword had previously demonstrated an unusual secondary function.

Despite appearing to be a conventional sword-shaped anomalous weapon, the object is capable of releasing an immense concentrated celestial discharge.

In practical terms:

> **Jake's sword can function as a handheld howitzer.**

This capability had previously been demonstrated when Jake used the weapon to create an opening through a Woodland Coorp facility during an attempted kidnapping.

That incident established that the weapon was capable of extremely high destructive output.

It did not establish the upper limit.

C.O.R.E. therefore approved a controlled test.

The chosen testing environment was:

> **Z-556 — The Starlands.**

This decision was deliberate.

Testing the weapon inside the Blacksite was rejected.

Testing it on Earth was rejected.

Testing it anywhere remotely close to civilization was rejected.

The Starlands provided a sufficiently isolated environment containing large-scale natural formations capable of absorbing the weapon's output without endangering Earth or the Blacksite.

Jake was informed that the test was for measurement purposes.

He asked:

> **Jake:** “Can I shoot a mountain?”

C.O.R.E. Research:

> “...Yes.”

Jake:

> “Okay.”

---

# 1. TEST ENVIRONMENT

The selected area contained several large mountain formations separated from inhabited Starlands regions.

No residents were present within the designated testing zone.

A safety perimeter was established.

Guiding Light was notified.

Curious Light was notified.

Mischevious Light was notified.

This was considered sufficient authorization for testing within the Starlands.

The three Architects were specifically asked whether the selected region could withstand the experiment.

Guiding Light:

> “Yes.”

Curious Light:

> “Probably.”

Mischevious Light:

> “Do it.”

C.O.R.E. elected not to ask further questions.

---

# 2. SUBJECT ARRIVAL

Jake arrived carrying:

- his teddy bear;
    
- his fractured halo;
    
- his celestial earring;
    
- and the greatsword.
    

He appeared completely calm.

There was no visible apprehension.

No excitement.

No hesitation.

He simply looked at the mountain.

Then at the researchers.

Then back at the mountain.

> **Jake:** “That one?”

Research:

> “Yes.”

Jake nodded.

> **Jake:** “Okay.”

---

# 3. INITIAL OBSERVATION

The weapon was manifested normally.

The blade produced its characteristic blue celestial illumination.

The star-shaped structure incorporated into the hilt began glowing.

Energy accumulated around the weapon.

C.O.R.E. instrumentation immediately began recording values outside the expected range.

One researcher reportedly said:

> “That is considerably more energy than a sword should have.”

Another replied:

> “It stopped being a sword several tests ago.”

Jake continued holding it.

---

# 4. DISCHARGE

Jake raised the sword.

He did not swing it.

He simply aimed it toward the mountain.

There was a brief flash.

Then:

**IMPACT.**

The resulting discharge struck the mountain formation with extraordinary force.

The mountain did not merely fracture.

A substantial portion of the formation was completely annihilated.

Rock, dust, and debris were thrown outward across the test area.

The shockwave propagated through the surrounding terrain.

The Starlands monitoring instruments registered the event immediately.

Jake lowered the sword.

Silence followed.

---

# 5. RESEARCH RESPONSE

One researcher stared at the missing section of mountain.

Another checked the instrumentation.

Then checked it again.

Then checked whether the instruments were malfunctioning.

They were not.

> **Researcher:** “Did he just erase part of a mountain?”

> **C.O.R.E. Technician:** “Yes.”

> **Researcher:** “With a sword?”

> **Technician:** “Apparently.”

Jake looked at the destroyed formation.

> **Jake:** “It worked.”

---

# 6. SECOND TEST

C.O.R.E. initially intended to terminate the test.

Jake, however, pointed toward another mountain.

> **Jake:** “That one?”

The researchers looked at one another.

Guiding Light was watching.

Curious Light was watching.

Mischevious Light was watching.

Aeterna was also present.

Aeterna looked at the mountain.

Then at Jake.

Then at the sword.

> **Aeterna:** “One more.”

C.O.R.E. reluctantly authorized another discharge.

Jake raised the weapon.

The second mountain was struck.

The resulting event produced another enormous celestial discharge.

The formation suffered catastrophic structural destruction.

The upper section was effectively removed.

---

# 7. CAPABILITY CLASSIFICATION

Following the test, C.O.R.E. has formally classified the weapon's secondary function as:

> **CELESTIAL ARTILLERY**

The designation is intentionally descriptive.

The weapon remains a sword.

However, its anomalous properties allow it to produce ranged destructive effects comparable to extremely large-scale artillery.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

No conventional ammunition is involved.

No conventional propulsion system is present.

No conventional explosive mechanism was identified.

The weapon simply:

> **fires.**

---

# 8. MAXIMUM OUTPUT

The Starlands test established that the weapon possesses sufficient output to **annihilate substantial portions of mountain formations.**

C.O.R.E. has therefore placed the following restrictions on future testing:

### PROHIBITED

- testing near Earth;
    
- testing near the Blacksite;
    
- testing near populated areas;
    
- testing without Starlands authorization;
    
- testing without a designated target;
    
- testing merely because Jake is bored;
    
- testing indoors.
    

The final restriction was added after someone asked whether the weapon could be tested in a reinforced chamber.

The answer was:

> **No.**

---

That was enough.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The previous understanding of Jake's sword was incomplete.

It was initially classified as a celestial greatsword.

It was subsequently observed functioning as a form of anomalous artillery.

Following controlled Starlands testing, C.O.R.E. confirms that the weapon possesses enough output to **annihilate mountain-scale geological formations.**

The practical conclusion is therefore straightforward:

> **Do not test Jake's sword on Earth.**

The Starlands exist for exactly this sort of problem.
