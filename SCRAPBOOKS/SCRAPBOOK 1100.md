# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neri organized a mountain climbing contest.

The winner would receive:

> **1 NIGHT STALKER TICKET**

Elsher entered.

At the time of the contest, Elsher possessed:

> **4 / 5 NIGHT STALKER TICKETS**

He had previously won four consecutive competitions:

1. Cooking
    
2. CQB Simulation
    
3. Chess
    
4. Marksmanship
    

He did **not** win the mountain climbing contest.

His streak ended.

However, the reason he lost was not poor performance.

During the climb, another contestant fell from a cliff.

Elsher abandoned his competitive position and immediately intervened.

He successfully prevented his friend from falling to their death.

By doing so, Elsher lost the time advantage required to win the contest.

Neri was extremely pleased.

---

# 2. COMPETITION FORMAT

The contest took place on a controlled mountain course prepared by C.O.R.E. personnel.

The course included:

- steep terrain;
    
- climbing sections;
    
- narrow ridgelines;
    
- marked checkpoints;
    
- controlled hazards;
    
- emergency extraction personnel;
    
- and a final summit objective.
    

The competition was timed.

Fastest successful completion would normally determine the winner.

Neri additionally established one important rule:

> **"Don't let anyone die."**

This was initially considered obvious.

It was not.

---

# 3. ELSHER'S START

Elsher began near the front of the starting group.

Within the first section, he was among the leading contestants.

At the second checkpoint:

> **POSITION: 2ND**

At the third:

> **POSITION: 1ST**

# 4. THE ACCIDENT

Approximately halfway through the course, one of the contestants lost their footing near a cliff section.

The contestant fell beyond the safe path.

They managed to catch the edge briefly.

Their climbing equipment was insufficient to immediately recover their position.

The nearest contestants continued upward.

Elsher was ahead.

He heard the call for help.

He stopped.

---

# 5. THE DECISION

Elsher looked toward the summit.

Then toward the fallen contestant.

The timer continued.

He turned around.

He abandoned his route.

He moved toward his friend.

A nearby contestant shouted:

> **"ELSHER! KEEP GOING!"**

Elsher ignored them.

He reached the cliff section and assisted the fallen contestant back to a safe position.

The entire process cost him a significant amount of time.

His first-place position was gone.

---

"I know."
