# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The identity of **Elsher Newman** as **Elsher Tachyon** is no longer considered a private matter within the Blacksite.

Following confirmation that Elsher is the biological older brother of Neri and Nora Tachyon, information regarding his identity spread rapidly among Blacksite personnel.

The disclosure was not the result of an official announcement.

It simply...

**spread.**

One employee told another.

Another recognized the surname.

Someone mentioned that Elsher already owned multiple pieces of Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise.

Someone else connected the dots.

Within hours, a significant portion of the Blacksite knew:

> **Elsher Newman is a Tachyon.**

Elsher's original reason for maintaining the surname **Newman** was to avoid exactly this situation.

That objective has now failed.

---

# 1. THE DISCOVERY

The initial discovery occurred when Neri reviewed Elsher's personnel file.

She noticed a resemblance.

Not an identical appearance.

Not enough to establish anything independently.

Just enough to make her stop.

Nora subsequently opened her family journal.

One of the entries contained:

> **ELSHER TACHYON**

The personnel file contained:

> **ELSHER NEWMAN**

Further investigation confirmed the identities were the same individual.

The family order was established as:

### 1. ELSHER TACHYON

**Oldest**

### 2. NERI TACHYON

**Middle**

### 3. NORA TACHYON

**Youngest**

For the first time since the Tachyon family's history was reconstructed, the complete surviving sibling structure was known.

---

---

# 3. BLACKSITE REACTION

The general reaction was surprisingly consistent.

There was an initial period of:

> **"...Wait."**

followed by:

> **"ELSher is a TACHYON?"**

followed by various attempts to reconcile the information with existing observations.

Particularly:

- Elsher already owns **NS-006**;
    
- Elsher already owns **SOH-002**;
    
- Elsher recently received a **Spearhead**;
    
- Elsher has interacted with Neri repeatedly;
    
- Elsher was present during discussions concerning Tachyon Industries;
    
- Elsher had never once mentioned being related to Neri;
    
- Elsher had never once mentioned being related to Nora.
    

Several employees reportedly spent several minutes staring at Elsher after learning the information.

Elsher's response was consistent:

> **"What?"**

---

# 4. ELSHER'S RESPONSE

Elsher was eventually asked why he had never disclosed his surname.

His answer was simple.

> **Elsher:**  
> "Because I didn't want attention."

Pause.

> **C.O.R.E.:**  
> "Attention regarding what?"

Elsher looked toward the personnel conducting the interview.

> **Elsher:**  
> "The Tachyon name."

He explained that he had no interest in becoming:

- a family celebrity;
    
- a historical curiosity;
    
- a corporate heir;
    
- a symbol;
    
- or "the other Tachyon."
    

He simply wanted to remain:

> **Elsher.**

His decision to use **Newman** had therefore been deliberate.

Not an attempt to erase his family.

An attempt to live normally.

---

# 5. NERI'S REACTION

Neri's reaction to the disclosure was considerably quieter.

She was reportedly sitting at the Merch Store when the information began circulating.

Someone asked:

> "So Elsher is your brother?"

Neri looked up.

Paused.

Then nodded.

> **Neri:**  
> "...Yes."

She returned to what she was doing.

No further comment was provided.

Later, when asked whether she was happy to have found another surviving family member, Neri simply answered:

> **"He's my brother."**

No elaboration followed.

---

# 6. NORA'S REACTION

Nora reacted considerably differently.

Upon confirming the relationship:

> **Nora:**  
> "I HAVE A BIG BROTHER."

Pause.

> **Nora:**  
> "AND HE'S ELSHER."

Pause.

> **Nora:**  
> "AND HE HAS TWO OF SIS'S GUNS."

Neri immediately looked at her.

> **Neri:**  
> "Nora."

> **Nora:**  
> "What?"

> **Neri:**  
> "Don't."

> **Nora:**  
> "...Okay."

She waited approximately five seconds.

> **Nora:**  
> "He's still really cool."

Neri said nothing.

---

# 7. THE WATCH

Prior to the confirmation of Elsher's identity, Neri had been entrusted with a particular family artifact.

A watch.

The watch originally belonged to:

> **Lewis Tachyon.**

Lewis Tachyon had previously given the watch to Director Lucious.

Lucious retained it for years.

When Neri's identity as a Tachyon was established, Lucious returned the watch to her.

Neri recognized it immediately.

She knew how Lewis had worn it.

Face inward.

She subsequently wore it the same way.

For Neri, the watch represented more than an heirloom.

It represented proof that someone from the Tachyon family had remembered her.

---

# 8. THE RETURN

Following the confirmation that Elsher was the oldest surviving Tachyon sibling, Neri made an unexpected decision.

She removed the watch from her wrist.

She found Elsher.

She held it out.

Elsher initially did not understand.

> **Elsher:**  
> "What's this?"

Neri answered quietly:

> **Neri:**  
> "Dad's."

Elsher stopped.

He recognized the watch.

Not because he had seen it recently.

Because he remembered it.

> **Elsher:**  
> "...Lewis's?"

Neri nodded.

Elsher stared at it for several seconds.

> **Elsher:**  
> "Where did you get this?"

> **Neri:**  
> "Lucious gave it to me."

Pause.

> **Neri:**  
> "It was his."

Elsher looked at Neri.

> **Elsher:**  
> "You kept it?"

Neri nodded.

Then she placed the watch into his hand.

> **Neri:**  
> "You're older."

Elsher looked down at the watch.

> **Neri:**  
> "It should be yours."

---

# 9. ELSHER'S RESPONSE

Elsher did not immediately accept it.

> **Elsher:**  
> "Neri..."

Neri remained silent.

> **Elsher:**  
> "You don't have to give me this."

Neri looked at him.

> **Neri:**  
> "I know."

Pause.

> **Neri:**  
> "I want to."

Elsher closed his hand around the watch.

He did not say anything for several seconds.

Eventually:

> **Elsher:**  
> "...Thank you."

Neri nodded.

Then she walked away.

---

# 10. THE FAMILY JOURNAL

Nora later opened her family journal.

She added a new entry.

Under:

> **ELSHER TACHYON**

she drew a small picture of the watch.

Beside it, she wrote:

> **"Dad's watch."**

Below that:

> **"Big brother has it now."**

Nora showed the page to Neri.

Neri looked at it.

She did not correct anything.

---

# 11. LUCIOUS

Director Lucious was informed of the transfer.

He was silent for several seconds.

> **Lucious:**  
> "So it went back to the family."

C.O.R.E. confirmed.

Lucious nodded.

> **Lucious:**  
> "Good."

He later encountered Elsher wearing the watch.

Lucious immediately recognized it.

Neither spoke about it.

Lucious simply looked at the watch.

Then at Elsher.

Then nodded.

Elsher returned the gesture.

No further conversation occurred.

---

# 12. CURRENT STATUS

The watch is now registered as:

**TACHYON FAMILY ARTIFACT — FAMILY CUSTODIAN: ELSHER TACHYON**

The artifact is not considered Aperture property.

No containment restrictions apply.

No security lock has been placed upon it.

Its value is primarily historical and familial.

Neri no longer possesses the watch.

She has retained photographs and archival records documenting its previous custody.

Following the confirmation of Elsher's identity:

|**2**|**Neri Tachyon**|Middle sibling|

---

# 14. ARCHIVIST OBSERVATION

The irony has not been lost on Blacksite personnel.

Elsher spent years hiding the Tachyon surname because he did not want attention.

Then he discovered that:

- his younger sister is a brilliant chemist;
    
- his youngest sister is a six-year-old shark who apparently has no concept of indoor volume control;
    
- his younger sister created Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise;
    
- he already owned two of those products;
    
- his youngest sister apparently thinks he is extremely cool;
    
- and a substantial portion of the Blacksite now knows his family history.
    

Elsher's original plan was:

> **Remain Elsher Newman.**

Current situation:

> **Elsher Tachyon.**

The distinction is now mostly academic.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Neri once believed she was the last surviving Tachyon.

Then Nora returned.

Now Elsher has been identified.

The family that Neri believed had disappeared has become something considerably less abstract.

Three siblings.

One oldest.

One middle.

One youngest.

And an old watch that has finally returned to the oldest surviving member of the family.

Neri gave it away willingly.

Not because it mattered less to her.

Because it mattered enough to return it to someone who remembered Lewis too.

For years, the watch had been carried forward.

From Lewis.

To Lucious.

To Neri.

And now:

> **Elsher Tachyon.**

For the first time since the Tachyon family was believed lost, the watch is not being kept for someone who might still exist.

It is being worn by someone who does.
