# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon and SAFEHOUSE resident Alice have rapidly developed a close friendship.

The primary contributing factor appears to be extremely straightforward:

> **They are both six years old.**

Unlike most SAFEHOUSE residents, who are significantly older than either child, Alice and Nora operate on approximately the same social and developmental wavelength.

They share:

- similar energy levels;
    
- similar curiosity;
    
- similar attention spans;
    
- similar approaches to play;
    
- similar willingness to ask questions;
    
- similar enthusiasm for things they find interesting;
    
- and a mutual inability to remain completely serious for extended periods.
    

The result has been an unusually uncomplicated friendship.

When Neri is busy with chemistry, research, medical appointments, reading, or other activities, Nora frequently spends time with Alice.

Neri does not appear concerned.

If anything, she appears relieved.

# 14. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Nora and Alice's friendship appears healthy and mutually enjoyable.

There is no indication that either child views the other primarily through family history, medical status, or organizational importance.

They are simply peers.

Both are six.

Both have too much energy.

Both ask too many questions.

Both become distracted easily.

Both can spend an unreasonable amount of time investigating something completely unimportant.

And both appear perfectly comfortable with this arrangement.

For Neri, this has created an additional benefit.

She does not have to keep Nora entertained.

She does not have to monitor Nora continuously.

She does not have to answer every question.

She can work.

Nora can play.

And Alice has someone her own age to play with.
