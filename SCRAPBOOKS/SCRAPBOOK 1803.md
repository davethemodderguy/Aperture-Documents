# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately 08:40, Jake approached his older brother, Aries Akana, carrying a folded piece of paper in his mouth.

Jake was in feline form.

His teddy bear was not in his mouth. It was secured against his body.

The paper was.

Aries initially assumed that Jake had picked up a scrap of paper from the floor.

Upon retrieving it, he discovered that the paper contained a handwritten message.

The handwriting was consistent with Jake's previous written communications.

The message read:

> **"Too much false promises, stop"**

No further explanation was provided.

Jake did not speak.

He did not gesture toward Aries.

He did not remain to observe Aries's reaction.

After releasing the note, Jake turned around and returned to his usual resting place.

Aries remained standing in the middle of the room, holding the paper.

The incident lasted less than a minute.

Its significance is considerably greater.

Jake had apparently found a way to communicate an objection that he had not expressed verbally.

He did not ask Aries to try harder.

He did not request another apology.

He did not ask for reassurance.

He issued a direct instruction.

**Stop making promises you do not keep.**

# 1. INCIDENT RECONSTRUCTION

## 08:37 — The Note

Aries was preparing for work.

His equipment was arranged beside the door.

Jake was resting near the bed, his teddy bear beside him.

Aries checked his schedule.

He had intended to spend the morning with Jake, but an unexpected assignment had altered his plans.

He had not yet explained the change.

He was considering how to phrase it when he noticed Jake approaching.

The little cat walked slowly across the room.

A folded piece of paper was held between his teeth.

Aries stared.

**ARIES:**  
"...Jake?"

Jake stopped.

**ARIES:**  
"What have you got there?"

Jake continued looking at him.

Aries crouched.

**ARIES:**  
"Can I see?"

Jake released the paper.

It landed on the floor between them.

Aries picked it up.

He unfolded it.

Then he read it.

Once.

Twice.

His expression changed.

---

## 08:38 — The Message

The paper contained four words of criticism and one instruction.

> **"Too much false promises, stop"**

The sentence was grammatically imperfect.

Its meaning was not.

Aries read it again.

Jake stood in front of him.

His ears were upright.

His tail remained still.

His blue eyes watched Aries for several seconds.

**ARIES:**  
"Jake..."

Jake turned away.

Aries stopped speaking.

He looked down at the note.

Then at his brother.

**ARIES:**  
"Is this about the things I promised?"

Jake did not turn around.

Aries swallowed.

**ARIES:**  
"The briefings. The reading. Yesterday."

Jake continued walking.

**ARIES:**  
"Little star, I'm—"

Jake stopped.

He looked back at Aries.

Aries fell silent.

The note remained in his hand.

For the first time, he understood that beginning another apology might be precisely the wrong response.

Jake had not written _I'm upset._

He had not written _You hurt me._

He had not written _Please stay._

He had written:

**Stop.**

Aries lowered the paper.

**ARIES:**  
"...Okay."

Jake returned to his resting place.

The interaction was over.

# 2. FORENSIC EXAMINATION OF THE NOTE

C.O.R.E. examined the paper after the incident.

The note was written in Jake's established handwriting.

The lettering was small.

The pressure varied slightly between words.

No evidence indicated that another resident had written the message on Jake's behalf.

The paper had been folded twice, apparently to make it easier to carry.

The method of transport was considerably less conventional.

### Question: How did Jake write the note in feline form?

**Answer: Unknown.**

Jake has demonstrated an anomalous capacity for materialization and physical transformation.

However, no confirmed mechanism currently explains how he produced the note while displaying almost no spontaneous voluntary activity.

Possible explanations include:

- a brief, highly selective return of voluntary motor function;
    
- an anomalous interaction between his human and feline manifestations;
    
- an unobserved period of activity;
    
- or another mechanism not yet identified.
    

C.O.R.E. will not assume that the note was written through supernatural means merely because its production is difficult to explain.

Nor will the note be dismissed as impossible because Jake's current behavior is inconsistent with ordinary writing.

The evidence establishes one thing clearly:

**Jake produced a message and deliberately delivered it to Aries.**

The exact process remains unresolved.

---

# 3. THE GRAMMAR IS NOT THE ISSUE

One staff member initially described the message as "an incomplete sentence."

This observation was technically accurate and operationally irrelevant.

The intended meaning was readily understandable.

Jake did not need to produce a formal letter.

He did not need to explain every missed commitment.

He did not need to provide a chronological record of his disappointment.

He did not need to soften the criticism.

The note was not a writing exercise.

It was communication.

The message was sufficiently clear to require a response.

C.O.R.E. therefore rejects any attempt to minimize its significance because of its brevity, grammar, or unusual delivery method.

---

# 4. ARIES'S RESPONSE

Aries was interviewed approximately twenty minutes after the incident.

He had kept the original note.

He had not folded it away.

He held it between both hands throughout the interview.

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"What did you understand from the message?"

**ARIES:**  
"That he doesn't want me making promises anymore."

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"More specifically?"

Aries looked down.

**ARIES:**  
"He doesn't want me telling him things are going to happen when I don't know if they will."

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"Do you disagree?"

**ARIES:**  
"No."

A pause.

**ARIES:**  
"I keep telling him I'll come back soon. Then I get delayed. I tell him we'll do something tomorrow, then I get another assignment."

He rubbed his eyes.

**ARIES:**  
"I keep thinking that if I explain afterward, it'll be okay."

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"Do you still think that?"

Aries shook his head.

**ARIES:**  
"No."

He looked at the note again.

**ARIES:**  
"He had to write it down."

A long silence followed.

**ARIES:**  
"He had to find a way to tell me to stop."

The welfare officer did not interrupt.

**ARIES:**  
"And I was still going to tell him I'd be back later."

He folded the paper carefully.

Then unfolded it again.

**ARIES:**  
"I was going to do it again."

---

# 5. WHY THE NOTE MATTERS

Jake's current withdrawal has made his emotional state difficult to assess.

He rarely initiates communication.

He does not reliably display frustration in conventional ways.

He seldom demands attention.

His silence has sometimes allowed those around him to mistake the absence of visible protest for acceptance.

This incident challenges that assumption.

Jake may be largely inactive.

He may not respond to ordinary conversation.

He may not seek company.

But he was able to identify a repeated problem and communicate a boundary concerning it.

The message should not be interpreted as proof that Jake has fully recovered his voluntary abilities.

It should not be interpreted as proof that he is otherwise medically well.

It should not be interpreted as a complete account of his emotional state.

It does, however, establish that his lack of visible protest cannot be taken to mean that he has no objection.

He objected.

He made the objection explicit.

And he directed it toward the person whose behavior concerned him.

**Jake has not stopped communicating. Personnel have sometimes failed to recognize when he is communicating.**

---

# 6. THE WORD "FALSE"

The most important word in the note may be _false_.

Aries did not necessarily intend to deceive Jake.

He believed his promises when he made them.

He expected to return.

He expected to finish his work.

He expected that tomorrow would be different.

But Jake did not write:

> "Too many broken promises."

He wrote:

> **"Too much false promises, stop"**

The distinction matters because Jake appears to be responding not only to the missed activities, but also to the gap between what Aries says and what happens.

From Jake's perspective, a promise that repeatedly fails to describe reality may no longer be meaningfully different from a false statement.

C.O.R.E. cannot determine whether Jake consciously intended that distinction.

The wording nevertheless reflects the central problem.

Aries's intentions may be sincere.

His predictions have not been reliable.

To repair the relationship, Aries must address the discrepancy rather than defend the intention.

---

# 7. A CHANGE IN COMMUNICATION

Following the interview, Aries requested permission to speak with Jake privately.

The request was approved.

Aries entered the room without his tablet or work equipment.

Jake was resting beside his teddy bear.

Aries sat on the floor at a respectful distance.

He placed the note on the floor between them.

**ARIES:**  
"I read it."

Jake blinked.

**ARIES:**  
"I understand what you meant."

He paused.

**ARIES:**  
"I've been making promises I couldn't guarantee."

Jake remained still.

**ARIES:**  
"I kept telling you I'd be back soon because I wanted you to know I wasn't leaving you."

His voice became quieter.

**ARIES:**  
"But then I didn't come back when I said I would."

He looked at the paper.

**ARIES:**  
"And I kept doing it."

Jake watched him.

**ARIES:**  
"I'm sorry."

Aries stopped.

He did not add that he would never do it again.

He did not promise to spend the next day with Jake.

He did not promise to return early from work.

He did not ask Jake to forgive him.

After a few seconds, Aries continued.

**ARIES:**  
"I'll stop making promises about things I can't control."

Jake blinked.

**ARIES:**  
"If I don't know when I'll be back, I'll tell you I don't know."

A pause.

**ARIES:**  
"And if I can't stay with you, I'll make sure someone is actually here to look after you."

Jake's ears moved slightly.

Aries did not interpret the movement as agreement.

**ARIES:**  
"You don't have to believe me right now."

He looked directly at his little brother.

**ARIES:**  
"I'll just have to show you."

Jake turned his head toward the teddy bear.

The conversation ended there.

Aries remained seated for another minute before getting up.

He left the note where Jake could reach it.

He did not take it away as a keepsake.

It belonged to Jake.

# 8. WELFARE DIRECTIVE — PROMISES AND CAREGIVING

Effective immediately, Aries is instructed to observe the following requirements.

### 8.1 No Unsupported Time Guarantees

Aries must not give Jake a specific return time unless the schedule supports that commitment.

### 8.2 No Automatic Reassurance

Phrases such as _"soon," "just a minute,"_ and _"tomorrow, definitely"_ must not be used merely to prevent Jake from appearing disappointed.

If the timing is uncertain, Aries must communicate that uncertainty honestly.

### 8.3 Supervision Is Not Optional

The note does not authorize Aries to stop making caregiving arrangements.

Jake's request concerns false promises, not the withdrawal of necessary care.

If Aries cannot remain with Jake, an authorized caregiver must explicitly accept responsibility before Aries leaves.

### 8.4 No Demand for Forgiveness

Aries must not ask Jake to forgive him, reassure him, or confirm that their relationship remains intact.

Jake is not responsible for relieving Aries's guilt.

### 8.5 Respect the Boundary

Personnel must not turn the note into a group discussion, joke about its delivery method, or repeatedly ask Jake to explain it.

He has already communicated the essential point.

Further clarification may be invited if Jake chooses to provide it.

It must not be demanded.

# C.O.R.E. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Jake has established a clear boundary.

Aries must stop making promises that he cannot reliably fulfill.

The appropriate response is not another promise.

It is a change in behavior.

Aries may continue to love Jake.

He may continue to want to spend time with him.

He may continue to tell Jake when he hopes to return.

But hope must not be presented as certainty.

C.O.R.E. will monitor whether Aries's communication becomes more accurate and whether his caregiving arrangements remain dependable.

No immediate restoration of trust should be assumed.

Jake's note represents an act of communication, not a declaration that the problem has been resolved.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

That evening, Aries found Jake resting on the bed.

The note was beside him.

Aries stopped at the doorway.

For a moment, he considered saying that he would be home early tomorrow.

He even had a reason to believe it.

His schedule was relatively light.

Then he remembered that his schedule had been relatively light before.

He had said _tomorrow_ before.

He had said _soon_ before.

He had said _five minutes_ before.

He did not know what might change.

So he walked over and sat beside Jake.

He did not promise anything.

He simply stayed.

When he eventually needed to leave, he arranged a proper handover to Bao.

He told Jake he was leaving.

He told him who would be staying with him.

He did not give him a time he could not guarantee.

Jake remained quiet.

Aries stood at the door for a moment.

**ARIES:**  
"Goodnight, little star."

Jake blinked.

Aries left.

The door closed.

There was no promise to return in five minutes.

No promise to make tomorrow better.

No promise that nothing would go wrong.

Only an honest goodbye, followed by a caregiver who had actually taken responsibility for Jake's safety before leaving.

It was a small change.

Perhaps too small to matter immediately.

But for a child who has heard too many assurances become nothing, small changes may be the only ones worth believing.

On the bedside table, the note remained folded.

Four words and an instruction.

> **"Too much false promises, stop"**

Jake had not asked Aries to become perfect.

He had not asked him to abandon his work.

He had not asked him to prove his love with another grand declaration.

He had asked him to stop saying things that did not come true.

For once, the next step belonged entirely to Aries.

**He did not need to promise Jake that he would change.**

**He needed to change.**
