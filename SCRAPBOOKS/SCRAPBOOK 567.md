## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL / MEDICAL OBSERVATION REPORT**

# **CORIN — SLEEPLESSNESS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** Wings Healed / Severe Sleep Disturbance

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's injured wings have completely healed.

There is no remaining physical impairment.

She can fly again.

However, another problem has become apparent.

**Corin can no longer sleep.**

She appears to sleep normally when observed by others.

She closes her eyes.

She remains still.

Her breathing becomes slow and controlled.

Her wings fold around her.

But she is not actually asleep.

She is pretending.

---

# **THE NIGHT**

Corin has developed a routine.

When Orphie goes to sleep, Corin remains beside her.

She waits.

She keeps her eyes closed.

She maintains the appearance of sleep until she is certain Orphie is no longer awake.

Then she quietly gets up.

She moves to a corner of the room.

Her wings fold around her entire body.

And she sits there.

Awake.

Alone.

---

# **THE CORNER**

The corner has become Corin's preferred hiding place again.

She curls herself tightly against the wall.

Her knees are drawn close.

Her two tails remain tucked beneath her wings.

Her face disappears behind her feathers.

From outside, she appears to be sleeping.

She is not.

She simply does not want to be seen awake.

---

# **WHY?**

C.O.R.E. has not determined the precise reason for Corin's inability to sleep.

She has not reported physical pain.

Her healed wings function normally.

Her body is capable of entering a resting state.

But her mind refuses to follow.

She remains awake for hours.

Sometimes the entire night.

---

# **ORPHIE'S AWARENESS**

Orphie does not know.

When Orphie wakes, Corin is usually already back beside her.

Her eyes are closed.

Her breathing is steady.

Her wings are arranged as though she never moved.

To Orphie, Corin appears to have slept normally.

Corin has deliberately maintained that illusion.

---

# **WHY CORIN DOESN'T TELL HER**

When asked indirectly about her sleep, Corin always gives the same answer:

> **"I'm fine."**

She does not want Orphie to worry.

She does not want to become another problem for someone else.

Most importantly, she does not want Orphie to wake up and see her sitting alone in the corner.

So she pretends.

---

# **THE WINGS**

Corin's wings have returned to their normal condition.

She can fly.

She can launch feathers.

She can fold them normally.

And at night, she uses them for something entirely different.

She hides inside them.

The wings form a complete enclosure around her body.

It is almost impossible to see her face.

The behavior appears to provide a sense of privacy.

---

# **A TYPICAL NIGHT**

**23:14**

Orphie goes to sleep.

**23:27**

Corin closes her eyes.

**00:03**

Orphie is fully asleep.

**00:04**

Corin opens her eyes.

**00:05**

Corin leaves the bed.

**00:07**

Corin reaches the corner.

**00:08**

Her wings close around her.

**00:09–05:30**

Corin remains awake.

**05:31**

Corin quietly returns to bed.

**05:32**

Eyes closed.

Wings arranged naturally.

**05:33**

Orphie wakes later believing Corin slept beside her all night.

---

# **THE CONTRADICTION**

Corin hates being alone.

Yet she repeatedly chooses to spend the night alone.

She wants someone nearby.

But she does not want anyone to know how badly she is struggling.

So she stays close to Orphie while simultaneously hiding herself from her.

The result is a strange compromise:

> **She is not alone.**
> 
> **But she is still alone.**

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

The physical healing of Corin's wings has been successful.

Her psychological condition has not improved at the same rate.

Her inability to sleep is considered a serious concern.

C.O.R.E. is particularly concerned that Corin's apparent rest may cause others to underestimate the severity of her condition.

She is not recovering at night.

She is enduring it.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin can fly again.

Her wings are completely healed.

But when the lights go out, she does not sleep.

She waits until Orphie closes her eyes.

Then she quietly leaves the bed.

She curls into the corner.

Her wings close around her.

And she stays there until morning.

Orphie never knows.

Not because Corin doesn't want her there.

But because Corin has become very good at hiding when she needs someone most.

**End of Psychological / Medical Observation Report**
