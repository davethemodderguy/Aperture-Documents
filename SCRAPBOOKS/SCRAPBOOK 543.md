### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / ADAPTIVE TRAIT REPORT**

# **CORIN — POST-MORTEM ADAPTATIONS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **Living Anomalous Human**  
**Phenomenon:** **Persistent Post-Mortem Adaptations**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although Corin has now returned to genuine biological life, the years she spent in an anomalous dead state permanently altered her physiology.

Her body did not simply return to its original condition.

It retained several capabilities that developed while she was sustained primarily by Ether.

These adaptations remain fully functional despite her restored biological systems.

Confirmed abilities currently include:

- **Cold Resistance**
    
- **Heat Resistance**
    
- **Regeneration**
    
- **Water Breathing**
    

These traits are now considered permanent components of Corin's physiology.

---

# **COLD RESISTANCE**

Corin possesses exceptionally high resistance to low temperatures.

Extreme cold produces significantly less physiological stress than it would in an ordinary human.

This adaptation is particularly notable given her frequent exposure to snowy environments.

Corin can remain outdoors in conditions that would normally require substantial protective clothing.

She is still capable of becoming cold.

However, the physiological consequences are dramatically reduced.

Her wings also remain functional in temperatures that would normally interfere with biological tissue.

Corin's personal assessment:

> **"Snow is nice."**

---

# **HEAT RESISTANCE**

Corin possesses a corresponding resistance to extreme heat.

High environmental temperatures cause substantially less discomfort and physiological stress.

Her body can maintain stable function under conditions that would normally be dangerous to an ordinary human.

This does not make Corin completely immune to heat.

However, her tolerance is significantly beyond normal human limits.

C.O.R.E. believes this adaptation developed because her Ether-based physiology previously had little dependence on conventional temperature regulation.

---

# **REGENERATION**

Regeneration is one of Corin's most prominent retained abilities.

Her body can rapidly repair physical damage.

Unlike her previous state, however, regeneration now operates alongside genuine biological healing.

This means Corin's body can:

- Close wounds.
    
- Repair damaged tissue.
    
- Reconnect biological structures.
    
- Restore damaged organs.
    
- Regrow lost tissue.
    
- Maintain structural integrity without external stitching.
    

Her regeneration remains enhanced by Ether.

The difference is that her biological systems now participate in the process rather than being completely replaced by Ether.

---

# **WATER BREATHING**

Corin has retained another particularly unusual adaptation:

**She can breathe underwater.**

Her respiratory system is capable of extracting usable oxygen from water.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

The ability appears to have developed during her years of anomalous existence and persisted after her return to biological life.

Corin can therefore remain submerged for extended periods without conventional breathing equipment.

---

# **ORIGIN**

C.O.R.E. believes these abilities are the result of long-term exposure to Corin's unusually concentrated Ether.

Her body spent years operating under conditions where conventional human physiology was insufficient.

Rather than simply replacing biological functions, the Ether appears to have forced Corin's body to **adapt around its new existence.**

Those adaptations remained after biological life was restored.

---

# **SIGNIFICANCE OF THE ADAPTATIONS**

Corin's return to life did not reset her physiology.

Instead, her body appears to have incorporated the changes accumulated during her anomalous existence.

This produces a unique combination:

> **Human biology + Ether-based regeneration + Post-mortem adaptations.**

Corin is therefore not a restored copy of the person she once was.

She is a living organism that has been permanently changed by everything she experienced while dead.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

When informed that her abilities remained after her revival, Corin was surprisingly casual.

> **"So I'm alive now, but I kept the weird stuff?"**

Guiding Light nodded.

> **"Apparently."**

Corin considered this.

> **"...Good."**

She then added:

> **"I like the cold resistance."**

Nobody was surprised.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin spent years being dead.

Her body adapted to it.

Now that she is alive again, those adaptations have not disappeared.

She can survive the cold.

She can endure the heat.

She can regenerate.

She can breathe underwater.

The irony is not lost on C.O.R.E.

Corin's years as something that was **not quite alive** permanently changed the way her body functions as something that **is alive.**

She came back to life.

But she did not come back unchanged.
