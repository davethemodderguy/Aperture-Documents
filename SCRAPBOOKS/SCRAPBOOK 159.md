## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **VISITOR INTERACTION LOG**

## **SUBJECT: LUCY MERRYWEATHER – FIRST MEETING WITH LUMI & CORIN LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the approval of extended leave from her duties as **Warden of the Banlands** and **Head of the Merryweather Family**, Lucy Merryweather arrived at Heaven's Blacksite for a personal vacation.

As a frequent visitor to Project SAFEHOUSE, Lucy was already familiar with most of its residents.

However, this visit marked her first meeting with:

- **Lumia ("Lumi") Lockwood**
    
- **Corin Lockwood**
    

---

# **ARRIVAL**

Lucy entered the SAFEHOUSE carrying a travel bag over one shoulder.

Belle was the first to notice.

> "Mom!"

Belle immediately ran over and hugged her.

Endmin followed shortly after with a considerably more composed greeting.

Lucy laughed.

> "It's good to see both of you."

Within minutes, the rest of SAFEHOUSE had gathered.

Introductions were made.

Eventually Belle looked toward the lounge.

Corin was sitting quietly on the sofa with a mug of tea.

Lumi stood beside her.

Belle smiled.

> "Mom... there's two more people I want you to meet."

---

# **FIRST IMPRESSION**

Lucy approached without hesitation.

Corin immediately stood.

Her posture became noticeably tense.

She instinctively lowered her gaze.

> "...Hello."

Lucy smiled warmly.

> "You must be Corin."

Corin gave a small nod.

Before Corin could apologize—as she so often did—Lucy gently interrupted.

> "It's nice to finally meet you."

There was no fear in Lucy's voice.

No hesitation.

Only kindness.

Corin seemed momentarily caught off guard.

---

# **MEETING LUMI**

Belle then introduced the woman standing nearby.

> "This is Lumi."

Lucy turned toward her.

Lumi smiled politely.

> "Lumia Lockwood."

Lucy extended her hand.

> "Lucy Merryweather."

Lumi accepted it.

The handshake lasted only a moment.

Yet both mothers immediately understood something about the other.

Each had spent years protecting children.

Each had known loss.

Each still worried constantly about their family.

No further explanation was necessary.

---

# **THE CONVERSATION**

Later that afternoon, Lucy and Lumi shared tea while Corin quietly listened nearby.

Lucy spoke first.

> "Belle has told me quite a lot about you."

Lumi chuckled softly.

> "Hopefully the good parts."

Lucy smiled.

> "Mostly about how determined you are."

Lumi glanced toward Corin.

> "Determination runs in the family."

There was a brief silence.

Lucy then looked at Corin.

> "Belle worries about you."

Corin looked down.

> "...I know."

Lucy continued gently.

> "She worries because she cares."

> "So do my children."

Corin quietly nodded.

---

# **A MOTHER'S PERSPECTIVE**

As the conversation continued, Lucy noticed Corin unconsciously checking where Lumi was every few moments.

Whenever Corin became distracted, her eyes instinctively searched the room.

Only after confirming that Lumi was still nearby would she visibly relax.

Lucy noticed.

She did not mention it.

Instead, after Corin left briefly to refill her tea, Lucy quietly spoke to Lumi.

> "...She loves you very much."

Lumi smiled sadly.

> "She spent fifteen years believing she'd never see me again."

Lucy looked toward the kitchen where Corin was carefully preparing another cup.

> "...Then I'll make sure she always knows she's welcome here."

---

It was the first time she had been formally welcomed into the Mom Club by another mother.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Lucy Merryweather's visit concluded without incident.

Before departing, she approached Corin one final time.

She gently placed a hand on the teenager's shoulder.

> "Take care of yourself."

Corin instinctively began to answer,

> "...I'm sor—"

Lucy smiled and interrupted with a raised finger.

> "No apologizing."

Corin blinked.

After a brief pause...

She smiled.

A small one.

> "...I'll try."

Lucy nodded.

> "That's all anyone can ask."

Lumi watched the exchange from across the room.

For the first time in a long while...

She saw Corin accept kindness without immediately believing she had to earn it.

It was a small moment.

But everyone in SAFEHOUSE knew by now—

Those were the moments that mattered most.
