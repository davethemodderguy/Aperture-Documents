# **PHENOMENON DESIGNATION**

**Designation:** **Halo Manifestation – "Aureole-01"**

Following Norma Lockwood's recovery from Ether Regression Syndrome and the manifestation of **NULLZONE**, researchers documented a second, considerably more subtle phenomenon.

On rare occasions, a faint **teal halo** appears above Norma's head.

The manifestation lasts only a few seconds before fading completely.

---

# **DESCRIPTION**

The halo is consistently described as:

- Circular in shape.
    
- Glowing with a soft teal light.
    
- Composed of one central ring.
    
- Surrounded by **four perfectly symmetrical pointed triangles** positioned at the cardinal directions.
    
- Completely silent.
    
- Semi-transparent.
    

Unlike a physical object, the halo appears to phase into existence before gradually fading away.

No heat, radiation spikes, or measurable electromagnetic interference have been recorded during its appearance.

---

# **VISIBILITY**

One of the halo's most unusual characteristics is that **not everyone notices it**.

Video recordings confirm the halo is physically present during manifestations.

However, eyewitness reports vary dramatically.

Some personnel clearly describe its appearance.

Others insist they saw nothing unusual despite standing only a few meters away.

Current CCD theory suggests the phenomenon is unusually easy to overlook unless the observer possesses exceptional attention to detail or happens to be looking directly at Norma when it manifests.

As one researcher summarized:

> "It's not invisible."

> "It's just... remarkably easy to miss."

---

# **KNOWN TRIGGERS**

No definitive trigger has been identified.

Recorded appearances have occurred while Norma was:

- Deep in thought.
    
- Concentrating on engineering work.
    
- Quietly drinking tea.
    
- Looking at the Starlands sky.
    
- Activating or immediately deactivating NULLZONE.
    

Researchers have been unable to reproduce the phenomenon on demand.

The **Aureole-01** halo remains one of the least understood Ether-related phenomena documented following Norma Lockwood's recovery.

Its purpose, if any, has yet to be determined.

As one CCD researcher concluded in the report margin:
