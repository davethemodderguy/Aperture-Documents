---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

After several months of unsuccessful searches, Sora has finally been located.

He was discovered outside the Blacksite, beneath a tree, sitting in the snow.

The circumstances of his discovery were distressing.

Sora was hugging an old collar. He was sobbing uncontrollably, with tears having soaked through his clothing. His body was visibly shivering from the cold.

The search team approached and identified the name tag attached to the collar.

It read:

**MIA.**

Before personnel could establish contact, Sora noticed them and immediately fled.

He ran deeper into the snow, carrying the collar with him.

The recovery operation remains active.

---

## 2. DISCOVERY SCENE

The search team initially observed a small figure beneath a tree.

Upon approaching, they identified Sora.

He appeared to be crying intensely. His clothing was soaked with tears, and he was visibly affected by the cold.

He was not attempting to hide the collar.

He was holding it against himself.

The collar was examined visually from a distance. Its attached name tag displayed the inscription:

**Mia**

This is the first confirmed physical evidence identifying Sora's deceased younger sister by name.

For months, Sora's sister had been referred to in C.O.R.E. records as an unidentified Cat Thiren.

That designation is now superseded.

Her name was Mia.

The discovery team did not have sufficient time to conduct a proper examination of the collar before Sora fled.

The item remains in his possession.

---

## 3. SUBJECT FLIGHT RESPONSE

When Sora noticed the search team, he immediately ran away.

He did not approach personnel, respond to calls, or permit the team to establish contact.

He continued running deeper into the snow.

The search team has been instructed not to interpret this as deliberate hostility.

Considering Sora's history, the appearance of multiple people approaching him may have been sufficient to trigger an extreme fear response.

His current physical condition is particularly concerning.

He was already shivering before fleeing. Continued exposure to the cold, combined with wet clothing and prolonged emotional distress, places him at significant risk.

His unusual inability to die does not protect him from hypothermia, exhaustion, or the consequences of untreated injuries.

**Sora must be recovered, but he must not be chased into further danger.**

---

## 4. SIGNIFICANCE OF THE COLLAR

The collar bearing Mia's name is considered a highly significant personal possession.

Its presence raises several possibilities:

- Sora retained the collar from his sister's belongings.
    
- He recovered it at some point during his years away from the farm.
    
- He has been carrying it throughout his disappearance.
    
- He may have been attempting to remember or preserve his sister's identity.
    

None of these possibilities has yet been confirmed.

What is confirmed is that Sora was holding the collar while crying.

The item must not be confiscated during recovery.

It must remain with him unless immediate medical circumstances make temporary handling absolutely necessary. Even then, it must be returned as soon as safely possible.

The name tag also resolves one longstanding uncertainty.

His sister was not simply an unnamed figure in his history.

Her name was Mia.

---

## 5. IMMEDIATE RESCUE DIRECTIVES

The search operation is now transitioning into a low-stimulation child recovery procedure.

**Search team instructions:**

1. Do not surround Sora.
    
2. Do not pursue him at running speed or force him farther into the snow.
    
3. Maintain visual awareness where possible without making him feel cornered.
    
4. Keep warm blankets, dry clothing, and medical supplies ready.
    
5. Prepare a warm, quiet recovery area.
    
6. Do not take the collar away from him.
    
7. Use familiar, gentle voices if communication becomes possible.
    
8. If Sora stops moving or collapses, medical personnel must intervene immediately, using the least distressing safe approach.
    
9. Once recovered, assess his temperature, hydration, injuries, and general condition.
    
10. Do not demand an explanation for his disappearance.
    

If Jake is informed of the discovery, his participation must remain voluntary. He must not be sent into the snow without proper support or made responsible for bringing Sora back.

---

## 6. THE NAME WE DID NOT KNOW

C.O.R.E. has amended Sora's historical records.

**Former designation:** Unidentified younger sister  
**Confirmed name:** Mia  
**Species:** Cat Thiren  
**Relationship:** Sora's younger sister  
**Status:** Deceased

The name is to be preserved in all subsequent documentation.

No attempt should be made to force Sora to discuss Mia immediately after recovery.

If he wishes to speak about her, personnel should listen.

If he wishes to remain silent, personnel should respect that too.

The collar may be the only surviving possession he has that directly connects him to his sister.

It is not an ordinary lost-and-found item.

## 7. C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

We spent months looking for Sora.

We eventually found him beneath a tree, outside the place that was supposed to keep him safe.

He was crying.

He was cold.

And he was holding a collar.

A little name tag told us something he could not tell us himself.

Mia.

For months, we knew Sora had a little sister. We knew she had died. We knew he had lost her, and that he could no longer remember her name.

Now we know.

Perhaps he found the collar recently. Perhaps he had carried it for years. Perhaps he has been holding onto it because it is one of the few things he has left of her.

We do not know.

But when the search team approached, he ran.

Even now, after everything, he is still frightened of the people trying to help him.

We must not make the mistake of thinking that finding Sora means we have recovered him.

**We have found the child. We have not yet made him feel safe enough to come home.**

And this time, we must be patient.

Let him keep Mia's collar.

Let him cry.

Let him be frightened.

Just please, get him out of the snow.

— **C.O.R.E. Search & Rescue Division**
