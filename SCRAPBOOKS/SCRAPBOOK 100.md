# **SUBJECT**

**Designation:** AE-LK-01

**Common Name:** _The Lockwood Matriarch_

**Status:** Confirmed Anomalous Entity

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following several weeks of investigation by C.O.R.E. researchers, Medical Services, and Aperture's Ether Research Division, the phenomenon surrounding Corin Lockwood has been formally reclassified.

The manifestation is **not** considered a hallucination or a product of psychological illness.

It is recognized as an independent anomalous entity.

Genetic analysis was naturally impossible.

Physical interaction remains impossible.

However, the entity has repeatedly demonstrated knowledge inaccessible to Corin, accurately describing events occurring inside Heaven's Blacksite while Corin remained at KCA Headquarters.

The probability of coincidence was determined to be negligible.

---

# **IDENTITY**

After reviewing Corin's testimony, corroborating historical records, and numerous verified observations, C.O.R.E. reached the following conclusion.

> **The entity identifies itself as Corin Lockwood's deceased mother.**

No contradictory evidence has been discovered.

Whether the entity is a preserved consciousness, an Ether-based spiritual manifestation, or another unknown phenomenon remains under investigation.

Its identity, however, is considered authentic by the investigating team.

---

# **VISIBILITY**

One characteristic immediately distinguishes the manifestation from previously documented Ether entities.

The Lockwood Matriarch is perceptible **only** to Corin Lockwood.

No other individual has successfully observed the entity.

Attempts involving:

- Standard cameras.
    
- Thermal imaging.
    
- Ether spectrum imaging.
    
- Light amplification.
    
- Multidimensional scanners.
    

Have produced no visual evidence.

To everyone else...

Corin appears to be standing alone.

---

# **BEHAVIOR**

The entity has demonstrated exclusively protective behavior.

Documented actions include:

- Reminding Corin to take prescribed medication.
    
- Encouraging adequate sleep.
    
- Discouraging excessive overtime.
    
- Comforting Corin during panic episodes.
    
- Correcting negative self-beliefs.
    
- Encouraging reconciliation with family and friends.
    
- Providing accurate, real-time information regarding the wellbeing of Blacksite personnel.
    

The entity has never attempted to influence Corin for personal gain.

No hostile or manipulative behavior has been observed.

---

# **CORIN'S OBSERVATION**

When asked what the manifestation usually does, Corin answered simply.

> "...She acts like my mom."

The interviewer requested clarification.

Corin smiled faintly.

> "...She worries too much."

> "...Reminds me to eat."

> "...Tells me to sleep."

> "...Scolds me when I work all night."

After a long pause...

> "...It's exactly what she'd have done."

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL EFFECT**

Medical personnel report that the entity's presence has measurably reduced Corin's anxiety during periods of isolation.

Even so, therapists continue to encourage Corin to maintain relationships with living family members and trusted colleagues.

The manifestation itself appears to support this goal.

According to Corin, one of the entity's most frequent statements is:

> **"Go see your brother."**

Or...

> **"Norma will worry if you skip another meal."**

Or simply...

> **"They still love you."**

Researchers note that the entity consistently encourages Corin to reconnect with others rather than withdraw from them.

---

Director Lucious personally reviewed the completed investigation.

> "Sometimes..."

Then added one final sentence.

> "Whatever force allows a mother to continue watching over her child..."

> "...I have no intention of standing in its way."

The Lockwood Matriarch remains classified as a **Non-Hostile Ether Anomalous Entity**.

Observation continues.

No containment procedures have been authorized.

To remain beside her youngest daughter...

Until Corin no longer believes she has to face the world alone.
