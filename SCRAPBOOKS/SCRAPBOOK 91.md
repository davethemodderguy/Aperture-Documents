# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **CONFIDENTIAL PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – RETURN OF THE ETHER RESONANCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical Director & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **SUMMARY**

After approximately two weeks without experiencing the maternal Ether resonance previously documented in her psychological evaluations, Corin Lockwood reported its unexpected return.

The event occurred during the early hours of the morning while she was alone in her office at KCA Headquarters.

Medical staff cannot determine why the resonance disappeared or why it returned.

Its origin remains unknown.

---

## **PATIENT ACCOUNT**

Corin had fallen asleep at her desk after another overnight shift.

She later described waking to an unusual sense of familiarity.

Not a sound.

Not a dream.

Just...

A presence she had been desperately hoping to feel again.

Then she heard a quiet voice.

One she had feared she would never hear again.

> **"...I'm sorry."**

Corin remained still.

The voice continued.

> **"...I know I was gone for a long time."**

> **"...I'm sorry you had to carry those nights by yourself."**

Corin later stated she began crying before she could answer.

She covered her face with her hands.

After several minutes, she managed to whisper:

> "...Please..."

> "...Don't leave again."

The resonance replied gently.

> **"...I'll be here whenever I can."**

> **"...And when I can't..."**

> **"...Remember that you are not alone."**

---

## **CLINICAL OBSERVATION**

Following the reported return of the resonance, clinicians observed a modest but noticeable improvement in Corin's emotional state.

She appeared:

- More rested.
    
- More willing to engage in conversation.
    
- More receptive to support from trusted colleagues.
    
- Less withdrawn during routine interactions.
    

Counselors emphasize that Corin continues to require ongoing psychological care and support from the people around her.

The resonance appears to be a comforting influence, but it is not considered a replacement for treatment or human relationships.

---

## **SAFEHOUSE**

That evening, Corin did something she had not done in weeks.

She visited SAFEHOUSE.

There was no announcement.

No explanation.

She simply opened the door.

Belle looked up from the sofa.

She smiled.

> "...Welcome back."

Norma looked over from the kitchen.

> "...Tea?"

Corin nodded.

> "...Please."

Arcturus glanced up from the dining table.

He noticed something different immediately.

Not that Corin looked happy.

She still looked tired.

Still carried the weight of everything that had happened.

But...

She looked a little less alone.

He smiled quietly.

> "...Good to see you, kid."

Corin smiled back.

A small one.

But a genuine one.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the latest report before writing a brief note.

> "Recovery is rarely marked by dramatic victories."

> "Sometimes it is measured by a single conversation."

> "A shared cup of tea."

> "Or the decision to come home."

---

## **STATUS**

Corin continues to participate in long-term medical and psychological treatment.

The return of the maternal Ether resonance has coincided with an improvement in her overall wellbeing, though clinicians note that healing remains an ongoing process.

When asked what she remembered most from that night, Corin answered without hesitation.

> "...It apologized."

She smiled softly.

> "...No one had ever apologized to me for leaving before."

For the first time in many weeks, Corin slept through the night without waking from a nightmare.
