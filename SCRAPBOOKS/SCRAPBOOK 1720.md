# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

C.O.R.E. has identified a significant security concern regarding the anomalous blue dust associated with Jake.

The material is not confined to Jake's person.

It can be found in his room, particularly around his old mattress, where it has accumulated over an extended period.

The dust is a naturally occurring residue associated with Jake's anomalous nature. Jake has previously demonstrated that it can produce substantial sensory-disruption effects when used in an anomalous device.

Consequently, **Jake's room must no longer be treated as an environment free of anomalous material.**

The primary concern is not that Jake possesses the material.

It is that other individuals may discover it and attempt to misuse it.

---

# 1. ENVIRONMENTAL PRESENCE

Jake's original sleeping arrangement consisted of an old mattress placed directly on the grass floor of his room.

Over time, blue dust accumulated around the mattress and nearby surfaces.

The distribution appears consistent with prolonged environmental exposure to Jake and his anomalous activity.

The dust is visually distinctive, although its appearance alone is insufficient to establish its current potency.

No evidence indicates that Jake deliberately manufactures or stockpiles it.

He has largely treated it as an ordinary feature of his surroundings.

When asked about the residue, Jake reportedly answered:

> **JAKE:** "It's just dust."

This description is technically accurate.

It is also profoundly unhelpful from a containment perspective.

---

# 2. THE FLASHBANG INCIDENT

During the recent mercenary raid, Jake used an improvised device containing the blue dust to incapacitate hostile personnel through severe sensory disorientation.

The affected contractors experienced kaleidoscopic vision, impaired orientation, and anomalous star-shaped halos.

The effect was temporary, but sufficiently severe to prevent coordinated movement and combat.

Jake's later use of the device during the interrogation demonstrated that the phenomenon was repeatable.

However, C.O.R.E. has not established that every sample of blue dust will produce the same effect, or that the effect can be safely reproduced outside controlled conditions.

---

# 3. MATERIAL IDENTIFICATION

The blue dust remains classified as an **anomalous particulate associated with Jake**.

Its complete composition and formation mechanism have not been established.

C.O.R.E. distinguishes between three findings:

- **Confirmed:** Blue dust is present in Jake's room.
    
- **Confirmed:** Jake has used the material in an anomalous sensory-disruption device.
    
- **Unconfirmed:** Whether environmental samples retain the same properties, potency, or stability over time.
    

No further assumptions should be made until the material has been properly characterized.

---

# 4. SECURITY CONCERN

The discovery of environmental residue creates a new exposure pathway.

An individual entering Jake's room could encounter the material without being aware of its significance.

Unauthorized collection could then result in attempted experimentation or the construction of additional anomalous devices.

C.O.R.E. will not document improvised explosive assembly or provide a recipe combining the dust with conventional energetic materials. The relevant security issue is the potential interaction between an anomalous substance and ordinary hazardous components, not the specific procedure used to combine them.

**The blue dust is to be treated as potentially hazardous regardless of whether it is visibly active.**

---

# 5. RESIDENT WELFARE

Jake is not to be blamed for the material's presence.

He did not intentionally contaminate his room.

He did not conceal a dangerous stockpile.

He was living in his own residence, using his own mattress, long before the material became a subject of formal investigation.

Personnel must not respond by confiscating his belongings or treating his room as a hostile environment.

Any necessary collection or cleaning must be conducted with his knowledge and appropriate safeguards.

Jake's teddy bear, drawings, flowers, books, and other personal possessions are not to be disturbed unnecessarily.

---

# 6. REQUIRED ACTIONS

C.O.R.E. recommends the following measures:

1. Conduct a non-invasive survey of the room to determine the distribution of the blue dust.
    
2. Have qualified personnel collect and characterize representative samples.
    
3. Establish controlled storage for recovered material.
    
4. Restrict unauthorized access to collected samples.
    
5. Inspect relevant equipment for anomalous contamination before reuse.
    
6. Ensure Jake has a clean, comfortable sleeping area if environmental remediation becomes necessary.
    
7. Investigate whether similar residue exists in other locations Jake regularly occupies.
    

No resident is authorized to collect the dust for personal experimentation.

No personnel are authorized to reproduce Jake's improvised device independently.

---

# 8. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Jake's blue dust presents a manageable but significant security concern.

Its apparent abundance does not establish that it is harmless.

Its familiar appearance does not make it ordinary.

And its presence in a child's bedroom does not make it safe to handle casually.

The objective is not to restrict Jake's existence or turn his room into a laboratory.

The objective is to ensure that environmental residue associated with an anomalous resident does not become an uncontrolled source of exposure or misuse.

Jake may continue living in his room.

His mattress may require attention.

The material will be investigated.

**The child is not the contamination. The residue is the subject of the investigation.**
