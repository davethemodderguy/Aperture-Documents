## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE / TREEHOUSE INCIDENT REPORT**

# **ORPHIE — MISSING PERSON INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Location:** Treehouse  
**Status:** **MISSING**  
**Primary Affected Individual:** Corin  
**Current Risk:** **SEVERE PSYCHOLOGICAL DISTRESS**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie is missing.

She left the Treehouse to collect flowers for the residence.

It was supposed to be a short trip.

She never returned.

No message was left.

No emergency signal was triggered.

No confirmed sighting has been recorded since her departure.

The Treehouse has been searched repeatedly.

Orphie is nowhere to be found.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin noticed first.

At first, she simply waited.

She sat near the entrance.

Then she waited longer.

Eventually, she began checking the windows.

Then the door.

Then the windows again.

Her ears remained pointed toward every sound outside.

Every time she heard something approaching, she immediately looked up.

Every time it wasn't Orphie, her expression fell.

---

# **PANIC**

After several hours, Corin began to panic.

She flew outside.

Circled the Treehouse.

Returned.

Left again.

She called for Orphie repeatedly.

> **"Orphie?"**

No response.

She flew farther.

Then farther.

Eventually, the SAFEHOUSE residents had to stop her from continuing alone.

Corin struggled against them.

> **"I have to find her!"**

---

# **WHY CORIN IS TAKING THIS PARTICULARLY HARD**

Orphie has historically represented an exceptional source of safety for Corin.

When Corin is frightened, distressed, exhausted, or emotionally overwhelmed, Orphie's presence provides reassurance.

Her disappearance therefore represents more than the absence of another resident.

To Corin, the person associated with **absolute safety** has simply vanished.

And nobody knows where she went.

---

# **CORIN'S BEHAVIOR**

Corin has become increasingly restless.

She refuses to properly settle down.

She repeatedly checks the door.

She keeps looking toward the path Orphie normally takes.

She has asked the same question multiple times:

> **"When is she coming back?"**

Nobody has been able to answer.

Eventually:

> **"She said she'd be back."**

No one corrected her.

---

# **SEARCH OPERATION**

The Architects have been notified.

C.O.R.E. has initiated a full search.

Search teams are examining:

- The route between the Treehouse and nearby areas.
    
- Known flower-gathering locations.
    
- Dimensional access points.
    
- Potential anomalous disturbances.
    
- Orphie's last known location.
    
- Possible signs of forced displacement.
    
- Any unexplained rift activity.
    

Guiding Light is assisting with dimensional searches.

Star has offered access to the Starlands.

Neko is analyzing available tracking data.

The SAFEHOUSE residents have suspended nonessential activities.

---

# **CORIN'S ROLE**

Corin insists on participating.

She refuses to remain behind.

Her wings are fully healed.

Her flight capability is unrestricted.

She has been repeatedly told not to search alone.

Corin reluctantly agreed.

She remains visibly anxious.

Every few minutes, she looks toward the Treehouse.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

Orphie's absence has changed the atmosphere completely.

The flowers she intended to bring back are still missing.

The place where she normally sits remains empty.

Corin eventually found the basket Orphie had intended to use.

She picked it up.

She held it against herself.

Her voice became quiet.

> **"...She was supposed to come back."**

Nobody had an answer.

---

# **CORIN'S BREAKDOWN**

Eventually, Corin could no longer maintain her composure.

She curled up near the Treehouse entrance, wings wrapped tightly around herself.

Her tails were motionless.

Her ears were lowered.

She kept staring down the path.

> **"She wouldn't just leave."**

A pause.

> **"She wouldn't."**

Belle sat beside her.

Corin leaned against her.

But she continued watching the entrance.

Waiting.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie left the Treehouse for what should have been an ordinary errand.

She intended to return with flowers.

She never came back.

For everyone else, the disappearance is deeply concerning.

For Corin, it is terrifying.

She keeps waiting by the door.

She keeps listening for footsteps.

She keeps looking toward the path.

And despite everyone telling her that the search is underway, Corin continues whispering the same thing:

> **"Please come home, Orphie."**

The flowers can wait.

The search cannot.
