### **PERSONNEL ANOMALOUS OBSERVATION LOG**

# **LUNA — EXTENDED MANIFESTATION EVENT**

**Classification:** Level 4 – Internal Personnel Observation  
**Subject:** Corey / Luna  
**Event Type:** Extended Manifestation  
**Status:** Non-hostile

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has documented an unusual event involving **Luna**, Corey's lunar manifestation.

For the first time recorded, Luna remained manifested for an entire day.

Normally, Luna appears during nighttime hours due to Corey's exposure to moonlight while sleeping.

A full-day manifestation is considered extremely uncommon.

---

# **CAUSE**

The exact cause of the extended manifestation is unknown.

No external anomaly, equipment malfunction, or artificial influence was detected.

Corey was aware of the transformation and remained comfortable with Luna being present.

---

# **LUNA DURING DAYLIGHT**

A daytime Luna manifestation is considered a rare occurrence.

Although Luna's abilities are lunar-based, she is not harmed by sunlight.

However, direct sunlight causes noticeable differences.

Observed changes include:

- Softer azure glow.
    
- Reduced lunar intensity.
    
- Less prominent moon-based effects.
    
- A calmer, more subtle appearance.
    

The most notable observation:

> **Luna standing under the sun is extremely rare.**

---

# **SOLAR EXPOSURE**

Personnel initially expected sunlight to force Corey back into her normal form or trigger Solaria.

This did not occur.

Luna remained stable.

The solar and lunar aspects appear to be separate manifestations rather than opposing transformations.

---

# **LUNA'S BEHAVIOR**

Despite being a different manifestation, Luna behaves similarly to Corey.

Her personality remains:

- Calm.
    
- Patient.
    
- Quiet.
    
- Thoughtful.
    

She retains Corey's memories and preferences.

The primary differences remain:

- Increased wisdom.
    
- Greater confidence.
    
- Lunar magic.
    
- Slower, more peaceful demeanor.
    

---

# **LUNA COOKING**

One of the most notable observations occurred when Luna entered the SAFEHOUSE kitchen.

Despite being a different manifestation, Luna prepared food exactly as Corey would.

Her cooking style remained identical.

Observed similarities:

- Same techniques.
    
- Same timing.
    
- Same recipes.
    
- Same attention to detail.
    
- Same preference for warm meals.
    

When asked how she knew the recipes, Luna answered:

> "...Because I am Corey."

---

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

Marts reacted positively to the daytime Luna event.

The usual lunar attraction effect was weaker under the sun.

However, Marts still recognized her.

Their response:

> "...Luna."

> "...Still Luna."

Several Marts remained nearby throughout the day.

---

# **NORMA'S OBSERVATION**

Norma studied the event carefully.

Her conclusion:

> "The transformation changes the expression of her abilities, not her identity."

Luna agreed.

> "...Correct."

---

# **NEKO'S OBSERVATION**

Neko examined the situation from an engineering perspective.

His conclusion:

> "...Unexpected."

After observing Luna cooking:

> "...But not surprising."

---

# **THE END OF THE DAY**

As evening approached, Luna remained in the SAFEHOUSE kitchen.

No dramatic event occurred.

No emergency.

No anomaly.

She simply cooked.

She ate.

She talked with the residents.

She spent time with the people Corey cared about.

For one entire day, the SAFEHOUSE experienced something unusual:

Not Corey with Luna appearing at night.

Not Corey waiting for the moon.

Just Luna.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Luna is normally a nighttime presence.

A quiet figure beneath the moon.

A blue glow in the darkness.

A companion for those who wake from nightmares.

But on this day, Luna existed beneath the sun.

She cooked the same meals.

Shared the same memories.

Laughed at the same jokes.

And reminded everyone:

She is not a separate person.

She is another side of Corey.

**End of Personnel Anomalous Observation Log**
