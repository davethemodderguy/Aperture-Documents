### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE — FACILITY MODIFICATION & PERSONNEL ASSIGNMENT**

# **SAFEHOUSE — CHEF & TREEHOUSE RIFT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Facility:** Room N-07 / SAFEHOUSE  
**Additional Location:** Corin's Treehouse  
**Status:** Operational

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Project SAFEHOUSE has received two notable additions to its daily operations.

First:

**Corin is now officially recognized as SAFEHOUSE's primary chef.**

Second:

**Guiding Light has established a permanent dimensional connection between SAFEHOUSE and Corin's treehouse.**

The connection is disguised as an ordinary-looking **glowing blue door**.

During nighttime hours, the glow automatically disappears.

---

# **CORIN — SAFEHOUSE CHEF**

Corin has become the SAFEHOUSE's primary cook.

Her preferred activities include:

- Cooking dinner.
    
- Baking.
    
- Making soup.
    
- Roasting meat.
    
- Preparing sandwiches.
    
- Making tea.
    
- Experimenting with new recipes.
    

Her cooking has become one of the more reliable communal activities within SAFEHOUSE.

Corin particularly enjoys cooking when everyone is present.

---

# **COOKING STYLE**

Corin's cooking is notably careful.

She tends to prepare food for everyone rather than making individual meals.

Common SAFEHOUSE dinners include:

- Roasted meat.
    
- Soup.
    
- Fresh bread.
    
- Sandwiches.
    
- Baked goods.
    
- Various vegetable dishes.
    
- Tea.
    

Her feather-based knife remains her preferred kitchen tool.

The feather can be temporarily used like a cutting blade before being returned to its normal function.

---

# **THE FEATHER KNIFE**

Corin occasionally plucks or uses a suitable feather from her wings as a knife.

This has caused considerable confusion among new personnel.

The feathers remain dormant while detached unless deliberately launched.

Corin has demonstrated that she can safely use one as a cutting instrument without triggering its projectile properties.

Neko has repeatedly reminded everyone:

> **"Don't accidentally throw the chef's knives."**

This is considered sound advice.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S RIFT**

Guiding Light has created a stable dimensional rift connecting:

**SAFEHOUSE — Blacksite**

with

**Corin's Treehouse**

The rift has been intentionally disguised as a conventional architectural feature.

Its physical manifestation is a **glowing blue door**.

---

# **THE BLUE DOOR**

The door appears ordinary at first glance.

Its primary distinguishing feature is its soft blue glow.

During daylight hours, the glow is clearly visible.

At night, the door's anomalous illumination completely disappears.

The door remains physically present.

It simply becomes visually ordinary.

This was intentionally designed to make the treehouse connection less intrusive during nighttime.

---

# **FUNCTION**

Opening the door provides immediate passage between the two locations.

### **Blacksite Side**

The door opens into SAFEHOUSE.

### **Treehouse Side**

The door opens into Corin's treehouse.

The connection is effectively instantaneous.

No conventional corridor exists between the locations.

---

# **SECURITY**

The rift has been registered with C.O.R.E. security systems.

Unauthorized personnel cannot simply use the door to bypass Blacksite security.

The doorway recognizes SAFEHOUSE access permissions.

Attempting to interfere with the rift is prohibited.

Guiding Light has stated that she can close or relocate the connection if necessary.

---

# **DAILY USE**

The rift has dramatically changed how Corin interacts with SAFEHOUSE.

She no longer needs to fly to the Blacksite every time she wants to visit the residents.

She can simply walk through the blue door.

Likewise, SAFEHOUSE residents can visit her treehouse without requiring dimensional transportation.

This has made the two locations functionally part of the same living environment.

---

# **THE CHEF'S COMMUTE**

Corin's new routine is particularly convenient.

She can:

**Wake up in the treehouse.**

↓

**Walk through the blue door.**

↓

**Enter SAFEHOUSE.**

↓

**Cook dinner.**

↓

**Eat with everyone.**

↓

**Return to the treehouse.**

↓

**Sleep.**

This has become her preferred method of commuting.

---

# **NIGHTTIME APPEARANCE**

After sunset, the door loses its blue glow.

Residents have described the effect as slightly eerie.

During the day:

> **"There's a glowing blue dimensional door."**

At night:

> **"There's just a door."**

The transition occurs automatically.

No manual activation is required.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE REACTION**

The residents quickly became accustomed to the new connection.

Neko uses it frequently.

Belle likes visiting Corin's treehouse.

Endmin appreciates the convenience.

Bao occasionally checks on Corin.

Star has used the door to visit the Blacksite without needing to open another rift.

Orphie considers it useful.

Corin considers it:

> **"Really convenient."**

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S COMMENT**

When asked why she created the connection, Guiding Light simply replied:

> **"Because she shouldn't have to choose between her home and her family."**

No further explanation was provided.

---

# **CURRENT FACILITY CONFIGURATION**

SAFEHOUSE now effectively consists of:

### **Room N-07**

Primary communal residence.

### **Corin's Treehouse**

Secondary residence and personal retreat.

### **Dimensional Door**

Permanent connection between both locations.

The three are treated as one interconnected living environment for SAFEHOUSE purposes.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin no longer needs to fly across the Blacksite whenever she wants to see her friends.

She has a door.

A glowing blue door created by an Architect.

It connects her treehouse directly to the SAFEHOUSE.

And on most days, the first thing that comes through it is Corin—

followed shortly afterward by the smell of whatever she is cooking.

SAFEHOUSE has officially gained:

**A chef.**

**A dimensional doorway.**

And absolutely no additional evidence that this organization should be called a Mafia.
