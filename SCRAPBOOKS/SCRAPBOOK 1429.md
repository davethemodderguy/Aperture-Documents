# 1. OVERVIEW

**Nitro Fuel** is a specialized beverage commonly consumed by SAFEHOUSE resident **Iris**.

The drink is formulated from:

- sugar;
    
- fermented Banlands Roses;
    
- Artiso;
    
- lemon.
    

Its primary intended effect is a temporary increase in **focus and alertness**.

This comes at the expense of physical stamina.

---

# 2. EFFECT PROFILE

Nitro Fuel produces a distinctive two-stage effect.

### STAGE ONE — FOCUS

After consumption, the drinker experiences increased:

- focus;
    
- alertness;
    
- mental endurance;
    
- ability to maintain attention during prolonged activity.
    

The effect makes Nitro Fuel particularly useful during extended tasks requiring sustained concentration.

---

### STAGE TWO — STAMINA DEPLETION

While the focus effect remains active, the drink gradually drains the drinker's physical stamina.

The process is intentionally slow.

The drinker may therefore remain highly focused while becoming progressively more physically exhausted.

Once available stamina is depleted, the beverage's secondary effect becomes apparent.

---

### STAGE THREE — LIGHT INTOXICATION

After the drinker's stamina has been exhausted, Nitro Fuel produces **mild intoxication**.

The resulting intoxication is generally light rather than severe.

The drink therefore should not be interpreted as a conventional energy drink.

It is better described as:

> **A focus-enhancing beverage that converts physical endurance into prolonged concentration, followed by mild intoxication once the available stamina is exhausted.**

---

# 3. FLAVOR PROFILE

Nitro Fuel has a distinctive flavor.

The primary profile is:

> **Lightly bitter.**

This is followed by:

> **A touch of sweetness and sourness.**

The sugar prevents the beverage from becoming excessively bitter, while the lemon provides its sour component.

The fermented Banlands Roses and Artiso contribute much of the drink's unusual character.

Overall flavor description:

> **Lightly bitter, mildly sweet, mildly sour, and distinctly floral.**

---

# 4. IRIS

Iris is particularly fond of Nitro Fuel.

Her preference is practical.

Iris occasionally receives commissions requiring her to operate vehicles for several hours at a time.

Long-distance transportation can become physically and mentally exhausting.

Nitro Fuel allows Iris to maintain focus for considerably longer than she otherwise would.

The trade-off is stamina depletion.

Iris therefore understands the beverage's effect extremely well.

Her standard approach is essentially:

> **Stay focused. Keep driving. Deal with the exhaustion afterward.**

C.O.R.E. Medical has repeatedly advised against treating Nitro Fuel as a substitute for rest.

Iris has repeatedly acknowledged this advice.

Whether she follows it consistently remains questionable.

---

# 5. IRIS'S PREFERRED COMBINATION

Iris's established favorite combination is:

> **Nitro Fuel + Fries**

The pairing is apparently sufficient for her to consider the beverage a complete meal accompaniment.

Medical personnel disagree.

Iris does not.

---

# 6. SAFETY NOTE

Nitro Fuel is not inherently dangerous when consumed appropriately.

However, the following distinction is important:

> **Increased focus does not equal increased stamina.**

A person consuming Nitro Fuel may feel mentally capable of continuing a task while their physical endurance is simultaneously declining.

Once stamina reaches its limit, intoxication follows.

Personnel should therefore avoid using Nitro Fuel to compensate for inadequate rest during prolonged physical activity.

This is particularly relevant to vehicle operators.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Nitro Fuel is an unusual beverage because its primary benefit and primary drawback are directly connected.

It gives the drinker:

**More focus.**

While taking away:

**More stamina.**

Eventually:

**The stamina runs out.**

And then:

**The drinker gets mildly drunk.**

Iris considers this an acceptable arrangement.

C.O.R.E. Medical remains less enthusiastic.
