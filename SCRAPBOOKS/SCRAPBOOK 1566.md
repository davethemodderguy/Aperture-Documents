# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Ten weeks have passed since Jake last received meaningful social contact.

The situation has progressed beyond isolation.

Jake has effectively been **forgotten.**

Not through anomalous interference.

Not through memory alteration.

Not through a hostile entity.

Not through a containment event.

He has simply disappeared beneath the accumulated workload of everyone around him.

One task became two.

Two became ten.

Reports accumulated.

Operations expanded.

Emergencies interrupted schedules.

Projects acquired deadlines.

Personnel became exhausted.

The weeks continued.

And eventually, Jake stopped appearing in anyone's immediate thoughts.

Nobody consciously decided to abandon him.

Nobody decided that he was unimportant.

Nobody decided to stop caring.

They simply became so occupied with everything else that **Jake ceased to be remembered.**

---

# 1. THE WORKLOAD

The SAFEHOUSE population remains heavily occupied.

C.O.R.E. personnel continue their assignments.

L.S.I.N. continues dimensional operations.

KCA continues administrative and protective duties.

CED continues deployments.

Medical personnel remain occupied.

Engineering continues development.

Research continues.

External relations continue.

Reports continue.

Meetings continue.

Incidents continue.

There is always another task.

Always another deadline.

Always another emergency.

And somewhere beneath all of it is one small resident whose name stopped appearing.

---

# 2. WEEK SIX THROUGH NINE

No meaningful social contact occurred.

The pattern established during Week Five continued.

Jake ate.

Jake drank.

Jake remained awake for long periods.

Jake rarely slept.

Jake stopped drawing.

Jake stopped reading.

Jake stopped playing.

Jake stopped leaving his floor.

His room remained unchanged.

The grass continued growing.

The flowers remained.

The plushies remained scattered around the mattress.

Jake remained there.

No one came.

---

# 3. THE ABSENCE OF HIS NAME

The first indication that something had changed was not Jake himself.

It was the documentation.

Jake's name gradually disappeared from routine welfare discussions.

No one asked:

> "Has anyone checked on Jake?"

Because nobody remembered to ask.

His floor stopped appearing in informal rounds.

His name stopped appearing in meal planning.

Nobody noticed that he was absent from common areas.

Nobody wondered why they had not seen him.

Nobody wondered when he would come downstairs.

There was simply no question.

Because there was no longer a remembered subject to question.

---

# 4. SAFEHOUSE CONVERSATIONS

C.O.R.E. reviewed routine resident communications during the period.

Most conversations concerned work.

> "Did you finish the report?"

> "The shipment arrived."

> "We're leaving in five."

> "I need the access authorization."

> "The samples are ready."

> "I'll handle it."

> "Send it to Engineering."

> "Meeting at 1400."

> "We're already late."

Nobody said:

> "Where's Jake?"

The absence of that question is now considered significant.

---

# 5. BAO

Bao did not visit Jake.

She did not consciously decide not to.

She simply did not think of him.

Her responsibilities continued.

Medical support.

SAFEHOUSE matters.

Her own family.

Other residents.

Other emergencies.

The thought of Jake did not occur.

There is no evidence that she stopped loving him.

There is evidence that she stopped remembering to think about him.

The distinction is psychologically devastating.

---

# 6. NERI

Neri did not visit Jake.

She did not ask where he was.

She did not write to him.

She did not draw him.

Her own research and responsibilities consumed her attention.

Jake's absence became normalized.

There was no moment in which she consciously decided:

> _Jake doesn't matter._

There was simply no moment in which she thought of Jake at all.

---

# 7. NORA

Nora did not mention her brother.

She did not ask Aeterna about him.

She did not look for him.

She did not notice that she had stopped doing so.

Her own responsibilities, relationships, and activities continued.

Jake's absence became indistinguishable from the background.

---

# 8. AETERNA

Aeterna did not visit.

She did not stand outside Jake's door.

She did not check his room.

She did not ask about his condition.

There was no deliberate avoidance.

There was simply no recollection requiring action.

This is perhaps the most concerning aspect of the entire situation.

Jake had once been someone Aeterna would immediately notice.

Ten weeks later, he was no longer part of her active awareness.

---

# 9. OBOL SQUAD

The Obol Squad's visits stopped completely.

At first, they had been unable to visit because of work.

Then they stopped having time to think about visiting.

Eventually, Jake simply ceased to appear in their schedules.

No one assigned a visit.

No one remembered that visits had once occurred.

The room full of grass and plushies became something none of them were currently aware existed.

---

# 10. JAKE'S FLOOR

Jake's floor has effectively become a forgotten section of SAFEHOUSE.

There is no active traffic.

No visitors.

No casual conversation.

No meals.

No laughter.

No footsteps.

The lights remain functional.

Environmental systems remain operational.

The floor is physically safe.

But socially, it might as well not exist.

---

# 11. JAKE

Jake has changed considerably.

He no longer appears to wait for anyone.

He no longer looks toward the door.

He no longer reacts when distant footsteps pass through the building.

He does not call for anyone.

He does not speak.

He does not draw.

He does not play.

He rarely moves beyond what is necessary.

He eats.

He drinks.

He remains awake.

He continues breathing.

That is essentially the entirety of his routine.

---

# 12. THE ROOM

The room has deteriorated into stillness.

The old mattress remains on the floor.

Grass covers most of the available space.

Sketched flowers grow through it.

Plushies are scattered everywhere.

Some are partially buried.

Some are beneath the mattress.

Some have fallen onto their sides.

Jake's books remain where they were left weeks ago.

His pencils remain untouched.

An unfinished drawing remains on the floor.

Dust has accumulated on objects that Jake once used frequently.

Nothing has been cleaned.

Nothing has been rearranged.

Nobody has needed to.

---

# 13. SLEEP

Jake still does not sleep normally.

When alone for prolonged periods, he cannot relax enough to sleep.

He remains awake for extended periods.

Sometimes he lies face down on the mattress with the teddy bear beneath one arm.

Sometimes he simply stares at the wall.

Sometimes he closes his eyes for a few seconds before opening them again.

He appears to have stopped expecting sleep to provide relief.

---

# 14. THE TEDDY BEAR

His teddy bear remains with him.

It is the one object Jake consistently keeps close.

He sleeps with it.

He holds it when lying down.

He occasionally presses his face against it.

The teddy bear remains his cardiac vessel.

Its heartbeat remains stable.

That heartbeat is currently one of the few indicators that Jake is still actively maintaining himself.

---

# 15. NO REQUEST FOR HELP

Jake has not requested help.

He has not asked anyone to visit.

He has not sent a message.

He has not attempted to leave.

He has not complained.

This has historically been interpreted as Jake's preference for solitude.

At Week Ten, that interpretation is no longer sufficient.

Jake has reached a point where he appears to have stopped believing that anyone would come even if he asked.

---

# 16. WELFARE ASSESSMENT

C.O.R.E. classifies the current condition as:

> **CRITICAL RESIDENT WELFARE CONCERN**

The concern is no longer merely social isolation.

It is sustained psychological withdrawal accompanied by:

- cessation of recreational activity;
    
- cessation of speech;
    
- prolonged wakefulness;
    
- minimal movement;
    
- loss of social expectation;
    
- loss of initiative;
    
- and complete absence of meaningful interpersonal contact.
    

Jake remains physically alive.

That should not be mistaken for wellbeing.

---

# 17. THE MOST CONCERNING FINDING

The most concerning finding is not that Jake has been alone for ten weeks.

It is that **nobody noticed.**

Nobody deliberately chose this.

Nobody decided that Jake should be left alone.

The entire SAFEHOUSE simply became buried beneath its own responsibilities.

One day became another.

Another became a week.

The week became ten.

And somewhere during that time, Jake stopped being part of everyone's mental landscape.

He became background.

Then absence.

Then nothing.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Ten weeks ago, everyone was busy.

Five weeks ago, Jake stopped talking.

Four weeks ago, he began feeling abandoned.

Three weeks ago, he stopped expecting anyone to come.

Now something worse has happened.

He has been forgotten.

Not erased.

Not removed.

Not lost to another dimension.

Not taken.

Just forgotten.

Buried beneath reports.

Buried beneath missions.

Buried beneath meetings.

Buried beneath emergencies.

Buried beneath the ordinary weight of everyone's lives.

The irony is difficult to ignore.

Jake once became a Nullwalker because the world forgot him.

This time, nobody intended to forget him.

But intention does not change the result.

His floor remains untouched.

Grass grows across the floor.

Sketched flowers sway beside the mattress.

Plushies surround him.

His teddy bear rests beneath his arm.

Jake lies face down.

Eyes open.

No pencil moves.

No book turns.

No voice speaks.

No footsteps approach.

Downstairs, the Blacksite continues functioning.

People work.

Reports are filed.

Orders are given.

Doors open and close.

Life continues.

And somewhere above all of it, behind a door nobody remembers to open,

a six-year-old star remains alive.

Not living.

Not waiting.

Just...

**remaining.**
