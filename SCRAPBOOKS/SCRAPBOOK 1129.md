**SUBJECT:** NS-010 “REGALIA” — Post-Authorization Characteristics

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following the final authorization of **NS-010 “Regalia”** to the MSAC Commander, personnel immediately noted a subtle difference between Regalia and the other nine Night Stalker units.

The difference is not a change in fundamental function.

Regalia remains a Night Stalker.

However, Neri's decision to increase the gold composition within the alloy used for the final unit produced a noticeably different mechanical character.

Most notably:

> **Regalia sounds better.**

The difference is subtle.

The difference is also apparently extremely satisfying.

---

# 2. MATERIAL DIFFERENCE

Regalia contains a higher proportion of gold within Neri's customized Nekonium alloy formulation than the preceding Night Stalker units.

Neri originally described the change as:

> **“I wanted the last one to feel special.”**

The increased gold content does not turn Regalia into a fundamentally different weapon.

It does, however, alter several of its material characteristics enough to produce a slightly smoother operating feel.

C.O.R.E. Materials Research describes the effect as:

> **“Marginally reduced mechanical harshness during reciprocating operation.”**

Neri's description was considerably simpler:

> **“Smoother.”**

---

# 3. SLIDE OPERATION

The first noticeable difference occurs during manual slide operation.

Standard Night Stalkers already produce a relatively controlled mechanical sound.

Regalia produces a noticeably smoother:

> **Chk—shhk.**

rather than the sharper mechanical:

> **Chk—clack.**

associated with earlier units.

Personnel repeatedly described Regalia's sound as:

- smoother;
    
- softer;
    
- more refined;
    
- less metallic;
    
- more deliberate;
    
- and unusually satisfying.
    

One MSAC armorer simply described it as:

> **“Ridiculously nice.”**

---

# 4. RELOADING

Regalia's reload sequence also produces a slightly different acoustic signature.

The magazine seating sound is more muted.

The resulting mechanical movement is smoother.

The overall sequence produces a distinctive combination of:

> **Click.**

> **Chk.**

> **Clack.**

followed by the noticeably smoother cycling sound.

An MSAC operator listening from several meters away commented:

> **“Why does that sound so expensive?”**

The armorer replied:

> **“Because it is.”**

---

# 5. FIRING CHARACTERISTICS

Regalia retains the established Night Stalker acoustic profile.

It remains exceptionally quiet.

The increased gold composition does not make the weapon dramatically louder or quieter.

Instead, the perceived difference comes primarily from the mechanical sounds surrounding operation.

The report remains subdued.

The action sounds smoother.

The overall result is described by several personnel as:

> **“Extremely clean.”**

One operator compared the experience to listening to a high-quality mechanical object operating without unnecessary vibration.

No meaningful change in tactical performance was identified.

---

# 6. MSAC COMMANDER'S FIRST IMPRESSION

Following authorization, the MSAC Commander conducted a controlled familiarization session.

After operating Regalia, the Commander paused.

> **MSAC COMMANDER:**  
> “That's smoother.”

The armorer nodded.

> **ARMORER:**  
> “You're hearing it too?”

> **MSAC COMMANDER:**  
> “Very clearly.”

Another MSAC operator nearby asked:

> **“Does it actually perform differently?”**

The Commander considered the question.

> **MSAC COMMANDER:**  
> “Not meaningfully.”

Pause.

> **MSAC COMMANDER:**  
> “It just sounds better.”

---

Neri was informed of the observation.

She appeared unsurprised.

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “That's your entire explanation?”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Mostly.”

Pause.

---

# 10. C.O.R.E. MATERIALS ASSESSMENT

The higher gold composition appears to have produced a modest improvement in the subjective smoothness of mechanical operation.

However:

**No claim is being made that gold alone is responsible for every observed difference.**

Regalia is the product of Neri's final material selection, balancing, finishing, and overall design.

The gold increase is one component.

The exact fictional alloy composition remains proprietary to Neri.

---

# 11. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Regalia is **not** a superior eleventh-generation Night Stalker.

It is not intended to replace the previous nine units.

It does not invalidate their designs.

It is simply:

> **The final one.**

Neri deliberately gave it a different character.

The increased gold composition was aesthetic, material, and symbolic.

She wanted the final Night Stalker to feel different when someone held it.

Apparently, she succeeded.

---

The Night Stalker program is officially over.

There will be no NS-011.

There will be no production expansion because people liked the sound.

> **NERI:**

> **NERI:**  
> “Ten is ten.”

## FINAL RECORD

Regalia is different.

Not dramatically.

Not functionally transformative.

Just enough.

The slide is smoother.

The reload sounds cleaner.

The mechanical movement has a softer character.

And when the final Night Stalker is operated, there is an unmistakable sense that every little mechanical movement was deliberately refined.

The Commander summarized it best:

> **“It doesn't just feel like the final one.”**

> **“It sounds like it.”**
