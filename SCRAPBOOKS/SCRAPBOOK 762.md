# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE / PERSONNEL CARE DIVISION**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — TEN WEEKS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — CRITICAL / ANOMALOUS MISSING PERSON  
**Status:** **UNLOCATED**  
**Time Since Last Confirmed Presence:** **10 WEEKS**  
**Confirmed Traces:** **NONE**

---

# **TEN WEEKS**

Ten weeks have passed since Alice disappeared.

There is still nothing.

No footprints.

No clothing.

No journal pages.

No drawings.

No discarded belongings.

No security footage.

No biological traces.

No confirmed sightings.

No indication of where she went.

The search has produced absolutely nothing.

---

# **THE SCOOTER**

Alice's scooter remains docked at the SAFEHOUSE.

It has not moved.

The tracker has remained operational throughout the entire search.

There is no possibility that Alice secretly rode it somewhere.

She walked.

Whatever happened afterward occurred on foot.

---

# **THE SEARCH**

The search area has expanded repeatedly.

The Blacksite has been searched.

The surrounding grounds have been searched.

The expanded perimeter has been searched.

The Treehouse has been searched dozens of times.

Every location associated with Alice has been inspected.

No trace has been found.

Search teams have even returned to previously cleared areas.

Still nothing.

---

# **THE IMPOSSIBILITY**

Alice's disappearance has now exceeded every reasonable expectation.

Ten weeks without confirmed food.

Ten weeks without confirmed water.

Ten weeks without confirmed shelter.

For a child as physically fragile as Alice, conventional survival is considered effectively impossible.

And yet—

there is no body.

No remains.

No evidence of what happened.

Nothing.

C.O.R.E. cannot declare Alice deceased.

There is simply insufficient evidence.

---

# **THE EMPTY ROOM**

Alice's Treehouse room remains preserved.

Her bed remains untouched.

Her pencils remain in their container.

The new sketchbook is still blank.

The ruined sketchbook sits beside it.

Her room has become almost completely unchanged.

Nobody wants to move anything.

Nobody wants to turn the light off.

Nobody wants to be the person who finally accepts that she isn't coming back.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin still checks the Treehouse every morning.

She opens the door.

Looks inside.

And for a fraction of a second, there is always the same expectation.

Perhaps Alice is sitting there.

Perhaps she came back while everyone was asleep.

Perhaps she is curled up beneath her blanket.

Perhaps she is simply waiting.

The room is always empty.

Corin still says:

> "...Morning, Alice."

Then she leaves the door open.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle has stopped asking when Alice will return.

She instead says:

> "When Alice comes back..."

Everything is phrased that way.

Not _if._

**When.**

When Alice comes back, she'll need her scooter.

When Alice comes back, Belle will show her something.

When Alice comes back, they'll draw together.

When Alice comes back, they'll have movie night.

The future remains written around a person who isn't there.

---

# **STAR**

Star continues to search.

She no longer searches frantically.

She searches methodically.

Every day.

Every corridor.

Every garden.

Every quiet corner.

Every place a small child could possibly hide.

Sometimes she finds herself calling:

> "Alice?"

There is never an answer.

Still, she calls.

---

# **NEKO**

Neko has exhausted most conventional tracking methods.

The scooter tracker is useless.

There is nothing to track.

Environmental sensors have found nothing.

Security footage has found nothing.

No access records correspond to Alice leaving through a known route.

The disappearance remains unresolved.

Neko has begun investigating whether Alice's anomalous history could be involved.

That possibility is being treated seriously.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has preserved Alice's belongings.

Nothing has been discarded.

Not even damaged objects.

The ruined sketchbook remains exactly where it was placed.

Orphie refuses to let anyone throw it away.

> "She might come back for it."

Nobody argues.

---

# **THE STAFF**

The disappearance has affected people outside the SAFEHOUSE as well.

Employees who barely knew Alice still ask about her.

Some remember her riding her scooter through the corridors.

Some remember her sitting quietly near the garden.

Some remember her following them for several meters without saying anything.

Some remember finding her asleep somewhere she shouldn't have been.

A surprising number remember the same thing:

Alice never asked for anything.

---

# **THE REGRET**

Several employees have privately expressed the same regret.

They remember seeing Alice.

They remember walking past her.

They remember thinking:

> _Someone else is probably looking after her._

They remember assuming she was with somebody.

They remember being busy.

Now they wonder how many times Alice had been standing right beside them—

and nobody noticed.

---

# **THE SCOOTER**

The scooter has become an unofficial memorial.

Nobody uses it.

Nobody moves it.

Its docking position has become permanent.

A small label was eventually attached:

> **ALICE — RESERVED**

No one remembers who put it there.

Nobody removed it.

---

# **TEN WEEKS WITHOUT AN ANSWER**

The original question was:

> **Where is Alice?**

After ten weeks, there is still no answer.

The new question is worse:

> **Is Alice somewhere at all?**

No one knows.

C.O.R.E. has refused to close the case.

# **FINAL NOTE**

Ten weeks is long enough for the Blacksite to stop expecting a normal explanation.

But nobody has stopped expecting Alice.

Her chair is still there.

Her room is still there.

Her ruined sketchbook is still there.

Her scooter is still docked.

And somewhere in the SAFEHOUSE, someone still leaves a space open beside them.

Just in case.

Because Alice never demanded that anyone remember her.

She never demanded that anyone look for her.

She never demanded anything.

And perhaps that is why, ten weeks later, the people who loved her are still searching.

Not because they have evidence that she is alive.

Not because they have evidence that she is dead.

But because they refuse to make the same mistake twice.

They will not forget her.
