### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE LOG**

# **THE LITTLE RABBIT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin Lockwood  
**Secondary Subject:** Unidentified Snow Rabbit  
**Location:** Treehouse Perimeter  
**Status:** **UNDER CORIN'S CARE**

---

# **DISCOVERY**

At approximately ██:██, Corin was wandering around the Treehouse perimeter in her tiny cat form.

She stopped.

A small movement had caught her attention beneath the snow-covered roots of the nearby tree.

Corin approached.

There, partially concealed beneath the snow, was a **snow rabbit**.

The animal was alive.

Barely.

---

# **CONDITION**

The rabbit had sustained an injury to one of its hind legs.

It was unable to properly stand.

Its fur was covered in snow.

It was visibly frightened.

Corin did not approach immediately.

She sat several centimeters away.

And watched.

The rabbit watched her back.

Neither moved.

After approximately thirty seconds, Corin slowly extended one paw.

The rabbit did not flee.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin immediately stopped acting like a cat.

She became attentive.

Careful.

Quiet.

She curled herself beside the rabbit without touching the injured leg.

Her miniature wings unfolded slightly, shielding the animal from the wind.

She remained there.

---

# **THE DISCOVERY OF THE RABBIT**

Lumi eventually found Corin sitting beside it.

> **"Corin?"**

Corin looked up.

Then looked back at the rabbit.

> **"...It's hurt."**

Lumi crouched down.

> **"I see."**

Corin's ears lowered.

> **"Can we help it?"**

> **"Of course."**

Corin relaxed slightly.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

The rabbit was carefully brought inside for examination.

No anomalous properties were detected.

The injury was treatable.

C.O.R.E. veterinary personnel provided basic treatment and stabilized the leg.

Corin remained nearby for the entire procedure.

She watched every movement.

When the rabbit became frightened, Corin moved closer.

When it calmed down, she relaxed.

---

# **THE NAME**

Corin was asked what she wanted to call it.

She stared at the rabbit.

Then at the ceiling.

Then back at the rabbit.

Several seconds passed.

> **"...I don't know."**

Personnel suggested several names.

Corin rejected all of them.

> **"No."**

Eventually, she simply said:

> **"It doesn't need one yet."**

The rabbit remains officially **UNNAMED**.

---

# **CORIN'S NEW ROUTINE**

Since discovering the rabbit, Corin has developed a routine.

She checks on it every morning.

She checks again before sleeping.

She makes sure it has water.

She makes sure it has food.

She checks the injured leg.

She sits beside it.

Sometimes she talks.

The rabbit cannot understand most of what she says.

Corin does not seem to care.

---

# **A FAMILIAR PATTERN**

Lumi noticed something.

Corin used to be the fragile one.

The one who got sick.

The one everyone had to watch.

The one who needed someone to sit beside her.

Now Corin is sitting beside something smaller.

Something injured.

Something frightened.

Something that cannot take care of itself.

Lumi quietly remarked:

> **"She's doing what we used to do for her."**

---

# **CORIN'S WINGS**

Corin has begun using her wings as a shelter.

When the temperature drops, she curls herself around the rabbit.

Her feathers provide warmth.

No anomalous flame is produced.

No offensive feather abilities activate.

Her wings simply form a small enclosure around the animal.

The rabbit appears to have accepted this arrangement.

---

# **ORPHIE'S OBSERVATION**

Orphie found Corin asleep beside the rabbit one evening.

Corin was curled into a tiny ball.

The rabbit was tucked beneath one of her wings.

Orphie quietly took a blanket and placed it over both.

Corin opened one eye.

> **"...Don't wake it."**

> **"I won't."**

Corin closed her eye again.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

Corin has already begun documenting the rabbit.

The first page contains a crude drawing.

It is, as expected, terrible.

Underneath:

> **"Found a rabbit."**

Then:

> **"It's hurt."**

Another line:

> **"I'm going to take care of it."**

The next line is crossed out.

A replacement appears beneath it:

> **"We're taking care of it."**

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin found something small in the snow.

Something hurt.

Something that couldn't explain what was wrong.

Something that simply needed someone to stay.

So Corin stayed.

She didn't ask the rabbit to become stronger.

She didn't tell it to stop being afraid.

She didn't tell it that everything would immediately be okay.

She simply sat beside it.

The same way others once sat beside her.

Perhaps Corin remembers what it felt like.

Perhaps she doesn't.

Either way, she seems determined to make sure the little rabbit doesn't have to experience the kind of loneliness she once did.

For now, the rabbit remains unnamed.

Corin doesn't seem bothered.

She has more important things to do.

She has a little creature to look after.

And tonight, beneath one tiny wing...

the rabbit is warm.
