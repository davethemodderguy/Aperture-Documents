### SAFEHOUSE RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION DIVISION

**Document ID:** CORE-SH-OBS-145  
**Classification:** INTERNAL – RESIDENTIAL BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION  
**Status:** ACTIVE – POST-RECOVERY ADJUSTMENT  
**Subjects:** Jake Akana / Sora  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE, Floor 7  
**Observation:** Emerging Sibling-Like Attachment

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following Sora's successful recovery, personnel have observed a notable change in his behavior.

Sora has become exceptionally attached to Jake.

The two are approximately the same age. Nevertheless, Sora consistently behaves as though Jake is his older brother.

He frequently stays close to Jake, follows him around, and appears to prefer having Jake within sight.

This behavior is notably different from Sora's previous avoidance of human contact.

C.O.R.E. considers this a positive development in Sora's recovery, while recognizing that his attachment should be supported without encouraging unhealthy dependence.

---

## 2. OBSERVED BEHAVIOR

Sora's current behavior includes:

- Staying close to Jake whenever possible.
    
- Following Jake between residential areas.
    
- Preferring Jake's company over unfamiliar residents.
    
- Seeking proximity to Jake during ordinary activities.
    
- Treating Jake as a familiar source of reassurance.
    

The most notable characteristic is the apparent difference between their ages as perceived by Sora.

Although they are roughly the same age, Sora behaves as though Jake is the older sibling.

This is not a formal relationship designation.

It is an observed interpersonal dynamic.

Jake, for his part, has not been reported to reject Sora's closeness.

---

## 3. PSYCHOLOGICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Sora's behavior may reflect the development of a strong attachment following his prolonged period of isolation.

Before arriving at Aperture, Sora lost his grandmother and younger sister. He subsequently spent years without a reliable family environment.

His experiences at the Blacksite initially reinforced his avoidance of people.

Jake was among the few individuals who consistently attempted to establish contact without demanding immediate reciprocation.

Their correspondence, eventual cohabitation, separation, and reunion appear to have contributed to the relationship they now share.

Sora's tendency to treat Jake like an older brother may represent his own way of expressing trust and seeking familial security.

However, C.O.R.E. emphasizes that this interpretation is provisional.

Sora should be allowed to define the relationship through his own behavior and communication.

---

## 4. JAKE'S POSITION

There is an important consideration concerning Jake.

Jake is not actually older than Sora.

He is also a child with his own emotional and medical needs.

Although Jake has promised to remain by Sora's side, personnel must ensure that he is not unintentionally placed in the role of a permanent caretaker.

Sora deserves someone he can depend on.

Jake deserves to be someone who can depend on others, too.

Aries and Tom should continue supporting Jake as his older brothers, while Bao and other SAFEHOUSE residents provide additional care for both children.

Neither boy should be expected to manage the other's emotional wellbeing alone.

---

## 5. RESIDENTIAL RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Allow Sora to remain near Jake when both children are comfortable.
    
2. Do not deliberately separate them as a behavioral experiment.
    
3. Continue encouraging Sora to become familiar with other SAFEHOUSE residents at his own pace.
    
4. Preserve Sora's private den as a place where he can retreat independently.
    
5. Allow Jake to spend time with Tom, Aries, Clover, and other residents without Sora interpreting every separation as abandonment.
    
6. Maintain ordinary routines involving shared meals, recreation, and rest.
    
7. Do not tease either child about their apparent sibling roles.
    

The objective is to build a larger environment of trust around both children.

Jake can be Sora's favorite person without being his only safe person.

---

## 6. C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

There is something rather unusual about these two.

They are roughly the same age.

Jake is not Sora's older brother.

And yet, Sora seems to have decided otherwise.

He follows Jake around like a little brother following someone he trusts. He stays close. He seems more comfortable when Jake is nearby.

Perhaps age isn't particularly important to him.

Perhaps what matters is that Jake came back.

Jake visited when Sora wanted everyone gone.

Jake wrote to him when talking was difficult.

Jake made room for him.

And when Sora disappeared, Jake went looking.

Eventually, he found him.

Sora has already lost a grandmother and a little sister. We cannot know whether he sees some familiar comfort in this new relationship, and we should never expect Jake to replace either of them.

But perhaps he has found something new.

A friend who feels like family.

And Jake, who has spent so much of his life looking after everyone else, now has someone who wants to stay beside him just as much.

**They may be the same age. But apparently, Sora has already decided that Jake is his big brother.**

C.O.R.E. sees no reason to argue with him.
