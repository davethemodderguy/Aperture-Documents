---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Woodland Coorp personnel successfully abducted Jake.

This sentence continues to be increasingly difficult for C.O.R.E. to write.

Neko's comment upon receiving the initial notification was:

> **NEKO:**  
> “When will they have to give up, for God's sake?”

Woodland Coorp appears to have had **no meaningful understanding of what they had captured.**

They knew they had taken a child.

They did not know they had taken a child who was also:

- a Weeping Angel;
    
- a human manifestation of a literal star;
    
- an anomalous celestial entity;
    
- and, under sufficiently hostile circumstances, capable of reducing biological matter to blue stellar particulate.
    

This misunderstanding lasted until the moment Jake escaped.

It ended very quickly.

---

# 2. KIDNAPPING

Jake was transported to a Woodland-controlled facility.

The exact purpose of the kidnapping remains under investigation.

Personnel responsible for the operation apparently believed Jake to be a vulnerable anomalous child.

No evidence indicates that they understood the full extent of his stellar nature.

They restrained him.

Jake did not panic.

He did not scream.

He did not beg.

He simply waited.

This became the first major error made by Woodland personnel.

---

# 3. RESTRAINT FAILURE

At an unknown point during containment, Jake broke free of his restraints.

The facility immediately entered an armed response state.

Personnel were deployed into the corridor.

Jake remained stationary.

He looked toward them.

The mercenaries raised their weapons.

A nearby hijacked security camera was still accessible to Aperture systems.

Neko was watching.

---

# 4. NEKO'S RESPONSE

Neko initially believed Jake was about to be overwhelmed.

He immediately opened the emergency communications channel.

> **NEKO:**  
> “MSAC! SAFEHOUSE! We have a hostile facility with Jake! Backup, now!”

Multiple personnel began mobilizing.

Neko continued watching the camera.

Then Jake moved.

---

# 5. FIRST WAVE

Jake raised one hand.

He waved it once.

There was no visible projectile.

No flash.

No explosion.

The mercenaries' weapons began breaking apart into **blue glowing dust.**

The effect spread through their arms.

Weapons disappeared.

Hands and forearms were reduced to luminous particulate.

The blue material drifted briefly through the corridor before fading.

The surviving personnel froze.

They had apparently expected a frightened child.

They had not expected the child to simply remove their weapons from existence.

---

# 6. SECOND WAVE

Several mercenaries attempted to continue the attack.

Jake raised his hand again.

A single upward motion.

The heads of the remaining attackers disappeared.

No blood was observed.

No conventional wounds remained.

Only blue stellar particulate drifted through the corridor.

Neko stopped speaking.

The emergency channel remained open.

---

# 7. THIRD WAVE

The remaining personnel attempted to retreat.

Jake looked at them.

He snapped his fingers.

The entire group was immediately annihilated.

Their bodies were reduced to the same blue glowing particulate.

The corridor was left empty.

Except for their boots.

Approximately several dozen pairs remained standing or collapsed on the floor.

Everything above them was gone.

---

# 8. JAKE

Jake did not appear frightened.

He did not appear enraged.

He did not appear confused.

His breathing remained normal.

His expression remained calm.

He simply looked down the now-empty corridor.

Then he walked outside.

No sprinting.

No dramatic declaration.

No attempt to pursue anyone.

He simply left.

---

# 9. APERTURE CAMERA RECORDING

Neko remained connected to the hijacked camera until Jake exited the facility.

For several seconds, nobody on the MSAC channel spoke.

Then:

> **NEKO:**  
> “...What.”

Pause.

> **NEKO:**  
> “What did I just watch?”

A SAFEHOUSE responder asked:

> **“Is Jake okay?”**

Neko checked the camera.

Jake was walking outside.

> **NEKO:**  
> “He's fine.”

Another pause.

> **NEKO:**  
> “The mercenaries are not.”

---

# 10. JAKE'S STATEMENT

Jake was later asked why he had responded with such force.

His answer was quiet and matter-of-fact.

> **Jake:**  
> “If anything put me and all of you in danger, I will try my best to eliminate them.”

Personnel asked whether he had been afraid.

Jake shook his head.

> **Jake:**  
> “No.”

He paused.

> **Jake:**  
> “They were hurting me.”

Another pause.

> **Jake:**  
> “So I stopped them.”

No further explanation was provided.

---

# 11. WOODLAND COORP RESPONSE

Woodland Coorp's surviving personnel displayed considerable confusion.

They had entered the operation believing they were dealing with an anomalous child.

They had apparently not identified Jake's stellar classification.

Their communications following the incident were dominated by fragmented reports.

One recording contained:

> **WOODLAND OPERATOR:**  
> “What the hell was that?”

Another:

> **WOODLAND OPERATOR:**  
> “That wasn't a weapon.”

Another:

> **WOODLAND OPERATOR:**  
> “He just—”

The recording ends there.

C.O.R.E. has no evidence that Woodland understood what they were facing before the incident concluded.

---

# 12. JANITORIAL RESPONSE

The aftermath created an unusual situation for SAFEHOUSE and Blacksite Janitorial Services.

There were no bodies to recover.

There was no blood to remove.

There were no major biological remains.

There were simply scattered quantities of faintly glowing blue particulate.

And several pairs of boots.

The janitorial team was reportedly **extremely pleased.**

One employee reportedly stated:

> **JANITORIAL STAFF:**  
> “This is the easiest Woodland cleanup we've ever had.”

Another:

> **JANITORIAL STAFF:**  
> “No bodies.”

Pause.

> **JANITORIAL STAFF:**  
> “Just blue dust.”

The particulate was collected and disposed of according to anomalous-material procedures.

The boots were catalogued separately.

---

# 13. ANOMALOUS ANALYSIS

Jake's stellar nature provides a plausible explanation for the phenomenon.

Jake is not simply an angel with unusual abilities.

He is a **literal star manifested in human form.**

His stellar nature appears capable of expressing itself through extremely localized matter disintegration.

The incident suggests a three-stage manifestation pattern.

### FIRST WAVE

**Limbs and held objects.**

Blue stellar particulate replaces affected matter.

### SECOND WAVE

**Head and upper structures.**

Rapid localized annihilation.

### THIRD WAVE

**Remaining body.**

Complete conversion into stellar particulate.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

Further testing is not authorized without Jake's consent.

---

# 14. BEHAVIORAL ASSESSMENT

Jake demonstrated no significant panic response.

This is noteworthy.

The incident involved:

- kidnapping;
    
- physical restraint;
    
- armed personnel;
    
- direct threat;
    
- and an attempted assault.
    

Jake did not freeze.

He did not flee blindly.

He did not lash out indiscriminately.

He identified the threat.

He neutralized it.

Then he left.

This should not be interpreted as evidence that Jake is emotionally unaffected by violence.

His response appears more consistent with a simple internal calculation:

> **They are hurting me.**

> **They may hurt everyone else.**

> **Stop them.**

---

# 15. SECURITY IMPLICATIONS

Woodland Coorp now possesses evidence that Jake is significantly more dangerous than previously understood.

C.O.R.E. recommends assuming that future hostile attempts against Jake will be met with comparable defensive capability.

However, personnel should **not** encourage Jake to use this ability.

Jake remains six years old.

He is a resident.

He is not a weapon.

He is not a security system.

He is not an emergency asset.

His ability to annihilate hostile personnel does not change his status as a child requiring protection.

---

After reviewing the full recording, Neko remained silent for approximately thirty seconds.

He eventually said:

Pause.

> **NEKO:**  
> “They restrained the six-year-old star.”

Another pause.
