# **OVERVIEW**

Following the failed robbery involving fifteen armed suspects, Intelligence Division monitored communications between several criminal organizations.

The incident has become widely discussed among individuals involved in armed robbery and organized crime.

Contrary to expectation, discussion rarely focuses on KCA officers.

Instead...

Almost every conversation eventually turns to the Marts.

Within several weeks, intelligence sources documented a noticeable change in how criminal groups describe KCA Headquarters.

Common descriptions include:

> "The place with the blue rocks."

> "The blob building."

> "Don't rob the Mart place."

> "You'll lose before you touch the door."

One intercepted conversation summarized the general opinion.

# **KNOWN EXPECTATIONS**

Among organized criminal groups, attempting to rob the KCA is now commonly expected to result in the following sequence of events:

1. Camera Mart notices the approach.
    
2. Rapid Response Marts disappear into the ventilation system.
    
3. The robbers continue toward the entrance.
    
4. Hundreds of blue rocks suddenly arrive.
    
5. Everyone is disarmed.
    
6. Someone probably faints.
    
7. The Marts become concerned that they caused serious injury.
    
8. Medical personnel reassure the Marts.
    
9. Everyone goes to jail.
    

Intelligence analysts have noted that this summary is, unfortunately, very accurate.

Since Incident KCA-IR-127, no organized group has attempted another direct assault on KCA Headquarters.

The event has become a cautionary story among would-be robbers.
