# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### ANOMALOUS RESIDENT & WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-JAKE-441  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESTRICTED — PSYCHOLOGICAL / ANOMALOUS WELFARE  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE MONITORING  
**SUBJECT:** Jake  
**EVENT:** Persistent Hunger, Grief, and Maternal Attachment

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake is rarely observed happy.

His current emotional state remains remarkably consistent across his documented manifestations.

He is:

**Hungry.**

**Sad.**

**Lonely.**

And, most consistently:

**Missing his mother.**

This remains true even when Nora is present.

It remains true when Aeterna occasionally accompanies them.

Companionship reduces Jake's isolation.

It does not remove his grief.

---

# 2. MANIFESTATION WINDOW

Jake continues to manifest exclusively during the approximate period of:

> **02:00–05:00 HOURS**

During this window, he can be found wandering throughout the Blacksite.

His usual behavior includes:

- searching for food;
    
- looking into cafeterias;
    
- standing near occupied rooms;
    
- watching people from behind glass;
    
- wandering without a clear destination;
    
- sitting alone;
    
- asking about food;
    
- talking about his mother;
    
- or simply standing somewhere quietly.
    

He does not appear to have a specific objective.

He wanders because there is nowhere else for him to go.

---

# 3. PERSISTENT HUNGER

Jake remains hungry.

This is one of the most consistent aspects of his manifestation.

Physical food cannot nourish him.

It passes through his spectral form.

He can smell it.

He can see it.

He can appreciate it.

He cannot consume it in the manner he remembers.

This creates an unusual form of distress.

Jake is capable of recognizing food.

His memories associate food with survival.

His body, however, is no longer capable of receiving it.

---

# 4. OBSERVED FOOD-SEEKING

Jake frequently visits the cafeteria.

He may stand outside the entrance and watch residents eat.

Sometimes he asks:

> **“What's that?”**

Sometimes:

> **“Does it taste good?”**

Sometimes he simply watches.

Nora has occasionally offered him food despite knowing that he cannot eat it.

Jake generally accepts.

He may hold it briefly.

He may smell it.

He may say:

> **“It smells nice.”**

Then the food eventually passes through his hands.

Jake never becomes angry.

He simply looks disappointed.

---

# 5. NORA'S ROLE

Nora remains Jake's primary companion.

She is the only resident who can consistently accommodate his manifestation schedule.

She stays awake during his active hours.

She walks with him.

She talks with him.

She brings him things.

She sits with him when he does not want to walk.

She has become familiar with his silences.

But Nora has learned something difficult:

> **She cannot make Jake happy.**

Not permanently.

She can make him less alone.

She can distract him.

She can give him company.

She can listen.

But eventually, Jake's sadness returns.

---

# 6. OBSERVED INTERACTION

At 02:34, Nora found Jake sitting beneath a stairwell.

She sat beside him.

> **Nora:**  
> “Want to go somewhere?”

Jake shook his head.

> **Nora:**  
> “Want food?”

Jake nodded.

Nora gave him a piece of cake.

Jake held it.

He stared at it.

> **Jake:**  
> “I can't eat it.”

> **Nora:**  
> “I know.”

Jake smelled it.

> **Jake:**  
> “It smells good.”

A pause.

> **Jake:**  
> “My mom used to make food.”

Nora became quiet.

Jake looked down at the cake.

> **Jake:**  
> “I miss her.”

Nora stayed beside him.

Jake continued staring at the cake.

---

# 7. MATERNAL ATTACHMENT

Jake's attachment to his mother remains extraordinarily strong.

This is despite the historical evidence establishing that she abandoned him and did not want him.

Jake does not know this.

He continues remembering her as his mother.

He continues associating her with safety.

He continues expecting that she would eventually return.

He continues missing her.

The contradiction is central to his condition.

The person Jake misses most is the same person who abandoned him.

---

# 8. THE MOTHER HE REMEMBERS

Jake does not describe his mother with complex language.

He does not discuss the failures of their relationship.

He does not question her motives.

He remembers:

> **“My mom.”**

That is enough for him.

When asked what he misses:

> **Jake:**  
> “Her.”

When asked what he would say if she came back:

Jake answered:

> **“Hi, Mom.”**

When asked whether he would be angry:

Jake looked confused.

> **Jake:**  
> “Why?”

---

# 9. THE ONE-SIDED ATTACHMENT

C.O.R.E. has confirmed through restricted historical records that Jake's mother did not share the attachment Jake continues to hold.

She described him as:

> **“A problematic and accidental child.”**

She openly stated that she hated him.

Jake is unaware of these statements.

This information remains restricted because Jake does not psychologically accept the possibility that his mother did not love him.

For him, the relationship remains real.

For his mother, it apparently did not carry the same meaning.

This asymmetry is one of the most painful aspects of Jake's current existence.

---

# 10. HAPPINESS

Jake does occasionally display brief positive reactions.

Examples include:

- discovering something interesting;
    
- hearing Nora tell him a story;
    
- looking at the Bullseye;
    
- asking Aeterna questions;
    
- seeing something unusual;
    
- hearing something funny;
    
- receiving food despite being unable to eat it.
    

These moments are genuine.

They are also brief.

C.O.R.E. has not observed sustained happiness comparable to his persistent sadness.

A typical pattern is:

> **Curiosity → brief interest → distraction → memory → sadness.**

This cycle may repeat multiple times during a single manifestation.

---

# 11. AETERNA'S OBSERVATION

Aeterna has occasionally accompanied Nora during Jake's manifestation period.

She has observed the same pattern.

Jake can become interested in something.

Then his expression changes.

Then he becomes quiet.

Then he begins thinking about his mother.

Aeterna described it simply:

> **“His happiness has nowhere to stay.”**

No further interpretation was provided.

---

# 12. STAR'S ASSESSMENT

Star remains concerned about Jake's condition as a Weeping Angel.

She explained that the defining characteristic is not simply crying.

It is the inability to escape the sorrow that caused the angel's spiritual deterioration.

Jake's wings are gone.

His connection to the Starlands is gone.

His path home is gone.

His emotional attachment to the past, however, remains.

Star stated:

> **“He keeps reaching backward because he can't go forward.”**

---

# 13. WHY NORA IS NOT ENOUGH

This distinction has been emphasized in Jake's welfare records.

Nora has done nothing wrong.

She is providing something Jake desperately needs:

**Someone who stays.**

But Jake's suffering predates their friendship by more than seventy-five years.

His pain is not caused by the absence of Nora.

His pain is rooted in:

- starvation;
    
- abandonment;
    
- death;
    
- isolation;
    
- unresolved maternal attachment;
    
- loss of his wings;
    
- loss of access to the Starlands;
    
- and decades spent wandering.
    

Nora cannot undo these things.

She can only walk beside him through them.

---

# 14. CURRENT NIGHTLY PATTERN

Jake's typical manifestation now follows a recognizable pattern.

**02:00 — Manifestation**

Jake appears.

**02:00–03:00 — Wandering**

He searches the Blacksite.

**03:00–04:00 — Food-seeking**

He frequently visits the cafeteria or Tachyon Merch Store.

**04:00–05:00 — Increased emotional distress**

Jake becomes quieter.

References to his mother become more frequent.

He may sit somewhere alone.

Nora generally remains nearby.

Aeterna occasionally joins.

**05:00 — Disappearance**

Jake ceases to manifest.

---

# 15. PERSONNEL GUIDANCE

Personnel who encounter Jake should not attempt to force happiness upon him.

Do not say:

> “Cheer up.”

Do not say:

> “You have Nora now.”

Do not say:

> “You should be happy you're not alone.”

Do not tell him:

> “Your mother wasn't worth missing.”

Do not attempt to replace his mother.

Jake's grief cannot be argued away.

Instead:

Sit with him.

Listen.

Allow silence.

Offer company.

Offer food alternatives where possible.

If he talks about his mother, let him talk.

---

# 16. FINAL OBSERVATION

At 04:48, Jake was sitting outside the cafeteria.

Nora was beside him.

Aeterna stood several meters away.

Jake had been given another piece of cake.

He knew he could not eat it.

He held it anyway.

After several minutes, he whispered:

> **Jake:**  
> “I wish Mom was here.”

Nora looked at him.

She did not correct him.

She did not tell him what C.O.R.E. knew.

She did not tell him that his mother had hated him.

She simply remained beside him.

Jake looked at the cake.

Then toward the cafeteria.

Then down at the floor.

He was hungry.

He was sad.

He missed his mother.

And nothing about the presence of his friends could completely change that.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake is not waiting for a solution.

He does not know there is no solution.

He is waiting for his mother.

He has been waiting for more than seventy-five years.

He still loves her.

He still misses her.

He still believes she will return.

And the cruelest part is that the person he remembers is not the person who actually wanted him.

But Jake cannot accept that.

Perhaps his mind shattered because accepting it would have meant losing his mother twice:

**once when she left,**

and again when he finally understood why.

So he keeps her.

The mother in his memories.

The mother who loved him.

The mother who would come back.

The mother who, in reality, never wanted him.

Every night, between **02:00 and 05:00**, Jake wanders.

Nora walks beside him.

Sometimes Aeterna joins them.

He is rarely happy.

He is almost always hungry.

He is almost always sad.

And somewhere beneath everything his broken mind still carries,

a six-year-old boy is still waiting for his mother to come home.

**She isn't coming.**

**Jake does not know that.**

And C.O.R.E. will not be the ones to tell him.

**— END DOCUMENT**
