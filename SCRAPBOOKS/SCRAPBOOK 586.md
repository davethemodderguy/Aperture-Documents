# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **MEDICAL / SECURITY INCIDENT REPORT**

# **CORIN — MERCURY EJECTION & FOOD CONTAMINATION INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Condition:** **Recovering / Severely Weak**  
**Mercury Status:** **Successfully Ejected**  
**Secondary Incident:** **Food Contamination**  
**Status:** Under Investigation

---

# **MEDICAL BREAKTHROUGH**

Corin has successfully eliminated the mercury from her body.

C.O.R.E. medical monitoring confirmed that no significant mercury remains within her system.

Her regenerative factor ultimately forced the remaining contamination out.

The prolonged regenerative cycle has therefore ended.

For the first time since the poisoning began, Corin's body is no longer fighting the toxin.

---

# **RECOVERY**

Despite the successful removal of the mercury, Corin remains **extremely weak**.

The damage-repair process consumed an enormous amount of her body's available energy.

Her organs are functioning.

Her Ether is stable.

Her regeneration is no longer continuously repairing mercury-induced damage.

But Corin's physical reserves have not yet recovered.

As a result:

> **Corin cannot currently return to her normal form.**

She remains in her miniature feline form.

---

# **CURRENT CONDITION**

Corin spends most of her time resting.

Her wings remain folded around her body.

She still drinks from her cup.

Small meals remain preferable.

She can be held without difficulty and continues to tolerate gentle contact from SAFEHOUSE residents.

Her strength is expected to return gradually.

---

# **THE FOOD CONTAMINATION INCIDENT**

While Corin was eating alone, her food was accidentally contaminated by a researcher.

The researcher did not intend to harm Corin.

The contamination was discovered after the incident and immediately reported.

Because Corin had only recently survived mercury poisoning, the event triggered an immediate emergency response.

Her remaining food was removed.

The container and surrounding area were secured.

Medical personnel examined Corin immediately.

---

# **INVESTIGATION**

The researcher responsible has been identified.

The incident is currently being treated as an **accidental contamination event**, rather than a confirmed deliberate poisoning.

The exact circumstances surrounding how the contaminant entered Corin's food are under investigation.

C.O.R.E. has initiated a review of:

- Food preparation procedures.
    
- Researcher access to SAFEHOUSE supplies.
    
- Storage practices.
    
- Contamination safeguards.
    
- Personnel handling procedures.
    

No conclusions regarding intent have been made.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The residents were understandably alarmed.

Corin had only just escaped one prolonged poisoning.

Nobody wanted another medical crisis.

Belle immediately took responsibility for making sure Corin was monitored.

Orphie stayed beside her.

Neko began checking anything Corin might eat.

The SAFEHOUSE kitchen was temporarily placed under additional supervision.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin was too weak to understand the entire situation.

She simply remained curled up beneath her wings.

When Belle picked her up, Corin rested against her hands.

No meow came.

She was still unable to vocalize.

Her only response was a tiny movement of her ears.

---

# **SECURITY DIRECTIVE**

Until Corin has fully recovered, all food intended for her is to be:

**Prepared separately.**

**Verified before serving.**

**Monitored during consumption when possible.**

Any suspected contamination is to be reported immediately.

This measure is precautionary and is not intended to imply deliberate action by the researcher involved in the incident.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The mercury is gone.

That alone is a major victory.

But Corin's body has spent a considerable amount of time fighting a toxin, repairing itself over and over again.

She needs time now.

Time to rebuild her strength.

Time to recover.

Time before she can become the normal-sized Corin again.

Unfortunately, almost immediately after the mercury was successfully ejected, another food contamination incident occurred.

This time, it was accidental.

Still, after everything Corin has already endured, C.O.R.E. has decided that **"accidental" is no longer an acceptable standard for food safety.**

For now, the tiny white winged cat remains under close care.

Her poison is gone.

Her recovery has begun.

**End of Medical / Security Incident Report**
