# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **VISITOR ARRIVAL LOG**

### **SPECIAL VISIT – LUCY MERRYWEATHER, WARDEN OF THE BANLANDS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Internal Archive

---

# **VISITOR IDENTIFICATION**

**Name:** Lucy Merryweather

**Title:** Warden of the Banlands

**Affiliation:** Aperture Industries

After an extended absence due to her duties overseeing the Banlands, Lucy Merryweather arrived at Heaven's Blacksite for a long-awaited family visit.

Although many residents already knew her by reputation, this would be the first time several of the newer members met her in person.

---

# **ARRIVAL**

The dimensional gateway opened with its familiar hum.

Lucy stepped through carrying little more than a travel bag and her sword.

Before anyone could speak—

Belle practically launched herself toward her.

> "Mom!"

Lucy caught her in a hug with practiced ease.

> "Hello, sweetheart."

Endmin followed at a calmer pace.

Lucy smiled warmly.

> "Good to see you too."

The SAFEHOUSE common room immediately felt livelier.

---

# **MEETING THE NEW ARRIVALS**

## **Kent (Keith)**

Kent offered a respectful handshake.

> "It's an honor to finally meet you, Warden."

Lucy returned it firmly.

> "Belle speaks highly of you."

Kent glanced at Belle.

> "...Hopefully only the good parts."

Belle nodded enthusiastically.

> "Mostly."

---

## **Remielle (Cinder)**

Cinder stood unusually straight.

> "Ma'am."

Lucy studied her for a moment before smiling.

> "Belle trusts you."

> "That's enough for me."

Cinder visibly relaxed.

---

## **Norma Lockwood**

Lucy approached Norma with a gentle smile.

> "So..."

> "You're the engineer I've heard so much about."

Norma laughed nervously.

> "...I hope it wasn't all from Neko."

> "Thankfully, no."

The two quickly found themselves discussing research, engineering, and Belle's habit of taking on too much work.

---

## **Arcturus Lockwood**

Arcturus...

Completely froze.

Belle noticed immediately.

> "...Arcturus?"

Norma quietly whispered.

> "...He's a fan."

Lucy blinked.

> "...A fan?"

Arcturus removed his blue fedora out of pure respect.

For several seconds...

Nothing came out.

Finally—

> "...W-Warden Merryweather."

> "...It's... genuinely an honor."

Neko quietly leaned toward Kent.

> "...The legendary Gunslinger has been stunned."

Kent nodded.

> "...Didn't think that was possible."

---

# **THE CONVERSATION**

As everyone settled around the SAFEHOUSE table with tea and snacks, Lucy thanked Director Lucious for his continued support of Aperture Industries.

Arcturus looked up.

> "...Support?"

Lucious smiled.

> "We've simply helped where we could."

Belle tilted her head.

> "...You didn't know?"

Arcturus looked between everyone in the room.

Norma quietly shook her head.

Apparently...

She had never mentioned the full story.

---

# **THE REVELATION**

Lucy calmly explained.

Years earlier...

Endmin had suffered from blindness.

Through Aperture Industries' research and Director Lucious' resources, treatment was developed that restored her sight.

Later...

Norma's Ether Regression Syndrome, a disease she had endured for fifteen years, was cured through the combined efforts of Belle, the CCD, and Aperture's research teams into stabilized Ether.

The room grew quiet.

Arcturus slowly looked toward his sister.

Then toward Endmin.

Then finally toward Director Lucious.

---

He stood.

Walked over.

And offered Lucious a firm handshake.

> "...Thank you."

Lucious looked slightly surprised.

> "For what?"

Arcturus answered without hesitation.

> "For giving my sister another chance."

He paused.

> "And for giving Endmin hers."

Lucious returned the handshake.

> "They earned those chances themselves."

# **FINAL ENTRY**

As evening settled over Heaven's Blacksite, Lucy looked around the SAFEHOUSE common room.

Belle was laughing with Norma.

Endmin was speaking with Kent.

Neko was already trying to convince Arcturus to "just consider one tiny modification."

Arcturus was, as expected, saying no.

For the first time in months, Lucy allowed herself to simply enjoy the moment.

Her daughters had found more than colleagues.

They had found a family.

And seeing them surrounded by people willing to fight, laugh, and care for one another made the long journey from the Banlands more than worthwhile.
