# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **INTERNAL DISCIPLINARY INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: HARASSMENT OF CORIN LOCKWOOD BY RESEARCH PERSONNEL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

A newly assigned researcher, unfamiliar with Corin Lockwood's personnel file and psychological history, engaged in repeated verbal harassment while Corin was passing through one of the Blacksite's research corridors.

The researcher later stated that they believed Corin was "just another unusual employee" and did not realize the seriousness of the remarks.

The incident resulted in immediate disciplinary action.

---

# **INCIDENT**

Witnesses reported the researcher making several comments about Corin's appearance.

Among the recorded remarks were:

> "You look like a corpse."

> "Did someone forget to bury you?"

> "Are you even human?"

Corin stopped walking.

She lowered her head.

She did not argue.

She did not defend herself.

After several seconds, she quietly replied,

> "...I'm sorry."

She then turned around and walked away.

Several witnesses noted that Corin's hands were visibly shaking.

---

# **IMMEDIATE RESPONSE**

Mart, who had been returning from the library, witnessed the exchange from the opposite end of the corridor.

He immediately followed Corin rather than confronting the researcher.

His priority was ensuring Corin was not left alone.

Belle arrived shortly afterward.

She found Corin sitting on the floor of a quiet hallway, hugging her knees.

Without asking questions, Belle sat beside her.

Corin leaned against Belle's shoulder.

Neither spoke for several minutes.

---

# **DISCIPLINARY ACTION**

The researcher was escorted to Human Resources pending investigation.

During questioning, they admitted making the remarks but maintained they had intended them as jokes.

They further stated they were unaware of Corin's history.

Human Resources concluded that ignorance of a colleague's history did not excuse workplace harassment.

Mandatory disciplinary measures were issued, including formal sanctions and required professional conduct training.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi later found Corin in SAFEHOUSE.

Corin quietly asked,

> "...Was he right?"

Lumi immediately answered.

> "No."

Corin continued staring at the floor.

> "...People keep saying those things."

Lumi knelt in front of her daughter.

> "People can be wrong."

She gently held Corin's hands.

> "What happened to you does not decide your worth."

Corin didn't answer.

But she didn't pull away.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin recovered from the immediate distress with support from SAFEHOUSE residents, though Medical Services noted a temporary increase in anxiety and self-isolation over the following days.

The incident prompted C.O.R.E. to reinforce staff training on respectful workplace conduct and the importance of treating every colleague with dignity, regardless of their appearance or medical condition.
