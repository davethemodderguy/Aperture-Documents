# 1. OVERVIEW

Josh Newman, MSAC **Overwatch**, codename **Snake**, has begun making extensive use of his newly awarded:

> **FSR DEADBOLT — FSR-001**

His enthusiasm is considered understandable.

His decision to immediately experiment with the **unsuppressed configuration** is considered significantly less understandable.

---

# 2. FIRST IMPRESSION

Snake removed the Deadbolt from its presentation case and inspected it for several minutes.

He examined the rifle.

Checked the magazines.

Looked over the certificate.

Read Neri's letter again.

Then he noticed the alternate barrel configuration.

> **SNAKE:**  
> “It has an unsuppressed option?”

Neri nodded.

> **SNAKE:**  
> “How loud?”

Neri paused.

> **NERI:**  
> “Very.”

Snake apparently interpreted this as:

> **“Probably loud.”**

This interpretation was incorrect.

---

# 3. UNSUPPRESSED TEST

The first controlled test of the unsuppressed Deadbolt was conducted at the designated range.

The first report was described by witnesses as:

> **“Obnoxious.”**

The second description was:

> **“EXTREMELY obnoxious.”**

The third was:

> **“WHY DID WE LET HIM DO THAT?”**

The underlying reason is straightforward.

The FSR Deadbolt fires the enormous:

> **.458 Tachyon Magnum**

The cartridge produces a report substantially louder than the suppressed Deadbolt configuration.

The combination of the large cartridge and unsuppressed configuration results in an exceptionally loud firing signature.

---

# 4. RANGE OBSERVATIONS

Personnel outside the immediate firing area reported hearing the shot.

One MSAC operator several sections away reportedly stopped walking.

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “What was that?”

Another replied:

> **“Snake.”**

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “Of course it was.”

A cafeteria worker subsequently reported hearing the shot.

The worker looked toward the ceiling.

> **CAFETERIA WORKER:**  
> “Was that the armory?”

The answer was:

> **“Range.”**

The worker nodded.

> **“Oh. Deadbolt.”**

---

# 5. SNAKE'S REACTION

Snake removed his hearing protection after the controlled test.

He stared at the rifle.

Then at the range.

Then back at the rifle.

> **SNAKE:**  
> “That's loud.”

Neri, standing nearby, nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “I said very.”

Snake looked at the ammunition.

> **SNAKE:**  
> “It's because of the .458?”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Massive cartridge.”

Snake smiled.

> **SNAKE:**  
> “I like it.”

Neri stared at him.

> **NERI:**  
> “Of course you do.”

---

# 6. SUPPRESSED CONFIGURATION

Snake subsequently tested the suppressed configuration.

The difference was immediately apparent.

The firing signature was substantially reduced compared with the unsuppressed configuration.

Snake looked genuinely impressed.

> **SNAKE:**  
> “That's much better.”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “That's why it has a suppressor.”

Snake looked at the unsuppressed barrel.

Then the suppressor.

Then the rifle.

> **SNAKE:**  
> “But the loud one is fun.”

Neri:

> **“...I know.”**

---

# 7. THE CAFETERIA INCIDENT

Later that day, Snake entered the cafeteria carrying the Deadbolt case.

Several residents immediately recognized it.

Nora pointed.

> **NORA:**  
> “SNAKE!”

Snake opened the case slightly.

Nora immediately became excited.

> **NORA:**  
> “YOU GOT IT!”

Snake nodded.

> **SNAKE:**  
> “First one.”

Nora looked inside.

> **NORA:**  
> “Did you shoot the target?”

> **SNAKE:**  
> “Yep.”

Nora looked impressed.

> **NORA:**  
> “Twenty-five kilometers!”

Neri was drinking coffee nearby.

She quietly added:

> **NERI:**  
> “He did.”

Nora immediately yelled:

> **“MY SISTER MADE A RIFLE AND SNAKE GOT ONE!”**

Neri lowered her head.

---

# 8. NERI'S ASSESSMENT

Neri was asked whether she was satisfied with Snake's use of the Deadbolt.

She replied:

> **“He's having fun.”**

Pause.

> **“That's okay.”**

When asked about the unsuppressed configuration:

> **“It's loud.”**

> **“It's .458.”**

When asked whether she expected someone to complain about the noise:

Neri looked toward the range.

> **“Probably.”**

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Snake wanted the Deadbolt.

He earned the Deadbolt.

He opened the case.

He admired the Deadbolt.

He tested the Deadbolt.

Then he discovered something important.

The FSR Deadbolt's suppressed configuration is remarkably restrained.

The unsuppressed configuration, however, has absolutely no interest in being subtle.

The reason is sitting inside the rifle:

> **.458 Tachyon Magnum.**

It is a massive cartridge.

And when the Deadbolt is fired without suppression, the resulting report is correspondingly enormous.

Snake's response?

> **“I like it.”**

Neri's response?

> **“Of course you do.”**

FSR-001 is therefore considered to be in excellent hands.

And probably very loud hands.
