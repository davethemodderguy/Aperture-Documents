# 10. OBSERVER ASSESSMENT

The repeated failures have revealed a consistent pattern.

Jane does not rely on a single interrogation technique.

She changes tactics according to the participant.

If the participant talks:

**Jane listens.**

If the participant refuses to talk:

**Jane waits.**

If the participant becomes defensive:

**Jane becomes patient.**

If the participant becomes aggressive:

**Jane becomes calmer.**

If the participant tries to manipulate her:

**Jane allows them to try.**

If the participant attempts silence:

**Jane becomes quieter.**

If the participant tries to reverse the interrogation:

**Jane lets them speak until they expose their own weakness.**

And if the participant eventually surrenders:

Jane simply accepts the surrender.

She does not celebrate.

She does not gloat.

She does not need to.
