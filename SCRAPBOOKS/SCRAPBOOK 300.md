# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – SAFETY BULLETIN**

### **SUBJECT: MARTS IN CUPS & HANDLING PROCEDURES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – All Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

A recurring sight throughout Heaven's Blacksite is a Mart happily sitting inside a mug, cup, bowl, watering can, or other small container.

This behavior is entirely normal.

Marts enjoy enclosed spaces, particularly containers that allow them to keep cool while observing nearby personnel.

**This does not make them a beverage.**

---

# **DO NOT DRINK THE MARTS**

Personnel are reminded that a Mart is:

- A living Ether-sustained organism.
    
- Composed of magically stabilized water.
    
- Internally maintained at temperatures comparable to **liquid nitrogen**.
    

Although their containment field prevents this cold from affecting the outside world under normal circumstances, ingesting a Mart would expose a person to catastrophic internal cold.

Possible consequences include:

- Severe frostbite to the mouth and throat.
    
- Cryogenic tissue damage.
    
- Airway injury.
    
- Internal organ damage.
    

Medical Division has emphasized that this constitutes a life-threatening emergency.

---

# **CONTAINMENT FIELD FAILURE**

Under normal conditions, a Mart's magical containment field safely isolates its internal temperature.

If that field were to collapse suddenly while the Mart was in direct contact with a person, the rapid release of cryogenic energy could cause severe localized frostbite.

Fortunately, spontaneous containment failures are exceptionally rare.

Research has recorded no such events during ordinary daily activity.

Personnel should nevertheless avoid rough handling that might interfere with a Mart during self-maintenance or other vulnerable states.

---

# **COMMON MISUNDERSTANDING**

A typical incident proceeds as follows:

1. A Mart climbs into someone's coffee mug.
    
2. The employee becomes distracted.
    
3. The employee nearly drinks the Mart.
    
4. The Mart happily says:
    

> "Hello!"

5. The employee experiences immediate panic.
    
6. The Mart is gently removed and placed somewhere safe.
    

Research estimates this has occurred **far more often than anyone wishes to admit**.

# **LUMI'S GREATEST ACHIEVEMENT**

When Lumi first attempted to recreate the lost arts of the Ice Mages, she hoped to preserve an ancient magical tradition.

Instead...

She accidentally created an entirely new species.

A species that asks for nothing.

Hurts no one.

Cleans the halls.

Comforts the lonely.

Cools overheated machines.

Helps complete strangers.

And spends its brief life trying to make the world around it just a little happier.

When asked whether she still considered the failed experiment a disappointment, Lumi looked across the courtyard at hundreds of blue blobs bouncing happily through the snow.

She smiled.

> "...I was trying to bring back old magic."

A Mart climbed into her hands.

> "...Instead..."

> "...I made friends."

Among the many accomplishments of the last Ice Mage...

The Marts are now universally regarded as her greatest.

Not because they are the most powerful.

But because they are the kindest.
