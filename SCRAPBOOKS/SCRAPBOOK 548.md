### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / COMBAT CAPABILITY REPORT**

# **CORIN — DUAL-PURPOSE WING FEATHERS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Capability:** **Multifunctional Feather Deployment**  
**Status:** Operational

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's wing feathers possess two distinct offensive applications.

The feathers naturally vary in shape and sharpness across the wings, allowing Corin to utilize them according to their position and intended purpose.

The two confirmed applications are:

1. **Thrown Feather Blades**
    
2. **Airburst Ether-Infused Projectiles**
    

These functions appear to be intrinsic properties of Corin's anomalous physiology.

---

# **1. FEATHER BLADES**

The feathers closest to the outer edges of Corin's wings become exceptionally sharp.

Their edges are comparable to knives.

Corin can deliberately detach and throw these feathers.

Once separated, they retain their rigidity and sharpness, allowing them to function as **throwing blades**.

Unlike her Ether-infused projectiles, these feathers do not require an Ether payload to remain dangerous.

They are simply exceptionally sharp pieces of her own wing.

---

# **DEPLOYMENT**

Corin can remove an edge feather deliberately and throw it.

She can perform this with either hand or directly through wing movement.

Her control over the wings allows her to select individual feathers rather than indiscriminately shedding them.

Detached feathers eventually become dormant unless Corin deliberately activates them for their anomalous projectile function.

---

# **2. AIRBURST ETHER-INFUSED PROJECTILES**

Corin can also launch feathers that have been charged with Ether.

When activated, these feathers function as **airburst projectiles**.

Rather than simply striking a target, the projectile releases its stored Ether payload upon activation or impact, producing a localized burst.

This capability is separate from the physical sharpness of the feather.

A feather can therefore be:

- Used as a physical blade.
    
- Charged with Ether.
    
- Launched as an Ether projectile.
    

---

# **WING MOVEMENT**

Corin launches Ether-infused feathers through a rapid flick of her wings.

The motion provides the initial acceleration.

A single forceful wing movement can release a feather at considerable speed.

This allows Corin to deploy multiple projectiles without manually removing every feather.

---

# **COMBAT FLEXIBILITY**

The two functions give Corin considerable flexibility.

### **Feather Blade**

Best suited for:

- Precision throwing.
    
- Silent deployment.
    
- Physical targets.
    
- Situations where an Ether discharge is undesirable.
    

### **Ether-Infused Feather**

Best suited for:

- Area denial.
    
- Ranged attacks.
    
- Airburst effects.
    
- Targets requiring an anomalous payload.
    

Corin can therefore choose between **physical precision** and **Ether-based explosive force**.

---

# **FEATHER REGENERATION**

The use of feathers does not permanently reduce Corin's wing capacity.

Detached feathers naturally regenerate.

Her Ether-enhanced biological regeneration allows the wings to replace lost feathers considerably faster than ordinary biological growth would permit.

This makes feather deployment a renewable capability.

---

# **NEKONIUM ARMOR INTERACTION**

The Nekonium nano-forcefield wing armor remains compatible with both functions.

The armor automatically accommodates feather deployment.

When a feather is launched, the protective field adjusts around the newly created gap.

No manual adjustment is necessary.

---

# **CORIN'S CONTROL**

Corin possesses unusually precise control over her feathers.

She can distinguish between:

- Dormant feathers.
    
- Blade feathers.
    
- Ether-infused feathers.
    

She can also prevent a feather from activating accidentally.

This is particularly important when Corin is around other SAFEHOUSE residents.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RULE**

A simple rule has been established:

> **Do not touch Corin's wings without permission.**

This applies regardless of whether the feathers are currently charged.

The sharp edges alone are sufficient reason.

---

During a controlled test, Corin selected an outer feather and threw it at a designated target.

The feather struck cleanly.

She then selected another feather, charged it with Ether, and launched it using a sharp wing flick.

The resulting airburst demonstrated the significant difference between the two applications.

Corin looked at the two impact sites.
