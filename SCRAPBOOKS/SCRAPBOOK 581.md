# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE HEALTH OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — THE QUIET KITTEN**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Form:** Miniature Feline  
**Status:** Ill / Resting

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin spent the entire day in her miniature feline form.

She was visibly unwell.

Normally, even in her tiny form, Corin is energetic.

She flies around.

She plays with yarn.

She nibbles people's fingers.

She follows residents around.

Today, none of that happened.

There was no Corin running around SAFEHOUSE.

No talking.

No cooking.

No flying through the halls.

No playful backflips.

There was simply:

> **A tiny white cat with wings.**

---

# **BEHAVIOR**

Corin spent most of the day sitting quietly.

Her ears remained lowered.

Her two tails stayed close to her body.

Her wings were folded around herself, forming a small feathery enclosure.

Occasionally, she would shift position.

Then she would settle down again.

She did not appear interested in food.

She barely reacted to toys.

Even yarn failed to get much of a response.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The residents quickly noticed that something was wrong.

Belle approached first.

She carefully picked Corin up.

Corin did not resist.

She simply curled into Belle's hands.

Belle gently stroked her fur.

> **"You don't feel good, huh?"**

Corin gave a quiet:

> **"...mew."**

Belle immediately became worried.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie spent much of the day nearby.

She did not attempt to force Corin back into her normal form.

Instead, she simply stayed close.

Corin eventually crawled onto Orphie's lap.

Her wings wrapped around her tiny body.

Orphie covered her with a blanket.

For once, Corin did not move away.

---

# **THE WINGS**

Even while sick, Corin's wings continued functioning normally.

However, she kept them folded most of the time.

The feathers were slightly puffed up, giving her the appearance of an unusually fluffy kitten.

Her wings occasionally twitched.

Otherwise, she remained still.

---

# **MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

C.O.R.E. medical personnel monitored Corin throughout the day.

No immediate life-threatening abnormality was detected.

Her Ether remained stable.

Her heartbeat and breathing were monitored continuously.

The primary recommendation was simple:

> **Rest.**

No unnecessary activity.

No flight.

No training.

No feather testing.

No backflips.

---

# **THE AFTERNOON**

Corin eventually fell asleep.

She remained curled into a tiny ball.

Her wings completely covered her.

Only her face, ears, and the tips of her two tails remained visible.

SAFEHOUSE became unusually quiet around her.

Nobody wanted to wake her.

---

# **EVENING**

Corin remained asleep for several hours.

When she briefly woke, she looked around the room.

Belle was nearby.

Orphie was nearby.

Neko was sitting across the room.

Everyone was there.

Corin quietly relaxed again.

Her wings closed around herself.

Then she went back to sleep.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

There are days when SAFEHOUSE is filled with noise.

Cooking.

Laughter.

Arguments.

Games.

Flying feathers.

Today was not one of those days.

Today, everyone simply let Corin rest.

There was no energetic Cat Thiren running around.

No chef.

No aerial acrobat.

No feather-wielding anomaly.

Just a tiny white kitten with wings, curled up somewhere warm, waiting until she felt like herself again.

**End of SAFEHOUSE Health Observation**
