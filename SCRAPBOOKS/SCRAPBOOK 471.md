# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL CONDUCT / INCIDENT REPORT**

# **THE SCARF INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Personnel Involved:** Junior Researcher ████████  
**Location:** Research Sector  
**Status:** **RESOLVED**

---

# **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

A junior researcher made what they apparently considered a harmless joke toward Corin.

It was not harmless.

The researcher approached Corin and deliberately removed both her **scarf** and **white hat** without permission.

They proceeded to tease her about her appearance and scars.

The researcher was unaware of the significance of the clothing.

Corin was not.

---

# **THE INCIDENT**

The researcher pulled Corin's scarf away.

> **Researcher:**  
> "What's with the scarf all the time?"

Corin immediately reached for it.

> **Corin:**  
> "...Give it back."

Instead, the researcher laughed and removed her hat as well.

> **Researcher:**  
> "And this too?"

The researcher looked at the scars on Corin's head and neck.

> **Researcher:**  
> "You hide all that?"

Corin froze.

Her expression changed.

> **Corin:**  
> "...Stop."

The researcher continued joking.

> **Researcher:**  
> "Come on, I'm just messing with you."

Corin backed away.

> **"Give them back."**

---

# **PANIC RESPONSE**

The situation escalated rapidly.

Corin's Ether began fluctuating.

Her hands shook.

Her posture became defensive.

Her eyes became increasingly distant.

The researcher finally realized that something was seriously wrong.

> **Researcher:**  
> "...Corin?"

Corin could no longer properly distinguish the laboratory around her from the memories being triggered.

The exposed scars became the focus of her attention.

The scarf was gone.

The hat was gone.

She felt exposed.

And the old memories returned.

The laboratory.

The procedures.

The people looking at her body.

The feeling of being examined.

The feeling of having no control.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT INTERVENES**

A soft blue glow appeared in the room.

Guiding Light manifested immediately.

Her expression was unusually cold.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Put them back."

The researcher froze.

> **Researcher:**  
> "I—I was only joking—"

> **Guiding Light:**  
> **"Put. Them. Back."**

The researcher immediately complied.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin had already become highly unstable.

She clutched the scarf against her neck.

Her Ether continued fluctuating.

Guiding Light approached slowly.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Corin."

No response.

> **"You're here."**

Corin's eyes remained fixed on the floor.

> **"You're in the Blacksite."**

A pause.

> **"Nobody is experimenting on you."**

Corin's breathing gradually slowed.

> **"Nobody is taking you anywhere."**

Her Ether began stabilizing.

Guiding Light remained beside her until the panic subsided.

---

# **THE RESEARCHER**

The junior researcher was immediately removed from the area.

Their explanation:

> **"I didn't know."**

Guiding Light's response was simple.

> **"Then you should have asked."**

The researcher received disciplinary action for:

- Unauthorized physical contact.
    
- Removing personal belongings without consent.
    
- Harassment.
    
- Improper treatment of a vulnerable resident.
    
- Failure to follow personnel conduct requirements.
    

---

# **NEW PERSONNEL DIRECTIVE**

A new directive was issued following the incident.

### **CORIN'S PERSONAL CLOTHING IS NOT TO BE REMOVED WITHOUT HER EXPLICIT CONSENT.**

This includes:

- Scarf.
    
- Hat.
    
- Hair accessories.
    
- Other personal items.
    

Personnel are additionally prohibited from deliberately drawing attention to Corin's scars.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Later that evening, Corin sat quietly in the treehouse.

Her scarf was back around her neck.

Her hat was back on her head.

Orphie sat nearby.

She noticed the blue-glowing hairclip in Orphie's hair.

Neither spoke about the incident.

After a while, Corin quietly said:

> **"...I hate being looked at like that."**

Orphie nodded.

> **"I know."**

That was all.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S PRIVATE NOTE**

> **"Her scars are not entertainment."**
> 
> **"Her body is not public property."**
> 
> **"And her trauma is not a joke."**

A final sentence followed:

> **"If someone wants to understand Corin, they can start by asking permission."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The junior researcher intended to make a joke.

Instead, they accidentally recreated a situation that Corin's mind associated with some of the worst moments of her life.

For Corin, removing the scarf and hat was not simply exposing scars.

It was removing the small amount of control she had over who saw them.

The incident has been resolved.

But the lesson remains:

**A person's boundaries do not become less important because someone else doesn't understand why they exist.**

**End of Personnel Conduct / Incident Report**
