**INCIDENT TITLE:** _The Ghost Carrying His Own Body_

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately 21:40, Tom Akana was observed carrying a sleeping Jake Akana through the Blacksite.

The scene was, in isolation, relatively harmless.

Tom was a ghostly child. Jake was asleep in his arms. Both appeared physically comfortable, and Tom showed no apparent difficulty carrying him.

Unfortunately, the sight of a ghost carrying the apparently lifeless body of the very person he resembled proved considerably less harmless to the mental well-being of nearby personnel.

The first employee who encountered them dropped a tablet.

The second screamed.

The third initiated an unnecessary emergency notification before realizing that the ghost was walking calmly through a brightly lit corridor, holding the sleeping child with extraordinary care.

Within approximately fifteen minutes, the incident had spread through the residential wing by word of mouth. Descriptions varied from _a ghost carrying a dead child_ to _Jake has died again_ to _I am not paid enough to understand what I just saw._

Tom, for his part, appeared to regard the entire situation as an ordinary evening walk.

Jake remained asleep.

**No injuries, physical damage, or confirmed anomalous disturbances resulted from the incident. Several employees may require additional time to recover their composure.**

C.O.R.E. has determined that the incident was an accidental consequence of Tom's appearance and the unusual circumstances of the two manifestations.

Whether Tom realized how frightening the scene looked before the first encounter remains uncertain.

Whether he realized it afterward is considerably less uncertain.

---

## 2. CHRONOLOGY OF EVENTS

### 21:37 — SAFEHOUSE Residential Wing

Jake had fallen asleep after an evening of activity.

Tom was present when he noticed that Jake was no longer responding to attempts to persuade him to move to a more comfortable sleeping location. Rather than wake him, Tom picked him up.

This action was not considered unusual by SAFEHOUSE standards. The two manifestations are known to care for one another, and Tom has demonstrated a practical concern for Jake's comfort and safety.

What made the situation unusual was the resulting visual.

Tom's ghostly form was carrying Jake's embodied form. From a distance, the distinction between the two was not immediately obvious to personnel unfamiliar with their current arrangement.

The sleeping child looked entirely lifeless to an uninformed observer.

Tom looked entirely like a ghost.

Together, they resembled the aftermath of a tragedy that had already happened.

### 21:41 — Administrative Corridor A-3

The first recorded encounter occurred when a records technician rounded a corner and saw Tom approaching.

The technician stopped.

Tom stopped.

Jake slept peacefully against Tom's shoulder.

**TECHNICIAN:** ...

**TOM:** Excuse me.

**TECHNICIAN:** ...

**TOM:** I need to get past you.

The technician screamed, backed into a wall, and dropped a tablet.

Tom paused to avoid stepping on it.

**TOM:** Careful. You dropped that.

The technician screamed again.

Tom continued walking.

### 21:44 — Security Checkpoint

A security guard noticed Tom approaching with Jake in his arms.

The guard immediately reached for the radio.

**GUARD:** Security to Control. I have a visual on—

Tom walked into the light.

**GUARD:** ...Oh.

**TOM:** Evening.

**GUARD:** Is that—

**TOM:** Jake.

**GUARD:** Is he—

**TOM:** Sleeping.

**GUARD:** And you're—

**TOM:** Tom.

**GUARD:** Right. Yes. Of course. Tom.

A long pause followed.

**GUARD:** Why are you carrying him like that?

Tom glanced down at Jake.

**TOM:** Because he's asleep.

**GUARD:** No, I mean—

**TOM:** I know what you mean.

Tom adjusted Jake's position to keep his head supported.

**TOM:** He's comfortable. I'm not waking him up just because everyone else is having a difficult evening.

The guard lowered the radio.

**GUARD:** I'm going to need to write a report.

**TOM:** That's fine.

**GUARD:** I don't know what to put in it.

**TOM:** Try writing what happened.

Tom walked on.

The guard remained at the checkpoint for several seconds, apparently reconsidering his career.

### 21:48 — Residential Lift Lobby

Two SAFEHOUSE residents encountered Tom at the lift lobby.

One recognized him immediately.

The other did not.

The second resident looked at Tom, then at Jake, then back at Tom.

**RESIDENT:** Why are you carrying a dead boy?

**TOM:** He's asleep.

**RESIDENT:** But you're a ghost.

**TOM:** Correct.

**RESIDENT:** And that's your body?

**TOM:** That's Jake.

**RESIDENT:** THAT DOESN'T ANSWER THE QUESTION!

Tom looked at the lift doors.

**TOM:** Are you getting in?

Both residents stepped aside.

**RESIDENT:** No.

**TOM:** All right.

Tom waited for the lift alone.

The doors closed.

One of the residents immediately began explaining the encounter to a third person who had not asked.

### 21:53 — Corridor B-2

By this point, several employees had received conflicting descriptions of the event.

Some believed Jake had died.

Some believed Tom had somehow stolen Jake's body.

One employee had heard that a ghost was carrying a corpse through the Blacksite and had begun searching the internal incident database for the appropriate response protocol.

The actual scene remained unchanged.

Tom continued walking.

Jake continued sleeping.

Tom occasionally adjusted his grip to prevent Jake's head from lolling uncomfortably.

At no point did Tom appear rushed, distressed, or aware that he had become the center of an informal emergency.

### 22:01 — SAFEHOUSE Common Room

Tom eventually reached the common room.

By then, Neko had received three separate reports, Miss Neko had been asked whether the Blacksite was experiencing a new manifestation event, and at least one security employee had prepared to request medical assistance for a child who was, in fact, simply asleep.

Neko arrived to find Tom sitting on the sofa with Jake still in his arms.

**NEKO:** Tom.

**TOM:** Neko.

**NEKO:** Why are you carrying Jake?

**TOM:** He's asleep.

**NEKO:** I gathered that.

**TOM:** Then I think we've established the situation.

Neko stared at him.

Tom stared back.

Jake made a small noise in his sleep and tucked his face closer against Tom.

Tom immediately adjusted the blanket over him.

Neko's expression softened.

Then he remembered the security reports.

**NEKO:** You scared half the residential wing.

**TOM:** I walked down a corridor.

**NEKO:** While carrying a sleeping child who looks exactly like you.

**TOM:** He doesn't look exactly like me.

**NEKO:** Tom.

**TOM:** Fine. Similar enough.

**NEKO:** One of the guards thought Jake had died.

Tom looked down at Jake.

For a moment, his expression became more serious.

**TOM:** He's fine.

**NEKO:** I know. We know. They didn't.

**TOM:** ...Oh.

A pause.

**TOM:** I didn't think about that.

Neko folded his arms.

**NEKO:** I believe you.

**TOM:** I wasn't trying to scare anyone.

**NEKO:** I believe that, too.

**TOM:** Should I wake him up?

Neko looked at Jake, who was sleeping soundly.

**NEKO:** Absolutely not.

Tom nodded.

**TOM:** Good. Because he gets grumpy when people wake him up.

**NEKO:** Then we'll let him sleep.

**TOM:** Thank you.

Neko turned to leave, then stopped.

**NEKO:** Tom?

**TOM:** Yes?

**NEKO:** Next time, perhaps use the better-lit corridors.

Tom glanced toward the brightly illuminated ceiling.

**TOM:** This _was_ the better-lit corridor.

Neko left without answering.

## 3. PERSONNEL IMPACT REPORT

C.O.R.E. conducted a brief review of the reports generated by the incident.

|Category|Finding|
|---|---|
|Physical injuries|None reported|
|Damage to equipment|One dropped tablet; no confirmed damage|
|Medical emergencies|None confirmed|
|Security response|Unnecessary radio notifications|
|Personnel distress|Multiple startled employees|
|Harm to Jake|None observed|
|Harm to Tom|None observed|
|Intentional intimidation|No evidence established|

The incident demonstrates a recurring difficulty in Blacksite operations: personnel may correctly identify an anomalous individual while incorrectly interpreting an otherwise harmless situation.

Tom was not concealing Jake's condition. Jake was not deceased. No evidence suggested that Tom was attempting to imitate a death scene or deliberately frighten the people he encountered.

Nevertheless, the reactions were understandable.

Personnel who see a ghost carrying a sleeping child resembling that ghost are not necessarily expected to respond with perfect composure, particularly when they have not yet fully adjusted to the distinction between Jake and Tom.

The appropriate response is improved familiarity and clear identification protocols, not punishment.

C.O.R.E. also notes that Tom's calm behavior may have inadvertently worsened the initial confusion. To an unfamiliar observer, a ghost carrying a seemingly lifeless child while casually saying _excuse me_ is not reassuring in the way Tom appears to believe it should be.

## 4. FOLLOW-UP INTERVIEW — TOM AKANA

**Interviewer:** Miss Neko  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE Common Room  
**Purpose:** Clarification of the incident and preventive guidance

**MISS NEKO:** Did you know people were frightened?

**TOM:** Not at first.

**MISS NEKO:** When did you realize?

**TOM:** When someone screamed the second time.

**MISS NEKO:** And what did you think?

**TOM:** That they had dropped something.

**MISS NEKO:** They screamed because they saw you carrying Jake.

**TOM:** I understand that now.

**MISS NEKO:** Did you consider how it might look?

**TOM:** I considered that Jake needed to sleep somewhere comfortable.

**MISS NEKO:** And you decided carrying him was the best option?

**TOM:** Yes.

**MISS NEKO:** That's a reasonable decision. But perhaps you could announce yourself before turning a corner.

**TOM:** What would I say?

**MISS NEKO:** Something like, "It's Tom. Jake is asleep. Nobody needs to panic."

Tom considered the suggestion.

**TOM:** That sounds like something a suspicious person would say.

Miss Neko paused.

**MISS NEKO:** I see your point.

**TOM:** Also, if I say "nobody needs to panic," people will probably panic.

**MISS NEKO:** Unfortunately, you may be right.

**TOM:** Could I just carry a sign?

**MISS NEKO:** A sign?

**TOM:** Something that says Jake is sleeping.

**MISS NEKO:** That would be difficult to manage while carrying him.

**TOM:** I could put it on my back.

Miss Neko closed her eyes for a moment.

**MISS NEKO:** We'll consider other options.

**TOM:** All right.

**MISS NEKO:** One more question. Did you enjoy frightening the staff?

**TOM:** No.

**MISS NEKO:** Not even a little?

Tom glanced toward the doorway, where a guard was very obviously pretending not to listen.

**TOM:** ...It was a little funny when they asked why I was carrying myself.

**MISS NEKO:** Tom.

**TOM:** I said a little.

**MISS NEKO:** Thank you for your honesty.

**TOM:** You're welcome.

_Interview concluded._

## 5. JAKE AKANA — POST-INCIDENT RESPONSE

Jake was informed of the incident the following morning after waking.

He listened to Tom's explanation with increasing interest.

**JAKE:** You carried me all the way here?

**TOM:** Yes.

**JAKE:** While I was sleeping?

**TOM:** Yes.

**JAKE:** And everyone screamed?

**TOM:** Several people, yes.

Jake stared at him.

Then he began laughing.

**JAKE:** YOU SCARED EVERYONE BY CARRYING ME?!

**TOM:** I didn't mean to.

**JAKE:** YOU WERE CARRYING YOURSELF!

**TOM:** I was carrying you.

**JAKE:** BUT YOU'RE ME!

**TOM:** That is not how we describe it anymore.

**JAKE:** IT'S STILL FUNNY!

Jake rolled onto his back, laughing so hard that he had to stop to catch his breath.

Tom waited patiently.

**TOM:** Are you done?

**JAKE:** No!

**TOM:** I thought so.

Jake sat up and grinned at him.

**JAKE:** We should do it again!

**TOM:** Absolutely not.

**JAKE:** But imagine everyone's faces!

**TOM:** I already saw their faces. That's why we're not doing it again.

**JAKE:** We could make a sign!

Tom looked at him.

**TOM:** Don't start.

**JAKE:** "DON'T WORRY! I'M JUST CARRYING MYSELF!"

**TOM:** That would make it worse.

**JAKE:** "PLEASE DO NOT PANIC! THE CORPSE IS ASLEEP!"

**TOM:** Jake.

**JAKE:** "GHOST ON DUTY! BODY OFF DUTY!"

Tom pressed a hand over his face.

**TOM:** I'm going to bed.

**JAKE:** You don't sleep!

**TOM:** Then I'm going somewhere quiet.

**JAKE:** CAN I COME?

**TOM:** You can walk.

Jake immediately jumped off the sofa and followed him.

**JAKE:** WAIT FOR ME!

Tom slowed down so Jake could catch up.

## 6. DIRECTIVES AND RECOMMENDATIONS

Following the incident, C.O.R.E. issues the following guidance:

1. Personnel encountering Tom carrying Jake are to verify their condition before initiating an emergency response.
    
2. Tom is encouraged to give unfamiliar personnel a brief verbal explanation when practical, particularly in poorly lit corridors or areas with limited visibility.
    
3. No requirement is imposed for Tom to stop carrying or comforting Jake when Jake is tired or asleep, provided the activity remains safe.
    
4. Jake is not to be encouraged to recreate the incident merely to frighten personnel.
    
5. Personnel are reminded that both manifestations remain children and should not be treated as threats solely because their appearance is unsettling.
    
6. Emergency protocols remain available if genuine signs of injury, distress, or anomalous danger are observed. Familiarity must not become complacency.
    

C.O.R.E. does not recommend issuing Tom a sign.

The proposal was discussed.

It was rejected.

The proposed wording was deemed likely to increase panic rather than reduce it.

## 7. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Tom did not set out to frighten anyone.

He wanted Jake to sleep comfortably, and he carried him.

That was all.

The problem was that the Blacksite contains a ghostly child who can carry his embodied counterpart, and the resulting image is considerably more alarming than either of them appears to appreciate.

Tom's actions were protective. Jake's reaction was delighted. The staff's reactions were understandable.

Nobody was harmed.

The incident is therefore closed, pending any further observations.

One final note has been added to the file: Tom's tendency to anticipate physical danger does not necessarily extend to anticipating how other people will interpret his behavior.

He can recognize a fall before Jake takes it.

He can recognize an unsafe game before it begins.

He can carry a sleeping child without waking him.

But apparently, he cannot reliably predict that walking through a corridor as a ghost carrying a sleeping version of himself will frighten the people who see him.

This is not considered a major deficiency.

It is, however, something C.O.R.E. would prefer not to test again.

### C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Tom carried Jake because Jake was asleep.

He held him carefully, supported his head, and made sure he was comfortable.

That is the part we want to remember.

The screaming, the dropped tablet, the confused security reports, and the employee who apparently considered filing a missing-body complaint are all secondary details.

A ghost carried his little brother home.

The Blacksite saw a horror story.

Jake woke up and heard the story of his life.

And Tom, who had spent the entire journey making sure Jake didn't fall, discovered that the most dangerous thing he had done all evening was walk around a corner without warning anyone.

For the record, Jake has already suggested doing it again.

Tom has already refused.

**One of these boys needs to think before acting. The other needs to think before laughing.**

Unfortunately for the Blacksite, neither lesson has been fully learned.

— **C.O.R.E., Central Operations & Research Executive**
