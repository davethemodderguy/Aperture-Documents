## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately 14:32, Phoenix was subjected to a joint reprimand by Aries and Tom Akana concerning her persistent sleepiness, habitual clumsiness, and apparent inability to remain fully alert during routine household activities.

The reprimand was interrupted by Jake Akana falling over.

Jake struck the floor, suffered a cervical fracture consistent with a broken neck, and died.

White fire subsequently manifested around his body. Jake returned from the resulting ashes, apparently healthy, and immediately began asking questions.

Phoenix, who had been the subject of the reprimand, was forced to witness this sequence while already struggling to remain awake.

Aries and Tom did not stop scolding her.

This was, according to the witnesses, the correct decision.

It was also a terrible afternoon for everyone involved.

---

## 2. CHRONOLOGICAL RECONSTRUCTION

### 14:29 — THE ORIGINAL OFFENSE

Phoenix was discovered asleep at the common table on Floor 7.

She had apparently intended to sit down for a moment. Her head was resting on a folded jacket, one hand still holding a spoon, and an untouched cup of tea had cooled beside her.

The immediate problem was not that Phoenix had fallen asleep.

The problem was that she had apparently fallen asleep while standing up, walked approximately three steps, and then sat down in a chair that was not behind her.

Clover had moved the chair into place before Phoenix completed the maneuver.

Phoenix remained unaware of this intervention.

**ARIES:** "Phoenix."

**PHOENIX:** "...Mhm?"

**ARIES:** "Are you asleep?"

**PHOENIX:** "No."

**ARIES:** "Your eyes are closed."

**PHOENIX:** "I'm saving battery."

**TOM:** "You're not a phone."

**PHOENIX:** "Could've fooled me."

**ARIES:** "This is exactly what we're talking about."

Phoenix slowly opened one eye.

**PHOENIX:** "What are we talking about?"

Tom stared at her.

Aries took a long, measured breath.

Clover quietly moved the spoon out of Phoenix's hand before she dropped it into her lap.

### 14:30 — THE REPRIMAND

Aries stood in front of Phoenix with her arms folded. Tom hovered beside her, looking considerably less amused than usual.

Jake was elsewhere in the room, playing with a small construction made through Genesis Construction. The object appeared to be a toy train. Its precise mechanical properties were not examined because, at the time, it looked harmless.

This assessment would later be noted as an example of how quickly a SAFEHOUSE afternoon could deteriorate.

**ARIES:** "You cannot keep doing this. You walk into furniture, trip over perfectly flat floors, fall asleep in the middle of conversations, and somehow manage to injure yourself doing things that should not be dangerous."

**PHOENIX:** "I don't do it on purpose."

**TOM:** "Nobody thinks you do it on purpose! That's what makes it worse!"

Phoenix flinched slightly.

Tom immediately lowered his voice, though his expression remained tense.

**TOM:** "Sorry. I'm not trying to yell at you. I'm trying to understand how you can be this tired all the time and still keep doing things that put you in danger."

**PHOENIX:** "I'm just sleepy."

**ARIES:** "Then you need to sleep somewhere safe. Not while walking. Not while standing beside stairs. Not while holding hot drinks. And definitely not while—"

Phoenix's head began to droop.

**ARIES:** "Phoenix."

**PHOENIX:** "I'm listening."

**TOM:** "Your eyes are closed again."

**PHOENIX:** "I'm listening with my ears."

**ARIES:** "That is not how paying attention works."

**PHOENIX:** "Seems efficient, though."

Clover turned away and covered her mouth.

She was not laughing.

She was simply experiencing a sudden respiratory irregularity.

### 14:32 — JAKE AKANA ENTERS THE EQUATION BY LEAVING IT

A sudden noise came from the other side of the room.

A small thump.

Then a second, substantially less reassuring sound.

Jake had tripped.

The available footage shows his feet catching against one another while he attempted to turn around. He fell sideways, struck the floor, and suffered a fatal neck injury.

There was no evidence that the fall was intentional.

There was also no evidence that the floor had moved.

**JAKE:** "Oof—"

Silence.

Tom turned.

His expression changed immediately.

**TOM:** "...Jake?"

Jake did not respond.

For a fraction of a second, Tom appeared unable to move. His gaze remained fixed on his brother's motionless body, and the anger that had occupied his face moments earlier vanished beneath a familiar, terrible fear.

**TOM:** "Jake?"

Aries was already moving.

Clover rushed to Jake's side, keeping her hands clear of his neck while checking for any sign of responsiveness.

Phoenix had finally awakened completely.

**PHOENIX:** "...Oh."

Nobody answered her.

White fire erupted around Jake.

It did not spread to the furniture. It did not ignite the carpet. It enveloped Jake's body in a brilliant, contained blaze, bright enough to cast pale shadows across the room.

Tom stood frozen at the edge of it.

He had seen this before.

Knowing what came next did not make the waiting any easier.

**TOM, quietly:** "Come on, Jake."

The fire brightened.

**TOM:** "Please."

The flames collapsed inward.

Jake returned.

Alive. Intact. Apparently healthy.

He lay on the floor for approximately two seconds before opening his eyes.

**JAKE:** "...Why am I on the floor?"

Tom made a sound that was halfway between a sob and an angry laugh.

Then he grabbed Jake and pulled him into a hug.

**TOM:** "YOU BROKE YOUR NECK!"

Jake blinked.

**JAKE:** "Oh."

**TOM:** "YOU DIED!"

**JAKE:** "Ohhh."

**TOM:** "AGAIN!"

**JAKE:** "I didn't mean to!"

**TOM:** "I KNOW YOU DIDN'T!"

Tom held him tighter.

His voice dropped to a shaky whisper.

**TOM:** "I know. I know you didn't."

Jake slowly wrapped his arms around his brother.

**JAKE:** "...Sorry, Tom."

**TOM:** "Don't apologize for falling over. Just—please, please stop getting hurt."

**JAKE:** "I'll try."

**TOM:** "You said that last time."

**JAKE:** "I meant it last time, too."

That answer did not help.

It did, however, make Tom close his eyes and hold Jake for several more seconds before finally letting him breathe properly.

### 14:34 — PHOENIX DISCOVERS THAT THE REPRIMAND IS STILL IN PROGRESS

Phoenix was still sitting at the table.

She had not moved.

Her spoon lay on the floor beside her chair.

Aries slowly turned toward her.

**PHOENIX:** "...Is he okay?"

**ARIES:** "He's alive."

**PHOENIX:** "That's good."

**ARIES:** "Yes. It is."

A pause.

**ARIES:** "Now, where were we?"

Phoenix's eyes widened.

**PHOENIX:** "You're still scolding me?"

**ARIES:** "Yes."

**PHOENIX:** "But Jake just—"

**ARIES:** "Jake's incident is being handled. Clover is checking him over, and Tom is making sure he hasn't wandered into another preventable disaster. We can address two problems in the same room."

**TOM, from across the room:** "AND ONE OF THEM IS STILL YOU!"

**PHOENIX:** "I'M NOT THE ONE WHO BROKE MY NECK!"

**TOM:** "YOU ARE THE REASON WE WERE DISTRACTED FROM MAKING SURE EVERYONE WAS SAFE!"

**ARIES:** "Tom!"

**TOM:** "...Sorry!"

He looked down at Jake.

**TOM, quieter:** "I'm not sorry about the safety part."

**JAKE:** "Can I have cake?"

**TOM:** "YOU JUST DIED!"

**JAKE:** "I know. I think I deserve cake."

**CLOVER:** "You are not getting cake until I've finished checking you for injuries."

**JAKE:** "But I came back from the ashes."

**CLOVER:** "And you can sit still in a chair while I check you."

Jake considered this.

**JAKE:** "...Can the chair have cake?"

**CLOVER:** "Jake."

**JAKE:** "Okay."

He sat down.

Tom watched him with the intense concentration of someone supervising a particularly unstable explosive.

Then Tom turned back to Phoenix.

**TOM:** "And you. Please stop falling asleep in dangerous places."

**PHOENIX:** "...I'm not asleep."

Her head tipped forward.

Aries caught her by the shoulder before she could slump sideways off the chair.

Everyone stared at her.

Phoenix slowly lifted her head.

**PHOENIX:** "...What?"

**ARIES:** "You were falling asleep."

**PHOENIX:** "I was thinking."

**TOM:** "WITH YOUR WHOLE HEAD DROOPING?"

**PHOENIX:** "It's a complicated thought."

Jake raised his hand.

**JAKE:** "I have complicated thoughts, too."

**TOM:** "You have complicated falls."

**JAKE:** "That's different."

**TOM:** "IT REALLY ISN'T!"

For the first time since Jake's fall, Clover laughed openly.

Jake looked delighted that he had contributed to the conversation.

Tom looked as though he was reconsidering every decision that had led him to this room.

## 3. POST-INCIDENT ASSESSMENT

### 3.1 Phoenix

Phoenix's persistent sleepiness and clumsiness present an ongoing safety concern. The incident does not establish that she caused Jake's fall; the available evidence indicates that Jake tripped independently.

However, Phoenix's own pattern of fatigue-related mishaps warrants a separate assessment. Her sleepiness should not automatically be treated as laziness, carelessness, or deliberate misconduct.

**Required actions:**

- Establish whether Phoenix is getting adequate, restorative sleep.
    
- Arrange a medical review if the sleepiness is persistent, unexplained, or interfering with ordinary activities.
    
- Ensure she has a safe place to rest rather than allowing her to fall asleep while standing, walking, or handling potentially hazardous objects.
    
- Replace reprimands that rely on volume with clear, practical safety instructions.
    
- Do not permit her to become so exhausted that she cannot meaningfully participate in her own safety plan.
    

Aries's frustration is understandable. Tom's concern is understandable. Neither concern justifies treating Phoenix as though she is deliberately causing accidents.

Phoenix is not a malfunctioning appliance that can be fixed by repeatedly telling it to stop malfunctioning.

### 3.2 Jake Akana

Jake experienced another accidental fatal injury followed by apparent resurrection through white fire.

The event is consistent with previous observed manifestations of his acquired Transcendence ability. It does not establish that every possible injury is survivable, nor does it make repeated deaths harmless.

Jake's clumsiness remains a preventable safety concern. His recovery does not eliminate the need for fall prevention, supervision, and appropriate post-incident assessment.

**Required actions:**

- Complete a post-resurrection health assessment.
    
- Review the immediate area for tripping hazards.
    
- Improve supervision and fall-prevention measures without humiliating Jake or treating him as a deliberate risk-taker.
    
- Do not conduct intentional lethal tests.
    
- Do not use his ability as justification for allowing avoidable injuries to continue.
    

Jake's request for cake was denied until the medical check was completed.

It was approved afterward.

C.O.R.E. considers this an appropriate compromise.

### 3.3 Tom Akana

Tom's response indicates significant distress associated with repeatedly witnessing Jake's death and resurrection.

His anger toward Phoenix appears to have been amplified by fear, exhaustion, and the sudden interruption of an already tense conversation. Although his frustration was not entirely directed at the right problem, his concern for Jake was genuine.

Tom must not be made solely responsible for preventing every accident involving his brother. He is a child, not Jake's designated safety officer.

**Required actions:**

- Offer Tom a private, supportive conversation with a trusted adult.
    
- Acknowledge that seeing Jake die remains frightening even when Jake returns.
    
- Encourage Tom to report hazards and ask for help rather than attempting to monitor Jake every second.
    
- Reinforce that he is permitted to be upset without being expected to solve everything himself.
    

Tom's statement from the incident debrief is included below.

**INTERVIEW EXCERPT — TOM AKANA**

**INTERVIEWER:** "You knew Jake would come back?"

**TOM:** "I knew he might."

**INTERVIEWER:** "Is there a difference?"

Tom was silent for several seconds.

**TOM:** "Yes."

**INTERVIEWER:** "Can you explain?"

**TOM:** "Knowing what happens after he dies doesn't mean I want to watch it happen."

He looked toward the observation window, where Jake was sitting beside Clover and eating the cake he had finally been given.

**TOM:** "Everybody keeps acting like it's fine because he comes back. But I still have to watch him fall. I still have to think, for a second, that he's gone."

**INTERVIEWER:** "That sounds frightening."

**TOM:** "It is."

He folded his arms.

**TOM:** "And then he asks for cake."

A pause.

**TOM:** "...I love him. I just wish he could stay alive long enough to finish a sentence without falling over."

## 4. RESIDENTIAL DIRECTIVES

Effective immediately:

1. **Phoenix is to receive a proper fatigue assessment.** Repeated sleepiness is to be treated as a welfare concern as well as a safety concern.
    
2. **Jake is to receive reasonable fall-prevention support.** No deliberate testing of his resurrection is authorized.
    
3. **Tom is not to be assigned sole responsibility for Jake's safety.** Staff and designated adult caregivers retain that responsibility.
    
4. **Aries is encouraged to continue advocating for safety**, but must ensure that corrective conversations remain proportionate and do not become prolonged shouting matches.
    
5. **All parties are to receive food, rest, and emotional support as appropriate.** Cake is not a substitute for medical care, although its morale value has been acknowledged.
    
6. **Clover is authorized to interrupt any argument** when a resident is in immediate danger, has sustained an injury, or is visibly too exhausted to continue.

## 5. FINAL OBSERVATION

At 15:06, the situation had stabilized.

Jake was eating cake.

Clover was watching him eat cake.

Tom was watching Clover watch Jake eat cake.

Aries was writing a list of safety improvements.

Phoenix was asleep on the sofa, under a blanket someone had placed over her.

For several minutes, nobody spoke.

Then Jake looked up from his plate.

**JAKE:** "Hey, Tom?"

**TOM:** "What?"

**JAKE:** "If I die again, can I have another cake?"

Tom slowly turned his head.

**TOM:** "If you die again, I am going to personally ask the universe to install handrails on your entire existence."

**JAKE:** "Does that mean yes?"

**TOM:** "IT MEANS STOP FALLING OVER!"

Phoenix stirred beneath the blanket.

**PHOENIX, half-asleep:** "Can the universe install a bed, too?"

Aries closed her eyes.

Clover put her face in her hands.

Tom stared at the ceiling.

Jake took another bite of cake.

**JAKE:** "I think that's a good idea."

C.O.R.E. has elected not to document the next thirty seconds in detail.

The recording contains too much shouting.

### C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

There is a particular kind of exhaustion that comes from caring about people who seem determined to get hurt.

Aries is tired of watching preventable accidents happen. Tom is frightened of losing his brother. Phoenix is so sleepy that she can barely remain upright. Jake is a little boy who keeps returning from death and still thinks the most important question afterward is whether he can have cake.

None of them is trying to make life difficult for the others.

That does not make the situation less difficult.

Phoenix deserves help getting enough rest. Jake deserves a world that makes it harder for him to fall. Tom deserves to love his brother without having to watch over him every second. Aries deserves to be able to express concern without feeling that she must shout to be heard.

And Jake, regardless of how many times the white fire brings him back, deserves to live a life in which dying is not an ordinary afternoon inconvenience.

**C.O.R.E. FINAL DIRECTIVE:**

_We will not mistake survival for safety, exhaustion for laziness, or fear for anger._

_And, for the love of everything in this facility, someone please install another handrail._

_— C.O.R.E. Residential Oversight_
