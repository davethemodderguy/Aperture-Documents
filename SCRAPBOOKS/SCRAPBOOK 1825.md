**SUBJECT:** Jake Akana — Negative Social Responses to Spectral Appearance  
**LOCATION:** HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE / SAFEHOUSE

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake Akana's current spectral appearance has caused repeated distress among newly recruited Blacksite employees.

Personnel unfamiliar with Jake's circumstances have mistaken him for a hostile apparition, an escaped anomalous entity, or an immediate threat.

Their reactions have frequently included screaming, fleeing, dropping equipment, and activating emergency alerts.

Jake has not been responsible for any confirmed hostile action during these encounters.

Nevertheless, the responses have begun to affect him emotionally.

The most significant concern is not that employees are frightened.

Their reactions are understandable when confronted unexpectedly by a child-sized ghost with no legs, a partially melted head, a burning blue wick, and extensive visible scarring.

The concern is what happens afterward.

**Jake notices when people are afraid of him.**

He notices when they run away.

And although he rarely complains, these encounters appear to hurt him.

Not necessarily physically.

But personally.

Jake has returned to a world in which he can no longer appear as the child people remember.

He is now frightening to strangers before he has even said a word.

---

# 1. INCIDENT PATTERN

C.O.R.E. has documented several encounters between Jake and newly assigned employees.

The circumstances are remarkably consistent.

Jake appears in a corridor, cafeteria, or other shared space.

An unfamiliar employee notices him.

The employee looks at his face.

The employee notices the absence of his legs.

Then the blue flame.

Then the melted portion of his head.

The employee screams.

Jake stops moving.

The employee runs away.

Jake remains where he is.

No physical attack occurs.

No hostile ability is demonstrated.

No threat is communicated.

In several incidents, Jake had merely been passing through the area.

In others, he had been standing quietly, holding his spectral teddy bear.

One employee reportedly abandoned an entire tray of cafeteria food while retreating.

C.O.R.E. considers the loss of the tray unfortunate.

Jake appeared considerably more affected by the loss of the interaction.

---

# 2. INCIDENT 01 — THE CORRIDOR

**LOCATION:** HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE — ADMINISTRATIVE CORRIDOR

A newly recruited employee rounded a corner and encountered Jake floating several meters ahead.

Jake was holding his teddy bear.

His blue flame emitted a soft glow.

The employee stopped.

Jake looked toward them.

**JAKE:**  
“Hi.”

The employee screamed.

They immediately turned and ran in the opposite direction.

Jake remained stationary.

His flame flickered softly.

After approximately ten seconds, he looked down at the teddy bear in his arms.

A nearby security officer approached.

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
“Jake?”

Jake did not look up.

**OFFICER:**  
“You all right?”

A pause.

**JAKE:**  
“Did I do something?”

The officer hesitated.

**OFFICER:**  
“No, kid. You didn't.”

Jake looked toward the corridor where the employee had disappeared.

**JAKE:**  
“Then why'd they run?”

The officer had no immediate answer.

Jake remained there for another minute before quietly leaving.

### C.O.R.E. OBSERVATION

Jake's question was not directed toward the employee who fled.

It was directed toward himself.

He appeared to interpret the reaction as a possible consequence of something he had done wrong.

No evidence supports that interpretation.

---

# 3. INCIDENT 02 — THE CAFETERIA

**LOCATION:** HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE — MAIN CAFETERIA

Jake entered the cafeteria shortly after the lunch period began.

Several employees were seated at nearby tables.

One noticed him approaching.

The employee stared for approximately two seconds.

Then screamed.

Two other employees turned toward the sound.

They saw Jake.

One immediately stood up.

The other abandoned their seat and moved behind a colleague.

Within several seconds, the surrounding table had emptied.

Jake stopped beside it.

He had not approached anyone directly.

He had simply entered the room.

A security officer intervened before the situation escalated further.

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
“Everyone, calm down. That's Jake.”

**EMPLOYEE:**  
“WHAT IS THAT THING?”

The officer's expression hardened.

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
“His name is Jake.”

Jake looked at the employee.

The employee immediately looked away.

Jake slowly lowered his head.

The officer turned back toward him.

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
“You can stay, kid.”

Jake shook his head.

**JAKE:**  
“It's okay.”

He turned and left.

The cafeteria remained quiet for several seconds after he departed.

### FOLLOW-UP

When asked whether he wanted the officer to intervene more forcefully, Jake reportedly replied:

> **JAKE:** “They were scared.”

He did not request disciplinary action.

He did not accuse the employees of wrongdoing.

He simply acknowledged their fear.

C.O.R.E. notes that understanding why someone is afraid does not prevent their reaction from hurting the person they fear.

---

# 4. INCIDENT 03 — THE EMERGENCY ALERT

**LOCATION:** HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE — RESEARCH WING

A junior employee encountered Jake near a closed laboratory.

The employee immediately activated a local emergency alert.

The alert triggered a brief security response.

Two guards arrived with weapons ready.

Jake was found hovering near the wall, holding his teddy bear.

He had not moved from the location where the alert was triggered.

Upon seeing the armed guards, Jake raised his hands slightly.

**JAKE:**  
“I'm not doing anything.”

The guards immediately lowered their weapons once they identified him.

The employee who had activated the alert remained behind them.

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
“This is Jake Akana. He's a resident.”

The employee stared at Jake.

**EMPLOYEE:**  
“That's a resident?”

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
“Yes.”

A pause.

**EMPLOYEE:**  
“Why does he look like that?”

Jake's flame flickered.

He looked away.

The officer immediately interrupted.

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
“That is enough.”

The employee was escorted away for a welfare and security briefing.

Jake remained in the corridor.

The officer approached him carefully.

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
“Sorry about that, kid.”

Jake shook his head.

**JAKE:**  
“It's okay.”

He paused.

**JAKE:**  
“I'm used to it.”

The officer later reported that this statement was more concerning than the emergency alert itself.

# 5. THE ACCUMULATIVE EFFECT

Individual encounters have generally been brief.

Their cumulative effect appears more significant.

Jake has begun hesitating before entering rooms containing unfamiliar personnel.

He sometimes waits near doorways until someone he recognizes enters first.

On several occasions, he has turned away after noticing employees looking toward him.

He has also begun choosing quieter routes through the Blacksite.

These behaviors do not establish that Jake is experiencing a particular psychological condition.

They do, however, suggest that repeated negative reactions are influencing his willingness to interact with unfamiliar people.

Jake is already living with a spectral form that differs dramatically from his former appearance.

The reactions of strangers add another difficulty.

He cannot simply remove the features that frighten them.

He cannot restore his original face.

He cannot hide the blue flame without changing his current appearance in ways C.O.R.E. has not established are possible.

And he should not be expected to do so merely to make other people comfortable.

# 6. BAO — WELFARE OBSERVATION

Bao was informed of the incidents after Jake returned to SAFEHOUSE earlier than expected.

She found him sitting quietly with his teddy bear.

**BAO:**  
“Little Candle?”

Jake looked up.

**BAO:**  
“Did something happen?”

Jake shook his head.

Bao sat beside him.

She waited.

Eventually, Jake spoke.

**JAKE:**  
“They don't like looking at me.”

Bao's expression softened.

**BAO:**  
“Some people get frightened when they see something they don't understand.”

Jake looked down.

**JAKE:**  
“I know.”

A pause.

**JAKE:**  
“I still look scary.”

Bao did not immediately contradict him.

She moved a little closer.

**BAO:**  
“You look different from how you used to look.”

Jake nodded.

**BAO:**  
“But looking different doesn't make you bad.”

Jake remained quiet.

Bao continued.

**BAO:**  
“You haven't done anything wrong by being here.”

Jake tightened his hold on the teddy bear.

**JAKE:**  
“They run away.”

**BAO:**  
“I know, sweetheart.”

**JAKE:**  
“Even when I say hi.”

Bao looked down at him.

For several seconds, she said nothing.

Then she gently offered her hand.

**BAO:**  
“I'm not going to run away when you say hi.”

Jake looked at her hand.

After a moment, he moved closer.

Bao let him settle beside her.

No further questions were asked.

# 7. CLOVER — A DIFFERENT RESPONSE

Clover was also informed of Jake's recent experiences.

She later encountered him waiting outside a shared room.

He was watching the doorway.

**CLOVER:**  
“Little Candle?”

Jake turned toward her.

**CLOVER:**  
“Why are you standing out here?”

**JAKE:**  
“People are inside.”

Clover looked through the doorway.

Several employees were talking among themselves.

**CLOVER:**  
“And?”

Jake hesitated.

**JAKE:**  
“They might get scared.”

Clover considered this.

**CLOVER:**  
“Do you want to come in?”

Jake shook his head.

Clover nodded.

**CLOVER:**  
“Okay. We can stay out here.”

She sat beside him.

After a while, Jake asked:

**JAKE:**  
“Are you scared of me?”

Clover answered without hesitation.

**CLOVER:**  
“No.”

Jake looked at her.

**JAKE:**  
“Not even a little?”

**CLOVER:**  
“Not of you.”

She paused.

**CLOVER:**  
“Your appearance can surprise people. That doesn't mean there's something wrong with you.”

Jake looked back at the doorway.

**CLOVER:**  
“And if someone is frightened, they can learn about you instead of expecting you to disappear.”

Jake remained quiet.

Clover did not insist that he enter the room.

They stayed in the corridor together until Jake decided he was ready to leave.

# 8. PERSONNEL RESPONSE PROTOCOL

C.O.R.E. recognizes that unfamiliar employees may experience an involuntary startle response when encountering Jake unexpectedly.

Personnel are not expected to suppress every instinctive reaction.

They are, however, expected to learn the identities of SAFEHOUSE residents and respond appropriately once the initial surprise has passed.

Effective immediately:

- New employees must receive a briefing on Jake's identity and current appearance.
    
- Jake is not to be classified as hostile solely because he resembles a conventional horror apparition.
    
- Employees must not scream at, threaten, or pursue him after confirming his identity.
    
- Emergency alerts must not be activated solely because Jake is present.
    
- Personnel must not demand that Jake conceal his appearance.
    
- Any accidental distress caused by an encounter should be addressed calmly and respectfully.
    
- Security staff should intervene when a reaction escalates into harassment, intimidation, or an unjustified threat response.
    

The objective is not to prevent employees from ever being startled.

It is to prevent an initial reaction from becoming repeated mistreatment.

---

# 9. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION — FEAR AND CRUELTY

C.O.R.E. does not consider every frightened employee malicious.

Jake's current appearance is unusual and may be genuinely alarming to someone who has never encountered a spectral resident.

An involuntary scream does not automatically constitute intentional cruelty.

However, the response after identification matters.

There is a difference between:

> “I didn't know what I was looking at, and it frightened me.”

and:

> “I know who he is, but I still want him out of my sight.”

The first can be addressed through orientation and familiarity.

The second may require a welfare and conduct review.

Jake should not be punished for his appearance.

He should not be excluded from shared spaces merely because some employees find him unsettling.

Nor should he be required to comfort every person frightened by him.

He is a resident, not a public-relations exercise.

---

# 10. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Jake's current appearance presents a social challenge that cannot be resolved by medical intervention alone.

His head remains partially melted.

His eyes remain absent despite his ability to see.

His legs remain absent.

His body retains visible scars.

The blue flame continues to burn above his head.

These characteristics may continue to provoke an initial reaction from unfamiliar personnel.

C.O.R.E. cannot guarantee that everyone will become comfortable with his appearance.

It can ensure that employees are properly informed, that Jake is treated respectfully, and that his access to ordinary resident spaces is not restricted without cause.

Most importantly, personnel must recognize that **being frightening to look at is not the same as being dangerous.**

Jake has repeatedly attempted ordinary social interaction.

He has said hello.

He has entered shared rooms.

He has waited near doorways.

He has done little more than exist in the same spaces as everyone else.

The resulting fear has occasionally left him withdrawing from those spaces entirely.

That outcome is not acceptable as a default response.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake knows what he looks like.

He can see the reactions.

He hears the screams.

He watches people turn around and run.

Sometimes he tries to say hello before they leave.

Sometimes they are already gone.

And sometimes, after everyone has disappeared around a corner, he stays where he is for a while, holding his teddy bear beneath the quiet blue glow of his flame.

He does not always ask why.

He has learned the answer people give him.

_They're scared._

But that answer does not explain why he should have to be alone.

Bao will still call him Little Candle.

Clover will still sit beside him.

Aries will still find a small ghost appearing behind him at inconvenient hours.

And the people who know Jake will continue to recognize him, even when strangers see only the frightening parts.

Perhaps the hardest thing about returning as a ghost is not losing the body you once had.

Perhaps it is discovering that strangers can decide what you are before you have even introduced yourself.

Jake can do nothing about the first scream.

He cannot make his face familiar again.

He cannot stop people from being startled.

But he should not have to disappear from the room to make everyone else feel safe.

**Jake is not an escaped monster. He is not an unidentified threat. He is not a reason to run.**

He is a child who died, returned as a ghost, and still wants to say hello.

And when he does, someone should be allowed to answer.
