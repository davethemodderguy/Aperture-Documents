# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **POST-INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: ETHER DEPLETION EVENT – CORIN LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

One question continued to trouble investigators following Corin Lockwood's recovery.

Corin possessed an exceptionally large Ether reserve.

She had survived injuries that would have been fatal to ordinary personnel.

Her regenerative capabilities were well documented.

Yet she was found critically injured, her Ether reserves nearly exhausted.

The circumstances did not match any known failure mode.

An investigation was opened.

---

# **FORENSIC FINDINGS**

Medical examination revealed numerous surgical wounds.

The injuries were precise.

Methodical.

Several incision patterns closely resembled those documented from the organization responsible for Corin's childhood experimentation.

Unlike ordinary combat wounds, these injuries showed little evidence of defensive struggle.

Investigators believe Corin was restrained during at least part of the procedure.

---

# **ETHER ANALYSIS**

Residual Ether signatures recovered from the wounds indicated active extraction rather than natural depletion.

Researchers concluded that Corin's Ether had not dissipated on its own.

It had been deliberately removed.

Gradually.

Systematically.

Medical Services described the process as:

> **"Forced Ether drainage while the subject remained physiologically intact."**

The amount extracted remains unknown.

Only trace quantities remained within Corin's body when she was recovered.

Without emergency intervention, complete depletion would likely have resulted in irreversible tissue failure.

---

# **ABANDONMENT**

Evidence gathered from the recovery site suggests Corin was transported after the extraction process had concluded.

She was left in an alleyway during freezing weather.

There was no attempt to conceal her.

No attempt to administer aid.

Investigators believe those responsible assumed she would not survive.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL IMPACT**

When portions of the investigation were explained to Corin, she became unusually quiet.

After several minutes, she asked only one question.

> "...So they opened me again?"

Medical staff answered truthfully.

> "...Yes."

Corin nodded once.

She did not cry.

She simply looked at her hands for a long time.

The session was concluded early at the recommendation of Psychological Services.

---

# **LUMI**

Later that evening, Lumi found Corin awake despite medical instructions to rest.

Corin spoke before Lumi had the chance.

> "...I thought it was over."

Lumi sat beside her.

> "I know."

Corin closed her eyes.

> "...They did it again."

Her voice barely rose above a whisper.

> "...I couldn't stop them."

Lumi gently embraced her daughter.

This time...

She did not try to reassure her that everything was okay.

Because it wasn't.

Instead, she quietly said,

> "You survived something no child should ever have endured."

Corin leaned against her mother.

For several minutes, neither of them spoke.

---

# **INVESTIGATION STATUS**

The identities of those responsible remain under active investigation.

Recovered forensic evidence indicates extensive planning and technical knowledge of Ether physiology.

Director Lucious has authorized a full interagency investigation involving C.O.R.E., Blacksite Security, and the Krampus Compliance Authority.

Those responsible are to be apprehended and prosecuted.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin continues recovering under close medical supervision.

While her Ether reserves have been successfully restored, the psychological consequences of the incident remain severe.

Medical Services note that the greatest injury was not the loss of Ether itself.

It was the realization that, years after escaping her childhood captivity, someone had once again treated her body as something to be opened, emptied, and discarded.

**End of Post-Incident Investigation Report**
