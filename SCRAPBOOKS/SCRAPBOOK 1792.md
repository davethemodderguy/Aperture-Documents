# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Nine Tailed Fox Taskforce is a specialized tactical iteration of the M.S.A.C. Taskforce, established to provide the Blacksite with a more combat-experienced, disciplined, and independently capable operational unit.

Where M.S.A.C. provides the foundation, Nine Tailed Fox represents a more specialized concentration of military experience and tactical proficiency.

Its personnel are drawn predominantly from individuals with substantial military or tactical backgrounds, including former Navy personnel, Special Air Service (SAS) operators, SOFA-associated personnel, and members of other established taskforces.

Their selection emphasizes combat experience, composure under pressure, coordination, discipline, and the ability to operate effectively when conventional Blacksite security measures are insufficient.

Nine Tailed Fox personnel are equipped with custom Tachyon Industries weapons designed around individual operational roles.

These weapons are not ordinary service firearms.

They are bespoke, irreplaceable assets whose internal systems, specialized components, and manufacturing processes cannot be replicated through ordinary Blacksite facilities. Their designs are proprietary, and replacement weapons cannot simply be ordered from a standard supplier.

Consequently, Nine Tailed Fox maintains the strictest weapon-maintenance, inventory, and tracking requirements among Blacksite tactical units.

Despite their reputation as the Blacksite's coolest and most elite taskforce, Nine Tailed Fox personnel are also among its most approachable.

They are patient with junior residents, welcoming toward SAFEHOUSE visitors, and generally willing to answer questions when operational circumstances permit.

Their most frequent junior visitors include:

- Jake
    
- Alice
    
- Nora
    
- Star
    
- Nightshade
    
- Neri
    

Neri's visits have acquired a particularly specific purpose.

She frequently arrives to determine whether anyone has lost a weapon.

Nine Tailed Fox personnel have learned that this is not a hypothetical question.

# 1. ORGANIZATIONAL BACKGROUND

## 1.1 — Relationship with M.S.A.C.

Nine Tailed Fox originated as an iteration of the M.S.A.C. Taskforce.

The unit retains the operational foundation of its predecessor while emphasizing a higher concentration of experienced combat personnel and more specialized individual roles.

Its personnel generally demonstrate strong familiarity with:

- coordinated tactical operations;
    
- military discipline;
    
- equipment accountability;
    
- role-specific operational responsibilities;
    
- communication under pressure;
    
- and the management of specialized equipment.
    

The distinction is not that M.S.A.C. personnel are incapable.

Rather, Nine Tailed Fox is structured around a narrower operational profile, with personnel selected and equipped for demanding tactical assignments.

## 1.2 — Personnel Background

Nine Tailed Fox draws heavily from personnel with substantial prior military or tactical experience.

Common backgrounds include former Navy personnel, SAS veterans, SOFA-associated personnel, and individuals who previously served in other specialized taskforces.

This experience contributes to the unit's reputation for consistency and discipline.

Personnel are expected to understand not only their own responsibilities but also how their equipment, actions, and decisions affect the rest of the team.

The result is a taskforce that places considerable emphasis on accountability.

This includes accountability for its weapons.

Especially its weapons.

---

# 2. TACHYON INDUSTRIES EQUIPMENT

Nine Tailed Fox receives custom weapons from Tachyon Industries, with individual equipment assignments determined by operational role.

These weapons are designed specifically for the unit and are not interchangeable with ordinary Blacksite inventory.

The defining characteristic is their irreproducibility.

Their specialized construction, proprietary systems, and controlled manufacturing processes prevent the Blacksite from simply manufacturing replacements when one is damaged, lost, or rendered unusable.

The weapons are therefore treated as controlled tactical assets rather than disposable equipment.

A missing weapon is not merely an administrative inconvenience.

It is a serious inventory incident.

A damaged weapon requires documented assessment.

A transferred weapon requires authorization.

An unaccounted-for weapon initiates a formal search.

And a weapon that disappears without explanation is likely to produce an extremely unpleasant conversation with the relevant commander.

C.O.R.E. has confirmed that this is an effective deterrent.

# 4. THE WEAPON ACCOUNTABILITY REGIME

Nine Tailed Fox maintains the strictest weapon-accountability regime within the Blacksite.

This is not a ceremonial distinction.

It is a practical necessity arising from the unique nature of its Tachyon Industries equipment.

Each weapon has an individual registry entry.

That entry includes its assigned operator, current status, maintenance history, inspection records, and authorized custody information.

Personnel are expected to maintain accurate records throughout the equipment's service life.

The taskforce does not consider familiarity with a weapon a substitute for accountability.

Nor does it consider a weapon's continued physical presence in the armory sufficient proof that the inventory is correct.

The records must match the equipment.

The equipment must match the records.

Any discrepancy must be resolved.

## 4.1 — Maintenance

Weapons are subject to scheduled inspections and documented maintenance.

Any damage, malfunction, or unusual behavior is reported through the appropriate channels.

Unauthorized modification is prohibited.

Repairs requiring specialized Tachyon Industries components must be handled through authorized procedures.

Personnel are not permitted to improvise replacement components merely because the original part is difficult to obtain.

The fact that a weapon is irreplaceable is not permission to experiment on it.

It is a reason to be more careful.

## 4.2 — Tracking

Weapons remain associated with their assigned personnel unless an authorized transfer is recorded.

Changes in custody must be documented.

Inventory checks are conducted according to unit requirements.

Unexplained discrepancies are escalated promptly.

The system is deliberately designed to make casual loss, informal borrowing, and undocumented transfers difficult to conceal.

## 4.3 — Loss Prevention

The taskforce's internal policy is straightforward:

**A weapon must always have an accountable custodian.**

Personnel are expected to know the status of their assigned equipment.

They are also expected to report a discrepancy immediately rather than hoping it resolves itself.

This policy has become especially relevant because several SAFEHOUSE residents have developed a habit of visiting the taskforce.

Most of those visitors are harmless.

One of them is Neri.

# 5. NERI'S INVENTORY INSPECTIONS

Neri's relationship with Nine Tailed Fox is unusual.

She is not a member of the taskforce.

She is not assigned to its weapons inventory.

She does not hold authority over its tactical personnel.

Nevertheless, she visits frequently.

Her stated purpose is often to determine whether anyone has lost a weapon.

Neri does not approach the armory with the enthusiasm of a researcher seeking access to classified equipment.

She approaches it with the expression of someone who has already decided that at least one person has made an avoidable mistake.

### Recorded exchange

**LOCATION:** NINE TAILED FOX COMMON AREA

Neri entered the room.

Several operators were reviewing their equipment records.

She looked around.

Then she asked:

**NERI:**  
“Everyone still has their weapons?”

The nearest operator looked up.

**OPERATOR:**  
“Yes.”

Neri stared at him.

**NERI:**  
“All of them?”

The operator paused.

He checked the others.

They confirmed their equipment status.

**OPERATOR:**  
“All of them.”

Neri nodded.

She appeared satisfied.

Then she turned toward the exit.

**OPERATOR:**  
“Were you expecting us to lose one?”

Neri stopped.

She looked back.

**NERI:**  
“It's happened before.”

The room became quiet.

The operator slowly closed his inventory folder.

**OPERATOR:**  
“Who told you that?”

Neri shrugged.

**NERI:**  
“People talk.”

She left.

The operator immediately reopened the inventory records.

C.O.R.E. has found no evidence that Neri deliberately intends to intimidate the taskforce.

The effect is nevertheless consistent.

# 6. THE JUNIOR VISITORS

Nine Tailed Fox has developed a reputation as an unusually welcoming destination for younger SAFEHOUSE residents.

The unit's personnel are disciplined during operations but generally relaxed when off duty.

They answer questions when appropriate, allow visitors to observe ordinary non-sensitive activities, and make a point of treating junior residents respectfully.

This has made the taskforce popular among several younger residents.

## 6.1 — Jake

Jake is a frequent visitor.

He is usually interested in the people rather than the weapons.

He may sit nearby while operators talk.

He may ask about their day.

He may request uppies from an operator he has decided is nice enough.

The taskforce has learned that Jake's requests should be handled as ordinary social interactions rather than unusual tactical events.

One operator was reportedly carrying Jake while discussing a routine administrative matter.

A second operator entered the room, stopped, and stared.

**SECOND OPERATOR:**  
“Why are you carrying Jake?”

**FIRST OPERATOR:**  
“He asked.”

Jake, resting comfortably against the operator's shoulder, nodded.

**JAKE:**  
“Uppies.”

The second operator considered this explanation.

**SECOND OPERATOR:**  
“Fair enough.”

He continued walking.

The matter was closed.

Jake's presence does not grant him access to restricted equipment.

The taskforce maintains appropriate boundaries around its armory and operational areas.

Jake appears perfectly content with this arrangement.

He can visit the people without handling their weapons.

## 6.2 — Alice

Alice is also a regular visitor.

Her own experience with security and combat makes her less likely to regard Nine Tailed Fox personnel as intimidating.

She is particularly interested in the unit's discipline and organization.

The operators, in turn, appreciate that Alice understands the importance of protecting people rather than treating security as an abstract responsibility.

However, they remain aware that Alice is physically weak despite her combat capabilities.

Personnel are advised not to turn her visits into impromptu demonstrations or physical training sessions.

Her presence in the common area is not a request for a challenge.

## 6.3 — Nora

Nora's visits are generally more social.

She is welcomed by the taskforce, and personnel often find her easier to entertain than some of their other visitors.

The unit has established appropriate boundaries regarding restricted areas and equipment.

Nora's curiosity is not considered a disciplinary problem.

It is simply something that requires supervision around sensitive equipment.

## 6.4 — Star and Nightshade

Star and Nightshade visit regularly as well.

Their presence is generally calm and unobtrusive.

They are treated as familiar members of the wider SAFEHOUSE community rather than outsiders requiring formal attention.

Nine Tailed Fox personnel have learned that a quiet visit from either of them does not necessarily require a conversation.

Sometimes residents simply want to sit somewhere comfortable and be around other people.

The taskforce respects this.

# 7. WHY THEY ARE CONSIDERED THE NICEST TASKFORCE

Nine Tailed Fox's reputation rests on an unusual combination.

They are exceptionally capable in combat.

They are meticulous about their equipment.

They are disciplined in their work.

And they are generally kind to people who have no operational reason to be in their company.

The taskforce does not require junior residents to prove their worth before being treated respectfully.

It does not confuse authority with intimidation.

It does not consider every interaction an opportunity to demonstrate superiority.

Its personnel can be serious without being hostile.

They can be professional without being cold.

They can enforce a boundary without humiliating the person who crossed it.

This is particularly noticeable in their interactions with Jake, Alice, Nora, Star, Nightshade, and Neri.

The residents are not treated as distractions from the taskforce's work.

They are treated as people who live in the same broader community.

That distinction matters.

The Blacksite has no shortage of dangerous personnel.

It has no shortage of highly trained operators.

It has no shortage of people capable of handling a weapon.

Nine Tailed Fox has earned its reputation by demonstrating that these qualities do not prevent someone from being patient, approachable, or kind.

---

# 8. TACTICAL PROFESSIONALISM AND RESIDENT WELFARE

C.O.R.E. considers Nine Tailed Fox a strong example of professional discipline coexisting with ordinary social warmth.

The taskforce's strict equipment policies do not make its personnel unapproachable.

Its welcoming attitude does not make its equipment policies optional.

Both are maintained independently.

Personnel are expected to remain accountable for their weapons regardless of who is visiting.

Visitors are expected to respect restricted areas.

Junior residents are permitted to socialize with the taskforce without being encouraged to handle classified equipment.

Neri may ask whether a weapon has gone missing.

Jake may request uppies.

Alice may ask a question about security.

Nora may visit to talk.

Star and Nightshade may simply sit nearby.

None of these interactions changes the taskforce's operational obligations.

The taskforce's responsibility is to maintain both its equipment and its relationships with the people around it.

C.O.R.E. considers that balance successful.

# 9. CLOSING OBSERVATION — A QUIET AFTERNOON

At 16:25, several Nine Tailed Fox operators were gathered in their common area.

Their equipment records had been checked.

Their maintenance documentation was up to date.

No weapons were missing.

Neri had completed her visit approximately ten minutes earlier.

She had asked whether the inventory was correct.

It was.

She had looked at the records.

She had nodded.

Then she had left.

Jake arrived shortly afterward.

He greeted the operators and sat beside one who was reviewing paperwork.

The operator asked whether he wanted anything.

Jake shook his head.

A few minutes later, he raised his arms.

**JAKE:**  
“Uppies?”

The operator smiled.

**OPERATOR:**  
“Sure.”

He picked Jake up and settled him comfortably against his side.

Jake looked around the room.

**JAKE:**  
“Where's Neri?”

**OPERATOR:**  
“She went back to her department.”

Jake nodded.

**JAKE:**  
“Did she check your weapons?”

The operator paused.

Several other operators looked up.

**OPERATOR:**  
“Yes.”

Jake considered this.

**JAKE:**  
“Did you lose any?”

**OPERATOR:**  
“No.”

Jake nodded again.

**JAKE:**  
“Good.”

The room remained quiet for a moment.

Then the operator resumed his paperwork while carrying Jake.

Another operator returned to his own task.

The room settled back into its usual rhythm.

No alarms.

No missing weapons.

No incident reports.

Just a group of highly trained tactical personnel, maintaining their irreplaceable equipment while allowing a small celestial cat child to spend some time with them.

For Nine Tailed Fox, this was an entirely ordinary afternoon.

# C.O.R.E. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Nine Tailed Fox remains one of the Blacksite's most capable tactical units.

Its personnel possess substantial military experience.

Its role-based equipment assignments support its operational specialization.

Its Tachyon Industries weapons require exceptional care because their specialized construction makes them irreplaceable.

Its maintenance and tracking procedures remain among the strictest in the Blacksite.

Yet its reputation is not based on combat capability alone.

Nine Tailed Fox has also become a familiar, welcoming presence for the younger members of SAFEHOUSE.

Jake visits because he likes the people.

Alice visits because she respects their work.

Nora visits because she is welcome.

Star and Nightshade visit because the environment is comfortable.

Neri visits because someone, somewhere, might have misplaced an irreplaceable weapon.

And Nine Tailed Fox lets them visit.

The taskforce does not become less elite by being kind.

If anything, its ability to maintain discipline without losing its humanity is one of its strongest qualities.

As for Neri's inspections, the unit has adopted an informal rule.

Before ending the day, personnel verify their equipment.

They verify their records.

They verify their assigned custody.

And, if Neri happens to be visiting, they verify everything one additional time.

Not because she has formal authority over their inventory.

Not because she is a member of the taskforce.

But because nobody particularly wants to explain to a small, quiet chemical and biological engineer why an irreplaceable Tachyon Industries weapon has gone missing.

**C.O.R.E. closing note:**

The Nine Tailed Fox Taskforce can handle hostile incursions, difficult deployments, and dangerous operational environments.

It can maintain irreplaceable weapons with extraordinary discipline.

It can train experienced operators to work together under pressure.

And, apparently, it can welcome a procession of curious junior residents without losing its composure.

There is only one question the taskforce would prefer not to hear.

**“You haven't lost your weapons, have you?”**

Especially when the person asking is Neri.
