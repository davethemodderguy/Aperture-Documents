# 1. SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon continues to experience recurrent nightmares.

The nightmares are associated with memories from the period immediately preceding her disappearance and subsequent rescue by **Guiding Light**.

Nora was killed before her arrival at SAFEHOUSE.

She remembers this.

She remembers the pain.

She remembers being stabbed.

She remembers being discarded.

She remembers falling into the anomalous blue portal.

And, most significantly:

> **She remembers Neri's face.**

Nora was approximately six years old when the event occurred.

The fact that she survived does not mean the experience has been psychologically erased.

Guiding Light rescued Nora.

She did not erase what happened to her.

---

# 2. THE MEMORY

Nora's recollection is unusually vivid.

She does not describe the event as a vague childhood memory.

She remembers individual details.

The environment.

The people around her.

The physical sensation.

The fear.

The moment she realized what was happening.

Being stabbed.

Being thrown away afterward.

And Neri.

Particularly Neri.

Nora remembers looking toward her older sister during the attack.

She remembers **Neri's expression.**

That memory appears to be more persistent than almost every other part of the event.

---

# 3. WHY NERI'S FACE MATTERS

Nora does not remember only that Neri was present.

She remembers what Neri looked like.

The shock.

The fear.

The helplessness.

The realization.

The expression of someone watching her little sister die and being unable to stop it.

Nora has never described Neri's face as angry.

She does not remember hatred.

She remembers:

> **“Sis looked scared.”**

That sentence has been repeated several times.

---

# 4. NIGHTMARE PATTERN

Nora's nightmares vary.

However, several elements recur.

### Recurring imagery:

- darkness;
    
- falling;
    
- cold;
    
- the sensation of being unable to reach Neri;
    
- Neri calling her name;
    
- the blue light;
    
- the feeling of being discarded;
    
- the sensation of falling through empty space;
    
- and Neri's face immediately before the memory ends.
    

The nightmare does not always reproduce the event exactly.

Sometimes it changes.

Sometimes Nora is back in the original environment.

Sometimes she is falling.

Sometimes she is trying to reach Neri.

Sometimes Neri is the one falling.

Sometimes Nora wakes before the stabbing occurs.

Sometimes she wakes afterward.

---

# 5. THE MOST COMMON DREAM

One recurring nightmare has been documented several times.

Nora is standing in darkness.

She cannot see her surroundings.

She hears Neri.

> **NERI:**  
> “Nora?”

Nora attempts to answer.

No sound comes out.

She tries again.

Nothing.

Then she sees Neri.

Neri is looking directly at her.

Nora attempts to run toward her.

She cannot move.

Neri reaches toward her.

The scene changes.

Nora falls.

The last thing she sees is Neri's face.

She wakes immediately afterward.

---

# 6. POST-NIGHTMARE RESPONSE

Nora generally wakes abruptly.

Common observations include:

- rapid breathing;
    
- crying;
    
- immediately searching for Neri;
    
- grabbing nearby blankets;
    
- calling for Aeterna;
    
- asking where she is;
    
- or simply repeating:
    

> **“Sis?”**

She does not always remember that she is currently safe.

The distinction between **remembering** and **understanding** is important.

Nora knows she survived.

But immediately after waking, the memory can temporarily feel present again.

---

# 7. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri occasionally wakes when Nora has a nightmare.

When this occurs, Neri does not attempt to interrogate her.

She does not ask:

> “What did you dream about?”

She does not demand an explanation.

She simply stays.

One documented instance occurred at approximately 03:14.

Nora woke crying.

Neri was already awake.

Nora looked toward her.

> **NORA:**  
> “Sis?”

Neri nodded.

Nora crawled toward her.

Neri allowed Nora to sit beside her.

No further conversation occurred.

After several minutes:

> **NORA:**  
> “You were there.”

Neri remained silent.

> **NORA:**  
> “When it happened.”

Neri nodded.

Nora looked down.

> **NORA:**  
> “You looked scared.”

Neri looked away.

After several seconds:

> **NERI:**  
> “I was.”

Nora leaned against her.

Neri remained beside her until she fell asleep.

---

# 8. ELSHER

Elsher is not normally present during these incidents.

His primary quarters remain within the **MSAC residential quarters**, separate from the SAFEHOUSE.

He therefore cannot respond as immediately as Neri or Aeterna.

This does not indicate emotional distance.

Elsher remains aware of Nora's nightmares and has repeatedly checked on her during periods when he is present at SAFEHOUSE.

His approach is considerably quieter.

He does not attempt to force Nora to discuss the memories.

He simply reminds her:

> **“You're here.”**

And:

> **“We're here.”**

---

# 9. AETERNA

Aeterna is the most consistent responder.

She appears to recognize Nora's nightmares almost immediately.

No alarm is necessary.

No notification is necessary.

If Nora wakes frightened, Aeterna is frequently already moving toward her.

She kneels beside the bed.

Her armored form can appear intimidating under normal circumstances.

At night, however, Aeterna deliberately lowers her presence.

Her voice becomes quiet.

Her movements become slow.

The stars within her eyes dim.

She does not attempt to make herself frightening.

She simply stays.

# 11. THE MEMORY OF BEING THROWN AWAY

Nora's memory does not end with the stabbing.

She remembers being discarded afterward.

This is particularly significant.

The memory is not simply:

> **“I died.”**

It is:

> **“They killed me and threw me away.”**

Nora has described the experience as feeling like she had been treated as something that no longer mattered.

This may contribute to her unusually strong attachment to Neri, Bao, Elsher, and Aeterna.

They do not treat her as disposable.

They actively demonstrate the opposite.

---

# 12. THE BLUE LIGHT

Nora also remembers the moment after being discarded.

She fell into darkness.

Then:

**Blue.**

The blue light.

The blue moon.

Guiding Light.

Nora remembers hearing her.

She remembers being told not to be afraid.

She remembers that someone came for her.

This memory exists alongside the traumatic memories rather than replacing them.

Nora therefore does not describe her story as simply:

> “I died.”

She describes it more accurately:

> **“I died, and then the blue moon found me.”**

---

# 13. AETERNA'S ROLE

Aeterna appears particularly important because she provides something Neri cannot always provide.

Neri understands the memory.

She was there.

Elsher understands the family loss.

He was not there.

Aeterna understands something different.

She understands what it means to have once been part of a world where death was routine.

But she no longer belongs to that world.

When Nora wakes from a nightmare, Aeterna does not tell her:

> “Don't think about it.”

She does not tell her:

> “It wasn't real.”

It was real.

Instead:

> **“It happened.”**

And:

> **“You survived.”**

# 15. NERI'S OWN MEMORY

Neri has never forgotten the original event either.

She remembers believing Nora was dead.

She remembers the loss.

She remembers the expression on Nora's face.

Nora's return therefore carries an unusual emotional contradiction.

Neri knows her sister survived.

Neri can see her.

Talk to her.

Watch her play with Alice.

Watch her annoy Neko.

Watch her sleep.

And yet part of Neri still remembers the moment she believed Nora was gone forever.

The two memories coexist.

---

# 16. THE SIBLINGS

The Tachyon siblings therefore carry three different relationships with the same event.

### ELSHER

Knows what happened.

Was separated from it.

Protects his younger sisters now.

### NERI

Witnessed Nora's death.

Believed she had lost her permanently.

Now has her back.

### NORA

Experienced the death directly.

Remembers being discarded.

Remembers Neri's face.

Was rescued by Guiding Light.

Now lives with the people who believed she was gone.

None of these perspectives cancel the others.

# 17. CURRENT WELFARE GUIDANCE

Personnel are instructed:

- Do not tell Nora to “forget it.”
    
- Do not tell her it was “just a dream.”
    
- Do not pressure her to describe the event.
    
- Do not repeatedly ask about Neri's expression.
    
- Do not treat nightmares as attention-seeking.
    
- Do not separate Nora from trusted support following a nightmare unless medically necessary.
    
- Do not treat her memory as proof that she is permanently damaged.
    

If Nora asks to talk:

Listen.

If Nora asks for Neri:

Locate Neri when practical.

If Nora asks for Aeterna:

Allow Aeterna to respond.

If Nora simply wants someone nearby:

Stay.

No interrogation is required.
