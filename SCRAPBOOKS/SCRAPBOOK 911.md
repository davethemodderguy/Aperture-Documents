# C.O.R.E. ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY REPORT

**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**SPECIES:** Artificial Seal Thiren  
**INCIDENT TYPE:** Architect Fragment Ingestion  
**ASSOCIATED ENTITY:** Guiding Light  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE OBSERVATION  
**CLASSIFICATION:** ANOMALOUS / ARCHITECT-RELATED  
**RESTRICTION:** C.O.R.E. MEDICAL & RESEARCH PERSONNEL

---

# 1. Incident Summary

Neri consumed a cupcake prepared by **Guiding Light**.

At the time of consumption, there was no indication that the food contained any anomalous material.

The incident initially appeared completely ordinary.

Neri ate the cupcake.

She continued her normal activities.

Later, while walking through the SAFEHOUSE alongside Alice, Alice noticed an unusual change in Neri's eyes.

### Observed changes:

- Both eyes had developed a distinct **blue coloration**.
    
- Neri's **right iris had changed shape**, becoming visibly reminiscent of a **crescent moon**.
    
- No obvious pain or distress was reported by Neri.
    
- Neri's vision appeared unaffected.
    

Alice immediately reported the change.

Guiding Light was subsequently asked to inspect Neri.

Her examination produced an unexpected explanation.

---

# 2. Guiding Light's Finding

Guiding Light determined that the cupcake Neri had consumed contained a microscopic fragment of her own wing.

The fragment had separated from Guiding Light at some point when her wings broke apart.

The piece had apparently become incorporated into the cupcake without either Guiding Light or Neri noticing.

There was no malicious intent.

There was no deliberate attempt to transfer power.

Neri had simply eaten it.

---

# 3. Architect Fragment Phenomenon

Architects possess bodies and structures fundamentally different from conventional biological organisms.

Their fragments are therefore not necessarily inert biological material.

A fragment of an Architect may retain some degree of the properties belonging to its original owner.

More importantly, there is an unresolved phenomenon surrounding **ingestion or incorporation of Architect fragments by other beings.**

In some circumstances, an individual may experience no noticeable effect.

In others, the fragment may produce temporary anomalous effects.

In particularly unusual cases, the recipient may acquire an ability associated with the Architect.

The most concerning aspect is that C.O.R.E. has **not established whether such abilities are permanent.**

---

# 4. Possible Outcomes

Current research recognizes three theoretical outcomes following Architect-fragment ingestion.

### OUTCOME A — NO EFFECT

The fragment is metabolized, rejected, or otherwise loses its anomalous properties.

The recipient returns to baseline.

### OUTCOME B — TEMPORARY EFFECT

The fragment temporarily alters the recipient's physiology or grants access to some portion of the Architect's properties.

The effect eventually disappears.

### OUTCOME C — PERMANENT INTEGRATION

The fragment becomes permanently incorporated into the recipient.

Any resulting anomalous ability may consequently become a permanent part of the recipient's physiology.

**Neri's current condition has not yet been assigned to any of these categories.**

---

# 5. Neri's Eyes

The changes to Neri's eyes are presently considered the strongest evidence that some degree of integration has occurred.

The alteration is notably specific.

Her right iris now resembles a **crescent moon**, while the overall coloration of her eyes has shifted toward blue.

The morphology does not correspond to normal Seal Thiren anatomy.

It also does not appear to be a consequence of Neri's pre-existing hematological anomaly.

Guiding Light's fragment is therefore considered the most probable cause.

Whether the eyes will eventually return to their original appearance remains unknown.

---

# 6. The More Concerning Possibility

The visual change itself is not necessarily dangerous.

The possibility that it represents **successful Architect-fragment integration** is.

If Neri has permanently integrated part of Guiding Light's fragment, there may be consequences beyond her eyes.

Potential effects could include:

- altered interaction with Light,
    
- increased compatibility with Architect constructs,
    
- manifestation of an Architect-derived ability,
    
- changes to Neri's existing anomalous physiology,
    
- or an entirely new property not previously observed.
    

There is currently insufficient evidence to determine which, if any, will occur.

---

# 7. Guiding Light's Assessment

Guiding Light was reportedly less alarmed by the visual alteration than the medical and research staff.

Her assessment was effectively:

> "Oh."

Followed by an inspection of Neri.

And then:

> "There's a piece of my wing in you."

Neri reportedly did not immediately understand why everyone else was suddenly concerned.

---

# 8. Neri's Response

Neri's primary concern was considerably less scientific.

She wanted to know whether she was still allowed to eat Guiding Light's cupcakes.

### C.O.R.E. NOTE

**Yes.**

There is no current evidence that Guiding Light's baking is inherently hazardous.

However, Guiding Light has been asked to check for detached Architect material before serving baked goods to SAFEHOUSE residents.

This request was received with considerable embarrassment.

---

# 9. Research Status

At present, C.O.R.E. cannot determine whether Neri has actually acquired an Architect-derived power.

The eye alteration demonstrates that **something happened.**

It does not establish permanence.

The fragment may:

- disappear,
    
- be absorbed,
    
- remain dormant,
    
- produce temporary changes,
    
- or permanently alter Neri.
    

Long-term observation is therefore required.

No attempt should be made to remove the fragment.

No attempt should be made to deliberately introduce additional Architect fragments into Neri.

The phenomenon is to be observed naturally.

---

# 10. Special Consideration

Neri's body is already exceptionally abnormal as a result of the experimentation and abuse she survived.

Her blood spontaneously degrades.

Her genetic structure is irregular.

Her organs exhibit nonstandard characteristics.

Her physiology has already demonstrated the ability to incorporate unusual biological material.

Consequently, researchers cannot assume that Neri will respond to an Architect fragment in the same manner as an ordinary person.

This incident may therefore represent a previously undocumented interaction between:

**Artificial Thiren physiology + Architect material + anomalous integration.**

## 12. Final Note

There is something strangely appropriate about the circumstances.

Neri did not steal the fragment.

She did not ask for it.

Nobody performed an experiment on her.

She simply ate a cupcake made by someone she trusted.

And now C.O.R.E. has to determine whether that cupcake accidentally gave a ten-year-old Seal Thiren a permanent piece of an Architect.

For the moment, the only confirmed change is her eye.

**Whether the rest of the story has already been written remains unknown.**
