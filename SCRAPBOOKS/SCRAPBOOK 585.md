### **MEDICAL / SAFEHOUSE CARE OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — PAIN RESPONSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Condition:** **Mercury Poisoning / Severe Weakness / Pain**  
**Communication:** Nonverbal

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's condition remains serious.

She spends most of her time lying down, conserving what little strength she has.

She cannot meow anymore.

She cannot reliably communicate verbally.

And she is in pain.

The only clear indication of that pain is her behavior.

Corin sometimes quietly cries while gently nibbling on a plushie.

Other times, she reaches for Belle's hand and lightly nibbles that instead.

---

# **THE NIBBLING**

The behavior is considerably different from Corin's normal playful nibbling.

There is no playfulness behind it.

She simply holds the plushie between her tiny paws and gently bites it.

Sometimes she does not even bite down.

She simply keeps it in her mouth while trembling slightly.

If Belle offers her a hand, Corin may release the plushie and gently nibble Belle instead.

Belle understands.

It is not a request to play.

It is Corin trying to cope with the pain.

---

# **BELLE'S ROLE**

Belle has spent much of her available time caring for Corin.

This is particularly difficult because Belle is also the **Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority**.

Her responsibilities there have not disappeared.

Reports still need reviewing.

Personnel still require direction.

The Authority still operates.

Yet whenever possible, Belle returns to SAFEHOUSE.

And when she returns, she checks on Corin first.

---

# **BELLE'S HAND**

Belle has discovered that Corin often prefers holding onto her hand rather than a toy.

She carefully places one finger near Corin.

Corin gently bites it.

Belle does not pull away.

She simply lets Corin hold onto her.

> **"It's okay."**

Corin remains silent.

A tiny tear runs down her face.

Belle gently strokes her head with her other hand.

---

# **THE PLUSHIE**

When Belle has to leave for work, she leaves Corin with one of her soft plushies.

Corin has become attached to it.

She curls against it beneath her wings.

Sometimes she gently nibbles the plushie's ear.

Sometimes she simply rests her face against it.

It provides something for Corin to hold onto when Belle is away.

---

# **ARCHITECT OBSERVATION**

Guiding Light has confirmed that Corin is experiencing genuine physical pain.

Her silence should not be mistaken for improvement.

She cannot complain.

She cannot explain where it hurts.

She cannot ask for help in words.

She can only react.

Guiding Light therefore remains available to interpret Corin's condition when the SAFEHOUSE residents cannot determine what she needs.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie occasionally sits beside Belle while she cares for Corin.

Neither speaks much.

They simply watch her.

Corin eventually shifts closer to Belle's hand.

Her wings tremble slightly.

Belle lets her hold on.

---

# **BELLE'S DOUBLE LIFE**

At the Krampus Compliance Authority, Belle is a Director.

She handles serious matters.

She makes decisions.

She manages personnel.

She is responsible for an entire organization.

At SAFEHOUSE, however, she spends hours sitting beside a tiny white winged cat.

Giving her water.

Offering tiny pieces of food.

Keeping her warm.

Holding her hand.

Waiting for the pain to pass.

The contrast has become almost surreal.

---

# **CURRENT CARE PRIORITIES**

SAFEHOUSE personnel are instructed to prioritize:

- Pain monitoring.
    
- Hydration.
    
- Small, manageable meals.
    
- Warmth and comfort.
    
- Minimal physical exertion.
    
- Quiet surroundings.
    
- Continuous medical observation.
    
- Immediate Architect assistance when Corin's condition becomes unclear.
    

Corin should not be left alone for prolonged periods.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin does not have the strength to do much anymore.

So she lies down.

She curls beneath her wings.

Sometimes she cries quietly.

Sometimes she holds a plushie between her paws and gently nibbles it.

Sometimes she reaches for Belle's hand instead.

Belle lets her.

Even with the responsibilities of directing the Krampus Compliance Authority, Belle keeps returning to SAFEHOUSE.

Because right now, Corin doesn't need a Director.

She needs someone to stay.

And Belle stays.
