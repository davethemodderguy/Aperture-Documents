# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE / MEMORIAL ARCHIVE**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — RETURN TO NORMALCY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** **PRESENT / STABLE / IMPROVING**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Eventually, things returned to normal.

Not exactly as they were before Alice disappeared.

But close enough.

The alarms stopped.

The stabilization equipment was no longer running constantly.

Personnel stopped checking empty spaces for signs of Alice.

The Treehouse became a home again rather than a memorial.

And Alice—

was simply Alice again.

---

# **PHYSICAL CONDITION**

Alice remains fragile.

She is still noticeably weaker than an ordinary child.

She still tires quickly.

She still needs help with certain things.

She still spends a considerable amount of time resting.

However, there has been a small but consistent improvement.

She can walk for longer.

She can stay awake longer.

Her stamina has increased slightly.

Her regeneration appears somewhat more efficient.

Even her appetite has improved by a small amount.

Corin considers this a major victory.

---

# **THE SCOOTER**

Alice has started using Neko's electric scooter regularly again.

She still cannot travel particularly far.

She still needs supervision.

And she still occasionally gets tired halfway through a trip.

But now she can make it around the expanded SAFEHOUSE perimeter without immediately needing to stop.

Neko is quietly pleased.

He pretends not to be.

---

# **THE JOURNAL**

Alice's journal remains fully restored.

She continues writing in it.

Her grammar is still imperfect.

Star, Lumi and Corin continue helping her.

Progress remains slow.

But Alice no longer seems as afraid of forgetting.

She writes down important things when she wants to remember them.

Sometimes she draws instead.

Her drawings have become more detailed.

---

# **THE HAT**

Alice wears her hat again.

Almost constantly.

Nobody complains.

It has become an ordinary part of her appearance again.

People no longer stare at it with sadness.

They see Alice wearing it.

That's enough.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin is still protective.

Perhaps excessively so.

She still checks on Alice frequently.

She still makes chicken potato soup.

She still worries whenever Alice gets tired.

But she has started relaxing again.

Sometimes she even lets Alice sit nearby while she cooks without constantly checking on her.

Alice seems to enjoy this.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle has resumed giving Alice piggyback rides.

Alice insists she can walk herself.

Belle insists that Alice is tiny.

The argument has never been resolved.

---

# **STAR**

Star has resumed grammar lessons.

Alice still doesn't understand half of them.

Her latest written attempt was:

> **"Alice is learning grammer."**

Star stared at it.

Alice stared back.

Star sighed.

> "Close enough."

Alice drew a :P beside it.

---

# **NEKO**

Neko checked the scooter.

Then checked it again.

Then upgraded the safety system.

Alice asked why.

Neko answered:

> "Because you keep doing stupid things."

Alice wrote:

> **"Alice smart."**

Neko looked at her.

> "...Debatable."

Alice smiled.

---

# **THE SAFEHOUSE**

Movie nights have returned.

People eat together again.

The kitchen is noisy.

The living room is crowded.

Someone is always arguing over what to watch.

Someone is always stealing food.

Someone is always sitting in the wrong place.

It feels normal.

The way it should.

---

# **ONE SMALL DIFFERENCE**

Alice has become slightly more willing to care about herself.

Not much.

But enough to notice.

When she gets tired, she sometimes stops.

When she's hungry, she occasionally asks for food.

When something hurts, she sometimes tells someone.

Once, Corin asked:

> "Are you okay?"

Alice nodded.

Then paused.

She wrote:

> **"Alice tired."**

Corin smiled.

> "Then let's rest."

Alice nodded again.

She didn't argue.

That alone was progress.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Alice's disappearance changed everyone.

But it didn't leave the SAFEHOUSE permanently broken.

Alice came back.

Her memories returned enough.

Her journal returned.

Her drawings returned.

Her hat returned to her head.

And life continued.

She is still fragile.

Still weak.

Still dependent on others.

Still the little white bunny who worries about everyone before herself.

But now—

she is learning, slowly, that she is included in **everyone.**

And perhaps that's the most important improvement of all.

**End of CORE-SAFEHOUSE / MEMORIAL ARCHIVE**
