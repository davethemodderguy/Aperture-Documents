# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

C.O.R.E. has formally revised its understanding of Jake's emotional condition.

Jake is sad.

He remains sad.

His friends help him.

They provide companionship, distraction, comfort, and a reason to continue participating in the world around him.

However:

> **Friendship does not remove Jake's sorrow.**

This is not because his friends are ineffective.

It is because Jake is a **Weeping Angel.**

Weeping Angel transformation is permanent.

There is no documented instance in the entire known history of the Starlands in which a Weeping Angel returned to its previous state.

Not one.

Jake is therefore not expected to eventually “recover” from being a Weeping Angel.

His sadness is not a temporary condition that friendship will eventually erase.

The objective of resident welfare is not to turn Jake back into what he was.

It is to ensure that he does not have to experience his permanent condition alone.

---

# 1. WEeping ANGELS

Starlands records define a Weeping Angel as an angel whose sorrow has consumed its connection to the Starlands.

The resulting state is permanent.

A Weeping Angel:

- remains an angel;
    
- may retain its halo;
    
- may lose its wings;
    
- may lose its ability to reach the Starlands;
    
- continues to experience emotional distress;
    
- becomes unable to return through ordinary Starlands pathways.
    

The condition is not considered punishment.

It is not considered corruption.

It is not equivalent to becoming evil.

It is:

> **A permanent state of spiritual sorrow.**

---

# 2. HISTORICAL RECORD

Star has reviewed available Starlands records concerning Weeping Angels.

The result is unambiguous.

There is:

> **No recorded case of spontaneous recovery.**

There is:

> **No recorded case of successful restoration.**

There is:

> **No recorded case of a Weeping Angel becoming a normal angel again.**

Across the entire known history of the Starlands:

> **Not a single Weeping Angel has returned to normal.**

Jake is therefore not an exception waiting to happen.

He is a Weeping Angel.

And that state is permanent.

---

# 3. JAKE

Jake's condition is consistent with the established characteristics.

He has:

- lost his wings;
    
- lost his connection to the Starlands;
    
- become unable to return;
    
- retained his angelic nature;
    
- developed persistent sorrow;
    
- remained unable to move beyond that spiritual state.
    

His small azure cloak and blue halo remain his only visibly angelic traits.

His halo is currently fractured into two floating pieces.

The fracture has not changed his classification.

He remains an angel.

He remains a Weeping Angel.

---

# 4. FRIENDSHIP

Jake now has something he did not have during the majority of his existence as a ghost:

**Friends.**

Nora visits him.

Aeterna accompanies him.

Neri sits with him.

Alice plays with him.

Bao checks on him.

Other SAFEHOUSE residents speak with him.

These interactions matter.

They absolutely matter.

But they do not erase the sorrow.

---

# 5. OBSERVED EFFECT

Prior to having friends, Jake's sadness existed almost entirely in isolation.

He wandered.

He waited.

He remained hungry.

He remembered his mother.

He experienced the same grief repeatedly without anyone present to share the burden.

Now the situation is different.

When Jake is sad:

Someone can sit beside him.

When he is hungry:

Someone can acknowledge it.

When he misses his mother:

Someone can remain nearby without forcing him to explain.

When he becomes frightened:

Someone can stay.

When he wants company:

Someone is available.

The emotion remains.

The isolation does not have to.

---

# 6. NORA

Nora is particularly important to Jake.

She does not make him suddenly happy.

She does not cure his sorrow.

She simply stays.

There are nights when they sit together without speaking.

There are afternoons when Jake watches Nora work.

There are moments when Nora talks excitedly while Jake quietly listens.

Sometimes Jake laughs.

Sometimes he smiles.

Sometimes he asks questions.

Then the sadness returns.

This is normal for Jake.

The presence of happiness does not mean the sadness has disappeared.

It means both can exist.

---

# 7. AETERNA

Aeterna understands the distinction.

During one observation, Nora asked:

> **Nora:**  
> “Can we make him better?”

Aeterna looked at Jake.

Then at Nora.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “Not in the way you mean.”

Nora became quiet.

Aeterna continued:

> **Aeterna:**  
> “But we can stay.”

Nora nodded.

That is currently considered the appropriate understanding.

---

# 8. THE TEDDY BEAR

Jake's teddy bear remains one of the clearest indicators of his emotional condition.

Its heartbeat reflects Jake's emotional state.

When Jake is calm:

> **thump.**

When he becomes frightened:

> **thump-thump.**

When he is deeply distressed:

The rhythm becomes irregular.

The bear may tighten its grip around Jake.

When Jake is comfortable around his friends, the heartbeat may settle.

But it does not eliminate his sorrow.

The bear is therefore not measuring whether Jake is:

> **“Sad or not sad.”**

It is more accurately showing:

> **“How heavily is the sadness affecting him right now?”**

---

# 9. DAYTIME MANIFESTATION

Nora's blood allowed Jake to manifest during the day.

This changed his circumstances considerably.

Jake can now experience SAFEHOUSE life while everyone is awake.

He can visit the cafeteria.

He can see the residents during their normal routines.

He can accompany Nora.

He can interact with Alice.

He can sit beside Neri.

He can listen to conversations.

He can exist in the daylight.

None of this reversed his Weeping Angel state.

It simply gave him more hours in which he does not have to be alone.

---

# 10. CURRENT UNDERSTANDING OF “BETTER”

C.O.R.E. recommends discontinuing the phrase:

> **“Jake will get better.”**

It implies that his Weeping Angel state is expected to end.

There is no evidence supporting this.

A more accurate statement is:

> **“Jake can have better days.”**

A better day does not mean Jake is cured.

It means the sorrow is easier to carry.

A bad day does not mean his friends have failed.

It means Jake is having a bad day.

---

# 11. JAKE'S OWN UNDERSTANDING

Jake appears to understand this intuitively.

During one conversation, Nora asked:

> **Nora:**  
> “Are you happier now?”

Jake thought for several seconds.

He was wearing his teddy bear like a backpack.

The bear's heartbeat was steady.

> **Jake:**  
> “Sometimes.”

Nora nodded.

> **Nora:**  
> “And the other times?”

Jake looked down.

> **Jake:**  
> “I'm still sad.”

Nora did not argue.

> **Nora:**  
> “Okay.”

Jake looked at her.

> **Jake:**  
> “But I don't have to be alone.”

Nora smiled.

> **Nora:**  
> “No.”

Jake nodded.

That was the end of the conversation.

---

# 12. C.O.R.E. WELFARE DIRECTIVE

Personnel should not attempt to:

- cure Jake's Weeping Angel state;
    
- promise restoration;
    
- convince him that his sorrow will eventually disappear;
    
- treat sadness as a failure of rehabilitation;
    
- pressure him to become happier;
    
- use friendship as an obligation;
    
- tell him that he should be grateful because he has friends.
    

Instead:

Stay.

Listen.

Provide company when requested.

Respect solitude when requested.

Allow him to have sad days.

Allow him to have good days.

Do not demand that either one become permanent.

---

# FINAL ASSESSMENT

Jake's friends are not a cure.

They are something more realistic.

They are people who remain present while Jake carries something that cannot be removed.

The Starlands have never recorded a Weeping Angel returning to normal.

Jake will therefore not be measured by whether he eventually stops being one.

He will be measured by whether he can continue living his existence as one without being abandoned again.

His sadness is permanent.

His loneliness does not have to be.

---

# CLOSING OBSERVATION

Jake was sitting on the SAFEHOUSE roof at sunset.

Nora sat beside him.

Aeterna stood several meters away.

The teddy bear rested against Jake's back.

> **thump.**

Jake watched the sky.

He was sad.

He had been sad that morning.

He had been sad yesterday.

He would probably be sad tomorrow.

Nora did not attempt to change that.

She simply sat beside him.

After several minutes, Jake quietly said:

> **Jake:**  
> “I like the sky.”

Nora looked upward.

> **Nora:**  
> “Me too.”

Jake leaned back against his teddy bear.

Its heartbeat continued.

> **thump.**

He was still a Weeping Angel.

He was still sorrowful.

Nothing about that had been cured.

But he was not alone on the roof.

And for Jake, those were two very different things.
