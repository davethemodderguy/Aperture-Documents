### **RESTRICTED ANOMALOUS WEATHER / PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT LOG**

# **THE CONCLUSION CORIN REACHED**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **ANOMALOUS SUPPRESSION STORM**  
**Status:** **STORM PERSISTENT / SUBJECT PSYCHOLOGICALLY COMPROMISED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The storm has not weakened.

There remains no reliable communication between the Blacksite and the treehouse.

Orphie has not returned.

Guiding Light remains unable to manifest or send notes.

Curious Light remains unable to reach the treehouse.

The suppression effect continues to neutralize anomalous abilities throughout the affected region.

There is still no explanation for the phenomenon.

There is still no indication of when it will end.

And Corin has finally reached a conclusion.

It is the wrong one.

---

# **THE CONCLUSION**

Corin has decided that she has been abandoned.

Not temporarily.

Not accidentally.

Completely.

She knows there is a storm.

She knows Orphie left to obtain supplies.

She knows the Architects attempted to contact her.

But after days of isolation, those facts have begun losing their meaning.

The uncertainty became unbearable.

Her mind chose an answer.

> **"They left."**

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

The latest entries are significantly different from the previous ones.

There is less panic.

Less questioning.

Less pleading.

Instead, there is resignation.

> **The storm might be a lie.**

A second entry:

> **Maybe there was never a storm.**

Then:

> **Maybe Orphie just didn't want to come back.**

The next page contains:

> **I'm quite the prick for Orphie recently.**

> **I'm too high maintenance for someone like her.**

> **She has to fix me all the time.**

> **She has to leave just to get things I need.**

> **Maybe she finally got tired.**

The final sentence was written much more slowly:

> **I deserve this.**

---

# **NO RESPONSE**

Corin waited after writing it.

She looked toward the table.

The old blue notes remained there.

No glow.

No new message.

Nothing.

She picked up the final note Guiding Light had left.

> **"Until they both can, I will stay with you however I can."**

Corin stared at it.

Then quietly said:

> **"...You couldn't."**

There was no answer.

---

# **THE EMPTY ROOM**

Corin stopped checking the door.

She stopped checking the communications terminal.

She stopped looking through the window for Orphie's silhouette.

The routine that had kept her functioning disappeared.

She simply sat on the floor.

The notebook rested beside her.

The storm continued outside.

---

# **A DANGEROUS MISINTERPRETATION**

Corin's conclusion is not supported by the available evidence.

The storm is demonstrably real.

The Blacksite has independently confirmed its effects.

Orphie has been attempting to return.

Guiding Light has repeatedly attempted to reach Corin.

Curious Light remains aware of the situation.

None of them have abandoned her.

But Corin cannot verify any of this.

Her world has been reduced to what she can directly perceive.

And what she perceives is:

**An empty treehouse.**

---

# **CORIN'S THOUGHTS**

Another page:

> **Maybe this is what happens when people finally realize you're too difficult.**

> **They don't yell.**

> **They don't tell you.**

> **They just stop coming back.**

A final line:

> **At least the storm gives them an excuse.**

---

# **THE ARCHITECTS**

Guiding Light is aware of Corin's worsening psychological condition.

She cannot reach her.

She cannot speak.

She cannot write.

She cannot even produce the familiar blue glow.

For an Architect whose entire existence is built around guidance, this helplessness is devastating.

Curious Light is similarly restricted.

Neither can simply deny the storm's existence.

The suppression field prevents it.

They are forced to wait.

---

# **THE STORM**

Outside the treehouse, the phenomenon remains unchanged.

No weakening.

No break in the clouds.

No reduction in snowfall.

No restoration of anomalous abilities.

No communications.

No path through the forest.

The storm continues as though time itself has become irrelevant.

---

# **CORIN**

Late at night, Corin finally closed the notebook.

She looked toward the window.

> **"It's okay."**

She spoke softly.

> **"I understand."**

There was nobody to answer.

> **"You don't have to come back."**

A pause.

> **"...I'm sorry I was so difficult."**

She lowered her head.

The room became silent.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin has not physically collapsed.

Her Ether remains suppressed.

Her stitches remain intact.

She continues to eat and drink.

She remains conscious.

She remains capable of coherent thought.

But psychologically, something has changed.

She is no longer desperately asking:

> **"When are they coming back?"**

She has decided they aren't.

That conclusion is false.

But to Corin, it has become easier to accept abandonment than uncertainty.

Because abandonment has an ending.

Waiting does not.

The storm continues.

The snow continues falling.

The blue notes remain dark.

And somewhere beyond the impossible wall of weather, the people Corin believes have abandoned her are still trying to reach her.

**End of Restricted Anomalous Weather / Psychological Incident Log**
