# 4. COMMUNICATION PRINCIPLE

Personnel should **not overact.**

Jane does not need a complicated fabricated story.

The cover works because the conversation is mundane.

A normal call might involve:

> “How are you?”

> “What did you have for dinner?”

> “How's everyone doing?”

> “Tell everyone I said hi.”

> “I'll call again later.”

These conversations establish a believable personal relationship.

Intelligence can then be exchanged naturally within the conversation.

The objective is not to make the call sound like an intelligence operation.

The objective is to make it sound **boringly familial.**
