# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following the first-day distribution of five **Spearheads**, the Tachyon Industries Merch Store experienced a significant increase in visitor traffic.

Neri determined that operating the store alone was becoming inefficient.

Nora agreed.

Together, the sisters began searching for personnel capable of assisting with:

- customer inquiries;
    
- manual sales;
    
- accessory catalog management;
    
- acquisition-trial information;
    
- inventory documentation;
    
- display maintenance;
    
- and general store operations.
    

The recruitment process has been considerably less successful than anticipated.

The primary reason:

> **Applicants keep trying to steal the merchandise displayed on the far wall.**

---

# 2. THE DISPLAY WALL

The rear wall of the Tachyon Merch Store contains secured displays of functioning Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise.

Current display inventory includes:

- **Night Stalker**
    
- **Sleight of Hand**
    
- **Ceberus**
    
- **FSR Deadbolt**
    
- **Deadeye**
    
- **Spearhead**
    

These displays are intentionally visible to visitors.

They are also intentionally **not self-service.**

The weapons remain secured behind the store's controlled display system.

Personnel may inspect them when authorized.

They may not simply remove them.

This distinction appears to be difficult for prospective employees to understand.

---

# 3. RECRUITMENT METHOD

Neri initially attempted a straightforward hiring process.

She placed a notice near the store entrance:

> **HELP WANTED**  
> **TACHYON INDUSTRIES MERCH STORE**  
> **Responsibilities: Customer Service, Documentation, Inventory**  
> **Ask Neri or Nora**

Nora added a handwritten note underneath:

> **“YOU GET TO SEE THE COOL GUNS.”**

Neri did not remove it.

Applications increased immediately.

So did attempted theft.

---

# 4. APPLICANT #01

The first applicant appeared enthusiastic and knowledgeable about Tachyon Industries products.

He correctly identified:

- the Night Stalker;
    
- the Sleight of Hand;
    
- the Ceberus;
    
- the Deadeye;
    
- and the FSR Deadbolt.
    

Neri was impressed.

She briefly left to retrieve employment paperwork.

When she returned:

The applicant was standing directly in front of the display wall.

One hand was reaching toward the Night Stalker.

Neri stared at him.

He froze.

> **Neri:** “What are you doing?”

> **Applicant:** “Looking.”

> **Neri:** “With your hand?”

He withdrew it.

Application rejected.

---

# 5. APPLICANT #02

Applicant #02 made it considerably farther.

They passed the initial interview.

They demonstrated familiarity with the catalog.

They correctly explained the difference between permanent Spearhead availability and limited-production Tachyon merchandise.

They were hired on a provisional basis.

Approximately fourteen minutes later, Nora found them attempting to open the display containing the Ceberus.

> **Nora:** “HEY.”

The applicant immediately stopped.

> **Applicant:** “I was just checking something.”

Nora:

> **“You're stealing.”**

> **Applicant:** “No.”

Nora pointed at the display.

> **“Your hand is inside.”**

The applicant withdrew their hand.

Employment terminated.

---

# 6. APPLICANT #03

Applicant #03 apparently believed the display weapons were decorative replicas.

This assumption was corrected.

They then asked whether they could hold the Night Stalker.

Neri explained that authorization was required.

The applicant nodded.

Five minutes later, they attempted to remove it anyway.

The display's security system prevented removal.

Neri approached.

> **Neri:** “No.”

The applicant:

> **“Okay.”**

Neri:

> **“You're fired.”**

---

# 7. APPLICANT #04

Applicant #04 never actually attempted to remove anything.

They simply stared at the display wall for several minutes.

Nora observed them.

> **Nora:** “What are you looking at?”

> **Applicant:** “The guns.”

> **Nora:** “You want one?”

> **Applicant:** “Yes.”

> **Nora:** “Then earn it.”

The applicant nodded.

They left the store.

They later registered for the Spearhead qualification simulation.

C.O.R.E. considers this applicant a successful recruitment outcome despite the fact that they were not hired.

---

# 8. APPLICANT #05

Applicant #05 made an especially poor decision.

They attempted to remove a **Sleight of Hand** from its display while Neri was standing approximately two meters away.

Neri watched.

Nora watched.

Neither said anything.

The applicant slowly pulled their hand back.

Neri:

> **“Were you trying to steal it?”**

Silence.

Neri:

> **“Okay.”**

The applicant:

> **“Okay?”**

Neri:

> **“You're not working here.”**

Applicant #05 left.

Nora followed them to the door.

> **“Bye.”**

---

# 9. THE REPEATING PATTERN

After several attempts, C.O.R.E. identified a consistent pattern.

Applicants generally behave normally until they see the display wall.

At that point, their thought process appears to become:

> **“That's a really cool weapon.”**

followed by:

> **“Can I touch it?”**

followed by:

> **“Probably not.”**

followed by:

> **“...What if I just take it?”**

This final step has eliminated the majority of otherwise promising applicants.

---

# 10. NORA'S INTERVIEW TECHNIQUE

Nora eventually developed her own method for screening candidates.

She points toward the display wall and asks:

> **“If you worked here, would you steal that?”**

If the applicant says:

> **“No.”**

Nora asks:

> **“Promise?”**

If they say:

> **“Yes.”**

Nora nods.

If they hesitate:

Nora immediately looks at Neri.

Neri then handles the rest.

Nora's screening method has proven surprisingly effective.

---

# 11. NERI'S INTERVIEW TECHNIQUE

Neri's method is considerably simpler.

She asks:

> **“Do you understand that the display weapons are not for sale?”**

If the applicant says yes:

> **“Good.”**

If the applicant asks whether they can steal one anyway:

> **“No.”**

If they attempt to steal one anyway:

> **“You're fired.”**

C.O.R.E. has determined that Neri's hiring standards are extremely straightforward.

---

# 12. WHY THE DISPLAY WALL IS SO TEMPTING

The display wall contains some of the rarest weapons in the Tachyon Industries catalog.

Several are individually awarded.

Some are permanently out of production.

Some have unique owners.

All are visually distinctive.

The presence of actual functioning merchandise therefore creates a natural temptation for visitors.

Neri refuses to replace the display with replicas.

Her reasoning:

> **“They're supposed to see the real ones.”**

She then added:

> **“Just don't take them.”**

---

# 13. SUCCESSFUL APPLICANTS

Despite the repeated failures, several candidates eventually passed.

Successful candidates demonstrated that they could:

- remain professional around the display wall;
    
- answer customer questions;
    
- handle documentation;
    
- distinguish display inventory from merchandise available for purchase;
    
- explain qualification requirements;
    
- and, most importantly:
    

> **Leave the weapons where they are.**

Nora considers this an important qualification.

> **“That's silly.”**

Neri:

> **“Yes.”**

> **“Can I take one?”**

> **“I'm joking.”**

---

# 15. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The Tachyon Industries Merch Store requires additional staff.

However, recruitment remains ongoing.

Candidates must understand that the store's purpose is to **sell, document, and manage access to Tachyon Industries products.**

It is not:

> **“A place where you can steal Tachyon Industries products.”**

The display wall is particularly important.

The merchandise displayed there is intended to demonstrate the quality and history of the Tachyon Industries catalog.

It is not a free inventory shelf.
