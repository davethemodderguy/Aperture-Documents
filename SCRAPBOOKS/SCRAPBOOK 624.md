### **TREEHOUSE / MISSING PERSON CONTINUATION REPORT**

# **ORPHIE — TWO-WEEK ABSENCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Time Missing:** **14 Days**  
**Last Known Location:** Treehouse vicinity  
**Status:** **MISSING**  
**Corin:** **Severe prolonged withdrawal**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Two weeks have passed since Orphie left the Treehouse to collect flowers.

She has not returned.

No confirmed sighting has been recorded.

No message has been received.

No reliable trace of her whereabouts has been discovered.

The search remains active.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

Corin has remained inside the Treehouse for the entire duration.

She still refuses to leave.

The door remains accessible.

The outside world remains only a few steps away.

Corin simply does not take them.

Her usual routine has deteriorated into a repetitive cycle:

> Wake.

> Check the entrance.

> Wait.

> Listen.

> Eat when reminded.

> Sleep.

> Wake.

> Check the entrance again.

---

# **CORIN'S WAITING**

Corin still behaves as though Orphie might return at any moment.

She has not accepted the possibility that Orphie may be gone permanently.

The flowers remain a particularly painful reminder.

The original trip was supposed to be simple.

Orphie was going to bring flowers back to the Treehouse.

Corin still occasionally looks toward the place where Orphie normally puts them.

Nothing has appeared.

---

# **FAMILY CONTACT**

Lumi continues visiting.

Arcturus and Norma continue checking on Corin.

The SAFEHOUSE residents continue providing food, water, company, and updates.

Corin generally does not leave her room to meet them.

Even when Lumi calls from outside:

> **"Corin, I'm here."**

The response is often:

> **"...I know."**

But she does not come out.

---

# **SEARCH STATUS**

The search has expanded significantly.

C.O.R.E. personnel and the Architects have examined:

- The Treehouse surroundings.
    
- Known travel routes.
    
- Dimensional pathways.
    
- Guiding Light rift activity.
    
- Anomalous locations.
    
- Possible displacement events.
    
- Orphie's known destinations.
    
- Environmental disturbances.
    
- Historical dimensional signatures.
    

No confirmed explanation has been established.

---

# **CORIN'S CONDITION**

Corin remains physically stable.

Her wings are healthy.

Her anomalous abilities remain functional.

She can still fly.

She can still transform.

She can still use her feathers.

She simply does not want to.

Her emotional state is considerably worse.

She spends long periods curled beneath her wings.

Sometimes she cries.

Sometimes she simply stares at the door.

---

# **THE QUESTION**

Several residents have tried asking Corin the same question:

> **"What do you need?"**

Her answer has remained consistent.

> **"...Orphie."**

Nothing else seems to matter.

---

# **A SMALL ROUTINE**

Every evening, Corin places a small light-azure feather lantern near the entrance.

She then sits beside it.

The flame burns quietly.

The Treehouse remains illuminated by its soft blue glow.

Corin watches the door.

She does not know whether Orphie will return tonight.

She waits anyway.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Fourteen days.

No Orphie.

Corin still waits.

She has stopped asking when Orphie will come back.

Instead, she simply watches the door.

Perhaps because asking the question would require admitting that nobody knows.

Tonight, the Treehouse is quiet.

A small blue feather burns beside the entrance.

Corin is curled next to it, wings wrapped around herself.

Her eyes remain fixed on the doorway.

Waiting for the sound of footsteps that have not come for two weeks.
