# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has returned.

The Nullwalker state has been successfully reversed.

The solution was identified through Aeterna's sword.

Contrary to the initial assumption that the sword was unsuitable because of its ability to repurpose the existential purpose of anything it strikes, that same property proved to be the mechanism required to free Jake.

The sword did not destroy Jake.

It did not destroy the Nullwalker.

It repurposed the **purpose of the Null Shell itself**.

The shell ceased existing as a container for Jake.

Jake was released.

His Humanized Star manifestation returned.

His stellar core reactivated.

His teddy bear began glowing again.

And Jake woke up.

---

# 1. THE FINAL ATTEMPT

Guiding Light, Curious Light, and Aeterna prepared the restoration attempt.

The residents were instructed to remain outside the exposure zone.

Jake remained in the center of the circular clearing.

The Null Shell remained motionless.

Black.

Silent.

Unresponsive.

Aeterna stood several meters away.

Her sword was drawn.

For once, she did not treat it as a weapon.

She treated it as a key.

---

# 2. GUIDING LIGHT'S INSTRUCTION

Guiding Light looked at Aeterna.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Don't destroy him.”

Aeterna nodded.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “I know.”

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Don't destroy the shell either.”

Aeterna looked toward Jake.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Then what do I do?”

Guiding Light answered:

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Change what it exists to do.”

Aeterna understood.

Her sword was capable of doing exactly that.

---

# 3. THE NULL SHELL

The Nullwalker remained completely still.

The black surface surrounding Jake produced the same anomalous radiation-like effect.

The air around it distorted slightly.

The grass near the circular boundary had begun dying.

Aeterna raised her sword.

Nora immediately grabbed Bao's hand.

Neri watched silently.

Anby stared at Jake.

Nobody spoke.

---

# 4. THE STRIKE

Aeterna moved.

The sword made contact with the Null Shell.

There was no explosion.

No conventional impact.

No visible destruction.

Instead—

The black surface **stopped.**

For a fraction of a second, everything became completely silent.

Then the sword's energy began spreading through the shell.

Not destroying it.

**Changing it.**

The Null Shell's purpose was being rewritten.

It had existed to contain what remained of a forgotten star.

Aeterna's sword gave that existence a different purpose.

> **Release.**

The shell cracked.

---

# 5. THE BREAK

A thin line of light appeared across the black surface.

Then another.

Then dozens.

Azure light began leaking through the cracks.

Guiding Light's eyes widened.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “He's waking up.”

The Null Shell fractured.

The black material dissolved outward.

It did not fall apart like a physical object.

It simply ceased to have a reason to remain.

The darkness vanished.

---

# 6. JAKE

Inside the shell was Jake.

Small.

Cold.

Exhausted.

His white hair was visible again.

His azure eyes were closed.

His body was no longer black.

His wings were present.

His clothes had returned.

The celestial glow surrounding him was extremely faint.

The teddy bear remained beside him.

Its core suddenly flickered.

Once.

Twice.

Then—

> **THUMP.**

A heartbeat.

---

# 7. THE HEART

The teddy bear's core reignited.

A small azure glow appeared.

Then another heartbeat.

> **THUMP.**

The glow became brighter.

> **THUMP.**

Jake's stellar energy began returning.

The shooting-star patterns appeared within his eyes despite his eyelids remaining closed.

The anomalous radiation-like field disappeared.

The grass stopped dying.

The air stabilized.

The circle ceased being an exclusion zone.

Jake's existence was no longer actively damaging the environment.

---

# 8. JAKE WAKES

Jake's fingers moved.

Bao immediately stood.

> **BAO:**  
> “Jake?”

His eyes opened.

Azure.

Bright.

Confused.

He looked around.

The first thing he saw was everyone.

Dozens of people.

Standing around him.

Jake blinked.

He looked at Bao.

Then Neri.

Then Nora.

Then Aeterna.

Then the others.

His voice was weak.

Almost inaudible.

But it worked.

> **JAKE:**  
> “...Mom?”

Bao broke.

She immediately ran toward him.

Guiding Light did not stop her.

---

Bao dropped to her knees beside Jake.

Jake barely had enough strength to hold the teddy bear.

Bao held him carefully.

> **BAO:**  
> “I'm here.”

Jake's eyes remained half-open.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I thought...”

He stopped.

His voice was damaged.

Bao held him tighter.

> **BAO:**  
> “You're home.”

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> “...Okay.”

Then he fell asleep.

Bao immediately checked his breathing.

Stable.
