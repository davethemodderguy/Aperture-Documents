# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL ADDENDUM**

## **SUBJECT: Z-314-S "STAR" – CUMULATIVE EMOTIONAL STRESS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – Executive & Medical Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

This addendum supplements Star's existing psychological profile following review of several emotionally significant incidents involving Corin Lockwood.

Psychological Services emphasize that no single event caused Star's increasing emotional burden.

Rather, numerous events accumulated over time.

Together, they overwhelmed coping strategies that had remained effective for years.

---

# **THE BIRTHDAY INCIDENT**

The event most frequently referenced during interviews is the cancelled birthday gathering held for Corin Lockwood at Shelter T-01.

The celebration was intended to surprise Corin.

Instead, attendees discovered her unconscious following a medical emergency.

After receiving treatment, Corin openly stated that she hated birthdays.

She further expressed that she did not deserve to have one.

Multiple witnesses reported prolonged silence following those statements.

For Star...

The moment proved especially difficult.

---

# **PERSONAL SIGNIFICANCE**

To Star, birthdays have always represented something simple.

Proof that a person's life matters.

An opportunity to celebrate that they are still here.

Corin's rejection of that idea directly conflicted with one of Star's deepest personal beliefs.

Following the incident, Star privately remarked:

> "I can't believe someone would hate their own birthday."

After a long pause, she quietly continued,

> "...Why?"

No one present had an answer that eased her heart.

---

# **CORIN & COREY**

The later manifestation of Corin and Corey brought relief to many personnel.

Star was genuinely happy to see both sisters finding stability together.

However, the event also carried an unintended emotional consequence.

To Star...

Corey represented many of the qualities Corin might have enjoyed throughout her life had the childhood abuse never occurred.

Every joyful conversation between the twins reminded Star of what had been taken from Corin long before anyone at the Blacksite met her.

She never blamed Corey.

Nor did she pity Corin.

Instead, she mourned the years that could never be restored.

---

# **HER MOTHER**

Star eventually achieved the goal she had pursued since childhood.

An official review completely cleared her mother's name.

The historical record was corrected.

The accusation was withdrawn.

The injustice was acknowledged.

The truth was finally recorded.

Star attended the ceremony confirming the decision.

Witnesses described her as smiling quietly throughout.

Afterward, she stood alone before her mother's memorial for nearly an hour.

When asked how she felt, she answered:

> "...I'm happy."

She paused.

Then looked down.

> "...But she's still gone."

Psychological Services noted that this marked the first time Star openly acknowledged the distinction between **justice** and **restoration**.

Justice had been achieved.

The loss remained.

---

# **CUMULATIVE EFFECT**

Medical Services believe these experiences gradually reinforced the same painful realization.

Some things can be made right.

Some things cannot be undone.

Corin's childhood cannot be returned.

Star's mother cannot come home.

The past can be understood.

It cannot be rewritten.

For someone whose identity is built around giving hope...

Accepting that reality has proven exceptionally difficult.

---

# **NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade reviewed this report before its final submission.

She requested only one amendment.

The following statement was appended without revision.

> "Hope is not measured by whether every tragedy is reversed."

> "It is measured by whether someone still chooses to stand afterward."

Nightshade declined further comment.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Star continues carrying out her duties as the Aegis of Light.

She still celebrates birthdays.

She still encourages others.

She still fills Project SAFEHOUSE with laughter.

Yet those closest to her now understand something they did not before.

Even the Bringer of Hope sometimes needs someone to remind her that hope is not the same as certainty.

Some wounds heal.

Some losses remain.

And even when justice finally arrives...

It cannot always return what was taken.

**End of Psychological Addendum**
