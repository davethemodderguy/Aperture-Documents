**SUBJECT:** Jake — Teddy Bear Sensory Synchronization

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A previously undocumented property of Jake's teddy bear has been identified.

Jake's teddy bear has developed a **direct sensory connection to Jake.**

Anything physically experienced by the teddy bear is simultaneously experienced by Jake.

The effect appears to operate in real time.

The bear does not appear to have developed independent consciousness.

Rather:

> **The bear's sensory input has become Jake's sensory input.**

This includes tactile sensation, impact, pressure, temperature, and contact with surrounding materials.

The discovery occurred accidentally.

---

# 1. INITIAL INCIDENT

Jake was carrying his teddy bear by one hand while walking through his room.

He dropped it.

The bear landed on the grass covering the floor.

Jake immediately stopped.

He felt something.

Specifically:

> **A slight impact against his chest.**

Jake looked down.

Nothing had struck him.

He then noticed another sensation.

Grass.

It felt as though grass had briefly covered his body.

Jake looked at the teddy bear.

The teddy bear was lying face-down in the grass.

Jake stared at it.

Then picked it up.

---

# 2. THE EXPERIMENT

Jake patted the teddy bear's head.

He immediately felt the sensation of being patted on his own head.

Jake froze.

He slowly looked upward.

Nobody was touching him.

He looked at the bear.

He patted its head again.

The sensation repeated.

Jake stared at the bear for several seconds.

Then:

> **JAKE:**  
> "Oh."

This was followed by approximately thirty seconds of silent experimentation.

Jake gently pressed the bear's paw.

He felt pressure against his own hand.

He placed the bear against the grass.

He felt grass against his body.

He lifted the bear.

The sensation disappeared.

Jake had identified a sensory link.

---

# 3. NOTIFICATION OF PERSONNEL

Jake subsequently left his room and informed several residents.

The order was:

**Clover.**

**Bao.**

**Neko.**

**Neri.**

**Nora.**

**Alice.**

Each received essentially the same explanation.

> **JAKE:**  
> "The bear can feel things."

Pause.

> **JAKE:**  
> "And I feel them."

Clover stared at the bear.

Then Jake.

Then the bear again.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "...Everything?"

Jake shrugged.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I think."

---

# 4. BAO'S RESPONSE

Bao immediately became concerned.

> **BAO:**  
> "Jake, does it hurt?"

Jake thought about it.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I don't know."

Bao carefully touched the teddy bear.

Jake felt the contact.

Bao stopped.

> **BAO:**  
> "Okay."

She gently picked up the bear.

> **BAO:**  
> "Then we're going to be careful with him."

Jake nodded.

This appeared to be the correct answer.

---

# 5. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri examined the teddy bear carefully.

She touched one paw.

Jake looked at his own paw.

Then she touched the bear's ear.

Jake touched his ear.

Neri understood.

She looked at Jake.

Then the bear.

Then Jake again.

> **NERI:**  
> "You share senses."

Jake nodded.

Neri considered this.

> **NERI:**  
> "Don't drop him."

Jake nodded again.

---

# 6. NORA'S RESPONSE

Nora was fascinated.

She gently poked the bear.

Jake jumped.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Hey."

Nora immediately withdrew her hand.

> **NORA:**  
> "Sorry!"

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "It's okay."

Nora looked at the bear.

> **NORA:**  
> "So if I hug him..."

She hugged the teddy bear.

Jake immediately felt the hug.

His expression softened.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Yeah."

Nora smiled.

> **NORA:**  
> "That's useful."

Jake hugged the bear back.

---

# 7. ALICE'S RESPONSE

Alice approached the bear.

She stared at it.

Then at Jake.

Then back at the bear.

She very carefully poked it.

Jake felt the poke.

Alice's eyes widened.

She poked it again.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Alice."

She stopped.

> **ALICE:**  
> "Sorry."

Pause.

She poked it one more time.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Alice."

> **ALICE:**  
> "...Sorry."

---

# 8. NEKO'S ANALYSIS

Neko performed the most restrained examination.

He did not attempt to dissect the bear.

He did not attempt to separate the bear from Jake.

He did not attempt to reproduce the phenomenon.

Instead, he documented the observations.

### Current confirmed sensory categories:

- touch;
    
- pressure;
    
- impact;
    
- temperature;
    
- surface contact;
    
- movement-related physical sensation.
    

The exact upper limit remains unknown.

Neko believes the connection may extend to essentially all physical sensations experienced by the bear.

He emphasized that this remains theoretical until tested safely.

---

# 9. THE RAIN SCENARIO

A significant concern was immediately identified.

The bear is frequently carried outside.

If the bear becomes wet:

**Jake feels the wetness.**

If the bear becomes cold:

**Jake feels the cold.**

If the bear is placed on rough ground:

**Jake feels the contact.**

If someone accidentally drops the bear:

**Jake feels the impact.**

And if someone were to throw the bear against a wall—

Neko stopped writing.

> **NEKO:**  
> "Nobody is testing that."

Everyone agreed.

---

# 10. RESIDENT WELFARE IMPLICATION

The teddy bear has historically been Jake's primary comfort object.

It is also his cardiac vessel.

It is now additionally functioning as a **remote sensory extension of Jake's body.**

This creates a new welfare concern.

Personnel are instructed:

> **Treat the teddy bear as though Jake is physically holding it.**

Do not:

- throw it;
    
- kick it;
    
- drop it deliberately;
    
- expose it to dangerous temperatures;
    
- place it somewhere hazardous;
    
- use it as a test object;
    
- or perform "experiments" without authorization.
    

The bear is not disposable.

Neither is Jake.

---

# 11. JAKE'S RESPONSE

After everyone finished discussing the implications, Jake picked up his teddy bear.

He held it against his chest.

He could feel its fabric against himself.

The bear's heartbeat continued.

Jake smiled faintly.

> **JAKE:**  
> "At least I know when he's okay."

Bao looked at him.

> **BAO:**  
> "And he knows when you're okay."

Jake nodded.

He hugged the bear slightly tighter.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The teddy bear has always been important to Jake.

It carries his heartbeat.

It has survived being lost.

It has survived damage.

It has been repaired.

It has remained beside him through some of the worst moments of his existence.

Now Jake can feel it.

Every pat.

Every hug.

Every patch of grass.

Every raindrop.

Every accidental bump.

Every impact.

Which means that on a rainy day, if someone were to carelessly throw the bear against a wall—

Jake would feel it too.

The appropriate response to this information is therefore extremely simple:

**Do not throw the bear against a wall.**

**Seriously.**
