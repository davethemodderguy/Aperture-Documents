# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – OPERATIONAL STATUS UPDATE**

### **SUBJECT: MART PUBLIC INTEREST & AMBASSADOR LOCATIONS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal Personnel

---

# **PUBLIC INTEREST UPDATE**

Several months after the introduction of the Mart Outreach Program, the initial surge of visitors to the Krampus Compliance Authority gradually subsided.

Customer Services metrics have returned to expected levels.

Current observations indicate:

- Most citizens once again prefer contacting the KCA by telephone.
    
- Walk-in traffic has normalized.
    
- Mart-related visits have become uncommon.
    

Researchers believe the novelty simply wore off.

The Marts themselves appear completely unconcerned.

Whether helping ten visitors or none at all, their daily routine remains unchanged.

As one Mart put it:

> "Friend."

Research concluded that, to a Mart, helping one person is just as worthwhile as helping one hundred.

---

# **KCA AMBASSADOR**

The KCA Ambassador continues to maintain its unofficial office beside Dialyn's desk.

Although visitor traffic has decreased, the Ambassador still assists with:

- Greeting guests.
    
- Answering simple questions.
    
- Calming anxious visitors.
    
- Keeping Dialyn company during long shifts.
    

When the phone rings, the Ambassador simply smiles and quietly returns to organizing paperwork until Dialyn finishes the call.

---

# **BLACKSITE AMBASSADOR**

Contrary to expectations, the Blacksite Ambassador has **not** established its office within the Mart Nest.

Instead, it has permanently settled inside:

> **Project SAFEHOUSE**

More specifically...

The Ambassador spends nearly all of its time alongside **Mart**, the Owl Thiren.

No official assignment exists.

No explanation has ever been requested.

The arrangement simply became normal.

---

# **THE SAFEHOUSE OFFICE**

The Ambassador has claimed a small corner of the SAFEHOUSE common room.

The area now contains:

- A small desk.
    
- A stack of books.
    
- Several miniature chairs built specifically for Marts.
    
- A bowl of sugary cheese crackers.
    
- An oversized chair frequently occupied by Mart.
    

The Ambassador can usually be found reading while Mart works nearby.

Occasionally, one will glance at the other.

Neither says anything.

The silence appears comfortable.

---

# **WHY MART?**

Researchers eventually asked the Ambassador why it preferred staying near Mart instead of leading the colony from the Nest.

The Ambassador answered simply:

> "He listens."

After a moment, it added:

> "He talks to us."

Another pause.

> "...He's family."

No further explanation was given.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The excitement surrounding the Marts has settled into everyday life.

The phones at the KCA ring as they always have.

Dialyn answers them with the same calm professionalism.

The Marts continue helping wherever they are needed.

At the Blacksite, the Ambassador still spends each day at Project SAFEHOUSE, not because it was ordered to, but because that's where it feels it belongs.

When asked if it planned to move into the Mart Nest someday, it simply smiled.

> "Home is where friends are."
