# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **MEMORY STABILITY INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – TEMPORAL MEMORY DISPLACEMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following Corin Lockwood's failed return to Heaven's Blacksite, Medical Services identified a new complication in her recovery.

Corin's memories of her family and the people she met after the operation have become increasingly fragmented.

The issue is not a complete loss of memory.

Rather, certain periods of her life feel distant, disconnected, or unreal.

The most affected memories are those involving:

- Norma Lockwood.
    
- Arcturus Lockwood.
    
- The residents of Project SAFEHOUSE.
    
- Her life after the Ether experimentation.
    

---

# **THE MEMORY GAP**

C.O.R.E. researchers believe the cause is connected to the unusual circumstances surrounding Corin's life.

A significant portion of her childhood memories are preserved.

The tragedy is preserved.

The operation is preserved.

But the years afterward are inconsistent.

Corin remembers events.

She remembers names.

She remembers facts.

Yet the emotional connection attached to those memories feels weakened.

As she described it:

> "...I know they matter."

> "...I know I love them."

> "...But sometimes it feels like I'm remembering someone else's life."

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

The discovery deeply distressed Corin.

For someone already afraid of losing everything, realizing that memories were becoming unclear felt like another loss.

She attempted repeatedly to force herself to remember.

She looked through old records.

Photographs.

Reports.

Family documents.

Anything that could reconnect the missing pieces.

During moments of extreme frustration, staff observed Corin becoming overwhelmed and struggling to regulate her emotions.

Medical personnel intervened to ensure she remained safe and supported during these episodes.

---

# **LUMI'S ROLE**

For the first time since her return, Lumi admitted something painful.

There were things she could not fix.

She could remind Corin.

She could tell stories.

She could hold her hand.

She could stay beside her.

But she could not force memories to return.

Fifteen years had passed since Lumia Lockwood's death.

Fifteen years since Corin had heard her mother's voice.

Fifteen years since the family had been whole.

Lumi remembered.

Corin did not always.

And that hurt both of them.

---

# **RECORDED CONVERSATION**

During one evening inside Corin's office, Corin looked through an old family photograph.

She pointed toward Arcturus.

> "...I know him."

A pause.

> "...I know he's my brother."

She looked toward Norma.

> "...I know she's my sister."

Her voice became quieter.

> "...Why does it feel like I'm meeting them for the first time?"

Lumi sat beside her.

For once...

She had no answer.

She only reached out and held Corin's hand.

---

# **LUMI'S STATEMENT**

Later, when asked about the incident, Lumi said:

> "I spent so long trying to bring her back to everyone."

> "I forgot that she was still trying to find herself."

She looked toward Corin's office door.

> "She doesn't need someone telling her who she was."

> "She needs someone to remind her that she is still here."

---

# **NORMA AND ARCTURUS**

Neither sibling was informed immediately of the full extent of Corin's memory difficulties.

Lumi requested patience.

Not because she wanted to hide the truth.

Because she knew how much Corin feared disappointing them.

Arcturus' response after learning was simple.

> "Then we'll introduce ourselves again."

Norma responded:

> "As many times as she needs."

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the report.

His note was brief.

> "Memory is not the only foundation of a relationship."

He continued:

> "A person is not erased because they forget."

> "And a family is not gone because someone needs to find their way back."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin remains under active medical observation.

Lumi continues staying with her, not attempting to force memories back, but providing consistent support.

The focus has shifted.

Not recovering every lost moment.

Not recreating the past.

But helping Corin build a present where she does not feel alone.

For Lumi, the hardest realization was not that Corin forgot.

It was understanding that love could not always restore what was lost.

Sometimes...

Love could only stay beside someone while they searched for it again.

**End of Memory Stability Incident Report**
