## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Mia has received her replacement left eye from Ronova.

The exchange has been completed.

However, the replacement is considerably more sophisticated than a conventional prosthetic. Ronova provided Mia with a slightly blessed version of the eye she surrendered as payment for Sora's recovery.

The replacement retains the essential functionality of an ordinary eye while possessing two additional anomalous properties:

- Mia can change its iris color at will.
    
- Mia can change its pupil shape at will.
    

The replacement is therefore both functional and customizable.

The intervention also attracted the attention of three Architects:

- Guiding Light.
    
- Curious Light.
    
- Mischievous Light.
    

All three reportedly reprimanded Ronova for accepting a six-year-old child's eye as payment.

Ronova was not particularly successful in defending herself.

The incident has been informally described by personnel as _the three Architects scolding their little sister_.

C.O.R.E. considers the replacement a successful resolution of the immediate physical consequence of the agreement, while noting that its anomalous properties require routine observation.

## 2. THE REPLACEMENT

Ronova fulfilled her promise.

Mia received a replacement for her surrendered left eye.

The replacement resembles her original eye closely enough to preserve her appearance, although its blessing grants her control over certain visual characteristics.

Mia can change the iris color and pupil shape whenever she wants.

The changes are voluntary.

No evidence currently indicates that the eye changes independently of her wishes.

### Initial demonstration

**RONOVA:** There. All done.

**MIA:** That's my new eye?

**RONOVA:** A slightly blessed version of the one you gave me.

**MIA:** Slightly blessed?

**RONOVA:** Don't make me explain the terminology.

Mia blinked.

**MIA:** I can see!

**RONOVA:** Of course you can. I told you it would be usable.

**MIA:** Both eyes?

**RONOVA:** Both eyes.

Mia looked around the room.

Then she noticed something.

**MIA:** Wait.

Her left iris changed color.

**MIA:** Oh!

It changed again.

**MIA:** I can change it!

**RONOVA:** Yes.

Mia concentrated.

Her pupil changed shape.

She gasped.

**MIA:** RONOVA!

**RONOVA:** I know. It's quite convenient.

**MIA:** LOOK!

**RONOVA:** I'm looking.

**MIA:** IT CHANGED!

**RONOVA:** Yes, Mia. That's what I just explained.

Mia giggled.

**MIA:** This is so cool!

Ronova smiled.

**RONOVA:** I'm glad you like it.

## 3. THE THREE ARCHITECTS ARRIVE

The replacement might have been accepted without further incident.

Unfortunately for Ronova, the other Architects found out.

Guiding Light, Curious Light, and Mischievous Light were not particularly pleased with the arrangement.

Their objection was straightforward.

Ronova had accepted the eye of a six-year-old child as payment.

The fact that she had provided a replacement did not make the original agreement any less questionable.

### Reconstructed conversation

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Ronova.

**RONOVA:** Oh. Hello, big sister.

**CURIOUS LIGHT:** We heard about the agreement.

**RONOVA:** Which one?

**MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT:** The one involving a six-year-old's eye.

Ronova paused.

**RONOVA:** Ah.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** You accepted Mia's eye?

**RONOVA:** It was the agreed price.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** She is six.

**RONOVA:** I know.

**CURIOUS LIGHT:** You know, and you still accepted it?

**RONOVA:** She wanted Sora back.

**MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT:** That is not a defense!

**RONOVA:** I found him, didn't I?

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Ronova.

**RONOVA:** ...

**GUIDING LIGHT:** You cannot treat a child's desperation as an ordinary negotiating position.

**RONOVA:** I gave her a replacement.

**CURIOUS LIGHT:** A replacement does not retroactively make the agreement appropriate.

**MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT:** You could have asked for something else!

**RONOVA:** Like what?

**MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT:** I don't know! Something that doesn't involve taking a child's body parts!

**RONOVA:** Well, that's rather specific.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Ronova.

**RONOVA:** Yes?

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Stop trying to be clever.

Ronova looked away.

**RONOVA:** ...

**CURIOUS LIGHT:** And what was the other condition?

**RONOVA:** They have to visit me a few times every week.

**MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT:** That's fine.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** That's not the problem.

**RONOVA:** I thought you would appreciate that I'm getting visitors.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** We do. But you cannot make children feel responsible for your loneliness.

Ronova's expression softened slightly.

**RONOVA:** I didn't mean it that way.

**CURIOUS LIGHT:** Then make sure they understand that they are welcome, not obligated to sacrifice their childhood for you.

**RONOVA:** ...

**MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT:** And next time you need something, ask us.

**RONOVA:** You're not going to give me an eye.

**MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT:** THAT IS NOT THE POINT.

Mischievous Light's voice reportedly echoed through several archival aisles.

Mia, who had been watching from behind Guiding Light, quietly raised her hand.

**MIA:** Um...

All three Architects turned toward her.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Yes, sweetheart?

**MIA:** I like my new eye.

A pause.

**CURIOUS LIGHT:** We're glad.

**MIA:** And I can see Sora.

She turned toward her brother.

**MIA:** That's what matters.

Guiding Light knelt beside her.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** I'm glad you're together again, little one. But you must never think that you need to give up parts of yourself to deserve having someone you love.

Mia nodded.

**MIA:** Okay.

Ronova watched the exchange quietly.

**MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT:** And you.

**RONOVA:** Yes?

**MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT:** We're not finished talking.

**RONOVA:** I figured.

## 5. THE SECOND CONDITION — VISITORS WELCOME

The requirement that Mia and Sora visit Ronova several times a week remains in effect as an arrangement between the parties.

However, following the Architects' intervention, the nature of the visits has been clarified.

They are to be treated as social visits.

Mia and Sora are welcome to visit.

They may bring Jake and Tom when appropriate.

Mischievous Light may personally transport them to the Archives.

The children are not responsible for maintaining Ronova's emotional well-being, and missing a visit must not be treated as a breach of affection or a moral failure.

## 6. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The replacement eye is functioning as intended according to the demonstrated capabilities.

The intervention has also revealed an important concern regarding Ronova's judgment.

Her work may involve forgotten memories, but her agreements still affect living individuals.

The Architects' reprimand was justified.

C.O.R.E. particularly notes Guiding Light's statement:

> "You must never think that you need to give up parts of yourself to deserve having someone you love."

This is to be treated as the central welfare principle of the incident.

Mia's willingness to sacrifice her eye was motivated by her desire to recover Sora. It must not become an expectation that she should endure further harm to preserve their reunion.

Likewise, Ronova's loneliness is a legitimate concern, but companionship should be freely given.

The arrangement can continue in a healthy manner provided these boundaries are respected.
