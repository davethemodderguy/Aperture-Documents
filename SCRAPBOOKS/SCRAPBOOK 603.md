### **SAFEHOUSE PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — EMOTIONAL DECLINE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE  
**Status:** **Severe Emotional Distress / Wing Regeneration**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's physical condition continues to stabilize.

Her emotional condition does not.

The prolonged pain, inability to fly, and memories associated with her past have caused Corin to become increasingly overwhelmed by:

- **Sadness**
    
- **Depression**
    
- **Resentment**
    
- **Frustration**
    
- **Exhaustion**
    
- **Helplessness**
    

She rarely expresses these feelings outwardly.

Instead, she becomes quieter.

---

# **THE SILENCE**

Corin spends long periods sitting alone even when other people are present.

She does not necessarily want to be alone.

She simply does not know what to say.

Her usual little meows have become infrequent.

Her wings remain folded.

Her eyes are often lowered.

Sometimes she simply stares at the floor.

---

# **RESENTMENT**

The resentment is difficult to identify.

Corin does not appear to resent any particular resident.

She does not blame Belle.

She does not blame Orphie.

She does not blame the people caring for her.

The resentment seems directed toward her circumstances.

Toward her own body.

Toward the pain.

Toward the fact that something she relied upon—her wings—was taken away again.

And perhaps most painfully:

Toward the past that she had spent so much effort trying to escape.

---

# **LOSS OF FLIGHT**

Corin's inability to fly has had a profound psychological impact.

Flying was more than an ability.

It was her escape.

When she flew, she could stop thinking about everything else.

The wind gave her something to focus on.

The sky gave her somewhere to go.

Now her injured wing keeps her grounded.

The sky remains visible.

She cannot reach it.

---

# **ORPHIE'S ATTEMPTS**

Orphie remains close.

She tries to comfort Corin.

Sometimes she succeeds for a few minutes.

Sometimes Corin leans against her.

Sometimes she cries into Orphie's shoulder.

But the sadness always returns.

Orphie cannot simply erase years of memories.

She cannot heal emotional wounds as easily as Corin's regeneration heals physical ones.

---

# **BELLE'S OBSERVATION**

Belle continues caring for Corin.

She notices that Corin sometimes touches her bandaged wing absentmindedly.

Not because she expects it to heal immediately.

Almost as though she is checking that it is still there.

Belle never tells her to stop.

She simply sits beside her.

---

# **ARCHITECT ASSESSMENT**

The Architects have determined that Corin's current emotional state is not merely a reaction to physical pain.

The injury has removed one of her most reliable coping mechanisms.

The resulting isolation from flight has allowed previously suppressed memories and emotions to return.

Guiding Light's assessment was brief:

> **"She is hurting in places regeneration cannot reach."**

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin is surrounded by people who care about her.

She is not abandoned.

She is not forgotten.

But sometimes being surrounded by people is not enough to silence what is happening inside someone's mind.

Corin sits quietly beneath the Treehouse's lights.

Her injured wing rests beside her.

Her other wing remains folded around her body.

She looks toward the window.

The sky is still there.

She cannot fly.

And for the first time since her recovery, Corin begins to wonder whether getting stronger will ever be enough to escape the things that hurt her.
