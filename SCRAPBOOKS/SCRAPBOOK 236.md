## **SUBJECT: DIALYN**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **EMPLOYEE OVERVIEW**

**Preferred Name:** Dialyn

**Legal Name:** Unknown

**Position:** Customer Support Specialist

**Department:** Public Relations & Customer Support

**Employment Status:** Full-Time

---

# **BACKGROUND**

Despite the Krampus Compliance Authority processing thousands of inquiries each month, the entire Customer Support Department consists of a single employee.

That employee is Dialyn.

When asked where her name came from, she once laughed and replied:

> "...Because everyone has to dial in eventually."

Whether this is actually the origin of her name remains unknown.

Her legal name has never appeared on any internal records.

She has never corrected anyone for calling her Dialyn.

Eventually, everyone simply accepted that as her name.

---

# **APPEARANCE**

Dialyn is immediately recognizable by her distinctive hairstyle.

Her long braided hair is evenly divided into two contrasting halves.

- Right side — Black.
    
- Left side — White.
    

The symmetry is remarkably precise.

She is also almost always seen wearing high heels regardless of the situation.

Facilities Management has repeatedly questioned how she walks so quickly in them.

No satisfactory explanation has been provided.

---

# **PERSONALITY**

Dialyn is widely regarded as one of the most energetic employees within the Authority.

She enthusiastically greets nearly everyone entering the office.

She somehow remembers the names of nearly every recurring caller.

She has developed a reputation for resolving problems before they become complaints.

When not occupied with customer support duties, she frequently checks on senior staff.

This includes ensuring they have:

- Food.
    
- Coffee.
    
- Water.
    
- Reasonable working hours.
    

Success regarding the final point remains limited.

---

# **RELATION TO THE LOCKWOODS**

Numerous employees have speculated that Dialyn is distantly related to the Lockwood family.

The rumor originated due to similarities in appearance and several undocumented family anecdotes.

No evidence has confirmed the claim.

No evidence has disproved it either.

When asked directly, Dialyn simply smiles.

> "...Maybe."

She has never elaborated.

---

# **ANOMALOUS EQUIPMENT**

Dialyn carries a circular device resembling a hula hoop approximately fifty centimeters in diameter.

Research Division has designated the object:

> **Portal Hoop**

The device normally functions as a single ring.

Upon activation, it duplicates into two identical rings connected through an extradimensional portal.

Objects entering one ring emerge from the other regardless of distance or orientation.

The connected portals preserve momentum.

The exit portal can also enlarge whatever passes through it.

For example:

A normal kick through one ring may result in an enormous foot emerging from the other portal while retaining the original motion.

Engineering continues to describe the underlying mechanics as:

> "Completely unreasonable."

---

# **AUTHORIZED USES**

The Portal Hoop has proven useful for:

- Moving oversized cargo.
    
- Maintenance in difficult-to-reach locations.
    
- Delivering supplies between offices.
    
- Emergency rescue operations.
    
- Demonstrations during safety training.
    

Unauthorized practical jokes remain prohibited.

# **NOTABLE INCIDENT**

During a facilities inspection, a delivery crate too large for the office doorway delayed work for nearly thirty minutes.

Dialyn quietly placed one Portal Hoop in front of the crate.

The second appeared inside the storage room.

The crate was pushed through without incident.

The operation took approximately twelve seconds.

Facilities Management officially described the event as:

> "Unexpectedly efficient."

Engineering requested the device for study.

Dialyn politely declined.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Dialyn continues serving as the Krampus Compliance Authority's sole Customer Support Specialist.

Despite being a department of one, customer satisfaction remains consistently high.

Whether answering calls, checking on exhausted coworkers, or casually carrying a reality-bending hula hoop over one shoulder, Dialyn has become one of the Authority's most familiar and dependable faces.

As for her true name...

No one has ever learned it.

At this point, even Human Resources has stopped asking.
