# **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

The unidentified symbol recovered from the grave site has been positively associated with a previously undocumented cult known as:

> **SPAWN**

The organization's existence has been independently corroborated through documents lent to Aperture Industries by the **Icelandic Government**.

This confirms that the cult hypothesis surrounding Alice and her mother was not speculative.

**SPAWN is real.**

Available records indicate that the organization has operated through multiple regions while maintaining an intentionally limited public presence.

Its central belief concerns the concept of obtaining a:

> **"Second Chance at Life."**

The methods employed by SPAWN to achieve this objective involve the hunting and killing of animals and supernatural beings.

The organization possesses specialized weaponry apparently designed specifically for hunting anomalous or otherwise non-human targets.

The manufacturer or origin of this equipment remains unknown.

---

# **ALICE'S RESPONSE TO THE SYMBOL**

The symbol was shown to Alice only once during a controlled medical assessment.

Her reaction was immediate.

Alice became visibly distressed.

Her breathing accelerated.

She attempted to turn away from the document and repeatedly covered the visible portion of the symbol with her hands.

When asked whether she recognized it, Alice responded:

> "...No."

A short pause followed.

She then whispered:

> "...Don't."

Medical personnel immediately removed the document.

Alice subsequently became increasingly withdrawn.

When reassured that the symbol would not be brought near her again, she quietly stated:

> "...They hurt people."

This is currently the strongest direct indication that Alice possesses some memory or association with SPAWN.

---

# **SPAWN — BELIEF SYSTEM**

Recovered Icelandic records describe SPAWN as a religious or cultic organization centered around the concept of **renewal through death**.

The organization's doctrine appears to teach that life is not necessarily permanent.

Instead, death is considered a transitional state.

According to translated documentation, members believe that sufficient sacrifice, hunting, or other forms of ritual action can earn an individual a:

> **"Second Chance."**

The exact meaning of this phrase remains unclear.

It may refer to:

- Resurrection.
    
- Reincarnation.
    
- Physical restoration.
    
- Spiritual rebirth.
    
- Anomalous reconstruction.
    
- Another phenomenon currently unknown to Aperture.
    

No reliable evidence has yet established which interpretation is correct.

---

# **TARGETS**

SPAWN does not exclusively target humans.

Available intelligence indicates that the organization hunts:

### **ANIMALS**

Particularly unusual, rare, or otherwise significant specimens.

### **SUPERNATURAL BEINGS**

Targets may include anomalous entities, supernatural organisms, and other non-human lifeforms.

This appears to be intentional.

SPAWN's doctrine reportedly does not consider ordinary biological life and supernatural life fundamentally different in value.

Both can apparently serve as targets for their rituals.

---

# **SPECIALIZED WEAPONRY**

One of the most concerning discoveries within the Icelandic documentation is evidence of specialized weapons utilized by SPAWN hunters.

These weapons are not consistent with ordinary civilian or military equipment.

Recovered descriptions indicate equipment specifically adapted for:

- Penetrating anomalous defenses.
    
- Tracking supernatural targets.
    
- Restricting unusual movement.
    
- Inflicting damage against non-standard biological structures.
    
- Hunting entities resistant to conventional weapons.
    

The technology is sufficiently advanced and specialized that C.O.R.E. believes SPAWN is **not manufacturing all of its own equipment.**

---

# **UNKNOWN THIRD PARTY**

The origin of SPAWN's specialized weaponry remains unknown.

Icelandic intelligence records repeatedly reference an unidentified supplier or manufacturer.

No confirmed name has been recovered.

No known corporate identity has been connected to the equipment.

No definitive manufacturing location has been identified.

The third party appears to provide SPAWN with weapons specifically suited for hunting supernatural targets.

Its relationship with SPAWN is therefore considered a major intelligence priority.

### **CURRENT STATUS:**

> **UNKNOWN WEAPONS SUPPLIER — UNIDENTIFIED**

- A ritual site.
    
- A site connected to an anomalous event.

Alice's manifested documents found around the grave remain highly relevant.

Several papers contain:

> **"Who am I?"**

> **"Who is Mom?"**

> **"Who are they?"**

Other drawings depict unidentified individuals with blackened faces and white question marks.

Given Alice's reaction to the SPAWN symbol, C.O.R.E. now considers it possible that these drawings represent fragmented memories of individuals associated with the organization.

This remains unconfirmed.
