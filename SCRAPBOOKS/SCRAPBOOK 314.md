# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – RESEARCH ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: MART IDENTIFICATION & LUMI'S OBSERVATIONS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Research Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

As the Mart colonies continue to expand and mature, one fact has remained unchanged.

To the overwhelming majority of personnel...

A Mart is simply a Mart.

Despite extensive study, researchers have identified only one Mart subtype that can be consistently recognized by sight.

---

# **THE ONLY IDENTIFIABLE MART**

The sole Mart that can be immediately identified without specialized observation is the **Ambassador**.

This is due to several obvious characteristics:

- Fluent English.
    
- Human-level intelligence.
    
- Calm conversational manner.
    
- Regular interaction with personnel.
    
- A fixed workplace.
    

The KCA Ambassador can almost always be found beside Dialyn in Customer Services.

The Blacksite Ambassador is usually somewhere around Project SAFEHOUSE, most often accompanying Mart, the Owl Thiren, or quietly spending time with Dialyn, Corin, or Corey whenever they need emotional support.

Beyond the Ambassadors...

Every other Mart is effectively indistinguishable.

---

# **THE ELDER MYSTERY**

Research has conclusively confirmed the existence of exactly **five Elder Marts**.

They are believed to be the original five created during Lumi's first successful summoning.

Unfortunately...

No reliable method exists to identify them.

The slight increase in size and opacity is so subtle that it becomes almost impossible to distinguish once they mingle with the rest of the colony.

Every attempted tracking project has eventually failed.

---

# **LUMI'S ATTEMPT**

Believing that their creator might instinctively recognize them, Research Division asked Lumi to identify the five Elders.

She accepted.

For nearly an hour, Lumi carefully watched hundreds of Marts playing throughout the Mart Nest.

Occasionally she would point toward one.

Then hesitate.

Then lower her hand.

Finally she quietly admitted:

> "...I..."

She smiled apologetically.

> "...I don't know anymore."

The room remained silent.

One nearby Mart happily waved at her.

> "Mother!"

Lumi waved back.

> "...Hello."

Research officially marked the identification attempt as:

> **Inconclusive.**

---

> "...Not really."

She watched dozens of Marts helping each other organize books.

> "...They're all my children."

> "...I don't think the first five would want to stand apart."

She quietly asked the Blacksite Ambassador:

> "...Could Lumi identify them if they wanted her to?"

The Ambassador considered the question carefully.

Then answered:

> "Maybe."

A pause.

> "...But they don't want to be found."

Research could not verify the statement.

No one found it difficult to believe.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Lumi continues to regard the Mart colony as her greatest achievement.

Not because of their unusual biology.

Not because of their intelligence.

Not because there are now **3,500** of them across two facilities.

But because they chose kindness.

The Ambassadors may speak fluent English.

The Elder Marts may remain forever anonymous.

Yet to Lumi, there is very little difference.

Whether they are the first Mart...

...or the three-thousand-five-hundredth...

Every cheerful "Hello!"

Still makes her smile with the same quiet pride.

> "...I'm glad you're all here."

**End of Research Addendum**
