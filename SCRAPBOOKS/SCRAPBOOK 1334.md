---

# 1. SUMMARY

Following the restoration of Jake's wings and continued stabilization of his stellar manifestation, several additional physical characteristics have appeared.

These changes are considered part of Jake's current manifested form.

They are not believed to represent a loss of his previous identity.

Jake remains:

- a six-year-old child;
    
- a Weeping Angel;
    
- a human manifestation of a literal star;
    
- and a SAFEHOUSE resident.
    

His appearance has simply become more distinctly celestial.

---

# 2. EYES

Jake's irises are now distinctly **blue.**

The coloration is brighter than his previous appearance and appears to emit a very faint azure glow under low-light conditions.

The change is subtle during normal lighting.

In darkness, however, his eyes become considerably more noticeable.

The effect resembles reflected starlight rather than an artificial illumination source.

---

# 3. STAR EARRING

Jake now wears a small **blue, star-shaped earring.**

The earring appears to be made from the same general class of celestial material associated with Aeterna's armor.

It emits a soft blue glow.

The object is physically present and remains attached to Jake's ear normally.

C.O.R.E. has not determined whether the earring is:

- independently generated;
    
- formed from Jake's stellar manifestation;
    
- or somehow connected to his broader celestial abilities.
    

Jake has not provided a detailed explanation.

When asked about it:

> **Jake:**  
> “I like it.”

This response was considered sufficient.

---

# 4. CELESTIAL GREATSWORD

The most significant change is Jake's new weapon.

Jake can manifest a **large celestial greatsword.**

The weapon is considerably larger than would normally be practical for a child of Jake's size.

This does not appear to inconvenience him.

The blade is composed of a glowing blue celestial metal.

Its appearance is visually similar to the unknown celestial material comprising Aeterna's armor.

The metal emits a persistent azure glow.

---

# 5. HILT

The sword's hilt contains a distinctive feature.

At the center of the hilt is another **star-shaped structure.**

The star functions as the visual centerpiece of the weapon.

It emits a brighter glow than the rest of the sword.

When the weapon is manifested, the star appears first, followed by the remainder of the blade.

The result resembles a star forming a weapon around itself.

---

# 6. MANIFESTATION

Jake can summon the sword at will.

The weapon does not require a physical sheath or storage location.

When Jake wishes to manifest it, blue celestial light gathers around his hand.

The star-shaped hilt forms first.

The rest of the weapon then materializes from the same luminous material.

The entire process takes only moments.

Jake can also dismiss the weapon.

Upon dismissal, the blade breaks apart into blue stellar particles and disappears.

No residue remains.

---

# 7. OBSERVED INTERACTION

During the initial observation, Jake was asked to demonstrate the manifestation.

He looked at his empty hand.

A blue star appeared.

The greatsword formed around it.

Jake held the weapon naturally.

Neko stared.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Where did you even get that?”

Jake looked at him.

> **Jake:**  
> “I made it.”

Neko paused.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Of course you did.”

Jake dismissed it.

The sword disappeared.

He then manifested it again.

Neko sighed.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Please stop doing that.”

Jake dismissed it again.

---

# 8. AETERNA

Aeterna was subsequently asked to inspect the material.

She immediately recognized the resemblance.

She placed one armored hand against the blade.

The celestial metal responded with a faint increase in luminosity.

Aeterna remained silent for several seconds.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “It is similar.”

Neko asked:

> **“The same material?”**

Aeterna shook her head.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “Not exactly.”

Pause.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “But it belongs to the same family of things.”

Jake looked between them.

> **Jake:**  
> “Is that good?”

Aeterna looked at him.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “It is yours.”

Jake nodded.

That appeared to satisfy him.

---

# 9. WEAPON CLASSIFICATION

C.O.R.E. has **not** assigned the weapon an official name.

Jake has simply referred to it as:

> **“My sword.”**

For the moment, this is sufficient.

The weapon is not considered ordinary equipment.

It is a manifestation of Jake's anomalous abilities and should therefore be documented as part of his physical profile.

---

# 10. SAFETY CONSIDERATION

Personnel are reminded that Jake's sword should not be interpreted as evidence that Jake has become a combat asset.

Jake already possesses extremely dangerous anomalous capabilities.

The sword is another manifestation of those capabilities.

No resident or researcher is authorized to request that Jake use it against a person for testing purposes.

No attempt should be made to forcibly remove it from him.

If Jake dismisses it, it disappears.

If Jake manifests it, personnel should treat the situation according to standard anomalous-safety procedures.

---

Jake's current appearance can now be summarized as:

**Six-year-old human child.**

**White hair.**

**Blue irises.**

**Azure celestial glow.**

**Blue star-shaped earring.**

**Fractured halo.**

**Restored angelic wings.**

**Teddy bear containing his stellar core.**

**Celestial greatsword capable of manifestation and dismissal.**

**Star-shaped hilt.**

**Blue celestial-metal construction.**

And, most importantly:

**Still Jake.**
