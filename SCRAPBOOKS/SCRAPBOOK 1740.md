# EMERGENCY SUMMARY

Jake is dying.

This is the current assessment of the Medical Division.

Not recovering slowly.

Not experiencing a temporary loss of strength.

Not merely suffering from the psychological consequences of his recent experiences.

**Jake is now critically weak, and his condition has deteriorated to the point that his life is in immediate danger.**

The intravenous treatment previously administered has been discontinued. His earlier stabilization was not followed by meaningful overall recovery.

His eyes no longer emit their characteristic azure light.

His teddy bear's heartbeat, which corresponds to Jake's own cardiac activity, has weakened considerably.

His physical activity has diminished further. He spends most of his time lying down or sitting with support. Even movements that previously required little effort now appear exhausting.

His responses have become increasingly limited.

He has not returned to his former interests.

He has not regained his sense of safety.

And the medical team can no longer assume that he will remain conscious, responsive, or physically stable without warning.

The cause of this deterioration remains unknown.

C.O.R.E. has declared a critical anomalous medical emergency.

---

# 1. THE MOMENT THE SITUATION CHANGED

The first indication was not a dramatic collapse.

There was no sudden explosion of anomalous energy.

No burst of blue light.

No warning from the Blacksite perimeter.

Jake was simply sitting on a bed in the medical ward.

His teddy bear rested against his chest.

A medical staff member entered to conduct a routine assessment.

**MEDICAL STAFF:**  
"Jake? How are you feeling?"

Jake looked toward the voice.

He did not answer.

The staff member approached slowly and crouched where Jake could see them.

**MEDICAL STAFF:**  
"Can you look at me?"

Jake's eyes moved toward them.

Barely.

His head remained tilted forward.

The staff member noticed that Jake was no longer holding the teddy bear as securely as before. His arms had relaxed around it, and his fingers moved sluggishly when he attempted to adjust its position.

The staff member reached for the monitoring equipment.

The bear's heartbeat was weaker than the previous reading.

Significantly weaker.

A second assessment was initiated.

Then a third.

The results were consistent.

Jake's condition was deteriorating.

The medical ward was placed on emergency protocol.

---

# 2. CURRENT PHYSICAL CONDITION

Jake's human manifestation remains physically present.

However, its ability to sustain itself is now in serious question.

The following observations have been documented.

- **Strength:** Severely diminished.
    
- **Mobility:** Extremely limited by weakness.
    
- **Responsiveness:** Reduced from his already quiet baseline.
    
- **Ocular emission:** Absent.
    
- **Cardiac-vessel activity:** Detectable, but critically weak.
    
- **Interest in surroundings:** Minimal.
    
- **Voluntary communication:** Rare and increasingly difficult to elicit.
    
- **Overall condition:** Critical and potentially fatal.
    

The absence of visible distress must not be mistaken for comfort.

Jake is not necessarily calm.

He may simply lack the strength to communicate what he is experiencing.

His silence is no longer being treated as a reliable indicator of his preferences or level of discomfort. Medical staff continue to seek his assent whenever possible while recognizing that his deteriorating condition may limit his ability to respond.

He is not being asked to walk.

He is not being encouraged to exercise.

He is not being told to push through the weakness.

Every unnecessary physical demand has been suspended.

---

# 3. THE TEDDY BEAR — CRITICAL FINDING

The bear's heartbeat remains present.

Barely.

The signal is now so weak that the medical team has increased monitoring frequency to detect further deterioration.

This is the most concerning measurable finding.

The bear is Jake's cardiac vessel. Its heartbeat is not decorative, metaphorical, or independent of him.

**When the bear's cardiac activity changes, Jake's medical condition must be considered to have changed as well.**

The team has not established whether the weakening reflects a failing physical manifestation, an anomalous cardiac process, a disruption of Jake's stellar existence, or some combination of factors.

No intervention has yet demonstrated a reliable means of reversing the decline.

Neko's engineering team has been consulted regarding non-invasive monitoring. Their role is limited to observation and support; no device is to be connected to the bear without medical authorization.

The bear will not be dismantled.

It will not be opened to inspect its internal structure.

It will not be removed from Jake merely to simplify testing.

It is part of the patient.

Not a piece of equipment.

Not a convenient experimental object.

A part of Jake.

---

# 4. THE LIGHT THAT DID NOT RETURN

Jake's eyes remain blue.

They no longer glow.

The shooting-star streaks that once moved through them are no longer visibly illuminated.

Repeated observation has not established whether this change is reversible.

Previously, personnel could recognize Jake from the faint azure light reflected in his glasses or on the pages of the books he held.

That light is gone.

During the latest assessment, a clinician briefly illuminated a nearby wall with a standard examination light. Jake's eyes reflected it normally.

There was no anomalous response.

No celestial emission.

No visible sign of the stellar phenomenon that once made his gaze resemble Aeterna's.

The team has not concluded that Jake's star has extinguished.

Appearance alone cannot establish that.

However, the disappearance of the light alongside the weakening heartbeat and severe physical decline has raised the possibility that the changes share an underlying cause.

That possibility is being investigated as an emergency.

---

# 5. THE QUESTION OF JAKE'S EXISTENCE

Jake is a literal star manifested in a fragile, childlike physical body.

That fact has complicated every aspect of his treatment.

His physical manifestation can suffer injuries.

It can become exhausted.

It can experience cold, weakness, and other forms of physiological distress.

His anomalous nature does not make these conditions harmless.

At the same time, C.O.R.E. cannot assume that conventional medical stabilization alone will resolve a crisis involving his underlying existence.

The team is therefore investigating two related but distinct questions:

1. What is causing Jake's physical condition to deteriorate?
    
2. Is the anomalous process sustaining his manifestation also failing?
    

Neither question currently has a definitive answer.

The second question is especially difficult because the absence of visible celestial light is not proof of the absence of a star.

Jake has not been declared dead.

He has not been declared irreversibly lost.

He remains alive, with cardiac-vessel activity still detectable.

But the possibility of death is no longer theoretical.

---

# 6. AETERNA'S ARRIVAL

Aeterna arrived after being notified of the emergency.

She entered the medical ward without her usual questions about the equipment or the examination results.

She looked directly at Jake.

His white hair lay untidily against the pillow.

His blue eyes were open, but unfocused.

The teddy bear rested against his chest.

Aeterna approached slowly enough for him to see her coming.

**AETERNA:**  
"Jake?"

His gaze moved toward her.

For a moment, there was no response.

Then his fingers shifted against the bear.

Aeterna stopped beside the bed.

She did not touch him immediately.

**AETERNA:**  
"It's me."

Jake's lips moved.

The first attempt produced no audible sound.

He swallowed and tried again.

**JAKE:**  
"...Aeterna."

Her expression changed.

She sat beside him.

**AETERNA:**  
"I'm here."

Jake looked at her for several seconds.

Then his eyes moved toward the teddy bear.

**JAKE:**  
"Bear..."

Aeterna leaned slightly closer.

**AETERNA:**  
"What about him?"

Jake's fingers curled weakly into the fabric.

**JAKE:**  
"...Quiet."

Aeterna looked at the bear.

She could hear the medical monitor.

She could see the readings.

She understood what he meant.

**AETERNA:**  
"I know."

Jake's eyes returned to her.

**JAKE:**  
"Make it... not quiet."

Aeterna did not answer immediately.

She was one of the most powerful beings known to C.O.R.E.

She had survived events that would have destroyed entire civilizations.

She carried a sword capable of acting upon existential purpose.

And she could not promise a frightened child that she could restore the heartbeat of the only object that had always beaten with him.

She took a careful breath.

**AETERNA:**  
"We're trying."

Jake watched her.

**JAKE:**  
"Okay."

His eyes slowly closed.

Aeterna leaned forward.

**AETERNA:**  
"Jake?"

He did not respond immediately.

The medical team began an urgent reassessment.

Aeterna remained beside the bed while the clinicians worked.

She did not attempt to interfere.

She did not draw her sword.

She did not demand that the universe return what it had taken.

For once, she had nothing to threaten.

Only someone to stay beside.

---

# 7. CLOVER — THE ROOM THAT REMAINS HIS

Clover was informed after the initial emergency response had begun.

She arrived carrying a small book Jake had previously left in his room.

It was one of the books he used to read before his withdrawal became severe.

She had brought it because she did not know what else to bring.

When she saw the monitoring equipment, she stopped at the entrance.

**CLOVER:**  
"Is he...?"

The clinician did not finish the sentence for her.

**MEDICAL OFFICER:**  
"He's alive. His condition is critical."

Clover closed her eyes briefly.

Then she entered.

She placed the book on the bedside table, within Jake's reach if he regained enough strength to take it.

She did not open it.

She did not read aloud without his permission.

She sat in the chair beside the bed.

For a long time, she watched his hands.

They were so still.

The same hands that had once drawn flowers into existence.

The same hands that had turned ordinary sand into clear glass.

The same hands that had held his teddy bear while he fell asleep.

Clover lowered her gaze.

**CLOVER:**  
"Your room is still there, Jake."

No response.

**CLOVER:**  
"I haven't moved your things."

She swallowed.

**CLOVER:**  
"You can come back whenever you want."

The monitor continued recording the weak cardiac activity.

Clover pressed her hands together.

**CLOVER:**  
"You don't have to do anything when you come back."

She did not ask him to wake up.

She did not ask him to smile.

She did not ask him to forgive anyone, trust anyone, or prove that he still wanted to live in the SAFEHOUSE.

She simply remained in the chair.

The book stayed on the table.

His room remained his.

And Clover waited without pretending that waiting was enough to treat him.

---

# 8. BAO AND NERI

Bao arrived shortly afterward.

She did not enter the room loudly.

She spoke to the medical staff first, listened to the current assessment, and asked what they were doing to stabilize him.

She did not demand a miracle.

She demanded that every medically appropriate option be considered.

Neri arrived with her.

She stopped outside the room.

Through the open doorway, she could see Jake lying beneath the blankets.

His eyes were no longer glowing.

His teddy bear was held against him by one weak arm.

Neri stood perfectly still.

Bao glanced toward her.

**BAO:**  
"You can come in if you want."

Neri did not move.

Her eyes remained fixed on Jake.

After several seconds, she spoke quietly.

**NERI:**  
"Is he dying?"

Bao did not give her a comforting lie.

**BAO:**  
"He's critically ill. They're trying to save him."

Neri looked down.

Her hands tightened around the edge of her sleeve.

She did not ask whether he blamed her.

She did not ask whether he would forgive her.

She did not make the emergency about her own guilt.

Eventually, she entered the room and stood where Jake could see her if he opened his eyes.

She did not approach the bed without permission.

She did not touch him.

She simply stayed.

For the first time in several days, Neri had something to say and could not find the words.

So she said nothing.

# 9. EMERGENCY RESPONSE DIRECTIVE

The Medical Division has placed Jake under continuous clinical observation.

The immediate objectives are:

- identify and treat any reversible causes of his deterioration;
    
- monitor his cardiac-vessel activity continuously;
    
- assess his physical manifestation and anomalous stability without invasive experimentation;
    
- evaluate whether his absent ocular emission is associated with the current decline;
    
- prevent avoidable exhaustion, injury, or environmental stress;
    
- and preserve Jake's dignity, comfort, and access to familiar objects.
    

His previous IV treatment is not being automatically reinstated merely because his condition has worsened. The medical team will determine whether fluids, medication, or other interventions are indicated based on current findings.

Likewise, no one is authorized to administer blood, anomalous energy, or another resident's biological material on the assumption that it might help.

Any intervention must have a defensible medical rationale and an appropriate assessment of its risks.

Jake's condition is too serious for improvised treatment.

His existence is too important to justify treating him as an experiment.

---

# 10. RESIDENT WELFARE WARNING

Personnel are reminded that Jake's current condition is not a disciplinary matter.

He is not refusing to recover.

He is not withholding a smile.

He is not being difficult.

He is not responsible for reassuring the people who are frightened for him.

He is critically ill.

His recent psychological deterioration remains relevant to his care, but it must not distract from the immediate medical emergency.

Do not tell him that he needs to fight harder.

Do not tell him that everyone needs him to wake up.

Do not promise that everything will be fine.

Do not demand that he forgive those who have hurt him before he receives care.

Do not imply that his survival is a debt he owes the SAFEHOUSE.

If Jake is conscious, speak to him calmly.

Explain procedures.

Allow him to indicate preferences wherever possible.

If he cannot respond, provide necessary care while preserving his dignity.

The objective is not to make Jake prove that he wants help.

The objective is to help him.

---

# 11. CURRENT STATUS

At the time of this report:

- Jake remains alive.
    
- His physical condition remains critical.
    
- His strength is severely diminished.
    
- His eyes no longer glow.
    
- His teddy bear's heartbeat remains detectable but exceptionally weak.
    
- The cause of the decline remains undetermined.
    
- No reliable reversal has been established.
    
- His prognosis is guarded and may worsen rapidly.
    

The Medical Division cannot guarantee survival.

It can guarantee that the situation will not be minimized merely because Jake is quiet.

It can guarantee that he will not be abandoned to manage the crisis alone.

And it can guarantee that no one will deliberately remove his teddy bear, his belongings, or his remaining choices simply because he is too weak to argue.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake no longer needs the IV.

That should have been good news.

His eyes no longer glow.

That might once have been an interesting anomaly.

His teddy bear barely beats.

That is no longer an observation to file away and revisit tomorrow.

Jake is lying in a medical bed, too weak to do much more than hold the one object that has remained with him through every manifestation, every injury, every frightening night, and every return.

He is no longer asking why people leave him outside.

He is no longer asking whether they are trying to kill him.

He is barely asking for anything at all.

And that silence has become terrifying.

Because the boy who once filled rooms with drawings, flowers, books, and small questions has grown so tired that even asking someone to stay seems beyond him.

Aeterna is beside his bed.

Clover is waiting with his book.

Bao is working with the medical team.

Neri stands nearby, unable to undo what happened but unwilling to leave the room.

None of them can promise him a happy ending.

None of them can promise that his eyes will shine again.

None of them can promise that the heartbeat will strengthen.

For now, the monitor continues to register a faint rhythm.

A little heartbeat.

A little boy.

A star whose light is no longer visible.

**Jake is still here.**

The Medical Division will continue fighting for his survival for as long as there is a medically and anomalously defensible path to do so.

Not because he is powerful.

Not because he is a star.

Not because of what he can create or destroy.

Because he is Jake.

And that has always been enough.

**— C.O.R.E. Medical & Anomalous Welfare Division**
