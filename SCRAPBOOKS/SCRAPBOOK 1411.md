# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake was left outside the Blacksite during the recent perimeter lockdown.

The Blacksite subsequently became sealed by a massive blizzard.

Jake remained outside for approximately:

> **FIVE WEEKS.**

No resident intentionally abandoned him.

No evacuation order specifically identified Jake as missing.

No welfare check identified his absence.

No SAFEHOUSE resident currently knows that he was outside.

As of the time of this document's initial filing:

> **The SAFEHOUSE does not know Jake is missing.**

Jake survived the entire five-week period by taking shelter inside a **Perimeter Security Outpost**.

The outpost contained enough food for approximately one week.

Jake therefore spent the remaining four weeks surviving with severely limited provisions.

The prolonged exposure to extreme cold has caused significant physiological damage.

Multiple organs show evidence of cold-related injury.

Most notably:

> **Jake's vocal cords have been severely damaged.**

Jake is currently unable to speak.

This represents a significant change from his established condition.

---

# 1. PERIMETER LOCKDOWN

The Blacksite perimeter was sealed following the onset of an extreme blizzard.

Visibility dropped rapidly.

External movement became unsafe.

Perimeter personnel were ordered to remain inside designated shelters and security structures.

Access between several perimeter sectors was suspended.

At some point during the initial sealing process, Jake remained outside.

The exact circumstances remain under investigation.

The currently established fact is simply:

> **Jake was not inside when the perimeter became inaccessible.**

No alarm was triggered.

No resident immediately reported him missing.

No automated system identified his absence.

---

# 2. THE OUTPOST

Jake eventually reached a Perimeter Security Outpost.

The structure provided:

- shelter;
    
- limited heating;
    
- emergency lighting;
    
- basic medical supplies;
    
- water;
    
- emergency food provisions;
    
- communications equipment.
    

The outpost was not designed for long-term habitation.

Its emergency food supply was intended to sustain a small number of personnel for approximately one week.

Jake remained there for five.

---

# 3. FIRST WEEK

During the first several days, Jake appears to have remained relatively functional.

He consumed the available food carefully.

He remained close to the heating system.

He slept intermittently.

His teddy bear remained secured against his body.

The bear's heartbeat remained detectable.

Jake apparently expected someone to come looking for him.

No one did.

---

# 4. SECOND WEEK

Food supplies became critically limited.

Jake began rationing what remained.

Available records indicate no successful communication with the SAFEHOUSE.

The outpost's communication equipment was not configured for direct resident welfare communication.

Jake was also increasingly reluctant or unable to interact with the equipment.

His physical condition deteriorated.

The cold began producing increasingly serious physiological effects.

---

# 5. THIRD WEEK

By the third week, Jake was no longer functioning normally.

His movements became slower.

He spent prolonged periods wrapped in available thermal materials.

His extremities showed evidence of prolonged cold exposure.

His energy levels became severely reduced.

The teddy bear's heartbeat became noticeably weaker.

Jake continued keeping it against his chest.

There is no evidence that he abandoned it.

---

# 6. FOURTH WEEK

By the fourth week, Jake's condition had become critical.

Food was nearly exhausted.

The heating system was functioning below optimal capacity.

Jake's body began showing evidence of systemic cold injury.

Several internal organs were affected.

His cardiovascular system remained functional only because his stellar physiology continued compensating for the damage.

However, his human manifestation was not designed to withstand prolonged exposure to this environment.

His vocal apparatus was also damaged.

Jake attempted to speak at least once during this period.

No usable voice was produced.

Afterward, he stopped trying.

---

# 7. FIFTH WEEK

The fifth week was the most severe.

Jake had almost no food remaining.

He remained inside the outpost for extended periods without moving.

His teddy bear's heartbeat had become:

> **Barely audible.**

Jake continued holding it against his chest.

The bear's physical condition deteriorated alongside him.

The distinction between Jake's physical heartbeat and the bear's heartbeat remained functionally irrelevant.

The teddy bear continued to contain Jake's stellar core.

As the core's external vessel weakened, Jake's condition deteriorated accordingly.

---

# 8. BLIZZARD SEAL LIFTED

After approximately five weeks, the blizzard subsided sufficiently for the Blacksite perimeter seal to be lifted.

Perimeter Security personnel began conducting routine inspections.

One female Perimeter Security Guard entered the outpost.

She found Jake.

He was alive.

Barely.

He was sitting quietly near the heating unit.

His white hair was covered in frost.

His azure eyes were extremely dim.

The shooting-star patterns remained visible but moved sluggishly.

His teddy bear was held tightly against his chest.

The Guard immediately approached.

---

# 9. PERIMETER SECURITY GUARD INTERACTION

The Guard crouched beside Jake.

She spoke gently.

> **GUARD:**  
> “What's your name?”

Jake shook.

The Guard paused.

She immediately remembered.

Jake could no longer speak.

> **GUARD:**  
> “I forgot you can't speak. Sorry.”

Jake looked toward her.

The Guard continued.

> **GUARD:**  
> “Where are your friends and family?”

Jake shook his head.

The Guard tried again.

> **GUARD:**  
> “Busy?”

Jake nodded.

The Guard looked at him for several seconds.

She did not know that the answer was incorrect.

She did not know that Jake's friends and family had no idea where he was.

She did not know that nobody had been looking for him.

She simply sat beside him.

For a while.

Neither spoke.

Eventually, the Guard stood.

> **GUARD:**  
> “I'll get someone to check this place.”

Jake looked up.

The Guard left.

She did not realize that Jake would not ask her to stay.

---

# 10. AFTER THE GUARD LEFT

Jake remained where he was.

He looked toward the doorway.

Nothing happened.

No one returned immediately.

Jake lowered his gaze.

His hands tightened around the teddy bear.

The heartbeat was barely noticeable.

He held it closer.

His azure eyes moved slowly across the room.

He looked toward the door.

Then toward the empty communications terminal.

Then toward the snow-covered window.

There was nobody there.

---

# 11. RETURN TO SAFEHOUSE

Jake eventually stood.

It took considerable effort.

He left the outpost.

The perimeter was finally accessible.

Jake began walking toward the SAFEHOUSE.

No one stopped him.

No one recognized that anything was wrong.

He crossed the familiar corridors.

He passed security checkpoints.

He entered the SAFEHOUSE.

No resident noticed his condition.

Jake did not approach anyone.

He did not seek medical assistance.

He did not look for Bao.

He did not look for Phoenix.

He did not look for the Obol Squad.

He did not look for Neri.

He did not look for Nora.

He did not look for Alice.

He simply walked.

---

# 12. JAKE'S ROOM

Jake reached his floor.

He entered his room.

The door closed.

Then locked.

The sign remained in place:

> **Jake's Room**  
> _please knock, I can't take suprises_

Jake sat on the floor.

His teddy bear remained against his chest.

The heartbeat was almost imperceptible.

His eyes were dim.

His body was shaking.

Jake looked toward the room.

The mattress.

The books.

The plushies.

The empty space.

Everything was familiar.

Everything was safe.

And yet nobody knew he had returned.

# 14. MEDICAL OBSERVATION

Jake's current physical state is extremely concerning.

Confirmed or suspected injuries include:

- extensive cold-related tissue damage;
    
- multiple organ injuries;
    
- severe systemic exhaustion;
    
- prolonged nutritional deprivation;
    
- dehydration;
    
- impaired circulation;
    
- severe hypothermic exposure;
    
- damage to the vocal cords;
    
- reduced stellar manifestation;
    
- weakening of the teddy bear's cardiac rhythm.
    

The full extent of the internal damage remains unknown.

Jake's stellar nature has allowed him to survive circumstances that would ordinarily be fatal.

This should **not** be interpreted as evidence that he is unharmed.

His human manifestation remains physically vulnerable.

His body is currently paying the cost of surviving the exposure.

---

# 15. VOCAL DAMAGE

Jake's inability to speak is currently believed to be physical.

Prior to the incident, Jake could speak normally.

He has now lost the ability to produce usable speech.

Attempted vocalization produces little or no sound.

Further medical examination is required.

However, Jake has not requested assistance.

More accurately:

> **Jake currently has no effective way to request assistance verbally.**

He can still communicate through:

- gestures;
    
- writing;
    
- facial expressions;
    
- nodding;
    
- shaking his head;
    
- pointing.
    

Whether he currently has the physical strength to use those methods consistently remains unknown.

---

# 16. THE TEDDY BEAR

The teddy bear remains Jake's most important physical object.

Its heartbeat is currently extremely weak.

The bear itself shows signs of prolonged exposure to the cold.

Jake has refused to put it down.

At one point after returning to the SAFEHOUSE, Jake pressed the bear against his chest and remained completely motionless for several minutes.

He appeared to be listening.

The heartbeat continued.

Barely.

Jake closed his eyes.

He held it tighter.

---

# 17. BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION

The most significant concern is not simply Jake's physical condition.

It is his behavior.

Jake did not return to the people he trusted.

He did not seek Bao.

He did not seek Phoenix.

He did not seek Neri.

He did not seek Nora.

He did not seek Aeterna.

He did not seek the Obol Squad.

He did not seek medical assistance.

He returned to his room.

And locked the door.

This suggests that Jake may currently believe:

> **Nobody noticed.**

Or possibly:

> **Nobody needs to notice.**

The distinction cannot yet be established.

---

# 18. INCIDENT RESPONSIBILITY

Anby is currently the last known resident associated with Jake immediately prior to the perimeter lockdown.

Available information does not establish deliberate abandonment.

There is currently no evidence that Anby knowingly left Jake outside.

The incident should therefore be treated as a:

> **Welfare failure.**

Not an intentional act.

A full investigation is pending.

---

# 19. CRITICAL INFORMATION GAP

C.O.R.E. has identified a significant systemic failure.

The SAFEHOUSE maintains extensive awareness of resident activity.

However:

> **No system currently verifies that every resident is physically inside the SAFEHOUSE during a perimeter emergency.**

Jake's absence was therefore not detected.

His return was not detected.

His medical deterioration was not detected.

His five-week isolation was not detected.

The system functioned exactly as designed.

The design was insufficient.

---
