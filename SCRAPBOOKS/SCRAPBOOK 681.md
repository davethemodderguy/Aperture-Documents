### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / ENTITY LOG**

# **BUNNY — HUMANOID MANIFESTATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 — Internal Personnel  
**Primary Subject:** Bunny  
**Associated Subject:** Corin Lockwood  
**Location:** Treehouse → SAFEHOUSE, Room N-07  
**Status:** **NON-HOSTILE / RESIDENT**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The morning after Corin had gone to sleep hugging Bunny, an unexpected transformation was discovered.

Bunny was no longer a rabbit.

Corin woke up and immediately noticed the empty space beside her.

Bunny was gone.

Corin looked around.

Then she noticed someone standing nearby.

A small girl.

Pale skin.

White bunny ears.

A small rabbit-like nose.

The same general appearance and features associated with Bunny.

Corin stared.

Orphie stared.

The girl stared back.

Then she asked:

> **"Why you staring at Bunny?"**

Corin and Orphie remained silent.

The girl tilted her head.

---

# **IDENTIFICATION**

Orphie eventually asked:

> **"Who are you?"**

The girl pointed directly at herself.

> **"Bunny."**

Corin's eyes widened.

> **"...Bunny?"**

The girl nodded.

> **"Bunny."**

Corin immediately recognized the voice.

It was the same rabbit.

The same mannerisms.

The same personality.

Just...

humanoid.

---

# **LINGUISTIC CAPABILITY**

Bunny possesses extremely limited grammatical ability.

She understands human speech remarkably well.

However, her own speech consists primarily of individual words assembled together.

Her vocabulary appears considerably larger than her ability to construct sentences.

Examples include:

> **"Bunny hungry."**

> **"Want carrot."**

> **"Corin sleep."**

> **"Bunny cold."**

> **"Big sis here."**

She does not appear frustrated by her limited grammar.

She simply communicates using the words she knows.

---

# **FAMILY DESIGNATIONS**

During the initial conversation, Corin asked:

> **"Who am I?"**

Bunny pointed at Corin.

> **"Big sis."**

Corin froze.

> **"...I'm your big sister?"**

Bunny nodded.

> **"Big sis."**

Corin smiled.

Orphie then asked:

> **"And me?"**

Bunny pointed at Orphie.

A brief pause.

> **"Bigger sis."**

Orphie stared at her.

Corin started laughing.

> **"You're bigger sis!"**

Orphie:

> **"...Apparently."**

---

# **THE RELATIONSHIP**

The exact nature of Bunny's transformation remains unknown.

However, Bunny's behavior suggests that her perception of Corin has not changed.

Corin remains:

**Big Sis.**

Orphie remains:

**Bigger Sis.**

Bunny continues to demonstrate the same attachment she showed in rabbit form.

She follows Corin.

She sits beside Corin.

She sleeps near Corin.

She asks Corin for carrots.

Nothing about the transformation appears to have altered the bond.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin was initially stunned.

Then fascinated.

Then extremely happy.

She spent much of the morning asking Bunny questions.

> **"Can you still hop?"**

Bunny nodded.

> **"Can you eat carrots?"**

Bunny immediately nodded harder.

> **"Do you still like sleeping under my wing?"**

Bunny:

> **"Wing warm."**

Corin hugged her.

Bunny hugged back.

---

# **AGAINST ALL EXPECTATIONS**

The transformation did not appear painful.

No injuries were detected.

No abnormal stress response was observed.

Bunny simply woke up as a humanoid rabbit girl.

C.O.R.E. medical personnel confirmed:

- Normal vital activity.
    
- Functional organs.
    
- Stable physiology.
    
- Fully intact rabbit characteristics.
    
- Human-level bipedal mobility.
    
- Retention of anomalous linguistic comprehension.
    

The classification was updated accordingly.

---

# **THIREN CLASSIFICATION**

Bunny has been classified as a:

> **FULL-BODY RABBIT THIREN**

The classification is consistent with other humanoid-animal individuals residing within SAFEHOUSE, including **Belle Merryweather**.

Bunny retains:

- Rabbit ears.
    
- Rabbit nose.
    
- Rabbit behavioral traits.
    
- Rabbit dietary preferences.
    
- Enhanced auditory awareness.
    
- Strong affinity for carrots.
    

She otherwise possesses a humanoid body.

---

# **TRANSFER TO SAFEHOUSE**

After determining that Bunny could safely reside alongside the other inhabitants, Corin brought her to the Blacksite's SAFEHOUSE.

The response was immediate.

Everyone stared.

Nobody had been informed that the rabbit was capable of becoming a person.

Neko reportedly spent several seconds attempting to understand the situation.

Miss Neko simply looked at Bunny.

> **"...Rabbit."**

Bunny pointed at herself.

> **"Bunny."**

---

# **BELLE'S REACTION**

Belle was delighted.

She immediately approached Bunny.

> **"Another rabbit in town!"**

Belle laughed.

Bunny looked at her.

Then pointed toward herself.

> **"Bunny."**

Belle laughed even harder.

> **"Yes! Bunny!"**

The two immediately got along.

---

# **"ANOTHER BELLE"**

Several SAFEHOUSE residents began informally referring to Bunny as:

> **"Another Belle."**

The comparison was primarily based on their shared rabbit traits.

Belle did not object.

In fact, she appeared rather happy about it.

> **"I have rabbit friend!"**

Bunny pointed at Belle.

> **"Rabbit."**

Belle pointed at herself.

> **"Belle."**

Bunny pointed at herself.

> **"Bunny."**

They shook hands.

The introduction was considered successful.

---

# **CORIN AND BUNNY**

Corin has remained extremely close to Bunny since the transformation.

Bunny continues following her everywhere.

The only significant difference is that Bunny can now participate in activities that were previously difficult in rabbit form.

She can:

- Talk.
    
- Draw.
    
- Open doors.
    
- Help in the garden.
    
- Carry things.
    
- Sit at the table.
    
- Play games.
    
- Hug Corin properly.
    

The carrot garden has consequently become considerably more difficult to protect.

Bunny can now reach the shelves.

---

# **THE FIRST MORNING**

Before leaving the Treehouse, Corin asked:

> **"You still my Bunny?"**

Bunny looked confused.

She pointed at herself.

> **"Bunny."**

Then pointed at Corin.

> **"Big sis."**

Then pointed toward Orphie.

> **"Bigger sis."**

Corin smiled.

> **"Okay."**

Bunny smiled too.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Yesterday, Corin hugged a little rabbit to sleep.

This morning, she woke up beside a little girl with white bunny ears.

The rabbit was still there.

The same personality.

The same eyes.

The same love for carrots.

The same attachment to Corin.

Just... taller.

When asked what she wanted to be called, she pointed at herself.

> **"Bunny."**

When asked who Corin was:

> **"Big sis."**

And when asked who Orphie was:

> **"Bigger sis."**

Later that day, Belle found her.

Belle laughed.

> **"Another rabbit in town!"**

Bunny pointed at herself.

> **"Bunny."**

Belle laughed again.

And just like that, SAFEHOUSE gained another resident.

A rabbit who somehow became a girl overnight.

A girl who understands everyone perfectly.

A girl who speaks in little pieces of words.

A girl who loves carrots.

And, apparently...

**a girl who has decided Corin is her big sister.**
