# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon has acquired another firearm from the Blacksite Armory.

The weapon is a **Marlin Model 1894 chambered in .357 Magnum**, fitted with an Armory-approved tactical configuration.

The acquisition was initially considered unremarkable.

This assessment was incorrect.

Nora has somehow made a lever-action rifle look like it belongs in a futuristic tactical demonstration.

The Armory staff have subsequently added another entry to Nora's unofficial personnel description:

> **TACTICOOL.**

---

# THE ARMORY VISIT

Nora entered the Armory with Frost.

She approached the rifle rack.

Stopped.

Looked at the Marlin.

Looked at the Armory technician.

Pointed.

> **NORA:**  
> “That one.”

The technician retrieved the rifle for the supervised inspection.

Nora examined it quietly.

She checked the fit.

Looked over the tactical configuration.

Then looked at the lever.

Her expression changed slightly.

The technician immediately knew something was about to happen.

> **TECHNICIAN:**  
> “Nora.”

Nora looked at him.

> **TECHNICIAN:**  
> “Don't.”

Nora blinked.

> **NORA:**  
> “Don't what?”

> **TECHNICIAN:**  
> “Whatever you're thinking.”

Nora smiled faintly.

---

# THE FLICKOCK

During the controlled demonstration, Nora cycled the lever with a quick, stylized motion.

Rather than treating the action as a slow mechanical demonstration, she incorporated it into a smooth **flick-cocking flourish**.

The lever moved.

**Click.**

The rifle returned to its ready configuration.

Nora barely moved her upper body.

She simply looked down the range.

The room went silent.

The technician slowly lowered his clipboard.

> **TECHNICIAN:**  
> “...Why did that look cool?”

Nora shrugged.

> **NORA:**  
> “Lever.”

That was apparently her entire explanation.

---

# OBSERVER REACTION

An MSAC operator watching from behind the safety line looked at the rifle.

Then at Nora.

Then at the rifle again.

> **MSAC OPERATOR:**  
> “She's doing it again.”

**FROST:**  
“Doing what?”

> **MSAC OPERATOR:**  
> “Making everything look tactical.”

Frost looked at Nora.

Nora was standing there with the Marlin.

Completely expressionless.

> **FROST:**  
> “That's just Nora.”

---

# TACTICAL CONFIGURATION

The Armory configuration gives the traditionally styled lever-action platform a distinctly modern appearance.

The result is an unusual combination:

**Classic lever-action silhouette.**

**Modern tactical equipment.**

**Six-year-old shark operating it.**

The combination should not work.

Somehow, it does.

Nora appears particularly fond of the contrast.

She reportedly described the rifle as:

> **“Old gun. New stuff.”**

C.O.R.E. considers this an accurate summary.

---

# RANGE OBSERVATION

Nora's behavior with the Marlin differs slightly from her shotgun handling.

With the Slugger, she tends to emphasize speed.

With the Marlin, she appears more interested in rhythm.

The lever-action movement becomes almost casual.

She cycles it.

Pauses.

Inspects.

Cycles again.

At one point, she performs another exaggerated flick-cocking motion.

The range instructor immediately points at her.

> **RANGE INSTRUCTOR:**  
> “Nora.”

Nora stops.

> **RANGE INSTRUCTOR:**  
> “You know you don't have to make it look cool.”

Nora considers this.

> **NORA:**  
> “I know.”

> **RANGE INSTRUCTOR:**  
> “Then why?”

Nora looks at the rifle.

> **NORA:**  
> “Because it looks cool.”

The instructor had no counterargument.

---

# NEKO'S REACTION

Neko was later shown the footage.

He watched Nora cycle the lever.

Then watched the replay.

Then watched it again.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Why does a six-year-old have better cinematic timing than most of our promotional department?”

Miss Neko, standing beside him:

> **MISS NEKO:**  
> “Because she's six.”

Neko looked confused.

> **NEKO:**  
> “That doesn't answer anything.”

> **MISS NEKO:**  
> “It doesn't need to.”

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Nora's acquisition of the Marlin does not appear to represent a replacement for her existing equipment.

She simply likes the rifle.

The tactical configuration has also clearly appealed to her.

Most importantly, her behavior remains consistent with her established **TACTICALITY** aptitude.

She observes.

She adapts.

She learns.

And, apparently, she has decided that every lever-action rifle deserves a dramatic flick.

Nora has now acquired:

**A shotgun.**

**A pistol.**

**A lever-action rifle.**

And somehow, every time she picks one up, the entire range starts looking like a movie set.

She doesn't announce herself.

She doesn't pose.

She doesn't boast.

She simply performs the action.

**Click.**

The lever moves.

Nora looks forward.

Everyone else stares.

Nora:

> **“What?”**

C.O.R.E. has officially concluded:

> **Nora Tachyon remains extremely tacticool.**
