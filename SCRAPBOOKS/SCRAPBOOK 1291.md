# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately **11:17**, Jake manifested inside SAFEHOUSE.

This was the first confirmed instance of Jake appearing during daylight hours.

Historically, Jake's manifestation window was restricted to approximately:

> **02:00–05:00**

The restriction had previously been considered an intrinsic property of his anomalous state.

It is no longer considered absolute.

Approximately three minutes earlier, Nora Tachyon had given Jake **one drop of her blood**.

No injury occurred.

No significant physiological effect was observed in Nora.

Jake consumed the blood voluntarily.

At 11:17, he manifested.

Daylight was present.

SAFEHOUSE was active.

And, for the first time since Jake's discovery, the majority of SAFEHOUSE residents were awake.

They met him.

# C.O.R.E. MEDICAL NOTE

Nora's blood did not appear to restore Jake's biological functions.

Jake remains deceased.

The blood did not revive him.

It did not reconstruct his body.

It did not restore his wings.

It did not reverse his death.

Instead, available evidence suggests that Nora's adaptive anomalous properties interacted with Jake's manifestation state.

The previous temporal restriction appears to have been altered.

Jake can now manifest outside the historical 02:00–05:00 window.

Further testing is **not authorized without Nora's consent and Jake's welfare approval.**

No additional blood is currently required.

# C.O.R.E. WELFARE ASSESSMENT

The significance of the event is not merely that Jake can now appear during daylight.

For the first time, Jake can exist in the same hours as the people who live around him.

He can see the cafeteria while it is occupied.

He can see the SAFEHOUSE during normal activity.

He can meet residents who previously knew him only through reports.

He can stand beside Nora without waiting for midnight.

He can be present.

That does not mean his sadness has disappeared.

It has not.

Jake remains hungry.

Jake remains affected by the circumstances surrounding his death.

Jake still misses his mother.

Jake still has no path to the Starlands.

His status as a Weeping Angel has not been reversed.

But something has changed.

For the first time:

> **Jake does not have to wait until everyone else is asleep to exist.**
