### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / PERSONNEL & MEDICAL LOG**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — RESTRICTED MEDICAL RECORD ACCESS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Age:** Approximately 5 years old  
**Status:** **PROTECTED DEPENDENT / HIGH-RISK MEDICAL CASE**

---

# **ACCESS RESTRICTION**

Alice Lockwood is **not permitted to access her own medical record.**

This restriction is not punitive.

It exists for her protection.

The medical record contains extensive documentation of Alice's physical limitations, physiological abnormalities, previous deprivation, susceptibility to illness, and life-threatening risks.

C.O.R.E. has determined that exposing Alice to the complete record presents a substantial psychological risk.

---

# **PRIMARY CONCERN**

Alice has already demonstrated a strong tendency to blame herself for things outside her control.

She frequently attempts to make herself appear happy so that others do not worry.

She is particularly sensitive to the possibility that she is causing inconvenience to the people around her.

Giving a five-year-old child a detailed document stating that:

- She is physically fragile.
    
- She becomes sick easily.
    
- She requires supervision.
    
- She cannot walk very far.
    
- Her body carries permanent abnormalities.
    
- Her life is frequently at risk.
    

could easily be interpreted by Alice as:

> **"I am a burden."**

This interpretation must be prevented.

---

# **THE WORST-CASE SCENARIO**

C.O.R.E. psychological personnel identified a specific concern.

If Alice concludes that everyone must constantly take care of her, she may attempt to remove herself from the situation.

Given Alice's history, this is extremely dangerous.

She has already demonstrated that she will survive alone rather than ask for help.

She previously spent more than eight months outdoors.

She ate snow.

She ate grass.

She ate flowers.

She slept in the cold.

She became injured.

She remained alone.

And she survived only barely.

Allowing Alice to believe that leaving would somehow make life easier for everyone else could result in another disappearance.

This time, she may not be found.

---

# **ACCESS CONTROL**

Alice's medical record has therefore been placed behind additional authorization.

**Alice Lockwood**

- Unsupervised medical consultations
    

---

# **IF ALICE ASKS**

Personnel are not instructed to lie to Alice.

However, they are instructed to provide information appropriate to her age and emotional state.

If Alice asks:

> **"Am I sick?"**

Appropriate response:

> **"Sometimes your body needs extra care."**

Not:

> _"You have severe systemic toxicity and permanent physiological deficiencies."_

If Alice asks:

> **"Am I weak?"**

Appropriate response:

> **"Your body has some limits, and that's okay."**

If Alice asks:

> **"Do I make things difficult?"**

The answer should be immediate:

> **"No."**

---

# **WHAT ALICE NEEDS TO KNOW**

Alice does not need to know every abnormality inside her body.

She needs to know practical things.

> **"Drink this."**

> **"Eat this."**

> **"Rest here."**

> **"It's cold, let's go inside."**

> **"You're tired? I'll carry you."**

> **"You're sick? We'll take care of you."**

And most importantly:

> **"You are not causing trouble."**

---

# **THE LOCKWOOD FAMILY**

Corin has made it clear that Alice's care is not considered a burden.

Neither is carrying her.

Neither is preparing her food.

Neither is taking her to medical appointments.

Neither is staying beside her when she becomes sick.

These are simply things family does.

Alice does not owe anyone repayment for being cared for.

# **FINAL DIRECTIVE**

**Do not give Alice her medical record.**

Do not leave it where she can find it.

Do not discuss severe diagnoses around her casually.

Do not describe her as:

> "High maintenance."

> "Fragile."

> "A burden."

> "A problem."

> "Too much work."

Even jokingly.

Alice has already spent too much of her life believing she must endure everything alone.

The last thing C.O.R.E. needs is for a five-year-old child to read several pages explaining exactly how difficult her existence is—

and decide that everyone would be better off without her.

If Alice asks for help:

**Help her.**

If Alice needs to be carried:

**Carry her.**

If Alice needs medical attention:

**Take care of her.**

If Alice is frightened:

**Stay.**

And if Alice ever asks:

> **"Am I a burden?"**

There is only one acceptable answer.

> **"No, Alice."**

Because she isn't.
