# NAME RECOVERY

The surname **Luminare** has been accepted as Clover's family name.

The origin of the name has not been conclusively established.

Clover herself appears comfortable with it.

When asked whether she wanted personnel to continue calling her simply "Clover," she answered:

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Clover is fine."

When asked whether Luminare should be included on official records:

> **CLOVER:**  
> "...Yes."

Pause.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "It feels like mine."

---

# PERSONAL SIGNIFICANCE

Clover does not remember her original birth name.

She does not remember her parents' faces.

She does not possess reliable records identifying the name she was given at birth.

For most of her adult life, **Clover** was the only name she could confidently identify as belonging to herself.

The addition of **Luminare** therefore represents more than an administrative correction.

It is a name she has chosen to carry forward.

Not necessarily because it restores something forgotten.

But because it gives the person she is now something that belongs to her.

She did not correct his handwriting.
