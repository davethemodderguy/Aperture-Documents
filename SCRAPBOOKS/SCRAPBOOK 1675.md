# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora told everyone.

She did not keep the encounter secret.

The following morning, she gathered the residents and explained that Jake had returned during the full moon.

She confirmed that:

- Jake had physically manifested;
    
- he had remained in the SAFEHOUSE for several hours;
    
- he could not speak;
    
- he did not appear hostile or distressed enough to leave immediately;
    
- he listened while Nora talked;
    
- he did not ask her to leave;
    
- he did not ask her to keep his presence secret;
    
- and he ultimately left before dawn.
    

The information produced an immediate change in resident behavior.

For the first time since Jake returned to the sky, the SAFEHOUSE residents knew that the blue star was not merely an astronomical phenomenon.

Jake was still coming home.

---

# 1. MORNING MEETING

Nora entered the common area carrying her family journal.

Several residents were already present.

Bao was preparing breakfast.

Jane was drinking coffee.

Neri was sitting nearby with her own cup.

Aeterna was standing beside the window.

Nora stopped in the middle of the room.

> **NORA:**  
> “I saw Jake last night.”

Everything stopped.

Bao turned around.

> **BAO:**  
> “What?”

Nora repeated herself.

> **NORA:**  
> “Jake.”

Silence.

> **NORA:**  
> “He came back.”

---

# 2. IMMEDIATE QUESTIONS

Several people began speaking at once.

> **JANE:**  
> “You saw him?”

> **BAO:**  
> “Where?”

> **NERI:**  
> “When?”

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Was he physically manifested?”

Nora raised both hands.

> **NORA:**  
> “One at a time!”

Everyone became quiet.

Nora took a breath.

> **NORA:**  
> “He was in the cafeteria.”

Bao stared at her.

> **BAO:**  
> “Was he okay?”

Nora hesitated.

> **NORA:**  
> “I don't know.”

She looked down.

> **NORA:**  
> “He didn't talk.”

---

# 3. THE CONVERSATION

Nora explained what happened.

She had been unable to sleep.

She had gone to the cafeteria.

Jake was there.

She sat with him.

He stayed.

She talked.

He listened.

That was essentially the entire encounter.

Nora emphasized this several times.

> **NORA:**  
> “I didn't make him explain anything.”

> **NORA:**  
> “I didn't ask why he left.”

> **NORA:**  
> “I didn't ask him to come back.”

> **NORA:**  
> “I just talked.”

Bao listened silently.

---

# 4. BAO'S RESPONSE

Bao looked down at the counter.

> **BAO:**  
> “Did he look scared?”

Nora thought about it.

> **NORA:**  
> “No.”

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> “Sad, maybe.”

Bao closed her eyes.

Nora continued:

> **NORA:**  
> “But he stayed.”

Bao looked at her.

> **NORA:**  
> “That's good, right?”

Bao nodded slowly.

> **BAO:**  
> “Yes.”

Her voice was quiet.

> **BAO:**  
> “That's very good.”

---

# 5. JANE'S QUESTION

Jane asked:

> **JANE:**  
> “Did he say anything?”

Nora shook her head.

> **NORA:**  
> “He can't.”

Jane nodded.

She understood.

> **JANE:**  
> “Did he communicate?”

> **NORA:**  
> “A little.”

> **JANE:**  
> “How?”

Nora shrugged.

> **NORA:**  
> “Mostly looking.”

Jane smiled faintly.

> **JANE:**  
> “Yeah.”

She looked away.

> **JANE:**  
> “Sounds like him.”

---

# 6. NERI

Neri had been silent throughout the conversation.

She eventually asked:

> **NERI:**  
> “Did he leave?”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “Before morning.”

Neri looked down at her coffee.

After several seconds:

> **NERI:**  
> “He came back.”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “Yeah.”

Neri took another sip.

> **NERI:**  
> “That's good.”

---

# 7. AETERNA

Aeterna asked the most direct question.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Did he seem comfortable with you?”

Nora considered the question.

> **NORA:**  
> “I think so.”

Aeterna waited.

> **NORA:**  
> “He didn't leave.”

That answer appeared sufficient.

Aeterna nodded.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Then you did well.”

Nora smiled.

---

# 8. THE JOURNAL

Nora opened her family journal.

She showed everyone the blank page.

It was no longer blank.

A tiny blue star occupied one corner.

Nobody immediately spoke.

Bao stared at it.

Jane leaned closer.

Neri adjusted her glasses.

Aeterna simply looked at the page.

Nora explained:

> **NORA:**  
> “I left it empty because I didn't have a picture.”

She touched the drawing.

> **NORA:**  
> “When I woke up, this was there.”

---

# 9. REACTION

Nobody claimed to know how the drawing appeared.

No one suggested removing it.

Neko later confirmed that the page contained no conventional ink residue consistent with ordinary drawing.

The mark was anomalous.

But its meaning was obvious enough.

Jake had seen the journal.

Jake had seen the empty page.

And Jake had chosen to leave something behind.

---

# 10. NEW FULL-MOON PROTOCOL

C.O.R.E. has revised the existing observation protocol.

On nights preceding a full moon:

- the cafeteria will remain accessible;
    
- food will be available without requiring interaction;
    
- lighting will remain low;
    
- no interception team will be deployed;
    
- no resident will be instructed to remain awake;
    
- no attempt will be made to force contact;
    
- cameras will continue passive observation;
    
- any direct encounter will be treated as a resident interaction, not a containment event.
    

Most importantly:

> **Jake is not to be hunted.**

If he comes home, he is home.

---

# 11. RESIDENT RESPONSE

The response from the SAFEHOUSE was unexpectedly unanimous.

Nobody suggested setting a trap.

Nobody suggested confronting Jake.

Nobody suggested locking the exits.

Nobody suggested forcing him to stay.

Instead, residents quietly began considering something simpler.

If Jake came back again—

Someone could leave food out.

Someone could leave a book.

Someone could leave a drawing.

Someone could simply sit nearby.

Not to make him return.

Just to make sure the room did not feel empty.

---

# 12. BAO

Later that afternoon, Bao entered the cafeteria.

She opened the refrigerator.

She placed a small plate inside.

Sandwich.

Fruit.

Cake.

Milk.

She closed the door.

Then she stopped.

She looked at the food.

And quietly added another slice of cake.

She left without saying anything.

---

# 13. NERI

Neri later entered the cafeteria.

She noticed the extra food.

She did not ask who it was for.

Instead, she took a small piece of paper from her pocket.

She drew a tiny blue flower.

She placed it beside the plate.

Then she left.

---

# 14. JANE

Jane noticed the flower.

She added something beside it.

A folded napkin.

On it, she had written:

> **“You don't have to stay.”**

Below it:

> **“But you can.”**

She left it there.

---

# 15. NORA

Nora eventually returned.

She looked at the growing collection.

Food.

Flower.

Note.

She smiled.

Then she added one final thing.

A small drawing of a blue star.

She placed it beside the others.

---

# FINAL ASSESSMENT

Nora's report changed the SAFEHOUSE's understanding of Jake's current behavior.

He is not merely a star that occasionally appears in the sky.

He is still capable of returning to the place he once called home.

He still remembers the people inside it.

He still eats their food.

He still visits his old room.

He still listens.

And, apparently, he is still capable of leaving tiny reminders that he was there.

The residents now understand something important:

They cannot undo twenty-five weeks in one night.

They cannot force Jake to trust them again.

They cannot demand that he come home.

But they can stop making the SAFEHOUSE empty for him.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

That evening, Nora stood outside beneath the rising moon.

She looked upward.

The blue star was already visible.

She raised a hand.

> **NORA:**  
> “See you tonight, Big Brother.”

She paused.

Then laughed softly.

> **NORA:**  
> “If you're coming.”

High above her, the blue star remained still.

No answer came.

But Nora stayed outside for another minute anyway.

Then she went inside.

For once, she did not wonder whether Jake remembered them.

She knew he did.
