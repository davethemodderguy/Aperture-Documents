# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **SECURITY PERSONNEL RECORD – ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: DIALYN – EMERGENCY DEFENSIVE EQUIPMENT**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although Dialyn's primary responsibilities are administrative and public-facing, KCA policy permits designated personnel to maintain emergency defensive equipment within secured workspaces.

Unlike field agents, Dialyn does not routinely carry firearms while performing her daily duties.

Her preference is to remain visibly approachable to both employees and visitors.

---

# **AUTHORIZED EMERGENCY EQUIPMENT**

Secured beneath Dialyn's desk is an Authority-issued emergency shotgun reserved for situations involving immediate threats to life.

Configuration:

- Compact profile.
    
- Shortened stock for confined office environments.
    
- One-handed operation possible in emergencies through the weapon's enhanced action design.
    
- Stored in a locked quick-release retention bracket beneath the desk.
    

The firearm is inspected on a regular maintenance schedule and is never left unsecured.

---

# **AMMUNITION**

The weapon is normally stored unloaded.

Emergency ammunition is secured separately within the same locked compartment.

Authorized load:

- **Eviscerator-Tipped Slugs** _(Restricted KCA Defensive Ammunition)_
    

These specialized rounds are classified for emergency defensive use by authorized personnel and are subject to strict inventory control.

Use requires immediate incident reporting and a full post-incident investigation.

---

# **RATIONALE**

When questioned why she selected a shotgun instead of a sidearm, Dialyn answered:

> "...If I ever need it..."

She looked toward the office entrance.

> "...Something has already gone very, very wrong."

She later clarified that she hopes never to remove it from its storage bracket.
