# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / PERSONAL RESEARCH ARCHIVE**

# **THE NOTES SHE LEFT BEHIND**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY
**Subjects:** Corin / Orphie
**Status:** **ONGOING**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie reads Corin's research notes every day.

It has become a ritual.

Not because the notes contain instructions for saving her.

They don't.

They contain something much more difficult to understand:

**Corin before she stopped believing in herself.**

The notes document her wings, body, two hearts, feathers, regeneration, and countless smaller observations about her existence.

But scattered between the scientific observations are pieces of the person Corin used to be.

Little jokes.

Little discoveries.

Little hopes.

And occasionally, a sentence that makes Orphie stop reading.

---

# **THE DAILY ROUTINE**

Every evening, Orphie finds Corin outside.

She brings the notebook.

She sits beneath the tree.

Corin usually remains wrapped in her wings.

Sometimes Orphie reads aloud.

Sometimes she simply reads silently while Corin listens.

Sometimes they talk about whatever happens to come to mind.

These conversations are important.

They are also dangerous.

Orphie has learned that one poorly chosen sentence can undo hours of progress.

---

# **THE RESEARCH NOTES**

Corin's notes cover almost every part of herself.

### **WINGS**

She documented their structure, movement, regeneration, flight, feather behavior, and anomalous properties.

### **BODY**

She recorded physical changes, environmental responses, regeneration, blood composition, and other abnormalities.

### **HEARTS**

She carefully studied the relationship between her two hearts and their response to physical and emotional stress.

### **FEATHERS**

She documented their many functions before they became dormant.

Some entries read like scientific papers.

Others read like a child excitedly discovering something new.

---

# **THE SENTENCE**

Orphie still returns to the same page.

The handwriting is bright and energetic.

> **"I live on hope, as long as I spread hope and I have hope of my own, I will move on, live with Orphie :D"**

The **":D"** remains unmistakably intentional.

Orphie has read it so many times that she no longer needs to look at the page.

She knows every word.

---

# **THE CONVERSATIONS**

Sometimes Corin asks what Orphie is reading.

> **"Your research."**

> **"Why?"**

> **"Because I like knowing what you were thinking."**

Corin looks away.

> **"I don't remember thinking that much."**

Orphie smiles.

> **"You wrote hundreds of pages."**

> **"...Oh."**

Sometimes Corin almost smiles.

Those moments are rare.

Orphie treasures them.

---

# **THE WRONG WORDS**

But Orphie does not always know what to say.

She wants Corin to recover.

She wants her to eat properly.

She wants her to come inside.

She wants her to care about something again.

And sometimes, without realizing it, she turns those wishes into words that hurt.

---

## **INCIDENT 01 — "TRY HARDER"**

Corin had refused another warm meal.

Orphie eventually said:

> **"You have to try harder, Corin."**

Silence.

Corin looked down.

> **"...I'm trying."**

Orphie immediately realized what she had implied.

But the damage was already done.

Corin quietly asked:

> **"Is it not enough?"**

---

## **INCIDENT 02 — "YOU USED TO"**

Orphie once mentioned flying.

> **"You used to love flying."**

Corin looked at her dormant wings.

> **"I know."**

> **"Don't you want to fly again?"**

> **"No."**

Orphie hesitated.

> **"I think you'd be happier if you did."**

Corin's wings closed around herself.

> **"...Then I guess I can't make you happy."**

Orphie immediately apologized.

---

## **INCIDENT 03 — "THE OLD CORIN"**

This was the worst one.

Orphie said:

> **"I just want the old Corin back."**

Corin became completely silent.

She eventually whispered:

> **"Then you don't want me."**

Orphie denied it.

Corin did not believe her.

---

# **THE PATTERN**

Orphie has begun noticing something.

Corin does not usually become angry when hurt.

She becomes smaller.

Her wings close.

Her ears lower.

Her tails become still.

Her voice becomes quieter.

And then she says:

> **"It's okay."**

It rarely is.

---

# **ORPHIE'S FEAR**

Orphie has become afraid of speaking.

Not because Corin is dangerous.

Because she knows how fragile Corin's remaining hope is.

A sentence can become evidence.

A hesitation can become rejection.

A poorly phrased attempt at encouragement can become:

> *"I'm not good enough."*

---

# **CORIN'S SIDE**

Corin knows Orphie is trying.

She has said so.

She knows Orphie loves her.

She has said so.

But knowing something intellectually does not always make her believe it emotionally.

That distinction remains one of the greatest obstacles to her recovery.

---

# **ONE EVENING**

Orphie was reading the research notes again.

Corin sat beside her.

After a while, Corin asked:

> **"Do you hate talking to me?"**

Orphie looked confused.

> **"No."**

> **"You always have to be careful."**

Orphie was silent.

Corin continued:

> **"Because I'm fragile."**

> **"No."**

> **"Because everything hurts me."**

Orphie hesitated.

Corin looked away.

> **"See?"**

Orphie immediately regretted the hesitation.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

Orphie closed the notebook.

She looked at Corin.

> **"I don't know how to talk to you without accidentally hurting you."**

Corin remained silent.

Then:

> **"You don't have to know."**

Orphie frowned.

> **"What?"**

Corin pulled her wings closer.

> **"You can just talk."**

---

# **THE IMPORTANT PART**

That sentence stayed with Orphie.

Because Corin wasn't asking for perfect words.

She wasn't asking Orphie to repair her.

She wasn't asking for the correct explanation.

She simply wanted someone to remain present.

Even if they made mistakes.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Every day, Orphie reads about the Corin who once believed in hope.

The girl who studied her wings.

The girl who was fascinated by her two hearts.

The girl who experimented with her feathers.

The girl who wanted to live with Orphie.

The girl who added:

> **":D"**

Then Orphie closes the notebook.

And talks to the Corin who sits beneath the snow.

Sometimes she says exactly the right thing.

Sometimes she says the wrong thing.

Sometimes Corin smiles.

Sometimes she cries.

Sometimes her wings close completely around her.

But they keep talking.

Because Orphie has finally understood something.

She cannot choose every word perfectly.

She cannot repair what happened.

She cannot manufacture hope.

But she can continue sitting beside Corin.

And Corin can continue answering.

Even if the answers are quiet.

Even if they hurt.

Even if neither of them knows where the conversation is going.

For now, the conversation itself is proof that Corin has not completely disappeared.
