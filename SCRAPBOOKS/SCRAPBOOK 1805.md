# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The investigation into the assault against Jake Akana has identified a potentially critical piece of evidence.

**The wounds are consistent with injuries inflicted by a sacrificial dagger.**

A dagger matching the relevant wound characteristics was subsequently discovered inside Jake's sealed residence.

The weapon was not initially identified during the first response.

It was located during a later, methodical forensic sweep after investigators expanded their examination beyond the immediate area where Jake had been found.

The dagger has been secured for specialist examination.

Its construction, markings, and possible anomalous properties remain under investigation. C.O.R.E. has not yet established whether it was used in a conventional ritual, an anomalous procedure, or an assault in which the weapon's sacrificial design served a different purpose.

The discovery changes the classification of the incident.

This may not have been an ordinary stabbing committed by an assailant who happened to possess an unusual weapon.

The weapon itself may be part of the motive.

Meanwhile, the residential floor remains sealed as a crime scene.

Jake is still receiving medical care.

Aries Akana and Clover, who ordinarily reside with Jake, have been temporarily displaced from their rooms. With the relevant residential area inaccessible, both have been sleeping on a couch outside the sealed sector.

Neither has complained formally.

Both have been observed remaining awake well beyond their usual hours.

C.O.R.E. Welfare has been instructed to address their immediate accommodation needs without compromising the investigation.

---

# 1. MEDICAL FORENSIC FINDING

**SOURCE:** C.O.R.E. Medical Division  
**PATIENT:** Jake Akana  
**STATUS:** Critical; ongoing treatment

A review of the wound characteristics identified similarities to injuries associated with a narrow sacrificial dagger.

The assessment considered wound geometry, penetration characteristics, and the pattern of injuries documented during emergency treatment.

The conclusion is provisional but sufficiently supported to justify specialist forensic analysis.

The medical team has not attempted to reconstruct the assault solely from the wounds.

The injuries establish that a bladed weapon was used.

They do not independently establish the assailant's identity, intention, or the circumstances under which the weapon was obtained.

However, the possibility that the weapon was specifically designed for sacrificial use warrants further investigation.

## Specialist Questions

The Medical and Anomalous Forensics divisions have been asked to determine:

- Whether the wounds are consistent with the recovered dagger.
    
- Whether the dagger has properties that could account for any unusual aspects of Jake's condition.
    
- Whether the weapon left anomalous residues detectable through existing laboratory procedures.
    
- Whether the injuries show evidence of an effect beyond ordinary physical trauma.
    
- Whether the weapon's design has any known connection to previous Aperture incidents or documented anomalous traditions.
    

No conclusion regarding ritual activity has been approved.

The term _sacrificial dagger_ currently describes the suspected weapon class, not a confirmed explanation for the attack.

# 2. RECOVERY OF THE DAGGER

**LOCATION:** Jake's private residence  
**DISCOVERY:** Subsequent forensic sweep  
**CUSTODY:** C.O.R.E. Anomalous Forensics

The dagger was found during a later examination of the sealed room.

Its location had not been recorded during the initial emergency response.

This does not establish that the weapon appeared after the first examination. Investigators are reviewing the original scene photographs, response notes, and available recordings to determine whether it was present but obscured, overlooked, or otherwise missed.

The weapon was documented in place before recovery.

Its position was photographed, its surrounding area was examined, and its chain of custody was established.

The dagger was then secured for specialist analysis.

No resident was permitted to handle it.

No personnel member was authorized to test its properties outside an approved forensic environment.

### Initial Assessment

The recovered weapon is consistent with the class of weapon indicated by Jake's wounds.

A definitive match has not yet been declared.

The weapon's possible connection to the assault is considered highly significant.

Its discovery inside the room is also consistent with the current hypothesis that the assailant departed without taking it.

That interpretation remains provisional.

Investigators have not established whether the weapon was deliberately left behind, dropped during the struggle, or abandoned for another reason.

---

# 3. ANOMALOUS FORENSIC PRECAUTIONS

Because the weapon may have been used for sacrificial purposes, C.O.R.E. has imposed additional handling restrictions.

The dagger is to be treated as potentially anomalous until its properties are understood.

Personnel are prohibited from:

- Performing unsanctioned rituals or symbolic tests.
    
- Allowing blood or other biological material to contact the weapon outside controlled analysis.
    
- Using the weapon against any living subject.
    
- Removing it from secure storage without authorization.
    
- Assuming that its appearance accurately describes its function.
    
- Attempting to activate any suspected property through trial and error.
    

The weapon will undergo non-destructive examination before any procedure that might alter it.

C.O.R.E. is particularly concerned about the possibility that the dagger could interact with biological, spiritual, or anomalous systems in ways not captured by conventional weapons analysis.

This concern is relevant to Jake's status as a Weeping Angel and a human manifestation of a literal star.

It is not proof that the weapon was designed specifically for him.

That possibility is being investigated.

The affected residential floor remains closed to ordinary access.

The restriction is necessary to preserve evidence associated with:

- The assault itself.
    
- The recovered dagger.
    
- Bloodstain distribution.
    
- The sequence of movement through the room.
    
- Potential anomalous residues.
    
- The unexplained spatial discontinuity associated with the assailant's arrival and departure.
    

Cleaning, furniture relocation, and routine maintenance have been suspended within the sealed area.

The restriction also affects residents whose personal belongings and usual sleeping arrangements are located within the restricted zone.

This has produced an immediate welfare problem.

The investigation must preserve the scene.

The residents still need somewhere to sleep.

Those two requirements are not mutually exclusive, but they have not yet been reconciled adequately.

**LOCATION:** Corridor outside the sealed sector

The corridor lights had been dimmed.

The couch was occupied.

Aries was sitting at the other end, his feet on the floor.

**CLOVER:**

# 8. WELFARE ASSESSMENT

Aries and Clover are experiencing understandable distress following the assault.

Their proximity to the sealed residence appears to provide a sense of continuity while Jake remains hospitalized.

It may also reflect uncertainty about what will happen when he wakes.

Neither resident has stated that they believe Jake will interpret their absence as abandonment.

C.O.R.E. will not assume that interpretation on their behalf.

However, both have previously demonstrated that Jake's presence and welfare are important to them.

The temporary couch arrangement appears to be a choice made under emotionally difficult circumstances.

That choice should be respected, provided the residents are offered reasonable alternatives and are not neglecting their own basic needs.

## Welfare Directives

- Offer both residents proper temporary accommodation.
    
- Provide clean bedding, food, and access to washrooms.
    
- Ensure they receive accurate medical updates through authorized personnel.
    
- Do not promise a recovery timeline that medical staff cannot support.
    
- Do not pressure them to discuss the assault.
    
- Do not allow their temporary accommodation to become an unofficial security post.
    
- Ensure they are not expected to monitor the sealed scene themselves.
    

Aries is Jake's older brother.

He is not responsible for solving the investigation.

Clover is Jake's roommate.

She is not responsible for remaining awake until he recovers.

Both are allowed to be frightened.

Both are allowed to sleep.

Neither needs to prove how much they care by making themselves miserable.

# 9. SECURITY REVIEW — POSSIBLE CONNECTION BETWEEN WEAPON AND TELEPORTATION

The discovery of a sacrificial dagger raises a further question.

Could the weapon have facilitated the assailant's anomalous entry or departure?

At present, there is no evidence establishing that connection.

The spatial discontinuity and the dagger are being investigated as separate evidence categories until a relationship can be demonstrated.

C.O.R.E. has identified three broad possibilities:

**Hypothesis A — Independent Features**

The assailant possessed a teleportation ability and carried a sacrificial dagger as a separate weapon.

**Hypothesis B — Anomalous Weapon**

The dagger possesses a property related to spatial displacement, and the assailant used it as part of the intrusion.

**Hypothesis C — Shared External Mechanism**

Both the teleportation and the dagger may be associated with a larger anomalous system or ritual.

None of these hypotheses is confirmed.

Investigators are specifically prohibited from selecting the most dramatic explanation simply because it connects two unusual findings.

The weapon will be examined.

The spatial data will be examined.

The conclusions will follow the evidence.
