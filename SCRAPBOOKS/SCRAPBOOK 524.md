# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **RECOVERY / PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT REPORT**

# **THE RETURN, AGAIN**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **RECOVERED**  
**Condition:** Severely deteriorated / Ether-stable  
**Primary Concern:** **Acute Self-Worthlessness**

---

# **RECOVERY**

Corin was found beneath another tree, several kilometers from the previous recovery site.

She was sitting against the trunk.

Her eyes were open.

She was asleep.

Her **right arm and left leg were missing**.

A thin layer of frost had formed across both eyes, sealing them shut beneath a translucent layer of ice.

She did not respond when the search team approached.

Her remaining body was cold.

Her Ether signature, however, remained stable.

---

# **THE NOTE**

A small piece of paper was found beside Corin.

The message was written in her handwriting.

It contained only:

> **"Don't, I'm not worth your time."**

There was no additional explanation.

No request for help.

No indication of where she had been going.

The wording was interpreted as another attempt to discourage anyone from searching for her.

It failed.

---

# **RECOVERY**

Guiding Light immediately approached Corin.

She carefully removed the accumulated snow.

The frost covering Corin's eyes remained intact.

Guiding Light placed a hand against her face.

The ice began melting almost immediately.

Corin's eyelids remained closed.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Corin."

No response.

> **"We're taking you home."**

Corin eventually opened her eyes.

She looked at Guiding Light.

Then at the recovery team.

Her expression immediately changed.

She understood what had happened.

She had been found again.

---

# **THE MISSING LIMBS**

Corin's right arm and left leg were recovered separately.

Both remained viable for reattachment due to her Ether-sustained physiology.

The limbs were carefully transported back to the treehouse.

Guiding Light reconstructed the damaged connections.

Orphie assisted with the stitching.

The process was successful.

Corin's body was restored to its previous configuration.

---

# **THE FROST**

The frost covering Corin's eyes caused no permanent damage.

Once returned to a stable environment, the remaining ice melted completely.

Corin's vision returned to normal.

There was no evidence of injury to the eyes themselves.

She simply stared at the ceiling afterward.

She did not speak.

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

Orphie was frightened.

Then angry.

Then overwhelmed.

She had spent days searching.

She had found Corin missing pieces of herself.

She had read the notes.

She had imagined the worst.

When Corin was finally returned, Orphie confronted her.

> **"What were you thinking?"**

Corin remained silent.

> **"You keep disappearing!"**

Corin looked down.

> **"You leave your arms and legs behind like they mean nothing!"**

Corin's shoulders tightened.

Orphie continued.

> **"Do you have any idea how scared we were?"**

Corin finally whispered:

> **"...I'm sorry."**

Orphie was still upset.

> **"Sorry isn't enough!"**

Corin stopped looking at her.

---

# **THE DAMAGE**

Orphie did not intend to hurt Corin.

She wanted Corin to understand how much her disappearance affected everyone.

Instead, the confrontation reinforced the exact belief Corin had already been struggling with.

Corin's internal reasoning was immediate:

> **I caused another problem.**

Then:

> **They had to search again.**

Then:

> **I made Orphie angry.**

Then:

> **I'm still a burden.**

The scolding therefore had the opposite effect from what Orphie intended.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light noticed almost immediately.

She gently interrupted.

> **"Orphie."**

Orphie stopped.

Guiding Light looked at Corin.

Corin had pulled her arms against herself.

Her eyes were fixed on the floor.

Guiding Light understood.

> **"She already thinks she's a burden."**

Orphie's expression changed.

> **"...I know."**

> **"Then yelling at her won't convince her otherwise."**

Orphie looked devastated.

> **"I was scared."**

Guiding Light nodded.

> **"I know."**

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin eventually spoke.

Her voice was quiet.

> **"I didn't want you to waste your time."**

Orphie immediately looked at her.

> **"You are not a waste of my time."**

Corin shook her head.

> **"You had to find me."**

> **"Yes."**

> **"Again."**

> **"Yes."**

Corin looked away.

> **"I'm sorry."**

Orphie sat beside her.

This time, she did not scold her.

She simply stayed.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT**

The incident demonstrates a recurring cycle:

**Corin feels worthless.**

↓

**Corin withdraws.**

↓

**Others search for her.**

↓

**Corin interprets the search as proof that she causes problems.**

↓

**Corin feels even more worthless.**

↓

**Corin withdraws further.**

The cycle is self-reinforcing.

The recovery itself does not resolve the underlying belief.

# **FINAL NOTE**

Corin left behind another message:

> **"Don't, I'm not worth your time."**

She expected them to listen.

They didn't.

They found her sleeping beneath a tree with frost sealing her eyes and half her limbs missing.

They brought her home.

They fixed her.

And then, for a moment, the person who loved her most was so frightened that she accidentally confirmed everything Corin already believed about herself.

Corin did not need to be told that Orphie was scared.

She needed to understand **why Orphie was scared.**

Not because Corin was a burden.

Because Corin mattered.

That distinction remains difficult for her to believe.

**End of Recovery / Psychological Incident Report**
