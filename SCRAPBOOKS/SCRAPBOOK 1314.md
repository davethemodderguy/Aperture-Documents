# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The SAFEHOUSE has recently undergone a substantial residential revamp.

As part of the renovation, previously unused and improvised resident spaces have been replaced with dedicated accommodation.

Jake has consequently been assigned a **proper private room.**

The former storage locker has been retired from residential use.

The wooden sign has been preserved.

It has been transferred to Jake's new door.

It still reads:

> **Jake's Room**  
> **please knock, I can't take suprises**

The spelling remains unchanged.

---

# 1. THE REVAMP

The SAFEHOUSE residential wing has been reorganized to provide residents with more appropriate private spaces.

The renovation includes:

- dedicated bedrooms;
    
- improved sound insulation;
    
- individual lighting controls;
    
- improved ventilation;
    
- private storage;
    
- safer doors;
    
- accessible emergency release mechanisms;
    
- improved common areas;
    
- quieter corridors;
    
- and clearer separation between residential and operational spaces.
    

The purpose is straightforward:

> **Residents should have somewhere that feels like home.**

Jake's accommodation received particular attention.

Not because he requested anything elaborate.

Because he requested:

> **"A quiet room."**

---

# 2. JAKE'S NEW ROOM

Jake's new room is small.

It is not luxurious.

It is simply comfortable.

The room contains:

- a single bed;
    
- warm blankets;
    
- a pillow;
    
- a small bedside table;
    
- a reading lamp;
    
- a bookshelf;
    
- a small desk;
    
- a chair;
    
- personal storage;
    
- a wardrobe;
    
- a waste bin;
    
- a small refrigerator;
    
- and a soft floor mat.
    

The walls are intentionally plain.

Jake requested no decorative theme.

He did, however, request:

> **"Not too bright."**

The lighting was adjusted accordingly.

---

# 3. THE DOOR

The door is now significantly more substantial than the former storage locker door.

It closes quietly.

It does not slam.

It cannot be accidentally opened from outside without normal access.

Most importantly:

**It provides privacy.**

Jake's request remains simple.

> **Knock first.**

Personnel must wait for a response before entering unless an emergency requires immediate access.

The rule is now part of the standard SAFEHOUSE residential policy.

---

# 4. THE SIGN

Jake's original wooden sign has been mounted on the new door.

It was not replaced with a professionally printed sign.

C.O.R.E. considered doing so.

The proposal was rejected.

The original sign is part of Jake's room.

It was made by him.

It says what he wants.

Therefore, it stays.

> **Jake's Room**  
> **please knock, I can't take suprises**

---

# 5. WARM MILK

Jake has also been provided with a small refrigerator.

The first item placed inside it was:

**Milk.**

Warm milk remains available to him upon request.

A small insulated container is kept in the kitchen specifically for this purpose.

Personnel are instructed not to make an event out of it.

If Jake asks for warm milk:

Give Jake warm milk.

No interrogation.

No jokes.

No:

> "Aren't you a little old for that?"

No:

> "You need to drink something healthier."

No:

> "Why warm milk?"

Just warm milk.

---

# 6. FIRST EVENING

Jake entered his new room shortly after the renovation was completed.

He stood in the doorway.

Looked inside.

Looked at the bed.

Then the bookshelf.

Then the window.

Then the door.

He walked inside.

He placed his teddy bear on the bed.

Then picked it back up.

He apparently decided it belonged with him.

He sat down.

The mattress compressed beneath him.

Jake looked surprised.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...It's soft."

A nearby welfare officer nodded.

> **WELFARE OFFICER:**  
> "It's yours."

Jake looked at the room again.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Mine?"

> **WELFARE OFFICER:**  
> "Yes."

Jake was silent for several seconds.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Okay."

---

# 7. THE MILK

Approximately twenty minutes later, Jake requested warm milk.

The request was made through a handwritten note.

It contained two words:

> **"Warm milk."**

The request was fulfilled.

Jake sat on his bed with the cup in both hands.

His teddy bear rested beside him.

He drank slowly.

No one entered.

No one watched.

No one asked questions.

For approximately forty minutes, Jake simply sat there.

---

# 8. PRIVACY

The most important improvement appears to be the absence of interruption.

Jake is no longer sleeping in an improvised storage space.

He is no longer dependent on a hallway-adjacent locker.

He no longer has to worry about someone opening a storage door because they need something from inside.

He has a proper room.

A proper door.

A proper bed.

And a clear expectation that people will respect his privacy.

This is particularly important given his established response to unexpected entry.

---

# 9. NORA

Nora immediately attempted to visit.

She approached the door.

Read the sign.

Knocked.

Waited.

Nothing.

She knocked again.

Still nothing.

Nora waited.

Approximately three minutes later, the door opened.

Jake looked out.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Hi."

> **NORA:**  
> "Hi."

She looked inside.

> **NORA:**  
> "Can I come in?"

Jake nodded.

Nora entered.

She looked around.

> **NORA:**  
> "You have a real room now!"

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Yeah."

Nora pointed at the bed.

> **NORA:**  
> "Can I jump on it?"

Jake immediately shook his head.

> **JAKE:**  
> "No."

Nora accepted this.

> **NORA:**  
> "Okay."

She sat down.

---

# 10. NERI

Neri did not enter.

She knocked once.

Jake opened the door slightly.

Neri held out a cup.

Warm milk.

Jake looked at it.

Then at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Thank you."

Neri nodded.

She handed it over.

Then left.

Jake closed the door.

---

# 11. AETERNA

Aeterna approached later that evening.

She knocked once.

Waited.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Come in."

Aeterna entered.

Jake was sitting on his bed.

His white hair was slightly messy.

His azure eyes glowed faintly in the dim room.

The tiny celestial particles surrounding him were barely visible.

Aeterna looked around.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Better."

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> "It's quiet."

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Good."

Aeterna sat beside the door.

She did not stay long.

Before leaving:

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Good night, Jake."

> **JAKE:**  
> "Good night."

The door closed.

---

# 12. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The SAFEHOUSE revamp has produced a significant improvement in Jake's living conditions.

The change is not primarily architectural.

It is psychological.

Jake now has:

**A room that belongs to him.**

**A door that others respect.**

**A bed.**

**A place for his teddy bear.**

**Warm milk.**

**Privacy.**

And, importantly:

**The ability to decide when someone enters.**

The storage locker served as a temporary refuge.

The new room is a home.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Late that evening, a security camera recorded Jake sitting beside his window.

He had a cup of warm milk.

His teddy bear was beside him.

The room was quiet.

No one entered.

No one called for him.

No one needed anything.

Jake looked around the room once.

Then he quietly said:

> **JAKE:**  
> "I like it."

He climbed into bed.

Pulled the blanket over himself.

His teddy bear was placed beside his pillow.

The light went out.

The door remained closed.

The sign remained visible.

> **Jake's Room**  
> **please knock, I can't take suprises**

For once, the quiet wasn't because everyone had forgotten him.

The quiet was because everyone understood:

**Jake was home.**
