### RESIDENT WELFARE & MEDICAL DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-JAKE-238

**SUBJECT:** Jake / Nora — First Confirmed Full-Moon Contact  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — SECTOR 4-C

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

For the first time since Jake began returning to SAFEHOUSE during full-moon nights, a resident encountered him while he was physically manifested.

The resident was **Nora**.

Nora was awake.

Jake was already in the cafeteria.

Unlike previous encounters, Jake did not leave when another resident appeared.

He remained.

Nora sat with him.

Jake could not speak.

Nora talked.

For approximately forty-three minutes, Nora spoke about whatever came to mind.

Jake listened.

That was the entirety of the interaction.

No confrontation occurred.

No questions were demanded.

No attempt was made to convince Jake to return.

They simply sat together beneath the cafeteria's dim lighting.

---

# 1. 00:17

Nora was unable to sleep.

She left her room.

She intended to get something to drink.

The SAFEHOUSE was quiet.

Most residents were asleep.

She entered the cafeteria.

The lights were off except for the low overnight illumination.

Nora stopped.

Someone was sitting at one of the tables.

She recognized him immediately.

White hair.

Azure eyes.

Small wings.

The familiar arrow through his chest.

Jake.

Nora froze.

Jake looked up.

Their eyes met.

Neither moved.

---

# 2. INITIAL RESPONSE

Nora's first instinct was to speak.

She almost called his name.

Instead, she quietly said:

> **NORA:**  
> “...Oh.”

Jake continued looking at her.

He did not leave.

Nora slowly approached.

She pulled out the chair opposite him.

> **NORA:**  
> “Can I sit?”

Jake gave a small nod.

Nora sat.

Neither spoke for several seconds.

Then Nora noticed something.

Jake's mouth did not move.

She remembered.

He could not speak.

---

# 3. NO QUESTIONS

Nora did not ask:

> “Why did you leave?”

She did not ask:

> “Are you coming back?”

She did not ask:

> “Are you okay?”

She did not ask:

> “Do you still love us?”

She did not ask him to forgive anyone.

Instead, she looked down at the table.

And began talking.

---

# 4. NORA TALKS

> **NORA:**  
> “I can't sleep.”

Jake listened.

> **NORA:**  
> “I haven't been sleeping very well lately.”

Jake remained still.

> **NORA:**  
> “Aeterna tells me I should.”

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> “She says lying in bed with your eyes open doesn't count.”

Jake's expression changed slightly.

Not quite a smile.

But something close.

Nora noticed.

> **NORA:**  
> “Yeah. I know.”

She rested her arms on the table.

---

# 5. RANDOM SUBJECTS

Nora continued.

She talked about everything.

Nothing seemed too insignificant.

She complained about food.

She talked about Alice.

She mentioned Frost.

She described something ridiculous Iris had done earlier.

She talked about the Tachyon store.

She talked about Neri.

She talked about Bao.

She talked about Aeterna.

She talked about how annoying it was when everyone kept telling her to sleep.

Jake listened.

Sometimes he nodded.

Sometimes he looked at her.

Sometimes he looked down at the table.

He never spoke.

---

# 6. NORA'S FAMILY JOURNAL

Eventually, Nora pulled the family journal from her waist.

She placed it on the table.

> **NORA:**  
> “I added something.”

Jake looked at it.

Nora opened the journal.

She showed him several new pages.

Photographs.

Drawings.

Notes.

Family memories.

A page containing a photograph of Neri.

Another of Elsher.

Another of Bao.

A photograph of Nora herself.

Then a page that had been left mostly blank.

Nora touched it.

> **NORA:**  
> “I don't know what to put here.”

Jake looked at the page.

Nora continued:

> **NORA:**  
> “I wanted to put you.”

Jake remained silent.

> **NORA:**  
> “But I don't have a new picture.”

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> “So I left it empty.”

Jake looked at the blank page for a long time.

---

# 7. THE STAR

Nora eventually looked through the cafeteria window.

The blue star was visible.

> **NORA:**  
> “That's you, right?”

Jake nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “I knew it.”

She smiled faintly.

> **NORA:**  
> “You know, you're really bright.”

Jake looked away.

Nora laughed softly.

> **NORA:**  
> “Sorry.”

---

# 8. NORA'S CONFESSION

The conversation eventually became quieter.

Nora stopped talking about ordinary things.

She looked down at her hands.

> **NORA:**  
> “I thought you were gone.”

Jake looked at her.

> **NORA:**  
> “Not dead.”

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> “Just... gone.”

She rubbed her eyes.

> **NORA:**  
> “I kept thinking maybe you'd come back.”

Jake remained still.

> **NORA:**  
> “And then you did.”

Her voice became quieter.

> **NORA:**  
> “But you don't talk anymore.”

Jake lowered his gaze.

Nora immediately shook her head.

> **NORA:**  
> “That's okay.”

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> “You don't have to.”

---

# 9. LISTENING

Nora continued talking.

Jake continued listening.

He did not attempt to communicate through gestures beyond occasional nods.

He did not write.

He did not draw.

He did not leave.

He simply listened.

For someone who had spent months believing nobody would listen to him, the reversal was notable.

Nora did not ask him to explain his pain.

She simply filled the silence.

---

# 10. 00:49

Nora eventually leaned back in her chair.

> **NORA:**  
> “I don't really know what I'm doing.”

Jake looked at her.

> **NORA:**  
> “I'm talking to a star at midnight.”

Jake tilted his head slightly.

> **NORA:**  
> “A star that used to be a kid.”

She smiled.

> **NORA:**  
> “You're still a kid, though.”

Jake looked mildly offended.

Nora laughed.

> **NORA:**  
> “Okay, okay.”

---

# 11. SILENCE

Eventually, Nora stopped talking.

For approximately two minutes, neither moved.

The cafeteria was silent.

Moonlight entered through the windows.

Jake's eyes reflected the blue light.

Nora looked at him.

> **NORA:**  
> “You can stay.”

Jake looked toward the hallway.

Then back at her.

Nora continued:

> **NORA:**  
> “I won't tell anyone.”

Jake remained still.

> **NORA:**  
> “Unless you want me to.”

Jake nodded once.

---

# 12. THE LAST THING NORA SAID

At approximately 01:00, Nora stood.

Jake looked up.

> **NORA:**  
> “I'm going back to bed.”

She hesitated.

> **NORA:**  
> “You can keep sitting here.”

Jake nodded.

Nora started walking away.

She stopped at the doorway.

Turned around.

And said:

> **NORA:**  
> “I'm glad you're still here.”

Jake did not respond.

He could not.

Nora smiled faintly.

> **NORA:**  
> “Goodnight, Big Brother.”

She left.

---

# 13. AFTER NORA LEFT

Jake remained seated.

He did not immediately return to the cafeteria's food.

He did not leave the SAFEHOUSE.

He simply remained at the table.

Approximately three minutes later, he looked toward the doorway through which Nora had left.

Then he looked toward the window.

The blue star remained visible in the night sky.

Jake stayed until approximately 03:42.

Then he quietly left.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The interaction produced no measurable anomalous event.

No dimensional fluctuation.

No stellar discharge.

No restoration effect.

No verbal communication from Jake.

From a conventional operational perspective, almost nothing happened.

A resident who could not speak sat at a table.

Another resident talked.

They remained together.

That was all.

It may also have been the most meaningful interaction Jake has had since leaving SAFEHOUSE.

Nora did not try to bring him home.

She did not demand answers.

She did not ask him to forgive anyone.

She simply gave him something he had been missing for months:

**Someone who stayed long enough to talk.**

And Jake, for once, did not have to say anything.

He only had to listen.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

At 03:43, the cafeteria was empty again.

The chair where Nora had been sitting remained slightly pulled away from the table.

Jake had left no note.

No drawing.

No message.

Nothing.

Except one small detail.

The next morning, Nora found her family journal on her bedside table.

The previously blank page was no longer blank.

There was a tiny blue star drawn in the corner.

No signature.

No explanation.

Just a star.
