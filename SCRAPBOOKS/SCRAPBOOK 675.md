### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-LOG**

# **CORIN — TINY CAT FORM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin Lockwood  
**Status:** **FULLY RECOVERED / CAT FORM ACTIVE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has returned to her tiny cat form.

There was no emergency.

No anomalous incident.

No medical reason.

She simply decided:

> **"I wanna be a cat."**

And became one.

---

# **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

Corin's cat form is extremely small.

Small enough to:

- Fit comfortably in one hand.
    
- Curl up inside a hoodie pocket.
    
- Sleep on someone's shoulder.
    
- Disappear beneath a blanket.
    
- Sit inside a cereal bowl if nobody stops her.
    

Her wings remain present in miniature form.

Her twin tails remain.

Her cat ears remain.

Her anomalous feather properties remain available, although Corin rarely uses them while in this form.

---

# **BEHAVIOR**

Corin immediately became considerably less concerned about everything.

She naps.

She wanders.

She climbs onto furniture.

She watches people.

She occasionally knocks things over.

She follows people around without explanation.

She will also sit somewhere completely inconvenient and refuse to move.

When asked to return to humanoid form:

> **"No."**

When asked why:

> **"Cat."**

No further reasoning was provided.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie found Corin asleep on her shoulder.

She didn't move for approximately forty minutes.

When someone asked why she wasn't moving, Orphie quietly replied:

> **"She's comfortable."**

Corin's tiny tail flicked once.

She continued sleeping.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi immediately recognized the behavior.

> **"Oh, she's back to this."**

Corin opened one eye.

> **"Mrrp."**

Lumi smiled.

> **"Yep. Definitely."**

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The SAFEHOUSE has collectively accepted the situation.

Nobody knows where Corin is at any given moment.

She may be:

- On the couch.
    
- Inside a blanket.
    
- On the kitchen counter.
    
- In someone's lap.
    
- On top of a bookshelf.
    
- Sleeping inside an empty box.
    
- Following Neko.
    
- Following Orphie.
    
- Following Lumi.
    
- Somewhere she absolutely should not be.
    

The final possibility has occurred frequently.

---

# **FEATHER LANTERNS**

Interestingly, the Feather Lanterns remain active while Corin is in cat form.

Their azure light has not diminished.

Several lanterns actually brighten whenever Corin becomes particularly content.

C.O.R.E. has stopped attempting to explain this.

---

# **CORIN'S NOTEBOOK**

A new entry was discovered.

The handwriting is noticeably smaller.

> **"Being a cat is easier."**

Below it:

> **"I can sleep whenever I want."**

Then:

> **"People pet me."**

A final line:

> **"Good."**

A tiny paw print was stamped beneath the entry.

Nobody knows how she managed it.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin spent a year beneath the snow.

She lost her hope.

She stopped flying.

She stopped drawing.

She stopped eating properly.

She stopped believing she deserved happiness.

Then she slowly found herself again.

She flew.

She laughed.

She drew terrible pictures.

She collected flowers.

She filled notebooks.

She returned to her family.

And now?

She's a tiny cat again.

She is currently asleep in Orphie's lap.

Her tail is twitching.

One tiny wing is hanging over the edge of her hoodie.

The Feather Lantern beside them is glowing softly.

For once, nobody is worried.

Corin is simply being a cat.

And apparently...

**that's enough.**
