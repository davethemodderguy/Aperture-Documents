## 1. DISCOVERY

Following recovery of Neri's fish-bone hairclip from the abandoned backup generator room, C.O.R.E. initiated a broader examination of the surrounding sector.

The investigation was suspended after personnel began experiencing an anomalous neurological phenomenon while approaching a location designated:

> **[REDACTED]**

The first affected investigator reported sudden, extreme cranial pain.

The investigator described the sensation as:

> "Like somebody is driving a wood saw through my skull."

The pain ceased almost immediately after the investigator retreated.

A second investigator experienced the same effect.

A third experienced it.

Then everyone.

The phenomenon does not appear to discriminate based on:

- species;
    
- age;
    
- clearance;
    
- anomalous status;
    
- relationship with Neri;
    
- resistance to memetic effects;
    
- or prior exposure.
    

**The effect applies universally to personnel entering the affected range.**

---

# 2. PRIMARY EFFECT

Upon approaching [REDACTED], subjects experience an abrupt onset of severe head pain.

The sensation is not consistent with ordinary headache, migraine, or known physical injury.

No external injury is present.

Medical examination following exposure has failed to identify corresponding tissue damage.

Despite this, subjects consistently describe the pain as intensely physical.

Common descriptions include:

> "Being stabbed in the head."

> "A saw being dragged through my skull."

> "Like something is cutting behind my eyes."

> "It felt like my brain was being torn apart."

> "I could feel it, but there was nothing there."

The effect increases with proximity.

Personnel are therefore prohibited from attempting to determine the exact center through continued exposure.

---

# 3. VISUAL-MENTAL PHENOMENON

Simultaneously with the onset of pain, subjects report seeing **blue text inside their field of mental perception.**

The text does not appear on nearby surfaces.

It is not projected by a conventional display.

Subjects describe it as appearing directly within their thoughts or visual awareness.

The following messages have been independently reported:

> **GO AWAY**

> **YOU DON'T NEED ME**

> **STAY AWAY**

> **DON'T GO NEAR ME**

> **I'M A MONSTER**

> **ABANDON ME**

The wording has remained remarkably consistent between witnesses.

This is significant.

The phenomenon does not appear to simply cause generalized fear.

It communicates.

---

# 4. MESSAGE CHARACTERISTICS

The messages are consistently described as:

- blue;
    
- textual;
    
- extremely clear;
    
- impossible to physically locate;
    
- transient;
    
- and presented without an identifiable speaker.
    

Several investigators reported that the messages appeared one after another.

Others reported multiple messages simultaneously.

No investigator has reported seeing a message containing information unknown to the subject.

No investigator has reported seeing their own name.

The messages appear directed toward **discouraging approach.**

---

# 5. PSYCHOLOGICAL RESPONSE

The messages produce an unusually strong emotional reaction.

Subjects commonly experience:

- fear;
    
- distress;
    
- confusion;
    
- sudden reluctance to continue;
    
- and an overwhelming instinct to retreat.
    

However, the effect does not appear to directly control behavior.

Personnel remain capable of making decisions.

One investigator continued forward for several seconds despite the phenomenon.

They subsequently described the experience as:

> "I knew I could keep walking."

> "I just didn't think I should."

> "The words felt like someone begging me to stop."

This distinction remains under investigation.

---

# 6. UNIVERSALITY

Initial assumptions suggested that Neri's closest friends might be resistant.

They are not.

Bao experienced the effect.

Frost experienced the effect.

Alice experienced the effect.

Neko experienced the effect.

Miss Neko experienced the effect.

The same messages appeared.

The same pain occurred.

No known individual has demonstrated complete immunity.

This is inconsistent with the previously observed phenomenon in which strong emotional bonds provide partial resistance to Neri's memory suppression.

### IMPORTANT

The fact that Bao, Frost, Alice, and Neko are affected **does not establish that Neri is absent.**

It only establishes that whatever phenomenon is occurring here does not respect the same resistance mechanism.

---

# 7. ELECTRONIC FAILURE

The neurological phenomenon is accompanied by severe electronic disruption.

Electronic devices begin malfunctioning before personnel reach the apparent center of the anomaly.

Observed failures include:

- cameras shutting down;
    
- radios becoming unusable;
    
- digital displays going blank;
    
- recording equipment failing;
    
- portable computers becoming nonfunctional;
    
- access-control systems losing operation;
    
- battery-powered equipment becoming unreliable;
    
- storage devices becoming unreadable;
    
- and several pieces of equipment failing to restart after removal from the affected zone.
    

The effect superficially resembles an **EMP**.

However, C.O.R.E. Engineering has not classified it as a conventional electromagnetic pulse.

The failure pattern is inconsistent with a single instantaneous discharge.

Some equipment simply ceases functioning.

Other equipment remains powered but becomes incapable of performing meaningful operations.

A small number of devices recovered from the outer edge of the zone subsequently failed completely.

---

# 8. NO SURVEILLANCE

This has created a major investigative problem.

The area cannot currently be monitored reliably using electronic equipment.

Attempts to deploy:

- CCTV;
    
- autonomous drones;
    
- wireless sensors;
    
- wired cameras;
    
- microphones;
    
- tracking beacons;
    
- and remote observation systems
    

have failed.

Consequently:

> **C.O.R.E. currently has no reliable electronic confirmation of what exists at [REDACTED].**

All observations must be obtained through personnel or non-electronic means.

---

# 9. RADIUS

The affected area is significantly larger than initially anticipated.

Early investigators believed the anomaly might represent a localized manifestation surrounding Neri.

That hypothesis has been rejected.

The radius extends far beyond the abandoned generator room.

It is sufficiently large that assuming:

> **"Neri must be standing somewhere inside it."**

would be scientifically unjustified.

The anomaly may occupy a substantial portion of the sector.

Alternatively, the apparent radius may not correspond to the location of its source.

# 11. THE HAIRCLIP

The fish-bone hairclip remains the only confirmed physical object belonging to Neri recovered from the sector.

It was found outside the presently designated center of [REDACTED].

# 12. MESSAGE INTERPRETATION

C.O.R.E. Psychological Analysis currently considers three broad interpretations.

### THEORY 1 — NERI IS RESPONSIBLE

The messages may represent Neri deliberately communicating through the same Light-derived abilities responsible for her visual and memory suppression.

Under this interpretation, the messages are effectively a warning:

> **Do not look for me.**

---

### THEORY 2 — NERI IS PRESENT BUT NOT IN CONTROL

The messages may originate from Neri's abilities without representing deliberate communication.

The words could instead be an involuntary expression of her psychological state.

If so, the anomaly may be communicating what Neri herself believes:

> **Go away.**

> **You don't need me.**

> **I'm a monster.**

> **Abandon me.**

This possibility is considered significantly more concerning.

---

### THEORY 3 — SOMETHING ELSE IS USING HER POWER

The possibility that the phenomenon is not Neri herself remains open.

The presence of Light-derived effects does not automatically establish Neri as their source.

Until direct evidence is obtained, personnel are prohibited from referring to the anomaly as **"Neri"** in official reports.

The proper designation remains:

> **[REDACTED]**

---

# 13. ABANDONMENT MESSAGE

The final message has received particular attention.

> **ABANDON ME**

This is inconsistent with Neri's previously documented behavior.

Neri has historically demonstrated an extreme fear of abandonment.

She has repeatedly attempted to protect friends because she believes losing someone means she failed them.

The possibility that she is now actively communicating a request to be abandoned is therefore psychologically significant.

It may represent a reversal of her established behavior.

Or it may represent exactly what she believes she deserves.

Neither conclusion can currently be confirmed.
