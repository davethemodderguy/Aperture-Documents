---

# 1. VISUAL REASSESSMENT

Following complete memory restoration among the SAFEHOUSE residents, the figure within the grassless circle was examined again.

The figure's identity is now confirmed by all residents present.

It is **Jake**.

His appearance is consistent with previously recovered memories.

However, one significant difference has been documented.

The teddy bear is absent.

---

# 2. SUBJECT CONDITION

Jake remains seated in the center of the grassless circle.

Observed characteristics:

- white hair;
    
- azure eyes;
    
- folded wings;
    
- shattered halo;
    
- childlike physical appearance;
    
- motionless posture;
    
- no visible response to nearby residents.
    

An arrow is embedded directly through his chest.

No blood is present.

No visible physical wound surrounds the arrow.

The arrow appears to remain in place without conventional support.

---

# 3. TEDDY BEAR ABSENCE

The teddy bear is not present anywhere within the immediate area.

The residents searched the surrounding grass.

No trace was found.

This is significant.

The teddy bear had historically functioned as the physical vessel for Jake's stellar core.

Its absence therefore prevents C.O.R.E. from determining whether:

- the core remains within Jake;
    
- the core has been separated;
    
- the core is located elsewhere;
    
- or the anomalous environment has temporarily altered the relationship between Jake and the vessel.
    

No conclusion is currently possible.

---

# 4. RESIDENT RESPONSE

The discovery produced an immediate emotional reaction.

Everyone remembered.

Nobody needed to ask who the child was.

Nobody needed an explanation.

Bao remained closest.

Neri stared silently.

Nora began crying.

Jane did not move.

Twiggy appeared unable to look away.

The arrow was particularly difficult for her to process.

She remembered the circumstances surrounding his disappearance.

She remembered what she had said.

And now she was looking directly at the result.

---

# 5. TWIGGY

Twiggy eventually approached the edge of the circle.

She stopped.

Her eyes remained fixed on the arrow.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "...Why is that there?"

No response.

She looked toward Bao.

Bao did not know.

Twiggy looked back at Jake.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "Did I do that?"

Nobody answered.

The question remained unanswered.

---

# 6. NERI

Neri approached next.

She remained outside the circle.

Her expression was unreadable.

After several seconds:

> **NERI:**  
> "Jake."

The child did not move.

Neri's eyes lowered toward the arrow.

Then toward his empty hands.

She looked around the grass.

> **NERI:**  
> "Where's his bear?"

Nobody knew.

Neri remained standing there.

---

# 7. BAO

Bao was unable to approach further.

She remembered the first time she had seen him here.

She remembered every memory that had returned.

She remembered him calling her Mom.

She remembered every time she had assumed there would be another opportunity.

Now she looked at the empty space where his teddy bear should have been.

> **BAO:**  
> "...He doesn't have it."

Frost stood beside her.

Neither knew what to say.

---

# 8. AETERNA'S ABSENCE

Aeterna was not present during the initial observation.

No conclusion can therefore be drawn regarding whether her sword could interact with the arrow or the environment.

C.O.R.E. specifically prohibits attempts to remove the arrow without further investigation.

The object may be:

- symbolic;
    
- physical;
    
- metaphysical;
    
- part of the environment;
    
- or inseparable from the subject's current state.
    

---

# 9. THE GRASSLESS CIRCLE

The grassless region remains unchanged.

The doodled flowers remain around its perimeter.

The drawings continue to appear two-dimensional despite existing within a three-dimensional environment.

No deterioration has been observed.

No new drawings have been documented.

The music continues.

Birdsong continues.

The environment remains peaceful.

The contrast is increasingly difficult for personnel to reconcile.

---

# 10. CURRENT HYPOTHESIS

The grassland does not appear to be a conventional location.

It may represent a preserved state.

The environment contains:

- recovered memories;
    
- a preserved representation of Jake;
    
- symbolic objects;
    
- and a physical manifestation of events that cannot currently be explained.
    

The arrow may therefore not represent an injury in the conventional sense.

It may represent something that happened to Jake.

Or something that happened **because of** Jake.

Further investigation is required.

---

# CLOSING OBSERVATION

Jake remains seated.

His wings are folded.

His halo is broken.

His eyes remain faintly azure.

The arrow remains through his chest.

The teddy bear is nowhere to be found.

Around him, the flowers remain.

The music continues.

Birds continue singing somewhere in the distance.

And for the first time, every person standing in the grass remembers exactly who they are looking at.

Nobody leaves.

Not yet.
