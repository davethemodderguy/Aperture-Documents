**SUBJECT:** Nora Tachyon — Progressive Physical Decline During Recovery

---

# Executive Summary

Nora Tachyon's physical condition has continued to deteriorate despite the expectation that her recent illness would gradually resolve.

She is becoming **sicker by the day.**

Her recurrent fainting has continued.

Her sleep remains severely disrupted.

Her appetite remains inconsistent.

Her energy has decreased further.

Periods of weakness are becoming longer, while periods of normal activity are becoming shorter.

The change has become sufficiently noticeable that all three Tachyon siblings are now aware of the severity of the situation.

**Elsher is worried.**

**Neri is worried.**

Aeterna is considerably more worried than either of them.

The difference is not subtle.

---

# 1. PROGRESSIVE DECLINE

Nora's condition initially appeared consistent with a temporary illness complicated by prolonged sleep deprivation.

That interpretation is now considered insufficient.

Her recovery has not followed the expected pattern.

Instead, personnel have observed:

- increasing fatigue;
    
- recurrent fainting;
    
- reduced appetite;
    
- inconsistent hydration;
    
- prolonged periods in bed;
    
- decreased activity;
    
- worsening sleep disruption;
    
- increased dependence on assistance;
    
- reduced verbal energy;
    
- and increasingly frequent periods in which Nora simply lies still.
    

Her normal periods of activity have become increasingly brief.

She may appear almost like herself for several minutes.

Then the exhaustion returns.

---

# 2. THE FAINTING

The fainting episodes remain one of the most concerning features.

Nora may be standing normally one moment and unconscious the next.

She has fainted:

- in the Merch Store;
    
- in corridors;
    
- while attempting to move between rooms;
    
- and during otherwise routine activity.
    

Aeterna has caught her during several episodes.

Other residents have assisted during others.

The frequency has made independent movement increasingly impractical.

Nora has been repeatedly instructed to ask for assistance.

She does not always do so.

---

# 3. ELSHER

Elsher has become increasingly concerned.

He does not express worry dramatically.

He simply appears more frequently.

Despite his responsibilities with MSAC, he has begun visiting the SAFEHOUSE more often than his normal schedule would suggest.

During one visit, he stood beside Nora's bed for several minutes without speaking.

Nora was awake.

She noticed him.

> **NORA:**  
> "Hi, Elsher."

> **ELSHER:**  
> "Hey."

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> "You're staring."

Elsher looked away.

> **ELSHER:**  
> "You're getting worse."

Nora was quiet.

> **NORA:**  
> "...Yeah."

Elsher sat down.

> **ELSHER:**  
> "You should've told me."

Nora looked at the blanket.

> **NORA:**  
> "I thought I'd get better."

Elsher did not respond immediately.

Then:

> **ELSHER:**  
> "I know."

He remained beside her.

---

# 4. NERI

Neri's response has been quieter.

She continues working on her chemistry research.

However, her visits to Nora have become more frequent.

She has also begun leaving her work earlier when Nora's condition noticeably worsens.

Neri does not ask Nora many questions.

She watches.

She checks whether Nora is awake.

She checks whether she has eaten.

She checks whether the water beside the bed has been touched.

She checks whether Nora is breathing comfortably.

Then she sits down.

During one visit, Nora asked:

> **NORA:**  
> "Big Sis?"

Neri looked at her.

> **NORA:**  
> "Am I getting better?"

Neri paused.

She did not immediately answer.

Nora noticed.

> **NORA:**  
> "...I'm not, am I?"

Neri shook her head slightly.

Then reached for Nora's hand.

Nora held it.

Neither spoke for several minutes.

---

# 5. AETERNA

Aeterna's behavior has changed more visibly.

She has always monitored Nora.

She has always remained close.

She has always responded immediately when Nora needed assistance.

That is no longer sufficient to describe her current behavior.

Aeterna now watches Nora almost continuously.

She notices every change.

Every pause.

Every missed meal.

Every untouched cup.

Every time Nora closes her eyes.

Every time Nora stands.

Every time Nora becomes unusually quiet.

She has begun carrying Nora without waiting to be asked.

She brings water before Nora requests it.

She brings food before Nora says she is hungry.

She checks the room repeatedly during the night.

She remains awake when Nora cannot sleep.

Her concern is becoming increasingly difficult to conceal.

---

# 6. AETERNA'S WORRY

During one medical observation, Aeterna was asked whether she was concerned.

Her response was immediate.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Yes."

The welfare officer asked:

> **"How concerned?"**

Aeterna remained silent for several seconds.

The blue crescent sigils across her body were faint.

Her voice was not.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "She is becoming weaker."

Pause.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "She is not recovering."

The officer asked whether Aeterna believed Nora was in immediate danger.

Aeterna looked toward Nora.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "I do not know."

That answer was considered significant.

Aeterna is rarely uncertain.

---

# 7. NIGHTTIME

The nights remain the worst period.

Nora still struggles to sleep.

The nightmares continue.

Sometimes she remains awake because she is afraid of sleeping.

Sometimes she finally falls asleep and wakes only minutes later.

Sometimes she wakes crying.

Sometimes she wakes completely silent.

Aeterna remains beside her through all of it.

On one occasion, Nora woke after a particularly severe nightmare.

She looked around the room.

Aeterna was sitting beside her bed.

Nora reached out.

Aeterna immediately took her hand.

> **NORA:**  
> "...You're still here."

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Always."

Nora closed her eyes.

She did not sleep.

But she stopped shaking.

---

# 8. THE CARDS

The wall of get-well cards remains.

More have been added.

Nora occasionally looks at them.

She has stopped asking who sent some of them.

She already knows.

She has also begun keeping certain cards closer to the bed.

Neri's.

Elsher's.

Bao's.

Jane's.

Alice's.

And one particularly messy card from several SAFEHOUSE residents that simply says:

> **"GET BETTER SOON NORA!!!"**

Nora has not removed any of them.

---

# 9. FAMILY RESPONSE

The Tachyon siblings are responding differently.

### Elsher

Becomes more present.

Asks more questions.

Tries to hide his concern.

Fails.

### Neri

Becomes quieter.

Visits more often.

Monitors Nora without making her feel watched.

### Aeterna

Does not leave.

The distinction is important.

Aeterna has always protected Nora.

She is now actively **worrying about her.**

---

# 10. AETERNA'S PRIVATE OBSERVATION

Aeterna was later found sitting alone outside Nora's room.

A welfare officer approached.

> **OFFICER:**  
> "You should rest."

Aeterna did not move.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "She may need me."

> **OFFICER:**  
> "She's sleeping."

Aeterna looked toward the closed door.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "She does not sleep for long."

The officer remained silent.

Aeterna continued:

> **AETERNA:**  
> "When she wakes, she is afraid."

Pause.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "When she is awake, she is exhausted."

Another pause.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "When she becomes exhausted, she faints."

Aeterna's voice became quieter.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "And then she wakes afraid again."

She looked down at her hands.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "I do not like this cycle."

---

# 11. CURRENT MEDICAL STATUS

Nora remains under elevated medical observation.

Her recurrent fainting, prolonged sleep disruption, and worsening physical weakness require continued assessment.

Personnel have been instructed to report immediately if Nora develops:

- prolonged unconsciousness;
    
- significant changes in breathing;
    
- inability to maintain hydration;
    
- inability to eat;
    
- new or rapidly worsening symptoms;
    
- unusual confusion;
    
- or any other substantial change from her current baseline.
    

Nora is not to be left to determine the seriousness of an episode herself.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The most significant change is not any single symptom.

It is the **trajectory.**

Nora is not recovering at the rate previously expected.

She is becoming weaker.

The fainting continues.

The sleep disturbance continues.

The nightmares continue.

The memories continue.

And the periods in which she resembles her usual energetic self are becoming increasingly rare.

This has affected the entire family.

Elsher has stopped pretending he is not worried.

Neri has begun rearranging her schedule.

Aeterna has effectively stationed herself beside Nora.

None of them know how long the illness will last.

None of them are willing to leave Nora alone while they find out.

---

# FINAL OBSERVATION

Nora used to make noise.

A lot of it.

Questions.

Laughter.

Running footsteps.

Arguments.

Excited announcements.

**"MY SISTER IS A GENIUS!"**

The SAFEHOUSE became accustomed to knowing where Nora was because they could usually hear her.

Now they know where she is because someone is usually sitting beside her.

Aeterna.

Neri.

Elsher.

Sometimes Bao.

Sometimes someone else.

The cards still cover the wall.

The water remains beside the bed.

The family journal remains within reach.

And Nora remains in the middle of all of it.

Getting weaker.

Sleeping less.

Fainting more.

Remembering too much.

Aeterna watches every breath.

Neri watches the door.

Elsher keeps coming back.

And Nora, despite everything, keeps trying to get better.

For now, that is enough.

But C.O.R.E. is no longer documenting a routine recovery.

This is an **active medical concern.**
