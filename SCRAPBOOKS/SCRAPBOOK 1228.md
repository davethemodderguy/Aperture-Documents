# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The unidentified **white-feathered falcon** previously documented in Woodland Coorp communications has been identified.

It is a familiar belonging to:

> **Nora Tachyon.**

The familiar was provided to Nora by **Guiding Light**.

It is a small white-feathered falcon with:

- bright blue eyes;
    
- a faint blue luminescence;
    
- a moon-shaped collar;
    
- claws capable of gripping surfaces without causing injury;
    
- and anomalous compatibility with Nora's clothing and physical form.
    

The familiar's claws do not damage Nora's head, hair, or white beret when perched on her.

It is similarly capable of gripping the hat without tearing or puncturing the material.

Nora appears completely accustomed to its presence.

---

# 1. INITIAL OBSERVATION

The entity was first positively identified inside the **Tachyon Industries Merch Store**.

Elsher Tachyon was present at the store as a customer.

Nora was operating the counter.

Elsher approached the counter.

Then stopped.

There was a falcon sitting on Nora's head.

Specifically:

> **On top of her white beret.**

Elsher stared at it.

The falcon stared back.

Nora continued reading a merchandise manual.

---

# 2. ELSHER'S RESPONSE

Elsher remained silent for approximately five seconds.

> **Elsher:**  
> "...Nora."

Nora looked up.

> **Nora:**  
> "Yeah?"

Elsher pointed upward.

> **Elsher:**  
> "There's a bird on your head."

Nora looked upward as far as physically possible.

> **Nora:**  
> "I know."

Elsher paused.

> **Elsher:**  
> "You know?"

> **Nora:**  
> "Yeah."

The falcon shifted its feet.

Its claws gently gripped the beret.

No damage occurred.

Elsher looked at the hat.

Then at the bird.

Then at Nora.

> **Elsher:**  
> "Does it normally do that?"

Nora nodded.

> **Nora:**  
> "Yep."

---

# 3. FAMILIAR IDENTIFICATION

C.O.R.E. later confirmed the entity's identity through Nora.

> **C.O.R.E.:**  
> "Nora, what is the bird?"

Nora answered immediately:

> **Nora:**  
> "My familiar."

> **C.O.R.E.:**  
> "Who gave it to you?"

Nora pointed upward.

The falcon remained perched on her head.

> **Nora:**  
> "Blue Moon."

C.O.R.E. required approximately two seconds to determine that "Blue Moon" referred to:

> **Guiding Light.**

---

# 4. PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

The familiar is approximately consistent with a small falcon.

Its feathers are predominantly white.

The eyes are:

> **Bright blue.**

Under normal lighting, the blue coloration is clearly visible.

Under low-light conditions, the entity begins producing a soft blue glow.

The luminescence is sufficient to make the familiar visible in darkness without functioning as a conventional light source.

The effect is most noticeable around:

- the eyes;
    
- the edges of the feathers;
    
- and portions of the body.
    

---

# 5. THE MOON COLLAR

The familiar wears a small collar.

Attached to the collar is a:

> **Moon-shaped ornament.**

The ornament is consistent with Guiding Light's established visual symbolism.

Nora reportedly refers to it simply as:

> **"The moon collar."**

The collar does not appear to interfere with flight, movement, breathing, or feeding.

No conventional fastening mechanism has been identified.

---

# 6. CLAW INTERACTION

The familiar's claws initially caused concern.

The claws are sufficiently sharp to grip Nora's beret securely.

However, they do not:

- puncture the hat;
    
- tear fabric;
    
- damage Nora's hair;
    
- scratch Nora's skin;
    
- or otherwise injure her.
    

This behavior appears anomalous.

The entity can perch on Nora for extended periods without causing harm.

It can also relocate from:

> **beret → shoulder → arm → nearby surface → beret**

without incident.

---

# 7. NERI OBSERVATION

Neri entered the Merch Store shortly after Elsher noticed the familiar.

She stopped.

Looked at Nora.

Looked at the falcon.

Then looked back at Nora.

> **Neri:**  
> "...What's that?"

Nora smiled.

> **Nora:**  
> "My familiar."

Neri looked at the moon collar.

Then at the blue eyes.

Then at the blue glow beginning to appear as the store lights dimmed.

Neri immediately appeared to understand.

She left the store.

---

# 10. FAMILIAR BEHAVIOR

The entity has demonstrated several consistent behaviors around Nora.

It frequently:

- perches on Nora's head;
    
- sits on her beret;
    
- follows her through the Blacksite;
    
- remains nearby while she plays;
    
- observes unfamiliar people;
    
- accompanies Nora outdoors;
    
- stays near her during periods of distress;
    
- and glows faintly in darkness.
    

It does not appear to require constant physical contact.

It simply prefers to remain within Nora's vicinity.

---

# 11. RELATIONSHIP WITH NORA

Nora appears to regard the familiar as a companion rather than equipment.

She has been observed speaking to it.

The familiar does not respond verbally.

Nora does not appear bothered by this.

A representative interaction:

> **Nora:**  
> "We're going to the store."

The falcon looks at her.

> **Nora:**  
> "You're coming."

The falcon remains on her beret.

> **Nora:**  
> "Okay."

She walks away.

---

# 12. NIGHTTIME OBSERVATION

During a nighttime inspection, the familiar was observed resting on Nora's bed.

The room was dark.

The entity's eyes produced a soft blue glow.

Nora was asleep nearby.

No other illumination was active.

The familiar remained awake for several minutes before eventually settling down.

C.O.R.E. personnel noted that the effect was visually similar to a small blue star resting in the room.

No anomalous radiation or hazardous emission was detected.

---

# 13. CONNECTION TO THE ABDUCTION

The identification resolves a significant portion of the mystery surrounding the Woodland communications.

Woodland personnel repeatedly reported:

> **"White-feathered falcon."**

They were not hallucinating.

They had encountered Nora's familiar.

The familiar was present during the incident.

However, it was **not present when Nora was recovered by MSAC, Neri, and Elsher.**

Its location during the final stage of the incident remains unknown.

Nora has not provided a detailed account of where it went.

When asked:

> **C.O.R.E.:**  
> "Where was your familiar when MSAC arrived?"

Nora shrugged.

> **Nora:**  
> "Somewhere."

---

# 14. SECURITY CLASSIFICATION

The familiar is not classified as a hostile entity.

It is classified as:

> **RESIDENT-ASSOCIATED ANOMALOUS COMPANION**

The entity is not to be:

- captured;
    
- restrained;
    
- separated from Nora without cause;
    
- treated as an ordinary animal;
    
- or deliberately provoked.
    

Personnel should not attempt to test the limits of its claws, flight capability, anomalous properties, or apparent protective behavior.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The unidentified white-feathered falcon has been positively identified as **Nora Tachyon's familiar**, provided to her by Guiding Light.

Its known characteristics include:

**White feathers.**

**Blue eyes.**

**Blue nocturnal luminescence.**

**Moon-shaped collar.**

**Non-injurious claws.**

**Strong attachment to Nora.**

The familiar's presence also explains the previously unexplained Woodland communications.

The "white-feathered falcon" was real.

It was simply never identified as a familiar until now.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The Blacksite spent days trying to identify the white-feathered falcon from Woodland's communications.

The answer was considerably simpler than expected.

It wasn't a hostile infiltrator.

It wasn't an unknown predator.

It wasn't another unexplained Blacksite anomaly.

It was Nora's familiar.

Guiding Light gave it to her because Nora had spent too long being afraid of disappearing.

Now she has something that stays.

A small white falcon.

Blue eyes.

A moon around its neck.

And a faint blue glow in the dark.

It sits on Nora's head while she runs around the Blacksite.

It follows her when she plays.

And when the lights go out, it glows beside her.

For once, the unexplained thing following Nora appears to exist for one very simple reason:

> **She doesn't have to be alone.**
