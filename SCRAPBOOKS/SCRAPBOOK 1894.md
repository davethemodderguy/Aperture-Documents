### SEARCH, RESCUE & MEDICAL RESPONSE DIVISION

**Status:** PARTIALLY RESOLVED – SORA STILL MISSING  
**Subjects:** Jake Akana / Sora  
**Location:** Snow-covered terrain outside the Blacksite perimeter  
**Incident:** Failed Reunion and Subsequent Collapse

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The search for Jake Akana has concluded with his recovery.

However, the search for Sora remains active.

Jake successfully located Sora somewhere within the snow-covered terrain outside the Blacksite. The two children were observed together before both began running.

The circumstances surrounding their decision to flee remain unclear.

During the subsequent movement through the snow, Jake eventually exhausted himself and collapsed.

He fainted in the snow.

Jake was later recovered and returned to the Blacksite entrance.

Sora was not recovered.

His current location remains unknown.

**The operation has therefore achieved the recovery of one missing child while the second remains at large.**

---

## 2. RECONSTRUCTION OF EVENTS

The following sequence has been established from the available information.

**Stage I – Contact**

Jake located Sora.

This is significant because it confirms that Sora was still within the surrounding search area and that Jake's decision to search independently resulted in direct contact.

The exact circumstances of their encounter remain undocumented.

**Stage II – Flight**

Both children ran.

It is not yet established whether Sora initiated the movement, whether Jake followed him, or whether an external stimulus caused them to flee.

The search team must not assume that Sora deliberately rejected Jake.

Likewise, Jake's decision to run alongside Sora should not be interpreted as evidence that he was physically capable of continuing.

**Stage III – Collapse**

Jake eventually gave out.

He fainted in the snow.

His collapse ended his ability to continue moving under his own power.

Given the environmental conditions and his prolonged emotional distress preceding the search, this incident is considered a serious medical event.

**Stage IV – Recovery**

Jake was subsequently returned to the Blacksite door.

His recovery has been confirmed.

Sora, however, was not brought back.

---

## 3. MEDICAL PRIORITY – JAKE AKANA

Jake is to receive immediate medical assessment following his collapse.

Required assessments include:

- Core body temperature and signs of hypothermia.
    
- Hydration and blood glucose.
    
- Respiratory function.
    
- Physical injuries sustained during the search.
    
- General exhaustion and neurological status following fainting.
    
- Function of his existing medical monitoring strap.
    

His anomalous abilities must not be treated as substitutes for ordinary medical care.

In particular, his self-revival capability does not make exhaustion or exposure harmless.

Jake must not be sent back outside to continue searching.

This restriction is not a punishment.

He has already demonstrated that he will continue searching beyond his physical limits if given the opportunity.

**Jake's recovery must take precedence over any further involvement in the search for Sora.**

---

## 4. SORA – STATUS UNCHANGED

Sora remains missing.

The fact that Jake found him provides an important investigative lead, but does not establish his current location.

C.O.R.E. must consider that Sora may have moved farther into the surrounding snow after their encounter.

His previous behavior suggests that an approaching search party could trigger another flight response.

Personnel are reminded:

- Do not surround him.
    
- Do not shout or pursue him unnecessarily.
    
- Do not attempt to seize Mia's collar.
    
- Maintain access to warm clothing, medical assistance, and food.
    
- Avoid treating his continued flight as deliberate hostility.
    

His physical condition remains unknown.

The possibility that he is still carrying Mia's collar must be taken into account.

---

## 5. RESIDENTIAL IMPACT

The incident is expected to have a substantial emotional impact on SAFEHOUSE Floor 7.

Jake had gone searching because he could not bear the uncertainty surrounding Sora.

He found him.

But the encounter ended with Jake unconscious in the snow and Sora still missing.

Aries and Tom are to be notified of Jake's medical condition and recovery.

Clover, Phoenix, and Bao are to be included in the residential support response.

Jake must not be interrogated immediately after waking.

In particular, personnel must not begin with questions such as:

- "Why did you run?"
    
- "Why didn't you bring Sora back?"
    
- "Why did you keep chasing him?"
    
- "Where exactly did he go?"
    

The last question may eventually be necessary for reconstructing the encounter, but it must wait until Jake is medically stable and emotionally ready.

He is not an investigative instrument.

He is a child who has just fainted in the snow while trying to find his friend.

## 7. C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake found him.

After all those months, after all those nights crying, after leaving home and searching through the snow, Jake actually found Sora.

And then they both ran.

We do not know why.

We do not know what happened between them.

We only know that Jake eventually couldn't continue.

A six-year-old boy, exhausted and cold, collapsed in the snow.

He was brought back to the Blacksite.

Sora wasn't.

There is something particularly painful about that distinction.

Jake went looking for someone he loved. He found him, but he couldn't bring him home. And now he is back in the very place where Sora's little den remains empty.

When Jake wakes up, he may ask where Sora is.

He may ask whether they found him.

He may ask whether Sora was okay.

We must be prepared for those questions.

And we must not make him feel that he failed.

**Jake found Sora. That matters.**

It means Sora was not beyond reach. It means their paths crossed again. Whatever happened during those moments, their friendship was not simply erased by months of separation.

But Jake has done enough.

He has searched until his body gave out.

Now it is our turn.

Let the adults search. Let the medical staff take care of Jake. Let the people who built this facility take responsibility for finding the child who is still outside.

The little boy who went looking for his friend deserves to wake up somewhere warm.

And the little boy still in the snow deserves the same.

— **C.O.R.E. Search & Rescue Division**
