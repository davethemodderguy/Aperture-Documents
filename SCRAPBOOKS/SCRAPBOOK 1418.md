# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following Jake's successful recovery from the Nullwalker state, the **Obol Squad** has become heavily involved in his day-to-day recovery.

All six members have contributed.

However, two members have become particularly important:

> **Anby.**

and

> **Twiggy.**

Anby has developed a quiet, almost constant presence around Jake.

Twiggy has effectively become his primary non-Bao medical support.

Neither was formally assigned to him.

Neither requested an official designation.

They simply started helping.

The remaining members of Obol Squad have followed their lead.

---

# 1. ANBY

Anby's involvement is the most personal.

She already had an established relationship with Jake before the Nullwalker incident.

She also carries the memory of the sleepwalking incident in which she accidentally attacked him.

Although Jake forgave her long before the Nullwalker event, Anby had never completely stopped thinking about it.

His disappearance affected her particularly strongly.

Now that he has returned, she appears unwilling to take his presence for granted.

She does not hover.

She simply stays nearby.

---

# 2. ANBY'S NEW ROUTINE

Anby has developed an unofficial routine.

Every morning, she checks whether Jake is awake.

She does not wake him.

She simply looks inside.

If he is sleeping:

She leaves.

If he is awake:

She gives a small wave.

If Jake waves back:

She enters.

If he doesn't:

She leaves him alone.

The procedure is remarkably simple.

It is also remarkably consistent.

---

# 3. ANBY AND SILENCE

Anby's voice is particularly useful around Jake.

She speaks quietly.

Often softly enough that personnel have difficulty hearing her.

She does not demand responses.

Typical interactions include:

> **ANBY:**  
> “Morning.”

Jake waves.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Okay.”

Later:

> **ANBY:**  
> “Hungry?”

Jake shakes his head.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Okay.”

Several minutes later:

> **ANBY:**  
> “Water?”

Jake nods.

Anby gets him water.

No additional conversation occurs.

---

# 4. ANBY'S COOKIES

Anby has also developed a habit of leaving small snacks near Jake.

Usually cookies.

This is notable because cookies were involved in the original sleepwalking incident.

Jake eventually noticed.

He looked at the cookie.

Then at Anby.

Then back at the cookie.

Anby became visibly nervous.

> **ANBY:**  
> “...You don't have to.”

Jake picked it up.

He ate it.

Then gave Anby a thumbs-up.

Anby's shoulders visibly relaxed.

---

# 5. TWIGGY

Twiggy's involvement is considerably more medical.

Unlike most of Obol Squad, Twiggy actively sought medical knowledge during the years the sisters were separated.

She therefore has enough medical understanding to recognize when Jake is:

- becoming fatigued;
    
- dehydrated;
    
- unusually cold;
    
- struggling to maintain posture;
    
- becoming overstimulated;
    
- or simply in need of rest.
    

She does not treat herself as Jake's doctor.

She works alongside the medical personnel.

Her role is primarily observation, support, and immediate assistance.

---

# 6. TWIGGY'S APPROACH

Twiggy is considerably more direct than Anby.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> “Jake.”

Jake looks up.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> “Water.”

Jake reaches for the cup.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> “Good.”

Later:

> **TWIGGY:**  
> “Cold?”

Jake nods.

Twiggy immediately adds another blanket.

There is no argument.

Jake has learned that Twiggy's questions are usually followed by something useful.

---

# 7. THE TWO OF THEM

Anby and Twiggy have naturally divided responsibilities.

### Anby

- companionship;
    
- quiet presence;
    
- snacks;
    
- helping Jake move;
    
- sitting with him;
    
- keeping him company without demanding conversation;
    
- noticing when he wants someone nearby.
    

### Twiggy

- hydration reminders;
    
- monitoring fatigue;
    
- checking his general condition;
    
- assisting medical personnel;
    
- ensuring he does not overexert himself;
    
- helping establish a consistent recovery routine.
    

Neither arrangement was formally authorized.

It simply happened.

---

# 8. SOLIDER 11

Solider 11 approaches Jake differently.

She is more practical.

If Jake needs to get somewhere:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> “Come on. I'll carry you.”

Jake usually reaches up.

She carries him.

If he falls asleep:

She continues carrying him.

If someone asks why:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> “He's tired.”

End of explanation.

She has also developed a habit of quietly positioning herself between Jake and crowded areas.

Not because Jake is incapable of handling crowds.

Because she knows he currently gets tired easily.

---

# 9. SORA

Sora has become Jake's quiet evening companion.

She often brings food and sits somewhere nearby.

Jake does not always eat.

Sora does not pressure him.

Instead, she occasionally opens the nearest portal window and lets him look at the stars.

Jake watches them.

Sora watches them too.

Neither needs to speak.

On one occasion, Jake pointed toward a particularly bright star.

Sora looked.

Then nodded.

> **SORA:**  
> “Pretty.”

Jake smiled faintly.

---

# 10. PHAETHON

Phaethon is responsible for practical errands.

If Jake needs something from another part of the SAFEHOUSE:

Phaethon gets it.

If Jake's room needs something moved:

Phaethon moves it.

If Bao needs something carried:

Phaethon carries it.

She has also become particularly good at recognizing when Jake is too tired to ask for something.

Her approach is simple.

> **PHAETHON:**  
> “Need anything?”

Jake shakes his head.

> **PHAETHON:**  
> “Alright.”

She leaves.

No pressure.

---

# 11. IRIS

Iris has somehow become Jake's transportation specialist.

She is exceptionally good at getting people from one location to another.

This has resulted in a recurring arrangement where Jake is placed into an appropriate vehicle or carried to wherever he needs to go while Iris handles transportation.

She takes the job extremely seriously.

> **IRIS:**  
> “Seatbelt.”

Jake looks at her.

> **IRIS:**  
> “Seatbelt.”

Jake obeys.

> **IRIS:**  
> “Good.”

---

# 12. OBOL SQUAD GROUP DYNAMIC

The unusual part is that none of them appear to regard Jake as a responsibility.

They treat him as:

> **their little brother.**

There is no formal declaration of this relationship.

Nobody voted on it.

Nobody wrote it down.

It simply became obvious.

Anby checks on him.

Twiggy monitors him.

Solider 11 carries him.

Sora sits with him.

Phaethon gets what he needs.

Iris transports him.

And Jake accepts all of it.

---

# 13. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake remains unable to speak.

His vocal cords have not recovered sufficiently.

He therefore communicates through gestures.

The Obol Squad has become unusually good at interpreting them.

A raised hand:

**Help.**

A point toward the door:

**Go somewhere.**

A point toward his teddy bear:

**Where is he?**

A hand against his throat:

**Can't talk.**

A small wave:

**Hi.**

And, occasionally—

Jake simply reaches toward one of them.

That usually means:

> **Carry me.**

---

# 14. ANBY'S RESPONSE TO THAT

The first time Jake reached toward Anby without saying anything, Anby froze.

She looked at him.

Then his hands.

Then back at him.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Me?”

Jake nodded.

Anby crouched.

Jake climbed onto her back.

She stood.

And carried him.

No hesitation.

No fear.

No flinching.

Just Anby carrying Jake through the SAFEHOUSE.

Jake rested his head against her shoulder.

After several minutes, Anby quietly said:

> **ANBY:**  
> “You're safe.”

Jake closed his eyes.

---

# 15. TWIGGY'S RESPONSE

Twiggy eventually discovered that Jake sometimes tries to hide his exhaustion.

This is unsurprising.

Jake has a long history of not wanting to inconvenience people.

Twiggy has therefore developed one simple rule.

If Jake starts swaying while standing:

> **TWIGGY:**  
> “Sit.”

Jake shakes his head.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> “Sit.”

Jake looks at her.

Twiggy looks back.

Jake sits.

This is apparently the first medical instruction Jake has ever followed without attempting to negotiate.

---

# 16. CURRENT RECOVERY ENVIRONMENT

Jake's room has changed slightly.

Not dramatically.

There are now:

- extra blankets;
    
- water within easy reach;
    
- snacks;
    
- medical supplies;
    
- a comfortable chair;
    
- additional lighting;
    
- and a small place where visitors can sit.
    

None of these items were requested by Jake.

They simply appeared.

Anby placed the first blanket.

Twiggy placed the water.

Sora added a small star projector.

Phaethon brought the chair.

Iris fixed the lighting.

Solider 11 moved everything into a more practical arrangement.

Nobody asked who was responsible.

---

# 17. C.O.R.E. OBSERVATION

There is a notable difference between Jake's current recovery and his previous periods of illness.

Previously, Jake frequently attempted to conceal his condition.

He would remain quiet.

Wait.

Assume someone else would notice.

Or simply continue until he could no longer do so.

The Obol Squad appears to have recognized this pattern.

They do not wait for him to reach the point of collapse.

They watch.

Not constantly.

Not intrusively.

Just enough.

---

# 18. MEDICAL DIVISION ASSESSMENT

The Obol Squad is **not** replacing professional medical care.

Their involvement is considered beneficial because they provide something medical personnel cannot continuously provide:

**familiarity.**

Jake is more comfortable around them.

He accepts assistance from them.

He communicates with them despite being unable to speak.

He permits them to carry him.

He allows Twiggy to monitor him.

He actively seeks Anby's presence.

These behaviors are clinically relevant to his recovery environment.

---

# 19. FINAL OBSERVATION

One evening, Anby was sitting beside Jake.

Twiggy was checking his temperature.

Jake was holding his teddy bear.

Anby quietly asked:

> **ANBY:**  
> “Feeling better?”

Jake shook his head.

Anby nodded.

She did not look disappointed.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Okay.”

Twiggy finished her check.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> “Still sick.”

Jake nodded.

Twiggy placed the thermometer away.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> “We'll keep taking care of you.”

Jake looked at both of them.

He slowly reached out.

One hand caught Anby's sleeve.

The other caught Twiggy's hand.

Neither moved.

They simply stayed there.

Jake eventually fell asleep.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake is still sick.

He cannot speak.

He remains weak.

His recovery will take time.

But he is no longer recovering alone.

Anby stays.

Twiggy watches.

Solider 11 carries.

Sora sits beneath the stars.

Phaethon handles the practical things.

Iris gets everyone where they need to go.

And Jake—

Jake simply lets them.

For someone who once believed that everyone would eventually be too busy to notice him,

that may be the most important part.
