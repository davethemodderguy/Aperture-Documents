# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon was deployed on a single-operator mission against a Woodland Coorp apartment facility.

The objective was limited:

> **Enter. Retrieve the designated intelligence package. Exit.**

No additional MSAC personnel were deployed alongside her.

Nora selected two weapons from her authorized equipment:

- **Marlin Model 1894 — .357 Magnum**, fitted with her preferred tactical configuration;
    
- **Personal pistol.**
    

She deliberately declined a suppressed shotgun.

When asked why, Nora's response was:

> **“Not effective.”**

No further explanation was provided.

The operation was subsequently completed without requiring reinforcement.

The most notable aspect of the mission was not the equipment.

It was the reaction of the Woodland personnel.

They panicked.

---

# PRE-MISSION

Nora arrived at the staging area wearing her usual cold-weather clothing.

White hair.

Glasses.

Coat.

Scarf.

The Marlin secured alongside her equipment.

Her pistol remained available as a secondary weapon.

An MSAC supervisor reviewed the mission parameters with her.

> **SUPERVISOR:**  
> “You understand you're going alone?”

Nora nodded.

> **SUPERVISOR:**  
> “You understand the objective?”

Another nod.

> **SUPERVISOR:**  
> “Questions?”

Nora looked at the apartment building.

> **NORA:**  
> “No.”

She then checked her equipment.

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> “Can I go now?”

The supervisor sighed.

> **SUPERVISOR:**  
> “Yes.”

---

# ENTRY

Woodland Coorp personnel initially detected nothing unusual.

The first security team expected a conventional infiltration attempt.

They were not expecting Nora.

The first report received by Woodland command was brief:

> **WOODLAND OPERATOR:**  
> “Contact.”

A second voice:

> **“How many?”**

Pause.

> **WOODLAND OPERATOR:**  
> “...One.”

The response was reportedly:

> **“One what?”**

> **“One person.”**

---

# FIRST CONTACT

Nora entered the building.

The first engagement lasted only seconds.

The Woodland personnel were immediately forced to react to an opponent who moved considerably faster than expected.

Nora's behavior was not aggressive in the conventional sense.

It was **decisive.**

She did not hesitate when moving through confined spaces.

She did not freeze when confronted unexpectedly.

She transitioned between her primary and secondary equipment naturally.

She maintained awareness of multiple threats without appearing overwhelmed.

The Woodland team began losing confidence almost immediately.

# CLOSE-QUARTERS PERFORMANCE

Nora's greatest advantage during the operation was not range.

It was confined-space movement.

Apartment corridors, stairwells, doorways and small rooms provided little room for Woodland personnel to coordinate.

Nora adapted immediately.

She did not attempt to overpower groups through brute force.

Instead, she exploited the confusion created by their own reactions.

One operator would turn toward her.

Another would lose sight of her.

A third would attempt to reposition.

By the time they had established where she was, Nora had already changed position.

C.O.R.E. observers described her behavior as:

> **“Extremely economical.”**

She did not waste movement.

She did not waste attention.

She did not waste time.

---

# THE MARLIN

The Marlin proved particularly psychologically effective.

Woodland personnel had apparently expected modern tactical equipment.

Instead, they repeatedly encountered the distinctive sound of a lever-action rifle being cycled.

One recovered transmission contained:

> **“Is that a fucking lever gun?”**

Another operator responded:

> **“I think so.”**

> **“Why does she have a lever gun?”**

No answer was provided.

Nora apparently did not consider the question important.

---

# TACTICOOL INCIDENT

At one point, Nora cleared a transition area and briefly paused.

She performed a quick inspection of her rifle.

Then:

**Flick.**

The lever cycled.

She continued forward.

An MSAC observer watching through the remote feed said:

> **“Did she seriously just do the thing?”**

The second observer sighed.

> **“Yes.”**

> **“During an actual mission?”**

> **“Apparently.”**

---

# WOODLAND MORALE

The psychological effect became increasingly obvious.

Woodland personnel began communicating less about the objective and more about Nora herself.

Recovered transmissions included:

> **“She's downstairs.”**

Then:

> **“No, she's upstairs.”**

Then:

> **“She's behind us.”**

Then:

> **“How is she everywhere?”**

The answer was eventually identified.

She wasn't.

The Woodland team simply could not maintain a reliable picture of her position.

Nora's movement and their own panic created the illusion that she was appearing everywhere simultaneously.

---

# PISTOL TRANSITION

Nora eventually transitioned to her pistol when the situation required a more compact weapon.

The change was smooth.

No dramatic pause.

No hesitation.

One moment she was carrying the Marlin.

The next, the pistol was in use.

A Woodland operator later described the experience:

> **“You'd think we'd have an advantage because she's smaller.”**

Pause.

> **“We didn't.”**

---

# OBJECTIVE

Nora reached the designated apartment.

The intelligence package was recovered.

She verified it.

Then she began leaving.

At this point, Woodland personnel were still attempting to locate her.

One final transmission was captured:

> **WOODLAND COMMAND:**  
> “Where is the intruder?”

Long pause.

> **WOODLAND OPERATOR:**  
> “Leaving.”

> **WOODLAND COMMAND:**  
> “How many?”

> **WOODLAND OPERATOR:**  
> “Still one.”

---

# EXTRACTION

Nora exited the building alone.

No reinforcement was required.

No rescue operation was necessary.

She returned to the extraction point carrying the recovered intelligence package.

Her first question upon arrival was:

> **NORA:**  
> “Do we have cake?”

The MSAC supervisor stared at her.

> **SUPERVISOR:**  
> “You just raided a Woodland apartment complex by yourself.”

Nora nodded.

> **SUPERVISOR:**  
> “You made an entire mercenary team panic.”

Nora nodded again.

> **SUPERVISOR:**  
> “And your first question is whether there's cake?”

Nora looked at him.

> **NORA:**  
> “Yes.”

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The operation further demonstrates Nora's established aptitude for **tacticality and adaptation**.

Her effectiveness did not come from overwhelming firepower.

It came from her ability to rapidly understand changing circumstances and remain composed inside confined environments.

The Woodland personnel, conversely, became increasingly reactive.

Once they realized their opponent was a six-year-old girl, their uncertainty increased rather than decreased.

This appears to have contributed significantly to the breakdown of their coordination.

Nora's use of a lever-action rifle also had an unexpected psychological effect.

The weapon was unconventional enough that several Woodland operators became distracted by identifying it.

Nora did not appear distracted at all.
