---

# 1. CLASSIFICATION

Following formal investigation, the environment previously accessed through the missing SAFEHOUSE elevator floor has been classified as a:

> **LIMINAL SPACE**

The space has been designated:

> **LSIN-001 — “THE GRASSLAND”**

The classification was established by the **Liminal Space Investigation and Neutralization Executive (L.S.I.N.)**

L.S.I.N. is responsible for the investigation, documentation, containment, and neutralization of anomalous liminal spaces encountered by C.O.R.E.

The Executive is jointly led by:

**STAR**

and

**NIGHTSHADE**

---

# 2. ENVIRONMENTAL DESCRIPTION

LSIN-001 consists of an apparently infinite grassland.

Observed environmental characteristics include:

- uninterrupted grassland extending beyond visual range;
    
- soft natural sunlight;
    
- persistent gentle wind;
    
- ambient birdsong;
    
- unidentified relaxing music;
    
- no visible sun source;
    
- no visible artificial structures;
    
- no conventional horizon termination;
    
- no measurable boundary;
    
- stable atmospheric conditions;
    
- no conventional day/night cycle observed.
    

Despite its apparent size, the environment does not demonstrate conventional spatial behavior.

Personnel may walk for extended periods without observing meaningful environmental variation.

---

# 3. PRIMARY LANDMARK

The most significant feature within LSIN-001 is a circular region where grass does not grow.

The area is surrounded by numerous small, two-dimensional drawings resembling flowers.

The drawings are anomalous.

They possess almost no measurable depth while remaining physically present within the environment.

The grassless circle remains stable regardless of environmental conditions.

No natural explanation for its existence has been identified.

---

# 4. PREVIOUS OCCUPANT

The grassless circle previously contained an anomalous manifestation of **Jake**.

The manifestation consisted of:

- Jake's physical form;
    
- folded wings;
    
- shattered halo;
    
- azure eyes;
    
- an anomalous arrow passing through his chest.
    

The teddy bear normally associated with Jake was absent during this period.

Following Jake's return to the SAFEHOUSE, the circle is now unoccupied.

The environment itself remains.

---

# 5. COGNITIVE PROPERTIES

LSIN-001 possesses a significant mnemonic effect.

Personnel exposed to the central region may experience sudden restoration of suppressed or inaccessible memories.

During the original investigation, the effect occurred simultaneously across multiple SAFEHOUSE residents.

Recovered memories included personal experiences involving Jake.

The effect was sufficiently powerful to restore extensive autobiographical information previously believed to have been permanently inaccessible.

This property is now classified as:

> **LSIN-001-C — Mnemonic Restoration Effect**

---

# 6. MEMORY SAFETY

L.S.I.N. has determined that the mnemonic effect should not be intentionally triggered without authorization.

The restoration is not selective.

It may return:

- ordinary memories;
    
- emotionally significant memories;
    
- traumatic memories;
    
- previously suppressed information;
    
- memories associated with anomalous events.
    

The subject may have no opportunity to prepare for the information being restored.

Accordingly:

> **Unauthorized exposure to LSIN-001-C is prohibited.**

---

# 7. ACCESS

The primary access point remains the SAFEHOUSE elevator.

One elevator button remains anomalous.

It does not respond to ordinary interaction.

Under certain conditions, however, selecting the button results in transportation to LSIN-001.

The mechanism remains unknown.

The button itself cannot be assigned a conventional floor designation.

It is therefore referred to by L.S.I.N. as:

> **UNASSIGNED DESTINATION — U-01**

---

# 8. BLUE STARDUST

Faint blue Stardust continues to accumulate around U-01.

The particles:

- cannot be permanently removed;
    
- reappear after cleaning;
    
- do not interfere with elevator operation;
    
- possess anomalous properties inconsistent with ordinary particulate matter.
    

L.S.I.N. has determined that the Stardust is likely related to the liminal space.

Its exact origin remains unknown.

---

# 9. RESIDENT ACCESS

Jake's return has not caused LSIN-001 to disappear.

This is considered significant.

The space therefore appears to be independent of the individual who previously occupied its central landmark.

Jake is **not** classified as the source of LSIN-001.

Likewise, the space is not considered a conventional afterlife environment, storage location, or personal containment area.

Its relationship to Jake remains unresolved.

---

> **STAR:**

Nightshade pointed toward the grass.

---

# 12. NEUTRALIZATION STATUS

L.S.I.N. has determined that **neutralization is not currently required.**

LSIN-001 demonstrates no confirmed hostile behavior.

No predatory entities have been identified.

No environmental attacks have been observed.

No structural instability has been detected.

Its primary anomalous effects are cognitive and spatial.

The space will therefore remain under observation rather than being forcibly neutralized.

---

# 13. VISITATION POLICY

Authorized personnel may enter LSIN-001 for:

- investigation;
    
- controlled cognitive research;
    
- environmental mapping;
    
- anomaly monitoring;
    
- resident welfare purposes.
    

Residents are not to be forced into the environment.

Personnel experiencing distress during exposure are to return immediately.

The central circle is considered a sensitive location.

No objects are to be removed from it without L.S.I.N. authorization.
