**SUBJECT:** Clover Luminare — Culinary Competency

---

# 1. SUMMARY

Clover Luminare is an **exceptionally competent cook.**

This was initially considered an incidental skill.

It is not.

Clover demonstrates strong understanding of:

- flavor balance;
    
- ingredient selection;
    
- cooking temperatures;
    
- texture;
    
- timing;
    
- seasoning;
    
- presentation;
    
- portioning;
    
- and meal planning.
    

Her cooking consistently receives positive responses from SAFEHOUSE residents.

---

# 2. DISCOVERY

Clover's culinary ability was discovered when she prepared dinner for herself.

Several residents noticed the smell.

Neri appeared first.

Then Jane.

Then Bao.

Then approximately half the SAFEHOUSE.

Clover looked up from the kitchen.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "What?"

Jane looked at the food.

> **JANE:**  
> "You made that?"

Clover nodded.

> **JANE:**  
> "For yourself?"

Another nod.

Jane stared at her.

> **JANE:**  
> "You're going to have to start cooking more."

Clover looked mildly concerned.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "...Why?"

---

# 3. CULINARY STYLE

Clover's cooking tends to be practical rather than extravagant.

She does not require unusual ingredients.

She can produce excellent meals from ordinary SAFEHOUSE supplies.

Her strongest characteristic is consistency.

Clover rarely produces a meal that is merely "good."

It tends to be:

> **Very good.**

---

# 4. LUCK AND COOKING

C.O.R.E. initially questioned whether Clover's anomalous luck contributed to her cooking.

Controlled observation suggests that it does **not** directly create culinary skill.

Clover actually understands what she is doing.

Her luck may occasionally assist with minor circumstances:

- finding an unexpectedly good ingredient;
    
- avoiding a minor kitchen mishap;
    
- having something finish cooking at exactly the right moment;
    
- discovering that the last remaining ingredient happens to be exactly what she needed.
    

However:

> **Clover can cook.**

Her meals are not successful merely because reality likes her.

---

# 5. RESIDENT FEEDBACK

### BAO

Bao was particularly impressed.

After tasting Clover's cooking:

> **BAO:**  
> "You learned this yourself?"

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Mostly."

Bao smiled.

> **BAO:**  
> "You're good."

Clover appeared genuinely pleased.

---

### NERI

Neri is difficult to impress with food.

She ate Clover's meal.

Finished it.

Then quietly pushed her plate forward.

Clover understood.

She wanted more.

No verbal compliment was provided.

Clover considered the empty plate sufficient.

---

### JAKE

Jake was given a small portion.

He ate quietly.

Then looked at Clover.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Can you make this again?"

Clover smiled.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Sure."

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Okay."

He returned to his grass.

This was considered a strong endorsement.

---

### JANE DOE

Jane's response was considerably less subtle.

> **JANE:**  
> "You're cooking again tomorrow."

Clover blinked.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Was that a request?"

> **JANE:**  
> "No."

Pause.

> **JANE:**  
> "That was me volunteering you."

Clover laughed.

---

# 6. KITCHEN BEHAVIOR

Clover is unusually calm in the kitchen.

She does not rush.

She prepares ingredients in advance.

She keeps her workspace organized.

She cleans as she goes.

This contrasts significantly with several other SAFEHOUSE residents.

C.O.R.E. kitchen staff have described Clover's workspace as:

> **"Suspiciously civilized."**

---

# 7. CLOVER'S FAVORITE PART

When asked what she likes most about cooking, Clover answered:

> **"Knowing exactly what I'm making."**

She paused.

Then added:

> **"And watching people eat it."**

This was noted as significant.

Clover spent much of her childhood being treated as though her existence itself was a problem.

Cooking gives her something simpler.

She makes something.

Someone enjoys it.

There is no hidden requirement.

No test.

No accusation.

Just dinner.

---

# 8. JAKE'S ROOM

Clover occasionally cooks small snacks specifically for Jake.

She brings them to his room.

Jake usually eats them while sitting on the grass.

Clover sits nearby with her notebook.

One afternoon:

> **JAKE:**  
> "This is good."

Clover smiled.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Thanks."

Jake looked at the plate.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Can you make more?"

Clover nodded.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Of course."

Jake returned to eating.

A four-leaf clover grew beside one of his blue flowers.

Neither commented on it.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Clover's culinary ability has become another unexpected contribution to SAFEHOUSE life.

She does not cook because she is required to.

She does not cook because she needs to prove she is useful.

She cooks because she enjoys it.

And because, apparently, everyone likes eating what she makes.

C.O.R.E. therefore recommends that personnel **stop asking Clover whether she can cook and simply assume the answer is yes.**

There is one final complication.

Clover has recently begun experimenting with desserts.

Bao has expressed interest.

Jane has expressed extreme interest.

Neri has expressed interest without admitting it.

Jake has simply asked:

> **"Is there cake?"**

Clover answered:

> **"Eventually."**

Jake nodded.

He appears willing to wait.
