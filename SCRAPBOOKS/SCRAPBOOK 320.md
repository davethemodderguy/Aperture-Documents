# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **SECURITY DIVISION – MAJOR INCIDENT REPORT**

### **CASE FILE: KCA-IR-127 – MASS ROBBERY ATTEMPT**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

**Date:** ██/██/20██

At approximately **09:41**, fifteen armed individuals attempted a coordinated assault on KCA Headquarters.

The group intended to force entry through the main entrance.

They never made it past the front doors.

No shots were fired.

---

# **TIMELINE**

**09:40:53**

Camera Mart observed multiple armed individuals exiting two vehicles.

Without taking its eyes off the monitors, it calmly announced:

> "Bad."

Security immediately acknowledged the alert.

---

**09:40:56**

The Mart hivemind classified the event as an active hostile intrusion.

Approximately **450 Rapid Response Marts** entered the ventilation system.

Estimated deployment time:

> **Under four seconds.**

---

**09:41:07**

As the lead robber reached for the front entrance...

The ceiling moved.

Hundreds of tiny blue figures silently emerged from ventilation grilles, support beams, decorative ledges, and overhead lighting.

Security footage later showed that most of the suspects looked upward at exactly the same moment.

---

**09:41:09**

The ambush commenced.

Approximately 450 Rapid Response Marts entered Rock Mode simultaneously.

The robbers were struck from multiple directions with carefully controlled force.

Within seconds:

- Every firearm had been knocked away.
    
- Every suspect had been pinned to the ground.
    
- Every escape route had been blocked.
    

No lethal force was used.

No KCA personnel were injured.

No civilian injuries were reported.

No suspect managed to enter the building.

---

# **UNEXPECTED DEVELOPMENT**

During the restraint process, **five suspects lost consciousness.**

Medical personnel later confirmed the cause.

They had not suffered significant physical trauma.

Instead...

They had fainted after looking up and witnessing several hundred blue boulders launching themselves from the ceiling.

---

# **THE MARTS' REACTION**

The moment the five suspects stopped responding...

The entire Rapid Response group immediately abandoned tactical formation.

Rock Mode ceased.

Hundreds of Marts hurried toward the unconscious individuals.

Security audio recorded dozens of worried voices.

> "Wake?"

> "Friend?"

> "Sorry."

> "Please?"

> "Okay?"

One Rapid Response Mart gently patted an unconscious robber's shoulder.

Another attempted to offer him a cheese cracker.

Several simply stood nearby looking visibly anxious.

---

# **MEDICAL RESPONSE**

KCA Medical arrived moments later.

After a brief examination, the attending medic announced:

> "...They're alive."

> "...They fainted."

The nearest Rapid Response Mart immediately sighed in relief.

> "...Sleep."

Within seconds, the atmosphere changed.

Many of the Marts visibly relaxed.

One quietly said:

> "Good."

---

# **CASE STATUS**

All fifteen suspects were arrested without a single shot being fired.

The attempted attack ended entirely at the front entrance.

The greatest source of concern during the incident was not the robbery itself...

...but convincing several hundred worried Marts that the unconscious robbers had merely fainted from shock.

The attending physician later summarized the event in a single sentence:

> "The robbers were never in danger."

> "...Their pride was."

**End of Major Incident Report**
