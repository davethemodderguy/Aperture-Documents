**SUBJECT:** _One Whole Day as a Little White Cat_  
**INVOLVED RESIDENTS:** Jake Akana, Camelia Candleheart  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — RESIDENTIAL WING  
**EVENT DURATION:** One day

---

### I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following the restoration of Jake Akana's physical body by Guiding Light, Jake elected to remain in his Cat Thiren form for an entire day.

His chosen form was that of a white cat with blue eyes.

For Camelia Candleheart, this was a significant experience. Although she had previously seen photographs of Jake before his death, those images could not reproduce the experience of interacting with him in his living form. She had also seen his ghostly cat form, which retained four legs and a tail but lacked visible eyes and ears.

This time, Jake was alive.

His white fur, blue eyes, and cat ears were physically present. He could move, play, respond to touch, and interact with Camelia as an ordinary-looking cat.

Jake allowed his older sister to spend the day playing with him.

Camelia took full advantage of the opportunity.

The event was characterized by sustained play, affectionate interaction, and a marked reduction in Camelia's usual preoccupation with her missing memories. She did not express jealousy toward Jake's restored body. Instead, she appeared delighted by the opportunity to spend time with him in a form she had previously known only through images and recollection.

**C.O.R.E. assessment:** The event represents a positive family interaction following an extraordinary change in Jake's condition. No adverse effects were reported.

---

### II. EVENT LOG

#### 08:05 — An Unexpectedly Fluffy Morning

Camelia entered the shared residential area and found a white cat sitting on the sofa.

The cat had blue eyes and upright ears. Its tail moved slowly from side to side.

Camelia stopped in the doorway.

The cat looked at her.

**CAMELIA:** “Jake?”

The cat meowed.

Camelia approached slowly.

**CAMELIA:** “You're actually a cat.”

_The cat meowed again._

**CAMELIA:** “A real, fluffy, alive cat.”

_Jake's tail flicked._

**CAMELIA:** “Can I touch you?”

_Jake walked toward her and bumped his head against her hand._

Camelia immediately crouched down.

**CAMELIA:** “I'm taking that as a yes.”

_She gently stroked the top of his head, running her fingers between his ears._

She stopped almost immediately and stared at them.

**CAMELIA:** “Your ears are so soft!”

_Jake's ears rotated toward her voice._

**CAMELIA:** “They move! They actually move!”

_Jake blinked slowly._

**CAMELIA:** “Oh, that's adorable.”

_Jake gave a small, indignant-sounding meow._

**CAMELIA:** “What? You are!”

_Jake turned his head away, but did not leave._

**Observation:** Camelia spent several minutes examining Jake's ears, paws, and fur with his cooperation. She appeared particularly fascinated by his blue eyes, repeatedly moving slightly to either side to see how he tracked her.

At no point did she attempt to force him to remain still. Jake was free to move away, but repeatedly returned to her.

---

#### 08:42 — The First Attempt at Play

Camelia retrieved a small fabric ribbon from a collection of SAFEHOUSE recreational materials.

She held it in front of Jake.

Jake watched it.

She moved it to the left.

His head turned left.

She moved it to the right.

His head turned right.

She slowly raised it above his head.

Jake's ears tilted forward.

**CAMELIA:** “Oh, you're watching it.”

_Jake's pupils widened._

**CAMELIA:** “You're really watching it.”

_She moved the ribbon again._

Jake pounced.

Camelia gasped.

**CAMELIA:** “YOU POUNCED!”

_Jake caught the ribbon between his front paws._

**CAMELIA:** “You have little paws! And claws! Wait, are you supposed to have claws?”

_Jake released the ribbon and looked at her._

**CAMELIA:** “Sorry. I'm asking too many questions.”

_She wiggled the ribbon again._

Jake immediately pounced a second time.

**CAMELIA:** “Never mind. More questions later.”

The game continued for approximately twenty minutes.

Camelia's laughter was audible in the corridor.

A passing resident later reported seeing her lying on the floor, moving the ribbon while Jake attempted to capture it with increasing determination.

The resident described the scene as “the happiest either of them had looked in a while.”

---

#### 09:30 — A Cat on the Lap

After playing, Jake climbed onto Camelia's lap and settled down.

Camelia froze.

She looked down at him, then toward the corridor, as if worried that moving even slightly might disturb him.

**CAMELIA:** “Jake?”

_Jake's eyes were half-closed._

**CAMELIA:** “Are you comfortable?”

_He purred._

Camelia covered her mouth.

**CAMELIA:** “Oh, no.”

_Jake continued purring._

**CAMELIA:** “That's not fair.”

_Jake opened one eye._

**CAMELIA:** “You're telling me you can do that whenever you want?”

_Jake's tail curled around his body._

**CAMELIA:** “I could get used to this.”

_She gently stroked his back._

**CAMELIA:** “I used to look at your pictures and try to imagine what you were like before you died.”

_Jake's ears turned toward her._

**CAMELIA:** “Now you're just sitting on me.”

_She smiled._

**CAMELIA:** “I think I like this better.”

Jake remained on her lap until he chose to get up.

Camelia did not attempt to keep him there when he moved.

---

#### 10:14 — The Ears, Again

Camelia appeared unable to stop looking at Jake's ears.

While he sat beside her, she watched them rotate toward sounds from different parts of the room.

**CAMELIA:** “Do you know you're doing that?”

_One ear turned toward her._

**CAMELIA:** “That! You did it again!”

_Jake stared at her._

**CAMELIA:** “Your ears are listening to me.”

_Jake meowed._

**CAMELIA:** “I know the rest of you is listening, too. It's just—”

_She paused, trying to find the words._

**CAMELIA:** “I remember the photographs. I remember knowing you used to have these.”

_She gently touched one ear, then withdrew her hand._

**CAMELIA:** “But I never thought I'd get to see them move.”

_Jake leaned his head against her arm._

**CAMELIA:** “Thank you for letting me do this.”

Jake responded with a quiet purr.

**C.O.R.E. note:** Camelia's response was not simply fascination with an unusual biological feature. Her repeated attention to Jake's ears and eyes appeared connected to the contrast between his former living appearance, the photographs she had studied, and the ghostly form in which she had first known him.

She was experiencing something she had previously been able only to imagine.

---

#### 11:20 — The Cat-and-String Incident

Camelia found a suitable toy and attempted to play a more conventional game.

Jake demonstrated an immediate interest.

The first few minutes proceeded without difficulty.

Then Jake caught the toy, rolled onto his side, and kicked at it with his hind legs.

Camelia stared at him.

**CAMELIA:** “What are you doing?”

_Jake kicked the toy again._

**CAMELIA:** “Jake.”

_Another kick._

**CAMELIA:** “You're being ridiculous.”

_Jake looked at her with bright blue eyes._

**CAMELIA:** “You're a whole cat. I can't even argue with you.”

_Jake made a small chirping sound._

**CAMELIA:** “Did you just laugh at me?”

_Jake immediately returned to attacking the toy._

Camelia laughed so hard that she had to put it down for a moment.

When she recovered, she resumed playing.

Jake did not appear interested in ending the game.

---

#### 12:06 — Lunch Break

Camelia took a break for lunch.

Jake remained in his cat form nearby.

She placed her food on the table and looked down at him.

**CAMELIA:** “You know, if you're going to stay a cat all day, I need to know what you can and can't eat.”

_Jake meowed._

**CAMELIA:** “That isn't an answer.”

_Jake sat beside her chair._

**CAMELIA:** “No stealing from my plate.”

_Jake looked at the plate._

**CAMELIA:** “I'm serious.”

_Jake looked at her._

**CAMELIA:** “Jake.”

_He blinked._

**CAMELIA:** “You're doing that on purpose.”

_Jake turned away and began grooming one paw._

Camelia laughed again.

Staff ensured that Jake had appropriate food and water available for his living form. Camelia was encouraged to enjoy his company without feeling responsible for independently managing his needs.

The lunch period remained peaceful.

---

#### 13:18 — A White Cat in the Sunlight

During the early afternoon, Jake settled near a window where sunlight fell across the floor.

His white fur caught the light, and his blue eyes followed small movements outside.

Camelia sat beside him.

For a while, neither of them played.

She simply watched him breathe.

**CAMELIA:** “You're warm.”

_Jake turned his head toward her._

**CAMELIA:** “I mean, you're alive. I knew that, obviously, but it's different.”

_She held out her hand._

Jake pressed his head against it.

**CAMELIA:** “When you were a ghost, I could sit with you. I could talk to you. You were still you.”

_She stroked the side of his face._

**CAMELIA:** “But I couldn't do this.”

_Jake's eyes closed as she scratched gently beneath his chin._

**CAMELIA:** “I can't explain it properly.”

_Jake purred._

**CAMELIA:** “You don't need to understand. Just stay there.”

Jake stayed.

Camelia leaned back against the sofa, one hand resting on his fur.

No discussion of death, forgotten memories, or the circumstances of Jake's murder occurred during this period.

For several minutes, they were simply siblings sharing a quiet afternoon.

---

#### 14:02 — The Cat Is Missing

A short period of confusion occurred when Camelia returned from retrieving a blanket and could no longer see Jake.

She searched the immediate area.

**CAMELIA:** “Jake?”

No response.

**CAMELIA:** “Jake, where did you go?”

A soft sound came from beneath the sofa.

Camelia crouched down.

Two blue eyes stared back at her from the shadows.

**CAMELIA:** “There you are!”

_Jake remained beneath the sofa._

**CAMELIA:** “Are you hiding?”

_Jake blinked._

**CAMELIA:** “From me?”

_Jake's tail appeared at the other end of the sofa._

**CAMELIA:** “Oh, you're playing hide-and-seek!”

_Jake retreated farther beneath the furniture._

**CAMELIA:** “All right. I can play that.”

The game lasted approximately fifteen minutes.

Camelia did not attempt to drag Jake out of his hiding place. Instead, she waited until he came out on his own.

When he finally emerged, she held out both hands.

**CAMELIA:** “Found you.”

_Jake walked past her, tail held high._

**CAMELIA:** “Excuse me! I won!”

Jake continued walking.

**CAMELIA:** “You can't just leave after losing!”

_Jake looked back at her._

Camelia burst out laughing.

---

#### 15:11 — The White Cat and His Older Sister

By mid-afternoon, Camelia had become more comfortable interacting with Jake in his living form.

She no longer stopped every few minutes to marvel at his ears. Instead, she incorporated them into the game, gently scratching behind them when Jake approached and stroking his head when he settled beside her.

She also continued to examine his eyes with quiet fascination.

At one point, Jake lay beside her with his head on her thigh.

**CAMELIA:** “Jake?”

_One blue eye opened._

**CAMELIA:** “I'm glad you're here.”

_Jake's eye closed again._

**CAMELIA:** “Not just because of today. I mean... I'm glad you got to come back.”

_She pauses._

**CAMELIA:** “I know it doesn't change what happened to you.”

_Jake remains still._

**CAMELIA:** “But you get to be alive now. You get to have warm fur and little ears and paws that can actually touch things.”

_She strokes his head._

**CAMELIA:** “You get to be a cat again.”

_Jake purrs._

**CAMELIA:** “And I get to play with you.”

_She smiles down at him._

**CAMELIA:** “That's enough for me.”

Jake remains beside her.

---

#### 16:40 — An Afternoon Nap That Wasn't Sleep

Jake eventually curled into a small white ball on Camelia's lap.

Camelia remained still, afraid of disturbing him.

After several minutes, she looked down at his closed eyes.

**CAMELIA:** “Are you sleeping?”

Jake's ear twitched.

**CAMELIA:** “I think that's a yes.”

_She lowered her voice._

**CAMELIA:** “All right. I'll be quiet.”

She picked up her notebook with her free hand and began writing.

The entry was short:

> Jake stayed in his cat form today.
> 
> He is a white cat with blue eyes. His ears move when he hears things. His fur is soft, and he purrs when he is comfortable.
> 
> We played with a ribbon. He chased a toy. He hid under the sofa even though I knew where he was.
> 
> He fell asleep on my lap.
> 
> I didn't mind staying still.
> 
> I hope he had fun, too.

Jake continued resting until he chose to get up.

Camelia did not wake him deliberately.

---

#### 18:05 — Evening

As the day drew to a close, Jake remained in his cat form.

Camelia had spent most of the day in his company, playing with him, petting him, and simply watching him move around the SAFEHOUSE.

She was not disappointed that he had not returned to his humanoid form sooner.

When asked whether she wanted Jake to change back, she replied:

**CAMELIA:** “Why would I? He's having fun.”

Jake responded by rubbing his cheek against her hand.

**CAMELIA:** “Besides, I haven't finished playing with him.”

The remark was delivered with enough seriousness that the staff member asking the question elected not to interfere.

---

#### 20:16 — End of the Event

Jake remained a white cat for the remainder of the day, allowing Camelia to continue playing with him.

Before they separated for the evening, Camelia crouched in front of him.

**CAMELIA:** “Thank you for today.”

_Jake meowed._

**CAMELIA:** “I know you can't exactly tell me what you're thinking right now.”

_Jake's tail flicked._

**CAMELIA:** “But I hope you had fun.”

_Jake stepped forward and pressed his head against her chest._

Camelia wrapped her arms around him carefully.

**CAMELIA:** “Good night, little brother.”

_Jake purred._

**CAMELIA:** “And tomorrow, if you want to be a cat again, you can.”

Jake gave a short meow.

Camelia smiled.

**CAMELIA:** “I'll take that as a maybe.”

### III. WELFARE ASSESSMENT

The event produced several positive observations.

**1. Camelia responded positively to Jake's living form.**

Her reaction was characterized by curiosity, delight, affection, and gratitude. She did not express jealousy regarding his restoration.

**2. The cat form provided a new shared experience.**

Although Camelia had previously seen photographs of Jake before his death and had observed his ghostly cat form, she had not been able to interact with him as a living white cat with blue eyes and visible cat ears.

The experience allowed her to encounter that part of Jake's identity directly.

**3. Jake appeared comfortable with the interaction.**

He repeatedly approached Camelia, initiated play, rested on her lap, and remained in her company. No distress associated with staying in cat form was reported.

**4. Camelia's enjoyment did not eliminate her existing needs.**

The event was a positive experience, but it should not be interpreted as a resolution to her memory impairment or nighttime distress. C.O.R.E. recommends allowing her to retain her notebook and continue receiving appropriate welfare support.

**5. Jake's form selection remains his choice.**

The fact that Camelia enjoyed his cat form does not obligate Jake to remain in that form in the future. His willingness to spend the day this way was a gift to his sister, not a new expectation imposed upon him.

---

### IV. FINAL OBSERVATION

Camelia had once looked at a photograph of Jake and wished she could have met him as he had been.

She had seen his ears in the image.

She had seen his eyes.

She had seen a little boy who was alive, who had a body, and who had once lived a life she could only learn about through other people's memories.

Then Guiding Light returned that body to him.

And today, Jake chose to spend the entire day as a white cat.

He let Camelia touch his ears.

He let her admire his blue eyes.

He chased ribbons, attacked toys, hid under the sofa, and fell asleep on her lap.

For hours, Camelia had no reason to search her notebook for forgotten details or struggle to make sense of a memory fragment. She could simply look at Jake and discover something new about him.

His ears twitched when she spoke.

His paws caught the ribbon.

His fur warmed her hands.

And when he was comfortable, he purred.

None of these things could undo what happened to him. A single happy day could not restore the years he had lost, identify his murderer, or guarantee that Camelia's nightmares would stop.

But the day did not need to accomplish any of those things.

It was enough that Jake was alive.

It was enough that Camelia was happy.

And it was enough that, for one whole day, a little white cat let his older sister spoil him to her heart's content.

**C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE:**

_The photographs showed Camelia what Jake used to look like._

_Today, she got to play with him._

_Those are two very different things._
