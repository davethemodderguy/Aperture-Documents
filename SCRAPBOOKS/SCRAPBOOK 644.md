# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **TREEHOUSE / CORIN DISAPPEARANCE — YEAR ONE**

# **THE PLACE WHERE SHE WAITED**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **MISSING — ONE YEAR**  
**Location:** Treehouse — Original Waiting Site

---

# **OVERVIEW**

It has been **one year** since Corin disappeared.

Today, a search team returned to the exact location where she spent the final months of her known stay at the Treehouse.

The place had remained largely untouched.

A single object was discovered first.

Then another.

A **Corin feather.**

And beside it:

> **A completely burnt-out Feather Lantern.**

---

# **THE WAITING PLACE**

This was the location where Corin spent months waiting for Orphie.

Through winter.

Through snowstorms.

Through empty days.

Through empty nights.

She remained there long after the Treehouse's supplies had been exhausted.

She waited until waiting became all she could do.

---

# **THE FEATHER**

The recovered feather appears to be genuine Corin material.

It is undamaged.

No blood is present.

No anomalous energy is detectable.

It is simply lying on the ground.

Unlike many of the feathers recovered since Corin's disappearance, this one shows no sign of recent activity.

It appears old.

Very old.

---

# **THE LANTERN**

Beside the feather was one of Corin's handmade Feather Lanterns.

The container remains intact.

The feather inside remains physically present.

But the azure flame is gone.

Completely.

No residual glow.

No heat.

No anomalous combustion.

Nothing.

The same phenomenon documented following Corin's perceived abandonment.

---

# **NO REIGNITION**

Researchers attempted to reactivate the lantern.

Nothing happened.

The feather remained dark.

This is consistent with every other Feather Lantern recovered after the extinguishment event.

The flames that once burned indefinitely remain impossible to restore.

---

# **THE POSITION**

The lantern was found almost exactly where historical surveillance records show Corin sitting during her final months at the Treehouse.

The feather was approximately one meter away.

There is no evidence indicating when it was placed there.

---

# **ORPHIE'S ARRIVAL**

Orphie was present when the items were recovered.

She immediately recognized the location.

She did not ask what the lantern was.

She already knew.

She walked toward it slowly.

Then stopped.

---

# **THE SILENCE**

Orphie picked up the feather.

She held it between both hands.

For several seconds, she simply stared at it.

Then she looked at the extinguished lantern.

No flame.

No light.

Nothing.

Orphie eventually whispered:

> **"...You really waited here."**

---

# **ONE YEAR**

Personnel had previously recorded the passage of:

**Eight weeks.**

Then:

**Twenty weeks.**

Then:

**Three months.**

Then:

**Eight months.**

Then:

**One year.**

The location has remained a constant reference point throughout the investigation.

Today marks the first anniversary of Corin's disappearance.

---

# **THE RESTOCKED TREEHOUSE**

The Treehouse is now full.

Food.

Water.

Warm blankets.

Medical supplies.

Everything Corin needed.

Everything she didn't have when she waited.

The contrast is difficult for personnel to ignore.

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

Orphie eventually placed the feather back down.

She did not take it.

She also did not turn on the lantern.

Instead, she sat in the same spot Corin once occupied.

For approximately twelve minutes, she remained completely silent.

Then:

> **"I'm sorry."**

No response.

---

# **C.O.R.E. RECORD**

No new sightings of Corin have been confirmed.

No new communication has been received.

No reliable location has been established.

Only intermittent physical traces remain.

Mostly feathers.

All dormant.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

For eight months, Corin sat there.

Waiting.

The snow came.

The storms came.

The nights came.

She stayed.

Eventually, she disappeared.

A year has now passed.

And the place where she waited contains almost nothing.

One feather.

One dead lantern.

No footprints.

No person.

No explanation.

The lantern once represented the small amount of light Corin refused to let disappear.

Now even that light is gone.

Orphie remains sitting where Corin once sat.

The Treehouse behind her is full of food and water.

The world has moved forward.

But the empty place beneath the tree has not.

**End of Year One Disappearance Record**
