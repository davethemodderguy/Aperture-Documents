# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

At approximately **00:00**, Invictus Aeterna accompanied Nora during one of her routine nighttime walks through the Blacksite.

This was not a security patrol.

It was not an investigation.

Aeterna was simply following Nora.

Nora has increasingly spent nighttime hours wandering the SAFEHOUSE and surrounding Blacksite corridors due to her adaptation eliminating her need for sleep.

Aeterna had previously allowed Nora considerable nighttime independence.

On this occasion, however, she decided to accompany her.

Approximately twelve minutes into the walk, Nora encountered the entity previously designated:

> **JAKE**

Aeterna had not previously met him.

---

# 2. INITIAL OBSERVATION

Nora was walking several meters ahead.

Aeterna followed behind her.

The Blacksite was quiet.

Most residents were asleep.

The Tachyon Merch Store was closed.

Nora stopped.

Aeterna stopped.

Nora was looking toward the glass storefront.

Jake was standing on the opposite side.

Blue spectral light illuminated the hallway around him.

He was staring through the security glass.

More specifically:

At the display containing **Bullseye — Unit 001.**

Nora raised a hand.

> **Nora:**  
> “Jake.”

Jake turned.

He looked at Nora.

Then looked at Aeterna.

He stared for several seconds.

---

# 3. FIRST CONTACT

Jake slowly pointed at Aeterna.

> **Jake:**  
> “Who is that?”

Nora turned around.

> **Nora:**  
> “That's Aeterna.”

Aeterna looked down at Jake.

Her celestial eyes glowed faintly.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “And you are Jake.”

Jake nodded.

> **Jake:**  
> “Yeah.”

A pause.

Jake looked at Aeterna's armor.

Then at her sword.

Then back at her face.

> **Jake:**  
> “You're really tall.”

Nora immediately laughed.

Aeterna remained completely still.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “I am.”

Jake looked at the sword.

> **Jake:**  
> “Is that heavy?”

> **Aeterna:**  
> “No.”

Jake nodded.

> **Jake:**  
> “Cool.”

---

# 4. NORA'S EXPLANATION

Nora appeared to realize that Aeterna had not been informed about Jake.

She pointed toward the ghost.

> **Nora:**  
> “He's Jake.”

> **Aeterna:**  
> “I gathered that.”

> **Nora:**  
> “He's an angel.”

Aeterna looked at Jake again.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “An angel.”

Jake nodded.

> **Jake:**  
> “I think so.”

Aeterna's posture changed slightly.

Not defensive.

Interested.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “You think so?”

Jake shrugged.

> **Jake:**  
> “That's what I am.”

Nora nodded.

> **Nora:**  
> “He's dead.”

Jake added:

> **Jake:**  
> “I died a long time ago.”

Aeterna was silent.

---

# 5. THE QUESTION

Aeterna eventually asked:

> **Aeterna:**  
> “How long have you remained here?”

Jake thought about it.

> **Jake:**  
> “I don't know.”

> **Aeterna:**  
> “You don't remember?”

> **Jake:**  
> “I remember dying.”

Pause.

> **Jake:**  
> “I don't remember when.”

Aeterna looked toward the surrounding Blacksite.

Jake continued.

> **Jake:**  
> “This wasn't here when I was alive.”

Aeterna understood.

The land had existed before the Blacksite.

Jake had remained after his death.

---

# 6. FOOD

Aeterna noticed something else.

Jake was staring toward the cafeteria.

Nora noticed too.

> **Nora:**  
> “He's hungry.”

Aeterna looked at Jake.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “You are hungry?”

Jake nodded.

> **Jake:**  
> “A lot.”

Aeterna glanced toward Nora.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “He cannot eat?”

> **Nora:**  
> “Not normal food.”

Jake looked slightly embarrassed.

> **Jake:**  
> “It goes through me.”

Aeterna paused.

Then nodded.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “Understood.”

There was no disgust.

No fear.

No hesitation.

Just understanding.

---

# 7. THE BULLSEYE

Jake returned his attention to the storefront.

Nora followed his gaze.

Aeterna did as well.

The Bullseye remained displayed behind the glass.

Jake pointed.

> **Jake:**  
> “I like that one.”

Aeterna looked at it.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “Why?”

Jake shrugged.

> **Jake:**  
> “It's pretty.”

Nora smiled.

> **Nora:**  
> “He thinks it's cool.”

Jake nodded.

> **Jake:**  
> “Yeah.”

Aeterna considered the rifle for several seconds.

Then looked back at Jake.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “It is visually pleasing.”

Nora stared at her.

> **Nora:**  
> “You agree?”

> **Aeterna:**  
> “I did not say it was tactically superior.”

Nora grinned.

> **Nora:**  
> “Tacticool.”

Jake repeated the word.

> **Jake:**  
> “Tacticool.”

Aeterna sighed.

---

# 8. AETERNA'S ASSESSMENT

Aeterna eventually crouched.

Her armor lowered until she was closer to Jake's height.

Jake looked at her.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “Nora has spoken about you.”

Jake looked at Nora.

> **Jake:**  
> “Good things?”

Nora nodded.

> **Nora:**  
> “Yeah.”

Jake smiled faintly.

Aeterna continued:

> **Aeterna:**  
> “You have been wandering here alone for a long time.”

Jake nodded.

> **Jake:**  
> “Yeah.”

> **Aeterna:**  
> “You do not need to do that anymore.”

Jake stared at her.

He looked confused.

> **Jake:**  
> “Why?”

Aeterna looked toward Nora.

Nora was already looking at Jake.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “Because you have friends now.”

Jake remained silent.

Then:

> **Jake:**  
> “Oh.”

---

# 9. NORA'S RESPONSE

Nora walked over and stood beside Jake.

She pointed at herself.

> **Nora:**  
> “Me.”

Then toward the SAFEHOUSE.

> **Nora:**  
> “Them.”

Then toward Aeterna.

> **Nora:**  
> “Her.”

Jake looked at Aeterna.

> **Jake:**  
> “She's your mom?”

Nora nodded.

> **Nora:**  
> “Basically.”

Aeterna did not correct her.

Jake smiled.

> **Jake:**  
> “Okay.”

---

# 10. RETURN TO WALKING

The three remained outside the storefront for several minutes.

Eventually Nora turned around.

> **Nora:**  
> “Come on.”

Jake followed.

Aeterna followed behind both of them.

Nora suddenly looked back.

> **Nora:**  
> “You're following us?”

> **Aeterna:**  
> “Yes.”

> **Nora:**  
> “Why?”

> **Aeterna:**  
> “It is midnight.”

Nora considered this.

> **Nora:**  
> “I'm fine.”

> **Aeterna:**  
> “I know.”

> **Nora:**  
> “Then why?”

Aeterna paused.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “Because I want to.”

Nora went quiet.

Jake looked between them.

Then smiled.

---

# 11. C.O.R.E. OBSERVATION

The encounter produced no hostile behavior.

Jake did not react negatively to Aeterna.

Aeterna did not react negatively to Jake.

The interaction was notably uneventful.

This is considered positive.

There is also an interesting social development.

Prior to this encounter:

> **Nora → Jake**

represented Jake's primary nighttime companionship.

Following the encounter:

> **Nora + Aeterna → Jake**

may become a recurring nighttime arrangement.

Aeterna's presence did not appear to make Jake uncomfortable.

If anything, Jake appeared curious about her.

---

# 12. CLOSING OBSERVATION

At approximately 00:37, Nora, Jake, and Aeterna were observed walking through Sector 4-C.

Nora was talking.

Jake was listening.

Aeterna was walking behind them.

Eventually Jake asked:

> **Jake:**  
> “Aeterna?”

> **Aeterna:**  
> “Yes?”

> **Jake:**  
> “Can you fly?”

Aeterna looked at him.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “Yes.”

Jake looked at Nora.

Nora looked back.

> **Nora:**  
> “She can.”

Jake thought for a moment.

> **Jake:**  
> “Cool.”

Aeterna continued walking.

Nora continued walking.

Jake continued walking.

Three anomalous beings.

Three different histories.

One very quiet Blacksite corridor.

And, for perhaps the first time since Jake's death:

**He was not walking alone.**

she would walk with her.

And Jake, apparently, has decided that a fifteen-thousand-year-old celestial guardian is:

> **“Cool.”**
