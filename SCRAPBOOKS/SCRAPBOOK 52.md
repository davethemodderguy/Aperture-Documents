# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **TACTICAL EQUIPMENT PROFILE**

### **SUBJECT: ARCTURUS LOCKWOOD – "THE COWBOY LOADOUT"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Personnel Reference

---

# **OVERVIEW**

While many KCA and Blacksite operators carry increasingly specialized or unconventional weaponry, **Security Advisor Arcturus Lockwood** remains committed to a philosophy of simplicity.

His loadout has changed very little over the years.

As Arcturus puts it:

> "If it works, keep it working."

Among Blacksite personnel, his equipment is affectionately referred to as:

> **"The Cowboy Loadout."**

---

# **PRIMARY SIDEARM**

## **.357 Magnum Revolver**

**Action:** Double-Action / Single-Action Revolver

**Caliber:** .357 Magnum

**Capacity:** 6 Rounds

**Purpose:**

- Everyday sidearm
    
- Precision engagements
    
- Emergency response
    

The revolver has no anomalous properties.

No hidden mechanisms.

No electronic assistance.

No transformation capabilities.

It is maintained to an immaculate standard, with Arcturus personally cleaning and inspecting it after every range session or mission.

Despite its simplicity, the revolver is feared by hostile entities because of the specialized ammunition it fires.

---

# **PRIMARY LONG GUN**

## **Modernized Lever-Action Rifle**

**Action:** Lever-Action

**Caliber:** .357 Magnum

Unlike the revolver, the rifle is equipped with practical modern accessories, selected according to mission requirements.

Common attachments include:

- Variable-power optic.
    
- Backup iron sights.
    
- Weapon light.
    
- Sling.
    
- Muzzle device suited to the operation.
    

The rifle itself remains mechanically conventional.

Arcturus has repeatedly refused proposals to convert it into anything more elaborate.

---

# **AMMUNITION**

Both firearms utilize **Norma Lockwood's custom-engineered .357 Magnum cartridges**.

These rounds are optimized for:

- High armor penetration.
    
- Reliable accuracy.
    
- Effective engagement of Ether Walkers.
    
- Consistent ballistic performance.
    

The ammunition is considered the only "special" aspect of Arcturus' arsenal.

Everything else depends entirely on the shooter.

---

# **SUPPORT EQUIPMENT**

In addition to his firearms, Arcturus routinely carries:

- Leather cartridge bandolier concealed beneath his jacket.
    
- Speed loaders for his revolver.
    
- Compact field maintenance kit.
    
- Multi-tool.
    
- Binoculars.
    
- First aid kit.
    

His gear is arranged with meticulous consistency, allowing him to retrieve any item by touch alone.

---

# **PERSONAL PHILOSOPHY**

Arcturus has often summarized his views on firearms with a few simple rules:

> "Carry what you trust."

> "Know every part of it."

> "Take care of it."

> "Hit what you aim at."

He places greater value on discipline and practice than on technological complexity.

# **RANGE REPUTATION**

Watching Arcturus shoot has become a favorite pastime for newer Blacksite personnel.

His demonstrations are characterized by:

- Smooth, economical movements.
    
- Rapid but controlled cycling of the lever-action.
    
- Exceptionally fast revolver draws.
    
- Careful shot placement rather than excessive fire.
    

As one KCA instructor observed:

> **"He doesn't make the rifle look fast."**

> **"He makes it look effortless."**

---

# **STATUS**

Arcturus Lockwood remains one of the few senior operators whose reputation rests almost entirely on personal skill instead of increasingly sophisticated equipment.

While others wield transforming weapons, Ether-powered devices, or oversized melee arms, the KCA's cowboy continues to rely on a revolver, a lever-action rifle, and decades of experience.

To Arcturus, that's all he needs.

To Neko...

It's still a work in progress.
