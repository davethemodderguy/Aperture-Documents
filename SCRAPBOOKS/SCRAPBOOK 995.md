# INCIDENT LOG

**09:00 HOURS**

Jane Doe enters the cafeteria.

She discovers Neri.

Neri is conscious.

Barely.

Jane immediately approaches.

---

**[09:00:██]**

**NERI:**  
"Jane..."

Jane turns.

Neri is visibly distressed.

She attempts to sit upright but lacks the strength to do so.

**NERI:**  
"Jane, help..."

Jane immediately moves toward her.

**JANE:**  
"Neri?"

Neri attempts to breathe.

She cannot draw enough air.

**NERI:**  
"I can't breathe, please hel—"

Neri's voice breaks.

Jane reaches for her communications device.

---

**[09:00:██]**

**JANE:**  
"MEDICAL! I NEED MEDICAL IN THE CAFETERIA, NOW!"

No response.

Jane tries again.

**JANE:**  
"MEDICAL RESPONSE TO THE CAFETERIA! NERI IS DOWN!"

Nothing.

The weather has disrupted portions of the facility's communications network.

Jane attempts another channel.

Nothing.

She tries a direct emergency call.

Nothing.

Jane looks back at Neri.

---

**[09:01:██]**

Neri is still struggling to breathe.

She attempts to speak.

No intelligible words are produced.

Jane continues attempting to contact assistance.

**JANE:**  
"Come on..."

Another attempt.

Nothing.

**JANE:**  
"COME ON!"

Neri's condition deteriorates rapidly.

Jane drops the communicator.

She stays beside Neri.

**JANE:**  
"Hey. Hey, stay with me."

Neri looks toward Jane.

Her eyes remain open.

For several seconds.

Then they close.

---

# MEDICAL EVENT

Neri loses consciousness.

Her respiratory function ceases.

Cardiac activity subsequently stops.

**09:02 HOURS — CARDIAC ARREST CONFIRMED.**

Jane immediately attempts to summon assistance again.

---

**JANE:**  
"HELP!"

No response.

**JANE:**  
"SOMEBODY, PLEASE!"

No response.

Jane remains beside Neri.

---

## 09:██ HOURS

Additional personnel finally reach the cafeteria.

Emergency medical response begins.

Bao is contacted.

Upon arrival, Bao immediately recognizes the severity of Neri's condition.

No hesitation is recorded.

Bao initiates emergency stabilization using her compatible blood supply.

Neri's cardiovascular system responds.

---

**09:██:██**

Cardiac activity returns.

Respiratory function resumes shortly afterward.

Neri remains unconscious.

Bao does not leave her side.

---

# POST-RESUSCITATION OBSERVATION

Neri survives.

She is transferred for continued medical observation.

Her anemia remains severe.

The underlying autoimmune-like process remains active.

Bao's blood has temporarily weakened the erroneous immune response and replenished Neri's red blood cells sufficiently to allow recovery.

This is **stabilization**, not a cure.

---

# JANE DOE — POST-INCIDENT OBSERVATION

Jane does not leave the cafeteria immediately.

She remains seated beside the location where Neri collapsed.

Her communicator remains on the floor.

Personnel report that Jane is unusually quiet.

When asked if she requires medical attention, she gives no immediate response.

Eventually:

**JANE:**  
"She called me."

No response.

**JANE:**  
"She actually called me."

Jane looks toward the medical team transporting Neri away.

**JANE:**  
"And I couldn't get anyone."

---

## C.O.R.E. NOTE

This incident differs substantially from Neri's previous medical episodes.

Neri did not conceal her condition.

She recognized that something was wrong.

She attempted to seek assistance.

She specifically called for Jane.

Jane responded immediately.

The failure occurred **after** Neri successfully sought help: the emergency communication system did not provide an adequate response during the weather-related operational reduction.

This distinction is considered critical.

Neri did what personnel have repeatedly asked her to do.

She asked for help.

And for several minutes, **help did not arrive.**

### C.O.R.E. INTERNAL COMMENT

Jane Doe has spent years working in environments where failure means someone dies.

She has always been good at preventing that outcome.

This time, she was there.

Neri asked her for help.

Jane tried.

And for several minutes, the only thing Jane could do was watch.

Neri survived because Bao arrived.

That fact does not appear to make Jane feel better.
