# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE – INTERPERSONAL INCIDENT LOG**

## **SUBJECT: NORMA & CORIN LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical & Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The following conversation occurred in the SAFEHOUSE lounge during the late evening.

Only Norma and Corin were present at the beginning of the interaction.

Security cameras recorded the exchange.

No audio abnormalities were detected.

---

# **TRANSCRIPT**

Norma quietly walked over to where Corin was sitting.

Corin was reading through accounting paperwork, though she had not turned a page in several minutes.

Norma sat beside her.

Neither spoke for a while.

Eventually, Norma broke the silence.

> **Norma:** "Corin..."

Corin looked up.

Norma smiled gently.

> **Norma:** "Look in my eyes."

Corin did.

Norma asked softly.

> **"Tell me what you see."**

Corin looked for several seconds.

Then quietly answered.

> **Corin:** "I don't know..."

She tilted her head slightly.

> **"...What about me?"**

Norma's smile never faded.

She answered immediately.

> **"I see a young girl who fought hard."**

Corin froze.

Her expression slowly fell.

She looked away.

After several seconds, she whispered.

> **"I see..."**

A long silence.

> **"...Someone who was forced to be alive."**

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Norma's expression immediately broke.

Tears formed almost instantly.

She covered her mouth, trying to stop herself from crying.

She couldn't.

She had spent years trying to convince Corin that she was more than the experiments.

More than the Ether.

More than the scars.

Hearing Corin describe herself in that way shattered her composure.

Norma quietly sobbed.

> "...No..."

> "...No..."

Corin stared at the floor.

She had not intended to hurt Norma.

She had only answered honestly.

Seeing her older sister cry because of those words overwhelmed her.

Corin stood abruptly.

> "...I'm sorry."

Before Norma could respond—

Corin ran.

---

# **AFTERMATH**

Norma remained seated in the lounge, crying openly.

She repeatedly whispered the same sentence.

> "...You're my little sister..."

> "...You're still my little sister..."

Several minutes later, Belle found Norma alone.

She sat beside her without asking questions.

Norma leaned against Belle's shoulder and continued crying.

---

# **CORIN**

Security cameras later located Corin sitting alone in an empty hallway.

She had pulled her knees against her chest.

Her face was hidden.

Lumi quietly appeared beside her.

Corin spoke first.

> "...I made Norma cry."

Lumi gently shook her head.

> "No."

> "Your pain did."

Corin covered her face.

> "...I didn't want her to hear what I really think."

Lumi rested a comforting hand on Corin's shoulder.

> "She cried because she loves you."

> "Not because she was disappointed in you."

Corin remained silent.

---

# **MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

Psychological Services note that the incident highlights the stark contrast between Corin's self-perception and the way those closest to her perceive her.

To Norma, Corin is a younger sister who endured unimaginable hardship.

To Corin, she remains someone defined by the experiments that took her childhood and changed her life.

Bridging that gap continues to be one of the primary goals of Corin's long-term psychological recovery.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Norma later apologized to Corin for crying.

Corin apologized for making her cry.

Neither believed they had done anything wrong.

They simply hugged each other.

Neither sister let go for a long time.

**End of Project SAFEHOUSE Interpersonal Incident Log**
