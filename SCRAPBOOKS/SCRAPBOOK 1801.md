# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

C.O.R.E. has received a concerning hypothesis regarding Jake Akana's current condition.

**Jake may have withdrawn from conscious engagement with his surroundings.**

The phrase _turned off his consciousness_ originated with Aries Akana during a private conversation with the Welfare Division.

It is not a confirmed medical diagnosis.

However, it may describe the visible result of Jake's current condition more accurately than the term _social withdrawal_.

Jake is not merely refusing to speak.

He is not simply choosing to be alone.

He appears to have stopped participating in the world around him to an extent that was not previously observed.

He sits where he is placed.

He allows people to move him.

He does not initiate familiar interactions.

He does not reliably respond to his name.

His facial expression remains largely unchanged.

His previous preferences have become difficult to identify.

Medical causes of altered responsiveness remain under investigation. C.O.R.E. cannot yet determine whether Jake is experiencing a severe stress-related shutdown, a dissociative state, an anomalous alteration of consciousness, or another condition.

Nevertheless, the possibility raised by Aries warrants serious consideration.

Jake has experienced too much.

And now, the child who once sought comfort from the people around him appears to have withdrawn from the act of seeking anything at all.

---

# 1. THE HYPOTHESIS

Aries was the first person to describe Jake's condition in these terms.

During an interview, he was asked whether Jake's behavior appeared deliberate.

Aries initially shook his head.

Then he reconsidered.

**ARIES:**  
"I don't think he's choosing to ignore us."

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"Why?"

**ARIES:**  
"Because he isn't ignoring us."

He paused.

**ARIES:**  
"He's just... not there."

The officer asked him to clarify.

Aries looked toward the observation window.

Jake was sitting in the lounge.

A resident had placed a blanket around his shoulders.

Jake had not adjusted it.

His teddy bear rested against his side.

He had not reached for it.

His eyes remained open.

**ARIES:**  
"It's like he turned something off."

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"What do you mean?"

Aries struggled to find the words.

**ARIES:**  
"His consciousness. Maybe."

He immediately looked uncomfortable with his own statement.

**ARIES:**  
"I don't mean he's dead. I know he's alive."

He looked back at Jake.

**ARIES:**  
"I mean the part of him that... wants things. Notices things. Decides things."

A long pause followed.

**ARIES:**  
"He's still sitting there. He's still breathing. He can still move if somebody moves him."

His voice became quieter.

**ARIES:**  
"But I can't tell if he's actually choosing to do any of it."

The officer did not immediately respond.

Aries continued.

**ARIES:**  
"Before, if he didn't want something, he'd tell me. Or he'd move. Or he'd grab my sleeve."

He looked down at his hands.

**ARIES:**  
"Now, I could sit beside him for an hour, and I don't know if he'd even care that I was there."

He stopped.

**ARIES:**  
"I don't think he stopped caring."

His eyes returned to the observation window.

**ARIES:**  
"I think he stopped being able to do anything with it."

# 2. AN IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

C.O.R.E. cannot confirm that Jake has voluntarily or involuntarily suspended his consciousness.

His current appearance does not establish that his awareness has disappeared.

A person may be awake without appearing emotionally engaged. Severe stress, dissociation, neurological impairment, exhaustion, and other conditions can produce different forms of reduced responsiveness.

Jake's anomalous physiology introduces additional uncertainty.

The Medical Division must therefore determine whether Jake's condition involves an alteration in alertness, awareness, memory, voluntary movement, or another neurological or physiological function.

The psychological team must separately assess the possibility of a severe stress response.

Neither team is authorized to assume that the other has already explained the behavior.

However, Aries's description identifies a particularly important concern.

**Jake appears to have lost much of his observable participation in his own life.**

Whether that reflects an altered state of consciousness, an inability to respond, or a withdrawal from overwhelming experiences remains unknown.

The distinction matters medically.

It does not make the situation less urgent.

---

# 3. WHAT HAPPENED BEFORE

Jake's current condition must be understood in the context of his history.

Aries was absent for years.

During that period, Jake experienced multiple frightening and destabilizing events.

He was vulnerable.

He was hurt.

He encountered situations in which his safety depended on other people.

Some people failed him.

Some made mistakes.

Some were unable to prevent circumstances beyond their control.

Jake understands this.

He has repeatedly demonstrated an ability to recognize that someone did not intend to hurt him.

That understanding has never guaranteed that he escaped the consequences.

When Aries returned, Jake finally had the opportunity to rebuild a relationship that had once been central to his life.

He embraced him.

He introduced him to the residents.

He called him his big brother.

He followed him.

He asked for uppies.

He fell asleep against him.

He began treating Aries as someone he could depend upon without having to explain himself.

Then Aries accidentally left him outside.

Five hours passed.

Jake remained in the snow.

He was eventually found shivering beneath a substantial accumulation of snow, unable or unwilling to speak.

He followed personnel indoors.

He collapsed several minutes later.

The incident was not the beginning of Jake's suffering.

It may have been another event added to a burden that was already too heavy.

C.O.R.E. cannot conclude that the incident alone caused his present condition.

But its timing, severity, and effect on a relationship that mattered deeply to Jake make it impossible to dismiss as irrelevant.

---

# 4. THE POSSIBILITY OF WITHDRAWAL

One hypothesis is that Jake has become so overwhelmed that he is no longer engaging with his surroundings in his usual manner.

If this is the case, his current passivity may not represent a conscious decision to stop caring.

It may instead reflect an inability to respond normally while under extreme distress.

The following observations support further investigation:

- Jake has stopped initiating most familiar interactions.
    
- He no longer reliably responds to people he previously sought out.
    
- He allows himself to be moved without expressing a preference.
    
- He has stopped showing several of his usual signs of irritation or discomfort.
    
- He does not reliably seek his teddy bear or other familiar sources of comfort.
    
- His expression and posture remain unusually static.
    
- The change occurred following a serious medical incident and in the context of an already concerning psychological history.
    

None of these observations proves that Jake has turned off his consciousness.

Together, they indicate a substantial departure from his established baseline.

The possibility that Jake's apparent withdrawal is a response to overwhelming distress must be investigated without assuming that it is the only explanation.

# 6. ARIES'S GUILT

Aries has begun interpreting Jake's withdrawal as a possible consequence of his own actions.

This is understandable.

It is also potentially dangerous if allowed to become the sole explanation for Jake's condition.

Aries has acknowledged that he made a serious mistake.

He has not attempted to deny responsibility for leaving Jake outside.

However, the Welfare Division has observed that he is increasingly focused on whether Jake's current state represents a loss of their relationship.

He repeatedly asks whether Jake recognizes him.

He asks whether Jake can hear him.

He asks whether Jake knows he is there.

He has begun treating every lack of response as possible evidence that Jake has withdrawn further.

The resulting distress has made it difficult for Aries to remain in the room without seeking some indication that Jake is responding.

He has been instructed to avoid turning visits into informal tests of Jake's affection.

Jake must not become responsible for demonstrating that Aries is forgiven.

Aries may sit beside him.

He may speak calmly.

He may offer familiar objects or ordinary companionship.

But he must not demand that Jake look at him, reach for him, or behave as he did before the incident.

The purpose of visiting is to provide Jake with a safe and predictable presence.

It is not to obtain proof that their relationship can be restored.

---

# 7. THE TEDDY BEAR

The teddy bear remains an important point of observation.

It is Jake's cardiac vessel.

Its heartbeat is Jake's actual heartbeat.

It is also one of the few objects that has remained consistently associated with Jake across his previous experiences.

Personnel initially expected Jake to hold it when he was distressed.

He has not consistently done so.

At times, it rests beside him without being touched.

At other times, he permits personnel to place it near him without reacting.

This change is concerning, but its meaning is uncertain.

Jake may still recognize the bear as familiar and important.

He may be too physically or psychologically unwell to reach for it.

He may be experiencing an altered state of awareness.

C.O.R.E. will not assume that his failure to reach for the bear means he no longer cares about it.

The bear must remain with him as medically appropriate.

Personnel must not repeatedly place it in his hands to provoke a response.

It is not a diagnostic instrument.

It is part of Jake's life.

---

# 8. WHAT THE SAFEHOUSE MUST NOT DO

Jake's current condition has produced understandable distress among residents.

People want him to respond.

They want him to speak.

They want him to look at them.

They want him to ask for something, complain about something, or demonstrate that the familiar little boy is still present.

These wishes are understandable.

They must not become demands.

Personnel are prohibited from:

- repeatedly calling Jake's name in an attempt to force engagement;
    
- shaking, poking, or startling him to obtain a reaction;
    
- moving him unnecessarily because he does not object;
    
- interpreting stillness as permission;
    
- demanding that he look at a familiar person;
    
- telling him to "come back" as though he is deliberately choosing to distress everyone;
    
- forcing physical affection;
    
- using his teddy bear to test his responsiveness;
    
- or treating a brief movement as proof that the problem has resolved.
    

If Jake appears unresponsive or his level of awareness changes, personnel must seek medical assessment rather than attempting to provoke him.

If he is medically stable, the environment should remain calm, predictable, and free of unnecessary demands.

He should continue to be offered ordinary choices.

He should not be forced to answer them.

The Welfare Division asked Aries what he believed Jake was experiencing.

Aries was silent for a long time.

**ARIES:**

**ARIES:**

**ARIES:**

Another pause.

**ARIES:**  
"He's been through so much."

His voice became quieter.

**ARIES:**

Aries rubbed his eyes.

**ARIES:**

He stopped.

**ARIES:**  
"But he still has to live through it."

Neither spoke for several seconds.

**ARIES:**  
"I think he's tired of having to understand."

The statement was not entered into the report as a clinical finding.

It was entered as an expression of concern from someone who knew Jake before the incident and had noticed how dramatically his behavior had changed.

The distinction is important.

Aries cannot diagnose his brother.

But he can recognize that the person sitting in front of him no longer behaves like the brother he remembers.

And that observation deserves to be taken seriously.

---

# 10. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The phrase _turned off his consciousness_ remains an unverified description.

Medical investigation must determine whether Jake's altered responsiveness reflects a neurological or physiological problem, a stress-related state, an anomalous effect, or a combination of factors.

Psychological assessment must proceed without presuming that Jake is deliberately refusing to participate.

The current priority is not to restore Jake's previous personality as quickly as possible.

It is to establish his medical safety, understand his present condition, and provide care without placing additional demands upon him.

If Jake is experiencing a severe stress response, he requires safety and support.

If he is experiencing a neurological or physiological complication, he requires appropriate treatment.

If the explanation involves both, both must be addressed.

C.O.R.E. cannot yet establish which possibility is correct.

It can establish that the change is serious.

**Jake's apparent absence from ordinary interaction must not be mistaken for an absence of need.**

# FINAL CLOSING NOTE

Aries remembers Jake as a little star who wanted everything from him.

His attention.

His company.

His arms.

His back.

Sometimes a piggyback ride across an entire corridor because walking was apparently an unreasonable request.

Jake would climb onto him without ceremony.

He would hold his sleeve.

He would settle against him and fall asleep.

He would sometimes say nothing at all, but Aries could still tell when he wanted to be close.

There was a kind of certainty in those little gestures.

Jake wanted him.

Jake trusted him.

Jake expected him to be there.

Then came the incident.

Now Aries can sit beside his brother for an hour and receive nothing that tells him whether Jake wants him there.

He can speak, and Jake may not answer.

He can offer his hand, and Jake may not take it.

He can promise to stay, and Jake may not visibly react.

Aries has begun to fear that his brother has gone somewhere he cannot follow.

C.O.R.E. cannot confirm that this is what has happened.

We cannot say that Jake has deliberately withdrawn his consciousness.

We cannot say that he has stopped feeling.

We cannot say that the boy Aries remembers is gone.

But we can say that Jake is not behaving as he did before, and that the change requires urgent attention.

Perhaps Jake is overwhelmed.

Perhaps he is physically unwell.

Perhaps his mind is protecting itself from experiences it has not been able to process.

Perhaps the answer is something else entirely.

We do not know yet.

And that uncertainty must be respected.

Jake does not need everyone to pull him back into the world by force.

He needs people to make sure the world is safe enough for him to participate in it again.

He needs medical care that does not mistake silence for stability.

He needs companionship that does not demand a response.

He needs choices that remain his, even when he cannot express them.

And Aries needs to understand that he cannot repair his brother by making Jake prove that he is still there.

For now, Aries sits beside him.

Sometimes he talks.

Sometimes he says nothing.

He tells Jake when he is leaving.

He tells him when he will return.

He keeps his promises whenever he can.

He does not demand a hug.

He does not demand forgiveness.

He does not ask Jake to reassure him that he is still his big brother.

He simply remains someone who can be found.

Whether Jake can fully register that presence remains uncertain.

But the responsibility to offer it does not depend on whether he can acknowledge it.

**Jake may have withdrawn from the world around him.**

**The world around him must not withdraw from Jake.**
