# 06:47 — WAKE-UP

Jane wakes up.

She stares at the ceiling.

She checks the time.

She considers going back to sleep.

She doesn't.

Jane gets out of bed, puts on her clothes, checks that her equipment is where she left it, and leaves her room.

Her tail trails behind her.

She walks through the SAFEHOUSE hallway.

A newly hired Blacksite employee turns the corner.

They see Jane.

Jane sees them.

The employee freezes.

Jane keeps walking.

Three seconds later, she stops.

Turns around.

Walks back.

**JANE:**  
"Morning."

The employee nearly jumps.

**JANE:**  
"...You okay?"

**EMPLOYEE:**  
"Y-yes."

Jane smiles.

**JANE:**  
"Good."

She walks away.

The employee remains standing there for another ten seconds.

---

# 07:15 — BREAKFAST

Jane enters the cafeteria.

She has one objective.

Food.

She looks at the breakfast selection.

She looks at the leftovers.

She looks at the leftovers again.

She takes them.

A cafeteria worker watches.

**WORKER:**  
"Those are from yesterday."

**JANE:**  
"I know."

**WORKER:**  
"You want them?"

**JANE:**  
"Yes."

**WORKER:**  
"...Why?"

Jane looks at him as though he has asked the stupidest question imaginable.

**JANE:**  
"They're food."

---

# 07:42 — NERI CHECK

Jane passes through the SAFEHOUSE residential area.

She checks whether Neri is awake.

Neri is sitting somewhere quietly reading.

Jane stops.

**JANE:**  
"Morning, My Cute Seal."

Neri looks up.

**NERI:**  
"Don't call me that."

**JANE:**  
"Good morning to you too."

Jane continues walking.

Neri watches her leave.

Then quietly smiles.

---

# 08:30 — WORK

Jane enters her office.

The playful SAFEHOUSE resident disappears.

The intelligence operative takes over.

Reports are reviewed.

Information is cross-referenced.

Personnel movements are checked.

Woodland Coorp intelligence is examined.

Old infiltration records are updated.

Jane is extremely competent at her job.

She spends several hours doing what she spent years doing inside Woodland:

**Observe.**

**Understand.**

**Remember.**

**Connect the pieces.**

Nothing about her work requires her to raise her voice.

That is part of what makes her good at it.

# 10:56 — INTERRUPTION

Someone knocks.

Jane doesn't look up.

**JANE:**  
"Come in."

The door opens.

Neri stands there.

**NERI:**  
"Are you busy?"

Jane stops typing.

She looks at Neri.

**JANE:**  
"Yes."

Neri immediately begins backing away.

**NERI:**  
"Oh. Okay."

Jane sighs.

**JANE:**  
"That doesn't mean leave."

Neri stops.

**JANE:**  
"Come in."

Neri enters.

**JANE:**  
"What do you need?"

**NERI:**  
"...Nothing."

**JANE:**  
"Then why are you here?"

Neri shrugs.

Jane looks at her for a moment.

**JANE:**  
"You can sit here."

Neri sits.

Jane returns to work.

Neither speaks.

For the next twenty minutes, Neri reads while Jane works.

Jane doesn't mind.

In fact, she seems happier.

# 14:00 — INTELLIGENCE WORK

Jane returns to her office.

Another report.

Another security review.

Another set of information requiring analysis.

Her Woodland experience makes her particularly good at identifying inconsistencies.

Something doesn't match.

Jane notices.

She checks it again.

Then again.

She eventually finds the mistake.

It is not sabotage.

It is an employee entering the wrong date.

Jane fixes the report.

She sends a message explaining the error.

No drama.

No interrogation.

Just:

> **"Check your dates before submitting these."**

Jane leaves her office.

**JANE:**  
"...You know I can see you, right?"

**EMPLOYEE:**  
"...Yes."

**JANE:**  
"Good."

# 23:02 — LIGHTS OUT

Jane returns to her room.

She sits down.

Her tail curls around the side of the chair.

For a moment, she thinks about her old life.

Woodland.

Five years undercover.

The constant observation.

The constant pretending.

The knowledge that one mistake could expose everything.

Then she looks around.

This room is hers.

The SAFEHOUSE is hers.

People know who she is.

Nobody here needs her to pretend.

Jane lies down.

She closes her eyes.

# 23:47 — FINAL ENTRY

Jane is asleep.

No mission.

No infiltration.

No interrogation.

No intelligence operation.

Just sleep.

Tomorrow will probably be similar.

She'll work.

She'll eat.

She'll scare somebody.

She'll bother Neri.

Neri will probably bother her back.

She'll make cheese sauce and insist that this makes her a chef.

And somewhere in the Blacksite, someone will inevitably discover Jane standing silently behind them.

Jane will smile.

And they will probably scream.
