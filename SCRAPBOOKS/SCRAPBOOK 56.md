# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **CIVILIAN LIAISON ADDENDUM**

### **TRUSTED VISITORS – ROSTER UPDATE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The identities of the five Trusted Visitors have been partially declassified for internal personnel.

Although they continue to travel as wandering merchants, they are now recognized throughout Heaven's Blacksite as familiar, welcome faces.

The group consists of:

- **Three adult men**
    
- **One adult woman**
    
- **One young boy**, the youngest member of the caravan
    

None of the five are affiliated with any government, military organization, or research institution.

They are ordinary civilians whose trust was earned through a single act of compassion.

---

# **GROUP DYNAMICS**

### **The Three Men**

The three men handle most of the caravan's heavy work.

Their responsibilities include:

- Driving wagons.
    
- Trading goods.
    
- Hunting.
    
- Repairing equipment.
    
- Protecting the caravan during travel.
    

All three have become particularly fond of Arcturus, often seeking his advice on navigation, camping, and firearms maintenance.

---

### **The Woman**

The sole woman in the caravan serves as the group's organizer.

She manages:

- Trade negotiations.
    
- Supplies.
    
- Medical necessities.
    
- Food preparation.
    
- Route planning.
    

Norma and Belle frequently exchange recipes with her whenever she visits.

She has also become one of the few civilians allowed to observe portions of the CCD's non-classified workshops.

---

### **The Young Boy**

The youngest member of the caravan is curious about absolutely everything.

During his visits, he can usually be found asking questions such as:

> "Why is Belle so soft?"

> "How does Miss Neko's giant staff work?"

> "Can horses understand people?"

> "Does Neko ever sleep?"

The answer to the last question remains under investigation.

---

# **RELATIONSHIPS WITH SAFEHOUSE**

### **Belle**

The young boy immediately attached himself to Belle.

Belle didn't mind.

In fact...

She often lets him sit beside her while eating lunch.

She also makes sure he leaves with a bag of carrots and homemade snacks for the road.

---

### **Endmin**

She naturally slips into an older-sister role whenever the caravan visits, ensuring the boy stays away from hazardous areas of the Blacksite.

---

### **Norma**

Norma enjoys answering his engineering questions—provided they're age-appropriate.

If the conversation becomes too technical, she simplifies it until he understands.

---

### **Arcturus**

The boy absolutely idolizes Arcturus.

Whenever the Gunslinger mounts a horse, the boy's eyes light up.

On one visit, Arcturus lifted him into the saddle under careful supervision and slowly led the horse around the training grounds.

The boy later declared:

> "I'm gonna be a cowboy someday!"

Arcturus smiled beneath his fedora.

> "Then start by learning to take care of the horse."

---

### **Neko**

Neko quickly became the boy's favorite inventor.

Much to Miss Neko's relief, Neko has learned to demonstrate only his **safe** inventions during these visits.

---

### **Star**

Star and the boy inevitably end up playing games together.

Usually ending with both of them laughing uncontrollably.

---

### **Nightshade**

The boy was initially intimidated by Nightshade's quiet demeanor.

She eventually offered him a small Night Blossom she'd grown herself.

He still keeps it pressed inside a book.

---

### **Taiga**

Taiga taught him how to identify animal tracks.

He now proudly announces every footprint he finds.

Whether he's correct is another matter.

---

### **Mart**

Mart introduced him to the Blacksite library.

The boy now insists on borrowing a book every visit.

---

### **Frost**

Frost always prepares hot chocolate during the colder months.

The boy now associates visiting the Blacksite with warm drinks and warm welcomes.

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' REMARK**

During one of the caravan's departures, Director Lucious watched them prepare for the road.

The young boy waved enthusiastically toward the Blacksite.

Lucious smiled.

> "He'll remember this place."

Endmin nodded.

> "Hopefully for the people."

Belle added:

> "And the hugs."

---

# **STATUS**

The five Trusted Visitors remain the only civilian caravan with recurring access to Heaven's Blacksite.

What began as an emergency rescue has grown into a lasting friendship.

Among Blacksite personnel, they are no longer referred to as "the rescued traders."

They're simply known as:

> **"Our traders."**
