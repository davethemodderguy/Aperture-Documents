# INCIDENT REPORT

## MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT — “THE SNAP”

**CLASSIFICATION:** C.O.R.E. INTERNAL — ARCHIVAL  
**ACCESS:** ARCHIVIST / EXECUTIVE  
**STATUS:** CLOSED — HOSTILE RESPONSE  
**LOCATION:** SPAWN HEADQUARTERS  
**ENTITY:** MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT  
**ENTITY CLASS:** ARCHITECT OF REALITY  
**C.O.R.E. OBSERVER:** Neko

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Mischevious Light returned to SPAWN Headquarters.

The previous visit had been classified as **non-hostile mischief**.

This visit was not.

At approximately 19:42, a SPAWN officer attempted to physically tackle Mischevious Light.

The attempt lasted approximately **less than one second.**

Mischevious Light responded with a single snap of his fingers.

The officer was immediately **pulverized into red glowing particulate matter.**

No conventional weapon was used.

No visible ritual was performed.

No projectile was fired.

No physical struggle occurred.

The officer simply ceased to exist as a coherent physical body.

Mischevious Light then reportedly looked around the room and said:

> **“I did warn you.”**

SPAWN morale has subsequently fallen to what C.O.R.E. considers **dangerously low levels.**

---

# 2. PREVIOUS CONTEXT

SPAWN had already been exposed to the following facts:

- Guiding Light can ignore their containment rituals.
    
- Curious Light can ignore their containment rituals.
    
- Mischevious Light can casually manipulate their facility.
    
- The Architects possess capabilities vastly beyond SPAWN's containment systems.
    
- The Architects had previously demonstrated considerable restraint.
    

Unfortunately, one officer apparently interpreted this restraint as weakness.

This interpretation was incorrect.

---

# 3. THE INCIDENT

At 19:42:11, Mischevious Light manifested in the western administrative corridor.

There was no breach.

There was no portal.

He simply appeared.

A security officer approached him.

> **SPAWN OFFICER:**  
> “You again.”

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Me again.”

The officer reportedly appeared increasingly agitated.

> **SPAWN OFFICER:**  
> “You are not authorized to be here.”

Mischevious Light looked around.

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Neither are half the things you've summoned.”

The officer moved toward him.

A nearby colleague reportedly said:

> **“Don't.”**

The officer ignored him.

---

# 4. THE TACKLE

At 19:42:17, the officer attempted to tackle Mischevious Light.

The attempt did not connect.

Mischevious Light remained standing.

He looked down at the officer.

Then up.

Then sighed.

He snapped his fingers.

**SNAP.**

The officer's body immediately disintegrated into a cloud of **red glowing dust.**

The particulate matter remained suspended in the air for approximately 4.2 seconds before dispersing.

No blood was observed.

No conventional remains were recovered.

The surrounding environment sustained **zero physical damage.**

Mischevious Light had evidently selected an effect precise enough to destroy exactly one individual.

---

# 5. SILENCE

The corridor became completely silent.

One officer dropped his weapon.

Another slowly stepped backward.

A third reportedly whispered:

> **“He didn't even touch him.”**

Mischevious Light looked at the remaining personnel.

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “I really didn't want to do that.”

Nobody moved.

He continued:

> **“I was having fun.”**

Another long silence.

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Don't ruin it.”

---

# 6. C.O.R.E. MONITORING

Neko was watching.

There was initially no laughter.

According to the audio recording, Neko remained silent for approximately six seconds.

Then:

> **Neko:**  
> “Oh.”

Pause.

> **Neko:**  
> “Okay.”

Another pause.

> **Neko:**  
> “They really did it.”

Neko then reportedly began laughing.

Not because of the officer's death.

Because of the sheer absurdity of SPAWN having been repeatedly warned that the Architects were not entities they could physically confront.

His final handwritten observation was:

> **“THEY WERE TOLD.”**

---

# 7. SPAWN RESPONSE

SPAWN Headquarters entered an immediate internal lockdown.

Not because Mischevious Light had ordered one.

Because nobody knew what else to do.

Security personnel were instructed to:

- Lower weapons.
    
- Avoid approaching Mischevious Light.
    
- Avoid physical contact.
    
- Avoid activating containment rituals.
    
- Avoid challenging him.
    
- Avoid “testing” defensive measures.
    
- Avoid saying anything that could be interpreted as a threat.
    
- **Stop trying to fight the Architects.**
    

One particularly distressed officer reportedly asked:

> **“What are we supposed to do if he attacks us?”**

The response from High Command:

> **“Don't give him a reason.”**

---

# 8. MORALE ASSESSMENT

SPAWN morale is now considered **dangerously low.**

The primary cause is not the casualty itself.

It is the realization that the casualty was apparently unnecessary.

Mischevious Light had previously spent an extended period inside Headquarters without harming anyone.

He had joked with personnel.

He had altered signs.

He had played with their security systems.

He had turned ritual circles into flowers.

He had made vending machines dispense tea.

He had essentially treated SPAWN Headquarters as a playground.

SPAWN personnel had therefore gradually begun to interpret his behavior as evidence that he was harmless.

The tackle disproved this assumption instantly.

# 10. C.O.R.E. ANALYSIS

The incident provides useful clarification regarding Mischevious Light's previous behavior.

His prior harmless behavior should not be interpreted as evidence of limited capability.

It was evidence of **restraint.**

The distinction is critical.

Mischevious Light did not escalate gradually.

He did not require preparation.

He did not require a ritual.

He did not require a weapon.

He did not require physical contact.

He simply decided that the officer should no longer possess a physical form.

And reality complied.

No fatigue.

No injury.

No visible concentration.

No recovery period.

No apparent cooldown.
