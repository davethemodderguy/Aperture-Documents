### MSAC / RESIDENT WELFARE & SECURITY DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-MSAC-4C-NORA-321

**STATUS:** ACTIVE REVIEW  
**SUBJECT:** Nora Tachyon — Night Stalker Qualification  
**RELATED ITEM:** Tachyon Industries Night Stalker Series

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon has formally demonstrated an unusual level of seriousness, discipline, and responsibility regarding firearms.

This observation is significant.

Nora is six years old.

She is also the same child who recently chased an MSAC Scout through the Blacksite with Alice's battle axe after he stole her training replica.

Those two facts should not be confused.

Nora's behavior around firearms is markedly different from her behavior around almost everything else.

When presented with a firearm, Nora does not treat it as a toy.

She does not wave it around.

She does not play pretend.

She does not make jokes.

She listens.

She asks questions.

She follows instructions.

She waits for authorization.

And she takes every safety warning seriously.

---

# 2. QUALIFICATION

Following the completion of controlled evaluations, Neri requested that Nora be considered for a genuine Tachyon Industries Night Stalker.

The request was initially rejected.

Neri rejected it again.

Then again.

Nora did not argue.

Instead, she continued participating in supervised training with NS-T-001.

Her conduct remained consistent.

She demonstrated patience, restraint, attention to instruction, and an unusually strong understanding that a firearm is not a toy.

C.O.R.E. subsequently approved a highly restricted family-authorization arrangement.

The resulting Night Stalker remains under **adult-controlled storage and authorization protocols**.

Nora does not possess unrestricted access.

---

# 3. NORA'S ATTITUDE

The most notable aspect of the evaluation was not Nora's accuracy.

It was her attitude.

During one briefing, an MSAC instructor told her:

> **"This isn't a toy."**

Nora immediately replied:

> **"I know."**

The instructor continued:

> **"Then what is it?"**

Nora answered:

> **"Something dangerous."**

Pause.

> **"So I have to be careful."**

The instructor confirmed the answer.

Nora nodded.

No further explanation was required.

---

# 4. THE RULE

Neri personally established one rule above all others.

> **"If you don't know, stop."**

Nora asked:

> **"And ask?"**

> **NERI:**  
> "Yes."

> **NORA:**  
> "Okay."

That rule has remained intact throughout every supervised session.

---

# 5. THE NIGHT STALKER

The authorized unit was not counted as part of the original ten-unit Limited Merchandise production series.

It is a separate family-authorized unit.

Its existence does not alter the historical production count.

The weapon bears the **TACHYON INDUSTRIES** identity and was personally prepared by Neri.

Neri reportedly spent considerably longer preparing this unit than she normally does.

When asked why:

> **NERI:**  
> "Because it's hers."

---

# 6. NORA'S RESPONSE

Nora was presented with the case.

She opened it.

She immediately stopped smiling.

She stared at the Night Stalker for several seconds.

Then:

> **NORA:**  
> "...Really?"

Neri nodded.

Nora looked at her.

> **NORA:**  
> "Mine?"

> **NERI:**  
> "Yours."

Nora did not immediately touch it.

Instead, she asked:

> **NORA:**  
> "Can I?"

Neri nodded.

Only then did Nora proceed.

The observing personnel noted the contrast.

The same child who routinely climbs onto Neri's back without warning, builds blanket forts with Alice, and bites Neri's seal tail had become completely still.

Focused.

Quiet.

Serious.

---

# 7. MSAC OBSERVATION

An MSAC operator who witnessed the presentation later remarked:

> **"I've never seen her that serious."**

Another operator replied:

> **"She knows what it is."**

The first operator nodded.

> **"Yeah."**

Pause.

> **"That's what makes it weird."**

---

# 8. THE SCOUT

The MSAC Scout previously responsible for stealing NS-T-001 was informed that Nora had received a family-authorized Night Stalker.

He stared at the notification.

Then asked:

> **"The same Nora?"**

The officer confirmed.

The Scout slowly put his face in his hands.

> **"I stole her toy."**

A nearby operator replied:

> **"You did."**

> **"And then she chased me."**

> **"She did."**

> **"With an axe."**

> **"Correct."**

Long pause.

> **"And now she has an actual one?"**

The operator nodded.

The Scout looked toward the ceiling.

> **"...I am never living this down."**

He was correct.

---

# 9. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Nora's exceptional marksmanship ability was **not** the sole reason for authorization.

Skill does not automatically constitute authorization.

Her authorization depends on:

- demonstrated responsibility;
    
- consistent supervised conduct;
    
- adherence to safety requirements;
    
- adult oversight;
    
- restricted storage;
    
- continued evaluation;
    
- and Neri's explicit family authorization.
    

Nora remains a child.

She is not an MSAC operator.

She is not cleared for combat deployment.

She is not permitted to independently carry the weapon throughout the Blacksite.

The Night Stalker is not a toy.

---

# 10. NERI'S FINAL STATEMENT

Neri was asked whether she was comfortable giving her six-year-old sister a real Night Stalker.

She remained silent for several seconds.

Then:

> **NERI:**  
> "No."

The interviewer looked surprised.

> **NERI:**  
> "I'm not comfortable."

Pause.

> **NERI:**  
> "But I trust her."

Another pause.

> **NERI:**  
> "That's different."

---

# 11. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Nora's behavior demonstrates an unusual separation between **playfulness** and **serious responsibility**.

Around Alice:

She is loud.

Energetic.

Chaotic.

Frequently covered in blankets.

Around Neri:

She is affectionate.

Curious.

Occasionally bites her tail.

Around firearms:

She becomes quiet.

Attentive.

Deliberate.

Serious.

Personnel are instructed not to interpret her age as evidence that she does not understand the seriousness of the equipment.

Conversely, personnel are also instructed not to interpret her demonstrated skill as evidence that she should be treated like an adult operator.

Both observations are true.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Nora's first reaction to receiving a real Night Stalker was not:

> **"COOL!"**

It was:

> **"...Really?"**

Then:

> **"Mine?"**

Neri said yes.

Nora looked at the weapon.

Then at her sister.

And, for perhaps the first time since arriving at SAFEHOUSE, she seemed to fully understand the significance of being trusted with something that her sister had made.

She did not brag.

She did not run around showing everyone.

She simply nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> "I'll be careful."

Neri quietly replied:

> **"I know."**
