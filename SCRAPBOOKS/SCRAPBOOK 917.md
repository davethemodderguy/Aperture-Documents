**EQUIPMENT CLASS:** Specialized Archery Ammunition  
**PRIMARY WEAPON:** Mechanical Compound Magazine-Fed Crossbow

**SUPERVISION:** REQUIRED FOR EXPERIMENTAL TYPES

---

# 1. Overview

Following her adoption of archery and subsequent acquisition of a mechanical compound magazine-fed crossbow, Neri began developing specialized ammunition.

This development was, predictably, heavily influenced by her exceptional understanding of chemistry.

Neri does not consider arrows to be merely projectiles.

She considers them:

> **"Chemistry with a pointy end."**

C.O.R.E. currently recognizes several specialized arrow types.

Some are conventional.

Some are engineered.

Some are anomalous.

And at least one exists because Neri accidentally created something extremely dangerous and then **forgot to document the formula.**

# 3. Normal Arrow

The simplest member of Neri's inventory.

A conventional arrow.

No anomalous properties.

No chemical payload.

No unexpected behavior.

It is used for ordinary archery practice and situations where Neri does not need anything more complicated.

Neri reportedly considers these:

> **"Boring."**

Star disagrees.

Nightshade strongly agrees with Star.

---

# 4. Nekonium-Tipped Arrow

A specialized arrow incorporating a **Nekonium tip**.

The purpose is primarily to provide a projectile capable of interacting with targets that conventional arrowheads cannot reliably penetrate or damage.

The arrows are manufactured under controlled conditions.

Neri's molecular restructuring ability makes material experimentation particularly easy for her, although she is prohibited from producing Nekonium ammunition without authorization.

Neko has specifically emphasized:

> **"Nekonium is expensive."**

Neri's response:

> **"I'll make more."**

Neko:

> **"...That's not the point."**

---

# 5. Rocket-Propelled Arrow

A mechanically assisted projectile incorporating a small rocket propulsion system.

The weapon exists because Neri apparently decided that an arrow should have **more speed.**

The propulsion system provides additional acceleration after launch.

Because the projectile contains an active propulsion mechanism, it is classified as restricted ammunition.

Neri is permitted to use it only under appropriate supervision and in designated testing environments.

---

# 6. Hydrochloric Acid Payload Thermobaric Arrow

**Classification: HIGHLY RESTRICTED**

This arrow combines a chemical payload with a thermobaric-style effect.

The payload involves hydrochloric acid and is designed to produce a destructive chemical/pressure effect upon activation.

Due to the hazards associated with corrosive chemicals and explosive effects, C.O.R.E. does not permit Neri to freely manufacture or modify this ammunition.

The exact construction and formulation are maintained in restricted technical records.

Neri understands the chemistry involved.

That is precisely why she is not allowed to improvise with it.

---

# 7. Explosive Arrow

A conventional explosive projectile adapted into arrow form.

Unlike Neri's more unusual ammunition, this one requires little explanation.

It explodes.

Neri likes it.

Neko does not particularly like the phrase:

> **"Neri's explosive arrows."**

Mostly because it makes paperwork considerably more complicated.

---

# 8. Trirrow

The **Trirrow** is one of Neri's more unusual designs.

The projectile incorporates multiple arrow structures into a single firing unit, allowing it to function as a specialized multi-projectile arrow.

The exact configuration is maintained in the equipment registry rather than being treated as a conventional arrow.

Neri named it.

Nobody knows why.

Neri refuses to elaborate.

> **"It's a Trirrow."**

And apparently that is sufficient documentation.

---

# 9. White-Flame Arrow

## STATUS: UNKNOWN

This is the most concerning arrow in Neri's inventory.

The arrow contains a chemical compound accidentally created by Neri during one of her experiments.

The problem is simple:

**Neri forgot to document what she made.**

She remembers creating the compound.

She remembers observing its unusual properties.

She does not have a complete record of its composition.

The resulting compound can produce an unusual **white flame** when applied to a target.

---

# 10. Unusual Combustion Behavior

The flame does not behave like ordinary fire.

The most unusual characteristic is its interaction with water.

Ordinary fire is generally suppressed by the addition of water.

The white flame does the opposite.

### More water produces a stronger reaction.

The flame becomes increasingly intense as additional water comes into contact with the affected material.

This makes conventional firefighting procedures inappropriate.

Personnel are specifically instructed:

> **DO NOT ASSUME WATER WILL EXTINGUISH THE WHITE FLAME.**

The precise mechanism is unknown.

---

# 11. Neri's Reaction

When asked how she created the compound, Neri reportedly stared at the sample for several seconds.

Then:

> **"I don't remember."**

The research team asked whether she had written down the formula.

> **"Probably."**

The relevant documentation was searched.

Nothing was found.

Neri eventually admitted:

> **"I forgot to document it."**

There was a prolonged silence.

---

# 12. Neko's Response

Neko was reportedly extremely unhappy.

Not because Neri had experimented.

Neri is already authorized to conduct supervised chemistry work.

The problem was that she had accidentally created a previously undocumented compound with highly unusual combustion characteristics and then failed to record its composition.

Neko's response was reportedly:

> **"YOU MADE AN UNKNOWN FIRE CHEMICAL AND DIDN'T WRITE IT DOWN?"**

Neri:

> **"...I was busy."**

Neko:

> **"WITH WHAT?"**

Neri:

> **"Chemistry."**

---

# 13. Guiding Light and Miss Neko

The discovery also became another point of contention regarding Neri's growing arsenal.

Guiding Light was already concerned about Neri receiving advanced ranged weaponry.

Miss Neko was already concerned about Neri possessing:

- numerous concealed knives,
    
- a Starlight katana,
    
- a bow,
    
- a mechanical magazine-fed crossbow,
    
- molecular material manipulation,
    
- and an apparent inability to recognize when she has made something sufficiently dangerous to warrant documentation.
    

Neither was reassured by the addition of a mysterious white-flame projectile.

---

# 14. Current Restrictions

Neri is permitted to maintain conventional arrows and approved ammunition.

Experimental ammunition requires authorization.

Unknown chemical compounds are **not** to be incorporated into ammunition until properly identified and documented.

The White-Flame Arrow is currently isolated pending characterization.

No personnel are to conduct water-based suppression tests.

No personnel are to reproduce the compound independently.

And most importantly:

**Neri is to document her chemistry experiments.**

---

# 15. Final Assessment

Neri's archery has evolved considerably since Star and Nightshade first taught her that melee combat cannot solve every problem.

She now possesses a projectile arsenal ranging from completely ordinary to extremely questionable.

Her exceptional chemistry knowledge has made her particularly capable of developing unusual ammunition.

Her anomalous molecular manipulation ability makes that capability even more significant.

The combination has produced one unfortunate reality:

> **Neri can invent ammunition faster than C.O.R.E. can write the paperwork for it.**

And somewhere in the laboratory is presumably a chemical compound that Neri created, forgot to document, and may eventually remember how to make.

For everyone's sake, Neri has been instructed to start writing things down.

**She has promised to try.**
