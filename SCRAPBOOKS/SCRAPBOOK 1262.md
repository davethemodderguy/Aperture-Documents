# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following the Banlands Harbinger incident involving Nora Tachyon, C.O.R.E. has completed additional analysis of the location where Invictus Aeterna defeated the Harbinger.

The area contains a permanent anomalous mark.

A single, perfectly straight line extends across the battlefield.

The line does not resemble:

- a physical cut;
    
- an impact crater;
    
- an energy discharge;
    
- spatial damage;
    
- or conventional weapon damage.
    

It is a **residual phenomenon.**

The mark indicates one fact:

> **Aeterna's sword was used at this location.**

---

# 1. THE PERMANENT MARK

The line measures several meters in length.

No material degradation has occurred around it.

The surrounding structure remains intact.

However, the marked section exhibits unusual properties:

- no natural repair;
    
- no regeneration;
    
- no reality correction;
    
- no material restoration;
    
- no anomalous healing.
    

Attempts to repair the mark have failed.

The damage is not physical.

It is a record.

The environment itself remembers the action.

---

# 2. THE INVlCTUS BLADE

Initial reports incorrectly classified Aeterna's sword as an execution weapon.

This description is incomplete.

The sword does not execute in the conventional meaning.

It does not simply destroy.

It performs a process C.O.R.E. has classified as:

> **Existential Repurposing.**

---

# 3. EXISTENTIAL REPURPOSING

Everything that exists possesses a purpose.

Not necessarily a conscious purpose.

Rather:

A function.

A reason.

A place within reality.

Aeterna's sword interacts with this fundamental property.

When the sword strikes a target, it does not merely remove the target.

It converts the target's existence into energy.

The purpose of the target's existence is redirected.

The remaining energy becomes part of the sword.

In simplified terms:

> **The sword does not destroy existence.**

> **It changes what that existence becomes.**

---

# 4. EFFECT ON THE HARBINGER

The Banlands Harbinger possessed immense resistance.

Normal weapons were ineffective.

Reality-based attacks failed.

Containment failed.

The Harbinger survived because its existence was built around chaos.

Aeterna's sword did not attempt to overpower that chaos.

It repurposed it.

The Harbinger's accumulated destructive purpose was converted into energy.

The energy was absorbed.

The result:

- complete disappearance of the entity;
    
- no resurrection;
    
- no remaining Banlands core;
    
- no residual consciousness;
    
- permanent strengthening of the Invictus blade.
    

---

# 5. WHY THE MARK REMAINS

The battlefield mark is not damage left behind.

It is evidence of conversion.

The location contains a small amount of residual sword energy.

The line represents the exact path where Aeterna redirected the Harbinger's existence.

The environment was not cut.

The environment was altered by witnessing the conversion.

---

# 6. THE LAST INVICTUS

Historical investigation has provided additional context regarding Aeterna's identity.

Aeterna was not simply a powerful ancient spirit.

She was one of the original:

> **Five Invictus.**

The Invictus were artificial beings forged from the Banlands during the ancient Banlands–Starlands war.

They served as:

- primary combatants;
    
- executors of Banlands authority;
    
- battlefield entities;
    
- weapons against Starlands forces.
    

The war caused catastrophic casualties on both sides.

The Invictus were among the primary forces responsible.

---

# 7. THE STARLANDS REVOLT

Following the Starlands revolt, the Invictus were eventually destroyed.

One by one.

The others fell.

Their names were lost.

Their purposes ended.

Their existence was consumed by the conflicts they were created to fight.

Only one remained.

> **Invictus Aeterna.**

---

# 8. AETERNA'S CHANGE

Unlike the other Invictus, Aeterna changed.

She retained her memories.

Her abilities.

Her power.

But not her original purpose.

The other Invictus continued following the role they were created for.

Aeterna rejected it.

She became something different.

A guardian.

A protector.

A caretaker.

A motherly figure.

Not because she was forced.

Because she chose to.

---

# 10. THE SWORD'S CURRENT STATUS

The Invictus sword remains bonded to Aeterna.

No other individual has successfully wielded it.

Nora Tachyon can interact with Aeterna's equipment but does not possess control over the blade.

When separated from Aeterna, the sword remains inactive.

It does not respond to force.

It does not respond to authority.

It responds only to its owner.

---

# 11. RELATIONSHIP WITH NORA

The contrast between Aeterna's origin and current role is considered significant.

A weapon created for war now protects:

A child.

A family.

A home.

A future.

The same entity that once converted enemies into energy now spends most of its time:

- carrying Nora;
    
- reminding her to eat;
    
- helping with the Merch Store;
    
- allowing Nora to add cat ears to her armor;
    
- standing beside her during nightmares.
    

C.O.R.E. notes the contradiction.

It is not a weakness.

It is evidence of change.

---

# FINAL ASSESSMENT

The line left behind after the Harbinger battle is not a scar.

It is a reminder.

A reminder that:

- the Harbinger existed;
    
- Aeterna fought;
    
- the Invictus power remains;
    
- and the last Invictus still walks among the living.
    

However, the most important fact is not what the sword did.

It is what Aeterna did afterward.

The other Invictus were remembered as weapons.

Aeterna chose to be something else.
