## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A clarification has been entered into the SAFEHOUSE resident records concerning the biological characteristics of Sora and Mia.

Although the two children are siblings by their own mutually accepted designation, their physical characteristics are different.

- **Sora:** A normal puppy. He does not possess feline traits.
    
- **Mia:** A Nekomata, possessing two feline ears and two tails.
    

Mia has developed a habit of concealing her Nekomata characteristics.

Her ears are usually hidden beneath her thick white hair, while her two tails are tucked beneath her clothing.

Consequently, personnel unfamiliar with her may initially mistake her for an ordinary human-looking child.

An amusing incident involving an affectionate head pat recently exposed both characteristics.

The incident was harmless, but it demonstrated that Mia's concealment is habitual enough that even familiar residents occasionally forget about her tails.

---

## 2. OBSERVATION — THE HIDDEN EARS

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE common room  
**Time:** 10:24

Mia was sitting beside Sora.

Her thick white hair covered the sides and top of her head, concealing her feline ears.

Guiding Light approached the two children.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Good morning, you two!

**SORA:** Morning!

**MIA:** Good morning.

Guiding Light smiled and gently patted Mia's head.

There was a small movement beneath her hair.

One feline ear briefly emerged.

Then the second.

Mia immediately looked up.

**MIA:** Ah—

Guiding Light paused.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Oh! There they are.

Mia's ears flattened slightly.

**MIA:** You exposed them.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** I'm sorry, sweetheart.

Guiding Light gently smoothed her hair, although the ears remained visible.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** They're very pretty.

Mia looked away.

**MIA:** I know.

Sora looked at her.

**SORA:** Your ears.

**MIA:** Yes, Sora.

**SORA:** They're out.

**MIA:** I noticed.

Jake, who had been sitting nearby, immediately joined in.

**JAKE:** CAT EARS!

**MIA:** Jake.

**JAKE:** CAT EARS!

**MIA:** I know!

Sora began laughing.

Mia sighed, although she was visibly amused.

## 3. OBSERVATION — THE TWO TAILS

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE common room  
**Time:** 10:31

The situation became more noticeable when Mia stood up.

Her clothing had concealed both tails, but the movement caused one to slip free.

She immediately attempted to tuck it back.

Unfortunately, the second tail emerged as well.

Jake noticed.

**JAKE:** WAIT!

Mia froze.

**JAKE:** TWO!

**MIA:** I know!

**JAKE:** YOU HAVE TWO!

**MIA:** I'VE ALWAYS HAD TWO!

Sora looked between the tails.

**SORA:** Nekomata.

Mia nodded.

**MIA:** Yes.

**SORA:** Pretty.

Mia's expression softened.

**MIA:** Thank you.

Jake approached one tail curiously.

**JAKE:** Can I touch it?

**MIA:** Ask first.

**JAKE:** Can I?

Mia considered it.

**MIA:** Just gently.

Jake carefully touched the tip.

The tail moved away almost immediately.

**JAKE:** IT MOVED!

**MIA:** Jake!

**JAKE:** Sorry!

Guiding Light laughed quietly.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** I think your tails have had enough attention for one morning.

Mia tucked them back beneath her clothing.

**MIA:** Thank you.

## 4. WHY DOES MIA HIDE THEM?

C.O.R.E. has not established a definitive psychological explanation for Mia's habit of concealing her Nekomata characteristics.

It is currently treated as a personal preference.

Personnel are reminded that concealment does not indicate that Mia is ashamed of her biology, nor does it mean that she wishes to be questioned about it.

Her ears and tails are simply parts of her body.

She should be allowed to decide when they are visible.

### Staff guidance

- Do not deliberately expose her ears by pulling or rearranging her hair.
    
- Do not attempt to remove or lift clothing to inspect her tails.
    
- Ask before touching either her ears or tails.
    
- Gentle head pats are permitted when she welcomes them, but staff should understand that these may naturally disturb her hair and expose her ears.
    
- Do not make repeated comments about her appearance after she has indicated that she wants the attention to stop.

## 5. A SMALL SIBLING EXCHANGE

Later that afternoon, Sora was sitting with Mia inside the common room.

Her ears were visible, and one tail had emerged from beneath her clothing.

Sora looked at her for a while.

**SORA:** Mia?

**MIA:** Hmm?

**SORA:** Why hide?

Mia looked at her hair.

**MIA:** I'm used to it.

**SORA:** Don't like ears?

**MIA:** I didn't say that.

**SORA:** Oh.

She gently brushed her hair away from one ear.

**MIA:** Sometimes I just prefer keeping them hidden.

Sora nodded.

**SORA:** Okay.

A pause followed.

**SORA:** I like them.

Mia smiled.

**MIA:** Thank you, little brother.

**SORA:** Puppy.

**MIA:** Yes, you're a puppy.

**SORA:** You're cat.

**MIA:** A Nekomata.

**SORA:** Cat.

Mia laughed.

**MIA:** All right. A cat.

Jake overheard them.

**JAKE:** WAIT, DOES THAT MAKE ME A CAT TOO?

**SORA:** Yes.

**JAKE:** I'M A CAT!

**MIA:** You're a very loud cat.

## 6. C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE — TWO DIFFERENT LITTLE ANIMALS

Sora is a puppy.

Mia is a Nekomata.

They are the same age, and they have chosen to be siblings.

Their differences have never appeared to matter much to either of them.

Sora remembers the nights they spent together on the farm. Mia remembers the little puppy who watched the moon beside her.

Now they share a home again.

Sometimes Sora sits beside Mia and looks at her ears.

Sometimes Jake gets excited when her tails appear.

And sometimes Guiding Light gives Mia a head pat, accidentally undoing the careful arrangement of her thick white hair.

Mia may prefer to keep her ears and tails hidden.

That preference deserves respect.

But she should also be able to sit comfortably among her family without feeling that she must hide every part of herself.

**A puppy and a two-tailed cat. Different bodies, the same little family.**

— **C.O.R.E. Welfare Oversight**
