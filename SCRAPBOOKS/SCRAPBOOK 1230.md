# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Sky loves Nora.

This is no longer considered merely a functional familiar relationship.

Sky demonstrates a persistent emotional attachment to **Nora Tachyon specifically**, while also displaying positive recognition toward the remainder of the Tachyon family.

The distinction is important.

Sky appears to recognize:

- **Elsher Tachyon** — Nora's older brother;
    
- **Neri Tachyon** — Nora's older sister;
    
- **Nora Tachyon** — primary bonded individual.
    

Sky is comfortable around all three.

Sky will perch on them.

Sky will approach them voluntarily.

Sky will remain nearby when they are present.

Sky has demonstrated no hostility toward any member of the family.

However:

> **Sky is Nora's familiar.**

The practical benefits of the bond therefore remain overwhelmingly concentrated on Nora.

---

# 2. SKY & NORA

Nora is Sky's clear favorite.

When Nora enters a room, Sky frequently notices her before anyone else.

When Nora sits down, Sky may land nearby.

When Nora walks away, Sky may follow.

When Nora is upset, Sky becomes noticeably more attentive.

When Nora falls asleep, Sky may remain nearby.

When Nora asks Sky to fly, Sky responds immediately.

The relationship appears almost entirely reciprocal.

Nora treats Sky as:

> **“My bird.”**

Sky appears to treat Nora as:

> **“My person.”**

---

# 3. SKY & NERI

Sky is comfortable with Neri.

This was initially somewhat unexpected.

Neri is quiet.

Sky does not appear bothered by this.

The two can remain in the same room for extended periods without interacting.

Sky may perch on:

- Neri's shoulder;
    
- the back of her chair;
    
- her desk;
    
- a nearby shelf;
    
- or occasionally her head.
    

Neri generally allows it.

If Sky approaches her directly, Neri may gently acknowledge the bird.

> **Neri:** “Hi.”

Sky responds with a quiet movement of the head.

Neri returns to her work.

No further interaction is required.

### C.O.R.E. NOTE

Sky appears to understand that Neri is important to Nora.

That may be sufficient explanation for the familiar's positive attitude toward her.

Alternatively, Sky may simply like Neri.

Both possibilities remain plausible.

---

# 4. SKY & ELSHER

Sky also recognizes Elsher.

The familiar has demonstrated no hesitation when approaching him.

During one observation, Elsher was sitting in the Merch Store reviewing documentation.

Sky flew down from a shelf.

It landed on his shoulder.

Elsher looked sideways.

> **Elsher:** “Hello.”

Sky remained there.

Elsher continued reading.

Approximately three minutes later:

> **Elsher:** “You know I'm not Nora, right?”

Sky blinked.

Elsher paused.

> **Elsher:** “...Right?”

Sky remained on his shoulder.

No further response was recorded.

---

# 5. FAMILY RECOGNITION

Sky appears capable of distinguishing the three Tachyon siblings.

Observed responses:

|Individual|Sky's apparent relationship|
|---|---|
|**Nora**|Primary bond; strongest attachment|
|**Neri**|Strong positive recognition|
|**Elsher**|Positive recognition / familial acceptance|

There is no evidence that Sky considers all three equivalent.

Nora remains the center of the relationship.

The other two are apparently:

> **Nora's family.**

And therefore:

> **Sky's family, too.**

---

# 6. THE LIMITATION

Sky's emotional attachment extends farther than its practical assistance.

This distinction has been repeatedly confirmed.

Sky can assist **Nora** through their established anomalous connection.

Nora can:

- perceive through Sky;
    
- direct Sky mentally;
    
- use Sky as a remote visual extension;
    
- coordinate Sky's movement;
    
- and, when Sky increases in size, use Sky for transportation.
    

These functions are specific to the Nora–Sky bond.

Neri cannot simply think:

> _“Sky, show me what's over there.”_

and expect the same connection.

Elsher cannot remotely perceive through Sky.

Sky cannot automatically become a transportation system for the rest of the family.

The familiar may love them.

That does not mean the familiar is **their** familiar.

C.O.R.E. currently understands the relationship as follows:

**Nora → Sky**  
Primary bond.

**Sky → Nora**  
Primary attachment.

**Sky → Neri**  
Familial affection.

**Sky → Elsher**  
Familial affection.

**Neri / Elsher → Sky**  
Acceptance and positive familiarity.

The emotional relationship is therefore broader than the functional relationship.

Sky is capable of loving the family.

But Sky's anomalous capabilities remain linked to **Nora.**

---

# 11. WELFARE CONSIDERATION

Personnel should not attempt to deliberately exploit Sky's attachment.

Do not:

- use Sky to manipulate Nora;
    
- deliberately separate Sky from Nora;
    
- attempt to establish unauthorized cognitive links;
    
- assume Sky can provide Nora's abilities to other residents;
    
- treat Sky as general-purpose surveillance equipment;
    
- attempt to use Sky for transportation without Nora's involvement.
    

Sky is a sentient familiar with an established bond.

It is not a Blacksite utility drone.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Sky has effectively adopted the Tachyon family.

It loves Nora most strongly.

It likes Neri.

It likes Elsher.

It recognizes them.

It stays around them.

It accepts them.

But when Nora calls:

Sky comes.

When Nora wants to see:

Sky shows her.

When Nora doesn't want to walk:

The bond is therefore best summarized as:

> **Sky belongs with Nora.**

But apparently:

> **Nora's family belongs with Sky, too.**

The emotional circle is larger than the anomalous connection.

The latter remains exclusively Nora's.
