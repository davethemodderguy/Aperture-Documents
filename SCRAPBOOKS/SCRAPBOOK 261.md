# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – ANOMALOUS ABILITY DOSSIER**

### **SUBJECT: DIALYN – "RESONANCE"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Executive Personnel & Research Division

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following extensive Ether analysis after Dialyn's employment with the Krampus Compliance Authority, Research Division confirmed the manifestation of a unique Ether-based ability.

The phenomenon has been designated:

## **RESONANCE**

Unlike conventional Ether manipulation, Resonance does not directly create force.

Instead, it manipulates one of physics' most fundamental properties.

**Velocity.**

---

# **ABILITY DESCRIPTION**

Resonance allows Dialyn to transfer velocity from one object to another through Ether.

The velocity itself is conserved.

It is merely relocated.

In practical terms:

If one object is moving quickly...

Dialyn may cause another object to inherit part or all of that speed.

Conversely, she may remove velocity from a moving object, slowing or stopping it while transferring that motion elsewhere.

Research Division has found no evidence that the process violates conservation laws.

Rather, Ether appears to act as the transfer medium.

---

# **APPLICATIONS**

Documented uses include:

- Accelerating stationary objects.
    
- Slowing incoming projectiles.
    
- Redirecting momentum.
    
- Enhancing thrown equipment.
    
- Assisting evacuation by rapidly accelerating vehicles.
    
- Reducing fall impacts by redistributing velocity.
    
- Increasing mobility during combat.
    

The ability has proven exceptionally versatile.

---

# **PORTAL INTERACTION**

Dialyn's portals naturally inherit the properties of Resonance.

Any object passing through one portal may exit another while retaining, removing, or acquiring velocity depending on Dialyn's intent.

Examples include:

- A slow-moving object exiting at extreme speed.
    
- A fast projectile emerging almost motionless.
    
- A falling object exiting horizontally while maintaining its transferred momentum.
    
- Redirecting movement around obstacles without significant energy loss.
    

Researchers consider this one of the most tactically valuable aspects of the ability.

---

# **SELF-AMPLIFICATION**

Resonance becomes substantially stronger as Dialyn herself moves faster.

Running.

Jumping.

Falling.

Being propelled by external forces.

All increase the amount of transferable velocity available.

Field testing demonstrated that Dialyn frequently builds momentum herself before redistributing it elsewhere.

Personnel have compared the technique to "charging" the ability.

---

# **LIMITATIONS**

Current observations indicate several constraints.

- Velocity cannot be created from nothing.
    
- Greater transfers require greater available momentum.
    
- Continuous large-scale transfers place significant strain on Ether reserves.
    
- Precision decreases when multiple transfers occur simultaneously.
    
- Maintaining numerous active portals while performing high-energy transfers is mentally demanding.
    

---

# **THE HULA HOOP**

Dialyn's signature hula hoop serves as an ideal focus for Resonance.

Originally used simply to create paired portals, it now functions as both a portal generator and a kinetic conduit.

Research has documented several advanced techniques.

### **Momentum Relay**

A moving object enters one portal.

Its velocity is transferred through Ether.

A different object exits another portal carrying the transferred speed.

---

### **Stationary Launch**

A stationary object can be accelerated instantly using velocity previously stored from another source.

---

### **Velocity Sink**

Incoming projectiles are dramatically slowed before impact.

The removed momentum is redirected elsewhere.

---

### **Resonant Step**

Dialyn briefly redirects her own momentum to perform rapid directional changes that appear impossible under normal human biomechanics.
