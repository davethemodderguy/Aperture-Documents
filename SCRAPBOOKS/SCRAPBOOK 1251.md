# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### MEDICAL & RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-MED-4C-NORA-395  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — MEDICAL / RESIDENT WELFARE  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**SUBJECT:** Nora Tachyon  
**OBSERVATION:** Residual scarring following pre-rescue fatal injury

---

# 1. SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon retains physical scarring from the injuries that caused her death prior to her rescue by Guiding Light.

The scars are permanent.

They are primarily located around:

- the **neck**;
    
- and the **cardiac/heart region of the chest**.
    

Both areas are normally concealed by Nora's clothing.

Her preferred **white scarf** provides additional coverage around the neck.

As a result, the scars are rarely visible during ordinary SAFEHOUSE activity.

---

# 2. NECK SCARRING

Nora has visible scar tissue around the neck corresponding to the fatal injury she sustained prior to her disappearance.

The scarring is not normally visible beneath her clothing.

Her white scarf covers most of the affected area during normal wear.

Nora generally keeps the scarf on.

Personnel should not assume that the scarf is purely an aesthetic accessory.

It provides both practical coverage and a degree of personal privacy.

---

# 3. CARDIAC-REGION SCARRING

Additional scarring is present around the region of the heart.

The scars correspond to the trauma associated with Nora's death.

They are normally covered by:

- her shirt;
    
- outer clothing;
    
- and other normal layers.
    

The area is therefore not visible during routine interactions.

No ongoing cardiac impairment has been established solely as a result of the scarring.

The scars are physical reminders of an event that Nora remembers vividly.

---

# 4. RELATIONSHIP TO HER MEMORIES

The scars and memories are directly connected.

Nora does not merely remember being killed.

Her body retains evidence that it happened.

This appears to be one reason she occasionally touches her scarf or chest after waking from a nightmare.

During one observation, Nora woke from a nightmare and briefly placed her hand against her chest.

Aeterna noticed.

She did not ask what happened.

She simply remained beside her.

---

# 5. NERI'S AWARENESS

Neri is aware of the scars.

She does not stare at them.

She does not ask Nora to show them.

She does not treat them as something frightening.

During one conversation, Nora asked:

> **NORA:**  
> “Does it look bad?”

Neri looked at her.

Pause.

> **NERI:**  
> “It's a scar.”

Nora waited.

Neri continued:

> **NERI:**  
> “You're still here.”

Nora nodded.

The conversation ended there.

---

# 6. AETERNA'S RESPONSE

Aeterna is similarly protective of Nora's privacy.

If Nora's scarf shifts and exposes part of the neck scarring, Aeterna does not react with alarm.

She simply helps Nora adjust the scarf if Nora permits it.

On one occasion:

_Nora's scarf slipped slightly._

Aeterna noticed.

She gently repositioned it.

> **NORA:**  
> “Thanks.”

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Of course.”

Nothing more was said.

---

# 7. PERSONNEL GUIDANCE

Personnel are reminded:

- Do not ask Nora to display the scars.
    
- Do not touch the affected areas without medical necessity and appropriate consent.
    
- Do not repeatedly ask how she received them.
    
- Do not describe the scars as “ugly,” “disturbing,” or similar.
    
- Do not treat the scarf as something that must be removed for ordinary interaction.
    
- Do not assume the scars indicate current injury.
    
- Do not interpret Nora's choice to cover them as shame.
    

If medical examination requires access to the affected areas, standard resident medical protocols apply.

Otherwise:

**Leave them covered.**

---

# 8. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT

The scarring is considered **stable residual tissue damage.**

There is currently no evidence that the scars themselves represent an active medical emergency.

Their significance is primarily:

**historical, physical, and psychological.**

Nora survived an event that was expected to end her life.

Her body remembers.

So does she.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Nora's white scarf is easy to overlook.

It is simply part of her usual appearance.

White fabric.

A small child.

Long white hair.

A beret.

A shark tail.

A family journal at her waist.

Sky frequently sitting above her head.

But beneath that scarf is evidence of something much older than the six-year-old who runs through the SAFEHOUSE.

A scar around her neck.

And beneath her clothes, another around her heart.

They are usually hidden.

They do not need to be shown.

Nora does not owe anyone a demonstration of what happened to her.

She survived it.

That is enough.
