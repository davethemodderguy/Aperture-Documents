### **CORIN / TREEHOUSE INCIDENT — AFTERMATH**

# **WHY IS HOPE SO HARD TO FIND?**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subjects:** Corin / Orphie  
**Location:** Treehouse Exterior  
**Status:** **EMOTIONAL COLLAPSE**

---

# **AFTERMATH**

The conversation ended.

The snow did not.

Corin remained beneath the tree.

For several minutes, she appeared completely still.

Then she began crying.

Quietly at first.

Then uncontrollably.

Her wings folded tighter around her body as she tried to hide herself.

---

# **CORIN**

The words continued repeating inside her head.

> **"I just want the old Corin back."**

She knew Orphie had not intended to hurt her.

She knew what Orphie meant.

But knowing did not make it hurt less.

Instead, Corin interpreted the statement as confirmation of everything she had written about herself.

That she had changed too much.

That she was too damaged.

That she had become someone nobody wanted.

That even Orphie would rather have the person she used to be.

---

# **SELF-HATRED**

Corin's thoughts became increasingly hostile toward herself.

She whispered:

> **"Of course."**

Then:

> **"Why wouldn't she?"**

Her hands trembled beneath her wings.

> **"I'm not even me anymore."**

A pause.

> **"I'm just what's left."**

She cried harder.

---

# **THE WINGS**

Corin buried her face beneath one wing.

The feathers remained completely dormant.

They could not ignite.

They could not glow.

They could not protect her from the thoughts in her head.

They could only cover her.

So they did.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie remained beside her.

She wanted to fix it.

She wanted to take the sentence back.

She wanted to replace it with something better.

But every attempt at reassurance seemed inadequate.

> **"Corin, I'm sorry."**

No response.

> **"I didn't mean that."**

Corin continued crying.

> **"I love you exactly as you are."**

A pause.

Corin whispered:

> **"...You shouldn't."**

Orphie froze.

---

# **HELPLESSNESS**

Orphie had faced dangerous situations before.

She had survived things that should have killed her.

She had fought.

She had endured.

She had rescued people.

None of it prepared her for this.

There was no enemy to defeat.

No wound to bandage.

No machine to repair.

No door to unlock.

She couldn't fight Corin's memories.

She couldn't force hope back into her.

She couldn't undo eight months of waiting.

She couldn't erase one sentence.

So Orphie sat there.

Helpless.

---

# **THE QUESTION**

Eventually, Orphie whispered to herself:

> **"Why is hope so hard to get now?"**

There was no answer.

She looked at Corin.

The girl who once wrote:

> **"I live on hope..."**

was now crying beneath the same tree.

---

# **THE OLD NOTE**

Orphie remembered the rest of that sentence.

She could still picture the handwriting.

> **"As long as I spread hope and I have hope of my own, I will move on, live with Orphie :D"**

The little **":D"** suddenly hurt more than anything else.

Because Corin had once believed it.

Completely.

---

# **THE CONTRADICTION**

Hope had once been easy for Corin.

She could find it in:

- Flying.
    
- Flowers.
    
- Friends.
    
- Her feathers.
    
- The Treehouse.
    
- Orphie.
    

Now none of those things seemed capable of reaching her.

Orphie was sitting right beside her.

And Corin was still hopeless.

---

# **ORPHIE'S REALIZATION**

Orphie finally understood something.

Hope could not be demanded.

It could not be explained into existence.

It could not be handed to Corin like a gift.

And saying:

> **"You need to be hopeful."**

would only make the wound deeper.

So Orphie stopped trying.

She simply stayed.

---

# **NO PROMISES**

Orphie did not tell Corin:

> _Everything will be okay._

She did not know that.

She did not say:

> _You'll be happy again._

She couldn't promise it.

She did not say:

> _You'll become the old Corin again._

She knew that wasn't what Corin needed.

Instead:

> **"You don't have to be the old Corin."**

Corin continued crying.

Orphie added:

> **"You don't have to be happy right now."**

A pause.

> **"You don't even have to believe me."**

Orphie placed her hand beside Corin's wing.

> **"I'll still be here."**

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie once thought hope was something she could give Corin.

A flower.

A warm meal.

A birthday.

An explanation.

A promise.

She now understands that none of those things can simply create hope.

Corin's hope was not lost because she didn't have enough reasons to be happy.

It was worn down by months of waiting.

By believing she had been forgotten.

By surviving long enough to stop expecting anyone.

And now the person she waited for is sitting beside her.

Still, Corin cannot believe that she will stay.

That is why hope is so difficult now.

It isn't missing because nobody loves Corin.

It is missing because Corin no longer knows how to **believe** that love will remain.

Under the snow, Corin continues crying.

Orphie remains beside her.

She cannot fix the wound.

She cannot make Corin hopeful.

She cannot give her back the girl she used to be.

So she does the only thing she still can.

She stays.

And perhaps, for now...

that is the smallest beginning hope could possibly have.

**End of Aftermath**
