# **Z-335 — "MARTS"**

**Classification:** Allied Entity / Benign Anomalous Species

---

# **OVERVIEW**

**Z-335**, commonly referred to as **"Marts,"** are small, sapient, anomalous entities consisting primarily of magically sustained water and Ether.

They were unintentionally created by **Lumi**, an ice mage and the last known surviving member of her magical lineage, during an attempt to recreate her lost magic.

The resulting entities were not hostile.

Instead, they displayed an immediate tendency toward:

- Cooperation.
    
- Curiosity.
    
- Happiness.
    
- Assistance.
    
- Social bonding.
    
- Emotional support.
    

Marts have since become permanent residents of both the **Heaven Blacksite** and the **Krampus Compliance Authority (KCA)**.

They are considered one of C.O.R.E.'s most reliable allied anomalous populations.

Lumi considers them her greatest achievement.

---

# **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

A standard Mart consists of a small, blue, gelatinous body.

Marts possess:

- No arms.
    
- No legs.
    
- No conventional facial anatomy.
    
- No conventional skeletal structure.
    
- Small deer antlers.
    
- A miniature top hat.
    

Their bodies behave similarly to water while maintaining a stable, cohesive form.

Despite their apparent lack of limbs, Marts are capable of interacting with their environment through deformation, movement, Ether manipulation, and later-developed anomalous abilities.

Their bodies do not behave like ordinary water.

A Mart can:

- Sit on furniture.
    
- Sleep on computers.
    
- Move through ventilation systems.
    
- Carry objects.
    
- Enter narrow spaces.
    
- Remain stable outside conventional containers.
    
- Exist without being affected by gravity.
    

---

# **TOP HATS**

The miniature top hat worn by every Mart is not merely decorative.

The hat functions as an integral component of the Mart's anomalous stability.

If a Mart melts itself down while its hat remains nearby, it can reform.

If its hat is removed or separated during destabilization, the Mart may fail to reform.

A Mart that loses its hat during a destabilization event may experience catastrophic internal failure, resulting in an outward burst of its contained material.

Personnel are therefore reminded:

> **DO NOT TAKE A MART'S HAT.**

This rule applies even as a joke.

---

# **PHYSIOLOGY**

Marts are not conventional biological organisms.

Their bodies are sustained by a combination of:

- Water.
    
- Ether.
    
- Magical containment.
    
- Anomalous self-stabilization.
    

Their internal temperature is approximately equivalent to **liquid nitrogen**.

Direct contact is normally harmless while the Mart remains stable.

However, sudden destabilization or violent rupture can result in severe cold exposure and frostbite.

Marts themselves appear completely unaffected by their internal temperature.

---

# **DIET**

Marts can consume and dissolve an unusually wide variety of substances.

Their exact digestive mechanism is unknown.

Their preferred food is:

> **Sugary cheese crackers.**

Cheese crackers are commonly used as:

- Rewards.
    
- Salaries.
    
- Gifts.
    
- Emotional incentives.
    
- General Mart currency.
    

Marts will attempt to eat almost anything offered to them.

Personnel are therefore advised to avoid feeding them unknown substances.

---

# **LIFE CYCLE**

A normal Mart possesses an approximate natural lifespan of **five weeks**.

Lifespan may be extended through:

- Happiness.
    
- Social interaction.
    
- Regular exposure to water.
    
- General wellbeing.
    

Marts appear to derive metabolic and anomalous stability from both **water and positive emotional states**.

Extreme sadness can cause catastrophic destabilization.

Marts themselves do not normally regard death as tragic.

They possess a collective understanding that new Marts will eventually appear.

---

# **REPRODUCTION**

Marts naturally increase their population through anomalous duplication.

Under normal circumstances, population growth is self-regulating.

The mechanism responsible for maintaining stable colony populations remains unknown.

Despite their ability to duplicate, both major colonies have maintained a stable population of:

> **1,750 Marts each.**

# **INTELLIGENCE**

Standard Marts possess intelligence approximately comparable to an average mouse.

Their original vocabulary was extremely limited.

Common phrases included:

> "Hello."

> "Yes."

> "No."

> "Mart!"

> "We'll be back."

> "Goodbye, we'll meet again."

Following exposure to human language, particularly through **Star**, and later through a dictionary obtained from the Blacksite library, Mart linguistic capability expanded considerably.

However, ordinary Marts still generally prefer communicating through extremely simple statements.

Their conversations frequently contain:

> **100% words.**

> **0% grammar.**

Despite this, communication between Marts appears remarkably effective.

---

# **ELDER MARTS**

Five Marts are confirmed to be the original individuals created when Lumi first summoned the species.

These individuals are designated:

> **Elder Marts**

Elder Marts are only subtly different from standard Marts.

Known characteristics include:

- Slightly larger bodies.
    
- Slightly greater opacity.
    
- Marginally deeper blue coloration.
    

The difference is almost impossible to perceive without prolonged observation.

All five Elder Marts possess fluent English-speaking ability.

Their identities were initially unknown for an extended period.

The complete Elder population has now been identified.

### **Confirmed Elder Marts**

1. **Camera Mart** – KCA Security Terminal.
    
2. **KCA Ambassador** – KCA Customer Services.
    
3. **Blacksite Ambassador** – Project SAFEHOUSE.
    
4. **Rapid Response Elder I** – KCA Security.
    
5. **Rapid Response Elder II** – KCA Security.
    

The Elders do not display any significant supernatural abilities beyond those possessed by ordinary Marts.

Their primary distinction is their age and accumulated experience.

---

Stationed primarily at the Krampus Compliance Authority.

Stationed primarily within **Project SAFEHOUSE**.

- Corey.
    
- SAFEHOUSE residents.

---

# **CAMERA MART**

**Camera Mart** is an Elder Mart stationed permanently at the KCA Security Terminal.

It possesses fluent English but rarely chooses to speak.

Its primary responsibility is monitoring KCA security cameras.

Camera Mart has developed extensive knowledge of:

- Facility layouts.
    
- Security systems.
    
- Ventilation routes.
    
- Personnel movement.
    
- Potential intrusion patterns.
    

Camera Mart serves as the primary early-warning system for the Mart security network.

---

# **RAPID RESPONSE MARTS**

Rapid Response Marts constitute the KCA's primary anomalous security support population.

They specialize in:

- Ventilation ambushes.
    
- Threat interception.
    
- Disarmament.
    
- Immobilization.
    
- Emergency response.
    

Marts can enter a hardened state commonly referred to as:

> **Boulder Mode.**

In this state, their bodies become sufficiently solid to function as high-velocity projectiles.

Because gravity does not meaningfully affect Marts, they can launch themselves through the facility at extreme speed.

They generally target:

- Legs.
    
- Arms.
    
- Shields.
    
- Equipment.
    
- Non-vital areas.
    

Their objective is immobilization rather than lethal harm.

---

# **KCA MART SPECIALIZATIONS**

The KCA maintains four recognized Mart roles.

### **1. Ambassador**

One Mart assigned to translation and human interaction.

### **2. Rapid Response Mart**

Security and emergency response.

### **3. Camera Mart**

Security surveillance.

### **4. Resident Mart**

General-purpose Mart.

These classifications describe roles rather than biological subspecies.

# **VENTILATION COLONY**

The KCA ventilation network has developed into a permanent Mart habitat.

A dedicated chamber exists within the vents known informally as:

> **Mart Meeting Room**

The room is used for gatherings, conversations, resting, and unknown Mart activities.

The KCA also contains a secondary Mart Nest inside the former office of **Belle Merryweather**.

The office remains otherwise unchanged.

Marts occupy:

- The chair.
    
- The desk area.
    
- Most of the floor.
    

No significant damage has been recorded.

---

# **BLACKSITE COLONY**

Unlike the KCA, the Heaven Blacksite is located on a remote island in Iceland and is concealed from the public.

Consequently, Blacksite Marts encounter very few hostile intruders.

Their activities primarily involve:

- Helping personnel.
    
- Emotional support.
    
- Maintenance.
    
- Gardening.
    
- Library assistance.
    
- Carrying supplies.
    
- Companionship.
    

The Blacksite colony is regarded as unusually peaceful.

---

# **GRAVITY ANOMALY**

Gravity has little to no effect on Marts.

A Mart may therefore:

- Float.
    
- Drift.
    
- Remain suspended in air.
    
- Fall sideways.
    
- Leave balconies.
    
- Become airborne unintentionally.
    

Personnel encountering a drifting Mart outside a safe area are advised to return it to a stable location.

The Mart will generally interpret the assistance as kindness.

---

# **PHYSICAL RESILIENCE**

Marts are effectively physically indestructible under ordinary circumstances.

They can survive:

- Being thrown.
    
- Being dropped.
    
- Being crushed.
    
- Being boiled.
    
- High-velocity impacts.
    
- Boulder Mode impacts.
    

They generally do not perceive these actions as hostile.

However, their **hat-dependent existence** and emotional stability remain significant vulnerabilities.

---

# **HIVEMIND**

Marts share a collective cognitive structure resembling a flock of corvids.

This does not eliminate individual personality.

Marts can:

- Like individuals.
    
- Avoid individuals.
    
- Dislike individuals.
    
- Prank individuals.
    
- Cooperate.
    
- Share information.
    
- Develop preferences.
    

They cannot currently merge with one another.

The possibility remains under investigation.

---

# **SOCIAL BEHAVIOR**

Marts are overwhelmingly friendly.

They frequently approach individuals who appear:

- Sad.
    
- Lonely.
    
- Stressed.
    
- Depressed.
    
- Injured.
    
- Overworked.
    

Their preferred form of assistance is usually simple companionship.

A Mart may sit beside someone for hours without saying anything.

The behavior is believed to be instinctive rather than taught.

---

# **RELATIONSHIP WITH LUMI**

Lumi is considered the mother of the Mart population.

All Marts refer to her as:

> **"Mother."**

Lumi remains extremely proud of their existence.

The Marts themselves appear strongly attached to her.

Despite having created thousands of individuals, Lumi does not attempt to distinguish between generations.

She has admitted that she cannot reliably identify the Elder Marts by appearance.

---

# **KNOWN THREAT PROFILE**

Marts are classified as **Benign** under normal circumstances.

However, their security capabilities can become significant when defending allied personnel.

Documented hostile-response capabilities include:

- Coordinated ambushes.
    
- Ventilation infiltration.
    
- Boulder Mode.
    
- High-velocity movement.
    
- Swarming.
    
- Equipment disarmament.
    
- Immobilization.
    
- Tactical deception.
    

Marts have demonstrated the ability to deliberately pretend to be asleep in order to lure hostile individuals into a controlled ambush.

This behavior indicates significantly greater tactical intelligence than previously assumed.

---

# **CRIMINAL DETERRENCE**

The KCA Mart colony has developed a substantial reputation among criminal organizations.

Common criminal designations include:

> **"Blue Rocks."**

> **"Blue Devil Blobs."**

> **"The Detergent."**

The reputation originates from repeated failed robbery attempts.

In multiple incidents, hostile groups were subdued before firing a shot.

Heavy armor and riot shields have proven ineffective against coordinated Mart swarming.

Sedatives have also demonstrated negligible effectiveness.

---

# **ALLIED STATUS**

Z-335 is formally classified as a:

> **C.O.R.E. Allied Entity**

Marts are not to be treated as disposable assets.

Personnel are prohibited from:

- Removing Mart hats.
    
- Intentionally causing emotional distress.
    
- Experimenting on Marts without authorization.
    
- Attempting to weaponize Mart biology without C.O.R.E. approval.
    
- Consuming Marts.
    
- Using Marts as unauthorized ammunition.
    

The final rule was added after repeated incidents involving personnel asking whether a Mart in a cup was drinkable.

It is not.

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

Z-335 represents an unusually successful example of a benign anomalous species integrating into human society.

Despite their unusual physiology and considerable defensive capabilities, Marts consistently demonstrate:

- Loyalty.
    
- Cooperation.
    
- Empathy.
    
- Curiosity.
    
- Social attachment.
    
- Protective behavior.
    

Their presence has measurably improved morale at both the Heaven Blacksite and KCA.

C.O.R.E. therefore recommends continued protection and integration of Z-335 into allied facility operations.
