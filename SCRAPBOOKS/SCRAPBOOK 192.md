### **WILDLIFE OBSERVATION NOTE**

## **SUBJECT: "THE OWL AND THE BIG HOUSE CAT"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Personnel stationed near Shelter T-01 have become accustomed to an unusual sight.

A large white owl.

A ninety-five-kilogram white tiger.

Neither appears particularly interested in the other.

They simply...

Exist together.

---

# **MORNING ROUTINE**

Most mornings begin the same way.

The tiger stretches beneath the treehouse.

Corin climbs down the ladder carrying breakfast.

A few moments later...

The owl silently lands on the highest branch overhead.

No announcement.

No sound.

Just...

There.

The tiger glances upward.

The owl looks back.

Neither reacts further.

---

# **LUNCH**

Corin usually prepares enough food for herself.

Some inevitably goes to the tiger.

Some inevitably disappears.

Nobody needs to ask where.

High above, the owl can often be seen happily eating whatever it managed to steal.

The tiger watches.

Entirely uninterested.

The owl appears to understand one unwritten rule.

Never steal the tiger's food.

Only Corin's.

---

# **WINTER**

During heavy snowfall, the owl puffs its feathers into a nearly perfect white sphere.

The tiger curls beneath the tree, becoming an equally enormous striped blanket.

Corin has been observed reading quietly between them.

Personnel jokingly describe the arrangement as:

> "One accountant."

> "One giant house cat."

> "One airborne food thief."

---

# **VISITORS**

Mart usually receives a greeting from the owl before he reaches the treehouse.

Taiga usually receives a greeting from the tiger.

Corin receives both.

Belle has learned to keep one cookie in her pocket specifically for the owl.

The owl still steals another one.

# **CORIN**

When asked why the two animals stayed around her, Corin looked between the owl above and the tiger below.

She smiled faintly.

> "...Maybe they just like the quiet."

The owl blinked once.

The tiger closed its eyes.

Neither objected.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The owl continues to perch in the branches above Shelter T-01.

The big striped "house cat" continues sleeping beneath it.

Visitors have become accustomed to looking both up and down before approaching the treehouse.

One watches from above.

The other watches from below.

Corin simply calls them...

> "...My neighbors."

**End of Wildlife Observation Note**
