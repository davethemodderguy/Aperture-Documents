# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – RESEARCH ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: ELDER MART IDENTIFICATION UPDATE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Research Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the confirmed identification of **Camera Mart** as an Elder Mart, Research Division continued reviewing long-term observational data.

The investigation has resulted in two additional positive identifications.

The current confirmed Elder Mart population is now as follows:

- **Camera Mart** – KCA Security Terminal
    
- **KCA Ambassador**
    
- **Blacksite Ambassador**
    

The identities of the remaining **two Elder Marts** remain unknown.

---

# **DISCOVERY**

Unlike ordinary Marts, both Ambassadors remain assigned to consistent locations and are regularly observed interacting with personnel.

Years of accumulated observations allowed researchers to compare subtle physical characteristics over time.

The conclusion was unanimous.

Both Ambassadors possess the same nearly imperceptible traits previously documented in Camera Mart:

- Slightly larger body.
    
- Marginally more opaque coloration.
    
- A faintly deeper blue appearance under direct lighting.
    

Without prolonged observation, these differences remain effectively invisible.

---

# **OBSERVATION**

The discovery has prompted an interesting realization.

Three of the five original Marts naturally settled into roles requiring patience, communication, and long-term responsibility.

Research emphasizes that there is no evidence this occurred because they are Elders.

Rather, it appears they simply chose these roles over time.

---

# **REACTION OF THE COLONY**

When informed that both Ambassadors had been identified as Elders, the surrounding Marts displayed no visible reaction.

One Resident Mart simply looked at the Ambassador and happily said:

> "Mart."

The Ambassador smiled.

> "Mart."

The conversation was apparently sufficient.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Three of the five original Marts have now been conclusively identified.

Despite this revelation, nothing about their daily lives has changed.

Camera Mart continues watching the security monitors.

The KCA Ambassador continues helping visitors beside Dialyn.

The Blacksite Ambassador continues spending its days wherever someone needs company—most often at Project SAFEHOUSE alongside Mart, or quietly sitting with Dialyn, Corin, or Corey.

When asked whether becoming publicly known as Elders bothered them, both Ambassadors gave the same answer.

> "We're still Mart."

Research agreed.

They always were.
