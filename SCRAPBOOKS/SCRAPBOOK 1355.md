# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Phoenix Raffedella tripped.

She fell.

She died.

C.O.R.E. medical personnel would like to emphasize that this sequence was entirely unsurprising.

Phoenix subsequently remained deceased for approximately four hours before her established **Transcendense** response restored her.

She woke with:

- no permanent injuries;
    
- no significant residual trauma;
    
- substantial confusion;
    
- and an apparently unrelated desire for coffee.
    

---

# 1. INCIDENT

Phoenix was walking through an internal Blacksite corridor.

There was no combat.

No hostile activity.

No anomalous interference.

No malfunctioning equipment.

No suspicious individual.

There was simply:

> a floor.

Phoenix's foot caught against an uneven section.

She lost her balance.

She fell.

The resulting impact was sufficient to cause fatal trauma.

Phoenix died almost immediately.

---

# 2. INITIAL RESPONSE

A nearby resident discovered her.

Emergency medical personnel were called.

The response team arrived quickly.

They confirmed:

> **NO VITAL SIGNS**

> **CARDIAC ACTIVITY: ABSENT**

> **RESPIRATION: ABSENT**

The team then checked the identity.

> **PHOENIX RAFFEDELLA**

There was a pause.

One medic sighed.

> **MEDIC:**  
> "Again?"

---

# 3. TRANSCENDENSE

Phoenix's body remained stable.

There was no immediate revival.

This was not unusual.

Her Transcendense response does not necessarily activate instantaneously.

Ether gradually accumulated around the body.

The process continued for several hours.

No intervention was required.

At approximately four hours after the incident:

Phoenix inhaled.

Her eyes opened.

She stared at the ceiling.

Then:

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "...What happened?"

The medical responder looked down at her.

> **MEDIC:**  
> "You fell."

Phoenix blinked.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "Oh."

She sat up.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "Did I die?"

The medic nodded.

> **MEDIC:**  
> "Yes."

Phoenix considered this.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "...Again?"

> **MEDIC:**  
> "Yes."

---

# 4. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT

Phoenix's body had completed the expected Transcendense recovery cycle.

All major injuries had healed.

No permanent neurological damage remained.

No internal organ damage remained.

No fractures remained.

No external trauma remained.

Phoenix's only reported complaint was:

> **"I'm tired."**

This was considered consistent with her established medical history.

---

# 5. JANE DOE'S RESPONSE

Jane arrived after receiving the medical alert.

She looked at Phoenix.

Then at the staircase.

Then back at Phoenix.

> **JANE:**  
> "You died falling down stairs."

Phoenix nodded.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "Apparently."

> **JANE:**  
> "You survived getting shot in the head."

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "Yes."

> **JANE:**  
> "But stairs got you."

Phoenix looked at the staircase.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "...Apparently."

Jane stared at her.

> **JANE:**  
> "That's almost impressive."

Phoenix did not respond.

She was already falling asleep.

---

---

# 7. C.O.R.E. MEDICAL NOTE

Phoenix's condition continues to demonstrate the unusual distinction between:

> **injury resistance**

and

> **post-mortem restoration.**

Phoenix possesses the latter.

She does not possess the former.

She can still be injured.

She can still experience trauma.

She can still die.

Her body simply possesses an abnormal mechanism that restores her after biological death.

The fact that she survived the Winter's Howl assassination attempt does not mean ordinary accidents are incapable of killing her.

The staircase demonstrated this adequately.

---

# 8. FACILITY RESPONSE

Following the incident, the staircase was inspected.

No structural fault was discovered.

No anomalous activity was detected.

No sabotage was identified.

The staircase was functioning normally.

C.O.R.E. therefore concludes:

> **Phoenix simply tripped.**

A warning sign was nevertheless placed nearby.

It reads:

> **PLEASE WATCH YOUR STEP.**

Someone subsequently wrote underneath:

> **"Apparently Phoenix can't."**

The handwriting has not been identified.
