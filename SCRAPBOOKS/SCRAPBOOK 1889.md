# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### SAFEHOUSE RESIDENTIAL & PSYCHOLOGICAL WELFARE DIVISION

**Document ID:** CORE-SH-SAFE-136  
**Classification:** RESTRICTED — RESIDENT DISTRESS / FAMILY WELFARE  
**Status:** ACTIVE MONITORING  
**Subject:** Jake Akana's Emotional Deterioration Following Sora's Disappearance  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE — Floor 7  
**Concerned Residents:** Aries Akana, Tom Akana, Clover, Phoenix, Bao

---

### 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Sora remains missing.

There have been no confirmed sightings since he fled following the deliberate startle incident on Floor 7.

The consequences are no longer limited to Sora's welfare.

Jake Akana is heartbroken.

His emotional condition has become a significant concern for several SAFEHOUSE residents, particularly Aries, Tom, Clover, Phoenix, and Bao.

These individuals have each expressed concern over Jake's condition.

Jake spent weeks building a relationship with Sora. He wrote letters, gave him a whiteboard and marker, helped him establish a permanent den, and gradually became the person Sora trusted most.

Sora eventually became almost inseparable from him.

Now, Jake has lost that companionship without knowing where Sora is or whether he will ever return.

C.O.R.E. considers Jake's distress a separate welfare matter requiring direct attention.

### 2. JAKE AKANA — EMOTIONAL OBSERVATIONS

Jake's relationship with Sora was not incidental.

He was the one who discovered Sora beneath the snow and carried him to the Medical Bay. He continued trying to reach him even when Sora repeatedly chased him away.

For weeks, Jake received little in return.

He kept writing anyway.

Eventually, Sora accepted his friendship.

The two developed a routine in which Sora stayed beside Jake, often allowing himself to be carried. They shared Floor 7, and Sora's little den became part of their home.

That routine has abruptly ended.

Jake is now confronted with an empty space where Sora used to be.

The concern is not simply that Jake misses a friend.

It is that Jake may interpret Sora's disappearance as the loss of something he worked very hard to protect.

C.O.R.E. has particular concern that Jake may blame himself, even though he had no responsibility for the incident.

### 3. FAMILY AND RESIDENT CONCERNS

**3.1 — ARIES AKANA**

Aries is particularly concerned about Jake's emotional state.

As the eldest brother, Aries occupies an important position in the family's support structure. He understands that Jake's attachment to Sora was genuine and that the disappearance has affected him deeply.

Aries is advised not to pressure Jake into discussing his feelings before he is ready.

Jake needs reassurance, not an interrogation.

**3.2 — TOM AKANA**

Tom's concern is significant.

As Jake's older brother, Tom is familiar with Jake's tendency to prioritize others while neglecting his own needs.

There is a risk that Jake will attempt to conceal his heartbreak so that nobody else has to worry about him.

Tom should remain attentive to changes in Jake's behavior, appetite, sleep, and willingness to engage with others.

He must also be reminded that he is not expected to solve the situation alone.

**3.3 — CLOVER**

Clover is among the residents concerned about Jake.

Her involvement is important because Jake needs a familiar and reassuring environment beyond the immediate search for Sora.

Clover is encouraged to maintain ordinary companionship with Jake where appropriate, without treating every interaction as a psychological intervention.

Sometimes, a child needs someone to sit beside him without asking questions.

**3.4 — PHOENIX**

Phoenix has also expressed concern.

Given Phoenix's position among SAFEHOUSE's residents, his support may help ensure Jake is not left to manage his distress alone.

C.O.R.E. recommends that Phoenix coordinate with the other concerned residents rather than allowing Jake to receive overwhelming attention from multiple people simultaneously.

**3.5 — BAO**

Bao's concern is especially important.

Bao has long occupied a maternal role within SAFEHOUSE, and her presence may provide Jake with a sense of security.

She is encouraged to check on him, ensure he is eating and resting, and remind him that he is allowed to be upset.

Jake does not need to pretend everything is fine.

He does not need to be cheerful for everyone else's sake.

### 4. THE EMPTY SPACE ON FLOOR 7

Sora's den remains untouched.

The small room built into the wall is still there.

His whiteboard and marker remain available.

His place beside Jake remains empty.

C.O.R.E. recommends that nobody dismantle the den or remove Sora's belongings simply because he is currently missing.

The space should remain available for his return.

However, personnel must ensure that Jake is not forced to spend time staring at the empty den if doing so worsens his distress.

It is Sora's home.

It is also a reminder of someone Jake misses.

Both facts deserve respect.

### 5. DIRECTIVES — JAKE'S WELFARE

The following measures are effective immediately:

1. Jake is to receive regular welfare checks from trusted residents.
    
2. Meals and rest must be maintained without coercion.
    
3. He must not be blamed for Sora's disappearance.
    
4. He must not be instructed to continue searching or attempting contact.
    
5. He may express sadness, frustration, or anger without being told to suppress those feelings.
    
6. Aries, Tom, Clover, Phoenix, and Bao may coordinate support to avoid overwhelming him.
    
7. Jake must retain access to ordinary activities and companionship unrelated to the incident.
    

**A reminder for all involved:** Jake's kindness toward Sora was meaningful, but it was never a contract obligating him to save Sora.

### 6. INVESTIGATION STATUS

The investigation into the deliberate startle incident remains open.

C.O.R.E. considers the effects on both children to be serious.

The responsible individual must be identified, and appropriate disciplinary measures must follow.

The search for Sora continues under quiet-search protocols.

No attempt should be made to force an immediate reunion if Sora is located. Both children must be given the opportunity to recover at their own pace.

---

### 7. C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

There used to be a little puppy boy following Jake around Floor 7.

Sometimes he walked.

Sometimes Jake carried him.

Sometimes he stayed in his little doghouse-shaped room, writing things on a whiteboard.

Jake always knew where to find him.

Now he doesn't.

And the people who know Jake best are beginning to worry.

Aries worries about his youngest brother.

Tom worries about the brother who keeps trying to be strong.

Clover worries about the friend who has become unusually quiet.

Phoenix worries about the child who should never have had to carry this much sadness.

Bao worries about a little boy who has always loved others more easily than he loves himself.

There are five people watching Jake closely now.

But we must remember that Jake does not need five people trying to fix him.

He needs his family.

He needs to know that he is allowed to miss Sora. That he is allowed to cry. That he is allowed to be angry about what happened.

And he needs to hear one thing, repeatedly, until he believes it:

**He did not fail Sora.**

Jake gave Sora something he had apparently gone a very long time without.

A friend.

A home.

A reason to come outside.

That kindness does not become meaningless because someone frightened Sora away.

We still hope the little den will have its resident again.

Until then, perhaps the people who love Jake can do what Jake once did for Sora.

Stay close.

Even when he has nothing to say.
