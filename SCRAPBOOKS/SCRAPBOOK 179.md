# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **ANOMALOUS ENTITY STATUS REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: LUMIA ("LUMI") LOCKWOOD – POST-RESTORATION SEARCH**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Executive & Medical Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

At 09:17 hours, Research Division confirmed the successful re-stabilization of the Ether-Ghost manifestation designated **Lumia ("Lumi") Lockwood**.

Personnel expected Corin Lockwood to be located shortly afterward.

Historically, Lumi had always been able to find her daughter.

This time...

She couldn't.

---

# **SEARCH**

Lumi did not wait for a formal briefing.

Immediately after confirming her manifestation was stable, she began searching.

She visited every place Corin had ever considered safe.

The old tree.

Shelter T-01.

The botanical gardens.

The frozen lakeside.

The abandoned office.

The library.

The rooftops.

Nothing.

The treehouse stood empty.

The journal had already been recovered.

The blankets were gone.

Only the marks where the structure had once been lived in remained.

---

# **THE FIRST DAY**

By evening, Lumi had searched nearly every location Corin had ever shown her.

Security offered assistance.

She declined.

> "I know my daughter."

She searched until sunrise.

She found nothing.

---

# **THE THIRD DAY**

Lumi's search expanded beyond Corin's familiar routes.

Maintenance tunnels.

Storage buildings.

Observation towers.

Old service roads.

She began asking Security officers if they had seen a pale young woman with chalk-lime hair.

Every answer was the same.

"No."

---

# **PERSONNEL OBSERVATION**

For the first time since her manifestation began, personnel observed Lumi visibly exhausted.

Not physically.

Emotionally.

She stopped speaking during briefings.

She lingered over maps long after everyone else had left.

More than once, Security cameras recorded her standing silently beneath the abandoned tree where Corin used to sleep.

---

# **LUMI**

Late one evening, Mart found Lumi sitting alone beneath the tree.

She was holding the recovered sketch from Corin's journal.

Without looking up, she spoke.

> "...She's always been able to hide from the world."

A long silence followed.

Then, barely above a whisper,

> "...She never hid from me."

Her hands tightened around the paper.

> "...Until now."

Another long silence.

> "...I came back."

She closed her eyes.

> "...And I still couldn't find her."

Tears fell onto the sketch.

> "...Corin..."

> "...I'm so sorry."

Mart quietly sat beside her.

Neither spoke again.

---

# **MEDICAL COMMENT**

Psychological Services note that Lumi expressed profound guilt following her unsuccessful search.

Clinicians emphasized that Corin's disappearance resulted from a complex progression of trauma, isolation, and withdrawal over a long period, and should not be understood as the consequence of any one person's actions or inability to locate her.

Lumi acknowledged the assessment.

She did not argue with it.

She simply continued searching.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Lumia Lockwood's manifestation has been fully restored.

Search operations continue with her active participation.

Corin Lockwood remains missing.

Each morning, Lumi begins her search again.

Each evening, she returns without her daughter.

She has not stopped believing Corin can be found.

She has only stopped believing it will be easy.

**End of Anomalous Entity Status Report**
