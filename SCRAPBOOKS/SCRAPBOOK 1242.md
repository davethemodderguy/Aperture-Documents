# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

C.O.R.E. has confirmed that **Jane Doe and Invictus Aeterna have begun working together to scare people.**

Neither individual appears to consider this unusual.

Both appear to consider it extremely entertaining.

The pairing is particularly effective due to their complementary approaches.

Jane relies on:

- stealth;
    
- misdirection;
    
- psychological timing;
    
- environmental awareness;
    
- unexpected appearances;
    
- and an extensive understanding of how people react to being frightened.
    

Aeterna relies on:

- being approximately 185 cm tall;
    
- ancient-spirit intimidation;
    
- complete silence;
    
- glowing blue runes;
    
- appearing where she was not previously present;
    
- and possessing a voice that sounds as though it could announce the end of civilization.
    

Together, they have become significantly more difficult to predict.

# 2. INITIAL COLLABORATION

The partnership began when Jane found Aeterna standing motionless in a dark hallway.

Jane stopped.

Aeterna looked at her.

Jane smiled.

**JANE:**  
“You know, you're really good at this.”

**AETERNA:**  
“At what?”

**JANE:**  
“Scaring people.”

Pause.

**AETERNA:**  
“Yes.”

Jane thought for several seconds.

> **JANE:**  
> “Want to have some fun?”

Aeterna nodded.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Yes.”

The first joint operation occurred approximately twelve minutes later.

---

# 3. TARGET ONE

The first target was an MSAC operator who had previously attempted to obtain Tachyon merchandise from Nora through persistent requests.

Jane waited around a corner.

Aeterna stood at the opposite end of the corridor.

The operator walked toward them.

Jane remained hidden.

Aeterna remained motionless.

The operator noticed Aeterna.

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “...Aeterna?”

No response.

He continued walking.

Jane quietly appeared behind him.

> **JANE:**  
> “Looking for someone?”

The operator screamed.

He turned.

Jane was smiling.

He turned back.

Aeterna was standing directly in front of him.

The operator screamed again.

Jane laughed.

Aeterna remained completely serious.

---

# 4. TARGET TWO

The second target was a Blacksite technician.

Jane sent a message requesting that he inspect a supposedly malfunctioning security terminal.

The technician arrived.

The room was empty.

He checked the terminal.

Nothing appeared wrong.

He turned around.

Aeterna was sitting in the chair behind him.

The technician froze.

**TECHNICIAN:**  
“How did you get in here?”

Aeterna said nothing.

Jane's voice came through the intercom.

> **JANE:**  
> “She likes you.”

The technician looked around.

> **TECHNICIAN:**  
> “Who?”

Aeterna slowly tilted her head.

The technician left.

Jane was heard laughing over the intercom for approximately thirty seconds.

---

# 5. TARGET THREE

The third incident involved two MSAC operators.

Jane hid behind a doorway.

Aeterna waited around the next corner.

The operators walked past Jane.

Nothing happened.

They continued.

Aeterna appeared.

Both stopped.

One operator whispered:

> **“She's here.”**

The second replied:

> **“I know.”**

Aeterna stared at them.

Then:

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Good evening.”

Both immediately walked away.

Jane emerged from the doorway.

> **JANE:**  
> “That was almost too easy.”

Aeterna nodded.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “They were already afraid.”

Jane smiled.

> **JANE:**  
> “Exactly.”

# 6. THE ELEVATOR

The most elaborate incident occurred in an elevator.

Jane entered first.

Aeterna followed.

Three operators entered afterward.

The doors closed.

Nobody spoke.

The elevator began moving.

Jane casually looked at the floor display.

Aeterna stood in the corner.

The operators slowly became aware of her.

One looked at Jane.

Jane smiled.

The operator looked back at Aeterna.

Aeterna remained perfectly still.

The elevator stopped.

The doors opened.

Jane stepped out.

Aeterna remained inside.

The doors closed.

The operators looked at each other.

One whispered:

> **“Why didn't she leave?”**

Aeterna slowly turned her head toward them.

The elevator continued downward.

Approximately thirty seconds later, Jane received a message.

> **JANE:**  
> “You scared them?”

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Yes.”

> **JANE:**  
> “How?”

Pause.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “I looked at them.”

Jane reportedly laughed hard enough that nearby personnel checked whether she required medical assistance.
