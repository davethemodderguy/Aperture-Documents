### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / MEDICAL & PERSONNEL LOG**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — SOFT HAT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Age:** Approximately 5 years old  
**Status:** **CRITICAL / STABLE WITH PERMANENT DAMAGE**

---

# **HEAD INJURY UPDATE**

Alice's previously persistent cranial injury has finally closed.

Her regeneration eventually succeeded in restoring the damaged area.

However, the site remains extremely fragile.

Occasional bleeding continues to occur, particularly following physical strain or minor impacts.

The injury is no longer considered an open cranial wound.

It is instead classified as:

> **Healed tissue with persistent vulnerability.**

Medical personnel continue to monitor it.

---

# **THE HAT**

Alice now frequently wears a soft protective hat.

It provides:

- Gentle protection for the healed area
    
- Additional cushioning against accidental impacts
    
- Protection from environmental exposure
    
- A comfortable alternative to continuous medical bandaging
    

The hat is deliberately soft.

No rigid components.

No uncomfortable straps.

Nothing that could put unnecessary pressure on Alice's head.

Alice seems to like it.

---

# **APPEARANCE**

The SAFEHOUSE has reached a unanimous conclusion:

**Alice looks adorable in it.**

Her white hair pokes out from underneath the soft hat.

Her long rabbit ears usually remain visible above it.

Combined with her eyepatch and pale appearance, the hat has become one of Alice's most recognizable features.

Belle's assessment:

> **"She looks like a little snow bunny."**

Alice responded:

> **"Alice bunny."**

Belle laughed.

> **"Exactly."**

---

# **CORIN**

Corin was the first person to compliment it.

> **"It looks good on you."**

Alice touched the hat.

> **"Really?"**

> **"Really."**

Alice smiled.

She has worn it more frequently ever since.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle occasionally adjusts Alice's hat when it becomes crooked.

Alice usually doesn't notice.

Belle does.

> **"There. Perfect."**

Alice:

> **"Perfect?"**

Belle:

> **"Perfect."**

Alice smiles.

---

# **MEDICAL NOTE**

The hat is **not** considered a treatment for the underlying vulnerability.

It is protective equipment.

Alice's healed cranial tissue remains susceptible to reopening under sufficient trauma.

Given her history of falls and poor physical stability, continued protection is recommended.

The hat should therefore be treated as part of Alice's ordinary daily equipment.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

For months, Alice's head was wrapped in medical bandages.

Now those bandages are mostly gone.

In their place is a small, soft hat.

White hair spills out beneath it.

Her rabbit ears poke through the top.

Her eyepatch remains.

She still looks fragile.

But somehow, she looks a little more like a child again.

Corin once watched Alice walk past wearing the hat and quietly smiled.

Alice noticed.

> **"Why smile?"**

Corin answered:

> **"You look cute."**

Alice's ears perked up.

> **"...Cute?"**

> **"Very."**

Alice smiled.

> **"Good."**

And for once, she wasn't smiling to reassure anyone.

She was smiling because she liked the hat.
