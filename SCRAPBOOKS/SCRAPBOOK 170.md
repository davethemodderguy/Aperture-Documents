The note was discovered beneath the large snow-covered tree where Corin Lockwood had spent many nights sleeping.

It was folded neatly between the roots, protected from the snow by a flat piece of bark.

The handwriting was immediately identified as Corin's.

No date.

No signature.

Only a single page.

---

**To whoever finds this.**

If you're looking for Corin...

I don't know where she went.

The girl with black hair and blue eyes disappeared a long time ago.

People keep pointing at me and calling me by her name.

I answer because it's easier than explaining.

They tell me I'm healing.

They tell me I'm alive.

They tell me I'm home.

I believe they believe that.

I just don't know if they're talking to the right person.

Everyone remembers a little girl who laughed.

Mom remembers brushing her hair.

Norma remembers thunderstorms.

Arcturus remembers horses.

I remember operating lights.

Cold tables.

White ceilings.

When I close my eyes...

Those memories always arrive first.

The rest have to fight to be remembered.

Sometimes they win.

Most days they don't.

People say I'm strong.

I don't feel strong.

Strong people don't spend years wondering whether they're still themselves.

They don't look in a mirror and search for someone who isn't there.

The old family photograph still exists.

The faces are damaged.

Maybe that's fitting.

Maybe memories fade the same way photographs do.

You stop seeing the people.

You only notice what's missing.

If one day you find the little girl with black hair and blue eyes...

Tell her I'm sorry.

She deserved to grow up.

I only inherited what was left.

— C.
