# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – PSYCHOLOGICAL STATUS REPORT**

### **SUBJECTS: CORIN LOCKWOOD & COREY LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – Executive, Medical & Psychological Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

This report documents the continued deterioration of Corey Lockwood's psychological condition following the Courtyard Incident.

Previous interventions have yielded only temporary improvement.

Corey's declining mental state has begun producing measurable secondary effects on Corin due to the anomalous dependency linking the two paradoxes.

Medical Division considers the situation critical.

---

# **CURRENT OBSERVATIONS**

Personnel familiar with Corey unanimously report the same change.

She laughs less.

She smiles less.

She speaks less.

Long periods of silence have become increasingly common.

While Corey continues to perform her duties competently, coworkers have noted that she appears emotionally distant even during activities she previously enjoyed.

Several employees independently described her expression as:

> "...Like she's somewhere else."

---

# **BEHAVIORAL CHANGES**

Recent observations include:

- Reduced enthusiasm for daily activities.
    
- Declining participation in SAFEHOUSE gatherings.
    
- Frequent insomnia.
    
- Increased time spent alone outside the treehouse.
    
- Persistent emotional withdrawal.
    
- Difficulty accepting praise.
    
- Speaking about herself as though she has already been left behind.
    

No deterioration has been observed in her cognitive abilities.

Academically and professionally...

Corey remains exceptional.

Psychologically...

She continues to decline.

---

# **CLINICAL NOTE**

Psychological Services unanimously agree on one conclusion.

Corey's problem is **not** capability.

She is intelligent.

Highly adaptive.

Compassionate.

Resourceful.

She has demonstrated every skill necessary to build a fulfilling life.

The obstacle is not what Corey can do.

It is what Corey believes she deserves.

One senior psychologist summarized the matter simply.

> "There is almost nothing Corey cannot accomplish."

A pause.

> "She just keeps believing she'd be better off dead."

---

# **EFFECT ON CORIN**

Due to the Corin Paradox, Corey's worsening condition has begun affecting Corin.

Corin has become noticeably quieter.

She smiles less frequently.

Her sleep quality has declined.

Medical monitoring has documented elevated stress markers whenever Corey's condition worsens.

Neither sister verbally blames the other.

Instead...

Each quietly worries about the other.

The paradox appears to amplify this cycle.

Corey's condition continues to deteriorate despite consistent support from family, friends, and Psychological Services.

Medical Division emphasizes that she is **not** incapable of living.

Quite the opposite.

The tragedy is that Corey increasingly struggles to imagine herself being part of it.

Until that changes, Corin's recovery remains inseparable from Corey's own.
