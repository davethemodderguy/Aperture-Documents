# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL / MEDICAL CRISIS REPORT**

# **CORIN — TWENTY-FIVE WEEKS AWAKE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Incident:** **Extreme Prolonged Sleep Deprivation**  
**Duration:** **25 weeks since confirmed sleep**  
**Status:** Critical / Unresolved

---

# **OVERVIEW**

It has been confirmed that Corin has not genuinely slept for approximately **twenty-five weeks**.

For the first several weeks, personnel believed she was sleeping normally.

She was not.

Corin had been deliberately pretending to sleep beside Orphie while remaining conscious throughout the night.

Eventually, the pattern became impossible to dismiss.

Corin does not sleep.

She simply continues functioning.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL DECLINE**

The effects have become severe.

Corin's mental state has deteriorated far beyond her previous periods of depression.

Her behavior has become increasingly:

- Erratic.
    
- Disorganized.
    
- Paranoid.
    
- Emotionally unstable.
    
- Withdrawn.
    
- Confused.
    
- Detached from reality.
    

Personnel have described her as **mentally unstable and increasingly deranged**.

However, C.O.R.E. emphasizes that these behaviors are occurring after an extraordinary period without sleep.

This is not simply Corin "becoming a different person."

Her mind is deteriorating under conditions that no normal human brain could tolerate.

---

# **PHYSICAL IMPOSSIBILITY**

Under ordinary biological conditions, remaining awake for twenty-five weeks would be impossible.

Corin's anomalous physiology has prevented the expected fatal consequences.

Her Ether continues sustaining her body.

Her organs continue functioning.

Her heart continues beating.

Her regeneration remains active.

She can still fly.

She can still eat and drink.

She can still speak.

But her mind is not functioning normally.

C.O.R.E. has therefore reached an uncomfortable conclusion:

> **Her body can survive what her mind cannot.**

---

# **BEHAVIORAL CHANGES**

Corin has begun behaving unpredictably.

She may suddenly stop speaking during conversations.

She sometimes stares at empty spaces for extended periods.

She occasionally reacts to things nobody else can perceive.

She has been observed whispering to herself.

At other times, she becomes strangely energetic, talking rapidly before abruptly becoming silent again.

Her emotional state can change without warning.

One moment she may laugh.

The next, she may curl beneath her wings and cry.

---

# **THE WINGS**

Corin increasingly uses her wings as a physical barrier between herself and everyone else.

When overwhelmed, she folds them around herself.

Sometimes only her eyes remain visible.

Other times, she disappears completely beneath the feathers.

The behavior has become so frequent that SAFEHOUSE residents have begun recognizing it as a warning sign.

When Corin's wings close completely:

> **Something is wrong.**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie is deeply concerned.

For weeks, she believed Corin was sleeping beside her.

Learning that Corin had instead spent every night awake and hiding in the corner devastated her.

Orphie now regularly checks on her.

She stays nearby.

She tries talking.

She tries silence.

She tries simply sitting beside her.

Nothing has restored Corin's ability to sleep.

Orphie has begun expressing something rarely heard from her:

> **"I don't know what to do."**

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light is equally helpless.

She has attempted to determine whether the Architects can directly restore Corin's sleep.

They cannot find a safe method.

Forcing unconsciousness through anomalous means has been rejected.

Suppressing Corin's consciousness could interfere with her Ether-dependent physiology.

Manipulating her mind could cause permanent psychological damage.

Guiding Light has repeatedly examined Corin.

Every time, the conclusion remains the same.

> **"Her body is surviving."**

> **"...Her mind is not."**

---

# **THE ARCHITECTS**

The Architects have attempted everything they can reasonably justify.

Nothing has worked.

They can repair physical damage.

They can manipulate Ether.

They can alter environments.

They can provide protection.

But sleep is not merely a mechanical function that can be switched back on.

Corin's mind appears to have become trapped in a state of perpetual vigilance.

The very concept of allowing herself to become unconscious seems to frighten her.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi has become increasingly protective of Corin.

She frequently stays near the treehouse.

She brings food and drinks.

Sometimes she simply sits nearby.

Corin does not always acknowledge her presence.

Lumi continues anyway.

She refuses to treat Corin's withdrawal as abandonment.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The SAFEHOUSE residents have collectively become concerned.

Neko has stopped making jokes around Corin when she appears unstable.

Miss Neko quietly checks on her.

Endmin leaves things Corin might find comforting.

Belle sometimes sits beside her without saying anything.

Even the normal SAFEHOUSE chaos has become noticeably quieter whenever Corin is nearby.

Nobody knows what to do.

Everyone wants to help.

Nobody knows how.

---

# **THE HELPLESSNESS**

This has become the most disturbing part of the situation.

Aperture Industries has resources capable of solving problems that should be impossible.

The Architects are beings capable of manipulating forces beyond ordinary comprehension.

Yet everyone eventually reaches the same wall.

They cannot simply command Corin:

> **"Sleep."**

They cannot erase her memories.

They cannot force her mind to stop hurting.

They cannot repair twenty-five weeks of psychological deterioration with a medical procedure.

They can only remain beside her.

---

# **A RECENT INCIDENT**

Corin was found sitting beneath the treehouse window.

She had been awake for an unknown number of hours.

Orphie approached.

> **"Corin."**

Corin looked at her.

Her eyes were exhausted.

Yet there was no normal tiredness in them anymore.

> **"I'm okay."**

Orphie sat beside her.

> **"You haven't slept."**

Corin smiled faintly.

> **"I don't need to."**

Orphie looked away.

Neither of them believed that.

# **C.O.R.E. DIRECTIVE**

Corin is not to be abandoned.

She is not to be isolated as punishment.

She is not to be treated as dangerous simply because her behavior has become erratic.

Personnel are instructed to maintain a calm environment and remain available.

If Corin becomes unable to distinguish reality from hallucination, personnel should remain with her and avoid escalating the situation.

The objective is no longer simply:

> **"Get Corin to sleep."**

It is:

> **"Keep Corin safe until she can sleep again."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Twenty-five weeks ago, Corin stopped sleeping.

Nobody noticed at first.

She became very good at pretending.

Now the consequences are impossible to hide.

Her mind is fractured.

Her behavior is erratic.

Her perception of reality is deteriorating.

She is exhausted in ways her anomalous body cannot properly express.

And everyone who cares about her is watching it happen.

Orphie is worried.

Lumi is worried.

The Architects are worried.

The SAFEHOUSE residents are worried.

And perhaps the most frightening part is that none of them know how to fix it.

They can only stay.

So they do.

Because even if Corin cannot sleep—

**she should not have to face the endless night alone.**

**End of Psychological / Medical Crisis Report**
