# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **LUMI ENTITY OBSERVATION LOG – ADDENDUM 02**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **ENTITY PROFILE UPDATE**

**Legal Name:** **Lumia Lockwood**

**Preferred Name:** **Lumi**

Personnel records recovered from historical archives confirm that the Lockwood matriarch's given name was **Lumia Lockwood**.

However, during interviews with the Lockwood siblings, it became apparent that she almost never used her full name within the family.

The nickname **"Lumi"** originated years before the family's tragedy.

According to Arcturus, Corin was still very young and had difficulty pronouncing "Lumia."

She repeatedly shortened it to:

> "...Lumi."

The nickname remained.

Over time, even Arcturus and Norma stopped calling her Lumia.

When informed that C.O.R.E. intended to classify her as "Lumia Lockwood," the entity reportedly smiled and laughed softly.

> "I've always liked 'Lumi' more."

The official designation was amended accordingly.

---

# **ONGOING OBSERVATIONS**

Lumi continues to appear beside Corin with remarkable regularity.

She remains calm.

Patient.

Never raising her voice.

Never forcing conversation.

Yet one request has become increasingly frequent.

> "Go see Norma."

Corin continues reading financial reports.

No response.

Several minutes later.

> "Arcturus misses talking with you."

Corin signs another document.

Still no response.

Another evening.

> "They've been waiting."

Corin quietly closes the folder in front of her.

> "...I know."

Nothing more is said.

---

# **PATIENT RESPONSE**

During a counseling session, Corin was asked why she ignored Lumi whenever the subject turned toward her siblings.

Corin answered honestly.

> "...Because if I answer..."

She paused.

> "...I'll want to go."

Another silence followed.

> "...And I don't know if I can."

The attending psychologist noted that Corin's refusal is not rooted in resentment.

Rather, it reflects her fear that reconnecting with her family will require confronting emotions she has spent months avoiding.

---

# **ENTITY BEHAVIOR**

Lumi has never attempted to compel Corin's decisions.

She has never issued demands.

She has never expressed anger when ignored.

Instead, she simply remains nearby.

Sometimes reading over Corin's shoulder.

Sometimes making tea.

Sometimes sitting quietly in the office while Corin works late into the night.

If Corin refuses to speak, Lumi respects the silence.

When Corin finally does speak again, Lumi answers as though no time has passed.

---

# **THE LOCKWOOD SIBLINGS**

Norma remains unaware of how many times Lumi has encouraged Corin to visit.

Arcturus does not know that nearly every evening ends with the same quiet conversation.

Neither sibling blames Corin for staying away.

Both continue to hope she will return when she is ready.

Lumi has never asked either of them to pressure Corin.

Instead, she repeatedly tells them only one thing.

> "Keep the door open."

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the latest observation logs before adding a handwritten note.

> "Persistence should never become pressure."

He continued.

> "Lumi has demonstrated remarkable restraint."

> "She reminds."

> "She encourages."

> "She waits."

He signed beneath the note.

> "Sometimes waiting for someone is the greatest act of love."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Lumi continues to accompany Corin throughout her daily routine while remaining a familiar presence within Heaven's Blacksite.

Although Corin has repeatedly ignored Lumi's gentle encouragement to reunite with Norma and Arcturus, the entity has never stopped asking.

Not because she expects an answer every time.

But because she hopes that one day...

Corin's answer will be,

> "...Okay."

**End of Lumi Entity Observation Log – Addendum 02**
