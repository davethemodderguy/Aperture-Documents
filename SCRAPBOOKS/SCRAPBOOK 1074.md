# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### SECURITY, WELLNESS & RESIDENTIAL OPERATIONS DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-NERI-179  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESIDENTIAL / PERSONNEL INTERACTION  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**SUBJECT:** Plush Neri — Increased Attention From General Blacksite Personnel  
**LOCATION:** Sector 4-C SAFEHOUSE / ADJACENT STAFF AREAS

---

# Executive Summary

Plush Neri has attracted a considerable amount of attention from personnel who do not normally interact with SAFEHOUSE residents.

This includes:

- maintenance workers;
    
- cafeteria staff;
    
- security personnel;
    
- laboratory technicians;
    
- archivists;
    
- cleaners;
    
- administrative employees;
    
- engineering staff;
    
- delivery personnel;
    
- and several employees who had apparently found a reason to be in Sector 4-C despite having no operational reason to be there.
    

The cause is straightforward.

Plush Neri is a miniature, chibi version of Neri with:

- long white hair;
    
- an oversized hoodie;
    
- a seal tail;
    
- fabric skin;
    
- round glasses;
    
- tiny limbs;
    
- and an extremely soft voice.
    

She is, objectively, difficult to ignore.

The resulting personnel behavior has been described as:

> **"Everyone wants to see the tiny Neri."**

---

# 1. Initial Contact

The first notable increase in attention occurred when a maintenance worker encountered Neri sitting on the floor outside the SAFEHOUSE.

The worker stopped.

Neri looked up.

The worker looked down.

Approximately six seconds passed.

The worker eventually asked:

> "Uh... is that Neri?"

Neri nodded.

The worker immediately crouched down.

> "You're... really small."

Neri stared at him.

The worker realized this was probably not an appropriate thing to say.

He stood back up.

He then spent the next thirty seconds looking at her anyway.

---

# 2. The Rumor Spread

Within several hours, employees who had never previously met Neri had heard about the new plush form.

The most common descriptions included:

> "Tiny Neri."

> "The little seal girl."

> "The plush researcher."

> "The tiny scientist."

> "Neri, but portable."

The final description was rejected by Neko.

His exact response:

> "She's not equipment."

---

# 3. Employees Started Stopping

Personnel began encountering Neri in ordinary areas of the Blacksite.

She could be found:

- sitting outside the cafeteria;
    
- lying on a couch;
    
- wandering through a hallway;
    
- sitting on a table;
    
- resting in the library;
    
- drinking coffee;
    
- eating;
    
- or simply lying on the floor.
    

Workers would frequently stop when they noticed her.

Some simply stared.

Others asked if they could say hello.

A smaller number asked whether they could pick her up.

Neri generally responded according to her preference.

If she raised her arms:

**Permission granted.**

If she remained where she was:

**Leave her there.**

If she moved away:

**Conversation concluded.**

---

# 4. The Cafeteria Problem

The cafeteria became the most common location for personnel encounters.

Neri would sit at a table with her coffee.

Employees would walk past.

Then stop.

Then look back.

Then realize she had noticed them looking.

Neri would continue drinking her coffee.

One employee eventually asked:

> "Does she always drink coffee?"

Cafeteria staff answered:

> "Yes."

The employee looked at the tiny plush seal holding a coffee cup with both hands.

> "...That's adorable."

Neri's ears flattened.

The employee immediately corrected himself.

> "I mean—uh—professionally notable."

Neri continued drinking.

---

# 5. The "Can I Pet Her?" Problem

This question became sufficiently common that C.O.R.E. issued a clarification.

Neri is not a facility animal.

She is not a mascot.

She is not a toy.

She is not public property.

She is a resident.

Personnel are therefore **not** authorized to touch, pick up, hug, carry, or otherwise handle her without her consent.

Neri's plush appearance does not change this.

The relevant rule remains:

> **If Neri wants physical contact, she will indicate it.**

---

# 6. Surprisingly, Neri Does Not Seem to Mind

The increased attention does not appear to bother Neri as much as expected.

This is notably different from her normal-form behavior.

She may simply sit there while employees walk past and quietly observe her.

She does not appear obligated to interact.

She does not need to answer questions.

She does not need to entertain anyone.

If she wants to leave, she leaves.

If she wants to stay, she stays.

If someone asks whether she is okay with them sitting nearby, she can simply nod or shake her head.

The attention therefore remains tolerable largely because Neri retains control over the interaction.

---

# 7. A Particularly Popular Activity

Neri has unintentionally developed a following among cafeteria employees.

Not an official following.

Not a club.

Not a fan group.

C.O.R.E. explicitly refuses to acknowledge the existence of the unofficial document titled:

> **"Neri Sightings — Sector 4-C"**

The document contains timestamps and locations where employees have encountered Plush Neri.

It has been confiscated twice.

It has reappeared three times.

Neko has requested that whoever keeps recreating it stop.

They have not.

---

# 8. Normal Employees Meeting Neri

A common interaction now follows this pattern:

**EMPLOYEE:**  
"Hello, Neri."

**NERI:**  
_looks up_

**EMPLOYEE:**  
"How are you?"

**NERI:**  
_shrugs_

**EMPLOYEE:**  
"Okay."

_Neri continues drinking coffee._

**EMPLOYEE:**  
"...Can I sit here?"

_Neri considers._

_Nods._

The employee sits.

Neither speaks.

After approximately ten minutes, the employee leaves.

Neri continues drinking coffee.

This is considered a successful interaction.

---

# 9. Neri's Reputation Among Staff

Neri's reputation among ordinary personnel has changed considerably.

Before the plush transformation, many employees knew her primarily as:

> **The unusually quiet ten-year-old chemistry researcher who somehow consumed twelve terabytes of storage in one month.**

Now they know her as:

> **The unusually quiet tiny chemistry researcher who somehow consumes no storage whatsoever while being a plushie.**

The latter is considerably more popular.

---

# 10. Neko's Position

Neko has expressed mixed feelings.

On one hand:

**Neri is adorable.**

On the other:

**Everyone keeps interrupting his work to tell him that Neri is adorable.**

Neko's official statement:

> "Yes, I know."

> "Yes, I've seen her."

> "Yes, she's cute."

> "No, you don't need to send me another picture."

> "Please stop."

A message was subsequently sent to Neko containing another picture.

---

# 11. Security Considerations

Security personnel have been instructed that increased personnel interest does not constitute an incident.

However:

- crowds are not permitted to form around Neri;
    
- personnel must not block corridors to observe her;
    
- personnel must not follow her;
    
- personnel must not repeatedly request interaction;
    
- personnel must not attempt to carry her without permission;
    
- and personnel must not interfere with her decision to leave.
    

Neri is permitted to be alone.

Being popular does not mean she is available.

---

# 12. Most Recent Observation

At approximately 17:42, Plush Neri was sitting on a cafeteria table.

Three employees walked past.

All three stopped.

Neri looked at them.

One employee waved.

Neri waved back.

The second employee quietly said:

> "Oh my god."

The first employee whispered:

> "She's waving."

The third employee asked:

> "Is she allowed to have coffee?"

Cafeteria staff answered:

> "She's been having coffee for ages."

Neri continued drinking.

The three employees eventually left.

Approximately two minutes later, Neri rolled onto her back and fell asleep.

The cafeteria continued operating around her.

Nobody woke her.

---

# Final Assessment

Plush Neri has become unexpectedly popular among ordinary Blacksite personnel.

She attracts attention simply by existing.

There is no indication that she is deliberately seeking attention.

She does not perform for personnel.

She does not socialize on command.

She does not need to be entertaining.

She simply happens to be:

**tiny, soft, quiet, white-haired, hoodie-wearing, seal-tailed, glasses-wearing Neri.**

And apparently that is enough to stop half the Blacksite workforce in their tracks.

C.O.R.E. therefore issues the following reminder:

> **You may think Plush Neri is adorable.**

> **You may quietly appreciate Plush Neri.**

> **You may say hello if she permits interaction.**

> **You may not turn her into a workplace attraction.**

Neri is a resident.

Not a mascot.

Not a toy.

And definitely not a break-room exhibit.
