# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL HEALTH OBSERVATION LOG**

# **LUMI — TEMPORARY ILLNESS EVENT**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Lumi  
**Status:** Under Observation

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has documented a rare event involving **Lumi**, the creator of the Z-335 Mart colony and mother of:

- Arcturus.
    
- Norma.
    
- Corin.
    
- Corey.
    

Lumi has become ill.

The event has attracted significant attention from both personnel and the Mart colony.

---

# **MEDICAL STATUS**

The illness has been determined to be temporary.

No anomalous deterioration has been detected.

Lumi remains conscious and capable of communication, but has reduced activity levels compared to normal.

Medical staff have recommended:

- Rest.
    
- Hydration.
    
- Reduced workload.
    
- Avoiding unnecessary exertion.
    

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

The Mart colony reacted immediately upon learning Lumi was unwell.

Despite normally being distributed throughout the KCA and Blacksite, Marts began quietly gathering near Lumi's location.

Their behavior changed noticeably.

Normally energetic Marts became:

- Quieter.
    
- More careful.
    
- More attentive.
    

They refer to Lumi as:

> "...Mom."

---

# **MART CARE BEHAVIOR**

Marts have attempted to assist Lumi in their own ways.

Observed behaviors include:

- Bringing blankets.
    
- Staying nearby.
    
- Maintaining comfortable room temperatures.
    
- Sitting beside her.
    
- Keeping the area calm.
    
- Attempting to provide companionship.
    

Several Marts attempted to cook.

The results were described by personnel as:

> "Surprisingly acceptable."

---

# **LUMI'S REACTION**

Despite being the one requiring care, Lumi remained more concerned about the Marts.

When she saw hundreds gathered nearby, she attempted to reassure them.

> "...I'm okay."

A Mart immediately responded:

> "...Mom sick."

Lumi smiled.

> "...Only for a little while."

---

# **COREY'S RESPONSE**

Corey was especially concerned.

After seeing the Marts surrounding Lumi, she quietly stayed nearby.

When asked why she was not working in Engineering, she answered:

> "...She made all of this."

She looked toward the Marts.

> "...And she made me."

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin temporarily reduced her KCA accounting workload to spend more time with Lumi.

Her approach was practical:

- Managing schedules.
    
- Ensuring Lumi receives proper care.
    
- Organizing assistance.
    

When asked if she was worried, Corin replied:

> "...Of course."

---

# **ARCTURUS AND NORMA**

Both Arcturus and Norma were informed of Lumi's condition.

Their responses were similar.

They immediately prioritized visiting her.

The family gathering was noted as unusual due to how rarely all members are simultaneously available.

---

# **MART COLONY OBSERVATION**

Researchers noted an important behavioral pattern.

The Marts, despite being a hivemind colony, displayed individual expressions of concern.

They did not simply receive an instruction to care for Lumi.

They chose to.

This reinforces previous observations that Marts possess emotional understanding despite their simple communication style.

---

# **NEKO'S COMMENT**

Neko inspected the situation from the engineering area.

He observed hundreds of Marts quietly surrounding Lumi's room.

After a moment, he stated:

> "...They really care."

---

# **LUNA'S RESPONSE**

During nighttime, Corey manifested as Luna while visiting Lumi.

The lunar gravitational field caused many Marts to gather around both of them.

Lumi looked at the scene and smiled.

> "...Even when I'm sick..."

> "...They still make things brighter."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Lumi remains under observation.

Recovery is expected.

The Blacksite has temporarily adapted around her condition.

The Marts have established an unofficial rule:

> **"Mom rests."**

Personnel have been instructed not to disturb Lumi unless necessary.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

For the first time since their creation, the Marts were not the ones being protected.

They were the protectors.

Thousands of small blue figures quietly surrounded their creator.

No orders were given.

No protocol was activated.

They simply stayed.

Because to them, Lumi was not just their creator.

She was:

> **Mom.**

**End of Personnel Health Observation Log**
