# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **SECURITY DIVISION – MAINTENANCE NOTICE**

### **FACILITY MEMORANDUM**

# **RAPID RESPONSE MART RETRIEVAL**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following Incident KCA-IR-163, Security personnel noted that several Rapid Response Marts failed to report back to the ventilation network.

An immediate security review confirmed:

The missing Marts had not disappeared.

They had simply...

Locked themselves inside the KCA vault.

---

# **DISCOVERY**

Approximately twenty minutes after the incident, Camera Mart noticed several familiar blue shapes on the vault's internal security cameras.

One Mart was bouncing against the inside of the vault door.

Another appeared to be inspecting shelves.

A third was sitting on top of a stack of secured gold bars.

Camera Mart calmly reported:

> "...Vault."

---

# **HOW IT HAPPENED**

Security footage revealed that, during the response to the robbery, several Rapid Response Marts pursued fleeing suspects directly into the vault area.

When the automatic security lockdown engaged, the reinforced vault door closed behind them.

The suspects had already been subdued outside.

The Marts had not.

---

# **INSIDE THE VAULT**

When the vault was reopened, personnel found six Rapid Response Marts.

Their activities included:

- Sitting on gold bars.
    
- Looking at stacks of cash.
    
- Organizing cheese crackers they had somehow brought with them.
    
- Quietly talking to each other.
    
- Waiting patiently.
    

No attempts had been made to damage or access secured property.

One Mart had constructed a very small chair out of bundled paperwork.

Its structural integrity was surprisingly impressive.

---

# **RECOVERY**

Upon opening the vault door, one Mart cheerfully announced:

> "Hello!"

Another drifted toward the exit.

> "Outside!"

A third looked at Security personnel.

> "...Sorry."

All six voluntarily exited the vault.

No further action was required.

---

Watching the recording remotely:

---

# **SECURITY ASSESSMENT**

The incident exposed no weaknesses in the vault's security.

The Marts:

- Did not bypass security systems.
    
- Did not interfere with stored assets.
    
- Did not attempt to leave by force.
    

They simply waited until someone opened the door.

Security classified the incident as:

> **"Accidental self-containment."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

As the last Mart left the vault, it looked back at the enormous stacks of valuables.

A security officer jokingly asked:

> "...Didn't you want the gold?"

The Mart tilted its head.

Then reached into a small pouch.

It proudly held up a sugary cheese cracker.

> **"Better."**

The vault door closed.

The Marts happily bounced back toward the ventilation system, apparently convinced that the mission had been completed successfully.

**End of Maintenance Notice**
