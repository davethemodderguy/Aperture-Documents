# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / BLACKSITE LUMINOSITY EVENT**

# **GIVE CORIN HOPE, SHE GIVES YOU LIGHT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **RECOVERY CONFIRMED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The change was gradual.

Then one evening, the Blacksite noticed.

A Feather Lantern that had remained dim for months suddenly became brighter.

Then another.

Then another.

Within minutes, the effect had spread throughout the Blacksite.

**Corin's Feather Lanterns were glowing again.**

Not at their former intensity.

But significantly brighter than before.

---

# **THE LIGHT**

The azure light returned first to the lanterns closest to the Treehouse.

Then to those positioned throughout the surrounding corridors.

Then to the distant lanterns maintained in other sections of the Blacksite.

No physical connection exists between them.

No centralized activation signal was detected.

The only common factor was their origin.

**Corin's feathers.**

---

# **THE CAUSE**

C.O.R.E. initially suspected a change in the feathers themselves.

Testing found no meaningful alteration in their physical composition.

The feathers remained dormant.

They were simply...

**brighter.**

When Corin was asked why, she shrugged.

> **"I don't know."**

She thought for a moment.

Then:

> **"Maybe I'm feeling better."**

---

# **THE PATTERN**

Personnel began noticing a correlation.

When Corin was happy, the lanterns became slightly brighter.

When she laughed, they brightened further.

When she became upset, they dimmed.

When she spent time with people she trusted, they stabilized.

The effect was subtle.

But unmistakable.

---

# **THE OLD CORIN**

Corin has slowly begun doing things she used to do.

She draws.

Badly.

She collects flowers.

She flies.

Poorly.

She makes Feather Lanterns.

She talks more.

She complains more.

She laughs more.

She occasionally gets sick and immediately complains about it.

Lumi described this as:

> **"A good sign."**

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi has begun drawing with Corin again.

Corin's artistic ability has not improved significantly.

Lumi remains amused by this.

> **"She still doesn't have a natural for arts."**

This time, Lumi was smiling when she said it.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has also noticed.

Corin now occasionally approaches her first.

Sometimes she sits beside her.

Sometimes she asks her to read the research notes.

Sometimes she simply says:

> **"Wanna go somewhere?"**

The question is small.

But Orphie understands what it means.

Corin is beginning to **want things again.**

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

Corin added a new entry.

> **"I think I'm getting better."**

Underneath:

> **"Not fixed."**

Then:

> **"Just better."**

She paused.

Then wrote:

> **"That's okay."**

---

# **THE FEATHER LANTERNS**

The lanterns throughout the Blacksite now provide a faint azure illumination.

Personnel have begun deliberately leaving several corridors dark at night just to observe the effect.

Corin's feathers illuminate them naturally.

No batteries.

No fuel.

No external power.

Just the strange light that comes from something once thought to be completely dormant.

One technician summarized the phenomenon:

> **"We gave her a little hope."**

He looked at the lantern above him.

> **"She gave the whole Blacksite a night-light."**

---

# **THE MEANING**

The phrase has since become informally associated with Corin:

> **"Give Corin hope, she gives you light."**

It was never intended as a scientific principle.

But the Blacksite has begun treating it like one.

Not because Corin is a power source.

Because her light seems to reflect something much simpler.

When Corin has something worth looking forward to...

she shines.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin isn't completely healed.

She still has bad days.

She still remembers the snow.

She still remembers waiting.

She still has moments where her wings close around her and she needs to be alone.

But now she comes back.

She draws.

She flies.

She collects flowers.

She talks.

She laughs.

She hopes.

And throughout the Blacksite, tiny azure lights continue appearing in the darkness.

One lantern.

Then another.

Then hundreds.

The same feathers that once went completely dark are illuminating the halls again.

Corin once lived on hope.

Then she lost it.

Now she has begun finding it again.

And as she does...

the Blacksite gets a little brighter.

> **"Give Corin hope, she gives you light."**

For once, the saying isn't a warning.

It's a promise.
