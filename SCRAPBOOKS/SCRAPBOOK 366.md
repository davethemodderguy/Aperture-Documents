# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL PROFILE REVISION**

# **COREY / SOLARIA — FIRST CONFIRMED MANIFESTATION**

**Classification:** Level 4 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corey / Solaria

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has now documented a confirmed manifestation of **Solaria**.

Previous observations established that Solaria can manifest when Corey is exposed to sunlight.

A further condition has now been identified:

> **Corey's head must be exposed to sunlight.**

Hats, including Corey's preferred beanies, berets, oversized Mart top hat, and other headwear, appear to prevent or interrupt the manifestation.

Once sufficient sunlight reaches Corey's head, Solaria can manifest normally.

---

# **MANIFESTATION**

The transformation was observed during an outdoor laboratory test.

Corey was standing in direct sunlight while wearing her usual beret.

No manifestation occurred.

After removing the beret, sunlight reached her head.

The surrounding temperature began increasing.

Within seconds, Corey's appearance changed.

The **right eyepatch** manifested.

Her posture straightened.

Her demeanor became noticeably more assured.

The individual standing before the researchers identified herself as:

> **"Solaria."**

---

# **SOLAR SPEAR**

During the manifestation, Solaria produced a weapon previously unknown to C.O.R.E.

A **spear constructed entirely from sunlight** appeared in her hand.

The weapon emits intense radiant energy while maintaining a solid physical form.

Its exact composition remains unknown.

The spear appears to be directly connected to Solaria's solar magic rather than being a conventional physical object.

When Solaria releases it, the spear dissipates into concentrated light.

No permanent material remains.

---

# **SOLARIA'S PERSONALITY**

Further interaction has confirmed that Solaria differs from Corey beyond simple confidence.

Compared with Corey, Solaria demonstrates:

- Greater confidence.
    
- Greater decisiveness.
    
- Higher apparent intelligence.
    
- Greater wisdom.
    
- More direct communication.
    
- Greater comfort with her magical abilities.
    

Despite these differences, Solaria remains recognizably Corey.

Her memories, values, and relationships remain intact.

---

# **THE MEMORY CONNECTION**

Solaria demonstrated complete awareness of Corey's memories.

When asked about Corey's recent work with Neko, Solaria accurately described several ongoing engineering projects.

When asked about the Marts living with Corey and Corin, she smiled.

> "...They're adorable."

A Mart immediately replied:

> "...Solaria."

She looked down.

> "...Yes?"

> "...Warm."

Solaria laughed.

> "...You noticed."

---

# **THE SPEAR**

Researchers attempted to determine whether the spear was permanently stored somewhere.

It is not.

Solaria explained:

> "...I make it."

The researcher asked:

> "From what?"

She raised the spear slightly.

> "...Sunlight."

The researcher asked whether it could exist without sunlight.

Solaria considered the question.

> "...Probably."

She paused.

> "...But I wouldn't recommend testing that."

The researcher immediately stopped asking.

---

# **ACTIVATION REQUIREMENT**

Current evidence indicates the simplest known activation method is:

> **Expose Corey's uncovered head to sunlight.**

Headwear appears to prevent the transformation.

This explains why Corey's extensive collection of hats has historically prevented accidental manifestations.

C.O.R.E. has therefore updated Corey's profile accordingly.

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

The Marts were fascinated by the spear.

Several gathered around Solaria while maintaining a safe distance.

One pointed toward the weapon.

> "...Big light."

Another:

> "...Spear."

A third:

> "...Pretty."

Solaria smiled.

> "...Thank you."

The Marts immediately began repeating:

> "...Spear."

> "...Sun spear."

> "...Big light spear."

The Ambassador eventually intervened.

> "...Please stop naming it."

They did not stop.

---

# **STAR'S COMMENT**

Star watched Solaria demonstrate the spear.

> "...That's new."

Solaria smiled.

> "...Apparently."

Star looked toward Corey—who, at this point, was technically not the one standing there.

> "...Are you stronger than her?"

Solaria paused.

> "...That's not really the right question."

Star raised an eyebrow.

Solaria continued:

> "...We're the same person."

Star smiled.

> "...Fair."

---

# **COREY'S POST-MANIFESTATION RESPONSE**

When Solaria eventually returned control to Corey, Corey retained all memories of the manifestation.

She immediately looked down at herself.

Then at her missing spear.

Then at her beret.

> "...So I made a sunlight spear."

The nearby Mart nodded.

> "...Big spear."

Corey pulled her beret back onto her head.

> "...I'm keeping the hat on."

The Mart nodded.

> "...Good hat."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Solaria phenomenon is now classified as a **stable alternate manifestation state**.

### **Confirmed**

- Solaria is Corey's alter ego.
    
- Sunlight can trigger the transformation.
    
- Corey's head must be exposed to the sunlight.
    
- Headwear prevents activation.
    
- Solaria manifests with a right eyepatch.
    
- Her presence increases local temperature.
    
- She possesses solar-based magic.
    
- She can create a sunlight-based spear.
    
- Corey and Solaria share memories.
    
- Solaria is wiser, smarter, and more confident than Corey's normal state.
    

### **Unknown**

- When Solaria first developed.
    
- Why the alter ego exists.
    
- Why the right eyepatch manifests.
    
- The full capabilities of the solar spear.
    
- The upper limits of Solaria's magic.
    
- Whether additional solar weapons or abilities exist.
    

---

A small warning has been added to Corey's personnel profile:

> **DO NOT REMOVE COREY'S HAT WITHOUT HER PERMISSION.**

Not because the hat is dangerous.

Quite the opposite.

It may be the only thing keeping a solar mage from accidentally manifesting in the middle of a laboratory.

Neko reportedly read the warning, looked at Corey, then at her oversized Mart hat.

> "...Useful."

Corey nodded.

> "...Very."

A Mart sitting on Corey's shoulder added:

> "...Hat protects."

Corey smiled.

> "...Exactly."
