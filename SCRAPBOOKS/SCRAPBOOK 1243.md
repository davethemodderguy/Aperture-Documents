# 1. SUMMARY

Further observation of Invictus Aeterna has confirmed that conventional damage assessment methods are insufficient.

Aeterna's armored body demonstrates an extreme level of durability.

For practical purposes:

> **Invictus Aeterna is considered functionally indestructible.**

However, "indestructible" does not mean her armor can never experience visible damage.

Her manifested armor may:

- scratch;
    
- deform;
    
- lose surface material;
    
- sustain cosmetic damage;
    
- require restoration after extreme events.
    

When this occurs, Aeterna does not require conventional repairs.

She restores her armor through:

> **Material assimilation.**

---

# 2. ARMOR REGENERATION

Aeterna's armor is not repaired by external machinery.

No welding is required.

No forging process is required.

No replacement plates are installed.

Instead, Aeterna consumes compatible materials and converts them into her own armor structure.

The process appears to restore:

- damaged armor plating;
    
- surface markings;
    
- structural integrity;
    
- damaged sections;
    
- and anomalous properties.
    

---

# 3. COMMON MATERIALS

Aeterna has demonstrated a preference for:

### Platinum

Platinum is the most commonly accepted material for routine restoration.

Reasons appear to include:

- high purity;
    
- durability;
    
- resistance to corrosion;
    
- compatibility with Aeterna's spiritual structure.
    

### Nekonium

Nekonium is considered the highest-quality restoration material currently available.

When Aeterna consumes Nekonium:

- regeneration speed increases;
    
- armor stability improves;
    
- anomalous properties strengthen;
    
- restoration quality exceeds normal levels.
    

C.O.R.E. notes that Aeterna does not require Nekonium.

She simply considers it preferable.

---

# 4. OBSERVED RESTORATION EVENT

Following the missile interception incident involving Nora Tachyon, Aeterna sustained minor armor damage.

The damage consisted of:

- surface scoring;
    
- small plate deformation;
    
- cape damage.
    

Personnel expected Aeterna to request maintenance.

She did not.

Instead, several hours later, Aeterna entered a storage area.

She acquired several platinum bars.

A security officer observed the event.

---

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
“Aeterna?”

Aeterna stopped.

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
“What are you doing?”

Pause.

**AETERNA:**  
“Repairing.”

The officer looked at the platinum bars.

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
“You mean...”

Aeterna nodded.

**AETERNA:**  
“Yes.”

---

The event was documented.

The platinum was consumed.

No conventional digestion process occurred.

The material simply disappeared into Aeterna's manifested form.

Within several minutes:

- armor scratches disappeared;
    
- damaged plating restored itself;
    
- structural readings returned to previous levels.
    

---
