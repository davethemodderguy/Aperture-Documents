# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Phoenix has died 2,580 times.

This is an established and accurately maintained count.

Her ability to revive has made death a recurring inconvenience rather than a permanent conclusion. Unfortunately, it has also created a tendency among certain residents to regard potentially fatal accidents as problems that can be resolved after the fact.

Jake does not share this attitude.

When Phoenix recently lost her footing on the SAFEHOUSE stairwell, Jake intervened before she could complete the fall.

He opened a spatial rift.

He repositioned himself beneath her trajectory.

He caught her in his arms.

And then, while still holding her, he kissed her.

The kiss was not accidental this time.

It was deliberate.

Brief, gentle, and apparently motivated by the immediate relief of having caught her safely.

Phoenix's response was considerably more complicated.

She did not fall down the remaining stairs.

She did not require medical attention.

She did not die for the 2,581st time.

Instead, she remained in Jake's arms, staring at him with an expression that several witnesses independently described as **"a complete system failure."**

C.O.R.E. has determined that Jake's intervention was appropriate, his spatial manipulation was effective, and Phoenix's subsequent behavior was not conducive to maintaining a normal conversation.

# 1. THE STAIRWELL INCIDENT

**TIME:** 16:42  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — CENTRAL STAIRWELL

Phoenix was descending the stairs.

She was carrying several items and apparently paying insufficient attention to the steps ahead.

Her foot slipped.

She attempted to recover her balance.

She failed.

For approximately one second, Phoenix was falling backward down the stairwell.

Given the height involved, the possibility of serious injury was real.

Phoenix's regenerative and revival capabilities would have made even a fatal outcome survivable in the long term.

However, that was not a particularly good reason to allow it to happen.

Jake happened to be nearby.

He looked up.

Saw Phoenix falling.

And moved.

There was no shouted warning.

No attempt to grab the railing.

No time for either.

The air beside the stairwell distorted.

A narrow spatial rift opened.

Jake disappeared from his original position and emerged directly beneath Phoenix, already moving to intercept her.

He caught her securely.

One arm supported her back.

The other caught her beneath her knees.

Phoenix's momentum carried them a short distance before Jake stabilized himself.

His feet touched the landing.

The rift closed behind him.

Silence.

Phoenix stared at Jake.

Jake stared back.

**JAKE:**  
"Got you."

Phoenix blinked.

**PHOENIX:**  
"...You—"

**JAKE:**  
"Are you hurt?"

Phoenix opened her mouth.

Closed it.

Looked down at the floor.

Looked at Jake again.

**PHOENIX:**  
"I..."

Jake adjusted his hold to make sure she was comfortable.

**JAKE:**  
"Your head hit anything?"

**PHOENIX:**  
"No."

**JAKE:**  
"Neck?"

**PHOENIX:**  
"No."

**JAKE:**  
"Okay."

He exhaled.

His ears, which had flattened during the rescue, slowly returned to their usual position.

His tail flicked once behind him.

He had been frightened.

Not because Phoenix was incapable of surviving a fatal accident.

Because she had been falling, and he had not wanted her to get hurt.

Phoenix noticed the expression on his face.

Her own expression softened.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Jake..."

He looked at her.

Then, without warning, he leaned forward and kissed her cheek.

A deliberate, affectionate kiss.

He pulled back.

**JAKE:**  
"Glad I caught you."

Phoenix stopped functioning.

Not metaphorically.

Well, metaphorically.

She was still breathing. Her pulse was present. Her regenerative abilities were irrelevant.

But her ability to produce a coherent sentence appeared to have disappeared.

Jake waited.

**JAKE:**  
"...Phoenix?"

She stared at him.

Her face became progressively redder.

**PHOENIX:**  
"You—"

A pause.

**PHOENIX:**  
"You kissed me."

Jake blinked.

**JAKE:**  
"Yeah."

**PHOENIX:**  
"YOU KISSED ME."

Jake's ears tilted backward.

**JAKE:**  
"...Yes?"

Phoenix buried her face against his shoulder.

Jake froze.

He looked down at her.

**JAKE:**  
"Are you hurt?"

From the stairwell entrance, a voice answered before Phoenix could.

**CLOVER:**  
"NO, SHE'S JUST EXPERIENCING A DIFFERENT KIND OF FATALITY."

Jake looked up.

Clover was standing at the top of the stairs.

She had witnessed the entire incident.

She was already laughing.

# 2. PHOENIX'S SECOND CRISIS

Clover descended the stairs.

Slowly.

Deliberately.

She stopped several steps away from Jake, who was still holding Phoenix.

Phoenix had not lifted her head.

**CLOVER:**  
"So."

No response.

**CLOVER:**  
"You almost fell down the stairs."

Phoenix made a muffled noise against Jake's shoulder.

**CLOVER:**  
"Jake caught you."

Another muffled noise.

**CLOVER:**  
"Then he kissed you."

Phoenix tightened her grip on Jake's shirt.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Please stop talking."

Clover smiled.

**CLOVER:**  
"Should I call the medical department?"

**PHOENIX:**  
"NO."

**CLOVER:**  
"Psychological welfare?"

**PHOENIX:**  
"CLOVER."

**CLOVER:**  
"Cardiac monitoring?"

Jake immediately looked concerned.

**JAKE:**  
"Wait. Is your heart okay?"

Phoenix lifted her head so quickly that her hair shifted out of place.

**PHOENIX:**  
"YES!"

Jake stared.

Phoenix stared back.

Her face was bright red.

**JAKE:**  
"...Okay."

He looked toward Clover.

**JAKE:**  
"Why's she acting weird?"

Clover made a noise that was almost certainly not a normal human laugh.

**CLOVER:**  
"OH, YOU POOR, POOR BOY."

Jake's ears flattened.

**JAKE:**  
"What?"

**CLOVER:**  
"NOTHING."

Phoenix looked ready to launch Clover into another dimension.

Jake, meanwhile, had begun to wonder whether catching Phoenix had somehow injured her after all.

He looked down at her again.

**JAKE:**  
"Do you want me to put you down?"

Phoenix immediately shook her head.

Then realized what she had done.

Then froze.

Jake waited patiently.

**PHOENIX:**  
"...No."

**JAKE:**  
"Okay."

And just like that, he continued holding her.

Phoenix's face disappeared against his shoulder again.

Clover had to sit down on the stairs.

# 3. THE DEATH COUNT

C.O.R.E. considers it necessary to clarify one point.

Phoenix's established death count remains **2,580**.

The stairwell incident did not result in death.

Jake's intervention prevented the fall from progressing into an uncontrolled impact.

While Phoenix's revival capability would likely have allowed her to recover even from a catastrophic injury, revival is not an acceptable substitute for accident prevention.

Phoenix's previous deaths have not rendered her immune to pain, fear, or the consequences of traumatic injuries.

Nor does her ability to return make every preventable accident inconsequential.

Jake's decision to intervene was therefore correct.

The alternative would have been allowing Phoenix to fall, potentially suffer a serious injury, revive if necessary, and then explain to her why he had chosen not to catch her.

Jake evidently considered this alternative unacceptable.

His own explanation was considerably simpler.

**C.O.R.E. WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"You knew Phoenix could revive?"

**JAKE:**  
"Yes."

**OFFICER:**  
"Then why intervene?"

Jake looked genuinely confused by the question.

**JAKE:**  
"Because she was falling."

**OFFICER:**  
"But she could have survived."

Jake's ears tilted slightly backward.

**JAKE:**  
"She could've gotten hurt."

A pause.

**JAKE:**  
"I didn't want her to."

The officer did not pursue the question.

No further explanation was required.

# 4. PHOENIX'S ACCOUNT

Phoenix was interviewed approximately twenty minutes later.

She had recovered sufficiently to speak in complete sentences.

She had not recovered sufficiently to discuss the kiss without blushing.

**C.O.R.E. WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"Do you understand why Jake intervened?"

**PHOENIX:**  
"Because I was falling."

**OFFICER:**  
"And why did he kiss you?"

Phoenix went silent.

Her face gradually turned red.

**PHOENIX:**  
"...He was relieved."

**OFFICER:**  
"That is your interpretation?"

**PHOENIX:**  
"He said he was glad he caught me."

**OFFICER:**  
"Did he explain the kiss?"

**PHOENIX:**  
"No."

**OFFICER:**  
"Have you asked him?"

Phoenix looked horrified.

**PHOENIX:**  
"HOW AM I SUPPOSED TO ASK HIM THAT?"

**OFFICER:**  
"Verbally."

Phoenix covered her face.

The officer waited.

After several seconds, she lowered her hands.

**PHOENIX:**  
"He caught me."

A pause.

**PHOENIX:**  
"He could've just let me fall and waited for me to get back up."

**OFFICER:**  
"But he did not."

**PHOENIX:**  
"No."

Her expression softened.

**PHOENIX:**  
"He looked scared."

The officer made a note.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Not scared of me. He was scared something would happen to me."

She looked down at her hands.

**PHOENIX:**  
"And then he kissed me like it was the most normal thing in the world."

A long silence followed.

**PHOENIX:**  
"...I don't know what I'm supposed to do with that."

The officer closed the report.

**OFFICER:**  
"You do not have to do anything immediately."

Phoenix nodded.

She appeared relieved.

Then she remembered the kiss again.

Her face turned red.

The interview was terminated shortly afterward.

# 5. JAKE'S ACCOUNT

Jake was interviewed separately.

He arrived carrying his teddy bear.

He sat in the chair.

His tail curled around one leg.

His ears remained upright.

He appeared mildly confused about why the incident required documentation.

**C.O.R.E. WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"Jake, do you remember catching Phoenix?"

**JAKE:**  
"Yeah."

**OFFICER:**  
"Do you remember kissing her?"

**JAKE:**  
"Yeah."

**OFFICER:**  
"Was that accidental?"

Jake considered the question.

**JAKE:**  
"No."

The officer paused.

**OFFICER:**  
"You intended to kiss her?"

**JAKE:**  
"On the cheek."

**OFFICER:**  
"Why?"

Jake looked down at his teddy bear.

He adjusted one of its ears.

**JAKE:**  
"I was glad she was okay."

**OFFICER:**  
"Was that the only reason?"

Jake thought about it for a moment.

**JAKE:**  
"...I like Phoenix."

The officer remained silent.

Jake looked up.

**JAKE:**  
"She's nice."

A pause.

**JAKE:**  
"She likes cats."

Another pause.

**JAKE:**  
"She doesn't make me do things when I don't want to."

He stroked the teddy bear's head.

**JAKE:**  
"And she looks happy when I stay with her."

The officer wrote this down.

**OFFICER:**  
"Do you understand that the kiss may have surprised her?"

**JAKE:**  
"Yeah."

**OFFICER:**  
"Would you ask before doing it again?"

Jake nodded.

**JAKE:**  
"Okay."

He considered something else.

**JAKE:**  
"But I can still catch her if she falls, right?"

The officer looked at him.

**OFFICER:**  
"Of course."

Jake relaxed.

**JAKE:**  
"Good."

The interview ended.

# 6. THE SECOND CONVERSATION

Later that evening, Phoenix found Jake sitting on the floor of his room.

His teddy bear was beside him.

He was reading.

His white hair fell over his forehead, and his ears occasionally twitched at sounds in the hallway.

Phoenix stood at the doorway.

She had rehearsed what she wanted to say.

Several times.

She had even written it down.

Unfortunately, she forgot every word as soon as Jake looked up.

**JAKE:**  
"Hi."

Phoenix gripped the doorframe.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Hi."

Jake waited.

**JAKE:**  
"You okay now?"

Phoenix's expression softened.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Yes. I'm okay."

**JAKE:**  
"Good."

He returned to his book.

Phoenix remained in the doorway.

Jake glanced up again.

**JAKE:**  
"Did you need something?"

Phoenix took a breath.

**PHOENIX:**  
"About earlier..."

Jake closed his book.

He gave her his full attention.

**JAKE:**  
"What about it?"

Phoenix's cheeks turned pink.

**PHOENIX:**  
"The kiss."

Jake waited.

**PHOENIX:**  
"I wasn't upset."

**JAKE:**  
"Okay."

**PHOENIX:**  
"I was just surprised."

**JAKE:**  
"Okay."

Phoenix looked at him helplessly.

**PHOENIX:**  
"You're not going to say anything else?"

Jake thought about it.

**JAKE:**  
"Do you want me to?"

Phoenix opened her mouth.

Closed it.

Looked away.

**PHOENIX:**  
"...I don't know."

Jake nodded.

He did not push.

After a moment, he spoke quietly.

**JAKE:**  
"I didn't want you to get hurt."

Phoenix looked back at him.

**JAKE:**  
"And I was happy I caught you."

He hesitated.

**JAKE:**  
"So I kissed you."

A small pause.

**JAKE:**  
"Sorry if it was weird."

Phoenix immediately shook her head.

**PHOENIX:**  
"No! You don't have to apologize."

Jake's ears lifted slightly.

**PHOENIX:**  
"I just... wasn't expecting it."

**JAKE:**  
"Okay."

He picked up his book again.

Phoenix remained standing there.

Then she asked, very quietly:

**PHOENIX:**  
"Can I hug you?"

Jake looked up.

He considered the question.

Then nodded.

**JAKE:**  
"Yeah."

Phoenix approached and knelt beside him.

She hugged him gently, allowing him room to move away whenever he wished.

Jake leaned into the hug.

His tail settled around his left leg.

After a moment, he rested his head against her shoulder.

**JAKE:**  
"...You're warm."

Phoenix smiled.

**PHOENIX:**  
"You've mentioned that."

**JAKE:**  
"Still true."

Phoenix closed her eyes.

For once, she managed not to turn bright red.

Almost.

# 7. CLOVER'S FINAL INTERVENTION

Clover learned about the conversation approximately fifteen minutes later.

She found Phoenix sitting quietly in the common room.

Phoenix looked peaceful.

Almost normal.

Clover sat beside her.

**CLOVER:**  
"So, did you survive?"

Phoenix stared ahead.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Mostly."

**CLOVER:**  
"Did you ask him about the kiss?"

**PHOENIX:**  
"Yes."

Clover leaned forward.

**CLOVER:**  
"And?"

Phoenix smiled faintly.

**PHOENIX:**  
"He said he was glad I was okay."

Clover waited.

**PHOENIX:**  
"And that he likes me."

Clover's eyes widened.

Phoenix immediately pointed at her.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Do not."

Clover pressed both hands over her mouth.

Her shoulders began shaking.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Clover."

Clover stood up.

She walked to the other side of the room.

She took several deep breaths.

Then she turned back.

**CLOVER:**  
"I'm happy for you."

Phoenix's expression softened.

**PHOENIX:**  
"...Thank you."

A pause.

**CLOVER:**  
"BUT YOU SHOULD HAVE SEEN YOUR FACE."

Phoenix threw a cushion at her.

Clover dodged.

The cushion struck the wall.

From the corridor, Jake's voice called out:

**JAKE:**  
"Are you two fighting?"

Both residents froze.

**PHOENIX:**  
"No!"

**CLOVER:**  
"YES!"

Phoenix stared at Clover in disbelief.

Clover smiled innocently.

Jake appeared in the doorway, holding his teddy bear.

He looked at the cushion on the floor.

Then at Phoenix.

Then at Clover.

**JAKE:**  
"...Okay."

He picked up the cushion, handed it to Phoenix, and returned to his room.

Phoenix watched him leave.

Her expression became soft again.

Clover noticed.

**CLOVER:**  
"Oh, you're completely gone."

**PHOENIX:**  
"I'm going to throw you down the stairs."

**CLOVER:**  
"YOU CAN'T. JAKE WILL CATCH ME."

Phoenix buried her face in the cushion.

Clover laughed until she could barely stand.

# C.O.R.E. FINAL ASSESSMENT

The stairwell incident has been classified as a successful emergency intervention followed by an unexpected affectionate interaction.

Jake's use of spatial rifting prevented a potentially serious accident.

Phoenix sustained no injury.

Her revival ability was not required.

Her established death count remains **2,580**.

Jake's actions demonstrate that he understands Phoenix's capacity to recover from fatal injuries does not mean he should permit preventable harm.

He did not catch her because she was incapable of surviving the fall.

He caught her because he cared whether she fell at all.

The subsequent kiss was deliberate, brief, and apparently intended as an expression of relief and affection.

Phoenix was surprised, but she has since communicated her feelings directly to Jake. Both residents appear comfortable with the interaction, and no further intervention is indicated.

C.O.R.E. recommends no restrictions on ordinary, consensual affection between residents, provided appropriate boundaries continue to be respected.

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Phoenix has died 2,580 times.

She has survived circumstances that would have killed most people permanently.

She has returned from death enough times that her own mortality is no longer a particularly reliable way to frighten her.

But when she slipped on the stairs, Jake did not calculate the probability of her revival.

He did not consider whether another death would meaningfully change the count.

He saw Phoenix falling.

He opened a rift.

He caught her.

And when he realized she was safe, he kissed her because he was happy she was still there.

Perhaps that was the part Phoenix found so difficult to process.

Not the rift.

Not the impossible speed.

Not even the kiss itself.

It was the fact that a little boy who had known loss, loneliness, and the fear of being left behind had looked at her and decided that **he did not want to lose her, either.**

Phoenix did not need to die for the 2,581st time to be reminded that someone cared whether she came back.

For once, someone had made certain she never had to leave in the first place.

And Jake?

Jake went back to his room, curled up beside his teddy bear, and fell asleep.

Completely unaware that Phoenix was sitting in the common room, smiling into a cushion while Clover repeatedly whispered the words _"He caught you and kissed you"_ until she was threatened with a second cushion.
