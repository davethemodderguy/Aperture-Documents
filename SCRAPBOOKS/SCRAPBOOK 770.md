### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE / PERSONNEL CARE DIVISION**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — RIGHT EYE INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — ANOMALOUS ABILITY / PERSONNEL SAFETY  
**Status:** Resolved  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Affected Personnel:** Corin

---

# **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

Alice accidentally used her anomalous right eye on Corin.

There was no malicious intent.

There was no deliberate command.

Alice had simply removed her eyepatch.

She looked directly at Corin.

And something happened.

---

# **THE ACCIDENT**

Corin was sitting with Alice in the SAFEHOUSE.

They were having an ordinary conversation.

Alice had been talking considerably more lately.

She was happy.

At some point, Alice's eyepatch shifted.

She reached up to fix it.

Instead, she accidentally removed it.

Corin looked toward her.

Their eyes met.

For a moment—

nothing happened.

Then Corin became unusually still.

Alice immediately noticed.

> "Big sis?"

Corin didn't respond.

Alice's expression changed.

> "...Big sis?"

---

# **ANOMALOUS RESPONSE**

The right eye's influence activated.

Corin entered a brief hypnotic state.

The exact command Alice unconsciously issued remains unclear.

No harmful command was given.

The effect ended shortly afterward.

Corin regained normal awareness.

Alice was horrified.

---

# **ALICE'S RESPONSE**

Alice immediately covered her right eye with both hands.

She began trembling.

> "Alice didn't mean to."

Corin reached toward her.

Alice backed away.

> "Alice didn't mean to!"

Her voice became louder.

> "I'm sorry!"

Corin immediately reassured her.

> "Alice, you're okay."

Alice shook her head.

> "I made Big sis do something."

> "It was an accident."

> "Still Alice's fault."

Corin disagreed.

---

# **THE EYEPATCH**

Alice picked up the eyepatch.

She put it back on.

Then tightened it.

She refused to remove it again.

Corin tried to reassure her.

> "Alice, you don't have to be afraid of your eye."

Alice shook her head.

> "No."

She held the eyepatch firmly.

> "It stays."

---

# **PERMANENT DECISION**

Alice has since made her position extremely clear.

She does not want to remove the eyepatch again.

Not for convenience.

Not to demonstrate her ability.

Not because someone asks.

Not even because she wants to see through the eye.

The eyepatch is staying on.

---

# **MEDICAL RESPONSE**

Medical personnel confirmed that Alice's right eye remains functional.

The eyepatch itself causes no medical complications.

There is therefore no medical reason to force Alice to remove it.

Her decision has been accepted.

---

# **SECURITY RESPONSE**

The incident has prompted additional restrictions concerning Alice's anomalous ability.

Personnel are prohibited from:

- Asking Alice to demonstrate the eye.
    
- Encouraging her to use it.
    
- Removing the eyepatch.
    
- Touching the eyepatch without permission.
    
- Deliberately establishing prolonged eye contact after the eyepatch has been removed.
    

If the eyepatch becomes displaced, personnel are instructed to calmly notify Alice rather than attempting to handle the situation themselves.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin does not blame Alice.

Not even slightly.

When Alice apologized again later, Corin simply hugged her.

> "You didn't do it on purpose."

Alice remained quiet.

> "But I did."

> "It was an accident."

Alice looked down.

Corin gently touched her beanie.

> "You're still my little sister."

Alice eventually whispered:

> "...Promise?"

> "Promise."

---

# **AFTER THE INCIDENT**

Alice became noticeably more protective of the eyepatch.

She checks it periodically.

If it shifts, she immediately fixes it.

If someone approaches her face, she instinctively covers the right side.

She no longer treats the eyepatch as merely clothing.

It is now a boundary.

---

# **ONE WEEK LATER**

Star asked:

> "Alice, do you still want to keep the eyepatch on?"

Alice nodded.

> "Forever?"

Alice nodded again.

Star:

> "Even when you're alone?"

> "Yes."

> "Even if you want to use your right eye?"

Alice thought about it.

Then:

> "No."

She touched the eyepatch.

> "Alice doesn't want to control people."

---

# **CORIN'S NOTE**

> "She thinks the eye makes her dangerous."

> "It doesn't."

> "Alice is dangerous to herself before she's dangerous to anyone else."

> "She has spent her entire life trying not to inconvenience people."

> "Now she's terrified that even looking at someone could hurt them."

> "We're going to make sure she understands that the accident wasn't her fault."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Alice possesses an ability that could force another person to obey her.

Yet she has never wanted that power.

She voluntarily kept it covered long before anyone required her to.

After accidentally affecting Corin, that choice became absolute.

Alice has decided:

> **The eyepatch stays.**

And nobody is going to make her take it off.

Not because the eye is evil.

Not because Alice is dangerous.

But because Alice deserves to feel safe with the parts of herself she doesn't fully understand.

The right eye remains capable of seeing.

The ability remains present.

The eyepatch remains firmly in place.

And Alice is still Alice.

**End of CORE-SAFEHOUSE / PERSONNEL CARE DIVISION**
