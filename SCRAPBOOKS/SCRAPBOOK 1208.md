# 1. OFFICIAL ANNOUNCEMENT

Neri Tachyon has officially announced the next addition to the **Limited Tachyon Industries Merchandise** collection.

Following the community survey in which **Sniper Rifle** received the highest number of votes, Neri has completed her design.

The result is:

# **FSR DEADBOLT**

The rifle is a highly customized Tachyon Industries platform incorporating design influences from multiple existing rifle concepts while introducing Neri's own materials, systems, and design philosophy.

---

# 2. NEKO'S DESCRIPTION

Neri's announcement included a short technical observation from Neko.

His description has been preserved verbatim:

> **“Imagine the M82A1, ORISIS have a kid, and it have all of their best traits.”**

C.O.R.E. notes that the grammatical structure of the statement is less technically precise than Neko's usual documentation.

The description is nevertheless considered remarkably effective.

---

# 3. PLATFORM CLASSIFICATION

The FSR Deadbolt occupies an unusual position between traditional sniper and marksman rifle classifications.

It can function as a long-range precision platform while also allowing the operator to mechanically alter its barrel velocity characteristics.

This allows the rifle to occupy different operational profiles without requiring a completely different platform.

C.O.R.E. therefore classifies the Deadbolt as:

> **SNIPER / MARKSMAN HYBRID PLATFORM**

Neri simply calls it:

> **“A rifle.”**

# 5. NEKONIUM ALLOY FRAME

The Deadbolt uses a **Nekonium Alloy frame**.

This continues Neri's established use of the material family originally developed by Neko.

The Deadbolt does not simply use raw Nekonium.

Neri's established alloy formulation incorporates additional materials to address characteristics that make raw Nekonium unsuitable for many mechanical applications.

The resulting frame provides the Deadbolt with its distinctive appearance and material identity.

Neko has confirmed that Neri's implementation is considerably more refined than the earliest Nekonium experiments.

His assessment:

> **“She actually learned how to use it.”**

Neri responded:

> **“Obviously.”**

---

# 6. INTEGRAL SUPPRESSION

The Deadbolt incorporates an **integrally suppressed configuration**.

The suppression system is part of the rifle's overall design rather than being treated as a conventional aftermarket accessory.

This contributes to the rifle's distinctive silhouette.

It also means that the Deadbolt does not require the user to select a separate conventional suppressor as part of its standard configuration.

---

# 7. OPTICS

The Deadbolt does **not** include a scope.

Neri deliberately chose not to bundle a specific optic.

Instead, the rifle incorporates an:

> **Aperture-grade Picatinny rail**

This allows authorized users to select an appropriate aftermarket optic according to their intended use.

Neri's explanation:

> **“Everyone likes different scopes.”**

Neko agreed.

The Armory has recorded this as one of the rare occasions where Neri's design decision eliminated an argument rather than creating one.

---

# 8. STOCK AND BIPOD

The Deadbolt includes:

- a foldable stock;
    
- recoil-absorbing stock characteristics;
    
- and an integrated/issued bipod.
    

The stock continues the skeletonized visual language established during Neri's study of the ORSIS T-3000.

However, the final configuration is distinctly Tachyon Industries.

Neko described the overall design as:

> **“A very strange combination that somehow works.”**

---

# 9. VELOCITY CONFIGURATION

One of the Deadbolt's defining characteristics is its mechanically adjustable barrel-velocity configuration.

The system allows the rifle to change its velocity profile without requiring the operator to replace the entire weapon.

This contributes directly to the Deadbolt's hybrid classification.

The result is a platform that can occupy a broader performance envelope than a conventional fixed-profile precision rifle.

Detailed mechanical implementation remains restricted to authorized technical documentation.

---

# 10. .458 TACHYON MAGNUM

The Deadbolt's primary ammunition system is:

> **.458 Tachyon Magnum**

The standardized NATO designation is:

> **9.5×70 mm NATO**

Neri has developed multiple specialized ammunition categories for the platform.

These are considered part of the Deadbolt's Limited Merchandise ecosystem.

---

# 11. .458 TACHYON MAGNUM S

### **S — SUBSONIC**

Designed for reduced-velocity use.

Neri describes its intended application simply as:

> **“Close range.”**

The ammunition is intended for situations where the Deadbolt's conventional high-velocity characteristics are unnecessary.

---

# 12. .458 TACHYON MAGNUM D

### **D — DECIMATOR**

A specialized anti-materiel ammunition category intended primarily for use against vehicles and other appropriate hard targets.

Neri has been unusually explicit about its restrictions.

Her note reads:

> **“Use towards vehicles, or it will be a war crime.”**

Neri has apparently read the Geneva Conventions.

C.O.R.E. has therefore classified this ammunition as **restricted**.

It is not intended for ordinary personnel use.

---

# 13. .458 TACHYON MAGNUM HE

### **HE — HIGH EXPLOSIVE**

The Deadbolt's conventional explosive ammunition category.

Neri describes it as:

> **“Classic explosive ammo.”**

No additional description was supplied.

Access remains subject to Blacksite armament controls.

---

# 14. .458 TACHYON MAGNUM GC

### **GC — GLASS CASCADE**

A highly unusual ammunition category.

The projectile produces a volley of sharpened, Nekonium-framed glass shards.

The resulting effect is sufficiently unconventional that the Armory initially questioned whether the ammunition belonged in a rifle inventory.

Neri confirmed:

> **“Yes.”**

The Armory subsequently stopped asking.

The ammunition remains restricted and is not intended for general distribution.

---

# 15. .458 TACHYON MAGNUM NDR

### **NDR — NEKONIUM DECIMATOR ROUND**

The most restricted Deadbolt ammunition currently documented.

The designation stands for:

> **Nekonium Decimator Round**

Neri's entire public explanation was:

> **“For emergencies.”**

The interviewer asked what it does.

Neri paused.

> **“Let's just say it eats.”**

No further explanation was provided.

C.O.R.E. has elected not to request clarification.

The NDR category is consequently classified as **emergency-restricted ammunition**.

# 20. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The FSR Deadbolt represents a substantial evolution of the Tachyon Industries product line.

The Night Stalker established the first generation.

Sleight of Hand expanded the concept.

Ceberus demonstrated that the Tachyon Industries identity could be applied to an entirely different platform.

Deadeye established the second-generation pistol.

Deadbolt now extends the collection into long-range rifle territory.

Its design is intentionally difficult to place into one conventional category.

It combines:

> **Sniper rifle characteristics**

with:

> **Marksman rifle flexibility**

while incorporating:

> **Nekonium construction**

> **integral suppression**

> **mechanically adjustable velocity characteristics**

> **a foldable recoil-absorbing stock**

> **a modular optic interface**

and:

> **five specialized .458 Tachyon Magnum ammunition categories.**
