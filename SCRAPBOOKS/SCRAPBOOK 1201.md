**SUBJECT:** Nora Tachyon  
**INCIDENT:** Woodland Coorp Mercenary Ambush  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE EXTERIOR RECREATIONAL AREA

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon was playing outside.

She was six years old.

She had her family journal clipped to her waist.

She was carrying her authorized Night Stalker.

She was not participating in an operation.

She was not on a mission.

She was playing.

Woodland Coorp mercenaries subsequently entered the area and attempted to ambush her.

The engagement ended with:

- **5 Woodland Coorp mercenaries killed by Nora;**
    
- additional mercenaries fleeing the area;
    
- multiple incoming projectiles intercepted by Nora;
    
- no injuries to Nora;
    
- no injuries to SAFEHOUSE residents;
    
- the surviving mercenaries retreating before MSAC intervention became necessary.
    

Neri subsequently confiscated Nora's Night Stalker.

Nora did not appear particularly concerned.

Her response to the entire incident was approximately:

> **"They attacked me."**

---

# 2. INITIAL CONDITIONS

At approximately 14:20, Nora was observed outside the SAFEHOUSE perimeter recreational area.

She had been playing independently.

Witnesses report that she had been:

- running around;
    
- examining plants;
    
- drawing in the dirt;
    
- throwing small objects;
    
- and occasionally practicing imaginary scenarios with her toy equipment.
    

Her actual Night Stalker was present due to the existing family-authorized arrangement.

No hostile activity had been detected.

At approximately 14:27, perimeter sensors detected unidentified personnel.

The individuals were subsequently identified as Woodland Coorp mercenaries.

---

# 3. THE AMBUSH

The mercenaries attempted to surround Nora.

Nora initially appeared confused.

One witness reported hearing:

> **NORA:**  
> "Who are you?"

The mercenaries did not answer.

The first attack followed shortly afterward.

Nora immediately changed behavior.

Her playful demeanor disappeared.

She became completely still.

Her posture changed.

Her attention focused entirely on the incoming threat.

This was consistent with her previously documented range behavior.

The child who had been playing moments earlier was no longer present psychologically.

The range-focused Nora had returned.

---

# 4. PROJECTILE INTERCEPTION

The initial attack included incoming projectiles.

Nora responded with remarkable precision.

She used her Night Stalker to intercept incoming threats before they could reach her.

C.O.R.E. Security review confirms that multiple incoming rounds were successfully intercepted.

A subsequent attack included an incoming rocket.

Nora reacted almost immediately.

The rocket was intercepted before reaching its intended target.

The event was recorded by perimeter surveillance.

One security officer reviewing the footage later described it as:

> **"The smallest air-defense system I've ever seen."**

The statement has not been adopted as an official designation.

---

# 5. ENGAGEMENT

Nora subsequently returned fire.

Five Woodland Coorp mercenaries were killed.

The engagement was brief.

Nora demonstrated the same extremely controlled behavior previously observed during supervised range assessments.

No unnecessary movement was observed.

No visible hesitation was observed.

No evidence indicates that Nora was firing indiscriminately.

She engaged identified hostile personnel until they were no longer an immediate threat.

The remaining mercenaries fled.

---

# 7. NORA'S BEHAVIOR

Nora remained remarkably calm after the engagement.

There was no celebration.

No excitement.

No visible distress.

No apparent attempt to conceal what had happened.

She simply looked toward the direction in which the mercenaries had fled.

Then lowered the Night Stalker.

A nearby security officer approached.

> **SECURITY:**  
> "Nora."

Nora looked at him.

> **SECURITY:**  
> "Are you hurt?"

Nora shook her head.

> **NORA:**  
> "No."

> **SECURITY:**  
> "Did they hurt you?"

> **NORA:**  
> "No."

The officer paused.

> **SECURITY:**  
> "...You killed five of them."

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> "Yes."

No additional emotional response was observed.

---

# 8. NORA'S LACK OF DISTRESS

C.O.R.E. Welfare personnel specifically evaluated Nora following the event.

Nora did not appear traumatized by the fact that she had killed the attackers.

She did not appear proud of it either.

Her response was remarkably simple.

> **NORA:**  
> "They attacked me."

She did not describe the deaths in detail.

She did not ask about the bodies.

She did not appear interested in discussing the engagement.

When asked whether she was scared:

> **NORA:**  
> "A little."

When asked whether she was angry:

> **NORA:**  
> "No."

When asked whether she understood that the mercenaries were dead:

> **NORA:**  
> "Yes."

When asked how she felt about it:

Nora shrugged.

> **NORA:**  
> "They were trying to hurt me."

The assessment concluded that Nora was not presently displaying acute psychological distress related to the incident.

Continued age-appropriate welfare monitoring remains appropriate.

---

# 9. NERI ARRIVES

Neri arrived shortly afterward.

She looked at Nora.

Then at the Night Stalker.

Then at the damaged exterior area.

> **NERI:**  
> "...What happened?"

A security officer explained.

Neri became quiet.

She looked at Nora.

> **NERI:**  
> "You're okay?"

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> "Yeah."

Neri looked at the Night Stalker.

> **NERI:**  
> "Give it to me."

Nora immediately handed it over.

There was no argument.

No resistance.

Neri secured the weapon.

---

# 10. THE CONVERSATION

Nora looked up at her sister.

> **NORA:**  
> "Why?"

Neri answered quietly.

> **NERI:**  
> "Because you're done with it."

Nora frowned.

> **NORA:**  
> "But I can use it."

> **NERI:**  
> "I know."

> **NORA:**  
> "I'm good."

> **NERI:**  
> "I know."

Neri paused.

> **NERI:**  
> "You'll get something more useful."

Nora's eyes widened.

> **NORA:**  
> "More useful than a Night Stalker?"

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> "Yes."

Nora immediately became interested.

> **NORA:**  
> "What?"

Neri looked at her.

> **NERI:**  
> "Later."

Nora frowned.

> **NORA:**  
> "What is it?"

> **NERI:**  
> "Something you can actually use."

Nora considered this.

> **NORA:**  
> "...Okay."

She then returned to playing.

Neri carried the Night Stalker away.

---

# 11. SECURITY RESPONSE

MSAC personnel initially requested permission to pursue the fleeing Woodland Coorp mercenaries.

C.O.R.E. Security declined to authorize Nora's participation in any further engagement.

Nora was returned to the SAFEHOUSE.

Her Night Stalker was placed under restricted custody pending further review.

No further confrontation occurred.

---

# 12. WELFARE OBSERVATION

The most unusual aspect of the incident was not Nora's marksmanship.

That capability had already been documented.

It was her emotional response afterward.

Nora did not behave like someone who had just completed an operation.

She behaved like a child whose afternoon had been interrupted.

Approximately twenty minutes after returning inside, she asked:

> **NORA:**  
> "Can I have cake?"

Neri looked at her.

> **NERI:**  
> "Yes."

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> "Okay."

And went to the cafeteria.

---

# 13. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The incident confirms several previously documented observations.

Nora possesses extraordinary marksmanship ability.

She can remain exceptionally focused under threat.

She can respond rapidly to incoming threats.

She is capable of intercepting incoming projectiles with extraordinary precision.

She is also still six years old.

That distinction remains extremely important.

Nora's capability does not constitute unrestricted authorization.

Her ability to defend herself does not make her an MSAC operator.

Her ability to handle a weapon does not mean she should routinely carry one.

And her lack of immediate distress following the engagement should not be interpreted as evidence that repeated exposure to lethal violence is harmless for a child.

C.O.R.E. Welfare recommends continued supervision and age-appropriate psychological observation.
