### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE — RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION LOG**

# **COREY — FRIENDLY ROOMMATES DINNER**

**Classification:** Level 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE — Communal Kitchen / Central Living Area  
**Subject:** Corey  
**Event:** Group Meal

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corey has established a recurring habit of cooking meals for the residents and frequent occupants of the SAFEHOUSE.

The group has informally become known as the:

> **Friendly Roommates**

The meals are often prepared using recipes from the cookbook inherited from Corey's mother.

The resulting dinner is one of the few occasions where nearly everyone gathers in the same room without an operational reason.

---

# **THE FRIENDLY ROOMMATES**

The current group consists of:

|Resident|General Disposition|
|---|---|
|**Bao**|Cheerful|
|**Frost**|Quiet|
|**Astral**|Wise|
|**Taiga**|Positive and happy|
|**Mart**|Quiet|
|**Star**|Constantly positive, similar to Marts|
|**Nightshade**|Quiet|
|**Belle**|Always happy|
|**Endmin**|Grounded|
|**Arcturus**|Wise|
|**Norma**|Smart|
|**Corin**|Practical / composed|
|**Lumi**|Warm and caring|
|**Cinder**|Professional|
|**Keith**|Professional|

Despite their vastly different personalities, the group functions surprisingly well together.

---

# **DINNER PREPARATION**

Corey usually begins cooking several hours before dinner.

The kitchen gradually fills with the smell of food.

Bao is usually the first to notice.

> "Corey, what are you making?"

Corey:

> "...Dinner."

Bao:

> "I know, but what kind?"

Corey:

> "...You'll see."

Bao immediately becomes more interested.

---

# **THE MARTS**

Marts are frequently present in the kitchen.

They do not necessarily help.

They simply watch.

A typical Mart may sit on the counter wearing its tiny top hat and quietly observe Corey cooking.

Another may float beside her.

Another may attempt to carry an ingredient.

Eventually:

> "...Food?"

Corey:

> "...Not yet."

> "...Okay."

The Mart waits.

---

# **BAO**

Bao is one of the most enthusiastic participants.

She often talks with Corey while she cooks.

She may assist with:

- Preparing ingredients.
    
- Carrying dishes.
    
- Setting the table.
    

She also tends to compliment nearly everything.

> "That smells amazing!"

Corey:

> "...You haven't tasted it."

> "I know."

---

# **FROST**

Frost is much quieter.

She generally sits nearby and watches.

She rarely interrupts Corey.

If she likes the food, her response is usually simple:

> "...Good."

For Frost, this is considered a strong compliment.

---

# **ASTRAL**

Astral usually observes the entire process calmly.

She sometimes offers advice, but rarely interferes.

When Corey successfully prepares a complicated recipe, Astral simply smiles.

> "...You've learned well."

Corey understands what she means.

---

# **TAIGA**

Taiga is enthusiastic from beginning to end.

She frequently asks:

> "Is it ready?"

> "Now?"

> "How about now?"

Corey's answer remains consistent:

> "...Not yet."

Taiga continues waiting happily.

---

# **STAR**

Star is almost impossible to discourage.

She approaches dinner with the same enthusiasm Marts possess.

> "Food!"

A Mart beside her:

> "...Food!"

Star:

> "FOOD!"

The Mart:

> "...Food."

Corey eventually has to remind them:

> "...It's still cooking."

---

# **NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade usually sits quietly.

She rarely speaks during preparation.

However, she remains close to Star and occasionally helps without being asked.

Her preferred contribution is simply making sure Star does not accidentally interfere with the cooking.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle is enthusiastic about everything.

She frequently compliments Corey before the food is even finished.

> "It smells so good!"

> "...Can I help?"

Corey:

> "...You can set the table."

Belle immediately begins doing so.

---

# **ENDMIN**

Endmin provides a stabilizing presence to the room.

While everyone else becomes increasingly excited about dinner, Endmin remains grounded.

> "...Let her cook."

This statement has prevented multiple unnecessary kitchen incidents.

---

# **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus approaches cooking from a more thoughtful perspective.

He occasionally discusses recipes with Corey.

His comments tend to be surprisingly insightful.

> "...Your mother's recipes have a very distinct character."

Corey:

> "...Yeah."

She smiles.

> "...That's why I like them."

---

# **NORMA**

Norma is particularly interested in the technical side of cooking.

She sometimes asks Corey about:

- Ingredient ratios.
    
- Cooking temperatures.
    
- Timing.
    
- Why particular steps are performed.
    

Corey eventually hands her the cookbook.

> "...Just read the notes."

Norma does.

> "...Oh."

> "...That actually makes sense."

---

# **CORIN**

Corin usually helps organize the meal.

She ensures everyone has a place to sit and that nobody accidentally forgets to eat.

She also keeps the Marts from occupying every available chair.

This is considerably harder than it sounds.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi is particularly fond of Corey's cooking.

The connection is somewhat sentimental.

Corey's cookbook originally belonged to Lumi's family, making the recipes more meaningful than ordinary meals.

Lumi occasionally recognizes recipes from Corey's mother.

When she does, Corey listens carefully.

---

# **CINDER**

Cinder maintains her professional demeanor throughout most of dinner preparation.

She rarely becomes visibly excited.

However, she always arrives on time.

When asked whether she enjoys Corey's cooking:

> "...It is excellent."

A pause.

> "...Thank you."

This is considered Cinder's version of enthusiasm.

---

# **KEITH**

Keith behaves similarly.

He maintains a professional attitude until the food is served.

Then:

> "...This is good."

Corey:

> "...Thanks."

Keith:

> "...Very good."

Corey:

> "...You can stop being professional now."

Keith:

> "...Understood."

---

# **THE DINNER TABLE**

Once dinner is ready, the entire room changes.

Everyone sits together.

The professionals stop discussing work.

The researchers stop discussing experiments.

The KCA personnel stop discussing accounting.

The Marts stop floating around the kitchen and gather around the table.

For a while, everyone is simply eating.

---

# **COREY'S OBSERVATION**

Corey rarely sits at the head of the table.

She prefers sitting among everyone else.

She watches the group eating the food she prepared.

Bao is smiling.

Frost is quietly eating.

Astral is enjoying the meal.

Taiga is already asking about dessert.

Star is happily talking.

Nightshade listens.

Belle is delighted.

Endmin is relaxed.

Arcturus and Norma discuss the recipe.

Corin keeps everyone organized.

Lumi smiles.

Cinder and Keith have temporarily abandoned their professional expressions.

And the Mart sits quietly beside Corey.

Corey looks around the table.

> "...This is nice."

The Mart beside her nods.

> "...Happy."

Corey smiles.

> "...Yeah."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The Friendly Roommates do not have much in common.

Some are cheerful.

Some are quiet.

Some are wise.

Some are professional.

Some are strange.

Some are literal magical water blobs.

But dinner has become their common ground.

For Corey, cooking is more than preparing food.

It is a way of bringing everyone together.

And every time the entire table is full, the SAFEHOUSE feels a little more like what it was always meant to be:

> **A home.**

**End of Project SAFEHOUSE Residential Observation Log**
