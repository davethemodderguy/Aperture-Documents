# 1. SUMMARY

Aeterna's execution blade possesses an additional property that was previously undocumented.

When Aeterna is not using the weapon, she may deliberately place it into a stationary surface and leave it there.

The sword then becomes functionally equivalent to the traditional:

> **“Sword in the Stone.”**

Except there is one significant difference.

There is no prophecy.

There is no chosen king.

There is no legendary kingdom.

There is simply a six-year-old Shark Thiren named **Nora Tachyon.**

Nora is currently the **only known individual capable of lifting the sword.**

---

# 2. INITIAL DISCOVERY

The phenomenon was discovered accidentally.

Aeterna had been standing in the SAFEHOUSE courtyard.

She manifested her sword.

Rather than dismissing it, she placed the blade vertically into the ground.

The weapon remained completely stable.

Nora approached.

She looked at it.

> **NORA:**  
> “What's that?”

> **AETERNA:**  
> “My sword.”

Nora looked at the sword.

Then at Aeterna.

> **NORA:**  
> “Can I pull it out?”

Aeterna paused.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Try.”

Nora grabbed the hilt.

She pulled.

The sword came free immediately.

Nora stared at it.

Aeterna stared at Nora.

Neither spoke for several seconds.

# 4. AETERNA'S EXPLANATION

Aeterna eventually clarified that the sword can recognize its intended bearer.

Nora is currently accepted by the weapon.

No other SAFEHOUSE resident has demonstrated the ability to remove it once Aeterna deliberately establishes the recreational binding state.

The sword does not become physically heavier.

Instead, the weapon simply refuses to separate from the surface for unauthorized individuals.

Attempts to use mechanical assistance have produced no meaningful result.

C.O.R.E. therefore classifies the phenomenon as **anomalous authorization**, rather than conventional weight.

---

# 5. THE RECREATIONAL FUNCTION

Aeterna has confirmed that the sword can be placed into the ground specifically for recreational purposes.

This has resulted in an unexpected SAFEHOUSE activity.

Nora places the sword into the ground.

Residents attempt to remove it.

They fail.

Nora removes it.

She puts it back.

They try again.

They fail again.

Nora finds this extremely funny.

---

# 6. NORA'S FAVORITE GAME

Nora has officially named the activity:

> **“Sword Game.”**

Her current preferred procedure is:

1. Aeterna places the sword into the ground.
    
2. Nora announces:
    
    > “Who's strong enough?”
    
3. Someone attempts to remove it.
    
4. They fail.
    
5. Nora laughs.
    
6. Nora pulls it out effortlessly.
    
7. Nora puts it back.
    
8. Repeat.
    

The activity has already attracted several SAFEHOUSE residents.

# 9. IMPORTANT LIMITATION

The sword remains an extremely dangerous anomalous artifact.

Its recreational use does **not** reduce its original capabilities.

Personnel are therefore prohibited from:

- striking objects with it;
    
- testing its execution properties;
    
- attempting to damage it;
    
- attempting to reproduce its energy discharge;
    
- treating it as an ordinary training sword;
    
- or encouraging Nora to use it as a weapon outside authorized circumstances.
    

The “Sword in the Stone” activity is permitted only because Aeterna deliberately establishes the recreational state.
