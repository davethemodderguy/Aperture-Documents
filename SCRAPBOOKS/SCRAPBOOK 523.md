### **MISSING PERSON / ANOMALOUS EVIDENCE FOLLOW-UP**

# **THE RED NOTE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **MISSING**  
**Recovered Evidence:** **Left Leg / Ether Message**  
**Location:** Forest Perimeter — Unknown Distance from Treehouse

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Another part of Corin has been recovered.

Her **left leg** was found beneath a tree in a remote section of the forest.

Corin herself was not present.

Unlike the previously recovered right arm, the location contained an additional piece of evidence.

A note.

The writing was made using **red Ether**.

The substance had been deliberately altered to resemble blood.

It was not blood.

It was Corin's Ether.

---

# **THE NOTE**

The message was written directly onto a piece of paper.

The red Ether had soaked into the page and remained unusually stable.

The handwriting was unmistakably Corin's.

The note reads:

> **"Don't look for me."**

Below it:

> **"I'm not worth finding."**

Then, after a significant gap:

> **"I'm sorry for making everyone worry."**

The final line is smaller than the others.

> **"Please don't make me come back."**

---

# **THE RED ETHER**

Curious Light immediately identified the substance.

It possesses no biological properties.

It only resembles blood in appearance.

The choice appears intentional.

Corin knew exactly what the sight would communicate to anyone who found it.

She wanted the discovery to look frightening.

She wanted whoever found it to understand that she was leaving pieces of herself behind.

The symbolism is considered more important than the physical substance itself.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light stared at the note for a long time.

She eventually touched the red writing.

> **"She made it look like blood."**

Curious Light nodded.

> **"Yes."**

> **"Why?"**

He did not immediately answer.

> **"Because she wanted us to think about what happened to her."**

Guiding Light shook her head.

> **"No."**

She looked toward the forest.

> **"She wants us to think she's already gone."**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie read the message.

She immediately recognized the handwriting.

She turned the page over, as if expecting another message.

There was none.

She quietly said:

> **"She's trying to make us give up."**

Neko answered:

> **"Then we don't."**

Orphie looked down at the detached leg.

> **"She thinks this will prove she doesn't need anyone."**

Nobody corrected her.

---

# **THE PATTERN**

Two separate locations have now yielded portions of Corin's body.

**Right arm.**

**Left leg.**

Corin herself remains unaccounted for.

The pattern suggests that the detached limbs are not random occurrences.

Corin may be deliberately leaving them behind.

Possible purposes include:

- Creating distance between herself and the searchers.
    
- Convincing others that she cannot return.
    
- Symbolically demonstrating that she is "not a person."
    
- Attempting to discourage further pursuit.
    
- Leaving evidence without directly communicating.
    
- Expressing her belief that her body is merely an object.
    

No single interpretation has been confirmed.

---

# **CORIN'S SELF-PERCEPTION**

The note is consistent with her previous statements.

Corin has repeatedly described herself as:

> **"A thing."**

> **"A doll."**

> **"Something made in a laboratory."**

The detached limbs may therefore carry a symbolic meaning beyond their physical condition.

She may be attempting to demonstrate:

> **"This is all I am."**

A collection of damaged parts sustained by Ether.

The search team has been instructed not to adopt this interpretation.

---

# **SEARCH RESPONSE**

The recovery site has been secured.

The search perimeter has been expanded.

Ether-sensitive personnel are monitoring the surrounding forest.

The Architects are continuing their search.

The recovered limbs will be preserved and stabilized for eventual reattachment.

No part of Corin's body will be treated as disposable.

---

# **FINAL ASSESSMENT**

The red Ether was deliberate.

Corin wanted it to look like blood.

She wanted the message to look like a final warning.

But the substance gives away something important.

**It was Ether.**

The same Ether that sustains her.

The same Ether she can manipulate.

The same Ether that refuses to let her body simply stop functioning.

Corin may believe she is leaving pieces of an object behind.

Everyone searching for her sees something else.

They see pieces of **Corin**.

And they are going to keep looking.
