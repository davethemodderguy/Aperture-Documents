# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE BEHAVIORAL INCIDENT REPORT**

# **CORIN — CATNIP INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Incident Type:** Feline Behavioral Anomaly  
**Status:** **Resolved**

---

# **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

A SAFEHOUSE resident made an unfortunate discovery:

> **Do not feed Corin catnip.**

Corin's existing feline traits already include cat ears, two tails, feline fangs, a miniature cat form, a fondness for yarn, and an inexplicable tendency to occupy glass jars.

Catnip was therefore considered, by someone, to be a harmless enrichment item.

This assessment was incorrect.

---

# **INITIAL EXPOSURE**

Corin was given a small quantity of catnip.

Within a short period, her behavior changed dramatically.

She became unusually energetic.

Her ears perked up.

Her tails began moving rapidly.

Her wings started fluttering.

Then she began running.

---

# **THE INCIDENT**

Corin proceeded to sprint around SAFEHOUSE at an alarming speed.

She climbed onto furniture.

Jumped off.

Ran across the floor.

Changed direction.

Ran back.

Then flew.

The wings made the situation considerably worse.

Corin began performing erratic aerial maneuvers throughout the SAFEHOUSE.

At one point, she flew through the central living area, performed a backflip, landed, immediately sprinted underneath a table, and emerged several seconds later carrying a ball of yarn.

Nobody had given her the yarn.

Nobody knows where she found it.

---

# **THE WINGS**

Corin's wings became particularly active.

The increased movement produced enough airflow to scatter loose objects throughout the room.

Several papers were blown off tables.

A blanket was lifted from a couch.

One researcher lost their hat.

Corin appeared delighted.

---

# **THE JAR**

Corin eventually spotted one of her favorite glass jars.

She immediately climbed inside.

She loafed.

For approximately five seconds.

Then she launched herself back out.

The jar survived.

The surrounding furniture was less fortunate.

---

# **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION**

Under normal circumstances, Corin is relatively calm and affectionate.

Under the effects of catnip, she became:

- Hyperactive.
    
- Extremely playful.
    
- Highly energetic.
    
- Easily distracted.
    
- Much more interested in chasing objects.
    
- More likely to climb things.
    
- More likely to fly without warning.
    
- Considerably harder to catch.
    

Her usual gentle nibbling also became more frequent.

Fortunately, the bites remained playful.

---

# **BELLE'S RESPONSE**

Belle eventually managed to catch Corin.

Corin immediately attempted to climb out of her arms.

Belle held on.

Corin squirmed.

Belle:

> **"What did you give her?"**

The responsible resident:

> **"...Catnip."**

Belle looked down at the wildly energetic winged cat in her hands.

Then back at them.

> **"Never again."**

---

# **RECOVERY**

After the effects subsided, Corin became tired.

She curled up against Belle.

Her wings folded around herself.

She fell asleep.

Upon waking, she appeared completely normal.

She showed no lasting effects.

---

# **NEW SAFEHOUSE RULE**

### **CATNIP IS PROHIBITED FOR CORIN.**

This includes:

- Catnip leaves.
    
- Catnip toys.
    
- Catnip-infused treats.
    
- Catnip sprays.
    
- Catnip-containing enrichment products.
    

Any item containing catnip must be kept away from Corin.

---

# **LESSON LEARNED**

Corin is already a winged cat with:

- Supernatural physiology.
    
- Ether abilities.
    
- Extremely sharp feline fangs.
    
- Two tails.
    
- Extremely powerful wings.
    
- A tendency to climb into containers.
    
- A tendency to loaf.
    
- A fondness for yarn.
    

Adding catnip to this equation was, in retrospect, **obviously a terrible idea.**

---

# **FINAL STATEMENT**

The SAFEHOUSE has officially reached the following conclusion:

> **DON'T FEED CORIN CATNIP.**

Not because it is poisonous.

Not because it damages her.

Simply because nobody wants to experience **Corin at maximum zoomies** ever again.

**End of Safehouse Behavioral Incident Report**
