# **OVERVIEW**

Since relocating to **Shelter T-01**, Corin Lockwood has demonstrated a marked improvement in emotional stability.

While she continues to prefer the quiet of the forest and the harsh winter weather, personnel report that she is more socially engaged than at any previous point since withdrawing from Project SAFEHOUSE.

She regularly receives visitors.

She occasionally invites them inside.

Most importantly...

She no longer treats every approaching person as a threat.

---

# **CURRENT RESIDENCE**

Shelter T-01 has been formally registered as Corin's assigned residence.

Engineering inspections are conducted periodically with Corin's permission.

The reinforced structure has proven capable of withstanding the Blacksite's severe winter conditions.

Corin has repeatedly stated that she prefers the sound of wind through the trees to the sound of ventilation systems.

Medical Services have confirmed that the environment is safe, provided routine welfare checks continue.

---

# **VISITATION**

Personnel wishing to visit Corin are reminded to observe basic etiquette.

- Knock before entering.
    
- Wait for a response.
    
- Respect her decision if she declines visitors.
    
- Do not enter uninvited.
    

These procedures exist to maintain the trust Corin has slowly rebuilt over many months.

---

# **UNAUTHORIZED ENTRY**

Several newer personnel have jokingly suggested climbing into the treehouse without knocking "to surprise her."

Security strongly advises against this.

Not because Corin is aggressive.

But because startling someone with a documented trauma-related startle response is both inconsiderate and potentially dangerous.

Personnel are reminded that Corin possesses extraordinary physical strength.

Her involuntary defensive reactions have previously generated enough kinetic force to destroy reinforced training equipment during controlled demonstrations.

Security has no interest in discovering what would happen if someone unexpectedly burst through her front door.
