---

# 1. SURVEY POSTING

Neri has officially opened a survey asking SAFEHOUSE and authorized MSAC personnel what category should receive the next **Limited Tachyon Industries Merchandise** release.

The survey was posted without additional explanation.

The entire announcement consisted of:

> **NERI:**  
> “What should I make next?”

Four options were provided.

### AVAILABLE OPTIONS

- **Sniper Rifle**
    
- **Assault Rifle**
    
- **Marksman Rifle**
    
- **Something Else**
    

Neri did not specify what she meant by “something else.”

This has resulted in considerable speculation.

---

# 2. CURRENT RESULTS

After significant participation, three categories have separated themselves from the remaining options.

### CURRENT TOP THREE

**1. SNIPER RIFLE**  
**2. ASSAULT RIFLE**  
**3. MARKSMAN RIFLE**

The exact vote totals remain subject to change while the survey remains open.

The three categories have nevertheless established a substantial lead.

---

# 3. SNIPER RIFLE

The Sniper Rifle category has attracted significant attention from personnel interested in long-range platforms.

Several respondents appear particularly interested in seeing how Neri would interpret the Tachyon Industries design philosophy in a much larger platform.

One MSAC operator submitted:

> **“Give us something ridiculous.”**

Another:

> **“Neri has already made pistols and a shotgun. Give her a rifle.”**

Neri's response to the latter comment was:

> **“I know.”**

No clarification followed.

---

# 4. ASSAULT RIFLE

The Assault Rifle category is receiving similarly strong support.

The primary argument from respondents is straightforward:

> **“We don't have a Tachyon assault rifle yet.”**

This has been repeated frequently.

Several Night Stalker and Deadeye owners have also expressed interest in seeing a Tachyon Industries rifle platform despite already being excluded from certain pistol allocations.

One anonymous response read:

> **“Can I be excluded from the pistol blacklist if it's a rifle?”**

Neri replied:

> **“No.”**

The respondent subsequently withdrew the question.

---

# 5. MARKSMAN RIFLE

The Marksman Rifle category has developed its own following.

Respondents appear particularly interested in a platform positioned between the existing close-range Tachyon Industries designs and a dedicated sniper rifle.

Elsher submitted:

> **“Marksman rifle.”**

No explanation was provided.

Jake submitted:

> **“Sniper.”**

The MSAC Commander submitted:

> **“Marksman.”**

The three subsequently disagreed about which category would be more interesting.

No argument was recorded.

---

# 6. NORA'S VOTE

Nora was asked which category she selected.

She pointed at:

> **SNIPER RIFLE**

Neri asked why.

Nora responded:

> **“Because it sounds cool.”**

Neri accepted this reasoning.

---

# 7. ALICE'S VOTE

Alice selected:

> **ASSAULT RIFLE**

Her reasoning:

> **“Rifle.”**

When asked to elaborate:

> **“A really big rifle.”**

Neri asked whether Alice understood what an assault rifle was.

Alice replied:

> **“Yes.”**

She then attempted to describe one as:

> **“A rifle that assaults things.”**

The survey administrator ended the conversation.

---

# 8. BAO'S RESPONSE

Bao selected:

> **MARKSMAN RIFLE**

Her reasoning was considerably more practical.

> **BAO:**  
> “You already have several things designed around close-range use.”

> **“Try something different.”**

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Okay.”

Bao subsequently asked whether Neri intended to make whatever won.

Neri answered:

> **“Maybe.”**

This is currently the only official statement regarding production.

---

Neko initially refused to participate.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Fine.”
