### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE — RESIDENTIAL ROUTINE LOG**

# **COREY — DAILY BREAKFAST**

**Classification:** Level 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE — Communal Kitchen  
**Subject:** Corey  
**Routine:** Morning Meal

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corey has established a consistent breakfast routine within the SAFEHOUSE.

Every morning, she prepares:

> **Chicken, Potato & Mushroom Soup**

The recipe has become one of her most reliable daily meals.

It is warm, filling, simple, and easy to prepare in large quantities.

More importantly, Corey likes having something warm in the morning.

---

# **REASON**

Corey's preference for a warm breakfast has become more pronounced since her lunar manifestations became a regular nighttime occurrence.

Corey does not wear a hat while sleeping.

Consequently, exposure to moonlight frequently causes her to unintentionally transition into **Luna** during the night.

Luna's lunar energy naturally produces a cooler surrounding environment.

Corey therefore often wakes up feeling colder than she would prefer.

Her solution is remarkably simple:

> **Make soup.**

---

# **MORNING CYCLE**

Corey's typical morning now follows a predictable pattern.

### **Night**

Corey sleeps without headwear.

Moonlight reaches her.

She gradually becomes Luna.

Her lunar gravitational field activates.

She floats peacefully while sleeping.

Marts often gather around her.

---

### **Morning**

The moon's influence fades.

Corey returns to her normal state.

She wakes up.

She realizes she's cold.

She goes to the kitchen.

She makes soup.

---

# **THE SOUP**

The standard recipe contains:

- Chicken.
    
- Potatoes.
    
- Mushrooms.
    
- Water or stock.
    
- Seasoning.
    

The recipe is based on the cooking traditions preserved in Corey's inherited family cookbook.

Corey generally prepares enough for anyone else who wants breakfast.

---

# **MART INVOLVEMENT**

Marts have become extremely familiar with the breakfast routine.

Once Corey enters the kitchen, Marts begin appearing.

One Mart:

> "...Soup?"

Corey:

> "...Yes."

Another:

> "...Chicken?"

> "...Yes."

Another:

> "...Potato?"

> "...Yes."

A final Mart:

> "...Mushroom?"

Corey:

> "...You know the recipe."

The Mart nods.

> "...Soup."

---

# **LUNA OBSERVATION**

The Marts have noticed that Corey is particularly fond of warmth after waking from Luna form.

They sometimes respond by surrounding her with their own bodies.

Because Marts can adjust their internal temperature, they can function as convenient living heating pads.

Corey occasionally wakes up surrounded by them.

Her usual response:

> "...Morning."

The Marts:

> "...Cold."

Corey:

> "...A little."

The Marts immediately move closer.

---

# **COREY'S COMMENT**

When asked why she makes the same soup every morning, Corey answered:

> "...It's warm."

The researcher asked whether she gets tired of eating it.

Corey shook her head.

> "...No."

She paused.

> "...I like it."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The morning soup has become a small but consistent part of SAFEHOUSE life.

Every night, Corey may unintentionally become Luna.

She may float beneath the moon.

Marts may gather around her.

The night may become quiet and blue.

But every morning, Corey returns to the kitchen.

The pot goes on the stove.

Chicken.

Potatoes.

Mushrooms.

Warm broth.

And eventually, the SAFEHOUSE wakes to the same familiar smell.

For Corey, it is more than breakfast.

It is her way of warming herself back up.
