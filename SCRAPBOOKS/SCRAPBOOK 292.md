# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – PERSONNEL ADVISORY**

### **SUBJECT: MART TOP HATS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – All Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Personnel are reminded that every Mart naturally possesses a miniature black top hat.

The hat is **not** clothing.

It is a natural part of a Mart's Ether-stabilized body.

Although removable, it should **never** be taken without the Mart's permission.

---

# **OBSERVATIONS**

Each Mart manifests with:

- One blue gelatinous body.
    
- Two deer antlers.
    
- One miniature black top hat.
    

No two hats are completely identical.

Minor variations in shape, height, and brim size have been documented.

Researchers are currently unable to explain how the hats form.

---

# **REMOVAL**

While a Mart can voluntarily remove its own hat and occasionally offers it as a friendly gesture, personnel are instructed **not** to take or keep one.

Removing a Mart's hat without permission causes immediate confusion and distress.

Observed reactions include:

- Looking around in confusion.
    
- Patting the top of their own head.
    
- Quietly saying:
    

> "No..."

Until the hat is returned.

Once reunited with their hat, the Mart immediately returns to its usual cheerful behavior.

---

# **AUTHORIZED EXCEPTIONS**

A Mart may voluntarily hand its hat to another person.

This behavior has only been observed toward individuals the Marts deeply trust.

Recipients have included:

- Lumi.
    
- Mart.
    
- Corin.
    
- Corey.
    
- Miss Neko.
    

The Mart will patiently wait until the hat is returned.

Personnel are advised to treat this as a significant gesture of trust.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The exact biological or magical purpose of the tiny top hats remains unknown.

Whether they serve a physiological function, an Ether-stabilizing role, or are simply an unusual trait of the species has yet to be determined.

Until further notice, all personnel are reminded of the official care guideline:

> **Do not remove a Mart's hat.**

It may be tiny.

It may seem insignificant.

But to a Mart...

It's their hat.

And that's reason enough.

**End of Personnel Advisory**
