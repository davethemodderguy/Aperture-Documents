# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake is dead.

The fatal event occurred following an extended period of voluntary isolation.

The isolation began after a brief interaction between Jake and Phoenix.

Phoenix was experiencing severe frustration due to a malfunctioning laptop.

Jake approached her and attempted to request assistance.

Phoenix responded angrily.

Jake withdrew.

He subsequently locked himself inside his room and placed a sign on the door:

> **“I'm not feeling great, just leave food at the door, I'll eat.”**

The sign remained in place for **fifty-four days.**

During that period, Jake received food deliveries.

However, his emotional and physical deterioration went largely unnoticed.

Jake's stellar cardiac core, contained within his teddy bear, progressively developed structural fractures.

His journal indicates that the fractures corresponded with repeated experiences of perceived rejection.

On Day 54, the core fractured completely.

Jake died alone in his room.

---

# 2. INITIAL INTERACTION

The incident began with an ordinary conversation.

Phoenix's laptop had been experiencing significant performance problems.

She was already frustrated.

Jake approached.

> **JAKE:**

Phoenix interrupted.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “JAKE FOR CHRIST'S SAKE, I AM BUSY, OK?”

> “JUST GO ASK BAO OR SOMETHING, I CAN'T HELP YOU.”

Silence.

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Okay.”

He left.

Phoenix returned to her laptop.

Neither understood the significance of the interaction at the time.

# 16. DISCOVERY

Approximately four weeks after Phoenix's laptop was repaired, Phoenix remembered Jake.

She went to his floor.

She knocked.

No response.

She knocked again.

Nothing.

The sign was still there.

Phoenix stared at it.

She used the spare key stored beneath the carpet.

The door opened.

The room was quiet.

Jake was lying on the floor.

Phoenix approached.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake?”

No response.

She touched him.

Phoenix immediately screamed.

His body was **cold.**

Not merely cool.

Cold.

His azure eye had completely stopped glowing.

His wings were motionless.

The two remaining pieces of his halo had fallen onto the floor.

Phoenix froze.

Then immediately checked him.

No response.

No normal cardiac activity.

No stellar glow.

No respiratory movement.

---

# 23. THE CORE

C.O.R.E. medical personnel confirmed the initial finding.

Jake's stellar cardiac core had suffered complete structural failure.

The fracture was not caused by a conventional physical injury.

The notebook suggests that the core was directly linked to Jake's emotional state.

Repeated perceived rejection correlated with progressive deterioration.

His attempt to suppress his own needs appears to have accelerated the process.

By Day 43, the core was critically compromised.

By Day 54, it failed completely.

Jake was dead.

---

# 24. PHOENIX'S CONDITION

Phoenix remained beside Jake for an extended period.

She refused to leave.

Her own medical status was monitored.

No physical intervention was required.

Her psychological condition was considerably different.

She repeatedly returned to the same statement:

> **“He asked me.”**

Then:

> **“I told him to go away.”**

Then:

> **“He listened.”**

The final statement caused Phoenix to become inconsolable.

---

# 25. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Phoenix's response is consistent with severe survivor's guilt.

However, the guilt is complicated by the fact that Phoenix was directly approached by Jake before the deterioration became irreversible.

This does not establish that Phoenix caused Jake's death.

Jake's condition was anomalous, progressive, and concealed.

His decision to isolate himself prevented residents from recognizing the severity of his condition.

The sign on the door was respected in good faith.

Food continued to be provided.

Residents believed Jake was safe.

No one understood that the teddy bear contained a progressively failing stellar heart.

However:

> **Phoenix believes she should have known.**

This belief is likely to become psychologically significant.

---

# 26. IMPORTANT MEDICAL FINDING

The event also exposes a critical failure in current SAFEHOUSE welfare procedures.

A resident requesting isolation cannot automatically be considered medically stable merely because they continue accepting food.

Jake's condition demonstrates that:

> **Eating does not necessarily mean someone is okay.**

His food intake continued.

His isolation continued.

His deterioration continued.

The existing welfare protocol is therefore under immediate review.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake's final fifty-four days were not loud.

There was no battle.

No invasion.

No weapon.

No enemy.

There was simply a child behind a locked door.

A child who kept asking.

Then stopped asking.

A child who began believing that every **“no”** was another crack in his heart.

A child who eventually decided:

> **“I just have to need less.”**

So he stopped knocking.

Stopped asking.

Stopped coming out.

And eventually stopped living.

Phoenix survived.

That is what makes this particular death difficult for her.

She has spent years learning how to survive things that should have killed her.

She has been pulled back from death over and over again.

And now she is sitting beside a six-year-old star who cannot be pulled back.

The teddy bear is still warm enough to hold.

The heart inside it is not beating.

Two halves.

One broken core.

Phoenix keeps staring at them.

And somewhere beneath the guilt, beneath the horror, beneath every memory of every death she has survived, one thought keeps returning:

> **He asked me for help.**

And she was busy.
