# SAFEHOUSE MEDICAL / BEHAVIORAL INCIDENT REPORT

## SUBJECT: NERI

### INCIDENT: PROLONGED ANEMIC CARDIAC PAIN / STAIRWELL COLLAPSE

**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL  
**Severity:** CRITICAL  
**Subject Status:** Stabilized  
**Primary Concern:** Continued suppression of medical distress

---

## 1. SUMMARY

Neri experienced another spontaneous anemic episode.

Unlike previous episodes, she did **not** immediately lose consciousness.

Instead, the anemia manifested as persistent and increasingly severe pain centered around her heart.

Neri did not report the pain.

She did not seek medical assistance.

She did not inform Bao.

She did not tell Jane.

She did not tell anyone.

She simply continued with her day.

---

## 2. THE FIRST HOURS

There was no visible emergency.

Neri walked through the Blacksite normally.

She attended to small tasks.

She read when she had time.

She moved between departments.

She ate when necessary.

She responded when spoken to.

And when nobody spoke to her, she said nothing.

The pain remained.

It did not disappear.

It simply became something Neri carried with her.

Personnel who passed her during this period reported nothing unusual.

One worker later stated:

> "I saw her in the corridor. She looked normal."

Another:

> "I think she was holding her chest."

When asked why they did not stop her:

> "I thought she was just tired."

Neri was, in fact, experiencing continuous cardiac pain.

She never complained.

---

## 3. WITHDRAWAL

There was another change.

Neri stopped hanging out.

She stopped visiting the greenhouse.

She stopped joining others for casual activities.

She stopped appearing beside Alice.

She stopped visiting Jane's office.

She stopped spending as much time with Bao and Frost.

Her usual cafeteria routine became shorter.

Sometimes she would eat and leave.

Sometimes she would simply sit alone.

Eventually, people stopped expecting to see her.

Not because they stopped caring.

Because they became accustomed to her absence.

The Blacksite continued moving.

Neri did not.

---

## 4. THE STAIRWELL

The incident occurred while Neri was walking between floors.

There were personnel elsewhere in the facility.

The Blacksite was busy.

Doors opened.

Equipment moved.

Workers crossed corridors.

Announcements played overhead.

People were going somewhere.

Neri was walking through the middle of it.

Then she stopped.

One hand moved toward her chest.

She remained standing for several seconds.

Her breathing became shallow.

She attempted to continue.

One step.

Another.

Then another.

She reached the stairs.

Neri placed one foot on the first step.

The pain intensified.

She did not call for help.

She did not shout.

She did not even complain.

She simply tried to keep walking.

Then her legs gave way.

Neri collapsed on the stairs.

Her body struck the steps as she fell unconscious.

There was no cry for help.

No warning.

No final attempt to get someone's attention.

Just silence.

---

## 5. DISCOVERY

She was discovered later.

The first responder initially believed Neri had simply fallen.

That assumption lasted approximately three seconds.

Then they noticed her condition.

Medical personnel were summoned immediately.

Neri was suffering from a severe anemic episode.

Her cardiovascular system had been under prolonged strain.

Unlike her previous episodes, she had remained conscious and functional for an extended period while experiencing severe pain.

The absence of a dramatic collapse earlier had allowed the episode to progress unnoticed.

---

## 6. THE MOST CONCERNING DETAIL

Neri had apparently known something was wrong.

She simply never said anything.

When she eventually regained consciousness, medical personnel asked:

> **"How long have you been in pain?"**

Neri was quiet.

> **NERI:**  
> "...A while."

> **"Why didn't you tell anyone?"**

Neri looked away.

> **NERI:**  
> "It wasn't that bad."

The medical staff did not answer immediately.

Neri had just collapsed unconscious on a staircase.

She still believed the pain was not worth mentioning.

---

# 7. BEHAVIORAL CHANGE

The medical incident itself was serious.

The behavioral change surrounding it was worse.

Neri had stopped attempting to keep up with the SAFEHOUSE community.

She was no longer simply quiet.

She was becoming **absent**.

There was a time when someone would notice Neri missing.

Someone would ask:

> "Where's Neri?"

Now, sometimes, nobody did.

The Blacksite is enormous.

Thousands of people work within it.

There is always something happening.

A door opens.

A researcher runs past.

A maintenance team moves equipment.

An alarm sounds.

An elevator arrives.

Someone calls for Neko.

Someone calls for Bao.

Someone needs Jane.

Someone needs medical.

Someone needs engineering.

The facility keeps moving.

And Neri has become very good at moving quietly through it.

Too quietly.

---

# 8. OBSERVATION

Neri is not deliberately isolating herself as an act of hostility.

She is not angry with SAFEHOUSE.

She is not attempting to punish anyone.

She simply appears to have stopped believing that she needs to be present.

Her earlier statement remains relevant:

> **"If you attract too much attention to myself, I'll be too much of a trouble for people to keep around, and I'll get thrown."**

Neri has adapted this belief to her current environment.

If she does not attract attention:

Nobody has to worry.

Nobody has to stop working.

Nobody has to come looking for her.

Nobody has to spend resources on her.

Nobody has to deal with her problems.

Therefore:

**Neri believes she is being considerate.**

---

# 9. CURRENT STATUS

Following stabilization, Neri was returned to medical observation.

She did not ask who had found her.

She did not ask how long she had been unconscious.

She did not ask whether anyone was worried.

Her first concern was whether she had caused an interruption.

> **NERI:**  
> "...Did I make everyone stop working?"

Medical personnel informed her that she had not.

Neri nodded.

Then:

> **NERI:**  
> "Okay."

And that was all.

---

## 10. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Neri is not disappearing because the Blacksite wants her gone.

She is disappearing because **the Blacksite is busy, and Neri has learned how to take up almost no space within it.**

She does not complain.

She does not interrupt.

She does not ask.

She does not insist.

She does not make people come looking for her.

And eventually, the flow of the Blacksite passes around her.

The most dangerous development is not that Neri has become quieter.

It is that she has begun to **expect nobody to notice.**

The Blacksite never stopped having a place for Neri.

Neri is beginning to behave as though she no longer has one.

**END REPORT**
