# SAFEHOUSE FIELD NOTE

## JANE DOE — COLLAR OVERRIDE

**Subject:** Jane Doe  
**Height:** 185 cm  
**Status:** Permanent SAFEHOUSE Resident  
**Blacksite Height Ranking:** Tallest known non-Architect resident  
**Known Exception:** Architects  
**Classification:** Embarrassing  
**Threat Level:** None when properly executed

---

## 1. DISCOVERY

Jane Doe is exceptionally difficult to intimidate.

She is an experienced intelligence operative, highly agile, physically capable, and possesses a tail capable of restraining an adult.

Most Blacksite personnel therefore assume that attempting to physically restrain Jane would be a very poor life decision.

They are correct.

There is, however, one remarkably simple exception.

### **The Shirt Collar.**

If a sufficiently tall person can reach the back of Jane's shirt collar and get a secure grip, Jane can be temporarily immobilized in a manner disturbingly similar to picking up a cat.

The primary limiting factor is height.

At **185 cm**, Jane is the tallest known ordinary Blacksite resident.

Consequently, most personnel cannot comfortably reach the appropriate position.

---

# 2. EFFECT

When the collar is successfully grabbed:

Jane immediately loses most of her intimidating posture.

Her tail usually stops moving.

Her arms may hang at her sides.

Her feet may leave the floor if the person lifting her is strong enough.

She then becomes extremely confused.

The resulting expression is generally described as:

> **"Why am I being held like this?"**

This is particularly amusing because Jane is normally the person making everyone else nervous.

---

# 3. FIRST DOCUMENTED OBSERVATION

A maintenance worker discovered the technique completely accidentally.

Jane had been teasing him in a hallway.

Her tail was moving behind her.

She was smiling.

The worker was clearly nervous.

Jane stepped closer.

> **Jane:** "You're scared."

> **Worker:** "A little."

> **Jane:** "Why?"

The worker suddenly reached upward and grabbed the back of her shirt collar.

Jane stopped.

The worker lifted.

Jane's feet left the floor.

Silence.

Jane stared at him.

He stared at Jane.

> **Jane:** "...Put me down."

> **Worker:** "Oh."

He immediately did.

Jane straightened her shirt.

> **Jane:** "Never do that again."

The worker nodded.

> **Worker:** "Sorry."

Jane walked away.

Three seconds later, she turned around.

> **Jane:** "...How did you even reach that?"

---

# 4. THE HEIGHT PROBLEM

The technique is significantly less practical against Jane than it initially sounds.

At 185 cm, Jane is extremely tall.

Most residents simply cannot reach the back of her collar without assistance.

This has led to an unofficial SAFEHOUSE classification:

|Person|Likelihood of Reaching Collar|
|---|--:|
|Average Blacksite worker|Very low|
|Short resident|Practically impossible|
|Tall resident|Possible|
|Very tall resident|Likely|
|Architect|Irrelevant|
|Jane herself|Unfortunately aware of this|

---

# 5. NERI'S DISCOVERY

Neri eventually learned about the weakness.

She looked at Jane.

Then looked at her own height.

Then looked at Jane again.

> **Neri:** "I can't reach."

> **Jane:** "Correct."

Neri thought for a moment.

> **Neri:** "Can I use a chair?"

> **Jane:** "Absolutely not."

> **Neri:** "Ladder?"

> **Jane:** "No."

> **Neri:** "Subspace?"

Jane immediately pointed at her.

> **Jane:** "Especially no."

Neri sighed.

> **Neri:** "Okay."

Jane smiled.

> **Jane:** "Besides, you're my Cute Seal."

Neri's eyes narrowed.

> **Neri:** "I hate you."

> **Jane:** "No you don't."

> **Neri:** "...No."

---

# 6. IMPORTANT LIMITATION

The collar technique is not considered a reliable combat countermeasure.

It depends on:

- The person being tall enough to reach the collar.
    
- The person being close enough to Jane.
    
- Jane not noticing the attempt beforehand.
    
- The shirt collar being accessible.
    
- The person having sufficient strength to actually lift or control her.
    

Jane remains highly capable of avoiding physical contact.

Additionally, attempting to grab her collar without permission is strongly discouraged.

The fact that it works does not mean it is safe to repeatedly test.

---

# 7. JANE'S OPINION

Jane officially considers the entire phenomenon:

> **"Humiliating."**

She has nevertheless admitted that she finds it funny when SAFEHOUSE residents joke about it.

She is considerably less amused when Blacksite workers discuss it.

One worker was overheard telling another:

> **"Apparently you can just pick her up."**

Jane appeared behind him.

> **Jane:** "Apparently you can also lose your lunch privileges."

The worker immediately stopped talking.
