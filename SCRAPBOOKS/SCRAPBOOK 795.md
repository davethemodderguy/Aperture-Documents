# **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

Following Director Lucious' authorization of the current **SHOOT ON SIGHT** directive, Heaven's Blacksite perimeter security has successfully eliminated more than **fifty confirmed SPAWN combatants**.

Despite the number of confirmed casualties, C.O.R.E. has recovered:

> **ZERO SPAWN BODIES.**

In every documented case where security personnel attempted to approach a confirmed casualty following an engagement, the body was no longer present.

The disappearance occurs rapidly enough that conventional extraction by surviving SPAWN personnel has been considered unlikely in the majority of cases.

No confirmed explanation has been established.

---

# **1. CASUALTY COUNT**

As of the latest report:

> **50+ SPAWN COMBATANTS CONFIRMED ELIMINATED**

Confirmed eliminations are based on direct observation, surveillance footage, or other reliable battlefield confirmation.

The number is considered conservative.

Several engagements occurred outside optimal surveillance conditions, making the actual number potentially higher.

Despite this, no intact SPAWN remains have been recovered.

---

# **2. POST-ENGAGEMENT OBSERVATION**

The disappearance phenomenon follows a remarkably consistent pattern.

### **Observed Sequence**

> SPAWN combatant engaged  
> ↓  
> Combatant becomes incapacitated / confirmed deceased  
> ↓  
> Security maintains visual observation  
> ↓  
> Personnel approach casualty  
> ↓  
> **BODY IS ABSENT**

In several cases, security personnel reached the location within seconds.

No body was found.

No blood trail consistent with physical removal was discovered in several of the examined incidents.

Equipment recovery has also been inconsistent.

Some engagements have resulted in weapons or shields being left behind.

Others have resulted in the disappearance of both combatant and equipment.

---

# **3. NO CONFIRMED EXTRACTION**

C.O.R.E. initially considered the possibility that surviving SPAWN personnel were recovering their dead.

This hypothesis has not been supported by available evidence.

Security recordings have failed to identify consistent extraction teams.

In several incidents, the area surrounding the casualty remained under direct observation.

No individual was observed approaching the body.

Nevertheless, the body subsequently disappeared.

This has resulted in the classification:

> **SPAWN POST-MORTEM DISPLACEMENT PHENOMENON — UNCONFIRMED**

---

# **4. THE MASKS**

Every confirmed SPAWN combatant encountered by Blacksite security has worn a mask.

No unmasked SPAWN combatant has been positively identified.

The masks vary somewhat in appearance, but all appear intentionally designed to conceal the wearer's face.

Observed characteristics include:

- Full facial coverage.
    
- Limited visible skin.
    
- Integrated eye protection.
    
- Reinforced construction.
    
- In some cases, apparent environmental protection.
    
- No visible identifying insignia beyond SPAWN-associated markings.
    

As a result:

> **NO SPAWN COMBATANT HAS BEEN VISUALLY IDENTIFIED BY FACE.**

---

# **5. IDENTITY PROBLEM**

The combination of masked personnel and disappearing bodies has created a significant intelligence problem.

C.O.R.E. cannot currently determine whether the fifty-plus eliminated combatants represent:

> **50+ unique individuals**

or:

> **A substantially smaller number of individuals repeatedly returning to the Blacksite.**

There is currently no reliable biometric database for SPAWN personnel.

DNA recovery has been unsuccessful due to the absence of recoverable remains.

Facial recognition is impossible.

Fingerprint identification has produced no usable results.

---

# **6. EQUIPMENT RECOVERY**

The disappearance phenomenon does not appear to affect all equipment consistently.

Following some engagements, security personnel have recovered:

- Firearms.
    
- Ammunition.
    
- Melee weapons.
    
- Shields.
    
- Miscellaneous equipment.
    

Other incidents have produced no recoverable equipment whatsoever.

This inconsistency is currently unexplained.

One possibility is that SPAWN personnel possess a mechanism capable of selectively removing themselves and designated equipment.

Another is that the disappearance is an anomalous property of the combatants themselves.

Neither hypothesis has been confirmed.

# **8. ANOMALOUS CONSIDERATIONS**

C.O.R.E. Anomalous Investigation has identified several possible explanations.

### **HYPOTHESIS A — REMOTE EXTRACTION**

SPAWN possesses technology capable of retrieving incapacitated personnel remotely.

**Assessment:** Possible.

**Problem:** No detectable extraction mechanism has been identified.

---

### **HYPOTHESIS B — PRE-ARRANGED CORPSE REMOVAL**

SPAWN personnel may carry technology that activates upon death and relocates the user's remains.

**Assessment:** Possible.

**Problem:** No recovered equipment has demonstrated this capability.

---

### **HYPOTHESIS C — ANOMALOUS SELF-ERASURE**

The combatants may possess an anomalous property causing their physical remains to cease existing following death.

**Assessment:** Possible.

**Problem:** Would not immediately explain inconsistent equipment disappearance.

---

### **HYPOTHESIS D — EXTERNAL RECOVERY**

A third party may be retrieving the bodies.

**Assessment:** Possible.

**Problem:** No recovery teams have been observed.

---

### **HYPOTHESIS E — "SECOND CHANCE"**

SPAWN's stated belief in:

> **"Earning a second chance at life"**

may be directly connected to the phenomenon.

**Assessment:** **UNCONFIRMED — HIGH INTEREST**

C.O.R.E. strongly recommends further investigation into this possibility.

No assumption should currently be made that SPAWN's doctrine is metaphorical.

---

# **9. DIRECTORIAL RESPONSE**

Director Lucious has been briefed regarding the disappearance of SPAWN remains.

The SHOOT ON SIGHT directive remains unchanged.

The inability to recover bodies is **not** considered a reason to reduce engagement authority.

Instead, C.O.R.E. has been ordered to determine:

1. Where the bodies go.
    
2. Why they disappear.
    
3. Whether the disappearance is voluntary or automatic.
    
4. Whether SPAWN can return after being eliminated.
    
5. Whether the phenomenon is connected to their stated "second chance at life."
    
6. Whether the masks serve a purpose beyond concealment.
    

---

# **10. NEW FIELD PROCEDURE**

Following any confirmed SPAWN elimination, security personnel are now instructed to:

- Maintain visual surveillance of the casualty location.
    
- Record the exact time of incapacitation.
    
- Photograph the area before approaching.
    
- Record environmental conditions.
    
- Search for anomalous disturbances.
    
- Recover equipment where safe.
    
- Avoid disturbing unusual markings.
    
- Immediately report any unexplained disappearance.
    

Personnel are **not** to attempt to restrain or transport an apparently incapacitated SPAWN combatant without authorization.

If the body disappears:

> **DO NOT PURSUE.**

The disappearance itself is to be treated as an anomalous event.
