### **Z-335 PSYCHOLOGICAL & PHYSIOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

# **EMOTIONAL STABILITY OF MARTS**

**Classification:** Allied Entity Research  
**Subject:** Z-335 (Marts)

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although Z-335 entities possess remarkable physical resilience, research has confirmed that their greatest vulnerability is neither physical damage nor environmental hazards.

It is **psychological distress**.

Marts derive anomalous stability from a combination of Ether, water, and sustained positive emotional states.

Unlike most known organisms, prolonged or overwhelming negative emotional experiences can destabilize their magical structure.

---

# **EMOTIONAL STABILITY**

Under normal circumstances, Marts are consistently cheerful.

However, researchers have identified several conditions that may place a Mart at risk of destabilization.

Documented causes include:

- Severe or prolonged stress.
    
- Witnessing death or believing someone has died.
    
- Intense or prolonged worry.
    
- Extreme sadness.
    
- Losing hope for another person's safety.
    

If these emotional states become overwhelming, a Mart's magical containment field may begin to fail.

In severe cases, the Mart will catastrophically destabilize.

Personnel commonly describe this as the Mart "bursting."

---

# **RAPID RESPONSE BEHAVIOR**

This vulnerability has significantly influenced the behavior of Rapid Response Marts.

Their security doctrine places exceptional emphasis on **non-lethal restraint**.

Their objective is always to:

- Immobilize.
    
- Disarm.
    
- Protect.
    
- De-escalate.
    

Never to seriously injure.

Researchers believe this instinct is not merely ethical—it is also self-preservation.

---

# **POST-ENGAGEMENT CHECKS**

Following every security incident, Rapid Response Marts consistently perform the same routine.

Once a threat has been subdued, several Marts approach each restrained individual.

They gently nudge the person.

Typical dialogue includes:

> "Hello?"

> "Friend?"

> "Wake?"

> "Okay?"

If the individual responds, the Marts immediately relax.

If there is no response, visible concern spreads rapidly through the nearby colony.

---

# **DOCUMENTED INCIDENT**

During a previous attempted robbery of the KCA, one robber lost consciousness after being pinned beneath numerous Boulder Mode Marts.

The unconscious individual had merely fainted.

The Marts did not know this.

Security footage shows dozens of Rapid Response Marts gathering around the individual.

Several repeatedly nudged the robber.

One quietly asked:

> "...Wake?"

Another responded:

> "...Please?"

A third looked increasingly distressed.

Fortunately, the robber regained consciousness shortly afterward.

The moment he opened his eyes, the surrounding Marts collectively exclaimed:

> **"Happy!"**

One Mart immediately apologized.

> "Sorry."

The robber later stated:

> "...I've never had the things that knocked me out apologize for checking if I was okay."

---

# **MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

Medical Division concludes that the Marts' concern is genuine.

They appear incapable of emotionally separating their actions from the wellbeing of others.

If they believe they have caused serious harm, they experience measurable psychological distress.

For this reason, security personnel are instructed to promptly inform nearby Marts whenever an unconscious individual is medically stable.

Doing so noticeably reduces colony-wide anxiety.

It answered simply:

A researcher replied:

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

The emotional vulnerability of Z-335 is not considered a design flaw.

Rather, it appears inseparable from the very qualities that define the species.

Their compassion, empathy, and desire to protect others are also what make them susceptible to emotional collapse.

For this reason, personnel are reminded:

- Do not intentionally frighten Marts.
    
- Do not deceive them into believing someone has died.
    
- Reassure them promptly following emergency situations.
    
- Treat their emotional wellbeing as seriously as their physical stability.
    

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Following every successful security response, the same scene repeats itself.

A robber lies restrained on the floor.

A small blue Mart cautiously approaches.

It gives the person's arm a gentle nudge.

Then, in a quiet voice, it asks:

> **"...Okay?"**

The question is never asked to confirm victory.

It is asked because the Mart genuinely hopes the answer is:

> **"Yes."**
