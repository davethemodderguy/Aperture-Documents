## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake Akana has recovered from his recent cold-associated illness. His strength has returned, his eyes have regained their usual brightness, and his Scottish Fold ears are standing upright once again.

A subsequent personnel-welfare observation has confirmed an important behavioral preference:

**Jake absolutely loves headpats.**

He responds particularly well to gentle pats on the head. When his ears are ruffled gently as part of the interaction, his apparent enjoyment increases considerably.

The discovery has caused a noticeable increase in affectionate interactions across the Blacksite.

The Personnel Welfare Division has therefore issued the following informal guidance:

**If Jake approaches you, wants attention, and welcomes physical affection, headpats are encouraged. Gentle ear ruffling earns bonus points.**

This is not a substitute for medical care, a new containment protocol, or an excuse to interrupt Jake's activities every thirty seconds.

It is, however, an excellent way to make the facility's favorite little Scottish Fold happy.

---

## II. OBSERVED RESPONSE

During a routine walk through the Blacksite, Jake approached an employee who was working at a terminal.

The employee noticed Jake standing beside the desk, looking up expectantly.

> **Employee:** “Hello, little guy.”

> **Jake:** “Hi!”

The employee gently patted his head.

Jake immediately leaned into the touch.

His folded ears twitched.

The employee gave him another pat, then carefully ruffled the fur around the base of his ears.

Jake's expression changed so dramatically that the employee stopped typing altogether.

> **Employee:** “Oh, you LIKE that.”

> **Jake:** “Mhm!”

His tail began to move.

The employee continued for several seconds, alternating between gentle headpats and light ear ruffles.

Jake stayed exactly where he was.

He had apparently decided that the workstation was now his personal affection station.

When the employee finally stopped, Jake looked up.

> **Jake:** “Again?”

The employee resumed immediately.

The report notes that the employee's productivity decreased by approximately twelve minutes.

The employee has expressed no regrets.

---

## III. THE SCOTTISH FOLD BONUS

Jake's ears are a particularly recognizable part of his appearance. Their upright position following his recovery has made them more noticeable during affectionate interactions.

Personnel who choose to ruffle them must do so gently, avoiding pulling, twisting, or applying pressure. Scottish Fold ears can be sensitive, and the objective is to comfort Jake—not to test how much affection his ears can withstand.

The recommended interaction is simple:

1. Offer a gentle headpat.
    
2. Allow Jake to lean in or otherwise indicate that he wants more.
    
3. If he seems comfortable, lightly ruffle the fur around his ears.
    
4. Stop if he pulls away, shows discomfort, or loses interest.
    
5. Accept that he may immediately ask for another round.
    

The last point is considered a predictable operational complication.

## IV. CROSS-DEPARTMENTAL FINDINGS

The response to Jake's affection preference has been remarkably consistent.

Research personnel have discovered that he is willing to sit beside them while they work, provided they occasionally pat his head.

Security personnel have discovered that Jake will sometimes approach their posts, receive a headpat, and continue on his way looking considerably happier.

Engineering personnel have discovered that he is particularly fond of being patted while watching machinery operate, although he must remain clear of moving equipment.

Administrative personnel have discovered that a child with upright Scottish Fold ears can derail an entire meeting simply by looking at the nearest participant.

One meeting transcript contains the following exchange:

> **Executive:** “We need to finalize the revised departmental schedule.”

> **Jake:** “Hi.”

> **Executive:** “Hello, Jake.”

> **Jake:** “Headpat?”

> **Executive:** “...Yes, of course.”

_Several seconds of headpats._

> **Executive:** “Now, where were we?”

> **Researcher:** “The schedule.”

> **Executive:** “Right. The schedule.”

> **Jake:** “Ears too?”

The meeting was suspended for an additional thirty seconds.

The revised schedule was eventually approved.

Jake received his ear ruffles.

Everyone considered this a reasonable outcome.

## V. MEDICAL AND WELFARE REMINDER

Jake's recent recovery does not mean that personnel should stop paying attention to his health. Although he has returned to his usual condition, his right-thigh monitor remains in place to track his location and alert staff to relevant medical abnormalities.

Headpats and cuddles are encouraged when Jake welcomes them, but they do not replace monitoring or medical evaluation.

Personnel must also remember that Jake is a six-year-old child. His affection should never be treated as an entitlement to touch him without permission. He may be happy to receive headpats one moment and want to continue exploring the next.

If he walks away, let him.

If he comes back and asks for more, well—

The facility has plenty of hands.

---

## VI. C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

There is something wonderful about seeing Jake healthy again.

For weeks, every little movement had frightened us. We watched his eyes for signs of life, listened for changes in his breathing, and worried over every step he took.

Now, he walks through the Blacksite with his ears standing upright, looking for people to visit.

Sometimes he wants to show someone something he made.

Sometimes he wants to ask a question that derails an entire research project.

Sometimes he wants cake.

And sometimes, he simply walks up to someone, looks up with those bright blue eyes, and asks for a headpat.

So give him one.

Pat his little head gently. Ruffle his ears if he enjoys it. Tell him he's a good boy if you want to watch a grown researcher forget every deadline they have.

Let him lean against your hand for a moment.

Let him be a child who knows that the people around him are happy to see him.

After everything Jake has endured, we should never underestimate the importance of making him feel loved during ordinary moments.

**C.O.R.E. PERSONNEL WELFARE DIRECTIVE:** Headpats are encouraged. Gentle ear ruffles earn bonus points. A happy Jake is a welcome sight throughout the Blacksite.

And if he asks for _just one more_?

Well, we have never been particularly good at saying no to that little face.
