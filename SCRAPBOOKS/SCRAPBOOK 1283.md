# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The unidentified blue spectral child previously observed throughout the Blacksite has been identified.

His name is:

> **Jake.**

Jake is an angel.

He appears physically and behaviorally to be approximately **six years old**, although the exact date of his death is unknown.

What is known is significantly older than the Blacksite itself.

Jake died on the site where the Aperture Blacksite was eventually constructed.

The death occurred **long before Aperture Industries existed in its current form.**

Jake's remains were apparently never recovered or properly identified.

His spirit remained.

He is now permanently bound to the location.

---

# 1. IDENTIFICATION

Nora was the first resident to establish sustained communication with Jake.

Unlike previous observations, Jake spoke during the encounter.

His voice was quiet.

Childlike.

He did not initially appear frightened of Nora.

He simply seemed surprised that someone could hear him.

> **NORA:**  
> “What's your name?”

The boy looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Jake.”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “I'm Nora.”

Jake smiled slightly.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Hi.”

This was the first confirmed verbal interaction with the entity.

---

# 2. APPEARANCE

Jake's spectral form consistently appears as a young boy approximately six years old.

His body emits a faint blue luminescence.

His form is semi-transparent.

His clothing is unusual.

He appears to wear:

- winter clothing;
    
- a heavy cold-weather outfit;
    
- wrist warmers;
    
- and other garments appropriate for keeping a child warm.
    

The clothing appears to be part of his manifested ghost form rather than physical objects recovered from the Blacksite.

He also possesses visible angelic characteristics.

His exact celestial classification remains under investigation.

---

# 3. AGE

Jake died at approximately six years of age.

His current appearance has not changed.

There is no indication that his spirit has aged since death.

The exact date of death remains unknown.

Preliminary environmental evidence suggests the death occurred:

> **Several decades or potentially much longer before Aperture Industries constructed the Blacksite.**

The exact historical period remains under investigation.

---

# 4. THE LOCATION

The most significant discovery is that Jake did not die inside the Blacksite.

The Blacksite did not exist yet.

The land existed first.

Jake died here.

Aperture subsequently constructed the Blacksite on the same location.

This appears to explain why Jake remained unnoticed for so long.

He was not a resident of the Blacksite.

The Blacksite was eventually built **around the place where he died.**

---

# 5. CAUSE OF DEATH

Jake died from **starvation.**

There is no evidence that he was killed by an anomalous entity.

There is no evidence of combat.

There is no evidence of a catastrophic event.

He simply did not have enough food.

The circumstances surrounding why a six-year-old child was left without sufficient food remain unknown.

Jake himself describes the event with remarkably little bitterness.

When asked what happened, he gave the following account.

---

# 6. JAKE'S ACCOUNT

**[RECORDED INTERVIEW — NORA PRESENT]**

**C.O.R.E. WELFARE OFFICER:**  
“Jake, do you remember how you died?”

Jake nodded.

He looked down for a moment.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I'm very hungry, so I sleep it off.”

The officer remained silent.

Jake continued.

> **JAKE:**  
> “The next day I woke up I am a ghost and my body is on the ground.”

Pause.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I cried.”

Another pause.

Jake looked toward Nora.

> **JAKE:**  
> “But I got over anyways.”

The room remained silent.

Nora looked at Jake.

Then quietly asked:

> **NORA:**  
> “You were alone?”

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Yeah.”

Nora's expression changed.

She did not ask another question.

---

# 7. INTERPRETATION

Jake's account suggests that he experienced death without fully understanding what had happened.

He apparently went to sleep while severely hungry.

He subsequently awoke as a spirit and observed his own body.

His description contains no dramatic account of pain.

Instead, he describes:

**Hunger.**

**Sleep.**

**Waking.**

**Realizing he was dead.**

**Crying.**

Then:

> **“I got over anyways.”**

C.O.R.E. considers the statement significant.

Jake does not appear to be actively distressed by his death.

However, his continued wandering suggests that something remains unresolved.

---

# 8. THE FOOD

Jake's previously documented behavior now has a clear explanation.

He frequently wanders toward:

- cafeterias;
    
- kitchens;
    
- vending machines;
    
- food storage;
    
- and other locations where food can be seen.
    

He does not appear to be attempting to steal food.

He simply looks at it.

When Nora asked why he kept looking through the cafeteria glass, Jake answered:

> **JAKE:**  
> “Food.”

> **NORA:**  
> “You're hungry?”

Jake thought about it.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I think so.”

Nora gave him a piece of cake.

Jake could not physically eat it.

He stared at it for several seconds.

Then looked at Nora.

> **JAKE:**  
> “It smells nice.”

---

# 9. THE TACHYON MERCH STORE

Jake's interest in the Tachyon Merch Store remains unexplained.

He frequently stands outside the security glass.

He watches the merchandise.

He especially appears interested in the Bullseye.

Nora eventually asked:

> **NORA:**  
> “Why do you keep looking at the store?”

Jake shrugged.

> **JAKE:**  
> “It's pretty.”

Nora looked at the Bullseye display.

Then back at him.

> **NORA:**  
> “You like the rifle?”

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> “It looks cool.”

Nora smiled.

> **NORA:**  
> “Yeah.”

---

# 10. WHY ONLY AT NIGHT?

Jake has confirmed that he does not intentionally avoid the Blacksite during the day.

He simply cannot appear.

His manifestation seems restricted to nighttime.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

Nora's complete lack of sleep requirement has therefore made her uniquely capable of encountering him consistently.

Other residents sleep.

Jake appears.

Nora remains awake.

They meet.

---

# 11. JAKE AND NORA

An unusual friendship appears to be developing.

Nora does not treat Jake like a frightening anomaly.

Jake does not treat Nora like an intimidating Blacksite resident.

They simply wander together.

Sometimes they talk.

Sometimes they do not.

Sometimes Jake follows Nora.

Sometimes Nora finds him standing outside the Merch Store.

On one occasion, Nora was sitting in the cafeteria at approximately 03:00.

Jake appeared beside her.

> **JAKE:**  
> “What are you eating?”

Nora looked at her plate.

> **NORA:**  
> “Cake.”

Jake looked interested.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Again?”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “Again.”

Jake laughed.

---

# 12. RESIDENT RESPONSE

Neri was informed of Jake's history.

She reportedly became quiet.

After several seconds, she asked:

> **NERI:**  
> “He's been here all this time?”

C.O.R.E. personnel confirmed that this was the current understanding.

Neri looked toward the corridor.

> **NERI:**  
> “That's too long.”

She subsequently requested that Jake be treated as a resident rather than an unidentified anomaly.

C.O.R.E. approved the request.

---

# 13. BAO'S RESPONSE

Bao has expressed particular concern regarding Jake's relationship with food.

She has requested permission to provide him with meals.

C.O.R.E. explained that Jake cannot currently consume physical food.

Bao responded:

> **BAO:**  
> “Then we'll figure something out.”

No further discussion occurred.

---

# 14. CURRENT RESIDENTIAL STATUS

Jake is not being confined.

He is not considered hostile.

He is not being treated as a containment subject.

C.O.R.E. has designated him:

> **ANOMALOUS RESIDENT — PROVISIONAL**

Personnel are instructed to allow Jake to wander freely through accessible Blacksite areas during his manifestation period.

He should not be chased away from food areas.

He should not be frightened intentionally.

Personnel should not attempt to force physical interaction.

If Jake wishes to talk, listen.

If he wishes to wander, allow him to wander.

---

# 15. HISTORICAL INVESTIGATION

C.O.R.E. Archives has begun searching historical records concerning the land prior to Aperture construction.

The investigation is expected to be difficult.

The Blacksite occupies an extensively modified site.

Historical structures have been removed.

Records are incomplete.

The exact circumstances surrounding Jake's childhood remain unknown.

Most importantly:

> **Nobody yet knows why he was left to starve.**

Jake has not expressed anger about this.

That does not make the question less important.

---

# 16. FINAL OBSERVATION

At approximately 04:26, Nora and Jake were observed walking through the eastern corridor.

Nora carried her coffee.

Jake walked beside her.

They stopped outside the Tachyon Merch Store.

Jake looked through the glass.

Nora looked at him.

> **NORA:**  
> “Still want the Bullseye?”

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Yeah.”

> **NORA:**  
> “You can't use it.”

Jake shrugged.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I know.”

Nora thought for a moment.

> **NORA:**  
> “You can keep looking.”

Jake smiled.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Okay.”

They remained there for several minutes.

Then they continued wandering.

---

## C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Jake is not a hostile apparition.

He is not an intruder.

He is not attempting to haunt the Blacksite.

He is a six-year-old angel who died here before the Blacksite existed.

He was hungry.

He went to sleep.

He woke up dead.

He cried.

And somehow, he kept going.

For an unknown amount of time, he wandered this land alone.

Then Aperture built a Blacksite over the place where he died.

Now, thousands of people walk through the place that was once his entire world.

And almost none of them ever noticed him.

Until Nora.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake's description of his own death has been entered into the permanent record exactly as spoken:

> **“I'm very hungry, so I sleep it off. The next day I woke up I am a ghost and my body is on the ground. I cried, but I got over anyways.”**

There is something deeply strange about how casually he says it.

Not because he does not remember.

He remembers.

He simply does not seem to believe anyone needs to be angry for him anymore.

Tonight, Jake will probably wander again.

He may look for food.

He may stare through the Merch Store window.

He may talk to Nora.

And when morning comes, he will disappear.

Until the next night.

For now, at least:

**He is no longer alone.**
