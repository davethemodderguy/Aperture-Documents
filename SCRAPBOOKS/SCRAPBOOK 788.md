### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE / MISSING PERSONNEL DIVISION**

# **ALICE — RECOVERY & GRAVE SITE INVESTIGATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Restricted Personnel / Anomalous Investigation  
**Subject:** Alice  
**Status:** **RECOVERED — MEDICALLY CRITICAL**  
**Location:** Unknown Grave Site, Blacksite Perimeter Region  
**Associated Investigation:** Unknown Cult Activity

---

# **RECOVERY**

After more than fifteen weeks of continuous searching, **Alice has been located.**

She was discovered partially buried beneath a deep layer of snow near a grave believed to belong to her mother.

At the time of recovery, only a small portion of Alice was visible above the snow.

She was alive.

Barely.

Alice was extremely cold, severely weakened, and largely unresponsive.

Her clothing was heavily saturated from prolonged exposure to snow and ice.

Personnel immediately began excavation and emergency medical treatment.

No attempt was made to question Alice at the scene.

---

# **THE GRAVE**

The grave was immediately identified as unusual.

The marker appeared to indicate the resting place of Alice's mother.

However, excavation of the grave produced an unexpected result.

### **There was no body.**

Further excavation was conducted under controlled C.O.R.E. supervision.

The entire burial area was examined.

No human remains were discovered.

No rabbit remains were discovered.

No skeletal fragments were discovered.

No coffin or conventional burial container was recovered.

### **EXCAVATION RESULT**

> **NO HUMAN OR RABBIT REMAINS PRESENT.**

The grave is therefore currently classified as an **unverified burial site**.

It is unknown whether a body was ever placed there.

---

# **RECOVERED PAPERS**

Numerous sheets of paper were found scattered around Alice beneath and within the snow.

Most contained extremely simple handwritten statements.

Several were repeated multiple times.

Notable examples include:

> **"Who am I?"**

> **"Who is Mom?"**

> **"Where is Mom?"**

> **"I don't remember."**

> **"Who are they?"**

Some pages contained drawings of people.

However, the faces had been completely blacked out.

Each blacked-out face contained a single white question mark.

---

# **VISUAL RECORD**

The drawings appear to depict individuals Alice may have previously known.

The identities of these individuals cannot currently be determined.

The repeated use of blacked-out faces is particularly concerning.

C.O.R.E. has not established whether the markings were:

- Created by Alice.
    
- Created by another individual.
    
- Part of an anomalous process.
    
- Intended to conceal identities.
    
- Symbolic representations of Alice's memory loss.
    

No recovered document explicitly identifies the individuals.

---

# **THE SYMBOL**

Additional drawings were discovered in the immediate vicinity.

Unlike Alice's personal drawings, these depict a recurring symbol.

The symbol appears repeatedly on:

- Loose paper.
    
- Snow-covered stones.
    
- Portions of nearby trees.
    
- Several pieces of discarded material.
    
- The ground surrounding the grave.
    

Initial analysis has identified similarities between the symbol and imagery associated with a **previously undocumented cultic organization**.

The organization has not been identified.

Its name, origin, membership, purpose, and operational history remain unknown.

---

# **CULT HYPOTHESIS**

C.O.R.E. currently considers the following possibility:

> **Alice and her mother may have been hunted by an unidentified cult.**

This remains a **theory**, not a confirmed historical fact.

The hypothesis is based on several circumstantial findings:

1. Alice's unexplained memory loss concerning her mother.
    
2. The anomalous grave containing no remains.
    
3. Repeated drawings depicting unidentified people with obscured faces.
    
4. The recurring unidentified symbol.
    
5. The apparent concentration of the symbol around the grave.
    
6. Alice's prolonged presence at the site.
    
7. The possibility that the site was significant to Alice before her disappearance.
    

At present, there is insufficient evidence to determine whether the cult:

- Pursued Alice.
    
- Pursued her mother.
    
- Was responsible for her mother's disappearance.
    
- Was responsible for Alice's memory loss.
    
- Constructed or altered the grave.
    
- Is still active.
    

All possibilities remain under investigation.

---

# **ALICE'S CONDITION**

Alice remains medically fragile.

Following recovery, she displayed significant confusion regarding her surroundings.

When asked whether she recognized the grave, Alice reportedly stared at the marker for an extended period.

She eventually whispered:

> "...Mom?"

She did not elaborate.

When shown one of the recovered papers containing **"Who is Mom?"**, Alice reportedly became visibly distressed.

She was not questioned further.

C.O.R.E. medical personnel have ordered that:

- Alice not be interrogated regarding the grave.
    
- Alice not be shown recovered cult imagery without medical approval.
    
- No recovered documents be placed within her immediate living environment.
    
- Memory-recovery attempts be conducted only if medically appropriate.
    
- Alice's physical recovery take priority over investigative questioning.

---

# **CURRENT INVESTIGATION**

The grave site has been placed under C.O.R.E. security.

All recovered papers have been catalogued.

The unidentified symbol has been designated:

> **CULT SYMBOL — UNCLASSIFIED**

Until the organization associated with the symbol can be identified, personnel are prohibited from attempting independent investigation or contacting individuals claiming affiliation with it.

The possibility that the organization remains active has **not** been ruled out.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin was present when Alice was formally confirmed recovered.

She did not immediately approach the grave.

Her attention remained on Alice.

After medical personnel moved Alice to safety, Corin reportedly looked toward the empty grave and said:

> "...There wasn't anyone here."

She paused.

> "...Then where did Mom go?"

No answer was available.

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

The recovery of Alice has answered one question.

### **She is alive.**

It has created considerably more.

Who was Alice's mother?

Why does Alice no longer remember her?

Why is the grave empty?

Who are the people whose faces have been erased from Alice's drawings?

What does the symbol represent?

And most importantly:

### **Who was hunting them?**

At this time, C.O.R.E. cannot determine whether Alice was merely hiding at the grave...

...or whether she had been trying to return to the only place she believed her mother might still be.

The investigation remains active.

Alice is safe for the moment.

The grave is not.
