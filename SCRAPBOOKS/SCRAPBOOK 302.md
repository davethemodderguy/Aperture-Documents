# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – NUTRITIONAL OBSERVATION REPORT**

### **DESIGNATION: M.A.R.T. BLOBS – FEEDING HABITS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although Marts primarily sustain themselves through water and positive emotional stimulation, they have been observed voluntarily consuming conventional food.

The nutritional purpose of this behavior remains unknown.

Research has found no evidence that Marts require food to survive.

Instead, it appears they simply...

Enjoy eating.

---

# **DIET**

Marts display remarkably broad tastes.

Documented foods include:

- Bread.
    
- Rice.
    
- Fruits.
    
- Vegetables.
    
- Soup.
    
- Meat.
    
- Fish.
    
- Cookies.
    
- Chocolate.
    
- Ice cream.
    
- Noodles.
    
- Moss.
    
- Flowers.
    
- Candy.
    

To date, no edible item has been rejected.

When offered food, the usual response is an enthusiastic:

> "Food!"

Followed immediately by the Mart engulfing the item.

---

# **FAVORITE SNACK**

Across both Heaven's Blacksite and the Krampus Compliance Authority, one food consistently receives the greatest reaction.

> **Cheese crackers.**

Opening a box of cheese crackers is often enough to attract nearby Marts within seconds.

Researchers are unsure how they recognize the sound of the packaging.

Attempts to disguise the crackers inside identical containers have failed.

The Marts always find them.

---

# **DIGESTION**

Exactly how Marts process food remains one of the colony's greatest biological mysteries.

After consuming an item, the food rapidly disappears within their body.

No crumbs.

No residue.

No waste products.

No measurable change in body composition.

Chemical analysis has failed to identify any digestive organs or metabolic pathways.

The food simply...

Ceases to exist.

Research currently lists the mechanism as:

> **Unknown.**

---

# **FEEDING ETIQUETTE**

Marts are exceptionally polite during meals.

They have never been observed:

- Fighting over food.
    
- Hoarding snacks.
    
- Stealing meals from personnel.
    
- Demanding additional portions.
    

Instead, they patiently wait to be offered something.

If they receive food, they happily respond:

> "Thank you!"

If they do not, they simply continue with their day.

---

> "Yes!"

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Mart colony continues to puzzle researchers.

An organism sustained by water and happiness should have no biological need for conventional food.

Yet they eagerly accept almost anything offered to them, somehow dissolving it completely through a process no instrument has successfully measured.

Research has many theories.

The Marts have only one.

When a fresh box of cheese crackers is opened somewhere in the Blacksite...

Hundreds of tiny voices can often be heard echoing down the hallway.

> "Food!"

**End of Nutritional Observation Report**
