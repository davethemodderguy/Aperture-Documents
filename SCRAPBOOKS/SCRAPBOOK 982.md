# BLACKSITE ENVIRONMENTAL ANOMALY REPORT

## AZURE DUST

**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE / BLACKSITE INTERNAL  
**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Phenomenon:** UV-Reactive Azure Particulate  
**Associated Subject:** Neri  
**Detection Method:** Ultraviolet illumination

---

## I. DISCOVERY

The phenomenon was discovered accidentally.

A maintenance worker was performing a routine ultraviolet inspection of the SAFEHOUSE after a minor biological contamination alarm.

Under normal lighting, the facility appeared completely clean.

Under UV illumination—

**the Blacksite was covered in blue.**

Not stained.

Not painted.

Not glowing under ordinary light.

Dust.

Fine, microscopic-to-visible particulate scattered across surfaces throughout the facility.

It appeared as a faint **azure-blue shimmer** under ultraviolet light.

The effect was initially assumed to be residue from an unrelated anomalous material.

It was not.

---

# II. DISTRIBUTION

The particulate was found throughout nearly every examined section of the Blacksite.

It was present on:

- Floors.
    
- Walls.
    
- Door handles.
    
- Railings.
    
- Elevator interiors.
    
- Cafeteria tables.
    
- Laboratory equipment.
    
- Archive shelves.
    
- Maintenance corridors.
    
- Security checkpoints.
    
- Ventilation structures.
    
- SAFEHOUSE residential areas.
    
- Neri's former room.
    
- The memorial display.
    
- Her fishbone hairclip.
    
- Her Starlight Katana.
    
- Her crossbow.
    
- Her knives.
    

The concentration varied.

Some areas contained barely detectable traces.

Others showed substantial accumulation.

Most disturbing was the fact that **no visible blue material existed under normal illumination.**

The facility appeared perfectly clean.

The moment UV light was introduced, the Blacksite seemed to have been dusted with stars.

---

# III. PHOTOGRAPHIC DESCRIPTION

Under ordinary lighting:

> **Nothing unusual.**

Under UV:

> **Azure particulate covering the environment.**

The particles do not behave like conventional dust.

They appear to cling to surfaces without regard to gravity.

Some particles remain suspended in the air.

Others slowly drift through corridors.

Occasionally, a cluster can be observed moving against normal airflow.

No mechanical source has been identified.

---

# IV. GUIDING LIGHT

Guiding Light was brought in immediately.

She entered the cafeteria.

A technician activated the UV inspection system.

The walls illuminated.

The floor illuminated.

The tables illuminated.

Neri's old chair illuminated.

Guiding Light stared.

There was azure dust across almost everything.

For several seconds, she said nothing.

Then:

> “...Oh.”

The technician asked whether she recognized it.

Guiding Light nodded.

> “It's Light.”

A pause.

> “But not exactly mine.”

She moved closer to the cafeteria table.

There was considerably more particulate around Neri's usual seat.

Guiding Light touched the table.

The azure dust reacted.

For approximately two seconds, the entire surface became brighter.

Then it returned to its previous state.

---

# V. THE QUESTION

Neko immediately asked the question everyone else was thinking.

> “How much of the Blacksite is contaminated?”

Guiding Light looked around.

> “All of it.”

Silence.

Neko checked the instrumentation.

> “Define all.”

Guiding Light pointed down the corridor.

> “The whole place.”

---

# VI. IT WASN'T THERE BEFORE

Historical surveillance footage was reviewed.

No visible azure particulate could be identified under normal lighting.

Archived UV inspection footage was then examined.

The result was unexpected.

The dust was already present.

Months before Neri disappeared.

Small quantities.

Almost undetectable.

The concentration gradually increased over time.

The earliest reliable record showed only a handful of particles.

Later inspections showed hundreds.

Then thousands.

Nobody had noticed because **nobody had been looking with UV.**

The phenomenon had apparently been accumulating quietly for a very long time.

---

# VII. NERI'S TRAIL

A specialized UV survey was conducted.

The results produced a strange pattern.

The highest concentrations corresponded to places Neri frequently occupied.

Her cafeteria table.

Her room.

The laboratory.

The greenhouse.

Several corridors she regularly used.

Her subspace access point.

The SAFEHOUSE common areas.

But the dust did not stop there.

There were traces in places Neri had only visited briefly.

There were even traces in locations where no surviving record confirms that she had ever been.

This made the phenomenon significantly harder to interpret.

---

# VIII. THE MEMORIAL

The memorial became the most heavily concentrated location.

Under normal lighting, the display looked unchanged.

Under UV—

the fishbone hairclip appeared surrounded by a dense cloud of azure particulate.

The Starlight Katana emitted a faint blue outline.

The crossbow displayed scattered points of light.

The knives remained mostly unaffected.

And the blueberry cupcake—

had a small amount of azure dust resting around its base.

Guiding Light stared at it.

She quietly said:

> “She really did leave pieces of herself everywhere.”

Neko corrected her.

> “We don't know that.”

Guiding Light smiled sadly.

> “I know.”

> “I just like thinking about it.”

---

# IX. NO CLEANUP ORDER

A request was submitted to remove the particulate.

It was rejected.

**Reason:**

> “Unknown relationship to missing SAFEHOUSE resident.”

A second request was submitted.

Rejected.

A third request was submitted with a proposed containment protocol.

Rejected.

The final administrative response was considerably shorter:

> **LEAVE IT.**

---

# X. SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE

The discovery changed the way people walked through the Blacksite.

Workers began carrying small UV inspection lights.

Not for security.

Not for contamination.

Just to look.

Someone would occasionally shine one across a hallway.

Azure would appear.

A faint trail.

A cluster.

A handful of particles suspended in the air.

Sometimes someone would stop.

Sometimes they would smile.

Sometimes they would just stand there.

Nobody calls it contamination anymore.

The unofficial name has become:

### **Neri Dust**

Neko has formally prohibited the term from appearing in scientific documentation.

Nobody listens.

---

# XI. FINAL OBSERVATION

At 02:13, a technician conducted a UV inspection of the cafeteria.

The room was empty.

Neri's chair remained empty.

The blueberry cupcake had been replaced with a fresh one.

The technician switched on the UV lamp.

Azure dust appeared across the room.

Then something unusual happened.

For approximately **four seconds**, the particles around Neri's chair began drifting upward.

They formed a loose, incomplete shape.

Almost like two small wings.

Then they dispersed.

The technician did not attempt to collect them.

He simply turned off the lamp.

The cafeteria returned to normal.
