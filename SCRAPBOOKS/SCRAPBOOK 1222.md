# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A previously unknown member of the Tachyon family has been identified within the existing SAFEHOUSE population.

The individual is:

> **Elsher Newman**

Previously known to C.O.R.E. as a longtime SAFEHOUSE resident, MSAC participant, and holder of multiple Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise items.

He is, in fact:

> **Elsher Tachyon**

and is the **oldest known surviving sibling** of:

> **Neri Tachyon**

and

> **Nora Tachyon.**

The resulting sibling order is:

# **ELSHER → NERI → NORA**

No personnel had previously connected Elsher Newman to the Tachyon family.

This was intentional.

Elsher had deliberately avoided drawing attention to his family identity.

---

# 2. INITIAL DISCOVERY

The discovery occurred inside the Tachyon Industries Merch Store.

Neri was reviewing personnel documentation relating to one of the store's existing customers and Limited Merchandise holders.

The file belonged to:

> **ELSHER NEWMAN**

Neri noticed something unusual.

The face photograph appeared familiar.

Not merely familiar in the ordinary sense.

There was a resemblance.

Neri continued looking at the photograph.

She then looked toward Elsher.

Then back at the file.

Then at Elsher again.

No conclusion was initially recorded.

---

# 3. NORA'S JOURNAL

Nora happened to be present.

She noticed Neri examining the file.

> **Nora:** “What?”

Neri pointed at the photograph.

> **Neri:** “Him.”

Nora approached.

She looked at the photograph.

Then at Elsher.

Then at Neri.

She immediately pulled the **Tachyon family journal** from the clip at her waist.

Nora began turning pages.

Photographs.

Drawings.

Names.

Family relationships.

Old records.

She stopped.

---

# 4. THE NAME

Nora placed one finger against a handwritten entry.

The entry read:

> **ELSHER TACHYON**

Nora looked at Neri.

Then at the personnel file.

The file read:

> **ELSHER NEWMAN**

> **Nora:** “That's him.”

# 5. CONFIRMATION

C.O.R.E. initiated a restricted family-relationship review.

Available archival records, biological evidence, historical photographs, and Tachyon family documentation were cross-referenced.

The conclusion was unequivocal.

> **Elsher Newman is Elsher Tachyon.**

He is the biological older brother of Neri Tachyon.

He is also the biological older brother of Nora Tachyon.

The family relationship is therefore:

|Birth Order|Name|Status|
|--:|---|---|
|1|**Elsher Tachyon** / Elsher Newman|Oldest|
|2|**Neri Tachyon**|Middle|
|3|**Nora Tachyon**|Youngest|

# 6. WHY NOBODY KNEW

The investigation determined that Elsher had deliberately maintained the surname:

> **Newman**

rather than publicly identifying himself as Tachyon.

This was not an attempt to deceive SAFEHOUSE personnel for malicious purposes.

Elsher simply did not want the attention associated with the Tachyon family name.

His position was summarized during interview:

> **“I didn't want everyone treating me differently.”**

When asked whether he had known about his relationship to Neri and Nora:

Elsher paused.

> **“I knew I was a Tachyon.”**

> **“Why didn't you tell anyone?”**

> **“Because then I'd be the Tachyon guy.”**

Pause.

> **“I'm already Elsher.”**

---

# 10. THE TACHYON MERCHANDISE CONNECTION

The discovery creates an unusual historical coincidence.

Elsher already owns:

- **NS-006 — Night Stalker**
    
- **SOH-002 — Sleight of Hand**

He subsequently acquired a **Spearhead** after completing its qualification requirements.

At the time of acquisition, neither Elsher nor Neri had publicly documented the sibling relationship.
