## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL / SAFEHOUSE INCIDENT REPORT**

# **CORIN — THE NIGHTMARE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Incident Type:** **Acute Psychological Episode / Nightmare**  
**Status:** Stabilized

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin experienced an unexpected psychological breakdown during sleep.

There was no apparent external trigger.

No anomalous attack occurred.

No Ether instability was detected.

Corin simply fell asleep.

Then she dreamed.

And in that dream, her past found its way into her present.

---

# **THE NIGHTMARE**

Corin's dream began normally.

She was in the SAFEHOUSE.

The lights were on.

The kitchen was warm.

Someone was talking nearby.

Her wings were folded comfortably against her back.

Her ears moved at every little sound.

Everything felt normal.

Then something changed.

The SAFEHOUSE disappeared.

The walls became unfamiliar.

The warmth vanished.

The familiar voices became distant.

Corin found herself standing somewhere she recognized.

A laboratory.

---

# **THE PAST**

The dream reconstructed fragments of Corin's past.

The laboratory.

The equipment.

The isolation.

The feeling of being observed rather than treated as a person.

The memories were incomplete.

Some were distorted.

Others were painfully clear.

Corin looked down at herself.

Her wings were gone.

Her cat ears were gone.

Her tail was gone.

She was back to the thing she had once believed herself to be.

Something created in a laboratory.

Something that was neither alive nor dead.

Something that only continued moving because Ether forced it to.

---

# **THE VOICE**

A voice came from somewhere behind her.

> **"You're not supposed to be alive."**

Corin turned.

Nobody was there.

Another voice followed.

> **"You're not a person."**

Then another.

> **"You're just a thing."**

Corin covered her ears.

Her ears were not there.

She tried to move her wings.

There were no wings.

She tried to wake herself.

She couldn't.

---

# **THE PRESENT RETURNS**

Then something appeared.

A familiar blue glow.

Guiding Light.

The SAFEHOUSE.

Her wings.

Her cat ears.

Her tail.

The smell of jasmine tea.

The sound of Belle laughing.

Neko complaining about yarn.

Orphie calling her name.

All of it appeared at once.

But the laboratory remained.

Her past and present existed simultaneously.

Corin could not tell which one was real.

---

# **THE BREAKDOWN**

Corin began panicking.

She tried to run.

The laboratory followed her.

She flew.

The laboratory followed her into the sky.

She tried to hide beneath her wings.

The laboratory was still there.

Her past had become impossible to escape.

Corin eventually collapsed.

> **"Stop..."**

Her voice shook.

> **"Please stop..."**

The voices continued.

> **"You're an abomination."**

> **"You're a thing."**

> **"You shouldn't exist."**

Corin screamed.

---

# **THE SAFEHOUSE**

Corin woke suddenly.

Her wings exploded outward.

Several feathers scattered across the room.

Her heart was racing.

She was breathing heavily.

Her ears were flattened completely against her head.

Her tail was wrapped tightly around herself.

She stared at the room.

For several seconds, she didn't recognize it.

Then she whispered:

> **"...SAFEHOUSE?"**

No answer came immediately.

Corin began crying.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie eventually found her.

Corin immediately tried to apologize.

> **"I'm sorry."**

Orphie sat beside her.

> **"For what?"**

Corin couldn't answer.

She simply held onto Orphie.

Her wings wrapped around both of them.

She shook for several minutes.

Orphie stayed.

She did not ask Corin to explain.

She did not tell her to calm down.

She simply remained there.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light arrived after being informed.

She immediately recognized the significance.

The nightmare had not been an anomalous intrusion.

It was Corin's own memory.

Years of experiences that had never completely disappeared.

Guiding Light quietly said:

> **"Your past is still part of you."**

She paused.

> **"But it isn't where you live anymore."**

Corin looked at her.

Her eyes were still wet.

> **"...It felt real."**

> **"I know."**

---

# **THE ARCHITECTS**

The Architects were unable to simply erase the nightmare.

They could suppress anomalous effects.

They could manipulate Ether.

They could alter reality.

But this was not an anomaly that needed to be destroyed.

It was Corin remembering.

And none of them wanted to erase part of her simply because it hurt.

Guiding Light remained particularly quiet.

She knew that her original blessing had helped Corin become who she was now.

But she also knew that she could not simply bless away every painful memory.

---

# **AFTERWARD**

Corin eventually calmed down.

She remained awake for the rest of the night.

She made herself a cup of jasmine tea.

Her wings remained wrapped around her like a blanket.

Orphie stayed nearby.

Eventually, Corin quietly said:

> **"I thought I left that place behind."**

Orphie answered:

> **"You did."**

Corin looked down.

> **"...Then why does it keep following me?"**

Nobody had an easy answer.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin has changed enormously.

She is alive.

She has blood.

She breathes.

She cooks.

She flies.

She laughs.

She plays with yarn.

She has cat ears and a tail.

She has people who care about her.

But becoming something new did not erase what came before.

Sometimes the past returns without warning.

Sometimes it appears in dreams.

Sometimes it takes the face of the place Corin escaped from.

And sometimes, for a few terrible minutes, the past and present overlap until Corin cannot tell which one she belongs to.

Tonight, she eventually remembered.

She was not in the laboratory.

She was not alone.

She was not a thing.

She was Corin.

And the wings wrapped around her were real.

**End of Psychological / SAFEHOUSE Incident Report**
