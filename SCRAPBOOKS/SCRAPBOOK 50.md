# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **SAFEHOUSE ACTIVITY LOG**

### **SPECIAL EVENT – SENIOR MANAGER NORMA LOCKWOOD'S BIRTHDAY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Internal (Happy Birthday Edition)

---

## **DATE**

Today marks the birthday of **Senior Manager Norma Lockwood**, KCA Senior Manager, CCD Researcher, Engineer, Chemist, Blacksmith, Locksmith, Biochemist...

...and, according to Neko,

> "The only person capable of understanding half of my blueprints."

---

## **PREPARATIONS**

The celebration was planned in complete secrecy.

Unfortunately...

Keeping it a secret from Norma proved nearly impossible.

She noticed:

- The unusually quiet kitchen.
    
- Belle disappearing every few minutes.
    
- Neko carrying suspiciously large boxes.
    
- Cinder refusing to let anyone into a storage room.
    
- Star humming "Happy Birthday" without realizing it.
    

Norma looked at Belle.

> "...You're planning something."

Belle looked away.

> "...No."

A pause.

The entire room looked away with her.

Norma smiled.

> "...You're all terrible at hiding surprises."

---

## **THE PARTY**

At precisely 7:00 PM, the lights dimmed.

The room fell silent.

Then—

> **"SURPRISE!"**

The lights came back on.

Colorful decorations covered the SAFEHOUSE common room.

A homemade banner stretched across the ceiling.

> **HAPPY BIRTHDAY, NORMIE!**

Norma stood frozen for several seconds.

Then quietly laughed.

> "...You really did all this..."

---

## **THE CAKE**

Belle and Norma are both excellent cooks.

Naturally...

Belle insisted on making the birthday cake herself.

It was a layered vanilla cake decorated with blue icing, white chocolate gears, and tiny edible wrench decorations.

On top sat a small fondant machine gun beside an even smaller fondant sledgehammer.

Belle proudly pointed at them.

> "...They're cute."

Norma nodded.

> "...They are."

---

## **THE GIFTS**

### **Belle**

A hand-knitted teal hoodie.

Inside the pocket was a handwritten note.

> _"Thank you for always being my little sister."_

Norma hugged Belle immediately.

---

### **Endmin**

A custom leather-bound engineering notebook.

Every page was waterproof.

Every measurement grid was perfectly aligned.

Norma spent several minutes admiring the craftsmanship.

---

### **Neko**

A toolbox.

Everyone became nervous.

Norma slowly opened it.

Inside...

Was a perfectly organized collection of precision engineering tools.

No hidden explosives.

No experimental devices.

No spring-loaded surprises.

Norma looked at him.

> "...You made a normal gift?"

Neko smiled proudly.

> "...Miss Neko supervised."

---

### **Miss Neko**

A new laboratory coat embroidered with:

> **Norma Lockwood**

> **Senior Manager**

On the inside collar was stitched one small sentence.

> _"Please remember to rest."_

---

### **Kent**

A handcrafted wooden display stand for Arcturus' custom .357 cartridges.

> "...Figured you'd appreciate the craftsmanship."

She did.

---

### **Remielle (Cinder)**

A beautifully forged utility knife.

Simple.

Elegant.

Practical.

Exactly the kind of tool Norma liked.

---

### **Star**

A glass jar filled with shimmering Stardust.

The label simply read:

> **"Emergency Happiness."**

---

### **Nightshade**

A pressed Night Blossom sealed inside a glass frame.

No words.

She quietly handed it to Norma.

Norma smiled.

> "...Thank you."

---

### **Taiga**

A giant fluffy blanket.

> "...For naps."

---

### **Mart**

A first-edition engineering textbook.

> "...I found it in the library archives."

Norma nearly gasped.

---

### **Frost**

A handcrafted crystal teacup.

Designed to keep tea warm without burning the hands.

---

### **Bao**

An assortment of homemade desserts.

"...Because one cake isn't enough."

---

## **SPECIAL GUEST**

A dimensional gateway quietly opened.

Arcturus stepped through.

Blue fedora.

Glasses.

Gift box under one arm.

Norma blinked.

> "...You came."

He smiled.

> "...Wouldn't miss my little sister's birthday."

Inside the box...

Was an old family photograph.

One Norma hadn't seen in years.

For a long moment...

She couldn't say anything.

Then she hugged him.

> "...Thanks, big brother."

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Lucious raised a teacup.

> "To Senior Manager Norma Lockwood."

> "May your curiosity never fade."

Everyone raised their drinks.

Some held tea.

Some held milk.

Arcturus...

Held a can of soda.

## **FINAL ENTRY**

Late that night, after everyone had gone to bed, Norma sat quietly in the SAFEHOUSE common room.

A cup of warm milk tea rested beside her.

The old family photograph lay on the table.

She looked toward the group bedroom, where laughter from earlier had given way to peaceful silence.

She smiled to herself.

For years, the KCA had been her workplace.

Now...

SAFEHOUSE was her home.

And for the first time in a very long time, she celebrated her birthday surrounded not only by coworkers...

...but by family.

**Happy Birthday, Norma Lockwood.**
