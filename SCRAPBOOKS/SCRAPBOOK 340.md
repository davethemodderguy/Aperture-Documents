# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **HUMAN RESOURCES & SECURITY DIVISION**

### **PERSONNEL PROFILE**

# **FROM ROBBERS TO GUARDS**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Former KCA Robbery Crew

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Several current KCA Security Officers were, at one point, members of separate robbery crews that attempted to rob the Krampus Compliance Authority.

Each attempt ended similarly:

- The robbery failed.
    
- The suspects were subdued by Rapid Response Marts.
    
- No shots were fired.
    
- No serious injuries occurred.
    
- The suspects surrendered.
    

After review by **Belle Merryweather** and **Dialyn**, it was determined that these individuals had caused no lasting damage to the facility or its personnel.

Rather than pursue severe criminal charges, they were offered an opportunity to rebuild their lives through legitimate employment.

Several accepted.

They now serve as members of KCA Security.

---

# **CURRENT RESPONSIBILITIES**

Former robbery crew members now perform the same duties as other KCA security staff, including:

- Lobby security.
    
- Patrols.
    
- Visitor assistance.
    
- Security monitoring.
    
- Emergency response.
    
- Mart care and welfare.
    
- Refilling Mart water stations.
    
- Distributing sugary cheese crackers.
    
- Retrieving drifting Marts from safety nets.
    
- Helping Rapid Response Marts return to the ventilation network after deployments.
    

Most have become particularly protective of the Marts.

---

# **RELATIONSHIP WITH MARTS**

Initially, interactions were understandably awkward.

The Marts clearly remembered them.

However, they did not appear to remember them as enemies.

Instead, they greeted them with:

> "Friend!"

One former robber later admitted:

> "...That somehow made me feel worse than if they'd been angry."

Within weeks, the Marts had completely accepted them.

Many now follow them during patrols.

Several Rapid Response Marts have developed favorite security officers.

---

# **PERSONNEL OBSERVATIONS**

One former robber is almost never seen without at least three Marts following him.

When asked why, the Ambassador explained:

> "...He gives good head pats."

---

Another officer habitually carries an emergency box of sugary cheese crackers.

He insists it is:

> "...Official security equipment."

No one has argued otherwise.

---

A third officer has become skilled at untangling Marts from Neko's window safety nets.

The Marts now seek him out whenever one becomes stuck.

---

# **THE FIRST SHIFT**

Records from the crew's first official security shift note an unusual interaction.

One Rapid Response Mart approached a former robber.

It looked up.

> "Friend?"

The officer hesitated.

Then knelt down.

> "...Yeah."

The Mart immediately climbed onto his shoulder.

It remained there for the rest of the patrol.

---

# **RAPID RESPONSE TRAINING**

Because they had personally experienced Rapid Response deployments, the former robbers possess unique insight into Mart tactics.

They now assist in training new security personnel.

Common advice includes:

> "If the vents suddenly become quiet..."

> "...They're already behind you."

---

> "Don't panic if one turns into a rock."

> "Panic when three hundred of them do."

---

> "If they ask whether you're okay..."

> "...Answer them."

> "...Otherwise they'll worry."

---

# **DIALYN'S COMMENT**

> "...Most security guards learn how the Marts work from manuals."

She looked toward one of the former robbers laughing as a Mart sat on his helmet.

> "...These guys learned the hard way."

---

# **BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

> "...People can change."

She watched one of the officers refill a Mart water basin before continuing his patrol.

> "...Sometimes all they need is someone willing to believe they can."

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi visited the KCA shortly after the officers completed their probation.

She noticed several Marts happily accompanying them.

> "...Looks like they've forgiven you."

One former robber smiled.

> "...I'm still trying to forgive myself."

A Mart gently bumped against his leg.

> "Friend."

He quietly replied:

> "...Thanks."

---

# **CAMERA MART**

Camera Mart now recognizes the former robbers as authorized personnel.

During one morning shift, it greeted them over the internal security system.

> "Good morning."

One officer looked toward the camera.

> "...Morning."

Camera Mart paused.

> "...Don't rob today."

The entire security office burst into laughter.

The officer raised his hands.

> "...Wasn't planning on it."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Visitors occasionally ask why several KCA security officers already seem to know exactly how to deal with Rapid Response Marts.

The answer is surprisingly simple.

They've been on both sides.

Once...

They tried to get past the blue blobs.

Now...

They stand beside them.

As one officer was finishing his patrol, a Mart climbed onto his shoulder and proudly announced to a passing visitor:

> **"Security!"**

The officer smiled.

He gently adjusted the Mart's tiny top hat.

> "...Yeah."

> "...Security."

**End of Personnel Profile**
