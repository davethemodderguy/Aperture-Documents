### **RESIDENTIAL / BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION LOG**

# **CORIN VS. DIALYN — THE SPICE INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Internal Personnel  
**Subjects:** Corin / Dialyn  
**Affiliation:** Kramus Compliance Authority  
**Status:** **ONGOING COMPETITION**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has demonstrated an unusual tolerance for spicy food.

This would not normally be noteworthy.

However, her tolerance appears to be approximately equal to that of **Dialyn**, a Customer Support Representative for the **Kramus Compliance Authority** who is already infamous among Blacksite personnel for consuming extremely spicy food without visible discomfort.

The discovery was accidental.

It has since become a problem.

---

# **THE FIRST ENCOUNTER**

During a shared meal, Dialyn brought an exceptionally spicy dish.

Several personnel immediately declined.

Corin looked at it.

Then at Dialyn.

Then back at the food.

> **Corin:**  
> "Is it spicy?"

Dialyn nodded.

> **"Very."**

Corin took a bite.

She paused.

Everyone waited.

Corin calmly continued eating.

Dialyn stared.

> **"...You okay?"**

Corin nodded.

> **"It tastes good."**

Dialyn smiled.

> **"Oh."**

A brief silence followed.

> **"...We need to test this."**

---

# **THE COMPETITION**

What began as curiosity became an unofficial contest.

Dialyn increased the spice level.

Corin kept eating.

Corin increased the spice level.

Dialyn kept eating.

Eventually, everyone else stopped participating.

Neko simply watched from a safe distance.

> **Neko:**  
> "This is stupid."

Orphie nodded.

> **"Very."**

Neither left.

---

# **CORIN'S ADVANTAGE**

Corin's unusual physiology provides her with an interesting advantage.

She does not digest food normally.

Anything consumed is eventually dissolved into Ether.

However, this does **not** eliminate taste.

Corin can still experience flavor.

She therefore experiences the enjoyable part of spicy food without experiencing many of the conventional consequences associated with digestion.

Dialyn considers this unfair.

Corin considers this hilarious.

---

# **DIALYN'S RESPONSE**

Dialyn refuses to concede.

As a Kramus Compliance Authority Customer Support Representative, she has absolutely no professional reason to participate in competitive spice consumption.

She does it anyway.

> **Dialyn:**  
> "I'm not losing to a dead girl."

Corin slowly looked at her.

Then wrote:

> **"That's rude."**

Dialyn laughed.

> **"Sorry."**

Corin wrote:

> **"Still winning."**

---

# **NEKO'S OBSERVATION**

Neko eventually became the unofficial provider of sandwiches during these contests.

He specifically includes pickles for Corin.

He has also established a rule:

> **"Nobody is allowed to use anything that can permanently damage the table."**

This rule exists because of Dialyn.

Nobody has asked for clarification.

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

Orphie has attempted to discourage the competition.

It has not worked.

> **Orphie:**  
> "You two know you don't have to prove anything, right?"

Dialyn:

> **"I know."**

Corin:

> **"I know."**

They continued eating.

Orphie sighed.

---

# **CORIN'S ASSESSMENT**

When asked why she likes spicy food so much, Corin simply wrote:

> **"It tastes good."**

After a moment, she added:

> **"And Dialyn makes it fun."**

Dialyn immediately smiled.

> **"See?"**

Orphie:

> **"I regret introducing you two."**

---

# **CURRENT RECORD**

No official maximum has been established.

C.O.R.E. strongly recommends against attempting to establish one.

Previous attempts have resulted in:

- Several empty bottles of hot sauce.
    
- One destroyed measuring spoon.
    
- Neko refusing to participate.
    
- Orphie leaving the room.
    
- Dialyn demanding "one more round."
    
- Corin calmly asking for another sandwich.
    

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin does not need to eat.

She does not gain meaningful nutrition from food.

Her body simply dissolves what she consumes into Ether.

But she can still taste.

And apparently, she can taste **painfully spicy food** just as well as Dialyn.

Nobody knows why.

Nobody particularly cares.

Because watching a dead Ether-sustained girl and a Kramus Compliance Authority customer service representative calmly eating increasingly ridiculous amounts of spicy food has somehow become one of the more normal things happening around the Blacksite.

**End of Residential / Behavioral Observation Log**
