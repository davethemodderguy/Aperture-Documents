# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION REPORT**

# **CORIN — BALLS OF YARN**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Observation:** **Unexpected Feline-Like Interest in Yarn**  
**Status:** Harmless

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has developed an unusual fondness for **balls of yarn**.

The behavior appears to be spontaneous.

There is no known anomalous explanation.

Corin simply enjoys playing with them.

The behavior has been repeatedly compared to that of a cat.

---

# **OBSERVED BEHAVIOR**

When presented with a ball of yarn, Corin will often:

- Pick it up.
    
- Roll it across the floor.
    
- Chase it.
    
- Pull at loose strands.
    
- Wrap the yarn around her fingers.
    
- Attempt to unravel it.
    
- Toss it into the air.
    
- Chase it again.
    

Her wings occasionally become involved.

Corin has been observed using a wingtip to nudge a ball of yarn across the floor before chasing it.

---

# **THE CAT COMPARISON**

The comparison became unavoidable after Neko witnessed Corin playing with yarn for approximately twenty minutes.

He watched silently.

Eventually:

> **Neko:**  
> "...You're acting like a cat."

Corin stopped.

She looked at the yarn.

Then at Neko.

> **"No."**

Neko pointed at the ball.

> **"You're chasing yarn."**

Corin hugged the yarn against herself.

> **"...Cats don't own yarn."**

Neko paused.

> **"That's your defense?"**

Corin nodded.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

Belle finds the behavior adorable.

She occasionally rolls yarn toward Corin just to watch her chase it.

This has resulted in several informal competitions to see how far a ball can be rolled before Corin notices.

Corin usually notices immediately.

Her wings make chasing it considerably easier.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has discovered that leaving a ball of yarn near Corin's usual sitting area almost guarantees that Corin will eventually pick it up.

She has therefore started keeping several small balls of yarn around the SAFEHOUSE.

Corin has never questioned where they come from.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light was initially confused.

> **"Why do you like yarn?"**

Corin shrugged.

> **"It's fun."**

Guiding Light accepted the answer.

After watching Corin enthusiastically chase a rolling ball across the room, she added:

> **"...You really do act like a cat sometimes."**

Corin frowned.

> **"Fish does too."**

This was technically true.

---

# **POSSIBLE EXPLANATIONS**

C.O.R.E. has considered several explanations.

### **1. Instinct**

Corin may simply find the texture and movement stimulating.

### **2. Comfort**

The repetitive motion may have a calming effect.

### **3. Anomalous Influence**

No evidence currently supports this explanation.

### **4. She Just Likes Yarn**

This remains the leading hypothesis.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The behavior is considered completely harmless.

No anomalous properties have been detected in the yarn.

Corin has been permitted to continue enjoying it.

A small basket of yarn has been added to the SAFEHOUSE.

It is now officially classified as:

> **CORIN'S YARN**

Neko has requested that nobody leave it near the stairs.

Nobody asked why.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin is capable of astral projection.

She possesses regenerative abilities.

She can fly.

Her feathers can function as blades or Ether-infused projectiles.

She has a biologically restored body.

And apparently, none of that prevents her from sitting on the floor for twenty minutes chasing a ball of yarn.

Perhaps the Architects were right about one thing.

Corin may have accidentally acquired a few more traits than anyone expected.

Including:

**Cat behavior.**

**End of Behavioral Observation Report**
