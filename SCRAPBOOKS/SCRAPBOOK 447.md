# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CELESTIAL ARCHITECTURE / PERSONNEL INTERACTION REPORT**

# **MARTS — FIRST ENCOUNTER WITH GUIDING LIGHT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Celestial Authority  
**Subject:** Guiding Light  
**Personnel:** Marts  
**Status:** **NON-HOSTILE / FRIENDLY**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Marts has officially met **Guiding Light**.

The encounter was expected to be unusual.

It was not expected to reveal that Guiding Light already had close personal relationships with individuals inside the SAFEHOUSE.

Specifically:

**Lumi** and **Astral**.

Both are apparently among Guiding Light's **closest friends**.

This revelation came as a considerable surprise to Marts.

---

# **THE ENCOUNTER**

Marts encountered Guiding Light inside the SAFEHOUSE central living area.

A familiar soft blue glow appeared near the doorway.

Marts looked over.

Guiding Light was floating quietly above the floor.

Her shattered halo and fragmented wings slowly drifted around her.

Marts froze.

> **Marts:**  
> "...You're Guiding Light."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "I am."

Marts stared at her for several seconds.

> **Marts:**  
> "...You're much more sparkly than I expected."

Guiding Light laughed.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "I'll take that as a compliment."

---

# **THE INTRODUCTION**

Marts approached cautiously.

> **Marts:**  
> "...So you're the one leaving all those blue notes?"

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Yes."

> **Marts:**  
> "...And you're one of the Architects?"

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Yes."

Marts nodded slowly.

> **Marts:**  
> "...Cool."

Guiding Light smiled.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "You don't have to be nervous."

> **Marts:**  
> "I'm standing in front of someone who helped make reality."

A pause.

> **Marts:**  
> "I'm allowed to be nervous."

Guiding Light laughed again.

---

# **THE SURPRISE**

The conversation changed completely when **Lumi** entered the room.

She stopped.

Guiding Light turned toward her.

The two simply stared at one another.

Then Guiding Light smiled.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Lumi."

Lumi immediately smiled back.

> **Lumi:**  
> "You finally decided to show yourself."

Marts looked between them.

> **Marts:**  
> "...Wait."

Lumi walked over to Guiding Light.

There was no fear.

No hesitation.

They greeted each other like old friends.

Marts became increasingly confused.

> **Marts:**  
> "...You two know each other?"

Lumi laughed.

> **Lumi:**  
> "Know each other?"

She looked at Guiding Light.

> **Lumi:**  
> "She's my best friend."

Marts froze.

> **Marts:**  
> "...WHAT?"

---

# **ASTRAL**

The situation became even more confusing when Astral arrived.

Guiding Light immediately turned toward her.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Astral."

Astral smiled.

> **Astral:**  
> "It's been a while."

Marts stared.

> **Marts:**  
> "...You too?"

Astral nodded.

> **Astral:**  
> "Of course."

> **Marts:**  
> "...YOU'RE FRIENDS WITH AN ARCHITECT?!"

Astral shrugged.

> **Astral:**  
> "She's nice."

Marts looked at Guiding Light.

Then Lumi.

Then Astral.

Then back at Guiding Light.

> **Marts:**  
> "...How?"

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S FRIENDSHIP**

The revelation confirms that Guiding Light's relationship with Lumi and Astral predates her public manifestation.

Their friendship appears to be genuine.

Guiding Light behaves noticeably differently around them.

She is more relaxed.

More conversational.

And significantly less cryptic.

Lumi is apparently comfortable teasing her.

Astral appears completely accustomed to her presence.

---

# **MARTS' REALIZATION**

Marts eventually sat down.

> **Marts:**  
> "...So while everyone else was treating you like some mysterious cosmic entity..."

Lumi nodded.

> **Marts:**  
> "...you were just hanging out with my friends?"

Guiding Light smiled.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Pretty much."

Marts stared at the ceiling.

> **Marts:**  
> "...I need a minute."

---

# **AN UNEXPECTED CONVERSATION**

Marts eventually asked:

> **Marts:**  
> "So what are you actually like?"

Guiding Light thought for a moment.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Ask Lumi."

Lumi immediately answered:

> **Lumi:**  
> "Annoying."

Guiding Light gasped.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Excuse me?"

Astral laughed.

Marts stared at them.

Then started laughing too.

The atmosphere became considerably less intimidating.

---

# **MARTS' FINAL COMMENT**

Before leaving, Marts looked back at Guiding Light.

> **Marts:**  
> "...You're not what I expected."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Is that good?"

Marts smiled.

> **Marts:**  
> "...Yeah."

They paused.

> **Marts:**  
> "You're actually kind of nice."

Guiding Light smiled.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Thank you, Marts."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Marts expected to meet an incomprehensible celestial being.

Instead, they met someone who could forge reality itself...

and discovered that she already had **best friends** inside the SAFEHOUSE.

Lumi and Astral's casual familiarity with Guiding Light was perhaps more surprising than Guiding Light herself.

The most powerful being Marts had ever encountered was apparently just another member of their friends' social circle.

Marts' final assessment:

> **"She's still an Architect."**
> 
> **"...But apparently she's also just Lumi and Astral's best friend."**

**End of Personnel Interaction Report**
