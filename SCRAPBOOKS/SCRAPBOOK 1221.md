# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The first Tachyon Industries Merch Store recruitment campaign has produced:

> **ZERO SUCCESSFUL HIRES.**

Every applicant failed the selection process.

The majority of applicants demonstrated acceptable customer-service potential.

Several demonstrated excellent knowledge of Tachyon Industries products.

Unfortunately, a recurring behavioral issue eliminated every candidate:

> **They attempted to steal the merchandise displayed on the far wall.**

Neri and Nora have therefore continued operating the store themselves.

This has created a second problem.

---

# 2. THE CURRENT STAFF

The Tachyon Merch Store is currently operated by:

### **Neri Tachyon**

Primary developer, catalog manager, technical specialist.

### **Nora Tachyon**

Assistant shopkeeper, family representative, unofficial greeter, cake-dependent recruitment officer.

There are currently:

> **No additional employees.**

---

# 3. NORA'S HEIGHT

Nora Tachyon is approximately:

> **135 cm**

This places her considerably below the height expected for the standard Tachyon Merch Store sales counter.

Nora is otherwise capable of performing the majority of her assigned duties.

The problem is simply that:

> **She cannot comfortably see over the counter.**

---

# 4. THE FIRST INCIDENT

During normal store operation, Nora attempted to stand behind the sales counter.

Only the top of her white hair was visible to approaching customers.

One customer initially assumed that the store was unattended.

They looked around.

Then heard:

> **Nora:** “Hello.”

The customer looked down.

Then looked around again.

> **Customer:** “Where are you?”

Nora raised one hand.

> **Nora:** “Here.”

The customer finally looked over the counter.

Nora was standing directly behind it.

> **Customer:** “Oh.”

Nora:

> **“Hi.”**

---

# 5. THE STOOL SOLUTION

Neri initially provided Nora with a standard stool.

This was insufficient.

Nora sat down.

Her head disappeared completely.

A customer approached the counter.

Silence.

The customer looked around.

> **Customer:** “Is anyone working here?”

From behind the counter:

> **Nora:** “Me.”

> **Customer:** “Where?”

> **Nora:** “Here.”

> **Customer:** “I can't see you.”

> **Nora:** “I'm sitting.”

Neri immediately began looking for another solution.

---

# 6. THE TALL CHAIR

A significantly taller chair was eventually provided.

The chair allows Nora to sit high enough to:

- see customers;
    
- reach the counter;
    
- access the store terminal;
    
- handle manuals;
    
- organize smaller merchandise;
    
- and observe the display wall.
    

The chair has been designated:

> **TACHYON MERCH STORE — NORA ELEVATED SEATING UNIT**

Nora calls it:

> **“My tall chair.”**

---

# 7. NORA'S FIRST DAY WITH THE CHAIR

Nora climbed into the chair.

She looked around.

She could see the entire store.

She looked toward Neri.

> **Nora:** “I can see everything.”

Neri:

> **“Good.”**

Nora looked toward the display wall.

> **“I can see the guns.”**

Neri:

> **“You could already see them.”**

Nora:

> **“Better now.”**

---

# 8. CHAIR SECURITY

The chair has since become an important part of the store's operational environment.

Nora is not permitted to climb onto unstable furniture in order to compensate for her height.

The chair is stable, dedicated to her use, and positioned behind the counter.

Neko additionally installed a small footrest.

Nora immediately approved.

> **Nora:** “This is good.”

Neko:

> **“It's a chair.”**

Nora:

> **“It's a tall chair.”**

Neko:

> **“Yes.”**

Nora:

> **“Good chair.”**

Neko walked away.

---

# 9. CUSTOMER INTERACTION

With the elevated chair, Nora is now able to properly interact with visitors.

A typical exchange is now:

> **Customer:** “Excuse me.”

Nora looks up.

> **Nora:** “Hi.”

> **Customer:** “Are you the shopkeeper?”

> **Nora:** “Yes.”

> **Customer:** “How old are you?”

Nora pauses.

> **Nora:** “Six.”

The customer looks at the store.

Then at Neri.

Then back at Nora.

> **Customer:** “You work here?”

> **Nora:** “Yes.”

> **Customer:** “...Okay.”

Nora:

> **“What do you want?”**

---

# 10. THE DISPLAY WALL REMAINS A PROBLEM

The elevated chair has not solved the original staffing problem.

Nora now has a better view of the display wall.

This has made her considerably better at noticing suspicious behavior.

One visitor stared at the displayed **Sleight of Hand** for an unusually long period.

Nora noticed immediately.

> **Nora:** “Don't.”

The visitor froze.

> **Visitor:** “I wasn't doing anything.”

> **Nora:** “You were looking at it.”

> **Visitor:** “Yes.”

> **Nora:** “Too much.”

The visitor walked away.

Neri continued working.

---

Neri does not appear particularly concerned by Nora's height.

> **“A little.”**

The interviewer asked whether she intended to find a more permanent solution.

Neri:

> **“We got a chair.”**

> **“Is that enough?”**

The Tachyon Industries Merch Store currently has two operational limitations.
