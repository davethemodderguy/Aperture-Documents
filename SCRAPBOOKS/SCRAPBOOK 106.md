# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL OBSERVATION LOG**

## **PROJECT: MOM CLUB**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Bedroom B of **Project SAFEHOUSE**, unofficially referred to by residents as the **"Mom Club Room,"** was originally reserved for:

- Astral
    
- Bao
    

With the confirmed presence of **Lumi Lockwood**, the room has informally gained a third regular occupant.

No formal vote was ever held.

According to Bao:

> "...She's a mom."

Astral nodded.

> "...She qualifies."

The matter was considered settled.

---

# **CURRENT MEMBERS**

### **Astral**

Mother of:

- Mart
    
- Taiga
    

Despite her ghostly nature, Astral remains an active maternal figure within SAFEHOUSE.

---

### **Bao**

Hybrid Whale.

Starladian Goddess.

Mother of Frost.

Frequently seen preparing meals, checking on residents, and ensuring nobody skips breakfast.

---

### **Lumi**

Mother of:

- Arcturus Lockwood
    
- Norma Lockwood
    
- Corin Lockwood
    

Classified as an Ether-Superseded Ghost.

Often visits SAFEHOUSE despite one noticeable absence.

---

# **COMMON TOPICS**

Personnel have repeatedly overheard conversations between the three.

Topics include:

- Children refusing to rest.
    
- Children forgetting meals.
    
- Whether tea solves everything.
    
- Whether engineers require supervision.
    
- How impossible it is to convince adults that they also need taking care of.
    

Research staff have unanimously concluded:

> "Parents are apparently the same across dimensions."

---

# **CORIN**

Although Lumi has become a familiar face throughout Heaven's Blacksite...

Corin almost never accompanies her.

Most evenings, Lumi is seen alone.

She smiles politely at residents.

She joins Bao and Astral for tea.

She laughs during conversations.

Yet whenever discussion turns toward her youngest daughter...

Her expression softens.

---

# **KCA HEADQUARTERS**

Corin continues treating her office as her home.

The office is rarely silent.

Lumi is almost always there.

Sometimes reading.

Sometimes quietly knitting.

Sometimes simply sitting nearby while Corin completes another financial report.

Many evenings pass with only a handful of words exchanged.

Corin has become deeply accustomed to Lumi's company.

When she reaches for tea, there is often another cup already waiting.

When she forgets her medication, Lumi reminds her.

When nightmares wake her before dawn, Lumi is already beside her.

Corin once admitted during counseling:

> "...If she wasn't here..."

She stopped.

After several moments she quietly finished the sentence.

> "...It would be unbearably lonely."

---

# **A MOTHER'S REQUEST**

Despite spending nearly every day beside Corin, Lumi never stops asking the same question.

Not every hour.

Not even every day.

Only when the moment feels right.

> "Norma made your favorite tea today."

Corin continues writing.

> "...I know."

Another evening.

> "Arcturus drove past your office again."

Silence.

Then...

> "...I know."

Sometimes Lumi simply says:

> "They miss you."

Corin never argues.

She never claims otherwise.

She only lowers her head a little further.

---

# **ASTRAL'S OBSERVATION**

One afternoon, Astral quietly asked Lumi:

> "...Do you think she'll come back?"

Lumi looked toward the empty hallway leading outside SAFEHOUSE.

She smiled gently.

There was hope in her voice.

> "She still knows the way home."

---

# **BAO'S COMMENT**

Bao poured another cup of tea before setting it beside Lumi.

> "Then we'll keep making enough for one more."

Lumi smiled.

> "Thank you."

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the report with a faint smile.

His final note consisted of a single observation.

> "The Mom Club has gained another member."

He paused before continuing.

> "Now all that's left..."

> "...is for one daughter to believe she's welcome enough to visit."

---

# **PROJECT STATUS**

The Mom Club continues meeting regularly within SAFEHOUSE.

Astral.

Bao.

Lumi.

Three mothers from very different origins.

Yet each shares the same quiet habit.

Whenever dinner begins...

Each instinctively glances toward the doorway.

Just in case someone they love finally walks through it.
