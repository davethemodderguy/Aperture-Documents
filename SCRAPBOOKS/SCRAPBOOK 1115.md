- **Elsher — NS-006**
    
- **Don — NS-008**
    
- **Two additional authorized MSAC operators — NS-002 and NS-005**

No operational activity was taking place.

No training exercise was underway.

# 7. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri encountered the group approximately ten minutes later.

She looked at the four operators.

Then at the Night Stalkers.

She appeared confused.

One operator asked:

> "You like them?"

Neri nodded.

Another asked:

> "Are you going to make more?"

Neri immediately shook her head.

She then wrote:

> **"NO."**

Underneath:

> **"I won't make anymore Night Stalkers."**

Then:

> **"They are..."**

Neri paused.

She looked at the four operators.

Then at the remaining inventory.

Finally:

> **"Limited Merchandise."**

She left.

# 8. ADMINISTRATIVE INTERPRETATION

The term **"Limited Merchandise"** has no official C.O.R.E. classification.

Nevertheless, it accurately describes the current situation.

The Night Stalker is now:

- finite;
    
- numbered;
    
- individually assigned;
    
- historically documented;
    
- owner-engraved;
    
- non-replaceable;
    
- impossible to reorder.
    

The weapons have effectively become a ten-unit collector's series.

Except the collectors happen to be armed operators.
