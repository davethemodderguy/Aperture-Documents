# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **EVIDENCE RECOVERY LOG**

## **SUBJECT: SHELTER T-01 – ABANDONED JOURNAL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following Corin Lockwood's relocation from Shelter T-01, Security personnel conducted a welfare inspection of the abandoned treehouse.

The shelter had been left exactly as it was.

The blankets remained folded.

The lantern had long since burned out.

A kettle still sat beside an empty mug.

Most personal belongings had been taken.

One item remained.

A cloth-bound journal.

It had been left open on the small wooden desk.

Medical and Psychological Services jointly reviewed the contents.

The journal was subsequently sealed due to the severity of its contents.

---

# **PHYSICAL CONDITION**

The journal showed extensive signs of prolonged exposure to moisture.

Many pages had warped from melted snow.

Numerous entries were blurred by water damage later determined to be tears.

Several pages contained small brown-red stains.

Medical examination concluded they were consistent with dried blood from a minor hand laceration.

There was no indication the blood had been placed there intentionally.

---

# **SELECTED ENTRIES**

Many pages contained only short sentences.

Some appeared repeatedly.

Some were written dozens of times.

---

**Page 17**

> Where's my mom?

The sentence was written once.

A small bloodstain crossed the final word.

Water damage had caused the ink to run downward.

---

**Page 29**

A crude pencil drawing of Lumi.

The outline was unmistakable.

Long hair.

A gentle smile.

Hands folded together.

The face had been heavily scribbled over until the paper nearly tore.

No explanation accompanied the drawing.

---

**Page 43**

> I forgot my mom.

Below it, written much smaller,

> Am I a good daughter?

No further text appeared on the page.

---

**Page 58**

> I remember her voice.

> I forget her face.

> I remember her smile.

> I don't know if I remember it correctly anymore.

---

**Page 81**

> If I keep forgetting...

> ...

> Was she ever really here?

The final line was never finished.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services concluded that the journal reflects Corin's growing fear that grief and memory loss were slowly erasing parts of her relationship with Lumi.

The repeated questions are not interpreted as factual statements.

Rather, they are expressions of Corin's anxiety that she might fail the people she loves simply because her memories have become unreliable.

Clinicians noted that this fear is common in individuals coping with traumatic loss and disrupted autobiographical memory.

---

# **PERSONNEL RESPONSE**

Lumi's recovered sketch was not removed from the journal.

Instead, the entire notebook was carefully preserved.

When asked why, the attending psychologist answered,

> "Even painful memories deserve to be handled gently."

The journal was placed into secure archival storage.

Its location remains restricted.

Not because it contains classified information.

But because it records, page by page, the thoughts of someone trying desperately not to lose the last pieces of a person she loved.

**End of Evidence Recovery Log**
