# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **ARCHITECTAL PERSONNEL & COMPANION RECORD**

# **THE THREE LIGHTS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Known Architects:** Guiding Light / Curious Light / Mischevious Light  
**Associated Companion:** Jay — Blue Jay  
**Status:** **ACTIVE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Three Architects are currently known to operate under the collective designation of **The Lights**.

Despite sharing a naming convention, their responsibilities and personalities differ considerably.

The trio consists of:

- **Guiding Light** — Caretaker / Protector
    
- **Curious Light** — Researcher / Observer
    
- **Mischevious Light** — Enforcer
    

Mischevious Light is the youngest of the three.

Ironically, he is arguably the least mischievous.

His name refers more to his personality than his occupation.

His actual duties involve enforcement, punishment, and dealing with entities or individuals that violate rules established by higher authorities.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light is the most frequently encountered of the three around Corin and Orphie.

She regularly visits the treehouse.

Her primary concerns include:

- Corin's wellbeing.
    
- Orphie's wellbeing.
    
- Maintaining peace between the two.
    
- Protecting the surrounding area.
    
- Caring for Jay.
    

She is also responsible for Corin's limited color restoration.

Her intervention remains the safest modification she can provide without risking Corin's Ether-sustained existence.

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT**

Curious Light is the investigative member of the trio.

He is intensely curious about practically everything.

His interests frequently include:

- Ether.
    
- Anomalies.
    
- Unusual entities.
    
- Reality.
    
- Corin's unusual physiology.
    
- Unexplained phenomena.
    

His curiosity has occasionally caused problems.

Guiding Light has repeatedly reminded him that **Corin is a person first and a phenomenon second.**

Curious Light has improved considerably.

He remains fascinated by the world around him.

---

# **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT**

**Designation:** Mischevious Light  
**Position:** Enforcer  
**Age Classification:** Youngest of the Three Lights  
**Primary Function:** Enforcement / Punitive Operations

Mischevious Light is responsible for enforcing rules and administering consequences when necessary.

Despite his name, he is not primarily a troublemaker.

In fact, he is usually extremely professional while performing his duties.

His name originates from his personality outside of work.

He enjoys teasing the other Architects.

He occasionally plays harmless pranks.

He has a particularly dry sense of humor.

But when an actual violation requires enforcement, the joking stops immediately.

---

# **THE ENFORCER**

Mischevious Light's work can take him far away from the Blacksite.

He may spend extended periods dealing with:

- Rogue entities.
    
- Reality violations.
    
- Unauthorized anomalous activity.
    
- Entities violating Architectal restrictions.
    
- Individuals requiring punishment.
    
- Situations that cannot be resolved through conventional containment.
    

Because of this, he rarely visits Corin and Orphie's treehouse.

This is not because he dislikes them.

He is simply busy.

---

# **THE THREE'S DYNAMIC**

The trio has a relatively simple division of responsibilities.

**Guiding Light:**

> "Someone needs to take care of them."

**Curious Light:**

> "Someone needs to understand them."

**Mischevious Light:**

> "Someone needs to make sure nobody causes problems."

Guiding Light usually sighs at the last statement.

Curious Light usually asks what qualifies as a "problem."

Mischevious Light usually refuses to elaborate.

---

# **JAY**

Jay is officially **Guiding Light's pet**.

He is not an anomalous creature.

He is a normal blue jay that has been properly domesticated and trained by Guiding Light.

His mischievous behavior is therefore not the result of negligence.

Guiding Light has trained him extensively.

Jay understands:

- Basic commands.
    
- Boundaries.
    
- Where he is allowed to fly.
    
- Which objects he may interact with.
    
- Which areas are restricted.
    
- When he is expected to return.
    
- When he is being intentionally mischievous.
    

The final point is particularly important.

Jay knows exactly what he is doing.

---

# **WHY JAY STILL CAUSES TROUBLE**

Jay's behavior should not be mistaken for poor training.

Guiding Light has taught him the rules.

Jay simply enjoys testing them.

There is a difference.

He knows he should not steal Corin's hairclip.

He does it anyway.

He knows he should not take Neko's keycard.

He knows.

He knows he should not steal Marts' hats.

He knows.

Jay has effectively learned:

> **"I am not supposed to do this."**

And occasionally decided:

> **"But it would be funny."**

---

# **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT & JAY**

Mischevious Light has a complicated relationship with Jay.

He respects Guiding Light's training.

He also finds Jay's behavior amusing.

During one rare visit, Mischevious Light watched Jay steal a small object from Curious Light.

Curious Light immediately noticed.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Jay."

Jay flew away.

Mischevious Light laughed.

> **"He's getting better."**

Guiding Light looked at him.

> **"That's not what you're supposed to encourage."**

Mischevious Light shrugged.

> **"I'm the youngest. I'm allowed."**

Guiding Light stared at him.

> **"...No."**

---

# **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT'S VISITS**

Because of his work, Mischevious Light rarely visits the treehouse.

When he does, Corin notices immediately.

He tends to arrive without warning.

His visits are generally short.

Sometimes he simply checks on everyone.

Sometimes he brings something from wherever he was working.

Sometimes he sits with the others for a while before leaving again.

He does not make a habit of announcing when he will return.

His work rarely permits reliable schedules.

---

# **CORIN'S VIEW**

Corin does not know Mischevious Light particularly well.

She has seen him enough to recognize him.

She understands that he is busy.

She also knows that despite his intimidating occupation, he is generally kind to her.

During one visit:

> **Mischevious Light:**  
> "How are you?"

> **Corin:**  
> "Better."

> **Mischevious Light:**  
> "Good."

He glanced at Jay.

> **"...Is he still causing trouble?"**

Corin looked at the bird.

> **"Constantly."**

Jay chirped.

Mischevious Light smiled.

> **"Sounds healthy."**

---

# **THE THREE TOGETHER**

When all three Architects are present, the dynamic is noticeably different.

Guiding Light is usually attempting to maintain order.

Curious Light is asking questions.

Mischevious Light is quietly observing everything.

Then Jay inevitably does something.

Guiding Light:

> **"Jay."**

Curious Light:

> **"How did he even get that?"**

Mischevious Light:

> **"...I respect the commitment."**

Guiding Light:

> **"Don't."**

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

Mischevious Light should not be classified as a chaotic or uncontrolled Architect merely because of his name.

He is an **enforcer**.

His occupation requires discipline, consistency, and the ability to administer consequences when necessary.

His mischievous personality is largely separate from his professional role.

Likewise, Jay's mischievous behavior should not be interpreted as inadequate training.

Guiding Light has trained him properly.

Jay simply possesses a personality.

A very troublesome one.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

**Guiding Light:** Active — Frequently present at the treehouse.

**Curious Light:** Active — Visits periodically and continues research.

**Mischevious Light:** Active — Frequently deployed elsewhere; infrequent visits.

**Jay:** Active — Under Guiding Light's ownership and training; currently residing with Corin while Guiding Light is occupied.

**Corin:** Stable.

**Orphie:** Stable.

**Treehouse:** Operational.

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

The three Architects are very different.

One protects.

One observes.

One enforces.

Yet all three remain connected.

Guiding Light spends her time caring for Corin, Orphie, and her troublesome blue jay.

Curious Light continues trying to understand everything he encounters.

And Mischevious Light remains somewhere beyond the Blacksite, carrying out work that keeps him away from the other two.

He does not visit often.

But when he does, he is always welcomed.

Usually followed by Jay stealing something.
