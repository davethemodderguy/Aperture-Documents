# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL RETURN INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – FIRST BLACKSITE RETURN**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **SUMMARY**

After an extended absence from Heaven's Blacksite, Corin Lockwood finally returned.

The event was not planned.

There was no announcement.

No ceremony.

No official welcome.

Lumi simply accompanied her to the entrance.

For the first time in weeks...

Corin walked through the doors of SAFEHOUSE.

---

# **INITIAL RESPONSE**

The reaction was immediate.

Everyone noticed.

Not because of alarms.

Not because of security systems.

Because someone shouted:

> "Corin's here."

Within minutes, nearly everyone in SAFEHOUSE knew.

The people who had been waiting for her finally saw her again.

---

# **THE REUNION**

Belle was the first to approach.

She stopped herself from running forward.

She remembered Corin's boundaries.

Instead, she smiled.

> "...Welcome back."

Norma followed.

She tried to act normal.

She failed.

> "You took long enough."

Her voice was slightly shaky.

Arcturus simply stood there for a moment.

The person who had spent months watching the lights of KCA Headquarters finally saw his little sister standing in front of him again.

> "...Hey, kid."

---

# **THE PROBLEM**

Nobody intended to overwhelm Corin.

Everyone was careful.

Everyone was happy.

Everyone wanted to show her that she was missed.

But for Corin...

It was too much.

Too many voices.

Too many faces.

Too many emotions arriving at once.

Questions.

Hugs.

Memories.

Apologies.

Relief.

All at once.

The room that was supposed to feel like home suddenly felt enormous.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin tried.

She genuinely tried.

She answered questions.

She smiled.

She said she was fine.

But Lumi noticed first.

The small signs.

The way Corin's hands tightened.

The way she looked toward the exit.

The way she became quieter with every passing second.

Lumi stepped closer.

> "Corin."

Corin looked at her.

No words came.

Then she whispered:

> "...I can't."

---

# **DEPARTURE**

Before anyone could stop her, Corin left.

Not angrily.

Not because she hated being there.

Because she was overwhelmed.

The Blacksite doors closed behind her.

Everyone remained silent.

---

# **AFTERMATH**

No one blamed Corin.

Not Belle.

Not Norma.

Not Arcturus.

Not Lumi.

Everyone understood what had happened.

They had spent so long waiting for her return that they forgot something important.

Coming home after being away for so long was not always simple.

Sometimes...

The place you missed the most could still feel unfamiliar.

---

# **LUMI'S OBSERVATION**

Later that evening, Lumi spoke privately with the others.

> "She wasn't running away from you."

A pause.

> "She was running away from everything she felt."

Belle lowered her head.

> "...We were just happy."

Lumi nodded.

> "I know."

> "That's why it hurt."

---

# **CORIN'S DECISION**

After returning to KCA Headquarters, Corin did not visit the Blacksite again.

She continued her work.

She continued speaking with Lumi.

She continued receiving messages from her family.

But she stopped walking through the SAFEHOUSE entrance.

Not because she no longer cared.

Not because she regretted returning.

Because the experience confirmed her greatest fear.

That she did not know how to return to the life she once had.

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the incident report.

His note was brief.

> "A door opening does not mean someone is ready to walk through it."

He continued:

> "Do not close the door."

> "Do not drag her through it."

> "Leave it open."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin remains at KCA Headquarters.

Lumi continues accompanying her.

SAFEHOUSE remains open to her.

Her place remains unchanged.

No one has removed her chair.

No one has stopped expecting her.

The first return did not succeed.

But it proved something important.

Corin was willing to try.

And everyone at the Blacksite knows that, someday, she may try again.

**End of Personnel Return Incident Report**
