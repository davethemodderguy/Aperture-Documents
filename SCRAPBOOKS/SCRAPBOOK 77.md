# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **CONFIDENTIAL PSYCHOLOGICAL RISK ASSESSMENT**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – THERAPEUTIC SUPPORT REQUIREMENTS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical Director & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **CLINICAL SUMMARY**

Corin Lockwood continues to live with the long-term psychological consequences of severe childhood trauma, prolonged dehumanization, and forced Ether experimentation.

Although she remains highly functional in her professional role, her psychological state is considerably more fragile than her outward demeanor suggests.

She consistently performs her duties with precision, patience, and kindness.

This should not be mistaken for an absence of suffering.

---

## **SELF-PERCEPTION**

The central theme observed throughout Corin's therapy is not fear.

It is grief.

She struggles with the persistent belief that the child she once was died during the experiments and that the person standing here now is only a continuation sustained by Ether.

During one counseling session, she quietly stated:

> "Everyone says I'm alive."

> "I want to believe them."

> "Some days... I just don't know how."

Clinical staff recognize these thoughts as trauma-related beliefs rather than objective reality.

Even so, they can become especially intense when Corin is reminded of the laboratory or the loss of her childhood.

---

## **THE ETHER RESONANCE**

The maternal Ether resonance documented in previous reports has become an important protective factor in Corin's recovery.

Whenever it manifests, it consistently encourages:

- Self-care.
    
- Rest.
    
- Connection with trusted people.
    
- Acceptance of support.
    
- Hope for the future.
    

It never encourages withdrawal from others.

Instead, it repeatedly directs Corin toward the people who care about her.

Corin herself described it simply.

> "...It reminds me of things I forget when the memories get loud."

---

## **LIMITATIONS**

Medical staff emphasize that the Ether resonance is **not** a substitute for genuine human relationships or ongoing treatment.

It does not appear constantly.

Its presence is intermittent and unpredictable.

For that reason, Corin's recovery cannot depend on the phenomenon alone.

Her support network remains essential.

---

## **PROTECTIVE FACTORS**

The psychological team identified several factors that consistently help stabilize Corin during periods of distress.

These include:

- Regular contact with Arcturus and Norma.
    
- Visits to Heaven's Blacksite.
    
- Quiet evenings shared with SAFEHOUSE residents.
    
- Structured work within the Accounting Department.
    
- Continued counseling and medical follow-up.
    
- The supportive Ether resonance when it manifests.
    

Counselors noted that Corin responds best when reminded that she is valued for who she is today rather than defined by what was done to her.

---

## **BELLE'S OBSERVATION**

After several weeks of spending time with Corin, Belle summarized her thoughts in a single sentence.

> "She doesn't need people to pretend she's never been hurt."

> "She needs people who'll stay beside her even when she remembers."

---

## **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus requested that a standing instruction be added to Corin's file.

> "If she ever tells you she's having a bad day..."

> "...Don't assume she'll ask for help."

> "Go check on her anyway."

The request was approved.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' DIRECTIVE**

Director Lucious authorized a permanent wellness protocol for Corin.

Whenever practical:

- Corin is not to be left professionally isolated for extended periods during known periods of heightened stress.
    
- Supervisors are encouraged to maintain regular, ordinary contact with her rather than treating her differently.
    
- Counseling resources are to remain immediately available.
    
- Medical staff are to continue routine follow-up regarding both her Ether physiology and psychological health.
    

His closing annotation reads:

> "The people who harmed her convinced a child that she was disposable."

> "Our responsibility is not merely to say otherwise."

> "It is to demonstrate, through our actions every day, that she belongs."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin continues to recover.

Her progress is neither quick nor linear.

There are days when the memories feel distant.

There are days when they return with painful clarity.

Through both, she remains surrounded by people who know her not as an experiment, not as a designation, and not as a relic of the past.

They know her as Corin Lockwood.

A sister.

A friend.

A colleague.

A person whose future is worth protecting just as much as her past deserved to be.
