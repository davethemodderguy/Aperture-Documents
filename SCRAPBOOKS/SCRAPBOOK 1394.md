# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Solider 11 has been located.

The subject was discovered living independently within a Banlands urban district and working as a **contractor for a local gang**.

Initial contact did not involve Project: Silverline personnel.

Solider 11 had apparently established an independent existence following her separation from the Silverline program.

She was armed, employed, and operating under her own decisions.

When KCA personnel identified her as a probable Silverline survivor, Solider 11 initially displayed significant caution.

That changed immediately after she was informed of one fact:

> **Anby was alive.**

Upon hearing Anby's name, Solider 11 voluntarily surrendered herself to KCA custody.

No force was required.

---

# 1. INITIAL DISCOVERY

KCA personnel were conducting a routine investigation into anomalous individuals operating within the district when a contractor matching Silverline physical and behavioral records was identified.

The subject was operating under the name:

> **SOLIDER 11**

The designation remained unchanged.

She was working independently for a local gang as a contract operative.

The exact nature of her assignments is still being investigated.

No evidence currently indicates that she remained affiliated with Project: Silverline.

---

# 2. INITIAL CONTACT

KCA personnel approached Solider 11 after confirming sufficient identifying characteristics.

Her immediate response was defensive.

She did not attack.

She did not flee.

She simply asked:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Who's asking?"

KCA identified themselves.

Solider 11 stared at the personnel member for several seconds.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Silverline?"

> **KCA:**  
> "No."

A pause.

> **KCA:**  
> "We're not Silverline."

Solider 11's posture relaxed slightly.

She remained cautious.

---

# 3. IDENTIFICATION

KCA presented the recovered Silverline documentation.

Solider 11 recognized the records almost immediately.

She did not appear surprised.

Instead, she asked:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "How many?"

The KCA representative initially did not understand.

> **KCA:**  
> "How many what?"

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "How many are left?"

The representative informed her.

Five confirmed sisters had been identified.

Six were confirmed deceased.

Solider 11 remained silent.

Then she asked:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Who?"

The KCA representative listed the confirmed identities.

When the name **Anby** was spoken, Solider 11 immediately looked up.

---

# 4. ANBY

The conversation changed completely.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "...Anby?"

> **KCA:**  
> "Yes."

Solider 11 stared at the representative.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "She's alive?"

> **KCA:**  
> "Yes."

Another pause.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Where?"

The KCA representative explained that Anby was currently under KCA protection and residing within the Aperture SAFEHOUSE.

Solider 11 did not hesitate.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Take me there."

The KCA representative explained that custody was voluntary and that Solider 11 was not being arrested.

Solider 11 answered:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "I know."

She then surrendered herself voluntarily.

---

# 5. CONTRACTOR STATUS

Solider 11's employment with the local gang has been documented.

The gang was informed that she was leaving.

No resistance was recorded.

Solider 11 apparently had no outstanding obligation preventing her departure.

Her contract was considered terminated.

C.O.R.E. has found no evidence that the gang knew her connection to Project: Silverline.

The gang has not been granted access to her current location.

---

# 6. TRANSFER

Solider 11 was transported under KCA protection.

During the journey, she remained unusually quiet.

She eventually asked:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "She really survived?"

> **KCA:**  
> "Yes."

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "She's safe?"

> **KCA:**  
> "Yes."

Solider 11 looked out the window.

After several minutes:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Good."

That was the only comment she made for the remainder of the journey.

---

# 7. SAFEHOUSE ARRIVAL

Solider 11 arrived at the SAFEHOUSE under controlled conditions.

She was not placed in containment.

She was not restrained.

She was not treated as hostile.

Her weapons were temporarily secured during intake as standard KCA procedure.

She was informed that she could request their return once the welfare and security assessment was completed.

Her first question was not about her room.

It was:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Where's Anby?"

---

# 8. REUNION

Anby was notified.

She arrived accompanied by Phoenix.

Solider 11 was standing in the SAFEHOUSE corridor.

Anby stopped.

For several seconds, neither moved.

Anby stared at her.

Solider 11 stared back.

Then Anby spoke.

> **ANBY:**  
> "...11?"

Solider 11's expression changed.

The designation was suddenly less clinical.

More familiar.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Anby."

Anby walked toward her.

Solider 11 opened her arms.

Anby stepped into them.

Neither spoke for several seconds.

---

# 9. ANBY'S RESPONSE

Anby eventually asked:

> **ANBY:**  
> "Where were you?"

Solider 11 answered honestly.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Everywhere."

Anby looked at her.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Mostly wherever people paid."

Anby understood.

She did not ask for details.

Instead:

> **ANBY:**  
> "You're here now."

Solider 11 nodded.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Yeah."

A pause.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "I'm here."

---

# 10. PHOENIX OBSERVATION

Phoenix remained nearby but did not interrupt the reunion.

Later, she documented:

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "Anby changed the moment she saw her."

The welfare officer asked:

> **"How?"**

Phoenix answered:

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "She stopped looking like someone who was waiting for people to disappear."

---

# 11. INITIAL WELFARE ASSESSMENT

Solider 11 currently demonstrates:

- strong situational awareness;
    
- extensive combat experience;
    
- independent survival skills;
    
- established familiarity with contract work;
    
- distrust of institutional authority;
    
- strong attachment to her identified sisters;
    
- no immediate indication of hostility toward KCA;
    
- immediate willingness to cooperate once Anby's safety was confirmed.
    

Her transition into protected residency will therefore focus on:

- autonomy;
    
- consent;
    
- personal security;
    
- reconnecting with family;
    
- medical evaluation;
    
- establishing independent living arrangements;
    
- and determining whether she wishes to remain within the SAFEHOUSE.
    

She will not be treated as a Silverline asset.

---

# 12. THE FIVE

Solider 11 has now personally confirmed one of the five surviving sisters.

When informed that Twiggy, Sora, Phaethon, and Iris remain unaccounted for, she became visibly concerned.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "You're looking for them?"

> **KCA:**  
> "Yes."

Solider 11 nodded.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Then I'll help."

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Solider 11 was not recovered from a laboratory.

She was not found inside a containment chamber.

She was not waiting for rescue.

She had built a life elsewhere.

It was not necessarily a peaceful life.

But it was hers.

She worked.

She survived.

She made contracts.

She stayed alive.

Then someone told her that Anby was alive.

And the decision took approximately one second.

She left everything behind.

Not because KCA ordered her to.

Not because she was forced.

Because her sister was there.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Six sisters are confirmed dead.

Five remain to be located.

One has now come home.

Solider 11 did not ask what accommodations she would receive.

She did not ask what KCA could give her.

She did not ask whether she could keep working.

She asked one question:

> **"Where's Anby?"**

And when she found her, she stayed.
