**SUBJECT:** Neri — Plush Form / MSAC Interaction During Lottery Suspension

---

# 1. SUMMARY

The Hell Fire Lottery remains temporarily paused.

Neri remains in her voluntary plush form.

She is extremely tired.

MSAC personnel have subsequently discovered that having an exhausted, miniature plush version of Neri sitting around the facility creates an entirely different operational environment.

In particular:

> **MSAC personnel are having fun with her.**

Neri appears to tolerate this.

She has also developed a concerning tendency to simply...

> **fall over.**

---

# 2. PLUSH NERI — CURRENT STATE

Neri is currently approximately miniature/chibi-sized.

Current appearance:

- long white hair;
    
- oversized hoodie;
    
- round glasses;
    
- seal tail;
    
- soft fabric body;
    
- tiny limbs;
    
- very soft construction.
    

She remains fully autonomous.

She can walk.

She can crawl.

She can climb onto people.

She can transform back into normal form.

She can speak.

She can bite.

She can also simply decide that walking is too much effort and lie down.

Today, however, exhaustion has significantly reduced her movement.

---

# 3. THE FALLING

Neri's current method of locomotion can best be described as:

> **walk → wobble → stop → continue → wobble → fall**

The falls are generally minor.

Plush form significantly reduces ordinary impact consequences.

However, the resulting sound has become extremely recognizable.

### **SQUEAK.**

Neri squeaks when sufficiently jostled, dropped, or falling due to exhaustion.

This is not an intentional sound.

It is a distress response.

Personnel have therefore been instructed:

> **Do not intentionally make Neri squeak.**

---

# 4. FIRST MSAC DISCOVERY

An MSAC operator was walking through the lounge when Neri attempted to cross the floor.

Neri took approximately six steps.

Stopped.

Wobbled.

Attempted another step.

Her legs gave out.

She fell forward.

### **SQUEAK.**

The entire room went silent.

The operator stared at her.

Neri remained face-down on the floor.

After several seconds, she slowly lifted her head.

> **NERI:**  
> “...Tired.”

The operator immediately picked her up.

> **MSAC OPERATOR:**  
> “Okay. You're done walking.”

Neri did not argue.

She simply rested against his arm.

---

# 5. MSAC DISCOVERS THE "FLOOR NERI"

A recurring pattern has now emerged.

Neri will sometimes sit down.

Then lie down.

Then remain there.

Sometimes she falls asleep.

MSAC personnel have begun referring to this as:

> **“Floor Neri.”**

The name is unofficial.

Neri has not objected.

Mostly because she was asleep when it was coined.

---

# 6. HEADPATS

Gentle headpats remain one of the safest forms of interaction.

Several MSAC personnel have taken turns giving Neri gentle headpats when she approaches them.

Neri's typical responses include:

- lowering her head;
    
- closing her eyes;
    
- leaning into the hand;
    
- remaining completely still;
    
- quietly falling asleep.
    

One operator asked:

> **“Does she like this?”**

Another answered:

> **“She keeps coming back.”**

Neri returned approximately thirty seconds later.

---

# 7. THE "BACKPACK" INCIDENT

Neri eventually climbed onto an MSAC operator's back.

She gripped the operator's clothing.

The operator continued walking normally.

Neri remained attached.

> **MSAC OPERATOR:**  
> “Are you comfortable?”

Neri nodded.

The operator continued.

Five minutes later:

> **MSAC OPERATOR:**  
> “Still good?”

Neri nodded again.

Another five minutes passed.

No response.

The operator checked.

Neri was asleep.

---

# 8. SQUEAK FROM EXHAUSTION

Later, Neri attempted to walk from one side of the room to another.

She became visibly unsteady.

An MSAC operator noticed.

> **MSAC OPERATOR:**  
> “Neri?”

Neri looked up.

She took one step.

Then another.

Then simply tipped sideways.

### **SQUEAK.**

The operator caught her before she reached the floor.

Neri blinked.

> **NERI:**  
> “...Sorry.”

The operator immediately replied:

> **“Don't apologize.”**

Neri rested against his arm.

She did not attempt to walk again.

---

# 9. IMPORTANT MEDICAL DISTINCTION

The squeak itself is not considered a medical symptom.

It is a physical response produced by the plush form under sufficient impact or compression.

However:

### Repeated falling due to exhaustion is significant.

Personnel should not interpret:

> **“She's a plushie.”**

as:

> **“She's fine no matter what happens.”**

Neri is still a person.

She is simply currently much softer.

If she repeatedly loses the ability to remain upright, personnel should allow her to rest and notify appropriate medical staff if the condition appears abnormal or concerning.

---

# 10. MSAC'S NEW RULE

A temporary informal rule has emerged:

> **IF NERI FALLS, PICK HER UP.**

Not:

> “Make her stand.”

Not:

> “Tell her to keep walking.”

Not:

> “See if she does it again.”

Just:

> **Pick her up.**

Neri appears perfectly willing to accept this arrangement.

---

# 11. NERI'S CURRENT FAVORITE ACTIVITY

At present, Neri appears to have settled into a particularly simple routine:

**Be carried.**

**Get headpats.**

**Drink coffee.**

**Sleep.**

**Wake up.**

**Be carried again.**

Occasionally:

**Walk three meters.**

**Fall over.**

**Squeak.**

**Get carried.**

The Hell Fire Lottery remains completely irrelevant to her current priorities.

---

# 12. MSAC OBSERVATION

One operator summarized the situation:

> **MSAC OPERATOR:**  
> “She's basically taken the day off.”

Another replied:

> **“She's earned it.”**

No objection was recorded.

---

# ARCHIVIST NOTE

The Hell Fire Lottery was paused because Neri was too tired.

MSAC subsequently discovered that this means they get:

> **Exhausted Plush Neri Day.**

She sits on desks.

She rides on people's backs.

She gets headpats.

She falls asleep.

She occasionally falls over.

And every now and then:

### **SQUEAK.**

Everyone immediately freezes because they know that sound means:

> **Someone needs to catch the plushie.**

Neri herself appears completely unbothered by the attention.

At one point she was found lying on an MSAC operator's lap, glasses slightly crooked, hoodie covering most of her tiny body.

Someone asked:

> **“Is she okay?”**

Neri, eyes closed, quietly answered:

> **“Mm-hm.”**

Then fell asleep again.

The lottery can wait.

Today, MSAC has apparently been assigned:

> **Plush Neri babysitting duty.**

Nobody has complained.
