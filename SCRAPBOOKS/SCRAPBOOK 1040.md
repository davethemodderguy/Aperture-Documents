**SUBJECT:** Neri — Appearance, Demeanor, and Vocal Characteristics During Low-Stamina States

---

## Executive Summary

Neri is, objectively, very cute.

This observation has become increasingly noticeable during periods when her stamina is low.

This is **not** an encouragement for Neri to become weaker, sicker, or medically unstable.

C.O.R.E. explicitly rejects that interpretation.

Her declining stamina is a medical problem and should be treated as such.

The observation is instead that certain aspects of Neri's normal appearance and behavior become particularly noticeable when she has less energy available.

Most prominently:

- sitting flat rather than standing;
    
- remaining still for extended periods;
    
- her plushie form;
    
- and, above all, **her voice.**
    

---

## I. SITTING FLAT

When Neri's stamina is low, she frequently chooses to sit flat rather than remain standing.

There is something particularly distinctive about the posture.

The oversized light-azure coat settles around her.

Her sleeves often continue to cover her hands.

Her ears and hairclips remain visible above the coat.

Her glasses may slip slightly down her face.

She usually has her coffee, notebook, or nothing at all.

And she simply sits there.

No dramatic expression.

No attempt to attract attention.

Just Neri, sitting quietly.

Personnel have described the resulting appearance as:

> **"Unreasonably small."**

This description is not to be communicated to Neri.

---

## II. PLUSHIE FORM

Neri's voluntary plushie transformation remains one of the most obvious examples.

In plushie form, she becomes a small seal/teddy-bear hybrid approximately teddy-bear sized.

She can still move.

She can still eat and drink.

She remains conscious and capable of communication.

She simply becomes significantly softer.

Her preference is generally to be carried rather than walk herself around.

Alice remains the resident most frequently observed carrying Plushie Neri.

Jane can and occasionally does carry her, although this is considerably less common.

Neri appears to enjoy the experience.

Importantly, plushie form is **not being treated as a medical treatment for anemia.**

Its reduced physical strain and other anomalous properties are incidental benefits of the transformation.

---

## III. THE VOICE

This remains the most significant observation.

Neri's voice is unusually soft.

Even when speaking about something mundane, she tends to sound gentle and quiet.

When her stamina is low, she speaks even less frequently.

When she does decide that something is important enough to spend the energy speaking aloud, the result is often only a few words.

Examples include:

> **"Tired."**

> **"Ow."**

> **"Stop."**

> **"Don't turn so fast."**

> **"I'm okay."**

The volume is generally low.

The tone is gentle.

There is an almost comforting quality to it.

Several personnel have independently described her voice as **"soft," "gentle,"** or **"comforting."**

One researcher described it as:

> **"Like someone quietly talking to you while you're half asleep."**

---

## IV. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

C.O.R.E. has identified a potential problem with how personnel discuss this observation.

There is a difference between:

> **"Neri looks especially cute when she's tired."**

and:

> **"Neri should stay tired because she's cute."**

The first is an observation.

The second is unacceptable.

Neri's weakness is not desirable.

Her anemia is not desirable.

Her declining stamina is not desirable.

Her cardiovascular strain is not desirable.

Personnel are not to encourage exhaustion for the sake of observing her appearance.

If Neri is weak, she receives appropriate care.

If Neri is healthy enough to walk around, she is allowed to walk around.

Neither state is something she needs to perform for anyone.

---

## V. PERSONNEL RESPONSE

Attempts to point out Neri's appearance directly remain discouraged.

Especially the word:

> **"Cute."**

Neri does not appreciate being called cute.

This remains true even when the observation is objectively correct.

Jane is permitted considerably more teasing latitude than most personnel, although even Jane understands that there are limits.

The preferred approach is therefore simple:

**Notice quietly.**

**Do not make it her problem.**

---

## VI. WHY THE OBSERVATION EXISTS

Neri has a naturally quiet presence.

Her preferred state is often already:

- sitting somewhere quiet;
    
- reading;
    
- drinking coffee;
    
- working;
    
- drawing;
    
- resting;
    
- or simply doing nothing.
    

Low stamina amplifies this.

She becomes even more still.

Her movements become smaller.

Her speech becomes rarer.

She may sit flat for long periods.

And when she speaks, everyone nearby has to listen carefully.

Not because she demands attention.

Because she barely uses her voice at all.

That combination makes her unusually endearing to observers.

---

## Final Assessment

Neri does not need to be sick to be cute.

She does not need to be weak to be cute.

She does not need to be exhausted to be cute.

She simply **is**.

Low stamina merely makes certain characteristics more visually and audibly noticeable:

**the little coat.**

**the sitting-flat posture.**

**the plushie form.**

**the quiet movements.**

**and that exceptionally soft voice.**

The medical objective remains unchanged:

> **Keep Neri as healthy and comfortable as possible.**

The aesthetic observation can remain what it is:

> **Neri is very cute.**

Preferably somewhere Neri cannot hear it.
