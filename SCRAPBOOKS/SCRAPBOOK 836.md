### ARCHIVAL & INTELLIGENCE DIVISION

# POST-REFORM SPAWN OBSERVATION PROTOCOL

## “WE CHECK ON THEM. THEY'RE DOING FINE.”

**CLASSIFICATION:** C.O.R.E. INTERNAL  
**ACCESS:** Level 4+ Personnel  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**DATE:** 03/09/2026  
**SUBJECT:** Periodic visits to SPAWN following cessation of hostilities  
**PARTICIPANTS:** Guiding Light, Curious Light, Mischevious Light, Neko

---

# 1. OVERVIEW

Following Guiding Light's successful reform of SPAWN, Aperture has ceased treating the organization as an active hostile target.

However, the relationship has not become entirely distant.

The three Architects of Reality — **Guiding Light, Curious Light, and Mischevious Light** — along with **Neko**, periodically check on SPAWN.

These visits are not inspections.

They are not surveillance operations.

They are not containment procedures.

They are best described as:

> **“Checking to make sure SPAWN hasn't done anything stupid again.”**

The visits have become increasingly casual.

---

# 2. PURPOSE

The visits serve several purposes:

- confirming that SPAWN remains non-hostile,
    
- ensuring the doctrinal reforms are holding,
    
- answering questions regarding anomalous phenomena,
    
- providing occasional guidance,
    
- checking the condition of SPAWN facilities,
    
- and, in Neko's case, seeing what they are doing on their computers.
    

Mischevious Light has an additional purpose.

He likes visiting them.

---

# 3. THE FOUR VISITORS

## GUIDING LIGHT

Guiding Light generally approaches SPAWN calmly.

Her visits are usually educational.

She may:

- speak with senior clergy,
    
- answer questions,
    
- discuss supernatural phenomena,
    
- correct dangerous misconceptions,
    
- or simply sit with SPAWN personnel.
    

She has become a trusted figure within the organization.

SPAWN personnel no longer regard her appearance as an existential crisis.

This is considered significant progress.

---

## CURIOUS LIGHT

Curious Light is considerably more casual.

His visits often involve conversation.

He has been observed asking SPAWN personnel about:

- their rituals,
    
- their equipment,
    
- their daily routines,
    
- their cooking,
    
- and whether anyone has figured out why one of their servers keeps rebooting.
    

He occasionally gives advice.

He occasionally asks questions.

Sometimes he simply watches.

His presence no longer causes panic.

Mostly.

---

## MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT

Mischevious Light remains Mischevious Light.

His visits are therefore unpredictable.

He has been instructed not to:

- erase infrastructure,
    
- alter weapons,
    
- rewrite history,
    
- modify personnel,
    
- or “improve” SPAWN's building.
    

He has agreed.

C.O.R.E. does not entirely trust this agreement.

SPAWN personnel have nevertheless become accustomed to him.

One priest reportedly described his visits as:

> **“Like having a very powerful, very annoying relative.”**

Mischevious apparently considered this a compliment.

---

## NEKO

Neko's visits are primarily technological.

He checks SPAWN's network infrastructure.

He checks their servers.

He checks their security.

He checks their cameras.

He occasionally checks whether they have changed their passwords.

They usually have.

The new passwords are usually terrible.

Neko remains disappointed.

---

# 4. FIRST JOINT VISIT

The first recorded visit involving all four occurred approximately two weeks after the doctrinal reform.

Guiding Light entered through the main entrance.

Curious Light followed.

Mischevious Light appeared beside them.

Neko walked behind them carrying a laptop.

A SPAWN guard immediately recognized the group.

He stared.

Then slowly reached for his radio.

> **GUARD:**  
> “They're here.”

> **RADIO:**  
> “Who?”

The guard looked at the four.

> **GUARD:**  
> “All of them.”

Long silence.

> **RADIO:**  
> “Do we evacuate?”

The guard looked toward Guiding Light.

She smiled.

> **GUARD:**  
> “No.”

---

# 5. THE NEW NORMAL

The visit proceeded peacefully.

Guiding Light spoke with SPAWN leadership.

Curious Light inspected their kitchen.

Mischevious Light wandered around the building.

Neko connected his laptop to the local network.

Approximately thirty seconds later:

> **NEKO:**  
> “Your password is still terrible.”

> **SPAWN TECHNICIAN:**  
> “We changed it.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “I know.”

> **SPAWN TECHNICIAN:**  
> “Then how did you—”

> **NEKO:**  
> “You used the name of the building.”

The technician looked embarrassed.

Mischevious laughed.

Guiding Light sighed.

---

# 6. SPAWN'S RESPONSE

SPAWN has gradually stopped regarding these visits as incursions.

Instead, they have become something of a recurring event.

Personnel sometimes prepare tea.

Someone usually brings food.

A chair is reserved for Guiding Light.

Curious Light frequently ends up near the kitchen.

Mischevious is told not to touch anything.

Neko immediately goes looking for the nearest computer.

This arrangement has become remarkably stable.

---

# 7. OCCASIONAL CHECK-IN

A typical visit now lasts between thirty minutes and several hours.

The Architects may appear without warning.

There is no fixed schedule.

SPAWN has learned not to interpret the lack of warning as hostile intent.

One recent encounter consisted entirely of:

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “How are things?”

> **SPAWN HIGH PRIEST:**  
> “Better.”

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Good.”

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Anyone hungry?”

> **SPAWN PRIEST:**  
> “We have food.”

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Can I cook?”

Everyone:

> **“No.”**

Neko laughed.

---

# 8. MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT'S RESTRICTIONS

Following his previous activities, SPAWN maintains a written list of prohibited Mischevious behavior.

### HE MAY NOT:

- delete hallways,
    
- delete armories,
    
- erase weapons from history,
    
- turn security systems into flowers,
    
- modify reality inside the chapel without permission,
    
- make furniture move,
    
- make food sentient,
    
- alter religious texts,
    
- or “just see what happens.”
    

Mischevious signed the document.

He added:

> **“:(”**

Guiding Light added beneath it:

> **“Behave.”**

Mischevious wrote:

> **“Okay.”**

Neko later added:

> **“This won't last.”**

---

# 9. ARCHITECT RELATIONS WITH SPAWN

The relationship has evolved into an unusual arrangement.

SPAWN no longer actively targets Aperture.

Aperture does not actively target SPAWN.

The Architects occasionally provide guidance.

SPAWN occasionally provides food.

Neko occasionally fixes their technology.

Mischevious occasionally causes problems.

Guiding Light occasionally fixes those problems.

Curious Light occasionally watches.

Nobody is entirely sure when this became normal.

---

# 10. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

SPAWN is no longer considered an immediate hostile organization.

Their operational philosophy has changed considerably since their encounters with the Architects.

The organization is now:

**LESS HOSTILE**  
**LESS EXTREMIST**  
**MORE CAUTIOUS**  
**MORE OPEN TO GUIDANCE**  
**STILL TERRIBLE AT CYBERSECURITY**

Their relationship with Aperture is best described as:

> **Cautious friendship.**

The periodic visits appear to reinforce this stability.

# 12. FINAL STATUS

The Architects and Neko will continue making periodic visits to SPAWN.

No fixed frequency has been established.

No formal diplomatic agreement exists.

No treaty has been signed.

SPAWN knows that if something goes catastrophically wrong, Guiding Light may appear.

If they have a question, Curious Light may answer.

If something inexplicably disappears, Mischevious Light may be nearby.

And if their server starts behaving strangely:

> **Neko is probably already watching.**
