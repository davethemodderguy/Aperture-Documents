# 1. CURRENT STATUS

Phoenix successfully located Jake within his cognitive environment.

The environment remains an expansive winter wasteland.

Jake was found sitting alone within the snowstorm.

Initial communication was established.

Jake acknowledged Phoenix's presence.

However, he remained unwilling to accept the possibility of returning.

---

# 2. JAKE'S CONDITION

Jake's physical appearance within the cognitive environment was inconsistent.

His body remained recognizable.

His white hair.

His wings.

His small frame.

His familiar clothing.

However, his **face was obscured by static.**

The static did not behave like ordinary visual interference.

It continuously moved across his face, preventing Phoenix from seeing his expression.

His eyes could not be clearly identified.

His mouth could not be seen.

His face was simply—

**gone behind static.**

Phoenix attempted to approach.

Jake did not move.

---

# 3. THE CONVERSATION

Phoenix sat across from him.

The snow continued falling.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake.”

No response.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “We can fix this.”

Jake remained still.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Curious Light can make you a new core.”

Silence.

Phoenix continued.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “You don't have to stay here.”

Jake finally responded.

> **JAKE:**  
> “No.”

Phoenix paused.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake—”

> **JAKE:**  
> “No.”

His voice was quiet.

Not angry.

Not frightened.

Simply certain.

---

# 4. PHOENIX ATTEMPTS AGAIN

Phoenix moved closer.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “You don't have to forgive me.”

No response.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “You don't have to forgive anyone.”

The static across Jake's face intensified.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “You don't even have to understand everything right now.”

She held out her hand.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Just let go.”

Jake remained motionless.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Please.”

Jake answered:

> **JAKE:**  
> “No.”

---

# 5. THE MEANING

Phoenix realized that Jake was not rejecting her specifically.

He was rejecting the idea of returning.

His mind had already reached a conclusion.

He had spent fifty-four days convincing himself that needing people was harmful.

The broken core had become part of that conclusion.

A new core represented something Jake was no longer certain he deserved.

Life.

Care.

Attention.

Dependency.

Love.

All of it.

Phoenix tried again.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake, you belong with us.”

Jake's static-covered face tilted slightly.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I don't.”

Phoenix's expression broke.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Yes, you do.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “No.”

---

# 6. THE ENVIRONMENT CHANGES

The snow suddenly stopped.

For approximately two seconds, the entire wasteland became completely still.

Then—

**CRACK.**

Phoenix looked down.

A thin fracture had appeared in the ground beneath her.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake?”

Another crack appeared.

Then another.

The frozen ground began splitting apart.

Phoenix stepped backward.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “What are you doing?”

Jake remained seated.

The static continued obscuring his face.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Go.”

Phoenix shook her head.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “No.”

The ground fractured again.

A deep blue light appeared beneath the cracks.

---

# 7. FORCED REJECTION

Phoenix attempted to reach Jake.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “I'm not leaving you!”

The ground beneath her collapsed.

Phoenix fell.

She caught the edge of the fractured surface.

Snow poured around her.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake!”

Jake remained in the distance.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “PLEASE!”

Jake's voice remained quiet.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Go home.”

Phoenix held on.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “I'm trying to bring you home!”

Jake answered:

> **JAKE:**  
> “I don't want to.”

---

# 8. PHOENIX'S LAST ATTEMPT

Phoenix pulled herself upward.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “You don't have to want it yet!”

Jake remained motionless.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “You can be angry!”

No response.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “You can be scared!”

The cracks expanded.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “You can hate me!”

The environment began collapsing around her.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Just don't disappear!”

Jake finally looked directly toward her.

His face remained hidden behind static.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I'm tired.”

Phoenix froze.

---

# 9. EXTRACTION

The ground beneath Phoenix gave way completely.

She fell into darkness.

The last thing she heard was Jake's voice.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I'm sorry.”

Phoenix reached toward him.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake!”

The cognitive environment disappeared.

---

# 10. RETURN

Phoenix's body violently re-entered physical reality.

She collapsed beside Jake's motionless body.

Guiding Light immediately caught her.

Phoenix gasped.

Her hands were shaking.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “He wouldn't come.”

Curious Light approached.

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “What happened?”

Phoenix looked toward Jake.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “He rejected it.”

Curious Light examined the fractured core.

It remained broken.

However—

one small portion of the fracture was glowing.

Curious Light noticed immediately.

---

# 11. THE CORE RESPONSE

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Wait.”

Phoenix looked up.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “What?”

Curious Light carefully examined the core.

The fractured halves were no longer completely inert.

A faint pulse moved through them.

Then stopped.

Then returned.

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “He's still connected.”

Guiding Light looked toward the core.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “He hasn't accepted the new core.”

Curious nodded.

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “No.”

Pause.

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “But he hasn't rejected existence completely either.”

Phoenix stared at the broken heart.

---

# 12. PHOENIX'S REALIZATION

Phoenix finally understood what had happened.

Jake had not destroyed the connection.

He had pushed her away.

He had forced her out because he believed staying near him would only hurt her.

Even now—

even dead—

Jake was still trying to protect everyone from himself.

Phoenix lowered her head.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “He was protecting me.”

Curious Light did not answer immediately.

Then:

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Perhaps.”

Phoenix looked toward Jake.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “He thinks I'm going to die because of him.”

She touched the teddy bear.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “He still thinks he's dangerous.”

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

She offered him a new heart.

He said no.

He said no.

She offered company.

He said no.

Then he broke the ground beneath her feet and forced her out.

Not because he hated her.

Not because he wanted her dead.

Because somewhere inside that broken little star was the same thought that had been there for fifty-four days:

> **“If I disappear, nobody has to worry about me.”**

Jake is still there.

His face is hidden.

His core is broken.

His mind is trapped behind a winter that refuses to end.

And somewhere beneath all of it—

his heart is still beating.

Barely.
