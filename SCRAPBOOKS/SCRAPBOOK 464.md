### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-APPEARANCE RECORD**

# **ORPHIE — CURRENT HEADWEAR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Status:** **CURRENT**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie's current appearance includes two items that have become closely associated with her:

- A **soft white beanie with cat ears**.
    
- A **blue-glowing hairclip gifted by Guiding Light**.
    

The combination has become her preferred way of keeping her hair comfortable and her bangs in place.

---

# **THE WHITE BEANIE**

Orphie wears a soft, woolen **white beanie with small cat ears**.

The material is deliberately comfortable.

She prefers wearing it even when the temperature does not require additional warmth.

The cat ears appear to be purely decorative.

When asked why she chose that particular design, Orphie's response was brief:

> **"They're cute."**

No further explanation was provided.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S HAIRCLIP**

The hairclip given to Orphie by Guiding Light is still worn regularly.

Unlike an ordinary hairclip, it emits a **soft blue glow**.

The light is gentle rather than bright, making it more noticeable in darkness.

The clip is primarily used to keep Orphie's bangs in place.

Guiding Light has confirmed that the glow is intentional.

No other significant anomalous properties have been documented.

---

# **APPEARANCE**

The current arrangement is simple:

**White cat-eared beanie.**

**Bangs held neatly in place by a softly glowing blue hairclip.**

The blue light occasionally reflects against the white fabric of the beanie, creating a faint blue illumination around Orphie's face.

---

# **ORPHIE'S SCAR**

The large scar across Orphie's head remains present beneath the hat.

The beanie does not completely conceal it.

Orphie simply prefers not to look at it.

The hairclip likewise does not exist to hide the injury.

It is simply something Guiding Light gave her.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S OBSERVATION**

Guiding Light appears particularly fond of seeing Orphie wear the clip.

She once remarked:

> **"It suits you."**

Orphie responded:

> **"...Thanks."**

She continued wearing it afterward.

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

Corin immediately approved of the cat ears.

> **Corin:**  
> "Oh my gosh, you look adorable."

Orphie:

> **"...Don't make it weird."**

Corin:

> **"I'm not!"**

She absolutely was.

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT'S REACTION**

Curious Light examined the glowing clip.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Interesting."

Orphie:

> **"...Don't take it apart."**

Curious Light:

> **"I wasn't going to."**

A pause.

> **"...Yet."**

Orphie immediately covered the clip with her hand.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie's current headwear has become a quiet combination of comfort and companionship.

The **white cat-eared beanie** is something she chose for herself.

The **blue-glowing hairclip** is something Guiding Light chose for her.

Neither erases the scars beneath them.

Neither needs to.

They simply represent where Orphie is now.

And for the first time in a long while, she seems comfortable wearing something simply because she likes it.
