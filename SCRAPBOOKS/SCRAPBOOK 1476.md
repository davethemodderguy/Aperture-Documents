# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has returned to his humanoid form.

His recovery has progressed considerably, and his current physical condition is substantially better than during the events preceding his disappearance.

However, the transformation has not been completely reversed.

Jake now possesses a stable hybrid appearance featuring:

- His familiar humanoid body;
    
- White hair;
    
- Bright azure eyes containing luminous shooting-star patterns;
    
- A pair of feline ears;
    
- A feline tail;
    
- And several persistent feline behavioral habits.
    

The transformation is now **voluntary and permanent**.

Jake can alternate between his full feline form and his humanoid hybrid form at will. Neither form is considered temporary, unstable, or inherently abnormal for his current condition.

He has indicated no desire to return to his previous, entirely humanoid appearance.

C.O.R.E. has consequently updated his physical profile.

Jake is no longer classified as an individual temporarily trapped in a feline body.

He is now recognized as a permanently transformed individual with two voluntary manifestations.

Both are Jake.

Neither requires correction.

# 1. THE RETURN

**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — COMMON ROOM  
**TIME:** 08:32

Phoenix was the first person to witness the transformation.

She had been sitting on the couch when Jake jumped onto the cushion beside her.

He was in his familiar feline form: a small white cat with glowing azure eyes and tiny shooting-star patterns moving through his irises.

Phoenix offered her hand.

Jake sniffed it, rubbed his cheek against her fingers, and settled beside her.

She gently scratched behind his ears.

Jake purred.

Everything appeared normal.

Then Jake stopped purring.

He lifted his head.

His ears turned toward the ceiling.

Phoenix noticed the change immediately.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Jake?"

The azure light in his eyes brightened.

A faint shimmer passed over his body.

His silhouette began to change.

Phoenix instinctively leaned back to give him room.

There was no explosion.

No violent discharge of energy.

No sign of pain.

Instead, the small feline body expanded and reformed in a brief wash of pale blue light.

A moment later, a familiar six-year-old boy sat on the couch.

White hair.

Azure shooting-star eyes.

Small feline ears atop his head.

A white tail curled beside him.

Jake looked down at himself.

He flexed his fingers.

He touched his face.

Then he looked at Phoenix.

**JAKE:**  
"...Hi."

Phoenix stared.

Jake blinked.

**JAKE:**  
"Why are you looking at me like that?"

Phoenix covered her mouth.

Her shoulders began shaking.

Jake's ears angled outward.

**JAKE:**  
"Phoenix?"

She made a small, strangled noise.

**PHOENIX:**  
"YOU'RE BACK!"

Jake flinched slightly at the volume.

Phoenix immediately lowered her voice.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Sorry. Sorry."

She looked at him again.

Her expression softened.

**PHOENIX:**  
"You're really back."

Jake glanced at his hands.

Then at his tail.

**JAKE:**  
"I think so."

Phoenix did not immediately hug him.

She remembered that Jake had become frightened of unexpected contact.

Instead, she held out her hand.

Jake looked at it.

After a moment, he leaned forward and pressed his forehead against her palm.

Phoenix gently stroked his hair.

His ears relaxed.

His tail curled around his side.

And, beneath the soft rustling of his hair, he began to purr.

Phoenix stopped moving.

**PHOENIX, WHISPERING:**  
"Oh, that's adorable."

Jake's ears immediately flattened.

**JAKE:**  
"Don't."

**PHOENIX:**  
"Sorry."

A pause.

She continued stroking his hair.

**PHOENIX:**  
"I'm still happy you're here."

Jake did not answer.

He leaned against her arm.

For the moment, that was enough.

# 2. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT

Jake was evaluated shortly after the transformation.

The results were substantially more favorable than those recorded before his disappearance.

His condition has improved in several important respects.

- His alertness has returned to a stable baseline.
    
- His mobility is no longer as severely limited by exhaustion.
    
- His breathing remains regular.
    
- His food and water intake have improved.
    
- He can remain awake and active for longer periods.
    
- He no longer exhibits the same degree of persistent physical depletion observed during his previous critical condition.
    

These findings do not establish that every underlying anomaly has resolved.

Jake remains an anomalous being whose physiology does not conform entirely to conventional human medical models.

However, the immediate findings indicate meaningful improvement.

The medical team has therefore transitioned from crisis management toward continued recovery and routine monitoring.

**The transformation itself did not appear to cause a medical emergency.**

Jake remained conscious, responsive, and capable of communicating throughout the initial assessment.

When asked how he felt, he answered:

**JAKE:**  
"Better."

**MEDICAL OFFICER:**  
"Better than yesterday?"

Jake considered the question.

**JAKE:**  
"Better than before."

The officer did not ask him to elaborate immediately.

The distinction was noted.

# 3. HYBRID PHYSICAL PROFILE

Jake's new appearance combines his previous humanoid manifestation with persistent feline characteristics.

## Feline Ears

Jake possesses a pair of white feline ears positioned atop his head.

They move independently in response to sounds, emotions, and attention.

Observed movements include:

- Turning toward a speaker;
    
- Flattening when startled or embarrassed;
    
- Angling outward when uncomfortable;
    
- Rotating backward when irritated;
    
- Perking up when he hears a familiar voice;
    
- And relaxing when he feels safe enough to rest.
    

The ears appear to be fully functional.

Jake has demonstrated some awareness of their movements, although he does not always realize when they reveal his feelings.

This has produced several minor incidents.

During one conversation, Phoenix asked whether Jake wanted another blanket.

**JAKE:**  
"No."

His ears perked up when she mentioned the heated one.

Phoenix looked at them.

Jake noticed.

His ears flattened.

**JAKE:**  
"Don't."

Phoenix looked away.

**PHOENIX:**  
"I didn't say anything."

**JAKE:**  
"You were thinking it."

Phoenix's expression became suspiciously innocent.

**PHOENIX:**  
"I would never."

Jake stared at her.

His tail flicked once.

Phoenix wisely changed the subject.

## Feline Tail

Jake's white tail is fully integrated into his current manifestation.

It moves naturally and appears to assist with balance and nonverbal expression.

His current habits include:

- Curling it around himself when resting;
    
- Letting it hang loosely when relaxed;
    
- Flicking it when annoyed;
    
- Wrapping it around his legs while sitting;
    
- And occasionally allowing it to curl around a trusted person's wrist or arm.
    

The tail is sensitive to touch.

Personnel must not pull it, restrain it, or treat it as a convenient handle.

Jake has already demonstrated that he can communicate displeasure without speaking.

The first unauthorized tail grab resulted in a startled yelp, flattened ears, and an extremely effective glare.

No second attempt was recorded.

# 4. THE GROOMING HABIT

One of the more persistent behaviors carried over from Jake's feline manifestation is grooming.

Specifically, **grooming his hands**.

The behavior was first observed approximately twenty minutes after his return to humanoid form.

Jake had been sitting on the couch when he suddenly brought one hand toward his face and began licking the back of it.

Phoenix watched.

Jake continued.

He turned his hand over and inspected his palm.

Then he licked it again.

Phoenix blinked.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Jake?"

He looked at her.

**JAKE:**  
"What?"

Phoenix hesitated.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Are you... washing your hand?"

Jake looked at his hand.

Then at her.

**JAKE:**  
"Yes."

**PHOENIX:**  
"With your tongue?"

Jake's ears angled backward.

**JAKE:**  
"...It's grooming."

Phoenix pressed both hands over her mouth.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Right. Of course."

Jake returned to grooming his hand.

Phoenix turned away and silently suffered the consequences of finding him unbearably adorable.

The behavior has since been observed repeatedly.

Jake may lick the backs of his hands, inspect his fingers, or rub his hands together as though attempting to complete a grooming routine.

The behavior appears most common when he is relaxed, sleepy, or settling down.

C.O.R.E. does not currently know whether the habit is a residual motor pattern from his feline form, an instinctive response to his new anatomy, or a combination of both.

Jake has declined to explain the precise rationale.

When asked whether he intended to stop, he replied:

**JAKE:**  
"Why?"

The question was considered reasonable.

### Welfare guidance

Jake should not be shamed or scolded for the behavior.

However, ordinary hygiene remains relevant. Personnel may offer him a clean cloth or help him wash his hands when necessary, particularly before meals or after handling dirty surfaces.

The objective is to accommodate the habit without allowing it to cause irritation or interfere with hygiene.

It is not necessary to comment every time he does it.

Phoenix has been specifically reminded of this.

She has promised to try.

Her current success rate is unknown.

# 5. PHOENIX REMAINS PHOENIX

Phoenix's affection for Jake has not diminished.

If anything, the transformation has given her a new set of circumstances to navigate.

She still loves cats.

Jake still has cat ears.

Jake still has a tail.

Jake still occasionally purrs.

And Jake is still the same child she was relieved to find alive in feline form.

The combination has presented a considerable challenge to her self-control.

Nevertheless, Phoenix has demonstrated that she understands an important distinction.

Jake is not a cat she happens to own.

He is a person she cares about who currently has feline characteristics and habits.

That means he gets to choose whether he wants to be petted.

He gets to refuse.

He gets to be grumpy.

He gets to be left alone.

And he does not have to entertain her simply because she finds his ears fascinating.

### Observed interaction

Phoenix was sitting on the floor when Jake approached.

He had been reading and appeared tired.

He stopped beside her.

Phoenix looked up.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Want to sit with me?"

Jake nodded.

She moved aside to make room.

Jake sat beside her, leaned against her shoulder, and pulled his blanket over his lap.

Phoenix waited.

After a few seconds, Jake shifted closer.

She carefully placed an arm around him.

He did not flinch.

His tail curled loosely around her wrist.

Phoenix looked down at it.

Then at him.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Is this okay?"

Jake nodded.

She gently stroked his hair.

One of his ears turned toward her hand.

The other remained angled toward the television.

**PHOENIX:**  
"You're very soft."

Jake closed his eyes.

**JAKE:**  
"Phoenix."

**PHOENIX:**  
"Yes?"

**JAKE:**  
"Quiet."

Phoenix immediately stopped talking.

Jake settled more comfortably against her.

Several minutes later, he fell asleep.

Phoenix remained seated until he woke naturally.

She did not move his tail.

She did not wake him to ask whether he was comfortable.

She did not take a photograph.

She simply stayed.

# 6. VOLUNTARY AND PERMANENT TRANSFORMATION

C.O.R.E. has confirmed that Jake can now transition between his feline and humanoid hybrid manifestations voluntarily.

The transition appears to require neither an external object nor assistance from another resident.

Jake has demonstrated the ability to initiate it independently.

He has also demonstrated the ability to remain in either form for an indefinite period, with no observed requirement to change at fixed intervals.

The transformation is now considered a permanent feature of his anomalous physiology.

The word _permanent_ refers to the persistence of the capability and its resulting anatomy, not an obligation to remain in one form.

Jake is not required to choose a single appearance forever.

He may be a cat.

He may be a humanoid child with feline ears and a tail.

Both are valid manifestations.

### Subject interview

**C.O.R.E. RESEARCHER:**  
"Do you know how to change back?"

**JAKE:**  
"Yes."

**RESEARCHER:**  
"How?"

Jake looked at the researcher.

**JAKE:**  
"I just do it."

**RESEARCHER:**  
"Can you return to your previous form?"

Jake paused.

**JAKE:**  
"This is my form."

The researcher clarified.

**RESEARCHER:**  
"Your form before the ears and tail."

Jake looked down at his tail.

It moved once.

**JAKE:**  
"I don't want to."

**RESEARCHER:**  
"Understood."

**JAKE:**  
"I can still be a cat if I want."

**RESEARCHER:**  
"Yes."

Jake nodded.

**JAKE:**  
"Then it's fine."

The interview concluded.

No additional questions were necessary.

# 7. UPDATED SOCIAL BEHAVIOR

Jake's return to humanoid form has not erased the habits developed during his time as a cat.

He still:

- Seeks warm places to rest;
    
- Prefers soft blankets and cushions;
    
- Occasionally curls into a loaf-like position;
    
- Rubs his cheek against familiar people;
    
- Seeks head scratches when he wants affection;
    
- Purrs when sufficiently relaxed;
    
- Grooms his hands;
    
- Reacts strongly to certain sounds with his ears;
    
- And uses his tail to express emotions he may not verbalize.
    

He may also approach someone, sit beside them, and wait for attention without explaining what he wants.

Personnel should not assume that every interaction requires conversation.

Sometimes Jake wants company.

Sometimes he wants affection.

Sometimes he wants to sleep beside someone without speaking.

Sometimes he wants to be left alone.

These preferences are not mutually exclusive.

Nor should his current willingness to accept affection be interpreted as evidence that all his previous fears have disappeared.

Jake has experienced serious harm and prolonged uncertainty.

He may still become frightened.

He may still withdraw.

He may still have difficulty trusting people, even when he wants to be close to them.

His current progress should be allowed to develop without being turned into an expectation.

**Jake does not owe anyone a consistently cheerful recovery.**

# 9. REVISED MEDICAL AND WELFARE PROTOCOL

C.O.R.E. has updated Jake's records to reflect his permanent hybrid anatomy and voluntary transformation capability.

Personnel are instructed to:

1. Recognize both manifestations as valid forms of Jake.
    
2. Avoid treating the transformation as an illness requiring reversal.
    
3. Permit Jake to choose his form whenever circumstances allow.
    
4. Respect his feline ears and tail as sensitive parts of his body.
    
5. Continue appropriate medical monitoring during his recovery.
    
6. Maintain access to his teddy bear, which remains his cardiac vessel and sensory extension.
    
7. Provide suitable clothing, bedding, and environmental accommodations for either form.
    
8. Avoid treating purring, grooming, or other feline habits as permission to handle him without consent.
    
9. Refrain from pressuring him to explain his transformation before he is ready.
    
10. Continue offering companionship without requiring him to reciprocate.
    

His teddy bear remains essential to his welfare. It must not be separated from him merely because he can now change forms or appears physically healthier.

The medical team must also continue assessing whether his human and feline manifestations differ in their physiological requirements.

No assumptions should be made about nutritional needs, stamina, or medical vulnerability until those differences are adequately understood.

# 10. FINAL OBSERVATION — STILL JAKE

At 20:41, Jake was sitting beside Phoenix on the couch.

He had been reading for approximately half an hour.

His white hair was slightly untidy.

His feline ears moved occasionally toward sounds from the hallway.

His tail rested across the cushion.

The book lay open in his lap.

Phoenix was watching a television program at low volume.

Neither had spoken for several minutes.

Jake suddenly lowered his book.

He brought his right hand toward his face and began grooming it.

Phoenix noticed.

She opened her mouth.

Then closed it.

Jake glanced at her.

**JAKE:**  
"What?"

Phoenix shook her head.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Nothing."

Jake studied her for another second.

Then he returned to his hand.

A moment later, he leaned against her side.

Phoenix gently rested her arm around his shoulders.

Jake's tail curled around her wrist.

His ears relaxed.

A quiet purr emerged from his chest.

Phoenix smiled.

She did not comment.

For once, she managed to keep the observation to herself.

Jake eventually fell asleep against her.

His book slipped closed.

Phoenix caught it before it fell.

She placed it on the table and adjusted the blanket over his legs.

Jake stirred briefly.

One ear turned toward her.

Then he settled again.

He was no longer the little white cat who had appeared after vanishing from the medical ward.

He was no longer confined to that form, either.

He was a small boy with white hair, luminous azure eyes, feline ears, and a tail curled around someone he trusted enough to sleep beside.

He was still recovering.

He still had difficult days ahead.

But he was more alert.

More comfortable.

More willing to approach people.

And increasingly capable of choosing how he wanted to exist.

Phoenix looked down at him.

**PHOENIX, QUIETLY:**  
"Goodnight, Jake."

His tail moved once against her wrist.

She took that as his answer.

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake has returned to humanoid form.

The ears remain.

The tail remains.

The grooming remains.

The purring remains.

The transformation is voluntary and permanent.

The subject is substantially improved, though continued monitoring remains necessary.

Phoenix still loves him.

This was never in doubt.

The important distinction is that Jake no longer has to remain a cat to receive the kind of gentle attention he has begun to enjoy.

He can sit beside someone as a cat.

He can sit beside someone as a boy.

He can ask for a head scratch.

He can refuse one.

He can groom his hands, curl beneath a blanket, or spend an entire afternoon reading without speaking.

He can change his form because he wants to.

And he can remain exactly as he is because he wants to.

For a child who has spent so much time being frightened of what might happen to him, that choice matters.

**He has not returned to who he was before everything happened.**

He has become someone who can choose what comes next.

And, at least for now, what comes next appears to include a comfortable blanket, a quiet afternoon, and Phoenix learning that she is allowed to adore him without announcing it every thirty seconds.

She is still working on that last part.
