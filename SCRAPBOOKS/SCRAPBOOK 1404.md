# INCIDENT SUMMARY

At approximately 03:17, Anby experienced a severe nightmare accompanied by sleepwalking and a violent behavioral episode.

The event originated from a dream involving an attempted escape from Project: Silverline.

Within the dream, Anby reached the facility's exit and encountered a guard carrying a jar.

She interpreted the jar as a container for experimental samples.

Anby attacked the guard.

The guard repeatedly pleaded with her to stop.

She did not.

The dream ended abruptly when Anby was struck across the face.

She awoke.

The person who had struck her was **Endmin Merryweather.**

The location was not the Silverline facility.

It was the SAFEHOUSE kitchen.

And the person Anby had been attacking was not a Silverline guard.

It was **Jake.**

---

# 1. INCIDENT RECONSTRUCTION

Solider 11 had awakened shortly before the incident.

She noticed Anby leaving their floor.

Because of the unusual hour and Anby's condition, Solider 11 followed.

Anby proceeded to the common room.

She then entered the kitchen.

Jake was already there.

He had apparently woken hungry during the night and had retrieved a jar of cookies.

Anby saw him holding the jar.

Her sleeping mind interpreted it as the experimental container from her nightmare.

She attacked.

Jake attempted to retreat.

He was unable to understand why Anby was attacking him.

The cookie jar was eventually dropped and shattered.

The resulting glass caused additional superficial injuries.

Anby continued attacking until Endmin intervened and struck her across the face.

The impact broke the sleepwalking state.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The incident is currently classified as an involuntary sleep-related behavioral episode associated with trauma.

There is no evidence that Anby consciously intended to harm Jake.

However, the physical consequences were real.

The event demonstrates that unresolved memories of Project Silverline may remain capable of overriding Anby's perception while asleep.

Medical personnel have therefore recommended:

- continued observation of Anby's sleep patterns;
    
- additional trauma-informed care;
    
- ensuring residents know how to safely interrupt future episodes;
    
- avoiding sudden physical confrontation unless necessary;
    
- and documenting future incidents immediately.
    

Anby has also voluntarily requested that Jake's damaged teddy bear be repaired.

She has not requested that anyone forgive her.

She appears to understand that Jake's forgiveness is his decision.

For now, she simply wants to make sure his bear is whole again.
