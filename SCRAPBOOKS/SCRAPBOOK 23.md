**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal Personnel

---

**Visitor:** Senior Manager Norma Lockwood  
**Codename:** JUGGERNAUT

---

## **SUMMARY**

Senior Manager Norma Lockwood arrived at Heaven's Blacksite for a scheduled coordination meeting with Director Belle Merryweather.

As was typical, the meeting itself lasted less than fifteen minutes.

The remainder of the afternoon was spent socializing with SAFEHOUSE residents, touring Engineering, and catching up with personnel.

Norma arrived in casual clothing—a gray hoodie and dark trousers—with her standard **35 kg belt-fed 7.62 mm machine gun** slung across her back.

As required by Blacksite regulations during non-operational visits, the weapon was **unloaded**.

Her **Juggernaut Armor** remained in its compact nanotechnological storage state and was not deployed.

---

## **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE**

> **Belle:** "Norma!"

The two shared a quick hug before heading inside.

Belle then introduced Norma to the SAFEHOUSE residents.

## **THE QUESTION**

While Norma was helping Engineering move several heavy equipment crates, one junior researcher quietly stared at the scene.

On one side...

Belle casually rested her **50 kg hybrid sword** against her shoulder.

Nearby...

Remielle's **55 kg multifunction combat briefcase** sat on a workbench.

Kent carried his combat shotgun with practiced ease.

Norma adjusted the **35 kg machine gun** on her back as though it weighed almost nothing.

The researcher finally asked aloud:

> "...Why does everyone we've seen working at the KCA have exponentially heavy equipment..."

He looked around the room.

> "...Yet carry it with little to no effort?"

The workshop fell silent.

Belle slowly raised a hand.

> "...We do a lot of training."

Remielle added:

> "...A lot."

Kent simply nodded.

Norma laughed.

> "You'd be surprised how quickly 'heavy' becomes 'normal' after enough years."

Neko looked between the four KCA personnel.

Then looked down at Belle's sword.

He decided against trying to lift it again.
