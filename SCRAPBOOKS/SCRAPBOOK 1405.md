# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The six members of **Obol Squad** recently contacted **Tachyon Industries** regarding the acquisition of Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise.

The request was initially interpreted as a routine procurement inquiry.

It was not.

The six artificial-human sisters had apparently discovered the existence of Tachyon Industries merchandise and collectively decided that they wanted some.

The interaction quickly became less of a corporate inquiry and more of a coordinated attempt by six highly capable CED personnel to convince Tachyon Industries representatives to let them buy things.

They were successful.

---

# 1. INITIAL CONTACT

The Obol Squad arrived at the Tachyon Industries Merchandise Store together.

The group consisted of:

- **Anby**
    
- **Solider 11**
    
- **Twiggy**
    
- **Iris**
    
- **Phaethon**
    
- **Sora**
    

Their stated purpose was:

> **“We want Tachyon merchandise.”**

No additional justification was initially provided.

---

# 2. TACHYON RESPONSE

The store employee on duty requested clarification.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “What kind of merchandise?”

There was a brief silence.

Six sisters looked at one another.

Anby answered first.

> **ANBY:**  
> “All of it.”

The employee paused.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “...All of it?”

Anby nodded.

> **ANBY:**  
> “If possible.”

Solider 11 immediately added:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> “Preferably.”

---

# 3. MERCHANDISE DISCOVERY

The sisters proceeded to examine the available merchandise.

Items of interest included:

- Tachyon Industries shirts;
    
- jackets;
    
- hats;
    
- branded accessories;
    
- limited-edition items;
    
- Tachyon Industries insignia;
    
- weapon-themed collectibles;
    
- technical merchandise;
    
- store-exclusive items;
    
- and other corporate memorabilia.
    

Twiggy became particularly interested in the technical-themed products.

Sora immediately began comparing prices.

Phaethon inspected the limited-edition section.

Iris found a Tachyon-branded vehicle accessory.

Solider 11 began asking whether there were discounts for bulk purchases.

Anby quietly picked up a shirt.

---

# 4. THE BULK PURCHASE QUESTION

Solider 11 approached the counter.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> “Do you offer group discounts?”

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “For how many people?”

Solider 11 pointed behind herself.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> “Six.”

The employee looked at the rest of Obol Squad.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “Are you all buying?”

Six simultaneous nods.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “...I can ask.”

Solider 11 smiled.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> “Thank you.”

The employee immediately contacted Tachyon Industries management.

---

# 5. TACHYON INDUSTRIES RESPONSE

The response from Tachyon Industries was unexpectedly enthusiastic.

The six were recognized as:

- CED personnel;
    
- protected artificial humans;
    
- residents associated with the SAFEHOUSE;
    
- and the newly established **Obol Squad**.
    

The company representative authorized a merchandise package for the group.

The package included a combination of:

- standard merchandise;
    
- limited-edition merchandise;
    
- Obol Squad-related items;
    
- Tachyon-branded clothing;
    
- accessories;
    
- and several promotional items.
    

The sisters were informed that certain items were limited in quantity.

This immediately became important.

---

# 6. LIMITED EDITION PROBLEM

Phaethon found a limited-edition Tachyon item.

She picked it up.

Sora looked at it.

Twiggy looked at it.

Iris looked at it.

Solider 11 looked at it.

Anby looked at it.

There was a brief silence.

> **SORA:**  
> “How many are there?”

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “One.”

Six sisters looked at one another.

The employee immediately intervened.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “Please don't fight over it.”

Anby slowly put her hands together.

> **ANBY:**  
> “We won't.”

Iris quietly whispered:

> **IRIS:**  
> “We should buy another.”

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “There isn't another.”

> **IRIS:**  
> “Make another.”

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “That's... not how limited editions work.”

---

# 7. ANBY'S PURCHASE

Anby ultimately selected a simple Tachyon Industries jacket.

She held it against herself.

Twiggy looked at it.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> “You like it?”

Anby nodded.

> **ANBY:**  
> “It's soft.”

Solider 11 smiled.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> “Then that's the one.”

Anby looked at the price.

She hesitated.

The employee noticed.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “You can get it.”

Anby looked up.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Really?”

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “Really.”

Anby quietly nodded.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Okay.”

---

# 8. IRIS

Iris had a completely different priority.

She found Tachyon Industries vehicle merchandise.

She examined it.

Then asked:

> **IRIS:**  
> “Do you have anything for actual vehicles?”

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “We have some accessories.”

Iris immediately became interested.

> **IRIS:**  
> “Show me.”

The employee later described the exchange as:

> **“The moment the driver discovered the automotive section.”**

Iris purchased several items.

---

# 9. SORA

Sora spent considerably more time than the others examining technical merchandise.

She eventually approached the employee.

> **SORA:**  
> “Do you sell mathematical-themed merchandise?”

The employee stared at her.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “Mathematical-themed?”

> **SORA:**  
> “Yes.”

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “We have engineering designs.”

Sora looked interested.

> **SORA:**  
> “Good enough.”

She selected one.

Phaethon selected another.

The two then spent several minutes comparing the printed technical diagrams.

Neither explained what they were discussing.

---

# 10. TWIGGY

Twiggy gravitated toward medical and utility-themed merchandise.

She found a Tachyon Industries medical-style bag.

She opened it.

Looked inside.

Closed it.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> “This is actually useful.”

Anby looked over.

> **ANBY:**  
> “You're buying it?”

Twiggy nodded.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> “Yes.”

Solider 11 looked at her.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> “That's the most practical thing anyone has bought today.”

Twiggy smiled.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> “Thank you.”

---

# 11. PHAETHON

Phaethon was considerably more selective.

She examined nearly everything before choosing.

Eventually, she selected a limited-edition Tachyon item.

The employee informed her that only a small number remained.

Phaethon nodded.

> **PHAETHON:**  
> “I'll take one.”

No further discussion occurred.

She paid.

She kept it.

---

# 12. SOLIDER 11

Solider 11's purchases were less predictable.

She selected several items and then returned to the counter.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> “Do you sell anything with the Tachyon logo that doesn't look like merchandise?”

The employee considered this.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “...We have some jackets.”

Solider 11 smiled.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> “Perfect.”

She bought one.

---

# 13. THE SIX SISTERS

Eventually, all six stood together at the counter.

Several bags had accumulated.

The employee looked at the pile.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “Anything else?”

The six looked at one another.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “You're welcome.”

Iris stopped.

> **IRIS:**

---

# 17. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The incident presents no security concern.

The Obol Squad's interaction with Tachyon Industries was entirely voluntary and civilian in nature.

No classified information was exchanged.

No restricted equipment was requested.

No weapons were purchased.

The six simply wanted merchandise.

This is considered a normal recreational activity.

The only unusual factor was the degree of collective enthusiasm displayed by six individuals who have historically spent considerably more time discussing combat equipment than corporate clothing.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Obol Squad entered Tachyon Industries with a simple objective:

> **Get merchandise.**

They negotiated.

They compared.

They browsed.

They argued over one limited-edition item without actually fighting.

They bought enough merchandise to require multiple bags.

Then they went home.

Anby wore her new jacket.

Twiggy carried her utility bag.

Iris immediately began planning where to put her vehicle accessories.

Sora and Phaethon continued discussing technical designs.

Solider 11 claimed she had negotiated the discount.

Nobody could prove this.

And somewhere behind them, the Tachyon Industries employee watched six artificial humans leave the store with their new merchandise and quietly realized:

They were probably coming back.
