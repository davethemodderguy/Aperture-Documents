# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **SECURITY & CUSTOMER SERVICES DIVISION**

### **SUBJECT: MART COLONY ROLE CLASSIFICATIONS**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

While all Marts belong to a single Ether-linked hivemind and remain equal members of the colony, those residing at the Krampus Compliance Authority have naturally adopted specialized roles.

These roles are **voluntary**.

No Mart is assigned to a position.

Instead, individuals simply gravitate toward tasks they enjoy, with the hivemind ensuring every responsibility is covered.

Current observations identify **four primary roles**.

---

# **TYPE I – AMBASSADOR**

**Known Individuals:** **1**

The Ambassador serves as the colony's primary representative.

Responsibilities include:

- Speaking fluent English.
    
- Translating Mart speech for personnel.
    
- Greeting visitors.
    
- Assisting Customer Services.
    
- Explaining Mart customs.
    
- Coordinating the colony during unusual situations.
    

The Ambassador's unofficial office is located beside Dialyn's desk.

Unlike other Marts, the Ambassador is capable of holding lengthy conversations with personnel.

Despite this, it often chooses to greet other Marts with the traditional:

> "Hello!"

---

# **TYPE II – RAPID RESPONSE MARTS**

**Known Individuals:** Variable

Rapid Response Marts constitute the colony's defensive element.

During normal operations, they appear indistinguishable from Resident Marts.

Upon detecting an active threat, they immediately relocate through the building's ventilation network and prepare defensive positions.

Documented responsibilities include:

- Setting up non-lethal ambushes.
    
- Protecting civilians.
    
- Disarming hostile individuals.
    
- Blocking escape routes.
    
- Coordinating with Camera Mart.
    
- Entering "Rock Mode" when necessary.
    

Rapid Response Marts are identified by their miniature **KCA Security hats**.

They remain entirely peaceful outside emergency situations.

---

# **TYPE III – CAMERA MART**

**Known Individuals:** **1**

Camera Mart is permanently stationed inside the Security Control Room.

Responsibilities include:

- Monitoring surveillance cameras.
    
- Detecting suspicious activity.
    
- Alerting Security personnel.
    
- Tracking intruders.
    
- Coordinating Rapid Response Marts through the hivemind.
    

Camera Mart also assists in cooling surveillance equipment simply by remaining nearby.

Like the Rapid Response Marts, Camera Mart wears a miniature KCA Security hat.

Unlike them, it almost never leaves the Security Control Room.

---

# **TYPE IV – RESIDENT MARTS**

**Known Individuals:** Approximately **497**

Resident Marts make up the majority of the KCA colony.

They perform no dedicated security function.

Instead, they spend their time:

- Helping employees.
    
- Greeting visitors.
    
- Cleaning dust from equipment.
    
- Carrying small objects.
    
- Comforting distressed personnel.
    
- Sharing cheese crackers.
    
- Making the workplace feel welcoming.
    

If an emergency occurs, Resident Marts will prioritize escorting civilians to safety before assisting Rapid Response Marts if needed.

---

# **COLONY STRUCTURE**

Despite these apparent specializations, researchers emphasize that no hierarchy exists.

The Ambassador does not command the colony.

Camera Mart does not supervise Security.

Rapid Response Marts do not outrank Resident Marts.

Every Mart considers itself simply...

> "Mart."

Their specialization is best described as preference rather than rank.

The Mart colony at the Krampus Compliance Authority continues to operate as a single, unified community.

Watching security cameras...

Preparing to protect civilians...

> "Mart."
