# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following the formation of **Obol Squad**, C.O.R.E. has documented the individual combat specialties of the six recovered Project: Silverline artificial humans.

Although all six share the same physical template and extensive foundational combat training, they have developed distinct preferred combat roles.

Their current specialties are:

|Member|Specialty|
|---|---|
|**Anby**|Dual Energy Blades|
|**Solider 11**|Heavy Machete|
|**Twiggy**|Compound Bow|
|**Phaethon**|Sniper Rifle|
|**Sora**|Magazine-Fed Shotgun|
|**Iris**|Vehicle Operations / Driving|

The sisters do not appear to regard these specialties as strict limitations.

They are simply the roles in which each member is most comfortable and experienced.

---

# 1. ANBY — DUAL ENERGY BLADES

**SPECIALTY:** Dual Energy Blades

Anby favors close-range combat using two energy blades simultaneously.

Her fighting style is highly mobile and controlled.

Unlike Solider 11, who favors overwhelming physical force, Anby generally relies on:

- speed;
    
- positioning;
    
- precise movement;
    
- rapid transitions;
    
- and coordinated use of both blades.
    

Her dual-blade specialization appears to have developed naturally from Silverline's foundational combat training.

Anby herself describes the weapons simply as:

> **ANBY:**  
> "Fast."

When asked why she uses two:

> **ANBY:**  
> "One isn't enough."

---

# 2. SOLIDER 11 — HEAVY MACHETE

**SPECIALTY:** Heavy Machete

Solider 11 favors a large, heavy machete.

Her fighting style is noticeably more direct than Anby's.

She places emphasis on:

- strength;
    
- momentum;
    
- close-range pressure;
    
- decisive strikes;
    
- and intimidation.
    

The weapon's size makes it visually distinct from a conventional machete.

Solider 11 does not appear particularly concerned about this distinction.

When asked why she chose it:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "It works."

Jane subsequently asked:

> **JANE:**  
> "That's your whole explanation?"

Solider 11 nodded.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Yep."

Jane approved.

---

# 3. TWIGGY — COMPOUND BOW

**SPECIALTY:** Compound Bow

Twiggy's preferred weapon is a compound bow.

The selection is somewhat unexpected given her current civilian background as a nurse.

However, recovered Silverline records indicate that Twiggy received extensive ranged-combat training.

Her current specialization emphasizes:

- controlled ranged engagement;
    
- patience;
    
- accuracy;
    
- observation;
    
- and maintaining distance from threats.
    

Twiggy's medical background also appears to influence her general combat behavior.

She tends to avoid unnecessary escalation.

When asked why she prefers the bow:

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "It's quiet."

Solider 11 responded:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "You say that like the rest of us aren't."

Twiggy looked at her.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "You're carrying a machete."

Solider 11 paused.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Fair."

---

# 4. PHAETHON — SNIPER RIFLE

**SPECIALTY:** Sniper Rifle

Phaethon specializes in long-range precision using a sniper rifle.

Her role within Obol Squad is therefore substantially different from Anby and Solider 11.

Phaethon favors:

- observation;
    
- patience;
    
- precision;
    
- long-range positioning;
    
- and deliberate engagement.
    

She is generally quiet when preparing for a mission.

She does not appear to enjoy unnecessary conversation during operations.

Anby described Phaethon's specialty as:

> **ANBY:**  
> "She waits."

Phaethon corrected her:

> **PHAETHON:**  
> "I observe."

Anby nodded.

> **ANBY:**  
> "She waits while observing."

Phaethon accepted this.

---

# 5. SORA — MAGAZINE-FED SHOTGUN

**SPECIALTY:** Magazine-Fed Shotgun

Sora favors a magazine-fed shotgun.

Her specialization provides Obol Squad with a close-to-mid-range option distinct from both Anby and Solider 11.

Sora's combat behavior is generally described as energetic and aggressive.

She prefers:

- rapid target transitions;
    
- sustained engagement;
    
- mobility;
    
- and immediate response to threats.
    

Her choice of weapon has also produced a recurring argument within the group.

> **SORA:**  
> "It's practical."

Twiggy looked at the weapon.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "It's loud."

Sora smiled.

> **SORA:**  
> "Exactly."

Twiggy sighed.

---

# 6. IRIS — VEHICLE OPERATIONS

**SPECIALTY:** Vehicle Operations

Iris is the outlier.

Her primary specialization is not a conventional weapon.

It is **driving.**

Her reputation was established during her time working as a contractor.

Witness accounts consistently describe Iris as an exceptionally capable driver.

She has demonstrated:

- excellent vehicle control;
    
- strong route awareness;
    
- rapid environmental assessment;
    
- calm decision-making;
    
- and unusually high confidence behind the wheel.
    

Solider 11 has repeatedly described Iris as:

> **"The best driver I've ever worked with."**

Iris generally responds:

> **IRIS:**  
> "I know."

---

The six specialties provide a broad distribution of capabilities.

### CLOSE RANGE

**Anby** — Dual Energy Blades

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The six members of Obol Squad possess highly distinct combat preferences despite sharing a common artificial-human template and training background.

Their specialties are:

**Anby — Dual Energy Blades**  
**Solider 11 — Heavy Machete**  
**Twiggy — Compound Bow**  
**Phaethon — Sniper Rifle**  
**Sora — Magazine-Fed Shotgun**  
**Iris — Vehicle Operations**

The differences are significant.

They are not six copies performing the same role.

They are six individuals who happen to have started from the same physical foundation.

Their specialties further demonstrate the same principle represented by their hairclips:

> **Identical origin does not mean identical identity.**
