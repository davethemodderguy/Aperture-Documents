### ANOMALOUS RESEARCH & RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**LOCATION:** BLACKSITE PERIMETER

---

# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

At 02:17, a luminous object entered Blacksite airspace.

Initial classification:

> **METEORIC OBJECT**

Secondary classification:

> **ANOMALOUS CELESTIAL BODY**

Final classification:

> **JAKE**

The object impacted the ground approximately 43 meters outside the Blacksite perimeter.

No crater was produced.

No explosion occurred.

No structural damage was recorded.

Instead, the impact produced a brief blue flash.

When the light dissipated, a small figure was standing in the center of the impact site.

White hair.

Azure eyes.

Shooting-star patterns moving across his irises.

Wings.

Black clothing.

Small body.

Alive.

Intact.

Jake had returned.

---

# 2. INITIAL RESPONSE

Perimeter personnel immediately alerted C.O.R.E.

The automated threat system identified the object as anomalous.

Security personnel prepared to respond.

Neko arrived first.

He stopped approximately ten meters from the impact site.

Jake looked toward him.

Neither moved.

Then Jake raised one hand.

A small wave.

Neko lowered his weapon.

> **NEKO:**  
> "...Jake?"

Jake nodded.

That was enough.

---

# 3. PHYSICAL CONDITION

Jake appears physically intact.

No visible:

- fractures;
    
- burns;
    
- lacerations;
    
- frostbite;
    
- dehydration;
    
- stellar destabilization;
    
- dimensional corruption;
    
- Null Shell formation;
    
- or other obvious injuries.
    

His halo is present.

His wings are present.

His eyes are illuminated.

His stellar manifestation is stable.

His human manifestation is also stable.

His teddy bear is notably absent.

This was immediately identified as unacceptable.

---

# 4. THE TEDDY BEAR

The teddy bear had remained in SAFEHOUSE custody throughout Jake's absence.

It was never considered abandoned property.

It was never dismantled.

It was never studied beyond necessary welfare monitoring.

It remained in the common room.

Its heartbeat continued.

When Neko received confirmation that Jake had returned, he immediately contacted Bao.

Bao arrived carrying the bear.

She did not run.

She did not shout.

She simply walked toward Jake.

Jake saw the teddy bear.

He stopped.

For several seconds, he did nothing.

Then he walked toward Bao.

Bao held the bear out.

Jake took it.

He pressed it against his chest.

The teddy's heartbeat remained steady.

Jake closed his eyes.

---

# 5. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake did not speak.

He simply held the bear.

Bao placed one hand gently on his head.

> **BAO:**  
> "There you are."

Jake's shoulders moved slightly.

He hugged the bear tighter.

Bao did not ask where he had been.

She did not ask why he left.

She did not ask why he came back.

She did not ask whether he was okay.

She simply remained there.

---

# 6. RESIDENT RESPONSE

The information spread through SAFEHOUSE rapidly.

Nora arrived first.

She stopped when she saw Jake.

Her eyes widened.

> **NORA:**  
> "...Big Brother?"

Jake looked at her.

Nora immediately covered her mouth.

She was crying.

Jake walked over.

Nora hugged him.

Jake returned the hug.

Neither spoke.

---

# 7. CLOVER

Clover arrived several minutes later.

She stopped at the perimeter entrance.

Jake was standing there.

Alive.

Intact.

Holding his teddy bear.

Clover's face went completely pale.

She looked at him.

Then at the sky.

Then back at him.

Her eyes filled with tears.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "You're here."

Jake nodded.

Clover took one step forward.

Stopped.

She clearly wanted to approach him.

She clearly did not know whether she was allowed to.

Jake looked at her.

Then held up one hand.

Not pushing her away.

Just waiting.

Clover slowly approached.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "I'm sorry."

Jake remained silent.

Clover's voice shook.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "I thought..."

She couldn't finish.

Jake looked at her for several seconds.

Then shook his head.

Clover began crying.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "You don't have to forgive me."

Jake shook his head again.

Then he pointed toward himself.

Then toward Clover.

Then shook his head one more time.

Clover understood.

He was telling her:

**It wasn't you.**

She covered her face.

---

# 8. JANE DOE

Jane arrived shortly afterward.

She stared at Jake.

Then at the teddy bear.

Then back at Jake.

> **JANE:**  
> "You really couldn't just use the door?"

Jake looked toward the Blacksite.

Then at the craterless impact site.

Jane sighed.

> **JANE:**  
> "Right."

She crouched slightly.

> **JANE:**  
> "Welcome home, kid."

Jake looked down.

A tiny smile appeared.

It was small.

Brief.

But genuine.

Jane noticed.

She did not comment.

---

# 9. NERI

Neri arrived quietly.

She stopped several meters away.

Jake noticed her.

Neri looked at him.

Then at the teddy bear.

Then back at him.

She walked closer.

She pointed at the bear.

Jake hugged it.

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> "...Good."

Jake looked at her.

Neri added:

> **NERI:**  
> "You kept it."

Jake nodded.

Neri reached into her pocket.

She produced a tiny folded piece of paper.

She handed it to him.

Jake opened it.

A small blue star.

Nothing else.

He held it carefully.

---

# 10. AETERNA

Aeterna arrived last.

She looked at Jake.

Then at the sky.

Then back at Jake.

For several seconds, she said nothing.

Finally:

> **AETERNA:**  
> "You disappeared."

Jake nodded.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "You came back."

Another nod.

Aeterna approached.

She placed one armored hand gently against the top of his head.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Good."

Jake leaned against her armor.

Aeterna remained still.

---

# 11. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT

Phoenix performed a basic welfare examination.

Jake cooperated.

No significant abnormalities were detected.

His stellar energy remains stable.

His physical manifestation is stable.

His teddy bear's cardiac activity is synchronized with Jake's known baseline.

No evidence of Nullwalker transformation was detected.

No evidence of dimensional contamination was detected.

No evidence of forced manifestation was detected.

The current assessment is:

> **JAKE IS ALIVE.**

---

# 12. WHERE HE WAS

Jake was asked where he had been.

He did not answer.

He shook his head.

C.O.R.E. did not press him.

This information is currently considered:

> **NOT REQUIRED FOR IMMEDIATE WELFARE.**

His location during the period of absence remains unknown.

His route to the Blacksite remains unknown.

His reason for returning remains unknown.

Those questions can wait.

Jake is here.

---

# 13. THE SKY

At 02:41, astronomical monitoring detected no corresponding stellar body.

The blue star has not reappeared.

This confirms a significant distinction.

Jake did not return by becoming the same visible star again.

The star is still absent.

Instead, Jake arrived as a physical manifestation.

Whether the celestial body still exists elsewhere remains unknown.

C.O.R.E. will not speculate publicly.

---

# 14. THE PERIMETER

The original impact site has been preserved temporarily.

Not as a containment zone.

Not as a research specimen.

Simply because several residents requested that it remain untouched for the morning.

A small depression in the soil remains.

There is no crater.

There is no radiation.

There is only a faint blue residue that is already fading.

Jake eventually looked at it.

Then looked toward SAFEHOUSE.

Bao held out her hand.

Jake took it.

The teddy bear remained tucked against his chest.

Together, they walked home.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

For months, the sky was the only place anyone could find Jake.

Then the sky became empty.

Everyone wondered whether he was gone.

Clover blamed herself.

Bao kept leaving food.

Nora kept looking at the cafeteria door.

Neri kept drawing little stars.

Jane kept pretending she wasn't watching the sky.

And the teddy bear kept beating.

Nobody knew whether any of it mattered.

Apparently, it did.

At 02:17 this morning, a shooting star came down.

It did not burn.

It did not explode.

It simply landed outside the Blacksite.

And there he was.

Small.

White-haired.

Blue-eyed.

Alive.

Holding nothing.

Until Bao gave him his bear back.

Jake hugged it.

The heartbeat continued.

For once, nobody asked where he had been.

Nobody asked why he left.

Nobody asked whether he was staying.

They just let him come inside.

Because after everything that happened, the most important thing was not understanding his disappearance.

It was finally getting one thing right.

**When Jake came home, they gave him his teddy bear back.**
