# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – ANOMALOUS PHENOMENA OBSERVATION**

### **SUBJECT: DIALYN – FLOATING HEAD ACCESSORIES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

An unusual but harmless phenomenon has been consistently observed around Dialyn.

Objects intended to be worn on her head frequently fail to make physical contact.

Instead...

They float.

The effect has been documented with hats, ribbons, decorative clips, flower crowns, and most notably, miniature crowns.

No known gravitational or telekinetic anomaly has been identified.

The phenomenon appears to affect only items Dialyn intentionally wears.

---

# **OBSERVATIONS**

When placed on Dialyn's head, qualifying accessories slowly rise several centimeters before stabilizing in midair.

The floating distance remains remarkably consistent.

Average height:

**2–5 centimeters above her hair.**

The objects remain perfectly aligned with her movements.

They do not wobble.

They do not fall.

Even while running or bending over, the accessories maintain their position.

Attempts to manually push them down are unsuccessful.

The object simply returns to its usual height once released.

---

# **MINIATURE CROWNS**

The phenomenon is most noticeable with small decorative crowns.

During office celebrations, coworkers occasionally place tiny crowns on Dialyn for birthdays, holidays, or simply because they think they suit her.

Without exception...

The crowns begin floating almost immediately.

The sight has become so familiar that nobody reacts anymore.

# **DIALYN'S RESPONSE**

When asked whether the floating accessories bothered her, Dialyn looked upward at the tiny crown hovering above her head.

She smiled.

> "...Not really."

Another pause.

> "...I've gotten used to it."

She adjusted a loose strand of hair.

The crown quietly followed her movements, maintaining the same distance.

> "...Besides..."

> "...It saves me from fixing my hair."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

No scientific explanation has been accepted for the phenomenon.

Research Division has officially classified it as a benign personal anomaly.

Employees have simply learned to expect it.

If someone places a flower crown, winter wreath, or tiny jeweled crown upon Dialyn's head...

They no longer wait for it to fall.

They wait for it to float.

And, as always...

It does.
