## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / POST-INCIDENT OBSERVATION LOG**

# **ORPHIE — LEFT BEHIND**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Bereavement  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Lumi / Arcturus / Norma / SAFEHOUSE Residents / C.O.R.E.  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **ALONE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following Corin's death, Orphie was left alone.

Not deliberately.

Not as punishment.

There was simply nothing anyone knew how to say to her anymore.

The family returned to the SAFEHOUSE.

The medical personnel returned to their duties.

The KCA continued operating.

The Marts returned to their colony.

Everyone had somewhere else to go.

Orphie did not.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

The treehouse became completely silent.

Corin's belongings remained where she had left them.

Her chair remained at the table.

Her room remained untouched.

The kitchen still contained the remnants of meals neither sister had finished.

Orphie did not move anything.

She simply remained there.

---

# **THE FIRST NIGHT**

Orphie slept on the floor.

The same place she had chosen when she began avoiding Corin.

She pulled a blanket over herself.

For a while, she stared at the opposite side of the room.

Then she whispered:

> "...I'm still here."

No response.

---

# **THE NEXT MORNING**

Nobody came.

Orphie waited.

She told herself someone would eventually visit.

Lumi had always worried about her.

Arcturus would normally check on her.

Norma would probably bring food.

Star would probably cry.

Someone would come.

Nobody did.

---

# **THE SAFEHOUSE**

The SAFEHOUSE had become strangely quiet.

Everyone was grieving.

Nobody knew how to approach Orphie.

Some were angry.

Some were heartbroken.

Some were afraid that seeing her would bring everything back.

So they stayed away.

Not forever.

Just for the moment.

But moments became hours.

Hours became days.

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts did not return.

They remembered Orphie's frightening behavior.

They remembered Corin.

They did not understand death.

They only understood that someone was gone.

Their colony remained together.

Orphie remained alone.

---

# **ORPHIE'S REALIZATION**

Eventually, Orphie understood.

Nobody was coming.

She looked around the treehouse.

> "...So this is it."

She laughed once.

There was no humor in it.

> "...I wanted everyone to leave me alone."

A pause.

> "...They did."

She looked at Corin's empty chair.

> "...Except you."

Silence.

> "...You never left."

Her voice became quieter.

> "...Until you did."

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi eventually visited.

She stood outside the treehouse for several minutes.

She could not bring herself to enter.

Not yet.

Inside, Orphie heard her.

> "...Mom?"

No answer.

Orphie walked toward the door.

By the time she opened it, Lumi had already left.

---

# **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus came once.

He stood beneath the treehouse.

He looked upward.

He wanted to say something.

Anything.

Instead, he turned around and walked away.

Orphie watched from the window.

She did not call after him.

---

# **NORMA**

Norma left a package outside.

Food.

Blankets.

Some basic supplies.

No note.

Orphie found it later.

She carried it inside.

She did not open it.

---

# **STAR**

Star wanted to visit.

Nightshade stopped her.

Not because Star didn't care.

Because Star was still grieving too deeply.

> "...Give her time."

Star cried.

> "...But she's alone."

Nightshade:

> "...I know."

---

# **ORPHIE**

For the first time, Orphie understood the irony.

She had spent months believing:

> **"Everyone would be better without me."**

Now everyone had left.

And it didn't feel like freedom.

It felt empty.

---

# **THE HOUSE**

Orphie eventually began moving through the rooms alone.

She saw the places where Corin had sat.

The table.

The kitchen.

The bedroom.

The window.

The floor.

Everything carried a memory.

She hated it.

She hated the silence.

She hated the empty chair.

She hated the house.

Most of all...

She hated that she was still there.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie once wanted to disappear.

She wanted everyone to stop caring.

She wanted the Marts to stay away.

She wanted Corin to leave her alone.

She wanted the SAFEHOUSE to forget her.

Now she had almost exactly what she wanted.

Everyone had gone.

Nobody was speaking to her.

Nobody was demanding that she get better.

Nobody was telling her they cared.

Nobody was asking her to come home.

Orphie sat alone in the treehouse.

She looked at the empty chair.

Then quietly said:

> **"...I didn't mean it."**

For the first time, there was nobody left to hear her.

**End of Post-Incident Observation Log**
