# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **CAFETERIA RESEARCH MEMO**

### **SUBJECT: DIALYN'S "GUESSWORK"**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the unexpected success of Dialyn's chicken and potato stew, Cafeteria Services began observing her cooking techniques more closely.

The findings were... unusual.

Despite never measuring ingredients, Dialyn consistently produces well-balanced meals.

Repeated testing suggests her "guesswork" is significantly more accurate than chance would predict.

---

# **OBSERVATIONS**

During meal preparation, Dialyn almost never uses:

- Measuring cups.
    
- Measuring spoons.
    
- Kitchen scales.
    
- Timers.
    

Instead, she estimates everything by eye.

When questioned, she generally replies:

> "...It looks about right."

or

> "...Enough."

Independent observations have shown that these estimates are remarkably consistent.

---

# **TEST RESULTS**

Over twenty supervised cooking sessions, Dialyn was asked to estimate ingredient quantities before they were measured.

Average deviations were as follows:

|Ingredient|Average Error|
|---|---|
|Flour|±4%|
|Rice|±3%|
|Water|±2%|
|Potatoes|±5%|
|Chicken|±4%|
|Cooking Oil|±3%|

The only consistent exception was **salt**.

Unable to taste it herself, Dialyn's estimates varied more than other ingredients.

However, even those estimates generally remained within acceptable culinary ranges.

Researchers believe years of cooking without the ability to rely on taste have forced Dialyn to develop an exceptional intuitive sense for ingredient proportions.

---

# **THEORY**

Cafeteria Services proposes that Dialyn cooks primarily through observation.

She watches:

- The color of the broth.
    
- Steam production.
    
- Ingredient density.
    
- Texture.
    
- Aroma.
    
- How ingredients move while simmering.
    

Rather than tasting throughout the process, she builds an internal prediction of the finished dish.

The method is difficult to teach but has proven highly reliable.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Although kitchen scales and measuring tools remain available for all personnel, they have become largely optional whenever Dialyn is cooking.

The only standing recommendation from Cafeteria Services is unchanged:

> **Someone else should taste the soup before it's served.**

Not because Dialyn is a poor cook.

But because, despite her remarkable intuition...

She still can't tell if it needs more salt.
