### RESIDENT WELFARE & MEDICAL DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-JAKE-036

**STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**SUBJECT:** Jake — Daily Observation  
**LOCATION:** ANOMALOUS GRASSLAND / MISSING ELEVATOR FLOOR

---

# 1. CURRENT VISITATION

The SAFEHOUSE residents now visit the grassland every day.

No formal schedule was established.

Nobody assigned shifts.

Nobody ordered them to return.

They simply began coming.

The missing elevator button remains inaccessible by conventional means.

However, when selected, it continues to provide access to the grassland.

The environment remains unchanged.

Soft sunlight.

A gentle breeze.

Birdsong.

Quiet music.

Endless grass.

And, at the center of the grassless circle:

**Jake.**

---

# 2. JAKE'S CURRENT STATE

Jake has not acknowledged the residents.

He does not greet them.

He does not look toward them.

He does not respond when his name is spoken.

He does not approach them.

He does not appear interested in their presence.

He simply sits.

His knees are pulled against his chest.

His arms are wrapped tightly around his legs.

His wings remain folded behind him.

His shattered halo remains suspended above his head.

The arrow remains embedded through his chest.

His azure eyes remain open.

Occasionally, tears form.

Occasionally, his shoulders move slightly.

He is quietly sobbing.

The sound is extremely faint.

Almost completely lost beneath the wind.

---

# 3. THE ENVIRONMENT

The grass moves gently around him.

Sunlight passes across the field.

The doodled flowers remain around the edge of the grassless circle.

They sway despite having no visible three-dimensional structure.

The music continues at the same volume.

Birds occasionally chirp.

Nothing in the environment appears hostile.

There is no storm.

No darkness.

No threat.

No dramatic distortion.

It is simply peaceful.

Perhaps too peaceful.

---

# 4. RESIDENT BEHAVIOR

The residents initially attempted conversation.

It did not work.

Bao called his name.

No response.

Neri sat nearby.

No response.

Nora spoke to him.

No response.

Jane apologized.

No response.

Twiggy attempted to explain herself.

No response.

Eventually, the residents stopped trying to force interaction.

They simply stayed.

---

# 5. BAO

Bao visits almost every day.

She usually sits several meters away.

She does not attempt to touch him.

She does not demand that he look at her.

She does not ask him to forgive her.

Sometimes she talks quietly.

Sometimes she says nothing.

One day:

> **BAO:**  
> "I'm here."

Jake did not react.

Bao remained seated.

After approximately forty minutes, she left.

The next day, she returned.

---

# 6. NERI

Neri generally brings a book.

She sits near the edge of the circle.

She reads.

She does not interrupt Jake's silence.

Occasionally she looks up.

Jake remains curled against himself.

One afternoon, Neri quietly placed a small drawing beside the edge of the circle.

She did not explain it.

She simply left it there.

The drawing depicted a small star.

---

# 7. NORA

Nora finds the visits more difficult.

She frequently sits farther away than everyone else.

She watches Jake.

Sometimes she cries.

Sometimes she talks despite receiving no response.

She tells him about ordinary things.

The weather.

Food.

What happened in the SAFEHOUSE.

Things she saw that day.

Nothing important.

Just things.

One afternoon:

> **NORA:**  
> "I had cake today."

Jake did not move.

> **NORA:**  
> "It was good."

She smiled weakly.

> **NORA:**  
> "...I wish you could've had some."

Jake continued staring downward.

Nora remained there anyway.

---

# 8. JANE DOE

Jane has stopped attempting to approach him.

She sits near the perimeter of the circle.

Sometimes she watches him.

Sometimes she watches the sky.

She has not asked him to forgive her.

She has not said:

> "It wasn't your fault."

She has not said:

> "We're sorry."

She understands that none of those statements require an answer.

One day, she simply said:

> **JANE:**  
> "I'll be here."

Then she stopped talking.

---

# 9. TWIGGY

Twiggy's visits are the quietest.

She often sits farther away than the others.

She does not know what to say.

She remembers everything.

Every word.

Every expression.

Every consequence.

She once spent nearly an hour sitting in the grass without saying anything.

Eventually:

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "...I don't expect you to forgive me."

Jake continued crying quietly.

Twiggy looked down.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "I just wanted you to know I remember."

No response.

She stayed until sunset.

---

# 10. THE FIRST SIGN

After several days, something changed.

Not Jake.

The environment.

A small blue particle drifted across the grass.

It passed between the residents.

Then disappeared.

No one reacted.

A second followed.

Then a third.

They resembled the Stardust found around the missing elevator button.

No source was identified.

Jake did not appear to notice.

---

# 11. NO PROGRESS

Despite daily visits, Jake has shown no observable change.

He still:

- remains seated;
    
- keeps his knees against his chest;
    
- keeps his arms wrapped around them;
    
- does not acknowledge the residents;
    
- does not speak;
    
- does not approach anyone;
    
- continues quietly sobbing.
    

The residents continue visiting anyway.

There is no evidence that the visits are helping.

There is also no evidence that they are hurting.

For now, they remain.

---

# 12. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Personnel are reminded that the grassland does not appear to operate according to ordinary psychological or temporal rules.

The residents should therefore avoid treating Jake's lack of response as a conventional rejection.

Likewise, they should not interpret his continued presence as an invitation.

The current approach is simple:

> **Be present.**

Do not demand.

Do not force.

Do not attempt to remove the arrow.

Do not attempt to physically move Jake.

Do not insist on forgiveness.

Do not expect immediate improvement.

Just remain nearby.

---

# CLOSING OBSERVATION

Every day, they come.

Every day, Jake remains where he is.

Knees against his chest.

Arms wrapped around his legs.

Wings folded.

Halo broken.

Azure eyes open.

The soft wind moves through his hair.

The sunlight rests gently across his face.

His tears disappear into the grass.

He does not look at them.

He does not speak to them.

He does not seem to care that they are there.

And yet—

They keep coming.

Not because he asks them to.

Not because they expect anything from him.

Not because they know how to fix what happened.

They simply sit in the grass.

Sometimes together.

Sometimes apart.

Sometimes speaking.

Mostly silent.

And Jake continues to sob softly beneath the warm sunlight.

The music keeps playing.

The birds keep singing.

The wind keeps moving.

And every morning, the elevator opens again.
