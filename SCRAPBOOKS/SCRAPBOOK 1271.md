# 1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Tachyon Industries has introduced its second **“Not Limited”** merchandise weapon:

> **SLUGGER**

Developed by Neri Tachyon using the **M1014 shotgun platform as its foundation**, the Slugger is a semi-automatic 12-gauge shotgun designed around reliability, modularity, and rapid follow-up fire.

Unlike several earlier Tachyon Industries products, the Slugger is not restricted to a predetermined production quantity.

It is intended to remain part of the regular Tachyon Industries merchandise lineup.

---

# 2. DESIGN

The Slugger retains the recognizable general configuration of its M1014-derived foundation while incorporating several Tachyon Industries modifications.

Notable features include:

- **Semi-automatic action**
    
- **Railed handguard**
    
- **Railed sight mounting system**
    
- **8-shell internal capacity**
    
- **+1 chambered capacity**
    
- **9-shell capacity when fully loaded**
    
- **Dedicated 9-shell ammunition rack**
    
- **12-gauge compatibility**
    
- **Magnesium Magnum ammunition compatibility**
    
- **Ceberus ammunition compatibility**
    

The resulting platform is considerably more modular than the standard foundation.

---

# 3. CAPACITY

The Slugger's standard ammunition capacity is:

> **8 + 1**

Eight shells are held in the shotgun's magazine system, with one additional round available in the chamber.

Tachyon Industries additionally supplies a:

> **9-SHELL RACK**

The rack provides convenient additional ammunition storage for the operator.

Neri's internal product documentation refers to the arrangement simply as:

> **“Eight in, one ready, nine nearby.”**

Neko has stated that this is unnecessarily obvious.

Neri has retained the wording.

---

# 4. AMMUNITION COMPATIBILITY

The Slugger supports standard 12-gauge ammunition.

More notably, it is compatible with the same fictional ammunition ecosystem used by the Tachyon Industries **Ceberus**.

This includes:

> **12 Gauge Magnesium Magnum**

The ammunition produces the characteristic magnesium-based effect associated with the Ceberus platform.

This makes the Slugger the second Tachyon Industries merchandise platform officially authorized to use the ammunition.

### C.O.R.E. NOTE

The Slugger is **not** a Ceberus replacement.

It is a separate platform with its own handling characteristics and intended role.

---

# 5. RELATION TO THE CEBERUS

The Ceberus remains one of Tachyon Industries' **Limited** merchandise products.

The Slugger does not replace it.

Instead, the Slugger provides a more accessible Tachyon Industries shotgun platform capable of sharing part of the Ceberus ammunition ecosystem.

This was intentional.

Neri's development notes contain the following:

> **“Ceberus is special.”**
> 
> **“Slugger should be something people can actually get.”**

This distinction is reflected in the production model.

### CEBERUS

**Limited**

### SLUGGER

**Not Limited**

# 6. PRODUCTION STATUS

The Slugger is the **second Not Limited Tachyon Industries merchandise weapon.**

The designation means:

- no fixed production cap;
    
- continued availability through Tachyon Industries;
    
- replacement units can be produced;
    
- standard merchandise authorization applies.
    

The first Not Limited product established the category.

The Slugger expands it.

# 10. C.O.R.E. OBSERVATION

The Slugger represents another step in Tachyon Industries' merchandise strategy.

Rather than making every product an extremely rare experimental item, Neri has begun producing more accessible platforms that retain Tachyon Industries' distinctive characteristics.

The Slugger is therefore unusual for what it **isn't**.

It isn't a one-off.

It isn't restricted to a handful of owners.

It isn't a special prototype.

It isn't locked behind an elaborate qualification process.

It is simply:

> **A Tachyon Industries shotgun.**

And unlike the Ceberus:

> **There will be more.**
