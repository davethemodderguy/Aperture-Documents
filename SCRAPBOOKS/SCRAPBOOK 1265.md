# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### RESIDENT WELFARE & MEDICAL DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-MED-4C-NORA-416  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — MEDICAL / RESIDENT WELFARE  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**SUBJECT:** Nora Tachyon — Persistent Cold State  
**RELATED INCIDENT:** Prolonged exterior exposure

---

# 1. CURRENT CONDITION

Nora remains extremely cold.

The previous assumption that she was simply experiencing residual cold exposure has been rejected.

Nora's temperature is not returning to normal.

She is **freezing from the outside inward.**

Her skin remains cold.

Frost continues to form along exposed areas.

Her hair occasionally develops small ice crystals.

Her fingers remain stiff and trembling.

Most importantly:

> **Nora feels cold all the time.**

Even after being indoors for an extended period.

Even beneath multiple blankets.

Even after warm drinks.

The sensation appears to have progressed beyond the original environmental exposure.

---

# 2. THE COLD

Nora's cold is not localized.

It begins at the surface.

Her skin is cold to touch.

Then the cold appears to persist deeper beneath the surface.

Medical scans indicate that her peripheral tissues remain abnormally cold despite attempts to warm her externally.

Her body is effectively maintaining the cold state.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

Nora's anomalous cold aura continues to resemble Frost's.

The comparison has become unavoidable.

There is, however, one important difference.

Frost is accustomed to her own temperature.

Nora is not.

---

# 3. HUGGING NORA

Nora has become noticeably more receptive to physical affection during the current episode.

Normally, Neri's preference for solitude and Nora's energetic personality make prolonged physical contact situational.

Currently, Nora actively seeks it.

When someone offers a hug:

Nora accepts.

When someone sits beside her:

Nora moves closer.

When someone wraps an arm around her:

Nora stays there.

She does not necessarily speak.

She simply holds on.

This appears to provide significant comfort.

### MEDICAL NOTE

A hug does **not** necessarily raise Nora's body temperature.

It does, however, appear to reduce distress associated with the cold.

For Nora, this distinction matters.

She may still be freezing.

She simply does not have to feel alone while she is freezing.

---

# 4. AETERNA

Aeterna initially became Nora's primary source of physical comfort.

This was reasonable.

Aeterna is large.

Aeterna is stable.

Aeterna is strong enough to hold Nora securely.

Aeterna is also extremely protective.

There is only one problem.

### AETERNA IS COLD.

Aeterna is not a warm-bodied organism.

Her physical form is manifested armor.

There is no conventional biological heat source beneath it.

Her white unknown-metal exterior is approximately as cold as the surrounding anomalous environment.

In practical terms:

> **Nora is freezing.**

> **Aeterna is also freezing.**

And yet Nora still wants to be held by her.

---

# 5. OBSERVED INTERACTION

Nora was sitting beneath three blankets.

Aeterna sat beside her.

Nora slowly reached toward Aeterna.

Aeterna opened one arm.

Nora immediately crawled into the space beneath it.

She pressed herself against Aeterna's chest plate.

There was a pause.

Nora shivered.

Aeterna looked down.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “I am not warm.”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “I know.”

Aeterna paused.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “I am cold too.”

Nora nodded again.

She hugged her anyway.

> **NORA:**  
> “Still nice.”

Aeterna said nothing.

She simply held her more securely.

---

# 6. BAO'S SOLUTION

Bao eventually noticed that Nora was essentially hugging a very large refrigerator.

She did not comment on this.

She simply added another blanket.

Then she placed a warm drink into Nora's hands.

> **BAO:**  
> “Here.”

Nora looked at the cup.

> **NORA:**  
> “Hot chocolate?”

> **BAO:**  
> “Yes.”

Nora took a sip.

She remained pressed against Aeterna.

Bao looked at the two of them.

Then at the frost forming around Aeterna's armor.

Then back at Nora.

> **BAO:**  
> “At least you're both consistent.”

---

# 7. FROST

Frost's involvement has been somewhat unusual.

Unlike everyone else, Frost is not particularly bothered by the surrounding temperature.

She can sit beside Nora without discomfort.

She can hold Nora's hand without reacting to the cold.

She can even recognize subtle changes in the cold field that other residents cannot.

Frost therefore provides an additional form of reassurance.

Not warmth.

**Understanding.**

She knows what it feels like to be naturally cold.

She knows what it is like when everyone else notices the temperature before you do.

And she knows that sometimes the best response is simply:

> “I'm here.”

---

# 8. NORA'S PREFERENCE

Personnel observations indicate the following preference order:

**1. Hug**

**2. Blanket**

**3. Hot chocolate**

**4. Sitting near someone**

**5. Everything else**

This should not be interpreted as a medical treatment hierarchy.

It is simply what Nora appears to appreciate most.

When someone asks:

> “Do you want anything?”

Nora may shake her head.

If they instead ask:

> “Can I hug you?”

She usually nods.

---

# 9. MEDICAL GUIDANCE

Nora's persistent cold requires continued medical monitoring.

Physical affection should **not** replace medical treatment.

However, it should also not be withheld merely because it does not directly increase her temperature.

Nora's current condition is physically uncomfortable and frightening.

Comfort matters.

Personnel should therefore provide:

- controlled environmental warmth;
    
- blankets;
    
- appropriate warm fluids;
    
- medical monitoring;
    
- physical contact when Nora wants it;
    
- and continuous observation.
    

Aeterna may continue holding Nora.

However:

> **Aeterna should not be considered a heat source.**

Her manifested body is armor.

The armor is cold.

Nora knows this.

She simply does not care.

---

# 10. CURRENT OBSERVATION

Nora is currently sitting between Bao and Aeterna.

A blanket covers most of her body.

A cup of hot chocolate rests between her hands.

Frost is sitting nearby.

Nora is still shivering.

Aeterna's armor is still cold.

The room remains warmer than both of them.

None of this has stopped Nora from holding onto Aeterna's arm.

After several minutes, Nora quietly speaks.

> **NORA:**  
> “Can I stay like this?”

Aeterna looks down at her.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “For as long as you wish.”

Nora nods.

She holds tighter.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Nora is cold.

Not metaphorically.

Not mildly.

She is **cold enough that frost forms on her skin.**

The cold has persisted long enough that it now appears to be progressing inward rather than simply remaining an external consequence of exposure.

And somehow, the thing Nora appreciates most is still remarkably simple.

Someone sitting beside her.

A blanket.

Something warm to drink.

And a hug.

Even when the person hugging her is an ancient suit of armor that is **just as cold as she is.**

Aeterna cannot make Nora warm.

She can make sure Nora does not have to be cold alone.

For tonight, that appears to be enough.
