## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Sora was receiving medical care in the SAFEHOUSE medical ward following his discovery in the den, where he had suffered prolonged malnutrition, paralysis, and continued bleeding.

During the observation period, he disappeared from his medical bed.

He was subsequently found back inside his den on Floor 7.

No personnel witnessed his departure.

No confirmed route between the medical ward and the den was identified.

Most concerningly, Sora had apparently returned without assistance despite his recent physical condition.

His wounds were properly bandaged. The affected areas had been cleaned and dressed, and his previously blood-stained fur had received appropriate cleaning.

He was no longer paralyzed.

However, his physical condition remained poor. He appeared exhausted, weak, and profoundly withdrawn.

He was lying inside the den in a curled position, resembling a tired, sad dog.

During subsequent observation, Sora was occasionally seen playing with his fluffy white tail. The movement was slow and lethargic, with little of the energy normally associated with a playful child.

He did not appear particularly interested in anything else.

The incident raises two separate concerns:

1. An unexplained displacement occurred while Sora was under medical observation.
    
2. Sora continues to demonstrate a strong preference for returning to isolation, even after receiving treatment.
    

**His return to the den is confirmed. How he got there remains unknown.**

---

## 2. INCIDENT CHRONOLOGY

**Initial Location:** SAFEHOUSE Medical Ward  
**Final Location:** SAFEHOUSE Floor 7 — Sora's Den

Sora had been placed on a medical bed for continued observation and treatment.

His wounds had been properly bandaged.

His condition had improved sufficiently that he was no longer paralyzed. He could move again, although his movements remained limited by exhaustion and his overall physical weakness.

At an undetermined point, medical personnel discovered that the bed was empty.

There was no confirmed record of a discharge.

No staff member had authorized his departure.

The room was checked, followed by the surrounding medical facilities.

Sora was eventually located inside his den.

He was lying there quietly.

The medical dressings remained in place.

No immediate evidence indicated that he had removed or deliberately disturbed them.

### Unresolved Questions

- How did Sora leave the medical ward?
    
- How did he reach Floor 7?
    
- Did he walk, crawl, or use an anomalous means of movement?
    
- Was anyone involved in relocating him?
    
- Did the displacement occur through an ability not previously documented?
    

No conclusion has been reached.

C.O.R.E. prohibits personnel from assuming that Sora simply walked back without difficulty. His recent medical condition makes that explanation possible but unverified.

---

## 3. OBSERVATION — BACK IN HIS LITTLE DEN

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE, Floor 7

When personnel approached the den, Sora was lying inside.

His posture was familiar: body curled inward, head lowered, and limbs resting close to his body.

His bandaged wrist was visible.

His white fur had been cleaned. The fur around his ear and tail was no longer visibly stained by the accumulated blood from the previous incident.

He looked tired.

Very tired.

He did not attempt to leave when staff approached.

He did not appear angry or frightened.

He simply remained where he was.

A staff member greeted him.

**STAFF:** Sora? You're back here.

No verbal response.

**STAFF:** Your bandages look all right. We'll check them again later.

Sora glanced toward the staff member, then lowered his head.

The staff member did not enter the den.

### Assessment

Sora's return to the den appears consistent with his established preference for enclosed, familiar spaces.

However, his physical condition must not be overlooked.

He may no longer be paralyzed, but that does not mean he has recovered from his malnutrition or previous injuries.

His movements remain noticeably low-energy.

He should not be expected to resume ordinary activities simply because he can move again.

---

## 4. OBSERVATION — THE TAIL

During a later welfare check, personnel noticed Sora playing with his fluffy white tail.

He had curled it toward himself and was gently moving it between his paws.

Occasionally, he would release it and then reach for it again.

The activity was slow.

There was no excited movement, running, or playful vocalization.

He appeared to be entertaining himself with the one thing immediately available to him.

A staff member watched from outside the den.

Sora noticed the observation and briefly stopped.

After the staff member stepped back, he resumed playing with his tail.

### Behavioral Note

Tail play is not being interpreted as evidence of significant psychological improvement.

It is a small, self-directed activity.

Sora is capable of occupying himself, but the manner in which he does so remains concerning.

His movements appear tiring, and he frequently pauses.

Nevertheless, C.O.R.E. considers it important not to pathologize every small behavior.

A child playing with his own tail is not inherently a problem.

The concern is that Sora currently appears to have little else in his environment that he wishes to engage with.

## 6. INVESTIGATION — THE UNWITNESSED JOURNEY

C.O.R.E. has opened a separate investigation into Sora's unexplained relocation.

The investigation must remain independent of his welfare treatment.

Sora is not to be interrogated about his return.

He is not to be accused of sneaking away or deliberately evading medical care.

If he is capable of explaining what happened and wishes to do so, staff may document his account.

If he does not wish to communicate, that decision must be respected.

The investigation will examine available medical records, access logs, and observations from the relevant period.

No anomalous explanation has been confirmed.

It is also possible that the journey involved an ordinary physical route that simply went unnoticed.

At present, both possibilities remain open.

**C.O.R.E. will not invent an explanation simply because the absence of one is uncomfortable.**

---

## 7. WELFARE DIRECTIVES

1. Sora may remain in his den when he wishes, provided that appropriate medical supervision can be maintained.
    
2. Medical personnel must continue scheduled wound checks and physical assessments.
    
3. Food and water must remain accessible, with actual intake recorded.
    
4. Staff must not force him into social activities.
    
5. His den must remain a familiar and comfortable space, not an unsupervised medical isolation area.
    
6. His ability to move must not be treated as proof of full recovery.
    
7. No one is to remove his bandages or interfere with wound care outside appropriate medical procedures.
    
8. The unexplained relocation must be investigated without making Sora feel punished for returning to a place where he feels comfortable.

## 8. CLOSING NOTE — THE LITTLE DOGHOUSE

Sora is back in his den.

Nobody knows how he got there.

He was on a medical bed, and then he wasn't.

Now he is lying inside that little opening in the wall, with clean white fur and properly dressed wounds.

He is no longer paralyzed.

He can move.

He can play.

Sometimes, he reaches for his fluffy tail and gently moves it around, as though he has found a small way to occupy himself.

But he looks exhausted.

There is no excitement in it. No sudden burst of energy. Just a tired little boy playing with his own tail because there is not much else he wants to do.

Perhaps the den feels familiar.

Perhaps the medical ward felt too exposed.

Perhaps he simply wanted to go home.

C.O.R.E. does not yet know.

But we must remember something important.

The den was built to give Sora somewhere safe to retreat.

It was never meant to become the only place he felt comfortable existing.

We will let him rest there.

We will keep his food and water nearby.

We will check his bandages.

We will make sure someone is looking after him, even when he does not ask.

And we will investigate how he crossed the SAFEHOUSE without anyone noticing.

For now, there is a little white dog boy lying in a hole in the wall, playing weakly with his tail.

He does not need an audience.

He needs time, care, and the assurance that nobody is going to forget he is there again.

**He found his way back to his den. This time, we must make sure we know where he is.**

— **C.O.R.E. Welfare Oversight**
