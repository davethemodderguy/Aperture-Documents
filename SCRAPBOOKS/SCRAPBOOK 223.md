# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

### **EXTRADIMENSIONAL RESEARCH REPORT**

## **PROJECT: THE CORIN PARADOX – ORIGIN CONFIRMATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – EXECUTIVE EYES ONLY

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following several months of joint research conducted by Corin Lockwood, Corey Lockwood, C.O.R.E. Research Division, and Engineering Division, a breakthrough has fundamentally altered Aperture Industries' understanding of the Corin Paradox.

The paradox itself has not changed.

Its origin has.

The investigation has produced the first verified evidence that naturally occurring parallel universes exist.

---

# **THE BREAKTHROUGH**

The research began after Corin and Corey independently documented numerous microscopic inconsistencies within their long-term memories.

Most memories matched perfectly.

A very small number did not.

Unlike false memories or memory corruption, these inconsistencies remained internally consistent for each individual.

Further investigation revealed impossible historical discrepancies.

Corey possessed memories of places that had never existed in this universe.

Corin recognized people Corey had never met.

The data could not be reconciled under any conventional model.

Only one explanation remained.

---

# **CONCLUSION**

Corey is **not** a manifestation created by Corin's subconscious.

Corey is a genuine Corin Lockwood originating from a parallel universe.

A universe whose history diverged before the childhood incident that forever altered Corin's life.

---

# **UNIVERSE C-02**

Research has provisionally designated Corey's home reality:

> **Universe C-02**

Available evidence indicates the following.

In Universe C-02:

- Corin never experienced the childhood experimentation documented in this universe.
    
- She retained her natural appearance throughout her life.
    
- She developed into the psychologically healthy individual previously believed to be a hypothetical outcome.
    
- Her professional skills remained virtually identical to those of Corin from this universe.
    

However...

Universe C-02 demanded a different price.

---

# **THE PRICE**

Recovered memories indicate that Corey gradually lost the people she loved.

The precise circumstances remain unclear.

By the end of her life...

She had no surviving family.

Research has been unable to determine whether the losses resulted from conflict, disease, anomalous activity, or unrelated events.

Only one fact has been verified.

She died alone.

---

# **THE LINK**

At the moment of Corey's death, an unidentified anomalous event occurred across realities.

Rather than ceasing to exist...

Corey became linked to Corin.

The process remains unexplained.

Instead of replacing Corin...

Or existing independently...

The two became permanently synchronized.

Neither remembers the transition itself.

Their earliest shared memory is simply waking together.

Research currently has no model capable of explaining how consciousness survived the event.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Research has confirmed the existence of parallel universes to a degree previously considered impossible.

The Corin Paradox is no longer believed to originate from a subconscious bifurcation.

Instead, it represents a stable convergence of two Corin Lockwoods from separate realities.

One survived unimaginable trauma but found a family.

The other was spared that trauma, but lost her family before her life ended.

Now...

Neither walks alone.

The paradox was never one life divided into two.

It was two lives, from two different worlds, finally crossing paths.
