**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Medical Reference

# **POST-TREATMENT FOLLOW-UP**

Following the successful elimination of **Ether Regression Syndrome (ERS)**, Senior Manager Norma Lockwood continues to attend routine medical evaluations.

While the disease itself is no longer present, Medical Services has documented occasional episodes of **residual pain**.

These episodes are believed to be lingering aftereffects of the fifteen years Norma lived with ERS rather than evidence of the disease returning.

Current examinations show no signs of active Ether instability.

---

# **RESIDUAL PAIN**

The discomfort is:

- Mild to moderate in intensity.
    
- Random in occurrence.
    
- Short in duration.
    
- Not associated with active Ether Regression Syndrome.
    

The episodes can interrupt Norma's concentration, but she remains fully capable of continuing her duties once the discomfort subsides.

---

# **UNUSUAL OBSERVATION**

During routine follow-up, Medical staff noted an unexpected and repeatedly observed phenomenon.

Warm beverages—particularly those containing milk—consistently reduced Norma's discomfort.

The most effective drinks reported were:

- Warm milk.
    
- Hot tea.
    
- Milk tea.
    

Norma herself was the first to notice the pattern after treatment.

Subsequent observations by Medical confirmed that she appeared noticeably more comfortable after drinking them.

The exact physiological or Ether-related mechanism remains unknown.

Researchers have jokingly labeled it:

> **"The Tea Effect."**

Despite the humorous nickname, the observation has been reproduced often enough to be included in Norma's medical recommendations.

---

# **RECOMMENDED COMFORT MEASURES**

If Norma experiences a residual pain episode, Medical recommends:

- Allowing her to sit and rest briefly.
    
- Providing a warm drink if available.
    
- Monitoring the episode if it persists unusually long.
    

These measures are intended for comfort and are not considered emergency treatment.
