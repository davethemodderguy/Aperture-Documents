## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL OBSERVATION LOG**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – EMOTIONAL REGULATION METHOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following Corin Lockwood's relocation to Heaven's Blacksite, Medical Services observed an unusual but highly effective coping behavior.

When experiencing emotional distress, intrusive memories, or overwhelming feelings of loneliness...

Corin seeks out Belle Merryweather.

Not for conversation.

Not for advice.

Not for explanations.

Simply...

A hug.

---

# **BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

Among Blacksite personnel, Belle has acquired many unofficial titles.

The most common:

> **"The Blacksite's Sentient Bunny Plushie."**

The nickname originated from her unusual combination of:

- Rabbit Thiren traits.
    
- Constant warmth.
    
- Extremely approachable personality.
    
- Willingness to comfort almost anyone.
    

Despite possessing combat abilities far beyond normal personnel expectations, Belle is widely considered one of the safest people in the facility to approach when someone needs emotional support.

---

# **CORIN'S METHOD**

When Corin feels overwhelmed, she rarely explains what is wrong.

She does not say:

> "I'm struggling."

She does not say:

> "I need help."

Instead, she quietly appears near Belle.

At first, Belle did not understand.

Then Corin simply asked:

> "...Can I?"

Belle immediately understood.

The answer has always been:

> "Of course."

---

# **OBSERVATION**

The effect is immediate.

Corin's posture relaxes.

Her breathing slows.

Her thoughts become quieter.

For a short period of time, she is not thinking about:

- The experiment.
    
- The lost years.
    
- The Ether.
    
- The memories she cannot recover.
    
- The fear that she is no longer herself.
    

She is simply holding someone who reminds her:

She is still alive.

She is still cared for.

She is still part of a family.

---

# **BELLE'S RESPONSE**

When asked why she allows Corin to use her as an emotional support method, Belle answered:

> "Because everyone needs someone."

After a pause, she added:

> "Corin spent too long without anyone."

---

# **LUMI'S OBSERVATION**

Lumi initially worried that Corin was avoiding deeper conversations.

However, after observing the interactions, she changed her opinion.

The hugs were not replacing recovery.

They were helping Corin endure it.

Lumi later commented:

> "When she was little, I used to hold her when she was scared."

> "Now someone else is doing the same."

She smiled.

> "I'm grateful."

Director Lucious reviewed the observation.

His note was short.

> "Not every form of treatment requires words."

He continued:

> "Sometimes recovery begins with remembering that someone is willing to stay."

---

# **STATUS**

Corin continues receiving professional support and family assistance.

However, one of her most reliable grounding methods remains surprisingly simple.

When the memories become too heavy.

When the loneliness becomes too loud.

When she begins questioning whether she belongs...

She finds Belle.

And the Blacksite's "sentient bunny plushie" does what she has always done.

She holds on.
