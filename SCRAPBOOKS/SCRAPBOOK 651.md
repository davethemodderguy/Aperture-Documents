# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / POST-RECOVERY INCIDENT**

# **SHE WAS STILL WAITING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **CRITICAL PSYCHOLOGICAL / CARDIAC INSTABILITY**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin is physically present.

Orphie is physically present.

The Treehouse is occupied.

The food is stocked.

The water is available.

The bed is warm.

Nothing is missing.

And yet Corin continues to sit beneath the tree.

Under the snow.

Her wings wrapped around herself.

Trembling.

Waiting.

---

# **WAITING FOR WHAT?**

That question has become increasingly difficult to answer.

Orphie is here.

Lumi is here.

The SAFEHOUSE is here.

The door is open.

There is no reason for Corin to continue waiting.

Yet she does.

She sits in the exact posture she developed during her disappearance.

Wings around herself.

Head lowered.

Body trembling.

Eyes fixed somewhere beyond the snow.

Waiting.

For what?

Nobody knows.

Perhaps Corin doesn't know either.

---

# **"CORIN IS NOT CORIN."**

This statement has appeared repeatedly in recent psychological assessments.

The person sitting beneath the tree possesses Corin's memories.

Corin's body.

Corin's wings.

Corin's voice.

Corin's mannerisms.

But much of the person everyone knew is absent.

The curious girl.

The playful girl.

The girl who flew.

The girl who filled notebooks with terrible drawings.

The girl who loved being placed inside jars.

The girl who laughed at her own feathers.

She is still somewhere inside.

But she rarely comes forward.

---

# **THE CONVERSATION**

The incident occurred beneath the Treehouse.

Orphie approached Corin.

Corin did not look up.

Her wings were wrapped around her.

Orphie sat beside her.

For several minutes, neither spoke.

Eventually, Corin asked:

> **"Do you hate me?"**

Orphie immediately responded:

> **"No. Why would you think so?"**

Corin remained silent.

Then:

> **"Are you lying?"**

Orphie hesitated.

> **"Uhh... what?"**

Corin looked at her.

Her expression changed.

> **"Just as I thought."**

---

# **THE ARGUMENT**

The conversation escalated.

Orphie attempted to explain herself.

Corin rejected the explanations.

Every reassurance was met with another question.

Every answer was examined.

Every hesitation became evidence.

Every pause became suspicion.

The argument was not really about hatred.

It was about trust.

Corin wanted certainty.

Orphie could not provide the kind of certainty Corin demanded.

Eventually, Corin said something that caused Orphie to stop speaking.

> **"You don't mean it."**

Orphie tried to respond.

Corin turned away.

> **"Nobody means it."**

---

# **THE BREAK**

Corin's expression changed almost immediately.

Her anger disappeared.

So did her suspicion.

What remained was hurt.

Her eyes filled with tears.

Her wings closed tighter around her body.

She began trembling harder.

Then she whispered:

> **"...I knew it."**

Orphie reached toward her.

Corin recoiled.

Not violently.

Just enough.

That movement devastated Orphie.

---

# **HEARTBREAK**

Corin's emotional response was severe.

Her breathing became irregular.

She clutched the front of her clothing.

Her body shook uncontrollably.

Then her cardiac monitors began registering abnormalities.

Corin's heartbeat became irregular.

Weak.

Uneven.

Her two hearts no longer appeared to synchronize properly.

Medical personnel were immediately called.

---

# **CARDIAC INSTABILITY**

Corin's anomalous physiology has previously demonstrated an unusually strong connection between emotional state and bodily function.

The current episode represents the most severe manifestation observed since her recovery.

Her heart rhythm became unstable following the argument.

She did not appear physically injured.

There was no external trauma.

The injury was emotional.

And her body reacted as though it were physical.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie immediately realized what had happened.

She stayed beside Corin.

She did not argue anymore.

She did not attempt to explain.

She simply said:

> **"I'm here."**

Corin did not respond.

Orphie repeated it.

> **"I'm here."**

Again.

> **"I'm not leaving."**

---

# **THE WINGS**

Corin's wings tightened around herself.

Then slowly loosened.

One wing remained between her and Orphie.

The other rested against the snow.

Neither moved much.

The feathers remained dormant.

No glow.

No fire.

No anomalous response.

Just wings.

---

# **THE HEARTS**

Corin's cardiac condition stabilized eventually.

But not completely.

Her heartbeat remains irregular.

Medical personnel have not identified a purely physical cause.

The working hypothesis is that prolonged trauma has permanently altered how Corin's anomalous physiology responds to emotional distress.

The heart may recover.

But the psychological trigger remains.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie is here.

That is the cruelest part.

Corin spent a year waiting for her.

She searched the mountains.

She waited beneath the snow.

She survived without food or water.

She kept a drawing of Orphie.

She eventually found her again.

And yet some part of Corin is still sitting beneath that tree.

Still waiting.

Still expecting Orphie to disappear.

Still expecting the answer to be:

> **"Yes."**

So when Orphie said:

> **"No."**

Corin couldn't believe her.

The argument broke whatever fragile trust had begun forming.

And the consequences were immediate.

Her heart began failing to beat properly.

Not because Orphie actually hated her.

Not because Orphie abandoned her.

But because Corin's mind could no longer distinguish the present from the year she spent waiting.

Orphie is here.

Corin knows this.

And somehow...

she is still waiting.

**End of Corin / Post-Recovery Incident**
