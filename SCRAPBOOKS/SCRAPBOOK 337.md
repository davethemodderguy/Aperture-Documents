# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **SECURITY & ANOMALOUS RESEARCH DIVISION**

### **SECURITY ASSESSMENT MEMORANDUM**

# **KCA VAULT – IDENTIFIED VULNERABILITY ASSESSMENT**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following routine security reviews, the KCA Vault has been evaluated against conventional, technological, and anomalous threats.

The assessment concludes that the vault remains resistant to all tested forms of unauthorized entry.

One theoretical vulnerability has nevertheless been identified.

Its designation is:

> **Z-335 (Marts).**

Not because they are capable of defeating the vault's security.

But because they are among the very few entities routinely permitted inside.

---

# **THEORETICAL SCENARIO**

Researchers proposed the following question:

> **"What if a Mart decided to steal from the vault?"**

The question prompted a formal behavioral review.

After examining several years of observations from both the Heaven Blacksite and the KCA, C.O.R.E. reached a unanimous conclusion.

---

# **BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS**

Marts have demonstrated:

- Altruism.
    
- Empathy.
    
- Cooperation.
    
- Emotional support.
    
- Protective instincts.
    
- Curiosity.
    
- Harmless pranking.
    

They have never demonstrated:

- Greed.
    
- Malice.
    
- Revenge.
    
- Cruelty.
    
- Personal ambition.
    
- Intentional theft.
    

Even when accidentally locked inside the vault, the Marts:

- Left cash untouched.
    
- Left precious metals untouched.
    
- Left confidential materials untouched.
    
- Waited patiently to be let out.
    

One Mart reportedly used a stack of bundled paperwork as a chair.

After leaving the vault, it carefully put the paperwork back exactly where it had found it.

---

# **CAN MARTS BECOME EVIL?**

This question has become a recurring topic among KCA personnel.

Current evidence suggests:

> **No.**

Lumi created the Marts with the unintended consequence of giving rise to a species fundamentally oriented toward helping others.

Their instincts consistently favor:

- Kindness.
    
- Friendship.
    
- Cooperation.
    
- Making others happy.
    

Researchers have found no evidence that individual Marts possess the psychological framework required for malicious intent.

---

# **SELF-CONSCIOUSNESS**

While Marts are intelligent enough to:

- Learn.
    
- Plan.
    
- Communicate.
    
- Coordinate.
    
- Solve simple problems.
    

They do not appear to possess a strong sense of individual ego or self-importance.

Their colony functions through a shared social awareness rather than individual ambition.

As one researcher summarized:

> "A Mart rarely asks, 'What do _I_ want?'"

> "It usually asks, 'Who needs help?'"

---

# **ETHICAL REVIEW**

The Ethics Committee was consulted regarding hypothetical attempts to weaponize or manipulate Mart behavior.

Their conclusion was brief.

> "Do not."

The committee noted that deliberately attempting to corrupt or exploit a species whose defining characteristics are compassion and trust would constitute a severe ethical violation.

---

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

When asked whether she believed one of her creations could become evil, Lumi thought quietly before answering.

> "...I don't think so."

She looked toward a nearby Mart helping someone pick up dropped papers.

> "...I didn't create soldiers."

> "...I accidentally created little helpers."

She smiled.

> "...I hope they always stay that way."

---

# **DIALYN'S COMMENT**

> "...Our biggest vault vulnerability..."

She looked through the observation window at two Marts quietly dusting a shelf inside the vault after maintenance.

> "...Is that they're allowed inside."

She paused.

> "...The funny thing is..."

> "...They're probably the last things I'd ever worry about stealing from us."

---

# **BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

> "...Trust isn't just something they give."

> "...It's what they are."

---

# **CAMERA MART**

Camera Mart was asked directly:

> "...Would you ever steal from the vault?"

It appeared genuinely confused.

After several seconds, it replied:

> "...Why?"

No further explanation was offered.

---

# **FINAL ASSESSMENT**

From a purely technical perspective, Z-335 entities represent the greatest internal access vulnerability to the KCA vault.

From a practical perspective, they are also among its most dependable protectors.

The contradiction is resolved by one simple observation:

The Marts have never expressed any desire to possess what they guard.

If presented with a choice between a stack of gold bars and a sugary cheese cracker...

Every recorded Mart has chosen the cheese cracker.

Without hesitation.

---

### **Researcher's Closing Note**

One researcher left a handwritten comment at the end of the report.

> _"Sometimes the strongest security isn't a stronger lock."_

> _"Sometimes it's knowing that the ones inside have no reason to open the door."_

**End of Security Assessment Memorandum**
