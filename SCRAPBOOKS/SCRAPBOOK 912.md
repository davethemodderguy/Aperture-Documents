# C.O.R.E. ANOMALOUS INCIDENT REPORT

**INCIDENT ID:** NERI-GL-02  
**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**ASSOCIATED ENTITY:** Guiding Light  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — INTERNAL CORRIDOR  
**CLASSIFICATION:** ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / ARCHITECT FRAGMENT  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE INVESTIGATION  
**RESTRICTION:** C.O.R.E. PERSONNEL

---

# 1. Incident Summary

At approximately [REDACTED], Neri and Alice were walking together through an internal SAFEHOUSE corridor.

Neither individual was engaged in combat.

Neither was expecting an incident.

The incident began when a **Nekonium Blast Door** unexpectedly detached from its mounting assembly.

The door fell directly toward them.

### Estimated mass:

**500 kg**

Had the door struck either Neri or Alice directly, the resulting impact would likely have been fatal.

Alice had insufficient time to react.

Neri did.

---

# 2. The Catch

Neri caught the falling blast door with one hand.

No visible strain was observed.

No significant displacement of the floor was recorded.

No anomalous energy discharge was initially detected.

Alice reportedly stared at Neri.

Neri reportedly stared at the door.

Then something considerably stranger happened.

The entire Nekonium blast door began to break apart.

Not into fragments.

Not into dust.

Not through an explosion.

The material progressively transformed into extremely fine **sand-like particles**.

The particles fell harmlessly to the floor.

They did not float.

They did not disperse through the corridor.

They simply accumulated on the ground as though gravity still applied normally to the transformed material.

The 500 kg blast door was therefore no longer a door.

It was now approximately 500 kg of **Nekonium particulate material.**

---

# 3. Initial Assumption

The first assumption was that Neri had somehow destroyed the door.

This was incorrect.

Material analysis demonstrated that the resulting substance retained characteristics consistent with Nekonium.

Neri had not simply broken the door apart.

She had altered its **physical structure.**

The incident prompted an immediate review of the Architect-fragment exposure documented previously.

The possibility emerged that Neri had acquired an ability associated with Guiding Light.

---

# 4. Controlled Test

Neri was subsequently asked to demonstrate what she had done.

A solid Nekonium cube was provided under controlled laboratory conditions.

Neri placed her hand against it.

The cube changed state.

It became **liquid Nekonium.**

Importantly, the material was not molten.

Its temperature did not increase to the levels normally required to melt Nekonium.

No conventional thermal phase transition was observed.

The Nekonium simply existed as a liquid.

### Laboratory Result

**SOLID NEKONIUM**

↓

**Neri's manipulation**

↓

**LIQUID NEKONIUM**

No significant thermal increase.

No conventional melting process.

No apparent chemical decomposition.

The substance remained Nekonium.

Its physical state had simply been changed.

---

# 5. Molecular Restructuring

Further testing established the current working theory:

> **Neri can alter the molecular structure and physical configuration of objects and materials through direct anomalous manipulation.**

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

Whether Neri is directly manipulating molecules, intermolecular forces, atomic arrangements, or some deeper property of matter has not yet been established.

What matters is the result.

Neri can look at a material and effectively tell it:

**"Be different."**

And apparently, it listens.

---

# 6. Demonstrated Applications

Neri quickly discovered that the ability was not restricted to turning solid objects into particulate material.

Several extremely unusual samples were produced during subsequent supervised experimentation.

### 6.1 Unradioactive Uranium

Neri successfully produced uranium exhibiting no detectable radioactive emissions.

The sample remained chemically identifiable as uranium.

The anomalous property appeared to concern its nuclear/radioactive behavior rather than simply removing the material entirely.

This immediately caused approximately [REDACTED] researchers to reconsider several fundamental assumptions about nuclear stability.

Fortunately, most SAFEHOUSE residents possess significant resistance or immunity to radiation.

This did not stop Neko from asking Neri to **please stop casually rewriting nuclear physics.**

---

### 6.2 Liquid Nekonium

Neri repeatedly demonstrated the ability to produce Nekonium in a liquid state without melting it.

The material:

- remained chemically consistent with Nekonium,
    
- remained at ordinary laboratory temperatures,
    
- displayed liquid behavior,
    
- and did not require conventional heating.
    

Neko described the sample as:

> "Technically impossible."

Neri responded:

> "Works though."

Neko:

> "...Unfortunately."

---

### 6.3 Mercury Bar

Neri also produced a **solid bar of mercury.**

This was arguably less alarming than the uranium.

It was nevertheless scientifically absurd.

Mercury retained its identity as mercury while behaving as a rigid solid under ordinary laboratory conditions.

Neri appeared particularly pleased with this demonstration.

---

# 7. Current Limitations

One significant limitation has been identified.

### Neri cannot currently transform matter into a gas.

She can manipulate materials into unusual solid and liquid configurations.

She has demonstrated:

- solid → particulate
    
- solid → liquid
    
- radioactive → non-radioactive
    
- normally liquid → solid
    

But no successful **gas-state transformation** has been recorded.

Attempts to produce gaseous matter have failed.

Whether this represents a genuine limitation of the ability or simply a limitation of Neri's current understanding remains unknown.

---

# 8. No Observable Upper Limit

More concerningly, C.O.R.E. has not yet identified a meaningful upper limit to the ability.

Neri has demonstrated manipulation of:

- ordinary materials,
    
- specialized materials,
    
- anomalous materials,
    
- radioactive elements,
    
- and Nekonium.
    

No obvious exhaustion has been observed.

No visible cooldown period has been observed.

No measurable loss of concentration has been established during the current tests.

At present:

**Maximum range: UNKNOWN**

**Maximum mass: UNKNOWN**

**Maximum complexity: UNKNOWN**

**Maximum duration: UNKNOWN**

**Maximum number of simultaneous alterations: UNKNOWN**

This is not considered evidence that the ability is literally limitless.

It means that **a limit has not yet been found.**

C.O.R.E. strongly recommends that researchers avoid discovering one by giving Neri an entire building.

---

# 9. Relationship to the Architect Fragment

The temporal relationship is highly significant.

### Event 01

Neri consumes cupcake containing a fragment of Guiding Light's wing.

### Event 02

Neri develops unusual ocular characteristics.

### Event 03

Neri demonstrates direct physical manipulation of Nekonium.

### Event 04

Neri demonstrates state manipulation at the molecular/material level.

The correlation is currently considered extremely strong.

However, it remains unknown whether:

1. the ability was directly transferred from Guiding Light,
    
2. the fragment activated an ability already latent within Neri,
    
3. Neri's anomalous physiology transformed the fragment's properties,
    
4. or the interaction produced an entirely new phenomenon.
    

---

# 10. Neri's Explanation

When asked how she was able to understand the ability so quickly, Neri gave a remarkably simple answer.

> **"Love Chemistry and you get it."**

The statement was initially interpreted as a joke.

It became considerably less funny when Neri proceeded to explain the behavior of the liquid Nekonium sample correctly.

She appears to possess an intuitive understanding of the material changes she is creating.

This may be related to her exceptional aptitude for chemistry.

Neri does not necessarily understand the full theoretical mechanism behind her anomalous ability.

She simply seems to understand **what matter can become.**

---

# 11. Safety Advisory

Neri is **not** to be permitted unsupervised access to:

- nuclear materials,
    
- high-grade anomalous materials,
    
- critical structural components,
    
- containment infrastructure,
    
- experimental samples,
    
- or anything Neko specifically labels _"PLEASE DON'T TOUCH."_
    

The latter category is particularly important.

Neri has already demonstrated that a material's conventional physical properties are not necessarily meaningful restrictions for her.

A locked Nekonium container may be extremely secure to everyone else.

To Neri, it may simply be:

> "A box that needs to become liquid."

---

# 12. Incident Conclusion

The falling blast door was initially classified as a structural failure.

It is now considered the first confirmed field demonstration of Neri's newly manifested anomalous capability.

Neri did not merely survive the impact.

She **caught approximately 500 kg of Nekonium with one hand and changed the material into a harmless particulate state.**

The subsequent laboratory tests confirmed that the phenomenon extends far beyond self-defense.

Neri can alter matter.

How far this ability extends remains unknown.

For now, C.O.R.E. has established one thing with reasonable certainty:

**Neri has become considerably more dangerous to the laws of physics than she is to the people around her.**

And she is having an excellent time in the chemistry lab.
