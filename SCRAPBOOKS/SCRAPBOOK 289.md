# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – LINGUISTIC & COGNITIVE OBSERVATION REPORT**

### **DESIGNATION: M.A.R.T. BLOBS – COMMUNICATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Friendly Anomalous Organism

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Despite possessing a species-wide Ether-linked hivemind, Marts display extremely limited spoken language.

This limitation does **not** appear to affect their ability to cooperate, perform helpful tasks, or recognize individuals.

Research believes most communication occurs silently through their shared Ether network, with spoken words serving only as simple expressions directed toward non-Mart species.

---

# **KNOWN VOCABULARY**

Across every observed individual, only the following spoken phrases have ever been documented.

> "Hello."

> "Yes."

> "No."

> "Goodbye, we'll meet again."

> "We'll be back."

> "Mart!"

No additional words, names, or sentences have been reliably recorded.

Attempts to teach new vocabulary have been unsuccessful.

When presented with flashcards containing new words, Marts generally respond by enthusiastically saying:

> "Mart!"

---

# **COGNITIVE ABILITY**

Behavioral testing indicates that an individual Mart possesses cognitive abilities roughly comparable to an average mouse.

A single Mart can:

- Recognize familiar faces.
    
- Learn simple routines.
    
- Remember safe locations.
    
- Follow basic gestures.
    
- Carry out uncomplicated tasks.
    
- Understand emotional cues.
    

However, individual problem-solving ability is limited.

More complex activities are typically accomplished through many Marts working together via their shared Ether network.

---

# **COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE**

While a single Mart is relatively simple, a large group can display surprisingly coordinated behavior.

Examples include:

- Forming bucket brigades to move water.
    
- Coordinating cleanup efforts.
    
- Guiding lost personnel through familiar corridors.
    
- Simultaneously comforting multiple distressed individuals.
    
- Gathering tools required for simple maintenance.
    

Research attributes this to the hivemind rather than higher individual intelligence.

---

# **SOCIAL BEHAVIOR**

Marts remain consistently cheerful regardless of circumstances.

They greet nearly everyone with:

> "Hello!"

When asked simple yes-or-no questions, they answer appropriately.

At the conclusion of interactions, they nearly always wave a tiny limb and say:

> "Goodbye, we'll meet again."

If one is nearing the end of its lifespan, it instead smiles and says:

> "We'll be back."

Researchers have noted that this phrase is never spoken with sadness.

Only certainty.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Research Division has ceased efforts to expand the Mart vocabulary.

There is no evidence that the species experiences any disadvantage from its limited speech.

They communicate what they need to communicate.

They help where they can.

They comfort those who are hurting.

And whenever one quietly reaches the end of its brief five-week life, it leaves with the same simple promise spoken by every Mart before it.

> **"Goodbye, we'll meet again."**

Followed, without fail, by one final cheerful reminder.

> **"We'll be back."**
