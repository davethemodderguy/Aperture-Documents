# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At 04:17 UTC, an unidentified entity subsequently classified as a **Banlands Harbinger** breached containment within the Banlands.

The entity entered baseline reality.

Its stated objective was not territorial acquisition.

It was **enslavement and destabilization.**

The entity demonstrated sufficient anomalous capability to:

- destabilize local reality;
    
- override conventional containment;
    
- disrupt dimensional boundaries;
    
- incapacitate MSAC personnel;
    
- resist conventional anomalous countermeasures;
    
- traverse between realities;
    
- and generate large-scale structural damage.
    

The engagement lasted **seven days.**

By the end of Day 3, SAFEHOUSE personnel had sustained significant injuries.

By Day 4, the majority of deployed MSAC Operators were confirmed killed or incapacitated.

Lucy Merryweather, Warden of the Banlands, was also injured.

The entity remained active.

The situation changed when the Harbinger physically attacked **Nora Tachyon.**

Nora was picked up by the Harbinger and thrown into the ground.

She lost consciousness.

Aeterna witnessed the event.

The Harbinger then recognized Aeterna.

It knew exactly who she was.

It knew what she had once been.

And it deliberately attempted to provoke her by invoking the **Banlands–Starlands War**.

This was a critical error.

---

# 2. BANLANDS HARBINGER DEFINITION

Following the incident, Lucy Merryweather provided the following clarification.

> **LUCY MERRYWEATHER:**  
> “A Harbinger is a Chaosmaker.”

A Harbinger is not simply an unusually powerful Banlands entity.

Harbingers are entities capable of disrupting established realities.

Known activities include:

- destabilizing the Banlands;
    
- destabilizing Starlands;
    
- disrupting dimensional boundaries;
    
- inducing large-scale chaos;
    
- enslaving lesser dimensions;
    
- converting inhabited realities into controlled territories;
    
- and deliberately provoking conflict between established cosmic systems.
    

A Harbinger may therefore be considered a **cross-reality destabilization threat.**

Lucy further clarified that Harbingers are not representative of ordinary Banlands inhabitants.

---

# 3. HISTORICAL CONTEXT — THE FIVE INVICTUS

The Harbinger identified Aeterna as one of the original **Five Invictus.**

The Five Invictus were artificial entities forged during the ancient conflict between the Banlands and Starlands.

Their purpose was military.

Their capabilities were developed for warfare on a scale vastly exceeding ordinary physical combat.

The Five Invictus were responsible for a significant portion of the casualties during the war.

Their forces included artificial beings forged within the Banlands.

These beings served as:

- Executors;
    
- Mainline combatants;
    
- assault entities;
    
- dimensional occupation forces;
    
- and enforcement units.
    

The resulting conflict caused casualties on a scale described by Lucy as:

> **“Billions upon billions.”**

The war occurred billions of years ago.

Aeterna was one of the Five.

---

# 4. THE DIFFERENCE

There is one critical distinction.

Aeterna changed.

The Harbinger did not.

Aeterna abandoned the purpose for which she had originally been created.

She no longer seeks conquest.

She no longer serves the old war.

She no longer considers slaughter an acceptable solution to conflict.

Her current purpose is protection.

The Harbinger interpreted this change as weakness.

It was mistaken.

# 5. DAY ONE

## 04:17 UTC

The Banlands containment network registered an anomalous breach.

Initial classification:

> **UNKNOWN BANLANDS ENTITY**

Within seventeen minutes, the entity had crossed into baseline reality.

MSAC deployed an initial response team.

SAFEHOUSE personnel were placed under emergency shelter protocols.

Lucy Merryweather entered the engagement personally.

---

## 08:31 UTC

The Harbinger demonstrated its first major anomalous capability.

A conventional containment barrier was established.

The entity touched the barrier.

The barrier destabilized.

It did not break.

It simply ceased to behave according to its intended physical rules.

MSAC command immediately upgraded the threat classification.

---

## 13:06 UTC

First major engagement.

No meaningful containment was achieved.

The Harbinger demonstrated extreme resistance to conventional attacks.

Several operators were injured.

Lucy ordered withdrawal.

The Harbinger pursued.

# 23. FINAL ASSESSMENT

The seven-day Harbinger Event resulted in:

- severe MSAC casualties;
    
- significant SAFEHOUSE injuries;
    
- major infrastructure damage;
    
- dimensional instability;
    
- Banlands containment failure;
    
- direct threat to Earth;
    
- and the temporary incapacitation of multiple senior personnel.
    

The threat was ultimately eliminated by **Invictus Aeterna.**

The final engagement is particularly significant because the Harbinger attempted to force Aeterna back into the identity she once possessed.

It believed that reminding her of the war would awaken the Invictus.

The Harbinger believed Aeterna had become soft.

It misunderstood what had actually happened.

She had become **different.**

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Billions of years ago, Aeterna was built to kill.

She fought.

She conquered.

She watched Starlands soldiers fall.

She was one of five artificial weapons responsible for a war whose casualties are still difficult to quantify.

Then something changed.

She was restored.

She was given another purpose.

And eventually, she found a family.

An adult son who quietly accepts her coffee.

A daughter who prefers silence and pretends she doesn't need help.

And a six-year-old girl who asks her to carry her because she doesn't feel like walking.

The Harbinger saw the old Invictus.

It remembered the weapon.

It tried to bring her back.

It failed.

Because when Aeterna stood over that ancient enemy, sword raised and golden light burning from its tip, she was not fighting for the Banlands.

She was not fighting for the Starlands.

She was not fighting for an ancient war.

She was fighting because **Nora was lying unconscious on the ground.**

And when the Harbinger was gone, Aeterna did not celebrate.

She put away her sword.

She went back to her family.

That is the difference between what Aeterna **was**

and what she **became.**
