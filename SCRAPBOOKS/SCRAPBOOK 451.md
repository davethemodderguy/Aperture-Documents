# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CELESTIAL ARCHITECTURE / BLACKSITE INTERACTION REPORT**

# **THE ARCHITECTS MEET APERTURE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Celestial Authority  
**Subjects:** Guiding Light / Curious Light  
**Personnel:** Director Lucious / Neko / Miss Neko  
**Location:** Heaven's Blacksite  
**Status:** **NON-HOSTILE / FRIENDLY**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Two of reality's Architects have formally met **Director Lucious**, the owner, creator, founder, and CEO of **Aperture Industries** and the individual responsible for establishing **Heaven's Blacksite**.

The meeting was notable for another reason.

**Curious Light and Neko immediately got along.**

Their shared interests in research, invention, experimentation, and unconventional engineering resulted in an unusually strong synergy.

Unfortunately for everyone else...

**Miss Neko had to supervise them.**

---

# **MEETING DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

The meeting took place inside the Blacksite.

Lucious was reviewing facility operations when a familiar golden light appeared.

A rhombus-shaped star formed in the air.

Curious Light manifested.

Blue light appeared moments later.

Guiding Light manifested beside him.

Lucious looked at the two Architects.

> **Lucious:**  
> "...So you're the Architects."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "One of them."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "The other one."

Lucious nodded.

> **Lucious:**  
> "Guiding Light. Curious Light."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Director Lucious."

Curious Light looked around the facility.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "...You built all this?"

> **Lucious:**  
> "I did."

Curious Light's expression immediately changed.

He was interested.

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Curious Light quickly began examining the Blacksite.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "How many sectors?"

> **Lucious:**  
> "Multiple."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Dimensional infrastructure?"

> **Lucious:**  
> "Extensive."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "...Can I see the engineering facilities?"

Lucious paused.

> **Lucious:**  
> "You were going to ask that regardless."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Yes."

Lucious smiled slightly.

> **Lucious:**  
> "Fine."

Guiding Light quietly laughed.

---

# **LUCIOUS AND THE ARCHITECTS**

Lucious and the two Architects developed a mutual respect surprisingly quickly.

Lucious understood creation.

The Architects understood creation on an entirely different scale.

Rather than treating each other as superior and inferior beings, the conversation became one between creators discussing their work.

Lucious explained how Aperture Industries was founded.

Curious Light asked increasingly technical questions.

Guiding Light mostly listened.

At one point, Lucious asked:

> **Lucious:**  
> "Do you two consider yourselves the owners of the realms you created?"

Guiding Light shook her head.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "No."

Curious Light added:

> **Curious Light:**  
> "We built them."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "That doesn't mean we own them."

Lucious nodded.

> **Lucious:**  
> "I understand."

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT MEETS NEKO**

The real surprise came later.

Curious Light encountered **Neko**.

Neko is a **cat Thiren** with an unusual fascination for engineering, research, and technology.

Curious Light immediately noticed.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "...You're a cat."

Neko stared.

> **Neko:**  
> "...Yes."

Curious Light crouched slightly.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Interesting."

Neko tilted his head.

> **Neko:**  
> "...You're an Architect."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Yes."

> **Neko:**  
> "...You build things?"

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Constantly."

Neko's ears immediately perked up.

> **Neko:**  
> "...Want to see something?"

Curious Light smiled.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Absolutely."

---

# **THE SYNERGY**

Within minutes, the two were discussing engineering.

Then research.

Then dimensional mechanics.

Then anomalous materials.

Then completely unrelated inventions.

Neither seemed to notice how much time had passed.

Their similarities were immediately obvious.

### **Neko**

- Cat Thiren.
    
- Research-oriented.
    
- Inventive.
    
- Curious.
    
- Loves experimenting.
    
- Constantly finds unusual engineering solutions.
    

### **Curious Light**

- Architect.
    
- Research-oriented.
    
- Inventive.
    
- Curious.
    
- Loves experimenting.
    
- Constantly finds unusual engineering solutions.
    

The combination was dangerous.

Not because either was malicious.

Because **neither knew when to stop.**

---

# **MISS NEKO'S PROBLEM**

Miss Neko quickly realized what was happening.

She found the two of them in a workshop surrounded by equipment.

> **Miss Neko:**  
> "...What are you doing?"

Neko looked up.

> **Neko:**  
> "Research."

Curious Light added:

> **Curious Light:**  
> "We're improving it."

Miss Neko looked at the machine.

Then at Neko.

Then at Curious Light.

> **Miss Neko:**  
> "...What did you change?"

Silence.

> **Miss Neko:**  
> "Neko."

> **Neko:**  
> "...Nothing dangerous."

Miss Neko looked at Curious Light.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Probably."

Miss Neko immediately shut the machine down.

> **Miss Neko:**  
> "No."

---

# **THE CAT PROBLEM**

Curious Light also developed an immediate fondness for Neko's feline traits.

He repeatedly asked questions about:

- Thiren biology.
    
- Cat behavior.
    
- Neko's senses.
    
- His ears.
    
- His tail.
    
- His ability to interact with machinery.
    

Neko was perfectly happy to answer.

Miss Neko was less enthusiastic.

> **Miss Neko:**  
> "Stop distracting him."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "I'm learning."

> **Miss Neko:**  
> "You're petting him."

Curious Light paused.

His hand was, in fact, resting on Neko's head.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "...Both can be true."

Neko purred.

Miss Neko sighed.

---

# **THE INVENTION INCIDENT**

Eventually, Curious Light and Neko attempted to build something together.

Nobody was told exactly what it was.

Miss Neko discovered them working on it.

> **Miss Neko:**  
> "No."

> **Neko:**  
> "You haven't even seen it."

> **Miss Neko:**  
> "I know you."

She looked at Curious Light.

> **Miss Neko:**  
> "And I know him."

Curious Light looked offended.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "I haven't done anything."

> **Miss Neko:**  
> "Yet."

Curious Light went silent.

Neko slowly put the tool down.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S REACTION**

Guiding Light eventually found the three of them.

She looked at the unfinished invention.

Then at Neko.

Then Curious Light.

Then Miss Neko.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "...What happened?"

Miss Neko answered immediately.

> **Miss Neko:**  
> "Your brother found Neko."

Guiding Light paused.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "...Oh."

She looked at the machine.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "How bad?"

> **Miss Neko:**  
> "Not yet."

Guiding Light nodded.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Good luck."

And left.

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ASSESSMENT**

Lucious later reviewed the incident report.

His only comment:

> **"Let them collaborate."**

Miss Neko stared at him.

> **Miss Neko:**  
> "...Really?"

Lucious nodded.

> **Lucious:**  
> "Their research may be useful."

A pause.

> **Lucious:**  
> "Just keep them away from anything that can destroy the Blacksite."

Miss Neko:

> "...That's a very large category."

Lucious:

> "...Then supervise them."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The meeting established an unexpectedly amusing combination.

**Director Lucious** represents Aperture Industries and the Blacksite.

**Guiding Light** represents guidance and observation.

**Curious Light** represents creation and discovery.

**Neko** represents curiosity, research, and invention.

And **Miss Neko** represents the increasingly necessary safety mechanism preventing the last two from accidentally building something catastrophic.

Curious Light has apparently found his favorite researcher.

Neko has found someone who can finally keep up with him.

And Miss Neko has acquired a new problem.

A very large, golden, celestial problem.

**End of Celestial Architecture / Blacksite Interaction Report**
