## 2. POST-CONVERSATION EVENT

Immediately following the conversation, a significant spatial change occurred.

The fragmented architecture of Neri's room began collapsing inward.

The floating islands disappeared.

The artificial stars disappeared.

The localized gravitational regions ceased.

The rope bridges vanished.

The subspace boundaries re-integrated with conventional Blacksite space.

No destructive event occurred.

The room simply became a normal room again.

Neri's subspace had effectively **closed itself.**

---

## 3. RETURN

Neri subsequently returned to the Blacksite with Bao.

She was physically exhausted but conscious.

Her azure wings remained present during the initial return.

No hostile behavior was observed.

No anomalous resistance occurred.

No attempt was made to prevent Neri from leaving.

She had simply decided to come home.

---

## 4. FINAL OBSERVATION

The room that had remained empty for two months was no longer empty.

Neri was back.

The fifteen islands remained accessible only as historical records of the subspace.

The hole at the transformation site ceased to function as an active route.

The original room was once again an ordinary SAFEHOUSE room.

And, for the first time since her disappearance, there was no longer a reason to search for Neri.

She had found her way home.
