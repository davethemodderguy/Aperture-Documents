## **SUBJECT: CORIN & COREY – MUTUAL PROTECTIVE EFFECT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Since the Dual Manifestation Event, Medical Services have documented a significant reduction in Corin Lockwood's most dangerous self-isolating behaviors.

The improvement is not attributed to medication changes or environmental factors.

Instead, it appears to result from the continuous presence of Corey Lockwood.

Psychological Services have informally described Corey as:

> **Corin's safeguard.**

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Unlike other personnel, Corey recognizes changes in Corin's emotional state almost immediately.

Often before Corin herself acknowledges them.

When Corin begins withdrawing, Corey notices.

When Corin skips meals, Corey notices.

When Corin forgets her medication, Corey notices.

When Corin quietly starts believing she should disappear...

Corey notices.

No documented false positives have been recorded.

---

# **MUTUAL TRUST**

Personnel have repeatedly observed that Corin and Corey possess an unusual degree of trust in one another.

Neither attempts to dominate the other's decisions.

Instead, they consistently accept one another's judgment during periods of emotional difficulty.

One physician summarized it simply.

> "If Corin says she can't do something..."

> "...Corey believes her."

> "If Corey says Corin needs to rest..."

> "...Corin listens."

The same pattern occurs in reverse.

The trust appears instinctive rather than learned.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

One incident was witnessed by Mart during a routine visit to Shelter T-01.

Corin quietly climbed the ladder carrying several days' worth of paperwork.

Without speaking, she closed the door.

A few moments later, Corey climbed after her.

The conversation inside was inaudible.

Approximately twenty minutes later, both sisters emerged.

Corin was carrying two mugs of tea.

Corey was carrying the paperwork.

Mart later noted only one observation.

> "...She didn't stay locked inside."

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services have concluded that the relationship between Corin and Corey provides a uniquely stabilizing influence.

The effect is not one-sided.

While Corey often interrupts Corin's self-isolating behavior, Corin likewise grounds Corey during periods of stress or emotional overload.

Each naturally compensates for vulnerabilities in the other.

Their relationship functions less as caretaker and patient...

And more as two complementary individuals maintaining one another's well-being.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Medical Services continue to discourage unnecessary separation between Corin and Corey.

Beyond the previously documented physiological effects, prolonged separation would also remove one of Corin's strongest protective factors.

Corey cannot erase Corin's past.

She cannot stop every nightmare.

She cannot solve every difficult day.

But she has repeatedly accomplished something no one else consistently could.

When Corin reaches for isolation...

Corey reaches back.

And, more often than not...

Corin takes her hand.
