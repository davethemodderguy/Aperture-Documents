## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following her discovery by Jake Akana in an unused equipment room at Heaven's Blacksite, Camelia Candleheart was introduced to the SAFEHOUSE residential community.

The introduction was arranged to familiarize Camelia with her new surroundings and allow residents to meet her in a controlled, comfortable environment.

Camelia is an approximately sixteen-year-old ghost with a partially melted head and hair, three burning magenta wicks, twin ponytails, and a Japanese-style uniform. Her appearance shares several characteristics with Jake's own ghostly form.

Unlike Jake, however, Camelia has no established history with the residents.

She does not remember much about her previous life and is uncomfortable discussing the circumstances of her murder. Accordingly, the introduction was conducted without requiring her to recount her death.

The meeting was informal.

It was also, by SAFEHOUSE standards, surprisingly normal.

---

## II. THE INTRODUCTION

**TIME:** 1100 HOURS  
**ATTENDEES:** Camelia Candleheart, Jake Akana, Aries Akana, Phoenix, Clover, Neko, Miss Neko, and available SAFEHOUSE residents.

The common room had been prepared for the introduction.

Jake was beside Camelia, serving as her guide. His small blue flame illuminated the space around his melted head, while Camelia's three magenta flames produced a faint pink glow.

The resemblance between them was immediately noticeable.

Jake had no visible eyes or legs, and half his head had melted away. Camelia's appearance was similarly candle-like, though her twin ponytails, Japanese-style uniform, and three wicks gave her a distinct silhouette.

For several moments, the residents simply looked at the newcomer.

Camelia looked back.

Then she leaned slightly toward Jake.

**CAMELIA:** "...Are they always like this?"

**JAKE:** "Like what?"

**CAMELIA:** "Staring."

**JAKE:** "Oh. Yeah, sometimes."

**CAMELIA:** "That's not reassuring."

**JAKE:** "They're nice."

**CAMELIA:** "You said that about the place, too."

**JAKE:** "It is nice."

Camelia looked at the assembled residents again.

Jake gave her a small, encouraging nod.

She took a breath out of habit, then stepped forward.

### CAMELIA'S INTRODUCTION

**CAMELIA:** "Um... hello."

The room quieted.

**CAMELIA:** "My name is Camelia Candleheart. You can call me Camelia."

She briefly adjusted her uniform.

**CAMELIA:** "I'm... a ghost. I don't really remember much about my life before this."

Her three magenta flames flickered softly.

**CAMELIA:** "I was murdered in my house. Then I woke up here. That's all I remember."

A pause followed.

**CAMELIA:** "I don't like talking about it, so please don't ask me when it happened or how I died. I can't give you an answer anyway."

Nobody interrupted her.

**CAMELIA:** "Jake found me in one of the equipment rooms. He talked to me, and then he brought me here."

She glanced at Jake.

**CAMELIA:** "...Thank you, by the way."

**JAKE:** "You're welcome."

**CAMELIA:** "You didn't have to do that."

**JAKE:** "I know."

**CAMELIA:** "...Oh."

For the first time since entering the room, Camelia seemed uncertain what to say next.

**CAMELIA:** "Well... I guess I'll be staying here now."

She looked around at the residents.

**CAMELIA:** "I hope we can get along."

Another brief silence followed.

Then Neko spoke.

---

## III. MEETING THE RESIDENTS

### 1. Neko and Miss Neko

Neko, the male Head of Engineering and Archives, was the first to approach.

He studied Camelia's manifestation with professional curiosity, although he made no attempt to examine her without permission.

**NEKO:** "Welcome to SAFEHOUSE, Camelia. I'm Neko."

He gestured toward the woman standing nearby.

**NEKO:** "And this is Miss Neko. She's my big sister."

**MISS NEKO:** "A pleasure to meet you, dear."

Camelia looked between the two.

**CAMELIA:** "Your... big sister?"

**NEKO:** "Yes."

**CAMELIA:** "But you're—"

She stopped herself.

Miss Neko smiled.

**MISS NEKO:** "A woman? Yes, I am."

**CAMELIA:** "...I wasn't going to say that."

**MISS NEKO:** "Of course not."

**NEKO:** "She does that."

**MISS NEKO:** "And he complains about it."

**NEKO:** "Because you do it on purpose."

**MISS NEKO:** "Naturally."

Camelia stared at them for a moment.

Then she quietly leaned toward Jake.

**CAMELIA:** "Are they always like this, too?"

**JAKE:** "Yep."

**CAMELIA:** "You really should have warned me."

**JAKE:** "You would've come anyway."

Camelia paused.

**CAMELIA:** "...Yeah, probably."

Neko welcomed her to the residential complex and explained that she could approach him if she encountered any technical or facility-related problems.

Miss Neko, meanwhile, assured Camelia that she could speak to her if she needed assistance adjusting to SAFEHOUSE.

Neither asked about her death.

Camelia appeared to appreciate that.

### 2. Aries Akana

Aries approached next.

As Jake's older brother, he had a particular interest in the child who had apparently discovered another ghost while wandering the Blacksite at two in the morning.

He looked at Camelia, then at Jake.

**ARIES:** "So, you found another ghost."

**JAKE:** "Mhm."

**ARIES:** "In an unused equipment room."

**JAKE:** "Yeah."

**ARIES:** "At two in the morning."

**JAKE:** "Yeah."

**ARIES:** "While everyone was asleep."

**JAKE:** "...Yeah?"

Aries folded his arms.

**ARIES:** "Do you ever go to bed?"

**JAKE:** "I'm a ghost."

**ARIES:** "That doesn't answer my question."

**JAKE:** "It answers some of it."

Camelia covered her mouth, suppressing a small laugh.

Aries noticed.

**ARIES:** "Sorry. I'm Aries, Jake's older brother."

**CAMELIA:** "Oh. You're his brother?"

**ARIES:** "Unfortunately for my sleep schedule, yes."

**JAKE:** "Hey!"

**ARIES:** "What? You were wandering around an abandoned corridor in the middle of the night."

**JAKE:** "I found someone!"

**ARIES:** "And that's a good thing. I'm glad you did."

His expression softened.

Aries turned toward Camelia.

**ARIES:** "Welcome. If you need anything, let us know. You don't have to figure everything out by yourself."

Camelia was quiet for a moment.

**CAMELIA:** "...Thank you."

**ARIES:** "You're welcome."

For someone who had awakened in an unfamiliar facility with little memory of her former life, the reassurance seemed to mean more than she could comfortably express.

### 3. Phoenix

Phoenix approached with the air of someone who had already decided that the newcomer needed to be welcomed properly.

As Jake's caretaker, Phoenix was accustomed to looking after a child whose unusual condition could make ordinary situations complicated. Phoenix also knew what it meant to experience death and revival firsthand.

Neither fact made Phoenix an expert on Camelia.

It did, however, make the importance of patience immediately apparent.

**PHOENIX:** "Hello, Camelia. I'm Phoenix."

**CAMELIA:** "Hello."

**PHOENIX:** "Jake has told us a little about how you met."

**CAMELIA:** "I hope he didn't say anything embarrassing."

**JAKE:** "I didn't!"

**PHOENIX:** "He said you were nice."

Camelia looked at Jake.

**CAMELIA:** "That's it?"

**JAKE:** "What else was I supposed to say?"

**CAMELIA:** "I don't know. Something."

**JAKE:** "You're nice."

**CAMELIA:** "...Thank you, Jake."

Phoenix smiled.

**PHOENIX:** "You don't need to explain your past to earn a place here. If you need time to settle in, take it. If you need help, ask. And if you want some quiet, that's fine, too."

Camelia lowered her gaze.

**CAMELIA:** "Even if I don't remember who I was?"

**PHOENIX:** "Even then."

**CAMELIA:** "Even if I can't tell you anything about how I died?"

**PHOENIX:** "You don't owe us that story."

Camelia's three flames flickered.

For a moment, she seemed to struggle with a response.

**CAMELIA:** "...Okay."

**PHOENIX:** "And if Jake gives you trouble, you can tell me."

**JAKE:** "Hey!"

**PHOENIX:** "Especially if he gives you trouble."

**CAMELIA:** "...I'll keep that in mind."

Jake looked betrayed.

Camelia looked slightly more comfortable.

Phoenix considered that a successful introduction.

### 4. Clover

Clover approached with considerably less formality.

She was a girl known for producing actual lucky clovers—real clovers that could grant good luck for approximately five hours before wilting.

She was also Jake's roommate.

**CLOVER:** "Hi! I'm Clover!"

**CAMELIA:** "Hello."

**CLOVER:** "You have really pretty flames."

Camelia blinked.

**CAMELIA:** "...My flames?"

**CLOVER:** "Mhm! The magenta looks nice."

Camelia glanced upward, as though she could see the three wicks herself.

**CAMELIA:** "Oh. Thank you."

**CLOVER:** "Jake's blue ones are pretty, too. You two look like you belong together."

**JAKE:** "We're both ghosts."

**CLOVER:** "I know."

**JAKE:** "That's probably why."

**CLOVER:** "I know, Jake."

Clover smiled at Camelia.

**CLOVER:** "Do you like clovers?"

**CAMELIA:** "I... don't know?"

**CLOVER:** "That's okay! I can show you some sometime. The real ones, not drawings."

**CAMELIA:** "You make real clovers?"

**CLOVER:** "Mhm! And the lucky ones actually work."

**CAMELIA:** "...That's a strange thing to be able to do."

**CLOVER:** "You're a ghost with three burning wicks in your head."

**CAMELIA:** "...Fair enough."

Jake let out a small laugh.

Clover then glanced between the two ghosts.

**CLOVER:** "Oh! And if you want to hang out with Jake, that's fine. Just don't steal all his plushies."

**JAKE:** "I don't have that many!"

Clover gave him a look.

**CLOVER:** "Jake."

**JAKE:** "...Okay, I have a lot."

**CAMELIA:** "You have plushies?"

**JAKE:** "Yeah."

**CAMELIA:** "A lot?"

**JAKE:** "...Yeah."

**CAMELIA:** "Can I see them sometime?"

Jake immediately brightened.

**JAKE:** "Sure!"

Clover smiled knowingly.

**CLOVER:** "See? You're already making friends."

Camelia did not answer immediately.

She simply looked at the people around her.

Nobody was demanding that she explain her death. Nobody was asking her to remember a life she could no longer recall. They were asking about her interests, offering help, and talking about plushies and lucky clovers.

It was unfamiliar.

But it was not unpleasant.

### 5. The Rest of SAFEHOUSE

Other residents present during the introduction offered their own greetings.

Some were curious about Camelia's appearance. Others were interested in how she had reached the Blacksite without being formally identified. A few were simply pleased that Jake had found someone new to talk to.

Questions about her abilities were deferred until Camelia felt comfortable discussing them.

Questions about her murder were not pursued.

The distinction was deliberate.

Camelia was a new resident, not an open investigation waiting to happen.

She would be given time to become familiar with SAFEHOUSE before anyone attempted to understand the full extent of her anomalous nature.

---

## IV. A SMALL QUESTION

Once the introductions had concluded, Camelia found herself sitting beside Jake.

Clover had already begun telling her about the different parts of SAFEHOUSE. Aries had returned to his previous activities, while Phoenix remained nearby in case either ghost needed assistance.

Camelia watched the others moving around the room.

**CAMELIA:** "Jake?"

**JAKE:** "Mhm?"

**CAMELIA:** "Are they really okay with me staying here?"

**JAKE:** "Yeah."

**CAMELIA:** "You don't think I'm strange?"

Jake turned his melted head toward her.

**JAKE:** "You're a ghost."

**CAMELIA:** "I know that."

**JAKE:** "And I'm a ghost."

**CAMELIA:** "I know that, too."

**JAKE:** "So we're both strange."

Camelia stared at him.

**CAMELIA:** "...That is not reassuring."

**JAKE:** "But you can stay."

He said it so simply that Camelia had no immediate answer.

Jake continued.

**JAKE:** "You don't have to sleep in that room anymore if you don't want to."

**CAMELIA:** "I wasn't sleeping."

**JAKE:** "You know what I mean."

**CAMELIA:** "Yeah."

A pause.

**CAMELIA:** "Thank you for finding me."

Jake's little blue flame flickered.

**JAKE:** "You're welcome."

Camelia looked down at her hands.

**CAMELIA:** "I don't remember my old home. I don't remember my family. I don't even know when I died."

**JAKE:** "That's okay."

**CAMELIA:** "It doesn't bother you?"

**JAKE:** "No."

**CAMELIA:** "Why?"

Jake thought about it.

**JAKE:** "Because you're here now."

Camelia went silent.

For once, she did not seem to have anything sarcastic to say.

After a moment, she leaned slightly closer to him.

Not quite a hug.

Just enough to make it clear she did not mind his company.

Jake stayed beside her.

Neither ghost said anything else for a while.

They did not need to.

---

## V. FINAL RESIDENT WELFARE ASSESSMENT

Camelia Candleheart's introduction to SAFEHOUSE concluded without incident.

She has been accepted as a resident and has been informed that assistance is available should she require it.

Her reluctance to discuss her death will be respected. Her memory loss will not be treated as a failure to cooperate. Further investigation into her arrival and abilities will proceed separately from her residential integration.

Jake Akana's involvement in the discovery is also noted.

His decision to approach Camelia gently appears to have prevented an otherwise uncertain first encounter from becoming a frightening one. Rather than treating her as a threat or demanding answers, he introduced himself and offered her a connection to the place he calls home.

The two ghosts share several visible characteristics, but C.O.R.E. will not assume that this automatically makes them close.

Friendship is not an assigned duty.

It is something they may choose to develop.

Nevertheless, Camelia has already begun interacting with residents in a manner consistent with an initial willingness to settle in.

She has met Jake's older brother, Aries; Jake's caretaker, Phoenix; Clover, Jake's roommate and the resident capable of producing actual lucky clovers; and Neko and Miss Neko.

She has also learned that SAFEHOUSE is a place where she can decline to discuss her past without forfeiting her place among its residents.

That is a promising beginning.

---

## VI. CLOSING NOTE — C.O.R.E. RESIDENT WELFARE

When Camelia first awakened at the Blacksite, she had an unused equipment room.

She had a door she could seal shut.

She had the corridors to wander at night.

And she had no one who knew her name.

Then Jake found her.

A six-year-old ghost with a melted head, a little blue flame, and a spectral teddy bear looked into a dark room and discovered someone who looked a little like him.

He introduced himself.

He asked her name.

And when she had nowhere else to go, he brought her home.

Camelia may never remember her former life. We may never learn the circumstances of her murder. Those questions remain unanswered, and she deserves the freedom to decide how much of that uncertainty she wishes to confront.

But she has a new beginning.

She has people who have introduced themselves without asking her to justify her existence.

She has Phoenix, who has offered to help.

She has Aries, who has welcomed her.

She has Neko and Miss Neko, who have made it clear that she can approach them when she needs assistance.

She has Clover, who is already prepared to show her real lucky clovers.

And she has Jake.

Perhaps, in time, she will find herself laughing at the residents' peculiar habits. Perhaps she will become comfortable enough to wander SAFEHOUSE without feeling the need to seal herself away. Perhaps she and Jake will become friends who understand each other's unusual appearances without needing to explain them.

There is no need to rush any of that.

For now, she has been introduced.

She has been welcomed.

And she has been given a place to stay.

**C.O.R.E. RESIDENT WELFARE — FINAL STATEMENT**

_We do not know who Camelia was before she became a ghost._

_We do not know what she lost, what she remembers, or what she may someday recover._

_We know that she was alone in an unused room._

_We know that Jake found her._

_And we know that, when she introduced herself, people listened._

_For someone who has forgotten so much of her life, perhaps that is a good place to begin._
