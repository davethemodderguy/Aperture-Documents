# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### ANOMALOUS RESEARCH & RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-ARC-4C-COG-032  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — COGNITIVE ANOMALY  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE / UNRESOLVED  
**SUBJECT:** Bao  
**ANOMALY TYPE:** Cognitive / Spatial / Mnemonic

---

# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

Bao entered the SAFEHOUSE elevator alone.

The elevator displayed the same anomalous configuration that has persisted since the disappearance of an unidentified floor.

One button remained present.

It had no readable floor designation.

It had previously been considered non-functional.

Bao pressed it.

The elevator moved.

---

# 2. DESTINATION

The doors opened onto an environment not corresponding to any known SAFEHOUSE floor.

The visible environment consisted of:

> **An infinite grassland.**

No walls.

No buildings.

No visible horizon.

The grass extended in every direction.

A quiet musical composition could be heard throughout the environment.

Birds could be heard chirping somewhere beyond visual range.

The atmosphere was calm.

No immediate hazards were detected.

Bao exited the elevator.

The doors closed behind her.

They did not reopen.

---

# 3. EXPLORATION

Bao walked.

There was no apparent destination.

The landscape remained almost completely unchanged regardless of distance traveled.

Eventually, she noticed an irregularity in the grass.

A circular region appeared ahead.

The grass did not grow inside it.

The circle was approximately several meters in diameter.

Around its perimeter were several small objects resembling flowers.

Closer inspection revealed that they were not conventional flowers.

They appeared to be:

> **Two-dimensional doodles.**

Simple drawings.

Flat.

Childlike.

Some resembled flowers.

Others were too abstract to identify.

They appeared physically attached to the environment despite possessing no apparent depth.

Bao approached the circle.

She stopped.

---

# 4. THE FIGURE

A child was sitting in the center.

Male.

Small.

Wings folded behind him.

A shattered halo rested above his head.

His eyes emitted a faint azure light.

He was motionless.

Bao initially did not recognize him.

Then she noticed the object protruding from his chest.

An arrow.

It had passed through him.

No blood was present.

No visible wound was present.

The child simply remained seated.

Broken.

Silent.

Bao stared at him.

Then:

> **BAO:**  
> "...Jake?"

The figure did not respond.

---

# 5. MEMORY EVENT

The moment Bao spoke the name, the environment destabilized.

A large-scale mnemonic event occurred.

Previously inaccessible memories returned simultaneously.

Not gradually.

Not sequentially.

**All at once.**

Bao remembered:

Jake.

His room.

His teddy bear.

His voice.

His questions.

His habit of following her.

The times he asked for help.

The times she told him she was busy.

The times she assumed someone else would help.

The days he disappeared.

The blizzard.

The elevator.

The empty room.

The missing floor.

The birthday.

The apples.

The card.

The words.

Everything.

And then—

Twiggy.

Bao remembered what Twiggy had said.

She remembered the expression on the child's face.

She remembered the sound of the bowl hitting the floor.

She remembered the card.

She remembered the elevator doors closing.

She remembered what came afterward.

The memories continued arriving.

Bao attempted to process them.

She could not.

Her vision blurred.

Her knees gave way.

She collapsed.

---

# 6. UNKNOWN COGNITIVE SPACE

Bao regained partial awareness.

She was no longer in the grassland.

There was no visible environment.

Only darkness.

Then blue text appeared.

One line at a time.

> **YOU FINALLY REMEMBERED**

Pause.

Another line.

> **TOOK YOU A WHILE**

Bao attempted to move.

She could not.

Another line appeared.

> **PLEASE FORGET**

Pause.

> **DON'T COME BACK**

The final line appeared more slowly.

> **PLEASE**

The text disappeared.

---

# 7. RETURN

Bao regained consciousness inside her bedroom.

She was lying on her bed.

Frost was standing nearby.

Neri was sitting at the edge of the room.

Nora was present as well.

Bao immediately sat upright.

> **BAO:**  
> "Where am I?"

Frost looked concerned.

> **FROST:**  
> "Your room."

Bao looked around.

> **BAO:**  
> "What happened?"

Neri answered quietly.

> **NERI:**  
> "You were tired."

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> "You probably should've gone to sleep earlier."

Bao stared at them.

> **BAO:**  
> "I wasn't tired."

Frost frowned.

> **FROST:**  
> "You were in the elevator."

> **BAO:**  
> "I know."

> **FROST:**  
> "Then you came back looking exhausted."

Bao looked at her.

> **BAO:**  
> "I saw someone."

Nobody responded.

---

# 8. MEMORY RETENTION

The memories did not disappear.

They remained.

Every detail.

Every conversation.

Every interaction.

Every moment surrounding the missing child.

Bao remembered.

She remembered who he was.

She remembered what happened.

She remembered why the missing elevator floor existed.

She remembered why the button was surrounded by blue Stardust.

She remembered the grassland.

She remembered the child sitting inside the circle.

She remembered the arrow.

And she remembered his name.

> **Jake.**

---

# 9. DISCLOSURE ATTEMPT

Bao attempted to explain the event.

She told Frost.

Then Neri.

Then Nora.

Her explanation produced immediate concern.

Not because they remembered.

Because they did not.

> **BAO:**  
> "It was Jake."

Frost stared at her.

> **FROST:**  
> "Who?"

Bao froze.

> **BAO:**  
> "...Jake."

> **FROST:**  
> "Bao, there isn't anyone here named Jake."

Bao's breathing became uneven.

> **BAO:**  
> "Yes there is."

Neri shook her head.

> **NERI:**  
> "I think you are confused."

> **BAO:**  
> "No."

Nora approached her.

> **NORA:**  
> "Mom, you were unconscious."

Bao looked at her.

> **BAO:**  
> "I remember him."

No response.

> **BAO:**  
> "I remember everything."

Neri exchanged a glance with Frost.

---

# 10. PRELIMINARY INTERPRETATION

The SAFEHOUSE residents currently believe Bao experienced a significant cognitive anomaly.

Their interpretation is understandable.

Bao entered an inaccessible elevator destination.

She lost consciousness.

She returned claiming to remember an individual nobody else can identify.

She describes an environment nobody else has documented.

She insists that the individual was a child named Jake.

No current SAFEHOUSE record confirms such a resident exists.

No resident currently recalls him.

From their perspective, Bao's memory may therefore represent:

- an anomalous false memory;
    
- an externally induced cognitive construct;
    
- a hallucination;
    
- contamination from the missing-floor anomaly;
    
- or an unidentified mnemonic phenomenon.
    

Bao disagrees.

Strongly.

---

# 11. BAO — PRIVATE OBSERVATION

After everyone left, Bao remained awake.

She stared at the ceiling.

She remembered a small boy sitting beside her.

She remembered him calling her:

> **"Mom."**

She remembered carrying him.

Feeding him.

Talking to him.

Watching him sleep.

She remembered every time he asked for help.

Every time she said:

> **"Later."**

She remembered the last time.

And she remembered what happened afterward.

Bao covered her face with both hands.

She began crying.

Not because she was uncertain.

Because she was certain.

She remembered him.

---

# 12. C.O.R.E. STATUS

Bao is currently under observation for continued cognitive instability.

The missing elevator floor remains inaccessible through conventional means.

The blue Stardust remains present.

The button remains physically non-functional.

The grassland destination remains undocumented.

The identity of the child described by Bao remains unconfirmed by current resident records.

Bao continues to insist:

> **"His name was Jake."**

No one else remembers.

Bao does.

And she cannot make herself forget.

---

## CLOSING NOTE

At approximately 03:17, Bao returned to the elevator.

She stood in front of the missing button.

She did not touch it.

She simply stared at it.

A single particle of blue Stardust drifted downward.

It landed against the button.

Bao whispered:

> **BAO:**  
> "I'm sorry."

Nothing happened.

She remained there for several minutes.

Then returned to her room.

She did not sleep.
