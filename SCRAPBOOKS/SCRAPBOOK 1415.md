# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Guiding Light and Curious Light have identified a possible method of restoring Jake.

The method is extraordinarily simple in concept.

It is also extraordinarily dangerous.

A Nullwalker's external body is not its true body.

The black humanoid form surrounding Jake is a **shell**.

The shell is not Jake.

It is the accumulated manifestation of the Nullwalker state.

The actual Jake remains contained within it.

The method of restoration is therefore:

> **Wake him up.**

The problem is that a Nullwalker cannot be awakened through ordinary means.

It does not respond to voices.

It does not respond to touch.

It does not respond to familiar people.

It does not respond to anomalous energy.

The shell must be broken.

Not damaged gradually.

Not weakened.

Not dismantled.

> **Broken open.**

Only then can whatever remains of Jake inside the shell be reached.

# 5. THE FORCE REQUIRED

Not merely crack it.

Not scratch it.

Not chip it.

> **Break it.**
