### **PERSONAL EFFECTS / FAMILY ARCHIVE**

# **CORIN — THE SUNFLOWER POT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Memorial / Personal Effects  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Associated Personnel:** Orphie / Lumi  
**Location:** Former Treehouse Residence  
**Status:** **PRESERVED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Among Corin's belongings was a small sunflower pot.

It was one of the few plants she genuinely cared for.

Corin kept it somewhere where it could receive sunlight.

She watered it regularly.

She checked its leaves.

She occasionally moved it closer to the window.

It was not particularly valuable.

It was simply hers.

And she loved it.

---

# **THE TAG**

Attached to the stem was a small handwritten tag.

The handwriting belonged to **Lumi**, Corin and Orphie's mother.

The tag simply read:

> **"For my girls.**
> 
> **Keep growing, even when things get difficult.**
> 
> **Love, Mom."**

Corin had never removed it.

The tag remained attached to the plant throughout its entire life.

---

# **BEFORE ORPHIE LEFT**

Corin cared for the sunflower carefully.

She would sometimes talk to it.

Not seriously.

Just small comments.

> "...You're looking better today."

Or:

> "...Don't die on me."

She would smile afterward.

The plant became part of the room.

Part of the routine.

Part of home.

---

# **AFTER ORPHIE LEFT**

Corin stopped taking care of it.

At first, she simply forgot.

Then she stopped noticing.

Then she stopped caring.

The soil dried.

The leaves drooped.

The sunflower slowly wilted.

Nobody intervened.

Nobody knew how important it had been to her.

---

# **THE WILTED PLANT**

By the time the treehouse was sealed, the sunflower was already dead.

The petals had fallen.

The leaves had dried.

The stem bent toward the soil.

Yet the tag remained attached.

The words were still readable.

> **"For my girls."**

---

# **THE IRONY**

The plant had been given to both sisters.

Not just Corin.

Not just Orphie.

**Both.**

Lumi had intended it as a reminder that they were connected.

That they were family.

That they could keep growing together.

Instead, one sister left.

The other stopped caring for the plant.

And eventually...

the sunflower died.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie eventually found it.

She recognized the tag immediately.

She touched it carefully.

> "...Mom gave this to us."

She looked at the dead plant.

> "...You loved this."

Silence.

She carefully lifted the pot.

The soil was completely dry.

Orphie stared at it for a long time.

---

# **THE TAG**

She read the message again.

> **"Keep growing, even when things get difficult."**

Orphie laughed quietly.

It was a broken sound.

> "...You couldn't."

She paused.

> "...Neither could I."

---

# **THE CONNECTION**

The sunflower pot became another reminder of the same thing the dream had shown her.

Sunflowers had once represented hope to Corin.

Lumi had given Corin one as a reminder of that hope.

Corin stopped caring for it after Orphie left.

It died.

Then Corin died.

And now Orphie was left staring at the remains of both.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The sunflower pot remains in the sealed treehouse.

The flower is gone.

The leaves are gone.

The soil is dry.

But the tag remains.

A tiny piece of paper attached to a dead stem.

> **"For my girls."**

Orphie has not removed it.

She cannot bring herself to.

For all the things she threw away...

this is one thing she refuses to throw away.
