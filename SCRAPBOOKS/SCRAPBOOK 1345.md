# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Director of **T.O.P.S. — The Order of Perpetual Salvation** visited the Aperture Blacksite in person.

The visit was authorized through the existing KCA–T.O.P.S. oversight arrangement.

The primary purpose was an introductory meeting between:

> **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**  
> Aperture Industries

and

> **DIRECTOR — T.O.P.S.**  
> The Order of Perpetual Salvation

This represents the first documented in-person meeting between the two directors.

Dialyn was present.

Phoenix Raffedella was technically present within the SAFEHOUSE.

Phoenix was asleep.

She remained asleep for the majority of the meeting.

Nobody woke her.

---

# 1. ARRIVAL

The T.O.P.S. Director arrived at the Blacksite accompanied by Dialyn.

Security procedures were completed without incident.

The Director was provided temporary access appropriate for an approved interorganizational visitor.

Upon entering the main Blacksite interior, the Director stopped briefly.

The facility was considerably different from the conventional Banlands military installations they were accustomed to.

Dialyn noticed.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Different, isn't it?”

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “Very.”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “You'll get used to it.”

A pause.

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “I'm not sure that's possible.”

Dialyn smiled.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Probably not.”

---

# 2. EXECUTIVE MEETING

Director Lucious was waiting in the executive conference room.

The two directors approached one another.

Lucious extended his hand.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “Welcome to Aperture.”

The T.O.P.S. Director accepted.

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “Director Lucious.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “I've heard a great deal about T.O.P.S.”

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “Likewise.”

They sat.

Dialyn remained nearby.

The first several minutes consisted of conventional diplomatic discussion.

Organization.

Jurisdiction.

Research.

Security.

Interreality cooperation.

Then the conversation became considerably more interesting.

---

# 3. APERTURE AND T.O.P.S.

Lucious brought up the obvious comparison.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “We're both research organizations.”

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “Among other things.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “You pursue things most organizations wouldn't touch.”

The Director considered this.

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “And Aperture doesn't?”

Lucious smiled.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “Fair point.”

Dialyn quietly looked between them.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “This is going to be a long meeting.”

Neither director disagreed.

---

# 4. THE QUESTION OF MORALITY

The discussion eventually reached T.O.P.S.'s relationship with KCA oversight.

Lucious asked directly:

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “Do you consider yourselves dangerous?”

The T.O.P.S. Director paused.

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “Potentially.”

Lucious raised an eyebrow.

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “Any organization capable of changing the conditions of existence can become dangerous.”

Lucious nodded.

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “The question isn't whether we can do dangerous things.”

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “The question is whether someone is watching when we do them.”

Dialyn quietly smiled.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “That's why I'm here.”

---

# 5. DIALYN'S INVOLVEMENT

Lucious looked toward Dialyn.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “You really do trust them.”

Dialyn nodded.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “I trust my friend.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “The organization?”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “That's different.”

The T.O.P.S. Director laughed.

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “She says that constantly.”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Because it's true.”

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “You still monitor us.”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Of course.”

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “Even me?”

Dialyn looked directly at them.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Especially you.”

The room went quiet.

Lucious smiled.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “I like her.”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Don't encourage me.”

---

# 6. THE SAFEHOUSE

Following the formal discussion, Lucious suggested that the Director see the SAFEHOUSE.

The Director agreed.

Dialyn accompanied them.

The elevator opened into the residential complex.

The Director looked around.

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “This is residential?”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Yes.”

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “It looks like a military installation.”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “It is an apartment complex.”

A resident walked past carrying a weapon.

Another followed carrying another weapon.

The Director watched them disappear around a corner.

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “You weren't joking.”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “No.”

---

# 7. PHOENIX

They eventually reached Phoenix's temporary workspace.

Phoenix was not working.

She was asleep.

Her head was resting against the back of her chair.

Her KCA work device remained open on the desk.

A medical bag sat beside it.

The T.O.P.S. Director stopped.

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “Is that Phoenix?”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Yes.”

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “She's asleep?”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Yes.”

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “During work?”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “She doesn't exactly choose when she sleeps.”

The Director looked concerned.

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “Still?”

Dialyn nodded.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Still.”

Phoenix shifted slightly in her chair.

Nobody disturbed her.

---

# 8. LUCIOUS ARRIVES

Lucious entered the room shortly afterward.

He glanced toward Phoenix.

Then toward Dialyn.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “She's asleep again?”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Apparently.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “How long?”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Unknown.”

The T.O.P.S. Director looked at Phoenix.

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “Her condition was one of our research outcomes.”

Lucious looked at them.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “The Trancendense program?”

The Director nodded.

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “Among other research.”

Lucious looked at Phoenix again.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “She doesn't look particularly pleased about it.”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “She isn't.”

---

# 9. PHOENIX REMAINS ASLEEP

The three executives continued discussing T.O.P.S. research in low voices.

Phoenix did not wake.

At one point, her hand moved slightly.

Her fingers briefly touched the edge of the medical file beside her.

She remained asleep.

Dialyn quietly moved the file away from the edge of the desk.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Let her sleep.”

Lucious nodded.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “Agreed.”

The T.O.P.S. Director looked at Phoenix for another moment.

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “She volunteered for the research.”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “She did.”

> **T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR:**  
> “I hope she doesn't regret it.”

Dialyn's expression became slightly more serious.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “She has complicated feelings about it.”

No further comment was made.

---

# 10. FINAL DISCUSSION

Before departing, the T.O.P.S. Director and Lucious returned to the executive conference room.

The meeting concluded with agreement to maintain communication between Aperture and T.O.P.S. under existing KCA oversight.

No formal alliance was established.

No military cooperation agreement was signed.

No research permissions were exchanged.

The meeting was primarily diplomatic.

It established something more basic:

> **The two organizations now had faces to associate with one another.**

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The meeting was considered successful.

Director Lucious demonstrated interest in T.O.P.S.'s research philosophy without endorsing every component of it.

The T.O.P.S. Director demonstrated willingness to discuss controversial research openly.

Dialyn continued acting as the primary interpersonal bridge between T.O.P.S. and KCA.

No security incidents occurred.

No anomalous containment events occurred.

Phoenix slept through approximately half of the executive-level discussion.

This was considered medically normal for Phoenix.

They discussed:

**research.**

**morality.**

**immortality.**

**military power.**

**oversight.**

**the future of existence.**

A serious meeting.

A meeting involving two of the most unusual organizations operating across the boundaries between worlds.

Phoenix slept through it.

Being allowed to sleep was the least anyone could give her.
