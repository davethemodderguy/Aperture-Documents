# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **EXECUTIVE OPERATIONS DIVISION**

### **TEMPORARY EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVE – DIRECTORIAL DUTY REASSIGNMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Executive Personnel

---

## **SUMMARY**

Effective immediately, Director **Belle Merryweather** is **temporarily exempt from day-to-day operational duties** within the Krampus Compliance Authority.

This decision was jointly approved by Executive Administration following recommendations concerning workload management and resident welfare.

The exemption is temporary and is intended to allow Belle adequate time to recover from accumulated fatigue while remaining available for executive consultation when required.

Belle retains her title, authority, and position as Director.

Only her routine operational workload has been delegated.

---

# **ACTING EXECUTIVE LEAD**

## **Senior Manager Norma Lockwood**

**Position:** Senior Manager, Krampus Compliance Authority

Norma Lockwood has been appointed as the acting operational lead during Belle's temporary exemption.

Among KCA personnel, Norma has earned a reputation for being remarkably dependable.

Many employees jokingly describe her as:

> **"A second Belle."**

Not because she replaces Belle...

But because she approaches leadership with the same willingness to help others, solve problems, and remain involved with every department.

---

# **BACKGROUND**

Norma has served under the KCA for many years and is one of Belle's most experienced seniors.

She possesses extensive knowledge of:

- Executive administration.
    
- Tactical logistics.
    
- Interdepartmental coordination.
    
- Emergency response planning.
    
- Engineering.
    

Engineering, however, is less of a job...

And more of a hobby.

It is common to find Norma visiting fabrication workshops simply because she enjoys building things.

---

# **BLACKSITE VISITS**

With Belle temporarily working from Heaven's Blacksite, Norma frequently travels there to coordinate executive matters in person.

Official meetings often conclude surprisingly quickly.

The remainder of the visit usually consists of:

- Talking with Belle over tea.
    
- Looking at Neko's latest invention.
    
- Helping with engineering projects.
    
- Wandering through Project SAFEHOUSE.
    
- Accidentally spending far longer at the Blacksite than originally planned.
    

Norma has admitted that she enjoys the atmosphere.

> "It's difficult to leave once everyone starts making pancakes."

---

# **KCA OPERATIONS**

Operational audits conducted since the temporary reassignment indicate that the KCA continues to function normally.

No measurable reduction in efficiency has been observed.

Department heads have reported that Norma transitioned into the role with little difficulty.

One executive summarized the situation simply:

> **"The KCA is safe in her hands."**
