## Executive Summary

Neri is **not physically incapable of speech**.

Following her previous loss of voice, her vocal function has partially returned. However, speaking consumes a noticeable amount of her limited stamina.

As a result, Neri generally remains silent.

Her preferred methods of communication remain:

- Nodding.
    
- Shaking her head.
    
- Gestures.
    
- Pointing.
    
- Facial expressions.
    
- Writing when absolutely necessary.
    

She will use her voice when something is sufficiently important to justify the energy expenditure.

Most commonly:

> **Discomfort.**

---

## Incident Details

**LOCATION:** Sector 4-C, SAFEHOUSE Corridor  
**CARRIERS:** Jane Doe  
**SUBJECT:** Neri

Jane was transporting Neri via piggyback.

The transportation was proceeding normally until Jane misjudged a turn.

**THUD.**

Neri's head made contact with the wall.

A brief silence followed.

Then:

> **Neri:** “Ow.”

Jane stopped immediately.

She stared forward for approximately two seconds.

Then turned her head as far as physically possible.

> **Jane:** “What did you say?”

Jane's expression indicated genuine disbelief.

Neri was not known to casually vocalize.

Neri lightly clutched the side of her head.

Her eyes had begun watering.

Then, in an unusually soft voice:

> **Neri:** “You hit a wall...”
> 
> _small pause_
> 
> **Neri:** “I'm on your back, you know?”

Neri began sobbing quietly.

Jane immediately stopped moving.

> **Jane:** “...Oh my God.”
> 
> **Jane:** “I'm sorry.”

Neri did not answer.

She simply rested her head against Jane's shoulder, still holding the side of her head.

---

## Observations

The incident provides an important clarification regarding Neri's current vocal condition.

Neri **can speak**.

She simply does not consider speech worth spending stamina on unless necessary.

The distinction is significant.

Neri's silence should therefore not automatically be interpreted as:

- inability to speak;
    
- refusal to communicate;
    
- emotional shutdown;
    
- lack of awareness;
    
- inability to understand someone.
    

In many circumstances, she simply has nothing important enough to say.

Her voice is also notably different from what personnel might expect.

Rather than harsh, strained, or mechanical vocalization, Neri's voice is:

**Very soft.**

**Gentle.**

**Comforting.**

And, according to Jane's unofficial description:

> “She sounds like someone turned a cake into a person and taught it to whisper.”

This description has not been entered into the medical database.

---

## Medical/Welfare Consideration

Neri's speech should be treated as another form of **energy expenditure**.

Personnel should not:

- ask Neri to repeat herself unnecessarily;
    
- encourage her to “talk more”;
    
- demand verbal answers when gestures would suffice;
    
- treat every vocalization as a major emotional breakthrough;
    
- make her speak simply because she technically can.
    

If Neri chooses to speak, **listen**.

If she chooses not to speak, **don't make her.**

Her occasional verbal complaints are particularly important because she is more likely to spend stamina speaking when something is genuinely bothering her.

Therefore:

> **If Neri says “Ow,” believe her.**
