# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A conversation between Neri Tachyon and Bao has resulted in a significant emotional misunderstanding.

The subject's concern was straightforward.

Her interpretation was not.

Neri has recently become increasingly aware that personnel frequently refer to her as:

> **“The Last Tachyon.”**

The designation originally emerged following confirmation that Neri is the final surviving member of the Tachyon family.

However, medical personnel have repeatedly used the phrase when discussing her survival.

Examples include:

> “Neri, you have to be careful.”

> “You're the Last Tachyon.”

> “We can't afford to lose you.”

The statements were intended to emphasize the seriousness of Neri's medical condition.

Neri appears to have interpreted them differently.

She appears to believe that much of the care she receives is directed toward preserving the **Tachyon family**, rather than preserving **her.**

This culminated in a conversation with Bao.

---

# 1. THE QUESTION

**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — KITCHEN

**PRESENT:**  
Bao  
Neri

Neri had been sitting quietly at the table.

She had not been eating.

Bao noticed.

She approached but did not immediately speak.

After several minutes, Neri finally looked up.

She spoke very quietly.

> **Neri:**  
> “Do you love me because you love me...”

Pause.

Neri looked down.

> **Neri:**  
> “Or I'm the Last Tachyon?”

Bao immediately understood what Neri was asking.

She did not, however, understand how carefully she needed to answer.

---

# 2. BAO'S RESPONSE

Bao sat across from her.

> **Bao:**  
> “Neri, you're the last Tachyon.”

Neri's expression changed.

Barely.

But it changed.

Bao continued.

> **Bao:**  
> “Your family matters.”

Neri looked down at the table.

> **Bao:**  
> “What happened to them matters.”

Neri's fingers tightened around her sleeve.

Bao then said:

> **Bao:**  
> “And losing you would mean losing the last Tachyon.”

Silence.

Neri looked at Bao.

> **Neri:**  
> “So...”

She hesitated.

> **Neri:**  
> “That's why?”

Bao immediately realized she had chosen the wrong words.

> **Bao:**  
> “No, that's not what I—”

Neri interrupted.

> **Neri:**  
> “It's okay.”

She stood.

> **Bao:**  
> “Neri.”

Neri shook her head.

> **Neri:**  
> “I understand.”

She left the room.

She did not understand.

# 3. THE MEDICAL LANGUAGE PROBLEM

Subsequent review identified a recurring pattern in Neri's medical records.

Whenever Neri experienced an episode of severe anemia, cardiac distress, collapse, or loss of consciousness, medical personnel frequently reassured her with statements such as:

> **“You have to be careful.”**

> **“You're the Last Tachyon.”**

> **“We can't afford to lose you.”**

From the medical perspective, these statements were intended to communicate:

> **Your life matters. Please allow us to protect you.**

From Neri's perspective, the wording may instead have communicated:

> **Your value is that you are the Last Tachyon.**

This distinction is critical.

Neri already possesses an established tendency to equate her existence with usefulness.

Her historical internal framework has repeatedly been:

> **If I am useful, I deserve to remain.**

The phrase **“Last Tachyon”** may therefore have become another version of the same concept.

Not:

> _“I am loved.”_

But:

> _“I am important because I am the last one.”_

# 4. THE MEMORIES NERI DOES NOT HAVE

Neri remembers very little of her biological family visually.

She does not remember her mother's face.

She does not remember her father's face.

She does not remember her grandparents' faces.

And most painfully:

She does not remember her little sister's face.

She remembers fragments.

A voice.

A name.

A fishbone hairclip.

A love of sweets.

Cake.

Small pieces of childhood.

But the faces are gone.

The people whose blood made her who she is exist primarily as incomplete memories.

The Tachyon family has become a historical designation.

A surname.

A collection of archived documents.

A lineage.

A watch.

An engraving.

A family tree with almost every branch marked:

> **DECEASED**

And Neri is the final surviving name on it.

---

# 5. THE EXPLOSION

Neri was eventually informed that she and her sister had been kidnapped approximately three months before the Tachyon family residence was bombed.

The kidnapping unintentionally saved their lives.

Everyone remaining at the residence died.

Neri survived.

Her sister survived for a time.

Neri eventually lost her as well.

This has created a particularly painful contradiction.

The historical record says:

> **Neri survived.**

Neri's memories say:

> **Almost everyone she loved did not.**

And now personnel repeatedly tell her:

> **“We can't afford to lose you.”**

Neri appears to have begun asking herself:

> _Why?_

Not:

> _Why do you care about me?_

But:

> _Why do you care about the last Tachyon?_

# 8. C.O.R.E. REVIEW

The phrase **“Last Tachyon”** is historically accurate.

It is also becoming psychologically hazardous when used as a substitute for Neri's identity.

Neri is:

- a Tachyon;
    
- a survivor;
    
- a chemist;
    
- a daughter;
    
- a sister;
    
- a friend;
    
- a resident of SAFEHOUSE;
    
- and an individual who exists independently of her family history.
    

She is not a biological preservation project.

She is not a museum artifact.

She is not the final remaining specimen of a deceased family.

She is not required to carry the Tachyon name on behalf of everyone who died.

Most importantly:

> **Neri does not owe the dead her survival.**

And the people caring for her must not accidentally communicate that her survival belongs to them.

---

# 9. MEDICAL COMMUNICATION DIRECTIVE

Effective immediately, medical personnel are instructed to discontinue phrases such as:

> “We can't afford to lose the Last Tachyon.”

> “You're the last Tachyon.”

> “The Tachyon family can't lose you.”

when discussing Neri's personal medical condition.

Instead:

> **“We don't want to lose you.”**

or:

> **“You're hurt. Let us help.”**

or simply:

> **“Neri, stay with us.”**

The distinction is deliberate.

The objective is to communicate concern for **Neri**, rather than the historical value attached to her surname.

Neri is the final Tachyon.

That is a fact.

It is part of her history.

It explains the watch on her wrist.

It explains the engraving on her weapons.

It explains why Lucious recognized her.

It explains why the Tachyon archives suddenly mattered again.

It does not explain why Jane waits for her.

It does not explain why Alice wants her around.

It does not explain why Frost worries when she disappears.

It does not explain why SAFEHOUSE made room for her.

Those things are not owed to the Tachyon family.

They are given to Neri.

The girl who drinks coffee.

The chemist who fills twelve terabytes with unanswered questions.

The seal who sometimes says only one word.

The child who doesn't remember her sister's face.

The survivor who sometimes wishes she had not survived.

The person who keeps asking whether she has earned the right to remain.

She has not been kept alive because she is:

> **The Last Tachyon.**

She is being kept alive because she is:

> **Neri.**

And C.O.R.E. recommends that everyone start saying it that way.
