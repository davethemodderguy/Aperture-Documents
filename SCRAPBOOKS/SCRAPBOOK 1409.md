# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

C.O.R.E. has identified an unusual social phenomenon surrounding **Anby's requests**.

Personnel consistently report difficulty refusing her.

The issue does not appear to involve coercion, anomalous influence, Ether manipulation, or deliberate persuasion.

The primary contributing factors appear to be:

- Anby's naturally soft voice;
    
- her tendency to speak quietly;
    
- frequent mumbling;
    
- her generally gentle manner;
    
- and the fact that she often asks for things with very little expectation that she will actually receive them.
    

The result is an increasingly documented phenomenon in which personnel initially intend to refuse a request and subsequently find themselves agreeing to it.

---

# 1. VOCAL CHARACTERISTICS

Anby's voice is notably soft.

Not merely quiet.

**Soft.**

Her tone is gentle enough that personnel frequently lower their own voices when speaking with her.

She also has a tendency to mumble.

This becomes particularly noticeable when she is:

- tired;
    
- embarrassed;
    
- asking for something;
    
- uncertain;
    
- or quietly expressing a preference.
    

Her words may become partially obscured.

Personnel then ask:

> “What?”

Anby repeats herself.

Usually even more quietly.

This has been identified as a significant contributing factor to the phenomenon.

---

# 2. THE REQUEST PATTERN

A typical Anby request follows a predictable structure.

She approaches.

Pauses.

Looks at the person.

Then quietly asks:

> **ANBY:**  
> “Can I...?”

The sentence may trail off.

Personnel frequently respond:

> “Sure.”

Only afterward do they realize they have agreed before hearing the complete request.

---

# 3. EXAMPLE — SNACK

Anby approached Bao in the kitchen.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Bao...”

> **BAO:**  
> “Hm?”

Anby looked at the counter.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Can I have...”

She mumbled the final words.

Bao couldn't hear them.

> **BAO:**  
> “What was that?”

Anby looked slightly embarrassed.

> **ANBY:**  
> “...Cake.”

Bao immediately reached for a plate.

> **BAO:**  
> “Of course.”

Anby nodded.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Thanks.”

Bao later reported that she had intended to ask Anby whether she wanted something to eat.

The distinction was apparently irrelevant.

# 7. THE “MUMBLE EFFECT”

C.O.R.E. Welfare personnel have informally designated the phenomenon:

> **THE MUMBLE EFFECT**

The pattern is as follows:

**Step 1:**  
Anby asks for something.

**Step 2:**  
Anby speaks quietly.

**Step 3:**  
Anby mumbles.

**Step 4:**  
The recipient asks her to repeat herself.

**Step 5:**  
Anby becomes slightly embarrassed and speaks even more softly.

**Step 6:**  
The recipient gives up.

**Step 7:**  
The request is approved.

The efficiency of this process remains unexplained.

# 14. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

C.O.R.E. has found **no evidence** that Anby's voice possesses a supernatural persuasive effect.

There is no confirmed:

- vocal compulsion;
    
- emotional manipulation;
    
- anomalous charisma;
    
- Ether-based influence;
    
- hypnotic effect;
    
- or cognitive alteration.
    

Personnel are simply reacting to Anby's manner of speaking.

Her voice is soft.

She mumbles.

She asks politely.

She rarely demands anything.

And, apparently, that combination is devastating.

---

# 15. PERSONNEL RECOMMENDATION

Personnel are advised to listen carefully to Anby's entire request before responding.

Recommended procedure:

> **“What do you need?”**

Wait for the complete answer.

If she mumbles:

> **“Could you say that again?”**

If necessary:

> **“I want to make sure I understood you.”**

Do not simply say yes because she asked quietly.

This recommendation has not been successful.

Anby does not manipulate people into helping her.

She doesn't need to.

She walks up.
