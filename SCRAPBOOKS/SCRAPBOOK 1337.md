# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At 14:03, Director Lucious initiated a direct call with the CEO of Woodland Coorp.

The stated purpose was to address Woodland's repeated attempts to abduct SAFEHOUSE resident Jake.

The call lasted:

> **03 HOURS, 07 MINUTES, 42 SECONDS.**

It was originally expected to last approximately ten minutes.

It did not.

Both organizations are longstanding rivals.

The hostility between Aperture Industries and Woodland Coorp predates the current Jake incidents by several years.

Most significantly, Woodland Coorp was responsible for a missile strike that nearly wiped out the Tachyon family.

The relationship between the two corporations has therefore never been particularly cordial.

Despite this, the call began with both executives attempting to maintain professional conduct.

This lasted approximately:

> **Four minutes.**

---

# 2. OPENING

**LUCIOUS:**  
“Good afternoon.”

**WOODLAND CEO:**  
“Director Lucious.”

**LUCIOUS:**  
“I believe we need to discuss your recent activities concerning one of my residents.”

**WOODLAND CEO:**  
“I assume you mean the child.”

**LUCIOUS:**  
“I mean Jake.”

Pause.

**WOODLAND CEO:**  
“Yes.”

**LUCIOUS:**  
“You kidnapped him.”

**WOODLAND CEO:**  
“We attempted to extract him.”

**LUCIOUS:**  
“You kidnapped him.”

**WOODLAND CEO:**  
“We—”

**LUCIOUS:**  
“You kidnapped him.”

Silence.

**WOODLAND CEO:**  
“Fine.”

---

# 3. THE FIRST ARGUMENT

Lucious presented the first incident.

Woodland attempted to defend the operation on the basis that their personnel had not known the full extent of Jake's capabilities.

Lucious reportedly stared at his phone for several seconds.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “You kidnapped a six-year-old because you didn't know what he could do.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “We had reason to believe he was anomalous.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “He is.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “Exactly.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “He's also six.”

Pause.

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “That doesn't make him harmless.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “I am aware.”

---

# 4. PULVERIZATION IS MENTIONED

Lucious eventually brought up the first escape.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “Your guards fired at him.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “They were responding to a containment breach.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “And then he turned them into blue dust.”

Silence.

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “We have reviewed the footage.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “Good.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “What exactly is he?”

Lucious paused.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “That's classified.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “You called me to complain about us kidnapping him while refusing to tell me what he is?”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “Yes.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “That's incredibly unhelpful.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “You're welcome.”

---

# 5. THE SECOND INCIDENT

Approximately twenty-three minutes into the call, Lucious brought up the second kidnapping attempt.

This was where the conversation deteriorated.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “Then you tried again.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “That incident is being reviewed.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “He shot a hole through your building with his sword.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “We know.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “And flew home.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “We know.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “He didn't even fight your people.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “We know.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “He made a door.”

Long pause.

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “We know.”

Lucious reportedly began laughing.

The Woodland CEO did not.

For approximately thirty seconds.

Then he also started laughing.

---

# 6. PROFESSIONALISM FAILURE

At approximately **00:31:14**, both parties stopped maintaining formal executive speech.

The transcript becomes progressively less useful.

Examples include:

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “You kidnapped the star child.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “We didn't know he was a star child!”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “You didn't know he had wings either?”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “WE THOUGHT THEY WERE DECORATIVE!”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “DECORATIVE WINGS?”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “I DON'T KNOW!”

---

# 7. THE TACHYON INCIDENT

At approximately **01:02**, the conversation shifted toward the historical conflict between the two corporations.

Lucious reminded Woodland that their rivalry was not merely commercial.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “Let's also remember why Aperture doesn't exactly send you holiday cards.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “You mean the Tachyon incident.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “Yes.”

The Tachyon family had nearly been wiped out by a Woodland missile strike.

The incident remains one of the most serious historical grievances between the organizations.

The Woodland CEO attempted to explain the circumstances.

Lucious interrupted.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “You fired a missile at them.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “That is an oversimplification.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “It was a missile.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “Yes.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “It struck the Tachyon position.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “Yes.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “Nearly killed the entire family.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “Yes.”

Pause.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “Then you kidnapped their six-year-old relative.”

Another pause.

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “When you put it like that—”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “How else would I put it?”

---

# 8. SOMEONE LOSES IT

At 01:18:09, an unidentified Woodland executive reportedly entered the room.

The CEO muted the call.

The microphone remained active for approximately three seconds.

A voice was heard asking:

> **“Are you still arguing about the star child?”**

The CEO responded:

> **“YES.”**

The door apparently closed.

Lucious laughed for approximately twenty seconds.

---

Eventually, Woodland asked the question everyone had been avoiding.

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “What does Jake actually want?”

Lucious paused.

His response was noticeably calmer.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “To be a kid.”

Silence.

> **LUCIOUS:**

> **LUCIOUS:**

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “The sword is a problem.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “The sword is not your biggest problem.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “I know.”

The conversation somehow reached the subject of the blue dust.

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “I hate this conversation.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “It's been three hours.”

# 11. THREE HOURS IN

At approximately **02:41**, neither executive appeared capable of remembering why the call had originally been scheduled for ten minutes.

The conversation had expanded to:

- Jake;
    
- Woodland's intelligence failures;
    
- Aperture security;
    
- the Tachyon missile strike;
    
- corporate rivalry;
    
- Jake's wings;
    
- the greatsword;
    
- Pulverization;
    
- the fact that Jake apparently learned to fly very quickly;
    
- Woodland's inability to successfully contain him;
    
- Janitorial Services;
    
- and whether a six-year-old star should be allowed to own a sword.
    

The final question produced the longest argument.

---

**LUCIOUS:**

# 13. FINAL AGREEMENT

At 03:06:57, the conversation finally returned to its original purpose.

A tentative agreement was reached.

Woodland Coorp would:

**Not intentionally abduct Jake.**

**Not conduct further unauthorized containment operations against SAFEHOUSE residents.**

**Not assume that Jake's childlike appearance indicates vulnerability.**

**And, most importantly:**

> **Stop kidnapping Jake.**

Lucious added one final condition.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “If you see him, leave him alone.”

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “Understood.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “Good.”

Pause.

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “What if he approaches us?”

Lucious thought for a moment.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “Give him cake.”

The Woodland CEO laughed.

> **WOODLAND CEO:**  
> “Deal.”

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Aperture Industries and Woodland Coorp remain rivals.

The history between them includes sabotage, armed conflict, and the missile strike that nearly wiped out the Tachyon family.

There is no expectation that the rivalry will disappear.

However, for three hours, two executives who have spent years opposing one another managed to find common ground over a very specific issue:

**Neither of them wants to deal with Jake's increasingly creative methods of leaving Woodland facilities.**

Woodland thought they had captured a vulnerable child.

They discovered a star.

They thought his wings were strange.

They discovered he could fly.

They thought his sword was a sword.

They discovered it could breach a building.

They thought the first incident was an anomaly.

They tried again.

They are now officially advised to stop.

C.O.R.E. has appended the following note to the incident record:

> **“If Woodland Coorp attempts to kidnap Jake a third time, please direct them to this document before they begin.”**
