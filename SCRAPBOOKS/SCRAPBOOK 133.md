# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL NIGHT OBSERVATION**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – NOCTURNAL ROUTINE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical & Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Since moving into Project SAFEHOUSE, Corin Lockwood has developed a consistent nighttime routine.

Long after the rest of the residents have gone to bed, Corin is frequently observed awake in the central lounge.

The room is usually quiet.

Only a single lamp remains on.

Corin almost always prepares herself a warm glass of milk before sitting on the sofa or near one of the windows.

She rarely turns on the television.

She rarely reads.

Most nights...

She simply thinks.

---

# **NIGHTLY OBSERVATION**

Between approximately 01:00 and 04:00, Corin can often be found silently staring out into the Blacksite.

She occasionally wraps both hands around her mug, more for comfort than warmth.

The milk gradually cools.

She often forgets to drink it.

Personnel reviewing overnight security footage have noted that Corin's expression rarely changes during these periods.

She appears lost in thought.

Medical Services believe these periods are dominated by memories of her childhood and the years she spent in captivity.

When she finally leaves the lounge, she often appears emotionally exhausted despite having done nothing physically demanding.

---

# **HER EYES**

Corin possesses strikingly beautiful eyes.

Their unusual coloration is one of her most distinctive features, and they are often the first thing people notice after spending time with her.

Star once remarked:

> "They're really pretty."

Belle agreed immediately.

Even Taiga, who rarely comments on appearances, quietly nodded.

Corin's response was immediate.

She lowered her gaze.

> "...I don't think so."

When Belle asked why, Corin answered without hesitation.

> "...These are the same eyes that watched everything happen."

The room became silent.

No one tried to argue.

---

# **LUMI**

One particularly quiet night, Lumi found Corin in the lounge.

The glass of milk in front of her was untouched.

Lumi sat beside her.

After several minutes, she gently asked:

> "What are you thinking about?"

Corin didn't answer at first.

Eventually she whispered,

> "...I keep remembering."

Lumi waited.

> "...And every time I remember..."

Corin looked down into the glass.

> "...I end up hating my life a little more."

Lumi's expression filled with sorrow.

She reached over and rested a hand on Corin's shoulder.

> "You don't deserve to carry those memories alone."

Corin leaned slightly toward her mother.

Not because the memories disappeared.

But because, for a little while, they became lighter to carry.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION**

The residents gradually became aware of Corin's habit.

Without discussing it, small changes appeared.

Fresh milk was always available.

The lounge lights were left dim instead of turned off completely.

A blanket somehow found its way onto the sofa every night.

No one admitted responsibility.

No one needed to.

Corin noticed.

She never mentioned it.

But she stopped sitting quite so rigidly.

She had begun to understand that even when the house was asleep...

Someone had thought about her before going to bed.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services consider Corin's late-night routine to be a coping mechanism rather than a behavioral concern in itself.

The periods of reflection often coincide with increased sadness and intrusive memories.

Staff are advised not to interrupt these moments unnecessarily, but to ensure Corin knows companionship is available if she seeks it.

Several residents have occasionally chosen to join her for a quiet cup of milk or tea without attempting to "fix" how she feels.

These simple acts of presence have consistently been associated with improved emotional stability.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin still spends many nights awake.

She still remembers.

She still grieves the childhood that was taken from her.

Yet increasingly, she is no longer the only one awake.

Sometimes Belle quietly sits beside her.

Sometimes Mart reads across the room.

Sometimes Taiga simply occupies the other end of the sofa.

Sometimes Lumi is there before Corin even arrives.

On those nights, Corin still remembers the past.

But she remembers it while surrounded by people who are helping her build a future.

**End of Psychological Night Observation**
