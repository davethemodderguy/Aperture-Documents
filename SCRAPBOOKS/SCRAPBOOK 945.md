# SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL DOCUMENT

## NERI SUBSPACE — INTERIOR STELLARIZATION UPDATE

**Document ID:** SH-SUB-044  
**Subject:** Neri's Subspaced Room  
**Modification:** Artificial Stellar Environment  
**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL  
**Prepared by:** Neko / SAFEHOUSE Archives

---

## 1. SUMMARY

Neri has made another modification to her personal subspace.

Her room now contains a visible **stellar sky** consisting of numerous artificial stars distributed throughout the space surrounding the floating islands.

The stars are visible from essentially every unobstructed angle within the room.

Unlike the stars of Starlands, these are not independently confirmed celestial bodies.

They are part of Neri's subspace environment.

---

# 2. VISUAL APPEARANCE

The previous interior of the subspace consisted primarily of open space surrounding the floating islands.

Neri has now populated that space with stars.

From the surface of an island, the environment appears similar to standing beneath a clear night sky.

There is no visible ceiling.

There is no conventional atmospheric boundary.

Instead, the islands appear to float within an immense dark expanse containing countless points of light.

The effect is particularly noticeable from the larger islands.

---

# 3. STAR DISTRIBUTION

The stars are distributed throughout the apparent sky rather than being concentrated into a single decorative panel.

They appear at varying distances and brightness levels.

Some are barely visible.

Others are considerably brighter.

This prevents the environment from appearing like a simple projection screen.

The exact number of stars has not been determined.

Neko's initial attempt to count them was abandoned.

> **Neko:** “There are too many.”

> **Neri:** “Yeah.”

> **Neko:** “Did you make all of them?”

> **Neri:** “I think so.”

> **Neko:** “You _think_ so?”

> **Neri:** “They're stars.”

Neko subsequently stopped asking.

---

# 4. FUNCTION

The stars currently appear to be primarily **environmental and aesthetic**.

No confirmed navigational, gravitational, astronomical, or anomalous function has been identified.

They do not appear to:

- generate significant heat;
    
- emit dangerous radiation;
    
- alter local gravity;
    
- interfere with the existing subspace network;
    
- affect the rope bridges;
    
- interfere with the 250-meter recovery zone.
    

At present, they simply provide illumination and atmosphere.

---

# 5. LIGHTING

The stars provide a very low level of ambient illumination.

They are bright enough to make the surrounding environment visually apparent but do not replace conventional lighting.

Neri's room therefore retains its normal sources of illumination.

The result is effectively:

**Room lighting + distant stars + floating islands**

rather than complete darkness.

---

# 6. RELATIONSHIP TO THE FLOATING ISLANDS

The stellar environment surrounds all fifteen islands.

The stars therefore provide a consistent backdrop across the entire subspace archipelago.

From one island, a visitor can see stars behind another island.

From the larger islands, it is possible to look outward and see multiple islands suspended against the stellar background.

This has substantially improved the visual distinction between:

**The room**

and

**The space around the room.**

---

# 7. NERI'S REASONING

When asked why she added the stars, Neri gave a surprisingly simple answer:

> **“It looked empty.”**

When asked whether she intended to add anything else:

> **“Maybe.”**

No additional details were provided.

---

# 8. RESIDENT RESPONSE

The modification has generally been well received.

Several residents have begun spending additional time in the subspace simply because the environment is more pleasant to look at.

The stars have also made the empty islands appear less unfinished.

They now resemble deliberately isolated pieces of land suspended beneath an artificial night sky rather than incomplete sections of an unfinished room.

---

# 9. OBSERVATION FROM THE PRIMARY ISLAND

At night-cycle equivalent lighting, the view from Neri's bedroom has become particularly distinctive.

The bed, bookshelf, laboratory equipment and other belongings remain on the island while the open space beyond the island contains the artificial stars.

The effect has led to the room being informally described by residents as:

> **“Neri's little space observatory.”**

Neri has not officially accepted this name.

She also has not rejected it.

---

# 10. ARCHIVAL NOTE

Neri continues to insist that the environment is simply her room.

This classification is becoming increasingly difficult to reconcile with the actual dimensions of the space.

Current known characteristics include:

- **15 floating islands**
    
- Variable island sizes
    
- Localized gravity
    
- Rope bridges
    
- A 250-meter zero-gravity recovery zone
    
- Multiple connected subspaces
    
- Laboratory facilities
    
- Residential space
    
- Artificial stellar environment
    

Despite this, the official SAFEHOUSE designation remains:

> **NERI'S SUBSPACED ROOM**

Because changing the designation would apparently annoy her.
