## **NOTICE**

It has since been preserved for historical purposes.

### **ENGINEERING WORKSHOP**

**Norma** (looking through historical weapon designs)

> "...Huh."

> "This says something called a _minigun_ existed."

She looked up.

> "...What's a minigun?"

The entire workshop fell silent.

---

Neko slowly turned around.

A wrench slipped out of his hand.

His eyes lit up.

> "...You don't know what a minigun is?"

Miss Neko immediately looked up from her work.

> "...Neko."

He was already grabbing a notebook.

> "Imagine your machine gun..."

He drew six barrels.

> "...But more barrels."

---

Another page.

Motor.

---

Another page.

Power supply.

---

Another page.

Cooling system.

---

Another page.

> "...I CAN BUILD THAT."

---

Miss Neko calmly stood up.

She reached over.

Closed the notebook.

> "...No."

---

Neko opened another notebook.

Miss Neko took that one too.

---

Norma, now curious.

> "...How many barrels?"

---

Miss Neko looked directly at Norma.

> "...Enough."

---

Norma smiled.

> "...Interesting."

---

Miss Neko immediately pressed the Engineering emergency intercom.

> "Belle."

A pause.

> "Please come to Engineering."

---

Belle arrived less than a minute later.

> "...What's wrong?"

Miss Neko silently pointed.

Norma.

Neko.

A whiteboard covered in sketches labeled:

> **"PROJECT: REALLY BIG GUN?"**

Belle looked at the board.

Then at Neko.

Then at Norma.

She sighed.

> "...No."

---

Norma raised a hand.

> "...Hypothetically—"

Belle answered before the sentence finished.

> "No."

---

Neko tried another angle.

> "...What if it was for research?"

> "...No."

---

> "...Mining?"

> "...No."

---

> "...Decorati—"

> "...Absolutely not."
