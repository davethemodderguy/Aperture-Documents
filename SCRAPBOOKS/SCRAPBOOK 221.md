## **SUBJECT: THE CORIN PARADOX**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – Executive & Research Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following prolonged observation of Corin Lockwood and Corey Lockwood, Research Division has formally designated their condition as:

> **The Corin Paradox**

Unlike previously documented complementary manifestations, Corin and Corey do not merely coexist.

Their continued existence appears to be fundamentally dependent upon one another.

Current evidence indicates they represent two possible developmental outcomes of the same individual, made physically manifest through an unknown anomalous process.

The mechanism remains unknown.

---

# **DEPENDENT EXISTENCE**

Medical examination has failed to identify either sister as the "primary" individual.

Research has therefore abandoned attempts to classify one as original and the other as derivative.

Instead, both are recognized as indispensable components of the same anomalous system.

The current working model concludes:

> **If Corin lives, Corey lives.**

> **If Corey lives, Corin lives.**

Neither can permanently outlive the other.

The phenomenon appears to maintain both individuals as long as either remains alive.

How this occurs is unknown.

Research has not authorized lethal testing, and no such testing will be conducted.

The conclusion is based solely on long-term anomalous observation.

---

# **TWO POSSIBLE STATES**

Psychological Services describe the sisters as representing two divergent outcomes of Corin's life.

### **Corin**

Embodies the life shaped by trauma.

Characteristics include:

- Anomalous physiology.
    
- Chalky lime hair.
    
- Pale skin.
    
- Extensive traumatic experience.
    
- Exceptional resilience despite suffering.
    

---

### **Corey**

Embodies the life Corin may have lived without that defining trauma.

Characteristics include:

- Ordinary human physiology.
    
- Natural black hair.
    
- Healthy complexion.
    
- Optimistic disposition.
    
- Strong emotional resilience.
    

Research emphasizes that Corey is **not** an illusion, hallucination, or imaginary construct.

She is a fully independent person.

However, her existence cannot presently be explained without reference to Corin.

---

# **MUTUAL COMPLEMENT**

Observation demonstrates that the sisters naturally compensate for one another's weaknesses.

### **Corey provides:**

- Emotional reassurance.
    
- Encouragement.
    
- Social confidence.
    
- Recognition of Corin's declining mental state.
    
- Consistent interruption of self-isolating behaviors.
    

Corey's greatest concern is Corin's emotional well-being.

---

### **Corin provides:**

- Mathematical expertise.
    
- Administrative precision.
    
- Technical knowledge.
    
- Practical judgment.
    
- Professional mentoring.
    

Corin frequently assists Corey with work requiring advanced accounting, logistics, and analytical reasoning.

Personnel often joke that Corey handles people while Corin handles numbers.

Neither disagrees.

---

# **SUBCONSCIOUS MANIFESTATION THEORY**

Research Division's leading hypothesis proposes that the original Corin's subconscious underwent an anomalous bifurcation.

Rather than producing two personalities within one body, the process manifested two complete individuals.

Each inherited emotional and cognitive qualities necessary to stabilize the other.

Corey appears to embody Corin's capacity for hope, warmth, and emotional recovery.

Corin retains the memories, experience, and professional competence forged through hardship.

Separated, each becomes progressively unstable.

Together, both function normally.

Research therefore concludes that Corey should not be regarded merely as Corin's companion.

She is an essential component of the paradox itself.

Corin and Corey continue residing together at Shelter T-01.

They continue working together at the Krampus Compliance Authority.

They continue arguing over insignificant matters, sharing responsibilities, and quietly supporting one another in ways few others notice.

Research has not solved the Corin Paradox.

One conclusion, however, is no longer disputed.

Corin and Corey are not simply sisters.

They are a life divided into two paths that now walk side by side, each ensuring the other can continue forward.
