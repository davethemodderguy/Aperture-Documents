# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **VISITOR ARRIVAL LOG**

### **FIRST INTERDIMENSIONAL VISIT – SECURITY ADVISOR ARCTURUS LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel Archive

---

# **VISITOR IDENTIFICATION**

**Name:** Arcturus Lockwood

**Callsign:** **GUNSLINGER**

**Position:** Security Advisor – Krampus Compliance Authority

For years, the Security Advisor was known only by his callsign.

Today, for the first time within Heaven's Blacksite, he introduced himself simply as:

> "Arcturus Lockwood."

The name spread quickly through the facility.

---

# **APPEARANCE**

Arcturus arrived wearing practical civilian attire beneath a long dark jacket.

Notable equipment included:

- A **blue fedora**.
    
- Rectangular glasses.
    
- A **.357 Magnum double-action revolver** carried on his right hip.
    
- A **modernized lever-action rifle chambered in .357 Magnum** slung across his back.
    
- A leather **bandolier of .357 Magnum cartridges**, concealed beneath his jacket.
    

Unlike many heavily armed visitors, Arcturus moved with a relaxed, almost effortless confidence.

Several guards admitted they nearly forgot he was carrying two firearms.

---

# **ARRIVAL**

Stepping through the dimensional gateway, Arcturus looked around Heaven's Blacksite.

He adjusted his glasses.

> "...So this is where Norma's been living."

Norma smiled.

> "Welcome to the Blacksite, big brother."

Arcturus looked at her for a second.

Then...

He smiled.

> "...Good to finally see the place."

---

# **MEETING – DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Director Lucious greeted him personally.

The two shook hands.

Lucious spoke first.

> "I've heard good things about the KCA's Security Advisor."

Arcturus chuckled.

> "Hopefully only the good ones."

The two quickly fell into conversation regarding organizational security and interdepartmental cooperation.

Observers noted the discussion lasted nearly forty minutes.

---

# **THE MOMENT**

Later that evening, while sharing tea in Project SAFEHOUSE, Belle casually mentioned:

> "...By the way..."

> "...Norma's Ether Regression Syndrome is gone."

Silence.

Arcturus slowly looked toward his younger sister.

> "...Gone?"

Norma nodded.

> "...Completely."

He didn't answer.

Instead...

He stood up.

Walked over.

And wrapped Norma in a tight hug.

For several long seconds...

He couldn't say a word.

When he finally spoke, his voice was noticeably unsteady.

> "...Fifteen years..."

He smiled, though his eyes had begun to water.

> "...You don't have to hurt anymore."

Norma hugged him back.

> "...I know."

> "...I'm okay now."

The room stayed quiet.

No one interrupted.
