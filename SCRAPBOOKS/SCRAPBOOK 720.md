### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / INCIDENT LOG**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — FIVE-HOUR EXPOSURE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Incident:** Loss of consciousness outside SAFEHOUSE perimeter  
**Status:** **RECOVERED / CRITICAL**

---

# **INCIDENT**

Alice had taken Neko's electric scooter toward the Treehouse.

The trip itself was uneventful.

The scooter's safety systems were functioning normally.

Alice eventually stopped near the Treehouse and dismounted.

At some point, she attempted to continue on foot.

The distance was not particularly large.

For Alice, it was enough.

Her stamina gave out.

She collapsed forward.

Fortunately, she fell into soft snow.

No major impact injury was immediately detected.

Alice nevertheless lost consciousness.

---

# **FIVE HOURS**

Alice remained outside for approximately **five hours**.

She was unconscious in the snow.

The weather remained cold.

Her body temperature fell dangerously.

Her already compromised metabolism had little reserve available to compensate for the exposure.

She did not wake.

She did not call for help.

She simply remained there.

---

# **THE TRACKER**

Alice was eventually located because of a feature Neko had installed months earlier.

The scooter contains an encrypted location tracker.

Neko had originally installed it for safety reasons.

It was never intended to monitor Alice.

It was intended to answer one question:

> **"Where is the scooter?"**

That question became much more important than expected.

When Alice failed to return, Neko checked the scooter's location.

The tracker showed that it had remained stationary for an abnormal length of time.

Neko immediately alerted SAFEHOUSE.

---

# **RECOVERY**

Neko reached the location first.

The scooter was still there.

Alice was several meters away.

Half-covered in snow.

Neko froze.

> **"Alice."**

No response.

He immediately called for medical assistance.

Corin arrived shortly afterward.

She dropped to her knees beside Alice.

> **"Alice..."**

Nothing.

Corin carefully brushed the snow away from Alice's face.

Her soft hat was covered in snow.

Her eyepatch remained in place.

Her white hair was damp and frozen against her face.

---

# **MEDICAL CONDITION**

Alice was unconscious but alive.

She was suffering from:

- Severe cold exposure
    
- Hypothermia
    
- Extreme exhaustion
    
- Dehydration
    
- Critically low nutritional reserves
    
- Further metabolic depletion
    

No major new head injury was detected.

The soft snow had likely prevented a more severe impact.

Her regeneration had already begun addressing minor injuries.

But the process was extremely slow.

Alice simply did not have much left for it to use.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin held Alice carefully.

> **"Why did you walk?"**

Alice was unconscious.

There was no answer.

Corin looked toward the scooter.

Then back at Alice.

She understood.

The scooter could carry her.

Alice simply decided to walk.

And her body had stopped her.

Again.

---

# **NEKO**

Neko stood beside the scooter.

He stared at the tracker display.

Then at Alice.

The device he built to give her mobility had ended up being the thing that allowed them to find her.

He quietly said:

> **"...Good thing I put that in."**

Nobody disagreed.

---

# **AFTER RECOVERY**

Alice eventually regained consciousness.

Her first words were barely audible.

> **"Sorry."**

Corin answered immediately:

> **"No."**

Alice blinked.

> **"I got tired."**

> **"I know."**

> **"Alice stopped."**

> **"I know."**

Alice looked frightened.

> **"Everyone worried?"**

Corin nodded.

> **"Yes."**

Alice's expression fell.

Corin quickly added:

> **"But we're glad we found you."**

Alice was quiet.

Then:

> **"...The scooter found Alice?"**

Neko answered from the doorway.

> **"Yeah."**

Alice looked toward him.

> **"Good scooter."**

Neko smiled.

> **"Very good scooter."**

---

# **NEW SAFETY MEASURES**

Following the incident, Neko implemented additional safeguards.

The scooter now:

- Detects prolonged inactivity
    
- Detects when Alice dismounts
    
- Monitors distance from the scooter
    
- Sends an alert if Alice remains away from the vehicle for too long
    
- Warns Alice when her estimated physical endurance is becoming dangerously low
    
- Automatically contacts SAFEHOUSE if Alice becomes unresponsive
    

The Treehouse remains within the authorized mobility perimeter.

The Blacksite perimeter remains completely inaccessible.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Alice didn't get lost.

She didn't intentionally disappear.

She simply ran out of strength.

Her body gave up before she reached somewhere safe.

Five hours passed before anyone found her.

The snow kept her from suffering a catastrophic impact.

Neko's tracker kept her from remaining there indefinitely.

And when Alice finally woke up, she wasn't worried about herself.

She asked:

> **"Everyone worried?"**

That question remains the clearest summary of Alice's current psychological condition.

Even after spending five hours unconscious in the snow—

her first concern was everyone else.
