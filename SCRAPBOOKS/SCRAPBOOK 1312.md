# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake's physical appearance has changed.

The alteration occurred following the recent unauthorized intrusion into his room.

No deliberate transformation was observed.

No known procedure was performed.

No known anomalous object was introduced into Jake's system.

The change appears to have occurred spontaneously.

Jake's hair has become **white**.

His eyes have changed from their previous appearance to a **bright azure glow**, with luminous streaks resembling **shooting stars**.

The ocular pattern is notably similar to that observed in **Invictus Aeterna**.

C.O.R.E. has not yet established whether this similarity is cosmetic, energetic, symbolic, or indicative of a deeper anomalous relationship.

---

# 1. INITIAL OBSERVATION

The first personnel member to notice the change was Neri.

She encountered Jake in the SAFEHOUSE hallway.

Jake was standing beside a window.

Neri stopped.

She looked at him.

Then looked again.

Jake noticed.

> **JAKE:**  
> "What?"

Neri pointed toward his hair.

Jake touched it.

He looked at his fingers.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...It's white."

Neri nodded.

Jake then looked toward the window reflection.

He became quiet.

---

# 2. HAIR ALTERATION

Jake's hair is now completely white.

The change is not consistent with ordinary aging.

No gradual pigmentation loss was recorded.

Available footage indicates that the alteration occurred between two observations.

At 09:14:

**Previous appearance.**

At 09:47:

**White hair.**

There is no footage showing a conventional transition.

The new pigmentation appears stable.

Attempts to determine whether the hair is biologically or anomalously generated have been deliberately limited.

C.O.R.E. Welfare Division has requested that Jake **not be subjected to unnecessary testing.**

---

# 3. OCULAR ALTERATION

The ocular alteration is considerably more unusual.

Jake's eyes now emit a soft azure light.

Within the glow are thin luminous streaks.

The streaks appear to move slowly across the visible surface of the eyes.

They resemble small shooting stars.

The effect is remarkably similar to Aeterna's eyes.

Aeterna's eyes are characterized by celestial shooting-star phenomena.

Jake's version is substantially smaller and less intense.

Nevertheless, visual comparison produced a strong correspondence.

---

# 4. FIRST CONTACT WITH AETERNA

Aeterna was notified.

She arrived approximately eleven minutes later.

Jake looked up at her.

Aeterna looked down at him.

Neither spoke initially.

Jake eventually pointed toward his own eyes.

> **JAKE:**  
> "They're doing that."

Aeterna examined them.

The shooting-star streaks moved slowly through Jake's azure irises.

Aeterna remained silent.

Jake asked:

> **JAKE:**  
> "Do yours do that?"

Aeterna's eyes brightened slightly.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Yes."

Jake looked at her.

Then at his reflection in the window.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Mine look like yours."

Aeterna nodded.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "They do."

Jake considered this.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Is that bad?"

Aeterna answered immediately.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "No."

Jake relaxed slightly.

---

# 5. NO KNOWN AETERNA TRANSFER

C.O.R.E. has found no evidence that Aeterna intentionally transferred energy, abilities, material, or other properties to Jake.

Aeterna herself denies performing any such action.

There was no direct contact immediately preceding the transformation.

Therefore, the similarity between their eyes should **not** currently be interpreted as evidence that Jake has become an Invictus.

He has not been designated an Invictus.

He remains classified as a **Weeping Angel**.

His established spiritual condition remains unchanged.

---

# 6. NO CHANGE TO WEEPING ANGEL STATUS

The following characteristics remain unchanged:

- Jake remains unable to return to the Starlands;
    
- Jake remains permanently bound to this reality;
    
- Jake remains wingless;
    
- Jake's sorrow remains persistent;
    
- his connection to the Starlands remains absent;
    
- his teddy bear remains his cardiac vessel;
    
- his physical materialization remains stable;
    
- his ability to eat remains intact;
    
- his physical body remains functional;
    
- no known cure or reversal exists.
    

The appearance alteration has not changed these findings.

---

# 7. THE TEDDY BEAR

Jake's teddy bear appears unaffected.

Its fabric remains physically real.

Its heartbeat remains Jake's actual heartbeat.

During the initial discovery of the transformation, the bear's cardiac rhythm remained normal.

This is important.

There was no corresponding cardiac crisis.

Jake himself also showed no immediate distress.

He was primarily confused.

---

# 8. RESPONSE FROM NORA

Nora was considerably less analytical.

She saw Jake.

Stopped.

Stared.

Then:

> **NORA:**  
> "Your hair!"

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Yes."

Nora approached.

She looked at his eyes.

Her own expression became fascinated.

> **NORA:**  
> "Your eyes too!"

Jake blinked.

> **JAKE:**  
> "They're weird."

Nora shook her head.

> **NORA:**  
> "They're cool."

Jake looked away.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Okay."

Nora smiled.

> **NORA:**  
> "You look like Aeterna."

From several meters away:

> **AETERNA:**  
> "He does."

Jake quietly sighed.

---

# 9. RESPONSE FROM NERI

Neri's response was considerably more restrained.

She examined Jake for approximately ten seconds.

Then she pointed at his hair.

Then his eyes.

Then Aeterna.

Then Jake again.

Jake understood the comparison.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I don't know."

Neri nodded.

She handed him a small mirror.

Jake looked at himself.

For several seconds, he said nothing.

Then:

> **JAKE:**  
> "I look different."

Neri nodded.

Jake continued looking.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I still look like me."

Neri nodded again.

That appeared to be sufficient.

---

# 10. C.O.R.E. ANALYSIS

Several possible explanations remain under investigation.

### Hypothesis A — Manifestation Stabilization

The alteration may represent a change in Jake's physical manifestation following his materialization.

### Hypothesis B — Anomalous Adaptation

Jake's body may be adapting to its new physical state.

### Hypothesis C — Environmental Influence

The alteration may be related to prolonged exposure to other anomalous residents.

### Hypothesis D — Aeterna Resonance

The visual similarity to Aeterna raises the possibility of an energetic or metaphysical resonance.

This remains unconfirmed.

### Hypothesis E — Unknown

C.O.R.E. cannot currently exclude an entirely unrelated mechanism.

---

# 11. IMPORTANT LIMITATION

The resemblance should not be exaggerated.

Jake does **not** currently possess:

- Aeterna's armor;
    
- Aeterna's sword;
    
- Aeterna's physical durability;
    
- Aeterna's Invictus classification;
    
- Aeterna's historical abilities;
    
- Aeterna's existential weapon;
    
- or any confirmed portion of Aeterna's power.
    

The similarity currently concerns:

> **Hair pigmentation and ocular appearance.**

Nothing more has been established.

---

# 12. RESIDENT WELFARE NOTE

Jake has already experienced repeated situations in which his appearance became the subject of unwanted attention.

Personnel are therefore reminded:

Do not stare.

Do not repeatedly ask him whether he is becoming "like Aeterna."

Do not touch his hair without permission.

Do not shine lights into his eyes.

Do not attempt to reproduce the effect.

Do not tell him that he looks frightening.

And especially:

Do not use the alteration as an excuse to surprise him.

The sign on his door still applies.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING OBSERVATION

Jake eventually returned to his room.

He sat on his bedroll.

His teddy bear rested against his back.

The small azure glow from his eyes illuminated the pages of his book.

The shooting-star streaks moved slowly across his irises.

For the first time, the reflection looking back at him was not the same child he remembered.

White hair.

Azure eyes.

Celestial light.

A face that looked slightly less like the frightened boy who had once wandered the Blacksite at night.

Jake stared at himself for a while.

Then he touched his hair.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Still me."

He picked up his book.

The door closed.

A few seconds later:

**Knock. Knock.**

Jake looked up.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Who?"

From outside:

> **Aeterna:**  
> "Aeterna."

A pause.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Come in."

The door opened.

Aeterna entered.

For several seconds, the two stood together beneath the room's dim light.

White hair.

Azure shooting-star eyes.

Two very different beings.

Yet, visually, strangely similar.

Jake looked up at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "We're matching."

Aeterna paused.

Then nodded.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Apparently."

Jake returned to his book.

The shooting stars in his eyes continued moving.
