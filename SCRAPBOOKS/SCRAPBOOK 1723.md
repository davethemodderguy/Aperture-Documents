# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has received a new mattress.

The replacement was authorized after an inspection of his previous mattress revealed that it had become **heavily saturated with glowing blue dust**.

The material was present across the mattress surface, embedded within the fabric, and accumulated in substantial quantities beneath it.

Initial observations indicate that prolonged exposure to the residue can cause hallucinations.

The precise mechanism remains unknown.

C.O.R.E. has therefore classified the old mattress as a potentially hazardous anomalous object pending further investigation.

Jake has been informed that the mattress was replaced for safety reasons.

He has not been informed that his room is being treated as an active source-investigation site.

This distinction was deliberate.

**Jake is a resident. He is not a research specimen.**

---

# 1. DISCOVERY

The problem was identified during a routine inspection of Jake's sleeping area.

A maintenance employee noticed a faint blue glow beneath the old mattress.

Initially, the light was attributed to Jake's naturally occurring anomalous residue.

Further inspection revealed that the material had accumulated far beyond the quantities previously documented.

The dust was present:

- across the upper mattress fabric;
    
- within the seams;
    
- beneath the mattress;
    
- around the bedroll;
    
- and in several concentrated patches on the floor.
    

The mattress appeared to have absorbed the material over an extended period.

When a maintenance employee leaned closer to inspect one of the brighter patches, they reported seeing movement in the corner of their vision.

They turned.

Nothing was there.

They looked back at the mattress.

The blue particles appeared to move.

The employee withdrew from the room and reported the incident.

A second employee subsequently experienced the same visual disturbance.

At that point, the inspection was suspended.

---

# 2. INITIAL HAZARD ASSESSMENT

The residue resembles the blue dust previously observed around Jake's old sleeping area.

However, its effects appear to extend beyond simple illumination or ordinary physical contact.

Reported symptoms include:

- peripheral movement where no movement is present;
    
- fleeting silhouettes;
    
- visual distortions;
    
- apparent movement in stationary objects;
    
- and brief hallucinations involving objects or figures that are not physically present.
    

The symptoms varied between affected personnel.

No consistent hallucination has yet been identified.

One employee reported seeing a cat sitting on the mattress.

Another insisted that the room's ceiling had opened onto a star-filled sky.

A third reported hearing someone quietly asking where their shoes were.

No shoes were missing.

No cat was present.

C.O.R.E. has not established whether the hallucinations originate from the dust itself, prolonged exposure, an anomalous response to the observer, or another mechanism entirely.

The material is not to be deliberately inhaled, touched, or used in unsupervised experiments.

---

# 3. THE MATTRESS

The old mattress has been removed from Jake's sleeping area.

It has not been destroyed.

Given the uncertain nature of the residue, immediate destruction could produce unpredictable effects or disperse the material into the surrounding environment.

The mattress is currently isolated in a designated containment room.

Access is restricted to authorized anomalous-material personnel.

Personnel have been instructed not to sleep on it, sit on it, or conduct informal experiments.

One proposed disposal method involved simply throwing the mattress into an industrial incinerator.

Neko rejected the proposal.

**NEKO:**  
"We do not know whether the dust burns, evaporates, multiplies, or turns the incinerator into a second mattress."

**MAINTENANCE:**  
"Could it do that?"

**NEKO:**  
"That is precisely why we are not finding out by accident."

The proposal was withdrawn.

---

# 4. JAKE RECEIVES A REPLACEMENT

Jake was present when the replacement mattress arrived.

He examined it carefully.

He pressed one hand against the surface.

Then he sat down.

He bounced once.

Then twice.

He looked at Bao.

**JAKE:**  
"Soft."

**BAO:**  
"Good. That's what a mattress is supposed to be."

Jake lay down.

He stared at the ceiling for several seconds.

**JAKE:**  
"This one's clean."

Bao paused.

**BAO:**  
"Yes. It is."

Jake turned onto his side and hugged his teddy bear.

**JAKE:**  
"Can I keep it?"

**BAO:**  
"Of course you can keep it."

A brief pause followed.

**JAKE:**  
"The old one too?"

Bao looked toward the containment team waiting outside.

Then back at Jake.

**BAO:**  
"We're going to keep the old one somewhere safe while we figure out what's happening to it."

Jake considered this answer.

**JAKE:**  
"Okay."

He did not argue.

He did, however, look toward the door several times after the containment team left.

C.O.R.E. notes that Jake appears to regard the old mattress as a personal possession rather than a potential hazard.

Personnel are reminded not to treat this attachment as a behavioral problem.

---

# 5. RESIDENT WELFARE CONSIDERATIONS

The mattress was Jake's.

It had been part of his private living space for an extended period.

Its replacement was necessary, but the decision should not imply that Jake himself is dangerous.

The residue is anomalous material associated with his presence.

That does not establish that Jake intentionally produced the hallucination effect, understood the risk, or had any reason to suspect that the mattress had become hazardous.

Jake has not been reprimanded.

He has not been required to surrender his other belongings.

His room has not been stripped of its furnishings.

The replacement mattress was installed without changing the overall arrangement of his sleeping area.

His books, drawings, plushies, and other personal possessions remain where he expects them to be.

The objective is to make his room safe without making him feel that his room is being taken away from him.

---

# 6. THE BLUE DUST INVESTIGATION

The discovery has raised several new questions.

### Question A — Accumulation

Why did the residue accumulate so heavily around the old mattress?

Was the mattress simply an effective surface for collecting the material, or did prolonged contact alter the dust's properties?

### Question B — Exposure Duration

Did the hallucination effect develop gradually?

Could personnel have experienced milder symptoms without recognizing them as anomalous?

### Question C — Jake's Exposure

Has Jake been exposed to the same effect while sleeping?

If so, does the material affect him differently from other residents?

Jake has not reported persistent hallucinations.

However, the absence of a report does not establish that he is unaffected.

A welfare assessment will be conducted through ordinary, non-coercive conversation rather than repeated questioning.

### Question D — Environmental Persistence

Can the residue contaminate other materials?

The replacement mattress will be monitored for anomalous deposits, but no assumption has been made that the material will necessarily accumulate in the same way.

### Question E — Relationship to Existing Anomalies

The residue's properties may be related to Jake's unusual nature as a literal star manifested in human form.

This remains a hypothesis.

C.O.R.E. will not assume that every unusual property of the dust is a direct expression of stellar physics.

**The material is anomalous. Ordinary physical explanations have not yet been established.**

# 7. INCIDENT LOG — THE HALLWAY

During the initial containment operation, two personnel members reported seeing a small figure standing near the end of the corridor outside Jake's room.

The figure appeared to have white hair.

Both employees initially believed Jake had left his room.

They called to him.

The figure did not respond.

When the employees approached, the corridor was empty.

Both subsequently reported nausea and visual distortion.

C.O.R.E. has not established whether the event was a shared hallucination, an anomalous projection, or an unrelated occurrence.

Jake was located in the cafeteria at the time.

He was eating cake.

When informed that personnel had apparently seen him in two places at once, he looked confused.

**JAKE:**  
"I was eating."

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"We know."

**JAKE:**  
"Then why were they looking for me?"

The officer did not have a particularly satisfying answer.

---

# 8. C.O.R.E. DIRECTIVES

Effective immediately:

1. The old mattress remains in controlled containment.
    
2. Personnel experiencing visual disturbances must leave the exposure area and report their symptoms.
    
3. No resident is to be deliberately exposed to the residue for testing.
    
4. No attempt is to be made to recreate the hallucinations without an approved safety assessment.
    
5. The replacement mattress is to be inspected periodically for anomalous residue.
    
6. Jake is to retain access to his room and personal belongings.
    
7. Personnel must not imply that Jake is responsible for the effects simply because the dust is associated with him.
    
8. Any future residue accumulation is to be documented without disturbing Jake's belongings unnecessarily.
    

C.O.R.E. Research has been instructed to determine whether the material can be handled and contained safely before investigating its full capabilities.

The investigation is not a justification for turning Jake's bedroom into a laboratory.

# 9. PRIVATE WELFARE NOTE

Later that evening, Bao checked on Jake.

He was lying on his new mattress with his teddy bear tucked beneath one arm.

His old mattress had been removed.

His room was otherwise unchanged.

**BAO:**  
"How's the new bed?"

**JAKE:**  
"Good."

**BAO:**  
"Anything bothering you?"

Jake thought about it.

**JAKE:**  
"No."

Bao sat beside him.

After a moment, Jake asked:

**JAKE:**  
"Will the old one get better?"

Bao did not answer immediately.

**BAO:**  
"We don't know yet, sweetheart."

Jake nodded.

**JAKE:**  
"Okay."

He looked down at his teddy bear.

**JAKE:**  
"Don't let anyone sleep on it, though."

Bao's expression changed slightly.

**BAO:**  
"We won't."

**JAKE:**  
"It might show them weird things."

**BAO:**  
"That's why we're keeping it somewhere safe."

Jake seemed satisfied.

He settled beneath his blanket.

A few seconds later, he spoke again.

**JAKE:**  
"Can I have cake tomorrow?"

Bao sighed.

**BAO:**  
"Yes, Jake."

**JAKE:**  
"Chocolate?"

**BAO:**  
"Yes."

**JAKE:**  
"Okay. Goodnight."

**BAO:**  
"Goodnight, sweetheart."

Jake closed his eyes.

For the first time since the investigation began, the room was quiet.

No blue glow seeped through the mattress fabric.

No one reported seeing figures in the corners.

No stars appeared on the ceiling that were not supposed to be there.

There was only a six-year-old boy, his teddy bear, and a clean mattress.

For now, that was enough.

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING ASSESSMENT

The old mattress is not currently considered safe for ordinary use.

The blue residue has demonstrated a potential to cause hallucinations, and its full effects remain unknown.

The new mattress has been installed successfully.

Jake's sleeping arrangements have been restored without unnecessary disruption to his daily routine.

The investigation remains active.

**One final distinction must be maintained:**

The mattress became hazardous.

Jake did not.
