## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / FELINE FORM UPDATE**

# **CORIN — VOCALIZATION RESTORED**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Form:** Miniature Feline  
**Status:** **Recovering / Vocalization Restored**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin can **meow again**.

After losing her ability to vocalize during the worst period of her mercury poisoning, her feline vocalizations have gradually returned.

Her first successful meow was quiet and somewhat weak.

But it was unmistakably:

> **"Mew."**

The SAFEHOUSE residents immediately recognized it.

---

# **FIRST MEOW**

Corin was resting in someone's hands when her ears suddenly perked up.

She opened her mouth.

For a moment, nothing happened.

Then:

> **"...mew."**

Everyone in the room stopped.

Belle immediately smiled.

Orphie looked relieved.

Neko simply said:

> **"She's back."**

Corin then gave another, slightly louder:

> **"Mew."**

---

# **VOCAL RECOVERY**

Her voice is still weaker than before.

She cannot yet produce loud or sustained vocalizations.

However, she can now communicate through:

- Meows.
    
- Quiet chirps.
    
- Small feline noises.
    
- Body language.
    
- Wing movements.
    
- Ear and tail positioning.
    

This has made understanding her considerably easier.

The Architects are no longer required for every minor request.

---

# **CONTAINER PREFERENCE**

Unfortunately, Corin has **not** abandoned her habit of entering small containers.

If anything, her ability to meow again has made the behavior more obvious.

Corin still enjoys enclosed spaces.

She particularly likes:

- Glass jars.
    
- Small boxes.
    
- Open containers.
    
- Sheltered corners.
    
- Anything that feels enclosed and secure.
    

She often curls herself into the smallest possible space, folds her wings against her body, and simply sits there.

---

# **THE NEW PROBLEM**

Personnel can now hear Corin when she becomes stuck.

This has proven useful.

A faint:

> **"Mew."**

coming from inside a cabinet is generally a reliable indication that Corin has found another place she was not supposed to be.

---

# **JAR INCIDENT**

Corin was once discovered inside a glass jar again.

This time, everyone heard:

> **"Mew."**

Belle immediately walked over.

She looked through the glass.

Corin was sitting comfortably at the bottom with her wings folded.

Belle opened the container.

Corin did not leave.

Belle waited.

Corin looked at her.

> **"...Mew."**

Apparently, she was comfortable.

---

# **CONTAINER SAFETY**

SAFEHOUSE personnel have been instructed not to allow Corin to remain inside sealed containers.

She may enjoy enclosed spaces, but containers must remain:

- Open.
    
- Well ventilated.
    
- Large enough for her to move.
    
- Easy to exit.
    
- Free of anything that could injure her wings.
    

Corin's preference for confined spaces is considered harmless as long as she can leave whenever she wants.

---

# **CURRENT RECOVERY**

Her vocal recovery is considered another positive sign.

Corin remains physically weak and continues recovering slowly from her previous poisoning.

She still requires:

- Adequate hydration.
    
- Small meals.
    
- Plenty of rest.
    
- Minimal strenuous activity.
    

But she is becoming more expressive again.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin can finally meow again.

The tiny sound has become one of the most welcome noises in SAFEHOUSE.

Unfortunately, the first thing everyone learned after her voice returned was that she still really likes jars.

So now, when someone hears:

> **"Mew..."**

coming from somewhere suspicious, the first response is no longer panic.

It's:

> **"Where did Corin put herself this time?"**

**End of Anomalous Physiology / Feline Form Update**
