**SUBJECT:** Neri Z-4C-NERI  
**INCIDENT:** Accidental confinement in equipment locker  
**DURATION:** Approximately 25+ hours  
**PRIMARY INVOLVED PARTY:** Alice

---

## Executive Summary

Plush Neri was accidentally left inside an equipment locker by Alice.

The locker was subsequently closed and secured.

Alice forgot that Neri was inside.

Neri remained inside the locker for **over 25 hours** before being discovered.

No permanent injury occurred.

No significant medical deterioration was identified upon recovery.

The incident nevertheless represents a serious failure of the existing precautions surrounding Alice's persistent memory anomaly and Neri's unusually small physical profile while in plush form.

---

## Incident Reconstruction

### T+00:00 — Initial Placement

Alice was working in an equipment storage area while carrying Plush Neri.

Neri was reportedly sitting against Alice's clothing rather than being actively held.

At some point, Alice opened an equipment locker.

Neri entered the locker.

The reason for entry is unclear.

Possible explanations include:

- Neri voluntarily climbed inside;
    
- Alice placed her inside temporarily;
    
- Neri entered while Alice was retrieving equipment.
    

Available footage does not establish malicious intent or deliberate confinement.

Alice closed the locker.

She then left the area.

Within a relatively short period, Alice's memory of Neri's presence was lost.

---

## T+03:00 to T+12:00

No personnel reported Neri missing.

Neri remained inside the locker.

Because she was in plush form, she was capable of remaining stationary for extended periods without the ordinary discomfort associated with prolonged confinement.

She reportedly spent portions of the period:

- sitting;
    
- lying down;
    
- sleeping;
    
- repositioning herself;
    
- apparently doing nothing.
    

No attempt to force the locker open was detected.

---

## T+12:00 to T+25:00

The locker remained closed.

Neri continued to remain inside.

At no point did she activate a general emergency alarm.

This is consistent with established behavior.

Neri has historically been reluctant to interrupt personnel or create what she perceives as unnecessary trouble.

Additionally, she was not experiencing a medical emergency at the time.

Therefore, from Neri's perspective, there was apparently no sufficiently urgent reason to demand assistance.

---

## Discovery

The locker was eventually opened during routine equipment retrieval.

The employee initially believed the locker contained only stored equipment.

Instead, a very small figure wearing an oversized hoodie was found sitting inside.

Neri reportedly looked at the employee.

The employee looked at Neri.

Neri reportedly said:

> "...Hi."

The employee immediately contacted SAFEHOUSE personnel.

---

## Recovery

Neri was removed from the locker without resistance.

She was examined by welfare personnel.

### Findings

- No major injury.
    
- No acute anemic episode identified.
    
- No evidence of dehydration requiring emergency intervention.
    
- No significant respiratory abnormality.
    
- No structural injury.
    
- Plush form remained stable.
    
- Neri was tired but responsive.
    
- Neri did not appear frightened.
    

When asked how long she had been inside, Neri did not know.

When asked whether she wanted to leave the locker, she nodded.

When asked whether she had been trying to get someone's attention, she shook her head.

---

## Alice's Involvement

Alice was informed after Neri had been recovered.

Alice initially did not remember putting Neri in the locker.

Upon being shown the relevant footage, Alice reportedly became extremely distressed.

She repeatedly asked:

> "I left her there?"

Neri was present during the conversation.

Alice approached her.

Neri did not retreat.

She also did not display hostility.

After several seconds, Neri climbed onto Alice's clothing herself.

This interaction was not interpreted as Neri forgetting the incident.

Rather, Neri appears to have chosen not to treat it as a personal betrayal.

This is consistent with the current state of their relationship.

---

## Important Distinction

Personnel are reminded:

**Neri being unharmed does not make the incident acceptable.**

Her plush form provides considerable protection from ordinary environmental hazards.

It does not eliminate:

- confinement risks;
    
- environmental hazards;
    
- accidental crushing;
    
- temperature hazards;
    
- equipment activation;
    
- chemical exposure;
    
- suffocation hazards;
    
- delayed discovery during an actual medical emergency.
    

A locker is therefore **not an acceptable storage location for Neri.**

This should not require clarification.

It apparently does.

---

## Revised Handling Protocol

Effective immediately:

### 1. Equipment lockers are not approved Neri locations.

If Neri enters one voluntarily, personnel must ensure she exits before closing it.

### 2. Check small enclosed spaces.

Before closing:

- lockers;
    
- cabinets;
    
- equipment cases;
    
- storage bins;
    
- maintenance compartments;
    

personnel should visually confirm that Neri is not inside.

### 3. Alice-specific precaution

Because Alice can lose memories involving Neri without warning, Alice must not be considered a reliable confirmation that Neri is no longer present.

A second person should verify Neri's location whenever Alice is storing or transporting equipment in areas containing small enclosed spaces.

### 4. Do not punish Neri.

Neri did not intentionally create a safety incident.

She is not to be blamed for being forgotten.

### 5. Do not overcorrect.

Neri is still allowed to move independently and occupy spaces of her choosing.

The objective is not to restrict her movement.

The objective is to prevent another **25-hour locker incident.**

---

## Post-Incident Observation

Approximately thirty minutes after recovery, Neri was found sitting on a nearby bench drinking coffee.

She appeared substantially more interested in the coffee than in the preceding day's events.

A technician asked:

> "Neri, were you uncomfortable in there?"

Neri considered the question.

She shook her head.

After a pause, she added quietly:

> "...Boring."

The technician reportedly laughed.

Neri then returned to drinking her coffee.

---

**Psychological response:** No acute distress observed.

Again.

This time, she was found.

That distinction matters.
