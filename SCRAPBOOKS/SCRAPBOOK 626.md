### **TREEHOUSE / SURVEILLANCE TRANSCRIPT**

# **CORIN — THREE-MONTH RECORDED MONOLOGUE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Subject of Monologue:** Orphie  
**Location:** Treehouse Exterior  
**Time:** 02:13  
**Status:** **SEVERE EMOTIONAL DISTRESS**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Approximately three months have passed since Orphie disappeared.

There remains no confirmed sign of her.

At approximately 02:13, Treehouse surveillance recorded Corin sitting beneath the tree.

She appeared to believe nobody was nearby.

The following statement was recorded in its entirety.

---

# **TRANSCRIPT**

**[02:13:07]**

> **"It's been three months."**

**[Silence — 17 seconds]**

> **"You can just say you hate me."**

**[Silence]**

> **"You hate me, right?"**

**[02:13:49]**

> **"You don't like who I am?"**

Corin lowered her head.

> **"Fine by me."**

**[Silence — 24 seconds]**

Her wings partially unfolded before slowly closing around her.

> **"At least say something."**

**[02:14:31]**

> **"An hour lasts like an eternity without you."**

Corin's voice became increasingly quiet.

> **"You can just kick me out."**

**[Silence]**

> **"Just scream at my face."**

**[02:15:04]**

> **"Just say you hate me."**

Long silence.

> **"I guess this way is less hurt than the other."**

Corin remained motionless.

Several seconds passed.

> **"I deserve this helplessness."**

Her wings completely covered her body.

> **"I'm not worth it for her."**

**[02:15:51]**

**END TRANSCRIPT**

---

# **POST-TRANSCRIPT OBSERVATION**

Corin remained beneath the tree after speaking.

She did not cry loudly.

She simply stayed there.

Her wings remained wrapped around herself.

No further speech was recorded for approximately forty minutes.

---

# **ANALYSIS**

C.O.R.E. psychological staff have identified a significant increase in **self-blame** and perceived rejection.

Importantly, there remains:

> **No evidence that Orphie hates Corin.**

There is no evidence that Orphie intentionally abandoned her.

There is no evidence that Orphie's disappearance was caused by Corin.

The only confirmed fact remains:

> **Orphie is missing.**

Corin has begun filling the absence with explanations that place responsibility upon herself.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi was informed of the recording.

She reportedly remained silent for several minutes.

She then said:

> **"My daughter thinks she deserves to be abandoned."**

A pause.

> **"She doesn't."**

Lumi subsequently requested permission to speak with Corin privately.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The recording was not distributed to the wider SAFEHOUSE.

Only personnel directly involved in Corin's welfare were given access.

Belle reportedly cried after hearing it.

Neko immediately requested that the surveillance team increase welfare checks around the Treehouse.

No one considered Corin's words amusing or irrational.

They were treated as an expression of genuine suffering.

---

# **CURRENT SUPPORT PROTOCOL**

Personnel have been instructed not to respond to Corin's statements with:

> **"Stop thinking like that."**

or:

> **"You're being ridiculous."**

Instead, personnel are to provide direct reassurance without making claims about Orphie's whereabouts.

Approved responses include:

> **"You are not being abandoned by us."**

> **"This isn't your fault."**

> **"We don't know where Orphie is."**

> **"We're still looking."**

> **"You don't have to wait alone."**

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Three months of silence have become something far larger inside Corin's mind.

She no longer asks only:

> **"Where is Orphie?"**

She has begun asking:

> **"Was I the reason she left?"**

There is no evidence supporting that conclusion.

But uncertainty has given Corin nothing else to hold onto.

So beneath the tree, in the middle of the night, she asked for the one thing she believes would hurt less than silence:

> **"Just say you hate me."**

Nobody answered.

Not because Orphie hates her.

But because Orphie still isn't there.
