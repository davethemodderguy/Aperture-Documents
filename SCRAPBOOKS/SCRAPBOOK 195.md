# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – SHELTER T-01 ISOLATION EVENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the resurgence of Corin Lockwood's trauma symptoms, personnel observed a significant behavioral change at Shelter T-01.

The most concerning development was not anger.

It was withdrawal.

Corin began believing that her presence was harmful to those around her.

This belief extended beyond people.

It extended to the animals that had chosen to stay beside her.

---

# **THE INCIDENT**

During an evening period of emotional distress, Corin became overwhelmed by memories of her past.

The white tiger and the snowy owl, normally constant companions, approached the treehouse as they had done countless times before.

However, Corin reacted differently.

She raised her voice.

She told them to leave.

Not out of hatred.

Out of fear.

Fear that they would eventually see her the way she saw herself.

---

# **THE TIGER**

The white tiger immediately recognized the change in Corin's behavior.

Unlike unfamiliar personnel, it did not become aggressive.

It simply stopped.

The animal watched her for several moments before slowly backing away from the treehouse.

It did not leave because it was afraid of Corin.

It left because Corin was asking it to.

That distinction was noted by Veterinary Services.

---

# **THE OWL**

The snowy owl reacted similarly.

Normally, it would have remained on the nearby branch, waiting for food or simply observing.

That night, it remained silent.

It did not steal anything.

It did not approach.

It simply flew to another tree.

For the first time since its arrival at Shelter T-01...

The owl chose distance.

---

# **AFTERMATH**

The silence that followed was immediate.

No wings above the treehouse.

No heavy footsteps beneath it.

No familiar presence waiting outside.

Corin had achieved exactly what she believed she wanted.

She was alone.

And the result was worse than she expected.

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Personnel later noted that Corin's condition deteriorated after the incident.

The absence of the animals removed two of the few sources of comfort she accepted without hesitation.

She had not lost them because they stopped caring.

She had pushed them away because she believed she did not deserve their care.

---

# **LUMI**

When Lumi arrived, she found Corin sitting quietly outside the treehouse.

The usual sounds were missing.

No tiger beneath the tree.

No owl on the branches.

Lumi did not immediately speak.

She simply sat beside Corin.

After a long silence, Corin whispered:

> "I scared them away."

Lumi looked toward the empty branches.

Then back at her daughter.

> "No."

> "You were hurting."

Corin looked down.

> "Same thing."

Lumi shook her head.

> "No."

> "It isn't."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The white tiger and snowy owl have not abandoned Shelter T-01.

They remain within the surrounding forest.

They have simply given Corin space.

Veterinary Services believe both animals are likely waiting for the same thing they have always waited for.

For Corin to stop believing that she has to be alone.

The treehouse is quiet now.

But the branches are not empty forever.

And the snow outside still carries tracks leading back home.

**End of Psychological Incident Report**
