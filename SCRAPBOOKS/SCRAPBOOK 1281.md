# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The rumored new Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise has been confirmed.

It is not merely a lever-action rifle.

It is the:

# **BULLSEYE**

A highly modular lever-action platform designed by **Neri Tachyon**, chambered in the proprietary:

> **.50 TE — Tachyon Express**

Only **six** Bullseye rifles currently exist.

One belongs to Nora Tachyon.

The remaining **five** are available for controlled trial distribution.

There will be no additional units unless Tachyon Industries authorizes another production run.

Nora has been placed in charge of distributing the five trial units.

This decision was made by Neri.

Nobody is entirely sure why.

Nora is extremely pleased about it.

---

# 1. THE BULLSEYE

The Bullseye is Tachyon Industries' first dedicated lever-action Limited Merchandise platform.

Its design philosophy is:

> **Classic mechanism. Modern modularity. Tachyon engineering.**

The rifle retains the recognizable lever-action identity that Nora has become particularly fond of while incorporating a highly configurable external architecture.

The result is considerably more adaptable than a conventional lever-action platform.

---

# 2. .50 TE — TACHYON EXPRESS

The standard chambering is:

> **.50 TE**

or:

> **.50 Tachyon Express**

The cartridge is a proprietary Tachyon Industries ammunition standard.

Despite the designation, **.50 TE is not intended to be treated as an ordinary real-world cartridge designation.**

It exists specifically within the Tachyon Industries equipment ecosystem.

The Bullseye's modular architecture also allows approved caliber configurations to be used during authorized trials.

---

# 3. MODULAR PLATFORM

Neri deliberately designed the Bullseye around configurability.

Authorized configurations can change several major components.

### OPTICS

The Bullseye can accept different approved optic configurations.

### RAILS

Its external rail configuration can be changed according to the intended setup.

### GRIPS

The grip configuration can be replaced.

### BARRELS

Approved barrel configurations can be swapped.

### STOCK

The stock is replaceable.

More importantly:

> **The stock folds.**

This was one of Nora's requirements.

---

# 4. THE FOLDING STOCK

The folding stock has a practical purpose.

It also has a very specific secondary purpose.

Nora.

During development, Nora asked whether the rifle could be configured to make her preferred lever-action flourish easier.

Neri asked:

> **“You mean the flick-cocking?”**

Nora nodded.

Neri stared at her.

Then, apparently against her better judgment, incorporated the requirement into the design.

The folding stock provides Nora with a configuration that allows her characteristic lever-action movement to remain comfortable and visually unobstructed.

The feature has subsequently been described by one Armory technician as:

> **“The most Nora engineering requirement imaginable.”**

---

# 5. NERI'S DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Neri's design notes contain a surprisingly simple explanation.

> **“The point is modularity.”**

She wanted the Bullseye to avoid becoming a weapon that was permanently locked into one configuration.

Instead, the same platform can be adapted for different authorized users and roles.

The external configuration can therefore change substantially without requiring an entirely different Tachyon product.

Neri summarized the concept as:

> **“One platform. Different configurations.”**

Nora summarized it as:

> **“One really cool lever gun.”**

Neri did not dispute this.

# 7. NORA'S ROLE

Nora has been assigned responsibility for the five trial units.

Her responsibilities include:

- presenting the Bullseye to prospective users;
    
- recording trial participants;
    
- observing user feedback;
    
- reporting unusual issues;
    
- returning trial units to Tachyon Industries;
    
- and determining whether the candidate understands what makes the Bullseye different from conventional equipment.
    

The final approval authority remains with Tachyon Industries.

Nora does **not** independently authorize permanent ownership.

She simply manages the trial process.

This distinction was explained to Nora several times.

Nora understands it.

Probably.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The Bullseye represents a significant expansion of Tachyon Industries' Limited Merchandise program.

Its most notable feature is not its limited production count.

It is its flexibility.

A single platform can accept multiple approved configurations, allowing it to serve different users without abandoning its defining lever-action identity.

Neri's engineering philosophy is evident throughout the design.

Nora's influence is also evident.

Perhaps more than either of them would like to admit.

The rifle is modular.

The rifle is limited.

The rifle is distinctly Tachyon.

And the folding stock exists, at least partially, because one six-year-old shark insisted that:

> **“Lever guns should be tacticool.”**
