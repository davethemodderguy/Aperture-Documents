# 1. THE QUESTION

Neri's proposal was presented to the active contestants.

The two options were displayed on the Cargo Bay 4 announcement board.

### OPTION A

Increase the haystack to:

> **50,000,000,000 pieces**

Winner receives:

> **SOH-004 — Sleight of Hand**

**AND**

> **NS-009 — Night Stalker**

---

### OPTION B

Keep the existing:

> **25,000,000,000 pieces of hay**

Winner receives:

> **SOH-004 — Sleight of Hand**

NS-009 remains docked.

---

# 2. IMMEDIATE REACTION

There was silence.

Not because nobody understood the question.

Because everyone understood it immediately.

One contestant finally spoke.

> **CONTESTANT:**  
> “She's asking us whether we want to double the haystack.”

Another looked toward the enormous pile.

> **SECOND CONTESTANT:**  
> “Yes.”

> **CONTESTANT:**  
> “For another gun.”

> **SECOND CONTESTANT:**  
> “A Night Stalker.”

Pause.

> **CONTESTANT:**  
> “...That's worse.”

# 5. THE ARGUMENT

The contestants eventually divided into two camps.

### CAMP ONE

> **“KEEP IT AT TWENTY-FIVE BILLION.”**

Their argument:

> “We're already searching through twenty-five billion.”

> “Why make it worse?”

> “The Sleight of Hand is already an extremely rare prize.”

> “At some point, the Night Stalker isn't worth another twenty-five billion pieces of hay.”

---

### CAMP TWO

> **“FIFTY BILLION.”**

Their argument:

> “It's a Tachyon family heirloom.”

> “There are only ten.”

> “One just became available.”

> “When are we ever going to get another chance?”

> “We're already here.”

The final argument became particularly popular:

> **“We're already here.”**

---

The competition is now officially expanded.

### FALSE TARGETS

### TRUE TARGET

> **1 Nekonium needle**

### EQUIPMENT

> **None**

### MAGNETS

> **Useless**

### WIN CONDITION

Locate the **single Nekonium needle.**

# 11. PRIZE

The winner will receive **both**:

> **SOH-004 — SLEIGHT OF HAND**

and

> **NS-009 — NIGHT STALKER**

NS-009 will therefore no longer remain docked if the competition produces a winner.

The two weapons will become the largest prize package yet offered through the Sleight of Hand program.

# 13. THE HAYSTACK EXPANSION

Cargo Bay 4 personnel have been instructed to expand the competition area.

The original 25-billion-piece haystack will remain intact.

Additional hay will be added until the estimated total reaches:

> **50,000,000,000 pieces.**

The fifty iron needles will remain distributed throughout the search area.

The single Nekonium needle will remain hidden.

Neri will not disclose its location.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The contestants were given a choice.

They chose the harder challenge.

Not unanimously.

Not happily.

But decisively.

The reasoning was simple:

**A Sleight of Hand is already rare.**

**A Tachyon family Night Stalker is even rarer.**

And NS-009 is one of the original ten.

For the contestants, an additional 25 billion pieces of hay became an acceptable price.
