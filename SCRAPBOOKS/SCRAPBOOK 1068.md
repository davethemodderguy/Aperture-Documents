## Executive Summary

Neri experienced a severe **Resentment-class anemic episode**.

Unlike her other documented episode classification, Resentment does not cause immediate unconsciousness.

Instead, the affected individual remains conscious while experiencing **extreme cardiac pain** for an extended period.

Neri endured the episode for approximately **five hours**.

She did not seek assistance.

She was eventually discovered by Alice.

Alice, however, no longer remembers Neri.

After briefly encountering her, Alice left.

Neri remained alone.

The incident represents a serious escalation of the existing medical and psychological concerns surrounding Neri.

---

# 1. Anemic Episode Classification

C.O.R.E. Medical has formally separated Neri's anemic episodes into two observed categories.

### I. TRANSCENDENCE

**Primary characteristic:** Immediate loss of consciousness.

Upon activation, Neri rapidly becomes unconscious.

There is little or no meaningful period during which she can communicate the onset of the episode.

The primary danger is therefore sudden incapacitation.

---

### II. RESENTMENT

**Primary characteristic:** Prolonged, extreme cardiac pain followed by loss of consciousness.

Unlike Transcendence, Neri remains conscious after the episode begins.

The pain is severe enough to dominate her attention and significantly impair her ability to function.

The episode may continue for an extended period before Neri eventually loses consciousness from overwhelming pain and systemic distress.

The present incident was classified as:

> **RESENTMENT**

# 3. The Most Significant Finding

Neri endured approximately five hours of extreme pain without asking for help.

This is not being interpreted as evidence that the episode was tolerable.

It was not.

The opposite is indicated.

Neri appears to have become increasingly accustomed to treating severe personal suffering as something she is expected to handle privately.

Her behavior remains consistent with the previously documented belief that drawing attention to herself makes her a burden.

The present episode therefore represents both a medical emergency and a behavioral concern.

---

# 4. Alice's Role

Alice's departure must be documented carefully.

She did **not** intentionally ignore Neri.

She did **not** knowingly abandon a friend in medical distress.

Alice's memory anomaly remains active.

She genuinely does not remember Neri.

Therefore, when Alice found Neri, she did not possess the context necessary to understand the significance of the situation.

This distinction does not make the event less devastating to Neri.

It makes it more devastating.

Neri did not need Alice to hurt her intentionally.

She simply needed Alice to remember.

She didn't.

---

# 5. Neri's Perspective

No verbal statement was obtained from Neri immediately following recovery.

However, her behavior provides sufficient context.

She had already begun avoiding Alice specifically because Alice no longer remembered her.

During the most painful medical episode documented in this period, Alice became the person who found her.

And then Alice left because she did not know who she was.

There was no argument.

No rejection.

No accusation.

Just absence of recognition.

For Neri, that distinction is almost certainly meaningless emotionally.

# 6. Medical Assessment

The Resentment classification is considered significantly more dangerous from a behavioral-monitoring perspective than Transcendence.

With Transcendence:

> Neri becomes unconscious immediately.

With Resentment:

> **Neri remains awake long enough to decide whether she will ask for help.**

Current evidence suggests she may choose not to.

This is unacceptable as a long-term pattern.

Neri's ability to endure pain should not be interpreted as resilience that can safely be relied upon.

Her five-hour endurance demonstrates the opposite:

**She can remain in catastrophic distress without telling anyone.**

---

# 7. Post-Episode Condition

Following the episode, Neri remained physically exhausted.

Her cardiac pain gradually subsided.

She was extremely quiet.

When asked whether she wanted someone nearby, she shook her head.

When asked whether she wanted Alice nearby, she again shook her head.

No further questioning was conducted.

She was permitted to remain alone while medical personnel maintained appropriate observation.

---

# 8. Medical Directive

Personnel are reminded that Neri's silence does not indicate that she is comfortable.

In particular:

> **"She hasn't complained" is not an acceptable medical assessment.**

Neri has demonstrated that she can experience extreme pain for hours without requesting assistance.

Likewise, personnel must not assume that an episode will resemble the previous one.

A Transcendence episode and a Resentment episode present differently.

Both require medical intervention.
