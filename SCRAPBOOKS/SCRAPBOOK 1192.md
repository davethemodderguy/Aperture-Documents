# 1. OBSERVATION

Recent SAFEHOUSE observations have identified several immediately recognizable similarities between the Tachyon sisters.

The most noticeable are:

**White hair.**

**A permanently attached item at the waist.**

And, despite their considerably different personalities:

**An unmistakable resemblance to one another.**

---

# 2. NERI TACHYON

Neri has long, flowing white hair.

It is usually left loose rather than tied back, allowing it to fall around her shoulders and down her back.

Her standard SAFEHOUSE attire remains recognizable by:

- round prescription glasses;
    
- crossed hairclips;
    
- fishbone hairclip;
    
- light-azure coat;
    
- blue shirt and tie;
    
- matching scarf;
    
- earrings;
    
- seal tail.
    

Her **keycard** is clipped directly to her waist.

This arrangement was established by Bao after Neri repeatedly encountered situations in which she could not access secured areas because she had misplaced or forgotten where her keycard was.

The current arrangement has proven considerably more reliable.

Neri's keycard is therefore now almost as visually associated with her as her glasses.

---

# 3. NORA TACHYON

Nora also has long, flowing white hair.

Unlike Neri, Nora's hair is often more visibly disorganized.

This is primarily due to:

- running;
    
- playing;
    
- climbing;
    
- sleeping;
    
- crawling through blanket forts;
    
- hiding;
    
- chasing Alice;
    
- chasing MSAC personnel;
    
- and general six-year-old behavior.
    

Her hair nevertheless retains the same distinctive white coloration as Neri's.

Nora's most recognizable personal possession is her **family journal.**

The journal is attached to her waist using a secure clip.

She carries it almost everywhere.

---

# 4. THE JOURNAL

The journal is small enough for Nora to carry comfortably.

It contains:

- photographs;
    
- drawings;
    
- names;
    
- family relationships;
    
- remembered stories;
    
- information about deceased Tachyon family members;
    
- information about Neri;
    
- information about Nora;
    
- and newly discovered family history.
    

Nora treats the journal as something important.

She does not generally leave it unattended.

If she does remove it from the clip, she usually knows exactly where she placed it.

This is notably different from her tendency to forget where she put practically everything else.

---

Personnel have begun informally describing the sisters as:

The description is technically accurate.

### NORA

**White flowing hair**

The resemblance becomes particularly obvious when they are standing together.

# 6. A HALLWAY OBSERVATION

A maintenance employee once encountered the sisters walking together.

Neri was carrying a cup of coffee.

Her keycard was clipped to her waist.

Nora was holding a piece of cake.

Her journal was clipped to her waist.

Both had their long white hair moving behind them as they walked.

The employee stopped.

Looked at them.

Then quietly said:

> **"You two really are sisters."**

Nora looked at Neri.

Neri looked at Nora.

Nora smiled.

> **NORA:**  
> "Yep."

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> "Yeah."

They continued walking.

---

# 7. NIGHTTIME OBSERVATION

The similarity becomes particularly striking during Nora's nighttime storytelling routine.

Nora sits beside Neri.

Both have their long white hair spread across the bedding.

Neri's keycard remains clipped to her coat.

Nora's journal remains clipped to her clothing until she removes it.

Nora opens the journal.

Then begins:

> **NORA:**  
> "Okay, Sis."

Neri looks over.

> **NORA:**  
> "Tonight I'm going to tell you about Grandpa Lewis."

Neri listens.

The journal rests open between them.

The keycard remains at Neri's waist.

Two small objects.

Two different purposes.

One represents access to the place where they live.

The other represents the family they came from.

---

# 8. NERI'S KEYCARD

The keycard has an additional significance.

Neri's history includes repeatedly being denied access to places where she was supposed to belong.

Bao's decision to keep the keycard physically attached to Neri was originally practical.

It has since become symbolic in SAFEHOUSE observation.

Neri can go where she is authorized to go.

She does not have to wait outside.

She does not have to ask someone to let her in.

She has her own access.

---

# 9. NORA'S JOURNAL

Nora's journal carries a different meaning.

It does not grant access to anything.

It opens no doors.

It authorizes nothing.

It simply contains names.

Stories.

Faces.

Memories.

Things that might otherwise disappear.

When Neri forgets something, Nora opens it.

Not to correct her.

Not to reprimand her.

Simply to remind her.

> **"This was our family."**

---

# 10. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The two objects have consequently become useful visual identifiers.

If personnel see:

**White hair + keycard + glasses + azure coat + seal tail**

the resident is almost certainly Neri.

If personnel see:

**White hair + journal clipped at waist + shark features + six-year-old energy**

the resident is almost certainly Nora.

If personnel see both:

They have probably just encountered the Tachyon sisters.

Personnel are advised not to assume that because the sisters look similar, their behavior will be similar.

Neri is quiet.

Nora is not.

Neri prefers solitude.

Nora will follow her sister around the Blacksite for hours.

Neri drinks coffee.

Nora steals cake.

Neri carries research.

Nora carries family history.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

There is something strangely fitting about the two sisters.

Neri carries a **keycard**.

Nora carries a **journal**.

One lets Neri enter the places she belongs.

The other reminds her of the people she belongs to.

And both walk through the Blacksite with the same flowing white hair behind them.

Sometimes Neri walks ahead.

Sometimes Nora runs to catch up.

Sometimes they walk side by side.

Usually, Nora is talking.

Usually, Neri is listening.

And occasionally, late at night, the arrangement reverses.

Nora opens the journal.

Neri listens.

The little sister remembers for the big sister.
