# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **CONFIDENTIAL THERAPEUTIC OBSERVATION**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – SUPPORTIVE ETHER MANIFESTATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical Director & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **OBSERVATION**

Over the course of several weeks, Corin has described a consistent pattern of interaction with the Ether manifestation resembling her late mother.

Unlike previous reports, the manifestation no longer speaks primarily about the past.

Instead, nearly every conversation concerns the present.

Its messages are remarkably ordinary.

Gentle.

Practical.

---

## **RECORDED STATEMENTS**

According to Corin, the figure frequently reminds her to care for herself.

Examples include:

> **"Your medication, sweetheart."**

If Corin forgets, the figure quietly points toward the bottle resting on her desk.

---

When Corin continues working late into the night.

> **"You've worked enough for today."**

> **"The reports will still be here tomorrow."**

---

If Corin skips meals.

> **"Tea isn't dinner."**

> **"Go eat something proper."**

---

When Corin stares silently at her own reflection.

> **"You're still you."**

> **"What happened to you changed many things."**

> **"It did not erase the person I love."**

---

On particularly difficult evenings.

> **"The past happened."**

> **"It cannot hurt you in this moment."**

> **"You are here."**

> **"You are safe."**

---

Whenever Corin quietly refers to herself as a corpse.

The figure reportedly kneels beside her.

Looks directly into her eyes.

And says the same sentence every time.

> **"You are not a corpse."**

> **"You are my daughter."**

Corin stated that this is the only sentence she has never argued with.

---

## **THE PEOPLE AROUND HER**

The manifestation also speaks often about the people still present in Corin's life.

> **"Belle checks your chair every evening."**

> **"Norma still worries when you skip meals."**

> **"Arcturus still waits until your office light turns off before he goes home."**

Then, almost every night...

The same quiet reminder.

> **"There are still people looking after you."**

---

## **PATIENT RESPONSE**

During her latest counseling session, Corin admitted something she had never said before.

> "...I know."

The counselor asked gently.

> "Then why don't you let them in?"

Corin's answer came almost immediately.

> "...Because I waited too long."

Another pause.

> "...I've been gone for so long."

> "...I don't know how to go back."

The attending psychologist documented that Corin was expressing **shame**, not rejection.

She does not believe the others have abandoned her.

She fears she has withdrawn so completely that she no longer knows how to reconnect.

The treatment team notes that this feeling, while deeply painful, does not mean reconnection is impossible.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the report before writing his closing observation.

> "Trauma has convinced Corin that distance becomes permanent if enough time passes."

He underlined the next sentence.

> "Human relationships do not work that way."

He closed the file.

> "She believes the bridge has collapsed."

> "Everyone on the other side is still waiting."

---

## **STATUS**

Corin continues to receive ongoing psychological and medical care.

She remains emotionally distant from many of the people who care deeply about her, despite recognizing their concern.

Her treatment team considers this not a sign that those relationships have ended, but that Corin's trauma has made the path back feel impossibly long.

The focus of therapy is not to force her across that distance.

It is to help her discover, one small step at a time, that the people waiting for her never stopped hoping she would come back.

**End of Confidential Therapeutic Observation**
