# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Medical analysis of the six recovered members of **Obol Squad** has confirmed an unusual hematological characteristic.

All six possess:

> **O-negative blood.**

Under conventional human transfusion classification, O-negative blood is compatible as a red-cell donor type for recipients of all ABO/Rh blood groups, subject to ordinary transfusion requirements and compatibility considerations.

The Obol Squad's blood possesses an additional anomalous property.

Their blood demonstrates **extreme regenerative capabilities**.

Samples exposed to damaged biological tissue have repeatedly demonstrated unusually rapid restorative activity.

The combination of:

- universal-donor-type blood;
    
- extreme regenerative properties;
    
- six genetically related artificial humans;
    
- and a history of exploitation by Project Silverline;
    

is the primary reason the **Krampus Compliance Authority (KCA)** maintains direct protective oversight of all six.

They are not protected because they are valuable biological resources.

They are protected because they are people who have already been exploited for what their bodies could provide.

---

# 1. BLOOD TYPE

All six Obol Squad members have been confirmed as:

> **O-negative (O−)**

The finding is consistent across:

- Anby;
    
- Solider 11;
    
- Twiggy;
    
- Iris;
    
- Phaethon;
    
- Sora.
    

No member has produced a conflicting ABO/Rh result.

This characteristic appears to have originated from their shared artificial-human lineage.

---

# 2. TRANSFUSION COMPATIBILITY

O-negative red blood cells are conventionally regarded as suitable for emergency transfusion to recipients of any ABO/Rh blood group when appropriately screened and administered.

However, C.O.R.E. Medical does **not** treat Obol Squad blood as ordinary O-negative blood.

The regenerative properties introduce additional considerations.

Their blood must therefore be handled under anomalous medical protocols.

A standard blood-type result does not make an anomalous blood sample medically ordinary.

---

# 3. REGENERATIVE PROPERTY

The most significant discovery concerns tissue repair.

Obol Squad blood demonstrates extreme regenerative activity.

Observed effects include unusually rapid:

- wound closure;
    
- tissue restoration;
    
- cellular repair;
    
- structural recovery;
    
- and regeneration of damaged biological material.
    

The exact mechanism remains under investigation.

C.O.R.E. has deliberately limited invasive experimentation.

No member of Obol Squad is to be injured for the purpose of producing a blood sample.

---

# 4. WHY THIS MATTERS

The combination is medically unusual.

A blood type compatible with recipients across the ABO/Rh system would already have significant emergency utility.

Adding extreme regenerative properties makes the blood considerably more anomalous.

This means that, without appropriate safeguards, Obol Squad members could become targets for:

- unauthorized blood collection;
    
- coercive medical experimentation;
    
- trafficking;
    
- biological exploitation;
    
- kidnapping;
    
- or attempts to reproduce their physiology.
    

These risks are not hypothetical within the historical context of Project Silverline.

---

# 5. PROJECT SILVERLINE

Project Silverline repeatedly treated artificial humans as experimental products rather than people.

The six recovered Obol Squad members were among its surviving subjects.

Their sisters were not all recovered.

Several were lost.

The existence of a biological property capable of accelerating regeneration therefore represents precisely the kind of characteristic that could attract renewed exploitation.

C.O.R.E. has accordingly classified their blood as:

> **PROTECTED ANOMALOUS BIOLOGICAL MATERIAL**

The classification applies to samples as well as the individuals themselves.

---

# 6. KCA PROTECTION

The **Krampus Compliance Authority** maintains protective responsibility for the six sisters.

This protection exists for multiple reasons.

### 1. Personhood

They are people.

Their bodies are not public resources.

### 2. Previous exploitation

They have already experienced experimentation and forced deployment.

### 3. Biological risk

Their blood possesses properties that could make them targets.

### 4. Security

Unauthorized acquisition of their blood could create significant medical and anomalous risks.

### 5. Recovery

The sisters require a stable environment in which they can develop lives outside Project Silverline.

---

# 7. NO BLOOD HARVESTING

C.O.R.E. policy is explicit:

> **Obol Squad members are not blood banks.**

Routine medical testing may require blood samples.

Emergency transfusion may be considered under appropriate medical circumstances.

Voluntary donation is a personal decision subject to medical supervision.

However:

No personnel may pressure an Obol Squad member into donating blood.

No resident may request blood as a favor.

No organization may request samples directly from an individual member without appropriate authorization.

No experiment may use their blood without full ethical and security review.

The O-negative classification is medically notable.

The regenerative property is anomalous.

Together, they make Obol Squad blood unusually valuable from a purely biological perspective.

The six sisters have already spent a significant portion of their lives being treated as experiments.

KCA protection exists to ensure their new lives do not become another version of the same arrangement.

Their blood can heal.

That does not mean people are entitled to it.
