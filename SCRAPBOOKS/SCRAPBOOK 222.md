# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CRITICAL RESEARCH DIRECTIVE**

## **SUBJECT: THE CORIN PARADOX – LIFE PRESERVATION PROTOCOL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – EXECUTIVE EYES ONLY

---

# **OVERVIEW**

This directive supersedes previous documentation referring to Corin Lockwood and Corey Lockwood as "twins" or "sisters."

Those terms remain acceptable for casual conversation.

They are **scientifically inaccurate.**

Corin Lockwood and Corey Lockwood are formally classified as a **Paradox Pair**.

They are not two people who resemble each other.

They are not clones.

They are not alternate-universe counterparts.

They are not manifestations of dissociative identity.

They are a single paradoxical existence expressed as two physically independent individuals.

Neither can be fully understood without the other.

---

# **THE PARADOX**

Current research concludes that Corin and Corey represent two mutually dependent outcomes of the same life.

Corin reflects the life that endured severe childhood trauma.

Corey reflects the life that might have developed had that trauma never occurred.

Both outcomes now exist simultaneously.

This condition violates conventional biological and temporal models.

No known anomalous classification fully explains the phenomenon.

For this reason, the designation **Paradox Pair** has been adopted.

---

# **IDENTICAL CAPABILITY**

Despite marked differences in emotional disposition and physiology, comprehensive testing has demonstrated no measurable difference in aptitude.

Both Corin and Corey possess equivalent:

- Mathematical ability.
    
- Accounting expertise.
    
- Administrative competence.
    
- Tactical reasoning.
    
- Memory retention.
    
- Professional training.
    
- Problem-solving capability.
    

Neither is "the intelligent one."

Neither is "the emotional one."

Their personalities differ.

Their abilities do not.

Research personnel are reminded that outward behavior must not be mistaken for differences in competence.

---

# **MUTUAL STABILIZATION**

Although equally skilled, each naturally supports aspects of the other that become strained under stress.

Corin recognizes subtle changes in Corey's thinking before others do.

Corey recognizes subtle changes in Corin's emotional state before anyone else.

Their relationship functions as a continuous stabilizing feedback system.

Neither consciously performs this role.

It occurs instinctively.

---

# **CRITICAL DEPENDENCY**

The paradox remains stable only while both manifestations remain together.

Research has identified two conditions presenting an unacceptable level of risk.

## **Condition One**

**Permanent loss of either manifestation.**

Potential consequences remain unknown.

Modeling predicts catastrophic destabilization of the remaining manifestation.

No further testing has been authorized.

---

## **Condition Two**

**Prolonged involuntary separation.**

Even relatively brief separation has produced measurable neurological and psychological deterioration in both individuals.

Symptoms escalate with time.

Research Division considers forced separation medically and ethically unacceptable except where immediate preservation of life requires temporary evacuation.

---

# **EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVE**

Effective immediately:

- Corin and Corey are to be assigned together whenever operationally feasible.
    
- Housing assignments are not to separate them.
    
- Medical treatment plans shall account for both individuals.
    
- Psychological evaluations shall consider both manifestations collectively.
    
- Intentional separation for research purposes is permanently prohibited.
    
- Any operation placing one manifestation at substantially greater risk than the other requires Executive authorization.
    

Failure to comply with these directives constitutes a Level 10 protocol violation.

# **FINAL ASSESSMENT**

The Corin Paradox remains one of the least understood anomalous phenomena documented by Aperture Industries.

Research cannot explain why two contradictory outcomes coexist.

Research cannot explain why they share one existence while possessing two bodies.

Research cannot explain what would occur if either manifestation were permanently lost.

Accordingly, the standing policy is intentionally simple.

> **Keep Corin alive.**

> **Keep Corey alive.**

> **Keep them together.**

Everything else...

Can wait until the paradox is understood.

**End of Critical Research Directive**
