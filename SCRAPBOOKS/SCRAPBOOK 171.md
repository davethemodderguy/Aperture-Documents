## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE – STAFF OBSERVATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – SOCIAL WITHDRAWAL WITHIN BLACKSITE PERIMETER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following her disappearance, Corin Lockwood was eventually confirmed to have remained within Heaven's Blacksite's secured perimeter.

She had not left the facility grounds.

Instead, she avoided populated areas, remaining in forests, gardens, rooftops, and other secluded locations.

Although her whereabouts were eventually known, her social isolation continued.

---

# **DAILY ROUTINE**

Corin rarely entered occupied buildings.

She avoided the SAFEHOUSE.

She attended only the minimum meetings required for safety or administrative reasons.

Most days were spent outdoors.

Reading.

Walking.

Sitting beneath the tree she had long claimed as her sleeping place.

If she saw groups of people approaching, she usually chose another path before they noticed her.

---

# **CHANGING PRIORITIES**

As weeks passed, the Blacksite gradually returned to its normal rhythm.

Research resumed.

Containment work continued.

Engineering deadlines accumulated.

Security rotations continued around the clock.

Residents of SAFEHOUSE returned to their own responsibilities.

Not because Corin had become unimportant.

But because life inside the Blacksite could not remain paused indefinitely.

Psychological Services note that this change was difficult for Corin to witness.

To her, it sometimes felt as though everyone had moved on.

To everyone else, it was the reality of balancing work, family, and concern for someone they could not force to accept help.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi was the exception.

Whenever her responsibilities allowed, she searched the usual places.

Sometimes she found Corin.

Sometimes she didn't.

If she did, she rarely asked Corin to come inside anymore.

Instead, she simply sat beside her.

Whether they talked or remained silent depended entirely on Corin.

One evening, Corin quietly asked,

> "...Why are you still here?"

Lumi answered without hesitation.

> "Because I'm your mother."

Corin looked toward the falling snow.

> "...Everyone else has work."

Lumi nodded.

> "They do."

> "And when they're finished..."

> "...they still hope you'll be here."

---

# **PERSONNEL OBSERVATION**

Medical and Psychological Services caution against interpreting the reduction in constant attention as a lack of care.

Residents continue to check on Corin when opportunities arise, but they also recognize that repeatedly interrupting her solitude has often caused her additional distress.

Their support has become quieter.

Less visible.

But it has not disappeared.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin continues to remain within the Blacksite perimeter while keeping considerable distance from others.

Lumi remains her most consistent companion.

The rest of Project SAFEHOUSE continues with its daily responsibilities, leaving a place for Corin whenever she chooses to return.

Medical Services summarize the situation as follows:

> **"Recovery does not stop because people return to work."**

> **"Nor does caring require someone to stand beside another person every hour of every day."**

**End of Project SAFEHOUSE Staff Observation Report**
