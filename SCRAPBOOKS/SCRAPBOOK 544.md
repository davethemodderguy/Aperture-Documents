### **DAILY LIFE / ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY REPORT**

# **CORIN — THE CLOTHING PROBLEM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Issue:** **Clothing Compatibility With Wings**  
**Status:** Ongoing

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's return to biological life has introduced an unexpectedly mundane problem:

**Clothes don't fit properly anymore.**

Her wings extend directly from her back and require considerable freedom of movement.

Standard shirts, coats, dresses, and other conventional clothing were not designed for someone with a pair of large, highly flexible wings.

As a result, Corin's wardrobe has become increasingly impractical.

---

# **THE BASIC PROBLEM**

A normal shirt assumes that the wearer's back is relatively flat.

Corin's back is not.

Her wings emerge from it.

Trying to wear ordinary clothing creates several problems:

- The fabric presses against the wing roots.
    
- Sleeves and seams can interfere with wing movement.
    
- Tight clothing restricts folding.
    
- Back panels become stretched around the wings.
    
- Removing clothing can catch feathers.
    
- Coats can become especially restrictive.
    
- Wet clothing becomes considerably more uncomfortable against the wings.
    

Corin's wings are flexible.

Her clothes are not.

---

# **THE FIRST INCIDENT**

Corin attempted to wear one of her old coats after her wings developed.

She managed to get it on.

She then tried to unfold her wings.

The coat stretched.

The feathers pushed outward.

The back seam gave up.

**RIP.**

Corin stared at the damaged coat.

> **"...Oh."**

Orphie looked at it.

> **"That was your favorite coat."**

Corin quietly replied:

> **"...Was."**

---

# **COATS ARE THE WORST**

Outerwear has proven particularly problematic.

A conventional coat must either:

1. Cover the wings completely, restricting them.
    
2. Sit underneath the wings, which is uncomfortable.
    
3. Be modified with openings.
    
4. Be specially designed around them.
    

Corin generally chooses option four.

Her wings need to remain free even when she is simply walking.

---

# **WING OPENINGS**

The first successful solution involved creating large reinforced openings in the back of Corin's clothing.

The wings pass through these openings naturally.

The fabric is reinforced around the edges to prevent tearing when Corin moves.

The design also leaves enough space for the wings to fold.

This has become the basic template for Corin's clothing.

---

# **NEKONIUM FABRIC**

Neko has proposed incorporating specialized materials into Corin's clothing.

The material must be:

- Flexible.
    
- Lightweight.
    
- Resistant to tearing.
    
- Safe around sharp feathers.
    
- Resistant to Ether exposure.
    
- Comfortable against Corin's skin.
    
- Capable of stretching with wing movement.
    

The same philosophy used for her wing armor applies:

> **Don't make Corin's wings accommodate the equipment.**

Make the equipment accommodate the wings.

---

# **SLEEPWEAR**

Even pajamas require modification.

Corin cannot comfortably wear conventional sleepwear if the fabric presses against the wing roots.

Her preferred sleepwear therefore leaves the wing area unobstructed.

This is particularly important now that she sometimes sleeps sitting upright with her wings wrapped around herself.

---

# **ORPHIE'S SOLUTION**

Orphie eventually began modifying Corin's clothes herself.

The modifications are simple.

Large reinforced openings.

Flexible fabric.

Extra room around the shoulders.

No unnecessary seams around the wing roots.

The result looks mostly normal.

Until Corin unfolds her wings.

Then everyone remembers why the modifications are necessary.

---

# **BELLE'S OBSERVATION**

Belle watched Orphie modify one of Corin's shirts.

She asked:

> **"Why don't you just make shirts with wings?"**

Orphie paused.

> **"...That's basically what we're doing."**

Belle nodded.

> **"Oh."**

---

# **CORIN'S PREFERENCE**

Corin does not particularly care about fashion.

Her requirements are much simpler:

> **"Don't squeeze my wings."**

> **"Don't pull my feathers."**

> **"Don't make me unable to fly."**

Everything else is negotiable.

---

# **CURRENT WARDROBE**

Corin's clothing is gradually being replaced with wing-compatible versions.

Current designs include:

- Wing-compatible shirts.
    
- Modified coats.
    
- Open-back jackets.
    
- Flexible sleepwear.
    
- Reinforced travel clothing.
    
- Weather-resistant outerwear.
    

Her old clothes are still kept.

Mostly because Corin likes them.

They simply cannot be worn normally anymore.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin spent years dealing with problems nobody else could understand.

Her body was held together by stitches.

Ether replaced her biology.

She could not sleep normally.

She eventually grew wings.

Now that she is alive again, one of her biggest problems is considerably more ordinary:

**Her shirts don't fit.**

Corin can survive extreme temperatures.

She can regenerate.

She can breathe underwater.

She can fly.

She can launch Ether-charged feathers.

But ask her what the most annoying part of having wings is?

She will probably answer:

> **"Clothes."**
