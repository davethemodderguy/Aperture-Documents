### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / PERSONNEL CLASSIFICATION UPDATE**

# **CORIN — DUAL TAIL MORPHOLOGY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Classification:** **Cat Thiren**  
**Update:** **Two Feline Tails Confirmed**  
**Status:** Stable

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin possesses **two feline tails**.

Both tails are fully developed, functional, and responsive.

The second tail was initially assumed to be an unusual manifestation of Corin's Ether physiology.

Further observation has confirmed that it is simply part of Corin's current Cat Thiren form.

No Architect caused the development.

No connection to Guiding Light's original blessing has been identified.

Corin simply has two tails.

---

# **TAIL STRUCTURE**

Both tails are covered in fur consistent with Corin's current appearance.

They possess independent movement and can be controlled voluntarily.

They also react automatically to Corin's emotions.

Common observed behaviors include:

- Curving upward when curious.
    
- Drooping when tired.
    
- Flicking when irritated.
    
- Moving rapidly when excited.
    
- Wrapping around herself when comfortable.
    
- Becoming completely still when Corin is concentrating.
    

The two tails do not always move identically.

---

# **BALANCE AND FLIGHT**

The additional tail provides Corin with improved balance.

This is particularly noticeable during flight.

Corin can use both tails to make small adjustments to her center of gravity while airborne.

Combined with her wings, this gives her unusually precise aerial maneuverability.

Her tails are not responsible for propulsion.

They primarily assist with:

- Balance.
    
- Directional adjustment.
    
- Stabilization.
    
- Landing.
    

---

# **SAFEHOUSE DISCOVERY**

The second tail was first noticed when Corin was sitting on the floor playing with yarn.

Neko noticed something unusual.

> **Neko:**  
> "...Wait."

Corin looked up.

> **"What?"**

Neko pointed behind her.

There were two tails.

Corin looked over her shoulder.

She stared for several seconds.

> **"...Oh."**

Miss Neko examined them.

> **"You have two."**

Corin nodded.

> **"Apparently."**

Endmin quietly added:

> **"That's cute."**

Corin's ears immediately perked up.

---

# **YARN PROBLEM**

Two tails have made Corin considerably more vulnerable to getting tangled in yarn.

She has already managed to wrap yarn around both tails simultaneously.

The resulting knot took several minutes to remove.

Neko found this extremely amusing.

Corin did not.

> **"Don't laugh."**

Neko laughed harder.

---

# **SLEEPING**

Corin occasionally wraps both tails around herself while sleeping.

When combined with her wings, this creates an unusually secure enclosure around her body.

Her preferred sleeping arrangement is now:

- Sitting or lying on her side.
    
- Wings folded around her body.
    
- Both tails curled nearby.
    

She appears comfortable this way.

---

# **EMOTIONAL RESPONSE**

The two tails provide additional visible indicators of Corin's emotional state.

Corin's attempts to hide her feelings are therefore considerably less effective.

When she is happy:

**Both tails move.**

When she is embarrassed:

**Both tails curl inward.**

When she is irritated:

**One tail tends to flick repeatedly.**

When she is genuinely angry:

**Both become completely still.**

Neko has learned to recognize the last one.

He does not test it.

---

# **CURRENT MORPHOLOGY**

Corin's confirmed Cat Thiren characteristics now include:

- **Cat ears**
    
- **Two feline tails**
    
- **Feline physiology**
    
- **Semi-transparent wings**
    
- **Luminescent feathers**

Corin originally became a Cat Thiren because she wanted to.

Nobody instructed her to stop at one tail.

Apparently, she didn't.

She now has:

**Two ears.**

**Two tails.**

And an increasingly serious yarn addiction.
