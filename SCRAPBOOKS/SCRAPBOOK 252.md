# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **INTERNAL OBSERVATION LOG**

### **SUBJECT: LOCATING DIALYN**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although Dialyn now spends a significant portion of her time outside the Customer Support Office as the Authority's Human Representative, coworkers have discovered an unusually effective method of locating her.

They simply call her phone.

This has gradually become the standard practice throughout both the Krampus Compliance Authority and Heaven Blacksite.

---

# **BACKGROUND**

Dialyn has an unusual habit of unintentionally wandering between departments.

She may be found:

- Helping Corin and Corey organize paperwork.
    
- Drinking tea with Belle.
    
- Speaking with visitors.
    
- Checking on Norma.
    
- Looking for Neko.
    
- Petting her white cat.
    
- Quietly sitting beneath Corin's treehouse.
    

Attempting to locate her by physically searching the facility is generally considered inefficient.

Calling her takes only a few seconds.

---

# **THE PHONE**

Dialyn answers her phone with remarkable consistency.

Even during meetings, she usually responds with:

> "...One moment, please."

Before stepping outside to answer.

Employees have noted that calling her directly is often faster than asking nearby personnel where she is.

# **THE RUNNING JOKE**

A long-standing joke has developed among KCA employees.

Whenever someone asks:

> "Has anyone seen Dialyn?"

The immediate response is almost always:

> "...Have you tried calling her?"

On more than one occasion, Dialyn's phone has rung while she was standing in the same room as the person asking.

She simply smiled, answered the call, and said:

> "...Hi."

The caller looked up.

Dialyn waved.

Neither hung up immediately.

---

# **DIALYN'S RESPONSE**

When asked whether she found the habit unusual, Dialyn laughed.

> "...Not really."

She shrugged.

> "...People spent years calling me because I answered every customer support line."

> "...Now they call me because they can't find me."

Another smile.

> "...I think that's an improvement."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Calling Dialyn's phone has become the unofficial standard procedure for locating her.

Human Resources has informally acknowledged that it is, in fact, more efficient than calling out her name across the office.

As one anonymous employee summarized in a suggestion box:

> "If you shout 'Dialyn,' five people will ask 'Which room?'"

> "If you call Dialyn..."

> "...Dialyn answers."
