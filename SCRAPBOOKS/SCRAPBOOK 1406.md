# 4. NEKONIUM MAGNUM DECIMATOR — TECHNICAL NOTE

The designation **Magnum Decimator** refers to the ammunition configuration rather than an unusually complicated action.

The platform uses:

- an M4-style shotgun frame;
    
- magazine-fed operation;
    
- 12-gauge ammunition;
    
- specialized Nekonium shells.
    

The Nekonium ammunition is the primary anomalous component.

The weapon therefore represents an unusual collaboration between Tachyon Industries equipment design and C.O.R.E. research.

Neko contributed the engineering.

Neri contributed the chemical/material research.

Sora receives the finished platform.
