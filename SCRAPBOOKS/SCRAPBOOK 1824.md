# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake Akana has returned as a ghost.

His reappearance has not restored his physical body. His current manifestation is visibly damaged, anatomically incomplete, and substantially different from his former living appearance.

The most notable characteristics are:

- Complete absence of legs.
    
- Severe melting-like deformation affecting approximately half of his head, resembling wax softened by a candle flame.
    
- A wick protruding from the top of his head, bearing a small blue flame.
    
- A soft, persistent glow emitted by the flame.
    
- Extensive scarring across his body, including the heart region, neck, thighs, arms, and stomach.
    
- Possession of a spectral teddy bear resembling his original companion.
    

Despite the apparent absence of functioning eyes, Jake retains vision.

He has explicitly acknowledged his condition:

> **JAKE:** “No legs to walk with and no eyes to see.”

The statement is understood to describe his appearance rather than his actual sensory capabilities.

Jake can see without eyes.

The absence of legs does not currently establish whether he can move independently, although his present form does not permit ordinary walking.

**Assessment:** Jake is present as a postmortem spectral entity. His condition is stable enough to permit observation and communication, but the extent of his abilities and the nature of his injuries remain under investigation.

---

# 1. CURRENT APPEARANCE

Jake's current manifestation retains his recognizable child-sized silhouette.

However, the resemblance to his former appearance is disrupted by the extensive damage affecting his spectral body.

The most immediately apparent feature is his head.

Approximately half of it appears melted, as though the material forming his body had been exposed to sufficient heat to deform and run downward.

The deformation resembles a partially melted candle.

It is not accompanied by any confirmed evidence of ordinary combustion.

A wick protrudes from the top of his head.

At its tip burns a small blue flame.

The flame emits a soft, cool-looking glow that illuminates the immediate area around Jake.

No heat emission has been established.

Personnel are not authorized to test the flame by touching it.

The flame's visual similarity to an ordinary candle does not establish that it follows the same physical rules.

---

# 2. THE BLUE FLAME

The blue flame is a persistent feature of Jake's current manifestation.

It is positioned above his head, attached to the visible wick.

Its illumination is soft rather than intense.

In low-light environments, the glow makes Jake easier to identify at a distance.

The flame does not appear to compensate for the damage to his head by restoring his former facial features.

The melted region remains visibly deformed.

C.O.R.E. has not established whether the wick and flame serve a biological, spiritual, symbolic, or purely anomalous function.

No assumptions should be made about whether extinguishing the flame would affect Jake's existence.

**Directive:** Do not attempt to extinguish, ignite, manipulate, or remove the wick.

Jake is not to be subjected to flame-related experiments without a clearly established medical or protective necessity.

---

# 3. ABSENCE OF LEGS

Jake has no legs in his current spectral form.

The absence is complete enough that ordinary walking is not possible.

This is not simply a matter of Jake refusing to stand or choosing not to use his legs.

The necessary anatomy is absent.

Consequently, personnel should not instruct him to walk, ask him to stand up, or assume that he can use ordinary mobility equipment in the same manner as a physically embodied child.

His actual method of movement remains to be documented.

Until then, personnel should allow Jake to indicate how he wishes to move and provide assistance only when appropriate.

His lack of legs must not be interpreted as evidence that he is immobile.

His lack of eyes must not be interpreted as evidence that he is blind.

Both assumptions would contradict the currently available observations.

---

# 4. OCULAR ABSENCE AND VISUAL PERCEPTION

Jake has no functioning visible eyes in the conventional anatomical sense.

Nevertheless, he can see.

This capability has been directly acknowledged by Jake and is not presently considered speculative.

His statement—

> **“No legs to walk with and no eyes to see.”**

—initially appeared to describe a contradiction.

The first part is anatomically accurate.

The second is not functionally accurate.

Jake lacks eyes, yet retains visual perception.

C.O.R.E. cannot currently determine how this perception operates.

Possible explanations include an anomalous sensory mechanism associated with his spectral state, but no specific mechanism has been confirmed.

Personnel should communicate with Jake normally.

There is no need to shout, wave objects in front of his face, or repeatedly ask whether he can see.

He can.

**Current finding:** Visual perception remains functional despite the absence of conventional eyes.

---

# 5. SCARRING AND VISIBLE DAMAGE

Jake's body is covered in scars.

The documented locations include:

- **Heart region**
    
- **Neck**
    
- **Thighs**
    
- **Arms**
    
- **Stomach**
    

The scars remain visible in his spectral manifestation.

Their appearance is consistent with the preservation of damage associated with his death and other physical injuries.

However, C.O.R.E. will not assume that every scar corresponds to a separately documented incident.

The exact number, depth, age, and cause of all visible marks have not been established in this assessment.

The persistence of these injuries in a ghostly body raises significant questions regarding the relationship between Jake's physical death and his present manifestation.

It does not, by itself, establish that his ghost experiences every injury in the same manner as a living body.

Nor does it establish that the scars are merely cosmetic.

Further observation must prioritize Jake's welfare over the acquisition of additional data.

No personnel member is authorized to deliberately inflict new injuries to determine whether spectral damage can occur.

---

# 6. THE SPECTRAL TEDDY BEAR

Jake carries a ghostly version of his teddy bear.

The object resembles the teddy bear that accompanied him during his life, but it now exists as part of his spectral presentation.

The original teddy bear has been placed in safekeeping.

It is no longer being carried by Jake.

This decision was made because Jake no longer requires the original object in the same way he did while alive, following his death and the loss of his physical body.

The original item remains preserved.

It is not to be discarded, dismantled, or treated as an ordinary piece of obsolete personal property.

Its significance to Jake warrants continued respectful handling.

Meanwhile, the spectral version is the bear Jake currently carries.

C.O.R.E. has not established whether the spectral bear is a manifestation of the original object's identity, a spiritual counterpart, or a separate anomalous creation.

Visual resemblance alone is insufficient to determine its origin.

**Handling restrictions:**

- Do not forcibly separate Jake from the spectral bear.
    
- Do not attempt to exchange it for the original.
    
- Do not assume the spectral bear contains Jake's former heartbeat.
    
- Do not assume the original bear has become anomalous merely because Jake now carries a spectral counterpart.
    

The original is in safekeeping.

The ghostly version remains with Jake.

These are the current facts.

---

# 7. RESIDENT INTERACTION

Jake's return is likely to provoke strong reactions among residents who remember him before his death.

His current appearance may be difficult to reconcile with their memories of him as a living child.

Personnel are reminded that Jake should not be treated as an experiment simply because his present condition is anomalous.

Do not:

- stare persistently at the melted portion of his head;
    
- repeatedly ask him how he sees;
    
- demand that he explain the absence of his legs;
    
- touch the wick or flame;
    
- probe his scars;
    
- take the spectral teddy bear away for analysis;
    
- assume that his ghostly condition makes him immune to distress;
    
- or insist that he explain his appearance to reassure others.
    

Questions may be asked when appropriate, but Jake retains the right not to answer.

His ability to communicate does not create an obligation to participate in an interview.

---

# 8. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Jake's current manifestation demonstrates a separation between ordinary anatomy and retained function.

He has no legs, yet this does not establish that he cannot move.

He has no eyes, yet he can see.

His head is visibly melted, yet the blue flame continues to burn.

His body retains extensive scarring, yet he remains present as a recognizable individual.

He carries a spectral teddy bear, while the original object remains safely preserved elsewhere.

These characteristics are not sufficient to establish the full mechanics of his existence.

They are, however, sufficient to reject the assumption that Jake's ghost must function according to ordinary biological limitations.

C.O.R.E. will therefore document observed abilities rather than infer incapacity from appearance.

---

# CLOSING OBSERVATION

Jake has lost his physical body.

He has lost his legs.

He has lost his eyes.

Half his head resembles a candle that has melted too far.

A small blue flame burns above him, casting a quiet glow over a body marked by scars.

And yet, he can see.

He can speak.

He can remain present.

The original teddy bear that once accompanied him has been placed somewhere safe, no longer needed to sustain the body that carried it.

Jake now holds a spectral counterpart instead.

It is a small distinction, but an important one.

The original belongs to the life he had.

The ghostly version belongs to the existence he has now.

C.O.R.E. does not yet understand what Jake has become, or why his death has left him in this particular form.

For now, the appropriate response is not to demand that he explain every wound or demonstrate every ability.

It is to acknowledge the facts without reducing him to them.

Jake is dead.

Jake is a ghost.

Jake has no legs to walk with.

Jake has no eyes, and yet he sees.

And, for reasons C.O.R.E. has not yet understood, **Jake is still here.**
