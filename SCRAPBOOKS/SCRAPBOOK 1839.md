### I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake Akana has regained his physical body.

The restoration was performed by Guiding Light, one of the three Architects of Reality responsible for the forging of reality and the Starlands. According to the available account, Guiding Light took pity on Jake and simply gave him his body back.

No procedure was requested from Aperture Industries. No reconstruction project was undertaken by C.O.R.E. Research and Development. No explanation of the precise mechanism has been provided.

One moment, Jake was a ghost. After Guiding Light's intervention, he was alive in his physical body again.

The restoration represents a significant change to Jake's established condition. His physical body had remained buried in the Starlands following his murder at six years old. His return to that body does not, by itself, resolve the circumstances of his death or identify the person responsible.

Following the restoration, Camelia Candleheart was able to see Jake in his living appearance, including his Cat Thiren form. The change gave her an opportunity to see her little brother as he had been before his death, rather than only as the ghost she had known since his return.

Camelia also met all three Architects of Reality:

- **Guiding Light** — female; the Architect who restored Jake's body.
    
- **Curious Light** — male.
    
- **Mischevious Light** — male.
    

Despite the apparent disparity between their conditions, Camelia did not express jealousy toward Jake. She stated that she liked being a ghost because no one could hurt her anymore.

C.O.R.E. notes that this statement should not be interpreted as evidence that Camelia is free from fear or trauma. Rather, it provides important context for her relationship with her own condition: she does not necessarily regard physical restoration as a desirable outcome for herself.

Jake has regained his body. Camelia has not requested one.

Both positions are to be respected.

### II. EVENT LOG

#### 09:12 — The Return of Jake Akana

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE

Jake's restoration was reported following an intervention by Guiding Light.

The available account identifies compassion as the reason for the intervention: Guiding Light took pity on Jake and returned his body to him.

The process was described as straightforward in outcome, despite the extraordinary nature of the event.

Jake was no longer confined to his previous ghostly appearance. His restored physical form allowed him to exist as a living child again.

His Cat Thiren form was also available to him.

The significance of the restoration was immediately apparent to Camelia, who had previously known Jake only through his ghostly manifestation.

For the first time since their relationship began, she could see him as a living Cat Thiren: with his cat ears, tail, and eyes, rather than the melted head and blue flame that had characterized his ghost form.

No indication was recorded that Camelia considered the change unfair.

Instead, her response centered on the fact that Jake was alive.

---

#### 09:26 — Camelia Sees Jake Alive

Camelia encountered Jake after the restoration.

For several moments, she simply looked at him.

She had seen the photographs of Jake before his death. She had studied the images of his former appearance and recorded details in her notebook so that she would not lose them.

Those photographs had shown her what he looked like.

Now she could see him directly.

**CAMELIA:** “Jake?”

**JAKE:** “Yeah?”

_Camelia approaches him cautiously, studying his face._

**CAMELIA:** “Your ears.”

**JAKE:** “They're back.”

**CAMELIA:** “And your eyes.”

**JAKE:** “Still mine.”

_Camelia looks at him for another moment._

**CAMELIA:** “You look like your pictures.”

**JAKE:** “I was... in the pictures.”

**CAMELIA:** “I know. It's just different seeing you like this.”

_Jake waits._

**CAMELIA:** “You're really alive.”

**JAKE:** “Guiding... helped.”

**CAMELIA:** “She gave you your body back?”

**JAKE:** “Mm-hm.”

_Camelia looks down briefly, as if trying to organize the significance of what she is seeing._

**CAMELIA:** “I'm happy for you.”

_Jake looks at her._

**JAKE:** “You're not... sad?”

**CAMELIA:** “About what?”

**JAKE:** “You... still ghost.”

_Camelia shakes her head._

**CAMELIA:** “Jake, I like being a ghost.”

_She says it without hesitation._

**CAMELIA:** “No one can hurt me anymore.”

_Jake's expression changes. The remark does not sound like a complaint about her condition. It sounds like a reason she is grateful for it._

**CAMELIA:** “I'm happy you got your body back. Really. But I don't need one just because you have yours.”

**JAKE:** “Okay.”

**CAMELIA:** “Besides, you can be the living little brother, and I can be the ghost older sister.”

**JAKE:** “Still... sister.”

**CAMELIA:** “Always.”

_Jake moves closer, and Camelia embraces him._

_No jealousy, resentment, or request for physical restoration was observed._

---

#### 10:03 — First Contact with the Architects of Reality

**Location:** Domain associated with the Architects of Reality

Camelia accompanied Jake to meet the three beings recognized as the Architects—or Creators—of Reality.

These entities are identified as the beings responsible for forging reality and the Starlands. Their capabilities and ontological status exceed the ordinary scope of Aperture's research.

The meeting introduced Camelia to three figures whose significance was substantially greater than that of ordinary anomalous residents or contained entities.

The participants were:

**Guiding Light** — female. The Architect who restored Jake's body.

**Curious Light** — male. One of the three Creators of Reality.

**Mischevious Light** — male. One of the three Creators of Reality.

Camelia was able to meet all three in person.

The meeting also gave her an opportunity to understand that Jake's restoration had not been performed by an unknown medical team or a conventional anomalous procedure. It had been granted by one of the beings responsible for the reality in which both children existed.

The available account does not establish that the Architects offered Camelia restoration, nor that they suggested she should cease being a ghost.

No such request was made by Camelia.

---

#### 10:11 — Camelia Meets Guiding Light

Camelia's introduction to Guiding Light carried particular significance because Guiding had directly changed Jake's condition.

Camelia was able to meet the Architect responsible for giving her little brother his life back.

**CAMELIA:** “You're Guiding Light?”

**GUIDING LIGHT:** “I am.”

**CAMELIA:** “You're the one who gave Jake his body back.”

**GUIDING LIGHT:** “Yes.”

_Camelia glances at Jake, who is standing beside her._

**CAMELIA:** “Thank you.”

_Guiding Light receives the thanks._

**CAMELIA:** “I don't know how to repay something like that.”

**GUIDING LIGHT:** “You do not need to.”

_Camelia falls quiet._

**CAMELIA:** “I don't remember much about my own life. But I know Jake wanted more time.”

_She looks toward her brother._

**CAMELIA:** “I think this means a lot to him.”

_Jake remains beside her._

**JAKE:** “It does.”

_Camelia turns back to Guiding Light._

**CAMELIA:** “I'm glad he got another chance.”

The exchange was brief. Camelia did not ask Guiding Light to restore her own body or recover her memories.

Her gratitude was directed toward the outcome for Jake, not toward the possibility of obtaining the same outcome for herself.

---

#### 10:19 — Camelia Meets Curious Light

Camelia was introduced to Curious Light, one of the three Creators of Reality.

The meeting was a new experience for her. Although she had encountered many unusual individuals during her time at SAFEHOUSE, meeting one of the beings responsible for forging reality and the Starlands was a distinct matter.

**CAMELIA:** “So you're one of the Creators, too?”

**CURIOUS LIGHT:** “One of the three, yes.”

**CAMELIA:** “You helped make reality?”

**CURIOUS LIGHT:** “Among other things.”

_Camelia considers the answer._

**CAMELIA:** “That's a lot to be responsible for.”

**CURIOUS LIGHT:** “It certainly sounds that way when put into ordinary words.”

**CAMELIA:** “I don't think I have any extraordinary questions.”

**CURIOUS LIGHT:** “Ordinary questions are welcome.”

_Camelia glances at Jake._

**CAMELIA:** “I'm mostly just happy to meet the people who helped make the world my brother gets to live in again.”

_Jake looks toward her._

**JAKE:** “Our world.”

**CAMELIA:** “Right. Our world.”

The exchange did not produce any new information about Camelia's forgotten past. No attempt to recover her memories was recorded during this part of the meeting.

---

#### 10:27 — Camelia Meets Mischevious Light

Camelia was then introduced to Mischevious Light, the third Architect of Reality.

The introduction completed her first meeting with all three Creators.

**CAMELIA:** “You're Mischevious Light?”

**MISCHEVIOUS LIGHT:** “That is what they call me.”

**CAMELIA:** “Should I be worried?”

_The question was delivered cautiously, but without visible panic._

**MISCHEVIOUS LIGHT:** “That depends on what you consider worrying.”

_Camelia looks at Jake._

**CAMELIA:** “Is he always like this?”

**JAKE:** “Don't... know.”

**MISCHEVIOUS LIGHT:** “A perfectly reasonable answer.”

_Camelia looks back at Mischevious Light._

**CAMELIA:** “I'm not very good at guessing what people mean.”

**MISCHEVIOUS LIGHT:** “Then it is fortunate that you can ask.”

_Camelia considers this before nodding._

**CAMELIA:** “All right. I'll do that.”

The interaction concluded without a reported incident.

### III. CAMELIA'S POSITION ON HER GHOSTLY CONDITION

Following the meeting, C.O.R.E. personnel recorded a direct statement from Camelia regarding her reaction to Jake's restoration.

She expressed happiness for Jake and did not display resentment toward him or Guiding Light.

Her reasoning was straightforward:

She likes being a ghost because no one can hurt her anymore.

This statement must be understood in the context of Camelia's established condition. She has severe Etheric Retrograde Amnesia, experiences frightening fragments of her past life, and cannot reliably identify or reconstruct the memories that emerge.

Her ghostly existence is not simply a visual form. It is also a condition she associates with safety from physical harm.

It would be inappropriate to conclude that she has no difficulties because she prefers remaining a ghost. Her nightmares remain distressing. Her memory difficulties remain significant. Her need for support remains unchanged.

Likewise, it would be inappropriate to assume that she secretly wants a body simply because Jake has regained his.

**C.O.R.E. determination:** Camelia's preference is valid and does not require correction.

Her decision to remain a ghost must not be treated as a symptom of jealousy, denial, or an obligation to follow her brother's path. Unless Camelia herself expresses a desire for a different condition, no restoration should be presumed necessary or requested on her behalf.

She is not waiting for Jake's outcome to become her own.

She is happy that he has received something he wanted, while retaining something she values herself.

---

### IV. FAMILY AND WELFARE IMPLICATIONS

Jake's return to his physical body changes his circumstances, but it does not change his relationship with Camelia.

She remains his officially recognized older sister.

The restoration does not erase the period in which Jake existed as a ghost, the suffering associated with his death, or the support Camelia provided during that time. Nor does it make their shared experiences less meaningful.

Camelia knew Jake when his physical body was absent. She sat beside him during the night when neither of them could sleep. She saw his ghostly cat form, learned about the photographs of his former life, and accompanied him to his grave.

Now she can see him alive.

The relationship continues across that change.

C.O.R.E. further recommends that Jake be allowed time to adjust to having his body back without pressure to behave as though nothing happened. Restoration is an extraordinary event, but it does not automatically settle every emotional or practical consequence of his former condition.

Similarly, Camelia should not be expected to respond to his restoration with grief or envy merely because she remains a ghost.

Both children should be allowed to define what their conditions mean to them.

---

### V. CLOSING OBSERVATION

Camelia had spent time looking at photographs of Jake before his death.

She had studied his ears, his eyes, and his tail. She had seen him beside Phoenix, Clover, and Bao. She had written down what she learned because her memories could not be trusted to remain.

Now she no longer needed a photograph to see what Jake had looked like alive.

He was standing in front of her.

His ears were there. His eyes were there. His Cat Thiren form was his own again.

And Camelia was happy.

Not because she had been promised the same thing.

Not because she expected Guiding Light to restore her body next.

She was happy because her little brother had been given another chance to live.

When asked about her own condition, she gave an answer that was simple enough to be misunderstood.

She liked being a ghost.

No one could hurt her anymore.

That did not mean she had forgotten her nightmares. It did not mean she had made peace with every unknown part of her history. It did not mean she would never want anything to change.

It meant that, for now, she had found something in her existence that made her feel safe.

C.O.R.E. will respect that.

Guiding Light gave Jake his body back out of compassion. That act has changed his life in a way no ordinary intervention could have accomplished.

Camelia's response offers a different reminder: compassion does not always mean giving everyone the same thing. Sometimes it means allowing someone to keep what makes them feel safe, even when another person has received something extraordinary.

Jake is alive again.

Camelia remains a ghost.

Neither condition diminishes the bond between them.

And when Camelia looks at her little brother now, she no longer has to imagine the living boy from the photographs.

She can simply look at Jake.
