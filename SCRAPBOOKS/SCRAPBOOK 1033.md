## Archive Note

Neri's motion sickness is a recurring welfare consideration.

The condition is most noticeable during:

- sudden acceleration;
    
- rapid turning;
    
- spinning;
    
- jumping;
    
- unstable transportation;
    
- unexpected movement while being carried.
    

Neri does not usually complain immediately.

She often tolerates discomfort silently until it becomes difficult to ignore.

When she does speak about it, personnel should listen.

# 01 — Jane Doe: The First Warning

**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE Corridor

Jane was carrying Neri piggyback.

Jane turned quickly around a corner.

Neri became noticeably quieter.

Jane noticed.

> **Jane:** “You okay?”

A pause.

> **Neri:** “...”

Jane slowed down.

> **Jane:** “Neri?”

Very quietly:

> **Neri:** “Don't turn so fast.”

Jane immediately stopped.

> **Jane:** “Motion sickness?”

> **Jane:** “Oh.”

Jane adjusted her pace.

> **Jane:** “Sorry.”

After a few seconds:

> **Neri:** “It's okay.”

Jane smiled.

> **Jane:** “You actually said that?”

Neri immediately looked away.

# C.O.R.E. Assessment

Neri's motion sickness is not severe enough to prevent transportation.

However, it is severe enough that personnel should take precautions.

The most important factor remains:

Neri will often endure discomfort silently.

Therefore, a lack of complaint does not necessarily mean she is comfortable.

If Neri says:

> “Stop.”

Stop.

If Neri says:

> “Slow down.”

Slow down.

If Neri says:

> “Ow.”

Find out why.
