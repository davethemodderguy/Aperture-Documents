# 1. SUMMARY

The six members of Obol Squad were observed discussing Jake.

The conversation began as a routine discussion regarding his recovery.

It quickly became considerably more personal.

The squad has developed a collective understanding that Jake is:

- physically fragile at present;
    
- unusually quiet;
    
- still recovering from the Nullwalker incident;
    
- increasingly dependent on others for transportation;
    
- capable of speaking again;
    
- and, according to Anby and Twiggy, considerably more stubborn than his current physical condition would suggest.
    

The squad's general conclusion was that Jake has effectively become their little brother.

No formal vote was conducted.

Nobody disagreed.

> **SORA:**  
> "Hi, Jake."

Anby carried him away.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Obol Squad's relationship with Jake has developed beyond simple medical assistance.

They treat him as a younger sibling.

Not because he requires constant protection.

Not because he is incapable of functioning independently.

And not because they consider him fragile by identity.

Rather, they recognize that Jake is currently recovering from something extraordinarily severe.

So they help.

They carry him when his legs give out.

They bring him food.

They sit with him.

They talk when he cannot.

They listen when he can.

And they let him decide when he wants to be alone.

Most importantly, they do not treat his dependence as something shameful.
