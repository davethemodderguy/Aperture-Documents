# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – BEHAVIORAL ADDENDUM**

### **DESIGNATION: M.A.R.T. BLOBS – SOCIAL MEMORY & COLLECTIVE BEHAVIOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Further research has revealed that, despite their friendly disposition, the Mart hivemind possesses an exceptionally long collective memory.

Individual Marts live only a few weeks.

The colony, however, remembers indefinitely.

Memories are inherited throughout the hivemind, meaning experiences with one Mart rapidly become experiences shared by every Mart.

---

# **IDENTITY**

Repeated attempts have been made to individually identify, number, or name Marts.

Every attempt has failed.

Examples include:

- Numbered collars.
    
- Colored ribbons.
    
- Small stickers.
    
- Temporary paint markings.
    
- RFID tags.
    

Without exception, the Mart either removes the item, loses it naturally, or simply introduces itself as:

> "Mart!"

When shown a photograph of itself and asked,

> "Is this you?"

The response is invariably:

> "Yes!"

The same question, asked while showing a completely different Mart, receives the exact same answer.

Research has concluded that individual identity has little meaning within the species.

To them...

They are all Mart.

---

# **COLLECTIVE MEMORY**

The Mart hivemind functions similarly to the flock intelligence observed in corvids.

Positive and negative experiences spread rapidly throughout the colony.

If one Mart enjoys spending time with someone...

Soon, many Marts will seek that person out.

Conversely, if one Mart has a genuinely unpleasant experience with someone, the entire colony appears to remember.

---

# **AVOIDANCE BEHAVIOR**

Personnel who consistently mistreat Marts are not attacked.

Instead...

They become mysteriously unpopular.

Documented observations include:

- Marts quietly rolling away upon seeing the individual.
    
- Empty hallways suddenly becoming Mart-free.
    
- No Mart accepting food or toys offered by that person.
    
- Marts choosing different routes through the facility.
    

The avoidance is calm, silent, and complete.

Psychological Services has noted that this social exclusion often has a greater emotional impact on personnel than any direct retaliation would have.

---

# **PLAYFUL RETALIATION**

For individuals whom the colony merely finds annoying—but not genuinely malicious—the Marts occasionally engage in harmless collective pranks.

Examples include:

- Hiding pens beneath themselves.
    
- Gently moving office chairs a few centimeters.
    
- Rearranging coffee mugs into perfect circles.
    
- Occupying keyboards exactly when someone sits down.
    
- Floating just out of reach until someone notices.
    
- Gathering outside an office window and silently watching.
    

No property damage has ever been recorded.

The Marts appear to find these activities endlessly entertaining.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Mart colony remains one of the friendliest species documented within Aperture Industries.

However, personnel should remember that friendliness should not be mistaken for forgetfulness.

Treat a Mart with kindness, and the entire colony may become your friend.

Treat one with cruelty, and the colony will remember long after that individual Mart is gone.

Fortunately, most Marts would much rather spend their time making people laugh than holding grudges.

After all, they have flowers to carry, computers to cool, and sad people to cheer up.

And occasionally...

A coffee mug to move just enough to be mildly confusing.
