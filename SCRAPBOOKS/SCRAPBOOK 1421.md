# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following Jake's recovery from the Nullwalker state, C.O.R.E. has confirmed that several of his stellar abilities remain functional.

One newly documented ability involves the creation and projection of a small **ball of azure light**.

Jake can flick the ball toward a target.

Upon contact, the target experiences a severe but temporary sensory disorientation.

The effect primarily manifests as:

- a kaleidoscope-like visual distortion;
    
- severe difficulty determining direction;
    
- apparent reversal or displacement of directional perception;
    
- confusion regarding left and right;
    
- difficulty judging where objects and people are located;
    
- and general spatial disorientation.
    

The effect lasts approximately:

> **30 minutes.**

The effective range appears comparable to that of an ordinary flashlight.

The phenomenon is currently designated:

> **STELLAR DISORIENTATION EFFECT**

---

# 1. INITIAL DISCOVERY

The ability was discovered accidentally.

Jake was sitting in the SAFEHOUSE lounge with Anby.

He was holding a small sphere of azure light between his fingers.

Anby watched.

> **ANBY:**  
> “What's that?”

Jake shrugged.

He flicked it.

The sphere traveled across the room.

It struck a nearby training target.

Nothing happened.

Anby looked at Jake.

Jake looked at Anby.

Then Jake created another.

Anby immediately raised an eyebrow.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Don't.”

Jake stopped.

---

# 2. CONTROLLED OBSERVATION

C.O.R.E. later conducted a controlled test using an appropriate non-resident test system.

The light sphere made contact with the target.

The visual sensors immediately began registering abnormal image patterns.

The recorded image resembled a rapidly shifting kaleidoscope.

Directional tracking simultaneously became unreliable.

The target's orientation sensors began producing contradictory results.

The effect gradually disappeared after approximately thirty minutes.

No permanent damage was detected.

---

# 3. HUMAN OBSERVATION

A later supervised test confirmed that the effect can occur in living targets.

The subject reported:

> “Everything looks like it's splitting apart.”

Additional descriptions included:

> “I can't tell which way I'm facing.”

and:

> “I know where the door is, but I keep turning the wrong way.”

The subject remained conscious.

No loss of identity or memory was observed.

The primary impairment was **sensory and spatial disorientation.**

---

# 4. VISUAL EFFECT

The affected person's vision does not simply become blurry.

Instead, the visual field appears to fragment.

Objects may appear duplicated or divided into multiple overlapping patterns.

Colors can appear separated.

Edges appear to bend or shift.

Movement may leave unusual visual trails.

The resulting appearance has been compared to:

> **A kaleidoscope superimposed over normal vision.**

The effect makes ordinary navigation extremely difficult.

---

# 5. DIRECTIONAL REVERSAL

The second component is more unusual.

The target's sense of direction becomes unreliable.

A person attempting to walk forward may believe they are moving in another direction.

Left and right can feel reversed.

Turning toward a familiar location may result in the person moving away from it.

Compass-based directional references are also difficult to interpret while the effect remains active.

The person is not physically forced to move.

Their perception of where they are moving is simply unreliable.

---

# 6. DURATION

The effect lasts approximately:

> **30 minutes.**

It does not appear to become significantly stronger over time.

It gradually disappears.

The target's vision and spatial orientation return to their normal baseline afterward.

No permanent neurological or visual damage has been observed.

---

# 7. RANGE

The light sphere has an effective range approximately comparable to:

> **A conventional flashlight.**

It does not possess unlimited stellar range.

Jake must be reasonably close to the intended target.

The sphere also does not appear capable of independently tracking targets over long distances.

---

Jake appears capable of creating the light deliberately.

However, his current physical condition significantly affects how frequently he can use the ability.

After producing several light spheres during testing, Jake became tired.

> **TWIGGY:**

---

# 11. MEDICAL CONSIDERATIONS

Because Jake is still recovering, C.O.R.E. does not currently consider repeated use advisable.

The ability appears to consume some portion of Jake's available energy.

The exact mechanism is unknown.

It is therefore classified as:

> **Anomalous light projection with sensory-disorientation properties.**

The effect should not be used casually against SAFEHOUSE residents.

Particularly:

- residents with existing visual anomalies;
    
- residents currently recovering from illness;
    
- residents operating vehicles;
    
- residents near stairs or elevated areas;
    
- or anyone whose disorientation could create secondary hazards.
    

---

# 12. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

The ability does **not** appear to control the target's mind.

It does not implant commands.

It does not alter memory.

It does not compel behavior.

The target remains mentally conscious.

They simply cannot reliably interpret what they are seeing or where they believe they are going.

---

# 13. CURRENT SAFETY CLASSIFICATION

**Effect:** Severe temporary sensory/spatial disorientation  
**Duration:** Approximately 30 minutes  
**Range:** Flashlight-equivalent  
**Permanent injury:** None currently observed  
**Memory alteration:** None observed  
**Mind control:** None observed  
**Required contact:** Yes  
**Known countermeasure:** Time / removal from hazardous surroundings  
**Current recommendation:** Controlled use only during recovery
