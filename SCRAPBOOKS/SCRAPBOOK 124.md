# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **TRAINING EVALUATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – KATANA PROFICIENCY DEMONSTRATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the structural damage caused during Corin Lockwood's previous unarmed training evaluation, instructors requested a second demonstration.

This time, Corin was specifically instructed to bring her primary weapon.

She arrived carrying her katana.

Before the exercise began, she quietly apologized in advance.

---

# **INITIAL OBSERVATION**

Unlike most sword practitioners within the Blacksite, Corin displayed no excitement.

No confidence.

No competitive spirit.

She simply stood in front of the training range, waiting for instructions.

When asked if she was ready, she answered:

> "...As ready as I'll ever be."

---

# **THE DEMONSTRATION**

Five reinforced combat dummies were placed downrange.

Each was rated to withstand anti-material rifle fire.

Corin drew her katana.

There was no dramatic flourish.

No stance meant to impress.

Just one slow breath.

Then...

One swing.

Personnel near the range reported hearing almost nothing.

The blade passed through the air with barely a sound.

Nearly a full second later—

A crimson Ether slash appeared across the range.

All five dummies separated cleanly.

The reinforced steel frames behind them split apart.

The impact barriers behind the frames were cut into smooth, polished sections.

The final blast wall sustained a shallow diagonal cut approximately six centimeters deep before the Ether dissipated.

Silence filled the room.

Again.

---

# **ENGINEERING NOTE**

Inspection revealed that the blade itself showed no signs of damage.

The cuts displayed almost no deformation, indicating an extremely clean transfer of force.

Engineering concluded that the majority of the destructive effect originated from Ether released during the strike rather than from the blade's edge alone.

Neko's written conclusion consisted of a single sentence:

> "...I'm making the walls thicker."

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Everyone looked at the range.

Corin looked at the floor.

She slowly sheathed the katana.

Then quietly asked:

> "...How expensive was that?"

No one answered.

Corin sighed.

> "...Sorry."

---

# **LUMI'S OBSERVATION**

Lumi approached after the exercise.

She noticed Corin avoiding the damaged range entirely.

Instead, Corin stared at the katana resting in its sheath.

> "...I don't even swing that hard."

Lumi gently rested a hand on Corin's shoulder.

> "You weren't trying to destroy anything."

Corin nodded.

> "...I know."

A pause.

> "...That's the part that scares me."

---

# **INSTRUCTOR'S ASSESSMENT**

Contrary to expectations, instructors concluded that Corin possessed exceptional control.

Every strike landed precisely where intended.

No collateral damage occurred outside the projected Ether path.

The damage was immense.

The accuracy was absolute.

The concern was never Corin's discipline.

It was the overwhelming amount of power accompanying even restrained movements.

Director Lucious reviewed the report.

He added:

> "Power without restraint is dangerous."

> "Power with restraint can still frighten the person who possesses it."

He continued:

> "Corin has mastered the first."

> "She is still learning the second."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The training evaluation confirmed that Corin's swordsmanship remains extraordinarily precise.

No concerns regarding discipline or control were identified.

Medical Services continue to monitor the psychological impact these demonstrations have on Corin herself.

The greatest damage recorded that day was not the ruined training range.

It was the familiar expression on Corin's face as she quietly asked:

> "...Did I break it again?"

**End of Training Evaluation Report**
