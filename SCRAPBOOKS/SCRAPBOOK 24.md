## **PURPOSE**

A joint training day was organized at a remote Starlands range.

While most participants observed, the primary demonstrations consisted of:

- Norma conducting a sustained live-fire exercise with her assigned machine gun.
    
- Miss Neko demonstrating the **Bliss of Whitefall**, a handheld terraforming device, in a separate safe zone.
    

The demonstrations were conducted independently with strict safety boundaries.

---

## **LIVE-FIRE EXERCISE**

Norma arrived wearing her fully deployed **Juggernaut Armor**.

The nanotechnological armor unfolded from its compact state and locked into place within seconds.

Her **35 kg belt-fed 7.62 mm machine gun** was loaded and inspected.

Targets for the exercise consisted of reinforced steel silhouettes and simulated Ether Walker training dummies positioned throughout the range.

At the command to begin...

Norma shouldered the machine gun.

Then...

The valley echoed with sustained automatic fire.

---

## **AFTER-ACTION RESULTS**

**Rounds Fired:**

**550**

Training observers noted remarkably consistent recoil control throughout the exercise.

Every firing sequence was deliberate, with Norma adjusting her bursts as target distances changed.

By the conclusion of the course:

- Every designated target had been neutralized.
    
- No safety violations were recorded.
    
- The firing line remained under complete control.
    

Several of the reinforced training dummies resembling Ether Walkers were left with little more than their supporting frames still standing.

---

## **MEANWHILE...**

Several hundred meters away, safely outside the danger area...

Miss Neko activated the **Bliss of Whitefall**.

The handheld terraformer reshaped a section of the Starlands terrain, raising gentle hills, redirecting a small stream, and causing flowers to bloom across freshly formed ground.

The contrast was... noticeable.

One side of the valley echoed with disciplined automatic fire.

The other was peaceful enough that Star, Bao, Frost, and Taiga sat on a picnic blanket watching the landscape change.

Belle summed it up perfectly.

> "...One side is tactical training."

She pointed toward Miss Neko.

> "...The other side is gardening."
