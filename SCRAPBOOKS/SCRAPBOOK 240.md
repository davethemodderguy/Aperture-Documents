### **RESTRICTED PERSONNEL DOSSIER**

## **SUBJECT: DIALYN – PRE-EMPLOYMENT HISTORY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Medical, Executive & Psychological Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although Dialyn is widely recognized throughout the Krampus Compliance Authority as an endlessly cheerful customer support specialist, very few personnel are aware of her documented history prior to joining the Authority.

This information remained sealed at Dialyn's request.

Only personnel with Level 9 clearance or higher may access this file.

---

# **ORIGIN**

Dialyn was born in a remote village within the Banlands.

The settlement was isolated, self-sufficient, and largely disconnected from the outside world.

Historical records concerning the village are sparse.

Its official name has since been lost.

---

# **THE HOLLOW INCIDENT**

During Dialyn's childhood, an anomalous phenomenon known as a **Hollow** manifested near the village.

A Hollow is described as a spherical region of absolute darkness that continuously leaks concentrated Ether into its surroundings.

The phenomenon rapidly destabilized the area.

As conditions worsened, fear spread throughout the village.

Rather than evacuate...

The villagers concluded that a sacrifice was necessary.

Dialyn was chosen.

The reasons for her selection remain unknown.

No surviving witness has been identified.

She was thrown into the Hollow.

---

# **SURVIVAL**

Against all recorded expectations...

Dialyn survived.

How she endured exposure to the Hollow remains unexplained.

Research Division has found no existing model capable of accounting for her survival.

When she eventually emerged...

The village was silent.

The Hollow had continued expanding after her sacrifice.

The settlement had been destroyed.

Its inhabitants had been killed.

Among the dead were Dialyn's family.

No survivors were located.

---

# **AFTERMATH**

Recovery teams found Dialyn wandering alone several days later.

Medical examinations recorded severe exhaustion and dehydration.

Despite the circumstances, no physical injuries consistent with prolonged Ether exposure were identified.

Psychological evaluations from the period describe Dialyn as remarkably composed.

One physician wrote:

> "She cries when speaking about her family."

> "She smiles when speaking to everyone else."

That pattern has remained largely unchanged.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL NOTES**

Modern Psychological Services believe Dialyn's exceptional patience and empathy are, in part, shaped by her past.

She has repeatedly expressed the belief that ordinary frustrations should not be taken out on others.

During one interview, she explained:

> "Everyone who calls me is having a bad day."

She smiled softly.

> "...I'd rather not make it worse."

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Dialyn has declined repeated offers to investigate the remains of her former village.

Her stated reason is brief.

> "...There's nobody waiting for me there."

Instead, she has devoted herself to helping complete strangers—one phone call at a time.

Few of those callers will ever know the life she lived before answering their questions.

Fewer still would guess that the woman who greets every conversation with warmth once survived an event that should have claimed her life.

Her past remains a private sorrow.

Her kindness remains a deliberate choice.
