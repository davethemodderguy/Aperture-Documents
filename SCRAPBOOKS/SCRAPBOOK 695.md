### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / SECURITY INCIDENT LOG**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — ARCHIVE INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Age:** Approximately 5 years old  
**Incident Type:** **UNAUTHORIZED MEDICAL RECORD ACCESS / MISSING CHILD**  
**Status:** **CRITICAL — SEARCH ACTIVE**

---

# **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

Alice Lockwood entered the Blacksite Archives while wandering the facility.

A restricted medical terminal containing her complete medical record had been left open.

Alice found it.

She read it.

The access restriction existed specifically because of this possibility.

It failed.

The record contained extensive descriptions of Alice's:

- Physical weakness
    
- Permanent limitations
    
- Severe immune deficiency
    
- High blood toxicity
    
- Extreme Ether concentration
    
- Susceptibility to illness
    
- Medical risks
    
- Dependence on caregivers
    
- Previous survival conditions
    

Alice read everything.

Nobody noticed.

---

# **DISCOVERY**

Security footage shows Alice standing in front of the terminal for approximately **nineteen minutes**.

She read the document from beginning to end.

She did not appear frightened at first.

She simply stared at the screen.

Occasionally, she scrolled upward.

Then downward.

Then returned to certain sections.

The footage shows her reading the phrase:

> **"HIGH-RISK DEPENDENT"**

several times.

She remained motionless.

Eventually, she whispered:

> **"...High risk."**

She continued reading.

---

# **THE LAST SECTION**

The final portion of the record contained instructions regarding Alice's care.

One line appeared to hold her attention for an unusually long period:

> **"The objective is to keep Alice alive."**

Alice stared at it.

She then whispered:

> **"Keep Alice alive..."**

A few seconds later:

> **"...because Alice is dangerous?"**

The terminal contained no such statement.

Alice had interpreted the medical language through the understanding of a five-year-old.

That interpretation was enough.

---

# **AFTERMATH**

Alice closed the terminal.

She left the Archives.

She did not return to SAFEHOUSE.

She did not tell Corin.

She did not tell Belle.

She did not tell Orphie.

She did not ask anyone for help.

Security footage shows her walking through several corridors.

She encountered personnel.

Every time someone looked toward her, Alice smiled.

> **"Hi!"**

Nobody suspected anything was wrong.

---

# **THE LAST KNOWN SIGHTING**

Approximately five hours later, Alice was reported missing.

Corin initially assumed Alice was with Belle.

Belle assumed she was with Corin.

Orphie assumed she was in the Treehouse.

None of them had seen her.

The realization came slowly.

Then all at once.

**Alice was nowhere.**

---

# **SAFEHOUSE SEARCH**

The Treehouse was searched first.

Empty.

Her usual sleeping area:

Empty.

Garden:

Empty.

Central Living Area:

Empty.

Kitchen:

Empty.

Alice's usual hiding places:

Empty.

The search expanded into the Blacksite.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin eventually reached the Archives.

She saw the terminal.

Still open.

She read the access history.

Then the footage.

She watched Alice reading her medical record.

Corin went completely silent.

The screen displayed the final entry.

Corin whispered:

> **"...No."**

She immediately understood.

Alice had seen everything.

The things nobody wanted her to see.

The things written by adults attempting to protect her.

The words that were supposed to remain private.

And now Alice was gone.

---

# **THE SEARCH**

C.O.R.E. activated a full missing-person protocol.

All available personnel were notified.

The search includes:

- SAFEHOUSE
    
- Treehouse perimeter
    
- Blacksite residential sectors
    
- Archives
    
- Maintenance corridors
    
- Exterior grounds
    
- Mountain routes
    
- Forested areas
    
- Known hiding locations
    
- Previously visited locations
    

Alice's physical condition makes the situation particularly dangerous.

She cannot walk for extended periods.

Her stamina is extremely low.

Her immune system is severely compromised.

Her body is vulnerable to injury and illness.

Cold exposure represents a significant threat.

---

# **CORIN'S FEAR**

Corin has refused to return to SAFEHOUSE.

She continues searching.

When someone suggests resting, she refuses.

> **"She's five."**

> **"She can't go far."**

> **"Then we find where she stopped."**

Nobody has managed to convince her to stop searching.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle has been visibly distressed since the incident.

She keeps repeating:

> **"She wouldn't leave because she wanted to."**

> **"She thinks she has to."**

That statement has become the central assumption of the investigation.

Alice has previously demonstrated a tendency to hide distress and avoid becoming a burden.

Reading her medical record may have reinforced that belief.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has ordered every available surveillance system to be reviewed.

No assumption has been made that Alice is safe simply because she can survive harsh conditions.

Quite the opposite.

Her ability to survive alone is considered irrelevant.

She is five years old.

She is medically fragile.

And she is currently alone.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT**

The most likely motivation is not rejection of SAFEHOUSE.

It is **self-removal.**

Alice may believe:

> **"Everyone has to take care of Alice."**

> **"Alice makes things difficult."**

> **"Alice is dangerous to herself."**

> **"Alice is a burden."**

> **"If Alice leaves, everyone can be happy."**

These conclusions are false.

But Alice does not necessarily know that.

---

# **SECURITY FAILURE**

The Archives incident has been formally classified as a major security failure.

The medical record should never have been accessible to Alice.

The responsible terminal has been locked down.

All copies of Alice's medical records are undergoing an access audit.

No disciplinary action will interfere with the ongoing search.

Finding Alice takes priority.

---

# **MESSAGE TO ALL PERSONNEL**

If Alice is located:

**Do not chase her.**

**Do not surround her.**

**Do not immediately question her.**

**Do not mention the medical record.**

**Do not tell her she caused everyone to panic.**

**Do not tell her she made things difficult.**

**Do not make her feel responsible for the search.**

Approach slowly.

Let her recognize someone she trusts.

Then say:

> **"Alice, we're here."**

Nothing more is required immediately.

# **FINAL NOTE**

The record was written to protect Alice.

It described every weakness in her body so that the adults around her would know how to keep her safe.

Alice did not read it that way.

She read:

**Weak.**

**Fragile.**

**Sick.**

**High risk.**

**Dependent.**

She saw everything everyone had been trying to protect her from seeing.

Then she disappeared.

Not because she hated SAFEHOUSE.

Not because she stopped loving Corin.

Not because she wanted to abandon everyone.

But because a frightened five-year-old may have reached the simplest conclusion available to her:

> **"If Alice is the problem, Alice should leave."**

C.O.R.E. formally rejects that conclusion.

Alice is not a problem.

Alice is not a burden.

Alice is not disposable.

And wherever she is now—

**someone is coming to find her.**
