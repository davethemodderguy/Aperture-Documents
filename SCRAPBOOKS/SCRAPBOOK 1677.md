# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Clover was initially unaware of **Jake**.

This was not considered unusual.

Jake's current existence within SAFEHOUSE is complicated.

He is not currently a conventional full-time resident.

He does not remain physically present continuously.

He does not communicate during his full-moon manifestations.

He does not possess a conventional sleeping schedule.

And, following the events surrounding his prolonged isolation, he now spends most of his time in his original stellar manifestation.

On full-moon nights, however, Jake can return to SAFEHOUSE in his childlike physical manifestation.

Clover learned about him after noticing an object in the common room:

> **Jake's teddy bear.**

She asked who it belonged to.

The answer required considerably more explanation than expected.

---

# 1. THE TEDDY BEAR

Clover was sitting in the SAFEHOUSE common room.

She had her notebook open.

A candle was positioned beside her despite the room being adequately illuminated.

She was drawing a small plant.

After several minutes, she noticed the teddy bear resting on a nearby chair.

She stared at it.

The bear was old.

Its fabric had been repaired.

Several scars remained around the joints of its left arm and right leg.

It was clearly cared for.

Clover looked around.

Nobody appeared to be holding it.

She pointed.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Whose is that?"

The room became quiet.

Several residents looked at one another.

Jane eventually answered.

> **JANE:**  
> "Jake's."

Clover waited.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Who's Jake?"

Another pause.

The answer was eventually provided by Bao.

> **BAO:**  
> "He's... complicated."

Clover nodded slowly.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Okay."

---

# 3. THE FULL-MOON PATTERN

Clover was then informed of Jake's current manifestation pattern.

On full-moon nights, Jake can return to SAFEHOUSE.

He appears in his childlike form.

He cannot currently speak during these visits.

He may:

- walk through the common areas;
    
- eat food left for him;
    
- look around;
    
- visit familiar rooms;
    
- sit quietly;
    
- listen if someone speaks to him;
    
- and eventually leave before dawn.
    

He does not remain permanently.

Nobody is permitted to chase him.

Nobody is permitted to force interaction.

Nobody is permitted to treat his appearances as containment breaches.

Clover listened carefully.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "So he's a ghost?"

Neri, sitting nearby, quietly corrected her.

> **NERI:**  
> "Not exactly."

Clover looked at her.

Neri pointed upward.

Then made a small circular motion with her finger.

> **NERI:**  
> "Star."

Clover understood.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "A ghost star."

Neri considered this.

Then nodded.

Nobody corrected her further.

# 4. THE STORY

Clover was eventually given a simplified explanation of what happened to Jake.

She was told that he had once lived in SAFEHOUSE normally.

He had friends.

He had people who cared about him.

He had a room.

He had a teddy bear.

Then, over time, people became busy.

They stopped visiting him.

They stopped thinking about him as often.

Jake became increasingly isolated.

Eventually, he believed everyone had forgotten him.

The resulting psychological collapse was severe.

He eventually left.

His physical manifestation disappeared.

A blue star later appeared in the sky.

Clover's expression became increasingly serious.

She looked at the teddy bear again.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "They forgot him?"

Bao answered carefully.

> **BAO:**  
> "They didn't mean to."

Clover remained quiet.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "But they did."

Bao nodded.

> **BAO:**  
> "Yes."

Clover looked down.

She understood the distinction.

Intent did not erase the result.

# 6. THE TEDDY BEAR'S HISTORY

Clover was later told that the teddy bear is more than a toy.

It is Jake's cardiac vessel.

Its heartbeat represents Jake's actual core activity.

When Jake's physical manifestation is present, the teddy bear is connected to him.

When the bear's heartbeat stops, Jake's condition can become critical.

The bear has previously been lost.

Jake nearly died.

The bear was eventually recovered.

It was repaired.

It still carries scars.

Clover carefully approached it.

She did not touch it.

Instead, she looked at the damaged joints.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Someone repaired him."

> **BAO:**  
> "Yes."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Good."

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Clover's reaction to Jake's story has been notably quiet.

This is unsurprising.

Her own history contains a similar emotional concept:

> **Being blamed for something you did not intend.**

Jake was not intentionally abandoned.

Clover did not intentionally cause her father's death.

Both events involve outcomes that were not maliciously chosen.

But both still caused permanent damage.

Clover also understands something else.

She knows what it is like to lose people and retain only fragments of them.

Jake lost people who still remembered him.

Clover lost the memories of the people she loved.

The circumstances are different.

The pain is not necessarily comparable.

C.O.R.E. therefore does not intend to manufacture a friendship between them.

If they meet, they meet.

If they do not, they do not.

There is no need to force another relationship onto either resident.

# CLOSING OBSERVATION

Later that night, Clover returned to the common room.

She placed a small pot of dirt on the table.

She touched the soil.

A tiny four-leaf clover emerged.

She looked at it.

Then at Jake's teddy bear.

Clover carefully placed the pot beside the chair.

She did not attach a note.

She did not write Jake's name.

She simply left it there.

A small green plant.

Four leaves.

A quiet offering from someone who knew what it was like to lose everything and still keep going.

The next morning, the clover was still there.

Nobody had touched it.

Nobody knew whether Jake had seen it.

But the pot remained exactly where Clover had left it.

And on the next full moon—

Clover would know who the blue star was.
