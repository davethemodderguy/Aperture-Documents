# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **EXECUTIVE INCIDENT REPORT**

### **DISCLOSURE OF CONFIDENTIAL MEDICAL CONDITION**

**Subject:** Senior Manager Norma Lockwood  
**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Executive Personnel

---

## **SUMMARY**

For several years, Senior Manager **Norma Lockwood** successfully concealed her diagnosis of **Ether Regression Syndrome (ERS)** from nearly everyone around her.

Most personnel knew her as the dependable senior manager who could solve almost any problem, spend an afternoon engineering with Neko, and carry a 35 kg machine gun as though it were an ordinary backpack.

Very few ever questioned the emergency medical injector she always carried.

Most assumed it was standard emergency equipment.

It was not.

Following a recent medical review, Norma's condition was formally disclosed to a limited group of personnel whose responsibilities required them to know.

For many, it was the first time they had learned that Norma lived with chronic, unpredictable pain.

---

## **REACTION**

The announcement was met with silence.

Several personnel later admitted they initially thought it was a mistake.

Norma had never complained.

She had never asked for lighter duties.

She had never mentioned the pain.

Looking back, however, many recalled moments where she had quietly excused herself for a few minutes before returning as though nothing had happened.

Those moments suddenly made sense.

---

## **THE INJECTOR**

Medical staff confirmed that the injector Norma carries is prescribed specifically for managing severe ERS pain episodes.

It is not a stimulant for combat performance.

It is emergency medication intended to help her function through sudden episodes until the pain subsides.

## **STATUS**

Senior Manager Norma Lockwood remains on active duty under medical supervision.

Following the disclosure, personnel have been instructed on the appropriate response to an ERS episode and to notify Medical immediately if Norma requires assistance.

Around the Blacksite and the KCA, one thing quietly changed.

People stopped assuming Norma was always the one looking after everyone else.

Sometimes...

She needed someone to look after her, too.
