# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **TRAINING INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – UNARMED STRENGTH DEMONSTRATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

As part of a routine skills assessment, Corin Lockwood was invited to participate in a combat refresher.

The intent was to evaluate her current physical condition following her relocation to Heaven's Blacksite.

Corin arrived at the training hall.

Without her katana.

---

# **INITIAL EVENTS**

When informed that her primary weapon remained in her office, Corin simply sighed.

> "...I forgot it."

A staff member offered to retrieve it.

Corin shook her head.

> "...Too far."

She looked toward the reinforced training dummy standing in the center of the range.

Then quietly muttered:

> "...One punch is probably enough."

---

# **INCIDENT**

Corin stepped forward.

No visible Ether manifestation was detected beforehand.

No preparation.

No combat stance.

She simply threw a straight punch.

The instant her fist connected—

A crimson Ether shockwave erupted from the point of impact.

The reinforced training dummy disintegrated almost instantly.

The shockwave continued forward.

It tore through the reinforced concrete wall behind the target before dissipating safely inside an empty containment chamber.

Emergency alarms briefly activated due to structural damage.

No personnel were injured.

The training hall, however, required immediate repairs.

---

# **POST-INCIDENT SILENCE**

The room remained completely silent.

One instructor slowly lowered their clipboard.

Another quietly asked Engineering how much replacing the wall would cost.

Neko, after seeing the damage, reportedly said:

> "...Well."

> "...That explains why we overbuild the training rooms."

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin stared at the empty space where the dummy had stood.

She did not appear impressed.

She did not appear proud.

Instead, she lowered her hand.

Her shoulders slumped.

She whispered quietly enough that only Lumi, standing nearby, heard her.

> "...I hate this."

Lumi looked at her.

Corin continued.

> "...I'm supposed to be an accountant."

She looked at the hole in the wall.

Then at her own hand.

> "...Or maybe..."

A long pause.

> "...I'm just a corpse whose punch hits harder than a truck."

No one answered immediately.

Because no one knew what to say.

The destruction around them wasn't what worried the room.

Corin's reaction was.

---

# **LUMI'S RESPONSE**

Lumi walked over without looking at the damaged wall.

She looked only at Corin.

> "Do you know what I saw?"

Corin shook her head.

> "I saw someone who forgot her sword."

For a brief moment, Corin almost laughed.

Almost.

Lumi gently held her daughter's hand.

> "What happened to that dummy doesn't tell me who you are."

> "It tells me what your body can do."

> "Those are different things."

Corin quietly looked away.

She wasn't convinced.

But she listened.

---

# **MEDICAL NOTE**

Medical Services concluded that the demonstration was not the result of anger or a loss of control.

Corin exercised deliberate restraint throughout the exercise.

The destructive output was attributed to her abnormal Ether-enhanced physiology rather than hostile intent.

Medical staff recommended limiting unnecessary demonstrations, as Corin consistently experiences increased self-loathing after witnessing the scale of her own strength.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The damaged training hall has been repaired.

Corin returned to her accounting office shortly after the exercise.

No further combat testing was requested that day.

While the event demonstrated extraordinary physical capability, Medical Services emphasized a different conclusion.

Corin's greatest struggle is not controlling her strength.

It is believing that possessing it has made her less human.

**End of Training Incident Report**
