# Executive Summary

Phoenix accidentally injured Jake.

More precisely:

She accidentally threw his teddy bear.

The intended target was a nearby ball.

Phoenix reached for what she believed was the ball, picked up Jake's teddy bear instead, and threw it with considerable force toward a nearby wall.

The teddy bear struck the wall.

Jake immediately experienced severe pain.

He was not permanently injured.

His physical condition is currently stable.

The incident nevertheless caused Jake to cry.

Phoenix was profoundly distressed by what happened.

The following report was written by Phoenix herself.

---

# PHOENIX — PERSONAL INCIDENT REPORT

**AUTHOR:** Phoenix Raffedella  
**TIME:** 18:26

I hurt Jake today.

I need to write this down because I don't trust myself to explain it properly out loud.

I was in the common area.

There was a ball on the floor.

Jake's teddy bear was nearby.

I wanted the ball.

I picked up the wrong thing.

I threw it.

Hard.

I thought it was the ball.

It wasn't.

It was his teddy bear.

It hit the wall.

Jake screamed.

---

I have never wanted to reverse time more badly.

---

# WHAT HAPPENED AFTERWARD

Jake immediately grabbed his chest.

He started crying.

He wasn't badly injured.

He was in pain.

There is a difference.

His teddy bear contains his heart.

I knew that.

I knew that.

And I still threw it.

Not intentionally.

I know that.

But knowing that doesn't make me feel better.

---

# JAKE

Jake looked at me.

He was crying.

He said:

> **Jake:** “My heart...”

I froze.

I checked him immediately.

His core was still functioning.

The teddy bear was intact enough to remain stable.

Jake himself was physically okay.

But he was hurting.

And scared.

I apologized.

I kept apologizing.

He didn't say anything for a while.

Then he hugged his teddy bear.

I don't think he was angry.

That somehow made me feel worse.

# I KNOW IT WAS AN ACCIDENT

I know.

Everyone keeps telling me that.

Bao told me.

Neri told me.

Even Jake eventually told me.

> **Jake:** “You didn't mean it.”

I know I didn't.

But I still did it.

There's a difference between intention and result.

The result was that Jake was crying because I threw his heart at a wall.

That sentence is horrible.

---

# I SHOULD HAVE CHECKED

I should have looked.

I should have checked what I was grabbing.

I was careless.

Maybe I was tired.

Maybe I was distracted.

It doesn't matter.

I picked up something without checking what it was and threw it.

That is exactly the sort of stupid accident I get angry about when someone else does it.

There was a ball.

A perfectly ordinary ball.

I missed it.

And somehow I managed to throw the one thing I absolutely should not have thrown.

---

# JAKE FORGAVE ME

Eventually, Jake came back.

He was still holding the teddy bear.

He looked at me.

Then he said:

> **Jake:** “It's okay.”

I told him it wasn't.

He said:

> **Jake:** “You said sorry.”

I said:

> **Phoenix:** “That's not enough.”

He looked confused.

Then he hugged the teddy bear.

> **Jake:** “It is for me.”

I didn't know what to say.

So I sat beside him.

I am writing this because I don't want anyone to treat Jake differently because of what happened.

He is fine.

He doesn't need everyone hovering over him.

He doesn't need everyone asking if he's okay every five minutes.

And he definitely doesn't need people blaming themselves forever.

Especially me.

But I am still going to feel terrible about it.

Because he trusted me.

And I hurt him.

Even accidentally.

# FINAL NOTE

I am going to be more careful.

Not because Jake is fragile.

Not because his heart is in a teddy bear.

Not because everyone is going to remind me about this forever.

Because he is a child.

And he trusted me.

That's enough reason.
