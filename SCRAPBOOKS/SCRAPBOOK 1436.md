# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Jake is gone.**

No confirmed location has been established.

No accessible elevator coordinate leads to him.

No SAFEHOUSE system currently contains a destination corresponding to his floor.

No spatial signature has been successfully reacquired.

The disappearance occurred immediately after Jake overheard statements made about him during Anby's birthday celebration.

The statements were made by Twiggy while intoxicated.

C.O.R.E. cannot establish that Twiggy intended Jake to disappear.

It can establish something else.

Jake heard her.

Jake believed what he heard.

And Jake left.

---

# 1. THE LAST CONFIRMED SIGHTING

The final confirmed visual record of Jake occurred at approximately 20:19.

He was standing in the doorway of the birthday gathering.

He was carrying:

- one ceramic bowl;
    
- several fresh apples;
    
- one handwritten birthday card.
    

He had recently recovered from five weeks of severe illness.

His halo was active.

His stellar glow was stable.

His teddy bear's heartbeat was audible.

He appeared healthy.

He had come voluntarily.

He had come for Anby.

He did not remain for the party.

---

# 2. THE STATEMENTS

The following statements were made by Twiggy while intoxicated:

> **"Who cares?"**

> **"Friends my ass."**

> **"He's so annoying."**

> **"I don't even bother to check on him for the last five weeks."**

> **"I just assign a nurse to do stuff while I handle the actual important stuff."**

And finally:

> **"If he dies right now nobody will actually care and move on."**

Jake heard all of them.

---

# 3. THE CARD

The card Jake had brought for Anby was recovered from the floor.

It read:

> **"Hi Anby, Happy Birthday. I'm better now, can we go somewhere sometime? Have fun! Knew apples was for your favorite so I brought some."**

There are no corrections.

No angry additions.

No accusations.

No indication that Jake had intended anything other than a birthday surprise.

The card was written by someone who believed he was welcome.

---

# 4. THE EXIT

Jake did not speak.

He turned around.

He entered the elevator.

Anby attempted to stop him.

The doors closed.

The elevator descended.

His floor button disappeared.

No destination was displayed.

No coordinates were available.

Jake was gone.

---

# 6. WHAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT

This is not the previous Nullwalker incident.

Jake has not transformed.

No Null Shell has been detected.

No anomalous stellar collapse has been observed.

His teddy bear remains physically present.

His medical records remain intact.

His existence has not been erased from the world.

He is simply:

**Somewhere else.**

And C.O.R.E. cannot currently reach him.

---

# 7. TWIGGY

Twiggy is no longer intoxicated.

She has been medically assessed.

She has not attempted to defend her statements.

She has repeatedly stated:

> **"I didn't mean it."**

> **"I was drunk."**

> **"I didn't think he was there."**

> **"I didn't know he could hear me."**

None of these statements change what happened.

Jake heard her.

He believed her.

And he left.

Twiggy has requested permission to search for him.

Permission has not yet been granted.

---

# 8. BAO

Bao has not left the elevator.

She has remained beside the controls for an extended period.

She repeatedly checks the destination panel.

The missing button remains absent.

At one point, she quietly asked:

> **"What if he thinks I agreed with her?"**

Nobody answered.

Bao then asked:

> **"What if he thinks we all agreed?"**

Still no answer.

---

# 9. PHOENIX

Phoenix has remained unusually quiet.

She has reviewed Jake's medical records repeatedly.

Five weeks of observations.

Five weeks of recovery.

Five weeks of nurses documenting his condition.

Five weeks in which Jake had almost no freedom.

And then, on the first evening he was allowed to leave:

He chose to attend Anby's birthday.

Phoenix eventually closed the file.

She said:

> **"We should have checked on him ourselves."**

No further statement was recorded.

---

# 10. ANBY

Anby has retained Jake's card.

She has also retained one of the apples.

She has not eaten it.

She has not put it away.

She simply carries it.

Her birthday celebration was formally terminated.

No one attempted to restart it.

Anby has repeatedly asked:

> **"Can we find him?"**

The current answer remains:

> **"We're trying."**

---

# 11. NERI

Neri has said almost nothing.

She found the broken bowl.

She found the remaining apples.

She found the card.

She read it several times.

Eventually, she wrote a single sentence on a piece of paper:

> **"He came here because he trusted us."**

She then folded the paper.

No further statement was made.

---

# 12. THE PREVIOUS INCIDENT

The comparison to Jake's previous disappearance has been raised repeatedly.

Previously, Jake was forgotten.

He was left outside.

He was isolated.

He became a Nullwalker.

He was eventually recovered.

The lesson established at the time was supposed to be simple:

> **Never assume Jake will ask for help.**

The current incident demonstrates a different failure.

Jake did not need help.

He needed to know that he was wanted.

Instead, he heard someone explicitly state that nobody would care if he died.

---

# 13. RESPONSIBILITY

C.O.R.E. cannot reasonably assign the entirety of this incident to a single person.

Twiggy made the statements.

However:

Bao was busy.

Phoenix was busy.

Other residents were busy.

Nurses cared for Jake because the primary caregivers had other responsibilities.

Nobody deliberately abandoned him.

Nobody deliberately wanted him gone.

And yet Jake spent five weeks receiving care from people who were not the people he expected to care about him.

Then he returned to his friends.

And heard that even those friends had stopped checking.

The incident therefore represents more than one person's mistake.

It represents a collective failure of communication.

---

# 14. THE MOST PAINFUL DETAIL

Jake had recovered.

That fact should have been the beginning of something good.

His halo had returned.

His teddy bear's heart had returned.

He could walk again.

He could leave the Medical Bay.

He could finally do something ordinary.

So he chose something ordinary.

A birthday.

A friend.

Apples.

A handwritten card.

He did not ask anyone to take care of him.

He did not ask anyone to apologize.

He did not ask anyone for anything.

He simply wanted to give Anby a birthday present.

And he heard that his existence was something people could simply move on from.

# FINAL OBSERVATION

There is no body.

There is no Null Shell.

There is no grave.

There is no death confirmation.

There is only an empty elevator destination.

An empty place where Jake's floor used to be.

And a birthday card sitting on a table.

Anby has read it so many times that she no longer needs to look at the words.

She knows them.

> **"Hi Anby, Happy Birthday."**

> **"I'm better now, can we go somewhere sometime?"**

> **"Have fun!"**

> **"Knew apples was for your favorite so I brought some."**

He had recovered.

He had remembered her birthday.

He had remembered her favorite food.

He had wanted to spend time with her.

And now nobody knows where he is.

Twiggy has apologized.

Bao has cried.

Phoenix has blamed herself.

Anby has stopped celebrating her birthday.

Neri has not left the elevator.

Everyone is searching.

But the most frightening part is not that Jake is missing.

It is that he left **after finally getting better.**

He did not disappear because his body failed.

He disappeared because, for one terrible moment, he believed the people waiting for him had stopped caring.

And this time—

**there was nobody waiting at the other end of the elevator.**
