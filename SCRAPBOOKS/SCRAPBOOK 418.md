## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT LOG**

# **CORIN — PSYCHOLOGICAL COLLAPSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Critical Psychological Concern  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Associated Personnel:** Orphie / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Marts  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **CRITICAL**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's condition has deteriorated significantly following Orphie's disappearance.

The isolation that was previously identified as a serious risk has now progressed beyond ordinary grief, loneliness, or emotional distress.

Corin is no longer behaving as a psychologically stable individual.

Personnel who have interacted with her describe her as:

> **"Not herself anymore."**

She remains functional enough to perform basic tasks.

Her emotional and behavioral state, however, has become increasingly detached from reality and from her previous personality.

---

# **THE CHANGE**

At first, Corin simply became quiet.

Then withdrawn.

Then exhausted.

Eventually, something changed.

She began speaking to the empty rooms.

Not occasionally.

Regularly.

She sometimes addresses Orphie as though she is still present.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

Corin has started maintaining Orphie's side of the treehouse.

She prepares two portions of food.

She places one on the opposite side of the table.

Sometimes she waits.

Sometimes she speaks.

> "...You're late."

Silence.

> "...You always come back late."

Silence.

> "...It's okay."

She continues eating.

---

# **THE EMPTY CHAIR**

Corin has begun treating the empty chair as occupied.

She moves around it rather than through it.

She leaves space beside it.

She sometimes asks:

> "...Are you comfortable?"

No response.

She nods anyway.

> "...Good."

---

# **THE COOKBOOK**

The cookbook remains where Orphie discarded it.

Corin has not used it.

She has, however, begun reading it.

Not for herself.

She reads recipes aloud.

> "...This one was good."

> "...You made this before."

> "...Remember?"

There is nobody there to answer.

---

# **THE MISSING PERSON**

Corin has been informed repeatedly that Orphie has not returned.

She understands this.

When asked where Orphie is, she sometimes answers:

> "...She went for a walk."

When reminded that Orphie has been gone for an extended period:

> "...She'll come back."

When asked whether she believes Orphie is alive:

> "...She has to be."

---

# **CONTRADICTORY BEHAVIOR**

Corin demonstrates awareness of reality in some circumstances.

She knows Orphie is missing.

She knows nobody has found her.

She knows the treehouse is empty.

Yet she simultaneously behaves as though Orphie remains present.

C.O.R.E. considers this a significant psychological deterioration.

---

# **MART OBSERVATION**

The Marts have noticed.

They have begun approaching Corin more frequently.

Unlike their interactions with Orphie, they do not appear frightened of Corin.

One Mart climbed onto the empty chair.

Corin looked at it.

> "...You're sitting in her spot."

The Mart looked at the chair.

Then at Corin.

It moved.

Corin quietly said:

> "...Thank you."

The Mart remained nearby.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

SAFEHOUSE personnel have become increasingly concerned.

Star attempted to speak with Corin.

Corin listened.

She answered normally.

For several minutes, everything appeared fine.

Then Star asked:

> "...Do you want to come stay with us?"

Corin smiled.

> "...Orphie doesn't like leaving the treehouse."

Star froze.

> "...Corin."

> "...She'll be upset if I leave."

---

# **THE PROBLEM**

Corin has begun structuring her life around an absent person.

Her routines revolve around Orphie's presumed return.

She cooks for two.

She keeps the second side of the room untouched.

She leaves the door unlocked.

She sometimes waits awake until morning.

She has stopped allowing herself to believe that Orphie may never return.

---

# **SLEEP DEPRIVATION**

Corin's sleep has deteriorated severely.

She frequently remains awake throughout the night.

When asked why:

> "...Someone has to be here when she comes back."

She does not accept the suggestion that Orphie could return while she is asleep.

> "...What if she comes home?"

> "...What if nobody is here?"

---

# **LUNA'S OBSERVATION**

Luna is deeply disturbed.

> "...Corin is breaking."

She pauses.

> "...She waited too long."

---

# **SOLARIA'S OBSERVATION**

Solaria has been considerably more direct.

> "...She lost the one person she was living with."

> "...Then she lost the ability to accept that person was gone."

> "...Now she's living around the absence."

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

Corin's condition is considered **critical**.

Primary contributing factors include:

- Prolonged isolation.
    
- Severe emotional attachment to Orphie.
    
- Sleep deprivation.
    
- Lack of meaningful companionship.
    
- Unresolved uncertainty regarding Orphie's disappearance.
    
- Increasing fixation on Orphie's return.
    
- Deterioration of normal routines.
    
- Increasingly blurred distinction between absence and presence.
    

---

# **THE MOST CONCERNING DEVELOPMENT**

Corin has stopped asking:

> **"Where is Orphie?"**

She now behaves as though the question has already been answered.

> **"She's coming back."**

There is no evidence supporting this conclusion.

But Corin no longer seems able to tolerate any other possibility.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie wanted everyone to leave her alone.

Corin respected that.

She did not follow.

She did not search.

She did not force Orphie to return.

She simply waited.

And waited.

And waited.

The waiting became loneliness.

The loneliness became obsession.

The obsession became something Corin could no longer control.

Now she talks to an empty chair.

She cooks for someone who is not there.

She keeps the door open for someone who may never return.

She sleeps poorly because she is afraid that Orphie might come home while she is asleep.

Corin once remained beside Orphie because she refused to abandon her.

Now she remains in the treehouse because she cannot accept that there may no longer be anyone to remain beside.

The person who was once the last connection keeping Orphie grounded is now beginning to lose her own grip on reality.

**End of Personnel Welfare / Psychological Incident Log**
