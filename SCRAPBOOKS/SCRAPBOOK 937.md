# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL NOTICE

**SUBJECT:** Neri's Return to Cafeteria Routine  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE CAFETERIA  
**STATUS:** NORMAL OPERATIONS RESTORED

---

## 1. OBSERVATION

Neri has returned to one of her oldest SAFEHOUSE routines.

She can now regularly be found sitting at her usual cafeteria table, quietly:

1. Eating cake.
    
2. Reading a chemistry book.
    
3. Occasionally turning a page.
    
4. Eating more cake.
    
5. Continuing to read.
    

Her azure Light wings are generally hidden.

No anomalous activity is usually associated with the behavior.

She simply sits there.

For long periods.

---

## 2. THE THREE-WEEK PROBLEM

Following Neri's disappearance, cafeteria staff had discontinued the additional dessert portions that had previously been prepared specifically because of her unusually consistent consumption.

When Neri returned, however, the cafeteria did **not** immediately restore the extra portions.

Nobody noticed.

For approximately three weeks.

Neri simply continued eating the standard portions.

Then, during a routine inventory review, one of the cafeteria workers apparently noticed the discrepancy.

> **Worker:** "Wait."

> **Worker:** "Where are the extra portions?"

Another worker looked confused.

> **Worker:** "What extra portions?"

A third worker checked the old production records.

> **Worker:** "...Neri."

Silence.

> **Worker:** "Oh."

---

## 3. THE REALIZATION

The cafeteria staff subsequently reviewed their previous production schedule.

Before Neri disappeared, additional portions had routinely been prepared because:

> **Neri ate them.**

Not occasionally.

Not as a special event.

Regularly.

The staff had simply forgotten how much dessert had historically been allocated to the little Seal Thiren who spent enormous amounts of time sitting in the cafeteria.

One worker reportedly summarized the situation as:

> **"We spent three weeks wondering why we had extra cake."**

Another responded:

> **"Because Neri wasn't eating it."**

> **"...She's back."**

> **"Yes."**

> **"So why aren't we making it?"**

> **"...I don't know."**

---

# 4. RESTORED PRODUCTION

Additional portions have now been restored.

No special announcement was made to Neri.

There was no ceremony.

Nobody informed her that the cafeteria had changed its production schedule.

The following afternoon, an extra slice of cake simply appeared on her tray.

Neri looked at it.

Looked at the worker.

Looked back at the cake.

> **Neri:** "...Extra?"

> **Worker:** "Yeah."

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:** "Okay."

She proceeded to eat it while continuing to read.

---

# 5. THE CHEMISTRY BOOK

Neri's current cafeteria reading material is generally chemistry-related.

This has produced an unusual combination of activities:

**Cake consumption:**  
Normal.

**Chemistry textbook:**  
Normal.

**Ten-year-old partially Architect entity:**  
Also apparently normal.

Her wings remain hidden beneath her Light-manipulation capability.

Her reality-displacement abilities remain unused.

Her molecular restructuring abilities remain unused.

Her Architect-derived Light remains inactive.

Meanwhile, Neri is intensely focused on a chapter concerning chemistry.

The cafeteria has elected not to question this.

---

# 6. STAFF COMMENT

One worker was asked whether they found Neri's return strange after everything that had happened.

Their response:

> **"Honestly? Not really."**

> **"She came in, sat at her table, asked for cake, and started reading."**

> **"That's Neri."**

---

## 7. CURRENT CAFETERIA STATUS

**Neri:** Present.

**Cake:** Being consumed.

**Chemistry book:** Being read.

**Wings:** Hidden.

**Subspace:** Not currently being created.

**Reality:** Intact.

**Cafeteria:** Has finally remembered to make enough cake.

**Staff:** Three weeks late.

---

### FINAL NOTE

The cafeteria has officially reinstated the additional dessert allocation associated with Neri.

The reason is no longer considered a special accommodation.

It is simply part of the cafeteria's understanding of normal SAFEHOUSE operations.

After all, if Neri is sitting at her usual table, quietly reading chemistry and eating cake, then apparently everything is finally back to normal.
