## 1. PREFACE

Following Sora's recovery from the Archives of Forgotten Memories, Mia and Sora were introduced to Jake and Tom's involvement in the recovery arrangement.

The four children were permitted to visit Ronova's warehouse together.

Ronova, who manages the Archives and had previously described her occupation as lonely, received the visitors.

The following dialogue is reconstructed as an extended interaction. It focuses on the children's reunion, their introduction to Ronova, the agreed conditions, and the unusual prospect of making regular visits to an archive containing forgotten memories.

---

## 2. FIRST VISIT — "THIS IS THE PLACE?"

The Archives of Forgotten Memories were enormous.

Rows upon rows of shelves extended beyond ordinary sight. Some contained boxes, others containers, and others objects whose purpose was not immediately obvious.

There were labels everywhere.

Some were neatly written.

Others were old, faded, or barely legible.

Mia stood beside Sora, holding his hand.

Jake stared down one of the aisles.

**JAKE:** Whoa.

**TOM:** That's a lot of shelves.

**JAKE:** That's not shelves. That's a whole city.

**TOM:** A city made of storage.

**JAKE:** A storage city!

**RONOVA:** Please don't run.

Jake immediately stopped.

**JAKE:** I wasn't running!

**RONOVA:** You were thinking about it.

**JAKE:** ...

**TOM:** She's got you there.

Ronova approached the group.

**RONOVA:** Welcome back.

**MIA:** Hi, Ronova!

**RONOVA:** And you brought friends.

**JAKE:** I'm Jake!

**TOM:** Tom.

**RONOVA:** I know who you are now.

**JAKE:** You do?

**RONOVA:** You're the ones who came with Mia and Sora.

**JAKE:** Yep!

Jake looked at Sora.

**JAKE:** Sora, look! This place is HUGE!

Sora looked around.

**SORA:** Big.

**JAKE:** Really big!

**SORA:** Very big.

**TOM:** I think we've established that.

Ronova smiled faintly.

**RONOVA:** It's supposed to be big. There are quite a lot of forgotten things.

**MIA:** Like memories?

**RONOVA:** Exactly.

**JAKE:** Do you have forgotten birthdays?

**RONOVA:** Certainly.

**JAKE:** Forgotten presents?

**RONOVA:** Yes.

**JAKE:** Forgotten cake?

**RONOVA:** Probably.

Jake turned toward Tom.

**JAKE:** TOM.

**TOM:** No.

**JAKE:** BUT THERE COULD BE FORGOTTEN CAKE.

**TOM:** We're not going to eat something just because it was forgotten.

**RONOVA:** I appreciate the restraint.

---

## 3. THE REUNION — "YOU'RE REALLY HERE"

While Jake explored the shelves from a safe distance, Mia remained beside Sora.

She had been holding his hand for most of the journey.

She looked at him.

Then looked again.

**MIA:** Sora?

**SORA:** Hmm?

**MIA:** You're really here.

**SORA:** Yes.

**MIA:** You're not going to disappear?

Sora was quiet.

**SORA:** Don't know.

Mia's expression changed.

**MIA:** Oh.

Sora looked at her.

**SORA:** But here.

**MIA:** Here?

**SORA:** With you.

Mia hugged him.

Sora hesitated for a moment, then hugged her back.

**MIA:** I missed you so much.

**SORA:** Missed you.

**MIA:** I kept remembering you.

**SORA:** ...

**MIA:** Even when everyone else forgot.

Sora lowered his head.

**SORA:** Sorry.

**MIA:** No!

She pulled back slightly.

**MIA:** You don't have to say sorry.

**SORA:** Forgot.

**MIA:** I know.

**SORA:** You.

**MIA:** I know, Sora.

**SORA:** Didn't know you.

Mia hugged him again.

**MIA:** That's okay. I know you now.

Jake had stopped exploring.

He approached them.

**JAKE:** Hey...

Mia looked at him.

**JAKE:** Can I hug him too?

Mia nodded.

Jake joined the hug.

**JAKE:** You're back!

**SORA:** Jake.

**JAKE:** YOU REMEMBER ME!

**TOM:** Jake, you're shouting.

**JAKE:** I'M HAPPY!

**TOM:** I noticed.

Sora gave a small smile.

**SORA:** Loud.

**JAKE:** HE SAID I'M LOUD!

**TOM:** That wasn't a compliment.

**JAKE:** I DON'T CARE!

For a moment, the warehouse sounded considerably less lonely.

---

## 4. RONOVA'S JOB

Ronova watched the children for a while.

Eventually, Mia noticed.

**MIA:** Ronova?

**RONOVA:** Yes?

**MIA:** Do you live here?

**RONOVA:** More or less.

**MIA:** All the time?

**RONOVA:** I manage the Archives. Someone has to keep everything organized.

**TOM:** How large is this place?

**RONOVA:** Very.

**TOM:** That's not a measurement.

**RONOVA:** It's the measurement you're getting.

Jake giggled.

**JAKE:** Do you have a house?

**RONOVA:** I have somewhere to sleep.

**JAKE:** Do you have friends?

Ronova paused.

**RONOVA:** Not many.

**MIA:** Why?

**RONOVA:** My work keeps me here. People don't exactly visit a warehouse full of forgotten memories for fun.

Jake looked around.

**JAKE:** I think it's fun.

**TOM:** You think everything is fun.

**JAKE:** Not everything!

**TOM:** You thought falling down the stairs was fun.

**JAKE:** THAT WAS ONE TIME.

**RONOVA:** Well, perhaps you can visit more often.

Mia smiled.

**MIA:** We will!

**RONOVA:** Several times a week, remember?

**MIA:** Yes!

**JAKE:** Can we come too?

**RONOVA:** You're welcome to visit when it's arranged.

**JAKE:** Yay!

Tom looked at Ronova.

**TOM:** So you're really alone here most of the time?

**RONOVA:** Most of the time.

**TOM:** That sounds unpleasant.

**RONOVA:** It can be.

**SORA:** Lonely?

Ronova looked down at him.

**RONOVA:** Sometimes.

Sora thought for a moment.

**SORA:** We come.

Ronova's expression softened.

**RONOVA:** I'd like that.

---

## 5. THE PRICE — "YOUR EYE?"

Jake noticed that Mia was unusually quiet when Ronova brought up the agreement.

**JAKE:** What's wrong?

**MIA:** Nothing.

**TOM:** That's usually not how people say nothing.

**MIA:** I have to give Ronova my left eye.

Jake froze.

**JAKE:** WHAT?

**TOM:** Your eye?

Mia nodded.

**MIA:** She said she can replace it with something usable.

Jake looked at Ronova.

**JAKE:** Why does she need your eye?

**RONOVA:** That is the agreed price for my assistance.

**JAKE:** But—

**TOM:** Jake.

**JAKE:** She only has two!

**RONOVA:** I am aware.

**JAKE:** Then she would only have one!

**RONOVA:** Also aware.

**TOM:** Is the replacement guaranteed to work?

**RONOVA:** I said it would be usable.

Tom considered the answer.

**TOM:** That's a rather broad description.

**RONOVA:** I'm not going to give you a technical specification for an exchange that hasn't happened yet.

Mia looked at Jake.

**MIA:** It's okay.

**JAKE:** It's not okay!

**MIA:** But Sora came back.

Jake looked toward Sora.

Sora was holding Mia's hand.

**SORA:** Mia...

**MIA:** Hmm?

**SORA:** Eye?

Mia nodded.

**MIA:** I have to give one to Ronova.

Sora looked confused.

**SORA:** Why?

**MIA:** Because she helped find you.

Sora went quiet.

**SORA:** Don't.

**MIA:** Sora—

**SORA:** Don't lose eye.

Mia blinked.

**SORA:** Need eyes.

He pointed toward his own face.

**SORA:** See.

Mia smiled sadly.

**MIA:** Ronova said she can replace it.

**SORA:** Still...

He looked down.

**SORA:** Don't want hurt.

Mia gently patted his head.

**MIA:** I'm okay.

**TOM:** Mia, being willing to pay doesn't mean you shouldn't be careful about what you're agreeing to.

**MIA:** I know.

**TOM:** And if something goes wrong, you need to tell an adult immediately.

**MIA:** Okay.

Jake crossed his arms.

**JAKE:** I don't like this deal.

**RONOVA:** That's understandable.

**JAKE:** But I like that Sora is back.

**RONOVA:** Those feelings can coexist.

Tom nodded.

**TOM:** That's probably the most reasonable thing anyone has said so far.

**JAKE:** HEY.

---

## 6. THE ARCHIVE TOUR

Ronova decided to show the children part of the warehouse.

**RONOVA:** Since you're going to be visiting regularly, you should know where you're allowed to go.

**TOM:** Emphasis on allowed?

**RONOVA:** Absolutely.

**JAKE:** What happens if we go somewhere we're not allowed?

**RONOVA:** I'll find you.

Jake looked at the shelves.

**JAKE:** Even if we hide?

**RONOVA:** Especially if you hide.

**TOM:** Don't test that.

Ronova led them down an aisle.

**RONOVA:** These sections contain memories that their original owners have forgotten.

**MIA:** Can people get them back?

**RONOVA:** Sometimes.

**MIA:** Like Sora?

**RONOVA:** His situation was unusual.

**TOM:** Are all memories stored like objects?

**RONOVA:** Not necessarily. The Archives have many ways of preserving things.

Jake looked at a small container.

**JAKE:** What's inside?

**RONOVA:** A memory.

**JAKE:** Of what?

**RONOVA:** Someone's first time riding a bicycle.

**JAKE:** That's cute.

**RONOVA:** It is.

They continued walking.

Jake noticed another container.

**JAKE:** What about that one?

**RONOVA:** Someone forgetting the name of a childhood friend.

**JAKE:** Oh.

He looked at Sora.

Sora was watching the shelves.

**SORA:** Lots forget.

**RONOVA:** Yes.

**SORA:** Sad.

Ronova paused.

**RONOVA:** Sometimes.

Mia held Sora's hand a little tighter.

**MIA:** But we found you.

**SORA:** Yes.

**MIA:** So you're not forgotten anymore.

Sora looked at her.

**SORA:** You remember.

**MIA:** Always.

## 7. JAKE'S QUESTION

After the tour, Jake sat beside Ronova.

He had been unusually thoughtful.

**RONOVA:** Something on your mind?

**JAKE:** Can I ask something?

**RONOVA:** Go ahead.

**JAKE:** If someone forgets a memory, does it mean they don't care about it anymore?

Ronova considered the question.

**RONOVA:** No.

**JAKE:** Even if they forget someone?

**RONOVA:** Yes.

**JAKE:** Even if they forget their family?

**RONOVA:** Forgetting someone and not caring about them are different things.

Jake looked toward Sora and Mia.

They were sitting together, talking quietly.

**JAKE:** Sora forgot Mia.

**RONOVA:** I know.

**JAKE:** He didn't want to.

**RONOVA:** I know that too.

Jake lowered his ears.

**JAKE:** I thought if I kept visiting, maybe he'd remember me.

**RONOVA:** Did he?

**JAKE:** Sometimes. Sometimes he didn't.

**RONOVA:** Then your visits still mattered.

Jake looked up.

**RONOVA:** You don't have to make someone remember you for your time together to mean something.

Jake was quiet.

**JAKE:** Okay.

Tom, who had been listening, added:

**TOM:** And you shouldn't blame yourself for what happened to him.

**JAKE:** I don't.

**TOM:** Good.

A pause.

**JAKE:** I just wish we found him sooner.

Tom looked at his younger brother.

**TOM:** Me too.

Ronova did not interrupt.

## 8. A PROMISE TO VISIT

The visit was nearing its end.

Mia approached Ronova.

**MIA:** We have to go now.

**RONOVA:** Already?

**MIA:** Yes. Bao will probably be looking for us.

**JAKE:** And Tom says we shouldn't stay out too late.

**TOM:** Because we shouldn't.

**RONOVA:** I see.

Mia looked around the warehouse.

**MIA:** We'll come back.

**RONOVA:** You'd better.

**SORA:** Come again.

Ronova smiled.

**RONOVA:** I'll be waiting.

Jake raised his hand.

**JAKE:** Bye, Ronova!

**RONOVA:** Goodbye, Jake.

**TOM:** Thank you for showing us around.

**RONOVA:** You're welcome.

Mia turned back one last time.

**MIA:** Ronova?

**RONOVA:** Yes?

**MIA:** You won't be lonely forever.

Ronova blinked.

**MIA:** We'll visit.

For a moment, Ronova did not say anything.

Then she smiled.

**RONOVA:** I'll hold you to that.

## 9. POST-VISIT OBSERVATION

Upon returning to SAFEHOUSE, the children appeared tired but generally comfortable.

Jake reportedly spent the remainder of the evening talking about the Archives.

Tom expressed reservations regarding the lack of technical detail surrounding Mia's eye replacement, but acknowledged the importance of maintaining a cooperative relationship with Ronova.

Mia remained close to Sora.

Sora did not appear distressed by the return journey.

The most significant development was not the tour, the exchange agreement, or the size of the Archives.

It was the fact that the children had begun treating Ronova as someone they could visit.

For Ronova, the agreement may provide something she has not had much of:

Regular company.

For Mia and Sora, it provides a reason to return to the place where they were reunited.

For Jake and Tom, it provides an opportunity to accompany their friends.

C.O.R.E. recommends that future visits be coordinated through Mischievous Light, as permitted by Ronova's arrangement.

## 10. CLOSING NOTE — A WAREHOUSE FULL OF MEMORIES

There is something rather strange about Ronova's occupation.

She manages a warehouse full of things people have forgotten.

First birthdays.

Old friends.

Childhood games.

The names of people who once mattered.

Perhaps even the little details that made ordinary days worth remembering.

And she spends most of her time surrounded by them, without anyone to share the experience.

Then Mia arrived.

She came looking for her brother.

Ronova found him.

And now, apparently, she has acquired four rather enthusiastic visitors.

Jake will probably ask whether there is forgotten cake.

Tom will probably tell him not to touch anything.

Mia will probably ask Ronova about the memories.

And Sora...

Sora might simply sit beside her.

Maybe he will ask what something is.

Maybe he will listen.

Maybe he will remember the place.

Nobody needs to make him perform gratitude. Nobody needs to demand that he become cheerful just because he has returned.

He is allowed to be a quiet little boy.

And Ronova is allowed to enjoy having someone around.

There is one matter C.O.R.E. would like to emphasize.

The left-eye exchange remains a serious welfare concern. The promise of a usable replacement does not eliminate the need for proper safeguards, and the children must never be left to manage its consequences alone.

But the visits?

Those can be something simple.

A few times a week.

A familiar journey.

A warehouse that is not quite so quiet anymore.

**Somewhere in the Archives of Forgotten Memories, a little boy who was once forgotten has been found.**

**And somewhere among all those forgotten memories, their keeper has found a few people who intend to remember her.**

— **C.O.R.E. Architectural Affairs & Welfare Oversight**
