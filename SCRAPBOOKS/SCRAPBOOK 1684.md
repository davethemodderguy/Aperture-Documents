# 1. EQUIPMENT SUMMARY

Clover Luminare's primary weapon consists of two components:

1. **A modernized lever-action rifle**
    
2. **An anomalous coin**
    

The rifle is a contemporary adaptation of the traditional lever-action platform.

It retains the recognizable lever-action operating principle while incorporating modern materials, optics compatibility, improved ergonomics, and contemporary Blacksite manufacturing standards.

The rifle itself is not considered anomalous.

The coin is.

---

# 2. THE COIN

Clover carries a single coin specifically associated with the rifle.

Before engaging a target, she flips the coin.

The result determines the anomalous effect applied to the ammunition.

### HEADS

The fired projectile becomes **poison-tipped**.

The effect is anomalous rather than conventional ammunition modification.

The resulting projectile is capable of delivering a toxic anomalous effect upon impact.

### TAILS

The fired projectile becomes capable of **exploding on impact**.

The resulting detonation is localized to the point of impact.

The effect does not require Clover to manually modify the ammunition.

---

# 3. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

The coin does not determine whether Clover hits her target.

It determines **what happens if the shot connects.**

Her passive luck anomaly and the coin's anomalous function appear to operate independently.

C.O.R.E. has found no evidence that the coin itself manipulates probability.

It simply establishes the ammunition's effect.

---

# 4. COIN BEHAVIOR

The coin must actually be flipped.

Clover cannot simply declare the desired result.

Attempts to manipulate the result through deliberate catching, repeated flipping, or similar behavior have produced inconsistent outcomes.

Clover therefore generally accepts whatever side appears.

When asked why she does not simply choose the effect she wants:

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Because then it wouldn't be a coin toss."

When asked whether she considers this unnecessarily risky:

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Probably."

Pause.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "But it's fun."

---

# 5. WEAPON PERSONALITY

Despite the rifle's modernized construction, Clover does not treat it as a purely tactical instrument.

She maintains it carefully.

The rifle is kept clean, secured when not in use, and stored separately from the coin.

Clover has also been observed absent-mindedly flipping the coin while thinking.

This has resulted in repeated personnel requests that she **not perform coin tosses over the armory floor.**

Clover agreed.

She subsequently began doing it over her desk.

C.O.R.E. considers this an improvement.

---

# 6. CLOVER'S COMBAT STYLE

Clover's approach is notably different from conventional tactical personnel.

She is patient.

She rarely rushes.

She allows circumstances to develop before acting.

Her anomalous luck appears to complement this temperament.

The coin introduces an additional element of uncertainty.

Consequently, Clover's combat profile can be summarized as:

> **Preparation + Precision + Chance**

She does not necessarily know what effect the next shot will produce.

She simply knows that something will happen.

# 8. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The combination of Clover's passive luck anomaly and the anomalous coin presents an unusual psychological contradiction.

Clover has spent much of her life believing that her luck is responsible for things she cannot control.

The coin, however, introduces a mechanism where uncertainty is deliberately accepted.

She does not know whether the next shot will produce one effect or another.

She simply accepts the result.

This may represent a subtle change in Clover's relationship with her own anomaly.

Rather than attempting to control every possible outcome:

> **She lets chance decide.**
