# 1. CORRECTION

Further observation has clarified an important characteristic of Invictus Aeterna's armored manifestation.

Aeterna **has a face.**

Previous documentation describing her armor as a completely featureless armored body was incomplete.

Aeterna is a ghost inhabiting an armored body.

Her face is therefore visible within the armor.

She is not an empty suit.

She is not a machine.

There is a distinct feminine face manifested within the armored form.

---

# 2. FACIAL APPEARANCE

Aeterna's facial manifestation is consistent with her status as a spirit.

The face appears pale and spectral.

It does not behave like ordinary biological flesh.

There is no conventional evidence of:

- skin metabolism;
    
- respiration;
    
- blood circulation;
    
- or ordinary biological aging.
    

Nevertheless, facial expressions are possible.

Aeterna can:

- look toward someone;
    
- raise an eyebrow;
    
- narrow her eyes;
    
- smile;
    
- frown;
    
- and otherwise communicate emotion through facial expression.
    

This is particularly notable because her armored body otherwise maintains an extremely controlled appearance.

---

# 3. THE EYES

The most unusual feature is Aeterna's eyes.

Within her eyes is a persistent anomalous phenomenon resembling:

> **Shooting stars.**

Small luminous streaks appear to move continuously across the visible surface of her eyes.

They resemble distant stars crossing a night sky.

The phenomenon is most apparent when Aeterna is looking directly at someone.

---

# 4. VISUAL CHARACTERISTICS

The shooting-star effect demonstrates several consistent properties.

The streaks:

- move independently;
    
- do not follow normal eye movement;
    
- occasionally cross in opposite directions;
    
- vary in brightness;
    
- appear to originate from within the eyes;
    
- do not appear to illuminate surrounding objects significantly.
    

No physical particles have been detected.

No thermal emission has been measured.

No retinal structure has been identified.

The phenomenon appears to be entirely anomalous.

---

# 5. EMOTIONAL RESPONSE

C.O.R.E. has observed changes in the ocular phenomenon corresponding with Aeterna's emotional state.

### CALM

The shooting stars move slowly.

The effect is subtle.

### AMUSED

The streaks become slightly more frequent.

### ALERT

The movement becomes noticeably faster.

### ANGRY

The luminosity increases significantly.

The eyes can appear almost celestial.

### PROTECTIVE RESPONSE

The phenomenon becomes particularly intense.

Multiple streaks may appear simultaneously.

This has been observed when Nora is threatened.

---

# 6. FIRST DOCUMENTED OBSERVATION

The phenomenon was first formally noted when Aeterna was speaking with Jane Doe.

Jane stared at Aeterna's face for several seconds.

Aeterna noticed.

**AETERNA:**  
“What?”

Jane continued staring.

> **JANE:**  
> “Your eyes.”

Aeterna remained silent.

> **JANE:**  
> “They have stars in them.”

Aeterna looked away.

Jane followed her gaze.

> **JANE:**  
> “No, seriously.”

Aeterna looked back.

A shooting-star streak crossed one eye.

Jane smiled.

> **JANE:**  
> “That is extremely cool.”

Aeterna's expression remained neutral.

The shooting-star effect became slightly faster.

Jane immediately laughed.

> **JANE:**  
> “You like being complimented.”

Aeterna said nothing.

---

# 7. NORA'S REACTION

Nora appears completely accustomed to Aeterna's eyes.

When asked about them:

> **NORA:**  
> “They're pretty.”

The interviewer asked:

> “You've noticed the stars?”

Nora looked confused.

> **NORA:**  
> “Of course.”

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> “She's always had them.”

Nora later described them as:

> **“Tiny shooting stars.”**

The description has been adopted by C.O.R.E. documentation.

---

# 8. JANE'S OBSERVATION

Jane appears particularly interested in Aeterna's facial expressions.

**JANE:**  
“She looks terrifying when she's completely still.”

Pause.

**JANE:**  
“Then you notice the face.”

Another pause.

**JANE:**  
“And then the eyes have little shooting stars.”

The interviewer asked whether this made Aeterna less intimidating.

Jane considered the question.

> **JANE:**  
> “No.”

---

# 9. AETERNA'S RESPONSE

Aeterna was asked whether she could control the shooting-star effect.

**C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
“Can you stop them?”

Aeterna paused.

**AETERNA:**  
“Yes.”

**C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
“Do you?”

**AETERNA:**  
“No.”

**C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
“Why?”

Aeterna looked toward the interviewer.

A bright streak crossed her eye.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “I like them.”

The interviewer did not ask further questions.

---

# 10. FACIAL EXPRESSION AND SPEECH

The presence of a visible face also clarifies an important aspect of Aeterna's communication.

Her silence does not mean she is incapable of expression.

Aeterna can communicate extensively without speaking.

Her face can convey:

- amusement;
    
- concern;
    
- irritation;
    
- affection;
    
- curiosity;
    
- displeasure;
    
- approval;
    
- and warning.
    

This is particularly useful when interacting with Nora.

Nora frequently understands what Aeterna means without verbal communication.

---

# 11. VISUAL DESCRIPTION

Current approved personnel description:

> **A tall, white-armored female spirit with a visible spectral face, glowing blue runes, and eyes containing constantly moving shooting stars.**

When manifested in full guardian form, Aeterna can appear less like a conventional armored warrior and more like:

> **A ghost wearing the memory of a suit of armor.**

The latter description is informal but has been repeatedly used by personnel.

---

# 12. FINAL OBSERVATION

Aeterna was observed standing beside Nora in the cafeteria.

Nora was eating cake.

Aeterna was watching the room.

Jane approached.

> **JANE:**  
> “Nice eyes.”

Aeterna looked at her.

A shooting star crossed her left eye.

Then another crossed her right.

Jane smiled.

> **JANE:**  
> “Still pretty.”

Aeterna looked away.

Nora glanced up from her cake.

> **NORA:**  
> “She likes compliments.”

Aeterna looked at Nora.

Nora grinned.

Aeterna's eyes became slightly brighter.

Jane laughed.

Aeterna remained silent.

Invictus Aeterna is not an empty suit of armor.

She has a face.

She has expressions.

And she has stars in her eyes.

Literally.
