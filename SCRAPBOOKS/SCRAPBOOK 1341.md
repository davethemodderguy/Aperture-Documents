# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately 14:26, Jake entered the NOST office expecting to find **Dialyn**, the Customer Support representative assigned by Bao.

Jake had previously been given a description of Dialyn:

- hair divided into two distinct halves;
    
- left side black and white;
    
- right side white and black;
    
- left eye white;
    
- right eye black;
    
- casual dress.
    

The individual Jake encountered did not match this description.

Unfortunately, Jake did not have time to determine this before opening the door.

The resulting incident was entirely accidental.

Jake opened the door.

A woman was standing immediately behind it.

The door struck her.

She fell unconscious.

Jake, believing he had just seriously injured an unknown person, panicked.

He subsequently ran through the Blacksite shouting:

> **“BAO!”**

---

# 1. THE DOOR

Security footage provides a remarkably simple explanation.

Jake approached the NOST office.

He stopped in front of the door.

He reached for the handle.

He opened it.

The door swung inward.

Phoenix Raffedella had been standing extremely close to the other side.

The door struck her.

She immediately lost consciousness and collapsed.

Jake froze.

He looked at her.

Then at the open door.

Then back at her.

His expression changed immediately.

> **Jake:** “...Oh no.”

He crouched beside her.

No response.

Jake's panic escalated.

> **Jake:** “Oh no.”

Pause.

> **Jake:** “OH NO.”

He ran.

---

# 3. RECOVERY

Phoenix was recovered from the corridor.

Fortunately, the Blacksite's pedestrian bridges are equipped with **grass-covered safety platforms beneath them.**

This was a deliberate architectural choice made by Neko.

Traditional safety nets were rejected.

Neko's documented reasoning:

> **“They don't fit the aesthetic.”**

The grass platforms therefore function as concealed fall protection while maintaining the visual appearance of the Blacksite's walkways.

Phoenix had landed on one of these platforms.

She was unconscious.

Bao immediately began assessing her.

Jake stood nearby, crying.

> **Jake:** “I didn't mean to.”

> **Bao:** “I know.”

> **Jake:** “I just opened the door.”

> **Bao:** “I know.”

> **Jake:** “I didn't see her.”

> **Bao:** “I know.”

Jake looked at Phoenix.

> **Jake:** “Is she dead?”

Bao checked again.

> **Bao:** “No.”

Jake remained unconvinced.

# 12. THE CURSE

Belle explained that Phoenix's condition does not function like conventional invulnerability.

She does not regenerate instantly.

> **“And a really stubborn will to live.”**

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The incident resulted from a simple chain of unfortunate circumstances:

1. Dialyn was unavailable.
    
2. Phoenix was instructed to enter the NOST office.
    
3. Phoenix stood directly behind the door.
    
4. Jake opened it.
    
5. The door struck Phoenix.
    
6. Phoenix entered unconsciousness.
    
7. Jake panicked.
    
8. Jake summoned Bao.
    
9. Belle arrived.
    
10. Everyone learned that Phoenix is extremely difficult to permanently kill.
    

No hostile action occurred.

No security breach occurred.

No resident intentionally harmed Phoenix.

The primary failure was:

> **Poor door awareness.**

C.O.R.E. recommends that personnel avoid standing directly behind doors.
