# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Phoenix has been found.

Approximately several hours after her phone lost power, the Krampus Compliance Authority received an unexpected civilian arrival.

A Banlands farmer arrived at the KCA office carrying Phoenix's KCA identification card.

Phoenix herself was in the farmer's vehicle.

She was unconscious.

The farmer reported that he had discovered her inside his barn.

He did not know who she was.

He found her ID card nearby.

After identifying the organization listed on the card, he transported her directly to the KCA office.

The farmer was provided with a substantial reward for his assistance.

Phoenix was subsequently transported back to the SAFEHOUSE.

She remains unconscious.

She is alive.

---

# 1. THE FARMER

The farmer arrived at the KCA office shortly after midday.

He entered carrying a small, damaged identification card.

The card was immediately recognized.

> **KCA**
> **PHOENIX RAFFEDELLA**
> **MEDICAL ASSISTANT**

The farmer explained:

> **FARMER:**
> "Found her in my barn."

KCA personnel immediately asked:

> **"Is she alive?"**

The farmer nodded.

> **FARMER:**
> "Breathing."

A brief pause.

> **FARMER:**
> "Didn't wake up."

KCA personnel immediately followed him outside.

---

# 2. PHOENIX

Phoenix was lying in the back of the farmer's vehicle.

She was unconscious.

Her clothing was heavily damaged from her prolonged travel through the Banlands.

Multiple injuries were visible.

However, none appeared immediately fatal.

Her Ether restoration remained active.

Her breathing was stable.

Her pulse was present.

She did not respond to verbal commands.

She did not respond to her name.

She did not wake when moved.

The KCA medical team immediately took custody of her.

---

# 3. THE BARN

The farmer provided additional information.

Phoenix had apparently entered the barn sometime during the night.

The farmer did not know how she got there.

He discovered her when he went to check his animals.

She was lying on the floor.

Her phone was not with her.

Her KCA identification card was nearby.

The farmer initially assumed she was an injured traveler.

He attempted to wake her.

She did not respond.

He then noticed the KCA identification.

Rather than leave her where she was, he transported her to the organization listed on the card.

---

# 4. FARMER INTERVIEW

**KCA:**
"You drove her here yourself?"

**FARMER:**
"Yes."

**KCA:**
"Why?"

The farmer looked confused by the question.

> **FARMER:**
> "Because she was unconscious."

Pause.

> **FARMER:**
> "What else was I supposed to do?"

No further explanation was required.

---

# 5. REWARD

The farmer was provided with a formal civilian assistance reward.

KCA additionally compensated him for:

* transportation;
* fuel;
* time;
* emergency assistance;
* and disruption to his normal agricultural activities.

The farmer initially attempted to refuse the payment.

KCA insisted.

He eventually accepted.

Before leaving, he asked:

> **FARMER:**
> "She's going to be okay?"

The attending medical officer answered:

> **"She's alive."**

The farmer nodded.

> **FARMER:**
> "Good."

He left.

---

# 6. BELLE'S REACTION

Belle was notified immediately.

She did not ask for details.

Her first question was:

> **BELLE:**
> "Where is she?"

The answer:

> **"KCA medical facility."**

Belle immediately left for the KCA office.

When she arrived, Phoenix was already being prepared for transportation.

Belle stopped when she saw her.

Phoenix was unconscious.

Her face was pale.

Her clothes were torn and dirty.

There were visible signs of repeated physical trauma.

Belle stood silently for several seconds.

Then:

> **BELLE:**
> "...You idiot."

She stepped closer.

> **BELLE:**
> "You really scared everyone."

Phoenix did not respond.

---

# 7. RETURN TO SAFEHOUSE

Phoenix was transported back to the SAFEHOUSE under medical supervision.

The journey was quiet.

Residents had gathered when the vehicle arrived.

Nobody spoke initially.

The doors opened.

Phoenix was carried inside.

Still unconscious.

Jake was the first resident to approach.

He stopped several feet away.

He looked at her.

Then at Bao.

> **JAKE:**
> "She's alive?"

Bao nodded.

> **BAO:**
> "She's alive."

Jake's shoulders lowered.

---

# 8. NERI

Neri approached more slowly.

She looked at Phoenix.

Then at the injuries.

Then at the medical equipment.

She said nothing.

After several seconds:

> **NERI:**
> "...She made it."

Bao nodded.

Neri looked down.

> **NERI:**
> "She walked all that way."

No one answered.

---

# 9. JAKE AND PHOENIX

Jake remained beside Phoenix's bed.

He did not touch her immediately.

He remembered the last time Phoenix had been responsible for bringing someone back.

He remembered her entering his mind.

He remembered her refusing to leave.

Now she was the one lying unconscious.

Eventually, Jake reached out.

He carefully held one of her fingers.

> **JAKE:**
> "You're back."

Phoenix did not move.

Jake remained there.

> **JAKE:**
> "You can sleep."

A pause.

> **JAKE:**
> "I'll stay."

---

# 10. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT

Phoenix's injuries are extensive.

However:

**No immediate life-threatening injury is currently identified.**

Her Ether restoration remains functional.

The majority of injuries are already showing signs of recovery.

Her body appears severely exhausted.

Her prolonged period of walking, repeated trauma, inadequate rest, and unknown nutritional intake have placed significant physiological stress on her.

The primary immediate objective is therefore:

> **Rest.**

No attempt will be made to wake Phoenix unnecessarily.

No questioning will occur until she is sufficiently recovered.

---

However, this is no longer classified as an immediate search emergency.

Phoenix herself has been recovered.

# 13. THE FARMER'S ROLE

C.O.R.E. has formally recorded the farmer as the civilian responsible for Phoenix's recovery.

Without his intervention, Phoenix's location would have remained unknown.

He did not know Phoenix.

He did not know KCA.

He did not know Aperture.

He saw an unconscious person in his barn.

He found an identification card.

He drove her to the organization named on it.

That was enough.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Phoenix walked until she couldn't anymore.

Then, somehow, she made it into a farmer's barn.

She did not call anyone.

She did not send a message.

She did not turn on a backup device.

She simply collapsed somewhere she happened to reach.

A farmer found her.

He found her identification.

And instead of leaving her there, he drove her to the organization written on the card.

He asked for nothing.

He simply wanted her to be somewhere safe.

KCA rewarded him anyway.

Phoenix is now back at the SAFEHOUSE.

She is unconscious.

Her body is damaged.

Her phone is still missing.

Her resignation remains on Belle's desk.

But she is here.

For the first time since she disappeared, nobody needs to watch a location marker to know where Phoenix is.

They only need to look at the bed.

Jake is still sitting beside her.

Bao is checking her condition.

Neri is sitting quietly nearby.

Belle has not left.

Phoenix is not walking anymore.

For now—

**she is finally allowed to stop.**
