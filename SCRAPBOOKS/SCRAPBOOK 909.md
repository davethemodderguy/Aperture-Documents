# SAFEHOUSE CRITICAL MEDICAL INCIDENT REPORT

## NERI — SIX-HOUR UNCONSCIOUS PERIOD

**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL — MEDICAL / WELFARE  
**Subject:** Neri  
**Incident Type:** Spontaneous Anemic Episode  
**Duration of Unconsciousness:** Approximately 6 hours  
**Cause:** Spontaneous blood degradation / critical anemia  
**External Injury:** None identified  
**Current Status:** Alive / Under observation  
**Overall Concern:** **CRITICAL**

---

# 1. SUMMARY

Neri experienced another spontaneous anemic episode while inside SAFEHOUSE.

There was no combat.

No injury.

No strenuous activity.

No chemical exposure.

No apparent trigger.

Her blood simply degraded until her cardiovascular system could no longer maintain adequate circulation.

Neri lost consciousness.

She remained unconscious for approximately **six hours**.

The incident caused significant distress throughout SAFEHOUSE.

The people most visibly affected were:

- **Alice**
    
- **Corin**
    
- **Bao**
    
- **Frost**
    
- **Star**
    

Their concern was quickly followed by essentially everyone else in the facility.

For approximately six hours, SAFEHOUSE was unusually quiet around the medical area.

Nobody wanted to be the person who walked away.

---

# 2. DISCOVERY

Neri was discovered unconscious at approximately [REDACTED].

Initial examination found:

- No significant external injuries.
    
- No evidence of physical assault.
    
- No evidence of laboratory accident.
    
- No indication that Neri had exhausted herself through combat or exercise.
    
- Severe functional anemia.
    
- Critically compromised cardiovascular function.
    

The medical team immediately initiated emergency treatment.

Bao was contacted.

---

# 3. THE WAIT

The most difficult part of the incident was not the initial emergency.

It was the waiting.

Neri did not wake.

One hour passed.

Then another.

Then another.

Medical personnel continued monitoring her.

Her condition remained unstable but recoverable.

The six-hour period became increasingly frightening for everyone who knew what spontaneous anemia could do to her.

Neri had previously been warned to be considered in grave danger if she could not be awakened after a prolonged anemic shock.

Now that warning was no longer theoretical.

---

# 4. ALICE

Alice refused to leave.

She remained close to Neri for most of the incident.

At one point, someone suggested that Alice should go rest.

She refused.

> **Alice:** "I'm staying."

When asked why, Alice answered:

> **Alice:** "Because she stayed for me."

No further argument was attempted.

Alice repeatedly checked whether Neri was awake.

Every time the answer was no, she became visibly more distressed.

The memory of her own disappearance remained painfully relevant.

Alice had already experienced what it felt like to nearly lose Neri.

She was not willing to experience it again.

---

# 5. CORIN

Corin remained with Alice.

She attempted to reassure her while also clearly struggling with her own fear.

At one point:

> **Corin:** "She's still here."

Alice:

> **Alice:** "I know."

> **Corin:** "Then we wait."

Alice nodded.

Neither left.

Corin's concern was compounded by her understanding of Neri's history.

Neri's fear of losing people had nearly consumed her during Alice's disappearance.

Now Corin was watching Neri herself become the person everyone feared losing.

---

# 6. BAO

Bao was visibly frightened.

Neri's blood compatibility with Bao makes Bao uniquely important during severe episodes.

She remained immediately available throughout the incident.

Bao repeatedly checked Neri's condition.

Her usual calm demeanor was noticeably absent.

Frost stayed beside her.

At one point Bao quietly said:

> **Bao:** "Come on, Neri."

There was no response.

Bao remained there.

---

# 7. FROST

Frost was equally distressed.

She remained close to Bao while repeatedly looking toward Neri.

Frost's relationship with Neri is particularly important to both of them.

Neri calls her:

> **"Big sis."**

Frost has come to regard Neri as family.

The possibility of losing her therefore affected Frost personally rather than simply medically.

Frost reportedly spent much of the waiting period saying very little.

She simply stayed.

---

# 8. STAR

Star was also deeply worried.

As one of Neri's friends, Star appeared unable to understand why Neri simply would not wake up.

She remained near the medical area.

At one point she asked:

> **Star:** "She's going to wake up, right?"

No one immediately answered.

Star asked again.

> **Star:** "Right?"

A staff member eventually reassured her that the medical team was still treating Neri and that she was alive.

Star stayed.

---

# 9. EVERYONE ELSE

As word spread, more residents began quietly appearing nearby.

Neko.

Miss Neko.

Niko.

Orphie.

Mart.

Astral.

Nightshade.

Belle.

Endmin.

Taiga.

Arcturus.

Norma.

Lumi.

And others.

Nobody was particularly loud.

Nobody wanted to disturb the medical staff.

The normal activity of SAFEHOUSE gradually continued farther away from the medical area, but the people closest to Neri remained.

The facility felt different.

---

# 10. NEKO

Neko was unusually quiet.

He had spent considerable time working alongside Neri in the laboratory.

He had watched her solve chemistry problems that he could not solve as quickly.

He had watched her make chemical equipment.

He had watched her become excited when something worked.

Now she was unconscious in a medical bed.

Neko reportedly asked the medical team repeatedly whether there was anything else he could do.

The answer remained the same:

> **"We're treating her."**

Neko stayed.

---

# 11. MISS NEKO

Miss Neko was similarly concerned.

Her usual frustration with Neri's weapons, laboratory experiments, and general tendency to find dangerous things interesting was completely absent.

She simply watched.

At one point she quietly said:

> **Miss Neko:** "Wake up, idiot."

There was no response.

She remained beside Neko.

---

# 12. THE SIXTH HOUR

Approximately six hours after Neri lost consciousness, her condition began improving.

The medical team noticed the change first.

Her cardiovascular function stabilized.

Circulation improved.

The immediate danger began to recede.

Then Neri moved.

Barely.

Alice immediately noticed.

> **Alice:** "Neri?"

Neri's eyes opened slightly.

She appeared confused.

The first thing she saw was a room full of people.

She blinked.

> **Neri:** "...Why is everyone here?"

Alice nearly cried.

---

# 13. NERI WAKES

Neri remained weak and disoriented.

Medical staff instructed everyone to remain calm.

Alice moved closer.

Corin stayed behind her.

Bao remained nearby.

Frost watched carefully.

Star smiled through obvious relief.

Neri looked around at everyone.

She slowly realized how many people had been waiting.

> **Neri:** "Did I do something?"

Neko answered:

> **Neko:** "You scared everyone."

Neri looked confused.

> **Neri:** "I didn't mean to."

Neko:

> **Neko:** "I know."

---

# 14. ALICE'S RESPONSE

Alice eventually reached for Neri's hand.

Neri weakly held it.

Alice did not say much.

She simply stayed there.

After everything that had happened between them—the misunderstanding, the disappearance, the search, the snowstorm, and Neri nearly dying while trying to find her—the situation had become painfully symmetrical.

Neri had once been terrified that Alice would disappear.

Now Alice was terrified that Neri would.

Neither wanted to experience that again.

---

# 15. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT

The episode reinforces several established facts regarding Neri's physiology:

### The anemia remains spontaneous.

No external trigger was identified.

### Eating does not prevent it.

Neri's diet was not responsible for the episode.

Cake is not a cure.

### Rest does not guarantee safety.

Neri can become critically anemic while doing absolutely nothing strenuous.

### Emotional distress may complicate recovery.

Neri's psychological state remains relevant, but no single emotional trigger was identified in this episode.

### Bao remains uniquely compatible.

Bao's blood remains the most reliable known method of restoring Neri's functional circulation during critical episodes.

---

# 16. PSYCHOLOGICAL IMPACT

The incident is expected to have a significant psychological impact on Neri.

However, the larger concern is the effect it had on those around her.

Neri has spent much of her life believing:

> **"If someone disappears, I failed them."**

This incident demonstrated the opposite.

Neri did not disappear.

She became unconscious.

And people came.

They waited.

They stayed.

They worried.

Nobody treated her as an expendable asset.

Nobody walked away because she was inconvenient.

Nobody decided she was no longer worth saving.

They simply waited for Neri to wake up.

---

# 17. FOLLOW-UP OBSERVATION

Later, after Neri was sufficiently stable, Alice remained beside her.

Neri eventually asked:

> **Neri:** "Did you stay?"

Alice answered:

> **Alice:** "Of course."

Neri was quiet.

Then:

> **Neri:** "Everyone?"

Alice nodded.

Neri looked around.

She appeared unable to formulate a response.

Eventually she whispered:

> **Neri:** "...Oh."

It was a very small response.

But the significance was considerable.

For a child who spent her earliest years learning that people could simply be discarded, waking up to find an entire community waiting for her may be one of the most important experiences she has ever had.

---

# 18. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Neri survived another severe anemic episode.

But the incident serves as another reminder that her condition cannot be predicted by activity level.

She can be fighting.

She can be working.

She can be eating cake.

Or she can simply be sitting quietly.

Her blood can still begin to fail.

The medical team therefore recommends continued monitoring while preserving Neri's ability to live normally.

She is not to be confined to a medical ward simply because her body is unpredictable.

She is to be cared for as a person.

---

## FINAL NOTE

Neri once believed that when people disappeared, nobody came.

This time, she was unconscious for six hours.

And when she opened her eyes—

**Alice was there.**

**Corin was there.**

**Bao was there.**

**Frost was there.**

**Star was there.**

And behind them was almost everyone else in SAFEHOUSE.

Neri lost consciousness.

She did **not** lose everyone.
