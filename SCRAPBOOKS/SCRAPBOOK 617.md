### **SAFEHOUSE PERSONNEL / APPEARANCE OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — NEW OUTFIT ASSESSMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Current Attire:** Dress / Pink Thigh-High Socks  
**Status:** **Exceptionally Cute**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has been observed wearing a new, more comfortable outfit consisting of a **dress and pink thigh-high socks**.

The outfit was originally selected because Corin did not feel like driving or flying and simply wanted to be comfortable.

However, SAFEHOUSE personnel have collectively reached another conclusion:

> **Corin looks extremely cute.**

The addition of her white wings, cat ears, and twin tails makes the outfit particularly distinctive.

---

# **VISUAL ASSESSMENT**

The combination of:

- White hair/fur.
    
- Cat ears.
    
- Twin tails.
    
- Large wings.
    
- Dress.
    
- Pink thigh-high socks.
    

has resulted in what several personnel have described as:

> **"Unfair levels of cute."**

Corin herself does not appear to understand why everyone keeps staring at her.

---

# **WING COMPATIBILITY**

Despite the outfit being highly suitable for casual activities, C.O.R.E. continues to advise against attempting high-speed flight while wearing a loose dress.

Corin's wings generate substantial airflow.

The resulting aerodynamic interaction remains:

> **Strongly discouraged.**

The outfit is therefore considered suitable for:

**Walking.**

**Cooking.**

**Relaxing.**

**Using the Guiding Light rift.**

**Being adorable.**

It is not considered suitable for:

**Flying at high speed.**

---

# **CORIN'S OPINION**

When asked whether she liked the outfit, Corin responded:

> **"Yeah."**

She adjusted one of her pink thigh-high socks.

Her wings folded comfortably behind her.

Her twin tails swayed slightly.

> **"It's comfy."**

That was apparently sufficient justification.

---

# **C.O.R.E. CONCLUSION**

The new outfit has no tactical advantage.

It provides no additional armor.

It increases no measurable anomalous capability.

It does, however, produce one extremely consistent result:

> **Corin looks cute.**

C.O.R.E. therefore sees no reason to recommend changing it.

**Final assessment:**

> **Comfortable.**

> **Practical for ground travel.**
