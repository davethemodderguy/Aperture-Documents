---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following the recent Woodland Coorp kidnapping attempt involving Nora Tachyon, Sky's behavior has changed.

The familiar was previously affectionate, attentive, and closely bonded to Nora.

It is now:

> **Extremely protective.**

The change is most apparent whenever Nora is outside SAFEHOUSE-controlled areas.

Sky remains affectionate toward Neri and Elsher.

It remains friendly toward other SAFEHOUSE residents.

But its attention toward Nora has become considerably more persistent.

Sky now monitors Nora.

Constantly.

---

# 2. PREVIOUS BEHAVIOR

Before the kidnapping attempt, Sky's normal behavior around Nora included:

- perching on her head;
    
- sitting on her shoulder;
    
- landing on her finger;
    
- flying nearby;
    
- occasionally scouting ahead;
    
- carrying her when she did not want to walk;
    
- remaining nearby while she played;
    
- sleeping near her.
    

Sky would sometimes leave Nora's immediate vicinity.

It was comfortable allowing other residents to supervise her.

This is no longer consistently the case.

---

# 3. CURRENT BEHAVIOR

Sky now appears to maintain a continuous awareness of Nora's location.

When Nora walks:

Sky follows.

When Nora stops:

Sky lands nearby.

When Nora enters a room:

Sky checks the room.

When Nora exits:

Sky follows.

When unfamiliar personnel approach:

Sky watches them.

When Nora is outside:

Sky frequently remains airborne.

When Nora is playing with Alice:

Sky may circle overhead.

When Nora is being carried:

Sky stays close.

When Nora is asleep:

Sky may remain nearby.

The familiar appears to have adopted a simple operational principle:

> **If Nora is present, Sky is present.**

---

# 4. RESPONSE TO UNFAMILIAR PERSONNEL

A notable change has occurred around strangers.

A normal employee approaching Nora may now attract Sky's attention.

Sky does not immediately attack.

Instead, it watches.

Head movement increases.

Body orientation follows the individual.

The familiar may reposition itself between Nora and the approaching person.

If the person continues approaching, Sky may spread its wings.

This behavior has been observed even when the employee has completely innocent intentions.

### Security clarification

Sky has not demonstrated indiscriminate aggression toward SAFEHOUSE personnel.

Its behavior is primarily:

**observe → assess → position → warn**

rather than immediate attack.

This distinction is considered important.

---

# 5. INCIDENT — CAFETERIA

Nora was sitting at a cafeteria table eating cake.

Sky was perched on the back of her chair.

A newly assigned employee approached.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “Hey, Nora. Can I ask you something?”

Sky immediately turned.

The employee stopped.

Sky stared.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “...I'm just asking her a question.”

Sky remained still.

Nora looked upward.

> **Nora:**  
> “Sky.”

Sky continued staring.

> **Nora:**  
> “It's okay.”

Sky relaxed.

The employee slowly approached.

Sky continued watching.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “Your bird doesn't like me.”

Nora took another bite of cake.

> **Nora:**  
> “She likes me.”

The employee considered this.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “That's not reassuring.”

---

# 6. INCIDENT — OUTDOOR RECREATION AREA

Nora was playing outside.

Sky was flying above her.

Nora suddenly ran toward the edge of the designated recreation area.

Sky immediately descended.

The familiar flew directly toward her and repositioned itself between Nora and the boundary.

Nora stopped.

> **Nora:**  
> “Sky?”

Sky remained there.

Nora looked toward the boundary.

Then back at Sky.

> **Nora:**  
> “I'm not going there.”

Sky remained in place for several seconds.

Only after Nora stepped backward did Sky move away.

C.O.R.E. interpretation:

Sky appears to have developed increased sensitivity toward locations associated with Nora's previous abduction.

---

# 7. RESPONSE TO THE WORD "WOODLAND"

The most significant behavioral change occurs when Woodland Coorp is mentioned.

During one conversation, a security employee stated:

> “Woodland personnel were detected near—”

Sky immediately reacted.

The familiar's head snapped toward the speaker.

Its wings partially opened.

Nora looked at Sky.

> **Nora:**  
> “Woodland?”

Sky remained focused on the speaker.

The employee stopped speaking.

Nora gently touched Sky.

> **Nora:**  
> “They're gone.”

Sky remained tense.

> **Nora:**  
> “I'm okay.”

Several seconds passed.

Sky eventually folded its wings.

It returned to Nora's shoulder.

---

# 8. THE KIDNAPPING ATTEMPT

The behavioral change is believed to be directly associated with the incident in which Woodland Coorp successfully abducted Nora from the authorized outdoor recreation area.

During the incident:

- Nora was separated from SAFEHOUSE personnel;
    
- Woodland personnel attempted to restrain and transport her;
    
- approximately 1,250 hostile personnel were incapacitated or killed during the subsequent confrontation;
    
- Nora was eventually recovered;
    
- Sky was not present at the final recovery.
    

Sky's absence during the rescue appears significant.

The familiar was not able to remain with Nora throughout the incident.

Current behavior suggests it does not intend to allow that situation to occur again.

---

# 9. SKY'S CURRENT FLIGHT PATTERN

Sky's flight behavior has changed.

Previously:

**Free flight → return to Nora.**

Currently:

**Nora → perimeter → Nora → surrounding area → Nora.**

The familiar frequently makes short reconnaissance loops around Nora.

It may land briefly before taking off again.

This behavior occurs without Nora explicitly instructing it.

Nora does not appear to be consciously controlling these movements.

Sky appears to be acting independently.
