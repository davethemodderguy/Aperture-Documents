# **THE COUGH**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Condition:** **Persistent Aphonia / Ether Coughing**  
**Voice Status:** **Absent**  
**Ether Expulsion:** **Benign / Non-Depleting**  
**Status:** **Ongoing**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin remains unable to speak.

Her condition has not improved, and no reliable explanation for the loss of her voice has been identified.

She continues to communicate through writing and gestures.

The occasional coughing of fluid Ether also continues.

However, the expelled Ether itself has been determined to have **no measurable effect on Corin's health.**

It does not weaken her.

It does not reduce her Ether reserves.

It does not destabilize her body.

It simply happens.

The coughing, however, is uncomfortable.

---

# **CORIN'S REPORT**

During one episode, Corin coughed several times.

She immediately placed a hand against her neck.

Guiding Light asked whether she was hurt.

Corin reached for her notebook.

She wrote:

> **"My neck hurts when I cough."**

Guiding Light immediately became concerned.

She examined Corin's neck.

The existing scar tissue around her neck remains unchanged.

No new structural damage was found.

Corin's discomfort appears to be associated with the act of coughing itself rather than the Ether being expelled.

---

# **ETHER EXPULSION**

The sequence remains consistent:

**Cough.**

**Fluid Ether expelled.**

**Temporary discomfort around the neck.**

**Ether gradually destabilizes.**

**Ether disintegrates.**

**Corin returns to baseline.**

No measurable physiological decline follows.

Guiding Light has confirmed that Corin's body does not appear to rely on retaining this expelled Ether.

It is not comparable to blood loss.

It is not draining her life force.

It is simply material that her unusual Ether-sustained body occasionally expels.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light carefully monitored Corin after the episode.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Does it hurt anywhere else?"

Corin shook her head.

> **"Only your neck?"**

Corin nodded.

She then wrote:

> **"It's annoying."**

Guiding Light smiled slightly.

> **"I'll take annoying."**

Corin gave her an unimpressed look.

---

# **CORIN'S FRUSTRATION**

Corin's greatest frustration is not the Ether itself.

It is the combination of symptoms.

She cannot speak.

She occasionally coughs.

Her throat hurts afterward.

And she cannot explain what is happening verbally.

Her notebook contained a short entry:

> **Can't talk.**

> **Can't stop coughing.**

> **Coughing hurts.**

Then:

> **At least the Ether isn't hurting me.**

She paused before adding:

> **Still hate it.**

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT**

Curious Light confirmed the expelled Ether has no meaningful effect on Corin's health.

He therefore considers it a secondary symptom rather than an immediate medical threat.

He has also been specifically instructed not to repeatedly ask Corin to reproduce the coughing.

He agreed.

After a pause, he added:

> **"...Probably for the best."**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has become particularly attentive whenever Corin coughs.

She usually waits until Corin has recovered before asking anything.

Corin once wrote:

> **"You don't have to worry every time."**

Orphie responded:

> **"I'm going to anyway."**

Corin stared at her.

Then wrote:

> **"Fine."**

---

# **JAY**

Jay remains confused by the coughing.

Whenever Corin coughs, he stops whatever he is doing and watches her.

Once Corin finished coughing, Jay hopped onto her shoulder.

Corin scratched his head.

She wrote:

> **"I'm okay."**

Jay chirped.

> **"Really."**

Another chirp.

Corin smiled.

# **FINAL NOTE**

The Ether itself is not the problem.

Corin's body can expel it without consequence.

The only significant physical complaint associated with the episodes is the discomfort in her neck when she coughs.

Unfortunately, even something as simple as coughing has become another reminder of the damage her body has endured.
