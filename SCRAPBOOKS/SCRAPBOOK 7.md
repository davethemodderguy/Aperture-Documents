# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **BLACKSITE CULTURE ARCHIVE**

### **UNOFFICIAL DESIGNATION – "THE BLACKSITE'S RABBIT"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Internal Culture Record

---

## **OVERVIEW**

Although Director **Belle Merryweather** officially works alongside the Krampus Compliance Authority and the Central Execution Department, she spends enough time at Heaven's Blacksite that personnel have given her an affectionate unofficial title:

> **"The Blacksite's Rabbit."**

The nickname originated among security staff before gradually spreading throughout Engineering, Research, Medical, and Cafeteria Services.

It is considered a term of endearment.

---

## **WHY THE NAME STUCK**

Personnel cite several reasons:

- Belle is a Rabbit Full Body Thiren.
    
- She is one of the Blacksite's most familiar visitors.
    
- She knows many employees by name.
    
- She is frequently seen walking through the halls carrying her 50 kg sword over one shoulder while greeting everyone she passes.
    
- She has an uncanny ability to make even stressful days feel a little lighter.
    

Many newer employees are surprised to discover that someone capable of carrying such a massive weapon is also known for giving warm hugs and happily accepting carrots from the cafeteria.

---

## **COMMON SIGHTINGS**

Personnel have reported seeing Belle:

- Sitting with researchers during lunch breaks.
    
- Chatting with Engineering while Neko is working on another invention.
    
- Reading documents beside Mart.
    
- Sharing coffee with Endmin.
    
- Talking with Star in the hallways.
    
- Visiting Medical simply to say hello.
    
- Wandering into the cafeteria asking, "...Do we have pancakes today?"
    

---

## **BLACKSITE SAYING**

A phrase has become common among long-term employees:

> **"If you haven't seen Belle today, check the cafeteria first."**

If she isn't there...

Check Project SAFEHOUSE.

## **BELLE'S COMMENT**

When asked what she thought of the nickname, Belle tilted her head.

> "...The Blacksite's Rabbit?"

She smiled warmly.

> "...I like it."

After a moment, she added:

> "...It feels like home."

---

## **STATUS**

"The Blacksite's Rabbit" remains an unofficial nickname with no administrative significance.

Despite this, it is widely recognized throughout Heaven's Blacksite and is understood by personnel to refer to Director Belle Merryweather whenever she is staying at the facility.
