### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / MART BEHAVIOR INCIDENT LOG**

# **CORIN — MARTS NO LONGER APPROACH**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Critical Psychological Concern  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Associated Personnel:** Marts / Orphie / SAFEHOUSE Residents  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **ESCALATING**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The Marts had previously begun approaching Corin after Orphie's disappearance.

They recognized that Corin was alone.

They attempted to provide the same quiet companionship they had once attempted to provide Orphie.

This has now stopped.

Corin's deteriorating mental state has begun frightening the Marts away.

---

# **THE INCIDENT**

Several Marts approached the treehouse.

One carried a small piece of food.

Another was wearing its tiny top hat crookedly.

They entered cautiously.

Corin was sitting at the table.

She was speaking to the empty chair.

> "...She'll be back."

The Marts stopped.

Corin looked toward them.

She smiled.

It was not a normal smile.

> "...You came to see her?"

The Marts looked at each other.

Corin continued.

> "...She's not here."

A pause.

> "...But she will be."

---

# **THE MISTAKE**

One Mart approached the empty chair.

Corin suddenly stood.

> "...Don't."

The Mart froze.

Corin's voice became noticeably unstable.

> "...That's her seat."

The Mart backed away.

Corin followed it with her eyes.

> "...Don't touch her things."

The Marts began retreating.

---

# **ESCALATION**

Corin's expression changed.

She began laughing.

Not loudly.

Not violently.

Just quietly.

> "...Why are you scared?"

The Marts retreated further.

> "...You weren't scared of me before."

One Mart bumped into another.

Corin stepped toward them.

> "...Don't run."

They ran.

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

The entire group fled the treehouse.

Several Marts bounced erratically through the doorway.

One lost its top hat.

Another immediately turned around to retrieve it.

It saw Corin approaching.

It abandoned the hat.

The Marts continued running.

---

# **AFTERMATH**

The colony regrouped at a safe distance.

They were confused.

Orphie had frightened them before.

Now Corin had done the same.

One Mart said:

> "...Treehouse scary."

Another:

> "...Corin scary."

A third:

> "...No go."

The group unanimously avoided returning.

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

Corin watched the Marts disappear into the forest.

She did not appear angry.

Instead, she smiled faintly.

> "...Good."

She returned inside.

She sat beside the empty chair.

> "...Now they'll leave us alone."

---

# **THE PROBLEM**

Corin has unintentionally reproduced the same isolation pattern that destroyed Orphie's social connections.

The difference is that Corin is not deliberately trying to prove that she is unimportant.

She is simply no longer behaving predictably enough for others to feel safe around her.

The result is identical.

People leave.

---

# **MARTS**

The Marts now avoid:

- The treehouse.
    
- Corin.
    
- Orphie's belongings.
    
- The empty chair.
    
- Any situation involving Corin's attempts to communicate with Orphie.
    

They have effectively lost both residents as safe points of contact.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The Marts eventually returned to SAFEHOUSE.

They refused to explain what happened beyond repeated statements:

> "...Scary."

> "...Corin scary."

> "...No treehouse."

Star reportedly became silent after hearing this.

Nightshade simply said:

> "...First Orphie."

> "...Now Corin."

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

The situation has entered a dangerous feedback loop.

**Orphie:**  
Isolated herself and frightened away the Marts.

**Corin:**  
Lost Orphie and deteriorated psychologically.

**Marts:**  
Attempted to support Corin.

**Corin:**  
Frightened the Marts away.

The result:

> **Both residents are now isolated.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The Marts had tried again.

They had already lost Orphie.

They had been afraid of her.

But they eventually decided Corin might still need someone.

So they approached.

They brought food.

They stayed nearby.

They tried to help.

And now they are afraid of Corin too.

The same tiny creatures that once surrounded the treehouse with warmth and noise now refuse to go near it.

The treehouse has become silent again.

Only this time, there is nobody left inside who is mentally stable enough to notice how empty it has become.

**End of Personnel Welfare / Mart Behavior Incident Log**
