**STATUS:** ACTIVE SAFETY ADVISORY  
**SUBJECT:** Neri Z-4C-NERI  
**FORM:** Plush / Chibi Humanoid  
**OBSERVATION:** Vocal response to excessive handling

---

# 1. SUMMARY

Plush Neri is soft.

Plush Neri is durable.

Plush Neri is **not indestructible.**

Personnel have confirmed that Neri produces a small, high-pitched **squeak** when subjected to certain sudden or excessive physical forces.

Observed triggers include:

- being swished or swung around too vigorously;
    
- being hugged excessively tightly;
    
- being dropped;
    
- sudden impacts;
    
- being squeezed beyond comfortable limits.
    

The squeak is not considered a novelty sound.

It is a distress response.

---

# 2. THE SQUEAK

The sound is generally brief.

Typical observation:

> **"Squeak!"**

Neri may immediately become still afterward.

She may also:

- look startled;
    
- cling more tightly to whoever is holding her;
    
- reposition herself;
    
- quietly complain;
    
- or simply stare at the person responsible.
    

Personnel are instructed to treat the sound as an immediate indication that the handling has become excessive.

**Stop.**

Do not attempt to reproduce the sound.

Do not intentionally make Neri squeak.

---

# 3. SWISHING INCIDENT

Alice has occasionally treated Neri like an ordinary plush toy and swung her gently through the air while playing.

Gentle movement has not produced a problem.

Excessive movement has.

During one incident, Alice swung Neri around too quickly.

Neri produced a sharp:

> **"Squeak!"**

Alice immediately stopped.

Neri remained in Alice's hands.

Alice looked concerned.

> **ALICE:** "Did I hurt you?"

Neri shook her head.

Pause.

Then:

> **NERI:** "...Too fast."

Alice apologized.

The activity was discontinued.

---

# 4. EXCESSIVE HUGGING

Neri enjoys being hugged.

This does **not** mean she can tolerate unlimited compression.

During one observation, Alice hugged Plush Neri considerably tighter than usual.

Neri's body compressed visibly.

A small squeak followed.

Alice immediately loosened her arms.

Neri remained against her.

No injury was identified.

However, the incident demonstrates an important distinction:

> **Neri liking hugs does not mean she likes being crushed.**

---

# 5. DROPPING

Neri's plush form provides increased impact resistance.

It does not provide absolute protection.

A short accidental fall onto an ordinary safe surface is generally tolerated.

Repeated or severe impacts are not.

Neri has sufficient internal structure and vulnerable anatomy that a sufficiently violent impact can still cause serious injury.

Therefore:

**DO NOT DROP HER.**

Not intentionally.

Not as a joke.

Not to "see if she's okay."

Not because she is currently in plush form.

---

# 6. WHY THIS MATTERS

There is a persistent misconception among personnel that:

> **"She's a plushie, so she can't get hurt."**

This is incorrect.

Neri's plush form changes her body's physical properties.

It does not remove her organs.

It does not remove her vulnerability to severe trauma.

It does not make her immortal.

She can still die.

The fact that she can survive impacts that would normally be dangerous does not establish an unlimited tolerance threshold.

---

# 7. ALICE-SPECIFIC ADVISORY

Alice is six years old.

She naturally interacts with Plush Neri like a favorite stuffed toy.

This is permitted.

The following are **not** permitted:

- throwing Neri;
    
- swinging her violently;
    
- squeezing her excessively;
    
- dropping her intentionally;
    
- tossing her between people;
    
- using her as a projectile;
    
- testing her durability;
    
- intentionally attempting to make her squeak.
    

Alice has been informed of the distinction.

Her current understanding is reportedly:

> **"Soft does not mean unbreakable."**

C.O.R.E. considers this an acceptable summary.

---

# 8. NERI'S OWN RESPONSE

When asked whether she wanted people to stop treating her like a plushie, Neri shook her head.

When asked whether she wanted people to stop carrying her, she shook her head.

When asked whether she wanted people to be gentler, she nodded.

After a pause, she added:

> "...Please."

This distinction is considered definitive.

Neri likes being a plushie.

She likes being carried.

She likes being hugged.

She likes being treated like someone's favorite stuffed animal.

She does **not** like being handled as though she were indestructible.

---

# 9. HANDLING STANDARD

### ACCEPTABLE

- carrying;
    
- cuddling gently;
    
- placing her on furniture;
    
- letting her ride on someone's back;
    
- tucking her into blankets;
    
- allowing her to sleep in comfortable locations;
    
- ordinary walking while she is attached;
    
- gentle repositioning.
    

### NOT ACCEPTABLE

- violent swinging;
    
- hard squeezing;
    
- throwing;
    
- dropping from significant heights;
    
- crushing;
    
- kicking;
    
- striking;
    
- using her as a toy projectile.
    

### IF NERI SQUEAKS

**STOP IMMEDIATELY.**

Check that she is responsive.

Allow her to reposition herself.

If she indicates pain or appears medically unwell, contact welfare/medical personnel.

Do not continue the activity to determine whether she squeaks again.

---

# 10. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Neri being a plushie is real.

Neri enjoying plushie treatment is real.

Neri being unusually durable is real.

None of these facts change the final one:

**Neri can still die.**

Her plush form is a comfort state, not an invulnerability state.

The appropriate attitude is therefore:

> **Treat her like a beloved plushie.**

Not:

> **Treat her like an indestructible plushie.**

There is a difference.

A fairly important one.
