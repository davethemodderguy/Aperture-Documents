### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / FLIGHT DEVELOPMENT REPORT**

# **CORIN — HOVERING DEVELOPMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Ability:** Flight / Aerial Maneuvering  
**Development:** **Hovering — In Training**  
**Status:** Unstable but Improving

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin can fly normally but currently **cannot maintain a stationary position in midair**.

Her wings require continuous movement to keep her airborne.

If she stops actively flying, she begins to descend.

Corin has recently begun attempting to learn how to hover.

Progress has been slow.

---

# **CURRENT FLIGHT MECHANICS**

Corin's normal flight relies on continuous wing movement.

She can:

- Fly forward.
    
- Turn rapidly.
    
- Climb.
    
- Dive.
    
- Brake in midair.
    
- Change direction.
    
- Carry passengers.
    
- Launch feathers while airborne.
    

However, she cannot yet remain at a fixed altitude without moving horizontally.

---

# **HOVERING ATTEMPTS**

Corin's current technique involves rapidly adjusting the angle and movement of both wings.

In theory, this should allow her to generate enough upward force to counteract gravity.

In practice:

She wobbles.

A lot.

Her first attempts usually look like:

**Up.**

**Down.**

**Left.**

**Right.**

**PANIC.**

**Flap harder.**

**Almost hover.**

**Fall.**

---

# **TRAINING INCIDENT**

During one training session, Corin attempted to remain stationary approximately two meters above the ground.

She successfully remained in one location for approximately **three seconds**.

She became extremely excited.

> **"I'M DOING IT!"**

She immediately lost control.

Corin dropped straight down.

Fortunately, she landed safely.

Neko applauded anyway.

---

# **PROGRESS**

Corin's control has gradually improved.

She can now briefly reduce her horizontal movement while maintaining altitude.

Her longest confirmed stationary aerial interval is currently several seconds.

She has not yet achieved stable sustained hovering.

---

# **WING CONTROL**

The primary difficulty is coordination.

Corin's wings are powerful enough to generate the necessary lift.

The problem is maintaining a precise balance between:

**Lift**

and

**Gravity**

while preventing unwanted horizontal movement.

Her wings are also extremely flexible, which gives her considerable control potential but increases the complexity of learning precise movements.

---

# **WHY CORIN WANTS TO LEARN**

Hovering would provide several practical advantages.

It would allow Corin to:

- Stop in midair.
    
- Inspect locations without landing.
    
- Remain airborne while carrying someone.
    
- Launch feathers from a stationary position.
    
- Make precise aerial movements.
    
- Land more smoothly.
    
- Reach elevated locations without repeatedly flapping.
    

Most importantly:

Corin thinks hovering looks cool.

That is currently considered her primary motivation.

---

# **TRAINING METHOD**

Corin practices at low altitude.

Training generally occurs over:

- Grass.
    
- Soft flooring.
    
- Open areas.
    
- SAFEHOUSE-adjacent spaces.
    
- The Starlands.
    

She is discouraged from practicing over hard surfaces until her control improves.

---

# **STARLANDS ADVANTAGE**

The Starlands have proven particularly useful for training.

The open fields provide enormous amounts of unobstructed space.

Corin can practice without worrying about:

- Buildings.
    
- Trees.
    
- Blacksite infrastructure.
    
- People walking underneath her.
    

She especially enjoys practicing above the sunflower field.

---

# **CURRENT LIMITATION**

Corin should not be considered capable of true hovering yet.

Her current ability is better described as:

> **Controlled low-speed aerial suspension.**

She can temporarily counteract her descent.

She cannot reliably remain stationary indefinitely.

---

# **EXPECTED DEVELOPMENT**

C.O.R.E. believes sustained hovering is achievable with continued practice.

Corin's existing flight control is already highly advanced.

Her difficulty appears to be primarily a matter of technique rather than insufficient physical capability.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin can cross the distance from her treehouse to the Blacksite in approximately twenty minutes.

She can carry people.

She can launch explosive feathers.

She can perform sharp aerial maneuvers.

But ask her to stop moving in midair?

Suddenly, the world's most capable flying Cat Thiren becomes:

> **"Wait—wait—WAIT—"**

_thump._

She's getting there.
