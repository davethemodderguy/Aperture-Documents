## 3. VISUAL CONFIRMATION TRAITS

Jane's tail frequently wraps around her **left leg**.

This is partly habitual and partly practical.

The tip of her tail is extremely sharp and pointed. As a result, prolonged contact with her stocking frequently causes visible damage.

Personnel attempting to visually confirm Jane should therefore be aware that:

> **Damaged stocking on the left leg + tail wrapped around left leg**

is considered a strong identifying characteristic.

The damaged stocking is not considered evidence of combat activity by itself.
