### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / FEATHER MECHANICS REPORT**

# **CORIN — VELOCITY-BASED FEATHER ACTIVATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **Velocity-Dependent Feather Functions**  
**Status:** Confirmed

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has identified the primary factor governing Corin's various feather projectile functions:

> **Velocity.**

The behavior of a detached feather is determined largely by how quickly it is launched or thrown.

The feathers themselves do not appear to require separate mechanisms for each function.

Instead, their Ether response changes according to the velocity reached during deployment.

---

# **VELOCITY RESPONSE**

Corin's feathers can exhibit different behaviors depending on their launch velocity.

At low velocities, a detached feather generally remains dormant.

At higher velocities, its Ether begins responding.

At sufficiently high velocities, the feather can trigger one of its anomalous projectile effects.

The exact thresholds vary between feathers.

---

# **CURRENTLY IDENTIFIED FUNCTIONS**

### **LOW VELOCITY — DORMANT**

A gently removed or slowly thrown feather remains inert.

It behaves like an ordinary feather.

No Ether reaction occurs.

This explains why Corin can safely shed feathers without accidentally detonating them.

---

### **HIGH VELOCITY — BLADE**

Edge feathers traveling at sufficient velocity can function as extremely sharp throwing weapons.

Their natural feather structure provides the cutting surface.

Ether reinforcement appears to prevent the feather from deforming during flight.

---

### **HIGHER VELOCITY — ETHER AIRBURST**

At greater launch speeds, the feather can transition into its explosive state.

Upon impact, it releases an Ether-infused airburst.

This is the same phenomenon Corin has previously used for:

- Combat.
    
- Emergency flight recovery.
    
- Breaking falls.
    
- Destroying targets.
    

---

### **SPECIFIC VELOCITY — FLASH**

At a particular velocity range, some feathers can trigger the newly discovered flash effect.

Upon impact, the feather produces:

- An intense flash.
    
- A powerful sensory disturbance.
    
- Temporary blindness.
    
- Severe disorientation.
    

The average duration of the effect observed during the SAFEHOUSE incident was approximately **40 seconds**.

---

# **WHY THE FLASH OCCURS**

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

The feather appears to convert kinetic energy into an Ether reaction.

However, instead of producing a conventional explosive airburst, the Ether appears to discharge primarily as:

**Light + sensory interference.**

C.O.R.E. has provisionally classified this as a **velocity-induced Ether state change**.

---

# **THE DART INCIDENT — RECONSTRUCTION**

The original incident can now be explained.

Corin jokingly threw a feather at the dartboard.

The feather reached the necessary velocity.

Upon impact, its Ether reacted.

Instead of producing a conventional explosion, it generated the flash effect.

The entire SAFEHOUSE was temporarily blinded.

Corin had unintentionally discovered a new velocity threshold.

---

# **IMPORTANT VARIABLE**

Velocity does not appear to be the **only** variable.

Testing indicates that other factors may influence the exact result, including:

- Feather type.
    
- Feather age.
    
- Launch angle.
    
- Impact surface.
    
- Ether concentration.
    
- Corin's wing movement.
    
- Possibly Corin's intent.
    

However:

> **Velocity is the primary confirmed trigger.**

---

# **CORIN'S WING FLICK**

Corin's normal method of launching feathers is a sharp flick of her wings.

This produces considerably more velocity than simply removing and throwing a feather by hand.

Consequently, Corin can intentionally generate the necessary speed for her projectile functions.

---

# **TRAINING IMPLICATIONS**

Corin is now capable of selecting approximate feather behavior by controlling launch velocity.

She has begun practicing this deliberately.

Her current goal is to become capable of determining:

> **"Knife, airburst, or flash."**

before launching the feather.

Consistency remains imperfect.

---

# **CURRENT MODEL**

The simplified model is:

**Feather**

↓

**Launch Velocity**

↓

**Ether Response**

↓

**Result**

**Low:** Dormant

**Moderate:** Cutting / projectile behavior

**High:** Ether airburst

**Specific high-velocity threshold:** Flash effect

The precise numerical thresholds remain classified pending further testing.

---

# **SAFETY NOTE**

Because the flash effect is velocity-dependent, personnel are reminded that a seemingly harmless feather can become anomalous once sufficiently accelerated.

Corin has therefore been instructed not to casually flick feathers inside crowded rooms.

Neko's official recommendation remains:

> **"Please stop weaponizing the bird parts."**

Corin's response:

> **"They're mine."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's feathers are not simply carrying several unrelated abilities.

They appear to be responding dynamically to **velocity**.

The faster they move, the more aggressively their Ether reacts.

C.O.R.E. therefore considers Corin's wings less like a conventional biological structure and more like a collection of naturally occurring **Ether-responsive projectiles**.

The feather does not decide what it is.

**The velocity does.**
