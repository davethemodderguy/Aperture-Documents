## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Sora's medical condition has deteriorated.

Despite emergency intervention following his discovery inside his den, his wrist injuries have proven extremely severe. The bleeding has not been successfully controlled, and his condition remains critical.

His anomalous physiology has made the situation exceptionally difficult.

Sora cannot die. However, he does not regenerate naturally. His body does not automatically repair damaged tissue, replace lost blood, or close wounds.

He remains capable of experiencing every consequence of his injuries.

During medical intervention, Sora repeatedly expressed a desire to be left alone.

One statement was particularly concerning:

**"Just... leave... me... in... the snow.... please."**

The reference to snow appears connected to his childhood memories of surviving outdoors with Mia. It may also reflect his desire to withdraw from the present situation and return to a place associated with their earlier life.

His statements were accompanied by severe self-deprecating beliefs.

Sora reportedly described himself as worthless and questioned how he could possibly be a good older brother after making Mia angry.

The medical emergency is therefore accompanied by an acute psychological crisis.

C.O.R.E. has classified the situation as requiring immediate, continuous medical and psychological intervention.

---

## 2. MEDICAL UPDATE — BLEEDING THAT DOES NOT STOP

<badge color="danger">Critical condition</badge>

The initial treatment has not produced sufficient control of the hemorrhage.

The injuries are too severe for routine observation, and Sora's lack of natural regeneration means that his body cannot be expected to repair them independently.

Medical personnel have been instructed to continue emergency treatment and escalate care as necessary.

The following considerations remain central:

- Persistent blood loss presents a serious medical danger.
    
- Sora's inability to die does not prevent physiological deterioration.
    
- His injuries require active treatment rather than passive observation.
    
- Pain management remains necessary.
    
- His unconsciousness and changing responsiveness require continuous assessment.
    

The medical team has specifically rejected the suggestion that his condition can simply be allowed to resolve through his anomalous immortality.

It cannot.

**He cannot die, but he can remain severely injured indefinitely.**

That distinction is now being treated as a central medical consideration.

---

## 3. INCIDENT — "LEAVE ME IN THE SNOW"

During treatment, Sora became intermittently responsive.

He appeared distressed by the attention being directed toward him and repeatedly expressed a desire for the medical personnel to leave.

His speech was weak and fragmented.

**SORA:** Just...

He struggled to continue.

**SORA:** Leave...

A member of the medical team reassured him that he was receiving treatment.

Sora turned his head slightly.

**SORA:** Me...

He paused to breathe.

**SORA:** In... the snow...

A longer silence followed.

**SORA:** Please.

The medical personnel remained with him.

**MEDICAL STAFF:** Sora, you're not outside. You're safe. We're helping you.

He did not appear reassured.

**SORA:** I want...

His voice weakened.

**SORA:** The snow...

No further coherent explanation was obtained at that time.

### Assessment

The statement is consistent with an attempt to retreat into a familiar memory.

Sora spent years surviving outdoors with Mia after their home was destroyed. Snow and cold were part of the environment in which they endured some of their most difficult experiences.

It is not possible to establish exactly what he meant by the request.

He may have been expressing exhaustion, a desire for isolation, a wish to return to a familiar place, or an overwhelming inability to tolerate his present circumstances.

Staff must not interpret this as a harmless nostalgic request.

His words occurred during a life-threatening medical emergency and must be taken seriously.

---

## 4. PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION — THE BROTHER WHO THINKS HE FAILED

Further statements from Sora revealed a deeply troubling pattern of self-blame.

He repeatedly returned to the argument with Mia.

His reasoning appeared to follow a destructive sequence:

1. Mia became angry.
    
2. Sora believed he had made her angry.
    
3. He concluded that he had failed as her older brother.
    
4. He interpreted that failure as evidence that he had little or no worth.
    

One fragmented statement was recorded:

**SORA:** I'm...

He stopped.

**MEDICAL STAFF:** You're what, Sora?

**SORA:** Worth...

He struggled to speak.

**SORA:** Nothing.

The staff member gently corrected him.

**MEDICAL STAFF:** That's not true.

Sora turned away.

**SORA:** I made Mia mad.

A pause followed.

**SORA:** How...

His voice became quieter.

**SORA:** How can a big brother do that?

The medical staff member did not attempt to argue with him immediately.

**MEDICAL STAFF:** Being a big brother doesn't mean you never make mistakes.

Sora closed his eyes.

**SORA:** I hurt her.

**MEDICAL STAFF:** You're hurting too. We need to take care of you.

Sora did not respond.

### Assessment

Sora appears to have interpreted the conflict as a fundamental failure of his identity as Mia's older brother.

This is particularly concerning given the importance he places on their sibling relationship.

His belief that he is worthless must not be dismissed as ordinary childhood guilt.

He requires sustained psychological support, particularly concerning self-worth, responsibility, and the belief that he must endure suffering to compensate for upsetting someone he loves.

---

## 5. MIA — THE OTHER CHILD IN THE ROOM

Mia has been informed that Sora's condition is critical.

She is aware that he is receiving emergency medical treatment and that the injuries are severe.

Her reaction has been profoundly distressed.

The welfare team has not permitted her to assume responsibility for his recovery.

She is not expected to provide medical care, persuade him to recover, or personally repair the relationship.

If Mia is permitted to visit, the interaction must be supervised and based on Sora's medical and emotional readiness.

C.O.R.E. specifically prohibits statements such as:

- "You have to get better for Mia."
    
- "Mia needs you."
    
- "You cannot leave your sister."
    
- "You need to forgive her."
    

Such statements could reinforce Sora's belief that his worth depends on fulfilling his role as an older brother.

He needs to understand that he deserves care regardless of his relationship with Mia.

---

## 6. EMERGENCY DIRECTIVES

The following orders supersede previous residential observation procedures.

### Medical intervention

- Continue emergency treatment for persistent hemorrhage.
    
- Escalate specialist intervention as required.
    
- Monitor blood loss, circulation, consciousness, pain, and other complications.
    
- Do not rely on anomalous immortality as a substitute for treatment.
    

### Psychological intervention

- Maintain a calm, reassuring environment.
    
- Do not shame, interrogate, or challenge Sora aggressively.
    
- Do not make him responsible for reassuring Mia.
    
- Do not interpret his request to be left alone as permission to abandon medical care.
    
- Provide appropriate psychological assessment once his medical condition allows.
    

### Family support

- Bao and other trusted adults should receive updates.
    
- Jake and Tom must be supported separately.
    
- Mia must not be left alone with overwhelming guilt.
    
- Any contact between Sora and Mia must be carefully managed.
    

**Sora's refusal of care must be understood as a sign of severe distress, not a reason to withdraw care.**

---

## 7. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT — A MISUNDERSTANDING OF WHAT IT MEANS TO BE A BROTHER

The most concerning part of Sora's statements is not simply that he blames himself.

It is what he believes he has done wrong.

He thinks that making Mia angry means he has failed her.

He thinks that a good older brother should never cause his little sister pain.

And because he believes he has failed, he has begun treating himself as though he no longer deserves care.

That belief is profoundly unfair to a six-year-old child.

Sora and Mia are both children. They are allowed to argue. They are allowed to become frustrated with one another. They are allowed to make mistakes.

Being an older brother does not mean Sora must absorb every hurt, accept every accusation, or sacrifice his own well-being to keep Mia happy.

His worth is not determined by whether Mia is angry with him.

His right to receive care is not conditional on being a perfect brother.

And the responsibility for his present medical condition cannot be placed on his shoulders.

The adults around him must help him understand these things.

Not through lectures.

Through consistent care.

## 8. CLOSING NOTE — THE SNOW IS NOT HOME ANYMORE

There was a time when Sora and Mia played in the snow.

They were children together.

They had their little games, their messages, and their own ways of reassuring each other when one became upset.

Sora remembers those days.

Perhaps that is why he keeps asking to be left in the snow.

But the snow was never kind to them.

It was part of a life in which two children had to survive without the protection they deserved.

Sora has a home now.

He has a little den on Floor 7.

He has Jake, Tom, Bao, Guiding Light, and people who care about him.

He has Mia, too, even if their relationship is currently hurting.

He does not need to return to the cold to prove that he can endure it.

He does not need to bleed to prove that he regrets an argument.

He does not need to disappear because he believes he has failed as a brother.

And he certainly does not need to earn the right to be cared for.

The medical team continues working to control his bleeding. His condition remains critical, and no immediate recovery can be promised.

But C.O.R.E. will not abandon him.

Not because he is immortal.

Not because he is an anomaly.

Not because he is someone's older brother.

Because he is Sora.

A six-year-old boy who is hurting, who believes he is worth nothing, and who needs someone to stay.

**The snow can wait. Sora deserves to come home.**

— **C.O.R.E. Medical & Welfare Oversight**
