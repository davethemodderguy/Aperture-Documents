# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE – RESIDENT INTRODUCTION LOG**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following Corin Lockwood's relocation to Heaven's Blacksite, Lumi spent more time within Project SAFEHOUSE, allowing both mother and daughter to become more familiar with the residents.

While Corin had previously met many of them, months of isolation and fragmented memories made several introductions feel almost new.

Lumi approached each meeting with the same warmth she had shown throughout the facility.

By the end of the week, she had learned one important fact.

SAFEHOUSE was not simply employee housing.

It was a family.

Just a very unusual one.

---

# **CAT THIRENS**

## **NEKO**

**Position:** Head of Engineering

The first introduction lasted approximately forty-three seconds before Neko began explaining three separate inventions, two engineering theories, and one firearm concept involving an excessive number of moving parts.

Corin quietly listened.

Lumi smiled politely.

Neko eventually stopped and asked:

> "...Too much?"

Lumi laughed.

> "A little."

Neko scratched the back of his head.

> "...Sorry."

Despite his endless curiosity and tendency to invent nearly anything when bored, Lumi quickly recognized something beneath the chaos.

A genuinely kind heart.

---

## **MISS NEKO**

Miss Neko introduced herself shortly afterward.

Professional.

Grounded.

Calm.

Where Neko generated ideas faster than anyone could write them down, Miss Neko quietly determined which ones were actually safe.

Lumi noticed the way Miss Neko occasionally glanced toward Neko whenever he became too excited.

Not controlling him.

Simply making sure he did not accidentally dismantle the kitchen to "improve airflow."

Lumi smiled.

> "You're a wonderful older sister."

Miss Neko paused.

Then quietly replied.

> "...Someone has to be."

---

# **SNOW LEOPARD THIREN**

## **TAIGA**

Taiga's introduction was... brief.

She looked at Corin.

Then at Lumi.

Then simply said:

> "...Hi."

After a comfortable silence, she invited them to sit with her.

Conversation came slowly.

Thoughtfully.

Lumi later remarked that Taiga reminded her of someone who preferred listening over speaking.

Corin eventually admitted she liked sitting near Taiga because neither of them felt pressured to fill every silence.

---

# **OWL THIREN**

## **MART**

Mart welcomed both of them with a quiet smile.

Unlike many others, Mart never asked Corin difficult questions.

He simply asked:

> "Tea?"

Corin nodded.

That was enough.

Lumi observed that Mart possessed a remarkable ability to make people feel heard without requiring them to explain themselves.

She later thanked him.

Mart simply replied:

> "Sometimes listening helps more."

---

# **ANGELS OF STARLANDS**

## **STAR**

Star entered the room with enough enthusiasm for three people.

Before Lumi could properly introduce herself—

Star hugged her.

> "HI!"

Lumi laughed in surprise.

Within minutes, Star was enthusiastically explaining Blacksite traditions, SAFEHOUSE movie nights, and why everyone should always eat dessert first.

Lumi later described Star as:

> "Sunshine with wings."

Corin quietly agreed.

---

## **NIGHTSHADE**

Where Star filled the room with energy, Nightshade filled it with calm.

She greeted Lumi with a respectful nod.

She greeted Corin the same way.

Their conversation was gentle.

Measured.

Nightshade never tried to force Corin into conversation.

She simply remained nearby.

Lumi thanked her afterward.

Nightshade answered softly.

> "Sometimes presence is enough."

Lumi smiled.

> "I couldn't agree more."

---

# **GODDESSES**

## **BAO**

Bao welcomed Lumi immediately.

No introductions were necessary.

As fellow mothers, they understood one another almost instantly.

Bao prepared tea.

Lumi accepted.

Within minutes they were discussing children, cooking, and how difficult it was convincing adults to take care of themselves.

Bao laughed.

> "Apparently they're all the same."

Lumi laughed with her.

The Mom Club had officially gained another regular.

---

## **FROST**

Frost greeted Corin with quiet politeness.

Reserved but kind.

She later demonstrated a small ice sculpture she had made.

Corin studied it carefully.

For the first time that day, she became genuinely absorbed in something.

Lumi noticed.

She smiled.

It reminded her of the curious child Corin had once been.

This time...

She kept that thought to herself.

---

# **GHOSTS**

## **NIKO**

Niko introduced himself with a relaxed smile.

Although a ghost, he carried himself with the ease of an older brother accustomed to looking after others.

He jokingly apologized in advance for whatever Neko might invent next.

Lumi laughed.

> "That sounds familiar."

Niko replied:

> "Being an older sibling usually does."

The two shared a knowing smile.

---

## **ASTRAL**

Astral welcomed Lumi warmly into the Mom Club.

The two had spoken before.

Now, with Corin living at SAFEHOUSE, they spent more time together.

Astral quietly mentioned that both Mart and Taiga had also experienced difficult periods in their lives.

Without comparing experiences, she simply said:

> "Healing takes different shapes."

Lumi appreciated the sentiment.

---

# **CORIN**

Throughout the introductions, Corin remained quiet.

She rarely initiated conversation.

She listened.

She observed.

She occasionally smiled.

No one pressured her.

No one demanded she become outgoing again.

The residents accepted her exactly as she was that day.

For Corin...

That made all the difference.

---

# **LUMI'S OBSERVATION**

Later that evening, Lumi sat with Corin in the SAFEHOUSE lounge.

She looked around at the residents laughing together.

The cats arguing over engineering.

Star enthusiastically telling another story.

Bao pouring tea.

Astral watching over the younger residents.

Taiga quietly reading.

Mart listening more than speaking.

Frost carving another small piece of ice.

Lumi smiled.

> "It's a strange family."

Corin looked around.

Then, after a long pause...

She smiled too.

A small one.

> "...Yeah."

> "...It is."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin continues adapting to life at Heaven's Blacksite.

The residents of Project SAFEHOUSE have made no attempt to rush her recovery.

Instead, they have simply welcomed her into their daily lives.

Lumi later summarized the atmosphere in a single sentence.

> "Families don't have to be ordinary."

> "They just have to make room for one more."

**End of Project SAFEHOUSE – Resident Introduction Log**
