# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / TREEHOUSE INCIDENT LOG**

# **THE WRONG WORDS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subjects:** Corin / Orphie  
**Location:** Treehouse Exterior  
**Conditions:** **Heavy Snowfall**  
**Status:** **ESCALATED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin and Orphie spent the evening beneath the Treehouse tree.

Snow continued falling around them.

Corin sat exactly as she had for months.

Wings surrounding her body.

Head lowered.

Body trembling.

Orphie sat beside her.

For a while, they simply listened to the snow.

Eventually, they began talking.

It started peacefully.

It did not remain that way.

---

# **THE CONVERSATION**

Orphie asked:

> **"Do you still remember what you wrote in your notebook?"**

Corin nodded.

> **"Some of it."**

Orphie smiled faintly.

> **"You used to be so hopeful."**

Corin's expression barely changed.

> **"Used to."**

Orphie looked at her wings.

> **"You could be that person again."**

Silence.

Corin's wings tightened slightly.

Orphie noticed.

She continued anyway.

> **"You just need to try."**

Corin looked at her.

---

# **THE WRONG WORDS**

Orphie meant encouragement.

She wanted Corin to know that she wasn't permanently broken.

But the words came out differently.

> **"You can't stay like this forever."**

Corin stared at her.

Orphie immediately realized how it sounded.

She attempted to correct herself.

> **"I mean—I'm worried about you."**

Corin looked away.

> **"You don't have to be."**

> **"I do."**

> **"Why?"**

Orphie hesitated.

Then:

> **"Because I want you to get better."**

Corin went completely still.

---

# **THE INTERPRETATION**

Corin heard something different.

Not:

> _I want you to heal._

But:

> _I don't want you like this._

Not:

> _I love you._

But:

> _I need you to become someone else._

Her wings closed tighter around herself.

---

# **CORIN**

> **"So you don't like me like this."**

Orphie immediately shook her head.

> **"That's not what I said."**

> **"But that's what you meant."**

> **"No."**

> **"You said I need to get better."**

> **"Because you're hurting."**

Corin's voice became quieter.

> **"I'm always hurting."**

Orphie had no immediate answer.

---

# **THE SILENCE**

Snow accumulated on Corin's wings.

She did not brush it away.

Orphie tried to move closer.

Corin did not move away.

But she did not lean toward her either.

The distance between them suddenly felt enormous.

---

# **THE SENTENCE**

Orphie finally said:

> **"I just want the old Corin back."**

Silence.

Absolute silence.

Corin's face went blank.

Her ears lowered.

Her tails stopped moving completely.

Her wings closed around her.

---

# **THE DAMAGE**

Corin whispered:

> **"...The old Corin."**

Orphie realized immediately.

> **"Corin, I didn't mean—"**

> **"No."**

A pause.

> **"I understand."**

> **"That's not what I meant."**

Corin shook her head.

> **"It's okay."**

Her voice was completely empty.

> **"I'm not her anymore."**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie reached toward her.

> **"Corin."**

Corin moved one wing between them.

Not violently.

Just enough to stop the hand.

> **"Don't."**

Orphie froze.

---

# **THE OLD FEAR**

Corin's thoughts appeared to return to the same place.

The same belief documented repeatedly in her notebook.

That she was too heavy.

Too fragile.

Too damaged.

Too difficult.

That people would eventually prefer a version of her that was easier to love.

She whispered:

> **"You want her."**

Orphie answered:

> **"I want you."**

Corin looked at her.

> **"Then why did you say it?"**

Orphie couldn't answer.

---

# **THE WINGS**

Corin's wings trembled for the first time.

Only slightly.

A few feathers shifted.

No light appeared.

No anomalous energy responded.

They were completely ordinary.

Yet Corin pulled them tighter around herself.

---

# **THE CONVERSATION ENDS**

Orphie wanted to apologize.

She wanted to explain.

She wanted to take the words back.

But she understood that continuing to talk might only make things worse.

So she stopped.

Corin remained beneath the snow.

Orphie stayed beside her.

Neither spoke again for a long time.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie wanted to help.

She wanted Corin to heal.

She wanted her to laugh again.

To fly again.

To smile again.

To become happy again.

But she used the wrong words.

> **"I just want the old Corin back."**

To Orphie, it meant:

> _I miss who you used to be._

To Corin, it meant:

> _Who you are now isn't good enough._

And that was the one thing Corin could not bear to hear.

She sat beneath the snow.

Her wings closed around her.

Her body trembled.

And quietly, almost too quietly to hear, she said:

> **"I'm sorry I'm not her anymore."**

Orphie immediately answered:

> **"You don't have to be."**

But Corin had already stopped listening.

**End of Treehouse Incident Log**
