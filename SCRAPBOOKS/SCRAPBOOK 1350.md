# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Phoenix Raffedella has been formally briefed on the biological significance of Jake's teddy bear.

Her initial assumption was that the object was:

> **A comfort item.**

This assumption was incomplete.

The teddy bear contains Jake's **heart.**

Not metaphorically.

Not symbolically.

Not an anomalous representation of his emotional state.

His actual cardiac core is physically separated from his manifested body and housed within the teddy bear.

Jake's continued survival therefore depends upon the connection between himself and the bear.

The basic rule is exceptionally simple:

> **Separate Jake from the bear → Jake dies.**

This is the reason the teddy bear has been fitted with backpack straps.

Jake does not carry his heart because he likes carrying a teddy bear.

He carries his heart because he has to.

---

# 1. PHOENIX'S INITIAL BRIEFING

Phoenix was reviewing resident medical records when she encountered Jake's file.

She stopped.

Read the relevant section again.

Then looked across the room.

Jake was sitting on the floor.

His teddy bear was strapped securely against his back.

Phoenix stared at it.

Then at Jake.

Then back at the file.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “His heart is where?”

The medical officer repeated the information.

Phoenix blinked.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Inside the teddy bear?”

> **MEDICAL OFFICER:**  
> “Yes.”

Phoenix looked at Jake again.

Jake looked back.

> **JAKE:**  
> “What?”

Phoenix slowly closed the file.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Nothing.”

Pause.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “I need to sit down.”

# 2. THE CARDIAC CORE

Jake's anatomy is unusual even by Blacksite standards.

His human-form body possesses conventional biological structures sufficient for physical manifestation.

However, the organ responsible for sustaining his stellar-human manifestation is not located within his chest.

His **cardiac core** is externalized.

It resides inside the teddy bear.

The bear therefore performs several functions simultaneously:

- emotional comfort object;
    
- cardiac core housing;
    
- vital-status indicator;
    
- anomalous life-support component;
    
- physical connection point between Jake and his stellar manifestation.
    

The heartbeat audible from the bear is not an imitation.

It is Jake's heartbeat.

---

# 3. WHY THE BEAR HAS BACKPACK STRAPS

The backpack straps were not added for convenience.

They were added because the SAFEHOUSE eventually identified an obvious problem:

> **Jake is six years old.**

A six-year-old carrying a teddy bear everywhere is normal.

A six-year-old carrying his own externalized heart is considerably less normal.

The straps allow Jake to keep the bear physically attached to himself while leaving both hands free.

This is particularly useful when Jake:

- plays;
    
- eats;
    
- flies;
    
- carries objects;
    
- sleeps;
    
- walks around SAFEHOUSE;
    
- or uses his celestial greatsword.
    

The arrangement also dramatically reduces the possibility of accidental separation.
