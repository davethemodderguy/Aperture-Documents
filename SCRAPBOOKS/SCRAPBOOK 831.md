# SPAWN ARMORY INCIDENT

## “WHAT WEAPONRY?”

**CLASSIFICATION:** C.O.R.E. INTERNAL — ARCHIVAL

**STATUS:** CLOSED — REALITY ALTERATION  
**LOCATION:** SPAWN HEADQUARTERS  
**SUBJECT:** Mischevious Light  
**OBSERVING ARCHIVIST:** Neko

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following the previous confrontation in which SPAWN's Dark Matter weaponry successfully injured Mischevious Light, the Architect subsequently returned to SPAWN Headquarters.

SPAWN personnel attempted to secure their remaining weaponry and armory assets.

Mischevious Light objected.

Not by destroying them.

Not by confiscating them.

Not by disabling them.

He simply **denied that they had ever existed.**

At approximately 11:26, all SPAWN records, storage systems, inventory manifests, photographs, security footage, and physical evidence relating to the organization's Dark Matter weapons began contradicting their own existence.

When questioned, Mischevious Light reportedly responded:

> **“What weaponry?”**

Neko found this extremely funny.

---

# 2. THE ARMORY

SPAWN's primary armory was inspected following the previous incident.

Personnel confirmed that multiple Dark Matter weapons remained secured inside.

At 11:26:14, Mischevious Light entered the facility.

He approached the armory door.

A guard attempted to stop him.

> **SPAWN GUARD:**  
> “The armory is restricted.”

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Oh?”

He looked at the door.

Then at the guard.

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “What's inside?”

The guard answered:

> **“Weapons.”**

Mischevious Light paused.

> **“No, there aren't.”**

The armory door opened.

---

# 3. THE EMPTY ARMORY

The armory was empty.

Not emptied.

**Empty.**

The weapon racks were still present.

The storage containers were still present.

The inventory terminals were still present.

The weapons were not.

Personnel immediately checked the inventory system.

The system displayed:

> **TOTAL WEAPONS: 0**

> **DARK MATTER WEAPONS: 0**

> **ARMORY CONTENTS: NONE**

An officer reportedly shouted:

> **“THEY WERE HERE FIVE SECONDS AGO!”**

Mischevious Light looked at him.

> **“Were they?”**

---

# 4. DOCUMENTATION FAILURE

SPAWN personnel attempted to prove that the weapons had existed.

They opened archived photographs.

The photographs showed empty racks.

They opened procurement records.

The records contained no Dark Matter purchases.

They opened deployment logs.

The logs contained no weapon deployments.

They opened casualty reports from the previous incident.

The reports still described injuries caused by unknown phenomena.

The words **“Dark Matter weapon”** had disappeared from the relevant documents.

The same occurred across:

- Armory manifests
    
- Security footage
    
- Research reports
    
- Personnel briefings
    
- Weapons testing records
    
- Maintenance documentation
    
- Command directives
    
- Backup servers
    
- Printed records
    

SPAWN had effectively lost the historical existence of their own weapons.

---

# 5. THE ARGUMENT

A senior SPAWN officer confronted Mischevious Light.

> **SPAWN OFFICER:**  
> “You can't just erase our weapons.”

Mischevious Light looked confused.

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Erase?”

> **SPAWN OFFICER:**  
> “Our Dark Matter weapons!”

Mischevious Light looked toward the empty racks.

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Those?”

> **SPAWN OFFICER:**  
> “YES.”

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “I don't see anything.”

The officer reportedly stared at him.

Mischevious Light smiled.

> **“Maybe you never had any.”**

---
