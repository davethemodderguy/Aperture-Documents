# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Another Silverline sister has been located.

**Iris** was discovered by **Solider 11** under circumstances that C.O.R.E. considers unusually ironic.

Solider 11 had returned to her former gang to retrieve personal belongings following her voluntary departure.

During the visit, she encountered another contractor.

That contractor was Iris.

Neither subject had recognized the other beforehand.

The reason was simple:

They were working for the **same gang**.

They had apparently been operating within the same organization without realizing that they were sisters.

The discovery occurred when Solider 11 noticed Iris's hairclip.

The resulting reunion was described by witnesses as:

> **"Extremely awkward for approximately five seconds, followed by complete disbelief."**

Iris is additionally documented as an **exceptionally skilled driver.**

---

# 1. RETURN TO THE GANG

Following her recovery, Solider 11 returned briefly to her former employer.

The purpose was not to resume contract work.

She had left several personal belongings behind and wanted to retrieve them.

The gang permitted her entry.

No conflict occurred.

Solider 11 entered the building, collected her belongings, and began preparing to leave.

Then she saw another contractor.

A woman with white hair.

A familiar face.

A different hairclip.

Solider 11 stopped.

---

# 2. THE DRIVER

Iris was sitting in the driver's seat of one of the gang's vehicles.

She had apparently just returned from an assignment.

Solider 11 looked at her.

Iris looked at Solider 11.

Neither recognized the other immediately.

Then Solider 11 noticed the hairclip.

She froze.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "...No way."

Iris looked confused.

> **IRIS:**  
> "What?"

Solider 11 stepped closer.

She looked at Iris's face.

Then her hair.

Then the hairclip.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Iris?"

Iris went completely still.

> **IRIS:**  
> "...11?"

Silence.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Iris."

> **IRIS:**  
> "You're alive?"

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Apparently."

Iris stared at her.

> **IRIS:**  
> "You're working here?"

Solider 11 looked around the building.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Was."

Iris looked at the vehicle.

Then back at Solider 11.

> **IRIS:**  
> "I've been working here for months."

Solider 11 stared.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "So have I."

Neither spoke.

---

# 3. THE REALIZATION

The two eventually compared their histories.

Both had been hired as contractors.

Both had operated under independent identities.

Both had been assigned to different teams.

Both had rarely interacted directly.

Most importantly:

Neither had realized that the other was a Silverline survivor.

The gang had unknowingly employed **two sisters who looked almost identical.**

C.O.R.E. considers this an extraordinary failure of organizational recordkeeping.

Jane's response was less professional.

> **JANE:**  
> "You two were literally working in the same building?"

Solider 11 nodded.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Apparently."

> **JANE:**  
> "And neither of you noticed?"

Iris answered:

> **IRIS:**  
> "We weren't exactly comparing family histories between jobs."

Jane paused.

> **JANE:**  
> "...Fair."

---

# 4. IRIS — DRIVER PROFILE

Iris has developed a considerable reputation within the gang as a driver.

Available witness statements consistently describe her as:

- exceptionally calm behind the wheel;
    
- highly attentive;
    
- extremely familiar with difficult routes;
    
- capable of navigating under pressure;
    
- unusually good at judging vehicle movement;
    
- and remarkably difficult to surprise while driving.
    

One gang member reportedly described Iris as:

> **"The only person I'd trust to drive through that district at full speed."**

Iris reportedly responded:

> **IRIS:**  
> "That's because I know where I'm going."

---

# 5. THE DRIVE TO SAFEHOUSE

KCA initially intended to transport Iris separately.

Solider 11 objected.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "I'll bring her."

The KCA officer asked:

> **"You're driving?"**

Solider 11 looked toward Iris.

Iris smiled.

> **IRIS:**  
> "I'm driving."

The officer reconsidered.

Iris was subsequently permitted to operate the vehicle under supervision.

The journey was uneventful.

The driving was reportedly extremely smooth.

Solider 11 spent most of the trip staring out the window.

After approximately ten minutes:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "You're really good."

Iris smiled.

> **IRIS:**  
> "I know."

---

# 6. ARRIVAL

Anby was informed that another sister had been recovered.

She arrived at the SAFEHOUSE entrance.

Iris stepped out of the vehicle.

Anby stopped.

Iris stopped.

They looked at one another.

Anby recognized the hairclip first.

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Iris."

Iris smiled.

> **IRIS:**  
> "Hi, Anby."

Anby immediately approached.

The two embraced.

Solider 11 stood nearby.

For several seconds, she simply watched.

Then:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "I found her."

Anby looked at her.

> **ANBY:**  
> "You did."

---

# 7. THREE SISTERS BECOME FOUR

Twiggy arrived shortly afterward.

She looked at Iris.

Then at Solider 11.

Then at Anby.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "Another one."

Iris nodded.

> **IRIS:**  
> "Another one."

Twiggy smiled.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "Welcome home."

Iris's expression softened.

> **IRIS:**  
> "Thanks."

---

# 8. JANE'S REACTION

Jane approached Iris.

She looked at her.

Then at Solider 11.

> **JANE:**  
> "So you're the driver."

Iris nodded.

> **IRIS:**  
> "Apparently."

Jane looked impressed.

> **JANE:**  
> "How good?"

Iris shrugged.

> **IRIS:**  
> "Good."

Solider 11 immediately interrupted.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "She's underselling it."

Jane looked at Iris again.

> **JANE:**  
> "Oh?"

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "She's terrifying."

Iris laughed.

> **IRIS:**  
> "I'm a safe driver."

Jane stared at her.

> **JANE:**  
> "Those two statements don't contradict each other, do they?"

Iris smiled.

> **IRIS:**  
> "Not at all."

---

# 9. SOLIDER 11'S PERSONAL OBSERVATION

Later, Solider 11 was asked whether she found the coincidence strange.

She answered:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Very."

> **"You worked together for months."**

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Yeah."

> **"And never knew."**

Solider 11 shrugged.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "We were contractors."

Pause.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "You don't exactly ask everyone where they grew up."

She looked toward Iris.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Apparently I should've asked."

---

# 10. IRIS'S OBSERVATION

Iris was asked why she had never recognized Solider 11.

She answered:

> **IRIS:**  
> "Everyone in Silverline looked like us."

She touched her hairclip.

> **IRIS:**  
> "The clip is what mattered."

She looked toward Solider 11.

> **IRIS:**  
> "I didn't know hers."

Solider 11 nodded.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "I didn't know yours."

A pause.

Iris smiled.

> **IRIS:**  
> "Now we do."

# 12. WELFARE ASSESSMENT

Iris has voluntarily entered KCA protection after learning that Anby and her other sisters had been recovered.

As with Solider 11 and Twiggy, no force was required.

Iris has expressed interest in continuing to drive if appropriate.

KCA has not prohibited the activity.

Her previous gang employment will be documented but is not considered sufficient evidence of ongoing criminal affiliation.

Iris's current priority appears to be:

> **Reuniting with her sisters.**

Solider 11 went back to her old gang to collect her belongings.

She found her sister instead.

The funny part?

They had already been working together.

Same gang.

Same city.

Same physical appearance.

Different hairclips.

Different jobs.

Neither knew.

Solider 11 was the contractor.

Iris was the driver.

Four identical faces.

Four different lives.

And two of them apparently spent months working for the same gang without realizing they were family.

C.O.R.E. is beginning to suspect that the Silverline sisters have a remarkable talent for finding each other in the most ridiculous ways possible.
