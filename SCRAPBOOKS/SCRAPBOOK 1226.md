# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### MSAC / RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-INC-4C-NORA-363  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESTRICTED — RESIDENT SECURITY / CHILD WELFARE  
**STATUS:** CLOSED — SUBJECT RECOVERED  
**SUBJECT:** Nora Tachyon  
**INCIDENT:** Woodland Coorp Abduction Attempt  
**LOCATION:** BLACKSITE EXTERIOR / PERIMETER RECREATION AREA

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately **15:42**, Nora Tachyon was abducted from an authorized outdoor recreation area by a Woodland Coorp hostile element.

Nora was playing outside at the time.

The hostile group successfully removed her from the immediate perimeter before Blacksite security detected the intrusion.

MSAC was dispatched immediately.

Neri Tachyon and Elsher Tachyon subsequently joined the response.

Upon reaching the hostile staging location, personnel discovered that the situation had already changed substantially.

The Woodland Coorp personnel assigned to the abduction had been incapacitated.

Several were deceased.

The remainder were unconscious.

Nora was found standing among the incapacitated hostile personnel.

She was carrying:

- **one emptied Deadeye pistol;**
    
- **one bloodied knife;**
    
- and an expression described by multiple witnesses as **unusually happy.**
    

Blood was present on Nora's face.

Medical examination later determined that the blood was **not hers.**

Nora herself had no significant injuries.

---

# 1. INITIAL INCIDENT

Nora had been permitted to play outside under normal SAFEHOUSE supervision.

The activity was routine.

She had reportedly been:

- running around the recreation area;
    
- examining plants;
    
- playing with Alice earlier in the afternoon;
    
- and attempting to convince a maintenance employee that a decorative stone was "probably important."
    

No threat was initially detected.

The Woodland Coorp team entered the area using a temporary breach in the outer security perimeter.

The group isolated Nora from nearby personnel and removed her from the recreation zone.

The entire event lasted only several minutes.

---

# 2. SECURITY RESPONSE

Blacksite security identified the unauthorized movement shortly afterward.

MSAC was immediately deployed.

The response team included:

- MSAC personnel;
    
- Security personnel;
    
- **Elsher Tachyon**;
    
- **Neri Tachyon.**
    

The initial assumption was that Nora remained under Woodland custody.

That assumption was incorrect.

---

# 3. ARRIVAL AT THE SITE

The first MSAC team reached the suspected Woodland location.

The exterior was quiet.

No active resistance was immediately detected.

One MSAC operator reported:

> **"I don't see anyone."**

Another replied:

> **"There are bodies inside."**

The team entered.

The first Woodland mercenary was unconscious.

The second was also unconscious.

The third was deceased.

Then another.

Then another.

The pattern continued.

There had been no organized Woodland defensive response.

Something had already happened.

---

# 4. NORA

Nora was eventually located in the central section of the building.

She was standing upright.

She was holding her **Deadeye**.

The magazine was empty.

Her other hand held a knife.

The blade was bloodied.

There was blood across part of her face and clothing.

Nora herself appeared completely unharmed.

More concerningly:

She was smiling.

Not her usual excited smile.

Not the expression she makes when playing with Alice.

Not the expression she uses when showing Neri something she discovered.

It was an unusually calm, satisfied expression.

She looked toward the approaching MSAC personnel.

Then:

> **Nora:**  
> "Hi."

Silence.

The MSAC operator nearest to her simply stared.

---

# 5. NERI ARRIVES

Neri entered shortly afterward.

She stopped immediately upon seeing Nora.

Nora looked toward her.

Her expression changed.

Not to fear.

Not to distress.

Recognition.

> **Nora:**  
> "Sis!"

Neri approached carefully.

She looked at the blood.

Then at the empty Deadeye.

Then at the knife.

Then back at Nora.

> **Neri:**  
> "...Are you hurt?"

Nora shook her head.

> **Nora:**  
> "No."

Neri examined her more closely.

> **Neri:**  
> "Are you sure?"

> **Nora:**  
> "Yeah."

Neri remained silent.

She reached toward Nora's face and wiped away a small amount of blood.

Nora did not resist.

> **Neri:**  
> "Not yours."

Nora nodded.

---

# 6. ELSHER'S ARRIVAL

Elsher entered several seconds later.

He immediately began assessing the room.

His attention moved across:

- exits;
    
- incapacitated personnel;
    
- available cover;
    
- Nora;
    
- Neri;
    
- the empty Deadeye;
    
- and the overall condition of the site.
    

His expression changed.

> **Elsher:**  
> "...What happened?"

Nora looked at him.

> **Nora:**  
> "They took me."

Elsher nodded.

> **Elsher:**  
> "I know."

Nora pointed toward the surrounding Woodland personnel.

> **Nora:**  
> "Then they stopped."

Elsher looked around again.

> **Elsher:**  
> "Apparently."

---

# 7. THE DEADeye

The Deadeye was recovered from Nora.

The weapon had been completely expended.

No ammunition remained in the magazine.

Its condition was otherwise within expected limits.

Nora appeared to have used it during the confrontation.

When asked why she had emptied it:

> **Nora:**  
> "They wouldn't stop."

Elsher looked at Neri.

Neri looked at Nora.

Neither responded immediately.

---

# 8. THE KNIFE

The knife was subsequently secured by MSAC personnel.

No operational explanation was provided by Nora beyond:

> **Nora:**  
> "It was there."

When asked whether she had taken it from the Woodland personnel:

> **Nora:**  
> "Yes."

No further information was volunteered.

---

# 9. NORA'S EXPRESSION

The most unusual aspect of the incident was not the condition of the hostile personnel.

It was Nora's emotional presentation.

Throughout the initial recovery period, she remained unusually cheerful.

She was not crying.

She was not shaking.

She did not appear confused.

She was not asking where Neri was.

She was not asking whether she could go home.

She simply appeared...

**happy.**

This was considered sufficiently abnormal that MSAC requested an immediate welfare evaluation.

---

# 10. WELFARE INTERVIEW

Nora was seated with Neri nearby.

The interviewer asked:

> **C.O.R.E. WELFARE OFFICER:**  
> "Nora, are you happy right now?"

Nora nodded.

> **Nora:**  
> "Yeah."

> **OFFICER:**  
> "Why?"

Nora looked toward Neri.

> **Nora:**  
> "Because Sis came."

Neri's expression remained unchanged.

Nora continued:

> **Nora:**  
> "And Big Brother came."

Elsher looked away.

The officer paused.

> **OFFICER:**  
> "Were you scared before they arrived?"

Nora thought for a moment.

> **Nora:**  
> "A little."

> **OFFICER:**  
> "And now?"

Nora smiled.

> **Nora:**  
> "Not anymore."

---

# 11. THE ADAPTATION RESPONSE

C.O.R.E. does not currently interpret Nora's behavior as evidence that she enjoyed being abducted or that she was emotionally unaffected by the event.

Rather, the incident appears to demonstrate her previously documented adaptive response.

Nora was placed into an unfamiliar and dangerous environment.

She adapted.

She recognized available resources.

She used what she had.

She survived until help arrived.

However, there is one important complication.

Help did not arrive first.

Nora appears to have **become the immediate problem for the abductors.**

---

# 12. ELSHER'S ASSESSMENT

Elsher reviewed the scene before leaving.

His assessment was brief.

> **Elsher:**  
> "They made one mistake."

The officer asked:

> **"Which one?"**

Elsher looked toward Nora.

> **Elsher:**  
> "They assumed taking a six-year-old would make her helpless."

Pause.

> **Elsher:**  
> "They didn't account for what she'd do after that assumption stopped being true."

He did not elaborate.

---

# 13. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri remained beside Nora throughout the medical evaluation.

She did not congratulate her.

She did not criticize her.

She did not ask for details.

She simply stayed there.

Eventually:

> **Neri:**  
> "You're home."

Nora nodded.

> **Nora:**  
> "Yeah."

A few seconds passed.

> **Nora:**  
> "Can we go back outside?"

Neri looked at her.

> **Neri:**  
> "...Not today."

Nora considered this.

> **Nora:**  
> "Okay."

She leaned against Neri.

Neri allowed her to.

---

# 14. MEDICAL FINDINGS

Nora sustained no significant injuries during the incident.

Blood found on her clothing and face was confirmed to originate from hostile personnel.

Medical personnel found:

- no significant external injury;
    
- no evidence of major trauma;
    
- no indication of acute medical instability;
    
- no immediate neurological concerns.
    

Psychological welfare monitoring remains recommended.

The absence of physical injury does not mean the event is considered inconsequential.

Nora was abducted.

That fact remains the primary welfare concern.

---

# 15. SECURITY CONSEQUENCES

The perimeter recreation area has been temporarily closed.

Security has initiated a full review of:

- perimeter integrity;
    
- outdoor recreation procedures;
    
- hostile infiltration detection;
    
- resident movement monitoring;
    
- emergency response times;
    
- and child-resident security protocols.
    

Woodland Coorp has been formally classified as having demonstrated direct hostile interest in a SAFEHOUSE child resident.

Additional protective measures are therefore being implemented.

---

# 16. MSAC RESPONSE

The MSAC personnel who arrived at the scene were visibly unsettled.

One operator later summarized the experience:

> **"We went in expecting to rescue Nora."**

Pause.

> **"We found Nora had already handled it."**

Another operator described the scene as:

> **"The strangest child recovery operation I've ever been part of."**

A third refused to discuss the incident beyond:

> **"I am never underestimating that kid again."**

---

# 17. NORA'S POST-INCIDENT BEHAVIOR

Approximately forty minutes after returning to SAFEHOUSE, Nora was observed sitting beside Neri.

She had received a replacement magazine for her Deadeye through normal controlled procedures.

She was no longer visibly distressed.

She was talking about something completely unrelated.

> **Nora:**  
> "Sis."

Neri looked at her.

> **Nora:**  
> "Can we have cake?"

Neri paused.

> **Neri:**  
> "...Yes."

Nora immediately brightened.

> **Nora:**  
> "Yay."

Neri stood.

Nora followed.

The incident was not discussed again that evening.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The Woodland Coorp abduction attempt failed.

Nora Tachyon was recovered alive and medically stable.

The hostile element suffered substantial casualties before MSAC arrived.

The precise sequence of events remains under restricted review.

The incident nevertheless reinforces a previously documented characteristic of Nora:

> **She adapts.**

She was removed from familiar surroundings.

She was separated from her family.

She was placed in an unfamiliar hostile environment.

And she adapted to survive it.

This does not make the abduction acceptable.

It does not make Nora invulnerable.

It does not justify allowing her to face hostile personnel alone.

It simply means that Woodland Coorp personnel made an extremely serious miscalculation regarding who they had taken.

---

# ARCHIVIST NOTE

There is now an unofficial distinction among MSAC personnel.

Before the incident:

> **"Protect Nora."**

After the incident:

> **"Protect Nora."**

The instruction has not changed.

Only the amount of confidence attached to the assumption that Nora needs rescuing has.

One additional note has been added to the security briefing:

> **DO NOT UNDERESTIMATE THE SIX-YEAR-OLD.**

Underneath it, someone added:

> **"Seriously."**

C.O.R.E. has not removed the annotation.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Nora was taken because Woodland Coorp believed they had found an easy target.

They were wrong.

When Neri arrived, she found her sister alive.

When Elsher arrived, he found the situation already resolved.

And when MSAC finally reached Nora, she was standing there with an empty Deadeye, a bloodied knife, and an expression that was almost disturbingly cheerful.

Neri's first concern was simple:

> **"Are you hurt?"**

Nora's answer was equally simple:

> **"No."**

Then she asked for cake.

For everyone involved, that was enough to know that Nora was still Nora.

**— END INCIDENT RECORD**
