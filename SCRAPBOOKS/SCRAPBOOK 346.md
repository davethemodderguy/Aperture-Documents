# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELLNESS OBSERVATION**

# **DIALYN — MART MAGNET PHENOMENON**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Dialyn

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following a recent anomalous physiological change, personnel documented a minor cosmetic alteration affecting Dialyn.

Already recognizable by her distinctive hair—**black on the left and white on the right**—Dialyn now exhibits an additional feature.

The eyelashes and outer outline surrounding her **left eye** have gradually turned white.

The remainder of her appearance is unchanged.

Medical examination found no adverse effects.

The change has been classified as another benign anomalous side effect.

---

# **PERSONNEL RESPONSE**

The alteration immediately became a topic of discussion throughout the Blacksite.

Common comments included:

> "...That actually looks really nice."

> "...Wait, wasn't that side black before?"

> "...I thought she'd always looked like that."

Dialyn herself remained largely indifferent.

> "...As long as nothing else changes."

Medical staff continue routine observation.

---

# **UNRELATED... OR PERHAPS RELATED**

During the same week, Dialyn was noticeably less energetic than usual.

The cause was not medical.

She was simply feeling a little down.

The Marts noticed.

Immediately.

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

Within approximately fifteen minutes, Dialyn unintentionally became what Security has officially nicknamed:

> **A Mart Magnet.**

Every Mart passing through the area seemed to decide that Dialyn required emotional support.

One Mart sat beside her.

Then another.

Then five.

Then twenty.

Eventually, researchers stopped counting.

---

# **OBSERVED BEHAVIOR**

Unlike their usual energetic greetings, the Marts became unusually gentle.

They:

- Quietly sat beside her.
    
- Rested in her lap.
    
- Floated nearby.
    
- Used Blanket Form around her shoulders.
    
- Offered cheese crackers.
    
- Brought flowers.
    
- Tidied her desk without being asked.
    
- Sat silently while she worked.
    

Very few words were spoken.

Whenever one Mart left, another quietly took its place.

---

# **THE OFFICE**

By mid-afternoon, Dialyn's office contained enough Marts that opening the door required careful consideration.

One researcher opened it slightly.

Several blue blobs looked up.

One politely whispered:

> "...Shhh."

The researcher quietly closed the door again.

---

# **AMBASSADOR'S EXPLANATION**

The Blacksite Ambassador was later asked why so many Marts had gathered.

It answered simply:

> "...Dialyn sad."

The researcher replied:

> "...Only a little."

The Ambassador nodded.

> "...Little sad still sad."

---

# **DIALYN'S COMMENT**

Later that evening, Dialyn admitted she had noticed the constant stream of visitors.

> "...I couldn't stay sad."

She looked at the sleeping Marts scattered throughout her office.

> "...Not with this many trying."

One Mart quietly stirred.

Without opening its eyes, it mumbled:

> "...Teacher..."

Then fell back asleep.

Dialyn smiled.

---

# **STAR'S COMMENT**

Star stopped by to check on Dialyn.

She found the office almost completely occupied by Marts.

She quietly backed out.

Then laughed.

> "...Yeah."

> "...I'll come back later."

---

# **LUMI'S RESPONSE**

Lumi wasn't surprised in the slightest.

> "...That's what they do."

She watched another Mart arrive carrying a single flower.

> "...They always know."

She smiled proudly.

> "...I'm glad."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The following morning, Dialyn appeared much more like herself.

The Marts noticed immediately.

One by one, they happily returned to their normal duties throughout the Blacksite.

Before leaving, the last Mart looked up at her.

> "...Happy?"

Dialyn smiled.

> "...Yeah."

The Mart gave a satisfied nod.

> **"Good."**

It then drifted out of the office, leaving behind a single cheese cracker on her desk.

No one ever determined which Mart had left it there.

Dialyn ate it anyway.

**End of Personnel Wellness Observation**
