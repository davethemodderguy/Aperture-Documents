# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately 02:00, Aries Akana reported encountering an unidentified blue apparition while returning from the SAFEHOUSE cafeteria.

The apparition displayed several unusual physical characteristics.

Its legs were absent, with the lower portion of its body terminating before the point at which legs would ordinarily begin. It floated rather than walked.

A wick protruded from the top of its head. The wick carried a dim blue flame that emitted no detectable heat.

The upper portion of its head, extending downward to the eye region, appeared melted or consumed by the flame. Despite this apparent damage, the flame did not continue melting the remaining structure of the head.

The apparition held a teddy bear.

Aries froze upon seeing it.

He reportedly addressed the figure with a single word.

> **ARIES:** "Jake?"

The apparition turned toward him.

It then responded:

> **APPARITION:** "It took you so long to find me, yet so short to let me die in the hands of someone wearing your face."

The apparition subsequently demanifested.

No physical remains were recovered from the location.

No confirmed explanation for the phenomenon has been established.

The encounter lasted only briefly.

Its psychological impact on Aries was immediate and severe.

He reportedly remained motionless after the apparition disappeared, then began exhibiting signs of acute distress. His subsequent statements indicate that he interpreted the encounter as confirmation of his worst existing fear: that Jake's final understanding of him was one of betrayal and abandonment.

C.O.R.E. cannot currently establish whether the apparition was Jake, an anomalous reconstruction of Jake, an external entity exploiting Aries's grief, or another phenomenon entirely.

The statement attributed to the apparition must be preserved exactly as reported.

It must not, however, be treated as independently verified testimony from the deceased.

Aries encountered the blue figure.

The apparition disappeared.

Aries remained at the location.

The investigation is attempting to determine whether the relevant security systems recorded any visual, thermal, electromagnetic, or other anomalous evidence.

# 2. PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

The reported apparition possessed four principal characteristics.

## 2.1 — Lower Body

The apparition had no visible legs.

Its lower body appeared to terminate above the floor, with no conventional feet or limbs.

It moved through the corridor by floating.

No footsteps were reported.

No contact with the floor was observed.

This characteristic distinguishes the apparition from a conventional human manifestation wearing a costume or disguise, although it does not independently establish a supernatural origin.

## 2.2 — Cranial Wick

A wick protruded from the top of the apparition's head.

A dim blue flame burned at its tip.

The flame emitted no detectable heat during the reported encounter.

No verified measurements are available.

The absence of detectable heat may indicate an anomalous combustion process, a nonthermal luminous phenomenon, or a visual effect that only resembled fire.

Further investigation is required.

## 2.3 — Cranial Damage

The region extending from the top of the head down to the eyes appeared melted.

The damage was visually consistent with material having been softened or consumed by the flame.

However, the flame did not continue melting the remaining structure of the head.

The boundary between the damaged and undamaged regions appeared stable.

This is inconsistent with ordinary fire behavior.

C.O.R.E. cannot determine whether the apparent damage was physical, symbolic, or an intrinsic property of the manifestation.

No conclusion concerning cause of death, method of transformation, or postmortem condition can be drawn from appearance alone.

## 2.4 — Teddy Bear

The apparition held a teddy bear.

The significance of this detail is considerable.

Jake's teddy bear was not an ordinary possession. During his life, it contained his actual heartbeat and functioned as his cardiac vessel.

It was also one of the most familiar objects associated with him.

However, the apparition's teddy bear has not been conclusively identified as the original object recovered following Jake's death.

The resemblance may be genuine.

It may be symbolic.

It may be deliberately imitative.

Until the object can be examined or otherwise verified, personnel must not assume that it is the original bear.

Aries was returning from the cafeteria when he noticed the blue figure.

He stopped.

Its blue flame burned quietly above its head.

Aries stared at it.

His posture became rigid.

He did not attempt to approach.

He did not attempt to leave.

After several seconds, he spoke.

The apparition turned toward him.

**APPARITION:**

# 4. THE WORDS

The apparition's statement contains two distinct accusations.

> **"It took you so long to find me..."**

And:

> **"...yet so short to let me die in the hands of someone wearing your face."**

The first refers to the period before Aries found Jake.

The second refers to the period after they were reunited.

Together, they compress the entire history of their relationship into a single sentence.

Aries spent a considerable amount of time trying to find his younger brother.

He finally succeeded.

They began rebuilding their relationship.

Then Jake was attacked by someone who resembled Aries.

Jake survived the earlier incident.

He was later left alone again and killed in a separate attack.

The apparition's statement uses these events to construct a particularly painful accusation:

That Aries took too long to arrive when Jake needed him, and too little time to protect him after arriving.

This interpretation is consistent with Aries's existing guilt.

It is not proof that the apparition possesses Jake's memories, identity, or consciousness.

The words may have originated from Jake.

They may have originated from an entity familiar with the incident.

They may have been generated from Aries's own grief.

Or they may have been deliberately chosen by an external intelligence to inflict maximum psychological harm.

C.O.R.E. cannot yet distinguish between these possibilities.

**The statement is evidence of what the apparition communicated. It is not, by itself, evidence that Jake's consciousness was responsible for the communication.**

# 5. PSYCHOLOGICAL IMPACT ON ARIES

The encounter occurred during an already severe period of bereavement.

Aries had been staying awake late into the night and repeatedly consuming Nitro Fuel to maintain focus.

His sleep remained disrupted.

His grief remained unresolved.

He had repeatedly expressed guilt concerning the circumstances of Jake's death.

The apparition's appearance and statement directly targeted those vulnerabilities.

## 5.1 — Immediate Response

Following demanifestation, Aries displayed an acute distress response.

He reportedly remained frozen for several seconds.

He then appeared to lose his composure.

When approached by the first responding personnel member, he initially did not answer questions.

He repeatedly looked toward the point where the apparition had disappeared.

His hands were shaking.

His breathing was irregular.

When he eventually spoke, his statements were brief.

**ARIES:**

"He was here."

The officer asked whom he meant.

**ARIES:**

"Jake."

A pause.

**ARIES:**

"He was here, and I couldn't even—"

He stopped.

The sentence was not completed.

The officer asked whether Aries wanted medical assistance.

He shook his head, then covered his face.

**ARIES:**

"He knows."

The officer asked what he believed Jake knew.

Aries did not answer immediately.

**ARIES:**

"He knows I left him."

The officer attempted to clarify that the apparition's identity had not been established.

Aries became visibly distressed.

**ARIES:**

"Don't."

The officer stopped speaking.

After several seconds, Aries continued.

**ARIES:**

"Please don't tell me it wasn't him."

He began crying.

**ARIES:**

"Please. Not right now."

The officer remained nearby without attempting to resolve the identity of the apparition during the acute distress response.

This was considered appropriate.

The question of identity requires investigation.

It did not need to be settled while Aries was in the middle of a psychological crisis.

# 6. WELFARE ASSESSMENT

Aries's response should be understood in the context of his existing grief.

Before this encounter, he was already struggling with the loss of his brother.

He had been attempting to remain awake, occupy himself with work, and avoid the quiet periods in which his thoughts returned to Jake.

The apparition introduced a new and particularly distressing possibility.

Not merely that Jake was dead.

Not merely that Jake had suffered.

But that Jake might somehow remain aware of what happened and hold Aries responsible.

The encounter therefore threatens to transform Aries's existing guilt into something he may perceive as confirmation.

This is a significant welfare concern.

If Aries accepts the apparition's words as definitive proof that Jake blames him, his grief may become increasingly organized around self-condemnation.

He may begin treating every prior mistake as evidence that he failed Jake as a brother.

He may interpret his own face as a symbol of the attack.

He may conclude that finding Jake was meaningless because he could not keep him safe.

These conclusions are not established facts.

They are possible psychological consequences of the encounter and must be monitored.

Personnel should not reinforce them.

They should also avoid dismissing Aries's experience as imaginary merely because it has not been independently verified.

The encounter was real as an experience reported by Aries.

The nature and identity of the apparition remain unconfirmed.

Both statements can be true simultaneously.

# 7. ANOMALOUS INVESTIGATION

C.O.R.E. Anomalous Research Division has opened a dedicated investigation.

The apparition will not be designated as Jake's confirmed postmortem manifestation at this stage.

The following hypotheses remain under consideration.

### Hypothesis A — Postmortem Manifestation

The apparition may represent a continued manifestation of Jake following his death.

The teddy bear and apparent familiarity with the circumstances of his death support further examination of this possibility.

They do not prove it.

### Hypothesis B — Residual Anomalous Imprint

The apparition may be a nonconscious reconstruction formed from information associated with Jake, his death, or his anomalous existence.

Under this hypothesis, the statement could be a residual expression rather than a deliberate accusation.

### Hypothesis C — External Entity

An unrelated anomalous entity may have assumed Jake's appearance or used recognizable details to approach Aries.

The entity may possess information concerning the attack or may be capable of extracting information from its target.

Neither capability has been established.

### Hypothesis D — Deliberate Psychological Manipulation

The encounter may have been produced by an entity or individual capable of exploiting grief.

The wording is unusually well aligned with Aries's existing guilt.

This warrants investigation but does not establish that the event was intentionally engineered.

### Hypothesis E — Unknown Phenomenon

C.O.R.E. cannot exclude mechanisms outside the currently available models.

No hypothesis is to be treated as confirmed until supported by independent evidence.

# 8. INVESTIGATIVE PRECAUTIONS

Until the apparition's nature is better understood, personnel are instructed to:

- preserve security footage and sensor logs from the relevant corridor;
    
- document any subsequent manifestations without provoking them;
    
- avoid attempting to restrain or attack the apparition if it reappears, unless immediate defensive action is necessary;
    
- avoid promising Aries that the figure was Jake;
    
- avoid insisting that the figure could not have been Jake;
    
- avoid treating its statement as a verified message from the deceased;
    
- document the teddy bear's appearance and any correspondence with the original object;
    
- and notify Anomalous Research Division if similar manifestations occur elsewhere.
    

Aries must not be used as bait to provoke another appearance.

His grief does not make him an investigative instrument.

If further contact occurs, personnel should prioritize his immediate safety and ensure that another trusted person is available to support him afterward.

The apparition's statement may be important evidence.

It is not worth deliberately exposing a bereaved resident to another potentially destabilizing encounter.

---

# 9. THE NITRO FUEL CONCERN

The timing of the encounter raises an additional welfare concern.

Aries had been awake late into the night, consistent with his established pattern.

He has repeatedly consumed Nitro Fuel to remain focused despite exhaustion.

C.O.R.E. has not established whether he consumed Nitro Fuel immediately before this particular encounter or whether any resulting intoxication was present.

The investigation must not assume that the apparition was caused by Nitro Fuel.

However, his sleep deprivation and possible substance-related exhaustion may affect his ability to process a highly distressing experience.

Medical personnel are to assess his condition without treating the encounter itself as proof of intoxication or hallucination.

Aries should be offered water, food, a quiet environment, and medical attention if indicated.

He should not be encouraged to remain awake in the hope that the apparition will return.

He should not be permitted to use additional Nitro Fuel as a means of forcing himself through the immediate aftermath.

If he wishes to discuss what he saw, personnel should listen without suggesting details that he has not reported.

If he does not wish to discuss it, the interview should stop.

---

# 10. THE CORRIDOR AFTERWARD

The corridor was examined after Aries had been escorted to a private area.

No physical remains were recovered.

No confirmed traces of blue flame were identified in the initial examination.

The absence of such traces does not establish that the event was nonphysical.

Likewise, the lack of a physical explanation does not establish that it was supernatural.

The location has been preserved for review of the available security records.

Personnel who use the corridor are not to be told that Jake's ghost has been confirmed.

The correct description is:

**An unidentified blue apparition, reportedly resembling Jake Akana, was observed by Aries Akana and subsequently demanifested.**

This language is deliberate.

It preserves the event without deciding its meaning prematurely.

---

# 11. C.O.R.E. PSYCHOLOGICAL WELFARE DIRECTIVE

Aries is to receive immediate follow-up care.

The following measures are mandatory:

1. A welfare officer must remain available to him during the immediate aftermath.
    
2. He must be assessed for acute distress, sleep deprivation, and any indication that he may harm himself.
    
3. He must not be left without access to support merely because he requests that others stop talking.
    
4. Personnel must distinguish between respecting his need for quiet and abandoning him during a crisis.
    
5. No one is to confront him with the apparition's statement as though it were an established verdict on his conduct.
    
6. No one is to demand that he explain, defend, or justify his relationship with Jake.
    
7. His Nitro Fuel consumption and sleep pattern must be reassessed.
    
8. He must be offered the opportunity to speak with a qualified clinician familiar with traumatic bereavement.
    
9. If he requests privacy, personnel should arrange it while maintaining an appropriate welfare plan.
    
10. If another apparition appears, the incident must be documented separately, and Aries must not be pressured into further contact.
    

The purpose of these measures is not to persuade Aries to disregard what he saw.

It is to prevent an unverified encounter from becoming the sole authority through which he understands his brother's life and death.

---

# 12. FINAL ASSESSMENT

The encounter is classified as an **unidentified anomalous manifestation with significant psychological impact**.

Its identity remains unresolved.

Its apparent resemblance to Jake, the blue flame, the missing legs, the melted cranial region, and the teddy bear warrant further investigation.

The statement attributed to the apparition is especially significant because it directly references the circumstances of Jake's death and the attacker's impersonation of Aries.

However, C.O.R.E. cannot determine whether the words originated from Jake's consciousness, an anomalous imprint, an external entity, or another mechanism.

Aries is experiencing severe grief and guilt.

The apparition has intensified both.

His interpretation of the encounter must be treated with compassion, but it must not be confused with a confirmed conclusion about Jake's postmortem state.

**The investigation must establish what appeared in the corridor.**

**The welfare team must help Aries survive what the encounter has done to him.**

Neither responsibility should be neglected.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Aries had been awake again.

Another late night.

Another container of Nitro Fuel.

Another attempt to occupy himself until the hours passed.

He had been trying to find something that could keep his attention away from the empty space Jake had left behind.

Then, on the way back to his room, he saw a small blue figure floating in the corridor.

No legs.

A dim flame burning from a wick on its head.

A face that appeared to have been consumed by that same flame.

A teddy bear held against its body.

Aries stopped.

For a moment, the impossible seemed to offer him something he had wanted more than anything else.

A chance to see his little brother again.

He said Jake's name.

The figure turned.

And instead of an embrace, instead of a quiet greeting, instead of the small voice he remembered, Aries heard an accusation.

_It took you so long to find me._

He had traveled so far to find Jake.

_Yet so short to let me die._

He had only recently begun to understand how to be the brother Jake needed.

_In the hands of someone wearing your face._

Someone had used his appearance to hurt the child he loved.

Then the figure disappeared.

No explanation.

No opportunity to ask another question.

No way to determine whether Jake had truly been there.

Only the words.

Only the corridor.

Only Aries, standing alone where his brother had appeared.

And the terrible possibility that he had finally been given the chance to speak to Jake again, only to hear the thing he had always feared most.

That he had failed him.

C.O.R.E. cannot tell Aries what the apparition meant.

It cannot promise him that Jake was not there.

It cannot promise him that Jake was.

It cannot turn a brief, unexplained encounter into a reunion simply because the alternative is painful.

But it can establish one important distinction.

**The attacker chose to impersonate Aries.**

**The attacker chose to hurt Jake.**

Those choices were not Aries's.

The apparition's words may reflect Jake's feelings.

They may reflect something else entirely.

Until the evidence establishes its nature, no one has the right to present its accusation as the final judgment of a dead child.

Aries has already spent too many nights punishing himself for failing to save his brother.

He must not be left alone to decide that a few seconds in a corridor have settled every question about whether he deserved to be Jake's family.

He loved Jake.

He searched for him.

He found him.

He tried.

Those facts do not erase the mistakes, and they do not undo the tragedy.

But neither are they erased by the appearance of a blue ghost.

Tonight, Aries may remember only the accusation.

Tomorrow, he may remember the little boy who climbed onto his back and fell asleep against his shoulder.

Both memories may hurt.

Both may remain.

And until the investigation can establish what happened in that corridor, C.O.R.E. will not pretend to know which part of the encounter belonged to Jake.

**The ghost has disappeared.**

**The question remains.**

And Aries, who traveled so far to find his brother, is once again left waiting for an answer.

— **C.O.R.E. Anomalous Investigation & Psychological Welfare Division**
