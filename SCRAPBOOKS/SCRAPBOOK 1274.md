# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neri and Nora Tachyon were observed conducting independent research in the SAFEHOUSE science laboratory.

Neko was also present.

This was his first mistake.

The resulting interaction lasted approximately forty-seven minutes.

During that period:

- Neri corrected Neko's chemistry;
    
- Nora identified an adaptation-related biological inconsistency in Neko's model;
    
- Neri solved the chemistry component of the problem;
    
- Nora independently reproduced the environmental adaptation;
    
- Neko attempted to regain control of the discussion;
    
- Neri produced additional evidence;
    
- Nora asked a question that Neko could not immediately answer;
    
- Neri answered it instead;
    
- and Neko eventually stopped talking.
    

No equipment was damaged.

No experiment became unstable.

Neko's dignity did not survive.

---

# 2. INITIAL CONDITIONS

Neko was working on a model concerning anomalous material behavior under extreme thermal conditions.

Neri was working at the adjacent workstation.

Nora was sitting nearby wearing her thick coat, scarf, boots, and beanie.

A small patch of frost had formed beneath her chair.

Neko noticed it.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Nora.”

Nora looked up.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Stop freezing my floor.”

Nora looked down.

> **NORA:**  
> “Sorry.”

The frost stopped spreading.

Neko returned to his work.

Approximately thirty seconds later:

A new patch appeared.

Neko closed his eyes.

---

# 3. THE CHEMISTRY ERROR

Neko eventually presented his model to Neri.

> **NEKO:**  
> “This should explain the reaction.”

Neri looked at the screen.

She read the model.

She remained silent.

Neko waited.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Well?”

Neri pointed at one section.

> **NERI:**  
> “Wrong.”

Neko blinked.

> **NEKO:**  
> “What?”

Neri pointed again.

> **NERI:**  
> “Here.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “That's the coefficient.”

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “It's correct.”

Neri stared at him.

Then she opened another document.

She showed him the calculation.

Neko stared.

Then stared longer.

> **NEKO:**  
> “...Oh.”

Neri returned to her work.

---

# 4. NORA'S CONTRIBUTION

Nora had been watching the exchange.

She looked at Neko's model.

Then at Neri.

Then at the sample container.

> **NORA:**  
> “That's wrong too.”

Neko slowly turned toward her.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Which part?”

Nora pointed.

> **NORA:**  
> “That.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “Why?”

Nora paused.

> **NORA:**  
> “Because it changes when cold.”

Neko stared.

> **NEKO:**  
> “The material?”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “It adapted.”

Silence.

Neko looked at Neri.

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “She's right.”

Neko looked back at Nora.

> **NEKO:**  
> “You figured that out from one observation?”

Nora shrugged.

> **NORA:**  
> “It changed.”

---

# 5. NEKO'S SECOND MISTAKE

Neko attempted to defend his original model.

He explained the assumptions.

He explained the environmental conditions.

He explained the expected behavior.

Neri listened.

Nora listened.

When he finished:

> **NERI:**  
> “You're treating the material as static.”

Neko paused.

> **NEKO:**  
> “It is.”

Nora shook her head.

> **NORA:**  
> “Not anymore.”

Neko looked at her.

> **NORA:**  
> “It learned.”

Neko stopped.

---

# 6. THE DEMONSTRATION

Nora was asked to demonstrate what she meant.

She approached the sample.

Her hand touched the container.

A thin layer of frost appeared.

The temperature dropped.

The material changed.

Neko watched the instrumentation.

His expression gradually became more concerned.

Neri adjusted the measurement parameters.

> **NERI:**  
> “There.”

Neko stared at the new readings.

Neri pointed at the screen.

> **NERI:**  
> “Your model doesn't account for adaptive restructuring.”

Neko said nothing.

Nora looked at him.

> **NORA:**  
> “You forgot.”

Neko slowly leaned back in his chair.

> **NEKO:**  
> “I did not forget.”

Neri looked at him.

> **NERI:**  
> “You forgot.”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “You forgot.”

Neko sighed.

---

# 7. THE FINAL BLOW

Neko attempted one final question.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Fine. If I'm wrong, what is the correct model?”

Neri began typing.

Nora watched.

Neri completed the revised model.

She turned the monitor toward Neko.

The new model matched the observed behavior.

Neko stared at it.

Then at Neri.

> **NEKO:**  
> “How long did that take?”

Neri checked the timestamp.

> **NERI:**  
> “Eight minutes.”

Neko looked defeated.

Nora quietly added:

> **NORA:**  
> “I knew it before she typed it.”

Neko turned toward her.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Please stop helping.”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “Okay.”

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> “You're wrong about the second part too.”

Neko put his face in his hands.

---

# 8. OBSERVER COMMENTARY

A C.O.R.E. technician entered approximately three minutes later.

They observed:

Neri typing.

Nora sitting beside her.

Neko staring at the monitor.

The technician asked:

> **TECHNICIAN:**  
> “What happened?”

Neko answered without looking up.

> **NEKO:**  
> “The children are bullying me with science.”

Neri immediately corrected him.

> **NERI:**  
> “We're correcting you.”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “Scientifically.”

The technician left.

# 9. AFTERMATH

The revised model was incorporated into the research database.

Neri received full credit for the chemistry correction.

Nora received an annotation for identifying the adaptive behavior.

Neko received:

> **“Model requires revision.”**

He objected to the wording.

Neri refused to change it.

Nora suggested:

> **“Neko was wrong.”**

Neri considered it.

> **NERI:**  
> “Too informal.”

Neko looked relieved.

Then Neri added:

> **NERI:**  
> “We'll keep the first one.”

Neko sighed again.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Neko remains one of Aperture's most capable engineers and researchers.

Neri remains exceptionally specialized in chemistry.

Nora possesses unusually rapid adaptive reasoning.

When all three are placed in the same laboratory, the result is apparently:

**Neko attempting to explain science.**

**Neri correcting the chemistry.**

**Nora explaining why the original assumptions were obsolete.**

This incident was not malicious.

Neither Neri nor Nora appeared to intentionally humiliate Neko.

They simply kept being correct.

Unfortunately for Neko, that distinction did not make the experience any less humiliating.
