# 1. OVERVIEW

Neri is prone to sudden fainting episodes.

While these episodes can initially appear to be ordinary exhaustion, medical observation has established that Neri possesses an unusually fragile cardiovascular response to physical and emotional stress.

Her heart can tolerate normal activity.

It does **not** tolerate excessive strain particularly well.

Two major triggers have been identified:

- Severe emotional stress.
    
- Excessive stamina expenditure.
    

When either condition becomes sufficiently severe, Neri's cardiovascular system may abruptly destabilize.

The result can range from:

- Dizziness.
    
- Weakness.
    
- Loss of coordination.
    
- Pallor.
    
- Temporary loss of consciousness.
    
- Severe cardiovascular instability.
    
- Full systemic crash.
    

The final stage is considered an emergency.

Fortunately, SAFEHOUSE personnel have become highly familiar with the condition.

---

# 2. WHY THIS IS NOT CONSIDERED A CONSTANT EMERGENCY

Despite the alarming description above, Neri does not collapse continuously.

Most days, she is perfectly capable of:

- Walking around SAFEHOUSE.
    
- Eating normally.
    
- Playing with other residents.
    
- Performing chemistry work.
    
- Fighting when necessary.
    
- Running.
    
- Sliding.
    
- Air-jumping.
    
- Carrying considerably more knives than any reasonable person should own.
    

The problem occurs when Neri pushes herself too far.

Her body appears to have a hard limit.

Once that limit is crossed, her cardiovascular system essentially responds with:

> **"No."**

And shuts down.

---

# 3. SUGAR RESPONSE

One unusual characteristic of Neri's condition is her rapid response to readily available sugar.

When Neri begins displaying symptoms of an impending collapse, providing a high-sugar food or drink may assist in restoring her energy availability.

Commonly used foods include:

- Cake.
    
- Candy.
    
- Pudding.
    
- Sweet drinks.
    
- Chocolate.
    
- Pastries.
    
- Ice cream.
    

This has resulted in an unfortunate but effective SAFEHOUSE rule:

> **If Neri looks like she is about to faint, find sugar.**

It should be noted that sugar is **not a universal replacement for medical treatment**.

If Neri has already lost consciousness or is experiencing a severe cardiovascular event, personnel must follow the emergency procedure rather than attempting to force food into her mouth.

# 4. SUPPLEMENTAL BLOOD REQUIREMENT

Neri possesses another unusual physiological requirement.

For reasons currently unknown, she periodically requires **supplemental blood from an external source**.

Standard blood supplies have demonstrated inconsistent or ineffective results.

Blood from **Bao**, however, works.

Very well.

For reasons nobody has adequately explained.

---

# 5. THE BAO EXCEPTION

Bao is currently the only confirmed individual whose blood can reliably supplement Neri's condition.

Both Neri and Bao tolerate the transfer without apparent adverse effects.

Bao does not suffer significant weakness from providing small quantities.

Neri does not exhibit rejection or complications afterward.

The exact reason remains unknown.

### Current hypotheses include:

**Hypothesis B — Artificial Transformation**

**Hypothesis C — Anomalous Compatibility**

There may be no conventional biological explanation whatsoever.

**Hypothesis D — They Are Both Aquatic Thirens and the Universe Has Decided This Is Fine**

# 6. NERI'S METHOD

Neri has developed a remarkably casual approach to the situation.

If she requires a small amount of Bao's blood, she may simply approach Bao and place one of Bao's fingers into her mouth.

Bao understands what this means.

Neri understands what this means.

Everyone else has learned to simply continue with their day.

### Typical interaction:

> **Neri:** "Mom."

> **Bao:** "Hm?"

> **Neri:** _takes Bao's finger_

> **Bao:** "Again?"

> **Neri:** _nods_

> **Bao:** "Okay."

And that is generally the entire conversation.

---

# 7. MEDICAL SIGNIFICANCE

This behavior should **not** be interpreted as recreational, affectionate, or disciplinary.

It is a functional response to Neri's unusual physiology.

Neri appears to regard Bao as a safe and reliable source of something her body needs.

The arrangement has also contributed to the strong bond between the two.

Neri refers to Bao as:

> **"Mom."**

Bao has consequently become one of the people Neri instinctively seeks when she feels physically unwell.

# 9. COMMON PRE-COLLAPSE SIGNS

Personnel familiar with Neri have identified several warning signs.

These may include:

- Becoming unusually quiet.
    
- Slower movement.
    
- Loss of balance.
    
- Pale appearance.
    
- Difficulty standing.
    
- Heavy breathing.
    
- Sudden weakness.
    
- Holding onto nearby objects.
    
- Sitting down without explanation.
    
- Attempting to insist that she is "fine."
    

The final symptom is particularly unreliable.

> **Archivist Note:**  
> Neri saying "I'm fine" has historically meant anything from "I'm completely fine" to "my cardiovascular system is currently negotiating with God."

# 10. THE PROBLEM WITH HER COMBAT STYLE

Neri's condition creates a significant contradiction.

She is exceptionally capable in close combat.

She is fast.

She is mobile.

She can dodge incoming fire.

She can air-jump.

She possesses extraordinary melee proficiency.

She is also a ten-year-old whose heart may simply decide to stop cooperating if she spends too much stamina.

Consequently:

> **Neri's combat capability does not equal unlimited endurance.**

Personnel must never interpret her ability to fight effectively as evidence that she can fight indefinitely.

Neri has repeatedly demonstrated a tendency to push herself beyond sensible limits when protecting someone.

This is considered one of the highest-risk behavioral factors associated with her condition.

---

# 11. STRESS RESPONSE

Physical exhaustion is not the only concern.

Neri's cardiovascular system also responds poorly to severe emotional stress.

Given her history, this is particularly significant.

Threats to her friends can produce extremely intense emotional reactions.

The same people she desperately wants to protect are therefore also capable of becoming the reason she pushes herself too far.

This creates a dangerous feedback loop:

> **Friend threatened**  
> ↓  
> Neri becomes highly stressed  
> ↓  
> Neri expends excessive stamina protecting friend  
> ↓  
> Cardiovascular strain increases  
> ↓  
> Neri collapses

This is why SAFEHOUSE personnel are instructed to intervene before Neri reaches exhaustion.
