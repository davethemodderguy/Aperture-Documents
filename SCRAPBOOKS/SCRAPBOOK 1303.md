# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### ANOMALOUS MEDICAL & RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-ANM-4C-JAKE-145  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESTRICTED — ANOMALOUS MEDICAL EVENT  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE OBSERVATION  
**SUBJECT:** Jake — Restoration of Physical Interaction  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE / SECTOR 4-C

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has acquired a new anomalous capability following administration of **one additional drop of Nora Tachyon's blood.**

The resulting change is significant.

Jake can now:

- physically interact with solid objects;
    
- maintain physical contact with his surroundings;
    
- remain in stable contact with other residents;
    
- and, most importantly,
    

**eat food.**

This is the first time Jake has been capable of consuming physical food since his death approximately seventy-five years ago.

The change has produced an unexpected secondary effect.

For the first time in approximately seventy-five years:

> **Jake is no longer hungry.**

---

# 2. PREVIOUS CONDITION

Jake's manifested body previously lacked normal physical collision.

Objects passed through him.

Food passed through him.

He could see food.

He could smell food.

He could hold certain anomalous objects through manifestation rules.

But ordinary food could not remain inside his manifested body.

Consequently, eating was impossible.

Jake remained perpetually hungry.

This hunger was one of the oldest constants of his existence.

He had died from starvation.

He remained hungry after death.

And he continued experiencing hunger for decades afterward.

No conventional intervention had been able to change this.

---

# 3. BLOOD ADMINISTRATION

The decision to administer another drop of Nora's blood was made following the recent deterioration of Jake's manifested state.

The procedure was minimal.

A single drop.

No additional quantity was considered necessary.

The effect began almost immediately.

Jake's manifestation briefly became unstable.

Then:

**stabilized.**

The change was subtle at first.

Jake reached toward the table.

His hand touched it.

It did not pass through.

Jake stopped.

He stared at his hand.

---

# 4. FIRST COLLISION

Neri was present.

Jake slowly placed his palm against the tabletop.

Nothing unusual happened.

His hand remained there.

Jake pushed downward.

The table did not move.

Jake stared at it.

> **Jake:**  
> "...It stayed."

Neri looked at him.

> **Neri:**  
> "Yes."

Jake pressed his hand against the table again.

Then lifted it.

Then touched it again.

He appeared confused.

> **Jake:**  
> "I can touch it."

Neri nodded.

Jake looked at his own hand.

For perhaps the first time in seventy-five years, the physical world was no longer passing through him.

---

# 5. SECOND OBSERVATION

Jake walked toward a chair.

He reached out.

His fingers wrapped around the backrest.

He pulled.

The chair moved.

Jake froze.

He looked at the chair.

Then at Neri.

Then back at the chair.

> **Jake:**  
> "I moved it."

Neri nodded.

Jake pushed it back.

Then pulled it again.

A small smile appeared.

It lasted only briefly.

But it was genuine.

---

# 6. FOOD

The first food offered to Jake was intentionally simple.

A small piece of cake.

The choice was deliberate.

Jake had spent decades seeing other people eat cake.

He had smelled it.

He had been offered it.

He had been unable to consume it.

The cake was placed in front of him.

Jake stared at it.

> **Neri:**  
> "You can try."

Jake looked uncertain.

He picked up the piece.

It remained in his hand.

He stared at it again.

Then took a bite.

---

# 7. FIRST BITE

Jake stopped moving.

He chewed.

Swallowed.

Nothing passed through him.

The food remained inside his manifested body.

He stared forward.

Nobody spoke.

Jake took another bite.

Then another.

His eyes widened.

> **Jake:**  
> "...I ate it."

Nora, standing nearby, immediately smiled.

> **Nora:**  
> "Ja."

Jake looked at the cake.

He took another bite.

---

# 8. THE HUNGER

After several minutes, Jake stopped eating.

This was unusual.

Normally, food could never satisfy him.

He would continue wanting it because the hunger never disappeared.

This time, he simply put the cake down.

Neri noticed.

> **Neri:**  
> "More?"

Jake shook his head.

Neri paused.

> **Neri:**  
> "You're full?"

Jake looked confused.

He placed one hand against his stomach.

He waited.

Then:

> **Jake:**  
> "...I think so."

Silence.

Jake looked down.

> **Jake:**  
> "I'm not hungry."

Nobody immediately responded.

Jake repeated it.

> **Jake:**  
> "I'm not hungry."

---

# 9. MEDICAL SIGNIFICANCE

The statement was treated as medically significant.

Jake has experienced hunger continuously for approximately seventy-five years.

It was previously considered inseparable from his anomalous condition.

It was not.

The new manifestation state has apparently restored sufficient physical interaction to permit:

**consumption → digestion → satiety.**

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

Nora's blood appears to have altered the boundary between Jake's spiritual manifestation and physical reality.

This effect is distinct from resurrection.

Jake remains a deceased anomalous entity.

His body has not been restored.

His heart remains within the teddy bear.

His Weeping Angel condition remains unchanged.

He has simply gained the ability to physically interact with the world.

---

# 10. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake did not immediately celebrate.

He sat quietly.

His teddy bear remained around his shoulders.

He touched his stomach again.

Then looked at the remaining cake.

> **Jake:**  
> "It doesn't hurt."

Bao looked at him.

> **Bao:**  
> "What doesn't?"

Jake looked down.

> **Jake:**  
> "Being hungry."

His voice became quieter.

> **Jake:**  
> "I forgot what this felt like."

---

# 11. NORA

Nora sat beside him.

> **Nora:**  
> "You can eat now."

Jake nodded.

> **Jake:**  
> "Yes."

Nora smiled.

> **Nora:**  
> "Want more cake?"

Jake looked at the cake.

Then at Nora.

> **Jake:**  
> "...Yes."

Nora immediately gave him another piece.

Jake ate it.

This time, he laughed quietly.

It was brief.

Almost surprised.

But it was real.

---

Jake looked up.

> **Aeterna:**

Aeterna looked at the cake.

> "Indeed."

---

# 13. THE TEDDY BEAR

The teddy bear remains unchanged.

It continues to contain Jake's heart.

It continues beating.

It continues reflecting his emotional state.

However, for the first time, Jake's hunger is no longer constantly reflected in his behavior.

He no longer stares at other people's food with the same desperate longing.

He can eat his own.

He can stop when full.

He can return to food later.

He can experience taste.

He can physically hold a plate.

He can drink.

He can sit at a table and participate in something that ordinary residents have always taken for granted.

---

# 14. FIRST MEAL

C.O.R.E. subsequently recorded Jake's first complete meal.

It was modest.

Cake.

Bread.

Soup.

Fruit.

Milk.

Jake ate slowly.

Nobody rushed him.

Nobody told him how much to eat.

Nobody made a spectacle of the occasion.

At the end of the meal, Jake leaned back.

His teddy bear rested against the chair.

He looked tired.

But not hungry.

Bao asked:

> **Bao:**  
> "How do you feel?"

Jake thought for a moment.

Then:

> **Jake:**  
> "Full."

Bao smiled.

Jake looked down at his plate.

> **Jake:**  
> "I like this."

---

# 15. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The significance of this event extends beyond nutrition.

For approximately seventy-five years, Jake's hunger was a permanent reminder of how he died.

He died hungry.

He remained hungry.

He watched other people eat while being unable to participate.

Food became something he could see and smell but never truly possess.

That has now changed.

Jake can eat.

Jake can become full.

Jake can experience physical contact with the world.

This does **not** reverse his death.

It does **not** cure his Weeping Angel condition.

It does **not** restore his wings or connection to the Starlands.

It does not remove his grief.

It does not erase the years he spent alone.

But it removes one of the oldest physical sufferings attached to his existence.

---

# 16. WELFARE OBSERVATION

Jake was observed later that night sitting in the cafeteria.

He had a small plate beside him.

His teddy bear was on his back.

He was not hiding.

He was not staring at someone else's food.

He simply sat there.

After several minutes, Nora joined him.

Jake looked at her.

> **Jake:**  
> "I'm still not hungry."

Nora smiled.

> **Nora:**  
> "Good."

Jake nodded.

He took a sip of milk.

Then quietly added:

> **Jake:**  
> "I think I like being full."

Nora laughed.

Jake smiled faintly.

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

For seventy-five years, Jake was hungry.

Not occasionally.

Not metaphorically.

Always.

He died starving.

Then remained hungry as a ghost.

He watched people eat.

He smelled cake.

He was offered food he could never consume.

And eventually, he stopped expecting food to mean anything to him.

Today, Nora gave him one drop of blood.

And something changed.

Jake touched a table.

He moved a chair.

He picked up a piece of cake.

He ate it.

And then he stopped.

Because he was full.

For the first time in seventy-five years, Jake did not need more.

He simply sat there with his teddy bear and said:

> **"I'm not hungry."**

It may be one of the smallest sentences Jake has ever spoken.

It may also be one of the most important.

Because for once, something he has been missing since the day he died is no longer missing.

**Jake is full.**
