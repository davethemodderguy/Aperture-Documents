# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

SPAWN's recent transition from hostile occult organization to feline-worshipping cult did not last.

The organization has now undergone a second major doctrinal transformation.

This time, **Guiding Light personally intervened.**

Unlike Mischevious Light, whose influence primarily consisted of terrifying SPAWN into reconsidering its life choices, Guiding Light approached the organization directly and **guided its members away from cult-like behavior.**

She did not demand worship.

She did not establish a replacement religion.

She did not punish SPAWN.

She simply spoke with them.

SPAWN has consequently:

- abandoned cult practices,
    
- discontinued compulsory worship,
    
- ended ritualistic devotion toward cats,
    
- removed several unnecessary religious hierarchies,
    
- retained its existing spiritual beliefs in a non-cultic form,
    
- and adopted a substantially healthier understanding of its relationship with the supernatural.
    

The official internal assessment is:

> **“Guiding Light talked them out of being weird.”**

---

# 2. THE ARRIVAL

Guiding Light manifested inside SPAWN's central chapel.

No portal appeared.

No alarm triggered.

No ward reacted.

She simply appeared at the front of the room.

Approximately sixty SPAWN members were present.

They had been conducting a feline worship ceremony.

A cat was sitting on the altar.

Guiding Light looked at the congregation.

Then at the cat.

Then back at the congregation.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “What are you doing?”

Nobody answered.

One priest eventually raised his hand.

> **PRIEST:**  
> “Worshipping.”

Guiding Light tilted her head.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Why?”

Silence.

The priest looked at the cat.

The cat looked back.

> **PRIEST:**  
> “Mischevious told us to.”

Guiding Light sighed.

---

# 3. GUIDING LIGHT'S FIRST LESSON

Guiding Light sat down among them.

She did not stand above the congregation.

She did not demand obedience.

She simply began explaining.

She explained that:

- fear is not faith,
    
- power does not automatically equal moral authority,
    
- worship is not a substitute for understanding,
    
- supernatural beings should not automatically be treated as gods,
    
- and blindly following an entity because it is powerful is not wisdom.
    

Then she addressed the cat.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “And that is just a cat.”

The cat yawned.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “A very nice cat.”

The congregation nodded.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “But still a cat.”

---

# 4. THE SECOND LESSON

Guiding Light explained that the supernatural world was substantially larger and more complicated than SPAWN's previous doctrine had assumed.

She did not provide them with forbidden knowledge.

She did not reveal the full nature of the Architects.

She instead taught them how to approach anomalous phenomena responsibly.

Her central principle was recorded as:

> **“You don't have to understand everything to respect it.”**

This statement was later adopted as a formal SPAWN doctrinal principle.

# 6. THE MOST IMPORTANT LESSON

Guiding Light spent several hours speaking with SPAWN leadership.

Her primary concern was not their beliefs.

It was their **behavior.**

She explained that their fear of Aperture, the Architects, and other supernatural entities had gradually caused them to abandon independent judgment.

SPAWN had gone from:

> **“We must destroy Aperture.”**

to:

> **“We must worship Mischevious.”**

to:

> **“We must worship cats.”**

Guiding Light pointed out that none of these represented genuine understanding.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “You don't have to replace one extreme with another.”

That sentence became the foundation of the reform.

# 8. DOCTRINAL REFORM

SPAWN's revised doctrine now emphasizes:

### 1. Respect over worship

Supernatural entities should be treated with respect without automatically being deified.

### 2. Understanding over fear

Unknown phenomena should be studied before being assigned religious meaning.

### 3. Free will

Personnel are not required to surrender their judgment to supernatural beings.

### 4. Non-aggression

Entities and organizations that are not actively threatening SPAWN should not automatically be targeted.

### 5. Responsible anomalous interaction

Rituals and supernatural practices must have clearly understood purposes and consequences.

### 6. Cats

Cats are permitted to remain cats.

This final point was personally requested by Guiding Light.

# 13. CURRENT SPAWN STATUS

SPAWN is no longer considered a cult by C.O.R.E.

Its personnel continue to practice their original spiritual traditions, but these traditions are now explicitly separated from compulsory devotion and blind obedience.

Their relationship with supernatural entities has fundamentally changed.

They no longer ask:

> **“How do we destroy it?”**

or:

> **“How do we worship it?”**

> **“What is it?”**

> **“What does it want?”**

> **“Is it dangerous?”**

> **“How should we interact with it responsibly?”**

Guiding Light considers this sufficient progress.
