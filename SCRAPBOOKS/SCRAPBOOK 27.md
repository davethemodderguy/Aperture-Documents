# **ADDENDUM 03 – UNARMED COMBAT**

Although most personnel associate Senior Manager Norma Lockwood with her Juggernaut Armor and belt-fed machine gun, tactical evaluations note that she remains highly proficient in unarmed combat.

Her philosophy is simple:

> **"If I don't need the weapon, I won't use it."**

---

# **FIGHTING STYLE**

Norma favors a practical, disciplined fighting style emphasizing:

- Powerful straight punches.
    
- Clinch control.
    
- Throws and takedowns.
    
- Joint manipulation for restraint.
    
- Efficient footwork.
    
- Minimal wasted movement.
    

Unlike Remielle (Cinder), whose close-quarters combat becomes increasingly aggressive under pressure, Norma maintains her composure throughout an engagement.

Every strike is deliberate.

Every movement has a purpose.

---

# **PHYSICAL STRENGTH**

Years of wearing and operating the Juggernaut Armor, combined with regular physical conditioning, have given Norma considerable natural strength.

Even without her armor, she is capable of:

- Moving heavy equipment unaided.
    
- Carrying her unloaded **35 kg machine gun** comfortably.
    
- Assisting Engineering with lifting machinery.
    
- Sparring with trained operators without difficulty.
    

---

# **TRAINING MATCHES**

Norma regularly participates in friendly sparring sessions with KCA personnel.

She approaches every match as an opportunity to teach rather than dominate.

A common sentence heard in the training room is:

> "Again."

> "This time, keep your guard up."
