# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake's cardiac manifestation has remained irregular for an extended period.

The heartbeat originates from the teddy bear carried by Jake.

It has repeatedly exhibited:

- irregular rhythm;
    
- brief pauses;
    
- sudden acceleration;
    
- periods of weak, barely audible beats;
    
- occasional rapid sequences followed by prolonged pauses.
    

Jake himself has become increasingly quiet.

He appears frightened.

He frequently holds the teddy bear against his chest even though the bear already contains his heart.

When asked whether he is in pain, Jake has occasionally nodded.

When asked whether he is scared, he has also nodded.

He has not been able to explain exactly what he is afraid of.

Recent observations indicate that his distress may be connected to a memory he had previously been unable to describe.

A memory of the **Starlands.**

---

# 2. THE CURSE

Further review of Jake's anomalous physiology has clarified an important distinction.

Jake's heart being contained within the teddy bear is not merely an unusual manifestation.

It is part of the **curse associated with his death.**

The teddy bear became the vessel containing Jake's heart after his death.

The separation was not voluntary.

It was not something Jake chose.

It is not something that can simply be medically corrected.

The bear is therefore simultaneously:

> **Jake's heart.**

and

> **a physical manifestation of the curse that followed his death.**

The curse has remained with him for decades.

---

# 3. THE YEARS AFTER DEATH

Jake did not immediately become the resident currently known to SAFEHOUSE personnel.

Following his death from starvation, his spirit remained tied to the location where his body had died.

He could not simply leave.

He remained near his body.

For years.

Then decades.

He wandered around the place where he had died, unable to understand why he was still there and unable to properly move on.

He had his teddy bear.

He had his memories.

And he had his body lying somewhere nearby.

The distinction between the two became increasingly painful.

Jake was dead.

His body was dead.

But his spirit continued to exist.

---

# 4. THE CRYO CHAMBER

At some point during the Blacksite's history, Jake's physical remains were discovered.

Rather than being respectfully disposed of, his body was transferred into an old cryogenic chamber.

The reasons for the transfer remain under investigation.

The important fact is that Jake's body was subsequently preserved there for an extended period.

Jake remained spiritually connected to the location.

He could see his body.

He could not interact with it.

He could not restore it.

He could not enter it.

He could not leave the area simply because the body was preserved elsewhere.

The result was effectively a prolonged period in which Jake existed between:

**his body,**

**his teddy bear,**

and

**the place where he died.**

---

# 5. THE STARLANDS MEMORY

Jake has recently begun describing something else.

The Starlands.

Previously, Jake could not provide a coherent account of having been there.

Recent conversations indicate that he remembers it.

Not clearly.

Not completely.

But enough.

He remembers light.

He remembers being somewhere that felt different from the Blacksite.

He remembers looking upward.

He remembers that he was not alone.

And most importantly:

> **He remembers being there before being sent back.**

---

# 6. JAKE'S ACCOUNT

Jake was sitting beneath a table when Neri found him.

His teddy bear was held tightly against his chest.

The heartbeat was irregular.

**THUMP.**

**...**

**THUMP-THUMP.**

**...**

**THUMP.**

Neri sat beside him.

> **Neri:**  
> "Jake?"

He looked at her.

> **Neri:**  
> "What's wrong?"

Jake was silent for several seconds.

Then:

> **Jake:**  
> "I remember."

Neri waited.

> **Jake:**  
> "The Starlands."

His voice was barely audible.

> **Neri:**  
> "You remember being there?"

Jake nodded.

> **Jake:**  
> "I was there."

Pause.

> **Jake:**  
> "Then I came back."

Neri looked at him.

> **Neri:**  
> "Back here?"

Jake nodded again.

He looked down at the bear.

> **Jake:**  
> "Back to where I died."

---

# 7. WHAT JAKE REMEMBERS

Jake cannot currently provide a complete chronological account.

His memories appear fragmented.

He remembers being surrounded by light.

He remembers the sensation of being somewhere peaceful.

He remembers that his body was not hurting.

He remembers seeing something that he cannot adequately describe.

He remembers feeling as though he had finally arrived somewhere he was supposed to be.

Then:

> **He was sent back.**

Jake cannot identify who sent him back.

He does not describe the event as violent.

He describes it as confusing.

# 8. THE HEARTBEAT

The cardiac rhythm became significantly more irregular while Jake was describing the memory.

Personnel initially assumed this was simply an emotional response.

However, the pattern has persisted for several days.

The bear's heartbeat now changes noticeably whenever Jake discusses:

- the Starlands;
    
- his death;
    
- his body;
    
- the cryogenic chamber;
    
- his mother;
    
- being sent back;
    
- or the years he spent near his corpse.
    

Jake does not always consciously react.

The bear does.

This reinforces the existing assessment that the teddy bear is not merely carrying Jake's heart.

It is carrying a substantial portion of his anomalous existence.

---

# 9. THE MOST DISTURBING POSSIBILITY

The curse may explain why Jake remained tied to his body for so long.

It may also explain why his eventual separation from the location was incomplete.

Jake's spirit did not simply die and move on.

He remained.

He waited.

He wandered.

He watched the world around the place where he died.

And eventually, his body was placed into cryogenic storage.

The body survived physically.

Jake survived spiritually.

Neither was truly free.

---

# 10. JAKE AND THE CRYO CHAMBER

When the cryogenic remains were discovered, Jake was eventually brought to see them.

He recognized himself immediately.

He did not approach.

He simply stood several meters away.

His bear's heartbeat became extremely slow.

> **Jake:**  
> "That's me."

No one corrected him.

Jake stared at the chamber.

> **Jake:**  
> "I stayed here."

Pause.

> **Jake:**  
> "For a long time."

His voice became quieter.

> **Jake:**  
> "I didn't know where else to go."

---

# 11. CURRENT FEAR

Jake has not explicitly stated that he is afraid of returning to the Starlands.

He has not explicitly stated that he wants to return either.

His fear appears to be centered on something simpler.

**Being sent somewhere he cannot understand.**

He already experienced one transition he cannot explain:

Death.

Then another:

The Starlands.

Then another:

Being returned to the place where he died.

And finally:

Decades of remaining there.

Jake's mind has never developed a stable understanding of where he belongs.

---

# 14. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Jake's current condition is not consistent with a simple grief reaction.

His grief remains significant.

His mother's death remains a major emotional wound.

However, the newly recovered Starlands memories introduce another unresolved component:

**Jake remembers having briefly reached somewhere beyond the place of his death.**

He remembers being there.

He remembers being returned.

And he remembers spending years afterward near the remains of the body he could no longer inhabit.

The teddy bear remains the physical vessel through which his heart continues to manifest.

Its separation from Jake remains potentially fatal.

The curse therefore cannot be treated as an incidental feature of his existence.

It is fundamental to it.

---

# 15. WELFARE DIRECTIVE

Personnel should not tell Jake:

> "You're going back."

or:

> "You're staying here forever."

Neither statement can currently be established.

Personnel should also not speculate about whether Jake is "supposed" to be in the Starlands.

Do not promise him passage.

Do not promise him release.

Do not promise him return.

If Jake asks where he belongs, the appropriate response is:

> **"You're here with us right now."**

That is something personnel can actually establish.

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake spent years beside a dead body.

He carried a teddy bear that contained the heart he no longer had in his chest.

Someone eventually found his body and put it into a cryogenic chamber.

Jake remained.

He waited.

And somewhere in the fractured pieces of his memory, there is another place.

The Starlands.

A place he remembers reaching.

A place he remembers leaving.

A place he cannot explain.

And now, every time he thinks about it, the heart inside his teddy bear stutters.

**THUMP.**

...

**THUMP. THUMP.**

...

**THUMP.**

Jake holds the bear tighter.

He is scared.

He is sad.

And for perhaps the first time, the question troubling him is not:

> **"Where is my mother?"**

It is:

> **"Where am I supposed to be?"**

C.O.R.E. has no answer yet.

For now, the only answer that matters is considerably simpler.

Jake is here.

His bear is with him.

And nobody is taking it away again.
