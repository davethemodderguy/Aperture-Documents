# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – RESEARCH MEMORANDUM**

### **SUBJECT: ELDER MART IDENTIFICATION PROGRAM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

After multiple years of observation, interviews, tracking attempts, photographic analysis, Ether scans, behavioral studies, and exactly zero lasting success...

The C.O.R.E. Research Division has officially terminated the **Elder Mart Identification Program**.

Official reason:

> **Insufficient probability of meaningful progress.**

Unofficial reason:

> **They gave up.**

---

# **PROJECT HISTORY**

The project attempted to:

- Continuously identify the five Elder Marts.
    
- Track their movements.
    
- Document individual behavior.
    
- Distinguish them from the rest of the colony.
    

Results:

- Camera Mart identified.
    
- KCA Ambassador identified.
    
- Blacksite Ambassador identified.
    
- Two Elder Marts confirmed to exist.
    
- Two Elder Marts successfully identified...
    

Exactly never.

---

# **FINAL CONCLUSION**

Research now accepts the following facts:

- There are exactly five Elder Marts.
    
- Three are positively identified.
    
- Two continue living anonymously among the colonies.
    
- Both remaining Elders can speak fluent English.
    
- If either chooses to reveal themselves, researchers will know immediately.
    

Until then...

There is nothing left to investigate.

---

# **OFFICIAL POLICY**

Personnel are no longer expected to identify the remaining Elder Marts.

If a Mart unexpectedly begins speaking fluent English and is not one of the three known Elders...

Please notify C.O.R.E.

Otherwise...

Treat it like every other Mart.
