### **POST-INCIDENT / PERSONNEL ARCHIVE**

# **ORPHIE — ANOTHER BIRTHDAY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Bereavement / Personnel Welfare  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / Lumi / SAFEHOUSE Residents  
**Location:** Sealed Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **ISOLATED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Another birthday arrived.

Nobody came.

No cake.

No decorations.

No birthday messages.

No one knocked on the sealed treehouse.

The Blacksite had continued moving forward.

And, eventually, so had everyone else.

---

# **THE SNOW**

Orphie left the treehouse.

There was fresh snow outside.

She gathered a handful.

Then another.

She sat beneath the trees and ate it quietly.

There was nothing celebratory about it.

It was simply something cold.

Something that required no preparation.

Something that asked nothing from her.

She eventually whispered:

> "...Happy birthday."

There was nobody around to hear it.

---

# **THE PREVIOUS BIRTHDAY**

Orphie remembered the previous year.

She had still had Corin.

Her sister had still been there.

The treehouse had still felt like a home.

There had been an opportunity to celebrate.

Orphie hadn't shown up.

At the time, she had thought there would always be another birthday.

Another opportunity.

Another year.

She had been wrong.

---

# **THE REGRET**

Orphie looked down at the snow in her hands.

> "...I should've gone."

She remembered Corin waiting.

The possibility of Corin smiling.

A cake.

A stupid birthday song.

A photograph that never existed.

A normal memory that she had simply decided wasn't important enough to attend.

> "...I should've been there."

Her voice became quieter.

> "...You would've wanted me there."

---

# **CORIN**

Orphie had spent so long telling herself that Corin's death wasn't entirely her fault.

And that was true.

Corin had been struggling long before Orphie could have understood the extent of it.

There had been circumstances beyond Orphie's control.

There had been other failures.

Other wounds.

Other choices.

But Orphie could no longer escape one fact:

She had hurt Corin.

She had rejected her when she was vulnerable.

She had contributed to the isolation that preceded her death.

She could not undo that.

So, in her own mind, Corin's blood was on her hands.

---

# **THE MEMORY**

Orphie closed her eyes.

She remembered Corin's voice.

> **"I like sunflowers."**

Then her own response:

> **"A flower can't bring hope."**

She remembered laughing.

She remembered Corin smiling.

She remembered the sunflower field.

Then the dead field.

Then the letter.

> **"Thanks for proving it to me."**

Orphie opened her eyes.

Snow covered her hands.

---

# **THE BLACKSITE**

Life continued.

The SAFEHOUSE remained active.

Meals were prepared.

People talked.

Marts returned to their usual routines.

Work continued.

New memories replaced old ones.

Corin's absence gradually became less frequently mentioned.

Even the people who had once cried for her eventually had other things to worry about.

---

# **STAR**

Star had been the last one to refuse to let Orphie be forgotten.

She had wanted to visit.

She had wanted to help.

Everyone had stopped her.

Eventually, even Star stopped asking.

Time passed.

The grief became quieter.

Star still remembered Corin.

She still remembered Orphie.

But she no longer stood at the window looking toward the forest.

She had finally moved on.

Not because she stopped caring.

Because life kept demanding that she continue living.

---

# **THE FORGOTTEN BIRTHDAY**

Nobody at the Blacksite knew it was Orphie's birthday.

There was no notification.

No celebration.

No cake.

No one realized that somewhere beyond the perimeter, Orphie was sitting alone in the snow.

The day passed normally for everyone else.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie eventually returned to the treehouse.

She looked at the dead sunflower pot.

The tag was still attached.

> **"For my girls."**

She touched it.

> "...Sorry, Mom."

She looked at the empty chair.

> "...Sorry, Corin."

Then she sat on the floor.

The same floor she had chosen when she wanted to stay away from Corin.

Now there was nobody left to stay away from.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie had once believed being forgotten would be freedom.

Now she understood what it actually meant.

No one came.

No one remembered.

No one celebrated.

The Blacksite continued without her.

Even Star had eventually moved forward.

Orphie sat alone in the sealed treehouse on her birthday, surrounded by the remnants of a life she had once wanted to escape.

She looked at the dead sunflower.

Then at the empty chair.

Then at the snow melting in her hands.

> **"...I would've gone this time."**

There was no answer.

There would never be another birthday with Corin beside her.
