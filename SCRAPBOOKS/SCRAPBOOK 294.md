# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – ANOMALOUS SPECIES ADDENDUM III**

### **DESIGNATION: M.A.R.T. BLOBS – SELF-MAINTENANCE & HAT STABILIZATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Research Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Long-term observation has revealed additional aspects of Mart biology, explaining both their unusual cleanliness and the critical importance of their miniature top hats.

The findings have resulted in updated handling procedures for all personnel.

---

# **SELF-CLEANING**

As Marts travel throughout the Blacksite, they naturally collect:

- Dust.
    
- Dirt.
    
- Loose fibers.
    
- Fine debris.
    

Rather than allowing these contaminants to accumulate, a Mart periodically performs a self-cleaning cycle.

The process is simple.

The Mart moves to a convenient source of water, lowers itself into it, and intentionally dissolves into an ordinary-looking puddle.

After several seconds, provided its tiny top hat remains nearby, the Mart reforms completely clean.

The absorbed dust and debris remain behind in the water.

Janitorial Services has noted that this unintentionally makes Marts surprisingly effective at removing fine dust from offices and hallways.

---

# **THE TOP HAT**

Research has determined that the miniature top hat is more than a distinctive feature.

It functions as the Mart's primary Ether anchor.

While the exact mechanism remains unknown, the hat maintains the magical pattern that defines an individual Mart.

During normal self-cleaning, the hat remains beside the dissolved body.

Once reformation is complete, the hat is worn again as if nothing happened.

---

# **ANCHOR FAILURE**

If a Mart dissolves while separated from its top hat, the reformation process cannot complete correctly.

Without its anchor, the Mart's Ether pattern rapidly destabilizes.

Rather than reforming, the stored magical energy disperses violently from within the dissolving body, ending the organism's life.

For this reason, personnel are reminded once again:

> **Do not remove a Mart's hat.**

Even a seemingly harmless prank can have fatal consequences if performed at the wrong time.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

Marts are naturally optimistic.

They readily share.

They rarely become frightened.

They do not appear to worry about their own wellbeing.

Most remarkably, they possess almost no innate concept of death as something permanent or tragic.

To them, existence is a cycle.

Their common farewell reflects this belief.

> "Goodbye, we'll meet again."

And...

> "We'll be back."

---

# **EXCEPTION**

Behavioral researchers documented one unprecedented event.

A group of Marts witnessed another Mart perish following catastrophic anchor failure.

Unlike the peaceful dissolution associated with their natural life cycle, this death ended with no reformation.

The nearby Marts remained completely silent.

For nearly a minute.

Several cautiously approached the fallen top hat.

One gently nudged it.

Nothing happened.

Eventually, one quietly said:

> "…No."

Another looked around before asking:

> "We'll... be back?"

There was no response.

Research noted that this was the first recorded instance of Marts appearing to understand that not every ending leads to another beginning.

The colony resumed normal activity later that day, but personnel observed an unusual behavior for several days afterward.

Many Marts began instinctively checking that one another's hats were still in place.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Mart Care Guidelines have been revised.

Personnel are instructed to allow Marts to complete their self-cleaning cycles undisturbed and to ensure their top hats remain nearby throughout the process.

Though cheerful and seemingly fearless, the Marts are now recognized as depending on a delicate magical anchor for their continued existence.

A tiny black top hat may not look important.

To a Mart, it is everything.
