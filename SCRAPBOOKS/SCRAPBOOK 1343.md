# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Phoenix Raffedella's temporary residence at the SAFEHOUSE has continued without major incident.

Shortly after her relocation, **Dialyn**, KCA Customer Support, arrived to check on Phoenix and establish a temporary communication point with the KCA.

The visit also resulted in an unexpected discovery.

Phoenix's medical records were reviewed by Neko.

Neko had previously been informed that Phoenix possessed several unusual medical conditions.

He had not, however, reviewed the complete record.

He has now reviewed the complete record.

His reaction can be summarized as:

> **“What the hell happened to you?”**

Phoenix's response:

> **“A lot.”**

# 3. PHOENIX'S SLEEP CONDITION

Approximately forty minutes into the visit, Phoenix suddenly stopped typing.

Her head lowered.

Her hand remained on the keyboard.

Her eyes closed.

Dialyn noticed.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Phoenix?”

No response.

Phoenix had fallen asleep.

Instantly.

Without warning.

Dialyn looked at Belle.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Did she just—”

> **BELLE:**  
> “Yes.”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Again?”

> **BELLE:**  
> “Apparently.”

Phoenix remained asleep for several minutes.

Then she abruptly woke.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “What happened?”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “You fell asleep.”

Phoenix looked at the clock.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Oh.”

She resumed typing.

Dialyn stared.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “You can't voluntarily sleep, can you?”

Phoenix shook her head.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Not reliably.”

> **DIALYN:**

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Yes.”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Whenever?”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Whenever my body decides.”

Dialyn looked deeply unimpressed with the concept.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “That's horrible.”

Phoenix shrugged.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “I've gotten used to it.”

# 5. TRANSCENDENCE RECORD

Neko reached the T.O.P.S. section.

He read the description of Phoenix's condition.

Her body could die.

If her vital signs completely failed, her body would automatically draw Ether around itself.

The process could be accelerated through adrenaline.

The resulting **Trancendense** state restored her life while retaining the physical consequences of the injury, with pain severely numbed.

Her body did not age.

Neko read the paragraph twice.

Then a third time.

He looked at Phoenix.

> **NEKO:**  
> “So you're immortal.”

Phoenix shook her head.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Not exactly.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “You die.”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Yes.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “Then your body revives you.”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Yes.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “That's immortality.”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “It's more like being forcibly denied a permanent death.”

Neko stared at her.

> **NEKO:**  
> “That's worse.”

Phoenix shrugged.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Sometimes.”

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Phoenix's arrival has introduced an unusual medical situation into the SAFEHOUSE.

She is highly medically knowledgeable.

She is also medically complicated enough that Neko required several minutes of silence after reading her file.

Her most immediately noticeable condition is her inability to voluntarily maintain a conventional sleep schedule.

Phoenix can become exhausted.

She cannot reliably choose when to sleep.

Her body may simply decide:

> **Now.**

And shut down.

This can happen while working.

While talking.

While reading.

While sitting at a table.

Potentially anywhere.

Phoenix has adapted to this reality.

The SAFEHOUSE will need to do the same.
