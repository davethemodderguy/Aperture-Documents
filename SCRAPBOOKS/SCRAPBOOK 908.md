# BLACKSITE HISTORICAL RECORD

## THE DAY NEKO LOST

**Classification:** BLACKSITE INTERNAL  
**Record Type:** Historical / Scientific Achievement  
**Subject:** Neri  
**Secondary Subject:** Neko  
**Field:** Chemistry  
**Status:** CONFIRMED  
**Historical Significance:** **EXTREMELY HIGH**

---

# 1. SUMMARY

For the first time in recorded Blacksite history, an individual has demonstrated superior intelligence to **Neko** within a specialized field in which Neko was previously considered effectively unmatched.

That individual is:

> **Neri.**

The field is:

> **Chemistry.**

Neri did not merely perform well.

She **outperformed Neko.**

The distinction is important.

Neko remains substantially more knowledgeable across engineering, technology, archival systems, anomalous research, and numerous other disciplines.

However, within the specific field of chemistry, Neri demonstrated a level of intuitive understanding and problem-solving ability that exceeded Neko's.

The result has been formally recognized as the first documented case of a SAFEHOUSE/Blacksite resident surpassing Neko in a field other than the already-established exception of **Miss Neko**.

---

# 2. HISTORICAL CONTEXT

Neko has historically occupied an unusual position within Blacksite intellectual hierarchy.

He is exceptionally capable across numerous disciplines, including:

- Engineering
    
- Computer systems
    
- Anomalous technology
    
- Materials research
    
- Archives
    
- Security systems
    
- Dimensional technology
    
- Experimental systems
    
- Applied sciences
    

Neko's reputation is such that staff commonly assume that if a technical problem cannot be solved by him, it probably cannot be solved normally.

There is, however, one established exception.

### Miss Neko

Miss Neko has always been capable of matching or exceeding Neko in certain areas.

This is not considered particularly surprising by either party.

Consequently, the unofficial historical rule became:

> **"Neko is the smartest person here, except when Miss Neko is involved."**

That rule is now obsolete.

---

# 3. THE CHEMISTRY SESSION

The event occurred during a routine laboratory session involving Neko and Neri.

Neri had been provided with a series of chemistry problems intended to determine the extent of her knowledge.

The exercise quickly stopped being an assessment of Neri.

It became an assessment of Neko.

Neko presented increasingly complicated problems.

Neri solved them.

Neko increased the complexity.

Neri continued solving them.

Eventually, Neri began identifying flaws in Neko's assumptions.

---

# 4. THE FIRST WARNING

Neko presented Neri with an unfamiliar compound and asked her to predict its behavior under controlled conditions.

Neri examined the information.

She gave her answer.

Neko checked the expected result.

Correct.

A second problem was presented.

Correct.

A third.

Correct.

Neko modified the conditions.

Neri adjusted her prediction.

Correct again.

Neko reportedly became increasingly interested.

> **Neko:** "How do you know that?"

> **Neri:** "Because that's what it'll do."

> **Neko:** "Why?"

Neri explained.

Neko checked her reasoning.

It was correct.

---

# 5. THE TURNING POINT

The decisive moment occurred when Neko presented a problem involving an unusual reaction model.

Neri examined it.

She did not immediately answer.

Instead, she pointed at one of Neko's assumptions.

> **Neri:** "That part is wrong."

Silence.

Neko looked at the equation.

Then at Neri.

> **Neko:** "Which part?"

Neri pointed again.

She explained why the assumption would produce a different result under the stated conditions.

Neko recalculated.

Neri was correct.

This was the first recorded instance of Neri **correcting Neko's chemistry independently.**

---

# 6. NEKO ESCALATES

Neko, understandably, did not immediately concede.

He provided a more complicated problem.

Neri solved it.

Another.

Solved.

Another.

Solved.

Eventually Neko began deliberately introducing unfamiliar variables and anomalous material properties.

Neri continued adapting.

She did not appear to be recalling memorized answers.

She was **deriving them.**

This distinction became the primary reason the laboratory team took the results seriously.

---

# 7. THE FINAL TEST

Neko eventually presented Neri with a problem neither of them had previously encountered.

The problem involved an unfamiliar compound and incomplete experimental information.

Neri studied the available data.

She asked several questions.

Neko answered them.

Neri began writing.

After several minutes, she provided a predicted reaction pathway and expected outcomes.

Neko independently calculated the same problem.

Their results were compared.

Neri's model was more accurate.

Neko's model had made an incorrect assumption regarding the behavior of one component.

Neri had accounted for it.

---

# 8. THE RESULT

The laboratory record concludes:

> **Neri outperformed Neko in chemistry.**

Not because Neko lacks chemical knowledge.

Not because Neri possesses more total knowledge.

Not because Neko deliberately allowed her to win.

Rather, Neri demonstrated a particularly powerful form of chemical reasoning.

She appears capable of rapidly understanding unfamiliar chemical systems without requiring extensive prior exposure.

Her ability is especially pronounced when dealing with unusual or anomalous compounds.

---

# 9. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri did not understand why everyone was making such a big deal about it.

After the results were confirmed, Neko reportedly told her:

> **Neko:** "You beat me."

Neri looked confused.

> **Neri:** "At chemistry."

> **Neko:** "Yes."

> **Neri:** "...Okay."

She returned to her work.

Neko remained standing there for several seconds.

---

# 10. THE LABORATORY'S REACTION

The laboratory staff were considerably more impressed.

One Archivist reportedly asked:

> **"Did Neri just beat Neko?"**

Another answered:

> **"At chemistry."**

The first:

> **"Neko?"**

The second:

> **"Yes."**

A third Archivist reportedly entered the room specifically because someone had yelled:

> **"NEKO LOST."**

They were disappointed to discover that Neko had not lost a fight.

He had lost a chemistry problem.

They were nevertheless impressed.

---

# 11. NEKO'S REACTION

Neko did not appear offended.

Quite the opposite.

He was fascinated.

Neri had demonstrated something Neko values extremely highly:

**competence.**

Neko reportedly spent the remainder of the session asking Neri increasingly technical questions.

Neri answered them.

At one point Neko simply smiled.

> **Neko:** "You're really good at this."

Neri shrugged.

> **Neri:** "I told you."

---

# 12. MISS NEKO'S REACTION

Miss Neko was informed afterward.

Her response:

> **Miss Neko:** "Finally."

Neko objected.

> **Neko:** "Hey."

Miss Neko continued:

> **Miss Neko:** "You finally found someone."

Neko:

> **Neko:** "I still beat her in everything else."

Miss Neko:

> **Miss Neko:** "Probably."

Neri:

> **Neri:** "What's everything else?"

Miss Neko:

> **Miss Neko:** "Don't worry about it."

---

# 13. HISTORICAL RECLASSIFICATION

The Blacksite intellectual competency record has been amended.

### PREVIOUS

**Neko:**  
Highest demonstrated general technical/scientific competence.

**Exception:** Miss Neko.

### CURRENT

**Neko:**  
Highest demonstrated general technical/scientific competence across multiple disciplines.

**Miss Neko:**  
Established exception in relevant areas.

**Neri:**  
**Highest demonstrated chemical reasoning competency.**

This is the first time a third individual has been formally recognized as exceeding Neko within a defined intellectual specialty.

---

# 14. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

The Blacksite does **not** classify Neri as "smarter than Neko" in an overall sense.

That conclusion would be inaccurate.

Neko remains vastly more experienced and knowledgeable in numerous areas.

The finding is considerably more interesting:

> **Neri has a specialized intellectual strength that exceeds Neko's own specialization in that field.**

Neri does not need to be better at everything.

She is simply **exceptional at chemistry.**

---

# 15. WHY THIS MATTERS

The significance of this discovery extends beyond laboratory competition.

Neri's childhood records indicate that she was naturally talented in chemistry.

Her family dismissed this ability as useless.

The Blacksite has now demonstrated otherwise.

The same skill that was once described to her as having no value has become one of the strongest scientific abilities currently documented among its residents.

Neri did not become valuable because she became good at chemistry.

She was always valuable.

The Blacksite simply finally gave her somewhere that her ability could matter.

---

# 16. CURRENT LABORATORY POLICY

Neri is now permitted to participate in advanced chemistry research under appropriate supervision.

She is **not** to be treated as an experimental subject.

She is a resident assisting with scientific work.

Neko has also voluntarily designated her as a legitimate scientific collaborator in chemistry-related projects.

This designation reportedly made Neri extremely happy.

She has not openly admitted this.

---

# 17. HISTORICAL QUOTE

The following exchange was recorded after the laboratory session.

> **Archivist:** "So, Neko. How does it feel to finally meet someone smarter than you?"

> **Neko:** "She's smarter than me at chemistry."

> **Archivist:** "That's what I said."

> **Neko:** "No. There's an important distinction."

> **Archivist:** "Which is?"

> **Neko:** "She's terrifyingly good at chemistry."

Neri, from across the laboratory:

> **Neri:** "I can hear you."

Neko:

> **Neko:** "Good."

---

# 18. FINAL ASSESSMENT

For years, Neko occupied a unique position within Blacksite scientific culture.

Now there is a second name on the board.

**Neri.**

Not an experiment.

Not an asset.

Not 002.

Not expendable.

Just a ten-year-old Seal Thiren who happens to be **absurdly good at chemistry.**

And, for the first time in Blacksite history:

> **Neko has met his match.**

At chemistry.

Only chemistry.

For now.

**END OF HISTORICAL RECORD**
