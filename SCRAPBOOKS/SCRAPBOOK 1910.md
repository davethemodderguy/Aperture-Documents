## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Sora has developed a renewed interest in the moon.

This is not a new preference.

Before arriving at the Blacksite, Sora and Mia frequently spent evenings together on the farm where they were raised. The two children enjoyed watching the night sky, particularly the moon and the stars.

Stargazing was one of the few activities they could share without needing much conversation.

Following Mia's return, the two siblings had occasionally spoken about their childhood. However, Sora's recent recovery of his voice has made it possible for him to express some of these memories more directly.

A new association has now emerged.

Sora has become particularly interested in the familiar blue crescent moons associated with Guiding Light.

The connection appears to be both personal and symbolic.

He already loved moons because of his time with Mia. Now, Guiding Light's familiar blue crescent imagery has given him another reason to look up at the night sky.

---

## 2. HISTORICAL CONTEXT — THE FARM'S NIGHT SKY

Sora and Mia's childhood on the farm included periods when the two children would spend time outside at night, watching the stars.

They were particularly fond of the moon.

During a quiet conversation, Mia recalled their old routine.

**MIA:** Remember when we'd sit outside at night?

Sora nodded.

**SORA:** The farm.

**MIA:** Yes. We'd look at the stars.

**SORA:** And the moon.

**MIA:** You always looked for it first.

Sora smiled faintly.

**SORA:** It was big.

**MIA:** Sometimes.

**SORA:** Sometimes it was a little one.

Mia laughed.

**MIA:** A little one?

**SORA:** Crescent.

**MIA:** Oh. Right.

Sora looked toward the window.

**SORA:** I liked those.

Mia sat beside him.

**MIA:** Me too.

For a moment, neither spoke.

**SORA:** We should do it again.

Mia looked at him.

**MIA:** Stargazing?

Sora nodded.

**SORA:** Together.

**MIA:** I'd like that.

This exchange was particularly notable because Sora initiated the conversation himself.

He was not merely responding to questions about his past. He was expressing an interest in recreating something meaningful from it.

## 3. A NEW ASSOCIATION — GUIDING LIGHT

Sora's interest in the moon became more pronounced following his encounter with Guiding Light.

During a later visit, he noticed the familiar blue crescent moons associated with her.

He stared at them for some time.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Something caught your attention?

Sora nodded.

**SORA:** Moon.

Guiding Light looked toward the blue crescent imagery.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Ah. These?

Sora nodded again.

**SORA:** Blue moon.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Do you like them?

**SORA:** Yes.

Guiding Light smiled.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Why?

Sora took a moment to answer.

**SORA:** I liked moons before.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Before we met?

**SORA:** Farm. Me and Mia.

He gestured toward Mia, who was sitting nearby.

**SORA:** We watched stars.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** That sounds lovely.

**SORA:** But now...

He looked back at the blue crescent moons.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Now?

Sora smiled.

**SORA:** New moon.

Guiding Light tilted her head.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** A new reason to like them?

Sora nodded.

**SORA:** You.

There was a brief silence.

Guiding Light's expression softened.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Oh, sweetheart.

She gently patted his head.

**GUIDING LIGHT:** Then I'm honored.

Jake, who had been listening, immediately joined the conversation.

**JAKE:** WAIT! Does that mean you like Guiding Light because she's a moon?

**SORA:** No.

**JAKE:** Oh.

**SORA:** I like moons.

He paused.

**SORA:** And Guiding Light.

Jake considered this.

**JAKE:** Ohhh!

**MIA:** That makes more sense.

**JAKE:** I thought he was going to start collecting moons.

Sora looked at him.

**SORA:** Can I?

**JAKE:** WHAT?!

Mia laughed.

Guiding Light joined them.

For once, the conversation continued without Sora becoming overwhelmed or withdrawing.

## 4. OBSERVATION — THE BLUE CRESCENT

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE common room  
**Time:** 19:42

Sora was observed looking at a depiction of Guiding Light's familiar blue crescent moons.

He appeared unusually focused.

A staff member asked whether he wanted to return to his den.

**STAFF MEMBER:** Sora, are you finished here?

Sora shook his head.

**STAFF MEMBER:** Would you like to stay a little longer?

He nodded.

**SORA:** Pretty.

**STAFF MEMBER:** The moon?

**SORA:** Yes.

He pointed toward the blue crescent.

**SORA:** Guiding's moon.

The staff member smiled.

**STAFF MEMBER:** You like it that much?

Sora nodded.

**SORA:** Makes me happy.

This was recorded as a positive emotional response to a familiar visual association.

C.O.R.E. does not consider the attachment concerning. There is no indication that Sora has confused Guiding Light with the moon itself or developed an inability to distinguish symbolic imagery from reality.

He simply likes something that reminds him of someone who helped him.

### Welfare considerations

The activity is to remain informal.

No one should pressure Sora to discuss his childhood or recount traumatic experiences while stargazing. The purpose is to recreate a pleasant shared activity, not conduct a memory assessment.

The activity should also be arranged around the children's comfort and ordinary welfare needs.

## 6. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

This development is noteworthy for three reasons.

**First: Sora is reconnecting with a positive memory.**

His memories of the farm are not exclusively associated with the abuse and loss he experienced there. His time with Mia also included ordinary childhood experiences, such as watching the night sky.

The ability to recall and express these memories is encouraging.

**Second: Sora is forming new positive associations.**

Guiding Light's blue crescent moons have become associated with the restoration of his voice and the kindness she showed him.

This is a natural emotional association and should not be discouraged.

**Third: Sora is expressing preferences again.**

He is beginning to say what he likes, what he wants to do, and whom he wants to spend time with.

These are small but meaningful signs of renewed participation in everyday life.

His amnesia remains unresolved, and his recovery should not be overstated. Nevertheless, his ability to express happiness about something as ordinary as a moon is worth documenting.

## 7. CLOSING NOTE — TWO REASONS TO LOOK UP

Once, on a farm far away from the Blacksite, two little children would sit outside at night.

Sora and Mia.

They watched the stars.

They watched the moon.

They did not need much to make those evenings special. They had each other, the open sky, and the quiet of the countryside.

Those memories belong to a time they cannot return to.

But they can still look up.

Now Sora has another reason to like the moon.

A familiar blue crescent.

A person who listened when he could not speak, offered him something he desperately wanted, and gave him his voice back—even when the result did not appear immediately.

He likes the moon because of Mia.

He likes the blue crescent because of Guiding Light.

And perhaps, one evening soon, he will sit outside again with both of them.

Jake will probably talk too much.

Mia will probably laugh.

Guiding Light will probably smile.

And Sora might simply point upward and say:

"Look. Moon."

For a child who has lost so much of his past, it is a comforting thought that not every new memory has to be about what happened to him.

Some can simply be about something beautiful.

**He used to look at the moon because Mia was beside him. Now he has another reason to look up.**
