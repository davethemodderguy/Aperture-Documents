# SAFEHOUSE EQUIPMENT RECORD

## NERI — STARLIGHT KATANA

**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL  
**Equipment Type:** Light-Constructed Katana  
**Assigned User:** Neri  
**Issuing Party:** Curious Light  
**Current Status:** Active  
**Material Classification:** Architect-Light Construct  
**Known Symbol:** ◆✦ Rhombus Star  
**Administrative Approval:** **UNDER DISPUTE**

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

Following Curious Light's decision to provide Resident Neri with a katana, subsequent examination revealed that the weapon is **not a conventional physical blade**.

The katana possesses a **golden Starlight sheath**, while the blade itself is primarily composed of concentrated Light.

Very little conventional material appears to exist within the blade.

In practical terms, the weapon is closer to a **manifested Light construct shaped as a katana** than a metal sword.

This places it within the same broad material category as the substance composing much of an **Architect's body**.

The weapon also bears the Architect's identifying symbol:

> **◆✦ — The Rhombus Star**

The symbol is integrated into the weapon rather than being painted, engraved, or otherwise externally applied.

---

# 2. APPEARANCE

The weapon consists of two primary components.

### Sheath

The sheath is constructed from what appears to be **golden Starlight**.

It possesses a subtle luminous quality without producing enough light to interfere with normal vision.

The sheath remains physically stable despite the unusual nature of its material.

### Blade

The blade appears pale and luminous rather than metallic.

Under examination, conventional spectroscopy and material analysis provide inconsistent results.

The current best description is:

> **"Light behaving as matter."**

The blade retains the physical properties necessary to function as a sword while simultaneously behaving unlike conventional matter.

It does not appear to be merely coated in Light.

**The Light is the blade.**

---

# 3. ARCHITECT COMPOSITION

A particularly notable discovery was the blade's similarity to the material comprising most Architect bodies.

This explains several otherwise unusual characteristics.

Architect bodies are not constructed from ordinary biological matter in the conventional sense.

The Starlight Katana appears to utilize the same fundamental class of Light-based substance.

Consequently, the weapon should not be classified as:

- Steel
    
- Alloy
    
- Ether
    
- Plasma
    
- Conventional energy weaponry
    
- Standard anomalous material
    

Current classification:

> **ARCHITECT-LIGHT CONSTRUCT**

Further testing is suspended until Curious Light provides an explanation that does not begin with:

> "It's fine."

---

# 4. THE RHOMBUS STAR

The Architect's symbol is present on the weapon.

The mark resembles a **rhombus containing a star**, consistent with the symbol associated with the Architect.

It is visible on the weapon's construction and appears to exist as part of the Light itself.

Attempts to remove, obscure, or physically separate the symbol have been unsuccessful.

Neko's assessment was brief:

> **Neko:** "That's actually really cool."

Miss Neko disagreed with the timing of this observation.

---

# 5. NERI'S REACTION

Neri appears to have accepted the weapon without fully understanding its significance.

She reportedly spent considerable time examining the golden sheath.

After drawing the blade, she stared at the luminous edge.

> **Neri:** "...It's light."

Curious Light confirmed this.

> **Neri:** "Can I keep it?"

> **Curious Light:** "Yes."

Neri returned the blade to its sheath.

> **Neri:** "Okay."

She then continued with her day.

The equipment department has noted that Neri's reaction was considerably less dramatic than everyone else's.

---

# 6. GUIDING LIGHT'S REACTION

Guiding Light was **not pleased**.

The primary concern was not Neri's ability to use a sword.

The concern was that Curious Light had apparently handed a traumatized ten-year-old resident a weapon constructed from **Architect-level Light** without first consulting anyone responsible for resident welfare.

Guiding Light's position was summarized as:

> **"You cannot just give children things like this."**

Curious Light attempted to explain.

This did not improve the situation.

---

# 7. MISS NEKO'S REACTION

Miss Neko independently reached essentially the same conclusion.

Her objection was considerably more direct.

> **Miss Neko:** "Why did you give Neri an Architect-Light sword?"

> **Curious Light:** "She needed one."

> **Miss Neko:** "She had knives."

> **Curious Light:** "This one is better."

> **Miss Neko:** "THAT IS NOT THE POINT."

The argument continued.

Neri was observed standing several meters away, quietly holding the golden sheath.

She did not appear particularly concerned.

---

# 8. NEKO AND MISCHEVIOUS LIGHT

Neko, meanwhile, was having an excellent day.

Mischevious Light was equally pleased.

While Guiding Light and Miss Neko continued scolding Curious Light, the two quietly moved aside.

A brief whisper was recorded.

> **Neko:** "Worth it."

> **Mischevious Light:** "Worth it."

Both immediately returned to looking innocent.

Neither successfully looked innocent.

---

# 9. ADMINISTRATIVE CONCERNS

The following concerns have been raised:

### A. Neri's Medical Condition

The weapon does not increase Neri's stamina or prevent her spontaneous anemia.

Use must therefore remain subject to her existing medical limitations.

### B. Weapon Classification

The SAFEHOUSE equipment department does not currently possess an appropriate maintenance protocol for a blade that is partially equivalent to Architect anatomy.

### C. Containment

The weapon does not appear to require conventional containment.

This is arguably more concerning.

### D. Ownership

The weapon is now considered **Neri's personal property**.

Attempts to confiscate it without a legitimate safety reason are discouraged.

### E. Curious Light

Curious Light is temporarily prohibited from giving Neri additional anomalous weapons without consulting SAFEHOUSE administration.

This restriction was proposed immediately after the katana incident.

---

# 10. CURRENT STATUS

Neri has retained the Starlight Katana.

She has been permitted to carry it under normal SAFEHOUSE weapon-safety rules.

The golden sheath remains intact.

The Light blade remains stable.

The Rhombus Star remains visible.

Neri appears happy with her new weapon.

Neko is happy that Neri has a cool sword.

Mischevious Light is happy that this happened.

Guiding Light is still scolding Curious Light.

Miss Neko is still scolding Curious Light.

Curious Light has not yet demonstrated sufficient remorse.
