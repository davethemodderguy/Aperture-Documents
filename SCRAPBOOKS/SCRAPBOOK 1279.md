**SECONDARY SUBJECT:** Neri Tachyon  
**PROJECT:** Proposed Lever-Action Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon has submitted a request to Tachyon Industries for the development of another **Limited Merchandise** item.

Her proposal is simple:

> **A lever-action rifle.**

The request follows Nora's recent acquisition and field deployment of a Marlin Model 1894.

Nora has apparently become sufficiently fond of the platform that she would like Tachyon Industries to produce its own proprietary lever-action design.

Neri is currently considering the proposal.

She has not approved it.

She has also not rejected it.

This distinction has caused Nora to remain extremely persistent.

---

# THE REQUEST

Nora approached Neri while Neri was working in the Tachyon development area.

Neri was reviewing product specifications.

Nora placed a sheet of paper on the desk.

Neri looked at it.

The entire proposal consisted of:

> **LEVER ACTION**

Underneath it was a drawing of a rifle.

The drawing included:

- a lever;
    
- a scope;
    
- a tactical handguard;
    
- a stock;
    
- several unexplained attachments;
    
- and a shark wearing sunglasses.
    

Neri stared at the page.

Then at Nora.

> **NERI:**  
> “You want to make a lever-action?”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “Yes.”

> **NERI:**  
> “As Tachyon merchandise?”

Another nod.

> **NORA:**  
> “Limited.”

Neri looked back at the drawing.

> **NERI:**  
> “You already have one.”

> **NORA:**  
> “This one would be Tachyon.”

Neri paused.

> **NERI:**  
> “That's not a technical justification.”

Nora thought about it.

> **NORA:**  
> “It would be cool.”

Neri stopped reading.

---

# WHY NORA WANTS IT

Nora's reasoning appears to be largely aesthetic.

She enjoys the mechanical character of the lever-action platform.

She also believes the platform fits Tachyon Industries' merchandise identity.

Her proposal specifically emphasizes:

> **“Old style. New technology.”**

This appears to be the intended design philosophy.

Nora has also requested that the finished product retain the recognizable lever-action silhouette rather than simply becoming another modern rifle with a lever attached.

---

# NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri did not immediately reject the concept.

Instead, she began asking questions.

> **NERI:**  
> “Why would people buy it?”

Nora answered immediately:

> **NORA:**  
> “Because lever.”

Neri stared at her.

> **NERI:**  
> “That's not enough.”

Nora thought again.

> **NORA:**  
> “Because it's fun.”

Neri continued staring.

> **NORA:**  
> “And tacticool.”

A pause.

Neri looked back at the proposal.

> **NERI:**  
> “...That is slightly better.”

---

# PRELIMINARY DESIGN CONCEPT

No final specifications have been approved.

However, Neri has begun considering several broad characteristics.

The proposed Tachyon lever-action would likely feature:

- a proprietary Tachyon Industries receiver design;
    
- modern materials;
    
- modular external equipment;
    
- an intentionally compact overall profile;
    
- compatibility with the Tachyon product ecosystem;
    
- distinctive Tachyon visual markings;
    
- and a lever-action mechanism retained as a defining feature.
    

No production design currently exists.

No manufacturing authorization has been issued.

---

# NORA'S ADDITIONAL REQUEST

Nora has also requested one specific feature.

She wants the rifle to be capable of being **flick-cocked.**

Neri looked at her.

> **NERI:**  
> “That's not a technical specification.”

> **NORA:**  
> “It should be.”

Neri sighed.

> **NERI:**  
> “I'll write it down.”

Nora smiled.

---

# POTENTIAL PRODUCT NAME

Several names have been proposed.

Nora suggested:

> **TACHYON LEVER**

Neri rejected it immediately.

Nora suggested:

> **TACHYON 1894**

Neri rejected that as well.

Nora then wrote:

> **“BIG CLICKY”**

Neri crossed it out.

After several minutes, Neri wrote a temporary development designation:

> **TLR — Tachyon Lever Rifle**

Nora approved.

Neri has emphasized that this is **not** a final product name.

Nora has already started calling it:

> **“TLR.”**

---

# NERI'S CONSIDERATION

Neri has not yet committed to developing the rifle.

Her primary concern is differentiation.

Tachyon Industries already produces several specialized products.

A new limited item must therefore justify its existence rather than simply duplicate an existing weapon.

However, Neri has acknowledged one significant advantage:

The lever-action platform gives Tachyon Industries an opportunity to produce something that is visually and mechanically distinct from its other merchandise.

She has therefore moved the proposal from:

> **REJECTED**

to:

> **UNDER CONSIDERATION**

Nora immediately celebrated.

Neri clarified:

> **NERI:**  
> “That doesn't mean yes.”

Nora:

> **“I know.”**

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> “It means maybe.”

Neri:

> **“...Yes.”**

Nora walked away looking extremely pleased.

---

# ARCHIVIST NOTE

Neko was informed that Tachyon Industries may be developing another limited product.

He asked:

> **NEKO:**  
> “What is it?”

Neri:

> **“Lever-action rifle.”**

Neko paused.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Why?”

Nora, from across the room:

> **“Lever.”**

Neko closed his eyes.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Of course.”

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The proposed product remains in the conceptual stage.

Neri is evaluating whether the platform offers sufficient distinction from existing Tachyon merchandise.

Nora, meanwhile, appears to have already decided that it should exist.

Her reasoning remains remarkably consistent:

> **Lever.**

> **Tacticool.**

> **Tachyon.**

For the moment, the project remains:

### **PENDING NERI'S APPROVAL**

Nora has apparently interpreted this as:

### **“PROBABLY YES.”**

Neri has not corrected her.

Yet.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Nora wanted a lever-action rifle.

Neri asked why.

Nora said:

> **“Because it's cool.”**

Neri considered it.

And, for perhaps the first time in Tachyon Industries product development history, that answer was not immediately rejected.

The lever-action concept remains alive.

The shark has already started drawing prototypes.

Neri has already started thinking about them.

And somewhere in the Tachyon archive, a new folder now exists:

> **TLR — LEVER-ACTION CONCEPT**

**STATUS: MAYBE.**
