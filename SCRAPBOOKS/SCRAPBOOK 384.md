### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE — CULINARY OBSERVATION LOG**

# **COREY — NORMA'S BIRTHDAY CAKE**

**Classification:** Level 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE — Communal Kitchen  
**Subject:** Corey / Norma  
**Event:** Birthday Celebration

---

# **OVERVIEW**

For **Norma's birthday**, Corey prepared a cake entirely from scratch.

Unlike many of Corey's previous meals, this recipe did **not** come from her mother's cookbook.

The cake was an original recipe created by Corey herself.

The recipe had never been documented before the event.

---

# **THE RECIPE**

Corey designed the cake specifically for Norma.

Rather than adapting an existing recipe, she experimented with the ingredients until she was satisfied with the result.

The final recipe was written down in Corey's own handwriting.

It has since been added to her personal recipe collection.

The cookbook now contains a new page:

> **"Norma's Birthday Cake"**

---

# **PREPARATION**

Corey began preparing the cake early in the day.

She carefully measured each ingredient and repeatedly checked the consistency of the batter.

Unlike her usual cooking, Corey appeared unusually focused.

A Mart observing from the counter asked:

> "...What?"

Corey answered:

> "...Birthday."

The Mart immediately understood.

> "...Norma."

> "...Yes."

---

# **NO MANIFESTATION**

As with Corey's other cooking sessions, neither **Solaria** nor **Luna** manifested while she was preparing the cake.

Even though the cake was prepared during the day, Corey remained entirely herself.

No solar wings appeared.

No lunar field developed.

No transformation occurred.

Corey's concentration remained fixed on the cake.

---

# **THE DECORATION**

Corey personally decorated the cake.

She did not attempt anything excessively elaborate.

Instead, she focused on making it look carefully prepared.

The final cake was decorated with:

- Homemade frosting.
    
- A simple decorative pattern.
    
- A birthday message for Norma.
    

Written across the top:

> **HAPPY BIRTHDAY, NORMA!**

Corey also added a small detail that only Norma would immediately recognize.

---

# **THE SURPRISE**

Norma was unaware that Corey had been preparing the cake.

When she entered the communal living area, the lights were dimmed.

The Friendly Roommates were gathered around the table.

Marts were hiding nearby.

This was considerably difficult because Marts are not particularly good at hiding when hundreds of them are involved.

Norma looked around.

> "...What's happening?"

The lights turned on.

Everyone:

> **"HAPPY BIRTHDAY!"**

A group of Marts enthusiastically joined in:

> "...BIRTHDAY!"

Norma was visibly surprised.

---

# **COREY'S GIFT**

Corey brought the cake forward.

> "...I made this for you."

Norma looked at it.

Then at Corey.

> "...You made this yourself?"

Corey nodded.

> "...It's my own recipe."

Norma carefully examined the cake.

> "...You created the recipe?"

> "...Yeah."

Norma smiled.

> "...Thank you."

---

---

# **NORMA'S REACTION**

Norma was the last person to finish her slice.

She seemed to be analyzing the recipe as she ate.

Eventually, she looked toward Corey.

> "...You should write this down properly."

Corey laughed.

> "...I already did."

Norma smiled.

> "...Good."

She looked at the cookbook.

> "...Keep it."

---

# **THE NEW RECIPE**

After the celebration, Corey placed the handwritten recipe among the pages of her mother's cookbook.

It was not her mother's handwriting.

It was hers.

For the first time, the cookbook contained a recipe that belonged entirely to Corey.

The page was titled:

> **"Norma's Birthday Cake — Original Recipe"**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corey's mother's cookbook taught her how to cook.

Her mother's recipes gave her something to carry forward.

But Norma's birthday marked something different.

Corey created something of her own.

A recipe that did not come from the past.

A recipe made for someone she cared about.

And now, written among the recipes inherited from her mother, there is one page written entirely by Corey.

> **Her first original recipe.**
