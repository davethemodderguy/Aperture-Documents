**SUBJECT:** Neri — Accidental Fall While in Plush State  
**LOCATION:** Sector 4-C Cafeteria  
**DISCOVERY TIME:** 10:03

---

## Executive Summary

Neri was once again left on the cafeteria table while voluntarily occupying her plush form.

Unlike the previous occurrence, she did not remain on the tabletop.

At an undetermined time during the night, Neri lost her balance and fell from the table.

She landed flat on the cafeteria floor.

No injury occurred.

No distress signal was produced.

Neri simply remained where she had landed.

She was eventually discovered the following morning.

The incident would have been considerably more serious if the first person to encounter her had not noticed her in time.

That person was almost Jane Doe.

---

## Incident Reconstruction

At some point after cafeteria operations ended, Plushie Neri was left unattended on one of the tables.

Neri apparently attempted to reposition herself.

The available camera footage shows a small movement toward the table's edge.

A pause.

Another movement.

Then:

**plop.**

Neri fell approximately table height onto the floor.

She landed flat on her stomach.

She did not bounce particularly far.

She did not appear startled.

She did not attempt to immediately get back up.

Instead, she remained completely motionless.

---

## 02:18–09:59 — Floor Occupancy

Neri remained on the floor for several hours.

She occasionally moved one paw.

At approximately 03:07, she rolled onto her side.

At 04:12, she rolled back onto her stomach.

At 05:33, she briefly lifted her head.

At 05:34, she put it back down.

No other significant movement was recorded.

Neri was not unconscious.

She was simply comfortable enough with her current situation that she apparently saw no reason to correct it.

---

## 09:57 — Near Miss

Jane Doe entered the cafeteria.

She was carrying a coffee.

She walked toward the counter.

Plushie Neri was directly within her walking path.

Jane took one step.

Then another.

Her foot came within approximately several centimeters of Neri.

Jane stopped.

She looked down.

There was a pause.

> **JANE:** "...Neri?"

Neri lifted her head.

Jane stared at her.

Neri stared back.

> **JANE:** "Why are you on the floor?"

Neri remained silent.

Jane crouched down.

> **JANE:** "Did you fall?"

Neri gave a tiny nod.

> **JANE:** "Are you hurt?"

Neri shook her head.

> **JANE:** "Okay."

Jane carefully picked her up.

---

## Immediate Assessment

Neri displayed:

- no visible injury;
    
- no apparent pain response;
    
- no abnormal movement;
    
- no signs of acute distress;
    
- normal awareness.
    

Her plush-state impact resistance functioned as expected.

Jane nevertheless conducted a basic visual check before carrying her away.

> **JANE:** "You know, you're incredibly easy to step on when you're the size of a teddy bear."

Neri stared at her.

> **JANE:** "...That wasn't an insult."

Neri continued staring.

> **JANE:** "I know."

---

## 10:03 — Discovery Confirmed

Jane carried Neri toward the cafeteria seating area.

Alice entered shortly afterward.

She immediately noticed Neri.

> **ALICE:** "Neri!"

Alice approached.

> **ALICE:** "What happened?"

Jane pointed toward the floor.

> **JANE:** "She fell off the table."

Alice looked horrified.

> **ALICE:** "Again?"

Neri was still being held by Jane.

She gave a small shrug.

Alice carefully took her from Jane.

Neri immediately settled into Alice's arms.

---

## Security Review

The incident has resulted in a minor revision to cafeteria safety guidance.

### PREVIOUS GUIDANCE

> If Plushie Neri is left unattended on a table, she will probably remain on the table.

### UPDATED GUIDANCE

> **Do not assume this.**

Neri can move while in plush form.

Neri can also lose her balance.

A plushie capable of moving independently remains capable of falling off furniture.

---

## Additional Observation

The most concerning portion of this incident was not the fall.

It was the discovery delay.

Neri spent several hours on the cafeteria floor without anyone noticing.

She was unharmed, but the situation created an unnecessary pedestrian hazard.

Had Jane not looked down, Neri could have remained there considerably longer.

The possibility of accidental contact with a person's foot was therefore substantially higher than acceptable.

---

## Final Assessment

**Neri:** Uninjured.  
**Plush form:** Stable.  
**Medical intervention:** Not required.  
**Jane Doe:** Nearly stepped on a child.  
**Neri's response:** Apparently unconcerned.  
**Alice:** Now checking the floor before walking anywhere in the cafeteria.
