# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon possesses multiple outfits for different circumstances.

Her wardrobe currently includes dedicated configurations for:

- **Indoor / SAFEHOUSE use**
    
- **Outdoor use**
    
- **Formal appearances**
    
- **Tachyon Merch Store duties**
    
- **Agent Nora Tachyon**
    

Nora can change between these outfits remarkably quickly.

The process is not conventional.

She does not require:

- a changing room;
    
- assistance;
    
- luggage;
    
- visible clothing storage;
    
- or an extended interruption to whatever she is doing.
    

Instead:

> **White smoke surrounds Nora.**

The smoke completely obscures her for several seconds.

When it clears:

> **Nora is wearing a different outfit.**

---

# 2. STANDARD CHANGE SEQUENCE

The observed sequence is consistent.

### STEP 1

Nora decides she wants to change.

### STEP 2

A cloud of opaque white smoke rapidly surrounds her.

### STEP 3

Nora disappears completely from view.

### STEP 4

The smoke clears.

### STEP 5

Nora is standing in the requested outfit.

No transition between individual clothing items has been visually observed.

The change appears instantaneous from the perspective of an observer.

---

# 3. INDOOR OUTFIT

Nora's indoor configuration prioritizes comfort.

It is generally used within:

- SAFEHOUSE;
    
- her room;
    
- Neri's subspace;
    
- the Merch Store when no formal presentation is required;
    
- and other controlled indoor environments.
    

The outfit is noticeably more relaxed than her outdoor or agent configurations.

Nora is particularly likely to remain in this configuration when spending time with Neri, Alice, or Sky.

Sky has demonstrated no preference regarding clothing configuration.

It will perch on Nora regardless.

---

# 4. OUTDOOR OUTFIT

Nora's outdoor configuration is more practical.

It incorporates additional coverage and weather-appropriate clothing while retaining her recognizable personal aesthetic.

The white beret remains a frequent component.

The **navy-blue strap** and **golden moon medal** remain visible.

Her family journal remains clipped to her waist.

Sky frequently chooses the beret as a landing point.

This occasionally results in the following combination:

> **White beret**  
> **Golden moon medal**  
> **White falcon**  
> **Nora's long white hair**

C.O.R.E. considers this configuration highly recognizable.

---

# 5. CAPTAIN-COMMANDER PRESENTATION

Nora's more formal configuration continues to resemble a miniature military Captain-Commander.

Common visual elements include:

- white beret;
    
- navy-blue accents;
    
- formal clothing;
    
- genuine MSAC and Blacksite medals;
    
- family identification items;
    
- and her holstered **Deadeye** when authorized for the relevant activity.
    

This is not an official rank.

Nora simply happens to look like she has one.

She does not possess command authority by virtue of the outfit.

---

# 6. "AGENT NORA TACHYON"

The most unusual configuration is informally designated:

> **AGENT NORA TACHYON**

This outfit is specifically intended to make Nora look like an intelligence operative.

The overall presentation is substantially more serious than her normal clothing.

The change is especially noticeable when combined with Nora's range behavior.

Her usual energetic personality remains unchanged outside operational contexts.

However, the visual transition is dramatic.

One moment:

> **Nora Tachyon.**

The next:

> **Agent Nora Tachyon.**

---

# 7. FIRST OBSERVED CHANGE

The first documented spontaneous demonstration occurred in the Merch Store.

Nora was wearing her normal outfit.

She suddenly looked toward Corin.

> **NORA:**  
> “Wait.”

Corin stopped.

> **CORIN:**  
> “What?”

Nora stepped backward.

White smoke immediately surrounded her.

Corin stared.

The smoke cleared.

Nora was wearing her Agent configuration.

Corin remained silent for several seconds.

> **CORIN:**  
> “...Where did that come from?”

Nora adjusted her clothing.

> **NORA:**  
> “Agent.”

> **CORIN:**  
> “I gathered that.”

Sky landed on Nora's head.

Nora looked upward.

> **NORA:**  
> “Sky, you don't match.”

Sky remained there.

> **TECHNICIAN:**

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Nora's wardrobe is considerably more extensive than previously documented.

She possesses multiple clothing configurations and can transition between them through an anomalous white-smoke transformation.

The phenomenon appears:

- voluntary;
    
- rapid;
    
- harmless;
    
- repeatable;
    
- and entirely under Nora's control.
    

Most importantly, the outfits are not merely cosmetic variations.

They reflect different aspects of Nora's life.

**Indoor Nora** — comfortable little sister.

**Outdoor Nora** — adventurous child.

**Captain-Commander Nora** — decorated, formal, unmistakably Tachyon.

**Agent Nora Tachyon** — suddenly much more serious-looking than anyone expected.

And underneath all of them:

> **Still Nora.**
