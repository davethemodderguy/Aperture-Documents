### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / FEATHER FUNCTION REPORT**

# **CORIN — FLASH FEATHER DISCOVERY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **Feather-Based Flashbang Effect**  
**Status:** Confirmed / Mechanism Unknown

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's feathers have demonstrated an additional anomalous function.

They can apparently produce a **flashbang-like effect** upon impact.

The discovery was completely accidental.

No controlled experiment was being conducted.

No combat scenario was involved.

The entire incident began because Corin wanted to play darts.

---

# **THE DART GAME**

Several SAFEHOUSE residents were playing darts.

Corin decided to participate.

Instead of using conventional darts, she jokingly removed several feathers from her wings and used them as improvised darts.

Nobody expected anything unusual.

The first feather struck the dartboard.

There was a brief flash.

Then:

**BANG.**

The entire room was illuminated.

Every ordinary resident immediately lost their vision and became severely disoriented.

The effect lasted approximately:

> **40 seconds.**

---

# **THE RESULT**

The SAFEHOUSE effectively stopped functioning for forty seconds.

Residents were:

- Temporarily blinded.
    
- Disoriented.
    
- Unable to determine where the flash originated.
    
- Experiencing temporary ringing and sensory confusion.
    

Corin was equally confused.

She reportedly said:

> **"...Oops."**

---

# **EXCEPTIONS**

Not everyone was affected.

The following residents remained completely unaffected:

### **Lumi**

No observable visual or sensory impairment.

### **Astral**

Unaffected.

### **Niko**

Unaffected due to his ghostly nature.

### **Endmin**

Unaffected.

Her eyes are composed of Ether rather than conventional biological structures.

### **Belle**

Unaffected.

Approximately half of Belle's body is composed of Ether, apparently providing sufficient resistance to the flash effect.

---

# **THE FEATHER**

The most confusing aspect is that the feather itself did not appear to contain a conventional flashbang mechanism.

There were no chemical compounds.

No mechanical trigger.

No miniature device.

No conventional explosive.

The feather simply struck the target and produced the effect.

---

# **ETHER SIGNATURE**

C.O.R.E. detected an Ether discharge accompanying the flash.

However, it was **not identical to Corin's normal Ether explosion.**

The effect appears to involve a different configuration of Ether energy.

The current hypothesis is that the feather possesses a dormant anomalous property that can manifest under specific impact conditions.

Why?

Unknown.

---

# **THE TRIGGER**

The exact trigger remains unidentified.

Possible factors include:

- Impact velocity.
    
- Impact angle.
    
- Corin's intent.
    
- Emotional state.
    
- Feather age.
    
- Ether concentration.
    
- A combination of several factors.
    
- Something entirely unrelated.
    

Attempts to deliberately reproduce the exact first incident have produced inconsistent results.

---

# **IMPORTANT DISTINCTION**

Corin's feathers now have at least **three confirmed projectile functions**:

### **1. Throwing Knife**

Edge feathers can function as extremely sharp projectiles.

### **2. Ether Airburst Projectile**

Launched feathers can detonate and produce an Ether-infused airburst.

### **3. Flash Feather**

Certain impacts can trigger an intense flash accompanied by a disorienting effect.

The third function appears to be the least predictable.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The dartboard has been removed.

Neko has officially banned:

> **"Feather darts."**

Corin maintains that this is unfair.

Belle reportedly asked whether the game could be played again if everyone wore sunglasses.

The suggestion was rejected.

---

# **TESTING**

Controlled tests have since confirmed that the flash effect is real.

However, the exact conditions required to activate it remain unknown.

Some feathers produce the effect.

Others behave normally.

The same feather may behave differently depending on how it is thrown.

C.O.R.E. has therefore classified the phenomenon as:

> **Unpredictable Ether-Based Secondary Function.**

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin was fascinated by the discovery.

She initially wanted to figure out how to deliberately reproduce the effect.

After several unsuccessful attempts, she became frustrated.

Then she simply shrugged.

> **"I guess my feathers just do that now."**

C.O.R.E. has no objection to this assessment.

---

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's feathers were already capable of being knives and Ether-infused explosives.

Apparently, that was not enough.

During a completely harmless game of darts, Corin accidentally discovered that some of them can also function as **flashbangs**.

Nobody knows why.

Nobody knows exactly how.

And nobody is allowed to play feather darts anymore.

Corin is disappointed.

The SAFEHOUSE residents are relieved.

C.O.R.E. Engineering has added another line to Corin's growing anomalous profile:

> **"Do not assume a feather is harmless."**
