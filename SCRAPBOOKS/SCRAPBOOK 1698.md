# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Belle Merryweather and Linderverne "Endmin" Merryweather visited their mother, **Lucy Merryweather**, Warden of the Banlands.

They brought two SAFEHOUSE residents with them:

- **Jake**
    
- **Clover Luminare**
    

The visit was uneventful from a security perspective.

It was considerably more significant from Clover's perspective.

Clover has been a devoted admirer of Lucy Merryweather since childhood.

She had seen photographs.

She had heard stories.

She had read publicly available accounts concerning the Warden.

She had followed Lucy's appearances and reputation for years.

She had never expected to actually meet her.

During the visit, Lucy discovered this.

Clover discovered that Lucy was willing to sign an autograph.

The resulting reaction after returning to SAFEHOUSE has been classified as:

> **EXTREME FAN RESPONSE.**

The group arrived in the Banlands under authorized Merryweather escort.

Belle walked beside her younger sister.

# 4. THE AUTOGRAPH

The autograph occurred almost accidentally.

Clover had brought a small notebook.

She had originally intended to use it for notes.

Lucy noticed it.

> **LUCY:**  
> "Is that your notebook?"

Clover nodded.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Yes."

Lucy smiled.

> **LUCY:**  
> "Would you like me to sign it?"

Clover stopped breathing for approximately two seconds.

Belle immediately noticed.

> **BELLE:**  
> "Mom."

Lucy looked at her.

> **LUCY:**  
> "What?"

Belle pointed toward Clover.

Clover was staring at Lucy.

Completely motionless.

> **BELLE:**  
> "You broke her."

Endmin looked at Clover.

> **ENDMIN:**  
> "She appears to have stopped functioning."

Clover finally spoke.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "...Please."

Lucy took the notebook.

She signed her name.

Then added a short message.

She returned it.

Clover looked down.

The page contained:

> **"To Clover — Keep finding your own luck.  
> With affection, Lucy Merryweather."**

Clover stared at it.

Her eyes widened.

She carefully closed the notebook.

Then held it against her chest.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "...Thank you."

Lucy smiled.

> **LUCY:**  
> "You're very welcome."

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The visit was entirely successful.

Belle and Endmin were able to visit their mother.

Jake was introduced to Lucy Merryweather without incident.

Clover met someone she had admired since childhood.

Clover received an autograph.

No diplomatic complications occurred.

No Banlands incident occurred.

No anomalous escalation occurred.

The only significant post-visit event was Clover's sudden transition from:

> **Professional KCA-affiliated accountant**

to:

> **Extremely excited Lucy Merryweather fan.**

The autograph is currently being treated by Clover as a priceless personal possession.

She has reportedly checked the signature multiple times.

She has shown it to numerous residents.

She has discussed the exact circumstances of the autograph repeatedly.

She has not yet stopped smiling.

C.O.R.E. expects this condition to persist.

## CLOSING NOTE

Clover had admired the Warden of the Banlands since she was a child.

For years, Lucy Merryweather existed in her mind as a distant figure:

A powerful Warden.

A political authority.

A legendary name.

Someone seen in documents and stories.

Today, Lucy simply looked at Clover and said:

> **"Would you like me to sign it?"**

That was enough.

Clover came home carrying one notebook.

One autograph.

And approximately the emotional energy of someone who had just discovered that her childhood hero was, in fact, a real person who had willingly written her name.

She has been showing everyone the page ever since.

At last report, Clover had opened the notebook again.

For the **twelfth** time.

Jake was sitting beside her.

He looked at the signature.

Then at Clover.

> **JAKE:**  
> "You really like that."

Clover hugged the notebook.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "You have no idea."
