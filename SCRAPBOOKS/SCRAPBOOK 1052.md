# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### SAFEHOUSE WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-NERI-148  
**CLASSIFICATION:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**SUBJECT:** Neri — Temporary Full-Day Care Assignment  
**ASSIGNED CARETAKER:** Bao  
**DURATION:** One Day

---

## Executive Summary

A scheduling conflict across SAFEHOUSE personnel resulted in an unusually simple arrangement today:

**Everyone was busy.**

Neko and Miss Neko were occupied with engineering work.

Alice had her own activities.

Frost was occupied elsewhere.

Jane Doe was handling intelligence-related work.

Star had responsibilities of her own.

With most of Neri's usual companions unavailable, Bao volunteered to handle Neri for the day.

This was not considered a medical assignment.

It was simply:

> **Mom has the plushie today.**

---

## 08:14 — Morning

Bao found Neri sitting on a couch in her plush form.

Neri looked up.

Bao held out both hands.

Neri immediately crawled toward her.

Bao picked her up.

No discussion occurred.

Bao tucked Neri against her side and continued with her morning activities.

Neri remained there.

---

## 09:02 — Breakfast

Bao prepared breakfast while carrying Neri under one arm.

Neri watched.

She occasionally repositioned herself.

Bao eventually placed her on the counter.

> **BAO:** "Stay there."

Neri stared at her.

> **BAO:** "Please don't fall off."

Neri looked toward the edge of the counter.

Then back at Bao.

She moved approximately twenty centimeters away from the edge.

> **BAO:** "...Thank you."

---

## 10:31 — Work

Bao spent part of the morning working.

Neri sat beside her.

She did not interrupt.

She did not demand attention.

She simply existed.

Occasionally, Bao reached over and scratched the top of Neri's plush head.

Neri leaned into the hand.

---

## 11:47 — Relocation

Neri apparently decided she was finished sitting beside Bao.

She crawled across the couch.

Then onto the floor.

Then toward another chair.

Bao watched her.

> **BAO:** "Where are you going?"

Neri continued walking.

Bao waited.

Neri eventually stopped underneath the chair.

> **BAO:** "...Of course."

Five minutes later, Bao picked her up and relocated her to the couch.

Neri accepted this without protest.

---

## 13:06 — Lunch

Neri was placed beside Bao during lunch.

She ate normally in plush form.

Bao did not attempt to feed her.

Neri is fully capable of eating and drinking while transformed.

The only intervention required was moving her coffee slightly farther from the edge of the table.

Bao has apparently learned from the cafeteria incidents.

---

## 14:20 — Nap

Neri became noticeably less active.

She climbed into Bao's lap.

Bao adjusted her position.

Neri curled up.

Within several minutes, she was asleep.

Bao remained completely still for approximately forty minutes.

When asked why she had not moved:

> **BAO:** "I'm holding my daughter."

No further explanation was considered necessary.

---

## 15:18 — Awake

Neri woke up.

She remained in Bao's lap.

Bao continued working.

Neri watched her for several minutes.

Then she rolled onto her back.

Bao looked down.

> **BAO:** "Comfortable?"

Neri nodded.

Bao returned to work.

---

## 17:03 — Walk

Bao carried Neri through the SAFEHOUSE.

Several personnel noticed.

One attempted to ask whether Neri was okay.

Bao answered:

> **BAO:** "She's fine."

Neri gave a small wave from Bao's arms.

The personnel continued walking.

---

## 18:41 — Evening

Neri spent the evening mostly being carried.

She was passed between:

**Bao's arms → couch → Bao's lap → table → Bao's arms.**

She showed no preference for any particular location.

As long as Bao was nearby, she appeared content.

---

## 21:16 — End of Day

Most SAFEHOUSE personnel had finished their work.

Alice eventually arrived.

She immediately noticed Neri in Bao's arms.

> **ALICE:** "Can I have her?"

Bao looked at Neri.

Neri looked at Alice.

Neri remained in Bao's arms.

Alice paused.

> **ALICE:** "...Okay."

Bao smiled.

> **BAO:** "She's had a quiet day."

Neri reached one plush paw toward Alice.

Alice's expression changed.

> **ALICE:** "Oh."

Bao carefully handed Neri over.

Alice hugged her.

Neri settled comfortably against her.

---

## Final Assessment

The temporary arrangement was successful.

No medical incidents occurred.

No accidental falls were recorded.

No one attempted to put Neri in a washing machine.

Neri spent most of the day either:

- beside Bao,
    
- on Bao,
    
- in Bao's arms,
    
- or quietly wandering around before returning to Bao.
    

The SAFEHOUSE Welfare Division therefore considers Bao's full-day handling assignment:

**Completed successfully.**
