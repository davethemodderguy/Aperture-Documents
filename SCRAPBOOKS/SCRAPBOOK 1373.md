There is no cure.

> **IRREVERSIBLE.**

# 2. THE MISPLACED ZERO

The original mistake remains understood.

A single misplaced zero altered the Transcendense threshold.

The intended system was:

> **Critical injury → Ether activation → regeneration → survival.**

The resulting system became:

> **Critical injury → death → Ether activation → restoration → survival.**

The error can be identified.

The original calculations can be reconstructed.

The experiment can be understood.

But understanding the mistake does not provide a way to undo its consequences.

---

# 3. WHY IT CANNOT BE REMOVED

T.O.P.S. determined that Transcendense is no longer an external system operating on Phoenix.

It has become integrated into her.

Her physiology.

Her Ether.

Her restoration process.

Her response to death.

Her continued existence.

There is no longer a clean boundary between:

> **Phoenix**

and

> **Transcendense.**

Removing the condition would therefore require removing a fundamental part of the mechanism currently keeping Phoenix alive.

T.O.P.S. considers such an attempt unsafe.

---

# 4. THE MOST DEVASTATING FINDING

The final report contains one sentence that has been highlighted repeatedly:

> **“There is no dormant cure waiting to be discovered.”**

T.O.P.S. does not believe the answer is merely unknown.

They believe the answer **does not exist within the systems currently available to them.**

Their previous attempts were not unsuccessful because they lacked sufficient time.

They were unsuccessful because the condition has become fundamentally inseparable from Phoenix.
