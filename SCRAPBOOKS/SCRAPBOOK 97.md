# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **CONFIDENTIAL ETHER OBSERVATION LOG**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – COGNITIVE DISTRESS EVENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical Director & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **SUMMARY**

During another overnight shift at KCA Headquarters, Corin Lockwood experienced a significant emotional episode following an interaction with the Ether manifestation she identifies as resembling her late mother.

Unlike previous encounters, the exchange resulted in immediate psychological distress.

Corin later described it as:

> "...The first time her words hurt."

---

## **OBSERVED ACCOUNT**

Corin had remained at her desk well past midnight.

Several medication reminders had already been acknowledged.

She had eaten.

She had finished the day's reports.

The office was quiet.

The figure stood nearby.

After a prolonged silence, it spoke.

> **"You know how much being abandoned hurts."**

Corin quietly nodded.

The figure continued.

> **"...And yet..."**

Another pause.

> **"...You've been leaving the people who love you behind."**

The room fell silent.

Corin did not answer.

---

## **PATIENT RESPONSE**

Corin later described what she felt in that moment.

> "...It wasn't angry."

> "...It wasn't trying to be cruel."

She lowered her head.

> "...But it chose the worst possible words."

She began crying during the interview.

> "...I didn't leave because I stopped loving them."

> "...I left because I thought they'd be happier without seeing me like this."

The attending psychologist noted that Corin interpreted the statement as confirmation that she had become the very thing she feared most.

For someone whose trauma is deeply tied to abandonment, the wording intensified her existing guilt rather than encouraging reconnection.

---

## **CLINICAL INTERPRETATION**

The treatment team concluded that the event illustrates how even well-intentioned messages can be experienced very differently by someone carrying severe trauma.

Corin's core belief is not that she wants to abandon others.

It is that **she is afraid of being abandoned because she believes she is no longer worthy of staying close to the people she loves.**

When that fear is expressed in terms of blame, it reinforces shame rather than hope.

Future counseling sessions will continue to help Corin distinguish between:

- Choosing isolation because of fear.
    
- Believing that isolation is the only option.
    

These are not the same.

---

## **LATER THAT NIGHT**

Several minutes passed.

Corin remained seated on the floor.

The figure did not speak.

Eventually...

It knelt beside her.

Its voice was much quieter.

> **"...I said that poorly."**

Another pause.

> **"...You didn't leave because you stopped loving them."**

> **"...You left because you were hurting."**

Corin covered her face.

She did not look up.

The figure continued.

> **"...Those are different things."**

No further conversation was reported that evening.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the report before adding a brief comment.

> "People in pain often hear guilt much louder than compassion."

> "When someone already blames themselves for everything, adding more blame rarely helps them heal."

He signed the report.

> "Keep showing Corin that returning is still possible."

---

## **STATUS**

Corin remains under active psychological care.

The incident has been incorporated into her treatment plan as an example of how strongly shame influences her interpretation of events.

The clinical team's focus remains helping Corin recognize that withdrawing from others out of fear does not mean those relationships are beyond repair, and that healing begins not with self-condemnation, but with allowing herself to reconnect safely.

**End of Ether Observation Log**
