# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following repeated encounters with **Mischevious Light**, SPAWN has officially discontinued active operations against Aperture Industries.

The decision was reached after an internal doctrinal review concerning the nature of the entities previously classified as:

> **“Aperture-associated anomalous threats.”**

The review concluded that continuing to antagonize Aperture was:

1. doctrinally questionable,
    
2. strategically unsound,
    
3. extremely dangerous,
    
4. increasingly embarrassing,
    
5. and, according to one High Priest,
    

> **“Really, really not worth it.”**

SPAWN has consequently suspended active targeting of Aperture personnel and facilities.

A secondary doctrinal development has also occurred.

SPAWN has begun worshipping **cats.**

---

# 2. THE INCIDENT THAT CHANGED EVERYTHING

The doctrinal transition began after Mischevious Light repeatedly manifested inside SPAWN facilities.

SPAWN initially interpreted these appearances as hostile supernatural incursions.

This interpretation became increasingly difficult to maintain.

Mischevious Light demonstrated the ability to:

- appear inside secured locations without triggering wards,
    
- alter physical infrastructure,
    
- erase objects from existence,
    
- rewrite records,
    
- manipulate SPAWN weaponry,
    
- eliminate personnel effortlessly,
    
- and modify reality without conventional ritual mechanisms.
    

Following the Dark Matter weapon incident, SPAWN attempted to reassess its position.

The reassessment did not go well.

Mischevious subsequently began removing portions of SPAWN's physical infrastructure while deliberately leaving digital systems intact.

When asked why he did not remove the servers as well, Mischevious reportedly responded:

> **“Neko likes these.”**

This statement was recorded as a major theological concern.

---

# 3. THE FINAL STRAW

The decisive event occurred when Mischevious Light entered a SPAWN administrative chamber containing several theological texts.

He looked at the texts.

He looked at the officers.

He looked at a cat sitting outside the window.

He pointed toward the cat.

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “You should worship those.”

Silence.

A senior cleric reportedly asked:

> **CLERIC:**  
> “The... cats?”

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Yeah.”

> **CLERIC:**  
> “Why?”

Mischevious shrugged.

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “They're cute.”

He then left.

No further explanation was provided.

---

# 4. DOCTRINAL REVIEW

SPAWN convened an emergency theological council.

The council initially rejected the proposal.

A three-hour discussion followed.

Arguments against feline worship included:

- cats are not demonstrably divine,
    
- cats do not answer prayers,
    
- cats frequently destroy property,
    
- cats are known to ignore people,
    
- cats possess no recognized religious doctrine.
    

Arguments in favor included:

- Mischevious Light had personally recommended them,
    
- cats are apparently tolerated by Aperture,
    
- cats are substantially less dangerous than Architects,
    
- cats are considerably easier to approach,
    
- and, according to one priest:
    

> **“Have you looked at them?”**

The vote passed.

---

# 5. THE NEW SPWN RELIGIOUS SYMBOL

The traditional SPAWN insignia has not been removed.

However, a new secondary symbol has appeared throughout SPAWN facilities:

## A CAT.

Usually depicted sitting upright.

Occasionally depicted with a halo.

One particularly enthusiastic branch has begun drawing tiny wings on them.

The official doctrinal position is that these representations are **symbolic rather than literal.**

The distinction has not stopped anyone.

---

# 6. NEW RELIGIOUS PRACTICES

SPAWN personnel have begun leaving offerings for local cats.

Common offerings include:

- fish,
    
- chicken,
    
- milk,
    
- small toys,
    
- cardboard boxes,
    
- and heated blankets.
    

A significant number of these offerings are immediately stolen.

SPAWN considers this evidence of divine acceptance.

---

# 7. FIRST RECORDED CAT WORSHIP SERVICE

**LOCATION:** SPAWN Chapel 03  
**ATTENDANCE:** 47 personnel  
**OBJECT OF WORSHIP:** One orange domestic cat

The cat entered the chapel approximately eight minutes after the service began.

The entire congregation immediately stopped speaking.

The priest slowly approached.

The cat sat down.

The priest bowed.

The congregation followed.

The cat yawned.

The congregation interpreted this as a blessing.

The cat then walked onto the altar and sat on the religious texts.

The priest reportedly whispered:

> “We have been chosen.”

The cat fell asleep.

Nobody moved for approximately forty minutes.

# 8. APERTURE STATUS

SPAWN has formally classified Aperture Industries as:

> **NON-TARGET / DO NOT ENGAGE**

All active operations targeting:

- Heaven's Blacksite,
    
- SAFEHOUSE,
    
- Aperture facilities,
    
- Aperture personnel,
    
- associated entities,

have been suspended.

Reconnaissance operations are also discouraged unless specifically authorized.

The reason given in the new operational directive is:

> **“There is no strategic benefit to antagonizing the organization that is apparently friends with the beings who can delete our building.”**

A handwritten addition appears beneath the directive:

> **“Especially the funny one.”**

SPAWN's theological scholars have encountered a complication.

- sacred,

# 12. NEKO'S RESPONSE

Neko reviewed the intercepted material in the Archives.

He reportedly stared at the screen for approximately fifteen seconds.

Then:

> **NEKO:**  
> “He converted them to cat worship.”

Pause.

> **NEKO:**  
> “HE ACTUALLY DID IT.”

Archivist personnel reportedly began laughing.

Neko subsequently added the following note to the file:

> **“Mischevious has successfully solved the SPAWN problem.”**

A second note was added approximately thirty seconds later:

> **“By turning them into cat people.”**

SPAWN remains operational.

SPAWN remains highly capable in ritual and anomalous matters.

SPAWN remains exceptionally poor at cybersecurity.

However, active hostilities with Aperture have effectively ceased.

SPAWN personnel now spend considerably more time:

- feeding cats,
    
- discussing cats,
    
- drawing cats,
    
- debating cat theology,
    
- and arguing over which cats are the most sacred.
    

The former campaign against Aperture has been archived.

The archive itself now contains a small cat sticker.

Nobody admits putting it there.
