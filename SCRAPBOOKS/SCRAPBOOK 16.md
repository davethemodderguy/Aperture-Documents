# **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE**

## **RESIDENT PROFILE ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: BELLE MERRYWEATHER – OFF-DUTY BEHAVIOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Resident Reference

---

## **OVERVIEW**

While on duty, Director **Belle Merryweather** is composed, responsible, and professional.

Once her workday ends and she returns to Project SAFEHOUSE, however, there is a noticeable change in demeanor.

She largely stops acting like the Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority and instead behaves much closer to her actual age—a cheerful, energetic **20-year-old** enjoying life with family and friends.

Residents often describe it as:

> "Director Belle stays at work. Belle comes home."

---

## **OFF-DUTY PERSONALITY**

Within SAFEHOUSE, Belle is commonly seen:

- Laughing and joking with the other residents.
    
- Sitting on the floor instead of the sofa.
    
- Playing games with Star.
    
- Reading beside Mart.
    
- Helping Bao prepare meals.
    
- Hugging Endmin whenever the opportunity presents itself.
    
- Carrying snacks or carrots around the house.
    
- Falling asleep against Endmin during movie nights.
    

Her formal speech also becomes noticeably more casual.

---

## **MORNING ROUTINE**

Due to her recurring nightmares, Belle occasionally sleeps fewer hours than intended.

Although the frequency of the nightmares has gradually decreased, they still leave her with shortened nights from time to time.

On those mornings, Belle is typically:

- Sleepy.
    
- Quiet.
    
- Slower to wake up.
    
- Less energetic than usual.
    

Residents have learned not to rush her during the first hour after waking.

Once she's fully awake, her usual cheerful personality gradually returns.

---

## **COMFORT DRINKS**

When feeling tired or after a restless night, Belle almost always reaches for one of two drinks.

### 🥛 Warm Milk

Her preferred choice before bed or after waking from a nightmare.

Belle describes it as:

> "...Comforting."

---

### 🍵 Tea

Usually enjoyed during quiet mornings.

Belle often drinks tea while reading or watching the sunrise from SAFEHOUSE.

She prefers milder teas and tends to sip them slowly rather than rushing.

---

## **SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION**

It has become an unspoken routine among the residents.

If Belle appears unusually sleepy in the morning, someone will quietly prepare either:

- A warm mug of milk, or
    
- A cup of tea.
    

No one asks why.

They simply place it beside her.

Belle always notices.

She always smiles.

## **STATUS**

Outside of her official responsibilities, Belle is encouraged by the residents of Project SAFEHOUSE to simply be herself rather than the Director.

Her recurring nightmares have become less frequent over time, though they occasionally leave her tired in the mornings. A warm cup of milk or tea has become a familiar source of comfort, and the rest of SAFEHOUSE has quietly made it part of their routine to ensure one is waiting whenever she needs it.
