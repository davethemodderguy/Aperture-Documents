# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### ANOMALOUS RESEARCH & RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-ARC-4C-JAKE-191  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — ANOMALOUS RESIDENT  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**SUBJECT:** Jake  
**EVENT:** Restoration of Angelic Wings

---

# 1. CLARIFICATION

Jake's current classification has been updated.

He is now simultaneously confirmed as:

> **A Weeping Angel.**

> **A human manifestation of a literal star.**

> **An angel with restored wings.**

These classifications are not mutually exclusive.

The restoration of Jake's wings does **not** indicate that his Weeping Angel condition has been cured.

Likewise, his stellar nature remains unchanged.

Jake is still Jake.

---

# 2. WINGS

Jake has regained his wings.

The wings are fully manifested and physically present.

They retain the same general celestial characteristics associated with Jake's original angelic nature:

- azure-white coloration;
    
- faint internal luminosity;
    
- subtle stellar particles;
    
- soft celestial glow;
    
- and a distinctly non-human structure.
    

The wings appear capable of normal movement.

Jake can fold them against his back.

He can extend them when desired.

He can also apparently forget that they are there and walk directly into a doorway with them.

This has already occurred twice.

---

# 3. WEeping ANGEL STATUS

Despite the restoration of his wings, Jake remains classified as a **Weeping Angel.**

The defining condition has not been reversed.

Jake remains permanently bound to his current existence.

He remains unable to simply return to Starlands.

His long-established emotional condition and supernatural status remain.

The wings therefore represent:

> **Restoration of an angelic characteristic.**

Not:

> **Removal of the Weeping Angel condition.**

C.O.R.E. has found no evidence that the two are contradictory.

---

# 4. STELLAR STATUS

Jake also remains a literal star.

His current body is a human-shaped manifestation of his stellar existence.

He is not metaphorically a star.

He is not merely star-themed.

He is not an ordinary child with anomalous energy.

He is a star that manifests as a six-year-old human child.

His stellar core remains located within his teddy bear.

The restoration of his wings has produced no measurable change to this arrangement.

---

# 5. VISUAL OBSERVATION

The combination is somewhat unusual.

Jake now possesses:

**White hair.**

**Glowing azure eyes.**

**A fractured halo.**

**Restored angelic wings.**

**Stellar particles.**

**A teddy bear containing his heart.**

And the general appearance of a very small six-year-old who would probably rather be eating cake.

He remains physically child-sized.

He remains capable of becoming tired.

He remains capable of being frightened.

He remains capable of crying.

He remains capable of being picked up.

The presence of wings does not make him any less of a child.

---

# 6. JAKE'S RESPONSE

When asked how he felt about the wings returning, Jake reportedly stared at them for several seconds.

He moved one wing.

Then the other.

He looked over his shoulder.

Then said:

> **Jake:** “They're back.”

Personnel asked:

> **“Are you happy?”**

Jake thought about it.

> **Jake:** “A little.”

He then folded his wings.

A few seconds later:

> **Jake:** “Can Bao see?”

Personnel informed him that Bao was available.

Jake immediately left to find her.

---

# 7. BAO

Bao's response was considerably less clinical.

She stopped when she saw Jake.

She looked at his wings.

Then at Jake.

Then back at the wings.

> **Bao:** “Oh.”

Jake looked up at her.

> **Jake:** “They're back.”

Bao smiled.

> **Bao:** “I see that.”

Jake opened his wings slightly.

Bao crouched down.

> **Bao:** “They're beautiful.”

Jake's wings slowly folded again.

He stepped closer.

Bao opened her arms.

Jake accepted the hug.

No further discussion was necessary.

---

# 8. NERI

Neri's response was considerably shorter.

She examined the wings.

Looked at Jake.

Looked at the wings again.

Then:

> **Neri:** “Symmetrical.”

Jake nodded.

> **Jake:** “Yes.”

Neri looked at him for another second.

> **Neri:** “Good.”

Jake seemed satisfied with this assessment.

---

# 9. AETERNA

Aeterna's response was different.

She immediately recognized the significance.

Aeterna remained silent for several seconds.

Then:

> **Aeterna:** “You have returned to yourself.”

Jake looked up.

> **Jake:** “Mostly.”

Aeterna lowered herself to his height.

> **Aeterna:** “Mostly is sufficient.”

Jake leaned against her armor.

His wings folded around himself.

Aeterna did not move.

---

# 10. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Jake's restoration should not be interpreted as a reversal of his history.

He remains a Weeping Angel.

He remains permanently bound to his current existence.

He remains a literal star manifesting as a human child.

He remains emotionally affected by the events that created his current condition.

The return of his wings simply means that one part of Jake that had been absent is present again.

It is significant.

But it does not erase everything else.

---

## 11. CURRENT CLASSIFICATION

C.O.R.E. records shall now identify Jake as:

> **JAKE**  
> **Six-year-old human manifestation of a stellar entity**  
> **Weeping Angel**  
> **Winged / Celestial Form Restored**  
> **Stellar Core Contained Within Teddy Bear**  
> **SAFEHOUSE RESIDENT**

The previous designation:

> **“Wingless Weeping Angel”**

is now obsolete.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake spent a very long time without wings.

He learned how to live without them.

He learned how to walk.

How to sit.

How to sleep.

How to carry a teddy bear.

How to exist as a little boy in a place that was never supposed to become his home.

Now he has them again.

They do not fix everything.

They do not undo what happened.

They do not make him stop being a Weeping Angel.

They do not change the fact that beneath the little boy is something much older and stranger:

A star.

But when Jake spreads his wings, there is one simple difference.

For the first time in a very long time—

**he looks like an angel again.**

And apparently, that matters to him.
