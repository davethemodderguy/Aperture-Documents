# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **TREEHOUSE / MISSING PERSON INCIDENT**

# **ORPHIE — RETURN / CORIN — MISSING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Orphie Status:** **LOCATED / RETURNED**  
**Corin Status:** **MISSING**  
**Time Since Orphie's Disappearance:** **8 Months**  
**Current Threat Level:** **CRITICAL**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

After approximately eight months of unexplained absence, **Orphie has returned to the Treehouse.**

She was carrying a bundle of rare **blue flowers**.

The flowers were specifically chosen because they were known to be among Corin's favorites.

Orphie appeared confused when informed of Corin's condition.

Her immediate reaction was:

> **"WHAT? I got lost. Where is she?"**

Personnel immediately rushed with her toward the Treehouse.

They arrived too late.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

Corin was nowhere to be found.

The Treehouse was empty.

No sign of struggle was discovered.

No obvious damage was present.

The only illumination came from Corin's familiar feather lanterns.

Something was wrong with them.

---

# **THE FEATHER LANTERNS**

Corin's feather lanterns normally produce a bright, persistent azure flame.

On this occasion, every lantern was burning unusually weakly.

The flames were still light-azure.

They were still burning.

But their intensity had fallen dramatically.

No known environmental condition explains the change.

C.O.R.E. has classified this as:

> **ANOMALOUS DEVIATION — INVESTIGATION REQUIRED**

---

# **THE FEATHER**

One freshly shed feather was discovered beside the lanterns.

It showed no signs of physical damage.

It was unmistakably recent.

Personnel immediately recognized it as belonging to Corin.

The feather remained dormant.

No activation attempt was made.

---

# **THE NOTE**

A small handwritten note was found beside the feather.

It contained only one message.

> **"You proved I'm right, thank you"**  
> **—Corin**

The note has been preserved as evidence.

---

# **ORPHIE'S REACTION**

Orphie read the note.

She immediately went silent.

She looked around the Treehouse.

Then at the feather.

Then at the flowers still held in her hands.

> **"...No."**

She dropped the flowers.

> **"No, no, no."**

She began searching the Treehouse herself.

Every room.

Every corner.

Every familiar hiding place.

Corin was nowhere.

---

# **THE FLOWERS**

The blue flowers remained scattered across the floor.

They had been brought back specifically for Corin.

They were exactly the flowers Corin had wanted.

They had finally arrived.

Eight months too late.

Orphie reportedly picked one up.

She held it tightly.

> **"I came back."**

No response.

> **"I came back, Corin."**

Silence.

---

# **SEARCH INITIATED**

C.O.R.E. immediately activated a full missing-person response.

Search teams have been deployed around:

- The Treehouse.
    
- The surrounding wilderness.
    
- Known dimensional routes.
    
- Guiding Light rift locations.
    
- SAFEHOUSE.
    
- Starlands access points.
    
- Known anomalous spaces.
    
- Every location associated with Corin.
    

The Architects have been notified.

Lumi, Arcturus, and Norma have been contacted.

SAFEHOUSE residents have suspended nonessential activities.

---

# **ORPHIE'S STATEMENT**

Orphie has provided an initial account of her disappearance.

She states that she:

> **"Got lost."**

Further questioning has been postponed.

Her first priority is finding Corin.

She repeatedly asks the same question:

> **"Where did she go?"**

Nobody currently knows.

---

# **THE LANTERNS**

Researchers have begun studying the weakened flames.

This phenomenon has never previously been documented.

Corin's feathers have demonstrated remarkable consistency throughout every prior observation.

They burn indefinitely.

They do not weaken.

They do not consume fuel.

They do not extinguish.

Yet now:

> **They are dimming.**

The timing is considered highly significant.

No causal relationship has been established.

---

# **THE EMPTY TREEHOUSE**

For eight months, Corin waited for Orphie beneath the Treehouse tree.

Now Orphie has returned.

The flowers have returned.

The Treehouse has finally received the thing Corin spent months waiting for.

But Corin is gone.

The irony has not been lost on anyone involved in the investigation.

---

# **ORPHIE AND CORIN**

Orphie's last known absence lasted eight months.

Corin's subsequent disappearance occurred sometime before Orphie's return.

This creates a deeply concerning possibility:

Corin may have left deliberately after reaching a psychological breaking point.

However, C.O.R.E. has **no confirmed information regarding her destination or intentions.**

The note alone cannot establish where she went.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Eight months ago, Orphie disappeared while going to collect flowers.

Corin waited.

She waited until hope became painful.

She blamed herself.

She believed Orphie hated her.

She believed she was not worth returning to.

And then, after eight months, Orphie finally came home.

She came carrying the exact flowers Corin had been waiting for.

She asked:

> **"Where is she?"**

But there was no answer.

Only a quiet Treehouse.

A bundle of blue flowers lying on the floor.

A freshly shed feather.

Several lanterns burning with an unfamiliar, weakening glow.

And one note.

> **"You proved I'm right, thank you."**  
> **—Corin**

Orphie has finally returned.

Now everyone is searching for Corin.

**End of Treehouse / Missing Person Incident**
