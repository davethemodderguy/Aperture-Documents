### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / SOUL SEPARATION REPORT**

# **CORIN — ASTRAL PROJECTION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **Voluntary Astral Projection**  
**Status:** Confirmed / Separation Time Limited

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has demonstrated the ability to temporarily separate her consciousness and soul from her physical body.

The resulting state is classified as **Astral Projection**.

When separated, Corin's physical body remains behind while her soul manifests as a translucent, ghost-like version of herself.

The projected form retains Corin's recognizable appearance.

It is simply:

> **Transparent.**

---

# **ASTRAL FORM**

Corin's projected body closely resembles her normal physical appearance.

Observed characteristics include:

- Transparent body.
    
- Visible silhouette.
    
- Normal facial features.
    
- Visible hair.
    
- Visible wings.
    
- Ether-like translucency.
    
- Normal movement.
    
- Ability to pass through most ordinary physical objects.
    

The form does not appear to be a separate entity.

It is still **Corin.**

Her consciousness remains fully intact.

---

# **SEPARATION**

Astral projection does not destroy or permanently alter Corin's physical body.

While projected, her biological body remains behind in a dormant state.

Her heartbeat continues.

Respiration continues automatically.

Her regenerative systems remain active.

However, the body becomes noticeably less responsive to external stimuli.

---

# **THE CONNECTION**

A faint anomalous connection remains between Corin's astral form and her physical body.

C.O.R.E. currently refers to this as the:

> **Soul-Body Tether**

The tether allows Corin to return to her physical body.

It also appears to be necessary for maintaining the stability of the projection.

---

# **SEPARATION LIMIT**

The soul and body **must not remain separated for excessive periods.**

The longer Corin remains projected, the less stable the connection becomes.

Extended separation may result in:

- Reduced control.
    
- Difficulty returning.
    
- Disorientation.
    
- Ether instability.
    
- Physical weakness upon reintegration.
    
- Potentially severe complications.
    

The exact maximum safe duration has not yet been established.

Until further testing is completed:

> **Extended astral projection is prohibited.**

Corin is not permitted to treat her ghost form as a permanent alternative body.

---

# **REINTEGRATION**

Returning to the body appears to be relatively simple.

Corin moves back toward her physical form.

Upon contact, the astral body and physical body rapidly synchronize.

The soul returns to its biological vessel.

The process is generally painless.

Corin describes it as:

> **"Like putting yourself back into your body."**

---

# **THE ARCHITECTS' CONCERN**

The discovery immediately raised an uncomfortable question among the Architects.

Corin's ability to astrally project indicates that her **soul exists independently enough to separate from her body.**

This was not expected.

Guiding Light was particularly interested.

She remembered the first time she had blessed Corin.

The purpose had been extremely limited:

> **Give Corin her ability to see color back.**

But the consequences had eventually become much larger.

Corin's Ether had become supercharged.

Her organs had revitalized.

Her body had become alive.

Her wings had developed.

Her regenerative capabilities had stabilized.

And now—

Corin could separate her soul from that body.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S QUESTION**

Guiding Light eventually asked:

> **"When I gave you color..."**

Corin looked at her.

> **"Yeah?"**

Guiding Light hesitated.

> **"...Did I accidentally give you anything else?"**

Corin thought about it.

> **"I don't know."**

Guiding Light looked deeply concerned.

---

# **THEORY**

The Architects currently suspect that Guiding Light's original blessing may have interacted with more than Corin's visual system.

The blessing may have affected the relationship between:

**Soul**

**Ether**

and

**Body.**

If so, Corin's astral projection may be another delayed consequence of the same anomalous chain reaction that eventually restored her biological life.

This remains a hypothesis.

There is currently no evidence that Guiding Light intentionally granted Corin astral projection.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S CONCERN**

Guiding Light has become increasingly cautious about performing additional blessings on Corin.

Not because the original blessing was harmful.

Quite the opposite.

It helped Corin enormously.

The concern is that nobody knows exactly what else the blessing may have changed.

Guiding Light's current position is:

> **"I'm not touching anything unless we know what it does."**

Curious Light agreed.

Mischevious Light did not.

> **"Do it again."**

Both Architects immediately told him no.

---

# **CORIN'S VIEW**

Corin is relatively casual about the discovery.

She considers the ghost-like form interesting.

She has already used it to look around places she normally cannot reach.

She has also discovered that her transparent wings remain visible while projected.

Her first major complaint was:

> **"I can't pick anything up."**

The second was:

> **"I look cool, though."**

Nobody disagreed.

---

# **SAFETY PROTOCOL**

C.O.R.E. has established the following requirements:

1. Corin must not astrally project without informing another resident.
    
2. The physical body must remain in a secure location.
    
3. A time limit must be established before projection.
    
4. The body and soul must not be deliberately separated indefinitely.
    
5. Guiding Light is **not** permitted to perform additional experimental blessings on Corin without authorization.
    
6. Any unusual changes following astral projection must be reported immediately.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Guiding Light originally wanted to give Corin one simple thing:

**Color.**

Instead, something far more complicated happened.

Corin's Ether changed.

Her body changed.

She became alive.

She developed wings.

Her regeneration became extraordinary.

And now she can leave her body behind and walk around as a transparent version of herself.

Nobody knows how many of these changes came from Guiding Light's original blessing.

Guiding Light is beginning to suspect that the answer might be:

**More than anyone realized.**

For now, C.O.R.E. has one simple rule regarding Corin's astral form:

> **The soul can leave.**
> 
> **The soul must come back.**
