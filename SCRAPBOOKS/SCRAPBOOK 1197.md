# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neko is panicking.

The cause is not a security breach.

It is not a containment failure.

It is not a server failure.

It is not another anomalous rift.

It is, somehow, worse.

Neri has apparently created another Neri.

Her name is:

> **Nora.**

Neko's immediate response was:

> **"NO."**

---

# 2. THE DISCOVERY

The incident began when Neko entered Neri's laboratory.

Neri was sitting at her workstation.

Nora was beside her.

Both were looking at a chemical notebook.

Neko stopped.

He looked at Neri.

Then Nora.

Then Neri again.

> **NEKO:**  
> "...Why are there two of you?"

Neri looked up.

> **NERI:**  
> "There aren't."

Neko pointed at Nora.

> **NEKO:**  
> "THEN WHO IS THAT?"

Nora raised her hand.

> **NORA:**  
> "Nora."

Neko stared at her.

> **NEKO:**  
> "That's not helping."

---

# 3. THE PROBLEM WITH NORA

Neko's concern became more understandable after approximately thirty seconds of observation.

Nora possesses several characteristics remarkably similar to Neri.

### Both:

- are Tachyons;
    
- are exceptionally intelligent;
    
- are unusually interested in chemistry;
    
- learn rapidly;
    
- have strong analytical abilities;
    
- possess an alarming willingness to investigate things they probably shouldn't;
    
- have demonstrated exceptional focus under specific circumstances;
    
- have little respect for the concept of leaving interesting information alone;
    
- and have already caused Neko significant administrative concern.
    

There is, however, one important distinction.

Neri is quiet.

Nora is not.

Neko immediately recognized the implications.

> **NEKO:**  
> "You made a louder version."

Neri blinked.

> **NERI:**  
> "...No."

Nora raised her hand again.

> **NORA:**  
> "I'm loud."

Neko pointed at her.

> **NEKO:**  
> "THANK YOU."

---

Neko listened.

His ears lowered.

> **NERI:**

> **NORA:**

Neko eventually remembered something.

Neri had previously filled a **12 TB SSD** with chemistry research in approximately one month.

He looked at Nora.

Then at Neri.

Then at the notebook.

> **NEKO:**

> **NORA:**

> **NORA:**

> **NORA:**

> **NERI:**

Nora's eyes widened.

> **NORA:**  
> "That's a lot."

> **NEKO:**  
> "DO NOT GET IDEAS."

# 10. C.O.R.E. CLARIFICATION

For archival purposes:

**Nora Tachyon is not a clone of Neri.**

She is not an artificial copy.

She was not manufactured by Neri.

She is not a laboratory-created duplicate.

She is Neri's biological younger sister who was previously presumed deceased and later returned through anomalous circumstances.

The similarity in intelligence and scientific aptitude is therefore familial rather than the result of deliberate replication.

Neko has been informed of this distinction.

He understands it.

He is still panicking.

## C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Neri has not created another Neri.

She has, however, apparently acquired a younger sister who possesses:

**Neri's intelligence.**

**Neri's interest in chemistry.**

**Neri's curiosity.**

And significantly more willingness to announce her discoveries to everyone within hearing distance.

Neko has formally requested that nobody:

- give Nora unrestricted server access;
    
- give Nora a 12 TB SSD;
    
- give Nora 250 TB of reserved storage;
    
- allow Nora to mistake research archives for diaries;
    
- or tell Nora that Neri once filled 12 TB in a month.
    

Nora has already learned about the 12 TB.

Neko has therefore failed at the final requirement.
