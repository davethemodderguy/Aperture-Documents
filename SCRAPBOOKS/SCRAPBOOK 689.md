### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / PERSONNEL & FAMILY RECORD**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — NAME RESTORATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Former designation:** Bunny  
**Adoptive Guardian:** Corin Lockwood  
**Status:** **SAFEHOUSE RESIDENT / ADOPTED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Bunny now has a proper name.

Her name is:

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD**

Corin Lockwood has formally adopted her.

The adoption was not prompted by Alice recovering her past.

In fact, Alice still remembers almost nothing about her life before Corin found her.

Instead, Corin decided that if Alice could not remember the name she was born with...

she could have a new one.

A name chosen for the life she has now.

---

# **THE NAME**

Corin sat with Alice beneath the Treehouse.

> **"Bunny."**

Alice looked up.

> **"Yeah?"**

Corin smiled.

> **"You're Alice."**

Alice blinked.

> **"...Bunny."**

> **"No."**

Corin pointed gently toward her.

> **"Alice."**

Alice looked confused.

> **"Bunny Alice?"**

> **"Just Alice."**

Alice thought about it.

> **"...Alice."**

Corin nodded.

> **"That's your name."**

Alice smiled.

> **"Alice."**

---

# **THE ADOPTION**

Corin formally registered Alice as part of the Lockwood family.

The new family designation is:

> **Alice Lockwood**

Her relationship with Corin is officially recorded as:

> **Adoptive Sister / Daughter-equivalent dependent**

However, Alice's preferred terminology remains much simpler.

Corin:

> **"Big Sis."**

Orphie:

> **"Bigger Sis."**

Everyone else:

> **Family.**

---

# **THE CONFUSION**

The transition was not immediate.

Alice had spent considerable time being called Bunny.

She had also referred to herself as Bunny.

Her own identity had become associated with the word.

For several days, she continued correcting people.

> **"Bunny."**

> **"Alice."**

> **"Bunny."**

> **"Alice."**

> **"...Bunny Alice?"**

Corin laughed.

> **"Nope."**

---

# **MEMORY EXERCISES**

Corin began helping Alice associate the new name with herself.

Every morning:

> **"What's your name?"**

Alice:

> **"Bunny."**

Corin:

> **"Try again."**

Alice:

> **"...Alice."**

> **"Correct."**

Alice smiled.

> **"Alice!"**

The improvement was gradual.

Eventually, Alice began responding automatically.

> **"Alice, come here."**

She came.

> **"Alice, dinner."**

She came.

> **"Alice, do you want carrots?"**

Immediate response:

> **"YES!"**

---

# **THE FIRST TIME**

One evening, Corin called from another room:

> **"Alice!"**

Alice looked up.

She immediately realized something.

She had responded before thinking.

She stared at Corin.

> **"...That's me."**

Corin smiled.

> **"Yep."**

Alice touched her chest.

> **"Alice."**

Corin nodded.

Alice smiled.

This time, there was no forced happiness behind it.

Just recognition.

---

# **BELLE'S RESPONSE**

Belle was delighted by the new name.

She approached Alice and announced:

> **"You're Alice now!"**

Alice nodded.

> **"Alice."**

Belle pointed at herself.

> **"Belle."**

Alice pointed at herself.

> **"Alice."**

Belle laughed.

> **"Two names!"**

Alice shook her head.

> **"One name."**

Belle paused.

Then laughed again.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RECORD UPDATE**

Previous:

> **BUNNY**

Current:

> **ALICE LOCKWOOD**

"Bunny" remains an accepted nickname.

Alice does not dislike it.

In fact, she sometimes introduces herself jokingly as:

> **"Alice Bunny."**

Corin has allowed this.

The official record, however, remains:

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD**

---

# **WHAT THE NAME MEANS**

The name does not restore Alice's forgotten childhood.

It does not tell her who her biological mother was.

It does not recover the memories surrounding her mother's death.

It does not explain where she originally came from.

But it gives her something she did not have before.

A name she can choose to remember.

A family name.

A place where she belongs.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

For a long time, she was simply called Bunny.

She accepted it because she had nothing else.

Then Corin gave her something new.

Not a recovered memory.

Not an explanation of her past.

Not an answer to what happened to her mother.

A name.

> **"Alice."**

It took her time to remember that Alice was her.

Sometimes she still responds to Bunny automatically.

Sometimes Corin calls:

> **"Alice!"**

And she turns around.

Every time she does, she's learning something.

That the name belongs to her.

That she belongs somewhere.

That she has a family.

And that she doesn't need to remember who she used to be in order to become someone new.

She is no longer simply the little rabbit Corin found in the snow.

She is:

# **Alice Lockwood.**

And she's still Bunny to the people who love her.
