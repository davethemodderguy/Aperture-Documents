# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Aeterna recently discovered the mechanism responsible for Neri's sudden recovery and Nora's subsequent anemia.

Nora had effectively absorbed Neri's condition.

The action was not deliberate in the conventional sense.

Nora appears to have instinctively redirected the condition away from Neri and onto herself.

Upon learning this, Aeterna confronted Nora.

The intention was protective.

The execution was unsuccessful.

More precisely:

> **Aeterna accidentally used precisely the wording most likely to reinforce Nora's current psychological state.**

Aeterna told Nora that Neri was fine now.

She told Nora that Neri would not want her to do this.

She attempted to explain that Nora did not need to sacrifice herself for her sister.

All of these statements were medically reasonable.

Nora did not interpret them that way.

She interpreted them as confirmation of her deepest fear:

> **Neri does not need me anymore.**

---

# 1. AETERNA DISCOVERS THE TRUTH

Aeterna was informed that Nora's anemia was likely connected to Neri's previous condition.

She remained motionless for several seconds.

Then:

**AETERNA:**  
“She took it.”

**C.O.R.E. MEDICAL OFFICER:**  
“Apparently.”

Aeterna's eyes brightened.

Not with anger.

With realization.

She immediately went to find Nora.

---

# 2. THE CONFRONTATION

Nora was sitting quietly in her room.

Her family journal was beside her.

She was not reading it.

Aeterna entered.

Nora looked up.

**NORA:**  
“Hi, Aeterna.”

Aeterna did not answer immediately.

She stood beside the doorway.

**AETERNA:**  
“I know what you did.”

Nora became still.

**NORA:**  
“...What?”

**AETERNA:**  
“What happened to Neri.”

Silence.

Nora looked down.

Aeterna continued.

**AETERNA:**  
“You took her condition.”

Nora's fingers tightened around the edge of her sleeve.

**NORA:**  
“I didn't mean to.”

**AETERNA:**  
“I know.”

Aeterna's voice remained controlled.

**AETERNA:**  
“But you cannot do this again.”

Nora looked up.

**NORA:**  
“Why?”

---

# 3. THE MISTAKE

Aeterna intended to reassure her.

Instead, she chose the wrong words.

**AETERNA:**  
“Because Neri is fine now.”

Nora said nothing.

Aeterna continued.

**AETERNA:**  
“She doesn't need you to carry this for her.”

Nora's expression changed.

Only slightly.

Aeterna did not notice.

**AETERNA:**  
“She would not want you to make yourself sick for her.”

Nora lowered her head.

**AETERNA:**  
“You need to understand that Neri is capable of taking care of herself now.”

Pause.

Then:

**AETERNA:**  
“She is fine even without you.”

Silence.

Aeterna continued speaking.

She did not realize she had already lost the conversation.

---

# 4. NORA'S INTERPRETATION

Aeterna meant:

> **“Neri loves you. She does not want you to sacrifice yourself for her.”**

Nora heard:

> **“Neri is fine without you.”**

Aeterna meant:

> **“You are allowed to recover.”**

Nora heard:

> **“You are no longer necessary.”**

Aeterna meant:

> **“You don't have to protect everyone.”**

Nora heard:

> **“Nobody needs you to stay.”**

This distinction is critical.

Nora's current psychological state is already organized around perceived burden and usefulness.

Her mind is actively searching for evidence supporting the belief:

> **“Everyone would be better off if I were less present.”**

Aeterna inadvertently provided it.

---

# 5. NORA'S RESPONSE

Nora remained silent for approximately eleven seconds.

Then:

**NORA:**  
“Okay.”

Aeterna looked at her.

**AETERNA:**  
“Do you understand?”

Nora nodded.

**NORA:**  
“Yes.”

Aeterna relaxed slightly.

This was premature.

Nora picked up her family journal.

She placed it beside her pillow.

Then quietly said:

> **“I'm sorry.”**

Aeterna immediately responded:

**AETERNA:**  
“You have nothing to apologize for.”

Nora nodded.

> **“Okay.”**

She turned away.

Aeterna left.

---

# 6. IMMEDIATE BEHAVIORAL CHANGE

Within the following hours, personnel observed:

- Nora requesting less assistance;
    
- Nora declining food;
    
- Nora declining water;
    
- Nora remaining in her room;
    
- Nora not asking Aeterna to carry her;
    
- Nora not visiting the Merch Store;
    
- Nora not approaching Neri;
    
- Nora turning several family cards face-down;
    
- Nora placing her journal farther away than usual.
    

Most notably:

Nora stopped calling for Aeterna.

This was unusual.

Aeterna normally receives at least one request from Nora during the day.

That day:

None.

---

# 7. NERI NOTICES

Neri noticed within approximately two hours.

She entered Nora's room.

Nora was sitting on the bed.

**NERI:**  
“Nora.”

Nora looked up.

**NORA:**  
“Hi, Sis.”

Neri waited.

**NERI:**  
“What's wrong?”

**NORA:**  
“Nothing.”

Neri stared at her.

Nora looked away.

Neri sat beside her.

Neither spoke for several seconds.

Then:

**NORA:**  
“It's okay.”

Neri's expression changed.

**NERI:**  
“What is?”

Nora hesitated.

Then:

> **“You don't need me anymore.”**

Neri immediately understood.

---

# 8. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri did not become angry.

She did not lecture Nora.

She simply moved closer.

**NERI:**  
“Who said that?”

Nora remained silent.

Neri waited.

Eventually:

**NORA:**  
“Aeterna.”

Neri looked toward the doorway.

Then back at Nora.

**NERI:**  
“She didn't mean it.”

Nora shook her head.

**NORA:**  
“She said you're fine without me.”

Neri paused.

Then:

**NERI:**  
“I'm fine because you're here.”

Nora looked at her.

Neri continued.

**NERI:**  
“Not because I need your blood.”

Pause.

> **“Because you're my sister.”**

Nora's eyes began watering.

---

# 9. AETERNA RETURNS

Aeterna had been standing outside the room.

She heard everything.

She entered slowly.

For perhaps the first time since her restoration, Aeterna appeared genuinely uncertain about what to say.

She looked at Nora.

Then Neri.

Then Nora again.

**AETERNA:**  
“I made a mistake.”

Nora looked at her.

Aeterna knelt.

Her armored form lowered until she was closer to Nora's eye level.

**AETERNA:**  
“I was trying to tell you that you did not have to hurt yourself for Neri.”

Nora said nothing.

**AETERNA:**  
“I told you that Neri would be fine without you.”

Pause.

**AETERNA:**  
“That was wrong.”

Nora's eyes remained fixed on the floor.

Aeterna continued.

**AETERNA:**  
“Neri does not need your illness.”

Pause.

> **“But she needs her sister.”**

Nora's expression tightened.

Aeterna placed one armored hand against her own chest.

**AETERNA:**  
“And so do I.”

---

# 10. THE CORRECTION

Aeterna carefully explained what she had meant.

She did not tell Nora:

> “You are necessary.”

That would reinforce the same problem from the opposite direction.

Instead:

**AETERNA:**  
“You do not have to make yourself useful to stay.”

Nora looked at her.

**AETERNA:**  
“You do not have to save Neri.”

**AETERNA:**  
“You do not have to protect everyone.”

**AETERNA:**  
“You do not have to be healthy for everyone else.”

**AETERNA:**  
“You do not have to earn your place here.”

Then:

> **“You are allowed to simply be Nora.”**

---

# 11. NORA'S QUESTION

Nora eventually asked:

> **“Even if I don't do anything?”**

Aeterna answered immediately.

**AETERNA:**  
“Yes.”

> **“Even if I'm sick?”**

**AETERNA:**  
“Yes.”

> **“Even if everyone has to take care of me?”**

**AETERNA:**  
“Yes.”

> **“Even if Neri doesn't need me?”**

Aeterna looked toward Neri.

Neri answered this one.

**NERI:**  
“I need you.”

Nora's voice became very small.

> **“Why?”**

Neri answered:

> **“Because you're Nora.”**

No further explanation was necessary.

---

# 12. C.O.R.E. ANALYSIS

Aeterna's initial mistake was understandable.

The statement:

> **“Neri is fine without you.”**

would normally communicate a healthy boundary.

It tells a child:

> You do not need to sacrifice yourself for someone you love.

However, Nora is currently operating under a distorted framework:

> **My value comes from what I provide.**

Therefore:

> **Neri doesn't need what I provide**

becomes:

> **Neri doesn't need me.**

This is precisely why personnel must be extremely careful when discussing Nora's role within the family.

The goal is not to convince Nora that she is **needed**.

The goal is to convince her that she is **wanted without conditions.**

---

# FINAL ASSESSMENT

Aeterna attempted to protect Nora from the belief that she needed to sacrifice herself for Neri.

Instead, she accidentally reinforced Nora's belief that her existence has value only when someone requires something from her.

Fortunately, Neri recognized the misunderstanding almost immediately.

Aeterna subsequently corrected herself.

No permanent psychological deterioration has been established from the incident.

However, personnel are advised that Nora's current state makes seemingly reassuring statements unusually easy to misinterpret.

Especially statements involving:

**usefulness.**

**necessity.**

**burden.**

**being needed.**

And particularly:

> **“They're fine without you.”**
