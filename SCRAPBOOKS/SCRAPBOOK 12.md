### **BIOLOGICAL OBSERVATION – BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Medical Reference

---

## **SUMMARY**

Medical evaluations have documented that Director Belle Merryweather possesses an unusually high natural resistance to **blunt-force impacts**.

This resilience is attributed to the combination of her unique Rabbit Full Body Thiren physiology and the stabilized Ether integrated throughout her body.

Researchers emphasize that this observation is **descriptive**, not an invitation to perform impact tests.

---

## **OBSERVED CHARACTERISTICS**

Belle's physiology includes:

- Extremely soft, plush-like fur.
    
- Thick, highly resilient skin.
    
- Exceptional impact absorption.
    
- Dense underlying musculature developed through years of physical training.
    
- Stabilized Ether that further enhances her overall durability.
    

Together, these characteristics allow her body to dissipate blunt impacts far more effectively than an ordinary human.

---

## **CONTROLLED TESTING**

During controlled medical and engineering evaluations using calibrated impact-testing equipment, Belle consistently demonstrated a remarkable tolerance to blunt-force strikes that would ordinarily cause significant bruising or injury.

Researchers observed:

- Minimal superficial injury.
    
- Rapid recovery from non-penetrating impacts.
    
- Excellent force distribution across the affected area.
    

Her body effectively absorbs and spreads impact energy instead of concentrating it at a single point.

---

## **LIMITATIONS**

While Belle is exceptionally resistant to blunt-force trauma, she is **not invulnerable**.

Medical staff continue to treat any injury seriously, and standard safety procedures remain in effect.

Personnel are reminded that deliberately striking coworkers is prohibited regardless of their documented resilience.
