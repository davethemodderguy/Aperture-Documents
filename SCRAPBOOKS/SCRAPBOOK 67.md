## **PHYSIOLOGICAL CHARACTERISTICS**

Following her restoration through stabilized Ether, Corin's body exhibits properties that differ significantly from those of an ordinary human.

Medical examinations have documented an exceptionally resilient physiology.

### **Observed Traits**

- Extremely rapid tissue regeneration.
    
- Resistance to conventional physical trauma.
    
- Stabilized Ether continuously sustains bodily functions.
    
- Pain perception remains normal despite accelerated recovery.
    

As a result, injuries that would ordinarily be fatal can often be recovered from given sufficient time.

Researchers emphasize that this **does not make Corin invincible**. She can still be injured, experience pain, and be temporarily incapacitated. However, conventional methods are generally ineffective at causing permanent death.

Current KCA doctrine states that **Evisceration ammunition**, specifically designed to neutralize stabilized Ether within a target, represents the most reliable means of permanently stopping individuals whose physiology depends on stabilized Ether.

Corin herself is fully aware of this vulnerability.

Her response when informed that the documentation had been updated was characteristically calm.

> "...Good."

> "Everyone should know their own weaknesses."

---

## **PRIMARY WEAPON**

Unlike her siblings' preference for firearms, Corin carries a single weapon.

### **Standard Equipment**

**Katana**

The blade is meticulously maintained and rarely leaves its sheath unless absolutely necessary.

Corin's swordsmanship emphasizes precision, efficiency, and ending confrontations as quickly as possible.

She has no interest in flashy techniques.

As she once explained:

> "If I draw it..."

> "...I'd rather not need a second strike."

---

## **VISITS TO THE BLACKSITE**

Although her primary workplace remains KCA Headquarters, Corin has become a frequent visitor to Heaven's Blacksite.

Officially, her visits are to review departmental expenditures and financial reports.

Unofficially...

She's there to spend time with her siblings and the SAFEHOUSE residents.

Her arrival is usually announced by a quiet knock on the SAFEHOUSE door.

Belle is almost always the first to answer.

> "Corin!"

Within seconds, Belle is already hugging her.

Corin, now accustomed to this greeting, simply smiles and returns the embrace.

---

## **HEIGHT**

**Height:** **165 cm (5 ft 5 in)**

Among the SAFEHOUSE residents, Corin is considered relatively short.

She is, however, still taller than Belle.

This has become the source of numerous jokes.

Belle has accepted them with good humor.

The recruit wisely chose not to test the lesson.

## **STATUS**

Corin Lockwood continues to divide her time between KCA Headquarters and Heaven's Blacksite.

Her calm demeanor, remarkable resilience, and disciplined swordsmanship have earned her quiet respect among Blacksite personnel.

New recruits quickly learn an important lesson after meeting the Lockwood siblings:

One commands a machine gun.

One outdraws almost anyone with a revolver.

One balances the organization's finances while carrying a katana.

None of them fit the stereotypes people expect.

As a plaque in the KCA training hall humorously reads:
