# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake is currently **bedridden**.

Following his prolonged exposure to the blizzard, severe deprivation, Nullwalker transformation, restoration, subsequent exterior exposure, and resulting fever, Jake's physical condition has deteriorated to the point where independent activity is no longer considered appropriate.

He is currently being kept in the SAFEHOUSE Medical Bay.

The room is secured.

Jake is not permitted to leave without medical authorization.

He is barely permitted to do anything beyond:

- sleep;
    
- drink;
    
- eat when able;
    
- receive treatment;
    
- read for short periods;
    
- sit upright with assistance;
    
- and rest.
    

This is not intended as punishment.

It is medical confinement.

---

# 1. CURRENT CONDITION

Jake remains conscious and responsive.

His condition is nevertheless poor.

Current observations include:

- extreme fatigue;
    
- profound weakness;
    
- minimal spontaneous movement;
    
- persistent exhaustion;
    
- thick darkened eye bags;
    
- reduced stellar glow;
    
- heavy halo resting against his head;
    
- slightly tilted head posture;
    
- inability to speak due to damaged vocal cords;
    
- low physical endurance;
    
- prolonged periods of inactivity;
    
- reduced appetite;
    
- and an overall emotionally flat presentation.
    

Jake frequently remains motionless beneath the blankets.

He may stare at the ceiling for several minutes.

Sometimes longer.

He does not appear to be sleeping.

He simply does not have enough energy to do anything else.

Following the recent exterior lockout incident, personnel determined that allowing Jake unrestricted access to the SAFEHOUSE while he is this physically compromised presents an unacceptable welfare risk.

> **Jake stays inside the Medical Bay.**

He does not need to negotiate this.

He does not need to request permission every few minutes.
