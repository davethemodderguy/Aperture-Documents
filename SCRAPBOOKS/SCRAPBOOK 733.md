### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / PERSONNEL WELFARE OBSERVATION**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — SILENCE AS A VULNERABILITY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** **PROTECTED MINOR / HIGH SOCIAL VULNERABILITY**

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Alice has a habit of becoming completely quiet whenever she is uncomfortable, annoyed, frightened, or simply doesn't like what is happening around her.

She rarely protests.

She rarely confronts anyone.

She does not raise her voice.

She usually just lowers her ears and becomes quiet.

Before losing her voice, this behavior was already present.

Now that Alice is nonverbal, the problem has become significantly worse.

People can mistake her silence for agreement.

They cannot.

---

# **THE SCOOTER**

Alice has recently begun using Neko's electric scooter more frequently.

It allows her to travel around the Blacksite without exhausting herself.

She uses it for one primary reason:

> **She wants to see her friends.**

Alice will ride from the Treehouse toward SAFEHOUSE, workshops, gardens, offices, and other approved areas simply because she wants to visit someone.

She enjoys seeing familiar faces.

She likes waving.

She likes sitting beside people.

She likes being carried home when she becomes tired.

Unfortunately, the scooter also means Alice is increasingly visible throughout the Blacksite.

And that creates a new vulnerability.

---

# **SOCIAL RISK**

Alice is a very small, visibly fragile rabbit girl.

She is nonverbal.

She is physically weak.

She rarely objects when uncomfortable.

She has difficulty defending herself.

And she often assumes that other people mean well.

That combination makes her an extremely easy target for bullying.

Someone could:

- Block her path
    
- Make fun of her scooter
    
- Mock her appearance
    
- Tease her about her ears
    
- Take something from her
    
- Follow her
    
- Intentionally frighten her
    
- Say something cruel
    
- Attempt to touch her tail
    

Alice's usual response would probably be silence.

That silence must **never** be interpreted as permission.

---

# **WHAT ALICE ACTUALLY DOES**

When uncomfortable, Alice often:

1. Stops talking.
    
2. Lowers her ears.
    
3. Looks away.
    
4. Holds her notebook closer.
    
5. Attempts to leave.
    
6. Pretends she is okay.
    

The last step is particularly concerning.

Alice has spent so long trying to keep everyone else happy that she sometimes smiles when she is upset.

---

# **BLACKSITE POLICY**

Personnel are expected to intervene if they observe Alice being harassed.

Do not wait for Alice to complain.

Do not ask:

> "Are you okay?"

and leave simply because she nods.

Observe her behavior.

If Alice is visibly uncomfortable:

**Intervene.**

If someone is bullying her:

**Intervene immediately.**

If someone touches her tail:

**Intervene immediately.**

If someone attempts to separate her from her scooter:

**Intervene immediately.**

Alice does not need to prove that she is uncomfortable before someone helps her.

---

# **HER FRIENDS**

The SAFEHOUSE residents have collectively become very protective of her.

Belle is particularly quick to notice Alice's lowered ears.

Corin can usually tell from Alice's posture.

Lumi watches for her forced smile.

Orphie tends to notice when Alice suddenly becomes unusually quiet.

Star can usually tell when Alice is pretending to be happy.

Neko has also ensured Alice's scooter contains emergency tracking and safety features.

Alice may be traveling independently.

She is **not** without protection.

---

# **A SMALL DETAIL**

Alice doesn't ride her scooter around because she wants to be alone.

Quite the opposite.

She rides it because she wants to find people.

She will sometimes stop outside someone's room.

Wait.

Then wave.

She may sit there for a while without saying anything.

Sometimes she simply wants company.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Alice's silence is not weakness.

But unfortunately, other people may treat it as such.

She is learning how to communicate without a voice.

Everyone else must learn how to listen without requiring one.

Alice should be able to ride her little scooter around the Blacksite looking for her friends without having to fear becoming someone's target.

She has already been through enough.

The Blacksite is her home.

She should be allowed to feel safe in it.
