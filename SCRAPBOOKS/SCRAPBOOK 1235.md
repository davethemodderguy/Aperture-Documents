# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon is not merely fluent in Swedish.

She can also communicate in:

- **English**
    
- **Swedish**
    
- **Russian**
    
- **Vietnamese**
    
- **Japanese**
    

The origin of these additional language abilities is currently unknown.

No conventional educational history has been identified that adequately explains the breadth of Nora's linguistic knowledge.

The anomaly is particularly notable because Nora is approximately six years old.

Her English is strongly Swedish-accented.

Her Swedish is fluent.

Her Russian, Vietnamese, and Japanese are sufficiently developed for ordinary conversation.

C.O.R.E. has therefore opened a limited investigation into the source of her linguistic knowledge.

---

|Language|Current proficiency|Notes|
|---|---|---|

|**Vietnamese**|Conversational to fluent|Bao can also speak Vietnamese|
|**Japanese**|Conversational|Source unknown|

Nora does not appear to consciously translate every sentence before speaking.

She simply speaks.

---

# 3. SWEDISH

Swedish remains the most straightforward case.

It is the Tachyon family's original language.

Nora retained it naturally.

Her older siblings, Elsher and Neri, gradually lost much of their fluency after prolonged English use.

Nora did not.

She therefore remains the strongest Swedish speaker among the three known Tachyon siblings.

Her Swedish becomes especially common when she is:

- annoyed;
    
- embarrassed;
    
- scolding someone;
    
- excited;
    
- tired;
    
- or speaking about family.
    

---

# 4. RUSSIAN

The origin of Nora's Russian is unknown.

No confirmed Russian-speaking instructor has been identified in her known history.

Nora nevertheless demonstrated the ability to hold a conversation in Russian.

When asked where she learned it:

> **NORA:**  
> “I don't know.”

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “Did someone teach you?”

Nora shrugged.

> **NORA:**  
> “Maybe.”

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “Who?”

Nora thought for several seconds.

> **NORA:**  
> “I don't remember.”

No further information was obtained.

---

# 5. VIETNAMESE

Vietnamese presents a somewhat less mysterious situation.

Bao can speak Vietnamese.

This provides Nora with a known Vietnamese-speaking resident with whom she could potentially interact.

However, Bao has stated that she did not deliberately teach Nora Vietnamese.

When asked:

> **BAO:**  
> “I didn't teach her.”

Pause.

> **BAO:**  
> “She just started talking.”

Nora subsequently held a short Vietnamese conversation with Bao.

Bao understood her perfectly.

Neri was present.

Neri understood approximately none of it.

---

# 6. BAO & NORA

The first recorded extended Vietnamese exchange between Bao and Nora occurred in the SAFEHOUSE kitchen.

Nora approached Bao while she was cooking.

Nora began speaking Vietnamese.

Bao immediately responded in Vietnamese.

The exchange lasted approximately several minutes.

Neri entered halfway through.

She stopped.

Looked at Bao.

Looked at Nora.

Looked back at Bao.

> **Neri:**  
> “What are you two doing?”

Bao answered in Vietnamese.

Neri stared.

> **Neri:**  
> “I have no idea what you just said.”

Nora laughed.

Bao continued cooking.

Neri eventually left.

---

# 7. JAPANESE

Japanese is the least explainable language currently documented.

No known Tachyon family member has identified Japanese as a family language.

No confirmed Japanese-language education appears in Nora's available records.

Nevertheless, Nora can speak Japanese naturally enough to:

- greet people;
    
- answer questions;
    
- hold basic conversations;
    
- understand ordinary instructions;
    
- and switch between Japanese and English without obvious difficulty.
    

When asked why she knows Japanese:

> **NORA:**  
> “I don't know.”

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “Do you remember learning it?”

Nora shook her head.

> **NORA:**  
> “I just know it.”

# 16. CURRENT HYPOTHESES

C.O.R.E. has not established a definitive explanation.

Potential explanations remain under investigation.

Possibilities include:

- undocumented childhood exposure;
    
- anomalous memory retention;
    
- linguistic information acquired during Nora's time beyond the known rift;
    
- anomalous cognitive adaptation;
    
- exposure to individuals speaking these languages;
    
- or another mechanism not yet identified.
    

No hypothesis has been confirmed.

C.O.R.E. explicitly advises against assuming that multilingual proficiency necessarily indicates an anomalous ability.

Nora may simply have learned these languages somehow.

The problem is that nobody knows **how**.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Nora Tachyon is currently confirmed to speak five languages.

**Swedish.**

**English.**

**Russian.**

**Vietnamese.**

**Japanese.**

Her Swedish is the clearest connection to her family's heritage.

Her English is her primary SAFEHOUSE language.

Her Vietnamese gives her a particularly strong linguistic connection with Bao.

Her Russian and Japanese remain unexplained.

Nora herself does not seem particularly impressed.

When informed that multilingual proficiency is unusual for her age, she simply responded:

> **“Okay.”**

Then she asked whether she could go play with Alice.
