# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CENTRAL COMPUTING DEPARTMENT**

### **ANOMALOUS OBSERVATION REPORT**

#### **SUBJECT: BELLE MERRYWEATHER – PHOTONIC ETHER PHENOMENON**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Research Personnel

---

## **SUMMARY**

During routine environmental observations in the Starlands, researchers documented an unusual interaction between the stabilized Ether integrated throughout Director Belle Merryweather's body and naturally occurring water mist.

When Belle directs the palm of her **right hand** toward suspended water droplets, the Ether within her body emits a stable, broad-spectrum light closely resembling natural sunlight.

Under suitable atmospheric conditions, this produces a visible rainbow.

---

## **INITIAL DISCOVERY**

The phenomenon was discovered entirely by accident.

Following a light rain shower in the Starlands, Star remarked that it was a shame the sun was hidden behind the clouds.

Belle jokingly raised her right hand toward the lingering mist.

A bright, warm light spread from her palm.

Seconds later...

A rainbow appeared.

Everyone stopped talking.

---

## **RESEARCH FINDINGS**

The Ether integrated throughout Belle's body has previously demonstrated stable photonic properties.

Testing indicates that the Ether can emit visible light without generating hazardous levels of heat or radiation.

When projected through airborne water droplets, the emitted light undergoes natural refraction and dispersion, producing a rainbow identical in appearance to one formed by sunlight.

Researchers have unofficially nicknamed this effect:

> **"Palmbow."**

The nickname has not been adopted in official documentation.

---

## **CHARACTERISTICS**

Observed properties include:

- Requires fine water droplets or mist.
    
- Functions indoors with artificial mist generators.
    
- Produces no harmful emissions.
    
- Does not damage surrounding vegetation.
    
- Emits a pleasantly warm light.
    
- Can be sustained for several minutes without apparent discomfort to Belle.
    

Belle reports that activating the effect feels no different than "holding a flashlight."

---

## **FIELD DEMONSTRATION**

Location:

Starlands Waterfall.

She raised her right hand.

Within moments...

A vivid rainbow stretched across the valley.
