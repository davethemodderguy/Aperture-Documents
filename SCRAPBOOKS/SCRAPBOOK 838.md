## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### SAFEHOUSE RESIDENTIAL SERVICES

# NERI — AQUATIC ACCOMMODATION ADAPTATION

**STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**SPECIES:** Seal Thiren  
**IMPLEMENTED BY:** Neko  
**PURPOSE:** Clothing waterproofing / aquatic accommodation

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

Following her relocation to the SAFEHOUSE, Neri demonstrated a strong preference for spending the majority of her free time in water.

This was not considered unusual.

Neri is a Seal Thiren.

She swims frequently, spends extended periods submerged, and has repeatedly been observed treating available pools, baths, and other suitable bodies of water as perfectly acceptable places to spend several hours.

The only recurring inconvenience was her clothing.

Prior to modification, Neri's clothes would become saturated after entering the water.

Neko considered this inefficient.

He subsequently developed a waterproofing spray capable of treating fabrics without significantly altering their appearance, texture, flexibility, or breathability.

Neri's entire regular wardrobe was treated.

---

## 2. THE SPRAY

The waterproofing compound is currently designated:

**CORE-WP-01 — "Please Stop Getting Your Clothes Wet."**

The name was assigned by Neko.

The compound creates a microscopic anomalous barrier around treated fibers.

Water, moisture, and most other ordinary liquids are unable to penetrate the treated material.

The treatment does **not**:

- make the clothing heavier;
    
- make the fabric rigid;
    
- alter its appearance;
    
- interfere with normal movement;
    
- prevent Neri from swimming;
    
- affect Neri's ability to enter or exit water.
    

The treatment is also safe for repeated immersion.

Neko's initial demonstration consisted of spraying a shirt, placing it in a container of water, removing it several seconds later, and shaking the water off.

Neko:

> "See?"

Neri:

> "Dry."

Neko:

> "Exactly."

Neri:

> "Good."

Neko:

> "You're welcome."

Neri immediately went swimming.

---

## 3. RESIDENTIAL IMPLEMENTATION

Neri's clothes are now routinely waterproofed.

This includes:

- shirts;
    
- shorts;
    
- dresses;
    
- jackets;
    
- socks;
    
- personal accessories;
    
- sleepwear.
    

Her fish-bone hairclip was **not** treated.

Neko determined that it was unnecessary.

Neri disagreed.

Neri:

> "Fish bone should be waterproof."

Neko:

> "It's already waterproof."

Neri:

> "Good."

Neko:

> "...Why did you ask?"

Neri:

> "Fish."

No further clarification was provided.

---

## 4. EFFECT ON DAILY LIFE

The modification has significantly reduced the inconvenience associated with Neri's aquatic habits.

Neri can now leave the water without having to immediately change clothes.

This has resulted in a noticeable increase in the amount of time she spends swimming.

C.O.R.E. residential monitoring records indicate that Neri has adopted the following routine:

**Wake → Eat fish → Swim → Eat fish → Swim → Socialize → Swim → Eat fish → Sleep**

The schedule is not considered medically concerning.

Bao has reportedly encouraged it.

Frost has also participated in several swimming sessions.

Neri and Bao are now frequently observed together in aquatic areas of the SAFEHOUSE.

---

## 5. UNINTENDED CONSEQUENCES

The waterproofing spray has created one minor problem.

Neri has discovered that she can enter the kitchen immediately after swimming without leaving water-soaked clothing behind.

She has consequently begun appearing in common areas while still dripping water from her hair.

Neko has stated that the spray was intended to solve **wet clothes**, not **wet floors**.

Neri's response:

> "Clothes dry."

Neko:

> "That's not the problem."

Neri:

> "Floor wet."

Neko:

> "YES."

Neri:

> "Sorry."

She continued walking.

---

## 6. ARCHIVIST'S NOTE

The waterproofing project was originally considered a minor quality-of-life modification.

It has since become one of the more successful examples of SAFEHOUSE accommodation.

Neri is comfortable.

Neri is safe.

Neri has unrestricted access to water.

And, most importantly, Neri no longer has to deal with the terrible injustice of putting on dry clothes after swimming.
