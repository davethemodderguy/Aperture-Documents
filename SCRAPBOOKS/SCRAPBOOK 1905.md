**SUBJECT:** Sora Akana — Birthday Celebration / Failure to Attend

**PRIORITY:** HIGH

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

According to Mia, today is Sora's birthday.

The date was not independently verified through official records. Mia nevertheless insisted that it was his birthday and organized a small celebration for him on Floor 7.

The intention was simple: bring everyone together, celebrate Sora, and give him a reason to spend time with the people he cared about.

Sora never arrived.

Initially, the residents assumed he was still asleep or had not noticed the preparations. Jake expected him to appear eventually. Mia repeatedly checked the corridor, believing her brother might simply need more time.

However, Sora remained inside his den.

He did not respond to invitations to join the celebration.

He did not approach the common room.

He did not come out to see the decorations, the food, or the people waiting for him.

The atmosphere changed remarkably quickly.

What began as an enthusiastic birthday celebration became a quiet gathering of people who gradually realized that the person they had prepared everything for was too frightened to attend.

Sora was not absent because he had forgotten the occasion, rejected his friends, or disliked the celebration.

He was frightened.

His original whiteboard remains missing. His attempts to communicate without it have repeatedly resulted in frustration and panic. He has consequently withdrawn almost entirely from social interaction.

The birthday celebration exposed the emotional consequences of that withdrawal.

**Everyone had gathered to celebrate Sora. Sora could not bring himself to join them.**

---

## 2. PREPARATIONS

Mia began organizing the celebration earlier in the day.

She insisted that Sora deserved something special, even if the exact date of his birth remained uncertain.

**MIA:** It's his birthday.

**STAFF MEMBER:** Are you certain?

**MIA:** Yes. It's today.

**STAFF MEMBER:** Do we have a recorded date?

Mia hesitated.

**MIA:** Not really.

She looked toward the corridor.

**MIA:** But I know. It's his birthday.

C.O.R.E. did not challenge her assertion during the preparations. The event was treated as a personal celebration based on Mia's account.

Jake enthusiastically participated.

He helped arrange decorations, prepared a place for Sora, and repeatedly asked whether the food was ready.

**JAKE:** Is the cake ready?

**STAFF MEMBER:** Almost.

**JAKE:** Does Sora know?

**STAFF MEMBER:** Mia plans to tell him.

**JAKE:** He's going to like it!

Mia smiled.

**MIA:** I hope so.

A place was reserved for Sora beside Jake and Mia.

The intention was to make the celebration feel familiar rather than overwhelming.

No large gathering was planned. The residents wanted Sora to feel comfortable.

Unfortunately, even a small celebration required something Sora currently struggled to do:

Leave his den and interact with other people.

---

## 3. INCIDENT A — THE EMPTY PLACE

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE Floor 7, common room  
**Time:** 14:05

The celebration began.

Jake was excited.

Mia was smiling.

The cake was placed on the table, and Sora's reserved seat remained empty.

**JAKE:** Where's Sora?

**MIA:** I'll get him.

Mia walked toward his den.

She stopped at the entrance.

Sora was inside, sitting beneath his blanket.

**MIA:** Sora?

He looked toward her.

**MIA:** Happy birthday!

Sora stared at her.

Mia smiled and gestured toward the common room.

**MIA:** Come on! We made something for you.

Sora did not move.

**MIA:** There's cake.

He looked down.

**MIA:** Jake's waiting.

Sora's hands moved slightly.

Mia waited, hoping he might gesture an answer.

He pointed toward the empty space beside him.

His original whiteboard was still missing.

**MIA:** Your board?

Sora nodded.

**MIA:** I know. We still haven't found it.

His expression changed.

Mia noticed his breathing becoming quicker.

**MIA:** It's okay. You don't have to write anything. Just come sit with us.

Sora shook his head.

Mia paused.

**MIA:** You don't want to?

Sora looked at her, then toward the common room.

He began moving his hands, attempting to communicate something.

Mia could not understand.

**MIA:** I'm sorry. I don't understand.

Sora's gestures became more urgent.

Mia immediately stopped asking questions.

**MIA:** It's okay. You don't have to explain.

She looked toward the common room.

**MIA:** I'll come back, okay?

Sora did not respond.

Mia returned to the celebration without him.

## 4. INCIDENT B — THE PARTY BEGINS TO FALL APART

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE Floor 7, common room  
**Time:** 14:17

Jake was waiting.

**JAKE:** Is he coming?

Mia hesitated.

**MIA:** Not yet.

**JAKE:** Why?

**MIA:** He doesn't want to leave his den.

Jake looked toward the corridor.

**JAKE:** Maybe he's shy.

**MIA:** Maybe.

Jake picked up a small decoration.

**JAKE:** We can wait!

For a while, everyone continued.

The cake remained untouched.

The reserved seat remained empty.

Eventually, Jake looked toward the entrance again.

**JAKE:** It's taking a long time.

Mia sat down.

**MIA:** I know.

**JAKE:** Did he say no?

**MIA:** He couldn't tell me.

Jake looked confused.

**JAKE:** Then how do we know?

Mia did not have an answer.

A staff member approached.

**STAFF MEMBER:** Would you like us to bring the cake to his den?

Mia shook her head.

**MIA:** I don't think he wants everyone coming in.

Jake looked at the cake.

**JAKE:** But it's his birthday.

His voice became quieter.

**JAKE:** He should be here.

Nobody contradicted him.

A few minutes passed.

The decorations remained in place, but the excitement had disappeared.

People stopped talking.

The cake was no longer the center of attention.

Everyone kept looking toward the corridor.

## 5. INCIDENT C — JAKE GOES TO SEE HIM

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE Floor 7, Sora's den  
**Time:** 14:29

Jake eventually left the common room.

He approached Sora's den alone.

**JAKE:** Sora?

Sora looked up.

Jake crouched at the entrance.

**JAKE:** It's your birthday.

Sora watched him.

**JAKE:** We made a cake.

He smiled weakly.

**JAKE:** It's really big.

Sora glanced toward the common room.

Jake noticed.

**JAKE:** You don't have to talk.

Sora lowered his eyes.

**JAKE:** I mean... you can't talk. Sorry.

Jake shifted slightly.

**JAKE:** You can just sit with us.

Sora shook his head.

Jake waited.

Sora pointed toward the missing space where his original whiteboard had been kept.

Jake understood.

**JAKE:** You still want your board.

Sora nodded.

**JAKE:** I know.

Jake looked down.

**JAKE:** I tried to find it.

Sora watched him.

**JAKE:** I really did.

Sora's expression softened, but he still did not move.

Jake looked toward the common room.

**JAKE:** Everyone's waiting for you.

Sora began trembling.

Jake immediately stopped.

**JAKE:** Oh. Sorry.

He moved back slightly.

**JAKE:** You don't have to come.

Sora looked at him.

Jake smiled, though his eyes were beginning to water.

**JAKE:** Happy birthday, Sora.

He remained there for a little longer.

Then he returned to the common room.

## 6. INCIDENT D — THE CAKE NOBODY WANTED TO CUT

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE Floor 7, common room  
**Time:** 14:46

Jake returned.

Mia looked at him.

**MIA:** Did he come?

Jake shook his head.

**MIA:** Oh.

The cake remained on the table.

A staff member gently suggested that they could still cut it.

**STAFF MEMBER:** We could save him a slice.

Mia looked at the cake.

**MIA:** It's his cake.

**STAFF MEMBER:** We can keep some for him.

**MIA:** I know.

She lowered her head.

**MIA:** I wanted him to see it.

Jake looked at the empty seat.

**JAKE:** Maybe he thinks we don't want him there.

Mia immediately looked at him.

**MIA:** No. We want him there.

**JAKE:** Then why won't he come?

Mia's expression tightened.

**MIA:** He's scared.

**JAKE:** Of us?

**MIA:** I don't think so.

She looked toward the corridor.

**MIA:** I think he's scared because he can't tell us anything.

Jake went quiet.

**JAKE:** But we know him.

**MIA:** We do.

**JAKE:** We know what he means sometimes.

**MIA:** Sometimes.

**JAKE:** Then why can't we help?

Mia did not answer.

She looked at the empty seat again.

**MIA:** I don't know.

The room became quiet.

Nobody sang.

A slice was reserved for Sora.

The celebration continued in name only.

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE Floor 7

Sora lowered his head.

Before leaving, she spoke again.

## 8. WELFARE ASSESSMENT

C.O.R.E. considers the incident a significant indicator of Sora's current psychological distress.

The birthday celebration was designed to provide positive social contact. Instead, it exposed the extent to which Sora has withdrawn from his previous relationships and activities.

The following distinctions must be maintained:

- Sora did not explicitly refuse the celebration.
    
- He was unable or unwilling to communicate his reasons for remaining in his den.
    
- His visible distress increased when Mia encouraged him to attend.
    
- He appeared particularly affected when he attempted to communicate and was not understood.
    
- He remained absent despite the presence of people he previously spent considerable time with.
    

It would be inaccurate to describe this as Sora simply not wanting a birthday party.

He may have wanted to attend.

He may have wanted to see the cake, sit beside Jake, or spend time with Mia.

C.O.R.E. cannot establish his exact wishes because he could not communicate them.

**His absence must not be mistaken for indifference.**

### Required measures

1. Continue searching for the original whiteboard as a priority welfare matter.
    
2. Do not pressure Sora to attend future gatherings.
    
3. Avoid announcing celebrations or social expectations directly at his den entrance if doing so causes distress.
    
4. Continue offering quiet companionship without requiring participation.
    
5. Ensure food and other birthday gifts remain available without making their acceptance conditional on social interaction.
    
6. Provide emotional support to Jake and Mia, who may experience sadness or confusion over Sora's absence.
    
7. Arrange an appropriate assessment of Sora's increasing social withdrawal and communication-related panic.
    

Jake and Mia must not be expected to resolve this situation themselves.

## 9. CLOSING NOTE — A BIRTHDAY WITHOUT THE BIRTHDAY BOY

Mia wanted to celebrate her brother.

Jake wanted to play with his friend.

The others wanted to make a little boy feel special.

There was a cake.

There were decorations.

There was a seat reserved just for him.

And there was an empty space where Sora should have been.

The saddest part was how quickly everything changed.

At first, everyone was excited. They talked about the cake, waited for Sora, and imagined how he might react.

Then Mia returned without him.

Jake went to ask again.

Sora still did not come.

And gradually, nobody seemed to know what to do anymore.

It is difficult to celebrate someone who cannot bring himself to join you.

It is even more difficult when you know he is only a short walk away.

Sora was not locked away. Nobody prohibited him from attending. The common room was open, his friends were waiting, and the celebration was prepared for him.

But the distance between his den and the common room had become something much larger than a hallway.

He could not speak.

His original whiteboard was missing.

The replacement, even with Jake's signature, remained unacceptable to him.

And every attempt to communicate without it risked another frightening experience in which he tried to explain himself and failed.

So he stayed inside.

Perhaps he wanted to come out.

Perhaps he wanted to tell Mia that he appreciated the cake.

Perhaps he wanted to tell Jake that he missed playing with him.

Perhaps he wanted to say something as simple as _thank you_.

Nobody knows.

That uncertainty is part of the tragedy.

Mia can celebrate her brother's birthday, but she cannot make him feel safe enough to attend.

Jake can sit beside his den, but he cannot give Sora back the object he lost.

And the adults can prepare as many replacement boards as they wish, but none of them can force Sora to accept something that does not feel like his own.

The cake was cut.

A slice was saved.

The decorations were eventually taken down.

Sora never came out.

**A birthday should remind a child that he is loved. It should not become another occasion on which he discovers how difficult it has become to reach the people who love him.**

— **C.O.R.E. Welfare Oversight**
