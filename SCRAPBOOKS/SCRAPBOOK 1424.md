# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neko has been outperformed in **Physics**.

The individual responsible is:

**Jake.**

The incident was not intentional.

Jake did not set out to study physics.

He did not challenge Neko.

He did not even initially realize he had picked up a physics textbook.

He simply grabbed the wrong book.

The book belonged to Neri.

It was a physics reference text that Neri had been using alongside her chemistry research.

Jake began reading it.

He continued reading it.

Then he began asking questions.

Then he began solving problems.

Then C.O.R.E. Research personnel realized that Jake had developed a level of physics understanding sufficient to exceed Neko's established proficiency in the field.

This makes Jake the **fourth known resident to exceed Neko in a specific academic or technical field.**

---

# 1. THE BOOK

The incident began in the SAFEHOUSE library.

Jake had been looking for something to read.

His usual selections were located on the upper shelf.

He reached for a book.

He missed.

His hand caught another book instead.

The cover read:

> **ADVANCED PHYSICS — FOUNDATIONAL & APPLIED PRINCIPLES**

Jake looked at it.

He apparently assumed it was one of his usual books.

He sat down.

He opened it.

And began reading.

Nobody noticed.

---

# 2. FIRST TWO HOURS

Jake read quietly for approximately two hours.

He made no attempt to speak.

His recovery remained physically demanding, so he took frequent breaks.

During those breaks, he would:

- drink water;
    
- rest his head against his teddy bear;
    
- stare at the ceiling;
    
- return to the book.
    

Neri eventually entered the library.

She stopped.

She looked at Jake.

Then at the book.

Then back at Jake.

She immediately recognized it.

> **NERI:**  
> "...That's mine."

Jake looked at the cover.

Then at Neri.

Then back at the book.

He appeared mildly confused.

Neri approached.

She checked the pages.

Several sections had already been annotated.

Not by her.

By Jake.

Neri stared.

---

# 3. THE FIRST PROBLEM

Neri asked Jake how far he had gotten.

Jake pointed toward a section.

Neri looked.

It was considerably farther than she expected.

She then noticed several calculations written in the margins.

The calculations were correct.

She looked at Jake.

Jake looked back.

He pointed toward one equation.

Then toward another.

Neri understood.

He had found an inconsistency in the way the textbook introduced one of the concepts.

Neri checked.

Jake was correct.

Neri immediately became interested.

She took the book.

She checked the calculation again.

Then again.

Finally:

> **NERI:**  
> "...Huh."

Jake tilted his head.

---

# 4. NEKO IS INFORMED

Neri eventually brought the book to Neko.

Neko examined the annotations.

He recognized the material immediately.

> **NEKO:**  
> "Where did Jake get this?"

Neri pointed toward the library.

Neko looked at the book.

Then at Neri.

> **NEKO:**  
> "He read all of this?"

Neri nodded.

Neko became skeptical.

He asked Jake several physics questions.

Jake answered through writing.

The first few answers were straightforward.

Then Neko increased the difficulty.

Jake continued answering.

Neko increased it again.

Jake solved the problems.

Neko stopped.

---

# 5. THE TEST

A formal assessment was conducted.

The assessment covered:

- classical mechanics;
    
- electromagnetism;
    
- thermodynamics;
    
- wave mechanics;
    
- optics;
    
- relativity;
    
- astrophysics;
    
- fluid dynamics;
    
- mathematical modeling.
    

Jake's performance was initially comparable to Neko's.

Then the advanced sections began.

Jake's performance improved.

Neko's did not.

By the final section, Jake had demonstrated a stronger understanding of several advanced concepts.

The most significant difference was not memorization.

It was intuition.

Jake appeared to understand physical systems almost instinctively.

---

# 6. THE STAR PROBLEM

The explanation for this remains under investigation.

Jake is not a conventional child.

He is a literal star manifested into human form.

His underlying existence is fundamentally astronomical.

Consequently, concepts involving:

- stellar physics;
    
- energy;
    
- gravity;
    
- radiation;
    
- mass;
    
- pressure;
    
- fusion;
    
- orbital mechanics;
    

appear unusually intuitive to him.

He does not necessarily know the terminology immediately.

But when presented with the physical problem itself, he often understands what should happen.

Neri summarized the phenomenon simply:

> **NERI:**  
> "He knows what stars do."

---

# 7. NEKO'S RESPONSE

Neko reviewed the results.

He remained silent for approximately thirty seconds.

Then:

> **NEKO:**  
> "So."

Pause.

> **NEKO:**  
> "I've been beaten at physics."

Neri nodded.

Neko looked at Jake.

Jake looked back.

Neko pointed at the book.

> **NEKO:**  
> "You accidentally picked up the wrong book."

Jake nodded.

> **NEKO:**  
> "And then you learned enough physics to beat me."

Jake hesitated.

Then nodded again.

Neko sighed.

> **NEKO:**  
> "...Of course you did."

---

# 8. THE FOURTH

C.O.R.E. records now identify **four residents** who have exceeded Neko in a specific field.

### NERI

**Field:** Chemistry

Neri's chemistry proficiency exceeds Neko's.

Her research output and theoretical understanding are exceptionally advanced.

### SORA

**Field:** Mathematics

Sora has demonstrated mathematical proficiency exceeding Neko's in several advanced areas.

### PHAETHON

**Field:** Mathematics

Phaethon independently demonstrated comparable mathematical superiority.

### JAKE

**Field:** Physics

Jake has now exceeded Neko's established proficiency in physics.

The distinction is important.

Neko remains exceptionally capable across engineering, research, technology, archival systems, and general scientific application.

Being surpassed in one field does not diminish his overall expertise.

It does, however, make the fourth occurrence increasingly funny.

---

# 9. NERI'S REACTION

Neri retrieved her physics book.

She looked at the annotations.

Several pages had been filled with Jake's handwriting.

She stared at him.

Jake looked apologetic.

Neri closed the book.

Then reopened it.

She pointed at a particularly complicated section.

Jake looked.

He wrote a short explanation beneath it.

Neri read it.

Pause.

She nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> "...You can keep reading."

Jake smiled.

Neri handed the book back.

---

# 10. JAKE'S CURRENT STATUS

Jake has not been instructed to continue intensive study.

His physical recovery remains the priority.

He is still:

- recovering from the Nullwalker incident;
    
- physically weak;
    
- easily fatigued;
    
- unable to speak normally;
    
- dependent on adequate rest and nutrition.
    

His physics proficiency does not change those medical considerations.

C.O.R.E. therefore recommends allowing Jake to study only when he chooses to do so.

If reading makes him tired:

**He rests.**

If he wants another book:

**Someone brings it to him.**

If he wants to stop:

**The lesson ends.**

---

# 11. ANBY'S OBSERVATION

Anby later entered the library.

She saw Jake reading.

She saw Neko sitting across from him.

She looked between them.

> **ANBY:**  
> "What happened?"

Neko pointed toward Jake.

> **NEKO:**  
> "He beat me at physics."

Anby stared.

Then looked at Jake.

Jake looked up from the book.

Anby smiled.

> **ANBY:**  
> "Good job."

Jake's smile widened slightly.

He quietly returned to reading.

Neko looked at the book.

Then at Jake.

> **NEKO:**  
> "Please stop accidentally becoming smarter than me."

Jake looked at him.

After a moment, he shook his head.

Anby laughed.

Neko sighed.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Jake's achievement is unusual but not entirely unexpected.

He is a literal stellar entity whose human manifestation provides him with a form capable of interacting with conventional knowledge.

Physics is therefore an unusually compatible field for his natural understanding.

The most remarkable part of the incident is not that Jake surpassed Neko.

It is how casually it happened.

Neri had a physics book.

Jake wanted something to read.

He picked it up.

He read it.

And somewhere along the way, C.O.R.E. acquired its **fourth recorded instance of someone beating Neko at something.**

No challenge was issued.

No competition occurred.

There was not even an intention to study.

Jake simply grabbed the wrong book.
