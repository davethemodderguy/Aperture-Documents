# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CRITICAL INCIDENT / PERSONNEL LOSS REPORT**

# **CORIN — FINAL INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Loss / Internal Review  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Associated Personnel:** Orphie / Lumi / Arcturus / Norma / SAFEHOUSE Residents / KCA  
**Location:** C.O.R.E. Containment Wing  
**Status:** **DECEASED — BODY PRESERVED IN CRYOSTASIS**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin was found deceased inside her containment cell the following morning.

Medical personnel immediately initiated emergency preservation procedures.

Despite all intervention, Corin could not be revived.

Her body was subsequently placed into **cryogenic suspension** pending further investigation and determination of long-term preservation status.

No revival procedure is currently authorized.

---

# **THE DISCOVERY**

The morning inspection initially appeared routine.

The observation system showed Corin lying motionless.

Medical personnel entered the cell.

Within seconds, the room was placed under emergency protocol.

The corridor was sealed.

Personnel were removed from the area.

The SAFEHOUSE was notified.

---

# **THE NEWS**

Lumi was the first family member informed.

She did not respond for several seconds.

> "...No."

The medical officer repeated the statement.

> "...I'm sorry."

Lumi shook her head.

> "...No."

Arcturus arrived shortly afterward.

He did not speak.

Norma simply stood beside him.

Nobody knew what to say.

---

# **CRYOSTASIS**

C.O.R.E. preserved Corin's body in cryogenic suspension.

The procedure was not performed with the intention of reversing her death.

It was performed because Corin was family.

Because her death required investigation.

And because nobody was prepared to permanently dispose of her remains while the circumstances surrounding her death remained unresolved.

Her cryogenic chamber was subsequently placed under restricted access.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie was informed separately.

At first, she showed almost no reaction.

> "...She's dead?"

The medical officer nodded.

> "...Yes."

Silence.

Orphie looked toward the floor.

> "...I see."

She began walking away.

Then stopped.

---

# **THE REALIZATION**

For the first time since her emotional withdrawal began, Orphie began reconstructing everything.

Not just Corin's final days.

Everything.

The disappearance.

The two weeks.

Corin waiting.

Her return.

The scolding.

The rejection.

The hatred.

The conversations.

The sleeping arrangements.

The things she had said when Corin wasn't supposed to hear.

And finally:

> **"I deeply hate her."**

Orphie remembered saying it.

She remembered meaning it.

She remembered believing Corin was simply making herself everyone else's problem.

Now there was no argument left.

No response.

No second chance to explain.

---

# **THE CONTRADICTION**

Orphie had spent months believing:

> **"I don't matter."**

She had believed that removing herself from everyone's lives would make things easier.

She had believed Corin's suffering was Corin's responsibility.

She had believed her own actions were separate from the consequences.

Now she finally understood.

Not everything was her fault.

Corin had her own struggles.

Corin had been vulnerable long before Orphie's disappearance.

There were circumstances Orphie could never have controlled.

But Orphie could no longer deny her own part.

She had disappeared.

She had watched Corin deteriorate.

She had scolded her.

She had rejected her.

She had told her she was hated.

And when Corin was at her most vulnerable...

**Orphie had given her one more reason to believe she was unwanted.**

---

# **ORPHIE'S BREAK**

Orphie returned to the treehouse.

She stood beside the bed.

Her usual expression was gone.

She looked at the empty space where Corin had once been.

> "...You weren't supposed to die."

Silence.

> "...I was angry."

Her voice began trembling.

> "...I didn't mean for this."

She sat on the floor.

The same floor where she had been sleeping to avoid Corin.

She stared at the opposite side of the room.

> "...You were supposed to get better."

Nothing answered.

---

# **THE EMPTY CHAIR**

Orphie looked at the chair.

For weeks, Corin had stared at it.

Now Orphie understood why.

She slowly sat down.

> "...I hated you."

A pause.

> "...I really did."

Her voice broke.

> "...Why did I say that?"

No answer.

> "...Why did I say that where you could hear me?"

She covered her face.

For the first time in a very long time...

Orphie cried.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi found Orphie sitting there.

She did not yell.

She did not blame her.

She simply looked at her daughter.

Orphie whispered:

> "...Mom."

Lumi sat beside her.

> "...I did this."

Lumi answered quietly:

> "...You were part of what happened."

Orphie looked at her.

> "...That's worse."

> "...Yes."

A long silence.

> "...But Corin was hurting long before any one person could fix her."

Orphie began crying harder.

> "...I should've cared."

Lumi:

> "...You should have."

---

# **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus could barely look at Orphie.

Not because he hated her.

Because he was grieving.

> "...She's gone."

Orphie nodded.

> "...I know."

> "...And you're going to have to live with that."

Orphie looked down.

> "...I know."

---

# **NORMA**

Norma remained outside the room.

She was angry.

Heartbroken.

Exhausted.

But she understood something important.

Blame would not bring Corin back.

Neither would hatred.

Neither would pretending Orphie had caused everything.

The truth was more complicated.

And therefore harder to live with.

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts eventually gathered outside the treehouse.

They did not enter.

They simply sat together.

Their tiny top hats were unusually still.

One Mart whispered:

> "...Corin gone."

Another:

> "...Yes."

A third:

> "...Sad."

Nobody moved.

---

# **THE KCA**

The Krampus Compliance Authority was formally notified.

Corin's position as Lead Accountant was closed following confirmation of her death.

Her personnel records were sealed.

Her work was redistributed.

No replacement was immediately appointed.

The KCA left her office untouched for the time being.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie finally understood the full weight of what she had done.

Not because someone explained it.

Not because someone blamed her.

Because Corin was no longer there to answer.

Orphie had spent so long believing that she was insignificant.

That she was an orphan.

That she was a burden.

That nobody needed her.

She never understood that, to Corin, she had been the opposite.

She had been important.

Perhaps too important.

And now Corin was gone.

The person Orphie had spent months trying to push away...

was the person she could never push away again.

Orphie remained sitting on the floor until morning.

For the first time, she no longer wanted to be alone.

There was simply nobody left to sit beside her.

**End of Critical Incident / Personnel Loss Report**
