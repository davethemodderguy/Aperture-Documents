# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CRITICAL INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – RETURN TO KCA HEADQUARTERS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Approximately forty-eight hours after leaving Project SAFEHOUSE, Corin Lockwood was confirmed to have returned to KCA Headquarters.

She did not notify anyone of her arrival.

She immediately resumed work.

Accounting output increased dramatically.

Communication with other personnel ceased almost entirely.

---

# **OBSERVED BEHAVIOR**

Security footage shows Corin entering her office carrying several boxes of unfinished financial reports.

Within minutes she initiated full office lockdown.

All doors engaged company security locks.

Visitor access was denied.

Only completed paperwork emerged from beneath the office door.

Meals delivered by staff were often left untouched for hours before being collected.

Corin spoke only through the intercom.

Her answers were brief.

Professional.

Emotionally distant.

---

# **WORK PATTERN**

Corin worked for extended periods without meaningful breaks.

Expense reports.

Payroll.

Procurement.

Mission budgets.

Ammunition authorizations.

Every document was completed with flawless accuracy.

Medical Services noted a familiar pattern.

Work had once again become a refuge.

As long as she was calculating numbers...

She did not have to think about herself.

---

# **SECURITY DISCOVERY**

During a routine inventory audit, Security Division identified an item inside Corin's office that immediately raised concern.

Several **Anti-Ghost Devices** had been signed out from secure storage under legitimate accounting authorization.

The devices remained inactive.

No evidence suggested they had been deployed.

Their presence alone prompted an immediate review.

---

# **ASSESSMENT**

The devices posed no immediate operational danger while inactive.

However, their presence was considered deeply concerning because Lumia Lockwood, an Ether-stabilized ghost, had become one of Corin's primary sources of emotional support.

Medical Services concluded that Corin's possession of the equipment likely reflected a worsening psychological state rather than any operational requirement.

No attempt was made to forcibly enter the office.

Psychology Division advised that doing so would likely escalate the situation.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi stood outside the locked office door.

She knew Corin could hear her.

She did not ask the door to be opened.

She simply spoke through it.

> "Corin..."

Silence.

> "I'm not here to argue."

Silence.

> "I'm not here to make you come back."

Several more seconds passed.

Then Lumi quietly continued.

> "I'm just here because you're my daughter."

Nothing came from the other side except the faint sound of papers turning.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle arrived later that evening carrying Corin's favorite tea.

She placed the thermos outside the office door.

She did not knock.

Attached to it was a handwritten note.

> "For when you remember to take a break."

When Belle returned the next morning, the thermos was empty.

The note had been carefully folded and placed on top of it.

No reply was written.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin remains inside her office at KCA Headquarters, continuing her accounting duties while refusing nearly all direct contact.

Medical Services have classified the situation as a high-priority psychological concern.

Personnel have been instructed to maintain regular, non-confrontational contact, ensure Corin has access to food, medication, and medical care, and avoid actions that could increase her isolation.

The locked office has become more than a workplace.

For Corin, it has become another place to hide from the people who care about her.

**End of Critical Incident Report**
