**SUBJECT:** Nora Tachyon  
**OBSERVATION:** Familiarity with Press-Check Procedure

---

# 1. SUMMARY

During a supervised weapons-handling observation, Nora demonstrated that she already understood the concept of a **press check**.

The observation was not intended as weapons training.

Nora simply demonstrated familiarity with the terminology and purpose of the check while discussing her personal Deadeye.

Her understanding appeared to come from previous exposure to firearm handling and her unusually strong interest in the equipment associated with the Tachyon family.

---

# 2. OBSERVATION

An MSAC instructor was discussing basic firearm safety with several personnel.

Nora was nearby.

The instructor mentioned confirming the weapon's condition before handling it further.

Nora immediately said:

> **NORA:**  
> “Press check.”

The instructor paused.

> **MSAC INSTRUCTOR:**  
> “You know what that is?”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “Yeah.”

> **MSAC INSTRUCTOR:**  
> “How?”

Nora pointed toward her Deadeye.

> **NORA:**  
> “I've done it before.”

The instructor looked toward Neri.

Neri looked back.

> **NERI:**  
> “She pays attention.”

---

# 3. KNOWLEDGE ASSESSMENT

Nora demonstrated conceptual understanding of the purpose of a press check:

> **Confirming the weapon's condition without assuming it is in the expected state.**

She also correctly understood the more important underlying principle:

> **Never assume a firearm is unloaded simply because someone says it is.**

When asked why she performs a check, Nora answered:

> **“Because checking is better than guessing.”**

The instructor documented the response as appropriate.

---

# 4. SAFETY EMPHASIS

C.O.R.E. notes that Nora's familiarity with the procedure does **not** constitute authorization for unsupervised weapons handling.

Nora is six years old.

Her unusual competence does not alter her age.

Her demonstrated marksmanship does not alter her age.

Her ability to understand firearm terminology does not alter her age.

Her Tachyon family authorization does not automatically constitute unrestricted MSAC authorization.

Any live-fire activity remains subject to established controlled-range procedures and direct adult supervision.

---

# 5. NORA'S ATTITUDE

The most notable aspect of the observation was that Nora did not consider the press check impressive.

She considered it obvious.

When asked whether she had learned it from Neri:

> **NORA:**  
> “No.”

> **MSAC INSTRUCTOR:**  
> “Elsher?”

> **NORA:**  
> “No.”

> **MSAC INSTRUCTOR:**  
> “Who taught you?”

Nora shrugged.

> **NORA:**  
> “I watched.”

The instructor paused.

> **MSAC INSTRUCTOR:**  
> “You just watched people?”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “And remembered.”

---

# 6. TACHYON OBSERVATION

Neri was later asked about Nora's ability.

**C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
“Did you teach her firearm handling?”

**NERI:**  
“Some.”

**C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
“Did you teach her the press check?”

Neri paused.

**NERI:**  
“Probably.”

**C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
“You don't remember?”

Neri shook her head.

**NERI:**

---

# 7. MSAC NOTE

The MSAC instructor submitted the following observation:

> _“Nora's knowledge is unusual for her age, but the concerning part would be treating that unusual knowledge as evidence that she no longer requires supervision.”_

This statement was accepted by C.O.R.E.

Nora's abilities are exceptional.

She remains six.

Those two facts are not contradictory.

---

Nora was later seen sitting beside Neri in the SAFEHOUSE.

For now.
