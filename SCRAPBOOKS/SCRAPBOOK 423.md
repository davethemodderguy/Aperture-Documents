### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / EMPLOYMENT STATUS REPORT**

# **CORIN — KCA TEMPORARY REMOVAL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Occupational Concern  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Associated Personnel:** Orphie / KCA / Lumi / Arcturus / Norma / SAFEHOUSE Residents  
**Status:** **TEMPORARILY REMOVED FROM DUTY**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie's return did **not** restore Corin's mental stability.

Although Corin had spent two weeks waiting for Orphie to return, her reunion with her sister failed to reverse the psychological deterioration that had already occurred.

Corin remains severely unstable.

As a result, she has been temporarily removed from her position as **Lead Accountant of the KCA — Krampus Compliance Authority**.

The decision was made as a welfare and operational-safety measure.

Corin is not considered fit to resume her full accounting responsibilities at this time.

---

# **KCA EMPLOYMENT STATUS**

**Position:** Lead Accountant  
**Organization:** Krampus Compliance Authority  
**Current Status:** **TEMPORARILY LAID OFF**

The KCA has placed Corin on temporary leave rather than permanently terminating her position.

Her employment remains intact.

Her responsibilities, however, have been reassigned until she is considered capable of reliably performing them.

---

# **THE PROBLEM**

Corin's deterioration has affected:

- Concentration.
    
- Sleep.
    
- Emotional regulation.
    
- Routine.
    
- Decision-making.
    
- Communication.
    
- Ability to maintain professional composure.
    

Even simple accounting work has become increasingly difficult.

Numbers that Corin once processed effortlessly now require repeated checking.

Reports remain unfinished.

Meetings have been missed.

Documents have been returned incomplete.

At one point, Corin reportedly stared at an accounting spreadsheet for nearly an hour without entering a single value.

---

# **THE DECISION**

The KCA attempted to accommodate her condition.

Eventually, however, continuing to place Corin in charge of financial operations became impractical.

Her temporary removal was formally approved.

When Corin was informed:

> "...You're taking my job."

The KCA representative corrected her:

> "Temporary leave."

Corin:

> "...Same thing."

> "It isn't."

> "...Feels like it."

---

# **ORPHIE'S REACTION**

Orphie was furious.

Not because Corin had been removed from the KCA.

Because she believed Corin had allowed herself to deteriorate to the point where it happened.

Her response was harsh.

> **Orphie:** "You lost your job."

> **Corin:** "...Temporarily."

> **Orphie:** "Because you couldn't keep yourself together."

Corin went silent.

Orphie continued.

> "...You knew this was happening."

> "...You knew you were getting worse."

> "...And you did nothing."

Corin looked down.

> "...I tried."

Orphie:

> "...Not enough."

---

# **THE SCOLDING**

Orphie began repeatedly scolding Corin.

She criticized her for:

- Not sleeping.
    
- Not eating properly.
    
- Missing work.
    
- Staying in the treehouse.
    
- Obsessing over her disappearance.
    
- Refusing help.
    
- Allowing herself to deteriorate.
    
- Neglecting responsibilities.
    

Her reasoning was not entirely wrong.

Her method was.

---

# **THE EFFECT**

Corin did not respond well.

Every criticism reinforced what she already believed about herself.

> **"I'm useless."**

> **"I'm a burden."**

> **"I ruined everything."**

> **"I couldn't even keep my job."**

Orphie's intention was to force Corin to regain control.

Instead, Corin became even more withdrawn.

---

# **A PARTICULARLY BAD CONVERSATION**

Orphie found Corin sitting at the kitchen table.

> "...Have you done anything today?"

Corin shook her head.

> "...No."

> "...Why?"

> "...I don't know."

Orphie sighed.

> "...You can't keep doing this."

Corin remained silent.

> "...You have responsibilities."

No response.

> "...People are depending on you."

Corin's hands tightened.

> "...I know."

> "...Then act like it."

Silence.

Corin eventually whispered:

> "...I'm trying."

Orphie:

> "...Then try harder."

Corin stood up and left.

Orphie did not follow.

---

# **ORPHIE'S MISJUDGMENT**

Orphie does not appear to understand how severely damaged Corin's emotional state currently is.

She sees a person who needs to regain control.

She does not see someone who is barely maintaining it.

Her emotional detachment also prevents her from recognizing the impact of her words until after the damage has already been done.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin's response has become increasingly concerning.

She no longer argues with Orphie.

She simply accepts the criticism.

> "...You're right."

> "...I'll do better."

> "...Sorry."

The statements are becoming automatic.

There is little evidence that they represent genuine recovery.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi eventually confronted Orphie.

> "...Stop."

Orphie looked at her.

> "...She's failing."

> "...She's sick."

> "...That doesn't excuse it."

Lumi's expression hardened.

> "...And your anger doesn't excuse hurting her."

Orphie said nothing.

Lumi continued:

> "...You were gone for two weeks."

> "...I know."

> "...She broke while you were gone."

A pause.

> "...I know."

> "...Then don't be the thing that keeps breaking her."

Orphie looked away.

---

# **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus was similarly concerned.

> "...You're making it worse."

Orphie:

> "...I'm telling her the truth."

> "...Truth doesn't have to be a weapon."

Orphie did not answer.

---

# **NORMA**

Norma was more direct.

> "...Corin doesn't need another person telling her she's failing."

> "...She needs someone to help her stand."

Orphie:

> "...She can't stay like this."

Norma:

> "...Neither of you can."

---

# **SAFEHOUSE**

The SAFEHOUSE has become increasingly careful around both sisters.

Everyone understands that Orphie's return did not restore the old status quo.

There are now **two** people in need of serious support.

Orphie remains emotionally detached.

Corin remains psychologically unstable.

Their interactions can easily worsen each other's condition.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Everyone expected Orphie's return to fix something.

It didn't.

Corin waited two weeks for her sister.

When Orphie finally came home, Corin was still broken.

And Orphie, unable to understand how deep the damage had gone, responded the only way she currently knew how:

By demanding that Corin become functional again.

She scolds her.

She criticizes her.

She tells her to try harder.

She tells her that people depend on her.

She tells her that she cannot keep living this way.

Every word contains a piece of truth.

And every word makes Corin sink a little further.

Orphie came back from the snow.

Corin never truly came back from what happened while she was gone.
