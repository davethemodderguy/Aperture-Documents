# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon bites Neri.

Frequently.

This behavior has become sufficiently routine that SAFEHOUSE personnel no longer consider every individual incident noteworthy.

The behavior is generally playful.

Neri generally tolerates it.

Nora's teeth are naturally sharp due to her Shark Thiren physiology.

However, Nora wears specially fitted **Invisalign-style protective braces** that cover the sharper portions of her teeth.

As a result, her bites generally do not cause significant pain or injury.

The sisters have consequently acquired an unofficial SAFEHOUSE designation:

> **THE SHARK AND SEAL DUO**

---

# 2. WHY NORA BITES NERI

There does not appear to be a single reason.

Nora may bite Neri when:

- she is excited;
    
- she wants attention;
    
- she is being playful;
    
- Neri is sitting still;
    
- Nora thinks Neri's seal tail looks biteable;
    
- Nora wants to annoy her sister;
    
- Neri says something embarrassing;
    
- Nora is being cheeky;
    
- or, occasionally, for no identifiable reason whatsoever.
    

Neri has stopped attempting to understand the reasoning.

---

# 3. THE TAIL REMAINS THE PRIMARY TARGET

Neri's seal tail is Nora's preferred target.

A typical interaction:

Neri is reading.

Nora walks behind her.

Nora looks at the tail.

Neri notices.

> **NERI:**  
> "Don't."

Nora pauses.

> **NORA:**  
> "I'm not doing anything."

Neri looks at her.

> **NERI:**  
> "You're looking at my tail."

> **NORA:**  
> "I'm allowed to look."

Pause.

**CHOMP.**

Neri's tail disappears from Nora's mouth.

> **NERI:**  
> "...Nora."

Nora smiles.

> **NORA:**  
> "Shark."

Neri sighs.

> **NERI:**  
> "Seal."

> **NORA:**  
> "Shark."

> **NERI:**  
> "Seal."

> **NORA:**  
> "Shark."

Neither appears interested in resolving the argument.

---

# 4. THE BRACES

Nora's sharp teeth are partially covered by specially fitted protective orthodontic appliances.

The design prevents the sharper portions of her teeth from making direct contact during ordinary playful biting.

This greatly reduces the likelihood of injury.

The braces do not remove Nora's natural teeth.

They simply cover the sharpest edges.

Nora therefore retains the ability to bite normally.

She simply does so with considerably less potential for accidental injury.

---

# 5. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri generally responds to biting with one of three reactions.

### RESPONSE A

She ignores it.

### RESPONSE B

She says:

> **"Nora."**

### RESPONSE C

She gently moves Nora away.

The third response is more common when Nora bites somewhere inconvenient.

Neri does not generally become angry.

She has apparently accepted that having a six-year-old Shark Thiren as a little sister comes with certain unavoidable occupational hazards.

---

# 6. EXAMPLE — THE SLEEVE

Neri was working at her computer.

Nora approached.

She looked at Neri's sleeve.

Then at Neri.

Then back at the sleeve.

Neri continued typing.

Nora bit the sleeve.

Neri stopped typing.

> **NERI:**  
> "...Why?"

Nora released it.

> **NORA:**  
> "Because."

> **NERI:**  
> "That's not an answer."

> **NORA:**  
> "It's a reason."

Neri stared at her.

> **NERI:**  
> "...Go play with Alice."

Nora immediately left.

---

# 7. EXAMPLE — THE HEAD

On another occasion, Nora attempted to bite Neri's hair.

Neri turned around.

> **NERI:**  
> "You can't bite my hair."

Nora considered this.

> **NORA:**  
> "Why?"

> **NERI:**  
> "Because it's hair."

Nora thought about it.

> **NORA:**  
> "...Okay."

Three seconds later:

> **NORA:**  
> "Can I bite your tail?"

Neri closed her eyes.

> **NERI:**  
> "You were going to anyway."

> **NORA:**  
> "Yep."

---

# 8. SAFEHOUSE TERMINOLOGY

The phrase **"Shark and Seal Duo"** originated with Nora.

She announced it one afternoon while standing beside Neri.

> **NORA:**  
> "We're Shark and Seal."

Neri looked at her.

> **NERI:**  
> "Why?"

> **NORA:**  
> "Because I'm a shark."

She pointed at Neri.

> **NORA:**  
> "And you're a seal."

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> "That's technically correct."

Nora smiled.

> **NORA:**  
> "Shark and Seal!"

The name spread immediately.

---

# 10. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Although the behavior is generally harmless, personnel are reminded that:

**Nora is not permitted to bite people who do not consent to playful contact.**

Her braces significantly reduce risk.

They do not eliminate it.

If Neri indicates that she wants Nora to stop, Nora is expected to stop.

In practice, Nora generally does.

Sometimes she waits several minutes.

Then tries again.

---

# 11. THE SISTERLY PATTERN

The biting appears to have become one of the ways Nora expresses familiarity with Neri.

It is not aggression.

It is not an attempt to injure.

It is simply one of the strange, physical ways a very energetic six-year-old interacts with the older sister she recently got back.

Neri, for her part, appears to have accepted it as part of having Nora around.

There is no indication that Neri wants the behavior eliminated entirely.

She simply wants Nora to stop biting her when she is trying to read.

This distinction remains difficult for Nora.
