### SECURITY, SEARCH & RESIDENTIAL WELFARE DIVISION

**Status:** OPEN – JAKE AKANA UNACCOUNTED FOR

**Incident:** Unauthorized Departure During Ongoing Search Operation

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake Akana has gone missing.

Following prolonged emotional distress concerning Sora's disappearance, Jake left SAFEHOUSE without authorization.

Initial investigation indicates that his departure was deliberate.

**Jake went looking for Sora.**

The circumstances suggest that Jake could no longer tolerate remaining at SAFEHOUSE while his friend remained missing.

His decision appears emotionally motivated rather than related to any known external threat.

This development has immediately escalated the existing search operation.

C.O.R.E. is now attempting to locate two missing children.

---

## 2. CIRCUMSTANCES OF DEPARTURE

Jake had spent considerable time crying over Sora's history and the apparent breakdown of their relationship.

Despite reassurance from Aries, Tom, Clover, Phoenix, and Bao, he continued struggling to understand why Sora had run away.

The explanations provided by the adults did not resolve his distress.

At some point, Jake left.

He did not wait for an organized search party.

He did not request permission.

He did not establish a search plan with SAFEHOUSE residents.

He simply went looking for his friend.

Personnel must consider the possibility that Jake believes he can personally reach Sora in a way that the previous search teams could not.

He may also believe that Sora is still frightened and needs someone familiar to find him.

---

## 3. IMMEDIATE CONCERNS

Jake's departure introduces several risks.

**Environmental exposure:** Sora was last found shivering beneath a tree in the snow. Jake may have entered the same environment without adequate preparation.

**Physical safety:** Jake is a young child. His anomalous abilities do not eliminate the dangers of exposure, exhaustion, injury, or becoming lost.

**Emotional distress:** Jake may be unwilling to return without Sora.

**Encounter risk:** If Jake locates Sora, the latter may flee again. A frightened Sora and a distressed Jake could unintentionally push each other into greater danger.

The search team must therefore avoid treating the two children as a single recovery target requiring immediate physical reunion.

Their safety must be assessed independently.

---

## 4. RESPONSE FROM SAFEHOUSE RESIDENTS

The disappearance has deeply alarmed the residents previously concerned about Jake.

**Aries:** Jake's eldest brother. Immediate priority is locating his youngest sibling and ensuring he does not enter dangerous terrain.

**Tom:** Jake's older brother. Must be included in the recovery effort and kept informed of confirmed developments.

**Clover:** To assist with residential coordination and communication.

**Phoenix:** To assist with the search and emergency response.

**Bao:** To be available for both children's recovery and subsequent care.

No resident is to blame themselves for Jake's departure.

However, personnel must recognize that Jake's emotional distress was severe and that his need to find Sora was not adequately relieved by verbal reassurance alone.

---

## 5. SEARCH DIRECTIVES

1. Initiate an immediate search for Jake.
    
2. Expand the existing Sora search to account for the possibility that Jake is following his last known route.
    
3. Maintain communication between all search teams.
    
4. Do not send Jake into hazardous terrain if his location is identified.
    
5. If Sora is found first, personnel must avoid frightening or cornering him.
    
6. If Jake is found first, personnel must prioritize his warmth, hydration, and physical condition.
    
7. If both are located together, allow them to remain within sight of one another if medically and operationally safe.
    
8. Do not reprimand Jake during initial recovery.
    
9. Do not make Sora feel responsible for Jake's disappearance.
    

**Critical instruction:** Neither child should be made to believe that the other child's safety is their personal responsibility.

---

## 6. C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake didn't understand.

We told him Sora was frightened. We told him Sora had suffered. We told him that none of this was his fault.

But Jake is six.

He doesn't understand why someone who used to stay beside him would run away.

He doesn't understand why the letters, the visits, and the little den weren't enough to keep Sora from being afraid.

And now he has decided to find him himself.

Perhaps he thinks Sora is somewhere cold.

Perhaps he thinks Sora is crying.

Perhaps he remembers how Sora used to hide, and believes that if he looks hard enough, he will find him again.

We cannot know exactly what Jake is thinking.

But we know what he is doing.

He is looking for his friend.

There is a painful irony here.

Sora spent months hiding because he was afraid of being found.

Jake has now disappeared because he cannot bear not finding Sora.

Two little boys, both frightened, both missing each other, somewhere beyond the safety of their home.

**Please find Jake before he gets lost looking for someone who is already lost.**

And if the two of them are found together, do not separate them unnecessarily.

Let the first thing Sora sees be someone familiar.

Let the first thing Jake hears be that his friend is safe.

For once, let the two children come home together.
