## 1. SUMMARY

On ██/██/████, Z-356-B **"Bao"** departed the SAFEHOUSE carrying a sealed biological sample containing her own DNA.

The sample was intended for delivery to **Neko** for routine comparative and anomalous-genetics analysis.

Bao did not reach the laboratory.

At approximately 14:██ local time, Bao reported that the sample container had been lost somewhere within the **Outer Perimeter** while she was returning toward the Blacksite.

The loss was initially considered a minor logistical incident.

It was not.

Despite an extensive search, the original sample was never recovered.

The circumstances surrounding its disappearance remain unresolved.

---

# 2. PURPOSE OF THE SAMPLE

The sample had been collected from Bao for routine analysis following several irregularities involving her physiology.

The stated objectives were:

1. Establish a baseline genetic profile for Z-356-B.
    
2. Compare Bao's genetic structure against existing Thiren samples.
    
3. Identify possible anomalous markers associated with her hydrological physiology.
    
4. Determine whether any portion of Bao's genome exhibited nonstandard integration with anomalous traits.
    
5. Maintain a reference sample for future medical and biological comparisons.
    

Nothing in the request indicated that the sample was expected to be dangerous.

The sample itself was contained in a standard C.O.R.E. biological transport vial.

**CHAIN OF CUSTODY:** Bao → Neko  
**STATUS:** BROKEN  
**FINAL CONFIRMED HOLDER:** Bao  
**FINAL CONFIRMED LOCATION:** Outer Perimeter  
**RECOVERY:** UNSUCCESSFUL

# 3. BAO'S ACCOUNT

Bao's initial report was recorded by a nearby perimeter officer.

> **BAO:** "I had the little tube with me."

> **OFFICER:** "The DNA sample?"

> **BAO:** "Yeah."

> **OFFICER:** "Where did you put it?"

> **BAO:** "In my coat pocket."

> **OFFICER:** "And then?"

> **BAO:** "I remember taking the perimeter path. I remember stopping because I thought I heard something outside the fence."

> **OFFICER:** "Did you remove the container?"

> **BAO:** "No."

> **OFFICER:** "Did you drop anything?"

> **BAO:** "I don't think so."

> **OFFICER:** "Then how did it disappear?"

> **BAO:** "...That's what I'm trying to figure out."

Bao subsequently retraced her route.

The sample could not be found.

---

# 4. SEARCH OPERATION

C.O.R.E. personnel initiated a localized search covering:

- Bao's confirmed route.
    
- Perimeter walkways.
    
- Drainage channels.
    
- Vegetation surrounding the path.
    
- Security checkpoints.
    
- Waste collection areas.
    
- Nearby anomalous activity zones.
    
- All locations where Bao had stopped.
    
- Approximately ██ meters beyond the point of last confirmed possession.
    

No intact vial was discovered.

No fragments of the container were discovered.

No evidence of deliberate tampering was identified.

No unauthorized individual was observed entering the relevant perimeter section.

Security footage was subsequently reviewed.

The footage produced an additional problem.

---

# 7. SEARCH FINDINGS

The search team eventually discovered a small depression in the soil approximately ██ meters from Bao's reported route.

The area contained:

- Disturbed grass.
    
- A small quantity of water.
    
- Trace anomalous residue.
    
- No biological container.
    
- No glass or polymer fragments.
    
- No evidence of impact.
    

The water sample was collected for analysis.

Results were inconclusive.

The residue was determined to be **biologically compatible with multiple Thiren and anomalous sources** and therefore could not conclusively identify the missing sample.

The investigation was subsequently suspended.

---

# 8. DISPOSITION

The incident was initially classified as:

> **LOST PROPERTY — BIOLOGICAL SAMPLE**

After review of the security footage, it was upgraded to:

> **UNEXPLAINED BIOLOGICAL MATERIAL DISAPPEARANCE**

No evidence currently supports the conclusion that:

- Bao intentionally discarded the sample.
    
- Neko received the sample.
    
- The sample was stolen by an identified individual.
    
- The sample was destroyed.
    
- The sample was consumed by an entity.
    
- The sample entered the SAFEHOUSE.
    
- The sample was transferred through a NOST rift.
    

The sample's ultimate location remains unknown.

---

# 9. SUBSEQUENT RELEVANCE

**ARCHIVAL NOTE — ADDED YEARS LATER**

The incident was largely forgotten until the medical examination of **Neri**, a 10-year-old Seal Thiren.

Genetic analysis performed after Neri's rescue identified **faint traces of Z-356-B Bao's DNA within Neri's biological system.**

The traces appeared to have become present **after Neri's transformation into a Thiren.**

Further investigation established that Bao's later blood donations to Neri **could not have introduced the genetic material.**

> **BLOOD DONATION AS CAUSE:** RULED OUT.

This produced an unresolved chronological anomaly.

A previous Bao biological sample had disappeared within the Outer Perimeter.

Years later, Bao's genetic material was detected inside an entirely different individual who had undergone an anomalous Thiren transformation.

No direct connection has been established.

However, the two events are now cross-referenced.

# 12. FINAL ARCHIVAL REMARK

The missing sample was never recovered.

At the time, the incident was embarrassing, inconvenient, and ultimately considered unimportant.

In retrospect, it represents one of several unresolved biological anomalies surrounding Neri's transformation.

There is currently **no evidence proving that the lost sample and Neri's later genetic anomaly are connected.**

There is also no evidence proving that they are unrelated.

Accordingly, C.O.R.E. Archives has retained the incident under active cross-reference.
