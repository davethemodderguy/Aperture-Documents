# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At 09:14, four SAFEHOUSE residents became cats.

Neko.

Miss Neko.

Endmin Merryweather.

Jake.

All four transformations were confirmed to be stable, voluntary where applicable, and non-life-threatening at the time of observation.

The resulting situation would ordinarily have been considered a manageable anomalous incident.

Unfortunately, Phoenix and Anby were present.

Phoenix and Anby are widely recognized as two of the SAFEHOUSE's most enthusiastic cat lovers.

Upon discovering that four of their friends had become cats simultaneously, both individuals experienced overwhelming emotional excitement.

Both fainted on the spot.

Medical personnel confirmed that neither suffered significant injury from the fall.

Both were subsequently placed under observation.

Both regained consciousness.

Both immediately attempted to return to the cats.

Medical personnel intervened.

The four feline residents were placed in a comfortable, supervised common area while Phoenix and Anby recovered.

No resident was required to perform their normal duties for the remainder of the day.

This decision was made for the benefit of the transformed residents.

It was also made because C.O.R.E. determined that allowing Phoenix and Anby to resume normal activities while four cats were present would be operationally impossible.

# 1. INITIAL TRANSFORMATION

**TIME:** 09:14:03

The first confirmed transformation occurred in the Research, Development and Engineering workspace.

Neko had been reviewing a maintenance report.

Miss Neko was standing beside his desk.

Endmin was visiting to discuss an unrelated administrative matter.

Jake had arrived earlier and was sitting on a chair, grooming his hands.

At 09:14:03, the room's lighting flickered once.

A faint blue shimmer passed across the floor.

Four separate transformation events were recorded.

At 09:14:05, the room contained four cats.

No humans remained in their previous positions.

The maintenance report lay on the desk.

A pen rolled onto the floor.

A small white cat sat beside the chair.

A second cat stood on the desk.

A third cat was investigating the fallen pen.

A fourth cat had apparently decided that the maintenance report was a suitable place to sit.

For approximately six seconds, no one in the room moved.

Then Neko meowed.

Miss Neko meowed in response.

Endmin sneezed.

Jake sat down.

The incident had officially begun.

# 3. PHOENIX AND ANBY

**TIME:** 09:16:41

Phoenix arrived in the workspace first.

She had been looking for Jake.

She opened the door.

She saw four cats.

She stopped.

Her eyes widened.

She took one step forward.

**PHOENIX:**

"..."

Miss Neko looked at her.

Neko looked at her.

Endmin sat down.

Jake blinked.

Phoenix's hands began shaking.

**PHOENIX:**

"Four."

A pause.

**PHOENIX:**

"THERE ARE FOUR."

She inhaled sharply.

**PHOENIX:**

"FOUR CATS."

At this point, Anby entered behind her.

**ANBY:**

"Phoenix, why are you—"

Anby looked into the room.

She saw the cats.

She stopped speaking.

Her expression remained neutral for approximately one second.

Then her eyes widened.

**ANBY:**

"...Four cats."

Phoenix turned toward her.

Anby turned toward Phoenix.

Neither spoke.

The four cats continued existing.

Neko's tail moved once.

Miss Neko began washing a paw.

Endmin chased the pen.

Jake yawned.

Phoenix made a sound that was later described by witnesses as _the last coherent noise produced by a human being who has seen too many cats at once._

Anby clutched Phoenix's sleeve.

**ANBY:**

"I can't—"

**PHOENIX:**

"I KNOW—"

Both collapsed.

They were unconscious before either could finish the sentence.

# 4. EMERGENCY RESPONSE

The incident initially triggered a medical response because two residents had suddenly collapsed.

Medical personnel arrived within two minutes.

Phoenix and Anby were found breathing normally, with no immediate evidence of traumatic injury.

Both were moved to a nearby medical room for assessment.

The preliminary diagnosis was acute emotional overwhelm with transient fainting.

No serious injury was identified.

Both individuals were monitored until they regained consciousness.

The first to wake was Anby.

She opened her eyes.

She looked at the ceiling.

She sat up.

**ANBY:**

"The cats."

The attending medic looked at her.

**MEDIC:**

"You're awake."

**ANBY:**

"The cats."

**MEDIC:**

"You fainted."

**ANBY:**

"I remember."

She attempted to stand.

The medic gently stopped her.

**MEDIC:**

"You need to remain seated until we're satisfied that you're stable."

Anby considered this.

**ANBY:**

"How long?"

**MEDIC:**

"A few more minutes."

Anby stared at the medic with the intensity of someone being denied access to the most important event in recorded history.

**ANBY:**

"...Understood."

She remained seated.

For approximately twelve seconds.

Then she asked:

**ANBY:**

"Can I see the cats from here?"

The medic closed their eyes.

---

# 5. PHOENIX REGAINS CONSCIOUSNESS

Phoenix woke approximately four minutes later.

Her first action was to sit up.

Her second action was to ask whether the cats were still present.

Her third action was to attempt to get out of bed.

Her fourth action was to be gently but firmly reminded that she had just fainted.

**PHOENIX:**

"But there are four."

**MEDIC:**

"We know."

**PHOENIX:**

"Four."

**MEDIC:**

"Yes."

**PHOENIX:**

"Four different cats."

**MEDIC:**

"Yes."

Phoenix covered her face with both hands.

Her shoulders began shaking.

**MEDIC:**

"Phoenix?"

**PHOENIX:**

"I'm trying to be normal."

**MEDIC:**

"Please continue trying."

A pause.

**PHOENIX:**

"I can't promise anything."

The medical team subsequently determined that Phoenix and Anby should not be permitted to approach the cats until they had fully recovered from their fainting episodes.

This restriction was not a punishment.

It was a sensible precaution.

Neither individual was considered capable of making a reliable assessment of their own physical limits while experiencing overwhelming excitement.

---

# 6. THE FOUR CATS ARE GIVEN THE DAY OFF

At 09:45, C.O.R.E. convened a brief internal discussion.

The immediate questions were:

- Were the transformations dangerous?
    
- Were the subjects in distress?
    
- Did any subject require urgent medical intervention?
    
- Could the transformations be reversed?
    
- Did the subjects wish to be left alone?
    

The preliminary findings indicated that all four cats were alert and behaving normally for their current forms.

No immediate distress was observed.

No urgent intervention was required.

The cause of the transformations remained unknown.

Most importantly, none of the four subjects demonstrated an interest in returning to work.

Neko had fallen asleep on a folded engineering jacket.

Miss Neko was resting on a cushion.

Endmin had discovered that the corner of a blanket could be attacked repeatedly without consequences.

Jake was loafing beside the window.

C.O.R.E. therefore issued the following temporary directive:

> **ALL FOUR SUBJECTS ARE RELIEVED OF THEIR NORMAL DUTIES FOR THE REMAINDER OF THE DAY.**

This applied to:

- Engineering and Archives;
    
- Engineering advisory responsibilities;
    
- CED administrative work;
    
- And any other routine obligations that might otherwise have required the subjects to work while transformed.
    

No attempt would be made to compel productivity.

No subject would be required to participate in interviews unless medically necessary.

No subject would be expected to explain the transformation before they were ready.

The directive was subsequently expanded.

**Phoenix and Anby were also relieved of their normal duties for the remainder of the day.**

This was necessary because neither individual was expected to be productive while four cats were available for observation.

C.O.R.E. considers this an appropriate use of personnel leave.

---

# 7. THE CAT ROOM

A comfortable room was prepared for the four transformed residents.

It contained:

- Several clean blankets;
    
- Soft cushions;
    
- Fresh water;
    
- Appropriate food;
    
- Safe resting surfaces;
    
- A selection of nonhazardous toys;
    
- And sufficient space for each cat to retreat from the others.
    

The room was not designated a containment area.

It was designated a **voluntary resting area**.

The distinction was important.

The cats were free to leave.

They were not confined.

Their medical needs remained subject to appropriate monitoring, but no one attempted to turn the situation into an experiment.

### Observed activity

Neko inspected the room.

Miss Neko selected a cushion.

Endmin discovered a toy and began batting it around.

Jake walked to the window, examined the sunlight, and lay down.

Approximately five minutes later, Neko joined him.

Miss Neko eventually settled beside Neko.

Endmin abandoned the toy and curled up on a blanket.

By 10:12, all four cats were resting.

No productivity was recorded.

No productivity was requested.

C.O.R.E. considered this an excellent outcome.

---

# 8. THE PHOENIX AND ANBY VISITATION PROTOCOL

Once medically cleared, Phoenix and Anby were permitted to visit the cat room.

They were given several instructions.

1. Approach calmly.
    
2. Do not shout.
    
3. Do not surround the cats.
    
4. Allow each cat to approach voluntarily.
    
5. Do not pick up any cat without permission or clear tolerance.
    
6. Do not wake sleeping cats unnecessarily.
    
7. Do not treat the incident as an excuse to ignore individual boundaries.
    
8. If either visitor feels faint, they must sit down immediately.
    

Phoenix read the instructions carefully.

Anby read them twice.

**PHOENIX:**

"Understood."

**ANBY:**

"Understood."

The attending medic looked between them.

**MEDIC:**

"Are you certain?"

**PHOENIX:**

"Yes."

**ANBY:**

"Yes."

They entered the room.

Neko was sitting on a cushion.

Miss Neko was grooming herself.

Endmin was playing with the toy.

Jake was asleep.

Phoenix stopped in the doorway.

Anby stopped beside her.

Both remained silent.

Phoenix slowly sat down.

Anby sat beside her.

For nearly a full minute, neither moved.

Then Jake woke up.

He stretched.

He walked toward Phoenix.

He sniffed her hand.

Then he rubbed his cheek against her fingers.

Phoenix's eyes filled with tears.

Anby's hands rose to her mouth.

**PHOENIX, WHISPERING:**

"Hi, Jake."

Jake meowed.

He climbed into her lap.

Phoenix's entire expression softened.

She gently stroked his back.

Anby looked at Neko.

Neko looked at Anby.

After several seconds, Neko walked over and sat beside her.

Anby offered a hand.

Neko sniffed it.

Then he leaned against her fingers.

Anby went completely still.

**ANBY, WHISPERING:**

"...He's warm."

Phoenix nodded without looking away from Jake.

**PHOENIX:**

"I know."

Miss Neko approached next.

She inspected Phoenix's shoes, then climbed onto the cushion beside her.

Endmin remained occupied with the toy for another several minutes before deciding that Anby's lap was a better place to rest.

She climbed up, turned around, and settled down.

Anby looked down at her.

Then at Phoenix.

Then at the other cats.

Her eyes began to water.

**ANBY:**

"Phoenix."

**PHOENIX:**

"Yes?"

**ANBY:**

"I think I'm going to faint again."

Phoenix immediately pointed toward the cushion beside her.

**PHOENIX:**

"Sit down."

Anby was already sitting.

She lay down instead.

Endmin remained on her lap.

Anby stared at the ceiling.

**ANBY:**

"...Worth it."

The medic supervising the visit made a note to continue monitoring both visitors.

# 9. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION — THEY ARE STILL PEOPLE

Despite the overwhelming cuteness of the incident, C.O.R.E. emphasizes that all four transformed residents retain their individual identities.

Neko is still Neko.

Miss Neko is still Miss Neko.

Endmin is still Endmin.

Jake is still Jake.

Their current feline forms do not remove their autonomy, personal histories, relationships, or established medical needs.

They are not property.

They are not laboratory animals.

They are not required to tolerate handling because others find them adorable.

The same applies to Phoenix and Anby.

Their enthusiasm is understandable, but their affection must remain respectful.

All four cats may choose whether to approach, accept attention, play, sleep, or leave.

The right to be a cat includes the right to decline being petted.

C.O.R.E. considers this distinction especially important for Jake, whose recent experiences have made control over his own circumstances a significant welfare concern.

He is permitted to enjoy being a cat without being reduced to one.

# 10. END-OF-DAY OBSERVATION

**TIME:** 17:30

The incident concluded without further medical emergencies.

The four cats spent most of the day resting, playing, grooming, and occupying comfortable surfaces.

Neko eventually slept beside Miss Neko.

Endmin alternated between playing with a toy and napping.

Jake spent a considerable amount of time near Phoenix.

Phoenix, after being medically cleared, remained in the room for most of the afternoon.

Anby stayed nearby, occasionally petting Neko or watching Endmin play.

Neither visitor resumed normal duties.

Neither appeared particularly interested in doing so.

At one point, Phoenix looked around the room.

Four cats.

Four separate personalities.

Four separate sets of ears.

Four tails.

Four individuals who had, for reasons still unknown, decided that today was an appropriate day to become cats.

Phoenix leaned back against the cushion.

Jake was asleep in her lap.

Neko was resting beside Anby.

Miss Neko was curled up on the other end of the blanket.

Endmin was batting a toy between her paws.

Phoenix closed her eyes.

**PHOENIX:**

"This is the best day of my life."

Anby, who was gently stroking Neko's back, nodded.

**ANBY:**

"Agreed."

A pause.

**ANBY:**

"We should probably be careful saying that."

**PHOENIX:**

"Why?"

**ANBY:**

"Because tomorrow there might be more cats."

Phoenix opened her eyes.

She slowly turned toward Anby.

Anby looked back.

Both considered the possibility.

Then Phoenix covered her mouth.

Anby lay down again.

The supervising medic immediately checked whether either was about to faint.

Neither did.

This was recorded as a significant improvement.

Four Cat Thirens.

Two fainting cat lovers.

One common room full of blankets.

No engineering deadlines.

No administrative obligations.

No CED paperwork.

No requirement to do anything productive.

For one day, the SAFEHOUSE became a place where four residents could simply rest in feline form, while two others experienced the greatest emotional challenge of their lives.

The cause of the transformations remains under investigation.

The medical status of all six individuals remains stable.

No subject was harmed.

No subject was forced to return to work.

And, for once, the incident did not end with an emergency requiring everyone to run.

It ended with four cats sleeping in the sunlight.

Phoenix quietly petting Jake.

Anby gently scratching Neko behind the ears.

Miss Neko curled up on a cushion.

Endmin playing until she grew tired.

The world continued outside the room.

The work could wait.

The reports could wait.

The explanations could wait.

**Today, they were all allowed to be cats.**

And Phoenix and Anby?

They were allowed to enjoy the greatest day of their lives.

Provided they remained conscious.
