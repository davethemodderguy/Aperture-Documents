### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY UPDATE**

# **CORIN — LUMINOUS HEMATOLOGICAL ALTERATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** Stable / Post-Recovery  
**Observed Alterations:** **Luminous Eyes, Wings, Veins / White Blood**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following Corin's recovery from mercury poisoning, C.O.R.E. medical personnel identified several previously undocumented physiological changes.

Corin's:

- **Eyes**
    
- **Wings**
    
- **Blood vessels**
    

now emit a visible glow in darkness.

Additionally, Corin's blood is no longer red.

It is now **white**.

---

# **LUMINOUS EYES**

Corin's eyes emit a faint glow in low-light environments.

The effect becomes considerably more noticeable in complete darkness.

The glow does not appear to impair her vision.

Corin has reported no discomfort associated with the alteration.

---

# **LUMINOUS WINGS**

The effect is particularly prominent in Corin's wings.

Individual feathers emit a soft luminescence.

The light becomes stronger when Corin actively uses her Ether.

When she spreads her wings in darkness, their silhouette becomes clearly visible.

The wings can therefore function as a surprisingly effective source of ambient illumination.

Corin has reportedly started using them as a flashlight.

---

# **LUMINOUS VEINS**

The blood vessels beneath Corin's skin are also visible in darkness.

They produce thin, glowing patterns following the natural structure of her circulatory system.

The effect is most obvious around her:

- Hands.
    
- Arms.
    
- Neck.
    
- Face.
    
- Legs.
    

Under sufficient darkness, Corin can appear almost internally illuminated.

---

# **WHITE BLOOD**

The most significant alteration concerns Corin's blood.

Her blood is now **white**.

Samples remain physically consistent with blood despite the unusual coloration.

The change is not superficial.

Corin's entire circulating blood supply has been altered.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

---

# **RELATION TO ETHER**

C.O.R.E. suspects that Corin's Ether is responsible for the alterations.

Her prolonged regenerative response may have permanently changed the interaction between her Ether and biological systems.

The current hypothesis is that the regeneration process effectively rebuilt portions of her physiology around an Ether-infused biological framework.

This remains unconfirmed.

---

# **POST-RECOVERY OBSERVATION**

The alterations were not present to this extent before Corin's poisoning.

They became apparent after her recovery.

This raises an important possibility:

> **Corin did not simply recover from the poisoning.**

> **Her body may have adapted to it.**

Whether the changes are permanent is currently unknown.

---

# **NIGHTTIME APPEARANCE**

Corin is now particularly noticeable in darkness.

A completely dark room may contain only:

- Two glowing eyes.
    
- A faint outline of wings.
    
- Thin luminous veins beneath her skin.
    

One SAFEHOUSE resident described the sight as:

> **"Equal parts angelic and mildly terrifying."**

Corin apparently found this amusing.

Corin survived the poisoning.

Her body repaired itself.

Her strength returned.

Her flight returned.

And then her biology apparently decided that normal human physiology was optional.
