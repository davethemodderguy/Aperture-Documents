# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **VISITOR LOG**

## **SUBJECT: FIRST VISIT – DIALYN**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the now-infamous customer support incident, arrangements were made for Dialyn to visit Heaven Blacksite in person.

For the first time since joining the Krampus Compliance Authority, another employee volunteered to temporarily manage Customer Support.

The volunteer reportedly stated:

> "Go."

> "You deserve a day off."

Dialyn accepted after confirming the phones would remain staffed.

It was her first day away from the Customer Support Office in several months.

---

# **ARRIVAL**

Dialyn arrived carrying only three items.

- Her shoulder bag.
    
- Her Portal Hoop.
    
- A box of homemade cookies "just in case."
    

Security screening proceeded without incident.

Upon entering SAFEHOUSE, she looked around the living room.

Then quietly laughed.

> "...So..."

> "...The prank call wasn't a prank."

---

# **THE RESIDENTS**

Belle introduced everyone one by one.

Star enthusiastically waved.

Nightshade gave a short nod.

Taiga was asleep on the rug.

Mart looked up from his book.

Bao offered tea before Dialyn had even sat down.

Astral greeted her like an old friend.

Frost quietly presented a small ice rose.

Keith and Cinder welcomed her to the Blacksite.

Neko smiled.

Miss Neko immediately apologized on Neko's behalf for the phone call.

Neko protested.

> "...I already apologized."

Miss Neko replied without looking at him.

> "Insufficiently."

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

Corin and Corey later offered to show Dialyn the treehouse.

After a lengthy walk through deep snow, the small wooden structure finally came into view.

Dialyn looked at it.

Then looked at Corin.

Then back at the treehouse.

> "...You actually live here."

Corin nodded.

> "...Yeah."

The large white house cat slowly emerged from beneath the stairs.

Dialyn instinctively froze.

The enormous feline calmly walked past her...

...and immediately leaned against Corin.

Corey scratched behind one ear.

> "...He's friendly."

Dialyn stared at the animal.

> "...That's not the part I'm questioning."

---

# **THE OWL**

As if on cue...

A white owl silently landed on a nearby branch.

It watched the group with enormous, curious eyes.

Dialyn smiled.

> "...You're adorable."

The owl immediately swooped down.

It grabbed one of the bread rolls sticking out of Dialyn's lunch bag.

Then flew back into the tree.

Dialyn blinked.

The owl happily began eating.

Corin quietly pointed upward.

> "...That's the potato thief."

Dialyn sighed.

> "...I don't even have potatoes."

---

# **THE CLOSET**

Endmin insisted that every first-time visitor should see "the closet."

Several minutes later...

Dialyn stood in front of the ten-floor extradimensional storage facility.

She slowly looked toward Endmin.

> "...This..."

She gestured at the elevators.

> "...Is still called a closet?"

Endmin nodded.

> "Yes."

Dialyn looked around the immense storage levels.

Then quietly muttered:

> "...I don't think any building here understands what words mean."

Dialyn looked around the SAFEHOUSE.

People talking.

Tea on the table.

Fresh cookies.

Snow falling outside.

Corin and Corey quietly discussing paperwork.

The tiger asleep near the fireplace.

The owl peeking through a window, still holding stolen bread.

She smiled.

> "...It feels like home."

The room became unusually quiet.

Lumi smiled first.

Belle smiled next.

Soon, nearly everyone else did as well.

---

# **DEPARTURE**

Before returning to the Krampus Compliance Authority, Dialyn stopped near the entrance.

She looked back toward the SAFEHOUSE.

> "...Now I understand why everyone stayed."

She gave one final wave.

> "...Take care of each other."

Corin and Corey answered together.

> "...We will."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Dialyn has returned to her duties at the Krampus Compliance Authority.

Her first visit to Heaven Blacksite concluded without incident.

She later updated the Customer Support knowledge base with one additional internal note.

> **"If someone reports a giant white cat, a potato-stealing owl, or an accountant living in a treehouse... ask for the location before assuming it's a joke."**

The note has proven unexpectedly useful.
