# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **BLACKSITE RECREATIONAL LOG**

### **FOLLOW-UP REPORT – "LEMONADE REDEMPTION"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Internal Recreation Archive

---

**Date:** One day after "The Lemon Incident."

**Location:** Project SAFEHOUSE Kitchen

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following widespread sympathy after Belle's voluntary consumption of an entire lemon, SAFEHOUSE residents unanimously agreed that she deserved something significantly less sour.

Endmin prepared a glass of fresh lemonade.

With sugar.

A lot of sugar.

---

## **EVENT LOG**

Belle entered the kitchen.

Endmin quietly slid a tall glass across the table.

A handwritten note was attached.

> **"This one is supposed to taste good."**

Belle blinked.

"...For me?"

Endmin nodded.

Belle took a sip.

Her rabbit ears immediately stood straight up.

Another sip.

A third.

She smiled.

> "...This is much better."

---

Taiga slowly looked away.

> "...Yeah..."

> "...Sorry about yesterday."

Belle laughed.

> "It's okay."

> "I accepted the challenge."
