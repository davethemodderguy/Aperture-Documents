# 1. CURRENT OBSERVATION

Nora has resumed working at the **Tachyon Merch Store**.

Her physical adaptation to cold has made the environment considerably easier for her to tolerate.

However, Nora has retained a new wardrobe preference.

She now routinely wears:

- a thick winter coat;
    
- a heavy scarf;
    
- insulated boots;
    
- a warm beanie;
    
- and her usual glasses.
    

Her previous white beret is currently being used less frequently.

The reason is simple.

Nora is cold.

Not because she is suffering from the cold.

Because **cold has become part of her normal physiology.**

She is comfortable at temperatures that would be unpleasant for most residents, but she still enjoys having herself wrapped up.

---

# 2. THE BEANIE

Nora's traditional white beret has been temporarily replaced by a thick beanie.

The beanie keeps her ears and head covered while allowing her long white hair to remain visible.

Nora appears to prefer it during Merch Store shifts.

Neko asked why.

Nora's response:

> **NORA:**  
> “Warm.”

Neko looked at the cold mist forming around her.

> **NEKO:**  
> “You know you're literally making the room colder, right?”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “Still warm.”

Neko decided not to argue.

---

# 3. FROST

Nora has also begun spending significantly more time with Frost.

The reason is obvious.

They have remarkably similar abilities.

Both can:

- manipulate cold;
    
- produce frost;
    
- interact with ice;
    
- function comfortably in temperatures that would be problematic for others;
    
- and unintentionally make nearby surfaces colder.
    

The difference is that Frost has considerably more experience controlling her abilities.

Nora is still learning.

This has resulted in Frost becoming an informal mentor.

---

# 4. MERCH STORE OBSERVATION

During one Merch Store shift, Frost visited Nora.

Nora was sitting behind the counter.

She was wearing:

- her thick coat;
    
- scarf;
    
- boots;
    
- beanie;
    
- glasses.
    

A small layer of frost had accumulated around her chair.

Frost looked at it.

> **FROST:**  
> “You're freezing the floor again.”

Nora looked down.

> **NORA:**  
> “Oh.”

She moved her foot.

The frost spread slightly farther.

Frost sighed.

> **FROST:**  
> “You're supposed to be learning control.”

> **NORA:**  
> “I am.”

> **FROST:**  
> “Then stop freezing the floor.”

Nora considered this.

> **NORA:**  
> “Okay.”

Three seconds later, another patch of frost appeared.

Frost stared at it.

Nora stared at it.

Neither said anything.

---

# 5. FROST'S TRAINING

Frost has begun teaching Nora how to control the intensity of her cryokinesis.

The first lesson was simple:

> **“Don't freeze everything.”**

The second lesson:

> **“Control where the cold goes.”**

The third lesson:

> **“Stop making ice because you're bored.”**

Nora apparently struggled with the third lesson.

She enjoys making small ice shapes.

Snowflakes are particularly popular.

During quiet periods at the Merch Store, Nora may create tiny crystalline shapes on the counter.

Frost occasionally joins her.

---

# 6. SHARED COLD

Their interactions have another unusual effect.

When Nora and Frost are together, the surrounding temperature drops noticeably.

Neither appears bothered.

Other residents are less enthusiastic.

A customer entering the Merch Store once stopped at the doorway.

> **CUSTOMER:**  
> “Why is it freezing in here?”

Nora and Frost looked at each other.

> **FROST:**  
> “Training.”

> **NORA:**  
> “Cake?”

The customer paused.

> **CUSTOMER:**  
> “...What?”

Nora pointed toward the snack shelf.

---

# 7. FROST'S OBSERVATION

Frost has noticed that Nora's adaptation works differently from her own abilities.

Frost naturally possesses control over cold.

Nora **adapted into it.**

This means Nora's abilities continue developing as she learns.

Frost can teach Nora techniques.

Nora can then experiment with them and potentially develop entirely new applications.

Frost finds this interesting.

Nora finds it fun.

C.O.R.E. finds it increasingly difficult to determine whether this constitutes training or two residents playing with ice.

---

# 8. MERCH STORE WORK

Nora has returned to her normal responsibilities at the Tachyon Merch Store.

She is capable of:

- managing the counter;
    
- handling inventory;
    
- assisting customers;
    
- organizing merchandise;
    
- monitoring store displays;
    
- and working alongside other staff.
    

Her cold adaptation does not interfere with her duties.

If anything, the store's cooler environment is now extremely comfortable for her.

She still wears the thick coat.

She still wears the scarf.

She still wears the boots.

She still wears the beanie.

And she still drinks hot chocolate.

Nobody has successfully explained that last one.

---

# 9. NORA & FROST

The two are increasingly seen together.

Sometimes they are training.

Sometimes they are talking.

Sometimes they are sitting beside one another without saying anything.

Sometimes they simply make tiny ice sculptures.

Nora appears particularly comfortable around Frost.

There is no need for Nora to explain why she is cold.

There is no need for Frost to explain why she likes the cold.

Neither has to worry about the other finding their abilities strange.

They simply understand.

---

# 10. CURRENT SCENE

Nora was sitting behind the Merch Store counter.

Her scarf was wrapped tightly around her neck.

Her beanie covered the top of her white hair.

Her boots rested beneath the counter.

Frost sat beside her.

A tiny snowflake formed above Nora's palm.

Frost created another beside it.

Nora looked at Frost.

Frost looked at Nora.

They continued making snowflakes.

After several minutes, Nora quietly said:

> **NORA:**  
> “We're both cold.”

Frost nodded.

> **FROST:**  
> “Very.”

Nora smiled faintly.

> **NORA:**  
> “Good.”

Frost smiled.

The Merch Store temperature dropped another two degrees.

Neko's remote environmental monitor immediately sent an alert.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Nora's adaptation has given her something she did not previously have:

Someone who naturally understands what it is like to live with the cold.

Frost does not need Nora to warm up.

Nora does not need Frost to cool down.

They can simply exist together.

For Nora, that appears to matter.

She is back at work.

She is wearing an unnecessarily thick coat indoors.

She has replaced her usual beret with a beanie.

She wears boots and a scarf despite the SAFEHOUSE being perfectly heated.

She leaves tiny frozen footprints around the Merch Store.

And, increasingly, there is usually another set of frozen footprints beside hers.

**Frost's.**
