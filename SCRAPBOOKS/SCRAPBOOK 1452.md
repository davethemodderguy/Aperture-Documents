# 1. OVERVIEW

The **Backrooms** is a non-Earth liminal environment consisting of an extensive network of interconnected anomalous spaces commonly referred to as **Levels**.

The environment was first documented by Aperture Industries researcher **Levery Lanman in 1945** following the investigation of a series of unexplained spatial inconsistencies.

Unlike many anomalous dimensions encountered by Aperture, the Backrooms do not appear to possess a single stable entrance, exit, or central governing structure.

Instead, access is possible through specific points in normal reality where the laws governing physical space become temporarily or permanently inconsistent.

These points are collectively designated:

> **FRONTROOM ANOMALOUS ENTRY POINTS**

The Backrooms are therefore not considered a naturally occurring location within conventional spacetime.

They are classified as a **Liminal Spatial Network**.

---

# 2. HISTORICAL DISCOVERY

In 1945, Researcher Levery Lanman was investigating several reports concerning buildings in which:

- corridors appeared longer from the inside than from the outside;
    
- rooms occasionally changed dimensions;
    
- objects disappeared after being placed against certain walls;
    
- individuals reported entering rooms that could not physically exist within the building's floor plan;
    
- and architectural structures appeared to briefly overlap.
    

Lanman's initial hypothesis was that these incidents represented localized spatial distortions.

This proved incorrect.

During one investigation, Lanman observed a section of concrete flooring in which his measuring equipment produced contradictory results.

The floor appeared to occupy one position physically.

Its measured spatial coordinates indicated another.

When Lanman placed a marked object against the affected area, the object partially disappeared.

It did not fall through the floor.

It did not break.

It simply ceased occupying the same spatial layer as the surrounding environment.

Lanman subsequently made physical contact with the affected region.

He disappeared.

---

# 3. FIRST BACKROOMS ENTRY

Lanman regained physical awareness inside an unfamiliar yellow architectural environment.

The environment consisted of:

- repetitive yellow walls;
    
- stained carpet;
    
- fluorescent lighting;
    
- repeating corridors;
    
- rooms without conventional architectural purpose;
    
- and spatial dimensions inconsistent with the building from which he had entered.
    

No natural exterior environment could be located.

No recognizable horizon existed.

Lanman spent approximately **eleven hours** within the environment before locating another spatial discontinuity.

He returned to the original reality through an unrelated section of wall.

His field notes contained the first known reference to:

> **"THE BACKROOMS."**

The name was retained.

---

# 4. NATURE OF THE BACKROOMS

The Backrooms should not be interpreted as a single room, building, dimension, or conventional alternate universe.

They are better understood as a **network of anomalous spatial environments**.

Individual environments are designated **Levels**.

Levels may differ dramatically in:

- architecture;
    
- geometry;
    
- atmosphere;
    
- environmental hazards;
    
- temporal behavior;
    
- physical laws;
    
- biological activity;
    
- accessibility;
    
- and exit conditions.
    

Some Levels appear to be nearly mundane.

Others violate conventional physical laws to such an extent that standard scientific terminology becomes insufficient.

Despite these differences, the Levels remain connected through anomalous transitional points.

---

# 5. FRONTROOM ENTRY PHENOMENA

The most important characteristic of the Backrooms is that entry does **not** require Aperture technology.

Under specific circumstances, ordinary people can enter.

This is the primary reason L.S.I.N. maintains active operations within normal reality.

The Backrooms occasionally produce localized areas where physical consistency breaks down.

These areas are known as:

> **NOC-LIMINAL ZONES**

Informally:

> **"Noclip zones."**

Within these zones, matter may fail to interact with surrounding matter normally.

Examples include:

- a hand passing through a wall;
    
- a person partially intersecting with a solid surface;
    
- objects disappearing into apparently solid architecture;
    
- floors failing to provide expected physical resistance;
    
- two surfaces occupying overlapping spatial coordinates;
    
- corridors connecting to locations that should not exist;
    
- or a person falling through a surface without the surface physically breaking.
    

A person who completely crosses such a boundary may enter the Backrooms.

---

# 6. NOC-LIMINAL ZONES

Not every spatial anomaly is connected to the Backrooms.

L.S.I.N. therefore does not automatically classify every case of unusual geometry as a Backrooms entrance.

An entry point requires confirmation of a spatial relationship between the affected Frontrooms location and a known Backrooms Level.

Confirmed entry points receive a unique designation.

Example:

> **BR-FRONT-019**

Where:

**BR** = Backrooms

**FRONT** = Frontrooms origin

**019** = individual entry-point number

---

# 7. THE FRONTROOMS

For L.S.I.N. purposes, conventional reality is designated:

> **THE FRONTROOMS**

The term is not intended to imply that the Frontrooms are inherently superior to the Backrooms.

It is simply an operational distinction.

**FRONTROOMS:** Conventional reality.

**BACKROOMS:** Anomalous liminal spatial network.

The boundary between the two is not necessarily permanent.

---

# 8. ENTRY POINT STABILITY

Backrooms entrances can be divided into three categories.

### TYPE I — TRANSIENT

The anomaly exists for a limited period.

It may disappear without intervention.

These are generally the most difficult to monitor.

### TYPE II — PERSISTENT

The anomaly remains accessible until deliberately neutralized.

These locations require continuous monitoring.

### TYPE III — STRUCTURAL

The anomaly has become embedded into the physical environment.

Examples include:

- permanently anomalous walls;
    
- floors with spatial inconsistencies;
    
- rooms containing impossible geometry;
    
- architectural structures that repeatedly reconnect to Backrooms Levels.
    

Structural entrances represent the greatest civilian exposure risk.

---

# 9. CIVILIAN DISAPPEARANCES

Prior to formal L.S.I.N. administration, Backrooms-related disappearances were frequently attributed to:

- missing-person cases;
    
- accidents;
    
- structural collapses;
    
- criminal activity;
    
- natural disasters;
    
- unidentified deaths;
    
- or unexplained abandonment.
    

Several cases were eventually discovered to have a common characteristic.

The missing individual had been observed near an area later confirmed to contain a Backrooms entry point.

The number of incidents increased sufficiently that Aperture Industries concluded that uncontrolled Backrooms access represented a global civilian safety problem.

---

# 10. INTERNATIONAL AGREEMENT

Following multiple civilian disappearances, Aperture Industries proposed an international containment agreement.

The agreement established that governments would:

- cooperate with Aperture investigations;
    
- permit controlled L.S.I.N. operations within affected territories;
    
- suppress public information regarding confirmed Backrooms incidents;
    
- assist with civilian evacuation when necessary;
    
- provide conventional emergency-service cover stories;
    
- and allow Aperture personnel to remove or neutralize anomalous entry points.
    

The agreement was accepted.

Consequently:

> **Backrooms operations are currently covered by participating governments worldwide.**

The public explanations surrounding disappearances or affected locations vary according to circumstances.

Common explanations include:

> Structural instability.

> Chemical contamination.

> Gas leaks.

> Construction accidents.

> Restricted hazardous areas.

> Missing-person investigations.

The purpose is not to conceal ordinary crimes.

It is to prevent civilians from intentionally investigating or attempting to enter the Backrooms.

---

# 11. L.S.I.N. AUTHORITY

Management of all confirmed Backrooms activity falls under:

> **L.S.I.N. — Liminal Space Investigation and Neutralization Executive.**

L.S.I.N. is responsible for:

- identifying Backrooms entry points;
    
- investigating newly discovered Levels;
    
- maintaining Level records;
    
- establishing safe routes;
    
- deploying outposts;
    
- rescuing personnel and civilians;
    
- establishing controlled exits;
    
- neutralizing hostile phenomena;
    
- destroying unsafe Frontrooms entrances;
    
- and coordinating international containment.
    

The Backrooms represent one of L.S.I.N.'s largest ongoing operational responsibilities.

---

# 12. FRONTROOMS PURGING OPERATIONS

When a Backrooms entrance is discovered within an accessible civilian environment, L.S.I.N. personnel are authorized to **purge the entrance point** when containment cannot otherwise be guaranteed.

A purge does not mean destroying the surrounding building.

The objective is to eliminate the anomalous spatial connection.

Depending on the entry point, this may involve:

- stabilizing the affected space;
    
- collapsing the anomalous boundary;
    
- severing its spatial connection;
    
- removing anomalous material;
    
- isolating the location;
    
- or rendering the affected geometry physically consistent again.
    

Personnel must distinguish between:

> **Destroying the entrance**

and

> **Destroying the location containing the entrance.**

The latter is permitted only under extreme circumstances.

---

# 13. CIVILIAN PROTECTION

L.S.I.N. personnel encountering civilians near a confirmed entry point are instructed to prioritize removal from the anomaly.

Civilians are not to be informed of the full nature of the Backrooms unless operational circumstances make concealment impossible.

If a civilian has already entered:

> **RESCUE TAKES PRIORITY OVER CONTAINMENT.**

L.S.I.N. personnel are not to assume that a civilian has become irrecoverable simply because they have crossed into a Level.

Known routes, stabilized exits, and controlled retrieval procedures are maintained for this purpose.

# 15. CONTROLLED EXIT SYSTEM

Contrary to early assumptions, the Backrooms do possess reliable exit routes.

Aperture has successfully established controlled access between selected Levels and the Frontrooms.

These exits are not necessarily naturally occurring.

Some have been:

- stabilized;
    
- reinforced;
    
- mapped;
    
- monitored;
    
- or technologically maintained.
    

L.S.I.N. therefore maintains a network of authorized entrance and exit points.

Personnel are expected to use approved routes whenever possible.

---

# 16. BACKROOMS TECHNOLOGY

Aperture's understanding of Backrooms physics has advanced considerably since 1945.

Modern L.S.I.N. equipment can identify several forms of spatial instability before personnel physically enter them.

Specialized systems are capable of detecting:

- abnormal spatial depth;
    
- overlapping geometry;
    
- discontinuous surfaces;
    
- dimensional displacement;
    
- impossible architectural volume;
    
- and localized physical-law inconsistencies.
    

These systems do not guarantee detection.

The Backrooms remain capable of producing previously undocumented phenomena.

# 18. IMPORTANT OPERATIONAL PRINCIPLE

The Backrooms are not treated as inherently hostile.

Some Levels are dangerous.

Some are relatively safe.

Some contain useful resources.

Some contain environments that are completely incompatible with human survival.

L.S.I.N. therefore does not operate under the assumption:

> **"Everything inside must be destroyed."**

The operational objective is:

> **UNDERSTAND → MAP → CONTAIN → CONTROL**

Neutralization is used when containment or civilian protection requires it.

---

# 19. RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER LIMINAL SPACES

The Backrooms are not currently considered identical to other liminal spaces encountered by Aperture.

For example:

> **LSIN-001 — THE GRASSLAND**

is classified independently.

Although both environments exhibit liminal characteristics, no confirmed direct connection between the Grassland and Backrooms has been established.

L.S.I.N. maintains separate classification systems for each anomaly until a shared origin can be demonstrated.

---

# 20. CURRENT STATUS

The Backrooms remain active.

New Levels continue to be discovered.

Existing Levels continue to produce previously undocumented spatial connections.

Frontrooms entry points continue to appear.

Most are detected before civilian exposure.

Some are not.

For this reason, L.S.I.N. maintains permanent monitoring operations across known high-risk regions.

---

# 21. L.S.I.N. DIRECTIVE

All L.S.I.N. personnel are reminded:

The Backrooms were discovered in **1945**.

They were not created by Aperture.

They were not created by L.S.I.N.

They have existed independently of modern containment efforts.

Aperture's responsibility is therefore not to determine whether the Backrooms **should exist.**

They already do.

The responsibility is to ensure that an ordinary person walking through an ordinary building does not accidentally step through a wall—

—and disappear.
