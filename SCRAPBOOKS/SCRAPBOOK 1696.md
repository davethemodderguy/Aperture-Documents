# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Clover Luminare was required to deliver a set of documents to the **Krampus Compliance Authority (KCA)**.

Jake accompanied her.

The visit was routine.

The visit ceased being routine when Jake encountered **Dialyn**, a KCA Customer Support representative and former SAFEHOUSE resident.

Dialyn was previously housed at SAFEHOUSE before the KCA completed its remodeling of the Customer Support Division.

Jake had not previously met her.

Dialyn had, however, already heard about him.

This resulted in an approximately twenty-minute interaction in which Dialyn discovered that Jake is considerably smaller, quieter, and more curious than expected.

Jake, meanwhile, discovered that Dialyn knows where the KCA keeps its snacks.

This information was immediately classified by Jake as important.

---

# 1. ARRIVAL

Clover entered the KCA administrative building carrying the required documentation.

Jake walked beside her.

He was carrying his teddy bear.

His wings were folded against his back.

His white hair and azure eyes attracted several brief glances from KCA personnel.

Clover ignored them.

Jake noticed.

He did not appear bothered.

He simply continued walking.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Are we there?”

> **CLOVER:**  
> “Almost.”

Jake nodded.

Approximately six seconds later:

> **JAKE:**  
> “Now?”

> **CLOVER:**  
> “Almost.”

Another six seconds.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Now?”

Clover looked down at him.

> **CLOVER:**  
> “You are very patient.”

Jake smiled.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I know.”

---

# 2. CUSTOMER SUPPORT DIVISION

The KCA's Customer Support Division has recently undergone substantial remodeling.

The previous arrangement was considered inadequate for the increasing number of Banlands administrative inquiries, resident requests, interdepartmental correspondence, and external complaints.

The remodeled division now contains:

- reception counters;
    
- customer assistance desks;
    
- document processing stations;
    
- waiting areas;
    
- administrative offices;
    
- public inquiry terminals;
    
- and dedicated personnel areas.
    

Dialyn works here.

Before the remodeling, she had been housed at SAFEHOUSE.

She is therefore familiar with the residents and the general culture surrounding them.

Jake was not aware of this.

---

# 3. DIALYN

Dialyn approached the reception area after noticing Clover.

She recognized her immediately.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Clover!”

Clover raised a hand.

> **CLOVER:**  
> “Hi.”

Dialyn then noticed Jake.

She stopped.

Jake looked up.

Dialyn looked down.

Jake's azure eyes slowly tracked upward toward her face.

Her hair was divided almost perfectly down the middle.

The **left half was black.**

The **right half was white.**

Jake stared for approximately three seconds.

Dialyn smiled.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Hello.”

Jake pointed.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Your hair.”

Clover immediately looked away.

Dialyn laughed.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Yes.”

Jake tilted his head.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Why?”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “That's a very complicated question.”

Jake considered this.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Oh.”

Pause.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Okay.”

Dialyn laughed again.

---

# 4. INTRODUCTION

Clover eventually intervened.

> **CLOVER:**  
> “Jake, this is Dialyn.”

Dialyn crouched slightly so that she was closer to Jake's eye level.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Nice to meet you, Jake.”

Jake looked at her.

Then at Clover.

Then back at Dialyn.

> **JAKE:**  
> “You're KCA?”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “I am.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “What do you do?”

Dialyn smiled.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “I help people.”

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Like Bao?”

Clover's expression softened.

Dialyn paused.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “...In a different way.”

Jake accepted this answer.

---

# 5. FORMER SAFEHOUSE RESIDENT

Jake eventually learned that Dialyn used to live at SAFEHOUSE.

This produced immediate interest.

> **JAKE:**  
> “You lived there?”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “I did.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “Where?”

Dialyn named her former room.

Jake thought about this.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I don't know that room.”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “That's because you weren't there when I lived there.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “Oh.”

Pause.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Why'd you leave?”

Dialyn glanced toward the Customer Support Division.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “The KCA needed me here.”

Jake looked around.

Then back at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Do you like it?”

Dialyn smiled.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Most days.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “That's good.”

Dialyn looked slightly surprised by the sincerity of the response.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Yeah.”

---

# 6. THE DOCUMENTS

Clover eventually remembered why they were there.

She placed the documents on Dialyn's desk.

> **CLOVER:**  
> “These need to reach Compliance.”

Dialyn looked through the paperwork.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Everything appears to be here.”

> **CLOVER:**  
> “Good.”

Jake leaned against the desk.

He watched Dialyn sort the documents.

After several seconds:

> **JAKE:**  
> “Is that accounting?”

Clover looked at him.

Dialyn looked at him.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Yes.”

Jake nodded.

He watched.

Clover immediately recognized the expression.

Jake was silently checking the numbers.

---

# 7. DIALYN'S DISCOVERY

Dialyn noticed Jake staring at the documents.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Do you like numbers?”

Jake shrugged.

> **JAKE:**  
> “They're okay.”

Clover coughed.

Dialyn looked at her.

Clover quietly said:

> **CLOVER:**  
> “He beat Neko.”

Dialyn stopped.

She looked at Jake.

Then Clover.

Then Jake again.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “...He what?”

Jake looked mildly embarrassed.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I watched Clover.”

> **CLOVER:**  
> “For several weeks.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “She works with numbers.”

> **CLOVER:**  
> “And apparently he decided to become better at them.”

Dialyn stared at the six-year-old.

Jake looked back.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I like patterns.”

Dialyn slowly nodded.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Right.”

---

# 8. CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Dialyn eventually returned the completed paperwork.

Jake remained beside the desk.

He looked around the office.

There were several signs directing visitors toward different customer-service stations.

Jake read them.

Then looked at Dialyn.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Do people yell at you?”

Dialyn blinked.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Sometimes.”

Jake frowned.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Why?”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Usually because they're unhappy.”

Jake thought about this.

> **JAKE:**  
> “That's mean.”

Dialyn smiled.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Sometimes.”

Jake nodded seriously.

> **JAKE:**  
> “You can tell them no.”

Dialyn laughed.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “I appreciate the advice.”

---

# 9. THE SNACK DISCOVERY

Jake eventually noticed a small container behind the reception desk.

His eyes moved toward it.

Then back toward Dialyn.

Then toward the container.

Dialyn noticed.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Hungry?”

Jake nodded.

Clover sighed.

> **CLOVER:**  
> “You just ate.”

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I'm hungry again.”

Dialyn opened the container.

There were individually wrapped snacks inside.

She offered one to Jake.

Jake looked at Clover.

Clover nodded.

Jake accepted it.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Thank you.”

> **DIALYN:**  
> “You're welcome.”

Jake ate the snack.

He looked at the container again.

Dialyn laughed.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “That's enough.”

Jake looked disappointed.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Okay.”

---

# 10. DIALYN'S OBSERVATION

Dialyn eventually looked at Clover.

Jake was sitting nearby with his teddy bear.

His feet did not reach the floor.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “He's smaller than I expected.”

> **CLOVER:**  
> “He is six.”

Dialyn nodded.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “I knew that.”

She looked at Jake again.

His azure eyes were faintly glowing.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “I just didn't expect...”

She gestured vaguely.

> **CLOVER:**  
> “The literal star?”

Dialyn nodded.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “The literal star.”

Jake looked up.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I'm not that big.”

Dialyn laughed.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Not physically.”

Jake looked confused.

Clover smiled faintly.

---

The documents had been successfully delivered.

Clover gathered her remaining belongings.

Jake stood beside her.

Dialyn waved.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “It was nice meeting you, Jake.”

Jake waved back.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Bye.”

He stopped.

Then looked at Dialyn's hair again.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Your hair is cool.”

Dialyn smiled.

> **DIALYN:**  
> “Thank you.”

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Bye.”

Clover began walking.

Jake followed.

After a few steps:

> **JAKE:**  
> “Clover?”

> **CLOVER:**  
> “Hm?”

> **JAKE:**  
> “Can we come back?”

Clover looked down at him.

> **CLOVER:**  
> “Probably.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “Okay.”

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The meeting produced no security concerns.

No anomalous incidents occurred.

No unauthorized equipment was accessed.

No documents were misplaced.

Jake demonstrated ordinary childlike curiosity throughout the visit.

Dialyn demonstrated familiarity with SAFEHOUSE residents and appeared comfortable interacting with Jake.

The encounter also established a potentially useful social connection between Jake and a former SAFEHOUSE resident who now works within the KCA.

This is considered beneficial.

Jake has spent considerable time isolated from the broader network of people connected to SAFEHOUSE.

Meeting former residents may help reinforce an important fact:

> **People can leave SAFEHOUSE without disappearing from his life.**

Dialyn still exists.

She simply works somewhere else now.

And, according to Jake's assessment:

> **Her hair is cool.**

C.O.R.E. considers this a satisfactory first meeting.

## CLOSING NOTE

Clover came to the KCA to deliver documents.

Jake came because Clover brought him.

Dialyn was simply doing her job.

Twenty minutes later, Jake had:

- met a former SAFEHOUSE resident;
    
- learned that customer support involves people yelling;
    
- inspected KCA paperwork;
    
- silently reviewed accounting;
    
- received a snack;
    
- complimented Dialyn's hair;
    
- and apparently decided he would like to visit again.
    

The documents were delivered successfully.

The child was not lost.

The teddy bear remained operational.

No one was yelled at.

**A successful administrative visit.**
