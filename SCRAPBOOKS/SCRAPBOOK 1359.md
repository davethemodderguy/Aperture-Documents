# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake possesses an additional anomalous ability involving his eyes.

The phenomenon does not appear to be conventional hypnosis.

Jake can deliberately **draw another person's attention toward his eyes**.

Once visual attention is established, the observer may become increasingly focused on Jake's gaze.

The effect can progress from:

> **noticing Jake**

to:

> **maintaining eye contact**

to:

> **becoming visibly mesmerized.**

The subject may temporarily lose awareness of surrounding distractions while concentrating on Jake.

---

# 1. INITIAL OBSERVATION

The ability was first formally documented after a researcher noticed that Jake's eyes appeared unusually difficult to look away from.

Jake was sitting quietly.

His blue irises were glowing.

The researcher looked toward him.

Then stopped speaking.

Several seconds passed.

> **RESEARCHER:**  
> “...Wow.”

Jake tilted his head.

> **Jake:**  
> “What?”

The researcher continued staring.

> **RESEARCHER:**  
> “Your eyes are...”

Pause.

> **Jake:**  
> “Pretty?”

The researcher blinked.

> **RESEARCHER:**  
> “Yeah.”

Jake smiled slightly.

The researcher remained focused on his eyes for several more seconds before realizing what had happened.

---

# 2. CONTROLLED OBSERVATION

A controlled test was conducted with an approved observer.

Jake was instructed to look directly at the observer.

No other anomalous abilities were used.

Within seconds, the observer's attention became increasingly fixed on Jake's eyes.

Peripheral distractions were noticeably ignored.

The observer's posture relaxed.

Speech became slower.

After approximately several seconds:

> **OBSERVER:**  
> “I can't really look away.”

Jake immediately looked elsewhere.

The effect ceased.

The observer blinked several times.

> **OBSERVER:**  
> “Oh.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “Sorry.”

> **OBSERVER:**  
> “You can do that?”

Jake shrugged.

> **Jake:**  
> “Apparently.”

---

# 3. EFFECT CHARACTERISTICS

Current observations indicate that Jake's gaze can:

- attract attention toward his eyes;
    
- make maintaining eye contact unusually compelling;
    
- reduce awareness of peripheral distractions;
    
- produce a mesmerized or entranced state;
    
- temporarily interfere with the observer's willingness to look away;
    
- and appear considerably stronger when Jake deliberately concentrates on the observer.
    

The effect does not appear to require spoken commands.

Jake does not need to tell someone:

> “Look at me.”

Their attention naturally moves toward his eyes.

---

# 4. VISUAL COMPONENT

Jake's eyes are already anomalous.

Current appearance includes:

- bright blue irises;
    
- celestial luminosity;
    
- subtle internal movement resembling stellar light;
    
- and occasional shooting-star-like streaks.
    

When the ability is active, the luminosity becomes more noticeable.

Observers have described the experience as:

> **“Mesmerizing.”**

One researcher described it as:

> **“Like looking into a tiny piece of the night sky that doesn't want you to look away.”**

Jake apparently liked that description.

---

# 5. JAKE'S OWN UNDERSTANDING

Jake does not appear to have developed a sophisticated understanding of the ability.

When asked whether he intentionally hypnotizes people, he responded:

> **Jake:**  
> “Sometimes.”

> **RESEARCHER:**  
> “Why?”

Jake thought about it.

> **Jake:**  
> “They keep looking.”

> **RESEARCHER:**  
> “And you don't tell them to?”

Jake shook his head.

> **Jake:**  
> “No.”

Pause.

> **Jake:**  
> “They just do.”

# 8. SAFETY GUIDANCE

Jake is not to be treated as a hostile hypnotic entity.

The ability appears to be an innate part of his anomalous physiology.

However, deliberate use against residents or personnel without consent is discouraged.

Personnel should not deliberately challenge the effect for entertainment.

Likewise, residents should not encourage Jake to use it on unsuspecting visitors.

Particularly:

> **Do not use the ability on National Guard representatives during weapons negotiations.**

This instruction was added following the recent visit.
