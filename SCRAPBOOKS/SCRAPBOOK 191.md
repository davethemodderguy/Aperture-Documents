## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **WILDLIFE OBSERVATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: UNIDENTIFIED SNOWY OWL – SHELTER T-01**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Several weeks after the white tiger became a permanent resident near Shelter T-01, personnel documented the regular appearance of a large white owl.

Unlike the tiger, the owl does not remain on the ground.

It is almost always observed perched on the upper branches surrounding the treehouse.

Its presence is quiet.

Its departure is usually unnoticed.

Except...

When someone's lunch is suddenly missing.

---

# **DESCRIPTION**

Species:

Large white owl resembling a snowy owl.

Distinguishing characteristics include:

- Bright white plumage.
    
- Exceptionally large eyes.
    
- Calm disposition.
    
- Silent flight.
    
- A persistent habit of observing people from nearby branches.
    

Personnel frequently report the sensation of "being watched."

In many cases...

They are.

---

# **BEHAVIOR**

The owl rarely vocalizes.

Instead, it spends hours watching activity around Shelter T-01.

It has developed several consistent habits.

These include:

- Perching near Corin's treehouse.
    
- Following patrols through the forest.
    
- Watching people from rooftops throughout the Blacksite.
    
- Appearing unexpectedly wherever food has been left unattended.
    

Its preferred method of theft is remarkably simple.

Wait.

Observe.

When no one is looking...

Take the food.

Leave.

Belle has lost three cookies.

Keith has lost half a sandwich.

Neko once turned around to discover an entire pastry had vanished.

The owl was observed eating it from a nearby branch while maintaining uninterrupted eye contact.

---

# **AFFINITY TOWARD MART**

The owl has displayed an unusual level of comfort around Mart.

When Mart visits Shelter T-01, the owl frequently relocates to the branch directly above him.

It occasionally lands beside him without hesitation.

Behavioral specialists offer no definitive explanation.

Personnel have instead adopted a simpler theory.

> "The owl likes the Owl Thiren."

Mart himself appears unsurprised by the arrangement.

---

# **MESSENGER DUTIES**

Due to its regular travel between the Blacksite and Shelter T-01, Logistics has occasionally taken advantage of the owl's predictable flight patterns.

Small lightweight messages, notes, or similarly sized items are sometimes attached using a specially designed harness.

The owl has shown no objection to the arrangement.

Curiously...

It often arrives at Corin's treehouse before the assigned courier does.

How it consistently finds Corin remains unknown.

---

# **CORIN**

The owl frequently remains near Corin even when no food is available.

It may perch above her while she reads.

Watch quietly while she drinks tea.

Or simply sit nearby without making a sound.

No clear explanation has been found for this behavior.

Veterinary Services have considered several possibilities.

None adequately explain why the owl appears to seek Corin's company so consistently.

Corin herself has only offered one explanation.

> "...Maybe he just likes sitting with someone."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The white owl continues to appear throughout the Blacksite.

It has become a familiar sight on rooftops, observation towers, and the branches surrounding Shelter T-01.

Personnel are advised that if they notice a pair of large golden eyes watching them from above...

Their lunch should probably be watched as well.
