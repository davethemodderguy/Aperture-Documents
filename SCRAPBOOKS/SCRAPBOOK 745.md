# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE / MEMORIAL ARCHIVE**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — MOVIE NIGHT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — MEMORIAL RECORD  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE, Room N-07  
**Date:** [REDACTED]  
**Event:** **COMMUNAL REMEMBRANCE**

---

# **TONIGHT**

It was supposed to be movie night.

The projector was already set up.

The lights were dimmed.

The couches had been arranged.

The central living area was ready.

But nobody started a movie.

Nobody brought snacks.

Nobody argued about what to watch.

Nobody complained about someone taking the good seat.

The screen remained black.

---

# **THE EMPTY PLACE**

Alice's usual place was left untouched.

Her little scooter wasn't there.

Her hat wasn't there either.

Corin had it with her.

The chair remained empty.

Nobody sat in it.

Nobody suggested moving it.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle brought a carrot.

She placed it beside Alice's empty place.

Nobody asked why.

She simply sat down.

After a while, Belle whispered:

> "Alice would've liked this."

Nobody knew exactly what she meant.

It didn't matter.

They all understood.

---

# **NEKO**

Neko brought Alice's scooter into the room.

He parked it beside the wall.

He checked the battery.

Still charged.

He had maintained it.

Of course he had.

Someone asked him if he planned on giving it away.

Neko immediately answered:

> "No."

Then, after a pause:

> "...It's hers."

Nobody argued.

---

# **STAR**

Star sat on the floor.

She looked toward the blank screen.

> "She would've asked why we're not watching anything."

A small laugh came from someone.

It didn't last long.

Star smiled anyway.

> "She'd probably pick something terrible."

Nightshade quietly replied:

> "Definitely."

For the first time that evening, someone laughed properly.

---

# **NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade remembered how Alice would ride her scooter through the SAFEHOUSE.

How she'd stop beside someone.

Look up.

Say nothing.

And simply stay.

Alice didn't need to talk to make people feel like they weren't alone.

Nightshade looked toward the empty chair.

> "She made this place warmer."

Nobody disagreed.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi sat beside Corin.

Corin was holding Alice's hat.

She hadn't put it down all evening.

Lumi didn't ask her to.

She simply rested a hand on Corin's shoulder.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie remembered how Alice always worried about everyone.

If someone looked tired:

> **"You tired?"**

If someone was hurt:

> **"You okay?"**

If someone was sad:

Alice would sit nearby.

Even when Alice herself was barely holding together.

She always noticed everyone else first.

Orphie stared at the empty chair.

> "She never realized how many people were watching out for her."

---

# **BAO**

Bao remembered cooking for Alice.

Alice always preferred Corin's chicken potato soup.

Bao still laughed about it.

> "I spent all that time cooking..."

She smiled sadly.

> "...and she picked Corin's soup."

Corin laughed through tears.

It was the first time she had laughed in days.

---

# **FROST**

Frost remembered Alice sitting near her when she was cold.

Alice had once told her:

> **"Frost make Alice less cold."**

Frost had answered:

> "That's what I'm here for."

She looked down.

> "I wish I could have done more."

---

# **TAIGA & MART**

Taiga remembered Alice sitting on her back.

Mart remembered her drawings.

They remembered how she wasn't afraid of them.

Even though Taiga was a tiger.

Even though Alice was a tiny rabbit.

Taiga quietly said:

> "She trusted me."

Mart nodded.

> "She trusted everyone."

---

# **ENDMIN**

Endmin remembered the journal.

All those pages.

Alice had written everyone's names.

Because she was terrified of forgetting.

Endmin looked toward the empty chair.

> "She wanted to remember us."

She paused.

> "She succeeded."

---

# **CORIN**

Corin said almost nothing.

She held Alice's hat.

Eventually, she looked around the room.

Everyone was there.

Everyone Alice had written about.

Everyone Alice had cared about.

Everyone Alice had worried about.

And for the first time, Corin understood something.

Alice's disappearance had not erased her.

It had done the opposite.

---

# **THE MEMORY**

People began talking.

Not formally.

Not one at a time.

Just memories.

Alice crashing her scooter into something soft.

Alice falling asleep sitting upright.

Alice drawing people she loved.

Alice trying to learn grammar.

Alice writing names in her journal.

Alice quietly following someone because she wanted company.

Alice giving her life force to a dying flower without considering herself.

Alice worrying that everyone else was tired.

Alice trying desperately to make sure everyone was okay.

Alice forgetting herself.

Alice still trying to remember everyone else.

---

# **THE SCREEN**

The projector remained off.

Nobody cared.

There was no movie tonight.

The movie would have been forgotten eventually.

This wouldn't.

---

# **CORIN'S WORDS**

Eventually, Corin placed Alice's hat in her lap.

She looked around the room.

> "She thought nobody loved her."

Her voice broke.

> "She couldn't have been more wrong."

Silence.

Then Belle moved closer.

Star moved closer.

Orphie moved closer.

Then everyone else.

Nobody left Corin alone.

Not tonight.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Alice spent her short life believing that her purpose was to make everyone else feel better.

She cared about everyone.

She worried about everyone.

She gave what little she had to everyone.

She never believed she deserved the same kindness in return.

But tonight, the SAFEHOUSE did nothing except remember her.

No movie.

No snacks.

No distractions.

Just stories.

Laughter.

Tears.

Silence.

And the memory of a weak little bunny girl who somehow managed to make an entire Blacksite feel a little more like home.

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

Alice is gone.

Her journal is blank.

Her drawings are gone.

Her handwriting is gone.

There is no photograph.

There is no birthday picture.

There is no recording of her smiling for a camera.

But Alice herself is not forgotten.

Not even close.

Her absence has made everyone remember more.

The little things.

The silly things.

The painful things.

The beautiful things.

Every person in that room carries a different Alice in their memories.

And together—

they remember all of her.

The movie screen stayed black that night.

Nobody needed it.

**Alice was the only story they wanted to remember.**

**End of CORE-SAFEHOUSE / MEMORIAL ARCHIVE**
