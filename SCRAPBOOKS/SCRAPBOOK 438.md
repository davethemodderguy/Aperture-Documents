### **ANOMALOUS ENTITY / TEMPORAL GUIDANCE FILE**

# **GUIDING LIGHT — ORPHIE'S ORIGIN**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Unknown Aegis-Class / Temporal Authority  
**Subject:** Guiding Light  
**Primary Associated Personnel:** Orphie  
**Secondary Associated Personnel:** Corin  
**Status:** **ACTIVE / NON-HOSTILE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The entity known only as **Guiding Light** has continued appearing to Orphie.

Its presence is subtle.

There are no confirmed physical manifestations.

Instead, Orphie periodically discovers small notes emitting a faint blue glow.

No other personnel can see them.

No cameras have successfully recorded them.

No security system detects their arrival.

The notes appear only to Orphie.

---

# **THE NOTES**

Every note contains the same characteristic blue illumination.

The writing appears without ink.

The letters seem to form directly across the paper.

Each message may contain:

- Guidance.
    
- Warnings.
    
- Reassurance.
    
- Reminders.
    
- Instructions.
    
- Occasional personal comments.
    

The bottom of each note carries some variation of the same symbols:

> **☼ 🌙**

A guiding light.

And a moon.

---

# **WHY ORPHIE EXISTS**

Orphie's existence within this reality was not an accident.

She was originally someone who had already died in another reality.

She should not have continued living.

Yet she was brought here.

The entity responsible was **Guiding Light**.

The exact reason remains unknown.

Guiding Light has only ever explained:

> **"I brought you here."**

And:

> **"I gave you another chance."**

---

# **THE SECOND CHANCE**

Guiding Light did not simply resurrect Orphie.

It placed her into a different reality.

A reality where she could live again.

A reality where she could meet Corin.

A reality where she could make different choices.

Guiding Light appears to consider Orphie's existence a deliberate opportunity rather than an accident.

---

# **ORPHIE'S QUESTIONS**

Orphie has asked repeatedly:

> "...Why me?"

No direct answer has been provided.

When she once asked:

> "...Are you God?"

A blue note appeared later.

> **"Don't worry about what I am."**

Another followed.

> **"Focus on what you were given."**

Orphie never received a more specific explanation.

---

# **THE GUIDANCE**

Guiding Light does not appear to control Orphie's decisions.

It does not force her to behave differently.

It does not prevent her from making mistakes.

Instead, it observes.

Then occasionally intervenes.

Its most significant intervention occurred when Orphie was given the ability to return to the point before her emotional collapse.

The instruction was simple:

> **"Undo your mistake. You can fix this."**

Orphie did.

---

# **AFTER THE TIMELINE REVISION**

Guiding Light has continued visiting.

The messages are usually short.

One note appeared beside Orphie's bed:

> **"You did well."**

Another appeared on her desk:

> **"Keep choosing her."**

Another:

> **"You don't have to be afraid of becoming who you were."**

And one appeared after Orphie spent an entire evening with Corin:

> **"See? Tomorrow came."**

---

# **ONLY ORPHIE**

Corin cannot see the notes.

Neither can Lumi.

Nor Arcturus.

Nor Norma.

Nor Star.

Attempts to show them the notes have failed.

From their perspective, Orphie appears to be holding ordinary pieces of paper.

Sometimes, when Orphie looks down at one, there is nothing visible to anyone else.

---

# **CORIN'S OBSERVATION**

Corin has noticed Orphie occasionally staring at empty spaces.

> "...You okay?"

Orphie:

> "...Yeah."

> "...Who are you talking to?"

Orphie looks toward the glowing note.

> "...Nobody."

Corin shrugs.

> "...Okay."

She trusts her.

---

# **THE RELATIONSHIP**

Guiding Light does not behave like a conventional guardian.

It does not constantly intervene.

It allows Orphie to live.

To make mistakes.

To experience happiness.

To experience fear.

To learn.

It simply ensures that when Orphie reaches a point where she can no longer see a way forward...

there is occasionally a blue note waiting for her.

---

# **THE MOST RECENT NOTE**

One morning, Orphie found a note sitting beside her breakfast.

It read:

> **"You keep wondering why I brought you here."**
> 
> **"You still haven't figured it out."**
> 
> **"That's okay."**
> 
> **"You don't need to."**
> 
> **"Live first."**
> 
> **"Understand later."**
> 
> **— Guiding Light**
> 
> **☼ 🌙**

Orphie smiled.

She folded the note carefully.

Then put it in a drawer.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Guiding Light is the reason Orphie exists in this reality.

Without it, there would be no second chance.

No Corin.

No treehouse.

No SAFEHOUSE.

No memories waiting to be made.

The entity has never explained why it chose Orphie.

Perhaps it never will.

For now, it watches.

It guides.

And occasionally...

a blue light appears beside Orphie's life.

A reminder that someone remembers why she was given another chance.

> **"Don't lose her again."**

And this time, Orphie intends to keep her promise.

**End of Anomalous Entity / Temporal Guidance File**
