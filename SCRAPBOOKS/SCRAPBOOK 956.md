# SAFEHOUSE ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY REPORT

## JANE DOE / JOHN DOE

### STRAWBERRY-INDUCED SEXUAL TRANSFORMATION

**Subject:** Jane Doe  
**Alternate Designation:** John Doe  
**Classification:** Anomalous Physiological Transformation  
**Trigger:** Consumption of an **unprocessed strawberry**  
**Duration:** Approximately 24 hours  
**Reversion:** Automatic  
**Identity Continuity:** Complete

---

# 1. OVERVIEW

Jane Doe possesses an unusual anomalous reaction to **unprocessed strawberries**.

Upon consuming one, Jane undergoes a temporary physiological transformation in which her body changes from female to male.

The transformation lasts approximately **24 hours**, after which Jane automatically returns to her normal female state.

The transformed state has been designated:

> **JOHN DOE**

This designation does **not** indicate the appearance of a second individual.

It is simply the male counterpart to Jane Doe.

### In simple terms:

**Jane Doe = female state**

**John Doe = male state**

**Both are the same person.**

---

# 2. IDENTITY CONTINUITY

There is no evidence of personality replacement, consciousness transfer, possession, or creation of a separate identity.

Jane remains completely aware throughout the transformation.

John remembers being Jane.

John remembers everything Jane experienced before the transformation.

John retains:

- Jane's memories.
    
- Jane's intelligence.
    
- Jane's personality.
    
- Jane's skills.
    
- Jane's relationships.
    
- Jane's knowledge.
    
- Jane's sense of humor.
    
- Jane's professional experience.
    
- Jane's opinions.
    

The only meaningful distinction is the subject's temporary **male physiological state**.

Accordingly, personnel should not treat John Doe as a separate resident.

---

# 3. PHYSICAL TRANSFORMATION

The transformation produces several observable changes.

### Sex

Jane's body temporarily changes from female to male.

This is the defining feature of the anomaly.

### Hair

Her hair becomes noticeably messier.

### Chest

Her chest becomes slightly flatter, consistent with the temporary male physiology.

### Voice

Her voice becomes significantly deeper and more muscular.

The voice change is particularly noticeable because Jane normally has a distinctly feminine voice.

Despite these changes, the subject remains immediately recognizable.

---

# 4. WHY "JOHN DOE"?

The designation was selected because of the obvious relationship between the two placeholder identities.

Jane Doe is the standard placeholder name used for an unidentified woman.

John Doe is the corresponding placeholder name used for an unidentified man.

Therefore:

> **Jane Doe → female**

> **John Doe → male**

The designation is descriptive rather than indicative of a separate identity.

Jane herself has accepted the designation.

---

# 5. STRAWBERRY-SPECIFIC TRIGGER

The anomaly is **not triggered by strawberries as a flavor.**

It requires the strawberry to remain **unprocessed**.

### Confirmed safe:

- Strawberry jam
    
- Strawberry-flavored foods
    
- Strawberry beverages
    
- Strawberry desserts
    
- Other processed strawberry ingredients
    

These do not produce the transformation.

### Confirmed trigger:

> **An intact, unprocessed strawberry.**

The current working conclusion is therefore:

> **Jane is not allergic to strawberries.**

> **Jane is anomalously affected by eating an unprocessed strawberry.**

---

---

# 7. JOHN DOE'S BEHAVIOR

John behaves exactly as Jane normally would.

He can interact with SAFEHOUSE residents normally.

He can perform his usual duties.

He retains Jane's intelligence-extraction abilities.

He retains Jane's intimidation skills.

He retains her memories.

He retains her relationships.

He even retains her habit of terrifying unsuspecting Blacksite workers simply by standing nearby.

The deeper voice does not appear to make him any more or less threatening.

It merely makes the experience significantly more confusing for anyone who has never seen the transformation before.

---

# 8. NERI'S UNDERSTANDING

Neri understands the situation considerably more simply than the Blacksite's medical staff.

When asked whether John was a different person, Neri answered:

> **Neri:** "No."

> **Archivist:** "Why?"

Neri pointed at John.

> **Neri:** "That's Jane."

> **Archivist:** "But he's a man."

Neri shrugged.

> **Neri:** "Jane turned into a man."

The Archivist paused.

> **Archivist:** "...Correct."

Neri returned to her chemistry book.

---

# 9. POST-TRANSFORMATION BEHAVIOR

The transformation itself does not cause Jane distress.

However, Jane strongly dislikes **being deliberately fed an unprocessed strawberry without her consent**.

If someone intentionally triggers the transformation, Jane will remember who did it after returning to her normal state.

Her anger is directed toward the person who deliberately caused the transformation, not toward the existence of John Doe itself.

Personnel are therefore reminded:

> **Do not deliberately trigger anomalous transformations in residents.**

---

The Blacksite Medical Division formally recognizes:

### JANE DOE

**Sex:** Female  
**Normal physiological state:** Yes

### JOHN DOE

**Sex:** Male  
**Physiological state:** Temporary anomalous transformation of Jane Doe  
**Separate individual:** **No**

John Doe is therefore not a second identity, clone, sibling, alter, replacement, or independent entity.

He is simply:

> **Jane Doe, but male.**

The transformation exists for approximately 24 hours before reversing.

The strawberry does not create John.

**The strawberry turns Jane into John.**
