# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Phoenix continues moving through the Banlands.

The civilian reports have become more frequent.

So have the accidents.

Her body is accumulating injuries.

And yet:

> **Phoenix keeps surviving.**

She has survived every documented incident since leaving the SAFEHOUSE.

She does not appear invulnerable.

She does not avoid injury.

She simply continues functioning after injuries that would ordinarily require immediate medical attention.

Her T.O.P.S. condition remains active.

Her body continues to recover through its established Ether-based restoration mechanism.

However, Phoenix is not behaving as though survival means anything.

She gets hurt.

She recovers.

She stands.

She walks.

# 1. INCIDENT — COLLAPSED WALKWAY

**BANLANDS CIVILIAN RECORD**

Phoenix was crossing an elevated pedestrian walkway when part of the structure failed.

She fell through the damaged section.

Multiple civilians reported the incident.

One witness stated:

> **"She should not have gotten back up."**

Approximately four minutes later, Phoenix emerged from below.

She was visibly injured.

She checked herself.

Then continued walking.

A civilian attempted to stop her.

> **CIVILIAN:**  
> "You need help."

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "I'm okay."

> **CIVILIAN:**  
> "You're really not."

Phoenix looked at them.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "I'll be okay."

She continued.

The driver stared.

> **DRIVER:**  
> "Don't move."

Phoenix looked at them.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "Why?"

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "...Oh."
