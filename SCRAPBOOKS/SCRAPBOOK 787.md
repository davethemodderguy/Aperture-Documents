---

# **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

Following the circulation of multiple reports concerning sightings of a small silhouette resembling **Alice**, C.O.R.E. has completed an initial investigation into the phenomenon.

The sightings have been confirmed as **MIRAGES**.

A **Mirage** is a known projection ability used by several SAFEHOUSE residents, most notably:

- **Neko**
    
- **Miss Neko**
    
- **Star**
    

Mirages are capable of producing a holographic or otherwise visually convincing projection of the user.

The projections can resemble the user's normal appearance closely enough to be mistaken for the individual themselves, particularly at a distance, in poor lighting, or during brief encounters.

---

# **CONFIRMED FINDING**

The Alice-like silhouettes observed throughout the Blacksite were **not confirmed sightings of Alice**.

Analysis of available reports and recordings indicates that the figures were Mirage projections.

No physical evidence has been recovered from any of the reported locations.

No biometric confirmation of Alice has been obtained.

No interaction with Alice has been established.

Therefore:

> **ALICE REMAINS MISSING.**

---

# **WHY THE SIGHTINGS WERE MISIDENTIFIED**

Several factors contributed to the confusion.

The projected figures were reported as:

- Small in stature.
    
- Rabbit-eared.
    
- Visually similar to Alice.
    
- Silent.
    
- Briefly visible.
    
- Frequently observed at a distance.
    
- Capable of disappearing almost immediately when approached.
    

These characteristics were sufficient for personnel to reasonably believe they had finally located Alice.

However, Mirages do not necessarily represent the user's current physical location.

A projected image may appear:

- Around corners.
    
- Inside corridors.
    
- At considerable distances.
    
- In areas where the user is not physically present.
    
- For only a short period of time.
    

Consequently, visual confirmation alone is no longer considered sufficient evidence that Alice has been located.

---

# **MIRAGE CAPABILITY**

The ability known as **Mirage** is an established anomalous technique within SAFEHOUSE personnel.

### **Known Users**

**Neko**

Frequently capable of producing highly convincing projections.

**Miss Neko**

Possesses the same general capability and has demonstrated controlled projection.

**Star**

Capable of creating projections through her anomalous abilities.

The exact mechanics differ between users.

However, the practical result is similar:

> **A visual representation of a person can exist independently of their physical presence.**

---

# **SEARCH STATUS**

The clarification does **not** reduce the severity of Alice's disappearance.

In fact, C.O.R.E. has issued an additional warning:

### **Personnel are no longer permitted to treat unidentified Alice-like silhouettes as evidence of recovery.**

Any future sighting must be verified through appropriate means.

Recommended confirmation includes:

- Direct physical contact where safe.
    
- Biometric confirmation.
    
- Verified response to controlled identification.
    
- Confirmation by authorized personnel familiar with Alice.
    
- Medical-sector verification following recovery.
    

Personnel are reminded that chasing a suspected Mirage may accomplish nothing.

---

Security personnel confirmed that no sighting had been verified as Alice.

# **BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

Belle was also informed.

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

The Mirage sightings created a dangerous form of false reassurance.

For several moments, personnel believed Alice might finally be nearby.

Each disappearance therefore produced another confirmation that she had **not** been found.

C.O.R.E. considers this psychologically significant for personnel involved in the search.

Search teams are reminded:

> **A Mirage is not a recovery.**

> **A silhouette is not confirmation.**

> **A report is not a reunion.**
