# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **MEDICAL / SAFEHOUSE INCIDENT REPORT**

# **CORIN — SEAFOOD ALLERGY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Incident Type:** **Severe Allergic Reaction**  
**Status:** Confirmed / Critical Medical Hazard

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has been confirmed to possess a **potentially fatal seafood allergy**.

The allergy was discovered accidentally when Dialyn prepared grilled crab during a visit.

Corin consumed a small amount.

Her reaction was almost immediate.

The severity of the reaction was completely unexpected.

Despite Corin's regenerative abilities and anomalous physiology, her body was unable to immediately compensate for the allergic response.

---

# **THE INCIDENT**

Dialyn had prepared grilled crab for several residents.

Corin was in the kitchen and accepted a portion without knowing that seafood was dangerous to her.

She took a bite.

Within moments, Corin began experiencing severe symptoms.

She stopped eating.

Her breathing became difficult.

Her body rapidly developed signs of systemic allergic reaction.

Dialyn immediately realized something was seriously wrong.

> **Dialyn:**  
> "Corin?"

Corin struggled to respond.

> **"...Something's wrong."**

---

# **MEDICAL RESPONSE**

Emergency treatment was initiated immediately.

Corin's anomalous regeneration began attempting to repair the physiological damage.

However, regeneration alone could not simply remove the underlying allergic reaction.

Her body was responding to the allergen itself.

This resulted in a dangerous interaction between:

- Corin's biological immune system.
    
- Her Ether-enhanced regeneration.
    
- The seafood allergen.
    

Medical personnel intervened before the reaction became irreversible.

Corin eventually stabilized.

---

# **WHY REGENERATION DID NOT PREVENT IT**

Corin's regeneration repairs damaged tissue.

An allergic reaction is different.

The problem is not simply damaged tissue.

Her immune system was actively reacting to the allergen.

Regenerating affected tissue did not immediately stop the immune response.

C.O.R.E. therefore considers Corin's allergy a genuine biological vulnerability despite her otherwise extreme regenerative capabilities.

---

# **DIALYN'S REACTION**

Dialyn was horrified after realizing what had happened.

> **Dialyn:**  
> "I didn't know!"

Corin, still recovering, reassured her:

> **"You couldn't have known."**

Dialyn immediately apologized anyway.

She then personally ensured that every remaining seafood product was removed from the kitchen.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE MEDICAL ALERT**

A permanent warning has now been added to Corin's medical profile:

> **⚠ SEVERE SEAFOOD ALLERGY**  
> **Potentially Fatal**  
> **Avoid Seafood Exposure**

The warning applies to all seafood, including but not necessarily limited to:

- Crab.
    
- Shrimp.
    
- Lobster.
    
- Prawns.
    
- Shellfish.
    
- Other marine food products pending medical confirmation.
    

No resident is permitted to intentionally expose Corin to seafood.

---

# **KITCHEN PROTOCOL**

The SAFEHOUSE kitchen has adopted additional precautions.

Residents preparing seafood must:

- Clearly identify it.
    
- Keep it separate from Corin's food.
    
- Avoid cross-contamination.
    
- Clean cooking surfaces afterward.
    
- Inform Corin if seafood has recently been prepared.
    

Corin's own cooking supplies are also kept separate when necessary.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Once fully recovered, Corin was surprisingly calm about the discovery.

She looked at the medical warning.

> **"...So I can't eat crab."**

Orphie nodded.

> **"You almost died."**

Corin paused.

> **"...Okay. No crab."**

She then looked at Dialyn.

> **"The seasoning was good, though."**

Everyone in the room stared at her.

Corin immediately added:

> **"I'm not going to eat it again."**

---

# **MEDICAL SIGNIFICANCE**

The incident provides an important reminder that Corin's return to biological life has also restored ordinary biological vulnerabilities.

She can:

- Bleed.
    
- Become injured.
    
- Need oxygen.
    
- Experience biological illness.
    
- Have allergic reactions.
    

Her anomalous abilities do not make her immune to every conventional medical hazard.

In fact, her regeneration may make some biological interactions more complicated.

---

# **FINAL STATUS**

**Seafood allergy:** Confirmed.

**Severity:** Potentially fatal.

**Regeneration:** Does not reliably prevent allergic reactions.

**Ether:** Does not neutralize the allergen automatically.

**Seafood consumption:** **Prohibited.**

**Current condition:** Stable.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin spent years believing that being dead made her immune to ordinary human problems.

Now she is learning the opposite.

She has a heartbeat.

She has blood.

She needs oxygen.

She can heal.

She can enjoy food.

And apparently...

She can also have a life-threatening seafood allergy.

The first confirmed trigger happened to be a plate of grilled crab prepared by Dialyn.

The crab was excellent.

Unfortunately, Corin will never be eating it again.
