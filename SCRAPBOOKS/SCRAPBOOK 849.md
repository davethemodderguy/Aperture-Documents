# NERI — DIETARY & DENTAL ANOMALY ADDENDUM

## **“SHE CAN EAT THE CAKE.”**

**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — SAFEHOUSE RESIDENTIAL  
**ACCESS:** Level 3+ Personnel  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**SPECIES:** Seal Thiren  
**DIETARY PROFILE:** Omnivorous; strong preference for sweets and fish  
**DENTAL STATUS:** Anomalously resistant to dental decay  
**PRIMARY FOOD PROVIDERS:** Bao / Curious Light

---

# 1. SUMMARY

Further biological observation has confirmed that Neri's enthusiasm for sweets is not accompanied by the dental consequences normally expected from such a diet.

Neri's teeth demonstrate an apparent **immunity to cavities**.

Repeated observation has failed to produce conventional dental decay despite her unusually high consumption of:

- Cake
    
- Candy
    
- Chocolate
    
- Pastries
    
- Pudding
    
- Ice cream
    
- Sweetened drinks
    
- Other foods that would normally be considered unfavorable for dental health
    

The mechanism remains unknown.

---

# 2. SEAL THIREN METABOLISM

Neri's Seal Thiren physiology appears to provide additional metabolic advantages regarding her diet.

The exact extent of these adaptations remains under investigation.

However, current observations suggest that her semi-aquatic/seal-derived physiology allows her to tolerate high quantities of sweets with considerably fewer negative consequences than would be expected in a baseline human.

This does **not** mean Neri can survive exclusively on sugar.

She still requires a nutritionally balanced diet.

She simply has an unusually impressive ability to follow dinner with an unreasonable amount of cake.

---

# 3. NORMAL DIET

Despite her reputation within SAFEHOUSE as:

> **“The girl who will eat an entire cake if you let her,”**

Neri does eat normal meals.

Her preferred ordinary foods include:

### Fish

Her strongest non-sweet preference.

Neri enjoys fish prepared in a variety of ways, including grilled, fried, baked, and incorporated into soups or other meals.

### Vegetables

Neri eats vegetables without significant complaint, particularly when prepared properly.

### Other Foods

Neri is not particularly restrictive regarding ordinary meals.

She generally eats whatever is prepared for her, provided it is something she likes.

---

# 4. IDEAL COOKS

Two individuals consistently produce meals that Neri considers particularly desirable.

### BAO

Bao's cooking is already strongly associated with comfort and safety for Neri.

This extends beyond baking.

Neri particularly enjoys normal meals prepared by Bao, especially fish and vegetables.

When Bao cooks:

> **NERI:** “Mom made it!”

Consumption rate increases significantly.

---

### CURIOUS LIGHT

Curious Light is similarly capable of producing meals that Neri enjoys.

His proficiency with grilling and frying is especially useful for preparing fish.

Whenever Curious Light visits SAFEHOUSE and prepares food, Neri has demonstrated a tendency to appear in the kitchen suspiciously quickly.

No notification system exists.

Nobody knows how she knows.

---

# 5. OBSERVED MEAL PATTERN

A typical Neri meal may therefore resemble:

**Fish + vegetables + normal side dishes**

followed by:

**Cake**

followed by:

**Candy**

followed by:

> **“Do we have more cake?”**

The answer is frequently yes.

This has not resulted in measurable dental deterioration.

Medical staff remain mildly offended by the biological unfairness of this.

---

# 6. DENTAL EXAMINATION

Routine dental examinations have produced consistently unusual results.

### Findings:

- No significant cavity formation.
    
- No conventional sugar-related dental deterioration.
    
- Enamel remains structurally robust.
    
- Tooth integrity remains unusually high.
    
- No correlation between sweet consumption and observed decay.
    

One dental examiner reportedly reviewed Neri's dietary records, reviewed her dental scans, and asked:

> **“How much cake does she actually eat?”**

The answer was provided.

The examiner stared at the chart.

Then at Neri.

Then back at the chart.

> **“That's medically rude.”**

---

# 7. IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION

Neri's dental immunity does **not** mean personnel should deliberately encourage an exclusively sweet-based diet.

Neri still benefits from:

- Protein
    
- Vegetables
    
- Fish
    
- General nutritional variety
    
- Hydration
    
- Proper meals
    

Her unusual physiology simply means that **sweets are considerably less problematic for her teeth and metabolism than they would ordinarily be.**

In practical terms:

> Cake is permitted.

> Candy is permitted.

> A balanced meal is still expected.

---

---

# 9. ARCHIVIST OBSERVATION

One particularly efficient SAFEHOUSE dinner was documented as follows:

Bao prepared fish and vegetables.

Neri ate the entire meal.

Curious Light subsequently arrived and brought additional food.

Neri ate that too.

Guiding Light later brought cake.

Neri immediately accepted it.

She finished her slice.

Then looked at Guiding Light.

> **NERI:** “More?”

Guiding Light gave her another slice.

Neri smiled.

No behavioral incident occurred for the remainder of the evening.

The Archivist responsible for the report added:

> **“We have accidentally discovered the most powerful containment protocol in C.O.R.E. history.”**

A second Archivist corrected this:

> **“It's not containment. She's just happy.”**

The first Archivist considered this.

> **“Even better.”**

---

# 10. CURRENT ASSESSMENT

Neri's diet should not be reduced to her love of sweets.

She is a normal eater in many respects.

She enjoys proper meals.

She likes fish.

She eats vegetables.

She especially appreciates food prepared by people she trusts.

The sweets are simply an unusually important source of happiness.

Her resistance to cavities makes the situation considerably easier.

Her Seal Thiren physiology makes the dietary situation even stranger.

C.O.R.E. therefore recommends continuing to provide Neri with a varied diet while allowing reasonable access to sweets.

Particularly cake.
