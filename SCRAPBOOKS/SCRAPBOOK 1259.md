The apparent contradiction surrounding Neri's complete recovery from her previously persistent anemia has been resolved.

Neri did not spontaneously recover.

Her anemia was **transferred.**

Specifically:

> **Nora took it.**

The mechanism appears to have occurred during the period in which Nora's own physical condition began deteriorating while Neri's long-standing anemia abruptly disappeared.

The transfer was not initially detected because neither subject experienced a conventional blood-loss event.

Instead, the Tachyon sisters appear capable of transferring certain physiological conditions through their anomalous biological connection.

In practical terms:

**Neri's anemia moved to Nora.**

Nora is therefore currently anemic.

Neri is not.
