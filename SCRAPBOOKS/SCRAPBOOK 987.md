# APERTURE BLACKSITE

## SUBSPACE EXPLORATION REPORT

### VISUAL CONFIRMATION OF NERI

**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE / HIGH PRIORITY  
**Subject:** Neri  
**Subspace:** Confirmed  
**Known Archipelago:** Confirmed Present  
**Subject Status:** **ALIVE — VISUALLY CONFIRMED**  
**Recon Asset:** Autonomous Survey Drone  
**Current Status:** **LOST**

---

# I. THE SEARCH

The exploration team continued traveling through Neri's subspace from the entrance discovered at the former Architect transformation site.

For a considerable distance, there was nothing.

No islands.

No structures.

No artificial stars.

Only empty subspace and increasingly intermittent concentrations of Azure Light.

The team began considering the possibility that Neri's archipelago had been moved.

They were wrong.

Eventually, the drone's long-range sensors detected something.

A gravitational anomaly.

Then another.

Then several more.

The instruments began reporting multiple large spatial structures.

The team stopped.

The drone continued forward.

---

# II. THE ARCHIPELAGO

The first island appeared on the drone's camera.

Then another.

Then another.

The rest followed.

### **The fifteen islands were still there.**

The primary inhabited island remained intact.

The rope bridges remained.

The artificial stellar sky remained.

The localized gravitational regions remained.

The environment showed no evidence of catastrophic collapse.

Neri had not destroyed her home.

She had not dismantled it.

She had not erased it.

### She had simply gone somewhere else.

---

# III. THE EMPTY HOME

The drone performed a slow survey of the primary island.

The area was remarkably quiet.

Neri's laboratory remained.

Her residential area remained.

Her belongings remained.

Nothing indicated that the subspace itself had been abandoned permanently.

The place looked less like a destroyed home—

and more like a home whose occupant had stepped outside.

Except nobody knew where she had gone.

---

# IV. THE SIGHTING

The drone began expanding its search radius.

At approximately ██:██, the camera detected movement.

A humanoid figure.

Small.

Wearing a familiar oversized coat.

The drone's recognition system initially flagged the subject as an unknown entity.

It then compared the silhouette against archived SAFEHOUSE footage.

The match exceeded the confirmation threshold.

The drone transmitted:

> **IDENTIFICATION: NERI**

The control room went silent.

The camera zoomed.

There she was.

---

# V. NERI

Neri was alive.

She appeared physically intact.

She was walking through the subspace.

She was not unconscious.

She was not restrained.

She was not being pursued.

She was simply—

**walking.**

Her oversized coat was visible.

Her blue eyes were visible.

Her glasses were visible.

The drone recorded approximately ██ seconds of footage.

Neri appeared to notice something in the distance.

She stopped.

Looked toward it.

The drone.

---

# VI. THE LAST TRANSMISSION

The drone's camera captured Neri staring directly at it.

For several seconds, neither moved.

The control room erupted.

> “THAT'S HER.”

> “Confirm the feed.”

> “It's Neri.”

> “She's alive.”

> “Get the drone closer.”

The drone moved forward.

Neri remained still.

She tilted her head slightly.

The drone's telemetry began degrading.

Azure particulate started appearing across the camera feed.

The image became increasingly obscured by blue static-like particles.

Then Neri raised one hand.

The transmission abruptly ended.

---

# VII. DRONE STATUS

**Connection:** Lost  
**Telemetry:** Lost  
**Visual feed:** Lost  
**Position:** Unknown  
**Recovery:** Unsuccessful

The drone is presumed destroyed or otherwise disabled.

No conventional projectile was detected.

No explosion was recorded.

The final frame showed a rapidly expanding concentration of Azure Light surrounding the drone.

The exact mechanism responsible for its destruction remains unknown.

---

# VIII. THE IMPORTANT PART

The drone's final transmission contained enough information to establish several facts.

### CONFIRMED:

**Neri is alive.**

**Neri remains inside her subspace.**

**The fifteen-island archipelago still exists.**

**The archipelago remains structurally intact.**

**Neri is capable of freely moving through her subspace.**

**Neri noticed the drone.**

And most importantly:

### **Neri knows someone has found her.**

---

# IX. THE DISTANCE

The location of the archipelago relative to the newly discovered entrance has finally been measured.

It is considerably farther away than previously estimated.

The entrance was not located near the islands.

It was effectively an extremely distant access point into the same spatial environment.

The previous assumption that the empty space represented a separate region was therefore incorrect.

The Blacksite had simply entered Neri's subspace **very far from home.**

---

# X. GUIDING LIGHT

Guiding Light was shown the final footage.

She watched it twice.

On the third viewing, she paused the recording.

Neri was looking directly at the drone.

Guiding Light smiled.

> “She knows.”

Neko asked:

> “Knows what?”

Guiding Light looked at the screen.

> “That we're looking for her.”

---

# XI. BAO

Bao watched the footage without speaking.

The moment Neri appeared on screen, she covered her mouth.

Her eyes filled with tears.

> “She's okay.”

Nobody corrected her.

Nobody told her that Neri's medical condition could not be determined from the footage.

For the first time in months, Bao had something she desperately needed.

Not hope.

### Evidence.

Neri was alive.

---

# XII. JANE

Jane watched the footage last.

She stared at Neri's face.

Then at the moment Neri looked directly into the camera.

Jane smiled.

> “There you are.”

She leaned back.

> “You little idiot.”

A pause.

> “You scared everyone half to death.”

She looked at the dead drone's final frame.

> “...Good to see you.”

---

# XIII. ALICE

Alice immediately asked:

> “Can we go get her?”

Neko answered:

> “We're working on it.”

Alice pointed at the screen.

> “She's right there.”

Neko sighed.

> “Yes.”

> “So go.”

> “It's not that simple.”

Alice folded her arms.

> “It should be.”

Nobody had a good response.

---

# XIV. THE NEW PROBLEM

The Blacksite has solved one mystery.

It has created another.

They now know exactly what they wanted to know:

**Neri is alive.**

But they also know something else.

Neri is not trapped inside the subspace.

She can move.

She can explore.

She can reach places the Blacksite cannot.

And when the drone approached her—

she did not immediately attempt to return.

She simply looked at it.

Then the drone was lost.

Whether Neri herself disabled it remains unknown.

Whether the subspace reacted automatically remains unknown.

Whether she was protecting herself, protecting the subspace, or simply avoiding contact remains unknown.

---

# XV. CURRENT OBJECTIVE

The rescue operation has been updated.

The objective is no longer:

> **FIND NERI.**

That objective has been completed.

The new objective is:

> **ESTABLISH SAFE COMMUNICATION WITH NERI.**

No further drones are to be sent without approval.

No attempt is to be made to forcibly breach the archipelago.

No personnel are to assume that Neri wants to be approached simply because she was observed.

The next step must be careful.
