# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### RESIDENT WELFARE & ANOMALOUS LIFE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-PHOENIX-216  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — RESIDENT WELFARE / ANOMALOUS FAUNA  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**SUBJECT:** Phoenix Raffedella — Unregistered Avian Companion  
**LOCATION:** BLACKSITE ROOFTOP

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Blacksite has apparently been housing a phoenix.

Nobody knew.

The animal had been living somewhere on the Blacksite roof for an undetermined period of time.

It successfully avoided:

- security personnel;
    
- maintenance teams;
    
- researchers;
    
- rooftop inspections;
    
- cameras;
    
- environmental surveys;
    
- and apparently everyone else.
    

Its concealment ability was considered unusually effective.

Until Phoenix Raffedella walked onto the roof and called:

> **“Phoenix.”**

The bird came over.

C.O.R.E. immediately began asking questions.

---

# 1. INITIAL DISCOVERY

The rooftop was undergoing routine inspection when Phoenix Raffedella accompanied a maintenance team.

She stopped approximately halfway across the roof.

She looked toward an area containing ventilation equipment.

The maintenance worker continued walking.

Phoenix did not.

> **MAINTENANCE WORKER:**  
> “Something wrong?”

Phoenix looked toward the equipment.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “No.”

Pause.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Phoenix?”

Nothing happened.

The maintenance worker looked confused.

> **MAINTENANCE WORKER:**  
> “Yes?”

Phoenix shook her head.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Not you.”

She called again.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Phoenix.”

A few seconds passed.

Then something moved behind the ventilation equipment.

---

# 2. THE BIRD

A large avian creature emerged.

It was unmistakably a phoenix.

Its plumage displayed brilliant red, orange, and gold coloration.

Its feathers appeared to produce a faint warmth despite the surrounding air.

The creature walked directly toward Phoenix.

The maintenance worker stared.

Phoenix crouched down.

The phoenix approached her without hesitation.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “There you are.”

The bird made a quiet chirping sound.

Phoenix reached out.

The phoenix lowered its head.

She gently stroked its feathers.

The maintenance worker remained speechless.

---

# 3. THE SECURITY PROBLEM

The maintenance worker immediately contacted Security.

Security contacted C.O.R.E.

C.O.R.E. contacted Medical.

Medical contacted KCA.

KCA contacted Phoenix.

Phoenix looked at everyone.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “What?”

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “Where did you get a phoenix?”

Phoenix looked at the bird.

Then back at the officer.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “He's mine.”

Silence.

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “You have a phoenix.”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Yes.”

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “On the roof.”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Yes.”

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “How long?”

Phoenix shrugged.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “A while.”

---

# 4. HOW DID NOBODY FIND IT?

This became the immediate investigative question.

The Blacksite possesses extensive security systems.

The rooftop is not normally considered an inaccessible area.

Yet the phoenix had apparently established a concealed nesting location without being detected.

C.O.R.E. reviewed:

- security footage;
    
- rooftop access logs;
    
- environmental monitoring;
    
- maintenance records;
    
- thermal scans;
    
- aerial observations;
    
- anomalous detection reports.
    

The results were inconclusive.

The bird had somehow avoided nearly every routine detection method.

It was exceptionally good at hiding.

---

# 5. PHOENIX'S EXPLANATION

Phoenix appeared completely unsurprised.

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “How did you know where it was?”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “I knew.”

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “How?”

Phoenix shrugged.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “It's my pet.”

This explanation was technically correct.

It was also not particularly useful.

---

# 6. THE CALL

C.O.R.E. requested that Phoenix demonstrate how she located the animal.

Phoenix turned toward the rooftop.

She called:

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Phoenix.”

Nothing.

She waited.

Then:

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Come here.”

The phoenix immediately emerged from behind the ventilation structure.

It crossed the roof.

Walked directly toward her.

And sat beside her.

The entire observation team stared.

Phoenix looked at them.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “See?”

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “That's how you found it?”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Yes.”

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “You just called it?”

Phoenix nodded.

---

# 7. JANE DOE

Jane was subsequently informed.

She went to the roof.

She looked at the phoenix.

Then at Phoenix.

Then back at the phoenix.

> **JANE:**  
> “You have a phoenix?”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Yes.”

> **JANE:**  
> “And you just... didn't tell anyone?”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Nobody asked.”

Jane stared at her.

> **JANE:**  
> “That's fair.”

The phoenix walked over to Jane.

Jane cautiously held out a hand.

The bird inspected it.

Then accepted the contact.

Jane smiled.

> **JANE:**  
> “Okay.”

Pause.

> **JANE:**  
> “I like him.”

Phoenix nodded.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “He likes you.”

---

# 8. JAKE'S REACTION

Jake was eventually brought to the roof.

He saw the phoenix.

His eyes widened.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Bird.”

Phoenix looked at him.

The phoenix looked at Jake.

Jake looked at Phoenix.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Your bird?”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Yes.”

Jake crouched down.

The phoenix approached.

Jake gently touched its feathers.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Warm.”

Phoenix nodded.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Very.”

Jake smiled.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Cool.”

---

# 9. NERI'S REACTION

Neri was less enthusiastic about the discovery.

She looked at the phoenix.

Then at Phoenix.

Then at the roof.

Then back at Phoenix.

> **NERI:**  
> “How?”

Phoenix shrugged.

Neri waited.

Phoenix shrugged again.

Neri adjusted her glasses.

> **NERI:**  
> “You just have a phoenix.”

Phoenix nodded.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Apparently.”

Neri stared at the bird.

The bird stared back.

Neri eventually gave it a small piece of cake.

The phoenix accepted it.

Neri looked at Phoenix.

> **NERI:**  
> “Okay.”

---

# 10. THE NAME

C.O.R.E. asked whether the phoenix had a name.

Phoenix responded:

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Phoenix.”

The room became silent.

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “Your name is Phoenix.”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Yes.”

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “And your pet phoenix is also named Phoenix.”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Yes.”

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “That's going to cause problems.”

Phoenix considered this.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Probably.”

---

# 11. COMMUNICATION PROBLEM

The problem became apparent almost immediately.

A KCA employee called across the roof:

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “Phoenix!”

Both the woman and the bird turned around.

The employee froze.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “...Phoenix the Medical Assistant.”

Phoenix pointed toward herself.

The bird remained still.

Another employee called:

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “Phoenix!”

The bird flew over.

The medical assistant remained where she was.

A third employee sighed.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “This is going to be awful.”

---

# 12. ROOFTOP RESIDENCY

C.O.R.E. has determined that the phoenix may remain on the Blacksite roof provided that appropriate welfare and safety measures are established.

The bird appears to have selected the rooftop voluntarily.

It has demonstrated:

- independent feeding;
    
- environmental awareness;
    
- strong attachment to Phoenix;
    
- high concealment ability;
    
- tolerance toward SAFEHOUSE residents;
    
- apparent intelligence;
    
- and no observed hostility toward personnel.
    

Phoenix visits the roof regularly.

The phoenix generally appears when she calls.

Otherwise, it disappears somewhere among the rooftop structures.

---

# 13. PHOENIX'S PET

Phoenix's relationship with the bird is notably quiet.

She does not constantly interact with it.

She does not carry it around.

She does not treat it as an unusual specimen.

She simply visits.

Sometimes she sits beside it.

Sometimes she feeds it.

Sometimes she talks to it.

Sometimes she falls asleep beside it.

On one occasion, Phoenix reportedly fell asleep sitting against the phoenix.

The bird remained beside her until she woke.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The Blacksite has gained an anomalous resident-adjacent animal.

It was not imported through official channels.

It was not registered with Security.

It was not detected by standard rooftop monitoring.

It apparently lives on the roof.

And it listens when Phoenix calls.

No immediate behavioral concerns have been identified.

The animal is therefore being classified as:

> **PHOENIX'S PET.**

Further naming conventions remain under review.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Phoenix has spent most of her life surrounded by things that should not have survived.

She survived childhood.

She survived abandonment.

She survived losing her cat.

She survived T.O.P.S.

She survived death.

She keeps helping other people survive.

And apparently, somewhere along the way, she acquired a phoenix.

Nobody knows where it came from.

Nobody knows how long it has been on the roof.

Nobody even knows how it avoided the entire Blacksite for so long.

It was simply there.

Waiting.

Until Phoenix called.

And it came running.

Which may be the strangest part of the entire discovery.

Not that the Blacksite had a phoenix.

Not that it was exceptionally good at hiding.

But that Phoenix apparently knew exactly where it was.

She didn't search for it.

She didn't track it.

She didn't need a scanner.

She just called its name.

And her phoenix came home.
