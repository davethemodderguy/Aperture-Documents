### **CORE-ANOMALOUS ABILITIES DIVISION**

# **ALICE — POST-RECOVERY ABILITY CORRELATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Restricted Personnel / Anomalous Investigation  
**Subject:** Alice  
**Status:** **RECOVERED — MEDICALLY CRITICAL**  
**Related Investigation:** Unknown Cult / Empty Grave Site

---

# **SUMMARY**

Following Alice's recovery from the snow-covered grave site, C.O.R.E. has identified a previously under-documented aspect of Alice's anomalous abilities.

Alice possesses the ability to manifest **paper and drawings directly from her mind**.

The materialization typically occurs in sudden bursts.

The resulting papers may contain:

- Drawings.
    
- Handwritten statements.
    
- Symbols.
    
- Sketches of people.
    
- Repeated words or phrases.
    
- Images corresponding to Alice's memories, thoughts, fears, or questions.
    

The paper itself appears to be physically real following manifestation.

This ability provides a likely explanation for the unusually large quantity of papers discovered surrounding Alice during her recovery.

---

# **ABILITY DESIGNATION**

### **MENTAL DOCUMENT MANIFESTATION**

**Informal Name:** Paper Burst

**Primary Effect:**

> Alice can externalize thoughts and imagery by producing physical sheets of paper containing drawings or written information.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

No conventional writing instrument has been identified as the source of the recovered material.

---

# **RECOVERED SITE — REASSESSMENT**

Prior to identification of this ability, investigators considered the large quantity of papers surrounding Alice to potentially indicate that another individual had been leaving documents at the site.

This hypothesis is now considered substantially less likely.

The papers recovered from the snow are consistent with Alice's known manifestation ability.

This includes several particularly significant documents.

### **"Who am I?"**

### **"Who is Mom?"**

### **"Where is Mom?"**

As well as numerous drawings containing blacked-out faces with white question marks.

These may therefore represent Alice's own attempts to process her memories and identity.

---

# **THE PAPERS WERE NOT NECESSARILY LEFT THERE BY SOMEONE ELSE**

This distinction is important.

Alice did not necessarily find these documents.

She may have **created them herself**.

The current working theory is that Alice's mental state, combined with her anomalous ability, resulted in repeated manifestations while she was isolated at the grave.

This could explain why the documents were found:

- Directly around Alice.
    
- Partially beneath her.
    
- Scattered through the surrounding snow.
    
- In varying orientations.
    
- In large quantities.
    
- Without evidence of conventional transportation to the location.
    

---

# **MEMORY CORRELATION**

The contents of the papers are consistent with Alice's established memory difficulties.

Several recovered sheets appear to repeatedly question her own identity.

Others focus specifically on her mother.

The recurring question:

> **"Who is Mom?"**

is considered particularly significant.

Alice previously demonstrated substantial loss of autobiographical memory concerning her mother.

The manifestation ability may provide a mechanism through which fragments of those memories are being externalized despite Alice's conscious inability to recall them.

C.O.R.E. cannot currently determine whether the drawings represent:

- Memories.
    
- Partial memories.
    
- Subconscious associations.
    
- Dreams.
    
- Fear responses.
    
- Fabricated imagery.
    
- Anomalous information.
    

Further analysis is required.

---

# **THE BLACKED-OUT FACES**

The drawings depicting people with completely black faces and white question marks remain under investigation.

Importantly, the blacked-out faces do not appear to have been physically obscured after the drawings were created.

The markings appear to have been **manifested as part of the original drawing**.

This suggests that Alice's ability does not simply reproduce what she sees.

It may instead reproduce what she **remembers, believes, or is capable of conceptualizing**.

If Alice cannot identify a person's face within her own memory, the resulting manifestation may reflect that uncertainty.

This remains theoretical.

---

# **CULT SYMBOL**

The discovery of the unidentified cult symbol remains separate from the paper-manifestation explanation.

Alice's ability explains **how the papers may have appeared**.

It does **not** explain who created the symbol found around the grave.

Several possibilities remain open:

### **Possibility A — Alice drew it**

Alice may have encountered the symbol previously and reproduced it through her ability.

### **Possibility B — The symbol predates Alice's arrival**

The grave site may have already been associated with the unidentified organization.

### **Possibility C — Alice remembers the symbol subconsciously**

The symbol may be connected to events she can no longer consciously recall.

### **Possibility D — External involvement**

Another individual or organization may have placed the symbol at the site.

No conclusion has been reached.

---

# **ABILITY OBSERVATION**

During medical observation, Alice produced a brief manifestation.

A small burst of paper appeared beside her.

The sheet contained a simple drawing.

No external writing instrument was present.

Alice looked at the paper for several seconds.

She then quietly said:

> "...I draw that?"

Medical personnel did not answer immediately.

Alice folded the paper and held it against herself.

No further manifestations were observed during the examination.

---

# **C.O.R.E. DIRECTIVE**

Personnel are instructed **not to request Alice to manifest documents** for investigative purposes.

The ability appears to be closely connected to her mental and emotional state.

Attempting to force manifestations may:

- Distress Alice.
    
- Produce unreliable material.
    
- Interfere with her recovery.
    
- Potentially expose additional traumatic memories.
    

Any spontaneously manifested material is to be collected and preserved without pressuring Alice for an explanation.

---

# **UPDATED INVESTIGATIVE ASSESSMENT**

The discovery changes the interpretation of the grave site.

The papers are no longer evidence that someone necessarily stayed with Alice.

Instead, they may be evidence that **Alice spent a prolonged period alone, repeatedly externalizing fragments of her thoughts onto paper.**

She may have been asking herself the same questions over and over.

> **Who am I?**

> **Who is Mom?**

> **Who are they?**

And perhaps most painfully:

> **Why can't Alice remember?**

The empty grave and unidentified cult symbol remain unexplained.

The papers, however, may have been Alice's own.

She may have been leaving pieces of her mind behind because she could no longer carry them herself.
