### **CELESTIAL PRESENCE / COMMUNICATION ADDENDUM**

# **GUIDING LIGHT — THE BLUE GLOW**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – CELESTIAL AUTHORITY  
**Designation:** **GUIDING LIGHT**  
**Role:** Celestial Advisor / Architect  
**Known Presence:** **OMNIPRESENT**  
**Communication Method:** **DIRECTED ATTENTION**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Guiding Light is not confined to a physical location.

She is, by all known observations, **everywhere**.

Personnel cannot determine where she is located because there is no evidence that she occupies a conventional location at all.

She can observe from anywhere.

She can communicate from anywhere.

And, when she wants someone to notice something...

they will.

---

# **THE BLUE GLOW**

Guiding Light's most recognizable sign is a **soft blue glow**.

It is not bright.

It does not illuminate an entire room.

It simply appears around whatever Guiding Light wants someone to notice.

The glow may surround:

- A letter.
    
- A key.
    
- An object someone has misplaced.
    
- A doorway.
    
- A person.
    
- A particular location.
    
- Something the recipient needs.
    
- Something the recipient should avoid.
    

The glow is subtle enough that it feels almost natural.

Until the observer realizes it wasn't there a moment ago.

---

# **DIRECTED GUIDANCE**

Guiding Light does not always leave written instructions.

Sometimes she simply highlights something.

For example, if someone is searching for their keys, they may suddenly notice a faint blue glow coming from underneath a chair.

The keys are there.

No note.

No explanation.

Just the glow.

If they pick them up, the glow disappears.

---

# **THE GLOW IS INTENTIONAL**

The phenomenon does not occur randomly.

Guiding Light decides who can perceive it.

If she wants a specific person to notice something, that person will see the glow.

Others may see nothing unusual.

This allows her to guide individuals without interrupting everyone around them.

---

# **EXAMPLE — ORPHIE**

Orphie once searched the treehouse for an important letter.

She couldn't find it.

A faint blue glow appeared beneath a stack of books.

She moved the books.

The letter was there.

Orphie stared at the glow.

> "...You could've just told me."

The glow disappeared.

A moment later, blue writing appeared on the letter.

> **"You found it."**

Orphie sighed.

> "...You're annoying."

The blue glow briefly returned.

Then vanished.

---

# **EXAMPLE — CORIN**

Corin once left her keys somewhere inside the treehouse.

She searched everywhere.

Eventually, a soft blue glow appeared beneath the couch.

Corin looked underneath.

Her keys were there.

> "...Really?"

The glow pulsed once.

Corin laughed.

> "...Thanks."

The glow disappeared.

---

# **NO FIXED LOCATION**

Attempts to locate Guiding Light have produced no useful results.

She does not appear to occupy:

- Starlands.
    
- Banlands.
    
- The Blacksite.
    
- Any known dimensional space.
    

She can appear to communicate from anywhere without physically traveling.

This is consistent with her status as one of the **Architects of reality**.

---

# **NOT AN ATTACK**

The blue glow itself is harmless.

It does not alter objects.

It does not manipulate the recipient.

It merely directs attention.

Guiding Light has demonstrated that she can influence reality far more directly if necessary.

She simply chooses not to.

Her preferred approach remains:

> **Guide, don't control.**

---

# **THE SOFTER SIDE**

The glow is not always practical.

Sometimes it appears simply to reassure someone.

A person sitting alone may notice a soft blue glow beside them.

Nothing is there.

No message.

No instruction.

Just a quiet blue light.

After several seconds, it disappears.

Guiding Light has never explained these occurrences.

She doesn't appear to need to.

---

# **PERSONNEL UNDERSTANDING**

Personnel have gradually learned what the glow means.

If you see it...

**Pay attention.**

It means Guiding Light wants you to notice something.

It might be important.

It might be useful.

It might simply be something she thinks you should see.

But if the glow appears somewhere...

there is a reason.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Guiding Light is everywhere.

She does not need to walk through a door.

She does not need to announce her presence.

She does not need to manifest before someone.

If she wants you to notice something, reality itself seems to quietly point toward it.

A key beneath a couch.

A letter on a desk.

A person who needs help.

A path that shouldn't be taken.

A reminder that someone is watching.

And sometimes...

nothing at all.

Just a soft blue glow.

A quiet indication from one of reality's Architects:

> **"Look here."**

**End of Celestial Presence / Communication Addendum**
