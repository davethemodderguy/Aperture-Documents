# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neri Tachyon has officially begun accepting orders for:

> **TACHYON INDUSTRIES LIMITED MERCHANDISE — AUTHORIZED ATTACHMENTS & ACCESSORIES**

The program was established following repeated compatibility issues between Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise and conventional aftermarket equipment.

Although conventional attachments may physically appear compatible with certain Limited Merchandise items, they are not guaranteed to function correctly.

More importantly:

> **The Limited Merchandise does not appear to particularly like them.**

Neri has therefore elected to personally approve and produce compatible accessories.

---

# 2. THE PROBLEM

The original Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise was designed as a series of highly individualized experimental weapons.

They were not developed around a conventional commercial aftermarket ecosystem.

Consequently, operators attempting to use ordinary accessories encountered several problems.

Reported examples include:

- poor compatibility;
    
- unusual mechanical behavior;
    
- unreliable attachment interfaces;
    
- accessories refusing to remain properly seated;
    
- anomalous material interactions;
    
- unexpected behavior from Tachyon-derived components;
    
- and, in one particularly unfortunate case, an optic that apparently decided it no longer wanted to cooperate.
    

No serious injuries resulted.

Neri's response was considerably simpler:

> **“I'll make them.”**

---

# 3. NEW PROGRAM

Neri has opened a formal ordering system.

Authorized owners of Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise may now submit requests for:

- optics;
    
- rail extensions;
    
- mounting equipment;
    
- stocks;
    
- grips;
    
- magazine-related accessories;
    
- protective equipment;
    
- carrying equipment;
    
- cases;
    
- cosmetic components;
    
- identification markings;
    
- and other platform-specific accessories.
    

All requests remain subject to Neri's approval.

---

# 4. CORE PRINCIPLE

The program operates under a straightforward rule:

> **If the merchandise does not like conventional attachments, give it attachments it does like.**

Neri's accessories are designed specifically around the individual Tachyon Industries platform.

They are not intended for general commercial distribution.

They are:

> **Limited Merchandise Accessories.**

---

# 5. PLATFORM-SPECIFIC DESIGN

Neri does not maintain a universal attachment catalogue.

Each request is evaluated according to the specific weapon.

For example:

### NIGHT STALKER

May receive platform-specific:

- optic mounts;
    
- laser assemblies;
    
- cosmetic components;
    
- carrying accessories;
    
- and other approved equipment.
    

### SLEIGHT OF HAND

May receive:

- optic-related equipment;
    
- stock accessories;
    
- mounting components;
    
- and platform-specific cosmetic modifications.
    

### CEBERUS

May receive:

- rail extension;
    
- scope mounting equipment;
    
- approved optics;
    
- stock components;
    
- and other compatible accessories.
    

The accessory is therefore treated as part of the **individual Limited Merchandise ecosystem**, rather than generic aftermarket equipment.

# 6. APPROVAL PROCESS

An owner submits an accessory request to Neri.

The request includes:

**OWNER**

**WEAPON**

**UNIT NUMBER**

**REQUESTED ACCESSORY**

**INTENDED USE**

**OPTIONAL COSMETIC PREFERENCES**

Neri reviews the request.

Possible responses include:

> **APPROVED**

> **APPROVED — MODIFIED**

> **DENIED**

> **ASK ME LATER**

> **I CAN MAKE SOMETHING BETTER**

The final decision belongs to Neri.

# 8. CEberus DMR CONFIGURATION

The new program immediately resolves a previously documented issue involving Ceberus.

Operators wishing to use:

> **Ceberus + slug ammunition + optic**

may now request an authorized:

> **Ceberus Rail Extension Kit**

from Neri.

Approved optics may subsequently be mounted using the authorized configuration.

This allows the previously demonstrated mid-range DMR-like configuration to become an officially supported Limited Merchandise configuration.

It does not create a new Ceberus variant.

---

# 9. CONVENTIONAL ATTACHMENT POLICY

Personnel are advised:

> **Do not assume conventional attachments are compatible.**

A conventional accessory should not be installed simply because it appears physically compatible.

Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise is individually controlled equipment.

Unauthorized accessory installation may result in:

- poor performance;
    
- equipment damage;
    
- incompatibility;
    
- loss of authorization;
    
- or Neri becoming visibly annoyed.
    

The final item is considered a legitimate operational concern.

# 12. LIMITED PRODUCTION

The program does not mean unlimited accessory production.

Neri has explicitly retained the **Limited Merchandise** philosophy.

Accessories are:

- individually produced;
    
- individually approved;
    
- platform-specific;
    
- owner-specific where appropriate;
    
- and limited in availability.
    

There is no public storefront.

There is no mass-market catalogue.

There will not suddenly be thousands of Tachyon-branded accessories.

# 14. AUTHORIZATION

All accessories personally approved by Neri under this program are considered:

> **AUTHORIZED TACHYON INDUSTRIES LIMITED MERCHANDISE ACCESSORIES**

They are recognized by C.O.R.E. and MSAC as compatible equipment.

Owners may use approved accessories without requiring separate authorization from Armory personnel, provided the accessory remains attached to the corresponding authorized Limited Merchandise item.

Transfer between platforms is not automatically authorized.
