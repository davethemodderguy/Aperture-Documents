## **OVERVIEW**

Following the successful treatment of Norma Lockwood's Ether Regression Syndrome, routine post-treatment examinations revealed an unforeseen development.

Residual stabilized Ether did not dissipate completely.

Instead, it integrated into Norma's nervous system, resulting in the manifestation of a previously undocumented anomalous ability designated:

> **NULLZONE**

Unlike conventional Ether manipulation, NULLZONE is a localized cognitive field centered on Norma herself.

When consciously activated, the field establishes a direct anomalous interaction with the nervous systems of individuals within its effective range. Activation is accompanied by a distinct change in the color of Norma's irises, each corresponding to a different effect.

Medical scans indicate the ability places measurable strain on Norma's nervous system with prolonged use. As a result, she is trained to use it only when operationally necessary.

---

# **ABILITY MODES**

## 🔴 RED – "HEED"

**Classification:** Cognitive Influence

Norma compels affected individuals to focus on her voice and follow her immediate direction.

Targets remain aware of their surroundings but exhibit an overwhelming inclination to obey instructions while the effect persists.

Primarily authorized for:

- Hostage rescue.
    
- Crowd control.
    
- De-escalation of dangerous situations.
    

---

## 🟢 GREEN – "CEASE"

**Classification:** Neural Suppression

The affected individual becomes completely immobilized while maintaining consciousness.

Motor signals are temporarily interrupted, preventing voluntary movement until the effect ends.

No lasting neurological damage has been observed during controlled testing.

Authorized primarily for:

- Arrests.
    
- Preventing imminent violence.
    
- Non-lethal containment.
    

---

## 🔵 BLUE – "HALT"

**Classification:** Etheric Suppression

The field rapidly weakens the target's physical capabilities by disrupting Ether-assisted biological activity.

This results in severe fatigue and collapse in affected subjects.

Due to its debilitating nature, HALT requires Executive-level authorization except in life-threatening emergencies.

---

## ⚪ WHITE – "SLIGHT"

**Classification:** Extreme Cognitive Disruption

This mode causes catastrophic disruption of sensory processing and higher neurological function.

Because of the high probability of permanent injury, **SLIGHT is prohibited for routine operational use**.

Authorization requires explicit approval from the highest executive command except where no other option exists to prevent catastrophic loss of life.

---

## 🟠 ORANGE – "RAGE"

**Classification:** Perceptual Distortion

The target's ability to correctly distinguish allies from enemies becomes severely distorted.

Because of the substantial risk to bystanders, use of RAGE is restricted to exceptional tactical circumstances.

---

## 🔵 LIGHT BLUE – "RESENT"

**Classification:** Psychological Anomaly

RESENT induces an anomalous psychological state that traps the affected individual within recurring traumatic memories and overwhelming grief.

Current research has **not identified a reliable method to reverse the effect once fully established**.

Due to its irreversible nature and profound ethical implications, **RESENT is permanently prohibited** under KCA and Aperture operational doctrine.

Any activation would require investigation by Executive Command.

---

# **OPERATIONAL RESTRICTIONS**

NULLZONE is considered one of the most tightly regulated anomalous abilities within the joint KCA–Aperture framework.

The following restrictions apply:

- Use only when conventional methods are insufficient.
    
- Medical monitoring after extended activation.
    
- Automatic incident review following every operational deployment.
    
- Permanent prohibition of RESENT outside research containment.
    
- SLIGHT restricted to extraordinary circumstances involving immediate preservation of life.
