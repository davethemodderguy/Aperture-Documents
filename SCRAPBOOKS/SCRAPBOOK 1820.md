# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake Akana has continued to appear within SAFEHOUSE following his confirmed death.

The initial encounter with Aries Akana was not an isolated event.

Subsequent observations have established a recurring apparition exhibiting Jake's recognizable appearance, personal characteristics, injuries, and possession of a teddy bear.

The apparition most frequently manifests in two locations:

- The SAFEHOUSE cafeteria, usually late at night.
    
- The exact location where Jake sustained the fatal attack.
    

He has also been observed by Bao, Frost, Neri, Nora, Clover, and Phoenix.

These encounters have provided sufficient consistency for C.O.R.E. to classify the phenomenon as **Jake Akana's postmortem manifestation**.

This classification identifies the apparition as Jake's continuing anomalous presence. It does not establish the precise mechanism by which his consciousness persists after death.

The apparition retains the scars accumulated during Jake's life. His body also displays the injuries associated with his death, including the ten stab wounds, additional gunshot wounds, and other visible damage documented during subsequent observations.

His head remains partially melted around the upper facial region, with a dim blue flame burning from a wick at the top.

His legs remain absent.

He floats rather than walks.

He continues to hold a teddy bear.

His voice is permanently disfigured. Speech appears to cause substantial pain, particularly when he attempts to speak above a whisper. Consequently, he rarely talks.

When he does speak, his voice is faint, strained, and difficult to hear.

His emotional presentation is consistently depressed.

He does not appear comforted by the fact that he continues to exist.

He does not appear relieved to see familiar people.

He does not behave as though returning to SAFEHOUSE has restored anything that was lost.

He is dead.

Yet he remains.

And the available observations suggest that his continued existence is not something he finds comforting.

**Jake died, but he has not been permitted to disappear completely.**

# 1. MANIFESTATION PROFILE

## 1.1 — General Appearance

The apparition consistently resembles Jake Akana.

He retains his small stature, white hair, and recognizable facial features where those features remain intact.

His lower body terminates without visible legs.

He floats above the floor without apparent effort.

A wick protrudes from the top of his head, supporting a dim blue flame.

The flame emits no detectable heat during observed encounters.

The upper portion of his head, extending down toward the eyes, appears melted. The damage remains stable despite the continued presence of the flame.

The apparition does not exhibit signs of ordinary respiration.

No reliable pulse has been detected.

His movements are generally slow and quiet.

He does not appear to require doors to be opened for him. However, the exact mechanism by which he enters or leaves observed locations remains unknown.

He has demanifested in the presence of witnesses without warning.

He has also appeared in locations where no personnel reported seeing him arrive.

No physical remains are left behind after demanifestation.

## 1.2 — The Teddy Bear

Jake continues to hold a teddy bear during most recorded appearances.

The object is visually consistent with the bear associated with him during his lifetime.

Before Jake's death, the teddy bear contained his actual heartbeat and functioned as his cardiac vessel.

The postmortem apparition's possession of the bear is consistent with continuity of identity.

However, the current relationship between the apparition and the original object's physical condition remains under investigation.

Personnel must not attempt to take the bear away from him.

The object appears to remain one of his strongest points of continuity with his former life.

Whether he holds it out of habit, attachment, fear, or some combination of these factors is unknown.

He has not provided an explanation.

Given the pain associated with his speech, C.O.R.E. will not require one.

---

# 2. WOUNDS AND SCARRING

The apparition's body bears extensive visible damage.

The injuries are not limited to the wounds responsible for his death.

## 2.1 — Preexisting Scars

Numerous scars remain visible across his body.

These correspond to the accumulated physical injuries documented during Jake's lifetime.

Their continued appearance suggests that his postmortem form preserves at least some characteristics of his previous physical condition.

The scars do not appear to be undergoing ordinary healing.

No tissue regeneration has been observed.

## 2.2 — Fatal Stab Wounds

The ten stab wounds associated with Jake's final attack remain visible.

The documented distribution is:

|Location|Wounds|
|---|---|
|Neck|2|
|Heart region|3|
|Thigh|5|
|**Total**|**10**|

The wounds remain present despite the absence of a conventional living body.

They are not accompanied by the expected physiological processes of an ordinary injury.

No confirmed bleeding has been observed during the apparition's recent appearances.

The wounds nevertheless remain visually apparent.

## 2.3 — Gunshot Wounds

Additional gunshot wounds are also visible on the apparition.

Their number, precise locations, and relationship to earlier incidents have not been fully established in the available postmortem observations.

C.O.R.E. will not assign them to a specific attacker or incident without supporting evidence.

Their presence is nevertheless significant.

Jake's postmortem appearance does not preserve only the injuries that killed him.

It also preserves other forms of violence inflicted upon him.

## 2.4 — Interpretation

The apparition appears to retain a record of physical suffering.

This does not establish that he is actively experiencing every injury as pain.

It does, however, raise a serious question concerning the nature of his continued existence.

If Jake's consciousness persists, why does his body continue to display the injuries that destroyed it?

If his appearance is an anomalous reconstruction, why does it reproduce damage from multiple incidents?

If the wounds are symbolic, what determines which injuries remain visible?

No answers have been established.

Personnel are instructed not to touch, probe, or otherwise test the wounds without explicit authorization and a clear research justification.

Jake is not to be treated as an experimental specimen simply because he can no longer sustain ordinary physical injury.

# 3. VOCAL DAMAGE

**Classification:** Persistent anomalous impairment

Jake's voice is permanently disfigured.

The damage is not merely a reduction in volume.

His voice is strained, rough, and faint, with a whisper-like quality that remains consistent across observed encounters.

He appears capable of producing speech, but doing so causes substantial discomfort.

Attempting to speak loudly produces considerably more pain.

During one observation, a resident called to him from the opposite side of the cafeteria.

Jake turned toward the voice.

He attempted to answer.

The sound was too faint to be understood.

He tried again, raising his voice slightly.

He immediately stopped and pressed a hand against his throat.

The resident approached, concerned.

Jake shook his head.

He did not attempt to speak again.

The interaction ended without further questioning.

### Medical and Welfare Interpretation

Jake's inability to speak comfortably must be treated as a persistent impairment.

Personnel must not ask him to repeat statements merely because they could not hear him.

Do not tell him to speak louder.

Do not demand that he explain himself verbally.

Do not interpret his silence as hostility, consent, or indifference.

Where communication is necessary, personnel may offer writing materials, simple questions answerable by gesture, or other nonverbal options.

Whether these methods remain accessible to him in his postmortem state must be established through observation rather than assumption.

His ability to speak does not imply that speaking is painless.

**Jake can talk. Talking hurts.**

That distinction must remain central to all welfare procedures.

# 4. OBSERVED MANIFESTATION LOCATIONS

## 4.1 — SAFEHOUSE Cafeteria

The cafeteria has become one of Jake's most frequently observed locations.

He usually appears late at night.

He may be found sitting at a table, floating just above a chair, or remaining motionless near an area he previously occupied during his lifetime.

He does not consistently interact with the environment.

He does not appear to eat.

He does not seek coffee, cake, or other food.

He does not initiate conversation.

He simply remains there.

Sometimes he looks toward the entrance.

Sometimes he looks down at the teddy bear.

Sometimes he stares at an empty chair.

He may remain for several minutes before demanifesting.

On other occasions, he remains until another resident notices him.

His presence has not been associated with any confirmed hostile action.

However, the cafeteria's repeated association with Jake's final period of life has made these encounters emotionally difficult for the residents who knew him.

The location may represent familiarity.

It may represent a residual pattern.

It may represent something Jake himself continues to seek.

C.O.R.E. cannot establish which interpretation is correct.

## 4.2 — Fatal Incident Location

Jake has also appeared at the exact location where he was stabbed to death.

His behavior there is generally even quieter.

He may float above the floor without moving.

He may look toward the place where his body was discovered.

He has occasionally been observed holding his teddy bear tightly against himself.

He does not appear to reenact the attack.

No confirmed hostile entity has appeared alongside him.

No direct explanation for these manifestations has been obtained.

The location remains associated with a fatal homicide investigation.

Personnel must not crowd the apparition or repeatedly ask him what happened there.

If he is experiencing distress, forced questioning may only compound it.

The presence of the apparition does not authorize personnel to assume that he is reliving the attack.

It establishes only that he continues to manifest at the location where he died.

# 6. INDIVIDUAL OBSERVATIONS

## 6.1 — BAO

Bao's encounter was recorded in the cafeteria.

She entered late at night and noticed Jake sitting alone.

She stopped immediately.

For several seconds, she did not approach.

Jake was holding his teddy bear.

His head was tilted downward.

The blue flame above him illuminated the tabletop.

Bao eventually moved closer.

**BAO:**

"Jake?"

He looked up.

Bao's expression changed.

She had recognized him.

She also understood that the figure before her was not alive in the ordinary sense.

She sat in the chair opposite him.

She did not attempt to touch him.

**BAO:**

"Mom's here."

Jake looked at her.

He did not answer.

Bao waited.

After a while, she asked:

**BAO:**

"Do you want me to stay?"

Jake's fingers tightened around the teddy bear.

He gave a small nod.

Bao remained with him.

No further conversation occurred.

Jake did not visibly brighten.

He did not smile.

He did not appear relieved.

But he did not immediately demanifest either.

Bao remained until he disappeared.

### Welfare Note

Bao's maternal relationship with Jake makes these encounters particularly distressing.

She should be permitted to remain with him when he appears, provided that doing so does not interfere with necessary duties or create a safety concern.

However, personnel must not encourage her to interpret his continued presence as proof that he can eventually be restored to life.

No such restoration has been established.

---

## 6.2 — FROST

Frost encountered Jake near the cafeteria entrance.

She recognized him immediately.

Her first response was to approach.

She stopped several steps away when she noticed the damage to his face.

Jake looked at her.

Frost appeared to struggle with what to say.

Eventually, she asked:

**FROST:**

"Can I sit with you?"

Jake gave a small nod.

Frost sat beside him.

She did not ask about his wounds.

She did not ask whether he was cold.

She did not ask whether he could feel pain.

For several minutes, they remained together in silence.

At one point, Frost looked toward the teddy bear.

Then back at Jake.

**FROST:**

"You don't have to talk."

Jake lowered his head slightly.

Frost remained beside him.

He demanifested several minutes later.

Frost did not attempt to follow.

### Welfare Note

Frost has expressed significant distress regarding Jake's death.

Personnel should not interpret her willingness to sit with the apparition as evidence that she has accepted the loss.

Her behavior demonstrates only that she has found a way to remain present during an encounter she finds emotionally difficult.

---

## 6.3 — NERI

Neri encountered Jake at the location of the fatal attack.

She stopped upon seeing him.

Her gaze moved from his face to the visible wounds.

Then to the teddy bear.

She did not approach immediately.

After several seconds, she moved closer and crouched at a respectful distance.

**NERI:**

"Jake."

He looked at her.

Neri waited.

Jake's mouth moved slightly.

The resulting sound was barely audible.

Neri leaned forward only enough to hear him, without touching him or demanding repetition.

The statement could not be reliably transcribed.

Jake appeared to experience discomfort while speaking.

Neri immediately stopped asking questions.

She sat on the floor nearby.

Neither spoke again.

After several minutes, Jake demanifested.

Neri remained at the location for some time afterward.

### Welfare Note

Neri's response is consistent with her established preference for quiet companionship.

She did not attempt to force conversation.

This was appropriate.

Jake's inability to communicate comfortably must not be treated as a problem that other residents are required to solve through repeated questioning.

Sometimes the most appropriate response is to remain nearby without asking anything.

---

## 6.4 — NORA

Nora was informed that Jake's apparition had been encountered repeatedly.

She subsequently observed him in the cafeteria.

She recognized the teddy bear and the general shape of his face.

The encounter was particularly distressing because her blood had been involved in the circumstances surrounding Jake's physical manifestation.

Nora approached slowly.

Jake looked toward her.

Neither spoke.

Nora appeared to want to say something but stopped before doing so.

She eventually sat at the opposite end of the table.

Jake remained motionless.

After a short period, he disappeared.

No physical interaction occurred.

### Welfare Note

Nora must not be held responsible for Jake's death merely because her blood was involved in his manifestation.

The available evidence establishes that Jake died from injuries inflicted by another party.

It does not establish that Nora's blood caused his death or that she is responsible for his current postmortem condition.

Personnel must not use the apparition to pressure Nora into seeking forgiveness, confessing responsibility, or providing explanations for circumstances beyond her established knowledge.

---

## 6.5 — CLOVER

Clover's encounter occurred late at night.

She found Jake floating near the cafeteria tables.

She stopped several meters away.

For several seconds, she appeared unable to move.

Then she approached.

**CLOVER:**

"Jake?"

He turned toward her.

Clover covered her mouth.

She did not immediately speak again.

Jake watched her.

His expression remained difficult to interpret.

After a prolonged pause, Clover asked:

**CLOVER:**

"Can I stay?"

Jake did not answer verbally.

He did not nod.

He did not shake his head.

Clover sat down anyway, leaving sufficient space between them.

**CLOVER:**

"You don't have to do anything."

She remained with him.

Jake eventually lowered his head and held the teddy bear closer.

He did not demanifest immediately.

Clover stayed until he disappeared.

### Welfare Note

Clover has been significantly affected by Jake's death and the circumstances in which he was discovered.

Her encounters with the apparition may intensify her grief.

She must be offered support without being required to stop seeing him or to continue seeing him for the sake of others.

Both choices must remain hers.

## 6.6 — PHOENIX

Phoenix encountered Jake during a late-night welfare round.

He initially assumed that the figure was a resident standing in a poorly illuminated section of the cafeteria.

The absence of legs became apparent when Jake moved.

Phoenix stopped.

He identified the apparition and approached cautiously.

He did not attempt to conduct a medical examination.

Instead, he asked whether Jake wanted him to remain.

Jake looked at him for several seconds.

Then he gave a slight nod.

Phoenix sat nearby.

He did not ask about the wounds.

He did not attempt to inspect the melted region of the head.

He did not ask Jake to demonstrate his anomalous properties.

After a while, Phoenix stated:

**PHOENIX:**

"You don't need to explain anything."

Jake remained silent.

Phoenix continued sitting with him.

The apparition disappeared without warning.

Phoenix did not pursue it.

### Welfare Note

Phoenix's medical background does not authorize him to treat the apparition as a conventional patient.

His presence may provide familiarity and reassurance, but the underlying condition is not understood.

Medical intervention must not be attempted merely because the apparition displays injuries.

The wounds may be physical, anomalous, or representative of his postmortem state.

No treatment has been shown to alter them.

# 7. ARIES AKANA — SUBSEQUENT CONTACT

Aries has continued to experience the most severe emotional response to Jake's manifestations.

His first encounter included the accusation:

> "It took you so long to find me, yet so short to let me die in the hands of someone wearing your face."

That statement remains unverified as a direct expression of Jake's consciousness.

However, the subsequent confirmation that the apparition is Jake's postmortem manifestation has intensified Aries's distress.

Aries has repeatedly expressed fear that Jake blames him for the circumstances of his death.

He has not established that the apparition's statement represents Jake's complete or enduring view of him.

Nevertheless, he appears to regard it as a judgment he cannot escape.

During a later encounter, Aries found Jake sitting in the cafeteria.

He stopped immediately.

Jake looked up.

Aries took one step forward.

Then another.

He did not attempt to touch him.

**ARIES:**

"Jake?"

Jake's expression did not change.

Aries's voice became unsteady.

**ARIES:**

"I'm here."

Jake looked down at the teddy bear.

Aries waited.

**ARIES:**

"I'm sorry."

Jake remained silent.

Aries swallowed.

**ARIES:**

"I should've taken you with me."

Jake's fingers tightened around the bear.

Aries continued, his voice breaking.

**ARIES:**

"I should've stayed."

He stopped himself from speaking further.

For several seconds, he appeared to struggle with the impulse to continue apologizing.

Then he lowered himself into the chair opposite Jake.

**ARIES:**

"You don't have to answer."

Jake did not answer.

Aries remained seated.

Neither spoke again.

After several minutes, Jake demanifested.

Aries did not follow.

He remained in the cafeteria until another resident found him.

### Welfare Assessment

The encounter did not resolve Aries's guilt.

It may have intensified it.

Aries's repeated apologies indicate that he is attempting to obtain a response that would clarify whether Jake blames him.

Jake's silence provides no such clarification.

Personnel must not interpret that silence as forgiveness.

They must not interpret it as condemnation.

They must not tell Aries that Jake has forgiven him merely to relieve his distress.

The absence of an answer is not an answer.

Aries must be supported in tolerating that uncertainty without allowing it to become proof that he deserves punishment.

---

# 8. THE PERSISTENT SADNESS

Across the recorded encounters, Jake's emotional presentation has remained consistently subdued.

He rarely speaks.

He does not initiate conversation.

He does not visibly express happiness at seeing familiar residents.

He does not appear comforted by the fact that several people continue to seek him out.

His posture is frequently lowered.

He often looks toward the floor or the teddy bear he carries.

When residents speak to him gently, he may remain present, but he rarely responds in a way that can be confidently interpreted as reassurance.

No reliable evidence of amusement, enthusiasm, or ordinary social engagement has been recorded.

These observations are consistent with persistent sadness.

They do not establish whether the apparition experiences emotion in precisely the same way Jake did while alive.

However, the consistency of his presentation is sufficient to warrant concern.

His continued existence has not restored his ability to live.

He does not eat with the residents.

He does not sleep in his room.

He does not read in the cafeteria.

He does not climb onto Aries's back when he becomes tired.

He does not ask Bao to stay.

He may remain in the places where he once lived, but he no longer participates in the life that gave those places meaning.

The distinction is profound.

**Jake has returned to SAFEHOUSE as an apparition. He has not returned to his life.**

# 9. WELFARE GUIDANCE FOR FUTURE ENCOUNTERS

Personnel are instructed to respond to Jake's manifestations with calm, respect, and restraint.

Do not:

- demand that he speak;
    
- ask him to speak louder;
    
- repeatedly question him about his death;
    
- touch his wounds without authorization;
    
- attempt to remove his teddy bear;
    
- crowd him;
    
- follow him when he demanifests;
    
- insist that he must be happy to see familiar residents;
    
- interpret his silence as hostility;
    
- or treat his continued presence as evidence that his death has been reversed.
    

Appropriate responses include:

> "You can stay here."

> "You don't have to talk."

> "I'll sit with you if you want."

> "You don't have to answer."

> "I'm not going to touch you."

> "You can leave whenever you want."

These statements should not be used as a script to force an interaction.

If Jake does not respond, personnel should allow the silence to remain.

If he demanifests, they should not attempt to prevent it.

If he appears distressed, a familiar resident may remain nearby, provided that Jake does not indicate otherwise.

The objective is not to make him more communicative.

It is to ensure that his continued presence does not become another situation in which he is expected to perform emotional labor for the people around him.

---

# 10. THE QUESTION OF SUFFERING

C.O.R.E. cannot currently determine whether Jake experiences physical pain from the injuries visible on his apparition.

His vocal impairment provides evidence that attempting to speak causes discomfort.

It does not establish the precise mechanism of that pain.

His consistently depressed presentation raises a separate question.

Does Jake remain emotionally distressed because he remembers his death?

Because he remembers the violence that preceded it?

Because he is aware that the people he loved continue living without him?

Because he cannot return to the life he had?

Or because his postmortem condition itself prevents him from experiencing anything resembling relief?

No confirmed answer is available.

The investigation must avoid assuming that a ghost cannot suffer simply because it is no longer alive.

It must also avoid assuming that every visible wound corresponds to a sensation of pain.

The evidence supports a narrower but still serious conclusion:

Jake remains in a persistent anomalous state characterized by visible injuries, impaired speech, minimal interaction, and consistent sadness.

That condition warrants observation and care.

It does not authorize experimentation without limits.

---

# 11. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Jake Akana's postmortem manifestation is now considered confirmed.

His identity is supported by consistent appearance, familiar possessions, recurring locations, and repeated encounters with residents who knew him during his lifetime.

The mechanism of his continued existence remains unknown.

The apparition retains extensive scarring, fatal stab wounds, gunshot wounds, cranial damage, and a permanently disfigured voice.

His speech is severely limited by pain.

His behavior is consistently withdrawn and depressed.

He frequently appears in the cafeteria or at the location of his death.

His presence has caused significant distress among the residents who loved him, particularly Aries, Bao, Clover, and the other members of his immediate circle.

The following conclusions are authorized:

1. Jake's postmortem manifestation is real and recurring.
    
2. His current condition is not a restoration of his former life.
    
3. His visible injuries remain present.
    
4. Speaking appears to cause him substantial discomfort.
    
5. His emotional presentation is consistently sad and withdrawn.
    
6. He does not consistently respond to attempts at interaction.
    
7. The reason for his continued manifestation remains unresolved.
    
8. No confirmed method of restoring his life has been identified.
    

Personnel must not confuse continued existence with recovery.

Jake is still present.

That does not mean he is well.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The first time Aries saw the ghost, he asked whether it was Jake.

The ghost turned toward him and accused him of taking too long to find his brother, only to lose him to someone wearing his face.

Then it disappeared.

Afterward, Jake continued to appear.

In the cafeteria.

At the place where he died.

Sometimes Bao found him.

Sometimes Frost.

Sometimes Neri, Nora, Clover, or Phoenix.

Sometimes Aries.

They would approach carefully.

They would ask whether he wanted company.

They would sit beside him.

And Jake would remain quiet.

His head was still melted.

His legs were still gone.

His body still carried the scars of everything that had happened to him.

The gunshot wounds remained.

The stab wounds remained.

The teddy bear remained in his arms.

Even his voice had not been spared.

If he tried to speak, he could barely produce a whisper.

If he tried to speak louder, it hurt.

So he stopped speaking.

He had spent his life enduring injuries that should never have been inflicted upon him.

He had spent his final days trying to find safety.

He had died from blood loss.

And now he could not even disappear completely.

He could not return to his room and sleep.

He could not sit at the cafeteria table and eat.

He could not ask Bao to stay until he felt better.

He could not lean against Aries and fall asleep while his brother carried him.

He could only appear in the places that remained familiar to him, carrying the same teddy bear, wearing the same wounds, and looking at the people who had survived him.

They could sit beside him.

They could speak gently.

They could tell him that he did not have to answer.

But they could not give him back the life he had lost.

And perhaps that is why he remains so quiet.

Not because he has nothing left to say.

Not because he has forgiven everyone.

Not because he has stopped caring.

But because the things he might want to say cannot be separated from the things that happened to him.

His voice hurts.

His body remembers.

And whatever remains of Jake Akana appears unable to find much comfort in being here.

The residents of SAFEHOUSE have begun to understand something that the initial investigation did not account for.

They did not get Jake back.

They got the part of him that remained after death.

And that part is still hurting.

**Jake died.**

**Jake returned.**

**Jake is still sad.**

And no one yet knows how to help him.
