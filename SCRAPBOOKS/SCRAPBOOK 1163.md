### RESIDENT WELFARE & MEDICAL DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-NERI-287

**STATUS:** ACTIVE OBSERVATION  
**SUBJECT:** Neri — Plush Form  
**PARTICIPANTS:** Elsher / Jake

---

# 1. SUMMARY

During the temporary suspension of the Hell Fire Lottery, Neri spent an extended period in her plush form.

Two MSAC personnel were particularly involved in keeping her company:

- **Elsher** — existing owner of NS-006, SOH-002, and HF-001.
    
- **Jake** — 15-year-old MSAC operator and owner of NS-009, SOH-004, and HF-003.
    

Unlike previous interactions involving Neri's medical condition, neither attempted to make her productive.

They simply:

> **Had fun with her.**

Neri appeared to enjoy herself.

---

# 2. INITIAL ENCOUNTER

Elsher found Neri sitting on the floor.

Jake was sitting nearby.

Neri was staring at both of them.

Elsher looked down.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “What?”

Neri pointed at Jake.

Then at Elsher.

Then at herself.

Jake tilted his head.

> **JAKE:**  
> “She wants something.”

Elsher crouched down.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “What do you want?”

Neri lifted both arms.

Elsher paused.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “...Carry?”

Neri nodded.

Jake immediately laughed.

> **JAKE:**  
> “She didn't even hesitate.”

Elsher picked her up.

Neri immediately settled against his shoulder.

---

# 3. THE HAND-OFF

After several minutes:

> **JAKE:**  
> “Can I hold her?”

Neri looked at Jake.

Then at Elsher.

Then back at Jake.

She reached toward him.

Elsher handed her over.

Jake carefully held Neri against his chest.

Neri adjusted herself until comfortable.

Her oversized hoodie sleeves covered most of her hands.

Her seal tail hung over Jake's arm.

> **JAKE:**  
> “She's really light.”

Elsher nodded.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “She's a plushie.”

Neri looked up.

> **NERI:**  
> “Person.”

Both stopped.

Jake immediately corrected himself.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Person.”

Neri nodded.

Then rested her head against him.

---

# 4. HEADPATS

Jake cautiously placed a hand on Neri's head.

Neri looked at him.

He stopped.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Is this okay?”

Neri nodded.

Jake gently patted her.

Neri's eyes slowly closed.

Elsher watched.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “She likes that.”

Jake smiled.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Yeah.”

Neri quietly murmured:

> **NERI:**  
> “...Warm.”

Jake continued gently.

---

# 5. ELSHER'S TURN

Eventually Neri reached toward Elsher.

Elsher immediately understood.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “My turn?”

Neri nodded.

Jake handed her over.

Elsher placed Neri on his shoulder.

She immediately wrapped her little arms around him.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “Comfortable?”

Neri nodded.

Elsher began walking.

Neri remained attached.

---

# 6. THE "TACHYON SET" JOKE

Elsher's Hell Fire was nearby.

His Night Stalker and Sleight of Hand were also secured according to MSAC equipment policy.

Jake looked at the equipment.

Then at Elsher.

Then at himself.

He was carrying his own three-item collection.

> **JAKE:**  
> “We both have the whole set now.”

Elsher looked at him.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “Technically.”

Jake looked at Neri.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Did you plan this?”

Neri shook her head.

> **NERI:**  
> “No.”

Pause.

> **NERI:**  
> “Lucky.”

Elsher laughed.

---

# 7. NERI'S OPINION

Jake asked:

> **JAKE:**  
> “Which one do you like better?”

Neri looked at him.

> **NERI:**  
> “What?”

Jake pointed toward the three pieces of Limited Merchandise.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Which one?”

Neri thought.

Then pointed at herself.

Jake blinked.

Elsher laughed.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “She picked herself.”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Me.”

---

# 8. THE FLOOR INCIDENT

Later, Neri decided she wanted to walk.

She climbed down from Elsher.

She stood upright.

Took one step.

Two.

Three.

Jake watched carefully.

> **JAKE:**  
> “You good?”

Neri nodded.

Four steps.

Five.

She stopped.

Her little body wobbled.

Elsher immediately reached out.

Too late.

Neri tipped sideways.

### **SQUEAK.**

Jake caught her.

There was a moment of silence.

Neri blinked.

Jake held her carefully.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Got you.”

Neri looked at him.

> **NERI:**  
> “...Thanks.”

Elsher sighed.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “Okay. No more walking.”

Neri did not object.

---

# 9. BACKPACK MODE

Jake eventually offered his back.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Want a ride?”

Neri nodded.

She climbed onto his back.

Her tiny arms wrapped around his shoulders.

Her seal tail rested against his side.

Jake stood.

Neri remained securely attached.

Elsher watched them.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “She's using you as transportation.”

Jake shrugged.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I'm okay with it.”

Neri quietly said:

> **NERI:**  
> “Good.”

---

# 10. THE THREE OF THEM

The remainder of the afternoon was largely uneventful.

Which, for Neri, was probably ideal.

She:

- rode on Jake's back;
    
- sat on Elsher's shoulder;
    
- received gentle headpats;
    
- drank some coffee;
    
- watched the two MSAC operators talk;
    
- occasionally contributed one or two words;
    
- and slept several times.
    

At one point Jake and Elsher were discussing the Hell Fire Lottery.

Neri was lying between them.

Jake said:

> **JAKE:**  
> “Think they'll resume tomorrow?”

Elsher shrugged.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “Depends on Neri.”

Neri, eyes closed, quietly said:

> **NERI:**  
> “No.”

Both looked down.

> **JAKE:**  
> “No?”

Neri pulled her hoodie sleeve over her face.

> **NERI:**  
> “Tired.”

Elsher laughed.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “Fair enough.”

---

# 11. NERI'S FINAL STATE

By the end of the observation period, Neri had fallen asleep while sitting between Elsher and Jake.

Jake was sitting against the couch.

Elsher was sitting beside him.

Neri was resting against Jake's side, with one hand loosely gripping Elsher's sleeve.

Neither moved.

Eventually Jake whispered:

> **JAKE:**  
> “She's holding both of us.”

Elsher looked down.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “Apparently.”

Neri shifted slightly.

Her glasses slipped down her face.

Elsher carefully fixed them.

Neri did not wake.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

No medical intervention was required.

Neri remained tired but stable.

Her interaction with Elsher and Jake was voluntary.

She approached both of them.

She accepted being carried.

She accepted headpats.

She climbed onto Jake's back voluntarily.

She remained near Elsher.

She communicated when she wanted something.

Most importantly:

> **She had fun.**

No productivity requirement was imposed.

No research was discussed.

No lottery administration was demanded.

No one asked her to perform.

For several hours, Neri was simply a tired little plush person spending time with two friends.
