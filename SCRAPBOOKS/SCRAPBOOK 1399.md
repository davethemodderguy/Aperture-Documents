# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The group previously referred to administratively as the:

> **"Silverline Sisters"**

has formally rejected the designation.

The six sisters reportedly consider the name excessively associated with the organization that created them.

They requested an alternative group identity.

The replacement was proposed by **Jake**.

His suggestion was:

> **OBOL SQUAD**

The name was immediately accepted.

The six sisters now formally recognize themselves as:

> **OBOL SQUAD**

The formation of the group was followed by another significant development.

Solider 11 and Anby introduced the other members to the existence of:

> **Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise**

after learning about it through MSAC Operators.

The two sisters were already particularly interested in MSAC Operators.

The discovery of an entire merchandise operation associated with Tachyon Industries therefore produced immediate interest.

This subsequently resulted in all six members of Obol Squad meeting:

> **Nora Tachyon**

and

> **Neri Tachyon**

for the explicit purpose of obtaining merchandise.

> **ANBY:**

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Exactly."

Iris crossed her arms.

> **IRIS:**  
> "So why are we still being called that?"

C.O.R.E. subsequently ceased using "Silverline Sisters" as the group's preferred name.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The formation of Obol Squad appears to have had a positive effect on the sisters' collective identity.

They have deliberately rejected the name associated with the organization that created them.

They chose their own.

They have also begun developing connections outside their family and workplace.

Their interest in MSAC Operators is particularly strong.

Solider 11 and Anby appear to be the primary enthusiasts.

The discovery of Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise has now resulted in all six sisters becoming regular customers.

Nora and Neri have therefore acquired:

> **Six new customers.**

And, apparently, six people who are very willing to ask:

> **"Do you have merch for this?"**
