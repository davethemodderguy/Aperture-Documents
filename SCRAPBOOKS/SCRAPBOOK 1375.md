# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### MEDICAL & RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-INC-4C-PHOENIX-239  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — RESIDENT WELFARE  
**STATUS:** CLOSED / NO FATALITY  
**SUBJECT:** Phoenix Raffedella  
**INCIDENT:** Stairwell fall / emergency rift interception

---

# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

Phoenix fell down the SAFEHOUSE stairs again.

This would ordinarily have resulted in another fatal accident followed by Transcendense restoration.

It did not.

Jake was nearby.

He reacted before Phoenix reached the floor.

Jake opened an **azure spatial rift** directly beneath her falling trajectory.

Phoenix disappeared through the rift.

A second rift opened beside Jake.

Phoenix emerged from it immediately.

Jake caught her.

The resulting posture looked remarkably similar to the dramatic rescue poses commonly observed in romantic films.

This resemblance was entirely accidental.

There was nothing romantic about the incident.

Phoenix was simply falling.

Jake was simply trying to prevent her from dying.

---

# 2. INCIDENT RECONSTRUCTION

Phoenix was carrying several medical supplies down the stairwell.

She was tired.

She missed a step.

Her balance failed.

Phoenix fell.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Oh—”

Jake, who was approximately one landing below, looked upward.

His expression changed immediately.

An azure rift opened.

Phoenix passed through it.

A second rift opened beside Jake.

Phoenix emerged from the rift directly into his arms.

Jake caught her securely.

There was approximately one second of complete silence.

Phoenix stared at him.

Jake stared at Phoenix.

---

# 3. THE POSE

Phoenix was being held completely off the ground.

One arm supported her back.

The other supported her legs.

Her medical supplies were still clutched in one hand.

Her hair had fallen slightly across her face.

Jake's wings were partially extended from the sudden movement.

For approximately three seconds, the scene looked absurdly cinematic.

Phoenix's face turned bright red.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “...”

Jake tilted his head.

> **JAKE:**  
> “You fell.”

Phoenix looked away.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “I know.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “Again.”

Phoenix became even more embarrassed.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Please don't say that.”

---

# 4. JAKE'S ASSESSMENT

Jake looked down at the stairwell.

Then back at Phoenix.

> **JAKE:**  
> “You're okay.”

Phoenix nodded.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Yes.”

Jake carefully lowered her onto the landing.

Phoenix immediately checked her medical supplies.

Everything was intact.

She then looked at Jake.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Thank you.”

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> “You're welcome.”

Pause.

> **JAKE:**  
> “You should use the stairs slower.”

Phoenix closed her eyes.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “I know.”

---

# 5. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT

Phoenix sustained:

**No fatal injuries.**

**No major injuries.**

**No Transcendense activation.**

This is notable.

Phoenix's previous stair-related accidents have resulted in fatal injury and subsequent restoration.

Jake's intervention prevented the fall from reaching the floor.

C.O.R.E. therefore records this as:

> **FIRST SUCCESSFUL PRE-IMPACT RIFT RESCUE OF PHOENIX**

Jake did not teleport Phoenix.

He opened a rift and redirected her trajectory.

The distinction remains important.

---

# 6. PHOENIX'S REACTION

Phoenix was subsequently observed avoiding eye contact with several residents.

When asked why, she replied:

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Nothing.”

When asked whether she was embarrassed about falling again:

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “No.”

Pause.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Maybe.”

When asked whether Jake's rescue had embarrassed her:

Phoenix became visibly red again.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “He caught me.”

The observer replied:

> “Yes.”

Phoenix looked away.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Like that.”

The observer understood.

No further questions were asked.

---

# 7. JAKE'S INTERPRETATION

Jake did not appear to understand why Phoenix was embarrassed.

Later, he described the incident to Bao.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Phoenix fell.”

> **BAO:**  
> “Again?”

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I opened a rift.”

> **BAO:**  
> “Good thinking.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “I caught her.”

Bao smiled.

> **BAO:**  
> “You did.”

Jake seemed pleased.

> **JAKE:**  
> “She got red.”

Bao paused.

> **BAO:**  
> “...Did she?”

> **JAKE:**  
> “Yes.”

Jake then wandered away.

---

# 8. C.O.R.E. OBSERVATION

There is no indication that Phoenix's embarrassment was caused by romantic attraction.

The situation was simply extremely awkward.

Phoenix is a twenty-five-year-old medical professional who has become accustomed to:

- falling down stairs;
    
- dying;
    
- waking up;
    
- treating herself;
    
- continuing her work.
    

She is considerably less accustomed to being caught in midair by a six-year-old child using an anomalous spatial rift.

The dramatic posture did not help.

---

# 9. SAFETY RECOMMENDATION

Phoenix is once again reminded:

> **The stairs are not your enemy.**

> **But they are winning.**

Personnel should continue encouraging Phoenix to use handrails, slow down when fatigued, and avoid carrying unnecessary supplies while exhausted.

Phoenix has acknowledged these recommendations.

Whether she follows them remains uncertain.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Phoenix has died from a staircase before.

Today, she almost did it again.

The difference was Jake.

One rift.

One redirected fall.

One extremely confused six-year-old.

And one deeply embarrassed medical assistant being held several feet above the floor in what looked, for approximately three seconds, like the climax of a romantic movie.

It was not.

It was a rescue.

Phoenix still blushed.

Jake still does not understand why.

And, most importantly:

**Phoenix did not die.**

For once, the stairs lost.
