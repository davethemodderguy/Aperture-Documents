# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Twiggy has been located.

She was discovered working as a **nurse at a Banlands hospital**, where she had apparently established a civilian life following her separation from Project: Silverline.

Unlike Solider 11, Twiggy was not working as a contractor or combat operative.

She had instead chosen a medical profession.

Hospital records indicate that she had been consistently assisting patients and medical personnel.

Twiggy has also sustained a permanent physical injury.

She is missing her **right eye** and currently wears a medical bandage over the affected area.

The cause of the injury remains under investigation.

Upon learning that Anby had been recovered and was currently living under KCA protection, Twiggy immediately agreed to surrender herself to KCA custody.

As with Solider 11, **no force was required.**

---

# 1. INITIAL IDENTIFICATION

C.O.R.E. personnel were investigating Project: Silverline survivors when a hospital employee was identified as matching the known artificial-human template.

The subject was wearing standard medical attire.

Her white hair was tied back for clinical work.

A medical bandage covered the right side of her face.

The hospital's records identified her simply as:

> **TWIGGY**

Further investigation confirmed the subject's identity.

---

# 2. THE RIGHT EYE

Twiggy has lost her right eye.

The injury appears permanent.

She currently uses a medical bandage over the affected area.

She has adapted to the loss sufficiently to continue working as a nurse.

Hospital personnel described her as highly capable despite the injury.

One nurse reportedly asked whether Twiggy wanted assistance with certain tasks.

Twiggy answered:

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "I'm fine."

The nurse reportedly responded:

> **"You always say that."**

Twiggy answered:

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "Because I usually am."

C.O.R.E. has not yet determined whether the injury occurred during her time with Silverline or afterward.

---

# 3. HOSPITAL LIFE

Twiggy appears to have established a remarkably ordinary routine.

She worked shifts.

She treated patients.

She assisted doctors.

She changed dressings.

She monitored recovery.

She ate meals with other hospital workers.

She went home afterward.

No indication was found that she was actively searching for Project: Silverline.

She had apparently chosen to remain away from it.

The hospital staff were unaware that Twiggy was an artificial human.

They simply knew her as a nurse.

---

# 4. DISCOVERY

When KCA personnel approached Twiggy, she initially became cautious.

She recognized the organization as unrelated to Silverline but did not immediately cooperate.

The conversation changed when the KCA representative provided the recovered family records.

Twiggy read the list.

She saw:

> **ANBY — CONFIRMED ALIVE**

Twiggy stopped reading.

She looked up.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "...Anby?"

The representative confirmed it.

Twiggy's expression changed immediately.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "She's alive?"

> **KCA:**  
> "Yes."

Twiggy remained silent.

Then:

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "Where is she?"

---

# 5. VOLUNTARY CUSTODY

Twiggy was informed that Anby was currently under KCA protection and residing within the Aperture SAFEHOUSE.

She immediately agreed to accompany KCA.

The representative clarified:

> **KCA:**  
> "You understand that you're not being arrested?"

Twiggy nodded.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "I know."

> **KCA:**  
> "You can remain here if you want."

Twiggy shook her head.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "No."

Pause.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "Take me to Anby."

Her hospital employment was subsequently placed on temporary leave pending the investigation.

The hospital administration was informed only that Twiggy was being transferred for protected-person welfare reasons.
