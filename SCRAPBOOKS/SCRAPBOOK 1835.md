# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. — CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### SAFEHOUSE WELFARE & ANOMALOUS RESIDENTS DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** CORE-SW-JA-029  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESTRICTED — RESIDENT OBSERVATION  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**SUBJECT:** _The Last Things the Snow Couldn't Take_  
**INVOLVED RESIDENTS:** Jake Akana, Camelia Candleheart

---

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Camelia Candleheart recently observed a feature of Jake Akana's ghost form that she had not previously examined closely: his tail.

Jake was a Cat Thiren before his death. In life, he possessed both cat ears and a tail, along with beautiful eyes. His current ghostly humanoid form no longer has visible ears. Half of his head has melted into a candle-like shape, leaving no visible eyes, while a small blue flame burns from the exposed wick.

His tail, however, remains.

Jake has also been observed using a separate ghostly cat form, moving on four legs with his tail intact. This form has neither visible ears nor eyes.

The discovery prompted Camelia to consider the difference between the boy Jake had been before his murder and the ghost she knows now.

The resulting interaction was not a medical examination. It was a conversation between a girl with fragmented memories and her younger brother, whose ability to describe his own past has been severely limited by permanent speech impairment.

The most significant finding was not the persistence of Jake's tail.

It was that Camelia wanted to know what he had looked like before.

---

### I. OBSERVATION

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE residential common area  
**Time:** Afternoon  
**Activity:** Unstructured interaction

Jake was in his ghostly cat form when Camelia noticed his tail moving behind him.

She watched it sway for a while before crouching down beside him.

**CAMELIA:** “Jake?”

_The ghost cat turns toward her. Despite having no visible eyes or ears, he appears to recognize her._

**JAKE:** “Mrr.”

**CAMELIA:** “Your tail.”

_Jake glances back at it—or rather, turns his head toward the part of his body where it extends. His tail curls slightly._

**JAKE:** “Tail.”

**CAMELIA:** “You have a tail.”

**JAKE:** “Mm.”

**CAMELIA:** “Did you always have one?”

_Jake pauses._

**JAKE:** “Cat... boy.”

**CAMELIA:** “You were a cat boy?”

**JAKE:** “Mm. Cat... ears. Tail.”

_Camelia looks at his head. The familiar ears are absent._

**CAMELIA:** “Your ears are gone.”

_Jake lowers his head a little._

**JAKE:** “Gone.”

_Camelia does not immediately ask another question._

**CAMELIA:** “I'm sorry.”

**JAKE:** “Not... you.”

**CAMELIA:** “I know. I just... I'm sorry it happened to you.”

_Jake moves closer and settles beside her._

**Observation:** Jake's short response was sufficient to communicate that Camelia was not responsible. No further questioning was conducted.

---

### II. THE EYES HE USED TO HAVE

Camelia had seen Jake's ghost form many times. She knew his melted head, his wick, his blue flame, and the absence of visible eyes.

Knowing what someone looks like now, however, is not the same as knowing what they looked like before.

**CAMELIA:** “Jake, what about your eyes?”

_Jake remains still._

**JAKE:** “Eyes...”

**CAMELIA:** “Did you have eyes when you were alive?”

**JAKE:** “Yes.”

**CAMELIA:** “What were they like?”

_Jake takes longer to answer._

**JAKE:** “Pretty...”

**CAMELIA:** “Your eyes were pretty?”

**JAKE:** “Mm.”

**CAMELIA:** “I wish I could have seen them.”

_Jake's tail curls around his paws._

**JAKE:** “Had... eyes.”

**CAMELIA:** “Yes. You did.”

**JAKE:** “Now... gone.”

_Camelia's expression changes._

**CAMELIA:** “I know, little brother.”

_She carefully rests her hand beside him, allowing him to decide whether to move closer._

**CAMELIA:** “You don't have to tell me anything else.”

_Jake moves against her hand._

**JAKE:** “Stay.”

**CAMELIA:** “I'll stay.”

_She remains beside him without asking him to describe the circumstances of his death._

---

### III. A DIFFERENT SHAPE

Later, Jake shifted into his ghostly cat form and walked across the room on four legs.

Camelia watched him circle the sofa before settling beside her feet.

**CAMELIA:** “You can become a cat?”

**JAKE:** “Cat... form.”

**CAMELIA:** “Is it more comfortable?”

**JAKE:** “Sometimes.”

**CAMELIA:** “Can you see in that form?”

_Jake looks toward her._

**JAKE:** “Yes.”

**CAMELIA:** “But you don't have eyes.”

**JAKE:** “Still... see.”

_Camelia considers this. She does not attempt to explain the mechanism or ask him to demonstrate it._

**CAMELIA:** “And your ears?”

**JAKE:** “No ears.”

**CAMELIA:** “But you can hear me?”

**JAKE:** “Mm.”

_Camelia smiles faintly._

**CAMELIA:** “You're full of things I don't understand.”

**JAKE:** “You... too.”

**CAMELIA:** “That's fair.”

_Jake's tail brushes against her ankle._

**CAMELIA:** “Oh! You did that on purpose.”

**JAKE:** “Maybe.”

**CAMELIA:** “You're teasing me!”

**JAKE:** “Heh.”

_Camelia laughs. Jake remains beside her, tail swishing slowly._

**Observation:** Jake's ghostly cat form is a distinct manifestation of his current anomalous appearance. His ability to see and hear despite lacking visible eyes and ears is an observed property; the underlying mechanism has not been established in this report.

---

### IV. WHAT CAMELIA WRITES DOWN

That evening, Camelia consulted her notebook.

She had already recorded that Jake was her little brother. She had written down his name, his relationship to her, and reminders to ask for help when she could not remember something important.

She turned to a new page.

**NOTEBOOK ENTRY — CAMELIA CANDLEHEART**

_Jake Akana._

_My little brother._

_He was a Cat Thiren before he died. He had cat ears, a tail, and beautiful eyes._

_His ghost form is different. Half of his head is melted, his ears and eyes are no longer visible, and he has a blue flame on a wick._

_He can also become a ghostly cat with four legs and a tail. He has no visible eyes or ears in that form, but he can still see and hear._

_Don't ask him to explain how he died unless he wants to talk about it._

_Don't assume he is okay just because he doesn't say much._

_Listen to him._

Camelia paused after writing the last line.

Then she added one more sentence.

_I wish I could have met him before._

She stared at the words for a long time.

Her notebook could preserve facts that her memory might lose. It could tell her what Jake had been, what he looked like, and what he could do.

It could not show her the boy he had been before his murder.

It could not restore his eyes.

And it could not give her the memories she never had.

---

### V. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Jake's remaining feline traits should be documented without reducing him to a collection of missing or altered body parts.

His tail persists in both his humanoid ghost form and his four-legged ghostly cat form. His ears are no longer visible, and neither form has visible eyes. Nevertheless, Jake retains the ability to perceive and interact with his surroundings.

The fact that Jake once had beautiful eyes is relevant to his personal history, but it must not become an excuse for invasive examination or repeated questioning.

Camelia's curiosity was appropriate to an ordinary sibling interaction. She asked about his past, accepted his limited answers, and stopped short of demanding further details.

Her fragmented memory means she may need to consult her notebook to recall this conversation later. That does not make the interaction meaningless.

Similarly, Jake's fragmented speech should not obscure the meaning of what he managed to share: he remembers that he had eyes, he identifies himself as a cat boy, and he recognizes that those features are gone.

C.O.R.E. will not assume that his ghostly appearance represents a complete or accurate restoration of his former body. His current manifestations are to be documented as observed.

---

### VI. CLOSING OBSERVATION

Camelia had seen Jake's ghostly tail before.

This time, she thought about the boy who had possessed it while he was alive.

A boy with cat ears.

A boy with beautiful eyes.

A boy who had once been able to look at the world with a face that had not been melted by death.

Now he moved through SAFEHOUSE as a little ghost cat, four paws touching the floor, tail trailing behind him, no visible eyes or ears, and only short bursts of words when he wanted to speak.

Camelia could not remember her own former life. She had chosen the name Camelia Candleheart from an anime because her original name and history were lost to her.

Jake, meanwhile, could remember enough to know that he had once been different, but he could not easily explain what that meant to him.

They could not give each other back what they had lost.

But when Camelia finished writing, Jake curled up beside her chair.

His tail rested across her feet.

She gently placed her hand beside him.

**CAMELIA:** “Goodnight, little brother.”

**JAKE:** “Night... Camelia.”

She looked down at him, making sure to remember the moment even if she could not guarantee that the memory would stay.

For now, Jake was there.

And for now, she knew him.
