### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE LOG**

# **BUNNY'S CARROT PROBLEM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 — Internal Personnel  
**Primary Subject:** Corin Lockwood  
**Secondary Subject:** Bunny  
**Location:** Treehouse Agricultural Plot  
**Status:** **CROPS ESTABLISHED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Bunny likes carrots.

This statement is technically accurate.

It is also an extreme understatement.

Bunny **loves** carrots.

Bunny appears to regard carrots as one of the greatest achievements of existence.

This has created a logistical problem.

---

# **THE CARROT CONSUMPTION RATE**

Bunny's appetite for carrots quickly exceeded the amount normally kept in the Treehouse.

Initial supplies were purchased from standard Blacksite stores.

They did not last.

A second supply was obtained.

It did not last.

A third supply was obtained.

Corin began to notice the pattern.

> **"Bunny eats a lot."**

Bunny continued eating.

---

# **CORIN'S SOLUTION**

Corin decided to grow carrots herself.

There was only one problem.

She had absolutely no idea how farming worked.

She asked Astral.

Astral considered the question.

Then smiled.

> **"I'll teach you."**

Frost joined shortly afterward.

> **"I'll help."**

Corin looked at both of them.

> **"...Okay."**

---

# **AGRICULTURAL TRAINING**

Corin received an accelerated introduction to basic cultivation.

Lessons included:

- Preparing soil.
    
- Planting seeds.
    
- Proper spacing.
    
- Watering.
    
- Weed removal.
    
- Crop maintenance.
    
- Identifying healthy plants.
    
- Harvesting.
    
- Not pulling carrots out of the ground too early.
    

The final lesson required repeated clarification.

Corin kept checking whether they were ready.

Astral repeatedly told her:

> **"Not yet."**

Corin:

> **"...Now?"**

> **"No."**

> **"...Now?"**

> **"Still no."**

---

# **FROST'S CONTRIBUTION**

Frost proved particularly useful for environmental management.

Her cryokinesis allowed her to carefully regulate the surrounding temperature without damaging the crops.

However, she was explicitly instructed not to freeze the soil.

She succeeded.

Mostly.

One small section became extremely cold.

Corin stared at it.

Frost stared at it.

Astral sighed.

> **"We'll fix it."**

---

# **CORIN LEARNS**

Corin initially struggled.

She overwatered several plants.

She planted some seeds too closely.

She accidentally stepped on one row.

She once spent nearly an hour searching for a carrot that she had already harvested.

Bunny watched all of this.

Judging silently.

Corin eventually pointed at it.

> **"You could help."**

Bunny continued chewing a carrot.

> **"...Right."**

---

# **THE FIRST HARVEST**

Several weeks later, Corin harvested her first successful crop.

She pulled the first carrot from the soil.

She stared at it.

Then looked toward Bunny.

> **"Bunny."**

The rabbit immediately approached.

Corin held the carrot out.

Bunny took it.

The rabbit ate it in approximately four seconds.

Corin smiled.

> **"I grew that."**

Bunny continued eating.

Corin looked incredibly proud.

---

# **A NEW ROUTINE**

Farming has now become part of Corin's daily routine.

She checks the crops in the morning.

She waters them.

She removes weeds.

She monitors the soil.

She checks for pests.

She harvests mature vegetables.

Then she brings some of the carrots to Bunny.

Bunny waits for her every morning.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE GARDEN**

The agricultural plot has expanded.

Current crops include:

- Carrots.
    
- Wild flowers.
    
- Grass.
    
- Several vegetables for Corin and the other residents.
    

The carrots remain the primary crop.

C.O.R.E. Agricultural Services has offered assistance.

Corin declined.

> **"I can do it."**

Astral later confirmed:

> **"She's actually getting pretty good."**

Frost added:

> **"She still talks to the plants."**

Corin:

> **"They grow better when I encourage them."**

No scientific evidence supports this.

The plants appear healthy anyway.

---

# **BUNNY'S CONTRIBUTION**

Bunny has become the unofficial supervisor of the garden.

It sits near the carrot patch.

It watches Corin work.

Occasionally it attempts to eat the leaves of immature plants.

Corin immediately intervenes.

> **"NO. Those aren't ready."**

Bunny stops.

It understands.

It then stares at Corin.

Corin stares back.

> **"...You can have one later."**

Bunny appears satisfied.

---

Corin added an entire agricultural section to her research notebook.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin once spent months trying to find a reason to keep moving.

Now she wakes up because Bunny is hungry.

She learned farming from Astral.

She learned environmental control from Frost.

She planted seeds.

She waited.

She failed.

She tried again.

And eventually, she grew something.

Not just carrots.

Something else.

A routine.

A responsibility.

A reason to get out of bed.

Every morning, Corin walks into the garden.

Bunny follows.

Corin checks the crops.

Bunny checks the carrots.

Corin waters the plants.

Bunny waits impatiently.

And when the harvest finally comes...

Corin gives Bunny the first carrot.

Bunny devours it.

Corin smiles.

> **"I'll grow more tomorrow."**

Bunny's ears perk up.

The Feather Lantern above the garden glows softly.
