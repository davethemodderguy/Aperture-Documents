## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Ten weeks after the permanent demanifestation of Tom Akana, and following a documented period of severe psychological deterioration involving more than five thousand recorded deaths, Jake Akana has successfully brought Tom back.

This statement is not provisional.

Tom Akana is present, conscious, independently responsive, and demonstrably the same individual previously designated as irrecoverable.

However, his returned manifestation differs from his former appearance.

Tom is now slightly older in appearance and noticeably taller. His facial features, posture, and overall presentation convey a more mature impression, resembling the older-brother figure he had informally become in his relationship with Jake.

He remains recognizably Tom.

He remembers Jake.

He remembers what happened.

And, most importantly, Jake did not have to accept the idea that his brother was gone forever.

**Amendment to CORE-SH-CASE-083:** The previous determination that Tom Akana could not be restored was accurate according to every method available at the time. The event documented here demonstrates that Jake eventually achieved a result previously considered impossible. The mechanism remains under investigation.

The following account prioritizes the immediate aftermath, including the first confirmed conversation between the two Akana manifestations.

---

## II. EVENT CHRONOLOGY

**TIME OF EVENT:** Not conclusively established  
**WITNESSES:** Initial contact was private. Additional SAFEHOUSE personnel arrived following notification of Tom's return.

For ten weeks, Jake had been told that there was no available method to restore Tom.

He had been told by C.O.R.E.

He had been told by the Architects.

He had been told by the people who understood Genesis Construction better than anyone else present.

And he had been told by the one person who had created Tom in the first place: himself.

Jake nevertheless continued trying.

The available evidence does not establish whether his attempts involved a new understanding of Genesis Construction, repeated reconstructions of Tom's original conceptual pattern, or an entirely unprecedented application of his abilities.

What is established is that Jake did not simply create an imitation and mistake it for the original.

The returned Tom possessed memories and personal continuity consistent with the missing individual. His recognition of Jake was immediate. Their interaction contained familiar habits, references, and emotional responses that could not be explained by a superficial resemblance alone.

At the time of initial observation, Jake was reportedly exhausted.

Tom, by contrast, was standing.

And he was taller than Jake remembered.

---

## III. INITIAL CONTACT — TRANSCRIPT

**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE, FLOOR 7  
**RECORDING STATUS:** Reconstructed from witness statements and a subsequently authorized interview. The opening exchange was not recorded directly.

The first sound reportedly heard was a sharp intake of breath.

Then silence.

Jake stared at the figure in front of him.

The figure stared back.

For several seconds, neither spoke.

Tom looked down at himself, then at Jake. His new proportions appeared to surprise him as much as they surprised everyone else who would later see him.

Finally, he said:

**TOM:** “Jake?”

Jake's face crumpled.

**JAKE:** “...Tom?”

**TOM:** “Yeah.”

Jake took one step forward.

Then another.

**JAKE:** “You're—”

His voice broke.

**JAKE:** “You're here.”

Tom's expression softened.

**TOM:** “I'm here.”

Jake reached for him so quickly that he nearly stumbled over his own feet.

Tom caught him.

The embrace was described by subsequent observers as immediate, forceful, and prolonged. Jake clung to Tom's clothes with both hands, pressing his face against his chest as if any distance between them might somehow undo what had just happened.

Tom wrapped both arms around him.

He did not ask Jake to calm down.

He did not tell him that everything was fine.

He simply held him.

**JAKE:** “I did it.”

**TOM:** “You did.”

**JAKE:** “I did it. I did it, I did it, I—”

Jake began crying too hard to finish the sentence.

Tom lowered his head and rested his cheek against Jake's white hair.

**TOM:** “Hey. I've got you.”

Jake shook his head against him.

**JAKE:** “They said you couldn't come back.”

Tom's arms tightened.

**TOM:** “I know.”

**JAKE:** “They said nobody could.”

**TOM:** “I know, Jake.”

**JAKE:** “I thought—”

He stopped.

He could not finish.

Tom waited.

After a long time, Jake managed to whisper the rest.

**JAKE:** “I thought I lost you forever.”

Tom closed his eyes.

For ten weeks, Jake had lived with that certainty. For ten weeks, every explanation he had received had ended in the same place. There was no hidden contingency, no alternate version waiting to be summoned, no promise that time would eventually correct the mistake.

And now Tom was holding him.

**TOM:** “I'm sorry.”

Jake immediately shook his head.

**JAKE:** “No! No, don't be sorry. I did it. I brought you back.”

**TOM:** “You did.”

**JAKE:** “I didn't mean to make you go away.”

**TOM:** “I know.”

**JAKE:** “I didn't want you to go.”

**TOM:** “I know, little star.”

Jake made a small, wounded sound at the familiar nickname.

He held on tighter.

**JAKE:** “Don't disappear.”

Tom looked down at him.

He could not honestly promise that no danger would ever reach them again. He could not undo the ten weeks that had already passed, nor could he make the grief those weeks had inflicted disappear with a single embrace.

But he could answer the request in front of him.

**TOM:** “I'm right here.”

**JAKE:** “Promise?”

Tom hesitated for a moment, then pressed a hand gently against the back of Jake's head.

**TOM:** “I'm here now. And we're going to figure out what happened together.”

Jake nodded against him.

It was not the same as the absolute promise Jake wanted.

It was honest.

For the moment, that was enough to let him keep breathing.

## IV. THE HEIGHT DIFFERENCE

The first prolonged embrace eventually ended, although Jake did not willingly move very far away from Tom.

He leaned back just enough to look at him.

Then he looked up.

Then further up.

Tom noticed.

**TOM:** “What?”

Jake blinked.

**JAKE:** “You're tall.”

**TOM:** “...Apparently.”

Jake took a small step backward and inspected him from head to toe.

Tom had a slightly older appearance. His proportions had changed, and his face carried a maturity absent from his previous manifestation. He was not suddenly an adult, nor had he become unrecognizable. Rather, he looked like an older version of the same boy—someone who could plausibly stand between his younger brother and the rest of the world.

Jake's expression slowly changed from tearful disbelief to bewilderment.

**JAKE:** “You're taller than me.”

**TOM:** “You noticed.”

**JAKE:** “You're supposed to be my height!”

**TOM:** “Was that a rule?”

**JAKE:** “YES!”

**TOM:** “I don't remember signing anything.”

Jake stared at him.

Tom stared back with an expression of practiced innocence.

Then Jake's lower lip began to tremble again.

Tom immediately crouched a little.

**TOM:** “Hey. Hey, no, I was kidding.”

**JAKE:** “You're different.”

The humor vanished from Tom's face.

**TOM:** “A little.”

**JAKE:** “But you're still you?”

Tom considered the question carefully.

**TOM:** “I'm still me.”

Jake searched his face.

**JAKE:** “You remember me?”

**TOM:** “You once tried to convince me that cake was a valid replacement for every meal of the day.”

**JAKE:** “It IS!”

**TOM:** “You ate frosting with a spoon and called it a balanced breakfast.”

**JAKE:** “It had dairy!”

Tom raised an eyebrow.

**TOM:** “You also tried to make a staircase out of pillows because you said the floor was too far away.”

**JAKE:** “It worked!”

**TOM:** “You fell off it.”

**JAKE:** “...It worked for a little bit.”

Tom smiled.

Jake stared at that smile.

Then he threw himself against Tom again.

**JAKE:** “YOU'RE STILL TOM!”

Tom staggered half a step from the impact.

**TOM:** “Yes! Yes, I am!”

**JAKE:** “YOU'RE REALLY TOM!”

**TOM:** “Jake, I think the entire building can hear you!”

**JAKE:** “GOOD!”

For the first time in ten weeks, Jake's loudness was not an attempt to cover up an empty room.

He was shouting because the person he had been desperate to reach could hear him.

And Tom was answering.

## V. FOLLOW-UP INTERVIEW — TOM AKANA

**INTERVIEWER:** C.O.R.E. Residential Oversight Representative  
**SUBJECT:** Tom Akana  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE, FLOOR 7  
**NOTE:** Jake declined to leave the room. He remained close enough to touch Tom throughout the interview.

**INTERVIEWER:** Do you understand what happened to you?

**TOM:** I know I was gone.

**INTERVIEWER:** Do you remember being demanifested?

**TOM:** I remember Jake. I remember what happened before. I remember being gone.

**INTERVIEWER:** Can you describe the period between your disappearance and your return?

Tom was quiet for several seconds.

**TOM:** Not properly. I don't have a useful description for it. I can't tell you I was waiting somewhere, because I don't know that I was. I can't tell you I was asleep, because I don't know that either.

**INTERVIEWER:** Do you remember your previous manifestation?

**TOM:** Yes.

**INTERVIEWER:** Your current appearance is different. Are you aware of that?

Tom glanced toward Jake.

Jake was holding his sleeve.

**TOM:** I noticed when I looked down.

**INTERVIEWER:** Do you know why your appearance changed?

**TOM:** No.

**INTERVIEWER:** Do you object to the change?

Tom looked down at himself again.

**TOM:** No. I think it's kind of funny.

**INTERVIEWER:** Funny?

**TOM:** I spent a long time being the one who had to look up at people. Now I can look down at Jake.

**JAKE:** YOU'RE NOT ALLOWED TO LOOK DOWN AT ME!

**TOM:** I literally have to look down to see you.

**JAKE:** THAT'S DIFFERENT!

**TOM:** How?

**JAKE:** BECAUSE!

The interviewer paused the questioning until Jake had finished explaining, at considerable volume, that the new height difference constituted an unacceptable violation of an unspecified sibling agreement.

No such agreement could be located in SAFEHOUSE records.

**INTERVIEWER:** Tom, do you consider yourself Jake's older brother?

Tom's expression became thoughtful.

**TOM:** I always did, in a way.

He glanced at Jake, whose hand had not left his sleeve.

**TOM:** He needed somebody who could be a little more careful when he wasn't. Somebody who could tell him when he was being ridiculous without making him feel like he was a problem for existing.

**INTERVIEWER:** And now?

**TOM:** Now I look the part, apparently.

**JAKE:** YOU LOOK LIKE A BIG BROTHER!

**TOM:** Thank you, I think.

**JAKE:** A REAL ONE!

Tom's smile faded slightly at the intensity behind the words.

**TOM:** Jake...

**JAKE:** You're my big brother.

**TOM:** I know.

**JAKE:** You still are.

**TOM:** Yes, little star. I still am.

Jake leaned against his arm.

Tom did not move away.

## VI. FOLLOW-UP INTERVIEW — JAKE AKANA

**INTERVIEWER:** C.O.R.E. Residential Oversight Representative  
**SUBJECT:** Jake Akana  
**STATUS:** Voluntary participation; breaks offered throughout.

Jake's interview was conducted with Tom present. When asked whether he would prefer to speak alone, Jake refused immediately.

**INTERVIEWER:** Jake, how did you bring Tom back?

Jake looked at Tom before answering.

**JAKE:** I tried.

**INTERVIEWER:** Can you explain what you tried?

**JAKE:** I tried really hard.

**INTERVIEWER:** For how long?

**JAKE:** I don't know.

**INTERVIEWER:** Days? Weeks?

**JAKE:** I don't know!

Jake's voice rose sharply. He immediately looked frightened by his own response and reached for Tom's hand.

**JAKE:** Sorry.

**TOM:** You don't have to apologize for not knowing.

**JAKE:** I was trying to do it right.

**INTERVIEWER:** We understand.

**JAKE:** No, you don't.

The room went quiet.

Jake looked down at his hands.

**JAKE:** Everybody said it couldn't work. Everybody said he was gone. I knew they weren't lying. They weren't trying to be mean. They just... they didn't know how to bring him back.

He swallowed.

**JAKE:** But I made him.

**INTERVIEWER:** You created Tom originally through Genesis Construction.

**JAKE:** He's not just something I made!

His response was immediate.

Tom's fingers tightened around his.

**JAKE:** He's Tom. He gets annoyed at me. He doesn't like when I eat too much cake before dinner. He knows when I'm pretending I'm not sad. He gets scared when I get hurt, even when I tell him I'm okay.

Jake's eyes filled again.

**JAKE:** He's my brother.

**INTERVIEWER:** We understand that distinction.

**JAKE:** I couldn't make everybody else understand it. I couldn't make anybody bring him back. So I kept trying.

**INTERVIEWER:** Did you believe it would work?

Jake took a long time to answer.

**JAKE:** No.

**INTERVIEWER:** Then why did you continue?

Jake looked at Tom.

**JAKE:** Because if I stopped, he still wouldn't be here.

No one in the room attempted to correct him.

**JAKE:** I didn't know how. I didn't know if I could. I just knew I wanted him back.

His voice became very small.

**JAKE:** I wanted my Tom.

Tom pulled him closer.

**TOM:** I'm here.

**JAKE:** I know.

Jake pressed his face into Tom's side.

**JAKE:** I know. I just... I need to hear it.

**TOM:** Then I'll tell you.

Tom rested his chin against Jake's hair.

**TOM:** I'm here, Jake.

A pause.

**TOM:** I'm here.

Jake cried again, but he did not let go of Tom's hand.

## VII. MEDICAL AND ANOMALOUS ASSESSMENT

The return of Tom Akana constitutes a major revision to C.O.R.E.'s understanding of Genesis Construction and its interaction with demanifested entities.

The following findings are supported by direct observation and witness testimony.

**1. Identity continuity**

Tom demonstrates recognition of Jake, shared memories, established relational habits, and responses consistent with the individual who was previously lost.

Current assessment identifies him as the returned Tom Akana, not a substitute or an imitation.

**2. Manifestation changes**

Tom's returned form is slightly older in appearance and taller than his previous manifestation. The alteration appears consistent with an older-brother presentation, although its cause is unknown.

No evidence currently establishes whether the change was intentional, incidental, or a consequence of the restoration process itself.

**3. Genesis Construction limits**

The prior conclusion that Tom could not be restored reflected the limits of knowledge and available methods at the time. Jake has now achieved an outcome previously considered impossible.

C.O.R.E. must not infer that all demanifested creations can be restored, or that this result can be reproduced on demand. The event's mechanism, prerequisites, and limits remain unknown.

**4. Psychological condition of Jake Akana**

Tom's return does not erase the ten weeks of bereavement, the repeated suicidal behavior documented in CORE-SH-CRISIS-093, or the psychological consequences of more than five thousand recorded deaths.

Jake is showing intense relief and attachment-seeking behavior. These responses are understandable given the circumstances, but they must not be mistaken for complete recovery.

The return of a loved one may reduce one source of acute distress without automatically resolving the wider crisis.

**5. Psychological condition of Tom Akana**

Tom has returned to a situation in which Jake has undergone a prolonged and dangerous crisis. Tom must receive support of his own. His relationship with Jake must not be treated as a substitute for clinical care, nor should he be assigned sole responsibility for preventing further incidents.

Tom is Jake's brother.

He is not a containment protocol.

He is not an emergency response team.

And he is not required to carry the entire burden of Jake's survival simply because he understands Jake in ways other people do not.

## VIII. EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVES

Effective immediately:

1. Tom Akana is to be formally reinstated as a SAFEHOUSE resident and recognized as an individual with independent agency, needs, and privacy.
    
2. The definitive loss determination in CORE-SH-CASE-083 is to be amended to reflect the successful return. Its historical account of the evidence available at the time is to remain intact.
    
3. No staff member is to demand that Jake reproduce the restoration process for demonstration, testing, or research purposes.
    
4. No deliberate demanifestation, lethal testing, or other hazardous experimentation is authorized.
    
5. Jake's existing crisis-care plan must continue. Tom's return is not grounds to terminate suicide-risk assessment, emergency response, or clinical support.
    
6. Tom must be offered independent support and a confidential opportunity to describe his return and current condition.
    
7. Neither brother is to be pressured into immediately discussing every detail of the loss. Both may choose when and how to speak about it.
    
8. SAFEHOUSE residents are to be reminded that celebrating Tom's return does not mean expecting Jake to become instantly well, nor expecting Tom to compensate for every moment of loneliness Jake experienced.
    
9. C.O.R.E. personnel must refrain from framing the event as proof that Jake should have succeeded earlier. He spent ten weeks trying to accomplish what every available authority considered impossible. The outcome does not retroactively make his grief, desperation, or prior suffering a failure of effort.

## IX. FINAL OBSERVATION — THE BROTHERS

The formal interviews concluded shortly before the evening meal.

The interviewer asked Jake whether he would like to return to the common room. There were other residents waiting to see Tom, and several had already sent messages asking whether the two brothers wanted company.

Jake considered the question.

Then he looked up at Tom.

**JAKE:** “Can we stay here a little longer?”

**TOM:** “Of course.”

**JAKE:** “Just us?”

**TOM:** “Just us.”

Jake hesitated.

**JAKE:** “You won't get bored?”

Tom gave him a look.

**TOM:** “Jake, I have spent an unreasonable amount of my existence watching you attempt to turn ordinary household objects into playground equipment.”

**JAKE:** “That was ONE TIME.”

**TOM:** “It was several times.”

**JAKE:** “THE PILLOW STAIRCASE WAS GOOD!”

**TOM:** “You fell off it.”

**JAKE:** “YOU ALREADY SAID THAT!”

**TOM:** “And I will continue saying it until you admit I was right.”

Jake huffed, but the corners of his mouth lifted.

He leaned against Tom again.

For a little while, neither of them spoke.

Then Jake reached up and touched Tom's sleeve, as if checking that it was still there.

Tom noticed.

He turned his hand over and let Jake hold it properly.

**TOM:** “You don't have to keep checking.”

Jake stared at their joined hands.

**JAKE:** “I know.”

**TOM:** “But you can, if you need to.”

Jake's eyes watered again.

**JAKE:** “I missed you.”

Tom's expression softened into something that had no room for teasing.

**TOM:** “I missed you too.”

**JAKE:** “A lot.”

**TOM:** “I believe you.”

**JAKE:** “More than cake.”

Tom went very still.

Then, with great solemnity, he said:

**TOM:** “Jake, I need you to understand that this is an extremely serious declaration.”

Jake gave a wet little laugh.

**JAKE:** “I KNOW.”

Tom drew him into another hug.

Outside the room, SAFEHOUSE continued its ordinary routines. Doors opened. Someone called from the kitchen. Residents argued over dinner. Life continued around a loss that had, until very recently, seemed permanent.

Inside, a boy who had spent ten weeks waking up without his brother finally had someone to wake up beside again.

Tom was taller now.

A little older.

A little different.

But when Jake's voice grew small and he asked one more time, _“You're really here?”_, Tom answered him with the same familiar patience.

“I'm really here, little star.”

And Jake, for once, did not have to imagine the reply.

### C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The previous report concluded that Tom Akana could not be brought home. At the time, that conclusion was supported by every available source of knowledge, including the statements of entities whose understanding of reality exceeds our own.

Jake did not overturn that conclusion because the people who delivered it were cruel, careless, or dishonest.

He overturned it because he kept trying to reach someone he loved, even when every available answer told him that the attempt was futile.

We must be careful with what we take from this.

We must not teach Jake that he has to suffer enough, try hard enough, or hurt himself enough to make the impossible yield. We must not turn his success into another impossible expectation placed upon a child who has already endured more than any child should.

Tom's return is not evidence that every loss can be undone.

It is not permission to disregard the danger Jake remains in.

It is not a reason to forget the ten weeks in which he needed help and did not know how to ask for it anymore.

It is, however, a reason to acknowledge that our understanding was incomplete.

And it is a reason to be grateful that, this time, when Jake reached for his brother, someone was there to take his hand.

**Tom Akana is home.**

**Jake Akana is still hurting.**

Both statements are true.

C.O.R.E. will protect the first without neglecting the second.
