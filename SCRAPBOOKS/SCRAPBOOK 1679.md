### RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-JAKE-252  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — RESIDENT WELFARE  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE MONITORING  
**SUBJECT:** Jake — Cessation of Full-Moon Manifestations  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — SECTOR 4-C

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has stopped returning to SAFEHOUSE during full moons.

The change was initially noticed because nothing happened.

No footsteps.

No movement in the hallway.

No small figure entering the common room.

No quiet visits to the cafeteria.

No child sitting alone at a table.

No food disappearing overnight.

No blue glow moving through the corridors.

Nothing.

The first full moon passed without Jake.

Then the second.

Then the third.

The fourth.

C.O.R.E. has confirmed that Jake's blue star remains visible in the night sky.

There is no evidence that he has ceased to exist.

He simply no longer returns in physical manifestation.

This has affected the SAFEHOUSE considerably.

Particularly because, of the residents currently present, only **Bao, Nora, and Clover have ever directly encountered Jake during this period.**

Everyone else knows him through memory, records, stories, and the accounts of those three.

And now they are left with an uncomfortable realization:

> **They had been waiting for him to come back.**

---

# 1. FIRST FULL MOON

The first missed full moon was not immediately considered significant.

The common room had been prepared as usual.

Food had been left out.

The lights were dimmed.

The environment was kept quiet.

Nobody formed an interception group.

Nobody searched for him.

The protocol remained unchanged.

C.O.R.E. simply waited.

Midnight passed.

01:00.

02:00.

03:00.

Nothing.

By morning, the food remained untouched.

The cafeteria was exactly as it had been the previous evening.

Nobody commented.

At first.

---

# 2. SECOND FULL MOON

The second full moon produced a different atmosphere.

People noticed.

Bao arrived in the common room shortly before midnight.

Nora was already there.

Clover was sitting at the table with her candle.

The three of them waited.

No one spoke much.

The moon crossed the window.

Nothing happened.

Nora eventually looked toward the hallway.

> **NORA:**  
> "...He's not coming."

Bao did not answer immediately.

> **BAO:**  
> "Maybe not tonight."

Nora nodded.

But she did not look convinced.

Clover continued holding her pencil.

She did not draw.

---

# 3. THIRD FULL MOON

By the third full moon, the residents had stopped pretending this was coincidence.

The food was still left out.

The lights were still dimmed.

The cafeteria remained accessible.

The doors remained unlocked.

The common room remained quiet.

But nobody expected the same outcome as before.

Jake did not come.

The blue star remained visible.

That was all.

---

# 4. BAO

Bao took the change hardest.

She had been one of the few residents who had actually seen Jake during his return period.

She had watched him walk through SAFEHOUSE.

She had seen him eat.

She had watched him sit quietly.

She had once been able to sit near him without him leaving immediately.

Now there was nothing.

Bao continued leaving food anyway.

Sandwich.

Fruit.

Cake.

Milk.

She knew he might never take any of it.

She left it anyway.

When asked why:

> **BAO:**  
> "Because he might."

That answer remained unchanged.

---

# 5. NORA

Nora was affected differently.

She had been the first resident to have an extended quiet conversation with Jake during one of his full-moon visits.

Jake could not speak.

Nora had talked anyway.

For forty-three minutes.

She had told him about ordinary things.

Food.

Family.

The store.

Her journal.

Nothing important.

Everything important.

Afterward, Jake had left a tiny blue star in her family journal.

Nora kept the page open.

She continued talking about him occasionally.

She also continued checking the cafeteria at midnight.

Eventually:

> **NORA:**  
> "Maybe he doesn't want to come back."

Nobody contradicted her.

---

# 6. CLOVER

Clover's reaction was the most complicated.

She knew why Jake might have stopped coming.

She had been the last resident to see him during his previous return.

She had screamed when she saw him.

She had been terrified.

She had not intended to hurt him.

But she knew what happened afterward.

He stopped coming.

Clover continued drawing him.

Her drawings changed over time.

Initially:

A frightened-looking child.

Then:

A child holding a teddy bear.

Then:

A child standing beneath a blue star.

Eventually, she began drawing him from farther away.

A tiny figure.

A large sky.

A lot of empty space.

Clover never showed the drawings to anyone.

---

# 7. JANE DOE

Jane did not witness any of Jake's full-moon manifestations.

She had heard about them.

She had remembered him.

She had visited the Grassland.

She had spoken to him before his departure.

But she had never personally seen his later returns.

That fact began bothering her.

She had initially joked about the situation.

Then stopped.

One evening, Jane sat in the common room beside the untouched food.

She stared at the empty chair.

> **JANE:**  
> "I keep thinking I'm going to look up and he'll be there."

No one answered.

Jane continued:

> **JANE:**  
> "That's stupid."

Bao quietly replied:

> **BAO:**  
> "No."

Jane looked at her.

Bao shook her head.

> **BAO:**  
> "It isn't."

---

# 8. NERI

Neri had never directly encountered Jake during the later full-moon visits.

She knew he returned.

She knew Nora had spoken with him.

She knew Bao had seen him.

She knew Clover had seen him.

But she never did.

Neri's reaction was characteristically quiet.

She began leaving small drawings in the common room.

Tiny blue stars.

Tiny flowers.

Sometimes both together.

Nobody knew whether she expected Jake to see them.

When asked:

> **NERI:**  
> "Maybe."

That was all.

---

# 9. FROST

Frost took the absence personally.

Not as anger.

As guilt.

She remembered being too busy.

She remembered how easily weeks had passed.

She remembered the silence.

Now the full moon came and went without him.

She eventually admitted:

> **FROST:**  
> "I wish I'd gone sooner."

Bao answered:

> **BAO:**  
> "We all do."

Frost looked down.

> **FROST:**  
> "But wishing doesn't bring him back."

Bao did not disagree.

---

# 10. ALICE

Alice was confused by the change.

She had never personally seen Jake during the full-moon period.

Her memories of him were complicated by her own memory anomaly.

When she was told that he had stopped returning, she stared at the moon outside.

> **ALICE:**  
> "So he's still up there?"

> **NORA:**  
> "Yeah."

Alice looked at the sky.

> **ALICE:**  
> "Then why doesn't he come down?"

Nobody had an answer.

Alice became unusually quiet afterward.

---

# 11. AETERNA

Aeterna had not directly encountered Jake during the later manifestations either.

She understood his behavior better than most.

She did not interpret his absence as disappearance.

She interpreted it as a choice.

When asked whether she believed Jake would return:

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Maybe."

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "That's it?"

Aeterna looked toward the sky.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "He decides."

She paused.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "We don't."

---

# 12. PHOENIX

Phoenix had never seen Jake during one of his later physical visits.

She knew him through reports.

She knew what he had survived.

She knew what his absence had done to the residents.

She also knew Clover was carrying guilt over their encounter.

Phoenix therefore refused to allow Clover to interpret the situation as entirely her fault.

One night:

> **CLOVER:**  
> "He hasn't come back."

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "No."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Because of me."

Phoenix looked at her.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "You don't know that."

Clover looked down.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "I scared him."

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "Yes."

Pause.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "But you aren't the only reason he hurts."

Clover said nothing.

Phoenix continued drawing her medical notes.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "Don't turn his entire life into one thirty-seven-second conversation."

Clover eventually nodded.

---

# 13. NEKO

Neko approached the matter differently.

He reviewed the available observations.

The star remained.

No abnormal stellar collapse had been detected.

No anomalous death event had been observed.

No dimensional displacement had been recorded.

No disappearance event had been confirmed.

Technically:

Jake was still there.

Neko knew this.

That did not make the absence easier.

He eventually summarized the situation:

> **NEKO:**  
> "He's alive."

Pause.

> **NEKO:**  
> "He's simply not coming home."

Nobody corrected him.

---

# 14. MISS NEKO

Miss Neko disliked the wording.

She eventually amended it.

> **MISS NEKO:**  
> "He may still consider it home."

Neko looked at her.

> **MISS NEKO:**  
> "He just may not believe he belongs here anymore."

The room became quiet.

---

# 15. THE COMMON ROOM

The common room gradually developed a strange routine.

Every full moon:

Food appeared.

A candle appeared.

The lights were dimmed.

A chair remained empty.

Nobody sat in it.

Not because it was forbidden.

Because everyone knew whose chair it had become.

Sometimes Nora sat nearby.

Sometimes Bao.

Sometimes Clover.

Sometimes nobody.

The food was removed in the morning.

Nothing was ever disturbed.

---

# 16. THE BLUE STAR

Jake's star remained visible.

This became the most painful part.

There was no disappearance.

No final event.

No dramatic farewell.

No message.

Just a star.

Every night.

Visible from the SAFEHOUSE windows.

It meant:

> **He is still somewhere.**

It did not mean:

> **He is coming back.**

The distinction gradually became understood.

---

# 17. WHAT THE RESIDENTS ACTUALLY KNOW

C.O.R.E. has compiled the following facts.

### Confirmed:

Jake exists.

Jake remains in stellar manifestation.

Jake's blue star remains observable.

Jake is capable of returning physically.

Jake previously returned during full moons.

Jake stopped doing so.

### Unknown:

Why he stopped.

Where he spends the full moons now.

Whether he watches SAFEHOUSE.

Whether he sees the food.

Whether he remembers Clover.

Whether he wants to return.

Whether he considers SAFEHOUSE home anymore.

No resident has been authorized to pursue him for answers.

---

# 18. THE THREE WHO SAW HIM

Only three current residents can honestly say:

> **"I saw him."**

### Bao.

She saw him because he still trusted her enough to appear.

### Nora.

She saw him because she stayed awake and simply talked to him.

### Clover.

She saw him because she happened to be there.

And Clover wishes desperately that her first encounter had gone differently.

Everyone else has only memories.

Stories.

A teddy bear.

Drawings.

A blue star.

And an empty chair.

---

# 19. CLOVER'S DRAWING

On the fourth full moon, Clover finished a drawing.

It showed the SAFEHOUSE common room.

There was an empty chair.

A table.

A plate of cake.

A glass of milk.

A small pot containing a four-leaf clover.

And outside the window:

A tiny blue star.

Clover stared at the drawing.

Then wrote beneath it:

> **"You don't have to come inside."**

She paused.

Then added:

> **"But you can."**

She closed the notebook.

She did not leave the drawing on the table.

She kept it.

---

# 20. BAO'S FOOD

Bao continued leaving food.

Nobody asked her to stop.

Nobody told her it was pointless.

She knew.

She also knew that it did not matter.

One evening, Nora asked:

> **NORA:**  
> "Mom?"

Bao looked at her.

> **NORA:**  
> "What if he never comes back?"

Bao was silent for several seconds.

Then:

> **BAO:**  
> "Then I'll keep leaving food."

Nora looked confused.

> **NORA:**  
> "Why?"

Bao looked toward the window.

The blue star was visible.

> **BAO:**  
> "Because I want him to know he can."

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Jake's cessation of full-moon manifestations should **not** be classified as disappearance.

It is an absence.

There is a difference.

He has not been erased.

He has not been destroyed.

He has not been forgotten.

He has simply stopped returning.

And for residents who once failed to notice his absence until it became catastrophic, this creates an uncomfortable new responsibility:

They must learn how to care about someone who is no longer physically present.

Not by chasing him.

Not by demanding that he return.

Not by forcing the door open.

Simply by leaving it open.

---

# CLOSING OBSERVATION

The next full moon arrived.

The common room was quiet.

Bao left food.

Nora brought her journal.

Clover brought her candle.

Neri left a small blue drawing.

Jane left a note.

Nobody knew whether Jake would see any of it.

Midnight passed.

Nothing.

01:00.

Nothing.

02:00.

Nothing.

The food remained untouched.

The chair remained empty.

Eventually, everyone went to bed.

Clover stayed awake.

She sat beside the window.

Outside, the blue star shone above the SAFEHOUSE.

She looked at it for a long time.

Then quietly said:

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Goodnight, Jake."

There was no answer.

There hadn't been one for a long time.

But the star remained.

And, for now, that was the only proof they had that he was still there.
