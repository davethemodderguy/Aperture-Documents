# 1. CLARIFICATION

C.O.R.E. has received several questions regarding the noises produced by Neri while in plush form.

The answer is simple:

> **Yes. Plush Neri squeaks like a plushie.**

This is a normal property of her current form.

A sufficiently firm squeeze, bump, fall, compression, or other ordinary plush interaction may produce:

> **Squeak.**

Neri is aware of this.

She does not appear particularly concerned about it.

---

# 2. FRIENDS ARE PERMITTED

Neri's friends have considerably more freedom regarding plush interaction.

If Neri is comfortable with someone, they may:

- gently squeeze her;
    
- gently poke her;
    
- pick her up;
    
- carry her;
    
- give her headpats;
    
- hug her gently;
    
- place her on a couch;
    
- move her somewhere comfortable;
    
- or otherwise interact with her in the ordinary manner one might interact with a willing plush toy.
    

If this produces:

> **SQUEAK**

Neri generally does not object.

She may simply stare at the person afterward.

---

# 3. THE IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

There is, however, a very simple rule:

> **If Neri doesn't want you doing it, stop.**

She communicates this extremely clearly.

The most common response is:

### **BITE.**

---

# 4. THE BITE

Plush Neri retains a functional jaw.

Her bite is strong enough to latch onto clothing, sleeves, hands, or other objects.

However:

> **It does not cause pain.**

It does not normally cause injury.

It is essentially Neri's plush equivalent of:

> **“Stop.”**

Personnel should therefore not panic if she bites them.

They should instead:

1. stop whatever they were doing;
    
2. gently allow her to release;
    
3. respect the boundary.
    

---

# 5. EXAMPLE

An unfamiliar employee attempted to squeeze Neri repeatedly.

### **SQUEAK.**

Neri stared at them.

The employee squeezed again.

### **SQUEAK.**

Neri stared harder.

The employee squeezed again.

Neri bit their sleeve.

The employee froze.

> **EMPLOYEE:**  
> “...Oh.”

Neri released the sleeve.

The employee stopped.

Neri immediately returned to sitting quietly.

No injury occurred.

---

# 6. FRIEND EXAMPLE

Jake laughed.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Sorry.”

Neri remained in his arms.

No bite.

This was interpreted as:

> **Approved.**

# 8. ALICE

Alice has considerably more experience with plush Neri.

Alice treats plush Neri much like an ordinary plush toy while still recognizing that Neri is a person.

Neri generally tolerates Alice:

- carrying her;
    
- hugging her;
    
- placing her on furniture;
    
- giving her headpats;
    
- putting her on her back;
    
- and occasionally squeezing her enough to produce a squeak.
    

If Neri becomes annoyed, Alice normally notices quickly.

If she does not:

### **NOM.**

Alice understands.

---

---

# 10. BITE SEVERITY

C.O.R.E. has determined that plush Neri's bite is:

### **Physically:**

Negligible.

### **Socially:**

Extremely clear.

### **Recommended interpretation:**

> **“Stop.”**

Personnel are not required to seek medical treatment after an ordinary plush Neri bite unless there is an unrelated injury.

Do not attempt to test how hard she can bite.

Do not deliberately provoke her into biting.

Do not treat her bite as a game unless Neri is clearly participating willingly.

---

# 11. SQUEAK INTERPRETATION

Not every squeak means the same thing.

### **Accidental squeak**

Neri falls over.

> **SQUEAK.**

Usually followed by someone picking her up.

### **Playful squeak**

Someone gently squeezes her with her permission.

> **SQUEAK.**

Neri remains where she is.

### **Distress squeak**

Neri is squeezed too hard, dropped, trapped, or otherwise physically uncomfortable.

> **SQUEAK.**

Neri attempts to get away.

**STOP IMMEDIATELY.**

The sound itself is not sufficient to determine intent.

Personnel should observe Neri's behavior.

---

# 13. CURRENT ADMINISTRATIVE RULE

The updated SAFEHOUSE guidance is therefore:

> **If Neri is your friend and she is comfortable with you, gentle plush interaction is permitted.**

> **If you're unsure, don't.**

> **If she bites you, stop.**

> **If she squeaks because she fell, pick her up.**

> **If she squeaks because you squeezed her too hard, stop squeezing.**

> **Do not intentionally make her squeak simply because the sound is amusing.**

The final rule was added after several employees apparently became fascinated by the noise.

## ARCHIVIST NOTE

Plush Neri has essentially developed the standard behavior of a very small, very soft animal:

You may pet her.

You may carry her.

You may gently squeeze her.

She may squeak.

If she likes it:

> **Everything is fine.**

If she doesn't:

> **BITE.**

The bite does not hurt.

But the message is apparently extremely effective.

One employee summarized the system perfectly:

> **“She's basically got a built-in mute button for people.”**

Neri overheard this.

She nodded.

Then bit his sleeve.
