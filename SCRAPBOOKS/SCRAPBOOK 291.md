# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – INCIDENT REPORT**

### **SUBJECT: DELIBERATE HARM TO A MART**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

At approximately 14:27, Security cameras recorded an unidentified individual intentionally harming a Mart.

The organism was cornered near the SAFEHOUSE courtyard before being deliberately struck.

The Mart did not attempt to flee.

Instead, it simply looked up at the individual.

Witnesses reported it quietly saying:

> "No..."

Its Ether containment field destabilized almost immediately.

Within seconds, it gently dissolved into ordinary water.

No physical remains were recovered beyond a small puddle and the tiny black top hat it had been wearing.

---

# **WITNESS RESPONSE**

Personnel nearby immediately responded.

Several Marts that had been working in the garden gathered around the puddle.

None displayed panic.

None attempted to retaliate.

Instead, they quietly picked up the tiny hat and placed it beside the water.

One after another, they softly said:

> "Goodbye..."

> "We'll meet again."

Then they dispersed.

---

# **HIVEMIND RESPONSE**

Despite sharing a collective consciousness, the remaining Marts exhibited no signs of fear or aggression.

Behavior remained normal.

When questioned by researchers, one Mart simply answered:

> "We'll be back."

Another nodded enthusiastically.

> "Yes!"

Research believes the hivemind perceives the loss of an individual as the conclusion of one life within a continuing collective, rather than the destruction of the whole.

Even so, Psychological Services noted a brief reduction in overall Mart activity for several hours following the incident, suggesting the colony does experience loss, albeit differently from humans.

---

# **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

**Lumi**

Lumi quietly picked up the tiny top hat.

She brushed a bit of dirt from it before placing it on a shelf in the SAFEHOUSE lounge.

After a long silence, she spoke.

> "...I know you'll come back."

Another pause.

> "...But you still mattered."

---

**Corin Lockwood**

Corin remained silent throughout the investigation.

She later placed a small flower beside the puddle where the Mart had dissolved.

---

**Corey Lockwood**

Corey watched the other Marts say goodbye.

She whispered:

> "...Even if they aren't afraid of dying..."

She looked away.

> "...They still deserved kindness."

---

**Belle Merryweather**

Belle ordered an immediate investigation into the identity of the responsible individual.

Her report contained a single handwritten sentence.

> **"There is no justification for cruelty."**

---

**Endmin Merryweather**

> "...The colony may endure."

> "...That does not excuse the act."

---

**Star**

Star spent the remainder of the afternoon playing games with every Mart she could find.

No one questioned why.

---

**Nightshade**

After reviewing the security footage, Nightshade made one comment.

> "...Find them."

No further remarks were recorded.

---

**Neko**

Neko retrieved the puddle for analysis.

His scientific conclusion was brief.

> "...The species survives."

His personal note, written beneath the report, was even shorter.

> "...Unnecessary."

---

**Miss Neko**

She carefully returned the tiny hat to Lumi.

> "...Keep it."

---

# **FOLLOW-UP**

Three weeks later, while personnel were tending the SAFEHOUSE garden, a newly divided Mart bounced toward Lumi.

It wore a tiny black top hat nearly identical to the one preserved on the lounge shelf.

The little blob smiled brightly.

> "Hello!"

Lumi smiled back.

> "...Hello."

The Mart tilted its head.

Then happily announced:

> "We'll be back!"

Lumi laughed quietly.

> "...I know."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Mart colony has fully recovered in number through its normal reproductive cycle.

However, Security Division has updated Blacksite policy to explicitly classify deliberate harm toward Marts as animal cruelty and misconduct subject to disciplinary action.

The incident also led to an unofficial tradition within SAFEHOUSE.

Whenever a Mart reaches the end of its life—or whenever one is lost unexpectedly—someone places its tiny hat somewhere peaceful until another Mart eventually claims it.

The Marts may not fear death.

But everyone else believes each one deserves to be remembered, however briefly it was here.

**End of Incident Report**
