## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### DIRECTORATE & RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-ARC-4C-NERI-270  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — HISTORICAL / RESIDENT WELFARE  
**STATUS:** ARCHIVED  
**SUBJECT:** Neri Tachyon — Personal Heirloom Returned by Director Lucious  
**OBJECT:** Personal Wristwatch — Former Property of Lewis Tachyon

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Director Lucious has returned a personal possession of **Lewis Tachyon** to his family.

Specifically:

**A wristwatch.**

The watch was previously owned by **Lewis Tachyon**, co-founder of Aperture Industries.

Prior to his death, Lewis gave the watch to Lucious.

Lucious retained it for years.

It has now been returned to its **original family.**

More specifically:

> **Neri Tachyon.**

The final surviving member of the Tachyon family.

---

# 2. HISTORICAL RECORD

Lewis Tachyon was one of the individuals responsible for the founding of Aperture Industries.

His contributions to the earliest organization were substantial.

At some point during his life, Lewis acquired the watch currently held by Lucious.

The precise circumstances surrounding its acquisition are considered unimportant.

What is documented is what happened afterward.

Lewis eventually gave the watch to Lucious.

Lucious accepted it.

Lewis later died of a heart attack.

The watch remained with Lucious.

For years.

---

# 3. THE RETURN

The exchange occurred without ceremony.

No board meeting.

No press release.

No formal presentation.

No security escort.

Lucious simply approached Neri.

Neri was sitting quietly with her coffee.

Lucious placed a small case on the table.

Neri looked at it.

Then at him.

> **Neri:**  
> “...What?”

Lucious opened the case.

Inside was the watch.

Neri immediately stopped moving.

---

# 4. CONVERSATION

> **Lucious:**  
> “This belonged to Lewis.”

Neri looked down.

She did not touch it.

> **Lucious:**  
> “He gave it to me a long time ago.”

Silence.

> **Lucious:**  
> “I've kept it ever since.”

Neri slowly reached toward the watch.

She picked it up.

Her expression remained almost completely neutral.

But she held it extremely carefully.

> **Neri:**  
> “Why?”

Lucious answered immediately.

> **Lucious:**  
> “Because it was never really mine.”

Neri looked at him.

> **Lucious:**  
> “I was keeping it for the Tachyon family.”

Pause.

> **Lucious:**  
> “I just didn't know there was still someone left to give it to.”

Neri lowered her eyes.

She continued holding the watch.

---

# 5. THE NAME

Lucious did not initially explain the significance of the object beyond its owner.

He did not need to.

The inside of the case contained a small handwritten notation from decades earlier:

> **LEWIS TACHYON**

Neri stared at the name.

For several seconds.

Then she quietly said:

> **Neri:**  
> “...Lewis.”

Lucious nodded.

> **Lucious:**  
> “Your family.”

Neri said nothing.

She simply held the watch closer.

---

# 6. HOW NERI WEARS IT

Neri began wearing the watch almost immediately.

However, she did not wear it conventionally.

Personnel familiar with historical photographs of Lewis Tachyon noticed the similarity.

Lewis had worn the watch with the **watch face turned inward**, toward the underside of his wrist.

Neri does the same.

The watch face is therefore normally hidden against her wrist.

The outside of her wrist shows only the rear of the watch.

This appears to be deliberate.

When asked why:

> **Neri:**  
> “That's how he wore it.”

No additional explanation was provided.

---

# 7. OBSERVATION

Neri does not frequently check the time using the watch.

She has a phone.

The Blacksite has clocks.

Her laptop displays the time.

The watch is therefore not particularly useful as a timekeeping device.

Its function appears to be entirely different.

It is something she carries because it belonged to someone who came before her.

Someone whose name she had forgotten.

Someone whose family name she now knows she carries.

Someone who helped build the organization that eventually rescued her.

---

# 8. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS

Lucious was asked whether he regretted giving away the watch.

He answered:

> **“No.”**

When asked whether he had considered keeping it as a historical artifact:

> **“It already was one.”**

Pause.

> **“Now it's with the person it belongs to.”**

No further statement was recorded.

---

# 9. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri has not publicly discussed the watch.

She does not show it to people.

She does not explain its history.

She does not refer to it as an heirloom.

She simply wears it.

Sometimes personnel can see her thumb briefly touch the edge of the watch beneath her sleeve.

She usually does this when she is sitting alone.

She never checks the time.

She simply touches it.

---

# 10. ARCHIVIST NOTE

The Tachyon family was once considered a significant part of Aperture's history.

Lewis Tachyon.

David Tachyon.

Lucia.

Nate.

Others whose names remain scattered throughout historical records.

For decades, their possessions became archival objects.

Photographs.

Documents.

Research.

Artifacts.

The watch was different.

It had remained with a living person.

Lucious could have kept it.

He could have placed it in the Aperture historical archive.

He could have locked it away as a relic of the company's founding.

Instead, he gave it to a ten-year-old seal who barely speaks.

Neri did not ask for it.

She did not know it existed.

She did not even remember Lewis when she first heard his name.

Now she wears his watch exactly as he did.

Face inward.

Quietly.

---

# C.O.R.E. FINAL ASSESSMENT

The watch is no longer classified as:

> **Historical Property of Aperture Industries.**

Its ownership has been amended to:

> **PERSONAL PROPERTY — NERI TACHYON**

No retrieval authorization exists.

No archival transfer is permitted without Neri's consent.

No personnel are to request that she remove it for inspection except where medically or operationally necessary.

It is hers.

It always should have been.

---

## DIRECTORATE CLOSING NOTE

Lewis Tachyon gave Lucious a watch.

Lucious kept it after Lewis died.

Years later, Lucious found the last Tachyon.

He gave it back.

Neri put it on.

The same way Lewis did.

And now, whenever she looks down at her wrist—

she isn't looking at a piece of Aperture history.

She's wearing something that belonged to her family.

For the first time in a very long time,

something Tachyon came back home.

**— END RECORD**
