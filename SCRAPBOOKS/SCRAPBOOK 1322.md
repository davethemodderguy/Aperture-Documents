# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The incident began with a jar of pepper.

It ended with C.O.R.E. discovering that one of its youngest residents may not have been born on Earth.

He may not have been born at all.

---

# 1. THE PEPPER INCIDENT

At approximately 19:14, an unidentified SAFEHOUSE resident accidentally threw a jar of pepper across the common room.

The jar missed its intended trajectory.

It struck Jake directly in the face.

Jake froze.

There was approximately a two-second delay.

Then:

> **Jake:**  
> “...Ah.”

Pause.

> **Jake:**  
> “Ah—”

Another pause.

> **Jake:**  
> “ACHOO!”

Jake sneezed violently.

Then again.

And again.

And again.

Within approximately thirty seconds, the sneezing had progressed into persistent coughing.

His eyes watered.

His nose became irritated.

His celestial particles began rapidly flickering around him.

His teddy bear's heartbeat accelerated.

---

# 2. INITIAL RESPONSE

Neri was the first resident to locate Jake.

She found him sitting against the wall, clutching his teddy bear while attempting unsuccessfully to breathe between sneezes.

> **Neri:**  
> “Jake?”

> **Jake:**  
> “Pepper.”

> **Neri:**  
> “I know.”

> **Jake:**  
> “It hurts.”

Nora arrived shortly afterward.

She immediately began searching for medical supplies.

The search took considerably longer than expected.

The SAFEHOUSE medical storage system contained:

- cough suppressants;
    
- antihistamines;
    
- throat medication;
    
- respiratory supplies;
    
- several varieties of children's medicine;
    
- and an unreasonable quantity of emergency chocolate.
    

What it did **not** contain was an immediately identifiable bottle of cough syrup.

Nora eventually found it.

Neri administered the appropriate dose.

Several minutes later, Jake's coughing began to subside.

His sneezing became less frequent.

Eventually:

Silence.

Jake sat quietly.

His hair was disheveled.

His eyes were watery.

His teddy bear remained tightly held against his chest.

> **Jake:**  
> “I don't like pepper.”

Neri wrote this down.

---

# 3. THE UNEXPECTED SAMPLE

During the incident, C.O.R.E. medical personnel collected a small sample of the fluid expelled during Jake's prolonged sneezing and coughing.

The intention was routine.

Determine whether the exposure had caused an anomalous reaction.

The laboratory results were not routine.

The sample contained traces of:

- helium;
    
- hydrogen;
    
- oxygen;
    
- carbon-based compounds;
    
- unidentified particulate matter;
    
- extremely small quantities of metallic elements;
    
- and a crystalline particulate structure not matching any known terrestrial biological contaminant.
    

The most unusual component was the helium.

Not the presence of helium itself.

The **isotopic and particulate arrangement** in which it was found.

Neri noticed it first.

She stopped reading the report.

Then read it again.

Then looked at Neko.

> **Neri:**  
> “Stars.”

Neko stared at her.

> **Neko:**  
> “What?”

Neri pointed at the analysis.

> **Neri:**  
> “Stars contain this.”

Silence.

Neko looked back at the report.

> **Neko:**  
> “That's not enough to conclude—”

> **Neri:**  
> “I know.”

She looked at Jake.

> **Neri:**  
> “But it's enough to check.”

---

# 4. THE STARDUST

Further analysis identified particles exhibiting characteristics consistent with what C.O.R.E. researchers informally described as **stardust**.

The term is not intended to imply literal magical dust.

The particles demonstrated:

- unusual elemental ratios;
    
- stellar nucleosynthesis signatures;
    
- isotope distributions inconsistent with normal terrestrial biology;
    
- and energy behavior resembling material previously observed in anomalous celestial phenomena.
    

Neri immediately requested permission to investigate astronomical records.

Director Lucious approved the request.

Neri then asked him for one additional thing.

> **Neri:**  
> “NASA.”

Lucious paused.

> **Lucious:**  
> “What do you need?”

> **Neri:**  
> “Two star maps.”

---

# 5. THE STAR MAPS

NASA provided historical astronomical datasets covering the relevant region of the night sky.

C.O.R.E. obtained two reference sets:

**MAP A:** Historical stellar positions, approximately **1950**.

**MAP B:** Current stellar observations.

Neri, Neko, Bao, Belle, Endmin, and Nora began comparing them.

The work took hours.

Then days.

The researchers initially found nothing.

Several stars had changed brightness.

Several observations contained positional discrepancies caused by measurement limitations.

Several catalog entries had been renamed or reclassified.

None matched the biological evidence.

Until Nora noticed one.

She placed her finger on the older map.

> **Nora:**  
> “That one.”

Neri looked.

> **Neri:**  
> “What about it?”

Nora pointed to the corresponding location on the modern map.

Nothing.

> **Nora:**  
> “Where'd it go?”

Neko checked the coordinates.

Then checked them again.

> **Neko:**  
> “That's... interesting.”

Belle leaned closer.

> **Belle:**  
> “Interesting how?”

Neko brought up the historical records.

The object was present in the 1950 dataset.

It had been observed.

Catalogued.

Recorded.

Then, at some point afterward—

It disappeared.

Not dimmed.

Not reclassified as another object.

Not obscured by known stellar phenomena.

**Gone.**

---

The team constructed a preliminary timeline.

### Later observations

### Earth-side historical record

His mother describes him as:

> “A child I found.”

Later:

> “Something I was given.”

No explanation exists for the statement.

---

# 7. DNA ANALYSIS

At this point, Jake voluntarily provided a DNA sample.

C.O.R.E. compared it against:

- human reference genomes;
    
- known Thiren genomes;
    
- known SAFEHOUSE residents;
    
- available Aperture personnel;
    
- Jake's mother's genetic records;
    
- archived anomalous biological samples;
    
- and known extraterrestrial biological sequences.
    

The result was unexpected.

Jake's DNA did **not** establish a biological relationship with his mother.

It did not establish a biological relationship with anyone else on Earth.

More importantly:

It did not behave entirely like conventional biological DNA.

Large sections possessed no meaningful terrestrial ancestry.

Some sequences appeared stable but functionally inexplicable.

Others demonstrated structural characteristics that researchers could not reconcile with conventional biological inheritance.

Neko stared at the results.

> **Neko:**  
> “So he's not related to his mother.”

Neri shook her head.

> **Neri:**  
> “No.”

> **Neko:**  
> “Not human?”

Neri looked at Jake.

> **Neri:**  
> “We don't know yet.”

That was when Guiding Light arrived.

---

# 8. GUIDING LIGHT

Guiding Light examined the samples.

She examined Jake.

She examined the astronomical records.

Then she examined the old star map.

She remained silent for an unusually long time.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “May I?”

Neri handed her the records.

Guiding Light compared the coordinates.

Then looked at Jake.

Then back at the coordinates.

Her expression changed.

Not dramatically.

But enough for everyone in the room to notice.

> **Neko:**  
> “You know something.”

Guiding Light did not immediately answer.

She reached toward Jake.

A small amount of azure light passed through him.

Jake's celestial particles responded.

They moved toward her hand.

For several seconds, they formed a tiny rotating pattern.

A star.

Guiding Light stared at it.

Then quietly said:

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Oh.”

Nora looked up.

> **Nora:**  
> “What?”

Guiding Light looked at Jake.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Jake is not human.”

Silence.

Jake blinked.

> **Jake:**  
> “I'm not?”

Guiding Light shook her head.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “No.”

She looked toward the astronomical map.

Then back at him.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “You are a star.”

Nobody spoke.

# 9. THE STELLAR THEORY

Guiding Light's explanation remains preliminary, but the evidence is increasingly consistent.

Jake appears to have originated as an actual celestial body.

Not metaphorically.

Not as a spirit associated with a star.

Not as a human possessing stellar energy.

**The original Jake was a star.**

At some unknown point in Earth's history, that stellar existence appears to have fallen from the sky.

Upon reaching Earth, it assumed a humanoid form.

Specifically:

**A child.**

The biological appearance was not inherited from human parents.

It was a manifestation.

A physical interpretation of a celestial existence.

The six-year-old boy known as Jake was therefore not born.

He **arrived.**

---

# 10. THE MISSING STAR

This provides a possible explanation for the astronomical anomaly.

The star recorded in historical observations eventually disappeared.

At approximately the same broad period, an unidentified child appeared on Earth with:

- no birth record;
    
- no biological parents;
    
- no ancestral DNA;
    
- no conventional origin;
    
- unusual celestial properties;
    
- and a body that does not conform completely to terrestrial biology.
    

The possibility that these are the same existence is now considered **highly significant.**

C.O.R.E. has designated the missing astronomical object:

> **J-ORIGIN**

Pending further confirmation.

# 12. THE TEDDY BEAR

One question remains particularly difficult.

Jake's heart.

The teddy bear contains it.

It beats.

It responds to his emotional state.

Its separation causes Jake's manifestation to fail.

If Jake originated as a star, the question becomes:

**What exactly is the heart?**

Guiding Light's answer was unexpectedly simple.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “A star needs something to hold its light.”

Neri looked at the teddy bear.

> **Neri:**  
> “His heart.”

Guiding Light nodded.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Perhaps what you call a heart is the part of Jake that remained closest to what he originally was.”

Further testing has been suspended.

The teddy bear is not a specimen.

It is Jake's heart.

That remains sufficient.

# FINAL ASSESSMENT

Jake was never born.

He was found.

For decades, that fact was treated as an unexplained anomaly.

There was no mother who gave birth to him.

No father.

No hospital.

No family tree.

No ancestry.

Only a small child who appeared somewhere on Earth and somehow became a person.

Now C.O.R.E. has a possible explanation.

He did not come from Earth.

He came from above it.

A star disappeared.

A child appeared.

And somewhere between those two events, something celestial became human enough to call someone:

> **“Mom.”**

Whether Jake remembers being a star is unknown.

Whether he ever truly was a star in the way humanity understands one is also unknown.

But Guiding Light's final statement has been entered into the record:

> **“He was never pretending to be a child.”**

> **“He became one.”**

Jake sat beneath the window afterward.

He watched the night sky.

His white hair caught the faint light.

Azure particles drifted quietly around him.

His teddy bear rested against his chest.

Its heart beat steadily.

Jake stared upward for a long time.

Then, very quietly:

> **Jake:**  
> “I wonder where I was.”

No one answered.

For once, nobody tried.

They simply sat with him.

And above the SAFEHOUSE, the stars continued shining.

One of them was missing.
