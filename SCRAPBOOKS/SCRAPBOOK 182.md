# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SEARCH OPERATION LOG**

## **SUBJECT: CONTACT ATTEMPT – CORIN LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The following report documents the closest Lumia ("Lumi") Lockwood came to re-establishing contact with Corin Lockwood since Corin's disappearance.

The encounter lasted less than a minute.

No physical contact was made.

Corin was lost from sight shortly afterward.

---

# **INITIAL CONTACT**

Heavy snowfall had reduced visibility across the northern woodland preserve.

Lumi continued searching despite recommendations to wait for improved weather.

At approximately 16:42 hours, she noticed movement between the trees.

A pale figure.

Chalk-lime hair.

A winter blanket wrapped tightly around her shoulders.

Corin.

She was standing only a short distance away.

For the first time in weeks...

Lumi had found her.

---

# **THE MOMENT**

Corin looked toward her.

Their eyes met.

Neither spoke.

Lumi smiled.

Not the relieved smile of someone who had solved a problem.

The trembling smile of a mother who had finally found her daughter.

She carefully took a single step forward.

Then another.

She kept her hands where Corin could see them.

She did not want to frighten her.

Softly, she spoke.

> "Corin..."

Corin didn't run.

She simply watched.

---

# **THE FALL**

As Lumi took another step, her boot caught beneath the snow-covered root of a fallen tree.

She stumbled.

Then fell into the deep snow.

It took only a second.

When she looked back up...

Corin had flinched.

Like a startled animal hearing a sudden noise.

Without a word...

She turned.

And disappeared into the white forest.

---

# **AFTERMATH**

Lumi stood immediately and called after her.

> "Corin!"

No answer.

Only the sound of wind moving through snow-covered branches.

Search teams arrived minutes later.

Fresh snowfall had already buried nearly every footprint.

No reliable trail could be established.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

Psychological Services noted that Corin's behavior during the encounter appeared driven by fear rather than hostility.

She did not threaten Lumi.

She did not shout.

She did not tell her to leave.

Instead, she reacted instinctively to the sudden movement and fled before she could process what had happened.

One clinician summarized the encounter as follows:

> "Corin no longer behaves like someone expecting to be rescued."

> "She behaves like someone expecting to be cornered."

---

# **LUMI**

That evening, Lumi returned alone to the place where she had fallen.

She looked at the disturbed snow.

Only a few steps.

That was all that had separated them.

She quietly whispered into the empty forest,

> "...I'm sorry."

She brushed the snow from her coat.

Not because she minded the cold.

But because she wished those few lost seconds had never happened.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin remains unlocated.

Medical Services believe the brief encounter demonstrates that Corin is still remaining within the Blacksite perimeter and is capable of recognizing familiar people, even if fear prevents her from approaching them.

Search efforts continue with revised guidance emphasizing slow, non-confrontational contact should Corin be sighted again.

**End of Search Operation Log**
