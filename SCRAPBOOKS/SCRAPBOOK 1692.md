# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake is currently asleep.

Following a recent illness, his body has entered an extended recovery period.

He is currently resting comfortably in his room.

His condition is stable.

Clover Luminare remained nearby as his assigned caretaker.

During this period, the **Obol Squad** visited Jake's room and held a quiet conversation with Clover.

Jake remained asleep throughout most of the discussion.

The conversation primarily concerned:

- Jake's current condition;
    
- Clover's caretaker responsibilities;
    
- the previous incident in which Clover accidentally left Jake outside;
    
- Jake's tendency to quietly endure discomfort;
    
- and the Obol Squad's willingness to help Clover prevent Jake from becoming isolated again.

Jake's current condition is stable.

His recent illness appears to have been sufficiently taxing that his body is prioritizing recovery.

The presence of trusted individuals appears to have had no negative effect on his rest.

Clover remains his primary caretaker.
