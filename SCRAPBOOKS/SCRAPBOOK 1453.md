A recreational excursion to **Backrooms Level 37**, commonly designated:

> **“THE POOLROOMS”**

was conducted by SAFEHOUSE residents under controlled L.S.I.N. supervision.

The purpose of the visit was entirely recreational.

No research objective was assigned.

No new areas were explored.

No anomalous specimens were collected.

> **POOL DAY.**

The excursion was therefore considered a complete success.

# 2. LEVEL 37 — THE POOLROOMS

Level 37 is characterized by an extensive network of interconnected indoor swimming facilities.

Typical environmental characteristics include:

- tiled floors;
    
- large swimming pools;
    
- shallow water areas;
    
- deep water areas;
    
- repeating architectural structures;
    
- fluorescent or diffuse artificial lighting;
    
- humid air;
    
- low ambient noise;
    
- and extensive corridors connecting individual pool areas.
    

The Level possesses architectural repetition sufficient to make conventional navigation difficult.

Aperture therefore maintains a controlled recreational section.

The area has been mapped.

Emergency exits are marked.

Communication systems remain operational.

Personnel are stationed nearby.

# 24. L.S.I.N. ASSESSMENT

The excursion demonstrates that controlled recreational access to certain Backrooms Levels is feasible.

Level 37 remains unsuitable for unsupervised civilian access.

However, the Aperture-managed recreational sector continues to demonstrate stable environmental conditions.

The existing system of:

> **Controlled Entry → Outpost Supervision → Designated Recreational Area → Controlled Exit**

is considered effective.

No changes to Level 37 recreational access are currently required.
