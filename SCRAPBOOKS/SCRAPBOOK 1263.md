**STATUS:** CONTAINED / UNDER OBSERVATION  
**SUBJECT:** Nora Tachyon  
**INCIDENT:** Accidental Creation of an Apparent Infinite Perpetual Motion Machine

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon has accidentally constructed what appears to be an **infinite perpetual motion system.**

She did not intend to create one.

She was not attempting to solve the problem of perpetual motion.

She was apparently trying to make a small rotating display for the Tachyon Merch Store.

The resulting device has continued operating without an observable external energy input.

C.O.R.E. has been unable to identify an energy source.

More concerningly:

> **The device does not appear to be losing energy.**

It has been operating continuously since activation.

---

# 1. ORIGINAL PURPOSE

Nora's stated objective was simple.

She wanted a small rotating display platform for merchandise.

The platform was intended to slowly rotate so customers could view an item without touching it.

The prototype was approximately the size of a dinner plate.

Nora assembled the device in the SAFEHOUSE workshop.

No unusual materials were initially involved.

No Tachyon weapon components were used.

No Banlands artifacts were used.

No Starlands components were used.

The project was classified as:

> **"A little spinny thing."**

---

# 2. INITIAL ACTIVATION

At approximately 14:22, Nora activated the prototype.

The platform began rotating.

Nothing appeared unusual.

After approximately thirty minutes, Neri noticed it was still moving.

After approximately two hours, Neko inspected it.

After approximately six hours, it was still rotating at approximately the same rate.

Neko disconnected the prototype from its external power source.

The platform continued rotating.

---

# 3. FIRST RESPONSE

Neko assumed there was residual electrical energy.

He checked:

- battery connections;
    
- capacitors;
    
- hidden power sources;
    
- electromagnetic interference;
    
- external induction;
    
- mechanical energy storage.
    

Nothing explained the continued motion.

The device was physically separated from all external equipment.

It continued rotating.

---

# 4. NORA'S RESPONSE

Nora was asked what she had done.

She looked at the device.

Then at Neko.

> **NORA:**  
> "I don't know."

Neko stared at her.

> **NEKO:**  
> "You built it."

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> "Yes."

> **NEKO:**  
> "You don't know how it works?"

Nora shook her head.

> **NORA:**  
> "I just made it."

Neko remained silent for approximately six seconds.

> **NEKO:**  
> "That's worse."

---

# 5. ATTEMPTED SHUTDOWN

The device was switched off.

The display stopped receiving commands.

The platform continued rotating.

The power cable was removed.

It continued.

The internal battery was removed.

It continued.

The entire device was placed inside an electrically isolated enclosure.

It continued.

The rotating assembly was physically separated from the control electronics.

It continued.

Neko eventually stopped touching it.

---

# 6. THE ENERGY PROBLEM

C.O.R.E. instrumentation detected no conventional energy source.

The system produces enough mechanical output to maintain its own motion.

However, measurements indicate something more unusual.

The device's apparent energy output does not measurably decrease over time.

The system behaves as though:

> **Energy enters the system from nowhere.**

This violates conventional thermodynamic expectations.

C.O.R.E. therefore does not classify the device as an ordinary machine.

It is currently classified as:

> **Anomalous perpetual-motion phenomenon.**

---

# 7. NERI'S INVOLVEMENT

Neri was eventually asked to inspect the device.

She stared at it for approximately three minutes.

She then requested a chemistry workstation.

Neko asked:

> **"You're going to figure it out?"**

Neri nodded.

Approximately twenty minutes later, she returned.

She pointed at one component.

Then wrote:

> **"This shouldn't work."**

Neko:

> **"I know."**

Neri pointed at another.

> **"This shouldn't work either."**

Neko:

> **"I know."**

Neri stared at the machine.

Then wrote:

> **"Why does Nora do this?"**

Neko had no answer.

---

# 8. THE DEVICE'S STRANGEST PROPERTY

The most significant discovery occurred when C.O.R.E. attempted to measure the system's total energy balance.

The result was inconsistent with a closed system.

The machine does not appear to be generating energy in the conventional sense.

Instead, it appears to be drawing upon a property that C.O.R.E. currently cannot quantify.

The device is effectively converting:

> **"continued existence"**

into mechanical motion.

The system does not seem to ask:

> "Where does the energy come from?"

It behaves as though the continued existence of the process itself is sufficient.

This remains entirely theoretical.

---

# 9. NORA'S EXPLANATION

Nora was asked how she designed the system.

She answered:

> **"I wanted it to keep spinning."**

The engineer asked:

> **"Forever?"**

Nora shrugged.

> **"I didn't want to keep turning it."**

The engineer stared at her.

> **"So you made a machine that turns itself forever?"**

Nora nodded.

> **"Apparently."**

---

---

# 12. CURRENT CONTAINMENT

The prototype has been moved to a controlled laboratory environment.

It is not connected to:

- the Blacksite power grid;
    
- the SAFEHOUSE network;
    
- external mechanical systems;
    
- Tachyon weapons;
    
- anomalous dimensional infrastructure.
    

Its only current function is:

> **Spinning.**

It has continued spinning.

---

# 13. CURRENT CONCERN

C.O.R.E. is not primarily concerned with the machine's energy output.

The greater concern is that Nora created it accidentally.

Nora is already known to possess exceptional aptitude in chemistry and anomalous research.

She has now demonstrated an apparent ability to produce an impossible engineering result without deliberately attempting to do so.

Neko has submitted a formal request:

> **"Please stop giving the child engineering problems."**

Nora has responded by asking:

> **"Why?"**

---

# FINAL ASSESSMENT

Nora did not solve perpetual motion.

She did not set out to overturn thermodynamics.

She did not discover an elegant grand unified theory.

She wanted a little platform that would keep spinning.

So she made one.

It keeps spinning.

It has no apparent fuel.

No apparent power source.

No measurable energy loss.

And no known method of explaining why it works.

C.O.R.E. has therefore designated the device:

> **NORA-PPM-01**

Unofficial SAFEHOUSE designation:

> **"The Spinny Thing."**
