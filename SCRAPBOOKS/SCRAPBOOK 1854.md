# THE SHEATH THAT BITES BACK

## Incident Report: Aries and the N.O.S.T. Office Breach

---

### 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The N.O.S.T. Office experienced a hostile incursion involving a horde of Banlands demons. The entities breached the facility's perimeter and entered occupied operational space, creating an immediate threat to personnel and the integrity of the office.

Aries engaged the invading entities using a specialized weapon previously manifested for him by Jake Akana and Tom Akana through Genesis Construction.

The weapon combines a conventional katana with an integrated .410 Bore pump-action shotgun mechanism housed within its sheath. It has a reported five-round capacity, supports multiple compatible ammunition types, and incorporates both impact-triggered and conventional firing modes. Its safety mechanism is located inside the sheath and is intended to prevent discharge while the katana remains sheathed.

The engagement revealed an additional and particularly significant property.

**The weapon possesses evisceration properties that allow it to inflict harm upon Banlands entities.**

This capability is noteworthy because the targets were not simply ordinary physical attackers. Their origin as Banlands entities presented a compatibility problem that conventional weapons might not necessarily resolve.

The hybrid weapon proved capable of affecting them.

Aries used it throughout the engagement, combining its bladed and firearm capabilities against the advancing horde. The resulting damage was sufficient to contribute materially to the suppression of the incursion.

The weapon's performance has elevated its status from an unusual Genesis-constructed artifact to a potentially important tool for defending against Banlands-origin threats.

The incident also raises an uncomfortable question for C.O.R.E.:

Jake and Tom created a weapon for Aries without a confirmed explanation for why they chose that particular design.

Now that weapon has demonstrated a capability against entities that breached an interdimensional office.

Whether the boys anticipated this application remains unknown.

---

### 2. INCIDENT CHRONOLOGY

**T+00:00 — INITIAL BREACH**

N.O.S.T. Office security registered a hostile incursion by a group of Banlands demons.

The invading entities breached the office's perimeter and entered the facility. The number of entities, precise breach point, and initial damage to the building remain subject to N.O.S.T.'s final accounting.

Personnel were confronted with a rapidly developing threat inside an occupied workplace.

The incident was no longer a perimeter-security concern. The threat had entered the facility.

**T+00 — ARIES ENGAGES**

Aries engaged the invading horde with the Genesis-constructed katana–shotgun hybrid.

The weapon's unusual construction initially distinguished it from conventional equipment carried by responding personnel. Its actual usefulness, however, was not merely visual.

Aries was able to employ its two principal capabilities during the engagement.

The katana provided a close-combat option, while the integrated shotgun offered a separate means of attacking the hostile entities.

The combination allowed Aries to respond without relying exclusively on one form of engagement.

**T+00 — EFFICACY AGAINST BANLANDS ENTITIES CONFIRMED**

During the confrontation, the weapon inflicted damage upon the invading demons.

The observed effect was consistent with the weapon's evisceration properties, which allowed it to harm Banlands entities rather than simply passing through them or failing to affect them.

This was the most important technical observation of the incident.

The weapon was not merely effective against an ordinary physical target. It was capable of interacting harmfully with the specific entities involved in the breach.

C.O.R.E. records this as a confirmed operational capability against the Banlands demons encountered during this incident.

The exact metaphysical mechanism responsible for this effect remains unknown.

**T+00 — INCURSION SUPPRESSED**

Aries continued his engagement until the immediate hostile threat was suppressed.

The weapon's combination of melee and firearm capabilities proved useful during the confrontation, although C.O.R.E. cannot attribute the entire outcome to the weapon alone without a complete reconstruction of the incident.

N.O.S.T. personnel must still establish the final number of hostile entities, any surviving threats, and the full extent of damage to the facility.

**T+POST-INCIDENT — ARTIFACT FLAGGED FOR REVIEW**

Following the engagement, the weapon was flagged for further assessment.

Its ability to harm Banlands entities significantly expands the practical importance of the original manifestation.

The previous evaluation focused on its unconventional construction, firearm safety, and combat versatility.

The new evaluation must also account for its demonstrated interaction with Banlands-origin targets.

---

### 3. THE WEAPON: POST-ENGAGEMENT ASSESSMENT

#### 3.1 — Hybrid Configuration

The weapon retains its katana blade and sheath-integrated .410 Bore pump-action shotgun.

Its reported five-round capacity and dual firing behavior remain part of its documented design.

During the N.O.S.T. incident, these features provided Aries with two distinct combat options within one integrated weapon.

The engagement demonstrated the practical value of that combination under hostile conditions.

However, the incident does not establish that the weapon is universally effective against all Banlands entities or every other anomalous target.

Its confirmed effectiveness is limited to the observed encounter until further evidence becomes available.

#### 3.2 — Evisceration Properties

The weapon's evisceration properties are the defining feature of this report.

For the purposes of this assessment, the term refers to the weapon's demonstrated ability to inflict meaningful harm upon Banlands entities.

C.O.R.E. does not currently know whether this property originates from the katana, the firearm mechanism, the weapon's manifested composition, or Genesis Construction as a whole.

It is also unknown whether both offensive components possess identical evisceration effects.

The incident establishes the result, not the underlying mechanism.

This distinction must be preserved in all future technical documentation.

#### 3.3 — Implications for Banlands Threat Response

A weapon capable of harming Banlands entities has potential value beyond its original recipient.

If its evisceration properties are reliable, it could provide a means of responding to hostile entities that might otherwise resist conventional defensive measures.

Nevertheless, C.O.R.E. does not recommend immediate mass production or distribution.

The weapon's performance requires additional documentation, and its anomalous properties must be understood as far as reasonably possible before broader operational adoption.

Genesis Construction is not a substitute for a controlled evaluation process.

### 4. AFTER-ACTION INTERVIEW — ARIES

**Interview Status:** Preliminary  
**Subject:** Aries  
**Interviewer:** C.O.R.E. Security Review  
**Location:** N.O.S.T. Office, Post-Incident Review Area

**C.O.R.E.:** "A more technical one would be appreciated."

**Aries:** "And I'm glad I had it."

_End of preliminary interview._

**Interviewer’s note:** Aries expressed clear satisfaction with the weapon's combat performance. His assessment emphasized its practical utility and its ability to affect the invading entities. No claim regarding the creators' original intentions could be established.

### 5. C.O.R.E. TECHNICAL REVIEW

The N.O.S.T. incident has established three important facts.

**First:** Genesis Construction produced a weapon with multiple integrated offensive capabilities.

**Second:** The weapon can inflict harm upon the Banlands demons encountered during the breach.

**Third:** Aries was able to employ the weapon during an actual hostile incursion and considered it effective.

Together, these observations justify further investigation.

They do not establish the precise nature of the weapon's evisceration properties, the limits of its effectiveness, or the full extent of Genesis Construction's influence over its operation.

C.O.R.E. must not confuse demonstrated success with complete understanding.

The weapon may be an exceptional defensive asset, but it remains an anomalous artifact whose internal behavior is not fully characterized.

**Recommended actions:**

1. Obtain N.O.S.T.'s complete incident reconstruction and correlate it with Aries's account.
    
2. Document the weapon's observed effects on the specific Banlands entities involved.
    
3. Preserve the distinction between confirmed capabilities and untested assumptions.
    
4. Assess safe handling and storage requirements without compromising the weapon's intended function.
    
5. Consult Jake and Tom regarding their design intentions through an ordinary, non-coercive conversation.
    
6. Do not compel either boy to reproduce the weapon or create additional weapons for testing.
    

The final directive is especially important.

Jake and Tom's ability to manifest objects through Genesis Construction does not make them an on-demand weapons-development department.

They are children who created something for someone they care about. Their extraordinary abilities do not remove their right to be treated accordingly.

---

### 6. SECURITY IMPLICATIONS

The N.O.S.T. breach demonstrates the importance of maintaining defensive options against interdimensional threats.

A conventional security response may be insufficient when an attacker possesses properties that ordinary equipment cannot adequately address.

The katana–shotgun hybrid provides evidence that a Genesis-constructed object can harm the Banlands entities encountered in this incident.

This finding should be shared with relevant security personnel under appropriate access restrictions.

It should not, however, be interpreted as permission to rely exclusively on this weapon in future Banlands incidents. No single artifact should become the sole basis of a defensive strategy, particularly when its operational limits remain unknown.

C.O.R.E. recommends treating the weapon as a promising specialized asset rather than a universal solution.

The N.O.S.T. Office must also review how the breach occurred. An effective weapon can help suppress an incursion, but it cannot replace perimeter security, early detection, personnel protection, and a response plan.

Aries's success should not obscure the fact that the demons reached the inside of the facility.

---

### 7. FINAL DETERMINATION

**Incident outcome:** Hostile incursion suppressed.

**Weapon performance:** Effective during the observed engagement.

**Evisceration capability:** Confirmed against the Banlands entities encountered.

**Underlying mechanism:** Unknown.

**Creator intent:** Unknown.

**Operational recommendation:** Retain the artifact's record as a specialized Genesis-constructed weapon and continue controlled evaluation.

The weapon has proven itself in a situation substantially more dangerous than its original design review anticipated.

Aries is pleased with its performance.

C.O.R.E. considers the weapon worthy of serious attention.

And the N.O.S.T. incident has established that Jake and Tom's creation is more than an eccentric combination of a sword and a shotgun.

It can harm the things that came through the breach.

That matters.

---

### C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The original documentation asked why Jake and Tom had made a shotgun that lived inside a katana's sheath.

At the time, it seemed like an unusual invention with an uncertain purpose.

Now Aries has used it to defend the N.O.S.T. Office against a horde of Banlands demons, and the weapon has demonstrated an ability to hurt entities that ordinary assumptions about weapon effectiveness cannot adequately describe.

We still do not know what the boys were thinking when they made it.

Perhaps they understood more about the weapon than we realized. Perhaps they simply imagined something useful and Genesis Construction made it possible. Perhaps the answer is less complicated than our reports make it sound.

We should ask them.

But we should also remember what the weapon represents to its intended user.

Jake and Tom made something for Aries.

Aries carried it when danger arrived.

And when the demons breached the office, he had a way to fight back.

**A weapon's significance is not measured only by what it can destroy. Sometimes, it is measured by the people who are still standing when the danger passes.**

C.O.R.E. recommends that the N.O.S.T. Office remember both the breach and the person who stood against it.`
