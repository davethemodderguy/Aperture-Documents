# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Seven days have elapsed since Jake disappeared from the medical ward.

There has been no confirmed sighting.

No communication.

No recovered biological material.

No detectable cardiac signal from his teddy bear.

No confirmed dimensional signature.

No evidence establishing whether he remains alive.

Operation STILL HERE remains active.

Despite extensive searches, C.O.R.E. has not identified a destination, mechanism, or responsible entity.

The initial medical crisis has become an unresolved existential incident.

However, the investigation is no longer the only concern.

**The SAFEHOUSE is beginning to adapt to Jake's absence.**

His place at the cafeteria table remains empty.

His room remains untouched.

Clover continues to occupy their shared room, although she has not reclaimed his side.

Aeterna has spent most of the past week participating in the search.

Bao continues requesting updates from Medical and Anomalous Research.

Neri has become quieter.

The other residents have resumed portions of their daily routines, but ordinary activities now occur around a conspicuous absence.

The most concerning development is not that the residents have stopped looking for Jake.

It is that, after seven days without finding him, they are beginning to understand that they may have to continue living without knowing where he went.

**No one has been authorized to declare Jake dead.**

**No one has been able to prove that he is alive.**

# 1. OPERATION STILL HERE — DAY SEVEN

The search began immediately after Jake's disappearance.

It has continued without interruption.

### Search results

|Investigation|Current result|
|---|---|
|SAFEHOUSE interior search|No trace of Jake|
|Perimeter and access records|No unauthorized departure identified|
|Medical ward recordings|No visible departure|
|Dimensional anomaly monitoring|No confirmed destination|
|Starlands inquiries|No verified response|
|Teddy bear cardiac-signal search|No signal detected|
|External anomalous intelligence|No confirmed information|
|Cause of disappearance|Unknown|

The absence of a signal from the teddy bear remains particularly troubling.

Before his disappearance, the bear's heartbeat had become exceptionally weak.

The last recorded signal ended at the same moment Jake vanished.

No subsequent reading has been obtained.

Researchers cannot establish whether the signal ended because Jake's cardiac activity ceased, because the bear left the detectable region of reality, or because the connection between the monitoring system and its source was interrupted by an unknown phenomenon.

All three possibilities remain open.

So do explanations C.O.R.E. has not yet considered.

### Search authorization

Operation STILL HERE will continue.

However, personnel have been instructed not to interpret the duration of the search as evidence for or against survival.

Seven days without a signal is significant.

It is not conclusive.

# 2. THE MEDICAL WARD

The bed has been cleaned.

The monitoring equipment has been reassigned.

The room has returned to its ordinary operational configuration.

Except for one detail.

The space where Jake's bed stood has been left empty.

The Medical Division initially intended to return the room to normal after the investigation secured all relevant evidence.

Aeterna objected.

**AETERNA:**  
"Why?"

**MEDICAL OFFICER:**  
"The room is no longer required for the investigation."

**AETERNA:**  
"That wasn't my question."

The officer paused.

Aeterna looked toward the empty space.

**AETERNA:**  
"Why are you trying to make it look as though nothing happened?"

The room remained silent.

The bed had not been preserved as a shrine.

No one was permitted to interfere with medical operations for symbolic reasons.

But the empty space was retained temporarily as part of the incident review.

The decision was practical.

At least, that was how it appeared in the administrative record.

The medical staff who had attended Jake's final hours have been offered confidential welfare consultations.

Several have accepted.

One has repeatedly reviewed the final seventeen seconds of footage.

The footage shows Jake present.

Then absent.

There is no intermediate frame that explains the event.

The staff member eventually stopped watching.

They have not requested the footage again.

# 3. AETERNA — SEARCH PARTICIPATION

Aeterna has not returned to her previous routine.

She has participated directly in Operation STILL HERE, examining known anomalous pathways and consulting entities capable of detecting changes in existential states.

Her own abilities have not produced a confirmed lead.

This has been difficult for her to accept.

Aeterna is accustomed to confronting problems that can be located, approached, and acted upon.

Jake's disappearance offers no such target.

There is nothing to strike.

No enemy to pursue.

No doorway to force open.

No identifiable mechanism to destroy.

Only an absence.

### Recorded exchange — Day 4

**C.O.R.E. RESEARCHER:**  
"We have no evidence that he is still in this reality."

**AETERNA:**  
"You have no evidence that he isn't."

**RESEARCHER:**  
"That is correct."

**AETERNA:**  
"Then keep looking."

### Recorded exchange — Day 6

**C.O.R.E. RESEARCHER:**  
"You should consider resting."

**AETERNA:**  
"I don't need to sleep."

**RESEARCHER:**  
"That isn't what I meant."

Aeterna looked toward the search display.

**AETERNA:**  
"I know."

She continued working.

### Private observation

On the seventh day, Aeterna returned to the medical ward alone.

She stood beside the empty space where Jake had disappeared.

For several minutes, she did not move.

Then she spoke.

**AETERNA:**  
"I still don't know where you are."

Pause.

**AETERNA:**  
"I'm still looking."

She waited.

There was no response.

She remained for another minute before returning to the search.

# 4. CLOVER — DAY SEVEN

Clover has not moved Jake's belongings.

His books remain on their shelf.

His drawing materials remain in their usual place.

His side of the room remains intact.

The wilted two-dimensional flowers are still where they were last observed.

Clover has not attempted to restore them.

She does not know whether their condition is related to Jake's disappearance, and she has no intention of experimenting with them without a defensible reason.

She continues to maintain the room.

She cleans her own side.

She prepares food.

She organizes her books.

She tends to the clovers growing near her desk.

Jake's side remains untouched except where necessary to prevent damage.

### Day 2

Clover left a glass of water on Jake's bedside table.

She removed it several hours later.

### Day 3

She placed a small plate of cake beside his books.

She removed it before it spoiled.

### Day 4

She sat on his side of the bed for the first time since the disappearance.

She remained there for approximately forty minutes.

### Day 5

She stopped leaving food.

### Day 6

She began speaking to the empty room.

Not continuously.

Not as though she expected an answer.

Occasionally, she would mention something that happened during the day.

A resident had broken a cup.

Someone had overcooked dinner.

The clovers near her desk had grown unevenly.

Things she might once have told Jake while he read beside her.

### Day 7

A Welfare Officer asked whether she wanted to move to another room temporarily.

**CLOVER:**  
"No."

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"You can keep his belongings here even if you move."

**CLOVER:**  
"I know."

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"Then why stay?"

Clover looked toward Jake's side.

**CLOVER:**  
"Because I live here."

The officer waited.

**CLOVER:**  
"And so does he."

A pause.

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"We don't know whether he can return."

**CLOVER:**  
"I know."

She looked down at her hands.

**CLOVER:**  
"I'm not pretending I know."

Another pause.

**CLOVER:**  
"I'm just not going to pretend he never lived here."

The officer did not press the issue.

Clover was not refusing to acknowledge the possibility of loss.

She was refusing to treat uncertainty as permission to erase someone.

# 5. BAO — THE QUESTIONS THAT REMAIN

Bao has continued cooperating with the medical investigation.

She has reviewed the available reports, answered questions concerning Jake's previous condition, and provided information about his behavior before the disappearance.

She has also asked the same three questions on multiple occasions.

1. Was Jake conscious when the final signal ended?
    
2. Was there any evidence of pain immediately before the event?
    
3. Is there any way to determine whether his cardiac activity continues elsewhere?
    

The first question remains unresolved.

The second has no definitive answer.

The third is the subject of ongoing research.

Bao has not accepted the lack of answers as satisfactory.

She has, however, stopped asking the medical team to provide certainty where none exists.

Instead, she requests updates whenever new evidence becomes available.

### Recorded exchange — Day 7

**BAO:**  
"Anything?"

**C.O.R.E. RESEARCHER:**  
"No confirmed findings."

Bao nodded.

**BAO:**  
"All right."

She remained in the doorway.

**C.O.R.E. RESEARCHER:**  
"Bao?"

**BAO:**  
"Hm?"

**C.O.R.E. RESEARCHER:**  
"You don't have to come here every day."

Bao looked back toward the empty bed.

**BAO:**  
"I know."

**C.O.R.E. RESEARCHER:**  
"Then why do you?"

Bao considered the question.

**BAO:**  
"Because yesterday there was nothing."

Pause.

**BAO:**  
"And today there is nothing."

She looked toward the researcher.

**BAO:**  
"I don't want to miss the day that changes."

She left shortly afterward.

# 6. NERI — ABSENCE AND RESPONSIBILITY

Neri has not made a public statement regarding Jake's disappearance.

She continues attending to her own needs and medical appointments.

Her daily routine remains limited.

She spends much of her time reading, drinking coffee, or sitting in quiet areas.

However, she has begun avoiding the medical ward.

On Day 2, she entered and stood beside the empty bed.

On Day 3, she reached the doorway but did not enter.

On Day 4, she walked past without stopping.

On Day 5, she asked Bao whether there had been any updates.

On Day 6, she did not ask.

On Day 7, she returned.

She stood in the doorway for approximately thirty seconds.

Then she looked toward the empty space.

**NERI:**  
"...Sorry."

No one else was in the room.

She did not elaborate.

C.O.R.E. cannot establish precisely what she intended by the statement.

It may have been an expression of guilt, grief, regret, or several emotions at once.

No evidence indicates that Neri caused Jake's disappearance.

The original incident review found no indication that she deliberately abandoned him during the earlier snowstorm. She had believed he was returning indoors.

Nevertheless, Jake's experience of that event may have remained part of his deteriorating sense of safety.

Neri is aware of this.

She has not attempted to make contact with an absent person.

She has not asked anyone to assure her that Jake forgives her.

She has not demanded that the investigation absolve her.

She simply left the room after a little while.

The Welfare Division recommends that personnel continue distinguishing between **responsibility for a specific mistake** and **responsibility for an unexplained disappearance**.

They are not the same thing.

# 7. THE OTHER RESIDENTS

The SAFEHOUSE has not stopped functioning.

Meals are prepared.

Medical appointments continue.

Security shifts change.

Residents attend to their responsibilities.

People still laugh occasionally.

Someone still complains about the cafeteria.

Alice still rides her scooter through the corridors when permitted.

The ordinary sounds of the SAFEHOUSE have returned.

But several small habits have changed.

### The cafeteria

No one has formally reserved Jake's usual seat.

Nevertheless, residents rarely choose it.

When someone does, they tend to move after noticing where they are sitting.

There is no written policy.

No one has discussed the behavior.

It simply happens.

### The library

Jake's books have not been removed from the shelves.

A few residents have noticed that they no longer see him reading in the quiet corners.

They have stopped expecting him to appear around every turn.

They have not stopped looking when they hear footsteps behind them.

### The corridors

People occasionally glance toward the entrance when a door opens.

For the first few days, several residents turned whenever they heard someone approaching with a small, dragging sound.

They were accustomed to hearing Jake's movements.

By the end of the week, the reactions had become less frequent.

Not because the residents had forgotten him.

Because no one can remain startled every time an absent person fails to appear.

### The medical ward

The ward has resumed its normal schedule.

The clinicians continue monitoring Jake's case remotely where possible.

The lack of a patient has not resulted in the closure of the file.

# 8. THE ROOM THAT STILL BELONGS TO HIM

On the seventh evening, Clover sat at her desk.

The room was quiet.

Her own belongings occupied one side.

Jake's belongings occupied the other.

Nothing had changed enough to make the space feel unfamiliar.

That was precisely what made it difficult.

Clover looked toward the bed.

She remembered the way Jake used to sit with his teddy bear while reading.

She remembered the small drawings.

The flowers.

The way he could turn a handful of sand into clear glass without heating it.

The way he had once looked at his reflection after his eyes changed and quietly said:

> "Still me."

She remembered the days before his fear became so severe that he could barely accept help.

She remembered telling him that his room would still be there.

She had meant it.

She still meant it.

Clover lowered her head.

**CLOVER:**  
"Seven days."

The room did not answer.

She looked toward the empty side again.

**CLOVER:**  
"I don't know if you can hear me."

Pause.

**CLOVER:**  
"I don't know if you're anywhere that has rooms."

She gave a small, strained laugh.

**CLOVER:**  
"That's a stupid thing to say to a star."

Her expression settled.

**CLOVER:**  
"But if you can hear me..."

She stopped.

There was no point in asking him to come back.

Not when she did not know whether he could.

Not when she could not know whether he was frightened, unconscious, or beyond the reach of her voice.

Instead, she finished with the only promise she could actually keep.

**CLOVER:**  
"I haven't moved your things."

She switched off her desk lamp.

Jake's side remained exactly as it was.

# 9. SCIENTIFIC ASSESSMENT — THE ABSENCE OF A SIGNAL

C.O.R.E. has reviewed whether the lack of a detectable cardiac signal can be interpreted as evidence of death.

The conclusion remains unchanged.

It cannot.

Jake's cardiac vessel was already displaying anomalous behavior before the disappearance. The final signal was exceptionally weak and ended simultaneously with the loss of his physical presence.

Existing monitoring systems were not designed to determine the status of a literal star whose physical manifestation may be sustained through unknown anomalous mechanisms.

The disappearance therefore exceeds the diagnostic capability of the current equipment.

Researchers have proposed additional methods of investigation, but none has yet produced a validated procedure capable of confirming Jake's status.

No destructive test will be conducted on the last recorded data or on unrelated anomalous objects in an attempt to reproduce the event.

The disappearance must be investigated on its own evidence.

### Current scientific position

C.O.R.E. cannot confirm:

- that Jake died;
    
- that Jake survived;
    
- that Jake returned to the Starlands;
    
- that Jake remains within this reality;
    
- that his manifestation was destroyed;
    
- or that his disappearance was intentional.
    

The investigation remains open precisely because none of these conclusions has been established.

# 10. WELFARE DIRECTIVE — DAY SEVEN

The Resident Welfare Division has issued updated guidance.

Personnel must not insist that the SAFEHOUSE residents remain visibly distressed to demonstrate that they remember Jake.

They must not interpret a return to routine as indifference.

They must not tell Clover to move on.

They must not tell Aeterna to accept that Jake is dead.

They must not tell Bao that worrying will not help.

They must not tell Neri that guilt serves no purpose.

These statements may sound practical.

They do not answer the questions the residents are actually facing.

Instead, personnel should:

- provide accurate updates when available;
    
- allow residents to participate in the search within reasonable safety limits;
    
- offer private support without forcing discussion;
    
- preserve Jake's belongings while the investigation remains active;
    
- maintain his room without turning it into a memorial against Clover's wishes;
    
- and recognize that uncertainty can be exhausting in ways that a confirmed answer, however painful, is not.
    

The objective is not to prevent grief.

It is to prevent uncertainty from becoming an additional source of harm.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Seven days ago, Jake disappeared from a bed in which he had been lying too weak to do much more than hold his teddy bear.

The medical monitor went silent.

The room became empty.

And everyone who had been trying to keep him alive was left staring at a space where a little boy had been.

For seven days, the SAFEHOUSE has looked for him.

For seven days, Aeterna has searched for a place she can reach.

For seven days, Bao has waited for a medical finding that might explain what happened.

For seven days, Neri has carried a question she cannot ask the person who might have answered it.

For seven days, Clover has kept Jake's side of their room untouched.

And for seven days, no one has heard the heartbeat of his teddy bear.

The difficult part is no longer simply the disappearance.

It is the absence of anything that can be done with certainty.

They cannot rescue him from a location they cannot find.

They cannot treat an illness they cannot examine.

They cannot comfort a frightened child whose whereabouts are unknown.

They cannot say goodbye to someone whose death has not been established.

So the SAFEHOUSE continues.

People eat.

People work.

People sleep.

Doors open and close.

The corridors grow quiet at night.

And somewhere in the middle of an ordinary day, someone still catches themselves looking toward a familiar seat, expecting white hair and blue eyes to appear above the edge of a book.

Then they remember.

Seven days.

No signal.

No answer.

No Jake.

C.O.R.E. will not write that he is waiting somewhere.

It will not write that he is gone forever.

Both would be inventions.

The only honest statement is the one that remains at the top of the file.

**JAKE IS MISSING.**

**HIS FATE IS UNKNOWN.**

**THE SEARCH CONTINUES.**

And in a room that still belongs to him, a book waits on the bedside table.

Not as proof that he will return.

Not as a promise that he can.

Simply because it was his book.

And nobody has found a reason to move it.

— **C.O.R.E. Anomalous Research & Resident Welfare Division**
