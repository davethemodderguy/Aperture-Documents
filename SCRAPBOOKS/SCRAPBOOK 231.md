# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CRITICAL RECOVERY REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: RECOVERY OF COREY LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – Executive & Medical Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

At 08:17 on ██/██/20██, Search Team Seven located Corey Lockwood approximately 12.6 kilometers from Project SAFEHOUSE.

She was discovered unconscious beneath a heavy accumulation of snow, partially sheltered by a fallen pine tree.

Initial examination confirmed she was alive.

However...

Her vital signs were critically weak.

She was immediately transported to Heaven Blacksite Medical Wing.

---

# **RECOVERY**

The discovery occurred after the white owl repeatedly circled the same section of forest despite previous searches yielding no results.

The behavior prompted Search Team Seven to investigate the area again.

Personnel uncovered Corey beneath nearly two meters of compacted snow.

She was unresponsive.

No obvious traumatic injuries were identified.

Core body temperature was dangerously low.

Despite aggressive emergency treatment, she did not regain consciousness.

---

# **MEDICAL STATUS**

Current condition:

- Unconscious.
    
- Stable airway.
    
- Assisted warming procedures ongoing.
    
- Severe hypothermia successfully reversed.
    
- No significant external injuries.
    
- Neurological activity present.
    
- Cardiac activity weak but regular.
    

Medical personnel have been unable to determine why Corey has not awakened.

---

# **THE PARADOX**

Research Division anticipated that recovering Corey would immediately stabilize Corin.

This prediction proved incorrect.

Corin's condition has continued to deteriorate.

Although the sisters are once again physically near one another, the paradox has not recovered.

Current theory suggests that simple proximity is insufficient.

The connection itself remains critically unstable.

---

# **CORIN LOCKWOOD**

Corin has remained almost entirely unchanged since Corey's recovery.

Observed symptoms include:

- Persistent emotional withdrawal.
    
- Continued physiological instability.
    
- Progressive reduction in measurable anomalous activity.
    
- Declining life signs despite no identifiable disease or injury.
    

Medical monitors have documented a slow but continuous decrease in Corin's biological activity.

Every treatment administered has addressed symptoms.

None have altered the underlying decline.

---

# **JOINT OBSERVATION**

At Medical Services' request, Corey's bed was placed beside Corin's.

Corin has remained seated beside Corey for nearly the entire duration of hospitalization.

She rarely speaks.

She rarely sleeps.

She simply watches the cardiac monitor beside Corey's bed.

When staff suggested she should rest, Corin quietly answered:

> "...She waited for me."

A pause.

> "...Now it's my turn."

No further conversation followed.

---

# **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

**Lumi**

Lumi remained with both daughters throughout the night.

After confirming that Corey had been found alive, she cried with visible relief.

The relief quickly gave way to renewed concern as Corin's condition failed to improve.

Medical staff recorded her whispering:

> "...Please..."

> "...Not again."

---

**Star**

Star smiled for the first time since the search began.

The smile faded as she looked toward Corin's monitor.

> "...Why isn't she getting better...?"

No one in the room could answer.

---

**Nightshade**

Nightshade remained stationed outside the intensive care room.

She declined relief from duty.

---

**Neko**

Neko reviewed every medical scan personally.

After several hours, he quietly closed the final report.

> "...We reunited them."

He looked toward Corin.

> "...So why is the paradox still breaking?"

---

**Miss Neko**

She immediately ordered continuous monitoring of both sisters.

Her written instruction contained only one sentence.

> **"Treat them as one patient occupying two beds."**

---

**Taiga**

Taiga refused to leave the room until Corin gently asked her to get some rest.

She returned less than an hour later.

---

**Belle**

Fresh cookies were quietly placed on the bedside table.

Neither sister was awake to eat them.

Belle left them anyway.

---

# **RESEARCH ASSESSMENT**

The recovery of Corey Lockwood confirms that physical separation alone does not fully explain the Corin Paradox.

Research now believes the paradox possesses a secondary stabilization mechanism that remains inactive.

Until that mechanism is restored, Corin's condition is expected to continue declining.

No existing treatment has successfully halted the progression.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corey Lockwood has been recovered alive but remains unconscious.

Corin Lockwood remains awake.

Her life signs continue to decline slowly despite every available medical intervention.

For the first time since Corey's recovery, Medical Services have issued a statement no physician wished to write.

> **"Recovery of one manifestation does not guarantee recovery of the other."**

The search has ended.

The emergency has not.

**Case Status:** **CRITICAL – BOTH PATIENTS UNDER CONTINUOUS OBSERVATION**

**End of Critical Recovery Report**
