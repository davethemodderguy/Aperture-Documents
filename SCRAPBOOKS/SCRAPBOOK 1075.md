# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### WELLNESS & RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-NERI-180  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESIDENTIAL / MINOR ACCIDENT  
**STATUS:** CLOSED — NO INJURY  
**SUBJECT:** Plush Neri — Accidental Fall and Contact With Jane Doe  
**LOCATION:** Sector 4-C Cafeteria  
**INVOLVED PERSONNEL:** Neri, Alice, Jane Doe

---

# Executive Summary

Plush Neri was once again inadvertently forgotten in the cafeteria by Alice.

Neri had been placed on a cafeteria table and subsequently left unattended.

At some point afterward, Neri fell from the table onto the floor.

While walking through the cafeteria, Jane Doe accidentally stepped on Neri's chest without seeing her.

Fortunately, Jane's step was not forceful.

Neri sustained **no injury** and remained in stable condition.

The incident nevertheless reinforces the previously established concern regarding Plush Neri being left in locations with ordinary foot traffic.

---

# 1. Initial Circumstances

Alice had been carrying Plush Neri through the cafeteria.

At approximately [REDACTED], Alice placed Neri on a cafeteria table.

Alice subsequently became distracted and left the area.

As has occurred previously, Alice did not remember that Neri was still there.

Neri remained on the table.

She did not attempt to attract attention.

She eventually moved toward the edge.

At some point, she fell.

**Impact:** Low.

**Observed injury:** None.

Neri remained on the cafeteria floor.

---

# 2. Jane Doe's Arrival

Jane entered the cafeteria approximately [REDACTED] later.

She was not aware that Neri was on the floor.

The cafeteria was operating normally.

Jane walked across the room.

Her foot landed directly on Neri's chest.

The contact lasted less than a second.

Jane immediately felt something soft beneath her foot and stopped.

She looked down.

There was a small, flattened Plush Neri staring back at her.

Jane froze.

Neri stared.

Jane stared.

Several seconds passed.

Jane eventually said:

> "Neri?"

Neri nodded.

---

# 3. Immediate Assessment

Jane immediately picked Neri up and examined her.

Neri showed:

- no visible damage;
    
- no abnormal movement;
    
- no distress;
    
- no apparent breathing difficulty;
    
- no signs of significant pain.
    

She remained conscious throughout.

Jane asked:

> "Did I hurt you?"

Neri shook her head.

Jane:

> "You're sure?"

Neri nodded.

Jane looked at the floor.

Then at Neri.

Then back at the floor.

> "...I really need to watch where I'm walking."

Neri did not respond.

---

# 4. Medical Evaluation

C.O.R.E. Wellness personnel performed a precautionary examination.

No injury was identified.

The force generated by Jane's accidental step was insufficient to cause damage to Neri's plush body.

The established impact-resistant properties of Plush Neri's current form likely contributed to the absence of injury.

No treatment was required.

Neri returned to normal activity immediately afterward.

---

# 5. Neri's Response

Neri displayed remarkably little concern.

She did not become frightened.

She did not become angry.

She did not blame Jane.

She did not appear particularly upset with Alice for leaving her behind.

After the examination, Neri simply settled into Jane's hands.

Jane asked:

> "Do you want me to put you somewhere safe?"

Neri nodded.

Jane carried her away from the main walking area.

Neri subsequently sat on a nearby couch.

Approximately ten minutes later, she fell asleep.

---

# 6. Alice's Discovery

Alice eventually returned to the cafeteria.

She looked around.

Then noticed Jane.

Then noticed Neri.

Her expression changed immediately.

> "I forgot her again."

Jane replied:

> "Yes."

Alice looked guilty.

Neri looked at Alice.

There was no hostility.

No anger.

No visible disappointment.

Neri simply raised one plush hand.

Alice approached.

Neri allowed herself to be picked up.

---

# 7. Current Relationship Context

This incident is notable because Neri's reaction is substantially different from earlier incidents.

Previously, being forgotten repeatedly reinforced feelings of abandonment and worthlessness.

That pattern is no longer apparent.

Neri now appears to regard Alice's memory failures as exactly that:

**memory failures.**

She does not appear to interpret them as evidence that Alice dislikes her.

She does not appear to interpret them as proof that she is unwanted.

She simply waits, moves if necessary, and continues with her day.

This does not eliminate the practical safety concern.

---

# 8. Corrective Measures

The following measures remain in effect:

### Cafeteria

Personnel should check tables, chairs, floors, and nearby seating areas before leaving or beginning cleaning operations.

### SAFEHOUSE

Residents carrying Plush Neri should make reasonable efforts to remember where they place her.

### General Personnel

Employees walking through Sector 4-C should remain aware of floor-level obstacles.

### Jane Doe

Jane has voluntarily added:

> **"Check floor before stepping."**

to her personal mental checklist.

She has also stated that she is now going to assume that any suspiciously soft object in Sector 4-C might be Neri.

---

# 9. Administrative Note

C.O.R.E. recognizes that this is becoming a recurring pattern.

Alice forgetting Neri remains an unresolved anomalous phenomenon.

Neri's apparent acceptance of the situation does **not** mean personnel should become complacent.

There is a substantial difference between:

> **Neri doesn't mind being forgotten.**

and:

> **It is acceptable to leave Neri where she can be stepped on.**

The latter remains false.

---

# Final Assessment

Neri fell from a cafeteria table.

Jane accidentally stepped on her chest.

The force was fortunately minimal.

Neri was completely fine.

Jane was horrified.

Alice was guilty.

Neri was mostly indifferent.

Afterward, she went back to sitting comfortably.

No injury.

No medical intervention.

No emotional escalation.

Just another remarkably strange day for Plush Neri.

C.O.R.E. would nevertheless like to formally remind all residents:

> **Please check where you put the tiny white-haired seal girl.**

And to all personnel:

> **Please check where you put your feet.**

**END OF DOCUMENT**
