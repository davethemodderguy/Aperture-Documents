# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has demonstrated a previously undocumented anomalous capability:

> **Telekinesis.**

Jake can manipulate physical objects without direct contact.

The ability appears to be a natural extension of his stellar manifestation rather than a separate piece of anomalous equipment.

No visible energy source is required.

No gesture appears strictly necessary.

Jake simply looks at something.

And, if he wants it to move:

**It moves.**

---

# 1. INITIAL DISCOVERY

The capability was discovered accidentally.

Jake was sitting in the cafeteria wearing his new top hat and holding his cane.

His teddy bear was secured to his back.

A cup had been left on the opposite side of the table.

Jake looked at it.

The cup moved.

It slowly slid across the table.

Then lifted into the air.

Then floated toward him.

Jake caught it.

He looked at the cup.

Then at Neko.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I can do that.”

Neko stared.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Apparently.”

---

# 2. CONTROL

Jake's telekinesis appears to be highly intuitive.

He does not appear to require formal concentration exercises.

He can:

- move objects;
    
- lift objects;
    
- hold objects suspended;
    
- pull objects toward himself;
    
- push objects away;
    
- manipulate several objects simultaneously;
    
- maintain objects in fixed positions;
    
- and retrieve objects from a distance.
    

The exact upper limit remains undetermined.

C.O.R.E. has specifically instructed personnel **not to determine the maximum by giving Jake increasingly large objects.**

Neko agreed.

---

# 3. THE CANE

Jake quickly discovered that telekinesis makes his cane considerably more entertaining.

He can leave the cane standing upright beside him.

Then move it without touching it.

He can make it float beside him while walking.

He can retrieve it from across a room.

He can even make it slowly rotate in the air.

Jane observed this behavior for approximately thirty seconds.

> **JANE:**  
> “You're showing off.”

Jake shook his head.

> **JAKE:**  
> “No.”

The cane rotated faster.

Jane raised an eyebrow.

> **JANE:**  
> “Sure.”

Jake stopped.

# 7. GRAVITY AND TELEKINESIS

C.O.R.E. has noted a conceptual relationship between Jake's telekinesis and his existing gravitational control.

Jake already possesses the ability to alter how his manifested body interacts with local gravity.

Telekinesis appears to represent a separate form of physical manipulation.

The distinction is therefore maintained:

### Gravity Control

Jake changes how **he** interacts with gravity.

### Telekinesis

Jake changes the position or movement of **external objects** without touching them.

The two abilities can theoretically be used together.

C.O.R.E. has not requested that Jake demonstrate this combination.

There is no operational need to find out what happens when a six-year-old Blue Dwarf Star with telekinesis decides to experiment with gravity.

# 8. THE TOP HAT INCIDENT

Jake was once standing in the hallway wearing his top hat.

He removed it.

Placed it on the floor.

Walked several steps away.

Then stopped.

He looked back.

The hat floated upward.

It moved through the air.

Then settled neatly onto his head.

Jake adjusted the glowing azure ribbon.

Jane watched.

> **JANE:**  
> “Okay.”

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> “What?”

> **JANE:**  
> “Nothing.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “Okay.”

Jake continued walking.

The cane floated beside him.

# 9. SAFETY CONSIDERATIONS

Jake's telekinesis introduces several additional considerations.

Personnel should not:

- intentionally throw objects at Jake to test his reaction;
    
- ask him to manipulate dangerous equipment;
    
- challenge him to lift increasingly heavy objects;
    
- attempt to determine his maximum capability without authorization;
    
- use his ability as a substitute for ordinary equipment;
    
- or treat him as a general-purpose anomalous utility.
    

Jake is a resident.

He is not a laboratory instrument.

He is not a lifting system.

He is not a security device.

He is a six-year-old who happens to be capable of moving things without touching them.
