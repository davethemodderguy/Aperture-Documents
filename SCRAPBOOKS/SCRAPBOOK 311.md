# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – BLACKSITE MART OPERATIONS**

### **SUBJECT: HEAVEN BLACKSITE COLONY STATUS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Unlike the Krampus Compliance Authority headquarters, Heaven Blacksite is not a public facility.

The Blacksite is:

- Located on a remote island off the coast of Iceland.
    
- Concealed through multiple security measures.
    
- Accessible only to authorized personnel.
    
- Unknown to the general public.
    

As a result, the resident Mart colony has developed very differently from its counterparts stationed at the KCA.

There are no visitors to greet.

No customer service lobby.

And almost no intruders.

---

# **DAILY LIFE**

Without routine security incidents to prepare for, the Blacksite Marts devote nearly all of their time to helping personnel.

Common activities include:

- Assisting Engineering.
    
- Helping Medical staff.
    
- Carrying supplies.
    
- Cleaning dust from equipment.
    
- Cooling overheating machinery.
    
- Returning misplaced tools.
    
- Helping maintain the gardens.
    
- Reading in the library.
    
- Playing with SAFEHOUSE residents.
    
- Accepting sugary cheese crackers.
    

Researchers describe the Blacksite colony as:

> "...Exceptionally relaxed."

---

# **SECURITY**

The Blacksite maintains its own professional security force.

The Mart colony does not participate in routine security operations.

Although every Mart is capable of entering Rock Mode if someone is in immediate danger, documented uses within the Blacksite are exceedingly rare.

One researcher summarized the situation simply:

> "...Nothing ever tries to rob a classified island in the North Atlantic."

---

# **THE AMBASSADOR**

Unlike its KCA counterpart, the Blacksite Ambassador maintains no formal office.

Instead, it simply spends time wherever it believes it is most needed.

Its most common locations include:

- Project SAFEHOUSE.
    
- Mart's reading corner.
    
- Dialyn's office when she is visiting from the KCA.
    
- Corin and Corey's treehouse.
    
- The gardens.
    
- The Mart Nest.
    

When asked where it works, the Ambassador answered:

> "Where someone needs company."

---

# **EMOTIONAL SUPPORT**

Personnel have observed that the Ambassador appears unusually perceptive regarding emotional wellbeing.

Without being called, it often arrives beside individuals experiencing grief, stress, exhaustion, or loneliness.

Its preferred method of assistance is remarkably simple.

It sits beside them.

Sometimes it speaks.

Sometimes it doesn't.

Research has found no evidence that the Ambassador attempts to "fix" emotions.

It simply ensures no one experiences them alone.

---

# **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

**Mart (Owl Thiren)**

Mart frequently finds the Ambassador reading quietly nearby.

Neither speaks much.

Neither seems to mind.

One evening, Mart finally asked:

> "...Why do you spend so much time here?"

The Ambassador looked up from its book.

> "It's peaceful."

---

**Dialyn**

Whenever Dialyn returns to the Blacksite after a particularly difficult week at the KCA, the Ambassador is often already waiting near SAFEHOUSE with two mugs.

One contains tea.

The other contains fresh water.

Dialyn has stopped asking how it knows she's coming.

---

**Corin Lockwood**

Corin once commented that the Ambassador had a habit of appearing whenever someone was having a bad day.

The Ambassador politely corrected her.

> "...Not a habit."

> "...A choice."

---

**Corey Lockwood**

Some afternoons, Corey sits beneath her treehouse without saying a word.

The Ambassador simply sits beside her.

After nearly an hour of comfortable silence, it quietly asks:

> "...Better?"

Most days...

She nods.

---

**Lumi**

Lumi watched the Ambassador quietly comforting Corey while several ordinary Marts played nearby.

She smiled to herself.

> "...You really did learn empathy."

---

**Belle Merryweather**

> "...It doesn't patrol."

> "...It doesn't supervise."

> "...It just... cares."

---

**Endmin Merryweather**

> "...An effective use of its abilities."

---

**Neko**

---

**Miss Neko**

Late one evening, she walked through SAFEHOUSE to find the Ambassador asleep on the armrest of Mart's chair while Mart quietly read a book beneath a lamp.

She deliberately left without making a sound.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Heaven Blacksite Mart colony continues to thrive in the quiet environment of the concealed island facility.

Freed from the responsibilities of public interaction and routine security incidents, the colony has settled into what researchers consider its natural role:

Helping.

Learning.

Making people smile.

And ensuring that, whenever someone quietly needs another presence beside them...

The Ambassador somehow always seems to arrive.

No one knows exactly how it decides.

No one has found a reason to question it.

**End of Blacksite Mart Operations Report**
