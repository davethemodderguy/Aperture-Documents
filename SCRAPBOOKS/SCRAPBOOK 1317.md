# 1. ROOM OVERVIEW

Following the SAFEHOUSE residential revamp, Jake's room has developed a very specific character.

It contains plushies.

A considerable number of them.

The room currently contains:

- multiple stuffed animals;
    
- soft pillows;
    
- several blankets;
    
- plush toys arranged around the bed;
    
- smaller plushies positioned on shelves;
    
- several plushies placed near Jake's reading area;
    
- and, most importantly:
    

> **Jake's teddy bear.**

The teddy bear is not considered a conventional plush toy.

It is part of Jake's body.

More specifically:

> **It contains Jake's heart.**

---

# 2. THE TEDDY BEAR

Jake's teddy bear predates his current residence.

It was originally given to him by his mother while he was alive.

Following Jake's death, the bear became the vessel containing his physical heart.

The separation is anomalous and permanent.

Jake's chest does not contain a conventional heart.

His actual heart is located inside the teddy bear.

The bear continues to exhibit a heartbeat.

This heartbeat corresponds directly to Jake's cardiac activity.

It is therefore not:

- symbolic;
    
- metaphorical;
    
- an imitation;
    
- a secondary anomalous heartbeat;
    
- or merely a manifestation effect.
    

It is Jake's actual heart.

---

# 3. MEDICAL SIGNIFICANCE

The teddy bear must therefore be classified as:

> **CRITICAL BIOLOGICAL COMPONENT — JAKE**

It is not property.

It is not a keepsake.

It is not an anomalous specimen.

It is not a removable accessory.

It is part of Jake.

### Separation Risk

If the teddy bear is removed from Jake's possession for a sufficient period, Jake's condition deteriorates.

Complete separation results in:

> **Cardiac failure.**

If the bear is lost, stolen, destroyed, or otherwise rendered inaccessible to Jake, the expected outcome is:

> **Jake will die.**

Previous observations have confirmed that separation of the bear can cause Jake's manifestation to fail.

Reintegration of the bear restored him.

---

# 4. NEW RESIDENTIAL PROTOCOL

The teddy bear is therefore subject to the same protective considerations as Jake himself.

Personnel are strictly prohibited from:

- taking the bear as a joke;
    
- hiding the bear;
    
- moving the bear without Jake's knowledge;
    
- lending the bear to another resident;
    
- placing the bear in storage;
    
- treating it as ordinary property;
    
- separating it from Jake during room maintenance;
    
- removing it for testing without emergency authorization.
    

Most importantly:

> **Do not lose Jake's teddy bear.**

This instruction has been repeated in large lettering on the SAFEHOUSE residential safety database.

---

# 5. JAKE'S RELATIONSHIP WITH THE BEAR

Jake generally keeps the bear extremely close.

When sleeping, the bear is usually beside him or held against his chest.

When reading, it may sit next to him.

When walking around the SAFEHOUSE, Jake may carry it or wear it against his back.

When frightened, Jake hugs it.

When distressed, he may pull the bear tightly against himself.

The behavior is understandable from both an emotional and medical perspective.

The bear is simultaneously:

**Jake's oldest comfort object.**

and

**Jake's heart.**

# 7. THE TEDDY BEAR'S HEARTBEAT

The bear remains one of the most reliable ways of monitoring Jake's emotional state.

Personnel may observe changes in its heartbeat during:

- fear;
    
- distress;
    
- anger;
    
- sadness;
    
- physical exhaustion;
    
- emotional relief;
    
- or sudden surprise.
    

A rapid heartbeat generally accompanies heightened distress.

An irregular heartbeat requires closer observation.

A significant reduction or complete absence of heartbeat constitutes an emergency.

Personnel should not attempt to open the bear to verify its condition.

The heart is anomalously integrated with Jake.

Physical examination of the bear is therefore unnecessary and potentially dangerous.

# 9. ROOM SECURITY

Jake's residential floor has therefore received an additional safety consideration.

The room itself remains private.

Personnel are not permitted to enter simply to check the bear.

> **THIS IS A MEDICAL EMERGENCY.**
