### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / RECOVERY OBSERVATION LOG**

# **ORPHIE — RETURN OF THE DISCARDED**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Concern  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / Marts / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Luna / Solaria  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **RECOVERY ATTEMPT**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Over the course of Orphie's withdrawal, she discarded, abandoned, or sealed away nearly everything that once brought her happiness.

Her sketchbook.

Her favorite pencil.

Her mother's cookbook.

Her cooking.

Her hats.

Her old interests.

Her connections with the Marts.

Even the parts of herself that once cared deeply for others.

Recently, those things have begun returning to her.

Not because Orphie suddenly asked for them.

Because the people around her never truly threw them away.

They kept them.

---

# **THE PENCIL**

The first thing returned was her favorite pencil.

Orphie had snapped it in half and thrown it away.

Corin had retrieved it while crying.

She kept both pieces.

For weeks, Orphie refused to acknowledge them.

Eventually, Corin placed them quietly on the table.

> **Corin:** "...It's yours."

Orphie stared at the broken pencil.

> "...I threw it away."

> "...I know."

> "...Why did you keep it?"

Corin answered:

> "...Because you didn't have to."

Orphie said nothing.

She did not throw it away again.

---

# **THE SKETCHBOOK**

Her old sketchbook was also returned.

The disturbing pages remained inside.

Nobody had removed them.

Nobody had erased them.

The earlier drawings were still there too.

Bright landscapes.

Small characters.

Marts.

People.

Food.

Places she remembered.

Things she once wanted to draw.

Orphie flipped through the pages without speaking.

She stopped at one of the older drawings.

A Mart wearing a tiny top hat.

She stared at it for several seconds.

Then closed the book.

She kept it.

---

# **THE COOKBOOK**

The cookbook belonging to her mother was perhaps the most difficult object to return.

Orphie had sealed it away because she could no longer tolerate what it represented.

Her mother.

Her old life.

Her old happiness.

Her old self.

Corin eventually brought it back.

She did not open it.

She simply placed it on the table.

> **Corin:** "...I'm not asking you to use it."

Orphie looked at the book.

> "...Then why give it back?"

> "...Because it belongs to you."

Orphie remained silent.

She eventually picked it up.

She did not throw it away.

---

# **THE HATS**

Orphie's collection of soft hats also returned.

Beanies.

Berets.

Her oversized Mart top hat.

Arcturus's fedora.

Other soft headwear she had accumulated over time.

She had stopped wearing them after intentionally forcing herself to remain in Luna or Solaria.

They were placed neatly in a box.

Orphie looked through them.

She picked up the beret.

For a moment, she held it against her chest.

Then she put it back.

---

# **THE MATS**

The Marts were different.

They could not simply be handed back.

They were afraid of her.

Orphie knew this.

She did not attempt to summon them.

She did not use her powers.

She did not call them.

Instead, one day, she quietly placed a small bowl of water outside the treehouse.

Then she went back inside.

No Marts came.

Not immediately.

---

# **THE FIRST MART**

Several hours later, a single Mart approached.

It stopped at a distance.

Orphie noticed.

She did not smile.

She did not frighten it.

She simply said:

> "...You don't have to come closer."

The Mart remained where it was.

That was enough.

---

# **WHAT SHE THREW AWAY**

The recovery process eventually revealed just how much Orphie had discarded.

She had not simply thrown away objects.

She had attempted to discard pieces of her previous identity.

She had thrown away:

- Her favorite pencil.
    
- Her enthusiasm for drawing.
    
- Her mother's cookbook.
    
- Her love of cooking.
    
- Her hats.
    
- Her attachment to the Marts.
    
- Her willingness to care for others.
    
- Her belief that happiness was worth pursuing.
    

The first several could be physically returned.

The others could not.

---

# **CORIN'S ROLE**

Corin never told Orphie:

> _"You need to become who you were."_

She understood that Orphie was not a lost version of herself waiting to be reconstructed.

Orphie had changed.

The goal was not to erase that change.

It was to give her back the things she had discarded so she could decide what they meant to her now.

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

When Corin finally asked:

> "...Do you want any of it?"

Orphie looked around the room.

The pencil.

The sketchbook.

The cookbook.

The hats.

The small bowl outside.

> "...I don't know."

Corin nodded.

> "...That's okay."

Orphie looked at her.

> "...You're not going to take them away?"

> "...No."

> "...Even if I don't use them?"

> "...They're yours."

---

# **LUNA**

Luna watched quietly.

> "...She's getting pieces of herself back."

Solaria corrected her.

> "...Not exactly."

> "...She's getting the choice back."

Luna smiled faintly.

> "...Fair."

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Returned items:**

✓ Favorite pencil  
✓ Sketchbook  
✓ Mother's cookbook  
✓ Soft hats  
✓ Personal belongings previously discarded

**Marts:**  
Still cautious. Initial contact has resumed at extreme distance.

**Corin:**  
Continuing support without forcing emotional recovery.

**Orphie:**  
Has not returned to her former emotional state. However, she is no longer actively destroying every reminder of her former life.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie spent weeks trying to erase everything that made her happy.

She threw things away.

She sealed things.

She stopped cooking.

She stopped drawing.

She stopped caring.

She frightened away the Marts.

She tried to convince herself that none of it mattered.

But the people around her remembered.

They remembered the pencil.

They remembered the drawings.

They remembered the recipes.

They remembered the hats.

They remembered the Marts sitting around her.

They remembered **Orphie before she decided she was unimportant.**

So they gave everything back.

Not to force her to become the person she used to be.

Not to demand that she become happy again.

Simply because those things were hers.

And this time, Orphie did not throw them away.

**End of Recovery Observation Log**
