# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **RESTRICTED ANOMALOUS WEATHER / PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT LOG**

# **THE POINTLESS QUESTION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **ANOMALOUS SUPPRESSION STORM**  
**Status:** **EXTREME ISOLATION / PSYCHOLOGICAL DETERIORATION**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Another day has passed.

The storm remains.

The treehouse remains isolated.

Orphie has not returned.

Guiding Light cannot communicate.

Curious Light cannot reach the treehouse.

There is no food.

There is no remaining drinking water.

Corin has not consumed either for an extended period.

Physically, this should be catastrophic.

For Corin, it isn't.

Her body does not require either to remain functional.

That fact, however, has become part of the problem.

---

# **THE ROUTINE THAT DISAPPEARED**

Corin used to eat because it made her feel normal.

She used to drink because it made her feel normal.

She would sit at the table.

Hold a cup.

Taste something warm.

Listen to Orphie moving around the kitchen.

Those things were not necessary for survival.

They were necessary for **feeling alive.**

Now there is nothing.

The kitchen is empty.

The cups remain.

The plates remain.

But the routine is gone.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

The notebook has become increasingly disturbing.

The entries are no longer primarily about Orphie's absence.

They are increasingly about Corin herself.

One page reads:

> **I don't need food.**

> **I don't need water.**

> **I don't need sleep anymore.**

> **I don't even need a working body.**

Then:

> **So what exactly am I surviving for?**

---

# **THE QUESTION**

Corin understands that conventional self-destruction would accomplish nothing.

Her body is already dead.

Her Ether reconstructs damage.

Wounds close.

Broken tissue repairs.

Her body continues functioning regardless of what happens to it.

The realization has produced a particularly cruel thought:

> **"Even dying isn't an option because I'm already dead."**

She wrote it several times.

---

# **THE DARKER ENTRIES**

The notebook contains increasingly explicit thoughts about wanting everything to stop.

They are not recorded here verbatim.

The recurring themes are clear:

**She wants the thoughts to stop.**

**She wants the waiting to stop.**

**She wants to stop feeling like a burden.**

**She wants the uncertainty to end.**

The underlying desire is less about death itself than escaping a state she can no longer tolerate.

---

# **CORIN'S FRUSTRATION**

One entry reads:

> **"Why do I even bother?"**

Then:

> **"My body won't die."**

> **"It doesn't care what I want."**

> **"It just fixes itself."**

The next sentence is written much smaller:

> **"I hate it."**

---

# **THE MIRROR**

Corin eventually stood in front of the mirror.

She looked at herself.

Pale skin.

Chalky lime hair.

The hairclip.

The scars hidden beneath her clothes.

The body that should not still be moving.

She touched her chest.

Nothing.

No heartbeat.

She touched her wrist.

Nothing.

No pulse.

She stared at her reflection.

> **"...I'm supposed to be dead."**

A pause.

> **"So why am I still here?"**

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK'S FINAL PAGE**

Corin wrote:

> **"I don't want to hurt myself."**

She paused for a long time.

Then:

> **"I want everything to stop hurting."**

Another pause.

> **"There's a difference."**

She closed the notebook.

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

The notebook should not be interpreted as evidence that Corin has simply decided she wants to die.

Her writing demonstrates severe psychological distress under prolonged isolation.

The recurring fixation on death appears closely tied to:

- Abandonment.
    
- Loss of routine.
    
- Loss of contact.
    
- Loss of normal human experiences.
    
- Hatred of her reanimated body.
    
- The belief that she is a burden.
    
- The inability to escape the situation through sleep.
    
- The belief that nobody is coming back.
    

Her body being incapable of conventional death has not protected her psychologically.

In some respects, it has made things worse.

---

# **THE OTHER SIDE**

The SAFEHOUSE remains tense.

Orphie knows Corin has run out of food.

She knows she has no water.

She knows the notebook has become increasingly disturbing.

She also knows Corin does not require either substance to physically survive.

That fact offers no comfort.

> **Orphie:**  
> "...She doesn't need to survive."

She looks toward the storm.

> **"...She needs a reason to want to."**

Guiding Light says nothing.

She cannot.

---

# **THE STORM**

The anomalous storm remains unchanged.

No weakening.

No opening.

No communications.

No return of anomalous abilities.

No explanation.

The phenomenon continues suppressing everything within its reach.

The world has effectively become divided into two places:

**The people desperately trying to reach Corin.**

And:

**Corin, who no longer believes anyone is trying.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's body continues functioning.

It does not hunger.

It does not thirst.

It does not need a beating heart.

It repairs itself.

It refuses to die.

But Corin never wanted merely to survive.

She wanted to **live.**

To eat something warm.

To drink something cold.

To have someone comb her hair.

To hear footsteps outside the door.

To know that someone would return.

The storm has taken away almost every ordinary thing that made existence feel worth experiencing.

And now the notebook contains the question she cannot answer:

> **"If my body can survive anything, what am I supposed to be living for?"**

The storm continues.

Corin remains alive.

And somewhere beyond the white wall of snow, everyone who loves her is still waiting for a way through.

**End of Restricted Anomalous Weather / Psychological Incident Log**
