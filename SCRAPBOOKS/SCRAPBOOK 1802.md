# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Aries Akana forgot Jake.

For more than three consecutive hours.

Jake, still in his feline form, had been placed in a quiet area of the administrative annex while Aries attended to several competing responsibilities.

His teddy bear was beside him.

Aries intended to retrieve him shortly afterward.

He did not.

One task became another.

A scheduled briefing ran longer than expected.

An evaluation required additional documentation.

A conversation led to another assignment.

Aries moved between locations, completed his obligations, and eventually returned to his residence.

Jake remained where he had been left.

The teddy bear remained beside him.

Jake did not attempt to move.

He did not call for Aries.

He did not seek another resident.

He did not attempt to retrieve the teddy bear if its position became inconvenient.

He did not make any observable effort to attract attention.

**He remained in the same place for more than three hours.**

The incident was discovered when Bao encountered Jake during a routine welfare check and recognized that his assigned caregiver was absent.

Jake was alive.

His teddy bear was present.

No immediate injury was identified.

However, the circumstances constituted a serious supervision failure.

The recent assignment of Aries as Jake's primary caregiver was established specifically because Jake currently does not reliably protect himself from environmental hazards.

Leaving him unattended defeated the purpose of the assignment.

C.O.R.E. acknowledges that Aries did not deliberately abandon Jake.

He became occupied with other responsibilities and lost track of time.

That explanation is relevant to determining intent.

It does not remove the danger created by the oversight failure.

---

# 1. INCIDENT TIMELINE

## 09:07 — Initial Placement

Aries entered the administrative annex carrying Jake.

Jake was in his feline form.

His teddy bear was secured beside him.

Aries stopped near a quiet seating alcove while preparing to attend a scheduled evaluation briefing.

He placed Jake on a padded bench.

The location appeared safe at the time.

There were no immediate hazards nearby.

Aries intended to return after completing a short task.

**ARIES:**  
"Stay here for a minute, little star."

Jake blinked.

Aries adjusted the teddy bear beside him.

**ARIES:**  
"I'll be back."

He then left.

No formal handover occurred.

No alternate caregiver was assigned.

No reminder was established.

Aries believed the absence would be brief.

It was not.

---

## 09:19 — First Missed Opportunity

Aries completed the initial portion of his briefing.

He was asked to remain for a discussion concerning his evaluation.

He agreed.

Jake did not appear in his immediate field of view.

No one asked where Jake was.

Aries did not independently remember that he had left him in the annex.

The briefing continued.

---

## 09:46 — Additional Assignment

Aries was directed to complete supplementary documentation.

The task was not unusual.

It required attention, several conversations, and a review of his recent evaluation results.

Aries moved to another room.

Jake remained on the bench.

His teddy bear was still beside him.

No movement was observed.

---

## 10:12 — Unrecorded Absence

Aries was seen entering the administrative corridor without Jake.

This was initially interpreted as an ordinary movement between assigned duties.

No one questioned it.

Personnel had become accustomed to seeing Aries carry Jake throughout the SAFEHOUSE.

However, no system existed to confirm whether Jake was with Aries at any given moment.

The absence therefore did not trigger an alert.

---

## 10:41 — Further Delay

Aries attended another discussion.

He then reviewed the documentation associated with his evaluation.

A colleague asked him to clarify one of his recorded results.

Aries remained to answer.

By this point, he had been away from Jake for more than ninety minutes.

He had not deliberately decided to leave Jake unattended for that duration.

He had simply failed to remember that Jake was still waiting.

---

## 11:28 — Three-Hour Threshold Approached

Jake remained on the padded bench.

His body had not meaningfully changed position.

His eyes opened and closed at intervals.

His breathing continued.

The teddy bear remained within immediate proximity.

No resident had approached him.

No employee had identified the situation as an emergency.

Jake did not attempt to leave.

He did not seek assistance.

He did not display any observable effort to communicate that he had been left alone.

The absence of movement was consistent with his current behavior.

Unfortunately, that same consistency made it easier for the situation to go unnoticed.

---

## 12:16 — Discovery by Bao

Bao entered the administrative annex while completing a routine welfare check.

She noticed Jake almost immediately.

A small white cat was lying on a bench.

His teddy bear was beside him.

He appeared to be alone.

Bao initially assumed that Aries was nearby.

She looked down the corridor.

No Aries.

She checked the adjacent room.

No Aries.

She returned to Jake.

**BAO:**  
"Sweetheart?"

Jake blinked.

Bao looked at the teddy bear.

Then at the empty corridor.

Her expression changed.

**BAO:**  
"Where's your brother?"

Jake did not respond.

Bao checked the time.

She then reviewed the available corridor observations and contacted Security.

The records established that Jake had been left in the annex at approximately 09:07.

It was now 12:16.

**Three hours and nine minutes had elapsed.**

Bao immediately contacted the medical team and the resident welfare officer.

She remained with Jake while the situation was assessed.

---

# 2. THE DISCOVERY

Bao sat beside Jake.

She did not immediately pick him up.

She first checked his breathing, visible condition, and the position of his teddy bear.

The bear remained present.

There was no obvious sign that it had been damaged or displaced.

Jake did not appear to have sustained an injury during the period of isolation.

Nevertheless, his inability or refusal to initiate movement meant that his apparent lack of distress could not be treated as proof that the situation had been safe.

Bao reached toward the bear, adjusted its position slightly, and ensured that it remained securely beside him.

**BAO:**  
"Hi, sweetheart."

Jake blinked.

**BAO:**  
"Mom's here."

No further response followed.

Bao looked at him for several seconds.

Then she checked the corridor again.

**BAO:**  
"Where on earth is Aries?"

She contacted Security.

She did not leave Jake to search for him.

She had no intention of repeating the same oversight while trying to determine how the first one had occurred.

---

Security began tracing Aries's movements through the administrative annex.

The records established that he had attended multiple scheduled activities after leaving Jake behind.

There was no evidence that he had attempted to conceal Jake's location.

There was also no indication that he had deliberately transferred responsibility to another resident.

The problem was simpler and more serious.

**Nobody had been assigned responsibility for Jake after Aries walked away.**

Aries was eventually located in an administrative office.

He was reviewing evaluation documentation.

A Security Officer entered.

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
"Aries."

**ARIES:**  
"Yeah?"

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
"Where is Jake?"

Aries looked up.

He answered immediately.

**ARIES:**  
"With me."

The officer remained silent.

Aries looked beside his chair.

Then behind it.

Then toward the door.

His expression changed.

**ARIES:**  
"...Oh, no."

He stood up so quickly that his chair moved backward.

**ARIES:**  
"I left him in the annex."

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
"At 09:07."

Aries stared at the officer.

**ARIES:**  
"What time is it?"

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
"12:19."

For several seconds, Aries said nothing.

Then he began moving toward the door.

**ARIES:**  
"Where is he?"

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
"Bao found him. He's with her."

Aries stopped.

His shoulders lowered slightly.

The relief lasted only a moment.

**ARIES:**  
"I forgot him."

The officer did not contradict him.

**ARIES:**  
"I actually forgot him."

He turned and hurried toward the medical observation area.

# 4. ARIES RETURNS

Aries arrived at the annex to find Bao sitting beside Jake.

The cat remained on the bench.

The teddy bear was still beside him.

Nothing about Jake's position suggested that he had attempted to leave.

Aries stopped several meters away.

**ARIES:**  
"Little star?"

Jake blinked.

Aries approached.

He crouched beside the bench and examined Jake carefully.

**ARIES:**  
"I'm sorry."

Jake did not respond.

Aries looked at the teddy bear.

Then at Bao.

**ARIES:**  
"I left him here."

**BAO:**  
"Yes."

**ARIES:**  
"I thought I'd only be gone for a few minutes."

**BAO:**  
"I know."

**ARIES:**  
"I got caught up in the evaluation, and then the paperwork, and then—"

He stopped.

There was no useful explanation to add.

Bao had already established what happened.

Jake had been left alone for more than three hours.

Aries had forgotten him.

**ARIES:**  
"I forgot my little brother."

Bao's expression softened, but her voice remained serious.

**BAO:**  
"You made a mistake."

Aries looked at her.

**BAO:**  
"A serious one."

He lowered his head.

**BAO:**  
"But we're going to deal with what happened. You need to make sure it doesn't happen again."

Aries nodded.

He reached toward Jake, then paused.

**ARIES:**  
"Can I pick him up?"

Bao checked Jake's position and nodded.

Aries carefully lifted him, supporting his body and ensuring that the teddy bear remained secure.

Jake did not resist.

He did not move closer.

He did not reach for Aries.

He simply allowed himself to be carried.

Aries held him against his chest.

**ARIES:**  
"I'm sorry, little star."

Jake blinked.

Aries closed his eyes briefly.

**ARIES:**  
"I should have come back."

No response.

**ARIES:**  
"I will next time."

He did not ask Jake to forgive him.

He did not ask Jake to reassure him.

He carried him to the medical observation room.

# 5. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT

Jake underwent a post-incident welfare assessment.

No immediate injury was identified.

His breathing continued independently.

His blinking remained consistent with the previously observed pattern.

His teddy bear remained present throughout the assessment.

No acute deterioration was detected during the initial examination.

However, the absence of an obvious injury does not make the period of unsupervised isolation acceptable.

Jake's current condition means that he cannot reliably:

- leave an unsafe location;
    
- reposition himself;
    
- retrieve an object that has moved out of reach;
    
- seek assistance;
    
- communicate discomfort;
    
- or summon help when his condition worsens.
    

The medical team therefore recommends continued supervision and reassessment of his environment.

The incident does not establish that Jake experienced distress during the three-hour period.

It also does not establish that he was comfortable.

His lack of visible reaction cannot resolve that question.

---

# 6. WHY THE INCIDENT IS SERIOUS

Personnel may reasonably ask why the situation constitutes a critical welfare incident if Jake remained physically unharmed.

The answer is that the outcome was fortunate.

The risk was not eliminated.

It simply did not produce an observed injury on this occasion.

Jake's teddy bear was present, but its presence alone did not make the environment safe.

A hazard could have emerged.

A person could have moved him.

An object could have fallen.

His physiological condition could have changed.

He could have required assistance without being able to request it.

None of those events needed to occur for the supervision failure to be serious.

The assigned caregiver's purpose was to prevent Jake from being left in a situation where he could not reliably protect himself.

That purpose was not fulfilled.

**A supervision protocol cannot depend on the resident being capable of rescuing himself when the protocol fails.**

The corrective response must therefore include procedural safeguards rather than relying exclusively on personal vigilance.

---

# 8. CORRECTIVE ACTIONS

The following measures have been ordered.

### 8.1 Mandatory Handover

Aries may not leave Jake unattended.

If Aries must attend an assignment, briefing, evaluation, or other obligation that prevents direct supervision, he must formally transfer responsibility to an authorized caregiver.

The receiving caregiver must explicitly acknowledge the handover.

An assumption that somebody else is watching Jake is insufficient.

### 8.2 Caregiver Availability

The SAFEHOUSE will establish a list of authorized relief caregivers.

Bao will remain a primary option when available.

Clover and other appropriately briefed residents may assist according to the approved care plan.

No single caregiver is expected to provide uninterrupted supervision without relief.

### 8.3 Location Confirmation

Jake's location and assigned caregiver must be recorded whenever responsibility changes.

This requirement is intended to prevent a repeat of the present incident.

### 8.4 Workload Management

Aries's evaluation schedule must account for his caregiving responsibilities.

If a task prevents him from maintaining supervision, a handover must occur before the task begins.

The burden of resolving scheduling conflicts must not fall on Jake.

### 8.5 Periodic Welfare Checks

Welfare personnel will conduct periodic checks while Jake remains in his current condition.

These checks supplement direct supervision; they do not replace it.

### 8.6 No Reliance on Jake's Response

Personnel must not assume that Jake will alert them if left alone.

His current nonresponse is an established welfare concern.

Any system that depends on him asking for help is inadequate.

### 8.7 Incident Review

Aries's primary-caregiver assignment will remain active but subject to review.

The objective is not to punish him for being imperfect.

It is to ensure that a preventable lapse does not recur.

---

# 9. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

This incident resulted from a failure of attention compounded by an inadequate handover procedure.

Aries did not intentionally abandon Jake.

He became occupied with competing responsibilities and forgot that Jake remained in the administrative annex.

His explanation is consistent with the available records.

Nevertheless, the duration of the absence exceeded three hours.

Given Jake's current condition, this represents a serious failure of supervision.

The absence of injury does not reduce the need for corrective action.

C.O.R.E. will address both the individual oversight and the procedural conditions that allowed it to continue unnoticed.

The revised system must not depend on Aries remembering every responsibility while simultaneously managing an unpredictable workload.

Nor may it assume that Jake will alert someone when the system fails.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING OBSERVATION

Aries had been carrying Jake everywhere.

Through corridors.

Into briefings.

Across the SAFEHOUSE.

He had adjusted his schedule around the little cat in his arms.

He had become so accustomed to checking Jake's position that he sometimes checked the teddy bear before checking his own equipment.

Then one busy morning, he put Jake down.

Just for a minute.

And forgot.

Three hours became three hours and nine minutes.

During that time, Jake did not get up.

He did not wander away.

He did not look for his brother.

He did not follow anyone.

He did not even attempt to make himself noticeable.

He remained on the bench, with his teddy bear beside him, breathing and blinking.

Bao found him.

Aries came running.

And Jake was still there.

That last fact was a relief.

It was not a defense.

Later, Aries sat on the edge of the bed with Jake resting securely in his arms.

He had checked the bear.

He had checked Jake's position.

He had checked the door.

He had checked the clock.

He checked the clock again.

Clover watched him for a while.

**CLOVER:**  
"He's not going anywhere."

**ARIES:**  
"I know."

**CLOVER:**  
"You're allowed to blink, you know."

Aries looked at her.

**ARIES:**  
"Very funny."

Clover pulled up a chair.

**CLOVER:**  
"I'm serious. You're not going to solve this by staring at him for the next twenty-four hours."

Aries looked down at Jake.

**ARIES:**  
"I left him for three hours."

**CLOVER:**  
"Yes."

**ARIES:**  
"I forgot."

**CLOVER:**  
"Yes."

**ARIES:**  
"What if Bao hadn't found him?"

Clover did not answer immediately.

Neither of them knew.

That was the problem.

Aries adjusted the blanket around Jake.

**ARIES:**  
"I'm not doing that again."

**CLOVER:**  
"Then don't rely on remembering. Make sure someone else knows they're responsible before you leave."

Aries nodded slowly.

It was not a promise that he would never make another mistake.

It was a plan to prevent one particular mistake from happening again.

Jake blinked.

Aries looked down.

**ARIES:**  
"Sorry, little star."

Jake did not respond.

Aries did not ask him to.

He remained seated, holding him securely, while Clover helped arrange the relief-care schedule.

The next time Aries needed to leave for a briefing, someone would explicitly take responsibility for Jake.

The next time a task ran long, there would be a handover.

The next time Aries put Jake down, the system would no longer depend on him remembering that the little cat was still there.

Because Jake cannot reliably get up and follow someone.

He cannot reliably call for help.

He cannot reliably protect himself.

And he should not have to remain perfectly motionless for three hours before somebody remembers that he needs looking after.

**Aries forgot.**

**Bao noticed.**

**Jake survived the oversight.**

C.O.R.E.'s responsibility is to make sure the next incident does not depend on somebody happening to walk past at the right time.
