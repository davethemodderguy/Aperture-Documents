# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

C.O.R.E. has identified a more specific function for J-4C-P, designated **The Remnant**.

The Remnant is a manifestation of Jake Akana's pain and suffering. It is not Jake's surviving consciousness, and Jake himself has confirmed no return from death.

However, the Remnant is not merely an expression of suffering without purpose.

**It is a part of Jake that tells everyone what to do and how to find him.**

Its behavior appears directive rather than purely observational.

It repeatedly visits Jake's grave in the Starlands. It speaks to individuals who encounter it. It provides instructions, indicates where people should go, and directs their attention toward information they need to discover.

Its communications are not necessarily complete.

Its voice remains severely damaged, and speaking causes it substantial pain. It often communicates through short phrases, gestures, and deliberate movements rather than extended explanations.

Nevertheless, its behavior suggests that the Remnant serves a function within the circumstances surrounding Jake's death.

It directs people.

It tells them where to look.

It tells them what they need to do.

And, most importantly, it tells them how to find him.

The exact meaning of _find him_ remains under investigation. C.O.R.E. must not automatically interpret the phrase as an instruction to retrieve Jake's remains, locate his consciousness, or attempt to reverse his death.

The Remnant's role is now classified as **anomalous postmortem guidance**.

The distinction remains essential:

**The Remnant is a part of Jake, but it is not Jake himself.**

---

# 1. REVISED UNDERSTANDING OF THE REMNANT

Previous observations established that the Remnant manifests with Jake's appearance, carries a teddy bear resembling his own, and bears extensive scars and injuries associated with suffering.

The Remnant subsequently confirmed that Jake was gone and that it was not him.

These statements remain valid.

However, the new behavioral evidence indicates that the Remnant's relationship with Jake is more specific than a general association with his pain.

It appears to retain, express, or enact a particular function connected to Jake.

That function is communication through direction.

The Remnant does not simply appear and suffer.

It directs attention.

It provides instructions.

It indicates locations.

It sometimes tells residents what they must do before they can understand why the instruction matters.

The phenomenon therefore appears to have an operational purpose, even though its underlying mechanism remains unknown.

C.O.R.E. has not established whether this function was deliberately created, emerged spontaneously from Jake's experiences, or represents an intrinsic property of the manifestation.

No conclusion has been drawn regarding whether Jake consciously intended the Remnant to perform this role.

---

# 2. THE GRAVE IN THE STARLANDS

The Remnant consistently returns to Jake's grave.

This behavior is now considered relevant to its directive function.

The grave is not merely a location where the phenomenon happens to manifest.

It appears to be a recurring destination associated with the Remnant's communications.

During one recorded encounter, a resident approached the figure while it hovered beside the grave.

**RESIDENT:**  
"Why do you always come here?"

The Remnant did not answer immediately.

Its head remained lowered.

**RESIDENT:**  
"Is there something we're supposed to find?"

The blue flame flickered.

The Remnant raised one hand and pointed toward the grave.

**RESIDENT:**  
"Jake?"

The figure nodded once.

The resident looked at the marker.

**RESIDENT:**  
"He's buried here. We know that."

The Remnant shook its head.

A pause.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"Look."

The resident looked down at the grave.

Then around the surrounding area.

**RESIDENT:**  
"Where?"

The Remnant pointed again.

Not at the grave marker.

Beyond it.

The resident followed the direction of its hand but found no immediately apparent destination.

When the resident looked back, the Remnant had demanifested.

The instruction was recorded.

Its meaning remains unresolved.

The encounter established that the Remnant's visits may be associated with directing others toward something rather than simply remaining at Jake's final resting place.

---

# 3. DIRECTIVE COMMUNICATION

The Remnant's speech is limited by the pain associated with its damaged voice.

Its instructions are consequently brief.

It rarely provides a complete explanation before directing someone to act.

Observed communication patterns include:

- Pointing toward a location.
    
- Repeatedly indicating a particular direction.
    
- Using a single word to identify a destination.
    
- Repeating an instruction when the recipient misunderstands.
    
- Remaining present until someone acknowledges the instruction.
    
- Returning to the same location when an instruction has not been followed or understood.
    

The final behavior has not been established in every encounter and must not be treated as universal.

However, the overall pattern suggests that the Remnant's communication is intentional.

Its limited speech should not be confused with an absence of purpose.

It may have only a few words available to it.

Those words may nevertheless be important.

# 5. WHAT DOES "FIND HIM" MEAN?

C.O.R.E. currently recognizes several possible interpretations.

### Hypothesis A — Locating Information About Jake

The Remnant may be directing residents toward evidence, records, or locations necessary to understand the circumstances surrounding Jake's death.

This would be consistent with its directive behavior without requiring Jake's consciousness to remain present.

### Hypothesis B — Locating Jake's Remains

The Remnant may be directing people toward Jake's physical remains or toward something associated with his burial.

However, Jake's grave is already known to be in the Starlands.

The phrase therefore may refer to something beyond simply locating the grave.

### Hypothesis C — Completing an Unfinished Action

The Remnant may be instructing residents to perform an action that remained incomplete before Jake's death.

No such action has yet been conclusively identified.

### Hypothesis D — Finding the Cause or Circumstances of His Death

The Remnant may be directing attention toward unresolved circumstances surrounding Jake's final moments.

This possibility warrants investigation, but no particular individual or organization is implicated by the current evidence.

### Hypothesis E — An Unknown Meaning

The instruction may have a meaning that cannot be understood through ordinary language alone.

C.O.R.E. cannot currently exclude this possibility.

No hypothesis has been confirmed.

Personnel must not choose an interpretation merely because it provides emotional closure.

---

# 6. THE REMNANT'S PAIN AND ITS COMMUNICATION FUNCTION

The Remnant's directive behavior does not eliminate its persistent suffering.

Its body remains covered in scars and visible injuries.

It continues to exhibit distress even when it is not being physically harmed.

Physical force can injure it further.

Its voice remains severely damaged, and attempts to speak appear painful.

These characteristics impose practical limitations on its ability to communicate.

The Remnant may know what it wants others to do without possessing the physical ability to explain the entire reason.

It may be able to indicate a direction but not describe the destination.

It may be able to say _find him_ but not explain what finding him entails.

This is a working interpretation, not a confirmed limitation of the phenomenon.

Nevertheless, personnel must account for it during interactions.

Repeated questioning may increase distress without producing useful information.

If the Remnant provides an instruction, personnel should record its exact words, observe its gestures, and ask only brief clarifying questions when appropriate.

Do not demand a complete explanation from a phenomenon that struggles to speak.

C.O.R.E. has also emphasized that Aries must not be made solely responsible for interpreting the Remnant's communications.

He is also a grieving sixteen-year-old.

The investigation must not turn his grief into an operational requirement.

# 8. THE REMNANT IS NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR JAKE

The directive function creates a risk that residents will begin treating the Remnant as though it can speak on Jake's behalf.

This is not supported by current evidence.

The Remnant may direct people toward something connected to Jake.

It may communicate information associated with his suffering.

It may provide instructions that are useful to the investigation.

None of these functions establish that its statements represent Jake's current wishes.

Personnel must not ask the Remnant to decide what Jake would have wanted.

They must not interpret every gesture as a message from Jake.

They must not assume that the Remnant can provide complete or reliable testimony about events it did not explicitly describe.

Its communications should be treated as anomalous information requiring careful interpretation and independent verification.

At the same time, its instructions must not be dismissed merely because the phenomenon is not Jake.

**The Remnant is not Jake. That does not mean its communications are meaningless.**

---

# 9. UPDATED RESPONSE PROTOCOL

The following procedures are now in effect.

## When the Remnant provides an instruction

1. Record its exact words.
    
2. Record the direction or location indicated.
    
3. Ask one short clarifying question if necessary.
    
4. Do not demand that it speak louder.
    
5. Do not physically restrain it to obtain further information.
    
6. Investigate the indicated location only when doing so is safe and authorized.
    
7. Record the outcome independently of the Remnant's interpretation.
    

## When the Remnant indicates that someone must be found

Personnel must clarify, where possible, whether the instruction concerns a person, an object, a location, or information.

Do not assume that _find Jake_ means Jake is alive.

Do not assume that the instruction refers exclusively to his grave.

Do not assume that the Remnant is asking for resurrection.

The phrase remains ambiguous until corroborating evidence establishes its meaning.

## When the Remnant becomes distressed

Stop questioning.

Maintain a safe distance.

Do not attempt to force additional speech.

Do not touch its scars, wounds, flame, or teddy bear.

Allow the manifestation to end without pursuit if it demanifests.

The priority is to preserve the communication already obtained without causing additional distress.

---

# 10. FINAL ASSESSMENT

C.O.R.E. now considers J-4C-P to have a directive function associated with Jake Akana.

The Remnant is a part of Jake expressed through his pain and suffering.

It is not Jake himself.

Its repeated visits to Jake's grave in the Starlands remain significant, but the reason for those visits has not been conclusively established.

Its instructions indicate that it wants others to act.

It tells people where to go.

It tells them what to do.

It tells them to find Jake.

What remains unclear is what it means by that instruction.

The Remnant may be directing residents toward unresolved information, an unfinished action, a location, or something C.O.R.E. has not yet considered.

The investigation will proceed without presuming that Jake's consciousness has survived.

Nor will it presume that the Remnant's directives can be safely ignored.

**The Remnant is not a replacement for Jake. It is a source of anomalous guidance connected to his suffering.**

Its instructions must be documented, evaluated, and followed up through appropriate investigative procedures.
