# 1. OVERVIEW

The **FSR Deadbolt** is the latest Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise platform developed by Neri Tachyon.

It is a precision-oriented rifle occupying an unusual position between a conventional sniper rifle and a marksman rifle.

Neri's design philosophy was not to create the largest or most powerful rifle possible.

Instead, she wanted a platform capable of adapting its ballistic characteristics mechanically while retaining the handling and precision expected from a long-range weapon.

The resulting platform was designated:

> **FSR DEADBOLT**

Only **two units** exist.

---

# 2. DESIGN ORIGIN

Following the Tachyon Industries community survey, three categories received the highest interest:

- Sniper Rifle
    
- Assault Rifle
    
- Marksman Rifle
    

The **Sniper Rifle** category ultimately received the highest number of votes.

Neri subsequently entered the Aperture Armory for research material.

The documented research pool included:

- Remington 700 rifles equipped with Cadex chassis — **7**
    
- TAC-50 AMRs — **2**
    
- ORSIS T-3000 rifles — **4**
    
- SCAR 20 DMRs — **2**
    
- M82A1 AMR — **1**
    
- assorted ammunition and research materials
    

Neri studied the platforms before beginning the Deadbolt project.

Nora Tachyon was permitted to observe portions of the development process.

Her recorded description of the process was:

> **“Sis made it from a bunch of other guns.”**

Neri's response:

> **“Something like that.”**

---

# 3. FINAL DESIGN

The Deadbolt combines characteristics inspired by multiple precision platforms.

Neko's informal assessment was considerably less technical:

> **“Imagine the M82A1, ORISIS have a kid, and it have all of their best traits.”**

Neri corrected none of this.

The final platform incorporates:

- a long precision-oriented configuration;
    
- **25-inch barrel**;
    
- **10-round magazine capacity**;
    
- **Nekonium Alloy frame**;
    
- integral suppression;
    
- rail-assisted accessory mounting;
    
- folding recoil-absorbing stock;
    
- integrated bipod;
    
- mechanically variable barrel-velocity configuration.
    

The scope is **not included**.

An Aperture-grade Picatinny rail is provided for compatible optics selected by the owner.

---

# 4. CALIBER

The FSR Deadbolt is chambered for:

> **.458 Tachyon Magnum**

Alternate designation:

> **9.5×70 mm NATO**

The cartridge is a Tachyon Industries proprietary ammunition family developed specifically around the Deadbolt platform.

---

# 5. AMMUNITION FAMILY

Five official ammunition variants have been designated.

### .458 TACHYON MAGNUM S — SUBSONIC

A reduced-signature ammunition variant intended for situations where lower acoustic output is desirable.

Neri's description:

> **“Subsonic. Close range.”**

---

### .458 TACHYON MAGNUM D — DECIMATOR

A specialized heavy-purpose variant intended primarily against vehicles and other hardened targets.

Neri's explanation was unusually direct:

> **“Otherwise it'll be a war crime.”**

When questioned about the comment, Neri stated:

> **“I read the Geneva Conventions.”**

No further explanation was provided.

---

### .458 TACHYON MAGNUM HE — HIGH EXPLOSIVE

The conventional explosive ammunition variant.

Neri's description:

> **“Classic Explosive Ammo.”**

Further technical details remain restricted.

---

### .458 TACHYON MAGNUM GC — GLASS CASCADE

A specialized ammunition variant capable of producing a volley of sharpened Nekonium-framed glass projectiles.

Neri named the ammunition herself.

No additional public technical documentation has been authorized.

---

### .458 TACHYON MAGNUM NDR — NEKONIUM DECIMATOR ROUND

The most restricted ammunition variant currently associated with the Deadbolt.

Neri's entire public explanation was:

> **“For emergencies.”**

When asked what it does:

> **“Let's just say it eats.”**

C.O.R.E. has elected not to request clarification.

---

# 6. NEKONIUM ALLOY

The Deadbolt's primary structural material is a refined **Nekonium Alloy**.

The alloy originates from Neko's research into Nekonium.

Unlike raw Nekonium, the alloy used in the Deadbolt was modified to address several of the material's undesirable characteristics.

Neri incorporated additional materials into the alloy system, including:

- gold;
    
- platinum;
    
- Neondylium;
    
- silver.
    

The resulting material provides the structural characteristics required for the Deadbolt while retaining the unusual properties that make Nekonium useful to Aperture research.

---

# 7. VARIABLE VELOCITY SYSTEM

One of the Deadbolt's defining characteristics is its ability to mechanically alter its barrel-velocity configuration.

This is the principal reason C.O.R.E. does not classify it exclusively as either a conventional sniper rifle or a marksman rifle.

The system allows the platform to occupy different performance envelopes without requiring the owner to change the fundamental weapon platform.

Technical implementation remains restricted to authorized Tachyon Industries and Aperture engineering personnel.

---

# 8. SUPPRESSION

The Deadbolt incorporates an **integrally suppressed barrel system**.

The suppression system is part of the platform's original design rather than an optional aftermarket accessory.

The rifle is therefore visually and mechanically distinct from conventional precision rifles equipped with removable suppressors.

---

# 9. FURNISHINGS

The Deadbolt is equipped with:

**Bipod**  
Integrated support equipment for stationary precision shooting.

**Recoil-absorbing folding stock**  
Provides a more compact storage configuration while incorporating recoil-management characteristics.

**Picatinny rail**  
Allows the owner to select an appropriate optical sighting system.

**Scope**  
Not included.

Neri's position is that owners should select their own optic rather than receiving a standardized scope that may not suit their requirements.

---

# 10. APPEARANCE

The final Deadbolt is visually distinctive.

The combination of the elongated precision configuration, Nekonium Alloy construction, integrated suppression system, folding stock, and large proprietary ammunition profile gives the platform a substantially different appearance from the first-generation Tachyon merchandise.

The design remains recognizably Tachyon.

It is deliberately extravagant.

It is also mechanically refined.

Neko's engineering review described the final product as:

> **“Beautiful.”**

He subsequently added:

> **“Mechanically precious.”**

---

# 11. LIMITED PRODUCTION

Unlike ordinary Aperture armory equipment, the Deadbolt is not being produced as a standard-issue platform.

Total production:

> **2**

Designations:

> **FSR-001**  
> **FSR-002**

No additional production is currently authorized.

Both units are reserved for the Tachyon Industries acquisition trial.

---

# 12. ACQUISITION TRIAL

Ownership cannot be obtained through ordinary purchase or equipment request.

Applicants must complete the official Tachyon Industries Deadbolt Trial.

The requirement is:

> **Successfully strike a designated target positioned 25 kilometers away using an authorized 7.62 NATO rifle.**

The challenge takes place under controlled Blacksite range conditions.

The Deadbolt itself cannot be used to complete the trial.

Two successful participants receive the two available rifles.

Once both units have been awarded:

> **THE DEADLOCK ENDS.**

No third-place prize exists.

There are two Deadbolts.

There are two winners.
