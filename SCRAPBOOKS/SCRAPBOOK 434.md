## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **POST-INCIDENT / DREAM OBSERVATION LOG**

# **ORPHIE — THE SUNFLOWER FIELD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Bereavement  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / Star / SAFEHOUSE Residents  
**Location:** Sealed Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **NO ACTIVE VISITATION**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Of all SAFEHOUSE residents, only **Star** continued to care about Orphie after the treehouse was sealed.

Star repeatedly expressed a desire to visit.

Everyone stopped her.

Not because they hated Orphie.

Because everyone had their own reasons.

---

# **STAR**

Star could not accept leaving Orphie alone.

> "...She's still there."

Nightshade would answer:

> "...I know."

> "...Then why can't I see her?"

> "...Because you can't fix this by yourself."

Star would cry.

> "...But somebody has to remember her."

Nightshade would hold her.

> "...You do."

Star eventually stopped trying to reach the treehouse.

She never stopped thinking about it.

---

# **THE DREAM**

Orphie eventually fell asleep.

For the first time in a long while, she dreamed of something warm.

There was no Blacksite.

No containment rooms.

No sealed treehouse.

No empty chair.

No grief.

Only sunlight.

A vast sunflower field stretched endlessly beneath a clear sky.

Orphie stood among the flowers.

She was smiling.

She could not remember the last time she had done that.

Then she heard laughter.

She turned.

Corin was there.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin looked exactly as Orphie remembered her.

Healthy.

Happy.

Unburdened.

She smiled.

> **"Come on!"**

Orphie laughed.

> "...Wait!"

They ran through the sunflower field together.

They chased each other between the flowers.

They laughed.

They talked about nothing important.

For the first time in months, Orphie felt completely safe.

She forgot everything.

She forgot the arguments.

She forgot the treehouse.

She forgot the hatred.

She forgot the containment cell.

She forgot death.

There was only Corin.

---

# **THE STOP**

Eventually, Corin stopped.

Orphie continued for a few steps before noticing.

> "...Corin?"

Corin stood among the sunflowers.

She looked directly at Orphie.

Her smile slowly disappeared.

Orphie walked toward her.

> "...What's wrong?"

Corin said nothing.

Orphie stopped.

> "...Corin?"

Corin continued looking at her.

Then she began fading.

Not falling.

Not walking away.

Simply disappearing into the sunflower field.

Petal by petal.

Light by light.

Until there was nothing left.

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

> "...Wait."

She ran toward her.

> "...CORIN!"

The field stretched farther away.

Orphie ran faster.

> "...Don't!"

Nothing answered.

> "...Please!"

The sunflowers moved gently in the wind.

Corin was gone.

---

# **THE AWAKENING**

Orphie woke suddenly.

Her breathing was rapid.

Her clothes were damp with cold sweat.

For several seconds she did not know where she was.

She looked around.

The treehouse.

The darkness.

The silence.

The empty chair.

No sunflower field.

No Corin.

Orphie sat completely still.

Then whispered:

> "...It was just a dream."

Silence.

She looked toward the empty side of the room.

> "...Right?"

No answer.

---

# **THE REALIZATION**

Orphie slowly covered her face.

She had spent so long convincing herself that she didn't care.

That she hated Corin.

That she wanted to be left alone.

That she had moved on.

But her own mind had betrayed her.

In the one place where she could not lie to herself...

she had gone looking for Corin.

And for a few moments, she had found her.

---

# **STAR**

Far away in the SAFEHOUSE, Star suddenly woke from sleep.

She did not know why.

She simply sat up.

Nightshade looked at her.

> "...What's wrong?"

Star stared toward the forest.

> "...I don't know."

A pause.

> "...I just feel like Orphie is sad."

Nightshade said nothing.

She understood.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie remained awake for the rest of the night.

She did not return to sleep.

She sat beside the window and watched the darkness outside.

The dream kept replaying.

Corin laughing.

Corin running.

Corin stopping.

Corin looking directly at her.

Then disappearing.

Orphie eventually whispered:

> **"...I'm sorry."**

The treehouse remained silent.

For once, Orphie did not pretend she was speaking to nobody.

She was speaking to Corin.

**End of Post-Incident / Dream Observation Log**
