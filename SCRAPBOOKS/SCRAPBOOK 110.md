# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CRITICAL MEDICAL INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – RETURN OF AE-LK-01 INTERACTION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **SUMMARY**

Following the temporary separation between Corin Lockwood and **AE-LK-01 "Lumi"**, the entity returned to KCA Headquarters after determining that Corin's isolation had become harmful to her recovery.

The reunion did not occur under ideal circumstances.

Instead, Lumi discovered Corin in a severe medical crisis inside her office.

The incident became the first confirmed case where Lumi's absence directly correlated with a decline in Corin's daily stability.

---

# **INCIDENT REPORT**

**Location:** KCA Headquarters – Corin Lockwood's Office

**Time:** 03:26

Lumi appeared inside Corin's office after several days of absence.

She expected to find Corin working.

She expected paperwork.

She expected the usual quiet refusal to leave.

Instead...

The office was silent.

Too silent.

Corin had collapsed after neglecting her medication and personal care for an extended period.

The emergency response system was activated immediately.

Lumi reacted before any automated system could complete its warning sequence.

---

# **LUMI'S RESPONSE**

Witnesses later described Lumi as visibly panicked.

For an entity normally characterized by calmness and patience, the reaction was unprecedented.

She called Corin's name repeatedly.

Not as an anomaly.

Not as a researcher.

Not as a ghost.

As a mother.

> "Corin."

> "Sweetheart."

> "Please."

> "Stay with me."

Lumi immediately moved Corin to Medical Services.

Despite being an Ether-superseded ghost, Lumi demonstrated enough physical interaction capability to assist with the emergency evacuation.

Medical staff noted that Lumi did not leave Corin's side during treatment.

---

# **MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

Corin regained consciousness after receiving treatment.

The first thing she noticed was Lumi.

Not the doctors.

Not the room.

Not the equipment.

Her mother.

For several seconds, Corin remained silent.

Then she spoke quietly.

> "...Sorry."

Lumi froze.

The medical staff present recorded that Corin's first instinct after waking was not fear.

Not anger.

Not confusion.

An apology.

---

# **CONVERSATION**

Lumi immediately responded.

> "No."

Corin looked away.

> "...I caused trouble."

Lumi shook her head.

> "Corin."

A pause.

> "Look at me."

Corin eventually did.

Lumi's expression was not angry.

Only hurt.

> "You don't apologize for needing help."

Corin remained silent.

> "You don't have to earn the right to be cared for."

Another pause.

> "You never did."

---

# **DISCHARGE**

After medical evaluation, Corin was cleared to leave under continued observation.

Medical staff recommended rest.

The executive team recommended returning to SAFEHOUSE.

Belle was notified.

Norma was notified.

Arcturus was notified.

Everyone expected Corin to finally accept support.

Instead...

The moment she was dismissed...

She returned to her office.

---

# **AFTERMATH**

The decision shocked many personnel.

After everything that happened...

After Lumi returned.

After everyone was reminded how fragile Corin's situation had become...

She still walked back to the same room.

The same desk.

The same isolation.

The same routine.

However, one detail had changed.

Lumi followed.

Not forcing.

Not demanding.

Not telling her to leave.

She simply walked beside her.

---

# **CORIN'S OFFICE**

Corin sat down at her desk.

The reports were still there.

The lights were still on.

Nothing had changed.

Except this time...

There was someone else in the room.

Lumi sat nearby.

Quietly.

Patiently.

After several minutes, Corin finally spoke.

> "...You're not going to tell me to go back?"

Lumi smiled sadly.

> "I want you to."

Corin looked down.

Lumi continued.

> "But I want you to come back because you want to."

> "Not because you think you have to."

Silence.

Then:

> "For now..."

> "I'll stay."

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the incident report personally.

His comment was brief.

> "Corin's greatest danger is not that she does not care about others."

> "It is that she believes others should not have to care about her."

He continued:

> "Recovery cannot be forced."

> "But neither can someone be left alone while learning how to accept it."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin remains assigned to KCA Accounting duties under adjusted wellness protocols.

Lumi has resumed regular presence within Corin's office.

She no longer focuses solely on convincing Corin to return to SAFEHOUSE.

Instead, she focuses on the immediate things Corin struggles with most:

- Taking medication.
    
- Eating regularly.
    
- Resting.
    
- Speaking when something is wrong.
    
- Remembering that she is still alive.
    

The distance between Corin and the people who love her remains.

But for the first time in several days...

She is no longer facing it alone.

**End of Critical Medical Incident Report**
