# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / POST-RECOVERY BEHAVIORAL LOG**

# **SHE STILL SITS OUTSIDE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **RECOVERING / CHRONIC COLD RESPONSE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's body has largely recovered from the immediate effects of her year-long disappearance.

Her behavior has not.

The prolonged exposure to the mountain's extreme cold appears to have left her with a persistent tendency to shiver.

Medical personnel have found no current environmental explanation sufficient to account for the severity of the response.

Even in comfortable temperatures, Corin frequently trembles.

Her response has become habitual.

---

# **THE WINGS**

Corin almost constantly keeps her wings wrapped around herself.

When sitting:

**Wings around her body.**

When sleeping:

**Wings around her body.**

When standing still:

**Wings around her body.**

When someone approaches:

**Wings around her body.**

The posture appears automatic.

She does not seem to consciously decide to do it anymore.

Her wings have effectively become her blanket.

---

# **A YEAR-LONG HABIT**

Personnel believe the behavior developed during the months she spent alone.

Corin had no blanket.

No heater.

No shelter capable of protecting her completely from the mountain storms.

She had only herself.

And her wings.

She learned to sleep wrapped inside them.

She learned to wait wrapped inside them.

She learned to cry inside them.

Now that she is safe, the habit remains.

---

# **THE BED**

Corin has a bed.

It is warm.

It is clean.

It has several blankets.

The room is temperature controlled.

She rarely uses it.

Instead, Corin frequently sits outside the Treehouse.

---

# **THE FOOD**

Warm meals are available.

Corin's favorite foods can be prepared.

She eats more consistently now.

But she often carries the food outside.

She sits beneath the tree.

Wings wrapped around herself.

Eating slowly.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie frequently sits beside her.

Sometimes she brings another blanket.

Corin usually accepts it.

But she still keeps her wings wrapped around herself underneath it.

Orphie once asked:

> **"Why don't you go inside?"**

Corin answered:

> **"I like it here."**

Orphie looked at the snow.

> **"It's cold."**

Corin quietly replied:

> **"I know."**

---

# **THE BEHAVIOR**

Personnel have compared Corin's current behavior to that of a stray animal.

Not because she is being treated as one.

Quite the opposite.

She has:

- A bed.
    
- Food.
    
- Water.
    
- Warmth.
    
- Medical care.
    
- Family.
    
- Friends.
    
- Orphie.
    

Yet she continues sitting outside.

Waiting.

Watching.

Wrapped in her wings.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

The irony has not been lost on anyone.

For months, Corin wanted nothing more than to return to the Treehouse.

Now that she can...

she sits outside it.

She does not appear to fear the building.

She simply seems unable to make herself enter.

---

# **ORPHIE'S OBSERVATION**

Orphie once found Corin asleep outside.

Snow had accumulated around her.

Her wings were tightly wrapped around herself.

Orphie carefully placed a blanket over her.

Corin did not wake.

Orphie sat beside her until morning.

---

# **THE COLD**

Medical examinations have confirmed that Corin is not currently suffering from untreated hypothermia.

Her body temperature remains within survivable parameters.

The shivering appears to have become a persistent neurological and behavioral response.

In simpler terms:

Her body remembers.

---

# **THE WINGS AS MEMORY**

C.O.R.E. believes the wings may serve more than a physical purpose now.

They are associated with:

**Safety.**

**Warmth.**

**Isolation.**

**Waiting.**

**Survival.**

Corin spent months using them to survive the cold.

Now she uses them even when she doesn't need to.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin has a warm bed.

She has warm food.

She has blankets.

She has people who love her.

She has Orphie.

And yet she still goes outside.

She sits beneath the tree like she did before.

Her wings wrap tightly around her body.

She shivers.

Sometimes she eats.

Sometimes she sleeps.

Sometimes she simply stares into the distance.

A year of cold taught her body something it has not forgotten.

Even when the world becomes warm again...

part of Corin still expects the cold.

So she keeps her wings wrapped around herself.

Not because she has nowhere warm to go.

Because for a very long time...

**her wings were the only place she had.**

**End of Corin / Post-Recovery Behavioral Log**
