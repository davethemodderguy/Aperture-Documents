# SAFEHOUSE BEHAVIORAL FILE

## NERI — PSYCHOLOGICAL INTIMIDATION AND ANTI-BULLYING TACTICS

**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL — BEHAVIORAL / WELFARE  
**Subject:** Neri  
**Age:** 10  
**Known Expertise:** Psychological intimidation, threat perception, social manipulation  
**Primary Application:** Anti-bullying  
**Risk Level:** HIGH if deliberately provoked

---

# 1. Background

Before her rescue, Neri attended school during the period in which her younger sister was still alive.

Despite her extremely difficult home life, Neri developed an unusual understanding of social behavior.

She learned how bullies behaved.

She learned how they chose targets.

She learned how intimidation worked.

More importantly, she learned that **fear does not necessarily require an actual attack.**

A person can be made to feel threatened simply by convincing them that something dangerous is about to happen.

Neri calls this:

> **“An overwhelming sense of incoming danger.”**

---

# 2. Neri's Explanation

During an informal conversation, Neri was asked where she learned her intimidation techniques.

She responded:

> **“School.”**

The interviewer asked why she needed them.

Neri answered:

> **“Bullies.”**

After receiving a slice of cake, Neri became considerably more willing to discuss the subject.

She explained:

> **“You don't always have to fight them.”**

> **“Sometimes you just make them think they're about to get hit.”**

The interviewer asked how.

Neri smiled.

> **“Lots of ways.”**

A pause.

> **“It helps with bullies.”**

---

# 3. Psychological Principle

Neri's methods generally revolve around manipulating a person's perception of uncertainty.

She understands that people become increasingly uncomfortable when they cannot determine:

- Where danger is coming from.
    
- Whether they are being watched.
    
- What someone is capable of.
    
- How close the threat is.
    
- Whether an apparently harmless action has another meaning.
    
- Whether they have already crossed a line.
    

Neri does not necessarily need to make a direct threat.

Sometimes she simply makes the bully believe that **something is about to happen**.

This can be enough to make them leave.

---

# 4. Why Neri Learned This

Neri's hatred of bullying is particularly strong.

She does not regard bullying as harmless teasing.

To Neri, bullying is a familiar pattern.

Someone identifies a weaker person.

They isolate them.

They frighten them.

They make them feel powerless.

Neri knows exactly what that feels like.

Consequently, she has little patience for anyone attempting to reproduce that dynamic around her.

Especially toward her friends.

---

# 5. The Neri Escalation Pattern

Personnel familiar with Neri describe her response as approximately:

**Bullying detected → intimidation → warning → confrontation → violence if threat persists**

The important distinction is that violence is **not her first response**.

Neri can scare someone away without touching them.

This is generally the preferred outcome.

If the bully leaves, Neri considers the problem solved.

If they continue—

The situation becomes considerably more serious.

---

# 6. The Hairclips

The two crossed clips holding Neri's bangs are commonly mistaken for ordinary paper clips or hair accessories.

They are not.

They are approximately finger-sized, extremely sharp concealed blades.

Neri can remove them quickly.

They are one of several concealed weapons available to her.

This is why personnel should not attempt to grab her hair, hairclips, or other accessories.

There is no reason to test whether the rumors are exaggerated.

---

# 7. Concealed Weapons

The hairclips are also **not** the extent of Neri's armament.

Neri has demonstrated an extraordinary ability to conceal knives and other sharp implements throughout her clothing and accessories.

The exact number is unknown.

It has been deliberately left unknown.

Attempts to determine the total number have produced no useful operational benefit and have mostly resulted in Neri asking why anyone cares.

The standing policy is therefore:

> **Do not count Neri's knives.**

---

# 8. Critical Warning

New personnel are reminded:

**Do not bully Neri.**

More importantly:

**Do not bully Neri's friends.**

Neri is not indiscriminately violent.

She is, however, extremely protective.

A person who harasses her may simply receive an intimidating stare.

A person who repeatedly bullies her may be frightened away.

A person who seriously threatens one of her friends may encounter a much more dangerous version of Neri.

Personnel should never deliberately test the boundary.

---

# 9. The Cake Interview

**INTERVIEWER:**  
“So these tactics are mostly psychological?”

**NERI:**  
“Yeah.”

**INTERVIEWER:**  
“And you learned them to protect yourself?”

Neri paused.

> **“And my sister.”**

Silence.

The interviewer did not continue that line of questioning.

Neri took another bite of cake.

After a moment:

> **“It worked sometimes.”**

**INTERVIEWER:**  
“Did you ever have to hurt anyone?”

Neri shrugged.

> **“Sometimes they didn't listen.”**

The interviewer looked at the cake.

Neri noticed.

> **“Can I have another piece?”**

**INTERVIEWER:**  
“...Yes.”

Neri smiled.

> **“Then I'll tell you another one.”**

---

# 10. Staff Guidance

Neri should not be treated as a hostile individual merely because she possesses these skills.

She is a child who learned defensive social tactics in an environment where intimidation was commonplace.

SAFEHOUSE's objective is to ensure she no longer needs those tactics to feel safe.

However, personnel should respect the capability.

### Recommended:

- Treat Neri respectfully.
    
- Treat her friends respectfully.
    
- De-escalate conflicts.
    
- Give her space when requested.
    
- Report genuine threats through normal channels.
    
- Allow Neri to disengage from confrontations when possible.
    

### Absolutely Not Recommended:

- Bullying her.
    
- Bullying her friends.
    
- Challenging her to “prove” the rumors.
    
- Trying to discover her concealed weapons.
    
- Taking her belongings.
    
- Threatening her friends as a joke.
    
- Assuming her small size makes her harmless.
    

---

# 11. Final Note

Neri's greatest talent is not actually knowing how to hurt someone.

It is knowing **how to make someone decide they don't want to find out what happens next.**

That distinction matters.

She learned it because she hated being powerless.

Now SAFEHOUSE gives her something she did not have back then:

People who will intervene before she has to become frightening.

Still, the simplest solution remains the best one.

> **Don't be a bully.**

If Neri is sitting in the cafeteria eating cake, leave her alone.

If she's walking around with her friends, leave them alone.

If she gives you an intimidating look, consider what you did to deserve it.

And if you are thinking about testing whether those harmless-looking hairclips are actually knives—

**don't.**
