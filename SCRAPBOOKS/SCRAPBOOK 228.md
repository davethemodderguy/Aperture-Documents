# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL ADDENDUM**

## **SUBJECT: COREY LOCKWOOD – FAMILIAL ADDRESS AND UNRESOLVED GRIEF**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical & Executive Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although Corey Lockwood has integrated exceptionally well into both Project SAFEHOUSE and the Lockwood family, Psychological Services have documented one consistent behavior since her arrival.

Corey does not use familial titles when addressing members of the Lockwood household.

Instead, she addresses them exclusively by name.

This behavior has remained unchanged despite otherwise strong emotional bonds.

---

# **OBSERVATIONS**

During routine interactions, Corey consistently uses the following forms of address:

- "Lumi."
    
- "Arcturus."
    
- "Norma."
    

She has never been recorded referring to them as:

- Mom.
    
- Big Bro.
    
- Big Sis.
    

No one within the family has attempted to correct her.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION**

Interviews suggest this behavior is not rooted in distrust or emotional distance.

Corey openly accepts affection from the family.

She attends family gatherings.

She participates in photographs.

She celebrates holidays alongside them.

She has repeatedly described the Lockwoods as "my family."

However, when asked why she avoids familial titles, Corey remained silent for nearly a minute before quietly answering:

> "...Those words already belong to someone."

No further explanation was given.

Medical Services elected not to press the interview.

---

# **GRIEF WITHOUT CONFLICT**

Corey has never compared the two families against one another.

She has never implied that one could replace the other.

Psychological Services believe Corey does not view family as interchangeable.

To her, accepting love from this universe does not erase the existence of the people she lost in Universe C-02.

Using the same titles feels, to her, as though she would be abandoning their memory.

This interpretation remains a working hypothesis.

Corey has neither confirmed nor denied it.

---

# **FAMILY RESPONSE**

No member of the Lockwood family has expressed disappointment regarding Corey's choice of words.

---

**Lumi**

When Medical Services asked whether the omission hurt her feelings, Lumi smiled gently.

> "No."

She looked toward Corey, who was laughing with Corin in the distance.

> "She doesn't have to call me 'Mom' for me to love her."

After a brief pause, Lumi added,

> "If one day she does..."

> "...I'll treasure it."

> "If she never does..."

> "...I'll treasure her anyway."

---

**Arcturus**

> "Titles don't make a family."

> "Showing up does."

---

**Norma**

> "She's healing."

> "Healing doesn't follow our schedule."

---

# **CORIN**

Corin has never encouraged Corey to change how she addresses the family.

When asked why, Corin answered simply:

> "...She'll know when she's ready."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Psychological Services consider Corey's use of names rather than familial titles to be a reflection of unresolved bereavement rather than rejection.

Her grief has not prevented her from forming new relationships.

It has only prevented her from replacing old ones.

Medical staff recommend that personnel continue allowing Corey to determine, in her own time, how she wishes to express those relationships.

Some wounds do not close because someone new arrives.

Sometimes they simply become easier to carry.

For now...

"Lumi."

"Arcturus."

"Norma."

Those are the names Corey can say without her voice breaking.

And for the Lockwood family...

That is enough.

**End of Psychological Addendum**
