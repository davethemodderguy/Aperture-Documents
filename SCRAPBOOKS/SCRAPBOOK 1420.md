# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Obol Squad recently visited the Tachyon Industries Merchandise Store to discuss available equipment and merchandise.

The visit was originally intended to be informal.

It was not.

Within approximately ten minutes, the six members of Obol Squad had collectively accumulated a list of requested merchandise, equipment, clothing, accessories, and several items that Tachyon Industries had apparently never intended to manufacture.

At the time of the visit, **Jake was not with the Obol Squad.**

He was spending time with **Nora Tachyon** elsewhere in the SAFEHOUSE.

This was significant.

Nora had apparently decided that Jake needed company.

Jake, still unable to speak and recovering from his Nullwalker state, remained with her throughout the merchandise discussion.

Meanwhile, the Obol Squad negotiated.

---

# 1. ARRIVAL

The six members entered the Tachyon Industries store together.

Anby walked at the front.

Twiggy followed.

Solider 11, Sora, Phaethon, and Iris followed behind.

The store was being staffed by **Nora Tachyon**, who had previously worked there as a shop assistant.

However, Nora was not behind the counter that day.

She was elsewhere with Jake.

Neri was therefore handling the store.

She looked up.

She saw six nearly identical artificial humans.

Then looked at their hairclips.

Then at the rapidly growing list in Solider 11's hand.

> **NERI:**  
> “...You have a list.”

Solider 11 nodded.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> “Yes.”

Neri looked at the list.

It was already several pages long.

> **NERI:**  
> “Why?”

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> “Merchandise.”

Neri stared at her.

> **NERI:**  
> “I know.”

---

# 2. IDENTIFICATION PROBLEM

Neri initially had difficulty remembering which sister was which.

This was not unexpected.

All six share extremely similar physical appearances.

The hairclips remain the primary visual identifier.

Neri therefore checked each one.

> **NERI:**  
> “Anby.”

Anby raised her hand.

> **NERI:**  
> “Twiggy.”

Twiggy nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Solider 11.”

She nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Sora.”

A nod.

> **NERI:**  
> “Phaethon.”

A nod.

> **NERI:**  
> “Iris.”

Iris raised two fingers.

> **IRIS:**  
> “Present.”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Okay.”

---

# 3. FIRST REQUEST — ANBY

Anby approached the counter.

She was holding a small notebook.

Neri looked at it.

> **NERI:**  
> “What do you want?”

Anby pointed at several Tachyon Industries products.

Then at herself.

Then at the products again.

> **ANBY:**  
> “Those.”

Neri looked at the selection.

> **NERI:**  
> “Which ones?”

Anby quietly listed several items.

Her voice was soft enough that Neri had to lean forward slightly.

Neri wrote them down.

> **NERI:**  
> “Anything else?”

Anby thought.

Then:

> **ANBY:**  
> “Cheeseburger.”

Neri paused.

> **NERI:**  
> “That's food.”

Anby nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “We don't sell cheeseburgers.”

Anby looked disappointed.

---

# 4. PHOTON BLADES

Anby eventually clarified her primary equipment request.

She wanted:

> **Two Photon Blades.**

The request was forwarded for Tachyon Industries equipment approval.

The weapons were not treated as ordinary merchandise.

They were categorized as functional equipment for an Obol Squad member.

Neri documented the request.

Anby quietly watched.

> **NERI:**  
> “You really want two?”

Anby nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Okay.”

Anby smiled.

---

# 5. SOLIDER 11

Solider 11 approached next.

Her list was considerably more practical.

She requested:

> **One Heavy Machete.**

Neri wrote it down.

> **NERI:**  
> “Anything else?”

Solider 11 considered the question.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> “Potato salad.”

Neri looked at her.

> **NERI:**  
> “That's not merchandise.”

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> “It should be.”

Neri stared for several seconds.

Then wrote something else down.

---

# 6. TWIGGY

Twiggy's requests were considerably less weapon-oriented.

Her primary interest was medical and utility equipment.

She explained that Jake's recovery had made her particularly interested in compact medical supplies that could be carried without interfering with normal movement.

Neri listened.

> **NERI:**  
> “For yourself?”

Twiggy nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Or Jake?”

Twiggy paused.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> “Both.”

Neri nodded.

That answer required no further clarification.

---

# 7. SORA

Sora approached with considerably more enthusiasm than expected.

Her request was:

> **Magazine-Fed Shotgun — Nekonium Magnum Decimator.**

Neri raised an eyebrow.

> **NERI:**  
> “You want the M4 one?”

Sora nodded.

> **SORA:**  
> “Yes.”

> **NERI:**  
> “12-gauge Nekonium shells.”

Another nod.

> **SORA:**  
> “Yes.”

> **NERI:**  
> “Anything else?”

> **SORA:**  
> “Something to eat while looking at stars.”

> **NERI:**  
> “That's not a product.”

> **SORA:**  
> “It should be.”

Neri sighed.

---

# 8. PHAETHON

Phaethon requested:

> **One Heavy Compound Bow.**

Her other requests were significantly less tactical.

She asked whether Tachyon Industries had any food merchandise involving:

- apples;
    
- pineapple;
    
- apple filling;
    
- pineapple filling;
    
- or both.
    

Neri looked through the store inventory.

There were several food products.

Phaethon inspected them carefully.

She selected one containing apple.

Then another containing pineapple.

Then one containing both.

Neri said nothing.

---

# 10. ELSEWHERE — NORA AND JAKE

While the merchandise discussion was taking place, Nora was sitting with Jake.

Jake was still recovering.

He was wrapped in a blanket.

His teddy bear rested against his chest.

His voice remained completely absent.

Nora had apparently decided that this did not make conversation impossible.

She simply talked.

A lot.

> **NORA:**  
> “So then Neri said the thing was probably chemically unstable, but I think she just didn't like the smell.”

Jake looked at her.

Nora continued.

> **NORA:**  
> “And then Elsher said—”

Jake pointed toward her.

Nora stopped.

> **NORA:**  
> “What?”

Jake pointed at her.

Then at his own throat.

Then shook his head.

Nora understood.

> **NORA:**  
> “Oh. Sorry. I forgot.”

Jake nodded.

Nora smiled.

> **NORA:**  
> “You can just listen.”

Jake nodded again.

Nora continued talking.

---

# 11. JAKE'S FAVORITE PART

Jake appeared particularly comfortable around Nora.

She was energetic enough to keep the silence from becoming uncomfortable.

She did not require him to answer.

Whenever she asked him a question, he could simply nod or shake his head.

At one point:

> **NORA:**  
> “Do you think Anby is going to buy something ridiculous?”

Jake immediately nodded.

Nora laughed.

> **NORA:**  
> “Yeah. Me too.”

Jake smiled.

---

Then at the six Obol members.

> “That's worse.”

# 15. JAKE RETURNS

Later, Nora and Jake returned.

Jake was still being carried.

Nora entered first.

The Obol Squad immediately turned toward him.

Jake looked at the collection of merchandise.

Then at the six sisters.

Then at Neri.

He raised an eyebrow.

Anby held up a small Tachyon Industries package.

Jake pointed at it.

Anby nodded.

She walked over and handed it to him.

Inside was a small star-themed Tachyon Industries plush.

Jake stared at it.

Then looked at Anby.

He hugged it.

Anby smiled.

> **ANBY:**  
> “For you.”

Jake nodded.

Then he reached toward Anby.

She crouched.

Jake hugged her.

Anby froze for half a second.

Then hugged him back.

---

# 16. NORA'S COMMENT

Nora watched the interaction.

She smiled.

> **NORA:**  
> “He likes it.”

Jake nodded.

Nora looked at Anby.

> **NORA:**  
> “He likes you too.”

Anby immediately turned red.

> **ANBY:**  
> “I know.”

Jake looked at her.

Then gave a tiny thumbs-up.

Anby became even more embarrassed.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The merchandise discussion was ultimately successful.

The Obol Squad acquired several requested items.

Tachyon Industries gained another group of satisfied customers.

Neri gained a considerably longer inventory list.

Iris gained one Nitro Fuel.

Phaethon gained several fruit-based snacks.

Sora gained a shotgun and apparently continued looking for something to eat beneath the stars.

Anby gained her Photon Blades.

And Jake gained a small star plush.

The most important observation, however, was not the merchandise.

It was the fact that while the Obol Squad discussed what they wanted,

**they also remembered to ask what Jake might want.**

He couldn't tell them himself.

So they asked anyway.

And when Anby handed him the little star plush,

Jake didn't need his voice.

He hugged it.

Then hugged her.

That was answer enough.
