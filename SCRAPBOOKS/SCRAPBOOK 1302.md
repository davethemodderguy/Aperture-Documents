# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### ANOMALOUS MEDICAL & RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-ANM-4C-JAKE-141  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESTRICTED — CRITICAL RESIDENT WELFARE  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE INCIDENT  
**SUBJECT:** Jake — Prolonged Unintentional Confinement  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE / SECTOR 4-C

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake was discovered inside a storage locker after being confined there for **more than five consecutive hours.**

The circumstances remain under investigation.

At present, there is no evidence that Jake entered the locker intending to remain inside for that duration.

It appears he entered the storage area while attempting to isolate himself.

The locker door subsequently became closed and could not be opened from the interior.

Jake did not call for assistance.

He did not attempt to attract attention.

He remained inside.

For over five hours.

His teddy bear remained with him.

---

# 2. DISCOVERY

The locker was discovered at approximately 17:41 during a routine inventory check.

A faint sound was detected from inside.

Not knocking.

Not shouting.

A heartbeat.

An irregular one.

The storage area was initially assumed to contain an active anomalous device.

Personnel opened the locker.

Jake was inside.

He was sitting on the floor with his knees pulled toward his chest.

His teddy bear was wrapped around his shoulders.

His halo fragments were flickering.

His entire body was intermittently disappearing.

He looked up when the door opened.

He did not immediately move.

---

# 3. INITIAL CONDITION

Jake's condition was significantly worse than his documented baseline.

Observed symptoms included:

- severe weakness;
    
- continuous manifestation instability;
    
- irregular cardiac rhythm;
    
- persistent pain;
    
- dehydration-like manifestation symptoms;
    
- trembling;
    
- reduced responsiveness;
    
- extreme fatigue;
    
- difficulty maintaining a stable posture;
    
- heightened fear response.
    

The teddy bear's heartbeat was extremely irregular.

**THUMP.**

...

...

**THUMP-THUMP.**

...

**THUMP.**

Then nothing for several seconds.

Jake immediately touched the bear.

The heartbeat resumed.

---

# 4. INITIAL TRANSCRIPT

**C.O.R.E. MEDICAL:**  
"Jake?"

Jake looked up.

> **Jake:**  
> "...Hi."

**C.O.R.E. MEDICAL:**  
"Can you stand?"

Jake attempted to stand.

His legs flickered.

He fell back down.

He shook his head.

**C.O.R.E. MEDICAL:**  
"How long have you been in here?"

Jake looked toward the door.

> **Jake:**  
> "I don't know."

**C.O.R.E. MEDICAL:**  
"Do you know when you came in?"

Jake shook his head.

> **Jake:**  
> "I wanted somewhere quiet."

Pause.

> **Jake:**  
> "Then the door closed."

---

# 5. WHY HE DID NOT CALL FOR HELP

Personnel asked Jake why he had not called out.

Jake was silent.

His bear's heartbeat became faster.

Eventually:

> **Jake:**  
> "I thought someone would come."

The welfare officer paused.

> **WELFARE OFFICER:**  
> "Why didn't you call?"

Jake looked down.

> **Jake:**  
> "I didn't want to bother anyone."

This response is consistent with Jake's established tendency to suppress requests for assistance.

---

# 6. THE FIVE HOURS

The duration of confinement is considered particularly concerning.

Jake was not continuously screaming.

He did not spend five hours pounding on the door.

He appears to have spent much of the time simply sitting in the darkness.

At some point, he attempted to sleep.

He was unsuccessful.

His cardiac manifestation remained unstable.

His manifestation flickered repeatedly.

His teddy bear remained wrapped around him.

At several points, Jake appears to have spoken to the bear.

No complete conversation was recovered.

One audible phrase was captured:

> **Jake:**  
> "Stay."

The bear obviously did not answer.

Jake held it tighter.

---

# 7. PSYCHOLOGICAL RESPONSE

Jake's reaction after the door was opened was not immediate relief.

He appeared frightened by the sudden light.

He covered his face.

His manifestation flickered violently.

A medical officer lowered the lighting.

Jake gradually looked up.

He did not immediately leave the locker.

He stared at the open doorway.

Then at the hallway.

Then back into the locker.

---

# 8. AETERNA

Aeterna arrived shortly afterward.

She did not immediately touch Jake.

She knelt beside the open locker.

> **Aeterna:**  
> "Jake."

Jake looked at her.

> **Aeterna:**  
> "You're out."

Jake nodded.

He remained seated.

> **Jake:**  
> "I thought nobody knew."

Aeterna's expression remained still.

> **Aeterna:**  
> "We know now."

Jake looked at the floor.

> **Jake:**  
> "Okay."

Aeterna extended one hand.

Jake eventually reached for it.

She helped him out.

He immediately adjusted the teddy bear around his shoulders.

---

# 9. NORA

Nora was informed after Jake had been medically stabilized.

She arrived carrying a blanket.

She did not ask what happened.

She simply wrapped it around Jake.

Jake looked at her.

> **Jake:**  
> "I was stuck."

> **Nora:**  
> "I know."

> **Jake:**  
> "For a long time."

> **Nora:**  
> "I know."

Jake held his bear.

> **Jake:**  
> "I didn't like it."

Nora sat beside him.

> **Nora:**  
> "Me neither."

Jake leaned against her.

---

# 10. NERI

Neri arrived later.

She examined the locker.

Then looked at Jake.

> **Neri:**  
> "You could've called."

Jake looked away.

> **Jake:**  
> "I know."

Neri sat beside him.

A few seconds passed.

> **Neri:**  
> "Next time."

Jake looked at her.

> **Neri:**  
> "Call."

Jake nodded.

> **Jake:**  
> "Okay."

Neri remained beside him.

---

# 11. MEDICAL OBSERVATION

Jake's condition improved slowly after removal from confinement.

However, the incident produced a significant increase in manifestation instability.

His flickering continued for several hours.

The cardiac rhythm inside the teddy bear remained irregular.

Jake repeatedly checked the bear.

He would touch it.

Listen.

Wait.

Then continue.

This behavior persisted even after medical personnel confirmed that the cardiac manifestation was functioning.

---

# 12. THE LOCKER

The storage locker has been removed from service.

Its door mechanism has been inspected.

No anomalous locking mechanism was identified.

The incident appears to have resulted from an ordinary mechanical failure combined with Jake's inability or unwillingness to request assistance.

The locker will not be returned to general use until an internal emergency release is installed.

---

# 13. WELFARE ASSESSMENT

The most concerning aspect of the incident is not simply the five-hour confinement.

It is Jake's response to it.

Jake was:

**alone.**

**afraid.**

**in pain.**

**physically deteriorating.**

And yet he did not call for anyone.

He waited.

This is consistent with his increasingly severe pattern of self-isolation.

Jake hates being alone.

He has repeatedly demonstrated this.

Yet when distressed, he increasingly removes himself from everyone around him.

The locker incident demonstrates how dangerous that behavior can become.

---

# 14. NEW GUIDANCE

Jake is **not** to be physically restricted from seeking solitude.

However, personnel are now required to distinguish between:

> **Jake choosing a quiet place**

and

> **Jake becoming inaccessible.**

If Jake enters:

- storage areas;
    
- maintenance spaces;
    
- restricted rooms;
    
- isolated corridors;
    
- unmonitored utility areas;
    

personnel should ensure that he has a safe means of leaving.

This is not permission to follow him.

It is simply a safeguard against another accidental confinement.

---

# 15. POST-INCIDENT OBSERVATION

Several hours after the incident, Jake was found sitting in the SAFEHOUSE lounge.

He was surrounded by people.

He was not speaking.

His teddy bear was wrapped around him.

His heartbeat was still irregular.

Nora was sitting nearby.

Aeterna stood several meters away.

Neri was reading at another table.

Jake looked around the room.

Then quietly asked:

> **Jake:**  
> "Can I stay here?"

Nora answered first.

> **Nora:**  
> "Yes."

Jake nodded.

He remained.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake spent more than five hours locked inside a metal box.

Nobody heard him calling.

Because he wasn't calling.

He was waiting.

Waiting for someone to notice.

Waiting for someone to open the door.

Waiting with the only thing he trusted completely:

the teddy bear wrapped around his shoulders.

The most troubling part is that Jake eventually explained why he had not called for help.

Not because he didn't know how.

Not because he couldn't.

Because:

> **"I didn't want to bother anyone."**

Jake hates being alone.

But when he is afraid, he still chooses isolation.

And now we know that he may remain there for hours without asking anyone to find him.

So the new rule is simple.

We will not chase Jake.

We will not force him into rooms full of people.

We will not take away the quiet places he needs.

But we will make sure that when he hides,

**there is always a way back out.**

And if he forgets to ask for help—

someone should still be close enough to notice.

**— END INCIDENT REPORT**
