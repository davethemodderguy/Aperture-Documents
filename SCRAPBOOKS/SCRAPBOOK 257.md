# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – PSYCHOLOGICAL & HISTORICAL DOSSIER**

### **SUBJECT: DIALYN – SURVIVAL HISTORY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – Executive, Medical & Psychological Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

This document summarizes the period between Dialyn's sacrifice to the Hollow and her eventual employment with the Krampus Compliance Authority.

The complete duration remains one of the longest verified survivals within an active Hollow by an unassisted civilian.

**Confirmed Duration:** Approximately **9 years**.

Medical personnel continue to regard the case as an anomaly.

---

# **THE HOLLOW**

Following her forced sacrifice by her home village, Dialyn became trapped within the Hollow.

No rescue operation was ever organized.

No one expected her to survive.

She did.

---

# **NINE YEARS**

Resources inside the Hollow were almost nonexistent.

Food was unavailable.

Clean water was scarce.

Survival became a matter of consuming anything organic that was not immediately lethal.

Recovered testimony and medical evaluation indicate Dialyn survived by eating:

- Wild flowers.
    
- Leaves.
    
- Moss.
    
- Tree bark.
    
- Vines.
    
- Young shoots.
    
- Roots.
    
- Fungi judged safe through trial and observation.
    
- Any other organic material capable of sustaining life.
    

Psychological Services stress that these actions were not born of desperation alone.

They became routine.

For nine years, survival meant never wasting anything edible.

---

# **RESCUE**

Approximately nine years after the Hollow first appeared, a licensed Hollow Diver detected signs of human life.

Against all expectations, the survivor was a young woman.

Dialyn.

Despite prolonged Ether exposure, she remained alive.

Medical examination later confirmed an extraordinary natural resistance to Ether corruption.

The mechanism remains unknown.

---

# **AFTERMATH**

Rescue did not immediately improve Dialyn's circumstances.

With no surviving family, no village, and no official records recognizing her identity, she received little assistance.

Within a short period, she found herself living on the streets.

For the second time in her life...

She survived alone.

---

# **LIFE AFTER RESCUE**

Dialyn gradually taught herself how to live in ordinary society.

She found temporary work where she could.

Learned administrative skills.

Studied during whatever free time she had.

Never complained.

Several years later, while handling routine administrative matters, she crossed paths with two individuals.

Belle Merryweather.

Endmin Merryweather.

---

# **THE OFFER**

Personnel accounts differ regarding who first suggested hiring Dialyn.

Belle insists it was Endmin.

Endmin insists it was Belle.

Neither appears interested in settling the matter.

What is known is that the Krampus Compliance Authority offered Dialyn a position.

She accepted immediately.

Her first role was in Customer Support.

She answered hundreds of calls each day.

No one realized how extraordinary she was.

Dialyn never volunteered the information.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Dialyn no longer lives in fear of finding her next meal.

She has a home.

A steady job.

Friends who worry if she skips lunch.

A white cat that follows her everywhere.

People who call her just to ask how she's doing.

Yet some habits remain.

She still instinctively checks whether food has gone to waste.

She still occasionally pauses before flower gardens.

She still thanks people for the smallest acts of kindness.

Not because she has forgotten the Hollow.

But because she remembers what it was like to have nothing.

The Krampus Compliance Authority does not regard Dialyn as remarkable because she survived nine years inside a Hollow.

They regard her as remarkable because, after everything she endured...

She chose to become someone who helps other people survive theirs.
