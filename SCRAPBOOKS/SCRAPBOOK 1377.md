# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### MEDICAL & RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-MED-4C-JAKE-242  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — CRITICAL MEDICAL INCIDENT  
**STATUS:** RESOLVED  
**SUBJECT:** Jake  
**INCIDENT:** Prolonged separation from teddy bear / cardiac core failure

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake's teddy bear was lost for approximately:

> **5 HOURS**

The teddy bear contains Jake's actual heart and stellar cardiac core.

Jake's body can remain functional for a limited period after separation.

It cannot remain stable indefinitely.

After approximately five hours without the teddy bear, the effects of cardiac-core separation became severe.

Jake began to die.

Phoenix found him first.

She caught him as his body began to weaken and held him in her arms while his condition deteriorated.

Phoenix understood immediately what was happening.

And panicked.

---

# 2. INITIAL DISCOVERY

The teddy bear was initially believed to be somewhere inside SAFEHOUSE.

Jake had searched for it.

He could not find it.

Several residents assumed it had simply been misplaced.

Jake eventually stopped searching.

This was the first significant warning sign.

He became increasingly quiet.

His movements slowed.

His azure eyes became dimmer.

His wings disappeared.

His halo weakened.

His body temperature began dropping.

Phoenix noticed him sitting alone.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake?”

Jake looked up.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I can't find him.”

Phoenix immediately understood who he meant.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Your teddy?”

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> “He's gone.”

---

# 3. PHYSIOLOGICAL DETERIORATION

At this point, approximately five hours had elapsed since the teddy bear was last confirmed in Jake's possession.

Jake's condition deteriorated rapidly.

His breathing became shallow.

His pulse weakened.

The celestial glow around his body began fading.

His eyes became increasingly dull.

His gravitational interaction became unstable.

Phoenix immediately picked him up.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake.”

No response.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake, look at me.”

His eyes opened slightly.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I'm tired.”

Phoenix's expression changed.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “No.”

She held him tighter.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “No, no, no. Stay awake.”

---

# 4. PHOENIX PANICS

Phoenix has experienced death thousands of times.

She has watched people die.

She has treated catastrophic injuries.

She has remained calm during medical emergencies.

This time, she was not calm.

Because this was Jake.

And she knew exactly what the missing teddy bear meant.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Where is his heart?”

Nobody answered.

Phoenix looked toward the hallway.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “WHERE IS HIS TEDDY?”

Several residents began searching immediately.

Phoenix remained seated with Jake in her arms.

His head rested against her chest.

His breathing became weaker.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake?”

His eyes barely opened.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Mom...?”

Phoenix froze.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “I'm here.”

Jake's voice was barely audible.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I'm cold.”

Phoenix's hands began shaking.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “I know.”

She pulled him closer.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “We're going to find it.”

Jake did not answer.

---

# 5. FIVE HOURS

The search continued.

Five hours had already passed.

Every minute mattered.

Phoenix knew that.

She also knew that she could not simply revive Jake herself.

Her Transcendense was irrelevant.

This was not a conventional injury.

Jake's heart was physically somewhere else.

His body was dying because the thing keeping it alive was missing.

Phoenix checked his pulse.

It was weaker.

She checked again.

Weaker.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “No.”

She pressed Jake against herself.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Come on.”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Come on, Jake.”

Her voice began breaking.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Please.”

---

# 6. NEKO'S SEARCH

Neko had joined the search.

He systematically checked every location where Jake had recently been.

Eventually, he reached the laundry area.

He looked beside the washing machines.

Nothing.

He checked behind them.

Nothing.

He checked the surrounding shelves.

Then he saw something.

A familiar teddy bear.

Sitting near the washing machine.

Neko stopped.

> **NEKO:**  
> “...No way.”

He picked it up.

The teddy bear's heart was barely beating.

Neko immediately ran.

---

# 7. CARDIAC FAILURE

Back in the common room, Phoenix was still holding Jake.

Jake's eyes had almost completely lost their glow.

His breathing had become extremely shallow.

Phoenix was crying now.

She was no longer attempting to hide it.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake, please.”

No response.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Please don't do this.”

She pressed her forehead against his.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “You're not going anywhere.”

Jake's body became limp.

Phoenix panicked.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake?”

Nothing.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake!”

She checked his pulse.

Almost nothing.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “No, no, no—”

---

# 8. NEKO RETURNS

The door opened.

Neko ran inside.

He was holding the teddy bear.

> **NEKO:**  
> “PHOENIX!”

Phoenix looked up.

She saw the bear.

Her expression changed instantly.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Give it to me.”

Neko handed it over.

Phoenix placed the teddy bear against Jake.

Nothing happened.

She froze.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Come on.”

Neko remembered the emergency procedure.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Shake it.”

Phoenix looked at him.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Gently. The bear.”

Phoenix carefully shook the teddy bear.

Once.

Nothing.

Again.

The bear's heart stopped.

Phoenix's breathing stopped.

Then—

**THUMP.**

The teddy bear's heart beat.

**THUMP.**

Another beat.

**THUMP.**

Jake inhaled sharply.

His eyes lit up.

His halo flickered.

Phoenix nearly collapsed from relief.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Oh my God.”

Jake coughed.

Phoenix held him tighter.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “You're okay.”

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Found him?”

Phoenix laughed through tears.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Yes.”

Jake looked at the teddy bear.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Good.”

Then he closed his eyes.

Phoenix immediately panicked again.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake?”

Jake opened one eye.

> **JAKE:**  
> “I'm sleepy.”

Phoenix stared at him.

Then exhaled.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Don't do that to me.”

---

# 9. AFTERMATH

Jake recovered gradually after the teddy bear was reunited with his body.

His pulse stabilized.

His glow returned.

His breathing normalized.

His wings remained absent for several minutes before returning.

Phoenix did not put him down.

She continued holding him.

The teddy bear remained firmly attached to Jake's back afterward.

---

# 10. NEW SAFETY PROTOCOL

Following the incident, C.O.R.E. has implemented additional procedures.

### JAKE'S TEDDY BEAR IS NOW CLASSIFIED AS:

> **CRITICAL CARDIAC COMPONENT**

It is not classified as:

- a toy;
    
- ordinary personal property;
    
- replaceable equipment;
    
- or recreational merchandise.
    

The teddy bear must remain physically attached to Jake whenever practical.

If separated:

> **LOCATE IMMEDIATELY.**

Do not assume Jake can simply wait for it to be found.

---

# 11. PHOENIX'S RESPONSE

Phoenix submitted a medical report after the incident.

The report contained extensive medical observations.

At the bottom, however, she wrote:

> **“Five hours.”**

Underneath:

> **“Five hours without his heart.”**

Then:

> **“I thought he was going to die in my arms.”**

Several minutes later, she added:

> **“I know what dying feels like.”**

Then:

> **“I don't want him to learn it.”**

The final sentence was written significantly more heavily:

> **“Please don't lose the bear again.”**

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

For five hours, Jake's teddy bear was sitting beside a washing machine.

For five hours, nobody knew where it was.

For five hours, Jake's actual heart was separated from his body.

By the time Neko found it, Jake was already dying.

Phoenix held him while his body slowly shut down.

She panicked.

Not because she did not understand what was happening.

Because she understood **exactly** what was happening.

Then Neko arrived.

A teddy bear was placed against a dying child's chest.

The bear's heart was restarted.

And Jake breathed again.

Afterward, Phoenix refused to let go of him for a while.

Jake eventually fell asleep in her arms.

The teddy bear remained securely strapped to his back.

C.O.R.E. has learned an important lesson:

**If Jake says he lost his teddy bear, stop everything.**

Do not assume it is somewhere nearby.

Do not assume there is time.

Find the bear.

**His heart is in it.**
