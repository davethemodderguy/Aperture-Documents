### **SAFEHOUSE PERSONNEL / DAILY ACTIVITY LOG**

# **CORIN — DRESS-BASED TRANSPORTATION INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Origin:** Treehouse  
**Destination:** Heaven's Blacksite / SAFEHOUSE  
**Transport Method:** Guiding Light Rift  
**Status:** **Completely Fine / Fashionably Questionable**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin arrived at the SAFEHOUSE wearing a dress and **pink thigh-high socks**.

There was nothing unusual about the clothing itself.

The unusual part was the fact that Corin had apparently decided she simply:

> **"Didn't feel like driving."**

Instead of using a vehicle, Corin utilized the **Guiding Light rift** connecting the Treehouse and the Blacksite.

The rift, normally disguised as a glowing blue door, provided direct transportation between the two locations.

---

# **WHY THE DRESS**

Corin's explanation was remarkably straightforward.

> **"I wanted to be comfortable."**

She had no mission.

No emergency.

No formal event.

No particular reason to wear tactical clothing.

She simply wanted to wear something comfortable.

And she happened to have wings.

---

# **THE FLIGHT PROBLEM**

A brief discussion was held regarding whether Corin could simply fly to the Blacksite while wearing the dress.

The discussion ended almost immediately.

**No.**

Absolutely not.

Corin's wings generate substantial airflow while flying.

A loose dress and two large wings are not considered a particularly compatible combination.

C.O.R.E. personnel have therefore collectively agreed that:

> **Flying while wearing a dress is a terrible idea.**

---

# **TRANSPORTATION SOLUTION**

Corin instead used Guiding Light's rift.

She walked through the glowing blue door at the Treehouse.

She emerged directly inside the Blacksite.

No flight required.

No driving required.

No aerodynamic complications.

Corin subsequently walked into the SAFEHOUSE as though nothing unusual had happened.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE REACTION**

Neko looked up.

Paused.

Looked at Corin.

Then at the pink thigh highs.

Then at the dress.

Then at the wings.

> **"...You flew here?"**

Corin:

> **"No."**

Neko:

> **"Good."**

Corin pointed toward the glowing blue doorway.

> **"I used the rift."**

Neko nodded approvingly.

> **"Excellent decision."**

---

# **BELLE'S REACTION**

Belle immediately approved of the outfit.

> **"You look cute."**

Corin smiled.

> **"Thanks."**

Belle then looked at her wings.

> **"...You weren't going to fly in that, were you?"**

Corin paused.

> "...Maybe."

Belle:

> **"No."**

Corin:

> "...Okay."

---

# **ENGINEERING NOTE**

The incident demonstrates one of the more mundane benefits of the Guiding Light rift.

Although Corin possesses fully functional wings and can travel between the Treehouse and Blacksite by flight, the rift allows her to bypass the journey entirely.

This is particularly useful when:

- Corin is carrying food.
    
- Corin is wearing delicate clothing.
    
- Corin does not feel like flying.
    
- Corin does not feel like driving.
    
- Corin simply wants to walk through a glowing blue door.
    

The final reason appears to be the most common.

---

# **CURRENT TRAVEL OPTIONS**

Corin currently has several methods of traveling between the Treehouse and Blacksite:

**Vehicle:**  
Functional.

**Flight:**  
Approximately 20 minutes.

**Guiding Light Rift:**  
Nearly instantaneous.

**Walking through the blue door:**  
Corin's preferred option when she is wearing a dress.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin possesses wings.

She can fly.

She can hover.

She can perform aerial maneuvers.

She can intercept missiles.

She can apparently weaponize individual feathers.

And yet, on this particular day, she decided:

> **"I don't feel like driving."**

Then:

> **"I don't want to fly in a dress."**

So she walked through a dimensional rift instead.

C.O.R.E. considers this an entirely reasonable use of anomalous transportation infrastructure.

The SAFEHOUSE residents agree.

Especially Belle.
