# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### ARMAMENTS & MATERIALS DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-ARM-4C-NERI-276  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — ARMORY / MATERIALS  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE INVESTIGATION  
**SUBJECT:** Neri — Unauthorized Armory Material Removal  
**INCIDENT TYPE:** Research-Related Inventory Discrepancy

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neri visited the Aperture Armory.

Again.

Armory personnel initially assumed the visit was routine.

It was not.

Upon completion of the visit, the following materials were discovered missing from inventory:

- **Remington 870 TAC shotguns**
    
- **STF-12 shotguns**
    
- **KelTec KSG 12 shotguns**
    
- **5 kg raw magnesium**
    
- **12-gauge shotshells**
    
- **multiple technical blueprints**
    

The removal was not associated with an MSAC deployment.

No requisition was filed.

No authorization was requested.

No armory technician reported issuing the equipment.

The material simply...

**left.**

With Neri.

---

# 2. SECURITY FOOTAGE

Armory footage was reviewed.

Neri entered normally.

She was wearing her oversized light-azure coat.

She walked toward the shotgun storage section.

She looked at the weapons.

She looked at the storage racks.

She looked around.

She began taking notes.

The supervising armorer noticed this approximately forty seconds later.

> **ARMORER:**  
> “Neri?”

Neri looked over.

> **ARMORER:**  
> “What are you doing?”

Neri pointed at the shotguns.

> **Neri:**  
> “Research.”

The armorer apparently interpreted this as:

> **“She's looking at the shotguns.”**

This interpretation was incorrect.

---

# 3. MATERIAL REMOVAL

The first weapons disappeared from the rack.

Then another.

Then another.

The armorer eventually realized that Neri was not merely examining the equipment.

She was **taking it.**

> **ARMORER:**  
> “Wait.”

Neri stopped.

> **ARMORER:**  
> “Are you taking those?”

Neri looked at the shotgun in her hands.

Then at the armorer.

> **Neri:**  
> “Yes.”

> **ARMORER:**  
> “Why?”

Neri paused.

> **Neri:**  
> “Research.”

The armorer stared at her.

> **ARMORER:**  
> “You can't just take armory equipment.”

Neri considered this.

She then looked toward the door.

The armorer reportedly chose not to physically interfere.

---

# 5. DESTINATION

The material was subsequently traced to Neri's research area.

More specifically:

> **Neri's subspace laboratory.**

The laboratory had already been configured for experimental work.

Personnel entering the area were instructed not to disturb anything.

This instruction was immediately expanded to:

> **“Do not touch anything unless Neri explicitly tells you to.”**

---

# 6. NEKO'S RESPONSE

Neko was informed.

He stared at the inventory report.

Then at the armory supervisor.

Then back at the report.

> **Neko:**  
> “She took five kilograms of magnesium?”

> **ARMORY SUPERVISOR:**  
> “Yes.”

> **Neko:**  
> “And shotguns.”

> **ARMORY SUPERVISOR:**  
> “Yes.”

> **Neko:**  
> “And blueprints.”

> **ARMORY SUPERVISOR:**  
> “Yes.”

Neko closed his eyes.

> **Neko:**  
> “Why does she keep doing this?”

The supervisor answered:

> **“Research.”**

Neko opened his eyes.

> **Neko:**  
> “I know.”

---

# 7. NERI'S EXPLANATION

Neri was later asked to explain the removal.

She was sitting at her workstation.

Several shotgun platforms were visible nearby.

She continued writing.

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “Neri.”

Neri looked up.

> **OFFICER:**  
> “Why did you take the shotguns?”

Neri answered simply:

> **Neri:**  
> “I want to know.”

> **OFFICER:**  
> “Know what?”

Neri looked toward the weapons.

> **Neri:**  
> “How they work.”

The officer paused.

> **OFFICER:**  
> “And the magnesium?”

> **Neri:**  
> “Material.”

> **OFFICER:**  
> “The blueprints?”

Neri looked at him as though the answer should have been obvious.

> **Neri:**  
> “Blueprints.”

The interview ended shortly afterward.

---

# 8. NO IMMEDIATE MODIFICATION AUTHORIZED

C.O.R.E. has confirmed that Neri is **not** currently authorized to manufacture or modify weapons from the removed materials.

The purpose of the inventory seizure is classified as:

> **Research and theoretical analysis.**

Any subsequent development involving weapons or hazardous materials requires appropriate authorization and controlled laboratory procedures.

No personnel are to assist with weapons fabrication outside approved facilities.

# 10. ARCHIVIST OBSERVATION

This is not the first time Neri has visited the Armory for “research.”

Previous incidents established that she has a tendency to see an interesting piece of equipment and immediately become curious about it.

Her research history suggests that this curiosity is rarely superficial.

Neri does not generally take things because she wants to own them.

She takes them because she wants to **understand them.**

Unfortunately, the Armory's inventory system does not currently contain a category for:

> **“Ten-year-old chemist has become curious.”**

It probably should.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The armory supervisor has been instructed to personally verify all inventory following future Neri visits.

Neri has been informed that she must request permission before removing equipment.

She nodded.

She appeared to understand.

She then asked:

> **Neri:**  
> “Can I request it now?”

The supervisor looked toward Neko.

Neko looked toward the ceiling.

Nobody answered immediately.

The magnesium remains in Neri's laboratory.

The blueprints remain with Neri.

The shotguns remain under C.O.R.E. review.

And the Armory has, once again, learned an important lesson:

> **If Neri says “research,” check the inventory.**
