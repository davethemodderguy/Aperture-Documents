# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has begun returning to the SAFEHOUSE.

He does not return during the day.

He does not announce his presence.

He does not knock on anyone's door.

He does not attempt to contact the residents.

He appears only on nights of a **full moon**.

The manifestation occurs at approximately **00:00**.

By morning, he is gone.

The first several occurrences were interpreted as isolated anomalous events.

They are no longer considered isolated.

Jake appears to have established a pattern.

---

# 1. FIRST RECORDED RETURN

The first confirmed manifestation occurred at **00:03** on a full-moon night.

Blacksite cameras recorded a brief distortion in the SAFEHOUSE corridor.

The distortion lasted approximately 1.7 seconds.

When the image stabilized:

Jake was standing in the hallway.

He appeared almost exactly as he had before his return to stellar form.

Small.

White-haired.

Azure-eyed.

His shooting-star irises were faintly illuminated.

His wings were present.

The anomalous arrow remained embedded through his chest.

His teddy bear was absent.

He looked around.

Nobody was awake.

Jake quietly walked toward the cafeteria.

---

# 2. THE CAFETERIA

Jake entered the cafeteria at 00:11.

The room was dark.

He stood beneath the emergency lighting for several seconds.

He looked at the tables.

Then at the kitchen.

Then at the refrigerator.

He opened it.

He stared inside.

After approximately ten seconds:

> **JAKE:**  
> “...Food.”

He took several items.

No anomalous activity was detected.

No attempt was made to conceal his presence beyond remaining quiet.

Jake simply ate.

---

# 3. FOOD SELECTION

Jake appears to prefer ordinary SAFEHOUSE food.

Observed selections include:

- bread;
    
- fruit;
    
- leftover cake;
    
- sandwiches;
    
- milk;
    
- packaged snacks;
    
- rice;
    
- soup;
    
- and, on one occasion, an entire container of fries.
    

He does not appear interested in nutritional optimization.

He appears interested in:

> **Food that is already there.**

The behavior is consistent with his earlier tendency to eat grass and manifested flowers when isolated.

The difference is significant.

Jake is now choosing conventional food when it is available.

---

# 4. THE CAKE

During one occurrence, Jake found a slice of cake in the refrigerator.

He stopped.

He stared at it.

For approximately twelve seconds.

Then he picked it up.

He sat at the cafeteria table.

He ate it slowly.

At the end, he remained seated for several minutes.

The camera recorded Jake looking at the empty plate.

He quietly said:

> **JAKE:**  
> “...Still good.”

He then left.

---

# 5. MOVEMENT THROUGH SAFEHOUSE

Jake does not appear to wander aimlessly.

His movements are deliberate.

He visits:

**Cafeteria.**

**Kitchen.**

Occasionally:

**Courtyard.**

On two occasions:

**His old room.**

He does not enter the rooms of other residents.

He does not attempt to wake anyone.

He does not leave notes.

He does not interact with security systems.

He simply moves through the SAFEHOUSE like someone who still remembers where everything is.

---

# 6. HIS OLD ROOM

The most significant observation occurred at 01:42.

Jake entered his former room.

The room remained largely unchanged.

Grass still grew across the floor.

Some manifested flowers remained.

His old mattress was still present.

Several drawings remained on the walls.

Jake stood in the doorway.

He did not enter immediately.

He looked around.

Then he walked inside.

He sat on the mattress.

For approximately four minutes, he did nothing.

Then he looked toward the empty space where his teddy bear had once rested.

His expression changed.

He looked away.

---

# 7. THE TEDDY BEAR

Jake's teddy bear remains stored under resident welfare custody.

It has not been returned to him.

This was not because C.O.R.E. intended to withhold it.

Personnel simply had not determined whether Jake wanted it back.

During the fourth full-moon manifestation, Jake approached the storage location.

He stopped several meters away.

He did not attempt to retrieve the bear.

He stared at the container.

Then whispered:

> **JAKE:**  
> “You kept him.”

He remained there for approximately thirty seconds.

Then left.

---

# 8. RESIDENT AWARENESS

The most unusual feature of the phenomenon is that **no SAFEHOUSE resident has actually encountered Jake.**

The manifestations consistently occur after midnight.

Most residents are asleep.

When someone is awake, Jake appears to avoid their location.

C.O.R.E. has not determined whether this is intentional.

It is possible that Jake does not want to be seen.

It is equally possible that he simply prefers the SAFEHOUSE when it is quiet.

---

# 9. BAO

Bao has repeatedly missed Jake by several minutes.

On one occasion, she entered the cafeteria at 23:54.

Jake's manifestation occurred at 00:03.

Bao left at 00:27.

Jake remained in the kitchen until 00:41.

Neither encountered the other.

The following morning, Bao noticed that a sandwich was missing.

She assumed someone had eaten it.

She did not know who.

---

# 10. NERI

Neri has noticed the food discrepancies.

She has not yet connected them to Jake.

On one occasion, she found a small plate on the cafeteria table.

A single crumb remained.

Neri stared at it.

Then looked around.

She eventually shrugged.

She returned to her coffee.

---

# 11. NORA

Nora has slept through every confirmed manifestation.

This has been particularly unfortunate.

During one occurrence, Jake stood outside her room for approximately twenty seconds.

He looked at the door.

He raised his hand.

He almost knocked.

He stopped.

His hand lowered.

He remained there for several seconds.

Then he walked away.

Nora did not wake.

---

# 12. AETERNA

Aeterna has not encountered Jake either.

However, she has begun noticing subtle changes.

A food item missing.

A chair moved.

A hallway light activated.

A refrigerator left open.

A small amount of grass found outside Jake's old room.

She has not yet connected these events conclusively.

She suspects.

She has not confronted the possibility.

---

# 13. THE FULL MOON PATTERN

The correlation is now confirmed.

Jake manifests physically only during full moons.

Approximate pattern:

**00:00–00:10:**  
Manifestation.

**00:10–02:00:**  
Food consumption / SAFEHOUSE wandering.

**02:00–04:00:**  
Quiet activity.

**04:00–05:00:**  
Departure preparation.

**Before sunrise:**  
Physical manifestation disappears.

The blue star remains visible afterward.

---

# 14. DEPARTURE

Jake's departure is remarkably quiet.

There is no rift.

No explosion.

No flash.

No teleportation effect.

The cameras simply record him walking toward an exterior access point.

He stops.

Looks toward the sky.

Then disappears.

Seconds later, the blue star becomes marginally brighter.

By morning, Jake is no longer physically present.

---

# 15. THE FOOD PROBLEM

C.O.R.E. has officially classified the missing food as:

> **“Anomalous Resident Consumption.”**

No disciplinary action is recommended.

The food is being replaced.

A small quantity of food has now been intentionally placed in the cafeteria on full-moon nights.

No labels are attached.

No instructions are provided.

The objective is simple.

If Jake returns, food will be available.

---

# 16. IMPORTANT RESTRICTION

Personnel are **not** authorized to deliberately stay awake to intercept Jake.

Do not:

- ambush him;
    
- block his route;
    
- surround him;
    
- attempt to force conversation;
    
- activate alarms;
    
- call his name repeatedly;
    
- attempt to physically restrain him;
    
- or deliberately manipulate the lighting to reveal his presence.
    

If Jake wants to remain unseen, that preference must currently be respected.

The purpose of observation is welfare.

Not capture.

---

# 17. C.O.R.E. INTERPRETATION

Jake's behavior suggests that some connection to SAFEHOUSE remains.

He could simply remain in stellar form.

Instead, he returns.

He returns to the place where he lived.

He eats its food.

He walks its halls.

He visits his old room.

He stands outside the doors of people he once loved.

Then he leaves before anyone can see him.

The behavior is difficult to interpret as complete detachment.

If Jake truly wanted nothing to do with SAFEHOUSE, there would be little reason to return.

---

# 18. THE MOST RECENT VISIT

At 03:16 during the most recent full moon, Jake returned to the cafeteria.

He opened the refrigerator.

A plate had been left inside.

On it was:

A sandwich.

A piece of cake.

A cup of milk.

Jake stopped.

He looked at the food.

Then at the camera.

He knew the camera was there.

He stared directly into it.

For several seconds, he said nothing.

Then:

> **JAKE:**  
> “Thanks.”

He took the plate.

He sat down.

He ate.

---

# 19. AFTERWARD

Jake finished the food.

He carefully placed the plate in the sink.

He washed it.

This was unnecessary.

He did it anyway.

Before leaving, he looked toward the hallway.

He stood there for a long time.

Then quietly:

> **JAKE:**  
> “Goodnight.”

Nobody answered.

Jake walked away.

---

# FINAL ASSESSMENT

Jake is not gone.

He is not fully back.

He exists somewhere between the two.

A star in the sky.

A ghost in the halls.

A child who returns after midnight because the SAFEHOUSE still contains things he remembers.

Food.

Flowers.

Rooms.

People.

Memories.

He does not wake anyone.

He does not ask to be found.

He does not stay until morning.

He simply comes home when nobody is looking.

And then he leaves again.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The next morning, residents woke normally.

Breakfast began.

People went to work.

Doors opened.

Conversations started.

Nobody knew that, several hours earlier, Jake had been sitting alone in the cafeteria eating cake.

Nobody knew he had stood outside Nora's door.

Nobody knew he had visited his old room.

Nobody knew he had washed his plate.

Nobody knew he had said:

> **“Thanks.”**

Outside, the full moon was fading.

And high above the Blacksite—

The blue star remained.
