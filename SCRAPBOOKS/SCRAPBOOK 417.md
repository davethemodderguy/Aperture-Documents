### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — RETURN TO SOLITUDE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Concern  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Associated Personnel:** Orphie / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Marts  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **SEVERE CONCERN**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

With Orphie's disappearance into the forest, Corin has returned to living alone.

Under ordinary circumstances, solitude would not necessarily constitute an emergency.

For Corin, it is different.

Her previous emotional history makes prolonged isolation particularly dangerous.

Orphie had become more than a roommate.

She had become Corin's constant.

The person who was there when Corin returned from work.

The person who occupied the other side of the treehouse.

The person who made the building feel inhabited.

Now she is gone.

---

# **THE EMPTY TREEHOUSE**

Corin returned from work and instinctively looked toward the usual places.

Nothing.

No Orphie.

No quiet footsteps.

No voice.

No cooking.

No sketchbook.

No arguments.

No one sitting somewhere in silence.

The treehouse had become exactly what Corin had feared.

Empty.

---

# **THE ROUTINE**

Corin continued going to work.

She continued performing her duties.

She continued answering messages.

From an external perspective, very little had changed.

But the moment she returned home, the difference became obvious.

There was nobody to come home to.

She began eating alone.

Sleeping alone.

Waking alone.

Working alone.

And increasingly, sitting in silence.

---

# **CORIN'S HISTORY**

C.O.R.E. records indicate that Corin has previously experienced significant periods of emotional hardship.

Extended isolation has historically worsened her emotional state.

Orphie's presence had provided an informal stabilizing influence simply by being there.

There was no formal treatment.

No structured program.

No intervention.

Just companionship.

Now that companionship is gone.

---

# **THE FIRST NIGHT**

Corin came home late.

She unlocked the treehouse.

> "...I'm home."

Silence.

She stopped.

The words had been automatic.

She had expected no response.

Still, she had said them.

Corin stood in the doorway for several seconds.

Then quietly closed it.

---

# **THE SECOND NIGHT**

She said it again.

> "...I'm home."

Nothing.

Corin looked toward Orphie's usual place.

Empty.

She eventually sat down.

She did not turn on the television.

She did not cook.

She simply remained there.

---

# **THE THIRD NIGHT**

Corin stopped saying it.

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts have noticed Corin's change.

They remain wary of the treehouse because of their previous experiences with Orphie.

However, they have begun approaching Corin instead.

One Mart appeared at the doorway.

Corin looked at it.

The Mart hesitated.

> "...Not Orphie."

Corin nodded.

> "...No."

The Mart entered.

It sat beside her.

Neither said anything.

---

# **THE CONTRADICTION**

The Marts are frightened of Orphie.

They are not frightened of Corin.

This has created an unusual situation.

The creatures that once tried to care for Orphie are now quietly beginning to care for Corin.

Not because they have forgotten Orphie.

Because they can see that Corin is alone.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The SAFEHOUSE residents have also noticed.

Star has suggested inviting Corin to stay at SAFEHOUSE temporarily.

Nightshade has offered to accompany her.

Bao has offered food.

Astral has offered quiet company.

Nobody has forced her to accept.

They know what happened when people ignored Orphie's boundaries.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

When asked whether she wanted to move temporarily:

> "...No."

> "...Why?"

> "...This is home."

A pause.

> "...Or it was."

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

Corin has not moved Orphie's belongings.

The broken pencil remains where it was left.

The sketchbook remains closed.

The cookbook remains untouched.

The hats remain in their discarded pile.

Corin has not thrown anything away.

She has also not restored anything.

She simply leaves them there.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT**

The primary concern is not that Corin is alone.

It is that Corin has returned to a form of isolation that previously proved harmful to her.

The loss is compounded by uncertainty.

Orphie is not confirmed dead.

She is not confirmed safe.

She is simply absent.

Corin therefore cannot properly grieve.

She cannot properly reunite.

She cannot properly move forward.

She is waiting for someone who may never return.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie believed that Corin would be better without her.

She believed that she was a burden.

She believed that leaving would solve the problem.

She walked into the snow expecting nobody to follow.

She was right.

But there is one thing Orphie did not account for.

Corin was not simply someone who refused to leave her.

Corin needed someone too.

Now the treehouse is quiet again.

Corin returns from work to an empty room.

She eats alone.

She sleeps alone.

She wakes alone.

And every evening, the same door opens.

For a brief moment, there is still the possibility that someone might walk through it.

But nobody does.

**End of Personnel Welfare / Psychological Observation**
