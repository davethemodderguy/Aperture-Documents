### **RESEARCH ADDENDUM**

## **SUBJECT: SNOW BLOSSOM (_Flora nivis_) – MEDICINAL PROPERTIES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Medical Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

For several years, Snow Blossoms were classified as an ornamental alpine flower with no significant practical application beyond their unusual appearance.

This assessment was incomplete.

Recent interviews with Lumi revealed that the species possesses mild medicinal properties when prepared correctly.

The information had never been formally documented.

When asked why she had not reported it sooner, Lumi answered simply:

> "...Because they're beautiful."

After a brief pause, she added:

> "...And because they're becoming rare."

---

# **MEDICINAL PROPERTIES**

When harvested at full bloom and processed using traditional methods, Snow Blossoms produce an herbal infusion capable of providing temporary relief from several minor conditions.

Documented effects include:

- Mild reduction of depressive symptoms.
    
- Relief from ordinary headaches.
    
- Temporary reduction of minor physical pain.
    
- A gentle calming effect without significant sedation.
    

Medical Division emphasizes that the flower is **not** a cure for chronic illness or severe psychiatric conditions.

Its effects are supportive rather than restorative.

---

# **PREPARATION**

The medicinal compounds are delicate.

Simply eating the petals produces almost no beneficial effect.

Instead, it results in an exceptionally bitter taste.

Proper preparation requires careful drying, controlled steeping temperatures, and precise timing.

Improper preparation destroys much of the flower's active compounds.

Lumi demonstrated the process only once.

Botanical Research Division successfully replicated the results under her supervision.

---

# **WHY IT REMAINED UNKNOWN**

When questioned about withholding this information, Lumi appeared thoughtful.

> "...If people only knew them as medicine..."

She looked toward a blooming Snow Blossom.

> "...There wouldn't be many left."

She explained that overharvesting had nearly eliminated the species in parts of the Banlands generations earlier.

By allowing most people to think of them as little more than beautiful winter flowers, the remaining populations were largely left undisturbed.

Lumi considered their continued survival more important than widespread use.

# **CONSERVATION STATUS**

Following Lumi's disclosure, Botanical Research Division unanimously rejected proposals for large-scale harvesting.

Instead, a conservation program was established.

Snow Blossoms are now cultivated in protected alpine gardens under controlled conditions.

Harvesting is permitted only for:

- Medical necessity.
    
- Propagation.
    
- Scientific study.
    

The majority of blooms are intentionally left untouched.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

A single potted Snow Blossom continues to bloom in Dialyn's office.

Its petals release tiny crystalline particles that drift like gentle snowfall.

Visitors admire its beauty.

Very few know it also carries centuries of forgotten medical knowledge.

Lumi has no regrets about keeping that secret.

In her own words:

> "Some flowers heal people."

She smiled softly.

> "Some heal the heart simply by blooming."

> "...Snow Blossoms have always done both."
