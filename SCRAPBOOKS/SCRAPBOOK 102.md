# **ADDENDUM 04**

Several days after the confirmation of **AE-LK-01 (The Lockwood Matriarch)**, an unexpected development occurred.

Until this point, the entity had been visible only to Corin Lockwood.

That assumption is no longer considered accurate.

---

# **INCIDENT A – NORMA LOCKWOOD**

**Location:** Engineering Laboratory

**Time:** 21:37

Norma had remained in the laboratory well after her scheduled shift, attempting to solve a fabrication issue.

Without warning, she noticed someone standing quietly beside the doorway.

A woman.

Familiar.

Warm eyes.

A gentle smile.

Norma reportedly froze.

She slowly lowered the screwdriver in her hand.

"...Mom?"

The figure nodded.

Norma later admitted she had not spoken that word aloud in years.

The entity looked around the workshop before smiling.

> **"You've become very clever."**

Norma laughed through tears.

"...You sound exactly the same."

The figure walked closer.

Instead of commenting on the machinery...

She asked only one question.

> **"When was the last time you saw your little sister?"**

Norma's smile faded.

"...It's been..."

She couldn't finish the sentence.

The figure gently rested a hand against Norma's shoulder.

> **"She's waiting for someone to believe she's worth coming home for."**

When Norma looked up again...

The room was empty.

---

# **INCIDENT B – ARCTURUS LOCKWOOD**

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE Balcony

**Time:** 23:14

Arcturus was finishing another can of soda while looking across the Blacksite grounds.

He sensed someone standing beside him.

Without turning around, he assumed it was Belle.

Instead...

He heard a familiar laugh.

One he had not heard since childhood.

He slowly turned.

His expression immediately changed.

"...No..."

"...That can't be..."

The figure smiled.

> **"You've gotten taller."**

Arcturus laughed despite himself.

> "...I certainly hope so."

Silence settled between them.

Finally, he spoke.

"...I've been trying."

The figure nodded.

> **"I know."**

> **"You became the big brother you always wanted to be."**

Arcturus lowered his head.

"...I couldn't protect them."

The figure interrupted gently.

> **"You never stopped trying."**

After a long pause, she asked him the same question she had asked Norma.

> **"When are you going to bring your little sister home?"**

Arcturus looked toward the lights of KCA Headquarters in the distance.

"...She won't come."

The figure smiled sadly.

> **"Then go sit beside her until she's ready."

---

# **CLINICAL OBSERVATION**

Neither Norma nor Arcturus described fear during their encounters.

Instead, both independently reported an overwhelming sense of familiarity, comfort, and longing.

Their descriptions of the figure's appearance, voice, mannerisms, and expressions matched with remarkable consistency.

Most notably, the entity did not dwell on the past.

Its conversations focused almost entirely on the present.

On healing.

On family.

On Corin.

---

# **ENTITY BEHAVIOR**

Researchers have identified a recurring pattern.

The Lockwood Matriarch never asks for anything for herself.

She never discusses her own death.

She never speaks of unfinished business.

Every documented appearance has centered on one objective.

To bring her children back together.

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the three encounter reports.

His conclusion consisted of only two sentences.

> "Whatever AE-LK-01 truly is..."

> "...its actions have remained consistent."

He signed the report.

Then added one final note.

> "It has never asked for power."

> "It has never asked for freedom."

> "It has only asked a family to find one another again."

---

# **STATUS**

AE-LK-01 is no longer classified as a Corin-exclusive phenomenon.

The entity has now been independently witnessed by all three Lockwood siblings under separate circumstances.

No hostile actions have been recorded.

No attempts at coercion have been observed.

Its documented purpose remains unchanged.

Not to solve their grief.

Not to erase their scars.

Only to guide three siblings, who have slowly drifted apart under the weight of tragedy, back toward one another.
