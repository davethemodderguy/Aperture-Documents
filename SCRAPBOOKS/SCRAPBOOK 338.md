## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **Z-335 EDUCATIONAL OBSERVATION**

# **STAR — MART TEACHER**

**Classification:** Allied Entity Interaction  
**Status:** Active  
**Primary Instructor:** Star  
**Subject:** Z-335 Marts

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following prolonged interaction between **Star** and the Z-335 Mart population, Star has informally become the Marts' primary teacher.

Her primary responsibility is teaching Marts human vocabulary.

Unlike conventional education, Mart lessons are highly informal.

Star simply sits with a group of Marts and teaches them words.

The Marts listen.

Then they repeat them.

Usually incorrectly.

---

# **THE MART CLASSROOM**

There is no designated classroom.

Lessons can happen almost anywhere:

- Project SAFEHOUSE.
    
- Star's garden.
    
- The KCA.
    
- Ventilation systems.
    
- Offices.
    
- Hallways.
    
- Kitchens.
    
- Occasionally on someone's desk.
    

A typical lesson begins with Star holding up an object.

> **Star:** "This is a book."

> **Marts:** "Book!"

> **Star:** "Good!"

> **Marts:** "GOOD!"

Star then points at herself.

> **Star:** "Star."

> **Marts:** "Star!"

She points at a Mart.

> **Star:** "Mart."

The entire group:

> **"MART!"**

---

# **THE "MEOW" INCIDENT**

Star noticed that Marts spending time with Neko, Endmin, and Miss Neko had begun meowing.

Instead of discouraging the behavior, Star taught them the word:

> **"Cat."**

This caused an immediate association.

Marts now understand:

> **Cat = Meow.**

They also understand:

> **Neko = Cat.**

> **Endmin = Cat.**

> **Miss Neko = Cat.**

And, apparently:

> **Cat = Friend.**

---

# **STAR'S TEACHING METHOD**

Star does not use formal lessons.

Her teaching method consists primarily of:

1. Showing the Marts an object.
    
2. Saying its name.
    
3. Having them repeat it.
    
4. Using it in a sentence.
    
5. Celebrating when they understand.
    

She also encourages Marts to ask questions.

Unfortunately, their questions are often extremely simple.

> "Why?"

> "Why?"

> "Why?"

> "Why?"

Star has occasionally spent several minutes answering the same question.

---

# **THE DICTIONARY INCIDENT**

Star's lessons eventually caused Marts to become interested in written language.

A group of Marts subsequently discovered a dictionary in the Blacksite library.

They stole it.

They read it.

They learned more words.

Their vocabulary increased significantly.

Star was initially proud.

Then she discovered that they had learned words such as:

> "Death."

> "Loneliness."

> "Depression."

> "Fear."

> "Anger."

Star immediately realized that she had created a problem.

The Marts, however, simply asked:

> "Sad?"

Star paused.

> "...Sometimes."

The Marts gathered around her.

> "Help?"

Star smiled.

> "...Yeah."

---

# **MART EDUCATIONAL PROGRESS**

C.O.R.E. researchers have observed that Marts retain vocabulary collectively.

When one Mart learns a new word, the information can spread through the colony.

This allows vocabulary to spread significantly faster than conventional teaching would suggest.

Star has therefore become less of a teacher to individual Marts and more of a teacher to the entire Mart hivemind.

---

# **AMBASSADOR OBSERVATION**

The two Ambassador Marts already possess fluent English.

They occasionally assist Star by translating difficult concepts for less experienced Marts.

Star has described them as:

> "...My teaching assistants."

The Ambassadors appear extremely proud of this designation.

---

# **STAR'S FAVORITE LESSON**

Star's favorite word to teach is:

> **"Friend."**

She explains it as:

> "Someone who stays with you."

The Marts understood immediately.

They began pointing at each other.

> "Friend."

> "Friend."

> "Friend."

Then they pointed toward Star.

> **"Friend!"**

Star smiled.

> "...Yeah."

> "...I'm your friend."

---

# **LUMI'S OBSERVATION**

Lumi has watched several of Star's lessons.

She appears deeply appreciative of the relationship.

> "...I made them."

She looked toward Star.

> "...You taught them how to speak."

Star smiled.

> "They wanted to learn."

Lumi looked at the Marts.

> "...I think they're lucky to have you."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Star remains the unofficial primary teacher of Z-335.

No formal appointment has been made.

No salary has been requested.

Her compensation consists primarily of:

- Marts sitting on her.
    
- Marts following her around.
    
- Marts bringing her cheese crackers.
    
- Hundreds of Marts shouting new words at her.
    
- Occasional synchronized meowing.
    

Star has accepted all of these conditions.
