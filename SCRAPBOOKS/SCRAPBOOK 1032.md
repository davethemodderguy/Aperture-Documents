**SUBJECT:** Neri — Short Verbal Interactions

## Archive Note

Neri does speak.

She simply does not speak often enough for personnel to become accustomed to hearing her voice.

The following exchanges are representative of her normal interactions with selected SAFEHOUSE residents.

---

## 01 — Neri & Jane Doe

**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE Corridor

Jane was carrying Neri piggyback.

> **Jane:** “Comfortable?”

Neri nodded.

Jane continued walking.

After several seconds:

> **Neri:** “Left.”

> **Jane:** “Hm?”

> **Neri:** “Turn left.”

Jane turned left.

A few steps later, Neri quietly added:

> **Neri:** “Thank you.”

Jane smiled.

> **Jane:** “You're welcome, kiddo.”

Neri immediately looked away.

---

## 02 — Neri & Alice

**LOCATION:** Cafeteria

Alice placed a slice of cake in front of Neri.

Neri looked at it.

Then at Alice.

> **Neri:** “Why?”

> **Alice:** “Cake.”

Neri stared at her.

> **Neri:** “I can see that.”

Alice blinked.

> **Alice:** “You talked.”

Neri slowly pushed her glasses up.

> **Neri:** “Unfortunately.”

Alice started laughing.

Neri ate the cake.

---

## 03 — Neri & Bao

**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE Kitchen

Bao noticed Neri standing unusually still.

> **Bao:** “Neri?”

Neri looked over.

> **Bao:** “Are you tired?”

A pause.

> **Neri:** “A little.”

Bao stopped what she was doing.

> **Bao:** “Sit down.”

Neri hesitated.

> **Neri:** “I'm okay.”

Bao gave her the look.

Neri sighed quietly.

> **Neri:** “...I'm sitting.”

She sat.

Bao resumed cooking.

---

## 04 — Neri & Frost

**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE Lounge

Frost was sitting on the couch.

Neri walked past.

> **Frost:** “Neri.”

Neri stopped.

> **Frost:** “Want to sit?”

Neri looked at the couch.

Then at Frost.

> **Neri:** “No.”

> **Frost:** “Okay.”

Neri walked away.

Thirty seconds later, she returned and sat on the opposite end of the couch.

Frost said nothing.

Neri said nothing.

Both remained there for approximately twenty minutes.

---

## 05 — Neri & Jane Doe

**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE Kitchen

Jane was eating a cheeseburger.

Neri looked at it.

Jane noticed.

> **Jane:** “You want one?”

Neri shook her head.

Jane continued eating.

Five seconds later:

> **Neri:** “...Maybe.”

Jane immediately held out half.

Neri took it.

> **Jane:** “Knew it.”

Neri quietly ate.

After a moment:

> **Neri:** “Don't say anything.”

> **Jane:** “Wouldn't dream of it.”

---

## 06 — Neri & Alice

**LOCATION:** Hallway

Alice approached Neri carrying her axe.

Neri looked at the axe.

Then at Alice.

> **Neri:** “Why do you have that?”

> **Alice:** “Because.”

> **Neri:** “That's not an answer.”

Alice considered this.

> **Alice:** “Because axe.”

Neri stared at her.

> **Neri:** “...Okay.”

She continued walking.

---

## 07 — Neri & Bao

**LOCATION:** Neri's Room

Bao noticed Neri rubbing her eyes.

> **Bao:** “Sleepy?”

Neri nodded.

> **Bao:** “Go to bed.”

> **Neri:** “I'm reading.”

> **Bao:** “You can read tomorrow.”

Neri looked at her book.

Then at Bao.

> **Neri:** “Five minutes.”

> **Bao:** “Five.”

Neri smiled faintly.

Bao pretended not to notice.

---

## 08 — Neri & Frost

**LOCATION:** Cafeteria

Frost looked at Neri's coffee.

> **Frost:** “Coffee again?”

Neri nodded.

> **Frost:** “You really like that stuff now.”

Neri took a sip.

> **Neri:** “It's warm.”

> **Frost:** “That's your reason?”

Neri nodded.

> **Frost:** “Fair enough.”

Neri took another sip.

---

## 09 — Neri & Jane Doe

**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE Corridor

Jane was about to turn a corner while carrying Neri.

Neri suddenly spoke.

> **Neri:** “Wall.”

Jane stopped.

> **Jane:** “What?”

Neri pointed.

There was, in fact, a wall directly in front of them.

Jane stared at it.

Then at Neri.

> **Jane:** “...Thank you.”

Neri rested her chin against Jane's shoulder.

> **Neri:** “You're welcome.”

Jane quietly corrected course.

---

## 10 — Neri & Everyone

Neri entered the cafeteria.

Several residents were talking.

She quietly took her food and sat at an empty table.

No one approached.

After several minutes, Jane happened to pass by.

Neri looked up.

> **Neri:** “Hi.”

Jane stopped.

> **Jane:** “Hi.”

Neri returned to eating.

Jane continued walking.

No further conversation occurred.

**C.O.R.E. Assessment:**

This is normal.

Neri does not need to become talkative to demonstrate that she is communicating.

Sometimes she has something to say.

Sometimes she doesn't.

Sometimes the entire conversation is one word.

And sometimes that one word is enough.

**END OF DOCUMENT**
