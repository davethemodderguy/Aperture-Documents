### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — WING SLEEPING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Observation:** **Seated Sleep / Wing Enclosure**  
**Status:** Stable

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has developed a new sleeping habit following the growth of her wings.

Rather than lying down, Corin can now fall asleep while **sitting upright**.

She uses her highly flexible wings to fold them around herself, effectively creating a natural blanket.

The behavior appears to be entirely voluntary and comfortable.

---

# **WING FLEXIBILITY**

Despite their size and structural strength, Corin's wings possess an unusually high degree of flexibility.

She can:

- Fold them tightly against her back.
    
- Bend them around her sides.
    
- Bring portions of the wings forward.
    
- Overlap the wings around her torso.
    
- Completely enclose much of her upper body.
    

The wings can maintain these positions without apparent strain.

Their flexibility does not interfere with their ability to generate lift during flight.

---

# **SLEEPING POSITION**

Corin typically sits somewhere comfortable, folds her legs beneath or in front of herself, and gradually wraps her wings around her body.

The feathers overlap.

The wings then settle around her like a large, layered blanket.

Her head usually rests against the folded wing structure.

Within minutes, she can fall asleep.

---

# **WHY SHE PREFERS IT**

The position solves several problems created by her wings.

She does not have to:

- Lie on her back.
    
- Compress the wings beneath herself.
    
- Carefully arrange them around a mattress.
    
- Worry about accidentally rolling onto them.
    

Instead, the wings remain around her and in a naturally supported position.

Corin has described the sensation as:

> **"Like having my own blanket."**

---

# **THERMAL INSULATION**

The dense feather layer provides considerable insulation.

Corin's body heat becomes trapped between the folded wings, keeping her warm during colder nights.

This is particularly useful during winter.

Corin has occasionally fallen asleep near an open window while wrapped entirely in her wings.

Orphie initially assumed she was simply sitting quietly.

Then realized Corin was asleep.

---

# **ORPHIE'S OBSERVATION**

Orphie found Corin asleep in this position one evening.

She approached quietly.

Corin's wings had completely covered her torso.

Only the top of her head was visible.

Orphie gently asked:

> **"Corin?"**

No response.

She was completely asleep.

Orphie smiled.

> **"...You're literally a blanket now."**

Corin did not respond.

She continued sleeping.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE REACTION**

The SAFEHOUSE residents have become accustomed to seeing Corin sitting quietly in a corner, completely wrapped in her wings.

Neko initially thought she was hiding.

Belle initially thought she was playing a game.

Eventually everyone learned:

**Corin is asleep.**

Nobody disturbs her unless necessary.

---

# **WING POSITIONING DURING SLEEP**

Corin retains enough unconscious control over her wings to prevent them from collapsing awkwardly.

The wings remain partially folded around her body throughout sleep.

Minor movements occur naturally as she adjusts her posture.

If she becomes uncomfortable, the wings reposition themselves before she fully wakes.

This suggests a degree of subconscious motor control.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

The behavior also appears to have become comforting.

Corin once spent much of her time uncomfortable with her own body.

Now she can literally wrap herself in part of it and use it for warmth and comfort.

The wings are no longer simply weapons or transportation.

They are also something she can rest inside.

When asked whether she preferred sleeping this way or in a normal bed, Corin answered:

> **"Depends."**

Then:

> **"But this is cozy."**

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

**Wing flexibility:** Extremely high.

**Seated sleep:** Confirmed.

**Wing enclosure:** Confirmed.

**Thermal insulation:** Significant.

**Sleep quality:** Appears improved.

**Risk of accidental wing compression:** Low.

**Corin's opinion:** **"Cozy."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's wings have become more than something she uses to fly.

They carry her.

They protect her.

They can launch feathers.

They can shield her from the environment.

And now they can function as a blanket.

Corin can simply sit down, fold her wings around herself, and disappear into a warm cocoon of feathers.

For someone who spent so long feeling uncomfortable inside her own body, the fact that she can now find comfort in it is considered a significant improvement.

**End of Anomalous Physiology / Behavioral Observation**
