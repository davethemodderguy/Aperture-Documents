### **SAFEHOUSE PHYSICAL CAPABILITY RECORD**

# **CORIN — BACKFLIP CAPABILITY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Ability:** **Backflip**  
**Status:** Confirmed

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin is currently the **only SAFEHOUSE resident capable of performing a complete backflip**.

No other resident has demonstrated the ability successfully.

Corin considers this completely normal.

Everyone else considers it unnecessarily impressive.

---

# **DISCOVERY**

The ability was demonstrated casually.

Corin was standing in the SAFEHOUSE living area when she suddenly performed a clean backflip without preparation.

She landed perfectly.

Nobody had asked her to do it.

There was a brief silence.

Neko eventually asked:

> **"...Why can you do that?"**

Corin shrugged.

> **"I have wings."**

---

# **WING ASSISTANCE**

Corin's wings provide additional control during the maneuver.

She can use them to:

- Initiate rotation.
    
- Adjust her center of gravity.
    
- Stabilize herself during rotation.
    
- Correct her orientation before landing.
    
- Reduce landing impact.
    

Her flexibility and anomalous physical capabilities make the maneuver considerably easier for her than for an ordinary human.

---

# **OTHER RESIDENTS**

Several SAFEHOUSE residents have attempted to reproduce the maneuver.

Results have been mixed.

Some stopped after seeing Corin perform one.

Others decided that gravity was not worth challenging.

Neko attempted once.

He has not attempted again.

---

# **CORIN'S MINIATURE FORM**

Corin can also perform a backflip in her miniature feline form.

The smaller body gives her exceptional rotational agility.

Her wings provide additional stabilization.

The resulting maneuver is approximately:

> **90% adorable.**

> **10% concerning.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

SAFEHOUSE has many residents with extraordinary abilities.

Some can manipulate Ether.

Some can manipulate light.

Some can fly.

Some are anomalous entities.

But only one person can casually stand in the living room and perform a perfect backflip.

Corin.

Nobody knows why this particular achievement matters.

Corin does.

She thinks it's cool.

**End of SAFEHOUSE Physical Capability Record**
