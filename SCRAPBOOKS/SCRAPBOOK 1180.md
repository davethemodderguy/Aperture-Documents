# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon has been formally integrated into SAFEHOUSE following her anomalous reappearance.

Initial observations indicate that Nora is:

- a Shark Thiren;
    
- approximately six years old;
    
- highly energetic;
    
- extremely social;
    
- considerably more impulsive than Neri;
    
- somewhat less academically inclined than her older sister;
    
- exceptionally attached to Neri;
    
- and apparently incapable of remaining quiet for more than approximately thirty seconds when excited.
    

The final observation has proven particularly relevant.

Nora has become almost permanently attached to Neri.

Where Neri goes:

> **Nora follows.**

Where Neri sits:

> **Nora sits nearby.**

Where Neri conducts research:

> **Nora watches.**

Where Neri sleeps:

> **Nora sleeps.**

Where Neri's tail is:

> **Nora bites it.**

Frequently.

# 1. SPECIES CONFUSION

Nora's initial understanding of Neri's physical appearance has produced an unusual complication.

Nora remembers Neri from before the events that resulted in her transformation into a Seal Thiren.

To Nora's memory:

> **Neri was human.**

Nora therefore initially interpreted Neri's current appearance as simply:

> **“Neri, but with weird ears and a tail.”**

She did not immediately understand that Neri had become a Seal Thiren.

This is understandable.

Neri's Seal Thiren traits are artificial.

They were not inherited from the Tachyon family.

Nora, being a Shark Thiren, has no reason to recognize them as part of her sister's original biology.

Her first prolonged examination of Neri's tail ended with:

_Chomp._

Neri looked behind herself.

Nora was still holding the tail.

Neri stared.

Nora stared back.

> **NERI:**  
> “Why?”

Nora released it.

> **NORA:**  
> “I wanted to see if it was real.”

Neri:

> **“It is.”**

Nora:

> **“Oh.”**

Five minutes later:

_Chomp._

# 2. TAIL-BITING INCIDENTS

Nora has developed a fascination with Neri's seal tail.

This is not malicious.

It is apparently a combination of:

- sibling familiarity;
    
- Shark Thiren instinct;
    
- curiosity;
    
- playfulness;
    
- and the fact that Neri's tail is apparently very tempting to a six-year-old shark.
    

Nora has bitten Neri's tail repeatedly.

Neri does not appear to find the bites particularly painful.

She does, however, find them irritating.

Typical exchange:

> **NERI:**  
> “Nora.”

_Chomp._

> **NERI:**  
> “Stop.”

Nora releases the tail.

> **NORA:**  
> “Okay.”

Five seconds later:

_Chomp._

Neri slowly looks over her shoulder.

> **NERI:**  
> “You just said okay.”

Nora:

> **“I know.”**

# 3. NORA'S INTELLIGENCE

Nora has demonstrated intelligence consistent with her age.

However, direct comparison with Neri is misleading.

Neri's intellectual ability is exceptional.

Her chemistry work, theoretical research, computational modeling, and scientific reasoning place her considerably outside normal expectations for her age.

Nora does not demonstrate the same level of academic ability.

She is simply:

**Obsessed.**

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Nora's arrival has produced an unexpected change in Neri's social behavior.

Neri remains quiet.

She still prefers solitude.

She still does not enjoy being surrounded by people.

She still speaks infrequently.

But Nora is different.

Nora is not approaching Neri as a medical case.

She is not approaching her as:

> **The Last Tachyon.**

She is not approaching her because she needs something.

She is approaching her because:

> **Neri is her big sister.**

That distinction appears significant.

Nora does not care about Neri's historical importance.

She does not care about Tachyon Industries.

She does not care about twelve terabytes.

She does not care that Neri is an exceptional chemist.

She certainly does not care about the complicated politics of her family history.

She simply thinks her sister is amazing.

And she has apparently decided that Neri's room is the coolest place in existence.
