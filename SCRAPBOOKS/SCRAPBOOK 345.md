# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **Z-335 HANDLING & RESEARCH MEMORANDUM**

# **TOP HAT SAFETY & THE REBIRTH HYPOTHESIS**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **PERSONNEL REMINDER**

Marts enjoy physical affection.

They actively seek:

- Head pats.
    
- Gentle petting.
    
- Being picked up.
    
- Sitting in laps.
    
- Being hugged.
    

However, personnel are reminded to exercise care when petting a Mart.

> **Avoid striking or removing its top hat.**

The hat is not decorative.

It is an essential component of a Mart's anomalous stability.

---

# **PROPER PETTING PROCEDURE**

When interacting with a Mart:

✓ Pet around the sides of its head.

✓ Gently scratch beneath its antlers if the Mart appears comfortable.

✓ Support the Mart if picking it up.

✗ Do **not** flick, knock, or remove its top hat.

Most Marts will instinctively protect their hats if they believe one is about to fall.

---

# **DOCUMENTED ACCIDENT**

Only one confirmed accidental fatality has been attributed to improper handling.

During an early interaction, a staff member enthusiastically patted a Mart on the head.

The Mart's top hat was unintentionally knocked several meters away.

The Mart immediately began destabilizing.

Nearby personnel attempted to return the hat.

They were too late.

The Mart catastrophically burst before its hat could be replaced.

The entire room reportedly fell silent.

It was the first time many Marts had witnessed one of their own disappear.

Several became visibly distressed.

The involved staff member required counseling.

The incident directly led to the current handling guidelines.

---

# **COLONY RESPONSE**

Unlike previous observations, the surrounding Marts did not simply say:

> "We'll be back."

Instead, they gathered around the empty hat.

No one spoke.

Eventually, one quietly asked:

> "...Friend?"

Another replied:

> "...Gone."

Researchers believe this was one of the first occasions on which the colony collectively experienced grief.

---

# **THE REBIRTH HYPOTHESIS**

The death of individual Marts has raised an unresolved scientific question.

Marts reproduce naturally, and the colony continues to maintain a stable population through the appearance of new individuals.

However...

Researchers cannot determine whether these newcomers are **entirely new beings** or the **return of previous ones**.

This uncertainty has become known as:

> **The Rebirth Hypothesis**

---

# **SUPPORTING OBSERVATIONS**

Evidence suggesting possible rebirth includes:

- The Mart hivemind never appears to permanently lose information after individual deaths.
    
- Newly appeared Marts sometimes demonstrate familiarity with locations or routines they should not have experienced independently.
    
- The colony continues to refer to itself collectively rather than emphasizing individual identity.
    
- Marts consistently speak of death as though it is temporary.
    

One common phrase remains:

> **"We'll be back."**

Researchers are no longer certain whether this statement is optimism...

...or literal.

---

# **COUNTERARGUMENTS**

Alternative explanations remain plausible.

The shared hivemind could simply transfer memories to newly formed Marts.

In this interpretation:

- An individual Mart dies permanently.
    
- A new Mart is born.
    
- The colony's collective memory continues uninterrupted.
    

At present, no experiment has conclusively distinguished between these possibilities.

---

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

Lumi was asked whether she believed her children were reborn.

She thought quietly for a long time.

Finally, she answered:

> "...I don't know."

She looked toward a group of Marts happily playing together.

> "...Maybe every new Mart is someone new."

She smiled softly.

> "...Or maybe they really do come back."

She gently patted the nearest Mart—carefully avoiding its top hat.

> "...Either way..."

> "...I'm happy every time I see one."

---

# **STAR'S COMMENT**

Star has become particularly careful when petting Marts.

She always rests her hand just below the tiny hat.

When asked why she was so precise, she replied:

> "...One accident was enough."

---

# **AMBASSADOR'S RESPONSE**

One Ambassador was asked directly:

> "...When a Mart dies..."

> "...Do they come back?"

The Ambassador remained quiet for several moments.

Then it smiled.

> "...We'll meet again."

Researchers noted that the answer, while sincere, was not scientifically conclusive.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Today, nearly every member of the Blacksite and KCA instinctively pets Marts the same way.

Their hands naturally avoid the tiny top hats.

It has become second nature.

Not because anyone is afraid of breaking a rule.

But because nobody wants to repeat the day an entire room of cheerful blue blobs fell silent.

And every time someone finishes petting a Mart correctly, the response is almost always the same.

> **"Happy."**

Then the Mart happily waddles away, its tiny top hat still perfectly in place.
