# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-ARC-4C-JAKE-241  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — MEDICAL / ANOMALOUS  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**SUBJECT:** Jake  
**EVENT:** Self-removable ocular structure / deployable visual reconnaissance

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has demonstrated another previously undocumented anomalous ability.

He can remove one of his own eyes.

The removed eye remains functional.

It can be positioned independently and used as a **deployable camera / remote visual sensor**.

Jake can subsequently return the eye to its original position.

The eye socket itself does not contain conventional biological anatomy.

Instead:

> **Jake's eye socket is a black hole.**

C.O.R.E. has therefore added ocular removal to the growing list of phenomena that personnel should **not attempt to explain using normal human anatomy.**

---

# 2. INITIAL DEMONSTRATION

The discovery occurred in the common room.

Phoenix was present.

Jake casually touched the area around his eye.

Phoenix immediately noticed.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake?”

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Hm?”

He removed his eye.

Phoenix froze.

The eye remained intact.

Jake was holding it in his hand.

Phoenix stared.

Jake stared back.

Except one of his eyes was now in his hand.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “...”

Jake held the eye up.

It continued looking around.

Phoenix's expression changed immediately.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “PUT THAT BACK.”

Jake blinked.

With his remaining eye.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Why?”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “BECAUSE THAT IS YOUR EYE.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “I know.”

---

# 3. THE EYE

The detached ocular structure remains visually identical to Jake's normal eye.

It retains:

- blue coloration;
    
- celestial luminosity;
    
- normal visual movement;
    
- apparent visual focus;
    
- and Jake's anomalous ocular properties.
    

The eye can apparently continue transmitting visual information to Jake despite being physically separated from him.

No visible cable, nerve, vessel, or conventional connection remains.

C.O.R.E. cannot identify a conventional mechanism.

The eye simply continues functioning.

---

# 4. THE SOCKET

The most disturbing component is not the detached eye.

It is the socket.

When the eye is removed, there is no exposed tissue.

No blood.

No muscle.

No optic nerve.

No bone.

Instead, observers see an area of absolute darkness.

Initial visual examination suggests the socket contains a localized **black-hole-like phenomenon**.

Light entering the opening does not appear to return normally.

C.O.R.E. has therefore classified the ocular socket as:

> **ANOMALOUS GRAVITATIONAL PHENOMENON — BLACK-HOLE TYPE**

Jake does not appear bothered by it.

---

# 5. PHOENIX'S RESPONSE

Phoenix was not calm.

Phoenix has treated:

- Ether instability;
    
- fatal trauma;
    
- exposed internal organs;
    
- repeated post-mortem restoration;
    
- anomalous cardiac structures;
    
- and a child whose actual heart is contained inside a teddy bear.
    

This was apparently the event that exceeded her current tolerance.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Jake.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “Yeah?”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Put your eye back.”

> **JAKE:**  
> “I'm using it.”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “PUT YOUR EYE BACK.”

Jake looked at the eye in his hand.

Then looked toward the opposite side of the room.

The eye independently looked toward the same direction.

> **JAKE:**  
> “It works.”

Phoenix covered her face.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “That is not the part I'm worried about.”

---

# 6. REMOTE RECONNAISSANCE

Jake subsequently demonstrated the eye's secondary function.

He placed the detached eye on a nearby surface.

The eye continued moving.

Jake could apparently see through it.

He described what was visible from the eye's position without turning his head.

C.O.R.E. therefore considers the detached eye functionally equivalent to a **deployable anomalous camera**.

Potential applications include:

- observing locations without entering them;
    
- monitoring corridors;
    
- checking rooms;
    
- visual reconnaissance;
    
- observing areas inaccessible to Jake's physical body.
    

However, the eye remains part of Jake.

It is not considered expendable equipment.

---

# 7. REASSEMBLY

After approximately several minutes, Jake picked up the eye.

He placed it against the empty socket.

The eye disappeared into the black opening.

It immediately returned to its normal position.

The black-hole-like phenomenon vanished.

Normal anatomy resumed.

No scar remained.

No pain was reported.

Phoenix watched the entire process.

She looked profoundly uncomfortable.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Does that hurt?”

Jake shook his head.

> **JAKE:**  
> “No.”

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Are you sure?”

> **JAKE:**  
> “Yeah.”

Phoenix exhaled.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Good.”

Pause.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> “Please don't do that again while I'm eating.”

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Okay.”

---

# 8. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT

Phoenix subsequently submitted an unusually short medical note.

> **“I don't understand his anatomy anymore.”**

After several minutes, she added:

> **“There isn't anatomy.”**

Then:

> **“His eye socket is a black hole.”**

Then, finally:

> **“I need coffee.”**

C.O.R.E. considers the report sufficiently comprehensive.

---

# 9. SAFETY CONSIDERATIONS

Personnel are explicitly prohibited from:

- attempting to remove Jake's eye;
    
- touching the eye without permission;
    
- inserting objects into the ocular socket;
    
- attempting to examine the black-hole phenomenon directly;
    
- treating the detached eye as ordinary camera equipment;
    
- or requesting Jake demonstrate the phenomenon for entertainment.
    

Jake's ability is voluntary.

He should be allowed to decide when and how he uses it.

The eye remains part of his body.

---

# 10. CURRENT CLASSIFICATION

C.O.R.E. has provisionally classified the phenomenon as:

> **DEPLOYABLE OCULAR RECONNAISSANCE**

Secondary properties:

> **REMOTE VISUAL SENSING**

> **ANOMALOUS OCULAR REASSEMBLY**

> **BLACK-HOLE-TYPE OCULAR SOCKET**

The final mechanism remains unknown.

Jake himself appears to consider the ability extremely ordinary.
