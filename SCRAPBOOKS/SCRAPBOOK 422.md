# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL RECOVERY / ANOMALOUS BIOLOGY REPORT**

# **ORPHIE — RECOVERY FROM THE SNOW**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Anomalous Biology  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / Lumi / Arcturus / Norma / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Marts  
**Location:** Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **FOUND — ALIVE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

After **fourteen days** without contact, Orphie has been found.

She was located deep within the Blacksite perimeter forest, partially buried beneath a thick layer of snow.

She was alive.

She was conscious.

And, according to the recovery team:

> **"She was extremely annoyed that we found her."**

---

# **DISCOVERY**

The search team initially believed the mound of snow was entirely natural.

Then something moved beneath it.

Personnel carefully cleared the snow away.

Orphie was underneath.

She had apparently been sleeping beneath the snow for an extended period.

Her body temperature was dangerously low.

Her body was covered in frostbite.

Yet she remained alive and responsive.

When asked how long she had been there:

> "...I don't know."

When asked what she had eaten:

> "...Snow."

The recovery team assumed she was joking.

She wasn't.

---

# **SURVIVAL**

For approximately two weeks, Orphie's only known food sources were:

- Snow
    
- Wild flowers
    
- Wild berries
    

No prepared food was found.

No equipment was found.

No shelter had been constructed.

She had simply buried herself beneath the snow and slept.

Medical personnel remain unable to fully explain how she survived the conditions.

Her anomalous physiology is believed to be a significant factor.

---

# **PHYSICAL CONDITION**

Orphie presented with extensive frostbite across her body.

Despite this, she remained remarkably stable.

Medical personnel documented:

- Severe cold exposure.
    
- Extensive frostbite.
    
- Reduced metabolic activity.
    
- Prolonged nutritional deprivation.
    
- Significant sleep irregularity.
    
- Anomalous physiological resilience.
    

Orphie herself appeared largely unconcerned.

> **Doctor:** "...You have frostbite."

> **Orphie:** "...I know."

> "...You're lucky to be alive."

> "...Okay."

> "...Do you understand how dangerous this was?"

> "...Yes."

> "...And you still did it?"

> "...Yes."

---

# **ANOMALOUS MORPHOLOGY**

During examination, previously undocumented physical characteristics were identified.

Orphie possesses:

### **Tail**

A fully developed tail.

Its exact biological function remains unknown.

### **Horns**

A pair of horns protruding from her head.

Their composition appears anomalous.

### **THIREN CLASSIFICATION**

Orphie has been formally identified as a **Thiren**.

However, her specific Thiren type remains **unknown**.

C.O.R.E. initially attempted to classify her based on her morphology.

The process was abandoned.

---

# **WHY CLASSIFICATION WAS ABANDONED**

Nobody particularly cared.

The medical staff were more concerned with the frostbite.

Arcturus was concerned about his sister being alive.

Norma was concerned about her condition.

Lumi was concerned about her daughter.

Star was crying.

Corin was trying to get through the medical staff.

Nobody was interested in determining what animal Orphie's traits corresponded to.

The official classification remains:

> **THIREN — TYPE UNKNOWN**

---

# **ORPHIE'S REACTION**

Orphie was extremely unhappy about being found.

She repeatedly asked to be returned to the forest.

> "...Why did you find me?"

> "...You shouldn't have."

> "...I was fine."

Personnel attempted to explain that she had been suffering from severe frostbite.

Orphie dismissed the concern.

> "...I'll recover."

---

# **THE CORIN INCIDENT**

Orphie remained hostile toward the recovery team.

She refused to cooperate.

She refused food.

She refused to answer questions.

She repeatedly demanded to be left alone.

Then someone mentioned Corin.

Everything changed.

> **"Corin is still at the treehouse."**

Orphie stopped.

Silence.

> "...What?"

> "...She's been there since you disappeared."

Orphie's expression changed.

For the first time since being found, she appeared genuinely concerned.

> "...She's alive?"

> "...Yes."

A long pause.

> "...Is she okay?"

Nobody answered immediately.

Orphie understood.

> "...She's not."

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

Her anger disappeared.

Not completely.

But enough.

She stopped fighting the medical staff.

She allowed them to treat her.

She ate.

She accepted a blanket.

She eventually asked:

> "...Can I see her?"

The answer was delayed.

> "...After you're medically stable."

Orphie frowned.

> "...Fine."

---

# **CORIN**

Corin was informed that Orphie had been found.

Her reaction was immediate.

She did not ask about the horns.

She did not ask about the tail.

She did not ask what kind of Thiren Orphie was.

She only asked:

> **"...She's alive?"**

The answer:

> "...Yes."

Corin began crying.

---

# **THE FAMILY**

### **Arcturus**

Arcturus became visibly emotional.

> "...She's alive."

He repeated it several times.

> "...She's actually alive."

---

### **Norma**

Norma immediately began asking about Orphie's medical condition.

She stopped herself after realizing the doctors were already handling it.

Then quietly:

> "...Thank God."

---

### **Lumi**

Lumi simply closed her eyes.

For the first time in two weeks, she appeared relieved.

> "...My daughter."

---

# **STAR**

Star cried again.

This time, they were tears of relief.

> "...She's alive!"

She hugged Nightshade.

> "...She's actually alive!"

Nightshade held her.

> "...Yes."

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts were informed.

Their reaction was complicated.

They were still afraid of Orphie.

But they were relieved that she was alive.

One Mart quietly asked:

> "...Coming back?"

Nobody knew.

---

# **THE RETURN**

Orphie has not yet been returned to the treehouse.

Medical personnel insist that she remain under observation until her frostbite and nutritional condition stabilize.

She continues to ask about Corin.

She has not asked about anyone else.

Not yet.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

For fourteen days, Orphie disappeared.

She slept beneath the snow.

She survived on snow, wild flowers, and berries.

She endured severe frostbite.

She developed previously undocumented Thiren characteristics.

And through all of it, she wanted one thing:

> **To be left alone.**

She was furious when she was found.

Until she heard one name.

**Corin.**

That single word was enough to break through everything else.

The anger disappeared.

The resistance stopped.

For the first time in two weeks, Orphie allowed someone to help her.

Not because she suddenly cared about herself.

Not because she changed her principles.

Because somewhere inside the person who had tried so hard to disappear...

**Corin still mattered.**

**End of Personnel Recovery / Anomalous Biology Report**
