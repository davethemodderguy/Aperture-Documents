# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following the recovery and integration of the six surviving former Project Silverline subjects, C.O.R.E. has documented a notable distinction between their physical appearances and their individual voices.

All six artificial humans share the same baseline physical appearance.

Their voices, however, are **highly distinct.**

This has proven useful for identification.

It has also resulted in several unexpected observations regarding personality.

---

# 1. ANBY

### Vocal Profile

Anby's voice is:

- soft;
    
- cute;
    
- quiet;
    
- frequently mumbled;
    
- and noticeably youthful.
    

Despite being the **eldest of the twelve sisters**, Anby consistently sounds like the youngest.

Personnel unfamiliar with the sisters have occasionally assumed that Anby is the youngest based solely on her voice.

This assumption is incorrect.

Anby is the eldest.

She simply sounds like she isn't.

### Speech Pattern

Anby frequently mumbles portions of sentences.

She may begin speaking normally and gradually become quieter until the final words are barely audible.

Example:

> **ANBY:**  
> "I think we should probably... um... go back..."

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "What was that last part?"

> **ANBY:**  
> "...go back."

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "I heard you the first time."

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Okay."

Her voice rarely carries significant volume unless circumstances require it.

### Identification Note

If personnel hear a very soft, cute voice mumbling somewhere nearby:

**Anby is the most likely source.**

---

# 2. TWIGGY

### Vocal Profile

Twiggy's voice is also soft.

However, unlike Anby, her voice carries considerably more maturity.

There is a noticeable calmness in her speech.

She sounds like someone accustomed to reassuring other people.

This is consistent with her experience working as a nurse following her separation from Project Silverline.

### Speech Pattern

Twiggy generally speaks clearly and patiently.

She does not rush her words.

When someone is distressed, her voice tends to become even softer.

Example:

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "You're okay."

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "I'm fine."

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "I know."

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "Then why are you looking at me like that?"

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "Because you're bleeding."

Pause.

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "...Oh."

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "Sit down."

Her tone rarely becomes threatening.

It does not need to.

### Identification Note

Soft voice + noticeably mature tone + professional reassurance:

**Twiggy.**

---

# 3. SOLIDER 11

### Vocal Profile

Solider 11's voice is markedly different from both Anby and Twiggy.

Her voice tends to sound:

- persuasive;
    
- confident;
    
- subtly manipulative;
    
- conversational;
    
- and extremely convincing.
    

The similarity to **Jane Doe's** vocal mannerisms has been independently noted by multiple personnel.

This is particularly noticeable when Solider 11 wants something.

### Speech Pattern

Solider 11 rarely sounds like she is giving an order.

She sounds like she is giving someone an opportunity to make what she already wants to happen appear to be their own decision.

Example:

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "You should probably come with us."

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "Why?"

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Because you don't really want to stay here alone."

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "I do."

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "Sure."

Pause.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "But you could come with us."

Pause.

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "...Fine."

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "See?"

### Jane Doe Comparison

Jane was informed of the similarity.

Her response:

> **JANE:**  
> "Oh, she's good."

Pause.

> **JANE:**  
> "I like her."

C.O.R.E. has declined to determine whether this should be considered a compliment.

### Identification Note

If someone sounds extremely convincing while making a suggestion that somehow becomes difficult to refuse:

**Solider 11.**

---

# 4. SORA

### Vocal Profile

Sora has one of the most openly caring voices in the group.

Her normal speech is warm, gentle, and reassuring.

She sounds like someone who is constantly checking whether everyone around her is okay.

### Normal Speech

> **SORA:**  
> "Did you eat?"

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "Yes."

> **SORA:**  
> "Are you sure?"

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "Yes."

> **SORA:**  
> "Okay."

Pause.

> **SORA:**  
> "Do you want anything?"

This behavior is common.

Sora appears to genuinely enjoy taking care of people.

### Anger Response

The difference when Sora becomes angry is significant.

Her voice does not necessarily become louder.

It becomes **colder.**

The caring tone disappears.

Example:

> **SORA:**  
> "Please put that down."

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "Or what?"

Pause.

> **SORA:**  
> "...Please."

The second version is considerably more concerning.

Personnel have been advised not to deliberately test this distinction.

### Identification Note

Warm, caring voice:

**Sora.**

Suddenly very quiet and frightening:

**Still Sora.**

---

# 5. IRIS

### Vocal Profile

Iris sounds like a gremlin.

This is the official unofficial description currently used by several residents.

It is also remarkably accurate.

Her voice is energetic, expressive, occasionally loud, and capable of rapidly shifting between excitement and chaos.

### Speech Pattern

Iris frequently talks faster than necessary.

She becomes particularly animated when discussing vehicles.

Example:

> **IRIS:**  
> "I CAN DRIVE IT."

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "Nobody said you couldn't."

> **IRIS:**  
> "GOOD."

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "That wasn't permission."

> **IRIS:**  
> "I KNOW."

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "...Why did you say it like that?"

> **IRIS:**  
> "Because I CAN DRIVE IT."

Her driving ability is considerably more disciplined than her voice suggests.

This remains one of C.O.R.E.'s stranger observations.

### Identification Note

If personnel hear what appears to be a small creature enthusiastically arguing about vehicles:

**Iris.**

---

# 6. PHAETHON

### Vocal Profile

Phaethon's voice is the calmest of the six.

She speaks with deliberate control.

Her tone remains remarkably stable regardless of circumstances.

She does not mumble like Anby.

She does not persuade like Solider 11.

She does not become energetic like Iris.

She simply speaks.

### Speech Pattern

Phaethon tends to use short, precise sentences.

Example:

> **PHAETHON:**  
> "We're leaving."

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "Why?"

> **PHAETHON:**  
> "It's getting late."

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "That's it?"

> **PHAETHON:**  
> "Yes."

Pause.

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "...Okay."

Her calmness can occasionally make otherwise dramatic situations feel strangely ordinary.

### Identification Note

If someone sounds completely composed while everyone else is losing their minds:

**Phaethon.**

# 8. IDENTIFICATION VALUE

Because all six sisters share nearly identical physical characteristics, vocal recognition has become an important secondary identification method.

Hairclips remain the primary visual identifier.

Voice is the preferred secondary identifier.

Personnel should **not** rely on facial appearance alone.

The six sisters themselves have repeatedly demonstrated that even they can occasionally mistake one another from a distance.

---

# 9. C.O.R.E. OBSERVATION

The contrast is particularly interesting given their shared origin.

Project Silverline created twelve artificial humans from the same baseline.

Yet the surviving six have developed remarkably different ways of expressing themselves.

Anby sounds younger than everyone.

Twiggy sounds like a nurse.

Solider 11 sounds like she could convince you to sign something without realizing you agreed.

Sora sounds like everyone's older sister until someone makes her angry.

Iris sounds like a gremlin who somehow obtained a driver's license.

Phaethon sounds like nothing in the world could possibly surprise her.

Their voices are therefore another indication that:

> **Identical construction does not produce identical people.**
