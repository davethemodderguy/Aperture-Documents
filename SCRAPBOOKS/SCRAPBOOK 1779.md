# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The feline situation has partially resolved.

Neko has returned to his normal form.

Miss Neko has also returned to her normal form.

Endmin Merryweather has not.

The Director of the Central Execution Department remains a cat.

Jake also remains a cat.

C.O.R.E. initially considered this a possible continuation of the anomalous transformation event documented in **C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-FELINE-001**.

However, subsequent observations suggest that Endmin's situation may have a rather less mysterious explanation.

Endmin has been overworking herself for several consecutive weeks.

She has been sleeping insufficiently, taking meals at irregular intervals, postponing personal activities, and continuing to work long after her scheduled hours.

Then she turned into a cat.

She has since shown no apparent interest in returning to her responsibilities.

Her mother, **Belle Merryweather**, has reviewed the situation.

Her decision was immediate.

> **BELLE:** "Approved."

Endmin is officially on leave.

No objections have been accepted.

Phoenix is experiencing the happiest day of her life.

---

# 1. TRANSFORMATION STATUS

|Resident|Current form|Status|
|---|---|---|
|Neko|Normal|Transformation resolved|
|Miss Neko|Normal|Transformation resolved|
|Endmin Merryweather|Cat|Resting; leave approved|
|Jake|Cat|Stable; feline form ongoing|

The mechanism behind the transformations remains unknown.

Neko and Miss Neko have returned to their normal forms without reported complications.

Endmin has not.

Unlike the other two, Endmin has made no meaningful effort to investigate her condition.

When asked whether she wished to undergo a preliminary examination, Endmin looked at the medical personnel.

She then looked at the examination table.

Then at the door.

Then she lay down on the nearest available cushion.

**C.O.R.E. MEDICAL OFFICER:** "Director, we need to check whether you're experiencing any adverse effects."

**ENDMIN:** "Meow."

**C.O.R.E. MEDICAL OFFICER:** "Is that a refusal?"

**ENDMIN:** "Meow."

**C.O.R.E. MEDICAL OFFICER:** "I see."

The examination proceeded with appropriate care, minimal disturbance, and no attempt to force Endmin to resume her normal form.

Preliminary findings indicate that she is comfortable, responsive, and capable of ordinary feline movement.

Her transformation remains unexplained.

Her desire to nap does not.

---

# 2. THE CENTRAL EXECUTION DEPARTMENT HAS A PROBLEM

The Central Execution Department is currently operating without its director.

Technically, this is a problem.

Operationally, it is manageable.

Administratively, it is hilarious.

For several weeks, Endmin had been attempting to manage an excessive workload personally.

She had repeatedly extended her working hours.

She had continued reviewing documents after other personnel had left.

She had postponed breaks to finish pending assignments.

She had accepted additional responsibilities despite already having too many.

When asked whether she needed assistance, she had repeatedly responded that she could manage.

She could not, in fact, manage indefinitely.

Her body eventually appears to have submitted a formal objection.

The objection took the form of four paws, a tail, and an overwhelming desire to sleep.

C.O.R.E. has no confirmed evidence that the transformation was caused by exhaustion.

However, the timing has prompted an immediate review of Endmin's workload and working conditions.

The review will continue regardless of whether Endmin turns back tomorrow.

**Especially if she turns back tomorrow.**

---

# 3. BELLE MERRYWEATHER'S DECISION

Belle was informed that her daughter had become a cat.

She was also informed that Endmin had spent the preceding weeks working excessively long hours.

Belle requested the workload records.

She reviewed them.

She reviewed them again.

She then looked at Endmin, who was curled into a small white-and-grey ball on a blanket.

**BELLE:** "How long has she been doing this?"

**C.O.R.E. WELFARE OFFICER:** "Several weeks, at minimum."

**BELLE:** "And nobody made her stop?"

**C.O.R.E. WELFARE OFFICER:** "She is the Director."

Belle slowly closed the report.

**BELLE:** "That is not an answer."

Silence.

Belle looked at her daughter.

Endmin opened one eye.

**BELLE:** "You're on leave."

Endmin closed her eye again.

**BELLE:** "Full leave. No department work. No reports. No meetings. No 'just one more thing.'"

Endmin's tail moved once.

**BELLE:** "And before anyone asks, yes, I approve it."

The welfare officer cautiously raised a hand.

**WELFARE OFFICER:** "What if she insists on returning to work?"

Belle looked at the cat.

Then at the officer.

**BELLE:** "Then we remind her that she is on leave."

**WELFARE OFFICER:** "And if she still insists?"

**BELLE:** "We remind her again."

A pause.

**BELLE:** "I raised a daughter. I can out-stubborn a cat."

Endmin's ears rotated toward her mother.

Belle pointed at her.

**BELLE:** "Don't even think about it."

Endmin put her head back down.

**LEAVE STATUS: APPROVED.**

---

# 4. OFFICIAL LEAVE ARRANGEMENTS

Endmin's leave is effective immediately.

The following restrictions apply:

- No routine CED paperwork.
    
- No remote access to departmental work queues.
    
- No attending meetings merely to listen.
    
- No reviewing reports from bed.
    
- No asking another employee to bring her documents.
    
- No waking up at three in the morning to solve an administrative problem.
    
- No declaring herself sufficiently rested after one nap and returning to work.
    

The last restriction was added at Belle's insistence.

Temporary departmental responsibilities have been delegated to authorized personnel.

Urgent matters will be handled through the existing chain of command.

Endmin is not required to supervise this arrangement personally.

That would defeat the purpose.

C.O.R.E. emphasizes that this is not disciplinary action.

It is recovery time.

Endmin does not have to earn the right to rest by becoming ill enough to justify it.

She does not need to prove that she has finished every task before she is allowed to stop.

And she does not have to turn back into a human before her leave becomes valid.

For now, she is a cat.

The department can survive without her for a few days.

If it cannot, that is a management problem—not a reason to wake her up.

---

# 5. THE CAT ROOM

A quiet resting area has been prepared for Endmin and Jake.

It contains:

- Soft blankets and cushions.
    
- Water and appropriate food.
    
- Several comfortable sleeping spots.
    
- Toys for anyone interested in playing.
    
- Quiet corners where nobody is required to socialize.
    
- Easy access to medical assistance without constant disturbance.
    

Endmin selected the largest cushion.

Jake selected a smaller one nearby.

After approximately four minutes, Jake abandoned his own cushion and curled up beside Endmin.

Endmin opened one eye.

Jake stared at her.

Endmin stared back.

Jake tucked his paws underneath himself.

Endmin closed her eye again.

The two cats remained together.

No conversation was recorded.

No disagreement occurred.

The arrangement was considered successful.

---

# 6. PHOENIX HAS NEVER BEEN HAPPIER

Phoenix was informed that Neko and Miss Neko had returned to normal.

She expressed mild disappointment that the number of cats had decreased.

Then she learned that Endmin would remain a cat for the foreseeable future.

Then she saw Jake.

Still a cat.

Then she saw Endmin.

Also a cat.

Phoenix stood in the doorway.

She looked at Jake.

She looked at Endmin.

She looked back at Jake.

Her hands slowly rose to cover her mouth.

**PHOENIX:** "There are two."

**C.O.R.E. WELFARE OFFICER:** "Two what?"

**PHOENIX:** "CATS."

**WELFARE OFFICER:** "Yes."

**PHOENIX:** "TWO CATS."

**WELFARE OFFICER:** "Correct."

Phoenix took one cautious step into the room.

Jake lifted his head.

His azure eyes, complete with their little shooting-star streaks, caught the light.

Phoenix made a noise that the recording system could not confidently classify as speech.

Jake's ears twitched.

Endmin opened her eyes.

Phoenix crouched beside the cushions, visibly attempting to contain herself.

**PHOENIX:** "Can I pet you?"

Jake approached first.

He sniffed her hand.

Then he rubbed his cheek against her fingers.

Phoenix froze.

Her expression became completely blank.

**WELFARE OFFICER:** "Phoenix?"

No response.

**WELFARE OFFICER:** "Phoenix, are you all right?"

Phoenix slowly turned toward the officer.

**PHOENIX:** "He likes me."

**WELFARE OFFICER:** "It appears so."

**PHOENIX:** "HE LIKES ME."

Jake climbed into her lap.

Phoenix's eyes widened.

She carefully supported him, allowing him to settle however he preferred.

A soft purr began.

Phoenix's face did something medically concerning.

**WELFARE OFFICER:** "Phoenix. Stay with us."

**PHOENIX:** "He's purring."

**WELFARE OFFICER:** "I know."

**PHOENIX:** "He's PURRING."

**WELFARE OFFICER:** "Breathe."

Phoenix obeyed.

Mostly.

Endmin watched the entire exchange from her cushion.

Then she stood, padded over, and placed her front paws against Phoenix's leg.

Phoenix looked down.

Endmin looked up.

**PHOENIX:** "...Hello, Director."

Endmin meowed.

Phoenix's hands trembled.

**PHOENIX:** "I am going to be so normal about this."

The welfare officer made a note.

> **ASSESSMENT:** Phoenix is not being normal about this.

---

# 7. A VERY IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

C.O.R.E. reminds personnel that Endmin and Jake are still individuals.

Their feline forms do not remove their autonomy.

They are not research specimens, departmental mascots, or public entertainment.

No one is entitled to pick them up, pet them, photograph them, or disturb their rest without appropriate consideration of their comfort and consent.

Phoenix has been reminded of this.

She agreed immediately.

She then asked whether sitting quietly beside two sleeping cats counted as disturbing them.

It does not.

She has therefore been permitted to sit on the floor nearby, provided she allows both cats to approach or leave freely.

Phoenix has accepted these terms with extraordinary seriousness.

She is currently sitting perfectly still.

Jake is asleep in her lap.

Endmin is asleep on the cushion beside her.

Phoenix has not moved for twenty-three minutes.

**WELFARE OFFICER:** "You can adjust your position if you're uncomfortable."

**PHOENIX:** "I am comfortable."

**WELFARE OFFICER:** "Your leg is asleep."

**PHOENIX:** "My leg can wait."

**WELFARE OFFICER:** "Phoenix."

**PHOENIX:** "I have been chosen."

The officer decided not to argue.

---

# 8. END­MIN'S FIRST DAY OFF

At 11:30, a CED representative arrived to provide a brief update on departmental operations.

Belle intercepted the representative before they entered the room.

**BELLE:** "Does Endmin need to hear this?"

**REPRESENTATIVE:** "Not urgently."

**BELLE:** "Can it wait?"

**REPRESENTATIVE:** "Yes."

**BELLE:** "Then it can wait."

The representative left.

Endmin slept through the entire exchange.

At 12:15, she woke up long enough to eat.

At 12:27, she returned to her cushion.

At 13:04, she moved into a patch of sunlight.

At 13:06, she began grooming one paw.

At 13:11, she fell asleep again.

At 14:20, she briefly watched Jake attempt to play with a toy.

At 14:22, she decided watching was too much effort and went back to sleep.

No departmental decisions were made.

No reports were reviewed.

No meetings were attended.

No one died because the director took a nap.

Belle was informed of this development.

**BELLE:** "Excellent."

---

# 9. MEDICAL AND ADMINISTRATIVE FOLLOW-UP

The anomalous transformation remains under investigation.

C.O.R.E. will continue non-invasive monitoring and arrange further assessment when appropriate.

However, the investigation will not become an excuse to interrupt Endmin's rest unnecessarily.

The more immediate concern is her recent pattern of overwork.

C.O.R.E. has initiated a workload review to determine:

- Which duties can be delegated permanently.
    
- Whether Endmin has been carrying responsibilities that should belong to other personnel.
    
- Why repeated opportunities for rest were missed.
    
- Whether departmental expectations have encouraged unsustainable working hours.
    
- What safeguards are needed to prevent a recurrence.
    

These measures will remain in place after Endmin returns to her normal form.

The objective is not merely to help her recover from the current incident.

It is to prevent the circumstances that preceded it from continuing unchanged.

**A cat-shaped director is an unusual administrative problem.**

**A director working herself into the ground is not an acceptable ordinary one.**

---

# 10. FINAL OBSERVATIONS

At the time of this report, Endmin remains a cat.

Jake remains a cat.

Neko and Miss Neko have returned to normal.

Phoenix has been given a day off and is using it to sit beside two cats.

Belle has approved Endmin's leave and has explicitly forbidden the CED from interrupting it for non-urgent matters.

The transformation's cause remains unknown.

Endmin's exhaustion is not.

For the first time in several weeks, she is not expected to finish anything.

She does not have to review another report.

She does not have to attend another meeting.

She does not have to justify a break.

She can sleep until she wakes naturally.

She can eat when she is hungry.

She can play if she wants.

She can do absolutely nothing.

And if she spends the entire day curled into a ball on a cushion, that is not a failure of leadership.

It is a day off.

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Phoenix has been seen sitting on the floor with Jake asleep in her lap and Endmin curled beside her.

She has declined three offers to bring her a chair because she does not wish to disturb either cat.

Belle has passed by twice.

The first time, she checked that Endmin was comfortable.

The second time, she placed another blanket beside her daughter.

Endmin woke briefly when the blanket settled over her.

She looked at her mother.

Belle gently stroked her head once, then left her alone.

Endmin went back to sleep.

Across the room, Jake's tail twitched in a dream.

Phoenix looked down at him with the expression of someone who had discovered the meaning of life and found it covered in fur.

**PHOENIX:** "Best day ever."

No further comment was necessary.
