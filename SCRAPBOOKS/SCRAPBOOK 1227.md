# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following the attempted abduction of **Nora Tachyon**, C.O.R.E. recovered a substantial quantity of Woodland Coorp communications from the hostile group's network.

The communications document the rapid deterioration of the operation.

Woodland initially reported successful acquisition of the target.

Shortly afterward, communications became increasingly confused.

Personnel began reporting two hostile entities:

1. **A white-feathered falcon-like individual carrying a firearm.**
    
2. **A young Shark Thiren carrying a knife.**
    

The second entity was positively identified as:

> **Nora Tachyon.**

The first entity has not been identified.

Notably, the white-feathered falcon was **not present at the location when MSAC, Elsher Tachyon, and Neri Tachyon arrived.**

The communications indicate that Nora subsequently moved through the hostile formation alone.

She repeatedly taunted Woodland personnel with the same phrase:

> **"Don't you dare touch me. Come here, let's play tag!"**

The resulting engagement left approximately **1,250 Woodland mercenaries either incapacitated or deceased.**

Nora was subsequently recovered alive.

# 16. ANALYSIS OF THE FALCON

C.O.R.E. has not identified the white-feathered falcon.

Possible explanations remain under investigation.

The communications establish only the following:

- Woodland personnel independently reported seeing it;
    
- multiple units described white feathers;
    
- multiple units described a firearm;
    
- the entity was capable of moving through the engagement area;
    
- it was not present when Nora was recovered;
    
- no remains corresponding to the entity were found;
    
- Nora did not mention the entity during initial recovery.
    

No conclusion regarding its identity has been authorized.

---

# 17. WELFARE CONSIDERATION

The communications contain an important distinction.

Nora's repeated statement:

> **"Don't you dare touch me."**

should not be interpreted as playful permission to pursue her.

The subsequent:

> **"Come here, let's play tag!"**

appears to have been directed toward hostile personnel attempting to physically restrain her.

The context is therefore significant.

Nora was not initiating ordinary play.

She was refusing to be captured.

Her unusual happiness during the engagement remains under welfare review.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Woodland Coorp entered the Blacksite perimeter intending to abduct a six-year-old resident.

Their communications initially describe a successful operation.

Within minutes, their reports changed.

First:

> **"White-feathered falcon."**

Then:

> **"The child has a knife."**

Then:

> **"She's smiling."**

Then:

> **"She's playing."**

Then:

> **"She's between us and the exit."**

Approximately **1,250 mercenaries** were subsequently reported incapacitated or deceased.

Nora was recovered alive.

The falcon was absent.

The exact circumstances remain unknown.

What is known is that Nora adapted.

And Woodland personnel discovered, considerably too late, that being six years old did not mean Nora would remain helpless after being taken.

Woodland thought they had taken a child.

Their communications tell a different story.

A white-feathered falcon appeared.

Then she started smiling.

> **"Don't you dare touch me."**

> **"Come here, let's play tag!"**
