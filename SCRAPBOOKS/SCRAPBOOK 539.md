### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / BIOLOGICAL REVITALIZATION REPORT**

# **CORIN — THE RETURN OF ORGANIC FUNCTION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **Ether-Induced Organ Revitalization**  
**Status:** **ONGOING / STABLE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's biological recovery has progressed significantly.

Her heart is now functioning.

Her body has begun producing and circulating **its own blood**.

Most significantly, Corin now requires **oxygen to remain biologically stable.**

Her organs, previously sustained primarily through Ether, have begun returning to functional biological states.

The current leading explanation is that Corin's Ether has become sufficiently concentrated and stable to **revitalize her previously nonfunctional organs.**

---

# **THE DISCOVERY**

The change was initially detected through routine monitoring.

Corin began exhibiting measurable oxygen consumption.

At first, this was considered anomalous background activity.

Further testing confirmed that oxygen was being actively utilized by her tissues.

Her lungs were functioning.

Her blood was carrying oxygen.

Her heart was circulating it.

The conclusion was unavoidable:

> **Corin's body had begun functioning as a biological organism again.**

---

# **BLOOD**

Corin now possesses her own circulating blood.

It is biologically functional and distinct from her Ether.

The blood:

- Circulates through her body.
    
- Carries oxygen.
    
- Supports organ function.
    
- Responds to normal physiological demands.
    
- Can clot and repair minor vascular damage.
    

Ether remains present throughout her circulatory system, but it no longer appears to be the sole mechanism sustaining her body.

For the first time, Corin has something resembling a conventional circulatory system.

---

# **THE HEART**

Corin's heartbeat has become stronger and more consistent.

It is no longer merely a weak anomalous rhythm.

Her heart responds to activity.

During flight, her heart rate increases.

During sleep, it decreases.

During excitement, it accelerates.

C.O.R.E. considers this significant.

Her body is no longer merely **imitating** biological function.

It is responding to stimuli as a living organism would.

---

# **OXYGEN DEPENDENCY**

Corin now requires oxygen.

This represents one of the largest changes to her physiology.

Previously, Corin could remain in environments without breathable air for extended periods because her body did not rely on conventional respiration.

That is no longer the case.

Her revitalized organs consume oxygen normally.

Extended oxygen deprivation is therefore considered medically dangerous.

Her Ether may provide limited emergency support, but it is no longer considered a substitute for respiration.

---

# **ETHER'S ROLE**

The current hypothesis is that Corin's Ether underwent a gradual transformation.

Rather than merely replacing biological functions, the Ether began **reconstructing and revitalizing them.**

Over time, increasingly strong Ether concentrations may have:

1. Stabilized damaged organs.
    
2. Reconstructed degraded tissue.
    
3. Reactivated dormant biological systems.
    
4. Restored cellular function.
    
5. Allowed the organs to resume normal operation.
    

The process appears to have occurred naturally.

No Architect initiated it.

No external procedure triggered it.

---

# **WHY NOW?**

C.O.R.E. cannot yet determine why the process accelerated recently.

Several major physiological changes have occurred within the same general period:

- Development of wings.
    
- Increasing Ether stability.
    
- Return of Corin's voice.
    
- Restoration of color perception.
    
- Return of biological heartbeat.
    
- Reestablishment of blood circulation.
    
- Restoration of oxygen-dependent organ function.
    

These changes may be interconnected.

They may represent different stages of the same underlying process.

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

Corin was initially fascinated by the discovery.

She placed a hand against her chest.

She could feel her heartbeat.

She then watched a medical monitor displaying her oxygen saturation.

> **"...I need air now?"**

Guiding Light nodded.

> **"Yes."**

Corin took a deliberate breath.

She paused.

Then smiled.

> **"That's weird."**

A second breath.

> **"...I like it."**

---

# **THE SYMBOLISM**

The development has had an unusually strong psychological effect on Corin.

For years, she believed that her body was merely an Ether-powered imitation of a person.

She had no heartbeat.

No blood.

No conventional biological function.

Now she has all three.

Her body is beginning to operate without requiring Ether to substitute for every biological process.

Ether is no longer simply **keeping her corpse moving.**

It appears to have helped restore the body itself.

---

Corin looked down at herself.

> **"...Apparently."**
