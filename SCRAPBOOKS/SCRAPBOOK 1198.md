# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon has formally requested her own Blacksite laptop.

The proposal was presented directly to:

- **Neko** — Head of Engineering & Archives;
    
- **Director Lucious** — Aperture Director / Overseer;
    
- **Head of the IT Department** — Blacksite Information Technology Administration.
    

Nora arrived prepared.

She had apparently spent considerable time thinking about the request.

Unfortunately for Nora, so had the people responsible for maintaining the Blacksite's computing infrastructure.

The final decision was:

> **DENIED.**

---

# 2. THE PROPOSAL

The meeting began in one of the SAFEHOUSE conference rooms.

Nora stood at the front of the table.

She had brought:

- one sheet of paper;
    
- one pencil;
    
- several handwritten points;
    
- and an expression of complete confidence.
    

Neko looked at the document.

> **NEKO:**  
> "What's this?"

> **NORA:**  
> "My laptop proposal."

Neko immediately looked toward Lucious.

Lucious looked toward the Head of IT.

The Head of IT quietly sighed.

> **HEAD OF IT:**  
> "We knew this was coming."

Nora smiled.

---

# 3. NORA'S ARGUMENT

Nora presented her case.

### POINT ONE — EDUCATION

> **NORA:**  
> "I want to learn."

Neko nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> "I want to learn chemistry."

Neko nodded again.

> **NORA:**  
> "And other things."

Lucious asked:

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> "Such as?"

Nora thought.

> **NORA:**  
> "Computers."

Neko immediately wrote something down.

The Head of IT looked concerned.

---

Nora continued.

> **NORA:**

Neko nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> "She does chemistry on it."

> **NEKO:**  
> "Yes."

> "I do chemistry."

> **NORA:**  
> "So I should have a laptop."

> **NEKO:**

> **NORA:**

> **NEKO:**

Nora looked confused.

> **NORA:**

> "You don't know what a terabyte is."

Nora paused.

> **NORA:**  
> "...No."

> **NEKO:**  
> "Exactly."

Nora presented her strongest argument.

> "I'm responsible."

> **REASON #1: BEHAVIORAL CONCERNS.**

Nora had apparently anticipated the storage problem.

She produced another page.

> **NORA:**  
> "I don't need twelve terabytes."

Then he noticed the final line.

> **NEKO:**  
> "Why does this say 'expand later'?"

> **NORA:**

> **NEKO:**  
> "No."

Nora then attempted her final argument.

> "Neri has one."

> **LUCIOUS:**

> **NORA:**  
> "I'm six."

> **LUCIOUS:**

---

# 8. THE ACTUAL PROBLEM

Neko finally explained.

The laptop itself was not the primary concern.

The concern was what unrestricted access would provide Nora:

- independent access to Blacksite computing systems;
    
- unrestricted research software;
    
- large-scale data storage;
    
- Internet access;
    
- laboratory documentation;
    
- internal research resources;
    
- and the ability to create, modify, and accumulate information without continuous supervision.
    

Nora listened carefully.

> **NORA:**  
> "So you don't trust me."

Neko shook his head.

> **NEKO:**  
> "That's not what I'm saying."

> **NORA:**  
> "Then what?"

Neko pointed toward her.

> **NEKO:**  
> "You're six."

Nora considered this.

> **NORA:**  
> "...Oh."

---

# 9. LUCIOUS' DECISION

Lucious reviewed the proposal.

He agreed that Nora demonstrated exceptional intellectual ability.

He also acknowledged her unusually strong interest in chemistry.

However:

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> "Being intelligent doesn't automatically mean you're ready for unrestricted equipment."

Nora looked disappointed.

Lucious continued.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> "You can learn."

Nora looked up.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> "You can study."

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> "You can work with Neri."

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> "But your own unrestricted Blacksite laptop is not being approved."

Nora sighed.

> **NORA:**  
> "...Okay."

---

The Head of IT then added:

> **HEAD OF IT:**

> **HEAD OF IT:**  
> "It's because you're six."

> **NORA:**

> **HEAD OF IT:**  
> "Because it's still true."

Nora crossed her arms.

> **NORA:**  
> "I can use computers."

> **HEAD OF IT:**  
> "I'm sure you can."

> **NORA:**  
> "I'm good at chemistry."

> **HEAD OF IT:**  
> "I'm sure."

> **NORA:**  
> "I'm good with guns."

Silence.

Neko slowly turned toward her.

> **NEKO:**  
> "That was not helping your case."

Nora immediately realized her mistake.

> **NORA:**  
> "...Right."

---

# 11. FINAL DECISION

The proposal was formally rejected.

### Decision:

**PERSONAL BLACKSITE LAPTOP:**  
**DENIED**

### Reasons:

1. Nora is six years old.
    
2. Unrestricted Blacksite computing access is unnecessary for her current needs.
    
3. Independent access to internal systems is inappropriate at her current age.
    
4. Her demonstrated intelligence does not eliminate supervision requirements.
    
5. Her existing behavior indicates a tendency toward curiosity-driven unauthorized exploration.
    
6. Neri already provides supervised access to chemistry research when appropriate.
    

---

# 12. APPROVED ALTERNATIVE

Nora is permitted to:

- use Neri's research materials under supervision;
    
- maintain her own physical chemistry notebook;
    
- study chemistry with Neri;
    
- use designated educational computing equipment when supervised;
    
- ask Neko, Miss Neko, or IT personnel for assistance with computer-related questions;
    
- access age-appropriate educational material under controlled conditions.
    

Nora is **not** receiving an unrestricted personal Blacksite laptop.
