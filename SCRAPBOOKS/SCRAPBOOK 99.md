# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **ANOMALOUS OBSERVATION RECORD**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – ETHER MANIFESTATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Director Authorization Required

---

## **SUMMARY**

A new development has been voluntarily reported by Corin Lockwood regarding the persistent Ether manifestation resembling her late mother.

Previous evaluations considered three primary hypotheses:

- An Ether-generated phenomenon.
    
- A psychological manifestation associated with trauma.
    
- An unidentified anomalous effect.
    

Recent observations have complicated all three.

---

## **THE CONVERSATIONS**

For several weeks, the figure continued speaking about Heaven's Blacksite.

At first, Corin assumed it was simply recalling memories.

Then the details became... current.

One evening, while Corin was reviewing financial statements, the figure quietly smiled.

> **"Belle burned today's cookies."**

Corin looked up.

"...Belle doesn't burn cookies."

The figure laughed softly.

> **"She was distracted."**

---

The following evening.

> **"Neko is being chased out of Engineering again."**

Corin sighed.

"...That could happen any day."

---

Another day.

> **"Norma made far too much lunch."**

> **"She still cooks as if you're going to walk through the door."**

---

Then...

The figure mentioned something impossible.

> **"Arcturus is pretending he isn't worried."**

> **"He's looking out the SAFEHOUSE window again."**

> **"He thinks nobody notices."**

Corin froze.

She had not spoken to Arcturus in days.

---

## **VERIFICATION**

Out of curiosity, Corin discreetly contacted Norma the following afternoon.

She intentionally asked unrelated questions before casually mentioning lunch.

Norma laughed.

> "...Yeah."

> "...I accidentally made enough for another person again."

Corin's expression changed.

Later that evening she spoke with Belle.

Belle apologized.

> "...Sorry."

> "...I ruined a batch of cookies yesterday."

Finally...

Arcturus admitted, after some reluctant prodding,

> "...I might've been staring out the SAFEHOUSE window."

> "...Don't tell anyone."

Corin ended the call in silence.

Every detail had been correct.

None had been shared with her beforehand.

---

## **PATIENT ACCOUNT**

Corin remained awake long after midnight.

The office was quiet.

She slowly turned toward the figure.

For the first time...

She asked a question she had avoided for weeks.

> "...Who are you?"

The figure looked at her with a gentle expression.

She did not answer immediately.

Instead, she walked closer.

Resting a hand lightly against Corin's shoulder.

> **"...Who do you think I am?"**

Corin felt tears forming.

She had spent weeks convincing herself the figure was only Ether.

Only grief.

Only loneliness.

But...

It knew things she could not have known.

It spoke with memories no record contained.

It remembered lullabies Corin had almost forgotten.

The way her mother used to tuck blankets around her when she was ill.

The nicknames only their family had used.

Corin's voice trembled.

> "...Mom?"

The figure smiled.

A smile Corin had not truly seen since she was eight years old.

> **"...Hello, sweetheart."**

Corin broke down crying.

Not because she was frightened.

Because for the first time...

She no longer believed she was speaking to something her mind had created.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed Corin's testimony together with the corroborated details.

He declined to draw a definitive conclusion.

His annotation was brief.

> "The origin of the manifestation cannot yet be established with scientific certainty."

He continued.

> "However..."

> "The accuracy of information unavailable to Corin at the time of each conversation warrants continued anomalous investigation."

He closed the file.

> "Some questions deserve careful study."

> "Others deserve quiet respect."

---

## **STATUS**

The Ether manifestation remains under observation.

No hostile behavior has ever been reported.

Corin describes its presence as calm, reassuring, and consistently encouraging her to care for herself and reconnect with the people who love her.

Research into the phenomenon continues.

For Corin, however, the investigation no longer matters as much as it once did.

Because regardless of what future research may conclude...

When she looked into the figure's eyes that night, she no longer saw a stranger.

She saw...

Her mother.

**End of Anomalous Observation Record**
