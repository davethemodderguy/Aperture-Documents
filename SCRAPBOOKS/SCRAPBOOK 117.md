### **FAMILY IDENTIFICATION RECORD**

## **SUBJECT: LOCKWOOD FAMILY NAMING HISTORY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the return of the Lockwood family to closer contact, C.O.R.E. documented a small but meaningful detail regarding their names.

For years, the Lockwoods carried their names without fully knowing their origins.

After Lumi's return, the family finally learned the meaning behind them.

Not as official titles.

Not as symbols.

As names given by a mother.

---

# **ARCTURUS LOCKWOOD**

**Name Meaning:** Associated with guardianship and the stars.

Lumi explained that Arcturus' name was chosen because she wanted her first child to represent someone who would always look after others.

The meaning became unexpectedly accurate.

From childhood, Arcturus showed a natural tendency to protect.

He watched over Norma.

He watched over Corin.

Even when he was too young to understand the problems around him, he always tried to stand between danger and his family.

The same personality followed him into adulthood.

His role as KCA's "Gunslinger" was not chosen because he wanted to appear intimidating.

It was because he became exactly what his name represented.

A guardian.

---

# **NORMA LOCKWOOD**

**Name Meaning:** Associated with guidance and order.

Lumi chose Norma's name because she believed Norma would become someone who could guide others.

The irony was that young Norma was often the one who needed guidance.

Especially during thunderstorms.

Despite being frightened, Norma always tried to act brave.

Over time, that fear became determination.

The same person who once hid from storms became someone capable of creating technology, leading projects, and protecting others.

---

# **CORIN LOCKWOOD**

**Name Meaning:** Associated with heart and strength.

Lumi explained that Corin's name was chosen because she was the most emotionally aware of the three.

Not the strongest physically.

Not the loudest.

But the one who cared the most.

Corin remembered details others forgot.

She noticed when someone was upset.

She wanted to help.

Even after everything she experienced, that part of her never disappeared.

---

# **LUMIA LOCKWOOD**

**Preferred Name:** Lumi

Lumi's original name is Lumia Lockwood.

However, she does not prefer being called Lumia.

The reason is simple.

When Corin was young, she struggled with pronouncing her mother's name.

"Lumia" became:

> "Lumi."

The nickname stayed.

Even after death.

Even after fifteen years.

Even after returning through Ether.

When personnel asked whether she wanted to be formally addressed as Lumia, her answer was immediate.

> "Corin named me first."

> "I want to keep that."

---

# **FAMILY OBSERVATION**

During the discussion, Corin quietly admitted something.

She had spent years believing she lost everything.

Her childhood.

Her family.

Her identity.

Her mother.

But hearing the meaning behind their names reminded her of something important.

Before the experiments.

Before the trauma.

Before the Ether.

They were a family.

Not subjects.

Not survivors.

Just a family.

---

# **PERSONNEL NOTE**

Belle summarized the situation simply:

> "Names are kind of like little gifts."

Neko added:

> "And Lumi kept hers."

After a pause:

> "Because Corin gave it to her."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Lockwood family continues rebuilding their relationship.

The past cannot be changed.

The lost years cannot be returned.

But the names remain.

Arcturus.

Norma.

Corin.

Lumi.

Four names connected by a family that survived losing each other.

And finally found their way back.
