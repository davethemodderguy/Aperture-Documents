# **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

Over the past several days, Heaven's Blacksite security has recorded a **steady and measurable increase in SPAWN activity along the facility's external perimeter.**

The encounters initially consisted of isolated individuals and small groups observed at considerable distance from the Blacksite.

This is no longer the case.

SPAWN personnel are now being observed:

- At multiple perimeter sectors simultaneously.
    
- Increasingly close to established security positions.
    
- Conducting prolonged surveillance of Blacksite infrastructure.
    
- Carrying specialized anomalous-targeting weaponry.
    
- Operating in larger and more organized groups.
    
- Withdrawing immediately following security detection rather than initiating prolonged engagements.
    

No confirmed SPAWN personnel have successfully penetrated the Blacksite.

However, the frequency and organization of these encounters indicate that their presence is **deliberate.**

---

# **1. ACTIVITY TREND**

The first confirmed encounters were treated as isolated incursions.

Security personnel were instructed to intercept approaching individuals and force them away from restricted territory.

Most encounters ended within several minutes.

SPAWN personnel generally responded to warning shots or direct engagement by retreating into surrounding terrain.

Over time, however, the frequency of sightings increased.

# **2. BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATIONS**

One detail has become increasingly concerning.

SPAWN personnel do not appear to be attempting to simply approach the facility.

They appear to be **watching it.**

Security recordings have documented personnel remaining concealed for extended periods while observing:

- Patrol routes.
    
- Vehicle movements.
    
- Security checkpoints.
    
- Searchlight patterns.
    
- Perimeter response times.
    
- Personnel deployment.
    
- Gate activity.
    

In several cases, SPAWN personnel withdrew before security teams could establish visual contact.

This behavior is inconsistent with a conventional assault.

It is more consistent with **reconnaissance.**

---

# **3. REPEATED CONTACTS**

Several SPAWN members appear to have been encountered more than once.

Despite being forced away during previous encounters, certain individuals have returned to the same perimeter sectors.

Their behavior suggests familiarity with the surrounding terrain.

One security officer described the situation as:

> _"They're not getting lost out there."_

Another stated:

> _"They already know where we look."_

C.O.R.E. considers these observations credible.

---

# **4. WEAPONRY**

SPAWN personnel continue to carry specialized equipment.

Recovered equipment has included weaponry unsuitable for conventional military classification.

The previously confiscated weapon:

> **SPAWN-DMEIR-01**  
> **DARK MATTER ENERGY IMPULSE RIFLE**

remains under C.O.R.E. analysis.

The weapon's existence indicates that SPAWN has access to a capable external weapons supplier.

Security teams have reported seeing weapons visually similar to SPAWN-DMEIR-01 during recent encounters.

This suggests that the confiscated rifle is **not a unique prototype.**

---

# **5. ESCALATION WITHOUT ASSAULT**

Despite the increased presence, SPAWN has not attempted a full-scale assault on Heaven's Blacksite.

This distinction is important.

Their current pattern is:

> **Approach → Observe → Remain briefly → Detect Security → Withdraw**

rather than:

> **Approach → Attack → Breach**

C.O.R.E. therefore assesses that SPAWN's immediate objective is currently unknown.

Possible explanations include:

- Reconnaissance.
    
- Target identification.
    
- Security assessment.
    
- Searching for an individual.
    
- Searching for an object.
    
- Waiting for a future opportunity.
    
- Monitoring Blacksite activity.
    

No explanation has been confirmed.

---

# **6. ALICE CONSIDERATION**

The recovery of **Alice** has introduced an additional concern.

Alice is currently under Aperture medical custody following her prolonged disappearance.

She was recovered near a grave believed to belong to her mother.

The site contained numerous anomalous papers and drawings, including repeated representations of the SPAWN symbol.

Alice has demonstrated a severe fear response when exposed to the symbol.

She has also stated:

> **"...They hurt people."**

C.O.R.E. cannot currently establish whether Alice's fear represents:

- A direct memory.
    
- A fragmented memory.
    
- An anomalous recognition response.
    
- A traumatic association.
    
- Or another phenomenon.
    

However, the increasing SPAWN presence around the Blacksite makes Alice's current status a **security consideration.**

No evidence currently confirms that SPAWN knows Alice has been recovered.

---

# **7. SECURITY RESPONSE**

Effective immediately, Blacksite perimeter security has been increased.

The following measures are now active:

- Increased patrol frequency.
    
- Additional observation posts.
    
- Expanded nighttime surveillance.
    
- Rotational deployment of anomalous-response personnel.
    
- Immediate confiscation of abandoned SPAWN equipment.
    
- Increased monitoring of surrounding terrain.
    
- Rapid-response teams placed on standby.
    

Personnel are instructed **not to pursue retreating SPAWN members beyond the established security perimeter** unless explicitly authorized.

The objective is containment and protection of the Blacksite.

Not retaliation.

---

# **8. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

The increasing number of SPAWN encounters cannot reasonably be considered coincidental.

SPAWN is becoming increasingly comfortable operating near Heaven's Blacksite.

They have not yet demonstrated the capability—or willingness—to breach the facility.

That may change.

The most concerning possibility is that the organization is gradually learning the Blacksite's defensive structure while avoiding unnecessary casualties.

In other words:

> **They may be waiting.**

For what remains unknown.
