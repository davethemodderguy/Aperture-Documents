# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORIZATION**

## **CONFIDENTIAL PSYCHOLOGICAL PROGRESS NOTE**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – SOCIAL WITHDRAWAL OBSERVATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical Director & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION**

Over the past several weeks, Corin Lockwood has increasingly responded to concern from colleagues with a consistent request.

> "...Please."

> "...Leave me alone."

She does not raise her voice.

She does not become angry.

Instead, she politely distances herself.

She closes her office door.

Engages the electronic locks.

Returns to her work.

To anyone passing by, it appears she simply prefers uninterrupted concentration.

Those closest to her understand the difference.

She is not pushing people away because she dislikes them.

She is withdrawing because she believes her pain should remain hers alone.

---

## **CLINICAL NOTE**

Counselors have documented that Corin's request for solitude often occurs during periods of heightened emotional distress.

While quiet time can be a healthy coping strategy, prolonged isolation has also been identified as a significant risk factor for worsening symptoms.

For this reason, staff have been advised to respect her need for privacy while also maintaining gentle, consistent contact.

The goal is not to force conversation.

It is to ensure she continues to know that support remains available whenever she is ready to accept it.

---

## **CORE TRAUMA BELIEF**

One belief has remained remarkably persistent throughout Corin's treatment.

She continues to describe herself as someone who died during the experiments.

From her perspective, the person walking the halls of KCA is only able to exist because of the Ether integrated into her body.

She once explained this conflict during therapy.

> "...Everyone tells me I'm alive."

> "...I understand why they say that."

A long silence followed.

> "...But when I think about what happened..."

> "...It feels like the girl I was never came back."

The attending psychologist documented that this statement reflects Corin's trauma and grief, rather than a factual assessment of her condition.

---

## **THE ETHER**

Medical analysis concludes that the stabilized Ether sustaining Corin's physiology has become part of her biological state.

Clinically, it is a life-sustaining phenomenon.

Corin, however, struggles to see it that way.

To her, it remains inseparable from the abuse she endured.

She once summarized that conflict quietly.

> "...People tell me it saved me."

> "...Some days..."

> "...All I can remember is that it was the thing they used."

This emotional association continues to be a central focus of therapy.

---

## **OBSERVATION FROM BELLE**

Belle was asked whether Corin's insistence on being left alone should always be taken literally.

Belle thought for several moments before answering.

> "When she says she wants to be alone..."

> "...I think what she really wants..."

> "...is for someone to stay without making her feel like she's a burden."

---

## **ARCTURUS' REQUEST**

Arcturus submitted a standing request to Executive Wellness.

> "If she tells you she needs space..."

> "Give her space."

He paused before adding:

> "...Just don't let that become the same thing as being forgotten."

The request was unanimously approved.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the assessment and added the following note.

> "Trauma often teaches people that accepting help inconveniences others."

> "Recovery begins when that belief is gently, consistently disproven."

> "Respect her boundaries."

> "Keep the door open."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin continues to fulfill her responsibilities with exceptional professionalism while participating in ongoing medical and psychological care.

Her colleagues have gradually adapted to supporting her in ways that respect both her independence and her wellbeing.

They no longer try to force open her office door.

Instead, someone quietly leaves a fresh cup of tea outside.

A handwritten note.

An invitation to lunch.

A reminder that there will always be a place waiting for her when she chooses to step back out.
