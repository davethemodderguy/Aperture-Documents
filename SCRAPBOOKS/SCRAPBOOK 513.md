# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **MISSING PERSON / ANOMALOUS INCIDENT FOLLOW-UP**

# **FOURTEEN DAYS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **MISSING — NO TRACE**  
**Time Since Disappearance:** **14 DAYS**  
**Search Status:** **ACTIVE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Two weeks have passed since Corin disappeared from the treehouse.

There has been no confirmed sighting.

No physical trace has been discovered.

No additional Ether residue has been found.

No communication has been received.

No known anomalous entity has claimed responsibility.

No Architect has been able to locate her.

The search continues.

There is still no explanation for how Corin left the treehouse without leaving a conventional trail.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

The treehouse remains almost exactly as it was on the day of her disappearance.

Her belongings have been left undisturbed.

Her spare clothes remain folded.

Her red wool beanie remains where she last left it.

Her scarf remains hanging near the entrance.

Her writing supplies have been replenished.

The chair where Corin usually sat remains empty.

Orphie refuses to move it.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

Corin's notebook remains secured by C.O.R.E.

The final pages have been reviewed repeatedly.

No hidden message has been discovered.

No anomalous writing behavior has been detected.

The entries remain what they appeared to be:

The writings of someone who had become deeply convinced that she was a burden.

Guiding Light has read the final pages several times.

She has not allowed anyone to destroy them.

> **"These aren't her last words."**

> **"They're the words she wrote when she was hurting."**

---

# **THE ETHER RESIDUE**

The faint Ether puddle discovered near the doorway remains the only physical anomaly associated with Corin's disappearance.

The sample eventually disintegrated.

Analysis revealed no usable trail.

No dimensional coordinates could be extracted.

No signature could be matched to another location.

Curious Light has been unable to reproduce its unusual persistence.

His current conclusion is simply:

> **"Something happened here."**

He cannot determine what.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light has continued searching every day.

She has expanded her search beyond the areas normally accessible to conventional tracking.

Nothing.

She has repeatedly attempted to sense Corin.

Nothing.

She has attempted to locate the Ether signature associated with Corin's body.

Nothing.

For perhaps the first time since meeting her, Guiding Light has encountered something she genuinely cannot reach.

She refuses to interpret that as proof that Corin is dead.

> **"If she were gone, we'd know."**

Curious Light asked:

> **"Are you sure?"**

Guiding Light did not answer.

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT**

Curious Light has become increasingly frustrated.

He has reconstructed the timeline.

He has examined the doorway.

He has examined the remaining Ether.

He has studied the notebook.

He has searched for dimensional disturbances.

Nothing connects.

The disappearance appears almost impossibly clean.

He eventually wrote:

> **"I hate unsolved problems."**

Then added:

> **"This one isn't a problem."**

> **"It's Corin."**

---

# **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT**

Mischevious Light has remained unusually serious.

His enforcement duties have continued.

But whenever possible, he returns to the search.

He has investigated places that even the other Architects considered unlikely.

Nothing.

During one return to the treehouse, Guiding Light asked:

> **"Any luck?"**

Mischevious Light shook his head.

> **"No."**

She looked toward the empty chair.

> **"Fourteen days."**

He remained silent.

Then:

> **"We'll keep looking."**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has been affected the most visibly.

She still returns to the treehouse.

She still leaves food available.

She still checks the doorway.

Sometimes she sits in Corin's usual place.

Sometimes she writes messages in Corin's notebook.

Not because she expects an immediate response.

Just in case.

One page contains only:

> **"I'm still here."**

Another:

> **"Come home."**

---

# **JAY**

Jay continues staying near the treehouse.

Guiding Light has tried taking him back with her.

He repeatedly returns.

He appears to associate the treehouse with Corin.

He frequently sits on the railing and watches the forest.

His usual mischievous behavior has decreased significantly.

He still steals things occasionally.

But he no longer takes Corin's belongings.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE**

The SAFEHOUSE residents have been informed.

The atmosphere has changed.

Neko has quietly checked security records multiple times.

Miss Neko has assisted with search coordination.

Belle has left small gifts at the treehouse.

Endmin has remained available to Orphie.

Bao and Frost have helped monitor the surrounding area.

Taiga has spent unusually long periods sitting outside.

Mart has even stopped stealing people's snacks.

For approximately three days.

Nobody commented on it.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

**Corin remains missing.**

There is still no evidence confirming her death.

There is still no evidence confirming her location.

There is still no explanation for the Ether residue.

There is still no explanation for how she disappeared.

The investigation remains open.

The search remains active.

Nobody has officially declared Corin deceased.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Fourteen days have passed.

The forest has returned to normal.

The weather has changed.

The treehouse remains standing.

The blue jay still waits on the railing.

The Architects continue searching.

Orphie still leaves the door unlocked.

And the empty chair remains exactly where Corin left it.

No one knows where she went.

No one knows whether she is alone.

No one knows whether she can still write.

But every evening, someone leaves the porch light on.

Just in case she finds her way home.
