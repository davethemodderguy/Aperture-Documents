# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Further observation of **Sky**, Nora Tachyon's anomalous falcon familiar, has established an additional capability.

Sky is capable of **changing its physical size**.

At its normal size, Sky behaves similarly to a small falcon and routinely perches on Nora's:

- finger;
    
- shoulder;
    
- head;
    
- or white beret.
    

Sky may also perch on nearby furniture, particularly tables and shelves.

When Nora requires transportation, Sky can increase its size sufficiently for Nora to safely grip its legs and be carried through the air.

This has become Nora's preferred alternative to walking when she does not feel like walking.

C.O.R.E. has informally classified this behavior as:

> **"Nora's Flying Transportation System."**

---

# 1. NORMAL PERCHING

Sky has demonstrated four preferred perching locations when at normal size.

### Finger

Nora can extend one finger.

Sky lands on it.

The familiar's claws maintain a secure but non-injurious grip.

---

### Shoulder

Sky frequently rests on Nora's shoulder while she walks.

Nora appears to treat this as completely ordinary.

---

### Head

Sky commonly sits on top of Nora's white beret.

The familiar's claws do not damage the hat.

Nora has apparently accepted having a falcon permanently attached to her head as part of her daily routine.

---

### Nearby Surface

Sky may also land on:

- tables;
    
- shelves;
    
- railings;
    
- chairs;
    
- counters;
    
- and other suitable nearby surfaces.
    

The familiar does not require Nora to remain physically close at all times.

---

# 2. SIZE CHANGE

Sky possesses a significant anomalous ability:

> **Variable physical scale.**

Its normal presentation is relatively small.

However, Sky can increase its size substantially.

The process does not appear to involve conventional growth.

There is no observed transitional biological process.

Sky simply becomes larger.

The familiar remains visually consistent at different scales.

Its white feathers, blue eyes, moon collar, and blue nocturnal luminescence remain present.

---

# 3. NORA'S MOBILITY METHOD

When Sky is sufficiently large, Nora can hold onto its legs.

Sky then flies.

Nora is carried beneath it.

From an external perspective, the arrangement resembles a child being transported by a very large flying drone.

The difference is that:

> **Sky is not a drone.**

It is a living anomalous familiar responding directly to Nora.

Nora does not require a harness or mechanical attachment.

She simply grips the familiar's legs.

---

# 4. FIRST OBSERVED FLIGHT

The first formal observation occurred when Nora was asked to walk from the Merch Store to another SAFEHOUSE section.

Nora looked down the corridor.

Then looked at Sky.

Sky looked back.

Nora sighed.

> **Nora:**  
> "I don't want to walk."

She held out her hand.

Sky landed on her finger.

Nora then whispered:

> **Nora:**  
> "Big."

Sky increased in size.

Several nearby employees immediately stopped moving.

Nora grabbed Sky's legs.

Sky lifted off.

Nora was carried down the corridor.

One employee reportedly said:

> **"Did the child just call her falcon like a taxi?"**

C.O.R.E. could not dispute the observation.

---

# 5. PRIMARY TRANSPORTATION

Nora now routinely uses Sky when she does not want to walk.

Her current mobility preference can therefore be summarized as:

> **Walk if convenient.**

> **Get carried if possible.**

> **Fly if Sky is available.**

Sky is available most of the time.

Consequently, walking has become increasingly optional from Nora's perspective.

# 8. SKY'S CONTROLLED FLIGHT

Sky's flight behavior appears to be coordinated with Nora's intent.

Nora can direct Sky through thought.

The same cognitive connection used for remote vision appears to apply to movement.

Nora does not need to shout:

> "Left."

or:

> "Go there."

She thinks about where she wants to go.

Sky responds.

This allows Nora to remain largely silent while being transported.

---

# 9. VISUAL EXTENSION + MOBILITY

The combination of Sky's abilities creates an unusual arrangement.

Nora can:

1. remain physically stationary;
    
2. close her eyes or wear a blindfold;
    
3. perceive through Sky;
    
4. direct Sky elsewhere;
    
5. observe an area;
    
6. return Sky;
    
7. enlarge Sky;
    
8. grip its legs;
    
9. and be transported elsewhere.
    

This is effectively:

> **Remote vision + anomalous flight + personal transportation.**

Nora has described the arrangement more simply:

> **"Sky carries me."**

---

# 10. FAMILIAR ATTACHMENT

Despite its size-changing ability, Sky remains gentle with Nora.

At small size, it can perch on her finger.

At larger size, it can carry her.

At either scale, the familiar's claws remain non-injurious.

Nora's clothing is similarly unaffected.

The moon collar remains proportionally consistent during size changes.

No damage to Nora's beret, clothing, or body has been observed during normal interaction.

---

# 11. MOBILITY WELFARE CONSIDERATION

Nora's use of Sky as transportation is not currently considered medically concerning.

In fact, avoiding unnecessary walking may be beneficial when Nora is tired.

However, normal precautions remain applicable.

Personnel should not:

- deliberately frighten Sky while Nora is airborne;
    
- interfere with the familiar during flight;
    
- attempt to pull Nora from Sky;
    
- encourage unsafe aerial stunts;
    
- or treat the familiar as an amusement ride.
    

Nora's grip is not considered an adequate substitute for safe environmental judgment.

Sky's ability to fly does not make Nora immune to falls or other hazards.
