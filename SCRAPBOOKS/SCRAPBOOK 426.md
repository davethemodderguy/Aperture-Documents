## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / FAMILY DYNAMICS INCIDENT LOG**

# **ORPHIE — SEPARATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Critical Personnel Welfare  
**Subjects:** Orphie / Corin  
**Associated Personnel:** Lumi / Arcturus / Norma / SAFEHOUSE Residents  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **CRITICAL — FAMILY RELATIONSHIP DETERIORATING**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The relationship between Orphie and Corin has deteriorated further.

Orphie now openly expresses hatred toward Corin.

This represents a major change from their previous relationship.

Despite everything that happened during Orphie's disappearance, Corin had remained emotionally attached to her.

Orphie no longer reciprocates that attachment.

She has begun actively distancing herself from Corin.

---

# **ORPHIE'S POSITION**

When asked about Corin, Orphie no longer describes her as her sister with affection.

Instead:

> "...I can't stand her."

When asked why:

> "...Because everything became my problem."

She believes Corin's deterioration has consumed everyone's attention and turned Orphie's return into another crisis.

> "...I came back."

> "...She's still broken."

> "...And somehow I'm still expected to fix it."

---

# **SLEEPING ARRANGEMENT**

The two sisters previously shared the treehouse.

They no longer sleep together.

Orphie has stopped using her bed.

Instead, she sleeps on the floor on the opposite side of the room.

She deliberately chooses a location as far from Corin as possible.

When asked why:

> "...I don't want to be near her."

---

# **THE FLOOR**

Orphie does not appear uncomfortable sleeping on the floor.

She places a blanket underneath herself.

Sometimes she uses another blanket as a cover.

Then she turns away from Corin.

No conversation.

No goodnight.

No acknowledgement.

Just distance.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin noticed immediately.

She asked:

> "...Why are you sleeping there?"

Orphie:

> "...Because I don't want to sleep next to you."

Corin became silent.

After several seconds:

> "...Okay."

She did not argue.

She did not ask again.

---

# **THE NEW ROUTINE**

The treehouse now has an invisible dividing line.

**Corin's side.**

**Orphie's side.**

Neither crosses it unless absolutely necessary.

Meals are no longer shared.

Conversations are minimal.

Eye contact is rare.

The atmosphere has become hostile without either sister openly fighting.

---

# **ORPHIE'S FRUSTRATION**

Orphie increasingly blames Corin for the situation.

> "...I didn't ask for this."

> "...I didn't ask to come back."

> "...I didn't ask to become responsible for her."

> "...I just wanted to disappear."

She views Corin's continued instability as an obstacle preventing her from returning to the detached existence she wanted.

---

# **THE IRONY**

Orphie originally believed that leaving would prevent herself from becoming a burden.

Instead, her disappearance caused Corin to deteriorate.

Now Orphie has returned and views the resulting deterioration as something Corin has imposed upon her.

The cycle continues.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi was deeply troubled when she learned that Orphie now hated Corin.

> "...You don't have to love her every moment."

Orphie:

> "...I don't love her."

Lumi paused.

> "...She's still your sister."

> "...That doesn't mean I have to like her."

Lumi did not argue.

She understood that forcing affection would accomplish nothing.

---

# **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus was visibly angry.

> "...She's sick."

Orphie:

> "...So?"

> "...You know what happened to her."

> "...And I'm supposed to feel guilty forever?"

Arcturus stopped.

He realized arguing would accomplish nothing.

---

# **NORMA**

Norma's response was quieter.

> "...You don't have to take care of Corin."

Orphie looked at her.

> "...Good."

> "...But you don't have to hurt her either."

Orphie said nothing.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION**

The SAFEHOUSE residents have begun treating the sisters as separate welfare cases.

Trying to force reconciliation is considered counterproductive.

Corin requires stabilization.

Orphie requires distance.

Unfortunately, their current living arrangement places them in constant proximity.

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts remain absent from the treehouse.

They have noticed the change.

One Mart reportedly observed Orphie sleeping on the floor from a distance.

It quietly asked:

> "...Why floor?"

Another answered:

> "...Corin."

The colony decided not to investigate further.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

### **ORPHIE**

- Physically stable.
    
- Emotionally detached.
    
- Openly hostile toward Corin.
    
- Sleeping on the floor to maintain distance.
    
- Avoiding shared activities.
    
- Increasingly resentful of Corin's condition.
    

### **CORIN**

- Severe psychological deterioration.
    
- Increasingly isolated.
    
- Still emotionally attached to Orphie.
    
- Highly sensitive to Orphie's rejection.
    
- Continues to struggle with basic functioning.
    

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie came home.

Corin did not recover.

Orphie tried to force her to recover.

That failed.

Then Orphie became angry.

Now she hates the person who spent two weeks waiting for her.

Every night, Corin sleeps in the bed.

Every night, Orphie sleeps on the floor.

Not because there is no room.

Because she wants distance.

The floor has become Orphie's boundary.

And Corin is left staring across the room at the sister she nearly lost...

...who now wishes she had never come back.

**End of Family Dynamics Incident Log**
