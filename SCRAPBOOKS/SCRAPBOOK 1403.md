# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The six surviving members of Obol Squad were originally trained and deployed together under Project: Silverline.

Following their separation, each sister developed independently.

This produced an unexpected result.

Rather than becoming less capable without the others, the six developed **distinct areas of expertise.**

Their individual experiences outside Silverline allowed them to acquire skills that were not necessarily part of their original shared training.

C.O.R.E. has identified several particularly significant cases.

---

# 1. ANBY

### Primary Observation

Anby appears to have acquired the widest variety of miscellaneous skills following her separation.

Unlike several of her sisters, whose post-Silverline development centered around one dominant field, Anby's skill set expanded across multiple subjects.

Observed competencies include:

- general problem solving;
    
- practical knowledge;
    
- analytical reasoning;
    
- situational adaptation;
    
- general household skills;
    
- social adaptation;
    
- tactical understanding;
    
- and various smaller skills acquired through ordinary life.
    

The precise list remains incomplete.

This is partially because Anby frequently acquires a skill without informing anyone that she learned it.

A resident may ask:

> **"How do you know how to do that?"**

Anby will often respond:

> **"...I learned it."**

No further explanation follows.

---

# 2. SOLIDER 11

### Primary Expertise: INTERROGATION

Solider 11 developed substantial knowledge of interrogation following her separation from the other sisters.

Her previous experience working as a contractor provided opportunities to interact with hostile individuals, criminals, and information-sensitive situations.

Her vocal characteristics appear to complement this skill.

She speaks persuasively.

She sounds confident.

She rarely needs to raise her voice.

And she can make a question sound considerably less threatening than it actually is.

C.O.R.E. assessment indicates that Solider 11 is particularly effective at extracting information through conversational pressure.

Jane Doe has described this as:

> **"Very familiar."**

Solider 11 apparently took this as a compliment.

---

# 3. TWIGGY

### Primary Expertise: MEDICAL KNOWLEDGE

Twiggy's time working in a hospital resulted in substantial medical development.

Her knowledge includes:

- patient care;
    
- medical assessment;
    
- basic treatment;
    
- hospital procedures;
    
- emergency response;
    
- medication awareness;
    
- and general nursing knowledge.
    

Her medical experience developed independently from her sisters.

This distinction is significant.

Project Silverline trained the sisters for combat.

Twiggy subsequently learned how to **keep people alive afterward.**

Her current knowledge has proven useful to both C.O.R.E. and KCA personnel.

---

# 4. IRIS

### Primary Expertise: VEHICLE OPERATION

Iris developed an unusually broad understanding of vehicle operation.

Her experience includes knowledge relevant to numerous classes of vehicles.

She is not simply a competent driver.

She demonstrates familiarity with the operational characteristics of **multiple vehicle types.**

This includes:

- conventional road vehicles;
    
- specialized vehicles;
    
- transportation equipment;
    
- and other vehicle systems encountered during her work.
    

Her exceptional driving ability was first documented when Solider 11 encountered her while retrieving belongings from her former workplace.

The most unusual part was that neither sister had initially realized they had been working in the same organization.

### C.O.R.E. NOTE

Iris's voice suggests chaos.

Her driving record does not.

This remains difficult to reconcile.

---

# 5. SORA

### Primary Expertise: MATHEMATICS

Sora independently developed exceptionally strong mathematical ability.

Her mathematical performance eventually exceeded Neko's in several areas.

This discovery was not initially believed.

A comparative assessment was therefore performed.

The result was conclusive.

Sora could solve certain mathematical problems that Neko could not solve as efficiently.

Neko's response was reportedly:

> **"What."**

Sora apologized.

> **SORA:**  
> "Sorry."

Neko:

> **"No, don't apologize. Do it again."**

She did.

Neko was less amused the second time.

---

# 6. PHAETHON

### Primary Expertise: MATHEMATICS

Phaethon independently developed mathematical knowledge comparable to Sora's.

Unlike Sora, Phaethon's approach is typically more controlled and methodical.

She is capable of handling advanced mathematical reasoning with considerable efficiency.

This makes Sora and Phaethon unusual within Obol Squad.

Both developed mathematical expertise independently.

Both ultimately exceeded Neko in their specific field of mathematics.

### Neko's Assessment

Neko initially assumed the results were an error.

They were not.

He repeated the evaluation.

The results remained consistent.

Neko then reportedly spent several minutes staring at the assessment screen.

---

# 7. THE NEKO BENCHMARK

Neko is broadly recognized as one of Aperture's most capable technical minds.

However, C.O.R.E. has identified exactly **three individuals** who currently exceed Neko in a specific established field.

|Individual|Field|
|---|---|
|**Neri**|Chemistry|
|**Sora**|Mathematics|
|**Phaethon**|Mathematics|

This distinction is important.

It does **not** mean Neri, Sora, or Phaethon are universally more intelligent than Neko.

It means that in their respective specialized fields, their demonstrated knowledge and performance exceed his.

Neri exceeds him specifically in **chemistry.**

Sora and Phaethon exceed him specifically in **mathematics.**

No other confirmed resident currently meets this criterion.

---

# 8. WHY THIS MATTERS

The sisters' development demonstrates an important characteristic of the former Silverline subjects.

Their abilities are not static.

They continue learning.

More importantly, their development appears strongly influenced by their individual circumstances.

Anby developed broadly.

Solider 11 learned interrogation.

Twiggy learned medicine.

Iris learned vehicles.

Sora learned mathematics.

Phaethon learned mathematics.

Their shared origin did not produce identical outcomes.

Their separation allowed them to become more individually specialized.

---

# 9. PROJECT SILVERLINE COMPARISON

Project Silverline primarily treated the twelve subjects as a coordinated group.

Their training emphasized:

> **Together.**

Their lives afterward produced:

> **Individually.**

This appears to have been significant.

When separated, each sister encountered different people, environments, jobs, problems, and opportunities.

Those experiences shaped their individual capabilities.

The result is six people who began from the same artificial baseline but developed into noticeably different individuals.
