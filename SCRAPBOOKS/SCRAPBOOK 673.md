# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / RECOVERY COMPLETION REPORT**

# **SHE'S BACK**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **RECOVERY COMPLETE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

For the first time since her disappearance, C.O.R.E. can confidently report:

**Corin is herself again.**

Not a reconstruction.

Not a temporary improvement.

Not a brief emotional high.

The old Corin has returned.

---

# **THE SIGNS**

It started with the little things.

Corin began talking more.

Then laughing.

Then complaining.

Then asking people questions nobody had asked her in months.

She started wandering around the Treehouse again.

She began collecting flowers.

She returned to drawing.

Her drawings remained terrible.

Lumi confirmed this personally.

> **"Yep. Still no natural for arts."**

Corin laughed.

> **"HEY!"**

Lumi laughed harder.

---

# **THE WINGS**

Corin flies again.

Properly.

Her wings have regained their flexibility and strength.

She can maneuver normally.

She can land without stumbling.

She can perform aerial turns.

She occasionally flies around the Treehouse for absolutely no reason.

When asked why she was flying indoors, Corin responded:

> **"Because I can."**

This response was considered sufficient.

---

# **THE FEATHERS**

The feathers have fully returned to their previous state.

They can once again:

- Illuminate.
    
- Burn with azure flame.
    
- Function as projectiles.
    
- Store and distribute kinetic force.
    
- Perform their anomalous functions.
    
- Be used as emergency lockpicks.
    

The feather lanterns around the Blacksite have also returned to normal brightness.

Some have become brighter than their historical baseline.

Engineering has stopped trying to determine why.

---

# **THE LANTERNS**

At night, the Blacksite is now dotted with Corin's azure light.

The lanterns illuminate corridors, stairwells, observation rooms, and several recreational areas.

Personnel have become accustomed to the glow.

Newer staff frequently ask why the Blacksite has blue lanterns everywhere.

Veteran personnel usually answer:

> **"Corin."**

No further explanation is generally necessary.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

Corin continues writing.

However, the tone has changed dramatically.

Her recent entries contain:

- Scientific observations.
    
- Bad jokes.
    
- Drawings.
    
- Complaints.
    
- Flowers taped to pages.
    
- Questions.
    
- Stories.
    
- Extremely unnecessary measurements.
    
- Occasional doodles of people she knows.
    

The black eyes have become rare.

Pages dominated by self-hatred have stopped appearing.

The notebook is no longer primarily a record of suffering.

It has become a record of living.

---

# **A NEW ENTRY**

One of Corin's newest pages contains only:

> **"I'm back."**

Below it is a poorly drawn picture of herself flying.

Underneath:

> **"My wings work again :D"**

The smiley face is intentional.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi was asked what she thought about Corin's recovery.

She smiled.

Then cried.

Then laughed because she was crying.

She remembered the girl who used to sit beside her drawing badly.

The girl who got sick constantly.

The girl who needed everyone to take care of her.

The girl who couldn't say "Lumia."

> **"She still calls me Lumi."**

Lumi smiled.

> **"Good."**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie no longer has to carefully measure every word.

Not because Corin is incapable of being hurt.

She still is.

But now she can recover from being hurt.

They can argue.

They can misunderstand each other.

They can apologize.

They can laugh afterward.

Corin no longer interprets every mistake as proof that she should disappear.

One evening, Orphie accidentally said something that annoyed her.

Corin stared at her.

> **"...That was a terrible thing to say."**

Orphie immediately apologized.

Corin crossed her arms.

> **"I'm still mad."**

> **"Fair."**

A few minutes later:

> **"...Wanna see the flower I found?"**

Orphie smiled.

> **"Sure."**

That interaction was considered one of the strongest indicators of recovery.

---

# **THE OLD CORIN**

She is loud again.

Curious again.

Playful again.

She complains when she's hungry.

She complains when she's cold.

She complains when someone touches her feathers without asking.

She draws terrible pictures.

She gets excited over flowers.

She flies simply because she can.

She bothers people.

She asks too many questions.

She laughs.

She smiles.

She lives.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

For a long time, everyone wondered whether Corin could ever come back.

Not physically.

They knew her body could recover.

The question was whether the person inside it could.

She did.

But she didn't return because someone found the perfect words.

She didn't return because the past was erased.

She didn't return because someone forced her to be happy.

She returned through little things.

A flower.

A drawing.

A flight.

A conversation.

A laugh.

A faint blue light.

One tiny piece of hope at a time.

Eventually, there were enough pieces.

Now Corin flies over the Treehouse again.

Her feathers glow.

Her lanterns illuminate the Blacksite.

Lumi hears her laughing.

Orphie hears her complaining.

And somewhere in the Treehouse, a terrible drawing has been pinned proudly to the wall.

Underneath it is a handwritten note:

> **"I'm back."**

**End of Recovery Completion Report**
