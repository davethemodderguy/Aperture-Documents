# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake lives alone.

This remains intentional.

His residence is the only SAFEHOUSE living space that was never conventionally furnished.

It contains an old mattress, grass, manifested sketched flowers, scattered plushies, books, drawings, and his personal belongings.

The arrangement suits him.

However, the remainder of SAFEHOUSE has become increasingly busy.

Residents have medical responsibilities.

Research responsibilities.

Government responsibilities.

CED deployments.

KCA obligations.

L.S.I.N. operations.

External relations.

Blacksite assignments.

And an assortment of other problems that appear to reproduce whenever nobody is looking.

As a result, the residents Jake normally spends time around are frequently unavailable.

This has resulted in an unexpected development.

**The Obol Squad has started spending time with him.**

Not as an assigned duty.

Not as a welfare rotation.

Not because anyone ordered them to.

They simply noticed that Jake was usually alone.

So they started showing up.

---

# 1. THE ARRANGEMENT

The pattern began gradually.

One Obol member would visit.

Then another.

Eventually, several began visiting together.

They do not consistently plan activities.

Most visits consist of:

- sitting on Jake's mattress;
    
- playing with his plushies;
    
- reading;
    
- talking quietly;
    
- eating snacks;
    
- stargazing from the balcony;
    
- listening to music;
    
- watching Jake draw;
    
- or simply existing in the same room.
    

Jake rarely asks them to leave.

This is considered significant.

He does not appear to interpret their presence as an obligation.

---

# 2. ANBY

Anby visits most frequently.

Her visits are generally quiet.

She does not attempt to fill silence.

She can sit on the grass for extended periods without speaking.

Jake appears comfortable with this.

During one observation, Anby was sitting against the mattress while Jake drew flowers.

Neither spoke for approximately forty minutes.

Eventually:

> **JAKE:**  
> "What are you doing?"

Anby looked at him.

> **ANBY:**  
> "Sitting."

Jake considered this.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Okay."

He continued drawing.

Anby continued sitting.

This appears to be their preferred form of conversation.

---

# 3. TWIGGY

Twiggy tends to bring food.

She claims this is because Jake "forgets to eat when he's drawing."

Jake denies this.

Available observation suggests Twiggy is correct.

Twiggy has also become responsible for rescuing plushies from increasingly questionable locations.

One plushie was found beneath the mattress.

Another was inside Jake's bookcase.

The problem is that Jake does not actually have a bookcase.

Twiggy eventually located it inside a cardboard box.

She returned it to the mattress.

Jake watched.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Thanks."

Twiggy smiled.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "You're welcome."

---

# 4. SOLIDER 11

Solider 11's visits are considerably less quiet.

She attempts conversation.

Jake frequently responds with one-word answers.

This does not discourage her.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "What are you drawing?"

> **JAKE:**  
> "Flower."

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "What kind?"

Jake looked at the drawing.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Blue."

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> "That's a color."

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Yes."

Solider 11 stopped.

Jake returned to drawing.

Five minutes later, Solider 11 began laughing.

Jake eventually joined her.

Quietly.

---

# 5. PHAETHON

Phaethon generally prefers quieter activities.

She has developed an interest in Jake's star-related drawings.

On one occasion, Jake drew several stars across a sheet of paper.

Phaethon watched.

> **PHAETHON:**  
> "Are those real?"

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Some."

> **PHAETHON:**  
> "Which ones?"

Jake pointed.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Those."

Phaethon looked at the drawing.

> **PHAETHON:**  
> "And the others?"

Jake shrugged.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I like them."

Phaethon nodded.

> **PHAETHON:**  
> "That's enough."

Jake smiled faintly.

---

# 6. SORA

Sora spends time with Jake primarily during evening hours.

She frequently brings him outside to look at the sky.

This is one of the few activities that consistently gets Jake to leave his room without being prompted.

Sora does not force conversation.

They sit together.

Sometimes they eat.

Sometimes Jake brings his teddy bear.

Sometimes Sora brings food.

On one occasion:

> **SORA:**  
> "See that one?"

Jake looked upward.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Yeah."

> **SORA:**  
> "It's bright."

Jake was quiet.

Then:

> **JAKE:**  
> "Not as bright as me."

Sora looked at him.

> **SORA:**  
> "You're technically a star."

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Exactly."

Sora laughed.

Jake looked pleased with himself.

---

# 7. IRIS

Iris is the least predictable visitor.

She enters Jake's room with approximately the same energy she brings to everything else.

Jake does not seem bothered.

Iris has attempted:

- plushie races;
    
- throwing soft toys at the mattress;
    
- making grass crowns;
    
- naming the flowers;
    
- inventing stories about the flowers;
    
- and convincing Jake that one particular plushie is "the boss."
    

Jake eventually accepted the last proposal.

The plushie is now referred to as:

> **"Boss."**

Nobody knows why.

---

# 8. GROUP VISITS

The most notable change occurs when the entire Obol Squad visits together.

Six artificial humans in one unfurnished room would normally be expected to create considerable noise.

Instead, the room becomes strangely comfortable.

Anby sits on the mattress.

Twiggy brings food.

Solider 11 talks.

Phaethon reads.

Sora looks through the window.

Iris plays with the plushies.

Jake sits in the grass.

Sometimes he draws.

Sometimes he reads.

Sometimes he simply watches everyone else.

He does not appear overwhelmed.

He appears...

**included.**

---

# 9. THE UNOFFICIAL RULE

The Obol Squad has developed one rule concerning Jake.

Nobody asks:

> **"Are you okay?"**

Not repeatedly.

Not every visit.

Not as a test.

If Jake wants to talk, he talks.

If he doesn't, nobody forces him.

Instead, they simply stay.

This appears to work considerably better.

---

# 10. ONE EVENING

Anby, Twiggy, Sora, and Jake were sitting in his room.

Solider 11 was away.

Phaethon was on assignment.

Iris had apparently fallen asleep somewhere else.

Jake was lying on the mattress.

His teddy bear was beside him.

Anby was sitting in the grass.

Twiggy was reading.

Sora was eating.

Nobody was speaking.

After several minutes, Jake quietly said:

> **JAKE:**  
> "Thanks."

Nobody immediately responded.

Jake looked at them.

> **JAKE:**  
> "For coming."

Anby looked over.

> **ANBY:**  
> "You're welcome."

Twiggy smiled.

Sora offered him a piece of food.

Jake took it.

No further discussion occurred.

---

# 11. WELFARE ASSESSMENT

The Obol Squad's presence should not be interpreted as a replacement for Jake's existing relationships.

Bao remains Bao.

Neri remains Neri.

Aeterna remains Aeterna.

Jane remains Jane.

The other SAFEHOUSE residents remain part of Jake's social environment.

The Obol Squad simply occupies a gap created by everyone's schedules.

They are often available when others are not.

More importantly, they do not appear to regard spending time with Jake as an obligation.

They seem to genuinely enjoy being around him.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Everyone is busy.

That is simply how SAFEHOUSE works.

People have places to be.

Things to repair.

People to protect.

Dimensions to investigate.

Paperwork to finish.

And, apparently, an infinite number of meetings.

Jake's room remains quiet.

Grass covers the floor.

Plushies are scattered across an old mattress.

Sketched flowers sway beside books.

Jake lives alone.

But increasingly, he is not **alone**.

Because whenever everyone else is busy, six artificial humans have apparently decided that an empty room full of grass and plushies is exactly where they want to spend their free time.

They do not fix him.

They do not interrogate him.

They do not demand that he explain himself.

They just sit with him.

Sometimes they talk.

Sometimes they play.

Sometimes they eat.

Sometimes they stare at the stars.

And sometimes six people and one little star spend an entire afternoon doing absolutely nothing.

Jake seems to like those days.

He doesn't say so very often.

But occasionally, just before everyone leaves, he quietly says:

> **"See you tomorrow."**

And they always answer:

> **"Tomorrow."**
