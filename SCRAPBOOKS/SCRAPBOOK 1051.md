## Behavioral Note

Neri's plush form has effectively made her **portable**.

This has resulted in an unusual operational problem.

Personnel may encounter a small seal/teddy-bear hybrid in a completely unexpected location and reasonably assume:

1. Someone left a plush toy there.
    
2. It belongs to Alice.
    
3. It is some other anomalous object.
    
4. It is Neri.
    

**Option 4 should not be dismissed.**

Before moving the object, personnel should verify that it is actually an inanimate plush toy.

Neri is capable of moving.

# HYGIENE NOTICE

### Issued by: Bao

The following message has been reproduced verbatim.

> **"Neri is a plush, but she can clean herself."**
> 
> **"Do not put her in a washing machine."**
> 
> **"You'll drown her."**

This notice has been elevated to an official SAFEHOUSE directive following multiple questions regarding whether Neri's fabric-like exterior requires conventional washing.

It does not.

Neri is fully capable of cleaning herself while in plush form.

Her ability to become fabric-like does **not** make her an ordinary stuffed animal.

She remains a living person.

---

## PROHIBITED ACTIONS

Personnel are specifically prohibited from:

- putting Neri in a washing machine;
    
- putting Neri in a dryer;
    
- placing Neri in a bathtub and leaving her unattended;
    
- treating Neri as laundry;
    
- placing Neri inside a laundry basket;
    
- attempting to "fluff" Neri using mechanical equipment;
    
- testing whether she can survive industrial washing equipment;
    
- saying "but she's waterproof" as justification.
    

### Clarification

**Waterproof ≠ aquatic.**

Neri's plush exterior being waterproof does not mean she should be submerged.

---

## If Neri Gets Dirty

Do not panic.

Do not reach for the washing machine.

Do not call Facilities to request a "plushie cleaning cycle."

Neri can clean herself.

If assistance is genuinely required, contact SAFEHOUSE Welfare personnel or Bao.

---

## Final Note

Neri's current plush-state existence has created a simple rule for the entire SAFEHOUSE:

> **You can find Neri almost anywhere.**

If a small blue seal plushie is sitting somewhere unusual, it may simply be Neri enjoying herself.

Leave her alone if she wants to be alone.

Pick her up if she clearly wants to be carried.

Hug her if she willingly settles into your arms.

And if someone suggests putting her in a washing machine:

**Stop them immediately.**
