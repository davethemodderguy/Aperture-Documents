# 1. CASE SPECIFICATION

Each **Sleight of Hand** PDW is issued inside a dedicated tactical presentation case.

The case is not a standard Blacksite weapons container.

It was commissioned specifically for the limited Sleight of Hand production run.

Each case contains:

- **1× Sleight of Hand PDW**
    
- **3× loaded magazines**
    
- **1× handwritten letter from Neri**
    
- designated storage compartments for the issued equipment
    

The interior is fitted specifically for the weapon and its accompanying equipment.

No two additional weapons are stored inside.

---

# 2. EXTERIOR IDENTIFICATION

The exterior contains exactly **one label.**

There are no:

- Aperture Industries logos;
    
- C.O.R.E. markings;
    
- MSAC identification plates;
    
- serial-number placards on the outside;
    
- advertisements;
    
- warning slogans;
    
- decorative plaques.
    

Instead, there is one simple label:

> **TACHYON INDUSTRIES**

Nothing else.

---

# 3. WHY TACHYON INDUSTRIES?

The designation was not selected by C.O.R.E.

It was selected by **Neri.**

The Sleight of Hand program originated from Neri's research following the Night Stalker project.

However, the significance of the label is considerably older than either weapon.

Neri Tachyon is the final surviving member of the Tachyon family.

The Tachyon family played a foundational role in the history of Aperture Industries.

Consequently, the marking is not intended to identify the manufacturing department responsible for the weapon.

It identifies its **heritage.**

---

# 4. OWNER REACTIONS

Several recipients initially assumed the label was a reference to a subcontractor.

One MSAC operator reportedly asked:

> **“Who's Tachyon Industries?”**

Neri stared at him.

For approximately five seconds.

She then pointed at herself.

The operator reportedly required several seconds to understand.

> **Operator:** “...Oh.”

Neri nodded.

> **Operator:** “Oh.”

Neri nodded again.

> **Operator:** “OH.”

Neri left.

---

# 5. CASE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

The Night Stalker presentation cases were designed to feel like **heirlooms.**

The Sleight of Hand cases follow the same philosophy.

They are tactical enough to be practical.

Formal enough to feel significant.

And intentionally restrained enough that the single label becomes the most noticeable feature.

There is no attempt to make the case look like mass-produced equipment.

Because it isn't.

Only five Sleight of Hand units currently exist.

Five cases were produced.

Five labels were applied.

All five read:

> **TACHYON INDUSTRIES**

---

# 6. ARCHIVIST NOTE

Neko stared.

Pause.

> **Neko:**
