# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Guiding Light and Curious Light arrived shortly after C.O.R.E. requested their assistance regarding Jake.

Guiding Light entered the anomalous grassy environment.

She saw the figure sitting within the circular region.

She immediately froze.

She stared at it for several seconds.

Then she asked:

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Do any of you here know what that is?”

Bao answered.

> **BAO:**  
> “That is Jake.”

Guiding Light's expression changed immediately.

She became furious.

Not confused.

Not concerned.

Furious.

What followed was an explanation of an anomalous phenomenon previously unknown to C.O.R.E.

The entity sitting inside the circle was not simply an injured Jake.

It was a:

> **NULLWALKER.**

The remains of a Humanized Star whose existence has been completely forgotten.

Guiding Light confirmed that Jake had undergone a catastrophic existential failure.

He was no longer merely unconscious.

He was no longer merely damaged.

He was no longer present in the form everyone remembered.

> **Jake was gone.**

---

# 1. ARRIVAL

Guiding Light arrived alongside Curious Light.

Both entered the anomalous space beyond Jake's elevator.

The residents remained gathered around the circular clearing.

Jake remained motionless.

His body was entirely black.

His knees were drawn against his chest.

His eyes remained closed.

His teddy bear sat nearby.

The bear's core remained intact.

It produced no light.

Guiding Light took one step forward.

Then stopped.

Her expression changed.

She looked at the figure.

Then at the teddy bear.

Then back at the figure.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Do any of you here know what that is?”

Bao answered immediately.

> **BAO:**  
> “That is Jake.”

Guiding Light did not respond.

For several seconds, she simply stared.

Then:

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “...Jake?”

Her voice was no longer calm.

---

# 2. THE QUESTION

Guiding Light turned toward Neko.

Her expression was severe.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Neko.”

Neko looked toward her.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Do you know how stars die out?”

Neko hesitated.

He answered from conventional stellar knowledge.

> **NEKO:**  
> “It's because the hydrogen and the minerals inside—”

Guiding Light interrupted.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “No.”

Neko stopped.

Guiding Light pointed toward the black figure.

---

# 3. THE TRUTH ABOUT HUMANIZED STARS

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Stars remain because they are remembered.”

Silence.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Every time a star is born, humans see it.”

She looked toward Jake.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “They remember it.”

Her voice became increasingly strained.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “They give it a place in the sky.”

Pause.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “And once humans slowly forget them...”

She looked at the figure again.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Its light dims.”

The residents remained silent.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Until it becomes that.”

She pointed toward Jake.

---

> “He became that.”

# 6. THE ANCHOR

Curious Light continued.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “**INFINITE!**”

Guiding Light stopped.

Her breathing became uneven.

She looked back toward Jake.

Her anger slowly collapsed into grief.

---

# 8. SILENCE

Nobody attempted to defend themselves.

Nobody had an explanation.

Because there was nothing to explain.

They had forgotten him.

Not intentionally.

Not maliciously.

But completely.

And Jake had remained alone long enough for that forgetting to become something much worse than absence.

It had become existential.

Her expression was no longer angry.

It was simply devastated.

Then she and Curious Light disappeared.

Almost.

No heartbeat.

No stellar radiation.

No response.

The core remained physically present.

But whatever had made it **Jake's heart** was gone.

# FINAL C.O.R.E. OBSERVATION

Nobody left.

Not Bao.

Not Neri.

Not Nora.

Not Anby.

Not Alice.

Not Phoenix.

Not Aeterna.

Not any of them.

They simply sat around the figure.

The same people who had once filled Jake's life with noise now surrounded him in complete silence.

There was no dinner.

No conversation.

No laughter.

No arguments.

No coffee.

No cake.

No cheeseburgers.

No books.

No cookies.

Only grass.

A circular patch of empty earth.

A teddy bear with an extinguished heart.

And a black figure sitting with its knees against its chest.

Jake's existence had once been defined by light.

Now the only thing anyone could still see of him was:

> **His eyes.**

Closed.

Jake was gone.

And everyone finally remembered him.
