### ANOMALOUS RESIDENT & WELFARE DIVISION

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake's emotional state has been reassessed following continued nighttime observation.

The previous description of Jake as occasionally distracted, curious, or capable of temporary emotional relief remains technically accurate.

However, those moments are sufficiently uncommon that they should **not** be considered representative of his normal condition.

Jake's baseline is:

> **Hungry.**

> **Sad.**

> **Lonely.**

> **Missing his mother.**

He is rarely observed genuinely happy.

He is almost always hungry.

He is almost always sad.

And despite more than seventy-five years having passed since his death, Jake continues to miss the mother who left him behind.

He still believes she loved him.

---

# 2. PHYSICAL PRESENTATION

Jake's appearance is notably simple.

Despite his classification as a **Weeping Angel**, he does not possess elaborate celestial features.

His only distinctly angelic characteristics are:

- a **small azure cloak**;
    
- a **blue angelic halo**.
    

He has no visible wings.

No celestial armor.

No elaborate divine markings.

No radiant weapon.

No other obvious manifestation of angelic status.

The missing wings are consistent with Star's explanation of the Weeping Angel condition.

Jake remains an angel.

He simply no longer has the means to return to the Starlands.

---

# 3. THE AZURE CLOAK

Jake's small azure cloak is consistently present during manifestation.

It appears to be part of his anomalous form rather than ordinary clothing.

The cloak is slightly oversized for his small body.

It moves subtly even when there is no detectable airflow.

Jake appears comfortable wearing it.

Personnel have occasionally observed him pulling the cloak closer around himself when distressed.

Whether this has any physical function is unknown.

It may simply be a familiar object associated with his identity.

---

# 4. THE HALO

Jake's halo is small.

It emits a soft blue light.

Unlike the intense manifestations associated with some Starlands entities, Jake's halo is dim.

It does not illuminate an entire room.

It simply remains above his head.

The halo becomes slightly brighter during periods of significant emotional distress.

It has not been observed producing any measurable offensive, defensive, or transportation capability.

Star confirmed that it is an authentic angelic halo.

> **STAR:**  
> “He's still an angel.”

That remains the most important observation.

---

# 5. HUNGER

Jake is almost always hungry.

This is one of the most consistent features of his manifestation.

He regularly:

- asks about food;
    
- watches residents eating;
    
- waits outside the cafeteria;
    
- stares through windows;
    
- follows the smell of food;
    
- asks whether something can be eaten;
    
- remembers foods he used to have;
    
- and occasionally asks Nora whether she has something sweet.
    

Physical food cannot satisfy him.

It passes through his spectral form.

This creates a particularly cruel contradiction.

Jake remembers hunger.

He experiences the desire for food.

But eating cannot resolve it.

---

# 6. THE QUESTION OF CAKE

Jake remains particularly interested in cake.

This is partially because Nora has repeatedly offered it to him.

He cannot physically eat it.

He can smell it.

He can see it.

He can hold his hand near it.

But he cannot consume it.

On one occasion, Nora offered him a slice.

Jake looked at it for several seconds.

> **Jake:**  
> “It smells nice.”

> **Nora:**  
> “You want it?”

Jake nodded.

He reached for it.

His hand passed through.

Jake stared at his hand.

Then at the cake.

His expression fell.

Nora quietly ate the cake herself.

Jake remained beside her.

He was still hungry.

---

# 7. SADNESS

Jake's sadness is not episodic.

It is persistent.

He does not need to be reminded of his death to become distressed.

He does not need to be alone.

He does not need to see his old body.

He does not need to hear his mother's name.

He simply wakes into the same existence every night.

And he is sad.

Nora can sit beside him.

Aeterna can occasionally accompany them.

Someone can talk to him.

Someone can listen.

Someone can give him attention.

Jake may appreciate those things.

But the sadness remains.

---

# 8. NORA CANNOT FIX IT

Nora has become Jake's closest companion.

She is the person most capable of meeting him during his **02:00–05:00** manifestation window.

She has given him food.

She has walked with him.

She has introduced him to people.

She has listened to him.

She has simply sat beside him when he does not want to talk.

None of this has made Jake consistently happy.

This is not because Nora has failed.

It is because Jake's suffering is not caused solely by loneliness.

Nora can make him **less alone.**

She cannot make him stop grieving.

---

# 9. THE MOTHER

Jake continues to miss his mother.

This remains the most persistent emotional attachment in his known psychology.

He speaks about her affectionately.

He remembers her.

He waits for her.

He believes she loved him.

The information obtained from his mother has not been disclosed to him.

She openly described Jake as:

> **“A problematic and accident child.”**

And stated that she hated him.

Jake does not know.

He continues to hold the opposite belief.

To Jake:

> **Mother loved me.**

That belief remains untouched.

---

# 10. THE ONE-SIDED ATTACHMENT

There is no indication that Jake's love for his mother has diminished.

More than seventy-five years have passed.

The relationship has not evolved.

Jake cannot build a new relationship with her.

He cannot receive closure.

He cannot ask her why.

He cannot understand what happened.

He simply misses her.

The cruelest part is that his memories preserve his affection while reality preserved her rejection.

He is grieving someone who, according to her own testimony, did not want him.

He does not know that.

So he continues waiting for the mother he remembers.

---

# 11. NIGHTLY OBSERVATION

**03:26 HOURS**

Jake was observed outside the cafeteria.

He was sitting on the floor.

Nora was beside him.

Jake watched someone inside eating breakfast preparations.

His halo was dim.

His cloak was wrapped around his shoulders.

> **Nora:**  
> “Hungry?”

Jake nodded.

Nora offered him food.

Jake reached for it.

His hand passed through.

He withdrew his hand.

> **Jake:**  
> “Still hungry.”

Nora nodded.

> **Nora:**  
> “I know.”

Jake looked toward the cafeteria.

After several seconds:

> **Jake:**  
> “I miss my mom.”

Nora did not answer.

Jake lowered his head.

> **Jake:**  
> “She'll come back.”

Nora remained beside him.

---

# 12. A RARE MOMENT

There are occasional exceptions.

They are extremely brief.

Jake may become fascinated by something.

He may ask a question.

He may laugh at something Nora says.

He may look particularly interested in the Bullseye displayed behind the Tachyon Merch Store glass.

He may ask Aeterna whether she can fly.

These moments are real.

They are simply rare.

C.O.R.E. therefore recommends that personnel avoid describing Jake as:

> **“Usually cheerful despite his circumstances.”**

That description is inaccurate.

A more accurate statement is:

> **Jake is usually sad, with occasional moments in which something temporarily distracts him from his sadness.**

---

# 13. WEeping ANGEL STATUS

Star has confirmed that Jake's emotional condition is consistent with a Weeping Angel.

His sadness has consumed his connection to the Starlands.

His wings are gone.

His path home is gone.

His halo remains.

His azure cloak remains.

The angel remains.

But he cannot return.

He can only wander.

---

# 14. GUIDING LIGHT / LUCY MERRIWEATHER

Neither Guiding Light nor Lucy Merryweather has identified a means of changing Jake's condition.

His existence cannot simply be ended.

His soul cannot simply be transferred.

His connection to the Starlands cannot currently be restored.

His sorrow cannot simply be removed.

There is no known cure.

Jake is therefore expected to continue manifesting indefinitely.

---

# 15. WELFARE DIRECTIVE

Personnel should not attempt to force happiness upon Jake.

Do not:

- tell him to cheer up;
    
- insist that he should be grateful;
    
- tell him to stop thinking about his mother;
    
- tell him that seventy-five years is long enough to move on;
    
- repeatedly remind him that she abandoned him;
    
- attempt to manufacture emotional reactions;
    
- or treat his sadness as an inconvenience.
    

Jake does not need to perform happiness for the SAFEHOUSE.

If he is sad:

**Let him be sad.**

If he wants company:

**Stay.**

If he wants to wander:

**Let him wander, provided there is no immediate danger.**

If he wants to talk about his mother:

**Listen.**

If he says nothing:

**Silence is acceptable.**

---

# 16. FINAL OBSERVATION

At 04:41, Nora and Jake were sitting beneath a security light.

Jake's halo was faintly glowing.

His azure cloak moved slightly in the night air.

Nora was eating cake.

Jake watched.

He was hungry.

He was sad.

He was thinking about his mother.

Nora offered him another piece.

Jake tried.

His hand passed through it.

He quietly withdrew his hand.

> **Jake:**  
> “I wish I could eat.”

Nora looked at him.

> **Nora:**  
> “Me too.”

Jake looked down.

A few seconds passed.

> **Jake:**  
> “I miss her.”

Nora knew who he meant.

She did not correct him.

She did not tell him the truth.

She simply moved a little closer.

Jake leaned against her.

He remained sad.

Jake is not occasionally sad.

**Jake is occasionally happy.**

The distinction matters.

His normal state is hunger.

His normal state is grief.

His normal state is missing someone who did not want him.

More than seventy-five years have passed.

The world changed.

The land changed.

A Blacksite was built around him.

People came.

People left.

Nora found him.

Aeterna occasionally walks beside him.

Star recognized what he had become.

Guiding Light could not release him.

Lucy could not release him.

His own body was eventually found and respectfully disposed of.

And still:

**Jake misses his mother.**

He does not know that she hated him.

He does not know that she called him an accident.

He remembers a mother who loved him.

So that is the mother he continues to miss.

Every night.

Between **02:00 and 05:00.**

A small blue halo above his head.

A small azure cloak around his shoulders.

No wings.

No road home.

No end to the wandering.

Just a six-year-old angel,

always hungry,

almost always crying,

still waiting for someone who is never coming back.
