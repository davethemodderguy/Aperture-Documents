# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### ANOMALOUS RESEARCH & RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-INC-4C-JAKE-320  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESTRICTED — CRITICAL RESIDENT WELFARE INCIDENT  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE SEARCH / RESIDENT MISSING  
**SUBJECT:** Jake Akana — Disappearance Following Extinguishment of Anomalous Flame  
**LOCATION:** HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE / SAFEHOUSE

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately 14:17, a newly assigned Blacksite employee encountered Jake Akana in a residential corridor.

The employee had not completed the revised orientation concerning Jake's current spectral appearance.

Upon seeing him, the employee panicked.

They retrieved a nearby fire extinguisher and discharged its contents directly at Jake's head.

The blue flame burning on the wick protruding from Jake's partially melted head was extinguished.

Jake disappeared immediately afterward.

No physical remains were recovered.

No spectral manifestation has been observed since the incident.

No confirmed explanation for the disappearance is currently available.

Jake's older brother, Aries Akana, was nearby when the incident was reported.

Upon learning what had happened, Aries entered a state of acute panic.

He repeatedly demanded to know where Jake had gone.

He attempted to search the surrounding corridors himself.

When personnel informed him that the search had not located Jake, Aries became increasingly distressed.

His response was not limited to concern over an unexplained anomalous event.

He believed he had lost his little brother again.

And this time, he could not bear the possibility.

---

# 1. INCIDENT CHRONOLOGY

**14:17:03 — Initial Encounter**

Jake was observed hovering in a residential corridor.

He was not behaving aggressively.

No threatening movement was recorded.

He was not pursuing anyone.

He was simply present.

**14:17:11 — Employee Reaction**

A newly assigned employee encountered Jake while carrying out routine duties.

The employee reportedly screamed and retreated several steps.

Jake turned toward them.

No verbal exchange was recorded.

**14:17:19 — Fire Extinguisher Discharged**

The employee retrieved a portable fire extinguisher from its wall-mounted station.

They directed the discharge toward Jake's head.

The agent struck the blue flame and surrounding area.

**14:17:22 — Flame Extinguished**

The blue flame disappeared.

Jake's spectral form vanished at approximately the same moment.

No audible distress call was recorded.

No residual manifestation was detected immediately afterward.

**14:18:06 — Emergency Notification**

Security personnel were notified.

The corridor was secured.

The fire extinguisher was recovered.

**14:19:31 — Aries Akana Informed**

Aries was informed that Jake had disappeared following an encounter with a newly assigned employee.

His initial response was disbelief.

He demanded immediate clarification.

**14:20 onward — Search Initiated**

Security, Welfare, and Anomalous Research personnel began searching the surrounding areas.

Aries attempted to join the search.

His emotional condition deteriorated as the search continued without results.

---

# 2. THE EMPLOYEE

The employee stated that they believed Jake was some kind of supernatural fire hazard.

This belief was based on his appearance.

They had not observed him setting anything alight.

They had not witnessed him threatening another resident.

They had not identified any active fire.

They saw a small, floating figure with a melted head and a blue flame.

They panicked.

They used the extinguisher.

The action was not authorized.

The employee has been removed from resident-facing duties pending investigation.

C.O.R.E. is reviewing why a new employee was permitted to begin duties without adequate instruction concerning the residents of SAFEHOUSE.

The investigation will distinguish between the employee's initial involuntary fear and their subsequent decision to use firefighting equipment against a resident without establishing that a fire emergency existed.

**Fear may explain the reaction. It does not establish that the action was justified.**

---

# 3. THE FLAME

The blue flame was a persistent feature of Jake's current spectral manifestation.

It burned from a wick protruding from the upper portion of his head.

Its light was soft.

No evidence had established that it was an ordinary combustion process.

No evidence had established that Jake's flame constituted a conventional fire hazard.

Nevertheless, the employee treated it as one.

The immediate disappearance following extinguishment has raised several possibilities:

- the flame may be integral to Jake's current manifestation;
    
- extinguishment may have disrupted the manifestation;
    
- Jake may have been displaced to an unknown location;
    
- the event may have triggered an unrecorded anomalous response;
    
- or another mechanism may be responsible.
    

None of these hypotheses has been confirmed.

C.O.R.E. will not declare Jake dead, destroyed, or permanently lost on the basis of this event.

His current status is **missing, presumed anomalously displaced**.

This designation describes the uncertainty.

It is not a prediction of the outcome.

---

# 4. SEARCH OPERATIONS

The search has been expanded beyond the immediate corridor.

Personnel are examining available security footage, anomalous monitoring records, and the circumstances surrounding the disappearance.

The following actions are prohibited:

- discharging additional extinguishers in an attempt to reproduce the event;
    
- attempting to reignite or reconstruct Jake's flame without authorization;
    
- treating the disappearance as proof of Jake's destruction;
    
- conducting uncontrolled anomalous experiments;
    
- informing Aries that Jake is permanently gone;
    
- or making assurances that Jake will certainly return.
    

The last restriction is particularly important.

Personnel must not replace uncertainty with false certainty simply because Aries is distressed.

He deserves honesty.

He also deserves people who will remain beside him while that uncertainty is unbearable.

---

# 5. ARIES AKANA — INITIAL RESPONSE

Aries was found in the residential corridor several minutes after receiving the notification.

He was attempting to determine the last confirmed location of his brother.

His speech was rapid and repetitive.

**ARIES:**  
"Where is he?"

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
"We're searching the area."

**ARIES:**  
"No. Where is Jake?"

**SECURITY OFFICER:**  
"We haven't found him yet."

Aries stared at the officer.

**ARIES:**  
"What do you mean, you haven't found him?"

No answer was immediately forthcoming.

Aries began moving toward the corridor where Jake had disappeared.

A welfare officer attempted to accompany him.

**ARIES:**  
"Move."

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"Aries, we're going to look for him."

**ARIES:**  
"Then look!"

He continued down the corridor.

**ARIES:**  
"He's here somewhere. He has to be."

He checked the adjoining room.

Then the next corridor.

Then the area around the extinguisher station.

He called Jake's name repeatedly.

There was no response.

---

# 6. ARIES AKANA — ESCALATING DISTRESS

At 14:31, Aries was informed that the initial search had produced no results.

He stopped moving.

For several seconds, he appeared unable to process the information.

Then he shook his head.

**ARIES:**  
"No."

The welfare officer did not contradict him.

**ARIES:**  
"No, no, no. That's not—"

He stopped speaking.

His hands were shaking.

**ARIES:**  
"He was just here."

A pause.

**ARIES:**  
"He was just here."

The statement became quieter the second time.

**ARIES:**  
"I was supposed to be here."

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"Aries—"

**ARIES:**  
"I should've been with him."

The officer attempted to explain that the incident was sudden and that Aries could not have known what would happen.

Aries did not appear to hear the explanation.

**ARIES:**  
"I should've been there."

He looked toward the empty corridor.

**ARIES:**  
"I can't do this again."

His breathing became irregular.

**ARIES:**  
"I can't."

He pressed both hands against his face.

**ARIES:**  
"Please."

The recording contains a prolonged silence.

Then, quietly:

**ARIES:**  
"Not Jake."

---

# 7. WELFARE INTERVENTION

Aries's response is consistent with an acute distress reaction following the apparent loss of a close family member.

His distress is compounded by his existing guilt surrounding Jake's death.

Jake's original death was a homicide.

His return as a ghost had not erased the circumstances of that death, but it had given Aries an opportunity to be with his brother again.

Aries had taken that opportunity seriously.

He had allowed Jake to follow him.

He had spoken with him.

He had accepted his occasional, non-harmful haunting.

He had also tried to establish ordinary boundaries without making Jake feel unwanted.

Now Jake had disappeared after someone deliberately discharged a fire extinguisher at him.

Aries's fear was immediate:

That Jake had suffered again.

That he had failed to protect him.

That he would never see him again.

Personnel are instructed not to reinforce these conclusions.

They must not tell Aries that he should have prevented the incident.

They must not tell him to calm down.

They must not ask him to behave maturely because he is Jake's older brother.

He is sixteen.

He is grieving, frightened, and facing the possibility of another loss he cannot control.

**He is not responsible for solving this incident alone.**

---

# 8. ARIES — PRIVATE WELFARE ROOM

Aries was eventually accompanied to a quiet welfare room away from the incident site.

He initially refused to sit.

He repeatedly asked whether there had been any updates.

Personnel provided the information available without making promises.

After several minutes, Aries sat down.

He stared at the floor.

**ARIES:**  
"He doesn't have legs anymore."

The welfare officer remained quiet.

**ARIES:**  
"He doesn't even have his body."

He swallowed.

**ARIES:**  
"And now someone puts out the only light he has?"

His voice broke.

**ARIES:**  
"Why would they do that?"

The officer did not attempt to justify the employee's actions.

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"They were frightened and made a terrible decision."

Aries looked up.

**ARIES:**  
"Jake was probably frightened too."

The officer had no answer.

Aries looked away again.

**ARIES:**  
"He probably didn't even know what was happening."

A long pause.

**ARIES:**  
"He was just standing there."

His hands closed around the fabric of his sleeves.

**ARIES:**  
"He wasn't doing anything."

Another pause.

**ARIES:**  
"He was just being Jake."

---

# 9. THE QUESTION PERSONNEL COULD NOT ANSWER

At 14:46, Aries asked whether Jake was dead.

The welfare officer explained that Jake had already died once, that his current existence was anomalous, and that the disappearance did not establish what had happened to him.

Aries listened.

Then asked again.

**ARIES:**  
"Is he gone?"

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"We don't know."

Aries's eyes filled with tears.

**ARIES:**  
"Can you find him?"

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"We're going to keep looking."

**ARIES:**  
"Until you find him?"

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"Yes."

Aries looked down.

**ARIES:**  
"Promise?"

The officer hesitated.

A promise of recovery would have been dishonest.

A promise that the search would continue was not.

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"I promise we won't stop looking just because the first search failed."

Aries nodded once.

He did not look reassured.

But he remained in the room.

---

# 10. C.O.R.E. DIRECTIVE — ARIES AKANA

Aries is not to be left alone during the immediate aftermath of this incident without first confirming that he is comfortable with the arrangement and has access to support.

This is not because he is incapable of functioning independently.

It is because he has explicitly demonstrated severe distress and is struggling with the possibility of losing Jake again.

A trusted resident or welfare officer should remain available.

Personnel should offer water, a quiet environment, and the opportunity to speak without requiring him to do so.

He should receive updates when meaningful new information becomes available.

Do not repeatedly summon him for speculative briefings.

Do not make him watch footage of the incident unless it becomes necessary and he has been appropriately supported.

Do not require him to identify Jake's belongings or participate in experimental procedures.

Do not make him responsible for comforting other residents about Jake's disappearance.

He is allowed to be the person who needs comfort.

---

# 11. THE FAILURE

The incident was preventable.

Not because personnel could have predicted the exact mechanism by which Jake would disappear.

They could not.

It was preventable because a resident was confronted by an employee who did not understand who he was, interpreted his appearance as a hazard without evidence, and used equipment against him.

Aperture had already issued instructions concerning Jake's appearance.

Those instructions were not sufficient if new employees were still able to encounter him without adequate preparation.

C.O.R.E. is therefore ordering:

1. Immediate review of resident-orientation procedures.
    
2. Mandatory briefing for new personnel assigned to SAFEHOUSE.
    
3. Clear identification of anomalous residents who are not active threats.
    
4. Review of access to firefighting equipment in residential areas.
    
5. A formal investigation into the employee's conduct.
    
6. Continued search operations until Jake's location and condition are established.
    

The purpose of these measures is not to punish fear.

It is to prevent fear from becoming another resident's injury.

---

# 12. FINAL OBSERVATION

At 15:02, Aries was sitting on the edge of a welfare-room chair.

He had stopped asking questions for several minutes.

A staff member placed a glass of water on the table beside him.

Aries did not touch it.

He looked toward the doorway.

For an instant, he appeared to expect a small figure to hover there.

A pale, half-melted face.

No visible eyes.

A spectral teddy bear.

A little blue flame.

Perhaps Jake would appear behind him and ask what he was doing.

Perhaps he would say something quietly sarcastic.

Perhaps he would simply remain nearby.

Aries kept looking.

The doorway remained empty.

**ARIES:**  
"He always comes back."

The staff member sat nearby.

**ARIES:**  
"Even when I tell him I need to sleep. Even when I tell him to stop following me."

His voice trembled.

**ARIES:**  
"He comes back."

He wiped his face with his sleeve.

**ARIES:**  
"So why isn't he coming back?"

No one answered.

There was no answer to give him.

Not yet.

Aries bent forward and covered his face.

**ARIES:**  
"I can't lose him again."

The staff member moved closer but did not touch him without permission.

Aries began to cry.

Not quietly this time.

Not in the controlled manner he had used when discussing his brother's death before.

He cried like a sixteen-year-old boy who had been given his little brother back and had just watched him disappear.

**ARIES:**  
"Please bring him back."

He repeated it.

Again.

And again.

**ARIES:**  
"Please."

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The investigation into Jake's disappearance remains active.

His condition is unknown.

His location is unknown.

His fate is unknown.

C.O.R.E. will not declare him lost beyond recovery while those questions remain unanswered.

Aries Akana has already lived through his brother's death.

He should not be expected to endure the possibility of another loss in isolation.

The search for Jake will continue.

The investigation will continue.

The failures that permitted this incident will be addressed.

And while those efforts proceed, Aries will have someone beside him.

Not someone who tells him to be strong.

Not someone who tells him that everything will be fine.

Someone who can sit with him in the uncertainty and let him grieve without making him feel that his grief is another problem to solve.

Because Jake matters.

Because Aries matters.

And because, somewhere in this Blacksite, a little boy whose only light was a small blue flame may still be waiting to come home.

**We do not know whether Jake can hear us.**

**We do not know whether he can find his way back.**

**But we will keep looking.**

And if he returns, he will not be greeted by an empty corridor.

He will be greeted by people who know his name.

People who know what happened.

People who will not mistake him for a fire that needs extinguishing.

People who will make room for him.

And his brother will be there.

**— C.O.R.E. Anomalous Research & Resident Welfare Division**
