### **ANOMALOUS ENTITY PROFILE**

# **Z-335 — MARTS**

**Classification:** Allied Entity / Anomalous Species  
**Creator:** Lumi  
**Status:** Cooperative

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Z-335, commonly known as **Marts**, are anomalous magical water-based entities created by **Lumi**.

Despite their unusual abilities and behavior, Marts are considered a peaceful and cooperative species.

Their basic nature is simple:

> **Magical water blobs that run on water and happiness.**

---

# **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

A standard Mart possesses the following features:

- Blue semi-transparent blob-like body.
    
- Small deer antlers.
    
- Tiny top hat.
    
- Soft, flexible body structure.
    
- No visible internal organs.
    
- No conventional skeletal structure.
    

The top hat and antlers are considered natural parts of the Mart appearance.

The origin of the top hats remains unknown.

When questioned, Lumi simply stated:

> "...They looked cute."

---

# **MAGICAL COMPOSITION**

Marts are composed primarily of magical water.

Their bodies do not behave like normal liquid.

They can:

- Change shape.
    
- Compress themselves.
    
- Stretch.
    
- Form temporary solid structures.
    
- Absorb impacts.
    
- Alter their density.
    

Despite being water-based, they do not require containers and maintain their own form.

---

# **ENERGY SOURCE**

Marts operate through two primary resources:

## **Water**

Water maintains their physical structure.

A Mart without sufficient water becomes less stable.

## **Happiness**

Positive emotions act as a secondary energy source.

Marts naturally absorb and respond to:

- Joy.
    
- Friendship.
    
- Comfort.
    
- Positive social interactions.
    

This is why Marts are often found near people they like.

---

# **GRAVITY RESPONSE**

One of the most notable properties of Marts is their relationship with gravity.

Marts are not fully affected by conventional gravity.

They are capable of:

- Floating.
    
- Drifting.
    
- Hovering.
    
- Moving through the air.
    
- Resting on ceilings.
    
- Ignoring normal falling behavior.
    

A Mart falling from a height will often simply float downward or adjust its movement.

The exact mechanism is unknown.

Researchers have classified this behavior as:

> **"Selective gravitational interaction."**

---

# **FLIGHT AND MOVEMENT**

Although Marts appear to lack wings in their normal state, they can move freely through the air.

When necessary, some Marts can reveal small wings for enhanced movement.

However, most simply float.

Their preferred movement method:

> Drifting.

---

# **EMOTIONAL LIMITATIONS**

Because Marts are powered by happiness, extreme negative emotions are dangerous.

A Mart exposed to excessive:

- Stress.
    
- Fear.
    
- Distress.
    
- Hopelessness.
    

May become unstable.

In extreme cases, the Mart will burst.

However, due to the nature of the Mart colony, current evidence suggests the individual Mart returns rather than being permanently lost.

---

# **BEHAVIOR**

Marts are naturally:

- Friendly.
    
- Curious.
    
- Helpful.
    
- Social.
    
- Affectionate.
    

They have simple speech patterns, usually consisting of short phrases.

Examples:

> "...Food."

> "...Warm."

> "...Friend."

> "...Happy."

Despite their simple communication style, Marts demonstrate advanced emotional understanding.

---

# **SPECIALIZED MART ROLES**

The Mart colony has developed several specialized roles.

## **Resident Mart**

Normal Mart behavior.

Focused on companionship and assistance.

---

## **Camera Mart**

Monitors security cameras and observes threats.

---

## **Rapid Response Mart**

Specialized security role.

Capable of:

- Hiding in ventilation systems.
    
- Coordinated ambushes.
    
- Immobilizing threats.
    

Despite their appearance, they remain friendly entities.

---

## **Ambassador Mart**

The only known Mart role capable of fluent English communication.

Only one confirmed Ambassador Mart exists.

Primary duties:

- Translation.
    
- Communication between Marts and humans.
    
- Emotional support.
    

---

# **ELDER MARTS**

Five original Marts created by Lumi are designated as:

> **Elder Marts**

Characteristics:

- Slightly larger.
    
- More opaque bodies.
    
- Identical deer antlers.
    
- Identical top hats.
    
- Fluent English ability.
    

Despite their age, they hold no authority over other Marts.

They are simply the first.

---

# **LUMI'S CREATION**

Lumi created Marts without the intention of creating powerful entities.

Their purpose was:

> **To make the world happier.**

The fact that they became capable of defending facilities, operating security systems, and supporting personnel was a secondary development.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

A Mart is, technically:

- A magical water entity.
    
- A reality-defying creature.
    
- A security asset.
    
- A sentient anomaly.
    

But according to most personnel:

They are simply:

> "A little blue blob with antlers and a tiny hat that wants to help."

And that is exactly what Lumi intended.

**End of Z-335 Entity Profile**
