### **RESIDENTIAL / BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION LOG**

# **CORIN'S SANDWICH**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Current Status:** Stable / Recovered  
**Dietary Requirement:** None  
**Observed Preference:** **Pickles and Sandwiches**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin does not require food.

Her biological systems are no longer conventionally functional, and her body does not process food through normal digestion.

Anything she eats is eventually broken down and dissolved into Ether.

There is no nutritional benefit.

There is no meaningful metabolic requirement.

She eats because she **can taste food.**

And after spending so much of her existence unable to experience ordinary things, that is reason enough.

---

# **THE SANDWICH**

The preference began after a visit to the Blacksite.

Corin had accompanied Orphie for supplies.

During lunch, Neko noticed Corin sitting nearby without anything to eat.

He looked at his own sandwich.

Then at Corin.

> **Neko:**  
> "Want half?"

Corin initially shook her head.

Neko simply held the sandwich out.

> **"It's just lunch."**

Corin eventually accepted.

She took a bite.

She stopped.

Neko looked at her.

> **"...Good?"**

Corin nodded.

She then pointed at the sandwich.

> **"You like it?"**

Corin nodded again.

Then she pointed at one ingredient.

> **"...Pickles?"**

Neko nodded.

Corin took another bite.

And another.

That was the beginning.

---

# **PICKLES**

Corin likes pickles.

A lot.

Nobody is entirely sure why.

Her current explanation is simply:

> **"They're crunchy."**

She also enjoys the sourness.

Since Corin's sense of taste remains functional despite her inability to process food normally, the flavor is one of the few completely ordinary sensory experiences she can still enjoy.

She considers that valuable.

---

# **THE IMPORTANT PART**

Corin knows the food will not nourish her.

She knows it will eventually dissolve into Ether.

She knows she could go indefinitely without eating.

She eats anyway.

Because eating a sandwich feels normal.

Because tasting something good feels normal.

Because someone sharing their lunch with her feels normal.

And after everything Corin has lost, **normal is precious.**

---

# **NEKO'S RESPONSE**

Neko has since started making sure there is an extra sandwich available whenever Corin visits.

He does not make a big deal out of it.

He simply leaves it nearby.

Corin once noticed.

> **"You bought two."**

Neko shrugged.

> **"I wasn't hungry."**

Corin stared at him.

> **"You always buy two."**

Neko paused.

> **"...Maybe I like sandwiches."**

Corin smiled.

---

# **CORIN'S PREFERRED SANDWICH**

Current preference:

**Bread**

**Cheese**

**Meat**

**Pickles**

**A little mustard**

Nothing particularly complicated.

Corin considers excessive ingredients unnecessary.

She has also made it very clear that removing the pickles is unacceptable.

---

# **ORPHIE'S OBSERVATION**

Orphie eventually noticed that Corin became noticeably happier whenever sandwiches were involved.

She asked:

> **"You really like them that much?"**

Corin nodded.

> **"Even though you don't need them?"**

Corin thought for a moment.

Then:

> **"I know."**

She took another bite.

> **"But I can taste it."**

Orphie smiled.

> **"Fair enough."**

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light considers Corin's eating habit harmless.

The food does not interfere with her Ether stability.

It does not provide meaningful nutrition.

It does not need to.

Guiding Light has therefore stopped treating Corin's meals as a medical necessity.

They are simply meals.

Something Corin enjoys.

Something the others can share with her.

---

# **THE BLACKSITE LUNCH TABLE**

Corin has gradually become comfortable eating around the SAFEHOUSE residents.

Sometimes she gets:

- Sandwiches from Neko.
    
- Snacks from Belle.
    
- Fruit from Bao.
    
- Whatever someone happened to bring.
    

She occasionally forgets that she does not need to eat.

Nobody reminds her.

There is no reason to.

---

# **CURRENT OBSERVATION**

Corin may not require food.

Her body may dissolve it into Ether.

She may never experience hunger in the conventional sense.

But she can still taste.

She can still enjoy.

She can still sit beside someone at lunch.

And she can still say:

> **"Can I have another pickle?"**

Which, according to Neko, is the most normal thing she has asked for all week.
