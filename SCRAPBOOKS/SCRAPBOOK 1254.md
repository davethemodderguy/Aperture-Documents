# Executive Summary

Nora Tachyon recently resumed limited duties at the **Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise Store** despite not having fully recovered from her recent illness.

The decision was voluntary.

Nora insisted that she was well enough to tend the store for a limited period.

She was not.

During the shift, Nora experienced another fainting episode.

Personnel present in the area witnessed **Invictus Aeterna** immediately retrieve Nora and carry her back toward the SAFEHOUSE residential quarters.

No further injury occurred.

The incident has nevertheless resulted in renewed restrictions on Nora's store duties.

The underlying concern remains unchanged:

> **Nora is exhausted.**

And, more importantly:

> **Nora still cannot sleep properly.**

---

# 1. RETURN TO THE MERCH STORE

Nora had been eager to return to the store.

The store had been quiet.

No significant customer traffic was expected.

Nora therefore argued that she could handle a short shift.

Her reasoning was straightforward:

> **"I can sit."**

This was technically true.

Nora was provided with a chair behind the counter.

She had water available.

Aeterna was aware of the shift.

C.O.R.E. personnel were aware of her condition.

The arrangement was considered tolerable only because the workload was expected to be minimal.

Nora appeared pleased to be doing something normal again.

For approximately forty minutes, everything appeared fine.

---

# 2. OBSERVED STORE ACTIVITY

Nora performed basic shopkeeping duties.

She:

- greeted several visitors;
    
- processed merchandise requests;
    
- answered questions about Tachyon products;
    
- organized several display items;
    
- checked the store inventory;
    
- and spent a considerable amount of time quietly reading behind the counter.
    

Her behavior appeared calm.

Her physical condition did not.

Several personnel noted that Nora had become increasingly still.

She was no longer reading.

She was simply sitting with one hand against the counter.

A nearby employee asked:

> **"Nora, are you okay?"**

Nora nodded.

A few seconds later, she quietly replied:

> **"Just tired."**

---

# 3. COLLAPSE

Approximately two minutes later, Nora attempted to stand.

She did not make it fully upright.

Her knees gave way.

She briefly caught the edge of the counter.

Then lost consciousness.

Personnel immediately moved to assist.

Before they could reach her, however—

Aeterna was already there.

She had apparently detected the change in Nora's condition from elsewhere in the facility.

Aeterna caught Nora before she struck the floor.

Nora remained unconscious.

Aeterna lifted her without difficulty.

There was no dramatic announcement.

No panic.

No raised voice.

Aeterna simply looked toward the store personnel and stated:

> **"She is done for today."**

No one argued.

---

# 4. THE WALK BACK

Multiple personnel observed Aeterna carrying Nora through the SAFEHOUSE corridors.

Nora was completely limp against her.

Her white hair hung over Aeterna's arm.

Her glasses had shifted slightly.

Her store identification remained clipped to her clothing.

Aeterna carried her carefully and steadily.

One employee asked:

> **"Is she okay?"**

Aeterna answered:

> **"She is breathing."**

Pause.

> **"She is exhausted."**

That was the extent of the explanation.

Aeterna continued toward the residential quarters.

---

# 5. RETURN TO NORA'S ROOM

Nora regained consciousness shortly before reaching her room.

Her eyes opened.

She looked up.

The first thing she appeared to notice was that she was being carried.

> **NORA:**  
> "...Aeterna?"

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Yes."

Nora remained quiet for several seconds.

Then:

> **NORA:**  
> "Did I faint?"

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Yes."

Nora closed her eyes again.

> **NORA:**  
> "...Store?"

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Closed."

A pause.

> **NORA:**  
> "...Sorry."

Aeterna's response was immediate.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "No."

Nora opened her eyes.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "You do not apologize for being ill."

Nora looked away.

She did not respond.

Aeterna continued carrying her.

---

# 6. THE STORE AFTERWARD

The Tachyon Merchandise Store remained closed for the remainder of the shift.

A temporary notice was placed on the counter:

> **TEMPORARILY CLOSED**  
> **SHOPKEEPER IS RESTING**  
> **PLEASE RETURN LATER**

The wording was reportedly selected by Alice.

Neko later changed it.

The final version reads:

> **TEMPORARILY CLOSED — MEDICAL REST PERIOD**

Nora's store duties have subsequently been suspended until medical personnel determine that she can safely resume them.

---

# 7. THE SLEEP PROBLEM

The fainting episode cannot be considered independently from Nora's continuing sleep deprivation.

Nora remains unable to obtain consistent restorative sleep.

The nightmares continue.

They have not become less significant simply because the physical illness has begun improving.

If anything, the accumulated exhaustion has made the nights more difficult.

Nora frequently lies awake for extended periods.

When she finally begins drifting toward sleep, the memories return.

Sometimes the nightmare begins immediately.

Sometimes she wakes before it fully develops.

Sometimes she wakes because she remembers enough to know what is coming.

---

# 8. MEMORY

Nora remembers her death.

She remembers the pain.

She remembers being unable to stop what was happening.

She remembers being discarded.

Most importantly, she remembers **Neri's face.**

That memory has become one of the most persistent components of her nightmares.

Nora does not simply dream about dying.

She dreams about:

> **Neri watching her die.**

This distinction has remained consistent across multiple observations.

The memory therefore does not function merely as a frightening event from the past.

It is tied directly to Nora's strongest remaining emotional attachment.

She survived.

Neri survived.

They found each other again.

But Nora's nightmares repeatedly reconstruct the moment before any of that happened.

---

# 9. NIGHTTIME OBSERVATION

One night, Aeterna found Nora awake at approximately 03:00.

Nora was sitting upright in bed.

Her blanket was pulled tightly around her.

Her family journal was open beside her.

Aeterna entered.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "You should be sleeping."

Nora shook her head.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Nightmare?"

Nora nodded.

Aeterna sat beside her.

Nora stared at the journal.

> **NORA:**  
> "I remember it."

Aeterna remained silent.

> **NORA:**  
> "Even when I'm awake."

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> "I don't want to remember."

Aeterna did not tell her to forget.

Instead:

> **AETERNA:**  
> "I know."

Nora's voice became quieter.

> **NORA:**  
> "It keeps happening."

> **AETERNA:**  
> "I know."

Nora looked toward her.

> **NORA:**  
> "Why?"

Aeterna had no simple answer.

She did not invent one.

---

# 10. THE MEMORIES DO NOT HELP

Nora's family journal was originally intended to help preserve memories.

It contains photographs.

Drawings.

Family records.

Names.

Important events.

The page documenting the family's reunion reads:

> **WE FOUND EACH OTHER AGAIN.**

For most circumstances, the journal is comforting.

During the current sleep disturbance, however, it has become complicated.

Nora sometimes reads the journal because she wants reassurance that her family exists.

But the photographs also remind her of what she almost lost.

She sees:

**Neri.**

**Elsher.**

**Bao.**

**Aeterna.**

**Alice.**

**Sky.**

And then remembers the time when she believed she would never see them again.

The memories she loves and the memories she fears are now tangled together.

---

# 11. AETERNA'S RESPONSE

Aeterna does not attempt to remove the journal.

She does not tell Nora to stop remembering.

She does not promise that the nightmares will never return.

Instead, she repeatedly anchors Nora to the present.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "You are here."

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Your sister is alive."

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Your family is here."

> **AETERNA:**  
> "The event is over."

And, when Nora asks whether she will dream again:

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Perhaps."

Then:

> **AETERNA:**  
> "But you will wake here."

---

# 12. NERI'S INVOLVEMENT

Neri has continued visiting Nora.

She does not always stay long.

Her chemistry work remains extensive.

When she does visit, however, she usually sits beside Nora's bed.

During one visit, Nora quietly asked:

> **NORA:**  
> "Big Sis?"

Neri looked at her.

> **NORA:**  
> "Do you remember?"

Neri nodded.

Nora looked down.

> **NORA:**  
> "I don't want you to remember."

Neri remained silent.

Then she reached over and gently touched Nora's hand.

Nora held it.

No further words were exchanged.

---

Until Nora's sleep and physical condition improve:

### Tachyon Merchandise Store

**Suspended.**

### Training

**Suspended.**

### Range activity

**Suspended.**

### Extended walking

**Discouraged.**

### Independent prolonged activity

**Discouraged.**

### Unsupervised attempts to resume normal duties

**Not permitted during the current recovery period.**

Nora has been informed.

She is not pleased.

This is considered acceptable.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Nora's fainting episode at the Merch Store is not being treated as an isolated incident.

It is part of a larger pattern.

She became ill.

She lost significant sleep.

Her nightmares intensified.

Her appetite and hydration declined.

Her strength decreased.

She became temporarily bedridden.

She began fainting repeatedly.

Then, as soon as she felt marginally better, she attempted to return to work.

The result was predictable.

She collapsed.

Nora's desire to resume normal activities is understandable.

It is also currently unsafe.

---

# FINAL OBSERVATION

Personnel have become accustomed to seeing Aeterna carry Nora.

Usually this occurs because Nora wants to be carried.

This time was different.

Nora was unconscious.

The image of Aeterna walking through the SAFEHOUSE with Nora in her arms has therefore become a familiar one for an entirely different reason.

The child who normally runs the Tachyon Merchandise Store was once again being carried back to bed.

Not because she had done anything wrong.

Not because she had failed.

Not because she was weak.

Because she had spent a week barely sleeping while her body was still recovering from illness.

And because the nightmares would not let her rest.

The worst part is that sleep itself has become frightening.

Nora knows that when she closes her eyes, she may see the moment she died.

She may see Neri's face.

She may remember being discarded.

She may wake crying.

And knowing this does not make her memories disappear.

It only makes her afraid to sleep again.

So for now, Aeterna carries her home.

The store can wait.

The customers can wait.

The merchandise can wait.

Everything can wait.

Nora needs to recover.

And tonight, if she cannot sleep—

someone will still be there when she opens her eyes.
