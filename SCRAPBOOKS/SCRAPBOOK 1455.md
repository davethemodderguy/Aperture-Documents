---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A delegation from the **Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (ATF)** conducted a scheduled visit to an Aperture Industries Blacksite.

The visit was arranged as part of an existing inter-organizational agreement concerning anomalous weapons, advanced technology, and civilian safety.

The delegation was briefed extensively before arrival.

The briefing was considered sufficient.

It was not.

Within the first hour, the delegation encountered:

- a six-year-old anomalous resident;
    
- a ten-year-old resident with extensive firearms knowledge;
    
- a child resident participating in advanced weapons research;
    
- a Blacksite armory containing anomalous weapons;
    
- multiple residents carrying weapons normally restricted to trained personnel;
    
- and an experimental railgun capable of producing effects on a scale more appropriately described as **localized terraforming**.
    

The delegation remained professional throughout.

Several members nevertheless required a moment to process what they were seeing.

---

# 2. ARRIVAL

The ATF delegation entered through the designated external-contact facility.

Standard procedures were followed.

Identification was verified.

Weapons were secured according to the agreed protocol.

The delegation was escorted through the Blacksite by External Contact personnel and a C.O.R.E. representative.

The initial tour was uneventful.

Then they reached Sector 4-C.

---

# 3. FIRST CONTACT WITH SAFEHOUSE RESIDENTS

The delegation encountered several SAFEHOUSE residents during the initial walk-through.

A six-year-old boy was sitting nearby reading.

The ATF representative paused.

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “Is he a resident?”

> **C.O.R.E. REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “Yes.”

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “He's six.”

> **C.O.R.E. REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “Correct.”

The representative looked at the child.

Then at the C.O.R.E. representative.

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “And he's allowed to be here?”

> **C.O.R.E. REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “He lives here.”

The representative nodded slowly.

The child continued reading.

His teddy bear remained strapped to his back.

---

# 4. JAKE

The delegation was formally introduced to **Jake**.

Jake is six years old.

He is also a literal star in human manifestation, a Weeping Angel, and an anomalous resident whose physical characteristics do not correspond to conventional human biology.

The delegation had received his file beforehand.

Seeing him in person was nevertheless different.

One representative asked:

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “Does he normally carry that?”

The C.O.R.E. representative looked at Jake's celestial equipment.

> **C.O.R.E. REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “Some of it.”

The ATF representative paused.

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “He's six.”

> **C.O.R.E. REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “Yes.”

Jake looked up.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Hi.”

The representative waved.

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “Hi.”

Jake returned to his book.

---

# 5. NERI

The second major point of concern was Neri.

Neri is ten years old.

She is also one of C.O.R.E.'s most accomplished chemistry researchers and has extensive familiarity with Aperture's anomalous weapons systems.

The delegation encountered her inside a supervised research area.

Neri was wearing her usual glasses and blue clothing.

She was reviewing documentation.

An ATF representative noticed the weapon displayed in the secured area.

Then noticed Neri.

Then looked back at the weapon.

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “She works here?”

> **C.O.R.E. REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “She's a resident and researcher.”

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “She's ten.”

> **C.O.R.E. REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “Correct.”

Neri looked up.

> **NERI:**  
> “What?”

The representative immediately realized she had heard the conversation.

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “Nothing.”

Neri stared at him.

Then returned to her work.

---

# 6. THE FIREARMS QUESTION

The delegation was subsequently informed that several SAFEHOUSE residents possessed significant firearms knowledge.

This did not mean that children were permitted unrestricted access to firearms.

Aperture maintains strict internal controls regarding weapons.

Training, authorization, storage, supervision, and anomalous compatibility are assessed individually.

Nevertheless, the existence of extremely young residents who understand firearms at a professional level was unexpected.

One ATF representative summarized the situation privately:

> **“I knew the briefing said anomalous residents.”**

Pause.

> **“I didn't realize anomalous meant a ten-year-old could explain the armory better than I could.”**

---

# 7. TACHYON INDUSTRIES

The delegation was subsequently introduced to equipment developed through **Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise**.

Several weapons were identified as highly unconventional.

The delegation was specifically informed that Aperture's anomalous weapons should not automatically be compared to conventional commercial firearms.

Many incorporate anomalous materials, energy systems, or fictionalized mechanisms that do not correspond directly to conventional weapons technology.

The delegation was instructed not to handle any weapon without authorization.

They complied.

---

# 8. THE DEADLOCK

The delegation was shown the **SRA Deadbolt** under controlled conditions.

The weapon's designation as an **anti-materiel rifle** was explained.

Its purpose was demonstrated through controlled documentation rather than live firing.

One representative looked at the specifications.

Then looked at the weapon.

Then looked at Neri.

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “She uses this?”

> **C.O.R.E. REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “She has used it.”

Silence.

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “She's ten.”

Neri quietly adjusted her glasses.

> **NERI:**  
> “It's heavy.”

The representative looked relieved.

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “Okay.”

---

# 9. THE RAILGUN

The most significant portion of the tour involved an experimental Aperture railgun.

The system had been developed as part of Aperture's advanced anomalous-energy research.

The delegation was informed beforehand:

> **This is not a conventional firearm.**

That statement was technically correct.

It was also insufficient.

The railgun's output can produce extremely large-scale environmental effects under controlled conditions.

At sufficient power, its effects can alter terrain on a scale that C.O.R.E. engineering documentation classifies as:

> **LOCALIZED TERRAFORMING CAPABILITY**

The delegation became noticeably quieter.

---

# 10. DEMONSTRATION

No personnel were present within the demonstration zone.

No civilian-accessible area was used.

The system was activated under controlled testing conditions.

The projectile impacted the designated test region.

The resulting effect substantially altered the terrain.

A previously level section of ground was transformed into a large, irregular geological formation.

The demonstration ended.

The delegation stared through the observation window.

Nobody spoke for approximately twelve seconds.

Finally:

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “You call that a railgun?”

> **C.O.R.E. ENGINEER:**  
> “Technically.”

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “What would you call it if you weren't being technical?”

The engineer considered this.

> **C.O.R.E. ENGINEER:**  
> “A very expensive landscaping device.”

No one laughed.

The engineer looked disappointed.

---

# 11. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri had been present during the demonstration.

She looked at the newly altered terrain.

Then at the railgun.

Then at the delegation.

> **NERI:**  
> “It's still being calibrated.”

The ATF representative slowly turned toward her.

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “Still?”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

The representative looked back at the crater.

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “That's the calibrated version?”

Neri shook her head.

> **NERI:**  
> “No.”

Silence.

---

# 12. APERTURE SAFETY POLICY

The delegation was subsequently briefed on Aperture's internal safety standards.

The existence of anomalous weaponry does not mean unrestricted access.

Aperture maintains:

- controlled storage;
    
- authorization requirements;
    
- test ranges;
    
- personnel restrictions;
    
- engineering review;
    
- medical support;
    
- anomalous compatibility assessments;
    
- and incident documentation.
    

Children residing within SAFEHOUSE are not considered automatically authorized merely because they possess unusual capabilities.

Their individual circumstances are evaluated separately.

---

# 13. THE ATF'S PRIMARY CONCERN

The delegation's concerns were not limited to the existence of advanced weapons.

The more significant issue was the combination of:

> **ANOMALOUS CHILDREN + EXTREME TECHNOLOGY + UNCONVENTIONAL PHYSIOLOGY**

Aperture clarified that SAFEHOUSE residents are not employees to be assigned as weapons personnel.

They are residents.

Some happen to possess exceptional abilities.

Some happen to have extensive technical knowledge.

Some happen to have combat experience.

Their age does not determine their anomalous capabilities.

Nor does their capability eliminate their status as residents requiring welfare protections.

---

# 14. NERI'S CHEMISTRY WORK

The delegation was also informed of Neri's position within the Chemical and Bio Engineering Executive.

Her research aptitude is exceptional.

She has independently produced substantial legitimate chemistry research and maintains a large personal research archive.

The ATF delegation was shown only information approved for external access.

One representative asked:

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “How old is she again?”

> **C.O.R.E. REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “Ten.”

The representative looked at Neri.

Neri looked back.

> **NERI:**  
> “What?”

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “Nothing.”

Neri returned to her work.

---

# 15. THE SIX-YEAR-OLD QUESTION

At the conclusion of the tour, one representative privately asked C.O.R.E. personnel:

> **“How do you maintain normal childhood development around all of this?”**

The response was straightforward.

> **“We try to let them be children.”**

The Blacksite contains:

- recreational areas;
    
- schools and educational resources;
    
- kitchens;
    
- common rooms;
    
- personal bedrooms;
    
- entertainment;
    
- medical services;
    
- and residents who actively look after one another.
    

A child knowing how to operate anomalous technology does not mean Aperture expects that child to spend every day operating anomalous technology.

---

# 16. PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT

Despite their initial surprise, the ATF delegation remained professional.

They asked questions.

They followed safety instructions.

They did not attempt to interfere with residents.

They did not request demonstrations outside the approved schedule.

They did not treat anomalous residents as exhibits.

C.O.R.E. considers this conduct appropriate.

---

# 17. FINAL MEETING

Before departure, the delegation met with C.O.R.E. representatives.

The discussion covered:

- anomalous weapons classification;
    
- civilian safety;
    
- international cooperation;
    
- controlled technology;
    
- firearms-related anomalies;
    
- and the legal difficulties surrounding entities that do not possess conventional human legal status.
    

No final policy changes were requested.

The existing agreement remains active.

---

# 18. DEPARTURE

The delegation prepared to leave.

As they passed through the Sector 4-C corridor, Jake was sitting on the floor reading.

Neri was nearby.

She was holding a research tablet.

The delegation stopped.

One representative looked at both children.

Then at the tablet.

Then at Jake's teddy bear.

Then at Neri.

He finally asked:

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “Does anyone here have a normal hobby?”

Neri thought about it.

> **NERI:**  
> “Coffee.”

Jake looked up.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Books.”

The representative nodded.

> **ATF REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> “Good.”

He continued walking.

---

# 19. POST-VISIT ASSESSMENT

The ATF delegation departed the Blacksite without incident.

No unauthorized technology was removed.

No resident was injured.

No security breach occurred.

No anomalous equipment was improperly handled.

The visit successfully demonstrated that Aperture's external-relations framework can accommodate conventional government agencies even when the Blacksite's technological and biological circumstances exceed conventional expectations.

The delegation's final informal observation was recorded as:

> **“We came expecting unusual firearms.”**

> **“We did not expect a six-year-old star, a ten-year-old chemist, and a railgun that can rearrange geography.”**

C.O.R.E. considers this an accurate summary.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The Blacksite contains things that would be impossible to explain to an ordinary firearms inspector.

Children who are not ordinary children.

Weapons that are not ordinary weapons.

A railgun that engineers have stopped describing in terms of firepower because:

> **“Firepower”**

does not adequately describe something that can change the landscape.

And yet, at the end of the tour, the strangest part was not the weapons.

It was watching Jake quietly turn a page in his book while Neri drank coffee beside him.

To them, this was simply another day.

To the visiting delegation:

It was apparently quite a day.

External Contact & Relations Executive**
