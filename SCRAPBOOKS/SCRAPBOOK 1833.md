### SAFEHOUSE WELFARE & ANOMALOUS RESIDENTS DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** CORE-SW-CC-027

**STATUS:** ACTIVE — FAMILY STATUS AMENDMENT  
**SUBJECT:** _An Older Sister, Officially_  
**RELATED INCIDENT:** CORE-SW-CC-026 — _Five Weeks of Snow_  
**INVOLVED PERSONNEL:** Jake Akana, Camelia Candleheart, Aries Akana

---

### I. PURPOSE OF AMENDMENT

Following the five-week exterior exposure incident, C.O.R.E. has revised its records concerning Camelia Candleheart, Jake Akana, and Aries Akana.

Three matters require formal clarification.

1. Camelia's memory is **fragmented**, with severe Etheric Retrograde Amnesia affecting her ability to retain and retrieve information about people, events, and everyday activities.
    
2. Jake Akana's speech has been permanently impaired. He can now communicate only through short bursts of words, making lengthy conversations and detailed verbal explanations difficult or impossible.
    
3. At the request and with the authorization of Aries Akana, Camelia Candleheart is now **officially recognized as Jake Akana's older sister**.
    

The third matter is not a medical designation or an attempt to assign Camelia responsibility for the incident.

It is a family decision.

Aries is Jake's older brother. His current responsibilities mean he cannot personally keep an eye on Jake at all times. He has therefore formally recognized Camelia as Jake's older sister, acknowledging the relationship the two had already begun to build.

This recognition does not make Camelia Jake's sole guardian, nor does it transfer all responsibility for Jake's safety to her. SAFEHOUSE staff and C.O.R.E. retain their obligations to both residents.

The title of _older sister_ is official.

The expectation that Camelia must never forget is not.

---

### II. CAMELIA CANDLEHEART — FRAGMENTED MEMORY

Camelia's condition must not be summarized as simply forgetting her past.

Her memories are fragmented. Information may be accessible at one moment and absent at another. She may remember a person without recalling the circumstances in which they met, remember an event without understanding its place in her life, or encounter familiar information without recovering the memories associated with it.

Her notebook remains essential.

However, the notebook cannot restore continuity to memories that are missing. It can provide facts, names, reminders, and instructions, but reading an entry does not guarantee that Camelia will recover the emotional context or experience of the event described.

This distinction is particularly important following the incident involving Jake.

Camelia may read that she left Jake outside. She may understand that he was exposed to the cold for five weeks. She may know that his speech was permanently damaged.

None of this guarantees that she can remember doing it.

Nor does an inability to remember make the consequences less real.

C.O.R.E. will not require Camelia to perform a particular emotional response to prove that she cares about Jake. Her distress, confusion, and recollections may vary as her memory fluctuates.

Her relationship with Jake must be supported through consistent information, accessible reminders, and opportunities to interact in the present—not through repeated interrogations about the past.

---

### III. JAKE AKANA — COMMUNICATION AFTER THE INCIDENT

Jake's speech impairment presents a separate challenge.

He can communicate only in short bursts of words. A question that once could have been answered in a sentence may now require several attempts, and some explanations may be beyond his current ability to express verbally.

Personnel are reminded that **limited speech is not limited understanding**.

Jake must not be treated as though he has become less intelligent, less aware, or less deserving of participation in decisions concerning him.

Staff must adapt their communication to his needs.

**Approved communication practices:**

- Ask one short question at a time.
    
- Allow sufficient time for Jake to respond.
    
- Do not finish his words for him unless he has indicated that he wants assistance.
    
- Accept gestures and other established forms of communication without forcing him to speak.
    
- Do not repeatedly ask him to recount the five-week incident.
    
- Never interpret an incomplete answer as consent, agreement, or proof that he has nothing more to say.
    

Most importantly, Camelia must not be expected to infer Jake's needs simply because she is now his sister. Her fragmented memory may prevent her from recalling a conversation, and Jake's damaged speech may make it difficult for him to repeat information.

Their relationship needs support from people who can reliably maintain continuity.

Neither child should be left to bridge that gap alone.

---

### IV. THE DECISION OF ARIES AKANA

**INTERNAL FAMILY STATUS RECORD — SUMMARY OF AUTHORIZATION**

Aries Akana's decision was submitted after consideration of his existing responsibilities and his ability to remain personally available to Jake throughout the day.

He cannot keep watch over his younger brother at all times.

This is not a withdrawal of affection or a relinquishment of his position as Jake's older brother. It is an acknowledgment that one person cannot provide continuous supervision indefinitely.

Aries formally recognized Camelia as Jake's older sister, establishing an official familial relationship where an informal one had existed before.

The decision recognizes what Camelia and Jake had already become to each other.

It does not erase the snow incident.

It does not imply that Camelia is incapable of making mistakes, nor that Jake must forgive her on anyone else's timetable.

And it does not make Jake responsible for helping Camelia remember that she is his sister.

**C.O.R.E. administrative note:**

The designation is to be honored in SAFEHOUSE records and ordinary resident interactions. Camelia is Jake's older sister. No resident is to describe the relationship as merely hypothetical or unofficial.

Support arrangements remain a separate matter and must be based on each resident's actual needs.

---

### V. WHAT THE TITLE MEANS

The title of _older sister_ carries emotional significance, but it cannot function as a substitute for a safety plan.

Camelia may forget that she is Jake's sister.

She may forget that he is waiting for her.

She may forget a promise they made yesterday.

Those possibilities must be addressed without making her feel that she has to earn her place in the family through perfect recall.

Likewise, Jake may be unable to tell her that something is wrong. He may manage only a few words, a fragment of a sentence, or no speech at all.

Those possibilities must be addressed without making him feel that he has become a burden.

The family arrangement will therefore operate alongside the existing SAFEHOUSE welfare system:

- Jake will continue to receive appropriate supervision and medical support.
    
- Camelia will receive practical help with memory-dependent tasks.
    
- Shared activities will use clear return checks and reliable communication.
    
- Other responsible residents and staff will remain involved.
    
- Neither sibling will be made solely responsible for the other's safety.
    

The purpose of recognizing Camelia as Jake's sister is not to appoint a replacement pair of eyes for Aries.

It is to recognize a bond that matters to both children—and to help that bond survive circumstances neither of them can fully control.

---

### VI. C.O.R.E. CLOSING OBSERVATION

There is a painful contradiction at the center of this family arrangement.

Camelia is now officially Jake's older sister.

She may not always remember that.

Jake is her little brother.

He may not always be able to tell her what he needs.

Aries has made the decision because he cannot be everywhere at once. He is still Jake's older brother, but he has acknowledged that Jake needs people around him who can help share the responsibility of keeping him safe.

This is not a story about replacing one sibling with another.

It is about a family attempting to care for a child who has already suffered a permanent injury—and making room for another child whose own disability affects how she understands and remembers the world.

Camelia's memories may remain fragmented. Jake's words may remain broken.

Their relationship does not have to depend on either of them being able to explain it perfectly.

Sometimes, being siblings will mean Camelia reading her own notebook to remember what happened.

Sometimes, it will mean Jake managing only a few words.

Sometimes, it will mean another resident staying nearby because neither child should have to manage everything alone.

And sometimes, it will mean Aries accepting that he cannot watch his little brother every second, while ensuring that Jake is still surrounded by people who care.

**FINAL RECORD AMENDMENT:**

**Camelia Candleheart — Older sister of Jake Akana. Officially recognized by Aries Akana.**

**Jake Akana — Younger brother of Camelia Candleheart and Aries Akana. Requires continued medical care, accessible communication, and reliable resident-welfare safeguards.**

_Family is not a guarantee that someone will always remember. It is also the decision to keep showing up when remembering becomes difficult._
