### **PERSONAL ITEM ARCHIVE**

# **COREY — INHERITED COOKBOOK**

**Classification:** Personal Property Record  
**Owner:** Corey  
**Item Type:** Family Artifact / Recipe Collection

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Among Corey's personal belongings brought from her original universe is a handwritten cookbook originally belonging to her mother.

The cookbook has remained with Corey throughout her travels between worlds.

Unlike many of Corey's possessions, which are primarily practical items, this object is considered deeply sentimental.

---

# **ITEM DESCRIPTION**

The cookbook is a worn but carefully maintained book.

Observed characteristics include:

- Handwritten recipes.
    
- Personal notes in the margins.
    
- Small corrections and additions made over time.
    
- Signs of frequent use.
    
- A noticeably cared-for condition despite its age.
    

The exact origin date is unknown.

Corey has never allowed the book to be discarded, replaced, or digitally copied.

---

# **CONTENTS**

The cookbook contains a large collection of recipes.

Known categories include:

- Everyday meals.
    
- Desserts.
    
- Baked goods.
    
- Family recipes.
    
- Seasonal dishes.
    
- Comfort food.
    
- Special occasion meals.
    

Many recipes contain additional notes written by Corey's mother.

Examples include:

> "Add a little more if you like it sweeter."

> "Don't rush this step."

> "This tastes better when shared."

---

# **COREY'S COOKING**

Corey learned many of the recipes directly from the cookbook.

Although she is primarily known for her work in Research and Engineering, she is also a capable cook.

Her cooking style is described as:

- Careful.
    
- Patient.
    
- Precise.
    
- Comfort-oriented.
    

She often follows recipes exactly, including small handwritten notes left by her mother.

---

# **DIALYN'S OBSERVATION**

Dialyn was one of the first Blacksite residents to notice the cookbook.

When asked about it, Corey explained that it belonged to her mother.

Dialyn immediately understood its importance.

> "...Then it should be kept safe."

The cookbook was subsequently stored in a protected but accessible location.

---

# **MART INVOLVEMENT**

The Marts quickly became interested in Corey's cooking.

They often gather nearby while she prepares recipes.

Common behaviors include:

- Watching ingredients being prepared.
    
- Carrying small items.
    
- Sitting on counters.
    
- Attempting to "help."
    
- Waiting for the finished food.
    

A Mart's usual response after smelling a recipe:

> "...Food."

Followed by:

> "...Corey food."

---

# **NEKO'S COMMENT**

Neko was initially skeptical when Corey brought the cookbook into Engineering.

After trying one of the recipes, his opinion changed.

> "...Good."

When asked if he wanted the recipe:

> "...Yes."

This was considered a strong endorsement.

---

# **CORIN'S COMMENT**

Corin understands the value of the cookbook more than most.

She recognizes that it is not simply a collection of recipes.

It is one of the few physical connections Corey still has to her family.

When asked if Corey should preserve it digitally, Corin replied:

> "...Maybe."

Then:

> "...But the handwriting matters."

---

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

Lumi considers the cookbook another example of Corey's connection to her past.

> "...Memories are not always stored in the mind."

She looked through the pages carefully.

> "...Sometimes they are stored in things."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The cookbook remains in Corey's possession.

It is frequently used.

It is not treated as a museum piece.

Meals prepared from it are regularly shared with:

- Corin.
    
- Neko.
    
- Marts.
    
- SAFEHOUSE residents.
    
- Blacksite personnel when appropriate.
    

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

One evening, Corey prepared a meal using one of the oldest recipes in the cookbook.

Several Marts gathered around the kitchen.

Corin helped with preparation.

Neko quietly observed the process.

For a moment, the Blacksite kitchen felt less like part of a hidden facility...

And more like a home.

When asked why she still carries the cookbook after everything she has experienced, Corey simply answered:

> "...Because my mom made these."

She paused.

> "...And now I can too."

A nearby Mart nodded.

> "...Good food."

Corey smiled.

> "...Yeah."
