## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / RECOVERY LOG**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — ONE WEEK AT THE TREEHOUSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Location:** Treehouse  
**Duration:** **7 DAYS**  
**Status:** **STABLE / BED REST**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following Alice's five-hour exposure to the cold, she was placed under extended observation.

Rather than returning immediately to SAFEHOUSE, Alice remained at the Treehouse.

For one full week, she stayed in bed.

Corin remained with her.

Orphie remained with both of them.

There were no attempts to make Alice walk.

No attempts to make her ride the scooter.

No pressure to leave.

No expectations.

She was simply allowed to rest.

---

# **DAY 1**

Alice slept for most of the day.

When awake, she barely moved.

Corin stayed beside her.

Orphie occasionally checked her condition and brought whatever was needed.

Alice ate a small amount of Corin's soup.

Then went back to sleep.

Her scooter remained outside.

---

# **DAY 2**

Alice slept less.

She was still extremely weak.

She spent most of the day looking out the window.

Snow covered the surrounding trees.

Corin sat beside her.

Neither spoke much.

Eventually Alice asked:

> **"Can Alice stay here?"**

Corin answered:

> **"Of course."**

Alice closed her eyes.

> **"Okay."**

---

# **DAY 3**

Orphie brought blankets.

Alice was already wrapped in several.

She didn't complain.

She simply accepted another.

Corin made soup.

Alice ate.

It remained the only substantial food she willingly consumed.

Orphie sat on the other side of the bed.

For several minutes, none of them spoke.

It was peaceful.

---

# **DAY 4**

Alice woke up feeling slightly better.

She attempted to sit upright.

Corin immediately noticed.

> **"Slowly."**

Alice stopped.

> **"I'm okay."**

Orphie replied:

> **"You don't have to prove that."**

Alice stared at her.

Then quietly lay back down.

---

# **DAY 5**

Alice asked about her scooter.

> **"Is scooter okay?"**

Orphie nodded.

> **"It's fine."**

Alice smiled.

> **"Good."**

She didn't ask to ride it.

She simply wanted to know it was still there.

---

# **DAY 6**

Alice spent part of the afternoon sitting between Corin and Orphie.

Her rabbit ears occasionally twitched.

Her white hair was messy from sleeping.

Her soft hat sat crookedly on her head.

Corin fixed it.

Alice smiled.

> **"Thanks."**

Orphie watched quietly.

It was one of the rare moments where Alice looked genuinely comfortable.

---

# **DAY 7**

Alice woke up before Corin.

She didn't get out of bed.

She simply looked around the room.

Corin was asleep beside her.

Orphie was sitting nearby.

The Treehouse was quiet.

Alice pulled the blanket closer.

Then whispered:

> **"I like here."**

Corin woke slightly.

> **"Hmm?"**

Alice repeated:

> **"I like here."**

Corin smiled.

> **"Me too."**

---

# **MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

The week produced no dramatic recovery.

Alice did not suddenly regain her stamina.

Her immune system remains fragile.

Her nutritional condition remains concerning.

Her permanent right-eye blindness remains unchanged.

Her body remains vulnerable.

But she did not worsen.

More importantly:

**She rested.**

She ate.

She slept.

She remained warm.

She did not attempt to run.

She did not attempt to walk beyond her limits.

She did not injure herself.

For Alice, that constitutes significant progress.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin spent most of the week within arm's reach.

She did not force conversation.

She did not bring up Alice's past.

She did not ask Alice to explain herself.

She simply stayed.

Sometimes she read.

Sometimes she cooked.

Sometimes she watched Alice sleep.

Sometimes Alice woke up and found Corin still there.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie remained equally quiet.

She handled practical matters.

Food.

Water.

Medical supplies.

Communication with SAFEHOUSE.

She made sure nobody disturbed them unnecessarily.

When Alice asked why she was staying:

> **"Because I want to."**

Alice seemed satisfied with that answer.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

For one week, Alice did nothing.

She didn't explore.

She didn't run.

She didn't try to prove she was fine.

She didn't worry about getting anywhere.

She stayed in a small bed inside the Treehouse with Corin and Orphie.

And nothing bad happened.

Sometimes Alice slept between them.

Sometimes she stared out at the snow.

Sometimes she ate soup.

Sometimes she talked.

Sometimes she simply existed.

For a child who spent so long believing she had to keep moving—

seven days of simply being allowed to stay was perhaps exactly what she needed.

**End of CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / RECOVERY LOG**
