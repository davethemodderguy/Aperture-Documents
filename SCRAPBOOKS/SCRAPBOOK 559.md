### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / PERSONNEL CLASSIFICATION REPORT**

# **CORIN — CAT THIREN CLASSIFICATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Classification Update:** **CAT THIREN**  
**Status:** Stable / Self-Determined

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has officially been classified as a **Cat Thiren**.

The change is confirmed to be genuine and permanent.

Corin now possesses:

- Feline ears.
    
- One or more feline tails.
    
- Corresponding feline physical characteristics.
    
- Cat-thiren traits consistent with her new classification.
    

Most importantly, this transformation was **not caused by Guiding Light.**

It was not an unintended consequence of the original blessing.

It was not an Ether accident.

It was not an anomalous chain reaction.

**Corin simply became what she wanted to be.**

---

# **THE CHANGE**

The development of Corin's feline characteristics was initially detected when personnel noticed a pair of cat ears appearing on her head.

The ears function normally.

They respond to sounds and move independently according to Corin's emotional state.

A feline tail subsequently manifested as well.

C.O.R.E. initially suspected another unexpected Ether-related physiological development.

That hypothesis was rejected.

No connection to Guiding Light's blessing could be established.

---

# **CORIN'S EXPLANATION**

When asked why she had become a Cat Thiren, Corin gave a surprisingly straightforward answer.

> **"Because I wanted to."**

That was the entire explanation.

When asked whether she had used Ether to force the transformation:

> **"No."**

When asked whether Guiding Light had done something:

> **"No."**

Guiding Light immediately confirmed:

> **"I didn't do anything this time."**

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S RELIEF**

Guiding Light was particularly relieved.

After discovering that her original blessing had accidentally contributed to Corin's biological restoration, she had become increasingly worried that every new development in Corin's body might somehow trace back to her.

Upon learning about Corin's Cat Thiren transformation, Guiding Light carefully examined the phenomenon.

The conclusion was clear.

**She had nothing to do with it.**

Guiding Light reportedly sighed in relief.

> **"Thank goodness."**

Mischevious Light immediately responded:

> **"So she did it herself."**

Guiding Light nodded.

> **"Yes."**

> **"That's hilarious."**

---

# **SELF-DETERMINED PHYSIOLOGY**

The transformation represents an unusual aspect of Corin's current anomalous biology.

Her body is capable of responding to her own desires at a fundamental physiological level.

Rather than being imposed externally, the change appears to have originated from Corin herself.

C.O.R.E. therefore classifies the phenomenon as:

> **Self-Determined Anomalous Morphogenesis**

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

---

# **CAT THIREN TRAITS**

Corin's new feline characteristics include:

### **Ears**

Her cat ears are fully functional.

They move in response to:

- Sound.
    
- Attention.
    
- Curiosity.
    
- Fear.
    
- Excitement.
    
- Irritation.
    

Her ears also make her emotional state considerably easier to read.

Corin does not appreciate this.

---

### **Tail**

Corin's tail is fully functional and expressive.

It moves naturally with her body and appears to assist with balance.

She has also discovered that her tail sometimes reacts independently of her conscious intentions.

This has occasionally betrayed her emotions.

For example, when Corin insists she is not interested in something while her tail is enthusiastically moving:

> **"...Stop looking at it."**

Nobody does.

---

# **THE WINGS**

Corin remains capable of having both her feline characteristics and her anomalous wings simultaneously.

The two systems do not interfere with one another.

Her wings remain:

- Semi-transparent.
    
- Luminescent.
    
- Regenerative.
    
- Flexible.
    
- Capable of flight.
    
- Capable of feather deployment.
    

Her cat ears and tail simply became additional parts of her physiology.

---

# **YARN INCIDENTS — EXPLAINED**

The SAFEHOUSE's previous observations regarding Corin's fascination with yarn have been revisited.

Previously:

> **"Corin likes yarn for some reason."**

That explanation is now considerably less mysterious.

Neko, Miss Neko, and Endmin are all Cat Thirens.

Corin is now one as well.

The SAFEHOUSE's existing yarn collection has therefore acquired a fourth permanent participant.

Neko's reaction was immediate:

> **"I knew it."**

Corin looked at him.

> **"Knew what?"**

> **"You were one of us."**

Corin smiled.

> **"...Apparently."**

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE TO HER NEW FORM**

Corin appears completely comfortable with the change.

She has not attempted to hide her ears or tail.

She frequently allows her ears to remain visible while walking through the SAFEHOUSE.

Her wings remain visible as well.

The combination has become an increasingly recognizable part of her appearance.

---

# **IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION**

C.O.R.E. formally rejects the following explanations:

> **Guiding Light created Corin's cat traits.**

**False.**

> **The original blessing caused the transformation.**

**False.**

> **The Ether chain reaction forced the transformation.**

**Unconfirmed and currently unsupported.**

> **Corin was accidentally transformed by another Architect.**

**False.**

The current conclusion is considerably simpler:

> **Corin wanted to be a Cat Thiren.**

And somehow, her body agreed.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin spent a long time trying to understand what she was.

For years, she believed she was neither alive nor dead.

Then her body became alive.

She developed wings.

She learned to fly.

She learned to eat, cook, bleed, heal, and live normally again.

Now she has made another choice about herself.

She wanted cat ears.

She wanted a tail.

She wanted to be a Cat Thiren.

So she became one.

Not because Guiding Light blessed her.

Not because an Architect changed her.

Not because her Ether malfunctioned.

But simply because:

> **"It's what I want to be."**

And for once, nobody is trying to explain it away.
