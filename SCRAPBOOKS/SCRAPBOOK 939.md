# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. DIVISION — SAFEHOUSE ARCHITECTURAL ANOMALY REPORT

**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — RESIDENTIAL WING  
**ANOMALY:** LOCALIZED REALITY DISPLACEMENT  
**SUBSPACE:** NERI-01  
**STATUS:** STABLE / HABITABLE

---

# 1. GENERAL DESCRIPTION

Neri's room no longer follows conventional architectural geometry.

The easiest visual description provided by observers is:

> **"It looks like the room broke in half during an earthquake, except the other side isn't the rest of the building."**

The original doorway remains connected to the SAFEHOUSE.

Upon entering, however, the observer finds themselves inside Neri's subspace.

The transition is abrupt.

There is no conventional hallway.

No portal.

No loading effect.

The normal room simply appears to have **broken apart**, revealing an entirely different environment on the other side.

The broken edges remain visually recognizable as portions of the original room.

Beyond them is the subspace.

---

# 2. THE SUBSPACE ENVIRONMENT

Neri's subspace is characterized by a large open void containing several isolated floating landmasses.

These have been informally designated **"islands."**

Each island contains a portion of Neri's belongings or living space.

The islands are not fixed to a floor.

They float independently within the subspace.

There is no observed gravitational requirement forcing objects toward a common floor plane.

Visitors can therefore move freely throughout the space.

### Movement is three-dimensional.

A person can:

- walk across an island;
    
- jump between islands;
    
- hover;
    
- fly;
    
- or simply float through the open space.
    

The subspace therefore functions less like a room and more like a **small personal environment suspended outside conventional geometry.**

---

# 3. PRIMARY ISLAND — BED

One of the largest islands contains Neri's bed.

The bed remains recognizable as the same furniture from her original room.

It has simply been separated from the rest of the room and placed within the subspace.

Neri can reach it normally from the surrounding space.

She occasionally uses the island to read.

She also appears to have discovered that she can simply float around the room instead of walking.

This has not resulted in a significant behavioral change.

She still frequently walks.

---

# 4. BOOKSHELF ISLAND

A separate floating island contains Neri's bookshelf.

The collection consists primarily of:

- chemistry books;
    
- general science material;
    
- personal reading;
    
- reference material;
    
- and various documents Neri has accumulated.
    

The bookshelf is secured to the island despite the lack of conventional architecture around it.

Neri appears to have intentionally separated the bookshelf from her bed.

When asked why:

> **Neri:** "So I have somewhere to read."

Neko reportedly responded:

> **"You have a bookshelf floating in a reality fracture."**

Neri:

> **"Yes."**

---

# 5. LABORATORY ISLAND

One of the more concerning—and simultaneously more useful—islands contains Neri's laboratory equipment.

The island functions as her personal chemistry workspace.

It contains equipment previously approved for her use, alongside materials she has accumulated through supervised experimentation.

Because Neri's chemistry ability and molecular restructuring ability overlap significantly, C.O.R.E. has classified this area as a **controlled workspace.**

Neri is permitted to experiment there under existing laboratory safety rules.

The principal rule remains:

> **Neri may invent something.**
> 
> **Neri must tell Neko before testing it.**

The existence of a private subspace has not changed this rule.

---

# 6. CROSSBOW ISLAND

Another island contains Neri's mechanical compound crossbow and associated equipment.

The crossbow remains stored when not in use.

Neri's Starlight Katana is also stored within her personal belongings, alongside her other equipment.

The island effectively functions as a personal equipment area.

This arrangement is considered preferable to allowing Neri's numerous concealed knives to remain scattered throughout the SAFEHOUSE.

It has not, however, resulted in Neri carrying fewer knives.

---

# 7. KNIFE STORAGE

Neri's knives are distributed throughout her belongings and equipment.

The exact number remains unknown.

Some are stored on the equipment island.

Others remain concealed in her clothing and accessories.

Her crossed hairclips remain functional blades.

Her fish-bone hairclip remains purely decorative and continues to be treated as a sentimental item belonging to her younger sister.

C.O.R.E. has decided not to ask Neri to count the knives.

This has historically produced no useful information.

---

# 8. THE OPEN SPACE

The most unusual feature of Neri's room is what **isn't** there.

There is no conventional floor connecting the islands.

There is no conventional ceiling.

There are no walls surrounding the entire environment.

Instead, the islands remain suspended within an open subspace.

The surrounding area appears to extend considerably farther than the original room should permit.

Yet the space remains completely accessible.

Neri can move through it freely.

Her Light wings make this particularly convenient.

She can simply manifest them and fly between the islands.

However, she frequently hides her wings and uses the natural floating properties of the subspace instead.

---

# 9. VISUAL APPEARANCE FROM THE SAFEHOUSE

The exterior remains almost completely normal.

A person walking past Neri's room sees:

**A normal SAFEHOUSE door.**

Nothing appears to indicate that the room contains an environment larger than the building itself.

Opening the door produces the anomaly.

The interior appears as though reality itself has been fractured.

One side still resembles the original SAFEHOUSE room.

The other side opens into Neri's floating island environment.

This makes the effect visually similar to an architectural cross-section created by an earthquake, except the "broken" boundary does not expose another part of the building.

It exposes **somewhere else.**

---

# 10. NERI'S EXPLANATION

Neri was asked to describe exactly what she did.

Her response:

> **"I broke the room."**

Archivist:

> **"And then?"**

> **Neri:** "I put it somewhere else."

Archivist:

> **"Where?"**

Neri gestured toward the subspace.

> **Neri:** "Here."

Archivist:

> **"...Why are there islands?"**

Neri:

> **"I wanted them."

This is currently the most complete explanation available.

---

# 11. GUIDING LIGHT'S REACTION

Guiding Light was extremely impressed.

> **Guiding Light:** "You made your own little world!"

> **Neri:** "It's my room."

> **Guiding Light:** "It's a WORLD."

> **Neri:** "It's my room."

> **Guiding Light:** "You separated the space into floating islands!"

> **Neri:** "It's easier to move around."

Guiding Light paused.

> **"...You really don't realize how powerful you are, do you?"**

Neri returned to her chemistry book.

> **"No."**

---

# 12. CURRENT FUNCTION

Neri's subspace is currently treated as a legitimate extension of her personal living area.

It provides:

- sleeping space;
    
- reading space;
    
- laboratory space;
    
- equipment storage;
    
- personal storage;
    
- unrestricted three-dimensional movement;
    
- and substantial additional room without physical SAFEHOUSE expansion.
    

No instability has been observed.

Neri retains the ability to reverse the displacement and restore the original room.

---

## FINAL ASSESSMENT

Neri has effectively turned her bedroom into a small personal **reality fragment.**

From the hallway:

**Normal room.**

Inside:

**The room has broken open.**

Beyond the fracture:

**Floating islands.**

On those islands:

**Bed.**

**Bookshelf.**

**Chemistry lab.**

**Crossbow.**

**Knives.**

And somewhere in the middle of all of it, Neri is usually floating around with a chemistry book.

She still insists she would rather be normal.

The fact that "normal" now apparently includes owning a private subspace is not something Neri appears to have considered.
