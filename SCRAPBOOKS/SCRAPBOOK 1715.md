# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has learned to consciously **dull the sensory information transmitted between himself and his teddy bear.**

The sensory connection remains fully intact.

However, Jake can now reduce the intensity of sensations experienced through the bear.

This produces a corresponding change in the bear's apparent sensitivity.

When Jake increases attenuation:

> **Jake feels less of what happens to the bear.**

And simultaneously:

> **The bear becomes less sensitive to external contact.**

The effect appears to be controlled entirely by Jake.

---

# 1. DISCOVERY

Jake discovered the ability while experimenting with the sensory connection.

He noticed that concentrating on the connection caused sensations from the bear to become quieter.

A gentle pat that would normally produce a clear sensation became barely noticeable.

Jake continued experimenting.

Eventually, he discovered that he could reduce the connection almost completely.

He immediately recognized the practical implication.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I can turn it down."

---

# 2. SENSORY ATTENUATION

The effect appears to function similarly to an adjustable sensitivity level.

At normal sensitivity:

**Bear touched → Jake clearly feels it.**

At reduced sensitivity:

**Bear touched → Jake feels a weaker sensation.**

At high attenuation:

**Bear touched → Jake barely registers it.**

The bear itself appears to mirror this change.

Its anomalous sensory responsiveness becomes weaker as Jake reduces the connection.

---

# 3. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Jake is not making the bear physically tougher.

He is not increasing its durability.

He is not creating a protective field.

He is simply reducing the **sensory coupling** between himself and the bear.

Therefore:

> **The bear can still be physically damaged.**

Jake simply does not experience the full sensory intensity of that damage while attenuation is active.

---

# 4. CLOVER'S RESPONSE

Clover was initially extremely interested.

She carefully touched the bear.

Jake barely reacted.

She touched it again.

Nothing.

> **CLOVER:**

> **JAKE:**  
> "A lot."

Clover looked relieved.

Then she picked up the bear normally.

No visible problem occurred.

For the first time since the previous incident, she was able to handle the bear without immediately worrying that Jake would experience every small movement.

# 7. NEKO'S ANALYSIS

Neko considers the discovery significant.

The previous model suggested that the sensory connection was largely automatic.

That model is now obsolete.

Jake possesses at least partial conscious control over the connection.

Neko currently believes the system may have three functional states:

### FULL SENSITIVITY

The bear and Jake share physical sensation with minimal attenuation.

### REDUCED SENSITIVITY

Sensory information is transmitted at diminished intensity.

### NEAR-SILENT

The connection remains present but produces little meaningful sensation.

Neko has deliberately avoided describing the final state as "off."

There is insufficient evidence that the connection can actually be severed.

---

# 8. CLOVER'S RECOVERY

The discovery has also helped Clover.

The previous accident left her deeply afraid of accidentally hurting Jake through the bear.

Knowing that Jake can now control the intensity of the connection has reduced some of that anxiety.

She can hold the bear.

Move it.

Carry it.

Set it down.

Without immediately worrying that every movement will hurt Jake.

Jake noticed the difference.

> **JAKE:**  
> "You're less scared."

Clover looked at him.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "A little."

Jake smiled.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Good."

---

# 9. LIMITATIONS

Jake's ability does not eliminate the need for caution.

The following remain prohibited:

- deliberately throwing the bear;
    
- intentionally striking the bear;
    
- exposing it to dangerous environments;
    
- testing extreme impacts;
    
- assuming attenuation prevents injury;
    
- treating the bear as disposable.
    

Jake's ability reduces **sensation**.

It does not necessarily prevent physical consequences.

This distinction remains critical.

---

# 10. THE UNEXPECTED BENEFIT

Jake has also discovered that reduced sensitivity makes ordinary activities easier.

If the bear is sitting on a rough surface:

He can reduce the sensation.

If the bear gets wet:

He can reduce the sensation.

If someone hugs the bear:

He can choose how strongly he experiences the contact.

And if Jake simply wants to carry his teddy bear without constantly being aware of every movement it makes—

He can.

This appears to be the most useful application so far.

For perhaps the first time since the connection appeared, Jake wasn't simply **enduring** the sensations coming from his teddy bear.

He was choosing how much of them he wanted to feel.

And that made all the difference.
