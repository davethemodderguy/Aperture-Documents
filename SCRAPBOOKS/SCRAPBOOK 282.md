# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – PERSONNEL OBSERVATION LOG**

### **SUBJECT: LUMI – CONCEALED PHYSICAL CHARACTERISTIC**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Personnel have noted that Lumi consistently wears a small hair clip positioned near the front of her bangs.

At first glance, the accessory appears to be purely decorative.

It is not.

The hair clip conceals several thin strands of hair that differ noticeably from the rest of her appearance.

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Hidden beneath the clip are a few narrow strands of **icy light-blue hair**.

The remainder of Lumi's hair retains its normal coloration.

The contrasting strands begin near the hairline and extend forward into her bangs.

Without the clip, they are immediately noticeable.

With it, they remain almost completely hidden.

Most personnel never realize they are there.

---

# **DISCOVERY**

The detail was first noticed accidentally when a gust of wind displaced Lumi's bangs during an afternoon in the SAFEHOUSE courtyard.

Several nearby residents briefly saw the blue strands before Lumi calmly adjusted her hair clip back into place.

She made no attempt to explain them.

Nor did anyone press the matter.

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

When Corin eventually asked about the hair clip in private, Lumi gently touched it.

After a moment, she answered.

> "...Everyone has something they keep close."

She smiled warmly.

> "...Mine just happens to be visible if I'm not careful."

She offered no further explanation.

Corin accepted the answer.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Lumi continues to wear the same hair clip each day.

Whether it serves as a keepsake, a reminder, or simply a preference remains known only to her.

Among those closest to her, however, the hidden blue strands have long since stopped being a mystery.

They are simply another small part of Lumi—

Seen only by the people she trusts enough not to ask.
