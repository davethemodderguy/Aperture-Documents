# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE PERSONNEL / BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION LOG**

# **CORIN IN A JAR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Form:** Miniature White Cat  
**Duration:** **5+ Hours**  
**Result:** **No Injuries / No Difficulties / No Explanation**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

At approximately ██:██, SAFEHOUSE personnel discovered Corin inside a glass jar.

The jar was sealed.

Corin had apparently placed herself inside it.

She then remained there for **over five hours**.

No distress was observed.

No physical complications occurred.

No attempt to escape was made.

There was no emergency.

There was no experiment.

There was no known reason.

It was simply:

> **Corin in a jar.**

---

# **DISCOVERY**

Belle was the first person to notice.

She walked into the room.

Stopped.

Looked at the table.

There was Corin.

Inside a glass jar.

Curled into a tiny loaf.

Belle stared for several seconds.

> **"...Corin?"**

Corin opened one eye.

> **"Mew."**

Belle:

> **"...Why are you in there?"**

Corin closed her eye again.

> **"Mrrp."**

No additional explanation was provided.

---

# **CONTAINMENT STATUS**

The jar was examined immediately.

The glass was intact.

The lid was properly sealed.

No pressure buildup was detected.

No oxygen deprivation occurred.

Corin showed no signs of discomfort.

Her miniature form's anomalous physiology apparently allowed her to remain inside the sealed container without difficulty.

C.O.R.E. therefore determined that the situation was not medically dangerous.

It was merely...

**Extremely strange.**

---

# **FIVE-HOUR OBSERVATION**

Personnel periodically checked on Corin.

### **Hour 1**

Corin was loafing.

### **Hour 2**

Corin was still loafing.

### **Hour 3**

Corin was sleeping.

### **Hour 4**

Corin woke up, stretched, and returned to loafing.

### **Hour 5**

Corin remained inside the jar.

At no point did she attempt to leave.

---

# **ATTEMPTED INTERACTION**

Neko eventually approached the jar.

> **"Do you want out?"**

Corin looked at him.

> **"Mew."**

Neko:

> **"...Is that yes or no?"**

Corin curled tighter.

Neko decided not to interfere.

---

# **ORPHIE'S REACTION**

Orphie eventually found Corin.

She stared at the jar.

Then at Corin.

Then back at the jar.

> **"...Why?"**

Corin's ears twitched.

No answer.

Orphie gently tapped the glass.

Corin responded with a tiny:

> **"Mrrp."**

Orphie left her there.

---

# **EXIT**

After approximately five hours, Corin finally decided to leave.

She simply pushed against the lid.

The jar opened.

Corin climbed out.

Stretched.

Walked three steps.

Then sat down.

No explanation was ever given.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

C.O.R.E. has determined that Corin's behavior was:

- Voluntary.
    
- Non-hostile.
    
- Non-harmful.
    
- Entirely unnecessary.
    
- Completely consistent with Corin.
    

No further investigation is currently considered necessary.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin possesses:

- Functional wings.
    
- A highly mobile miniature form.
    
- Dimensional transportation access.
    
- Multifunctional anomalous feathers.
    
- The ability to fly.
    

She nevertheless chose to spend more than five hours sealed inside a glass jar.

Nobody knows why.

Corin has not explained herself.

The official record therefore contains only one appropriate conclusion:

> ### **CORIN IN A JAR.**

**End of SAFEHOUSE Personnel / Behavioral Observation Log**
