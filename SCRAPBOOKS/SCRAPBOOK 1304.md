## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### RESIDENT WELFARE & MEDICAL DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-JAKE-151  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESTRICTED — RESIDENT PROTECTION  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE INVESTIGATION  
**SUBJECT:** Jake — MSAC Personnel Misconduct / Sorrow Escalation  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE / SECTOR 4-C

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A group of MSAC Operators subjected Jake to sustained bullying.

The incident was initially categorized as verbal harassment.

This classification was incorrect.

Jake is not psychologically resilient to prolonged interpersonal cruelty.

His condition is fundamentally different from that of an ordinary child.

He is a **Weeping Angel.**

His sorrow is not temporary.

His connection to the Starlands has already been consumed by it.

There is no documented instance in Starlands history of a Weeping Angel returning to its previous state.

Accordingly, deliberately increasing Jake's emotional distress does not constitute harmless teasing.

It constitutes a direct aggravation of an existing permanent anomalous condition.

The effects were therefore not unpredictable.

They were **consistent with Jake's established response to abandonment, rejection, and sustained sorrow.**

The unexpected part was how quickly the deterioration became physical.

---

# 1. THE INCIDENT

The involved MSAC Operators encountered Jake in a SAFEHOUSE corridor.

Jake had been wandering.

He was carrying his teddy bear across his chest.

The bear's arms were wrapped around his shoulders.

His azure halo was already present as two separate floating fragments.

Jake was not bothering anyone.

He was simply walking.

The Operators began mocking him.

Comments included references to:

- his appearance;
    
- his lack of wings;
    
- his ghostly nature;
    
- his age;
    
- his inability to return to the Starlands;
    
- his mother;
    
- and his tendency to hide.
    

One Operator reportedly told him:

> **"Nobody's coming back for you."**

Another said:

> **"You're just a ghost. What are you even doing here?"**

Jake did not respond.

He attempted to leave.

The Operators continued.

---

# 2. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake initially behaved as he commonly does when distressed.

He became quiet.

He lowered his head.

He stopped making eye contact.

His teddy bear tightened around his shoulders.

The heartbeat inside the bear became noticeably slower.

One Operator interpreted the silence as an indication that Jake was unaffected.

This was incorrect.

Jake eventually said:

> **"Please stop."**

The Operators continued.

Jake repeated:

> **"Please."**

He then attempted to walk away.

One Operator stepped into his path.

Jake stopped.

His halo fragments began drifting farther apart.

---

# 3. THE CHANGE

At approximately this point, Jake began crying.

Not loudly.

There was no screaming.

No attack.

No anomalous outburst.

He simply stood there.

Tears formed.

His hands tightened around the teddy bear.

Then Jake said:

> **"I don't want to be alone."**

The Operators reportedly laughed.

One replied:

> **"Then stop being so weird."**

Jake's heartbeat changed.

The bear began beating rapidly.

Then irregularly.

Then—

**painfully slowly.**

Jake suddenly dropped to his knees.

---

# 4. PAIN

This was the first recorded instance in which Jake's emotional sorrow produced a clearly observable **pain response**.

Jake clutched his chest.

There was no conventional heart there.

The pain was originating from the curse binding his heart to the teddy bear.

He gasped.

> **"It hurts."**

The bear's heartbeat became extremely irregular.

Jake curled around it.

> **"It hurts..."**

The Operators finally stopped laughing.

One attempted to approach him.

Jake recoiled.

> **"Don't."**

His voice was barely audible.

---

# 5. ANOMALOUS ESCALATION

C.O.R.E. monitoring detected a significant increase in anomalous instability.

Jake's manifestation began flickering.

His outline intermittently disappeared.

His cloak became partially transparent.

The two pieces of his halo moved farther apart.

Most concerningly, the pain did not appear localized.

Jake's sorrow and physical distress were becoming indistinguishable.

The teddy bear was beating rapidly enough to be visibly moving against his chest.

Jake repeatedly whispered:

> **"I'm sorry."**

Personnel arriving at the scene initially assumed he was apologizing for causing an incident.

He was not.

He was apologizing for **existing loudly enough to require attention.**

---

# 6. THE BEAR

The teddy bear became the clearest indicator of the deterioration.

Its arms tightened around Jake.

Its body pressed against him.

Its heartbeat became increasingly unstable.

C.O.R.E. medical personnel later described the sound as:

> **"A heartbeat struggling to maintain a rhythm."**

Jake continued holding it.

He would not let go.

When a medical responder asked whether the bear was hurting him, Jake shook his head.

Then quietly said:

> **"It's me."**

The distinction was important.

The bear was not hurting Jake.

**Jake was hurting through the bear.**

His heart was there.

His sorrow was there.

And now his pain was there as well.

---

# 7. NORA ARRIVES

Nora arrived shortly afterward.

She immediately recognized Jake's condition.

She did not ask what happened.

She sat down beside him.

Jake looked at her.

His voice was almost inaudible.

> **"Nora..."**

Nora moved closer.

Jake leaned against her.

The bear continued beating irregularly.

Nora placed a hand against its back.

> **NORA:**  
> "I'm here."

Jake began crying harder.

Not because the situation had become worse.

Because somebody had stayed.

---

# 8. AETERNA

Aeterna arrived several minutes later.

She surveyed the scene.

Then looked at the Operators.

She did not draw her sword.

She did not need to.

Her voice was sufficient.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Leave."

The Operators complied.

Aeterna then knelt beside Jake.

She did not touch him immediately.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "May I?"

Jake nodded.

Aeterna placed one armored hand beside him.

Jake leaned against it.

His crying gradually became quieter.

The pain did not disappear.

But the heartbeat inside the bear became steadier.

---

# 9. MEDICAL OBSERVATION

Jake's physical manifestation stabilized after approximately thirty-seven minutes.

However, emotional distress remained elevated for several hours.

Observed symptoms included:

- persistent crying;
    
- chest pain localized to the teddy bear;
    
- irregular cardiac manifestation;
    
- manifestation flickering;
    
- reduced speech;
    
- increased hiding behavior;
    
- reluctance to make eye contact;
    
- repeated apologies;
    
- heightened fear of being left alone;
    
- increased sensitivity to perceived rejection;
    
- and temporary refusal to eat despite no longer experiencing hunger.
    

The final observation is significant.

Jake had previously gained the ability to eat and no longer experiences physical hunger.

Nevertheless, emotional distress caused him to lose interest in food.

This demonstrates that the removal of starvation did not remove the psychological consequences associated with his history.

---

# 10. EFFECT ON HIS SORROW

The bullying did not create Jake's sorrow.

It **deepened it.**

His baseline emotional state was already severely compromised.

He is a Weeping Angel.

He cannot return to the Starlands.

He cannot restore his wings.

His mother's rejection has permanently wounded his understanding of himself.

He spent years waiting for someone who never returned.

He spent years near his own dead body.

He hates being alone but repeatedly isolates himself because he is afraid of becoming a burden.

The Operators did not need to invent a new wound.

They found the existing ones.

And pressed on them.

---

# 11. C.O.R.E. FINDING

The incident produced an effect that should have been entirely foreseeable.

Jake's sorrow intensified.

His fear of abandonment intensified.

His physical pain intensified.

His anomalous stability deteriorated.

His trust in unfamiliar personnel decreased.

His willingness to ask for help decreased.

His tendency to hide increased.

The only genuinely unpredictable element was the exact severity of the physical manifestation.

The direction of the response was not unpredictable.

---

# 12. JAKE'S STATEMENT

Jake was later asked whether he wanted the Operators punished.

He shook his head.

After a long pause:

> **"I just don't want them to do it again."**

The welfare officer asked:

> **"Are you angry with them?"**

Jake shook his head.

> **"No."**

Pause.

> **"I'm sad."**

Another pause.

Jake held the teddy bear closer.

> **"It hurts when people don't want me."**

No further statement was made.

---

# 13. MSAC RESPONSE

The involved Operators have been removed from SAFEHOUSE access pending disciplinary review.

All MSAC personnel assigned to SAFEHOUSE operations will receive updated resident-welfare instructions.

The following is now explicitly prohibited:

- mocking Jake's condition;
    
- mocking his inability to return to the Starlands;
    
- referencing his mother as a means of provoking him;
    
- intentionally frightening him;
    
- intentionally isolating him;
    
- treating his silence as consent;
    
- or deliberately escalating his emotional distress.
    

The instruction is not merely:

> **"Be nice to Jake."**

It is:

> **"Do not deliberately aggravate a permanently sorrow-bound anomalous resident."**

---

# 14. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Jake is physically no longer hungry.

He can eat.

He can touch objects.

He can walk through the SAFEHOUSE during the day.

He can hold people.

He can carry his teddy bear.

None of those developments changed the fundamental nature of his condition.

He remains a Weeping Angel.

He remains deeply traumatized.

He remains permanently separated from the Starlands.

And now C.O.R.E. has documented another consequence of his condition:

**Sorrow can become pain.**

Not metaphorically.

Not psychologically alone.

The curse itself appears capable of translating prolonged emotional distress into physical suffering through the heart contained within Jake's teddy bear.

This makes deliberate emotional abuse a direct welfare and medical concern.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The Operators apparently believed they were bullying a ghost.

They were not.

They were bullying a six-year-old child who already believed that nobody would come back for him.

A child who spent decades waiting.

A child who lost his wings.

A child whose heart is literally carried outside his body.

A child who finally stopped being hungry—

and still could not stop being sad.

Jake did not retaliate.

He did not attack them.

He did not even hate them.

He simply curled around his teddy bear and whispered:

> **"It hurts."**

And for the first time since his arrival at SAFEHOUSE,

everyone could hear exactly what his sorrow sounded like.

A heartbeat.

Inside a teddy bear.

Trying very hard to keep going.
