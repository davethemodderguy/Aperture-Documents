---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake Akana has been recovered.

Following the extinguishment of the blue flame on his head by a newly assigned employee, Jake disappeared from the Blacksite without leaving a detectable physical or spectral trace.

His older brother, Aries Akana, experienced severe distress during the subsequent search.

At 16:38, Bao discovered an unusual blue candle in an unoccupied area of SAFEHOUSE.

The candle possessed a distinct blueberry scent.

Its appearance, anomalous properties, and relationship to Jake were initially unknown.

Upon lighting the candle, it disappeared.

Jake returned.

Subsequent observation established a previously undocumented anomalous cycle:

**When the flame on Jake's head is extinguished, Jake disappears and an unusual blue, blueberry-scented candle manifests somewhere within the facility. Lighting that candle causes it to disappear and returns Jake.**

Jake has confirmed that the interval between disappearance and recovery is not painless.

He described the experience as:

> **"Like getting stabbed in the head and then knocked unconscious."**

The recovery mechanism is therefore functional but not harmless.

C.O.R.E. considers the discovery a significant breakthrough.

It also considers the circumstances surrounding its discovery a serious welfare concern.

Jake has returned.

Aries has been reunited with his brother.

Neither fact diminishes the need to investigate why Jake's continued existence appears to depend upon a recurring cycle of extinguishment, disappearance, and painful recovery.

---

# 1. INITIAL DISCOVERY

At approximately 16:38, Bao was searching an unoccupied section of SAFEHOUSE while personnel continued investigating Jake's disappearance.

She noticed an unfamiliar object resting on a low surface near the wall.

It was a candle.

The candle was blue.

Its wax appeared ordinary at first glance, although preliminary examination later established that its coloration was unusually consistent throughout the material.

Bao reported a strong blueberry scent.

No label was present.

No manufacturer information was visible.

No container or packaging accompanied the object.

No personnel had reported placing it there.

The candle had not been present in the last documented inspection of the area.

Bao initially assumed it was an abandoned item belonging to another resident.

She picked it up.

The blueberry scent remained noticeable.

She then observed that the wick was intact.

At this point, Bao contacted the welfare team.

She did not immediately light it.

---

# 2. THE DECISION TO IGNITE

The candle was brought under observation.

Given the recent disappearance of Jake, personnel considered the possibility that it was anomalous.

However, no available evidence established its purpose.

Bao was unwilling to leave an unidentified object unattended, but she was equally unwilling to experiment with it without a reason.

During the discussion, one researcher noted the apparent coincidence between the candle's appearance and the circumstances of Jake's disappearance.

The blue coloration was consistent with Jake's former flame.

The timing was consistent with the ongoing incident.

The blueberry scent, however, had no immediately established connection to his known characteristics.

The hypothesis remained speculative.

Bao examined the candle once more.

Then she made a decision.

**BAO:**  
"If this has something to do with Jake, we need to find out."

**RESEARCHER:**  
"And if it doesn't?"

**BAO:**  
"Then we stop and investigate it properly."

The candle was placed on a nonflammable surface.

A controlled ignition was attempted.

The wick caught.

A small blue flame appeared.

The candle disappeared.

Not melted.

Not consumed.

Not displaced through any observable physical process.

It simply ceased to be present.

At the same moment, a spectral manifestation appeared nearby.

White hair.

A partially melted head.

No visible eyes.

No legs.

A small blue flame burning from the wick protruding from his head.

A spectral teddy bear.

Jake Akana had returned.

---

# 3. RECOVERY OF JAKE AKANA

Personnel immediately halted the ignition procedure.

No additional action was taken.

Jake remained suspended a short distance above the floor.

He appeared disoriented.

His posture was unusually still.

For several seconds, he did not respond to verbal attempts to establish contact.

Bao approached slowly.

**BAO:**  
"Jake?"

No response.

**BAO:**  
"Little Candle?"

Jake's head moved slightly.

His blue flame flickered.

He turned toward her voice.

**JAKE:**  
"...Mom?"

Bao stopped.

The word was quiet.

Almost uncertain.

She moved closer.

**BAO:**  
"I'm here, sweetheart."

Jake stared in her direction despite having no visible eyes.

**JAKE:**  
"What happened?"

**BAO:**  
"We found you."

A pause.

**JAKE:**  
"...Found me?"

Bao's expression changed.

She had expected relief.

She had expected confusion.

She had not expected him to sound as though he did not understand why he had been missing in the first place.

**BAO:**  
"Yes. You're back."

Jake looked down at himself.

Then toward the candle's former location.

**JAKE:**  
"Oh."

He was quiet for several seconds.

**JAKE:**  
"...Okay."

His flame continued burning.

Jake was present.

Jake was responsive.

Jake had returned.

---

# 4. REUNIFICATION WITH ARIES AKANA

Aries was informed immediately.

He arrived before the initial examination had been completed.

The moment he saw Jake, he stopped in the doorway.

Jake turned toward him.

Neither spoke.

Aries appeared unable to reconcile what he was seeing with what he had been told.

Jake was standing—or, more accurately, hovering—exactly where he should not have been able to be.

His head was still partially melted.

His legs were still absent.

His spectral teddy bear was still with him.

And the little blue flame was burning again.

**JAKE:**  
"...Aries?"

Aries crossed the room.

He stopped just in front of his brother.

**ARIES:**  
"Jake?"

Jake tilted his head.

**JAKE:**  
"Yeah."

Aries reached toward him, hesitated for an instant, and then embraced him.

The contact was unusual, as it always was when someone hugged Jake's spectral form.

Aries did not seem to care.

He held on.

**ARIES:**  
"Oh, thank God."

Jake remained still.

**ARIES:**  
"I thought—I thought you were—"

He could not finish the sentence.

Jake's flame flickered softly.

**JAKE:**  
"I'm here."

Aries tightened his arms around him.

**ARIES:**  
"Don't do that again."

Jake paused.

**JAKE:**  
"...I didn't."

Aries began crying.

Jake did not understand the full extent of what his disappearance had done to his brother.

He only knew that Aries was holding him very tightly.

So he stayed.

For once, Aries did not ask him to stop following.

And Jake did not have to ask whether he could remain nearby.

---

# 5. POST-RECOVERY INTERVIEW

The initial welfare assessment was conducted only after Jake had been reunited with Aries and had sufficient time to orient himself.

Personnel were instructed not to crowd him.

The objective was to establish what he remembered without subjecting him to unnecessary distress.

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Jake, do you remember what happened before you disappeared?"

**JAKE:**  
"Someone sprayed me."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"With the fire extinguisher?"

Jake nodded.

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"What happened after that?"

Jake became quiet.

His flame remained steady.

**JAKE:**  
"I don't know."

A pause.

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Do you remember anything at all?"

**JAKE:**  
"It hurt."

The interviewer stopped writing.

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Where did it hurt?"

Jake raised a hand toward the side of his head.

**JAKE:**  
"Here."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Can you describe the feeling?"

Jake considered the question.

He did not appear to be searching for a technical explanation.

He was searching for words that made sense to him.

**JAKE:**  
"Like getting stabbed in the head."

The room became quiet.

Jake continued.

**JAKE:**  
"And then I got knocked unconscious."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"You lost consciousness?"

**JAKE:**  
"Yeah."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Do you remember anything after that?"

Jake shook his head.

**JAKE:**  
"No."

A pause.

**JAKE:**  
"Then I was here."

He looked toward Aries.

**JAKE:**  
"With you."

Aries lowered his head.

The interview was suspended.

No further questions were asked at that time.

---

# 6. ANOMALOUS CYCLE — PRELIMINARY MODEL

The combined observations support the following provisional sequence.

<escape> NORMAL MANIFESTATION | v BLUE FLAME EXTINGUISHED | v JAKE DISAPPEARS | v BLUE CANDLE MANIFESTS | v CANDLE IGNITED | v CANDLE DISAPPEARS | v JAKE RETURNS | v NORMAL MANIFESTATION </escape>

The mechanism remains incompletely understood.

However, the relationship between the flame, the candle, and Jake is now considered sufficiently strong to warrant formal anomalous classification.

### Established observations

- Jake disappeared immediately following extinguishment of his head flame.
    
- A blue, blueberry-scented candle subsequently appeared.
    
- Lighting the candle caused it to disappear.
    
- Jake returned when the candle was lit.
    
- Jake reported a painful sensation associated with the disappearance and recovery interval.
    
- Jake described the sensation as resembling a stabbing injury to the head, followed by unconsciousness.
    

### Unconfirmed assumptions

C.O.R.E. has not established whether the candle is generated by Jake, generated by an external anomalous mechanism, or produced by an unknown process linked to his manifestation.

It is also unknown whether the candle always appears in SAFEHOUSE, whether its location is predictable, or whether its ignition is the only possible means of recovery.

The mechanism must not be assumed to be fully understood simply because one successful recovery has occurred.

---

# 7. THE BLUEBERRY SCENT

The candle's scent is an unusual feature.

It smells distinctly of blueberries.

No explanation has yet been established for this property.

The scent does not appear necessary for ignition.

It may be incidental.

It may be part of the candle's anomalous signature.

It may serve as an identifiable characteristic that allows personnel to distinguish it from ordinary candles.

For now, the scent is to be documented as an observed property rather than assigned an unsupported explanation.

Personnel are instructed not to consume, ingest, or otherwise misuse any substance associated with the candle.

Its apparent familiarity does not establish that it is harmless.

---

# 8. MEDICAL AND WELFARE CONCERNS

Jake's return is not evidence that the recovery process is painless.

His description suggests a severe subjective experience immediately before the disappearance or during the interval preceding his return.

The precise sequence cannot yet be determined.

It is also unknown whether Jake's reported unconsciousness corresponds to a conventional loss of consciousness or an anomalous interruption of awareness.

Personnel must not dismiss the report because Jake is a ghost.

His ability to experience pain has already been demonstrated.

His current condition does not invalidate his account.

Further questioning should occur only when necessary and when Jake is willing to participate.

He is not to be subjected to repeated ignition and extinguishment trials merely to reproduce the experience.

**The ability to recover a resident does not constitute permission to endanger that resident.**

---

# 9. NEW SAFETY DIRECTIVES

Effective immediately:

1. Jake's head flame is not to be extinguished intentionally.
    
2. Fire extinguishers must never be discharged at Jake solely because of his appearance.
    
3. The blue candle is to be treated as an anomalous recovery object.
    
4. Any suspected manifestation of the candle must be reported to C.O.R.E. and Resident Welfare.
    
5. If Jake disappears and a matching candle is discovered, personnel must notify the designated response team.
    
6. Any ignition must occur under controlled conditions, with the objective of recovery rather than experimentation.
    
7. Jake must be given time to orient himself after returning.
    
8. Aries must be informed promptly of confirmed recovery, subject to Jake's immediate welfare needs.
    
9. No attempt may be made to deliberately trigger another disappearance without formal review and compelling justification.
    
10. The original incident involving the unauthorized use of a fire extinguisher remains under investigation.
    

These instructions do not authorize personnel to treat the candle as a routine reset mechanism.

Jake is a resident, not a system that may be repeatedly shut down and restarted.

---

# 10. ARIES AKANA — FOLLOW-UP OBSERVATION

Approximately twenty minutes after recovery, Aries remained beside Jake.

He had stopped crying, although his hands continued to tremble occasionally.

Jake hovered beside him.

For several minutes, neither spoke.

Eventually, Jake turned toward his brother.

**JAKE:**  
"Are you okay?"

Aries looked at him.

The question appeared to make the situation worse rather than better.

**ARIES:**  
"Am I—"

He stopped.

He looked away, trying to compose himself.

**ARIES:**  
"Jake, you disappeared."

Jake nodded slightly.

**JAKE:**  
"I know."

**ARIES:**  
"I thought I lost you."

Jake was silent.

Aries looked at him again.

**ARIES:**  
"I can't do that again."

Jake's flame flickered.

**JAKE:**  
"...Sorry."

Aries immediately shook his head.

**ARIES:**  
"No. No, don't apologize."

He leaned closer.

**ARIES:**  
"You didn't do anything wrong."

Jake did not answer.

Aries gently rested his forehead against Jake's spectral head, taking care not to disturb the wick.

**ARIES:**  
"Someone hurt you. You didn't choose to disappear."

A pause.

**ARIES:**  
"It's not your fault."

Jake remained quiet.

Then, softly:

**JAKE:**  
"Okay."

Aries stayed beside him.

He did not demand that Jake promise never to disappear again.

Neither of them knew whether Jake could make such a promise.

Instead, Aries held his brother for a little longer.

---

# 11. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The discovery of the blue candle provides a potential means of recovering Jake following extinguishment of his anomalous flame.

This is a significant development.

It is not a resolution of the underlying problem.

The candle appears to restore Jake's manifestation, but the process is associated with a reported experience of severe pain and unconsciousness.

Its origin remains unknown.

Its availability cannot yet be guaranteed.

Its behavior has not been independently reproduced.

Consequently, C.O.R.E. must treat the candle as an emergency recovery mechanism rather than a dependable substitute for protecting Jake in the first place.

The appropriate priority remains prevention.

Jake's flame should not be extinguished.

Jake should not be subjected to unnecessary anomalous disruption.

And Jake should not have to experience the recovery process again simply because personnel failed to recognize him.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Bao found a blue candle.

It smelled like blueberries.

There was nothing obviously extraordinary about it until she lit it.

Then it disappeared.

And Jake came back.

For a few seconds, nobody knew whether the candle had worked.

Then a small voice spoke.

"Mom?"

Bao heard him.

Aries heard him.

And for the first time since the incident, the Blacksite had an answer to the question that had been tearing Aries apart.

Jake was here.

He was frightened.

He was confused.

And, according to his own account, the process of returning had hurt.

But he was here.

Aries held him as though the world might take him away again.

Jake stayed close.

Neither needed to explain anything else.

The candle has been documented.

The mechanism is under investigation.

The employee's actions remain subject to review.

But the most important result is not the discovery of a new anomalous object.

It is not the recovery of a spectral manifestation.

It is not the confirmation that a blue flame can be restored through an unidentified process.

It is this:

**Aries asked for his brother to come back.**

And his brother came back.

C.O.R.E. will continue investigating the candle.

It will not, however, confuse the existence of a recovery mechanism with permission to let this happen again.

Jake should not have to disappear for someone to learn how to bring him home.

**END OF REPORT**
