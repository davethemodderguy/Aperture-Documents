# 1. ORGANIZATIONAL IDENTIFICATION

**T.O.P.S.** is the abbreviated designation for:

> **THE ORDER OF PERPETUAL SALVATION**

The organization operates within the Banlands and is considered one of the most heavily monitored military assets under the jurisdiction of the **Krampus Compliance Authority (KCA).**

T.O.P.S. is **not classified as an inherently hostile organization.**

Its personnel are not generally regarded as malicious.

Its stated objectives are, in many cases, intended to improve the condition of existence within the Banlands.

The concern is primarily **how T.O.P.S. defines improvement.**

---

# 2. KCA CLASSIFICATION

KCA maintains continuous oversight of T.O.P.S. operations.

This is not because T.O.P.S. is considered evil.

It is because T.O.P.S. possesses:

- significant military capability;
    
- advanced Ether research;
    
- extensive knowledge of Banlands phenomena;
    
- experimental methods capable of altering biological existence;
    
- research concerning immortality;
    
- research concerning post-mortem states;
    
- research concerning the reconstruction of life;
    
- and ideological concepts concerning the creation of a fundamentally different world.
    

The organization therefore occupies an unusual position.

> **T.O.P.S. is trusted enough to operate.**

But:

> **T.O.P.S. is monitored closely enough that it cannot operate without oversight.**

---

# 3. THE T.O.P.S. WORLDVIEW

T.O.P.S. has historically pursued several broad philosophical objectives.

Among them:

> **A world without death.**

> **A world without suffering.**

> **A world where existence can be reconstructed.**

> **A world where the limitations imposed by biology and mortality no longer apply.**

These objectives may sound benign when stated individually.

The complications begin when T.O.P.S. attempts to determine:

> **What should replace the existing system?**

T.O.P.S. researchers do not necessarily view death, individuality, pain, identity, or biological limitation in the same manner as ordinary Banlands residents.

Their concepts of:

**life,**

**death,**

**immortality,**

**salvation,**

and even:

**freedom**

can therefore differ substantially from conventional interpretations.

---

# 4. MORALITY

C.O.R.E. has found no sufficient basis to classify T.O.P.S. as malicious.

Their personnel can be compassionate.

Their researchers can genuinely want to save people.

Their medical programs have produced legitimate breakthroughs.

Their work concerning Ether has produced results that other organizations previously considered impossible.

The problem is that T.O.P.S. possesses an unusual willingness to cross conceptual boundaries.

Their reasoning can occasionally resemble:

> **“If this prevents death, then it is justified.”**

Even when the method itself would be considered unacceptable by most other organizations.

This creates a persistent ethical problem.

T.O.P.S. does not necessarily disregard morality.

It possesses **a different interpretation of where morality's boundaries should exist.**

---

# 5. TRANSCENDENSE PROGRAM

The most notable example is the T.O.P.S. research program concerning:

> **TRANSCENDENSE**

The program investigated whether Ether could be used to prevent permanent biological death.

The research succeeded.

The resulting process does not make a subject conventionally invulnerable.

Instead, a subject can experience fatal physiological failure and subsequently be restored through an Ether-driven process.

Phoenix Raffedella is one documented result.

Her body does not age normally.

She can die.

Her body can subsequently draw Ether around itself and force a revival.

The consequences of the original injury remain.

Pain is substantially reduced.

The process can be accelerated through appropriate medical intervention.

From a T.O.P.S. perspective:

> **Death has been made temporary.**

From the KCA perspective:

> **A person has been placed into a condition where death is no longer necessarily permanent, but neither is the experience of dying removed.**

The distinction is significant.

---

# 6. PHOENIX RAFFEDELLA

Phoenix is not considered a T.O.P.S. operative.

She is a KCA Medical Assistant.

However, her medical history is directly connected to T.O.P.S. research.

Phoenix previously volunteered for experiments involving Ether and Transcendense.

The experiments succeeded.

Their success resulted in the condition she currently possesses.

Following the experiments, Phoenix's medical knowledge and experience led to her reassignment within the KCA.

She now works as a Medical Assistant.

Her current position is therefore somewhat ironic.

The person who can explain T.O.P.S. medical experimentation in considerable detail is now employed by the organization responsible for monitoring it.

---

# 7. KCA OVERSIGHT

T.O.P.S. is subject to strict KCA monitoring.

Oversight includes:

- monitoring major military deployments;
    
- monitoring Ether experimentation;
    
- reviewing high-risk research;
    
- tracking anomalous weapons and technologies;
    
- reviewing experiments involving permanent alterations to living beings;
    
- monitoring dimensional activities;
    
- reviewing projects involving large-scale population changes;
    
- and maintaining authority to intervene when necessary.
    

The KCA does not automatically prohibit T.O.P.S. research.

The objective is to prevent experimentation from crossing established safety and ethical boundaries without authorization.

---

# 8. DIALYN / T.O.P.S. DIRECTOR RELATIONSHIP

An unusual factor significantly improves communication between T.O.P.S. and KCA.

The **Director of T.O.P.S. is Dialyn's best friend.**

This relationship is well known to KCA administration.

It does **not** exempt T.O.P.S. from oversight.

It does, however, provide an unusually reliable communication channel.

Dialyn can directly contact the Director when:

- clarification is required;
    
- an operation requires immediate discussion;
    
- a research concern arises;
    
- KCA monitoring identifies a potential problem;
    
- T.O.P.S. needs authorization;
    
- or the two organizations need to resolve a disagreement before it escalates.
    

The Director is considerably more willing to discuss controversial projects with Dialyn than with an unfamiliar KCA official.

Likewise, Dialyn possesses enough familiarity with the Director to recognize when:

> **“This is a legitimate scientific proposal.”**

and when:

> **“You are about to do something the KCA absolutely needs to know about.”**

This has proven valuable.

---

# 9. THE FRIENDSHIP DOES NOT REMOVE THE LEASH

KCA records explicitly state:

> **PERSONAL RELATIONSHIP ≠ REGULATORY EXEMPTION**

Dialyn's friendship with the T.O.P.S. Director does not provide immunity from KCA regulation.

If anything, it makes communication easier.

Dialyn can call the Director directly.

The Director can call Dialyn directly.

Arguments can occur.

Questions can be answered.

Warnings can be issued.

And, occasionally, Dialyn can simply tell her best friend:

> **“No.”**

The Director may complain.

The Director may attempt to explain the scientific reasoning.

Dialyn may continue saying:

> **“No.”**

The monitoring system remains functional.

---

# 10. KCA INTERNAL COMMENT

A senior KCA administrator summarized the relationship as:

> **“We're not watching T.O.P.S. because we think they're monsters.”**

> **“We're watching them because they genuinely believe they're building something better.”**

> **“Those are exactly the people who need someone checking where the road goes.”**

---

# 11. APERTURE COMPARISON

Aperture Industries and T.O.P.S. are sometimes compared because both possess advanced research capabilities and operate with unusually broad technological freedom.

The comparison is incomplete.

Aperture's primary concern is generally:

> **“Can this be done safely?”**

T.O.P.S. is more likely to ask:

> **“What becomes possible if we stop accepting the limitations?”**

Neither question is inherently malicious.

They simply produce very different research philosophies.

---

# 12. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

T.O.P.S. should not be treated as an enemy organization.

They should also not be treated as an ordinary research institution.

Their military capabilities, Ether research, philosophical objectives, and willingness to redefine fundamental concepts of existence place them in a unique category.

The KCA's current position is therefore:

> **COOPERATIVE — HIGH OVERSIGHT**

T.O.P.S. is permitted to exist.

T.O.P.S. is permitted to research.

T.O.P.S. is permitted to pursue its objectives within established boundaries.

And T.O.P.S. is monitored closely enough that the KCA will know when those boundaries are being approached.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

T.O.P.S. does not wake up every morning thinking:

> **“How can we ruin everyone's lives today?”**

They genuinely believe they can build something better.

A world without death.

A world without suffering.

A world beyond the limitations of ordinary existence.

The problem is that they are willing to ask questions that most people are uncomfortable asking.

And sometimes they are willing to follow those questions farther than most people would.

Fortunately, the person responsible for keeping an eye on them happens to be best friends with the person running them.

That does not make T.O.P.S. exempt.

It simply means that when T.O.P.S. starts doing something questionable, Dialyn does not need to send a formal letter.

She can call her best friend.

And say:

> **“Explain.”**
