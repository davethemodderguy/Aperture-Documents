### **RESTRICTED SAFEHOUSE / EXTREME WEATHER INCIDENT**

# **THE DAY ORPHIE DIDN'T COME HOME**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Architect-Sealed  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Subjects:** Corin / Orphie  
**Incident:** Severe Snowstorm  
**Status:** **COMMUNICATIONS LOST / ORPHIE DELAYED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie frequently travels between the treehouse and the Blacksite.

The trips are usually routine.

Sometimes she returns with:

- Medical supplies.
    
- Food.
    
- Stitching materials.
    
- Clothing.
    
- Household necessities.
    
- Books.
    
- Small gifts.
    
- Letters.
    
- Packages from Blacksite personnel wishing Corin well.
    

The trips have become part of their routine.

Orphie leaves.

Corin waits.

Orphie comes home.

Until today.

---

# **THE DEPARTURE**

Orphie left the treehouse in the morning.

Before leaving, she combed Corin's chalky lime hair and adjusted the hairclip Curious Light had given her.

She prepared Corin's food.

Checked her stitches.

Made sure everything she needed was within reach.

> **Orphie:**  
> "I'll be back later."

Corin nodded.

> **"Bring me something?"**

Orphie smiled.

> **"Maybe."**

> **"You always say maybe."**

> **"And I always bring something."**

Corin smiled faintly.

Orphie left.

---

# **THE STORM**

Several hours later, weather conditions around the Blacksite deteriorated rapidly.

Snowfall intensified beyond normal winter conditions.

Visibility dropped to almost nothing.

Wind speeds increased.

Road access became impossible.

Communications between the Blacksite and the remote perimeter were interrupted.

The forest became effectively impassable.

The treehouse was isolated.

The Blacksite was isolated from the treehouse.

And Orphie was somewhere between them.

---

# **CONTACT LOST**

Corin waited.

One hour.

Then two.

Then three.

She checked the window repeatedly.

Nothing.

No footsteps.

No familiar silhouette through the snow.

No knock on the door.

No voice.

She eventually checked the communications terminal.

**NO CONNECTION.**

She tried again.

Nothing.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

Corin opened her notebook.

The first entry was short.

> **Orphie is late.**

A second appeared later.

> **Snowstorm.**

Then:

> **She can't get back.**

Corin stared at the sentence.

She knew the explanation.

She understood what had happened.

That should have made the waiting easier.

It didn't.

---

# **HOUR FOUR**

The snow continued.

Corin sat beside the window.

Her thoughts began turning against her.

> **She's probably okay.**

> **She has survived worse.**

> **She knows the route.**

> **She'll come back when the storm stops.**

Then:

> **What if she can't?**

She crossed that sentence out.

Then wrote:

> **Don't think like that.**

---

# **HOUR SIX**

The notebook became darker.

> **The storm is still going.**

> **I can't see anything outside.**

> **I don't like this.**

> **I know why she's late.**

> **I know she's trying to come home.**

> **So why am I scared?**

A few minutes later:

> **Because I don't know where she is.**

---

# **HOUR EIGHT**

Corin stopped checking the terminal.

There was no point.

The connection remained dead.

She sat on the floor beside the window.

The snow outside had completely obscured the forest.

There was no world beyond the glass.

Only white.

Corin whispered:

> **"...Orphie?"**

Nothing answered.

---

# **THE GIFTS**

On the table were several things Orphie had brought back from previous trips.

A small package from Blacksite personnel.

A handwritten letter.

A little present someone had left for Corin.

Things people had sent because they remembered her.

Corin picked up one of the letters.

She held it against her chest.

> **"People remember me."**

She looked toward the door.

> **"...Right?"**

---

# **THE FEAR**

Corin eventually opened the notebook again.

Her handwriting was shaky.

> **Orphie always comes back.**

> **She has to come back.**

> **She said she would.**

Then:

> **But the storm doesn't care what she promised.**

The sentence remained untouched.

---

# **HOUR TEN**

Corin began pacing.

Her Ether fluctuated.

The familiar anxiety returned.

She checked the door.

Checked the window.

Checked the terminal.

Nothing.

She returned to her notebook.

> **I hate this.**

> **I hate waiting.**

> **I hate not knowing.**

Then, after several minutes:

> **Please come home.**

---

# **THE BLACKSITE**

At the Blacksite, Orphie was trying to return.

The storm had made the journey impossible.

She had attempted multiple routes.

All were blocked.

The communications network remained unstable.

She knew Corin was alone.

And she knew exactly what prolonged isolation did to her.

Orphie was not choosing to stay away.

She was trying to get home.

---

# **ARCHITECT RESPONSE**

Guiding Light became aware of the situation once communications failed.

She immediately attempted to reach the treehouse.

The storm interfered even with her normal manifestation.

Curious Light was similarly occupied with the Blacksite's emergency response.

For the first time, both Architects were dealing with a problem they could not simply solve without consequences.

The storm itself was not the true danger.

**Corin being alone during it was.**

---

# **CORIN'S LAST ENTRY**

Late into the night, Corin wrote:

> **"Orphie."**

> **"If you can hear me somehow..."**

> **"I'm scared."**

A long pause followed.

Then:

> **"But I'm going to wait."**

And finally:

> **"You always come back."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie's absence today is different from every previous absence.

She did not simply leave for supplies.

She did not get delayed by work.

She did not forget.

She did not choose to stay.

A severe snowstorm has physically severed the route between the Blacksite and the treehouse.

For Corin, however, the distinction is difficult.

She is alone.

She cannot see beyond the snow.

She cannot contact Orphie.

And every minute stretches into something enormous.

Yet one thing remains different from previous episodes.

Corin knows the reason.

She knows there is a storm.

She knows Orphie is trying to return.

And despite everything her mind tells her—

she chooses to wait.

The notebook remains open on the table.

The final sentence reads:

> **"She always comes back."**

Outside, the snow continues falling.

And somewhere beyond the white forest, Orphie is still trying to find her way home.

**End of Restricted Safehouse / Extreme Weather Incident**
