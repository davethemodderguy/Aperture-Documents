### **COMBAT TRAINING / ANOMALOUS CAPABILITY REPORT**

# **CORIN — FEATHER LAUNCH TEST**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Training Type:** **Controlled Anomalous Combat Exercise**  
**Status:** **Successful**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

A controlled combat exercise was conducted to determine the practical capabilities of Corin's anomalous feathers.

The test was performed against an isolated rock in a designated training area.

Corin demonstrated that her feathers can function as highly destructive Ether-based projectiles.

The test also confirmed an important activation requirement:

> **Corin must sharply flick her wings to launch a feather.**

A feather that is merely shed, plucked, or left stationary remains dormant.

---

# **LAUNCH MECHANISM**

Corin does not simply will a feather to fly from her wings.

She must physically **flick her wings with sufficient force**.

The movement dislodges and launches the selected feather.

Once launched, the feather becomes an active anomalous projectile.

The process appears almost instinctive.

Corin can select individual feathers without significantly disturbing the rest of her wings.

---

# **TEST TARGET**

**Target:** Large isolated rock  
**Range:** Controlled training distance  
**Environmental Conditions:** Stable  
**Observer:** C.O.R.E. personnel

Corin positioned herself several meters away from the target.

She unfolded her wings.

After briefly adjusting her stance, she gave one wing a sharp flick.

A single feather separated from the wing.

It accelerated toward the rock.

---

# **IMPACT**

The feather struck the rock.

The moment of contact produced an immediate Ether-mediated detonation.

There was no prolonged delay.

No visible conventional explosive material.

The rock was effectively destroyed.

The original stone was reduced to **fine dust and fragmented particulate matter.**

The resulting Ether discharge dissipated shortly afterward.

Corin stared at the remains.

> **"...Oops."**

---

# **DAMAGE ASSESSMENT**

The test demonstrated significantly greater destructive capability than previously estimated.

The projectile did not merely chip or fracture the target.

The entire rock was reduced to fine particulate material.

This indicates that the feather's Ether payload is capable of producing an extremely concentrated impact effect.

C.O.R.E. has therefore increased the threat classification of active feathers.

---

# **CORIN'S CONTROL**

Despite the destructive result, Corin demonstrated excellent control.

She successfully:

- Selected a single feather.
    
- Launched it deliberately.
    
- Directed it toward the target.
    
- Maintained control of the remaining feathers.
    
- Avoided accidental activation.
    

No unintended feathers were discharged.

---

# **TRAINING OBSERVATION**

Curious Light noted that Corin's combat capability does not require conventional weapon handling.

Her wings effectively function as an integrated anomalous weapons platform.

Her natural movement provides the launch mechanism.

Her feathers provide the projectiles.

Ether provides the payload.

Corin herself provides the targeting and control.

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

Orphie watched the rock disappear.

She looked at Corin.

Then at the dust.

> **Orphie:**  
> "...You destroyed the entire rock."

Corin looked embarrassed.

> **"I thought it would just explode."**

> **Orphie:**  
> "That _was_ exploding."

Corin considered this.

> **"...Oh."**

---

# **TRAINING RESTRICTIONS**

Following the test, C.O.R.E. established several restrictions.

Corin is prohibited from launching feathers:

- Inside the SAFEHOUSE.
    
- Near uninvolved personnel.
    
- Near structural supports.
    
- Without an appropriate training target.
    
- In populated areas.
    
- Toward unknown anomalous entities without authorization.
    

Naturally shed feathers remain harmless while dormant.

However, personnel are reminded:

> **Do not flick them.**
