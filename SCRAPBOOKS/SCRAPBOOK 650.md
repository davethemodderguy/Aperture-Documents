### **CORIN / POST-RECOVERY BEHAVIORAL LOG**

# **THE WINGS ARE ALL SHE FEELS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **RECOVERING / SEVERE EMOTIONAL WITHDRAWAL**

---

# **CURRENT OBSERVATION**

Corin continues to spend most of her time outside the Treehouse.

She sits in the snow.

Her wings are spread around her body, covering her like a living blanket.

She trembles constantly.

Her hands tremble.

Her legs tremble.

Her entire body shakes continuously beneath the cold.

There is, however, one exception.

**Her wings do not tremble.**

---

# **THE WINGS**

Corin's wings remain completely still.

Not rigid.

Not frozen.

Simply still.

Every feather is dormant.

The anomalous properties previously associated with her feathers have disappeared.

They can no longer:

- Ignite.
    
- Explode.
    
- Store or release kinetic force.
    
- Function as projectiles.
    
- Act as lockpicks.
    
- Produce light.
    
- Generate their previous anomalous effects.
    

They are simply feathers now.

Yet Corin continues to hold her wings around herself.

---

# **"MY WINGS ARE WHAT I FEEL."**

When asked why she continues keeping them around herself despite the loss of their abilities, Corin answered:

> **"They're what I feel."**

The statement was not elaborated upon.

C.O.R.E. interprets the wings as having become the primary physical expression of Corin's emotional state.

They were her warmth during the year she spent alone.

They shielded her from the snow.

They covered her while she cried.

They kept her together when she had nothing else.

Now they remain the one part of her that does not visibly shake.

---

# **THE BODY**

Corin's physical body continues to tremble almost constantly.

Her fingers shake even when resting against her knees.

Her legs tremble beneath her.

Her shoulders shake beneath her wings.

Her breathing occasionally becomes uneven.

She rarely attempts to stop the trembling.

She simply lets it happen.

---

# **THE CAT FEATURES**

Corin's cat ears remain.

They are noticeably still.

They no longer perk up at every sound as they once did.

Her twin tails rest against the snow beside her.

They occasionally move when she shifts position, but otherwise remain motionless.

The contrast is striking.

The rest of her body trembles.

Her ears remain still.

Her tails remain on the ground.

Her wings remain wrapped around her.

---

# **HER EXPRESSION**

Corin still smiles.

Just not very often.

The bright, expressive personality documented before her disappearance has become much less visible.

She does not laugh as frequently.

She does not become excited easily.

She does not react strongly to things she once enjoyed.

Most interactions receive only a quiet response.

> **"Okay."**

> **"I'm fine."**

> **"It's alright."**

---

# **ONE EXCEPTION**

There is one person who can still reliably make Corin smile.

**Orphie.**

The reaction is small.

Sometimes barely noticeable.

A slight upward movement of the mouth.

A brief softening of her expression.

Occasionally, her ears move.

But it happens.

When Orphie sits beside her, Corin's expression changes.

Not completely.

Not like before.

But enough.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie does not try to force Corin indoors anymore.

She does not insist that she stop sitting in the snow.

She simply sits beside her.

Sometimes she talks.

Sometimes she says nothing.

Sometimes she brings food.

Sometimes she brings warm water.

Sometimes she simply rests her hand against Corin's wing.

Corin usually allows it.

---

# **THE WINGS AS A LANGUAGE**

Personnel have begun observing that Corin's wings communicate more than Corin's words do.

When she is frightened:

**They close tighter.**

When she is exhausted:

**They droop.**

When she wants to be left alone:

**They form a wall around her.**

When Orphie approaches:

**They open slightly.**

When Orphie sits beside her:

**One wing sometimes rests against her.**

Corin rarely explains what she feels.

Her wings do.

---

# **THE HOLLOWNESS**

A psychological assessment asked Corin how she felt.

She thought for several seconds.

Then answered:

> **"Hollow."**

The examiner asked:

> **"Do you feel sad?"**

Corin replied:

> **"Sometimes."**

> **"Angry?"**

> **"Sometimes."**

> **"Happy?"**

A long pause.

> **"...Sometimes."**

The examiner asked what still made her happy.

Corin looked toward the doorway.

Orphie was standing there.

Corin smiled.

Very slightly.

She answered:

> **"Her."**

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin sits outside.

Snow surrounds her.

Her entire body trembles beneath the cold.

Her hands shake.

Her legs shake.

Her breathing shakes.

But her wings remain still.

They surround her.

Dormant feathers cover her body.

No light.

No fire.

No kinetic force.

No weapons.

No tricks.

Nothing anomalous remains in them.

Yet Corin holds them close anyway.

Because they are more than wings now.

They remember the cold.

They remember the waiting.

They remember the nights.

They remember protecting her when nobody else could.

And when Corin cannot explain what she feels...

she wraps herself in them.

Right now, she feels hollow.

Her cat ears are still.

Her twin tails rest quietly against the snow.

She doesn't smile much anymore.

But sometimes Orphie sits beside her.

And for a few seconds...

Corin smiles.

It isn't the old smile.

It isn't the bright, carefree expression everyone remembers.

It's smaller.

Weaker.

But it's there.

And for now, that is enough.

**End of Corin / Post-Recovery Behavioral Log**
