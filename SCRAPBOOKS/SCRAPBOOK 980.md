# SAFEHOUSE MEMORIAL RECORD

## BLUEBERRIES CUPCAKE

**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL  
**Status:** Memorial  
**Subject:** Neri  
**Prepared by:** SAFEHOUSE Administration  
**Date:** ██/██/████

---

### I. THE CUPCAKE

At 18:40, Guiding Light entered the SAFEHOUSE kitchen.

She did not announce herself.

She did not bring one of her usual experiments.

She simply placed a small tray on the counter.

On it were several cupcakes.

**Blueberry cupcakes.**

Neri's favorite.

The recipe was one Guiding Light had made specifically for her.

The frosting was slightly uneven.

The blueberries were visible through the batter.

They were not particularly impressive by Blacksite culinary standards.

But everyone in the room knew exactly what they were.

Guiding Light stood there for several seconds.

Then she quietly said:

> “She liked these.”

Nobody answered.

She picked one up.

For a moment, it looked as though she was going to eat it.

She didn't.

She put it back down.

---

### II. THE TABLE

The cupcakes were eventually moved to the cafeteria.

Nobody asked why.

Nobody needed to.

A single plate was placed at Neri's usual table.

There was one cupcake on it.

Her chemistry book was not there.

Neither was her oversized coat.

Neither were her glasses.

Only the cupcake.

The chair remained empty.

People continued working around it.

A maintenance worker walked past.

An archivist stopped for several seconds.

Bao stood at the entrance to the cafeteria for nearly a minute before sitting down.

She stared at the cupcake.

Then she quietly said:

> “You would've eaten the whole tray.”

She smiled.

Barely.

Then the smile disappeared.

---

# III. WHAT NERI LEFT BEHIND

During the inventory of Neri's belongings, several objects were recovered.

They had been left behind deliberately or unintentionally before her disappearance.

No determination could be made.

### FISHBONE HAIRCLIP

One of Neri's characteristic hairclips.

It was found in the SAFEHOUSE.

It was cleaned and placed inside a small archival container.

Neko initially attempted to return it to Neri's personal belongings.

He stopped.

There was nowhere to return it to.

The hairclip remains on display beside her memorial record.

---

### KNIVES

Neri's knives were recovered.

Several had clearly been used regularly.

They were not confiscated.

They were not destroyed.

They were simply put away.

Nobody in SAFEHOUSE has touched them since.

One staff member suggested placing them in storage.

Bao refused.

> “They're hers.”

That was the end of the discussion.

---

### STARLIGHT KATANA

The Starlight Katana was found exactly where Neri had left it.

It was cleaned, inspected, and placed into a secure display case.

There was initially debate over whether it should remain accessible.

The final decision was unanimous.

**It stays hers.**

Even if she never comes back.

---

### CROSSBOW

Neri's crossbow was also recovered.

It was placed beside the Starlight Katana.

The display looks strangely incomplete.

Not because anything is missing.

Because Neri is.

---

# IV. GUIDING LIGHT

Guiding Light returned to the cafeteria later that night.

She looked at the cupcake.

Then at the empty chair.

Then at the display containing Neri's equipment.

Her wings dimmed slightly.

> “I gave her a piece of my wing.”

Silence.

> “I thought it would make her happy.”

She looked down.

> “It did.”

A pause.

> “For a while.”

Guiding Light carefully picked up one of the remaining cupcakes.

She held it for several seconds.

Then placed it back.

> “I wish I had made more.”

---

# V. BAO

Bao eventually took the cupcake from the plate.

Nobody stopped her.

She broke it in half.

She stared at the inside.

Blueberries.

Exactly as Neri liked them.

Bao quietly laughed.

> “She would've complained if there weren't enough blueberries.”

She took one bite.

Her expression immediately broke.

She covered her face.

Nobody said anything.

After several seconds, Bao whispered:

> “My daughter liked these.”

Nobody corrected her.

Nobody ever would.

---

# VI. JANE DOE

Jane arrived after midnight.

She stopped when she saw the memorial.

Her eyes went immediately to the fishbone hairclip.

Then the knives.

Then the Starlight Katana.

Then the crossbow.

Finally, the cupcake.

Jane stood there silently.

She reached toward the cupcake.

Stopped.

She remembered Neri sitting at that table, sleeves covering her hands, quietly eating cake while reading chemistry.

Jane smiled faintly.

> “You know, My Cute Seal...”

She stopped herself.

Her voice became quieter.

> “...you were supposed to bother me.”

She remained there for a long time.

---

# VII. ALICE

Alice refused to let anyone move Neri's chair.

She sat beside it instead.

Her scooter was parked nearby.

She placed a small note on the table.

It contained only three words:

> **“Save me some.”**

Nobody asked what it meant.

Alice knew.

---

# VIII. THE BLACKSITE

The Blacksite continued operating.

Doors opened.

Elevators moved.

Researchers worked.

Engineers repaired machinery.

Archivists processed documents.

Security personnel changed shifts.

The cafeteria remained open.

The lights still turned on every morning.

The Blacksite had not stopped functioning.

But something had changed.

People started looking toward Neri's table.

Workers passing through the cafeteria occasionally slowed down.

Some still expected to see her.

A small girl in an oversized coat.

A chemistry book open in front of her.

Cake held between sleeves.

Quietly existing.

Quietly waiting.

---

# IX. THE MEMORIAL

The final arrangement was approved without a formal vote.

Neri's **fishbone hairclip** was placed at the center.

Her **knives** were displayed beside it.

The **Starlight Katana** rested vertically behind them.

Her **crossbow** was mounted underneath.

And in front of everything:

One blueberry cupcake.

Guiding Light replaces it every morning.

Someone eats it eventually.

Nobody knows who.

Another appears the next morning.

Nobody has questioned the practice.
