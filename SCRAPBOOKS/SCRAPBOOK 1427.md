# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake's ability to speak has returned.

His stamina has not.

Following the five-week blizzard, prolonged deprivation, Nullwalker transformation, and subsequent recovery, Jake remains **considerably weaker than his previous baseline.**

Walking is possible.

Walking for very long is not.

As a result, Jake is increasingly being picked up and carried whenever he needs to travel farther than his current stamina comfortably permits.

This has become sufficiently routine that several residents have stopped asking whether he needs assistance.

They simply pick him up.

Jake generally does not object.

---

# 1. CURRENT STAMINA

Jake's recovery has restored his ability to function normally in short periods.

It has not restored his endurance.

His current pattern is approximately:

> **WALK → WALK → WALK → TIRED → STOP.**

Sometimes the final stage occurs surprisingly quickly.

Jake may be perfectly alert and interested in something while simultaneously having almost no energy left to physically reach it.

This creates an unusual distinction:

> **Jake wants to go somewhere.**

but

> **Jake does not necessarily have the stamina to get there.**

---

# 2. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake has become considerably more accepting of assistance.

Previously, he would sometimes attempt to continue walking despite exhaustion.

Current behavior is different.

If someone offers a hand:

He takes it.

If someone offers to carry him:

He climbs up.

If someone picks him up without asking because he is visibly exhausted:

He generally just settles against them.

His teddy bear usually comes with him.

---

# 3. COMMON TRANSPORT METHODS

## ANBY

Anby is one of the most frequent carriers.

Jake appears particularly comfortable being carried by her.

The arrangement is usually simple.

Anby crouches.

Jake reaches up.

Anby picks him up.

Jake rests his head against her shoulder.

They continue walking.

No conversation is required.

Occasionally Jake quietly says:

> **"Thanks."**

Anby responds:

> "You're welcome."

And they continue.

---

# 4. TWIGGY

Twiggy frequently carries Jake when his fatigue becomes medically concerning.

Her approach is more practical.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "You're done walking."

Jake sometimes attempts to disagree.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...I'm okay."

Twiggy looks at him.

Jake pauses.

Then:

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Mostly."

Twiggy picks him up.

Jake does not argue further.

---

# 5. BAO

Bao carries Jake when he becomes exhausted enough that walking is no longer advisable.

Jake tends to relax almost immediately when Bao picks him up.

This is particularly common after meals, medical examinations, or extended periods outside the SAFEHOUSE.

Bao has noticed that Jake sometimes falls asleep before reaching his room.

She does not wake him.

She simply carries him the rest of the way.

---

# 6. NORA

Nora is considerably less practical about it.

When Jake becomes tired, Nora may immediately announce:

> **NORA:**  
> "Jake's tired!"

Jake looks at her.

Nora opens her arms.

> **NORA:**  
> "Come here."

Jake usually accepts.

She carries him for as long as she can.

When she becomes tired herself, someone else takes over.

Jake has apparently accepted this arrangement without complaint.

---

# 7. AETERNA

Aeterna can carry Jake with essentially no difficulty.

She is also one of the least likely residents to make a significant event out of it.

If Jake reaches toward her:

She picks him up.

If he falls asleep:

She continues carrying him.

If he wants to be put down:

She puts him down.

Her armor provides a stable surface, and Jake appears comfortable resting against it.

On several occasions, Jake has fallen asleep while being carried by Aeterna.

She simply continues whatever she was doing.

---

# 8. NERI

Neri rarely initiates physical assistance.

However, she has developed an interesting habit.

If Jake is sitting somewhere because he has run out of stamina, Neri may simply sit beside him.

She does not ask whether he wants help.

She waits.

Eventually, someone else usually arrives.

Jake has occasionally looked at Neri and pointed toward his room.

Neri understood.

She then went to find someone capable of carrying him.

No words were necessary.

---

# 9. NEKO

Neko has carried Jake exactly enough times to understand that Jake weighs less than expected.

This has resulted in several confused observations.

> **NEKO:**  
> "You're ridiculously light."

Jake looked at him.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Star."

Neko paused.

> **NEKO:**  
> "Right."

Jake rested his head against Neko's shoulder.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Tired."

Neko's expression softened.

> **NEKO:**  
> "Yeah. I know."

---

# 10. THE LIBRARY INCIDENT

One of the more common occurrences happens in the SAFEHOUSE library.

Jake will walk to the library.

Find a book.

Sit down.

Read.

Forget that he has limited stamina.

Eventually he will attempt to stand.

He gets halfway up.

Stops.

Sits back down.

Looks at the book.

Looks toward the door.

Then simply waits.

The first resident to notice usually carries him back.

Jake does not consider this embarrassing.

He simply considers it:

**transportation.**

---

# 11. THE ELEVATOR

Jake's reduced stamina has also produced an increasingly common elevator phenomenon.

Jake enters an elevator independently.

The elevator moves.

The doors open.

Jake exits.

He walks approximately ten meters.

Then becomes tired.

He sits down.

Someone eventually finds him.

The conversation is generally:

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "Jake?"

Jake looks up.

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "You tired?"

Jake nods.

> **RESIDENT:**  
> "Want a ride?"

Jake raises both arms.

The resident picks him up.

Problem solved.

---

# 12. JAKE'S NEW SIGNAL

Jake has developed a simple nonverbal request for assistance.

He raises both arms.

That's it.

No explanation is required.

The meaning has become universally understood:

> **"Carry me."**

The gesture has now been recognized by:

- Anby;
    
- Twiggy;
    
- Bao;
    
- Nora;
    
- Aeterna;
    
- Neri;
    
- Neko;
    
- Alice;
    
- Frost;
    
- and several other residents.
    

The response is generally immediate.

---

# 13. MEDICAL GUIDANCE

C.O.R.E. Medical personnel have emphasized that carrying Jake is not inherently problematic.

The primary concern is **unnecessary exertion.**

Jake should not be encouraged to:

- race;
    
- climb excessive distances;
    
- repeatedly walk when already fatigued;
    
- carry heavy objects;
    
- "push through" exhaustion;
    
- or treat accepting assistance as failure.
    

His current physical weakness is a recovery issue.

It is not a character flaw.

---

# 14. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Jake's dependence on assisted transportation should not be interpreted as permanent incapacity.

He can walk.

He can move independently.

He can participate in normal SAFEHOUSE activities.

He simply reaches his physical limit much faster than he used to.

His current recovery therefore requires flexibility.

If he wants to walk:

Let him walk.

If he becomes tired:

Let him stop.

If he raises his arms:

Pick him up.

There is no need to turn the process into a negotiation.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Jake's stamina has been significantly reduced by the events surrounding his disappearance and recovery.

His body has survived.

His stellar nature has returned.

His voice is returning.

His energy reserves, however, remain depleted.

As a result, Jake is increasingly becoming something of a **portable resident.**

He can be found:

- being carried down hallways;
    
- resting on Anby's shoulder;
    
- asleep against Bao;
    
- riding with Aeterna;
    
- being transported by Twiggy;
    
- sitting on someone's back;
    
- or simply being carried from one room to another because he ran out of energy halfway there.
    

Jake does not appear embarrassed by this.

If anything, he seems to have accepted a simple principle:

> **If my legs stop working, someone else has legs.**

## ARCHIVIST'S NOTE

There was once a time when Jake would have considered being carried a major inconvenience.

That time appears to have passed.

Current Jake sees someone approaching while he is tired.

He raises both arms.

They pick him up.

He rests against them.

Five minutes later, he is asleep.

The SAFEHOUSE has therefore accidentally developed a decentralized transportation network for one six-year-old star.

No schedule.

No assigned drivers.

No paperwork.

Just:

**Jake raises arms.**

**Someone picks up Jake.**

**Jake goes wherever he wanted to go.**

Considering the circumstances that led to his current weakness, C.O.R.E. considers this an acceptable development.
