## 1. SUMMARY

Following the implementation of localized gravity within Neri's personal subspace, an additional safety modification has been introduced beneath the primary floating islands.

A **250-meter vertical region beneath the lowest inhabited island has been designated as a zero-gravity recovery zone.**

The purpose of the zone is twofold:

1. **Prevent accidental deaths caused by falling into the lower void.**
    
2. **Make recovery and rescue considerably easier.**
    

Neri has additionally described a secondary purpose:

> **“Punishment for lack of situational awareness.”**

The modification was approved after consideration of the subspace's unusual geometry.

The floating islands remain suspended above an open void. Gravity now functions normally around the islands, meaning visitors can walk, jump, and use the rope bridges normally.

However, falling below the lowest island will eventually transition the individual into the designated **250-meter Zero-Gravity Recovery Zone**.

The individual will stop accelerating downward and remain suspended until rescued.

---

# 2. PREVIOUS HAZARD

The original subspace configuration had no meaningful gravitational reference.

Personnel who jumped away from an island could continue drifting indefinitely.

Neko designated this phenomenon:

> **OUT OF BOUNDS**

Miss Neko provided the following simplified explanation:

> “Just imagine you jumping in outer space, you'll keep floating until you die.”

Following the installation of localized gravity, the original problem was substantially reduced.

However, this introduced a new problem.

Gravity prevents people from floating upward indefinitely.

It also means gravity can make people fall.

The lower void therefore remained hazardous.

---

# 3. NERI'S SOLUTION

Neri implemented a **250-meter gravity-null region** underneath the floating-island network.

The transition is automatic.

### Gravity profile

|Location|Gravity|
|---|---|
|Floating islands|Normalized local gravity|
|Rope bridges|Normalized local gravity|
|Island interiors|Normalized local gravity|
|Immediate space around islands|Localized gravity|
|Lower 250 m recovery region|**0 gravity**|
|Beyond recovery region|Restricted / undefined|

The recovery zone does not remove gravity from the islands themselves.

Its effect is limited to the area beneath them.

---

# 4. WHY 250 METERS?

Neri's initial explanation was:

> “250 meters is enough.”

When asked why that particular distance was selected, Neri responded:

> “Because if you fall that far and still don't realize you're falling, that's a you problem.”

Neko subsequently confirmed that 250 meters provides sufficient vertical separation to:

- allow a person to clear the island structures;
    
- provide enough distance for the gravity transition to occur safely;
    
- prevent immediate collision with the underside of an island;
    
- give rescue personnel a large operating area;
    
- make a falling individual easier to locate.
    

The distance was therefore retained.

---

# 5. ZERO-GRAVITY RECOVERY BEHAVIOR

Upon entering the recovery zone, an individual experiences:

- immediate cessation of gravitational acceleration;
    
- continued existing momentum until naturally reduced;
    
- unrestricted three-dimensional movement;
    
- no automatic return to the islands;
    
- no additional downward acceleration.
    

The individual will therefore remain suspended rather than continuing to fall.

This does **not** mean the person can necessarily rescue themselves.

Without an ability to fly, climb, or otherwise propel themselves, they may simply remain suspended in the void.

This is intentional.

---

# 6. THE "SITUATIONAL AWARENESS" POLICY

Neri has explicitly stated that the recovery zone is **not intended to encourage careless behavior.**

Instead, she considers falling into it a consequence of failing to observe one's surroundings.

Her stated reasoning:

> “There are islands.”

> “There are ropes.”

> “There is gravity.”

> “How did you fall?”

When an Archivist suggested that the new system effectively guarantees their survival after falling, Neri reportedly answered:

> “Yes.”

After a pause:

> “You still fell.”

---

# 7. RESCUE ADVANTAGE

The previous configuration made rescue difficult because a missing individual could drift an unpredictable distance from the islands.

The new configuration creates a predictable recovery area.

### Previous situation

**Person falls → continues drifting → location becomes difficult to determine → rescue operation expands**

### Current situation

**Person falls → enters zero-G zone → remains localized → rescue personnel retrieve them**

This dramatically reduces search complexity.

Neri's subspace therefore now has a deliberate distinction between:

**SAFE AREA**

and

**YOU SHOULD HAVE BEEN PAYING ATTENTION AREA**

---

# 8. RESCUE PROCEDURE

If an individual enters the lower recovery zone:

### Step 1

Notify Neri or SAFEHOUSE personnel.

### Step 2

Determine the individual's approximate position.

### Step 3

Deploy a capable rescuer.

Suitable rescuers include personnel capable of:

- flight;
    
- controlled aerial movement;
    
- dimensional/subspace traversal;
    
- safely reaching the recovery zone by other means.
    

### Step 4

Secure the individual.

### Step 5

Return them to the nearest stable island.

### Step 6

Verify that no injuries occurred.

### Step 7

Determine why they were in the lower void in the first place.

# 9. IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTICE

The zero-gravity zone **does not mean the lower void is a recreational area.**

Personnel are prohibited from intentionally entering it without authorization.

The following arguments are not considered valid:

> “But I can't fall anymore.”

> “Neri made it safe.”

> “I wanted to see what happened.”

> “I was testing it.”

> “I thought the void looked interesting.”

Neri has specifically requested that the fifth excuse be treated with particular suspicion.
