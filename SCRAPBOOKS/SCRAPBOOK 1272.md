**SUBJECT:** Nora Tachyon — Advanced Weapon Handling Proficiency

---

# 1. OVERVIEW

Nora Tachyon possesses an unusually broad knowledge of firearm handling techniques.

Her knowledge extends well beyond simply understanding how individual weapons function.

Nora has demonstrated familiarity with numerous named tactical manipulation techniques across different weapon platforms.

Her proficiency appears to be partly attributable to her defining anomalous specialty:

> **Adaptation.**

Nora learns mechanical systems extremely quickly.

Once she understands the operating characteristics of a weapon, she can rapidly adapt her handling style to accommodate it.

---

# 2. DOCUMENTED TECHNIQUES

C.O.R.E. has observed or confirmed Nora's familiarity with:

- **Double loading**
    
- **Quad loading**
    
- **One-handed press checking**
    
- **Flip cocking with lever-action platforms**
    
- **Iraq reload**
    
- **Jungle-mag reload**
    
- **One-handed shotgun manipulation/pumping**
    

These techniques span multiple weapon configurations and loading systems.

Nora does not appear to regard them as particularly remarkable.

When asked how she learned them, she generally responds:

> **NORA:**  
> “Practice.”

When asked how much practice:

> **NORA:**  
> “Enough.”

---

# 3. SHOTGUN HANDLING

Nora's proficiency with shotguns is particularly notable.

She can adapt her handling to different action types and maintain control while using unconventional one-handed manipulation.

This is especially relevant to her newly issued **Slugger**.

The Slugger is semi-automatic, meaning Nora does not need to manually cycle it between shots.

Nevertheless, her familiarity with manual shotgun manipulation carries over to other Tachyon Industries platforms, including pump-action systems.

Nora treats the differences between them as mechanical characteristics rather than obstacles.

---

# 4. AMMUNITION HANDLING

Nora is also familiar with rapid ammunition-management techniques.

Her documented repertoire includes both double-loading and quad-loading methods.

C.O.R.E. does not consider the techniques themselves unusual among highly trained personnel.

What is unusual is Nora's ability to switch between them with very little apparent cognitive effort.

She can identify the relevant weapon configuration and immediately select an appropriate handling method.

---

# 5. ONE-HANDED MANIPULATION

Nora has demonstrated the ability to manipulate certain weapon systems with one hand.

This includes:

> **One-handed press checking**

and

> **One-handed shotgun pumping.**

These demonstrations were performed under controlled training conditions.

Personnel are reminded that Nora's small size should not be mistaken for lack of mechanical understanding.

Her technique is often more precise than expected.

---

# 6. LEVER-ACTION FAMILIARITY

Nora is also familiar with lever-action platforms.

Her preferred unusual manipulation is informally called:

> **“Flip cocking.”**

Nora appears to have learned the technique simply because she found the normal handling of the platform unnecessarily slow.

Neko reportedly watched her perform it once.

His response:

> **NEKO:**  
> “Why do you know that?”

Nora shrugged.

> **NORA:**  
> “It's a lever.”

Neko stared at her.

> **NEKO:**  
> “That doesn't answer the question.”

Nora walked away.

---

# 7. IRAQ AND JUNGLE-MAG RELOADS

Nora is also familiar with named reload conventions including the:

**Iraq reload**

and

**Jungle-mag reload.**

Her knowledge appears to come from exposure to a wide variety of weapon systems and training references rather than from a single standardized curriculum.

C.O.R.E. has not identified one individual responsible for teaching her the complete collection.

This remains under investigation.

Neri has suggested that Nora may simply have:

> “Learned everything she thought looked useful.”

Nora did not deny this.

---

# 8. ADAPTATION

Nora's ability to learn these techniques is consistent with her anomalous specialty.

Elsher's strength is tacticality.

Neri's is chemistry.

Nora's is:

> **Adaptation.**

When presented with an unfamiliar mechanism, Nora tends to study it briefly, identify its operating logic, and adjust her movements accordingly.

This applies outside of weapons as well.

The same trait has been observed in:

- mechanical systems;
    
- unfamiliar equipment;
    
- new environments;
    
- languages;
    
- movement patterns;
    
- and anomalous phenomena.
    

Weapon handling is simply one particularly visible application.

---

# 9. NORA'S ATTITUDE

Nora does not appear to view her skill as impressive.

During one evaluation, she was asked how she knew so many different techniques.

She responded:

> **NORA:**  
> “They're just different ways to do the same thing.”

The evaluator asked:

> **“Which is your favorite?”**

Nora thought for several seconds.

> **NORA:**  
> “The one that works.”

No further answer was provided.

---

# 10. SAFETY NOTE

Nora's proficiency should not be interpreted as authorization to perform unsupervised weapon demonstrations.

All weapons training remains subject to Aperture and MSAC safety procedures.

Nora's ability to adapt quickly does not eliminate the need for controlled environments, supervision, or appropriate handling protocols.

Her age is also relevant.

She may understand weapon systems exceptionally well.

She is still six years old.

C.O.R.E. therefore recommends maintaining a clear distinction between:

> **“Nora knows how this works.”**

and

> **“Nora should be allowed to do whatever she wants with it.”**

---
