# NERI — MOBILITY & PROTECTIVE AGGRESSION REASSESSMENT

**CLASSIFICATION:** C.O.R.E. INTERNAL — RESTRICTED  
**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**SPECIES:** Seal Thiren  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE SAFEHOUSE RESIDENT  
**THREAT LEVEL:** **HIGH — CONDITIONAL**  
**COMBAT SPECIALIZATION:** Close Quarters / Melee / Evasion  
**ANOMALOUS STATUS:** **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED**  
**PRIMARY CONCERN:** Protective Obsession

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Further observation of Neri has revealed that her combat capability extends beyond her previously documented exceptional melee and hand-to-hand proficiency.

Neri can reliably evade incoming gunfire under appropriate conditions.

Her small stature, unusually fast movement, sliding ability, and recently developed aerial mobility make her an exceptionally difficult target at close range.

The aerial ability developed after several months of being raised and cared for by **Bao**.

Neri's movement is superficially similar to the air-jumping capability demonstrated by Taiga.

Whether the two abilities share an underlying mechanism remains unknown.

The practical result is straightforward:

> **Neri is very difficult to hit.**

---

# 2. MOBILITY PROFILE

Neri's physical build initially caused some personnel to underestimate her mobility.

This was a mistake.

Her relatively short stature provides several practical advantages.

She can:

- accelerate rapidly;
    
- change direction quickly;
    
- slide beneath or around obstacles;
    
- maintain high movement speed over short distances;
    
- rapidly transition between standing, sliding, and aerial movement;
    
- exploit gaps that larger combatants cannot easily use.
    

Her movement is particularly effective indoors.

SAFEHOUSE corridors, doorways, furniture, and other environmental structures provide Neri with numerous opportunities to change direction unexpectedly.

---

# 3. AIR-JUMPING

Following several months of living with Bao, Neri developed the ability to perform an anomalous **air jump**.

The ability allows Neri to alter her movement while airborne in a manner broadly comparable to Taiga's known aerial mobility.

The exact mechanism has not been identified.

Neri herself does not appear particularly interested in explaining it.

When asked how she learned:

> **"Mom taught me."**

Bao's response:

> "I showed her how."

C.O.R.E. researchers subsequently spent several hours trying to determine whether this constituted training, inheritance, anomalous adaptation, or something else.

Neri was eating candy during the entire discussion.

---

# 4. BULLET EVASION

Neri's mobility has been demonstrated to be sufficient for evading gunfire.

This should not be interpreted as conventional invulnerability.

Neri can still be injured.

Her advantage comes from **movement and unpredictability**.

Her preferred response to ranged threats is generally to close distance rather than remain exposed.

She may:

**Run → Slide → Change direction → Air-jump → Reposition → Close distance**

The resulting movement pattern is extremely difficult for ordinary personnel to predict.

Her stature further complicates tracking.

One evaluator summarized it as:

> "You don't lose sight of her because she's fast."

> "You lose sight of her because she's suddenly somewhere else."

---

# 5. COMBAT SYNERGY

Neri's abilities become significantly more concerning when considered together.

### MOBILITY

Extremely high.

### MELEE

Exceptional.

### HAND-TO-HAND

Exceptional.

### WEAPON ACCESS

Extensive.

### RANGED WEAPON INTEREST

Minimal.

### PROTECTIVE AGGRESSION

Extreme.

Neri does not need a firearm to close distance against an armed opponent.

Her preferred combat style naturally encourages her to move toward the threat.

This is precisely what makes her preference for close confrontation so dangerous.

---

# 6. THE THREE-STAGE PROTECTIVE RESPONSE

C.O.R.E. Behavioral Division has developed the following simplified model of Neri's protective behavior:

> **"If her friend is threatened, she will fight."**

A threat alone is sufficient to provoke intervention.

However:

> **"If her friend is injured, you will not live to see the encounter."**

This statement, originally recorded during a behavioral interview, appears to accurately represent the escalation between perceived threat and actual harm.

The final stage is significantly more concerning.

> **"If her friend is dead, hell lets loose."**

This statement has been preserved verbatim.

C.O.R.E. considers it an accurate warning rather than hyperbole.

---

# 7. BEHAVIORAL INTERPRETATION

Neri's attachment system appears to have three distinct levels.

### LEVEL I — THREAT

Neri intervenes.

She attempts to protect the individual.

### LEVEL II — INJURY

Neri becomes substantially more aggressive.

Her willingness to pursue and confront the aggressor increases dramatically.

### LEVEL III — DEATH

Neri's normal behavioral restraint may effectively disappear.

This is considered the highest-risk scenario.

Personnel are therefore strongly advised to prioritize the safety of individuals Neri considers friends during emergencies.

Not merely for their sake.

For everyone's sake.

---

# 8. LUCY MERRYWEATHER

Lucy Merryweather has successfully established a strong relationship with Neri.

This occurred during one of Lucy's visits to the SAFEHOUSE.

The exact interaction was remarkably simple.

Lucy brought **Banlands candy**.

Neri tried it.

She liked it.

A significant amount of it.

Neri subsequently informed another resident:

> **"Banlands candies are good."**

This statement is now considered the first confirmed verbal indication that Neri had accepted Lucy into her trusted social circle.

Lucy has since been treated as a trusted figure by Neri.

---

# 9. THE CANDY INCIDENT

C.O.R.E. personnel initially assumed Lucy's interaction with Neri would require careful relationship-building.

It did.

For approximately five minutes.

Then Lucy produced a container of Banlands confectionery.

Neri's attention immediately shifted.

Lucy offered her one.

Neri ate it.

Silence.

Neri looked at Lucy.

> "More?"

Lucy gave her another.

Neri ate it.

Another.

Another.

Several minutes later:

> **Neri:** "Banlands candies are good."

> **Lucy:** "I'm glad you like them."

> **Neri:** "You're nice."

Lucy smiled.

> **Lucy:** "Thank you."

Neri:

> "Friend."

Lucy has apparently been accepted.

---

# 12. FINAL THREAT ASSESSMENT

Neri's apparent harmlessness remains one of the most misleading characteristics of her profile.

She is small.

She is cute.

She likes candy.

She likes fish.

She swims constantly.

She wears blue, azure, and white.

She calls Bao "Mom."

She calls Frost "Big sis."

She calls Alice "Bunny."

She rides around with Alice's scooter.

She gets along with almost everyone.

And she can apparently dodge bullets, air-jump, slide rapidly across the floor, and cross a room before most personnel have finished deciding whether she is actually a threat.

C.O.R.E.'s current assessment is therefore:

> **Neri is not dangerous because she is constantly violent.**
> 
> **Neri is dangerous because she is extremely capable of violence and has very strong emotional reasons to use that capability.**

Her friendships are her greatest stabilizing factor.

They are also the greatest trigger for her aggression.
