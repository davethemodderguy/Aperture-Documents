# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **ADMINISTRATIVE DIVISION**

### **INTERNAL INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: DUPLICATE ACCOUNTANT INCIDENT**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – KCA PERSONNEL

---

# **OVERVIEW**

On ██/██/20██, employees arriving for the morning shift reported an apparent anomaly involving Senior Accountant Corin Lockwood.

According to multiple witnesses...

There were two.

Initial concerns ranged from identity theft and anomalous infiltration to administrative errors.

The confusion persisted for approximately three hours before official clarification was issued by Aperture Industries.

---

# **08:02**

Reception reported Corin entering the building.

She signed the attendance log.

Nothing unusual.

---

# **08:07**

Reception reported...

Corin entering the building.

Again.

The second individual greeted reception politely and signed the attendance log.

When informed that Corin had already arrived, she looked confused.

> "...Yeah."

> "That's probably Corin."

> "I'm Corey."

Reception assumed this was a joke.

---

# **08:15**

Accounting staff reported seeing two identical women organizing different filing cabinets.

One possessed chalky lime hair and noticeably pale skin.

The other possessed black hair and a healthy complexion.

Both wore identical KCA uniforms.

Both answered to "Lockwood."

Both possessed valid identification.

Both had legitimate employee credentials.

Security authentication confirmed both identities as authentic.

This result did not reduce confusion.

---

# **08:41**

A junior accountant submitted the following incident report.

> "There are two Miss Lockwoods."

The report was returned by management.

With one handwritten note.

> "Please specify which one."

The employee submitted a second report.

> "...That's the problem."

---

# **09:13**

Payroll contacted Human Resources after discovering two active personnel records.

Human Resources contacted Aperture Industries.

Aperture responded with one sentence.

> "Yes."

No further explanation was initially provided.

---

# **OFFICIAL INTRODUCTION**

Later that morning, Corin and Corey addressed the Accounting Department together.

Corin spoke first.

> "...Sorry."

Corey continued.

> "This is probably confusing."

Corin nodded.

> "We're sisters."

Corey immediately looked at Corin.

> "...Close enough."

Neither elaborated further.

The explanation somehow raised fewer questions than expected.

---

---

# **PRODUCTIVITY**

Contrary to initial concerns, Accounting Division reported a measurable increase in efficiency.

Corin preferred reviewing financial records.

Corey preferred communicating with other departments.

Without planning, the pair naturally divided responsibilities according to preference.

Error rates decreased.

Document turnaround improved.

Employees eventually accepted the situation.

Mostly.

---

# **THE CONFUSION NEVER COMPLETELY STOPPED**

Common occurrences now include:

- Employees greeting the wrong sister.
    
- Corin answering questions intended for Corey.
    
- Corey finishing sentences Corin had already started.
    
- New employees believing mirrors are involved.
    
- Staff accidentally scheduling the same meeting twice because they assumed only one Lockwood would attend.
    

The twins usually respond with amusement.

Occasionally, they intentionally switch desks without telling anyone.

Human Resources has formally requested they stop doing this.

Neither has agreed.

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

Several months after the incident, dual attendance has become an ordinary part of life within the KCA.

New employees receive one simple instruction during orientation.

> "Yes."

> "There are two Lockwoods."

> "No."

> "You are not seeing double."

> "You'll get used to it."

According to veteran employees...

Everyone eventually does.

**End of Internal Incident Report**
