## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE / CRITICAL PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

# **"WHY WON'T YOU LET ME GO?"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Architect-Sealed  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Status:** **CRITICAL / UNDER OBSERVATION**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's condition has deteriorated beyond physical discomfort.

The greater problem is no longer her body.

It is what she has begun to believe her body makes her.

A burden.

A damaged thing.

Something requiring constant maintenance from everyone around her.

She has increasingly described herself as:

> **"A weight."**

> **"Something broken."**

> **"Something that takes too much work to keep around."**

These beliefs are becoming increasingly difficult for her to separate from reality.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi remains unable to provide the solution Corin wants.

She cannot restore Corin's original body.

She cannot simply reverse the experimentation.

She cannot make the Ether disappear without potentially destroying the consciousness it currently sustains.

And she cannot force Corin to accept a body she despises.

Lumi knows this.

The helplessness has become painful for her as well.

She is Corin's mother.

Yet there is no simple maternal action capable of fixing what happened.

---

# **PHYSICAL DEPENDENCY**

Corin's structural condition has continued to cause problems.

When major repairs loosen sufficiently, her limbs can separate from her body.

The Ether does not automatically reconstruct the connections.

Orphie therefore continues to perform necessary maintenance.

She carefully returns the affected limb to position and repairs the damaged stitches.

It has become routine.

That is precisely what bothers Corin.

Every time Orphie has to help her, Corin sees another reason to believe she is a burden.

---

# **THE KITCHEN**

Corin has also struggled with ordinary household tasks.

Cooking has become particularly difficult.

Her inability to perceive color makes certain visual distinctions impossible.

She cannot reliably judge ingredients by appearance.

She has gradually stopped attempting to cook.

One evening, she told Orphie:

> **"I can't even make dinner."**

> **"You have to fix me when I fall apart."**

> **"I can't even see properly."**

She looked down.

> **"What am I actually useful for?"**

Orphie had no immediate answer.

Not because Corin was useless.

Because the question itself was wrong.

---

# **THE BREAKING POINT**

Later that evening, Corin stood before the mirror.

She stared at herself.

The pale skin.

The scars.

The stitches.

The clothing she used to hide everything.

The face she could recognize but no longer felt connected to.

She eventually broke the mirror.

The resulting injuries were superficial and rapidly sealed by the Ether.

Within moments, her hands appeared almost completely unharmed.

That was what disturbed Corin.

Not the injury.

The fact that even that disappeared.

She stared at her hands.

Then whispered:

> **"Why can't I just give up?"**

Her voice broke.

> **"Why?"**

> **"Just let me die."**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie found her shortly afterward.

She did not react with anger.

She did not tell Corin to stop being dramatic.

She did not tell her that other people had it worse.

She simply sat beside her.

Corin looked at her.

> **"I'm tired."**

Orphie answered quietly:

> **"I know."**

> **"I'm tired of you having to fix me."**

> **"I know."**

> **"I'm tired of Mom being helpless."**

Orphie lowered her head.

> **"I know."**

Corin's voice became almost inaudible.

> **"...I'm tired of being me."**

Orphie remained beside her.

> **"Then you don't have to figure everything out tonight."**

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light appeared soon afterward.

For once, she did not try to offer an explanation.

She did not speak about second chances.

She did not tell Corin that everything would eventually be fine.

She understood that those words could sound like another demand to keep going.

Instead, she sat beside Corin.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "You don't have to prove that you're worth keeping."

Corin stared at the floor.

> **"Then why does everyone keep fixing me?"**

Guiding Light answered:

> **"Because you're someone they love."**

Corin shook her head.

> **"That's not a reason."**

> **"It is to me."**

---

# **THE PROBLEM WITH HER BODY**

Corin's body has become a cruel paradox.

It requires constant maintenance.

It cannot function normally.

It cannot perceive color.

It has no ordinary heartbeat or circulation.

It can physically come apart.

And yet it refuses to permanently fail.

Corin sees this as imprisonment.

Others see it as survival.

Neither interpretation completely captures what she is experiencing.

---

# **CURRENT SUPPORT MEASURES**

Following the incident, the treehouse has been placed under quiet observation.

No armed containment personnel have been assigned.

No laboratory examination has been authorized.

Corin is not being treated as a dangerous specimen.

Instead:

- Orphie remains nearby.
    
- Guiding Light maintains continuous awareness of Corin's condition.
    
- Curious Light has suspended all nonessential research involving Corin.
    
- Lumi has been informed of the deterioration.
    
- Corin is not to be left isolated for prolonged periods during acute episodes.
    

The purpose is not to control Corin.

It is to ensure she does not have to endure the worst moments alone.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin believes she is a burden because she requires help.

She believes her body is broken because it requires stitches.

She believes she is useless because she cannot perform ordinary tasks.

She believes her mother cannot help her because even Lumi cannot undo what happened.

And worst of all, she has begun interpreting everyone's attempts to care for her as evidence that she is difficult to keep alive.

That conclusion is wrong.

But knowing that does not make it feel wrong to Corin.

For now, the people around her cannot give her a normal body.

They cannot restore everything she lost.

They cannot make five months of absence disappear.

They cannot force her to love herself.

They can only remain.

And tonight, that is what Orphie does.

She stays beside Corin.

Not because Corin is easy to maintain.

Not because Corin owes anyone survival.

But because, to Orphie, **Corin is not a broken thing that needs fixing.**

She is her sister.

**End of Critical Psychological Observation**
