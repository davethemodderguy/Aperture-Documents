### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / MART BEHAVIOR INCIDENT LOG**

# **ORPHIE — MARTS DRIVEN AWAY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Behavioral Concern  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Marts / Corin / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Luna / Solaria  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **ESCALATING**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The Marts have continued attempting to care for Orphie despite her repeated demands that they leave her alone.

Orphie has now begun actively **bullying and frightening them** whenever they approach.

The behavior has been effective.

The Marts are now genuinely afraid of her.

---

# **THE INCIDENT**

Several Marts approached Orphie carrying food.

They had apparently decided that, despite previous encounters, she might accept something if they simply left it nearby.

One Mart approached first.

> "...Orphie."

She looked at it.

> "...Go away."

The Mart remained.

> "...Food."

> "...I don't want it."

The Mart moved closer.

Orphie stared at it.

The golden Ether lines beneath her skin began glowing.

The Mart immediately stopped.

---

# **DETERRENCE**

Orphie smiled.

The expression resembled Luna's infamous smile.

The surrounding atmosphere changed.

The Marts experienced the familiar anomalous sensation:

> **An overwhelming sense of incoming danger.**

One Mart dropped the food.

Another immediately backed away.

Orphie continued:

> "...I told you."

> "...Leave me alone."

---

# **THE BULLYING**

The interaction did not end there.

Orphie continued deliberately intimidating the Marts.

She mocked their attempts to help.

> "...Why do you keep coming back?"

> "...Do you not understand?"

> "...I'm not worth taking care of."

One Mart attempted to approach again.

Orphie stepped toward it.

The Mart immediately retreated.

> "...Scared?"

The Mart froze.

Orphie smiled again.

> "...Good."

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

That was enough.

The Marts ran.

Not their usual playful retreat.

Not their normal bouncing movement.

They fled.

Several collided with each other while trying to escape through the doorway.

One left its tiny top hat behind.

Another returned briefly to grab it.

Then immediately ran again.

---

# **AFTERMATH**

The Marts gathered well away from the treehouse.

None approached.

None attempted to return the food.

None tried to retrieve the abandoned items.

For several minutes, the colony remained unusually quiet.

One Mart eventually said:

> "...Scary."

Another:

> "...Don't go near."

A third:

> "...Orphie mean."

No Mart contradicted it.

---

# **THE CHANGE**

Previously, the Marts were persistent.

Even when Orphie pushed them away, they returned.

They did not understand rejection in the same way humans did.

They believed that staying nearby was enough.

That has changed.

They now associate approaching Orphie with fear.

The colony has begun avoiding her deliberately.

---

# **CORIN'S DISCOVERY**

Corin later found the Marts hiding together.

> "...What happened?"

Several Marts pointed toward the treehouse.

> "...Orphie."

Corin:

> "...She scared you?"

The Marts nodded.

One quietly added:

> "...She bullied."

Corin went silent.

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

When Corin confronted her, Orphie did not deny it.

> **Corin:** "...You scared them."

> **Orphie:** "...They wouldn't leave."

> "...They're afraid of you now."

> "...Good."

Corin stared at her.

> "...You wanted them to stop caring?"

Orphie answered without hesitation:

> "...Yes."

---

# **THE MARTS' NEW BEHAVIOR**

The colony has now established a clear avoidance pattern.

When Orphie is visible:

- Marts move away.
    
- Marts avoid eye contact.
    
- Marts do not approach the treehouse.
    
- Marts no longer bring food directly to her.
    
- Marts do not attempt physical affection.
    
- Marts do not attempt to cheer her up.
    

They have finally learned the lesson Orphie wanted them to learn.

---

# **THE TRAGEDY**

Orphie succeeded.

The Marts stopped trying to care for her.

They stopped following her.

They stopped bringing her things.

They stopped trying to make her happy.

They are afraid of her now.

And Orphie has gotten exactly what she repeatedly requested:

> **"Leave me alone."**

---

# **LUNA**

Luna was visibly disturbed when informed.

> "...She scared the Marts until they stopped caring."

She paused.

> "...That's not what she actually wanted."

The researcher asked what she meant.

Luna answered:

> "...She wanted proof that nobody cared."

---

# **SOLARIA**

Solaria was quieter.

> "...And now she has it."

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Orphie:**  
Actively intimidating and bullying Marts to prevent further attempts at companionship.

**Marts:**  
Frightened. Avoiding Orphie. No longer attempting to care for her directly.

**Corin:**  
Highly concerned.

**SAFEHOUSE:**  
Informed of the behavioral escalation.

**Luna:**  
Concerned.

**Solaria:**  
Concerned.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The Marts used to be the one group Orphie could never completely push away.

They were persistent.

They were affectionate.

They did not care whether she was important.

They simply cared about **her**.

So Orphie frightened them.

She mocked them.

She bullied them.

She made herself into something they were afraid to approach.

And eventually, the Marts ran.

Now they hide when she is nearby.

They no longer ask whether she has eaten.

They no longer bring her food.

They no longer climb onto her head.

They no longer try to make her smile.

They are scared of her.

For the first time, Orphie has succeeded in making the Marts stop caring.

And somewhere beneath all that emptiness, the person who once cared deeply for them is still there—

watching the last people who refused to leave finally run away.

**End of Personnel Welfare / Mart Behavior Incident Log**
