### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / FLIGHT DEVELOPMENT REPORT**

# **CORIN — HOVERING MASTERY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Ability:** Flight / Aerial Maneuvering  
**Development:** **Stable Hovering — Achieved**  
**Status:** Operational

---

# **OVERVIEW**

After approximately **500 recorded falls**, Corin has successfully learned how to hover.

The training process was considerably less graceful than anticipated.

Corin repeatedly lost altitude, tilted sideways, spun unexpectedly, and occasionally fell **headfirst** toward the ground.

Despite this, she continued practicing.

She eventually developed sufficient control over her wings to maintain a stable position in midair without forward movement.

---

# **THE FIVE HUNDRED FALLS**

Corin's training record contains approximately five hundred documented failed attempts.

The failures varied considerably.

Common outcomes included:

- Falling directly downward.
    
- Losing balance sideways.
    
- Spinning around.
    
- Accidentally flying backward.
    
- Descending too quickly.
    
- Falling headfirst.
    
- Briefly achieving stability before immediately losing it.
    
- Dramatically recovering at the last possible moment.
    

The final category became particularly common.

---

# **EMERGENCY RECOVERY**

Corin discovered an unconventional emergency technique during training.

If she began falling uncontrollably, she could rapidly flick her wings and launch feathers downward.

The feathers would detonate shortly afterward, producing **airbursts**.

The resulting bursts of air could provide enough sudden force to alter her trajectory.

This allowed Corin to recover from several otherwise dangerous falls.

---

# **HEADFIRST INCIDENTS**

Several of Corin's falls were particularly concerning.

During one attempt, she completely lost control and began falling headfirst.

She was approximately several meters above the ground.

Instead of attempting to correct her posture normally, Corin rapidly deployed multiple feathers.

**THUMP.**

The resulting airburst pushed her upward and sideways.

Corin rotated in midair.

She recovered.

Then immediately attempted hovering again.

Neko reportedly watched the entire event and said:

> **"...You could've just stopped training for the day."**

Corin replied:

> **"But I almost had it."**

---

# **THE BREAKTHROUGH**

During the approximately five-hundredth attempt, Corin stopped moving forward.

Her wings continued making precise, controlled movements.

She remained at the same altitude.

No drifting.

No falling.

No spinning.

No emergency airburst.

She was simply...

**hovering.**

Corin looked down.

She looked around.

Her ears perked up.

Her tails began moving excitedly.

> **"...I'm not falling."**

She had finally done it.

---

# **CURRENT HOVERING CAPABILITY**

Corin can now:

- Remain stationary in midair.
    
- Maintain altitude.
    
- Rotate while hovering.
    
- Change orientation without losing altitude.
    
- Hover while carrying light objects.
    
- Transition from hovering into forward flight.
    
- Transition from forward flight into a hover.
    
- Perform controlled emergency descents.
    

Her stability continues improving with practice.

---

# **COMBAT APPLICATION**

Hovering significantly expands Corin's aerial maneuvering capabilities.

She can now remain stationary while observing an area.

She can launch feathers without needing to maintain forward momentum.

She can also rapidly transition between:

**Hover → Flight → Dive → Airburst Recovery → Hover**

This makes her substantially more maneuverable than before.

---

# **CORIN'S OPINION**

When asked how she felt about finally learning to hover, Corin answered:

> **"My head hurts."**

After a short pause:

> **"...But I can hover."**

She then immediately demonstrated the ability.

She remained suspended approximately two meters above the ground for several minutes.

No falling occurred.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin did not learn to hover elegantly.

She learned through approximately five hundred mistakes.

Some were harmless.

Some were embarrassing.

Several involved falling headfirst toward the ground.

But every time she fell, she tried again.

And when she couldn't recover normally, she learned to use her wings' airburst capability to save herself.

Eventually, she stopped falling.

She simply stayed there.

Suspended in the air.

Corin's final comment to the observers was:

> **"Worth the five hundred falls."**

Then she flew away.
