# 1. CLARIFICATION

Previous observations of Invictus Aeterna have emphasized her extremely serious demeanor while manifested in armored form.

This remains accurate.

However:

> **Serious does not mean emotionless.**

Aeterna understands humor.

She understands play.

She understands teasing.

She understands the concept of having fun.

She simply does not display these behaviors in the same manner as most SAFEHOUSE residents.

Most notably, Aeterna appears to derive considerable amusement from **intimidation.**

---

# 2. THE SIX OPERATORS

The behavior was first formally documented following an incident involving six MSAC operators.

The operators had previously attempted to bully Nora into giving them access to Tachyon Industries merchandise.

Aeterna had intervened.

The operators subsequently avoided Nora.

They did not, however, avoid Aeterna.

This was their mistake.

---

# 3. FIRST INCIDENT

One operator was walking through a SAFEHOUSE corridor late in the evening.

The corridor was empty.

The operator heard footsteps behind him.

He turned.

Nothing.

He continued walking.

Footsteps again.

He turned.

Nothing.

He stopped.

The footsteps stopped.

He continued.

The footsteps resumed.

The operator eventually turned around and saw Aeterna standing approximately three meters behind him.

Completely motionless.

Blue runes glowing.

No sword.

No movement.

The operator froze.

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “...Aeterna?”

Silence.

Aeterna slowly tilted her head.

The operator immediately walked away.

Aeterna remained where she was.

---

# 4. SECOND INCIDENT

Another operator entered the cafeteria.

Aeterna was sitting at a table.

She was not eating.

She was simply sitting.

The operator noticed her.

He continued toward the kitchen.

Aeterna's head turned.

The operator stopped.

Aeterna continued staring.

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “What?”

Silence.

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “Why are you looking at me?”

Aeterna stood.

The operator immediately left.

Aeterna sat back down.

Nora, who had been sitting nearby, watched the entire exchange.

After several seconds:

> **NORA:**  
> “You're being mean.”

Aeterna looked at Nora.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Yes.”

Nora giggled.

---

# 5. THIRD INCIDENT

One operator reported hearing a deep voice behind him while walking through an empty hallway.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “You should not be here.”

The operator turned.

Aeterna was standing at the end of the corridor.

The operator immediately recognized her.

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “Aeterna, come on.”

Aeterna disappeared.

The operator stared.

A voice came from directly behind him.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “You should have listened.”

The operator screamed.

Security footage confirms Aeterna had moved behind him without producing an audible approach.

The operator ran.

Aeterna did not pursue.

She simply stood in the hallway.

---

# 6. FOURTH INCIDENT

The fourth operator attempted to avoid Aeterna entirely.

This proved ineffective.

He entered an elevator.

The doors closed.

He looked at the reflection of the elevator interior.

Aeterna was standing behind him.

He spun around.

Nobody was there.

He looked back at the reflection.

Aeterna was still there.

The elevator reached its destination.

The doors opened.

Aeterna was gone.

The operator stepped out.

Aeterna was standing directly outside the elevator.

The operator reportedly said:

> **“OH, COME ON.”**

Aeterna remained completely expressionless.

Then:

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Good evening.”

The operator left.

---

# 7. FIFTH INCIDENT

The fifth operator was more prepared.

He entered the cafeteria accompanied by three other MSAC personnel.

He looked around.

Nothing.

He checked behind him.

Nothing.

He checked under the table.

Nothing.

He looked toward the ceiling.

Nothing.

Aeterna was standing directly beside him.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Looking for me?”

The operator screamed.

Three other personnel immediately began laughing.

Aeterna remained completely serious.

This appears to have been the first occasion on which she intentionally delivered a punchline.

---

# 8. SIXTH INCIDENT

The sixth operator attempted to confront Aeterna.

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “You think you're funny?”

Aeterna looked at him.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Yes.”

The operator stared.

Aeterna stared back.

A long silence followed.

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “...That's it?”

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Yes.”

Nora, standing nearby, began laughing.

The operator eventually laughed as well.

The interaction ended without hostility.

---

# 9. INTERVIEW

C.O.R.E. later asked Aeterna why she repeatedly frightened the operators.

**C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
“You know they are scared of you.”

**AETERNA:**  
“Yes.”

**C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
“Do you enjoy that?”

Aeterna paused.

**AETERNA:**  
“Yes.”

**C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
“Why?”

Aeterna considered the question.

**AETERNA:**  
“It is amusing.”

**C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
“So you were playing with them?”

Aeterna nodded.

**AETERNA:**  
“Yes.”

---

# 10. NORA'S INVOLVEMENT

Nora was asked whether Aeterna was allowed to scare people.

Nora shrugged.

> **NORA:**  
> “She's having fun.”

The interviewer asked:

> “Does Aeterna have fun often?”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “Yeah.”

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> “She just looks scary when she does.”

---

# 11. AETERNA'S PERSONALITY

The discovery is significant.

Aeterna's default armored demeanor remains:

- serious;
    
- controlled;
    
- quiet;
    
- intimidating;
    
- disciplined;
    
- rarely verbal.
    

These characteristics do not indicate an absence of positive emotion.

Aeterna can:

- enjoy company;
    
- participate in games;
    
- tease people;
    
- intentionally frighten people;
    
- appreciate humor;
    
- spend time around SAFEHOUSE residents;
    
- entertain Nora;
    
- interact with Jane;
    
- and apparently find six frightened MSAC operators extremely entertaining.
    

She simply does so while maintaining an expressionless armored face.

---

# 12. JANE DOE'S ASSESSMENT

Jane was informed of the incidents.

She laughed.

**JANE:**  
“She finally figured out she can use the scary thing for fun.”

**C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
“You approve?”

**JANE:**  
“I scare people for fun too.”

Pause.

**JANE:**  
“I think she found her hobby.”

---

# 13. GUIDANCE

Aeterna is permitted ordinary social interaction.

However, personnel should understand that her idea of recreational intimidation may be considerably more intense than that of an ordinary resident.

Personnel are advised:

- do not intentionally provoke her;
    
- do not attempt to challenge her intimidation abilities;
    
- do not harass Nora;
    
- do not assume Aeterna is emotionless;
    
- do not mistake silence for hostility;
    
- and do not repeatedly ask her to scare people for entertainment.
    

Particularly:

> **Do not turn Aeterna into a haunted-house attraction.**

She will probably agree.

That is the problem.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Aeterna is not permanently serious.

She is simply serious-looking.

There is a difference.

She is a 15,000-year-old spirit inhabiting an armored body.

She has existed for longer than most civilizations.

She can manifest a sword.

She can transform into a falcon.

She can carry Nora through the air.

She can stand completely motionless in a dark hallway for several minutes.

And apparently:

> **She thinks scaring people is funny.**

The six MSAC operators who previously bullied Nora have learned this firsthand.

They have also learned an important rule:

> **If the 185-centimeter armored ancient spirit is standing silently behind you, she may not be angry.**

She might just be having fun.
