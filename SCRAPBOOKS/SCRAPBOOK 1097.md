# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neri hosted a cooking competition.

The winner would receive:

> **ONE (1) NIGHT STALKER TICKET**

Neri had previously established that:

> **5 NIGHT STALKER TICKETS = 1 NIGHT STALKER**

This meant the winner was not receiving a Night Stalker.

They were receiving **20% of a Night Stalker.**

This distinction became extremely important approximately thirty seconds after the competition was announced.

Neri received complaints.

A lot of complaints.

---

# 2. EVENT ANNOUNCEMENT

The original announcement was placed on the cafeteria notice board.

> ## COOKING COMPETITION
> 
> **Make food.**
> 
> **Best food wins.**
> 
> **Prize: 1 Night Stalker Ticket**
> 
> **5 tickets = Night Stalker**
> 
> **Have fun.**
> 
> — Neri

The announcement remained on the wall for approximately eight minutes before someone added:

> **"WAIT WHAT DO YOU MEAN ONE TICKET"**

Neri later added:

> **"One ticket."**

Someone responded:

> **"WE KNOW THAT."**

---

# 12. NERI'S ACTUAL POSITION

C.O.R.E. eventually determined that Neri did not intend for the ticket system to function as a conventional prize currency.

The tickets were effectively a **trust-based progression system**.

Neri wanted Night Stalker recipients to demonstrate qualities she considered important.

Her established requirements remained:

1. **Be Nice.**
    
2. **Be Efficient.**
    
3. **Protect My Friends.**
    
4. **Don't Make Director Lucious Mad.**
    
5. **Don't Be Afraid of Jane Doe.**
    

The cooking competition was simply one method of earning a ticket.

Winning did not guarantee eventual Night Stalker authorization.

Five tickets would only make someone **eligible for consideration**.

# 16. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The ticket system is confusing.

It is inefficient.

It has generated unnecessary paperwork.

It has caused several MSAC operators to become unexpectedly competitive about cooking.

It has also caused at least one senior engineer to threaten to establish a competing **"Neko Engineering Merit Token System"**, which was immediately rejected by C.O.R.E.

Despite this, Neri appears satisfied.

The system accomplishes its intended purpose:

**A Night Stalker is not something personnel are entitled to simply because they want one.**

It is something Neri chooses to entrust to people she considers worthy.

The ticket is therefore not a partial weapon.

It is a partial **recommendation.**

Five recommendations are required before Neri will even consider issuing the weapon.
