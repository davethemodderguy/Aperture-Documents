### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / ANOMALOUS PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

# **COREY — MANIFESTATION AVOIDANCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Personnel Welfare / Anomalous Risk  
**Subject:** Corey / Luna / Solaria  
**Status:** **ACTIVE CONCERN**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corey's condition has continued to deteriorate.

Rather than allowing herself to remain in her normal state, Corey has begun deliberately forcing herself into either **Luna** or **Solaria** for increasingly long periods.

This behavior is no longer primarily about controlling her transformations.

It appears to be an attempt to avoid being **Corey**.

---

# **THE HATS**

Corey has also stopped wearing hats.

Previously, hats were one of her most consistent habits.

She commonly wore:

- Beanies.
    
- Berets.
    
- Soft hats.
    
- Her enlarged Mart top hat.
    
- Arcturus's fedora.
    
- Occasionally another Mart.
    

The practical purpose was partly to control her exposure to moonlight and sunlight.

By covering her head, Corey could prevent accidental transitions into Luna or Solaria.

That behavior has now stopped.

---

# **CURRENT BEHAVIOR**

Corey no longer appears interested in preventing transformation.

Instead:

> **She wants to transform.**

If she notices sunlight, she may allow herself to become Solaria.

If moonlight is available, she may allow herself to become Luna.

Even without either source, she can now intentionally transform.

---

# **WHY**

Corey has not directly explained the behavior.

However, her actions suggest that she finds being Luna or Solaria easier than remaining herself.

When manifested:

- The Marts can safely approach.
    
- Her emotional instability is greatly reduced.
    
- She does not experience the same overwhelming sadness.
    
- She appears more confident.
    
- She can sleep more peacefully.
    
- She does not have to confront the memory as directly.
    

The manifestations appear to provide temporary relief.

---

# **LUNA'S CONCERN**

Luna has recognized the problem.

During one manifestation, she stated:

> "...She's using us to hide."

The researcher asked what she meant.

Luna answered:

> "...She doesn't want to be Corey."

She looked down.

> "...Not right now."

---

# **SOLARIA'S CONCERN**

Solaria was more direct.

> "...She keeps forcing us out."

When asked whether she disliked being manifested:

> "...No."

Pause.

> "...That's not the problem."

Solaria's expression became serious.

> "...She shouldn't have to disappear to feel okay."

---

# **THE MARTS RETURN**

The change is immediately noticeable whenever Corey becomes Luna or Solaria.

Marts cautiously approach again.

They float toward Luna.

They gather around Solaria.

They sit on their shoulders.

They follow them through the SAFEHOUSE.

The Marts appear relieved.

But the moment Corey returns:

They retreat.

This has become one of the clearest indicators of Corey's emotional state.

---

# **A MART'S RESPONSE**

One Mart approached Solaria.

> "...Corey?"

Solaria looked at it.

> "...She's here."

The Mart looked around.

> "...Where?"

Solaria placed a hand over her chest.

> "...Here."

The Mart became quiet.

> "...Okay."

---

# **THE HAT INCIDENT**

A staff member offered Corey one of her old soft hats.

Corey stared at it.

She did not take it.

> "...I don't need it anymore."

The staff member asked why.

Corey replied:

> "...I don't want to stop them."

The statement was interpreted as referring to Luna and Solaria.

---

# **LUNA AND SOLARIA'S POSITION**

Neither manifestation wants Corey to use them as a permanent escape.

They do not blame her.

They understand why she is doing it.

But both recognize the danger.

Luna:

> "...We are parts of her."

Solaria:

> "...We're not replacements for her."

Luna:

> "...She needs to come back."

---

# **SHARED BODY — SHARED PAIN**

The three forms remain connected.

Corey cannot truly escape herself by becoming Luna or Solaria.

They share:

- Memories.
    
- Experiences.
    
- Relationships.
    
- Emotional history.
    

Changing form changes how Corey experiences those things.

It does not erase them.

---

# **CURRENT PATTERN**

The following cycle has become increasingly common:

**Corey → Luna → Solaria → Luna → Solaria → Luna**

Corey remains in her normal state for progressively shorter periods.

The SAFEHOUSE has begun referring to this privately as:

> **"The Cycle."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corey used to wear hats because she wanted control.

A hat meant:

> **"I want to stay Corey."**

Now the hats are gone.

She no longer blocks the moon.

She no longer blocks the sun.

She does not wait for Luna or Solaria to appear.

She simply becomes them.

Again.

And again.

And again.

Luna and Solaria can feel what Corey is doing.

They know she is not choosing them because she is happy.

She is choosing them because being herself hurts.

And neither of them wants to become a place for Corey to hide forever.

**End of Personnel Welfare / Anomalous Psychological Observation**
