# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – FAMILY PHOTOGRAPH AVOIDANCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

One of the most persistent manifestations of Corin Lockwood's trauma is her refusal to participate in new family photographs.

Since reuniting with Norma, Arcturus, and Lumi, numerous opportunities have arisen to recreate the family portrait taken before Corin's abduction.

Every invitation has been declined.

Not out of resentment.

But because Corin believes the original family no longer exists.

---

# **CORIN'S PERSPECTIVE**

During therapy, Corin explained her reasoning.

> "...I hate being lied to."

When asked what lie she believed people were telling her, she answered without hesitation.

> "...That everything can go back to normal."

She looked at the old family photograph resting on the table.

> "...It can't."

Corin pointed toward the image of the young girl smiling beside her mother.

> "...She's gone."

After a long silence, she quietly added,

> "...If we take another picture..."

> "...it won't be the same family."

Psychological Services note that this reflects Corin's deeply held belief about herself and her past, rather than an objective assessment of her family's relationships.

---

# **THE PHOTOGRAPH**

The original family portrait remains heavily damaged.

Years earlier, during a severe panic episode, Corin crossed out both her own face and her parents' faces.

The photograph has never been repaired.

Lumi chose not to replace it.

She keeps it carefully stored instead.

Not because it is intact.

But because it is real.

---

# **THE INVITATIONS**

Norma has asked three times.

Arcturus has asked twice.

Lucy quietly suggested it once during a SAFEHOUSE gathering.

Even Belle gently offered to take the picture herself.

Each time, Corin smiled apologetically.

Each time, she declined.

Her answer never changed.

> "...I'm sorry."

> "...I can't."

No one pressured her further.

---

# **LUMI**

One evening, Lumi found Corin looking at the old photograph.

Corin traced the edge of the frame with her finger.

> "...Everyone keeps wanting a new picture."

Lumi nodded.

> "Because they love you."

Corin closed her eyes.

> "...They want to pretend nothing happened."

Lumi gently shook her head.

> "No."

She sat beside her daughter.

> "They know exactly what happened."

Another pause.

> "They don't want a picture because the past can be undone."

Lumi smiled softly.

> "They want one because, after everything that happened..."

> "...we're together again."

Corin stared at the photograph for a long time.

A tear fell onto the cracked glass.

> "...I'm not ready."

Lumi wrapped an arm around her shoulders.

> "Then we won't take one today."

> "Or tomorrow."

> "The camera can wait."

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services do not recommend encouraging or pressuring Corin to recreate the family portrait.

For Corin, the act symbolizes accepting that she still belongs in the family.

At present, she continues to struggle with that belief.

Therapeutic efforts remain focused on helping Corin recognize that the family's love for her is not dependent on her appearance, her past, or what was done to her.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Lockwood family has chosen to leave the matter entirely in Corin's hands.

The camera remains untouched.

The opportunity remains open.

No deadline has ever been set.

Lumi summarized the family's decision simply.

> "The day we take that picture..."

> "...I want it to be because my daughter wanted to stand beside us."

> "...Not because she felt she had to."

**End of Psychological Observation Report**
