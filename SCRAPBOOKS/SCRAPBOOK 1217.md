---

# 1. DEVELOPMENT INITIATION

Following the completion and distribution of the **Deadeye** and **FSR Deadbolt** programs, Neri Tachyon has initiated development of the next Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise platform.

The selected category is:

> **ASSAULT RIFLE**

Neri has formally returned to the Aperture Armory for comparative research material.

Her selected research pool consisted of:

- **SCAR-L ×5**
    
- **M4A1 ×2**
    
- **G3 ×2**
    
- **MCX ×5**
    

All equipment was removed under authorized development procedures and transferred for supervised research.

No final Tachyon Industries design has yet been announced.

---

# 2. RESEARCH OBJECTIVE

Neri has not specified a final configuration.

The collected platforms are being examined comparatively.

The current research appears to focus on characteristics including:

- ergonomics;
    
- platform architecture;
    
- handling;
    
- modularity;
    
- control placement;
    
- compatibility with existing Tachyon Industries design philosophy;
    
- material selection;
    
- accessory integration;
    
- and general platform behavior.
    

Neri has stated only:

> **“I want to make an AR.”**

When asked which one:

> **“Mine.”**

No further clarification was provided.

---

# 3. NORA — OFFICIAL OBSERVER

Nora Tachyon has been designated the project's official observer.

Her responsibilities are technically limited to:

> **Observe.**

She is not the designer.

She is not the engineer.

She is not authorized to independently alter the research equipment.

She is, however, extremely enthusiastic about the project.

Nora has also developed an unofficial secondary responsibility:

> **Spoiling the project.**

---

# 4. THE SPOILER PROBLEM

Nora has discovered that information about Neri's projects has significant value to MSAC personnel.

MSAC personnel, in turn, have discovered that Nora accepts:

> **Cake**

as a form of information exchange.

This has resulted in an extremely predictable cycle.

### DAY 1

MSAC:

> “Nora, what is Neri making?”

Nora:

> “I don't know.”

MSAC:

> “Want cake?”

Nora:

> “...What kind?”

---

### DAY 2

Nora arrives at the cafeteria.

A slice of cake is placed in front of her.

Nora eats it.

Five minutes later:

> **NORA:**  
> “Sis is looking at the SCAR.”

The MSAC personnel immediately begin taking notes.

---

# 5. CAKE-BASED INTELLIGENCE OPERATIONS

The practice has become sufficiently common to warrant documentation.

Nora has reportedly revealed information including:

- which platform Neri was studying;
    
- whether Neri was currently sketching;
    
- whether she was working with metal samples;
    
- whether a prototype existed;
    
- whether Neri had rejected a particular idea;
    
- whether Neri was currently happy with a design;
    
- and, on one occasion, the fact that Neri had stayed awake late working on something.
    

The information is generally incomplete.

It is nevertheless remarkably accurate.

---

# 6. MSAC INTERROGATION ATTEMPT

An MSAC operator attempted to obtain detailed information.

> **MSAC:**  
> “Nora, what does it look like?”

> **NORA:**  
> “A gun.”

> **MSAC:**

> **NORA:**

> **NORA:**

> **MSAC:**  
> “Nora.”

> **MSAC:**  
> “You already ate cake.”

> **NORA:**  
> “That was yesterday's cake.”

The operator reportedly surrendered.

---

# 7. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri became aware of the information leaks.

She looked at Nora.

> **NERI:**  
> “Are you telling people what I'm making?”

Nora froze.

> **NORA:**  
> “...Maybe.”

Neri stared.

Nora slowly added:

> **“They gave me cake.”**

Neri remained silent.

Several seconds passed.

> **NERI:**  
> “Classic Nora.”

Nora smiled.

No disciplinary action was recorded.

# 11. NERI'S SECURITY MEASURES

Neri has attempted to limit the information available to Nora.

This has proven ineffective.

Nora can simply observe the project.

Furthermore, Nora's memory appears exceptionally good when the subject interests her.

Attempts to distract her have produced limited success.

One technician reportedly attempted:

> **“Look, Nora! Cake!”**

Nora looked at the cake.

Then immediately looked back toward Neri's workstation.

> **NORA:**  
> “That's distraction.”

The technician asked how she knew.

> **NORA:**  
> “Because you said it like that.”

---

# 12. MSAC INFORMATION MARKET

A small informal market has developed around project information.

Current exchange rates are unofficial.

Examples include:

> **1 slice of cake**  
> = vague progress update

> **2 slices**  
> = platform being studied

> **3 slices**  
> = prototype status

> **4 slices**  
> = highly specific spoiler

Nora has denied that this constitutes bribery.

Her reasoning:

> **“It's not bribery if it's cake.”**

C.O.R.E. disagrees.
