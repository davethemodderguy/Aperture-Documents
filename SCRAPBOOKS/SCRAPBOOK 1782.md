**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — SECTOR 4-C

---

## 1. STATUS UPDATE

Jake has transitioned from his fully feline form into a **half-human, half-cat hybrid form**.

This form is expected to remain his primary appearance for the time being.

Current observable characteristics include:

- His usual humanoid body and proportions.
    
- White hair.
    
- Bright azure eyes with luminous shooting-star patterns.
    
- Feline ears atop his head.
    
- A feline tail.
    
- Retained feline behavioral tendencies, including purring, grooming his hands, and seeking warm, comfortable places to rest.
    

Jake retains his usual identity, memories, personality, and ability to communicate.

He has not returned to his previous entirely human appearance.

C.O.R.E. has not established whether the hybrid form is permanent, temporary, or subject to voluntary alteration.

**Current classification:** Human–feline hybrid manifestation.

**Current welfare status:** Stable.

---

## 2. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake appears to have accepted the change without significant concern.

Upon noticing his new form, he examined his hands, touched his ears, and looked over his shoulder at his tail.

He moved it once.

It moved again.

Jake stared at it.

**JAKE:** "Oh."

He attempted to catch it.

He missed.

He tried again.

He missed again.

After approximately thirty seconds, he abandoned the experiment and returned to his cat bed.

His teddy bear was already waiting there.

Jake settled beside it, tucked his tail around himself, and began reading.

No further comment was made.

C.O.R.E. considers this a reasonable response.

---

## 3. RESIDENTIAL ARRANGEMENTS

Jake will continue residing in his own private SAFEHOUSE residence.

His existing cat bed remains available.

His ordinary meals will continue unchanged, subject to his medical needs.

His teddy bear must remain within reach.

Personnel are reminded that Jake's hybrid form does not make him a pet, an exhibit, or a communal mascot.

He may receive affection when he welcomes it.

He may also spend time alone without being disturbed.

The presence of feline ears and a tail does not grant residents unrestricted permission to touch either.

This point has been emphasized following several inquiries from personnel who apparently considered his tail an invitation.

It is not.

---

## 4. PHOENIX'S REACTION

Phoenix was informed that Jake had returned to a partly humanoid form.

She entered his room after receiving permission.

Jake was sitting on his cat bed, holding his teddy bear.

His white hair was slightly untidy.

His feline ears twitched at the sound of the door.

His tail curled around his side.

Phoenix stopped in the doorway.

**PHOENIX:** "...Oh."

Jake looked up.

**JAKE:** "What?"

**PHOENIX:** "Nothing."

His ears tilted toward her.

Phoenix pressed both hands against her mouth.

**JAKE:** "You're staring."

**PHOENIX:** "I'm trying not to."

**JAKE:** "Why?"

Phoenix took a slow breath.

**PHOENIX:** "Because you're still a catboy."

Jake looked at his ears.

Then at his tail.

**JAKE:** "Yes."

A pause.

**JAKE:** "Is that bad?"

Phoenix immediately shook her head.

**PHOENIX:** "No! Absolutely not!"

Jake considered her response.

Then he held out one hand.

Phoenix approached and gently patted his head.

His ears lowered slightly.

A quiet purr escaped him.

Phoenix froze.

Jake looked at her.

**JAKE:** "...You can stop."

Phoenix withdrew her hand immediately.

**PHOENIX:** "Sorry."

Jake watched her for a moment.

Then he caught the edge of her sleeve with one hand.

**JAKE:** "Didn't say you had to."

Phoenix sat beside the bed.

Jake leaned against her arm.

His tail curled around his own legs, and his purring resumed.

Phoenix looked like she had just been granted a personal audience with the universe.

C.O.R.E. personnel present were instructed not to make sudden movements.

---

## 5. MEDICAL AND ANOMALOUS MONITORING

C.O.R.E. will continue monitoring Jake's hybrid form for changes in:

- Physical stability.
    
- Energy expenditure.
    
- Sleep and appetite.
    
- Feline sensory responses.
    
- Tail and ear movement.
    
- Any discomfort associated with the transformation.
    

Monitoring will remain proportionate and non-invasive wherever possible.

No attempt will be made to force Jake into either fully human or fully feline form for convenience.

His current appearance is not, by itself, evidence of medical deterioration.

Any genuine symptoms will be assessed on their own merits.

---

## 6. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Jake is currently a hybrid.

He has a human body, feline ears, a tail, bright azure eyes, white hair, and several increasingly familiar cat-like habits.

He still has his room.

He still has his cat bed.

He still has his teddy bear.

He still eats normal food.

And Phoenix is still delighted.

The primary difference is that Jake can now sit upright in his bed, hold a book in both hands, and flick his tail at someone who annoys him.

Jerry has already been warned not to interpret the tail flicking as an invitation to bring canned cat food.

**The warning remains in effect.**

---

### C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

At the end of the observation period, Jake was sitting against his pillows with his teddy bear in his lap.

Phoenix sat nearby, reading quietly.

Jake's ears occasionally turned toward the sound of a page being turned.

His tail moved lazily beside him.

After several minutes, he leaned against Phoenix's shoulder.

She did not interrupt his reading.

He did not interrupt hers.

Eventually, Jake's eyes began to close.

**PHOENIX:** "Sleepy?"

Jake nodded.

**PHOENIX:** "Want me to go?"

Jake shook his head.

Phoenix stayed.

Jake fell asleep with his teddy bear tucked against his chest and his tail curled comfortably around his legs.

For now, the hybrid form remains.

And for now, Jake is content to simply be himself.
