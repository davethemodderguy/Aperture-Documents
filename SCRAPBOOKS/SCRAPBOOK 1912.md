## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Two of SAFEHOUSE's youngest residents have recently demonstrated an unexpected interest in combat.

Sora and Mia, previously known primarily for their close sibling relationship and shared history, have begun participating in combat-oriented activities.

The development was encouraged by Tom and Jake Akana, who used **Genesis Construction** to create a welcome gift for the two children.

The gift was not a toy collection.

It was a set of weapons.

Tom and Jake apparently considered this an appropriate gesture.

C.O.R.E. has elected to document the resulting equipment, its intended users, and the concerns raised by introducing weapons to two young residents.

The assignments were based on the children's respective fighting characteristics and personal backgrounds:

- **Sora:** One pistol and two ballistic daggers.
    
- **Mia:** One katana.
    

The choices were deliberate. Sora's speed and agility made a lightweight, mobile fighting arrangement suitable for him. Mia's weapon was selected with consideration for her Japanese origins and the fighting style associated with the katana.

Neither child has been formally designated combat personnel.

---

## 2. GENESIS CONSTRUCTION — THE WELCOME GIFT

Tom and Jake jointly manifested the equipment through Genesis Construction.

The construction was reportedly intended as a gift rather than an operational requisition. Both brothers participated in the decision, although their individual contributions to the final designs have not yet been formally documented.

The resulting weapons were presented to Sora and Mia.

Jake was particularly enthusiastic.

**JAKE:** We made something for you!

Sora looked at the objects.

**SORA:** For us?

**TOM:** A welcome gift.

Mia examined the katana.

**MIA:** Is this mine?

**JAKE:** Yep!

**SORA:** And those?

Tom indicated the pistol and two daggers.

**TOM:** Yours.

Sora looked between the weapons.

**SORA:** All three?

**JAKE:** You get three!

**TOM:** Jake wanted to give you more.

**JAKE:** Because he needs options!

Tom gave Jake a look.

**TOM:** He needs training first.

Jake's enthusiasm did not appear diminished.

## 3. EQUIPMENT ASSIGNMENTS

### 3.1 Sora — Mobile Combat Configuration

<text weight="semibold">Assigned equipment: One pistol and two ballistic daggers.</text>

Sora's fighting arrangement was designed around mobility.

He is fast, agile, and generally more suited to movement than prolonged exchanges of force. His equipment therefore emphasizes a lightweight configuration that allows him to remain mobile.

**Pistol**

The pistol provides a ranged option, allowing Sora to engage targets without immediately closing distance.

**Two Ballistic Daggers**

The paired daggers provide close-range options when maintaining distance is no longer possible.

Their arrangement is consistent with Sora's agility and ability to maneuver around opponents.

Sora appeared particularly interested in the daggers.

**SORA:** They're light.

**TOM:** They're supposed to be.

**SORA:** I can move with them.

**JAKE:** See? We picked right!

Sora gave a small smile.

**C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT:** The configuration is thematically consistent with Sora's observed physical characteristics. Actual combat suitability remains unverified.

### 3.2 Mia — Katana Configuration

<text weight="semibold">Assigned equipment: One katana.</text>

Mia received a katana, selected in consideration of her Japanese origins.

The weapon provides a distinct fighting identity separate from Sora's mobile, multi-weapon configuration.

Mia appeared immediately interested in its appearance.

**MIA:** A katana...

**JAKE:** It's yours!

**MIA:** It's really pretty.

**TOM:** It isn't a decoration.

Mia looked at him.

**MIA:** I know.

She carefully examined the weapon.

**SORA:** Do you like it?

**MIA:** I love it.

Sora smiled.

**SORA:** Good.

**C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT:** The katana is a personal equipment assignment, not evidence of established swordsmanship. Mia's proficiency must be assessed independently.

## 4. OBSERVATION — TWO DIFFERENT FIGHTING IDENTITIES

The equipment assignments produce two noticeably different combat profiles.

|Subject|Equipment|Intended characteristic|
|---|---|---|
|Sora|Pistol and two ballistic daggers|Speed, agility, ranged and close-range flexibility|
|Mia|Katana|Dedicated melee configuration|

The distinction is intentional.

Sora's arrangement prioritizes adaptability and movement. Mia's arrangement gives her a single primary weapon with a recognizable fighting identity.

Neither configuration establishes that one child is more capable than the other.

C.O.R.E. also emphasizes that weapon possession does not establish combat readiness.

Both children remain minors. Any practical training must be age-appropriate, supervised, and conducted with appropriate safety measures. Their equipment must be secured when not in supervised use.

Genesis Construction can create equipment.

It does not automatically create the judgment, discipline, or experience required to use it safely.

---

## 5. WELFARE AND SECURITY CONSIDERATIONS

The decision to provide weapons to Sora and Mia raises several concerns.

1. **Supervision:** Neither child is permitted to conduct unsupervised live-fire or live-blade exercises.
    
2. **Storage:** Weapons must be secured when not in use.
    
3. **Training:** No assumptions may be made about proficiency based on physical characteristics or cultural background.
    
4. **Psychological readiness:** Both children have endured significant trauma. Combat participation must never become an expectation or obligation.
    
5. **Voluntary participation:** Either child may decline training or request that their equipment be removed without penalty.
    

C.O.R.E. additionally reminds Tom and Jake that Genesis Construction is not a substitute for responsible equipment handling.

The fact that the weapons were created instantaneously does not make their consequences any less real.

---

## 6. CLOSING NOTE — A GIFT FROM TWO BROTHERS

There is something rather unusual about the scene.

Two brothers used a power capable of turning imagination into reality to prepare gifts for two younger children.

One received a pistol and a pair of daggers.

The other received a katana.

Jake was delighted with the result. Tom was considerably more concerned about the responsibilities that came with it.

Sora seemed pleased that his equipment allowed him to move freely.

Mia seemed happy simply holding something that had been made specifically for her.

For the moment, that is what the gift represents.

Not a recruitment order. Not an expectation that they must fight. Not a declaration that their childhood is over.

Just two brothers saying:

_We thought about you. We made something for you. Welcome home._

C.O.R.E. will permit the equipment to remain assigned under supervised conditions.

We will also be keeping a close eye on the two individuals who decided that giving children weapons was an appropriate welcome gift.

**Especially the enthusiastic one.**

— **C.O.R.E. Security & Welfare Oversight**
