# SAFEHOUSE EQUIPMENT ASSIGNMENT

**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**EQUIPMENT:** Mechanical Compound Magazine-Fed Crossbow  
**ISSUED BY:** Neko & Curious Light  
**ADDITIONAL EQUIPMENT:** Arrow Quiver  
**STATUS:** ASSIGNED  
**CURRENT SUPERVISION:** RECOMMENDED

---

# 1. Equipment Assignment

Following Neri's successful introduction to archery, Neko and Curious Light decided that a conventional bow was apparently not sufficiently interesting.

They provided Neri with a **mechanical compound magazine-fed crossbow**.

Alongside the crossbow, Neri received a dedicated quiver of arrows.

The weapon combines mechanical assistance with a compound-style bow system and a magazine-fed mechanism, allowing Neri to fire multiple projectiles without manually nocking every arrow.

Neri was extremely pleased.

---

# 2. Administrative Consequences

Guiding Light was **not** pleased.

Miss Neko was also **not** pleased.

Both immediately questioned why two people had collectively decided that giving a ten-year-old with numerous concealed knives, a Starlight katana, exceptional combat ability, and newly acquired material manipulation powers a sophisticated ranged weapon was a good idea.

The current situation can therefore be summarized as:

**Neri:** Happy.

**Neko:** Happy.

**Curious Light:** Happy.

**Guiding Light:** Scolding.

**Miss Neko:** Also scolding.

The scolding is ongoing.

---

# 3. Neri's Reaction

Neri immediately wanted to understand how the mechanism worked.

She inspected the crossbow.

Then the magazine.

Then the arrows.

Then the mechanical components.

Then she asked Neko several technical questions.

This was not particularly surprising.

Neri's interest in chemistry and materials science makes her unusually curious about the physical properties of her equipment.

She does not simply want to know:

> "How do I use this?"

She wants to know:

> **"Why does it work?"**

---

# 4. The Chemistry Problem

Neri is exceptionally talented at chemistry.

This has become relevant to her archery training.

She already understands materials unusually quickly, and her newly acquired anomalous ability allows her to manipulate matter at a molecular level.

Consequently, Neri possesses an unusual combination of:

**Chemistry knowledge + material manipulation + archery skill.**

C.O.R.E. considers this combination potentially significant.

Not necessarily because Neri is going to weaponize everything she sees.

Rather, because she understands materials well enough to immediately recognize when something is behaving differently from how it should.

---

# 5. Supervised Experimentation

Neko established a strict rule:

> **Neri may experiment with chemistry.**

Then added:

> **"You tell me before you test anything."**

Neri agreed.

This rule is especially important now.

Neri has previously demonstrated the ability to manipulate materials into unusual physical states, including:

- liquid Nekonium without melting,
    
- solid mercury,
    
- and non-radioactive uranium.
    

Giving her a sophisticated projectile weapon creates an entirely new category of things she might become curious about.

Therefore:

**No unsupervised chemical modifications.**

**No unexplained ammunition experiments.**

**No turning the laboratory into something Neko has to explain to Miss Neko.**

---

# 6. The Quiver

The accompanying quiver contains conventional arrows suitable for Neri's training and regular use.

Neri was reportedly more interested in examining the construction of the arrows than immediately firing them.

She asked questions about their:

- materials,
    
- balance,
    
- weight,
    
- structural properties,
    
- and interaction with different surfaces.
    

Star and Nightshade approved of the curiosity.

Neko approved of the engineering questions.

Miss Neko remained suspicious.

---

# 7. Training Progression

Neri's progression has therefore been somewhat unusual.

### Stage I — Melee

Neri's natural combat specialty.

### Stage II — Conventional Archery

Taught by Star and Nightshade.

Neri learned exceptionally quickly.

### Stage III — Mechanical Crossbow

Provided by Neko and Curious Light.

Neri immediately began learning its operation and characteristics.

### Stage IV — Chemistry

Already an established specialty.

Neri can understand unfamiliar materials rapidly and apply her knowledge to practical problems.

---

# 8. Instructor Assessment

Star and Nightshade both agree that the crossbow is a natural extension of Neri's newly developed ranged-combat capability.

Neri no longer needs to rely exclusively on conventional archery.

However, the instructors emphasized that equipment does not replace fundamentals.

Neri still needs to understand:

**positioning, awareness, judgment, and restraint.**

Her ability to manipulate matter does not remove the need for good decisions.

---

# 9. Neko's Assessment

Neko's assessment was considerably simpler.

> **"She'll figure it out."**

Miss Neko immediately responded:

> **"THAT IS NOT REASSURING."**

Curious Light apparently agreed with Neko.

Guiding Light did not.

---

# 10. Current SAFEHOUSE Classification

Neri's current combat equipment now includes:

- concealed knives,
    
- blade hairclips,
    
- Starlight katana,
    
- conventional bow training,
    
- mechanical compound magazine-fed crossbow,
    
- quiver of arrows.
    

Her projectile-based combat preference remains firmly centered around archery and crossbow use.

Firearms remain largely uninteresting to her.

Her reasoning has not changed:

> **"Too far."**

---

## 11. Final Note

Neri's greatest advantage may not actually be the crossbow.

It may be the fact that she is a **very good chemist.**

Give most people an unusual material and they ask what it is.

Give Neri an unusual material and she asks:

> **"What can I make it do?"**

Then she starts thinking.

And somewhere nearby, Neko usually realizes that another safety form is about to become necessary.

Meanwhile, Guiding Light and Miss Neko continue asking the same question:

> **"WHY DID YOU GIVE HER THAT?"**

Neko and Curious Light have declined to provide a satisfactory answer.

They appear to believe the answer is self-evident.

**They thought it would be cool.**
