## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **ENTITY INTERACTION GUIDELINE**

# **Z-335 — MART HANDLING PROCEDURE**

**Classification:** Level 3 – Allied Entity Interaction  
**Subject:** Z-335 Marts  
**Status:** Approved Interaction

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Marts are highly social entities and generally enjoy physical interaction with trusted individuals.

One of the most common forms of interaction is:

> **Petting.**

Due to their soft magical water composition, Marts are safe to touch and often actively seek out attention.

---

# **PETTING PROCEDURE**

Personnel are permitted to pet Marts.

Recommended areas include:

- Upper body.
    
- Sides.
    
- Back.
    
- Around the antlers (with permission).
    

Marts commonly respond by:

- Relaxing.
    
- Floating closer.
    
- Making happy sounds.
    
- Remaining near the person afterward.
    

---

# **TOP HAT WARNING**

Personnel are reminded:

> **Do not knock off a Mart's top hat.**

A Mart's tiny top hat is not merely an accessory.

It is considered part of their physical and magical structure.

Accidentally removing or displacing the hat can cause severe instability.

---

# **PREVIOUS INCIDENT**

A previous incident occurred when a personnel member attempted to pet a Mart too aggressively.

The movement accidentally knocked the Mart's top hat off.

The Mart immediately destabilized.

The event resulted in the Mart bursting.

Fortunately:

- The colony recovered.
    
- No permanent loss occurred.
    
- The Mart returned through the colony's regeneration process.
    

The incident changed interaction guidelines permanently.

---

# **CORRECT MART PETTING**

Approved:

> Gentle pats.

> Slow strokes.

> Careful handling.

> Respecting the top hat.

Not approved:

> Scratching the hat.

> Pulling the hat.

> Trying to wear the hat.

> "Testing if it comes off."

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

Most Marts enjoy being petted.

A common response:

> "...Pet."

> "...Happy."

> "...Good."

Some Marts will actively approach personnel and lower themselves slightly, indicating they want attention.

---

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

When informed of the top hat incident, Lumi was noticeably concerned.

She explained:

> "...The hats are important."

When asked why she gave them hats in the first place:

> "...Because they needed one."

No further explanation was provided.

---

# **COREY'S COMMENT**

Corey is especially careful around Marts.

When a researcher attempted to inspect a Mart's hat, Corey immediately stopped them.

> "...Don't."

The researcher asked why.

Corey replied:

> "...Because it's part of them."

---

# **NEKO'S COMMENT**

Neko analyzed the Mart's structure after the incident.

His conclusion:

> "...Hat is not decoration."

After a pause:

> "...Do not remove hat."

---

# **FINAL REMINDER**

Marts are friendly.

Marts are soft.

Marts enjoy attention.

Marts enjoy being petted.

But remember:

The top hat stays on.

A simple rule:

> **Pet the Mart. Not the hat.**

**End of Z-335 Interaction Guideline**
