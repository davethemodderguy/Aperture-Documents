# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE / PERSONNEL CARE DIVISION**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — UNCONFIRMED SIGHTING REPORTS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Age:** 6  
**Species:** Full-Body Rabbit Thiren  
**Status:** **MISSING — POSSIBLE VISUAL CONTACT**

---

# **SUMMARY**

Following fifteen weeks of Alice's disappearance, C.O.R.E. has received a growing number of independent reports describing a small silhouette resembling Alice.

The reports originate from multiple locations.

The witnesses are unrelated.

Several reports were made by personnel who were unaware that other sightings had already occurred.

The descriptions remain remarkably consistent.

A small figure.

Rabbit-like ears.

Small stature.

Usually standing completely still.

And then—

**gone.**

---

# **COMMON DESCRIPTION**

Witnesses consistently describe the silhouette as:

- Approximately Alice's height.
    
- Small and childlike.
    
- Rabbit-like ears.
    
- Wearing clothing resembling Alice's usual outfit.
    
- Occasionally appearing to have something covering her head.
    
- Completely silent.
    
- Usually standing alone.
    
- Often observed at the end of a hallway or through a doorway.
    

Several witnesses specifically reported that the figure appeared to be **looking at its own hands.**

---

# **SIGHTING 01**

**Location:** Residential Corridor C-4  
**Witness:** Maintenance Technician

The technician reported seeing a small figure standing approximately 30 meters away.

The figure appeared to be facing away.

The technician called:

> "Alice?"

The figure turned its head slightly.

The technician looked down briefly to activate their radio.

When they looked back:

Nothing.

The corridor was empty.

No doors had opened.

No footsteps were heard.

---

# **SIGHTING 07**

**Location:** Cafeteria Service Corridor

A security officer reported seeing a small rabbit-like silhouette standing near the cafeteria entrance.

The officer initially assumed it was another child resident.

The figure reportedly remained motionless for several seconds.

The officer approached.

The silhouette moved behind a structural column.

When the officer reached the column:

Nothing.

No exit route was available.

No movement was detected on nearby cameras.

---

A resident reported seeing what appeared to be Alice standing beyond the perimeter fencing.

The witness described the figure as:

# **SIGHTING 18**

**Location:** Blacksite Medical Sector

A medical employee reported seeing a small figure sitting on the floor at the end of a corridor.

The employee believed the child was injured.

They approached slowly.

At approximately ten meters away, the figure appeared to become indistinct.

The employee blinked.

The corridor was empty.

No sound was heard.

---

# **SIGHTING 23**

**Location:** Exterior Loading Area

Two employees independently reported seeing a small rabbit-eared silhouette near a loading structure.

Both described the same behavior.

The figure was:

**Standing still.**

**Looking downward.**

**Hands held together.**

One employee called:

> "Alice?"

The figure reportedly raised its head.

The lights flickered.

The figure was gone.

---

# **PATTERN ANALYSIS**

C.O.R.E. has identified several recurring elements.

### **1. The figure rarely moves while observed.**

### **2. The figure is frequently seen alone.**

### **3. The figure does not appear to respond verbally.**

### **4. Witnesses frequently report seeing the figure near corridors, doors, or thresholds.**

### **5. The figure disappears rapidly when approached.**

### **6. Several witnesses independently described the figure as appearing sad or exhausted.**

### **7. Multiple witnesses reported the figure looking at its hands.**

The consistency of these reports has prevented C.O.R.E. from classifying them as ordinary misidentification.

---

# **SECURITY REVIEW**

Available surveillance footage has been reviewed where possible.

Results are inconclusive.

In several cases, witnesses are visibly reacting to something that cannot be clearly identified on camera.

In other cases, the footage shows a brief obstruction or shadow in the reported location.

No recording has yet provided definitive visual confirmation of Alice.

No confirmed biometric identification has been obtained.

Therefore:

## **Alice has NOT been officially located.**

However:

## **The sightings are considered credible enough to continue investigation.**

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin was shown several witness descriptions.

Corin reportedly became quiet.

Then asked:

> "...Does she run away when people approach?"

The investigator answered:

> "According to most reports, yes."

Corin lowered her head.

> "...Then she probably thinks they're going to be angry."

# **BELLE'S RESPONSE**

Belle has begun carrying carrots during search operations again.

When told that the silhouettes disappear whenever personnel approach, she reportedly answered:

> "Then don't chase her."

Personnel asked what they should do instead.

Belle responded:

> "Just sit down."

---

# **NEW SEARCH DIRECTIVE**

Personnel encountering a possible Alice sighting are instructed:

### **DO NOT CHASE.**

### **DO NOT SHOUT.**

### **DO NOT RUN TOWARD THE FIGURE.**

### **DO NOT CORNER THE FIGURE.**

Instead:

1. Stop moving.
    
2. Remain visible.
    
3. Lower yourself to Alice's approximate eye level if safe.
    
4. Speak calmly.
    
5. Use Alice's name.
    
6. Tell her that nobody is angry.
    
7. Tell her she is not in trouble.
    
8. Allow her to approach voluntarily.
    
9. Notify the search team without sudden movement.
    

Recommended statement:

> **"Alice, it's okay."**

> **"Nobody is angry."**

> **"You don't have to run."**

> **"We're just happy to see you."**

---

# **IMPORTANT CONSIDERATION**

The sightings may not represent Alice.

C.O.R.E. cannot currently determine whether they are:

- Actual sightings of Alice.
    
- Environmental misidentifications.
    
- Anomalous manifestations.
    
- Psychological phenomena.
    
- Something else entirely.
    

Until confirmed, the figure remains designated:

## **UNIDENTIFIED ALICE-LIKE SILHOUETTE**

However, every report is being investigated as though it could be Alice.

The cost of taking a false sighting seriously is minimal.

The cost of dismissing a genuine sighting is not.

# **FINAL NOTE**

Fifteen weeks have passed.

And now people are seeing her.

Or something that looks like her.

A small silhouette at the end of a hallway.

A little figure outside a door.

A rabbit-eared shape standing beneath a light.

Always alone.

Always quiet.

Always gone before anyone can reach her.

Perhaps Alice is trying to come home.

Perhaps she is afraid.

Perhaps she sees people searching for her and hides before they can get close.

Perhaps she is simply standing somewhere, staring at her hands, wondering whether she is still wanted.

No one knows.

But C.O.R.E. has changed the search protocol.

If that little silhouette appears again, personnel are not to chase it.

They are not to grab it.

They are not to demand answers.

They are to sit down.

And wait.

Because if it really is Alice—

the last thing she needs is another person running toward her.

She needs someone to stay still long enough to prove:

**Nobody is going to hurt her.**

**Nobody is going to punish her.**

**Nobody is going to leave her behind.**

**And she can come home.**
