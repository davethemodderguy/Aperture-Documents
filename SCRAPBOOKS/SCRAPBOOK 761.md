# **FIVE WEEKS**

Five weeks have passed.

There is still no Alice.

No confirmed sighting.

No recovered clothing.

No sketch.

No note.

No sound.

No trace.

The search has expanded far beyond the original Blacksite perimeter.

Nothing has been found.

---

# **THE SCOOTER**

Alice's scooter remains docked at the SAFEHOUSE.

The tracker confirms it has not moved since before her disappearance.

It has been checked repeatedly.

The battery is charged.

The tracker is functional.

The scooter itself is undamaged.

This confirms one important detail:

**Alice did not leave on the scooter.**

She walked.

---

# **THE IMPLICATION**

Alice walking away creates a disturbing possibility.

She may have simply followed someone.

She may have wandered.

She may have become distracted.

She may have entered an unfamiliar area.

She may have gotten lost.

And once she realized nobody was with her—

she may have simply stopped somewhere.

Waiting.

---

# **FIVE WEEKS WITHOUT FOOD**

Medical personnel have repeatedly reviewed Alice's known physiological condition.

The conclusion remains the same.

Alice could not reasonably survive five weeks without adequate nutrition.

Her body is too small.

Her reserves are too limited.

Her previous condition makes the situation even more concerning.

---

# **FIVE WEEKS WITHOUT WATER**

The situation becomes even more impossible.

A child cannot ordinarily remain alive for anything approaching this length of time without water.

There should be obvious physiological consequences long before this point.

There are none.

Because there is no body.

No remains.

No evidence of where Alice went.

---

# **THE SEARCH HAS CHANGED**

The Blacksite is no longer searching under the assumption that Alice is simply hiding somewhere.

It is now searching under the assumption that **something about Alice's disappearance is not behaving normally.**

Every previous explanation has failed.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin has stopped asking:

> "Where is she?"

She now asks:

> "What happened to her?"

The distinction matters.

Because Alice should have been found by now.

Or—

at minimum—

there should have been evidence.

There isn't.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle still checks the scooter every morning.

She knows it hasn't moved.

She still checks anyway.

Sometimes she sits beside it.

She talks as though Alice is sitting there listening.

> "You walked, huh?"

> "...You should've taken your scooter."

No response.

---

# **NEKO**

Neko has reconstructed Alice's probable route from the last available records.

The route ends abruptly.

There is no indication of where Alice went afterward.

Neko has reviewed the footage repeatedly.

At one point, he paused the recording.

Alice was walking alone.

Her ears were lowered.

Her hands were empty.

She wasn't running.

She wasn't frightened.

She was simply walking.

Neko stared at the frame.

> "...Where were you going?"

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has expanded the search to locations Alice would normally consider safe.

Places with trees.

Flowers.

Quiet corners.

Warm rooms.

Small hiding spaces.

Anywhere Alice might think:

> **"Nobody busy here."**

Nothing.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

Alice's room remains untouched.

Her bed has not been reassigned.

Her pencils remain in their container.

The new sketchbook remains empty.

The damaged original sketchbook remains beside her bed.

Nobody has thrown it away again.

---

# **CORIN'S RULE**

Corin has established one rule:

> **"Nobody closes Alice's room."**

The door remains open.

The light remains on.

Not because anyone seriously expects Alice to simply walk back in.

But because Corin refuses to let the room become another place where Alice can be forgotten.

---

# **THE IMPOSSIBLE QUESTION**

If Alice truly has been without food and water for five weeks—

then something else must be sustaining her.

Or—

something else has happened to her.

Neither possibility has been confirmed.

C.O.R.E. has therefore classified Alice's disappearance as:

> **ANOMALOUS MISSING PERSON — UNKNOWN MECHANISM**

---

# **PERSONNEL ARE NOT TOLD TO GIVE UP**

The search continues.

No remains have been found.

No evidence of death has been recovered.

No confirmed sighting has been dismissed solely because it seemed impossible.

Every report is investigated.

Every unexplained movement is reviewed.

Every possible hiding place is checked.

Because Alice has already demonstrated that she can disappear from places where people are actively looking for her.

She didn't take her scooter.

She didn't take food.

She didn't take water.
