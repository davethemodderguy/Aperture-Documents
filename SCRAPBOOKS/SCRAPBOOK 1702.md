# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Clover Luminare has moved into Jake's residence.

No relocation order was issued.

No architectural modification was requested.

No construction occurred.

The room simply...

**changed.**

Jake's previously unfurnished, grass-covered living space has spontaneously reorganized itself into two distinct residential areas.

One belongs to Jake.

The other belongs to Clover.

No clear anomalous mechanism has been identified.

More importantly:

> **Neither resident appears particularly concerned about it.**

---

# 1. PRIOR ROOM CONFIGURATION

Jake's room was previously one large, unconventional living space.

It contained:

- grass covering much of the floor;
    
- an old mattress;
    
- assorted plushies;
    
- books;
    
- drawings;
    
- manifested 2D flowers;
    
- miscellaneous objects;
    
- and little conventional furniture.
    

There was no proper bedroom arrangement.

No desk.

No bookshelf.

No wardrobe.

No formal seating area.

Jake considered this normal.

Clover frequently visited.

She gradually began spending longer periods there.

Eventually, she moved in.

---

# 2. THE CHANGE

The transformation occurred overnight.

No structural noise was recorded.

No construction materials were delivered.

No personnel entered the room.

At 06:43, the room remained in its previous configuration.

At 07:12, the internal space had reorganized itself.

The room now contains **two distinct sides.**

There is no physical wall separating them.

No partition.

No curtain.

No boundary line.

Yet the distinction is immediately obvious.

---

# 3. JAKE'S SIDE

Jake's half remains unmistakably Jake's.

The area contains:

- his old mattress;
    
- piles of plushies;
    
- books;
    
- drawings;
    
- patches of grass;
    
- and his manifested 2D blue flowers.
    

The flowers continue growing naturally from Jake's anomalous drawings.

Some are scattered around the mattress.

Others grow beside the plushies.

Several have been arranged into small clusters.

Jake's teddy bear is generally somewhere nearby.

The area remains somewhat messy.

Jake considers this acceptable.

---

# 4. CLOVER'S SIDE

Clover's half is considerably more organized.

It contains:

- a couch;
    
- a desk;
    
- a bookshelf;
    
- accounting materials;
    
- notebooks;
    
- drawing supplies;
    
- several books;
    
- and numerous real four-leaf clovers.
    

The clovers have grown directly from patches of soil placed throughout the area.

They are real.

They are touchable.

They are very much alive.

Clover appears to have accepted the room's spontaneous architectural accommodation without complaint.

She has also arranged her books by subject.

Jake has not.

---

# 5. THE DIVIDING LINE

The most unusual aspect is the boundary.

There is no actual barrier.

Jake can walk into Clover's side.

Clover can walk into Jake's side.

They do so regularly.

The distinction is therefore almost entirely **behavioral and aesthetic.**

Jake's side says:

> **"This is where I exist."**

Clover's side says:

> **"This is where I keep my things organized."**

The two areas coexist approximately one meter apart.

---

# 6. NO KNOWN REASON

C.O.R.E. has no confirmed explanation for the division.

Several hypotheses were considered.

### Hypothesis A — Resident Preference

The room may have responded to the established habits of its occupants.

### Hypothesis B — Anomalous Spatial Adaptation

Jake's manifestation abilities may have unconsciously altered the room to accommodate another permanent occupant.

### Hypothesis C — Clover's Influence

Clover's own anomalous properties may have contributed to the formation.

### Hypothesis D — Psychological Architecture

The room may be reflecting how both residents conceptualize personal space.

### Hypothesis E — Unknown

No explanation currently satisfies all observations.

Clover may sit on Jake's mattress while reading.

They simply return to their respective areas afterward.

The boundary appears to exist primarily for organization.

Not separation.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The room's division should not currently be treated as a security concern.

There is no evidence that the spatial division represents hostility between the residents.

Quite the opposite.

The two occupants cross the informal boundary constantly.

They borrow each other's space.

They sit together.

They work together.

They visit each other's sides.

They simply retain their own environments.

The most accurate description may therefore be:

> **Shared space with separate personal ecosystems.**

Jake has plushies and impossible flowers.

Clover has books, furniture, and real clovers.

Neither side requires the other to disappear.
