### **SEARCH STATUS REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – UNKNOWN LOCATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the abandonment of Shelter T-01, Corin Lockwood's location became unknown.

Unlike previous disappearances, no obvious pattern of movement could be established.

The usual locations were searched.

The forests.

The botanical preserve.

The rooftops.

The abandoned offices.

The lakeside.

Nothing.

Corin had disappeared without leaving another trace.

---

# **SEARCH EFFORTS**

Security initiated a full perimeter sweep.

Engineering deployed drones equipped with thermal imaging.

No useful results were obtained.

Corin's altered physiology continued to complicate conventional search methods.

Medical staff checked hospitals and treatment facilities within the Blacksite.

Researchers reviewed security recordings.

Even Neko volunteered several tracking devices of questionable legality.

None located Corin.

---

# **THE PROBLEM**

Corin knew the Blacksite.

Every maintenance corridor.

Every forgotten service tunnel.

Every neglected observation platform.

She had worked there for years.

If Corin wished to avoid being found...

She knew exactly how.

# **LUMI**

Under ordinary circumstances, Lumi would have been the first person able to locate Corin.

Corin never stayed hidden from her mother for long.

She never truly knew how.

But Lumi's manifestation remained unstable.

She was gone.

The one person Corin would almost certainly have allowed to approach...

Was also the one person who could not search for her.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services expressed concern that Corin's increasing isolation reflected a desire to withdraw completely from those around her rather than any immediate intent that could be inferred from her location alone.

Clinicians warned personnel against interpreting the absence of contact as evidence that Corin no longer cared about others.

Throughout previous incidents, Corin had consistently remained within places familiar to her, even while avoiding direct interaction.

The priority remained locating her safely and re-establishing contact without overwhelming her.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin Lockwood remains unlocated.

Her journal has been recovered.

Her treehouse stands abandoned.

Her room in SAFEHOUSE remains untouched.

Lumi has not yet returned.

For the first time since Corin began withdrawing from SAFEHOUSE...

No one knows where to look next.

**End of Search Status Report**
