### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT REPORT**

# **CORIN — FEATHER EDGES & NEKONIUM WING ARMOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Equipment:** **Nekonium Nano-Forcefield Wing Armor**  
**Status:** **Operational**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Further examination of Corin's wings has revealed that her feathers possess a naturally sharpened structure.

The feathers become progressively thinner and sharper toward their outer edges.

At their most extreme points, the edges are comparable to **knife blades** in sharpness.

This property exists independently of their Ether-based projectile capabilities.

---

# **FEATHER STRUCTURE**

Corin's wing feathers are not uniformly shaped.

Their structure gradually tapers toward the edges.

The outer portions are exceptionally thin and rigid.

Consequently, an exposed wing can potentially cause accidental cuts through ordinary contact.

This became particularly problematic around the SAFEHOUSE.

Personnel were repeatedly reminded:

> **"Don't grab the wings."**

---

# **CORIN'S CONTROL**

Corin has excellent awareness of the feather edges.

She can fold her wings tightly against her back when moving through confined spaces.

She can also adjust the angle of individual feathers to avoid accidentally contacting nearby people.

The feathers remain harmless while dormant unless deliberately launched.

Their sharpness therefore represents a **passive physical hazard**, separate from their Ether payload.

---

# **NEKONIUM WING ARMOR**

Neko and Curious Light developed a specialized protective system for Corin's wings.

The resulting equipment was designated:

> **NEKONIUM NANO-FORCEFIELD WING ARMOR**

The armor is specifically designed around Corin's unusual anatomy.

It does not encase the wings in conventional rigid plating.

Instead, the system uses an extremely lightweight **Nekonium-based nano-forcefield structure** that conforms to the wings as they move.

---

# **DESIGN REQUIREMENTS**

The armor was required to satisfy several conditions.

It had to:

- Protect Corin's wings.
    
- Preserve natural wing movement.
    
- Allow flight.
    
- Allow feather deployment.
    
- Avoid interfering with feather regeneration.
    
- Remain extremely lightweight.
    
- Prevent accidental contact with the sharp feather edges.
    
- Survive Corin's anomalous Ether environment.
    

Conventional armor failed several of these requirements.

Neko therefore proposed a different approach.

---

# **THE FORCEFIELD**

Rather than physically surrounding every feather with heavy material, the system generates an extremely thin protective field around the wing.

The field moves with Corin.

It provides a protective boundary without substantially increasing the wings' effective weight.

Curious Light described the result as:

> **"Armor that behaves more like an invisible second skin."**

---

# **WEIGHT**

The completed system is effectively **near-weightless** from Corin's perspective.

This is particularly important.

Corin's wings already become extremely heavy when saturated with water.

Adding conventional armor would have significantly worsened the problem.

Nekonium avoids this.

The armor provides protection without turning Corin's wings into additional weights.

---

# **FEATHER COMPATIBILITY**

The armor does not prevent Corin from using her feathers normally.

She can still:

- Fold her wings.
    
- Extend them.
    
- Fly.
    
- Launch feathers.
    
- Shed feathers.
    
- Regrow feathers.
    
- Perform rapid wing movements.
    

The forcefield automatically accommodates feathers leaving the wing.

Once a feather separates, the protective field closes around the remaining wing structure.

---

# **ACCIDENTAL CONTACT**

The armor also reduces the likelihood of accidental injury.

Personnel can safely approach Corin's wings without directly contacting the sharpest portions of the feathers.

This is particularly useful when:

- Orphie helps Corin arrange her wings.
    
- Medical personnel inspect the wing roots.
    
- Corin is resting.
    
- Corin is being transported through confined spaces.
    

---

# **NEKO'S CONTRIBUTION**

Neko designed much of the physical integration system.

His primary concern was simple:

> **"She needs to keep her wings."**

The armor was therefore designed around the wings rather than forcing the wings to accommodate the armor.

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT'S CONTRIBUTION**

Curious Light developed the forcefield architecture.

His objective was to make the protective layer effectively disappear from Corin's perspective.

During testing, Corin commented:

> **"I can't even feel it."**

Curious Light smiled.

> **"Good."**

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin initially looked suspiciously at the armor.

> **"Is it heavy?"**

Neko answered:

> **"No."**

She moved her wings.

Nothing felt different.

Corin unfolded them fully.

> **"...Oh."**

She smiled.

> **"I like it."**

She then immediately took off.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The armor has made everyone considerably less nervous around Corin's wings.

Belle is particularly pleased.

> **"Now I can hug her without getting poked!"**

Neko immediately corrected her:

> **"You still ask first."**

Belle nodded.

Corin laughed.

---

# **CURRENT CONFIGURATION**

**Wing feathers:** Naturally sharpened.

**Feather edges:** Knife-like.

**Detached feather:** Dormant until launched.

**Launched feather:** Ether airburst projectile.

**Armor:** Nekonium nano-forcefield.

**Armor weight:** Effectively negligible.

**Flight interference:** None observed.

**Feather deployment interference:** None observed.

**Protection:** Active.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's wings are simultaneously one of her greatest sources of freedom and one of her strangest physical hazards.

They carry her through the sky.

They can carry Orphie.

They can launch Ether-charged feathers.

They can also accidentally cut someone simply because their edges are exceptionally sharp.

Neko and Curious Light therefore decided that the obvious solution was not to change Corin's wings.

It was to protect them.

The result is a nearly weightless armor system that allows Corin to keep using her wings exactly as she normally does.

Corin's assessment was brief:

> **"My wings have armor now."**

Neko replied:

> **"Yep."**

Corin unfolded them proudly.

> **"...Cool."**
