**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal Personnel Notice

# **EQUIPMENT CHANGE**

Following extensive field trials and qualification testing, Security Advisor **Arcturus Lockwood** has officially updated his standard sidearm.

**Previous Primary Sidearm**

- .357 Magnum Revolver
    

**New Primary Sidearm**

- **Arcturus .357** Semi-Automatic Pistol
    

The change was approved after the pistol demonstrated exceptional reliability, accuracy, and compatibility with Arcturus' preferred style of precision shooting.

---

# **PRIMARY SIDEARM**

## **Arcturus .357**

**Type:** Semi-Automatic Pistol

**Caliber:** .357 Magnum

**Designer:** Neko

**Approved by:** Arcturus Lockwood

**Characteristics**

- Glock-inspired ergonomics.
    
- Heavy-duty construction capable of handling .357 Magnum pressures.
    
- Integrated compensator for reduced muzzle rise.
    
- Conventional mechanical operation.
    
- No electronics.
    
- No Ether systems.
    
- No AI assistance.
    
- No transformation mechanisms.
    

Arcturus remarked:

> "It feels familiar."

> "Just faster to reload."

---

# **SECONDARY SIDEARM**

## **.357 Magnum Revolver**

The revolver has **not** been retired.

Instead, it has been reassigned as Arcturus' emergency backup weapon.

He continues to carry it in a secondary holster during operations.

When questioned why he still carries it despite adopting the Arcturus .357, his answer was immediate:

> "Because it's never let me down."

The revolver remains meticulously maintained and ready for use if required.

---

# **PRIMARY LONG GUN**

No changes.

Arcturus continues to carry his modernized **.357 Magnum lever-action rifle** as his primary long gun.
