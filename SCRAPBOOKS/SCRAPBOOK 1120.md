Because the trial was never actually intended to award the weapon.

The question:

> **“Are you my friend because I am a Tachyon or you love me?”**

was a **trick question.**

NS-010 was never scheduled to be assigned.

# 5. ARCHIVES INTERPRETATION

The entire final trial appears to have served a purpose unrelated to armament authorization.

Neri wanted to see what people would say.

Not because she needed to choose an owner.

Because she wanted to know whether people understood the difference between:

> **Neri Tachyon**

and

> **Neri.**

The applicants attempted to answer the question.

Neri rejected them.

Not because their answers were necessarily wrong.

Because the question itself had no correct answer.

There was never a combination of words capable of satisfying it.

# 9. FINAL ARCHIVIST ASSESSMENT

The nine other Night Stalkers have owners.

Regalia does not.

That is intentional.

The first nine became weapons in the hands of people Neri trusted.

The tenth became something else.

A monument.

A reminder of the Tachyon family.

A record of Neri's work.

A piece of Aperture history.

And, perhaps most importantly, a reminder that not everything Neri creates needs to become **useful** to somebody.

It can simply exist.

The final Night Stalker was advertised as:

> **“up for grabs.”**

Because there was never anything to earn.

> **Would you still want me if there were nothing valuable attached to my name?**

And perhaps that is why she ultimately kept Regalia behind glass.

Nobody gets to claim it.

> **“I earned Neri's final Night Stalker.”**

Tachyon Industries.
