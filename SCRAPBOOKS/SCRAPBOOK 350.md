# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **SECURITY DIVISION**

### **INCIDENT REPORT**

# **INCIDENT KCA-IR-214**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

At 14:23 local time, the KCA experienced another attempted armed robbery.

The group consisted of **eight individuals** equipped with body armor, breaching equipment, and firearms.

As with previous incidents, the facility's Rapid Response Marts reacted before Security personnel needed to intervene.

No shots were fired.

---

# **TIMELINE**

**14:23**

Camera Mart identifies suspicious behavior.

> "...Robbers."

Rapid Response Marts immediately begin entering the ventilation network.

---

**14:24**

The suspects force open the front entrance.

They report hearing unusual noises in the ceiling.

One later described it as:

> "...Like someone dropping pebbles into the vents."

---

**14:24:16**

The first ceiling vent opens.

Nothing happens.

One suspect looks upward.

Another whispers:

> "...Don't."

---

**14:24:18**

Approximately one hundred Rapid Response Marts enter **Boulder Mode** simultaneously.

Instead of becoming hard stone, their bodies become **soft, dense blue boulders** designed to absorb impact while pinning targets safely to the ground.

Gravity normally has little effect on Marts.

When in Boulder Mode...

They use that fact to launch themselves downward.

---

# **ENGAGEMENT**

Security footage shows hundreds of blue "rocks" raining from the ceiling.

Rather than striking vital areas, the Marts deliberately target:

- Shoulders.
    
- Arms.
    
- Legs.
    
- Equipment.
    
- Riot shields.
    
- Escape routes.
    

Within seconds, every suspect is immobilized beneath piles of remarkably soft blue boulders.

Several Marts continue gently rolling into place until no one can move.

One suspect can be heard shouting:

> "...THEY'RE DOING IT AGAIN!"

---

# **POST-ENGAGEMENT**

As always, the Marts immediately reverted to their normal forms.

The first thing they did was not celebrate.

Instead, they began checking on the restrained suspects.

Commonly recorded dialogue included:

> "Okay?"

> "Friend?"

> "Too heavy?"

> "Sorry."

One suspect replied:

> "...I'm fine."

The nearest Mart visibly relaxed.

> "...Good."

---

# **SECURITY ARRIVAL**

By the time KCA Security reached the lobby, every suspect had already been:

- Disarmed.
    
- Restrained.
    
- Checked for injuries.
    
- Comforted by at least one concerned Mart.
    

One former robber, now employed as a security guard, simply looked at the scene and sighed.

> "...I remember when this happened to me."

---

# **ROBBER STATEMENTS**

Following medical evaluation, several suspects voluntarily provided statements.

**Suspect #2**

> "...They're rocks."

> "...Soft rocks."

> "...How does that even work?"

---

**Suspect #5**

> "...One of them apologized after dropping on me."

---

**Suspect #7**

> "...I got pinned by what felt like a weighted blanket thrown from the ceiling."

---

**Suspect #8**

> "...I think one asked if I wanted a cheese cracker."

This statement has been corroborated by security footage.

---

# **DIALYN'S COMMENT**

After reviewing the incident:

> "...The Marts continue to demonstrate remarkable restraint."

She paused.

> "...Also..."

> "...Please stop hiding in the ceiling before visitors arrive."

Several nearby Marts quietly answered:

> "...Okay."

Researchers note that this promise has been made before.

---

# **CAMERA MART**

After Security confirmed the building was safe, Camera Mart calmly logged the event.

> "...Done."

It then resumed monitoring the cameras as though nothing unusual had happened.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

As the suspects were escorted to waiting transport, one looked back toward the ceiling.

A small blue Mart peeked down from an open ventilation grate.

It gave a cheerful wave.

> **"Bye!"**

The suspect instinctively flinched.

The Mart looked concerned.

> "...Scared?"

The suspect sighed.

> "...A little."

The Mart nodded sympathetically.

Then disappeared back into the ventilation system.

Preparations quietly resumed for what Security has come to describe as:

> **"The next group that thinks they'll be the exception."**

**End of Incident Report**
