# 1. DEVELOPMENT

Nora's adaptation to extreme cold has progressed beyond simple resistance.

She is now demonstrating **active cryokinetic control.**

The change appears to be a natural extension of her established specialty:

> **Adaptation.**

Her body did not merely learn to survive sub-zero conditions.

It learned how to **control them.**

Nora can now manipulate ice and localized freezing effects with conscious intent.

---

# 2. FOOTSTEP EFFECT

The most common manifestation is extremely subtle.

When Nora walks across a normal floor, each footstep leaves behind a thin layer of frost.

The effect usually lasts only a few seconds before dissipating.

A typical trail resembles:

**step → frost → step → frost → step → frost**

The resulting pattern is usually small enough to be harmless.

Nora herself finds it amusing.

---

# 3. OBSERVED INCIDENT

Nora was walking through the SAFEHOUSE corridor.

A staff member noticed a series of small frozen footprints behind her.

> **STAFF:**  
> “Nora?”

Nora stopped.

She looked down.

Then behind herself.

A thin line of frost covered the floor.

> **NORA:**  
> “Oh.”

> **STAFF:**  
> “Are you doing that?”

Nora thought for a moment.

She lifted one foot.

The frost beneath it became slightly thicker.

> **NORA:**  
> “...Yes.”

She put her foot down.

The frost spread outward in a tiny circle.

Nora stared at it.

> **NORA:**  
> “That's new.”

- decorative shapes;

Nora appears to find the last category particularly entertaining.

C.O.R.E. previously classified Nora as possessing unusual cold resistance.

That classification is now incomplete.

Nora has effectively developed a second expression of her adaptation ability.

She is no longer simply:

> **Resistant to cold.**

> **A localized source of controlled freezing.**

> “Pretty.”

- stairs;
