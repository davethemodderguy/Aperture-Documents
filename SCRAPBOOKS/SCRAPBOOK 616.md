# **OVERVIEW**

The **"Things That ALMOST Destroyed the Sky Fortress"** museum is one of the Blacksite's stranger historical collections.

The museum exists to preserve objects involved in incidents that came dangerously close to causing catastrophic damage to the Sky Fortress, Blacksite, SAFEHOUSE, or surrounding infrastructure.

Some exhibits are genuinely terrifying.

Others are mundane objects that became anomalously dangerous under extremely specific circumstances.

A surprising number are connected to SAFEHOUSE residents.

An even more surprising number belong to **Neko**.

---

# **PRIMARY EXHIBITS**

## **01 — WHITEFALL'S BLISS**

### **Exhibit: One Magazine**

**Classification:** EXTREME

Whitefall's Bliss is the infamous railgun / handheld howitzer historically capable of **erasing mountains**.

The original weapon remains in **Miss Neko's possession**.

The museum therefore does not possess the weapon itself.

Instead, one magazine has been preserved as a historical artifact.

The ammunition component alone is considered dangerous enough to warrant independent containment.

The exhibit plaque simply reads:

> **WHITEFALL'S BLISS**
> 
> _One magazine from the weapon that made "mountain" a temporary geological condition._
> 
> **Original weapon currently belongs to Miss Neko.**
> 
> **Do not request a demonstration.**

---

# **02 — CORIN'S KINETIC-INFUSED FEATHER**

**Classification:** HIGH

The feather responsible for the infamous Railgun Impact Event.

After a railgun projectile struck Corin's Nano Nekonium wing armor, the armor absorbed and distributed the kinetic force.

Corin subsequently launched a feather.

The feather:

- Penetrated five walls.
    
- Triggered five alarms.
    
- Remained structurally intact.
    
- Became one of the strangest projectile incidents in recent Blacksite history.
    

The feather is currently inert.

Its display includes photographs of the five damaged walls.

The plaque ends with:

> **"Do not fire a railgun at Corin."**

---

# **03 — THE PRESSURE COOKER**

### **Exhibit: Remaining Components**

**Classification:** HISTORICAL / MEDICAL

The remains of the pressure cooker responsible for severely injuring Corin's left wing.

The original device was destroyed during the incident.

Only several recognizable components remain.

The exhibit includes:

- Damaged pressure vessel fragments.
    
- Broken mechanical components.
    
- Burned sections.
    
- Residual debris recovered from the kitchen.
    
- Photographs documenting the aftermath.
    

The incident is remembered primarily because Corin shielded Belle with her wing.

Her wing subsequently required extensive regeneration.

The museum plaque reads:

> **"One pressure cooker. One catastrophic malfunction. One wing used as a shield."**

---

# **04 — Neko Collection**

The museum contains an unusually large section dedicated to objects associated with Neko.

This was not intentional.

It simply kept happening.

Among the exhibits are:

- Failed prototypes.
    
- Experimental components.
    
- Discarded engineering projects.
    
- Broken test equipment.
    
- Unidentified devices.
    
- Strange ammunition.
    
- Damaged drones.
    
- Unauthorized-looking machinery that was, according to Neko, completely authorized.
    
- Several objects whose purpose Neko refuses to explain.
    

The museum staff eventually created a dedicated subsection:

> **"Neko: Why Do We Have This?"**

Neko has never answered.

# **SAFEHOUSE RESIDENT COLLECTION**

The museum also maintains exhibits related to **every SAFEHOUSE resident**.

These range from genuinely dangerous artifacts to extremely mundane personal objects that somehow became historically significant.

---

## **CORIN**

Current exhibits include:

- Kinetic-infused feather.
    
- Pressure cooker remains.
    
- Collected inert feathers from her damaged wing.
    
- Documentation of her wing regeneration.
    
- Photographs from her first successful post-injury flight.
    

The feather remains the most dangerous object in her collection.

---

## **BELLE**

Belle's section contains several objects connected to her activities, including equipment and artifacts from incidents involving her.

One display also documents the pressure cooker incident because Belle was directly involved.

Belle reportedly dislikes this part of the museum.

---

## **ENDMIN**

Endmin's exhibits contain several Ether-related objects and artifacts.

A small black-hole motif appears repeatedly throughout the display.

---

## **MISS NEKO**

Miss Neko's section is heavily secured.

The museum does **not** display Whitefall's Bliss itself.

The original remains under Miss Neko's control.

Museum personnel have repeatedly emphasized:

> **"The magazine is enough."**

---

## **NEKO**

Neko's collection is the largest.

Nobody knows exactly why.

The museum has stopped trying to reduce it.

---

## **NIKO**

Niko's collection includes several objects associated with his ghostly nature and SAFEHOUSE activities.

Some exhibits are displayed behind transparent containment despite apparently having no physical mass.

---

## **FROST**

Frost's exhibits require environmental monitoring.

One sign reads:

> **"Please do not lick the exhibit."**

This instruction was added after an incident.

---

## **BAO**

Bao's collection contains several water- and cloud-related artifacts.

Humidity control is required.

---

## **TAIGA**

Taiga's section contains several objects associated with her unusual habits.

Nobody is certain why.

## **MART**

Mart's exhibits include equipment associated with SAFEHOUSE operations and several objects involved in previous incidents.

---

## **ASTRAL**

Astral's section contains several anomalous and spectral artifacts.

# **PILLARS AND DUMMIES**

Not everything in the museum is an actual weapon or anomalous object.

Several exhibits are simply:

- Pillars.
    
- Training dummies.
    
- Walls.
    
- Doors.
    
- Furniture.
    
- Pieces of machinery.
    
- Random debris.
    

Their historical significance comes from **what happened to them**.

One particular training dummy has survived so many SAFEHOUSE-related experiments that it has been designated a permanent exhibit.

Its official name is:

> **"The Dummy That Refused To Die."**

---

# **THE NIKO-NEKO PROBLEM**

Museum administrators have repeatedly attempted to organize the collection by resident.

This works poorly.

Neko's equipment tends to become involved in everyone else's incidents.

As a result, many exhibits have multiple labels.

One object currently carries:

> **Neko**

> **Corin**

> **SAFEHOUSE**

> **Engineering**

> **"Do Not Reproduce"**

> **"Why Is This Here?"**

---

# **MUSEUM SECURITY**

Despite the humorous nature of several exhibits, the collection is heavily secured.

Particularly dangerous exhibits receive independent containment.

The museum contains multiple warning signs.

The most common is:

> **DO NOT TOUCH.**

The second most common is:

> **DO NOT TEST.**

The third most common is:

> **NEKO ALREADY TESTED IT.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The museum began as a collection of dangerous historical artifacts.

It eventually became something else.

A record of SAFEHOUSE history.

Every resident has left something behind.

Some exhibits represent triumphs.

Some represent accidents.

Some represent mistakes.

Some represent extremely questionable engineering decisions.

And some are simply objects that somehow survived being involved with Neko.

The current collection includes:

> **One magazine from Whitefall's Bliss.**

> **One kinetic-infused feather.**

> **The remains of a pressure cooker.**

> **Pillars.**

> **Training dummies.**

> **Random debris.**

> **And countless artifacts belonging to the SAFEHOUSE residents.**

The museum curator's final assessment is simple:

> **"If something almost destroyed the Sky Fortress, we keep it."**

Then, after reviewing Neko's section:

> **"...If Neko touched it, we probably keep that too."**

**End of Blacksite Archival / Anomalous History Division**
