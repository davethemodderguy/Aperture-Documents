# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The search for Jake within LSIN-001 produced no direct contact.

It did, however, produce two discoveries.

The first was Jake's teddy bear.

The second was a handwritten note.

The note was found beside the teddy bear in the rain.

It read:

> **“Twiggy once said why I am down here. I don't really know. I should return. Just look up at night, you can see me somewhere, or see me nowhere. I'm too tired for this. I gone back. I will die soon, at least you all will be safe.”**

That evening, all personnel present in the Grassland looked upward.

The following night, a blue star appeared in the sky.

It was unmistakably Jake.

---

# 1. THE TEDDY BEAR

At approximately 05:31, Nora discovered an object lying beneath a cluster of wilted flowers.

She stopped.

> **NORA:**  
> “Wait.”

Everyone turned.

The object was partially covered by wet grass.

It was Jake's teddy bear.

The same teddy bear that had accompanied him through:

- his original materialization;
    
- his years in the SAFEHOUSE;
    
- his previous death;
    
- the loss of his original stellar core;
    
- the Nullwalker incident;
    
- the Grassland;
    
- his return to the SAFEHOUSE;
    
- and the twenty-five weeks of isolation.
    

The bear's fabric was soaked.

Its familiar scars remained.

Its heartbeat was still present.

Weak.

But present.

Nora immediately picked it up.

She held it against herself.

> **NORA:**  
> “He's here.”

Bao approached.

She immediately recognized it.

Her expression changed.

> **BAO:**  
> “Where's Jake?”

No one answered.

---

# 2. THE NOTE

Jane noticed something beneath the teddy bear.

A folded piece of paper.

She picked it up carefully.

The handwriting was unmistakable.

Jake's.

The paper was wet but remained readable.

Jane unfolded it.

Nobody spoke.

She read it aloud.

> **“Twiggy once said why I am down here.”**

Pause.

> **“I don't really know.”**

Another pause.

> **“I should return.”**

Jane's voice became quieter.

> **“Just look up at night, you can see me somewhere, or see me nowhere.”**

She stopped.

Her eyes remained on the paper.

Then continued.

> **“I'm too tired for this.”**

A longer pause.

> **“I gone back.”**

And finally:

> **“I will die soon, at least you all will be safe.”**

Silence.

---

# 3. BAO

Bao stared at the note.

Then at the sky.

Then at Jake's teddy bear.

> **BAO:**  
> “No.”

Nobody responded.

Bao's voice became firmer.

> **BAO:**  
> “No.”

She took the note from Jane.

Read it herself.

Again.

And again.

Her hands began shaking.

> **BAO:**  
> “He thinks he's dangerous.”

Aeterna looked at her.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “He thinks his death will protect us.”

Bao closed her eyes.

> **BAO:**  
> “He thinks we're safer without him.”

---

# 4. NERI

Neri remained completely still.

She stared at the teddy bear.

Then at the note.

Her eyes stopped at one sentence.

> **“Just look up at night.”**

Neri's expression changed.

She understood.

She looked upward.

The sky above the Grassland was beginning to brighten.

No star was visible.

Not yet.

Neri quietly spoke.

> **NERI:**  
> “He went home.”

Bao looked at her.

Neri swallowed.

> **NERI:**  
> “To where he came from.”

---

# 5. AETERNA

Aeterna remained silent for an extended period.

Then:

> **AETERNA:**  
> “If he has returned to his stellar manifestation...”

She stopped.

Jane looked toward her.

> **JANE:**  
> “What?”

Aeterna looked upward.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “He may no longer be here.”

Nora tightened her grip around the teddy bear.

> **NORA:**  
> “But he's still Jake.”

Aeterna looked at her.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Yes.”

---

# 6. NO RETURN

The residents searched the Grassland until morning.

Jake did not appear.

There was no additional note.

No rift opened.

No figure emerged from the grass.

No footsteps were detected.

The teddy bear remained the only physical object associated with Jake.

Eventually, the residents returned to SAFEHOUSE.

The bear was taken to Medical.

Its heartbeat remained stable.

Jake did not return with them.

---

# 7. THE FOLLOWING NIGHT

The next night, residents began gathering outside.

No one had formally organized it.

They simply remembered the note.

> **“Just look up at night.”**

At 22:17, Bao stood outside.

Neri stood beside her.

Nora held Jake's teddy bear.

Jane stood nearby.

Aeterna looked toward the sky.

The Obol Squad joined them.

Eventually, almost the entire SAFEHOUSE was outside.

Nobody spoke.

They waited.

---

# 8. 22:31

Nothing.

The sky was dark.

Stars were visible.

Hundreds.

Then thousands.

But none were Jake.

Nora looked increasingly anxious.

> **NORA:**  
> “Maybe he meant another night.”

Bao placed a hand on her shoulder.

> **BAO:**  
> “Maybe.”

Nobody left.

---

# 9. 23:04

A faint blue point appeared.

At first, it was impossible to distinguish from the other stars.

Then it brightened.

A deep azure glow emerged around it.

The star became noticeably larger.

Brighter.

More distinct.

The surrounding stars remained unchanged.

The blue star continued increasing in apparent luminosity.

Aeterna looked upward.

Her celestial eyes widened slightly.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “That's him.”

Neri stared.

She recognized it immediately.

So did everyone else.

---

# 10. JAKE

The star was unmistakable.

Blue.

Brilliant.

Familiar.

It was the same celestial body documented in historical astronomical records.

The same anomalous stellar signature associated with Jake.

The same presence that had once existed above Earth before disappearing.

Jake had returned to the sky.

Not as a human child.

Not as a Weeping Angel.

Not as a small boy carrying a teddy bear.

But as a star.

---

# 11. NORA

Nora looked up.

Her eyes filled with tears.

She raised Jake's teddy bear toward the sky.

> **NORA:**  
> “Jake!”

No response came.

The star continued shining.

Nora laughed once through her tears.

Then cried.

> **NORA:**  
> “You're there.”

Bao placed an arm around her.

Neither looked away.

---

# 12. BAO

Bao stared at the blue star.

For several minutes, she said nothing.

Eventually:

> **BAO:**  
> “You came back.”

Her voice broke.

> **BAO:**  
> “You really came back.”

She looked down at the teddy bear.

Then upward again.

> **BAO:**  
> “But you shouldn't have had to.”

---

# 13. NERI

Neri remained beside Bao.

Her glasses reflected the blue light.

She watched the star.

Then quietly said:

> **NERI:**  
> “He's too far away.”

Jane heard her.

> **JANE:**  
> “Yeah.”

Neri's voice became quieter.

> **NERI:**  
> “But he's still here.”

Jane nodded.

> **JANE:**  
> “Yeah.”

# 14. C.O.R.E. ASTRONOMICAL ANALYSIS

Initial readings indicate that the blue star is not a conventional stellar object.

Its signature matches historical data associated with Jake.

The object's position corresponds to the previously documented location of the anomalous star believed to have disappeared approximately decades ago.

The star is:

- stable;
    
- emitting anomalous blue light;
    
- exhibiting a signature consistent with Jake;
    
- detectable through conventional astronomical observation;
    
- detectable through anomalous-energy monitoring;
    
- not currently exhibiting hostile behavior.
    

No evidence currently indicates that the star is physically approaching Earth.

No evidence currently indicates that Jake has ceased to exist.

However—

The note's final statement remains unresolved.

> **“I will die soon.”**

C.O.R.E. cannot determine whether Jake meant:

1. his human manifestation would cease;
    
2. his current stellar manifestation would eventually deteriorate;
    
3. he believed returning to stellar form would kill him;
    
4. he was expressing emotional hopelessness;
    
5. or something else entirely.
    

No conclusion should currently be assumed.

---

# 15. THE MOST IMPORTANT OBSERVATION

Jake did not leave nothing behind.

He left his teddy bear.

He left a note.

He left the Grassland.

He left his flowers.

And he left a star.

Perhaps most importantly:

He left something everyone could still see.

Every night, the blue star remained visible.

Even through the Blacksite's reinforced observation systems.

Even through clouds.

Even through the darkness.

Jake was no longer walking through the SAFEHOUSE.

But he was not invisible.

C.O.R.E. has issued the following guidance:

Residents are not to attempt unauthorized dimensional travel to reach Jake.

Residents are not to attempt to physically retrieve the star.

Residents are not to treat the star as a research specimen.

No extraction attempts are authorized.

No experiments are authorized without direct welfare approval.

Jake remains a resident.

His stellar manifestation does not change that status.

# 17. THE FOLLOWING DAYS

Something unexpected happened.

People began looking up.

Not because they were ordered to.

Not because C.O.R.E. required observation.

They simply did.

Bao looked up while making breakfast.

Neri looked up while leaving the laboratory.

Nora looked up before sleeping.

Jane looked up from the cafeteria.

Aeterna looked up from the courtyard.

Even residents who had never known what Jake's star looked like began recognizing it.

There was no announcement.

There did not need to be.

Everyone knew.

The blue star was Jake.

---

# FINAL ASSESSMENT

For twenty-five weeks, Jake believed nobody remembered him.

He eventually stopped waiting for the door to open.

He returned to the form he had existed as before anyone knew his name.

A star.

He left behind a teddy bear.

A note.

And a sentence that nobody was prepared to hear:

> **“I will die soon, at least you all will be safe.”**

But the sky answered before anyone could.

A blue star appeared.

Unmistakably Jake.

He had not vanished.

He had not been erased.

He had not ceased to exist.

He was simply very, very far away.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

At 00:12, Bao was still standing outside.

Nora had fallen asleep against her side.

Jake's teddy bear was held between them.

Neri stood nearby.

Jane sat on the steps.

Aeterna remained silent.

Above them—

The blue star continued shining.

Bao looked upward.

For a long time, she said nothing.

Then, quietly:

> **BAO:**  
> “Goodnight, Jake.”

The star continued to shine.

And for the first time in twenty-five weeks—

Jake was somewhere everyone could see.
