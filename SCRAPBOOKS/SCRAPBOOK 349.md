# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **Z-335 RESEARCH UPDATE**

# **ELDER MART IDENTIFICATION – REVISED RECORD**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** The Five Elder Marts

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following several months of observation, C.O.R.E. has officially concluded the investigation into the identities of the Five Elder Marts.

All five Elders have now been positively identified.

Their roles are as follows:

- **Blacksite Ambassador**
    
- **KCA Ambassador**
    
- **Camera Mart (KCA Security Terminal)**
    
- **Rapid Response Mart (Blacksite)**
    
- **Rapid Response Mart (KCA)**
    

No Resident Mart has been confirmed as an Elder.

---

# **THE RAPID RESPONSE ELDERS**

The final two Elder Marts continue to serve as ordinary members of their respective Rapid Response groups.

Neither displays any authority over the others.

Both participate in:

- Vent patrols.
    
- Emergency deployments.
    
- Boulder Mode.
    
- Routine Mart duties.
    

The only notable distinction is their ability to speak fluent English when they choose.

Like Camera Mart, they generally prefer not to.

---

# **NO SPECIAL STATUS**

The Elder designation remains purely biological.

It does **not** confer:

- Leadership.
    
- Higher authority.
    
- Greater privileges.
    
- Priority access to food.
    
- Different treatment from the colony.
    

Other Marts interact with the Elders exactly as they do with every other Mart.

The Elders appear perfectly content with this arrangement.

---

# **LUMI'S BREAKTHROUGH**

After months of unsuccessful attempts, Lumi has finally learned how to distinguish the Elder Marts.

When asked how she accomplished this, she smiled.

> "...A mother notices."

She declined to elaborate further.

Researchers requested a technical explanation.

Lumi simply shook her head.

> "...You wouldn't see it."

> "...I barely do."

C.O.R.E. accepts this answer with mild frustration.

---

# **VERIFICATION TEST**

To verify Lumi's claim, a controlled identification exercise was conducted.

Approximately five hundred Marts gathered in the SAFEHOUSE Mart Nest.

Without hesitation, Lumi walked into the crowd.

She gently pointed to one Mart.

> "...Camera."

Correct.

She walked several steps farther.

> "...Blacksite Ambassador."

Correct.

Another.

> "...KCA Ambassador."

Correct.

She paused briefly before identifying the remaining two Rapid Response Elders.

Both were correct.

Five identifications.

Five successes.

No hesitation.

---

# **RESEARCH FINDINGS**

Personnel attempted to replicate Lumi's method.

Results were... less successful.

Average identification accuracy:

**0%.**

One researcher confidently identified a Mart as an Elder.

The Mart politely replied:

> "No."

It then pointed across the room.

> "...Friend."

The actual Elder gave a small wave.

---

# **DIALYN'S COMMENT**

> "...So only Lumi can reliably tell them apart?"

Lead Researcher:

> "...Correct."

Dialyn looked toward Lumi.

> "...Mother privileges."

Lumi laughed.

> "...Apparently."

---

# **STAR'S COMMENT**

Star immediately tested Lumi.

She randomly pointed at a Mart.

> "...Elder?"

Lumi glanced over.

> "...No."

The Mart looked at Star.

> "No."

Star laughed.

> "...Okay, okay."

---

# **CAMERA MART**

Camera Mart was asked if Lumi was correct.

It looked toward her.

Then nodded.

> "...Mom knows."

---

# **LUMI'S PERSONAL NOTE**

Later that evening, Lumi quietly sat among hundreds of sleeping Marts.

Five eventually drifted over and settled beside her.

Without opening their eyes, all five softly said:

> "...Good night, Mom."

Lumi smiled.

She already knew exactly which five they were.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

To everyone else in the Blacksite and KCA, the Five Elder Marts remain almost impossible to distinguish from the rest of the colony.

To Lumi...

The differences are now unmistakable.

No researcher has yet discovered what she sees.

When asked one last time to explain, Lumi simply smiled at the sea of tiny blue blobs.

> "...They're all my children."

She looked toward one particular Mart in the crowd.

It smiled back.

No one else noticed anything unusual.

She did.

**End of Research Update**
