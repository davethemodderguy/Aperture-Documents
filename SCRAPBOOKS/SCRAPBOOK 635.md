### **CORIN / ANOMALOUS FEATHER STATUS REPORT**

# **THE FLAMES HAVE GONE OUT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **EXTINGUISHMENT OF CORIN'S BURNING FEATHERS**  
**Status:** **CONFIRMED**  
**Cause:** **ABANDONMENT RESPONSE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

For the first time since Corin's anomalous feathers were documented, her permanently burning feathers have **gone out**.

Every known burning feather has extinguished.

The phenomenon occurred without:

- Water.
    
- Oxygen deprivation.
    
- Chemical suppression.
    
- Physical damage.
    
- Anomalous interference.
    
- Conventional extinguishing methods.
    

The flames simply stopped.

C.O.R.E. has now identified the apparent condition required to extinguish them:

> **Corin must experience abandonment.**

And the abandonment does not appear to require malicious intent.

---

# **THE DISCOVERY**

Researchers had spent considerable time attempting to extinguish Corin's feathers.

Nothing worked.

The flames survived every conventional method.

Even deliberately attempting to suppress the anomalous reaction produced no result.

Then the flames began weakening.

And eventually:

> **They went out.**

At first, personnel believed the phenomenon to be a spontaneous failure of Corin's anomalous properties.

Further comparison with historical records revealed something else.

Every extinguished specimen was associated with the period following Corin's prolonged belief that she had been abandoned.

---

# **THE TRIGGER**

C.O.R.E. now believes the flames are not truly independent sources of anomalous energy.

They appear to be connected to Corin's emotional state.

The flame may persist as long as Corin maintains some form of emotional attachment.

Hope.

Connection.

A reason to continue.

The flame does not necessarily require happiness.

It appears to require:

> **Someone to remain.**

---

# **ACCIDENTAL ABANDONMENT**

The most disturbing aspect of the discovery is that intentional abandonment is apparently **not required**.

A person does not need to deliberately reject Corin.

The emotional response can apparently be triggered by:

- Someone leaving unexpectedly.
    
- Corin believing she has been forgotten.
    
- Prolonged absence.
    
- Separation without explanation.
    
- Being left alone when she expected someone to remain.
    
- An accidental failure to return.
    

Intent does not appear to matter.

Corin's perception appears to be the determining factor.

---

# **ORPHIE INCIDENT**

This finding has completely changed C.O.R.E.'s interpretation of Orphie's disappearance.

Orphie did not abandon Corin.

She became trapped in a cave.

However, from Corin's perspective:

> **Orphie disappeared.**

For eight months.

Corin waited.

Corin blamed herself.

Corin eventually left.

The feathers weakened.

And now they have gone dark.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

The Treehouse lanterns were the first confirmed specimens to extinguish.

Personnel reported that the blue light gradually diminished.

Then, one by one:

**Gone.**

The Treehouse is now completely dark without conventional lighting.

The lanterns remain physically intact.

The feathers remain present.

They simply no longer burn.

---

# **RECOVERED FEATHERS**

All previously recovered feathers have been examined.

None will reignite.

Activation attempts have produced no flame.

This represents the first successful "extinguishment" of Corin's burning feathers in recorded history.

The success is considered deeply troubling.

---

# **SECURITY MEMO**

A researcher initially documented the discovery as:

> **"Successful suppression of anomalous combustion."**

The report was immediately rejected.

C.O.R.E. amended the classification to:

> **"Emotional termination event."**

Personnel are explicitly prohibited from attempting to reproduce the phenomenon.

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

Orphie was informed that the flames had gone out.

She immediately understood the implication.

She asked:

> **"...Because she thinks I left her?"**

No one answered immediately.

Orphie looked toward the darkened Treehouse.

> **"I didn't leave her."**

She then realized something else.

> **"But she doesn't know that."**

---

# **THE MOST CONCERNING DETAIL**

The flames did not become unstable.

They did not flicker violently.

They did not explode.

They did not fail mechanically.

They simply became weaker...

and weaker...

until there was nothing left.

The same way Corin gradually stopped waiting.

---

# **CORRELATION**

The current model is:

**Connection**

→ Feather burns.

**Hope weakened**

→ Flame weakens.

**Abandonment perceived**

→ Flame weakens severely.

**Complete emotional withdrawal**

→ Flame extinguishes.

This remains a hypothesis.

However, the correlation is now too strong to ignore.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

For years, Aperture Industries tried to extinguish Corin's feathers.

Nothing worked.

Not water.

Not chemicals.

Not containment.

Not suppression.

Not anomalous interference.

They finally discovered what could extinguish them.

Not a weapon.

Not a machine.

Not a laboratory procedure.

**Abandonment.**

And the cruelest part is that nobody has to mean it.

Someone can leave because they got lost.

Someone can fail to return.

Someone can simply be absent for too long.

If Corin believes she has been left behind...

the light begins to die.

The feathers that once burned forever are now dark.

And C.O.R.E. has documented a discovery that nobody wanted to make:

> **Corin's flames were never impossible to extinguish.**

> **They were only impossible to extinguish while Corin still believed someone would come back.**
