# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION LOG**

### **SUBJECT: NORMA LOCKWOOD – NOCTURNAL BEHAVIOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **OVERVIEW**

Following the recovery of memories relating to her childhood abduction, medical and psychological staff documented a recurring pattern in **Senior Manager Norma Lockwood's** sleep and nighttime behavior.

While Norma continues to perform her duties professionally during the day, her nights remain significantly more difficult.

Psychological assessment indicates two prominent trauma-related fears:

- **Fear of abandonment.**
    
- **Fear of prolonged isolation.**
    

These fears appear closely associated with the period during which she was abducted as a child and later abandoned before eventually being found by Arcturus.

---

## **RECURRING NIGHTMARES**

Norma rarely discusses the content of her nightmares.

With her permission, Belle confirmed only a consistent emotional theme.

The dreams are not always identical.

The locations change.

The people change.

The circumstances change.

One element never does.

Norma always ends the dream...

Alone.

No voices.

No footsteps.

No one coming to find her.

She wakes before the dream reaches its end.

Almost every time.

---

## **LATE NIGHTS**

SAFEHOUSE residents have quietly noticed a routine.

Long after everyone has gone to bed, the common lounge light occasionally remains on.

There, sitting on the sofa nearest the window, is Norma.

A warm mug of tea rests between her hands.

She rarely drinks it immediately.

Instead, she simply sits in the silence.

Sometimes looking out into the darkness.

Sometimes watching the steam rise from the cup.

On particularly difficult nights, quiet sobs can be heard.

Not loud enough to wake anyone.

Just enough to reveal that she's crying.

She always wipes away the tears before anyone can see.

Or at least...

She believes she does.

---

## **BELLE'S OBSERVATION**

Belle happened upon the lounge one night after waking from one of her own nightmares.

She found Norma exactly as described in the reports.

Curled slightly into herself.

Holding a cup of tea.

Trying very hard not to make a sound.

Belle did not ask what was wrong.

She simply walked over and sat beside her.

For nearly ten minutes, neither of them spoke.

Finally, Norma whispered:

> "...I didn't want to wake anyone."

Belle looked at her gently.

> "...You don't have to protect everyone all the time."

Norma lowered her eyes.

> "...I'm afraid..."

She hesitated.

> "...If I'm too much..."

> "...People will leave."

Belle immediately shook her head.

> "...We're not going anywhere."

---

## **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

After discussing the matter privately, the SAFEHOUSE residents reached an unspoken understanding.

No schedules were made.

No formal rotations.

No one announced anything.

Yet somehow...

On difficult nights, someone was almost always awake.

Sometimes Star would wander in for a late-night snack.

Sometimes Endmin would quietly read paperwork nearby.

Sometimes Frost would make another pot of tea.

Sometimes Arcturus would simply sit across from his little sister, polishing one of his revolvers in comfortable silence.

No one forced conversation.

They simply made sure she wasn't alone.

---

## **ARCTURUS**

One evening, Norma apologized.

> "...I'm sorry."

> "...I keep making everyone worry."

Arcturus set the revolver on the table.

He looked directly at her.

> "...Norma."

She looked up.

> "When I finally found you..."

His voice remained calm.

> "...I promised myself I'd never leave you behind again."

He smiled gently.

> "...I'm keeping that promise."

Norma quietly reached across the table and held his hand.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

After reviewing the report, Director Lucious added a single note to Norma's personnel file.

> **"No member of Heaven's Blacksite is expected to carry their burdens alone."**

> **"Support is not a privilege."**

> **"It is part of being a family."**

---

## **STATUS**

Norma continues to experience recurring nightmares and periods of emotional distress, particularly during the night.

She still prefers to face them quietly, believing she should not inconvenience others.

Those around her, however, have gently proven her wrong.

The lounge light still comes on from time to time.

The tea is still warm.

The nights are still difficult.

The difference now is that, more often than not, another chair is occupied.

Not because anyone was asked to be there.

Simply because no one in SAFEHOUSE wants Norma to feel alone again.

**End of Psychological Observation Log**
