# Executive Summary

Nora Tachyon has begun exhibiting behavioral characteristics strongly resembling the isolation pattern previously documented in Neri.

This change is not physical.

Nora has not become Neri.

Rather, Nora appears to have adopted a similar internal conclusion:

> **"If everyone worries about me, I am causing problems."**

Her recent illness, repeated fainting, inability to sleep, nightmares, and worsening physical condition have resulted in increasing attention from SAFEHOUSE residents.

Nora appears to have interpreted that attention as evidence that she is becoming a burden.

Her response has been predictable.

She is beginning to withdraw.

---

# 1. THE CHANGE

Nora was previously highly social.

She sought people out.

She asked questions.

She played with Alice.

She visited the Merch Store.

She followed Aeterna.

She called for Neri.

She loudly praised her siblings.

She wanted to be around her family.

That behavior has now changed.

Nora increasingly:

- remains in her room;
    
- stops conversations early;
    
- declines visitors;
    
- tells people she is "fine" when she clearly is not;
    
- refuses assistance until absolutely necessary;
    
- hides symptoms;
    
- stops asking for things she wants;
    
- and attempts to make herself as unobtrusive as possible.
    

The pattern is familiar.

It resembles Neri's established response to feeling like a burden.

---

# 2. THE INTERNAL LOGIC

Nora appears to be developing the following reasoning:

> **I am sick.**

↓

> **Everyone is worried.**

↓

> **Everyone has to take care of me.**

↓

> **Everyone is spending time worrying about me.**

↓

> **I am causing trouble.**

↓

> **If I stay alone, nobody has to worry.**

The conclusion is incorrect.

The reasoning is nevertheless internally consistent from Nora's perspective.

She is not withdrawing because she dislikes her family.

She is withdrawing because she believes her presence is creating work for them.

---

# 3. FIRST CLEAR INDICATION

Aeterna noticed the change first.

Nora had stopped calling for her during the night.

This was unusual.

Aeterna eventually found Nora awake.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Why did you not call?"

Nora shook her head.

> **NORA:**  
> "You were sleeping."

> **AETERNA:**  
> "I was awake."

Nora looked away.

> **NORA:**  
> "You should sleep."

Aeterna paused.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Nora."

No response.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Why did you not call me?"

Nora remained silent for several seconds.

Then:

> **NORA:**  
> "...Because everyone is already worried."

Aeterna's expression changed.

Nora continued quietly.

> **NORA:**  
> "I don't want to make it worse."

---

# 4. AETERNA'S RESPONSE

Aeterna sat beside her.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "You are not making anything worse."

Nora shook her head.

> **NORA:**  
> "You keep carrying me."

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Yes."

> **NORA:**  
> "Neri keeps leaving chemistry."

Aeterna said nothing.

> **NORA:**  
> "Elsher keeps coming here."

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> "Everyone keeps checking."

Her voice became quieter.

> **NORA:**  
> "It's because of me."

Aeterna answered:

> **"Yes."**

Nora looked at her.

Aeterna continued.

> **"Because you are our family."**

Nora looked away.

She did not appear convinced.

---

# 5. NERI RECOGNIZES IT

Neri recognized the behavior almost immediately.

She found Nora sitting alone in the cafeteria.

Nora had not touched her food.

Neri sat across from her.

Nora looked up.

> **NORA:**  
> "Big Sis."

Neri nodded.

Nora pushed the plate slightly away.

> **NORA:**  
> "I'm not hungry."

Neri looked at the untouched food.

Then at Nora.

Nora knew that look.

> **NORA:**  
> "...Don't."

Neri remained silent.

Nora lowered her head.

> **NORA:**  
> "Everyone is worrying."

Neri nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> "I don't want them to."

Neri reached toward the table.

She gently moved the plate back toward Nora.

Nora shook her head.

> **NORA:**  
> "If I stay in my room, nobody has to worry."

Neri froze.

The statement was painfully familiar.

---

# 6. NERI'S UNDERSTANDING

Neri understood immediately.

Not because Nora explained it particularly well.

Because Neri had thought the same thing.

Different circumstances.

Same conclusion.

> **If I disappear, I cannot inconvenience anyone.**

Neri did not argue.

She did not tell Nora that her thinking was stupid.

She simply remained there.

After several minutes, Neri quietly pushed the water closer.

Nora looked at it.

Eventually, she drank.

---

# 7. ELSHER

Elsher received the report later that evening.

He was visibly upset.

Not angry at Nora.

Angry at the situation.

> **ELSHER:**  
> "She's doing what Neri does."

The welfare officer nodded.

> **ELSHER:**  
> "She thinks she's helping by disappearing."

Pause.

> **ELSHER:**  
> "She's six."

He looked toward Nora's room.

> **ELSHER:**  
> "She shouldn't have to figure this out by herself."

---

# 8. THE CARDS

The get-well cards have become more complicated.

Nora still keeps them.

But she has begun turning some of them face-down.

When asked why, she answered:

> **"They'll stop worrying if they don't see me."**

This interpretation is incorrect.

However, it demonstrates how far the burden framework has progressed.

The cards were originally evidence that Nora was missed.

Nora has begun interpreting them as evidence that people are being forced to worry about her.

---

# 9. THE MERCH STORE

Nora has also stopped asking about the Merch Store.

Previously, she was eager to return.

Now she says:

> **"Someone else can do it."**

When asked whether she wants to return once healthy:

> **"Maybe not."**

When asked why:

> **"It's okay."**

No further explanation was provided.

C.O.R.E. considers this significant.

The store was one of Nora's strongest expressions of independence.

Her sudden willingness to abandon it suggests that the withdrawal is not limited to social interaction.

She is beginning to remove herself from activities that give her purpose.

---

# 10. AETERNA'S CONCERN

Aeterna has become increasingly protective.

However, she has also recognized that continuously hovering over Nora may reinforce Nora's belief that she is a medical problem requiring constant supervision.

This has created a difficult balance.

If Aeterna leaves:

Nora may interpret it as permission to isolate.

If Aeterna stays constantly:

Nora may interpret her presence as proof that she is burdensome.

Aeterna therefore remains nearby without forcing interaction.

She may sit outside Nora's room.

She may leave water nearby.

She may quietly check in.

She does not demand that Nora talk.

She does not demand that Nora come out.

She simply remains available.

---

# 11. AETERNA — PRIVATE OBSERVATION

> **AETERNA:**  
> "She believes solitude is kindness."

Pause.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "She thinks that if she removes herself, everyone else will be relieved."

The welfare officer asked:

> **"Does that sound familiar?"**

Aeterna looked toward Nora's closed door.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Very."

---

# 12. NORA'S CURRENT BEHAVIOR

The following behaviors have increased:

### Social Withdrawal

Nora avoids shared spaces unless necessary.

### Symptom Concealment

She increasingly says she is fine when experiencing discomfort.

### Reduced Requests

She avoids asking for water, food, assistance, or company.

### Self-Isolation

She spends prolonged periods alone.

### Emotional Suppression

She minimizes her own distress when questioned.

### Reduced Activity

She has stopped requesting many of the activities she previously enjoyed.

### Increased Resemblance to Neri's Coping Pattern

She appears to believe that reducing her presence reduces the burden placed on others.

---

# 13. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

C.O.R.E. does **not** interpret Nora's behavior as rejection of her family.

Quite the opposite.

Nora is withdrawing specifically because she cares about them.

She does not want:

Aeterna to worry.

Neri to leave chemistry.

Elsher to leave MSAC duties.

Bao to repeatedly check on her.

Alice to become upset.

The problem is that Nora has concluded:

> **"If they care about me, I am causing them trouble."**

This is the same fundamental error previously observed in Neri.

---

# 14. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri has not attempted to force Nora back into social activity.

She understands better than most that forced interaction can make withdrawal worse.

Instead, Neri has begun doing something much simpler.

She sits nearby.

Sometimes silently.

Sometimes while working.

Sometimes while reading.

She does not demand that Nora talk.

She does not demand that Nora eat immediately.

She does not demand that Nora leave the room.

She simply makes it clear that Nora can be alone **without being abandoned.**

This appears to be the approach Nora tolerates best.

---

# 15. CURRENT WELFARE GUIDANCE

Personnel are instructed:

**Do not chase Nora.**

**Do not guilt Nora.**

**Do not tell her everyone is worried because she is causing problems.**

**Do not demand that she socialize.**

**Do not force her to reassure everyone.**

Instead:

> "You don't have to come out."

> "I'm going to be nearby."

> "You don't have to talk."

> "You can ask for me whenever you want."

And most importantly:

> **"You are not making us take care of you."**

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Nora is beginning to resemble Neri.

Not physically.

Not neurologically.

Not in personality.

In one specific and deeply concerning way:

> **She has begun believing that disappearing is a form of kindness.**

Neri once withdrew because she believed she needed to earn her place.

Nora appears to be withdrawing because she believes she is consuming too much of everyone else's attention.

Both conclusions lead to the same behavior.

Silence.

Isolation.

Concealment.

And eventually:

> **"I'm fine."**

Even when they are not.

---

# FINAL OBSERVATION

Nora used to be the loudest person in the room.

Now she tries not to take up space.

She used to call for Aeterna.

Now she waits for Aeterna to check on her.

She used to ask Neri questions.

Now she worries about interrupting Neri's chemistry.

She used to wait for Elsher to visit.

Now she tells him he should go back to MSAC.

She used to love seeing all the get-well cards.

Now she turns some of them face-down because they remind her that people are worried.

And when someone asks why she is staying alone, she gives the simplest answer she can:

> **"It's better this way."**

It is the same answer Neri might have given.

And that is precisely why everyone who knows Neri is becoming concerned.

Nora has not stopped loving them.

She has simply decided that loving them means making herself smaller.

Quiet.

Invisible.

Easy to take care of.

Or, preferably—

not requiring care at all.

Aeterna remains outside her door.

Neri remains nearby when chemistry permits.

Elsher keeps coming back.

The cards remain on the wall.

And Nora remains inside.

For now, C.O.R.E.'s objective is not to drag her back into the room.

It is to make sure she eventually understands something Neri is still learning herself:

> **Being loved is not the same thing as being a burden.**
