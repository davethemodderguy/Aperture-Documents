### **RESTRICTED MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

# **ORPHIE — PHYSICAL DISCREPANCY REPORT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — SEALED  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Related Record:** Corey █████████ — Archived Medical Record  
**Access:** **Guiding Light / Architect-Level Authorization**  
**Status:** **PRIVATE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Guiding Light has observed a significant difference between the individual known today as **Orphie** and the five-year-old girl depicted in the recovered medical record.

The differences are not merely physical.

They extend to:

- Personality.
    
- Emotional expression.
    
- Behavior.
    
- Self-perception.
    
- Relationship with others.
    
- The way she responds to affection.
    
- The way she handles memories of her past.
    

The child in the photograph appears vulnerable and visibly injured.

Orphie does not resemble that child emotionally.

She has become considerably more guarded.

More distant.

More difficult to reach.

---

# **THE SCARS**

Despite the differences, one physical connection remains impossible to ignore.

**Orphie has extensive scarring across her body.**

The scars vary in appearance and severity.

Some are old.

Some are faint.

Others remain considerably more visible.

Their exact origins cannot be conclusively established from observation alone.

Orphie does not discuss them.

---

# **AVOIDANCE**

Orphie deliberately avoids looking at most of her scars.

This behavior appears intentional.

When changing clothes, bathing, treating an injury, or otherwise exposed to her own reflection, she tends to keep her attention away from the affected areas.

She does not appear unaware of the scars.

Quite the opposite.

She knows exactly where they are.

She simply does not want to look at them.

---

# **WHY**

The reason remains unknown.

Orphie has never provided a direct explanation.

However, Guiding Light has observed a consistent pattern:

When Orphie accidentally notices a scar, her emotional state may become noticeably more unstable.

Her expression becomes distant.

Her attention shifts elsewhere.

She may immediately cover the affected area.

Sometimes she simply leaves.

It appears that **looking at herself for too long can become emotionally overwhelming.**

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S OBSERVATION**

Guiding Light has deliberately avoided asking Orphie about the scars.

She understands that forcing the subject could do more harm than good.

Instead, she remains nearby.

If Orphie looks away, Guiding Light does not tell her to look back.

If Orphie covers herself, Guiding Light does not question why.

If Orphie leaves, Guiding Light lets her have the space.

There is no requirement for Orphie to explain herself.

---

# **THE DIFFERENCE**

The recovered photograph shows a five-year-old girl wearing a small white beanie.

Orphie is no longer that child.

She has grown into someone who has built considerable emotional distance between herself and the world around her.

Yet the scars remain.

They are physical evidence of a history she does not willingly examine.

Guiding Light understands the distinction.

The scars belong to Orphie's body.

They do not define who she is.

---

# **PRIVATE NOTE — GUIDING LIGHT**

A short blue note was attached to the observation.

> **"She doesn't need to become that little girl again."**
> 
> **"She doesn't need to remember everything just because I know it happened."**
> 
> **"If she doesn't want to look at her scars, I won't make her."**
> 
> **"I can simply stay beside her."**

A final sentence was written underneath.

> **"She is still Orphie."**

---

# **SECURITY DIRECTIVE**

This observation is to remain separate from the recovered medical record.

Orphie is **not aware of the archived record**.

No connection between the present subject and the archived identity is to be disclosed.

No personnel are authorized to deliberately draw Orphie's attention toward her scars.

No photographic comparison is permitted.

No attempt to recover additional memories is permitted without appropriate authorization.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The girl in the photograph and the person standing before Guiding Light may be separated by years, experiences, and an entirely different identity.

But the scars remain a quiet reminder that Orphie's story began long before anyone at the Blacksite knew her.

She does not look at them.

Guiding Light does not make her.

For now, that is enough.
