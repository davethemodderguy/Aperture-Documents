## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **Z-335 BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION**

# **SLEEPING HABITS OF MARTS**

**Classification:** Allied Entity Research  
**Subject:** Z-335 (Marts)

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although Marts do not appear to require sleep for survival, they frequently enter a dormant state resembling sleep.

Their sleeping habits can best be summarized as:

> **Anywhere.**

Researchers have documented sleeping Marts on:

- Desks.
    
- Chairs.
    
- Laptops.
    
- Computer servers.
    
- Bookshelves.
    
- Railings.
    
- Kitchen counters.
    
- Windowsills.
    
- People's laps.
    
- People's shoulders.
    
- Inside empty boxes.
    
- Ceiling pipes.
    
- Security monitors.
    
- Other sleeping Marts.
    

The location appears to matter very little.

If a place is comfortable enough...

A Mart may decide it is now a bed.

---

# **IMMOVABILITY**

One of the stranger properties of a sleeping Mart is that it becomes effectively impossible to move.

Attempts to:

- Lift it.
    
- Push it.
    
- Roll it.
    
- Pull it.
    
- Scoop it up.
    

Have all failed.

The Mart simply... remains exactly where it fell asleep.

Researchers have compared the effect to trying to move a mountain.

Despite the Mart weighing very little while awake.

No anomalous force has yet been identified.

---

# **HEAD INCIDENTS**

Personnel are advised to remain aware of nearby Marts when sitting still for extended periods.

If a Mart decides your head is comfortable...

It may fall asleep there.

Should this occur:

> **Do not expect it to move until it wakes up on its own.**

Repeated attempts to remove a sleeping Mart have consistently failed.

Most personnel eventually accept their temporary new hat.

---

# **DOCUMENTED EXAMPLES**

**Case 1**

A Mart fell asleep on Corin's shoulder.

Corin delayed a meeting by twenty-three minutes.

Her explanation:

> "...I was wearing a Mart."

---

**Case 2**

One Mart fell asleep on Camera Mart.

Neither moved for approximately six hours.

Security referred to the pair as:

> "Camera Camera Mart."

The name was not adopted officially.

---

**Case 3**

A Mart fell asleep on Neko's keyboard.

Neko completed his work one-handed rather than disturb it.

The Mart woke several hours later.

> "...Morning."

---

**Case 4**

A Mart selected Dialyn's head as its sleeping location during a paperwork session.

Dialyn finished every document while wearing what witnesses described as:

> "...A tiny blue water beret."

---

# **THE STAR INCIDENT**

One afternoon, Star sat in the SAFEHOUSE reading.

A Mart climbed onto her head.

Curled up.

And immediately fell asleep.

Star attempted to gently lift it.

Nothing happened.

She laughed.

> "...Guess I live with this now."

Approximately three hours later, the Mart yawned.

Stretched.

Looked down at Star.

> "Good nap."

Then floated away as though nothing unusual had happened.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Sleeping on a person's head poses no known health risk beyond mild inconvenience.

The Mart's weight remains negligible.

Personnel may experience:

- Slight neck fatigue.
    
- Curious looks from coworkers.
    
- Increased likelihood of being photographed by Star.
    

---

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

Lumi once entered a meeting wearing two sleeping Marts.

Nobody mentioned it for nearly twenty minutes.

Eventually she asked:

> "...Can someone tell me why everyone keeps smiling?"

One researcher pointed upward.

Lumi looked into a nearby mirror.

She sighed.

> "...Of course."

She finished the meeting before waiting for the Marts to wake naturally.

---

# **DIALYN'S COMMENT**

> "...You don't own your chair."

She looked toward a sleeping Mart.

> "...Or your desk."

She glanced upward.

> "...Or apparently your head."

---

# **RESEARCH NOTE**

Attempts to determine why sleeping Marts become immovable have been unsuccessful.

One researcher asked an Ambassador directly.

> "...Why can't we move sleeping Marts?"

The Ambassador thought for several seconds.

Then replied:

> "...Sleeping."

Researchers noted this explained absolutely nothing.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Personnel have gradually adapted to the phenomenon.

If someone walks through the hallway with a sleeping Mart balanced on their head, nobody reacts anymore.

The usual response is simply:

> "...Don't worry."

> "...It'll wake up eventually."

The Mart always does.

Usually after having an excellent nap.

**End of Behavioral Observation**
