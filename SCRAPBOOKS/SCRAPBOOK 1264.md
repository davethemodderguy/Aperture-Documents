# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### RESIDENT WELFARE & MEDICAL DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-NORA-414  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — SAFEHOUSE  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**SUBJECT:** Nora Tachyon — Cold Exposure Response  
**RELATED INCIDENT:** Unauthorized prolonged exterior exposure

---

# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

Nora was recovered after approximately **5 hours and 45 minutes** of exposure to outdoor conditions.

She was conscious.

She was responsive.

She was also **shivering continuously.**

Nora is a Shark Thiren.

She does not tolerate cold well.

This distinction has now been formally documented.

### C.O.R.E. MEDICAL NOTE

> **Nora being a shark does not make her resistant to cold.**

In fact, prolonged cold exposure appears to affect her particularly badly.

Upon recovery, Nora's hands were visibly trembling.

Her shoulders were shaking.

Her teeth were beginning to chatter.

She repeatedly attempted to hold her arms against her body in an effort to conserve warmth.

She did not complain.

She simply kept shivering.

---

# 2. RECOVERY

Aeterna located Nora outside and immediately approached.

Nora looked up.

Her glasses were fogged slightly from her breathing.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “Nora.”

Nora tried to answer.

Her voice came out quietly.

> **Nora:**  
> “I'm okay.”

She was still shaking.

Aeterna looked at her for several seconds.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “You are freezing.”

Nora shook her head.

Another shiver ran through her.

> **Nora:**  
> “A little.”

Aeterna immediately removed her outer covering and wrapped Nora in it.

Nora did not resist.

She simply leaned against Aeterna.

---

# 3. IMMEDIATE WARMING RESPONSE

Nora was brought indoors.

The response was deliberately simple.

No interrogation.

No questions about why she had waited outside.

No discussion of the security failure.

First:

**Warmth.**

A blanket was wrapped around Nora.

Then another.

Nora was placed somewhere warm and allowed to sit quietly.

Her hands were still trembling.

Aeterna sat beside her and pulled Nora into a firm embrace.

Nora immediately curled against her.

Her shark tail wrapped around the blanket.

The shaking did not stop immediately.

It gradually became less severe.

---

# 4. HOT CHOCOLATE

Bao prepared hot chocolate.

Nora initially stared at the cup.

She did not reach for it.

Bao placed it carefully between Nora's hands.

> **BAO:**  
> “Slowly.”

Nora nodded.

She took a small sip.

Her hands were still shaking badly enough that Bao kept one hand around the cup to prevent it from spilling.

After several minutes:

> **NORA:**  
> “Warm.”

Bao smiled faintly.

> **BAO:**  
> “That's the idea.”

Nora took another sip.

---

# 5. BLANKET RESPONSE

Nora was eventually almost completely concealed beneath the blanket.

Only her glasses, hair, and the tip of her shark tail were visible.

Aeterna looked down at her.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Better?”

Nora nodded.

A pause.

> **NORA:**  
> “Still cold.”

Aeterna tightened the blanket around her.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Then you remain here.”

Nora did not argue.

She simply moved closer.

---

# 6. OBSERVATION

The shivering persisted for some time.

Nora's condition gradually improved with:

- warm indoor temperature;
    
- blankets;
    
- hot chocolate;
    
- physical contact;
    
- rest;
    
- continuous observation.
    

The most effective intervention appeared to be **simple warmth combined with reassurance.**

Nora did not require an elaborate conversation.

She required someone to hold her while her body warmed back up.

---

# 7. AETERNA

Aeterna remained beside Nora throughout the recovery period.

She did not leave to continue maintenance.

She did not return to testing.

She did not attempt to explain the circumstances.

She simply held Nora.

Eventually, Nora's shaking became less frequent.

Her head rested against Aeterna's chest.

> **NORA:**  
> “Aeterna?”

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Yes?”

> **NORA:**  
> “Don't go.”

Aeterna's answer was immediate.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “I will not.”

Nora closed her eyes.

---

# 8. C.O.R.E. GUIDANCE

Following this incident, SAFEHOUSE personnel are reminded:

If Nora is found cold, shivering, or otherwise chilled, the preferred response is not complicated.

### Provide:

**A warm environment.**

**A blanket.**

**A warm drink such as hot chocolate.**

**Physical comfort if Nora accepts it.**

**Someone nearby.**

Do not leave her alone simply because she says:

> “I'm okay.”

Especially when she is visibly shivering.

Nora has demonstrated a repeated tendency to minimize her own discomfort.

Her verbal reassurance should therefore be considered alongside observable physical symptoms.

---

# 9. FINAL OBSERVATION

Nora is energetic.

Nora is resilient.

Nora is a Shark Thiren.

None of these facts make her immune to being cold.

After nearly six hours outside, she did not need another test.

She did not need another lecture.

She did not need to explain herself.

She needed a blanket.

Hot chocolate.

And someone willing to hold her until the shaking stopped.

For the remainder of the evening, Nora remained wrapped in blankets with Aeterna beside her.

The hot chocolate eventually disappeared.

The shivering eventually stopped.

Nora eventually fell asleep.

Still holding the cup.

**C.O.R.E. NOTE:**

> **If Nora is cold, warm Nora.**
> 
> **If Nora is shivering, hold Nora.**
> 
> **If Nora asks for hot chocolate, provide hot chocolate.**
> 
> **Everything else can wait.**
