## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### ARCHIVAL & INTELLIGENCE DIVISION

# ARCHITECT-GRANTED POWER MANIFESTATION EVENT

## “TWENTY-FOUR HOURS. RANDOMIZED. GOOD LUCK.”

**CLASSIFICATION:** C.O.R.E. INTERNAL / ARCHITECT-RELATED  
**ACCESS:** Level 4+ Personnel  
**STATUS:** CLOSED — TEMPORARY EFFECT EXPIRED  
**DATE:** 03/09/2026  
**SUBJECT:** Temporary power distribution event involving SAFEHOUSE residents  
**ARCHITECT RESPONSIBLE:** Curious Light  
**DURATION:** 24 Hours

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately 09:14, **Curious Light** visited SAFEHOUSE.

At approximately 09:16, he decided that the residents should temporarily possess supernatural abilities.

No experiment had been scheduled.

No authorization had been requested.

No emergency existed.

Curious Light simply stated:

> **“I wonder what everyone would get.”**

He then granted **every SAFEHOUSE resident a randomized supernatural power.**

The powers were temporary.

The duration was exactly **24 hours.**

Curious Light provided no control over what ability each person received.

He did, however, appear to have intentionally avoided granting abilities that would cause permanent harm.

The resulting event has been designated:

> **EVENT CL-24 — RANDOMIZED POWER DISTRIBUTION**

---

# 2. ARCHITECT CAPABILITY CONFIRMATION

The event confirmed a previously understood but rarely demonstrated capability of the Architects.

### ANY OF THE THREE ARCHITECTS CAN GRANT POWERS.

Guiding Light, Curious Light, and Mischevious Light possess the ability to bestow anomalous capabilities upon individuals.

The mechanism does not appear to involve:

- genetic modification,
    
- conventional augmentation,
    
- ritual transfer,
    
- possession,
    
- technological implants,
    
- or conventional energy infusion.
    

The recipient simply **has the ability.**

The Architects can subsequently remove or alter the granted ability.

Curious Light elected to make his distribution temporary.

---

# 3. THE ANNOUNCEMENT

Curious Light gathered the SAFEHOUSE residents.

He appeared unusually casual.

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “I'm going to give all of you a power.”

Several residents immediately became interested.

> **NEKO:**  
> “For what?”

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Twenty-four hours.”

> **FROST:**  
> “What powers?”

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Random.”

Silence.

> **NEKO:**  
> “You randomized them?”

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Yep.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “Why?”

Curious Light shrugged.

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “It'll be interesting.”

From somewhere behind them:

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Can I do it next?”

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “No.”

---

# 4. POWER DISTRIBUTION

At 09:16, Curious Light activated the effect.

There was no flash.

No sound.

No visible energy.

Every resident simply felt something change.

C.O.R.E. instruments detected a brief, simultaneous anomalous fluctuation throughout SAFEHOUSE.

Then the powers manifested.

---

# 5. RESIDENT MANIFESTATIONS

## NEKO

**POWER:** Object Duplication

Neko could duplicate any non-living object by touching it.

The duplicate was physically identical.

Neko immediately tested this on a screwdriver.

Then another screwdriver.

Then another.

Then approximately thirty-seven screwdrivers.

> **NEKO:**  
> “This is incredibly useful.”

Five minutes later, the Archives contained far too many screwdrivers.

---

## MISS NEKO

**POWER:** Perfect Sound Mimicry

Miss Neko could reproduce any sound she had heard with complete accuracy.

She spent approximately ten minutes imitating SAFEHOUSE residents.

Nobody was fooled.

Everyone knew it was her.

She continued anyway.

---

## NIKO

**POWER:** Temporary Gravity Manipulation

Niko could alter the gravitational force affecting objects they touched.

Their first experiment involved making a chair extremely light.

Their second involved accidentally making it extremely heavy.

The chair remained embedded in the floor for approximately two hours.

---

## BELLE MERRYWEATHER

**POWER:** Enhanced Luck

Belle experienced an extreme increase in probability in her favor.

Examples included:

- finding objects she had misplaced,
    
- guessing correct answers,
    
- avoiding falling objects,
    
- catching objects without looking,
    
- and winning several completely unnecessary games.
    

She eventually became suspicious.

> **BELLE:**  
> “This feels unfair.”

> **ENDMIN:**  
> “You won anyway.”

---

## LINDERVERNE “ENDMIN” MERRYWEATHER

**POWER:** Echolocation

Endmin could perceive her surroundings through emitted sound.

She quickly became extremely good at using it.

The ability was especially effective in dark environments.

> **ENDMIN:**  
> “This is actually really useful.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “You got the useful one.”

---

## FROST

**POWER:** Thermal Manipulation

Frost could alter the temperature of objects by touch.

She could heat or cool objects within a substantial range.

The ability was immediately useful.

Unfortunately, Frost discovered she could also heat beverages.

She spent the remainder of the morning making hot chocolate.

---

## BAO

**POWER:** Water Duplication

Bao could create temporary copies of any water she touched.

This resulted in the SAFEHOUSE swimming pool becoming significantly larger.

Bao stopped after someone pointed out that the floor could not support the additional weight.

---

## TAIGA

**POWER:** Enhanced Agility

Taiga became dramatically more agile.

She could jump, turn, and land with exceptional precision.

She spent most of the day climbing things she normally would not attempt.

---

## MART

**POWER:** Object Animation

Mart could temporarily cause inanimate objects to move on their own.

His first successful test animated a broom.

The broom cleaned the floor.

Mart stared at it.

> **MART:**  
> “I have created a worker.”

The broom subsequently stopped working when its power expired.

---

## ASTRAL

**POWER:** Short-Range Teleportation

Astral could teleport herself approximately twenty meters at a time.

She quickly mastered it.

She also quickly discovered that teleporting while carrying groceries was extremely convenient.

---

## STAR

**POWER:** Temporary Light Constructs

Star could create solid objects made entirely from light.

She immediately created:

- a chair,
    
- several stars,
    
- a sword,
    
- a tiny castle,
    
- and an unnecessarily elaborate light sculpture.
    

Nightshade stared at the sculpture.

> **NIGHTSHADE:**  
> “Why?”

> **STAR:**  
> “Because I can.”

---

## NIGHTSHADE

**POWER:** Shadow Manipulation

Nightshade could manipulate nearby shadows as physical extensions of herself.

The shadows could grab objects and move independently.

Star immediately attempted to poke one.

The shadow poked her back.

Star laughed for approximately five minutes.

---

## ALICE

**POWER:** Enhanced Perception

Alice's senses became extraordinarily sensitive.

She could detect movement, sound, and changes in her surroundings with remarkable accuracy.

Unfortunately, this also meant she could hear Neko opening snack wrappers from several rooms away.

She began appearing beside people almost instantly.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Alice, how did you get here?”

> **ALICE:**  
> “I heard you.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “From where?”

> **ALICE:**  
> “The Archives.”

Neko stopped eating.

---

## ORPHIE

**POWER:** Emotion Projection

Orphie could temporarily project a chosen emotional state onto nearby people.

She used the ability sparingly.

Mostly because she found it strange.

Her first successful use caused everyone nearby to become inexplicably calm.

Neko described the sensation as:

> **“Being wrapped in a blanket.”**

---

## CORIN

**POWER:** Plant Growth Acceleration

Corin could accelerate the growth of plants through physical contact.

The SAFEHOUSE garden expanded considerably.

Viridis was reportedly impressed.

---

## ARCTURUS LOCKWOOD

**POWER:** Enhanced Spatial Awareness

Arcturus gained an almost perfect awareness of his immediate surroundings.

He could identify movement and objects without directly looking at them.

His response:

> **ARCTURUS:**  
> “Useful.”

He proceeded to use it exactly as efficiently as expected.

---

## NORMA LOCKWOOD

**POWER:** Memory Replay

Norma could reconstruct and replay recent events as visual projections.

This quickly became extremely useful for resolving arguments.

It also became extremely dangerous to anyone claiming:

> “I didn't do that.”

Norma would simply replay the event.

---

## LUMIA “LUMI” LOCKWOOD

**POWER:** Probability Perception

Lumi could perceive approximate probabilities surrounding potential actions.

She immediately began making extremely efficient decisions.

When asked whether this was useful:

> **LUMI:**  
> “Very.”

She refused to elaborate.

---

# 6. THE POWER THAT NOBODY UNDERSTOOD

One ability was particularly difficult to categorize.

Curious Light had accidentally granted one resident a power that appeared to do absolutely nothing.

The resident spent several hours testing it.

Nothing happened.

Eventually Curious Light was asked about it.

He looked confused.

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Oh.”

Pause.

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “That's supposed to do something?”

Everyone stared at him.

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “I randomized it.”

---

# 7. MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT'S INVOLVEMENT

Mischevious Light repeatedly attempted to modify the temporary powers.

Curious Light prohibited this.

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Don't.”

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “I wasn't going to.”

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “You were.”

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Maybe.”

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “No.”

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Fine.”

Approximately twenty minutes later, Mischevious was caught holding a glowing object that had previously been a SAFEHOUSE toaster.

He claimed it was unrelated.

Nobody believed him.

---

# 8. GUIDING LIGHT'S ASSESSMENT

Guiding Light observed the experiment for most of the day.

She did not interfere.

When asked whether granting random powers was responsible, she replied:

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Curious knows what he's doing.”

> **C.O.R.E. STAFF:**  
> “Mostly?”

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Mostly.”

She then smiled.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Besides, they're having fun.”

---

# 9. THE 24-HOUR LIMIT

At exactly 09:16 the following morning, all temporary powers disappeared.

No side effects were detected.

Every resident returned to their normal state.

The duplicated objects produced by Neko vanished.

The animated broom stopped moving.

The additional water disappeared.

The light constructs dissolved.

The teleportation effect ceased.

Normal gravity returned.

The SAFEHOUSE returned to its usual state.

Mostly.

The screwdriver collection was gone.

Neko was disappointed.

---

# 10. CURIOUS LIGHT'S FINAL COMMENT

Curious Light was asked whether he would repeat the experiment.

He answered:

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Probably.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “Can we choose the powers next time?”

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “No.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “Can we at least request categories?”

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “No.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “Can we opt out?”

Curious Light paused.

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Sure.”

Neko looked surprised.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Really?”

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Yeah.”

Pause.

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “But then you'd miss the fun.”

Neko immediately reconsidered.

---

# 11. C.O.R.E. CONCLUSION

The event confirms that the Architects possess an extraordinary ability to grant anomalous powers to ordinary individuals.

More importantly, the event demonstrated that these abilities can be:

- temporary,
    
- individualized,
    
- randomized,
    
- removed without harm,
    
- and distributed simultaneously across numerous people.
    

Curious Light has stated that the experiment was conducted primarily out of curiosity.

No strategic objective was identified.

No permanent changes occurred.

The SAFEHOUSE residents have nevertheless requested another event.

Curious Light has not provided a date.

Mischevious Light has apparently volunteered to select the powers next time.

This request has been unanimously rejected.
