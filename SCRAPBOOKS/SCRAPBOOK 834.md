# 1. OVERVIEW

Further observation of the three known **Architects of Reality** has established that their culinary abilities are considerably more nuanced than previously documented.

All three Architects are capable of preparing a broad range of food.

None require food for survival.

Nevertheless, each has developed a particular dish that C.O.R.E. personnel consider their **signature preparation.**

The current list is:

|Architect|Signature Dish|Assessment|
|---|---|---|
|**Guiding Light**|**Moonlight Muffins**|Exceptional|
|**Curious Light**|**Hotdogs**|Surprisingly exceptional|
|**Mischevious Light**|**Anything made seriously**|Exceptional to concerning|

The distinction with Mischevious Light is particularly important.

His best food is generally produced when he is **not trying to have fun.**

---

# 2. GUIDING LIGHT — MOONLIGHT MUFFINS

## 2.1 Signature Dish

**Name:** Moonlight Muffins  
**Creator:** Guiding Light  
**Category:** Baked dessert  
**Status:** Highly recommended

Moonlight Muffins are Guiding Light's most renowned culinary creation.

They are small, soft muffins with a subtle sweetness and a light, almost airy texture.

Their exact recipe remains private.

However, known ingredients include conventional baking ingredients supplemented by several components whose origins C.O.R.E. has been unable to determine.

The muffins have a faint silvery appearance under low light.

They do not glow strongly.

They simply appear to retain a small amount of moonlight.

Guiding Light considers this perfectly normal.

---

## 2.2 Flavor

Personnel descriptions consistently mention:

- vanilla,
    
- cream,
    
- a mild fruit-like sweetness,
    
- toasted sugar,
    
- and an unusual flavor that several personnel described as:
    

> **“Like being outside at night when everything is peaceful.”**

This description is not scientifically useful.

It is, however, remarkably consistent.

---

## 2.3 Guiding Light's Preparation

Guiding Light prepares the muffins conventionally.

She measures ingredients.

She mixes the batter.

She fills the molds.

She bakes them.

She checks their texture.

She decorates them if desired.

She does **not** rewrite reality to make them perfect.

When asked why:

> **GUIDING LIGHT:**  
> “Because then I wouldn't really be baking.”

This appears to be a common philosophy among the Architects.

Despite possessing reality-altering capabilities, they frequently choose to perform ordinary activities normally.

---

# 3. CURIOUS LIGHT — HOTDOGS

## 3.1 Signature Dish

**Name:** Hotdogs  
**Creator:** Curious Light  
**Category:** Grilled food  
**Status:** Extremely popular

Curious Light's most successful dish is, unexpectedly, a hotdog.

Not an elaborate supernatural recipe.

Not a multidimensional delicacy.

Not some impossible cosmic food.

Just a really good hotdog.

Curious Light takes considerable pride in this.

---

## 3.2 Preparation

Curious Light is extremely particular about grilling.

He carefully controls:

- grill temperature,
    
- cooking time,
    
- browning,
    
- sausage rotation,
    
- bun temperature,
    
- and topping distribution.
    

The resulting hotdogs have a pronounced grilled flavor without becoming excessively charred.

He also insists that the bun be warmed on the grill.

According to Curious:

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Cold buns ruin the experience.”

No further explanation was considered necessary.

---

## 3.3 The Architects' Hotdog Argument

Guiding Light prefers a relatively simple hotdog.

Mischevious Light adds whatever he happens to find interesting.

Curious Light considers both approaches questionable.

One recorded conversation:

> “Blueberry jam?”

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**

> “You haven't tried it.”

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “I don't need to.”

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “I already put it on yours.”

Silence.

> **CURIOUS LIGHT:**

Mischevious ran.

# 4. MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT — THE UNINTENTIONAL MASTERPIECES

## 4.1 Signature Dish

**Name:** Everything  
**Creator:** Mischevious Light  
**Category:** Everything  
**Status:** Inconsistent

Mischevious Light possesses the broadest culinary skillset of the three.

He can prepare almost anything.

However, an unusual pattern has emerged.

His **best food is produced when he is not attempting to be entertaining.**

When Mischevious decides:

> _“I'm going to make something really good.”_

the result is often excellent.

When he decides:

> _“I'm going to have fun with this.”_

the result becomes unpredictable.

---

# 5. THE “FUN” VARIABLE

C.O.R.E. has identified two distinct Mischevious cooking modes.

### MODE A — SERIOUS

Mischevious prepares food normally.

He follows the recipe.

He considers seasoning carefully.

He checks temperatures.

He tastes the food.

He adjusts ingredients.

He produces exceptionally good meals.

Personnel have repeatedly described these dishes as **restaurant-quality or better.**

### MODE B — “FUN”

Mischevious becomes bored.

He starts experimenting.

The recipe becomes optional.

Physics becomes optional.

Sometimes the food becomes sentient.

Sometimes the plate becomes sentient.

Sometimes the kitchen becomes involved.

The food is not necessarily bad.

It is simply no longer predictable.

---

# 6. RECORDED EXAMPLE

Mischevious once prepared a completely ordinary roast.

No reality manipulation.

No jokes.

No unusual ingredients.

No dimensional anomalies.

He simply cooked.

The result was described by Director Lucious as:

> **“Probably the best roast I've ever had.”**

Mischevious smiled.

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Thanks.”

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> “Why don't you cook like this more often?”

Mischevious paused.

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Because it's less funny.”

Lucious sighed.

---

# 7. C.O.R.E. TESTING

A controlled test was conducted.

Mischevious was instructed:

> **“Prepare a meal. Do not alter it for entertainment purposes.”**

He complied.

The resulting meal received exceptionally high scores from all participating personnel.

A second test was conducted.

He was told:

> **“Now make something fun.”**

Mischevious smiled.

The resulting dish:

- changed color,
    
- changed flavor three times,
    
- briefly levitated,
    
- attempted to escape the plate,
    
- and complimented one of the researchers.
    

The researcher gave it a 9/10.

The food gave the researcher a 10/10.

The researchers have since requested that Mischevious stop making food that can evaluate them.

# 9. FINAL C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The Architects' culinary capabilities are now considered a legitimate recreational interest rather than an anomalous necessity.

Personnel are permitted to request food from them.

However, requests should be specific.

Particularly when addressing Mischevious Light.

### Recommended:

> **“Could you make us some normal pasta?”**

### Not recommended:

> **“Make something interesting.”**

### Absolutely not recommended:

> **“Do whatever you want.”**

The last instruction resulted in an incident involving a lasagna that occupied two dimensions simultaneously.

The lasagna was delicious.

The table was not.
