# 1. GENERAL OBSERVATION

Experienced Blacksite personnel have become accustomed to Plush Neri.

New employees have not.

As a result, Neri routinely produces some of the most confused reactions observed anywhere in Sector 4-C.

The common pattern is:

> **Employee sees tiny plush girl.**

> **Employee assumes ordinary plush.**

> **Plush girl moves.**

> **Employee experiences approximately three seconds of existential crisis.**

---

# 2. INCIDENT A — "IT MOVED"

**EMPLOYEE:** Cafeteria Assistant  
**TENURE:** 4 days

Employee was cleaning a table when they noticed a small plush figure sitting underneath it.

They picked Neri up.

Neri looked at them.

Employee froze.

Neri blinked.

Employee slowly put her back down.

Neri stood up.

Employee:

> "...No."

Neri tilted her head.

Employee:

> "Nope."

Neri walked away.

Employee immediately reported:

> **"There is a haunted stuffed animal in the cafeteria."**

C.O.R.E. clarified that the "haunted stuffed animal" was Neri.

Employee apologized to Neri.

Neri accepted the apology by nodding.

---

# 3. INCIDENT B — THE HEADPAT DISCOVERY

**EMPLOYEE:** Security trainee

The employee encountered Neri standing beside a corridor wall.

Employee:

> "Uh... hello?"

Neri looked up.

Employee crouched.

> "You're Neri, right?"

Neri nodded.

Employee hesitated.

> "...Can I pat your head?"

Neri lowered her head.

Employee gently patted her.

Neri's eyes closed.

Employee immediately looked at the nearby senior guard.

> "Oh my god."

Senior guard:

> "What?"

> "She's actually a plushie."

Senior guard:

> "We've been over this."

# 4. INCIDENT C — THE COFFEE PROBLEM

**EMPLOYEE:** Junior Laboratory Technician

Employee discovered Neri sitting on a laboratory stool.

Neri was holding a tiny cup of coffee.

The employee stared.

> "Is that coffee?"

Neri nodded.

> "You're ten."

Neri nodded.

> "You're a child."

Neri nodded.

> "And you're drinking coffee."

Neri nodded.

The employee eventually asked:

> "...Do you want water?"

Neri shook her head.

She took another sip.

The employee walked away.

Approximately thirty seconds later:

> "WHY DOES THE PLUSHIE HAVE COFFEE?"

# 6. INCIDENT E — THE 12 TB REVELATION

One new IT employee was assigned to inspect Neri's laptop.

They were informed:

> **Storage: 12 TB SSD**

They asked:

> "Neri."

> "Her?"

Neko:

> "Yes."

> "That plushie?"

> "Yes."

> "She filled twelve terabytes?"

> "Yes."

Neri looked up.

> "...Coffee."

The employee stared at Neko.

> "I'm going to pretend this is normal."

Neko:

> "That's the healthiest response."

# 7. INCIDENT F — THE BITE

**EMPLOYEE:** New maintenance worker

Employee reached down to move a cable.

Neri was nearby.

She walked over.

She bit the employee's sleeve.

Employee froze.

> "...Did the plushie just bite me?"

> "Yes."

# 14. FINAL OBSERVATION

New employees tend to arrive at the Blacksite expecting anomalous entities to be terrifying.

Then they encounter Neri.

Tiny.

Soft.

Wearing glasses.

Sometimes carrying coffee.

Sometimes being carried by Alice.

Sometimes biting someone.

Sometimes sitting inside a cardboard box.

Sometimes solving chemistry problems that make senior researchers uncomfortable.

The contrast is responsible for an unusually high number of confused employees.

Neri herself appears largely indifferent to the attention.

She does not seem interested in explaining herself.

She simply continues existing.

Sometimes she wants a headpat.

Sometimes she wants coffee.

Sometimes she wants to be carried.

Sometimes she bites someone.

Sometimes she sits inside a box for reasons known only to herself.

And sometimes she fills another hard drive.
