# SAFEHOUSE PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION

## SUBJECT: NERI

### RECOVERED MEMORY FRAGMENT

**Status:** Unprompted recollection  
**Source:** Subject Neri  
**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL — RESTRICTED

---

Neri was seated at her usual cafeteria table.

Cake occupied one hand.

A chemistry book occupied the other.

For several minutes, she appeared completely normal.

Then she stopped reading.

Her eyes remained fixed on the same line.

She slowly lowered the book.

> **NERI:**  
> "...I remembered something."

The attending personnel asked what she remembered.

Neri was quiet for several seconds.

Then:

> **NERI:**  
> "When I was little... I learned something."

She pulled her sleeves farther over her hands.

> **NERI:**  
> "If you attract too much attention to myself..."

She paused.

The sentence appeared difficult to finish.

> **NERI:**  
> "...I'll be too much of a trouble for people to keep around."

Another pause.

> **NERI:**  
> "And I'll get thrown."

No further explanation was initially provided.

When asked what "thrown" meant, Neri did not answer.

She simply looked down at her cake.

> **NERI:**  
> "So I learned not to be noticed."

---

### OBSERVED CORRELATION

The recollection provides a possible explanation for several previously documented behaviors.

Neri does not merely avoid asking for help.

She appears to have learned, during childhood, that **visibility itself was dangerous**.

Attention was not associated with protection.

Attention was associated with evaluation.

Evaluation was associated with usefulness.

And failure to remain useful was associated with disposal.

As a result, Neri's current behavior may follow an internal rule established during childhood:

> **Do not make yourself noticeable.**  
> **Do not become troublesome.**  
> **Do not interrupt people.**  
> **Do not require resources.**  
> **Do not make people come looking for you.**  
> **If something happens, endure it quietly.**

This would explain why Neri can be locked inside a room for hours and simply wait.

Why she can become trapped outside and remain there.

Why she can fall or injure herself without immediately seeking assistance.

Why she can experience an anemic episode and remain silent.

Why she can be sitting directly beside someone without speaking to them.

And why, when eventually discovered, her response is frequently:

> **"They were busy."**

---

### ADDITIONAL OBSERVATION

Neri was asked whether she believed people at SAFEHOUSE would actually "throw her away."

She immediately answered:

> **NERI:**  
> "No."

When asked why she continued behaving as though they might:

Neri became visibly uncomfortable.

> **NERI:**  
> "...I know they won't."

Another pause.

> **NERI:**  
> "But that's what I learned."

She picked up her fork.

She took a bite of cake.

Then, almost inaudibly:

> **NERI:**  
> "It's hard to forget something that kept you safe."

---

### ASSESSMENT

This memory substantially reframes Neri's increasing tendency to become a "background character."

She is not consciously attempting to disappear.

She is applying an old survival strategy to a new environment.

In her childhood environment:

**Attention could lead to disposal.**

At SAFEHOUSE:

**Attention generally leads to care.**

Neri understands the second statement intellectually.

Her behavior still follows the first.

This creates a dangerous contradiction.

She may genuinely believe that SAFEHOUSE is safe while simultaneously behaving as though being noticed is something she must avoid.

The result is a subject who can be surrounded by people who care about her while remaining convinced that **the safest thing she can do is require nothing from them.**

---

### FINAL NOTE

Neri was later asked whether she wanted people to notice her more.

She thought about it for a long time.

Then she answered:

> **NERI:**  
> "...Sometimes."

> **STAFF:**  
> "Why only sometimes?"

Neri looked toward the cafeteria entrance.

> **NERI:**  
> "Because if they notice me..."

She stopped.

Her fingers tightened around the sleeve covering her hand.

> **NERI:**  
> "...then they'll have to do something."

She looked down.

> **NERI:**  
> "And I don't want to be a problem."

**End of observation.**
