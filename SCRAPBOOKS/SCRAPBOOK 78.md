# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **PERSONNEL WELLNESS OBSERVATION**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – EXECUTIVE HEALTH MONITORING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Medical, Psychological & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **OVERVIEW**

Recent observations indicate that Head of Accounting **Corin Lockwood** has increasingly remained at KCA Headquarters long after normal working hours.

Although her work performance has remained exemplary, staff have noted a gradual increase in self-isolating behavior.

Most evenings, the Accounting Department is the first office to become quiet.

Corin's is often the last light to turn off.

Sometimes...

Not until sunrise.

---

## **WORK AS A COPING MECHANISM**

Corin has openly acknowledged that work helps her manage intrusive memories.

Numbers are predictable.

Budgets follow rules.

Spreadsheets cannot hurt people.

For several hours at a time, balancing accounts allows her to focus entirely on solving problems with clear answers.

During one counseling session, she explained:

> "...When I'm working..."

> "...My mind is quieter."

Unfortunately, this strategy is not always effective.

When the memories return, they do so regardless of how many reports remain on her desk.

On those nights, work becomes less about productivity...

And more about trying not to think.

---

## **AFTER-HOURS PATTERN**

Security records show a recurring routine.

Corin locks her office.

The access controls engage.

She reviews reports.

Makes corrections.

Organizes tomorrow's paperwork.

Then...

Eventually...

The work stops.

She remains seated in silence.

Some nights she cries.

Other nights she simply stares at the same page for several minutes without turning it.

By morning, every trace of emotion has disappeared.

The paperwork is complete.

The office is spotless.

And Corin greets everyone with her usual gentle smile.

---

## **THE SEALED OFFICE**

Corin's office is routinely secured from the inside while she is working after hours.

This is an intentional privacy measure she has used for years to prevent interruptions during financial reviews.

There is no indication that the practice itself is unusual.

However, following recent wellness concerns, Executive Command authorized a confidential welfare protocol.

If Corin remains isolated for an unusually prolonged period and fails to respond to routine check-ins, designated personnel may override the office lock solely for the purpose of confirming her safety.

The protocol is to be used sparingly and with respect for her privacy.

---

## **COGNITIVE PROFILE**

Corin is widely regarded as one of the KCA's most intellectually gifted executives.

Colleagues often describe her as a "walking calculator."

Her documented abilities include:

- Exceptional mental arithmetic.
    
- Near-perfect financial recall.
    
- Advanced logistical planning.
    
- Long-term strategic forecasting.
    
- Rapid identification of accounting discrepancies.
    

She is capable of processing complex financial reports with remarkable speed and accuracy.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE**

Despite her exceptional cognitive abilities, psychological evaluations consistently note that Corin remains emotionally vulnerable.

The same mind that effortlessly retains budgets, statistics, and operational data also retains painful memories with unusual clarity.

One psychologist summarized the contrast as follows:

> "Corin remembers almost everything."

> "Unfortunately, that includes the things no child should ever have had to remember."

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Following review of the latest wellness reports, Director Lucious issued an instruction to all executive staff.

> "Corin's intelligence has never been in question."

> "Her wellbeing should never be overlooked simply because she continues to perform exceptionally."

He added one final sentence.

> "The people who appear strongest are sometimes the ones who have become the most practiced at hiding when they are hurting."

---

## **STATUS**

Corin continues to serve as Head of Accounting with the same precision and professionalism that have defined her career.

To most employees, she is the calm executive who somehow keeps the KCA's finances balanced despite impossible demands.

To those closest to her, she is also someone who continues to heal from profound trauma.

The reports on her desk are always completed.

The budgets are always accurate.

The office is always immaculate.

Her friends have learned that none of those things necessarily mean she is carrying her burdens alone—and they continue to remind her that she doesn't have to.

**End of Personnel Wellness Observation**
