### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / WING STRUCTURE REPORT**

# **CORIN — LUMINESCENT SEMI-TRANSPARENT WINGS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **Semi-Transparent Wing Structure**  
**Status:** Stable

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's wings possess several unusual optical and structural properties that distinguish them from conventional avian wings.

The wings are **semi-transparent**, allowing their internal skeletal structure to become visible under sufficiently intense illumination.

Their feathers also produce a faint natural luminescence in darkness.

Despite their delicate appearance, the wings are substantially thicker and more structurally capable than their appearance suggests.

---

# **SEMI-TRANSPARENCY**

Under normal lighting, Corin's wings appear mostly opaque due to the density of their overlapping feathers.

However, when exposed to an intense light source, such as an industrial floodlight, light can penetrate through the feather layers.

The internal wing skeleton becomes visible.

The effect resembles a naturally illuminated anatomical diagram.

The bones and supporting structures can be seen beneath the feathers while the outer surface continues to retain its normal appearance.

---

# **WING SKELETON**

The internal structure is unusually robust.

The wing contains a complex arrangement of elongated bones and supporting structures that allow Corin to maintain flight despite the considerable size and weight of her wings.

The skeleton is also capable of withstanding the forces generated during:

- Rapid acceleration.
    
- Sudden changes in direction.
    
- High-speed flight.
    
- Feather deployment.
    
- Hard landings.
    

Despite this structural strength, the wings remain highly flexible.

---

# **LUMINESCENT FEATHERS**

Corin's feathers emit a faint glow in darkness.

The intensity is relatively low under ordinary conditions, but becomes clearly visible in a dark room.

The glow appears to originate from within the feathers themselves rather than from reflected light.

No external power source has been identified.

Ether is considered the most likely source.

The resulting appearance has been described as:

> **"A pair of glowing wings in the dark."**

Corin likes them.

---

# **LIGHT TRANSMISSION**

The combination of semi-transparent structure and feather luminescence creates an unusual visual effect.

Under strong external illumination:

**The skeleton becomes visible.**

Under darkness:

**The feathers become visible through their own glow.**

Under ordinary lighting:

**The wings appear mostly opaque and naturally feathered.**

Corin therefore has three noticeably different appearances depending on the lighting environment.

---

# **STRUCTURAL THICKNESS**

The wings are considerably thicker than their silhouette suggests.

The dense feather arrangement creates substantial internal space between layers.

This is partially responsible for their ability to function as a protective enclosure when Corin wraps them around herself.

The wings can effectively form a large feathered barrier around her body.

---

# **THE "WHY IS THAT IN THERE?" INCIDENT**

During a routine inspection, personnel discovered that Corin had somehow managed to conceal an **M1014 sawed-off shotgun** within the folded layers of one wing.

Nobody had previously realized the wings could accommodate something of that size.

Corin was asked why she had it.

She responded:

> **"...I don't remember."**

A second person asked how it got there.

Corin shrugged.

> **"Maybe I put it there."**

The inspection was immediately terminated after confirming that the weapon was unloaded and secured.

The incident is now informally referred to as:

> **THE WING STORAGE INCIDENT**

---

# **WING STORAGE CAPABILITY**

C.O.R.E. does **not** classify the wings as conventional storage compartments.

However, their unusual thickness and flexibility allow Corin to temporarily carry objects within the folded feather layers.

This is an incidental property rather than a confirmed biological function.

Personnel are reminded that Corin's wings are **not** to be treated as a general-purpose inventory system.

Especially not for firearms.

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

When informed that personnel had formally documented the incident, Corin appeared embarrassed.

> **"You didn't have to write that down."**

Neko replied:

> **"You hid a shotgun in your wing."**

Corin paused.

> **"...Fair."**

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's wings are difficult to classify.

They are biological.

They are anomalous.

They are semi-transparent.

They glow in darkness.

They can become invisible when folded.

They can phase through clothing.

They can regenerate feathers every 45 minutes.

They can launch feathers as blades or Ether-infused projectiles.

They can wrap around Corin like a blanket.

And, apparently, they have enough internal volume for Corin to somehow forget that she was carrying a shotgun.

C.O.R.E. has officially added one final note to the wing documentation:

> **"Please stop putting things in the wings."**

Corin has not confirmed that she intends to comply.
