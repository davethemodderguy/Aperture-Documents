# BLACKSITE INCIDENT REPORT

## WOODLAND COORP ASSAULT — NERI RESPONSE

**Incident Classification:** Major Hostile Incursion  
**Attacking Force:** Woodland Coorp Mercenary Detachment  
**Estimated Personnel:** 250 armed mercenaries  
**Defending Personnel:** Neri — **SOLO**  
**Outcome:** Woodland force routed  
**SAFEHOUSE Casualties:** None reported  
**Primary Defender:** Neri  
**Additional Support:** None during initial engagement

---

# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

Following the discovery of Jane Doe's five-year infiltration and the compromise of Woodland Coorp's internal systems, Woodland initiated an armed assault against the Blacksite.

Approximately **250 armed mercenaries** entered the operational perimeter.

The attackers evidently expected a conventional security response.

They did not receive one.

The first person they encountered was Neri.

And, for the initial engagement, **Neri was the only person who engaged them.**

---

# 2. INITIAL CONTACT

The mercenary formation advanced through the perimeter expecting resistance from Blacksite security.

Instead, they encountered a ten-year-old Seal Thiren standing alone.

Neri was carrying:

- A crossbow.
    
- Her Starlight Katana.
    
- Multiple concealed sharpened hair clips.
    
- Multiple concealed knives.
    
- Her scarf.
    
- Additional concealed equipment of unknown quantity.
    

The mercenaries initially assumed she was simply a resident who had failed to evacuate.

That assumption lasted approximately several seconds.

Neri looked at the approaching formation.

Then she smiled.

---

# 3. PSYCHOLOGICAL BREAK

Neri's reputation for intimidation is normally limited to SAFEHOUSE bullying incidents.

Woodland had never encountered it.

That changed immediately.

The first few mercenaries began noticing things that did not make sense.

Neri wasn't behaving like someone who was afraid.

She wasn't retreating.

She wasn't calling for help.

She was walking **toward them.**

The formation began losing cohesion.

A few soldiers raised their weapons.

Neri stopped.

Silence.

Then:

> **Neri:** "You shouldn't be here."

One mercenary shouted for the others to advance.

Nobody moved.

Neri tilted her head.

Her expression remained completely calm.

The psychological pressure intensified.

The soldiers began looking around.

There were 250 of them.

There was one of her.

And somehow, they were the ones who felt surrounded.

---

# 4. THE ROUT

The first group broke formation.

Then another.

Then several more.

Within minutes, the organized assault had become a retreat.

Some mercenaries abandoned equipment.

Others simply ran.

Neri pursued the retreating force only long enough to prevent them from regrouping.

The terrifying part was not that Neri had defeated a force of 250.

It was that:

> **Most of them had already decided they had lost before she needed to defeat them.**

The psychological effect of Neri's presence proved devastating.

---

# 5. COMBAT ENGAGEMENT

Not every mercenary fled.

Several attempted to engage Neri directly.

That decision resulted in the situation becoming substantially worse for them.

Neri transitioned between weapons with remarkable speed.

### CROSSBOW

Used for ranged engagement and disrupting advancing personnel.

Neri's preference for the crossbow was notably different from her usual dislike of firearms.

The weapon remained personal.

Close enough to see.

Close enough to control.

---

### STARLIGHT KATANA

Neri deployed the Starlight Katana once the engagement moved into close quarters.

The weapon allowed her to transition rapidly between defensive movement and close combat.

Her mobility made conventional attempts to surround her ineffective.

---

### HAIR CLIPS

The two crossed clips securing Neri's bangs are not decorative.

They are sharpened weapons.

Neri removed them when necessary and used them as close-range blades.

Several mercenaries apparently failed to realize this until far too late.

---

### CONCEALED KNIVES

The number of knives carried by Neri remains unknown.

This continues to be a recurring problem for anyone attempting to search her belongings.

She produced concealed blades from multiple locations during the engagement.

At least one responding observer reportedly stated:

> **"How many knives does that child have?"**

No answer was provided.

---

### SCARF

Neri's scarf should not be mistaken for harmless clothing.

She is capable of using it as a restraint.

Personnel are reminded that the scarf is **not** to be grabbed or pulled casually.

During the engagement, Neri used the scarf defensively to control an attacker before disengaging.

---

# 6. THE FINAL PSYCHOLOGICAL BREAK

The remaining mercenaries eventually realized that they were not dealing with an ordinary child.

They were dealing with a highly mobile, heavily armed Thiren who had absolutely no intention of allowing them to reach the SAFEHOUSE.

One soldier reportedly shouted:

> **"JUST RUN!"**

Another responded:

> **"WE ARE RUNNING!"**

The remaining formation collapsed.

Neri watched them retreat.

She did not chase them beyond the perimeter.

She simply stood there.

Her weapons remained ready.

---

# 7. BLACKSITE OBSERVATION

By the time Blacksite security personnel arrived, the engagement was effectively over.

Neri was standing alone.

Her equipment was scattered around her.

The hostile force was retreating.

An Archivist reportedly stared at the scene for several seconds.

> **Archivist:** "...Where is everyone?"

Neri pointed toward the perimeter.

> **Neri:** "They ran."

> **Archivist:** "All of them?"

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:** "Mostly."

The Archivist looked toward the retreating Woodland forces.

Then back at Neri.

> **Archivist:** "...You did this alone?"

Neri shrugged.

> **Neri:** "They were hurting my friends."

No further questions were asked.

---

# 8. POST-INCIDENT MEDICAL CHECK

Despite the apparent scale of the engagement, Neri's greatest danger remained her own physiology.

Medical personnel immediately checked her for signs of exhaustion or anemic decline.

This was considered mandatory.

Neri's combat capabilities do not eliminate her cardiovascular condition.

She can still experience sudden anemia shock.

She can still collapse without warning.

She can still push herself beyond safe limits when protecting someone.

The fact that she successfully defended the Blacksite does **not** mean she should have been allowed to continue fighting indefinitely.

---

# 9. PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT

Neri's response appears consistent with her established protective behavior.

Her reasoning was not:

> _"I can defeat 250 people."_

It was:

> **"They're threatening my friends."**

The distinction is significant.

Neri's combat behavior becomes substantially more aggressive when she believes someone she cares about is in danger.

This incident therefore represents another example of the same pattern observed previously:

> **Threaten Neri → she fights.**

> **Threaten her friends → she becomes considerably more dangerous.**

---

# 10. WOODLAND AFTER-ACTION ASSESSMENT

Woodland's surviving personnel provided remarkably consistent accounts.

The most common description was:

> **"There was a child."**

Followed by:

> **"She was smiling."**

And finally:

> **"Then everyone started running."**

Woodland's original objective was to force entry into the Blacksite.

Instead, their assault force encountered Neri and lost its cohesion before reaching the SAFEHOUSE.

---

# 11. APERTURE CONCLUSION

The incident has been classified as a **successful defensive engagement**.

However, Blacksite command has issued a reminder:

> **Neri is a resident.**

> **She is not a disposable defensive asset.**

> **Her willingness to protect SAFEHOUSE does not constitute authorization to use her as a frontline combatant.**

The fact that she _can_ fight does not mean she should have to.

---

## FINAL OBSERVATION

Woodland sent **250 armed mercenaries** to the Blacksite.

They expected Aperture to deploy its security forces.

Instead, they encountered Neri.

One ten-year-old Seal Thiren.

One crossbow.

One Starlight Katana.

Two sharpened hair clips.

An unknown number of knives.

A scarf nobody should underestimate.

And a psychological warfare capability that caused an entire armed formation to decide that retreat was the best available option.

The Blacksite did not need to send anyone else.

**Neri had already made her point.**
