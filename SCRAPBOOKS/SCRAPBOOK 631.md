### **TREEHOUSE / RECOVERED PERSONAL PROPERTY**

# **CORIN'S NOTEBOOK**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Item:** Personal Notebook belonging to Corin  
**Condition:** **Heavily Used / Emotionally Significant**  
**Status:** **Recovered**  
**Author:** Corin

---

# **OVERVIEW**

During the search for Corin, personnel recovered a small notebook belonging to her.

It was found inside the Treehouse.

The notebook contains ordinary notes, drawings, observations, lists, and miscellaneous thoughts.

However, entries made during Orphie's absence gradually become darker.

The notebook provides the clearest surviving record of Corin's emotional deterioration.

---

# **EARLY ENTRIES**

The first entries following Orphie's disappearance are relatively calm.

> **"Orphie went to get flowers."**

> **"She'll probably be back soon."**

> **"I hope she got the blue ones."**

Several pages contain drawings of flowers.

One page simply says:

> **"Don't forget to water the plants."**

The handwriting is neat.

---

# **AFTER SEVERAL WEEKS**

The tone changes.

> **"Still no Orphie."**

> **"Maybe she got lost."**

> **"Everyone says she's coming back."**

> **"I believe them."**

A later page reads:

> **"I think."**

The phrase has been written several times.

---

# **THREE MONTHS**

The handwriting becomes noticeably less consistent.

One page contains:

> **"It's been three months."**

Below it:

> **"Maybe she doesn't want to come back."**

Then:

> **"Maybe she doesn't like me anymore."**

The remainder of the page has been scratched out.

---

# **THE SAME QUESTION**

Several pages contain variations of the same thought:

> **"Did I do something?"**

> **"Was I too annoying?"**

> **"Was I too much?"**

> **"Did I make her tired?"**

> **"Why won't she say anything?"**

One page contains only:

> **"Please just tell me."**

---

# **SELF-BLAME**

Later entries become significantly more concerning.

> **"If she hates me, that's okay."**

> **"I just want to know."**

> **"I can handle being yelled at."**

> **"I can't handle silence."**

Another page reads:

> **"I think silence hurts more."**

---

# **THE WAITING**

Corin appears to have documented how long she spent waiting.

Entries include:

> **"Another day."**

> **"Another night."**

> **"Still nothing."**

> **"I thought I heard her."**

> **"It wasn't her."**

> **"I hate footsteps that aren't hers."**

One page contains dozens of small marks.

At the bottom:

> **"I counted every hour."**

---

# **THE TREE**

After Corin began spending more time beneath the Treehouse tree, she wrote:

> **"The tree is quiet."**

> **"I can wait here."**

> **"Maybe she'll see me when she comes back."**

Another entry:

> **"It's cold."**

> **"My wings help."**

> **"I don't want to go inside."**

---

# **THE FEAR**

One of the most heavily worn pages reads:

> **"What if she comes back and I'm not here?"**

This appears to have been one of Corin's primary reasons for remaining near the Treehouse.

She was afraid that leaving would mean missing Orphie's return.

---

# **THE NOTE**

Near the end of the notebook is the sentence previously recovered beside Corin's feather lanterns:

> **"You proved I'm right, thank you."**

The notebook provides additional context.

Several pages earlier, Corin had written:

> **"People always leave eventually."**

Another:

> **"Maybe I'm just someone people stay around until they don't need me anymore."**

And:

> **"I shouldn't get attached."**

The final entry before her disappearance contains only:

> **"I knew it."**

---

# **IMPORTANT FINDING**

The notebook contains **no evidence that Corin knew Orphie had fallen into the cave.**

Every entry was written from Corin's perspective based solely on Orphie's absence.

There is no indication that Corin possessed information regarding Orphie's survival.

Corin genuinely believed she had been abandoned.

---

# **ORPHIE READS THE NOTEBOOK**

Orphie was eventually permitted to read the recovered notebook.

She reached the pages concerning the three-month period.

She stopped.

She continued reading.

Then she reached:

> **"What if she comes back and I'm not here?"**

Orphie began crying.

She continued until reaching the final pages.

After reading:

> **"I knew it."**

she closed the notebook.

She held it against her chest.

For several minutes, she said nothing.

Then:

> **"...You were waiting for me."**

---

# **THE BLUE FLOWERS**

Orphie still has the flowers.

She has kept them intact since returning.

She has repeatedly stated that she intends to give them to Corin personally.

Nobody has told her when that will be possible.

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

The notebook demonstrates that Corin's disappearance was not sudden.

It was preceded by a prolonged deterioration characterized by:

- Isolation.
    
- Self-blame.
    
- Fear of rejection.
    
- Fear of abandonment.
    
- Persistent waiting.
    
- Increasing hopelessness.
    
- Emotional withdrawal.
    

The notebook is therefore considered critical evidence in understanding Corin's current state.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's notebook does not contain a dramatic confession.

It does not contain a final explanation.

It contains something much simpler.

A girl waiting for someone she loved.

A few flowers.

A few drawings.

A growing collection of unanswered questions.

And hundreds of pages documenting the slow transformation of:

> **"She'll be back soon."**

into:

> **"Maybe she doesn't want me anymore."**

and eventually:

> **"I knew it."**

Orphie was lost.

Corin did not know that.

She only knew that the person she trusted most had disappeared.

And the notebook shows exactly what eight months of silence did to her.
