# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **BLACKSITE RECREATIONAL DIVISION**

### **EVENT REPORT – STARLANDS 10 KM CHARITY RACE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Internal Recreation Archive

---

## **EVENT OVERVIEW**

To promote fitness and provide personnel with a relaxing break from their normal duties, the Blacksite Recreational Division organized a **10-kilometer race** across the open fields and floating pathways of the Starlands.

There were very few restrictions.

The most notable rule was:

> **Special abilities are permitted.**

Participants were free to use their natural abilities, equipment, or movement techniques, provided they did not interfere with other racers.

Not everyone chose to participate.

# **RACE HIGHLIGHTS**

## 🥇 Belle Merryweather

Belle's strategy confused nearly everyone.

Instead of maintaining a conventional running form, she accelerated into what several observers described as a **"cartoon sprint."**

Her legs became a blur, her rabbit ears streamed behind her, and she leaned so far forward it looked as though she should have fallen over.

She didn't.

She maintained the pace for nearly the entire race.

Neko later insisted that she looked like she had stepped out of an old animated cartoon.

Belle crossed the finish line first, still smiling.

> "...That was fun!"

---

## 🥈 Remielle "Cinder"

While others relied on unique abilities, Cinder simply...

Ran.

No firebending.

No equipment.

Just disciplined pacing and excellent endurance.

She maintained a remarkably consistent speed throughout the course and secured second place.

After finishing, she immediately asked if anyone needed water.

---

## 🥉 Mart

Mart took advantage of his owl physiology.

Using controlled bursts of flight where the course allowed, he conserved energy and maintained a smooth pace.

He landed just before the finish line to officially cross it on foot, satisfying race regulations.

---

## 4th — Taiga

Taiga's snow leopard agility let her weave effortlessly through rough terrain.

She admitted afterward that she became distracted chasing butterflies for a few seconds.

No one could confirm whether she was joking.

---

## 5th — Star

Star alternated between running and using short bursts of light-assisted movement.

She spent most of the race cheering for everyone else despite being a participant.

---

## 6th — Bao

Bao maintained an even, relaxed pace from start to finish.

She appeared more interested in enjoying the scenery than competing for first place.

---

## 7th — Frost

Frost ran calmly and consistently.

She later explained that she had intentionally avoided sprinting to conserve energy.

The Starlands 10 km Race was declared a complete success.

No injuries occurred.

No one fell off a floating island.

And Belle's **Signature Cartoon Sprint** has since become one of the Blacksite's most recognizable—and most amusing—running styles.
