## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### RESIDENT WELFARE & MEDICAL DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-JAKE-165  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — RESIDENT WELFARE / TRAINING INCIDENT  
**STATUS:** CLOSED — OBSERVATIONAL RECORD  
**SUBJECT:** Jake — Extreme Anger Response  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE TRAINING ROOM

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has demonstrated an unexpected capability.

Under ordinary circumstances, Jake is physically non-confrontational.

He avoids conflict.

He avoids attention.

He generally retreats when threatened.

This assessment changed during a single training-room incident.

The trigger was:

**A spilled cup of milk.**

The target was:

**A training dummy.**

The result was:

**A remarkably comprehensive demonstration of hand-to-hand combat.**

This has occurred **once.**

C.O.R.E. has found no evidence that Jake routinely behaves this way.

---

# 1. THE INCIDENT

Jake had entered the training room carrying a cup of milk.

He was not participating in training.

He was simply walking through the room.

A weighted training dummy had been positioned near the center of the floor.

Jake walked past it.

The dummy shifted unexpectedly.

Its arm struck Jake's cup.

The cup fell.

Milk spilled across the floor.

Jake stopped.

He looked at the milk.

Then at the dummy.

There was approximately five seconds of complete silence.

A nearby observer reported that Jake's expression changed.

Not fear.

Not sadness.

**Anger.**

---

# 2. THE FIRST ATTACK

Jake approached the dummy.

He stared at it.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...You spilled my milk."

The dummy, being a dummy, did not respond.

Jake kicked it.

The dummy rocked backward.

Jake immediately followed with a second strike.

At this point, personnel assumed Jake was simply venting frustration.

They were incorrect.

---

# 3. ESCALATION

Jake proceeded to attack the training dummy with a sequence of highly energetic movements.

The observed sequence included:

- multiple dropkicks;
    
- several upward strikes;
    
- a shoulder throw;
    
- repeated impacts against the padded dummy;
    
- and several additional strikes after the dummy had already fallen.
    

The exact sequence is difficult to reconstruct because Jake moved considerably faster than personnel expected.

The dummy was not capable of defending itself.

It did not technically need to.

It was still subjected to the full demonstration.

---

# 4. THE DROPKICKS

The most unusual portion of the incident involved Jake repeatedly launching himself at the dummy.

The first dropkick knocked the dummy backward.

Jake landed.

Recovered his footing.

Then immediately launched himself again.

A second.

Then a third.

The third impact caused the dummy's weighted base to shift several centimeters.

At this point, one observer reportedly said:

> **"Why is the six-year-old doing combo attacks?"**

No answer was provided.

---

# 5. UPPER STRIKES

Jake then closed distance and struck upward several times.

The movements were controlled enough to suggest that Jake understood what he was doing.

This was not random flailing.

He appeared to be deliberately targeting the upper portion of the dummy.

Personnel reviewing the footage later noted that Jake's movements became increasingly precise as his anger increased.

This is the first recorded instance of such behavior.

---

# 6. THE SHOULDER THROW

The incident culminated in Jake grabbing the dummy and throwing it over his shoulder.

The dummy landed heavily on the training mat.

Jake stood over it.

He looked down.

There was a long pause.

Then:

> **JAKE:**  
> "...My milk."

He kicked the dummy once more.

---

# 7. END OF INCIDENT

Jake eventually stopped.

His breathing was elevated.

His white hair was slightly disheveled.

His azure eyes remained brightly illuminated.

The celestial streaks within his eyes appeared significantly more active than normal.

The teddy bear remained securely attached to his back.

Jake looked around the room.

Then at the spilled milk.

His anger abruptly diminished.

He looked embarrassed.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...I got mad."

The instructor nodded cautiously.

> **INSTRUCTOR:**  
> "Yes."

Jake looked at the destroyed training dummy.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I didn't mean to."

The instructor looked at the dummy.

> **INSTRUCTOR:**  
> "I believe you."

---

# 8. DUMMY CONDITION

The training dummy remained structurally intact.

However, its outer padding had been substantially damaged.

Its weighted base had shifted.

One shoulder joint had become loose.

The facility maintenance report concluded:

> **"Dummy remains usable, but has clearly had a difficult afternoon."**

---

# 9. FOLLOW-UP INTERVIEW

C.O.R.E. later asked Jake why he reacted so strongly.

Jake appeared confused by the question.

> **C.O.R.E.:**  
> "Were you angry at the dummy?"

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> "It spilled my milk."

> **C.O.R.E.:**  
> "You understand that the dummy didn't intentionally spill it?"

Jake paused.

He looked down.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...It still spilled it."

No further comment was obtained.

---

# 10. IMPORTANT OBSERVATION

Jake's normal behavioral profile remains unchanged.

He is:

- quiet;
    
- non-confrontational;
    
- easily startled;
    
- uncomfortable with sudden events;
    
- reluctant to inconvenience others;
    
- generally avoidant of conflict;
    
- and unlikely to initiate physical confrontation.
    

The training incident should therefore **not** be interpreted as Jake becoming aggressive.

It represents an unusual response under extreme emotional activation.

The trigger was exceptionally minor.

The response was exceptionally disproportionate.

It has not been repeated.

---

# 11. CELESTIAL CORRELATION

Personnel reviewing the footage noticed that Jake's recent celestial manifestations became more pronounced during the incident.

His eyes brightened.

The shooting-star streaks accelerated.

Several small azure particles appeared around him.

No measurable increase in anomalous energy was detected.

C.O.R.E. therefore cannot currently establish whether anger caused the celestial response or merely coincided with it.

Further intentional testing is **not recommended.**

There is no reason to make Jake angry on purpose.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Jake possesses demonstrable hand-to-hand combat capability.

However, there is insufficient evidence to determine whether this is:

- an inherent ability;
    
- residual instinct;
    
- anomalous physical adaptation;
    
- an unconscious manifestation of his celestial state;
    
- or simply an extremely motivated response to losing a cup of milk.
    

Only one incident has occurred.

No training regimen is currently being assigned.

No combat testing is currently authorized.

Personnel are specifically instructed **not to spill Jake's food or drinks intentionally to reproduce the event.**

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The training room now has a replacement dummy.

Jake was asked whether he wanted to apologize to it.

He did.

He stood in front of the new dummy.

He looked at it.

Then quietly said:

> **JAKE:**  
> "Sorry."

He walked away.

Three steps later, he stopped.

Turned around.

And added:

> **JAKE:**  
> "Don't spill my milk."

The dummy remained silent.

This was considered an appropriate conclusion.
