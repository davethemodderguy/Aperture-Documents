# 1. APPEARANCE UPDATE

Jake has acquired a new set of accessories.

The additions are:

- a **black top hat**;
    
- a **glowing azure ribbon** wrapped around the hat;
    
- and a **cane**.
    

Combined with Jake's existing appearance, the result is immediately recognizable.

Jake now looks like a **ringmaster**.

There is, however, one significant difference.

He is not wearing a ringmaster's suit.

He is simply wearing:

> **The hat.**

> **The ribbon.**

> **The cane.**

And somehow, this is enough.

---

# 2. THE TOP HAT

The hat is a traditional-looking black top hat.

Its proportions are slightly oversized for Jake's six-year-old body.

The glowing azure ribbon provides the only obvious anomalous feature.

The ribbon emits a soft blue light consistent with Jake's existing stellar manifestation.

The light is not bright enough to interfere with normal vision.

It does, however, make Jake extremely easy to identify from a distance.

Especially when combined with:

- glowing blue eyes;
    
- fractured halo;
    
- celestial particles;
    
- white hair;
    
- restored wings;
    
- and a teddy bear strapped to his back.
    

---

# 3. THE CANE

Jake also carries a cane.

Its primary purpose appears to be aesthetic.

No medical requirement has been identified.

Jake does not require the cane for walking.

He does not use it to support his weight.

He simply carries it.

Occasionally, he taps it against the floor.

Occasionally, he spins it.

Occasionally, he holds it behind his back while walking.

Nobody appears to know why.

When asked:

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “Why do you have a cane?”

Jake looked at the cane.

Then at the officer.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Because.”

Pause.

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “That's not an answer.”

Jake looked at the cane again.

> **JAKE:**  
> “Ringmaster.”

The officer accepted this.

---

# 4. THE RINGMASTER EFFECT

The visual combination has produced an unintended consequence.

Jake now looks like he is about to announce the beginning of a circus.

This is particularly strange because he remains:

- six years old;
    
- quiet;
    
- usually carrying his teddy bear;
    
- and occasionally wearing an expression suggesting that he has absolutely no idea why everyone is staring at him.
    

The cane makes the effect significantly stronger.

The top hat makes it worse.

The glowing azure ribbon makes it unmistakable.

---

**Top hat.**

**Cane.**

For documentation purposes, Jake's current appearance is now recorded as:

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The accessories do not appear to alter Jake's abilities, medical condition, or stellar manifestation.

They are simply part of his current appearance.

No operational concern has been identified.

The cane is not a weapon.

The top hat is not anomalous equipment.

The ribbon is primarily decorative.

The teddy bear remains medically critical.

C.O.R.E. therefore recommends allowing Jake to continue wearing the accessories provided that they do not interfere with his safety.
