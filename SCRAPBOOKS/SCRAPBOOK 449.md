### **CELESTIAL ARCHITECTURE / PERSONNEL INTERACTION REPORT**

# **LUCY MERRYWEATHER — MEETING THE GUIDING LIGHT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Celestial Authority  
**Subject:** Guiding Light  
**Personnel:** Lucy Merryweather  
**Titles:** Warden of the Banlands / Mother of Lindervene "Endmin" Merryweather and Belle Merryweather  
**Status:** **NON-HOSTILE / FAMILIAR**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

**Lucy Merryweather**, known as the **Warden of the Banlands**, has formally met Guiding Light.

The encounter was notable not because Lucy was intimidated by Guiding Light's status as one of the two Architects...

but because Lucy appeared to already understand exactly what Guiding Light was.

As Warden of the Banlands, Lucy possesses extensive knowledge of the realm and its metaphysical structure.

Her meeting with one of its Architects was therefore unusually calm.

---

# **THE ENCOUNTER**

Lucy was visiting the SAFEHOUSE when she noticed the familiar soft blue glow.

Guiding Light's manifested form was standing near the central living area.

Her shattered halo floated above her head.

Fragments of her wings drifted gently behind her.

The crescent on her chest glowed faintly.

Lucy stopped.

> **Lucy:**  
> "...So you're finally showing yourself."

Guiding Light turned toward her.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Hello, Lucy."

Lucy smiled.

> **Lucy:**  
> "Guiding Light."

There was no surprise in her voice.

---

# **THE WARDEN AND THE ARCHITECT**

Lucy looked over Guiding Light's fragmented form.

> **Lucy:**  
> "You haven't changed much."

Guiding Light gave a small laugh.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "You remember me?"

> **Lucy:**  
> "I remember the person who helped shape the realm I protect."

Guiding Light smiled.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "You have taken good care of it."

Lucy nodded.

> **Lucy:**  
> "Someone had to."

---

# **THE BANLANDS**

Their conversation eventually turned toward the Banlands.

Lucy asked:

> **Lucy:**  
> "Do you still watch over it?"

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "I watch over everything."

Lucy raised an eyebrow.

> **Lucy:**  
> "That wasn't my question."

Guiding Light laughed.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Yes."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "I still watch."

Lucy seemed satisfied.

---

# **LUCY'S CHILDREN**

Guiding Light already knew about Lucy's family.

She mentioned them without being prompted.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Endmin has grown."

Lucy smiled.

> **Lucy:**  
> "She has."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "And Belle."

Lucy sighed fondly.

> **Lucy:**  
> "Still Belle."

Guiding Light laughed.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Still Belle."

The conversation immediately became less formal.

---

# **A SURPRISING QUESTION**

Lucy eventually looked directly at the headband covering Guiding Light's eyes.

> **Lucy:**  
> "Do you ever take that thing off?"

Guiding Light paused.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "No."

> **Lucy:**  
> "Can you?"

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Yes."

Lucy waited.

> **Lucy:**  
> "...And?"

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "I don't."

Lucy nodded.

> **Lucy:**  
> "Fair enough."

---

# **LUCY'S UNDERSTANDING**

Lucy appeared to understand something that most personnel had not yet grasped.

The form standing in front of her was not Guiding Light herself.

It was merely a manifestation.

Lucy therefore did not ask where Guiding Light had come from.

She already knew the answer.

Guiding Light was everywhere.

The manifestation was simply where she had chosen to be.

---

# **A FRIENDLY CONVERSATION**

Eventually, Lucy asked:

> **Lucy:**  
> "So, how long have you been keeping an eye on everyone here?"

Guiding Light smiled.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Long enough."

> **Lucy:**  
> "That sounds ominous."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "It wasn't meant to."

Lucy laughed.

> **Lucy:**  
> "You're still terrible at sounding mysterious."

Guiding Light:

> **"...I'm an Architect."**

Lucy:

> **"And?"**

Guiding Light laughed.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The meeting between Lucy Merryweather and Guiding Light was remarkably uneventful.

No confrontation occurred.

No display of power occurred.

No formal ceremony was required.

A Warden of the Banlands simply met one of the beings who helped forge the realm she protects.

They spoke about the Banlands.

They spoke about Lucy's children.

They joked.

And eventually, they simply continued their day.

Perhaps the most significant part of the encounter was how naturally Lucy treated Guiding Light.

Not as an unreachable cosmic force.

Not as an object of worship.

But as someone she could simply talk to.

For one of reality's Architects...

that appeared to be exactly how Guiding Light preferred it.
