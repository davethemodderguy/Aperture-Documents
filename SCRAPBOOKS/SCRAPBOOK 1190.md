# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The MSAC Scout involved in the recent theft of **Nora Tachyon's training Night Stalker replica** has become the subject of widespread ridicule among MSAC personnel.

The reason is simple.

He stole a toy.

From a six-year-old.

He was then discovered.

The six-year-old chased him.

The six-year-old subsequently recruited Alice.

Alice provided a battle axe.

The six-year-old chased him through the Blacksite while carrying said axe.

The Scout eventually surrendered the toy.

No personnel were injured.

Several walls were.

The incident has since become one of the most frequently referenced jokes within MSAC.

---

# 2. THE OFFICIAL EXPLANATION

The Scout's written incident statement reads:

> **"I believed the item was a restricted Tachyon Industries training device and intended to inspect it."**

The MSAC response was:

> **"You stole a child's toy."**

The Scout replied:

> **"It is technically a Tachyon Industries product."**

The MSAC response:

> **"YOU STOLE A CHILD'S TOY."**

No further argument was recorded.

---

# 8. MSAC COMMAND

MSAC Command has issued a reminder:

> **Personnel are not to take personal property belonging to SAFEHOUSE residents without authorization.**

A second reminder was issued specifically concerning children.

A third reminder was issued because apparently the first two were necessary.

The relevant Scout has been formally reprimanded.

No operational privileges were removed.

However, he has been informed that his decision to steal a six-year-old's training replica was:

> **"One of the least professionally defensible decisions made by an MSAC operator this quarter."**

# 10. THE CURRENT MSAC RUNNING JOKE

The incident has produced a new unofficial response whenever an operator makes a questionable decision.

Instead of asking:

> "What were you thinking?"

Personnel have begun asking:

> **"Did you steal Nora's toy?"**

The implication is clear.

If the answer is yes, the decision was probably questionable.

If the answer is no, the operator is encouraged to explain themselves anyway.

---

# 11. FINAL ASSESSMENT

The Scout remains a competent MSAC operator.

His professional record has not been invalidated by the incident.

However, his reputation within MSAC has sustained significant damage.

Not because he lost a fight.

Not because he was injured.

Not because the mission failed.

But because:

**he stole a six-year-old girl's training toy, got caught, ran away from her, and was chased through the Blacksite by that same six-year-old carrying a battle axe.**

The distinction between a tactical failure and an embarrassing afternoon is currently being debated.

MSAC personnel have reached a preliminary consensus.

It was both.
