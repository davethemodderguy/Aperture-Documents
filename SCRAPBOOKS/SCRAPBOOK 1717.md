# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake can parry.

More accurately:

**Jake is extremely good at parrying.**

The ability was previously considered a secondary defensive behavior associated with his celestial greatsword and shield.

This assessment is now considered inadequate.

Jake demonstrates an unusually high degree of timing, spatial awareness, and defensive precision.

During a recent controlled encounter, Jake successfully defeated an experienced Japanese swordsman through **parrying alone.**

No lethal force was used.

No deliberate killing strike was attempted.

The opponent was ultimately incapacitated by a minor concussion after being knocked down during the exchange.

The encounter has since become one of the more unusual demonstrations of Jake's combat ability recorded by C.O.R.E.

---

# 1. THE OPPONENT

The individual involved was not an ordinary swordsman.

He is a longtime friend of **Director Lucious** and the current leader of a Japanese samurai clan.

His identity remains restricted within this document.

For purposes of this report:

> **SAMURAI CLAN LEADER**

He possesses extensive traditional swordsmanship experience and agreed to the encounter under controlled conditions.

He was informed that Jake was a six-year-old child.

He was also informed that Jake was anomalous.

He was **not**, however, expecting Jake to be particularly skilled with a sword.

This assumption proved incorrect.

---

# 2. INITIAL CONDITIONS

Jake entered the training area carrying:

- his celestial greatsword;
    
- a shield;
    
- his teddy bear secured safely away from the immediate training area.
    

The greatsword was considerably larger than Jake himself.

Despite this, Jake handled it naturally.

The samurai initially appeared uncertain.

> **SAMURAI:**  
> "Are you certain you wish to use that?"

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Yeah."

> **SAMURAI:**  
> "And you understand this is only a demonstration?"

Jake nodded again.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I'm not going to hurt you."

The samurai smiled.

> **SAMURAI:**  
> "Very well."

---

# 3. FIRST EXCHANGE

The samurai initiated the encounter.

Jake did not attack.

He waited.

The first strike came toward him.

Jake raised his shield.

**CLANG.**

The strike was redirected.

Jake immediately moved the greatsword into position.

Another strike.

**CLANG.**

Parried.

Another.

**CLANG.**

Parried.

The exchange continued.

Jake never attempted to overpower the sword.

He simply redirected it.

---

# 4. OBSERVATION

C.O.R.E. analysts initially believed Jake was relying primarily on his anomalous reaction speed.

That interpretation was quickly discarded.

His movements demonstrated deliberate anticipation.

Jake was not merely reacting after each strike.

He appeared to understand where the attack was going before the weapon reached its final trajectory.

His shield handled direct defensive contact.

His greatsword redirected attacks arriving from angles where the shield would have been inefficient.

The two weapons appeared to function as a coordinated defensive system.

---

# 5. THE SAMURAI'S ADAPTATION

The samurai increased the complexity of his attacks.

Jake continued parrying.

He changed stance.

Jake adapted.

He changed timing.

Jake adapted.

He attempted feints.

Jake did not take the bait.

He attempted to force Jake backward.

Jake repositioned.

At one point, the samurai stopped.

> **SAMURAI:**  
> "...You have done this before."

Jake shook his head.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Not really."

The samurai stared at him.

> **SAMURAI:**  
> "Then how?"

Jake shrugged.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I watch."

---

# 6. JAKE'S DEFENSIVE STYLE

Jake's technique does not resemble a conventional martial art.

It is best described as:

> **Minimal movement. Maximum redirection.**

Jake rarely blocks an attack directly if he can redirect it.

He does not unnecessarily swing the greatsword.

He does not pursue openings simply because they exist.

He waits.

Deflects.

Repositions.

Waits again.

The shield provides a stable defensive anchor.

The greatsword provides additional interception and redirection.

Jake's objective appears to be extremely simple:

> **Do not get hit.**

---

# 7. THE FINAL EXCHANGE

After several minutes, the samurai launched another sequence.

Jake parried the first strike with the shield.

The second was redirected with the flat of the greatsword.

The third passed harmlessly beside him.

Jake stepped inside the opponent's balance.

He used the shield to redirect the incoming momentum.

The samurai stumbled.

Jake immediately moved the greatsword away from the opponent rather than striking.

The resulting displacement caused the samurai to lose his footing.

He fell.

His head made light contact with the padded training surface.

The medical team immediately assessed him.

Result:

**Minor concussion.**

No fracture.

No penetrating injury.

No significant blood loss.

No permanent injury.

The match was terminated immediately.

---

# 8. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake immediately dropped the defensive stance.

He looked alarmed.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Did I hurt him?"

The medical team confirmed that the injury was minor.

Jake still looked concerned.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Sorry."

The samurai, once conscious and medically cleared, laughed.

> **SAMURAI:**  
> "Do not apologize for winning."

Jake looked confused.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I didn't hit you."

The samurai smiled.

> **SAMURAI:**  
> "That may be the most embarrassing part."

---

# 9. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS

Director Lucious was present for the final portion of the demonstration.

He appeared unsurprised by the samurai's performance.

He was considerably more amused by Jake's.

Afterward:

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> "You beat him without attacking."

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I didn't want to hurt him."

Lucious looked toward his old friend.

Then back at Jake.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> "You realize you just parried a clan leader."

Jake shrugged.

> **JAKE:**  
> "He was trying to hit me."

Lucious paused.

> **LUCIOUS:**  
> "Fair."

---

# 10. COMBAT ASSESSMENT

Jake's parrying ability appears to arise from a combination of:

- extraordinary reaction speed;
    
- anomalous spatial awareness;
    
- extremely precise movement;
    
- strong visual tracking;
    
- instinctive timing;
    
- extensive observational learning;
    
- and his ability to manipulate the celestial greatsword and shield naturally.
    

C.O.R.E. has not established whether the skill is consciously learned, instinctive, or partially inherent to Jake's stellar nature.

Jake himself does not appear to understand why he is good at it.

His explanation remains:

> **"I watch."**

---

# 11. RULE OF ENGAGEMENT

Jake's demonstrated ability should **not** be interpreted as authorization to deploy him into combat.

Jake remains a six-year-old child.

His defensive capability is precisely that:

**Defensive.**

His preference during the encounter was clearly to avoid injuring his opponent.

He could have attempted offensive strikes.

He did not.

He instead maintained distance, redirected attacks, and waited for the opponent to stop.

This distinction is considered important.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING OBSERVATION

The most remarkable aspect of the encounter was not that Jake defeated an experienced swordsman.

It was **how** he did it.

He did not overpower him.

He did not unleash stellar energy.

He did not use the celestial greatsword to destroy anything.

He did not attempt to kill.

He simply stood there.

Shield raised.

Greatsword ready.

And every time the samurai attacked—

Jake answered:

**Parry.**

Again.

**Parry.**

Again.

**Parry.**

Until eventually there was nothing left to parry.

The samurai fell.

Jake immediately worried that he had hurt him.

The clan leader recovered, laughed, and admitted defeat.

Jake then picked up his teddy bear and went home.
