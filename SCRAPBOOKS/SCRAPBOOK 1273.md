# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon was placed into a controlled combat simulation designed to evaluate her tactical adaptability.

The scenario was straightforward:

> **CLEAR A WOODLAND COORP OFFICE BUILDING.**

Nora was provided with two simulated weapons:

- **M1014 Shotgun**, loaded with simulated Dragon's Breath ammunition;
    
- **Glock 27**, configured with a simulated 25-round magazine.
    

The expectation was that Nora's performance would demonstrate her previously documented weapon-handling knowledge.

The actual result exceeded the evaluation team's expectations.

Nora demonstrated:

- rapid weapon transitions;
    
- deliberate movement;
    
- environmental awareness;
    
- efficient use of cover;
    
- controlled pacing;
    
- immediate adaptation to changing threats;
    
- appropriate use of the shotgun at close range;
    
- rapid transition to the pistol when necessary;
    
- deliberate room-clearing behavior;
    
- and unusually strong situational awareness.
    

One evaluator summarized the recording with the following statement:

> **“John Wick, but a six-year-old girl.”**

The statement was not entered into the official scoring system.

It was, however, entered into the observation notes.

---

# 2. SIMULATION PARAMETERS

The simulated environment reproduced a multi-floor Woodland Coorp office building.

The building contained:

- offices;
    
- corridors;
    
- conference rooms;
    
- stairwells;
    
- storage areas;
    
- reception areas;
    
- administrative spaces;
    
- multiple entry points;
    
- simulated hostile personnel;
    
- and randomized encounters.
    

The objective was not simply to eliminate every simulated hostile.

Nora was required to:

> **Reach the designated extraction point after clearing the building.**

The simulation included unpredictable changes in hostile positioning.

Nora was not given a complete map.

---

# 3. INITIAL OBSERVATION

The simulation began.

Nora stood at the starting position.

She was wearing her usual training equipment.

She looked at the M1014.

Then at the Glock.

Then at the building.

She remained completely still for approximately three seconds.

The evaluator initially assumed she was waiting for further instructions.

She was not.

She was observing.

Nora then began moving.

---

# 4. MOVEMENT

Nora's movement was noticeably different from her normal behavior.

There was no hesitation.

There was no unnecessary rushing.

She moved through the environment deliberately, constantly checking her surroundings.

She rarely exposed herself unnecessarily.

When crossing open areas, she moved quickly.

When approaching enclosed areas, she slowed down.

When a simulated hostile appeared unexpectedly, Nora did not panic.

She immediately adjusted.

The evaluator noted:

> **“She's thinking several steps ahead.”**

---

# 5. M1014 PERFORMANCE

Nora initially favored the M1014.

The simulated Dragon's Breath ammunition produced a bright visual effect upon firing.

Nora did not become distracted by it.

She used the weapon primarily during close-range encounters.

Her movements remained controlled despite the simulated recoil and visual effects.

She demonstrated familiarity with the platform almost immediately.

No visible learning period was required.

---

# 6. WEAPON TRANSITIONS

Nora's transitions between the M1014 and Glock were particularly notable.

She did not remain committed to one weapon simply because it was already in her hands.

When the situation changed:

**M1014 → Glock.**

When close-range conditions returned:

**Glock → M1014.**

The transitions appeared to be based on environmental circumstances rather than preference.

One evaluator wrote:

> **“She isn't switching weapons because she likes one more.”**

> **“She's switching because the situation changed.”**

---

# 7. SIMULATED CONTACT

At approximately the second-floor office section, Nora encountered multiple simulated hostiles.

She immediately repositioned.

Rather than advancing directly, she used the available environment to break the encounter into smaller engagements.

The simulation's monitoring system recorded a significant reduction in Nora's exposure to hostile fire during this sequence.

She continued moving.

No unnecessary actions were observed.

---

# 8. ADAPTATION EVENT

Midway through the simulation, the scenario changed unexpectedly.

A previously cleared hallway was repopulated with simulated hostiles.

Nora stopped.

She looked toward the hallway.

Then toward an adjacent office.

She changed direction.

The evaluator initially believed she had forgotten the route.

She had not.

Nora had recognized that the previous route was no longer optimal.

She moved through the office instead.

The simulation later confirmed that the hallway had been intentionally repopulated.

### EVALUATOR COMMENT

> **“She adapted before she even knew why she needed to.”**

---

# 9. THE STAIRWELL

The stairwell produced one of the most notable moments of the evaluation.

Nora entered cautiously.

A simulated hostile appeared above her.

Nora immediately disengaged from the stairwell and repositioned.

Rather than continuing upward, she waited for the simulated hostile to move.

Once the threat changed position, Nora continued.

The evaluator paused the recording.

> **“Why did she stop?”**

Another evaluator answered:

> **“Because she didn't like the angle.”**

The recording resumed.

Nora continued.

---

# 10. PISTOL PHASE

The Glock became Nora's primary weapon during the upper floors.

Her handling remained calm.

The 25-round simulated magazine allowed her to maintain pressure without repeatedly interrupting movement.

When the simulation presented a close-range transition back to the shotgun, she switched again.

The evaluator's commentary became increasingly quiet.

At one point:

> **EVALUATOR:**  
> “She's six.”

Another evaluator:

> **“I know.”**

Pause.

> **EVALUATOR:**  
> “Why does she move like that?”

No answer was recorded.

---

# 11. FINAL FLOOR

The final floor contained the highest concentration of simulated Woodland Coorp personnel.

Nora entered.

The simulation became chaotic.

Multiple targets appeared from different directions.

Nora did not freeze.

She moved.

She changed direction.

She transitioned between weapons.

She used available cover.

She continued advancing.

The simulation's AI began increasing difficulty.

Nora adapted.

The AI increased difficulty again.

Nora adapted again.

---

# 12. EXTRACTION

Nora reached the designated extraction point.

The simulation ended.

# 14. C.O.R.E. ANALYSIS

The simulation provides additional evidence that Nora's specialty extends beyond physical adaptation.

Her primary ability appears to influence how rapidly she adjusts to unfamiliar situations.

She does not simply memorize procedures.

She recognizes patterns.

When circumstances change, she changes with them.

When a route becomes unsafe, she abandons it.

When a weapon becomes less suitable, she transitions.

When the environment changes, she reassesses.

This is consistent with the defining characteristic of Nora Tachyon:

> **Adaptation.**

---

# 15. IMPORTANT LIMITATION

The results should not be interpreted as evidence that Nora should participate in real combat.

The simulation was controlled.

The weapons were simulated.

The environment was artificial.

The consequences of failure were nonexistent.

Nora remains six years old.

Her tactical aptitude is remarkable.

Her judgment outside controlled environments still requires appropriate adult supervision.
