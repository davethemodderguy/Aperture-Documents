### **SAFEHOUSE HEALTH OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — DECLINING CONDITION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Form:** Miniature Feline  
**Status:** **Progressively Weakening**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's condition has continued to deteriorate.

What initially appeared to be a temporary illness has persisted.

She becomes weaker with each passing day.

Her miniature feline form remains active, but she spends increasingly more time resting.

She rarely flies.

She rarely plays.

She rarely moves unless necessary.

The energetic little winged kitten that normally wanders around SAFEHOUSE has become increasingly quiet.

---

# **DAILY DECLINE**

The changes are subtle but consistent.

Each day, Corin appears to have less energy.

She walks shorter distances before resting.

Her wings remain folded most of the time.

Her tails move less frequently.

She spends long periods curled up beneath her wings.

Even familiar activities fail to interest her.

---

# **DRINKING HABIT**

Despite her weakness, Corin continues to drink.

However, she refuses to drink from a conventional pet bowl.

She drinks from a **cup**.

This has been consistent with her normal preferences.

A cup is placed near her whenever she needs water.

Corin carefully approaches it and drinks from the edge.

---

# **THE CUP**

SAFEHOUSE residents have stopped trying to replace the cup with a bowl.

Several attempts were made.

Corin simply ignored the bowl.

When the cup was returned, she immediately drank.

Neko eventually summarized the situation:

> **"Apparently she's a tiny cat with standards."**

Nobody disagreed.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has increasingly remained close to Corin.

She keeps the cup within easy reach.

When Corin is too weak to walk to it, Orphie brings it directly to her.

Corin usually drinks quietly before settling back down.

She appears to appreciate the assistance.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle continues checking on her frequently.

She has become particularly careful around Corin's wings.

If Corin is curled up, Belle does not disturb her.

Instead, she sometimes sits nearby and quietly talks to her.

Corin occasionally responds with a faint meow.

---

# **MEDICAL CONCERN**

C.O.R.E. medical personnel remain concerned by the continued decline.

Corin's anomalous physiology is making the situation difficult to understand.

Her Ether remains present.

Her body continues functioning.

Yet her strength continues decreasing.

The cause remains undetermined.

Further examination is ongoing.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin still drinks.

She still responds.

She still recognizes everyone.

But each day, she seems a little weaker.

She remains curled up beneath her wings, taking small drinks from the same cup that has always been placed beside her.

Nobody knows why she is getting worse.

For now, the SAFEHOUSE residents simply stay close.

The cup remains beside her.

And the tiny white winged cat keeps fighting to remain herself.
