**SUBJECT:** Hell Fire — Standard 12-Gauge Ammunition Compatibility

---

# 1. NOTICE

Following the distribution of the first two Hell Fire units, C.O.R.E. has received an unusually large number of questions regarding ammunition.

The answer is simple:

> **Hell Fire can fire regular 12-gauge ammunition.**

The platform is not restricted exclusively to the specialized:

> **12 Gauge Magnesium Magnum “Hellfire”**

ammunition.

Standard 12-gauge loads may be used.

This includes ordinary:

> **12-gauge buckshot.**

The Hell Fire remains suppressed when using standard ammunition.

---

# 2. WHY THIS MATTERS

The Hell Fire's signature ammunition produces a rather noticeable effect.

Specifically:

> **A giant bundle of white flame.**

This is not particularly subtle.

At a firing range, this creates several predictable problems.

The range becomes extremely bright.

Nearby personnel notice.

Security personnel notice.

Maintenance personnel notice.

People in other rooms occasionally notice.

And Jane Doe has reportedly described the phenomenon as:

> **“A little excessive.”**

---

# 3. STANDARD RANGE CONFIGURATION

For ordinary training and range use, C.O.R.E. recommends standard 12-gauge buckshot.

The Hell Fire will still function as a suppressed shotgun platform.

The result is considerably less theatrical.

Instead of:

> **WHITE FIRE**

the range receives:

> **SHOTGUN.**

This is generally preferable.

---

# 4. OBSERVED TEST

An early range test was conducted using conventional 12-gauge ammunition.

The Hell Fire was fired.

The expected specialized Hellfire effect did not occur.

No large white flame plume appeared.

No five-hour burning projectile effect occurred.

No experimental ammunition effects were observed.

The range officer simply looked at the target.

Then looked at the Hell Fire.

Then looked at Neri.

> **RANGE OFFICER:**  
> “...It's just a suppressed shotgun.”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

The range officer paused.

> **RANGE OFFICER:**  
> “You could have said that.”

Neri shrugged.

---

# 5. OPERATOR GUIDANCE

Hell Fire owners should understand that the weapon effectively has two personalities.

### STANDARD 12-GAUGE

Quiet.

Practical.

Suitable for ordinary range activity.

Minimal spectacle.

### HELLFIRE AMMUNITION

Extremely bright.

Extremely hot.

Extremely noticeable.

Considerably more likely to result in someone asking:

> **“WHO AUTHORIZED THAT?”**

Both configurations remain part of the Hell Fire's intended fictional platform capability.

# 9. FINAL GUIDANCE

Hell Fire owners are reminded:

> **The shotgun does not require Hellfire ammunition to function.**

For routine range activity:

> **Standard 12-gauge buckshot is acceptable.**

For specialized authorized trials:

> **Hellfire ammunition may be used.**

Personnel are advised to select ammunition according to the intended activity.

There is no requirement to produce a five-hour white flame every time the Hell Fire is taken to the range.
