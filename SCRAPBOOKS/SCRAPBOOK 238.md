# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **INTERNAL INCIDENT LOG**

### **SUBJECT: UNAUTHORIZED CUSTOMER SUPPORT TEST**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

On ██/██/20██, Neko conducted an unauthorized "customer support resilience test."

According to Neko, it was:

> "...For science."

According to everyone else:

> "...It was a prank call."

The incident lasted approximately six minutes.

It has since become mandatory reading for new Customer Support employees.

Should the department ever have more than one.

---

# **THE CALL**

**09:43**

Dialyn answered the phone as usual.

> "Good morning! Krampus Compliance Authority, this is Dialyn speaking. How may I help you today?"

The caller identified himself only as:

> "...A concerned citizen."

Dialyn began taking notes.

---

> "I'd like to report a large white tiger."

Dialyn nodded while writing.

> "Certainly. Is it dangerous?"

> "...Not unless you try riding it."

> "...Pardon?"

---

> "There's also a white owl."

> "Is it injured?"

> "...No."

> "...It keeps stealing potatoes."

Dialyn stopped writing.

---

> "...Stealing... potatoes?"

> "Among other grain products."

---

> "...Has anyone been harmed?"

> "...Emotionally."

---

Dialyn politely continued documenting the report.

---

# **ESCALATION**

The caller continued.

> "There's also an accountant living in a treehouse."

Dialyn blinked.

> "...Excuse me?"

> "She refuses restaurant food."

> "Only cafeteria food."

> "She melts snow for drinking water."

Dialyn quietly wondered if someone was testing the emergency reporting system.

---

The caller continued without hesitation.

> "There's another accountant."

> "Same name."

> "Different person."

---

Dialyn slowly placed her pen down.

> "...I'm sorry..."

> "...There are... two?"

> "Yes."

> "They're a paradox."

---

Dialyn remained silent for several seconds.

---

# **BELLE ARRIVES**

At that exact moment, Belle entered Neko's workshop at Heaven Blacksite carrying fresh cookies.

She immediately recognized the voice coming through speakerphone.

She slowly looked toward Neko.

Then toward the phone.

Then back at Neko.

> "...Neko."

Neko froze.

---

Belle quietly asked:

> "...Are you prank calling our customer support agent?"

Neko answered honestly.

> "...Maybe."

Belle closed her eyes.

A long silence followed.

---

# **THE REVEAL**

Belle gently took the phone.

> "Hi, Dialyn..."

Dialyn sounded visibly relieved.

> "...Belle?"

> "Yes."

> "...Is... everything that man just told me true?"

Belle sighed.

A very long sigh.

> "...Unfortunately..."

Another pause.

> "...Yes."

Silence.

Dialyn quietly looked down at the report she had written.

It included the following entries.

- Giant white tiger.
    
- Potato-stealing owl.
    
- Treehouse accountant.
    
- Two accountants.
    
- Paradox.
    
- Snow.
    

She slowly asked:

> "...You work... where?"

Belle pinched the bridge of her nose.

> "...I'll explain everything when you visit."

---

# **AFTERMATH**

Dialyn accepted the explanation far more calmly than expected.

Her only response was:

> "...That explains... surprisingly many emails."

She archived the report under:

> **"Actually Genuine."**

---
