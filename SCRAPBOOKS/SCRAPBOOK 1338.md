# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following the recent series of incidents involving Woodland Coorp personnel and SAFEHOUSE resident Jake, Aperture Industries and Woodland Coorp have formally established a **mutual non-hostility treaty.**

The agreement has been accepted by both organizations.

All deliberate hostile activity has ceased.

Woodland personnel are no longer to conduct unauthorized extractions, infiltrations, kidnappings, sabotage, or other hostile operations against Aperture personnel or residents.

Aperture personnel are subject to the same restriction.

The relationship is therefore no longer classified as:

> **RIVAL ORGANIZATION — ACTIVE HOSTILITY**

It is now classified as:

> **EXTERNAL ALLY — LIMITED COOPERATION**

As part of the agreement, Woodland researchers are permitted to visit the Blacksite approximately several times per five-week operational cycle.

The stated purpose is simple:

> **Get to know what exactly Aperture is keeping around here.**

The first several orientation sessions were expected to be straightforward.

They were not.

---

# 1. WOODLAND ORIENTATION

Woodland researchers have been provided with a basic briefing.

The briefing begins with ordinary Blacksite information.

It then becomes progressively less ordinary.

Personnel are advised that the following entities are present within Aperture's sphere of operations:

- Architects of Reality;
    
- Banlands authorities;
    
- Starlands Guardian Angels;
    
- Tachyon family members;
    
- anomalous Thiren residents;
    
- artificial Thiren residents;
    
- supernatural entities;
    
- celestial entities;
    
- and other classified residents.
    

The researchers were specifically instructed:

> **Do not assume the strangest thing you encounter is the strangest thing present.**

This warning has proven necessary.

---

# 2. THE ARCHITECTS

Woodland researchers have been informed that Aperture maintains an established relationship with the three known Architects responsible for the creation and maintenance of reality.

### GUIDING LIGHT

Architect of Reality.

Oldest of the three.

Known for:

- creation;
    
- guidance;
    
- intervention;
    
- reality stabilization;
    
- and an apparent fondness for tea and baking.
    

Woodland personnel have been informed that Guiding Light is not a conventional resident.

She may simply appear.

She may also sign someone's notebook in glowing blue ink and leave.

---

### CURIOUS LIGHT

Architect of Reality.

Known for curiosity, observation, experimentation, and asking questions that occasionally make C.O.R.E. researchers regret answering.

Woodland personnel have been advised:

> **Do not lie to Curious Light.**

Not because punishment is expected.

Because Curious Light will probably ask approximately forty-seven additional questions.

---

### MISCHEVIOUS LIGHT

Architect of Reality.

Known for:

- mischief;
    
- enforcement;
    
- reality manipulation;
    
- unpredictable interventions;
    
- and being considerably more dangerous than her behavior initially suggests.
    

Woodland's orientation document contains the following handwritten annotation:

> **"Do not challenge the funny one."**

The author of this annotation has not been identified.

---

# 3. BANLANDS AUTHORITY

Woodland personnel are also informed that Aperture maintains a standing relationship with **Lucy Merryweather, Warden of the Banlands.**

This relationship is contractual.

Lucy owes Aperture Industries a **permanent favor.**

The circumstances surrounding the favor are classified.

Woodland researchers are not authorized to request assistance from Lucy.

They are, however, permitted to acknowledge her authority when encountered.

The orientation handbook contains a simple instruction:

> **If Lucy is present, be respectful.**

A second line was added later:

> **She is the Warden of an entire afterlife.**

A third line was added beneath it:

> **Seriously.**

---

# 4. STARLANDS GUARDIAN ANGEL LINEAGE

Woodland researchers were then introduced to another particularly unusual fact.

Two residents belong to the **653rd generation of the Starlands Guardian Angel lineage.**

The twins are recognized as legitimate descendants of that lineage.

Their existence represents an extremely old continuity of Guardian Angel ancestry.

Researchers were advised that their apparent age and physical appearance should not be used as indicators of their historical significance.

One Woodland researcher reportedly asked:

> "How old are they?"

The C.O.R.E. representative answered:

> "Old enough that you should probably stop trying to calculate it."

The researcher stopped.

---

# 5. THE TACHYON FAMILY

Woodland researchers were then reminded of a particularly uncomfortable portion of their own corporate history.

Three remaining members of the Tachyon family are currently known to be associated with Aperture.

This was met with approximately twelve seconds of silence.

One Woodland researcher eventually asked:

> "The Tachyon family?"

C.O.R.E. representative:

> "Yes."

Researcher:

> "The same Tachyon family?"

C.O.R.E.:

> "Yes."

Researcher:

> "...The family our company nearly wiped out?"

C.O.R.E.:

> "Correct."

No further questions were asked.

---

# 6. JAKE

The briefing concludes with:

> **JAKE**

**Classification:** Resident  
**Age:** Six  
**Nature:** Humanoid manifestation of a stellar entity  
**Known manifestation:** Blue dwarf star  
**Current form:** Human child  
**Behavioral classification:** Child

The Woodland researchers were visibly confused.

One eventually asked:

> "A star?"

C.O.R.E. replied:

> "Yes."

> "An actual star?"

> "Yes."

> "Like... an astronomical star?"

> "Yes."

> "And he's six?"

> "Approximately."

Silence.

> "Does he know?"

C.O.R.E.:

> "Yes."

> "Does he understand what that means?"

C.O.R.E.:

> "Probably less than you do."

The researcher looked at the file.

Then at the ceiling.

Then back at the file.

---

# 7. THE FIRST SITE VISIT

Woodland's first post-treaty research team arrived expecting a heavily fortified hostile environment.

Instead, they encountered:

Neri drinking coffee.

Nora eating cake.

Frost discussing ice with Nora.

Alice driving her scooter through a hallway.

Bao preparing food.

Aeterna standing motionless in a corner.

And Jake sitting on a mattress with his teddy bear.

The Woodland researchers stopped walking.

One whispered:

> "This is the Blacksite?"

Aperture escort:

> "Yes."

The researcher looked around.

> "You live like this?"

Escort:

> "Unfortunately."

---

# 8. THE REALIZATION

The researchers gradually came to understand the actual problem.

Aperture is not simply storing dangerous anomalies.

It is not merely containing supernatural entities.

It is not merely studying unusual residents.

It is, effectively, maintaining a functioning community composed of entities whose backgrounds range from:

> **"unusual Thiren child"**

to:

> **"literal stellar manifestation."**

And somehow, everyone is expected to coexist.

---

# 9. WOODLAND RESEARCHER OBSERVATION

One visiting researcher submitted the following report after their third visit.

> "We were originally told Aperture was a dangerous organization with dangerous residents."

> "This assessment was incomplete."

> "Aperture is a dangerous organization with dangerous residents."

> "However, it is also a place where a six-year-old star can sit on an old mattress holding a teddy bear while an ancient celestial being stands nearby, a Guardian Angel argues with a child over cake, and an Architect of Reality casually asks whether anyone wants a muffin."

> "I don't know how to categorize this."

C.O.R.E. response:

> **Neither do we.**

---

# 10. TREATY STATUS

The treaty remains active.

No hostile incidents have been reported since implementation.

Woodland researchers have continued making scheduled visits.

Aperture has continued allowing controlled access.

Both organizations have benefited from improved understanding of one another's operational capabilities.

The relationship remains unusual.

But it is stable.

That distinction is considered important.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Woodland Coorp came here expecting to learn what Aperture was hiding.

Instead, they discovered that Aperture is barely hiding anything anymore.

The issue is not secrecy.

The issue is scale.

Woodland's researchers arrived expecting:

> soldiers.

They found:

> families.

They expected:

> experiments.

They found:

> residents.

They expected:

> weapons.

They found:

> children who happen to possess extremely unreasonable abilities.

They expected:

> an organization.

They found:

> an ecosystem.

And somewhere inside that ecosystem is a six-year-old boy who is, quite literally, a blue dwarf star.
