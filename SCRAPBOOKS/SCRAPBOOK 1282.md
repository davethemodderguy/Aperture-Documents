# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A previously undocumented anomaly has been observed within the Blacksite during nighttime hours.

The entity appears to be a **young boy**, approximately Nora Tachyon's age.

He is consistently described as:

- humanoid;
    
- child-sized;
    
- semi-transparent;
    
- faintly glowing blue;
    
- apparently barefoot;
    
- quiet;
    
- non-aggressive;
    
- and capable of moving through portions of the Blacksite without triggering conventional security systems.
    

The entity has only been observed **at night**.

The majority of confirmed sightings have come from **Nora Tachyon**.

This is not considered surprising.

Nora's adaptive physiology has completely removed her biological requirement for sleep.

As a result, she is one of the few residents routinely awake throughout the entire night.

While most residents sleep:

> **Nora is still awake.**

And apparently:

> **So is he.**

---

# 1. INITIAL SIGHTING

The first documented sighting occurred at approximately **02:17**.

Nora was returning from the cafeteria.

She noticed movement at the opposite end of a corridor.

Initially, she assumed it was another resident.

Then the figure walked underneath a light.

Nora stopped.

The figure was a boy.

Approximately her height.

Blue.

Not wearing a Blacksite uniform.

Not carrying a keycard.

And apparently not concerned that he had just been observed.

Nora reportedly watched him for approximately thirty seconds.

He then walked around the corner.

When Nora followed:

**Nothing.**

---

# 2. SECOND SIGHTING

The following night, Nora encountered the boy again.

This time he was standing outside the cafeteria.

He was staring through the glass.

More specifically:

> **He was staring at the food.**

Nora stood beside him.

The boy did not react.

Nora looked through the glass.

Then at him.

Then back through the glass.

> **NORA:**  
> “Hungry?”

The boy remained silent.

After several seconds, he slowly nodded.

Nora opened the cafeteria door.

The boy disappeared.

---

# 3. FOOD

Subsequent sightings suggest the entity may be searching for food.

He has been observed:

- looking through cafeteria windows;
    
- standing near vending machines;
    
- staring into the kitchen;
    
- examining abandoned food containers;
    
- wandering through dining areas;
    
- and occasionally checking whether doors leading toward food storage areas are open.
    

He has never been observed actually eating.

He appears interested in food.

He simply does not appear capable of obtaining it.

---

# 4. TACHYON MERCH STORE

The most frequently documented location is the Tachyon Merch Store.

The store is closed at night.

Its security glass provides a clear view of the merchandise displays.

The boy has repeatedly been observed standing outside the glass.

He does not appear to be attempting to enter.

He simply watches.

For long periods.

His attention appears to move between the displayed Tachyon merchandise.

Particularly:

> **The Bullseye.**

Nora has repeatedly found him standing in front of the display containing the limited merchandise.

He does not touch the glass.

He does not knock.

He simply looks.

---

# 5. NORA'S OBSERVATION

Nora appears to have become accustomed to his presence.

During one recorded encounter, Nora was sitting nearby with a cup of coffee.

The boy stood outside the Tachyon Merch Store.

Nora looked at him.

> **NORA:**  
> “You like that one?”

The boy looked at the Bullseye display.

He nodded.

Nora looked at the rifle.

Then at him.

> **NORA:**  
> “It's mine.”

The boy looked at her.

Nora paused.

Then:

> **NORA:**  
> “...You can look.”

The boy remained there.

---

# 6. NIGHTTIME PATTERN

A review of security footage has identified a consistent pattern.

The entity appears exclusively during nighttime hours.

No confirmed daytime sightings exist.

During the day:

**Nothing.**

At night:

**He wanders.**

His route does not appear fixed.

He may appear in:

- corridors;
    
- cafeterias;
    
- unused rooms;
    
- stairwells;
    
- storage areas;
    
- vending areas;
    
- the Tachyon Merch Store;
    
- or other quiet sections of the Blacksite.
    

He never appears to be in a hurry.

He simply walks.

---

# 7. SECURITY FOOTAGE

Conventional cameras do not consistently record the entity.

Several cameras show empty corridors where Nora insists the boy was standing.

Others capture a faint blue distortion.

A small number record him clearly.

The inconsistency remains unexplained.

Interestingly, cameras located near the Tachyon Merch Store have the highest rate of successful recordings.

Neko has begun reviewing the footage personally.

He does not yet have an explanation.

---

# 8. BEHAVIORAL CHARACTERISTICS

The entity has demonstrated no overt aggression.

He does not attack personnel.

He does not attempt to access restricted areas.

He does not damage property.

He does not appear to intentionally frighten anyone.

He mostly:

> **Wanders.**

And:

> **Looks for food.**

And:

> **Stares at the Tachyon Merch Store.**

There is currently no evidence that these behaviors are connected.

---

# 9. NORA'S SECOND ENCOUNTER

At approximately 03:41, Nora encountered the boy sitting on the floor outside the cafeteria.

He was looking through the glass.

Nora sat down beside him.

Neither spoke.

Several minutes passed.

Nora eventually produced a piece of cake.

She looked at it.

Then at him.

The boy stared at the cake.

Nora held it out.

The boy reached toward it.

His hand passed through the food.

He stopped.

Nora stared.

The boy stared.

Nora looked down at the cake.

Then quietly said:

> **NORA:**  
> “Oh.”

She ate it herself.

The boy remained beside her.

---

# 10. NOCTURNAL EXCLUSIVITY

C.O.R.E. initially considered whether the entity might simply be avoiding populated areas.

This theory does not adequately explain the observations.

The entity does not merely become less visible during the day.

There are **no confirmed daytime appearances.**

His existence appears tied specifically to nighttime.

The reason remains unknown.

---

# 11. NORA'S ADAPTATION

Nora's complete lack of biological sleep requirement has made her the Blacksite's most reliable nighttime observer.

Other residents generally sleep.

Nora does not need to.

She can therefore encounter phenomena that may otherwise go unnoticed.

This has led to an unusual situation.

The anomaly is not necessarily appearing **because of Nora.**

Nora may simply be the first resident capable of consistently noticing it.

---

# 12. NORA'S DESCRIPTION

When asked what she thought the boy was doing, Nora answered:

> **“Looking.”**

The welfare officer asked:

> **“Looking for what?”**

Nora shrugged.

> **“Food, probably.”**

> **“Why do you think that?”**

Nora paused.

> **“He keeps looking at the cafeteria.”**

Pause.

> **“And the store.”**

The officer asked:

> **“Why the store?”**

Nora looked toward the hallway.

> **“I don't know.”**

---

# 13. CURRENT HYPOTHESES

C.O.R.E. has not assigned a definitive classification.

Current possibilities include:

### A. RESIDENT-RELATED ANOMALY

The entity may be associated with an existing resident or previous Blacksite event.

### B. LOST ENTITY

The boy may be an anomalous individual who has become trapped within the Blacksite.

### C. NIGHTTIME MANIFESTATION

The entity may only exist during a specific temporal condition.

### D. EXTERNAL ANOMALY

The Blacksite may be unintentionally intersecting with another location or reality during nighttime hours.

### E. UNKNOWN

Current evidence is insufficient.

---

# 14. PERSONNEL INSTRUCTIONS

Personnel encountering the entity are instructed to:

- remain calm;
    
- do not pursue aggressively;
    
- do not attempt to restrain him;
    
- do not assume hostility;
    
- document the location and time;
    
- notify C.O.R.E. Security;
    
- and, if possible, allow the entity to continue moving.
    

Nora has additionally requested:

> **“Don't scare him.”**

This request has been accepted as reasonable.

# 15. TACHYON MERCH STORE

The store has become the most interesting location in the investigation.

The entity repeatedly returns to the same security glass.

He looks at the merchandise.

Especially the Bullseye.

Neri was informed.

Her response was:

> **NERI:**  
> “He likes it?”

Nora nodded.

Neri looked toward the store.

Pause.

> **NERI:**  
> “Then let him look.”

No further instructions were given.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The blue child does not currently display hostile behavior.

He does not appear to be hunting personnel.

He does not appear to be attempting to breach the Blacksite.

He wanders.

He searches for food.

He watches the Tachyon Merch Store.

And then, eventually, he disappears.

The fact that he only appears at night remains unexplained.

The fact that almost every confirmed witness is Nora is also significant.

Not because Nora is necessarily connected to the entity.

But because she is awake when everyone else is asleep.
