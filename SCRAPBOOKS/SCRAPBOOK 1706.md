# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Clover Luminare's anomalous four-leaf clovers possess a temporary beneficial effect.

A naturally grown anomalous clover remains effective for approximately:

> **5 HOURS**

After this period, the clover wilts and its anomalous effect ceases.

A significant exception has now been confirmed.

When one of Clover's anomalous four-leaf clovers is crystallized by contact with **Jake's tears**, the clover becomes permanently preserved within a solid diamond-like crystalline structure.

More importantly:

> **The luck effect becomes permanent.**

The resulting object does not follow the normal five-hour limitation.

---

# 1. STANDARD CLOVER

Clover can produce four-leaf clovers from ordinary soil without requiring seeds.

The resulting clovers possess a localized anomalous luck effect.

The effect applies to the individual possessing or carrying the clover.

It does **not**:

- spread to nearby individuals;
    
- permanently alter the recipient;
    
- drain Clover;
    
- guarantee a specific outcome;
    
- provide absolute protection.
    

Under normal circumstances:

> **ACTIVE EFFECT: ~5 HOURS**

After approximately five hours, the clover wilts.

The anomalous effect disappears with it.

---

# 2. CRYSTALLIZED CLOVER

A clover exposed to one of Jake's tears behaves differently.

The tear crystallizes into solid diamond around the clover.

The clover itself becomes permanently preserved within the resulting crystalline structure.

The five-hour limitation no longer applies.

Observed specimen:

**AGE:** Extended observation period  
**PHYSICAL CONDITION:** Unchanged  
**WILTING:** None  
**ANOMALOUS LUCK EFFECT:** Active  
**PROJECTED EXPIRATION:** None identified

---

# 3. NEKO'S ANALYSIS

Neko was asked to determine whether the diamond merely preserved the clover or whether it altered the anomalous property itself.

His conclusion was immediate.

> **NEKO:**  
> "It's not just preserving it."

The specimen was examined again.

> **NEKO:**  
> "The five-hour decay is gone."

Pause.

> **NEKO:**  
> "Completely."

The crystalline structure appears to have stabilized whatever mechanism normally causes Clover's anomalous clovers to lose their effect.

Neko's current working interpretation is:

> **Clover creates the luck. Jake's crystallization prevents the luck from expiring.**

---

# 4. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

The diamond does **not** appear to create the luck.

The clover does.

The diamond simply makes the effect permanent.

Therefore:

### Ordinary Clover

**Clover → Four-leaf clover → Luck → ~5 hours → Effect ends**

### Crystallized Clover

**Clover → Four-leaf clover → Jake's tear → Diamond crystallization → Luck → Permanent**

This distinction is considered important.

Jake's tears are not currently classified as a source of luck.

Clover's anomaly remains the source of the effect.

---

# 5. NO OBSERVED DEGRADATION

Neko's analysis found no evidence that the crystallized specimen is approaching the normal five-hour expiration point.

The crystalline clover does not:

- wilt;
    
- dry out;
    
- lose coloration;
    
- become biologically inactive;
    
- lose its luck effect;
    
- or exhibit progressive anomalous decay.
    

The clover has effectively been removed from its normal lifecycle.

Neko described the result as:

> **"A permanent backup of Clover's anomaly."**

He immediately clarified:

> **"Not a backup of Clover herself."**

---

# 6. LUCY MERRYWEATHER SPECIMEN

Clover gave the first known permanent crystallized clover to **Lucy Merryweather** during her next visit.

Lucy accepted the gift personally.

The specimen remains in her possession.

No KCA inventory designation has been assigned.

No Aperture ownership claim has been made.

C.O.R.E. considers this appropriate.

The object was given as a gift.

It is therefore:

> **Lucy Merryweather's clover.**

---

# 7. SECURITY CONSIDERATION

Neko has specifically requested that the phenomenon **not** be turned into an industrial process.

The theoretical implications are obvious.

A permanent luck-bearing object would be considerably more significant than an ordinary five-hour clover.

However, attempting to manufacture them on demand would require:

1. Clover producing the anomalous clover;
    
2. Jake producing a tear;
    
3. direct interaction between the two anomalies;
    
4. successful crystallization.
    

More importantly, requiring Jake to cry for the purpose of producing permanent clovers would be completely unacceptable.

Neko's recommendation is therefore straightforward:

> **"Don't."**

---

# 8. FINAL TECHNICAL ASSESSMENT

The five-hour limitation belongs to **Clover's ordinary clovers.**

It is not an intrinsic limitation of the luck anomaly itself.

Jake's diamond crystallization somehow removes that limitation.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

The resulting permanent clover represents an anomalous interaction between two residents rather than a new independent power.
