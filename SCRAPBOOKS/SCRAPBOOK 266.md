# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT REPORT**

### **INCIDENT: COURTYARD ACCIDENT – COREY LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Medical & Psychological Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

On ██/██/20██, Corey Lockwood experienced a severe panic attack following a workplace accident in the Heaven Blacksite central courtyard.

The injured individual, a maintenance worker, later made a full recovery through the Banlands' **Soul Contract**.

However, the event itself proved sufficient to trigger Corey's trauma response.

---

# **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

At approximately ██:██ hours, Corey was walking through the courtyard alone.

Simultaneously, a maintenance technician working on an upper balcony lost his footing and fell several stories.

Personnel immediately initiated emergency response procedures.

The worker succumbed to his injuries shortly afterward.

As per Banlands emergency protocol, his Soul Contract successfully restored him.

No permanent injuries remained.

Corey witnessed the fall.

---

# **IMMEDIATE RESPONSE**

Several witnesses reported hearing Corey scream.

She immediately covered her ears despite there being no loud noise.

She backed away several steps before collapsing onto the courtyard path.

Medical personnel later noted symptoms including:

- Hyperventilation.
    
- Trembling.
    
- Loss of coordination.
    
- Crying.
    
- Inability to speak coherently.
    
- Repeated attempts to look away from the scene while simultaneously checking if the worker was still moving.
    

Despite repeated reassurance that the worker would return through the Soul Contract...

Corey appeared unable to process the information.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services determined that the visual stimulus—not the outcome—triggered the episode.

Corey's known history includes witnessing the violent deaths of her parents and later suffering a fatal gunshot wound herself in her original universe.

The sudden sight of someone falling to their death closely matched several aspects of those traumatic memories.

Although she intellectually understands the function of the Soul Contract...

Her trauma reacted before reason could.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin reached Corey before medical staff.

Without asking questions, she knelt beside her and wrapped her in a blanket retrieved from a nearby emergency cabinet.

She remained beside Corey throughout the incident.

Witnesses reported hearing Corin quietly repeat:

> "...You're here."

> "...You're safe."

> "...Don't look."

Corey clung to Corin's sleeve and did not let go for nearly an hour.

---

# **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

**Maintenance Worker**

Following his successful revival, the worker insisted on speaking with Corey personally.

After learning what had happened, he quietly apologized.

> "...I'm sorry."

> "...I didn't mean to scare you."

Corey immediately shook her head through tears.

> "...It wasn't your fault."

---

**Belle Merryweather**

Belle cancelled the remainder of Corey's scheduled work for the day without discussion.

---

**Endmin Merryweather**

Endmin instructed supervisors to reroute pedestrian traffic away from the courtyard until Corey had been escorted elsewhere.

---

**Bao**

Bao prepared warm tea.

Corey was unable to drink it immediately.

She held the warm cup in both hands until her breathing slowed.

---

**Star**

Star arrived several minutes later after hearing what had happened.

Seeing Corey in distress immediately brought back memories of Corin's collapse after Corey's disappearance.

Star quietly sat nearby.

She didn't try to cheer anyone up.

She simply stayed.

---

**Nightshade**

Nightshade positioned herself between Corey and the accident scene.

She remained there until cleanup was complete.

---

**Neko**

Neko later noted in his report:

> "...The trigger was visual."

> "...Not logical."

---

**Miss Neko**

> "...Trauma doesn't wait for explanations."

---

# **FOLLOW-UP**

Corey recovered physically within several hours.

Psychological recovery required considerably longer.

Medical Services recommended avoiding exposure to severe workplace accidents whenever reasonably possible.

The maintenance worker later visited Corey again after returning to work.

This time...

He waved from a safe distance.

Corey hesitated.

Then managed a small wave back.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The incident has been added to Corey's psychological profile as a significant trigger.

Personnel are reminded that although resurrection through the Banlands' Soul Contract is a recognized emergency protocol, witnessing a traumatic event can still have profound psychological consequences.

As Corin later summarized in her own report:

> "Just because someone comes back..."

> "...doesn't mean everyone else is okay."

**End of Psychological Incident Report**
