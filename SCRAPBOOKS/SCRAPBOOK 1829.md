## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately 0200 hours, a previously undocumented anomalous individual was discovered inside an unused equipment room at Heaven's Blacksite.

The discovery was made not by Security personnel, a containment team, or an active anomalous detection system, but by Jake Akana.

While wandering through the largely deserted facility during overnight hours, Jake noticed a small magenta glow emanating from an abandoned maintenance area. Upon investigating, he encountered a ghostly humanoid figure who appeared to be approximately sixteen years of age.

The figure identified herself as **Camelia Candleheart**, or simply Camelia.

Camelia's appearance shares several notable characteristics with Jake's current ghostly form. Both possess partially melted heads resembling candle wax, with burning wicks producing colored flames. Jake's flame is blue; Camelia's is magenta. Camelia has three wicks, with portions of her head and hair melted around them. Her remaining hair is styled in twin ponytails, and her manifested form wears a Japanese-style school uniform.

Camelia stated that she had been murdered in her home and subsequently awakened in her present ghostly state at the Blacksite. She could not recall further details of her former life.

The initial encounter was peaceful.

Following their introduction, Jake learned that Camelia occupied the unused equipment room during the day and wandered through the Blacksite at night. When inside the room during daytime hours, she used anomalous abilities to seal the door shut.

Jake subsequently introduced Camelia to the SAFEHOUSE residential complex.

Camelia was later accepted as a SAFEHOUSE resident.

**Initial assessment:** Camelia has demonstrated no confirmed hostile intent. Her apparent amnesia, unknown death circumstances, and unexplained presence within the Blacksite remain subjects for cautious, non-invasive investigation.

The discovery also presents an unusual development for Jake Akana: for the first time since his return as a ghost, he has encountered another individual whose visible condition closely resembles his own.

## II. INCIDENT CHRONOLOGY

### 0200 HOURS — THE EMPTY BLACKSITE

The Blacksite was largely quiet.

Personnel were asleep or off duty where their schedules permitted. Corridors that would ordinarily see regular traffic stood empty, and the facility's maintenance areas were mostly unoccupied.

Jake was wandering.

No emergency had been declared. No unusual activity had been reported through the ordinary security channels. Jake's movements appear to have been recreational rather than connected to a specific assignment.

While crossing an abandoned maintenance corridor, he noticed a faint pinkish glow coming from one of the unused equipment rooms.

The glow was small enough that it might easily have been overlooked by someone walking past without paying attention.

Jake stopped.

He approached the room and cautiously peeked inside.

There, surrounded by the dim interior, was a figure he had never seen before.

She was a ghost.

Not merely a ghost in the conventional sense of a translucent human silhouette, but one whose physical appearance bore a striking resemblance to his own.

Her head was partially melted. Three wicks protruded from the damaged portions, each burning with a magenta flame. Parts of her hair had melted away as well, although her twin ponytails remained recognizable. She wore a Japanese-style uniform, apparently forming part of her current manifested appearance.

For a brief moment, Jake observed her from the doorway.

Then the girl noticed him.

### 0202 HOURS — FIRST CONTACT

**CAMELIA:** "Huh? Another ghost, who's there?"

Jake did not run.

He did not shout for Security.

He did not appear to interpret her appearance as a threat.

Instead, he stepped inside.

**JAKE:** "Hi, I'm Jake Akana, I live here. What's your name?"

His voice was soft.

The approach was notably different from the procedures ordinarily used for an unidentified anomaly, but the encounter did not require immediate intervention. Jake's decision to introduce himself rather than startle the stranger allowed the conversation to proceed without escalation.

**CAMELIA:** "I'm Camelia Candleheart, or Camelia for short."

**JAKE:** "Why are you here?"

Camelia answered with an explanation that would become the principal starting point of the investigation.

**CAMELIA:** "I was murdered in my house, then I woke up here as a ghost. I don't remember anything more."

The conversation established three initial facts:

- Camelia believed that she had been murdered in her home.
    
- She had awakened at the Blacksite in her present ghostly form.
    
- She could not recall additional details about her previous life.
    

No further account of her death was obtained during the initial interaction.

Jake did not press her for one.

The two ghosts continued their conversation, and the encounter remained peaceful.

## III. SUBJECT PROFILE — CAMELIA CANDLEHEART

**Name:** Camelia Candleheart  
**Preferred name:** Camelia  
**Apparent age:** Approximately 16  
**Nature:** Ghost  
**Current status:** SAFEHOUSE resident  
**Threat assessment:** Undetermined; no confirmed hostile behavior  
**Former identity:** Unknown beyond her stated name  
**Cause of death:** Reported murder; details unverified  
**Memory status:** Reports substantial loss of autobiographical memory

### Physical Appearance

Camelia manifests as a humanoid girl with a partially melted head and hair.

Three wicks burn with magenta flames from the melted portions of her head. The flames provide the most distinctive visible feature of her manifestation, creating a small colored glow in otherwise dark environments.

Her hair is arranged in twin ponytails, although portions have been melted away. Her manifested clothing resembles a Japanese-style uniform.

Her overall appearance is similar to Jake Akana's ghostly form, particularly in the combination of a partially melted head, burning wicks, and candle-like characteristics.

The resemblance is substantial enough that Jake immediately recognized a similarity between them.

However, their shared appearance should not be interpreted as proof of a shared origin, identical abilities, or a common cause of death.

### Known Behavioral Characteristics

Camelia reportedly follows a recurring pattern:

- During the day, she occupies the unused equipment room where Jake found her.
    
- When inside the room during daytime hours, she uses her ghostly abilities to seal the door shut.
    
- During the night, she wanders through the Blacksite.
    

The precise mechanism by which she seals doors is unknown. The duration, strength, and limitations of this ability have not been established.

Her previous occupation of the equipment room appears to have been a form of self-directed habitation. C.O.R.E. has not established whether she chose the room because it offered security, because she believed she had nowhere else to go, or for another reason.

Her apparent memory loss may complicate attempts to reconstruct her history.

Camelia has also indicated that she does not like discussing how or when she died. She has forgotten much of her former life and does not welcome inquiries into the circumstances surrounding her death.

This preference is to be respected.

## IV. THE UNUSUAL SIMILARITY

The discovery of Camelia has prompted C.O.R.E. to examine the similarities between her and Jake.

Both are ghosts.

Both have partially melted heads.

Both possess burning wicks that emit colored flames.

Both retain recognizable humanoid forms despite their unusual physical conditions.

And both appear to exist independently of their former living bodies.

The differences are equally notable.

Jake is six years old and has a single blue flame. Camelia appears to be approximately sixteen and has three magenta flames. Her hair retains a twin-ponytail arrangement, while Jake's cat ears are absent due to the melted portion of his head.

The two have not been established to share any additional anomalous characteristics.

In particular, C.O.R.E. cannot conclude that Camelia experiences the same sensory capabilities, physical sensations, or limitations as Jake.

Their similarities are grounds for comparative observation, not assumptions.

Nevertheless, the implications for resident welfare are significant.

Jake has spent his time at SAFEHOUSE being cared for by people whose bodies and experiences differ from his own. Although those relationships have provided companionship, nobody can be assumed to share his precise experience of being a ghost.

Camelia may offer a different kind of companionship: not because she is necessarily able to understand everything Jake has experienced, but because she inhabits a visibly similar condition.

She does not need to be told what a burning wick looks like from the inside of an ordinary conversation.

She has three of her own.

Whether this similarity will develop into a deeper personal connection remains to be seen.

C.O.R.E. will not prescribe a relationship between the two. They are free to determine their own boundaries and companionship.

## V. THE ROOM THAT WAS NEVER ON THE ROSTER

The unused equipment room is now a location of interest.

Before her discovery, Camelia had apparently been occupying the room without any formal residential assignment.

Her ability to seal the door from within allowed her to maintain a degree of separation from the rest of the facility during daytime hours. At night, she left the room and wandered the corridors.

This arrangement raises operational questions.

How long had she been present?

How had she reached the Blacksite?

Why had her existence not been identified earlier?

And how did she come to occupy a room within a facility that maintains extensive security and anomalous monitoring systems?

None of these questions has been answered.

C.O.R.E. cautions against interpreting the discovery as evidence of a security breach until the circumstances are better understood. Camelia may have arrived through an anomalous process that conventional access controls cannot detect. That possibility remains unverified.

Likewise, the fact that she could seal the room does not establish that she was attempting to evade personnel or conceal hostile activity.

Her behavior may simply have reflected a desire for privacy.

The equipment room should not automatically be regarded as a containment site. Camelia has since been accepted as a resident, and any examination of the room must account for her former use of it as a place of habitation.

The facility has a responsibility to understand how she arrived.

It also has a responsibility not to treat her peaceful presence as a crime in itself.

---

## VI. TRANSFER TO SAFEHOUSE

Following their initial encounter, Jake introduced Camelia to the SAFEHOUSE residential complex.

The transfer represented a significant change in her circumstances.

Before the encounter, Camelia occupied an unused room and wandered the facility during hours when most personnel were asleep. Her existence had not been formally recognized, and she had no established place among the residents.

After meeting Jake, she was introduced to a residential environment where her presence could be acknowledged and her welfare considered.

Camelia was subsequently accepted as a SAFEHOUSE resident.

C.O.R.E. regards this as an appropriate provisional arrangement, given the absence of confirmed hostile behavior and her stated need for a place to remain.

Her integration should proceed at her own pace.

She is not required to recount her murder to justify receiving accommodation. She is not required to recover her memories before being treated as a resident. Nor should her similarities to Jake lead others to assume that she will automatically feel comfortable around him or the wider SAFEHOUSE community.

Introductions are not obligations to form friendships.

However, Jake's role in the discovery deserves particular recognition.

He encountered an unfamiliar ghost in an empty corridor at two in the morning and chose to introduce himself.

He asked her name.

He asked why she was there.

And, rather than leaving her alone in an abandoned room, he eventually offered her a connection to the place he calls home.

The outcome was not the result of a planned research operation.

It began with a child noticing a light and deciding to look closer.

## VII. INVESTIGATION AND RECOMMENDATIONS

C.O.R.E. will maintain an open investigation into Camelia's origin and the circumstances of her arrival.

The following priorities have been established:

**1. Identity and history**

Record only the information Camelia is willing and able to provide. Do not repeatedly question her about her murder, former life, or date of death.

**2. Anomalous capabilities**

Document observable characteristics of her manifestation, including her three magenta flames and reported ability to seal doors. Testing must be non-invasive and justified by a clear operational need.

**3. Blacksite arrival**

Review available facility records for evidence that could clarify how Camelia came to occupy the unused equipment room. Do not presume intentional infiltration or hostile intent.

**4. Resident integration**

Provide Camelia with a stable place to stay, access to ordinary resident activities, and the opportunity to establish her own relationships.

**5. Comparative observations**

If Jake and Camelia voluntarily discuss their shared characteristics, those conversations may inform future research. Neither resident is to be treated as a compulsory research partner for the other.

**6. Privacy**

Camelia's reluctance to discuss her death is to be respected by both research personnel and residents. Curiosity is not authorization to demand painful personal information.

## VIII. CLOSING NOTE — RESIDENT WELFARE

At two in the morning, Heaven's Blacksite is a very different place.

The corridors are empty. The offices are dark. Most of the people who fill the facility during the day are asleep.

For Jake, it was apparently a suitable time to wander.

He found a light in a room nobody was using.

Inside was a girl who had been murdered in her own home, awakened as a ghost somewhere she did not recognize, and lost the memories that might have told her who she used to be.

She had been living alone in an unused equipment room.

During the day, she sealed the door.

At night, she walked the corridors.

We do not know how long she had been doing this. We do not know whether she was frightened, lonely, confused, or simply accustomed to the arrangement.

We know only that when Jake found her, she spoke to him.

And he spoke back.

There is something quietly remarkable about the first words they exchanged.

_"Another ghost."_

Not another specimen.

Not another threat.

Another ghost.

Jake did not need to explain why his head was melted or why a blue flame burned above it. Camelia had her own magenta flames. Their appearances were different, but similar enough that the distinction did not need to be explained before they could begin talking.

For once, the unusual thing about Jake was not that he was a ghost.

It was that he had found someone else who was one, too.

Camelia is now a SAFEHOUSE resident. Her history remains incomplete, her death unexplained, and her memories largely absent.

She does not owe anyone a reconstruction of the life she has forgotten.

For now, she has a place to stay.

She has been introduced to people who can get to know her without demanding that she first explain everything that happened to her.

And she has met the little blue-flamed ghost who noticed her light in the darkness.

Perhaps they will become friends.

Perhaps they will simply be two ghosts who occasionally share the same room, the same corridor, or the same quiet hours when everyone else is asleep.

Either way, Camelia is no longer just an unexplained presence in an unused equipment room.

She is someone who has been found.

And this time, being found has led her home.

**C.O.R.E. — RESIDENT WELFARE FINAL ENTRY**

_The Blacksite has spent years learning how to find what does not belong._

_Perhaps we should also learn what to do when what we find is simply someone who has nowhere else to go._
