# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — FAREWELL LETTERS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — MEMORIAL RECORD

**Archive Note:** The intended recipient is no longer present to receive these letters.

# **LETTER 01 — BLACKSITE SECURITY**

**From:** Security Officer ██-███

Dear Alice,

You probably don't remember me.

That's okay.

I remember you.

You used to ride that little scooter through the corridors, and every time you passed my checkpoint, you would wave at me.

I always waved back.

Sometimes you stopped.

Sometimes you just kept going.

I used to think it was funny how you somehow managed to make a security checkpoint feel less like a security checkpoint.

It's been quiet since you disappeared.

I still look toward the corridor whenever I hear something that sounds like your scooter.

I know it's not you.

I still look.

Goodbye, little bunny.

You were always welcome here.

— Officer ██-███

---

# **LETTER 02 — ENGINEERING DEPARTMENT**

**From:** Engineering Technician ██-███

Alice,

I never properly introduced myself.

I'm one of the people who worked with Neko.

You probably saw me around the workshop sometimes.

You liked watching us build things.

You didn't understand half of what we were doing.

You still watched anyway.

I remember you pointing at something and asking what it did.

I gave you an explanation that was probably way too complicated.

You nodded like you understood everything.

I think you were just being polite.

I'm sorry we couldn't build something that could keep you here.

We build machines that can cross dimensions.

Machines that can withstand things that would kill ordinary people.

And somehow we couldn't build something for you.

I'm sorry.

Goodbye, Alice.

Your chair is still there.

Nobody is using it.

# **LETTER 03 — CAFETERIA STAFF**

Dear Alice,

We miss you.

That's really all we wanted to say.

You didn't eat much.

You were always such a tiny thing.

But whenever you came in, we'd ask:

> "Soup?"

And you'd nod.

Sometimes you'd sit there quietly.

Sometimes someone would talk to you.

Sometimes you'd draw.

We liked those days.

The cafeteria feels emptier now.

Even though you barely ate anything.

Thank you for visiting us.

We hope wherever you are, there is something warm waiting for you.

— Cafeteria Staff

# **LETTER 04 — MEDICAL DEPARTMENT**

Dear Alice,

We are sorry.

We spent so much time studying what was wrong with you that sometimes we forgot the most important thing.

You were a little girl.

Not a collection of symptoms.

Not a medical anomaly.

Not a regeneration case.

Not an Ether concentration.

Alice.

You.

We wish we had been better at remembering that.

You deserved better.

You deserved to grow up.

You deserved to complain about boring checkups.

You deserved to hate vegetables.

You deserved scraped knees that weren't life-threatening.

You deserved a normal birthday.

You deserved years.

We're sorry you didn't get them.

Goodbye, Alice.

---

# **LETTER 05 — NEKO**

Alice.

I don't like writing letters.

You know that.

Actually, you probably don't anymore.

I made your scooter.

You barely used it at first.

Then you started using it everywhere.

I remember being annoyed because you kept taking it into places where I specifically told you not to.

You always came back.

I tracked it anyway.

I always knew where you were.

I wish I'd been able to track you when you disappeared.

I wish I'd been able to track something that wasn't there anymore.

Your scooter is still here.

I haven't touched it.

I think I'll keep it charged.

Just in case.

— Neko

---

# **LETTER 06 — MISS NEKO**

Alice,

You always let me fix your hair.

You never complained.

You'd just sit there quietly while I brushed it.

Sometimes your ears would twitch.

I liked that.

I wish I had done it more.

Your hairbrush is still here.

I don't know why I haven't put it away.

Maybe because putting it away would make this feel more real.

I hope you knew you were loved.

Even when you forgot.

Especially when you forgot.

— Miss Neko

# **LETTER 07 — LUMI**

My little Alice,

I wish you could have remembered me until the end.

But I understand.

Your mind was tired.

You were tired.

You tried so hard to remember everyone.

You wrote us all down because you were afraid we would disappear from your memory.

You shouldn't have had to carry that fear.

You were loved even when you forgot someone's name.

You were loved when you forgot where you were.

You were loved when you forgot what you had done.

You were loved when you forgot yourself.

And you were loved when you disappeared.

I hope somewhere, somehow, you can hear this.

You were never unwanted.

Never.

— Lumi

# **LETTER 08 — STAR**

Alice!!

I don't know how to write a goodbye letter.

I don't want to write one.

I want you to come back.

I want you to ride your scooter into my room again.

I want you to ask me another grammar question.

Even though you never understood my explanations.

:P

You were supposed to get better at grammar.

You were supposed to learn more words.

You were supposed to fill your journal with more pages.

You were supposed to grow up.

You were supposed to be here.

You wrote:

I won't.

I promise.

I'll remember you.

Even if everyone else forgets.

Even if the universe forgets.

I'll remember.

Goodbye, little bunny.

I love you.

— Star

Alice.

# **LETTER 16 — ORPHIE**

Alice.

You called me Bigger Sis.

I never corrected you.

I liked it.

You were always worried about me being tired.

You worried about everyone being tired.

You worried about everyone except yourself.

I wish I could have convinced you that you mattered just as much.

Maybe more.

You wrote everyone's names down because you were afraid of forgetting them.

I wish I had written your name down for you.

Not because I could have prevented this.

But because I wanted you to know how important you were.

You were Alice Lockwood.

You were our little sister.

You were family.

You were loved.

You still are.

— Orphie

# **LETTER 17 — CORIN**

Alice.

I don't know how to say goodbye to you.

I don't think I can.

I found you in the snow.

You didn't know your name.

You didn't know where you came from.

You didn't remember your mother.

You didn't remember anything.

So I gave you a name.

Alice.

And eventually you learned to say it was yours.

I became your Big Sis.

You became my little sister.

I promised I would take care of you.

I promised I wouldn't leave you alone.

I promised you had a place to come home to.

I failed you.

You disappeared while I was still holding your hand.

And I couldn't stop it.

I keep thinking about your last sentence.

You wrote:

> **"I struggle to remember, no one loves me"**

Alice...

You were wrong.

You were so, so wrong.

Everyone loved you.

I loved you.

I still love you.

I'm sorry you couldn't remember.

I'm sorry your mind convinced you otherwise.

I'm sorry I couldn't make you stay.

Your hat is still beside my bed.

Your notebook is with me.

I read it sometimes.

I know you're not coming back.

But I still talk to you.

Maybe that's stupid.

I don't care.

You were my little sister.

You always will be.

Goodbye, Alice.

I love you.

— Corin

# **ARCHIVE CLOSING NOTE**

There is no recipient.

The letters cannot be delivered.

There is no address.

There is no room to place them in.

There is only an empty Treehouse.

A scooter that nobody rides.

A hat that nobody wears.

And a notebook filled with reminders from a little girl who was terrified of forgetting everyone.

She succeeded in making sure everyone remembered.

She simply didn't get to stay long enough to see it.

**Alice Lockwood is remembered.**

**Alice Lockwood was loved.**

**Alice Lockwood belonged here.**

**End of Memorial Archive.**
