# 1. EXHIBITION STATUS

The final manufactured Night Stalker has been removed from conventional armament storage.

It is now on **open public display within the Blacksite Museum.**

The displayed unit is:

> **NS-010 — NIGHT STALKER "REGALIA"**

This remains the final Night Stalker ever produced.

No NS-011 will be manufactured.

No replacement unit will be produced.

No additional competition will be held.

---

# 2. DISPLAY CASE

Regalia is displayed inside a custom-built **gold presentation case.**

The case is substantially more elaborate than those issued with the previous nine Night Stalkers.

The transparent portions of the case consist of **Nekonium-plated glass**, providing additional anomalous protection while preserving complete visibility of the contents.

The display is intentionally designed so that visitors can clearly see:

- NS-010 Regalia;
    
- its gold-dominant finish;
    
- the **TACHYON INDUSTRIES** engraving;
    
- Neri's personal signature;
    
- three loaded magazines;
    
- the accompanying handwritten letter;
    
- the presentation case itself.
    

The case is mounted above a dedicated museum podium.

---

# 3. PODIUM INSCRIPTION

The podium contains a single handwritten-style inscription.

It reads:

> **“The Final Sleight of Hand, up for grabs.**  
> **There will be no more competitions, you must earn it.**  
> **I'm always watching”**
> 
> **—Neri**

The quotation has been reproduced exactly.

No museum curator has been authorized to alter the wording.

---

# 4. INTERPRETATION

The wording has generated considerable discussion among MSAC personnel.

The first sentence establishes:

> **The Final Sleight of Hand**

as Neri's unofficial title for NS-010.

The second establishes that the previous Night Stalker competition structure has permanently ended.

There will be:

- no lottery;
    
- no cooking competition;
    
- no endurance competition;
    
- no chess tournament;
    
- no marksman competition;
    
- no hidden-object challenge;
    
- no replacement challenge.
    

The final Night Stalker cannot simply be won through another contest.

It must be:

> **earned.**

The final statement has produced considerably more concern.

> **“I'm always watching.”**

Neri has not clarified what she means by this.

---

# 5. SECURITY CONSIDERATIONS

The museum display does not override the Night Stalker's existing protection.

NS-010 remains an authorized Night Stalker.

Unauthorized attempts to remove the weapon will trigger its established defensive safeguard.

The Nekonium-plated glass provides additional physical security.

However, C.O.R.E. emphasizes:

> **The primary barrier is not the glass.**

The primary barrier is the fact that the weapon will not accept an unauthorized owner.

---

# 6. VISITOR REACTIONS

The exhibit has rapidly become one of the most frequently visited displays in the Blacksite Museum.

Common observations include:

> “That's the last one?”

> “Wait, it's actually gold?”

> “Who's going to get it?”

> “What does ‘earn it’ mean?”

> “Is there going to be another competition?”

Museum staff have been instructed to answer the last question with:

> **“No.”**

When asked what someone must do to earn NS-010, staff have no official answer.

Neri has provided none.

---

# 7. MSAC RESPONSE

MSAC personnel have reacted predictably.

Several operators have begun attempting to determine what qualifies as:

> **“Earn it.”**

One proposed another endurance competition.

Rejected.

Another proposed a tactical tournament.

Rejected.

A third suggested asking Neri directly.

This was considered.

The proposal was subsequently withdrawn.

One senior operator summarized the situation:

> **“So she put the final Night Stalker in a museum, told us to earn it, and then refused to tell us how.”**

A nearby operator replied:

> **“That's exactly what she wants.”**

Neither individual could provide evidence.

---

# 8. THE LETTER

The handwritten letter accompanying NS-010 remains inside the case.

Unlike the podium inscription, its contents have not been publicly reproduced.

The letter is considered part of the personal presentation package.

It is not intended to be read by museum visitors.

The case therefore contains something particularly unusual:

A weapon intended for someone who does not yet exist as its authorized owner.

The letter is waiting for them.

---

# 9. ARCHIVES OBSERVATION

The nine existing Night Stalkers are distributed among their authorized owners.

NS-010 is different.

It is not someone's weapon.

Not yet.

It is a **promise.**

Neri has effectively placed the final Night Stalker behind glass and announced to the entire Blacksite:

> _If you want it, prove that you deserve it._

And then she walked away.
