### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / FAMILY COMMUNICATION INCIDENT LOG**

# **ORPHIE & CORIN — THE WRONG WORDS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Concern  
**Subjects:** Orphie / Corin  
**Associated Personnel:** Lumi / Arcturus / Norma / SAFEHOUSE Residents  
**Status:** **CONDITION WORSENED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie and Corin finally had a private conversation regarding the events surrounding Orphie's disappearance.

Both sisters entered the conversation with the intention of addressing what happened.

Neither succeeded.

Orphie attempted to explain herself.

Corin attempted to explain what the disappearance had done to her.

The conversation instead became increasingly painful.

Orphie repeatedly chose the wrong words.

She did not intend to hurt Corin.

She simply did not understand how severely her words would affect her.

---

# **THE CONVERSATION**

The two sisters sat across from each other in the treehouse.

For several minutes, neither spoke.

Eventually, Corin broke the silence.

> **Corin:** "...Why did you leave?"

Orphie answered immediately.

> **Orphie:** "...Because I wanted to."

Corin went quiet.

Orphie realized the answer sounded harsh.

She attempted to clarify.

> "...I needed to be alone."

Corin:

> "...You could've told me."

Orphie:

> "...You wouldn't have let me go."

Corin looked down.

> "...Probably not."

---

# **THE FIRST MISTAKE**

Orphie continued.

> "...That's why I left without telling you."

Corin's hands tightened.

> "...So you knew I'd stop you."

> "...Yes."

> "...And you still did it."

> "...Yes."

Corin looked away.

Orphie did not understand why this was hurting her.

---

# **CORIN'S QUESTION**

> **Corin:** "...Did you ever think about me?"

Orphie paused.

> "...A little."

Corin looked at her.

> "...A little?"

Orphie:

> "...I was mostly thinking about myself."

Silence.

The words were honest.

They were also devastating.

---

# **THE SECOND MISTAKE**

Corin:

> "...Then why did you come back?"

Orphie:

> "...They found me."

Corin's expression changed.

Orphie continued:

> "...I didn't want to be found."

Corin:

> "...You didn't want to come back?"

> "...No."

Corin looked down.

> "...I see."

Orphie noticed something was wrong.

> "...Why are you upset?"

Corin laughed quietly.

It was not a happy laugh.

> "...Nothing."

---

# **ORPHIE'S ATTEMPT TO HELP**

Orphie became frustrated.

> "...You're the one who needs to get better."

Corin looked up.

> "...What?"

> "...You're still broken."

Corin went silent.

Orphie continued.

> "...I came back. So you should be getting better."

The statement was meant to be reassuring.

It wasn't.

---

# **THE THIRD MISTAKE**

Corin:

> "...You think I should be fine because you're back?"

Orphie:

> "...Yes."

Corin:

> "...After everything?"

Orphie:

> "...What else am I supposed to do?"

Corin looked at her.

> "...I don't know."

---

# **THE WORDS THAT BROKE THE CONVERSATION**

Orphie eventually said:

> "...You need to stop making my disappearance about you."

Silence.

Corin froze.

Orphie continued:

> "...I left because I couldn't stand being around anyone."

> "...It wasn't about you."

Corin stared at the floor.

> "...Okay."

Orphie:

> "...I didn't leave to hurt you."

Corin:

> "...I know."

> "...Then stop acting like I did."

Corin's voice became barely audible.

> "...Okay."

---

# **AFTERMATH**

Corin left the room.

She did not argue.

She did not cry in front of Orphie.

She simply walked away.

Orphie remained seated.

She genuinely believed the conversation had been necessary.

She did not understand why it had gone wrong.

---

# **CORIN**

Later that evening, Corin was found sitting alone.

She repeatedly whispered:

> "...It wasn't about me."

> "...It wasn't about me."

> "...It wasn't about me."

She appeared to be attempting to convince herself.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie later asked Norma:

> "...What did I say wrong?"

Norma stared at her.

> "...Which part?"

Orphie remained silent.

Norma sighed.

> "...Almost all of it."

Orphie frowned.

> "...I was being honest."

> "...Honesty isn't always enough."

---

# **NORMA'S EXPLANATION**

Norma sat beside her.

> "...Corin didn't need you to explain why you left."

> "...Then what did she need?"

> "...She needed you to understand what happened to her."

Orphie:

> "...I know what happened."

Norma:

> "...No."

> "...You know the sequence of events."

> "...You still don't understand what it felt like."

Orphie said nothing.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi later spoke with Orphie.

> "...You hurt her."

Orphie:

> "...I didn't mean to."

> "...I know."

> "...Then why are you angry?"

Lumi:

> "...Because intention doesn't erase the effect."

Orphie looked away.

---

# **THE CONTRADICTION**

Orphie wants Corin to recover.

She wants her to return to work.

She wants her to sleep.

She wants her to stop being unstable.

But she keeps approaching Corin as though recovery is a decision Corin can simply make.

> **"Get better."**

> **"Try harder."**

> **"Stop doing this."**

> **"You need to move on."**

To Orphie, these are instructions.

To Corin, they sound like confirmation that she has failed.

---

# **CURRENT EFFECT**

Following the conversation:

- Corin became more withdrawn.
    
- Communication between the sisters decreased.
    
- Corin's self-blame increased.
    
- Corin became less willing to discuss her condition.
    
- Orphie became frustrated and confused.
    
- Family members became more concerned.
    

The conversation did not resolve anything.

It made the situation worse.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie was finally given an opportunity to speak to Corin.

She told the truth.

But truth without understanding became cruelty.

She said she left because she wanted to.

She admitted she had mostly thought about herself.

She said she didn't want to come back.

She told Corin to stop making the disappearance about her.

None of these statements were necessarily lies.

But Corin did not need facts.

She needed her sister to understand that, while Orphie had been trying to disappear...

**Corin had been trying desperately to keep her alive in her mind.**

Orphie walked away from the conversation believing she had explained herself.

Corin walked away believing she had once again failed.

**End of Family Communication Incident Log**
