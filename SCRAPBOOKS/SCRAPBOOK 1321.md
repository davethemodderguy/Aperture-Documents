# 1. INVESTIGATION SUMMARY

Following Jake's recent account of his childhood and death, C.O.R.E. initiated a historical background investigation into his mother.

The investigation produced an important correction to Jake's existing historical profile.

### Jake was not born.

At least, there is no evidence that Jake was ever born through an ordinary biological process.

He was **found.**

This distinction was not previously known.

Jake himself has never described being born.

He remembers simply:

> **"Being there."**

He remembers his mother finding him.

He remembers being taken home.

He remembers being cared for.

He remembers being taken on a picnic.

But there is no memory of infancy, birth, pregnancy, or a conventional early childhood.

The newly recovered records support his version.

---

# 2. THE ORIGINAL RECORD

The earliest surviving record associated with Jake is not a birth certificate.

It is an emergency report.

Approximately six years before his death, his mother contacted local authorities regarding a child she had discovered alone.

The original report describes:

> **"A young male child of unknown origin found unattended."**

No parent was present.

No accompanying adult was identified.

No identification was found on the child.

No hospital record matched him.

No birth registration could be located.

No missing-child report corresponded to him.

The child himself could provide almost no information.

He knew his name.

He knew the word:

> **"Mom."**

But could not explain where he had come from.

---

# 3. THE DISCOVERY

According to the recovered statement from his mother, she found Jake alone near a wooded area.

She initially assumed he had been abandoned.

She searched the surrounding area for an adult.

Nobody came.

She contacted authorities.

The child remained unusually calm.

When asked where his parents were, he reportedly answered:

> **"I don't know."**

When asked where he lived:

> **"I don't know."**

When asked his name:

> **"Jake."**

When asked who she was:

The child pointed toward the woman who had found him.

> **"Mom."**

The statement became significant decades later.

Jake had apparently assigned the role immediately.

---

# 4. NO BIRTH RECORD

An extensive search of surviving civil records found:

**No birth certificate.**

**No hospital birth record.**

**No midwife record.**

**No adoption record.**

**No orphanage intake record.**

**No immigration record.**

**No corresponding maternal pregnancy record.**

**No documented biological father.**

There is therefore no conventional documentation establishing Jake's birth.

The earliest confirmed point of his existence is:

> **The moment he was found.**

---

# 5. HIS MOTHER

The woman who found Jake eventually became his legal guardian.

She identified herself as his mother.

Jake accepted this completely.

For him, there was no distinction between:

> "The woman who found me"

and

> **"Mom."**

She raised him.

She fed him.

She gave him clothing.

She took him outside.

She gave him his teddy bear.

She eventually took him on the picnic that preceded his death.

Whatever her later failures, the available records confirm that she was, for the majority of Jake's remembered childhood, the only mother he knew.

---

# 6. EARLY CHILDHOOD

The investigation found an unusual pattern in Jake's early records.

He displayed several characteristics that were difficult to explain biologically.

Among them:

- unusually stable physical development;
    
- inconsistent medical history;
    
- no documented infancy;
    
- no vaccination history;
    
- no genetic family history;
    
- no recorded childhood illnesses;
    
- unusual resistance to environmental conditions;
    
- and recurring statements from his mother that Jake was "not like other children."
    

None of these observations were sufficiently investigated at the time.

The family had little interaction with medical institutions.

This resulted in substantial gaps in his historical record.

---

# 7. WHY HIS MOTHER KEPT HIM

The investigation cannot establish with certainty why Jake's mother chose to keep him.

Her surviving records contain several contradictory explanations.

At different times she described Jake as:

> "A child I found."

> "My son."

> "A strange little boy."

And, significantly:

> "Something I was given."

The final phrase remains unexplained.

C.O.R.E. has found no evidence establishing who or what may have "given" Jake to her.

---

# 8. FAMILY LIFE

Despite the unusual circumstances surrounding his origin, Jake appears to have understood his household as normal.

His surviving memories include:

- meals;
    
- sleeping beside his teddy bear;
    
- playing outside;
    
- being taken places;
    
- asking his mother questions;
    
- waiting for her;
    
- and believing that she loved him.
    

He did not understand that he was different.

He was simply:

> **Jake.**

---

# 9. THE TEDDY BEAR

The teddy bear has also been traced to this period.

According to surviving information, Jake's mother gave it to him while he was still young.

The bear became his primary comfort object.

This predates Jake's death.

The cardiac anomaly developed later.

C.O.R.E. therefore considers the emotional significance of the bear to be independent of its later anomalous function.

Jake did not love the bear because it contained his heart.

His heart eventually became connected to it.

He loved it first.

---

# 10. THE PICNIC

Approximately seventy-five years ago, Jake's mother took him on a picnic.

This is the final confirmed event involving both of them.

According to Jake's own account:

> "My mom took me out on a picnic."

She subsequently told him that she had forgotten the food.

She instructed him to wait.

She said she would return.

Jake waited.

She did not return.

Jake remained at the location until he died.

His death occurred approximately two weeks later.

---

# 11. POSTHUMOUS BACKGROUND FINDING

The investigation also confirmed that Jake's mother died years ago.

She did not return to the picnic site.

She did not report Jake missing.

She did not make a documented attempt to locate him.

She subsequently lived a separate life.

The full circumstances surrounding her failure to return remain under review.

However, C.O.R.E. has already confirmed one important fact:

> **Jake spent approximately seventy-five years believing his mother might still come back.**

She did not.

---

# 12. PRIOR INTERVIEW

Before her death, Jake's mother was located and interviewed regarding her relationship with him.

Her statements were deeply unfavorable.

She described Jake as:

> **"A problematic and accidental child."**

She also stated that she hated him.

These statements were originally restricted from Jake.

Jake later overheard personnel discussing them.

This caused a significant deterioration in his emotional state.

The discovery that his mother did not regard him in the way he had believed remains one of the most painful events in his current psychological history.

---

# 13. THE CONTRADICTION

The historical record now presents an unusual contradiction.

Jake was:

**Found.**

Yet he remembers being:

**Raised.**

He was never documented as being born.

Yet he remembers being:

**A child.**

His mother apparently did not give birth to him.

Yet Jake genuinely regarded her as:

**His mother.**

He spent decades waiting for her return.

Yet she had apparently moved on long before his death was ever discovered.

The investigation cannot currently resolve how Jake came into existence.

---

# 14. C.O.R.E. HYPOTHESES

Several possibilities remain open.

### A. Anomalous Origin

Jake may have manifested or been created through an unknown anomalous process.

### B. External Creation

Jake may have been produced by an unidentified organization or entity before being abandoned.

### C. Dimensional Displacement

Jake may have originated somewhere outside normal local reality and appeared in the area where his mother found him.

### D. Unknown Biological Origin

Jake may have originated through a biological process that left no conventional documentation.

### E. Unknown

The available evidence is insufficient.

C.O.R.E. will not assign a definitive origin without evidence.

---

# 15. JAKE'S CURRENT KNOWLEDGE

Jake does not know that he was never documented as being born.

He knows only what he remembers.

His earliest memory is not:

> "I was born."

It is:

> **"My mom found me."**

This distinction should be preserved when speaking with him.

Personnel should not approach Jake with:

> "You weren't born."

or:

> "Your mother wasn't your real mother."

Such statements would unnecessarily destabilize his understanding of his own identity.

---

# 16. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri reviewed the recovered records.

She remained silent for several minutes.

Eventually she wrote:

> **"He doesn't have an origin."**

Then she crossed it out.

She wrote:

> **"We don't know his origin."**

She kept that version.

---

# 17. NORA'S RESPONSE

Nora was considerably less interested in the biological question.

Her response was:

> **NORA:**  
> "He's Jake."

The investigator explained that the purpose of the investigation was to understand where Jake came from.

Nora replied:

> **NORA:**  
> "Then find out."

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> "But he's still Jake."

No further comment was necessary.

---

# 18. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake was eventually told that C.O.R.E. had found records about his early childhood.

He listened quietly.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Did they find where I was born?"

The researcher hesitated.

> **RESEARCHER:**  
> "No."

Jake looked down.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Because I wasn't born."

The researcher looked at him.

Jake continued:

> **JAKE:**  
> "Mom found me."

Pause.

> **JAKE:**  
> "That's where I started."

The researcher did not correct him.

Jake hugged his teddy bear.

> **JAKE:**  
> "That's enough."

---

# 19. CURRENT ASSESSMENT

The investigation has changed one fundamental assumption about Jake.

Previously, C.O.R.E. records treated his childhood as an ordinary human childhood followed by anomalous death.

This is no longer supported.

Jake's anomalous nature predates his death.

His existence predates his documented relationship with Aperture Industries.

His origin predates every known Blacksite record.

And there is no evidence that he was ever born in the conventional sense.

He was:

> **Found.**

By a woman who became his mother.

He grew up believing that was the beginning of his life.

Whether it was the beginning of his existence remains unknown.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake spent seventy-five years believing that his story began with a picnic.

It didn't.

His story began earlier.

A child appeared somewhere.

A woman found him.

She took him home.

She called him her son.

He called her Mom.

Nobody knows where the child came from.

Nobody knows who made him.

Nobody knows why he existed.

And after seventy-five years, the only thing Jake himself seems certain about is remarkably simple:

> **"Mom found me."**

Perhaps that is all the origin he needs.

Until C.O.R.E. discovers otherwise.
