# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / ANOMALOUS RECOVERY EVENT**

# **JUST ENOUGH**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Location:** Treehouse Exterior  
**Status:** **HOPE RESTORED — LOW LEVEL**

---

# **EVENT SUMMARY**

At approximately ██:██, Corin moved her wings.

Not to cover herself.

Not to shield herself from the snow.

She **flapped them.**

Once.

Then again.

The motion was weak.

Unsteady.

Almost forgotten.

Then Corin rose from the ground.

---

# **FLIGHT**

For the first time in approximately one year, Corin flew.

She did not climb particularly high.

She did not travel far.

She simply hovered above the snow.

Her wings moved awkwardly at first.

Loose snow fell from between the feathers.

Several clumps scattered beneath her.

Corin looked down.

Then looked at her wings.

She kept flying.

---

# **THE FEATHERS**

The dormant feathers responded.

A faint azure glow appeared between them.

Not the intense light they once produced.

Not the endless burning flame.

Just...

**light.**

Soft.

Barely visible.

But unmistakable.

One feather detached from her wing.

It floated downward.

Instead of remaining completely dormant, it emitted a faint azure radiance.

Corin caught it.

She stared at it for several seconds.

---

# **THE LANTERNS**

Personnel inside the Treehouse began noticing something unusual.

The Feather Lanterns.

Every lantern containing one of Corin's feathers had previously gone completely dark.

They had remained that way for months.

Now...

They were glowing again.

Barely.

A weak azure light.

Almost like the feathers were remembering how to shine.

No ignition occurred.

No heat was detected.

No anomalous discharge was recorded.

Only light.

---

# **THE OBSERVATION**

Orphie noticed first.

She looked toward the windows.

Then outside.

Corin was in the air.

Orphie didn't move.

She simply stared.

Corin noticed her.

For a moment, neither said anything.

Then Corin smiled.

It was small.

Almost imperceptible.

But it was real.

---

# **THE CHANGE**

Corin landed.

Her wings were still stiff.

Her flight was still imperfect.

She nearly stumbled when her feet touched the ground.

But she remained standing.

Snow slid from her feathers.

She looked toward the Treehouse.

Then toward Orphie.

She whispered:

> **"...I flew."**

Orphie smiled through tears.

> **"Yeah."**

Corin looked down at the faintly glowing feather in her hand.

> **"They're glowing."**

> **"I see."**

Corin's smile grew slightly.

---

# **NOT A MIRACLE**

C.O.R.E. Medical Division has emphasized that this does not constitute a complete recovery.

Corin remains severely compromised.

Her hearts remain inactive.

Her organs remain nonfunctional by conventional standards.

Her regeneration remains largely dormant.

Her emotional condition remains fragile.

Nothing has suddenly become normal.

But something changed.

---

# **HOPE**

Corin did not suddenly become happy.

She did not forget what happened.

She did not stop hurting.

She did not forgive everything.

She did not become the old Corin.

She simply found something small.

Something barely large enough to move.

Something she had once believed was impossible.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

That evening, Orphie opened Corin's research notebook.

She found the familiar sentence.

> **"I live on hope, as long as I spread hope and I have hope of my own, I will move on, live with Orphie :D"**

Orphie stared at it.

Then looked outside.

Corin was sitting beneath the tree.

But this time, she wasn't hiding beneath her wings.

One wing was partially extended.

A faint azure glow illuminated the snow around her.

---

# **CORIN'S NEW ENTRY**

Later that night, Corin added a new page.

The handwriting was shaky.

The entry was extremely short.

> **"I flew today."**

A second line appeared beneath it.

> **"I thought I couldn't anymore."**

Then:

> **"Maybe I can still do some things."**

She paused for a long time.

Then added:

> **"Maybe."**

No elaborate conclusion.

No grand declaration.

No promise that everything would become better.

Just:

> **"Maybe."**

---

# **THE FEATHER LANTERNS**

That night, the Treehouse looked different.

The Feather Lanterns remained dim.

But they were no longer dark.

Every lantern carried the faintest trace of azure light.

Enough to illuminate almost nothing.

Enough to prove something was still there.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin did not regain hope all at once.

There was no sudden transformation.

No overwhelming happiness.

No miraculous return to who she used to be.

Just one flap.

Then another.

And suddenly, she was in the air.

The snow fell from her wings.

Her feathers glowed.

The lanterns answered.

And Corin realized something.

She could still move.

Maybe not far.

Maybe not quickly.

Maybe not the way she used to.

But she could move.

She had enough hope to take one step forward.

Not enough to forget.

Not enough to heal everything.

Just enough.

And for Corin...

**just enough was enough.**

**End of Anomalous Recovery Event**
