## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake Akana has revived.

The event occurred after approximately two months of confirmed absence, discovery of his body in an abandoned maintenance locker, and a subsequent period during which medical instruments detected only an extraordinarily faint silver life signal.

The signal had provided no reliable indication that Jake would recover. No conventional revival sequence had begun. No white fire had appeared. No meaningful restoration of normal biological function had been observed.

Tom Akana, Jake's older brother, remained beside him throughout the monitoring period.

At an unspecified point during the extended vigil, Tom's accumulated grief and frustration reached a breaking point. He struck Jake very hard in the right thigh.

The action was impulsive. It was not medically authorized, was not performed as part of a treatment protocol, and is not to be replicated.

**Immediately afterward, Jake revived.**

The first signs were movement and a sudden change in the monitoring data. Jake's vital signs began to return, followed by an observable restoration of consciousness. The precise physiological mechanism linking the impact to the revival remains unknown.

Tom's response was not triumph.

It was disbelief, followed by panic, followed by an overwhelming realization that his younger brother was looking back at him again.

The revival has since led to the implementation of a small strap worn around Jake's right thigh. The device serves two functions:

- **Medical monitor:** provides continuous tracking of selected physiological and anomalous indicators, including changes relevant to Jake's known instability.
    
- **Location tracker:** allows authorized personnel to determine Jake's location within the Blacksite and identify unexplained absences more promptly.
    

The device is not a restraint, disciplinary measure, or substitute for direct human care.

It exists because Jake's disappearance went unnoticed for two months, his death was not followed by a timely recovery, and the facility can no longer justify relying on assumptions about where he is or whether he will return.

**C.O.R.E. STATUS:** Jake Akana is alive and responsive. Post-revival medical assessment is ongoing. Tom Akana is not to be held responsible for providing future revival interventions.

## II. THE MOMENT OF REVIVAL

### Incident Reconstruction — SAFEHOUSE, Floor 7

Tom had been sitting beside Jake's mattress for hours.

The monitor continued to display its faint silver trace. It had not become a heartbeat. It had not developed into the familiar signs of regeneration. Every new reading seemed to offer the same cruel combination of possibility and uncertainty.

Jake was still there.

Jake was not waking up.

Tom had listened to specialists explain the limits of their knowledge. He had watched Aries try to remain composed. He had heard Bao reassure Frost that they needed to wait.

He had waited.

And waited.

And waited.

Eventually, Tom stood beside the mattress and stared at his younger brother.

**TOM:** "Come on."

No response.

**TOM:** "Jake."

The silver trace appeared.

Then disappeared.

Tom's hands tightened.

**TOM:** "You have GOT to be kidding me."

He leaned over Jake.

**TOM:** "You can't just—"

His voice failed.

For two months, Tom had believed Jake was gone. Then a signal had appeared, and he had allowed himself to hope. The hope had become another vigil, another series of measurements, another period of watching a body that would not move.

He was frightened.

He was exhausted.

He was angry—not at Jake, exactly, but at the situation, at the helplessness, at the unbearable possibility that the smallest indication of life might be all that remained.

**TOM:** "WAKE UP!"

He struck Jake very hard in the right thigh.

The sound was abrupt enough to make the attending nurse turn around.

**NURSE:** "TOM!"

Tom immediately froze.

He stared at his hand.

Then at Jake.

**TOM:** "I—"

A movement.

It was small, but unmistakable.

Jake's body jerked.

The monitor erupted into a series of readings that had not been present moments earlier. The silver trace strengthened, broke into a new pattern, and was followed by a rapid succession of physiological changes.

The specialist rushed to the bedside.

**SPECIALIST:** "He's responding! Get the equipment ready!"

Tom stumbled backward.

**TOM:** "What did I do?"

**SPECIALIST:** "Give us room!"

Jake drew a breath.

It was shallow and uneven, but it was a breath.

His fingers twitched against the bedding.

His eyes opened.

For the first few seconds, he appeared unable to focus. His blue constellation-like eyes shifted around the room, taking in the lights, the faces, and the unfamiliar density of medical equipment.

Then his gaze settled on Tom.

**JAKE:** "…Tom?"

Tom stared at him.

He did not answer.

**JAKE:** "Why're you crying?"

Tom made a sound that was halfway between a laugh and a sob.

**TOM:** "YOU—"

He covered his mouth.

Jake blinked slowly.

**JAKE:** "Did I do something?"

Tom looked at the hand he had used to strike Jake, then back at his brother.

**TOM:** "I'm sorry."

Jake's expression remained confused.

**JAKE:** "For what?"

Tom could not answer.

He dropped beside the mattress and clutched Jake's hand.

**TOM:** "I'm sorry. I'm sorry. I'm sorry."

Jake's fingers curled weakly around his.

**JAKE:** "Tom?"

**TOM:** "I'm here."

**JAKE:** "Okay."

Tom bent his head over their joined hands.

**TOM:** "I'm here."

Jake looked around the room again.

**JAKE:** "Why is everybody here?"

No one answered immediately.

Then Jake looked back at Tom.

**JAKE:** "Did I miss something?"

Tom began crying harder.

**TOM:** "Yeah, little brother."

He held Jake's hand as carefully as he could.

**TOM:** "You missed a lot."

## III. MEDICAL RESPONSE AND CAUSALITY REVIEW

The medical team immediately assessed Jake following the unexpected revival.

His return to consciousness was accompanied by renewed measurable physiological activity. The silver trace no longer represented the sole indication of life.

The team recorded the timing of the impact and the subsequent changes but did not conclude that the strike had caused the revival in a medically reproducible manner.

The event establishes a sequence:

1. Jake remained unresponsive with a minimal life signal.
    
2. Tom struck Jake's right thigh.
    
3. Jake displayed immediate observable changes.
    
4. Normal life signs began to return.
    
5. Jake regained consciousness.
    

It does **not** establish a safe or repeatable treatment.

The impact may have acted as an anomalous stimulus, coincided with a delayed regenerative transition, or triggered a process that current instrumentation cannot explain. No available evidence distinguishes these possibilities conclusively.

The right thigh was examined for injury. The team documented the impact and assessed Jake for resulting damage. Any residual bruising or soft-tissue injury is to be treated independently of the revival.

### Medical Directive

No personnel are authorized to strike Jake, deliberately injure him, or apply painful stimulation in an attempt to reproduce the event.

Tom is explicitly exempt from any expectation that he should repeat the action if Jake becomes unresponsive again.

If Jake experiences another apparent death or serious medical episode, personnel must initiate emergency assessment and appropriate care. The fact that an impulsive action coincided with this revival does not make that action an approved intervention.

During the subsequent interview, Tom repeatedly asked whether he had saved Jake.

The attending specialist answered:

**SPECIALIST:** "You were there when he revived. We cannot establish that the impact was what brought him back."

**TOM:** "But he woke up."

**SPECIALIST:** "Yes."

**TOM:** "Right after I hit him."

**SPECIALIST:** "Yes."

**TOM:** "Then how can you say it wasn't me?"

**SPECIALIST:** "I am not saying it wasn't. I am saying we don't know."

Tom looked down at his hands.

**SPECIALIST:** "You do not have to hit him again to prove anything. You do not have to recreate this. We will not ask you to."

Tom did not respond immediately.

**TOM:** "Good."

He looked toward Jake's bed.

**TOM:** "Because I don't ever want to do that to him again."

## IV. JAKE'S FIRST CONVERSATION AFTER REVIVAL

Jake remained under close observation while the medical team assessed his condition.

His awareness returned gradually. He appeared confused by the number of people in the room and by the length of time that had passed.

Tom stayed close, though he kept his hands to himself unless Jake reached for him.

Eventually, Jake looked down at his own body.

**JAKE:** "Why am I in bed?"

Tom's face tightened.

**TOM:** "You were very sick."

**JAKE:** "Oh."

A pause.

**JAKE:** "Did I die?"

Nobody in the room moved.

The question was asked in a small, uncertain voice.

Tom looked at the medical specialist. The specialist did not answer on his behalf.

**TOM:** "Yes."

Jake stared at him.

**JAKE:** "Oh."

He looked at the ceiling.

For a moment, he appeared to be thinking.

**JAKE:** "Did I come back?"

Tom swallowed.

**TOM:** "Yeah."

Jake turned toward him.

**JAKE:** "You're crying again."

Tom laughed weakly through his tears.

**TOM:** "Yeah. I am."

**JAKE:** "Why?"

Tom leaned closer.

**TOM:** "Because I missed you."

Jake's expression changed.

The confusion did not disappear, but something in his face softened.

**JAKE:** "I was gone?"

Tom nodded.

**JAKE:** "For how long?"

The room fell quiet.

Tom was unable to answer.

Aries, standing near the door, stepped forward.

**ARIES:** "A long time, little brother."

Jake looked from Aries to Tom.

**JAKE:** "Oh."

He seemed to understand that something terrible had happened, even if he could not yet comprehend its full extent.

Then, very quietly:

**JAKE:** "I'm sorry."

Tom immediately shook his head.

**TOM:** "No."

Jake flinched slightly at the force of the response.

Tom caught himself and lowered his voice.

**TOM:** "No, Jake. You don't apologize for this."

**JAKE:** "But—"

**TOM:** "You don't."

Tom took a breath.

**TOM:** "We're just glad you're here."

Jake watched him for a long moment.

**JAKE:** "Okay."

After another pause, he asked the question that nearly undid Tom all over again.

**JAKE:** "Can you stay?"

Tom sat down beside him.

**TOM:** "Yes."

**JAKE:** "Even if you have work?"

**TOM:** "We'll figure it out."

Jake looked at him uncertainly.

**TOM:** "And if I have to leave, I'll tell you. I'll make sure somebody checks on you. You don't have to keep asking everybody."

Jake's eyes lowered.

Tom gently squeezed his hand.

**TOM:** "You don't have to earn company, Jake."

Jake said nothing.

But he did not let go.

## V. IMPLEMENTATION OF THE RIGHT-THIGH MEDICAL STRAP

### Device Designation: SAFEHOUSE RESIDENT MONITOR — JAK-01

Following Jake's revival and stabilization, Medical Response and Engineering approved the fitting of a small strap around his right thigh.

The strap is lightweight and designed to remain unobtrusive beneath ordinary clothing. It is not intended to restrict movement, interfere with Jake's plant-based vascular structures, or alter his ability to transform into his white cat form.

The device combines two functions that were previously handled separately or inconsistently.

<box> </box>

### Function A — Medical Monitoring

The device monitors selected indicators associated with Jake's condition. The exact monitored parameters are maintained in the restricted medical specification.

Its primary purpose is to provide an early indication of significant physiological changes, including a return to an extremely weak life signal, sudden deterioration, or an unexplained interruption in the patterns associated with his current state.

It is not a guarantee that every crisis will be detected.

It is not a substitute for examination by medical personnel.

It is not a device capable of independently determining whether Jake is conscious, in pain, or psychologically distressed.

It is, however, an additional source of information that may help staff recognize an emergency before it becomes another prolonged absence.

### Function B — Location Tracking

The strap also provides authorized personnel with Jake's location within the Blacksite.

This function was implemented specifically because Jake remained undiscovered for approximately two months.

The tracker is intended to answer a basic question that should never have been left unanswered for so long:

**Where is Jake?**

If Jake is in his bedroom, the system should indicate that he is in his bedroom.

If he is visiting the kitchen, the system should indicate that he is in the kitchen.

If he enters a restricted or abandoned area, the system can help authorized staff recognize that he is there.

If his location cannot be confirmed for an unusual length of time, the system can initiate a welfare check rather than allowing an unexplained absence to become an administrative blind spot.

The tracker is not to be used to punish Jake for moving around the facility, visiting friends, or seeking solitude.

His freedom of movement remains intact.

### Fit and Comfort

The strap was fitted only after Jake was medically stable enough for the procedure.

Personnel explained its purpose to him in simple terms. He was not presented with the device as a punishment or as evidence that he could no longer be trusted.

Jake examined the strap with considerable curiosity.

**JAKE:** "What's this?"

**ENGINEER:** "A medical monitor. It also helps us find you if we need to."

**JAKE:** "Like a treasure hunt?"

The engineer hesitated.

**ENGINEER:** "Not exactly."

**JAKE:** "Can it tell where I am?"

**ENGINEER:** "Yes."

Jake looked down at his right thigh.

**JAKE:** "So if I go to the kitchen, it knows?"

**ENGINEER:** "It should."

**JAKE:** "What if I go under the bed?"

**ENGINEER:** "It should still know."

Jake considered this.

**JAKE:** "What if I'm a cat?"

**ENGINEER:** "We're testing that, too."

Jake's face brightened slightly.

**JAKE:** "Cool."

Tom, sitting nearby, did not smile.

Jake noticed.

**JAKE:** "Tom?"

**TOM:** "Yeah?"

**JAKE:** "You don't like it?"

Tom looked at the strap.

**TOM:** "I like that we'll know where you are."

He paused.

**TOM:** "I hate that we needed it."

Jake looked down again.

**JAKE:** "Oh."

Tom reached out, then stopped short of touching the device.

**TOM:** "It isn't because you're in trouble."

**JAKE:** "I know."

**TOM:** "And you can tell us if it hurts or bothers you."

**JAKE:** "Okay."

Jake looked back at the engineer.

**JAKE:** "Can it tell me where cake is?"

**ENGINEER:** "No."

Jake's expression fell.

**JAKE:** "Then it needs an upgrade."

For the first time since the revival, Tom let out a small, genuine laugh.

It did not erase the grief in the room.

But it was Jake's laugh waiting to happen, and for now, that was enough.

## VI. FAMILY RESPONSE

### Tom Akana

Tom has remained close to Jake during the initial recovery period.

He has expressed relief that Jake is alive, but his relief is accompanied by guilt over the circumstances of the revival. He is distressed by the fact that he struck his brother, even though the action was followed by Jake's return to consciousness.

C.O.R.E. has made clear that Tom is not expected to reproduce the incident.

Tom has also been instructed that the new monitor does not make him responsible for watching every reading or responding to every alert.

He is Jake's brother.

He is allowed to sleep.

He is allowed to leave the room.

He is allowed to be frightened without turning that fear into a permanent obligation to supervise Jake.

When asked whether he intended to remain beside Jake indefinitely, Tom answered:

**TOM:** "No."

He looked toward the bed.

**TOM:** "I'm going to stay until he doesn't need me right beside him."

A pause.

**TOM:** "Then I'm going to sleep for twelve hours."

**C.O.R.E. OFFICER:** "That is a reasonable plan."

**TOM:** "And then I'm checking on him."

**OFFICER:** "That is also reasonable."

**TOM:** "And if he's asleep, I'm not waking him up."

**OFFICER:** "Good."

Tom looked suspiciously at the officer.

**TOM:** "Why does this feel like a test?"

**OFFICER:** "Because you have not slept enough to pass one."

Tom did not disagree.

### Aries Akana

Aries has supported the medical team's efforts to ensure that Jake's recovery does not depend entirely on Tom's presence.

He has also requested that the strap's location function be governed by clear privacy rules. Only authorized personnel with a legitimate medical or welfare need are to access Jake's location.

The device is intended to prevent another disappearance, not to make Jake's every movement a subject of public attention.

**ARIES:** "He needs to know that we're looking out for him, not watching him because we think he's a problem."

### Bao and Frost

Bao has taken responsibility for helping Jake return to regular meals and a comfortable daily routine as his medical team permits.

Frost has offered to accompany him around the SAFEHOUSE, but has been reminded that companionship must remain an offer rather than a constant supervision duty.

When Jake asked whether the strap meant he could no longer go places by himself, Bao answered directly.

**BAO:** "You can still go places by yourself, sweetheart."

**JAKE:** "Even the kitchen?"

**BAO:** "Especially the kitchen."

**JAKE:** "Can I get cake?"

Bao smiled sadly.

**BAO:** "When the doctors say you're ready to eat normally, we'll talk about cake."

Jake looked deeply offended.

**JAKE:** "This is a terrible hospital."

Frost laughed, then immediately wiped her eyes.

Jake looked at her.

**JAKE:** "Why are you crying?"

Frost hugged him carefully.

**FROST:** "Because you're here."

Jake accepted the hug.

He did not ask her to explain further.

## VII. POST-REVIVAL ASSESSMENT

Jake's revival does not erase the circumstances that preceded it.

The two-month disappearance remains a critical failure.

The psychological distress associated with the final day remains a serious concern.

The exact source of the red text and whispers remains unresolved.

The new strap addresses two specific vulnerabilities: the lack of reliable information about Jake's location and the need for more consistent medical monitoring. It does not, by itself, address his fear of abandonment or guarantee that he will seek help when distressed.

Accordingly, the following support measures remain in effect:

- Jake is to receive regular medical assessments following revival.
    
- The medical strap is to be tested in both human and cat forms.
    
- Location alerts are to prompt a welfare check when warranted, not automatic disciplinary action.
    
- Jake is to be offered companionship without being made to feel that he must request it repeatedly.
    
- Staff are to respond seriously to new visual disturbances, whispers, or similar unexplained symptoms.
    
- Tom and Aries are to receive support independently of their role as Jake's family.
    
- The device must not be treated as a substitute for checking in with Jake as a person.
    

The strap can tell the Blacksite where Jake is.

It cannot tell the Blacksite whether he feels wanted.

That remains a responsibility shared by the people around him.

## VIII. C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE — HE CAME BACK

There is a temptation to describe this incident as a miracle.

Jake was dead. A thin silver signal remained. Tom struck him in a moment of desperation, and Jake opened his eyes.

The temptation is understandable.

It is also dangerous if it causes us to mistake the outcome for a method.

Tom did not discover a reliable way to revive Jake.

He did not become Jake's emergency treatment protocol.

He was a grieving brother who reached the end of his patience, acted impulsively, and was confronted with the impossible moment when his brother began breathing again.

We will not ask him to do it twice.

We will not tell Jake that he owes his return to a painful intervention.

We will not allow the circumstances of the revival to become another reason for either brother to live in fear.

Instead, we will do what should have been done before Jake disappeared.

We will monitor his condition.

We will respond when something changes.

We will notice when he is missing.

We will ask how he is doing, and we will take the answer seriously.

We will give him opportunities for companionship that do not require him to beg for them.

The small strap on Jake's right thigh is not a solution to everything that went wrong. It cannot undo two months in an abandoned locker. It cannot erase the messages that appeared before his death. It cannot guarantee that his body will never fail him again.

But it can help us find him.

It can tell us when his condition changes.

And when Jake wanders away to look for someone to play with, it can help ensure that we do not lose track of him again.

For now, Jake is alive.

Tom has heard his voice.

Aries has seen his eyes open.

Bao and Frost have held him.

The monitor continues to register him as a living resident.

And on his right thigh, beneath a small, unobtrusive strap, there is a device whose purpose is simple:

**If Jake is somewhere in the Blacksite, we should know where.**

Not because he is a prisoner.

Not because he is a problem.

Because he is Jake.

Because he belongs here.

And because we have already spent two months learning what happens when nobody realizes that he is gone.
