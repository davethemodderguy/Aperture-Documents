### **ANOMALOUS ENTITY ORIGIN RECORD**

# **Z-335 — MART CREATION HISTORY**

**Classification:** Level 3 – Allied Entity Archive  
**Subject:** Z-335 Marts  
**Creator:** Lumi  
**Status:** Active Colony

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The Z-335 Mart colony was created by **Lumi**, the mother of Arcturus, Norma, Corin, and Corey.

Unlike most anomalies documented by C.O.R.E., the Marts were not created as weapons, experiments, or containment solutions.

They were created with a singular purpose:

> **To spread happiness, comfort, and companionship.**

The Marts are considered one of Lumi's greatest creations.

---

# **THE CREATION OF THE MARTS**

The exact method used by Lumi to create the first Marts remains partially unknown.

Lumi has never provided a complete technical explanation.

When asked how she created them, her answer was:

> "...I made them."

Further questioning produced:

> "...They needed someone."

No additional details were provided.

---

# **THE FIRST FIVE**

The earliest known Marts were the five original Marts that appeared when Lumi created the colony.

These five are now known as:

> **The Elder Marts**

They are considered the oldest members of the Mart colony.

Unlike normal Marts, the Elder Marts possess:

- Greater opacity.
    
- Slightly larger bodies.
    
- Identical blue blob-like appearance.
    
- Identical top hats.
    
- The ability to speak fluent English.
    

Despite their age, they are not treated as rulers.

They are simply Marts.

---

# **LUMI'S INTENTION**

Lumi did not create the Marts to be powerful.

She created them to be kind.

Their original design philosophy included:

- Emotional support.
    
- Social connection.
    
- Comfort.
    
- Assistance.
    
- Friendship.
    

The Marts' simple behavior is intentional.

They were never meant to be soldiers, despite their later development into highly effective Rapid Response units.

---

# **THE MART FORM**

The basic Mart design reflects Lumi's intentions.

Their bodies are:

- Soft.
    
- Flexible.
    
- Non-threatening.
    
- Easy to approach.
    

Their top hats became a defining feature.

The reason for the hats is unknown.

Lumi has never explained why she gave them this design.

When asked, she simply said:

> "...They looked nice."

---

# **THE MART COLONY**

After creating the first Marts, the colony expanded.

The Marts do not appear to reproduce through conventional biological methods.

When a Mart is lost, current evidence suggests the colony creates a replacement carrying the memories and identity patterns of the previous Mart.

This has led to the theory:

> **Marts do not die and get replaced. They return.**

---

# **EMOTIONAL DESIGN**

Lumi intentionally gave Marts strong emotional sensitivity.

They are capable of:

- Happiness.
    
- Attachment.
    
- Concern.
    
- Curiosity.
    
- Friendship.
    

However, this sensitivity also created a weakness.

Extreme negative emotions can destabilize a Mart.

A Mart experiencing excessive:

- Stress.
    
- Fear.
    
- Worry.
    
- Distress.
    

May enter a critical state and burst.

This is why the Mart colony prioritizes maintaining positive environments.

---

# **LUMI AND THE MARTS**

The Marts refer to Lumi as:

> **"Mom."**

This is not an assigned designation.

It developed naturally.

Lumi is considered the origin point of their existence, but her relationship with them is closer to a parent than a creator.

---

# **THE KCA AND BLACKSITE COLONIES**

Following their creation, the Mart population expanded into two primary groups:

### **KCA Colony**

Located throughout the public KCA facility.

Roles include:

- Security assistance.
    
- Camera monitoring.
    
- Rapid Response.
    
- General interaction.
    

### **Blacksite Colony**

Located within Aperture's hidden Blacksite.

Roles include:

- Assistance.
    
- Emotional support.
    
- Resident companionship.
    
- Facility maintenance.
    

Both colonies maintain the same population:

> **1,750 Marts each.**

---

# **LUMI'S REFLECTION**

When asked if she was proud of creating the Marts, Lumi answered:

> "...Yes."

The interviewer asked why.

She looked toward a group of Marts helping another employee.

After a pause:

> "...Because they chose to be kind."

---

# **RESEARCHER NOTE**

The Marts are unusual among anomalous entities.

Their greatest capability is not their ability to defend facilities.

It is their ability to form connections.

The KCA learned this after numerous failed robberies.

The Blacksite learned this through daily life.

Lumi created something that was never meant to be feared.

She created something that simply wanted to help.

And eventually...
