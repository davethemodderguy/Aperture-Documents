# I. REVISION

The previously designated **SUBSPACE-Ø** has been reclassified.

It is **not** a separate subspace.

It is not a neighboring pocket dimension.

It is not an unrelated anomaly.

It is:

# **NERI'S SUBSPACE.**

The same spatial environment in which Neri's fifteen floating islands were previously observed.

The difference is that the newly discovered entrance does not open anywhere near the known islands.

At least—

**not anywhere immediately visible.**

---

# II. THE HOLE IN THE WALL

The opening remains located within the chamber where Neri's Architect transformation initially became unstable.

Under ordinary conditions, the hole resembles an impossible opening in the wall.

Beyond it is empty space.

No floor.

No ceiling.

No islands.

No artificial stars.

No rope bridges.

No laboratory.

No bedroom.

Nothing.

Yet the spatial signature is unmistakably Neri's.

Guiding Light confirmed it.

Neko confirmed it.

The Azure particulate confirmed it.

There is no remaining uncertainty regarding ownership.

---

# III. THE EMPTY SPACE

The exploration team initially interpreted the emptiness as evidence that Neri's islands had disappeared.

That conclusion is premature.

The subspace is significantly larger than previously believed.

The visible region surrounding the entrance contains absolutely nothing.

However, there is no known method of determining the total extent of Neri's subspace from the entrance.

The original fifteen islands may therefore still exist.

They could be:

### A. Extremely distant.

The entrance may simply be located far outside the inhabited region.

### B. Spatially separated.

Neri may have positioned her islands in a region inaccessible from this entry point without additional traversal.

### C. Reconfigured.

The islands may have been moved or rearranged.

### D. Removed.

Neri may have deliberately dismantled or relocated them.

### E. Hidden.

The subspace may possess additional spatial properties that conceal or isolate sections of itself.

At present, **none of these possibilities can be eliminated.**

---

# IV. THE MOST IMPORTANT DETAIL

The entrance is not necessarily where Neri's subspace **begins.**

It is simply where the Blacksite has managed to enter.

This distinction had initially been overlooked.

Neri's subspace does not appear to possess a conventional "front."

There is no reason for the first discovered entrance to be geographically close to the primary inhabited region.

The Blacksite may have effectively entered a building through a door and discovered that the building is several astronomical distances wide.

---

# V. NERIS'S AZURE TRAIL

The Azure particulate provides the strongest evidence.

Under UV illumination, the empty region contains traces of Neri's Light.

The concentration is extremely low near the entrance.

It increases intermittently farther into the subspace.

This suggests that Neri has been present—or that her Light has passed through—regions considerably beyond the immediate entry point.

The particles do not form a conventional continuous trail.

Instead, they occur in scattered concentrations.

Occasionally:

**A cluster appears.**

Then:

**Nothing for several hundred meters.**

Then:

**Another cluster.**

Then nothing again.

The phenomenon resembles footprints separated by enormous distances.

---

# VI. GUIDING LIGHT'S OBSERVATION

Guiding Light entered the space and looked around.

Nothing.

She turned in a full circle.

Still nothing.

> “This is hers.”

Neko nodded.

> “Yes.”

Guiding Light looked into the distance.

> “Then where are the islands?”

Neko answered:

> “We don't know.”

She stared ahead.

> “They're not gone.”

Neko looked at her.

> “You sure?”

Guiding Light smiled.

> “No.”

A pause.

> “But I don't think she would just erase everything she built.”

---

# VII. NERI'S ARCHIPELAGO

The last confirmed configuration of Neri's subspace consisted of:

- **15 floating islands**
    
- One primary inhabited island
    
- 14 largely undeveloped islands
    
- Localized gravity
    
- Rope bridges
    
- A 250-meter zero-gravity recovery region
    
- Laboratory equipment
    
- Residential areas
    
- Social spaces
    
- Artificial stars
    
- Connected subspace regions
    

None of these structures are visible from the newly discovered entrance.

This does **not** constitute evidence of destruction.

The known archipelago could simply be beyond the current exploration range.

---

# VIII. NEKO'S THEORY

Neko's current model is surprisingly simple.

He draws a large circle on the board.

Then a tiny dot.

> “This is the subspace.”

He points at the dot.

> “This is where we entered.”

He draws another dot on the opposite side.

> “And this is where her islands might be.”

Someone asks:

> “How far?”

Neko pauses.

> “We don't know.”

> “Can we calculate it?”

> “Not yet.”

> “Can we fly there?”

Neko looks at the empty space.

> “Probably.”

A pause.

> “But we don't know which direction.”

---

# IX. THE POSSIBILITY OF LOSS

There remains one possibility nobody wants to discuss.

The islands could be gone.

Neri may have dismantled her environment before sealing herself away.

She could have moved everything.

She could have destroyed the structures.

She could have altered the space itself.

Nothing presently confirms this.

Nothing presently disproves it.

The Blacksite therefore refuses to classify the archipelago as lost.

### It is classified as **UNLOCATED.**

---

# X. SEARCH AUTHORIZATION

A controlled exploration mission has been authorized.

The objective is **not** to locate Neri directly.

The objective is:

> **Determine the spatial relationship between the newly discovered entrance and Neri's known archipelago.**

Explorers will search for:

- Azure particulate concentrations.
    
- Changes in gravitational behavior.
    
- Artificial stellar illumination.
    
- Structural remnants.
    
- Rope bridges.
    
- Island-scale masses.
    
- Subspace boundaries.
    
- Signs of Neri's activity.
    
- Additional entrances or exits.
    

No personnel are to alter the environment unnecessarily.

---

# XI. THE FIRST EXPLORATION

The first team traveled approximately ██ kilometers from the entrance.

Nothing.

No islands.

No structures.

No stars.

Just empty space.

Then the UV sensors detected something.

A faint azure concentration.

The team followed it.

Another appeared.

Then another.

The particles were becoming more common.

The team stopped.

One member asked:

> “Do you think we're getting closer?”

The lead researcher looked at the readings.

> “I don't know.”

Then another particle drifted past.

And another.

And another.

---

# XII. THE POSSIBILITY

There is now a growing suspicion that Neri's archipelago was never located near the original entrance.

The Blacksite may have spent months searching for a doorway into her subspace without realizing that the doorway they eventually found could be **the equivalent of entering a continent from its most remote coastline.**

The islands may still be there.

Somewhere.

Very far away.
