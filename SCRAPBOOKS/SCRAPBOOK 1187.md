# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon encountered **Guiding Light** in the SAFEHOUSE cafeteria while Guiding was assisting with baking.

The encounter was initially unremarkable.

Nora was participating in routine cafeteria activity.

Guiding Light was preparing baked goods.

No rift was visible.

No unusual dimensional activity was detected.

No warning was issued.

The encounter became significant when Nora looked directly at Guiding Light and immediately recognized her.

Not from a photograph.

Not from a file.

Not because Neri had previously told her who Guiding was.

Nora recognized her from memory.

---

# 2. PRE-INCIDENT ACTIVITY

The cafeteria was relatively quiet.

Bao was preparing ingredients.

Guiding Light was assisting with the baking.

Nora had been given a small bowl and was attempting to help.

Her actual contribution consisted primarily of:

- stirring;
    
- asking questions;
    
- eating small amounts of ingredients;
    
- asking whether something was finished yet;
    
- and repeatedly attempting to look inside the oven.
    

Bao eventually moved the bowl slightly farther away from her.

> **Bao:**  
> “Nora.”

> **Nora:**  
> “What?”

> **Bao:**  
> “Stop eating the ingredients.”

> **Nora:**  
> “I'm checking them.”

> **Bao:**  
> “You've checked them six times.”

> **Nora:**  
> “They're still good.”

Guiding laughed.

Nora looked up.

She froze.

---

# 3. RECOGNITION

Nora stared at Guiding.

Guiding noticed.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Nora?”

Nora did not answer.

She simply stared.

Her expression changed from curiosity to recognition.

Then:

> **Nora:**  
> “...You.”

Guiding stopped what she was doing.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Me?”

Nora slowly pointed at her.

> **Nora:**  
> “You're the moon.”

Silence.

Bao looked at Guiding.

Guiding looked at Nora.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Oh.”

Nora's eyes widened.

> **Nora:**  
> “IT'S YOU!”

Several cafeteria occupants turned around.

---

# 4. THE BLUE MOON

Nora immediately ran toward Guiding.

She stopped just short of colliding with her.

> **Nora:**  
> “You're the blue moon!”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “I suppose I am.”

> **Nora:**  
> “You talked to me!”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “I did.”

> **Nora:**  
> “You told me not to be scared!”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Yes.”

> **Nora:**  
> “And you made the blue rift!”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Yes.”

Nora stared at her for another second.

Then she smiled brightly.

> **Nora:**  
> “I FOUND YOU!”

Guiding smiled.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “You did.”

---

# 5. NERI ARRIVES

Neri had been working elsewhere in the cafeteria.

She heard Nora's voice.

She looked over.

Then she saw Guiding.

Then Nora.

Neri immediately understood what had happened.

She walked over.

> **Neri:**  
> “You remember.”

Nora nodded rapidly.

> **Nora:**  
> “That's her!”

She pointed at Guiding.

> **Nora:**  
> “That's the blue moon!”

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:**  
> “I know.”

Nora looked between them.

> **Nora:**  
> “You knew?”

> **Neri:**  
> “I figured it out.”

> **Nora:**  
> “When?”

Neri paused.

> **Neri:**  
> “You told me.”

Nora seemed impressed.

> **Nora:**  
> “Oh.”

Pause.

> **Nora:**  
> “You're smart.”

Neri stared at her.

> **Neri:**  
> “I'm your sister.”

> **Nora:**  
> “You're still smart.”

Neri looked away.

---

# 6. GUIDING'S RESPONSE

Nora immediately began asking questions.

> **Nora:**  
> “Were you really a moon?”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Not literally.”

> **Nora:**  
> “Can you become one?”

Guiding paused.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Yes.”

Nora's eyes widened.

> **Nora:**  
> “Can you do it now?”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “We're in a cafeteria.”

Nora looked around.

> **Nora:**  
> “So?”

Bao immediately intervened.

> **Bao:**  
> “No moon in the cafeteria.”

Nora frowned.

> **Nora:**  
> “Why?”

> **Bao:**  
> “Because we're baking.”

Nora considered this.

> **Nora:**  
> “Okay.”

She immediately returned to the bowl.

---

# 7. THE BAKING CONTINUES

The encounter did not interrupt the baking for long.

Guiding returned to the mixture.

Nora remained beside her.

She watched closely.

> **Nora:**  
> “So you were watching me?”

Guiding nodded.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “For a little while.”

> **Nora:**  
> “Did you see Big Sis?”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Yes.”

Nora smiled.

> **Nora:**  
> “Was she okay?”

Guiding looked toward Neri.

Neri was quietly working on something at the other end of the counter.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “She is now.”

Nora nodded.

> **Nora:**  
> “Good.”

---

# 8. THE QUESTIONS

Nora continued.

> **Nora:**  
> “Why did you help me?”

Guiding answered simply.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Because you needed help.”

> **Nora:**  
> “Why?”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Because you were lost.”

Nora looked down.

> **Nora:**  
> “I was looking for Big Sis.”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “I know.”

> **Nora:**  
> “How did you know?”

Guiding smiled.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “You were very loud about it.”

Nora gasped.

> **Nora:**  
> “I was not!”

Neri quietly looked over.

> **Neri:**  
> “You were.”

Nora looked betrayed.

> **Nora:**  
> “Big Sis!”

Neri returned to her work.

> **Neri:**  
> “It's true.”

---

# 9. NORA'S MEMORY

C.O.R.E. later asked Neri whether Nora appeared frightened during the encounter.

Neri's answer:

> **Neri:**  
> “No.”

When asked why, she replied:

> **Neri:**  
> “She remembered the voice.”

This distinction appears significant.

Nora did not recognize Guiding because of appearance alone.

She recognized:

- the voice;
    
- the blue light;
    
- the feeling of safety;
    
- the rift;
    
- and the moon-like manifestation.
    

The visual resemblance was secondary.

---

# 10. CAKE

Eventually the baking was finished.

Guiding placed the tray down.

Nora stared at it.

Then at Guiding.

> **Nora:**  
> “Did you make cake?”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Yes.”

Nora immediately looked at Neri.

> **Nora:**  
> “Big Sis likes cake.”

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:**  
> “Yes.”

Nora looked back at Guiding.

> **Nora:**  
> “Can I have some?”

Guiding smiled.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Of course.”

Nora immediately took a piece.

She took one bite.

Her eyes widened.

> **Nora:**  
> “You're really good at this.”

Guiding looked pleased.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Thank you.”

Nora took another bite.

Then pointed at Guiding.

> **Nora:**  
> “You're my blue moon.”

Guiding smiled.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “I suppose I am.”

---

# 11. NERI'S OBSERVATION

Neri remained nearby throughout the interaction.

At one point, Nora looked toward Neri.

> **Nora:**  
> “Big Sis.”

> **Neri:**  
> “Hm?”

> **Nora:**  
> “She's nice.”

Then at Nora.

> **Neri:**  
> “Yeah.”

# 12. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The encounter confirms that Nora retains a coherent memory of her interaction with Guiding Light prior to arriving at SAFEHOUSE.

More importantly, the memory does not appear to be associated with fear.

Nora remembers the blue moon as:

> **Someone who found her when she was lost.**

She does not appear disturbed by the revelation that the “moon” was an Architect.

She is, in fact, considerably more interested in whether Guiding can make another cake.

Guiding Light has made no objection.
