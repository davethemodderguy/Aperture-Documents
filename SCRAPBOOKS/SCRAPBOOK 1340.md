# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neri and Nora Tachyon have jointly developed a prototype burst-fire rifle designed specifically for **Frost**.

The weapon has been designated:

> **PERMAFROST**

Unlike conventional firearms, Permafrost does not use conventional projectiles.

Its ammunition system produces highly chilled nitrogen-based anomalous "bullets" capable of delivering extreme cold directly to the target.

The resulting impact can cause severe tissue damage and structural injury.

The weapon is therefore classified as:

> **EXTREMELY DANGEROUS**

and is restricted exclusively to Frost.

---

# 1. DESIGN PURPOSE

The original design request was remarkably simple.

Neri and Nora wanted to create something that:

1. Frost could use naturally;
    
2. incorporated her cryogenic abilities;
    
3. complemented her existing combat capabilities;
    
4. functioned like a conventional customizable rifle;
    
5. and looked sufficiently ridiculous that Neri would consider the project worthwhile.
    

The resulting prototype exceeded expectations.

Nora named it:

> **Permafrost.**

Neri approved the name without modification.

---

# 2. AMMUNITION

Permafrost's ammunition is not conventional metallic ammunition.

The weapon produces compressed bursts of **dangerously cold nitrogen-based projectiles** through its anomalous firing system.

For documentation purposes, C.O.R.E. refers to these as:

> **Cryogenic projectiles**

or, informally:

> **Frozen bullets.**

Upon impact, the projectiles transfer extreme cold into the target area.

Observed effects include:

- severe tissue damage;
    
- rapid localized freezing;
    
- structural weakening;
    
- and fracture-related injury.
    

The weapon is therefore unsuitable for casual use.

---

# 3. BURST-FIRE SYSTEM

Permafrost is configured as a **burst-fire rifle.**

Rather than producing a continuous stream, each trigger activation releases a controlled burst of cryogenic projectiles.

This allows Frost to exploit her natural preference for controlled freezing effects without requiring her to manually generate every projectile.

The weapon's firing system is anomalous.

C.O.R.E. has not authorized conventional reverse-engineering of its internal mechanism.

Neri has already stated that she understands how it works.

Neko has chosen not to ask.

---

# 4. FROST COMPATIBILITY

The weapon was designed around Frost's existing cryogenic abilities.

As a result, Frost can interact with the weapon in ways ordinary users cannot.

She can:

- regulate its thermal output;
    
- stabilize its cryogenic ammunition;
    
- prevent unwanted icing;
    
- manipulate the weapon's external temperature;
    
- and integrate her own abilities with its effects.
    

The rifle does not replace Frost's powers.

It provides another method through which she can express them.

---

# 5. CUSTOMIZATION

Despite its unusual ammunition system, Permafrost follows the same general philosophy as a conventional modular rifle.

The platform supports customization across multiple equipment categories.

### AVAILABLE CATEGORIES

- **Sight**
    
- **Muzzle**
    
- **Barrel**
    
- **Grip / Underbarrel**
    
- **Magazine / Ammunition Module**
    
- **Stock**
    
- **Skin**
    
- **Charm**
    
- **Cryogenic Conversion Components**
    

The final category is unique to Permafrost.

Different approved configurations can alter the rifle's cryogenic behavior without changing its fundamental identity.

---

# 6. VISUAL DESIGN

The prototype features a distinctly cold-oriented appearance.

The weapon incorporates:

- frost-like surface detailing;
    
- pale blue illumination;
    
- cryogenic venting;
    
- reinforced components;
    
- and visible condensation during operation.
    

When Frost holds it, the weapon occasionally develops a thin layer of natural ice.

Neri reportedly considered this:

> **“Expected.”**

Nora considered it:

> **“Cool.”**

Frost simply asked:

> **“Can I keep it?”**

Neri answered:

> **“That's why we made it.”**

The initial test was conducted in a controlled environment.

Frost fired a short burst at a designated inert target.

The target immediately developed extensive frost formation.

The impact zone subsequently fractured under controlled testing conditions.

Frost lowered the rifle.

> **Frost:** “It works.”

Nora:

> “Told you.”

Neri:

> “Obviously.”

Nora:

> “You weren't sure.”

Neri:

> “I was sure.”

Nora:

> “You wrote 'probably'.”

Neri:

> “That means sure.”

---

# 8. SAFETY CLASSIFICATION

Permafrost is **not** approved for general SAFEHOUSE distribution.

It is not a recreational weapon.

It is not a demonstration weapon.

It is not to be loaned to visitors.

Its ammunition is not to be handled outside authorized equipment procedures.

Only Frost is currently authorized to possess and operate the prototype.

This restriction exists because the weapon's cryogenic effects can cause severe injury.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Permafrost represents an unusual intersection between:

**Tachyon engineering.**

**Neri's chemistry expertise.**

**Nora's adaptive design instincts.**

**And Frost's cryogenic abilities.**

The resulting weapon is highly specialized.

It is not intended to replace conventional firearms.

It is not intended to replace Frost's natural abilities.

It is a platform specifically designed around one resident.

That resident happens to be capable of manipulating ice.

Consequently, the rifle's name is not particularly subtle.

It is:

> **PERMAFROST.**

And, according to the designers, that is exactly the point.
