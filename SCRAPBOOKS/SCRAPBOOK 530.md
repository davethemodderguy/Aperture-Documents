# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL RECOVERY / ANOMALOUS DEVELOPMENT REPORT**

# **THE THOUGHT THAT FINALLY LET GO**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **Significant Psychological Improvement**  
**Primary Factor:** **Spontaneous Wing Development**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

For an extended period, Corin was haunted by a single, deeply entrenched belief:

> **"I'm not alive or dead."**

From this belief came others.

> **"I'm a thing."**

> **"I'm a doll with a conscious."**

> **"I'm an abomination from a lab."**

Corin had built much of her identity around these thoughts.

Her body seemed to provide endless evidence supporting them.

The scars.

The stitches.

The missing organs.

The Ether sustaining her.

The inability to die.

The inability to return to her former life.

For a long time, nothing could dislodge the belief.

Then Corin grew wings.

---

# **THE UNEXPECTED CHANGE**

The wings were not created by an Architect.

They were not implanted.

They were not repaired into her body.

They were not the result of another experiment.

They simply appeared while Corin was sleeping.

For the first time, Corin possessed something that could not be attributed to the laboratory or to someone else's intervention.

It was simply **hers.**

That distinction became increasingly important.

---

# **THE FIRST THOUGHT**

When Corin first discovered the wings, she was frightened.

She stared at them.

Touched the feathers.

Moved them.

Then she asked:

> **"Who did this?"**

Nobody knew.

Guiding Light said:

> **"Not me."**

Curious Light confirmed:

> **"Not me."**

Mischevious Light had no involvement.

There was no scientist.

No procedure.

No external cause.

Corin eventually realized:

> **"They just... grew."**

---

# **THE REALIZATION**

The wings gave Corin something she had never considered possible.

Her body could still change.

Not because someone was experimenting on her.

Not because someone was repairing her.

Not because someone was forcing Ether into her.

But because **her body itself was still capable of becoming something new.**

She was no longer only looking backward at what had been done to her.

She had something that belonged entirely to the present.

And possibly the future.

---

# **THE THOUGHT BEGINS TO FADE**

The change was gradual.

Corin still called herself a thing.

She still disliked her scars.

She still remembered the laboratory.

But the old thought became less convincing.

One evening, Corin looked into a mirror.

She saw:

Her pale skin.

Her chalky lime hair.

Her scars.

Her stitches.

Her wings.

She stared for a long time.

Then quietly said:

> **"...The lab didn't give me these."**

She touched one of the feathers.

> **"Nobody did."**

A pause.

> **"They're mine."**

---

# **THE FIRST FLIGHT**

When Corin first flew, something changed.

She was no longer thinking about whether she was alive.

She was thinking about where she wanted to go.

She was no longer thinking about whether her body was an abomination.

She was thinking about the wind.

She was no longer thinking about the laboratory.

She was thinking about the forest below her.

For several minutes, the thought simply wasn't there.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie noticed the change before anyone else.

During one flight, she asked:

> **"What are you thinking about?"**

Corin smiled.

> **"Nothing."**

Orphie looked confused.

> **"Nothing?"**

Corin looked toward the horizon.

> **"It's nice."**

She did not elaborate.

She didn't need to.

---

# **THE OLD BELIEF**

The thought did not disappear because Corin suddenly decided that her body was normal.

It wasn't.

She still isn't biologically normal.

She still requires stitches.

She still lives through Ether.

She still exists somewhere between conventional life and death.

The difference is that she no longer sees those facts as the entirety of her identity.

The thought:

> **"I'm an abomination from a lab."**

has slowly become:

> **"Something terrible happened to me in a lab."**

The distinction is enormous.

The first defines **what she is.**

The second describes **what happened to her.**

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light noticed the change during a conversation.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Do you still think you're a doll?"

Corin thought about it.

> **"...Sometimes."**

> **"Do you still think you're an abomination?"**

A longer pause.

> **"...Not as much."**

Guiding Light smiled.

> **"Why?"**

Corin looked at her wings.

> **"Because these weren't made by anyone."**

She folded them neatly against her back.

> **"They're mine."**

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT**

Curious Light considers the psychological significance remarkable.

His observation reads:

> **"The wings did not repair Corin's body."**

> **"They changed her relationship with it."**

He believes this distinction explains why the improvement has persisted.

Corin's scars remain.

Her anomalous physiology remains.

Her memories remain.

Nothing was erased.

But her interpretation of those things changed.

---

# **A NEW IDENTITY**

Corin has begun describing herself differently.

She still occasionally says:

> **"I'm a thing."**

But increasingly, she also says:

> **"I'm Corin."**

That statement is simple.

It is also significant.

She no longer needs to immediately explain what Corin is.

She can simply be Corin.

---

# **CURRENT PSYCHOLOGICAL STATE**

The previously dominant fixation has been significantly reduced.

### Previously:

> **"I'm not alive."**

> **"I'm not dead."**

> **"I'm a doll."**

> **"I'm an abomination."**

### Currently:

> **"I'm Corin."**

The old thoughts have not been completely erased.

They may return during periods of distress.

However, they no longer control her identity.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The Architects tried to help Corin.

They gave her a voice.

They gave her color.

They kept her safe.

They stayed beside her.

None of those things could completely break the thought that had possessed her.

Then, without anyone's intervention, Corin grew wings.

They did not make her human.

They did not make her alive.

They did not erase the laboratory.

They did something much simpler.

They gave Corin something that belonged entirely to herself.

She could fly.

She could carry Orphie.

She could visit Lumi.

She could reach the Blacksite whenever she wanted.

She could look at the sky instead of the scars.

And for the first time in a very long time, Corin stopped asking:

> **"What am I?"**

She started asking:

> **"Where should I go?"**

The thought that had haunted her for so long did not need to be fought.

It simply became quieter.

And eventually—

**Corin flew past it.**

**End of Psychological Recovery / Anomalous Development Report**
