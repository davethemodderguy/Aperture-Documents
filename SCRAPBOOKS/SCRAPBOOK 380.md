# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL ANOMALOUS ABILITY UPDATE**

# **COREY — SOLARIA / LUNA DISGUISE CAPABILITIES**

**Classification:** Level 4 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corey / Solaria / Luna  
**Status:** Confirmed

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has confirmed that both of Corey's manifested forms possess advanced **light-manipulation capabilities** that can be used to disguise their appearance.

Additionally, **Luna** possesses the ability to mimic voices.

These abilities appear to be extensions of the solar and lunar magic associated with Corey's manifestations.

---

# **LIGHT-BASED DISGUISE**

Both **Solaria** and **Luna** can manipulate surrounding light to alter how they appear.

The ability can be used to create convincing visual disguises, including:

- Altering apparent clothing.
    
- Changing visible colors.
    
- Concealing identifying features.
    
- Mimicking another person's general appearance.
    
- Creating visual distortions.
    
- Making themselves difficult to recognize.
    

The effect does not physically change Corey's body.

Instead, light surrounding her is manipulated to produce the desired appearance.

---

# **OPEN-SKY LIMITATION**

The disguise ability has one major limitation:

> **It only functions properly under an open sky.**

The forms require direct access to natural celestial light.

The ability becomes ineffective or severely limited when Corey is:

- Inside buildings.
    
- Underground.
    
- Inside enclosed rooms.
    
- Beneath solid ceilings.
    
- In areas without access to the open sky.
    

Windows alone are generally insufficient.

A room with a large glass roof may qualify, provided the sky is sufficiently unobstructed.

---

# **SOLARIA**

Solaria uses **solar light** for her disguises.

Her disguises tend to appear:

- Brighter.
    
- Warmer.
    
- More vivid.
    
- Sharply defined.
    

She can manipulate sunlight around herself to create an alternate visual appearance.

The resulting image remains an optical effect rather than a physical transformation.

---

# **LUNA**

Luna uses **moonlight** in the same general manner.

Her disguises are considerably softer.

Common characteristics include:

- Azure illumination.
    
- Soft shadows.
    
- Reduced visual contrast.
    
- Subtle distortions.
    
- A slightly dreamlike appearance.
    

Under a clear night sky, Luna can make herself surprisingly difficult to identify.

---

# **VOICE MIMICRY — LUNA**

Luna possesses an additional ability not currently confirmed in Solaria:

> **Voice mimicry.**

Luna can reproduce the voice of another individual with remarkable accuracy.

She can imitate:

- Pitch.
    
- Tone.
    
- Speaking rhythm.
    
- Accent.
    
- Emotional delivery.
    
- Speech patterns.
    

The ability appears to work through magical manipulation rather than conventional vocal anatomy.

---

# **COMBINED DISGUISE**

Luna can combine her voice mimicry with light manipulation.

This allows her to imitate both the **appearance and voice** of another person.

For example:

> Visual appearance: Individual A  
> Voice: Individual A  
> Environment: Open night sky

The resulting disguise can be extremely convincing.

However, the disguise does not reproduce the target's memories, abilities, mannerisms, or knowledge.

---

# **SOLARIA'S LIMITATION**

Solaria has not demonstrated reliable voice mimicry.

She can manipulate light extremely effectively, but attempts to reproduce another person's voice have produced only ordinary variations of her own voice.

Researchers currently consider voice mimicry a **Luna-specific ability**.

---

# **WHY THE SKY MATTERS**

C.O.R.E. believes the open-sky requirement is directly connected to the source of the two manifestations.

### **Solaria**

Draws upon the **Sun**.

### **Luna**

Draws upon the **Moon**.

Their light manipulation therefore appears to require a sufficiently direct connection to their respective celestial source.

This also explains why their disguises are significantly weaker indoors.

---

# **FIELD TEST**

A controlled test was conducted outdoors.

Luna stood beneath a clear night sky.

Her appearance gradually changed.

Her azure glow shifted.

Her silhouette became indistinct.

Then she spoke.

Using the voice of the observing researcher, she said:

> "...Is this convincing?"

The researcher immediately stepped backward.

Luna returned to normal.

> "...It worked."

A nearby Mart looked at her.

> "...Luna funny."

Luna smiled.

> "...Thank you."

---

# **COREY'S RESPONSE**

Corey was asked whether she considered the ability useful.

She answered:

> "...Definitely."

She was then asked whether she intended to use it for practical purposes.

Corey paused.

> "...Maybe."

A Mart nearby immediately responded:

> "...Prank."

Corey looked at it.

> "...I wasn't going to say that."

The Mart appeared satisfied.
