# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / RECOVERY INCIDENT**

# **THE GIRL AT THE MOUNTAIN PEAK**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **FOUND / ALIVE**  
**Location:** Mountain Peak — Approximately **5 km above ground level**  
**Condition:** **SEVERE PHYSICAL AND EMOTIONAL DISTRESS**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

After one year of searching, Corin has been found.

She was located at an isolated mountain peak approximately **5 kilometers above the surrounding ground level**.

There was no shelter.

No active fire.

No food.

No water.

No equipment.

Only Corin.

She was alive.

Barely moving.

Her wings were wrapped around her body in an attempt to protect herself from the cold.

She was trembling.

And crying.

---

# **THE DISCOVERY**

The search team initially detected an anomalous biological signature near the summit.

They expected debris.

Instead, they found Corin.

She was sitting against the mountain surface with her knees drawn close to herself.

Her wings completely covered her body.

Snow had accumulated across her feathers.

Her breathing was shallow.

She did not initially react to the search team's presence.

She simply continued sobbing.

---

# **THE COLD**

Corin had apparently remained exposed to the mountain's extreme conditions for an extended period.

Her wings had become her only meaningful source of protection.

She held them tightly around herself.

Despite this, she continued to shiver uncontrollably.

When a rescue worker approached, Corin weakly moved one wing farther around herself.

Not defensively.

Instinctively.

---

# **THE DRAWING**

The most significant discovery was found directly beside her.

A sketchbook.

Open to a single page.

The drawing depicted **Orphie**.

It was unlike Corin's earlier drawings.

Her previous sketches had been crude.

Uneven.

Poorly proportioned.

This one was different.

It was still imperfect.

But it was unmistakably beautiful.

Orphie was drawn beneath the Treehouse tree.

Her expression was soft.

The surrounding landscape was filled with flowers.

The lighting was carefully rendered.

Corin had spent an extraordinary amount of effort on it.

---

# **THE MOST BEAUTIFUL DRAWING**

The page contains more detail than any other surviving drawing.

Every part appears deliberate.

The hair.

The eyes.

The clothing.

The tree.

The flowers.

The sky.

Even the small shadows beneath the leaves.

Corin had finally learned how to draw.

Or perhaps she simply had something important enough to make her try.

---

# **THE WRITING**

Beneath the drawing is a single sentence.

The handwriting is extremely faint.

> **"I wanted to show Orphie this."**

Nothing else.

---

# **THE RESCUE**

When Orphie arrived at the summit, Corin initially did not look at her.

She continued crying.

Orphie approached slowly.

She knelt several feet away.

For several seconds, neither spoke.

Then Orphie said:

> **"Corin."**

Corin's wings tightened.

Her crying became quieter.

Orphie continued:

> **"I'm here."**

Corin finally looked up.

---

# **THE RECOGNITION**

Corin stared at Orphie for several seconds.

Her eyes were wet.

Her expression was exhausted.

She appeared almost unable to understand what she was seeing.

Then she whispered:

> **"...You came back."**

Orphie began crying.

> **"I was trying to."**

---

# **CORIN**

Corin attempted to stand.

She could not.

Her legs gave out almost immediately.

Orphie caught her.

Corin's wings remained wrapped around herself for several seconds.

Then one slowly opened.

And wrapped around Orphie instead.

---

# **NO EXPLANATIONS**

Nobody asked Corin where she had been.

Nobody asked why she left.

Nobody asked why she waited on the mountain.

Nobody asked why she never returned.

Not then.

There would be time for questions later.

For now, there was only one priority.

Getting her warm.

Getting her hydrated.

Getting her home.

---

# **THE SKETCHBOOK**

The drawing of Orphie was carefully removed from the immediate environment and placed inside a protective sleeve.

It is currently being preserved.

Orphie has requested that the original eventually be returned to Corin.

Her request has been approved.

---

# **CURRENT MEDICAL STATUS**

Corin is alive.

Her body is severely weakened.

Hypothermia and prolonged deprivation are being treated.

Her regenerative factor remains active.

Her wings show signs of prolonged environmental exposure but remain functional.

Her anomalous abilities are currently significantly suppressed.

Her feather luminosity remains absent.

No burning feathers have been observed.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

For one year, everyone wondered where Corin had gone.

They searched forests.

Caves.

Blacksite sectors.

Dimensional spaces.

Mountains.

Everywhere.

She was found at the top of one.

Five kilometers above the ground.

Alone.

Cold.

Trembling.

Still waiting.

She had nothing left except her wings to keep herself warm.

And a sketchbook.

Open to the most beautiful drawing she had ever made.

Orphie.

When Orphie finally reached her, Corin did not ask why it took so long.

She did not ask where she had been.

She did not blame her.

She simply looked at her through tear-filled eyes and whispered:

> **"...You came back."**

And for the first time in a year...

Corin stopped waiting.

**End of Corin / Recovery Incident**
