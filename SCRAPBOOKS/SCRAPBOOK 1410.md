# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following continued observation of the six Obol Squad members, C.O.R.E. has documented their individual food preferences.

Despite sharing the same artificial-human origin and broadly similar physiology, their preferences are notably different.

The current records are:

|Resident|Preferred Food / Drink|
|---|---|
|**Anby**|Cheeseburgers|
|**Solider 11**|Potato salad and roasted chicken|
|**Twiggy**|Anything edible|
|**Phaethon**|Foods containing apple or pineapple|
|**Iris**|Nitro Fuel and fries|
|**Sora**|Anything eaten while stargazing|

No significant dietary restrictions have been identified that would prevent normal consumption of these foods, subject to ordinary medical considerations.

# 1. ANBY — CHEESEBURGERS

Anby's favorite food is:

> **Cheeseburgers.**

The preference appears straightforward.

She likes the combination of:

- bread;
    
- meat;
    
- cheese;
    
- vegetables;

She simply likes cheeseburgers.

# 5. IRIS — NITRO FUEL & FRIES

Iris has perhaps the most unusual documented preference.

Her favorites are:

> **Nitro Fuel**

and

> **Fries.**

Nitro Fuel is a beverage with a significantly softer alcohol profile than conventional beer.

Its flavor profile leans:

- sweet;
    
- soft;
    
- easy to drink.
    

It retains enough alcohol content to produce mild intoxication when consumed in sufficient quantities.

Accordingly, it is treated as an alcoholic beverage.

Iris appears to enjoy the combination of Nitro Fuel and fries.

---

## Observed Interaction

Iris was sitting in the cafeteria with a basket of fries.

She took a sip of Nitro Fuel.

Solider 11 looked at her.

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> “That's your dinner?”

Iris looked at the fries.

Then the drink.

> **IRIS:**  
> “Yes.”

> **SOLIDER 11:**  
> “That's not dinner.”

Iris picked up another fry.

> **IRIS:**  
> “It is now.”

Solider 11 sighed.

---

# 6. NITRO FUEL SAFETY NOTE

Although Nitro Fuel is considerably softer than conventional beer, it remains alcoholic.

Personnel are reminded that:

- it is not alcohol-free;
    
- mild intoxication remains possible;
    
- excessive consumption is prohibited;
    
- and ordinary SAFEHOUSE alcohol policies apply.
    

Iris is therefore not permitted unlimited access simply because she calls it:

> **“The soft beer.”**

Her terminology has no legal significance.

# 7. SORA — FOOD & STARGAZING

Sora's preference is less about a particular food.

Her favorite activity is:

> **Eating while stargazing.**

The actual meal appears secondary.

Sora can enjoy almost anything if she can sit somewhere quiet and look at the stars while eating.

This has become a recurring nighttime activity.

She often chooses a balcony, rooftop-accessible area, or SAFEHOUSE observation point.

---

# 8. SORA'S ROUTINE

A typical Sora evening consists of:

1. obtaining food;
    
2. finding a suitable place with a clear view of the sky;
    
3. sitting down;
    
4. eating;
    
5. looking at the stars;
    
6. continuing to eat.
    

Conversation is optional.

Company is optional.

The stars are not.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The dietary preferences of Obol Squad demonstrate another aspect of their individuality.

Despite their shared origin, they have developed distinct preferences.

**Anby** wants cheeseburgers.

**Solider 11** wants potato salad and roasted chicken.

**Twiggy** wants food.

**Phaethon** wants apple or pineapple.

**Iris** wants Nitro Fuel and fries.

**Sora** wants dinner beneath the stars.

None of these preferences are particularly unusual.

The important part is that they are **their own preferences.**

Project Silverline determined much of their lives for them.

Their food choices do not need to be optimized.

They do not need to be identical.

They do not need to serve a purpose.

Sometimes a person simply likes cheeseburgers.

Sometimes a person likes roasted chicken.

Sometimes a person will eat literally anything.

Sometimes a person wants pineapple.

Sometimes a person wants fries and a sweet alcoholic drink.

And sometimes a person just wants to eat dinner while looking at the stars.

That is enough.
