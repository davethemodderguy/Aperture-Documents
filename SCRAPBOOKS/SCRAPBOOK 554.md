### **SAFEHOUSE DOMESTIC ACTIVITY REPORT**

# **CORIN — DINNER SERVICE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Activity:** **Communal Dinner Preparation**  
**Status:** Successful

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin prepared dinner for the SAFEHOUSE residents.

The meal was entirely ordinary.

The preparation method was not.

Rather than using a conventional kitchen knife, Corin simply detached one of her feathers and used it as a cutting tool.

The feather's naturally sharpened edge proved more than sufficient.

---

# **PREPARATION**

Corin spent the afternoon preparing dinner in the communal kitchen.

She gathered the ingredients, washed the vegetables, prepared the meat, and began cutting everything into appropriate portions.

When she reached for a kitchen knife, she stopped.

She looked at one of the feathers along the edge of her wing.

Then she looked at the knife.

Then back at the feather.

She shrugged.

> **"This works."**

She detached the feather.

---

# **THE FEATHER KNIFE**

The feather selected was one of the naturally sharpened outer feathers.

Its edge is sufficiently sharp to function as a cutting blade.

Corin held it carefully between her fingers and began preparing the ingredients.

The feather remained rigid throughout the process.

It did not bend or lose its edge.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE REACTION**

Neko walked into the kitchen halfway through preparation.

He stopped.

Corin was calmly slicing vegetables with one of her own feathers.

> **Neko:**  
> "...Why?"

Corin looked at him.

> **"It's a knife."**

Neko stared at the feather.

Then at Corin.

> **"...You have actual knives."**

Corin glanced at the knife block.

> **"I know."**

She continued cutting.

Neko eventually gave up.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle was fascinated.

She watched Corin use the feather to slice vegetables.

> **Belle:**  
> "Can I try?"

Corin immediately shook her head.

> **"No."**

> **"Why?"**

Corin held up the feather.

> **"Because it's sharp."**

Belle nodded.

> **"Oh."**

She continued watching anyway.

---

# **FOOD PREPARATION**

Despite the unusual tool, the cooking itself was completely normal.

Corin prepared enough food for everyone in the SAFEHOUSE.

The kitchen gradually filled with the smell of roasting meat, vegetables, and soup.

Her wings remained partially folded while she worked.

The rest of her feathers remained safely out of the way.

---

# **THE FEATHER AFTERWARD**

Once preparation was complete, Corin placed the feather beside the cutting board.

It had fulfilled its temporary purpose.

The feather would eventually become dormant and could be left to naturally decompose or be discarded.

Corin simply regarded it for a moment.

> **"...Good knife."**

She then returned to cooking.

---

# **DINNER**

By evening, the residents gathered around the central living area's dining space.

Everyone received a serving.

The meal was well received.

Belle asked for seconds.

Bao complimented the soup.

Star particularly liked the roasted meat.

Neko remained more interested in the fact that Corin had prepared the entire meal using a feather.

---

# **NEKO'S FINAL COMMENT**

> **"You know we own knives, right?"**

Corin took a sip of tea.

> **"Yes."**

> **"And you still chose a feather."**

Corin nodded.

> **"It's convenient."**

Neko sighed.

> **"...Fair."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin has access to a fully equipped communal kitchen.

She has conventional knives.

She knows how to use them.

She simply decided that one of her naturally sharpened wing feathers would work just as well.

And it did.

The SAFEHOUSE dinner was prepared without incident.

The food was delicious.

Nobody was injured.

And one of Corin's feathers briefly became the strangest kitchen utensil in the building.

**End of SAFEHOUSE Domestic Activity Report**
