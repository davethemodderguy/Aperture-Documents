# 1. CONFIRMATION

Previous reports treated Neri's presence within [REDACTED] as one possibility among several.

That is no longer the case.

**Neri is inside the affected zone.**

C.O.R.E. has obtained sufficient anomalous evidence to establish her presence with high confidence.

The exact location of Neri within the zone remains unknown.

Attempts to approach her have consistently produced the previously documented neurological and cognitive effects:

> **GO AWAY**

> **YOU DON'T NEED ME**

> **STAY AWAY**

> **DON'T GO NEAR ME**

> **I'M A MONSTER**

> **ABANDON ME**

Neri has not directly appeared to investigators.

No conventional communication has been established.

---

# 2. ARCHITECT INVOLVEMENT

The most significant development occurred when three Architects independently attempted to enter the affected area.

### ARCHITECTS INVOLVED

- **Guiding Light**
    
- **Curious Light**
    
- **Mischievous Light**
    

All three were affected.

This finding immediately invalidated an earlier assumption that the anomaly could be safely approached by entities possessing superior Light authority.

It cannot.

---

# 3. GUIDING LIGHT

Guiding Light was the first Architect to approach.

Given her direct connection to the Light system from which Neri's anomalous abilities originated, she was considered the most suitable candidate for determining whether the phenomenon could be bypassed.

She could not.

At the outer boundary of the zone, Guiding Light reported an immediate neurological sensation.

She described it simply as:

> "Pain."

When asked to elaborate:

> "It feels like something is trying to cut through my head."

Blue text subsequently appeared within her perception.

The first message she reported was:

> **GO AWAY**

Guiding Light attempted to continue.

The pain intensified.

Additional messages appeared.

> **YOU DON'T NEED ME**

> **STAY AWAY**

Guiding Light stopped.

---

# 4. CURIOUS LIGHT

Curious Light subsequently attempted entry.

Unlike Guiding Light, Curious Light initially treated the phenomenon as an interesting interaction between Neri's borrowed Light capabilities and the surrounding environment.

This assessment lasted approximately several seconds.

Curious Light experienced the same effect.

He later described the experience as:

> "Okay."

Pause.

> "That is considerably less fun than I expected."

He reported the same blue textual manifestations.

He was unable to determine whether the messages originated from Neri herself or from an independent defensive mechanism.

When asked whether he could overpower the phenomenon:

> "Probably."

When asked whether he should:

> "No."

---

# 5. MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT

Mischievous Light's response was initially different.

He attempted to enter the zone without announcing his approach.

The attempt failed.

The electronic equipment accompanying the support team simultaneously malfunctioned.

Mischievous Light himself then stopped.

Witnesses reported that he became unusually quiet.

When asked what he saw:

> "She doesn't want us here."

He was asked whether he meant Neri.

After a prolonged pause:

> "Yes."

He did not continue.

---

# 6. ARCHITECT IMMUNITY — REJECTED

Prior C.O.R.E. assumptions operated on the principle that Neri's abilities originated from an Architect fragment and therefore an Architect should possess some degree of immunity or authority over them.

This assumption is incorrect.

The zone affects Architects.

It does not appear to distinguish between:

- ordinary personnel;
    
- anomalous residents;
    
- SAFEHOUSE residents;
    
- highly resistant entities;
    
- or Architects.
    

### CURRENT MODEL

The phenomenon does not appear to be asking:

> **"Are you powerful enough?"**

It appears to be asking:

> **"Are you approaching?"**

If the answer is yes, the response occurs.

---

# 7. SIGNIFICANCE OF THE LIGHT FRAGMENT

Neri's molecular restructuring and visual/memory suppression abilities originated after she consumed a fragment contained within one of Guiding Light's cupcakes.

That fragment provided Neri with access to a portion of Architect-derived Light functionality.

C.O.R.E. originally assumed Neri possessed only a limited subset of those capabilities.

Recent events indicate otherwise.

Neri has independently developed applications involving:

- visual information;
    
- memory;
    
- identity association;
    
- and now potentially **spatially localized defensive effects.**
    

She has apparently done so without formal Architect instruction.

Guiding Light's previous concern is now considered justified:

> **"The power isn't the problem. She's ten. She figured out how to use part of an Architect's power without anyone teaching her."**

The current zone represents the first known case in which Neri's borrowed abilities have apparently been used to establish a large-scale area effect.

---

# 8. ELECTRONIC DISRUPTION

The electronic interference is not limited to conventional SAFEHOUSE equipment.

Architect-associated systems are also affected.

Devices brought into the zone become unreliable or completely unusable.

This includes equipment normally capable of operating independently of conventional infrastructure.

Attempts to maintain an electronic link with the affected Architects have failed.

This creates a unique situation:

> **The zone can interfere with both conventional technology and systems associated with Architect activity.**

Whether this represents electromagnetic disruption, information-level interference, or another mechanism remains unknown.

C.O.R.E. Engineering currently refuses to classify it as a conventional EMP.

---

# 9. WHY THIS MATTERS

The presence of Neri has now been confirmed.

Therefore, the messages have acquired a significantly different meaning.

They are no longer merely unexplained statements originating somewhere within an anomalous zone.

They are occurring **around the location where Neri is hiding.**

The messages say:

> **GO AWAY**

> **YOU DON'T NEED ME**

> **STAY AWAY**

> **DON'T GO NEAR ME**

> **I'M A MONSTER**

> **ABANDON ME**

The simplest interpretation is that Neri does not want anyone approaching her.

However, the exact mechanism remains unknown.

It is unclear whether:

1. Neri is consciously generating the messages;
    
2. Neri is unconsciously generating them;
    
3. the zone is automatically expressing her emotional state;
    
4. the messages are a defensive mechanism;
    
5. or several of these mechanisms are occurring simultaneously.
    

---

# 10. ARCHITECT PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT

The Architects are not being treated as ordinary investigators in this case.

They possess an unusual relationship with Neri's abilities.

Most importantly:

**Guiding Light knows where the power came from.**

Neri did not steal the fragment.

It was accidentally transferred to her through the cupcake.

Guiding Light therefore carries a degree of personal responsibility for what Neri can now do.

During the post-exposure discussion, Guiding Light reportedly said:

> "I gave her the fragment."

Another Architect responded:

> "She was already hurting."

Guiding Light:

> "I know."

No further discussion was recorded.

# 12. CURRENT WORKING THEORY

C.O.R.E.'s current model is that Neri has established a large defensive field around herself.

The field appears to combine several previously observed properties of her Light-derived ability:

**IDENTITY SUPPRESSION**  
↓  
People forget Neri.

**VISUAL SUPPRESSION**  
↓  
Neri's face disappears from recordings.

**ELECTRONIC DISRUPTION**  
↓  
Observation equipment becomes unusable.

**NEUROLOGICAL DETERRENCE**  
↓  
Approaching personnel experience extreme pain.

**COGNITIVE COMMUNICATION**  
↓  
Subjects receive blue messages instructing them to leave.

Together, these mechanisms produce something much more effective than a physical barricade.

Neri has effectively made herself:

> **difficult to remember, difficult to see, difficult to track, and painful to approach.**

# 13. THE MOST CONCERNING POSSIBILITY

Neri may not have built the zone to keep people out.

She may have built it because she believes **people should leave her behind.**

That distinction is critical.

The messages do not say:

> **"I will hurt you."**

They do not say:

> **"You are not allowed here."**

They say:

> **"You don't need me."**

> **"I'm a monster."**

> **"Abandon me."**

These are not conventional territorial warnings.

They sound like self-rejection.
