# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

MSAC personnel have now been formally informed of a previously undocumented fact:

> **Neri can turn herself into a plushie.**

This information was not intentionally classified.

It was simply never mentioned.

Consequently, the majority of MSAC personnel had absolutely no idea.

The first several encounters produced significant confusion.

---

# 2. INITIAL SIGHTING

An MSAC patrol was moving through Sector 4-C when one operator noticed Alice walking toward them.

Alice was carrying something.

Something small.

Something soft.

Something with:

- long white hair;
    
- round glasses;
    
- an oversized hoodie;
    
- a seal tail;
    
- tiny limbs;
    
- and an expression of complete indifference.
    

The operator stopped.

> **MSAC Operator:**  
> “...Alice.”

Alice stopped.

> **Alice:**  
> “Hi!”

The operator pointed at the object in her arms.

> **MSAC Operator:**  
> “What is that?”

Alice looked down.

Neri looked back.

Neri blinked.

> **Alice:**  
> “Neri!”

The operator stared.

> **MSAC Operator:**  
> “...That's Neri?”

Alice nodded enthusiastically.

> **Alice:**  
> “She's a plushie!”

The operator looked at Neri again.

Neri gave a small wave.

> **Neri:**  
> “...Hi.”

The operator immediately stepped backward.

> **MSAC Operator:**  
> “THE PLUSHIE TALKS.”

---

# 3. CONFUSION ESCALATION

Three additional MSAC personnel were summoned.

None were aware of Neri's transformation capability.

The resulting conversation was recorded.

> **Operator 1:**  
> “Wait. That's actually Neri?”

> **Alice:**  
> “Yep!”

> **Operator 2:**  
> “She can become a plush?”

> **Alice:**  
> “Yep!”

> **Operator 3:**  
> “Since when?”

Alice shrugged.

> **Alice:**  
> “Always?”

Neri quietly shook her head.

> **Neri:**  
> “No.”

All three operators looked at her.

> **Operator 1:**  
> “How long can you stay like that?”

Neri held up her hands.

Then shrugged.

> **Neri:**  
> “As long as I want.”

Silence.

> **Operator 2:**  
> “...What?”

Neri adjusted her glasses.

---

# 4. PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

Plush Neri is not a separate entity.

She is Neri.

Her transformed appearance consists of a miniature, chibi version of her normal humanoid form.

Observed characteristics include:

- long white hair;
    
- oversized hoodie;
    
- round glasses;
    
- seal tail;
    
- fabric-like skin;
    
- soft plush construction;
    
- significantly reduced body proportions;
    
- oversized head relative to body;
    
- sleeves extending over or partially covering her hands.
    

Her transformation is voluntary.

She can return to normal form voluntarily.

She retains:

- cognition;
    
- personality;
    
- memory;
    
- speech;
    
- autonomy;
    
- mobility;
    
- and the ability to make decisions.
    

---

# 5. “SOFT TOY” MISUNDERSTANDING

The most common initial MSAC assumption was that Alice had somehow acquired a stuffed toy resembling Neri.

This was disproven when Neri climbed out of Alice's arms.

She sat on the floor.

Adjusted her glasses.

Looked at the assembled operators.

And said:

> **Neri:**  
> “You're loud.”

She then walked away.

The operators remained silent.

---

# 6. ALICE'S CARRYING HABIT

Alice's behavior was subsequently identified as normal.

Alice frequently carries Plush Neri around like a soft toy.

She may:

- hold Neri in her arms;
    
- carry her against her chest;
    
- place her on a couch;
    
- put her on her shoulders;
    
- carry her on her back;
    
- let Neri cling to her clothing;
    
- or simply walk around while Neri is attached to her.
    

Neri voluntarily permits this.

She can leave whenever she wants.

She does not currently object to being carried by Alice.

---

# 9. HEADPAT INCIDENT

One MSAC operator eventually made the mistake of asking:

> **MSAC Operator:**  
> “Can I...?”

He gestured toward Neri's head.

Neri stared at his hand.

Then at him.

Then lowered her head slightly.

The operator gave her one gentle headpat.

Neri remained still.

Her eyes slowly closed.

> **Neri:**  
> “...Again.”

The operator froze.

> **MSAC Operator:**  
> “Again?”

Neri nodded.

A second headpat was provided.

No further incident occurred.

The operator later described the experience as:

> **“Unexpectedly wholesome.”**

---

The operator froze.

> **MSAC Operator:**  
> “Did she just bite me?”

Alice nodded.

> **Alice:**  
> “Yep.”

> **Neri:**  
> “Don't.”

> **MSAC Operator:**

The operator did not attempt to touch her again.

# 11. IMPORTANT SAFETY CLARIFICATION

Plush Neri is **not a toy.**

Although she resembles one and voluntarily permits toy-like treatment in certain circumstances, she remains a resident of the SAFEHOUSE.

Personnel are therefore prohibited from:

- grabbing her without consent;
    
- throwing her;
    
- kicking her;
    
- shaking her;
    
- tossing her around;
    
- intentionally dropping her;
    
- treating her as disposable;
    
- attempting to test her durability;
    
- intentionally provoking her bite;
    
- or assuming that plush construction makes her indestructible.
    

Her transformed body is substantially more resistant to ordinary impacts.

It is not invulnerable.

> **SOFT DOES NOT MEAN UNBREAKABLE.**

# 13. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

There is no indication that Neri's transformation represents cognitive regression, loss of functionality, or anomalous distress.

She simply likes being comfortable.

Plush form provides:

- comfort;
    
- reduced physical strain;
    
- increased impact resistance;
    
- freedom from ordinary anemia while transformed;
    
- and the ability to spend extended periods doing absolutely nothing.
    

Neri appears particularly content when being carried.

She does not need to explain why.

# ARCHIVIST NOTE

MSAC has spent years learning how to identify anomalies.

They have procedures for:

- hostile entities;
    
- dimensional breaches;
    
- anomalous weapons;
    
- reality distortions;
    
- hostile Thirens;
    
- and supernatural phenomena.
    

Apparently nobody prepared them for:

**tiny Neri.**

The first MSAC operator saw Alice carrying her and assumed she was carrying a stuffed animal.

Then the stuffed animal waved.

Then it spoke.

Then it asked for coffee.

Then it bit somebody.

And somehow the strangest part was still discovering that this tiny plush creature was the same ten-year-old chemist whose research had previously consumed twelve terabytes of storage.

Neko has reportedly requested that personnel stop calling her:

> **“The world's smallest researcher.”**

Neri has reportedly requested that personnel stop calling her:

> **“Cute.”**

Both requests remain difficult to enforce.
