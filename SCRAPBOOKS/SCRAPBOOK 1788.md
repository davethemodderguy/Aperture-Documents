# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has recently resumed using his wings while in his human-hybrid form.

The wings themselves are not new. Their restoration was previously documented following Jake's return from the sky.

What has changed is how frequently he deploys them during ordinary activities.

Jake now occasionally stretches them after waking, wraps them around himself when cold, uses them to stabilize his balance, and partially spreads them when startled.

Combined with his feline characteristics, the result has produced an unexpected complication.

Phoenix can no longer be trusted to behave normally around him.

This assessment is not a medical finding.

It is an administrative conclusion supported by repeated observations, multiple witness statements, and an increasingly concerning collection of Phoenix's own written notes.

Jake, meanwhile, appears unaware that he is causing any difficulty whatsoever.

He has ears.

He has a tail.

He has retractable claws.

He has fangs.

He has luminous azure eyes with shooting-star patterns.

He has wings.

And he has apparently decided that all of these things are perfectly ordinary.

Phoenix disagrees.

Very loudly.

---

# 1. WING DEPLOYMENT

Jake's wings remain fully functional in his human-hybrid form.

Their deployment does not require him to transform completely into a star or abandon his humanoid appearance.

He can simply unfold them.

The transition is generally unremarkable from Jake's perspective.

He stretches.

His shoulders shift.

His wings emerge and open.

His tail moves to compensate for the change in balance.

His ears swivel toward any nearby sound.

Then he continues whatever he was doing.

The resulting appearance is considerably less unremarkable to observers.

Jake's white hair contrasts with his luminous azure eyes. His feline ears rise above his head, and his tail moves behind him. When his wings spread, the additional silhouette makes him look simultaneously celestial and unmistakably catlike.

The combination is particularly noticeable when he stretches.

Unfortunately, Phoenix witnessed this.

# 2. INCIDENT A — THE MORNING STRETCH

**TIME:** 07:14  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE COMMON ROOM  
**WITNESS:** Phoenix

Phoenix entered the common room to find Jake asleep on the couch.

His teddy bear was tucked beneath one arm.

His tail curled around his left leg.

His ears drooped sideways.

His wings were folded loosely behind him.

He looked comfortable.

Phoenix approached quietly.

She had intended to check whether he wanted breakfast.

Instead, she stopped several feet away and watched him sleep.

Jake eventually stirred.

One ear twitched.

Then the other.

His eyes opened halfway, revealing azure irises crossed by faint, moving shooting-star streaks.

He blinked.

Looked at Phoenix.

**JAKE:**  
"...Morning."

**PHOENIX:**  
"Good morning, sweetheart."

Jake yawned.

His small fangs became visible.

Phoenix pressed both hands against her mouth.

Jake noticed.

**JAKE:**  
"What?"

**PHOENIX:**  
"Nothing!"

Jake sat up.

His ears lifted.

His tail uncurled from his leg.

Then he stretched.

His wings unfolded behind him, spreading to their full comfortable span before he drew them back.

The movement was slow and instinctive.

His feathers shifted softly.

His feline ears tilted outward.

His tail lifted in a lazy curve.

He finished stretching and rubbed one eye with the back of his hand.

**JAKE:**  
"...I'm hungry."

Phoenix stared at him.

Jake stared back.

**JAKE:**  
"Phoenix?"

She made a sound that the recording equipment failed to classify as speech.

**JAKE:**  
"...You okay?"

Phoenix turned around.

Walked directly into the doorframe.

Stopped.

Placed one hand against the frame.

**PHOENIX:**  
"I'm fine."

**JAKE:**  
"You hit your head."

**PHOENIX:**  
"I KNOW."

Jake's ears flattened.

Phoenix immediately lowered her voice.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Sorry, sweetheart. I'm fine."

Jake studied her for another moment.

Then he picked up his teddy bear.

**JAKE:**  
"...Okay."

He walked past her toward the kitchen.

His tail swayed behind him.

Phoenix remained beside the doorframe.

A nearby witness heard her whisper:

**PHOENIX:**  
"He has WINGS."

The witness replied:

**RESIDENT:**  
"We knew that."

**PHOENIX:**  
"HE HAS CAT EARS TOO."

**RESIDENT:**  
"We also knew that."

**PHOENIX:**  
"AND HE STRETCHES."

The witness considered this.

**RESIDENT:**

# 3. INCIDENT B — THE WING WRAP

**TIME:** 15:32  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE LIBRARY

Jake was sitting in a chair reading.

Phoenix sat nearby.

For once, the situation was peaceful.

Nobody was falling down stairs.

Nobody was opening spatial rifts.

Nobody was accidentally kissing anybody.

Jake turned a page.

A draft passed through the room.

He shivered slightly.

Without looking up from his book, he drew his wings forward and wrapped them loosely around his shoulders.

His tail curled against the chair.

One feline ear poked through the feathers.

He continued reading.

Phoenix looked over.

Her expression changed.

She slowly lowered her own book.

Jake turned another page.

Phoenix leaned forward.

**PHOENIX:**  
"...Jake?"

**JAKE:**  
"Hm?"

**PHOENIX:**  
"Are you cold?"

**JAKE:**  
"A little."

**PHOENIX:**  
"Do you want a blanket?"

Jake looked at the wings wrapped around himself.

**JAKE:**  
"...Already have one."

Phoenix closed her eyes.

The wings shifted slightly as Jake adjusted his position.

His ears emerged more clearly.

His tail flicked once.

He returned to reading.

Phoenix opened her eyes.

**PHOENIX:**  
"You're doing it on purpose."

Jake looked up.

**JAKE:**  
"Doing what?"

**PHOENIX:**  
"THAT."

Jake glanced at his wings.

Then at his tail.

Then at Phoenix.

**JAKE:**  
"...Being cold?"

Phoenix put her book over her face.

Jake stared at her for several seconds.

**JAKE:**  
"Clover says you do this a lot."

**CLOVER:**  
"J A K E."

Jake looked toward her.

**JAKE:**

Phoenix was still hiding behind her book.

**CLOVER:**

**JAKE:**  
"I didn't do anything!"

# 4. INCIDENT C — THE CAT TRAITS

Phoenix's reaction is not limited to Jake's wings.

The feline characteristics appear to be an equally significant problem.

Jake occasionally exhibits ordinary catlike behavior without seeming to recognize how unusual it appears to other residents.

Examples include:

- swiveling his ears toward a conversation while pretending not to listen;
    
- curling his tail around his leg when sitting;
    
- rubbing his cheek against a familiar person's shoulder;
    
- stretching his fingers to extend and retract his claws;
    
- making small, contented purring sounds when comfortable;
    
- tucking his hands beneath his chest while resting;
    
- tilting his head when confused;
    
- and briefly exposing his fangs when yawning.
    

Phoenix has witnessed most of these behaviors.

She has not handled any of them particularly well.

# 5. INCIDENT D — THE WING FLUTTER

**TIME:** 18:06  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE CORRIDOR

Jake was walking beside Phoenix when a loud noise came from a nearby room.

He startled.

His ears flattened.

His tail puffed slightly.

His wings opened reflexively to stabilize his balance.

Jake took one step back.

Then looked toward the source of the noise.

Phoenix instinctively moved closer.

**PHOENIX:**  
"Jake? You okay?"

Jake nodded.

His wings gradually folded again.

**JAKE:**

Phoenix checked that he was steady on his feet.

**PHOENIX:**  
"All right. You're safe."

Jake looked up at her.

His ears lifted.

**JAKE:**

Phoenix's expression softened.

She rested a hand lightly against his shoulder, allowing him to move away if he wished.

His wings settled.

# 8. WELFARE CONSIDERATIONS

Although the situation has produced considerable humor among residents, C.O.R.E. emphasizes that Jake's hybrid physiology must be treated respectfully.

His wings are functional body parts.

His tail, ears, claws, and fangs are also functional.

They are not accessories.

Personnel should not:

- pull his tail;
    
- touch his ears without permission;
    
- tug on his feathers;
    
- force his wings open;
    
- demand that he purr;
    
- attempt to provoke feline behavior for entertainment;
    
- or treat his appearance as an invitation to handle him.
    

Phoenix has complied with these restrictions.

Her enthusiasm does not override Jake's autonomy.

Jake is permitted to approach her, lean against her, accept a hug, or ask to be carried when he wants to.

He is equally permitted to decline.

The distinction is important.

**Jake's affection is something he chooses to give, not something residents are entitled to receive.**

Phoenix appears to understand this, even when she struggles to maintain a neutral expression.

# C.O.R.E. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Jake's combination of celestial wings and feline physiology has produced a noticeable increase in Phoenix's visible emotional reactions.

This is not a problem requiring intervention.

Phoenix is enthusiastic, but she respects Jake's boundaries.

Jake appears comfortable around her and frequently chooses her company.

Their interactions are characterized by spontaneous affection, quiet companionship, and Phoenix's persistent inability to behave normally whenever Jake performs an ordinary bodily function.

C.O.R.E. recommends no changes to Jake's daily routine.

He should continue using his wings as needed, expressing his feline behaviors naturally, and choosing his own companions.

Phoenix has been advised to develop coping strategies.

She has requested that the strategies not involve looking away from Jake.

C.O.R.E. declined to endorse this approach.
