# **MERRYWEATHER MANOR**

Located within the Banlands, Merryweather Manor serves as both the ancestral residence of the Merryweather Family and the administrative headquarters for many of Lucy Merryweather's responsibilities as Warden.

Despite its size and security, the manor is notable for its welcoming atmosphere.

Unlike the Blacksite, there are no reinforced blast doors separating the dining hall from the living room.

No armed checkpoints between the library and the garden.

No warning sirens.

Only polished wooden floors, warm fireplaces, extensive gardens, and walls lined with generations of Merryweather family portraits.

Several visitors quietly remarked that the manor felt less like an official residence and more like a place people genuinely lived.

---

# **HEAD OF SECURITY**

Security for the manor is overseen by:

## **Alice**

**Species:** Rabbit Thiren

**Position:**

- Head of Security, Merryweather Manor
    
- Personal Secretary to Lucy Merryweather
    

Long before assuming her current responsibilities, Alice served as Belle Merryweather's primary caretaker after Belle was adopted into the Merryweather family.

She was responsible for much of Belle's daily routine during those early years, including meals, education, and ensuring Belle adjusted to life within the manor.

Although Belle is now fully independent, Alice continues to watch over her with quiet affection.

Belle still occasionally refers to Alice as:

> "...Auntie Alice."

Alice has never objected.

---

# **FIRST ARRIVALS**

Alice personally greeted every visitor at the entrance.

Each guest received the same warm welcome.

Professional.

Polite.

Organized.

When Belle arrived, however...

Alice smiled.

Before Belle could even say hello, Alice gently straightened Belle's collar.

> "You're still forgetting this."

Belle laughed.

> "...Some things never change."

Lucy guided the guests throughout the estate.

- The Banlands strategy room.

Director Lucious spent considerably longer than expected examining the manor's historical archives.

Neko attempted to estimate the age of the building.

Miss Neko quietly reminded him not to dismantle anything "for research."

---

# **THE DINING HALL**

The reunion concluded with a formal family dinner.

Unlike official Blacksite meals, the evening proceeded without briefings, incident reports, or emergency announcements.

Conversations ranged from recent operations to embarrassing childhood stories involving Belle and Endmin.

Keith and Cinder found themselves listening more than speaking.

Star was fascinated by the portraits covering the walls.

Taiga became distracted by the snowfall outside the large windows.

Mart disappeared into the library long enough that someone had to remind him dinner had already begun.

---

# **LUCY**

Toward the end of the evening, Lucy quietly looked around the dining hall.

The room was filled with conversation.

Laughter.

Friends.

Family.

People from organizations that would ordinarily meet only across conference tables.

Tonight...

They simply shared a meal.

Lucy smiled.

Then softly said to Alice,

> "The house feels alive again."

Alice glanced around the room before answering.

> "...It always was."

> "...It just needed everyone to come home."

---

# **VISIT STATUS**

The reunion concluded without incident.

No security breaches occurred.

No emergencies were reported.

The only formal complaint submitted to Alice concerned Neko's repeated requests to inspect the manor's maintenance tunnels.

The request was denied.

Immediately.

By both Alice...

And Miss Neko.
