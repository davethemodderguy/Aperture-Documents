**SUBJECT:** Neri — Overnight Plush-State Observation  
**LOCATION:** Sector 4-C Cafeteria

---

## Executive Summary

Neri remained in her voluntary plush form overnight.

She was alone in the cafeteria.

This was not unusual by itself.

Neri frequently chooses quiet locations at night and has no difficulty being alone.

What was unusual was that she appeared **unable to sleep**.

The reason was eventually determined to be environmental rather than medical.

---

## Background

When Neri is in plush form, Alice frequently picks her up at night.

Alice experiences recurring nightmares.

Plushie Neri has consequently become a reliable source of comfort for her.

The arrangement is entirely voluntary.

Alice sleeps more easily while holding Neri.

Neri appears comfortable being held by Alice and has repeatedly allowed Alice to carry her to bed without resistance.

On the night of this observation, however, Alice forgot to retrieve her.

---

## 01:47 — Cafeteria

Security cameras recorded Plushie Neri sitting on a cafeteria table.

No personnel were present.

Neri remained there for approximately eleven minutes.

She then slowly lowered herself onto the tabletop.

She ended up lying completely flat.

No movement was observed for several minutes.

At 02:06, Neri rolled slightly onto her side.

At 02:14, she returned to lying flat.

At 02:31, she turned onto her back.

At 02:46, she rolled onto her stomach.

At 03:02, she returned to lying flat.

---

## Observation

Neri was not displaying obvious distress.

There was no sign of an acute medical episode.

She was simply...

awake.

And unable to sleep.

Her usual nighttime routine while in plush form had unintentionally been disrupted.

Alice had forgotten her.

Neri did not attempt to leave the cafeteria.

She did not attempt to find Alice.

She did not summon anyone.

She simply remained on the table.

---

## 03:17 — Behavioral Note

Neri briefly lifted her head.

She looked toward the cafeteria entrance.

Nothing happened.

She lowered her head again.

Approximately thirty seconds later, she looked toward the entrance a second time.

Nothing happened.

She stopped looking.

---

## 03:41

Neri pulled her small plush body into a tighter position.

She remained there for approximately nine minutes.

She then flattened herself against the table again.

No vocalization was recorded.

---

## 04:12

Neri attempted to sleep.

She closed her eyes.

Approximately three minutes later, she opened them.

She shifted position.

Eyes closed again.

Eyes opened again.

This sequence repeated several times.

---

> **CAMERA OPERATOR:** "Looks like it."

> **SECURITY OFFICER:** "She's been lying there for hours."

> 
> **SECURITY OFFICER:** "Alice forgot her, didn't she?"
> 
> **CAMERA OPERATOR:** "Looks like it."

No intervention was initiated.

Neri was not in medical danger and had not requested assistance.

---

## 05:31 — Alice Returns

Alice entered the cafeteria.

She stopped immediately upon seeing the table.

> **ALICE:** "...Neri?"

Neri lifted her head.

Alice froze.

> **ALICE:** "Oh no."
> 
> **ALICE:** "I'm sorry."

Neri stared at her.

Alice approached the table.

She carefully picked Neri up.

Neri did not resist.

Alice held her against her chest.

Neri immediately settled into Alice's arms.

Alice's expression reportedly changed from concern to guilt.

> **ALICE:** "I forgot you."
> 
> **ALICE:** "I'm really sorry."
> 
> **ALICE:** "You couldn't sleep, could you?"

Neri remained silent.

She tucked herself closer into Alice.

Alice hugged her.

Neither spoke for several minutes.

---

## Welfare Assessment

There is no indication that Neri interpreted Alice's mistake as malicious.

However, the incident demonstrates something important about their nighttime routine.

Neri may genuinely enjoy being alone.

That does not mean she cannot also enjoy having someone hold her.

These are not contradictory states.

She spent several hours alone in the cafeteria without attempting to seek anyone out.

When Alice arrived, however, Neri immediately accepted the offered affection.

She did not need to be convinced.

She did not need to be persuaded.

She simply wanted the hug.

---

## Final Note

At approximately 05:42, Alice carried Neri back to her room.

Neri fell asleep shortly afterward.

Alice remained awake.

She reportedly spent the next several minutes staring at the ceiling.

> **ALICE:** "I'm putting an alarm on my phone."

Neri, already half-asleep:

> **NERI:** "...okay."

Alice hugged her a little tighter.

No further conversation was recorded.

---

### C.O.R.E. WELFARE NOTE

Neri being left alone is not inherently a problem.

Neri being **forgotten** is different.

In this case, the distinction was made particularly obvious by the fact that she spent the entire night perfectly capable of remaining alone—

and still couldn't sleep until Alice came back.
