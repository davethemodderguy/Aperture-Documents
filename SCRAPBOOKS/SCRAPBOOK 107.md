# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **LUMI ENTITY OBSERVATION LOG – CRITICAL INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **SUMMARY**

A significant change has been observed in the relationship between **AE-LK-01 "Lumi"** and Corin Lockwood.

For the first time since the entity's appearance, a direct disagreement occurred between mother and daughter.

The incident resulted in Lumi voluntarily withdrawing from Corin's immediate presence.

Researchers emphasize that no hostility or anomalous aggression was observed.

The event is considered an emotionally significant family conflict.

---

# **INCIDENT**

The exchange occurred during another overnight shift inside Corin's office.

Lumi quietly approached as Corin continued reviewing financial documents.

As she had many times before, Lumi spoke gently.

> "Norma would love to see you."

No response.

A few minutes later.

> "Arcturus still asks about you."

Corin continued writing.

Then Lumi softly added,

> "They'll always welcome you home."

Corin stopped.

She slowly set her pen down.

Without looking at Lumi, she spoke.

> "...Please stop."

The room fell silent.

Lumi waited.

Corin's voice shook.

> "...I don't want to hear it anymore."

She finally turned.

There were tears in her eyes.

> "...You keep telling me to go back."

> "...But you don't understand how hard it is."

Her breathing became uneven.

> "...Every single day..."

> "...You remind me of something I can't make myself do."

Another silence.

Then, in frustration, Corin whispered:

> "...Please..."

> "...Just leave me alone."

---

# **ENTITY RESPONSE**

Witness accounts are unavailable, as no one else was present.

According to Corin's later statement, Lumi did not argue.

She did not become angry.

She simply looked at Corin with unmistakable sadness.

After several seconds, she quietly answered.

> "If that's what you need right now..."

> "...I'll give you some space."

Lumi smiled gently despite the tears forming in her own eyes.

> "I'll still love you."

Then...

She disappeared.

---

# **AFTERMATH**

Since the incident, Lumi has not reappeared in Corin's office.

She continues to be seen throughout Heaven's Blacksite by personnel and the other Lockwood siblings.

However, every evening she is observed looking toward the direction of KCA Headquarters before returning to SAFEHOUSE.

Bao later asked whether Lumi intended to visit Corin again.

Lumi smiled sadly.

> "She asked me to leave."

> "I wanted to respect that."

Astral quietly replied,

> "Being a parent is difficult."

Lumi nodded.

> "Sometimes loving your child means giving them the room they asked for..."

She looked down.

> "...Even when it breaks your heart."

---

# **CORIN**

Medical staff noted a change in Corin's behavior following Lumi's absence.

The office felt noticeably quieter.

Several times Corin instinctively began speaking before remembering no one was there to answer.

She continued working.

Yet on more than one occasion she looked toward the empty chair where Lumi usually sat.

No one occupied it.

The silence she had asked for proved very different from the silence she expected.

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the report before adding a handwritten observation.

> "Families sometimes hurt one another without intending to."

He continued.

> "Respecting someone's request for space does not mean giving up on them."

He signed the report.

> "When the time is right, the first conversation should begin with understanding—not blame."

---

# **STATUS**

Lumi remains a confirmed non-hostile Ether-superseded ghost and continues to interact with personnel throughout Heaven's Blacksite.

Her temporary absence from Corin's office appears to be a deliberate choice made out of respect for Corin's request rather than a permanent separation.

Medical Services consider the situation emotionally significant and recommend that any future reunion between the two be approached with patience, compassion, and without pressure.

**End of Lumi Entity Observation Log – Critical Incident**
