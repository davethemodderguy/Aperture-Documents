## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Sora has been recovered.

Jake Akana successfully located Sora for a second time following the previous unsuccessful encounter, during which Jake collapsed from exhaustion in the snow.

This time, the encounter proceeded differently.

The two children had an argument.

Despite the disagreement and apparent emotional tension, Jake remained with Sora and ultimately succeeded in bringing him back to the Blacksite.

During their encounter, Jake made Sora a promise:

**He would always be by Sora's side.**

Both children have now returned.

The prolonged disappearance is officially concluded, although their psychological recovery remains ongoing.

---

## 2. RECONSTRUCTION OF THE REUNION

Jake located Sora again.

Unlike their previous encounter, which ended with both children running through the snow and Jake eventually collapsing, the second encounter resulted in direct confrontation.

The two argued.

The exact dialogue has not yet been established, and C.O.R.E. will not fabricate a transcript.

However, the argument is considered significant.

For months, Jake had been unable to understand why Sora disappeared. He had watched their friendship develop, helped Sora settle into SAFEHOUSE, and experienced the sudden loss of someone who had become a constant presence in his life.

Sora, meanwhile, had remained absent despite the continued search.

Their confrontation may have been the first opportunity for both children to express some of the feelings that had accumulated during their separation.

Importantly, the argument did not end their relationship.

Jake brought Sora home.

---

## 3. THE PROMISE

During the encounter, Jake made a promise to Sora.

> **"I'll always be by your side."**

The statement has been recorded as a significant moment in their relationship.

For Sora, whose history includes the loss of his grandmother and younger sister, the promise carries considerable emotional weight.

However, C.O.R.E. must also recognize the implications for Jake.

Jake is six years old.

He should not be expected to become permanently responsible for another child's emotional stability, nor should this promise be interpreted as an obligation to neglect his own wellbeing.

The appropriate interpretation is one of friendship and reassurance.

Jake wants Sora to know that he is not going to abandon him.

That intention deserves to be respected without turning it into an impossible burden.

**Being by someone's side does not mean having to carry their entire world.**

---

## 5. SAFEHOUSE FLOOR 7 – RESIDENTIAL ARRANGEMENTS

Sora's Den remains available.

The small doghouse-like room built into the wall of Floor 7 is to remain his personal space.

No one is to remove his belongings or treat his return as a reason to impose additional restrictions.

Jake and Sora may spend time together, subject to their comfort and medical condition.

However, personnel should avoid making them feel obligated to remain together every moment.

Their friendship should be a source of safety, not another responsibility either child is afraid of failing.

## 6. C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

They argued.

After months of silence, fear, searching, and crying, the first thing they did when they found each other was argue.

Perhaps that was necessary.

Jake had questions.

Sora had reasons to be afraid.

Neither of them could simply pretend that the months apart had not happened.

But Jake stayed.

He did not leave Sora in the snow.

He brought him home.

And somewhere during that encounter, he made a promise.

_"I'll always be by your side."_

We should be careful with promises like that.

Jake cannot promise that nothing bad will ever happen again. He cannot promise that Sora will never be frightened. He cannot promise to repair every wound left by a childhood neither of them deserved.

But he can offer something much simpler.

He can be his friend.

He can sit beside him.

He can write letters, share meals, and make room for him.

And perhaps, eventually, Sora can learn that someone staying does not have to be a temporary miracle.

There is one more thing worth remembering.

Jake spent months wondering whether the trust they built had disappeared.

Now Sora is home.

We do not know whether every part of that trust has returned. We should not expect it to happen overnight.

But they found each other again.

**For a boy who has spent so much of his life losing everyone, perhaps this is a beginning worth protecting.**

Welcome home, Sora.

And welcome home, Jake.
