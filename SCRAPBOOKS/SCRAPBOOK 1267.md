## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### RESIDENT WELFARE & MEDICAL DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-NORA-417  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — RESIDENT WELFARE  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**SUBJECT:** Nora Tachyon — Blanket Carrying / Maternal Comfort Behavior

---

# 1. OBSERVATION

Nora has developed a consistent response to her persistent cold state:

**She wants to be wrapped up.**

More specifically, she wants to be wrapped in a blanket and carried by Aeterna.

This has become sufficiently common that SAFEHOUSE personnel have begun referring to the behavior as:

> **“Blanket Nora.”**

The designation is unofficial.

Nora does not know about it.

It is probably better that way.

---

# 2. STANDARD CONFIGURATION

When Nora begins shivering significantly, the current procedure is remarkably simple.

1. Wrap Nora in a warm blanket.
    
2. Ensure her glasses remain accessible.
    
3. Give her a warm drink if she wants one.
    
4. Allow her to choose whether she wants to be carried.
    
5. If she reaches for Aeterna, Aeterna picks her up.
    

Nora generally curls into the blanket immediately.

Once secured, she tends to remain extremely still.

Her shark tail usually curls beneath the blanket.

Her hands are tucked close to her chest.

Her head rests against Aeterna.

The resulting appearance has been repeatedly described by personnel as:

> **“A mother carrying her baby.”**

The description is technically inaccurate.

It is also extremely difficult to argue with.

---

# 3. AETERNA'S CARRYING POSITION

Aeterna does not carry Nora over her shoulder.

She carries her in both arms.

One arm supports Nora's back.

The other supports her legs.

Nora remains wrapped completely within the blanket.

Her head rests against Aeterna's chest.

Because Aeterna's body is manifested armor rather than a warm biological body, this provides little actual heat.

Nora does not appear concerned.

She seems to value the security of being held more than the temperature of the person holding her.

---

# 4. OBSERVED EXAMPLE

Nora was found sitting beneath a blanket in the SAFEHOUSE lounge.

She was shivering.

Aeterna approached.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Would you like to be carried?”

Nora nodded.

Aeterna picked her up.

Nora immediately tucked herself deeper into the blanket.

Only her face and glasses remained visible.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Comfortable?”

A small nod.

> **NORA:**  
> “Cold.”

Aeterna adjusted the blanket.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “I know.”

Nora moved closer.

> **NORA:**  
> “Stay.”

> **AETERNA:**  
> “I am staying.”

Nora closed her eyes.

---

# 5. MOBILITY

The arrangement has produced an unexpected secondary benefit.

Nora does not need to walk when she is cold and exhausted.

Aeterna simply carries her.

She can transport Nora between:

- the SAFEHOUSE lounge;
    
- medical rooms;
    
- the kitchen;
    
- Nora's bedroom;
    
- the cafeteria;
    
- and other appropriate indoor areas.
    

Nora rarely objects.

In fact, she appears to actively enjoy being carried.

This is particularly noticeable when she is tired.

Instead of asking where they are going, Nora simply settles into the blanket and lets Aeterna decide the route.

---

# 6. THE INFANT COMPARISON

Personnel have repeatedly noted the resemblance to an adult carrying an infant.

The comparison is especially strong when:

- Nora is completely wrapped in blankets;
    
- her shark tail is hidden;
    
- her head is resting against Aeterna;
    
- she is half-asleep;
    
- her glasses are slightly crooked;
    
- and Aeterna is walking slowly through the SAFEHOUSE while carrying her.
    

One observer described the scene as:

> “A fifteen-thousand-year-old cosmic guardian carrying a freezing six-year-old shark like she just picked up her baby.”

The observer declined to provide a correction.

No correction was necessary.

---

# 7. NORA'S RESPONSE

Nora appears completely comfortable with the arrangement.

She does not consider being carried humiliating.

She does not attempt to prove that she can walk.

She does not insist that she is fine.

She simply allows herself to be cared for.

This represents a meaningful behavioral change.

Previously, Nora frequently attempted to minimize her own needs.

Currently, she is increasingly willing to communicate them through simple actions.

If she wants to be carried, she reaches toward Aeterna.

If she wants another blanket, she points toward one.

If she wants a drink, she points toward the cup.

If she wants a hug:

She simply holds out her arms.

---

# 8. AETERNA

Aeterna appears entirely comfortable with the arrangement.

She does not treat carrying Nora as an inconvenience.

She does not ask Nora to walk when she is clearly exhausted.

She does not complain about the blankets.

She does not complain about Nora falling asleep against her.

She simply carries her.

The only recurring problem is that Aeterna herself remains cold.

Nora therefore occasionally presses herself against Aeterna while shivering.

Aeterna remains equally cold.

Neither seems bothered.

---

# 9. BAO'S OBSERVATION

Bao witnessed Aeterna carrying Nora through the SAFEHOUSE.

Nora was completely wrapped in a blanket.

Only her face was visible.

Bao watched them pass.

> **BAO:**  
> “She looks tiny.”

Aeterna looked down.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “She is tiny.”

Bao smiled.

> **BAO:**  
> “You're spoiling her.”

Aeterna continued walking.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Yes.”

Bao blinked.

> **BAO:**  
> “You're not even going to deny it?”

> **AETERNA:**  
> “No.”

Nora quietly mumbled from inside the blanket:

> **NORA:**  
> “Good.”

Bao laughed.

---

# 10. CURRENT GUIDANCE

Nora should not be discouraged from accepting physical comfort.

If she is cold and requests to be carried:

**Carry her.**

If she wants a blanket:

**Give her one.**

If she wants a hug:

**Hug her.**

If she falls asleep while being carried:

**Let her sleep.**

The fact that Nora can walk does not mean she must walk.

The fact that she can tolerate discomfort does not mean she should.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Nora spends much of her time these days wrapped in blankets.

Sometimes she sits.

Sometimes she drinks hot chocolate.

Sometimes she sleeps.

And increasingly:

She is carried.

Aeterna walks through the SAFEHOUSE with a tiny shark wrapped securely in a blanket against her chest.

The armor is cold.

The room is warm.

Nora is still occasionally shivering.

But she is being held.

And for a child who spent far too long believing that needing someone was a burden, there is something important about simply being allowed to remain there.

No explanation.

No repayment.

No requirement to be useful.

Just:

**“Come here.”**

And Aeterna carrying her.
