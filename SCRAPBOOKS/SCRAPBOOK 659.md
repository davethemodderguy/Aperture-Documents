# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / POST-RECOVERY SUSTENANCE LOG**

# **THE THING THAT KEPT HER ALIVE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Observer:** Orphie  
**Status:** **CRITICAL — SEVERE WITHDRAWAL**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie eats.

Corin does not.

Not properly.

The Treehouse remains fully stocked with food and water.

Meals are prepared.

Warm drinks are available.

Nothing is locked away from her.

Yet Corin continues to obtain what she needs from the environment.

She eats snow.

She eats bark.

She eats flowers.

She eats grass.

Not because she prefers them.

Because they are there.

---

# **WATER**

Corin uses snow to replace drinking water.

She gathers it from the ground and eats it slowly.

Medical personnel have repeatedly attempted to replace this behavior with warm water.

The attempts have been inconclusive.

Corin sometimes accepts the cup.

She rarely finishes it.

Eventually, she returns outside.

And eats snow again.

---

# **FOOD**

The same pattern applies to food.

Corin has access to proper meals.

She has access to everything she previously enjoyed.

She rarely shows interest.

Instead, she consumes whatever vegetation is immediately available.

Bark.

Grass.

Wild flowers.

Small amounts at a time.

Enough to sustain herself.

Nothing more.

---

# **ATTEMPTS TO HELP**

Personnel have attempted:

- Warm meals.
    
- Favorite foods.
    
- Fresh fruit.
    
- Sweet drinks.
    
- Her favorite flowers.
    
- Comfort items.
    
- Blankets.
    
- Familiar objects.
    
- Company.
    
- Conversation.
    
- Reassurance.
    

Results have been inconsistent.

Nothing appears capable of producing sustained interest.

Corin does not actively reject every offering.

That would almost be easier.

She simply does not care.

---

# **THE DIFFERENCE**

Before her disappearance, Corin ate because she wanted to.

Now she eats because her body occasionally demands something.

Before, she drank because she enjoyed being alive.

Now she consumes snow because she needs water.

Before, she collected flowers because they were beautiful.

Now she eats them because they are food.

The distinction is subtle.

And devastating.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie continues eating normally.

She knows Corin watches sometimes.

She deliberately eats beside her.

Not to pressure her.

Simply to show her that eating can still be ordinary.

Corin occasionally looks over.

Then returns her attention to the snow.

---

# **THE EMPTY OFFERING**

One evening, Orphie brought Corin a warm meal.

Corin looked at it.

She recognized it immediately.

It was something she used to love.

Orphie placed it beside her.

> **"You don't have to eat it."**

Corin nodded.

Hours later, the meal remained untouched.

The snow beside Corin was gone.

---

# **THE WORD "ALIVE"**

Medical personnel continue to use the word **alive** when describing Corin.

It has become increasingly uncomfortable.

Corin's body no longer functions conventionally.

Her hearts have stopped.

Her organs have failed.

Her regeneration has ceased.

Yet her consciousness persists.

And somehow, she continues consuming enough material to maintain that consciousness.

Barely.

---

# **THE LAST THING**

Corin once wrote:

> **"I live on hope."**

That sentence has become one of the most frequently referenced lines in her entire archive.

Because it appears to have been true.

Hope made her move.

Hope made her wait.

Hope made her survive.

Hope made her believe Orphie would return.

Hope gave meaning to suffering.

Now there is none.

---

# **DROWNED**

Corin is not merely sad.

She is not simply grieving.

She appears completely submerged in hopelessness.

There is no visible struggle against it anymore.

She does not scream.

She does not fight.

She does not ask for help.

She simply exists inside it.

---

# **"DEAD INSIDE"**

Personnel have informally described Corin as:

> **"Dead inside."**

The phrase is not medically accurate.

But it captures the observation.

Her consciousness remains.

Her personality remains in fragments.

Her memories remain.

Her attachment to Orphie remains.

But the part of her that once reached toward the future appears absent.

---

# **THE WINGS**

Corin's wings remain wrapped around her.

They are dormant.

They cannot fly.

They cannot burn.

They cannot launch feathers.

They cannot protect her from the cold anymore.

Yet they remain the one thing she consistently holds close.

Perhaps because they remember what she cannot.

---

# **ORPHIE'S FEAR**

Orphie understands that Corin is still eating enough to remain present.

That should be reassuring.

It isn't.

Because Orphie knows why.

Corin is not eating because she wants to live.

She is simply consuming enough to continue existing.

There is a difference.

A terrible one.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie eats.

Corin eats snow.

Orphie drinks water.

Corin eats snow.

Orphie has a warm bed.

Corin sits beneath the tree.

Orphie has hope that tomorrow might be better.

Corin has none.

She eats bark.

She eats grass.

She eats flowers.

She eats snow.

Not because she enjoys them.

Because they keep her body functioning.

And that is the tragedy of Corin's current existence.

The one thing she once said kept her alive was **hope**.

She no longer has it.

Her body should have stopped long ago.

Her regeneration has already given up.

Her hearts have stopped.

Her organs have failed.

And yet she remains conscious beneath the snow.

Still breathing.

Still thinking.

Still holding her wings around herself.

Not because she wants a future.

Not because she expects someone to come.

Not because she believes things will improve.

She simply remains.

The girl who once wrote:

> **"I live on hope."**

now has no hope left.

And somehow...

she is still here.

**End of Post-Recovery Sustenance Log**
