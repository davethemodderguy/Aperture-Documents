# SAFEHOUSE MEDICAL & PSYCHOLOGICAL NOTE

## NERI AND BAO — THE PERSON WHO BRINGS HER BACK

**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL  
**Resident:** Neri  
**Age:** 10  
**Primary Care Relationship:** Bao — maternal figure  
**Subject:** Neri's attachment to Bao following spontaneous anemia episodes

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

There is a particularly strong bond between **Neri** and **Bao**.

Bao is not merely Neri's mother figure.

**Bao is one of the primary reasons Neri is still alive.**

Neri's abnormal blood condition can deteriorate spontaneously. When it reaches a critical point, her cardiovascular system can fail.

Bao's blood is the only known reliably compatible source capable of restoring enough functional blood to stabilize Neri.

Neri understands this in the simplest way possible:

> **Bao saves her.**

She does not need to understand the underlying medical terminology.

She remembers who was there.

---

# 2. A TYPICAL EPISODE

One incident was recorded as follows.

Neri approached Bao while she was working.

> **Neri:** “Mom.”

> **Bao:** “Yes, Neri?”

Neri paused.

She looked unsteady.

> **Neri:** “I'm not feeling too well...”

Neri collapsed.

Bao immediately recognized the situation.

> **Bao:** “NERI!”

Bao cut her finger and administered compatible blood to Neri.

After stabilization, Neri regained enough awareness to recognize Bao.

She reached for her.

> **Neri:** “Mom...”

Neri hugged Bao.

Bao held her.

No further explanation was necessary.

---

# 3. WHY NERI CLINGS TO BAO

Neri has lost too many people.

Her childhood taught her that people can disappear without warning.

Her younger sister disappeared.

Friends disappeared.

People she cared about died.

Neri developed the belief that losing someone meant she had failed to protect them.

Then she arrived at SAFEHOUSE.

For the first time, someone consistently demonstrated the opposite.

When Neri's body failed:

**Bao stayed.**

When Neri could not breathe:

**Bao helped.**

When Neri collapsed:

**Bao brought her back.**

That has enormous significance to a child who spent years believing that everyone eventually disappears.

---

# 4. NERI'S UNDERSTANDING

Neri does not describe this relationship using medical language.

She does not say:

> “Bao provides compatible transfusion support during acute hematological decompensation.”

She says:

> **“Mom.”**

And when Bao asks what is wrong:

> **“I'm not feeling too well.”**

Because that is what Neri understands.

Bao is safety.

---

# 5. AFTER AN EPISODE

Following an episode, Neri may become unusually clingy.

She may:

- hold Bao's hand,
    
- hug her,
    
- stay physically close,
    
- sit beside her,
    
- follow her around,
    
- ask where she is going,
    
- or simply remain beside her without saying anything.
    

This should not be discouraged.

For Neri, physical proximity after an episode appears to provide reassurance that the person who saved her is still there.

Sometimes she doesn't even say anything.

She just hugs Bao.

---

# 6. BAO'S RESPONSE

Bao understands.

She does not make Neri feel guilty for needing her.

She does not tell her to stop being afraid.

She does not tell her that she is being dramatic.

She simply lets Neri stay close.

If Neri hugs her after an episode, Bao hugs her back.

If Neri falls asleep against her, Bao lets her sleep.

If Neri asks:

> **“Mom?”**

Bao answers:

> **“Yes, Neri.”**

That answer matters.

It means:

**I'm still here.**

---

# 7. THE DEEPER PROBLEM

Neri's attachment to Bao is healthy in one sense: she has found someone she trusts completely.

But there is an underlying fear.

Neri has spent her entire life losing people.

Therefore, even when someone is present, part of her expects them to disappear eventually.

Her fear is not simply:

> _“What if I die?”_

It is also:

> _“What if Mom isn't there?”_

And underneath that:

> _“What if I lose her too?”_

This is why Neri may hold onto Bao particularly tightly after a medical episode.

She has just been reminded that her own body can take her away from the people she loves.

---

# 8. “I CAN'T LOSE ANYMORE.”

This is the central psychological theme surrounding Neri.

She has already lost too much.

Her sister.

Her childhood.

Her sense of safety.

Friends.

Years of normal development.

And, in her own mind, countless opportunities to save people she cared about.

She cannot emotionally tolerate the idea of another loss.

Consequently, Neri may push herself far beyond what is medically safe when someone she loves is threatened.

Her reasoning is simple:

> **“Not again.”**

She would rather put herself in danger than watch another person disappear.

This is precisely what SAFEHOUSE must teach her to overcome.

---

# 9. THE LESSON NERI STILL NEEDS TO LEARN

Neri believes protection means:

> **“I have to save everyone.”**

SAFEHOUSE needs to teach her:

> **“You don't have to save everyone by yourself.”**

Bao is living proof.

Neri does not save Bao every time something goes wrong.

Sometimes:

**Bao saves Neri.**

That is not weakness.

That is family.

Neri is allowed to be the person who needs help.

She is allowed to fall.

She is allowed to be scared.

She is allowed to cling to her mother.

She is allowed to survive.

---

# 10. C.O.R.E. NOTE

Neri's capabilities frequently make personnel forget how young she actually is.

She is an exceptional chemist.

She possesses anomalous molecular restructuring.

She is extremely dangerous in close combat.

She can use a Starlight katana.

She can use a bow and mechanical crossbow.

She can understand chemistry that exceeds the capabilities of many adults.

None of that changes the following fact:

**Neri is ten.**

Sometimes she is going to collapse, wake up frightened, and hug her mom.

And that's okay.

---

## FINAL OBSERVATION

Neri has spent most of her life believing that people disappear.

SAFEHOUSE cannot promise her that nobody will ever die.

No one can make that promise.

But it can give her something she never had before:

**People who stay.**

And when Neri whispers:

> **“Mom...”**

Bao answers:

> **“Yes, Neri.”**

For a child who has lost so much, that simple answer may be one of the most important things in the entire Blacksite.

**She's still here.**

**Bao is still here.**

**They're together.**

**And for now, that's enough.**

**END DOCUMENT**
