# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **MEDICAL OBSERVATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – PHYSICAL PRESENTATION AND SOCIAL IMPACT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin Lockwood's Ether-dependent physiology produces several visible characteristics that distinguish her from ordinary personnel.

These characteristics are harmless to those around her.

However, they frequently result in unintended fear among individuals unfamiliar with her medical history.

Medical Services have concluded that these reactions have become a significant source of psychological distress for Corin.

---

# **PHYSICAL CHARACTERISTICS**

Following her forced Ether revival, Corin exhibits several persistent physiological abnormalities.

Observed characteristics include:

- Chalky pale skin resulting from her altered circulation.
    
- Very pale chalk-lime hair.
    
- Skin temperature consistently far below average, making her noticeably cold to the touch.
    
- Little to no detectable thermal signature on standard thermal imaging equipment.
    
- An Ether-dependent biological state that differs substantially from ordinary human physiology.
    

These characteristics have remained stable for years.

Corin is fully aware of them.

---

# **FIRST IMPRESSIONS**

Personnel familiar with Corin rarely pay attention to her appearance.

New arrivals often react very differently.

One recurring incident involves handshakes.

Corin habitually greets people politely.

When an unsuspecting researcher shakes her hand, they are frequently startled by how cold she feels.

Several have instinctively withdrawn in alarm.

One junior researcher reportedly stepped backward so quickly that they nearly fell over.

Another apologized repeatedly after unintentionally yelling.

Neither reaction was malicious.

Both were simply unexpected.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Although Corin never criticizes those who react with surprise, the incidents affect her deeply.

Following one such encounter, she quietly returned to her office.

When Lumi found her, Corin spoke without looking up.

> "...I scared them again."

Lumi gently replied,

> "They were surprised."

Corin slowly shook her head.

> "No."

A long pause.

> "People always look at me differently after that."

She wrapped her hands around a warm cup of tea.

> "...I hate it."

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The residents of SAFEHOUSE gradually developed small habits without discussing them.

Belle often reaches for Corin's hand first, as though its temperature is completely ordinary.

Bao quietly warms tea whenever Corin visits the kitchen.

Frost jokingly insists Corin is "still warmer than my room."

Taiga has been observed sitting beside Corin without hesitation, even on particularly cold days.

Mart simply treats Corin exactly as he treats everyone else.

No one comments on her appearance.

No one stares.

To them, she is simply Corin.

---

# **LUMI'S OBSERVATION**

One evening, Corin apologized.

> "...I'm unpleasant to be around."

Lumi immediately disagreed.

She reached forward and held both of Corin's hands.

The cold did not bother her.

> "Your hands are cold."

Corin quietly nodded.

Lumi smiled.

> "Then I'll just hold them a little longer."

Corin lowered her head.

For several seconds, neither of them spoke.

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the report and added:

> "The first thing many people notice is her appearance."

> "The second thing they notice is her kindness."

> "Only one of those tells you who Corin really is."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Medical personnel are advised to brief new researchers before introducing them to Corin, reducing unnecessary alarm and preventing avoidable distress.

Corin herself continues to struggle with how others initially perceive her.

Within Project SAFEHOUSE, however, her unusual physiology has long since become unremarkable.

To the residents, she is not "the cold girl."

She is their accountant.

Their friend.

Their family.

**End of Medical Observation Report**
