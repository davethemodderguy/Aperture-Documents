# Executive Summary

Invictus Aeterna possesses a distinctive system of **blue crescent-moon sigils** distributed across her manifested body.

The same symbols are present upon her **Execution Blade**.

The sigils are not decorative markings.

They are an intrinsic component of Aeterna's manifested form and appear to function as a visible expression of her anomalous energy state.

Under ordinary circumstances, the markings emit a relatively subdued blue glow.

During combat, their luminosity increases dramatically.

At peak combat activity, the sigils become among the brightest visible features of Aeterna's manifested form.

The effect is particularly pronounced immediately before and during the use of her Execution Blade.

---

# 1. SIGIL APPEARANCE

The markings consistently appear as stylized:

> **Crescent moons.**

They are predominantly blue.

Their exact geometry varies slightly according to their location on Aeterna's body, but the central crescent motif remains consistent.

Observed locations include:

- helmet and facial plating;
    
- shoulders;
    
- upper arms;
    
- forearms;
    
- chest;
    
- back;
    
- waist;
    
- thighs;
    
- lower legs;
    
- and sections of her manifested armor plating.
    

Additional sigils are present along the Execution Blade.

The markings appear integrated into the material of her manifested body rather than painted onto its surface.

---

# 2. ORDINARY LUMINOSITY

Outside combat, the sigils emit a soft blue light.

The illumination is normally sufficient to make them clearly visible in darkness.

They do not, however, produce significant environmental illumination.

During routine SAFEHOUSE activity, Aeterna's markings are therefore generally perceived as:

> **A faint celestial-blue glow.**

Nora appears particularly fond of them.

She has occasionally traced the outline of a crescent with her finger while Aeterna is in a resting state.

Aeterna permits this.

---

# 3. COMBAT RESPONSE

The luminosity changes significantly when Aeterna enters combat.

The blue crescents brighten progressively as her combat state intensifies.

The increase is not limited to her arms or weapon.

The markings across her entire manifested body respond.

This creates the visual impression that Aeterna's entire body is becoming illuminated from within.

During the Harbinger incident, this phenomenon was particularly pronounced.

Witnesses described the sequence as:

**Dim blue.**

↓

**Bright blue.**

↓

**Intense blue-white luminosity.**

↓

**Full-body sigil illumination immediately preceding the Execution Blade strike.**

---

# 4. THE EXECUTION BLADE

The same crescent sigils are present along Aeterna's sword.

When the blade is inactive, they emit the same subdued blue glow as the markings on her body.

During combat, the sword's sigils brighten alongside Aeterna's own.

This synchronization appears deliberate.

The weapon and wielder effectively display the same anomalous energy response.

During the final confrontation with the Harbinger, observers recorded the blade's markings becoming extremely bright immediately before the execution strike.

The resulting emission substantially exceeded the sword's ordinary luminosity.

---

# 5. RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN AETERNA AND THE BLADE

C.O.R.E. has found no evidence that the sword is merely an ordinary weapon carried by Aeterna.

It is part of the same anomalous system.

Aeterna can manifest and dismiss it.

The weapon responds to her combat state.

The crescent sigils on the blade respond simultaneously with those on her body.

Most importantly:

> **The sword is an extension of the Invictus manifestation rather than conventional equipment.**

This is consistent with its demonstrated ability to permanently destroy entities that ordinary weapons cannot meaningfully eliminate.

---

# 6. HARBRINGER INCIDENT CORRELATION

During the seven-day Harbinger conflict, the sigils remained relatively subdued during periods in which Aeterna was not directly engaged.

When the Harbinger threatened Nora, this changed.

Aeterna entered combat.

The blue crescents across her body brightened.

The markings on her sword brightened simultaneously.

As the confrontation escalated, the illumination became intense enough to visibly outline Aeterna's entire form.

The final Execution Blade strike produced an additional golden energy discharge.

The blue sigils remained visible through the event.

Following the Harbinger's destruction, the luminosity gradually returned to its normal state.

---

# 7. NO INDICATION OF EMOTIONAL CONTROL

Although the increase in luminosity coincides with heightened combat activity, C.O.R.E. has not established that the sigils directly measure Aeterna's emotional state.

The markings should therefore not be interpreted as a simple:

> **"Anger meter."**

Aeterna can intentionally enter combat.

She can also become protective without immediately escalating into combat.

The available evidence supports a stronger relationship with:

> **Anomalous combat activation.**

rather than emotion alone.

When asked directly what the crescents represent, Aeterna gave the following response:

> **AETERNA:**  
> "The moon."

The interviewer asked whether there was a deeper meaning.

Aeterna paused.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "There always is."

No further clarification was provided.

---

# 10. WELFARE CONSIDERATION

Despite the intimidating appearance of the markings during combat, they do not appear to negatively affect Aeterna's normal SAFEHOUSE interactions.

Outside combat, the same markings provide a much softer visual effect.

This is particularly noticeable when Aeterna is carrying Nora at night.

The blue crescents remain faintly illuminated across her body.

Nora has reportedly fallen asleep while resting against Aeterna's chest while the markings were visible.

The contrast between Aeterna's ancient combat capabilities and her current role as Nora's guardian has been noted by multiple personnel.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The crescent-moon sigils are a permanent and defining characteristic of Invictus Aeterna's manifested form.

They appear on:

**Aeterna.**

**Her armor-body.**

**Her Execution Blade.**

They glow softly during ordinary activity.

They become dramatically brighter during combat.

The brightest recorded state occurred during the Harbinger confrontation, immediately preceding the Execution Blade's final strike.

The markings therefore provide a useful visual indicator that Aeterna has transitioned from her normal guardian state into an active combat state.

Personnel are reminded that this distinction is largely academic.

If Aeterna is carrying Nora through the SAFEHOUSE, the crescents are soft blue.

If Aeterna is standing between Nora and something attempting to hurt her—

**do not wait for the blue light to become bright before leaving the area.**
