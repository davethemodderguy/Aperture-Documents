## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **RESTRICTED ANOMALOUS WEATHER / MEDICAL & PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT LOG**

# **SHE WAS ASLEEP**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **ANOMALOUS SUPPRESSION STORM — TERMINATED**  
**Status:** **ALIVE / AWAKE / SEVERE PSYCHOLOGICAL DISTRESS**

---

# **CORRECTION TO INITIAL ASSESSMENT**

The initial response team incorrectly interpreted Corin's complete lack of movement as a potentially critical medical event.

She was not unconscious due to injury.

She was not dead.

She was asleep.

Her body had simply remained motionless for an extended period.

For perhaps the first time in days, Corin had managed to sleep naturally.

---

# **THE AWAKENING**

Orphie remained beside her.

She was holding Corin when Corin finally stirred.

Her eyes slowly opened.

For several seconds, she simply stared.

Then she noticed the people surrounding her.

Guiding Light.

Curious Light.

Lumi.

Orphie.

The SAFEHOUSE residents.

Everyone.

Corin's expression remained blank.

> **"...Oh."**

Orphie immediately hugged her.

Corin did not resist.

She simply remained still in Orphie's arms.

> **Orphie:**  
> "I'm here."

Corin stared past her.

> **"...I know."**

---

# **THE FIRST QUESTION**

Corin eventually looked around the room.

Her eyes stopped on the window.

The snow was gone.

Sunlight had returned.

She looked confused.

> **"The storm stopped?"**

Guiding Light nodded.

> **"Yes."**

Corin was silent.

Then:

> **"...You came back."**

Orphie answered immediately.

> **"Of course I did."**

Corin looked down.

> **"I thought you left."**

Orphie tightened her arms around her.

> **"Never."**

---

# **THE DAMAGE THAT REMAINED**

Corin's body could be repaired.

Her detached arm could be reattached.

Her stitches could be restored.

Her clothes could be cleaned.

Her room could be restocked.

The physical damage was manageable.

Her mind was not.

The storm had lasted long enough for Corin's beliefs about herself and everyone around her to deteriorate severely.

She still believed some of the things she had written.

She still believed she was a burden.

She still believed Orphie might eventually become tired of caring for her.

She still believed that her body was something fundamentally wrong.

And she had not forgotten the days spent believing nobody was coming.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

Corin saw the notebook in Guiding Light's hands.

Her expression immediately changed.

> **"...You read it."**

Guiding Light did not lie.

> **"Yes."**

Corin looked away.

> **"I'm sorry."**

Orphie gently held her.

> **"You don't have to apologize for being scared."**

Corin's voice was quiet.

> **"I wasn't just scared."**

She looked at the floor.

> **"I stopped caring."**

Nobody interrupted.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light sat beside her.

For once, she did not attempt to explain the situation with grand words.

She did not tell Corin that everything would magically become better.

She simply said:

> **"I couldn't reach you."**

Corin looked at her.

> **"I know."**

> **"I tried."**

A pause.

> **"...I know."**

Guiding Light looked down.

> **"I'm sorry."**

Corin's eyes lowered.

> **"I thought you stopped caring."**

Guiding Light answered quietly:

> **"I never stopped."**

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi remained nearby.

She looked at Corin for a long time.

There were many things she wanted to say.

Too many.

For now, she only managed:

> **"I'm here."**

Corin looked at her.

There was recognition.

But there was also hesitation.

Too much time had passed.

Too much had happened.

Corin eventually nodded.

> **"...Okay."**

It was not forgiveness.

It was not rejection.

It was simply acknowledgement.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie carefully combed Corin's hair.

She fixed the hairclip.

Then adjusted the red wool hat.

Corin remained quiet throughout.

Eventually she whispered:

> **"I thought you were tired of me."**

Orphie's hand stopped.

> **"Never."**

> **"I'm difficult."**

> **"Sometimes."**

Corin looked surprised.

Orphie continued:

> **"So am I."**

A tiny, exhausted smile appeared on Corin's face.

It disappeared almost immediately.

But it had been there.

---

# **THE DETACHED ARM**

Corin eventually noticed her left arm lying nearby.

She stared at it.

> **"...Oh."**

Orphie carefully retrieved it.

> **"We'll fix it."**

Corin shrugged.

> **"Okay."**

There was no panic.

No embarrassment.

No urgency.

That lack of concern worried the others more than the detached limb itself.

---

# **CURRENT PSYCHOLOGICAL STATE**

Corin is awake.

She is responsive.

She recognizes everyone.

She can communicate normally.

She is not currently attempting to harm herself.

However, her mental state remains **severely compromised**.

Observed symptoms include:

- Emotional numbness.
    
- Persistent feelings of worthlessness.
    
- Fear of abandonment.
    
- Profound exhaustion.
    
- Detachment from her own body.
    
- Difficulty accepting care.
    
- Reduced concern for her physical condition.
    
- Persistent thoughts about death and escape.
    
- Difficulty understanding why others continue to care about her.
    

The storm is gone.

The effects it left behind are not.

---

# **THE FIRST MEAL**

Someone eventually brought Corin food.

A simple meal.

Nothing extravagant.

Corin stared at it.

She did not immediately eat.

Orphie sat beside her.

> **"You don't have to finish it."**

Corin picked up the fork.

Took a small bite.

She stopped.

Her eyes watered.

> **"...I missed this."**

Orphie smiled.

> **"I know."**

Corin took another bite.

For a moment, the room felt almost normal.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin survived the storm.

She survived the isolation.

She survived her own worst thoughts.

She survived because her body refused to stop functioning.

But survival did not undo what happened.

The storm is gone.

The snow has stopped.

The Architects can reach her again.

Orphie is home.

Lumi is here.

The SAFEHOUSE residents are here.

Food is available.

Water is available.

The blue notes can glow again.

Everything that disappeared has returned.

Except one thing:

**Corin's sense that she is safe.**

That will take time.

For now, she is surrounded by people who stayed.

And for the first time in days, she does not have to stare at the ceiling alone.

**End of Restricted Anomalous Weather / Medical & Psychological Incident Log**
