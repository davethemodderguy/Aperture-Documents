---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

An MSAC Scout made the questionable decision to steal **NS-T-001**, Nora Tachyon's non-functional Night Stalker training replica.

The Scout apparently believed the item to be a harmless novelty.

This assessment was incorrect.

Nora became extremely angry.

She subsequently pursued the Scout through SAFEHOUSE while carrying **Alice's battle axe**.

The resulting pursuit was described by witnesses as:

> **“A cartoon chase.”**

No serious injuries occurred.

The Scout eventually returned the training replica.

Nora then discovered that she had, earlier that day, stolen **Neri Tachyon's keycard**.

She had apparently forgotten about it.

---

# 2. THE THEFT

NS-T-001 had been left in an approved location inside the SAFEHOUSE.

The replica is clearly marked as non-functional.

It belongs to Nora.

An MSAC Scout noticed it.

The Scout picked it up.

He then made the first mistake.

He walked away with it.

The second mistake was considerably worse.

He decided not to immediately return it.

---

The pursuit proceeded through:

- one cafeteria entrance;
    
- two corridors;
    
- a lounge;
    
- an intersection;
    
- and back into the cafeteria.
    

At one point, the Scout attempted to hide behind a doorway.

Nora walked past.

Stopped.

Backed up.

Looked at him.

The Scout slowly stepped out.

Nora raised the axe.

The Scout immediately ran again.

No actual strike was made.

No damage was inflicted.

# 9. C.O.R.E. REVIEW

The incident produced three separate security findings.

### Finding 1

Nora's training replica should not have been accessible to unauthorized personnel.

### Finding 2

The MSAC Scout should not have taken it.

### Finding 3

Nora apparently possesses the exact same attitude toward unauthorized property acquisition that she has previously demonstrated toward other people's belongings.

The third finding was considered particularly concerning.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The incident was ultimately harmless.

The training replica was recovered intact.

The Scout was unharmed.

Nora was unharmed.

Alice's axe was returned.

Neri's keycard was returned.

No operational equipment was compromised.

The primary concern is behavioral rather than physical:

Nora appears to have learned that chasing people through the SAFEHOUSE with a battle axe is an effective way to retrieve stolen property.

C.O.R.E. strongly recommends that this lesson not be reinforced.
