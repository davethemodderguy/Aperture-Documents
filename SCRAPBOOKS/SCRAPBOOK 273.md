## **SUBJECT: ETHER VISION & PASSIVE ETHER EMISSION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical, Research & Executive Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Medical and Research Division have confirmed that several individuals possess a form of natural night vision unrelated to retinal enhancement or thermal imaging.

Instead, the phenomenon is produced by passive Ether emission from living organisms.

This ability has been designated:

## **Ether Vision**

---

# **MECHANISM**

Individuals with sufficiently high Ether concentrations continuously emit an extremely weak form of Ether radiation.

The emission is:

- Invisible to ordinary humans.
    
- Harmless to biological tissue.
    
- Constant while alive.
    
- Non-ionizing.
    
- Extremely difficult to detect with conventional instruments.
    

The emitted Ether behaves similarly to light.

It reflects from surrounding surfaces, allowing certain Ether-sensitive individuals to perceive their environment even in complete darkness.

Researchers often compare the effect to carrying an invisible flashlight that illuminates the world only for those capable of seeing it.

---

---

# **OBSERVED CHARACTERISTICS**

Unlike conventional night vision devices, Ether Vision:

- Does not amplify visible light.
    
- Functions in complete darkness.
    
- Produces no visible glow to ordinary observers.
    
- Is unaffected by smoke or ordinary darkness.
    
- Cannot see through solid objects.
    

The perceived image is described as soft and monochromatic, with objects outlined by reflected Ether rather than visible photons.

Researchers note that bright visible light can temporarily dominate normal vision, causing Ether Vision to become less noticeable rather than disappear.

---

# **FIELD APPLICATIONS**

Ether Vision has proven valuable during:

- Power outages.
    
- Underground exploration.
    
- Blacksite maintenance.
    
- Search and rescue operations.
    
- Nighttime patrols.
    
- Hollow exploration.
    

Personnel possessing the ability often instinctively forget that others cannot see in the same conditions.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Research Division continues to investigate whether prolonged Ether exposure can induce Ether Vision or whether the ability requires a naturally high Ether affinity.

It has always been quietly illuminated by the Ether carried within every living thing.
