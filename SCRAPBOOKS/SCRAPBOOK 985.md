# APERTURE BLACKSITE

## SUBSPACE INVESTIGATION REPORT

### ORIGINAL ARCHITECT TRANSFORMATION SITE

**Classification:** HIGH PRIORITY — SAFEHOUSE  
**Subject:** Neri  
**Location:** Former Transformation Containment Zone  
**Status:** ACTIVE ANOMALOUS ACCESS POINT  
**Investigator:** Neko  
**Secondary Observer:** Guiding Light

---

# I. REVISITING THE SITE

Following confirmation that Neri remains alive within her detached subspace, Neko ordered a re-examination of the location where her failed Architect transformation originally occurred.

The location had previously been treated as a historical medical/anomalous site.

It was believed to contain evidence of Neri's initial transformation:

- Azure patches replacing portions of her skin.
    
- An incompletely formed left wing.
    
- A shattered halo.
    
- Unstable Architect-derived Light.
    
- Severe visual and structural instability.
    
- Subsequent intervention by Guiding Light.
    

The area had remained largely undisturbed.

Until now.

---

# II. THE HOLE

During the inspection, personnel discovered a previously undocumented structural anomaly.

There is a **hole in the wall.**

At first glance, it appears ordinary.

No broken concrete.

No debris.

No signs of impact.

No evidence that something physically punched through the wall.

The opening simply exists.

Behind it is not another Blacksite room.

It is not a corridor.

It is not an exterior environment.

### It is a subspace.

---

# III. THE OTHER SIDE

A remote probe was sent through the opening.

The probe crossed the wall.

Then continued.

There was no transition corridor.

No visible portal effect.

No change in pressure.

No apparent boundary.

The probe simply entered another spatial environment.

And found—

**nothing.**

There were no islands.

No buildings.

No laboratory.

No rope bridges.

No artificial stars.

No furniture.

No conventional floor.

No conventional ceiling.

Just an enormous, empty subspace.

---

# IV. DIFFERENCE FROM NERI'S CURRENT SUBSPACE

The distinction is immediately apparent.

### Neri's known subspace:

- Fifteen floating islands.
    
- Primary inhabited island.
    
- Rope bridges.
    
- Localized gravity.
    
- Zero-gravity recovery region.
    
- Laboratory.
    
- Residential space.
    
- Artificial stars.
    
- Multiple connected sections.
    

### Newly discovered subspace:

- No islands.
    
- No structures.
    
- No apparent inhabitants.
    
- No artificial stellar environment.
    
- No established gravity.
    
- No visible boundaries.
    
- No known endpoint.
    

It appears to be a **raw subspace.**

An empty spatial volume capable of existing independently from ordinary reality.

---

# V. NEKO'S THEORY

Neko spent several hours comparing the spatial characteristics of the empty subspace with archived measurements from Neri's room.

His preliminary conclusion:

> “This isn't another random pocket dimension.”

The empty subspace possesses structural characteristics remarkably similar to Neri's original subspace architecture.

Neko believes it may represent the **underlying space from which Neri's later subspace was constructed.**

In simpler terms:

Neri may not have created her fifteen-island environment from nothing.

She may have taken an existing empty subspace and **built inside it.**

---

# VI. THE TRANSFORMATION CONNECTION

This produces an uncomfortable possibility.

The empty subspace exists at the exact location where Neri's Architect-derived transformation first became unstable.

That transformation included:

- Blue Light replacing sections of her skin.
    
- A partially formed wing.
    
- A broken halo.
    
- Unstable Architect characteristics.
    
- Incomplete manifestation of her abilities.
    

Guiding Light subsequently corrected the transformation.

Neri eventually learned to manipulate subspace herself.

The temporal relationship is therefore significant.

### First:

**Architect Light enters Neri.**

### Then:

**Neri develops unstable Architect characteristics.**

### Later:

**Neri develops subspace manipulation.**

### Eventually:

**Neri creates her fifteen-island subspace.**

The newly discovered empty space exists at the earliest known point in that chain.

---

# VII. GUIDING LIGHT

Guiding Light entered the empty subspace.

She immediately became quiet.

She looked around.

There was nothing to see.

No islands.

No stars.

No structures.

Just empty space.

> “This feels familiar.”

Neko asked:

> “You recognize it?”

Guiding Light shook her head.

> “Not the place.”

She looked toward the entrance.

> “The Light.”

A pause.

> “This is the kind of space Neri learned to manipulate.”

---

# VIII. AZURE PARTICULATE

The investigation became considerably more interesting when UV illumination was activated.

The empty subspace was not completely empty.

Tiny azure particles were present.

Very faint.

Almost invisible.

But unmistakable.

**Neri's dust.**

The particles were not concentrated around a particular object.

There were no footprints.

No conventional trail.

Instead, they existed throughout the empty space.

Some drifted aimlessly.

Others appeared to move toward distant regions of the subspace.

No endpoint could be established.

---

# IX. THE HOLE IS NOT THE DOOR

Neko's previous conclusion regarding Neri's current entrance remains correct.

The apparent doorway identified by Guiding Light still leads nowhere.

This newly discovered hole is different.

### Current understanding:

**Neri's original entrance:**  
Connected to nothing.

**Transformation-site hole:**  
Connected to an empty subspace.

This means the Blacksite now possesses two spatial anomalies that appear related but behave fundamentally differently.

---

# X. THE EMPTY SUBSPACE

The subspace has been temporarily designated:

> **SUBSPACE-Ø**

It is not being treated as Neri's current location.

No evidence currently places Neri physically inside SUBSPACE-Ø.

However, the presence of her Azure Light particulate establishes a connection to her anomalous physiology.

The possibility remains that SUBSPACE-Ø is:

1. The original space used by Neri before constructing her island environment.
    
2. A connected layer beneath her current subspace.
    
3. A staging space through which Neri's subspace was created.
    
4. A residual fragment of Neri's earliest subspace manipulation.
    
5. Something entirely different.
    

Investigation remains ongoing.

---

# XI. THE FIRST SIGN

Approximately forty meters from the entrance, the exploration team discovered a single Azure shard.

It was larger than the surrounding dust.

It floated alone.

Guiding Light approached.

The shard moved.

Not toward her.

**Away from her.**

It drifted deeper into the empty subspace.

Guiding Light followed.

The shard continued moving.

Neko watched the tracking equipment.

The signal remained stable.

Then the shard disappeared into the darkness.

Neko looked at the readings.

> “It didn't disappear.”

Guiding Light turned around.

> “What?”

Neko pointed toward the instruments.

> “It went somewhere.”

---

# XII. CURRENT HYPOTHESIS

The Blacksite now has a possible new avenue of investigation.

The original entrance to Neri's subspace is inaccessible.

However, the old transformation site contains a separate opening into an apparently empty subspace containing **Neri's Azure Light.**

If SUBSPACE-Ø is structurally connected to Neri's current environment, it may provide an alternative route.

Not necessarily to the islands.

Not necessarily to Neri.

But possibly to the **space surrounding them.**

For the first time since Neri disappeared, the Blacksite has something more useful than a location.

It has a direction.

---

## XIII. FINAL NOTE

Guiding Light remained inside SUBSPACE-Ø after the investigation concluded.

She looked back at the opening in the wall.

Then into the empty darkness ahead.

> “Neri built something out of this.”

Neko nodded.

> “Looks like it.”

Guiding Light smiled faintly.

> “Then maybe we can figure out how she did it.”

She looked deeper into the empty subspace.

A few tiny blue particles drifted past her.

> “And maybe we can finally find the way back to her.”

**END REPORT**
