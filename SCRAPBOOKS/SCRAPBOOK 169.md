# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CRITICAL INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – DISAPPEARANCE FOLLOWING FAMILY CONVERSATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The following report documents the final confirmed interaction between Corin Lockwood and Lumia ("Lumi") Lockwood prior to Corin's disappearance.

The conversation was not hostile.

There were no raised voices.

No threats.

No anger.

Instead, it was a conversation in which a misunderstanding took root between a daughter carrying years of trauma and a mother trying to reach her.

Psychological Services emphasize that Corin's disappearance cannot be attributed to a single sentence alone.

Rather, the exchange occurred against the backdrop of longstanding trauma, severe self-hatred, and a persistent belief that she was a burden to those she loved.

---

# **CONTEXT**

In the weeks leading up to the incident, Corin had become increasingly withdrawn.

She spent most of her time outdoors.

She rarely entered SAFEHOUSE.

She frequently declined invitations to meals or social activities.

Although residents continued to keep her company whenever she allowed it, Corin's belief that she was a burden had become increasingly difficult to challenge.

---

# **TRANSCRIPT**

The following exchange was reconstructed from Lumi's statement shortly after the incident.

Corin was sitting outside beneath a tree.

Snow fell quietly around them.

For several minutes, neither spoke.

Then Corin broke the silence.

> **Corin:** "Mom..."

Lumi looked up from the book she had been holding.

> **Corin:** "Am I a burden to you?"

Lumi immediately answered.

> **"No."**

Corin didn't respond.

She simply continued looking at the snow.

Wanting to encourage her daughter to reconnect with others, Lumi continued speaking.

She chose her words carefully.

But not carefully enough.

> **Lumi:** "I just want my little girl to come back."

Corin's expression changed.

Almost imperceptibly.

She looked down at her own hands.

After a long silence, she quietly asked,

> "...So..."

> "...the one that's here isn't enough?"

Lumi immediately realized how Corin had interpreted her words.

She reached toward her.

> "Corin, that's not what I—"

Corin gently stood.

She gave a small, tired smile.

> "...I understand."

Lumi stood as well.

> "Please... let me explain."

Corin shook her head.

> "...I'm sorry."

She turned and began walking away through the falling snow.

Lumi followed for several steps.

> "Corin!"

Corin didn't turn around.

Those were the last words Lumi spoke to her.

---

# **AFTERMATH**

Corin did not return that evening.

Or the next morning.

Her usual places were empty.

The library.

The lakeside.

The botanical gardens.

The rooftops.

Her office.

All vacant.

Her backpack remained in SAFEHOUSE.

Her room was untouched.

No note was found.

---

# **LUMI'S STATEMENT**

Lumi voluntarily provided her account to Psychological Services.

Throughout the interview she repeatedly returned to the same thought.

> "I wanted to tell her..."

She stopped speaking.

After several moments, she continued.

> "...that I missed the little girl who used to laugh so easily."

A tear fell onto the report in front of her.

> "I never meant..."

She covered her face.

> "...to make her think I didn't love the daughter standing in front of me."

Psychological Services documented significant guilt in Lumi's statement while noting there was no indication of malicious intent.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services conclude that Corin's interpretation of the conversation was heavily influenced by her pre-existing trauma and negative self-beliefs.

Individuals experiencing chronic trauma and severe self-hatred may interpret even affectionate statements through the belief that they are unwanted or fundamentally changed beyond acceptance.

The exchange should therefore be understood within the broader context of Corin's longstanding psychological condition, rather than as the sole cause of her disappearance.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin Lockwood's whereabouts remain unknown.

Project SAFEHOUSE, Heaven's Blacksite, and the Krampus Compliance Authority have initiated welfare search procedures.

Lumi has remained at SAFEHOUSE.

Every evening, she sits near the entrance for a while before going inside.

Not because she believes Corin will certainly return that night.

But because, if she does...

Lumi wants the first thing her daughter sees to be someone waiting for her.

**End of Critical Incident Report**
