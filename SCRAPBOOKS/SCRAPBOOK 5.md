# **OVERVIEW**

Following multiple after-action reviews involving heavily armored hostiles, reinforced barricades, and anomalously durable targets, Executive Secretary **Remielle "Cinder"** submitted a request for a dedicated breaching impact weapon.

The result was the **KCA-BRH-12 "PILEDRIVER."**

Unlike conventional breaching hammers, the PILEDRIVER integrates a compact, magazine-fed 12-gauge firearm into a reinforced sledgehammer frame. The firearm is designed to discharge only after the hammer has already made contact with the target, delivering an impact and a close-range blast in rapid succession.

The platform is intended for specialized breaching and emergency operations and is issued exclusively to Remielle.

---

# **DESIGN PHILOSOPHY**

The development team's objective was straightforward:

> _"If one hit isn't enough, make the first hit count even more."_

The PILEDRIVER is not designed to replace standard firearms or Cinder's multifunction combat briefcase.

Instead, it fills a niche role where an immediate, forceful breach is required against heavily reinforced obstacles.

# **CONSTRUCTION**

## **Hammer Head**

Machined from a dense Nekonium alloy with a hardened striking face.

Designed to withstand repeated impacts against:

- Reinforced doors.
    
- Concrete.
    
- Steel barriers.
    
- Heavy locks.
    
- Other hardened obstacles encountered during rescue or containment operations.
    

The striking face incorporates a protected muzzle positioned to direct the firearm's discharge only after the hammer face has already contacted the target.

---

## **Receiver**

Housed within the hammer assembly.

Contains:

- Five-round magazine well.
    
- Firing mechanism.
    
- Impact interlock.
    
- Manual inspection port.
    

The system remains mechanically locked until its safety conditions are satisfied.

---

## **Handle**

A shock-absorbing composite handle with textured grips provides improved control during repeated impacts.

The balance point is intentionally located close to the hammer head to maximize striking power.

---

# **OPERATING MECHANISM**

The weapon employs a sequential operating cycle.

1. The operator delivers a hammer strike.
    
2. The hammer face makes solid contact with the intended surface.
    
3. If conditions are met, the operator may actuate the internal firing control.
    
4. The integrated firearm discharges.
    
5. The action is manually cycled before the next use.
    

This sequence prevents the firearm from functioning independently of the hammer's intended breaching role.

---

# **INTENDED ROLE**

The PILEDRIVER is assigned for situations including:

- Emergency breaching.
    
- Defeating reinforced entry points.
    
- Destroying hardened locking mechanisms.
    
- Creating access routes during rescue operations.
    
- Neutralizing heavily armored barriers that resist conventional breaching tools.
    

It is not intended as a general-purpose service weapon.

---

# **TRAINING REQUIREMENTS**

Because the platform combines a breaching tool with an integrated firearm, certification requires completion of additional KCA training.

Emphasis is placed on:

- Safe handling.
    
- Target identification.
    
- Controlled breaching techniques.
    
- Structural awareness.
    
- Team coordination.
    

---

# **FIELD EVALUATION**

Test operators consistently noted that the platform excels in its intended breaching role but requires considerable strength and specialized training to use effectively.

One evaluator summarized it as:
