# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL RECOVERY / FIELD INTERVENTION REPORT**

# **CORIN — THE SUNFLOWER FIELD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Intervention:** **Controlled Environmental Relocation**  
**Location:** Z-556 STARLANDS  
**Status:** **Successful**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

After twenty-five weeks without confirmed sleep and a severe deterioration in her psychological condition, C.O.R.E. personnel had reached the conclusion that conventional intervention was ineffective.

Corin's body was functioning.

Her Ether was functioning.

Her mind was not.

The solution was eventually discovered through a much simpler question:

> **"Where would Corin want to be more than anywhere else?"**

The answer was a sunflower field.

Fortunately, Star knew exactly where to find one.

---

# **THE STARLANDS**

Star identified a large sunflower field within **Z-556 Starlands**.

The field is located far from populated areas and major structures.

It is surrounded by open grassland beneath the unusual sky of the Starlands.

Thousands of sunflowers grow across the area.

There is little noise.

No laboratories.

No Blacksite.

No machinery.

No containment equipment.

Nothing demanding Corin's attention.

Just sunlight, flowers, grass, and open space.

It was exactly what she needed.

---

# **PREPARATION**

The Architects initially considered bringing Corin there immediately.

Star advised against making the trip feel like a medical procedure.

Corin was therefore not told:

> **"We're taking you somewhere to fix you."**

Instead, she was simply told:

> **"Come with us."**

Corin was suspicious.

She asked where.

Nobody gave her a detailed answer.

Eventually, she agreed.

Orphie accompanied her.

So did Guiding Light, Star, Lumi, and several SAFEHOUSE residents.

---

# **ARRIVAL**

The transition into the Starlands was quiet.

Corin stepped through the dimensional passage.

She stopped.

Nobody spoke.

In front of her stretched an enormous sunflower field.

Flowers extended toward the horizon.

The breeze moved through them in waves.

The sunlight illuminated the field.

Corin stared.

Her ears slowly lifted.

Her two tails became still.

For the first time in weeks, she did not look afraid.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin walked forward.

Slowly.

She reached the first row of sunflowers.

She touched one of the petals.

Then another.

Her expression changed.

There was no panic.

No anger.

No confusion.

Just recognition.

> **"...Sunflowers."**

Star smiled.

> **"I remembered."**

Corin looked at her.

> **"You remembered?"**

> **"Of course."**

---

# **THE FIELD**

Corin eventually walked deeper into the field.

She did not speak much.

She did not need to.

Her wings gradually opened.

The sunlight passed through their semi-transparent structures.

Their feathers glowed softly.

Corin stood among the flowers with her wings spread behind her.

For once, she did not use them to hide.

---

# **THE FIRST SIGN**

After several minutes, Corin became visibly tired.

Not the strange exhaustion she had experienced for months.

Real tiredness.

Her eyelids became heavy.

She sat beneath one of the larger sunflowers.

Orphie sat beside her.

Corin leaned against her shoulder.

Her eyes closed.

Everyone became completely silent.

---

# **SLEEP**

Corin fell asleep.

Not unconsciousness forced by an Architect.

Not Ether-induced stasis.

Not pretending.

**Actual sleep.**

Her breathing became slow.

Her wings relaxed.

Her tails curled naturally beside her.

Orphie stared at her for several seconds.

She was afraid to move.

Guiding Light quietly whispered:

> **"...She's sleeping."**

Nobody answered.

They were all afraid that speaking too loudly would wake her.

---

# **THE FIRST SLEEP IN TWENTY-FIVE WEEKS**

Corin slept beneath the sunflowers for several hours.

No nightmares were detected.

No anomalous instability occurred.

Her Ether remained stable.

Her heartbeat remained calm.

Her body finally entered the state it had been desperately seeking.

Rest.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie remained beside her the entire time.

She eventually leaned back against the same sunflower.

She watched Corin sleep.

For the first time in months, she did not have to wonder whether Corin was pretending.

She was actually asleep.

Orphie quietly smiled.

> **"You really needed this."**

Corin did not answer.

She was too deeply asleep.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light observed the situation from several meters away.

She had spent months trying to find a way to repair Corin.

In the end, the Architects had not needed to alter her.

They simply needed to give her somewhere she wanted to be.

Guiding Light quietly said:

> **"Maybe she didn't need to be fixed."**

Star looked at her.

> **"She needed somewhere safe enough to rest."**

Guiding Light nodded.

---

# **RECOVERY**

Corin remained in the Starlands for an extended period.

She slept repeatedly.

She ate when she was hungry.

She walked through the sunflower field.

She flew again.

She spent time with Orphie.

She occasionally sat alone, but no longer appeared compelled to hide.

Her psychological state did not immediately return to normal.

However, the severe deterioration stopped.

Her thoughts became clearer.

Her emotional responses became more stable.

The hallucinations and disorganized behavior diminished substantially after adequate sleep was restored.

---

# **THE KITTEN FORM**

After several days of proper rest, something unexpected happened.

Corin fell asleep beside Orphie beneath the sunflowers.

When she woke—

she was still in her normal form.

The miniature feline transformation did not return.

C.O.R.E. continues to consider the ability inaccessible.

However, Corin no longer appears distressed by its absence.

For now, she has something more important.

She can sleep.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

For twenty-five weeks, everyone tried to find a way to fix Corin.

The Architects searched for anomalous causes.

Medical personnel searched for physiological explanations.

The SAFEHOUSE residents tried to keep her company.

Orphie tried to stay beside her.

None of it was enough.

Then someone asked a simpler question.

> **"Where does Corin actually want to be?"**

The answer was a sunflower field.

Star knew one.

And beneath thousands of golden flowers, Corin finally closed her eyes.

No one forced her.

No one repaired her.

No one erased her memories.

She simply found somewhere beautiful enough to feel safe.

And finally—

**she slept.**

**End of Psychological Recovery / Field Intervention Report**
