# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – MEDICAL RESEARCH DIVISION**

### **PHYSIOLOGICAL OBSERVATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: DIALYN – HAIR PIGMENTATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Medical Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

A common misconception among newly hired personnel is that Dialyn dyes her hair.

This is incorrect.

The distinct coloration is entirely natural and is a permanent physiological consequence of prolonged Ether exposure during her nine-year confinement inside a Hollow.

The pigmentation has remained unchanged since her rescue.

---

# **DESCRIPTION**

Dialyn's hair is naturally divided almost perfectly down the center.

- **Right Half:** Black
    
- **Left Half:** White
    

The transition is exceptionally clean, with no visible gradient between the two colors.

Microscopic examination confirms the pigmentation exists within the hair itself.

No traces of artificial dye, bleaching agents, or cosmetic treatment have ever been detected.

---

# **MEDICAL FINDINGS**

Following her rescue, Medical Division documented several long-term effects of Dialyn's prolonged exposure to Ether.

Among the permanent changes were:

- Naturally bifurcated hair pigmentation.
    
- An abnormally high resistance to Ether corruption.
    
- Minor alterations in iris reflectivity under high Ether concentrations.
    
- Several benign anomalous phenomena associated with her presence.
    

Researchers believe the hair pigmentation resulted from Ether interacting with developing hair follicles during her childhood and adolescence.

No adverse health effects have been linked directly to the coloration.

---

# **PERSONNEL MISUNDERSTANDINGS**

New employees have repeatedly asked Dialyn questions including:

> "Who does your hair?"

> "What dye do you use?"

> "How long does it take to maintain?"

Dialyn's usual response is a polite smile.

> "...It's natural."

Most people initially assume she is joking.

Medical Division has confirmed she is not.

# **DIALYN'S COMMENT**

When asked whether she had ever considered dyeing her hair a single color, Dialyn gently held one of the white strands between her fingers.

She thought for a long moment before answering.

> "...No."

A small smile followed.

> "...This is part of me."

Another pause.

> "...It's where I've been."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Medical Division has determined that Dialyn's black-and-white hair requires no treatment and poses no health concerns.

To scientists, it is an unusual physiological adaptation.

To those who know her story, it is something more.

Every white strand.

Every black strand.

Marks a child who survived nine years where no child should have.

Dialyn herself has never attempted to hide it.

She wears her hair exactly as it grew.

Not as a badge of suffering.

But as quiet proof that she lived.
