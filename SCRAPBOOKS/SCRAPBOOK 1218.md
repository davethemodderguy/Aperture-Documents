# 1. STATUS CHANGE

The **Tachyon Industries Spearhead** has been assigned a unique status within the Limited Merchandise program.

Unlike previous Tachyon Industries products, the Spearhead is:

> **PERMANENTLY AVAILABLE FOR ACQUISITION.**

It is not a limited-number release.

It is not a lottery item.

It is not a one-time competition reward.

It is not subject to a temporary promotional window.

Provided an applicant satisfies the required conditions, the Spearhead may be acquired **at any time.**

Neri has explicitly stated:

> **“If you want one, earn one.”**

---

# 2. WHY THE SPEARHEAD IS PERMANENT

Neri's reasoning is straightforward.

The Spearhead was designed as Tachyon Industries' general-purpose modular assault-rifle platform.

Its purpose is not to be an exceptionally rare collector's item.

It is intended to be:

- configurable;
    
- accessible to qualified personnel;
    
- compatible with a broad range of approved accessories;
    
- suitable for different operational preferences;
    
- and continuously supported by Tachyon Industries.
    

The permanent availability therefore reflects the rifle's intended role.

---

# 3. THE CATCH

Permanent availability does **not** mean easy availability.

The acquisition requirements are deliberately severe.

Neri's position is that anyone who wants a permanent Tachyon Industries assault rifle should demonstrate that they are capable of responsibly possessing one.

The exact requirements are maintained in the restricted acquisition registry.

They are substantially more demanding than the requirements for ordinary Blacksite weapons.

C.O.R.E. summarizes the policy as:

> **“Always available.”**

followed immediately by:

> **“Extremely difficult to obtain.”**

---

# 4. ACQUISITION PRINCIPLE

The Spearhead does not operate on scarcity.

It operates on **qualification.**

An unsuccessful applicant does not lose the opportunity permanently.

They may attempt acquisition again after satisfying the applicable waiting period and requalifying.

Consequently:

> **There will always be another chance to earn a Spearhead.**

There will simply not necessarily be an easy chance.

---

# 5. UNDERBARREL CONFIGURATION PROGRAM

One of the Spearhead's most extensive customization categories is its **underbarrel system.**

Neri intentionally provided considerable space and compatibility for underbarrel equipment.

The approved Tachyon Industries configuration catalog currently includes numerous underbarrel options.

These include fictionalized platform-specific:

- compact launchers;
    
- utility systems;
    
- specialized support modules;
    
- breaching-oriented equipment;
    
- less-lethal systems;
    
- illumination systems;
    
- training devices;
    
- and other mission-specific accessories.
    

Exact configurations remain subject to C.O.R.E. and MSAC authorization.

The Spearhead is therefore capable of becoming considerably more than a conventional rifle depending on its approved configuration.

---

# 6. NERI'S DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Neri was specifically asked why she provided so much space for underbarrel equipment.

Her response:

> **“Because people keep asking for them.”**

The interviewer asked whether she had considered limiting the number of compatible systems.

Neri:

> **“No.”**

> **“Why?”**

Neri gestured toward the rifle.

> **“There's room.”**

# 8. TACHYON MERCH STORE

The Spearhead has been added to the **Tachyon Merch Store** as a permanent catalog item.

Unlike functioning Limited Merchandise displayed behind the secured display wall, acquisition remains controlled through the Tachyon Industries qualification system.

The store may provide:

- product documentation;
    
- approved accessory catalogs;
    
- maintenance documentation;
    
- configuration references;
    
- acquisition information;
    
- application materials.
    

The store does **not** allow unqualified personnel to simply walk in and purchase a Spearhead.

---

# 9. PERMANENT DOES NOT MEAN UNRESTRICTED

C.O.R.E. emphasizes the distinction.

**Permanent availability:**

> The product will remain available indefinitely.

**Unrestricted availability:**

> Anyone can acquire one.

The Spearhead has the first status.

It does **not** have the second.

---

# 12. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The Spearhead is now the most accessible Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise product **in terms of availability** while simultaneously being one of the more demanding products **in terms of qualification.**

This appears intentional.

Neri does not want the Spearhead to become an unattainable collector's item.

She also does not want it distributed casually.

The resulting model is simple:

> **The Spearhead will not run out.**

> **The opportunity will not expire.**

> **The requirements will not become easy.**

And once qualified:

> **The applicant can acquire one whenever the acquisition window is open.**
