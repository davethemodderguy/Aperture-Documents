# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Clover Luminare's anomalous four-leaf clovers possess an additional property previously undocumented.

They are **lucky.**

Not metaphorically.

Not culturally.

Not because residents believe four-leaf clovers are lucky.

The plants produced by Clover's anomalous ability inherit a localized version of **Clover's passive luck field.**

A person carrying one of Clover's four-leaf clovers receives a temporary beneficial-luck effect.

The effect lasts approximately:

> **5 HOURS**

Afterward, the clover wilts and loses its anomalous properties.

The person carrying it does not retain the effect.

---

# 1. ABILITY CONFIRMATION

Clover can grow four-leaf clovers from ordinary soil without requiring seeds.

Previously, C.O.R.E. classified these plants as anomalous botanical manifestations.

Further testing has established that they also carry a secondary anomalous property.

Each mature clover contains a localized version of Clover's passive luck field.

The effect is substantially weaker than Clover's own personal field.

It is also temporary.

---

# 2. EFFECT ON OTHER PEOPLE

Unlike Clover's personal anomaly, which is centered on Clover herself, the anomalous clover transfers its beneficial effect to whoever possesses it.

The recipient does not need to:

- believe in luck;
    
- know what the clover does;
    
- know Clover personally;
    
- activate the clover;
    
- or perform any deliberate action.
    

Simply carrying the clover is sufficient.

The effect appears to remain centered on the individual carrying it.

It does not spread to nearby people.

It does not create a shared luck field.

One clover benefits one carrier.

---

# 3. DURATION

The effect lasts approximately five hours.

The clover itself remains healthy for most of this period.

Near the end of the five-hour window, visible deterioration begins.

The leaves gradually lose their firmness.

The stem weakens.

The plant eventually wilts.

Once wilted:

> **The anomalous effect is gone.**

The clover does not recover when watered.

It does not regain its anomalous properties.

It is effectively expended.

---

# 4. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Clover herself does **not** lose her own luck when one of her clovers wilts.

Her personal anomalous field remains continuously active.

The clovers are therefore better understood as:

> **Temporary external manifestations of Clover's luck.**

They are not pieces of Clover.

They do not drain her.

They do not injure her.

Growing one does not appear to reduce the strength of her own anomaly.

---

# 5. OBSERVED USE

The first confirmed accidental demonstration occurred when Clover gave a four-leaf clover to a SAFEHOUSE resident.

The recipient carried it for several hours.

During that period, several unusually favorable circumstances occurred.

None were individually impossible.

Together, however, the pattern was statistically unusual.

The recipient eventually asked:

> "Does this thing actually work?"

Clover answered:

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Apparently."

The clover wilted later that evening.

---

# 6. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake was given one.

He immediately attached it to his clothing.

Clover watched him.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Don't lose it."

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Okay."

Several hours later, Jake was sitting on Clover's couch.

The clover remained attached.

Clover noticed it beginning to wilt.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "It's almost done."

Jake looked down.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Can I have another one?"

Clover smiled.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "When that one finishes."

Jake nodded.

---

# 7. CLOVER'S SIDE OF THE ROOM

The discovery has altered the purpose of the clover garden inside the shared room.

Clover's side previously contained four-leaf clovers primarily because she enjoyed growing them.

It now functions as a small renewable source of temporary luck-bearing plants.

Several mature clovers are allowed to grow naturally.

Clover does not harvest all of them.

Some remain simply because she likes having them around.

Jake's blue flowers continue growing on his side.

The two anomalous plant types therefore coexist within the shared residence.

---

# 8. NO COMMERCIALIZATION

C.O.R.E. explicitly recommends against turning Clover's clovers into a commercial product.

They are:

- temporary;
    
- anomalous;
    
- individually produced;
    
- dependent on Clover's ability;
    
- and not guaranteed to produce a specific outcome.
    

Most importantly, Clover is a person.

She is not a botanical resource.

No requests should be made for mass production.

---

# 9. RESIDENT RESPONSE

The discovery has predictably resulted in increased interest from SAFEHOUSE residents.

Clover's response has been consistent.

If she has a spare clover and wishes to give one away, she may.

If she does not, she does not.

She is not obligated to provide one because somebody wants additional luck.

When asked whether she would grow a large quantity for the SAFEHOUSE, Clover answered:

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Why?"

The requester explained.

Clover considered this.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Five hours isn't that long."

Then she returned to her desk.

---

# 10. LUCK LIMITATIONS

The clovers do not guarantee specific outcomes.

They cannot:

- make someone invincible;
    
- prevent every possible injury;
    
- guarantee victory;
    
- create impossible objects;
    
- override established physical conditions;
    
- or ensure that every event goes perfectly.
    

Their effect is better described as:

> **A temporary tendency toward favorable outcomes.**

The exact manifestation depends on circumstances.

Sometimes the benefit may be obvious.

Sometimes it may be extremely subtle.

Sometimes the best possible outcome may simply be that something bad does not happen.

---

# 11. FIVE-HOUR LIMIT

C.O.R.E. has confirmed that the duration is not based on the individual recipient.

The same approximate five-hour window has been observed across multiple trials.

At the end of the period, the clover wilts.

The effect terminates with it.

There is no withdrawal.

There is no rebound misfortune.

There is simply:

> **No more anomalous luck.**

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Clover's four-leaf clovers represent a previously undocumented extension of her anomaly.

Her personal luck field can, under certain circumstances, be temporarily externalized into a physical plant.

The resulting clover can provide another individual with a weaker version of Clover's beneficial luck field for approximately five hours.

Afterward, the clover wilts.

The phenomenon is currently classified as:

> **LUCK-BEARING BOTANICAL MANIFESTATION**

The discovery also provides an interesting explanation for why Clover's personal symbol has such strong meaning to her.

She does not merely carry a four-leaf clover.

She can grow one.

And, for five hours, she can let somebody else carry a little bit of her luck.
