---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

It took Aperture personnel approximately **three weeks** to properly understand Neri.

The initial assessment was straightforward.

Neri was a rescued Seal Thiren who enjoyed swimming, loved fish, quickly became attached to Bao and Frost, and generally presented as a quiet, somewhat unusual but friendly resident.

This assessment was incomplete.

Neri is affectionate.

Neri is friendly.

Neri is easily attached to people she considers friends.

Neri is also, by conventional psychological terminology, **yandere-adjacent**.

Once Neri considers someone part of her social circle, she develops an unusually strong protective attachment toward them.

Threats to those individuals are not treated by Neri as ordinary interpersonal conflicts.

They are treated as **threats to be removed.**

This distinction was not discovered through routine observation.

It was discovered when Neri encountered a facility bully.

# 3. PERSONAL AESTHETICS

Neri strongly favors three colors:

- **Azure**
    
- **Blue**
    
- **White**
    

These colors appear throughout her wardrobe and personal belongings.

Her clothing, accessories, and other possessions frequently incorporate some combination of the three.

C.O.R.E. personnel have noted that the color preference appears to be genuine rather than a deliberate attempt at intimidation.

There is currently no evidence that Neri associates the colors with violence.

This is fortunate.

---

# 4. THE HAIRCLIPS

Neri possesses **three hairclips**.

Two are crossed diagonally over her bangs.

The third is a decorative hairclip shaped like a **fish bone**.

The fish-bone clip is harmless.

The other two are not.

### HAIRCLIP-01

**Type:** Concealed edged weapon  
**Purpose:** Hair retention / emergency defense  
**Status:** Extremely sharp

### HAIRCLIP-02

**Type:** Concealed edged weapon  
**Purpose:** Hair retention / emergency defense  
**Status:** Extremely sharp

The clips are sufficiently small that they are frequently mistaken for ordinary hair accessories.

They are, in reality, compact knives.

Neri has demonstrated that she can remove either clip from her hair and deploy it almost immediately.

This fact has been added to SAFEHOUSE security training.

---

# 5. THE INCIDENT

## INCIDENT ID: SH-██-NERI-01

**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE Residential Facility  
**INVOLVED:** Neri, SAFEHOUSE Archivist, facility bully  
**RESULT:** One serious injury; subject subsequently revived  
**CURRENT STATUS:** Resolved

Prior to the incident, Neri was walking through the facility alongside an Archivist.

A known facility bully approached them.

The Archivist was shoved.

Neri was subsequently pushed and verbally antagonized.

Neri initially did nothing.

She simply looked at the individual.

The bully continued.

Neri then walked toward him.

The Archivist reportedly assumed she intended to confront him verbally.

She did not.

Neri stopped approximately one meter away.

She reached up.

She removed one of the crossed hairclips.

The next sequence occurred extremely quickly.

Neri attacked the bully with the hairclip, causing severe neck and lower-limb injuries before disengaging.

She then began laughing.

Not giggling.

Not nervous laughter.

**Maniacal laughter.**

Witness statement:

> "She was smiling."

> "She wasn't scared."

> "She looked genuinely happy that the threat was gone."

The bully was subsequently transported for emergency treatment.

Due to the severity of his injuries, standard medical intervention was insufficient.

---

# 6. REVIVAL

Lucy Merryweather was contacted.

The injured individual was subsequently **revived through Banlands-related intervention**.

Physical survival was achieved.

Psychological recovery has been considerably less successful.

The individual has developed an intense fear of seals.

When asked to explain why, the response was:

> "THE SEAL HAD KNIVES."

This statement is technically accurate.

The subject currently refuses to enter areas occupied by Neri.

Neri has been informed.

Neri's response:

> "Why?"

---

# 7. BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS

Following the incident, C.O.R.E. conducted a comprehensive review of Neri's previous behavior.

Several previously overlooked details became significant.

Neri's friendliness is genuine.

So is her affection.

However, her protective instincts appear to operate on a very simple internal framework:

**Friend → Protect.**

**Threat to friend → Remove threat.**

There is little evidence that Neri naturally considers proportionality during moments of perceived danger.

She does not necessarily seek violence.

She simply does not appear particularly hesitant to use it when she believes someone she cares about is being harmed.

This includes herself.

---

# 8. UPDATED RISK ASSESSMENT

Neri is **not considered hostile toward Aperture personnel.**

She is not considered generally dangerous during normal interaction.

Personnel should therefore **not treat Neri as an aggressive entity.**

However:

> **Do not bully her friends.**

> **Do not deliberately harm her friends.**

> **Do not threaten her friends as a joke.**

> **Do not attempt to test whether she is actually willing to use the hairclips.**

> **Do not corner her.**

> **Do not mistake her friendliness for helplessness.**

This applies particularly to individuals Neri has explicitly categorized as:

- "Mom" — Bao
    
- "Big sis" — Frost
    
- "Bunny" — Alice
    
- Friend / close friend — various SAFEHOUSE residents
    

---

# 9. PERSONNEL GUIDANCE

Personnel are encouraged to interact positively with Neri.

Getting on her **soft side** is considered the preferred method of maintaining a safe and comfortable relationship.

Recommended activities include:

- swimming;
    
- sharing food;
    
- discussing fish;
    
- complimenting her accessories;
    
- allowing her access to appropriate aquatic facilities;
    
- spending time with her;
    
- respecting her personal space;
    
- treating her friends appropriately.
    

Particularly effective:

**Fish.**

Neri's affection toward individuals who bring her fish has been repeatedly documented.

Neko has summarized the situation as:

> "She's actually really nice."

> "Just don't give her a reason to think you're hurting someone she likes."

> "Also the hairclips are knives."

---

# 10. CURRENT RESIDENTIAL POLICY

Following the incident, the following policy has been enacted:

### NERI PROTECTIVE BOUNDARY

**Personnel are prohibited from bullying, intentionally harming, threatening, or deliberately provoking any individual Neri has identified as a friend or family member.**

Personnel are also prohibited from deliberately provoking Neri herself.

This policy is not because Neri has been classified as an enemy.

It exists because C.O.R.E. has determined that **preventing a conflict is significantly easier than explaining another incident to Lucy Merryweather.**

# 11. ARCHIVIST COMMENTARY

The most concerning aspect of the incident was not the weapon.

It was the realization that Neri had been carrying two extremely sharp knives in her hair for the entire duration of her residency and nobody had questioned them.

The third clip is still a fish bone.

Neri insists this is important.

When asked why, she responded:

> "It's fish."

The Archivist asked why she needed a fish bone clip.

Neri:

> "I like fish."

The Archivist then asked why the other two clips were knives.

Neri stared at them.

> "Protection."

The interview ended shortly afterward.
