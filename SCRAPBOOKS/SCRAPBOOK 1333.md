# 1. ABILITY DESIGNATION

Following analysis of the Woodland Mercenary Incident, C.O.R.E. has assigned Jake's newly documented stellar ability the provisional designation:

> **PULVERIZATION**

Pulverization is Jake's ability to release a highly dangerous wave of anomalous energy pulses capable of disrupting biological matter at the cellular level.

Affected biological cells are destabilized and destroyed.

The resulting material manifests as:

> **Fine blue stellar dust.**

---

# 2. MECHANISM

Pulverization does not function as a conventional projectile.

Jake does not launch a physical object at a target.

Instead, he generates a localized wave of anomalous energy pulses.

When the wave encounters biological matter, the pulses disrupt the structural integrity of individual cells.

Continued exposure results in progressive cellular destruction.

At sufficient intensity:

> **The biological structure can no longer maintain itself.**

The affected matter collapses into blue particulate.

C.O.R.E. has confirmed that this is consistent with Jake's stellar nature.

---

# 3. VISUAL SIGNATURE

Pulverization produces a distinctive visual effect.

Affected biological matter rapidly transitions into:

**Azure-blue particulate.**

The dust is faintly luminous.

It may remain suspended in the air momentarily before settling.

The phenomenon resembles fine ash, except that the particles emit a subtle stellar glow.

No conventional residue associated with biological decomposition has been observed.

---

# 4. OBSERVED APPLICATION

During the Woodland incident, Jake demonstrated three distinct applications.

### PHASE I — LIMB DISRUPTION

Jake generated a pulse wave that affected the hostile personnel's limbs and carried weapons.

The affected biological material converted into blue particulate.

Their weapons were similarly rendered nonfunctional.

### PHASE II — TARGETED DISRUPTION

A second wave affected the heads of remaining hostile personnel.

The effect was immediate.

### PHASE III — COMPLETE PULVERIZATION

Jake subsequently released a larger pulse.

The remaining biological structures were completely annihilated.

The resulting scene contained primarily:

- blue glowing particulate;
    
- clothing remnants;
    
- and footwear.
    

---

# 5. CONTROL

Jake appears capable of controlling the **scope and direction** of Pulverization.

This is particularly significant.

The ability does not appear to be a constantly active field.

Jake must intentionally initiate it.

He can therefore remain physically close to other residents without automatically affecting them.

His ability appears to respond directly to his intent.

---

# 6. EMOTIONAL RESPONSE

Jake demonstrated an unusual lack of panic during the initial incident.

He did not display uncontrolled activation.

He did not lose awareness.

He did not indiscriminately attack nearby individuals.

He identified the hostile personnel as a threat and acted against them.

Afterward, he simply walked outside.

This suggests that Pulverization is not merely an uncontrolled stellar reaction.

Jake can deliberately employ it.

---

# 7. WARNING

Despite its apparent precision, Pulverization is classified as an **extremely dangerous anomalous ability.**

Personnel are not permitted to request demonstrations.

No recreational testing is authorized.

No resident is to intentionally provoke Jake into activating the ability.

C.O.R.E. specifically recommends against treating the ability as entertainment merely because its visual effect resembles glowing blue dust.

It is still the complete destruction of biological matter.

---

# 8. JAKE'S OWN DESCRIPTION

When asked what he called the ability, Jake reportedly shrugged.

> **Jake:**  
> “I don't know.”

Neri later suggested the name **Pulverization**.

Jake considered it.

> **Jake:**  
> “Okay.”

The name was subsequently adopted into C.O.R.E. documentation.

---

# 9. STELLAR ORIGIN

The ability is believed to originate from Jake's identity as a literal star.

His human manifestation possesses only a fraction of the physical characteristics expected from his true stellar nature.

Pulverization may therefore represent one of the ways Jake's stellar energy expresses itself through his human-form body.

Further investigation is ongoing.

No attempt should be made to determine the theoretical maximum output.
