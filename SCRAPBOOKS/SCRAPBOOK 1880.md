## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake Akana has undergone an unusual physiological alteration following exposure to Ether.

Unlike conventional Ether contamination cases, Jake did not transform into an Ether Walker or an Ethereal.

Instead, his body underwent an apparently stable anomalous adaptation.

The most visible changes include:

- Golden and sky-blue gradient stripes appearing on his left cheek.
    
- His eyes adopting the same golden and sky-blue coloration.
    
- Acquisition of an apparent ability to cure Ether Contamination in other affected individuals.
    
- Significant improvement in the efficiency of his existing self-revival capability.
    

The newly acquired curative property is comparable to the previously documented ability possessed by Belle Merryweather.

This development places Jake among an exceptionally small group of individuals capable of reversing Ether Contamination rather than succumbing to it.

**Preliminary conclusion:** Jake's response to Ether exposure represents an anomalous adaptation, not conventional contamination-induced transformation.

---

## II. EXPOSURE AND TRANSFORMATION

Following Ether exposure, medical personnel initially anticipated the possibility of conventional contamination progression.

Under ordinary circumstances, sufficiently severe Ether Contamination may result in the transformation of an affected individual into an Ether Walker or Ethereal.

Jake did not follow this progression.

No such transformation was observed.

Instead, the exposure was followed by the appearance of distinctive markings on his left cheek.

These markings consist of stripes with a gradient between golden and sky-blue coloration. His eyes subsequently exhibited the same color combination.

The changes appear integrated into his existing appearance rather than behaving like an external infection or temporary visual projection.

No evidence has been provided that Jake's personality, identity, or underlying consciousness has been replaced.

He remains Jake Akana.

The medical team has therefore classified the event as an **Ether-Associated Anomalous Adaptation** pending further investigation.

---

## III. PHYSICAL MANIFESTATIONS

### A. Facial Markings

Golden and sky-blue gradient stripes have appeared on Jake's left cheek.

The markings are visually distinct and constitute the most immediately recognizable indicator of his post-exposure condition.

Their exact composition and function remain unknown.

### B. Ocular Alteration

Jake's eyes now bear the same golden and sky-blue coloration as the markings.

The change is considered part of the same adaptation.

No assumption should be made that the alteration impairs his vision or changes his ability to recognize people.

### C. Existing Characteristics

Jake retains his established identity and recognizable characteristics.

His folded Scottish Fold ears, white hair, feline traits, and affectionate behavior remain associated with him.

Personnel should not interpret the new markings as evidence that Jake has become hostile or dangerous.

---

## IV. CURATIVE CAPABILITY — ETHER CONTAMINATION

<badge color="success">Anomalous curative capability confirmed by report</badge>

The most significant discovery is Jake's apparent ability to cure Ether Contamination.

This capability is comparable to that of Belle Merryweather, who possesses a similar curative property.

Rather than undergoing the conventional transformation associated with Ether exposure, Jake has developed the ability to reverse contamination in affected individuals.

This represents a major medical development.

### Research Implications

If the ability can be reliably demonstrated and understood, Jake may provide an additional means of treating Ether-contaminated personnel and other affected individuals.

However, several questions remain unanswered:

1. Does Jake require physical contact to administer the cure?
    
2. Can he cure different stages or severities of Ether Contamination?
    
3. Is the ability consciously activated, or does it occur automatically?
    
4. Does curing another individual impose a physical or anomalous cost on Jake?
    
5. Is the capability identical to Belle's, or merely similar in outcome?
    

Until these questions are answered, personnel must not assume that Jake can cure every possible Ether-related condition.

**Important:** Jake is not to be treated as a medical instrument. His ability does not establish an obligation to expose himself to contaminated individuals or perform repeated curative interventions.

His willingness to help others must not be exploited.

---

## V. TRANSCENDENCE — IMPROVED SELF-REVIVAL

A second major alteration concerns Jake's existing self-revival capability.

Prior to Ether exposure, Jake possessed an anomalous revival mechanism associated with his Transcendence.

His previous recovery process could involve substantial reconstruction of his body following death.

Following the adaptation, this capability has become considerably more efficient.

The improvement suggests that Jake's Ether-associated changes have affected not only his resistance to conventional contamination but also his ability to recover from otherwise fatal conditions.

However, the precise improvements have not yet been quantified.

C.O.R.E. distinguishes between the following observations:

- **Previous capability:** Jake could revive following death through his existing anomalous mechanism.
    
- **Current capability:** The same mechanism now operates more efficiently.
    
- **Unknown parameters:** Recovery duration, energy requirements, reconstruction limits, and whether the improvement is permanent.
    

This must not be interpreted as proof of invulnerability.

Jake can still suffer, experience pain, and require medical attention.

An improved ability to return from death does not make the experience of dying inconsequential.

Nor does it justify exposing him to unnecessary danger.

---

## VI. BELLE MERRYWEATHER — COMPARATIVE REFERENCE

Belle Merryweather is the closest known comparison for Jake's newly acquired curative ability.

Both individuals demonstrate an ability to cure Ether Contamination, distinguishing them from ordinary Ether-exposed personnel.

The Medical Division has recommended a controlled comparative study, subject to Belle's and Jake's consent.

The objective is to understand whether their abilities share a common mechanism.

No conclusion has yet been reached regarding the relationship between their respective powers.

The possibility that Jake's adaptation represents a previously undocumented expression of an existing anomalous phenomenon remains under consideration.

---

## VII. PERSONNEL WELFARE DIRECTIVES

Given Jake's established tendency to prioritize other people's well-being above his own, additional safeguards are necessary.

1. Jake must not be encouraged to deliberately expose himself to Ether.
    
2. His curative capability must not be treated as an unlimited medical resource.
    
3. Any request for him to assist an affected individual must be voluntary and medically supervised.
    
4. His improved self-revival must not be used to justify dangerous experiments.
    
5. Medical staff must continue monitoring his physical condition independently of his revival capability.
    
6. Jake must be informed that he is allowed to refuse requests for assistance.
    
7. Personnel must continue treating him as a child and resident, not as a containment countermeasure.
    

The seventh directive is considered particularly important.

Jake already has difficulty prioritizing his own needs.

The discovery of a power capable of saving other people could make that tendency substantially more dangerous.

**We must not turn his kindness into an institutional resource.**

---

## VIII. C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

There is an unusual irony in Jake's latest development.

Ether, something that ordinarily threatens to take a person's identity away, has left Jake with the ability to give something back.

Where others might become Ether Walkers or Ethereals, Jake has become something different.

Golden and sky-blue markings now decorate his cheek. His eyes carry the same colors. His body has adapted, and his ability to revive himself has become more efficient.

And apparently, he can help people who have suffered the very contamination that changed him.

It is an extraordinary discovery.

But there is something we must remember.

Jake is the same little boy who waits outside rather than ring a doorbell because he does not want to interrupt anyone.

The same little boy who gives people affection even when he is exhausted.

The same little boy who would probably agree to help someone long before considering whether helping them might hurt him.

Now imagine what happens when that little boy discovers he can cure people.

He will want to help.

Of course he will.

That is precisely why we must protect him from being asked to give more than he can afford.

His new ability may save lives. His improved revival may make recovery more reliable. His unusual resistance to Ether may teach us things that decades of research have failed to uncover.

But none of those things are more important than Jake himself.

**C.O.R.E. FINAL REMARK:**

_He was exposed to something that turns people into monsters. Instead, he became someone who can help bring them back._

_Let us make certain that, in saving everyone else, he does not forget that he deserves saving too._
