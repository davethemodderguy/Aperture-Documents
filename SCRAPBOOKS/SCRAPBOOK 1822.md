# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

C.O.R.E. has documented a consistent behavioral pattern involving J-4C-P, designated **The Remnant**.

The phenomenon repeatedly visits the grave of Jake Akana in the Starlands.

Available observations indicate that this is not an occasional manifestation or an isolated response to a particular incident.

**The Remnant always goes to Jake's grave.**

Its visits occur despite the fact that the Remnant has explicitly confirmed that it is not Jake himself.

During one recorded encounter, the phenomenon stated:

> **"Jake is gone. I am not him."**

It subsequently described itself as a manifestation of what Jake felt and what happened to him.

The Remnant's repeated presence at the grave therefore presents a significant investigative question: why does a manifestation of Jake's pain and suffering consistently return to the place where Jake is buried?

C.O.R.E. has not established whether the behavior is driven by an anomalous compulsion, an association between the manifestation and Jake's death, or another mechanism.

The Remnant has not provided a complete explanation.

Personnel must not assume that its visits indicate Jake's consciousness has survived.

The current determination remains unchanged:

**Jake Akana is gone. The Remnant is not Jake.**

Nevertheless, the Remnant continues to visit his grave.

---

# 1. LOCATION OF INTEREST — STARLANDS

Jake Akana's grave is located in the Starlands.

The location is significant because the Remnant's other known manifestations occur primarily within the SAFEHOUSE cafeteria and at the location associated with Jake's fatal attack.

The grave represents a separate location associated directly with Jake's death and burial.

The Remnant's repeated visits establish a connection between the phenomenon and Jake's final resting place.

However, C.O.R.E. cannot currently determine the nature of that connection.

The presence of the Remnant at the grave does not establish that Jake's soul is present, that the grave exerts a supernatural pull, or that the Remnant possesses Jake's complete memories.

It establishes only what has been repeatedly observed:

**The Remnant goes to Jake's grave.**

And it keeps returning.

---

# 2. OBSERVED BEHAVIOR

The Remnant's behavior at the grave differs slightly from its behavior during some SAFEHOUSE encounters.

It generally remains near the grave for an extended period.

It does not appear to interact with other visitors unless they approach it.

It rarely speaks.

It maintains its characteristic posture, with its arms wrapped around the teddy bear.

Its damaged body remains visible, including the numerous scars and injuries documented during previous encounters.

The blue flame atop its head continues to emit no detectable heat.

The Remnant does not appear to be attempting to disturb the grave.

It does not attempt to enter the burial site.

It does not remove or alter anything placed there.

Instead, it remains nearby.

Sometimes it hovers beside the grave.

Sometimes it lowers itself close to the ground.

Sometimes it simply stays still, holding the teddy bear.

No consistent pattern of movement has been established beyond its repeated arrival and continued presence.

---

# 3. FIELD OBSERVATION — FIRST DOCUMENTED GRAVE VISIT

**LOCATION:** Starlands — Jake Akana's Grave

A visiting observer reported seeing the Remnant near the grave during a routine inspection of the surrounding area.

The figure was already present when the observer arrived.

It hovered beside the grave, its head lowered.

The teddy bear was held tightly against its chest.

The observer initially remained at a distance.

After several minutes, the observer approached slowly.

**OBSERVER:**  
"Are you looking for someone?"

The Remnant did not respond.

The observer looked toward the grave.

**OBSERVER:**  
"...Jake?"

The figure's blue flame flickered.

For several seconds, there was no movement.

Then the Remnant answered in its damaged whisper.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"He's here."

The observer stopped.

**OBSERVER:**  
"Do you mean Jake?"

The Remnant looked toward the grave.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"His body."

A pause.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"Not him."

The observer did not ask another question.

The Remnant remained beside the grave for the remainder of the observation period.

It did not speak again.

---

# 4. REPEATED VISITS

Subsequent observations established that the first encounter was not an isolated event.

The Remnant returned to the grave on later occasions.

The duration of each visit varied.

The phenomenon did not consistently arrive at the same time, nor did it appear to follow a schedule that could be predicted with the available data.

However, its destination remained unchanged.

When the Remnant manifested in the Starlands, it repeatedly made its way to Jake's grave.

Personnel have not established whether it travels through conventional space, manifests directly at the location, or follows another anomalous process.

The distinction is important.

The Remnant's apparent ability to reach the grave does not establish that it can freely travel between the Starlands and the Blacksite under all circumstances.

It establishes that it can reach this particular location repeatedly.

No attempt should be made to prevent these visits merely to determine whether the phenomenon will seek an alternative route.

---

# 5. OBSERVED INTERACTION WITH THE GRAVE

During a later observation, the Remnant was found hovering immediately beside the grave.

Its head was lowered toward the marker.

The observer remained several meters away.

After approximately twelve minutes, the Remnant spoke.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"...He's gone."

The observer did not answer immediately.

**OBSERVER:**  
"Yes."

The Remnant's grip tightened around the teddy bear.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"Then why..."

The sentence stopped.

The observer waited.

No further words followed.

After several minutes, the observer asked quietly:

**OBSERVER:**  
"Why what?"

The Remnant shook its head.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"Nothing."

The observer did not press for an explanation.

The Remnant remained at the grave.

C.O.R.E. cannot determine what the unfinished question would have been.

It must not be completed in official records as though the missing words were known.

---

# 6. RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE REMNANT AND JAKE'S GRAVE

Several hypotheses have been considered.

### Hypothesis A — Emotional Association

The grave may represent a location strongly associated with the suffering the Remnant manifests.

Its repeated visits may reflect the relationship between the phenomenon and the circumstances of Jake's death.

This is currently plausible but unconfirmed.

### Hypothesis B — Anomalous Anchoring

The Remnant may be linked to Jake's remains or burial site through an unknown anomalous mechanism.

No direct evidence has established such a connection.

The fact that it repeatedly visits the grave does not, by itself, demonstrate that the grave sustains its existence.

### Hypothesis C — Repetitive Behavioral Manifestation

The phenomenon may reproduce a recurring behavior associated with loss, grief, or the finality of Jake's death.

This possibility is consistent with its known nature but does not explain why the grave is its consistent destination.

### Hypothesis D — Unknown Mechanism

C.O.R.E. cannot exclude an explanation outside the current hypotheses.

No hypothesis has been confirmed.

The investigation must not convert a compelling pattern into a definitive explanation without additional evidence.

---

# 7. DOES THE REMNANT BELIEVE JAKE IS STILL THERE?

The available evidence suggests that the Remnant recognizes Jake's death.

It has explicitly stated that Jake is gone.

It has also distinguished itself from him.

During one encounter at the grave, an observer asked whether the Remnant expected Jake to return.

**OBSERVER:**  
"Are you waiting for him?"

The Remnant remained silent.

**OBSERVER:**  
"You know he isn't coming back, don't you?"

The blue flame flickered.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"I know."

The observer looked toward the grave.

**OBSERVER:**  
"Then why do you keep coming here?"

The Remnant did not answer.

The observer waited.

Several minutes passed.

Eventually, the Remnant spoke.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"Where else?"

No further explanation was provided.

The phrase has been preserved exactly as recorded.

C.O.R.E. cannot establish whether it expresses a lack of alternative destinations, a behavioral compulsion, an association with the grave, or another meaning.

It does, however, demonstrate that the Remnant's continued visits should not be casually interpreted as a belief that Jake will return.

The phenomenon may understand that Jake is gone and still continue returning to his grave.

Those two facts are not mutually exclusive.

# 8. RESIDENT WELFARE CONSIDERATIONS

The Remnant's repeated visits have affected residents who have accompanied it or encountered it near the grave.

Several residents initially interpreted its presence as evidence that some part of Jake remained accessible.

That interpretation is no longer supported by the available evidence.

C.O.R.E. has reiterated that the Remnant is a manifestation of Jake's pain and suffering, not Jake himself.

Its behavior may resemble mourning.

Its words may refer to Jake.

Its presence at the grave may be emotionally significant.

None of these observations establish that Jake's consciousness survives within the phenomenon.

Residents are therefore advised not to:

- Ask the Remnant to deliver messages to Jake.
    
- Ask it to speak on Jake's behalf.
    
- Treat its responses as Jake's personal wishes.
    
- Attempt to obtain forgiveness from it on Jake's behalf.
    
- Demand that it stop visiting the grave.
    
- Physically remove it from the area solely because its presence is distressing.
    
- Use the grave visits as evidence that Jake can be restored.
    

Residents may remain nearby if the Remnant tolerates their presence.

They must not force conversation or physical contact.

The Remnant's scars, existing wounds, and persistent distress remain relevant to its welfare assessment. Physical harm can injure it, and deliberate aggression is prohibited.

Its continued suffering does not grant anyone permission to add to it.

---

# 9. THE QUESTION OF COMPANIONSHIP

C.O.R.E. has not determined whether the Remnant seeks companionship at the grave.

Its behavior is compatible with several interpretations.

It may prefer solitude.

It may tolerate company without actively seeking it.

It may be unable to communicate a preference clearly.

It may be following an anomalous behavioral pattern that does not correspond directly to human social needs.

Personnel must therefore avoid assuming that remaining beside it is necessarily comforting.

During one visit, a resident approached the grave and asked whether they could sit nearby.

**RESIDENT:**  
"Can I stay for a while?"

The Remnant did not respond.

The resident remained standing.

**RESIDENT:**  
"I won't touch you."

After several seconds, the Remnant moved slightly away from the grave marker, leaving space beside it.

The resident sat.

Neither spoke for approximately twenty minutes.

Eventually, the resident asked:

**RESIDENT:**  
"Do you want me to leave?"

The Remnant remained silent.

The resident waited another minute.

**RESIDENT:**  
"All right. I'll stay a little longer, unless you move away."

No further interaction occurred.

The Remnant eventually demanifested.

The encounter does not establish that the Remnant wanted company. It establishes only that the resident avoided forcing an answer and did not interpret silence as permission for physical contact.

That approach remains the recommended practice.

---

# 10. SECURITY AND RESEARCH DIRECTIVES

The Starlands grave site is to be included in the Remnant's ongoing observation records.

Personnel are instructed to document:

- The time of arrival and departure.
    
- The duration of each visit.
    
- The Remnant's visible condition.
    
- Any changes in its scars or other injuries.
    
- Any speech, recorded verbatim where possible.
    
- Any interaction with the grave or objects placed nearby.
    
- Any interaction with visitors.
    
- Any evidence of physical injury during or after a visit.
    

Personnel must not conduct destructive tests on the grave or its surroundings in an attempt to force a reaction.

No one is authorized to disturb Jake's remains to determine whether the grave is an anchor.

The current evidence does not justify such an intervention.

The Remnant's presence must also not be used as a reason to restrict ordinary, respectful visits to the grave by others.

Observation should be discreet.

The purpose is to understand the phenomenon, not to turn Jake's resting place into a laboratory.

---

# 11. FINAL ASSESSMENT

The Remnant's repeated visits to Jake Akana's grave constitute a persistent behavioral pattern.

The grave is located in the Starlands.

The Remnant consistently returns there.

It recognizes that Jake is gone.

It has explicitly denied being Jake himself.

Its continued presence at the grave therefore does not establish that Jake's consciousness remains.

The most defensible conclusion is that the phenomenon's manifestation is strongly associated with Jake's death and suffering, and that the grave occupies a recurring place in its behavior.

The reason remains unknown.

C.O.R.E. will continue investigating without assuming that the Remnant can explain its own existence or that its behavior has a single, comprehensible purpose.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING OBSERVATION

A final observation was recorded during a quiet period in the Starlands.

The Remnant was at Jake's grave.

Its damaged body hovered just above the ground.

The blue flame atop its head cast a faint light over the marker.

Its arms remained wrapped around the teddy bear.

A visitor approached slowly.

**VISITOR:**  
"You come here a lot."

The Remnant did not respond.

The visitor stopped beside the grave.

**VISITOR:**  
"I suppose this is where he belongs."

The Remnant's head lifted slightly.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"No."

The visitor looked at it.

**VISITOR:**  
"No?"

The Remnant turned toward the grave.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"He's dead."

A pause.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"That's all."

The visitor lowered their gaze.

**VISITOR:**  
"I'm sorry."

The Remnant said nothing.

The visitor sat a short distance away.

For a while, neither moved.

Eventually, the visitor spoke again.

**VISITOR:**  
"Do you come here because you miss him?"

The Remnant's grip tightened around the bear.

Its voice emerged as a thin, painful whisper.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"I'm not him."

**VISITOR:**  
"I know."

The Remnant looked toward the grave.

**THE REMNANT:**  
"Then stop asking me what he feels."

The visitor fell silent.

The Remnant remained beside the grave.

Its scars were visible in the dim light.

Its flame continued to burn without heat.

No miraculous change occurred.

The figure did not disappear into the grave.

Jake did not return.

After a long period, the Remnant demanifested.

The visitor remained seated beside the marker.

The grave was quiet.

The Starlands continued around it.

And the phenomenon that carried the shape of Jake's suffering was gone until its next appearance.

C.O.R.E. has no confirmed explanation for why it always returns.

It can only record that it does.

**Jake is gone.**

**The Remnant knows this.**

**And still, it returns to his grave.**
