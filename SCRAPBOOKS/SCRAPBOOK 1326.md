# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Two details regarding Jake's day-to-day care have been formally clarified.

First:

> **Picking Jake up is surprisingly pleasant.**

Despite his stellar nature, Jake's current physical form is that of a small six-year-old child.

He is light.

Warm.

Soft.

Physically tangible.

And, when he is comfortable with the person carrying him, he generally relaxes rather quickly.

Second:

> **Jake does not have a birthday.**

No birth date exists.

Jake was not born.

He was found.

He does not want personnel to invent a birthday for him.

C.O.R.E. has therefore formally discontinued all attempts to assign one.

---

# 1. PICKING UP JAKE

Jake's physical materialization has made carrying him considerably easier than expected.

He is approximately the size and weight appropriate for his manifested child form.

His stellar nature does not make him physically cumbersome.

In fact, personnel consistently describe carrying him as:

> **“Like carrying a very small child.”**

The only unusual part is that the very small child happens to have a stellar core.

---

# 2. COMFORT RESPONSE

Jake generally becomes very still when picked up by someone he trusts.

He may:

- rest his head against their shoulder;
    
- wrap his arms around them;
    
- hold his teddy bear against his chest;
    
- let his legs hang naturally;
    
- close his eyes;
    
- or simply stare over their shoulder.
    

His celestial particles often become more visible when he is relaxed.

The effect is somewhat unusual.

A six-year-old child is being carried through the SAFEHOUSE.

His white hair is resting against someone's shoulder.

Azure light is faintly glowing from his eyes.

Tiny star-like particles are drifting around him.

And his teddy bear is quietly beating against his chest.

---

# 3. BAO

Bao can pick Jake up without difficulty.

Jake generally accepts this immediately.

One evening, Bao found him sitting on the floor looking tired.

> **Bao:**  
> “Come here.”

Jake raised his arms.

Bao picked him up.

Jake immediately rested his head against her shoulder.

> **Jake:**  
> “Mom.”

> **Bao:**  
> “Yes?”

> **Jake:**  
> “I'm tired.”

> **Bao:**  
> “I know.”

Jake closed his eyes.

His teddy bear's heartbeat slowed.

Bao carried him back to his room.

---

# 4. NORA

Nora also carries Jake.

Her approach is considerably less dignified.

> **Nora:**  
> “Jake!”

She picks him up.

Jake:

> **“Oh.”**

Nora:

> **“Star baby!”**

Jake:

> **“I'm six.”**

Nora:

> **“Still baby.”**

Jake sighs.

He does not ask to be put down.

---

# 5. AETERNA

Aeterna can carry Jake effortlessly.

Because of her armor and physical construction, she is considerably stronger than necessary for the task.

Jake occasionally sits on her arm while she walks.

He appears completely comfortable doing so.

One observation recorded:

Jake was asleep.

Aeterna was carrying him.

His teddy bear was tucked against his chest.

Tiny celestial particles were drifting around both of them.

A staff member quietly remarked:

> “That's adorable.”

Aeterna looked at them.

The staff member immediately returned to work.

---

# 6. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake does not appear embarrassed by being carried when he trusts the person doing it.

He may even ask.

For example:

> **Jake:**  
> “Can you carry me?”

If the answer is yes, he simply raises his arms.

No explanation is necessary.

He is still six.

Sometimes walking is tiring.

Sometimes he wants comfort.

Sometimes he simply wants to be held.

There is nothing unusual about this.

The unusual part is only what Jake happens to be.

---

# 7. HANDLING GUIDELINES

Personnel are reminded:

### If Jake asks to be carried:

Carry him if practical.

### If Jake raises his arms:

This may indicate that he wants to be picked up.

Ask if uncertain.

### If Jake becomes uncomfortable:

Put him down.

### If Jake is asleep:

Do not wake him unnecessarily.

### If Jake's teddy bear is present:

Keep it with him.

The teddy bear remains his stellar core and must not be separated from him.

---

# 8. THE BIRTHDAY QUESTION

The second clarification concerns birthdays.

Jake does not have one.

There is no recorded date of birth.

No hospital records exist.

No birth certificate exists.

No parental birth record exists.

No conventional biological origin exists.

Jake was found.

That is where his known existence begins.

Therefore, there is no legitimate birthday to celebrate.

---

# 9. ATTEMPTS TO GIVE HIM ONE

A previous discussion proposed selecting a symbolic date.

Jake immediately rejected the idea.

> **Neri:**  
> “We could pick the day you arrived.”

Jake shook his head.

> **Jake:**  
> “That's not my birthday.”

> **Neri:**  
> “We could call it your star-day.”

Jake shook his head again.

> **Jake:**  
> “No.”

> **Neri:**  
> “Why?”

Jake looked down.

> **Jake:**  
> “I don't want one.”

Neri stopped.

> **Neri:**  
> “Okay.”

That was the end of the discussion.

---

# 10. WHY HE DOESN'T WANT ONE

Jake has never expressed a desire to know when he was born.

The concept appears uncomfortable to him.

He understands what birthdays mean.

He has seen other residents celebrate them.

He understands:

> cake;

> presents;

> candles;

> congratulations;

> another year.

But the concept does not apply naturally to him.

He does not remember being born.

He remembers being found.

His earliest known existence begins there.

He therefore does not want SAFEHOUSE personnel manufacturing a personal history that does not exist.

---

# 11. NO ARTIFICIAL BIRTHDAY

C.O.R.E. has formally prohibited assigning Jake:

- a fabricated date of birth;
    
- a symbolic birthday without his consent;
    
- an arbitrary “star birth” date;
    
- a celebration disguised as a birthday;
    
- or a surprise birthday party.
    

Especially the last one.

Jake specifically dislikes surprises.

His door literally says:

> **Jake's Room**  
> **please knock, I can't take suprises**

A surprise birthday party would therefore represent several poor decisions simultaneously.

---

# 12. WHAT JAKE DOES HAVE

Jake does have important dates.

They simply aren't birthdays.

For example:

- the day he was found;
    
- the day he arrived at the Blacksite;
    
- the day Nora first met him;
    
- the day he first ate food without being hungry;
    
- the day he first became physically tangible;
    
- the day he received his proper SAFEHOUSE room;
    
- and other significant events.
    

These may be remembered if Jake wishes.

But none of them are automatically considered his birthday.

---

# FINAL ASSESSMENT

Jake is six years old.

He is also a star.

His core is inside a teddy bear.

He can potentially generate extraordinary amounts of energy.

He could theoretically collapse into something far more dangerous if his core were permanently destroyed.

And yet:

When Bao picks him up, he is simply a small child resting against her shoulder.

When Nora carries him, he complains that he's not a baby.

When Aeterna carries him, he occasionally falls asleep.

When Neri sits beside him, he may lean against her.

The cosmic implications can wait.

Jake is still a kid.

And kids sometimes want to be carried.

---

# C.O.R.E. FINAL NOTE

Do not give Jake a birthday.

Do not invent one.

Do not surprise him.

Do not turn his origin into an obligation to celebrate.

If Jake ever decides that he wants to celebrate something, he can choose what that something is.

Until then, there is no birthday.

There is only Jake.

A six-year-old child.

A walking star.

A teddy bear containing his heart.

And, occasionally—

A very sleepy little resident who wants someone to carry him home.
