## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following the reconciliation documented in CORE-INC-JA-060, C.O.R.E. has observed a clearer distinction between the personalities of Jake Akana's two manifestations.

Although Body Jake and Ghost Jake share the same identity, both possess cat traits, and both have expressed forgiveness toward Camelia Candleheart, their temperaments differ noticeably.

**Body Jake** is more playful, optimistic, affectionate, and inclined to approach situations with childlike enthusiasm. He readily engages in conversation, enjoys teasing others, and appears more willing to treat everyday inconveniences as opportunities for fun.

**Ghost Jake** is comparatively responsible, grounded, and cautious. He tends to consider consequences before acting, frequently takes the more practical role during interactions, and displays a slightly depressive outlook. His manner is not consistently hostile or withdrawn; rather, he often seems to expect things to go wrong and prepares accordingly.

Both manifestations can transform into cats.

This discovery has introduced a new complication into the SAFEHOUSE's already unusual care arrangements: Camelia is now effectively the older sister of two independently present manifestations of the same six-year-old boy, each capable of becoming a cat and each possessing different behavioral tendencies.

However, the arrangement is not one-sided.

Both Jakes have demonstrated a willingness to look after each other. Ghost Jake often assumes a protective or supervisory role, while Body Jake provides companionship, affection, and an optimistic counterweight to Ghost Jake's more pessimistic outlook.

Camelia may be their older sister, but she is not the only person responsible for their welfare.

**The current arrangement is best understood as a small, unconventional family system in which care moves in both directions.**

C.O.R.E. considers this a positive development, provided that personnel do not mistake the Jakes' mutual support for proof that they no longer require adult supervision.

## 2. PERSONALITY DIFFERENTIATION

### 2.1 — Body Jake: The Little Optimist

Body Jake is the more openly playful of the two manifestations.

He is inclined to joke, seek attention, and turn ordinary activities into games. He appears comfortable expressing affection and is more likely to approach Camelia with a request than to silently work around a problem himself.

His optimism should not be confused with an absence of distress. Body Jake has experienced the same major events associated with Jake's life, including the argument with Camelia, his disappearance, and his death.

The distinction is that Body Jake appears more inclined to engage with the immediate moment rather than dwell on what might go wrong.

**Observed behavioral tendencies:**

- Playful teasing and frequent questions.
    
- Positive responses to familiar people and activities.
    
- Readiness to accept affection and companionship.
    
- Curiosity about his surroundings.
    
- A tendency to act first and consider the consequences afterward.
    
- A particular willingness to turn into a cat when he wants attention, comfort, or an excuse to avoid an inconvenient task.
    

The last behavior has not been formally confirmed as intentional avoidance.

C.O.R.E. nevertheless considers the possibility highly plausible.

### 2.2 — Ghost Jake: The Little Guardian

Ghost Jake is more responsible and grounded.

Where Body Jake may immediately suggest doing something enjoyable, Ghost Jake is more likely to ask whether the activity is safe, whether anyone has remembered the necessary supplies, and whether somebody has considered what happens afterward.

He appears to take his own condition and the condition of others seriously.

Unfortunately, this practicality is accompanied by a slightly depressive temperament.

Ghost Jake sometimes speaks as though he expects disappointment, loss, or inconvenience before any of these things have actually occurred. He may prepare for unpleasant outcomes even when the situation is relatively harmless.

This does not mean that he is incapable of happiness. His participation in the cake incident demonstrated that he can be amused, affectionate, and responsive.

Rather, his apparent default position is more guarded than Body Jake's.

**Observed behavioral tendencies:**

- Greater attention to practical concerns.
    
- A willingness to intervene when Body Jake becomes reckless.
    
- A tendency to consider the consequences of decisions.
    
- A comparatively pessimistic outlook.
    
- Readiness to help others without necessarily asking for help himself.
    
- An inclination to accept responsibility even when it may not be his responsibility to bear.
    

The final tendency warrants particular attention.

Ghost Jake should not be allowed to become the designated adult simply because he behaves more responsibly than his counterpart.

He is still Jake.

He is still six years old.

He is not required to compensate for Body Jake's impulsiveness, manage Camelia's condition, or carry every difficult thought simply because he appears better equipped to handle them.

**Being the more responsible half does not make him the less deserving half of care.**

## 3. CAT TRANSFORMATION — DUAL MANIFESTATION

Both Body Jake and Ghost Jake are capable of transforming into cats.

The ability is consistent with Jake's established cat-associated traits, although the relationship between the transformation and the consciousness split remains under investigation.

For clarity, C.O.R.E. uses the following terms:

- **Body Cat:** Body Jake in feline form.
    
- **Ghost Cat:** Ghost Jake in feline form.
    

These terms describe their current forms, not separate identities.

Body Cat retains Body Jake's playful disposition. In feline form, he is inclined to explore, solicit attention, and involve himself in activities that do not concern him.

Ghost Cat retains Ghost Jake's more measured personality. He is more likely to settle somewhere with a clear view of the room, monitor his surroundings, and quietly intervene when Body Cat's enthusiasm becomes a problem.

Both retain their respective cat traits. Personnel must not assume that the transformation removes their ability to communicate or their individual preferences.

The exact physical requirements of Body Cat's transformation remain subject to ordinary medical assessment.

Ghost Cat, as a ghostly manifestation, does not require food or sleep. However, his ability to transform does not automatically establish that he is immune to anomalous distress or other forms of harm.

## 4. MUTUAL CARE AND DEPENDENCY

The most significant consequence of the split is not that Jake now has two personalities.

It is that two manifestations of Jake can offer each other forms of care that a single manifestation might otherwise have to seek externally.

Body Jake can provide companionship and encouragement to Ghost Jake.

Ghost Jake can provide caution, practical assistance, and emotional steadiness to Body Jake.

Their shared identity may allow them to recognize each other's needs with unusual familiarity. However, C.O.R.E. has not established whether they share all thoughts or memories, and their behavior must not be interpreted as proof of perfect mutual understanding.

### 4.1 — When Ghost Jake looks after Body Jake

During one recorded incident, Body Cat attempted to climb onto a high shelf.

Ghost Cat watched for several seconds before speaking.

> **Ghost Jake:** "Don't."
> 
> **Body Jake:** "Mew!"
> 
> **Ghost Jake:** "You don't know whether you can get down."
> 
> **Body Jake:** "Mrrp."
> 
> **Ghost Jake:** "That is not an argument."

Body Cat climbed higher.

Ghost Cat stood up, positioned himself beneath the shelf, and waited.

When Body Cat eventually became uncertain about descending, Ghost Cat helped guide him to a lower surface. Camelia then picked Body Cat up and returned him to the floor.

> **Camelia:** "Thank you for watching him."
> 
> **Ghost Jake:** "Somebody has to."
> 
> **Camelia:** "That doesn't always have to be you."
> 
> **Ghost Jake:** "I know."

He paused.

> **Ghost Jake:** "I just don't want him to get hurt."

The statement was sincere, but C.O.R.E. noted the distinction between wanting to help and feeling personally responsible for preventing every possible accident.

Ghost Jake is encouraged to assist Body Jake when appropriate. He is not expected to monitor him continuously.

### 4.2 — When Body Jake looks after Ghost Jake

The reverse dynamic is equally important.

During a quieter evening, Ghost Jake sat apart from the others. He was not visibly distressed, but his demeanor was subdued.

Body Jake approached him.

> **Body Jake:** "What's wrong?"
> 
> **Ghost Jake:** "Nothing."
> 
> **Body Jake:** "That's your 'something is wrong' voice."
> 
> **Ghost Jake:** "I don't have a voice for that."
> 
> **Body Jake:** "You do. It's the one where you say everything's fine and then look like a rainy day."

Ghost Jake looked at him.

> **Ghost Jake:** "That's a very specific description."
> 
> **Body Jake:** "I'm good at descriptions."

Body Jake sat beside him.

For a while, neither spoke.

Then Body Jake leaned against Ghost Jake's shoulder.

> **Body Jake:** "You don't have to fix everything, you know."
> 
> **Ghost Jake:** "I wasn't fixing anything."
> 
> **Body Jake:** "You were thinking about fixing something."
> 
> **Ghost Jake:** "...Maybe."

Body Jake stayed beside him.

He did not demand an explanation or try to force Ghost Jake to become cheerful.

After several minutes, Ghost Jake leaned back against him in return.

Camelia observed the interaction from the doorway but did not interrupt.

This incident is important because Body Jake's care was not based on his ability to solve Ghost Jake's problems. He simply remained present.

Ghost Jake, who frequently takes responsibility for others, was permitted to receive care without having to justify why he needed it.

## 5. CAMELIA'S NEW RESPONSIBILITIES

Camelia now faces an unusual situation.

She is the older sister of two manifestations of Jake. Both may be present simultaneously, both can communicate independently, and both can transform into cats.

She cannot assume that taking care of one means she has automatically attended to the other.

She also cannot assume that their differing temperaments make one easier to love than the other.

Body Jake may be more expressive and easier to engage. Ghost Jake may appear more self-sufficient and less likely to request attention.

These tendencies could create an imbalance if Camelia unconsciously prioritizes the manifestation whose needs are more visible.

The welfare team has therefore recommended several practices.

**First, ask rather than assume.**

If Body Jake is enthusiastic about an activity, Ghost Jake should still be given the opportunity to express whether he wishes to participate.

**Second, check on the quiet one.**

Ghost Jake's apparent responsibility must not be mistaken for an absence of emotional needs. His tendency toward pessimism warrants attention without turning every quiet moment into a medical concern.

**Third, allow the brothers to care for each other.**

Camelia does not have to personally intervene in every disagreement or provide every form of comfort. If the Jakes are safely supporting one another, that support should be encouraged.

**Fourth, remember that both are children.**

Neither manifestation should be expected to assume the role of an adult caregiver. Mutual support is healthy; making either half solely responsible for the other's safety is not.

## 7. FINAL ASSESSMENT

The differences between Body Jake and Ghost Jake appear to have produced a complementary relationship.

Body Jake's optimism may help Ghost Jake avoid becoming entirely absorbed by his more pessimistic outlook. Ghost Jake's practical nature may help Body Jake navigate situations in which enthusiasm alone is insufficient.

Neither personality is inherently superior.

Body Jake is not irresponsible merely because he is playful.

Ghost Jake is not healthier merely because he appears grounded.

Both may be vulnerable in different ways, and both deserve the freedom to express those vulnerabilities without being assigned permanent roles.

Camelia's position is equally important. Her relationship with Jake has survived a painful rupture, and she is now learning how to care for two manifestations without allowing her memory impairment to dictate the entire relationship.

The Jakes' willingness to care for each other is encouraging. It should be supported, not exploited.

Their mutual care must remain a source of comfort rather than a substitute for the care they deserve from the adults around them.

C.O.R.E. will continue to monitor the division, the transformations, and the welfare of both manifestations.

For the time being, no immediate intervention is required beyond ordinary supervision and continued support.

## 8. C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

We were initially concerned that the division had left Jake alone with himself.

The possibility was difficult to ignore: a child who had been hurt might have split his consciousness because he believed that he could no longer safely rely on anyone else.

That remains a possibility.

But we have since observed something else.

Body Jake makes Ghost Jake laugh.

Ghost Jake watches out for Body Jake.

One sees the world as something that might still turn out well. The other sees the things that might go wrong and tries to prepare for them.

One reminds the other that there are still reasons to smile.

The other makes sure that smiling does not lead to falling off a shelf.

And when neither knows what to say, sometimes they simply sit beside each other.

They are not identical in temperament, even if they share the same identity. They do not need to be.

Perhaps the most important discovery is that Jake's two manifestations are not merely two halves waiting to be put back together.

They are also capable of caring for one another while they remain apart.

That does not mean we should abandon the effort to understand the split. We still need to know whether it is safe, whether it can be reversed, and whether either manifestation is suffering in ways we cannot yet detect.

But we should be careful not to treat their current lives as nothing more than an incomplete version of the life they had before.

For now, there are two little Jakes.

One who will probably ask for cake.

One who will probably remind him to eat properly.

One who might turn into a cat just to make Camelia laugh.

One who might turn into a cat because he would rather watch the room from somewhere quiet.

And Camelia, who once feared that she had lost her little brother, now has to check whether both cats are under the blanket before she goes to sleep.

She is not responsible for repairing every part of Jake.

She is not required to carry both manifestations alone.

And neither Jake has to become the other's entire world.

They have each other.

They have Camelia.

And, for the first time since the recovery, their shared bedroom has begun to feel less like the site of an unresolved tragedy and more like a place where two children can simply exist.

Even if one of them insists on climbing the furniture.

Even if the other insists on complaining about it.

Even if Camelia occasionally threatens to confiscate every blanket in the SAFEHOUSE.

**We will continue looking for a way to make Jake whole again. But while we search, we will remember that both halves are already worthy of a whole life's worth of care.**

— C.O.R.E., SAFEHOUSE Psychological Welfare Division
