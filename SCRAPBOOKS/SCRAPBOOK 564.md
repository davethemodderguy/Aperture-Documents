### **PSYCHOLOGICAL / TREEHOUSE OBSERVATION REPORT**

# **CORIN — WITHDRAWAL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Observation:** **Severe Social Withdrawal**  
**Status:** Ongoing

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has withdrawn to the treehouse once again.

She has stopped visiting the Blacksite.

She has stopped joining the SAFEHOUSE residents.

She rarely leaves her room.

Most of her time is spent in the same corner of the treehouse, curled tightly against herself.

Her wings are wrapped around her entire body.

Only occasional movement beneath the feathers reveals that she is still awake.

---

# **THE CORNER**

Corin has chosen one particular corner as her resting place.

She sits there for extended periods.

Her knees are drawn toward her chest.

Her two tails are curled close to her body.

Her wings fold around her like a blanket, completely covering her face and most of her body.

From a distance, she looks almost like a small mound of feathers.

She cries quietly beneath them.

She does not call for anyone.

She does not ask anyone to come.

---

# **THE CONTRADICTION**

Corin hates being alone.

This has been repeatedly demonstrated.

She becomes noticeably calmer when someone she trusts is nearby.

She sleeps more peacefully around others.

She is more likely to transform into her miniature feline form when she feels completely safe with someone.

Yet when she becomes depressed, she isolates herself anyway.

She wants company.

She simply does not believe she should ask for it.

---

# **ORPHIE'S ABSENCE**

The strongest example occurred when Orphie left the treehouse to obtain supplies.

Orphie informed Corin that she would be gone for a while.

Corin nodded.

> **"Okay."**

She did not protest.

Once Orphie left, Corin returned to her corner.

She curled herself up.

Her wings closed around her.

And she began crying.

She remained there for the entire duration of Orphie's absence.

---

# **ORPHIE RETURNS**

When Orphie eventually returned, she found Corin exactly where she had left her.

The wings were still wrapped around her.

The room was quiet.

Orphie approached slowly.

> **"Corin?"**

No response.

She sat down beside her.

After several seconds, one of Corin's wings shifted slightly.

Orphie could see her face.

Her eyes were red from crying.

> **"I'm back."**

Corin stared at her.

Then quietly:

> **"...You took a long time."**

Orphie nodded.

> **"I know."**

Corin looked away.

> **"...I didn't like it."**

Orphie understood.

---

# **WHY SHE HIDES**

C.O.R.E. believes Corin's withdrawal is not caused by a desire to be alone.

It appears to be a defensive response.

When Corin becomes overwhelmed by her thoughts, she retreats.

The wings provide physical cover.

The corner provides a confined space.

Silence reduces external stimulation.

She effectively creates a small environment where nothing can reach her.

Unfortunately, this also removes the companionship she desperately wants.

---

# **THE WINGS**

Corin's wings have become particularly important during these episodes.

She uses them almost like a physical shield.

They cover:

- Her face.
    
- Her torso.
    
- Her arms.
    
- Her legs.
    

The feathers form a dense barrier around her.

It is difficult to see her underneath them.

The behavior resembles an instinctive attempt to hide from the world.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The residents have been advised not to forcibly remove Corin from the treehouse.

Instead, they are encouraged to maintain gentle contact.

Someone may sit nearby.

Someone may leave tea.

Someone may leave food.

Someone may leave yarn.

Nobody is required to make her talk.

Sometimes the most effective intervention is simply:

**Stay.**

---

# **ORPHIE'S APPROACH**

Orphie has developed a habit of sitting beside Corin without immediately speaking.

She does not demand that Corin uncover herself.

She simply remains there.

Eventually, Corin's wings usually loosen.

Sometimes only slightly.

Sometimes enough for her to lean against Orphie.

On particularly difficult days, Corin simply stays hidden.

Orphie remains anyway.

---

# **A RECENT EVENING**

Orphie sat beside Corin for nearly an hour.

Neither spoke.

Eventually, Corin's wings opened just enough for one eye to become visible.

She looked at Orphie.

> **"...Are you leaving again?"**

Orphie answered immediately.

> **"Not yet."**

Corin's eye closed.

Her wings loosened slightly.

She leaned against Orphie.

That was all.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

Corin's current condition is concerning.

She is:

- Isolating herself.
    
- Crying frequently.
    
- Avoiding the Blacksite.
    
- Avoiding social activities.
    
- Spending prolonged periods in one location.
    
- Experiencing recurrent negative thoughts.
    
- Showing increased dependence on physical feelings of safety.
    

However, she continues responding positively to trusted companionship.

This suggests that her connection to others remains intact.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin hates being alone.

Yet when her past becomes too loud, she hides herself away.

She curls into a corner.

She wraps her wings around herself.

She cries where nobody can see her.

When Orphie leaves, she does not call her back.

She simply waits.

And when Orphie returns, the first thing Corin asks is not:

> **"Where were you?"**

It is:

> **"...Are you staying?"**

For now, Orphie stays.

And Corin slowly uncurls her wings.

**End of Psychological / Treehouse Observation Report**
