### SECURITY, ANOMALOUS RESEARCH & RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-SEC-4C-JAKE-197  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESTRICTED — ANOMALOUS INCIDENT  
**STATUS:** CLOSED  
**SUBJECT:** Jake  
**EVENT:** Second Woodland Coorp Kidnapping Attempt  
**ITEM:** Celestial Greatsword

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Woodland Coorp attempted to kidnap Jake again.

C.O.R.E. would like to formally record:

> **They tried again.**

Neko's response upon receiving the alert was reportedly:

> **NEKO:**  
> “Again?”

The second attempt differed significantly from the first.

Jake did not use **Pulverization.**

He did not engage the guards.

He did not attempt to fight his way through the facility.

Instead, Jake manifested his celestial greatsword.

Then C.O.R.E. discovered that the sword had another function.

---

# 2. THE INCIDENT

Woodland personnel successfully intercepted Jake and transported him to another secured facility.

As with the previous incident, the personnel involved demonstrated incomplete knowledge of Jake's capabilities.

Jake was contained inside an internal section of the facility.

Armed personnel were positioned between Jake and the exterior.

They expected resistance.

Jake looked at the walls.

Then at his sword.

Then at the walls again.

He apparently reached a simple conclusion:

> **There was no reason to use the hallway.**

---

# 3. SWORD MANIFESTATION

Jake manifested his celestial greatsword.

The weapon appeared in his hand as usual.

Blue light spread across the blade.

The star-shaped structure in the hilt brightened considerably.

Several Woodland personnel reportedly raised their weapons.

Jake did not react.

Instead, he turned toward the nearest exterior wall.

A guard reportedly shouted:

> **“Don't move!”**

Jake continued looking at the wall.

---

# 4. THE UNEXPECTED FUNCTION

Jake raised the sword.

The weapon changed configuration.

The blade remained visibly intact, but the celestial energy surrounding the weapon condensed toward the forward portion.

The resulting effect was not consistent with a conventional sword.

It functioned as a **handheld anomalous artillery system.**

C.O.R.E. has provisionally designated this secondary capability:

> **CELESTIAL ARTILLERY**

The sword discharged a concentrated blast.

The wall was breached.

A large opening appeared through the structure.

Jake stared through it.

There was now a direct route outside.

---

# 5. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake did not attack the Woodland personnel.

He did not use Pulverization.

He simply walked toward the newly created opening.

One guard reportedly shouted:

> **“Stop!”**

Jake looked back.

> **Jake:**  
> “No.”

He continued walking.

He reached the exterior.

His wings unfolded.

The sword remained in his hand.

Then Jake took off.

---

# 6. FLIGHT

Jake flew directly back toward the Blacksite.

No pursuit was attempted.

Woodland personnel were reportedly too confused to immediately understand what had happened.

The facility had been breached.

Jake was gone.

No personnel had been seriously injured.

No extended confrontation occurred.

The entire escape consisted of:

**Sword.**

**Wall.**

**Hole.**

**Wings.**

**Home.**

---

# 7. BLACKSITE ARRIVAL

Jake arrived at the Blacksite without incident.

He landed near the SAFEHOUSE entrance.

His wings folded.

The sword was still manifested.

Bao was among the first residents to see him.

> **Bao:**  
> “Jake?”

Jake looked at her.

> **Jake:**  
> “They tried again.”

Bao immediately approached him.

> **Bao:**  
> “Are you hurt?”

Jake shook his head.

> **Jake:**  
> “No.”

Bao looked at the sword.

Then at Jake.

> **Bao:**  
> “What happened?”

Jake pointed back toward the direction from which he had flown.

> **Jake:**  
> “I made a door.”

Bao stared at him.

---

# 8. NEKO'S REVIEW

Neko later reviewed the facility footage.

He watched Jake manifest the sword.

He watched the weapon change.

He watched the wall disappear.

He watched Jake fly away.

The footage ended.

Neko remained silent.

Then:

> **NEKO:**  
> “That sword is a howitzer.”

A nearby technician corrected him:

> **“Technically, it isn't—”**

Neko raised one hand.

> **NEKO:**  
> “I don't care.”

Pause.

> **NEKO:**  
> “The six-year-old's sword is a howitzer.”

---

# 9. WOODLAND COORP RESPONSE

Woodland Coorp has now encountered Jake's capabilities on multiple occasions.

However, their understanding of him remains incomplete.

The first encounter demonstrated **Pulverization.**

The second demonstrated **Celestial Artillery.**

Woodland personnel reportedly assumed Jake's greatsword was primarily a melee weapon.

This assumption is now considered obsolete.

The weapon is capable of functioning as both:

> **A celestial greatsword.**

and

> **A highly destructive ranged anomalous weapon.**

The precise internal mechanism remains unknown.

C.O.R.E. has no current intention of finding out by dismantling it.

---

# 10. AETERNA'S ASSESSMENT

Aeterna inspected the sword after Jake returned.

She looked at the weapon.

Then at Jake.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “You used it to escape.”

Jake nodded.

> **Jake:**  
> “Yes.”

> **Aeterna:**  
> “You could have fought them.”

Jake shook his head.

> **Jake:**  
> “I didn't need to.”

Aeterna paused.

Then nodded.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “Good.”

She looked toward the sword.

> **Aeterna:**  
> “You chose the shorter path.”

Jake nodded.

---

# 11. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The incident provides an important behavioral distinction.

Jake possessed the capability to engage the hostile personnel.

He chose not to.

Instead, he used the sword to create an escape route and returned home.

This is considered preferable from a resident-welfare perspective.

Jake's objective was not retaliation.

It was:

> **Go home.**

The sword allowed him to accomplish this rapidly.

---

The following properties are now confirmed:

### PRIMARY FUNCTION

**Greatsword**

A large celestial weapon composed of glowing blue anomalous metal.

### SECONDARY FUNCTION

### MANIFESTATION

### DISMISSAL

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Woodland Coorp tried to kidnap Jake again.

Jake had several options.

He could have used Pulverization.

He could have fought.

He could have waited for MSAC.

Instead, he looked at a wall and apparently thought:

> **“I can just leave.”**

So he pulled out a glowing celestial greatsword—

which turned out to also be a handheld howitzer—

blasted an opening through the facility,

spread his wings,

and flew home.

When he arrived, Bao asked what happened.

Jake's answer was remarkably simple:

> **“I made a door.”**

C.O.R.E. has reviewed the incident.

The official recommendation is as follows:

> **Stop kidnapping Jake.**

Woodland Coorp has now demonstrated that it is willing to repeatedly learn this lesson the hard way.
