# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The **Liminal Space Investigation and Neutralization Executive (L.S.I.N.)** is Aperture Industries' primary organization responsible for the investigation, documentation, containment, management, and neutralization of liminal spaces and other externally originating spatial anomalies.

L.S.I.N. personnel operate under an unusually strict professional standard.

This is intentional.

Liminal-space operations differ fundamentally from conventional field operations.

An ordinary building can be mapped.

A conventional battlefield can be surveyed.

A hostile facility can be secured.

A liminal space may change the meaning of:

> **distance, direction, architecture, time, identity, entry, exit, and physical location.**

An L.S.I.N. agent is therefore expected to maintain professional discipline even when the environment itself cannot be trusted.

---

# 2. OPERATIONAL PROFESSIONALISM

L.S.I.N. maintains one of the highest operational discipline standards within Aperture Industries.

This is reflected in:

- standardized field procedures;
    
- strict communication protocols;
    
- redundant navigation systems;
    
- continuous personnel accounting;
    
- documented entry and exit procedures;
    
- formal anomaly classification;
    
- controlled equipment handling;
    
- mandatory incident reporting;
    
- cross-dimensional navigation training;
    
- and strict separation between observation and assumption.
    

L.S.I.N. personnel are expected to remain professional regardless of the environment.

A corridor extending several kilometers where it should be twenty meters long is still documented as a corridor.

A room containing three different gravitational orientations is still documented systematically.

A door that opens into another universe is still treated as an access point.

The agent's job is not to panic about what the environment is doing.

The agent's job is to **record what it is doing.**

---

# 3. COMPARISON WITH CONVENTIONAL FIELD ORGANIZATIONS

L.S.I.N. personnel frequently operate alongside other Aperture-affiliated organizations.

Among these, the **M.S.A.C.** maintains extensive experience in conventional and anomalous combat operations.

M.S.A.C. personnel are highly capable field operators.

Their primary specialization, however, remains operational deployment within environments that can generally be understood through conventional tactical principles.

L.S.I.N. operates under a different doctrine.

Its personnel must be prepared for situations where:

> **The environment itself is the anomaly.**

This distinction has resulted in L.S.I.N. developing a professional standard that is often regarded internally as exceeding that of conventional field organizations.

This is not because L.S.I.N. personnel are necessarily superior combatants.

It is because their work requires an unusually high degree of procedural discipline.

---

# 4. THE L.S.I.N. FIELD AGENT

An L.S.I.N. agent is not simply an explorer.

They are simultaneously expected to function as:

- investigator;
    
- surveyor;
    
- containment specialist;
    
- navigator;
    
- rescue operator;
    
- anomaly observer;
    
- communications operator;
    
- emergency responder;
    
- and dimensional field specialist.
    

A single field team may enter an environment where there is no guarantee that:

- the floor will remain beneath them;
    
- the exit will remain behind them;
    
- the map will remain accurate;
    
- time will remain consistent;
    
- or the environment will remain the same after they leave.
    

Professional discipline is therefore not optional.

It is survival equipment.

---

# 5. STANDARD FIELD CONDUCT

L.S.I.N. agents are instructed to avoid unnecessary improvisation.

When entering an anomalous environment, personnel establish:

**IDENTITY**

**LOCATION**

**TIME**

**TEAM STATUS**

**ENVIRONMENTAL STATUS**

**KNOWN EXIT**

**SECONDARY EXIT**

**COMMUNICATION STATUS**

**ANOMALOUS CONDITIONS**

before proceeding.

Changes are reported immediately.

No agent is permitted to assume that an environment remains stable merely because it was stable during a previous expedition.

# 7. STARLANDIAN KNOWLEDGE

The Starlands are not merely another location.

They operate according to properties that cannot always be described using conventional terrestrial physics.

Star and Nightshade have spent significant portions of their existence navigating and protecting that environment.

They therefore understand a fundamental principle of dimensional investigation:

> **A foreign dimension should not be assumed to obey the rules of the dimension that discovered it.**

This principle forms part of L.S.I.N.'s operational doctrine.

---

# 8. STAR — COMMAND AUTHORITY

Star serves as one of L.S.I.N.'s primary commanders.

Her responsibilities include:

- dimensional anomaly assessment;
    
- external-space classification;
    
- personnel safety;
    
- interdimensional navigation policy;
    
- Level threat assessment;
    
- diplomatic interaction with external entities;
    
- and executive authorization for high-risk operations.
    

Star's experience allows her to recognize certain dimensional phenomena that conventional researchers may initially misinterpret.

She is also responsible for ensuring that L.S.I.N. personnel do not treat unknown dimensions as ordinary exploration sites.

---

# 9. NIGHTSHADE — COMMAND AUTHORITY

Nightshade serves alongside Star.

Her responsibilities include:

- dimensional reconnaissance;
    
- spatial behavior analysis;
    
- anomalous route assessment;
    
- containment planning;
    
- Level-to-Level relationship analysis;
    
- field-team coordination;
    
- and high-risk navigation oversight.
    

Nightshade's role is particularly important when an anomaly demonstrates unpredictable spatial behavior.

Where conventional mapping attempts to answer:

> **“Where are we?”**

Nightshade's analysis often begins with:

> **“Does 'where' still mean the same thing here?”**

This distinction has prevented multiple navigation failures.

---

# 10. STAR AND NIGHTSHADE — COMMAND RELATIONSHIP

Star and Nightshade operate as a joint command structure.

Neither functions as a ceremonial figurehead.

Both participate directly in:

- operational planning;
    
- Level classification;
    
- field review;
    
- personnel training;
    
- dimensional research;
    
- and emergency response.
    

Their responsibilities overlap intentionally.

L.S.I.N. considers redundant dimensional expertise essential.

If one commander becomes unavailable during a dimensional incident, the other can continue command without requiring immediate transfer to a conventional C.O.R.E. administrator.

---

# 11. GUARDIAN ANGELS

Star and Nightshade's title:

> **Guardian Angels of the Starlands**

is relevant to their L.S.I.N. authority.

Their responsibilities as Guardian Angels involve protecting an external dimensional environment and its inhabitants.

L.S.I.N. requires a similar philosophy.

The organization does not exist merely to destroy anomalies.

It exists to understand them, protect people from them, and prevent unnecessary destruction when containment is possible.

This alignment was one of the principal reasons their appointment was approved.

---

# 12. L.S.I.N. AGENT TRAINING

Personnel assigned to L.S.I.N. undergo specialized training beyond ordinary field certification.

Training includes:

### DIMENSIONAL NAVIGATION

Understanding that spatial relationships may not remain Euclidean or consistent.

### LIMINAL RECOGNITION

Identifying environmental characteristics associated with unstable dimensional boundaries.

### EXIT VERIFICATION

Confirming that an apparent exit actually returns to the expected destination.

### MEMORY AND COGNITIVE SAFETY

Recognizing environments capable of altering memory, perception, identity, or cognition.

### TEAM ACCOUNTING

Maintaining constant awareness of personnel locations.

### ENVIRONMENTAL DOCUMENTATION

Recording anomalies without introducing assumptions into official reports.

### CIVILIAN EXTRACTION

Recovering individuals who entered an anomalous environment without authorization.

# 13. PROFESSIONALISM DURING INCIDENTS

L.S.I.N. agents are specifically trained not to allow unusual circumstances to compromise operational discipline.

Example:

A hallway begins repeating itself.

The correct response is not:

> “What the hell?”

The correct response is:

> “Spatial repetition confirmed. Previous junction has reappeared. Team halt. Marking current position.”

An elevator opens to an ocean.

The correct response is not:

> “Nope.”

The correct response is:

> “Destination inconsistent with mapped Level topology. Door remains closed. Initiating verification.”

A wall begins behaving like water.

The correct response is:

> “Surface phase inconsistency confirmed. Establishing perimeter.”

Personnel may be frightened.

They are still expected to document it.

---

# 14. L.S.I.N. FIELD PHILOSOPHY

The organization operates under a simple principle:

> **UNKNOWN DOES NOT MEAN HOSTILE.**

And equally:

> **UNKNOWN DOES NOT MEAN SAFE.**

Every anomaly receives observation before judgment whenever operational circumstances permit.

This is particularly important within the Backrooms.

Some Levels are dangerous.

Some are harmless.

Some are merely strange.

Some cannot be safely categorized yet.

L.S.I.N. personnel are expected to preserve that distinction.

---

# 15. RELATIONSHIP WITH M.S.A.C.

M.S.A.C. and L.S.I.N. cooperate regularly.

Their areas of expertise differ.

### M.S.A.C.

Primarily optimized for:

- tactical operations;
    
- combat;
    
- hostile-target engagement;
    
- conventional field deployment;
    
- and rapid-response missions.
    

### L.S.I.N.

Primarily optimized for:

- dimensional investigation;
    
- liminal-space navigation;
    
- anomaly classification;
    
- spatial containment;
    
- rescue;
    
- and environmental management.
    

Neither organization replaces the other.

When a liminal environment contains an organized hostile force, M.S.A.C. may be requested.

When the battlefield itself cannot be trusted to remain spatially consistent, L.S.I.N. takes the lead.

---

# 16. OUTPOST PERSONNEL

Aperture's Backrooms outposts are staffed by personnel selected for exceptional reliability.

Outpost personnel are expected to maintain professionalism even during prolonged deployments.

They must be capable of:

- maintaining supplies;
    
- operating communications systems;
    
- monitoring exits;
    
- maintaining Level maps;
    
- supporting rescue operations;
    
- and recognizing deviations from established environmental behavior.
    

An outpost is not merely a shelter.

It is an anchor of conventional procedure inside an unconventional environment.

---

# 17. WHY THIS MATTERS

The Backrooms contain environments where a careless mistake can become a dimensional incident.

A wrong turn may result in:

- separation from the team;
    
- entry into another Level;
    
- loss of the known route;
    
- accidental Frontrooms exposure;
    
- or permanent displacement.
    

L.S.I.N. professionalism therefore exists for a practical reason.

The organization cannot afford personnel treating dimensional anomalies casually.

---

# 18. STARLANDIAN INFLUENCE

Several L.S.I.N. procedures originated from observations made by Star and Nightshade during their work within the Starlands.

These include:

- treating dimensional boundaries as potentially active phenomena;
    
- maintaining redundant navigation references;
    
- verifying exits independently;
    
- documenting environmental changes immediately;
    
- and never assuming that returning through the same opening guarantees returning to the same location.
    

These principles have become standard L.S.I.N. doctrine.

---

# 20. FINAL ASSESSMENT

L.S.I.N. has developed into one of Aperture Industries' most disciplined field organizations.

Its professionalism is not derived from military tradition alone.

It comes from necessity.

A conventional operation can sometimes recover from a mistake.

A dimensional operation may not provide that opportunity.

Star and Nightshade's leadership provides L.S.I.N. with expertise that cannot be replicated through conventional scientific training alone.

They understand external dimensions because they have lived within one.

They understand dimensional guardianship because they already perform it.

And they understand that exploration without discipline is simply another word for getting lost.
