# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Aries Akana has broken down.

Not in the manner personnel have previously observed when he was frustrated, frightened, or overwhelmed.

This is different.

Aries traveled an extraordinary distance to find his younger brother, Jake.

He searched for him.

He endured the uncertainty of not knowing where Jake was, whether he was safe, or whether he would ever see him again.

He eventually found him.

For a time, they were together.

Jake trusted him enough to hold his hand, lean against him, fall asleep beside him, and ask him to stay.

Aries had finally been given the opportunity to be Jake's older brother.

Then someone impersonating Aries used that trust to approach Jake and attack him.

Jake survived that earlier incident.

He did not survive the latest one.

He was found with ten stab wounds and died from blood loss after being left alone while the others departed.

Aries is now confronted with a reality that appears almost impossible for him to reconcile:

**He crossed the distance between himself and Jake, only to lose him to someone who deliberately wore his face.**

The person responsible took advantage of the very resemblance that should have made Jake feel safe.

And now Aries must live with the knowledge that Jake's final experiences of him may have been shaped by fear.

C.O.R.E. Welfare Division has classified Aries's current condition as an acute bereavement crisis requiring immediate, sustained support.

He is not to be left alone with his distress merely because he is physically capable of functioning.

He is not to be expected to return to normal duties.

And he is not to be told that everything happened for a reason.

There is no acceptable reason for what happened to Jake.

---

# 1. THE BROTHER WHO CAME LOOKING

Aries did not grow up beside Jake in the way a brother should have.

Their separation deprived them of years that cannot be recovered.

When Aries eventually found Jake, he did not find an older child who could explain everything that had happened.

He found a six-year-old.

A small boy with white hair, azure eyes, a teddy bear that contained his heartbeat, and a history of suffering that no child should have endured.

Jake was frightened.

He was quiet.

He needed care.

And Aries had to learn how to become his brother while Jake was still learning whether he could trust him.

There were mistakes.

There were misunderstandings.

There were times when Aries failed to realize how badly Jake needed someone to remain beside him.

But there were also moments when Jake reached for him.

Moments when he climbed onto Aries's back.

Moments when he rested against him without speaking.

Moments when he asked for _uppies_ and fell asleep in his arms.

Moments when he said:

> **JAKE:** "Love you."

And Aries answered:

> **ARIES:** "Love you too, little star."

Those moments mattered.

They were evidence that their relationship was not defined exclusively by its painful beginnings.

Aries had not simply found Jake.

He had begun building a life with him.

And then someone turned that life into something terrifying.

---

# 2. THE IMPERSONATION

The previous attack established a particularly cruel method.

The attacker resembled Aries closely enough to exploit Jake's trust.

Jake's account was simple.

Someone who looked like his brother approached him.

The person wore a cloak bearing an unfamiliar symbol.

Then the stabbing began.

Jake attempted to fight back.

The attacker cut his neck and fled when Clover approached.

Jake survived.

But survival did not restore the sense of safety that had existed before the attack.

The person responsible had taken something from Jake that could not be repaired with stitches alone.

The ability to look at Aries without remembering the attacker.

For Jake, the resemblance became a source of fear.

For Aries, that resemblance is now inseparable from the circumstances of his brother's death.

He knows that the attacker was not established to be him.

He knows that someone deliberately impersonated him.

But knowing the distinction intellectually does not necessarily make it easier to endure emotionally.

Because the person who hurt Jake did not merely wear a familiar face.

They used that face to get close enough to hurt him.

And Aries was the one whose face they chose.

---

# 3. THE MOMENT ARIES UNDERSTOOD

The following reconstruction is based on preliminary witness statements and the welfare officer's account. It is not a verbatim recording.

Aries had been informed that Jake's injuries were fatal.

He had already asked when Jake was last seen alive.

He had already asked whether anyone had stayed with him.

He had already asked why they had not brought Jake along.

For several minutes, he remained standing.

Then someone explained that the earlier attacker had resembled him.

Aries stopped moving.

**ARIES:**

"Resembled me?"

The officer clarified that the attacker had apparently impersonated him.

Aries looked down.

**ARIES:**

"He thought it was me."

No one answered immediately.

**ARIES:**

"When he saw him."

A pause.

**ARIES:**

"He thought it was me."

His voice began to break.

**ARIES:**

"Jake saw my face."

He pressed both hands over his mouth.

**ARIES:**

"And then he got hurt."

The officer attempted to explain that the attacker was responsible for the violence.

Aries shook his head.

**ARIES:**

"I know."

He repeated it.

**ARIES:**

"I know it wasn't me."

Another pause.

**ARIES:**

"But he saw me."

He looked toward the medical room.

**ARIES:**

"Or someone who looked like me."

His breathing became uneven.

**ARIES:**

"I went all that way to find him."

He could no longer maintain his composure.

**ARIES:**

"I went all that way."

He sank into the nearest chair.

**ARIES:**

"I finally found my little brother."

His hands began shaking.

**ARIES:**

"And someone used my face to hurt him."

He bent forward.

For several seconds, he was unable to speak.

Then the question came.

Not loudly.

Not angrily.

Almost as though he were asking the room to correct him.

**ARIES:**

"How am I supposed to live with that?"

The interview was terminated.

No one attempted to answer.

---

# 4. ACUTE BEREAVEMENT RESPONSE

Aries has displayed the following observed reactions:

- prolonged periods of silence;
    
- uncontrollable crying;
    
- difficulty maintaining attention;
    
- repeated requests for details already provided;
    
- fixation on the circumstances of the impersonation;
    
- repeated statements concerning the distance he traveled to find Jake;
    
- persistent counterfactual thoughts about what he could have done differently;
    
- difficulty separating his own appearance from the attacker's;
    
- and an inability to discuss Jake's death without returning to the fact that he had finally found him.
    

His distress is compounded by several overlapping losses.

## 4.1 — The Loss of Jake

This is the primary bereavement.

Aries has lost his younger brother.

Not a hypothetical future relationship.

Not the possibility of reconciliation.

The actual child with whom he had begun sharing his life.

The boy who had learned to recognize his footsteps.

The boy who could sit beside him without needing to speak.

The boy who had finally begun to trust that his brother would come back.

## 4.2 — The Loss of Their Future

Aries is also grieving experiences that will never occur.

Jake will not grow older beside him.

He will not become stronger.

He will not discover new favorite foods, complain about uncomfortable clothes, or quietly climb onto Aries's back when walking becomes tiring.

He will not have another opportunity to ask for _uppies_.

He will not fall asleep against Aries again.

There will be no ordinary future in which the brothers gradually learn how to live together without the weight of their separation.

The relationship had only recently begun to recover.

Aries has lost both the person he loved and the time he expected to spend with him.

## 4.3 — The Loss of Safety

The impersonation has introduced another layer of distress.

Aries may now perceive his own appearance as something that was used against Jake.

He cannot change the fact that the attacker resembled him.

He cannot return to the moment before the first attack.

He cannot explain to Jake that the person who hurt him was not his brother.

The opportunity to reassure Jake has been taken away permanently.

This is not merely guilt about an action.

It is grief over an understanding that can no longer be corrected between them.

---

# 5. THE GUILT

Aries has repeatedly returned to one statement:

> **"We could've brought him with us."**

This is not an unreasonable question.

Jake was asleep.

He could have been accompanied.

Someone could have remained with him.

A caregiver handover could have been confirmed.

The circumstances that left him unattended require a serious review.

But Aries's current thinking appears to extend beyond those practical questions.

He is attempting to reconstruct every decision as though there must be one moment in which he could have prevented the entire tragedy.

If he had arrived earlier.

If he had stayed longer.

If he had recognized the danger sooner.

If he had never left.

If he had taken Jake with him.

If he had somehow made it impossible for anyone else to resemble him.

If he had been a better brother.

If he had been there.

If he had been there.

If he had been there.

The repetition has become increasingly distressing.

### Welfare Officer's Assessment

Aries is conflating several different responsibilities.

**The attacker is responsible for the deliberate violence.**

**The security investigation is responsible for establishing how the attacker gained access.**

**The welfare review is responsible for determining why Jake was left unattended and whether failures in supervision contributed to the opportunity for the attack.**

**Aries is responsible for his own actions, not for every action committed by someone who impersonated him.**

These distinctions must be preserved.

It would be equally irresponsible to tell Aries that there was absolutely nothing anyone could have done differently before the evidence is complete.

The purpose of the investigation is to establish what happened, not to manufacture either total innocence or total blame.

Aries does not need a fabricated answer.

He needs the truth, delivered without cruelty.

---

# 6. THE FACE HE CANNOT ESCAPE

The most difficult aspect of the impersonation is that Aries cannot separate himself from the resemblance.

The attacker can remove the cloak.

The attacker can discard the disguise.

The attacker can eventually be identified, apprehended, and held accountable.

Aries will still have the same face.

He will still have the same horns.

He will still be the same older brother Jake traveled through his final months learning to trust.

And now he must confront the possibility that Jake's last memories associated with that appearance included fear.

C.O.R.E. cannot confirm what Jake believed during the final attack.

He was asleep when the latest incident occurred, and the available evidence does not establish whether he recognized the attacker before the fatal injuries were inflicted.

Personnel must not invent a final thought for him.

They must not claim that Jake died believing Aries had betrayed him.

They must not claim that he understood the impersonation.

They must not use speculation to make the tragedy more emotionally conclusive than the evidence permits.

However, Aries's fear that Jake may have associated his face with the attack is genuine.

It deserves to be acknowledged without being presented as an established fact.

During one observation, Aries stood before a mirror in an unoccupied room.

He stared at his reflection for several minutes.

A welfare officer eventually approached the doorway.

**OFFICER:**

"Aries?"

He did not respond.

**OFFICER:**

"Do you need someone to sit with you?"

Aries touched his own cheek.

**ARIES:**

"That's what he saw."

The officer remained quiet.

**ARIES:**

"That's what the person looked like."

He lowered his hand.

**ARIES:**

"Jake was supposed to see me and know he was safe."

His voice broke.

**ARIES:**

"Not be afraid."

He turned away from the mirror.

**ARIES:**

"I don't want him to remember me like that."

The officer did not tell him that Jake would never have been afraid of him.

That would have been an assumption.

Instead, the officer answered:

**OFFICER:**

"The person who hurt him chose to imitate you. That choice belonged to the attacker."

Aries began crying again.

The officer remained nearby.

No further questions were asked.

---

# 7. WHAT ARIES MUST NOT BE ASKED TO DO

Aries must not be expected to transform his grief into a public demonstration of strength.

He must not be told to be strong for Jake.

Jake is dead.

There is no remaining caregiving responsibility through which Aries must prove his love.

He must not be told to move on.

He must not be instructed to forgive the attacker.

He must not be encouraged to regard the incident as a lesson.

He must not be told that Jake would want him to stop crying.

C.O.R.E. has no evidence establishing what Jake would have wanted in this situation.

Most importantly, Aries must not be made responsible for reassuring the other residents that he is not the attacker.

He was impersonated.

The investigation must establish the facts.

He is not required to defend his existence every time someone remembers the crime.

His welfare plan will include:

- immediate access to a trusted support person;
    
- temporary relief from routine duties;
    
- regular checks on sleep, food intake, hydration, and physical condition;
    
- private grief counseling when he is willing to participate;
    
- a designated contact who can remain with him during periods of acute distress;
    
- and protection from intrusive questioning or unauthorized circulation of incident material.
    

These measures are not intended to suppress his grief.

They are intended to ensure that he does not have to endure it entirely alone.

---

# 8. THE QUESTION OF REVENGE

Aries has not made a confirmed threat against any identified individual in the available interviews.

Nevertheless, the intensity of his grief and the deliberate use of his appearance in the earlier attack justify a precautionary assessment.

Personnel must not interpret his distress as proof that he intends to harm someone.

They must also not ignore signs of escalating desperation.

If Aries requests access to restricted security information, weapons, or investigative material, those requests must be evaluated according to ordinary authorization procedures.

He must not be permitted to conduct an unsupervised investigation while in acute distress.

This is not because his grief makes him dangerous by definition.

It is because the person responsible for Jake's death must be identified through reliable evidence, and Aries deserves the opportunity to grieve without being drawn into another potentially fatal confrontation.

**Justice for Jake must not require another member of his family to die.**

---

# 9. THE LAST THING ARIES WANTED

During a later welfare check, Aries was found sitting on the floor beside Jake's recovered belongings.

He had not opened the storage container.

He was simply sitting beside it.

The officer asked whether he wanted the belongings moved to a private room.

Aries shook his head.

**OFFICER:**

"Do you want to keep them here?"

**ARIES:**

"I don't know."

A pause.

**ARIES:**

"I don't know what I'm supposed to do."

The officer sat several feet away.

Aries looked at the closed container.

**ARIES:**

"I came here to find him."

He swallowed.

**ARIES:**

"I thought if I found him, I could take care of him."

He wiped his face.

**ARIES:**

"He didn't need me to be perfect."

His voice became quieter.

**ARIES:**

"He just needed me to stay."

The officer did not interrupt.

**ARIES:**

"And now I can't."

He covered his face with both hands.

**ARIES:**

"I can't even tell him I'm sorry."

The officer remained beside him.

There was no instruction to move on.

No demand that he stop crying.

No suggestion that he look on the bright side.

For several minutes, Aries cried without speaking.

Eventually, he leaned against the wall and closed his eyes.

He did not fall asleep.

He remained there until another trusted resident arrived to sit with him.

---

# 10. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Aries's psychological collapse is consistent with an acute, severe bereavement response complicated by guilt, traumatic circumstances, and the deliberate impersonation of his identity.

The loss is particularly destabilizing because the relationship with Jake had only recently begun to develop into the ordinary sibling bond Aries had traveled so far to find.

The impersonation adds a uniquely cruel element.

The attacker did not simply harm Jake.

The attacker used a resemblance to someone Jake trusted.

That act has left Aries struggling with a form of guilt that cannot be resolved through ordinary apologies.

He cannot apologize to Jake.

He cannot clarify the deception to his brother.

He cannot go back and give Jake a different final experience.

He cannot become someone whose face was never used to frighten him.

He can only remain the person he has always been, while trying to reconcile that identity with what another person did.

C.O.R.E. emphasizes the following:

1. **Aries did not commit the earlier attack merely because the attacker resembled him.**
    
2. **The latest attacker has not been conclusively identified in the available evidence.**
    
3. **The circumstances that left Jake unattended require a separate, evidence-based review.**
    
4. **Aries's grief must not be used as evidence of guilt.**
    
5. **Aries's grief must not be used to excuse any independently established failures of responsibility.**
    
6. **He must receive sustained support rather than being expected to recover through willpower alone.**
    

Aries's distress cannot be resolved by insisting that he should not feel guilty.

The guilt is already there.

The appropriate response is to help him distinguish what he could realistically control from what was deliberately done by another person, while allowing the investigation to establish the remaining facts.

He deserves honesty.

He deserves care.

And he deserves to be remembered as Jake's brother, not permanently reduced to the face someone stole.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Aries went a long way to find Jake.

He did not know how much time they would have.

He did not know how difficult it would be to help a frightened little boy learn to trust him.

He did not know that a stranger would eventually exploit that trust by pretending to be him.

But he came anyway.

He found Jake.

He held him.

He carried him.

He learned which silences meant Jake was comfortable and which meant something was wrong.

He heard the small, quiet voice that Jake rarely used.

He was there for the moments when Jake allowed himself to be a child.

And, for a while, Jake had a brother beside him.

Now Aries is left with memories that have become painful to revisit.

A small hand holding his.

A little boy falling asleep against his shoulder.

A quiet voice saying, "Love you."

And the terrible knowledge that someone used his appearance to approach the child he had traveled so far to protect.

There is no sentence that can make that fair.

There is no explanation that can give Jake his life back.

And there is no requirement that Aries find peace with it today.

The attacker chose to imitate him.

The attacker chose to inflict violence.

Those choices belong to the person responsible.

Aries does not have to accept the attacker's guilt as his own in order to acknowledge how deeply Jake was hurt.

He does not have to stop loving Jake simply because loving him now hurts.

And he does not have to stop grieving to prove that he is still standing.

For now, the only requirement is that someone remains beside Aries while he faces the loss.

Someone who does not ask him to be strong.

Someone who does not ask him to explain himself.

Someone who does not leave simply because he has become difficult to comfort.

Because the last thing Aries needs is to discover that, after losing his little brother, he must also endure the aftermath entirely alone.

He went all that way to find Jake.

He found him.

He loved him.

And someone took him away.

**Jake was not a mistake.**

**Finding him was not a mistake.**

**Loving him was not a mistake.**

Aries must be allowed to grieve without being taught to regret the one thing that gave his brother a family.

— **C.O.R.E. Psychological Welfare Division**
