# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### ARCHIVAL STORAGE AND PRESERVATION EXECUTIVE

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-ARC-4C-UCA-014  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — ANOMALOUS OBSERVATION  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**SUBJECT:** SAFEHOUSE Elevator — Unassigned Floor  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE / Main Elevator

---

# 1. OBSERVATION

A faint accumulation of **blue particulate matter** has been detected around the elevator button corresponding to the missing floor.

The particles resemble fine Stardust.

They are visible only under certain lighting conditions.

At normal distance, the area appears unremarkable.

Closer inspection reveals small blue particles settled along:

- the button's outer edge;
    
- the surrounding panel;
    
- the seam between the button and its housing.
    

No corresponding particulate contamination has been detected elsewhere in the elevator.

---

# 2. BUTTON STATUS

The button remains non-functional.

Attempts to press it produce no response.

No floor number is displayed.

The button cannot be selected through the elevator's normal interface.

No destination is registered.

No elevator movement occurs.

The button is effectively:

> **There.**

But not usable.

Personnel have therefore largely stopped paying attention to it.

---

# 3. RESIDENT RESPONSE

No resident has expressed significant concern regarding the Stardust.

Most personnel who notice the blue particles assume they are:

- dust;
    
- residue;
    
- a reflection;
    
- an artifact of the elevator's lighting;
    
- or simply something that has always been there.
    

No one has connected the Stardust to the missing floor.

No one has connected the missing floor to a person.

No one remembers why the button should matter.

---

# 4. CLEANING ATTEMPT

A routine cleaning attempt was conducted.

The Stardust was wiped away.

Approximately six hours later:

**It returned.**

No source was identified.

A second cleaning attempt produced the same result.

The particles appear to reaccumulate without any detectable physical source.

C.O.R.E. has therefore suspended further cleaning attempts.

There is no immediate hazard.

---

# 5. N.O.S.T. OBSERVATION

N.O.S.T. personnel were notified due to the particulate's apparent astronomical characteristics.

Their preliminary response was brief.

> **N.O.S.T. ANALYST:**  
> "That's Stardust."

> **C.O.R.E. ARCHIVIST:**  
> "From what?"

Pause.

> **N.O.S.T. ANALYST:**  
> "Unknown."

No further analysis was requested.

---

# 6. RESIDENT INTERACTION

Alice was observed standing inside the elevator.

She looked at the missing button.

Then at the blue particles around it.

She reached toward it.

Stopped.

> **ALICE:**  
> "Oh."

She stared at it for several seconds.

> **ALICE:**  
> "Can't press it."

She lowered her hand.

Then selected another floor.

The elevator doors closed.

---

# 7. ADDITIONAL OBSERVATION

Neri later passed through the elevator.

She noticed the Stardust.

She stopped.

For approximately four seconds, she stared at it.

She raised one hand.

Touched a single particle.

It disappeared against her fingertip.

Neri looked at her hand.

Then at the button.

She appeared confused.

She eventually shrugged.

The elevator continued downward.

---

# 8. CURRENT ASSESSMENT

The Stardust presents no confirmed danger.

The missing elevator destination remains inaccessible.

The particulate has not interfered with elevator operation.

No resident has demonstrated sustained interest in the phenomenon.

The matter has therefore been classified as:

> **LOW PRIORITY — UNRESOLVED**

C.O.R.E. will continue passive observation.

No active intervention is currently authorized.

---

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

There is a button in the elevator that nobody can use.

Nobody remembers what it was for.

Nobody remembers who it belonged to.

Nobody knows why the floor disappeared.

And now there is faint blue Stardust gathering around it.

Nobody seems particularly concerned.

The elevator still works.

The button cannot be pressed.

So everyone has decided it is probably nothing.

The Stardust remains.

**— END DOCUMENT**
