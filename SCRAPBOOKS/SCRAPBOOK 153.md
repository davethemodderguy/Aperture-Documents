# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **JOINT MISSING PERSON INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – ACTIVE SEARCH OPERATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following Corin Lockwood's resignation from the Krampus Compliance Authority and her subsequent disappearance, a coordinated search operation was initiated.

Three independent organizations began searching simultaneously.

- Heaven's Blacksite.
    
- Krampus Compliance Authority (KCA).
    
- The Merryweather Family.
    

Although each organization operates independently, all agreed upon one objective.

**Find Corin alive.**

---

# **LAST CONFIRMED SIGHTING**

The final confirmed footage shows Corin leaving KCA Headquarters carrying a small backpack.

She wore ordinary civilian clothing.

She carried no visible weapons.

She declined company transportation.

She walked away alone.

After leaving the monitored district, no further confirmed sightings were obtained.

Her mobile devices were left behind.

No financial transactions have been recorded.

No official identification has been used.

Corin appeared to have intentionally made herself difficult to trace.

---

# **SEARCH OPERATION**

The search expanded across surrounding districts within hours.

Blacksite Security coordinated surveillance review.

KCA investigators examined transportation hubs, shelters, and public records.

The Merryweather Family mobilized their own trusted contacts.

No organization attempted to compete with the others.

Information was shared immediately.

Every lead was followed.

Every reported sighting was investigated.

None were confirmed.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE**

Corin's room remains exactly as she left it.

Her blanket remains folded.

Her notebooks remain on the shelf.

The small bowl of lemons in the kitchen continues to be replaced.

Nobody has suggested clearing the room.

Nobody has suggested assigning it to someone else.

As Belle quietly stated,

> "She's still one of us."

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi refused to stop searching.

She walked places Corin used to visit.

Quiet parks.

Gardens.

Train platforms.

The edge of the botanical dome.

She called her daughter's name countless times.

Only silence answered.

One evening, Lumi stood beneath falling snow and whispered,

> "You don't have to come home today."

A tear rolled down her face.

> "...Just let me know you're alive."

---

# **NORMA**

Norma blamed herself.

Repeatedly.

Medical staff noted she had begun sleeping poorly and skipping meals while participating in the search.

When reminded to rest, she answered only:

> "...How can I?"

> "...My little sister is alone."

---

# **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus rarely spoke during the operation.

He volunteered for every search assignment offered.

After another unsuccessful day, Kent quietly asked him if he wanted to take a break.

Arcturus looked toward the city skyline.

> "...Not until she's found."

---

# **BELLE**

Belle continued carrying one extra cup whenever tea was prepared.

No one commented on it.

The empty cup remained beside hers.

Every evening.

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ORDER**

Director Lucious suspended all disciplinary proceedings related to Corin's resignation.

He issued a standing directive to every participating organization.

> "This is not a retrieval operation."

> "This is a welfare search."

> "If Corin is located, her safety comes before her employment."

The order was unanimously accepted.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin Lockwood remains missing.

There is no evidence of criminal involvement or foul play at this time, though her welfare remains a matter of serious concern due to her documented psychological vulnerability and history of severe trauma.

The joint search operation remains active.

Throughout Heaven's Blacksite, KCA Headquarters, and the Merryweather Family estate, one hope is shared by everyone involved.

Not that Corin returns to work.

Not that Corin returns to Accounting.

Simply...

That Corin returns alive.

**End of Joint Missing Person Incident Report**
