# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – LINGUISTIC DEVELOPMENT UPDATE**

### **DESIGNATION: M.A.R.T. BLOBS – VOCABULARY EXPANSION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Research Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Contrary to previous conclusions, the Mart vocabulary is **not** permanently fixed.

Although the species remains incapable of understanding complex grammar, prolonged exposure to enthusiastic teaching has resulted in the acquisition of several additional words.

The instructor responsible has been identified as:

**Star.**

Research is still unsure how she succeeded after every previous attempt had failed.

---

# **NEW VOCABULARY**

The following words have been consistently observed across the Mart hivemind.

> "Friend!"

> "Pretty!"

> "Thank you!"

> "Yay!"

> "Happy!"

These words are generally used appropriately.

Sentence structure remains absent.

For example:

> "Hello!"

> "Friend!"

> "Happy!"

Is interpreted by nearby personnel as:

_"Hello, friend! I'm happy!"_

---

# **HOW THEY LEARNED**

For several weeks, Star spent portions of nearly every afternoon sitting with groups of Marts in the SAFEHOUSE courtyard.

Rather than conducting structured lessons, she simply talked to them while pointing at objects, smiling, laughing, and repeating simple words.

Eventually...

One Mart looked up at her and said:

> "Friend!"

Star reportedly cried tears of joy.

Within the next twenty-four hours, every Mart in the Blacksite knew the word.

The hivemind had spread it instantly.

---

# **RELATIONSHIP WITH STAR**

The Mart colony has developed an unmistakable fondness for Star.

Whenever she enters an area, nearby Marts frequently gather around her.

Observed behaviors include:

- Bouncing excitedly.
    
- Following her through hallways.
    
- Bringing flowers.
    
- Forming tiny circles around her while she sits.
    
- Attempting to imitate her laughter.
    

Researchers believe the Marts view Star as one of the most approachable individuals in the Blacksite.

---

# **COGNITIVE NOTE**

Despite learning new words, individual Mart intelligence remains approximately equivalent to that of an average mouse.

The additional vocabulary reflects improved communication rather than increased reasoning ability.

The colony simply has a few more ways to express what it already felt.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Mart vocabulary now extends beyond simple greetings and farewells.

Although still limited, their newfound words have made interactions with personnel noticeably more expressive.

Research Division has officially approved Star's informal lessons to continue.

There is one concern, however.

If Star continues at her current pace...

The Marts may eventually learn enough words to start telling jokes.

Several researchers have expressed uncertainty as to whether the Blacksite is prepared for that possibility.
