# 1. INFORMAL DESIGNATION

Nora Tachyon has acquired an unofficial SAFEHOUSE nickname.

It was assigned by:

**NEKO**

The nickname is:

> **“Nom Nom Shark.”**

The designation originated from Nora's eating habits.

It has since become surprisingly accurate.

---

# 2. DIETARY OBSERVATION

Nora eats.

A lot.

More specifically:

> **Nora eats an alarming amount of cake.**

She also enjoys:

- sweets;
    
- bread;
    
- pastries;
    
- snacks;
    
- chocolate;
    
- hot chocolate;
    
- regular meals;
    
- leftovers;
    
- food belonging to other people, provided she has permission;
    
- and apparently anything that Neko has not specifically told her is not edible.
    

Nora's appetite is considerably larger than expected for her size.

Her response to discovering food is remarkably consistent.

She eats it.

---

# 3. ORIGIN OF THE NICKNAME

Neko was working in the SAFEHOUSE kitchen when Nora entered.

There was a cake on the counter.

Nora saw it.

Neko saw Nora see it.

Nora walked toward the cake.

Neko moved the cake slightly.

Nora followed it.

Neko moved it again.

Nora followed again.

Finally:

> **NEKO:**  
> “You know what?”

Nora looked up.

> **NEKO:**  
> “You're just a nom nom shark.”

Nora blinked.

> **NORA:**  
> “...What?”

Neko pointed at the cake.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Nom nom.”

Nora looked at the cake.

Then back at Neko.

> **NORA:**  
> “Can I have it?”

Neko sighed.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Yes, Nom Nom Shark.”

Nora took the cake.

The nickname remained.

# 4. CAKE INCIDENT FREQUENCY

C.O.R.E. has identified a recurring sequence:

**Cake appears.**

↓

**Nora notices cake.**

↓

**Nora approaches cake.**

↓

**Nora consumes cake.**

↓

**Nora looks for more cake.**

The process is remarkably efficient.

Attempts to distract her with other activities have produced inconsistent results.

Cake remains the strongest known motivator.

---

# 5. GUIDING LIGHT / BAO CAKE

Nora's strongest enthusiasm is reserved for cake prepared by people she considers family.

In particular:

**Bao's baking**

and

**Guiding Light's Moonlight Muffins / related sweets**

are treated with considerable importance.

Nora will sometimes quietly wait nearby while they are being prepared.

She may pretend not to be waiting.

She is waiting.

# 10. C.O.R.E. OBSERVATION

Nora's appetite should not be interpreted negatively.

Her increased food intake is consistent with her unusually adaptive physiology and current growth requirements.

Additionally, food appears to have significant emotional importance to her.

Cake in particular carries associations with:

- family;
    
- her younger sister;
    
- celebrations;
    
- safety;
    
- and people making something specifically for her.
    

Therefore, while **“glutton”** is technically an accurate informal description of her eating habits, personnel should remember that food is also one of Nora's strongest sources of comfort.
