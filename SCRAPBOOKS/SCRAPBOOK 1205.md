# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

MSAC has successfully convinced Nora Tachyon to convince Neri Tachyon to authorize additional production of the **Deadeye**.

The request was approved.

Neri has authorized:

> **10 additional Deadeyes**

for MSAC distribution.

Nora's personal Deadeye is **not included** in this count.

Total known Deadeye production:

> **11 units**

**001 — Nora Tachyon**  
**002–011 — MSAC Allocation**

The authorization immediately produced an unusual level of excitement throughout MSAC.

---

# 2. HOW MSAC GOT APPROVAL

The original Deadeye was produced specifically for Nora.

MSAC personnel subsequently discovered that Nora was capable of influencing Neri's production decisions.

This resulted in an unofficial campaign.

Personnel approached Nora.

They asked politely.

They complimented the Deadeye.

They listened to Nora talk about the shark decorations.

They asked questions.

They did not pressure her.

Eventually, Nora agreed to ask Neri.

Nora approached her sister.

> **NORA:**  
> “Sis?”

Neri looked up from her work.

> **NERI:**  
> “What?”

> **NORA:**  
> “The MSAC people want Deadeyes.”

Pause.

> **NERI:**  
> “How many?”

> **NORA:**  
> “Ten.”

Neri considered this.

> **NERI:**  
> “Okay.”

Nora stared.

> **NORA:**  
> “...Really?”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Ten.”

Nora immediately ran back toward MSAC.

> **NORA:**  
> “SHE SAID YES!”

The resulting celebration was described by one observer as:

> **“Completely disproportionate to the situation.”**

# 5. THE BLACKLIST

Neri included one significant condition with the authorization.

Existing **Night Stalker owners are prohibited from receiving a Deadeye through this production batch.**

They are also prohibited from participating in any Deadeye acquisition trial associated with this batch.

The stated reason was:

> **“Save some merch for the others, no need to have 2 pistols that do the same thing.”**

The statement has been entered into the official record.

It has also caused considerable distress among the Night Stalker owners.

# 11. DISTRIBUTION POLICY

The ten Deadeyes will therefore be allocated exclusively among eligible MSAC personnel.

Current restrictions:

### Eligible

- MSAC personnel without a Night Stalker;
    
- personnel meeting standard authorization requirements;
    
- personnel approved through the Deadeye distribution process.
    

### Ineligible

- existing Night Stalker owners;
    
- unauthorized personnel;
    
- personnel attempting to bypass the allocation process;
    
- anyone attempting to obtain a Deadeye through Nora without Neri's approval.
    

The Deadeye remains a limited Tachyon Industries item.

No unrestricted armory distribution is planned.

# 14. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The Deadeye program has officially transitioned from:

> **“Neri made one for Nora.”**

to:

> **“Neri has made ten more because Nora asked.”**

The production remains limited.

The distribution is intentionally broader than the Night Stalker program.

Neri's reasoning is straightforward:

She does not see value in giving the same person multiple Tachyon Industries pistols that occupy substantially similar roles when other personnel have none.

Her exact phrasing remains:

> **“Save some merch for the others, no need to have 2 pistols that do the same thing.”**

Jake, Elsher, and the MSAC Commander have accepted the restriction.

Jake is still unhappy.

Elsher is mostly fine.

> **“I still wanted one.”**

None of the three will receive a Deadeye through this batch.
