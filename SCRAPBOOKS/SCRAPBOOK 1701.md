# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Clover Luminare accidentally caused Jane Doe to consume a strawberry.

This resulted in Jane's established anomalous transformation.

Jane Doe became:

> **John Doe.**

The transformation remains consistent with previous observations.

Upon consuming strawberries:

- Jane's physical sex characteristics temporarily change;
    
- her chest flattens;
    
- her facial structure becomes more masculine;
    
- her voice becomes distinctly masculine;
    
- her overall appearance changes sufficiently to be recognized as a separate presentation;
    
- her identity remains psychologically continuous.
    

Clover had never previously encountered John Doe.

She therefore initially believed she had just met a completely different person.

This misunderstanding lasted approximately forty seconds.

---

# 1. THE INCIDENT

Clover had been preparing food in the SAFEHOUSE kitchen.

Jane was nearby.

A bowl of fruit had been placed on the counter.

Among the fruit were several strawberries.

Clover picked one up.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Jane, do you want this?"

Jane glanced over.

> **JANE:**  
> "Sure."

Clover tossed it.

Jane caught it.

She ate it.

There was a pause.

Clover continued preparing food.

Then Jane's body changed.

Clover stopped.

Jane stopped.

The room became silent.

---

# 2. TRANSFORMATION

The transformation was rapid.

Jane's chest flattened.

Her facial structure shifted.

Her body changed into its established masculine configuration.

Her voice changed with it.

Jane looked down at herself.

Then toward Clover.

> **JOHN:**  
> "...Oh."

Clover stared.

John stared back.

Clover slowly lowered the utensil she was holding.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Who are you?"

John blinked.

> **JOHN:**  
> "Jane."

Clover stared harder.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "You're not Jane."

John sighed.

> **JOHN:**  
> "Yes, I am."

Clover looked toward the empty strawberry container.

Then back at John.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "...Oh."

---

# 3. FIRST IMPRESSION

Clover had never personally witnessed Jane's transformation.

She had heard references to it.

She had simply never connected the information to a practical situation.

The first thing she noticed was the voice.

It was unmistakably masculine.

Clover tilted her head.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Your voice."

> **JOHN:**  
> "Yeah."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "It's different."

> **JOHN:**  
> "That's usually the first thing people notice."

Clover nodded.

She then looked at him again.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "You're a man."

> **JOHN:**  
> "Temporarily."

Clover nodded slowly.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Okay."

---

# 4. THE STRAWBERRY

Clover looked at the remaining strawberries.

She immediately realized what had happened.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "I gave you one."

> **JOHN:**  
> "Yes."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "I forgot."

> **JOHN:**  
> "I noticed."

Clover looked genuinely apologetic.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Sorry."

John shrugged.

> **JOHN:**  
> "It happens."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "I've never seen it before."

> **JOHN:**  
> "First time?"

> **CLOVER:**  
> "First time."

John smiled.

> **JOHN:**  
> "Congratulations."

Clover looked confused.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "For what?"

> **JOHN:**  
> "Meeting John Doe."

---

# 5. JAKE ARRIVES

Jake entered the kitchen carrying his teddy bear.

He looked at John.

Then Clover.

Then John again.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Hi, John."

Clover immediately looked at Jake.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "You know him?"

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Yeah."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Why didn't anyone tell me?"

Jake shrugged.

> **JAKE:**  
> "You didn't ask."

Clover considered this.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "...Fair."

John laughed.

His voice remained completely masculine.

---

# 6. JANE / JOHN EXPLANATION

Clover eventually sat down.

John explained the situation.

> **JOHN:**  
> "Strawberries do this."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Every time?"

> **JOHN:**  
> "Every time."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "How long?"

> **JOHN:**  
> "Depends."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "On what?"

John shrugged.

> **JOHN:**  
> "Apparently reality's sense of humor."

Clover nodded.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "That sounds inconvenient."

> **JOHN:**  
> "It is."

---

# 7. CLOVER'S OBSERVATION

Clover studied him for several seconds.

Not suspiciously.

Professionally.

She was simply trying to understand the anomaly.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "So you're still Jane."

> **JOHN:**  
> "Yes."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Same memories?"

> **JOHN:**  
> "Yes."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Same personality?"

> **JOHN:**  
> "Unfortunately."

Clover smiled.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Okay."

---

# 8. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri eventually walked into the kitchen.

She looked at John.

Then at Clover.

Then at the strawberry.

Neri adjusted her glasses.

> **NERI:**  
> "Strawberry?"

John nodded.

Neri nodded back.

She understood immediately.

Clover looked at her.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "You knew?"

Neri nodded.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Why didn't you tell me?"

Neri shrugged.

> **NERI:**  
> "Didn't ask."

Clover stared at her.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "...You two are terrible."

Neri took a sip of coffee.

---

# 9. CLOVER'S SECOND ENCOUNTER

Approximately fifteen minutes later, Clover had become completely comfortable with John.

She handed him a cup.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Coffee?"

> **JOHN:**  
> "Sure."

He took it.

Clover sat opposite him.

There was no awkwardness.

She had already adjusted to the fact that Jane could temporarily become John.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "So."

John looked at her.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Do I call you John while you're like this?"

> **JOHN:**  
> "Please."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Okay."

Pause.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Nice to meet you, John."

John smiled.

> **JOHN:**  
> "Nice to meet you too."

---

# 10. JANE'S EVENTUAL RETURN

Later, the transformation ended.

Jane returned to her normal female form.

Clover looked at her.

Then at the strawberry bowl.

Then back at Jane.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "You're back."

> **JANE:**  
> "Yep."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Welcome back."

Jane smiled.

> **JANE:**  
> "Thanks."

Clover hesitated.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "John is nice."

Jane blinked.

Then smiled.

> **JANE:**  
> "He'd appreciate that."

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

No lasting medical consequences occurred.

Clover's accidental exposure of Jane to strawberries was not malicious.

The transformation was temporary and fully reversible.

Clover has now been formally introduced to both presentations of Jane Doe.

The incident also demonstrates that Clover adapts rapidly to unusual circumstances.

Her initial confusion lasted less than one minute.

After receiving a basic explanation, she simply accepted the situation.

No prolonged concern was observed.

---

## FINAL OBSERVATION

Clover's first meeting with John Doe lasted less than an hour.

Her initial response was:

> **"Who are you?"**

John's response was:

> **"Jane."**

Clover did not believe him.

Approximately forty seconds later, she understood.

By the end of the encounter, she was casually drinking coffee with him.

When Jane returned, Clover's final assessment was simple:

> **"John is nice."**

C.O.R.E. has no objections.

The remaining strawberries have been relocated.
