# SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT

## NERI — REPEATED SYNCOPE / BEHAVIORAL RESPONSE

**Subject:** Neri  
**Primary Observer:** Jane Doe  
**Individuals referenced:** Bao, Frost, Corin, Orphie, Alice  
**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL / HIGH CONCERN

---

## 1. IT KEPT HAPPENING

Jane started noticing a pattern.

Neri kept fainting around her.

Not always dramatically.

Sometimes Neri would be walking beside Jane and simply stop responding.

Sometimes she would sit down, rest her head against her hands, and remain completely still.

Sometimes she would be reading.

Sometimes she would be eating.

Sometimes she would simply be standing there.

And sometimes Jane wouldn't notice immediately.

That was what frightened her.

Neri had become very good at being unconscious while looking almost completely normal.

The episodes were also taking a toll.

Neri was tired.

Not merely sleepy.

**Exhausted.**

Her body was repeatedly forcing itself through episodes that left her drained afterward.

Yet whenever Jane asked:

> **Jane:** "How are you feeling?"

The answer remained:

> **Neri:** "Fine."

---

# 2. THE FIFTH TIME

Jane had been working.

Neri was nearby.

Jane heard a quiet sound behind her.

She turned.

Neri was sitting against the wall.

Eyes closed this time.

> **Jane:** "Neri?"

No answer.

Jane immediately stopped what she was doing.

She checked on her.

Again.

Another episode.

Again, medical.

Again, Bao.

Again, stabilization.

Again, Neri waking up.

And this time Jane was tired too.

Not physically.

Emotionally.

She had watched it happen too many times.

She had warned Neri.

She had explained it.

She had asked nicely.

She had begged.

She had established rules.

And Neri still kept doing the same thing.

When Neri finally became conscious, Jane was sitting beside her.

Neri opened her eyes.

> **Neri:** "…Hi."

Jane stared at her.

> **Jane:** "No."

Neri blinked.

> **Neri:** "What?"

> **Jane:** "No. Don't 'hi' me."

Neri became quiet.

---

# 3. JANE LOSES HER TEMPER

> **Jane:** "How many times do I have to tell you?"

Neri looked down.

> **Jane:** "How many?"

No response.

> **Jane:** "You keep fainting around me."

> **Neri:** "Sorry."

> **Jane:** "Stop apologizing!"

Neri flinched.

Jane immediately regretted raising her voice.

But she was already frustrated.

> **Jane:** "You don't tell me."

> **Jane:** "You don't tell Bao."

> **Jane:** "You don't tell anyone."

> **Jane:** "You just sit there until you collapse!"

Neri's shoulders lowered.

> **Neri:** "I'm sorry."

> **Jane:** "That's not the point!"

Neri went silent.

Jane realized she was doing exactly what she had promised herself she wouldn't do.

She was angry because she cared.

But Neri couldn't necessarily hear the second half.

She was only hearing:

**You're doing something wrong.**

---

# 4. JANE SEES IT

Neri's expression changed.

Not into anger.

Not fear.

Something much quieter.

She looked almost ashamed.

> **Neri:** "I'll try not to."

Jane stopped.

> **Jane:** "Not to what?"

> **Neri:** "Be a problem."

Jane's anger disappeared immediately.

> **Jane:** "Neri..."

> **Neri:** "I'll stay away when you're working."

> **Jane:** "That's not what I—"

> **Neri:** "I'll stop bothering everyone."

Jane stared at her.

> **Neri:** "I'm sorry."

Jane finally understood.

She hadn't made Neri think:

**"I need to take better care of myself."**

She had accidentally made Neri think:

**"I need to be less troublesome."**

Those were completely different conclusions.

---

# 5. JANE STARTS LOOKING BACK

Jane began asking herself how many times this had happened.

Not the fainting.

The scolding.

Bao had done it.

Frost had done it.

Corin had done it.

Orphie had done it.

Alice had done it.

Every one of them had their own reason.

Every one of them loved Neri.

Every one of them had eventually reached the same point:

> _"You have to stop doing this to yourself."_

> _"You need to tell us."_

> _"You can't keep treating yourself like you're disposable."_

> _"Why won't you let us help you?"_

Every sentence came from love.

Every sentence made perfect sense to the person saying it.

But Jane suddenly wondered what Neri heard.

---

# 6. THE PATTERN

Jane started reconstructing Neri's perspective.

Neri gets sick.

Neri hides it.

Someone discovers her.

Someone becomes frightened.

Someone gets angry.

Neri apologizes.

Everyone tells her she needs to change.

Neri concludes:

> **"I caused another problem."**

So next time—

She tries even harder not to bother anyone.

Which means she hides her symptoms longer.

Which means the next episode is worse.

Which means everyone becomes even more frightened.

Which means someone gets angry again.

The cycle repeats.

Jane stared at the floor.

> **Jane:** "Oh."

She finally understood.

They had been trying to pull Neri out of the hole.

But every time they shouted at her from above, Neri interpreted the shouting as another reason she didn't deserve to climb out.

---

# 7. WHAT NERI ACTUALLY BELIEVES

Jane eventually asked her.

Not angrily.

Not therapeutically.

Just quietly.

> **Jane:** "Neri."

> **Neri:** "Hm?"

> **Jane:** "What do you think happens when you need help?"

Neri thought.

> **Neri:** "People get annoyed."

Jane swallowed.

> **Jane:** "Why?"

> **Neri:** "Because I can't do things myself."

> **Jane:** "And?"

> **Neri:** "Because I get in the way."

Jane waited.

Neri's voice became quieter.

> **Neri:** "Because I'm not useful."

Jane's expression fell.

There it was.

Not _"I don't deserve help."_

Something deeper.

**"I don't have value unless I'm useful."**

---

# 8. "I'M NOT ANYONE'S."

Neri continued.

> **Neri:** "Everyone has someone."

Jane said nothing.

> **Neri:** "Mom has Frost."

> **Neri:** "Frost has Mom."

> **Neri:** "Alice has Orphie and Corin."

> **Neri:** "Everyone has someone."

She looked down at her hands.

> **Neri:** "I'm just Neri."

Jane's heart sank.

> **Neri:** "I'm not anyone's."

That sentence hurt Jane.

Because it wasn't literally true.

Neri had people who loved her.

Bao loved her.

Frost cared about her.

Alice cared about her.

Corin cared about her.

Orphie cared about her.

Jane cared about her.

The SAFEHOUSE cared about her.

But Neri didn't **feel** like she belonged to anyone.

And that was different.

---

# 9. THE WORD "EXPENDABLE"

Jane finally understood why the word mattered so much.

Neri didn't necessarily think:

> _"I want to die."_

She thought:

> _"If I disappear, things will continue."_

That was why her own death during anemic episodes didn't frighten her enough.

She considered herself replaceable.

Useful things were kept.

Important things were protected.

People who belonged somewhere were missed.

But Neri?

She believed she could simply be removed from the equation.

And the equation would still work.

Jane's stomach turned.

---

# 10. JANE TELLS HER THE TRUTH

Jane sat beside her.

> **Jane:** "I've been getting angry at you."

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:** "I know."

> **Jane:** "Because I'm scared."

Neri looked at her.

> **Jane:** "Not because you're useless."

Neri didn't speak.

> **Jane:** "Not because you're annoying."

> **Jane:** "Not because you're a burden."

Jane paused.

> **Jane:** "Because I care about you."

Neri looked away.

> **Neri:** "Then why does everyone get angry?"

Jane didn't have a quick answer.

Because Neri was right.

They did.

Not because they disliked her.

Because they were terrified.

But Jane realized something important:

**Neri couldn't be expected to automatically understand the difference.**

---

# 11. JANE'S APOLOGY

> **Jane:** "I'm sorry."

Neri looked surprised.

> **Neri:** "Why?"

> **Jane:** "I yelled at you."

> **Neri:** "I deserved it."

Jane immediately shook her head.

> **Jane:** "No."

Neri blinked.

> **Jane:** "You don't deserve to be yelled at for being sick."

> **Neri:** "But I didn't tell you."

> **Jane:** "That's something we need to work on."

Jane gently tapped the table.

> **Jane:** "But you're not in trouble for having a body that doesn't cooperate."

Neri remained silent.

> **Jane:** "And you're not in trouble for needing help."

---

# 12. "YOU ARE SOMEONE'S"

Jane looked directly at her.

> **Jane:** "You said you're not anyone's."

Neri nodded.

> **Jane:** "You're wrong."

Neri frowned.

> **Neri:** "How?"

Jane smiled faintly.

> **Jane:** "You're Bao's daughter."

Neri looked down.

> **Jane:** "You're Frost's family."

> **Jane:** "You're Alice's friend."

> **Jane:** "You're Corin's friend."

> **Jane:** "You're Orphie's family."

Neri's eyes began watering.

Jane continued.

> **Jane:** "And you're my friend."

Neri wiped her eyes.

> **Neri:** "But..."

> **Jane:** "No buts."

Jane paused.

> **Jane:** "You don't have to be useful to belong to somebody."

That sentence made Neri completely still.

---

# 13. THE PART JANE HAD TO LEARN

Jane had spent years treating information as something to extract.

If something was hidden, she dug deeper.

If somebody wouldn't talk, she found another way.

If there was a problem, she solved it.

Neri wasn't an intelligence problem.

She wasn't a lock.

She wasn't information that needed to be extracted.

Jane couldn't force Neri to suddenly believe she was valuable.

And yelling certainly wasn't going to do it.

So Jane changed her approach.

No more:

> **"Why didn't you tell me?"**

Instead:

> **"I'm glad you told me."**

No more:

> **"Stop being reckless."**

Instead:

> **"Let's keep you safe."**

No more:

> **"You're worrying everyone."**

Instead:

> **"People worry because they want you here."**

And most importantly:

No more making Neri feel like her illness itself was a failure.

---

# 14. THE NEXT TIME

Several days later, Neri was walking beside Jane.

Jane was working.

Neri became quiet.

Jane looked over.

> **Jane:** "Neri?"

Neri hesitated.

> **Neri:** "I feel weird."

Jane immediately stopped.

No frustration.

No lecture.

No questions about why she waited.

Just:

> **Jane:** "Okay."

Jane called for help.

Neri sat down.

Jane stayed beside her.

> **Neri:** "Sorry."

Jane shook her head.

> **Jane:** "Don't apologize."

> **Neri:** "But I interrupted you."

> **Jane:** "Good."

Neri looked confused.

> **Neri:** "Good?"

Jane smiled.

> **Jane:** "You bothered me."

Neri stared.

> **Jane:** "I'm proud of you."

Neri looked down.

A tiny smile appeared.

---

# 15. JANE'S FINAL ASSESSMENT

Jane's conclusion was not that Neri needed to be told more forcefully that she was important.

She had heard that already.

Repeatedly.

What Neri needed was **evidence**.

Evidence that asking for help did not make people leave.

Evidence that being sick did not make her worthless.

Evidence that being unable to contribute did not make her disposable.

Evidence that people wanted her around even when she wasn't doing anything.

Evidence that she belonged.

And above all:

> **Neri needs to experience being cared for without having to earn it.**

Jane's final note:

> _"We all kept telling her that she matters."_
> 
> _"She kept hearing that she was causing problems."_
> 
> _"We kept telling her to stop treating herself as expendable."_
> 
> _"She kept hearing that she was failing us."_
> 
> _"And I think I finally understand why."_
> 
> _"She doesn't need another person yelling that she should care about herself."_
> 
> _"She needs someone to stay."_
> 
> _"So I'm staying."_

**END OF OBSERVATION**
