# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The investigation into Project: Silverline has produced a significant update concerning Anby's eleven sisters.

Of the original twelve artificial humans deployed by Silverline, **six identities have now been confirmed.**

The confirmed survivors or known individuals are:

- **Anby**
    
- **Twiggy**
    
- **Solider 11**
    
- **Sora**
    
- **Phaethon**
    
- **Iris**
    

The remaining six sisters were not recovered.

Recovered Silverline records indicate that the remaining subjects were **killed and subsequently disposed of by Project: Silverline operators.**

Their remains have not been recovered.

Their deaths are considered confirmed based on internal Silverline documentation.

This means Anby's previous belief that all eleven sisters might still be alive is no longer supported by the recovered records.

---

# 1. THE ORIGINAL TWELVE

Project: Silverline maintained twelve artificial-human subjects as a connected experimental group.

The recovered records indicate that the twelve were repeatedly trained and deployed together.

Anby was one of them.

The known designations are now:

|Subject|Current Status|
|---|---|
|**Anby**|Recovered — SAFEHOUSE|
|**Twiggy**|Confirmed|
|**Solider 11**|Confirmed|
|**Sora**|Confirmed|
|**Phaethon**|Confirmed|
|**Iris**|Confirmed|
|**Six additional subjects**|Deceased — Silverline records|

The exact circumstances surrounding the five confirmed sisters remain under investigation.

---

# 2. TWIGGY

**STATUS:** CONFIRMED

Twiggy is now recognized as one of Anby's sisters.

The recovered records establish that Twiggy belonged to the same Silverline experimental group.

Additional information regarding her current whereabouts is being withheld from Anby until the information can be independently verified.

The KCA has not yet confirmed whether Twiggy is currently accessible to recovery personnel.

---

# 3. SOLIDER 11

**STATUS:** CONFIRMED

The subject designated:

> **SOLIDER 11**

is confirmed to be one of Anby's sisters.

The unusual spelling appears consistently within the recovered Silverline records.

C.O.R.E. will retain the designation exactly as documented unless additional evidence establishes a preferred personal name.

Silverline records indicate that Solider 11 participated in the same deployment structure as Anby and the other subjects.

---

# 4. SORA

**STATUS:** CONFIRMED

Sora is confirmed as another member of Anby's original group.

Records concerning Sora are incomplete.

However, enough documentation remains to establish the familial connection beyond reasonable administrative uncertainty.

Anby has been informed that Sora is confirmed.

She did not immediately ask for further information.

She simply asked:

> **ANBY:**  
> "She's alive?"

The response was:

> **"Her current status has not been fully established."**

Anby nodded.

She did not ask again.

---

# 5. PHAETHON

**STATUS:** CONFIRMED

Phaethon is confirmed as one of Anby's sisters.

Silverline documentation places Phaethon within the same experimental and deployment program.

The records repeatedly group Phaethon with Anby and the other subjects.

Further investigation is ongoing.

---

# 6. IRIS

**STATUS:** CONFIRMED

Iris is confirmed as one of Anby's sisters.

Her identity appears across multiple Silverline records.

As with the other confirmed subjects, her current location remains under investigation.

The KCA has classified all information concerning the five confirmed sisters as protected-person information.

---

# 7. THE REMAINING SIX

The remaining six subjects have a different status.

Recovered Silverline operational records contain explicit documentation indicating that the subjects were:

> **Killed by Project: Silverline operators.**

Following their deaths, the operators disposed of the remains.

The records do not currently establish a recoverable location.

No surviving biological material has been confirmed.

No evidence currently indicates that the six subjects escaped.

Their status is therefore:

> **CONFIRMED DECEASED.**

---

# 8. ANBY'S RESPONSE

Anby was informed privately.

Phoenix was present.

The information was delivered slowly.

Anby listened without interruption.

When informed that six of her sisters had been killed, she remained completely still.

Phoenix did not attempt to fill the silence.

After several seconds:

> **ANBY:**  
> "...All six?"

The KCA representative nodded.

Anby looked down.

> **ANBY:**  
> "They were together?"

A pause.

> **KCA REPRESENTATIVE:**  
> "The records indicate that they were."

Anby closed her eyes.

She did not cry.

She did not speak for approximately one minute.

Then:

> **ANBY:**  
> "So six are gone."

Phoenix quietly answered:

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "Yes."

Anby nodded.

---

# 9. THE FIVE

Anby's response changed when she was told the remaining five names.

> **Twiggy.**

> **Solider 11.**

> **Sora.**

> **Phaethon.**

> **Iris.**

Anby repeated each name quietly.

Not as a list.

As a memory.

> **ANBY:**  
> "Twiggy."

Pause.

> **ANBY:**  
> "Solider 11."

Pause.

> **ANBY:**  
> "Sora."

Her voice became quieter.

> **ANBY:**  
> "Phaethon."

Another pause.

> **ANBY:**  
> "Iris."

She stopped.

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Five."

Phoenix nodded.

Anby looked toward her.

> **ANBY:**  
> "There are five."

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "Yes."

Anby repeated:

> **ANBY:**  
> "Five."

---

# 10. WHAT THIS MEANS

Anby's previous understanding was simple:

> **Eleven sisters missing.**

She could therefore maintain the possibility that all eleven were somewhere.

That possibility has now changed.

There are:

**Five confirmed sisters whose current circumstances remain under investigation.**

And:

**Six confirmed deceased sisters.**

This distinction is important for Anby's recovery.

She is no longer waiting for eleven unknown people.

She is now facing the reality that six members of her family were deliberately killed by the organization that created them.

---

# 11. SILVERLINE RESPONSIBILITY

The recovered records indicate that the six deaths were not accidental.

They were documented as actions carried out by Silverline operators.

The subsequent disposal of the bodies was also recorded.

C.O.R.E. therefore considers the deaths part of Project: Silverline's operational history rather than an unexplained disappearance.

Further investigation will determine:

- who authorized the actions;
    
- why the subjects were terminated;
    
- where the remains were disposed of;
    
- whether additional records exist;
    
- and whether the five confirmed sisters can be located.
    

---

> **ANBY:**

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "Yes."

> "They killed them."

> **ANBY:**

> **PHOENIX:**

> "They didn't make your memories."

Phoenix continued:

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "And they don't get to decide what you are now."

Anby looked down.

After several seconds:

> **ANBY:**  
> "...Okay."

# 14. WELFARE DIRECTIVE

Personnel are instructed not to tell Anby:

> "At least you still have five."

The statement is technically true but psychologically inappropriate.

Anby has lost six sisters.

The existence of five others does not reduce that loss.

Personnel should also avoid treating the five confirmed sisters as automatically safe.

Their current circumstances remain under investigation.

Anby's family is not considered fully accounted for until the remaining five have been located and their conditions independently established.
