## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / RESIDENT OBSERVATION LOG**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — ABSENCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** **PRESENT — LOCATION UNKNOWN / ROUTINE DISRUPTION**

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Alice hasn't been around lately.

Her scooter is still in the Treehouse.

Her notebooks are still there.

Her soft hat is still on its usual place.

Nothing appears to have been taken.

But Alice isn't using any of it.

She hasn't been riding around the Blacksite to visit her friends.

She hasn't been sitting with Belle.

She hasn't visited Neko.

She hasn't gone looking for Star.

She hasn't asked Corin to carry her.

She simply stays somewhere out of sight.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

At first, everyone assumed Alice was resting.

Then a day passed.

Then another.

Nobody wanted to make her feel watched, so they gave her space.

Eventually, they realized something uncomfortable.

Alice's absence was itself unusual.

Alice normally seeks people out.

Even when she doesn't speak.

Even when she's tired.

Even when she doesn't feel well.

She likes being around everyone.

Now she isn't.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin checks the Treehouse repeatedly.

The bed is occupied sometimes.

Other times, it's empty.

Corin keeps asking:

> "Where's Alice?"

Nobody knows.

Corin has started leaving small notes around the Treehouse.

> **"Big Sis is here."**

> **"You can come find me."**

> **"No trouble."**

> **"You aren't bothering anyone."**

She never pressures Alice to answer.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle keeps Alice's scooter charged.

Every morning.

Even though Alice hasn't used it.

Belle refuses to let the battery run flat.

> "She's gonna want it eventually."

The scooter remains ready.

---

# **NEKO**

Neko checked the scooter's tracker.

It has not indicated any unauthorized movement.

He also checked the Treehouse perimeter.

Nothing unusual.

No evidence of Alice leaving the Blacksite.

That should have been reassuring.

It wasn't.

Because it meant Alice was somewhere inside.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi noticed something else.

Alice's journal has not been updated.

Not even once.

For someone who has been writing things down specifically because she is afraid of forgetting people, the silence is concerning.

Lumi doesn't touch the journal.

She simply leaves it where Alice can find it.

---

# **STAR**

Star asked:

> "Maybe she just wants some quiet?"

Orphie answered:

> "Maybe."

Neither sounded convinced.

Star eventually left one of her little rhombus-shaped star stickers beside Alice's pillow.

No message.

Just the sticker.

---

# **WHAT NOBODY KNOWS**

Alice hasn't told anyone why she's staying away.

She hasn't written it down.

She hasn't drawn it.

She hasn't tried to explain.

She simply isn't around.

And because Alice is so accustomed to hiding discomfort behind silence, nobody wants to assume that her absence means she is okay.

---

# **CURRENT DIRECTIVE**

Do not chase Alice aggressively.

Do not corner her.

Do not demand that she explain herself.

But maintain awareness of her location and condition.

If she is found:

Stay with her.

Make sure she is safe.

Give her food and water if she needs them.

Let her know that nobody is angry.

And most importantly—

**don't make her feel guilty for disappearing.**

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

The Blacksite is strangely quiet without Alice.

No tiny scooter passing through the corridors.

No little white ears appearing around a doorway.

No notebook being carried against her chest.

No small wave when she finds someone she likes.

Everyone keeps noticing the empty spaces where Alice normally is.

Nobody says it aloud.

But everyone is waiting for the same thing.

For Alice to come back.
