## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION LOG**

# **ORPHIE — MART COLONY CONCERN**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Concern  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Forms:** Luna / Solaria  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE / Remote Treehouse  
**Status:** **ACTIVE CONCERN**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The Mart colony has become increasingly concerned about Orphie's emotional state.

Although the Marts can now safely approach her, they have noticed that something is fundamentally different.

They remember the person Orphie was.

They remember Corey.

And they remember how much she loved drawing.

She does not anymore.

In fact:

> **Orphie now dislikes drawing.**

---

# **THE SKETCHBOOK**

Orphie's sketchbook remains one of the clearest records of the change.

The earlier pages contain things the Marts remember fondly:

- Marts.
    
- Friends.
    
- Flowers.
    
- Food.
    
- Landscapes.
    
- SAFEHOUSE.
    
- Happy memories.
    

The later pages contain nightmares and disturbing imagery.

The newest pages contain almost nothing.

Orphie has stopped drawing.

When asked whether she wanted to draw again:

> "...No."

When asked why:

> "...I don't like it."

No further explanation was given.

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

The Marts do not understand why something that once made Orphie happy has become something she dislikes.

They have attempted to encourage her.

A Mart once placed a pencil beside her.

Orphie looked at it.

> "...Take it away."

The Mart immediately picked it up.

> "...Okay."

It then carried the pencil away.

---

# **THE COLONY IS WORRIED**

Marts are normally exceptionally optimistic.

They are designed around happiness, affection, and companionship.

Even when something upsets them, they recover quickly.

Orphie's condition is different.

They cannot simply make her happy.

They cannot repair the problem by piling onto her.

They cannot safely overwhelm her with affection.

And they cannot understand why the person they love no longer seems to enjoy the things she once loved.

---

# **MART BEHAVIOR**

Several Marts have begun quietly monitoring Orphie.

They may:

- Sit nearby.
    
- Watch her from across a room.
    
- Follow her without making noise.
    
- Bring small objects to her.
    
- Sleep near her.
    
- Wait outside her room.
    

They rarely attempt to force interaction.

It appears the colony has learned that Orphie does not want to be pressured.

---

# **A SMALL ATTEMPT**

One Mart found an old drawing of itself wearing its top hat.

It carried the drawing toward Orphie.

Orphie looked at it.

The Mart waited.

Orphie eventually said:

> "...That's old."

The Mart:

> "...Happy."

Orphie:

> "...Maybe."

The Mart remained beside her.

Neither said anything else.

---

# **CORIN'S OBSERVATION**

Corin has noticed that the Marts behave differently around Orphie now.

They are no longer afraid of her emotional state.

Instead, they appear worried **for her**.

Corin commented:

> "...They know something is wrong."

She watched several Marts sitting around Orphie.

> "...They just don't know how to fix it."

---

# **LUNA'S RESPONSE**

Luna remains concerned about Orphie's loss of interest in drawing.

> "...She used to draw when she couldn't explain something."

> "...Now she doesn't even want the pencil."

Luna paused.

> "...That's not like her."

---

# **SOLARIA'S RESPONSE**

Solaria similarly noticed the change.

> "...Drawing was hers."

> "...Not mine. Not Luna's."

> "...Hers."

She looked toward Orphie's abandoned sketchbook.

> "...And now she hates it."

---

# **THE BROKEN PENCIL**

The broken halves of Orphie's favorite pencil remain preserved.

No one has thrown them away.

The Marts occasionally sit beside them.

They seem to recognize that the pencil is important, even if Orphie no longer wants it.

---

# **WHY THIS MATTERS**

Drawing was not merely a hobby.

It was one of the ways Orphie expressed herself.

When she was happy, she drew.

When she was lonely, she drew.

When she could not sleep, she drew.

When she remembered something important, she drew.

Now she has rejected the activity entirely.

The loss is therefore considered another indicator of her continuing emotional withdrawal.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Orphie:**  
Emotionally detached. Strong dislike of drawing. Increasingly withdrawn.

**Marts:**  
Safe around Orphie but deeply concerned. Attempting quiet companionship.

**Corin:**  
Highly concerned.

**Luna:**  
Concerned.

**Solaria:**  
Concerned.

**SAFEHOUSE:**  
Monitoring Orphie's condition closely.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The Marts have never been particularly good at understanding sadness.

They understand happiness.

They understand affection.

They understand comfort.

But Orphie has become something they cannot easily understand.

So they do the only thing they know.

They stay nearby.

A Mart sits beside her.

Another rests on the floor.

Another watches from the doorway.

None of them ask her to draw.

None of them force her to smile.

They simply remain.

Because even if Orphie no longer likes drawing...

And even if she no longer seems to care about the things she once loved...

**The Marts still care about her.**

**End of Personnel Welfare / SAFEHOUSE Observation Log**
