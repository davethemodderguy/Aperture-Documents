### RESIDENT WELFARE & MEDICAL DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-JAKE-037  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — RESIDENT WELFARE / COGNITIVE ANOMALY  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE OBSERVATION  
**SUBJECT:** Jake — First Verbal Interaction Following Memory Restoration  
**LOCATION:** ANOMALOUS GRASSLAND

---

# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

For the first time since the SAFEHOUSE residents began visiting the anomalous grassland, Jake spoke.

He spoke directly to Bao.

No preceding interaction indicated that he intended to communicate.

Bao was seated several meters from the grassless circle.

Jake remained in his usual position:

- knees pulled against his chest;
    
- arms wrapped around his legs;
    
- wings folded;
    
- shattered halo above his head;
    
- arrow embedded through his chest.
    

His eyes remained lowered.

Bao was speaking quietly.

Jake interrupted her.

---

# 2. FIRST WORDS

Bao had been talking about ordinary things.

She had mentioned breakfast.

The weather.

Frost.

Neri.

Nothing particularly important.

Jake remained silent.

Then, very quietly:

> **JAKE:**  
> "Why do you remember?"

Bao stopped.

She stared at him.

Jake did not look up.

His voice was weak.

Almost lost beneath the wind.

Bao's expression changed.

> **BAO:**  
> "...Jake?"

Jake's arms tightened around his legs.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Don't you see..."

Pause.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...how you all lived without me around?"

Bao did not immediately answer.

---

# 3. BAO'S RESPONSE

Bao slowly moved closer.

She stopped before entering the grassless circle.

> **BAO:**  
> "We didn't know."

Jake remained still.

> **JAKE:**  
> "That's the point."

His voice cracked slightly.

> **JAKE:**  
> "You forgot."

Bao's eyes filled with tears.

> **BAO:**  
> "We remember now."

Jake finally looked toward her.

His azure eyes were wet.

> **JAKE:**  
> "But you didn't before."

Silence.

> **JAKE:**  
> "You ate."

> **JAKE:**  
> "You slept."

> **JAKE:**  
> "You talked."

> **JAKE:**  
> "You laughed."

His voice became quieter.

> **JAKE:**  
> "You all kept living."

Bao could not answer.

---

# 4. JAKE'S QUESTION

Jake lowered his head again.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Nobody noticed."

His fingers tightened around his knees.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Nobody came."

Pause.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Nobody even knew I was gone."

Bao began crying.

Jake continued.

> **JAKE:**  
> "So why remember now?"

Bao wiped her face.

> **BAO:**  
> "Because you're our family."

Jake's expression did not change.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I was."

Bao shook her head.

> **BAO:**  
> "You still are."

Jake looked away.

---

# 5. THE CENTRAL QUESTION

Jake remained silent for several seconds.

Then:

> **JAKE:**  
> "If I mattered..."

He stopped.

His voice became almost inaudible.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...why was it so easy?"

Bao froze.

Jake did not elaborate.

He did not need to.

The question was not about the anomaly.

It was not about memory.

It was not about the grassland.

It was about every day before the disappearance.

Every time someone had been busy.

Every time someone had assumed someone else would help.

Every time Jake had quietly returned to his room.

Every time nobody noticed.

---

# 6. BAO

Bao sat down in the grass.

She did not attempt to enter the circle.

She did not try to touch him.

She simply sat.

> **BAO:**  
> "I don't have a good answer."

Jake said nothing.

> **BAO:**  
> "I wish I did."

The wind moved gently through the grass.

> **BAO:**  
> "I was wrong."

Jake's eyes remained lowered.

> **BAO:**  
> "We were wrong."

A long silence followed.

---

# 7. JAKE'S RESPONSE

Jake eventually spoke again.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I thought you'd be happier."

Bao looked at him.

> **BAO:**  
> "Without you?"

Jake nodded.

Barely.

> **JAKE:**  
> "You forgot me."

Bao's voice broke.

> **BAO:**  
> "We didn't choose to."

Jake looked toward the horizon.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I know."

Pause.

> **JAKE:**  
> "That's worse."

Bao closed her eyes.

She understood.

---

# 8. NO RESOLUTION

Bao did not attempt to convince Jake that everything would be fine.

She did not promise that the past could be undone.

She did not ask him to return.

She did not ask him to forgive anyone.

She simply remained seated.

After several minutes, Jake's quiet sobbing resumed.

Bao remained there.

Neither spoke again.

---

# 9. OBSERVATION

The interaction lasted approximately **three minutes and forty seconds.**

It is the first confirmed verbal communication from Jake since the residents regained their memories.

His voice was weak.

His speech was coherent.

No apparent cognitive impairment was observed during the exchange.

Most importantly, Jake demonstrated awareness of the events occurring outside the grassland.

He knows that the residents continued living without him.

He knows that they had forgotten him.

And he appears to understand that the memories have now returned.

---

# CLOSING NOTE

Bao remained beside the circle until the sunlight began to fade.

Jake did not ask her to leave.

He did not ask her to stay.

He simply sat there.

For the first time in many days, however, he was no longer completely silent.

Before Bao eventually left, Jake quietly said one final thing.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...I missed you."

Bao stopped.

She looked back.

Jake had already lowered his head again.

Bao remained silent for several seconds.

Then:

> **BAO:**  
> "I missed you too."

The elevator doors closed behind her.

Jake remained in the grass.

The wind continued.
