# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **NEUROPSYCHOLOGICAL STATUS REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – PROGRESSIVE MEMORY LOSS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Executive & Medical Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following Corin Lockwood's prolonged isolation and disappearance, Psychological and Medical Services concluded that her memory impairment had progressed significantly.

The deterioration no longer affected only isolated memories.

It had begun to erode entire relationships.

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Recovered writings and subsequent witness reports indicate Corin struggled to recall names, faces, and personal history.

Initially, she forgot conversations.

Later, she forgot places.

Eventually...

She began forgetting people.

Medical Services determined that the memory loss was inconsistent with ordinary forgetfulness and was likely influenced by the cumulative effects of severe psychological trauma and Corin's unique physiological history.

---

# **RECOGNITION FAILURE**

Personnel reviewing recovered notes identified repeated attempts by Corin to preserve memories before she believed they would disappear.

Several pages consisted only of names.

Some had check marks beside them.

Others had question marks.

Toward the end of the notebook...

Most names had been crossed out.

No explanation accompanied them.

---

# **LUMI**

The most concerning entry appeared near the final pages.

It contained only one sentence.

> "I think I had a mom."

The remainder of the page was blank.

Psychological Services believe the statement reflects Corin's fear that memories of Lumi were fading beyond recognition.

Whether the memory itself was completely lost or merely became difficult to access could not be determined.

---

# **OTHER RELATIONSHIPS**

Additional evidence suggests Corin's memory impairment extended beyond Lumi.

Names associated with SAFEHOUSE residents appeared increasingly fragmented.

Photographs no longer reliably triggered recognition.

Familiar places evoked little emotional response.

Corin reportedly stared at one recovered group photograph for several minutes before quietly asking a passerby,

> "...Do you know who these people are?"

When informed that she was in the photograph herself...

She continued looking at it.

As though searching for someone she ought to recognize.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services caution that memory impairment does not necessarily erase emotional attachment completely.

Individuals may retain feelings of comfort, familiarity, or safety even when specific names or autobiographical details become difficult to recall.

For this reason, clinicians recommend that anyone re-establishing contact with Corin introduce themselves patiently and avoid assuming that a lack of recognition reflects a lack of affection.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin's memory has become increasingly fragmented.

Many of the people who devoted years to helping her now exist only as incomplete impressions within her mind.

Whether those memories can be recovered remains unknown.

Medical Services continue to regard memory rehabilitation as a long-term objective should Corin be safely located.

Until then...

Somewhere within the Blacksite, a young woman continues walking forward while carrying fewer and fewer pieces of the life she once remembered.

**End of Neuropsychological Status Report**
