# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL STATUS REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – RECURRENCE OF MAJOR DEPRESSIVE SYMPTOMS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Despite months of documented recovery, Corin Lockwood's psychological condition has deteriorated once again.

The improvement observed after her unexplained revival has largely receded.

Personnel who interact with Corin daily consistently describe the change using the same words.

> "...She's back."

Not physically.

Emotionally.

---

# **OBSERVED CHANGES**

Over the past several weeks, the following behaviors have become increasingly common:

- Markedly reduced emotional expression.
    
- Minimal participation in conversation.
    
- Loss of interest in previously enjoyed activities.
    
- Extended periods of silence.
    
- Reduced eye contact.
    
- Persistent low mood.
    
- Social withdrawal outside of work-related communication.
    

Corin continues fulfilling her responsibilities for the Krampus Compliance Authority.

Her reports remain accurate.

Her deadlines continue to be met.

Outside of work...

Very little has changed.

---

# **SHELTER T-01**

The routine at Shelter T-01 has become familiar once more.

Morning.

Paperwork.

Silence.

Evening.

Tea grows cold before being finished.

The white tiger sleeps beneath the tree.

The snowy owl watches from the branches.

Corin occasionally acknowledges them with a gentle pat or a small piece of bread.

Beyond that...

The clearing is quiet again.

---

# **PERSONNEL OBSERVATIONS**

Several residents privately remarked that Corin reminds them of how she was before her recovery.

Not because of her appearance.

Because of the absence of joy that had briefly returned.

The laughter is gone.

The spontaneous smiles have become rare.

The warmth that had slowly returned to her conversations has faded into short, polite replies.

It is as though a light that had begun to shine has gradually dimmed again.

---

# **FAMILY RESPONSE**

The Lockwood family has chosen not to pressure Corin.

They continue visiting when she allows it.

They continue respecting her boundaries.

They continue reminding her that she is welcome at SAFEHOUSE whenever she wishes.

No one asks her to "go back to how she was."

They know recovery is not something that can be demanded.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services have not identified a single event responsible for Corin's decline.

No new traumatic incident has been documented.

No neurological abnormalities have been detected beyond those previously recorded.

Her medication regimen remains unchanged.

Her physical health is stable.

The nightmares continue.

Beyond that...

The cause remains unknown.

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

One physician summarized the situation during case review.

> "Sometimes we can identify why a patient improves."

> "Sometimes we can identify why they decline."

He closed Corin's file.

Then quietly added,

> "...And sometimes we can't."

No one in the room argued.

Because no one had an answer.

Why did she recover?

Why did the nightmares return?

Why did her appearance change again?

Why did the sadness come back?

No one knows.

Corin herself no longer asks those questions.

She simply lives with them.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin Lockwood remains employed.

She remains at Shelter T-01.

She remains surrounded by people who have not stopped caring.

Yet she once again feels distant from the life she had begun to rebuild.

The white tiger still waits beneath the tree.

The snowy owl still watches from above.

And every morning, Corin opens her office ledger, begins another day's work, and quietly hopes that tomorrow will feel a little lighter than today.

**End of Psychological Status Report**
