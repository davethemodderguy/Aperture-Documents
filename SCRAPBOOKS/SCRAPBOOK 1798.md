# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Aries Akana has not yet been assigned employment within Aperture Industries.

His anomalous capabilities remain under assessment, his background requires further documentation, and his weapon's properties have not been fully characterized.

Consequently, Aries currently has considerably more free time than most SAFEHOUSE residents.

Jake has noticed.

More specifically, Jake has apparently concluded that his older brother's free time belongs to him.

Since their reunion, Jake has demonstrated a substantial increase in physical attachment to Aries.

The behavior includes, but is not limited to:

- following Aries between rooms;
    
- holding onto his sleeves;
    
- sitting against him whenever a seat is available;
    
- climbing into his lap without warning;
    
- asking to be carried;
    
- falling asleep against his chest;
    
- holding his hand while walking;
    
- wrapping his tail around his wrist;
    
- leaning against his legs when Aries is standing;
    
- and becoming visibly displeased when Aries leaves without him.
    

This behavior is particularly pronounced when Clover, Phoenix, and Bao are occupied with their respective responsibilities.

Aries has therefore become Jake's primary available companion during the day.

He has also become, without formal appointment, a temporary full-time caretaker.

**C.O.R.E. has not assigned Aries a job. Jake has assigned him one.**

The distinction is currently irrelevant to Jake.

# 1. THE NEW ROUTINE

The first several days following Aries's arrival established a predictable pattern.

At approximately 07:00, Clover begins her daily work.

Phoenix leaves for her own responsibilities.

Bao attends to medical and household duties, including Neri's care.

Aries, meanwhile, remains at SAFEHOUSE.

He has no assigned workstation.

No scheduled shift.

No outstanding departmental deliverables.

No immediate operational obligations beyond familiarization with the facility and compliance with security procedures.

Jake considers this an ideal situation.

# 8. RESIDENT WELFARE ASSESSMENT

Jake's increased attachment to Aries is consistent with a child seeking security and closeness following a significant reunion.

It may also reflect the fact that Aries is currently more available than Jake's other primary caregivers.

Clover has responsibilities.

Phoenix has responsibilities.

Bao has medical and household responsibilities.

Aries has not yet begun working.

He is present, familiar in a way no other resident can be, and willing to respond when Jake asks for his attention.

This has created an arrangement that is currently beneficial to both brothers.

Jake receives companionship from someone he recognizes as family.

Aries has an opportunity to learn about his younger brother through ordinary daily interaction rather than through formal reports and medical briefings.

Nevertheless, several considerations remain important.

### Aries should not be expected to become Jake's sole caretaker.

He is Jake's older brother, not a replacement for every other person in Jake's life.

### Jake should not be discouraged from seeking comfort.

His requests to be carried, held, or accompanied should be treated as ordinary expressions of preference, provided both parties are comfortable and medical requirements are respected.

### Aries must retain reasonable personal boundaries.

He may rest, eat, leave the room, or pursue independent activities without guilt.

### Jake must continue developing relationships with other residents.

His attachment to Aries should not prevent him from spending time with Clover, Phoenix, Bao, or anyone else he wishes to see.

### Medical safety remains mandatory.

The teddy bear must remain with Jake, and any signs of physical distress must be addressed promptly.

At present, C.O.R.E. has observed no evidence that Aries is encouraging unhealthy dependence or that Jake is being prevented from interacting with others.

The brothers are simply making up for lost time.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Aries has not yet found a department to join.

He has not been issued a workstation.

He has not been assigned a formal role within Aperture Industries.

He has spent most of his time learning the Blacksite, meeting residents, familiarizing himself with Jake's medical needs, and discovering that his little brother has changed considerably since their separation.

He has also learned that Jake:

- has feline ears;
    
- has a tail;
    
- possesses claws;
    
- requires a teddy bear that is not, under any circumstances, to be treated as an ordinary toy;
    
- considers cake a reasonable interruption to almost any conversation;
    
- and apparently believes that an older brother's primary function is to provide transportation.

He carries Jake when asked.

He holds his hand when walking.

He lets Jake sit beside him while he reads.

He explains where he is going before leaving the room.

And Jake?

Aries is here.

He is sitting beside him.

He is holding his hand.

And when Jake asks whether he will still be there tomorrow, Aries gives him an answer he can trust.

For now, Aries does not need a job title.

He has already found something important to do.

**He is helping his little brother learn that he does not have to hold on so tightly to keep someone from disappearing.**

And until Jake is ready to loosen his grip, Aries does not mind holding his hand.
