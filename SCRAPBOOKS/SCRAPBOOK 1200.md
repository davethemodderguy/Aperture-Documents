# 1. INTERVIEW PURPOSE

Bao is currently recognized as the SAFEHOUSE resident with the largest number of children under her maternal care.

Her three daughters are:

- **Frost** — biological daughter;
    
- **Neri Tachyon** — adopted/familial daughter;
    
- **Nora Tachyon** — Neri's younger sister, subsequently accepted into Bao's family.
    

C.O.R.E. conducted the following interview to document Bao's perspective on her unusually large and increasingly chaotic family.

---

# 2. INTERVIEW

**INTERVIEWER:**  
Bao, how many children do you have?

Bao paused.

She counted on her fingers.

> **BAO:**  
> "Three."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Biologically?"

> **BAO:**  
> "One."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"And the other two?"

Bao smiled.

> **BAO:**  
> "Mine."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"That's not what I asked."

> **BAO:**  
> "It's the answer."

---

# 3. FROST

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Let's start with Frost."

Bao immediately smiled.

> **BAO:**  
> "Frost is my daughter."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"What's she like?"

Bao thought for a moment.

> **BAO:**  
> "Quiet."

Pause.

> **BAO:**  
> "Independent."

Another pause.

> **BAO:**  
> "Very stubborn."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Does she take after you?"

Bao laughed.

> **BAO:**  
> "Sometimes."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"And Neri?"

Bao immediately replied:

> **BAO:**  
> "Also stubborn."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Nora?"

Bao laughed again.

> **BAO:**  
> "Very."

---

# 4. NERI

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"When did you start thinking of Neri as your daughter?"

Bao became quieter.

> **BAO:**  
> "I don't remember a specific day."

She looked down at her hands.

> **BAO:**  
> "I think it happened gradually."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Because of the medical care?"

Bao shook her head.

> **BAO:**  
> "No."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Because she needed you?"

> **BAO:**  
> "No."

Pause.

> **BAO:**  
> "Because I wanted her."

The interviewer remained silent.

Bao continued.

> **BAO:**  
> "Those are different things."

---

# 5. NERI'S ORIGINAL QUESTION

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Neri has had difficulty believing that people can care about her without expecting something in return."

Bao nodded.

> **BAO:**  
> "Yes."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Did you ever expect her to earn her place with you?"

Bao looked genuinely confused.

> **BAO:**  
> "No."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Not even when she was difficult?"

Bao smiled.

> **BAO:**  
> "Especially then."

---

# 6. NORA

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"And then Nora returned."

Bao's expression immediately changed.

She smiled.

> **BAO:**  
> "Oh, Nora."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Your reaction was rather emotional."

> **BAO:**  
> "Of course it was."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Why?"

Bao answered without hesitation.

> **BAO:**  
> "Because she was Neri's sister."

Pause.

> **BAO:**  
> "And because she was a little girl who had already been through enough."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"So you simply accepted her?"

> **BAO:**  
> "Yes."

---

# 7. THE "MOM" INCIDENT

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Nora began calling you 'Mom' because Neri did."

Bao laughed.

> **BAO:**  
> "Yes."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"How did that happen?"

Bao recalled the incident.

> **BAO:**  
> "She asked Neri, 'Then she's my mom too?'"

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"And Neri?"

Bao smiled.

> **BAO:**  
> "She said, '...I guess.'"

The interviewer laughed.

Bao nodded.

> **BAO:**  
> "And that was apparently enough."

---

# 8. THREE DIFFERENT CHILDREN

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"How different are your three daughters?"

Bao thought carefully.

> **BAO:**  
> "Very."

She began counting.

> **BAO:**  
> "Frost is calm."

> **BAO:**  
> "Neri is quiet."

> **BAO:**  
> "Nora is..."

Bao paused.

> **BAO:**  
> "...Nora."

The interviewer laughed.

> **INTERVIEWER:**  
> "That's not a description."

> **BAO:**  
> "It is if you've met her."

---

# 9. ON NORA'S ENERGY

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Nora is considerably more energetic than her sister."

> **BAO:**  
> "Yes."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Does that cause problems?"

Bao smiled.

> **BAO:**  
> "Sometimes."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Examples?"

Bao counted.

> **BAO:**  
> "Stealing keycards."

Another finger.

> **BAO:**  
> "Chasing people with Alice's axe."

Another.

> **BAO:**  
> "Reading Neri's chemistry notes."

Another.

> **BAO:**  
> "Trying to get her own laptop."

Bao stopped counting.

> **BAO:**  
> "And generally discovering things."

---

# 10. THE LAPTOP REQUEST

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Do you think Nora should have her own laptop?"

Bao immediately shook her head.

> **BAO:**  
> "No."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Why?"

> **BAO:**  
> "She's six."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Neri had one."

Bao nodded.

> **BAO:**  
> "Neri is ten."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Nora is already remarkably capable."

Bao smiled.

> **BAO:**  
> "I know."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Then?"

> **BAO:**  
> "Being clever doesn't make you older."

---

# 11. ON NERI AND NORA TOGETHER

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"What is it like watching Neri and Nora together?"

Bao's expression softened.

> **BAO:**  
> "Wonderful."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Even when Nora bites Neri's tail?"

Bao laughed.

> **BAO:**  
> "Even then."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Even when Nora interrupts Neri's chemistry?"

> **BAO:**  
> "Usually."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Usually?"

> **BAO:**  
> "Sometimes Neri needs quiet."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"And what happens then?"

Bao smiled.

> **BAO:**  
> "Nora usually understands."

Pause.

> **BAO:**  
> "Eventually."

---

# 12. THE CHEMISTRY INCIDENT

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Nora appears to be unusually good at chemistry."

Bao nodded proudly.

> **BAO:**  
> "She is."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Does that concern you?"

> **BAO:**  
> "No."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Neko is concerned."

Bao laughed.

> **BAO:**  
> "Neko worries about storage."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Because of Neri?"

> **BAO:**  
> "Because of Neri."

---

# 13. THREE CHILDREN, THREE DIFFERENT NEEDS

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Do you treat all three the same?"

Bao shook her head.

> **BAO:**  
> "No."

The interviewer raised an eyebrow.

Bao clarified:

> **BAO:**  
> "I love them the same."

Pause.

> **BAO:**  
> "But they're different people."

> **BAO:**  
> "Frost doesn't need what Neri needs."

> **BAO:**  
> "Neri doesn't need what Nora needs."

> **BAO:**  
> "And Nora certainly doesn't need what either of them needs."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"So how do you handle that?"

Bao smiled.

> **BAO:**  
> "I pay attention."

---

# 14. IF ONE OF THEM IS HURT

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"What happens if one of your daughters is hurt?"

Bao answered immediately.

> **BAO:**  
> "I help them."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Does it matter which one?"

> **BAO:**  
> "No."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"Even if two of them need you simultaneously?"

Bao laughed.

> **BAO:**  
> "Then I have two problems."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"And if all three?"

Bao thought for a moment.

> **BAO:**  
> "Then someone else is helping me."

---

# 15. ON BEING CALLED MOM

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"How does it feel when they call you 'Mom'?"

Bao became visibly emotional.

She took a moment before answering.

> **BAO:**  
> "Nice."

Pause.

> **BAO:**  
> "Very nice."

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"All three?"

Bao nodded.

> **BAO:**  
> "All three."

---

# 16. THE INTERVIEWER'S FINAL QUESTION

**INTERVIEWER:**  
"If you could tell your three daughters one thing, what would it be?"

Bao thought for a long time.

Then answered:

> **BAO:**  
> "You don't have to be the same."

Pause.

> **BAO:**  
> "You don't have to be useful."

Another pause.

> **BAO:**  
> "You don't have to be perfect."

Bao smiled.

> **BAO:**  
> "Just come home."

---

# 17. POST-INTERVIEW OBSERVATION

The interview concluded.

Bao left the room.

Approximately thirty seconds later, Frost walked past.

> **FROST:**  
> "Where's Mom?"

Bao pointed down the hallway.

Frost nodded and left.

A few minutes later, Nora came running around the corner.

> **NORA:**  
> "MOM!"

Bao turned.

Nora ran toward her.

Behind Nora was Neri.

Neri walked at a considerably slower pace.

Bao looked at all three.

For approximately five seconds, nobody said anything.

Then Nora announced:

> **NORA:**  
> "Mom, Neri has chemistry!"

Bao looked at Neri.

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> "She does."

Frost appeared behind them.

> **FROST:**  
> "What's happening?"

Bao sighed.

> **BAO:**  
> "Nothing."

Nora raised her hand.

> **NORA:**  
> "I want a laptop."

> **BAO:**  
> "No."

> **NORA:**  
> "A small one?"

> **BAO:**  
> "No."

Neri quietly walked away.

Frost followed.

Bao watched Nora run after them.

She smiled.
