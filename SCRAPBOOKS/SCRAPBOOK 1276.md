# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon was asked to inspect her Slugger following the previous range exercise.

The inspection itself was routine.

Nora was not.

Rather than simply opening the action and visually checking the chamber, Nora performed the inspection with an unnecessary amount of theatrical flair.

The range cameras recorded the entire sequence.

C.O.R.E. has determined that the maneuver was:

> **Completely unnecessary.**

It was also extremely cool.

---

# RANGE OBSERVATION

Nora stood at the cleared inspection table with her Slugger.

The range had already been declared safe.

She began her inspection.

First, she checked the firearm normally.

Then she paused.

She looked at the chamber.

Then at the shell positioned within the action.

A nearby MSAC operator noticed.

> **MSAC OPERATOR:**  
> “Nora?”

Nora ignored him.

She opened the action.

The shell was released upward.

For approximately one second, it hung in the air above the firearm.

Nora's hand moved.

**Catch.**

She caught the shell cleanly.

The entire range went silent.

Nora looked at the shell.

Rotated it between her fingers.

Inspected it.

Then looked back at the firearm.

---

# THE SECOND SHELL

A second shell had been deliberately placed beside the chamber.

It remained untouched throughout the inspection.

Nora picked it up afterward.

She briefly compared the two.

One shell in her hand.

One shell beside the chamber.

She appeared completely satisfied.

She then returned the inspected shell to its appropriate position and completed the inspection.

No further theatrics occurred.

# C.O.R.E. OBSERVATION

The inspection itself was competent and controlled.

The theatrical element appears to have been entirely voluntary.

Notably, Nora did not lose track of the second shell positioned near the chamber.

She maintained awareness of both objects throughout the sequence.

This is consistent with her previously documented tendency to combine:

- rapid observation;
    
- object awareness;
    
- coordination;
    
- adaptation;
    
- and unnecessary levels of confidence.
    

The final category is not formally recognized by MSAC.

It should probably be.

# FINAL ASSESSMENT

Nora's inspection demonstrated no significant deficiency in weapon awareness or handling under the controlled range conditions.

The unusual component was the presentation.

She appears to have developed a habit of making otherwise mundane actions unnecessarily stylish.

C.O.R.E. therefore records the following:

> **Technical inspection:** Competent.  
> **Situational awareness:** High.  
> **Object retention:** High.  
> **Need to make everything look cool:** Extremely high.

The second shell remained beside the chamber throughout the entire sequence.

Nora noticed it.

The observers noticed it.

Neko noticed it.

Everyone noticed it.

Nobody knows why she needed it there.

Nora has declined to elaborate.
