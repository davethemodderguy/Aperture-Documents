### RESIDENT WELFARE & MEDICAL DIVISION

**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — SECTOR 4-C

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has informed the SAFEHOUSE that he can speak again.

He did so entirely on his own.

No announcement was requested.

No medical demonstration was scheduled.

Anby did not tell anyone.

Twiggy did not tell anyone.

Jake simply decided that he was ready.

The disclosure occurred during an otherwise ordinary SAFEHOUSE gathering.

---

# 1. PRECEDING CONDITIONS

The SAFEHOUSE residents were gathered in the common area.

Nothing unusual was occurring.

Bao was preparing food.

Neri was reading.

Nora was talking.

Alice was sitting on the floor.

Frost and Taiga were discussing something near the kitchen.

Neko was attempting to explain a technical issue to Miss Neko.

Anby and Twiggy were nearby.

Jake was sitting on the couch.

His teddy bear was resting against his side.

He had been quiet for most of the gathering.

As usual.

Nobody expected anything different.

---

# 2. THE INTERRUPTION

Nora was halfway through explaining something to Jake.

> **NORA:**  
> "...and then Alice said she could absolutely beat me in a race, which is ridiculous because I can obviously—"

Jake looked at her.

He smiled.

Nora continued.

> **NORA:**  
> "—win."

Jake raised one hand.

Nora stopped.

> **NORA:**  
> "Hm?"

Jake looked around the room.

Several residents noticed.

He appeared nervous.

Anby immediately recognized the expression.

She did not intervene.

Twiggy also recognized it.

She remained silent.

Jake took a breath.

Then spoke.

Very quietly.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Nora."

Silence.

Complete silence.

Nora froze.

Everyone froze.

---

# 3. CONFUSION

Nora stared at Jake.

Jake looked back.

She slowly pointed at him.

> **NORA:**  
> "...You talked."

Jake nodded.

Someone dropped a spoon.

Nobody reacted to the spoon.

Everyone was looking at Jake.

Bao's expression changed immediately.

> **BAO:**  
> "Jake?"

Jake looked toward her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Hi, Mom."

Bao stopped moving.

---

# 4. SAFEHOUSE REACTION

The room effectively ceased functioning.

Neko stared.

Miss Neko stared at Neko.

Neri lowered her book.

Alice's ears went straight up.

Frost looked between Jake and Anby.

Taiga stopped speaking.

Mart nearly dropped his drink.

Astral simply smiled.

Star stared.

Nightshade stared.

Belle, who had been visiting, looked completely stunned.

Even Aeterna became visibly surprised.

Nora was already crying.

> **NORA:**  
> "YOU CAN TALK!"

Jake winced slightly at the volume.

Nora immediately covered her mouth.

> **NORA:**  
> "...Sorry."

Jake smiled.

> **JAKE:**  
> "It's okay."

Nora began crying harder.

---

# 5. ANBY

Everyone eventually looked toward Anby.

Anby had been smiling quietly.

Neko narrowed his eyes.

> **NEKO:**  
> "...You knew."

Anby nodded.

> **NEKO:**  
> "How long?"

Anby held up five fingers.

Neko stared.

> **NEKO:**  
> "Five weeks?"

Anby shook her head.

Then pointed toward Jake.

Five fingers.

Then one finger.

Twiggy clarified:

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "Five weeks without speaking. He's been able to whisper for only a short time."

Neko looked at Anby.

Then Jake.

Then Anby again.

> **NEKO:**  
> "And you didn't tell us?"

Anby shrugged.

> **ANBY:**  
> "He didn't want us to."

Neko looked at Jake.

Jake nodded.

That ended the argument.

---

# 6. NERI

Neri had remained completely silent.

She slowly closed her book.

She looked at Jake.

Jake looked back.

Neri pointed toward herself.

Then toward him.

A question.

Jake understood.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Yeah."

Neri's eyes widened slightly.

She looked genuinely surprised.

Then she smiled.

Only faintly.

She reached over and gently tapped the cover of her physics textbook.

Jake looked at it.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Still reading it."

Neri nodded.

She opened the book again.

No further reaction was necessary.

---

# 7. NEKO

Neko appeared to have a different concern.

> **NEKO:**  
> "Wait."

Jake looked at him.

> **NEKO:**  
> "You can speak now."

Jake nodded.

> **NEKO:**  
> "And you still beat me at physics."

Jake paused.

Then:

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Apparently."

Anby covered her mouth.

Twiggy laughed.

Neko stared at the ceiling.

> **NEKO:**  
> "I don't like this week."

---

# 8. BAO

Bao approached Jake carefully.

She knelt beside the couch.

She did not immediately hug him.

Instead, she asked:

> **BAO:**  
> "Does it hurt to talk?"

Jake considered the question.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...A little."

Bao's expression immediately softened.

> **BAO:**  
> "Then don't force yourself."

Jake nodded.

Bao gently touched his hair.

> **BAO:**  
> "I'm glad I can hear you again."

Jake looked down.

Then quietly:

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Me too."

Bao hugged him.

Jake leaned into her.

---

# 9. AETERNA

Aeterna remained standing nearby.

Jake looked toward her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Hi."

Aeterna smiled.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Hello, Jake."

Jake looked at her for a moment.

> **JAKE:**  
> "You knew?"

Aeterna shook her head.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "No."

Jake nodded.

Aeterna placed one hand gently against his head.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Then I am glad you chose to tell us."

---

# 10. ALICE

Alice approached next.

She crouched in front of Jake.

She stared at him.

Jake stared back.

> **ALICE:**  
> "Say something."

Jake blinked.

Anby immediately interrupted.

> **ANBY:**  
> "Don't."

Alice looked at Anby.

Then Jake.

She realized her mistake.

> **ALICE:**  
> "...Sorry."

Jake smiled.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Hi."

Alice's ears shot upward.

> **ALICE:**  
> "HI."

Jake covered one ear.

Alice immediately lowered her voice.

> **ALICE:**  
> "...hi."

---

# 11. NORA

Nora eventually sat beside Jake.

She was still visibly emotional.

> **NORA:**  
> "Why didn't you tell me?"

Jake thought about it.

Then quietly:

> **JAKE:**  
> "Wanted to."

Pause.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Wasn't ready."

Nora nodded.

She wiped her eyes.

> **NORA:**  
> "Okay."

She leaned against his shoulder.

> **NORA:**  
> "I'm glad you are now."

Jake rested his head against hers.

---

# 12. MEDICAL RESPONSE

Twiggy immediately reminded everyone of the medical limitation.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "Everyone needs to calm down."

Nobody moved.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "He can speak."

Pause.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "That does not mean he can talk for three hours."

Several residents looked disappointed.

Twiggy continued:

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "His voice is still recovering."

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...She's right."

Twiggy smiled.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "Thank you."

Jake gave her a thumbs-up.

---

# 13. JAKE'S DECISION

Later that evening, C.O.R.E. personnel asked Jake why he had decided to reveal his voice now.

Jake thought for several seconds.

Then answered:

> **JAKE:**  
> "I wanted everyone to know."

That was the entire explanation.

No elaborate reasoning.

No hidden motive.

He simply wanted them to know.

After five weeks of silence, Jake had finally decided that he was ready to let everyone hear him.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The distinction is important.

Jake's vocal recovery was **not announced on his behalf.**

Anby and Twiggy had known.

They deliberately allowed him to decide when the information became public.

Jake eventually made that decision himself.

His first words were private.

His later disclosure was not.

He chose to let everyone hear him.

His voice remains:

- extremely quiet;
    
- easily fatigued;
    
- somewhat rough;
    
- physically uncomfortable after prolonged use.
    

But it exists.

And, for the first time in five weeks, the SAFEHOUSE no longer has to guess what Jake sounds like.
