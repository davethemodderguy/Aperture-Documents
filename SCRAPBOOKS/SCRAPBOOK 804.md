### **INFORMATION SECURITY DIVISION**

# **INTERNAL SECURITY NOTICE — SPAWN INTELLIGENCE**

**Classification:** C.O.R.E. INTERNAL  
**Access Restriction:** Level 4 — Authorized Personnel Only  
**Subject:** Unsecured SPAWN Servers  
**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Priority:** HIGH

---

## **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

Multiple documents originating from **SPAWN** have been discovered on several unsecured servers.

The material includes intelligence concerning Aperture Industries, Heaven's Blacksite, its residents, security infrastructure, and ongoing SPAWN operations.

While the discovery represents a significant intelligence opportunity, C.O.R.E. has identified an additional concern:

> **Under no circumstances are the recovered SPAWN documents to be made accessible to Alice.**

This restriction is not based upon Alice's inability to understand the documents.

It is based upon her ability to understand them **far too well**.

---

## **1. REASON FOR RESTRICTION**

Alice has demonstrated an unusually strong emotional attachment to Heaven's Blacksite and its residents.

Following confirmation that SPAWN intended to attack the facility, her behavior changed substantially.

Alice no longer views SPAWN as an abstract external threat.

She considers them a direct threat to:

- Her home.
- Her friends.
- The Blacksite's residents.
- Aperture personnel she has become attached to.

This distinction has already manifested in her behavior during the previous nighttime infiltration attempt.

Alice independently confronted a SPAWN combatant who had entered the Blacksite perimeter.

The encounter resulted in the combatant's death.

C.O.R.E. therefore considers it highly probable that providing Alice with actionable SPAWN intelligence would result in her attempting to act upon that information.

---

## **2. UNSECURED SERVERS**

The recovered servers were apparently insufficiently secured by SPAWN.

Personnel have discovered multiple files containing information that should never have been publicly accessible.

Recovered material currently includes:

- Internal SPAWN communications.
- Operational planning.
- Personnel information.
- Reconnaissance reports.
- References to Heaven's Blacksite.
- Security observations.
- Potential target information.
- Records concerning failed operations.

The Information Security Division is currently isolating and copying relevant material for analysis.

**Personnel are prohibited from casually sharing recovered documents with residents.**

This includes residents who are otherwise authorized to possess general information regarding the SPAWN conflict.

---

## **3. THE "ALICE PROBLEM"**

The restriction was specifically added after personnel raised the question:

> _"What happens if Alice finds the server?"_

The resulting discussion was sufficiently concerning that C.O.R.E. Security issued this notice.

Alice is not expected to misuse the information for personal gain.

That is precisely the problem.

If Alice discovers a document identifying:

- A SPAWN operative,
- A planned attack,
- An infiltration route,
- A vulnerable SPAWN facility,
- Or information indicating that someone intends to harm the Blacksite,

there is a substantial possibility that she will interpret the information as something requiring immediate action.

She may not wait for authorization.

She may not understand why C.O.R.E. would want her to wait.

And, based upon the previous incident, she is demonstrably capable of acting on her own.

---

## **4. INFORMATION HAZARD**

The primary concern is therefore classified as an **informational behavioral hazard**, rather than a conventional security breach.

The documents themselves are not anomalous.

**Alice's response to the information is the concern.**

C.O.R.E. personnel are reminded that withholding information from Alice is not intended as punishment or distrust.

It is intended to prevent an escalation in her behavior.

Alice currently believes that protecting the Blacksite means protecting the people inside it.

Giving her detailed knowledge of SPAWN's activities could transform that protective instinct into proactive aggression.

---

## **5. HANDLING PROCEDURE**

All recovered SPAWN material must be stored within the designated C.O.R.E. intelligence repository.

Personnel are instructed to:

- Keep SPAWN documents away from common-area terminals.
- Do not leave printed copies unattended.
- Do not discuss sensitive contents within Alice's hearing.
- Do not display SPAWN personnel photographs in public areas.
- Immediately report suspected unauthorized access.
- Maintain appropriate access controls on all recovered servers and copies.
- Refer residents requesting SPAWN intelligence to authorized C.O.R.E. personnel.

If Alice asks about the recovered documents, personnel are **not** to fabricate elaborate explanations.

A simple response such as:

> **"C.O.R.E. is handling it."**

is considered preferable.
