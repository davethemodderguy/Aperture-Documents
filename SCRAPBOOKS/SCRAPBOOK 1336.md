# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The longest recorded executive call between **Aperture Industries** and **Woodland Coorp** lasted approximately:

> **Three hours, seven minutes, and forty-two seconds.**

It was scheduled as a formal executive discussion.

It was not formal for very long.

The purpose of the call was ostensibly to address repeated Woodland Coorp operations against Aperture-affiliated residents.

Woodland Coorp has previously abducted multiple individuals associated with Aperture.

The resulting casualties have ranged from severe to catastrophic.

Unfortunately for Woodland Coorp, many of the abducted individuals were significantly more dangerous than Woodland personnel had apparently anticipated.

The call eventually became less of a corporate negotiation and more of an increasingly exhausted argument over the question:

> **“Why do you people keep kidnapping our residents?”**
