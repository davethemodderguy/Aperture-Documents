# Executive Summary

Plush Neri can bite people.

This discovery should not be considered particularly surprising.

Neri has a mouth.

Neri has teeth.

Neri retains voluntary control of her facial movements while in plush form.

Therefore:

**Neri can bite you.**

The fact that approximately 90% of her body is now soft fabric does not appear to have eliminated this capability.

---

# 1. Observed Behavior

Neri's plush bite is generally used for one of three reasons:

1. She is annoyed.
    
2. Someone is bothering her.
    
3. She has decided that biting someone is an acceptable response to the current situation.
    

The third category is currently the least scientifically understood.

She does not generally bite people without reason.

She is, however, perfectly capable of doing so.

---

# 2. The Alice Incident

Alice was carrying Plush Neri on her back.

Neri was holding onto Alice's hoodie.

Everything was normal.

Alice eventually began teasing Neri.

Neri looked over Alice's shoulder.

Alice continued.

Neri leaned forward.

**Chomp.**

Alice:

> "OW!"

Neri released her.

Alice turned around.

Neri stared at her with a completely neutral expression.

Alice:

> "...Did you just bite me?"

Neri nodded.

Alice:

> "Why?"

Neri shrugged.

No further explanation was provided.

---

# 3. Bite Force

Testing Neri's bite strength is prohibited.

Not because the ability is considered particularly dangerous.

Rather, because:

> **There is absolutely no reason to conduct controlled experiments on a ten-year-old plushie biting people.**

Casual observations indicate that the bite is capable of being felt by an adult human.

It is not currently considered a serious injury mechanism.

Neri's plush form does not transform her into an aggressive animal.

She is still Neri.

She simply has a surprisingly functional mouth.

---

# 4. Warning Signs

There are currently no reliable warning signs beyond Neri looking directly at the person she intends to bite.

Personnel have reported the following sequence:

**Neri looks at you.**

**Neri gets closer.**

**Neri opens her mouth.**

**You realize what is happening.**

At this point, the appropriate response is generally:

> **Stop bothering Neri.**

---

# 5. Backpack Configuration

The biting capability is particularly relevant when Neri is being carried as a backpack.

A person carrying Neri should remember that she is attached to their back.

Her face is therefore conveniently located near the carrier's shoulder.

This has resulted in the following unofficial safety rule:

> **Do not annoy the tiny seal on your back.**

Alice has acknowledged this rule.

She has not always followed it.

---

# 6. General Personnel Advisory

Employees are reminded:

**Do not poke Plush Neri.**

**Do not squeeze Plush Neri.**

**Do not pull her tail.**

**Do not repeatedly call her cute.**

**Do not attempt to provoke a bite to "see if she actually does it."**

**Do not put fingers near her mouth as a joke.**

If Neri bites you because you deliberately annoyed her, C.O.R.E. will have very little sympathy.

---

# 7. Is Neri Dangerous?

No.

Plush Neri remains a SAFEHOUSE resident.

Her ability to bite is simply another retained biological function.

The current classification remains:

**LOW RISK.**

The primary danger is not Neri.

The primary danger is an employee making the profoundly questionable decision to annoy a tiny plush seal and discovering that the tiny plush seal has opinions.
