# 1. AUTHORITY EXPANSION

The original Sleight of Hand Trial I permitted Jane Doe to conduct psychological interrogation within a controlled interview environment.

This restriction has now been **formally removed.**

Following review of the consistently unsuccessful attempts to defeat Jane through conventional interview resistance, Neri Tachyon and Director Lucious have jointly authorized Jane Doe to make full use of her established operational arsenal and field-interrogation capabilities during the trial.

This includes:

- interception of participants outside the interrogation room;
    
- surprise capture;
    
- controlled restraint;
    
- relocation to an interrogation facility;
    
- environmental manipulation;
    
- intimidation;
    
- psychological pressure;
    
- deception;
    
- misdirection;
    
- prolonged questioning;
    
- strategic silence;
    
- controlled use of Jane's equipment;
    
- and other established non-lethal intelligence-extraction methods available to her.
    

The trial remains **non-lethal.**

Participants are not authorized to be deliberately injured for the purpose of obtaining intelligence.

The objective remains unchanged:

> **Make the participant voluntarily surrender the intelligence.**

Jane does not need to physically force an answer.

She simply has considerably more freedom in determining **where the conversation begins.**

The original rule:
