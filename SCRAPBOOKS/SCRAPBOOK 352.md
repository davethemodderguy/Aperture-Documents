# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELLNESS OBSERVATION**

# **COREY — MART BLANKET EVENT**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corey  
**Associated Entity:** Z-335 (Marts)

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corey has recently been experiencing a prolonged period of low morale.

She has remained functional and continued her normal responsibilities, but personnel have noticed that she has had difficulty maintaining her usual happiness.

The Marts noticed as well.

They responded in their usual way.

They stayed close.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

One evening, Corey found herself unable to sleep.

Rather than remain in bed, she moved to the floor of the treehouse.

She brought a normal blanket with her.

The Marts did not approve.

Within minutes, several began gathering around her.

One quietly asked:

> "...Cold?"

Corey shook her head.

> "...No."

Another asked:

> "...Sad?"

She hesitated.

Then nodded.

The Marts immediately understood.

---

# **MART BLANKET**

One Mart flattened itself into Blanket Form.

Then another.

Then another.

Soon, a large group had formed a soft layer across Corey.

Unlike their normal cooling state, the Marts adjusted their internal temperatures to provide gentle warmth.

They arranged themselves carefully around her without covering her face.

No one had instructed them to do this.

They simply wanted her to be comfortable.

---

# **THE NIGHT**

Corey eventually fell asleep on the floor.

The Marts remained completely still.

Normally, a sleeping Mart cannot be moved.

Tonight, they seemed to make a deliberate effort to remain where they were.

Whenever one began drifting away, another gently nudged it back.

The treehouse became unusually quiet.

Corin found Corey several hours later.

She stopped at the doorway.

Corey was sleeping peacefully beneath what appeared to be a living blue blanket.

Several tiny top hats were visible around the edges.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin quietly whispered:

> "...You guys are ridiculous."

A Mart opened one eye.

> "...Help."

Corin smiled.

> "...Yeah."

She pulled another blanket over Corey and left them undisturbed.

---

# **MORNING**

Corey woke several hours later.

The Marts were still there.

She slowly sat up.

Several Marts remained attached to her like a blanket.

One sleepy voice asked:

> "...Better?"

Corey thought for a moment.

> "...A little."

The Marts immediately became happier.

> **"Happy!"**

Corey laughed quietly.

> "...I said a little."

One Mart replied:

> "...Enough."

---

# **LUMI'S OBSERVATION**

When Lumi heard about the incident, she was not concerned that the Marts had been unable to "fix" Corey's sadness.

Instead, she said:

> "...They don't need to fix everything."

She looked toward the treehouse.

> "...Sometimes staying beside someone is enough."

---

# **STAR'S COMMENT**

Star later visited Corey.

She found several Marts still sleeping around her.

Star quietly asked:

> "...Can I join?"

A Mart shifted slightly.

> "...Yes."

Star sat down beside Corey.

No one said anything for a while.

---

# **COREY'S COMMENT**

When asked about the experience afterward, Corey smiled faintly.

> "...I wasn't really looking for a solution."

She glanced toward the Marts.

> "...I just didn't want to be alone."

A Mart immediately climbed onto her shoulder.

> "...Not alone."

Corey gently touched its top hat.

> "...I know."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The Marts cannot always make Corey happy.

They cannot remove whatever is weighing on her.

They cannot guarantee that tomorrow will be better.

But they can stay.

So they do.

That night, Corey slept on the floor of the treehouse beneath a blanket made entirely of Marts.

For once, she didn't have to try to be happy.

She only had to rest.

**End of Personnel Wellness Observation**
