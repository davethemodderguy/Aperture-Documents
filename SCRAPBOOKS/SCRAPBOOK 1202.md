# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### ARMORY & RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-ARM-4C-NERI-334  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESTRICTED — ARMORY / RESIDENT WELFARE  
**STATUS:** UNDER REVIEW  
**SUBJECT:** Neri Tachyon  
**INCIDENT:** Unscheduled Armory Material Removal

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neri Tachyon visited the Blacksite Armory.

She did not request an escort.

She did not submit a conventional requisition.

She did, however, leave the Armory carrying several items.

The removed inventory consisted of:

- **Glock 17** — 1 unit;
    
- **Glock 18** — 1 unit;
    
- **Glock 19** — 1 unit;
    
- **Glock 20X** — 1 unit;
    
- several compatible Glock magazines;
    
- a quantity of **5.7×28 mm ammunition**;
    
- several **Osprey suppressors**.
    

The items were subsequently located in Neri's possession.

No personnel were injured.

No weapons were discharged.

The Armory is currently attempting to determine why Neri considered this a normal Tuesday activity.

---

# 2. ARMORY ACCESS

Security footage shows Neri entering the Armory during a period of low personnel traffic.

She presented her authorized credentials.

The system permitted her into the resident-access portion of the facility.

Neri proceeded directly toward the handgun storage area.

She examined several firearms.

She selected four.

The Armory inventory system subsequently registered the removals.

Approximately two minutes later, the system registered additional equipment being removed.

The system then generated an inventory discrepancy notification.

The Armory technician initially assumed it was a counting error.

It was not.

---

# 3. REMOVED FIREARMS

The following weapons were recorded as removed:

|Item|Quantity|Status|
|---|--:|---|
|Glock 17|1|Recovered / pending authorization|
|Glock 18|1|Recovered / pending authorization|
|Glock 19|1|Recovered / pending authorization|
|Glock 20X|1|Recovered / pending authorization|

No modifications were observed at the time of recovery.

No weapon was fired during the incident.

---

# 4. ADDITIONAL EQUIPMENT

Neri also removed several Glock magazines.

A quantity of 5.7×28 mm ammunition was removed from a separate secured ammunition inventory.

Several Osprey suppressors were also removed.

The combination of items immediately raised questions among Armory personnel.

One technician reportedly asked:

> **"Why does a ten-year-old need four different Glocks?"**

Another technician corrected him:

> **"She's not asking."**

---

# 5. INTERCEPTION

Neri was intercepted by Neko approximately fifteen minutes after leaving the Armory.

Neko looked at the equipment.

Then at Neri.

Then at the equipment again.

> **NEKO:**  
> "Neri."

Neri looked up.

> **NERI:**  
> "Hm?"

> **NEKO:**  
> "Why are you carrying four Glocks?"

Neri remained silent for several seconds.

> **NERI:**  
> "Research."

Neko stared.

> **NEKO:**  
> "Research."

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> "Yes."

> **NEKO:**  
> "And the ammunition?"

Neri looked at it.

> **NERI:**  
> "Research."

Neko closed his eyes.

> **NEKO:**  
> "And the suppressors?"

Neri paused.

> **NERI:**  
> "...Research."

Neko took a long breath.

---

# 6. ARMORY REVIEW

The Armory subsequently reviewed Neri's request history.

There was no corresponding approved research request.

No active experiment had been registered.

No laboratory allocation had been issued.

No weapons-development authorization had been attached to the removal.

The Armory therefore classified the event as:

> **UNSCHEDULED MATERIAL REMOVAL**

Neri was asked to explain.

She provided the following written statement:

> **"I wanted to compare them."**

No further explanation was provided.

---

# 7. THE 5.7×28 MM AMMUNITION

The ammunition presented an additional problem.

The recovered ammunition was not associated with the four Glock models removed.

Neri was asked why she took it.

She answered:

> **NERI:**  
> "Because I need it."

> **ARMORY OFFICER:**  
> "For what?"

Neri paused.

> **NERI:**  
> "Research."

The officer stared at her.

Neri stared back.

Neither elaborated.

---

# 8. OSPREY SUPPRESSORS

The suppressors were similarly categorized as research material pending review.

C.O.R.E. has not established that Neri intended to use them operationally.

No firing tests were conducted.

The equipment has been isolated pending authorization.

---

# 9. NERI'S LABORATORY

A subsequent inspection found several empty work surfaces in Neri's laboratory.

No weapons were found actively being modified.

No live-fire experiment was underway.

Several notebooks contained handwritten references to:

- comparative platform studies;
    
- mechanical differences;
    
- materials;
    
- acoustic behavior;
    
- ammunition compatibility;
    
- and general equipment observations.
    

The notes did not constitute an approved weapons-development program.

C.O.R.E. therefore suspended further access to Armory materials until Neri submits a formal research request.

---

# 12. CURRENT STATUS

All removed firearms and associated equipment have been recovered.

They remain under Armory custody.

Neri has been instructed that future research involving controlled equipment requires:

1. a documented research purpose;
    
2. an inventory request;
    
3. appropriate authorization;
    
4. supervised access where required.
    

Neri acknowledged the instructions.

Her response:

> **NERI:**  
> "...Okay."

She then asked:

> **NERI:**  
> "Can I submit the request?"

The Armory officer replied:

> **"Yes."**

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> "Okay."

She left.
