# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### RESIDENT WELFARE & MEDICAL DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-JAKE-038  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — RESIDENT WELFARE / ANOMALOUS RETURN  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**SUBJECT:** Jake — Return to SAFEHOUSE  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE / Sector 4-C

---

# 1. RETURN EVENT

Jake has returned.

The event occurred without warning.

No extraction procedure was performed.

No equipment was used.

No known portal was opened.

The anomalous grassland simply ceased to be the location in which Jake remained.

Jake was found inside the SAFEHOUSE elevator.

He was holding his teddy bear.

---

# 2. PHYSICAL CONDITION

Jake's appearance remains substantially consistent with the form observed within the grassland.

Notably:

- white hair;
    
- azure eyes;
    
- folded wings;
    
- shattered halo;
    
- familiar childlike appearance;
    
- faint stellar glow.
    

The arrow remains embedded through his chest.

It has not been removed.

It does not appear to cause conventional bleeding.

No visible blood loss is occurring.

No conventional wound closure has occurred around it.

The arrow appears to have become part of Jake's current state.

Attempts to determine whether it can be physically removed have been suspended.

Jake has not requested that it be removed.

---

# 3. THE TEDDY BEAR

The teddy bear has been returned to Jake.

Its condition is unchanged from its previously documented state.

Jake immediately recognized it.

He held it against his chest.

For several seconds, he simply stared at it.

Then he hugged it.

No verbal response was initially given.

The teddy bear's core subsequently became active.

A faint heartbeat was detected.

Once.

Then again.

Then steadily.

The sound was quiet.

But unmistakable.

---

# 4. FIRST MOMENTS

Jake remained inside the elevator.

Bao was the first resident to approach him.

She stopped several steps away.

Jake looked at her.

Neither spoke.

Bao's eyes immediately filled with tears.

Jake looked down at his teddy bear.

Then back at Bao.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Hi."

Bao covered her mouth.

> **BAO:**  
> "Hi."

Jake looked around the hallway.

The SAFEHOUSE was exactly as he remembered it.

People were there.

They remembered him.

Nobody appeared confused by his presence.

For the first time since his disappearance, Jake was standing among people who knew exactly who he was.

---

# 5. NERI

Neri approached next.

She stopped in front of him.

She stared at the arrow.

Then at the teddy bear.

Then at Jake's face.

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Hi, Neri."

Neri's expression remained almost completely still.

She nodded.

Then quietly:

> **NERI:**  
> "You're back."

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Yeah."

Neri stepped forward and hugged him.

Jake remained still for a moment.

Then wrapped one arm around her.

---

# 6. NORA

Nora was unable to remain composed.

She ran toward him.

She stopped just before colliding with him, apparently remembering the arrow at the last second.

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Hi."

Nora began crying.

> **NORA:**  
> "You're here."

Jake nodded.

Nora hugged him carefully.

Jake hugged her back.

His teddy bear remained between them.

---

# 7. JANE DOE

Jane did not immediately approach.

She stood several meters away.

Jake noticed her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Jane."

Jane's expression broke.

She walked over slowly.

She crouched in front of him.

For several seconds, neither spoke.

Then:

> **JANE:**  
> "You're really here."

Jake nodded.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Yeah."

Jane carefully placed one hand on his shoulder.

She did not say anything else.

---

# 8. TWIGGY

Twiggy remained in the hallway.

She could not bring herself to approach.

Jake noticed her.

He looked at her for several seconds.

Twiggy lowered her head.

> **TWIGGY:**  
> "I'm sorry."

Jake did not answer immediately.

He looked down at his teddy bear.

Then back at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I know."

Twiggy began crying.

Jake did not tell her that everything was okay.

He did not say that it didn't matter.

He simply remained there.

That was enough for the moment.

---

# 9. MEDICAL OBSERVATION

Medical personnel conducted a non-invasive assessment.

Jake is alive.

His stellar core is active.

The teddy bear's cardiac function is stable.

The arrow remains anomalous.

No acute deterioration has been observed.

His physical condition is considerably better than during the period immediately following his previous restoration.

However, the presence of the arrow remains unexplained.

It is currently classified as:

> **ANOMALOUS RESIDUAL STRUCTURE — NON-REMOVAL**

No attempt will be made to remove it without Jake's consent and a clear understanding of the consequences.

---

# 10. THE ELEVATOR

The missing floor remains present.

The button remains surrounded by faint blue Stardust.

It is still not functional through ordinary means.

However, the elevator no longer appears to be the only connection to the grassland.

C.O.R.E. has not determined whether the grassland still exists independently.

No personnel have attempted to access it since Jake's return.

The priority is currently the resident who has returned.

Not the place he came from.

---

# 11. JAKE'S ROOM

Jake eventually returned to his room.

The room remained almost exactly as it had been.

He stood in the doorway.

Looked inside.

Then looked down at his teddy bear.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...You kept it."

Bao was standing behind him.

> **BAO:**  
> "Of course."

Jake nodded.

He walked inside.

He placed the teddy bear on his bed.

Then picked it up again almost immediately.

Bao smiled faintly.

> **BAO:**  
> "Missed him?"

Jake hugged the bear.

> **JAKE:**  
> "A little."

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Jake has returned to the SAFEHOUSE.

His return appears stable.

His memories are intact.

The SAFEHOUSE residents remember him.

His identity is restored.

His teddy bear has been returned.

His stellar core is active.

The anomalous arrow remains embedded within his chest.

Its purpose remains unknown.

Whether it represents a permanent consequence of the grassland, a symbolic remnant of his disappearance, or an independent anomalous phenomenon remains undetermined.

For now, Jake has chosen to leave it alone.

---

# CLOSING OBSERVATION

That evening, Jake sat on his bed.

His teddy bear rested against his chest.

Its heartbeat was faint.

But steady.

His halo remained broken.

The arrow remained through his heart.

His wings rested quietly behind him.

Someone knocked.

Jake looked toward the door.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Come in."

The door opened.

Bao stood there.

She smiled.

Jake looked at her for a moment.

Then shifted slightly to make room on the bed.

Bao sat beside him.

Neither spoke.

They didn't need to.

Jake was home.
