# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The remaining two missing members of Anby's original Silverline sister group have been located.

**Phaethon** and **Sora** have been recovered.

Their recovery completes the known surviving members of the original twelve-subject Silverline group.

The current status is therefore:

> **Six sisters deceased.**  
> **Six sisters recovered.**

The recovered sisters are now:

1. **Anby**
    
2. **Twiggy**
    
3. **Solider 11**
    
4. **Iris**
    
5. **Phaethon**
    
6. **Sora**
    

All six are currently under protected-person status.

Anby's residential floor within the SAFEHOUSE has subsequently become considerably more crowded.

All six sisters now reside together.

Their current employment arrangements are also notable.

Multiple sisters have entered employment with the **Central Execution Department**.

The department's director is:

> **Linderverne "Endmin" Merryweather**

Endmin is herself a permanent SAFEHOUSE resident.

She is also the daughter of **Lucy Merryweather**, Warden of the Banlands.

The result is an unusual situation in which an entire family of former Silverline artificial humans now works for an organization directed by one of their own SAFEHOUSE roommates.

# 1. PHAETHON

Phaethon was located independently from the other sisters.

She had maintained a low-profile civilian existence following her separation from Silverline.

Unlike Twiggy, she had not entered medicine.

Unlike Iris and Solider 11, she had not established herself primarily through gang contracting.

Her circumstances were considerably more difficult to reconstruct.

However, identity confirmation was eventually achieved through:

- physical-template matching;
    
- Silverline records;
    
- personal memories;
    
- and the subject's recognition of Anby's name.
    

Upon being informed that Anby was alive and protected, Phaethon voluntarily agreed to relocate.

---

# 2. SORA

Sora was recovered shortly afterward.

Her situation was similarly independent from the other sisters.

She had avoided Project: Silverline entirely since separation and had established a separate identity.

When KCA personnel informed her that several of her sisters had been located, she initially asked:

> **SORA:**  
> "How many?"

She was told:

> **"Five."**

Sora immediately asked:

> **SORA:**  
> "Anby?"

The answer was yes.

Sora agreed to enter protection.

# 3. FINAL REUNION

The reunion of all six sisters occurred within the SAFEHOUSE.

Anby was already waiting.

Twiggy and Solider 11 were nearby.

Iris arrived shortly afterward.

Then Phaethon.

Then Sora.

For several seconds, all six simply looked at one another.

No one needed to ask who anyone was.

They knew.

The faces were not.

Anby stepped forward first.

> **ANBY:**  
> "You're both here."

> **SORA:**  
> "We're here."

Phaethon nodded.

> **PHAETHON:**  
> "All six."

Anby looked around.

For the first time since the Silverline group was separated:

> **Six sisters were standing together.**
