# 1. OVERVIEW

Neri's current standard outfit has remained remarkably consistent since her integration into SAFEHOUSE.

The overall appearance is dominated by a **light-azure color scheme**, with matching garments and accessories.

While aesthetically simple, several components of the outfit have personal or practical significance.

Most notably, one hairclip was given to Neri by her deceased younger sister.

Neri does not appear to remove it voluntarily.

---

# 2. HAIR ACCESSORIES

Neri currently wears **three hairclips**.

### 2.1 — CROSS-HOLDING HAIRCLIPS

Two small clips are positioned diagonally across one another, securing Neri's bangs.

These are not ordinary decorative accessories.

Both are functional blades.

They are extremely sharp despite their small size.

Their exact dimensions are not considered relevant to ordinary personnel.

Their existence is considered extremely relevant to anyone thinking:

> “It's just a hairclip.”

It is not.

There are two of them.

They cross over one another to keep Neri's bangs secured.

---

### 2.2 — FISH-BONE HAIRCLIP

A decorative fish-bone hairclip is positioned near the crossed clips.

Unlike the two crossed clips, this accessory is **not a weapon**.

Its significance is personal.

It was a gift from Neri's younger sister.

Neri continues to wear it.

She has been observed checking that it is still present after stressful events.

C.O.R.E. considers the item a protected personal possession.

Personnel are not to confiscate, remove, borrow, or otherwise interfere with it.

---

# 3. COAT

Neri wears an **oversized light-azure wool coat**.

The coat is large enough to cover most of her hands.

This contributes significantly to her distinctive silhouette.

It also creates an unusual practical problem:

> Neri cannot easily use her hands while wearing it.

Neri has solved this problem in a manner that C.O.R.E. has reluctantly classified as ingenious.

She uses the **collar of the coat to grab pieces of cake.**

---

# 4. THE WATERPROOF COAT INCIDENT

Neko applied a specialized waterproofing spray to the coat after observing Neri repeatedly getting food on it.

The treatment has proven highly effective.

Cake, frosting, candy residue, and other food substances generally do not adhere strongly to the treated fabric.

Neri therefore performs the following procedure:

**Cake located.**

↓

**Hands hidden inside oversized coat.**

↓

**Collar positioned around cake.**

↓

**Cake acquired.**

↓

**Cake consumed.**

↓

**Coat inspected.**

↓

**Residue wiped away.**

↓

**No meaningful staining.**

Neko reportedly described the result as:

> **“Peak engineering.”**

Miss Neko disagreed with the classification.

> **MISS NEKO:** “You waterproofed her coat so she can eat with it.”

> **NEKO:** “Yes.”

> **MISS NEKO:** “Why?”

> **NEKO:** “Because she kept getting cake on it.”

> **MISS NEKO:** “You could have taught her to use her hands.”

> **NEKO:** “But this is funnier.”

---

# 5. SHIRT & TIE

Underneath the coat, Neri wears a shirt matching the **light-azure color scheme**.

She also wears a matching **light-azure tie**.

The tie has become one of her most recognizable identifying features.

Personnel have occasionally described her overall appearance as unusually formal for a ten-year-old.

This is technically accurate.

It is also somewhat misleading.

Neri can look like a tiny office employee while carrying enough concealed weaponry to make Residential Security uncomfortable.

---

# 6. SCARF

Neri wears a scarf matching the same light-azure color scheme.

The scarf is primarily an article of clothing.

However, personnel should remember that Neri's clothing frequently contains concealed or potentially dangerous objects.

The scarf itself is **not classified as a blade**.

It should nevertheless not be grabbed, pulled, or used to restrain Neri without authorization.

---

# 7. EARRINGS

Neri also wears earrings as part of her standard outfit.

They complement the light-azure clothing scheme and are considered ordinary personal accessories.

No anomalous properties have been identified.

Unlike the hairclips, they are not currently believed to contain blades.

**Current recommendation: do not test this assumption by taking them.**

# 9. PERSONAL SIGNIFICANCE

Neri's clothing is not merely aesthetic.

The fish-bone hairclip represents one of the last physical objects connecting her to her sister.

Her oversized coat appears to provide comfort as well as warmth.

The matching light-azure accessories have become part of Neri's identity within SAFEHOUSE.

She has effectively developed a recognizable personal style:

> **Soft colors.**  
> **Oversized clothing.**  
> **Formal details.**  
> **Sentimental accessory.**  
> **Several knives.**

C.O.R.E. has elected not to question the last part.

---

# 10. CAFETERIA OBSERVATION

Neri's outfit has produced one particularly recurring SAFEHOUSE scene.

Neri enters the cafeteria.

Her hands are hidden inside the oversized sleeves.

She approaches the cake.

She grabs the cake with the coat collar.

She eats it.

Someone eventually asks:

> **“Why don't you just use your hands?”**

Neri looks at them.

> **NERI:** “Coat works.”

She continues eating.

The coat remains clean.

Neko is visibly proud of himself every time this happens.
