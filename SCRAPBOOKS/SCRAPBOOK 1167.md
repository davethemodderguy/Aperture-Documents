# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### MEDICAL & RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-MED-4C-NERI-292  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — MEDICAL / RESIDENT WELFARE  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE / RESTRICTED  
**SUBJECT:** Neri — Persistent Pain, Mobility Loss, Resident Support, and Hell Fire Lottery Cancellation

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neri remains in **significant pain** following the previous handling incident.

The pain is particularly severe around her heart.

In this case, the phrase has become difficult to separate into its two meanings.

There is the physical pain.

And there is the emotional one.

Both are currently present.

Neri is presently unable to walk independently.

She therefore requires assistance from other residents or authorized personnel whenever she needs to be moved.

Her Hell Fire Lottery has been **cancelled until further notice.**

The remaining Hell Fire shotguns have been removed from circulation and sealed inside Neri's room.

At the time of cancellation:

> **Josh was the only completed lottery winner.**

No further lottery winners will be selected.

---

# 2. PHYSICAL CONDITION

Neri's condition deteriorated following the previous day's incident.

Her ability to move independently has significantly decreased.

She is currently unable to walk.

She can still move her plush body to a limited degree, but attempting to travel any meaningful distance causes substantial discomfort and is not considered appropriate.

Her current movement therefore consists primarily of:

- being carried;
    
- being supported;
    
- being transported using appropriate mobility equipment;
    
- being repositioned by trusted personnel;
    
- or remaining stationary.
    

Neri is not resisting assistance.

This is notable.

Normally, Neri attempts to minimize the inconvenience she causes other people.

Currently, she appears too exhausted and uncomfortable to maintain that behavior.

---

# 3. CARDIAC PAIN

The most persistent physical complaint remains the area around Neri's heart.

She frequently curls inward and holds her chest.

At times she becomes completely still.

When asked whether she is experiencing pain elsewhere, she generally indicates that the cardiac region is the primary problem.

When asked how severe it is:

Neri does not answer numerically.

She simply closes her eyes.

---

# 4. THE OTHER KIND OF PAIN

Medical personnel have also observed a second component.

Neri is frightened.

She has become substantially more cautious around unfamiliar personnel.

The incident has also affected her willingness to be handled.

She does not appear to believe that every MSAC Operator is dangerous.

However, the experience has altered her expectations.

Someone was trying to handle her.

She became uncomfortable.

She communicated this.

Then she was suddenly thrown into a wall.

Her body hurt.

Her heart hurt.

And now:

> **Being picked up by someone unfamiliar is no longer automatically safe to her.**

This is not merely a medical problem.

It is an emotional injury.

---

# 5. MSAC RESPONSE

Following the incident, numerous MSAC Operators visited Neri.

They did not arrive as a formal delegation.

They came individually.

Some checked on her.

Some left small notes.

Some simply stood in the doorway and asked whether she needed anything.

Several did not speak at all.

One Operator left a cup of coffee nearby without entering the room.

Another left a small blanket.

A third simply said:

> **“Get better.”**

Then left.

Neri did not respond verbally.

She watched them leave.

---

# 6. IMPORTANT CHANGE

Neri has not requested that MSAC stop visiting entirely.

However, she has demonstrated a strong preference for:

> **Short visits.**

She does not currently have the stamina for prolonged interaction.

She may acknowledge someone with:

- a nod;
    
- a blink;
    
- a small movement of her hand;
    
- or a quiet “Mm.”
    

That is sufficient.

Personnel are reminded that visiting Neri is not an excuse to make her talk.

---

# 7. THE RESPONSIBLE OPERATOR

The MSAC Operator involved in the original incident subsequently resigned from service.

His resignation was voluntary.

Prior to leaving, he submitted a written statement acknowledging responsibility for his reaction.

The relevant portion reads:

> **“She was telling me to stop.”**
> 
> **“I reacted before thinking.”**
> 
> **“I hurt her.”**
> 
> **“I don't think I should be carrying a weapon or working around residents if I can react like that.”**

The resignation has been accepted.

No attempt has been made to bring him back into contact with Neri.

---

# 8. NERI'S RESPONSE TO THE RESIGNATION

Neri was informed.

She remained silent.

After several seconds, she quietly said:

> **“Okay.”**

She did not ask why he resigned.

She did not express satisfaction.

She did not request punishment.

She did not ask whether he was coming back.

She simply returned to resting.

C.O.R.E. considers this consistent with Neri's current emotional state.

She is concerned with the pain she is experiencing now.

Not with determining what should happen to the person who caused it.

---

# 9. TRANSPORTATION STATUS

Because Neri cannot currently walk, SAFEHOUSE residents have begun assisting with transportation.

The most common arrangement is simply:

> **Pick Neri up.**

Then:

> **Carry Neri.**

Then:

> **Put Neri somewhere comfortable.**

Neri generally does not complain.

She may rest her head against the carrier.

She may close her eyes.

She may quietly indicate where she wants to go.

Sometimes she simply points.

---

# 10. JANE DOE

Jane has been particularly careful.

She no longer automatically picks Neri up.

She asks first.

> **JANE:**  
> “Need a lift?”

Neri nods.

Jane carefully picks her up.

No sudden movement.

No jumping.

No spinning.

No unnecessary repositioning.

Neri usually remains quiet against her.

At one point:

> **Neri:**  
> “Slow.”

Jane immediately reduced her pace.

> **JANE:**  
> “Slow.”

---

# 11. BAO

Bao has assumed primary responsibility for Neri's medical monitoring.

She is also currently the person Neri most readily allows to reposition her when she is in significant pain.

Bao does not treat this as permission to constantly move her.

If Neri is comfortable:

> **Leave her there.**

If Neri needs to move:

> **Move her carefully.**

If Neri indicates pain:

> **Stop.**

The simplicity of the protocol has proven effective.

---

# 12. HELL FIRE LOTTERY

The Hell Fire Lottery has been **cancelled.**

It will not resume on its previous schedule.

The daily 7:30 PM draw has been discontinued.

No further tickets will be issued.

No additional streaks will be recorded.

No replacement administrator has been appointed.

---

# 13. FINAL LOTTERY RESULT

At the time of cancellation:

### Completed Winners:

**Josh — 1 Hell Fire**

Josh remains the only completed winner of the lottery.

His award remains valid.

No additional lottery winners will be recognized.

---

# 14. REMAINING HELL FIRE UNITS

The remaining Hell Fire shotguns have been removed from the lottery pool.

Neri personally sealed them inside her room.

The units are now classified:

> **WITHHELD — NERI AUTHORITY**

They are not available for:

- lottery;
    
- competition;
    
- requisition;
    
- reassignment;
    
- demonstration;
    
- or informal inspection.
    

They remain sealed until Neri determines otherwise.

---

# 15. NERI'S ADMINISTRATIVE ACTION

The decision was made while Neri was still in plush form.

She was lying on her bed.

The lottery terminal was beside her.

She looked at it for several seconds.

Then she shut it down.

She subsequently authorized the remaining Hell Fire units to be secured in her room.

When asked whether she intended to restart the lottery later:

Neri shook her head.

When asked whether she meant permanently:

Another shake.

When asked whether she simply did not know:

A small nod.

No further questions were asked.

---

# 16. OPERATIONAL EFFECT

The cancellation has generated considerable disappointment among MSAC personnel.

However, there has been almost universal agreement that the lottery can wait.

One operator summarized the situation:

> **“It's a shotgun lottery. She's hurt. We'll live.”**

Another responded:

> **“Exactly.”**

The remaining Hell Fire units therefore remain secured.

There is no operational urgency.

# 18. PERSONNEL GUIDANCE

Neri is not to be pressured into returning to normal activity.

Do not tell her:

> “You're fine.”

Do not tell her:

> “It's just a bruise.”

Do not tell her:

> “You're a plushie, you'll be okay.”

Do not encourage her to walk before she is ready and medically capable.

Do not repeatedly ask whether she is feeling better.

Do not make her explain what happened.

Do not turn her recovery into a public event.

If she wants to be carried:

> **Carry her carefully.**

If she wants to stay somewhere:

> **Let her stay.**

If she wants someone to leave:

> **Leave.**

If she asks someone to remain:

> **Remain.**

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Neri is normally very good at disappearing.

She finds somewhere quiet.

She sits down.

She drinks her coffee.

She reads.

She draws.

She becomes very small and very difficult to notice.

This time, she cannot even walk away.

So people are having to carry her.

Not because she has suddenly become helpless.

Not because she has forgotten how to be independent.

Because right now, **she hurts too much.**

Her heart hurts physically.

Her heart hurts emotionally.

And neither kind of pain can be fixed by telling her to simply get over it.

The Hell Fire Lottery can wait.

The remaining shotguns can wait.

MSAC can wait.

Neri needs to heal.

For now, that is the only priority.

**— C.O.R.E. Medical & Resident Welfare Division**
