# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. — CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### SAFEHOUSE WELFARE & ANOMALOUS RESIDENTS DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** CORE-SW-JA-028  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESTRICTED — MEDICAL / RESIDENT INTERACTION  
**SUBJECT:** _The Words He Has Left_  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE Residential Complex  
**PARTICIPANTS:** Jake Akana, Clover, Aries Akana, Phoenix, Bao, Frost, Neri, Nora, Camelia

---

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following the permanent deterioration of Jake Akana's speech after five weeks of exposure to the exterior cold, Jake has been observed communicating through short, fragmented bursts of words.

His speech is not merely quiet. Words may stop unexpectedly, syllables may be difficult to produce, and complete sentences are frequently beyond his current ability. Nevertheless, Jake continues to initiate conversations, express preferences, recognize people, and participate in SAFEHOUSE life.

The following transcripts document ordinary interactions with eight individuals familiar to Jake.

They are not intended to demonstrate what Jake can no longer say.

They document what he still manages to communicate.

---

### I. CLOVER — THE ROOMMATE

**Location:** Jake and Clover's shared room  
**Context:** Clover has returned with a small collection of lucky clovers.

_Clover sits beside Jake, carefully arranging the clovers on a table. Jake watches her for a while before pointing at one._

**JAKE:** “Clover... that one.”

**CLOVER:** “This one? Do you want it?”

**JAKE:** “For... me?”

**CLOVER:** “Of course! Here.”

_She places it in his hand._

**JAKE:** “Thank... you.”

**CLOVER:** “You're welcome, Jake.”

_Jake examines the clover._

**JAKE:** “Lucky... five hours?”

**CLOVER:** “About five hours, yes. Then it wilts.”

**JAKE:** “Keep... it?”

**CLOVER:** “You can keep it until it wilts.”

_Jake carefully sets it beside his teddy bear._

**JAKE:** “My... lucky.”

**CLOVER:** “Your lucky clover.”

_He nods._

**JAKE:** “Stay... here?”

**CLOVER:** “The clover?”

**JAKE:** “You.”

_Clover's expression softens._

**CLOVER:** “I'm right here, Jake. I'm not going anywhere.”

**JAKE:** “Okay.”

_He leans against her._

**Observation:** Jake's phrasing may initially be ambiguous. Clover did not immediately understand that his question concerned her presence rather than the clover. Once clarified, he appeared satisfied with the answer.

---

### II. ARIES AKANA — HIS OLDER BROTHER

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE common room  
**Context:** Aries has finished work and come to check on Jake.

_Aries crouches beside Jake's chair. Jake looks up at him._

**ARIES:** “Hey, little brother. How are you feeling?”

**JAKE:** “Tired.”

**ARIES:** “Anything hurting?”

**JAKE:** “No. Just... tired.”

**ARIES:** “All right. You don't have to talk if it's difficult.”

_Jake catches the edge of Aries's sleeve before he can stand._

**JAKE:** “You... go?”

**ARIES:** “I have a little more work to do. But I'll be around.”

**JAKE:** “Stay... more?”

_Aries pauses._

**ARIES:** “You want me to stay a little longer?”

**JAKE:** “Mm.”

**ARIES:** “Then I'll stay.”

_He sits beside Jake._

**JAKE:** “Aries?”

**ARIES:** “Yeah?”

**JAKE:** “My... brother.”

_Aries's expression changes. He puts an arm around Jake carefully._

**ARIES:** “Always, little brother.”

**JAKE:** “Love... you.”

**ARIES:** “I love you too.”

_Jake rests against him._

**JAKE:** “Don't... gone.”

**ARIES:** “I'm not gone. I'm right here.”

**Observation:** Jake continues to identify Aries as his older brother and can express affection in short phrases. The request not to leave should be understood as a need for reassurance, not as a demand that Aries abandon all other responsibilities.

---

### III. PHOENIX — THE CARETAKER

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE medical recovery area  
**Context:** Phoenix checks on Jake following a difficult morning.

**PHOENIX:** “How's my little guy doing?”

**JAKE:** “Bad.”

**PHOENIX:** “Bad how? Are you hurting?”

**JAKE:** “Words... hurt.”

_Phoenix stops asking questions._

**PHOENIX:** “Then we won't make you use them.”

_Jake looks at Phoenix for several seconds._

**JAKE:** “Not... your fault.”

**PHOENIX:** “I know, sweetheart.”

**JAKE:** “You... helped.”

**PHOENIX:** “I'm still here to help.”

**JAKE:** “Stay?”

**PHOENIX:** “Yes. I'll stay with you for a while.”

_Phoenix settles into the nearby chair. Jake pulls his spectral teddy bear closer._

**JAKE:** “Bear... too.”

**PHOENIX:** “You want me to stay with you and your bear?”

**JAKE:** “Yes.”

**PHOENIX:** “Then it's a very important meeting. The three of us.”

_A faint, almost imperceptible movement crosses Jake's face. It is unclear whether he is trying to smile._

**JAKE:** “Silly...”

**PHOENIX:** “There he is.”

_Phoenix does not ask Jake to repeat himself._

**Observation:** Phoenix's familiarity with Jake allows them to communicate without requiring every thought to be expressed fully. Phoenix must still avoid assuming that every short phrase has only one possible meaning.

### IV. BAO — THE PERSON WHO FOUND HIM

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE kitchen  
**Context:** Bao has brought Jake a warm drink and something sweet.

_Bao places the drink in front of him. Jake holds the cup with both hands._

**BAO:** “Careful. It's warm.”

**JAKE:** “Warm... good.”

**BAO:** “Would you like something to eat, too?”

**JAKE:** “Sweet... please.”

**BAO:** “Of course.”

_She places a small treat on his plate._

**JAKE:** “Bao?”

**BAO:** “Yes, sweetheart?”

**JAKE:** “Found... me.”

_Bao goes quiet._

**BAO:** “Yes. I found you outside.”

**JAKE:** “Cold... long.”

**BAO:** “I know.”

**JAKE:** “Flame... weak.”

**BAO:** “I saw, Jake.”

_He looks down at his hands._

**JAKE:** “You... came.”

**BAO:** “I did. And I'm glad I found you.”

**JAKE:** “Thank... you.”

_Bao reaches toward him, then pauses to let him choose. Jake leans into her embrace._

**JAKE:** “Warm... now.”

**BAO:** “You're safe here.”

_She holds him gently while he drinks._

**Observation:** Jake can refer to the circumstances of his rescue, but this does not establish that he can comfortably recount the entire incident. Staff should not turn spontaneous remarks into extended questioning.

### V. FROST — THE FRIEND WHO WANTS TO HELP

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE lounge  
**Context:** Frost has brought Jake a blanket.

**FROST:** “Here, Jake. I thought you might be cold.”

**JAKE:** “Thank... you.”

**FROST:** “Do you want me to tuck it around you?”

**JAKE:** “Yes... please.”

_Frost arranges the blanket around him._

**FROST:** “Anything else you need?”

**JAKE:** “No.”

_She begins to stand._

**JAKE:** “Frost?”

**FROST:** “Hmm?”

**JAKE:** “Sit... with me?”

**FROST:** “Sure!”

_Frost sits beside him, leaving him room to move if he wants._

**FROST:** “Want to play something? We don't have to talk much.”

**JAKE:** “Game... good.”

**FROST:** “Cards?”

**JAKE:** “Cards.”

_Frost brings over a deck._

**FROST:** “I'll let you pick first.”

**JAKE:** “Cheat... no.”

**FROST:** “Excuse me! I would never cheat!”

_Jake looks at her._

**JAKE:** “You... would.”

**FROST:** “Okay, rude. Accurate, but rude.”

_Jake gives a small, breathy sound that might be a laugh._

**Observation:** Jake's humor remains intact. His limited speech does not prevent him from teasing familiar people, although conversational pacing must accommodate his difficulties.

### VI. NERI — THE QUIET CONVERSATION

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE reading area  
**Context:** Neri is working quietly when Jake approaches.

_Jake stands beside Neri's table. She looks up from her work._

**NERI:** “Jake? What is it?”

**JAKE:** “Neri... help?”

**NERI:** “With what?”

_Jake holds out a small object he has been struggling to manage._

**JAKE:** “This... stuck.”

_Neri examines it and helps him._

**NERI:** “There. Try now.”

_Jake tries again. It works._

**JAKE:** “Work... now.”

**NERI:** “Obviously. I fixed it.”

_Jake looks at her._

**JAKE:** “Thank... you.”

**NERI:** “You don't have to thank me.”

_A pause._

**JAKE:** “Neri... sad?”

**NERI:** “What? No.”

_Jake watches her quietly._

**JAKE:** “Face... looks.”

**NERI:** “My face always looks like this.”

**JAKE:** “Oh.”

_He considers that._

**JAKE:** “Sorry.”

**NERI:** “You didn't do anything wrong.”

_Jake sits beside her. Neri returns to her work, making no demand that he speak._

After a while:

**JAKE:** “Stay... here.”

**NERI:** “I'm working here anyway.”

**JAKE:** “Good.”

_Neri slides a small treat toward him without looking up._

**NERI:** “Eat that. You look tired.”

**JAKE:** “Thanks... Neri.”

**NERI:** “...You're welcome.”

**Observation:** Neri's direct manner appears compatible with Jake's current communication needs. Neither resident is required to fill every silence. Jake can initiate contact without having to explain why he wants company.

### VII. NORA — THE GENTLE CHECK-IN

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE garden room  
**Context:** Nora finds Jake sitting quietly by a window.

**NORA:** “Hi, Jake. May I sit with you?”

**JAKE:** “Yes.”

_Nora sits beside him._

**NORA:** “Are you watching the snow?”

**JAKE:** “No... clouds.”

**NORA:** “Oh! The clouds, then.”

_They watch in silence._

**JAKE:** “Nora?”

**NORA:** “I'm listening.”

**JAKE:** “Snow... pretty.”

**NORA:** “It is.”

**JAKE:** “Bad... too.”

_Nora turns toward him, careful not to rush her answer._

**NORA:** “It can be. Something can be beautiful and still hurt you.”

_Jake looks out the window._

**JAKE:** “Mm.”

**NORA:** “You don't have to explain more if you don't want to.”

**JAKE:** “No... words.”

**NORA:** “That's okay. We can just sit here.”

_Jake leans against the side of the chair. Nora remains beside him._

**JAKE:** “Stay... little.”

**NORA:** “For as long as you want some company.”

**JAKE:** “Thank... you.”

**Observation:** Nora does not attempt to reinterpret Jake's statement as a request to discuss the incident. She allows him to determine whether the conversation continues.

### VIII. CAMELIA — HIS OLDER SISTER

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE common room  
**Context:** A quiet afternoon after Camelia has reviewed her notebook.

**Note on identity:** “Camelia Candleheart” is a name she chose after encountering it in an anime. It is not a verified birth name. Camelia cannot remember her real name or her former life, and no evidence currently establishes either. Her chosen name is to be respected.

_Camelia sits with her notebook open on her lap. One page contains a reminder of her relationship with Jake._

_Jake approaches._

**CAMELIA:** “Jake?”

**JAKE:** “Camelia.”

_She looks up and smiles faintly._

**CAMELIA:** “That's me.”

**JAKE:** “Sister...”

_Camelia glances at the notebook._

**CAMELIA:** “Yes. I'm your older sister.”

**JAKE:** “Big... sister.”

**CAMELIA:** “Your big sister.”

_Jake sits beside her._

**CAMELIA:** “How are you feeling today?”

**JAKE:** “Tired.”

**CAMELIA:** “Do you want to rest?”

**JAKE:** “With... you?”

_Camelia's fingers tighten slightly around the notebook._

**CAMELIA:** “Of course.”

_She sets it aside and makes room for him._

**JAKE:** “You... remember?”

**CAMELIA:** “I remember you right now.”

_She hesitates, then continues honestly._

**CAMELIA:** “Sometimes my memories get mixed up. Sometimes I can't find them at all. But I can be here with you now.”

_Jake looks at her for a long moment._

**JAKE:** “My... sister.”

**CAMELIA:** “Yes, Jake. I'm your sister.”

**JAKE:** “Love... you.”

_Camelia's eyes fill with tears._

**CAMELIA:** “I love you too, little brother.”

_Jake leans against her. She wraps an arm around him, taking care not to disturb his wick._

**CAMELIA:** “I'm sorry.”

_Jake's expression changes. He takes time to answer._

**JAKE:** “Don't... cry.”

**CAMELIA:** “I'm trying.”

**JAKE:** “Here... now.”

**CAMELIA:** “What?”

**JAKE:** “You're... here.”

_Camelia lowers her head against his._

**CAMELIA:** “Yes. I'm here.”

_After a while, she opens her notebook again. She writes a short reminder in the space reserved for important personal information:_

_J A K E — MY LITTLE BROTHER._

_Below it, she adds:_

_If I forget, I can read this. I can ask for help. I can spend time with him again._

_Jake watches her write._

**JAKE:** “Good... words.”

**CAMELIA:** “I hope so.”

**JAKE:** “Good... sister.”

_Camelia stops writing._

_For a moment, she cannot bring herself to answer._

**CAMELIA:** “I'll keep trying, Jake.”

_He rests against her again._

**JAKE:** “Okay.”

**Observation:** Camelia's memory remains fragmented. This interaction does not indicate recovery or guarantee that she will retain the conversation. The familial relationship is nevertheless real and officially recognized. Jake's limited speech does not prevent him from identifying Camelia as his sister or expressing affection for her.

### IX. CROSS-INTERACTION FINDINGS

Across the eight interactions, Jake consistently demonstrates the ability to:

- Recognize familiar individuals and address them by name.
    
- Express basic needs, preferences, discomfort, and affection.
    
- Ask for company and reassurance.
    
- Participate in humor and ordinary social activities.
    
- Refer to events and relationships using brief verbal fragments.
    
- Stop speaking when communication becomes difficult.
    

His speech is severely limited, but his relationships have not become meaningless or inaccessible to him.

The people around him must learn to listen differently. They must also resist the temptation to turn every fragmented phrase into a medical test.

A question such as “Stay?” may carry considerable emotional meaning despite containing only one word. A simple “Good” may be the whole of Jake's available response, not evidence that he has nothing more to say.

Likewise, the people who care for Jake should not assume that they always understand him. When meaning is unclear, they should ask gently, offer reasonable interpretations, and give him the opportunity to correct them.

Camelia's case adds a further consideration: her memory may not preserve the conversation in which she learns what Jake means. Written reminders and reliable support remain necessary.

---

### X. FINAL C.O.R.E. OBSERVATION

Before the incident, Jake could tell people more.

He could form longer sentences. He could explain himself with less effort. He could offer Camelia an entire conversation about the snow instead of the few words he might manage now.

Those words are gone, and C.O.R.E. will not pretend that a handful of affectionate exchanges makes that loss acceptable.

But Jake still asks Clover to stay. He still reaches for Aries. He still recognizes Phoenix's care, Bao's kindness, Frost's teasing, Neri's quiet company, and Nora's patience.

He still knows Camelia.

And Camelia, whose chosen name comes from a story she once watched and whose real name and former life remain beyond her memory, is still his officially recognized older sister.

Their family cannot depend on perfect speech or perfect memory.

It must depend on patient listening, dependable care, and the willingness to meet each other where they are today.

**C.O.R.E. DIRECTIVE:** Jake's fragmented speech is to be treated as a valid means of communication. His words must not be dismissed because they are short, and no resident is to be required to speak more than they comfortably can.

**C.O.R.E. ADDENDUM:** Camelia Candleheart remains Jake Akana's officially recognized older sister, as authorized by Aries Akana. Her chosen name is valid for all resident-facing records. Her unknown birth name and unrecalled past life remain unverified.

_Some conversations are now only three words long. That does not make them any less worth having._
