### **POST-INCIDENT PERSONNEL OBSERVATION**

# **ORPHIE — THE NAME**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Bereavement  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / Lumi / Arcturus / Norma / SAFEHOUSE Residents  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **ISOLATED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

For months, the name **Orphie** had been treated as nothing more than a personal choice.

A unique name.

A piece of wordplay.

Orphie herself had once explained it simply:

> **"Orphie. Orphan."**

Nobody thought much more of it.

After Corin's death, the meaning became painfully literal.

---

# **THE NAME**

Orphie changed her name from **Corey** to **Orphie** because she liked the uniqueness of it.

She was not creating another identity.

She was not becoming another person.

She was still Corey.

Only the name changed.

At the time, the wordplay seemed almost harmless.

Now it feels different.

---

# **THE IRONY**

Orphie had always believed she was an orphan in everything except the literal sense.

She believed she did not belong.

She believed she was something left behind from another reality.

She believed she was a burden.

She believed everyone would eventually be better without her.

The name reflected how she saw herself.

But she still had a family.

She had:

- Lumi.
    
- Arcturus.
    
- Norma.
    
- Corin.
    
- The SAFEHOUSE.
    
- The Marts.
    
- People who cared about her.
    

She simply could not see it.

---

# **NOW**

Corin is gone.

The family has withdrawn.

The SAFEHOUSE has become distant.

The Marts avoid the treehouse.

Orphie remains alone.

The name she once chose as a piece of wordplay now describes her life far too accurately.

---

# **THE REALIZATION**

Orphie eventually sat at the table.

She looked at Corin's empty chair.

Then at the reflection in the window.

> "...Orphie."

She paused.

> "...Orphan."

For a long time, she said nothing.

Then:

> "...I actually did it."

She looked around the empty room.

> "...I became one."

---

# **LUMI'S NOTE**

Lumi eventually wrote something in her private journal:

> _"She called herself Orphie because she thought she was an orphan."_
> 
> _"I wish she had understood that names don't have to become prophecies."_

---

# **NORMA**

Norma understood the irony immediately.

> "...She named herself after what she thought she was."

A pause.

> "...And then spent months making it true."

---

# **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus refused to accept it.

> "...She's not an orphan."

Someone asked:

> "...Then why is she alone?"

He had no answer.

---

# **THE MARTS**

A small group of Marts eventually gathered near the forest boundary.

They did not approach the treehouse.

One Mart quietly said:

> "...Orphie sad."

Another answered:

> "...Orphie alone."

A third adjusted its tiny top hat.

> "...Orphan."

The others became silent.

---

# **THE FINAL IRONY**

Orphie never intended her name to predict her future.

It was supposed to be unique.

Maybe clever.

Maybe a little dark.

Instead, it became a description of the person she believed herself to be.

She wanted to be forgotten.

She wanted everyone to stop caring.

She pushed away the people who loved her.

And eventually, the world around her became quiet.

Too quiet.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie sits alone in the treehouse.

Corin's chair remains empty.

The family is elsewhere.

The Marts remain distant.

Nobody calls her Corey anymore.

Nobody calls her Orphie, either.

She is simply alone.

The name that once sounded like a joke now sounds like a diagnosis.

> **Orphie.**

> **Orphan.**

For the first time, the wordplay makes sense.

And that is exactly why nobody finds it funny anymore.

**End of Post-Incident Personnel Observation**
