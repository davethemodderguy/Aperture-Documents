# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Despite Jake's current physical frailty and recurring exhaustion-related fainting episodes, he retains access to a previously documented anomalous ability of his own designation.

The ability is known as:

# PULVERIZE

_Pulverize_ produces a wave of radiation that pulverizes living matter into fine blue dust.

The resulting effect is extraordinarily destructive to living targets. Affected biological matter is reduced to a fine, blue particulate residue rather than undergoing an ordinary injury process.

The mechanism remains incompletely understood.

C.O.R.E. emphasizes that **Jake's weakened physical condition has not been established to mean that he has lost Pulverize.**

His endurance and his anomalous capabilities must be treated as separate considerations.

A child who can faint from exhaustion may still possess an ability capable of destroying living matter.

These facts are not mutually exclusive.

They are, however, a significant reason to maintain appropriate safeguards.

---

# 1. CAPABILITY DESCRIPTION

**Designation:** Pulverize  
**Classification:** Anomalous radiative phenomenon  
**Primary effect:** Pulverization of living matter into fine blue dust  
**Known user:** Jake  
**Current status:** Retained

Pulverize is a distinct ability associated with Jake.

It is not a conventional weapon, an externally supplied device, or a technique developed by C.O.R.E.

It belongs to Jake himself.

When manifested, the ability produces a wave of radiation with an anomalous biological effect.

Living matter exposed to the effect is pulverized into fine blue dust.

The resulting material resembles the blue particulate associated with Jake's broader anomalous phenomena. However, C.O.R.E. has not established whether the two substances are chemically, physically, or metaphysically identical.

Personnel are instructed not to assume that the residue is harmless merely because it resembles dust.

Its composition and post-event properties remain under investigation.

---

# 2. DISTINCTION FROM ORDINARY RADIATION

The term _radiation_ describes the observed phenomenon but does not adequately explain its mechanism.

The effect does not correspond to any confirmed conventional radiological process known to C.O.R.E.

In particular, the defining outcome is not merely cellular damage, burns, or delayed biological injury.

The observed anomalous endpoint is the conversion of living matter into fine blue particulate residue.

C.O.R.E. therefore classifies Pulverize as an anomalous radiative capability rather than attempting to force it into an ordinary medical or radiological category.

No conclusions should be drawn about its wavelength, energy output, propagation characteristics, or effective range without supporting observations.

---

# 3. RELATIONSHIP TO JAKE'S CURRENT CONDITION

Jake's current medical condition is a source of concern.

He is physically frail.

He becomes exhausted relatively easily.

He occasionally loses consciousness when his stamina is depleted.

His human manifestation remains vulnerable despite his celestial nature.

None of these observations establishes that Pulverize has weakened.

Likewise, retaining Pulverize does not establish that Jake can use it indefinitely without consequence.

C.O.R.E. currently distinguishes three separate questions:

|Question|Current assessment|
|---|---|
|Does Jake retain Pulverize?|Yes|
|Has his physical frailty eliminated the ability?|No evidence of this|
|Is repeated or prolonged use safe for Jake?|Undetermined|

The final question is particularly important.

Personnel must not encourage Jake to demonstrate Pulverize merely to prove that he still possesses it.

He is not required to perform his abilities on demand.

---

# 4. WELFARE IMPLICATIONS

Jake's physical vulnerability creates a potentially misleading impression.

Residents who see him struggling to walk may assume that he is equally incapable of defending himself.

That assumption is incorrect.

However, the opposite assumption is equally inappropriate.

Jake's ability to pulverize living matter does not make him invulnerable, physically robust, or immune to exhaustion.

**Pulverize is not a substitute for Jake's physical health.**

He still requires rest, appropriate medical care, and assistance when he is unable to move safely.

The presence of a highly destructive capability does not remove his right to be treated as a child.

Nor does it justify placing him in situations where he is expected to defend himself.

C.O.R.E. explicitly rejects the idea that Jake should be regarded as a deterrent, an emergency weapon, or a substitute for SAFEHOUSE security personnel.

He is a resident.

His ability is a characteristic of that resident, not his purpose.

---

# 5. RESIDENT SAFETY CONSIDERATIONS

Given the nature of Pulverize, personnel should avoid unnecessary provocation, demonstrations, or uncontrolled testing.

The following principles apply:

- Do not deliberately provoke Jake to observe whether Pulverize activates.
    
- Do not ask him to use the ability against another resident.
    
- Do not treat the blue dust as ordinary environmental debris.
    
- Do not assume that anomalous residue has become safe simply because the visible effect has ended.
    
- Do not conduct experiments involving living subjects.
    
- Do not interfere with Jake's rest or medical care to investigate the ability.
    

Any suspected activation must be documented and assessed through established anomalous incident procedures.

The priority is to protect residents and provide Jake with appropriate support—not to obtain a more dramatic demonstration.

# 6. INCIDENT REVIEW — CAPABILITY RETENTION

During a routine discussion concerning Jake's current physical condition, a welfare officer raised the possibility that his recent exhaustion might have affected his abilities.

Neko rejected the assumption.

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"He's been fainting more often. Could that mean his abilities are deteriorating?"

**NEKO:**  
"Not necessarily."

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"But his physical condition has declined."

**NEKO:**  
"His physical condition has declined. That is an observation."

Neko paused.

**NEKO:**  
"Pulverize remains a separate capability. We have no evidence that it has disappeared."

**WELFARE OFFICER:**  
"So he's still dangerous?"

Neko's expression changed.

**NEKO:**  
"That is not the question you should be asking."

Silence.

**NEKO:**  
"The question is whether he is safe, whether the people around him are safe, and whether we can maintain both without treating a six-year-old child like a containment problem."

The officer did not respond immediately.

**NEKO:**  
"Document the ability. Maintain reasonable safeguards. And let the boy rest."

---

# 7. CURRENT CLASSIFICATION

C.O.R.E. retains Pulverize on Jake's active anomalous capability record.

No reclassification of Jake's overall status is recommended solely because of this ability.

His existing designation and welfare requirements remain unchanged.

The following statements are all accurate:

- Jake is physically frail.
    
- Jake can faint from exhaustion.
    
- Jake requires careful handling when medically compromised.
    
- Jake retains his teddy bear's sensory connection.
    
- Jake retains Pulverize.
    
- Pulverize can destroy living matter by reducing it to fine blue dust.
    

The apparent contradiction is not grounds to discard any of these findings.

Jake is not less vulnerable because he possesses destructive power.

And his destructive power does not make his vulnerability less important.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake can become exhausted walking back to his room.

He can stumble.

He can faint.

He can fall asleep halfway through reading a book.

He still carries his teddy bear everywhere, and the bear still matters to him more than most of the extraordinary things he can do.

He also possesses Pulverize.

A wave of radiation.

Living matter reduced to fine blue dust.

An ability that remains profoundly destructive despite the frailty of the child who possesses it.

C.O.R.E. will neither ignore that capability nor allow it to become the entirety of Jake's identity.

The appropriate response is vigilance without provocation, documentation without exploitation, and protection without unnecessary restriction.

Because the same resident who possesses Pulverize may still need someone to carry him home when his legs give out.

**Both facts matter.**
