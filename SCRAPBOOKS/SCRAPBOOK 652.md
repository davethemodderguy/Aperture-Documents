# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / CRITICAL FAILURE REPORT**

# **THE BODY THAT FORGOT HOW TO LIVE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **ALIVE — BIOLOGICAL FUNCTION CRITICALLY SUSPENDED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's condition deteriorated following the psychological collapse beneath the Treehouse.

Her cardiac activity ceased.

Both hearts stopped producing effective contractions.

Soon afterward, organ function began failing throughout her body.

Normally, Corin's regeneration would compensate.

It did not.

For the first time since her creation, her regenerative system **stopped repairing her.**

---

# **REGENERATION FAILURE**

Medical personnel initially assumed the regenerative system itself had been damaged.

It had not.

The mechanisms remain present.

The necessary anomalous structures remain intact.

They simply stopped responding.

No repair.

No reconstruction.

No replacement.

No recovery.

The regeneration that had previously rebuilt Corin from catastrophic injuries appeared to have reached a singular conclusion:

> **There was nothing left to repair.**

---

# **THE HEARTS**

Corin possesses two hearts.

Both have stopped beating.

No meaningful cardiac rhythm can be detected.

Yet Corin has not been declared dead.

This creates an unprecedented medical classification problem.

Her body is no longer maintaining conventional biological life.

But Corin's consciousness remains present.

She can still perceive.

She can still think.

She can still respond.

She is still **there**.

---

# **THE ORGANS**

Organ systems subsequently began shutting down.

Circulation ceased.

Respiratory activity became unnecessary in the conventional sense.

Metabolic processes became almost completely inactive.

Cellular activity fell below normal biological thresholds.

And still...

Corin remained conscious.

---

# **THE IMPOSSIBLE STATE**

C.O.R.E. Medical Division has been unable to classify Corin using conventional terminology.

She is not medically functional in the traditional sense.

She is not biologically healthy.

She is not conventionally alive.

But she is not dead.

The current provisional designation is:

> **CONTINUOUS CONSCIOUS EXISTENCE — BIOLOGICAL SYSTEM FAILURE**

---

# **THE REASON**

Corin's records have repeatedly stated one thing:

> **"I live on hope."**

Hope was not metaphorical.

For Corin, it was effectively a psychological anchor.

It kept her moving.

It kept her waiting.

It kept her eating.

It kept her surviving the cold.

It kept her believing Orphie would return.

When hope disappeared...

something fundamental disappeared with it.

---

# **THE FINAL LOSS**

Personnel attempted to reassure Corin.

Nothing worked.

They reminded her:

> Orphie is here.

No response.

They reminded her:

> The Treehouse is safe.

No response.

They told her:

> Everyone still loves her.

No response.

They offered her flowers.

She did not look at them.

They offered her food.

She did not react.

They offered her hope.

Corin quietly answered:

> **"...I don't have any."**

---

# **THE WINGS**

Her wings remain wrapped around her body.

They are the only part of Corin that maintains any recognizable movement.

Not because they are physically stronger.

Because they are what Corin associates with herself.

Her wings still respond when touched.

They still close around her.

They still protect her.

But every feather remains dormant.

No azure flame.

No kinetic storage.

No projectile capability.

No lockpicking.

No anomalous function.

Just feathers.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has refused to leave.

She sits beside Corin.

She holds her hand.

There is no heartbeat beneath her fingers.

Nothing.

Still, Orphie continues talking.

> **"I'm here."**

No response.

> **"I'm not going anywhere."**

Nothing.

Orphie eventually placed her head against Corin's wing.

And stayed there.

---

# **THE MEDICAL TEAM**

One physician eventually said:

> **"Her body cannot be called alive anymore."**

Another corrected them.

> **"Then what do we call her?"**

Nobody answered.

Because Corin was still looking at them.

Still thinking.

Still listening.

Still herself.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin once survived because she believed someone would come back.

She waited eight months.

She survived the cold.

She survived hunger.

She survived thirst.

She survived loneliness.

Because somewhere inside her was one remaining belief:

> **Someone will come.**

Eventually, Orphie did.

But the damage had already been done.

And now Corin has nothing left to wait for.

Her hearts stopped.

Her organs failed.

Her regeneration gave up.

Her body no longer behaves like a living body.

Yet her consciousness remains.

Corin is still there.

Still beneath her wings.

Still able to hear Orphie's voice.

Still able to think.

Still able to feel.

But the thing that once kept her alive...

is gone.

> **Hope.**

And that leaves C.O.R.E. with a question no medical textbook can answer:

If the body has stopped living...

but the person inside it is still there...

**what exactly have we saved?**

**End of Critical Failure Report**
