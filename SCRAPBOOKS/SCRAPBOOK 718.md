### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / RECOVERY & MOBILITY LOG**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — RETURN OF MOBILITY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** **RECOVERING / MOBILITY IMPROVING**

---

# **HEAD INJURY — RESOLVED**

Alice's head has fully healed.

The previously vulnerable area has stabilized, and no further bleeding has been observed during normal activity.

The medical bandages are no longer required.

Alice still wears her soft hat.

When asked why she keeps wearing it:

> **"I like it."**

No further explanation was required.

The hat remains part of Alice's usual appearance.

Her white hair continues to spill out from underneath it, with her rabbit ears poking through the top.

---

# **THE SCOOTER**

Something else has changed.

Alice has finally begun using the electric scooter Neko built for her.

Previously, the scooter spent most of its time unused.

Now Alice regularly uses it to travel around SAFEHOUSE.

She has learned the controls.

She knows how to stop.

She knows how to turn.

She even knows how to activate the automatic safety systems.

Neko is extremely pleased.

---

# **NEKO'S REACTION**

Neko initially assumed Alice would only use the scooter occasionally.

Instead, he eventually noticed her riding it through the SAFEHOUSE corridor.

He stopped.

Looked at the scooter.

Looked at Alice.

> **"...You're using it."**

Alice nodded.

> **"Yeah."**

Neko smiled.

> **"Good."**

Alice tilted her head.

> **"You made it."**

> **"Yeah."**

> **"Thank you."**

Neko looked away.

> **"...You're welcome."**

He later spent approximately three hours making unnecessary improvements to an already perfectly functional scooter.

Nobody questioned it.

---

# **THE PERIMETER**

A new modification has been made to the scooter's authorized operating area.

The SAFEHOUSE mobility perimeter has been expanded.

Previously, Alice could only travel within the primary SAFEHOUSE area.

The new boundary includes:

> **The Treehouse.**

Alice can now safely travel between SAFEHOUSE and the Treehouse without requiring someone to carry her.

The boundary remains hard-locked.

The scooter still cannot leave the Blacksite.

It simply gives Alice access to a place that has become particularly important to her.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

The Treehouse is where Corin originally found Alice.

It is also where Alice spent much of her early recovery.

The location carries considerable emotional significance.

Alice now occasionally rides there herself.

She parks the scooter nearby.

Sometimes she sits outside.

Sometimes she goes inside.

Sometimes she simply watches the snow.

The important difference is:

**She chose to go there.**

She wasn't trying to escape.

She was simply going somewhere.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin noticed the difference immediately.

One afternoon, Alice returned from the Treehouse on her scooter.

Corin asked:

> **"Where were you?"**

Alice answered:

> **"Treehouse."**

> **"Why?"**

Alice thought for a moment.

> **"Wanted to go."**

Corin smiled.

That answer mattered more than she let on.

---

# **MOBILITY ASSESSMENT**

Alice remains physically fragile.

She still cannot walk for long distances.

Her stamina remains extremely poor.

Her immune system remains compromised.

Her right eye remains permanently blind.

Her body remains vulnerable to injury.

The scooter does not mean Alice has recovered completely.

It means she has found a way to move **without demanding that her body do something it cannot safely do.**

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

For months, Alice couldn't go anywhere without someone carrying her.

Then Neko built her a scooter.

She barely touched it.

Now she rides it.

She rides through SAFEHOUSE.

She visits the Treehouse.

She returns.

She doesn't use it to escape.

She uses it because she wants to go somewhere.

The perimeter was expanded not because Alice needed more room to run.

It was expanded because Alice finally started wanting to **go places.**

Her head has healed.

Her hat remains.

Her right eye will never recover.

Her body is still fragile.

But something important has changed.

Alice is moving again.

Not because someone told her to.

Because she wants to.
