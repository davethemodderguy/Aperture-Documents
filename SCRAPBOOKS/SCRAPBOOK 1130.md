When asked why the production quantity had increased from one to five, Neri gave a surprisingly straightforward answer.

> “I liked it.”

The officer conducting the interview waited.

> **OFFICER:**  
> “So you made four more?”

> **OFFICER:**  
> “For other people?”

Neri nodded again.

> **OFFICER:**  
> “Why?”

Neri considered the question.

> **NERI:**  
> “Limited.”

# 4. LIMITED MERCHANDISE

Following the Night Stalker program, personnel have become familiar with Neri's preferred method of distributing experimental armaments.

Neri does not appear interested in mass production.

Instead, she creates extremely small quantities and distributes them to individuals she considers deserving.

The Night Stalker established the precedent.

Sleight of Hand appears to be following it.

C.O.R.E. therefore classifies the five current units as:

> **LIMITED MERCHANDISE**

Neri has not announced whether five is the final quantity.

Personnel have been advised not to ask.

---

# 5. SHOOTING RANGE DEMONSTRATION

Neri personally demonstrated Sleight of Hand at the Blacksite shooting range.

She arrived carrying her personal unit:

> **SOH-001**

Several MSAC personnel were already present.

Neko was also present.

Jane Doe arrived shortly afterward.

Neri placed the PDW on the firing bench.

She adjusted her glasses.

Then looked at the observers.

> **NERI:**  
> “Watch.”

---

# 7. FIRING DEMONSTRATION

Neri conducted the demonstration under controlled range procedures.

The first thing observers noticed was the sound.

Or rather:

**The lack of it.**

The firing signature was heavily suppressed.

The mechanical operation remained audible, but subdued.

Several operators exchanged looks.

One quietly said:

> **“That's really quiet.”**

Another replied:

> **“That's Neri.”**

---

# 8. MECHANICAL CHARACTER

Neri then demonstrated the weapon's manual operation.

The action produced the same characteristic smooth mechanical quality found in the Night Stalker family.

However, the larger platform gave Sleight of Hand a somewhat different sound.

Not louder.

Different.

A controlled sequence of mechanical clicks and movement followed.

One MSAC operator immediately said:

> **“Okay, that's satisfying.”**

Jane nodded.

> **JANE:**  
> “She did it again.”

Neko sighed.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Of course she did.”

---

# 9. NERI'S SHOOTING

Neri proceeded through the demonstration calmly.

No dramatic presentation.

No showmanship.

She simply fired.

Reloaded.

Fired again.

Then lowered the weapon.

The range remained quiet for a moment.

Neri looked toward the observers.

> **NERI:**  
> “Questions?”

Neri looked mildly concerned.
