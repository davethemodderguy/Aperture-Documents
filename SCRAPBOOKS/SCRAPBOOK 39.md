# **SUMMARY**

Following discussions between the Krampus Compliance Authority and Aperture Industries, **Senior Manager Norma Lockwood** has officially transferred her primary residence to **Heaven's Blacksite**.

The decision was made to reduce travel between KCA Headquarters and the Blacksite, allowing Norma to work more closely with Belle, the CCD, and Project SAFEHOUSE.

Her application was unanimously approved.

---

# **ROOM ASSIGNMENT**

After reviewing Room N-07's fixed layout, one sleeping space remained available.

The **upper bunk directly above Belle and Endmin's bed**.

Norma accepted the assignment without hesitation.

> "Works for me."

# **FIRST NIGHT**

Norma climbed onto her new bunk, set her hoodie beside the pillow, and looked around.

From below came Belle's cheerful voice.

> "Goodnight, Norma!"

Norma smiled over the edge of the bunk.

> "Goodnight."

Endmin simply nodded.

> "Sleep well."

The room lights dimmed.

Within minutes, the familiar sounds of SAFEHOUSE settling in for the night filled the room.
