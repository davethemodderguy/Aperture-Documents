### **PHENOMENON OBSERVATION UPDATE**

# **LUNA — LUNAR ATTRACTION EFFECT**

**Classification:** Level 4 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corey / Luna  
**Related Entity:** Z-335 Marts

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has documented a recurring interaction between **Luna** and the Z-335 Mart colony.

When Corey manifests as Luna, Marts display a significantly increased tendency to gather around her.

This behavior is not caused by fear, command influence, or direct communication.

The primary cause appears to be Luna's unique lunar energy field.

---

# **LUNAR GRAVITATIONAL FIELD**

Luna generates a localized gravitational effect.

Unlike conventional gravity, the effect does not appear to significantly affect normal personnel or objects.

Instead, it primarily interacts with:

- Z-335 Marts.
    
- Lunar energy-sensitive materials.
    
- Certain lightweight objects.
    

The effect is gentle and non-harmful.

Common observations include:

- Marts slowly drifting toward Luna.
    
- Marts floating closer while sleeping.
    
- Marts remaining near her for extended periods.
    
- Small objects subtly shifting toward her location.
    

---

# **MART ATTRACTION**

The Marts appear naturally drawn to Luna.

Researchers initially believed this was simply because Luna was warm and calming.

Further testing revealed that the attraction continues even when temperature differences are minimized.

The current conclusion:

> **Marts are attracted to Luna's lunar field.**

---

# **COMMON BEHAVIOR**

When Luna is present, Marts commonly:

- Gather beneath her lunar wings.
    
- Rest near her.
    
- Follow her movement.
    
- Float alongside her.
    
- Sleep within her gravitational field.
    

A typical scene involves Luna peacefully sitting somewhere while dozens of Marts slowly gather around her.

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

When asked why they follow Luna, Marts provided several answers.

Examples:

> "...Moon."

> "...Soft."

> "...Quiet."

> "...Nice gravity."

The Ambassador was asked to clarify.

After observing Luna for several minutes, he replied:

> "They like being near her."

---

# **LUNA'S REACTION**

Luna is aware of the effect.

However, she does not appear bothered by it.

When asked whether she could reduce the attraction, she answered:

> "...Probably."

After a pause:

> "...But they like it."

The nearby Marts immediately confirmed this.

> "...Like Luna."

---

# **SLEEPING OBSERVATIONS**

The effect is strongest during nighttime.

When Corey sleeps without headwear and naturally becomes Luna:

- Her lunar field remains active.
    
- She continues to float.
    
- Marts often gather nearby.
    
- Some Marts drift alongside her.
    

This has resulted in several instances where personnel discovered Corey peacefully sleeping while surrounded by a floating group of Marts.

---

After several tests, he concluded:

> "...Not dangerous."

---

# **COREY'S RESPONSE**

When informed that her Luna form attracts Marts through a gravitational effect, Corey was surprised.

> "...I do?"

A Mart floating nearby answered:

> "...Yes."

Corey looked at the group surrounding her.

> "...Oh."

After a short pause:

> "...Okay."

She did not request the effect to be removed.

---

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

Lumi considers the phenomenon natural.

> "...Marts were created to seek connection."

She looked toward Luna surrounded by the colony.

> "...They found it."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Luna gravitational field is classified as:

**Passive / Non-hostile / Beneficial**

No negative effects have been recorded.

The Marts' attraction appears to be a natural response to Luna's lunar energy.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

During a quiet night at the Blacksite, Luna was found floating outside beneath the moon.

Her azure wings illuminated the area.

Slowly, one Mart drifted toward her.

Then another.

Then dozens.

Within minutes, Luna was surrounded by a peaceful cloud of blue Marts.

She looked around.

A Mart rested beside her shoulder.

> "...Many."

Luna smiled.

> "...Yes."

The Mart looked at the moon.

Then at Luna.

> "...Same."

Luna remained silent for a moment.

Then answered:

> "...Maybe."
