**SUBJECT:** Nora Tachyon / Invictus Aeterna  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — AUTHORIZED OUTDOOR RECREATION AREA

---

# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon was participating in outdoor recreational activity when an incoming missile entered the vicinity of the SAFEHOUSE.

The projectile came sufficiently close to Nora that personnel initially assessed a direct impact as possible.

Nora did not appear to recognize the danger immediately.

Aeterna did.

Within moments, Aeterna transitioned from Sky form into her armored manifestation and physically positioned herself between Nora and the incoming projectile.

She then covered Nora completely with her armored body and cape.

The resulting impact caused **minimal damage to Aeterna.**

Nora was unharmed.

---

# 2. INITIAL OBSERVATION

Nora had been outside for approximately twenty minutes.

Sky was nearby.

Nora was reportedly occupied with:

- playing;
    
- talking to Sky;
    
- examining something she had found on the ground;
    
- and attempting to convince Sky to carry her despite already having walked only a short distance.
    

Nothing unusual was initially observed.

Then:

**AIRBORNE OBJECT DETECTED.**

Security systems identified the object as a missile.

Estimated trajectory intersected the recreational area.

Security personnel began moving toward the area.

They did not arrive first.

Aeterna did.

---

# 3. AETERNA RESPONSE

Sky immediately moved toward Nora.

White light appeared.

The falcon disappeared.

Invictus Aeterna manifested.

The transition occurred in less than a second.

Nora looked up.

**NORA:**  
“Aeterna?”

Aeterna did not answer.

She moved directly toward Nora.

She placed herself between Nora and the incoming projectile.

Her cape expanded around Nora.

Aeterna then lowered herself over the child.

Her armored body completely shielded Nora.

The cape covered the remaining exposed area.

For several seconds:

> **Nora was completely underneath Aeterna.**

---

# 4. IMPACT

The projectile impacted Aeterna rather than Nora.

The exact nature of the missile remains under investigation.

The impact produced:

- significant noise;
    
- localized debris;
    
- temporary dust displacement;
    
- superficial damage to several exterior armor plates;
    
- minor deformation along one shoulder plate;
    
- superficial scoring across the cape;
    
- and no significant penetration.
    

Aeterna remained standing.

Nora remained underneath her.

No serious injury was detected in either individual.

---

# 5. POST-IMPACT

The first person to speak was Nora.

**NORA:**  
“Are you okay?”

Aeterna looked down.

**AETERNA:**  
“Yes.”

Nora looked at the damaged armor.

**NORA:**  
“Does it hurt?”

Aeterna paused.

**AETERNA:**  
“No.”

Nora touched one of the damaged plates.

Aeterna did not react.

**NORA:**  
“It's dirty.”

Aeterna looked at the armor.

**AETERNA:**  
“Yes.”

Nora nodded.

---

# 6. THE CLOTH

Approximately twenty minutes later, personnel observed Nora sitting beside Aeterna.

Nora had obtained a fabric cleaning cloth.

She was carefully wiping the damaged armor plates.

Aeterna remained completely motionless.

Nora wiped one section.

Then another.

Then stopped.

She inspected the cloth.

It was dirty.

Nora looked at Aeterna.

> **NORA:**  
> “You got really dirty.”

Aeterna looked down at her.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Yes.”

Nora resumed cleaning.

---

**SECOND OPERATOR:**

---

# 8. AETERNA'S RESPONSE

Nora eventually reached a damaged shoulder plate.

She stopped.

**NORA:**  
“This one is scratched.”

**AETERNA:**  
“Yes.”

**NORA:**  
“Can you fix it?”

**AETERNA:**  
“Yes.”

Nora nodded.

She continued cleaning.

After several seconds:

**NORA:**  
“Thank you.”

Aeterna remained silent.

Nora continued.

---

# 9. DAMAGE ASSESSMENT

Aeterna's condition was assessed following the incident.

### Armor:

- Minor surface scoring.
    
- Several superficial scratches.
    
- One plate showed minor deformation.
    
- Cape sustained localized superficial damage.
    
- No structural failure observed.
    
- No anomalous instability detected.
    
- No reduction in mobility.
    
- No reduction in transformation capability.
    

### Spirit:

No detectable destabilization.

No loss of consciousness.

No disruption to cognitive function.

No reduction in anomalous capability.

### Nora:

No physical injuries identified.

No loss of consciousness.

No significant psychological distress observed immediately following the incident.

Nora remained more concerned about Aeterna's armor than her own safety.

---

# 10. AETERNA'S PRIORITY

Security footage was reviewed afterward.

The footage showed that Aeterna did not hesitate.

She did not attempt to move Nora away first.

She did not attempt to evade.

She did not attempt to intercept the missile at range.

She simply placed herself between Nora and the projectile.

Then she covered her.

This behavior is consistent with Aeterna's previously documented protective hierarchy.

When asked why she did not simply carry Nora away:

Aeterna answered:

> **“There was no time.”**

When asked whether she considered the impact dangerous to herself:

> **“Yes.”**

When asked whether she would do it again:

Aeterna answered immediately:

> **“Yes.”**

---

# 11. NORA'S RESPONSE

Nora was later asked why she was cleaning Aeterna's armor.

Her answer was simple.

> **“She protected me.”**

The interviewer asked:

> “So you're helping her?”

Nora nodded.

> **“Yeah.”**

Pause.

> **“She's my Aeterna.”**

She then continued cleaning the armor.

---

# 12. FINAL OBSERVATION

Aeterna possesses sufficient anomalous durability to survive the incident with minimal damage.

That fact should not obscure the significance of the event.

Aeterna chose to take the impact herself.

She did so without hesitation.

Nora, in turn, did not treat Aeterna as an indestructible guardian.

She saw damaged armor.

She got a cloth.

And she cleaned it.

The interaction lasted approximately thirty-seven minutes.

At the conclusion, Nora inspected the armor one final time.

She nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “Better.”

Aeterna looked down at her.

> **AETERNA:**  
> “Thank you.”

Nora smiled.

> **NORA:**  
> “You're welcome.”

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The missile failed to seriously damage a 15,000-year-old spirit.

The missile also failed to hurt a six-year-old Shark Thiren.

The more interesting part of the incident occurred afterward.

Aeterna protected Nora with her body.

Nora protected Aeterna's armor with a cleaning cloth.

Neither was asked to do it.

Neither was compelled.

They simply did what they apparently considered obvious.

**Aeterna protects Nora.**

**Nora takes care of Aeterna.**

For the record, maintenance has requested that personnel stop asking whether Aeterna's armor can be cleaned with standard Blacksite supplies.

Nora has already decided:

> **“Fabric cloth.”**

Apparently, that is the approved procedure now.
