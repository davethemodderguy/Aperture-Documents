### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / FEATHER CONSUMPTION REPORT**

# **CORIN — EDIBLE FEATHERS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **Edible Dormant Feathers**  
**Status:** Confirmed / Safe Under Specific Conditions

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has confirmed an unexpected property of Corin's feathers:

> **They are edible.**

However, this is only considered safe when the feather has been properly **disarmed and is completely dormant**.

An active or improperly prepared feather remains an anomalous projectile and must not be consumed.

---

# **TASTE PROFILE**

Dormant feathers have an unexpectedly distinctive flavor.

Testing and Belle's accidental taste test indicate a combination resembling:

- **Sour apple**
    
- **Sugar**
    
- A small amount of **coffee-like bitterness**
    

The overall flavor has been described as:

> **Sweet and sour with a faint bitter aftertaste.**

Corin was not previously aware that her feathers tasted like anything.

---

# **BELLE'S ACCIDENT**

The discovery occurred when Belle accidentally ate one of Corin's feathers.

A dormant feather had been shed and was lying among several food ingredients.

Belle mistook it for something edible.

She took a bite.

There was a brief pause.

Belle looked at the feather.

Then at Corin.

> **"...Why does your feather taste like an apple?"**

Corin was equally confused.

> **"...You ate my feather?"**

Belle nodded.

> **"It's actually good."**

---

# **SAFETY CONDITION**

The feather must be:

**Disarmed**

and

**Dormant**

before consumption.

A dormant feather does not exhibit its projectile functions.

This means it will not:

- Produce an Ether airburst.
    
- Trigger the flash effect.
    
- Function as a projectile weapon.
    
- Generate an explosive reaction.
    

The exact method Corin uses to ensure a feather is fully dormant remains an Ether-related biological process.

---

# **PHYSIOLOGICAL RESPONSE**

Belle experienced no adverse effects after consuming the feather.

No Ether poisoning occurred.

No digestive abnormalities were observed.

The feather appeared to break down normally.

C.O.R.E. therefore considers properly dormant feathers **biologically edible**.

---

# **IMPORTANT WARNING**

Personnel are strictly prohibited from eating randomly discarded feathers.

Corin sheds feathers naturally, and their anomalous state can vary.

A feather that appears harmless may still possess sufficient Ether activity to become a projectile under the correct conditions.

Only feathers confirmed by Corin to be:

> **Disarmed and dormant**

may be considered safe for consumption.
