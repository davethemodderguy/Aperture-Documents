---

### I. EVENT SUMMARY

Following their visit to Jake Akana's grave in the Starlands, Jake brought his older sister, Camelia Candleheart, to view a small collection of photographs taken before his death.

The photographs depict Jake as he appeared while alive: a young Cat Thiren with cat ears, a tail, and beautiful eyes. Several photographs show him with Phoenix, Clover, and Bao.

Camelia had previously learned that Jake was murdered at six years old. However, she had never seen what he looked like before his death.

Until this event, her understanding of her little brother had been shaped almost entirely by his current ghostly appearance: the melted half of his head, the blue flame burning from his wick, the absence of visible eyes and ears, and the tail that remained from his former Cat Thiren body.

The photographs provided a different perspective.

They showed Jake not as a ghost, not as a victim, and not as a child whose speech had been permanently damaged.

They showed him alive.

The event was initiated by Jake himself. Despite his severe speech impairment, he wanted Camelia to see the photographs.

---

### II. EVENT LOG

**14:07 — Arrival at the personal archive room**

Jake led Camelia into the room where a small collection of personal photographs was being kept.

Camelia carried her notebook, as usual. She initially appeared to believe they were going to look at ordinary SAFEHOUSE records.

**CAMELIA:** “What are we looking for?”

**JAKE:** “Pictures.”

**CAMELIA:** “Pictures of what?”

_Jake points toward the collection._

**JAKE:** “Me... before.”

_Camelia looks at him._

**CAMELIA:** “Before you died?”

**JAKE:** “Mm.”

_She pauses before following him to the table._

**14:10 — First photograph: Jake alone**

The first photograph showed Jake before his death.

His cat ears were visible. His tail appeared behind him, and his eyes were clear and expressive. His face had none of the melted features that defined his current ghost form.

Camelia studied the photograph for several seconds.

**CAMELIA:** “That's you?”

**JAKE:** “Me.”

**CAMELIA:** “You look so different.”

**JAKE:** “Alive.”

_Camelia falls silent._

**CAMELIA:** “Your eyes...”

**JAKE:** “Pretty?”

**CAMELIA:** “Very pretty.”

_Jake remains beside her._

**CAMELIA:** “And your ears! You really had them.”

**JAKE:** “Cat... ears.”

**CAMELIA:** “I know. I just never saw them before.”

_She looks from the photograph to his current form._

**CAMELIA:** “I wish I could have met you like this.”

_Jake takes a moment to respond._

**JAKE:** “Me too.”

_The words are short, but Camelia does not ask him to elaborate._

---

**14:16 — Second photograph: Jake and Phoenix**

The next photograph showed Jake with Phoenix.

Jake appeared comfortable beside his caretaker. The image captured an ordinary moment from before his death, without any indication of the tragedy that would follow.

Camelia recognized Phoenix.

**CAMELIA:** “Phoenix was with you back then, too?”

**JAKE:** “Yes.”

**CAMELIA:** “You look happy.”

**JAKE:** “Was... happy.”

_Camelia looks at the photograph again._

**CAMELIA:** “Did Phoenix take care of you?”

**JAKE:** “Phoenix... cared.”

**CAMELIA:** “They still do.”

**JAKE:** “Know.”

_Jake touches the edge of the photograph carefully._

**CAMELIA:** “Do you miss being like this?”

_Jake does not answer immediately._

**JAKE:** “Sometimes.”

**CAMELIA:** “I'm sorry.”

**JAKE:** “Not... your fault.”

_Camelia nods, though the expression on her face suggests that the answer does not make the sadness disappear._

---

**14:23 — Third photograph: Jake and Clover**

The third photograph showed Jake with Clover.

The image captured the two of them together before Jake's death, preserving a moment from a life that continued to matter to the people who remembered him.

Camelia leaned closer to examine it.

**CAMELIA:** “Clover looks so happy here.”

**JAKE:** “Clover... happy.”

**CAMELIA:** “Were you two already close?”

**JAKE:** “Friends.”

**CAMELIA:** “You're still friends.”

**JAKE:** “Mm.”

_Camelia looks at Jake's ghostly cat form, then back at the photograph._

**CAMELIA:** “You had such a normal little life.”

_Jake becomes still._

**CAMELIA:** “Sorry. I didn't mean—”

**JAKE:** “Normal... good.”

_Camelia looks at him._

**JAKE:** “Wanted... more.”

_No further explanation is provided._

_Camelia does not ask him to continue._

---

**14:31 — Fourth photograph: Jake and Bao**

The final photograph showed Jake with Bao.

Camelia recognized Bao immediately. She had seen her care for Jake, and she knew Bao had been the one to find him outside after five weeks in the cold.

The photograph showed an earlier time, when Jake was alive and his current injuries had not yet occurred.

**CAMELIA:** “Bao knew you before?”

**JAKE:** “Yes.”

**CAMELIA:** “She must have been so happy to have you around.”

**JAKE:** “Bao... kind.”

**CAMELIA:** “She still is.”

**JAKE:** “I know.”

_Camelia looks at the photograph for a long time._

**CAMELIA:** “She found you in the snow, didn't she?”

**JAKE:** “Mm.”

**CAMELIA:** “And she saw you like this.”

_She gestures gently toward his current form._

**JAKE:** “Flame... weak.”

**CAMELIA:** “I'm glad she found you.”

**JAKE:** “Me too.”

_Camelia sets the photograph down carefully._

**CAMELIA:** “I wish she hadn't had to find you that way.”

_Jake gives no verbal response. He remains beside her._

---

**14:39 — Pause in the viewing**

After examining all four photographs, Camelia sat quietly.

She had now seen several versions of Jake's past: alone, with Phoenix, with Clover, and with Bao.

The images did not provide a complete account of his life. They did not identify his murderer or explain the circumstances of his death. They did not tell Camelia everything she had missed.

They showed only a few moments.

But those moments were enough to change how she understood the photographs' subject.

**CAMELIA:** “Jake?”

**JAKE:** “Hmm?”

**CAMELIA:** “You were really here.”

**JAKE:** “Yes.”

**CAMELIA:** “I mean... before all this. You had a life. People who loved you. You had your own memories.”

**JAKE:** “Mm.”

**CAMELIA:** “And someone took that away.”

_Jake lowers his head._

**JAKE:** “Murdered.”

_Camelia's hands close around her notebook._

**CAMELIA:** “I know.”

_She takes a moment before speaking again._

**CAMELIA:** “I don't remember my old life. I don't even know my real name. Sometimes I wonder what kind of person I was.”

_Jake looks toward her._

**JAKE:** “You're... Camelia.”

**CAMELIA:** “That's the name I chose.”

**JAKE:** “My... sister.”

_Camelia's expression softens._

**CAMELIA:** “Yes. I'm your sister.”

_She moves her chair closer._

**CAMELIA:** “I can't remember who I used to be. You can't go back to who you were. I suppose neither of us can fix that for the other.”

**JAKE:** “No.”

**CAMELIA:** “But I can know you now.”

**JAKE:** “Yes.”

**CAMELIA:** “And I want to.”

_Jake leans against her._

**JAKE:** “Good.”

---

**14:47 — Request to retain the photographs**

Camelia asked whether she could record the details of the photographs in her notebook.

Jake agreed.

She did not attempt to reproduce every detail. Instead, she wrote down the people in the photographs and the fact that the images showed Jake before his death.

She then added a separate note:

_Jake looked different when he was alive. He had cat ears and beautiful eyes. His tail was already part of him. The photographs show that he had a life before he became a ghost._

_Phoenix cared about him. Clover was his friend. Bao knew him, too._

_These are photographs of my little brother._

_If I forget what he looked like, I can ask to see them again._

_I must not assume that forgetting something means it was never important._

**14:52 — Departure**

Jake and Camelia left the archive room together.

The photographs remained in their original collection. No images were removed from the archive during the event.

### III. POST-EVENT ASSESSMENT

The viewing had no immediate medical objective. It was a personal interaction initiated by Jake and supported by SAFEHOUSE welfare staff.

Three observations are relevant.

**First: Jake retains an interest in his former life.**

His willingness to show Camelia these photographs demonstrates that he is prepared, at least in some circumstances, to share selected aspects of his life before death. This must not be interpreted as permission to question him about every detail of that life or the murder that ended it.

**Second: Camelia's fragmented memory requires careful support.**

The notebook allows her to preserve information she may later be unable to retrieve. Photographs can also provide accessible records of people and events, but they cannot restore memories she never possessed or guarantee that she will retain the emotional context of what she learns.

**Third: the photographs preserve Jake's personhood.**

They establish that his current ghostly appearance is not the only way he has existed. Before his death, he was a living child with recognizable feline features, personal relationships, and ordinary experiences.

The photographs should remain available to Jake as part of his personal history. Access should be controlled according to his wishes and ordinary privacy safeguards.

No personnel are to use the images to pressure him into discussing his death.

---

### IV. C.O.R.E. CLOSING OBSERVATION

Camelia had known Jake as a ghost.

She had seen his melted head, his blue flame, and his missing eyes. She had learned that his speech was permanently damaged. She had visited his grave with him and discovered that he had been murdered when he was six years old.

Now she had seen the photographs.

In one, he was with Phoenix.

In another, he was with Clover.

In another, he was with Bao.

And in the first, he was simply Jake: a little Cat Thiren with beautiful eyes, cat ears, and a tail.

The photographs did not make his present form any less real. They did not make him less her brother. They did not undo the violence that had taken his life.

They reminded Camelia that there had been a child before the ghost.

A child who had been loved.

A child who had friends.

A child who had wanted more time.

And now, that child was sitting beside her, speaking only in short bursts, trusting her enough to show her something personal.

Camelia could not recover her own past. Jake could not return to his.

But he could show her the photographs.

And she could look at them.

For two children whose histories had been taken from them in different ways, that small act was worth preserving.

**FINAL NOTE:**

_The photographs are not evidence of the life Jake should have lost. They are evidence of the life he actually lived._

_C.O.R.E. will preserve them accordingly._
