# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake can speak again.

Almost nobody knows.

Following the first vocalization after approximately five weeks of silence, only **two SAFEHOUSE residents** have directly confirmed that Jake's voice has returned:

- **Anby**
    
- **Twiggy**
    

Everyone else currently believes that Jake remains unable to speak.

This is not because Jake is deliberately deceiving the SAFEHOUSE.

He simply has not told anyone.

And nobody has asked him to.

---

# 1. CURRENT UNDERSTANDING

The general SAFEHOUSE assumption remains:

> **Jake cannot speak because his vocal cords were damaged during the five-week blizzard.**

This assumption is understandable.

Jake continues to communicate primarily through:

- nodding;
    
- shaking his head;
    
- pointing;
    
- facial expressions;
    
- gestures;
    
- writing;
    
- reaching for someone;
    
- tapping his teddy bear;
    
- and other nonverbal methods.
    

He has not suddenly become conversational.

His voice remains extremely weak.

Even when speaking to Anby, his voice is barely above a whisper.

Therefore, from everyone else's perspective:

**Nothing has changed.**

---

# 2. ANBY KNOWS

Anby was the first person Jake spoke to.

She heard:

> **"Anby."**

followed shortly afterward by:

> **"...missed you."**

Anby immediately understood that his voice was returning.

She did not tell the other residents.

When asked whether Jake had spoken yet, Anby has simply responded with variations of:

> "Not yet."

She is not lying about his overall condition.

Jake genuinely cannot sustain normal conversation.

Anby considers his few words something that belongs to **Jake**, rather than something the SAFEHOUSE is entitled to hear.

---

# 3. TWIGGY KNOWS

Twiggy became aware shortly afterward due to her involvement in Jake's medical assessment.

She heard the vocalization during the post-event evaluation.

Twiggy confirmed:

- Jake can produce sound;
    
- his vocal cords are functioning to some degree;
    
- speaking causes noticeable fatigue;
    
- his voice remains extremely weak;
    
- continued recovery is expected but uncertain.
    

Twiggy has therefore maintained the same medical recommendation:

> **Do not force vocal rehabilitation before Jake is ready.**

She has also kept the information restricted.

# 5. WHY JAKE HAS NOT TOLD THEM

No formal explanation has been obtained.

Jake was asked nonverbally whether he wanted Anby to tell anyone.

He shook his head.

Anby respected the decision.

The most likely explanation is simply that Jake does not want attention placed on his voice yet.

His first attempt at speaking was physically exhausting.

He may therefore be treating his voice as something private.

There is no requirement for him to announce his recovery.

---

# 6. INCIDENT — NERI

Neri recently handed Jake one of her physics books.

Jake communicated several complicated answers to her through writing.

At no point did he speak.

Neri therefore remains unaware that Jake can currently produce vocalizations.

She has no reason to suspect otherwise.

This is particularly notable because Neri is one of the residents most accustomed to communicating without speech.

Jake's silence therefore does not appear unusual to her.

---

# 7. INCIDENT — BAO

Bao remains Jake's primary source of medical and maternal care.

She frequently asks simple questions.

> **BAO:**  
> "Cold?"

Jake shakes his head.

> **BAO:**  
> "Hungry?"

Jake nods.

> **BAO:**  
> "Tired?"

Jake nods.

The interaction ends there.

Bao has not heard his voice.

She therefore continues assuming that verbal communication is still unavailable to him.

Jake has not corrected her.

---

# 8. INCIDENT — NORA

Nora frequently talks to Jake.

She does most of the talking.

Jake listens.

Sometimes she tells him stories.

Sometimes she complains about something.

Sometimes she simply sits beside him and talks about whatever comes to mind.

Jake occasionally smiles.

He occasionally gestures for her to continue.

Nora has apparently interpreted this as Jake enjoying listening to her.

She is correct.

She simply does not know that he could now answer.

---

# 9. INCIDENT — AETERNA

Aeterna has also not heard Jake speak since his return.

During one visit, she asked:

> **AETERNA:**  
> "How are you feeling?"

Jake gave a thumbs-up.

Aeterna nodded.

> **AETERNA:**  
> "Good."

She sat beside him.

Neither spoke.

Aeterna has not questioned the absence of his voice.

---

# 10. THE ANBY PROTOCOL

Anby has developed an unofficial rule.

If Jake speaks:

**She listens.**

If Jake stops:

**She does not ask him to continue.**

If someone else is present:

**She does not announce that he can speak.**

If Jake wants someone to know:

**He can tell them himself.**

Twiggy follows the same principle.

Neither resident has attempted to turn Jake's recovery into a public event.

---

# 11. MEDICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Jake's ability to whisper does **not** mean his vocal recovery is complete.

His current vocal ability remains limited.

Extended speaking may cause:

- throat discomfort;
    
- fatigue;
    
- coughing;
    
- loss of vocal strength;
    
- physical exhaustion.
    

His current recovery should therefore remain gradual.

The fact that Jake has successfully spoken at all is encouraging.

It is not permission to make him talk.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Jake has regained the ability to speak.

For now, that information belongs to:

**Jake.**

And, by extension:

**Anby and Twiggy.**

The rest of the SAFEHOUSE will eventually find out.

Probably.

But there is no urgency.

Jake spent five weeks unable to produce a single word.

His first words were spoken quietly to someone he trusted.

He did not make an announcement.

He did not call everyone into his room.

He did not prove anything.

He simply looked at Anby and whispered her name.

For now, that appears to be enough.
