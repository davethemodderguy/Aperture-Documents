---

# I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately 02:13 local time, a single SPAWN combatant attempted to infiltrate the Heaven's Blacksite under cover of darkness.

The infiltrator successfully approached the perimeter without immediately triggering a conventional security response.

They did, however, fail to account for one critical factor.

**Alice was awake.**

The combatant was intercepted by Alice before reaching the interior security boundary.

The encounter lasted only several seconds.

Alice transitioned ALICE-WPN-01 from its pencil configuration and **decapitated the infiltrator with a single strike.**

The hostile was confirmed dead.

C.O.R.E. containment personnel subsequently secured the remains.

A corpse-denial implant was discovered during examination.

The implant had been rendered nonfunctional by the nature of Alice's attack and did not activate.

The incident marks another successful interception of SPAWN personnel and further demonstrates the operational value of Alice's presence along the Blacksite perimeter.

---

# II. THE INFILTRATION

The SPAWN combatant approached during the early morning hours.

Environmental conditions were favorable for concealment.

Perimeter lighting was operating normally.

The forcefield was active.

No immediate indication of a large-scale assault was detected.

The combatant appears to have intended to enter the Blacksite quietly rather than initiate an overt attack.

Their equipment included:

- SPAWN combat mask.
    
- Dark Matter-based weaponry.
    
- Concealment equipment.
    
- Corpse-denial implant.
    
- Additional unidentified equipment.
    

The infiltrator successfully avoided direct contact with the primary security patrol.

They did not avoid Alice.

---

# III. ALICE'S PRESENCE

Alice was conducting an independent observation of the perimeter.

No conventional patrol schedule would normally place a six-year-old awake at 02:13.

Alice's presence was therefore initially considered unusual.

Upon investigation, C.O.R.E. confirmed that this was not an isolated occurrence.

Alice has **chronic insomnia.**

She routinely experiences difficulty sleeping and may remain awake for prolonged periods during the night.

SPAWN appears to have either:

1. Been unaware of Alice's insomnia.
    
2. Assumed she would be asleep.
    
3. Considered her presence irrelevant.
    

All three possibilities represent an intelligence failure.

---

# IV. CONTACT

Alice reportedly noticed movement near the eastern perimeter.

She did not immediately alert Security.

She watched.

The combatant continued approaching.

Alice eventually spoke:

> "...You're not supposed to be here."

The SPAWN combatant reportedly turned toward her.

The encounter escalated immediately.

Alice removed the pencil from her coat.

ALICE-WPN-01 transitioned into its battle axe configuration.

One strike was delivered.

The strike completely severed the combatant's head from the body.

No additional attack was required.

Alice remained at the scene until Perimeter Security arrived.

---

# V. POST-ENGAGEMENT OBSERVATION

Security personnel arriving at the location reportedly found Alice standing beside the remains.

The battle axe had already returned to its pencil configuration.

The pencil was clipped back onto Alice's coat.

Alice herself appeared calm.

One officer asked:

> "Alice, are you hurt?"

> "...No."

Another officer asked:

> "Did he attack you?"

Alice nodded.

> "...He was coming inside."

The officer acknowledged.

Alice then looked toward the body.

> "...He would've hurt someone."

No further comment was made.

---

# VI. CORPSE-DENIAL SYSTEM

The infiltrator's body was recovered before the previously documented Dark Matter corpse-denial mechanism could activate.

The implant was found to have been damaged during the decapitation.

Its activation circuitry had been physically separated from the primary system.

The device therefore failed to initiate its Dark Matter destruction sequence.

This represents another successful prevention of SPAWN's corpse-denial mechanism through Alice's attacks.

C.O.R.E. personnel were able to recover:

- Biological remains.
    
- SPAWN mask.
    
- Dark Matter weapon.
    
- Corpse-denial implant.
    
- Additional equipment.
    

All recovered materials have been transferred to C.O.R.E. laboratories.

---

# VII. THE INSOMNIA PROBLEM

The incident has resulted in an unusual security observation.

SPAWN appears to have incorporated nighttime operations into its infiltration strategy.

This is reasonable.

Human personnel sleep.

Security staffing is generally reduced.

Visibility is lower.

The Blacksite is significantly quieter.

The problem is that Alice does not reliably sleep.

Her chronic insomnia means that nighttime should **not** automatically be considered a period during which Alice is unavailable.

In practical terms, SPAWN has now encountered an unexpected perimeter variable:

> **A six-year-old rabbit girl who may simply be awake at 2 AM.**

And she is carrying a battle axe disguised as a pencil.

---

# VIII. ALICE'S NIGHTTIME BEHAVIOR

Interestingly, Alice does not appear to patrol at night specifically because she expects SPAWN.

She is simply awake.

When unable to sleep, she may:

- Sit quietly.
    
- Draw.
    
- Walk around the SAFEHOUSE.
    
- Observe the perimeter.
    
- Visit nearby personnel.
    
- Sit somewhere outside.
    
- Watch the night sky.
    

The incident occurred during one of these periods.

Alice was not conducting an organized hunt.

She was awake.

She noticed someone where they were not supposed to be.

And she acted.

---

# IX. CORIN — BIG SIS

Corin was understandably concerned upon learning that Alice had been awake at 02:13.

Her first question was not about the SPAWN combatant.

It was:

> "Why was Alice awake?"

The response:

> "Chronic insomnia."

Corin reportedly sighed.

She was then informed that Alice had intercepted and decapitated the infiltrator.

Another sigh followed.

When Corin spoke to Alice later, she asked:

> "You couldn't sleep again?"

Alice nodded.

> "...Mhm."

> "And then you found a SPAWN guy?"

> "...Mhm."

> "And you killed him?"

Alice looked slightly confused by the wording.

> "...He was coming inside."

Corin reportedly paused.

> "...Right."

She then hugged Alice.

---

# X. ORPHIE — BIGGER SIS

Orphie's reaction was quieter.

She understood that Alice's insomnia had inadvertently become an operational advantage.

She also understood the danger of relying on it.

Orphie reportedly told C.O.R.E. personnel:

> "Don't start treating her insomnia like a security system."

This statement has been added to the incident review.

Alice should not be intentionally kept awake for perimeter defense.

Her sleep difficulties are a health concern, not a resource.

The fact that SPAWN happened to encounter her during one of those sleepless periods should not become an operational expectation.

---

# XI. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The infiltration attempt demonstrates that SPAWN has begun experimenting with quieter methods of approaching the Blacksite.

This is a significant development.

The organization may be adapting to the strengthened perimeter defenses.

However, their adaptation has encountered an unexpected obstacle.

Alice.

SPAWN has previously demonstrated familiarity with:

- Perimeter patrol patterns.
    
- Security responses.
    
- Dark Matter weaponry.
    
- Conventional defenses.
    

They apparently do not yet understand Alice.

More specifically, they do not appear to understand that Alice can be awake at virtually any hour.

They also continue to underestimate what the small pencil clipped to her coat represents.

That combination proved fatal.

---

C.O.R.E. recommends:
