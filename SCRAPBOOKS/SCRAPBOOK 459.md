### **RESTRICTED BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION**

# **ORPHIE — PREFERENCE FOR SOFT, WOOLEN HEADWEAR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — SEALED  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Related Record:** Corey █████████ — Archived Medical Record  
**Access:** **Guiding Light / Architect-Level Authorization**  
**Status:** **PRIVATE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie has a noticeable preference for **soft, woolen hats**, particularly beanies and other forms of warm, comfortable headwear.

The preference was previously considered a simple matter of comfort.

Following Guiding Light's retrieval of the sealed medical record concerning **Corey █████████**, the preference has acquired a possible historical explanation.

The recovered photograph depicts the five-year-old subject wearing a **small white beanie** over her injured head.

---

# **THE CONNECTION**

The significance of the beanie cannot be confirmed.

However, the pattern is notable.

The child in the recovered photograph:

> **Wore a soft white beanie to cover her injuries.**

The adult Orphie:

> **Consistently prefers soft, woolen hats.**

No conscious connection has been established.

Orphie does not know about the recovered medical record.

She has never stated that her preference originated from the incident.

It is therefore possible that the preference is simply one of comfort.

It is also possible that some part of her remembers the feeling of safety associated with wearing one.

---

# **ORPHIE'S HATS**

Orphie tends to prefer hats that are:

- Soft.
    
- Warm.
    
- Woolen or knitted.
    
- Comfortable against the skin.
    
- Loose enough not to cause pressure.
    
- Simple in design.
    

She generally dislikes stiff or uncomfortable headwear.

She has occasionally been observed wearing a beanie even when it is not particularly cold.

When asked why, her response is usually brief.

> **"I like them."**

No further explanation is offered.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S OBSERVATION**

Guiding Light has noticed the preference.

She has not told Orphie about the photograph.

She does not intend to.

Instead, she simply accepts the preference.

If Orphie wants a soft hat, she gets a soft hat.

If Orphie wants to sleep wearing one, Guiding Light does not question it.

If Orphie replaces an old one with a new one, nobody makes a comment about it.

---

# **A SMALL DETAIL**

Guiding Light once noticed Orphie holding an old woolen beanie against her chest while sitting quietly in the treehouse.

She asked:

> **"Comfortable?"**

Orphie nodded.

> **"Yeah."**

Guiding Light smiled.

> **"Then keep it."**

Orphie did.

---

# **PRIVATE NOTE — GUIDING LIGHT**

> **"Maybe she remembers."**
> 
> **"Maybe she doesn't."**
> 
> **"It doesn't matter."**
> 
> **"If something as simple as a warm hat makes her feel safe, she deserves to have as many as she wants."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

A preference that once appeared completely ordinary may have a much older origin.

The small white beanie in the five-year-old photograph may have represented nothing more than a medical necessity.

Or perhaps, somewhere beneath memories Orphie can no longer consciously reach, it represents something else:

**warmth.**

**comfort.**

**protection.**

Whatever the reason, Orphie likes soft, wooly hats.

Guiding Light sees no reason to take that comfort away from her.

**End of Restricted Behavioral Observation**
