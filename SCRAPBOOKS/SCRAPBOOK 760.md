# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE / PERSONNEL CARE DIVISION**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — MISSING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — CRITICAL  
**Status:** **UNLOCATED**  
**Last Confirmed Presence:** SAFEHOUSE / TREEHOUSE AREA  
**Search Status:** ACTIVE

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Alice disappeared.

There was no incident.

No alarm.

No struggle.

No damaged equipment.

No abandoned scooter.

No message.

No indication that Alice had intentionally left.

She simply stopped being where everyone expected her to be.

At first, nobody was particularly alarmed.

Alice wandered frequently.

She could be in the Treehouse.

The garden.

The SAFEHOUSE.

The perimeter.

Somewhere nearby.

Someone would find her.

They always did.

---

# **THE FIRST HOUR**

Corin looked for her.

Nothing.

She checked the Treehouse.

Empty.

The SAFEHOUSE.

Empty.

The garden.

Empty.

She checked Alice's usual places.

Nothing.

Then she checked the scooter tracker.

The scooter was stationary.

Alice was not with it.

---

# **THE SEARCH**

The SAFEHOUSE residents began looking.

Belle checked the garden.

Neko checked the maintenance areas.

Miss Neko searched the residential corridors.

Star searched the perimeter.

Nightshade checked the surrounding grounds.

Frost searched the colder areas.

Bao checked the nearby water systems.

Orphie began reviewing security footage.

Personnel were notified.

The search expanded.

---

# **NOTHING**

There were no signs.

No footprints that could be confidently identified as Alice's.

No dropped belongings.

No drawing.

No note.

No whistle.

No flashlight.

No scooter movement.

Nothing.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

Alice's room was untouched.

Her bed was made.

Her hat was absent.

Her ruined sketchbook remained beside the bed.

The new sketchbook was still empty.

The pencils were still where they had been left.

---

# **THE SKETCHBOOK**

Corin picked up the damaged book.

She opened it.

The pages were still there.

Faded.

Warped.

Nearly unreadable.

The last surviving drawing was barely recognizable.

A small bunny.

Standing beside several larger figures.

Corin stared at it.

Then closed the book.

---

# **SECURITY FOOTAGE**

Hours of footage were reviewed.

Alice appeared several times throughout the previous day.

Walking.

Sitting.

Following people.

Standing beside the garden.

Riding her scooter.

Nothing appeared unusual.

There was no obvious moment when she left the Blacksite.

No clear indication of where she went.

No footage showing anyone taking her.

No evidence of an attack.

No evidence of deliberate disappearance.

She simply became absent.

---

# **THE PROBLEM**

Alice is exceptionally difficult to search for.

She does not call for help.

She does not make loud noises.

She does not attract attention.

She does not reliably use her emergency equipment.

She may hide when frightened.

She may sit somewhere and wait.

She may hear people searching and remain silent.

And now—

nobody knows where she is.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin refused to leave the Treehouse.

She sat beside Alice's bed.

Her hands remained around the ruined sketchbook.

Someone eventually asked:

> "What if she comes back?"

Corin answered immediately:

> "Then she'll find me here."

---

# **BELLE**

Belle refused to stop searching.

She checked places that had already been checked multiple times.

Every garden.

Every corridor.

Every storage room.

Every bench.

Every tree.

Every place a small bunny could hide.

She kept saying:

> "She's here somewhere."

Nobody corrected her.

---

# **STAR**

Star searched until exhaustion forced her to stop.

She repeatedly called Alice's name.

Even though she knew Alice couldn't answer.

Eventually she stood beneath a tree.

Rain began falling.

Star looked at the empty ground.

> "...Please."

Nothing answered.

---

# **NEKO**

Neko expanded the search through every available sensor network.

Thermal cameras.

Motion sensors.

Access records.

Environmental monitoring.

The scooter tracker.

Nothing.

The Blacksite had thousands of sensors.

Yet a five-year-old bunny girl could still disappear between them.

---

# **THE WORST POSSIBILITY**

The SAFEHOUSE cannot determine whether Alice is:

- Lost.
    
- Hiding.
    
- Trapped.
    
- Outside the perimeter.
    
- Somewhere inside the Blacksite.
    
- Somewhere no one has thought to search.
    

There is no evidence that she is dead.

There is also no evidence that she is safe.

That uncertainty has become unbearable.

---

# **ALICE'S ABSENCE**

The SAFEHOUSE has become noticeably quieter.

Her usual places are empty.

Her scooter is still there.

Her pencils remain untouched.

Her chair remains vacant.

Nobody moves the ruined sketchbook.

Nobody wants to disturb her room.

---

# **CORIN'S NOTE**

A piece of paper was left beside Alice's bed.

It wasn't addressed to anyone.

It simply read:

> **"Alice."**
> 
> **"If you can see this..."**
> 
> **"You didn't bother us."**
> 
> **"You never did."**
> 
> **"Please come home."**

---

# **CURRENT DIRECTIVE**

Search operations remain active.

Personnel are instructed to report **any** possible sighting immediately.

Do not assume an unusual shadow is unrelated.

Do not assume a small figure in the distance is someone else.

Do not assume Alice will approach you.

If she is found:

**Approach slowly.**

**Identify yourself.**

**Do not leave her alone.**

**Stay with her until SAFEHOUSE personnel arrive.**

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

Alice has disappeared before.

She has faded.

She has become invisible.

She has been forgotten.

She has hidden.

She has been found beneath trees, behind doors, underneath benches, and in places nobody thought to look.

But this time—

she hasn't been found.

The search continues.

The Treehouse light remains on.

Her ruined sketchbook remains beside her bed.

And for the first time, nobody can say:

> **"She's probably somewhere nearby."**

Because nobody knows where Alice is.

**End of CORE-SAFEHOUSE / PERSONNEL CARE DIVISION**
