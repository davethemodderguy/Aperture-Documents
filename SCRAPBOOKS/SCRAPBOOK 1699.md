# 1. SIGNATURE IDENTIFICATION

Clover Luminare possesses a distinctive personal signature.

It consists of:

> **Clover ☘**

The entire signature is written as **one continuous horizontal line**.

There is no separate emblem placed underneath.

There is no second line.

There is no additional notation.

The name **"Clover"** is written in an exceptionally elegant handwritten style, followed immediately by a small, carefully drawn **four-leaf clover**.

The four-leaf clover is part of the signature itself.

---

# 2. VISUAL CHARACTERISTICS

Clover's handwriting is considerably neater than her ordinary writing.

The letters are smooth and carefully proportioned.

The initial **C** is particularly distinctive, beginning with a broad curved stroke before transitioning naturally into the remainder of the name.

The final **r** flows directly into the four-leaf clover.

The clover is drawn without lifting the pen from the page.

The result resembles a single continuous flourish.

C.O.R.E. archival staff have noted that the signature is unusually consistent.

Clover can reproduce it almost identically without appearing to consciously think about the individual strokes.

---

# 3. THE FOUR-LEAF CLOVER

The final symbol contains four distinct leaves.

Unlike the real four-leaf clovers Clover can anomalously grow from soil, the symbol is simply ink.

Nevertheless, Clover considers it an important part of her identity.

When asked why she includes it, Clover answered:

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Because it's me."

No additional explanation was provided.

---

# 4. SIGNATURE USE

Clover uses the signature for:

- official KCA documents;
    
- accounting records;
    
- personal notes;
    
- drawings;
    
- correspondence;
    
- documents requiring acknowledgment;
    
- and occasionally gifts.
    

She does not generally sign objects merely because someone asks.

However, she is willing to provide her signature when the request is appropriate.

---

# 5. COMPARISON WITH LUCY MERRYWEATHER'S AUTOGRAPH

Following Clover's recent meeting with Lucy Merryweather, an interesting coincidence was observed.

Clover now possesses an autograph from someone she admired since childhood.

At the same time, Clover herself has developed a recognizable signature that could eventually become familiar to others.

Lucy signed Clover's notebook.

Clover's own signature is already present throughout numerous KCA and SAFEHOUSE documents.

Clover was asked whether having her own signature made Lucy's autograph feel less special.

Her response was immediate.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "No."

Pause.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Mine is mine."

She looked at Lucy's autograph.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Hers is hers."

Pause.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "That's different."

---

# 6. ARCHIVAL NOTE

C.O.R.E. recommends preserving an authenticated sample of Clover's signature for future document verification.

The combination of:

> **Clover + continuous flourish + four-leaf clover**

is considered sufficiently distinctive to function as a reliable personal identifier.

Forgery is possible.

However, personnel unfamiliar with Clover's handwriting would likely reproduce the visible shape incorrectly.

The four-leaf clover is deceptively difficult to draw in the same continuous motion.
