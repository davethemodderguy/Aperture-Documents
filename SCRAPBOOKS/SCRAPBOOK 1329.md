# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

At approximately **00:00**, Jake woke from a nightmare.

He did not leave his room.

He did not request assistance.

He did not go to the cafeteria.

He did not seek out Bao, Neri, Nora, Aeterna, or any other resident.

He simply got out of bed, walked into the living room, and curled himself into the corner.

He cried.

He remained there.

---

# 2. JAKE'S ROOM

Jake's assigned residence contains **eight rooms in total.**

Only **two are currently being used.**

### ROOM 01 — BATHROOM

Used normally.

Jake has no unusual modifications to the room.

### ROOM 02 — LIVING ROOM

This is where Jake actually spends most of his time.

The room contains:

- one old mattress;
    
- a large collection of plushies;
    
- blankets;
    
- Jake's personal belongings;
    
- and little else.
    

The mattress is not new.

It was simply the one Jake chose to use.

The plushies, however, have accumulated considerably.

They occupy portions of the mattress, floor, and surrounding area.

The arrangement is not particularly organized.

It does not appear that Jake cares.

The other **six rooms remain mostly empty.**

There is no apparent reason Jake could not use them.

He simply doesn't.

---

# 3. NIGHTTIME ENVIRONMENT

Jake does not use a nightlight.

He has never particularly needed one.

His fractured halo naturally produces enough illumination to navigate the room.

The two remaining halo fragments normally float above his head.

Tonight, after waking from the nightmare, Jake reached upward and took hold of one of them.

The fragment responded to his touch.

It remained faintly luminous in his hand.

Jake carried it from the bedroom into the living room.

He placed it beside the old mattress.

The small piece of his former halo illuminated the surrounding plushies.

Jake sat down.

Then curled up in the corner.

---

# 4. OBSERVATION

No alarm was triggered.

Jake's physical condition appeared stable.

His stellar core remained active.

His teddy bear's heartbeat was present.

His respiration was normal.

The distress was emotional.

Jake did not scream.

He did not attempt to leave the residence.

He did not call anyone.

He simply cried.

The old mattress remained surrounded by plushies.

One plushie was pulled against his chest.

The halo fragment rested nearby.

---

# 5. THE NIGHTMARE

Jake did not provide a complete account of the nightmare.

Fragments of speech were recorded while he was crying.

> **Jake:** “...Mom...”

Pause.

> **Jake:** “I waited.”

Several seconds passed.

> **Jake:** “I waited.”

Jake tightened his grip around the plushie.

> **Jake:** “Why didn't you come back?”

He stopped speaking afterward.

---

# 6. THE EMPTY ROOMS

The six unused rooms were completely silent.

No furniture.

No lights.

No activity.

Jake could have gone into any of them.

Instead, he stayed in the living room.

This is consistent with his established preference.

The living room is effectively Jake's entire residence.

The other rooms exist.

Jake simply has no use for them.

---

# 7. 00:17

At approximately **00:17**, Jake had stopped crying loudly.

He was still producing occasional quiet sobs.

One hand remained around the plushie.

The other rested against the mattress.

His teddy bear was pressed against his chest.

Its heartbeat remained steady.

Jake whispered:

> **Jake:** “I'm okay.”

There was no one in the room to hear him.

He said it anyway.

---

# 8. 00:31

Jake eventually lay down on the mattress.

He remained in the living room.

He did not return to the bedroom.

The room contained almost nothing except the old mattress, plushies, blankets, and the faint blue light of the halo fragment.

Jake pulled the blanket over himself.

The fragment remained beside him.

He closed his eyes.

A few minutes later, he opened them again.

He looked toward the doorway.

Nothing happened.

He looked away.

---

# 9. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Jake's response does not currently indicate a medical emergency.

However, the incident is consistent with his established nightmare pattern.

His nightmares remain strongly associated with:

- abandonment;
    
- waiting;
    
- isolation;
    
- his mother's disappearance;
    
- being left behind;
    
- his death;
    
- and the belief that nobody is coming back.
    

The absence of food-seeking behavior is also notable.

Jake normally enjoys food and has developed a particularly strong fondness for cake and other sweets.

Tonight, he did not appear interested.

He did not leave the room.

He did not ask anyone for anything.

He simply stayed in his little corner of the living room.

---

# 10. PERSONNEL GUIDANCE

Personnel should not immediately enter Jake's residence simply because he is crying.

Jake's room is his private space.

If he requests company, provide it.

If he approaches someone, respond.

If a genuine medical concern develops, intervene.

Otherwise:

**Knock.**

Wait.

Allow Jake to decide whether he wants someone inside.

A person being alone after a nightmare is not automatically a crisis.

For Jake, however, prolonged isolation should still be observed carefully.

There is a significant difference between:

> **“I want to be alone.”**

and:

> **“I don't think anyone will come.”**

Personnel should avoid assuming which one applies.

He remained curled around the plushie on the old mattress.

His teddy bear was against his chest.

The remaining six rooms were still empty.

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake technically has an eight-room residence.

He uses two.

One is the bathroom.

The other is a living room containing an old mattress and enough plushies to make the furniture largely irrelevant.

The remaining six rooms sit empty.

Tonight, after waking from a nightmare, Jake didn't explore any of them.

He didn't need more space.

He needed somewhere small.

Somewhere familiar.

So he curled up beside his plushies, picked up a fragment of his broken halo, and let the little piece of himself light the room.

A six-year-old star, sitting alone on an old mattress in an almost completely empty apartment.

At least the plushies were there.

And, for tonight, so was the light.
