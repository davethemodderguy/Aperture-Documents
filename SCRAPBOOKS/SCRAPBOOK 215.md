## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – PROJECTED DEVELOPMENT VS. DOCUMENTED HISTORY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

As part of the ongoing investigation into the unidentified organization responsible for the abuse and experimentation inflicted upon Corin Lockwood during her childhood, C.O.R.E. Research was requested to prepare a developmental comparison.

The purpose of this report is not to determine who Corin "should have been."

Rather, it is intended to illustrate the profound long-term effects of the incident.

The perpetrators remain unidentified.

The investigation remains active.

---

# **PROJECTED DEVELOPMENT**

Based on educational records, early developmental observations, genetic health assessments, and testimony from the Lockwood family, Psychological Services produced a projection of Corin's likely development had the incident never occurred.

The projected individual is described as:

- Cheerful.
    
- Curious.
    
- Socially confident.
    
- Physically healthy.
    
- Quick to trust others.
    
- Fond of reading outdoors.
    
- Naturally humorous.
    
- Comfortable in family photographs.
    
- Eager to celebrate birthdays.
    
- Openly affectionate with family.
    

Personnel who know Corey remarked that the projection resembles her personality in several respects.

Researchers caution that this comparison is descriptive rather than conclusive.

Corey is her own individual.

---

# **DOCUMENTED HISTORY**

The Corin documented in historical records developed under radically different circumstances.

Long-term consequences included:

- Severe depression.
    
- Chronic insomnia.
    
- Persistent self-hatred.
    
- Body dysmorphic disorder.
    
- Extreme social withdrawal.
    
- Recurrent trauma-related nightmares.
    
- Fear of medical examinations.
    
- Rejection of personal kindness.
    
- Extended periods of self-isolation.
    

Although recovery has occurred at various points, these experiences permanently altered the course of her life.

# **ONGOING INVESTIGATION**

The organization responsible has not been identified.

Evidence recovered over the years remains fragmented.

Recovered facilities contain signs of deliberate destruction.

Electronic records were systematically erased.

Witnesses are scarce.

Those who possess relevant information have either disappeared or remain unidentified.

Despite these obstacles, the investigation has not been closed.
