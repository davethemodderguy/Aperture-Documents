# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **POST-OPERATIVE RECOVERY REPORT**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – OCULAR REATTACHMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **SUMMARY**

After months of observation confirming the continued viability of Corin Lockwood's preserved left eye, Blacksite Medical concluded that surgical reattachment could be safely attempted.

Following extensive consultation, Corin gave her consent.

The procedure was performed under the supervision of Blacksite surgeons with assistance from Aperture Industries medical specialists specializing in Ether-integrated physiology.

---

## **SURGERY**

The operation required:

- Ether synchronization with Corin's stabilized physiology.
    
- Microsurgical reconnection of the optic structures.
    
- Reconstruction of damaged surrounding tissue.
    
- Continuous monitoring of Ether flow throughout the procedure.
    

The surgery lasted just under eleven hours.

No complications were recorded.

When Corin awoke, both eyes responded normally to light.

Over the following weeks, her depth perception gradually returned, followed by complete binocular vision.

Final medical evaluations concluded that the reattached eye had recovered beyond expectations.

---

## **MEDICAL CONCLUSION**

Corin has been declared fully recovered from the injury sustained during her rescue.

Her eyesight has returned to functional normality.

Minor sensitivity during the first stages of rehabilitation has completely resolved.

For the first time since the incident, Corin once again stood before a mirror with both of her own eyes.

She smiled quietly.

Not because she looked different.

Because she finally felt whole again.

## **PERSONAL PROFILE UPDATE**

During post-operative interviews, researchers asked Corin several lighthearted questions unrelated to her treatment.

One answer surprised almost everyone.

**Favorite Tool:** Chainsaw.

When asked why, Corin answered without hesitation.

> "...They're mechanically elegant."

She thought for a moment.

> "...Simple."

> "...Reliable."

> "...And surprisingly satisfying to maintain."

Norma laughed.

> "...Out of everything in the workshop..."

> "...You picked the chainsaw?"

Corin nodded.

> "...Yes."

Arcturus sighed.

> "...I'm beginning to think liking unusual weapons runs in the family."

Belle tilted her head.

> "...Just promise me you're only using it for trees."

Corin smiled innocently.

> "...Mostly."

The room immediately became much quieter.

## **STATUS**

Corin Lockwood has made a full medical recovery from the injuries sustained during her abduction.

Her left eye has been successfully reattached and restored, leaving no lasting visual impairment.

She has returned to her duties as Head of Accounting, where she has already resumed reviewing budgets—much to Norma's amusement whenever another unusually expensive ammunition request arrives on her desk.

An unofficial note was later added to her personnel profile by the SAFEHOUSE residents:

> **Likes: Tea, quiet evenings, balanced budgets... and, for reasons nobody fully understands, chainsaws.**
