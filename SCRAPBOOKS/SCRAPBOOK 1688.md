## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### MEDICAL RESEARCH & RESIDENT WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-MED-4C-JAKE-271  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — MEDICAL / STRUCTURAL INCIDENT  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE RESPONSE  
**SUBJECT:** Jake — Left Leg Fracture Following Catwalk Collapse  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — MAINTENANCE CORRIDOR 4-C

---

# INCIDENT SUMMARY

At approximately 14:32, Jake and Clover Luminare were walking through a maintenance corridor when an overhead catwalk section unexpectedly detached from its mounting structure.

The catwalk collapsed into the corridor.

Clover avoided the falling structure without injury.

Jake did not.

A section of the collapsed catwalk struck and **crushed Jake's left leg.**

Clover immediately remained with him and initiated a medical response.

Jake was conscious throughout the incident.

His teddy bear remained secured against his back.

---

# 1. INITIAL IMPACT

The collapse occurred with minimal warning.

Clover heard the structural failure first.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Jake—"

The catwalk came down.

Clover moved clear.

Jake attempted to move with her.

He was approximately one step too slow.

The metal structure struck his left leg and pinned it against the corridor floor.

Jake fell.

There was a brief period of silence.

Then:

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Ow."

Clover immediately turned around.

She found Jake lying on the floor beneath the collapsed section.

His left leg was visibly injured.

He was conscious.

He was breathing normally.

His azure eyes remained illuminated.

---

# 2. CLOVER'S RESPONSE

Clover approached carefully.

She did **not** attempt to pull Jake free.

She did not move the collapsed structure.

She instead knelt beside him.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Jake."

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I'm okay."

Clover looked at his leg.

Then back at him.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "No."

Jake frowned.

> **JAKE:**  
> "It's healing."

It was.

The injury was already beginning to regenerate.

However, the rate of regeneration did not make movement medically advisable.

Clover placed one hand near Jake's shoulder.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Then let it heal while you stay still."

Jake considered this.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Okay."

---

# 3. MEDICAL OBSERVATION

Jake's left leg sustained a significant fracture and crush injury.

Despite the severity of the impact, no major vascular failure was observed.

His anomalous regenerative system activated almost immediately.

Observed recovery indicators included:

- restoration of damaged tissue;
    
- stabilization of the fracture;
    
- gradual reconstruction of bone;
    
- reduction of swelling;
    
- restoration of normal coloration;
    
- decreasing pain response.
    

The regeneration is functioning correctly.

However:

> **Regeneration does not mean the injury should be disturbed.**

Jake's body can repair itself.

That does not mean the repair should be forced to occur while he is walking around on the damaged limb.

---

# 4. THE PROBLEM

Jake's first instinct was to get up.

This was immediately rejected.

He attempted to move his upper body.

Clover stopped him.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Don't."

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I can move."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Your leg is broken."

> **JAKE:**  
> "It's fixing itself."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Yes."

Pause.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "While you're lying down."

Jake looked at his leg.

The bone was visibly stabilizing beneath the damaged tissue.

He went quiet.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Oh."

---

# 5. CLOVER STAYS WITH HIM

Clover remained beside Jake while waiting for medical personnel.

She kept her candle away from the damaged structure and used it only to illuminate the darker portion of the corridor.

Jake looked toward the candle.

> **JAKE:**  
> "You always carry that?"

> **CLOVER:**  
> "At night."

> **JAKE:**  
> "It's not night."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "I know."

Jake nodded.

He then looked at the ceiling.

> **JAKE:**  
> "The building hates me."

Clover shook her head.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "The building doesn't hate you."

A pause.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "It's just badly maintained."

Jake considered this.

> **JAKE:**  
> "That's worse."

Clover almost laughed.

She caught herself.

---

# 6. MEDICAL TEAM ARRIVAL

Medical personnel arrived several minutes later.

They assessed Jake's condition without attempting to interfere with the regenerative process.

The damaged section of catwalk was stabilized and carefully removed.

Jake was instructed not to stand.

He obeyed.

This was considered notable.

Jake has previously demonstrated a tendency to underestimate the consequences of physical injuries because of his anomalous durability and regenerative capacity.

The current incident reinforces an important distinction:

> **Jake can survive things that would kill an ordinary child.**

That does not mean:

> **Jake cannot be hurt.**

---

# 7. TEDDY BEAR STATUS

Jake's teddy bear remained physically intact.

Its heartbeat remained steady.

During examination, Jake repeatedly checked that it was still attached to him.

Clover eventually noticed.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "It's okay."

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I know."

He touched the bear's head.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I just check."

Clover nodded.

She understood.

---

# 8. CURRENT CONDITION

Jake is currently stable.

His left leg remains injured but is actively regenerating.

No permanent damage is currently expected.

He has been instructed to remain stationary until the regeneration has progressed sufficiently to support normal movement.

C.O.R.E. medical personnel have specifically prohibited:

- walking;
    
- running;
    
- flying;
    
- jumping;
    
- attempting to "test" the leg;
    
- carrying heavy objects;
    
- allowing another resident to make him walk merely because he says he feels better.
    

Jake is permitted to:

- sit;
    
- lie down;
    
- read;
    
- draw;
    
- talk;
    
- eat;
    
- bother people from a stationary position.
    

The final item was added at Jake's request.

---

# 9. CLOVER'S ASSESSMENT

Clover was physically unharmed.

However, she remained visibly shaken by the incident.

She later admitted:

> **CLOVER:**  
> "I was fine."

Pause.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "He wasn't."

C.O.R.E. reminded her that she was not responsible for the catwalk failure.

Clover acknowledged this.

She still remained beside Jake.

When asked why:

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Because he shouldn't have to lie here alone."

No further explanation was provided.

None was necessary.

---

# 10. JAKE

Approximately twenty minutes into the regenerative process, Jake asked:

> **JAKE:**  
> "Can I walk now?"

Medical personnel answered:

> **"No."**

Jake frowned.

Five minutes later:

> **JAKE:**  
> "Now?"

> **"No."**

Another five minutes.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Now?"

> **"No."**

Clover finally looked at him.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Jake."

He stopped.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "You have one job."

Jake looked at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "What?"

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Lie there."

Jake sighed.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Okay."

He lay back down.

---

# C.O.R.E. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Jake's regeneration is functioning normally.

The injury is expected to resolve.

There is currently no indication of permanent impairment.

The incident nevertheless demonstrates that Jake's anomalous physiology should not be mistaken for invulnerability.

He can regenerate.

He can recover.

He can survive extraordinary damage.

But when something crushes his leg:

**he still gets a broken leg.**

And when that happens, the appropriate response is not:

> _"He'll regenerate."_

It is:

> **"Keep him still while he regenerates."**

---

The maintenance corridor has been closed.

The catwalk has been secured.

An engineering inspection is underway.

Jake is lying on a temporary medical cot.

Clover is sitting beside him.

His teddy bear is against his chest.

His left leg is slowly repairing itself.

Jake looks down at it.

Then at Clover.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Can I go now?"

Clover doesn't even look up from her notebook.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "No."

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Okay."

Five minutes pass.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Now?"

Clover finally looks at him.

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Jake."

He smiles faintly.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I'm bored."

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Good."

> **JAKE:**  
> "Why?"

> **CLOVER:**  
> "Because bored means you're not trying to walk."

Jake considers this.

Then settles back down.

His teddy bear's heartbeat continues steadily beneath his chin.

For once, the six-year-old star stays exactly where he is.
