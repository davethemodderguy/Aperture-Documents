# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately 01:00, resident Jake left his room to observe the night sky.

During this activity, he noticed an unusually bright white star.

Jake reported experiencing an unfamiliar but powerful sense of connection with the celestial object.

He subsequently returned to sleep.

At approximately 09:16 the following morning, an unidentified sixteen-year-old male appeared at the Blacksite's main entrance.

The individual was armed with an anomalous spear and demanded that Director Lucious disclose the location of his younger brother.

The individual threatened to use lethal force if the personnel present did not comply.

Perimeter Security, Nine Tailed Fox, and M.S.A.C. personnel responded immediately.

Before any shots were fired, Jake arrived at the scene.

Upon hearing Jake's voice, the unidentified individual lowered his weapon and ceased all hostile behavior.

He identified himself as **Aries Akana**.

Jake subsequently recognized Aries as his older brother, despite initially having no conscious memory of him.

Aries presented Jake with a letter containing instructions written by Jake himself.

The letter appeared to anticipate Jake's memory loss and the possibility that the brothers would become separated.

Jake embraced Aries.

He then introduced him to the assembled personnel as his older brother.

The encounter was witnessed by numerous SAFEHOUSE residents, including Bao, Clover, Phoenix, Star, Nightshade, Alice, Taiga, and Mart.

Aries has since been admitted to SAFEHOUSE under provisional residential status.

He currently resides with Jake and Clover.

C.O.R.E.'s preliminary assessment is that Aries is not an ordinary human teenager.

His physical appearance, anomalous energy signature, and apparent relationship with Jake suggest that both individuals may be manifestations of a shared celestial origin.

**The Blacksite has gained a new resident.**

Jake has gained his older brother back.

And Director Lucious has narrowly avoided being stabbed on his own doorstep before breakfast.

# 1. PRECEDING EVENT — THE WHITE STAR

**DATE:** Previous night  
**TIME:** 01:00  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — EXTERIOR OBSERVATION AREA

Security footage confirms that Jake left his residence shortly before 01:00.

He was wearing his usual sleepwear and carried his teddy bear.

His wings were folded against his back.

No security personnel reported unusual behavior.

Jake proceeded to an elevated observation area overlooking the surrounding Icelandic landscape.

He sat down and began observing the sky.

For approximately twelve minutes, his behavior remained consistent with previous stargazing activities.

At 01:13, Jake became visibly attentive to a particular point in the sky.

The object was recorded as a bright white star.

No unusual atmospheric disturbance was detected.

No incoming object was tracked.

No energy discharge was registered by the Blacksite's external monitoring systems.

Nevertheless, Jake reportedly experienced a strong emotional response.

In a later interview, he described the feeling as follows.

**C.O.R.E. INTERVIEWER:**

"What did you see?"

**JAKE:**

"A white star."

**INTERVIEWER:**

"Was it brighter than the others?"

**JAKE:**

"Yeah."

**INTERVIEWER:**

"Did it look different?"

Jake considered the question.

**JAKE:**

"Not really."

**INTERVIEWER:**

"Then why did you pay attention to it?"

Jake looked down at his teddy bear.

**JAKE:**

"I don't know."

A pause.

**JAKE:**

"It felt like I knew it."

**INTERVIEWER:**

"Did you recognize it?"

**JAKE:**

"No."

**INTERVIEWER:**

"Did you feel afraid?"

Jake shook his head.

**JAKE:**

"No. It was nice."

**INTERVIEWER:**

"What did you do afterward?"

**JAKE:**

"Went to sleep."

The interviewer waited for further explanation.

Jake offered none.

The interview concluded.

# 2. INCIDENT REPORT — UNIDENTIFIED VISITOR

**DATE:** Following morning  
**TIME:** 09:16  
**LOCATION:** MAIN BLACKSITE ENTRANCE

Director Lucious was personally present at the main entrance when the unidentified individual arrived.

The visitor appeared to be approximately sixteen years old.

He wore a white hoodie and white trousers.

His hair and facial features closely resembled Jake's, although he was taller and visibly older.

Unlike Jake's azure ocular glow, the visitor emitted a luminous white light.

Two horns were visible on his head.

He carried a spear of unknown construction.

The visitor approached the entrance without attempting to conceal his presence.

He appeared angry.

He also appeared to have been searching for someone for a considerable period.

The following transcript was reconstructed from entrance surveillance footage and the statements of personnel present.

"Are you the owner of this facility?"

The visitor continued holding the spear near the Director's neck.

No shots were fired.

Then someone approached from inside the facility.

# 3. JAKE ARRIVES

Jake had heard the commotion from within the Blacksite.

He ran toward the entrance.

His wings were partially extended, and his teddy bear was held tightly against his chest.

He arrived in time to see an unfamiliar older boy threatening Director Lucious.

Jake stopped.

His eyes widened.

**JAKE:**

"WHO ARE YOU? WHY ARE YOU THREATENING UNCLE LUCIOUS?"

The visitor froze.

His expression changed immediately.

The anger disappeared.

For several seconds, he simply stared at Jake.

Then his spear fell from its threatening position.

He lowered it completely.

The change in behavior was so abrupt that several security personnel initially suspected a tactical deception.

The visitor appeared not to notice the weapons aimed at him.

He took a single step toward Jake.

His voice was suddenly much quieter.

**UNKNOWN VISITOR:**

"I finally found you."

A pause.

**UNKNOWN VISITOR:**

"Took me a long time."

Jake stared at him.

The two looked remarkably similar.

White and azure light reflected across their faces.

Jake's ears tilted slightly backward.

**JAKE:**

"...Do I know you?"

The visitor's expression tightened.

Not with anger.

With recognition of something he had apparently expected.

He reached into his hoodie and removed a small envelope.

He held it out to Jake.

**UNKNOWN VISITOR:**

"I thought you might ask that."

Jake accepted the envelope.

The visitor waited.

Jake opened it.

Inside was a handwritten letter.

# 4. THE LETTER

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Personal Correspondence  
**AUTHOR:** Jake  
**RECIPIENT:** Future Jake  
**STATUS:** Original retained by resident; copy held by C.O.R.E. with permission

The letter contained the following message.

> Hey big bro.
> 
> I'm heading down.
> 
> It's my choice, I'll go alright.
> 
> If I forget, give this to me.

The handwriting was consistent with Jake's previously recorded handwriting samples.

No significant discrepancy was found in letter formation, spacing, or pressure patterns.

The paper itself displayed no unusual properties.

The envelope, however, appeared to have been preserved for an extended period without significant deterioration.

C.O.R.E. has not established how long Aries carried the letter.

Nor has it determined what Jake meant by "heading down."

The wording suggests that the separation was anticipated and possibly voluntary.

It does not establish why it occurred.

Jake read the letter twice.

He then looked up at the visitor.

For several seconds, he appeared unable to decide what to do.

Then he stepped forward and hugged him.

Aries immediately returned the embrace.

His spear remained on the ground beside them.

The armed personnel surrounding the entrance did not lower their weapons immediately.

They had just watched an unidentified individual threaten the Director of Aperture Industries.

Nevertheless, the nature of the encounter had changed.

The visitor was no longer attempting to reach Jake by force.

Jake had reached him first.

# 9. TWO STARS, ONE UNBREAKABLE BOND

The preliminary investigation suggests that Jake and Aries share an origin that differs substantially from conventional biological relationships.

Both individuals display characteristics associated with celestial manifestations.

Jake is a Weeping Angel and the human manifestation of a literal star.

Aries exhibits a luminous white energy signature and anomalous physical characteristics consistent with a related celestial entity.

Their resemblance is pronounced.

Their apparent emotional connection is stronger still.

Aries recognized Jake immediately.

Jake, despite his apparent memory loss, responded to Aries with immediate familiarity once the letter was presented.

The letter itself indicates that Jake anticipated the possibility of forgetting his older brother.

The phrase "I'm heading down" may refer to Jake's transition from a celestial existence into his present physical manifestation.

This interpretation remains unconfirmed.

What is clear is that the brothers did not appear to have developed their relationship through ordinary upbringing.

They describe themselves as siblings.

They share an evident emotional bond.

And their relationship appears to have persisted despite separation, memory loss, and a considerable period without direct contact.

C.O.R.E. currently considers the following hypothesis plausible:

**Jake and Aries are two distinct celestial beings whose relationship predates Jake's current life at the Blacksite.**

No evidence currently suggests that either individual is a creation or possession of the other.

Their bond appears mutual.

Neither is a substitute for the other.

They are brothers.

Aries's reaction to Bao requires further contextual explanation.

During the initial residential interview, Aries stated that he and Jake had not grown up with a mother.

He described their original relationship as that of two stars.

There was no conventional parental household.

No human mother.

No ordinary childhood home.

The brothers had each other.

Aries appeared unfamiliar with the idea that Jake had begun referring to another person as "Mom."

This was not expressed as hostility toward Bao.

Rather, Aries appeared surprised that Jake had developed a familial relationship independent of their original celestial bond.

**C.O.R.E. INTERVIEWER:**

# 11. RESIDENTIAL PLACEMENT

Following the initial security assessment, Director Lucious approved provisional SAFEHOUSE residency for Aries.

The arrangement was selected after consultation with Jake and Clover.

Aries will reside with Jake and Clover, subject to standard residential procedures and any additional requirements identified during the ongoing assessment.

The placement has several advantages.

First, it allows the brothers to remain together.

Second, it places Aries within an established residential environment.

Third, it provides an opportunity to evaluate Aries's anomalous characteristics without treating his presence as a reason for unnecessary separation from Jake.

Fourth, it allows the SAFEHOUSE staff to determine what assistance, if any, Aries requires.

Aries has not been assigned to a research program.

He has not been designated a weapon.

He has not been placed under permanent containment.

His provisional classification reflects the fact that his capabilities and origin remain incompletely understood.

The objective is to establish a safe living arrangement while the investigation proceeds.

Director Lucious reportedly summarized the decision in a brief conversation with C.O.R.E. personnel.

**DIRECTOR LUCIOUS:**

"He's a teenager who came looking for his brother."

A pause.

**DIRECTOR LUCIOUS:**

"A very dangerous teenager, apparently."

He glanced toward the residential corridor.

**DIRECTOR LUCIOUS:**

"But still a teenager."

The Director authorized the residential placement.

# 14. C.O.R.E. PRELIMINARY ASSESSMENT

Aries Akana presents several unresolved questions.

His origin remains uncertain.

His celestial characteristics require further study.

The relationship between his white stellar glow and Jake's azure manifestation has not been established.

His spear remains under controlled inspection until its properties and safe handling requirements can be determined.

His initial threat against Director Lucious requires a formal security review.

However, the evidence gathered so far also establishes several important facts.

Aries did not arrive seeking control of the Blacksite.

He arrived seeking his younger brother.

His hostility was directed toward the individual he believed responsible for Jake's disappearance.

Upon discovering Jake's presence, he immediately ceased the confrontation.

He surrendered the immediate threat posed by his spear.

He accepted residential arrangements that allow him to remain with Jake.

He has demonstrated no further hostile behavior toward Blacksite personnel.

His emotional response to Jake's presence appears genuine.

C.O.R.E. therefore recommends continued observation under standard resident welfare procedures rather than immediate escalation to permanent containment.

The security incident must remain documented.

The person involved must also be recognized as Jake's older brother.

Both facts matter.

Neither should erase the other.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

At 01:00, Jake looked at a white star.

He did not know why it felt familiar.

He did not know that someone was looking for him.

He did not know that the brother he had forgotten was somewhere beyond the Blacksite, carrying a small envelope and searching for a way to reach him.

Jake went to sleep.

The next morning, a sixteen-year-old boy arrived at the facility with a spear, a threat, and years of unanswered questions.

He demanded his brother back.

He was prepared to fight an entire Blacksite to find him.

Then Jake appeared.

And the boy who had threatened to turn the facility into a bloodbath dropped his weapon because his little brother had called out to him.

Not because of a containment field.

Not because of a security intervention.

Not because a superior officer ordered him to stand down.

Because Jake was there.

Because Jake was alive.

Because, after all that time, Aries had finally found him.

Jake had forgotten his brother.

Aries had not forgotten Jake.

And the letter Jake left behind had been waiting for the day they would meet again.

There are questions C.O.R.E. still needs to answer.

Where did Aries come from?

What happened when Jake went down?

What does it mean for two stars to become brothers?

And what exactly happened to Jake's memories?

Those questions will require time.

For now, the immediate situation is considerably simpler.

Jake has his big brother back.

Aries has found a place to stay.

Clover has acquired another person to supervise.

Bao has discovered that Jake has an older brother who apparently did not receive the memo about calling her Mom.

Alice is still trying to understand how Jake forgot his own sibling.

Director Lucious has requested that visitors refrain from pointing spears at his neck.

And the SAFEHOUSE has gained another resident.

Two stars.

One forgotten letter.

A reunion that nearly became a security disaster.

And a family bond that survived the distance between them.

**C.O.R.E. FINAL DETERMINATION:**

Aries Akana is to be treated as a resident pending completion of the anomalous assessment.

Jake's relationship with Aries is to be respected.

The brothers are not to be separated for investigative convenience.

No testing is to be conducted without appropriate authorization and consideration of both individuals' welfare.

The spear remains subject to security controls.

Director Lucious's neck is considered medically intact.

And the residential assignment stands.

After all, the purpose of SAFEHOUSE is not merely to protect people from what might be outside.

Sometimes, it is to give someone who has spent a very long time searching for family somewhere to finally come home.
