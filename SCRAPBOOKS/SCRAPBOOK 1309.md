# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has returned to behaving like a background character.

He does not seek attention.

He does not interrupt conversations.

He does not ask people to look at him.

He does not cause disturbances.

He does not deliberately involve himself in other residents' activities.

He simply exists within the SAFEHOUSE.

Unfortunately, the SAFEHOUSE appears to have developed a remarkable talent for accidentally hitting him.

The majority of recorded incidents involve doors.

Specifically:

**Jake's face.**

---

# 1. GENERAL BEHAVIOR

Jake can now frequently be found:

- sitting quietly in hallways;
    
- standing beside walls;
    
- walking through the cafeteria;
    
- sitting beneath tables;
    
- reading;
    
- carrying his teddy bear;
    
- drinking something;
    
- watching other residents from a distance;
    
- sitting in corners;
    
- wandering without a destination;
    
- or simply standing somewhere while everyone else goes about their business.
    

He rarely announces himself.

He does not demand interaction.

If nobody speaks to him, he generally remains perfectly content to continue whatever he was doing.

This has created an unusual problem.

People sometimes forget that he is there.

---

# 2. THE DOOR INCIDENTS

Jake's physical materialization has created an unexpected vulnerability.

He is now:

- solid;
    
- relatively small;
    
- quiet;
    
- and extremely unlikely to announce that he is standing behind a door.
    

This combination has produced numerous minor collisions.

### Incident #01

Jake was standing outside a room.

Someone opened the door outward.

**THUNK.**

The door struck Jake directly in the face.

Jake stumbled backward.

The person froze.

> **"OH MY GOD—JAKE!"**

Jake sat on the floor.

He held his nose.

> **"Ow."**

Pause.

> **"Door."**

The person apologized approximately fourteen times.

Jake nodded.

> **"Okay."**

He then stood up and walked away.

---

# 3. INCIDENT #02

Jake was standing beside a doorway while reading.

A resident opened the door.

The door caught Jake's shoulder.

Jake stumbled.

The resident immediately apologized.

Jake looked at them.

> **"It's okay."**

He returned to reading.

Approximately thirty seconds later:

**THUNK.**

The same door swung back and hit him again.

Jake slowly lowered his book.

He stared at the door.

Then at the resident.

> **"It did it again."**

---

# 4. INCIDENT #03

Jake was sitting on the floor near a corridor.

A Security Officer opened a door without noticing him.

The door struck Jake's forehead.

Jake fell backward.

The Officer immediately dropped to one knee.

> **"Jake! Are you hurt?"**

Jake blinked.

> **"No."**

Pause.

> **"...Maybe."**

The Officer asked:

> **"Where?"**

Jake pointed at the door.

> **"It hit me."**

---

# 5. INCIDENT #04

Jake was walking behind Neri.

Neri stopped suddenly.

Jake walked directly into her.

**THUMP.**

Jake bounced backward.

Neri turned around.

Jake was sitting on the floor.

Neri stared.

Jake stared back.

> **"You stopped."**

Neri nodded.

Jake stood.

> **"Okay."**

They continued walking.

No further discussion occurred.

---

# 6. INCIDENT #05

Jake attempted to sit in a chair.

Someone moved the chair at precisely the wrong moment.

Jake sat down.

There was no chair.

He landed on the floor.

The teddy bear remained securely attached to him.

Jake stared at the empty space where the chair had been.

Then looked at the person holding it.

> **"Why?"**

The person immediately apologized.

Jake accepted the apology.

He then sat on the floor instead.

---

# 7. INCIDENT #06

Jake was standing in the cafeteria waiting for Nora.

Alice ran past.

She did not see him.

She collided with him.

Jake fell.

Alice stopped.

> **"JAKE!"**

Jake sat on the floor.

Alice immediately helped him up.

> **"I'm sorry!"**

Jake nodded.

> **"It's okay."**

Alice paused.

> **"Are you sure?"**

Jake looked down at himself.

> **"I'm still here."**

Alice hugged him.

Jake tolerated it.

---

# 8. INCIDENT #07

A cabinet door was opened.

Jake happened to be standing beside it.

The cabinet door struck him.

Jake stared at it.

He slowly closed the cabinet.

Then moved two steps farther away.

C.O.R.E. considers this an appropriate adaptation.

---

# 9. THE MOST COMMON INCIDENT

A review of SAFEHOUSE security footage revealed an unexpectedly consistent pattern.

Jake is often present in locations where people do not expect him to be.

He does not announce himself.

He does not make noise.

He does not demand attention.

Therefore:

**People forget he is standing there.**

Then:

**Something hits him.**

The object varies.

The result is remarkably consistent.

Jake pauses.

Checks whether he is hurt.

Looks at the object.

Then continues whatever he was doing.

---

# 10. JAKE DOES NOT COMPLAIN

This has become the more concerning aspect.

Jake rarely reports minor injuries.

If something hits him, he may simply say:

> **"Ow."**

Then continue walking.

If someone asks whether he is hurt:

> **"A little."**

If asked whether he needs medical attention:

> **"No."**

If the injury is genuinely serious, he will usually report it.

However, his threshold for what constitutes:

> **"Worth bothering someone about"**

appears to be extremely high.

This is consistent with his established fear of being a burden.

---

# 11. THE TEDDY BEAR

The teddy bear has become useful in these situations.

Jake may not verbally react much.

The bear does.

Following a collision, its heartbeat may briefly accelerate.

If Jake is genuinely frightened or hurt, the change is much more pronounced.

Personnel have therefore been advised:

> **Watch the bear.**

If Jake says:

> **"I'm okay."**

but the heartbeat is highly irregular,

the statement should not automatically be accepted.

---

# 12. BACKGROUND JAKE

The term **"Background Jake"** has unofficially entered resident vocabulary.

It describes the phenomenon in which Jake is simply...

there.

Someone may be having a conversation.

Jake is sitting three meters away reading.

Someone may be cooking.

Jake is standing in the corner drinking something.

Someone may be repairing equipment.

Jake walks past.

Nobody notices.

Five minutes later:

> **"Wait. Was Jake there?"**

Usually:

**Yes.**

---

# 13. JAKE'S RESPONSE TO BEING NOTICED

Jake does not appear offended when people overlook him.

In fact, he sometimes seems to prefer it.

If someone suddenly says:

> **"Jake! I didn't see you there!"**

Jake may simply answer:

> **"That's okay."**

He does not interpret every instance of being unnoticed as rejection.

This is an important improvement from his earlier behavior.

He is increasingly capable of existing around people without requiring their attention.

---

# 14. THE DOOR SAFETY PROBLEM

Following repeated incidents, C.O.R.E. reviewed corridor architecture.

The recommendation was straightforward:

> **Install transparent panels in frequently used doors.**

Neko's response:

> **"We can do that."**

Pause.

> **"But why does Jake keep standing behind doors?"**

No one had a good answer.

Jake was asked.

He replied:

> **"I wasn't behind it."**

Pause.

> **"I was beside it."**

This distinction was apparently important to him.

---

# 15. CURRENT STATUS

Jake continues to move independently around SAFEHOUSE.

He eats.

He drinks.

He reads.

He sleeps.

He wanders.

He occasionally sits with others.

He sometimes follows Nora.

He sometimes sits with Aeterna.

He continues to hold his teddy bear.

His trust issues remain.

His sorrow remains.

His trauma remains.

But he is no longer constantly trying to make himself invisible.

He is simply quiet.

There is a difference.

---

# FINAL ASSESSMENT

Jake has become an unusually low-maintenance resident.

He does not demand resources.

He does not seek attention.

He does not interfere with operations.

He does not cause disturbances.

He simply exists.

Unfortunately, his preferred method of existing is apparently:

**Standing exactly where a door is about to hit him.**

C.O.R.E. cannot determine whether this is coincidence.

It is almost certainly coincidence.

The security footage nevertheless remains compelling.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

There are residents who announce their presence.

There are residents who demand attention.

There are residents who enter a room and immediately become the center of it.

Jake is none of these.

He walks through the SAFEHOUSE quietly.

He sits in corners.

He reads.

He eats cake.

He hugs his teddy bear.

He watches people work.

He rarely bothers anyone.

Sometimes nobody even notices him.

And then—

**BANG.**

Door.

Jake gets hit in the face.

He sits there for a moment.

Looks at the door.

Looks at the person who opened it.

And quietly says:

> **"You did it again."**

Then he picks up his teddy bear,

gets back up,

and continues walking.
