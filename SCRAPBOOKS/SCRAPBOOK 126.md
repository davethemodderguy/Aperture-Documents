# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT ADDENDUM**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – RELATIONSHIP WITH ANOMALOUS ABILITIES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Contrary to initial expectations, increases in Corin Lockwood's demonstrated capabilities have **not** resulted in increased confidence or self-esteem.

Medical Services report the opposite.

The more extraordinary Corin's abilities become...

The more negatively she views herself.

---

# **CLINICAL OBSERVATION**

Personnel unfamiliar with Corin often describe her abilities using terms such as:

- Incredible.
    
- Extraordinary.
    
- Powerful.
    
- Unmatched.
    

Corin never uses those words.

Instead, every new manifestation reinforces a different conclusion in her own mind.

> "They changed me."

To Corin, each ability is not a talent.

It is evidence.

Evidence of what was done to her as a child.

---

# **RECORDED CONVERSATION**

Following another successful evaluation of her kinetic Ether abilities, Corin returned to her office unusually quiet.

Lumi eventually asked:

> "What's wrong?"

Corin looked at her own hands.

> "...They keep finding new things."

A pause.

> "More strength."

> "More Ether."

> "More things I can do."

She slowly closed her hand into a fist.

> "...Every time they discover something..."

> "...it feels like more proof."

Lumi asked softly:

> "Proof of what?"

Corin's answer came almost immediately.

> "...That they violated me."

The room became silent.

Corin continued.

> "Every report."

> "Every test."

> "Every new ability."

> "...It's another reminder that they reached inside a little girl and changed her forever."

---

# **MEDICAL NOTE**

Psychological Services emphasize that Corin does not fear becoming more dangerous.

She fears what additional abilities represent.

In her perception, every anomalous characteristic is another permanent scar left by the experimentation.

As a result, research announcements regarding newly documented abilities are carefully communicated to Corin, as they have historically resulted in increased self-loathing rather than reassurance.

---

# **LUMI'S RESPONSE**

Lumi sat beside Corin.

She did not deny what had happened.

She knew it was real.

Instead, she quietly said:

> "What they did to you was unforgivable."

Corin lowered her head.

Lumi continued.

> "But every day after that..."

> "...every choice you've made..."

> "...has belonged to you."

Corin remained silent.

Lumi gently held her daughter's hand.

> "They changed your body."

> "They never earned the right to decide who you would become."

---

# **SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION**

The residents of Project SAFEHOUSE have gradually learned not to celebrate new discoveries about Corin's abilities in front of her.

Instead, they focus on Corin herself.

Her work.

Her kindness.

Her dry sense of humor when she relaxes.

Her habit of bringing accounting reports to Belle while asking for a hug.

Within SAFEHOUSE, Corin is valued for who she is—not for what Ether allows her to do.

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the assessment and added:

> "Researchers see new data."

> "Corin sees old wounds."

He concluded:

> "If we want her to heal, we must remember that every 'breakthrough' in our understanding of her abilities may also reopen memories she has spent years trying to survive."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin continues to associate her anomalous abilities with the abuse she endured as a child.

Recovery efforts therefore prioritize helping her distinguish between **the crimes committed against her** and **the person she has chosen to be despite them**.

Her abilities remain part of her life.

They do not define her.

**End of Psychological Assessment Addendum**
