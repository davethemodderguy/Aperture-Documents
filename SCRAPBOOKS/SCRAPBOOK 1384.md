# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The final cognitive intervention did not proceed as expected.

Phoenix entered Jake's cognitive space to complete the extraction.

Jake reacted with immediate anger.

He did not attempt to negotiate.

He did not repeat his previous refusals.

Instead, Jake made what appears to have been his final attempt to prevent Phoenix from reaching him.

He attacked her.

Not with a weapon.

Not with stellar energy.

Not with physical force.

Jake attempted to forcibly eject Phoenix from his cognitive space.

The attack repeatedly missed.

Phoenix remained connected.

Jake attempted again.

And again.

Each attempt failed to remove Phoenix.

During the final attempt, Jake's cognitive form destabilized.

The static obscuring his face disappeared.

Phoenix caught him before he could fall.

At the same moment, Jake accepted the replacement core.

The cognitive connection stabilized.

Jake returned.

# 1. FINAL ENTRY

Phoenix entered the white cognitive space.

Jake was already there.

He immediately recognized her.

His expression changed.

Unlike previous encounters, he did not look tired.

He looked angry.

> **JAKE:**  
> "No."

Phoenix stopped.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "Jake—"

> **JAKE:**  
> "I said no."

Phoenix remained where she was.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "I know."

Jake's hands tightened around his teddy bear.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Get out."

Phoenix shook her head.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "I'm not leaving you here."

Jake stared at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Then I'll make you."

# 2. FIRST ATTEMPT

The white void suddenly distorted.

A wave of cognitive force moved toward Phoenix.

She braced herself.

The force passed around her.

She remained standing.

Jake looked startled.

Phoenix looked equally surprised.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "Jake..."

> **JAKE:**  
> "GO!"

Phoenix was pushed backward.

But she remained connected.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "I'm not going."

Jake became visibly angrier.

---

# 3. SECOND ATTEMPT

The entire cognitive space shook.

The white void fractured into enormous geometric distortions.

Jake raised one hand.

The environment responded.

Phoenix was thrown sideways.

She disappeared from Jake's immediate field of view—

then reappeared several meters away.

Still inside.

Still connected.

Jake stared at her.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Why won't you leave?"

Phoenix stood.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "Because you keep letting me come back."

Jake's expression tightened.

> **JAKE:**  
> "I DON'T WANT YOU HERE!"

The void shook again.

---

# 4. THE FINAL ATTEMPT

Jake's cognitive form began destabilizing.

Static spread across his body.

The white environment darkened around the edges.

Phoenix recognized what was happening.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "Jake, stop."

Jake ignored her.

He gathered everything he could still control.

This was not an attack intended to hurt Phoenix.

It was an attempt to remove her.

To force her out.

To make certain that she could not reach him again.

> **JAKE:**  
> "GO HOME!"

The cognitive space erupted.

Phoenix was struck by the full force of the expulsion.

She flew backward.

Then—

**missed.**

The boundary opened.

Phoenix passed through it.

But instead of being expelled, she remained inside.

Jake stared.

His eyes widened.

He tried again.

Nothing.

---

# 5. JAKE BEGINS TO FALL

Jake's body flickered.

The static across his face intensified.

His outline became unstable.

Phoenix immediately ran toward him.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Don't!"

His voice cracked.

> **JAKE:**  
> "Don't come here!"

Phoenix continued.

Jake tried one final time to force her away.

The attack passed completely around her.

Phoenix reached him.

Jake's knees gave out.

Phoenix caught him in her arms.

---

# 6. THE STATIC DISAPPEARS

Jake stopped struggling.

Phoenix held him carefully.

The static covering his face flickered.

Once.

Twice.

Then—

**gone.**

For the first time since Phoenix entered Jake's cognitive space, there was no distortion between them.

She could see his face.

Completely.

Jake looked terrified.

Not angry.

Not distant.

Just a six-year-old child who had finally run out of strength.

Phoenix stared at him.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "...There you are."

Jake looked up at her.

His eyes were visible.

Clear.

Azure.

No static.

He whispered:

> **JAKE:**  
> "I'm sorry."

Phoenix shook her head.

> **PHOENIX:**  
> "Don't."

---

A pulse of azure light appeared behind Jake.

> **PHOENIX:**

> **JAKE:**

He reached toward it.

---

Jake touched the replacement core.

> **JAKE:**  
> "...Yeah."
