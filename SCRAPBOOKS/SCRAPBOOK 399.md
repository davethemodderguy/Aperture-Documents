### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION LOG**

# **ORPHIE — REJECTION OF FORMER COMFORTS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Concern  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Marts  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **ACTIVE CONCERN**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie's rejection of her former sources of happiness has continued.

The behavior is no longer limited to drawing.

Orphie now appears to actively dislike objects and activities that were previously associated with positive memories.

This includes:

- Her sketchbook.
    
- Her pencils.
    
- Drawing.
    
- Personal keepsakes.
    
- Certain hobbies.
    
- Objects associated with her childhood.
    
- Items connected to her mother.
    

The most significant incident involved **her mother's cookbook**.

---

# **THE COOKBOOK**

The cookbook originally belonged to Orphie's mother.

It was brought with Orphie when she entered the Blacksite.

The cookbook contained numerous recipes that Orphie had learned from and adapted over time.

It was particularly important because cooking had become one of Orphie's favorite activities.

She prepared food for the people around her.

She made:

- Chicken, potato, and mushroom soup.
    
- Barbecue ribs.
    
- Homemade fried chicken.
    
- Birthday cakes.
    
- Meals for the SAFEHOUSE residents.
    

The cookbook was a physical connection to her mother.

---

# **THE INCIDENT**

Orphie eventually decided that she wanted to destroy it.

She took the cookbook outside.

Her intention was apparently to burn it.

No explanation was given.

She simply carried it away from the treehouse and prepared to dispose of it.

Then she stopped.

---

# **UNKNOWN INTERVENTION**

Orphie later stated:

> "...Something stopped me."

She has been unable to identify exactly what prevented her from burning the cookbook.

No external individual was observed interfering.

No anomalous entity was detected.

No physical restraint was used.

The cookbook simply remained in her hands.

After several minutes, Orphie abandoned the attempt.

---

# **THE DECISION**

Orphie did not return the cookbook to its normal place.

Instead, she sealed it away.

The cookbook is now stored in a secured container.

It is not accessible during normal activities.

Orphie has not opened it since.

---

# **CORIN'S OBSERVATION**

Corin was deeply affected by the incident.

She asked:

> "...Why didn't you burn it?"

Orphie answered:

> "...I couldn't."

Corin:

> "...Why?"

A long pause.

> "...I don't know."

Corin did not press further.

---

# **THE COOKBOOK'S SIGNIFICANCE**

The incident is notable because Orphie has demonstrated an increasingly consistent pattern:

> **She rejects the things that once made her happy, but struggles to completely destroy the things connected to the people she loved.**

The cookbook is one of the clearest examples.

She could throw away a pencil.

She could abandon her sketchbook.

She could stop drawing.

But when confronted with something directly connected to her mother, she stopped.

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

Several Marts were present during the incident.

They remained unusually quiet.

One Mart watched Orphie carry the cookbook.

Another followed at a distance.

When Orphie sealed it away, a Mart approached the container.

It placed one tiny hand against it.

> "...Mom."

Orphie looked at the Mart.

> "...Not mine."

The Mart shook its head.

> "...Your mom."

Orphie said nothing.

---

# **LUNA'S RESPONSE**

Luna was informed afterward.

She immediately understood the significance.

> "...She couldn't destroy it."

The researcher asked why she thought that mattered.

Luna replied:

> "...Because some part of her still knows what it means."

---

# **SOLARIA'S RESPONSE**

Solaria gave a similar interpretation.

> "...She wants to forget."

Pause.

> "...But she doesn't actually want to lose everything."

---

# **THE SEALED COOKBOOK**

The cookbook has been placed into secure storage.

The container has no special anomalous properties.

Its purpose is simply to keep the cookbook safe.

No one has attempted to force Orphie to open it.

No one has attempted to return it to the kitchen.

For now, it remains sealed.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE REACTION**

The residents have become increasingly careful around Orphie.

They no longer attempt to force old hobbies upon her.

They do not tell her:

> "You used to love this."

They do not pressure her to draw.

They do not insist she cook.

They do not force old memories upon her.

They simply remain present.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Orphie:**  
Emotionally detached. Increasing rejection of former sources of happiness.

**Drawing:**  
Actively disliked.

**Favorite pencil:**  
Discarded and recovered by Corin.

**Mother's cookbook:**  
Nearly destroyed. Currently sealed away.

**Marts:**  
Deeply concerned.

**Corin:**  
Highly concerned.

**Luna:**  
Concerned.

**Solaria:**  
Concerned.

**SAFEHOUSE:**  
Monitoring closely.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie has spent considerable effort removing reminders of who she used to be.

The pencil was thrown away.

The sketchbook was abandoned.

Drawing was rejected.

Things that once made her happy have become things she dislikes.

Then came the cookbook.

She wanted to burn it.

She could not.

Whatever stopped her, she has not explained.

So instead, she sealed it away.

Not destroyed.

Not cherished.

Just...

**kept.**

Perhaps because somewhere beneath the emptiness, Orphie still knows that the cookbook is not merely an object.

It is something her mother gave her.

And that is apparently one thing she still cannot bring herself to erase.

**End of Personnel Welfare / SAFEHOUSE Observation Log**
