# 1. OFFICIAL PRODUCTION COUNT

Neri has finalized the production quantity for the **FSR Deadbolt**.

There are:

> **2 FSR Deadbolts.**

No additional units are currently scheduled.

Both rifles are being reserved as prizes for a Tachyon Industries acquisition trial.

Neri has explicitly declined to distribute either rifle through ordinary armory allocation.

---

# 2. THE TRIAL

To obtain an FSR Deadbolt, an eligible participant must successfully complete the following challenge:

> **Strike a designated target positioned 25 kilometers away using a 7.62 NATO rifle.**

The challenge must be completed under controlled Blacksite range conditions.

The trial is intended to test extreme-range marksmanship rather than simply determine who can operate a Deadbolt.

Neri's reasoning was:

> **“If they want the rifle, they should prove they can use it.”**

---

# 3. TRIAL EQUIPMENT

Participants will use an authorized **7.62 NATO rifle** supplied for the trial.

The Deadbolt itself cannot be used.

Participants must complete the challenge using the designated trial rifle.

The target location and range environment are controlled by MSAC personnel.

All other operational details remain restricted to authorized range staff.

---

# 4. TARGET

The target is positioned at:

> **25,000 meters**

or:

> **25 kilometers**

from the firing position.

The target is specifically designed for the trial and monitored by range personnel.

A successful attempt requires a confirmed strike.

Near misses do not qualify.

---

# 5. AVAILABLE PRIZES

Two successful participants will receive:

### **FSR DEADBOLT — UNIT 001**

### **FSR DEADBOLT — UNIT 002**

> **THE TRIAL ENDS.**

# 12. TRIAL LIMITATIONS

The trial is subject to standard Blacksite range safety controls.

Participants must:

- use authorized equipment;
    
- follow range officer instructions;
    
- remain within designated firing areas;
    
- comply with all applicable Blacksite safety procedures;
    
- stop immediately when instructed.
    

The challenge is a controlled competition.

It is not an open-field shooting exercise.
