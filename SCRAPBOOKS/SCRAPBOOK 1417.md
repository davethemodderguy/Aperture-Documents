# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jake has returned.

The Nullwalker state has been successfully reversed.

Aeterna's sword was used to repurpose the existential purpose of the Null Shell surrounding him.

The shell ceased functioning as a containment structure.

Jake was released.

His stellar core reactivated.

His teddy bear began glowing again.

His physical manifestation returned.

However, **Jake has not fully recovered.**

He is extremely weak.

He is visibly sickly.

His body remains affected by the five weeks he spent exposed to extreme cold, deprivation, and the Nullwalker state.

Most importantly:

> **Jake cannot speak yet.**

His vocal cords were severely damaged during the blizzard.

Although his voice may eventually return, C.O.R.E. currently has no basis for predicting when.

Personnel are therefore instructed to treat Jake as **infrequently verbal with complete current inability to produce speech**, rather than expecting him to communicate verbally.

---

# 1. IMMEDIATE CONDITION

When Jake emerged from the Null Shell, he was barely able to remain upright.

His physical appearance had returned to normal.

His hair remained white.

His eyes retained their azure glow and shooting-star pattern.

His wings were present.

His teddy bear was functional.

But the resemblance to his previous healthy condition ended there.

Jake appeared:

- extremely pale;
    
- weak;
    
- underweight;
    
- physically exhausted;
    
- cold-sensitive;
    
- visibly fatigued;
    
- and generally ill.
    

His movements were slow.

He had difficulty maintaining his posture.

He did not attempt to stand without assistance.

---

# 2. VOCAL FUNCTION

Jake attempted to speak shortly after waking.

No sound was produced.

He opened his mouth.

Nothing came out.

He tried again.

Still nothing.

Jake appeared confused.

He touched his throat.

Bao immediately understood.

> **BAO:**  
> “Don't force it.”

Jake looked at her.

He nodded.

No further attempts were made.

---

# 3. CURRENT COMMUNICATION

Jake is currently communicating through:

- nodding;
    
- shaking his head;
    
- pointing;
    
- facial expressions;
    
- eye contact;
    
- hand gestures;
    
- writing when sufficiently alert;
    
- and physical proximity to trusted residents.
    

He is not required to write constantly.

He is also not expected to communicate more than necessary.

His stamina is currently too limited for prolonged interaction.

---

# 4. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT

Jake's condition is best described as:

> **STABLE, BUT EXTREMELY FRAGILE.**

His stellar nature does not make him immune to physiological consequences.

His Humanized Star manifestation is functional, but his physical body has undergone substantial stress.

The medical team has identified several areas requiring monitoring.

### Vocal System

Severe impairment.

Speech currently impossible.

### Musculoskeletal System

Significant weakness.

Jake has difficulty standing and walking for extended periods.

### Circulatory Function

Stable, but unusually slow compared with his previous baseline.

### Core Activity

Active.

Weak.

Consistent.

### Temperature Regulation

Abnormal.

Jake remains unusually sensitive to cold.

### Energy Levels

Extremely low.

Jake requires frequent rest.

---

# 5. BAO

Bao has assumed primary bedside care.

She does not leave Jake unattended for long periods.

Jake currently spends much of his recovery time either:

- resting beside Bao;
    
- sleeping;
    
- holding his teddy bear;
    
- or sitting quietly while someone familiar remains nearby.
    

Bao has stopped asking him questions that require spoken answers.

Instead:

> **BAO:**  
> “Warm enough?”

Jake nods.

> **BAO:**  
> “Hungry?”

Jake shakes his head.

> **BAO:**  
> “Tired?”

Jake nods.

Bao simply adjusts his blanket.

No further conversation is required.

---

# 6. NERI

Neri has become one of the quieter people Jake allows near him.

She does not constantly ask whether he is okay.

She simply sits nearby.

At one point, Neri placed a cup of water beside him.

Jake looked at it.

Then at her.

Neri pointed toward the cup.

Jake drank.

Afterward, he nodded.

Neri nodded back.

That was the entire interaction.

---

# 7. NORA

Nora initially attempted to speak to Jake normally.

She quickly realized he could not answer.

Her behavior changed immediately.

She now sits beside him and talks **for him.**

Jake does not appear bothered by this.

Occasionally, when Nora says something incorrect, Jake gives her a look.

Nora usually notices.

> **NORA:**  
> “What?”

Jake shakes his head.

> **NORA:**  
> “Okay, okay.”

She continues talking.

Jake closes his eyes.

---

# 8. AETERNA

Aeterna has remained nearby without demanding interaction.

She appears particularly conscious of Jake's current weakness.

She does not ask him to speak.

She does not ask him to explain what happened inside the Null Shell.

She simply remains present.

At one point, Jake reached toward her.

Aeterna immediately crouched.

He placed one hand against her armor.

Then closed his eyes.

Aeterna remained still.

No words were exchanged.

---

# 9. ANBY

Anby has been unusually quiet around Jake.

She remembers the last time she saw him before the Nullwalker event.

She also remembers forgetting him.

Jake appears aware of her presence.

When Anby entered the room, Jake looked toward her.

She hesitated.

Then quietly said:

> **ANBY:**  
> “Hi.”

Jake raised one hand.

A small wave.

Anby smiled.

That was enough.

---

# 10. JAKE'S CURRENT MOBILITY

Jake can walk.

He should not be expected to.

Even short distances can leave him exhausted.

If he needs to move, personnel should provide assistance.

This may include:

- carrying him;
    
- supporting him while walking;
    
- using appropriate mobility equipment;
    
- or allowing him to remain where he is until he has recovered enough to move.
    

Jake currently accepts assistance without much resistance.

This is considered significant.

Previously, Jake often attempted to handle problems himself.

Currently, he simply reaches for someone when he needs help.

---

# 11. SLEEP

Jake sleeps frequently.

Sometimes for several hours.

Sometimes he wakes briefly, looks around, confirms someone familiar is nearby, and goes back to sleep.

His sleep is currently considered beneficial.

Personnel should not wake him unnecessarily.

If Jake falls asleep while being carried, the carrier should continue to the intended destination unless medical circumstances require otherwise.

---

# 12. TEDDY BEAR

The teddy bear remains Jake's most consistent source of comfort.

Its core is active.

The heartbeat is weak but steady.

Jake keeps one hand on it whenever possible.

When sleeping, he generally holds it against his chest.

The scars at the joints of its left arm and right leg remain.

No attempt will be made to remove them.

They are part of the bear's history.

---

# 13. RESIDENT RESPONSE

The SAFEHOUSE residents have collectively changed their behavior around Jake.

Not dramatically.

Not through constant supervision.

Instead, they are simply more attentive.

If Jake looks toward someone:

They notice.

If he points:

They check what he needs.

If he becomes tired:

Someone helps.

If he wants to be alone:

They give him space.

If he wants company:

Someone stays.

The distinction is important.

They are not treating Jake as fragile glass.

They are simply no longer assuming:

> **“Someone else will handle it.”**

---

# 14. C.O.R.E. WARNING

Personnel are specifically instructed:

**Do not ask Jake to speak.**

Do not repeatedly ask:

> “Can you say something?”

Do not ask:

> “What happened?”

Do not make jokes about his voice.

Do not pressure him to prove that he is recovering.

Do not interpret his inability to speak as unwillingness.

His vocal system is injured.

Recovery may take time.

Possibly considerable time.

---

Jake is:

**ALIVE.**

**CONSCIOUS.**

**RESPONSIVE.**

**HOME.**

**STABLE.**

But not healthy.

Not yet.

He remains sickly and physically exhausted.

# C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jake came back.

That does not mean everything went back to normal.

He is still weak.

He still gets tired quickly.

He still sleeps more than usual.

He still needs help moving.

He still holds his teddy bear against his chest whenever he is frightened or exhausted.

And when someone asks him something, he cannot answer.

Not yet.

So Jake simply looks at them.

Nods.

Shakes his head.

Points.

Reaches for a hand.

And sometimes—

He just closes his eyes.

The residents have learned to understand him without demanding words.

For now, that is enough.

He doesn't need to prove that he's back.

He's here.

And this time,

**everyone knows it.**
