# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### RESIDENT WELFARE & MSAC OPERATIONS DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-CAMP-299  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — SAFEHOUSE / MSAC  
**STATUS:** CLOSED — RECREATIONAL INCIDENT  
**SUBJECT:** Joint SAFEHOUSE / MSAC Camping Trip  
**INCIDENT:** Unauthorized Use of Hell Fire for Campfire Ignition

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

SAFEHOUSE residents and selected MSAC personnel conducted a recreational camping trip outside the Blacksite.

The trip was intended to provide:

- recreational time;
    
- social interaction;
    
- outdoor activity;
    
- food preparation;
    
- rest;
    
- and an opportunity for personnel to spend several days away from standard Blacksite operations.
    

The event proceeded without significant incident.

Except for one.

At approximately **18:42**, MSAC Operator **Elsher** used his authorized:

> **HF-001 — Hell Fire**

to start the campfire.

The resulting fire was established almost immediately.

C.O.R.E. personnel have confirmed that the method was:

> **Extremely effective.**

It was also entirely unnecessary.

---

# 2. PARTICIPANTS

The camping group consisted of selected SAFEHOUSE residents and MSAC personnel.

Notable attendees included:

- **Elsher**
    
- **Jake**
    
- **MSAC Alice**
    
- **SAFEHOUSE Alice**
    
- **Neri**
    
- **Jane Doe**
    
- **Bao**
    
- **Frost**
    
- additional SAFEHOUSE residents and MSAC personnel
    

Neri attended in her normal form.

She brought:

- her coat;
    
- glasses;
    
- coffee;
    
- several books;
    
- and absolutely no apparent intention of participating in outdoor activities.
    

She nevertheless remained with the group.

---

# 3. CAMP SETUP

The initial setup proceeded normally.

Tents were established.

Sleeping areas were arranged.

Food was prepared.

Wood was gathered for the campfire.

The only problem was that the wood was:

> **Extremely difficult to ignite.**

Multiple conventional attempts failed.

The group eventually had a respectable pile of firewood.

They did not, however, have a fire.

---

# 4. THE SOLUTION

Elsher was standing nearby.

He looked at the wood.

Then at his Hell Fire.

Then back at the wood.

Jane immediately noticed.

> **JANE:**  
> “Don't.”

Elsher looked at her.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “I didn't say anything.”

> **JANE:**  
> “You were thinking very loudly.”

Elsher raised the Hell Fire.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “It'll work.”

Bao immediately turned around.

> **BAO:**  
> “Elsher.”

> **ELSHER:**  
> “I'll be careful.”

> **BAO:**  
> “That's not what I asked.”

---

# 5. CAMPFIRE IGNITION

Elsher used the Hell Fire's authorized ammunition.

The resulting ignition was immediate.

The wood caught.

Then burned.

Then burned considerably more.

Everyone watching became silent.

The fire was:

**Large.**

Very large.

---

# 10. FOOD PREPARATION

The campfire was subsequently used for its intended purpose:

**Cooking.**

This proved significantly more reasonable.

The group prepared:

- grilled meat;
    
- vegetables;
    
- bread;
    
- marshmallows;
    
- and assorted camping food.
    

Jane attempted to cook.

Bao supervised.

Frost assisted.

Alice attempted to steal food.

MSAC Alice stopped her.

Jake discovered that marshmallows can, in fact, be burned.

Neri quietly drank coffee.

Elsher sat beside the fire he had created.

---

# 11. THE HELL FIRE QUESTION

At approximately 20:15, MSAC personnel began discussing whether the Hell Fire should become an accepted camping tool.

C.O.R.E. immediately rejected this proposal.

The following statement was issued:

> **HELL FIRE IS NOT A CAMPING UTENSIL.**

A second statement followed:

> **HELL FIRE IS NOT A FIRE STARTER.**

A third was added after several minutes:

> **PLEASE STOP ASKING.**

---

# 12. ELSHER'S DEFENSE

Elsher was asked whether he regretted using HF-001 to start the fire.

He answered:

> **“No.”**

When asked whether he would do it again:

> **“Probably.”**

When asked whether he understood why C.O.R.E. considered the decision excessive:

> **“Yes.”**

When asked whether he had a normal lighter available:

> **“Yes.”**

Long pause.

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “Then why?”

Elsher looked toward the fire.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “It worked very effectively.”

The interview was terminated.

---

After sunset, the group gathered around the fire.

The atmosphere became considerably calmer.

People talked.

MSAC Alice sat nearby.

Frost objected.

Neri remained on the edge of the group.

She did not participate in the conversation.

Nobody attempted to pull her into it.

She simply sat there.

Coffee in hand.

Fire in front of her.

People nearby.

---

# 14. OBSERVATION

At approximately 23:10, Jane noticed Neri staring at the fire.

> **JANE:**  
> “You okay?”

Neri nodded.

> **JANE:**  
> “Warm enough?”

Neri nodded again.

Jane left it there.

Neri continued watching the flames.

This was considered appropriate.

---

# 15. INCIDENT CONCLUSION

The Hell Fire was not damaged.

No participant was injured.

No tents were burned.

No trees were damaged.

The campfire remained controlled.

The camping trip therefore concluded without significant safety incidents.

C.O.R.E. nevertheless recommends that future camping equipment include:

- matches;
    
- lighters;
    
- proper fire starters;
    
- dry wood;
    
- and, if absolutely necessary, literally anything other than an experimental Tachyon Industries shotgun.
