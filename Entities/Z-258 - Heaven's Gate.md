## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Z-258, designated “Heaven's Gate,” is an anomalous door whose appearance resembles a divine gateway believed to lead to Heaven or the Starlands.

Despite its appearance and designation, Z-258 does not lead to the Starlands.

Any individual or entity that enters or makes contact with the gate is killed by a phenomenon identified as Neurotrauma after five seconds of contact.

The object was discovered in the prop room of an amusement park, where it had been left unused.

Due to its lethal anomalous properties, Z-258 has been assigned the classification ADVERSARY.

## 2. DISCOVERY

Z-258 was discovered inside an amusement park's prop room. At the time of discovery, the door had been left unused.

Its appearance resembled a divine entrance to Heaven or the Starlands, prompting the designation Heaven's Gate.

Despite this appearance, the object does not provide access to the Starlands.

Z-258 exhibits a lethal effect associated with Neurotrauma.

- Trigger: Entry into or contact with the gate.
    
- Time to fatality: Five seconds of contact.
    
- Outcome: Death of the affected individual or entity.
    
- Destination: The gate does not lead to the Starlands.
    

No additional properties have been established in this document.

## 4. THREAT ASSESSMENT

Threat Level: ADVERSARY

Z-258 is classified as ADVERSARY due to its lethal anomalous properties. Its outward appearance suggests a divine gateway, but its actual function is associated with a fatal outcome.

Personnel must not interpret the object's appearance or designation as evidence of safety.

## 5. CONCLUSION

Z-258 remains an anomalous door of unknown origin, distinguished by its deceptive appearance and lethal Neurotraumatic effect.

Its designation, Heaven's Gate, refers exclusively to its appearance and the impression it creates. It is not evidence of a connection to Heaven or the Starlands.
