# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. — CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### ANOMALOUS OBJECTS DIVISION

DOCUMENT: Z-357 — “Aegis” CLASSIFICATION: SAFE CONTAINMENT STATUS: CONTAINED THREAT LEVEL: SAFE SUBJECT: Five Anomalous Doors Connecting to Separate Dimensions

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Z-357, collectively designated “Aegis,” is a group of five anomalous doors, designated Z-357-A through Z-357-E. Each door provides access to a different dimension, with the properties and conditions of each destination varying significantly.

The objects were discovered following multiple cases of disappearances within a prestigious subdivision. Aperture Industries subsequently identified the anomalous properties of five doors located within the area.

All five doors were detached from their respective houses and transported to the Blacksite for research.

Z-357's teleportation properties remain functional provided the respective door remains intact.

Despite the potentially hazardous nature of several destinations, the collective classification assigned to Z-357 is SAFE.

## 2. CONTAINMENT AND OPERATIONAL STATUS

- All five doors are currently located at the Blacksite.
    
- The teleportation ability of each door remains functional while the door is intact.
    
- Z-357-A cannot be exited without using the Z-357-A door.
    
- Expeditions into Z-357-B have been halted due to environmental hazards.
    
- Z-357-C is currently used as a therapeutic environment.
    
- Z-357-D's destination information is classified.
    
- Z-357-E is designated for immediate extermination.
    

## 3. SUB-ENTITY INDEX

## Z-357-A

UNKNOWN

Destination: Apartment complex

Z-357-A leads to an apartment complex whose details remain unknown. Individuals entering this dimension cannot exit through any means other than returning through the Z-357-A door.

Operational concern: The inability to exit without the corresponding door.

## Z-357-B

EXPEDITIONS HALTED

Destination: Swamp

The dimension accessible through Z-357-B contains airborne neurotoxin at concentrations that have resulted in the suspension of further expeditions.

The neurotoxin possesses the ability to bypass protective suits, rendering existing protective measures insufficient for safe exploration.

Operational directive: Expeditions remain halted.

## Z-357-C

THERAPEUTIC USE

Destination: Floating island

Z-357-C leads to a floating island. Individuals inside the dimension experience a sense of serenity and calmness.

Aperture Industries currently uses this destination as a therapeutic method.

Operational status: Active therapeutic use.

## Z-357-D

REDACTED

Destination: ██████████ ███ ██████ ███████

## Z-357-E

EXTERMINATION ORDER

Destination: Alternate universe

Z-357-E leads to an alternate universe in which Lucy Lucious, Director Lucious's missing sister, has taken control of Earth using the power and resources of Aperture Industries.

The circumstances of this alternate universe constitute the stated basis for the immediate extermination directive associated with Z-357-E.

Operational directive: Z-357-E is to be exterminated immediately.

## 4. DISCOVERY

Following numerous disappearance cases within a prestigious subdivision, Aperture Industries investigated the affected area and discovered five doors exhibiting anomalous properties.

The doors were identified as Z-357-A through Z-357-E. Subsequent examination established that each door provided access to a different dimension.

Aperture Industries detached the doors from their respective houses and transported them to the Blacksite for further research.

The doors' teleportation properties remained functional after transportation, provided that the doors remained intact.

## 5. INVESTIGATION NOTES

The five destinations differ substantially in their known environmental conditions and operational implications.

Z-357-A presents an exit restriction. Z-357-B presents an environmental hazard capable of bypassing protective suits. Z-357-C provides an environment currently used for therapy. Information regarding Z-357-D remains redacted. Z-357-E provides access to an alternate universe associated with Director Lucious's missing sister.

No additional properties or connections between the five destinations are established in this document.
