# **Overview**

Z-885, designated **Taiga Sunbringer**, is a Sunbringer residing at APRT-70 "Heaven's Blacksite."

Originally rescued and brought to Aperture Industries following the death of her mother, Taiga has since become one of the Blacksite's most recognizable residents due to her cheerful personality, unwavering loyalty, and tendency to unintentionally cause engineering problems that Neko inevitably has to repair.

She is the older sister of **Mart Sunbringer** and considers every resident of Room N-07 part of her family.

---

# **Physical Description**

Taiga appears as a young leopard-human hybrid.

Notable characteristics include:

- Leopard ears and tail.
    
- Blonde hair.
    
- Athletic build.
    
- Golden eyes.
    
- Can transform into a full leopard.
    
- Possesses a pair of white Sunbringer wings.
    

Unlike most winged residents, Taiga's wings are primarily designed to enhance aerial mobility rather than sustained flight.

---

# **Personality**

Taiga is:

- Cheerful.
    
- Energetic.
    
- Compassionate.
    
- Protective.
    
- Impulsive.
    
- Stubborn.
    
- Surprisingly brave.
    

She has a habit of acting before thinking, especially when someone she cares about is in danger.

Despite occasional recklessness, she possesses remarkable emotional intelligence and is often the first person to comfort distressed residents.

---

# **Anomalous Biology**

## Leopard Transformation

Taiga can voluntarily transform into a leopard.

Applications include:

- High-speed movement.
    
- Enhanced senses.
    
- Improved agility.
    
- Close-quarters mobility.
    

---

## Sunbringer Wings

Unlike Mart's naturally effortless flight, Taiga's wings function differently.

Characteristics include:

- Cannot sustain true powered flight.
    
- Designed to work alongside her unique aerial mobility.
    
- Combined with repeated air jumps, they allow controlled gliding.
    
- Excellent maneuverability over long distances.
    
- Highly efficient during descents.
    

Lucy later trained Endmin to fly, but Endmin eventually adopted gliding as her preferred method—something that delighted Taiga.

---

## Air Jumps

Taiga possesses the unique ability to perform repeated mid-air jumps.

Each jump provides enough momentum for her wings to convert upward force into extended glides.

This combination makes Taiga exceptionally agile despite lacking sustained flight.

---

# **Psychological Profile**

Taiga previously suffered severe trauma related to her mother's death, abandonment, and gunfire.

Following reconciliation with Mart and continued support from Room N-07, these symptoms have resolved.

Current psychological evaluations indicate healthy emotional recovery.

She remains deeply protective of those she considers family.

---

# **Relationship with Mart**

Mart is Taiga's younger brother.

Their relationship has endured periods of severe emotional strain, particularly after Mart's psychological decline and recurring nightmares caused him to distance himself from her.

Following a lengthy reconciliation, the siblings rebuilt their bond.

Current observations include:

- Frequent training together.
    
- Regular meals together.
    
- Friendly arguments.
    
- Shared interest in firearms.
    
- Mutual emotional support.
    

Personnel are instructed to notify Taiga if Mart isolates himself for unusually long periods.

She remains the individual most capable of helping him through emotional episodes.
