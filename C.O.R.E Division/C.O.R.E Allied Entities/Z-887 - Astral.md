# **Z-887 — "ASTRAL"**

**Entity ID:** Z-887  
**Designation:** Astral  
**Threat Level:** Blue (Friendly)  
**Containment Status:** Resident – Room N-07 (Ghost Entity)  
**Division:** C.O.R.E. Residential Personnel

---

# **Overview**

Z-887, designated **Astral**, is a friendly ghost entity residing at APRT-70 "Heaven's Blacksite."

Before her death, Astral served as the adoptive mother and guardian of **Taiga Sunbringer** and **Mart Sunbringer**, raising the siblings after the death of their biological mother.

Astral was later murdered in an armed attack while protecting the children. Aperture Industries eventually identified and eliminated those responsible before Astral had the opportunity to seek revenge herself.

Following her revival as a ghost through anomalous means, Astral chose not to pass on, instead remaining with the family she had devoted her life to protecting.

She is considered one of the emotional pillars of Room N-07.

---

# **Physical Description**

Astral appears as a young adult woman.

Characteristics include:

- Long silver-white hair.
    
- Gentle golden eyes.
    
- Ethereal body composed of stable spiritual energy.
    
- Usually wears light-colored clothing.
    
- Semi-transparent when desired.
    
- Able to freely phase through most solid objects.
    

Unlike hostile spectral anomalies, Astral's presence is consistently warm and calming.

---

# **Personality**

Astral is:

- Gentle.
    
- Patient.
    
- Compassionate.
    
- Protective.
    
- Motherly.
    
- Soft-spoken.
    
- Emotionally resilient.
    

She rarely becomes angry.

When she does, it is almost always because someone has threatened her family.

---

# **History**

After the death of Taiga and Mart's biological mother, Astral became their guardian.

She raised both children during one of the most difficult periods of their lives.

Years later, she was ambushed and fatally shot directly in front of Taiga.

Mart also witnessed the event.

The trauma left permanent emotional scars on both siblings.

Astral herself remembers the incident clearly, though she no longer seeks revenge.

According to Astral:

> "Hatred wouldn't bring me back."

---

# **Ghost Physiology**

Astral currently exists as a stable ghost.

Capabilities include:

- Phasing through walls.
    
- Levitation.
    
- Immunity to conventional physical injury.
    
- Voluntary visibility.
    
- Spiritual interaction with physical objects.
    
- Stable communication with living individuals.
    

She is capable of physical contact when she wishes, allowing her to hug, comfort, or assist others normally.

---

# **Combat Ability**

Astral rarely participates in direct combat.

When necessary, she supports teammates through:

- Reconnaissance.
    
- Spiritual guidance.
    
- Civilian evacuation.
    
- Protecting non-combatants.
    

She prefers avoiding violence whenever possible.

---

# **Psychological Notes**

Astral displays remarkable emotional stability despite remembering her own violent death.

However, discussions involving:

- Her murder,
    
- The children's childhood,
    
- Taiga's scar,
    
- Mart's nightmares,
    

can visibly affect her.

She remains deeply concerned about Mart's deteriorating sleep and frequently checks on him during the night.

## **Addendum — Family**

One evening, a researcher asked Taiga a simple question.

> **"Who's actually related to who in Room N-07?"**

Taiga laughed.

She looked around the room.

Mart was reading.

Endmin was maintaining her rifle.

Star and Frost were discussing flowers.

Nightshade sat quietly nearby.

Astral was making tea.

Taiga shrugged.

> **"I don't know anymore."**

She smiled.

> **"...We're just family."**
