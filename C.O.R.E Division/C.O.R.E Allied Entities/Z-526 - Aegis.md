**Aegis** are a class of **high-authority entities** within C.O.R.E operations, designated for control, stabilization, and execution of large-scale system functions.

---

**Internal Classification Code:** **Z-526**  
_(Framework designation — does not overwrite individual entity IDs)_

Aegis operate at an elevated command level:

- **Above:** Archangels, Z-565-C (“The Devil”)
    
- **Below:** Daniel (Primary Authority)
    

---

> **Top-tier operational authorities with domain-level control, second only to primary command**

Each Aegis is assigned a **core aspect (“Binding”)**.

---

- Cannot surpass Daniel-level authority

Aegis serve as:
