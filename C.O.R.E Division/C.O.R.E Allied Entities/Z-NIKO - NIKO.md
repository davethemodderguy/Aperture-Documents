# **II. APPEARANCE**

### Standard Form

Niko appears as a translucent azure-colored humanoid resembling Snowy's former appearance.

Characteristics include:

- Soft blue-white glow
    
- Semi-transparent body
    
- Ectoplasmic composition
    
- Visible spectral particles during emotional stress
    
- Permanent collision capability
    

Unlike early post-separation records, Niko is now capable of physically interacting with the environment.

---

### Physical Properties

Niko possesses a pseudo-physical body composed primarily of stabilized ectoplasm.

This allows:

- Walking normally
    
- Holding objects
    
- Eating and drinking
    
- Physical contact
    
- Sleeping
    

Consumed materials are converted directly into ectoplasmic mass.

---

### Transformation Status

Unavailable.

Niko permanently lost access to all previous transformation forms.

Lost forms include:

- Wolf Form
    
- Hybrid Wolf Form
    
- Other Snowy-derived transformations
    

Repeated attempts have failed.

Current explanation remains unknown.

---

# **III. PERSONALITY**

Niko is generally considered the calmer of the two brothers.

Common traits include:

- Thoughtful
    
- Reflective
    
- Empathetic
    
- Protective
    
- Patient
    
- Emotionally intelligent
    

---

Unlike Neko, Niko tends to think before acting.

This distinction has saved Whitefall considerable property damage.

---

### Additional Traits

- Gives unsolicited advice
    
- Frequently worries about others
    
- Sometimes overthinks situations
    
- Often acts as emotional support for residents

# **V. SPECIAL ABILITIES**

## Spectral Composition

Niko's body consists primarily of stabilized ectoplasm.

Capabilities include:

- Partial intangibility
    
- Low-density movement
    
- Spectral regeneration
    
- Ectoplasmic restructuring
    

---

## Emotional Sensitivity

Niko demonstrates exceptional awareness of emotional states.

He is often capable of recognizing distress before others notice.

---

## Azure Glitter

Under significant emotional overload, Niko may release spectral particles designated:

### Azure Glitter

Effects include:

- Mild emotional calming
    
- Reduced stress
    
- Temporary comfort sensations
    

---

Niko strongly dislikes this phenomenon due to its association with earlier depressive episodes.

---

## Enhanced Memory Retention

Niko inherited the majority of Snowy's long-term memories.

This makes him the primary historical source regarding pre-separation events.
