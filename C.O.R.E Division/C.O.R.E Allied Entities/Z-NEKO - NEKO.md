# **V. SPECIAL ABILITIES**

## Morphosis

Acquired through training with Star.

Allows voluntary shifting between:

- Human/Cat Hybrid
    
- Scottish Fold
    
- Human/Wolf Hybrid
    
- Wolf
    

Forms may be partially combined.

---

## Enhanced Sensory Processing

Cat-derived senses grant:

- Improved hearing
    
- Improved reaction speed
    
- Enhanced environmental awareness
    

---

## Miosis Response

Unique involuntary and voluntary pupil constriction behavior.

Triggers include:

- Anger
    
- Annoyance
    
- Excessive comfort
    
- Bright light exposure
    
- Intentional intimidation
    

---

Observed Effect:

Produces extreme discomfort in approximately 99% of individuals.

Exceptions:

- Z-NIKO
    
- Star Dawnbringer
    

---

## Scientific Innovation Capability

Neko demonstrates unusually high rates of technological and chemical innovation.

Multiple Aperture systems currently originate from Neko's research.

This ability is considered anomalous due to the frequency of successful breakthroughs.

---

# **VI. LIGHT SENSITIVITY**

One of Neko's most notable weaknesses.

Bright lights can trigger:

- Immediate eye pain
    
- Temporary blindness (~3 seconds)
    
- Automatic miosis response
    

No permanent damage has ever been observed.

No adaptation has been observed despite repeated exposure.

---

# **VII. NOTABLE INCIDENTS**

## Whitefall EMP Event

While conducting chemical experimentation, Neko accidentally discovered an EMP-generating reaction.

Result:

- Destruction of all active lighting systems within Whitefall Cabin.
    
- Temporary facility-wide blackout.
    
- Emergency flashlight usage for one week.
    

---

Following this incident:

- Laboratory shielding upgraded.
    
- EMP-resistant systems installed.
    
- CORE-NEKO-PROT-01 established.
    

---

## Innovation Contributions

Neko's inventions, discoveries, and research contributions have significantly benefited Aperture Industries.

Achievements include:

- Multiple chemical advancements
    
- Laboratory safety improvements
    
- Research methodologies
    
- Experimental containment upgrades
    

These accomplishments directly resulted in his promotion to a named Z-class designation.
