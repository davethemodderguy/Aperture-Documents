**Entity ID:** Z-886

---

# **Overview**

Z-886, designated **Mart Sunbringer**, is a Sunbringer residing at APRT-70 "Heaven's Blacksite."

Mart is the younger brother of Z-885 (Taiga Sunbringer) and one of the youngest residents of Room N-07. Once known for his quiet confidence, intelligence, and calm demeanor, Mart underwent severe psychological deterioration following years of accumulated trauma and his inability to forget painful memories.

Despite this, he remains one of the Blacksite's kindest residents. His desire to protect others has never diminished, even if he often struggles to protect himself.

---

# **Physical Description**

Mart appears as a young owl-human hybrid.

Notable characteristics include:

- Brown owl ears.
    
- Brown owl tail.
    
- Soft brown hair.
    
- Golden eyes.
    
- Can transform into a full owl.
    
- Possesses brilliant azure-white **Sunbringer Wings of Light**.
    

Unlike his owl transformation, Mart can manifest his wings while remaining in human form.

---

# **Personality**

Mart is:

- Quiet.
    
- Gentle.
    
- Highly intelligent.
    
- Observant.
    
- Extremely polite.
    
- Selfless.
    
- Emotionally sensitive.
    

He has a habit of apologizing, even when unnecessary.

He fears burdening others and often believes he is an inconvenience despite reassurance from those around him.

---

# **Psychological Evaluation**

## Memory

Mart possesses an anomalous autobiographical memory.

He remembers virtually every significant event of his life with extraordinary clarity.

Positive memories remain vivid.

Unfortunately...

So do traumatic ones.

Repeated recollection of childhood events has contributed significantly to his current psychological condition.

---

## Trauma

Mart witnessed numerous traumatic events during childhood, including:

- The death of his mother.
    
- Taiga wandering alone through the snow.
    
- The aftermath leading to Taiga's rescue by Aperture Industries.
    

Although much of these events faded for Taiga and Astral over time, Mart remembers them in painful detail.

---

## Nightmares

Mart suffers from severe chronic nightmares.

Recurring themes include:

- Losing Taiga.
    
- Being abandoned.
    
- Childhood trauma.
    
- Watching loved ones die.
    
- Reliving past tragedies.
    

The nightmares frequently prevent meaningful sleep.

---

## Insomnia

Mart experiences severe chronic insomnia.

Episodes range from sleeping only a few hours to remaining awake for entire nights following particularly severe nightmares.

Medical intervention has provided only limited improvement.

Personnel are advised to avoid waking Mart abruptly after nightmares unless necessary.

---

## Emotional Episodes

During periods of acute emotional distress:

- Mart no longer transforms into his owl form.
    
- Instead, he instinctively runs away while crying.
    
- He often isolates himself inside locked rooms.
    
- Extended isolation is discouraged.
    

Should Mart fail to respond for an extended period, personnel are instructed to notify Taiga immediately.

---

# **Transformation**

## Owl Form

Mart retains the ability to transform into a full owl.

Originally, this form was frequently used for:

- Transportation.
    
- Observation.
    
- Emotional regulation.
    

Following worsening psychological health, Mart rarely transforms.

During panic attacks, the transformation no longer occurs automatically.

---

## Wings of Light

Mart possesses radiant Sunbringer wings composed of concentrated light.

Characteristics include:

- True powered flight.
    
- Exceptional maneuverability.
    
- Significant lifting capability.
    
- High durability.
    
- Able to comfortably support another person during flight.
    

These wings remain functional while Mart is fully human.

## **Addendum — The Photograph**

The wall of Room N-07 holds a neatly framed photograph.

Mart is smiling.

Taiga is laughing on his back.

His radiant Wings of Light support them both in flight.

The photograph replaced an older picture that had once been torn apart during one of the darkest periods of Mart's life.

When asked why he keeps the new one so carefully framed, Mart smiled softly.

> **"Because... this one is how I want to remember us."**
