### **Z-1932 — Codename: “Paranoia’s Box”**

**Classification:** Contained Anomalous Object  
**Threat Level:** Moderate (Psychological Hazard)  
**Status:** Secured / Inert-Active

---

### **OVERVIEW**

Z-1932, designated **“Paranoia’s Box,”** is an indestructible Soviet-era container of unknown origin that continuously emits a **green, psychoactive gas** from a permanently cracked lid.

Despite extensive testing, the box:

- **Cannot be opened**
- **Cannot be damaged**
- **Cannot be analyzed internally**

Its primary hazard lies in its **gas-induced phobia manifestation effect**.

---

### **DISCOVERY**

**Year:** 2016  
**Location:** “Cult of Noli” Compound, Rosebud, Montana

Z-1932 was recovered during a raid on a hostile cult encampment.

Key findings:

- Object heavily guarded by cult members
- All members **committed suicide** prior to capture
- No interrogation data recovered

N.O.S.T. later conducted an interview with **Noli**, confirming:

> He had **no knowledge** of the box or its purpose

Conclusion:

> **No confirmed link between Z-1932 and the Cult of Noli**

---

### **ORIGIN**

Material analysis indicates:

- **Soviet manufacture**
- Estimated origin: **~1932**

Exact source location:

> **Unknown**

---

### **ANOMALOUS EFFECT — GAS EXPOSURE**

Z-1932 emits a **green gaseous substance** with the following properties:

- Not immediately toxic
- **Psychologically active upon inhalation**

---

### **INDUCED CONDITIONS**

Exposure results in forced development of the following phobias:

- **Arachnophobia** (fear of spiders)
- **Cleithrophobia** (fear of being trapped)
- **Astrophobia** (fear of celestial bodies/space)
- **Erythrophobia** (fear of the color red)
- **Cherophobia** (fear of happiness)
- **Dystychiphobia** (fear of accidents)
- **Capnophobia** (fear of smoke)
- **Ommetaphobia** (fear of eyes)

---

### **EFFECT BEHAVIOR**

- Phobias remain **dormant until triggered**
- Reaction intensity scales with **gas exposure level**
- Effects are **temporary**, dissipating after several days without further exposure

Example:

> Subject develops arachnophobia → reaction only occurs when encountering spiders

---

### **CONTAINMENT PROTOCOL**

Z-1932 is stored in a **sealed containment chamber** equipped with:

- **Dual high-capacity ventilation fans**
- Continuous gas extraction system
- Airtight disposal chamber for captured fumes

---

### **FAILURE SCENARIO**

If ventilation fails:

- Gas will **gradually spread throughout the Blacksite**
- Personnel-wide exposure expected
- Resulting in:
    - Mass psychological instability
    - Operational inefficiency
    - Panic-induced incidents

---

### **STRUCTURAL PROPERTIES**

Z-1932 exhibits **complete physical invulnerability**

- Exterior: Undamageable
- Binding ropes: Undamageable and immovable
- Interior: **Invisible to X-ray imaging**

---
