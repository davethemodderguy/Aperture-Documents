# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

**C.O.R.E DIVISION — MUNITION REGISTRY**  
**SECTION: SIDEARM SYSTEMS**  
**Classification:** INTERNAL / WEAPON SYSTEM INDEX

---

## **OVERVIEW**

Sidearm calibers represent the **last line of defense** within Aperture’s combat doctrine. Compact, rapid, and adaptable, these munitions are designed for:

- Close-quarters engagement
- Emergency response scenarios
- Backup weapon systems across all personnel classes
- Integration with compact and utility-focused firearms

Despite their smaller size, sidearm rounds often carry **specialized enhancements** that allow them to scale beyond their physical limitations.

---

## **STANDARD ROUNDS**

---

### **9ss**

**Designation:** S-9SS  
**Classification:** Standard Sidearm Round

A typical pistol round.

Properties:

- Lightweight and highly reliable
- Low recoil
- Moderate stopping power

Operational Note:

> Efficient, predictable, and widely used across all non-specialized personnel.

---

### **.45 NOS**

**Designation:** S-45-NOS  
**Classification:** Heavy Sidearm Round

A heavier round than 9ss.

Properties:

- Increased stopping power
- Slower velocity compared to lighter rounds
- Improved target impact force

Usage:

- General-purpose sidearms
- Situations requiring stronger kinetic force

---

### **5.7×28ss**

**Designation:** S-57-NOS  
**Classification:** High-Velocity PDW Round

Used primarily in SMGs and PDWs.

Deployment:

- Deep Depths Security Units
- Rapid response and high-mobility personnel

Properties:

- High velocity
- Armor-piercing tendencies
- Reduced recoil

---

### **4.6×30ss**

**Designation:** S-46-NOS  
**Classification:** Compact High-Velocity Round

Functionally similar to 5.7×28ss.

Deployment:

- Internal Tram System Security Personnel

Properties:

- Compact casing
- High penetration-to-size ratio
- Optimized for controlled environments

---

### **.50 SA**

**Designation:** S-50-SA  
**Classification:** High-Caliber Sidearm Round

Used exclusively by high-authority personnel.

Properties:

- Extreme recoil
- Exceptional stopping power
- Capable of one-shot neutralization

Usage:

- Administrators
- High-level enforcement roles

Operational Note:

> “One shot, one kill” doctrine enforced.

---

### **10ss**

**Designation:** S-10SS  
**Classification:** Penetration-Optimized Round

Designed for revolver platforms and precision sidearms.

Properties:

- Enhanced penetration
- Reduced spread
- Maintains accuracy under stress

Usage:

- Operators preferring revolver systems
- Precision sidearm engagements

---

## **SPECIAL ROUNDS**

---

### **Leviathan’s Call**

**Designation:** SP-LEV-C  
**Classification:** Electrical Disruption

A shocking encounter.

Effects:

- Discharges electrical energy upon impact
- Disrupts electronic systems
- Effective against energy-based or mechanized targets

---

### **Inferno**

**Designation:** SP-INF  
**Classification:** Incendiary

Designed to ignite targets upon impact.

Effects:

- Sustained combustion
- Area ignition potential
- Effective for denial and suppression

---

### **Anti-Anomaly**

**Designation:** SP-ANA  
**Classification:** Anomaly Interaction

A specialized round designed to interact with entities unaffected by conventional weaponry.

Effects:

- Forces temporary interaction between target and physical reality
- Allows kinetic damage application to anomalous entities
- Enables engagement with otherwise untouchable targets


---

### **Frostbite**

**Designation:** SP-FRB  
**Classification:** Cryogenic Disruption

Effects:

- Rapid localized freezing upon impact
- Slows or immobilizes targets
- Causes material brittleness

---

### **Null Pulse**

**Designation:** SP-NLP  
**Classification:** Suppression / Energy Disruption

Effects:

- Emits a localized nullifying pulse
- Temporarily disrupts anomalous abilities
- Reduces energy-based output in targets

---

### **Echo Round**

**Designation:** SP-ECH  
**Classification:** Tracking / Rebound

Effects:

- Retains directional memory after firing
- Can slightly adjust trajectory mid-flight
- Improves hit probability in complex environments

---

### **Vortex**

**Designation:** SP-VRX  
**Classification:** Kinetic Distortion

Effects:

- Creates a localized gravitational distortion on impact
- Pulls nearby objects toward point of contact
- Can disrupt formations and spacing

---

### **Phantom**

**Designation:** SP-PHM  
**Classification:** Stealth / Penetration

Effects:

- Minimizes detection signatures
- Reduced auditory and visual trace
- Increased penetration through cover

---

## **FINAL NOTES**

Sidearm munitions, while compact, serve as **highly adaptable systems** capable of:

- Suppression
- Precision
- Anomaly interaction
- Environmental control

Their modular nature ensures compatibility with both standard and experimental platforms, making them an essential component of **C.O.R.E Division combat readiness**.
