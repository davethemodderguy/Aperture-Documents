# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

**C.O.R.E DIVISION — MUNITION REGISTRY**  
**SECTION: 12 GAUGE SYSTEMS**  
**Classification:** INTERNAL / WEAPON SYSTEM INDEX

---

### **OVERVIEW**

The **12 Gauge Platform** represents one of the most versatile ammunition calibers within the C.O.R.E arsenal. Its modularity allows for extreme variation in payload, ranging from baseline kinetic loads to highly specialized, anomalous, and experimental munitions.

Compatibility extends across:

- Standard shotguns
- Automatic shot systems
- Heavy machine systems (ARTEMIST-15 and derivatives)
- Warcasket-integrated weapon platforms

All 12 Gauge munitions are standardized in casing geometry but may vary significantly in:

- Internal composition
- Payload density
- Activation mechanics
- Target interaction profile

---

## **REGISTRY ENTRIES**

---

### **12 Gauge — Standard Ballistic**

**Designation:** C-12G-STD  
**Classification:** Baseline Ammunition

The standard 12 Gauge shell consists of a conventional powder charge and lead-based shot or slug.

Function:

- Reliable kinetic force delivery
- Effective against unarmored and lightly armored targets
- Serves as the control baseline for all other 12 Gauge variants

Despite its simplicity, this round remains one of the most widely deployed munitions due to its **predictable behavior and cost efficiency**.

---

### **12 Gauge — Steel Slug**

**Designation:** C-12G-STS  
**Classification:** High-Density Kinetic

A hardened steel projectile engineered for enhanced penetration.

Properties:

- Increased structural density over lead-based slugs
- Reduced deformation upon impact
- Improved armor penetration capability

Intended use:

- Medium armor engagement
- Structural breach points
- Reinforced target suppression

---

### **12 Gauge — Tungsten Slug**

**Designation:** C-12G-TGS  
**Classification:** Ultra-Density Kinetic

A precision-machined tungsten slug designed for extreme penetration.

Properties:

- Significantly higher mass-to-volume ratio
- Maintains velocity over extended range
- Capable of penetrating heavy armor plating and reinforced barriers

Operational note:

- Recoil intensity is substantially increased
- Requires stabilized platforms or Warcasket support systems

---

### **12 Gauge — Shredder**

**Designation:** C-12G-SHRD  
**Classification:** Armor Disruption / Area Control

A specialized shell containing **rhombus-prism-shaped micro-pellets** engineered for maximum shredding efficiency.

Mechanics:

- Pellets accelerate upon separation
- Angular geometry increases surface disruption
- Cutting efficiency scales with distance traveled

Effects:

- At range: Increased velocity and kinetic dispersion
- At close range: Extreme localized tearing and fragmentation

Result:

> Targets are subjected to **progressive structural disassembly**, making this round highly effective against both organic and armored entities.

---

### **12 Gauge — Dominator**

**Designation:** C-12G-DOM  
**Classification:** High-Cycle Combat Ammunition

Developed specifically for integration with the **ARTEMIST-15 Shotshell Machine Gun**.

Properties:

- Reinforced casing to prevent deformation
- Optimized feed geometry to reduce jamming
- Enhanced cycling reliability under rapid fire conditions

Performance:

- Sustains high-rate automatic fire
- Minimal feeding errors under stress conditions
- Designed for continuous suppression environments

---

### **12 Gauge — Ultimatum**

**Designation:** C-12G-ULT  
**Classification:** Explosive Breaching Munition

A compact explosive shell designed to mimic the yield of a **40mm grenade**.

Mechanics:

- Contains a micro-compressed explosive core
- Designed to detonate upon impact or after a brief internal delay

Effects:

- High-yield concussive blast
- Fragmentation spread equivalent to heavy ordnance
- Capable of breaching fortified doors, walls, and structural defenses

Operational risk:

- Classified as **high collateral risk**
- Requires strict handling protocols

---

### **12 Gauge — Biohazard**

**Designation:** C-12G-BHZ  
**Classification:** Anomalous Biological Munition

A biologically active shell infused with the **Starladian Plague**, developed in conjunction with Star and Viridis.

Behavior:

- Upon deployment, the payload disperses as a targeted pathogen
- Exhibits **selective hostility**, identifying and infecting hostile entities only

Observed properties:

- Does not affect allied personnel
- Appears capable of differentiating between “hostile intent” and “non-hostile presence”
- Rapid internal degradation of targeted biological systems

Classification:

> **Bio-miracle anomaly**

Handling:

- Restricted to authorized personnel
- Requires Warcasket environmental safeguards

### **12 Gauge — Starburst**

**Designation:** C-12G-STB  
**Classification:** Disorientation / Anomalous Explosive

A hybrid flash-burst round designed to replicate and amplify the disorienting effects of **Stardust**.

### **12 Gauge — Anti-Anomaly**

**Designation:** C-12G-ANA  
**Classification:** Anomalous Interaction Munition

The **Anti-Anomaly** round is a specialized 12 Gauge shell designed to interact with entities that **do not respond to conventional ballistic damage**.

---

#### **PRIMARY FUNCTION**

The round is engineered to:

- Establish **localized interaction vectors** with anomalous constructs
- Temporarily **anchor non-physical entities to physical rules**
- Enable kinetic and environmental forces to register against otherwise resistant targets

---

#### **MECHANICS**

Upon discharge, the shell:

1. Releases a micro-instability field upon firing
2. Adapts its payload to the target’s anomalous properties
3. Forces **momentary synchronization** between the target and baseline reality parameters

This results in:

- Anomalous entities becoming **momentarily “solid” or “interactable”**
- Resistance to conventional force being **partially suppressed**
- Kinetic impact achieving measurable effect where none previously existed

---

#### **LIMITATIONS**

- Effect duration is extremely short
- Performance varies depending on entity classification
- High-energy anomalies may resist full synchronization
- Multiple rounds may be required for sustained interaction

---

#### **USE CASES**

- Engagement with incorporeal or phase-based entities
- Combat against entities that ignore conventional weaponry
- Stabilization of unstable anomaly fields
- Emergency suppression of non-interactable threats

---

#### **CLASSIFICATION NOTE**

The Anti-Anomaly round does not “damage” anomalies in the traditional sense.

Instead:

> It forces the anomaly to **acknowledge the existence of the bullet**.
