# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

**C.O.R.E DIVISION — MUNITION REGISTRY (GENERAL INTRO)**  
**Classification:** INTERNAL / WEAPON SYSTEM INDEX  
**Clearance Requirement:** C.O.R.E AUTHORIZATION

---

### **PURPOSE**

The **C.O.R.E Munition Registry** serves as the centralized classification and tracking system for all munitions utilized, developed, or evaluated under the C.O.R.E Division.

This registry exists to:

- Standardize identification of all weapon payloads
- Track performance, deployment, and lifecycle data
- Ensure controlled access to high-risk armaments
- Maintain operational compatibility with Aperture combat systems

---

### **SCOPE**

The registry encompasses, but is not limited to:

- Standard-issue ballistic munitions
- High-yield explosive payloads
- Photonic and energy-based projectiles
- Experimental and anomalous ordnance
- Specialized warhead systems
- Prototype and discontinued munitions

All munitions within this system are categorized based on:

- Operational function
- Deployment method
- Energy or material composition
- Integration compatibility with Aperture systems

---

### **CLASSIFICATION STRUCTURE**

Munitions within the C.O.R.E system are assigned:

- **Registry Designation (Z-Series or C-ORED Index)**
- **Operational Category**
- **Compatibility Class**
- **Hazard Rating**
- **Deployment Restrictions (if applicable)**

---

### **HANDLING GUIDELINES**

- Unauthorized handling of registry-class munitions is prohibited
- All listed munitions require proper suit integration (see Warcasket documentation)
- Experimental and anomalous ordnance must be approved prior to deployment
- Improper use may result in **system-wide compromise or structural failure**
