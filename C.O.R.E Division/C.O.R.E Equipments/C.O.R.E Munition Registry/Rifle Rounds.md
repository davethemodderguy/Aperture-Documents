# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

**C.O.R.E DIVISION — MUNITION REGISTRY**  
**SECTION: RIFLE CALIBER SYSTEMS**  
**Classification:** INTERNAL / WEAPON SYSTEM INDEX

---

## **OVERVIEW**

The **Rifle Caliber Systems** form the backbone of Aperture’s mid-to-long-range combat doctrine. These rounds, co-developed with **Nosiny. inc**, are designed for reliability, scalability, and modular augmentation across multiple combat platforms.

Standard rifle munitions provide:

- Consistent ballistic performance
- Platform-wide compatibility
- High adaptability for specialized augmentations
- Integration with Warcasket and automated defense systems

---

## **STANDARD ROUNDS**

---

### **5.56×45ss NOS**

**Designation:** R-556-NOS  
**Classification:** Standard Service Round

The primary round issued for most Aperture assault platforms.

Properties:

- Balanced velocity and penetration
- High reliability across automatic systems
- Optimized for sustained engagements

Operational Summary:

> The workhorse of Aperture infantry operations—reliable, scalable, and efficient.

---

### **7.62×51ss NOS**

**Designation:** R-762-NOS  
**Classification:** High-Power Service Round

A heavier-caliber variant of the 5.56 platform.

Properties:

- Increased kinetic energy
- Improved armor penetration
- Reduced magazine capacity compared to 5.56

Usage:

- Elite operatives
- Designated marksman platforms
- Heavy engagement roles

---

### **5.45×39ss NOS**

**Designation:** R-545-NOS  
**Classification:** Light Service Round

A lighter, high-velocity round optimized for control and suppression.

Properties:

- Reduced recoil
- Increased controllability in full-auto fire
- Lower mass per projectile

Primary Users:

- Lab Security
- Internal containment personnel

---

### **.300 Blackout NOS**

**Designation:** R-300-NOS  
**Classification:** Tactical Subsonic / High-Velocity Hybrid

A modified evolution of the original .300 BLK platform.

Properties:

- Enhanced velocity variant
- Adaptable for both suppressed and supersonic roles
- Effective in close-quarters and medium-range engagements

---

### **12.5×46ss NOS**

**Designation:** R-125-NOS  
**Classification:** Heavy Assault / Anti-Armor Round

A high-caliber round utilized in:

- Machine Guns
- Internal Defense Systems
- External Air Defense Systems

Properties:

- Extreme penetration capability
- High structural damage output
- Designed for **armor dismantling**, not engagement fairness

Operational Note:

> Deployment against lightly armored targets is considered **excessively disproportionate**.

---

### **.50 AAC NOS**

**Designation:** R-50-NOS  
**Classification:** Heavy Devastation Round

A high-energy round designed for maximum destructive output.

Effects:

- Explosive kinetic transfer
- Structural annihilation on impact
- Terminal results typically involve complete target disintegration

Operational Summary:

> If it hits, the engagement is over.

---

### **.338 NOS**

**Designation:** R-338-NOS  
**Classification:** Precision Penetration Round

A sniper-caliber round designed for accuracy and penetration.

Properties:

- High ballistic stability
- Long-range consistency
- Capable of defeating moderate armor

Usage:

- Precision elimination
- Long-range overwatch
- Anti-material applications

---

### **.308 APRT**

**Designation:** R-308-APRT  
**Classification:** Rotational Penetration Round

An Aperture-developed round with unique internal mechanics.

Mechanics:

- Projectile spins at extreme rotational speeds
- Functions similarly to a **drilling mechanism** rather than a standard bullet

Effects:

- Penetrates through dense materials via rotational force
- Creates deep, narrow impact channels
- Effective against reinforced structures and armored targets

## **SPECIAL ROUNDS**

---

### **Hydra’s Hailstorm**

**Designation:** S-HYDRA  
**Applicable Calibers:** 5.56×45ss, 5.45×39ss  
**Classification:** Cryogenic / Biological Disruption

Properties:

- Projectile fractures into crystalline frost fragments on impact
- Rapidly lowers target temperature

Effects:

- Severe biological damage through cold exposure
- Movement impairment
- Structural weakening in materials

---

### **Hade’s Call**

**Designation:** S-HADE  
**Applicable Calibers:** .300 Blackout NOS, 12.5×46ss  
**Classification:** Anomalous Soul Displacement

Infused with Banlands soul-energy.

Effects:

- Causes targets to be forcibly displaced from physical existence
- Entity is transported to the **Banlands** upon impact

Operational Note:

> “There is no recovery process.”

---

### **Leviathan’s Devastation**

**Designation:** S-LEV  
**Applicable Calibers:** 5.56×45ss, 5.45×39ss  
**Classification:** Electrical Disruption

A heavy augmentation of Leviathan-class energy discharge.

Effects:

- Emits a high-voltage electrical surge on impact
- Disrupts mechanical and electronic systems
- Causes catastrophic failure in energy-based entities

---

### **Anti-Anomaly Rounds (Rifle Platform)**

**Designation:** S-ANA-R  
**Applicable Calibers:** ALL RIFLE CALIBERS  
**Classification:** Non-Euclidean Interaction Ammunition

Properties:

- Exhibits **non-Euclidean ballistic behavior**
- Designed to interact with otherwise untouchable anomalies

Mechanics:

- Temporarily forces anomalous targets into a **state of physical interaction**
- Enables kinetic forces to register against entities that typically ignore damage

Usage:

- Engagement with incorporeal or reality-resistant entities
- Suppression of high-tier anomalies
- Emergency containment scenarios

The rifle ammunition registry represents a critical layer of Aperture’s combat doctrine, bridging:
