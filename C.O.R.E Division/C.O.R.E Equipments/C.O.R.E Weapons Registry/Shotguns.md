# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

**C.O.R.E DIVISION — WEAPONS REGISTRY**  
**SECTION: SHOTGUN SYSTEMS**  
**Classification:** INTERNAL / WEAPON SYSTEM INDEX

---

### **OVERVIEW**

Shotguns are Aperture’s main method of delivering a **clear message**:

> “Knock. Knock.”

Designed for close-range brutality, Aperture shotgun systems have evolved beyond simple breaching tools into **multi-role, adaptable weapon platforms** capable of handling:

- Close-quarters engagements
- Controlled suppression
- Breaching and demolition
- Specialized anomaly interaction (via compatible munitions)

Their flexibility makes them one of the most widely deployed weapon categories within C.O.R.E operations.

---

## **PRIMARY SHOTGUN SYSTEMS**

---

### **MCX-570**

**Platform Origin:** Remington 870 (Modified by Nosiny. inc)  
**Classification:** Standard Security Shotgun

Specifications:

- Fixed stock
- 8+1 shell capacity
- Manual pump-action

A highly reliable shotgun used by **Security Personnel in sensitive zones** such as Sector 3-A, where:

- Precision
- Controlled force
- Asset protection

are prioritized over raw firepower.

Compatible only with **conventional rounds**.

---

### **SG-240**

**Classification:** Tactical / Multi-Role Shotgun

Specifications:

- 10+1 shell capacity
- Collapsible stock
- Integrated compensator

Usage:

- Internal Security Units
- Riot Control
- Operators requiring adaptable engagement tools

Limitations:

- Cannot utilize Anti-Anomaly rounds
- Reduced effectiveness against anomaly-class targets

---

### **Nosiny M4**

**Platform Origin:** Benelli M4  
**Classification:** Protection-Class Shotgun

A semi-automatic shotgun designed for **high-value asset protection**.

Deployment:

- Aperture Personal Protection Program
- Assigned bodyguards and escort units

Purpose:

- Defensive engagement
- Rapid response in protective scenarios

Not standard issue for general combat units.

---

### **C1215**

**Classification:** High-Fire Automatic Shotgun

Specifications:

- Magazine-fed
- Semi/Full-auto capability
- Integrated recoil dampening system

Function:

- Fires continuously until magazine depletion
- Designed for high-output engagements

Commonly referred to as:

> “The Armory Staple”

---

### **The Signature**

**Operator:** Henry  
**Classification:** Personal Weapon System

Specifications:

- Break-action
- Double-shot configuration
- Sawed-off profile

Despite its simplicity, it maintains:

- A **100% confirmed kill record**
- Exceptional operator familiarity

Operational Note:

> Some weapons are chosen. This one chose him.
