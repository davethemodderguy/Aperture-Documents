# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

**C.O.R.E DIVISION — WEAPONS REGISTRY**  
**SECTION: SIDEARMS**  
**Classification:** INTERNAL / WEAPON SYSTEM INDEX

---

## **OVERVIEW**

Sidearms serve as the **final layer of operational reliability**—compact, fast-access weapons designed for:

- Emergency engagements
- Backup defense
- Precision finishing
- Situational control when primary systems fail

In Aperture doctrine, a sidearm is not a downgrade.  
It is a **decision tool**.

---

## **PRIMARY SIDEARMS**

---

### **D14**

**Classification:** Standard Issue Pistol

Specifications:

- 17+1 round capacity
- Semi-automatic operation

Operational Philosophy:

> Pure skill. No gimmicks.

Reliability:

- Consistent
- Minimal mechanical complexity
- Trusted across all operational tiers

---

### **D15**

**Classification:** Full-Auto Sidearm Platform

An evolved variant of the D14.

Features:

- Full automatic firing capability
- Expanded attachment compatibility
- Enhanced modularity

Role:

- Close-quarters suppression
- Rapid response engagements

---

### **KC45**

**Classification:** High-Impact Pistol

A “classical” sidearm with low capacity but high stopping power.

Properties:

- Low magazine capacity
- Heavy impact per shot
- Requires high operator proficiency

Doctrine:

> The weapon is not what makes it deadly.  
> The wielder is.

---

### **L16**

**Classification:** Administrator-Class Revolver

Specifications:

- High recoil
- Devastating single-shot output
- Precision-based operation

Operational Use:

- Authorized personnel only (Administrators)

Recoil Profile:

> Immeasurable. Unnecessary. Intentional.
