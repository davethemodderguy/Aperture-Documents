# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

**C.O.R.E DIVISION — WEAPONS REGISTRY (INTRODUCTION)**  
**Classification:** INTERNAL / SYSTEM INDEX  
**Clearance Requirement:** C.O.R.E AUTHORIZATION

---

### **PURPOSE**

The **C.O.R.E Weapons Registry** is the centralized index for all weapon systems developed, deployed, evaluated, or integrated under the C.O.R.E Division.

This registry is responsible for:

- Standardizing weapon classification across all departments
- Tracking operational performance and deployment history
- Maintaining compatibility with Warcasket systems and entity integrations
- Controlling access to experimental and high-risk armaments

---

### **SCOPE**

The registry encompasses all categories of weapon systems, including:

- Small arms and sidearms
- Rifle and heavy-caliber systems
- Specialized and modular munitions
- Photonic and energy-based weaponry
- Anomalous and reality-interacting armaments
- Experimental prototypes and discontinued platforms

Each weapon is categorized based on:

- Function and intended role
- Power classification
- System compatibility
- Operational restrictions

---

### **CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM**

Weapons within the C.O.R.E registry are assigned:

- **Registry Designation (C / Z / R / S-series, etc.)**
- **Operational Class**
- **Compatibility Tier**
- **Hazard Rating**
- **Usage Restrictions**

---

### **INTEGRATION**

C.O.R.E weapons are designed for seamless integration with:

- **Warcasket Armor Systems**
- **Photonic Weapon Platforms**
- **Aperture Automated Defense Systems**
- **Entity-specific augmentation frameworks**

Certain weapons require specific suit configurations or external power sources to function at full capacity.

---

### **ACCESS & CONTROL**

- Weapon systems are **tier-locked by clearance level**
- Experimental and anomalous weapons require **explicit authorization**
- Unauthorized use of registry-class weapons is grounds for **immediate disciplinary action**

---

### **SAFETY NOTICE**

Due to the nature of C.O.R.E weapon systems:

- Improper handling may result in **facility-wide incidents**
- Anomalous weapon misuse may cause **reality instability**
- Experimental systems may behave outside predicted parameters

Personnel are advised:

> If a weapon behaves unexpectedly—**stop using it immediately.**
