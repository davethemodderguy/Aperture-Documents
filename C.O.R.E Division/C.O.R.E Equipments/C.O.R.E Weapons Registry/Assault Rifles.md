## **OVERVIEW**

Assault rifles form the **core of Aperture’s mid-range combat doctrine**, balancing:

- Accuracy
- Fire rate
- Reliability
- Adaptability across operational theaters

These systems are designed to perform consistently under extreme conditions, integrating seamlessly with Nosiny. inc engineering and Aperture combat frameworks.

---

## **PRIMARY RIFLE SYSTEMS**

---

### **MCA5**

**Platform Origin:** M4 Platform (Modified by Nosiny. inc)  
**Classification:** Standard Issue Rifle

The MCA5 is a highly reliable, modular rifle system built on the proven M4 framework.

Properties:

- Exceptional reliability
- Modular attachment compatibility
- Standard magazine-fed system

Operational Note:

> “It’s an M4. It works. That’s the point.”

---

### **AD56 — “The Unbreakable”**

**Classification:** High-Durability Rifle System

A rifle engineered to withstand extreme operational abuse.

Properties:

- Resistant to jamming under adverse conditions
- Maintains functionality regardless of debris, stress, or environmental damage
- Designed for continuous operation without maintenance cycles

Operational Summary:

> Throw everything at it. It will still fire.

---

### **LCX52**

**Classification:** Suppressed Precision Rifle

An internally suppressed system utilizing **.300 caliber rounds**.

Properties:

- Built-in suppression system
- Reduced acoustic signature
- High kinetic output

Performance:

- Hits harder than most rifles in its class
- Maintains stealth while delivering significant stopping power


---

### **BR-870 “Bullspike”**

**Classification:** Bullpup Assault Rifle

A compact, high-efficiency bullpup rifle designed for tight environments.

Features:

- Rear-positioned action
- Compact overall profile
- Improved maneuverability in confined spaces

Usage:

- Indoor operations
- Close-quarters engagements
- Rapid deployment units
