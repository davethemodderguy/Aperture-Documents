### **BEHAVIORAL ASSESSMENT ADDENDUM – EXECUTIVE SECRETARY "CINDER"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Executive Personnel File

---

## **SUMMARY**

Further review of Executive Secretary **Cinder's** combat behavior has identified a distinct psychological state observed only under exceptional circumstances.

The state has **never** been observed while Cinder voluntarily possessed her assigned Multi-Purpose Tactical Briefcase.

It has only been documented after the briefcase became unavailable during an active life-threatening engagement.

---

## **Combat State Designation**

**Internal Designation:** **Emergency Close-Quarters Response State (ECRS)**

When deprived of her primary weapon while protecting others, Cinder demonstrates an immediate and dramatic shift in combat behavior.

Characteristics include:

- Complete focus on the perceived hostile threat.
    
- Exceptional pain tolerance.
    
- Continuous offensive pressure.
    
- Reduced verbal communication.
    
- Disregard for personal safety while protecting others.
    

Medical evaluations indicate that Cinder does **not** lose awareness of friend-or-foe identification. She continues to recognize allies and avoids harming them.

However, she becomes extremely difficult to interrupt until the immediate lethal threat has been neutralized.

---

## **Operational Notes**

Despite rumors among junior personnel, Cinder does **not** intentionally abandon or discard her briefcase.

On the contrary, she has consistently demonstrated meticulous care in maintaining custody of KCA property.

There are documented occasions where she has voluntarily handed the briefcase to **Keith** during prolonged engagements.

This is done only when battlefield conditions make the transfer tactically advantageous.

Examples include:

- Keith's shotgun ammunition becoming depleted.
    
- A weapon malfunction preventing continued fire.
    
- Situations requiring heavier suppressive fire or breaching capability.
    

Keith is authorized and trained to operate the briefcase's weapon systems during such emergencies until it can be returned to Cinder.

---

## **FIELD OBSERVATION**

During one convoy escort, hostile forces launched a sustained attack from elevated positions.

Keith's modified M1014 had exhausted its ammunition after covering Belle's evacuation route.

Without hesitation, Cinder handed him the briefcase.

> **"Take it."

Keith caught it cleanly.

Within seconds, the briefcase unfolded into its Smart Explosive Ordnance Launcher configuration.

Keith immediately engaged hostile firing positions while Cinder equipped her reinforced gauntlets to defend Belle from attackers closing in at close range.

The coordinated response successfully protected the Director until reinforcements arrived.

The briefcase was returned to Cinder immediately after the engagement concluded.
