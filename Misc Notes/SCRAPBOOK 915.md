# SAFEHOUSE COMBAT DEVELOPMENT RECORD

**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**TRAINING TYPE:** Archery / Projectile Combat  
**INSTRUCTORS:** Star & Nightshade  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE TRAINING GROUNDS  
**STATUS:** COMPLETE  
**CURRENT PROJECTILE WEAPON:** Bow

---

# 1. Background

Neri has always demonstrated a strong preference for close-quarters combat.

Knives.

Blades.

Her katana.

Anything that allows her to get close enough to personally deal with a threat.

Her philosophy was simple:

> **"If you can reach them, you can beat them."**

Star and Nightshade disagreed.

Both are exceptionally accomplished archers and have extensive experience using ranged weapons.

They eventually convinced Neri of a rather important limitation of her preferred fighting style:

> **Melee cannot solve 100% of the problems.**

Neri initially wasn't particularly convinced.

Then Star handed her a bow.

---

# 2. Training

The initial expectation was that Neri would require substantial training.

She did not.

Neri picked up the fundamentals almost immediately.

Within a remarkably short period, she had learned:

- proper stance,
    
- bow handling,
    
- drawing technique,
    
- anchor point consistency,
    
- aiming,
    
- release timing,
    
- target tracking,
    
- and firing while moving.
    

Her hand-eye coordination made the transition especially easy.

Her existing combat instincts also translated surprisingly well into archery.

---

# 3. Rapid Adaptation

Star and Nightshade quickly realized that they weren't teaching Neri how to become an archer.

They were teaching her how to use a weapon she apparently already understood instinctively.

Neri rapidly began adjusting her shooting style according to distance and movement.

She could transition between:

**close-range → mid-range → long-range**

without significant hesitation.

Her ability to move rapidly while maintaining control of her bow made her particularly difficult to predict.

---

# 4. Neri's Reaction

After one particularly successful training session, Star asked:

> **Star:** "See? Isn't ranged combat useful?"

Neri looked at the target.

Then at her bow.

Then back at Star.

> **Neri:** "...Yeah."

Nightshade smiled.

> **Nightshade:** "Melee can't solve everything."

Neri considered this for several seconds.

> **Neri:** "Fine."

A pause.

> **Neri:** "But knives are still better."

Star laughed.

---

# 5. The Unexpected Result

Neri did not merely add archery to her arsenal.

She made a decision.

**Archery would be her only projectile-based combat method.**

Neri has access to firearms.

She dislikes them.

Her reasoning remains consistent:

> **"Too far."**

A bow solved the problem.

It gave her ranged capability without requiring her to abandon the combat style she was comfortable with.

She could still move.

She could still fight at close range.

She could still use her blades.

But now she could also eliminate a threat from a distance.

---

# 6. Combat Profile

Neri's current combat preference can therefore be summarized as:

### PRIMARY

**Melee**

Knives, concealed blades, katana, and close-quarters combat.

### SECONDARY

**Archery**

Her only dedicated projectile-based combat method.

### DISLIKED

**Firearms**

Neri considers them unnecessarily impersonal and prefers being closer to the problem.

---

# 7. Why Archery Works for Her

Archery happens to complement several of Neri's existing strengths.

She already possesses exceptional coordination and reaction speed.

Her mobility allows her to reposition quickly.

Her combat instincts allow her to recognize openings.

Her chemistry knowledge also gives her an unusual understanding of materials and how different arrow constructions behave, although modifications remain subject to SAFEHOUSE safety regulations.

Most importantly, archery gives Neri something she previously lacked:

**Distance without abandoning precision.**

---

# 8. Instructor Assessment — Star

> "She learns ridiculously fast."

Star initially expected to spend weeks teaching Neri the fundamentals.

Instead, Neri was already consistently hitting targets before Star had finished explaining several of the more advanced concepts.

Star's final assessment was straightforward:

> **"She's an archer now."**

---

# 9. Instructor Assessment — Nightshade

Nightshade was slightly more cautious.

She noted that Neri's natural talent should not be mistaken for unlimited capability.

Archery still requires:

- concentration,
    
- judgment,
    
- ammunition,
    
- appropriate equipment,
    
- and awareness of surroundings.
    

Nightshade therefore emphasized that Neri should treat archery as another tool rather than a replacement for every other combat method.

Neri agreed.

Mostly.

---

# 10. Current Status

Neri now trains regularly with Star and Nightshade.

Her bow has become a permanent part of her combat equipment.

The transition has been unusually successful.

Neri went from:

> **"Melee solves everything."**

to:

> **"Melee solves most things."**

and finally:

> **"Fine. I'll shoot things when they're too far away."**

This is considered significant progress.

---

## FINAL ASSESSMENT

Neri remains fundamentally a close-combat fighter.

She still prefers knives.

She still prefers her katana.

She still believes that being close to a problem makes it easier to solve.

But she has finally accepted Star and Nightshade's central argument:

> **You cannot stab everything.**

So now, when something is too far away to stab—

**Neri uses a bow.**

And considering how quickly she learned it, Star and Nightshade may have accidentally created a rather exceptional archer.
