# Executive Summary

Neri's reduced stamina has resulted in increased use of assisted transportation.

Among the most frequently observed methods is **piggyback transportation by Jane Doe.**

The arrangement is practical.

It is also, according to multiple personnel observations, unexpectedly adorable.

Neri remains physically weak and currently unable to speak.

Neither condition has eliminated her personality.

She remains, with remarkable consistency:

**a tsundere.**

---

# Piggyback Observation

When Neri is unable or unwilling to walk a significant distance, Jane may carry her on her back.

Neri generally settles onto Jane without much resistance.

Once moving, however, there is an unavoidable physical effect:

**Neri bounces slightly with every step Jane takes.**

Her body naturally shifts with Jane's walking rhythm.

She may gently rise and settle again with each step, occasionally adjusting her position or tightening her hold to remain comfortable.

The effect is particularly noticeable when Jane walks quickly.

Neri, meanwhile, usually appears completely unimpressed by the situation.

---

# Typical Appearance

Neri's oversized coat makes the situation even more visually distinctive.

Her sleeves often hang around her hands.

Her glasses remain in place.

Her head may rest near Jane's shoulder.

Her coat and hair move slightly with Jane's steps.

Every few steps, Neri gives a small involuntary bounce.

She usually responds to none of this.

She simply rides.

---

# Behavioral Response

The fact that Neri is being carried does not make her suddenly affectionate.

She remains Neri.

If Jane says:

> "Comfortable?"

Neri may give a small nod.

If Jane says:

> "Want me to slow down?"

Neri may shake her head.

If Jane says:

> "You're getting heavy."

Neri will immediately glare at her.

Jane has since stopped saying this.

---

# The Tsundere Remains Intact

Neri's inability to speak has not removed her tsundere tendencies.

They have simply become **nonverbal.**

Her current vocabulary includes:

- glaring;
    
- exaggerated sighs;
    
- looking away;
    
- folding her arms;
    
- refusing to acknowledge compliments;
    
- pushing someone's hand away;
    
- reluctantly accepting assistance;
    
- pretending she doesn't enjoy something;
    
- quietly staying anyway.
    

Her facial expressions have effectively become her dialogue.

# The "Don't Call Me Cute" Problem

Neri still strongly dislikes being called cute.

This creates an unfortunate situation.

Because objectively:

A tiny, mute, exhausted Seal Thiren in an oversized coat, quietly bouncing with every step while being carried around by Jane Doe is—

**Cute.**

Very cute.

But personnel are advised not to say this to Neri.

Especially not to her face.

---

# Jane Doe's Policy

Jane has developed a simple rule:

> **Think it. Don't say it.**

Jane is fully aware that calling Neri cute would probably result in an immediate glare.

Possibly a light smack.

Possibly both.

She therefore refrains from verbal commentary.

Mostly.

---

# Neri's Current Physical State

The cuteness of the situation should not obscure the underlying reason Neri is being carried.

Neri remains significantly weakened following her recent anemia shock.

She is:

- physically fragile;
    
- low in stamina;
    
- unable to speak;
    
- prone to exhaustion;
    
- still medically dependent on Bao's blood-support treatment.
    

The piggyback arrangement is primarily **functional assistance.**

The fact that she happens to look adorable while receiving that assistance is incidental.
