### **SAFEHOUSE PERSONAL ITEM RECORD**

# **CORIN — THE CURIOUS LIGHT HAIRCLIP**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Gifted By:** Curious Light  
**Status:** **CURRENTLY WORN**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the scarf incident, Curious Light noticed another small detail about Corin's appearance.

Her **chalky lime-colored hair** naturally grows into long bangs that fall over her eyes.

Corin rarely bothered correcting them.

She was accustomed to letting the bangs obscure much of her face.

Curious Light decided to change that.

Not through experimentation.

Not through analysis.

Simply by giving her something.

---

# **THE HAIRCLIP**

Curious Light created a small hairclip specifically for Corin.

The clip bears the **symbol associated with Curious Light**.

Its design is simple and unobtrusive, intended to hold Corin's bangs away from her eyes without requiring her to change her hairstyle.

Unlike Guiding Light's blue-glowing hairclip worn by Orphie, Curious Light's gift does not prominently glow.

Its most distinctive feature is its symbol.

A small representation of the **rhombus-shaped star** associated with Curious Light is engraved into its surface.

---

# **THE GIFT**

Curious Light approached Corin while she was sitting in the treehouse.

He held out the clip.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Here."

Corin looked at it.

> **Corin:**  
> "...What's this?"

> **Curious Light:**  
> "A hairclip."

Corin stared at him.

> **"I can see that."**

Curious Light paused.

> **"...Your bangs cover your eyes."**

Corin shrugged.

> **"I don't mind."**

> **Curious Light:**  
> "I do."

Corin raised an eyebrow.

> **"...Why?"**

Curious Light simply answered:

> **"I want to see your eyes."**

Corin became quiet.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin eventually accepted the clip.

She carefully gathered some of her bangs and secured them with it.

For the first time in a while, her eyes were clearly visible.

She looked at Curious Light.

> **Corin:**  
> "...There."

Curious Light nodded.

> **"Better."**

Corin smiled slightly.

> **"You're weird."**

> **Curious Light:**  
> "I know."

---

# **ORPHIE'S REACTION**

Orphie noticed the new accessory later.

She looked at Corin.

Then at the clip.

Then at Curious Light.

> **Orphie:**  
> "...You gave her a hairclip too?"

Curious Light:

> **"Yes."**

Orphie pointed toward her own blue-glowing clip.

> **"Mine was from Guiding Light."**

Curious Light nodded.

> **"I know."**

Corin looked between them.

> **"Did you two plan this?"**

Both Architects answered simultaneously:

> **"No."**

---

# **SYMBOLISM**

The two hairclips are now quietly associated with the two Architects.

### **Orphie**

- White cat-eared beanie.
    
- Guiding Light's blue-glowing hairclip.
    

### **Corin**

- Chalky lime hair.
    
- Curious Light's hairclip bearing his rhombus-star symbol.
    

Neither gift carries an obligation.

Neither is a symbol of ownership.

They are simply small reminders that both Architects care about them.

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT'S PRIVATE NOTE**

> **"I wanted to give her something."**
> 
> **"Not because I wanted to study her."**
> 
> **"Not because I wanted to understand her."**
> 
> **"Just because I thought she would look better with her eyes uncovered."**

A final addition appeared beneath it:

> **"She smiled."**
