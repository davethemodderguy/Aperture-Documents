## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **ANOMALOUS MEDICAL OBSERVATION LOG**

# **ORPHIE — GOLDEN SUBDERMAL MANIFESTATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Medical / Anomalous Observation  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Marts / Luna / Solaria  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **UNRESOLVED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

A new anomalous phenomenon has been observed on Orphie's body.

Thin **golden glowing lines** have begun appearing beneath her skin.

The lines resemble illuminated veins or branching pathways.

They emit a faint golden light visible through the skin.

Orphie does not appear concerned.

In fact, she appears to barely notice them.

---

# **PHYSICAL APPEARANCE**

The phenomenon consists of luminous golden lines running beneath Orphie's skin.

Observed characteristics include:

- Fine branching patterns.
    
- Irregular pathways following portions of the body's vascular structure.
    
- Variable brightness.
    
- Increased visibility in darkness.
    
- No observed external wound.
    
- No apparent skin damage.
    
- No measurable heat emission.
    

The lines occasionally brighten briefly before returning to their normal faint glow.

---

# **INITIAL DISCOVERY**

Corin first noticed the phenomenon while speaking with Orphie.

She noticed a faint golden glow beneath Orphie's wrist.

> **Corin:** "...What's that?"

Orphie looked down.

The golden lines were clearly visible beneath her skin.

> **Orphie:** "...What?"

> **Corin:** "Your arm."

Orphie examined it briefly.

> "...Oh."

She then pulled her sleeve back down.

---

# **ORPHIE'S REACTION**

Corin expected concern.

There was none.

> **Corin:** "...You're not worried?"

> **Orphie:** "...No."

> **Corin:** "...It's glowing."

> **Orphie:** "...Okay."

> **Corin:** "...That's not normal."

Orphie:

> "...Neither am I."

The conversation ended shortly afterward.

---

# **MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

C.O.R.E. medical personnel have been unable to determine the exact origin of the phenomenon.

Current observations indicate:

**Temperature:** Normal.

**Pain response:** None reported.

**Tissue damage:** None observed.

**Blood flow:** Normal.

**Energy emission:** Low-level anomalous radiation.

**External manifestation:** None.

**Known cause:** Unknown.

No immediate physiological threat has been identified.

---

# **THE GOLDEN LINES**

The pattern does not remain completely static.

It appears to gradually shift across Orphie's body.

Some lines fade.

Others appear.

The overall structure resembles a network of glowing pathways.

At certain times, the lines become visible around:

- Hands.
    
- Forearms.
    
- Neck.
    
- Face.
    
- Shoulders.
    
- Upper chest.
    

The glow is strongest during periods of emotional stress.

---

# **CORIN'S CONCERN**

Corin has become increasingly worried.

She attempted to convince Orphie to undergo a medical examination.

Orphie refused.

> **Corin:** "...Please let them check it."

> **Orphie:** "...Why?"

> "...Because something is happening to you."

> "...I don't care."

> "...I do."

Orphie looked away.

> "...That's your problem."

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

The Marts noticed the glow almost immediately.

Unlike Orphie, they were fascinated.

Several Marts gathered around her.

One touched the glowing area with a tiny hand.

> "...Pretty."

Orphie:

> "...Don't."

The Mart immediately stopped.

Another Mart stared at the golden lines.

> "...Warm?"

Orphie:

> "...No."

The Marts eventually accepted that Orphie did not want attention drawn to it.

---

# **LUNA'S RESPONSE**

Luna reacted differently.

When informed about the phenomenon, she became unusually serious.

> "...Golden light under her skin?"

The researcher confirmed it.

Luna paused.

> "...That's new."

> "Do you know what it means?"

> "...No."

She looked toward Orphie's location.

> "...And that's what worries me."

---

# **SOLARIA'S RESPONSE**

Solaria was also unable to identify the phenomenon.

> "...It's not my light."

She examined the description carefully.

> "...Whatever that is, it's coming from her."

---

# **ORPHIE'S ATTITUDE**

Orphie's response remains consistent:

She does not care.

She does not investigate it.

She does not ask questions.

She does not appear frightened.

If the lines brighten, she ignores them.

If someone points them out, she covers them with her sleeves.

If someone asks whether they hurt:

> "...No."

If someone asks whether she wants them removed:

> "...Why?"

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Phenomenon:** Golden subdermal luminous network.

**Cause:** Unknown.

**Symptoms:** None currently reported.

**Pain:** None reported.

**Physiological damage:** None observed.

**Orphie's concern:** None.

**Corin's concern:** High.

**Marts' concern:** Moderate.

**Luna:** Investigating.

**Solaria:** Unable to identify origin.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The golden lines continue to glow beneath Orphie's skin.

They are unmistakable.

They are anomalous.

They are new.

And Orphie does not care.

For the medical team, that is perhaps the most concerning part.

Not the glow.

Not the unknown origin.

Not the changing pattern.

But the fact that Orphie can look down at something visibly changing inside her body and simply say:

> **"...I don't care."**

**End of Anomalous Medical Observation Log**
