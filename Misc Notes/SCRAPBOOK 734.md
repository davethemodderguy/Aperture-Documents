# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — SOCIAL INCIDENT COLLECTION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** **PROTECTED MINOR / REPEATED BULLYING INCIDENTS**

---

# **INCIDENT 01 — "THE SLOW SCOOTER"**

Alice was traveling through a Blacksite corridor on her scooter.

Two junior personnel noticed her.

One stepped into her path.

> "Hey, bunny."

Alice stopped.

The other laughed.

> "Is that thing even fast enough to go anywhere?"

Alice looked down.

She didn't answer.

The first person moved closer.

> "Come on. Say something."

Alice remained silent.

Her ears lowered.

The personnel laughed again.

Eventually, a nearby security officer noticed the situation and intervened.

Alice immediately rode away.

She did not report the incident.

Later that evening, Corin found Alice sitting quietly in the Treehouse.

Alice wrote:

> **"Alice scooter slow."**

Corin replied:

> "Your scooter is fine."

Alice wrote:

> **"They laugh."**

---

# **INCIDENT 02 — "THE EARS"**

Alice was waiting outside a laboratory for Star.

A group of personnel passed by.

One stopped.

> "Are those real?"

Alice looked at them.

She nodded.

The person reached toward her head.

Alice moved backward.

The hand stopped.

> "What's wrong?"

Alice lowered her ears.

The person laughed.

> "They're just ears."

Alice didn't respond.

Star arrived several seconds later.

She immediately noticed Alice's posture.

> "What happened?"

Alice shook her head.

Star looked at the personnel.

They left.

Alice followed Star without saying anything.

---

# **INCIDENT 03 — "THE TAIL"**

Alice was sitting on a bench.

Another resident approached and jokingly reached toward her tail.

Alice immediately pulled it away.

She said nothing.

The person reached again.

Alice moved farther away.

Still nothing.

Belle happened to see the incident.

She stepped between them.

> "Don't."

The person stopped.

Belle looked down at Alice.

> "You okay?"

Alice nodded.

Her ears remained lowered.

Belle knew better.

She picked Alice up and carried her away.

---

# **INCIDENT 04 — "ANOTHER BELLE"**

Alice was riding through SAFEHOUSE when someone called:

> "Hey, another Belle!"

Alice stopped.

The person laughed.

> "You really do look like a tiny version of her."

Alice smiled politely.

They continued.

> "Does that mean you're the weak one?"

Alice's smile disappeared.

She looked down.

The person continued joking.

> "Can you even walk?"

Alice's hands tightened around the scooter controls.

She quietly rode away.

She did not look back.

That evening, Belle found Alice drawing.

The drawing depicted two rabbits.

One was pink.

One was white.

The white rabbit was noticeably smaller.

Alice had drawn a large black X over the white rabbit.

Belle immediately hugged her.

---

# **INCIDENT 05 — "SHE WON'T SAY ANYTHING"**

A group of personnel discovered something about Alice's behavior.

She rarely complained.

They began making jokes around her.

> "She won't say anything."

> "She can't even talk."

> "What's she going to do?"

Alice was present.

She heard everything.

She sat quietly.

Her ears lowered.

One person eventually noticed her eyes watering.

> "Oh."

They laughed.

> "She's crying."

Alice immediately wiped her face.

Then smiled.

> "See? She's fine."

She wasn't.

---

# **INCIDENT 06 — "THE NOTEBOOK"**

Someone took Alice's notebook from her scooter.

Alice immediately stood up.

She reached for it.

The person held it above her head.

> "What are you writing?"

Alice tried to reach.

She couldn't.

She eventually stopped.

Her ears dropped.

The person flipped through several pages.

Then stopped.

> "Wow."

They read one page aloud.

> **"Alice don't want forget Big Sis."**

The laughter stopped.

They realized what they had taken.

The notebook was returned.

Alice immediately hugged it against herself.

She left without looking at them.

---

# **INCIDENT 07 — "THE SMILE"**

A security camera recorded Alice being mocked by another group.

Alice smiled throughout the entire interaction.

She nodded.

She even laughed once.

To someone unfamiliar with Alice, she appeared completely fine.

The camera footage showed something else.

Her ears stayed lowered.

Her hands were shaking.

Her smile disappeared immediately after she turned the corner.

She stopped her scooter.

Sat against the wall.

And cried silently.

---

# **INCIDENT 08 — "CORIN FINDS HER"**

Corin found Alice behind a maintenance building.

Alice was sitting on the ground beside her scooter.

She was crying.

Corin crouched beside her.

Alice immediately wiped her face.

She smiled.

Corin frowned.

> "Don't."

Alice froze.

> "You don't have to smile for me."

Alice's smile disappeared.

Corin hugged her.

Alice buried her face against Corin.

Her shoulders shook.

She didn't write anything.

She didn't need to.

---

# **INCIDENT 09 — "THE NEXT DAY"**

The following morning, Alice rode her scooter again.

She still visited her friends.

She still waved at people.

She still went through the Blacksite.

She still smiled.

Nothing about her behavior suggested that she intended to stop going outside.

But the SAFEHOUSE noticed one difference.

Alice stayed closer to familiar people.

She stopped wandering into unfamiliar areas.

When she saw groups of strangers, she turned around.

When someone called her name unexpectedly, she became still.

She was learning who felt safe.

And who didn't.

---

# **INCIDENT 10 — "THE RESPONSE"**

Eventually, Alice was seen being harassed again.

This time, the response was immediate.

Security intervened.

Belle arrived.

Then Corin.

Then Star.

Then Orphie.

Alice sat quietly between them.

Nobody asked:

> "Why didn't you say something?"

Nobody blamed her.

Nobody told her she should have fought back.

Corin simply held her.

Belle sat beside her.

Star placed a hand gently on her head.

Orphie remained nearby.

Alice eventually opened her notebook.

She wrote:

> **"Alice sorry."**

Corin immediately shook her head.

> "No."

Alice looked confused.

Corin continued:

> **"You did nothing wrong."**

Alice stared at the sentence.

Then slowly wrote:

> **"Alice safe?"**

Corin answered:

> **"Yes."**

Belle added:

> **"Always."**

---

# **FINAL ASSESSMENT**

Alice's greatest vulnerability is not her physical weakness.

It is her tendency to endure suffering silently.

She doesn't complain.

She doesn't retaliate.

She doesn't ask for help.

She smiles.

She pretends everything is fine.

Then she goes somewhere private and falls apart.

Therefore, personnel are reminded:

**A smiling Alice is not necessarily a happy Alice.**

**A silent Alice is not necessarily a comfortable Alice.**

**A nod is not necessarily consent.**

**And Alice's inability or unwillingness to defend herself is never permission to mistreat her.**

She is a five-year-old child.

She deserves to ride her scooter around the Blacksite.

She deserves to visit her friends.

She deserves to laugh.

She deserves to be annoying sometimes.

She deserves to be quiet sometimes.

She deserves to say no.

And most importantly—

she deserves to feel safe enough that she doesn't have to hide when something hurts.
