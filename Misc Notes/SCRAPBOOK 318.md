# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – LINGUISTIC RESEARCH ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: ENGLISH FLUENCY AMONG ELDER MARTS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Research Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Recent interviews with the Blacksite and KCA Ambassadors have resolved a long-standing question regarding Mart language.

All **five Elder Marts** are fully capable of speaking fluent English.

This ability is not unique to the Ambassadors.

Rather, the Ambassadors are simply the only Elders who regularly choose to use it.

---

# **CONFIRMED ELDERS**

Current confirmed Elder Mart identities:

- Camera Mart
    
- KCA Ambassador
    
- Blacksite Ambassador
    
- Elder Mart #4 — Unidentified
    
- Elder Mart #5 — Unidentified
    

All five are confirmed to possess:

- Human-level intelligence.
    
- Fluent spoken English.
    
- Reading and writing ability.
    
- Full understanding of human conversation.
    

---

# **CAMERA MART**

The discovery regarding Camera Mart surprised very few people.

Despite spending years saying little more than:

> "Ready."

> "Person."

> "Bad."

> "Safe."

Both Ambassadors confirmed that Camera Mart has always been capable of holding ordinary conversations.

It simply...

Prefers not to.

When questioned directly, Camera Mart looked away from the surveillance monitors just long enough to answer.

> "Talking isn't my job."

It then resumed watching Monitor 4.

No further comments were provided.

---

# **IDENTIFICATION METHOD**

Research has determined the only reliable method of identifying an unknown Elder Mart.

If a previously ordinary-looking Mart suddenly speaks fluent English...

Personnel are advised to assume they have encountered one of the remaining Elders.

The probability is considered extremely high.

Researchers have jokingly referred to this event as:

> **"Hitting the jackpot."**

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Research now considers fluent English to be the single most reliable indicator of an Elder Mart.

The current tally is:

- **Camera Mart** — Speaks, but almost never chooses to.
    
- **KCA Ambassador** — Speaks regularly.
    
- **Blacksite Ambassador** — Speaks regularly.
    
- **Two unidentified Elders** — Known to speak fluent English, identities unknown.
    

Personnel are reminded that if an ordinary-looking Mart unexpectedly begins speaking in complete, grammatically correct English...

Congratulations.

You have almost certainly met one of the oldest beings in the entire Mart colony.

Whether you'll recognize them again afterward...

Is another matter entirely.
