### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT LOG**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — MISCOMMUNICATION CASCADE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** **CRITICAL / ACUTE EMOTIONAL DISTRESS**

---

# **CONTINUATION**

The conversation between Corin and Alice did not end after the initial misunderstanding.

They continued talking.

Corin tried to explain herself.

Alice tried to understand.

Neither succeeded consistently.

Every attempt at clarification produced another misunderstanding.

---

# **THE CASCADE**

Corin said:

> **"You can't keep doing this to yourself."**

She meant:

**You are hurting yourself and I want to protect you.**

Alice heard:

> **"You're doing something bad to everyone."**

Her ears lowered.

> **"Sorry."**

Corin immediately tried again.

> **"That's not what I meant."**

Alice heard:

> **"You don't understand anything."**

> **"Sorry."**

Corin became frustrated.

Not with Alice.

With herself.

She said:

> **"Why won't you listen to me?"**

Alice went silent.

To Alice, that became:

> **"I'm difficult to deal with."**

---

# **ANOTHER MISUNDERSTANDING**

Corin tried to reassure her.

> **"You have to take care of yourself."**

Alice looked confused.

> **"Why?"**

> **"Because nobody else can do it for you forever."**

Corin intended:

**Your body matters. You deserve to care for yourself.**

Alice heard:

> **"Eventually everyone will stop taking care of you."**

Her breathing became unsteady.

> **"Then Alice go before that."**

Corin immediately realized what had happened.

> **"No, that's not—"**

But Alice had already begun crying.

---

# **THE CRYING**

Alice's crying was quiet at first.

Her face tightened.

Her ears flattened.

Her hands began trembling.

Then tears started falling.

Corin froze.

Alice whispered:

> **"Alice trying."**

Corin looked at her.

> **"I know."**

> **"Alice trying be good."**

> **"I know."**

> **"Alice trying not make everyone sad."**

Corin's voice broke.

> **"Alice..."**

> **"Alice trying leave so everyone okay."**

> **"Stop."**

Alice flinched.

Corin immediately regretted the word.

She meant:

**Please stop hurting yourself.**

Alice heard:

**Stop talking.**

She stopped.

---

# **METABOLIC CONSEQUENCES**

Alice's crying quickly became medically concerning.

Crying itself is not normally dangerous.

Alice is different.

Her body is already operating with critically depleted nutritional reserves.

Emotional distress increases metabolic demand.

Her breathing became faster.

Her heart rate increased.

Her body began consuming energy it could not easily replace.

The medical monitor began registering deterioration.

Alice was crying harder.

Her body was becoming weaker.

The more distressed she became, the more resources she consumed.

And the less she had available.

---

# **CORIN BREAKS**

Corin tried again.

> **"Alice, please."**

Alice looked up.

> **"Please what?"**

Corin couldn't answer immediately.

She was terrified.

She was exhausted.

She had already said too many things incorrectly.

Alice interpreted the silence as another rejection.

> **"Corin doesn't want Alice."**

> **"No!"**

Corin stood abruptly.

> **"That's not true!"**

Alice recoiled.

Corin saw the fear in her face.

She realized she was making everything worse.

And then—

She made the worst decision possible.

---

# **CORIN LEFT**

Corin walked out.

Not because she wanted to abandon Alice.

Not because she stopped caring.

She simply couldn't think of another thing to say.

She needed a moment.

She needed to breathe.

She needed to stop talking before she caused more damage.

But Alice did not understand that.

She watched Corin leave.

The door closed.

Silence.

Alice stared at it.

Her crying stopped.

Not because she felt better.

Because something inside her simply went quiet.

---

# **ALICE'S CONCLUSION**

Alice whispered to herself:

> **"Corin left."**

She looked down.

> **"Alice did it."**

A pause.

> **"Alice made Corin leave."**

She pulled the blanket around herself.

> **"Sorry."**

Her body trembled.

She did not call anyone.

She did not ask for help.

She simply lay there.

---

# **THE MEDICAL MONITOR**

Her condition continued deteriorating.

Heart rate irregular.

Temperature unstable.

Metabolic reserves critically low.

Regenerative activity suppressed.

Hydration inadequate.

The system registered her distress before anyone returned.

Alice's body was exhausted from crying.

She had barely eaten.

She had barely drunk.

She had already been critically weak.

Now she had spent additional energy on an emotional crisis.

---

# **CORIN REALIZES**

Corin had barely made it down the corridor before she stopped.

She remembered Alice's face.

The silence after the door closed.

And then she realized what she had done.

> **"No..."**

She turned around.

She ran back.

---

# **THE ROOM**

Alice was still there.

Curled beneath the blanket.

Eyes open.

Silent.

Corin approached slowly.

> **"Alice?"**

No response.

Corin sat beside the bed.

> **"I'm sorry."**

Alice looked toward her.

> **"You left."**

Corin nodded.

> **"I did."**

> **"Because Alice bad?"**

Corin immediately shook her head.

> **"No."**

> **"Because Alice made you sad?"**

> **"No."**

> **"Because Alice talked wrong?"**

Corin swallowed.

> **"No."**

She took Alice's hand.

> **"I left because I was scared."**

Alice stared at her.

> **"Scared of Alice?"**

> **"No."**

> **"Scared of losing you."**

Alice became quiet.

---

# **CORIN'S CORRECTION**

Corin spoke slowly.

Every word deliberate.

> **"I was scared."**

> **"I said things wrong."**

> **"You understood them differently."**

> **"I got frustrated."**

> **"Then I left."**

She squeezed Alice's hand.

> **"That was my mistake."**

Alice whispered:

> **"Alice mistake?"**

Corin shook her head.

> **"Mine."**

---

# **THE MOST IMPORTANT SENTENCE**

Corin looked directly at Alice.

> **"When I leave a room, it doesn't mean I am leaving you."**

Alice stared at her.

Corin continued:

> **"Sometimes people need a moment."**

> **"Sometimes people get scared."**

> **"Sometimes people say the wrong thing."**

> **"None of those mean they stopped loving you."**

Alice's eyes filled again.

Corin immediately added:

> **"And you don't need to stop crying for me."**

Alice whispered:

> **"But crying make you worry."**

> **"Then I'll worry."**

Alice looked confused.

> **"That's okay?"**

Corin smiled sadly.

> **"Yes."**

---

# **MEDICAL NOTE**

Corin's departure significantly worsened Alice's emotional distress.

Although Corin's intention was to prevent further verbal escalation, leaving without explanation reinforced Alice's abandonment-related interpretation.

Future personnel are advised:

If overwhelmed, personnel may step away.

However:

**Explain the departure first.**

Use clear language.

Avoid ambiguous statements.

Example:

> **"I need five minutes to calm down. I am coming back."**

Not:

> **"I can't do this anymore."**

Not:

> **"I need to leave."**

Not:

> **"You have to stop."**

Alice may interpret such statements literally and catastrophically.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The conversation began with Corin trying to tell Alice:

> **"I don't want to lose you."**

It ended with Alice believing:

> **"I made Corin leave."**

Neither wanted that outcome.

Neither intended to hurt the other.

But Alice's understanding of language is currently filtered through fear, abandonment, and an overwhelming instinct to put everyone else before herself.

And Corin, terrified of losing Alice, became so afraid of saying the wrong thing that she eventually said nothing at all.

Then she left.

For Alice, that silence meant abandonment.

For Corin, it was only a few minutes.

For Alice, it felt like confirmation of everything she had been afraid of.

The next time they speak, every word will have to matter.
