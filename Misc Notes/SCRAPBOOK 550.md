## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE DOMESTIC ACTIVITY REPORT**

# **CORIN — RETURN TO COOKING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Activity:** **Cooking / Baking**  
**Status:** Routine

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has recently returned to cooking.

What began as an occasional activity has gradually become one of her favorite ways to spend time at the SAFEHOUSE.

Her preferred activities are:

- **Baking**
    
- **Making soup**
    
- **Roasting meat**
    

The activity appears to have particular emotional significance following her return to biological life.

---

# **BAKING**

Corin particularly enjoys baking.

She likes the slower pace of preparing dough, measuring ingredients, and waiting for something to finish in the oven.

She is especially fond of the smell that fills the kitchen while something is baking.

Her wings usually remain partially folded while she works, occasionally shifting when she needs to reach around herself.

Corin has also learned to keep loose feathers away from the cooking area.

This was necessary after one accidentally landed in a mixing bowl.

> **Corin:**  
> "...That's not supposed to be there."

---

# **SOUP**

Soup has become another favorite.

Corin enjoys preparing large pots that can be shared with everyone in the SAFEHOUSE.

Her soups tend to contain plenty of vegetables, meat, and whatever ingredients happen to be available.

She usually tastes the broth several times while cooking.

When asked whether she follows a recipe:

> **"Sometimes."**

When asked what the rest of the time involves:

> **"Guessing."**

The results are surprisingly consistent.

---

# **ROASTED MEAT**

Corin also enjoys roasting meat.

She particularly likes the process of preparing the meat, seasoning it, and watching it slowly cook.

She has developed a habit of preparing larger portions than she personally needs.

This is intentional.

Corin likes having everyone eat together.

---

# **THE SAFEHOUSE KITCHEN**

Corin has become a frequent presence in the kitchen.

Her return to cooking has been noticed by the other residents.

The kitchen is once again frequently occupied by:

- Corin.
    
- Belle.
    
- Bao.
    
- Star.
    
- Orphie.
    

Sometimes several of them cook together.

Sometimes Corin simply works quietly while everyone else talks.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle is particularly enthusiastic about Corin's cooking.

She frequently appears in the kitchen shortly after Corin begins baking.

Her usual question is:

> **"Is it ready yet?"**

Corin's usual answer:

> **"No."**

Five minutes later:

> **"Now?"**

> **"...No."**

This repeats until the food is finished.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie often helps with preparation.

The two have developed a comfortable routine.

Corin handles the cooking.

Orphie handles some preparation and cleanup.

Neither needs to say much.

Sometimes they simply work beside each other.

---

# **WHY CORIN COOKS**

Cooking has become more than a hobby.

For Corin, it represents another ordinary part of living.

She once consumed food only because she enjoyed its taste.

Her body did not require it.

Now her biology functions normally.

She can eat.

She can digest.

She can become hungry.

She can prepare food for herself.

And she can prepare food for other people.

The simple act of cooking has therefore become meaningful.

---

# **CORIN'S FAVORITE MOMENT**

Corin's favorite part is often not eating.

It is watching everyone else eat something she made.

She sometimes sits quietly with a cup of jasmine tea while the others talk around the table.

If someone compliments the food, she smiles.

If someone asks for another serving, she looks particularly pleased.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi has occasionally joined Corin in the kitchen.

The sight has become strangely nostalgic.

Lumi remembers when Corin was unable to perform many ordinary activities.

Now her daughter is standing in a kitchen, preparing food for everyone.

Lumi once watched Corin knead dough and quietly said:

> **"You've come a long way."**

Corin smiled.

> **"Yeah."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin has survived things that no ordinary person should ever have experienced.

She spent years dead.

She was sustained by Ether.

She lost limbs.

She lost her voice.

She believed she was nothing more than a conscious doll.

Now she has a heartbeat.

She bleeds.

She breathes.

She drinks tea.

She flies.

And lately, she has been spending her evenings in the kitchen.

Baking something sweet.

Preparing a pot of soup.

Or slowly roasting meat for everyone at the SAFEHOUSE.

It is a remarkably ordinary activity.

And that may be exactly why Corin enjoys it so much.

**End of SAFEHOUSE Domestic Activity Report**
