# **JOINT RECOVERY RECORD**

## **KCA / CENTRAL EXECUTION DEPARTMENT**

### **EXECUTIVE STATUS UPDATE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Executive Personnel Only

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following a private meeting between Director **Belle Merryweather** and **Lindervene "Endmin" Merryweather**, a marked improvement in Director Belle's behavior was observed.

The conversation was conducted without witnesses.

No audio or video recording was made.

By mutual agreement, the contents remain **CLASSIFIED**.

Only the outcome has been entered into the official record.

---

## **OBSERVED CHANGES**

In the days following the meeting, personnel documented gradual changes consistent with Belle's previous baseline.

Observed improvements included:

- Increased casual conversation.
    
- More frequent interaction with personnel.
    
- Return of spontaneous humor.
    
- Greater participation in social activities.
    
- Occasional laughter during meetings.
    
- Renewed visits to Heaven's Blacksite.
    

The Director's office also began to regain small personal touches.

The atmosphere throughout KCA Headquarters was widely noted as lighter.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL STATUS**

Medical staff concluded that Belle had made significant emotional progress.

However, they also noted that recovery should not be mistaken for complete resolution.

Belle remains particularly vulnerable to deliberate reminders of traumatic experiences associated with her former identity.

These topics are considered known psychological triggers and should be avoided unless discussed voluntarily by Belle in an appropriate therapeutic setting.

---

## **WORKPLACE GUIDANCE**

Personnel are reminded to maintain a professional and respectful working environment.

Deliberately using known personal trauma to provoke, intimidate, or harass another employee is strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action.

Respect for colleagues extends beyond physical safety to include psychological well-being.

---

## **STATUS**

Director Belle Merryweather has resumed her duties with her characteristic warmth and professionalism.

Joint operations between the Krampus Compliance Authority, the Central Execution Department, and Aperture Industries continue as normal.

The details of the conversation that led to Belle's recovery remain known only to Belle and Endmin.

Those records are sealed.

**End of Report.**
