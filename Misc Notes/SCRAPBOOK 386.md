# **COREY — VOLUNTARY CELESTIAL MANIFESTATION CONTROL**

**Classification:** Level 4 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corey / Solaria / Luna  
**Status:** Confirmed Development

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has confirmed a significant development in Corey's manifestation abilities.

Previously, Corey's transformations into **Solaria** and **Luna** were primarily dependent on environmental conditions:

- Sunlight exposure increased Solaria manifestation.
    
- Moonlight exposure increased Luna manifestation.
    

This limitation is no longer present.

Corey can now intentionally shift into either form regardless of the current time.

---

# **NEW CAPABILITY**

Corey is now capable of voluntary transformation into:

## **Solaria**

Solar manifestation.

Previously associated with:

- Daylight.
    
- Sun exposure.
    
- Solar magic.
    

## **Luna**

Lunar manifestation.

Previously associated with:

- Nighttime.
    
- Moonlight.
    
- Lunar magic.
    

The current time of day no longer prevents either transformation.

---

# **TRANSFORMATION CONTROL**

The transformation is now fully intentional.

Corey can choose:

- Whether to transform.
    
- Which manifestation to enter.
    
- When to return to normal.
    

No external trigger is required.

---

# **SOLARIA — UPDATED OBSERVATIONS**

Solaria can now appear during nighttime.

The manifestation remains stable without direct sunlight.

However, sunlight continues to naturally strengthen her abilities.

Observed characteristics:

- Golden solar glow.
    
- Sunlight-based magic.
    
- Light manipulation.
    
- Sunlight wings.
    
- Increased confidence and wisdom.
    

---

# **LUNA — UPDATED OBSERVATIONS**

Luna can now appear during daytime.

The manifestation remains stable without direct moonlight.

Moonlight continues to naturally strengthen her abilities.

Observed characteristics:

- Azure lunar glow.
    
- Moonlight-based magic.
    
- Lunar gravitational field.
    
- Voice mimicry.
    
- Calm and peaceful personality.
    

---

# **OPEN SKY REQUIREMENT UPDATE**

The previous requirement for celestial light access has changed.

Previously:

> Solaria and Luna required direct access to their respective celestial sources for certain abilities.

Current status:

Transformation:

> **No longer requires open sky.**

Corey can transform indoors.

---

Light-based disguise:

> **Still requires open sky.**

The reason is believed to be related to the need for external celestial light manipulation.

Researchers noted that Corey does not view Solaria and Luna as separate entities.

They remain:

- Her memories.
    
- Her emotions.
    
- Her identity.
    

The transformation is considered a change in expression rather than a replacement.
