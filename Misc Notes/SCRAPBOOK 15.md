# **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE**

## **RESIDENT WELFARE LOG**

### **ONGOING OBSERVATION – RECURRENT NIGHTMARES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Medical & Resident Privacy

---

## **SUMMARY**

Over several weeks, Director **Belle Merryweather** has experienced multiple episodes of interrupted sleep due to recurring nightmares.

The incidents have not been reported by Belle herself.

The pattern was observed solely by **Endmin Merryweather**, who shares a bed with Belle in Project SAFEHOUSE.

At Belle's request, the matter has remained private.

---

## **OBSERVED PATTERN**

The episodes generally follow the same routine.

- Belle awakens between **02:00 and 04:00**.
    
- She remains quiet and avoids making sudden movements.
    
- She carefully slips out of bed.
    
- She ensures Endmin remains asleep before leaving the room.
    
- She spends time alone in the SAFEHOUSE living area until she has calmed down.
    

On several occasions, Belle has returned before sunrise as though nothing had happened.

---

## **BEHAVIORAL NOTES**

Belle consistently attempts to avoid disturbing anyone else.

She has been observed:

- Walking barefoot to reduce noise.
    
- Closing doors slowly.
    
- Avoiding turning on bright lights.
    
- Sitting quietly beneath a blanket.
    
- Reading books until morning.
    
- Looking out the SAFEHOUSE windows toward the Starlands.
    

No other residents have reported being awakened by these events.

---

## **ENDMIN'S OBSERVATION**

Despite Belle's efforts, Endmin gradually noticed the pattern.

She became aware that Belle's side of the bed would occasionally be empty long before sunrise.

Rather than confronting her immediately, Endmin chose to quietly check on her whenever it happened.

Most nights, she would find Belle sitting alone in silence.

Sometimes neither of them spoke.

Endmin would simply sit beside her.

Belle would quietly lean against her.

---

## **CONVERSATION LOG**

During one early morning, Endmin softly asked:

> "...Another nightmare?"

Belle hesitated before giving a small nod.

> "...I didn't want to wake you."

Endmin gently replied,

> "...I'd rather wake up and know you're okay."

Belle looked down.

> "...Sorry."

Endmin shook her head.

> "...You never have to apologize for that."

The conversation ended there.

Nothing more needed to be said.

---

## **MEDICAL NOTE**

No physical abnormalities have been observed following these episodes.

The concern is limited to interrupted sleep and fatigue the following day.

Medical staff have **not** been informed, in accordance with Belle's wishes.

Endmin continues to monitor the situation informally.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

**Endmin**

> "She always tries to carry it by herself."

> "...She doesn't have to."

---

No additional personnel comments are attached.

At the time of this report, **no one else is aware of the recurring nightmares**.

---

## **STATUS**

Belle continues to perform her duties without complaint, often masking any signs of poor sleep.

The recurring nightmares remain a private matter shared only between Belle and Endmin.

Endmin has elected to quietly support Belle during these nights, respecting her privacy while ensuring she is never truly alone.

**End of Resident Welfare Log**
