### **PERSONNEL PROFILE REVISION**

# **COREY — THE HAT PROTOCOL**

**Classification:** Level 4 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corey / Solaria / Luna

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has identified the primary reason Corey consistently wears soft headwear.

The hats are not merely for warmth or comfort.

They serve as a **practical means of preventing accidental manifestation** of her alternate magical states.

Corey prefers remaining in her normal state.

---

# **COREY'S HEADWEAR**

Corey commonly wears:

- Beanies.
    
- Berets.
    
- Soft winter hats.
    
- Her oversized Mart-style top hat.
    
- Arcturus's fedora.
    
- Other comfortable headwear.
    
- Occasionally, a sleeping Mart.
    

The practical effect is the same:

> **No direct sunlight reaches her head.**

Without sunlight reaching her uncovered head, Solaria cannot normally manifest.

---

# **WHY COREY PREFERS THE HAT**

Corey's reason is simple.

She does not dislike Solaria or Luna.

She simply prefers being **herself**.

Solaria is more confident and capable.

Luna is wiser and more peaceful.

Both are aspects of Corey.

But Corey considers her normal state the most comfortable one for everyday life.

When asked why she always puts her hat back on after a manifestation, she answered:

> "...I like being Corey."

---

# **LUNA'S PERSONALITY**

Further observation has confirmed that Luna differs from both Corey and Solaria.

Luna is:

- **Wiser.**
    
- **Slower in movement and speech.**
    
- **More peaceful.**
    
- **Quieter.**
    
- More contemplative.
    
- Less reactive.
    

The reduced pace does not indicate weakness or impairment.

Luna simply appears to operate at a much calmer rhythm.

She will sometimes spend several minutes quietly observing something before responding.

---

# **SOLARIA VS. LUNA**

The differences between the two manifestations are now more apparent.

|Trait|Corey|Solaria|Luna|
|---|---|---|---|
|General demeanor|Quiet|Confident|Peaceful|
|Speech|Normal|Direct|Soft / Quiet|
|Decision-making|Careful|Decisive|Deliberate|
|Intelligence|Normal|High|High|
|Wisdom|Normal|High|Very high|
|Movement|Normal|Fast / energetic|Slow / graceful|
|Magic|Limited/latent|Sun magic|Moon magic|
|Radiance|None|Yellow|Soft azure|
|Wings|None|Solar|Lunar|

These distinctions are considered expressions of different aspects of the same individual.

---

# **LUNA AND THE MARTS**

The Marts appear particularly comfortable around Luna.

Her slower movements make her easy for them to approach.

They frequently gather beneath her lunar wings.

A typical interaction consists of:

> **Mart:** "...Luna."

> **Luna:** "...Yes?"

> **Mart:** "...Quiet."

> **Luna:** "...It is."

> **Mart:** "...Nice."

Luna generally responds with a small smile.

---

# **THE HAT INCIDENT**

One evening, Corey was sitting outside with Corin.

Her beret slipped off.

Before either of them could retrieve it, moonlight reached her uncovered head.

The temperature around her dropped.

The yellow glow did not appear.

Instead, a soft blue radiance formed.

Corin immediately recognized the change.

> "...Luna."

Luna quietly looked at her.

> "...Hello."

Corin sighed.

> "...You know why she wears the hats, right?"

Luna nodded.

> "...Yes."

> "...She wants to be Corey."

Corin smiled.

> "...Exactly."

Luna picked up the beret.

She placed it gently back onto Corey's head.

The transformation ended.

Corey blinked.

> "...What happened?"

Corin answered:

> "...You became Luna again."

Corey sighed.

> "...I really need a hat with a better strap."

---

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

Lumi understands why Corey prefers her hats.

> "...The sun and moon are both beautiful."

She smiled.

> "...But she shouldn't have to be either one all the time."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corey's hats have therefore acquired an unexpected purpose.

They are not merely clothing.

They are a boundary.

A simple way for Corey to decide:

> **"Today, I want to be me."**

Solaria can bring the confidence of the sun.

Luna can bring the wisdom and peace of the moon.

But beneath the beanie, beret, oversized Mart hat, or whatever else Corey happens to be wearing...

There is simply Corey.

And that is exactly how she prefers it.
