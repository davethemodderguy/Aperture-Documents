## **SUMMARY**

Director Belle Merryweather normally wears contact lenses to correct her eyesight.

During an otherwise harmless afternoon, Taiga accidentally damaged Belle's contact lenses beyond use.

With no spare contacts immediately available, Belle was issued a pair of prescription glasses until replacements could be obtained.

---

## **EVENT LOG**

The exact sequence of events remains disputed.

What is known is that, shortly afterward—

> "..."

Belle blinked several times.

> "...Everything's blurry."

The damaged contact lenses were removed by Medical.

Fortunately, Belle had previously kept an up-to-date pair of prescription glasses in her room.

She put them on.

Paused.

Looked around.

> "...I can see again."

---

## **TAIGA'S RESPONSE**

Taiga immediately realized what had happened.

> "...Belle."

> "...I'm really, really sorry."

Belle simply smiled.

> "It's okay."

> "Accidents happen."

Taiga still insisted on accompanying Belle to Medical to help arrange replacement contact lenses.

---

## **OBSERVATIONS**

Belle adapted to wearing glasses almost immediately.

The primary difference noted by personnel was aesthetic rather than behavioral.

Her work performance remained unchanged.

## **STATUS**

Replacement contact lenses were ordered the same day.

Belle continues to carry both her contact lenses and prescription glasses, wearing whichever is more convenient.

Medical recommends all personnel who rely on corrective lenses keep a backup pair available.

Taiga has since become noticeably more careful around Belle's face.
