### PSYCHOLOGICAL & RESIDENTIAL WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-NERI-103

**STATUS:** ACTIVE MONITORING  
**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**OBSERVATION:** Reduced visible presence within SAFEHOUSE

---

# 1. SUMMARY

Neri remains physically present within the Blacksite.

She has not disappeared.

She has not left SAFEHOUSE.

She continues eating, sleeping when able, receiving medical support, and maintaining her personal belongings.

Yet she has become unusually difficult to encounter.

Her visible movement throughout the Blacksite has decreased substantially.

At present, there are two locations where personnel are most likely to encounter her:

**Her room.**

And:

**The cafeteria.**

Very little occurs between those locations.

---

# 2. TYPICAL SIGHTINGS

A normal sighting of Neri now consists of one of the following:

### Her Room

Neri is inside, usually:

- reading;
    
- drawing;
    
- resting;
    
- working on chemistry;
    
- using her laptop or other belongings;
    
- or simply sitting quietly.
    

She rarely invites anyone inside.

She rarely leaves because somebody came to visit.

---

### The Cafeteria

Neri may be found sitting at a table.

Usually alone.

She may be:

- eating;
    
- drinking something;
    
- reading;
    
- drawing;
    
- or simply sitting there.
    

Her presence in the cafeteria is therefore not unusual.

What is unusual is what happens around her.

---

# 3. THE MISSING WALK

Personnel have begun noticing something else.

Neri is almost never seen **walking alongside another person.**

There are no ordinary hallway conversations.

No walking together toward the cafeteria.

No casual trips between rooms.

No:

> "Come on, Neri."

No:

> "I'll walk with you."

No:

> "Where are you going?"

No small conversations while moving from one place to another.

Neri appears at locations.

She rarely appears **between** locations.

---

# 4. ALICE

This is particularly noticeable with Alice.

Previously, the two could be seen together around SAFEHOUSE.

Alice might sit with Neri.

Neri might accompany Alice somewhere.

Alice might simply follow Neri because she wanted company.

These interactions have become rare.

It is now uncommon to see:

**Alice + Neri**

walking through the Blacksite together.

If Alice encounters Neri in a hallway, Neri may stop.

She may acknowledge Alice.

She may even remain nearby for a short period.

But she rarely initiates continuing the interaction.

If Alice begins walking somewhere, Neri generally does not follow.

---

# 5. JANE DOE

The change is even more pronounced with Jane.

Jane is one of the people who historically spent significant time around Neri.

That has largely stopped.

Neri may still encounter Jane.

Neri may still recognize her.

Neri may still care about her.

But Neri rarely chooses to walk alongside her.

Jane has occasionally entered a hallway and noticed Neri ahead.

Rather than calling out:

> "Hey, My Cute Seal!"

Jane has increasingly chosen to simply observe.

Because Neri often hears her anyway.

Neri will sometimes stop.

Look back.

And then continue walking alone.

---

# 6. BAO

Bao remains the major exception in terms of practical contact.

Neri still depends upon Bao for medical support.

She therefore cannot completely withdraw from her.

However, even Bao has noticed a difference.

Neri no longer casually follows Bao around the SAFEHOUSE.

She does not sit beside her while she works unless medically necessary.

She does not spontaneously enter the kitchen.

She does not simply wander over because she wants company.

Interactions with Bao have become increasingly functional.

> Medication.

> Blood support.

> Food.

> Medical observation.

Then Neri leaves.

Bao has stated that this is more concerning than Neri simply being quiet.

---

# 7. ABSENCE OF ORDINARY INTERACTION

The problem is not that Neri has no contact with other residents.

The problem is the **quality of that contact.**

There is little spontaneity.

Neri does not casually:

- join conversations;
    
- follow people;
    
- sit beside someone without a reason;
    
- ask what someone is doing;
    
- show someone a drawing;
    
- ask someone to come with her;
    
- or interrupt someone simply because she wants company.
    

Every interaction appears to require justification.

If she cannot identify a sufficient reason to approach someone, she generally does not.

# 9. THE ABSENCE BECOMES NOTICEABLE

This has produced an unusual phenomenon within SAFEHOUSE.

Everyone knows Neri is there.

Yet people are beginning to realize they almost never **see** her.

They see:

- her room;
    
- her empty chair;
    
- her books;
    
- her drawings;
    
- her cafeteria table;
    
- her cup;
    
- her coat;
    
- her keycard.
    

But they rarely see:

> **Neri walking with someone.**

That distinction has become increasingly uncomfortable.

---

---

# 12. PSYCHOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION

The reduced movement is consistent with Neri's recently established internal rule:

> **Do not bother anyone.**

Walking beside somebody has no practical purpose.

Following someone could interrupt them.

Joining someone could require conversation.

Entering someone's room could be intrusive.

Asking to accompany someone could be perceived as needy.

Therefore:

> **Stay where you are.**

This produces an unintended form of social disappearance.

Neri is not being excluded.

She is excluding herself.

---

# 13. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

C.O.R.E. does not currently assess Neri as intentionally attempting to disappear permanently.

She continues to:

- eat;
    
- receive medical care;
    
- maintain her room;
    
- draw;
    
- read;
    
- interact when necessary;
    
- and remain physically present.
    

Her behavior is better characterized as:

> **Minimizing her footprint.**

She appears to be attempting to occupy as little social space as possible.

---

# 14. THE UNINTENDED RESULT

Ironically, this behavior produces exactly the situation Neri fears.

She believes:

> "If I don't bother people, they'll have less reason to worry about me."

Instead:

People worry because they don't see her.

She believes:

> "If I stay in my room, I won't interrupt anyone."

Instead:

People wonder whether she is okay.

She believes:

> "If I don't follow anyone, I won't slow them down."

Instead:

People notice that she is always alone.

She believes:

> "If I only go to the cafeteria when I need food, I'm not causing problems."

Instead:

People notice an empty chair beside her.

---

# 15. C.O.R.E. RECOMMENDATION

Personnel are advised **not** to respond by forcing Neri into constant social interaction.

Do not:

- drag her into group activities;
    
- demand that she spend time with people;
    
- repeatedly ask why she is alone;
    
- follow her everywhere;
    
- or turn her reduced social activity into another source of pressure.
    

Instead, residents are encouraged to make ordinary companionship available without requiring Neri to request it.

For example:

Someone may simply sit at the same cafeteria table.

Someone may walk beside her without demanding conversation.

Someone may leave the door open.

Someone may invite her along without making her feel obligated.

The objective is not to make Neri socialize.

The objective is to demonstrate:

> **Being near someone is not the same thing as bothering them.**

---

# 16. FINAL OBSERVATION

Neri is still here.

That matters.

But she has become almost entirely stationary within the social geography of SAFEHOUSE.

She exists in her room.

She exists in the cafeteria.

Occasionally, she exists in the hallway between them.

What is missing is everything in between.

The casual moments.

The pointless walks.

The accidental conversations.

The:

> "Where are you going?"

The:

> "Can I come?"

The:

> "Wait for me."

The simple sight of Neri walking beside somebody because she wanted to.

Those moments used to happen.

They almost don't anymore.

And that absence may be more revealing than anything Neri has said.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Neri has not vanished.

She has simply made herself very difficult to find.

And perhaps that is exactly what she believes everyone wants.

— **END OBSERVATION**
