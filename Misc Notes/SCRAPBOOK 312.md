# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CROSS-COLONY OBSERVATION LOG**

### **SUBJECT: BLACKSITE & KCA MART INTERACTIONS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although the Heaven Blacksite and Krampus Compliance Authority colonies normally reside at separate facilities, they occasionally reunite.

This usually occurs when:

- Dialyn travels between the KCA and the Blacksite.
    
- Marts accompany supply transports.
    
- Joint training exercises are conducted.
    
- Personnel temporarily transfer between facilities.
    

Researchers initially expected territorial disputes.

None have ever been observed.

Instead...

The colonies greet one another like old friends.

---

# **FIRST CONTACT**

The moment two colonies become aware of each other's presence, they immediately begin bouncing toward one another.

Observers report the event resembles two groups of excited puppies meeting after a long separation.

The first words are almost always:

> "Hello!"

Followed by hundreds of additional greetings.

---

# **COMMUNICATION**

Despite sharing the same Ether-linked hivemind, Marts from different facilities still enjoy talking to one another.

Their conversations are...

Difficult to transcribe.

Example:

> "Hello!"

> "Friend!"

> "Book!"

> "Security!"

> "Cheese cracker!"

> "Happy!"

> "Mart!"

> "Mother!"

> "Safe!"

> "Tree!"

> "Together!"

> "Hello!"

Researchers have concluded these exchanges contain:

> **100% words.**

> **0% grammar.**

The participants nevertheless appear to understand each other perfectly.

---

# **TRANSLATION ATTEMPT**

During one reunion, the KCA Ambassador was asked to translate an ongoing discussion between approximately sixty Marts.

The conversation had already lasted nearly ten minutes.

After listening carefully, the Ambassador summarized:

> "They're catching up."

The researcher looked back at the colony.

One Mart had just shouted:

> "Cracker!"

Another enthusiastically replied:

> "Vent!"

The Ambassador nodded.

> "Exactly."

No further explanation was offered.

---

# **SHARED EXPERIENCES**

Topics commonly exchanged include:

Blacksite Marts:

> "Garden!"

> "Library!"

> "Mother!"

> "Mart!"

KCA Marts:

> "Camera!"

> "Security!"

> "Dialyn!"

> "Visitor!"

Every exchange ends the same way.

> "Friend!"

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Interactions between the Blacksite and KCA colonies remain entirely peaceful.

No disputes.

No territorial behavior.

No hierarchy.

Only enthusiastic reunions and conversations that continue to bewilder every linguist assigned to study them.

To human ears, the dialogue sounds like a random collection of unrelated words.

To the Marts...

It is a perfectly ordinary conversation between friends who haven't seen each other in a while.

The Ambassadors have politely declined to provide a full translation.

Their stated reason:

> "It's funnier this way."
