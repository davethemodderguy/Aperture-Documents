### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE / PERSONNEL CARE DIVISION**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — DENTAL CAPABILITY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — PERSONNEL CARE / MINOR SAFETY HAZARD  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** Normal

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Alice can bite.

This was already assumed to be physiologically possible.

It was not, however, fully appreciated by personnel until recently.

Alice possesses normal rabbit dentition.

Her front teeth are small.

Sharp.

Strong.

And capable of causing significantly more pain than her appearance would suggest.

---

# **THE INCIDENT**

An employee was attempting to move Alice's beanie after it had become slightly crooked.

Alice didn't want it moved.

She quietly said:

> "No."

The employee continued.

Alice bit their hand.

The employee immediately pulled away.

> "OW!"

Alice froze.

She looked at the employee.

Then at their hand.

> "...Sorry."

The employee stared at her.

> "You have _teeth._"

Alice blinked.

> "Alice is bunny."

> "...Right."

---

# **DENTAL ASSESSMENT**

Alice's teeth are functioning normally.

There is no anomalous enhancement detected.

They are simply rabbit teeth.

This means they can:

- Pierce skin.
    
- Cause bleeding.
    
- Leave significant bite marks.
    
- Hurt considerably.
    

Her size should **not** be used as an indication of bite strength.

---

# **WHY ALICE MAY BITE**

Alice does not normally bite people.

It is not considered typical behavior.

However, a bite may occur if Alice is:

- Frightened.
    
- Startled.
    
- Cornered.
    
- In pain.
    
- Extremely uncomfortable.
    
- Prevented from moving away.
    
- Having her boundaries ignored.
    

The bite should therefore be interpreted as a defensive response rather than aggression.

---

# **THE TAIL RULE**

Personnel are reminded that Alice's tail remains strictly off-limits.

This is particularly important.

If Alice does not want something touched, personnel should simply stop.

Do not attempt to restrain her unnecessarily.

Do not test whether she will bite.

Do not intentionally provoke a bite.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle was fascinated by the discovery.

> "Can you bite really hard?"

Alice nodded.

Belle immediately reconsidered her question.

> "...Never mind."

Alice giggled.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin's response was considerably simpler.

> "Alice, don't bite people."

Alice nodded.

> "Okay."

A pause.

> "...Unless they touch Alice's tail?"

Corin sighed.

> "Still try not to."

Alice thought about it.

> "Okay."

---

# **PERSONNEL WARNING**

Alice's appearance can create an inaccurate perception of vulnerability.

She is:

- Five years old.
    
- Lightweight.
    
- Weak.
    
- Frequently fatigued.
    
- Small enough to carry easily.
    

She also has:

- Rabbit teeth.
    
- An anomalous right eye.
    
- A surprisingly stubborn sense of personal boundaries.
    

Personnel should therefore avoid treating her like a plush toy.

**Alice is a child, not an object.**

If she says no—

stop.

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

Alice is still incredibly cute.

The white beanie.

The drooping ears.

The oversized hoodie.

The little boots.

The tiny feather-like tail.

The soft voice.

None of this changes one fundamental fact:

**She is a bunny.**

And bunnies have teeth.

Sharp little ones.

So please—

don't make her use them.
