# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-PSY-013**

### **ADDENDUM – OBSERVATIONAL NOTE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Restricted Psychological Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

Subsequent review of facility security footage and routine behavioral records found no documented indication that Blacksite personnel recognized the extent of Director Belle Merryweather's psychological state during the period following her isolation.

No incident report was filed at the time.

No medical referral was requested.

No welfare concern was formally raised.

---

## **POST-INCIDENT ANALYSIS**

Reviewers determined that Belle continued to perform her duties within expected operational standards.

She remained punctual.

She attended meetings.

She completed administrative work.

She responded appropriately when spoken to.

She continued to smile.

These outward behaviors resulted in no immediate suspicion from supervisors or colleagues.

---

## **BEHAVIORAL FINDINGS**

Only after later comparison with historical recordings did analysts identify subtle differences, including:

- Less spontaneous conversation.
    
- Shorter responses.
    
- Fewer expressions of excitement.
    
- Reduced initiation of social interaction.
    
- Increased preference for solitary activities.
    

Individually, each change appeared minor.

Collectively, they represented a significant departure from Belle's previously documented baseline.

At the time, however, the changes were not recognized as clinically significant.

---

## **CONCLUSION**

No evidence suggests that personnel intentionally ignored Belle's condition.

Rather, available records indicate that her outward presentation successfully concealed the severity of her emotional distress.

The absence of intervention was attributed to the lack of observable indicators that would have prompted immediate concern.

By the time the pattern became apparent through retrospective analysis, the period in question had already passed.

**End of Addendum.**
