### **CELESTIAL ARCHITECTURE / BEHAVIORAL REPORT**

# **GUIDING LIGHT & CURIOUS LIGHT — SIBLING CONFLICT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Celestial Authority  
**Subjects:** Guiding Light / Curious Light  
**Relationship:** Siblings / Co-Architects  
**Status:** **ACTIVE — NON-HOSTILE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Despite their status as the two Architects responsible for forging the foundations of reality and its realms, **Guiding Light and Curious Light are not perfectly harmonious.**

They are siblings.

They argue.

They disagree.

And sometimes...

**they fight.**

Their immense power does not make them immune to ordinary emotions, frustrations, or sibling disagreements.

As Guiding Light herself once put it:

> **"Being an Architect doesn't make you perfect."**

---

# **COMMON CAUSES**

Their disagreements generally originate from differences in personality and philosophy.

### **Guiding Light**

Prefers:

- Patience.
    
- Observation.
    
- Guidance.
    
- Careful decisions.
    
- Allowing things to develop naturally.
    

### **Curious Light**

Prefers:

- Experimentation.
    
- Immediate action.
    
- Construction.
    
- Discovery.
    
- Testing things himself.
    

This occasionally creates friction.

Guiding Light may believe Curious Light is acting recklessly.

Curious Light may believe Guiding Light is being unnecessarily cautious.

Neither is particularly fond of being told that they're wrong.

---

# **TYPICAL ARGUMENT**

A common disagreement may begin with something as simple as:

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "You shouldn't do that."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Why?"

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Because it's dangerous."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "That's why I want to test it."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "That's exactly why you shouldn't."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "You're overreacting."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "And you're underestimating it."

At this point, both usually stop listening.

---

# **WHEN ARGUMENTS BECOME FIGHTS**

Most disagreements remain verbal.

Occasionally, however, their frustration escalates.

Their manifested forms may clash.

Golden and blue light can fill the surrounding area as they fight.

Their abilities are powerful enough that ordinary personnel are instructed to **leave the area immediately** if this occurs.

Neither Architect typically intends to seriously injure the other.

The conflict is more comparable to an extremely powerful sibling argument that has gotten out of hand.

---

# **THE SHATTERED FORMS**

Their fights do not permanently damage either Architect.

Guiding Light's already-shattered halo and wings may scatter further during a confrontation.

Curious Light's golden manifestations may fracture or disperse.

Neither remains permanently injured.

Their forms simply reconstruct or return to their preferred state afterward.

---

# **BLACKSITE INCIDENT**

One incident occurred inside a Blacksite workshop.

Curious Light had been working on an experimental device.

Guiding Light arrived.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Curious."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "...What?"

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "You shouldn't activate that."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "I've already tested it."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Not enough."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "I know what I'm doing."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "I know."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Then let me finish."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "No."

Silence.

Curious Light stared at her.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "...You always do this."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Because you always do this."

The argument escalated.

Fortunately, the workshop was evacuated before either of them began using their abilities.

Miss Neko later found the two sitting several meters apart.

Neither was speaking.

---

# **LUMI'S ASSESSMENT**

Lumi is unsurprised by their arguments.

When asked how often the two Architects fight, she answered:

> **"Often enough."**

> **"They're siblings."**

When asked who usually wins:

> **"Neither."**

> **"They eventually get tired of arguing."**

Guiding Light immediately corrected her:

> **"I don't get tired."**

Lumi:

> **"...You know what I mean."**

---

# **ASTRAL'S OBSERVATION**

Astral has developed a simple rule.

If Guiding Light and Curious Light begin arguing:

> **"Don't get involved."**

If they start fighting:

> **"Definitely don't get involved."**

If Curious Light asks someone to help with his argument:

> **"Run."**

---

# **AFTERMATH**

The important distinction is that their conflicts do not represent hatred.

They remain siblings.

They still care about each other.

They still work together.

They still communicate.

And after particularly severe arguments, one of them will eventually approach the other.

Usually without an apology.

Usually with food, a new invention, or an unrelated question.

This is apparently their version of reconciliation.

---

# **AN EXAMPLE**

After one argument, Curious Light approached Guiding Light several hours later.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "...I made something."

Guiding Light looked at him.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "What is it?"

> **Curious Light:**  
> "You'll like it."

She looked at the golden device.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "...Is it dangerous?"

> **Curious Light:**  
> "...Not anymore."

Guiding Light sighed.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Come here."

Curious Light smiled.

The argument was over.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Guiding Light and Curious Light may be among the most powerful beings in existence.

They may have helped forge entire realms.

They may perceive and manipulate reality on a scale impossible for ordinary beings to comprehend.

But none of that makes them perfect.

They are still siblings.

They can disagree.

They can become frustrated.

They can hurt each other's feelings.

And sometimes they can fight.

What matters is that they always eventually come back to each other.

Reality may have two Architects.

But those Architects are still a brother and sister who occasionally need to be reminded that being powerful does not mean being right.
