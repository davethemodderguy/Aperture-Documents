# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – MEMORY RECONSTRUCTION DISTRESS EVENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **SUMMARY**

Following Corin Lockwood's increasing difficulty reconnecting with her past, Lumi attempted a new approach.

Instead of focusing on events, Lumi began describing who Corin used to be.

Her childhood.

Her personality.

Her habits.

Her appearance.

The intention was simple.

To remind Corin that the person she was before the operation was still part of her.

However...

The result was the opposite.

---

# **LUMI'S STORIES**

Lumi spoke gently.

She described a younger Corin.

A child who was always curious.

A child who carried books larger than herself.

A child who would become excited whenever she learned something new.

She described her smile.

Her expressions.

The way she would become focused whenever solving a problem.

> "You used to get so serious when you were thinking."

> "You would even forget to blink."

Lumi laughed softly.

> "You looked so proud whenever you figured something out."

---

She described Norma.

How young Norma would hide during thunderstorms.

How Corin would try to act brave for her sister.

How the two would sit together until the storm passed.

---

She described Arcturus.

How he would return from playing outside covered in dirt.

How every animal seemed to trust him.

How he always tried to act older than he was because he wanted to protect his sisters.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

At first, Corin listened.

She wanted to remember.

She wanted those memories to return.

But slowly...

The stories began to hurt.

Because Lumi was describing someone Corin could no longer see.

Someone who felt distant.

Someone who felt like a stranger.

Corin eventually interrupted.

> "...Stop."

Lumi immediately stopped.

Corin looked down.

> "...I don't remember her."

Silence.

> "...I know you're talking about me."

> "...But I don't feel like that's me."

---

# **THE PROBLEM**

Medical staff determined that the memories themselves were not the source of distress.

The distress came from the separation between:

**Who Corin was told she had been.**

And:

**Who Corin believed she was now.**

To Corin, the stories felt less like memories returning.

They felt like hearing about another person who had been taken away.

A person who was happy.

A person who was loved.

A person who did not carry the weight she carried now.

---

# **LUMI'S REALIZATION**

Lumi immediately stopped telling those stories without invitation.

During a private discussion, she admitted:

> "I wanted her to remember the daughter I lost."

She paused.

> "...I forgot she was sitting right in front of me."

The statement was recorded as a significant moment in Lumi's understanding of Corin's recovery.

Corin did not need to become the child she was before.

She needed to accept that the child and the person she was now were the same person.

---

# **CORIN'S STATEMENT**

Later that evening, Corin spoke quietly.

> "...I miss someone I don't remember being."

Lumi stayed silent.

Corin continued.

> "...Everyone keeps telling me she was wonderful."

> "...I believe them."

A pause.

> "...I just wish I could meet her."

Lumi answered softly.

> "You already did."

Corin looked confused.

Lumi smiled.

> "She grew up."

> "She survived."

> "She's sitting here."

---

# **CURRENT APPROACH**

Lumi has adjusted how she interacts with Corin.

She no longer tries to rebuild the past all at once.

Instead, she focuses on the present.

Small things.

Daily things.

New memories.

A cup of tea.

A conversation.

A quiet evening.

The goal is no longer to make Corin become the person she was.

It is to help Corin understand that she never stopped being that person.

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

> "A damaged memory does not mean a damaged person."

> "Corin is not a forgotten child waiting to be restored."

> "She is a person still writing her story."

---

# **STATUS**

Corin remains under observation with continued support from Lumi and KCA medical staff.

Memory recovery is no longer considered the primary objective.

The focus has shifted toward acceptance.

Not remembering every detail of who she was.

But allowing herself to believe that she is still someone worth knowing.

**End of Psychological Observation Report**
