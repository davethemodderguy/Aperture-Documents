# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **PERSONNEL APPEARANCE ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: DIALYN – SIGNATURE EARRINGS**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although Dialyn generally dresses in a practical and professional manner, she consistently wears one distinctive accessory.

A pair of rhombus-shaped star earrings.

Personnel have become so accustomed to seeing them that many employees remark Dialyn looks "incomplete" without them.

To date, no one has ever documented her without the earrings.

---

# **DESCRIPTION**

**Type:** Drop Earrings

**Shape:** Rhombus Star

**Outline:** Metallic Gold

**Center:** Deep Crimson Red

The polished gold border outlines a stylized four-pointed rhombus star, while the crimson center catches the light with a subtle shine.

Despite their elegant appearance, the earrings are lightweight enough that Dialyn comfortably wears them every day.

---

# **OBSERVATIONS**

The earrings are considered one of Dialyn's defining features alongside:

- Her braided black-and-white hair.
    
- Her warm smile.
    
- Her white fluffy cat.
    
- The soft clicking of her heels as she walks through the office.
    

Several coworkers have commented that the earrings are usually the first thing they notice when Dialyn enters a room.

# **DIALYN'S COMMENT**

When asked why she wears the same earrings every day, Dialyn gently touched one of the dangling stars.

She smiled.

> "...I just like them."

After a moment, she added with a small laugh:

> "...Besides..."

> "...People recognize me from across the hallway because they catch the light."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The rhombus star earrings have become part of Dialyn's recognizable appearance throughout both the Krampus Compliance Authority and Heaven Blacksite.

Artists preparing personnel portraits are specifically instructed not to omit them.

As Belle once remarked while reviewing an illustration:

> "...That's not Dialyn."

She pointed at the empty ears.

> "...She forgot her stars."

The portrait was corrected shortly afterward.
