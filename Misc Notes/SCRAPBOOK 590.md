### **SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION LOG**

# **CORIN — JAR OCCUPANCY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Form:** Miniature Feline  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE  
**Status:** Comfortable / Contained

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Corin has once again placed herself inside a glass jar.

The jar is open.

Corin is completely unharmed.

She is simply sitting at the bottom with her wings folded neatly against her body.

Her two tails are curled beside her.

Only her little head and ears are clearly visible above the rim.

---

# **BEHAVIOR**

Corin appears to genuinely enjoy the enclosed space.

She has positioned herself comfortably and shows no immediate desire to leave.

When someone approaches the jar, she looks up at them.

If they ask:

> **"Corin, do you want to come out?"**

She responds:

> **"Mew."**

She then remains exactly where she is.

---

# **WHY THE JAR?**

No definitive explanation exists.

Possible factors include:

- Security.
    
- Warmth.
    
- Reduced sensory stimulation.
    
- The enclosed feeling.
    
- Simple feline instinct.
    

The most likely explanation is considerably less complicated:

> **Corin likes being in jars.**

---

# **WINGS**

Her wings fold tightly enough that they fit inside the container with her.

The feathers remain completely dormant.

No Ether activity has been detected.

Corin occasionally shifts one wing to get comfortable before settling again.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

Belle discovered her first.

She looked into the jar.

Corin looked back.

Belle:

> **"...You're doing this again?"**

Corin:

> **"Mew."**

Belle sighed.

She left the jar where it was, provided the opening remained unobstructed.

---

# **SAFETY REQUIREMENTS**

Corin may remain inside an open container provided that:

- The opening remains unobstructed.
    
- She can exit independently.
    
- The container is adequately ventilated.
    
- Nothing can fall into it.
    
- Her wings are not compressed.
    
- Someone remains aware of her location.
    

The jar is **not** to be sealed.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin is currently sitting inside a glass jar.

She is comfortable.

She is safe.

She can leave whenever she wants.

She simply doesn't want to.

A tiny white winged cat sits quietly at the bottom of the jar, looking out through the glass.

Every few minutes, she gives a small:

> **"Mew."**

Then goes back to sitting there.

**End of SAFEHOUSE Observation Log**
