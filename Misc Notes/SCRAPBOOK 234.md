# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **INTERNAL ADMINISTRATIVE LOG**

### **PERSONNEL NOTICE**

## **SUBJECT: RETURN OF SENIOR PERSONNEL FROM HEAVEN BLACKSITE**

**Classification:** Internal Distribution

---

# **OVERVIEW**

For several months, a significant portion of the Krampus Compliance Authority's senior personnel maintained an unusually consistent presence at Heaven Blacksite.

Personnel frequently noted the absence of several key figures, including:

- Belle Merryweather
    
- Corin Lockwood
    
- Corey Lockwood
    
- Arcturus Lockwood
    
- Norma Lockwood
    
- Keith
    
- Cinder
    

Although all official duties continued to be completed on schedule, questions regarding their prolonged stay became increasingly common.

Management repeatedly declined to comment.

Today, that changed.

---

# **THE BRIEFING**

At 09:00, all available KCA personnel were assembled in the main conference hall.

Belle stood at the front of the room.

Beside her were Corin, Corey, Keith, Cinder, Arcturus, and Norma.

Many employees immediately noticed that Corin looked tired.

Others noticed Corey quietly reaching over to hold Corin's hand before the meeting began.

Neither commented.

Belle opened the briefing.

> "Many of you have been wondering why several members of senior staff have been working from Heaven Blacksite."

She paused.

> "You deserve an explanation."

---

# **THE EXPLANATION**

Belle did not disclose classified research.

She did not discuss the Corin Paradox.

She did not mention parallel universes.

She did not discuss ongoing investigations.

Instead, she spoke plainly.

> "One of our own needed help."

Silence filled the room.

Belle continued.

> "Sometimes helping someone means paperwork."

> "Sometimes it means staying nearby."

> "Sometimes it means making sure they aren't facing difficult days alone."

She looked toward Corin and Corey.

> "That's what we've been doing."

---

# **REACTIONS**

The room remained unusually quiet.

Several employees exchanged glances.

Many had expected an operational emergency.

Others had expected a classified investigation.

Very few had expected the answer to be so personal.

No further questions were asked.

---

# **AFTER THE MEETING**

As employees began returning to work, several quietly approached Corin and Corey.

No one requested classified information.

No one asked intrusive questions.

Most simply offered ordinary gestures.

A cup of coffee.

A greeting.

An invitation to lunch.

One accountant quietly placed a handwritten note on Corin's desk before leaving.

It read:

> **"We're glad you're both back."**

No signature was attached.

The handwriting was never identified.

The note remained pinned to Corin's workstation.

---

# **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

**Keith**

> "You don't always notice someone's carrying something heavy."

> "Sometimes the best thing you can do is carry a little of it with them."

---

**Cinder**

> "The work never stopped."

She smiled.

> "...We just made sure people came before paperwork."

---

**Arcturus**

> "The Authority exists because people protect people."

> "That shouldn't stop when the paperwork ends."

---

**Norma**

> "Thank you."

She offered no further explanation.

Everyone understood who she was thanking.

---

**Corin**

Corin stood before the employees for several moments.

She appeared unsure what to say.

Finally, she bowed slightly.

> "...I'm sorry for worrying everyone."

Several voices immediately answered from around the room.

> "Don't apologize."

> "Just take care of yourself."

Corin looked genuinely surprised.

---

**Corey**

Corey smiled at the gathered staff.

Her eyes were noticeably damp.

> "...Thank you for waiting for us."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Operations at the Krampus Compliance Authority have returned to normal.

Senior personnel have resumed their regular duties.

The questions surrounding their prolonged stay at Heaven Blacksite have been answered—not with classified details, but with a simple truth.

Sometimes the most important work an organization can do is not filing reports or completing investigations.

Sometimes...

It is making sure that one of its own is not left to face hardship alone.

**End of Personnel Notice**
