# **ADDENDUM – PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

**Attached to:** KCA-PSY-101  
**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Executive Personnel Only

---

### **Endmin Merryweather**

Endmin read the report several times before setting it down.

She remained silent for nearly a minute.

Finally, she spoke.

> "...I thought giving her space was the right thing."

She looked toward the window.

> "...Then I thought she'd tell us when she was ready."

Her voice grew quieter.

> "...She was waiting."

> "...And we didn't realize it."

After another long silence:

> "...I'm not losing my sister."

---

### **Keith**

Keith removed his gloves before speaking.

> "...I was assigned to protect the Director."

He stared at the dictionary.

> "...I kept watching for people trying to hurt her."

He slowly closed the book.

> "...I forgot pain doesn't always walk through the front door."

---

### **Cinder**

Cinder sat with her hands folded.

She had no paperwork in front of her.

No briefcase.

No fire.

Just the report.

> "...I've seen this before."

She took a slow breath.

> "...People stop asking."

> "...Then they stop expecting."

She looked at Endmin.

> "...We're late."

Another pause.

> "...Late doesn't mean finished."

---

### **Alice**

Alice finished reviewing Belle's medical file before speaking.

> "...The body tells us when a bone is broken."

She closed the folder.

> "...The mind isn't always that generous."

She looked around the room.

> "...This isn't something medicine fixes by itself."

---

### **Lucy**

Lucy remained composed.

When she finally spoke, her voice was calm.

> "...Regret is only useful if it changes what we do next."

She looked at the others.

> "...If Belle has built walls..."

> "...Then we knock."

> "...We don't leave."

---

### **Lucius Merryweather**

Lucius quietly picked up the dictionary.

He turned to the marked page.

Then closed it.

> "...If someone believes they've become a stranger..."

He rested a hand on the cover.

> "...You don't argue with them."

> "...You remind them they're expected home."

---

### **Star**

Star recognized the dictionary immediately.

Her smile disappeared.

> "...I gave her that..."

She gently touched the cover.

> "...I wanted her to learn new words."

She swallowed.

> "...Not live inside one."

---

### **Nightshade**

Nightshade spoke only once.

> "...She spent years fighting her past."

She looked toward the door.

> "...No one should have to fight it alone."

---

### **Neko**

Neko reviewed every incident report in chronological order.

When he finished, he simply stated:

> "...We analyzed every symptom."

He placed the reports back into their folder.

> "...We should have asked how she was."

---

### **Miss Neko**

Miss Neko quietly folded one of Belle's old blankets.

She smiled sadly.

> "...This room has been waiting for her."

She looked toward the empty seat Belle usually claimed.

> "...It can wait a little longer."

---

## **EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVE**

Following review of the accumulated reports, a joint recommendation was entered into the file.

> **"No assumptions."**

> **"No waiting for the 'right time.'"**

> **"No expecting Belle to make the first move."**

> **"Go to her."**

> **"Sit with her."**

> **"Listen more than you speak."**

> **"Remind her, through actions, that she still has a place to come back to."**

---

### **STATUS**

For the first time since the changes in Belle's behavior began, those closest to her reached the same conclusion.

They had missed opportunities.

They could not change that.

What remained was the opportunity still in front of them.

The next knock on Belle's office door would not be about work.

It would simply be because someone had come to see her.
