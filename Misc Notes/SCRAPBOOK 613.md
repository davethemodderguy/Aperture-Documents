### **ANOMALOUS EQUIPMENT / CORIN FEATHER CAPABILITY UPDATE**

# **CORIN — MULTIFUNCTIONAL FEATHER SYSTEM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Designation:** **MULTIFUNCTIONAL ANOMALOUS BIOLOGICAL TOOL**  
**Status:** **Operational**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has officially updated the classification of Corin's feathers.

They are no longer considered merely biological structures or anomalous projectiles.

Following repeated demonstrations of their increasingly diverse capabilities, Corin's feathers are now recognized as a **multifunctional anomalous system**.

Current confirmed functions include:

> **Light.**

> **Explosive.**

> **Projectile.**

> **Lockpick.**

The same feather can potentially fulfill entirely different roles depending on its activation state and how Corin manipulates it.

---

# **01 — LIGHT**

Dormant feathers can be activated into a persistent **light-azure flame**.

The flame:

- Produces no heat.
    
- Requires no oxygen.
    
- Produces a faint vanilla scent.
    
- Does not consume the feather.
    
- Cannot currently be extinguished.
    

Corin commonly places activated feathers inside glass containers as emergency lighting.

These are informally known as:

> **"Feather lanterns."**

---

# **02 — EXPLOSIVE**

Certain feathers can produce an anomalous Ether-based energy discharge.

The exact mechanism remains poorly understood.

Unlike the persistent flame state, this function is associated with an energetic release rather than continuous illumination.

C.O.R.E. therefore classifies appropriately activated feathers as **potentially explosive anomalous objects**.

Personnel are reminded:

> **Do not experiment with Corin's feathers without authorization.**

---

# **03 — PROJECTILE**

Corin can launch feathers at extremely high velocities.

Their durability allows them to survive impacts that would destroy conventional projectiles.

The Nano Nekonium wing armor introduces an additional capability.

Incoming kinetic energy can be stored and distributed through the armor before being transferred into compatible feathers.

This creates the previously documented:

> **Kinetic Feather System.**

This system has demonstrated sufficient performance to:

- Penetrate multiple walls.
    
- Intercept airborne threats.
    
- Intercept missiles.
    
- Redirect incoming kinetic force.
    

One particular kinetic feather is currently displayed in the Blacksite museum.

---

# **04 — LOCKPICK**

The newest confirmed capability is perhaps the most mundane.

Corin's feathers can function as **lockpicks**.

Their unusual flexibility, structural durability, and precise manipulation allow Corin to use individual feathers to interact with mechanical locking mechanisms.

The feathers can be positioned and manipulated with remarkable precision.

This makes them useful for situations where conventional tools are unavailable.

---

# **THE DISCOVERY**

The capability was discovered when Corin became separated from her normal equipment.

She needed to open a locked container.

She looked at her feathers.

Then tried something.

The lock opened.

Corin stared at the lock.

> "...Oh."

Neko subsequently examined the feather.

Then the lock.

Then Corin.

> **"You can pick locks with your feathers?"**

Corin:

> **"Apparently."**

# **THE PROBLEM**

C.O.R.E. is becoming increasingly concerned that there may be **more functions that have simply not been discovered yet**.

Corin herself does not appear to know the full capabilities of her feathers.

Researchers have therefore begun treating every newly discovered feather function as:

> **"Another thing Corin can apparently do."**

---

# **NEKO'S ASSESSMENT**

Neko has proposed that the feathers be classified as a modular anomalous system.

His reasoning:

> **"They're basically tools that happen to grow out of her wings."**

Curious Light agreed.

The official documentation subsequently adopted the term:

> **MULTIFUNCTIONAL FEATHER SYSTEM**

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

Corin read the updated report.

She reached the capability table.

Her ears perked at **Lockpicking**.

She looked at one of her feathers.

Then at the document.

> "...So they're lights."

She counted on her fingers.

> "And explosives."

Another finger.

> "And projectiles."

Another.

> "And lockpicks."

She paused.

> "...What else?"

Neko immediately answered:

> **"That's what we're trying to figure out."**

Corin stared at him.

> "...Please don't."

---

# **FINAL ASSESSMENT**

Corin's feathers are now officially classified as a **multifunctional anomalous biological system**.

Their known functions span several completely unrelated applications:

**Illumination.**

**Energy discharge.**

**Projectile deployment.**

**Aerial interception.**

**Kinetic energy storage and transfer.**

**Lock manipulation.**

And, apparently, eating them is possible under certain conditions.

C.O.R.E. strongly recommends that nobody attempts to discover additional functions by randomly experimenting with them.

The current working philosophy is:

> **"If Corin's feather does something unexpected, document it."**

Not:

> **"Let's find out what happens."**
