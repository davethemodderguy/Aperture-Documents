# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **PERSONNEL HABIT OBSERVATION**

### **SUBJECT: ARCTURUS LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Internal

---

# **OFF-DUTY ROUTINE**

When not serving as the KCA's Security Advisor, Arcturus prefers quiet, solitary activities.

His usual routine includes:

- Hiking through designated safe zones.
    
- Hunting for food in approved wilderness areas.
    
- Maintaining his firearms.
    
- Target practice using glass bottles and steel targets at the Blacksite range.
    
- Sitting outdoors with a notebook and a cold drink.
    

Personnel have commented that he appears perfectly content spending an afternoon with nothing more than his lever-action rifle and the sound of the wind.

---

# **MARKSMANSHIP PRACTICE**

Arcturus favors simple target drills over elaborate training courses.

Common exercises include:

- Engaging bottles at increasing distances.
    
- Practicing rapid target transitions.
    
- Reloading from his concealed bandolier.
    
- Shooting from improvised positions.
    

He often reminds younger operators:

> "Slow practice builds fast hands."

His range sessions are notably tidy—every bottle and spent casing is cleaned up before he leaves.

---

# **THE SODA INCIDENT**

While Arcturus has no issues with discipline in the field...

His coworkers have identified one personal weakness.

**Soda.**

He drinks an extraordinary amount of it.

The SAFEHOUSE refrigerator has, on more than one occasion, been found suspiciously low after one of his visits.

When asked about it, Arcturus simply replied:

> "...It tastes good."

Medical later recommended that he balance his diet with more water.

He agreed.

Then opened another soda.

# **MEDICAL NOTE**

Medical Services has confirmed that Arcturus is in excellent physical condition.

However, staff have gently encouraged him to reduce his soda consumption and substitute water or tea more frequently.

Norma has reportedly begun placing bottles of water beside the refrigerator with handwritten notes reading:

> **"Drink one of these first."**

Whether this strategy is effective remains under evaluation.

---

# **STATUS**

Arcturus continues to enjoy quiet hunting trips, precision target practice, and peaceful afternoons outdoors.

He also continues to consume enough soda that SAFEHOUSE residents instinctively check the refrigerator whenever he visits.

As Belle jokingly summarized:

> "The only thing faster than Arcturus' draw..."

> "...is how quickly the soda disappears."
