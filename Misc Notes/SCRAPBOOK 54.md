# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **INCIDENT & CIVILIAN RESCUE LOG**

### **OPERATION: WAYFARER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Archive

---

# **SUMMARY**

At approximately **14:23**, a mounted reconnaissance patrol led by **Security Advisor Arcturus Lockwood** discovered five unconscious civilians several kilometers from the Blacksite perimeter.

The civilians were identified as **wandering traders** traveling between settlements.

Environmental conditions had resulted in **severe hyperthermia**, leaving all five in critical condition.

Immediate medical evacuation was authorized.

---

# **THE HORSE**

Rather than utilizing a vehicle, Arcturus was conducting his patrol on horseback.

The horse, an otherwise unassigned mare belonging to a nearby stable, had approached him earlier that morning and calmly allowed itself to be saddled.

One stable hand simply shrugged.

> "...Told you."

> "...They all like him."

Arcturus gently patted the horse's neck.

> "...Good girl."

The mare remained perfectly calm throughout the rescue operation.

---

# **THE RESCUE**

Upon locating the traders, Arcturus immediately radioed the Blacksite.

> "Five civilians."

> "Critical heat exposure."

> "Need Medical and transport immediately."

Within minutes:

- Belle arrived with emergency medical personnel.
    
- Endmin coordinated the evacuation.
    
- Norma prepared the medical wing.
    
- Kent and Cinder secured the surrounding area.
    
- Neko somehow appeared carrying six portable cooling units.
    

No one asked where he got the sixth one.

---

# **MEDICAL RESPONSE**

All five civilians were successfully stabilized.

Treatment included:

- Controlled cooling.
    
- Intravenous fluids.
    
- Electrolyte replacement.
    
- Continuous monitoring.
    

All five ultimately recovered without lasting injury.

Medical later concluded that another hour in the field would likely have been fatal.

---

# **FIRST CIVILIAN VISITORS**

Because of the urgency of the rescue, the traders became the **first known civilians with no government affiliation** ever permitted inside Heaven's Blacksite.

Special authorization was granted by Director Lucious.

Security protocols remained in effect, but the priority was preserving life.

---

# **THEIR REACTION**

Once awake, the traders slowly looked around.

Clean hallways.

Advanced medical equipment.

Researchers walking past.

Thirens chatting in the common room.

A rabbit carrying a cup of tea.

A cowboy polishing a lever-action rifle.

One trader quietly whispered:

> "...Are we dead?"

Belle laughed.

> "...No."

> "You're just at the Blacksite."

The room fell silent.

---

One of the traders looked through a nearby window.

He saw technology decades beyond anything he had ever encountered.

Another quietly watched Miss Neko walk past carrying the _Bliss of Whitefall_.

A third noticed Neko enthusiastically explaining something involving antimatter to a very patient Norma.

The eldest trader simply removed his hat.

> "...I'm going to pretend I didn't see any of this."

---

# **CONFIDENTIALITY**

Before departing, Director Lucious met personally with the traders.

Rather than threatening or coercing them, he spoke plainly.

> "You were brought here because your lives mattered."

> "What you've seen today is entrusted to you."

The traders exchanged glances.

Their leader nodded.

> "You saved us."

> "...Our lips are sealed."

No formal secrecy agreement was required.

The gratitude they felt was reason enough.

# **FINAL ENTRY**

The five traders departed Heaven's Blacksite healthy, well-fed, and carrying enough supplies to safely continue their journey.

When asked one final time what they intended to tell others about the experience, the eldest trader smiled.

> "Nobody would believe us anyway."

He tipped his hat toward Arcturus.

> "Besides..."

> "...Some places deserve to stay hidden."

As they disappeared over the horizon, Arcturus mounted his horse once more.

The mare gave a soft snort before setting off at an easy pace.

Watching them leave, Belle smiled.

> "...He really is a cowboy."
