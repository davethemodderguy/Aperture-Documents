### **PERSONNEL ANOMALOUS IDENTITY / COMMUNICATION LOG**

# **COREY — INTERNAL SELF-REFERENCE BETWEEN FORMS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Internal Personnel  
**Subjects:** Corey / Solaria / Luna  
**Status:** Stable / Cooperative

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has confirmed that **Corey, Solaria, and Luna are capable of referring to one another during conversation**.

Despite sharing memories and fundamentally representing the same individual, each manifestation retains enough individuality to speak about the others as distinct aspects of Corey's identity.

This behavior is particularly common when discussing:

- Their different personalities.
    
- Their abilities.
    
- Their current manifestation.
    
- Events that occurred while another form was active.
    
- Personal habits.
    

---

# **"THE SCARY MOON LADY"**

Luna has acquired an informal nickname among some SAFEHOUSE personnel:

> **"The Scary Moon Lady."**

The name originated from her combination of:

- Azure lunar appearance.
    
- Quiet demeanor.
    
- Unsettling smile.
    
- Obsessive tendencies.
    
- Ability to impose an overwhelming sense of incoming danger.
    

Luna is aware of the nickname.

She does not appear offended.

In fact, she seems mildly amused by it.

---

# **LUNA'S RESPONSE**

When asked whether she knew people called her the Scary Moon Lady:

Luna replied:

> "...Yes."

The interviewer asked how she felt about it.

Luna smiled.

> "...I like it."

The interviewer immediately experienced a brief sense of danger.

Luna stopped smiling.

> "...Sorry."

---

# **SOLARIA REFERRING TO LUNA**

Solaria occasionally refers to Luna as though discussing another person.

Example:

> **Solaria:** "...Luna would probably like this."

> **Corey:** "Probably."

> **Solaria:** "...She gets weird about moonlight."

Corey:

> "...You're talking about yourself."

Solaria:

> "...I know."

---

# **LUNA REFERRING TO SOLARIA**

Luna does the same.

> **Luna:** "...Solaria would do that faster."

Corey:

> "...Probably."

> **Luna:** "...She's more confident."

Corey:

> "...You are too."

Luna:

> "...Not the same way."

---

# **REFERRING TO COREY**

Both manifestations can also refer to **Corey** directly.

This is particularly common when discussing their shared identity.

Luna may say:

> "...Corey likes this."

Solaria may respond:

> "...Corey doesn't like that."

When asked why they speak about Corey as though she were someone else, both have given essentially the same explanation:

> **"Because we're talking about that part of us."**

---

# **CURRENT STATE IDENTIFICATION**

The three identities can also explicitly identify Corey's current state.

Examples include:

> "...I'm Luna right now."

> "...Corey's back."

> "...Solaria is present."

This is particularly useful when personnel are uncertain which manifestation they are interacting with.

---

# **CONVERSATIONS BETWEEN FORMS**

When switching directly between manifestations, the transition can produce unusual conversations.

Example:

**Luna:**

> "...You can take over now."

**Corey:**

> "...Okay."

**Solaria:**

> "...Finally."

Corey:

> "...You were waiting?"

Solaria:

> "...Maybe."

---

# **SHARED MEMORY**

There is no evidence that the manifestations lose memories when switching.

Corey remembers what Luna and Solaria experienced.

Luna remembers Corey's experiences.

Solaria remembers Luna's experiences.

Consequently, referring to one another does not represent separate consciousnesses in the conventional sense.

It is better described as:

> **One person recognizing different expressions of herself.**

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

Marts have little difficulty understanding the distinction.

Their vocabulary remains simple.

When asked who is present, a Mart may simply state:

> "...Corey."

Or:

> "...Luna."

Or:

> "...Solaria."

If all three are being discussed:

> "...All Corey."

---

# **PERSONNEL OBSERVATION**

The ability to refer to one another appears to be psychologically healthy.

Rather than denying their differences, Corey appears increasingly comfortable acknowledging them.

Solaria represents Corey's more confident and solar side.

Luna represents her quieter, wiser, and more unusual lunar side.

Corey remains the central identity connecting both.

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

Personnel should not be alarmed if Luna says:

> "...Corey wouldn't like that."

Or if Solaria says:

> "...Luna would probably do something weird."

Or if Corey says:

> "...Solaria is being dramatic."

They are not discussing imaginary people.

They are discussing themselves.

And if someone calls Luna:

> **"The Scary Moon Lady,"**

Luna will probably just smile.

Which, unfortunately, is exactly why the nickname stuck.
