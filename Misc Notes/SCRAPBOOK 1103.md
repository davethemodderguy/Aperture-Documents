# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Night Stalker Hidden Authorization Trial has entered **Day 3.**

The plastic Night Stalker model remains undiscovered.

> **“Think outside the box.”**

No additional clarification was provided.

MSAC personnel have interpreted the hint as intentionally ambiguous.

---

# 2. DAY 3 — THE HINT

At approximately 08:00, Neri published the following message to the MSAC authorization channel:

> **Think outside the box.**

That was all.

No location.

No photograph.

No additional instructions.

No indication of whether the phrase should be interpreted literally, metaphorically, spatially, or otherwise.

Several MSAC Operators immediately began searching boxes.

C.O.R.E. considers this response predictable.

Whether it is correct remains unknown.

# 3. THE PERIMETER SECURITY ENCOUNTER

One Perimeter Security Guard assigned to assist the MSAC search encountered several MSAC Operators examining a secured equipment area.

The guard noticed a number of modified M1911-pattern pistols displayed nearby.

He looked at them.

Then looked at the operators.

### PERIMETER SECURITY GUARD:

> “Isn't that just some modded M1911s?”

The MSAC Operator paused.

### MSAC OPERATOR:

> “It's THE Night Stalker.”

The guard looked at the pistols again.

### PERIMETER SECURITY GUARD:

> “Functionality isn't the only thing?”

### MSAC OPERATOR:

> “It's THE Night Stalker, functionality isn't the only thing.”

The guard remained confused.

### PERIMETER SECURITY GUARD:

> “Then what gives?”

The operator sighed.

### MSAC OPERATOR:

> “There's only 10 of them. 5 is taken.”

The guard nodded slowly.

### PERIMETER SECURITY GUARD:

> “Oh.”

Pause.

### PERIMETER SECURITY GUARD:

> “Good luck.”

### MSAC OPERATOR:

> “Yeah.”

The guard continued his patrol.

The MSAC Operator returned to the search.

# 4. THE TEN

The exchange highlighted an important distinction.

The Night Stalker is not merely another modified M1911.

There are exactly:

> **10 Night Stalkers.**

Five are currently accounted for.

The remaining five are either reserved, pending authorization, or otherwise unavailable.

The hidden object is therefore significant because it represents one of the limited Night Stalker authorizations available to the MSAC program.

The plastic model is not itself a functional firearm.

It is effectively a **physical authorization marker.**

Find it first.

Earn the corresponding authorization.

# 7. SEARCH EXPANSION

MSAC personnel have begun examining locations that were previously considered unlikely.

This includes:

- exterior-facing areas;
    
- structural spaces;
    
- elevated locations;
    
- open areas;
    
- equipment surrounding rather than containing objects;
    
- locations adjacent to commonly searched storage;
    
- areas where the model could be visible without technically being “stored.”
    

Several operators have reportedly stopped searching **inside** things entirely.

Others have become suspicious of the phrase itself.

One operator reportedly stated:

> “What if ‘box’ doesn't mean box?”

Another answered:

> “Then we're screwed.”

Then she walked away.

No answer was recorded.
