# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **RESTRICTED SAFEHOUSE / EXTREME WEATHER INCIDENT**

# **DAY TWO — THE BLUE NOTES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Architect-Sealed  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Subjects:** Corin / Orphie / Guiding Light  
**Incident:** Severe Snowstorm  
**Status:** **COMMUNICATIONS REMAIN LOST**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The snowstorm did not weaken overnight.

If anything, conditions became worse.

The route between the Blacksite and the treehouse remains completely inaccessible.

Communications remain unavailable.

Orphie has not returned.

Corin remains alone.

The situation has produced an unusual anomaly of its own:

**Guiding Light cannot properly manifest.**

She can perceive the treehouse.

She can perceive Corin.

She can perceive the surrounding reality.

But the storm is interfering with her ability to physically reach the area.

Even more unusually, her normal communication methods have been reduced to a single option.

**Notes.**

---

# **THE ARCHITECT**

Guiding Light attempted to manifest beside Corin shortly after dawn.

The blue glow appeared.

Then flickered.

Her form failed to fully materialize.

Her voice could not cross the storm.

No physical manifestation occurred.

Instead, a small piece of paper appeared on the table.

Written in blue ink:

> **"Corin."**

Corin stared at it.

She immediately recognized the handwriting.

> **"Guiding?"**

Another note appeared.

> **"Yes."**

---

# **THE EXPLANATION**

Corin looked toward the window.

> **"Can you come here?"**

A pause.

Another blue note appeared.

> **"I'm trying."**

Then:

> **"Something about this storm is interfering with me."**

Corin looked confused.

> **"You're an Architect."**

The next note appeared almost immediately.

> **"I am aware."**

Another:

> **"I don't understand it either."**

---

# **AN IMPOSSIBLE PROBLEM**

Guiding Light has encountered environmental conditions capable of interfering with her manifestation before.

Nothing comparable to this.

The storm is not merely blocking roads.

It is disrupting:

- Dimensional traversal.
    
- Remote manifestation.
    
- Long-range perception.
    
- Conventional communication.
    
- Spatial anchoring.
    
- Ether-based signaling.
    

The phenomenon does not appear to be ordinary weather.

Yet no anomalous source has been identified.

Curious Light has been unable to investigate directly due to the emergency conditions at the Blacksite.

---

# **CORIN'S MORNING**

Corin had barely slept.

She remained near the window.

Snow covered everything.

The forest was almost completely invisible.

She asked:

> **"Is Orphie okay?"**

Guiding Light's response appeared on the table.

> **"I don't know."**

Corin stared at the words.

Another note appeared.

> **"I don't want to lie to you."**

Then:

> **"But I have no reason to believe she isn't."**

Corin nodded slowly.

> **"Okay."**

---

# **THE BLUE NOTES**

Throughout the morning, Guiding Light continued communicating through handwritten notes.

They appeared wherever Corin needed them.

Beside the notebook.

On the kitchen table.

Near the window.

Once, directly on Corin's blanket.

The blue ink glowed softly.

One note read:

> **"Eat something."**

Another:

> **"Drink water."**

Another:

> **"Your stitches are still holding."**

And another:

> **"You are not alone."**

---

# **CORIN'S NOTEBOOK**

Corin continued writing.

> **Day 2.**

> **Storm still going.**

> **Orphie still isn't here.**

> **Guiding is here, kind of.**

She paused.

Then:

> **She can't come inside.**

Another line:

> **I think she's scared too.**

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S RESPONSE**

A blue note appeared beside the notebook.

> **"I am."**

Corin looked surprised.

> **"You can be scared?"**

Another note:

> **"Of course."**

Then:

> **"Being an Architect doesn't make me immune to uncertainty."**

A final line appeared:

> **"I wish it did."**

---

# **THE STORM**

By afternoon, the storm had become even more severe.

The snow struck the windows hard enough to shake the treehouse.

The surrounding forest disappeared entirely.

The world outside looked like an endless white void.

Guiding Light attempted to manifest again.

Her blue glow appeared beside Corin.

For half a second, Corin could see the outline of her shattered wings.

Then the manifestation collapsed.

A note appeared on the floor.

> **"Sorry."**

Corin picked it up.

> **"Don't apologize."**

She placed it beside the notebook.

---

# **THE ARCHITECT'S LIMIT**

Guiding Light has never liked admitting that there are things she cannot simply solve.

But the storm has forced her to confront something uncomfortable.

She can perceive almost everything.

She can guide people across impossible distances.

She can manipulate reality.

Yet right now, she cannot walk through a forest to comfort a frightened girl.

She can only leave pieces of herself behind.

Ink on paper.

Blue light.

Words.

---

# **THE NOTE CORIN KEPT**

Late in the afternoon, one final note appeared.

The handwriting was smaller than usual.

> **"Orphie is still trying to come home."**

Underneath:

> **"I am still trying to reach you."**

And finally:

> **"Until they both can, I will stay with you however I can."**

Corin held the note against her chest.

For the first time that day, she smiled.

Only slightly.

But genuinely.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Day Two has confirmed that the storm is not ordinary.

It has interfered with systems that should be completely beyond the reach of weather.

Even Guiding Light, one of the Architects responsible for the structure of reality itself, has been partially obstructed.

She cannot properly manifest.

She cannot physically reach Corin.

She cannot reliably contact the Blacksite.

She can only communicate through blue handwritten notes.

And somehow, those notes are enough to give Corin something she desperately needs:

**proof that someone is still there.**

Outside, the storm continues.

Orphie remains separated from the treehouse.

Guiding Light remains trapped beyond the storm's interference.

Corin remains alone.

But she is no longer completely without company.

A blue note rests beside her notebook.

It reads:

> **"You are still here."**

**End of Restricted Safehouse / Extreme Weather Incident — Day Two**
