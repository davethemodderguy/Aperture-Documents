# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

There are few things in Heaven's Blacksite that can make Neko question his understanding of reality.

Neri is one of them.

More specifically:

**Plush Neri.**

A tiny, fabric-skinned, chibi Seal Thiren wearing an oversized hoodie and round glasses.

Approximately the size of a stuffed toy.

Frequently carried around by a six-year-old.

Occasionally bites people.

Likes headpats.

Can sleep inside a blanket pile.

And is simultaneously:

- an exceptionally advanced chemist;
    
- the holder of the Blacksite's current record for research storage consumption;
    
- the first Blacksite resident aside from Curious Light to demonstrably surpass Neko in a technical field;
    
- and the researcher responsible for filling a **12 TB SSD in approximately one month.**
    

The Architects were aware of Neri.

They had not, however, previously encountered her in plush form.

This meeting produced considerable confusion.

---

# 2. THE THREE ARCHITECTS

The meeting involved:

### GUIDING LIGHT

Oldest known Architect of Reality.

Calm.

Compassionate.

Exceptional baker.

Has a particular interest in Neri's wellbeing.

### CURIOUS LIGHT

Second-oldest Architect.

The most intellectually curious of the three.

Already established as one of the few entities capable of surpassing Neko in technical reasoning.

### MISCHEVIOUS LIGHT

Youngest Architect.

Enforcer.

Highly capable.

Consistently willing to make situations worse for entertainment.

---

# 3. INITIAL ENCOUNTER

The Architects were present in a SAFEHOUSE common area.

Neri entered in plush form.

She was approximately the size of a small stuffed animal.

She walked across the floor.

Guiding Light noticed her first.

Guiding looked down.

Neri looked up.

Several seconds passed.

**GUIDING LIGHT:**  
"...Neri?"

Neri nodded.

Guiding crouched.

**GUIDING LIGHT:**  
"You're tiny."

Neri stared.

She did not appear offended.

Mischevious Light immediately approached.

**MISCHEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
"That's not Neri."

Neri looked at her.

**MISCHEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
"That's a plushie."

Neri nodded again.

**MISCHEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
"...You admit it?"

Neri nodded.

---

# 4. CURIOUS LIGHT'S OBSERVATION

Curious Light crouched beside Neri.

She examined the plush construction.

Fabric skin.

Oversized hoodie.

Long white hair.

Seal tail.

Round glasses.

Tiny proportions.

Curious eventually asked:

**CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
"How long can you maintain this form?"

Neri held up one finger.

Curious interpreted the gesture.

> **Indefinitely.**

A pause.

**CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
"Convenient."

Neri nodded.

Mischevious picked Neri up.

Neri did not object.

Mischevious turned her over.

Neri's seal tail dangled downward.

**MISCHEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
"She's actually a plushie."

Guiding:

> "She's still Neri."

Mischevious:

> "I know."

Pause.

> "She's a very small Neri."

---

# 5. THE HEADPAT

Guiding Light gently placed a hand on Neri's head.

Neri immediately relaxed.

Her eyes closed.

Guiding gave her another gentle pat.

Neri leaned slightly into the hand.

Mischevious stared.

**MISCHEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
"...She likes that."

Curious:

> "Apparently."

Guiding smiled.

> "She always has."

Neri remained underneath Guiding's hand.

---

# 6. THE CHEMISTRY REVELATION

The conversation eventually shifted toward Neri's research.

Curious Light asked about her current work.

Neri responded by pointing toward the direction of her room.

Curious understood.

**CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
"You're still researching?"

Neri nodded.

**CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
"How much?"

Neri held up one finger.

Then made a rectangular shape with her hands.

Then pointed toward Neko's general work area.

Curious interpreted this as:

> **One 12 TB drive.**

Curious paused.

**CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
"You filled it?"

Neri nodded.

**CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
"How long?"

Neri held up one finger.

Then one month.

Curious stared.

Mischevious stared.

Guiding stared.

---

# 7. THE Neko PROBLEM

Neko was eventually informed that the Architects were discussing his latest technological competition.

He entered the room.

Curious Light looked at him.

**CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
"Neri has filled another twelve-terabyte drive."

Neko stopped.

> "I know."

Mischevious:

> "She beat you."

Neko:

> "In chemistry."

Curious:

> "Yes."

Neko:

> "That's not my field."

Curious:

> "You said that the first time."

Neko:

> "Because it's true."

Mischevious:

> "She still beat you."

Neko stared at her.

Neri quietly sat on the floor.

---

# 8. NERI'S PLACE IN THE BLACKSITE'S INTELLECTUAL HIERARCHY

C.O.R.E. formally recognizes the following distinction:

Neko remains one of the Blacksite's greatest minds in:

- engineering;
    
- computing;
    
- systems architecture;
    
- anomalous technology;
    
- infrastructure;
    
- security technology;
    
- archives.
    

Neri is not generally superior to Neko in these fields.

Chemistry is different.

In chemistry, Neri's capability is exceptional enough that:

> **Neri is currently the first known Blacksite resident, aside from Curious Light, to consistently surpass Neko's technical capability.**

This remains one of the more absurd facts in Blacksite records.

It is made substantially more absurd by the fact that the person responsible is sometimes found sitting inside a cardboard box.

---

# 9. MISCHEVIOUS LIGHT'S CONCLUSION

Mischevious eventually picked Neri up again.

Neri allowed it.

Mischevious held her at eye level.

**MISCHEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
"So you're telling me..."

She glanced toward Neko.

Then back at Neri.

> "This tiny thing beat him?"

Neri nodded.

Mischevious smiled.

She looked at Neko.

> "I like her."

Neko:

> "Please don't encourage her."

Mischevious:

> "I'm not."

Pause.

> "I'm encouraging you."

---

# 10. GUIDING LIGHT'S RESPONSE

Guiding Light remained considerably more interested in Neri herself than in the academic comparison.

She gently adjusted Neri's glasses.

Neri allowed it.

**GUIDING LIGHT:**  
"You've been working hard."

Neri nodded.

Guiding:

> "You don't have to work right now."

Neri looked at her.

Guiding pointed at the couch.

> "You can just be small."

Neri considered this.

Then climbed onto the couch.

She curled up.

Guiding placed a blanket over her.

Neri closed her eyes.

---

# 11. CURIOUS LIGHT'S RESPONSE

Curious Light remained fascinated.

She asked whether Neri's research had reached the limits of her current understanding.

Neri nodded.

Curious smiled.

**CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
"Good."

Neri looked up.

**CURIOUS LIGHT:**  
"Then you still have things to discover."

Neri's expression softened slightly.

She nodded.

Curious then asked:

> "Want to show me?"

Neri immediately became more alert.

She nodded.

Neko, from across the room:

> "Oh no."

---

# 12. TWO HOURS LATER

Curious Light and Neri were still discussing chemistry.

Neri remained in plush form.

She had been sitting on the table for most of the discussion.

At one point, she became tired.

She simply laid down.

Curious continued explaining a theoretical concept.

Neri listened while lying flat on her stomach.

Eventually:

**NERI:**  
"...Wait."

Curious stopped.

Neri sat up.

She reached for a nearby notebook.

She began writing.

Neko looked over.

> "She's doing it again."

Mischevious:

> "What?"

Neko:

> "She found another question."

Guiding smiled.

---

# 13. FINAL OBSERVATION

The meeting produced an unusual conclusion.

Neri does not become less intelligent when she becomes a plushie.

She simply becomes considerably less interested in demonstrating it.

She can spend an afternoon:

- being carried;
    
- receiving headpats;
    
- biting someone's sleeve;
    
- sleeping in a blanket;
    
- sitting inside a box;
    
- being treated like a six-year-old's favorite stuffed animal.
    

Then, without warning, she can look at Curious Light and begin discussing chemistry at a level that makes Neko deeply uncomfortable.

This is not contradictory.

It is simply Neri.
