# TRIAL II

Following the successful conclusion of the first Sleight of Hand interrogation trial, Neri has announced the second competition for the remaining units.

The objective is considerably simpler.

The execution is not.

Contestants must locate:

> **ONE NEKONIUM NEEDLE**

hidden somewhere inside:

> **25,000,000,000 PIECES OF HAY**

stacked throughout **Cargo Bay 4.**

---

# 1. THE OBJECTIVE

Neri's announcement consisted of a single sheet of paper placed on the Cargo Bay 4 entrance.

It read:

> **“Find the Nekonium Needle.”**
> 
> **“It's in the hay.”**
> 
> **“There are a lot of hay.”**
> 
> **“No equipment.”**
> 
> **“No magnets.”**
> 
> **“Good luck.”**
> 
> — **Neri**

---

# 2. THE HAYSTACK

Cargo Bay 4 has been temporarily converted into an extremely large search area.

Estimated hay quantity:

> **~25 BILLION INDIVIDUAL PIECES**

The hay has been distributed throughout the designated competition area.

The resulting structure has been described by participating personnel as:

> **“A haystack the size of a logistics problem.”**

The competition area is large enough that contestants cannot reasonably inspect the entire mass visually.

The needle is somewhere inside.

---

# 3. THE NEKONIUM NEEDLE

The target is a small Nekonium object fabricated by Neri specifically for the competition.

It is not marked with an obvious visual identifier.

It is also deliberately small.

Neri selected the hiding location herself.

Only Neri knows where it was placed.

C.O.R.E. does not currently possess the exact coordinates.

---

# 4. IMPORTANT: NEKONIUM IS NOT MAGNETIC

Several contestants immediately proposed magnetic detection.

This was rejected.

Nekonium has:

> **NO MAGNETIC PROPERTIES.**

Consequently:

- magnets cannot locate the needle;
    
- magnetic sweepers are prohibited;
    
- improvised magnetic detection is prohibited;
    
- electromagnetic searching provides no legitimate advantage;
    
- and anyone arriving with a magnet has misunderstood the material.
    

One contestant asked Neri:

> **CONTESTANT:**  
> “Can we use a magnet?”

Neri stared at them.

> **NERI:**  
> “No.”

> **CONTESTANT:**  
> “Because it's too easy?”

Neri shook her head.

> **NERI:**  
> “Because Nekonium isn't magnetic.”

The contestant looked at the haystack.

Then at Neri.

> **CONTESTANT:**  
> “Oh.”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

---

# 5. EQUIPMENT PROHIBITION

Contestants may not use equipment to locate the needle.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- metal detectors;
    
- scanners;
    
- drones;
    
- cameras used for automated searching;
    
- thermal imaging;
    
- X-ray equipment;
    
- magnets;
    
- robotic search systems;
    
- anomalous detection equipment;
    
- chemical detection systems;
    
- laboratory instruments;
    
- computing-assisted search systems;
    
- or any other device intended to identify the needle without physically searching the hay.
    

No external assistance.

No automated searching.

No technological shortcuts.

---

# 6. WHAT CONTESTANTS ACTUALLY GET

Each contestant receives:

- themselves;
    
- ordinary clothing;
    
- permission to enter Cargo Bay 4;
    
- permission to move the hay;
    
- permission to search;
    
- and absolutely no useful equipment.
    

They may use their hands.

That's it.

---

When the doors opened, the first group of contestants stared into Cargo Bay 4.

Nobody spoke.

One operator slowly looked upward.

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “How much hay is that?”

Another checked the competition notice.

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “Twenty-five billion pieces.”

Pause.

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “We're looking for one needle.”

> **SECOND OPERATOR:**  
> “Yep.”

Another operator stared into the enormous haystack.

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “I'm going to be here until retirement.”

# 8. NERI'S POSITION

Neri will not provide hints.

She will not confirm whether contestants are getting closer.

She will not answer questions about the hiding location.

She will not move the needle.

She will not participate in the search.

She will, however, observe the competition.

When asked whether she thought someone would actually find it, she answered:

> **NERI:**  
> “Someone will.”

> **MSAC OFFICER:**  
> “How long will that take?”

Neri looked at the haystack.

> **NERI:**  
> “I don't know.”
