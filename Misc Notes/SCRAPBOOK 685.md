### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / ENTITY PSYCHOLOGY LOG**

# **BUNNY — HAPPINESS FOR EVERYONE ELSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Bunny  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE / Treehouse  
**Status:** **EMOTIONAL SUPPRESSION CONFIRMED**

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Bunny has developed a consistent behavior.

When she is alone, she is often quiet.

She sits by the window.

She watches the snow.

Sometimes she hugs her knees.

Sometimes she simply stares at nothing.

She does not always cry.

She does not always appear distressed.

She simply looks...

**sad.**

But whenever someone enters the room and notices her—

Bunny smiles.

Immediately.

---

# **THE SMILE**

The transition is almost instantaneous.

Her ears rise.

Her expression brightens.

Her mouth forms a smile.

Her voice becomes cheerful.

> **"Bunny happy!"**

Even when she was visibly sad only seconds earlier.

Personnel initially assumed this meant Bunny was recovering.

It does not.

---

# **THE PATTERN**

C.O.R.E. began documenting the behavior.

### **When alone:**

- Quiet.
    
- Withdrawn.
    
- Frequently sad.
    
- Occasionally tearful.
    
- Little spontaneous activity.
    

### **When someone approaches:**

- Immediate smile.
    
- Increased vocal activity.
    
- Claims to be happy.
    
- Attempts to reassure the other person.
    
- Conceals distress.
    

The behavior occurs regardless of who approaches.

Corin.

Orphie.

Lumi.

Belle.

Neko.

Even unfamiliar personnel.

---

# **THE EXPLANATION**

Corin eventually asked Bunny why she did it.

> **"Why do you smile when you're sad?"**

Bunny looked at her.

The smile appeared.

> **"Bunny happy when others are happy."**

Corin frowned.

> **"But you're sad."**

The smile weakened.

Bunny looked down.

> **"Bunny scared."**

Corin reached toward her.

> **"Scared of what?"**

Bunny struggled to explain.

Her words came out slowly.

> **"Bunny scared..."**

She paused.

> **"Bunny happy."**

Corin:

> **"You don't have to be happy."**

Bunny shook her head.

> **"No Bunny scared."**

She repeated herself.

> **"Bunny happy no Bunny scared."**

---

# **INTERPRETATION**

The statement appears to represent Bunny's internal logic:

**If Bunny is happy, Bunny cannot be scared.**

**If everyone else is happy, Bunny believes she must be happy too.**

The smile is therefore not merely deception.

It is a coping mechanism.

Bunny appears to believe that displaying sadness is dangerous.

That her fear must be hidden.

That other people's happiness depends, at least partially, on her appearing happy.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin understood immediately.

She had once done something similar.

She sat beside Bunny.

> **"You don't have to make me happy."**

Bunny looked confused.

> **"But Big Sis happy."**

> **"I can be happy myself."**

Bunny's ears lowered.

> **"Bunny can sad?"**

Corin nodded.

> **"Yes."**

> **"Bunny can scared?"**

> **"Yes."**

Bunny stared at her.

The smile slowly disappeared.

She whispered:

> **"...Okay."**

---

# **BELLE'S RESPONSE**

Belle approached Bunny later.

Bunny immediately smiled.

Belle stopped.

> **"You're doing it."**

Bunny froze.

> **"Doing what?"**

> **"The fake smile."**

Bunny's ears lowered.

Belle sat beside her.

> **"You don't need that with me."**

Bunny stared at the floor.

Belle smiled.

> **"I'm happy."**

She pointed toward Bunny.

> **"You don't have to be happy too."**

Bunny's eyes filled with tears.

She nodded.

---

# **THE MOST CONCERNING DETAIL**

Bunny does not appear to understand that other people can remain happy while she is sad.

She treats emotions as though they are mutually exclusive.

If someone is happy:

> **Bunny should be happy.**

If someone is sad:

> **Bunny should become happy to make them feel better.**

If Bunny herself becomes scared:

> **Bunny must become happy immediately.**

This behavior appears deeply rooted.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

There are now two versions of Bunny that personnel regularly observe.

The Bunny who is alone.

And the Bunny who thinks someone is watching.

When alone, she may sit beneath Corin's wing and quietly stare outside.

When Corin walks in:

> **"BIG SIS!"**

Immediate smile.

When Orphie enters:

> **"Bigger Sis!"**

Immediate smile.

When Belle arrives:

> **"Pink rabbit!"**

Immediate smile.

When someone asks:

> **"Are you happy?"**

The answer is always:

> **"Bunny happy!"**

Even when it clearly isn't true.

---

# **CORIN'S RULE**

Corin has established a simple rule regarding Bunny.

> **"If Bunny is sad, let Bunny be sad."**

No forced cheering.

No demands to smile.

No questions about why she isn't happy.

No punishment for crying.

No expectation that Bunny must make everyone else feel better.

Corin has also begun deliberately sitting beside Bunny without saying anything.

Sometimes Bunny talks.

Sometimes she doesn't.

Sometimes she smiles.

Sometimes she doesn't.

Corin stays either way.

---

|Self-directed emotional care|**Poor**|
|Trust in Corin|**Extremely high**|
|Trust in SAFEHOUSE|**Increasing**|

|Genuine happiness|**Present but inconsistent**|

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Bunny doesn't smile because she is always happy.

She smiles because she wants everyone else to be happy.

She smiles because she is afraid.

She smiles because somewhere inside her, happiness has become armor.

> **"Bunny happy when others are happy."**

> **"Bunny scared."**

Then, as though correcting herself:

> **"Bunny happy."**

> **"No Bunny scared."**

Corin doesn't accept the correction anymore.

If Bunny is scared, she's scared.

If Bunny is sad, she's sad.

If Bunny is happy, she's happy.

She doesn't need to hide any of them.

And perhaps, slowly, Bunny will learn something she has never been allowed to believe:

**Other people's happiness does not require her to disappear behind a smile.**
