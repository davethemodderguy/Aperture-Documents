# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / POST-RECOVERY BEHAVIORAL LOG**

# **THE WINGS THAT NO LONGER FLY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **RECOVERING / FLIGHT CAPABILITY LOST**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's physical condition continues to stabilize.

Her behavior remains deeply concerning.

Despite having access to warm food and clean water, Corin has developed a habit of consuming **snow and wild flowers** while outside the Treehouse.

Medical personnel have repeatedly attempted to discourage this behavior.

Corin does not appear particularly interested in changing it.

---

# **SNOW**

Corin frequently gathers fresh snow with her hands and eats it.

When offered water instead, she usually accepts it eventually.

But when left alone, she returns to the snow.

Her explanation is simple:

> **"It's cold."**

She does not elaborate.

---

# **WILD FLOWERS**

Corin also consumes wild flowers growing around the Treehouse and surrounding terrain.

She appears to select flowers she recognizes as non-toxic.

The behavior may be partly nutritional.

However, personnel suspect it is primarily habitual.

The flowers remind her of the Treehouse and the environment in which she spent much of her missing year.

---

# **THE WINGS**

Corin's wings have changed.

One year of near-continuous cold exposure and complete absence of flight has left them noticeably dulled.

Their former brightness has faded.

The feathers are less vibrant.

Their movement is slower.

Their flexibility has decreased.

Most significantly:

> **Corin can no longer fly.**

---

# **FLIGHT TEST**

A controlled flight test was conducted after Corin's physical recovery reached an acceptable threshold.

She extended both wings.

Attempted to generate lift.

Nothing happened.

She tried again.

The wings moved.

But not with enough strength or coordination to support her weight.

Corin eventually stopped.

She sat down.

Wrapped her wings around herself.

And said:

> **"Okay."**

---

# **NO FRUSTRATION**

Medical personnel expected disappointment.

There was none.

Corin did not cry.

She did not become angry.

She did not attempt another test.

She simply accepted the result.

When asked whether she wanted rehabilitation, she replied:

> **"No."**

When asked whether she wanted her wings repaired or strengthened:

> **"They're fine."**

They are not fine.

Corin simply does not care.

---

# **THE CONTRAST**

This is markedly different from Corin's behavior before her disappearance.

Flying was once one of the most important things in her life.

She used flight to:

- Explore.
    
- Play.
    
- Travel.
    
- Carry SAFEHOUSE residents.
    
- Escape stressful situations.
    
- Clear her mind.
    
- Feel free.
    

She once described flying as:

> **"Something that keeps my mind away from remembering the past."**

Now she cannot fly.

And she does not appear to miss it.

---

# **WING CONDITION**

|Feature|Previous|Current|
|---|---|---|
|Feather brightness|High|**Dulled**|
|Flexibility|High|**Reduced**|
|Strength|Exceptional|**Reduced**|
|Flight|Fully functional|**Non-functional**|

|Corin's concern|Very high|**None**|

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie attempted to encourage Corin to undergo rehabilitation.

Corin listened.

Then:

> **"I don't need to fly."**

Orphie replied:

> **"You used to love it."**

Corin looked down at her wings.

> **"I used to love lots of things."**

Orphie had no response.

---

# **THE OUTSIDE**

Corin continues spending most of her time outside.

She sits beneath the Treehouse.

She wraps herself in her wings.

She eats snow.

Sometimes she picks a wild flower and quietly eats it.

Sometimes she simply holds one.

She rarely enters the Treehouse voluntarily.

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

The loss of flight itself is medically significant.

The lack of emotional response is more concerning.

Corin does not appear to perceive the loss as something worth recovering.

Her current behavior suggests a broader pattern of emotional disengagement.

Things that once mattered to her:

**Flying.**

**Flowers.**

**Celebrations.**

**Light.**

**Hope.**

**Home.**

have increasingly become things she can live without.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's wings once carried her across worlds.

They carried friends.

They carried family.

They gave her freedom.

They were the thing she used when she needed to escape her own thoughts.

Now they are dull.

Heavy.

Weak.

Unable to lift her from the ground.

Corin looks at them sometimes.

Then wraps them around herself.

She doesn't try to fly.

She doesn't ask for help.

She doesn't complain.

She simply sits beneath the tree, shivering in the cold, eating snow and wild flowers.

When Orphie asks whether she wants her wings to fly again, Corin gives the same answer.

> **"I don't care."**

And somehow, that worries everyone more than if she had cried.

**End of Corin / Post-Recovery Behavioral Log**
