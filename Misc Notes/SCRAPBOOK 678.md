### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE LOG**

# **BUNNY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 — Internal Personnel  
**Primary Subject:** Corin Lockwood  
**Secondary Subject:** Bunny  
**Location:** Treehouse  
**Status:** **STABLE / RESIDENT**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The injured snow rabbit discovered near the Treehouse has officially been named.

Not because Corin found an elaborate name.

Not because C.O.R.E. assigned one.

Corin simply looked at the rabbit and said:

> **"Bunny."**

And that was apparently the end of the discussion.

The rabbit is now officially designated:

# **BUNNY**

---

# **ANOMALOUS BEHAVIOR**

A more concerning discovery was made during Bunny's recovery.

Bunny appears to understand **human language**.

Not merely vocal tone.

Not merely familiar commands.

Actual spoken language.

The initial incident was considered coincidence.

It was not.

---

# **INITIAL TEST**

Lumi entered the Treehouse and said:

> **"Bunny, don't chew that."**

Bunny immediately stopped chewing the blanket.

Lumi froze.

Corin looked over.

> **"...See?"**

Lumi stared at the rabbit.

> **"I thought that was coincidence."**

Corin shook her head.

> **"Nope."**

---

# **CONFIRMATION**

Further informal testing was conducted.

Bunny demonstrated appropriate responses to:

- Its name.
    
- Requests to move.
    
- Requests to stay still.
    
- Warnings about dangerous objects.
    
- Questions regarding food.
    
- Requests to return to its resting area.
    
- Simple conversations.
    

The rabbit appears capable of understanding considerably more than expected.

No anomalous vocalization has been observed.

Bunny simply listens.

---

# **THE STRANGE PART**

Bunny does not appear surprised by human speech.

It behaves as though understanding people is completely normal.

When spoken to, Bunny will often:

- Tilt its head.
    
- Look directly at the speaker.
    
- Follow the instruction.
    
- Ignore the instruction.
    
- Occasionally stare at the speaker as though judging them.
    

The final behavior has been observed most frequently when Neko is nearby.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin doesn't seem particularly concerned.

When informed that Bunny's linguistic comprehension was unusual, she simply responded:

> **"Oh."**

Personnel asked:

> **"You're not surprised?"**

Corin:

> **"Bunny is smart."**

> **"That's not what we meant."**

Corin:

> **"Still smart."**

Discussion concluded.

---

# **TREEHOUSE RESIDENCE**

Corin now spends most of her time at the Treehouse.

This is considered a positive development.

Previously, the Treehouse represented isolation and waiting.

Now it represents something different.

A home.

Corin sleeps there.

She draws there.

She reads there.

She flies around the surrounding area.

She collects flowers.

And, increasingly...

she spends time with Bunny.

---

# **CORIN AND BUNNY**

The two have developed an unusually close relationship.

Bunny frequently follows Corin around the Treehouse.

When Corin sits beneath the tree, Bunny sits beside her.

When Corin reads, Bunny lies near her feet.

When Corin draws, Bunny watches.

When Corin sleeps in cat form, Bunny sometimes curls up beside her.

Corin appears completely comfortable with the arrangement.

---

# **THE OLD TREE**

The same tree beneath which Corin spent months waiting remains important.

The difference is striking.

Previously:

Corin sat beneath it alone.

Her wings covered her body.

She trembled.

She waited for someone who had already returned.

Now:

Corin sits beneath the same tree.

Her wings are usually relaxed.

Bunny sits beside her.

Her Feather Lanterns glow overhead.

And sometimes Corin draws.

---

# **A CONVERSATION**

One afternoon, Orphie found Corin and Bunny sitting together.

> **"What are you two doing?"**

Corin answered:

> **"Nothing."**

Bunny looked at Orphie.

Orphie crouched.

> **"Bunny, are you listening to me?"**

Bunny nodded.

Orphie froze.

Corin started laughing.

> **"See? I told you."**

---

# **CORIN'S NOTEBOOK**

A new section has been added to Corin's research notes.

### **SUBJECT: BUNNY**

> **"Bunny understands people."**

> **"I don't know how."**

> **"I asked if Bunny understands me."**

> **"Bunny nodded."**

A crude drawing follows.

It resembles a rabbit.

Barely.

Underneath:

> **"Bunny is smarter than my drawings."**

Another line:

> **"That's not hard."**

The last sentence is heavily scratched out.

---

# **BUNNY'S PREFERENCES**

Based on observation:

- Likes Corin.
    
- Likes warm areas.
    
- Likes being under Corin's wings.
    
- Likes flowers.
    
- Likes vegetables.
    
- Appears to understand human conversation.
    
- Does not appear interested in explaining itself.
    

Bunny has also demonstrated a particular fondness for sitting beside Corin rather than inside its designated recovery enclosure.

C.O.R.E. has stopped attempting to relocate it.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin spends most of her time at the Treehouse now.

But she no longer sits there waiting.

She lives there.

There is a difference.

She has Bunny beside her.

A little snow rabbit that somehow understands every word people say.

Nobody knows why.

Bunny isn't telling anyone.

And Corin doesn't seem interested in investigating.

She is too busy sitting beneath the tree with her new friend.

One afternoon, Lumi asked:

> **"Corin, are you lonely?"**

Corin looked at Bunny.

Bunny looked back.

Corin smiled.

> **"No."**

Then she returned to drawing.

The drawing was terrible.

Bunny stared at it.

Corin stared at Bunny.

> **"...Don't judge me."**

Bunny blinked.

Corin laughed.

The Feather Lantern above them glowed a little brighter.
