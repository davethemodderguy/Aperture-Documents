# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **LONG-TERM MEDICAL & PSYCHOLOGICAL REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – PERSISTENT SELF-RESENTMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although Corin Lockwood's major organ systems have resumed normal function, Psychological Services continue to identify her self-perception as the greatest obstacle to recovery.

From a medical perspective, Corin is now physiologically dependent on normal biological processes.

From Corin's perspective...

Very little has changed.

She continues to see the permanent changes to her body as evidence of the abuse she endured.

---

# **PERSISTENT SELF-RESENTMENT**

Corin is frequently observed engaging in prolonged periods of self-criticism.

The thoughts are often repetitive.

She revisits memories of the experimentation.

She remembers being restrained.

She remembers being cut open.

She remembers being discussed as though she were equipment rather than a frightened child.

Those memories shape the way she interprets her appearance.

Medical Services note that Corin's resentment is directed almost entirely inward.

She does not blame the mirror.

She blames the person reflected in it.

---

# **BODY IMAGE**

Although Corin's body now functions much like that of an ordinary human, several permanent physical changes remain.

These include:

- Chalk-pale skin.
    
- Chalk-lime hair.
    
- Extensive surgical scarring.
    

Medical examinations have found no evidence that her natural pigmentation will return.

These features are considered permanent.

To Corin, they are not simply cosmetic differences.

They are constant reminders of the years that were stolen from her.

---

# **COSMETIC RESTRICTIONS**

During one consultation, Corin quietly asked whether she could dye her hair black again.

Following extensive evaluation, Medical Services determined that conventional hair dyes react unpredictably with the altered structure of Corin's hair and residual Ether-associated tissue.

As a precaution, Corin has been instructed **not** to use commercial hair dyes or chemical bleaching agents until further research establishes a safe method.

The restriction is based on the current understanding of her unique physiology and the potential for severe adverse reactions.

When informed, Corin simply nodded.

She did not ask another question.

Later that evening, she told Lumi:

> "...Even my hair won't let me go back."

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi found Corin sitting in front of a mirror with a lock of her pale hair wrapped around her fingers.

Corin spoke without turning around.

> "...I don't hate mirrors."

A long pause.

> "...I hate what I remember when I look into them."

Lumi quietly walked behind her.

She rested both hands on Corin's shoulders.

> "The mirror shows your face."

> "Your memories tell you a story."

Corin lowered her head.

> "...And I can't stop hearing it."

Lumi gently embraced her from behind.

> "Then I'll keep telling you another one."

---

# **SAFEHOUSE**

The residents long ago stopped commenting on Corin's appearance.

To them, the pale skin and chalk-lime hair are simply Corin.

Star often says the color is pretty.

Bao occasionally braids Corin's hair while they chat.

Belle absentmindedly plays with it during movie nights.

Mart once remarked that the color made Corin easier to find in the library.

Corin rarely believes the compliments.

But she no longer argues with them.

She simply listens.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services emphasize that Corin's distress is not caused solely by her physical appearance.

Rather, it is the meaning she assigns to those permanent changes.

Treatment therefore focuses on addressing the trauma and beliefs associated with her appearance, rather than attempting to erase the appearance itself.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin continues to struggle with persistent self-resentment and body image disturbance despite significant physical recovery.

Medical staff remain cautiously optimistic that, over time, she may learn to see her appearance as evidence of survival rather than evidence of worthlessness.

For now, however, every glance into a mirror still asks Corin a question she has not yet learned to answer with kindness.

**End of Long-Term Medical & Psychological Report**
