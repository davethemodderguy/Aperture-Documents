# **COREY █████████ — FAMILY LOSS INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — SEALED

---

# **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

The recovered medical record concerning **Corey █████████** has been supplemented with previously unavailable information.

At approximately five years of age, Corey was having dinner with her family when an **artillery shell struck the area**.

The resulting explosion killed the other members of her immediate family who were present.

### **Confirmed deceased:**

- **Corey's little brother**
    
- **Corey's mother**
    
- **Corey's father**
    

Corey survived.

She was found among the remains of the incident with extensive injuries.

---

# **SURVIVOR INJURIES**

The original medical record documented:

- Direct exposure to the explosion.
    
- Approximately **50 mcl of Ether Radiation exposure**.
    
- Open head lacerations.
    
- Multiple bodily injuries.
    
- A ruptured neck vein.
    
- Additional injuries that remain ████████████████████.
    

Despite the severity of the injuries, Corey remained alive.

She was subsequently transported for emergency medical treatment.

---

# **THE PHOTOGRAPH**

A photograph was attached to the medical record.

It shows Corey at approximately five years old.

She is wearing a **small white beanie**, carefully positioned over her head.

The hat concealed much of the visible damage.

Her expression is difficult to interpret.

She appears young.

Small.

Quiet.

And completely unaware of how much her life had just changed.

---

# **AFTERMATH**

Corey survived the incident.

Her family did not.

The surviving documentation concerning the period immediately following the explosion remains heavily redacted.

The exact circumstances surrounding her subsequent recovery are unknown.

What is known is that the child who entered the medical system as **Corey █████████** eventually became the person now known as:

> **Orphie.**

---

# **THE NAME**

Orphie later changed her name.

The change did **not** create a new identity.

She did not become another person.

She did not gain a second personality.

**Orphie is Corey.**

The same individual who survived the explosion.

The same child depicted in the photograph.

The same person carrying the scars left by the incident.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S PRIVATE OBSERVATION**

Guiding Light reviewed the newly recovered information alone.

She remained silent for several minutes.

Then she wrote:

> **"So that's where it began."**

Another line followed.

> **"She was five."**

Then:

> **"She lost everyone at the table."**

The remainder of the note was left blank.

---

# **CONFIDENTIALITY**

Orphie remains **unaware of this record**.

She has not been informed that Guiding Light recovered documentation concerning the incident.

She has not been shown the photograph.

She has not been told that the medical record confirms the deaths of her parents and younger brother.

No personnel are authorized to disclose this information without explicit Architect-level approval.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Before SAFEHOUSE.

Before the Blacksite.

Before the name **Orphie**.

There was a five-year-old girl named **Corey** sitting at dinner with her family.

An artillery shell struck.

Her parents died.

Her little brother died.

And Corey survived.

Everything that came afterward began with the fact that she was the one who remained.
