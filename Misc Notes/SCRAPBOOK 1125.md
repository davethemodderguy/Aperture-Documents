### RESIDENT WELFARE & CULTURAL ARCHIVES DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-BAO-242

**SUBJECT:** Bao — Vietnamese Sticky Rice  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE CAFETERIA

---

# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

Bao was observed consuming Vietnamese sticky rice in the SAFEHOUSE cafeteria.

No anomalous activity occurred.

No containment breach occurred.

No medical intervention was required.

The event was nevertheless considered noteworthy because Bao became noticeably more animated than usual upon recognizing the food.

---

# 2. BACKGROUND

Bao was Vietnamese during her original human life.

Prior to her death, she was an ordinary human woman from Vietnam.

Following her death, Bao was reincarnated as a **Starladian Whale** before eventually assuming her current form and becoming the individual currently residing within SAFEHOUSE.

Although considerable time has passed since her original human life, Bao retains memories, cultural knowledge, and personal associations from that period.

Vietnamese food is therefore not merely an unfamiliar cultural artifact to her.

It is familiar.

Very familiar.

---

# 3. THE STICKY RICE

The food was identified as Vietnamese sticky rice.

Bao approached the cafeteria counter.

She stopped.

Looked at the serving tray.

Looked at the cafeteria worker.

Then back at the tray.

> **BAO:**  
> “Is that xôi?”

The cafeteria worker confirmed that it was.

Bao immediately took a serving.

She sat down.

She began eating.

For approximately thirty seconds, she said nothing.

She simply ate.

---

# 4. OBSERVATION

Neri was sitting several seats away with her coffee.

She watched Bao.

Eventually:

> **NERI:**  
> “Good?”

Bao nodded.

> **BAO:**  
> “Very.”

Neri looked at the bowl.

> **NERI:**  
> “What is it?”

Bao paused.

She looked mildly offended.

> **BAO:**  
> “Sticky rice.”

Neri considered this.

> **NERI:**  
> “I know.”

Bao laughed.

---

# 5. CULTURAL MEMORY

Bao later explained that sticky rice was one of those foods that produced an unusually strong sense of familiarity.

Not because it possessed anomalous properties.

Not because reincarnation had somehow preserved every detail of her former life.

Simply because some memories remain attached to ordinary things.

A smell.

A texture.

A particular meal.

The way food is prepared.

The experience of eating something that existed long before the Blacksite.

Long before Starladia.

Long before she became what she is now.

---

# 6. BAO'S COMMENT

When asked whether eating Vietnamese food ever made her miss her former life, Bao considered the question.

> **BAO:**  
> “Sometimes.”

Pause.

> **BAO:**  
> “But not always.”

She took another bite.

> **BAO:**  
> “Sometimes food is just food.”

C.O.R.E. personnel accepted this answer.

---

# 7. FROST'S OBSERVATION

Frost later entered the cafeteria and found Bao eating.

She looked at the bowl.

> **FROST:**  
> “What's that?”

Bao answered:

> **BAO:**  
> “Xôi.”

Frost looked at Neri.

Neri shrugged.

Frost looked back at Bao.

> **FROST:**  
> “Is it good?”

Bao smiled.

> **BAO:**  
> “Yes.”

Frost sat down.

> **FROST:**  
> “Can I try?”

Bao pushed the bowl toward her.

> **BAO:**  
> “Of course.”

---

# 8. ARCHIVIST NOTE

There is a tendency among newer personnel to treat reincarnated entities as though their previous lives have been completely erased.

This is incorrect.

Bao remembers being human.

She remembers being Vietnamese.

She remembers foods.

She remembers cultural habits.

She remembers pieces of a life that existed before her current existence.

Reincarnation changed what Bao **is.**

It did not necessarily erase everything she **was.**

---

# 9. CAFETERIA POLICY

Following the incident, the cafeteria has been encouraged to occasionally include culturally significant foods associated with the known backgrounds of SAFEHOUSE residents.

This is **not** being treated as a mandatory therapeutic program.

Bao specifically requested that personnel not turn every Vietnamese dish into:

> **“Bao's Nostalgia Meal.”**

Her exact response was:

> **BAO:**  
> “I just like Vietnamese food.”

This request has been respected.

---

# 10. FINAL OBSERVATION

Bao finished the sticky rice.

She looked at the empty bowl for several seconds.

Then quietly said:

> **BAO:**  
> “I haven't had that in a very long time.”

Neri, sitting nearby, looked at her.

She did not ask why.

She did not ask about Bao's human life.

She simply pushed her own unopened dessert toward Bao.

Bao looked at it.

> **BAO:**  
> “What's this?”

Neri shrugged.

> **NERI:**  
> “Cake.”

Bao smiled.

> **BAO:**  
> “You don't want it?”

Neri shook her head.

A pause.

> **NERI:**  
> “You look like you need it.”

Bao stared at her.

Then laughed.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Bao has been a human.

Bao has been a Starladian Whale.

Bao is now something else entirely.

But occasionally, none of those distinctions matter.

Sometimes she is simply a Vietnamese woman sitting in a cafeteria, eating xôi.

And apparently that's enough.
