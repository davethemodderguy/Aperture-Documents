# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z945-LOG-012**

### **RESIDENTIAL RECORD – BEDTIME STORIES OF THE BANLANDS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

During one winter evening, the residents of Room N-07 gathered together before bedtime.

At Endmin's request, Z-945 (Lucy) shared stories from the Banlands' distant past—long before Endmin was born, before the Friendly Roommates existed, and before many of the Blacksite's current residents had ever met one another.

The session was recorded with Lucy's permission for historical preservation.

---

## **Observation**

Outside, snow quietly fell over the Blacksite.

Inside Room N-07, the lights had been dimmed.

Taiga lay on the wool rug with a blanket wrapped around her.

Mart leaned against the wall with a book resting unopened in his lap.

Neko and Miss Neko sat near the window.

Star and Nightshade quietly listened from the couch.

Bao, Frost, Astral, Niko, and Aster all settled into comfortable places around the room.

Endmin rested beside Lucy, already leaning against her shoulder.

Lucy smiled.

> **"Ready?"**

Several voices answered together.

> **"Yeah."**

---

## **Lucy's Story**

Lucy looked toward the frosted window.

For a moment, she was silent.

Then she began.

> "Before Endmin..."

> "...before many of you..."

> "...there was only the Banlands."

> "Not a land of fire and punishment."

> "A prison."

> "A place where lost souls wandered, where Ether filled the air like mist."

The room remained quiet.

> "Back then..."

> "...I thought no one from another world would ever find us."

She chuckled softly.

> "Then one day..."

> "...someone did."

Taiga tilted her head.

> **"Director Lucious?"**

Lucy nodded.

> "Yes."

---

## **The First Meeting**

> "He arrived without an army."

> "Without demands."

> "Without fear."

Lucy smiled faintly.

> "I thought he was another fool."

> "He thought I looked exhausted."

Several people laughed quietly.

Lucy laughed as well.

> "Instead of asking how to defeat us..."

> "...he asked if we needed help."

Endmin looked up.

She had heard the story before.

But she still listened carefully.

---

## **A Different Kind of Visitor**

Lucy continued.

> "The Banlands had seen conquerors."

> "Mercenaries."

> "Researchers."

> "People chasing power."

She folded her hands.

> "Lucious was different."

> "He listened."

> "He learned."

> "He treated us as people before anomalies."

The room fell silent again.

---

## **Endmin's Birth**

Lucy looked down at Endmin.

She gently brushed a strand of white hair away from her daughter's face.

> "Years later..."

> "...this little troublemaker was born."

Endmin smiled sheepishly.

> "...Mom."

Lucy laughed.

> "She was curious."

> "Always wandering."

> "Always asking questions."

> "Always carrying something she wasn't supposed to."

Neko quietly whispered,

> "...That part hasn't changed."

Everyone chuckled.

---

## **The Blacksite**

Lucy looked around Room N-07.

> "When Endmin came here..."

> "...I wasn't sure I'd made the right decision."

She looked at Taiga.

Then Mart.

Then the others.

> "Now..."

> "...she laughs."

> "...She races through the halls."

> "...She eats ice cream."

> "...She argues about vegetables."

Endmin buried her face in a pillow.

The room laughed warmly.

Lucy smiled.

> "She gets embarrassed over the smallest things."

> "...And she has people she trusts."

> "That's something I couldn't give her back then."

---

## **A Quiet Ending**

The room gradually grew quieter.

Taiga was already asleep.

Mart's book had slipped from his hands.

Star rested against Nightshade's shoulder.

Aster was curled beside Endmin.

Lucy looked down.

Endmin's breathing had become slow and steady.

She had fallen asleep halfway through the story.

Lucy smiled gently.

Without waking her, she lay down beside her daughter and pulled the blanket over them both.

She softly stroked Endmin's hair.

> **"...Good night, Lindervene."**

For once, Endmin didn't correct her.

Half-asleep, she only mumbled,

> **"...Night... Mom..."**

Lucy closed her eyes.

Outside, the snow continued to fall.

Inside Room N-07, surrounded by friends who had become family, mother and daughter quietly drifted off to sleep together.

---

## **Archive Note**

Director Lucious later reviewed the recording.

He made no edits.

He only added a single handwritten note to the end of the file.

> **"History is important."**

> **"But it's moments like these that make it worth protecting."**
