# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE – INTERPERSONAL INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: DISPUTE BETWEEN NORMA & CORIN LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

A verbal argument occurred between Norma Lockwood and Corin Lockwood inside Project SAFEHOUSE.

The disagreement began as an attempt by Norma to confront Corin's increasingly negative view of herself.

The conversation escalated.

Both sisters were emotionally overwhelmed.

Neither intended to hurt the other.

Both left the conversation deeply hurt.

---

# **INCIDENT**

The discussion began in the SAFEHOUSE lounge after Corin once again dismissed any progress she had made.

Norma had spent months watching Corin reject compliments, isolate herself, and blame herself for the crimes committed against her as a child.

The frustration that had quietly accumulated finally surfaced.

Norma raised her voice.

> "How long are you going to let them keep controlling your life?"

Corin looked down.

She did not answer.

Norma continued.

> "They're gone."

> "You're here."

> "You're safe."

> "Why can't you see that?"

Corin's hands began to shake.

She answered quietly.

> "...Because I remember."

Norma stepped closer.

Tears had already formed in her eyes.

> "We all remember things!"

Corin flinched.

Norma immediately regretted raising her voice.

But the words had already left.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin stood silently for several seconds.

Then she spoke.

Her voice was calm.

Almost emotionless.

> "...You think I want this?"

She looked directly at Norma.

> "...I remember every time they treated me like I wasn't a person."

Another pause.

> "...Every new thing my body can do reminds me."

Her voice cracked.

> "...I can't leave it behind."

Norma tried to answer.

Corin took a step back.

> "...I'm trying."

Those were the last words she spoke before turning away.

---

# **AFTERMATH**

Corin did not return to her room.

She left SAFEHOUSE.

Security cameras tracked her exiting the residential wing and walking into the Blacksite's exterior grounds despite ongoing snowfall and freezing temperatures.

She eventually stopped beneath a snow-covered tree.

She sat down alone.

The cold did not seem to bother her altered physiology.

The silence did.

She remained there for a long time, watching snow settle around her.

---

# **NORMA**

Several minutes after the argument, Norma realized Corin had not returned.

She searched the building.

Corin's room was empty.

The lounge was empty.

The kitchen was empty.

Norma's frustration disappeared almost instantly, replaced by panic.

She whispered to herself,

> "...What have I done?"

Without waiting for anyone else, she ran outside into the snow.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi found Corin first.

She quietly sat beside her in the snow.

Neither spoke for several minutes.

Finally, Corin whispered,

> "...I made her hate me."

Lumi gently shook her head.

> "No."

> "She was frightened."

Corin stared at the falling snow.

> "...She sounded angry."

Lumi answered softly.

> "Sometimes fear sounds like anger."

---

# **RECONCILIATION**

Norma eventually reached them.

She stopped several meters away.

For a moment, she could not bring herself to speak.

Then, with tears already falling, she said the only words that mattered.

> "...I'm sorry."

Corin looked up.

Norma continued.

> "I wasn't angry because of your past."

> "I was angry because I can't take it away."

She covered her face.

> "I hate seeing you hurt."

Corin lowered her head.

After a long silence, she quietly replied,

> "...I'm sorry too."

Norma walked forward and embraced her little sister.

Corin hesitated only briefly before returning the hug.

Neither of them had won the argument.

Neither of them had solved the past.

But both understood something they had forgotten in the heat of the moment.

They were never fighting each other.

They were both fighting the same pain.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin returned to Project SAFEHOUSE later that evening alongside Norma and Lumi.

Psychological Services noted that disagreements are an expected part of close relationships, particularly when trauma affects both the person who survived it and the people who love them.

The incident concluded with no lasting division between the sisters.

It did, however, remind everyone of an important truth.

Trauma is not only carried by the survivor.

It is also felt by the family who wishes, more than anything, that they could have protected them.

**End of Project SAFEHOUSE Interpersonal Incident Report**
