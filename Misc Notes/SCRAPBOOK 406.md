### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / TREEHOUSE INCIDENT LOG**

# **ORPHIE — CORIN'S ABSENCE FROM WORK**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Concern  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Marts / Luna / Solaria  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **ACTIVE CONCERN**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin made an unusual decision.

She skipped one day of work at the KCA.

Her intention was simple:

> **Stay home with Orphie.**

After weeks of isolation, Corin believed that spending an entire day together might help.

It did not.

Instead, Orphie became **extremely angry**.

---

# **CORIN'S DECISION**

Corin informed the KCA that she would not be reporting for work.

She did not provide an elaborate explanation.

She simply wanted to remain with Orphie.

For once, there would be no late return from work.

No long hours alone.

No empty treehouse.

No waiting.

Corin thought her presence might make a difference.

---

# **ORPHIE'S REACTION**

When Orphie learned that Corin had stayed home specifically for her, her normally emotionless demeanor changed dramatically.

She became angry.

Not mildly irritated.

Not annoyed.

**Extremely angry.**

Her voice rose.

> "...Why aren't you at work?"

Corin:

> "...I stayed home."

> "...Why?"

> "...Because I wanted to be with you."

Orphie immediately responded:

> "...Don't."

---

# **THE ARGUMENT**

Corin attempted to explain.

> "...You've been alone for too long."

Orphie:

> "...So?"

> "...I thought you might want company."

> "...I didn't ask you to skip work."

Corin:

> "...I know."

Orphie:

> "...Then why did you?"

Corin:

> "...Because you're important to me."

Orphie became visibly more upset.

> "...Stop doing that."

---

# **WHY ORPHIE BECAME ANGRY**

Orphie's anger was not directed at Corin for wanting to spend time with her.

It came from the belief that Corin was sacrificing something important **because of her**.

Orphie's existing belief that she is a burden appears to have interpreted Corin's absence as confirmation.

In Orphie's reasoning:

> Corin missed work because of Orphie.

Therefore:

> Orphie is causing problems for Corin.

Therefore:

> Orphie is a burden.

Corin attempted to reject this logic.

Orphie would not accept it.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin remained calm despite Orphie's anger.

> "...I chose to stay."

Orphie:

> "...You shouldn't have."

> "...I wanted to."

> "...That's not the point."

Corin:

> "...Then what is?"

Orphie:

> "...You have your own life."

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts became extremely attentive during the argument.

They did not intervene.

They remained close to Orphie but gave her space.

Several Marts gathered near Corin.

Others stayed near Orphie.

None attempted their usual playful behavior.

They appeared to understand that this was not the time.

---

# **CORIN'S REALIZATION**

Corin eventually understood that her attempt had accidentally reinforced Orphie's worst belief.

She had intended to communicate:

> **"You matter enough for me to stay."**

Orphie heard:

> **"You are important enough to disrupt my life."**

Those statements are completely different to Orphie.

---

# **THE ARGUMENT ENDS**

Eventually, Orphie's anger subsided.

She did not apologize.

Corin did not demand one.

The two remained in the same room.

After several minutes, Orphie quietly said:

> "...Go to work tomorrow."

Corin:

> "...Okay."

Orphie:

> "...Don't skip it for me again."

Corin looked at her.

> "...Okay."

---

# **LUNA'S OBSERVATION**

Luna later described the incident:

> "...She doesn't want to be someone's responsibility."

> "...Even when they're choosing to stay."

Luna paused.

> "...She thinks being loved means making someone sacrifice something."

---

# **SOLARIA'S OBSERVATION**

Solaria gave a similar assessment.

> "...Corin tried to show her that she mattered."

> "...Orphie interpreted it as proof that she's a burden."

She sighed.

> "...That's how backwards everything has become."

---

# **CORIN'S WORK**

Corin returned to work the following day.

She did not stop caring about Orphie.

She simply understood that staying home indefinitely was not the answer.

The KCA remains part of Corin's life.

And Orphie, despite her anger, appears to understand that.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Orphie:**  
Emotionally detached but capable of intense anger when she believes others are sacrificing their lives for her.

**Corin:**  
Highly concerned. Attempted increased presence but learned that direct sacrifice can worsen Orphie's burden-related beliefs.

**Marts:**  
Concerned. Remained nearby without interfering.

**SAFEHOUSE:**  
Informed of the incident.

**Luna:**  
Concerned.

**Solaria:**  
Concerned.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin stayed home because she cared.

Orphie became furious because she cared about what Corin was giving up.

The tragedy is that Orphie can still recognize the value of someone else's life.

She simply cannot recognize the value of her own.

Corin did not convince Orphie that she was loved that day.

But she learned something important:

Sometimes staying beside Orphie means knowing when to stay—

and when to let her see that the people around her can continue living their own lives without abandoning her.

**End of Personnel Welfare / Treehouse Incident Log**
