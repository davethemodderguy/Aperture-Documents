# 1. DESIGN

The customization is centered around Bao's association with water.

The overall appearance incorporates:

- flowing wave-like decorative elements;
    
- aquatic motifs;
    
- subtle marine detailing;
    
- a clean, fluid visual style;
    
- and personalized markings associated with Bao.
    

The design deliberately avoids excessive ornamentation.

Rather than looking flashy, NS-007 has a calm and almost ceremonial appearance.

It looks less like something designed for intimidation.

More like something that belongs to the ocean.
