## **SUMMARY**

Additional background information has been added to the personnel records of **Keith** and **Cinder**.

Although the siblings chose different careers, both established distinguished records before serving alongside Director Belle Merryweather.

---

# **KEITH**

**Callsign:** Keith

**Current Position:** Executive Protection Officer, Director Belle Merryweather

### **Military Service**

Prior to joining the Central Execution Department, Keith served **five years in the Banlands Military**.

His service record includes numerous frontline deployments, convoy escort missions, urban combat operations, and civilian protection details.

Medical examinations document multiple healed combat injuries, including scars across his torso, arms, and back. None of these injuries affected his ability to return to active duty.

Personnel evaluations consistently describe him as:

- Calm under pressure.
    
- Highly disciplined.
    
- Exceptionally protective of civilians and teammates.
    
- Reluctant to discuss his own achievements.
    

His military experience is considered one of the primary reasons he was selected to protect the Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority.

# **CINDER**

**Callsign:** Cinder

**Current Position:** Executive Secretary to Director Belle Merryweather

### **Academic Background**

Before joining the Krampus Compliance Authority, Cinder graduated from college with distinction.

Her personnel file includes multiple academic awards, certificates, and diplomas recognizing excellence in computing, data management, and research methodology.

---

### **Previous Employment**

Following graduation, Cinder accepted a position as a **Researcher** within the **Central Banlands Computing Department (Central Computing Department).**

Her responsibilities included:

- Systems analysis.
    
- Technical documentation.
    
- Computational research.
    
- Administrative support for advanced computing projects.
    

Performance reviews consistently rated her work as outstanding.

---

### **Career Change**

Despite her success, Cinder voluntarily resigned from the Computing Department.

When asked for her reason during her exit interview, she answered simply:

> **"...The work never ended."

She explained that the constant workload left little opportunity for a healthy work-life balance.

Seeking a role where she could still contribute while maintaining a more sustainable routine, she accepted the position of Executive Secretary to Director Belle Merryweather.

Supervisors noted that although her new role remained demanding, she appeared noticeably happier after the transition.
