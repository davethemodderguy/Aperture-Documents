# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **CONFIDENTIAL WELFARE OBSERVATION**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – SOCIAL WITHDRAWAL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical Director & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **OVERVIEW**

Over the past several days, Corin Lockwood has significantly reduced contact with both KCA personnel and the residents of Heaven's Blacksite.

She continues to complete her assigned work to an exceptional standard.

She answers essential communications.

Beyond that...

She avoids people whenever possible.

Her office door remains closed.

Lunch is usually taken alone.

She leaves before anyone can invite her to join them.

No hostility has been observed.

Only distance.

---

## **THE ETHER RESONANCE**

Corin later described that the maternal Ether resonance has continued to appear during these periods.

It encourages her gently, never forcefully.

> **"They've been wondering where you are."**

Silence.

Corin continued sorting paperwork.

The voice spoke again.

> **"Belle will be worried."**

No response.

Another few moments passed.

> **"Norma made tea today."**

Corin stopped writing for only a second.

Then quietly resumed.

> "...Not today."

The voice did not argue.

It simply remained beside her.

---

## **CHILDHOOD HISTORY**

Psychological interviews have provided additional context regarding Corin's attachment to the maternal figure represented by the Ether resonance.

Among the three Lockwood siblings, Corin was the youngest.

As a child, she was often physically frail and frequently became ill.

Medical records from that time are incomplete, but family recollections are consistent.

Whenever Corin was sick, their mother would stay beside her.

She would read stories.

Brush Corin's hair away from her face.

Prepare warm soup.

Sit through long nights until the fever broke.

Those ordinary acts became some of Corin's strongest and safest memories.

Arcturus and Norma tried to help as well.

But they were children themselves.

Arcturus, though protective, was still learning what it meant to be an older brother.

Norma would bring blankets or water, convinced that somehow one more pillow would make everything better.

Their mother never expected perfection from either of them.

She simply smiled and thanked them for trying.

Those memories remain precious to all three siblings.

## **FAMILY**

**Arcturus Lockwood – Age 20**

The eldest sibling.

Protective to a fault.

He still instinctively checks that both of his sisters have eaten before worrying about himself.

---

**Norma Lockwood – Age 18**

The middle sibling.

Brilliant, energetic, and endlessly curious.

She often expresses care through practical acts—fixing equipment, making tea, or finding a problem she can solve.

---

**Corin Lockwood – Age 15**

The youngest.

Gifted beyond her years.

Quiet.

Thoughtful.

Still carrying memories no child should have.

---

## **COUNSELOR'S OBSERVATION**

During one session, Corin was asked why the maternal voice meant so much to her.

She answered almost immediately.

> "...Because it sounds like home."

After a pause, she added:

> "...I don't remember every word my mom ever said."

> "...But I remember how safe I felt when she was there."

The counselor noted that, regardless of the resonance's true origin, it appears to draw upon those memories of safety and comfort rather than replacing them.

---

## **SAFEHOUSE**

Belle noticed Corin's absence almost immediately.

The empty chair at lunch felt unusually conspicuous.

No one tried to pressure Corin into returning.

Instead, Norma quietly packed an extra meal each day.

Arcturus stopped by Accounting after work, sometimes saying nothing more than:

> "Just checking in."

If Corin only nodded, that was enough.

When she was ready to talk, they wanted her to know there would still be someone listening.
