# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **PERSONNEL MEDICAL ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: NORMA LOCKWOOD – SCAR ANALYSIS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Medical Personnel Only

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

During a routine medical examination following physical training, medical staff documented a series of long-healed surgical scars across **Senior Manager Norma Lockwood's** upper and lower back.

The scars did not match any known treatment associated with Ether Regression Syndrome (ERS).

Instead, they displayed characteristics consistent with extensive invasive surgery.

Medical personnel initially believed the scars were unrelated.

They were mistaken.

---

## **MEDICAL OBSERVATIONS**

Documented characteristics include:

- Multiple parallel surgical incisions.
    
- Precision stitching indicative of professional surgical techniques.
    
- Extensive scar tissue extending along the spine.
    
- Evidence of repeated procedures rather than a single operation.
    

There were no signs of accidental injury.

The pattern strongly suggested deliberate experimentation.

---

## **DISCUSSION**

When asked where the scars came from, Norma became unusually quiet.

She simply replied:

> "...They're old."

She declined to elaborate further.

No additional questions were asked at the time.

---

## **ARCTURUS' TESTIMONY**

Several days later, Security Advisor **Arcturus Lockwood** voluntarily requested to speak with Director Lucious, Belle, Endmin, and the medical department.

After reviewing the medical report, he quietly sighed.

Then he spoke.

> "...Those aren't from the ERS."

The room fell silent.

He removed his glasses and continued.

> "...When we were kids..."

> "...Norma and I were separated."

Belle looked up.

> "...For how long?"

Arcturus answered without hesitation.

> "...One week."

---

## **THE WEEK**

According to Arcturus:

The separation had been sudden.

Unexpected.

He searched constantly.

When he finally found his younger sister again...

She was alive.

Physically exhausted.

Covered in fresh bandages across her back.

She refused to explain what had happened.

He assumed she had simply been injured.

Years later...

The pain began.

Everyone called it Ether Regression Syndrome.

Arcturus had accepted that explanation.

Until now.

Looking at the scans, he quietly said:

> "...That wasn't a disease."

He clenched his fist.

> "...Someone did this to her."

---

## **NORMA'S RESPONSE**

Norma did not deny it.

She looked down at the floor for several moments before speaking.

> "...I didn't want you carrying that guilt."

Arcturus looked at her.

She continued.

> "You were looking for me."

> "It wasn't your fault."

> "You couldn't have known."

Arcturus's voice softened.

> "...You should've told me."

Norma smiled sadly.

> "...I wanted my big brother to keep smiling."

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

After hearing the account, Lucious quietly closed the medical file.

His expression remained composed, though noticeably more solemn.

> "Whatever was done to you..."

> "...Should never have happened."

He authorized the incident to be permanently archived as evidence of historical abuse.

---

## **BELLE**

Belle stepped forward without saying a word.

She gently hugged Norma.

Norma hesitated only briefly before returning the embrace.

> "...Thank you."

---

## **ENDMIN**

Endmin looked at the medical scans one final time.

> "...You carried this alone for fifteen years."

Norma nodded.

> "...I thought it was easier."

Endmin quietly replied:

> "...You don't have to carry it alone anymore."

---

## **FINAL NOTE**

Subsequent medical reviews concluded that Norma's former condition was **not** a naturally occurring illness.

Instead, it was the long-term consequence of unauthorized experimentation involving unstable Ether exposure and invasive surgical modification.

The successful stabilization treatment removed the active effects of the condition, but the scars remain as permanent reminders of what happened.

Norma has repeatedly declined cosmetic scar removal.

When asked why, she answered simply:

> "They're proof."

> "Proof that I survived."

---

## **STATUS**

Medical staff are instructed **not** to inquire further unless Norma chooses to discuss the subject herself.

Her scars are to be treated with the same confidentiality and respect afforded to any other sensitive medical history.

As Director Lucious noted in the file's closing remarks:

> **"Scars are not always signs of weakness."**

> **"Sometimes, they are evidence that someone endured the unimaginable—and lived."**

**End of Medical Addendum**
