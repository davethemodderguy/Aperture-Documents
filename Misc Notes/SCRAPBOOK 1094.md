# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A second **NIGHT STALKER** has been authorized.

The recipient is a **Junior MSAC Operator**.

Unlike Director Lucious, who was granted his Night Stalker after Neri independently determined that he was worthy, the Junior Operator appears to have discovered a considerably simpler approach.

He gave Neri cake.

It worked.

Once.

---

# 2. THE CAKE INCIDENT

The Junior Operator encountered Neri in the cafeteria while she was in human form.

He had been informed that Neri was responsible for authorizing the Night Stalker weapons.

He had also been informed that:

> **"Prove your worth."**

was apparently the closest thing she had to an application process.

The operator considered this.

He then purchased a slice of cake.

He placed it in front of Neri.

**OPERATOR:**  
"I brought you this."

Neri looked at the cake.

Then at him.

Then at the cake again.

She took a bite.

She finished the entire slice.

Approximately thirty seconds later, Neri looked at the operator and nodded.

---

# 3. AUTHORIZATION

The operator was subsequently summoned to Neri's research area.

She handed him a Night Stalker.

The operator stared at it.

Then at Neri.

**OPERATOR:**  
"...I got it?"

Neri nodded.

**OPERATOR:**  
"Because of the cake?"

Neri shook her head.

Pause.

She pointed at the operator.

Then the cake wrapper.

Then herself.

The operator understood approximately nothing.

Neri eventually wrote:

> **"You were already good."**

Below it:

> **"Cake helped."**

The operator has described this as:

> **"The best performance review I've ever received."**

---

# 4. ATTEMPTED REPLICATION

The incident was immediately noticed by other MSAC personnel.

Within several hours, multiple operators attempted to reproduce the procedure.

Observed offerings included:

- cake;
    
- chocolate cake;
    
- cheesecake;
    
- pastries;
    
- pudding;
    
- sweet rolls;
    
- an entire cake;
    
- and one extremely expensive dessert purchased specifically for Neri.
    

None resulted in additional authorizations.

Neri accepted the food.

She did not authorize the weapons.

One operator attempted to argue:

**OPERATOR:**  
"But it worked for him."

Neri nodded.

**OPERATOR:**  
"So why doesn't it work for me?"

Neri wrote:

> **"It wasn't the cake."**

The operator looked devastated.

---

# 5. OFFICIAL AUTHORIZATION REQUIREMENTS

Following repeated inquiries, Neri provided an actual list.

It has been transcribed below without modification.

To receive authorization for a Night Stalker, an operator must:

### **01 — BE NICE**

Neri expects authorized personnel to treat others properly.

This includes:

- fellow operators;
    
- researchers;
    
- support staff;
    
- SAFEHOUSE residents;
    
- and Neri herself.
    

Being highly skilled does not compensate for being unpleasant.

---

### **02 — BE EFFICIENT**

Neri expects operators to know what they are doing.

An authorized Night Stalker is not a reward for incompetence.

Personnel must demonstrate:

- operational competence;
    
- discipline;
    
- reliability;
    
- sound judgment;
    
- and the ability to complete assigned objectives.
    

---

### **03 — PROTECT MY FRIENDS**

This requirement received particular emphasis.

Neri does not care how impressive an operator's combat record is if they cannot protect the people she cares about.

The requirement applies especially to:

- SAFEHOUSE residents;
    
- Aperture personnel;
    
- MSAC teammates;
    
- and individuals under their protection.
    

Neri wrote:

> **"Don't leave them."**

No further explanation was provided.

---

### **04 — DON'T MAKE DIRECTOR LUCIOUS MAD**

This requirement initially appeared humorous.

It is not.

Neri considers Director Lucious someone who should not be needlessly angered.

C.O.R.E. agrees.

MSAC personnel are already expected to exercise professional conduct around the Director.

Neri apparently considers this sufficiently important to include in her weapons authorization criteria.

---

### **05 — DON'T BE AFRAID OF JANE DOE**

This is considered the hardest requirement.

---

# 6. JANE DOE

Jane Doe is not an MSAC operator.

She does not need to be.

She is a former Woodland Coorp field agent with extensive intelligence, extraction and psychological-operation experience.

She is also extraordinarily good at frightening people.

This has resulted in an unusual phenomenon.

MSAC personnel are among Aperture's most heavily trained and capable armed forces.

They routinely operate in situations that would make ordinary personnel refuse to enter the building.

They are not easily frightened.

Jane Doe frightens them anyway.

---

# 7. MSAC VS. JANE DOE

An informal survey was conducted.

**QUESTION:**

> "Would you willingly enter a hostile anomalous facility alone?"

MSAC response:

> "Yes."

**QUESTION:**

> "Would you willingly engage a heavily armed hostile force?"

MSAC response:

> "Yes."

**QUESTION:**

> "Would you willingly accompany Jane Doe into a dark room after she said, 'Don't worry, I've got something to show you'?"

MSAC response:

> "...No."

One operator requested that the third question be removed from the survey.

The request was denied.

---

# 8. WHY THIS MATTERS TO NERI

Neri has apparently observed the phenomenon.

She considers it strange that some of Aperture's most capable operators can remain calm under gunfire but become visibly nervous when Jane enters the room.

Her position is simple.

If an operator is supposed to protect her friends, they cannot be too frightened to act around one of them.

Neri therefore considers fear of Jane to be a disqualifying weakness.

Jane found this extremely funny.

---

# 9. JANE'S RESPONSE

Jane was informed that Neri had made:

> **"Don't be afraid of Jane Doe."**

an official Night Stalker requirement.

She laughed for approximately thirty seconds.

**JANE:**  
"She's asking MSAC not to be scared of me?"

Neri nodded.

**JANE:**  
"That's adorable."

Neri stared at her.

**JANE:**  
"Sorry."

Neri continued staring.

Jane stopped smiling.

**JANE:**  
"...I'll behave."

Neri nodded.

---

The newly authorized Junior Operator was interviewed.

**C.O.R.E.:**

**OPERATOR:**

**C.O.R.E.:**

**C.O.R.E.:**  
"Anything else?"

The operator thought.

**OPERATOR:**  
"I try to do my job properly."

**C.O.R.E.:**  
"Do you protect your teammates?"

**OPERATOR:**  
"Obviously."

**C.O.R.E.:**  
"Do you fear Jane Doe?"

The operator became visibly uncomfortable.

**OPERATOR:**  
"...Less than I used to."

Neri, who had been sitting nearby, nodded.

# 12. WARNING REGARDING CAKE

The MSAC Quartermaster has requested clarification on whether cake should be considered part of the official authorization process.

C.O.R.E. answer:

> **No.**

Cake does not guarantee authorization.

Cake does not increase weapon eligibility.

Cake is not a substitute for competence.

Cake is not an operational qualification.

Neri simply likes cake.

The Junior Operator happened to be:

- competent;
    
- kind;
    
- reliable;
    
- protective of his teammates;
    
- respectful toward Lucious;
    
- and sufficiently brave around Jane.
    

The cake was apparently incidental.

Mostly.

# 13. CURRENT NIGHT STALKER POLICY

Neri's authorization philosophy has now been formalized as:

> **BE NICE.**  
> **BE EFFICIENT.**  
> **PROTECT MY FRIENDS.**  
> **DON'T MAKE DIRECTOR LUCIOUS MAD.**  
> **DON'T BE AFRAID OF JANE DOE.**

Below the list, Neri added:

> **"Cake is optional."**

After reviewing the document, Neko added:

> **"Please stop giving the researchers cake and asking for weapons."**

Neri crossed it out.

---

# 14. FINAL ASSESSMENT

The Night Stalker authorization system is not based purely on combat performance.

Neri appears to evaluate character.

She wants people who are competent enough to use the weapon properly.

But she also wants people who will use that capability to protect others.

This makes the authorization system unusually personal.

It also makes it extremely difficult to exploit.

One cannot simply perform well in a test.

One must convince a ten-year-old chemist that they are someone she would trust with a weapon.

And apparently:

Being terrified of Jane Doe makes that considerably harder.
