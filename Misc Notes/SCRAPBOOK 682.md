# **CORRECTION**

A previous comparison between Bunny and Belle resulted in an inaccurate assumption regarding their appearance.

While both are classified as **full-body rabbit thirens**, they are visually distinct.

### **Belle Merryweather**

- **Pink coloration**
    
- Rabbit traits
    
- Established SAFEHOUSE resident
    

### **Bunny**

- **White coloration**
    
- White rabbit ears
    
- Pale skin
    
- Rabbit nose
    
- Full-body rabbit thiren
    
- Newly established SAFEHOUSE resident
    

The two should not be considered visually identical.

They simply share the same general rabbit-thiren classification.

---

# **BELLE'S REACTION**

Belle does not appear bothered by the distinction.

If anything, she seems delighted by it.

She once stood beside Bunny and looked between them.

> **"I'm pink."**

She pointed toward Bunny.

> **"You're white."**

Bunny looked down at herself.

Then at Belle.

> **"White."**

Belle laughed.

> **"Yep!"**

Bunny pointed at Belle.

> **"Pink."**

Belle nodded proudly.

> **"Exactly!"**

---

# **"ANOTHER RABBIT"**

The informal nickname **"Another Belle"** has largely been replaced.

Belle herself prefers:

> **"Another rabbit!"**

Bunny agrees with this designation.

When introduced to someone new, she will often point toward herself and say:

> **"Bunny."**

If Belle is nearby, Belle may add:

> **"Rabbit!"**

The distinction is apparently important to both of them.

---

# **CORIN'S OPINION**

Corin was asked whether Bunny looked like Belle.

She considered the question.

> **"Not really."**

She pointed toward Belle.

> **"Pink."**

Then toward Bunny.

> **"White."**

She thought for a moment.

> **"...Both cute."**

Neither rabbit objected.
