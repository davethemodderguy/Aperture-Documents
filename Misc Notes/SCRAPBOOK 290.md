# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – BIOLOGICAL RESEARCH ADDENDUM**

### **DESIGNATION: M.A.R.T. BLOBS – METABOLISM & EMOTIONAL DEPENDENCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Friendly Anomalous Organism

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Further biological observation has revealed that Marts do not sustain themselves through conventional nutrition.

Instead, their survival depends upon two primary factors:

- Water.
    
- Positive emotional stimulation.
    

Research has jokingly summarized the finding as:

> **"They run on water and happiness."**

Despite the informal wording, the statement is scientifically accurate.

---

# **HYDRATION**

Although composed primarily of magically stabilized water, Marts still benefit from regular contact with fresh water.

Favorite activities include:

- Swimming in sinks.
    
- Floating in ponds.
    
- Playing in fountains.
    
- Splashing in shallow streams.
    
- Sitting in buckets of clean water.
    

Following prolonged swimming sessions, Marts display increased activity and brighter coloration.

---

# **EMOTIONAL HEALTH**

A Mart's emotional condition directly influences its biological stability.

When surrounded by friendly people and positive interactions, Marts become noticeably healthier.

Documented effects include:

- Increased activity.
    
- Stronger Ether emissions.
    
- Greater enthusiasm.
    
- Longer lifespan.
    

Personnel have observed that Marts living in SAFEHOUSE frequently survive beyond the typical five-week expectancy.

Researchers attribute this to the consistently warm and welcoming environment.

---

# **LIFESPAN EXTENSION**

Although the average lifespan remains approximately five weeks, sustained positive emotional experiences combined with plentiful access to water can significantly extend an individual Mart's life.

Several individuals have exceeded the expected lifespan under these conditions.

Research has not yet determined an absolute upper limit.

---

# **CRITICAL OBSERVATION**

Unlike most organisms, Marts are extraordinarily vulnerable to severe emotional distress.

If a Mart experiences overwhelming sadness, despair, or believes it has deeply disappointed someone, its Ether containment field collapses almost immediately.

The organism gently melts into ordinary water within seconds.

No physical trauma is required.

Medical Division has confirmed that intense emotional shock alone is sufficient.

For this reason, personnel are instructed:

> **Do not intentionally frighten, bully, or emotionally distress a Mart.**

---

# **BEHAVIORAL NOTES**

Fortunately, making a Mart genuinely sad is remarkably difficult.

They are naturally optimistic and forgiving.

Common inconveniences such as:

- Being told "no."
    
- Accidentally falling over.
    
- Losing a tiny top hat.
    
- Getting stuck in a flower pot.
    

Result only in brief confusion before they happily continue with their day.

Only direct emotional rejection or sustained cruelty has been observed to cause severe distress.

No intentional testing in this area is authorized.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Marts continue to flourish throughout Aperture Industries, where abundant water and supportive companionship provide an ideal environment.

Their biology remains unique among all documented Ether lifeforms.

They require no elaborate care.

No specialized habitat.

No complex feeding schedule.

Simply clean water...

And kindness.

Research Division has therefore adopted the following official care guideline.

> **If you wish for a Mart to live a long life...**

> **Let it swim.**

> **Make it smile.**
