## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **ARCHITECTAL COMPANION / RESIDENTIAL CARE LOG**

# **JAY: A PROBLEM WITH WINGS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Internal Personnel  
**Primary Owner:** Guiding Light  
**Temporary Caretaker:** Corin  
**Status:** **HEALTHY / MISCHIEVOUS / ABSOLUTELY UNREPENTANT**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Guiding Light's blue jay, **Jay**, is healthy.

He is also a menace.

His behavior has been determined to be completely normal for a mischievous blue jay.

Unfortunately, Jay has access to:

- A treehouse.
    
- An Architect's pet status.
    
- Corin's belongings.
    
- The SAFEHOUSE.
    
- Several extremely powerful entities who refuse to discipline him.
    

This has resulted in a bird with effectively **no fear of consequences**.

---

# **KNOWN OFFENSES**

Jay has been observed:

- Stealing Corin's hairclip.
    
- Hiding Corin's notebook.
    
- Pulling threads from her scarf.
    
- Landing on Guiding Light's head during serious conversations.
    
- Stealing food directly from plates.
    
- Flying into the SAFEHOUSE with objects he found outside.
    
- Taking shiny equipment from Curious Light's workspace.
    
- Sitting on Neko's head.
    
- Attempting to steal Neko's keycard.
    
- Chasing Marts.
    
- Stealing Mart's tiny top hat.
    
- Dropping objects from the treehouse roof.
    
- Screeching immediately after Corin says "be quiet."
    
- Opening containers he absolutely should not be able to open.
    

---

# **THE HAIRCLIP INCIDENT**

Corin was sitting outside when Jay landed on her shoulder.

He immediately grabbed Guiding Light's blue-glowing hairclip from her bangs.

> **Corin:**  
> "Jay."

The bird flew away.

> **"Jay."**

He landed on the roof.

Corin stared at him.

> **"...Give it back."**

Jay dropped the hairclip.

It landed directly in the flowerpot.

Corin looked at the bird.

The bird looked at Corin.

> **"...You did that on purpose."**

Jay chirped.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK INCIDENT**

Corin once spent twenty minutes looking for her notebook.

She eventually discovered it inside Jay's preferred nesting area.

Several pages had tiny beak marks.

One corner had been torn off.

Corin stared at it.

> **"Why?"**

Jay chirped.

> **"...You can't even read."**

Jay chirped again.

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT'S INCIDENT**

Curious Light was working on a delicate device when Jay flew into the room.

Jay landed beside the equipment.

Curious Light immediately noticed.

> **"Don't touch that."**

Jay looked at him.

Curious Light continued working.

**Click.**

Jay had pressed something.

Curious Light stopped.

> **"...Why?"**

Jay flew away.

The device began making an alarming noise.

Curious Light stared after him.

> **"I understand why Corin complains."**

---

# **NEKO'S KEYCARD**

Jay once attempted to steal Neko's keycard.

He successfully grabbed it.

Neko stared at him.

Jay stared back.

Neko slowly reached toward him.

Jay flew away.

Neko followed.

Jay flew through a doorway.

Neko followed.

Jay flew through another doorway.

Neko stopped.

He looked at the access panel.

Then at the bird.

> **"...Did you just use my keycard to open that?"**

Jay chirped.

Neko sighed.

> **"...Of course you did."**

---

# **THE MART INCIDENT**

Jay has developed a strange rivalry with the Marts.

Nobody knows why.

He frequently steals their tiny top hats.

The Marts chase him.

Jay finds this hilarious.

The Marts do not.

One Mart eventually climbed onto Corin's shoulder and pointed angrily toward Jay.

Corin looked at it.

> **"...I'll talk to him."**

Jay chirped from the roof.

The Mart made an angry bubbling noise.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S RESPONSE**

Guiding Light has attempted to discipline Jay.

It has never worked.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Jay."

Jay looks at her.

> **"You shouldn't steal things."**

Jay chirps.

> **"You shouldn't bother Corin."**

Jay tilts his head.

> **"...Are you listening?"**

Jay flies onto her head.

Guiding Light sighs.

> **"...Apparently not."**

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin has become accustomed to him.

She complains constantly.

She also makes sure he has food.

One afternoon:

> **Corin:**  
> "You're annoying."

Jay hops onto her shoulder.

> **"You know that?"**

Jay chirps.

Corin scratches his head.

> **"...You're lucky you're cute."**

Jay chirps again.

> **"Don't push it."**

---

# **FINAL ASSESSMENT**

Jay is not anomalous.

He is not hostile.

He is not dangerous.

He is simply a blue jay with:

**Too much confidence.**

His status as Guiding Light's pet appears to have given him an unusual degree of social protection.

Nobody wants to punish him.

Nobody wants to scare him.

And Jay has figured this out.

His current behavioral assessment can therefore be summarized as:

> **"He knows exactly what he's doing."**

Guiding Light remains the official owner.

Corin remains the caretaker.

Jay remains a menace.

And somehow, everyone has grown fond of him anyway.

**End of Architectal Companion / Residential Care Log**
