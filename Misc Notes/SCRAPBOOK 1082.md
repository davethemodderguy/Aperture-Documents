# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### WELFARE & ANOMALOUS ENTITY MANAGEMENT DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-NERI-189  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — SAFEHOUSE / WELFARE  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE OBSERVATION  
**SUBJECT:** Neri Z-4C-NERI  
**SECONDARY SUBJECT:** Alice  
**FORM:** Plush / Chibi Humanoid  
**OBSERVATION:** Neri being treated as an ordinary plush toy by a six-year-old

---

# 1. SUMMARY

Alice is six years old.

Neri is a ten-year-old Seal Thiren who can voluntarily transform into a small, soft, chibi plush version of herself.

Alice has apparently decided that this means:

> **Neri is a plushie.**

Not metaphorically.

Not technically.

Not as a temporary nickname.

In Alice's hands, Neri is treated approximately the same way a six-year-old child would treat their favorite stuffed animal.

Neri does not mind.

In fact, she frequently appears to enjoy it.

---

# 2. STANDARD ALICE–NERI INTERACTION

When Alice finds Plush Neri, the following sequence is common:

1. Alice picks her up.
    
2. Alice hugs her.
    
3. Alice carries her around.
    
4. Alice puts her somewhere.
    
5. Alice forgets she put her there.
    
6. Alice remembers Neri exists.
    
7. Alice retrieves her.
    
8. Repeat.
    

Neri generally tolerates the entire cycle.

Sometimes she actively participates.

---

# 3. CARRYING

Alice carries Neri in essentially every way a small child might carry a plush toy.

Examples include:

- under one arm;
    
- against her chest;
    
- over one shoulder;
    
- held by the torso;
    
- sitting on Alice's shoulder;
    
- tucked underneath Alice's arm;
    
- carried upside down for several seconds;
    
- sitting on Alice's head;
    
- held against Alice's stomach while walking;
    
- placed on Alice's lap.
    

Neri rarely objects.

Her usual response is simply to adjust herself into a more comfortable position.

---

# 4. THE "BACKPACK"

Alice's preferred arrangement remains carrying Neri on her back.

Neri's grip is strong enough to hold onto Alice's clothing.

Alice therefore treats her approximately like a tiny living backpack.

Neri generally:

- climbs onto Alice;
    
- grips the hoodie;
    
- settles between her shoulders;
    
- rests her head against Alice;
    
- occasionally bites the fabric;
    
- remains there while Alice walks.
    

The arrangement is sufficiently common that several employees have stopped reacting to it.

---

# 5. PLAY

Alice occasionally plays with Neri.

Not dangerous play.

Childish play.

Neri has been observed being:

- placed inside blankets;
    
- wrapped up like a burrito;
    
- placed on pillows;
    
- tucked into a blanket nest;
    
- positioned beside stuffed animals;
    
- put into miniature beds;
    
- given pretend tea;
    
- "introduced" to other plush toys;
    
- carried into Alice's room;
    
- arranged in various sitting positions;
    
- given tiny accessories;
    
- placed under Alice's arm during television viewing.
    

Neri does not appear embarrassed.

She simply goes along with it.

---

# 6. THE PLUSH TOY COLLECTION

Alice has several ordinary plush toys.

Neri has occasionally been placed among them.

This produces an unusual problem.

From a distance, Neri is almost indistinguishable from the other toys.

Except for:

- her glasses;
    
- her ability to move;
    
- her ability to speak;
    
- her seal tail;
    
- her occasional biting;
    
- and the fact that she sometimes leaves the pile on her own.
    

One employee reported seeing Alice arranging several plush toys on her bed.

Neri was among them.

The employee watched Neri quietly turn her head toward the door.

Neri looked at the employee.

The employee looked back.

Neri then returned to the pile.

No assistance requested.

---

# 7. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri does not interpret this treatment as degrading.

This is significant.

Her normal self-perception is considerably harsher.

She has historically believed that her value depends upon:

- usefulness;
    
- productivity;
    
- intelligence;
    
- contribution;
    
- solving problems;
    
- being needed.
    

Alice's treatment contains none of these requirements.

Alice does not ask:

> "What can you do?"

She asks:

> "Can I carry you?"

She does not ask:

> "What did you accomplish today?"

She asks:

> "Do you want to watch TV?"

She does not care whether Neri is a world-class chemist.

She cares that Neri is soft.

---

# 8. INCIDENT — "TEA PARTY"

Alice once arranged a small tea party using several plush toys.

Neri was placed in the center.

Alice gave every plushie a cup.

Neri received one containing actual coffee.

Personnel later determined that Alice had not intentionally given Neri coffee.

She simply knew:

> "Neri likes coffee."

Neri drank it.

Alice then proceeded to hold a conversation with the plush toys.

Neri occasionally nodded.

When Alice asked:

> "Do you like the tea party?"

Neri quietly answered:

> "...Yes."

Alice smiled.

No further interaction was required.

---

# 9. INCIDENT — "BEDTIME"

Alice occasionally places Neri beside her when going to sleep.

Neri has historically been willing to remain there.

She may:

- lie beside Alice;
    
- sit against a pillow;
    
- curl into Alice's arm;
    
- sleep under the blanket;
    
- remain on the mattress;
    
- or climb onto Alice's chest.
    

Alice treats her exactly like a favorite stuffed animal.

Neri appears comfortable with this.

She has also been known to leave during the night if she wants coffee.

This has resulted in Alice waking up and asking:

> "Where did my plushie go?"

The answer is usually:

> **Cafeteria.**

---

# 10. THE FORGETTING PROBLEM

Unfortunately, Alice remains capable of forgetting Neri.

This includes forgetting:

- where she placed Neri;
    
- that she was carrying Neri;
    
- that Neri was sitting beside her;
    
- that Neri was included in whatever activity she was doing.
    

Neri no longer appears emotionally distressed by this in the manner previously documented.

She may simply remain where she was left.

If she can move, she eventually moves.

If she wants Alice again, she may approach her.

If she does not, she does not.

This represents a significant change from earlier periods of their relationship.

---

# 11. NERI'S AUTONOMY

Despite being treated like a plush toy, Neri retains complete control over whether she participates.

If she wants to leave:

She leaves.

If she wants to be carried:

She climbs onto Alice.

If she wants to sleep:

She sleeps.

If she wants coffee:

She finds coffee.

If she wants to bite Alice's sleeve:

She bites Alice's sleeve.

If she wants absolutely nothing:

She does absolutely nothing.

Alice generally accepts these decisions without question.

---

# 12. WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT FROM ACTUAL OBJECTIFICATION

C.O.R.E. has reviewed whether Alice's behavior creates an unacceptable reduction of Neri's personhood.

Current assessment:

**No.**

The defining factor is consent and Neri's ability to disengage.

Neri is not being forced to behave like a toy.

She is voluntarily participating in an interaction that requires her to do nothing except exist.

For someone whose self-worth has historically been tied to usefulness, this appears unusually comfortable.

---

# 13. OBSERVED EXCHANGE

**ALICE:**  
"Neri."

Neri looks up.

**ALICE:**  
"Can I carry you?"

Neri raises both arms.

Alice picks her up.

Alice hugs her.

Neri settles against Alice.

Several seconds pass.

**ALICE:**  
"You're my plushie."

Neri pauses.

She looks at Alice.

Then she nods.

Alice smiles.

Neri closes her eyes.

No correction was necessary.

---

# 14. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

There is something unusual about watching an exceptionally intelligent child being treated as though she is nothing more complicated than a stuffed animal.

Neri does not seem bothered.

She seems relieved.

For once, nobody needs her to solve anything.

Nobody needs her to understand anything.

Nobody needs her to be useful.

Alice can pick her up, carry her around, put her beside a pile of other plush toys, wrap her in a blanket, and tell her:

> "You're staying with me."

And Neri does not hear a demand.

She hears permission to stay.
