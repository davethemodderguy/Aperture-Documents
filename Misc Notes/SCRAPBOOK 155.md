# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **RECOVERY ROOM TRANSCRIPT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD & LUMI LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **CONTEXT**

The following conversation occurred several hours after Corin Lockwood regained consciousness following emergency Ether stabilization.

Medical personnel observed the exchange from outside the room.

No intervention was required.

---

# **TRANSCRIPT**

Corin slowly opened her eyes.

The room was quiet.

An IV line rested beside the bed.

Bandages covered much of her body.

Lumi sat in the chair next to her, exactly where she had been since the operation.

Corin stared at the ceiling for several minutes.

Neither spoke.

Finally, without looking toward her mother, Corin quietly asked:

> **Corin:** "Why won't you let me die?"

The words hung in the room.

Lumi closed her eyes.

She knew this question would come eventually.

She answered softly.

> **Lumi:** "Because Norma and Arcturus already lost me."

Corin remained silent.

Lumi continued.

> **"Do you really want them to lose another family member?"**

Corin's expression tightened.

She looked away.

> **Corin:** "...I'm tired."

Lumi nodded.

> "I know."

Another long silence.

> **Corin:** "...I'm so tired of remembering."

Lumi reached over and gently took her daughter's cold hand.

> "Then let us remember with you."

Corin's eyes filled with tears.

> "...You don't understand how heavy it is."

Lumi squeezed her hand gently.

> "Maybe not."

> "But you don't have to carry it by yourself."

Corin shook her head weakly.

> "...I was supposed to die."

Lumi looked directly into her daughter's eyes.

> "You were supposed to grow up."

> "You were supposed to come home from school."

> "You were supposed to complain when Norma stole your snacks."

> "You were supposed to argue with Arcturus over silly things."

> "You were supposed to become an accountant because you liked numbers."

Her voice faltered.

> "None of what happened to you was supposed to happen."

The room fell silent again.

Corin began crying.

Not loudly.

Just enough for the tears to run freely.

Lumi leaned forward and embraced her as carefully as the medical equipment allowed.

Corin did not resist.

For the first time since awakening...

She cried into her mother's shoulder.

---

# **MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

Psychological Services noted that Corin's question reflected profound hopelessness and exhaustion rather than a lack of affection for her family.

Lumi's response did not dismiss Corin's pain or argue that it was insignificant.

Instead, she reminded Corin that her life remained deeply meaningful to the people who loved her.

Medical staff considered the conversation an important step in helping Corin reconnect with those relationships while continuing intensive psychological treatment.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Following the conversation, Corin remained emotionally distressed but accepted continued medical care.

She did not ask the question again that evening.

Lumi stayed beside her until she finally fell asleep.

The first peaceful sleep Corin had experienced in many days.

**End of Recovery Room Transcript**
