# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / CRITICAL MEDICAL EVENT**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — CARDIAC ARREST**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Age:** Approximately 5 years old  
**Status:** **ALIVE / EXTREMELY CRITICAL**

---

# **THE EVENT**

Alice's condition suddenly deteriorated.

Her vital signs weakened.

Her breathing became shallow.

Her regenerative activity dropped sharply.

Then—

**Cardiac activity ceased.**

For a brief period, Alice was clinically dead.

The medical team initiated emergency resuscitation immediately.

The SAFEHOUSE was notified.

Corin arrived before the procedure was complete.

Belle followed shortly afterward.

Lumi and Orphie remained outside the treatment area.

Nobody spoke.

---

# **RETURN OF CARDIAC ACTIVITY**

Alice's heart eventually restarted.

Her breathing returned.

Her vital signs stabilized.

She survived.

However, she did not return to the condition she was in before the event.

She was noticeably weaker.

Her already-limited physical reserves had fallen further.

Her movements became slower.

Her ability to remain awake for extended periods decreased.

Her regenerative activity became weaker and less consistent.

The medical team classified her condition as:

> **"Post-arrest survival with significant physiological depletion."**

---

# **REGENERATION**

Alice's regeneration remains active.

But it is visibly diminished.

Previously, minor injuries would begin repairing almost immediately.

Now the process is slower.

Some injuries remain for extended periods.

Some wounds require conventional medical treatment before regeneration can meaningfully contribute.

The regenerative system appears to be conserving resources.

It is no longer behaving as though resources are plentiful.

Because they aren't.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin remained beside Alice after she regained consciousness.

Alice opened her eyes.

She looked at Corin.

For several seconds, she said nothing.

Then:

> **"Sorry."**

Corin immediately shook her head.

> **"Don't."**

Alice blinked.

> **"Alice made you worry."**

Corin held her hand.

> **"You don't have to apologize for being alive."**

Alice was silent.

Her eyes slowly closed again.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle broke down after being told Alice's heart had stopped.

She had already watched Alice become weaker for months.

She had watched the injuries accumulate.

She had watched Alice forget things.

She had watched the carrot memory disappear.

But seeing the words:

> **CARDIAC ARREST**

on the medical display was different.

When Belle was finally allowed inside, Alice was awake.

Barely.

Belle sat beside her.

She did not bring a carrot.

She knew Alice wouldn't remember why.

Instead, she simply held Alice's hand.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi remained unusually quiet.

She remembered Alice as a little girl who was always sick.

Weak.

Fragile.

But alive.

Now even being alive was becoming something Alice's body had to struggle to maintain.

Lumi whispered:

> **"She's still trying to spend everything she has on everyone else."**

Nobody disagreed.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie reviewed the emergency data personally.

The conclusion was unavoidable.

Alice had reached a point where her body could no longer reliably compensate for its own deficiencies.

Her regeneration had preserved her through countless injuries.

Her body had endured months of inadequate nutrition.

It had endured repeated trauma.

It had endured prolonged illness.

But there was a limit.

Alice had reached it.

# **POST-EVENT BEHAVIOR**

Alice has become quieter.

She sleeps for long periods.

When awake, she rarely moves without assistance.

Her eyes remain open for only short periods before exhaustion overtakes her.

She still tries to reassure everyone.

When someone asks:

> **"Are you okay?"**

Alice whispers:

> **"Alice okay."**

She isn't.

Everyone knows it.

Alice may not.

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

Alice flatlined.

For a brief moment, there was no heartbeat.

No pulse.

No circulation.

The body that had spent months refusing to give up finally reached the point where it could no longer continue on its own.

And then her heart started again.

Alice survived.

But survival came with a cost.

She returned weaker.

Her regeneration is weaker.

Her body is weaker.

Her already-fragmented reserves are smaller.

And yet, when she opened her eyes, the first thing she worried about wasn't herself.

It was everyone else.

> **"Alice made everyone scared?"**

Corin held her hand.

> **"Yes."**

Alice lowered her eyes.

> **"Sorry."**

Corin answered:

> **"Don't be."**

A pause.

> **"Just stay."**

Alice slowly closed her eyes.

For once, she didn't try to get up.

She didn't try to leave.

She simply stayed.

**End of CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / CRITICAL MEDICAL EVENT**
