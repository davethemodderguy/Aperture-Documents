# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL LOG**

## **SUBJECT: STORAGE ORIENTATION – "THE CLOSET"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following Corey's arrival at Project SAFEHOUSE and the subsequent reorganization of Corin's personal belongings, it was determined that both Corin and Corey should receive a complete orientation regarding SAFEHOUSE's storage facilities.

Traditionally, these tours are conducted by Neko.

Unfortunately, Neko was unavailable.

Official reason:

> "Busy."

No further explanation was provided.

Responsibility therefore fell to Endmin Merryweather.

---

# **THE CLOSET**

Endmin led Corin and Corey to the familiar wooden door beside the kitchen.

Corey looked around.

> "...This?"

Endmin nodded.

> "This."

Corin quietly opened the door.

Instead of shelves...

An elevator waited behind it.

Corey blinked.

She slowly looked back at the hallway.

Then back inside.

> "...That's cheating."

---

# **WELCOME**

The elevator descended.

Five seconds.

Ten seconds.

Twenty.

Corey looked toward Endmin.

> "...How deep is this thing?"

Endmin calmly answered.

> "Ten floors."

A pause.

> "...Currently."

---

# **FLOOR DIRECTORY**

A brass directory beside the elevator listed the contents.

**Floor 1**

Daily household supplies.

Cleaning equipment.

Kitchen overflow.

Laundry.

---

**Floor 2**

Emergency rations.

Seasonal ingredients.

Belle reportedly spends an unreasonable amount of time here.

---

**Floor 3**

Clothing.

Uniforms.

Winter gear.

Repair materials.

Corin immediately recognized several boxes containing her old SAFEHOUSE belongings.

---

**Floor 4**

Medical supplies.

Blankets.

Pillows.

Portable heaters.

Emergency shelter equipment.

---

**Floor 5**

Furniture storage.

Replacement appliances.

Decorations.

Holiday supplies.

---

**Floor 6**

Camping equipment.

Expedition supplies.

Snow survival equipment.

Taiga's oversized sleeping bags occupy an entire aisle.

---

**Floor 7**

Archived documents.

Historical SAFEHOUSE records.

Construction blueprints.

Previous renovation plans.

Access restricted.

---

**Floor 8**

Engineering overflow.

Spare electronics.

Mechanical components.

Several shelves bore warning signs reading:

> **PROPERTY OF NEKO**

Another shelf nearby read:

> **PROPERTY CONFISCATED FROM NEKO**

Miss Neko's handwriting was immediately recognizable.

---

**Floor 9**

Long-term storage.

Personal keepsakes.

Archived belongings belonging to current residents.

Access requires owner authorization.

---

**Floor 10**

Classified.

Corey looked toward Endmin.

> "...What's on Floor Ten?"

Endmin smiled politely.

> "...Need-to-know."

Corey sighed dramatically.

---

# **THE SCALE**

After nearly an hour of walking...

Corey finally stopped.

She looked down an apparently endless corridor.

Rows upon rows of organized shelving extended into the distance.

Elevated catwalks crossed overhead.

Autonomous storage robots quietly moved containers between aisles.

She slowly turned toward Endmin.

> "...You called this..."

She gestured around herself.

> "...A closet?"

Endmin nodded.

> "Yes."

Corey stared for several more seconds.

Then looked at Corin.

> "...Your definition of 'closet' is terrifying."

Corin simply shrugged.

> "...I got used to it."

# **RETURN**

As the tour concluded, Corey stepped back into the SAFEHOUSE hallway.

She looked at the ordinary wooden door.

Then looked at Endmin.

> "...If someone told me an entire ten-floor storage building was behind that door..."

Endmin waited.

Corey finished.

> "...I'd believe them now."

Endmin smiled.

> "Good."

> "Because that's exactly what it is."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin and Corey have completed SAFEHOUSE Storage Orientation.

Corey now understands why residents casually refer to a ten-floor extradimensional logistics facility as:

> **"The Closet."**

She still believes the name is misleading.

Everyone else insists it is perfectly accurate.

No formal vote has ever been held.
