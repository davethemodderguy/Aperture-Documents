**SUBJECT:** Notification of Former Family Fatalities

---

## Executive Summary

A question was raised regarding whether Neri should be informed that the family responsible for her childhood abandonment subsequently died in a residential fire approximately five years after abandoning her.

**Recommendation: Do not proactively inform Neri.**

The information provides no actionable benefit to Neri's current circumstances and is unlikely to meaningfully alter her relationship with her former family, her current life, or her ongoing recovery.

The family is already deceased. Their deaths cannot restore Neri's childhood, reverse their decision to abandon her, or provide her with anything she previously lacked.

---

## Background

Neri's former family abandoned her after determining that she was no longer considered sufficiently useful to them.

The abandonment occurred approximately five years before the destruction of the family residence.

A subsequent residential fire resulted in the deaths of the remaining family members present.

No evidence connects Neri to the incident.

The event appears to have been an unrelated tragedy.

---

## Assessment

### 1. The information changes nothing material.

Neri was already separated from her former family.

They cannot contact her.

They cannot apologize.

They cannot reclaim her.

They cannot explain their actions.

They cannot undo what happened.

Their deaths therefore do not materially change Neri's present circumstances.

### 2. The information may create unnecessary emotional burden.

Neri has demonstrated little interest in revisiting her former family's history.

Providing the information would potentially require her to process another significant event concerning people she has already spent years learning to live without.

There is no identified therapeutic, medical, residential, or operational requirement for her to know.

### 3. Neri does not owe them a reaction.

She does not need to grieve them.

She does not need to forgive them.

She does not need to be relieved.

She does not need to be angry.

She does not need to say anything.

She does not need to care.

There is no required emotional response.

### 4. Disclosure for the sake of disclosure is not justified.

The fact that information exists does not create an obligation to deliver it.

Neri's history contains enough information that cannot be changed.

Adding another irreversible fact simply because it is technically relevant to her past would serve no clear purpose.

---

## C.O.R.E. Determination

**Neri should not be proactively informed of the deaths of her former family.**

The information should remain archived and available should Neri independently ask about her former family or specifically request historical information concerning them.

If Neri eventually learns of the incident through another source, personnel should not attempt to manufacture a particular emotional response.

No statement equivalent to _"they got what they deserved"_ is appropriate.

No statement equivalent to _"you can finally move on"_ is appropriate.

No attempt should be made to turn the incident into closure.

---

## Final Note

The family that took Neri's childhood is gone.

That does not give Neri her childhood back.

It does not erase the abandonment.

It does not repair what was done to her.

And it does not require Neri to do anything about it.

**No action is recommended.**
