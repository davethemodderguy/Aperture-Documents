## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL ANOMALOUS PSYCHOLOGICAL / ABILITY UPDATE**

# **LUNA — PERCEIVED THREAT RESPONSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Luna  
**Associated Individual:** Corey / Solaria  
**Status:** Non-hostile — Psychological Disturbance Potential

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Further observation has confirmed that **Luna can unintentionally frighten personnel** despite having no intention of harming them.

Her slightly obsessive and unconventional personality, combined with her anomalous abilities, can create an unusually strong impression of danger.

The most frequently reported trigger is her smile.

---

# **THE LUNA SMILE**

Luna does not always smile when she is happy.

Her expressions can be difficult for personnel to interpret.

When Luna becomes particularly interested in something, she may develop a quiet smile while staring at the subject of her attention.

To Luna, this may simply mean:

> **"That's interesting."**

To everyone else, the interpretation can be considerably different.

Personnel have compared the expression to the emotional effect of receiving a death threat.

One security officer described it as:

> "The kind of smile that makes you wonder whether you should start running."

No actual threat had been made.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL EFFECT**

Luna possesses a confirmed anomalous ability to impose:

> **An overwhelming sense of incoming danger.**

The affected individual may experience the sudden and powerful certainty that something extremely dangerous is about to happen.

Reported sensations include:

- Extreme alertness.
    
- Sudden dread.
    
- Feeling watched.
    
- Expectation of imminent danger.
    
- Urge to flee.
    
- Heightened awareness of surroundings.
    

The sensation can occur even when no actual threat exists.

---

# **INTENT**

C.O.R.E. emphasizes:

> **Luna does not need to intend harm for this effect to occur.**

The ability appears to be partially connected to her emotional presence and anomalous nature.

Luna may simply be standing nearby.

She may be curious.

She may be thinking.

She may even be smiling because she has just remembered something amusing.

Personnel can nevertheless experience the sensation that:

> **Something terrible is approaching.**

---

# **SOLARIA'S STATEMENT**

Solaria was asked about Luna's tendency to frighten people.

Her response:

> "...Yeah."

She paused.

> "...She doesn't mean to."

When asked why Luna can appear so threatening, Solaria replied:

> "...Luna thinks too much."

Another pause.

> "...And sometimes she smiles while doing it."

---

# **LUNA'S RESPONSE**

Luna was informed that her smile frightened several personnel.

She appeared genuinely confused.

> "...My smile?"

The researcher confirmed this.

Luna thought about it.

Then smiled again.

The researcher immediately became uncomfortable.

Luna stopped.

> "...Oh."

She appeared to understand.

> "...Sorry."

---

# **CONTROL TEST**

A controlled test was performed with consenting personnel.

Luna was instructed to stand several meters away without speaking.

After approximately thirty seconds, the subject reported no unusual sensation.

Luna was then asked to smile.

The subject immediately reported:

> "I feel like something is coming."

No physical threat was present.

Luna was instructed to stop.

The sensation disappeared shortly afterward.

---

# **SECOND TEST**

Luna was asked to deliberately produce the sensation of incoming danger.

She did so without moving.

The subject reported an immediate and overwhelming feeling that:

> "Something is about to happen."

The subject repeatedly checked the doors and surrounding area despite knowing the test conditions.

Luna then released the effect.

The feeling vanished.

---

# **IMPORTANT DISTINCTION**

The phenomenon does **not** necessarily predict an actual threat.

It is an imposed perception.

The affected person's instincts effectively receive the message:

> **DANGER.**

Even when reality says:

> **Nothing is happening.**

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

Marts do not appear frightened by Luna's presence.

They can remain near her while the effect is active.

One Mart was observed sitting directly beside Luna while a test subject experienced the overwhelming danger sensation.

The Mart simply looked at the researcher.

> "...Luna."

The researcher:

> "Yes."

The Mart:

> "...Nice."

---

# **SAFEHOUSE GUIDANCE**

Personnel encountering Luna should not automatically assume that her expression or presence indicates hostile intent.

If Luna is smiling strangely:

**Ask what she is thinking.**

If an overwhelming sense of danger suddenly occurs around her:

**Remain calm and determine whether Luna is intentionally using the ability.**

If Luna says:

> "...I'm not doing anything."

Believe her.

She probably isn't.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Luna is capable of being frightening without being dangerous.

Her obsessive curiosity can produce unsettling expressions.

Her smile can make trained personnel nervous.

Her anomalous presence can impose the instinctive certainty that something terrible is coming.

And yet, in most cases, Luna is simply:

> **Thinking.**

Solaria summarized the phenomenon best:

> "...She's not threatening you."

> "...She just looks like she might."
