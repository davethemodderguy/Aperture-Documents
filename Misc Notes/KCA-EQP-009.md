# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **KCA-EQP-009**

### **TACTICAL EQUIPMENT DOSSIER – CINDER'S MULTI-PURPOSE BRIEFCASE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Restricted Equipment

---

## **SUMMARY**

The **Multi-Purpose Tactical Briefcase** assigned to Executive Secretary **Cinder** is a modular weapon platform developed for executive security personnel who require both concealment and versatility.

While outwardly resembling a reinforced executive briefcase, the system can rapidly reconfigure into multiple combat configurations depending on operational requirements.

---

# **WEAPON CONFIGURATIONS**

## **1. Compact Submachine Gun**

**Purpose:**  
General close-quarters defense.

Features:

- High rate of fire.
    
- Compact profile.
    
- Optimized for protecting the Director during evacuations.
    
- Standard ballistic ammunition.
    

---

## **2. Smart Explosive Ordnance Launcher (SEOL)**

**Purpose:**  
Counter-air and precision engagement.

Unlike the rocket launcher configuration, the SEOL launches **guided homing rockets**.

Features:

- Lock-on targeting.
    
- Tracks moving aerial targets.
    
- Effective against:
    
    - Drones.
        
    - Flying vehicles.
        
    - Low-altitude aircraft.
        
- Reduces collateral damage through guided flight.
    

The launcher is intended for situations where a direct-fire rocket would have a low probability of hitting a maneuvering target.

---

## **3. Rocket Launcher**

**Purpose:**  
Breaching and emergency escape.

This configuration fires rockets fitted with:

- High-explosive warheads.
    
- Volatile Ether compounds.
    

Rather than serving primarily as an anti-vehicle weapon, these rockets are commonly employed to:

- Breach fortified walls.
    
- Collapse unstable barriers.
    
- Create emergency escape routes.
    
- Open tunnels through obstructed structures.
    

Operational doctrine emphasizes using this mode to **create a path**, not simply destroy a target.

---

## **4. Reinforced Briefcase**

Even without deploying its internal weapon systems, the closed briefcase remains a formidable impact weapon.

Constructed from reinforced alloys, it can withstand substantial abuse and is capable of delivering heavy blunt-force strikes.

Personnel are reminded that appearances can be misleading.

---

# **SECONDARY EQUIPMENT**

## **Combat Gauntlets**

Should the briefcase become unavailable, damaged, or otherwise unusable, Cinder transitions to a pair of reinforced combat gauntlets.

Unlike Keith's disciplined defensive style or Belle's straightforward power, Cinder adopts a highly pragmatic approach.

She prioritizes ending confrontations quickly using any lawful advantage available.

Training evaluations consistently describe her style as:

- Opportunistic.
    
- Fast.
    
- Unpredictable.
    
- Difficult to counter at close range.

A reinforced training dummy was positioned approximately fifty meters away.

Cinder stood calmly with her briefcase.

### **SEOL Demonstration**

The briefcase transformed into the Smart Explosive Ordnance Launcher.

A small aerial drone was released above the range.

Cinder acquired the target.

> **"Lock confirmed."

She fired.

The rocket climbed sharply before curving through the air.

Despite the drone changing direction several times, the munition adjusted its flight path and struck it directly.

The drone disintegrated into fragments.

Mart looked upward.

> **"...That thing chased it."

Neko quietly nodded.

> **"...Guidance package."

---

### **Rocket Launcher Demonstration**

Next, engineers sealed a mock concrete passage.

Cinder transformed the weapon again.

> **"Breaching."

The rocket struck the wall.

A controlled explosion followed, assisted by volatile Ether within the warhead.

When the dust settled...

A clear tunnel had been blasted through the obstruction.

Belle smiled.

> **"...Shortcut."

---

### **Gauntlet Demonstration**

For the final exercise, Cinder placed the briefcase aside.

She fastened a pair of reinforced gauntlets over her hands.

Director Lucious activated several training drones programmed for close-quarters engagement.

The first rushed toward her.

Rather than meeting it head-on, Cinder sidestepped and struck its side, sending it off balance.

A second drone attempted to grab her from behind.

She ducked, drove an elbow into its center of mass, and swept its legs out from under it.

The third lunged forward.

Cinder redirected its momentum into another drone before finishing both with rapid strikes from her gauntlets.

Within moments, every training drone lay disabled.

The demonstration highlighted that, when deprived of her primary equipment, Cinder relied on speed, positioning, and exploiting openings rather than brute strength.
