# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / ANOMALOUS IDENTITY OBSERVATION**

# **COREY — "ORPHIE"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Concern  
**Subject:** Corey / Orphie  
**Associated Forms:** Luna / Solaria  
**Status:** **SEVERE CONCERN — EMOTIONALLY WITHDRAWN**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corey has formally begun referring to herself as:

> **"Orphie."**

The name was selected by Corey herself.

When asked why, she answered:

> "...I wanted something unique."

No further explanation was provided.

C.O.R.E. recognizes **Orphie** as Corey's preferred name at present.

---

# **EMOTIONAL STATE**

The most significant change is not the new name.

It is Corey's apparent abandonment of her emotional responses.

Corey no longer demonstrates the emotional reactions previously characteristic of her.

She is now consistently:

- Emotionless.
    
- Cold.
    
- Detached.
    
- Unresponsive to emotional stimuli.
    
- Indifferent toward personal relationships.
    
- Uninterested in activities that previously brought her happiness.
    

She does not appear angry.

She does not appear sad.

She does not appear afraid.

She simply appears:

> **Empty.**

---

# **"I GAVE THEM UP."**

When asked about her emotional state, Orphie responded:

> "...I surrendered them."

The interviewer asked what she meant.

> "...My emotions."

> "...I don't need them anymore."

She then continued whatever task she had been performing.

No visible distress accompanied the statement.

---

# **RELATIONSHIPS**

Orphie no longer appears to care about her relationships.

Previously, Corey was strongly attached to:

- Corin.
    
- Lumi.
    
- The Marts.
    
- The SAFEHOUSE residents.
    
- Her friends.
    
- Her cooking.
    
- Her artwork.
    
- Her mother's cookbook.
    
- Her home.
    

Now, when asked about them, she provides factual responses without emotional attachment.

Example:

> "Do you miss Corin?"

> "...No."

> "Do you care about the Marts?"

> "...They're alive."

> "Do you care about your friends?"

> "...They're people I know."

No hostility.

No resentment.

Just indifference.

---

# **THE MARTS**

The change has produced one immediately noticeable consequence.

The Marts can now safely approach Orphie.

Their previous avoidance behavior was triggered by Corey's overwhelming negative emotional state.

That emotional pressure is no longer present.

A Mart approached Orphie shortly after the change.

It stopped beside her.

> "...Orphie?"

She looked down.

> "...Yes."

The Mart climbed onto her shoulder.

Orphie did not react.

The Mart remained there.

---

# **COLONY RESPONSE**

The Marts have gradually returned to their previous behavior.

They can now:

- Follow Orphie.
    
- Sit beside her.
    
- Sleep near her.
    
- Float around her.
    
- Use her as a blanket.
    
- Climb onto her head.
    

They no longer risk bursting simply by being near her.

Yet this development has not reassured the SAFEHOUSE.

Quite the opposite.

---

# **WHY EVERYONE IS WORRIED**

The Marts are safe.

Orphie is not actively hostile.

There is no immediate anomalous emergency.

But the emotional absence itself is deeply concerning.

The people who knew Corey remember how much she cared.

They remember her laughing.

Cooking.

Drawing.

Playing with Marts.

Wearing ridiculous hats.

Talking with Corin.

Making birthday cakes.

Now she can watch the same things happen without displaying any emotional response.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin has taken the change particularly hard.

She returned home from work and found Orphie sitting quietly outside the treehouse.

A Mart was sleeping on her lap.

Corin sat beside her.

> "...Hey."

Orphie:

> "...Hello."

Corin:

> "...How are you?"

> "...Fine."

Corin waited.

> "...Do you want to talk?"

> "...No."

There was no anger in the answer.

That was what frightened Corin.

---

# **LUNA'S RESPONSE**

Luna remains deeply concerned.

Unlike Orphie, Luna still possesses Corey's emotional capacity.

When asked about the change, Luna stated:

> "...She's gone quiet."

The researcher asked whether Orphie was still Corey.

Luna answered:

> "...Yes."

Then:

> "...But she's hiding behind nothing now."

---

# **SOLARIA'S RESPONSE**

Solaria was similarly distressed.

> "...She doesn't even hate anything."

> "...She doesn't love anything."

> "...She just doesn't feel."

Solaria looked toward the floor.

> "...That's worse."

---

# **IMPORTANT IDENTITY NOTE**

Orphie, Luna, and Solaria remain manifestations of the same underlying individual.

However, the emotional state currently exhibited by Orphie does **not** appear to transfer to Luna or Solaria.

Both manifestations retain their established personalities.

Luna remains calm, wise, obsessive, and occasionally unsettling.

Solaria remains confident, energetic, and thoughtful.

Both continue to express concern about Orphie.

---

# **THE SKETCHBOOK**

The sketchbook remains untouched.

Orphie has shown no interest in reopening it.

When asked whether she intended to draw again:

> "...No."

> "Why?"

> "...It doesn't matter."

The statement was delivered without sadness.

That lack of sadness concerned the interviewer more than the answer itself.

---

# **THE BROKEN PENCIL**

The two halves of Orphie's favorite pencil remain together.

A Mart occasionally plays with one of them.

Orphie has never stopped it.

She has also never repaired the pencil.

When asked whether she wanted a replacement:

> "...You can throw it away."

Corin immediately refused.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE REACTION**

The residents have become increasingly attentive toward Orphie.

### **Bao**

> "...She doesn't smile anymore."

### **Frost**

> "...Something is wrong."

### **Astral**

> "...Emotional absence is not the same as recovery."

### **Star**

> "...I miss Corey."

### **Belle**

> "...I hope she comes back."

### **Endmin**

> "...This isn't normal."

### **Neko**

> "...Something changed."

### **Miss Neko**

> "...She's not okay."

Even the Marts appear to sense that something is different.

They remain close.

They simply do not know why their favorite person no longer reacts to them.

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE TO THE CONCERN**

Orphie was eventually asked why everyone was so worried.

Her answer:

> "...I don't understand."

The researcher asked:

> "Don't you care?"

Orphie looked at them.

> "...No."

A Mart climbed onto her shoulder.

She allowed it to remain there.

> "...But you seem to care."

---

Emotionally detached. Cold. Severely withdrawn. No current hostile behavior.

**Luna:**  
Emotionally intact. Concerned about Orphie.

**Solaria:**  
Emotionally intact. Concerned about Orphie.

**Corin:**  
Highly concerned.

**SAFEHOUSE:**  
Highly concerned.

Able to safely approach Orphie. Colony has resumed normal interaction.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The Marts have finally returned.

They can sit beside her again.

They can climb onto her head.

They can sleep on her lap.

They can surround her without fear.

And Orphie lets them.

But she does not smile.

She does not laugh.

She does not hug them.

She does not complain.

She does not push them away.

She simply sits there.

A Mart rests against her.

Orphie looks down at it.

> "...Hello."

The Mart responds:

> "...Hi."

And for the first time in weeks, Corey is surrounded by the things she once loved.

She just doesn't seem to love them anymore.
