# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **CONFIDENTIAL PSYCHOLOGICAL CASE REVIEW**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – PRIMARY EMOTIONAL ATTACHMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical Director & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **OVERVIEW**

Recent evaluations indicate a significant shift in Corin Lockwood's emotional support structure.

Although she continues to maintain cordial relationships with her siblings, colleagues, and SAFEHOUSE residents, she has increasingly withdrawn from them on an emotional level.

She answers questions.

She performs her duties.

She thanks people for their concern.

She rarely allows anyone to see what she is truly feeling.

---

## **PRIMARY ATTACHMENT**

During her latest counseling session, Corin was asked a simple question.

> "Who do you feel closest to?"

She remained silent for nearly a minute.

Then she answered.

> "...Someone you can't meet."

The counselor asked if she meant Belle.

Corin shook her head.

Norma?

Another shake.

Arcturus?

Again...

No.

After another pause, Corin quietly rested a hand over her chest.

> "...The one who stayed with me when nobody else could."

The attending clinician noted that Corin presently experiences the maternal Ether resonance as her closest emotional companion.

This reflects the depth of comfort she associates with that presence, particularly during periods of profound distress.

At the same time, the clinical team emphasized that recovery should continue to strengthen Corin's connections with the people around her rather than rely exclusively on any single source of comfort.

---

## **DISTANCE**

The change has been subtle.

Belle still hugs Corin.

Corin hugs her back.

But only for a moment.

Norma still brings tea.

Corin drinks it.

Then quietly returns to work.

Arcturus still checks on his youngest sister.

She always smiles.

She always says she is fine.

He has learned that those words often mean only that she does not wish to worry him.

No arguments occur.

No resentment exists.

Only distance.

A distance Corin created herself.

---

## **COUNSELOR'S NOTE**

The psychological team believes this withdrawal is rooted in fear rather than rejection.

Corin explained it herself.

> "...If I let people get too close..."

She struggled to continue.

> "...I'm afraid they'll watch me fall apart."

The counselor documented that Corin's instinct is to protect others from witnessing her suffering, even when that means carrying it alone.

---

## **THE ETHER RESONANCE**

During one reported conversation, the familiar voice spoke gently.

> **"Belle misses you."**

Corin quietly nodded.

> "...I know."

> **"Norma worries about you."**

Another nod.

> "...I know."

> **"Arcturus checks on you every day."**

Corin looked toward the office door.

> "...I know."

The voice was silent for a long moment.

Then it asked:

> **"Do you think they care because they have to..."**

> **"...or because they love you?"**

Corin had no answer.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the assessment before adding a final observation.

> "There is nothing wrong with finding comfort in hope, memory, or a gentle voice."

> "But no one should have to carry an entire life with only one place to lean."

He underlined the final sentence.

> "Continue helping Corin rebuild trust in the people who have never stopped waiting for her."

---

## **STATUS**

Corin remains emotionally withdrawn despite modest improvements in her day-to-day functioning.

Her treatment team considers rebuilding safe, trusting relationships with her family and colleagues to be an important long-term goal, while respecting the comfort she finds in the maternal Ether resonance.

Those closest to her have noticed the distance.

None of them have stopped leaving a place for her at the table.

They hope that, in time, Corin will no longer feel she has to choose between the comforting voice in her mind and the people waiting beside her.

**End of Confidential Psychological Case Review**
