# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **Z-335 BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION**

# **PERSISTENT COMPANION PHENOMENON — COREY**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel  
**Subjects:** Corey, Z-335 (Marts)

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Researchers have confirmed an unusual long-term behavioral pattern exhibited by the Z-335 colony.

Regardless of the time, location, or activity, there is **always one Mart accompanying Corey.**

This is not an official assignment.

No personnel member, including Lumi, has instructed the colony to do so.

Instead, the Marts appear to rotate the responsibility among themselves.

The colony simply refers to it as:

> **"...Corey's turn."**

---

# **ROTATION**

The companion changes regularly.

One Mart may remain with Corey for several hours before quietly departing.

Within moments, another Mart will arrive.

The transition is seamless.

Researchers have never documented a moment in which Corey was left entirely without a Mart nearby.

The rotations occur without any visible communication.

The colony's hivemind is presumed to coordinate them.

---

# **COMMON DUTIES**

The Mart accompanying Corey has no formal responsibilities.

Instead, it naturally performs small acts of companionship, including:

- Walking beside her.
    
- Sitting on her desk while she works.
    
- Helping carry light objects.
    
- Offering warmth or cooling by adjusting its temperature.
    
- Sleeping nearby during breaks.
    
- Quietly listening when Corey talks.
    
- Acting as an emergency "hat" if she happens not to be wearing one.
    

None of these behaviors have ever been requested.

---

# **WORKPLACE OBSERVATIONS**

Within the Research Laboratories and Engineering Division, it has become routine to see one Mart following Corey between workstations.

Personnel have stopped asking whose Mart it is.

The answer is always the same.

> "...Corey's."

Strictly speaking, this is inaccurate.

The Mart belongs to the colony.

It has merely decided that it is Corey's companion for the day.

---

# **TREEHOUSE**

The rotation continues after working hours.

When Corey returns home, the accompanying Mart usually remains until another arrives from the local treehouse group.

The exchange is almost ceremonial.

One Mart leaves.

Another immediately takes its place.

Neither speaks.

Both appear satisfied.

---

# **CORIN'S COMMENT**

Corin once tried to count how many different Marts accompanied Corey over the course of a week.

She lost track before lunchtime on the first day.

> "...They're changing shifts."

---

# **NEKO'S COMMENT**

Neko looked up from a workbench as one Mart quietly replaced another beside Corey.

She watched the exchange.

> "...Shift change."

Then returned to soldering.

---

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

Lumi was asked if she had instructed the colony to look after Corey.

She shook her head.

> "...No."

She smiled softly.

> "...They decided that themselves."

---

# **COREY'S COMMENT**

When asked whether having a constant Mart companion bothered her, Corey looked down at the tiny blue blob sitting on her sleeve.

It looked back.

She smiled.

> "...Not anymore."

She gently patted it around the sides of its head, carefully avoiding the top hat.

> "...It's... comforting."

The Mart happily replied:

> "...Here."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Researchers attempted one final experiment.

They discreetly encouraged the Mart accompanying Corey to leave for routine observations elsewhere.

It politely nodded.

Then floated away.

For exactly **three seconds.**

Another Mart immediately rounded the corner and settled beside Corey as if nothing had happened.

The observing researcher sighed and closed the notebook.

A note was added to the file:

> **"There is always a Mart assigned to Corey."**

The word **assigned** remains in quotation marks.

Because no one ever assigned them.

They simply chose.

**End of Behavioral Observation**
