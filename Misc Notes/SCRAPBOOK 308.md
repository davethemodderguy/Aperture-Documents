# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **SECURITY DIVISION – TACTICAL OBSERVATION REPORT**

### **SUBJECT: KCA MART COLONY – ENVIRONMENTAL FAMILIARITY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Security Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Routine inspection of the Krampus Compliance Authority's maintenance systems has revealed that the resident Mart colony possesses an extraordinarily detailed understanding of the building's internal layout.

This knowledge was not taught.

The Marts simply... learned it.

Over time, they have collectively memorized nearly every publicly accessible area, service corridor, maintenance tunnel, and ventilation shaft within KCA Headquarters.

---

# **VENTILATION NETWORK**

Maintenance personnel initially became concerned after repeatedly discovering tiny top hats inside ventilation ducts.

Subsequent inspections confirmed that Marts routinely travel through the ventilation system, not as a means of avoiding personnel, but because it allows them to move efficiently throughout the building.

The colony has effectively created its own mental map of the entire facility.

Tests indicate that any Mart can navigate from Reception to the Vault using only the ventilation system without making a single wrong turn.

---

# **COLLECTIVE PLANNING**

Analysis of previous robbery attempts has revealed that the colony does not respond randomly.

Instead, the Ether-linked hivemind rapidly develops defensive plans based on the unfolding situation.

Observed behaviors include:

- Positioning observers at key intersections.
    
- Quietly relocating Marts through ventilation ducts before suspects arrive.
    
- Blocking escape routes.
    
- Guiding civilians away from danger.
    
- Coordinating with "Camera Mart" in the Security Control Room.
    
- Waiting until suspects are committed before revealing their presence.
    

Researchers emphasize that these behaviors appear entirely defensive.

The colony has never staged an ambush outside of an active threat.

---

# **TACTICAL RESTRAINT**

Despite their growing sophistication, the Marts continue to follow an unwritten set of principles:

- Protect civilians first.
    
- Prevent unnecessary injuries.
    
- Disarm before incapacitating.
    
- Use only the force required to stop the threat.
    
- Assist the injured once the danger has passed.
    

Security psychologists note that this consistency has remained unchanged despite the colony's increasing intelligence.

---

# **SIMULATION EXERCISES**

With approval from Security Division, several training exercises were conducted using volunteer KCA officers acting as mock intruders.

The Marts were not informed that the scenarios were simulations.

Results:

- The volunteers were "captured" in every exercise.
    
- Average time to interception: **18 seconds**.
    
- Average number of Marts involved: **42**.
    
- No volunteer sustained more than minor bruising.
    

After learning the exercises were staged, the Marts collectively apologized to every participant.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The KCA Mart colony continues to evolve its defensive coordination while maintaining its fundamentally peaceful nature.

Their growing familiarity with the building has transformed them into an unofficial early-warning system, often identifying unusual activity before human personnel notice it.

Security Division has one standing recommendation for anyone considering committing a crime inside KCA Headquarters:

Do not assume that an empty hallway is actually empty.

The walls, ceilings, and ventilation shafts may already be occupied by several dozen tiny blue observers...

Quietly waiting to decide whether you're a friend.

Or not.
