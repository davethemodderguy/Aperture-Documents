# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### SUBJECT: ALICE — POST-RECOVERY BEHAVIORAL & EQUIPMENT PROFILE

**Classification:** C.O.R.E. Internal
**Status:** ACTIVE
**Subject:** Alice
**Age:** 6 Years
**Designation:** Anomalous Juvenile / SPAWN Counter-Threat

---

## Executive Summary

Following Alice's recovery from the extended period of disappearance and exposure to extreme environmental conditions, C.O.R.E. personnel initially observed a substantial reversal of the physical weakness previously associated with the subject.

Alice is no longer physically frail.

She is capable of sustained movement, combat activity, and handling of anomalous equipment previously considered entirely unsuitable for an individual of her size.

However, **Alice's emotional and developmental state has not undergone a comparable change.**

Alice remains a six-year-old child.

She is still quiet.

She is still gentle.

She still becomes embarrassed when receiving excessive attention.

She still asks for permission before taking things.

She still worries about bothering personnel.

She still enjoys simple things.

She still behaves like a little girl.

The primary difference is that Alice is no longer incapable of defending herself.

---

# I. CURRENT BEHAVIOR

Alice's recovery should not be interpreted as a psychological maturation event.

Personnel are specifically instructed **not to treat Alice as an adult combatant simply because she possesses exceptional physical capability.**

Outside of direct threats, Alice's behavior remains remarkably mundane.

She may sit quietly in a corner while drawing.

She may follow personnel she trusts without saying anything.

She continues to display strong attachment toward individuals she considers family.

When offered food, she may quietly accept it rather than request it herself.

When asked whether she needs something, common responses include:

> "...I'm okay."

or:

> "...Don't need it."

even when she demonstrably does.

The previously documented belief that her existence inconveniences others has improved but has **not completely disappeared.**

Personnel should continue reinforcing the concept that receiving care does not constitute causing trouble.

---

# II. THE PENCIL

Alice's anomalous weapon remains disguised as an ordinary pencil.

The object is typically kept **clipped directly onto the front or side of Alice's small coat.**

It is not stored in an armory.

It is not carried in a weapons case.

It is not secured to Alice with restraints.

It simply remains there.

A small pencil.

Almost comically small compared to the weapon it represents.

### Designation

**ALICE-WPN-01 — "THE PENCIL"**

### Default Appearance

* Standard pencil-like object
* Small enough for Alice to comfortably carry
* Attached to Alice's coat using its clip
* No obvious indication of its true nature
* Remains accessible to Alice at all times

### True Form

A massive battle axe capable of being wielded by Alice despite its disproportionate size.

The weapon has demonstrated sufficient cutting capability to completely penetrate a **1-meter Nekonium pillar** in a single strike.

Nekonium is currently regarded as the strongest metal engineered by Neko.

The successful penetration of the test specimen places ALICE-WPN-01's destructive capability substantially beyond conventional material tolerances.

---

# III. NORMALIZATION OF THE WEAPON

Of particular interest to C.O.R.E. personnel is Alice's apparent lack of concern regarding the weapon.

She does not carry it around openly.

She does not show it off.

She does not treat it as a symbol of power.

She simply keeps the pencil clipped to her coat.

When asked why she keeps it there, Alice reportedly looked down at the pencil before answering:

> "...So I don't lose it."

No further explanation was provided.

This behavior is considered consistent with Alice's established personality.

To Alice, the object is not necessarily "a weapon."

It is simply **her pencil.**

---

# IV. CHILDLIKE BEHAVIOR DURING ANOMALOUS EVENTS

Despite her demonstrated combat capability, Alice may immediately return to ordinary childlike behavior following a confrontation.

Observed or anticipated examples include:

* Asking whether she is allowed to keep something she found.
* Becoming distracted by drawings.
* Quietly following a trusted adult.
* Falling asleep after prolonged activity.
* Asking for a snack.
* Wanting someone to carry her.
* Becoming upset if someone damages something she considers important.
* Quietly apologizing after accidentally destroying something.
* Attempting to help injured personnel.
* Treating powerful anomalous entities as simply people she knows.

This contrast has caused repeated confusion among newly assigned personnel.

It is therefore emphasized:

**Alice being capable of killing a hostile entity does not mean Alice has stopped being a child.**

---

# V. RESPONSE TO SPAWN

Alice's attitude toward SPAWN represents a significant departure from her previous passive behavior.

She knows what SPAWN is.

She recognizes their symbol.

She recognizes their presence.

And, unlike before, she does not immediately attempt to flee.

The subject's previous response to the SPAWN symbol was characterized by severe distress and avoidance.

Her current response is considerably more controlled.

Alice is still afraid.

She simply refuses to allow that fear to control her actions.

Her weapon remains available should SPAWN threaten herself or individuals under her protection.

This behavior is believed to be connected to the previously documented statement:

> "...But I'm not running anymore."

---

# VI. C.O.R.E. HANDLING GUIDANCE

Alice is **not** to be incorporated into standard perimeter security operations.

She is not to be issued conventional combat orders.

She is not to be deployed solely because of her effectiveness against SPAWN.

She is six years old.

The fact that she happens to possess an anomalous battle axe capable of cutting through Nekonium does not change that.

Personnel encountering Alice outside of an active emergency are expected to treat her as a child first.

The pencil should remain clipped to her coat unless Alice herself chooses otherwise.

Attempts to confiscate ALICE-WPN-01 without immediate cause are discouraged and require C.O.R.E. authorization.

---

# VII. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Alice's recovery has produced an unusual combination of characteristics.

Physically, she is stronger.

Anomalously, she is considerably more dangerous.

Psychologically, she remains vulnerable.

Developmentally, she remains a child.

Emotionally, she is still learning that she is allowed to exist without apologizing for it.

Her ability to defend herself should therefore never be mistaken for independence from those around her.

Alice does not need to become less of a child to become stronger.

She has simply learned that she does not have to remain helpless.

And every day, she continues walking through the Blacksite with a tiny pencil clipped to her little coat.

Nobody unfamiliar with her would suspect what it can become.

**C.O.R.E. Assessment:****

> *"Do not mistake the axe for the girl.*
>
> *The axe is dangerous.*
>
> *Alice is still Alice."*
