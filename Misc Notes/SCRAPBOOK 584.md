# **CORIN — SILENT FELINE FORM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Condition:** Mercury Poisoning / Severe Weakness  
**Communication:** **Nonverbal**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's condition has progressed to the point where communication with her has become extremely difficult.

She can no longer meow.

Her miniature feline form previously allowed her to communicate through small vocalizations.

Those sounds have now disappeared.

Corin remains conscious and aware, but she has almost no conventional method of expressing what she wants.

---

# **UNDERSTANDING CORIN**

Understanding Corin has always been difficult.

In her miniature form, she cannot speak normally.

Previously, her meows, body language, ears, tails, and wings provided enough information for people familiar with her to understand her.

Now even her meows are gone.

Her communication has been reduced almost entirely to:

- Eye movement.
    
- Ear position.
    
- Tail movement.
    
- Wing movement.
    
- Body posture.
    
- Attempted gestures.
    
- Looking toward objects.
    
- Physical contact.
    

Even experienced SAFEHOUSE residents can misunderstand her.

---

# **ARCHITECT ASSISTANCE**

The Architects are currently the only reliable means of directly interpreting Corin's thoughts.

Guiding Light can establish a limited Ether-mediated connection with Corin and determine what she is attempting to communicate.

This does not mean the Architect speaks **for** Corin.

Rather, it allows the observer to perceive the intended meaning behind Corin's otherwise extremely limited signals.

For example, Corin may stare at her cup.

To most people:

> **"She is looking at the cup."**

To an Architect-assisted observer:

> **"She wants water."**

---

# **SAFEHOUSE INTERPRETATION**

The residents have developed their own system.

### **Cup**

Looking repeatedly toward the cup generally means she wants to drink.

### **Food**

Looking toward her food container generally indicates hunger.

### **Wings Wrapped Tightly**

Usually means Corin wants warmth, privacy, or comfort.

### **Light Nibble**

Usually means:

> **"I don't like that."**

### **Relaxed Wings**

Usually indicates that she is comfortable.

### **Slow Blinking**

Generally interpreted as a sign of calmness.

These interpretations are useful, but not guaranteed.

---

# **THE PROBLEM**

Corin's weakened condition makes even her body language less obvious.

She may stare at something without having enough strength to move toward it.

She may want to be picked up but lack the energy to reach for someone.

She may want water but be unable to communicate it clearly.

This makes constant observation necessary.

---

# **A RECENT INCIDENT**

Corin was lying beneath her wings.

Her cup was several meters away.

She repeatedly looked toward it.

Nobody initially noticed.

Eventually, Guiding Light entered the room.

She immediately understood.

> **"She's thirsty."**

Orphie brought the cup over.

Corin drank.

Afterward, she rested her head against Orphie's hand.

No sound came from her.

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

Orphie has become particularly attentive to Corin's smallest movements.

She watches her ears.

Her tails.

Her wings.

Her eyes.

She has begun asking questions and waiting for physical responses.

> **"Water?"**

Corin looks toward the cup.

> **"Food?"**

Corin looks away.

> **"Want me to hold you?"**

Corin slowly raises one wing.

Orphie understands.

She gently picks her up.

---

# **CORIN'S SILENCE**

The absence of her voice has made her miniature form feel strangely different.

She is still Corin.

Her memories remain.

Her personality remains.

Her thoughts remain.

But there is no little:

> _"Mew."_

anymore.

Only silence.

---

# **MEDICAL SIGNIFICANCE**

The inability to vocalize is being monitored alongside her mercury poisoning.

C.O.R.E. has not yet determined whether the loss of vocalization is:

- A direct consequence of the poisoning.
    
- A consequence of her physical weakness.
    
- A transformation-related effect.
    
- Or another anomalous complication.
    

Further examination is ongoing.
