# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / BIRTHDAY INCIDENT REPORT**

# **THE BIRTHDAY THAT NEVER HAPPENED**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Current Status:** **MISSING**  
**Event:** **Corin's Birthday**  
**Status:** **CANCELED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Today was supposed to be Corin's birthday.

The Treehouse had originally been scheduled to host a small celebration.

Food had been prepared.

Decorations had been stored.

A number of SAFEHOUSE residents had quietly discussed what to give her.

The celebration was canceled.

No formal reason was entered into the schedule.

The only explanation provided was:

> **"Nobody can bear it."**

---

# **THE EMPTY TREEHOUSE**

The Treehouse remains occupied.

But Corin's absence is impossible to ignore.

Her usual places remain untouched.

Her belongings remain where she left them.

Her sketchbook remains secured.

The blue flowers brought by Orphie remain nearby.

There is no birthday cake.

No candles.

No music.

No gathering.

Nobody wanted to pretend everything was normal.

---

# **THE RESIDENTS**

Several SAFEHOUSE residents reportedly visited the Treehouse at different times.

Nobody stayed for long.

Some simply stood there.

Some looked through Corin's belongings.

Some cried.

Others could not bring themselves to enter.

One resident reportedly said:

> **"If we celebrate without her, it feels wrong."**

Another:

> **"If we celebrate anyway, it hurts more."**

The celebration was therefore abandoned.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie arrived carrying a small package.

Inside was a birthday gift.

She did not open it.

She placed it on the table.

Then she sat there for several minutes.

Eventually she said:

> **"Happy birthday, Corin."**

No response.

Orphie waited anyway.

---

# **THE SEARCH**

C.O.R.E. continues searching for Corin.

No confirmed location has been established.

No reliable trail exists.

Yet evidence of her presence continues to appear intermittently.

Most notably:

**Feathers.**

A single Corin feather was discovered beneath the Treehouse this morning.

It was dormant.

No azure flame.

No heat.

No anomalous activity.

Just a feather.

---

# **THE FEATHER**

Orphie found it.

She immediately recognized it.

She picked it up carefully.

For several seconds, she said nothing.

Then:

> **"...You remembered."**

She looked around the Treehouse.

Nothing moved.

No one answered.

---

# **THE SENSATION**

Orphie has again reported the familiar sensation.

Someone watching.

She cannot see anyone.

Security cannot see anyone.

There is no confirmed observer.

Yet Orphie stopped near the Treehouse window and stared into the darkness.

> **"Are you there?"**

Silence.

She remained there.

---

# **THE SKETCHBOOK**

Researchers reviewed Corin's sketchbook today.

The later pages contain numerous Eyes.

Black Eyes.

Normal Eyes.

The imagined Observer.

The final pages remain unchanged.

The Observer's azure glow remains carefully drawn.

Underneath:

> **"I am always watching."**

> **"-NOT Corin"**

C.O.R.E. has found no evidence that this entity exists.

---

# **BIRTHDAY RECORD**

A small birthday record was found among Corin's belongings.

It appears to have been prepared by Corin herself.

The page contains a crude drawing of a cake.

There are two candles.

Underneath:

> **"Don't forget."**

The handwriting is unmistakably hers.

Nobody knows whether she wrote it for herself or for someone else.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Today should have been loud.

There should have been cake.

Bad drawings.

Someone complaining about decorations.

Someone accidentally eating the wrong food.

Someone making Corin laugh.

Someone telling her:

> **"Happy birthday."**

Instead, the Treehouse is quiet.

The gift sits unopened.

The table is empty.

The lights remain off.

And somewhere beyond every search perimeter, Corin is still missing.

Nobody knows where she is.

Nobody knows whether she can see them.

Nobody knows whether she knows what day it is.

But somewhere, unseen, there is a possibility that Corin knows.

And perhaps, somewhere in the darkness, she is wondering whether anyone remembered.

They did.

They simply couldn't bear to celebrate without her.

**End of Birthday Incident Report**
