# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **BLACKSITE RECREATIONAL LOG**

### **EVENT REPORT – "LOADOUT SWAP"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Internal Recreation Archive

---

**Event Name:** Loadout Swap

**Purpose:** Team building, weapon familiarization, and seeing what happens when everyone uses someone else's equipment.

**Rules:**

- Every participant places their primary weapon into a sealed mystery box.
    
- Each participant draws one box at random.
    
- The drawn weapon becomes their temporary loadout for the remainder of the day.
    
- No complaining.
    
- No trading.
    
- Try not to destroy the training grounds.
    

Medical Services added one additional rule:

> "Please return with the same number of limbs you started with."

---

# **RESULTS**

## 🐇 Belle

**Drew: M1014 Combat Shotgun (Keith)**

Belle stared at the shotgun.

"...It doesn't even weigh fifty kilograms."

She proceeded to perform surprisingly well, although she instinctively attempted to use it like a club after running dry.

Keith quietly reminded her that it reloads.

---

## 🦈 Kent (Keith)

**Drew: Bliss of Whitefall (Miss Neko)**

After a brief explanation of the handheld terraformer, Keith used it with remarkable precision.

Several engineers remarked that he somehow made terraforming look like a military drill.

Miss Neko simply nodded in approval.

---

## 🦈🔥 Remielle (Cinder)

**Drew: Compound Bow (Star)**

The first arrow landed dead center on the target.

The second split the first.

The third somehow pinned both to the target stand.

Star applauded enthusiastically.

Remielle admitted:

> "...This is actually pretty fun."

---

## 🐈 Neko

**Drew: Belle's 50 kg Hybrid Greatsword**

Neko confidently grabbed the handle.

Nothing happened.

He tried again.

Nothing happened.

Belle walked over.

Picked it up with one hand.

Handed it back.

Neko immediately returned it to the rack.

> "...Engineering has determined this sword is very heavy."

---

## 🐈 Miss Neko

**Drew: Plasma Scythe / Assault Rifle (Neko)**

She transformed it correctly on the first attempt.

Reloaded it correctly.

Engaged every safety correctly.

Neko looked mildly offended.

> "...You figured it out too quickly."

---

## 🌟 Star

**Drew: Multi-Functional Combat Briefcase (Cinder)**

Star attempted to lift it.

The briefcase did not move.

She tried again with both hands.

Still nothing.

Belle and Cinder eventually helped her carry it.

Star declared:

> "...It's really, really heavy..."

---

## 🌙 Nightshade

**Drew: Endmin's Nekonium Baseball Bat & Bullpup Sniper Rifle**

Nightshade stared at the sniper rifle.

"...Why is it so long?"

She achieved respectable accuracy after a short lesson from Endmin.

The baseball bat, however...

"...I prefer magic."

---

## 🐆 Taiga

**Drew: Frost's Katana**

Taiga spent most of the day admiring the craftsmanship.

She handled it carefully and returned it in perfect condition.

Frost appreciated that.

---

## 🦉 Mart

**Drew: Plasma Scythe / Assault Rifle (Neko)**

Mart immediately read the instruction manual before touching it.

Neko was impressed.

Mart successfully transformed the weapon on his first attempt.

Neko looked even more impressed.

---

## 🐈 Endmin

**Drew: "Star" Revolver (Taiga)**

Despite never using the revolver before, Endmin demonstrated exceptional marksmanship.

Taiga watched in silence before quietly saying:

> "...I should've expected that."

---

## ❄ Frost

**Drew: "Moon" Revolver (Mart)**

After a little practice, Frost developed a calm and measured shooting style.

Mart later commented that she treated every shot as if she were carefully placing it rather than firing it.

---

# **FUNNIEST MOMENTS**

**Strongest Weapon User**

Belle attempted to shoulder the M1014 exactly as Keith demonstrated.

The recoil barely moved her.

Keith:

> "...I forgot you're used to carrying a fifty-kilogram sword."

---

**Most Confused**

Star.

Specifically while trying to understand why Cinder willingly carries a fifty-five-kilogram briefcase every day.

---

**Most Unexpected Talent**

Remielle's archery.

Several personnel suggested she take it up as a hobby.

She replied:

> "...I already have enough dangerous hobbies."

---

**Most Predictable**

Endmin immediately grouping every shot with Taiga's revolver.

Nobody was surprised.

---

**Engineering Incident**

Neko spent twenty minutes trying to determine whether Belle's sword secretly contained a gravity generator.

It did not.

Belle is simply very strong.

---

# **EVENT CONCLUSION**

The event was considered an overwhelming success.

No injuries were reported.

No weapons were damaged.

The mystery boxes have since been stored for future team-building exercises.

Engineering has already proposed another event.

Miss Neko has requested to review the proposal first.

Everyone agreed this was probably for the best.

**End of Event Report.**
