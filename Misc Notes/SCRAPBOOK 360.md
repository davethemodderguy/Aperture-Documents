# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL ASSIGNMENT UPDATE**

# **COREY & NEKO — JOINT RESEARCH DETAIL**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following recent personnel restructuring, **Corey** has been permanently assigned to work alongside **Neko** between the Blacksite's **Research Laboratories** and **Engineering Division**.

The assignment combines Corey's methodical research approach with Neko's engineering expertise.

Separately, **Corin** continues to serve as the **Krampus Compliance Authority's Accountant**, remaining stationed primarily at the KCA.

The arrangement allows the sisters to continue seeing one another regularly while focusing on different responsibilities.

---

# **COREY**

**Current Assignment**

- Research Laboratories
    
- Engineering Division
    

Corey's work primarily involves:

- Assisting with experimental research.
    
- Equipment testing.
    
- Documentation.
    
- Prototype evaluation.
    
- Cross-department projects with Engineering.
    

She remains recognizable by:

- Her oversized lab coat.
    
- Soft hats.
    
- Quiet demeanor.
    
- Several Marts that frequently accompany her between departments.
    

---

# **NEKO**

Neko continues his work within Engineering.

He is responsible for a wide range of projects involving:

- Equipment fabrication.
    
- Facility maintenance.
    
- Prototype development.
    
- Specialized engineering support.
    

Although Neko is known for his unconventional methods, Corey has adapted surprisingly well to working alongside him.

---

# **WORKING RELATIONSHIP**

Researchers have noted that the two complement each other's strengths.

Typical workflow:

- Corey reviews plans and records observations.
    
- Neko builds or modifies the required equipment.
    
- Both evaluate the results together.
    

While their personalities differ considerably, disagreements are uncommon.

Communication is generally brief, efficient, and productive.

---

# **MART INVOLVEMENT**

Several Marts have naturally begun spending portions of the workday in the laboratories and engineering workshops.

Common activities include:

- Carrying small tools.
    
- Delivering paperwork.
    
- Fetching components.
    
- Keeping drinks warm or cool through thermal regulation.
    
- Quietly observing experiments from safe locations.
    

Personnel are reminded not to mistake them for misplaced blue cleaning supplies.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin remains employed by the **Krampus Compliance Authority** as its Accountant.

Her responsibilities include:

- Financial records.
    
- Budget management.
    
- Payroll oversight.
    
- Administrative accounting.
    

She continues to reside in the treehouse with Corey, commuting between the Blacksite and KCA as required.

Several resident Marts continue to accompany her during evenings.

---

# **NEKO'S COMMENT**

When asked how working with Corey had been, Neko replied:

> "...Quiet."

After several seconds, he added:

> "...Efficient."

Researchers interpreted this as exceptionally high praise.

---

# **COREY'S COMMENT**

Corey was asked whether she enjoyed the assignment.

She looked toward Neko, who was currently repairing a fabrication unit while three Marts watched with fascination.

> "...There's always something new to learn."

She smiled faintly.

> "...I like that."

---

# **CORIN'S COMMENT**

Corin was informed that her sister had settled comfortably into the laboratories.

She nodded.

> "...Good."

Then returned to reviewing a financial report.

A nearby Mart offered her a cheese cracker.

Without looking up, she accepted it.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Late each afternoon, Corey finishes organizing laboratory notes while Neko completes the last engineering tasks of the day.

As they leave together, several Marts quietly follow behind them through the corridors.

Some continue toward Project SAFEHOUSE.

Others drift toward the treehouse.

By evening, Corin has usually returned from the KCA.

The three eventually meet again over dinner.

Despite working in different places, the routine has become familiar.

And, according to the Marts...

> **"...Everyone home."**

**End of Personnel Assignment Update**
