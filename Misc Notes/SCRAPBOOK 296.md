# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – DURABILITY ASSESSMENT**

### **DESIGNATION: M.A.R.T. BLOBS – PHYSICAL RESILIENCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Research Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Controlled testing has demonstrated that Marts possess extraordinary resistance to conventional physical damage.

Despite appearing to be small blobs of water, their Ether containment field prevents normal mechanical forces from causing lasting harm.

Personnel are reminded, however, that this resilience **does not** justify rough handling.

---

# **PHYSICAL TESTING**

Under controlled laboratory conditions, Marts have been observed to withstand:

- Falling from significant heights.
    
- Being accidentally stepped on.
    
- Compression.
    
- Throwing.
    
- Bumping into machinery.
    
- Submersion in hot water.
    
- Rapid temperature changes.
    

Following each test, the Mart simply returned to its normal shape within moments.

No lasting injury was recorded.

---

# **TEMPERATURE RESISTANCE**

One unexpected finding was the species' resistance to heat.

Even when immersed in boiling water, a Mart's Ether containment field maintained its structure.

The Mart itself appeared more confused than distressed.

One specimen emerged from the pot, adjusted its tiny top hat, and happily said:

> "Hello!"

Medical staff unanimously agreed no further boiling experiments were necessary.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE**

Perhaps more remarkable than their durability is the Mart's remarkable trust in others.

Marts do not naturally interpret accidental physical harm as malicious.

For example, if a Mart is:

- Accidentally dropped.
    
- Bumped by equipment.
    
- Knocked off a table.
    
- Picked up awkwardly.
    

Its typical response is simply:

> "Hello!"

Or:

> "Mart!"

Behavioral specialists believe the species lacks a sophisticated concept of blame.

They generally assume that other people mean well.

---

# **IMPORTANT DISTINCTION**

Although physically resilient, Marts remain emotionally vulnerable.

Their greatest risks are **not** mechanical.

As documented previously:

- Separation from their top hat during dissolution is fatal.
    
- Extreme emotional distress can destabilize their Ether pattern.
    

Personnel should therefore prioritize kindness over concern for physical impact.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Research concludes that the Marts' bodies are among the most physically resilient benign organisms documented within the Blacksite.

Ironically, what threatens them most is not force, fire, or falling.

It is loneliness, despair, and the loss of the tiny Ether anchor they carry upon their heads.

For this reason, the official handling guidance remains unchanged.

> **Treat every Mart with kindness.**

Not because they're fragile.

But because they deserve it.
