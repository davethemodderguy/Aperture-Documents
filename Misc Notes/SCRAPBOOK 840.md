## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### RESIDENTIAL & RESEARCH PERSONNEL ARCHIVES

# NERI — SUPPLEMENTAL BEHAVIORAL & ACADEMIC PROFILE

**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**SPECIES:** Seal Thiren  
**STATUS:** SAFEHOUSE RESIDENT  
**PRIMARY INTERESTS:** Water, Fish, Sweets, Chemistry  
**KNOWN SOCIAL CONNECTIONS:** Bao, Frost, Alice, Neko, SAFEHOUSE Residents

---

# 1. SWEET TOOTH

Neri possesses an exceptionally strong preference for sweet foods.

While she enjoys fish considerably, fish is not the only way to gain her attention.

Desserts work.

Very well.

Neri particularly enjoys:

- Cakes
    
- Cookies
    
- Pastries
    
- Pudding
    
- Ice cream
    
- Chocolate
    
- Sweetened drinks
    
- Candy
    
- Fruit-based desserts
    
- Anything containing excessive amounts of sugar
    

There is no known point at which Neri will voluntarily describe a dessert as "too sweet."

When presented with a particularly sugary dessert, her usual response is simply:

> "Good."

Neko has consequently learned that **fish and sweets are extremely effective diplomatic resources**.

---

# 2. THE NEKO FRIENDSHIP

Neri became friends with Neko unusually quickly.

The initial interaction was completely ordinary.

Neko was working in the Archives when Neri approached him.

She watched him working.

For several minutes.

Neko eventually looked up.

> **Neko:** "Can I help you?"

> **Neri:** "What are you doing?"

> **Neko:** "Chemistry."

Neri looked at the work.

Then she pointed out an error.

Neko stopped.

He checked the calculation.

She was correct.

Neko asked her how she knew.

Neri explained.

Neko asked another question.

She answered it.

He asked another.

She answered that one too.

At this point, Neko stopped working entirely.

> **Neko:** "...How much chemistry do you know?"

Neri shrugged.

> **Neri:** "A lot."

Neko stared at her.

---

# 3. ACADEMIC REASSESSMENT

Neri's chemistry knowledge was initially assumed to be simply above average.

This assumption was quickly discarded.

Testing conducted by Neko indicated that Neri possesses **exceptionally advanced chemical knowledge**, including an intuitive understanding of:

- reaction mechanisms;
    
- stoichiometry;
    
- molecular interactions;
    
- thermodynamics;
    
- solution chemistry;
    
- organic chemistry;
    
- inorganic chemistry;
    
- material interactions;
    
- anomalous compounds;
    
- chemical stability;
    
- synthesis planning.
    

More unusually, Neri appears capable of understanding unfamiliar compounds extremely quickly.

She does not simply memorize chemical information.

She appears to **understand the underlying principles intuitively.**

Neko was impressed.

This is notable.

Neko is rarely impressed.

---

# 4. THE SWEET TOOTH CONNECTION

Neko eventually discovered that Neri's interest in chemistry extended into food.

Specifically:

**Candy chemistry.**

Neri has an unusual interest in the chemistry of confectionery.

She can discuss sugar crystallization, caramelization, emulsions, texture modification, flavor compounds, and temperature-dependent behavior with considerably more enthusiasm than expected.

Neko once asked why she knew so much about sugar chemistry.

Neri answered:

> "Sweets."

Neko:

> "...That's fair."

---

# 5. CURRENT RELATIONSHIP

Neko and Neri now frequently spend time together in the Archives and laboratory areas.

Their interactions generally fall into one of three categories:

### A. Chemistry

Neri assists Neko with research.

### B. Food

Neko experiments with recipes.

Neri provides extremely serious quality control.

### C. Nonsense

Neri sits nearby while Neko works.

Neither speaks for extended periods.

Both appear completely comfortable with this arrangement.

Neko has described Neri as:

> "A surprisingly competent little seal."

Neri considers this a compliment.

---

# 6. LABORATORY ACCESS

Due to her demonstrated competence, Neri has been granted **restricted supervised access** to appropriate chemistry facilities.

This does not mean unrestricted laboratory privileges.

Neko specifically emphasized that Neri's intelligence does not exempt her from laboratory safety procedures.

Neri accepted this without complaint.

She also apparently understood the safety requirements better than several personnel who had completed formal training.

Neko's response:

> "I'm starting to think we should've tested her sooner."

---

# 7. RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION

Neri's behavioral profile now presents an interesting contradiction.

She is simultaneously:

**Exceptionally intelligent**  
**Extremely affectionate**  
**Fond of sweets**  
**Obsessed with fish**  
**Comfortable spending hours underwater**  
**Protective of her friends**  
**Alarmingly willing to eliminate perceived threats**

And she is now particularly fond of Neko.

This has resulted in an unusual mutual arrangement.

Neko provides:

> Chemistry + sweets.

Neri provides:

> Chemistry + friendship + fish-related commentary.

Both parties appear satisfied.

---

# 8. ARCHIVIST NOTE

An Archivist once asked Neko why he had become friends with Neri so quickly.

Neko answered:

> "She's nice."

The Archivist pointed out that Neri had previously attacked a bully with a concealed knife.

Neko paused.

> "She's nice **to me.**"

Neri, who had been sitting nearby eating cake, nodded.

> "Neko is friend."

Neko:

> "Yep."

Neri:

> "Good friend."

Neko:

> "Yep."

Neri:

> "I protect."

Neko immediately stopped smiling.

> "...Okay, let's maybe not talk about that."

Neri continued eating her cake.

> "Okay."

# 9. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Neri's integration into SAFEHOUSE society has progressed considerably faster than initially expected.

Her strongest social anchors currently include:

**Bao — "Mom"**  
**Frost — "Big sis"**  
**Alice — "Bunny"**  
**Neko — Friend**

Her affection toward these individuals is genuine and strong.

Personnel should continue treating these relationships with appropriate respect.

In particular, the following remains true:

> **Do not harm Neri's friends.**

The addition of Neko to that list is considered noteworthy.

Not because Neko requires additional protection.

Rather because Neko has already begun giving Neri access to increasingly complicated chemistry discussions.

C.O.R.E. would prefer that both of them remain supervised.
