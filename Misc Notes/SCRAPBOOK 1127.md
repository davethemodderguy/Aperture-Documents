# 1. SUMMARY

Plush-form Neri became temporarily stuck with Neko.

No medical emergency occurred.

No distress was reported.

Neri subsequently received headpats.

She did not object.

---

# 2. INITIAL INCIDENT

Neko was working at his usual workstation.

Plush Neri had apparently followed him into the engineering area earlier in the day.

At some point, she climbed onto his clothing and settled against his side.

Neko continued working.

Approximately twenty minutes later, he attempted to stand.

Neri did not move.

He tried again.

Still nothing.

Neko looked down.

Neri was gripping his clothing with both plush hands.

Her seal tail was wrapped around the edge of his shirt.

She was completely comfortable.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Neri.”

Neri looked up.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Are you stuck?”

Neri nodded.

Pause.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Do you want me to get you off?”

Neri shook her head.

Neko sighed.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Okay.”

He sat back down.

---

# 3. THE HEADPAT

Neri remained attached to Neko for several minutes.

She eventually settled her head against him.

Neko looked down.

Then, seemingly without thinking, he placed one hand on top of her head.

And gently patted her.

Neri immediately went still.

Her eyes slowly closed.

Her glasses shifted slightly.

Her hoodie sleeves covered most of her hands.

Her tail relaxed.

Neko stopped.

Neri opened one eye.

She looked at him.

Then lowered her head again.

Neko resumed.

---

# 4. OBSERVATION

The headpats continued for approximately three minutes.

No conversation occurred.

Neri simply remained there.

Neko continued working with one hand while occasionally patting her with the other.

A nearby researcher eventually noticed.

The researcher stared.

Neko looked over.

> **NEKO:**  
> “What?”

The researcher pointed.

> **RESEARCHER:**  
> “You're petting Neri.”

Neko looked down.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Apparently.”

Neri's eyes remained closed.

---

# 5. NERI'S RESPONSE

After several minutes, Neri quietly spoke.

Her voice was extremely soft.

> **NERI:**  
> “...Again.”

Neko paused.

He looked mildly surprised.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Again?”

Neri nodded.

Neko continued patting her.

No further comment was made.

---

# 6. ALICE ARRIVES

Alice entered the room.

She immediately noticed Plush Neri.

> **ALICE:**  
> “Neri!”

Neri opened her eyes.

Alice approached.

Then noticed Neko's hand.

> **ALICE:**  
> “You're patting her!”

Neko looked at Alice.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Apparently she likes it.”

Alice smiled.

> **ALICE:**  
> “She does!”

Neri quietly looked away.

Alice crouched beside them.

> **ALICE:**  
> “Can I?”

Neri looked at Alice.

Then at Neko.

Then nodded.

Alice gently patted Neri's head.

Neri closed her eyes again.

---

# 7. UNEXPECTED DEVELOPMENT

Neko eventually attempted to return to work.

Neri remained attached.

He attempted to move his arm.

Neri adjusted her grip.

He moved again.

Neri followed.

Neko stopped.

> **NEKO:**  
> “You're really not getting off, are you?”

Neri shook her head.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Why?”

Neri thought for several seconds.

Then:

> **NERI:**  
> “Warm.”

Neko paused.

His expression softened.

> **NEKO:**  
> “...Fine.”

He continued working.

Neri stayed.
