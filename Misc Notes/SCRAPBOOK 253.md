# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – PERSONNEL ETIQUETTE BULLETIN**

### **SUBJECT: THIREN SOCIAL INTERACTIONS – FELINE VARIANTS**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following several incidents involving well-meaning personnel, C.O.R.E. has issued the following guidance regarding interactions with feline Thiren employees.

Please remember that Thiren are people.

Do not assume they enjoy being treated as animals solely because of their species.

That said...

There are exceptions.

---

# **NEKO**

Neko is a cat Thiren.

Despite his generally reserved personality, he displays a surprising tolerance for behavior commonly associated with domestic cats.

This includes:

- Head pats.
    
- Ear scratches (with permission).
    
- Chin scratches.
    
- Quietly sitting beside him.
    

When asked why he allows this, Neko answered simply:

> "...Feels nice."

He then returned to whatever he had been working on.

Personnel have since discovered that a gentle pat on the head is occasionally enough to interrupt one of Neko's marathon work sessions long enough for him to remember to eat or rest.

---

# **MISS NEKO**

Miss Neko is **not** receptive to the same treatment.

Attempts to pat her head have consistently resulted in visible annoyance.

Typical responses include:

> "...No."

Or:

> "...Don't."

On one documented occasion, she silently stared at the employee until they apologized.

The apology was accepted.

No further action was taken.

---

She then excitedly tried the same thing with Miss Neko.

There was a long silence.

> "...Educational."

---

**Nightshade**

> "...Don't."

When asked which one she meant, Nightshade answered:

> "...Miss Neko."

---

# **NEKO'S EXPLANATION**

When asked why the two reacted so differently, Neko thought for a moment.

> "...I'm comfortable with it."

He glanced toward Miss Neko.

> "...She isn't."

Miss Neko nodded once.

> "...Correct."

No further explanation was considered necessary.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Personnel are reminded that individual preferences always take precedence over species stereotypes.

If Neko permits a friendly head pat, that is his personal choice.

If Miss Neko declines, that choice is equally respected.

As one orientation manual now summarizes:

> **Neko:** You may ask.

> **Miss Neko:** Don't.

Thus far, this guidance has proven remarkably effective.

**End of Personnel Etiquette Bulletin**
