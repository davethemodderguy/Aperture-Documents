## **MISSION SUMMARY**

An extensive investigation conducted by the **Krampus Compliance Authority (KCA)** uncovered evidence linking the **Banlands Watchdogs** to a network trafficking prohibited **Etheric Contrabands**.

Further investigation identified that the organization had been receiving protection and material support from the **Head of the Department of Defense**, who authorized illegal experimentation involving Ether-based technology.

The stated objective of these experiments was the attempted recreation of **Endmin Merryweather's Ether Sense**, using unlawfully acquired Etheric materials.

Following verification of the evidence, the Krampus Compliance Authority authorized immediate intervention under its anti-corruption mandate.

---

## **PRIMARY OBJECTIVES**

**Objective Alpha**

- Neutralize the Banlands Watchdogs' headquarters.
    
- Arrest or eliminate hostile members offering armed resistance, in accordance with operational rules of engagement.
    

**Objective Bravo**

- Locate and destroy all confirmed Etheric Contrabands.
    
- Secure any research material for forensic analysis before destruction where practical.
    

**Objective Charlie**

- Apprehend or eliminate government personnel determined to be actively participating in the corruption network and presenting an immediate threat.

## **LEGAL AUTHORITY**

The Krampus Compliance Authority operates as an **independent government oversight body**.

Its jurisdiction includes:

- Investigating corruption.
    
- Preventing bribery.
    
- Enforcing governmental accountability.
    
- Executing lawful actions against corrupt officials under Banlands law.
    

Because the Authority functions independently from executive departments, personnel under investigation cannot legally assume operational command over KCA activities or halt an authorized investigation into themselves.

Attempts to fabricate evidence against the Krampus Compliance Authority constitute separate criminal offenses subject to independent investigation.

## **Pre-Deployment**

The operations room was quiet.

Evidence boards filled the walls.

Financial records.

Shipping manifests.

Recovered contraband.

Photographs.

Belle placed one final folder onto the table.

> **"The evidence is complete."**

Endmin looked across the room.

> **"...Department of Defense."**

Lucius frowned.

> **"...They're backing a gang?"**

Belle nodded once.

> **"Not the department as a whole."**

She pointed to a photograph.

> **"Its current Head."**

Another folder was opened.

Inside were documents detailing unauthorized Ether experiments.

Lucius read silently.

His expression hardened.

> **"...They wanted to recreate your Ether Sense."**

Endmin looked at the papers without speaking.

After a few moments, she quietly closed the folder.

> **"...Using innocent people."**

Belle's cheerful expression disappeared completely.

> **"...That's why we're going."**

---

## **Operational Briefing**

Belle stood before the assembled personnel.

Her tone was calm.

Professional.

Unwavering.

> **"Today's mission is not revenge."**

She looked around the room.

> **"It is law enforcement."**

She pointed toward the operation map.

> **"Anyone who surrenders will be taken into custody."

> **"Anyone who presents an immediate lethal threat will be dealt with according to our rules of engagement."**

Endmin nodded.

Lucius checked his pistols.

No one spoke after that.

---

## **Director Lucious' Authorization**

Director Lucious signed the cooperation agreement between Aperture Industries and the Banlands authorities before adding one final note.

> **"Justice requires evidence."**

> **"This operation proceeds because that evidence has been independently verified."**

Endmin checked the chamber of her rifle.

Lucius secured his twin pistols.

Three members of the Merryweather family walked toward the transport.

Not out of anger.

But because, after months of investigation, the evidence had spoken.
