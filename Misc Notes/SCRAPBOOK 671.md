## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / RECOVERY OBSERVATION LOG**

# **THE LITTLE THINGS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **EARLY RECOVERY / FRAGILE HOPE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's recovery did not begin with a revelation.

It began with small things.

Things she had done before.

Things she had stopped doing.

Things she once considered ordinary.

After her first flight in a year, Corin began slowly returning to them.

Not because someone told her to.

Not because she was trying to become her old self.

She simply discovered that some of those things still felt...

**nice.**

---

# **DRAWING**

The first was drawing.

Corin found her old sketchbook.

The pages were filled with drawings from before her disappearance.

Some were terrible.

Some were worse.

She stared at them for a long time.

Then she picked up a pencil.

Her first attempt was still terrible.

The proportions were wrong.

The hands looked strange.

One wing was significantly larger than the other.

Corin stared at the drawing.

Then quietly laughed.

> **"...Still bad."**

Lumi, sitting nearby, smiled.

> **"Very."**

Corin smiled back.

It wasn't a big smile.

But it was genuine.

---

# **FLOWERS**

Corin began collecting flowers again.

Not because she needed to eat them.

Not because they happened to grow nearby.

She simply liked them.

She started keeping small bundles beside her.

Blue flowers were still her favorite.

Sometimes she would place one beside the Treehouse window.

Sometimes she kept one tucked between her feathers.

---

# **FLIGHT**

Corin continued practicing flight.

She remained unsteady.

Her wings had been unused for too long.

But every attempt became slightly easier.

She would rise above the ground.

Circle the tree.

Land.

Rest.

Then try again later.

Nobody applauded.

Nobody made a celebration out of it.

That helped.

It was simply something Corin was doing again.

---

# **THE FEATHER LANTERNS**

The Feather Lanterns remained faint.

Corin noticed that their brightness increased slightly whenever she felt genuinely happy.

She began experimenting.

Not scientifically this time.

Just for fun.

She would sit beside a lantern.

Think about something pleasant.

Wait.

Sometimes the light became brighter.

Sometimes it didn't.

Corin eventually wrote:

> **"Maybe they aren't measuring hope."**

Then:

> **"Maybe they're just happy with me."**

---

# **DRAWING WITH LUMI**

One evening, Lumi brought out paper.

> **"Draw with me?"**

Corin hesitated.

Then nodded.

They sat together beneath the Treehouse.

Corin drew a tree.

It looked terrible.

Lumi looked at it.

> **"That's a tree?"**

Corin frowned.

> **"Yes."**

> **"Are you sure?"**

Corin laughed.

A real laugh.

Lumi started crying.

Corin immediately stopped.

> **"Why are you crying?"**

Lumi wiped her eyes.

> **"Nothing."**

Corin stared suspiciously.

Then continued drawing.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie noticed the change too.

She did not try to force Corin into doing anything.

She simply joined when invited.

Sometimes they sat together.

Sometimes they talked.

Sometimes they didn't.

One afternoon, Corin handed Orphie a drawing.

It was a picture of the Treehouse.

It was badly drawn.

Orphie stared at it.

> **"It's beautiful."**

Corin narrowed her eyes.

> **"You're lying."**

Orphie laughed.

> **"A little."**

Corin smiled.

> **"Okay."**

---

# **THE OLD THINGS**

Corin slowly returned to things she had once enjoyed.

Drawing.

Flowers.

Flying.

Sitting beneath the tree.

Watching snowfall.

Talking with Lumi.

Talking with Orphie.

Reading her own research.

Collecting feathers.

Making Feather Lanterns.

Even complaining about how terrible her drawings were.

None of these things cured her.

None erased the year she spent alone.

None repaired the wound in her heart.

But they gave her something.

Small reasons to remain present.

---

# **HOPE**

Corin eventually wrote another research entry.

It was not about her body.

Not about her wings.

Not about her feathers.

It was about hope.

> **"I thought hope had to be something big."**

Then:

> **"Maybe it isn't."**

She continued:

> **"Maybe it's drawing something stupid."**

> **"Maybe it's a flower."**

> **"Maybe it's flying for five seconds."**

> **"Maybe it's sitting with someone."**

She stopped writing.

Then added:

> **"Maybe I don't need a lot."**

---

# **THE LITTLE THINGS**

Corin's hope remained fragile.

It could disappear for hours.

Sometimes days.

A bad memory could still make her retreat beneath her wings.

A careless sentence could still hurt deeply.

The snow could still remind her of waiting.

But now there were things capable of reaching her.

Small things.

Quiet things.

Things that did not demand anything from her.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin once believed she needed hope to move forward.

Then she lost hope.

For a long time, nobody could give it back to her.

Eventually, she found something different.

She found a pencil.

A flower.

A little patch of snow.

A familiar voice.

A pair of wings that could still fly.

A badly drawn tree.

A laugh she had almost forgotten how to make.

None of them were enough on their own.

Together, they were something.

Not happiness.

Not yet.

Not the old Corin.

Something smaller.

Something more fragile.

But real.

**Hope.**

Just enough to keep moving.

**End of Recovery Observation Log**
