### **ARCHITECTAL COMPANION / RESIDENTIAL CARE LOG**

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S BLUE JAY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Internal Personnel  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Primary Owner:** Guiding Light  
**Temporary Caretaker:** Corin  
**Status:** **HEALTHY / SPOILED / TEMPORARILY IN CORIN'S CARE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The blue jay living around Corin's treehouse is not actually Corin's pet.

He belongs to **Guiding Light**.

Guiding Light acquired the bird some time ago and became rather attached to him.

Unfortunately, being an Architect comes with responsibilities.

Guiding Light is frequently occupied with Blacksite matters, dimensional incidents, protecting residents, and generally being an entity capable of doing far too many things at once.

She therefore needed someone she trusted to look after her bird.

Corin volunteered.

Or, more accurately:

Guiding Light asked.

Corin said yes.

And the blue jay apparently approved.

---

# **TEMPORARY ARRANGEMENT**

Guiding Light's schedule has become increasingly demanding.

Rather than leave her pet unattended, she entrusted him to Corin.

The arrangement is technically temporary.

Nobody has told the bird this.

He appears to believe he has simply acquired a second home.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSIBILITIES**

Corin is responsible for:

- Providing food.
    
- Providing fresh water.
    
- Keeping the bird's sleeping area clean.
    
- Making sure he remains safe inside and around the treehouse.
    
- Keeping him away from dangerous objects.
    
- Informing Guiding Light if anything unusual occurs.
    

Corin takes these responsibilities surprisingly seriously.

She keeps a small written schedule.

> **Morning:** Food  
> **Afternoon:** Water  
> **Evening:** Check nest  
> **Night:** Make sure he isn't doing anything stupid

The last item was added after the bird repeatedly attempted to sleep on the roof.

---

# **THE NAME**

Guiding Light never gave him a particularly complicated name.

She simply calls him:

> **Jay**

Corin kept the name.

She sees no reason to change it.

---

# **CORIN AND JAY**

Despite being Guiding Light's pet, Jay has become extremely comfortable with Corin.

He sits on her shoulder.

He follows her around the treehouse.

He occasionally lands on her red wool beanie.

He has also developed an unfortunate habit of stealing small objects.

Corin has learned to keep her hairclip secure.

Mostly.

---

# **THE FIRST DAY**

Guiding Light explained the arrangement before leaving.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Can you watch him for me?"

Corin nodded.

> **"Sure."**

Jay hopped onto Corin's shoulder.

Guiding Light smiled.

> **"He likes you."**

Corin looked at the bird.

> **"...Apparently."**

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S RETURN**

Guiding Light eventually returned to the treehouse.

She found Corin sitting outside.

Jay was perched on her shoulder.

He looked completely comfortable.

Guiding Light stared.

> **"He looks very happy."**

Corin nodded.

> **"He's been eating well."**

> **"Sleeping?"**

> **"Mostly."**

Corin paused.

> **"...He tried to eat my notebook."**

Guiding Light looked at Jay.

Jay chirped innocently.

> **"...I see."**

---

# **A SMALL PROBLEM**

Jay has apparently decided that Corin is an acceptable substitute perch.

He follows her constantly.

If Corin sits down:

**Jay sits on her shoulder.**

If Corin walks:

**Jay follows.**

If Corin cooks:

**Jay watches.**

If Corin sleeps:

**Jay attempts to sleep on the bedpost.**

Corin has accepted this.

Mostly.

---

# **THE COLOR**

There is one particularly meaningful part of caring for Jay.

Jay's feathers are blue.

Corin can see that blue now.

Before Guiding Light's intervention, she would have recognized him as a bird but would have been unable to perceive his actual coloring.

Now she can.

Sometimes she simply watches him.

Guiding Light noticed.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "You're staring."

> **Corin:**  
> "He's pretty."

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "He is."

Corin gently touched one of his feathers.

> **"...I like blue."**

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S REACTION**

Guiding Light is quietly pleased.

Not because Corin is caring for her pet.

Because Corin has begun caring about something outside herself again.

She isn't merely maintaining Jay.

She talks to him.

She worries about him.

She makes sure he's comfortable.

She gets mildly annoyed when he misbehaves.

She laughs when he does something ridiculous.

And, most importantly:

She looks forward to Guiding Light returning so she can report everything he did.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Jay belongs to Guiding Light.

Corin knows this.

Guiding Light knows this.

Jay apparently does not care.

For now, while Guiding Light is busy, Corin is his caretaker.

And Corin has taken the responsibility seriously.

A girl who once stopped caring whether her own limbs stayed attached now makes sure a small blue bird has enough food and water.

It's a very small responsibility.

But perhaps that's exactly why it matters.

Sometimes recovery isn't about fixing everything.

Sometimes it's simply having something small enough to care for.

**End of Architectal Companion / Residential Care Log**
