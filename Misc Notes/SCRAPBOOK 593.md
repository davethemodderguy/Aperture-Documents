## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **TREEHOUSE RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — RETURN TO NORMAL FORM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Location:** Treehouse  
**Subject:** Corin / Orphie  
**Status:** Resting

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin was sleeping peacefully on Orphie's lap at the Treehouse.

At some point, Orphie fell asleep as well.

Neither intended to sleep for very long.

Neither noticed how much time had passed.

---

# **THE DISCOVERY**

Orphie eventually woke up.

Her first thought was that something felt unusually heavy on her lap.

She opened her eyes.

Corin was no longer palm-sized.

She had returned to her **normal form** while asleep.

Her eyes remained completely closed.

She appeared deeply asleep.

Her wings were partially extended behind her, gently moving through the air in slow, unconscious flaps.

The movement was barely noticeable.

---

# **ORPHIE'S REACTION**

Orphie stared at her for several seconds.

She did not immediately move.

Corin's head rested comfortably against her.

Her breathing was steady.

Her wings continued their slow rhythm.

Orphie realized something important:

> **Corin had finally regained enough strength to return to her normal form.**

And she had done it without waking up.

---

# **THE WINGS**

Corin's wings were slightly spread.

Every few seconds, they gave a gentle flap.

Not enough to lift her.

Not enough to disturb the room.

Just enough to keep moving.

The behavior appeared entirely unconscious.

Her feathers rustled softly with each movement.

---

# **ORPHIE'S DECISION**

Orphie considered waking her.

She didn't.

Instead, she carefully adjusted her position to make Corin more comfortable.

She pulled a blanket over them.

Then she settled back.

> **"A few more minutes won't hurt."**

Corin remained asleep.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

The Treehouse was unusually quiet.

No SAFEHOUSE activity.

No researchers.

No alarms.

No experiments.

Just the two of them resting together.

For the first time in a long while, Corin looked genuinely peaceful.

There was no visible pain.

No trembling.

No desperate attempts to communicate.

Just sleep.

---

# **RECOVERY SIGNIFICANCE**

Corin's spontaneous return to normal form represents a major milestone.

Her body had previously been unable to sustain the larger form because of the severe weakness left by the mercury poisoning.

Her ability to return to it naturally, even temporarily, indicates that her physical reserves have recovered considerably.

The fact that she did so while asleep suggests the transformation was not forced.

Her body simply decided:

> **It was ready.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie could have woken her.

She could have moved.

She could have called the others.

Instead, she looked down at the sleeping Corin.

At the gently moving wings.

At the peaceful expression.

And decided that there was no reason to interrupt her.

So Orphie closed her eyes again.

A few more minutes wouldn't hurt.

And Corin kept sleeping on her lap, finally back to normal.

**End of Treehouse Residential Observation**
