# **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

A controlled demonstration of **ALICE-WPN-01**, previously observed in its disguised configuration as a standard pencil, has produced results significantly beyond initial estimates.

The weapon's true configuration is a massive battle axe.

During testing, Alice was presented with a **1-meter-thick solid Nekonium pillar**.

Nekonium is an extremely durable engineered metal developed by **Neko** and is currently recognized as the **strongest metal engineered by Neko**.

The purpose of the test was to determine whether Alice-WPN-01 could damage the material.

The result exceeded expectations.

> **Alice-WPN-01 cleanly sliced through the entire 1-meter Nekonium pillar.**

---

# **1. TEST MATERIAL**

### **NEKONIUM**

**Classification:** Ultra-Durable Engineered Metal  
**Developer:** Neko  
**Known Properties:** Exceptional structural durability  
**Status:** Highest-strength Neko-engineered metal currently documented

The test specimen consisted of a solid cylindrical pillar with a thickness of:

> **1.00 METERS**

No pre-existing fracture, cutting channel, structural weakness, or anomalous damage was present.

The pillar was verified intact immediately prior to testing.

---

# **2. TEST PROCEDURE**

Alice entered the testing chamber carrying the object previously believed to be an ordinary pencil.

At the direction of the supervising researcher, Alice transformed the object into its true configuration.

### **ALICE-WPN-01**

**Configuration:** Giant Battle Axe

Alice was instructed to strike the Nekonium pillar once.

She did.

---

# **3. RESULT**

The axe passed completely through the pillar.

The Nekonium specimen was separated into two sections.

There was no prolonged cutting process.

There was no visible deformation of the axe head.

There was no apparent loss of momentum sufficient to prevent penetration.

The result was effectively:

> **ONE STRIKE.**
> 
> **ONE-METER NEKONIUM.**
> 
> **CUT IN HALF.**

The weapon sustained no visible damage.

---

# **4. MATERIAL ANALYSIS**

Post-test examination of the cut surfaces produced an unexpected result.

The Nekonium did not appear to have been crushed apart.

It had been **cut.**

The resulting surfaces were comparatively clean relative to the enormous force required to breach the specimen.

Research personnel were unable to identify a conventional mechanism capable of explaining the result.

The axe's cutting capability therefore cannot currently be attributed solely to:

- Blade sharpness.
    
- Physical mass.
    
- Impact velocity.
    
- Conventional metallurgy.
    

An anomalous component is considered highly likely.

---

# **5. OBSERVED ALICE RESPONSE**

Following the test, Alice looked at the separated pillar.

She appeared confused by the reaction of the researchers.

When informed that the material was extremely difficult to damage, she reportedly responded:

> **"...Oh."**

She then looked down at the axe.

After several seconds, she asked:

> **"...Did I break it?"**

The supervising researcher clarified that the pillar had been cut rather than broken.

Alice appeared satisfied.

She subsequently transformed the weapon back into its pencil configuration.

---

# **6. COMPARATIVE SIGNIFICANCE**

The result is particularly significant because the test material was not an ordinary metal.

Nekonium was selected specifically because of its extreme durability.

A weapon capable of cutting through a solid one-meter specimen demonstrates a level of penetration capability that places ALICE-WPN-01 well outside the expected performance range of conventional melee weapons.

C.O.R.E. therefore recommends that the weapon **not** be subjected to destructive testing against additional high-value materials.

The first test has already provided sufficient evidence.

---

# **7. SECURITY IMPLICATIONS**

The implications extend beyond Alice herself.

SPAWN is actively operating around Heaven's Blacksite.

Their personnel have demonstrated access to:

- Specialized firearms.
    
- Dark Matter Energy weapons.
    
- Heavy melee equipment.
    
- Protective shields.
    
- Anomalous-targeting technology.
    

Alice now possesses a weapon capable of cutting through **one meter of the strongest metal engineered by Neko.**

Consequently, the previous classification of Alice as merely a vulnerable potential SPAWN target is increasingly inaccurate.

Should SPAWN personnel attempt to directly engage Alice:

> **The expected outcome may not favor SPAWN.**

---

# **8. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

The test has established three important facts.

### **1. ALICE-WPN-01 is extraordinarily powerful.**

Its cutting capability exceeds conventional expectations by a considerable margin.

### **2. Alice can wield it naturally.**

The weapon's enormous apparent size does not appear to impede the subject.

### **3. The pencil is an extremely effective disguise.**

No reasonable observer would expect a small child carrying a pencil to possess a weapon capable of cutting through a one-meter Nekonium pillar.

C.O.R.E. considers this final point operationally significant.
