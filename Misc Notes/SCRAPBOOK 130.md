# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **MEDICAL OBSERVATION ADDENDUM**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – DORSAL SURGICAL SCARRING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Medical Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

During a routine medical examination, extensive surgical scarring was documented across Corin Lockwood's back.

The pattern and distribution of the scars are inconsistent with accidental injury or emergency medical intervention.

Medical Services concluded that the scars are the result of multiple deliberate surgical procedures performed during the period in which Corin was held as an unwilling test subject.

---

# **PHYSICAL OBSERVATION**

The scarring extends across much of Corin's upper and lower back.

Numerous healed incision lines intersect one another, forming a complex network of surgical scars.

Several scars display evidence of having been reopened and closed multiple times.

The stitching patterns are inconsistent, suggesting procedures performed over an extended period rather than a single operation.

Medical personnel noted that the procedures appear to have prioritized experimental access over minimizing long-term tissue damage.

No attempt was made to reduce permanent scarring.

---

# **CORIN'S BEHAVIOR**

Corin rarely allows anyone to see her back.

She consistently wears clothing that conceals the scars.

During examinations, she remains cooperative but noticeably withdrawn.

She avoids mirrors whenever possible.

When asked whether the scars still hurt, Corin answered quietly:

> "...Not all the time."

After a long pause, she added:

> "...Sometimes they ache."

She did not clarify whether she meant physically or emotionally.

Medical staff did not press the question.

---

# **LUMI'S RESPONSE**

On one occasion, Corin hesitantly showed Lumi the scars.

She stood with her back turned, unable to look at her mother's face.

After several moments of silence, Corin whispered:

> "...They're ugly."

Lumi slowly stepped closer.

Without hesitation, she gently rested a hand between the old scars.

Her touch was careful.

Gentle.

Motherly.

> "These are not signs that something was wrong with you."

She spoke softly.

> "They're signs that someone did something terribly wrong to you."

Corin lowered her head.

Her shoulders began to shake.

Lumi wrapped her daughter in a careful embrace, mindful of the areas that remained sensitive.

> "I wish I could have protected you."

Corin quietly cried into her mother's shoulder.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

The dorsal scarring has no significant impact on Corin's day-to-day mobility.

Its primary clinical significance is psychological.

Medical Services recommend that examinations involving the affected area be conducted only when medically necessary and with Corin's informed consent.

Personnel are reminded that the scars are a source of profound emotional distress and should never become a topic of casual discussion.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The scars remain permanent.

While they no longer define Corin to those who know her, they remain a painful reminder to Corin of the abuse she survived.

Project SAFEHOUSE residents have universally respected her privacy regarding the injuries.

Among them, the scars are neither a curiosity nor a spectacle.

They are treated for what they are:

Evidence of crimes committed against a child.

**End of Medical Observation Addendum**
