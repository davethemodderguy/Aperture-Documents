# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **SEALED PSYCHOLOGICAL CASE FILE**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical Director & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **MEDICAL NOTICE**

The following record concerns the long-term psychological effects of childhood abuse and unauthorized experimentation.

This document is maintained to support Corin's continued care and is not to be circulated outside authorized personnel.

---

## **OVERVIEW**

Corin's greatest struggle is not physical.

It is the belief that what happened to her permanently took away the ordinary life she once had.

During therapy, she has repeatedly expressed grief over the life she believes was stolen from her.

She once summarized that feeling quietly.

> "...I just wanted to be normal."

---

## **TRAUMATIC HISTORY**

Recovered records and corroborated testimony indicate that Corin was abducted as a child and subjected to repeated unauthorized Ether experiments.

She was identified not by her name...

But by a designation.

Medical staff discovered the faded tattoo on the inside of her left forearm during a routine examination.

It is almost always concealed beneath long sleeves.

The faded black lettering reads:

> **SUBJECT 075**

Corin never tried to remove it.

When asked why, she answered softly.

> "...It reminds me of what they saw."

She looked at the tattoo for a long moment before adding:

> "...Not who I am."

---

## **THE WEIGHT OF MEMORY**

Unlike Norma, whose memories returned gradually, Corin remembers much of what happened with painful clarity.

She remembers being separated from her brother and sister.

She remembers waking in unfamiliar rooms.

She remembers being treated as though she were an experiment instead of a child.

The memories still visit her without warning.

Certain sounds.

Certain smells.

The sight of surgical lighting.

Each can bring fragments rushing back.

---

## **SELF-PERCEPTION**

Corin has shared with her therapist that there have been periods where she struggled profoundly with the fact that her body no longer functions in ordinary ways.

Her heart does not beat in the way a human heart once did.

She does not breathe to sustain herself.

She does not require food to survive, though she still joins others for meals because she enjoys their company.

Those differences have left her feeling isolated from the person she remembers being.

She once asked during a counseling session:

> "...If so much of me changed..."

> "...Am I still Corin?"

The attending psychologist replied:

> "Your body was changed without your consent."

> "Your kindness wasn't."

---

## **THE LOCKWOODS**

Arcturus still sees the little sister he searched desperately for.

Norma still sees the sister who taught her mathematics and quietly corrected her homework.

Neither of them has ever referred to her as "Subject 075."

Only Corin.

One afternoon, Belle gently took Corin's hand after noticing the edge of the concealed tattoo beneath her sleeve.

Belle didn't ask to see it.

She simply said:

> "...Whatever someone called you back then..."

> "...It isn't your name."

Corin was silent for a long while.

Then she nodded.

Very slowly.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' REMARK**

Director Lucious ordered that all official records referring to Corin by an experimental designation remain permanently sealed.

His annotation reads:

> "The perpetrators reduced children to numbers."

> "The KCA recognizes only their names."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin continues to receive regular medical and psychological care.

She remains one of the KCA's most capable executives, respected for her intelligence, patience, and unwavering professionalism.

The tattoo on her arm has never been removed.

Not because she accepts what was done to her.

But because it stands as evidence that those responsible failed in their ultimate goal.

They tried to erase a child's identity and replace it with a number.

Years later, she is known instead as:

A sister.

A colleague.

A trusted friend.

And the woman who keeps the KCA running when everyone else is busy saving the world.

Her personnel file bears only one name.

**Corin Lockwood.**

**End of Sealed Psychological Case File**
