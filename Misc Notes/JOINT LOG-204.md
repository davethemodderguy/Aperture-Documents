# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES / KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **JOINT LOG-204**

### **WELCOME HOME – DIRECTOR BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Internal Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

Director Belle Merryweather has officially resumed regular visits to Heaven's Blacksite.

Personnel from both Aperture Industries and the Krampus Compliance Authority reported that Belle's demeanor has returned to her familiar baseline.

The cheerful, fluffy bunny girl everyone had come to know was, at last, back.

---

## **BLACKSITE ARRIVAL**

At approximately 09:12, a KCA transport arrived outside Heaven's Blacksite.

Keith exited first, conducting his usual security sweep.

Cinder followed, carrying her 55-kilogram briefcase.

Finally—

Belle stepped out.

She stretched.

Looked at the Blacksite.

Smiled.

Then immediately broke into one of her signature cartoon-like sprints toward the entrance.

Halfway there—

She tripped over absolutely nothing.

Bounced twice.

Landed on her feet.

And continued running as if nothing had happened.

Several researchers watching from the lobby applauded.

One quietly said,

> "...She's back."

---

## **ROOM N-07**

The door slid open.

Belle poked her head inside.

> "...I'm home."

Within seconds, she found herself surrounded by familiar faces.

The room, which had felt unusually quiet for weeks, was suddenly anything but.

Someone laughed.

Someone cried.

Someone was already asking if Belle wanted snacks.

Belle accepted a carrot before anyone finished the question.

---

## **KCA HEADQUARTERS**

The change was noticed just as quickly back at Headquarters.

Employees reported:

- Casual greetings had returned.
    
- Hallway conversations lasted longer than ten seconds.
    
- The Director occasionally stopped to ask how people's days were going.
    
- Smiles had become a common sight again.
    

Administrative staff also made one additional observation.

Serious Belle had processed paperwork at an astonishing speed.

Cheerful Belle still completed everything on time...

...but now she occasionally walked around talking to people instead of signing forms non-stop.

One accountant sighed dramatically.

> "...Our paperwork backlog is increasing again."

Another immediately replied.

> "...Worth it."

The room unanimously agreed.

---

## **KEITH**

Keith remained exactly as professional as ever.

Almost.

During lunch, Belle handed him half of her dessert.

He accepted it.

One security camera recorded the faintest hint of a smile before his expression returned to normal.

Engineering later enhanced the footage to confirm it had, in fact, happened.

The clip was immediately archived under:

**"Rare Events."**

---

## **CINDER**

Cinder took one look at Belle cheerfully chatting with junior employees.

She quietly closed her planner.

Leaned back in her chair.

Smiled.

> "...There she is."

Later that afternoon, Belle invited her to play a board game after work.

Cinder accepted before Belle finished asking.

---

## **BLACKSITE ATMOSPHERE**

Researchers quickly noticed familiar habits returning.

Belle was once again:

- Walking around with a carrot.
    
- Hugging friends without warning.
    
- Emitting soft plushie squeaks when hugged.
    
- Using her 50-kilogram sword as a paperweight.
    
- Being carried around by whoever happened to be nearby because it was "more fun."
    

Room N-07 regained its usual noise.

No one complained.

---

## **FINAL NOTE**

The official record simply states:

> **Director Belle Merryweather has returned to her regular duties and social activities.**

The unofficial record, written in the margin by an unknown employee, reads:

> _"The Blacksite feels like home again."_

A second note appeared underneath.

Handwriting analysis identified the author as Cinder.

> _"And Headquarters finally has a Director who remembers to take coffee breaks instead of replacing them with paperwork."_

A third note, unmistakably Belle's, was added in bright pink ink.

> _"Also, someone stole my carrot."_

A reply appeared five minutes later.

> **Keith:** _"Negative. You already ate it."_

Belle responded beneath it.

> _"...Oh."_

Case closed.
