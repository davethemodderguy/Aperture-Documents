# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The examination was initially scheduled as a routine comprehensive health assessment.

It was not routine.

The examination revealed extensive physiological abnormalities affecting virtually every major biological system examined.

Neri's body does not simply possess a collection of medical abnormalities.

Rather, several of her biological systems appear to operate according to **different and occasionally contradictory physiological rules simultaneously.**

The most significant findings include:

- Progressive spontaneous degradation of circulating blood.
    
- Apparent evaporation/disintegration of blood **within intact blood vessels**.
    
- Severe cardiovascular vulnerability associated with loss of functional blood.
    
- Abnormal organ morphology and function.
    
- Extensive scar tissue affecting multiple anatomical regions.
    
- Irregular tissue regeneration.
    
- Genetic material exhibiting mixed and inconsistent signatures.
    
- Confirmed presence of faint Bao-derived DNA within Neri's body.
    
- Evidence of post-transformation genetic integration.
    
- Cellular structures inconsistent with a conventional Seal Thiren.
    
- Multiple physiological adaptations apparently originating from the experimental transformation that created her.
    

The medical team was prepared for abnormalities.

They were not prepared for **this many.**

# 3. HEMATOLOGICAL ABNORMALITY

## FINDING: SPONTANEOUS INTRAVASCULAR BLOOD DEGRADATION

Neri's most severe physiological abnormality involves her blood.

Her blood does not remain stable indefinitely.

Instead, portions of the circulating blood progressively become **nonfunctional and appear to disintegrate while still inside her vascular system.**

Microscopic observation demonstrated an extremely unusual phenomenon.

Red blood cells would progressively lose structural integrity.

Rather than being removed normally by the body's filtration systems, portions of the blood appeared to:

> **lose cohesion → become nonfunctional → disperse → effectively disappear**

while remaining within the bloodstream.

In certain samples, the process visually resembled **water evaporating.**

Except it was occurring inside a living human-like circulatory system.

One physician reportedly stared at the microscopy display for approximately twenty seconds before asking:

> **"Is that supposed to happen?"**

The laboratory technician replied:

> **"No."**

> **"Like, medically no?"**

> **"Like, biologically impossible no."**

---

# 4. THE BLOOD DOES NOT REQUIRE EXERTION

The process is not reliably triggered by:

- Exercise.
    
- Combat.
    
- Blood loss.
    
- Malnutrition.
    
- Dehydration.
    
- Injury.
    
- Stress.
    
- Physical exertion.
    

It can occur while Neri is completely inactive.

She could spend an entire afternoon sitting in the cafeteria eating cake and still experience progressive blood degradation.

This explains why Neri's anemia can seemingly occur **without warning or obvious external cause.**

# 5. CARDIOVASCULAR CONSEQUENCES

As functional blood volume decreases, Neri's cardiovascular system compensates.

For a time.

Eventually, compensation fails.

The heart requires sufficient circulating blood to maintain itself.

Once Neri reaches a critical threshold:

> **CARDIOVASCULAR FAILURE OCCURS.**

This explains her history of fainting and previous near-fatal episodes.

In extreme cases, her heart may stop.

Importantly, the heart itself is not necessarily the original cause.

It is often the final system to fail.

The blood disappears first.

The cardiovascular system follows.

---

# 6. BAO COMPATIBILITY

Testing confirmed that blood from **Z-356-B "Bao"** remains uniquely compatible with Neri.

Bao's blood can temporarily restore sufficient functional circulation and assist in restarting Neri's failing cardiovascular system.

This remains the only consistently successful biological intervention identified to date.

The underlying degradation process, however, resumes afterward.

### MEDICAL CONCLUSION:

**Bao's blood does not cure Neri.**

It keeps her alive.

# 10. CARDIAC REGION

Particular attention was given to Neri's heart.

The surrounding tissue contains extensive old scarring consistent with previous trauma and experimentation.

The heart itself demonstrates unusual compensatory behavior.

It can continue functioning under conditions that would normally cause severe cardiovascular collapse.

However, once circulating functional blood drops below a critical threshold, compensation rapidly becomes insufficient.

This produces a dangerous cycle:

> **Blood degradation**  
> ↓  
> **Reduced oxygen delivery**  
> ↓  
> **Cardiovascular strain**  
> ↓  
> **Compensation**  
> ↓  
> **Further instability**  
> ↓  
> **Potential cardiac arrest**

This process can occur even without physical activity.
