# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL CASE REVIEW – AE-LK-01**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **CLINICAL REVIEW**

Following the voluntary separation between **Lumi** and Corin Lockwood, the Psychology Department conducted a joint review with C.O.R.E. and Medical Services.

The review concluded that Lumi's decision, while well-intentioned, had an unintended consequence.

It removed one of Corin's primary sources of day-to-day emotional support during a period in which she remained psychologically fragile.

The review does **not** attribute blame to Lumi.

Instead, it concludes that both mother and daughter acted from places of pain rather than malice.

---

# **CORIN'S CONDITION**

Corin has lived for years carrying profound trauma, grief, and persistent feelings that she does not deserve the life she has.

Those beliefs do not disappear simply because someone reminds her she is loved.

They require ongoing treatment, trusted relationships, and time.

Medical staff noted that Corin's greatest struggle is not with other people.

It is with herself.

As one psychologist summarized:

> "Corin spends every waking hour arguing with her own worth."

---

# **LUMI'S DECISION**

During an interview, Lumi explained why she honored Corin's request.

> "She asked for space."

> "I thought respecting her choice was the kindest thing I could do."

When informed that Corin's condition had worsened after the separation, Lumi became visibly distressed.

She lowered her head.

> "I thought..."

A long pause followed.

> "...I thought giving her room to breathe would help."

Another pause.

> "...I didn't realize she felt so alone."

Researchers concluded that Lumi underestimated how much Corin relied upon her quiet presence.

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ASSESSMENT**

Director Lucious reviewed the findings personally.

His written assessment stated:

> "This was not a failure of love."

> "It was a misunderstanding between a mother trying to help..."

> "...and a daughter who did not know how to ask for what she truly needed."

He continued:

> "Neither of them wanted distance."

> "Both believed distance would protect the other."

---

# **RECOMMENDATION**

The treatment team unanimously recommended that Lumi resume gentle contact with Corin.

Not to persuade.

Not to lecture.

Not to convince.

Simply...

To be present.

To sit with her during quiet evenings.

To remind her to eat.

To remind her to rest.

And, when words are unnecessary, to let Corin know she is not facing those long nights entirely by herself.

Clinicians emphasized that this support should continue alongside Corin's ongoing psychological care and the steady support of her family and colleagues.

---

# **STATUS**

Lumi expressed immediate agreement with the revised recommendation.

Her final statement during the review was brief.

> "Then I'll go back."

> "Not to change her."

> "Just to stay beside my daughter."

**End of Psychological Case Review**
