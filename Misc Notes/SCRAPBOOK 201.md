# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CRITICAL INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: SHELTER T-01 – DELAYED MEDICAL DISCOVERY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The following report documents the events surrounding the death of Corin Lockwood.

The incident resulted from a prolonged medical emergency that went unrecognized during a period of reduced contact with Shelter T-01.

A subsequent internal review found no evidence that personnel intentionally abandoned Corin.

Instead, multiple independent decisions—each made out of concern for respecting her wishes and avoiding further emotional harm—combined into a catastrophic failure to recognize that a medical emergency had occurred.

---

# **TIMELINE**

### **Day 0**

Corin exhausted her remaining prescribed medication.

No resupply request was submitted.

No reminder acknowledgement was received.

This absence was initially attributed to intermittent communication problems following the severe winter weather.

---

### **Day 1**

Corin lost consciousness inside Shelter T-01.

The white tiger remained beneath the treehouse.

The snowy owl continued flying between the treehouse and nearby Blacksite structures.

Personnel interpreted the animals' presence as normal behavior.

---

### **Days 2–5**

Several individuals considered visiting.

Each independently reached the same conclusion.

Corin had repeatedly requested space.

Recent attempts to help had visibly distressed her.

No one wanted to ignore those boundaries.

No one wanted to become the person who pushed too hard.

Routine drone deliveries continued.

Documents accumulated unopened.

Meals remained untouched.

The significance of these signs was not recognized.

---

### **Day 6**

A scheduled welfare check was proposed.

Operational priorities and severe travel conditions delayed its execution.

---

### **Day 7**

Personnel reached Shelter T-01.

The front door remained locked.

No response was received.

Emergency entry was authorized immediately.

Corin was found inside.

Medical examination determined she had died several days earlier.

---

# **INVESTIGATION FINDINGS**

The review concluded that no single individual was responsible.

Instead, multiple reasonable assumptions combined into an irreversible outcome.

Personnel believed they were respecting Corin's autonomy.

Medical Services believed Corin was managing her treatment.

Friends believed someone else had already checked on her.

Everyone believed there would still be another opportunity tomorrow.

There was not.

---

# **THE WHITE TIGER**

The white tiger was found lying beneath the treehouse entrance.

It resisted attempts to approach until Corin was recovered.

Afterward, the animal quietly moved aside.

It did not display aggression.

It simply remained where it had waited for days.

Veterinary personnel noted signs consistent with prolonged refusal to leave the immediate area.

---

# **THE SNOWY OWL**

The owl was observed on the branch overlooking the entrance.

It did not flee during the recovery operation.

Personnel later reviewed security footage showing the owl making repeated flights toward occupied areas of the Blacksite during the previous week.

At the time, no one understood the significance of its behavior.

---

# **FAMILY NOTIFICATION**

Lumi arrived shortly after confirmation.

She entered Shelter T-01 without speaking.

She remained beside Corin for an extended period.

Witnesses reported that Lumi neither cried nor shouted.

She simply held Corin's hand.

Eventually, she whispered,

> "...I'm here."

There was no answer.

Norma and Arcturus arrived later.

Neither entered immediately.

Both remained outside for several minutes before quietly walking inside together.

---

# **BOARD REVIEW**

The Executive Review Board determined that future welfare procedures for medically vulnerable personnel would be revised.

Recommendations included:

- Mandatory welfare checks following missed medication confirmations.
    
- Immediate investigation of prolonged unanswered deliveries.
    
- Escalation procedures when established daily routines cease unexpectedly.
    
- Recognition that respecting a person's boundaries must be balanced with responding to signs of a possible medical emergency.
    

The review concluded:

> **"Compassion without verification can become neglect."**

> **"Verification without compassion can become intrusion."**

> **"Future policy must ensure neither occurs alone."**

---

# **CASE STATUS**

Corin Lockwood is deceased.

Her office at the Krampus Compliance Authority remained exactly as she left it.

Shelter T-01 was secured following the investigation.

The white tiger continued waiting beneath the tree for several days after recovery operations concluded.

The snowy owl remained on the surrounding branches.

Neither animal appeared to understand why Corin no longer came outside.

**End of Critical Incident Report**
