# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **C.O.R.E. – ELDER MART IDENTIFICATION REPORT**

### **SUBJECT: FINAL TWO ELDER MARTS IDENTIFIED**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The final two unidentified Elder Marts have been positively identified.

The discovery occurred entirely by accident.

Both had spent their entire known existence blending into the KCA's Rapid Response Mart population.

They were finally exposed when both accidentally spoke fluent English while inside **Dialyn's office**.

---

# **THE INCIDENT**

Dialyn was working at her desk when several Rapid Response Marts entered the office.

Nothing unusual.

They regularly visit Dialyn, particularly when she has been working for extended periods.

Several Marts were gathered near the ventilation entrance while Dialyn continued working.

One Mart suddenly looked toward another and said:

> "I think she needs a break."

Dialyn stopped typing.

She slowly looked up.

The Mart froze.

A second Mart immediately responded:

> "Probably."

Silence.

Dialyn stared at them.

The two Marts stared back.

Neither moved.

---

# **THE REALIZATION**

Dialyn slowly pointed at the first Mart.

> "...You just spoke English."

The Mart looked at the second.

The second looked at the first.

After several seconds, the first quietly replied:

> "...Yes."

Dialyn immediately reached for her phone.

> "...Oh my god."

The second Mart apparently realized what had happened.

> "...We messed up."

Dialyn:

> "...YES."

---

# **IDENTIFICATION**

C.O.R.E. personnel were notified immediately.

Physical analysis confirmed the subtle characteristics associated with Elder Marts:

- Slightly larger body.
    
- Marginally more opaque blue coloration.
    
- Slightly deeper coloration under direct light.
    
- Identical deer antlers.
    
- Identical miniature black top hats.
    

Both were subsequently confirmed as the final two Elder Marts.

---

The five original Marts have finally been identified.

---

# **THE RAPID RESPONSE DISGUISE**

The two Elders had never deliberately impersonated anyone.

They simply became Rapid Response Marts naturally.

Their identical appearance made them functionally impossible to distinguish from the other security Marts.

They participated in:

- Ventilation patrols.
    
- Ambush preparation.
    
- Boulder Mode operations.
    
- Security drills.
    
- Threat response.
    

Neither displayed any special abilities beyond those already possessed by ordinary Marts.

Their greatest ability was apparently:

> **Not getting noticed.**

Dialyn later described the incident as:

> "...The weirdest five seconds of my entire week."

She explained that the two Marts had been quietly standing near her desk when one suddenly commented on her workload.

> "I think she needs a break."

Dialyn initially assumed she had misheard.

Then the second Mart responded.

> "Probably."

She immediately knew something was wrong.

> "...I have worked with Marts for long enough to know when something is extremely wrong."

# **FINAL STATUS**

The Elder Mart mystery is officially closed.

All five original Marts have been identified.

Three had been obvious in hindsight:

- Camera Mart.
    
- KCA Ambassador.
    
- Blacksite Ambassador.
    

The final two had spent years blending into the KCA's Rapid Response units.

They were never discovered through advanced Ether analysis.

They were never discovered through surveillance.

They were never discovered through behavioral profiling.

They were discovered because they accidentally forgot to say:

> "Mart."

And instead said:

> "I think she needs a break."

The investigation is now permanently closed.

The two Elders have returned to their normal duties.

They remain Rapid Response Marts.

They still wear the same tiny security hats.

And they have been specifically instructed by Dialyn:

> "...If you're going to talk in my office, at least say hello first."

Both Elders agreed.

> "Okay."
