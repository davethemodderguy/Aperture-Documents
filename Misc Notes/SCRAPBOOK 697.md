### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / MISSING PERSONNEL LOG**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — TEN WEEKS MISSING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Age:** Approximately 5 years old  
**Last Confirmed Location:** Blacksite Archives  
**Time Missing:** **10 WEEKS**  
**Status:** **NOT FOUND**

---

# **TEN WEEKS**

Ten weeks have passed since Alice disappeared.

There is still no confirmed sighting.

No reliable trail has been discovered.

No surveillance system has captured her leaving the Blacksite.

No abandoned shelter has been identified.

No remains have been found.

Nothing.

Alice simply remains **missing**.

The case has not been closed.

It will not be closed.

---

# **SEARCH OPERATIONS**

The initial search has long since expanded beyond the areas Alice was known to frequent.

Search teams have repeatedly inspected:

- The Treehouse and surrounding forest
    
- Blacksite residential sectors
    
- Mountain routes
    
- Underground passages
    
- Maintenance tunnels
    
- Abandoned structures
    
- Remote exterior regions
    
- Snow-covered valleys
    
- Natural caves
    
- Previously inaccessible areas
    

Every confirmed lead has ended without result.

Search teams continue to return.

And continue finding nothing.

---

# **THE PROBLEM**

Alice is not equipped to survive indefinitely.

She is approximately five years old.

Her stamina is extremely limited.

Her immune system is severely compromised.

Her body remains unusually fragile.

She requires food and water.

She requires warmth.

She requires protection from infection and environmental exposure.

Her previous survival of eight months should not be treated as evidence that she can survive another prolonged period alone.

That survival was already considered medically extraordinary.

Ten weeks without a confirmed supply source is deeply concerning.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin continues to search.

She has memorized Alice's usual routes.

She knows which places Alice liked.

She knows which places Alice avoided.

She knows how Alice walks when tired.

She knows how Alice hides when frightened.

She knows the places Alice would probably choose if she wanted to be alone.

She has checked all of them.

More than once.

Sometimes she simply sits in the Treehouse.

Alice's old place remains empty.

---

# **THE CARROTS**

The garden is still maintained.

Nobody officially assigned anyone to do it.

They simply do.

The carrots continue growing.

Alice originally learned gardening because Bunny loved carrots.

Now the residents continue growing them anyway.

One carrot is always left unpicked.

It has become an unofficial tradition.

> **"For Alice."**

---

# **BELLE**

Belle has stopped asking whether the search will find Alice.

She simply asks:

> **"Did anyone see her?"**

Every morning.

Every evening.

The answer has been the same.

> **"No."**

Belle still keeps a carrot with her.

It has been replaced several times.

The first one went bad weeks ago.

She refuses to admit that this bothers her.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has reviewed the Archive footage repeatedly.

Not the disappearance itself.

The moment before it.

Alice standing at the terminal.

Reading.

Scrolling.

Stopping.

Reading again.

Orphie has become fixated on one detail.

Alice never cried.

She never screamed.

She never appeared angry.

She simply read.

Then she left.

Orphie has concluded that this was worse.

Alice did not run away in anger.

She left after reaching a conclusion.

---

# **THE CONCLUSION**

The psychological department continues to believe Alice may have convinced herself that disappearing was an act of kindness.

That she was protecting everyone from having to care for her.

That her medical condition made her a burden.

That leaving would make the SAFEHOUSE happier.

This remains only a hypothesis.

But nobody has found a better explanation.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

The Treehouse has become strangely quiet.

Alice's bed remains untouched.

Her belongings remain where she left them.

Her gardening tools remain near the door.

A small blanket remains folded at the foot of her bed.

Nobody has moved it.

Corin has explicitly prohibited the removal of Alice's belongings.

> **"She needs to come back to somewhere that still looks like hers."**

---

# **MEDICAL DIRECTIVE**

If Alice is located, personnel are reminded:

**Do not force her to walk.**

**Do not ask her to keep up.**

**Do not question her about why she disappeared.**

**Do not immediately discuss her medical record.**

**Do not tell her how much damage her disappearance caused.**

**Do not make her feel responsible for the search.**

The first priority is stabilization.

The second is safety.

The third is reassurance.

Everything else can wait.

# **FINAL NOTE**

Ten weeks.

The Blacksite has continued functioning.

People still go to work.

Meals are still served.

Doors still open and close.

The SAFEHOUSE still has movie nights.

Life continues.

But there is an empty place at the table.

An empty bed.

An empty space beside Corin.

Nobody says it aloud anymore.

They simply leave room.

Because Alice is still missing.

And until someone proves otherwise, there is still a five-year-old girl somewhere out there who belongs to them.

The search continues.
