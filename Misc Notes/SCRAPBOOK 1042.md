# Executive Summary

Neri has filled her **12 TB SSD**.

All of it.

The entire drive.

**12 terabytes.**

Not with games.

Not with movies.

Not with photographs.

Not with downloaded music.

Not with miscellaneous files.

**Actual chemistry research.**

Four months after receiving the largest internal SSD ever installed in a personal Blacksite laptop, Neri exhausted its entire available capacity.

The incident resulted in an emergency meeting involving:

- Information Technology;
    
- Archives;
    
- Research & Development;
    
- Storage Administration;
    
- SAFEHOUSE Administration;
    
- and one extremely confused Neko.
    

The Archivist and Storage Specialist reportedly experienced an emotional breakdown upon determining what had consumed the storage.

The Storage Specialist's first response was:

> **"HOW?"**

The Archivist's response was:

> **"WHY?"**

Neko's response was:

> **"I told you she was serious about the chemistry."**

# V. NEKO'S REVIEW

Neko performed his own investigation.

He expected to find obvious inefficiencies.

Instead, he discovered that Neri had developed a research workflow involving enormous quantities of generated data.

She was not merely saving conclusions.

She was saving:

- intermediate calculations;
    
- failed models;
    
- alternate models;
    
- simulation results;
    
- comparisons;
    
- parameter variations;
    
- historical revisions;
    
- reference material;
    
- experimental observations;
    
- and multiple iterations of the same theoretical problem.
    

In other words:

**Neri was keeping the entire process.**

Neko's final assessment:

> **"She's not storing twelve terabytes of answers."**

> **"She's storing twelve terabytes of questions, attempts, failures, results, and things she hasn't figured out yet."**

---

# VI. THE PROBLEM

Neri's research cannot simply be deleted.

Several files are still actively referenced by current research.

Others document failed experiments and therefore possess archival value.

Some contain original theoretical work.

Several research chains depend upon previous iterations.

Deleting old material would potentially destroy useful scientific history.

The solution was therefore not:

> **"Delete some files."**

The solution was:

> **"Give her more storage."**

---

# **250 TB**

of storage capacity from the Blacksite's **unused server arrays**.

The allocation is reserved specifically for Neri's research archive.

### Reserved capacity
