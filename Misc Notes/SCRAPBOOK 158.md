### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE – SOCIAL OBSERVATION LOG**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – POST-RECOVERY SOCIAL BEHAVIOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following her return to Project SAFEHOUSE and gradual reinstatement to duty, Corin Lockwood's social behavior changed noticeably.

While she continues to spend time with all SAFEHOUSE residents, Security and Medical Services have observed that she most frequently remains in the company of three individuals:

- Belle Merryweather
    
- Keith ("Kent")
    
- Remielle ("Cinder")
    

The pattern has remained consistent over several weeks.

---

# **BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

Belle remains Corin's primary source of physical comfort.

Corin is frequently observed sitting beside Belle in the lounge, reading reports while leaning against her shoulder.

When overwhelmed, Corin rarely asks for help verbally.

Instead, she quietly moves closer to Belle.

Belle understands the gesture immediately.

Sometimes she simply places an arm around Corin.

Sometimes she hands her a warm mug of tea.

Sometimes she says nothing at all.

Corin has commented on the arrangement only once.

> "...You're warm."

Belle smiled.

> "You're welcome to steal my warmth whenever you need it."

---

# **KEITH ("KENT")**

Kent has become someone Corin feels unusually comfortable sitting with.

Neither speaks very much.

They often share the lounge in complete silence.

Kent drinks coffee.

Corin drinks warm milk or tea.

Occasionally one of them makes a brief observation.

That is usually enough.

Medical Services note that Corin appears significantly less tense around Kent, likely due to his calm demeanor and tendency not to pressure conversation.

Kent summarized it simply.

> "Sometimes people don't need advice."

> "Sometimes they just don't want to be alone."

---

# **REMIELLE ("CINDER")**

Cinder and Corin frequently work together on administrative tasks.

Unlike most people, Cinder rarely treats Corin delicately.

She hands Corin paperwork.

Asks questions.

Complains about budgets.

Makes dry jokes about accounting.

Treats Corin as though nothing about her is unusual.

Corin has responded positively to this ordinary interaction.

One overheard exchange:

> **Cinder:** "Belle exceeded the snack budget again."

Corin sighed.

> "...Again?"

> **Cinder:** "Again."

Corin quietly smiled before reaching for her calculator.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION**

Residents have noticed that Corin naturally gravitates toward the three throughout the day.

If Belle is reading, Corin often sits nearby.

If Kent is making coffee, Corin may prepare tea at the same time.

If Cinder is sorting paperwork, Corin frequently offers to help before returning to Accounting.

None of the three actively encourage the behavior.

They simply leave room beside them.

Corin chooses to fill it.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi observed the group from across the lounge one afternoon.

Belle was reading.

Kent had a mug of coffee.

Cinder was reviewing schedules.

Corin sat quietly among them, balancing a folder on her knees while occasionally joining the conversation.

Nobody was talking about Ether.

Nobody was talking about experiments.

Nobody was talking about trauma.

They were discussing grocery expenses.

Lumi smiled.

> "That's exactly what she needs."

---

---

> "...I missed that."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Medical Services consider Corin's tendency to remain near Belle, Kent, and Cinder to be a healthy expression of trust rather than dependency.

She continues to broaden her interactions with the rest of Project SAFEHOUSE while maintaining close relationships with the three people who have consistently provided calm, stability, and acceptance during her recovery.

For Corin, they are not simply companions.

They have become people she no longer feels the need to run away from.
