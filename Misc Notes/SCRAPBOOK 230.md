# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **EMERGENCY MEDICAL & RESEARCH BULLETIN**

## **SUBJECT: THE CORIN PARADOX – EFFECTS OF PROLONGED SEPARATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – EXECUTIVE EYES ONLY

---

# **OVERVIEW**

This bulletin documents the consequences of the continued disappearance of Corey Lockwood.

At the time of writing, Corey has been missing for ██ days.

Despite exhaustive search efforts, no confirmed trace has been recovered.

The Corin Paradox has now entered a state previously considered theoretical.

The effects of prolonged separation are no longer hypothetical.

They are observable.

---

# **CORIN LOCKWOOD**

Corin's condition has deteriorated beyond all prior medical projections.

The decline is not merely psychological.

It is physiological.

Neurological.

Anomalous.

Medical personnel unanimously agree that Corin is no longer experiencing ordinary grief.

She appears to be losing the stabilizing influence that the paradox itself depends upon.

---

# **OBSERVED EFFECTS**

The following symptoms have been documented.

### Cognitive Decline

Corin has increasing difficulty concentrating on routine tasks.

She frequently stops in the middle of conversations.

She occasionally forgets where she is.

Several times, she has searched rooms while quietly calling Corey's name before realizing she was alone.

---

### Emotional Collapse

Corin has become almost completely withdrawn.

The small moments of optimism Corey routinely encouraged have vanished.

Personnel have reported that Corin no longer responds to jokes, celebrations, or attempts at conversation.

She speaks only when necessary.

---

### Behavioral Changes

Corin has resumed prolonged self-isolation.

Meals are often skipped.

Sleep has become irregular.

She repeatedly returns to locations Corey commonly visited, remaining there for extended periods.

The treehouse has once again become almost silent.

---

### Physical Instability

Medical examinations reveal intermittent anomalies with Corin's own body.

Episodes include:

- Unexplained drops in body temperature.
    
- Temporary loss of detectable heartbeat.
    
- Brief interruptions in measurable blood circulation.
    
- Sudden weakness without identifiable medical cause.
    

These events resolve spontaneously.

No conventional diagnosis explains them.

Research believes they are directly linked to the destabilization of the paradox.

---

# **THE PARADOX**

Research Division has reached a troubling conclusion.

Corin and Corey did not merely support one another emotionally.

They continuously stabilized each other's existence.

The paradox appears to function as a single interconnected system despite occupying two physical bodies.

Removing one component does not immediately destroy the other.

Instead...

The remaining manifestation begins to lose structural stability over time.

How long this process continues before becoming irreversible is unknown.

---

# **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE**

The atmosphere within SAFEHOUSE has noticeably changed.

Common areas remain occupied.

Movies still play.

Meals are still prepared.

Work continues.

Yet personnel consistently describe the residence as quieter.

No one has moved Corin's usual chair.

No one has touched Corey's favorite mug.

The hope that Corey may still return has prevented anyone from treating her absence as permanent.

---

# **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

**Lumi**

Lumi has participated in every search operation since Corey's disappearance.

When ordered to rest, she refused.

Her response was recorded.

> "She lost one family."

> "I'm not letting her lose another."

---

**Star**

Star has resumed searching the surrounding forests every evening.

She has become visibly exhausted.

When asked why she continues personally despite dedicated search teams, she answered quietly:

> "...Hope isn't something you delegate."

---

**Nightshade**

Nightshade has expanded security patrols far beyond standard operational limits.

She has not commented on Corin's condition.

Personnel note that she has become increasingly intolerant of interruptions during search operations.

---

**Neko**

Neko has suspended all multiversal research.

Every available computational resource is now dedicated to locating Corey.

He has spoken very little since the disappearance.

---

**Miss Neko**

She approved every request for additional search resources without objection.

The administrative record contains no denials.

---

**Taiga**

Taiga continues sleeping beneath the treehouse rather than returning to SAFEHOUSE.

When questioned, she answered simply:

> "...If Corey comes back..."

She looked toward the empty doorway.

> "...I don't want her to think nobody waited."

---

**Mart**

The library log records books being checked out under Corey's name.

The entries were made by Mart.

The books remain unread.

He returns them on their due date.

Then checks them out again.

---

**Belle**

Fresh cookies continue appearing at the treehouse every few days.

Most remain untouched.

Belle continues bringing them anyway.

---

# **EXECUTIVE ASSESSMENT**

Director Lucious has elevated the search to **Priority Omega-Black**.

His closing remarks during the emergency briefing were entered into the official record.

> "This report confirms what we feared."

> "Separating the paradox doesn't simply separate two people."

> "It begins destroying what they are."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corey Lockwood remains missing.

Corin Lockwood remains alive.

Research can no longer guarantee that those two statements will remain compatible indefinitely.

For the first time since the Corin Paradox was understood, Executive Command has acknowledged an uncomfortable possibility.

If Corey is not found...

The paradox itself may begin to fail.

And if the paradox fails...

Humanity may lose not one Lockwood...

But both.

**Case Status:** **PRIORITY OMEGA-BLACK – ACTIVE SEARCH CONTINUES**
