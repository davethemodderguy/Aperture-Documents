# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **SUPPLEMENTAL DISCOVERY REPORT**

### **SUBJECT: RECOVERED EQUIPMENT FROM DIALYN'S PERSONAL STORAGE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Executive Personnel & Security Division

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the discovery of Dialyn's archived Central Execution Department identification card, Security Division conducted a complete inventory of the remaining contents of the metal storage box.

Hidden beneath several folded documents was a rigid polymer rifle case.

The case was locked.

Dialyn voluntarily unlocked it upon request.

Inside was a single firearm.

She immediately recognized it.

> "...I haven't seen this in years."

---

# **RECOVERED FIREARM**

**Manufacturer:** Aperture Industries

**Model:** **MX-R**

**Configuration:** Assault Rifle / Designated Marksman Rifle Hybrid

**Operating Platform:** Enhanced M4A1 Derivative

**Status:** Decommissioned Personal Weapon

---

# **DESCRIPTION**

The MX-R is an Aperture Industries modernization of the traditional M4A1 platform, designed to transition rapidly between close-quarters engagements and precision fire.

Dialyn's personal configuration includes:

- Variable magnification optic.
    
- Offset backup iron sights.
    
- Free-floating handguard.
    
- Enhanced trigger assembly.
    
- Adjustable precision stock.
    
- Suppressor-compatible muzzle device.
    
- Ambidextrous controls.
    
- Reinforced bolt carrier group.
    

The finish shows almost no corrosion.

Mechanical wear is consistent with regular professional maintenance rather than neglect.

---

# **CONDITION**

Despite years in storage, the rifle remains in exceptional condition.

Inspection concluded:

- Barrel condition: Excellent.
    
- Bolt assembly: Excellent.
    
- Gas system: Excellent.
    
- Optics: Zero retained.
    
- Furniture: Minimal cosmetic wear.
    
- Internal components: Properly lubricated.
    

Security personnel described the weapon as looking "ready for inspection."

Dialyn admitted she had cleaned it before placing it into storage.

She simply never expected to open the case again.

---

# **HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE**

CED archives identify this MX-R as Dialyn's primary service rifle during her time operating under the callsign **Fairy**.

Mission reports repeatedly reference the rifle's adaptability during escort operations, Hollow reconnaissance, and long-range overwatch.

Although the platform has since been surpassed by newer Aperture weapon systems, it remains a reliable and highly capable design.

# **DIALYN'S STATEMENT**

When asked why she had never disposed of the rifle, Dialyn rested a hand on the closed case.

After a long silence, she replied:

> "...It reminded me of someone."

Belle asked quietly:

> "...Fairy?"

Dialyn smiled faintly.

> "...No."

Another pause.

> "...The girl who was trying her best to survive."

She gently latched the case shut.

> "...She deserves to be remembered."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The MX-R has been catalogued as Dialyn's personal historical property.

It is not issued for routine duty and remains stored in its protective case under secure conditions.

At Dialyn's request, the rifle has been preserved rather than retired or destroyed.

To Security Division, it is a well-maintained firearm.

To Dialyn, it is a reminder of a chapter she no longer wishes to return to—

But one she refuses to forget.
