# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-PSY-018**

### **SUPPLEMENTAL FINDINGS – BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Restricted Psychological Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following a joint review by Aperture psychological staff and Krampus Compliance Authority medical personnel, a previously accepted working theory regarding Belle Merryweather's condition has been revised.

Evidence no longer supports the hypothesis that residual Ether is the primary source of her psychological decline.

Instead, current findings indicate that the Ether has demonstrated an unexpected interaction with Belle's mental state.

---

## **ETHER RESPONSE**

Repeated medical examinations recorded increased Ether activity whenever Belle experienced severe emotional distress.

Contrary to initial expectations, the Ether did not amplify the distress.

On multiple occasions, neurological scans showed heightened activity associated with positive autobiographical memory.

Recorded recollections included:

- Room N-07.
    
- The Starlands retreat.
    
- Meals shared with the Merryweather family.
    
- Time spent with the Friendly Roommates.
    
- Quiet conversations at Heaven's Blacksite.
    
- Simple moments of peace.
    

Researchers concluded that the Ether consistently attempted to reinforce memories associated with Belle's current life.

Its activity appeared stabilizing rather than destructive.

One physician summarized the finding as follows:

> _"Whatever the Ether has become inside Belle, it repeatedly favors memories connected to safety, belonging, and companionship."_

---

## **REASSESSMENT OF THE TRIGGERING INCIDENT**

Attention subsequently returned to the interaction with the researcher.

Witness statements initially described the event as workplace harassment involving repeated use of Belle's former name.

Further review of surveillance footage and interviews suggested the incident extended beyond casual mockery.

The researcher deliberately continued after observing Belle's visible distress.

He repeatedly invoked details connected to her past despite clear indications that she wanted him to stop.

Reviewing psychologists concluded that the interaction represented a severe psychological provocation.

The specific remarks beyond those already documented remain restricted due to their highly sensitive nature.

---

## **CLINICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological staff determined that the incident did not merely remind Belle of her past.

It reopened unresolved traumatic memories that she had spent years trying to leave behind.

The emotional impact was described as disproportionate to a simple insult because it directly targeted experiences she had never fully healed from.

One clinician wrote:

> _"This was not an isolated hurtful comment."_

> _"It struck at the foundation of the identity Belle had painstakingly rebuilt."_

---

## **CURRENT UNDERSTANDING**

The available evidence supports the following conclusions:

- Residual Ether is not considered the primary cause of Belle's behavioral changes.
    
- The Ether consistently reacts by reinforcing memories associated with Belle's present life and emotional attachments.
    
- The triggering workplace incident caused profound psychological harm extending well beyond ordinary verbal harassment.
    
- Belle's subsequent withdrawal is assessed as the result of unresolved trauma resurfacing rather than Ether-induced personality alteration.
    

---

## **STATUS**

The investigation into the triggering incident has been archived under restricted psychological records.

Belle's condition continues to be monitored through joint medical and psychological evaluation.

The revised findings replace earlier theories attributing her decline primarily to Ether activity.

**End of Supplement.**
