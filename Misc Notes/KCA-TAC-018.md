## **SUMMARY**

Among KCA personnel, two individuals have earned a particular reputation in hand-to-hand combat:

- **Director Belle Merryweather**
    
- **Executive Secretary Cinder**
    

Although both are commonly associated with their primary weapons, operators are reminded that **disarming them does not make them harmless**.

If anything, the opposite may be true.

---

# **DIRECTOR BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

### **Primary Weapon**

Hybrid Greatsword (Approx. 50 kg)

Most personnel assume Belle relies entirely on her sword.

This assumption is incorrect.

Belle possesses extraordinary physical strength, further amplified by her unique Ether integration.

When forced into unarmed combat, she favors straightforward, high-impact strikes.

Documented techniques include:

- Heavy haymakers.
    
- Shoulder tackles.
    
- Elbow strikes.
    
- Grappling.
    
- Powerful dropkicks.
    
- Throws using her opponent's momentum.
    

Despite her soft, plush-like body, the force behind her punches has been compared to a vehicle collision.

One documented incident involved Belle striking an individual who had assaulted her.

The single punch fractured multiple bones, including ribs and the pelvis.

Medical review concluded the injuries were consistent with an extremely high-energy blunt impact.

---

# **EXECUTIVE SECRETARY CINDER**

### **Primary Weapon**

Multi-Purpose Tactical Briefcase (55 kg)

If deprived of her briefcase, Cinder immediately transitions to reinforced gauntlets.

Her fighting style changes completely.

Documented techniques include:

- Boxing combinations.
    
- Uppercuts.
    
- Kickboxing.
    
- Knee strikes.
    
- Dropkicks.
    
- Clinch fighting.
    
- Environmental attacks using walls, floors, and nearby structures.
    

Historical combat footage shows Cinder maintaining relentless offensive pressure until every confirmed hostile combatant has been neutralized.

Unlike Belle's measured approach, Cinder's gauntlet doctrine prioritizes immediate elimination of active threats.
