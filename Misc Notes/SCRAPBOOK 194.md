# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – RESURGENCE OF TRAUMA RESPONSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Despite significant improvements in Corin Lockwood's condition, Medical Services have documented a resurgence of depressive symptoms.

The change was unexpected by some personnel.

Corin had begun smiling more.

She accepted visitors.

She shared meals with the white tiger.

She allowed the owl to steal her food without complaint.

She laughed at Neko's failed inventions.

To many residents of Project SAFEHOUSE, Corin appeared to finally be moving forward.

However...

Healing did not erase what happened.

---

# **THE PAST RETURNED**

Psychological Services noted that Corin's recovery created a difficult contradiction.

For the first time in years, she had enough safety to process memories that she previously survived by suppressing.

The person she had become was finally able to look back at the person she used to be.

And she remembered.

The procedures.

The isolation.

The loss of her original appearance.

The feeling of being treated as something disposable rather than someone alive.

The memories did not return as new wounds.

They returned as old wounds finally being felt.

---

# **OBSERVED BEHAVIOR**

Recent observations include:

- Spending longer periods alone inside Shelter T-01.
    
- Becoming unusually quiet during conversations.
    
- Staring at old photographs for extended periods.
    
- Avoiding mirrors.
    
- Apologizing unnecessarily.
    
- Refusing to accept praise.
    

Personnel noted that Corin's kindness toward others remained unchanged.

The difference was that she had become much harsher toward herself.

---

# **THE CONTRADICTION**

Several residents struggled to understand the change.

They saw:

A person who cared for injured animals.

A person who shared her food.

A person who patiently listened to others.

A person who welcomed visitors into her home.

Corin saw something else.

A reminder of everything taken from her.

---

# **SHELTER T-01 OBSERVATION**

During one evening observation, Corin sat beneath the treehouse with the white tiger resting beside her.

The owl watched from above.

Both animals remained close.

Neither moved.

Corin quietly spoke:

> "Why are you both still here?"

No response was expected.

The tiger simply lowered its head beside her.

The owl blinked from the branch.

After several minutes, Corin placed a hand against the tiger's fur.

> "...I don't understand."

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi was among the first to notice.

Not because Corin stopped smiling.

But because she noticed when the smiles became forced.

During a visit, Lumi found Corin sitting outside with untouched tea beside her.

She did not ask Corin to be happy.

She did not tell her to forget.

She simply sat beside her.

After a long silence, Corin whispered:

> "I thought I was better."

Lumi answered:

> "You are."

Corin looked away.

> "Then why does it still hurt?"

Lumi remained quiet for a moment.

Then answered:

> "Because it mattered."

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services determined that Corin's depressive episode does not represent a failure of recovery.

The return of painful memories is a common part of processing severe trauma.

Corin has built connections.

She has built a home.

She has built trust.

Those achievements remain real even during periods where she cannot feel them.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin Lockwood remains a resident of Shelter T-01.

She continues working for the Krampus Compliance Authority.

She continues caring for the animals that chose to stay beside her.

She continues receiving visitors.

The cheerful Corin everyone knows is still there.

So is the hurt child she used to be.

The current objective is not to erase one in favor of the other.

It is to help them exist together.

**End of Psychological Observation Report**
