# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **MEDICAL / ANOMALOUS CONDITION RECORD**

# **THE SILENCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Condition:** **Complete Loss of Voice**  
**Cause:** **UNKNOWN**  
**Reversal Method:** **UNKNOWN**  
**Current Communication:** Written communication  
**Status:** **PERSISTENT / LONG-TERM**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has lost the ability to speak.

There was no clear incident.

No obvious injury.

No identifiable Ether surge.

No detectable damage to her throat.

No known anomalous attack.

She simply stopped being able to produce a voice.

Attempts to determine the cause have produced no useful results.

More concerningly, none of the known Architects have been able to determine how to reverse it safely.

For the foreseeable future:

**Corin is mute.**

---

# **THE FIRST MORNING**

Corin attempted to speak.

Nothing came out.

She tried again.

Nothing.

Her throat functioned.

Her breathing was normal.

There was no visible obstruction.

She could swallow.

She could breathe.

She simply could not produce sound.

Corin stared at Orphie.

Orphie immediately understood something was wrong.

> **"Corin?"**

Corin opened her mouth.

Silence.

She reached for her notebook.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

Her first written message was short:

> **I can't talk.**

Orphie stared at the sentence.

Then at Corin.

> **"Can you make any sound?"**

Corin shook her head.

She tried again.

Nothing.

She wrote:

> **No.**

---

# **ARCHITECTAL EXAMINATION**

Guiding Light examined Corin first.

Nothing obvious could be found.

Curious Light followed.

His examination was considerably more extensive.

Nothing.

Mischevious Light was eventually informed.

He immediately stopped joking.

The three Architects discussed the condition together.

None could provide an answer.

---

# **THE PROBLEM**

The condition does not behave like conventional vocal-cord damage.

Corin's throat is physically capable of functioning.

Her respiratory system remains stable.

Her Ether remains present.

Her perception is intact.

Her cognitive functions remain intact.

Yet the mechanism required to produce speech simply does not occur.

Attempts to force restoration have been rejected.

Guiding Light refuses to risk another alteration to Corin's already fragile system without understanding what is wrong.

Curious Light cannot identify what needs to be repaired.

Mischevious Light cannot enforce a solution against a problem that has no identifiable cause.

For once, none of them have an answer.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light was visibly frustrated.

Not with Corin.

With herself.

She could restore color.

She could protect Corin.

She could alter perception.

But this was different.

She could not safely determine what was missing.

She eventually told Corin:

> **"I don't know how to fix this."**

Corin looked at her.

Then wrote:

> **It's okay.**

Guiding Light knew it wasn't.

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT**

Curious Light initially wanted to investigate further.

Then he noticed Corin's expression.

He stopped.

This was not an interesting anomaly.

It was another thing happening **to her.**

He closed his notes.

> **"We'll figure it out."**

Corin wrote:

> **"You don't know that."**

Curious Light paused.

> **"...No. I don't."**

---

# **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT**

Mischevious Light had very little to say.

For once, he did not make a joke.

He sat beside Corin.

> **"We'll keep looking."**

Corin wrote:

> **"You have work."**

> **"I know."**

> **"You're busy."**

> **"I know."**

He looked at her.

> **"Doesn't mean I can't come back."**

Corin looked down at the notebook.

She wrote nothing.

---

# **A NEW REASON TO HATE HERSELF**

The loss of her voice has affected Corin more severely than the physical symptoms suggest.

She already hated her body.

She already disliked the scars.

She already struggled with her reanimated state.

Now she cannot even speak.

Her notebook has begun accumulating increasingly negative statements.

One entry reads:

> **Another thing wrong with me.**

Another:

> **Why can't my body just work?**

Then:

> **I can't even talk anymore.**

The final sentence on that page:

> **"What's wrong with me?"**

---

# **COMMUNICATION**

Corin now carries her notebook almost constantly.

She uses it for:

- Conversations.
    
- Requests.
    
- Questions.
    
- Expressing discomfort.
    
- Writing observations.
    
- Communicating with Orphie.
    
- Communicating with the Architects.
    
- Writing down things she would normally say aloud.
    

She has also started using gestures more frequently.

A nod.

A shake of the head.

A point.

A wave.

A written sentence.

Her world has become quieter.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

The treehouse has adapted.

A small writing board now sits near the kitchen.

Paper is kept in several rooms.

Pens are placed where Corin can easily reach them.

Orphie keeps spare notebooks available.

Guiding Light occasionally leaves written messages for her even when she is physically present.

Not because she has to.

Because she knows Corin sometimes misses hearing a voice.

---

# **THE BLUE JAY**

Jay has noticed the change.

He occasionally tilts his head when Corin attempts to speak and produces nothing.

One afternoon, he chirped.

Corin looked at him.

She tried to imitate the sound.

Nothing came out.

Jay chirped again.

Corin wrote:

> **"You're doing enough talking for both of us."**

Guiding Light laughed quietly.

Corin smiled.

---

# **THE LONG TERM**

There is currently no estimated recovery date.

The condition may last:

**Weeks.**

**Months.**

Possibly longer.

Nobody can honestly promise Corin that her voice will return.

Nobody can honestly promise that it won't.

For now, she must remain mute.

And the Architects must continue searching without putting her life at risk.

---

# **FINAL ASSESSMENT**

Corin has lost something incredibly ordinary.

Her voice.

There is no dramatic explanation.

No known culprit.

No obvious injury.

No reliable treatment.

And perhaps most frustratingly:

**Even the Architects don't know what to do.**

Corin already had difficulty accepting her body.

Now she has one more thing to resent about herself.

But her voice is gone.

**Corin is not.**

Until her voice returns, the treehouse will simply have to listen differently.

And if it never returns, they will learn.

They will read her handwriting.

They will understand her gestures.

They will wait for her answers.

They will continue talking to her.

Because silence does not mean Corin has stopped being heard.

**End of Medical / Anomalous Condition Record**
