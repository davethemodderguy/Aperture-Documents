# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / FAMILY DYNAMICS CRISIS REPORT**

# **THE ONE THING THAT MADE IT WORSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Critical Personnel Welfare  
**Subjects:** Corin / Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Lumi / Arcturus / Norma / SAFEHOUSE Residents  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **CRITICAL — EMOTIONAL SUPPORT COLLAPSE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has identified a single factor that has consistently amplified Corin's deterioration.

It is not the loss of her position.

It is not the isolation.

It is not the Marts refusing to approach her.

It is not even the psychological instability itself.

It is this:

> **Orphie hates her.**

For Corin, that realization has become devastating.

---

# **THE DIFFERENCE**

Corin had endured Orphie's disappearance.

She endured two weeks of uncertainty.

She endured the fear that Orphie was dead.

She endured waiting.

She endured watching Orphie return and remain emotionally distant.

She endured losing her job.

She endured losing the Marts' companionship.

She endured her own mind turning against itself.

But somewhere underneath everything, one belief remained:

> **"Orphie still loves me."**

That belief is now gone.

---

# **ORPHIE'S POSITION**

Orphie no longer hides her feelings.

When asked directly:

> **"Do you still care about Corin?"**

Orphie answered:

> "...No."

When asked:

> **"Do you hate her?"**

Orphie responded:

> "...Yes."

No hesitation.

No qualification.

---

# **CORIN HEARS IT**

Corin eventually heard Orphie say it.

She did not react immediately.

She simply stared at her.

> "...You hate me?"

Orphie:

> "...Yes."

Corin remained silent.

> "...Why?"

Orphie:

> "...Because I can't stand what you've become."

Corin lowered her head.

> "...Oh."

That was all.

---

# **THE INTERNAL CONFLICT**

Inside Corin, the two sides immediately reacted.

### **Sane Corin**

> **"She doesn't mean it."**

> **"She's angry."**

> **"She's still your sister."**

> **"People say things when they're angry."**

### **Insane Corin**

> **"She hates you."**

> **"She finally said it."**

> **"You knew."**

> **"Everyone leaves."**

> **"You ruined everything."**

The stable side tried to fight back.

> **"She came back."**

The response:

> **"Because someone found her."**

> **"She didn't want to return."**

Silence.

---

# **THE DAMAGE**

Corin's already fragile emotional stability deteriorated further.

She stopped trying to defend herself.

She stopped arguing.

She stopped asking Orphie for reassurance.

She stopped expecting affection.

The belief that had kept her connected to reality was gone.

---

# **CORIN'S THOUGHT**

Later that night, Corin sat alone.

She whispered:

> "...She really hates me."

A pause.

> "...So I really am a burden."

The thought repeated.

> "...I'm a burden."

Again.

> "...I'm a burden."

Again.

> "...I'm a burden."

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie remained on the other side of the room.

She heard Corin.

She did not respond.

She turned away.

She pulled her blanket closer.

Then went to sleep on the floor.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi eventually confronted Orphie.

> "...You know what that did to her."

Orphie:

> "...She needed to hear the truth."

Lumi:

> "...She needed her sister."

Orphie:

> "...I'm not responsible for her."

Lumi's expression became deeply saddened.

> "...You may not be responsible for everything that happened to her."

A pause.

> "...But you are the person she still looks for."

Orphie said nothing.

---

# **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus was furious.

> "...You don't have to love her."

Orphie looked at him.

> "...Good."

> "...But why do you have to make sure she knows you hate her?"

Orphie:

> "...Because it's how I feel."

Arcturus:

> "...And you think she can survive hearing that?"

Orphie did not answer.

---

# **NORMA**

Norma understood the problem immediately.

> "...Corin has nothing left to hold onto."

Orphie:

> "...That's not my problem."

Norma:

> "...It became your problem the moment you became the person she built her world around."

Orphie looked away.

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts have not returned.

They remain frightened of Corin.

They are also wary of Orphie.

The colony has effectively withdrawn from the entire situation.

One Mart was overheard saying:

> "...Too sad."

Another:

> "...Too scary."

A third:

> "...No go."

---

# **THE SAFEHOUSE**

The SAFEHOUSE has become painfully quiet whenever the sisters are mentioned.

Star cannot accept what is happening.

Nightshade continues calming her.

Bao worries silently.

Frost watches.

Astral understands that this is no longer something simple encouragement can solve.

Everyone knows that Corin is deteriorating.

Everyone knows that Orphie is making it worse.

And nobody knows how to make Orphie care again.

---

# **THE CRUEL IRONY**

Orphie once believed:

> **"I am an orphan."**

She believed she was unwanted.

She believed she was insignificant.

She believed nobody should have to carry her.

So she disappeared.

Corin broke trying to find her.

Orphie returned.

And now Orphie has become the person telling Corin:

> **"I don't want you."**

The exact rejection Orphie once feared for herself...

is now being experienced by Corin.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's mind was already fighting itself.

Reality against distortion.

Hope against hopelessness.

Memory against fear.

Then came the one thing that the stable side could not explain away.

Orphie came back.

Orphie was alive.

Orphie was physically there.

And yet she looked at Corin and said:

> **"...I hate you."**

There was no misunderstanding.

No disappearance.

No uncertainty.

No possibility that Corin had imagined it.

Her sister was there.

And her sister hated her.

For Corin, that sentence became heavier than everything that came before it.

The internal battle continues.

But one side has gained a devastating piece of evidence.

> **"See?"**

> **"Even she doesn't want you."**

**End of Family Dynamics Crisis Report**
