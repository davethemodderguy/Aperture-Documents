# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **KCA-SEC-044**

### **EXECUTIVE SECURITY INCIDENT REPORT – ATTACK ON DIRECTOR BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Executive Security Archive

**Date:** ██/██/████

**Status:** Attack Repelled

---

## **SUMMARY**

While escorting Director Belle Merryweather from Krampus Compliance Authority Headquarters to the Aperture Industries dimensional transit point, the executive protection detail came under a coordinated armed attack.

The assault is believed to have been carried out by remnants of an organized Ether trafficking network seeking to capture the Director before her departure to Heaven's Blacksite.

The attack failed.

Director Belle Merryweather sustained no injuries.

Executive Protection Officer **Keith** and Executive Secretary **Cinder** successfully executed emergency protection procedures, allowing Belle to reach the transit point safely.

---

## **INITIAL CONTACT**

At approximately ██:██ local time, three civilian vehicles simultaneously blocked the convoy's route.

Within seconds, multiple armed assailants emerged from surrounding buildings.

Keith immediately recognized the ambush.

> **"Director! Cover!"

Belle instinctively stepped behind the armored vehicle.

Keith moved in front of her without hesitation.

---

## **Protective Response**

Keith drew his modified M1014 combat shotgun.

Rather than pursuing the attackers, he remained close to Belle, methodically engaging only those who directly threatened her.

His priority remained unchanged throughout the encounter.

**Protect the Director.**

Not eliminate every hostile.

---

## **Administrative Response**

Inside the escort vehicle, Cinder remained remarkably composed.

Instead of panicking, she immediately initiated emergency procedures.

Actions included:

- Activating the convoy distress beacon.
    
- Contacting the Central Execution Department.
    
- Alerting nearby Banlands security forces.
    
- Locking down the transit route.
    
- Transmitting the attackers' positions to responding units.
    

Only after completing every emergency notification did she retrieve her tactical briefcase.

With practiced efficiency, it unfolded into its compact submachine gun configuration.

She exited the vehicle and took up a defensive position beside Belle.

---

## **Director's Response**

Belle removed the 50-kilogram hybrid sword from her back.

The blade struck the pavement with a heavy metallic impact.

She glanced toward Keith.

> **"...Let's get home."

Keith nodded.

> **"...Yes, Director."

Belle did not pursue fleeing attackers.

Instead, she remained within Keith's protective perimeter while defending herself against anyone who broke through.

---

## **Arrival of Reinforcements**

Within minutes, Central Execution Department response units reached the area.

The remaining attackers attempted to retreat.

Several were apprehended.

Others escaped into the city.

Recovered equipment included:

- Illegal Ether canisters.
    
- Contraband communications equipment.
    
- Unregistered firearms.
    
- Documents referencing the black-market bounty on Endmin Merryweather.
    

The investigation remains ongoing.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

No injuries were sustained by Belle, Keith, or Cinder.

Security footage confirmed that the executive protection detail followed established procedures throughout the attack.

The incident has since become a training case for both the Central Execution Department and the Krampus Compliance Authority, illustrating that successful executive protection depends not only on force of arms, but also on discipline, communication, and teamwork.

The final note in the report reads:

> **"The attackers planned for one target."**

> **"They failed to account for the people who stand beside her."**
