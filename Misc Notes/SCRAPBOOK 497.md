# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **RESTRICTED ANOMALOUS WEATHER / SAFEHOUSE INCIDENT LOG**

# **WHEN THE STORM FINALLY STOPPED**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **ANOMALOUS SUPPRESSION STORM — TERMINATED**  
**Status:** **CONTACT RESTORED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The storm stopped without warning.

After days of continuous snowfall, the anomalous suppression field simply disappeared.

The snow ceased.

The interference vanished.

Anomalous abilities returned.

Communications were restored.

The route to the treehouse became accessible again.

The response was immediate.

Nobody waited for official authorization.

---

# **THE RUSH**

Guiding Light manifested almost instantly.

Her shattered wings illuminated the clearing.

Curious Light followed.

Orphie was already moving before anyone could stop her.

Lumi joined them.

The SAFEHOUSE residents followed in rapid succession.

Neko.

Miss Neko.

Niko.

Nightshade.

Belle.

Endmin.

Frost.

Bao.

Taiga.

Mart.

Astral.

Lucy, Kent, and Remielle were not far behind.

Nobody spoke much during the journey.

They all knew what they might find.

Nobody was prepared for it.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

The door opened.

Silence.

No movement.

No voice.

No footsteps.

No familiar greeting from Corin.

The room looked almost exactly as it had been left.

Empty plates.

Empty cups.

The old blue notes.

The notebook.

Clothes scattered around the room.

And in the corner—

**Corin.**

---

# **CORIN**

She was lying on the floor.

Completely motionless.

Her pale body was curled slightly toward the wall.

Her chalky lime hair had fallen across her face.

Her red wool hat was nearby.

Her scarf had slipped partially away.

Her coat was rumpled.

Her body was cold.

Beside her lay her detached left arm.

For several seconds, nobody moved.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie froze.

Her expression changed immediately.

She had imagined hundreds of possibilities during the storm.

None of them had prepared her for seeing Corin like this.

> **"...Corin?"**

No response.

Orphie rushed forward.

She dropped beside her.

> **"Corin."**

Nothing.

She touched her shoulder.

Cold.

She looked toward the detached arm.

Then back at Corin.

> **"No..."**

Her voice broke.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light appeared beside them.

For the first time since the storm began, her full presence returned.

Her shattered halo illuminated the room.

Her broken wings unfolded.

She immediately examined Corin.

There was no conventional pulse.

No breathing.

No heartbeat.

That was normal for Corin.

But there was something else.

Her Ether was barely moving.

Guiding Light's expression fell.

> **"...She's here."**

Orphie looked at her.

> **"Then why isn't she moving?"**

Guiding Light had no immediate answer.

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT**

Curious Light immediately began examining the situation.

He stopped himself.

This was not an experiment.

This was Corin.

He looked at the detached limb.

Then at Corin.

His expression became unusually cold.

Not toward Corin.

Toward himself.

He had spent so much time studying how she worked.

Now he wished he had never needed to.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi stood at the entrance.

She saw Corin.

She saw the arm.

She saw the empty room.

Then she noticed something else.

The notebook.

She picked it up.

Her hands began shaking.

She read several pages.

Her face slowly changed.

> **"...What happened to you?"**

No one answered.

---

# **THE SAFEHOUSE**

The residents gradually entered.

Nobody knew what to say.

Belle covered her mouth.

Endmin immediately stayed beside her.

Bao's expression hardened.

Frost stared silently at Corin.

Niko stood completely still.

Nightshade looked toward Orphie.

Astral lowered her head.

Taiga did not move from the doorway.

Mart, normally energetic, remained completely silent.

Neko looked around the room.

He noticed the old blue notes.

He noticed the empty food containers.

He noticed the notebook.

Then he looked at Corin.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

Guiding Light eventually picked it up.

She read the final entries.

The pages documented the gradual collapse:

**The waiting.**

**The silence.**

**The empty pantry.**

**The loss of sleep.**

**The hopelessness.**

**The belief that everyone had abandoned her.**

And finally:

> **I don't care anymore.**

Guiding Light closed the notebook.

She held it against herself.

Her voice was barely audible.

> **"...I'm sorry."**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie carefully picked up Corin.

She was lighter than expected.

Much too light.

Orphie held her against herself.

> **"I'm here."**

No response.

> **"I'm home."**

Nothing.

Orphie tightened her grip.

> **"You don't have to wait anymore."**

---

# **THE ARM**

Guiding Light gently retrieved Corin's detached arm.

She placed it beside her.

The limb was intact.

There was no permanent damage.

It could be reattached.

But nobody immediately moved to do it.

Because the physical damage was no longer the most urgent problem.

---

# **LUMI'S REALIZATION**

Lumi looked at the notebook again.

Then at Corin.

Then at Orphie.

She finally understood what the storm had done.

It had not physically killed Corin.

It had done something much more cruel.

It had convinced her that everyone had left.

Lumi whispered:

> **"...She thought we abandoned her."**

Orphie looked at her.

> **"She did."**

Silence.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light knelt beside them.

Her shattered halo glowed softly.

She looked at Corin.

> **"Corin."**

No response.

She reached toward her.

Stopped.

Then gently touched her hair.

> **"I'm here."**

Nothing.

Guiding Light's expression broke.

> **"...I'm sorry I couldn't reach you."**

---

# **FINAL ASSESSMENT**

The storm is gone.

The anomalous suppression field has completely dissipated.

All abilities have returned.

The Blacksite can reach the treehouse again.

The roads are open.

The communications network is functioning.

The Architects can manifest.

The people who spent days waiting can finally enter.

But the victory feels hollow.

They arrived expecting to find Corin frightened.

Perhaps crying.

Perhaps angry.

Perhaps waiting by the door.

Instead, they found:

**A silent treehouse.**

**An abandoned notebook.**

**A detached arm.**

And Corin lying motionless in the corner.

The storm did not take her life.

It took something else.

For days, Corin had waited for everyone to come home.

Now everyone had finally come home to her.

And she did not move.

**End of Restricted Anomalous Weather / Safehouse Incident Log**
