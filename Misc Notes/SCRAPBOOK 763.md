# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE / PERSONNEL CARE DIVISION**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — CAFETERIA PANTRY INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — CRITICAL CHILD SAFETY  
**Location:** Cafeteria — Refrigerated Food Pantry  
**Incident Status:** **RESOLVED**  
**Subject Condition:** Weak / Dehydrated / Nonverbal  
**Potential Fatalities:** **1**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Alice was discovered inside the cafeteria's refrigerated food pantry.

The pantry is effectively a large industrial refrigerator used for storing vegetables, dairy products, and raw ingredients.

Alice had apparently entered while following an employee.

The employee left.

Alice remained.

She was not noticed for an extended period.

Once again, Alice did not attempt to attract attention.

She hid.

---

# **THE DISCOVERY**

An employee entered the pantry to retrieve ingredients.

While moving through the vegetable storage area, she noticed something move beneath the shelving.

She immediately suspected a rodent.

The cafeteria's **Anti-Rodent System (ARS)** was located nearby.

The employee approached the ARS controls.

Had the system been activated, the pantry would have automatically entered containment mode.

The doors would seal.

Food containers would be isolated.

The room would undergo its automated pest-remediation cycle.

The employee did not activate it.

Because Corin entered the pantry.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin had been searching for Alice after noticing that she had not been seen for some time.

She entered the pantry.

She immediately noticed something unusual beneath the carrot storage.

A small white ear.

Corin froze.

> "...Alice?"

The ear moved.

Alice slowly looked out.

She was curled underneath the shelving.

Still.

Silent.

She had apparently been there for a considerable period.

---

# **ALICE**

Alice had not eaten.

She had not drunk anything.

Despite being surrounded by food, she had consumed none of it.

Her behavior was consistent with her established reasoning:

> **"Not mine."**

She apparently did not consider food stored in the pantry to be something she was permitted to take.

Even more concerning—

she did not seem to consider her hunger sufficient reason to ask for help.

---

# **THE ARS**

The ARS was never activated.

This distinction is critical.

The system is designed specifically for pest-control emergencies and is **not considered a safe environment for human exposure.**

If activated while Alice was inside:

1. The pantry would enter automatic containment.
    
2. Access doors would seal.
    
3. Stored food would be isolated from the remediation process.
    
4. The automated pest-control cycle would begin.
    
5. Personnel would be unable to simply enter the room during the cycle.
    
6. Alice's survival would become immediately critical.
    

The system is not designed to recognize a small, silent human occupant hiding beneath shelving.

Alice's lack of movement or vocal response would not have helped.

She could easily have been mistaken for the very thing the system was designed to remove.

---

# **THE ALMOST-INCIDENT**

The employee had already reached toward the ARS control.

Corin arrived before activation.

The employee later stated:

> "I thought it was a rat."

When shown Alice's photograph, she reportedly became visibly distressed.

> "...I almost turned it on."

No disciplinary action was issued.

The employee had followed the expected response for suspected contamination.

The failure was not hers.

The failure was the possibility that Alice could be present somewhere nobody expected a child to be.

---

# **THE RAW-MEAT SECTION**

The pantry contains a secured section for raw meat.

This area is physically separated from the vegetable storage.

Unlike the vegetable shelving, the meat section requires additional authorization to access.

The locking mechanism is positioned beyond Alice's reach.

Alice cannot reasonably open it herself.

This separation likely prevented another potentially severe incident.

---

# **ALICE'S DIET**

Alice is physically capable of eating and digesting meat normally.

There is no dietary restriction preventing her from consuming it.

However—

**the problem is not digestion.**

It is what Alice sees.

---

# **IF ALICE SAW THE MEAT**

The raw-meat section contains prepared animal carcasses and animal products.

To an ordinary child, this might be unpleasant.

To Alice, it could be profoundly distressing.

Alice has an unusually strong attachment to living things.

She has previously sacrificed her own life force to revive a dead plant.

She cares about animals.

She cares about everyone around her.

And she places herself below essentially every other living thing.

Seeing the remains of animals—particularly rabbits—could therefore produce an extreme emotional response.

The concern is not that Alice would become violent.

The opposite.

She would likely blame herself for witnessing it.

---

# **THE RABBIT PROBLEM**

The presence of rabbit remains would be especially dangerous psychologically.

Alice is a rabbit.

She knows this.

She knows Belle is a rabbit.

She identifies strongly with other rabbits.

Seeing a rabbit carcass could therefore be interpreted by Alice in a deeply personal manner.

Personnel assessment suggests possible responses include:

- Immediate withdrawal.
    
- Hiding.
    
- Refusal to speak or write.
    
- Distress.
    
- Refusal to eat.
    
- Attempts to leave the area.
    
- Belief that she somehow caused the animal's death.
    
- Prolonged fixation on the remains.
    

None of these possibilities are considered acceptable.

---

# **FORTUNATE CIRCUMSTANCE**

Alice never entered the locked meat section.

She remained beneath the vegetable shelving.

The carrot storage was directly above her.

The meat section remained inaccessible.

Alice therefore never saw:

**Cows.**

**Chickens.**

**Rabbits.**

Or any other animal remains stored in the facility.

Corin considers this an extremely fortunate coincidence.

---

# **CORIN'S DISCOVERY**

Corin crouched beneath the shelving.

> "Alice, come here."

Alice crawled toward her.

Corin picked her up.

Alice immediately rested against her.

No complaint.

No anger.

No question about why she had been forgotten.

She simply allowed herself to be carried out.

---

# **AFTERWARD**

Alice was taken somewhere warm.

She was given water and food.

She initially refused.

Not because she wasn't hungry.

Because she had apparently convinced herself that she had already caused enough trouble.

Corin stayed with her.

Eventually, Alice ate.

---

# **THE PANTRY**

The pantry was temporarily closed.

The ARS was inspected.

The security procedure was reviewed.

A new requirement was introduced:

> **Before activating the ARS, personnel must confirm that no authorized or unauthorized human occupant is inside the pantry.**

Additional detection measures are being evaluated.

---

# **PERSONNEL REACTION**

The incident was discussed throughout the SAFEHOUSE.

Neko was particularly disturbed by the ARS almost being activated.

Miss Neko immediately demanded that the pantry's safety systems be reviewed.

Belle refused to let Alice go anywhere near the cafeteria without supervision.

Frost volunteered to accompany Alice during future cafeteria visits.

Corin simply said:

> "She was under the carrots."

Nobody laughed.

---

# **THE TWO NEAR-DISASTERS**

The incident produced two entirely separate dangers.

### **ONE**

Alice could have been inside when the ARS was activated.

She would have been trapped inside an automated pest-remediation system with no reliable means of calling for help.

### **TWO**

Alice could have reached or seen the raw-meat section.

Given her established attachment to living things, particularly rabbits, the psychological consequences could have been severe.

Neither happened.

By an extremely narrow margin.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Alice had been surrounded by food.

She was hungry.

She was thirsty.

She was cold.

And she still didn't take anything.

She simply hid beneath the carrots.

Waiting.

She did not know that the room was dangerous.

She did not know that someone was seconds away from activating a system that could have seriously endangered her.

She did not know what was behind the locked meat section.

She simply knew one thing:

> **"Alice wait."**

Corin found her before the pantry became a tragedy.

For once, someone arrived before Alice's quietness became fatal.
