# SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION REPORT

**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE  
**OBSERVATION:** Social Withdrawal / Unnoticed Presence  
**STATUS:** RESOLVED

---

## 1. Observation

Neri was lost in the background.

Not metaphorically.

Not because she left.

She was still there.

She was simply no longer being noticed.

The SAFEHOUSE continued operating around her as normal.

People walked past.

Doors opened and closed.

Conversations happened.

Work continued.

Neri was somewhere in the middle of it all.

And nobody realized how quiet she had become.

---

## 2. She Was Still Around

Neri did not disappear from the SAFEHOUSE.

She continued appearing in familiar places.

She would sit in the cafeteria.

Walk through the corridors.

Stand near the laboratory while Neko worked.

Pass by the greenhouse.

Occasionally watch people working.

Sometimes she would sit beside someone without saying anything.

If they were busy, she would eventually leave.

She stopped asking for attention.

---

## 3. The Background

After several days, people began unconsciously treating Neri like part of the environment.

A small figure sitting against a wall.

A pair of glasses catching the light.

An oversized azure coat.

Quiet footsteps in the corridor.

Someone would walk past her and say:

> "Morning."

Neri would quietly answer:

> "Morning."

And they would continue walking.

Nobody meant anything by it.

That was precisely the problem.

---

## 4. Cafeteria

The cafeteria became the clearest example.

Neri would arrive.

She would sit down.

Sometimes she had food.

Sometimes she didn't.

Sometimes she had cake.

Sometimes she simply stared at the table.

People came and went.

The cafeteria became crowded.

Then quiet.

Then empty.

Neri remained.

Eventually, she would stand up and leave.

Nobody had told her to leave.

Nobody had asked her to stay.

There simply wasn't anyone left to talk to.

---

## 5. No Complaint

There were no complaints from Neri.

No angry messages.

No demands.

No accusations.

No crying where anyone could see.

When someone asked:

> "You okay?"

she would usually answer:

> "Yeah."

And because Neri had become very good at hiding how she felt, the answer was accepted.

She was not okay.

But she wasn't asking anyone to fix it either.

---

## 6. What Made It Worse

Neri knew everyone was busy.

She knew the Blacksite had too much work.

She knew her friends had responsibilities.

She knew Alice had her own problems.

She knew Bao and Frost couldn't abandon the greenhouse.

She knew Star and Nightshade had Starlands matters.

She knew Belle and Endmin had Banlands responsibilities.

She knew Neko had work.

She knew everyone had something important to do.

Neri never considered herself more important than those responsibilities.

So she waited.

And waited.

And waited.

---

## 7. Eventually

Someone finally noticed something strange.

They realized that they hadn't actually **spoken** to Neri in several days.

They had seen her.

That was different.

They had passed her.

They had waved.

They had acknowledged her existence.

But they hadn't sat down.

They hadn't asked her to come along.

They hadn't asked what she was doing.

They hadn't asked whether she wanted company.

They had seen Neri everywhere.

And somehow, nobody had actually seen **Neri.**

---

## 8. Finding Her

She was eventually found sitting on the floor near one of the quieter corridors.

Her knees were pulled close to her chest.

Her coat covered most of her hands.

Her face was partially hidden.

There was no cake.

No candy.

No book.

Nothing.

Just Neri.

She looked up when someone approached.

> **"Oh."**

A pause.

> **"Hi."**

The person sat down beside her.

Neri looked away.

> **"You've been here a while, haven't you?"**

She nodded.

> **"Why didn't you tell anyone?"**

Another pause.

> **Neri:** "Everyone's busy."

---

## 9. The Answer

There wasn't really a good response to that.

Because she was right.

Everyone **was** busy.

The mistake wasn't being busy.

The mistake was assuming that because Neri wasn't asking for anything, she didn't need anything.

Someone finally said:

> **"You know you can still bother us, right?"**

Neri looked confused.

> **"Even when you're busy?"**

> **"Especially then."**

Neri was quiet for a moment.

Then she moved slightly closer.

She didn't say anything.

She didn't need to.

---

# 10. Closing Assessment

Neri was never actually gone.

She was never locked away.

She never ran away.

She never stopped being part of the SAFEHOUSE.

She simply became background noise.

And for someone with Neri's history, that distinction matters.

She spent years being treated as something disposable.

Something that could be ignored.

Something nobody needed to notice.

The SAFEHOUSE was supposed to be different.

That doesn't mean everyone must drop everything whenever Neri wants company.

It means someone occasionally needs to look up from their work and remember:

**She's still here.**

---

### FINAL NOTE

> **Neri:** "I thought nobody noticed me."

**Response:**

> "We noticed you."

A pause.

> **"We just didn't realize you were waiting for us."**

Neri lowered her head.

Then, very quietly:

> **"...I'm still here."**

> "Yeah."

> **"Okay."**

And this time, someone stayed.
