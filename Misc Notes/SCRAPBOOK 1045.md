# Executive Summary

The previous medical report contained an incorrect conclusion.

It stated that:

> **"From the first day Neri acquired her plushie form to the present, not a single anemic episode has been recorded."**

This was incorrect.

A painful anemic shock occurred **yesterday**.

The episode was significant enough that Neri voluntarily transformed into her plushie form afterward.

Today, Neri is sick.

The current sequence is therefore:

**Yesterday:** painful anemic shock  
↓  
**Neri transforms into plushie form**  
↓  
**Overnight stabilization**  
↓  
**Today:** Neri remains physically unwell despite no new documented anemic shock

The plushie transformation appears to have prevented the situation from becoming worse, but it did **not** retroactively erase yesterday's episode.

---

# I. YESTERDAY'S EPISODE

Neri experienced a severe and unusually painful anemic episode.

Unlike some previous incidents where she primarily became weak, pale, or lost consciousness, this episode was dominated by **pain**.

She was able to remain conscious.

She did not immediately seek assistance.

As has unfortunately been observed before, Neri attempted to tolerate the condition herself.

Eventually, she determined that remaining in her normal form was becoming increasingly difficult.

She transformed.

---

# II. PLUSHIE TRANSFORMATION

Neri entered her plushie form voluntarily.

The transformation produced the familiar physiological changes:

- pain became effectively numbed;
    
- her plushie physiology was not subject to the same anemia mechanism;
    
- physical strain was substantially reduced;
    
- she remained conscious;
    
- she remained capable of movement;
    
- she retained the ability to eat and drink;
    
- and her cardiovascular system was no longer being subjected to the same immediate demands of her normal physiology.
    

Neri did not transform because she wanted to be treated like a toy.

She transformed because she was hurting.

The distinction matters.

---

# III. OVERNIGHT OBSERVATION

Neri remained in plushie form through the relevant recovery period.

No additional anemic shock was observed during that period.

No emergency Administral intervention was required.

No cardiovascular collapse was recorded.

Her condition nevertheless remained under observation.

The absence of a new episode was not interpreted as proof that the previous one had been harmless.

---

# IV. TODAY

Neri returned to her normal form.

She was visibly unwell.

Her energy remained low.

She was considerably quieter than usual.

Movement was limited.

She did not appear interested in conversation.

At one point, Bao found Neri sitting flat on the floor rather than standing.

Bao asked:

> **"How are you feeling?"**

Neri was silent for several seconds.

Then:

> **"Sick."**

Her voice was extremely soft.

Bao did not ask her to elaborate.

---

# V. THE SURPRISING PART

Despite being sick today, Neri's condition did not immediately progress into another anemic shock.

Her body appeared to be recovering from yesterday's event.

This is an important distinction:

> **Neri is sick today because something happened yesterday.**

It does not necessarily indicate that she is currently experiencing another episode.

Medical personnel therefore continued observation without treating every symptom as a new emergency.

---

# VI. NERI'S BEHAVIOR TODAY

Neri spent much of the day doing very little.

She did not conduct her usual extended chemistry research.

Her 12 TB laptop remained closed for considerably longer than usual.

She drank slowly.

She rested.

She occasionally repositioned herself.

When someone approached, she generally gave minimal responses.

At one point, Alice asked:

> **"Do you want to be a plushie again?"**

Neri shook her head.

Alice accepted the answer.

Approximately an hour later, Neri transformed herself into her plushie form without being asked.

Alice did not comment.

She simply picked Neri up.

---

# VII. PLUSHIE FORM — SECOND USE

The second transformation occurred because Neri appeared to want the reduced physical strain.

She remained capable of moving herself.

She simply chose not to.

Alice carried her for part of the afternoon.

Neri eventually settled against Alice and became almost completely still.

She was conscious.

She was not unconscious.

She was not in immediate distress.

She was simply exhausted.

At one point, Alice quietly asked:

> **"Better?"**

Neri gave a small nod.

That was the entirety of the conversation.

---

# VIII. MEDICAL CORRECTION

The previous report must therefore be amended.

### Previous statement:

> **"Zero recorded episodes since acquisition of plushie form."**

### Corrected statement:

> **"A painful anemic shock has now been recorded following acquisition of plushie form. Neri subsequently used the plushie transformation as a means of reducing physical strain and remained stable afterward."**

This correction does not invalidate the observation that the plushie form has coincided with periods of unusual stability.

It simply establishes that:

**Plushie form is not an absolute guarantee against Neri experiencing an anemic episode before transforming.**
