---

## Executive Summary

Alice loves Neri.

Alice also forgot Neri outside for **more than two days**.

During that period, Plushie Neri was exposed to repeated rainfall and snowfall.

By the time she was discovered, Neri was completely soaked.

She was found sitting where Alice had apparently left her, wet enough that her coat/fabric had become visibly saturated and covered in accumulated precipitation.

Neri was alive.

Neri was conscious.

Neri was also extremely unimpressed.

---

## Timeline

### DAY 1 — 14:18

Alice was observed carrying Plushie Neri outside the SAFEHOUSE.

Neri appeared comfortable.

Weather conditions were deteriorating.

Alice reportedly intended to bring Neri back inside after "doing one thing."

The one thing became several things.

---

### DAY 1 — 16:02

Alice returned indoors.

**Neri did not.**

No resident immediately noticed.

Neri did not attempt to attract attention.

---

### DAY 1 — 21:40

Rain began.

Neri remained outside.

---

### DAY 2 — 07:15

Snowfall began.

Neri remained outside.

The temperature continued falling.

Neri's plush-state physiology prevented ordinary cold-related injury from becoming immediately catastrophic, but this did **not** make prolonged environmental exposure safe.

---

### DAY 2 — 18:30

Additional snowfall.

Neri remained in approximately the same location.

At this point, her exterior was heavily saturated.

No emergency signal was triggered.

Neri apparently considered moving indoors.

She did not.

---

### DAY 3 — 11:06

Jane Doe exited the SAFEHOUSE.

She noticed what appeared to be a severely soaked plush seal partially covered in snow.

Jane initially assumed it was an ordinary plush toy.

She then noticed the glasses.

Jane stopped.

> **Jane:** "...Neri?"

One eye opened.

> **Neri:** "Hi."

Jane immediately picked her up.

---

## Recovery

Neri was brought indoors and assessed.

She was:

- conscious;
    
- responsive;
    
- extremely wet;
    
- cold;
    
- exhausted;
    
- irritated;
    
- otherwise surprisingly stable.
    

Her plush form had protected her from many consequences that would have been considerably more serious in normal form.

It had **not** made the situation harmless.

Neri's fabric exterior was saturated.

Her glasses were covered in water droplets.

Her hairclips were wet.

Her fishbone clip was wet.

Her entire little body was essentially a damp seal-shaped sponge.

---

## Alice's Reaction

Alice was informed.

She froze.

Then:

> **Alice:** "I FORGOT NERI."

Nobody contradicted her.

Alice immediately ran to find Neri.

Upon seeing her, Alice looked horrified.

> **Alice:** "NERI!"

Neri looked up.

Alice picked her up.

> **Alice:** "I'M SO SORRY!"

Neri stared at her.

Alice hugged her carefully.

Neri remained still for several seconds.

Then she quietly said:

> **Neri:** "Cold."

Alice's expression reportedly collapsed.

---

## Bao's Assessment

Bao took custody of Neri for recovery.

After confirming that Neri was not experiencing an acute medical crisis, Bao issued the following statement:

> "She's fine."

Pause.

> "She's going to be fine."

Longer pause.

> "Alice."

Alice looked at her.

> "You cannot leave Neri outside for two days."

Alice nodded rapidly.

> "I know."

> "Good."

> "I'm sorry."

> "I know."

---

## Post-Incident Observation

Neri did not appear particularly angry with Alice.

She did not push Alice away.

She did not refuse to be held.

She simply remained unusually quiet while Bao dried and warmed her.

Alice stayed nearby for the remainder of the recovery period.

She repeatedly apologized.

Neri eventually reached one small plush paw toward Alice.

Alice immediately took it.

No further discussion occurred.

---

## Corrective Measures

C.O.R.E. Welfare has issued a new SAFEHOUSE guideline:

### **PLUSH NERI TRACKING RULE**

If a resident is currently carrying or supervising Plushie Neri:

1. Know where Neri is.
    
2. Know when Neri was last seen.
    
3. Do not assume another resident has her.
    
4. Do not assume Neri will follow you.
    
5. Do not leave her outdoors unattended.
    
6. If you forget where you put her, **find her immediately.**
    

Additional note:

> **Neri is waterproof. She is not weatherproof.**

---

Alice's affection for Neri remains unchanged.

Neri's willingness to be around Alice also remains unchanged.

However, C.O.R.E. recommends that Alice's future handling of Plushie Neri involve considerably more reliable memory retention.
