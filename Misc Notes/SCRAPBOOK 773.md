### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE / PERSONNEL CARE DIVISION**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — SOCIAL PROXIMITY GUIDELINE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — PERSONNEL CARE  
**Status:** ACTIVE

---

# **GUIDELINE**

Personnel are reminded:

> **Do not deliberately shoo Alice away when she follows you.**

Alice frequently follows people throughout the Blacksite.

This behavior is not generally associated with misbehavior.

She is usually simply curious.

Or she wants company.

Or she likes the person.

Sometimes she doesn't even appear to have a specific reason.

She just walks alongside them.

---

# **IMPORTANT CONTEXT**

Alice gets along remarkably well with almost everyone.

She has no particular preference for staying exclusively with SAFEHOUSE residents.

She may approach:

- Scientists.
    
- Engineers.
    
- Security personnel.
    
- Cafeteria staff.
    
- Archivists.
    
- Maintenance workers.
    
- Researchers.
    
- Administrative staff.
    
- Visitors.
    

She may quietly walk alongside them for several minutes.

Sometimes she asks questions.

Sometimes she talks.

Sometimes she says absolutely nothing.

---

# **DO NOT SHOO HER**

If Alice follows you, do not respond with:

> "Go away."

> "I'm busy."

> "Stop following me."

> "Go find someone else."

Unless there is a legitimate safety or operational reason for her to leave the area, personnel should simply acknowledge her presence.

A simple:

> "Hi, Alice."

is usually enough.

---

# **IF YOU ARE BUSY**

Personnel who genuinely cannot accommodate Alice may redirect her gently.

For example:

> "Alice, I'm working right now. You can sit over there and wait."

Or:

> "I'm going into a restricted area. I'll come find you afterward."

The important distinction is that Alice should not be treated as an unwanted nuisance.

---

# **WHY THIS MATTERS**

Alice spent a considerable amount of time being unintentionally forgotten.

She is therefore particularly sensitive to being dismissed.

She may not complain.

She may simply stop following.

She may walk away quietly.

Personnel are reminded that this does not necessarily mean she is fine.

She may simply believe:

> **"Alice bother them."**

---

# **AN UNEXPECTED ADDITION**

The original document was left unattended on an archivist's desk.

Alice found it.

The archivist returned approximately eleven minutes later.

The document had been modified.

The original text remained intact.

However, Alice had added a small section beneath the final guideline.

The handwriting was unmistakably hers.

---

> **Alice likes everyone.**
> 
> **Everyone is nice to Alice.**
> 
> **Sometimes Alice follow people because they look lonely.**
> 
> **Sometimes Alice follow because Alice like them.**
> 
> **Alice don't want to bother people.**
> 
> **If Alice follow you, you can say hi.**
> 
> **Alice will be happy.**
> 
> **Please don't send Alice away.**
> 
> **Alice can wait quietly.**
> 
> **Alice is good at waiting.**
> 
> **But Alice likes when people let Alice stay.**

There was a small drawing underneath.

It depicted Alice standing beside a very tall employee.

Alice had drawn herself holding their hand.

---

# **ONE FINAL SENTENCE**

At the very bottom of the page, written considerably larger than everything else:

> **"Alice likes being with everyone."**

Underneath it was a tiny heart.

---

# **ARCHIVIST'S NOTE**

The archivist discovered Alice sitting underneath the desk after retrieving the document.

She looked up.

> "Alice?"

Alice nodded.

The archivist held up the document.

> "Did you write this?"

Alice nodded again.

> "Can I keep it?"

Alice thought for a moment.

Then:

> "...Yes."

The archivist smiled.

> "Thank you."

Alice smiled back.

> "You're welcome."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The guideline remains active.

Alice's addition has been preserved.

The archivist has also added one handwritten note of their own:

> **"For the record: Alice is welcome here."**

It was immediately followed by another sentence in Alice's handwriting:

> **"Yay."**
