# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-LOG**

### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE**

#### **VISITOR LOG – STORAGE ANNEX ORIENTATION TOUR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal Personnel

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following numerous incidents involving personnel becoming lost inside the SAFEHOUSE Storage Annex (commonly referred to as "the closet"), an official orientation tour was organized for newer residents and visitors.

Tour Guides:

- **Endmin Merryweather**
    
- **Neko**
    

The decision was unanimous.

They are currently the only known residents capable of navigating the entire Storage Annex from memory.

---

## **TOUR START**

The group gathered at what appeared to be an ordinary closet.

Belle smiled.

> "...Ready?"

She opened the doors.

Beyond them...

Was an elevator lobby.

Several first-time visitors stood completely still.

One researcher quietly asked,

> "...How?"

No answer was provided.

---

## **FLOOR 1**

Neko immediately began explaining the layout.

Five minutes later...

He had already wandered off explaining the engineering behind the pocket dimension.

Miss Neko quietly redirected the tour.

---

## **FLOOR 3**

Belle enthusiastically showed everyone the blanket storage.

> "...This is where all the comfy blankets are!"

She disappeared into an aisle.

Returned thirty seconds later carrying three blankets.

No one had asked for blankets.

---

## **FLOOR 5**

Star immediately found the recreation section.

Five minutes later...

The tour had been temporarily paused because everyone was playing table tennis.

Nightshade insisted she wasn't participating.

She won two matches.

---

## **FLOOR 6**

Neko proudly pointed at several neatly organized storage shelves.

> "...This isn't my workshop."

Miss Neko looked directly at him.

> "...Because I stopped you."

Neko nodded.

> "...Because she stopped me."

---

## **FLOOR 7**

Endmin demonstrated the personal storage lockers.

Every locker opened only for its assigned resident.

Belle's locker contained:

- Carrots.
    
- Emergency carrots.
    
- Backup emergency carrots.
    
- Spare contacts.
    
- Glasses.
    
- A bunny-shaped pillow.
    

Endmin quietly closed the locker.

---

## **FLOOR 10**

The elevator doors opened.

The floor was almost completely empty.

Neko immediately pointed into the distance.

> "...Imagine what we could build here."

Miss Neko pressed the elevator button.

> "...We're leaving."

---

## **THE INCIDENT**

After the tour concluded...

Mart asked a simple question.

> "How do you two remember all of this?"

Endmin answered first.

> "...I've walked it enough."

Neko answered immediately afterward.

> "...I made shortcuts."

Everyone looked at him.

"...There are shortcuts?"

Neko nodded.

> "Three."

Endmin corrected him.

> "...Four."

Neko blinked.

"...There's a fourth?"

Endmin pointed toward an ordinary-looking bookshelf.

Neko stared.

"...I've lived here for years."

## **CONCLUSION**

Despite constructing much of the Storage Annex's infrastructure, Neko was observed getting lost near the end of the tour after taking what he described as "a shortcut."

Endmin led the group back to the SAFEHOUSE entrance without consulting a map.

As of this report, the only confirmed residents capable of navigating all ten floors entirely from memory remain:

- **Endmin Merryweather**
    
- **Neko** _(provided he does not attempt shortcuts)_
    

The remaining residents continue to rely on maps, signs, or simply asking one of those two for directions.
