### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / TREEHOUSE OBSERVATION LOG**

# **ORPHIE — THE LAST PERSON**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Critical Isolation Concern  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / Marts / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Luna / Solaria  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **CRITICAL**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following Orphie's continued efforts to drive the Marts away, one person remains consistently close enough to reach her:

> **Corin.**

Corin remains the only individual Orphie has not successfully driven away permanently.

She lives with Orphie.

She knows her routines.

She understands her silences.

She can enter the treehouse without immediately triggering Orphie's anomalous deterrents.

Most importantly, Corin is still capable of getting Orphie to talk.

That may be about to change.

---

# **THE LAST CONNECTION**

The Marts are gone.

The SAFEHOUSE is keeping its distance.

Luna and Solaria can observe Orphie's condition, but they cannot simply become separate people and intervene independently.

Corin is therefore the last meaningful connection Orphie has maintained.

Orphie knows this.

She has noticed that Corin is different from everyone else.

And recently, she has begun treating that fact as a problem.

---

# **ORPHIE'S NEW OBJECTIVE**

Orphie has not directly stated that she wants Corin gone.

She does not need to.

Her behavior has become increasingly deliberate.

She has begun testing ways to make Corin uncomfortable.

She becomes colder when Corin enters the room.

She uses Luna's frightening smile.

She occasionally allows the golden Ether lines beneath her skin to brighten.

She has begun using Solaria and Luna's abilities around Corin specifically.

The message is becoming increasingly clear:

> **"You should leave too."**

---

# **CORIN UNDERSTANDS**

Corin knows what Orphie is doing.

She has seen what happened to the Marts.

She knows Orphie can make her afraid.

She knows Orphie can force her to leave if she truly commits to it.

But she also knows something else.

Orphie is not doing this because she hates Corin.

She is doing it because Corin is the last person who still refuses to disappear.

---

# **THE CONVERSATION**

Corin eventually confronted her.

> **Corin:** "...You're trying to scare me away."

Orphie said nothing.

> **Corin:** "...Why?"

> **Orphie:** "...You should leave."

> "...No."

> "...The Marts left."

> "...They were scared."

> "...Exactly."

Corin looked at her.

> "...And you want me to be scared too?"

Orphie:

> "...Yes."

---

# **CORIN'S ANSWER**

Corin did not move.

> "...I'm not a Mart."

Orphie:

> "...I know."

> "...And I'm not leaving."

Orphie stared at her.

> "...Why?"

Corin answered:

> "...Because you're my family."

Orphie's expression hardened.

> "...Don't say that."

> "...Why?"

> "...Because it doesn't matter."

Corin:

> "...It matters to me."

---

# **THE THREAT OF DISTANCE**

Orphie has demonstrated that she possesses enough anomalous capability to make Corin leave.

The concern is no longer whether she **can**.

It is whether she will eventually succeed.

If Corin becomes too frightened to remain near her, Orphie will have achieved complete voluntary isolation.

There would be no Marts.

No SAFEHOUSE visits.

No regular companionship.

No one physically close enough to notice when something is wrong.

Only Orphie.

---

# **LUNA'S WARNING**

Luna has become increasingly worried.

> "...Corin is the last thread."

She paused.

> "...And Orphie is trying to cut it herself."

---

# **SOLARIA'S WARNING**

Solaria was more direct.

> "...If she succeeds, there's nobody left nearby."

> "...Not because everyone stopped caring."

> "...Because she made them leave."

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts remain frightened of Orphie.

They will not approach her.

But they still remember her.

They occasionally watch the treehouse from a distance.

They are too afraid to return.

Corin remains their only point of contact with Orphie.

---

# **ORPHIE'S CONTRADICTION**

Orphie insists:

> **"Leave me alone."**

But she continues interacting with Corin.

She answers her questions.

She notices when Corin comes home.

She knows when Corin is tired.

She occasionally waits for her.

She has not yet completely severed the connection.

This suggests that some part of Orphie still wants Corin nearby.

She simply refuses to acknowledge it.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Orphie:**  
Actively attempting to eliminate her remaining close interpersonal connection.

**Corin:**  
Currently the only person consistently capable of reaching Orphie.

**Marts:**  
Frightened and maintaining distance.

**SAFEHOUSE:**  
Unable to provide equivalent direct support.

**Luna:**  
Concerned.

**Solaria:**  
Concerned.

**Risk:**  
Complete self-imposed isolation.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The Marts were the first to leave.

Orphie made sure of it.

Now Corin is the only person left.

The only person who still walks through the treehouse door.

The only person who can sit beside her without being immediately driven away.

The only person who still knows how to reach the person underneath the silence.

And Orphie has decided that this is unacceptable.

She is preparing to make Corin leave too.

Not because Corin failed her.

Not because Corin stopped caring.

Not because Corin did anything wrong.

Because Orphie has become convinced that nobody should have to stay.

If she succeeds, there will finally be nobody left close enough to tell her otherwise.

**End of Personnel Welfare / Treehouse Observation Log**
