# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CRITICAL INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: DISAPPEARANCE OF COREY LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

At approximately 23:41 on ██/██/20██, Corey Lockwood departed her treehouse during an active snowstorm.

She has not returned.

Search operations commenced immediately after personnel determined her absence was not part of her usual nighttime routine.

As of the time of this report, Corey Lockwood remains **missing**.

---

# **LAST CONFIRMED SIGHTING**

The final confirmed sighting occurred outside the treehouse.

Weather conditions included:

- Heavy snowfall.
    
- Severe winds.
    
- Ambient temperatures below operational safety limits.
    
- Visibility below fifteen meters.
    

Corey was observed leaving alone.

She carried no emergency equipment.

No destination was stated.

No distress signal was transmitted.

Her footprints were quickly erased by the storm.

---

The white owl commonly seen near the treehouse failed to locate Corey.

The large white house cat searched continuously until exhaustion.

Neither found a trail.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin's condition deteriorated rapidly upon confirmation that Corey could not be located.

Personnel reported the following within the first several hours:

- Complete refusal to eat.
    
- Minimal verbal communication.
    
- Episodes of dissociation.
    
- Continuous pacing near the treehouse.
    
- Repeated attempts to re-enter the storm despite dangerous conditions.
    

Medical personnel attempted intervention.

Corin refused all assistance.

When informed that search teams would continue looking through the night, she replied:

> "...She came here because she died alone."

Several seconds passed.

She continued in a noticeably quieter voice.

> "...I'm not letting it happen twice."

Psychological Services immediately elevated Corin to continuous observation.

---

# **THE PARADOX**

Research Division has classified the disappearance as a potential **Paradox Stability Event**.

Because Corin and Corey are recognized as a Paradox Pair, Corey's prolonged absence introduces unknown risks.

No existing model predicts the consequences of an unresolved separation.

Research personnel have unanimously recommended that recovery efforts receive the highest operational priority.

# **CASE STATUS**

Corey Lockwood remains missing.

Her location is unknown.

The Corin Paradox remains stable only in the sense that Corin is still alive.

Research Division cannot determine how long that stability will persist.

Medical Services have expressed increasing concern not only for Corey...

But for Corin.

Every hour without news of Corey corresponds with measurable deterioration in Corin's physical and psychological condition.

Executive Command has therefore elevated the disappearance to **Priority Omega**.

Finding Corey is no longer considered solely a rescue operation.

It is considered essential to preserving both halves of the Corin Paradox.
