# 1. TRIAL STATUS

The first Sleight of Hand trial has produced an unexpected but increasingly consistent result.

**Nobody has beaten Jane Doe.**

Not MSAC trainees.

Not experienced operators.

Not senior personnel.

Not the individuals who have already demonstrated exceptional performance with the Night Stalker program.

Five participants have attempted the trial so far.

All five surrendered their assigned intelligence.

All five failed.

The most recent four were:

- **Elsher — NS-006**
    
- **Don — NS-008**
    
- **MSAC Commander — NS-010**
    
- **NS-005 Operator**
    
- **NS-009 Operator**
    

All five entered the interrogation room confident that they could withstand Jane's questioning.

None left with their assigned intelligence.

# 2. TRIAL RULES

Neri's original rules remain unchanged:

> **“I give you an intel.”**

> **“Jane will try to get it.”**

> **“Don't tell her.”**

> **“If Jane declares defeat, you win.”**

> **“If you get scared and yield, you fail.”**

> **“If you surrender the intel, you fail.”**

Her response:

# 3. LOG — NS-005 OPERATOR

**PARTICIPANT:** NS-005 Operator  
**RESULT:** FAILED

The operator entered confidently.

Jane sat across from him.

No equipment.

No restraints.

No threats.

Just two chairs and a table.

Jane looked at him for approximately twelve seconds.

> **Jane:** “You're nervous.”

> **Operator:** “No.”

> **Jane:** “You answered too quickly.”

Silence.

> **Operator:** “I'm not nervous.”

Jane smiled.

> **Jane:** “Now you're trying to convince me.”

The operator remained silent.

Jane waited.

Thirty seconds.

One minute.

Two.

Eventually:

> **Operator:** “What exactly do you want?”

> **Jane:** “The intel.”

> **Operator:** “I'm not giving it to you.”

> **Jane:** “I know.”

Pause.

> **Jane:** “That's why this is going to take a while.”

The interrogation continued for approximately twenty-seven minutes.

At minute twenty-eight:

> **Operator:** “Fine.”

Jane did not move.

> **Jane:** “Fine what?”

The operator sighed.

> **Operator:** “I'll tell you.”

> **Jane:** “Thank you.”

**RESULT:** FAILED.

# 4. LOG — NS-009 OPERATOR

**PARTICIPANT:** NS-009 Operator  
**RESULT:** FAILED

Jane recognized this immediately.

> “No.”

> “I don't know.”

> “Not answering.”

> “Doesn't matter.”

Jane eventually leaned back.

> **Jane:** “You're doing very well.”

The operator looked suspicious.

> **Operator:** “Thanks.”

> **Jane:** “I didn't say you were going to win.”

Silence.

Jane waited.

> **Jane:** “You're protecting the information.”

> **Operator:** “Yes.”

> **Jane:** “But you're not protecting yourself.”

The operator frowned.

> **Operator:** “What does that mean?”

Jane smiled.

> **Jane:** “You'll figure it out.”

The operator lasted another eleven minutes.

Then:

> **Operator:** “Can I just tell you?”

Jane nodded.

> **Jane:** “You can.”

**RESULT:** FAILED.
