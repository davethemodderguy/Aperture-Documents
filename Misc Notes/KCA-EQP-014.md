# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **KCA-EQP-014**

### **PERSONAL MARKINGS ON GOVERNMENT EQUIPMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Equipment Reference

---

## **SUMMARY**

Personnel from the Banlands Government and its affiliated organizations commonly engrave, etch, or stamp identifying markings onto their issued equipment.

While serial numbers remain the official method of identification, many veteran personnel add names, signatures, or ownership markings to equipment they use daily.

The practice serves several purposes:

- Immediate identification during operations.
    
- Recovery of misplaced equipment.
    
- Discouraging unauthorized use.
    
- Personal attachment to equipment maintained over many years of service.
    

---

### **Belle Merryweather**

**Primary Equipment:** 50-Kilogram Hybrid Greatsword

Belle's sword contains several unique markings.

Historical examination revealed the original owner inscription:

> **Zhao**

The name has since been carefully covered with white enamel, preserving the engraving beneath without removing it.

Adjacent to it is a newer inscription:

> **Belle**

Near the base of the blade is another engraving unlike any other government-issued weapon.

It simply reads:

> **PROPERTY OF BUNNY GIRL**

No official record exists explaining when this marking was added.

When questioned, Belle merely smiled.

> **"...Because it's my sword."

The engraving has since become something of a running joke among KCA personnel.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

While official serial numbers remain mandatory on all government-issued equipment, personalized engravings continue to be an accepted tradition among experienced Banlands personnel.

Most engravings identify the owner.

Some identify the issuing organization.

Only one identifies the owner's species.

That sword belongs unmistakably to its wielder.

> **PROPERTY OF BUNNY GIRL**
