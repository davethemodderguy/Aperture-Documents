### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / FELINE FORM UPDATE**

# **CORIN — MINIATURE FELINE NIBBLING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Form:** Miniature Feline  
**Behavior:** **Soft Nibbling**  
**Status:** Harmless / Benign

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's miniature feline form has developed another distinctly cat-like behavior:

**Soft nibbling.**

When sufficiently comfortable around someone, Corin may gently nibble their fingers, clothing, ears, or other accessible objects.

The behavior does not appear aggressive.

It is generally interpreted as a combination of feline curiosity, affection, and playfulness.

---

# **PHYSICAL EFFECT**

Corin's miniature form is approximately palm-sized.

Consequently, her bite force is extremely limited.

Her soft nibbling is highly unlikely to cause injury under normal circumstances.

No significant wounds have been recorded.

At most, the recipient may experience:

- A faint pressure.
    
- A tiny tickling sensation.
    
- A very small pinch.
    

---

# **BEHAVIORAL CONTEXT**

Corin is more likely to nibble someone when:

- She feels safe around them.
    
- She is playful.
    
- She wants attention.
    
- She is curious about something.
    
- She is being held.
    
- Someone has their fingers near her face.
    

The behavior appears especially common when she is relaxed.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE INCIDENT**

Belle discovered the behavior first.

While holding miniature Corin, she offered a finger.

Corin sniffed it.

Then gently:

**Nibble.**

Belle looked down.

> **"...Did you just bite me?"**

Corin stared back.

Then:

**Nibble.**

Belle immediately began laughing.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has also been nibbled.

Her reaction was considerably less dramatic.

She simply looked at Corin.

Corin continued nibbling her finger.

Orphie eventually said:

> **"...You're weird."**

Corin responded with another tiny nibble.

---

# **BITE FORCE**

C.O.R.E. has determined that Corin's miniature form does not possess enough jaw strength to be considered a meaningful physical threat through biting alone.

Her Ether-based abilities remain a separate matter.

Personnel should therefore distinguish between:

> **Tiny kitten bite**

and

> **Corin's actual anomalous weaponry.**

The former is harmless.

The latter should not be underestimated.

---

# **BEHAVIORAL INTERPRETATION**

The current consensus is that soft nibbling is simply another manifestation of Corin's feline instincts.

It does not appear connected to aggression or her Ether abilities.

Her normal personality remains present throughout the behavior.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's miniature feline form can:

- Fly.
    
- Perform aerial maneuvers.
    
- Hide inside her wings.
    
- Move with exceptional agility.
    
- Wear tiny bows on her twin tails.
    
- And now—
    

**gently nibble people.**

Fortunately, she is approximately palm-sized.

The SAFEHOUSE residents have therefore concluded that being attacked by Corin is currently less frightening and more like being **gently harassed by a very small flying cat.**
