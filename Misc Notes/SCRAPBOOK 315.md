# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **SECURITY & LEGAL DIVISION – INCIDENT REPORT**

### **CASE FILE: KCA-IR-156 – "THE ARMOR PLAN"**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Despite the increasingly widespread reputation of the KCA's Mart colony, attempted robberies continue.

The latest group arrived with what they believed to be a reliable countermeasure:

> **Heavy body armor and riot shields.**

The plan was reportedly based on the assumption that the Rapid Response Marts would be unable to physically move or restrain heavily protected targets.

They were incorrect.

---

# **THE ATTACK**

The crew entered the KCA wearing heavy protective equipment.

Each member carried a riot shield.

Their apparent objective was to withstand the initial Mart response long enough to reach the secured areas.

The plan lasted approximately:

> **Several seconds.**

Camera Mart detected the group almost immediately.

Rapid Response Marts were deployed through the ventilation network.

---

# **CONTACT**

The crew reached the interior corridor.

The vents opened.

Hundreds of blue figures emerged.

The crew raised their shields.

For approximately three seconds, nothing happened.

One robber reportedly shouted:

> "...See? It's working!"

Then the first Mart hit the shield.

Then another.

Then another.

Then approximately several hundred.

---

# **RAPID RESPONSE**

The Marts entered Boulder Mode and coordinated their movements.

The heavy armor prevented serious injury.

The riot shields prevented the robbers from being immediately knocked backward.

Unfortunately for the crew...

Neither prevented them from being **buried under blue rocks.**

The Marts continued pushing, bouncing, and piling onto the formation until the entire crew was immobilized.

Every member was eventually pinned.

Every weapon was removed.

Nobody reached the vault.

---

### **Robber #6**

Nobody answered.

---

# **SURRENDER**

After several minutes of being completely immobilized, the crew formally surrendered.

One member raised a hand as far as the surrounding Marts allowed.

> "...We surrender."

The nearest Mart immediately stopped moving.

> "Okay."

Another Mart asked:

> "Friend?"

The robber sighed.

> "...Yeah."

The Mart smiled.

> "Happy!"

---

# **DAMAGE ASSESSMENT**

Following the surrender, KCA personnel conducted a complete inspection.

**KCA Personnel Injuries:** None.

**Civilian Injuries:** None.

**Facility Damage:** None.

**Vault Damage:** None.

**Mart Injuries:** None.

**Robber Injuries:** Minor bruising from impact.

The crew had not fired a weapon.

They had not damaged KCA property.

They had not harmed any personnel.

---

# **LEGAL REVIEW**

After reviewing the circumstances, **Belle Merryweather** and **Dialyn** determined that the group had caused no actual damage and had surrendered peacefully.

The charges associated with property damage and assault were therefore cleared.

The crew was released under the condition that they remain cooperative with KCA personnel.

---

# **AN UNEXPECTED OFFER**

Several days later, the former robbers returned to the KCA.

Not to rob it.

To apply for employment.

Their reasoning was surprisingly straightforward.

> "...If you can't beat them..."

> "...Work with them."

The KCA approved their applications for security positions.

---

# **NEW SECURITY STAFF**

The former robbery crew now works alongside KCA Security.

Their responsibilities include:

- Feeding Rapid Response Marts.
    
- Refilling water stations.
    
- Monitoring ventilation access points.
    
- Helping maintain Mart resting areas.
    
- Assisting with security drills.
    
- Looking after Marts following deployments.
    
- Making sure exhausted Marts have somewhere safe to rest.
    

They have become particularly familiar with the Rapid Response colony.

One former robber now carries a large container of cheese crackers during every shift.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The crew that once attempted to defeat the KCA's Mart defenses is now part of them.

Their original plan failed.

Their armor failed.

Their shields failed.

Their robbery failed.

But their decision to surrender changed everything.

They now spend their shifts feeding, maintaining, and protecting the same blue blobs that once pinned them to the floor.

One former robber summarized the entire experience:

> "...We thought we were going to rob the KCA."

He looked toward the ventilation system.

A Mart waved.

> "...Now we work for the Marts."

A second Mart appeared beside him.

> "Friend!"

He smiled.

> "...Yeah."

> "...Friend."
