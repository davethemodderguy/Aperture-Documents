### **ANOMALOUS CAUSALITY / INCIDENT RECONSTRUCTION REPORT**

# **CORIN — THE GUIDING LIGHT CHAIN REACTION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Primary Entity:** Guiding Light  
**Incident Type:** **Unintentional Ether Supercharging**  
**Status:** **Cause Identified**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has identified the previously unknown origin of Corin's gradual biological restoration.

The process began when **Guiding Light restored Corin's ability to perceive color** while Corin was still in her anomalous, biologically dead state.

The intervention was intended to affect only Corin's visual perception.

It did not.

The blessing interacted with Corin's highly concentrated Ether and triggered an unexpected **chain reaction** throughout her entire anomalous energy system.

The resulting reaction progressively increased the density, stability, and effectiveness of Corin's Ether.

That supercharged Ether subsequently began revitalizing her body.

---

# **THE ORIGINAL INTERVENTION**

At the time, Corin was biologically dead.

Her body was sustained through Ether.

She had no functional heartbeat.

She possessed no conventional blood circulation.

Her organs did not operate normally.

Guiding Light determined that safely restoring Corin's ability to see color was possible.

The blessing was therefore designed to target her visual system.

The intended result was simple:

> **Restore color perception.**

The result was considerably larger.

---

# **THE FIRST ANOMALY**

Immediately following the blessing, Corin's vision returned to normal color perception.

No other obvious changes were detected.

Guiding Light therefore considered the intervention successful.

Unknown to everyone present, however, the blessing had interacted with the Ether already permeating Corin's body.

The interaction produced a microscopic increase in Ether activity.

That increase interacted with surrounding Ether.

Which produced another increase.

Then another.

Then another.

The process became self-reinforcing.

---

# **THE CHAIN REACTION**

The process can be summarized as:

**Guiding Light's blessing**

↓

**Ether interaction**

↓

**Increased Ether concentration**

↓

**Improved Ether stability**

↓

**Greater Ether propagation**

↓

**Further amplification**

↓

**System-wide Ether supercharging**

The reaction did not occur instantly.

It progressed gradually.

This explains why Corin's physical restoration appeared to happen over an extended period rather than all at once.

---

# **WHY THE EFFECT WAS SO LARGE**

Corin's body already contained an enormous concentration of Ether.

For years, Ether had been functioning as her substitute for conventional biology.

Guiding Light's blessing effectively introduced an anomalous interaction into an already unstable and extremely concentrated system.

The Ether did not simply absorb the blessing.

It **propagated it.**

The result was an amplification loop.

Eventually, the Ether became sufficiently powerful and stable to begin performing functions it had never previously been capable of.

---

# **THE FIRST MAJOR EFFECTS**

The supercharged Ether began affecting Corin's physiology.

Initially:

- Existing wounds became more stable.
    
- Regeneration became more efficient.
    
- Ether circulation increased.
    
- Damaged tissue became increasingly responsive.
    

Eventually:

- Organs began revitalizing.
    
- Biological tissue began regenerating.
    
- Blood production resumed.
    
- Her heart began beating.
    
- Her lungs began functioning.
    
- Her body began requiring oxygen.
    

The process culminated in the restoration of biological life.

---

# **THE WINGS**

C.O.R.E. now believes the spontaneous development of Corin's wings may also have been enabled by the same Ether amplification.

The wings were not created directly by Guiding Light.

Guiding Light did not design them.

She did not summon them.

The supercharged Ether simply gave Corin's body enough anomalous energy and stability to undergo an entirely new developmental process.

The wings subsequently became a permanent part of Corin's physiology.

---

# **WHY GUIDING LIGHT DID NOT NOTICE**

The original blessing was exceptionally small compared to the total amount of Ether present within Corin.

The initial change was therefore nearly undetectable.

The reaction accelerated slowly.

By the time the amplification became significant, the Ether had already integrated itself throughout Corin's body.

Guiding Light therefore believed she was observing Corin's natural recovery.

She was.

She simply did not realize that her original intervention had started it.

---

# **THE DISCOVERY**

The connection was discovered only after C.O.R.E. compared historical Ether readings.

A pattern was identified.

Corin's Ether concentration had been steadily increasing from the moment Guiding Light restored her color perception.

There was no corresponding external energy source.

The timing was too precise to dismiss.

Further analysis confirmed that the blessing had altered the behavior of Corin's Ether.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S REACTION**

Guiding Light was horrified when she learned what had happened.

> **"I only wanted her to see colors again."**

Curious Light reviewed the data.

> **"You accidentally turned her Ether into a self-amplifying system."**

Guiding Light stared at him.

> **"...I did what?"**

Mischevious Light reportedly found the situation hilarious.

He was subsequently asked to leave the laboratory.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin was told that Guiding Light was indirectly responsible for her biological restoration.

She became quiet.

Then looked at Guiding Light.

> **"So you accidentally made me alive?"**

Guiding Light hesitated.

> **"...Apparently."**

Corin smiled.

> **"Thank you."**

Guiding Light immediately became emotional.

---

# **IMPORTANT DISTINCTION**

C.O.R.E. emphasizes that Guiding Light did **not** intentionally revive Corin.

Her original action was specifically intended to restore color perception.

The biological restoration was an unintended consequence.

Therefore, Corin's return to life should be classified as:

> **An emergent consequence of anomalous interaction.**

Not a deliberate resurrection.

---

# **CURRENT ETHER STATE**

Corin's Ether is now considerably more powerful than it was during her dead state.

It is:

- Highly concentrated.
    
- Highly stable.
    
- Self-sustaining.
    
- Deeply integrated with her biology.
    
- Capable of supporting biological regeneration.
    
- Capable of interacting with anomalous physiology.
    
- Resistant to conventional disruption.
    

The original chain reaction appears to have stabilized.

There is currently no evidence that it is still increasing without limit.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Guiding Light wanted Corin to see color.

That was all.

She cast a blessing.

Corin opened her eyes.

The world became colorful again.

Nobody knew that somewhere inside Corin's body, her Ether had just begun a chain reaction.

It amplified.

It stabilized.

It spread.

It rebuilt.

It revitalized organs that had been dead for years.

It created new biological functions.

It allowed Corin's body to reconnect itself.

And eventually—

**it brought her back to life.**

Guiding Light did not intend to resurrect Corin.

She only wanted to give her colors back.

Somehow, that small act became the first step toward giving Corin something much greater:

**a living body.**
