# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **Z-335 RESEARCH UPDATE**

# **CONFIRMATION OF THE REBIRTH HYPOTHESIS**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Z-335 (Marts)

---

# **OVERVIEW**

An unfortunate incident has led to one of the most significant discoveries regarding Z-335 biology.

A Mart experienced severe emotional distress resulting from unnecessary stress and subsequently destabilized.

Although the loss was regrettable, subsequent observations have provided conclusive evidence regarding a long-standing scientific question.

> **The Rebirth Hypothesis has been confirmed.**

Individual Marts do not permanently cease to exist.

They are reborn.

---

# **THE INCIDENT**

The Mart became overwhelmed by sustained emotional stress.

Personnel intervened immediately.

The Mart attempted to reassure everyone.

> "...Happy..."

Moments later, its magical containment field failed.

The Mart destabilized and burst.

The room fell silent.

Medical intervention was impossible.

---

# **COLONY RESPONSE**

Researchers expected widespread distress throughout the colony.

Instead, the surrounding Marts reacted with surprising calm.

Several gathered around the location.

One quietly adjusted the Mart's fallen top hat.

Another simply said:

> "We'll be back."

There was sadness.

But there was no panic.

No confusion.

No fear.

The colony behaved as though it understood something the researchers did not.

---

# **THE RETURN**

Approximately eighteen days later, a new Mart emerged within the SAFEHOUSE Mart Nest.

At first glance, it appeared entirely ordinary.

However, researchers noticed something unusual.

The Mart immediately drifted toward the same personnel member the previous Mart had been especially attached to.

Without introduction, it cheerfully said:

> "Hello again."

The staff member froze.

That phrase had never been taught as a greeting.

---

# **VERIFICATION**

Over the following days, additional observations were recorded.

The newly appeared Mart demonstrated:

- Familiarity with previously learned routines.
    
- Recognition of personnel it had never officially met.
    
- Preference for the same locations.
    
- The same favorite snacks.
    
- The same habit of sleeping on a particular bookshelf.
    

When shown the tiny top hat recovered from the previous Mart, it smiled.

> "...Mine."

No hesitation.

No uncertainty.

---

# **CONCLUSION**

After exhaustive review, C.O.R.E. has officially revised its understanding of Z-335 reproduction.

Previous Theory:

> Old Mart dies.

> New Mart is born.

Current Understanding:

> A Mart's physical body eventually destabilizes.

> The individual later returns in a newly formed body.

The colony does not create replacement personalities.

It welcomes back returning ones.

---

# **THE HIVEMIND**

The shared hivemind now appears to function as more than a communication network.

Researchers theorize it serves as a stable Etheric archive, preserving an individual Mart's identity between physical forms.

Death, from the Mart perspective, appears to be...

A temporary absence.

---

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

When informed that the theory had finally been confirmed, Lumi became unexpectedly emotional.

She quietly asked:

> "...So..."

> "...They really do come back?"

The lead researcher nodded.

Lumi smiled through tears.

> "...I knew you meant it."

Nearby, a Mart gently bumped against her arm.

> "Back."

She laughed softly.

> "...Welcome home."

---

# **STAR'S COMMENT**

Star read the updated report twice.

She closed the folder.

Then smiled.

> "...I guess they were telling us the truth all along."

---

# **AMBASSADOR'S STATEMENT**

The Blacksite Ambassador was informed that the researchers had finally confirmed the theory.

It looked mildly confused.

> "...You didn't know?"

After several awkward seconds, it continued.

> "...We said..."

> **"We'll be back."**

---

# **RESEARCH NOTE**

Despite confirmation that Marts are reborn, personnel are reminded that this discovery **does not lessen the importance of protecting them.**

The experience of destabilization remains distressing.

Preventing unnecessary emotional suffering remains a priority.

As one Ethics Committee member summarized:

> "Knowing someone recovers from an injury is not an excuse to injure them."

The same principle applies to Marts.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Several weeks after the incident, the reborn Mart was seen wandering through the SAFEHOUSE.

It stopped beside the staff member who had been present on the day it burst.

The person knelt down and gently petted it, carefully avoiding its tiny top hat.

The Mart smiled.

Then, with complete certainty, it said:

> **"...Told you."**

> **"...We'll be back."**

For the first time since the species was discovered...

Everyone knew it wasn't a figure of speech.

**End of Research Update**
