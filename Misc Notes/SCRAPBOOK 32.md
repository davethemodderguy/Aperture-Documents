# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CENTRAL COMPUTING DEPARTMENT (CCD)**

### **JOINT RESEARCH PROPOSAL – ETHER REGRESSION SYNDROME**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Medical Research Authorization Required

---

## **BACKGROUND**

Following the disclosure of Senior Manager **Norma Lockwood's** diagnosis of **Ether Regression Syndrome (ERS)**, the CCD and KCA Medical Division convened to explore potential avenues for future research.

During the review, researchers identified one noteworthy detail.

Norma and Belle Merryweather share the **same blood type**.

Separately, previous CCD studies had already documented an unusual property of Belle's blood.

---

## **PREVIOUS FINDINGS**

Belle's blood contains an unusually high concentration of **stabilized Ether**, a form of Ether that has consistently demonstrated no harmful effects in laboratory observations.

Under controlled research conditions, Belle's blood has shown the ability to **neutralize residual unstable Ether**, converting it into a stable, non-harmful form.

The mechanism responsible for this phenomenon remains unknown.

To date, Belle is the only documented individual exhibiting this property.

---

## **RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS**

Researchers proposed the following hypothesis:

> **If ERS is driven, in part, by unstable Ether interacting with the body, could Belle's stabilized Ether influence that process?**

At the current stage, this is **only a research question**.

No conclusion has been reached, and no therapeutic effect has been demonstrated.

Further laboratory investigation would be required before any clinical application could be considered.

---

## **INITIAL DISCUSSION**

The proposal was reviewed jointly by the CCD, KCA Medical Division, and Executive Administration.

The consensus was cautious.

The possibility was considered scientifically interesting, but all parties agreed that any investigation would need to proceed gradually and under strict medical oversight.

No assumptions were made regarding effectiveness.
