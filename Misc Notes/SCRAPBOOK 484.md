# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **FAMILY / IDENTITY RECORD INCIDENT**

# **THE NAMES THAT WERE MISSING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Architect-Sealed  
**Subjects:** Lumi / Corin / Orphie  
**Location:** Blacksite Records Division → Remote Perimeter Treehouse  
**Status:** **CRITICAL**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Lumi was reviewing archived personnel documentation when she opened her own family record.

The document appeared ordinary.

Until she reached the section listing her known family members.

---

# **LUMI — FAMILY RECORD**

**Known Family Members:**

- **Arcturus** — Son  
    _Main Security Director, Kramus Compliance Authority_
    
- **Norma** — Daughter  
    _Senior Manager, Kramus Compliance Authority_
    

That was all.

Lumi stared at the screen.

She read it again.

Then again.

There was no:

**Corin.**

There was no:

**Orphie.**

No mention of either girl.

No adoption record.

No biological relationship.

No historical note.

Nothing.

---

# **THE REALIZATION**

Lumi's breathing stopped.

> **Lumi:**  
> "...No."

She searched the database.

Nothing.

She searched Corin's name.

No result.

She searched Orphie.

No result.

She searched their former name.

Still nothing.

Lumi stood so quickly that her chair fell backward.

> **"No, no, no..."**

She ran.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

Lumi reached the remote perimeter forest as quickly as she could.

The treehouse was exactly where it had always been.

She climbed the stairs.

The door opened.

Orphie looked up.

For several seconds, she simply stared.

Then recognition struck.

> **Orphie:**  
> "...Mom?"

Lumi froze.

Orphie immediately recognized her.

The name.

The face.

The memories.

Everything was still there.

At least for Orphie.

Lumi rushed forward.

She hugged her daughter.

> **Lumi:**  
> "Orphie..."

Orphie held onto her.

> **"You're here."**

Lumi was crying.

> **"I'm sorry."**

> **"I'm so sorry."**

---

# **CORIN**

Corin was sitting nearby.

She watched the reunion quietly.

She did not recognize Lumi.

Not emotionally.

Not personally.

She looked at the woman hugging Orphie.

Then calmly asked:

> **Corin:**  
> **"Hello, who are you?"**

Silence.

Lumi froze.

Orphie slowly looked toward Corin.

> **"Corin..."**

Corin tilted her head.

> **"Yes?"**

Lumi's expression collapsed.

---

# **THE DIFFERENCE**

Orphie remembered Lumi.

Corin did not.

The distinction was devastating.

Orphie could remember:

- Her mother.
    
- Her childhood.
    
- Her family.
    
- The time before the laboratory.
    
- Lumi holding her.
    
- Lumi being there.
    

Corin could not connect any of those memories to the person standing in front of her.

She knew the name **Lumi** only because Orphie said it.

Nothing more.

---

# **LUMI'S RESPONSE**

Lumi slowly approached Corin.

She stopped several steps away.

She did not touch her.

She did not want to frighten her.

> **Lumi:**  
> "...I'm Lumi."

Corin looked at her.

> **"Okay."**

Lumi's voice trembled.

> **"I'm your mother."**

Corin stared.

There was no recognition.

No sudden memory.

No emotional response.

Only confusion.

> **Corin:**  
> "...My mother?"

Lumi nodded.

> **"Yes."**

Corin looked toward Orphie.

> **"Is she telling the truth?"**

Orphie couldn't answer immediately.

Her eyes filled with tears.

> **"...Yes."**

---

# **LUMI'S PANIC**

Lumi looked around the treehouse.

The belongings.

The photographs.

The clothing.

The sunflower pot.

The evidence of a life that apparently no longer existed in the official records.

She understood something terrifying.

The problem wasn't simply that people had forgotten Corin and Orphie.

Their **records were being rewritten.**

Reality itself appeared to be treating their existence as something that had never belonged in the family record.

Lumi looked at Orphie.

> **"Why do I remember you?"**

Orphie shook her head.

> **"I don't know."**

Lumi looked at Corin.

Corin simply watched her.

> **"...And why doesn't she?"**

No one answered.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

A soft blue glow appeared in the room.

Guiding Light manifested beside the window.

For once, she looked genuinely troubled.

> **Lumi:**  
> "You know what's happening."

Guiding Light did not deny it.

> **"Yes."**

> **"Then fix it."**

Guiding Light looked toward Corin.

> **"...I don't know if I can."**

Lumi's expression hardened.

> **"You're an Architect."**

> **"I know."**

> **"You made reality."**

Guiding Light looked back at her.

> **"And that is exactly why I'm afraid."**

---

# **CORIN'S QUESTION**

Corin continued watching the three of them.

She seemed increasingly uncomfortable.

Finally:

> **Corin:**  
> "...Why is everyone crying?"

Orphie immediately moved beside her.

She took Corin's hand.

> **Orphie:**  
> "It's okay."

Corin looked at Lumi.

> **"Are you really my mother?"**

Lumi nodded.

She could barely speak.

> **"...Yes."**

Corin considered the answer.

Then quietly:

> **"I'm sorry."**

Lumi shook her head.

> **"Don't apologize."**

Corin looked confused.

> **"For what?"**

Lumi closed her eyes.

> **"...Nothing."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Lumi had spent months believing she had failed her daughters by staying away.

Now she discovered something far worse.

Her own records no longer acknowledged that they had ever been her daughters.

And Corin—

the child she had given birth to—

looked directly at her and asked:

> **"Hello, who are you?"**

Orphie still remembers.

Guiding Light still remembers.

Lumi remembers.

But the records do not.

And Corin's memories do not.

For the first time, Lumi understood that being forgotten was not the greatest danger.

The greater danger was that reality itself might be beginning to agree.

**End of Family / Identity Record Incident**
