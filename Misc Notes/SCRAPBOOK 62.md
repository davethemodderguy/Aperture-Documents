# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **PERSONNEL OBSERVATION LOG**

### **SUBJECT: NORMA LOCKWOOD – MOVING FORWARD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel Record

---

## **OVERVIEW**

Despite the discovery of her childhood trauma and the recurring nightmares that followed, **Senior Manager Norma Lockwood** has remained, in the eyes of nearly everyone at the Blacksite...

Simply **Norma**.

She still arrives early.

She still spends far too much time in the Engineering Department.

She still forges tools because she thinks it's relaxing.

She still helps researchers solve problems before they even finish explaining them.

And she still smiles whenever someone asks for her help.

Those who know her well, however, recognize the small changes.

---

## **THE NIGHTS**

The nightmares have not disappeared.

Some nights she wakes before dawn.

Some nights she sleeps only a few hours.

Occasionally, she can be found sitting quietly in SAFEHOUSE's common room with a warm mug of milk, tea, or milk tea, waiting for morning to arrive.

She rarely speaks about those nights.

If someone joins her, she appreciates the company.

If no one does, she simply enjoys the silence.

---

## **THE DAYS**

Norma continues to perform her duties to the same high standard expected of a Senior Manager.

To most people, nothing appears unusual.

She laughs at jokes.

She cooks with Belle.

She debates engineering ideas with Neko.

She teases Arcturus about his refusal to use automatic weapons.

She encourages younger researchers.

She keeps moving forward.

There are moments, however, when an old memory briefly catches up to her.

She may pause for a second longer than usual.

Take a slow breath.

Sip her tea.

Then continue as though nothing happened.

---

## **HER CHOICE**

Belle once quietly asked her:

> "...Why do you always try so hard to act normal?"

Norma thought for a long moment before answering.

> "Because I don't want everyone to feel like they have to walk on eggshells around me."

She smiled gently.

> "I'm healing."

> "Some days are harder than others."

> "But I don't want my pain to become someone else's burden."

Belle shook her head.

> "You're not a burden."

Norma smiled a little wider.

> "...I know."

> "I'm still going to keep trying."

---

## **THE WINGS**

Norma possesses a pair of feathered wings.

They are fully functional, allowing sustained flight and precise aerial maneuvering.

Yet, despite this, most people rarely realize she even has them.

She habitually keeps them folded neatly against her back beneath specially tailored clothing and her nanotech armor.

Only when flying, stretching after long hours of work, or in situations requiring rapid movement do they unfold.

One researcher who had worked with Norma for nearly six months was visibly startled the first time he saw them.

> "...You have wings?!"

Norma looked over her shoulder.

> "...Oh."

She blinked.

> "...I forgot people don't see them very often."

## **STATUS**

Norma continues to receive support from the Blacksite's medical and psychological staff.

Her nightmares remain an ongoing challenge, and some discomfort from her past still lingers.

Even so, she continues to build, cook, laugh, mentor, and care for those around her.

She has never asked to be seen as someone defined by what happened to her.

She would rather be known for what she chooses to do now.

Around the Blacksite, that choice is reflected in countless small moments—a repaired machine, a shared meal, a cup of tea offered to someone having a rough day, or a quiet smile after finishing another long shift.

Those who know her best understand that every one of those moments is its own kind of victory.
