# 1. BACKGROUND

Following Neri's return to her normal SAFEHOUSE routine, she began spending increasing amounts of time studying her newly acquired abilities.

Her preferred activity remains relatively mundane:

> Sit in the cafeteria.  
> Eat cake.  
> Read chemistry.

However, Neri has also become increasingly comfortable with her ability to manipulate localized reality.

This eventually led to an experiment involving two residential spaces:

1. An unused room within the SAFEHOUSE.
    
2. Neri's own room.
    

The experiment was successful.

Neri effectively **broke both rooms away from conventional interior space and converted them into interconnected subspaces.**

# 3. THE UNUSED ROOM

The first room Neri modified was completely empty.

It contained no resident belongings and was not actively assigned as anyone's bedroom.

Neri entered the room, examined the walls, and apparently decided:

> **"This can be bigger."**

She then activated her reality-displacement ability.

There was no conventional explosion, portal, or structural collapse.

Instead, the interior of the room became **disconnected from the normal geometry of the SAFEHOUSE.**

From the outside:

**Nothing appears unusual.**

The door remains where it always was.

The surrounding hallway remains intact.

The building's exterior geometry remains unchanged.

From inside:

**The room is no longer the room it used to be.**

---

# 4. SUBSPACE CONFIGURATION

The interior now exists as a localized subspace.

Neri described the process simply:

> **"I broke it apart."**

When asked what she replaced it with:

> **"Something bigger."**

The resulting space is physically disconnected from the dimensions implied by the room's exterior.

A person standing inside can therefore occupy an area substantially larger than the original room should physically permit.

There is no corresponding expansion visible from outside.

This is consistent with Neri's previously demonstrated ability.

---

# 5. NERI'S ROOM

Neri subsequently performed the same modification on her own room.

Unlike the unused room, this one contains her personal belongings.

The transformation therefore required greater precision.

Neri preserved:

- her belongings;
    
- furniture;
    
- personal items;
    
- chemistry materials;
    
- books;
    
- clothing;
    
- and other objects associated with her normal life.
    

The room's original doorway remains functional.

However, crossing the threshold now leads into Neri's modified interior space.

---

# 6. THE TWO ROOMS ARE CONNECTED

The most significant development is that Neri did not create two isolated subspaces.

She connected them.

The unused room now acts as a **communal subspace access point**, while Neri's bedroom forms another node within the same localized network.

The relationship can be represented as:

**SAFEHOUSE**

↓

**Unused Room**

↓

**Neri's Subspace Network**

↓

**Neri's Room**

The connections do not require conventional hallways between the rooms.

Neri's ability allows the subspaces to be interconnected independently of ordinary physical distance.

---

# 7. WHY THIS MATTERS

This is the first confirmed instance of Neri using her ability for something resembling **architectural expansion rather than emergency escape or self-isolation.**

Previously, her subspaces were associated with hiding and separation.

Now she is using the same ability to create additional usable space for the SAFEHOUSE.

This distinction is considered significant.

Neri is no longer only learning:

> **"I can break reality."**

She is learning:

> **"I can build with it."**

Neko has reportedly become extremely interested in this development.

---

# 8. NERI'S REASONING

When asked why she modified her room as well as the unused room, Neri gave a surprisingly practical answer:

> **"My room was small."**

Pause.

> **"The other room was empty."**

Pause.

> **"So I made them bigger."**

No further explanation was provided.

Neko reportedly stared at her for several seconds before responding:

> **"...That's actually a pretty good use of the ability."**

---

# 9. SAFETY CONSIDERATIONS

The modification is currently considered stable.

Neri demonstrated that she can reverse the process and restore the original configuration.

However, C.O.R.E. has established several restrictions.

Neri is not permitted to modify:

- structural supports;
    
- critical infrastructure;
    
- emergency exits;
    
- containment facilities;
    
- power systems;
    
- medical areas;
    
- or any occupied room without permission.
    

The current rooms remain approved because they are stable, reversible, and under Neri's direct control.

## FINAL OBSERVATION

Neri's development is beginning to show an interesting behavioral shift.

Her first major uses of her Architect-derived abilities were defensive:

**Hide.**

**Disappear.**

**Keep everyone away.**

Her current use is considerably simpler:

**Make the room bigger.**

She is still hiding her wings.

She is still insisting that she would rather be normal.

She is still sitting in the cafeteria eating cake while reading chemistry.

But now, apparently, if she thinks a room is too small, she can simply make reality disagree.
