# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **MEDICAL / PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT REPORT**

# **CORIN — WING INJURY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Incident Type:** **Self-Inflicted Wing Injury**  
**Status:** Stabilized / Flight Temporarily Suspended

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin sustained a serious injury to one of her wings after deliberately stabbing it with a knife.

The injury was self-inflicted.

The weapon penetrated the wing tissue and damaged several internal structures.

Corin survived the incident.

Her regenerative abilities have begun repairing the damage, but the injury is severe enough that she is currently **unable to fly**.

---

# **DISCOVERY**

Corin was found shortly after the incident.

The knife had already been removed.

She was sitting quietly, with one wing hanging unnaturally at her side.

Blood and Ether were present around the wound.

Her other wing remained folded tightly around her body.

Corin did not attempt to hide what had happened.

She simply looked down.

She did not provide an explanation.

---

# **MEDICAL RESPONSE**

C.O.R.E. medical personnel treated the injury immediately.

The damaged wing was carefully cleaned and stabilized.

The wound was closed and extensively bandaged.

Additional support was applied to prevent unnecessary movement while the tissue repairs itself.

Corin's regenerative abilities are functioning normally.

However, the wings are complex organs.

Regeneration cannot instantly restore them to full strength.

---

# **FLIGHT RESTRICTION**

Corin is temporarily prohibited from flying.

Attempting to fly before the injured wing has fully recovered could:

- Reopen the wound.
    
- Damage regenerating tissue.
    
- Cause additional bleeding.
    
- Delay recovery.
    
- Permanently complicate wing function.
    

Corin has been informed of the restriction.

She did not argue.

---

# **THE WINGS**

The injury is particularly significant because Corin relies heavily on her wings.

Flight has become her preferred method of transportation.

Her wings also provide comfort and protection.

She routinely uses them like a blanket when sleeping.

With one wing injured, she has lost both an important mobility method and one of her primary means of physically shielding herself.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

After being treated, Corin remained unusually quiet.

She carefully touched the bandages.

Then she looked at the injured wing.

> **"...I can't fly."**

The medical personnel confirmed that she would eventually be able to fly again.

Corin did not respond.

She simply lowered her head.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL SIGNIFICANCE**

The incident occurred during Corin's current period of severe emotional distress.

It is therefore being treated as a serious psychological warning rather than an isolated accident.

Her previous statements regarding self-worth, her past, and her inability to escape herself are considered relevant to the incident.

C.O.R.E. has increased observation and support around her.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The residents have been instructed not to shame or interrogate Corin.

The immediate priority is keeping her safe while her wing heals.

Orphie has remained nearby.

Neko has also taken additional precautions around potentially dangerous objects.

The SAFEHOUSE kitchen knives have been secured.

Other sharp equipment has similarly been placed out of casual reach.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie eventually sat beside Corin.

She looked at the bandaged wing.

Then at Corin.

> **"You don't have to explain right now."**

Corin remained silent.

Orphie continued:

> **"But I'm staying."**

Corin's eyes began to water.

Her uninjured wing slowly moved toward Orphie.

It wrapped around her.

---

# **RECOVERY**

Corin's regeneration is expected to restore the damaged tissue.

The exact recovery time remains uncertain.

During recovery:

- Flight is prohibited.
    
- Excessive wing movement is discouraged.
    
- Bandages must remain intact.
    
- The injured wing must be monitored.
    
- Corin should not be left completely isolated for prolonged periods.
    

Her normal mobility remains available.

She can walk normally.

She simply cannot take to the air.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

|Condition|Status|

|Bleeding|**Controlled**|
|Regeneration|**Active**|

|Flight|**Prohibited**|

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's wings once represented freedom.

They allowed her to leave the ground whenever she wanted.

They gave her a way to escape places that made her uncomfortable.

She could fly toward the Blacksite.

She could carry someone she cared about.

She could disappear into the sky.

For now, she cannot.

One of the things that made Corin feel most free has become another part of her that needs time to heal.

Her body will likely repair the wound.

The harder part will be helping Corin understand that she does not need to hurt herself to express how much she is hurting.

For now, she remains grounded.

And someone remains beside her.

**End of Medical / Psychological Incident Report**
