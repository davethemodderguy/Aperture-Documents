## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE — NIGHTTIME OBSERVATION LOG**

# **CORIN — NOCTURNAL LUNA ENCOUNTERS**

**Classification:** Level 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE  
**Subjects:** Corin / Corey-Luna

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has experienced recurring, severe nightmares.

When awakened during the night, she occasionally leaves her room before returning to sleep.

Due to Corey's tendency to naturally manifest as **Luna** during nighttime, Corin has repeatedly encountered Luna while wandering through SAFEHOUSE.

These encounters have become an unexpected source of comfort for Corin.

---

# **THE NIGHTTIME ROUTINE**

Corin generally wakes without immediately realizing where she is.

The SAFEHOUSE is quiet.

The lights are low.

Most residents are asleep.

Marts are usually scattered throughout the building, sleeping wherever they happen to have settled.

Corin may walk through the hallways until she encounters a faint azure glow.

That glow usually leads to Corey.

Or rather:

> **Luna.**

---

# **LUNA'S SLEEPING STATE**

Corey is normally unaware of Corin's presence when this occurs.

She is usually:

- Floating several centimeters above the floor.
    
- Surrounded by soft azure light.
    
- Sleeping peacefully.
    
- Covered by her lunar wings.
    
- Accompanied by several Marts.
    

The lunar gravitational field keeps her gently suspended.

Marts often float nearby.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin quickly learned that Luna does not pose a threat.

The first few encounters were startling.

Eventually, they became familiar.

When Corin sees Luna floating in the darkness, she usually stops nearby.

Sometimes she simply sits down.

Sometimes she quietly speaks.

> "...Luna?"

No response.

Luna continues sleeping.

Corin waits.

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts often notice Corin before Luna does.

A nearby Mart may slowly drift toward her.

> "...Bad dream?"

Corin may nod.

> "...Yeah."

The Mart moves closer.

> "...Okay."

More Marts sometimes follow.

Within minutes, Corin can find herself sitting beside Luna while surrounded by a small group of warm, quiet Marts.

---

# **LUNA'S GRAVITATIONAL FIELD**

Corin's presence does not appear to disturb Luna's sleep.

Instead, Luna's gravitational field gently pulls nearby Marts closer.

Corin occasionally finds herself surrounded by them as well.

The effect is subtle.

There is no forceful movement.

It simply feels as though the room has become quieter.

---

# **WHEN LUNA WAKES**

On some occasions, Luna eventually wakes.

Her first reaction is usually confusion.

> "...Corin?"

Corin:

> "...Sorry."

Luna looks at her.

> "...Nightmare?"

Corin nods.

Luna does not ask for details.

She simply moves closer.

> "...Stay."

Corin does.

---

# **SILENCE**

Their conversations are often very short.

Corin does not always want to talk about the nightmares.

Luna understands.

Sometimes they simply sit together.

No questions.

No explanations.

Just quiet.

Eventually, Corin begins to relax.

---

# **CORIN'S OBSERVATION**

Corin was asked why she keeps returning to Luna after waking from nightmares.

Her answer:

> "...She's quiet."

She paused.

> "...The Marts are quiet too."

Another pause.

> "...It feels safe."

---

# **COREY'S RESPONSE**

After learning about these encounters, Corey was surprised.

> "...I didn't know."

Corin replied:

> "...You were asleep."

Corey laughed softly.

> "...Fair."

She then added:

> "...You're always welcome."

---

# **LUMI'S OBSERVATION**

Lumi considers the behavior unsurprising.

She explained:

> "...Sometimes people don't need answers."

> "...They just need someone nearby."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Some nights, Corin wakes from a nightmare.

She leaves her room.

The SAFEHOUSE is dark.

She walks through the quiet halls.

Eventually, she finds the familiar azure glow.

Luna is floating peacefully.

Marts drift around her.

Corin sits down.

A Mart moves closer.

Luna slowly opens her eyes.

> "...Bad dream?"

Corin nods.

Luna gently reaches toward her.

> "...It's okay."

And for the rest of the night, Corin doesn't have to be alone.

**End of Project SAFEHOUSE Nighttime Observation Log**
