# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — THE RAIN DRAWING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** **PSYCHOLOGICAL CONCERN / CONTINUOUS SUPPORT**

---

Alice sat quietly on the edge of her bed.

Her small rabbit tail rested against the blanket behind her.

Her long ears had fallen naturally downward under their own weight.

Her face was soft.

Her eyes were wet.

She wasn't crying loudly.

Just small, quiet sobs.

Her pencil remained in her hand.

---

# **THE DRAWING**

Alice drew herself standing beneath heavy rain.

There was no umbrella.

No shelter.

No one beside her.

Her white hair was soaked.

Her clothes clung to her body.

Water ran down her face.

The expression was not exaggerated.

Alice simply looked tired.

Alone.

Cold.

Small.

At the bottom of the page was one sentence.

> **"Alice unwanted, Alice make everyone tired."**

---

# **OBSERVATION**

The drawing contains no depiction of Alice asking for help.

She does not draw herself reaching toward anyone.

She does not draw anyone rejecting her.

There is simply Alice beneath the rain.

Alone.

The absence of other people appears intentional.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin found Alice sitting with the finished drawing.

She looked at the page.

Then at Alice.

Alice quickly lowered the drawing.

Corin didn't take it away.

She sat beside her.

Alice's ears remained lowered.

Corin gently touched one.

> **"You know that's not true."**

Alice didn't answer.

She couldn't.

Her pencil moved instead.

She wrote beneath her original sentence:

> **"Feels true."**

Corin's heart sank.

She pulled Alice into a hug.

Alice resisted for only a moment.

Then folded into her.

Her tiny tail pressed against Corin's arm.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi eventually saw the drawing.

She didn't correct Alice immediately.

Instead, she sat on the floor beside her.

She took another sheet of paper.

She drew the same scene.

Rain.

Alice.

No umbrella.

Then Lumi added one thing.

A person standing beside Alice.

Lumi handed the drawing over.

Alice stared at it.

She pointed toward the person.

Lumi nodded.

> **"That's me."**

Alice looked down.

Then drew another person beside them.

Corin.

She added another.

Orphie.

Then Belle.

Then Star.

Soon the little empty scene had become crowded.

Alice stopped drawing.

Her eyes filled with tears again.

---

# **ALICE'S LAST ADDITION**

Alice carefully erased the words:

> **"Alice unwanted."**

She did not erase:

> **"Alice make everyone tired."**

Instead, she crossed it out.

Then underneath it, in small handwriting, she wrote:

> **"Alice learning."**

Lumi stared at the sentence.

She smiled through tears.

> **"Yeah."**

> **"You're learning."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Alice still believes she is difficult to care for.

She still believes her weakness exhausts everyone.

She still worries that needing help makes her unwanted.

But something has changed.

For the first time, when Alice drew herself alone—

she eventually drew someone beside her.

Then another.

Then another.

The rain remained.

Alice remained small.

Her ears remained lowered.

Her face remained sad.

But she was no longer alone beneath it.

And perhaps, for now, that is enough.
