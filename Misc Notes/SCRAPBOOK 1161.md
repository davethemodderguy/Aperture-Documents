# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

Following his successful completion of the Hell Fire Lottery, the fifteen-year-old MSAC operator now possesses three Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise items:

- **NS-009 — Night Stalker**
    
- **SOH-004 — Sleight of Hand**
    
- **HF-003 — Hell Fire**
    

Neri subsequently provided the operator with a rather unusual piece of advice.

She was not discussing tactical equipment arrangement.

She was discussing:

> **Flexing.**

---

# 2. NERI'S INSTRUCTIONS

During a conversation concerning the operator's newly acquired Hell Fire, Neri looked at him and stated:

> **NERI:**  
> “Shotgun on your back, Night Stalker on your waist and Sleight of Hand slung in front of your chest.”

The operator paused.

> **15-YEAR-OLD OPERATOR:**  
> “...Why?”

Neri answered without hesitation:

> **NERI:**  
> “That's how you flex the merch to the less fortunate ones.”

Silence.

The operator looked down at himself.

Then at Neri.

> **15-YEAR-OLD OPERATOR:**  
> “You're telling me to show off?”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

---

# 3. C.O.R.E. INTERPRETATION

The recommended arrangement is apparently intended purely as a visual display of ownership.

### BACK

**HF-003 — Hell Fire**

### WAIST

**NS-009 — Night Stalker**

### FRONT CHEST

**SOH-004 — Sleight of Hand**

The resulting configuration makes all three Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise items immediately visible.

It is, according to Neri:

> **“The flex.”**

---

# 4. MSAC REACTION

The operator subsequently walked through the MSAC area wearing the three items in the suggested arrangement.

Several personnel stopped talking.

One looked at the Hell Fire.

Then the Night Stalker.

Then the Sleight of Hand.

Then at the operator.

> **MSAC OPERATOR:**  
> “You've got all three?”

The fifteen-year-old nodded.

> **MSAC OPERATOR:**  
> “How?”

> **15-YEAR-OLD OPERATOR:**  
> “Won them.”

> **MSAC OPERATOR:**  
> “All three?”

> **15-YEAR-OLD OPERATOR:**  
> “Yeah.”

A second operator quietly walked away.

---

# 5. ELSHER'S RESPONSE

Elsher encountered him shortly afterward.

He stopped.

Looked at the three pieces.

Then sighed.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “You're doing the Neri thing.”

> **15-YEAR-OLD OPERATOR:**  
> “She told me to.”

Elsher nodded slowly.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “Yeah. That sounds like her.”

He then looked down at his own equipment.

Elsher currently possesses:

- NS-006;
    
- SOH-002;
    
- HF-001.
    

He stared at the fifteen-year-old.

The fifteen-year-old stared back.

There was a brief silence.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “...We're both ridiculous.”

> **15-YEAR-OLD OPERATOR:**  
> “Yeah.”

---

# 6. MSAC COMMANDER

The MSAC Commander was informed of the display.
