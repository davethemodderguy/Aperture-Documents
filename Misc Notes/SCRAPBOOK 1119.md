# 1. TRIAL ANNOUNCEMENT

The final trial for **Night Stalker "Regalia"** has begun.

There will be no competition.

No tournament.

No endurance test.

No marksmanship exercise.

No hidden-object challenge.

No prize pool.

No leaderboard.

There is only one question.

Neri personally authored it.

The question is:

> **“Are you my friend because I am a Tachyon or you love me?”**

The wording has been preserved exactly.

---

# 2. PURPOSE OF THE QUESTION

At first glance, personnel assumed the question was testing loyalty.

It is not.

It is testing something considerably more difficult.

Neri is attempting to determine whether people care about **Neri**—

or whether they care about what the name:

> **TACHYON**

represents.

This distinction has become increasingly important since the Blacksite learned that Neri Tachyon is the final surviving member of the Tachyon family.

The Tachyon name carries historical significance.

Aperture exists partly because of the work of her family.

Night Stalker carries the Tachyon Industries name.

Neri herself created the weapon.

She is therefore surrounded by people who suddenly have a reason to regard her as important.

And she does not know whether that importance belongs to:

> **Neri**

or:

> **Tachyon.**

---

# 3. THE PROBLEM WITH THE QUESTION

There is a complication.

Neri does not appear to believe that the answer is obvious.

She has received many answers.

None have satisfied her.

Some were technically correct.

Some were emotionally sincere.

Some were extremely thoughtful.

Some were clearly rehearsed.

Some were attempts to say exactly what Neri wanted to hear.

She rejected all of them.

---

# 5. PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION

C.O.R.E. believes the difficulty is not primarily with the answers.

It is with Neri's interpretation of herself.

Neri has spent a substantial portion of her life operating under a simple internal rule:

> **A person has value when they are useful.**

Her family reinforced this.

Her experiences reinforced it.

Loss reinforced it.

Her own survival reinforced it.

When she was useful, she had a reason to remain.

When she was not useful, she expected to be discarded.

This framework did not disappear when Neri entered SAFEHOUSE.

It merely became more complicated.

---

# 6. THE TACHYON PROBLEM

Neri now has something she never had before:

A name that people consider important.

**Tachyon.**

People know the family.

They know what the family contributed to Aperture.

They know what Neri created.

They know the significance of Night Stalker.

They know she is the final surviving Tachyon.

Neri notices all of this.

And it creates a new fear.

> **“Do they want me?”**

or:

> **“Do they want what I am?”**

To an emotionally secure individual, these questions might have a straightforward answer.

To Neri, they are nearly indistinguishable.

She does not possess a stable internal definition of why someone would love her without needing something from her.

---

# 7. NERI'S SELF-PERCEPTION

The most significant finding from the trial is that Neri does not consistently perceive herself as a person who can simply be loved.

She can understand the concept intellectually.

She struggles to apply it to herself.

When someone says:

> **“I love you.”**

Neri may unconsciously translate it into:

> **“You are useful to me.”**

When someone says:

> **“You're important.”**

She may hear:

> **“You have value because of what you can provide.”**

When someone says:

> **“You're special.”**

She may wonder:

> **“Because I'm a Tachyon?”**

When someone says:

> **“You're my friend.”**

She may ask:

> **“Why?”**

Not because she is being difficult.

Because she genuinely does not know.

---

# 8. STRUGGLE WITH SELF-CONTROL

Personnel have also observed that Neri is having difficulty **keeping herself emotionally regulated during the trial.**

She understands that she is supposed to evaluate the answers.

She tries.

But certain responses cause immediate emotional reactions before she can suppress them.

Her behavior has included:

- abruptly leaving;
    
- becoming visibly tense;
    
- staring at the floor;
    
- refusing to look at the respondent;
    
- gripping her coat sleeves;
    
- becoming unusually quiet;
    
- shaking her head repeatedly;
    
- becoming frustrated when someone gives an answer she cannot reconcile with her own beliefs;
    
- asking the same question in slightly different forms;
    
- and occasionally becoming angry before appearing upset with herself for becoming angry.
    

The last behavior is particularly significant.

Neri appears to believe that emotional reactions themselves are another form of burden.

She becomes upset.

Then becomes upset that she became upset.

Then attempts to suppress the entire reaction.

This frequently makes the original emotion worse.

---

# 9. OBSERVED INTERNAL CONFLICT

One interaction is considered particularly important.

A respondent answered:

> **“I'd still love you if the Tachyon name disappeared tomorrow.”**

Neri immediately replied:

> **“Why?”**

The respondent answered:

> **“Because you're still Neri.”**

Neri looked down.

Several seconds passed.

Then:

> **“But what if I wasn't worth it?”**

The respondent did not answer immediately.

Neri appeared to realize what she had said.

She covered her mouth.

> **“...Sorry.”**

The respondent:

> **“You don't need to apologize.”**

Neri:

> **“I know.”**

Pause.

> **“I just don't know how to stop.”**

The respondent did not pursue the conversation.

Neri eventually left.

---

# 10. THE QUESTION BENEATH THE QUESTION

C.O.R.E. believes the actual question is not:

> **“Are you my friend because I am a Tachyon or you love me?”**

The actual question may be:

> **“If you remove everything valuable about me, is there still enough left for you to want me?”**

Neri does not know how to answer that question herself.

That is why she cannot easily recognize a satisfactory answer from somebody else.

She is asking people to provide evidence for a premise she has not yet accepted:

> **That Neri herself is enough.**

---

# 11. WHY NO ANSWER HAS PASSED

The applicants continue trying to find the perfect wording.

There isn't one.

Neri has rejected:

> “You're my friend because you're you.”

She has rejected:

> “I'd love you regardless of your family.”

She has rejected:

> “Your name doesn't matter.”

She has rejected:

> “I love you.”

She has rejected:

> “Both.”

She has rejected increasingly elaborate explanations.

The common problem is that each answer attempts to **prove** something to Neri.

But Neri is not looking for proof.

She is looking for something she can believe.

Those are not the same thing.

---

# 12. NERI'S CURRENT STATE

The trial remains open.

Neri has not authorized NS-010.

She has not rejected the applicants permanently.

She continues listening.

She continues asking.

She continues becoming frustrated.

She continues trying to keep herself composed.

And she continues failing sometimes.

This is not considered a failure of the trial.

It may actually be the most important part of it.

For perhaps the first time, Neri is being forced to confront a question about herself that cannot be solved through chemistry.

She cannot calculate it.

She cannot research it.

She cannot optimize it.

She cannot manufacture an answer.

She cannot make herself useful enough to guarantee it.

She simply has to consider the possibility that someone might love:

> **Neri.**

Not the Tachyon.

Not the chemist.

Not the creator of Night Stalker.

Not the artificial Thiren.

Not the survivor.

Not the final member of an important family.

Just:

> **Neri.**

---

# 13. FINAL OBSERVATION

The museum remains open.

NS-010 Regalia remains behind Nekonium-plated glass.

The gold case remains untouched.

The three loaded magazines remain inside.

Neri's handwritten letter remains sealed with the presentation set.

The podium still reads:

> **“The Final Sleight of Hand, up for grabs. There will be no more competitions, you must earn it. I'm always watching”**
> 
> **—Neri**

But the final trial has revealed something nobody expected.

The person deciding who deserves Regalia is struggling with whether **she herself deserves to be wanted.**

And every time someone gives her an answer she cannot accept, Neri has to fight the same internal conclusion:

> **“They must want something from me.”**

She knows that conclusion is not necessarily true.

She simply does not know how to make herself believe otherwise.

For now, the final Night Stalker remains unassigned.

Not because nobody has answered.

But because Neri is still trying to figure out what answer she is actually capable of believing.
