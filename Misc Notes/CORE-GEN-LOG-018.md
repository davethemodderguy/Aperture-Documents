# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-GEN-LOG-018**

### **STAFF CULTURE – "THIREN PRIVILEGES"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Staff Welfare

---

## **SUMMARY**

An unofficial Blacksite tradition has developed regarding residents classified as **Thirens**.

Current Thiren residents include:

- Z-321 – Endmin (Cat Thiren)
    
- Z-885 – Taiga (Snow Leopard Thiren)
    
- Z-886 – Mart (Snow Owl Thiren)
    
- Z-901 – Neko (Cat Thiren)
    
- Z-902 – Miss Neko (Cat Thiren)
    

Due to their animal traits and generally approachable personalities, many researchers have jokingly coined what they call **"Thiren Privileges."**

Despite the name, these interactions always occur **with the resident's consent**.

---

## **The "Privileges"**

Among staff, the phrase usually refers to harmless, friendly interactions such as:

- Asking for permission to pat their ears.
    
- Receiving hugs.
    
- Cuddling while watching movies or reading.
    
- Sitting together during breaks.
    
- Occasionally brushing fur or feathers if requested.
    

Behavioral Sciences notes that these interactions have become an effective morale booster for many employees.

---

## **Perimeter Security**

The tradition is particularly popular among the Blacksite's Perimeter Security Division.

Due to rotating overnight shifts, extended patrols, and the stress associated with defending an anomalous facility, many guards struggle with chronic fatigue and poor sleep.

During scheduled downtime, several guards have remarked that spending a few quiet minutes with the Thiren residents helps them relax.

One veteran guard summarized it best.

> **"You'd be surprised how much better your day gets after a headpat from someone who's genuinely okay with it."**

Medical Division emphasizes that these interactions are voluntary and should never interfere with operational duties.

---

## **Individual Preferences**

### Endmin

Initially became extremely embarrassed whenever someone politely asked to pet her ears.

She often tried to act composed.

> "...I'm allowing this strictly for morale."

Seconds later she would be quietly purring without noticing.

When informed she was purring...

She became even more embarrassed.

---

### Taiga

Actively enjoys the attention.

> "Come here!"

> "Group hug!"

Taiga has unintentionally become one of the Blacksite's unofficial morale officers.

---

### Mart

Generally accepts hugs and headpats without complaint.

He does, however, prefer quiet company over large crowds.

---

### Neko

Pretends not to care.

He has nevertheless been observed sitting completely still while someone scratches behind one ear.

When asked about it, he replied:

> "...Five more seconds."

---

### Miss Neko

Calmly accepts affectionate gestures from staff she knows well.

She has also become popular among newer researchers due to her patient and reassuring personality.

---

## **Behavioral Sciences Note**

Researchers emphasize an important distinction.

The Thiren residents are **people**, not therapy animals.

Their willingness to share friendly affection is viewed as a sign of mutual trust rather than an obligation.

Staff members are expected to ask permission and respect boundaries.

A simple "no" is to be accepted immediately.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

The unofficial "Thiren Privileges" tradition remains a small but well-liked part of daily life at the Blacksite.

For many employees, especially those coming off long overnight patrols, a quiet conversation, a shared cup of coffee, or—if the Thiren is comfortable with it—a hug or headpat has become a welcome way to unwind.

### **Closing Observation**

At the end of a particularly long winter night, a Perimeter Security officer returned from patrol looking exhausted.

He noticed Endmin sitting in the lobby with Aster curled in her lap.

He hesitated.

> **Guard:** "...May I?"

Endmin looked up.

She smiled softly.

> "...Headpat?"

The guard nodded.

> "...If you're okay with it."

Endmin lowered her head slightly.

> "...Go ahead."

A gentle pat on her ears lasted only a few seconds.

When the guard thanked her and walked away, he looked noticeably more relaxed.

Taiga, who had witnessed the exchange, nudged Endmin with a grin.

> **Taiga:** "See? You made someone's day."

Endmin folded her arms, trying to look serious.

> "...It was an officially sanctioned morale inspection."

From her lap, Aster slowly lifted her whiteboard.

It read:

> **"Sure it was."**

Endmin sighed as everyone nearby chuckled.
