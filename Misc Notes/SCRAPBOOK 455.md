### **CELESTIAL ARCHITECTURE / CAPABILITY ADDENDUM**

# **THE ARCHITECTS — WEAPON STATUS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – CELESTIAL AUTHORITY  
**Subjects:** Guiding Light / Curious Light  
**Designation:** **ARCHITECTS**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The Architects do not require conventional weapons.

In practical terms, there is little purpose in providing either Guiding Light or Curious Light with a weapon when their authority extends to the fundamental existence of things.

Their capabilities can be summarized in one word:

> **Everything.**

---

# **EXISTENCE DENIAL**

The Architects possess the ability to **deny the existence of something**.

This is fundamentally different from destroying an object.

A weapon destroys.

An Architect can simply decide that something **does not exist**.

The distinction makes conventional weaponry effectively redundant.

---

# **WHY THEY DON'T NEED WEAPONS**

A firearm requires:

- Ammunition.
    
- Targeting.
    
- Physical interaction.
    
- A mechanism capable of causing damage.
    

An Architect requires none of these.

There is no meaningful advantage to handing an Architect a weapon when they can manipulate the underlying rules governing existence itself.

---

# **THEORETICAL EXAMPLE**

If an ordinary individual attempted to attack an Architect with a weapon, the Architect would not necessarily need to block the attack.

They could simply deny the relevant object existed.

The weapon could cease to exist.

The ammunition could cease to exist.

The attack could cease to exist.

The attacker could theoretically cease to exist.

The exact scope of the Architects' authority remains undefined.

---

# **PERSONNEL WARNING**

This does **not** mean personnel should treat the Architects as dangerous by default.

Both Guiding Light and Curious Light are generally friendly.

They can be hugged.

They can socialize.

They can cook.

They can experiment.

They can joke.

They simply possess an absurd level of authority that should not be mistaken for ordinary physical power.

Their restraint is therefore far more important than their weaponry.

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT'S RESPONSE**

When asked whether he wanted a weapon during a Blacksite visit:

> **Personnel:**  
> "Do you want us to issue you something?"

Curious Light looked confused.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "...Why?"

> **Personnel:**  
> "For protection."

He stared for a moment.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "...From what?"

The personnel member had no answer.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S RESPONSE**

When asked the same question, Guiding Light simply smiled.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "That's very kind."

> **"But I don't need one."**

---

# **FINAL ASSESSMENT**

The Architects possess no standard weapon classification.

Not because they lack the ability to use weapons.

Because they have **no practical need for them**.

Their authority predates conventional concepts of combat.

A weapon acts upon existence.

The Architects can decide what existence is allowed to contain.

Therefore:

> **Architects don't need weapons.**

> **They are the authority behind the rules weapons operate under.**

**End of Celestial Architecture / Capability Addendum**
