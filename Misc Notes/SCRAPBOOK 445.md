**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – CELESTIAL AUTHORITY

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Guiding Light has finally manifested herself.

For the first time, personnel are able to directly observe the Celestial Advisor rather than communicating through notes, letters, blue light, or her disembodied voice.

Her appearance is unmistakably celestial.

She manifests as a **blue spectral humanoid**, surrounded by a soft blue radiance.

Despite her ghostlike appearance, she is fully capable of interacting with her surroundings.

---

# **APPEARANCE**

Guiding Light possesses a humanoid form composed primarily of translucent blue spectral energy.

Her body emits a gentle blue glow.

She does not appear solid in the conventional sense, although she can choose to interact physically with objects.

Her most prominent features include:

- A **blue halo** floating above her head.
    
- Large celestial **wings**.
    
- A **crescent moon-shaped symbol** embedded on her chest.
    
- A dark headband covering her eyes.
    
- A distinctly spectral, ghostlike appearance.
    

The headband appears to be intrinsically connected to her manifested form.

It is not ordinary clothing.

Attempts to remove, damage, or otherwise interfere with it have not been successful.

---

# **THE HEADBAND**

Guiding Light's eyes remain completely covered.

No one has observed what lies beneath the headband.

This does not appear to limit her perception.

Quite the opposite.

She possesses what has been classified as:

> **ULTIMATE PERCEPTION**

---

# **ULTIMATE PERCEPTION**

Guiding Light does not require conventional eyesight.

She can perceive everything relevant to her regardless of whether she has a direct line of sight.

Known examples include:

- Observing events across vast distances.
    
- Perceiving activity across dimensions.
    
- Detecting individuals without physically seeing them.
    
- Perceiving concealed objects.
    
- Understanding events occurring outside conventional space.
    
- Observing temporal phenomena.
    
- Detecting changes to reality.
    

Her covered eyes therefore appear to be symbolic rather than functional.

When asked why she covers her eyes, Guiding Light simply replied:

> **"I don't need them."**

---

# **THE WINGS**

Guiding Light possesses a pair of large blue wings.

The inner portions resemble spectral feathers or luminous celestial structures.

The **outer edges are composed of floating shards**.

The shards do not remain physically connected.

They hover around the wings in organized formations, moving subtly as Guiding Light moves.

Despite the apparent lack of conventional structure, the wings function perfectly.

Guiding Light can fly.

---

# **FLIGHT**

Guiding Light does not require physical surfaces or propulsion to remain airborne.

She can:

- Hover.
    
- Fly silently.
    
- Change direction instantly.
    
- Remain stationary in midair.
    
- Move vertically without momentum.
    
- Traverse dimensional boundaries.
    

Her wings appear to be partly responsible for this ability, although it is unclear whether they are actually necessary.

When asked:

> **"Do you need your wings to fly?"**

Guiding Light replied:

> **"No."**

After a short pause:

> **"...They're pretty, though."**

---

# **THE CRESCENT**

A glowing **crescent moon** is present on Guiding Light's chest.

The symbol is integrated into her spectral body.

It appears to function as more than decoration.

Its luminosity changes subtly depending on Guiding Light's emotional state and level of activity.

The symbol has become her most recognizable identifying mark.

A soft blue glow appeared in the central living area.

The glow intensified.

Fragments of blue light gathered together.

Then—

she was there.

Floating several inches above the floor.

Her wings slowly unfolded.

The floating shards along their edges arranged themselves into place.

The crescent on her chest glowed softly.

Everyone stared.

Guiding Light looked around.

Her eyes remained covered.

> "...Hello."

Star stared upward.

> **Star:**

Guiding Light smiled.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "I've been real the entire time."
