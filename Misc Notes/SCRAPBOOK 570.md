## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE RECOVERY OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — SUNFLOWERS WERE THE ANSWER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Event:** **Post-Sleep Recovery**  
**Status:** Stable / Significantly Improved

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's first proper sleep in twenty-five weeks continued well into the evening.

She eventually fell asleep in Orphie's arms.

Her wings instinctively folded around herself, covering her body like a blanket.

For the first time in months, Corin looked completely peaceful.

---

# **RETURN TO THE TREEHOUSE**

Nobody wanted to wake her.

Orphie carefully carried Corin back from the sunflower field.

Corin remained asleep throughout the journey.

Her wings stayed wrapped around her.

Only the tips of her ears and part of her face were visible between the feathers.

The group returned her to her bed.

Orphie gently placed her down.

Corin barely moved.

She simply curled up again.

Her wings remained around her body.

---

# **THE NIGHT**

Corin slept through the entire night.

No nightmares were observed.

No sudden awakenings occurred.

No wandering.

No pretending.

No hiding in the corner.

Just sleep.

After twenty-five weeks of constant wakefulness, the change was remarkable.

---

# **THE NEXT MORNING**

Corin woke up normally.

She opened her eyes.

Looked around.

And smiled.

It was not the faint, forced smile she had occasionally used before.

It was genuine.

She stretched.

Her wings opened.

Her two tails moved happily behind her.

Then she looked at Orphie.

> **"Morning."**

Orphie stared at her.

> **"...Morning."**

Corin smiled wider.

> **"I'm hungry."**

Orphie almost cried.

---

# **THE RETURN OF CORIN**

Throughout the morning, Corin behaved like herself again.

She talked.

She laughed.

She wandered around the treehouse.

She made tea.

She played with yarn.

She opened her wings and stretched them.

She even started talking about returning to the sunflower field.

The difference was immediate.

The severe psychological symptoms observed during her prolonged sleeplessness had dramatically diminished after proper rest.

---

# **THE DISCOVERY**

The Architects eventually reached an unexpectedly simple conclusion.

Corin had not necessarily been searching for a medical solution.

She had been searching for something she loved.

Something peaceful.

Something beautiful.

Something that reminded her that she was allowed to exist somewhere without having to fight anything.

Apparently:

**Sunflowers.**

A lot of sunflowers.

---

# **STAR'S RESPONSE**

Star was extremely pleased with herself.

> **"I knew she'd like it."**

Neko looked at her.

> **"You just happened to know about a giant sunflower field."**

Star shrugged.

> **"It's the Starlands."**

Neko accepted this explanation.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie remained particularly relieved.

She had spent weeks watching Corin deteriorate.

Now Corin was sitting beside her, drinking tea and talking normally.

Orphie asked:

> **"Do you feel better?"**

Corin nodded.

> **"Yeah."**

She paused.

> **"...I really like that field."**

Orphie smiled.

> **"Then we'll go again."**

Corin's ears perked up.

> **"Really?"**

> **"Really."**

Corin immediately looked happy.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Twenty-five weeks of sleeplessness had left everyone convinced that something fundamentally wrong had happened to Corin.

They searched for anomalous causes.

They searched for medical explanations.

They searched for ways to repair her.

In the end, Corin apparently wanted something much simpler.

A quiet field.

Sunlight.

Thousands of sunflowers.

Someone she trusted.

And a place where she could finally stop being afraid for a while.

The next morning, she woke up happy.

Star has since jokingly proposed renaming the location:

> **"Corin's Field."**

Corin approved immediately.
