### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / UNREGISTERED DEVELOPMENT REPORT**

# **CORIN — WING DEVELOPMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **Spontaneous Wing Development**  
**Architectal Involvement:** **NONE CONFIRMED**  
**Status:** **ACTIVE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has developed a pair of fully functional wings.

The wings consist of both **living flesh and feathers**, attached directly to her back.

Their development was not caused by:

- Guiding Light.
    
- Curious Light.
    
- Mischevious Light.
    
- Any other Architect.
    
- Deliberate medical intervention.
    
- Ether manipulation performed by external personnel.
    

The wings appeared **spontaneously while Corin was asleep.**

No Architect was present when the development occurred.

No known procedure was performed.

C.O.R.E. currently has no confirmed explanation for their origin.

---

# **INITIAL DISCOVERY**

Corin went to sleep normally.

When she woke, she immediately noticed something pressing against the back of her clothing.

At first, she assumed one of her limbs had become displaced.

It was not.

Two large wings had emerged from her back.

Corin remained still for several seconds.

She eventually called for Orphie.

> **"Orphie?"**

Orphie entered the room.

She stopped.

> **"...What?"**

Corin turned slightly.

The wings unfolded.

Neither of them knew what to say.

---

# **PHYSICAL STRUCTURE**

The wings are composed of biological tissue integrated directly into Corin's anomalous physiology.

They possess:

- Muscular structures.
    
- Bone-like internal supports.
    
- Bloodless vascular architecture.
    
- Ether-integrated connective tissue.
    
- Feathers.
    
- Functional joints.
    
- Full independent movement.
    

The wings fold neatly against Corin's back when not in use.

Despite their size, they do not appear to interfere significantly with Corin's normal movement.

---

# **FLIGHT**

Corin can fly using the wings.

Initial attempts were accidental.

She instinctively moved the wings and discovered that they responded naturally.

Within a short period of experimentation, Corin was capable of controlled flight.

Her Ether-based physiology appears to compensate for the physical stresses involved.

Her flight capabilities include:

- Vertical takeoff.
    
- Sustained flight.
    
- Rapid directional changes.
    
- Controlled descent.
    
- Hovering for limited periods.
    
- High-altitude movement.
    

The exact maximum flight speed has not yet been established.

C.O.R.E. has no immediate plans to test it.

---

# **FEATHER PROJECTION**

The wings possess a secondary defensive capability.

Corin can deliberately launch individual feathers.

Once separated from the wings, the feathers remain under her anomalous influence.

Upon reaching a selected point, they can produce a localized **airburst effect**.

The resulting burst is capable of producing a sudden pressure impact.

The feathers therefore function as short-range anomalous projectiles.

Corin appears capable of controlling their general trajectory before detonation.

---

# **AIRBURST EFFECT**

The projectile effect does not appear to be conventional explosive behavior.

The feathers themselves remain largely intact until activation.

The resulting discharge consists primarily of compressed air and Ether-mediated force.

Observed effects include:

- Sudden pressure waves.
    
- Disruption of nearby objects.
    
- Knockback.
    
- Temporary destabilization of targets.
    
- Short-range area denial.
    

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

---

# **REGENERATION**

Detached feathers can eventually regenerate.

The regeneration rate is currently being monitored.

Corin has demonstrated no significant pain response when voluntarily shedding individual feathers.

However, unnecessary testing has been prohibited.

The wings are part of Corin's body.

They are not considered disposable ammunition.

---

# **ARCHITECTAL INVOLVEMENT**

This section is emphasized.

### **NO ARCHITECT CREATED THE WINGS.**

Guiding Light did not create them.

Curious Light did not create them.

Mischevious Light did not create them.

No Architect intentionally modified Corin.

Guiding Light was particularly disturbed by the discovery because she initially assumed that the development might somehow be related to her previous intervention.

It was not.

Her own examination confirmed that she had no involvement.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> **"I didn't do this."**

Curious Light confirmed the finding.

> **"Neither did I."**

Mischevious Light examined the wings.

> **"...Then where did they come from?"**

No answer was available.

---

# **ETHER ANALYSIS**

The wings contain significant Ether integration.

However, this does not mean they were created directly from Ether.

Their structure is partially biological.

Their feathers are physically real.

Their musculature is functional.

Their integration with Corin's body is anomalous.

C.O.R.E. therefore currently classifies the wings as:

> **Spontaneous Ether-integrated biological development.**

The exact trigger remains unknown.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin initially regarded the wings with suspicion.

She eventually asked:

> **"Do I look more like a monster now?"**

Orphie immediately answered:

> **"No."**

Corin looked at the wings.

She slowly unfolded them.

> **"...They're pretty."**

Orphie smiled.

> **"Yeah."**

Corin paused.

Then:

> **"Can I fly?"**

> **"Probably."**

Corin looked toward the ceiling.

> **"...I want to try."**

---

# **FIRST FLIGHT**

Corin eventually exited the treehouse for the first time in an extended period because she wanted to test the wings.

She stood outside.

The forest was quiet.

She unfolded both wings.

She hesitated.

Then pushed herself upward.

For several seconds, she simply hovered.

She looked down.

Then upward.

Then laughed.

It was one of the first times Corin had willingly gone outside since her withdrawal.

She flew again.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL SIGNIFICANCE**

The wings have produced an unexpected development.

Corin still considers herself neither alive nor dead.

She still refers to herself as a thing.

She still dislikes her body.

However, the wings appear to have given her something she had not considered possible:

A part of herself that was not created by the laboratory.

Not repaired by an Architect.

Not stitched together by someone else.

They simply appeared.

For once, Corin's body produced something she did not associate with the laboratory.

She has not yet decided what that means.

# **FINAL NOTE**

Corin spent a long time believing that everything unusual about her body was something done **to** her.

The scars were caused by the laboratory.

The stitches were caused by what happened afterward.

The Ether sustaining her was a consequence of her death.

Her reconstructed voice was the result of Guiding Light's desperate intervention.

Then, one night, while nobody was watching—

**she grew wings.**

No Architect made them.

No scientist implanted them.

No one gave them to her.

They are simply hers.

Flesh.

Bone.

Feathers.

Ether.

And for the first time in a long while, Corin has something about her body that she can look at and say:

> **"...I didn't hate this."**
