# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL SERVICES – PERSONNEL OBSERVATION**

### **SUBJECT: DIALYN – ELDER SIBLING BEHAVIOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical & Executive Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

One of the most frequently documented aspects of Dialyn's personality is her tendency to assume the role of an older sister.

The behavior is entirely voluntary.

It extends across departments and organizations.

Psychological Services believe this is closely connected to the loss of her younger sister during the Hollow Incident.

Importantly, this behavior is **not** considered unhealthy or obsessive.

Rather, it appears to be the way Dialyn naturally expresses care for the people around her.

---

# **OBSERVED BEHAVIOR**

Dialyn rarely refers to herself as an older sister.

Instead, the role becomes apparent through small actions.

Examples include:

- Checking whether coworkers have eaten.
    
- Bringing tea without being asked.
    
- Remembering birthdays and anniversaries.
    
- Quietly listening when someone needs to talk.
    
- Offering help before people ask for it.
    
- Walking people to their destination if they're exhausted.
    
- Making sure everyone gets home safely after late shifts.
    

None of these actions are exclusive to any single person.

She simply does them because she wants to.

---

# **NOTABLE INDIVIDUALS**

## **Norma Lockwood**

Norma is one of the few people who noticed the pattern early.

Although Norma is perfectly capable of caring for herself, Dialyn still occasionally reminds her to take breaks or eat during particularly busy workdays.

Norma usually responds with a laugh.

> "...You're doing it again."

Dialyn always gives the same embarrassed smile.

> "...Sorry."

---

## **Corin Lockwood**

Following Corin's psychological decline and eventual recovery into the Corin/Corey paradox, Dialyn's visits to the treehouse became increasingly frequent.

She never attempted to "fix" Corin.

She simply remained present.

Corin once commented:

> "...She never makes me feel like I'm a burden."

---

## **Corey Lockwood**

Corey quickly adapted to Dialyn's quiet, protective nature.

Unlike many others, Dialyn never treated Corey as "the happier Corin."

She treated her as her own person.

After learning of Corey's traumatic past, Dialyn became noticeably more attentive around sudden loud noises, often warning Corey before they occurred or gently steering conversations away from distressing topics.

She never mentioned why.

Corey noticed anyway.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT**

Counselors believe Dialyn's instinct to care for others is influenced by the life she imagined she might have shared with her younger sister.

She cannot be an older sister to the child she lost.

So when someone around her needs quiet support, that instinct naturally resurfaces.

The behavior is never possessive.

Never controlling.

Never overbearing.

It is simply... who she has become.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Dialyn has never publicly connected her behavior to the loss of her younger sister.

She insists she is "just checking in."

Most people accept that explanation.

Those closest to her know there is a little more to it.

Every cup of tea she brings.

Every quiet visit to Corin's treehouse.

Every gentle reminder to take a break.

Every reassuring smile after a difficult day.

They are all small expressions of the same promise she was never able to keep long ago:

To look after someone younger.

Even if the person she wanted to protect is no longer there.
