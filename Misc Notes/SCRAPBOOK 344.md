# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **Z-335 SKILL DEVELOPMENT REPORT**

# **MART CULINARY PROGRAM**

**Classification:** Allied Entity Observation  
**Primary Instructor:** Dialyn  
**Subject:** Z-335 (Marts)

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Despite lacking limbs and being composed primarily of anomalously stabilized water, Z-335 entities have successfully learned basic cooking.

The instructor responsible for this unexpected development is **Dialyn**.

What initially began as a simple activity to keep curious Marts occupied has since become a surprisingly effective culinary education program.

---

# **THE FIRST LESSON**

The first lesson was intended to be straightforward.

Dialyn demonstrated how to make grilled cheese sandwiches.

The Marts watched attentively.

One Mart asked:

> "Food?"

Dialyn smiled.

> "...Yep."

> "...Let's make lunch."

The lesson lasted nearly two hours.

Most of that time was spent answering repeated questions of:

> "Why?"

---

# **ADAPTIVE COOKING**

Since Marts have no conventional limbs, they developed their own methods.

Using controlled manipulation of their magical containment fields, they are capable of:

- Holding utensils.
    
- Stirring pots.
    
- Opening cabinets.
    
- Carrying bowls.
    
- Measuring ingredients.
    
- Flipping pancakes.
    
- Carefully decorating cakes.
    

Their precision is unexpectedly high.

Researchers believe the shared hivemind allows them to coordinate complex tasks with minimal communication.

---

# **TEAMWORK**

Marts almost never cook alone.

Instead, they naturally divide responsibilities.

One Mart gathers ingredients.

Another measures them.

Several prepare utensils.

Others clean as they go.

The kitchen often appears to function like a perfectly synchronized assembly line.

No Mart gives orders.

Everyone simply knows what to do.

---

# **SPECIALTIES**

Favorite Mart recipes currently include:

- Grilled cheese sandwiches.
    
- Vegetable soup.
    
- Pancakes.
    
- Scrambled eggs.
    
- Rice dishes.
    
- Cookies.
    
- Birthday cakes.
    
- Hot chocolate.
    

Their personal favorite remains...

Cheese crackers.

Despite learning to cook, they continue to consider cheese crackers the pinnacle of cuisine.

---

# **THE SAFEHOUSE KITCHEN**

The SAFEHOUSE kitchen has become a popular gathering place for Marts.

Dialyn occasionally hosts informal cooking lessons there.

Residents have grown accustomed to finding dozens of tiny blue blobs quietly preparing breakfast together.

Cleanup is usually finished before anyone asks.

---

# **HYGIENE**

The Marts maintain exceptional kitchen hygiene.

Before cooking, they clean themselves by temporarily melting and reforming, ensuring dust and debris are removed.

Afterward, they thoroughly clean the work area.

Several researchers have noted that kitchens used by Marts are often cleaner than before the cooking began.

---

# **DIALYN'S COMMENT**

When asked whether teaching Marts to cook had been difficult, Dialyn laughed.

> "...Teaching them wasn't."

She looked toward a group of Marts arguing over pancake decorations.

> "...Convincing them that cheese crackers don't belong in every recipe..."

She sighed.

> "...That took a while."

---

# **LUMI'S RESPONSE**

Lumi entered the SAFEHOUSE kitchen one afternoon expecting to prepare lunch.

Instead, she found nearly fifty Marts already cooking.

One turned toward her.

> "Mom!"

Another held up a wooden spoon.

> "Lunch!"

Lumi looked around the spotless kitchen.

> "...You made all this?"

The entire group proudly answered:

> **"Yes!"**

She smiled.

> "...I'm so proud of you."

---

# **STAR'S COMMENT**

Star has become an enthusiastic taste tester.

She claims the Marts' pancakes are:

> "...Suspiciously perfect."

When asked how they managed such consistency, one Mart replied:

> "...Recipe."

Another added:

> "...Dialyn."

---

# **PERSONNEL NOTICE**

While Marts are fully capable of preparing meals, personnel are reminded:

- Do not allow inexperienced Marts to operate industrial kitchen equipment without supervision.
    
- Do not remove a Mart's top hat while it is cooking.
    
- Do not distract a Mart carrying hot food.
    
- Complimenting a Mart's cooking significantly improves morale.
    

Positive feedback commonly results in excited responses such as:

> "Happy!"

> "Again!"

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

One evening, Dialyn entered the SAFEHOUSE kitchen after finishing work.

The lights were already on.

A group of Marts was quietly preparing dinner.

One noticed her.

> "Teacher!"

The others looked up.

> "Teacher!"

Dialyn blinked.

> "...Teacher?"

The Ambassador nodded.

> "...You taught us."

Dialyn smiled warmly.

> "...Looks like you didn't need me for long."

A Mart carefully placed a freshly made grilled cheese sandwich onto a plate and slid it toward her.

> "For Teacher."

Dialyn took a bite.

It was excellent.

She looked at the tiny blue cooks bustling happily around the kitchen.

> "...Class dismissed."

The kitchen erupted into cheerful cries of:

> **"Happy!"**
