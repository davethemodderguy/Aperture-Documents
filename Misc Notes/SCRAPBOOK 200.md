# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **INCIDENT AFTERMATH REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – POST-BIRTHDAY EVENT RESPONSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the failed birthday gathering at Shelter T-01, Project SAFEHOUSE and associated personnel experienced a significant emotional impact.

The event was intended to remind Corin Lockwood that she was surrounded by people who cared for her.

Instead, it became a reminder of how deeply her past continued to affect her.

Corin returned to isolation.

Everyone else returned carrying the weight of what they witnessed.

---

# **PERSONNEL RESPONSES**

## **Star**

Star was the most visibly affected.

For someone who represented hope and light to many within the Blacksite, seeing Corin reject something as simple as a birthday celebration was devastating.

She spent most of the evening crying.

Repeatedly asking:

> "How can someone hate their own birthday?"

Star struggled to understand how a day meant to celebrate existence could become something painful.

---

## **Nightshade**

Nightshade did not speak much after returning.

She remained beside Star but offered no usual commentary.

The normally protective presence became unusually quiet.

Personnel noted that Nightshade appeared to understand something Star struggled with:

Some wounds cannot be healed by simply showing someone they are loved.

---

## **Neko**

Neko was silent.

Completely silent.

No jokes.

No inventions.

No impossible engineering ideas.

No complaints about restrictions.

He requested a day off from his normal duties.

For once, there was nothing he could build to fix the problem.

---

## **Miss Neko**

Miss Neko remained with Neko throughout his day off.

She did not attempt to force him to talk.

She simply stayed nearby.

For once, she was not stopping an experiment.

She was stopping someone from carrying the weight alone.

---

## **Taiga**

Taiga did not return to her usual calm state.

After returning from Shelter T-01, she laid on the floor in silence.

Then she cried.

The sight was unusual enough that several personnel initially did not know how to react.

Taiga had always been one of the strongest people around Corin.

Seeing her hurt made the situation feel even more real.

---

## **Mart**

Mart sat quietly.

No books.

No conversation.

No distractions.

Just silence.

The owl that usually followed him remained nearby.

Even the owl seemed to understand that something was wrong.

---

## **Endmin & Belle**

Endmin returned to reviewing documents from the Central Execution Department.

Belle continued processing her own work.

Neither was unaffected.

They simply coped differently.

Work continued.

Because sometimes routine was the only thing keeping people standing.

---

## **Keith & Cinder**

Keith and Cinder spent the evening comforting each other.

They were joined by Norma and Arcturus.

None of them had an answer.

None of them could undo what happened.

They could only remain together.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi took the incident the hardest.

She had seen Corin at her weakest.

She had seen the child beneath the trauma.

She remembered the person Corin used to be.

Astral and Bao stayed with her afterward.

Neither attempted to give empty reassurance.

They simply listened.

Through tears, Lumi said:

> "I still remember..."

> "I still remember I held the little soul in my hands."

She looked down.

Her voice became quieter.

> "Those who did this to her will pay for it."

A pause.

Then:

> "I don't care about them."

> "I just want my little girl back."

---

# **SHELTER T-01 STATUS**

Corin returned to the treehouse after the incident.

The doors were locked.

Communication was reduced.

Her work continued.

KCA documents were reviewed.

Reports were completed.

Deadlines were met.

From the outside, Corin appeared functional.

But the treehouse remained quiet.

No birthday decorations.

No celebration.

No visitors.

Only paperwork.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The people around Corin wanted to give her a reason to celebrate.

They wanted her to see what they saw.

A daughter.

A friend.

A family member.

A person worth protecting.

But Corin still saw the person she lost.

The child who was hurt.

The life that was taken.

The memories that refused to disappear.

For now, the doors of Shelter T-01 remain closed.

The work continues.

The snow continues falling.

And beneath the treehouse...

The white tiger and the snowy owl remain where they have always been.

Waiting.

**End of Incident Aftermath Report**
