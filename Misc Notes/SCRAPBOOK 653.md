# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / POST-FAILURE OBSERVATION**

# **WAITING FOR SOMEONE WHO IS ALREADY HERE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **CONSCIOUS / BIOLOGICALLY NONVIABLE / HOPELESS**

---

# **CURRENT OBSERVATION**

Corin sits outside the Treehouse.

Again.

Snow covers the ground around her.

She does not appear to feel the cold anymore.

Her body is cold as ice.

Her hands are motionless.

Her legs are motionless.

Her breathing is barely perceptible.

Her two hearts remain silent.

She simply sits.

Her wings surround her body.

---

# **THE THREE WORDS**

Personnel have begun using three words to describe Corin's current state:

> **Cold.**

> **Hopeless.**

> **Heartless.**

The second is literal.

The third is both literal and metaphorical.

Corin's hearts no longer beat.

And whatever emotional warmth once existed inside her appears almost completely inaccessible.

---

# **SHE STILL WAITS**

The most disturbing part is that Corin continues doing the same thing she did during her disappearance.

She waits.

Under the tree.

Under the snow.

Inside her wings.

Except now...

**Orphie is sitting beside her.**

---

# **THE CONTRADICTION**

Corin is no longer waiting for Orphie to return.

Orphie already returned.

She is physically present.

She sits within arm's reach.

She speaks to Corin.

She touches her wing.

She tells her:

> **"I'm here."**

Corin hears her.

She knows she is there.

And still...

she waits.

---

# **ORPHIE'S QUESTION**

Orphie eventually asked:

> **"Who are you waiting for?"**

Corin stared into the snow.

No answer.

Orphie tried again.

> **"I'm here."**

Corin quietly replied:

> **"I know."**

Orphie froze.

> **"Then what are you waiting for?"**

A long silence.

Corin finally answered:

> **"...I don't know."**

---

# **THE WAITING**

There is no destination.

No person approaching.

No scheduled arrival.

No message expected.

No reason to remain outside.

Yet Corin continues waiting.

It appears to have become the final behavior she retained from the year she spent alone.

Waiting is no longer something she does.

It is something she **is**.

---

# **THE WINGS**

Her wings remain wrapped around herself.

The feathers are completely dormant.

They produce no light.

No fire.

No force.

No protection beyond their physical mass.

Yet Corin refuses to move them.

Orphie once attempted to gently pull one wing aside so she could see Corin's face.

Corin immediately pulled it back.

Not aggressively.

Not fearfully.

Just instinct.

The wings closed again.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie remains beside her.

She has stopped trying to convince Corin to come inside.

She has stopped promising things.

She has stopped arguing.

She simply sits.

Sometimes for hours.

Sometimes through the entire night.

If Corin waits...

Orphie waits with her.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

Behind them is everything Corin once needed.

Warmth.

Food.

Water.

A bed.

Blankets.

People.

Home.

The door remains open.

Corin does not enter.

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

Corin's behavior suggests that her original concept of "home" may have become permanently associated with waiting rather than safety.

The Treehouse is physically safe.

But Corin's mind remains in the place where she spent eight months expecting someone who never arrived.

The environment changed.

Her memories did not.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin is cold as ice.

She has no heartbeat.

No hope.

No apparent desire to recover.

She sits beneath the tree while snow gathers around her.

Her wings cover her.

She waits.

Orphie sits beside her.

She waits too.

There is no one else coming.

There is nothing left to arrive.

And yet Corin keeps looking into the distance as though someone is supposed to appear over the horizon.

Orphie whispers:

> **"I'm here."**

Corin answers:

> **"I know."**

And she keeps waiting.

For someone who is already here.
