# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### POST-ENGAGEMENT INCIDENT REPORT

## SPAWN COMBATANT — FIRST SUCCESSFUL BODY RECOVERY

**Classification:** C.O.R.E. Internal / Restricted  
**Status:** ACTIVE INVESTIGATION  
**Incident Date:** 01/09/2026  
**Location:** Heaven's Blacksite — Northern Perimeter  
**Primary Subject:** Alice  
**Hostile:** SPAWN Combatant  
**Recovery Status:** BODY SECURED

---

# I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

At approximately ██:██ local time on 01/09/2026, an authorized perimeter patrol consisting of Alice and assigned Perimeter Security personnel encountered a single SPAWN combatant near the northern perimeter.

The encounter lasted approximately several seconds.

The SPAWN combatant was engaged by Alice.

The combatant was **sliced completely in half by ALICE-WPN-01.**

Unlike previous SPAWN casualties, the combatant **did not disappear when approached.**

C.O.R.E. containment personnel were immediately dispatched.

The remains were successfully secured and transported to a controlled C.O.R.E. facility for examination.

This represents the **first confirmed physical recovery of a SPAWN combatant.**

The incident has also produced an unexpected behavioral change in Alice.

Following the engagement, Alice became noticeably more serious.

She remained quiet throughout the remainder of the patrol.

She did not appear frightened.

She did not appear distressed.

She simply stopped behaving like the usual Alice.

---

# II. ENCOUNTER

Perimeter surveillance detected movement approximately ██ meters beyond the northern perimeter.

The individual was subsequently identified as a SPAWN combatant based on:

- Distinctive mask.
    
- SPAWN-associated equipment.
    
- Known hostile behavior.
    
- Presence within the restricted perimeter vicinity.
    

Security personnel initiated standard identification procedures.

The combatant did not comply.

Alice reportedly looked toward the individual.

She did not immediately draw the weapon.

One security officer asked:

> "Alice?"

Alice responded:

> "...I know."

The pencil was removed from her coat.

ALICE-WPN-01 transitioned into its true configuration.

The engagement lasted only a few seconds.

One strike was delivered.

The combatant was cut through the torso and separated into two sections.

No additional strikes were required.

---

# III. UNEXPECTED REMAINS

Security personnel initially expected the combatant's remains to disappear.

Previous engagements had established a consistent pattern in which confirmed SPAWN casualties vanished when personnel approached them.

This time, that did not happen.

The remains remained physically present.

Security immediately established a perimeter around the location.

C.O.R.E. containment personnel were contacted.

No personnel were permitted to touch the remains without appropriate protective equipment.

Both sections of the combatant were successfully recovered.

The mask remained attached to the upper portion of the body.

The combatant's equipment was also recovered.

Unlike several previous encounters, **the hostile did not disappear.**

The reason remains unknown.

---

# IV. ALICE'S RESPONSE

Alice did not celebrate the engagement.

She did not appear excited by the successful strike.

She did not ask whether she had done a good job.

She did not make her usual quiet comments regarding whether she had caused trouble.

Instead, she stood silently beside the security personnel.

When asked whether she was injured, she answered:

> "...No."

When asked whether she was frightened:

> "...No."

When asked whether she wanted to return to the SAFEHOUSE:

> "...Not yet."

Her attention remained fixed on the recovered remains.

After several seconds, she quietly said:

> "...They don't usually stay."

The observing officer asked:

> "What?"

Alice looked toward the body.

> "...The bodies."

She paused.

> "...They always disappear."

This statement is significant.

Alice appears to possess knowledge of the phenomenon beyond what C.O.R.E. has previously established.

---

# V. BEHAVIORAL CHANGE

Since the encounter, personnel familiar with Alice have reported a noticeable change in her demeanor.

She remains polite.

She remains gentle.

She continues to interact with personnel normally.

However, she has become considerably more serious when SPAWN is mentioned.

Her usual childlike expressions and mannerisms largely disappear during these conversations.

She does not joke.

She does not become visibly frightened.

She simply listens.

When asked what she thought about the recovered combatant, Alice answered:

> "...I don't know."

A pause.

> "...Something is wrong."

When asked to clarify, Alice did not elaborate.

---

# VI. CORIN — BIG SIS

Corin was informed of the engagement shortly after Alice returned.

Her first response was not regarding the combatant.

It was:

> "Is Alice okay?"

After being informed that Alice was physically unharmed, Corin requested to speak with her.

Alice remained unusually quiet.

Corin eventually asked:

> "You okay, Alice?"

> "...Mhm."

> "You sure?"

A pause.

> "...Yeah."

Corin reportedly noticed the change immediately.

Alice was not behaving as though she had simply returned from another patrol.

Something about the encounter had affected her.

Corin did not press her for an explanation.

She simply stayed beside her.

---

# VII. ORPHIE — BIGGER SIS

Orphie similarly noticed the behavioral change.

Alice was reportedly sitting quietly with the pencil still clipped to her coat.

Orphie sat beside her.

Neither spoke for several minutes.

Eventually:

> **Orphie:** "You're thinking about it."

Alice nodded.

> **Alice:** "...They didn't disappear."

> **Orphie:** "No."

> **Alice:** "...Why?"

Orphie did not have an answer.

Alice looked down.

> "...I don't like it."

Orphie reportedly placed a hand on Alice's head.

Alice remained quiet.

She did not pull away.

---

# VIII. C.O.R.E. RECOVERY

The recovered combatant has been transferred to a secured C.O.R.E. examination facility.

Current recovery inventory includes:

- One SPAWN combatant body, separated into two sections.
    
- One SPAWN mask.
    
- One set of SPAWN combat equipment.
    
- One primary weapon.
    
- Additional unidentified equipment.
    
- Biological and anomalous residue samples.
    

The body has **not** exhibited spontaneous disappearance since recovery.

Continuous observation has been established.

C.O.R.E. personnel have been instructed not to assume that the phenomenon will remain absent indefinitely.

The possibility of delayed disappearance remains open.

---

# IX. INVESTIGATIVE SIGNIFICANCE

The recovery potentially answers one question while creating several more.

Previously, C.O.R.E. had no physical remains from which to determine:

- Whether SPAWN combatants are biologically human.
    
- Whether all combatants originate from the same population.
    
- Whether the masks are purely protective or anomalous.
    
- Whether the bodies disappear because of an external mechanism.
    
- Whether disappearance occurs at the moment of death or shortly afterward.
    
- Whether the "second chance at life" doctrine has any relationship to the phenomenon.
    

This specimen may provide the first opportunity to investigate these questions directly.

Of particular interest is the possibility that the disappearance phenomenon is **conditional.**

Why this combatant remained when more than fifty previous casualties did not remains unknown.

The engagement provides another demonstration of ALICE-WPN-01's effectiveness.

The weapon produced a complete separation of the target with a single strike.

No visible damage to the weapon was observed.

Alice demonstrated complete control over the weapon.

No excessive force was observed after the initial strike.

The combatant was already incapacitated following the first blow.

Alice did not continue attacking.

This behavior is consistent with previous assessments that Alice does not display indiscriminate aggression.

Something about this encounter was different.

The combatant died.

The body remained.

Alice noticed.

And whatever Alice recognized in that moment has apparently made her take SPAWN more seriously than before.

There is no evidence that Alice has become hostile toward non-SPAWN personnel.

There is no evidence that her personality has fundamentally changed.

She is still a child.

She still draws.

She still keeps her little pencil clipped to her coat.

She still lets Corin and Orphie look after her.

She listens.

She thinks.

And she watches.

The recovered body may finally give us a chance to find out.
