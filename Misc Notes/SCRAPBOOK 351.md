# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **Z-335 RESEARCH UPDATE**

# **THERMAL ADAPTATION & NEW RESIDENTIAL HABITATS**

**Classification:** Allied Entity Research  
**Subject:** Z-335 (Marts)

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Continued observation of Z-335 has resulted in two significant discoveries.

First, Marts are capable of **voluntarily regulating their internal temperature** over a considerable range.

Second, a small number of Marts have established a permanent residence with **Corin** and **Corey** in their treehouse.

Neither development required instruction.

Both appear to have occurred naturally.

---

# **THERMAL ADAPTATION**

Earlier research concluded that every Mart possessed an internal temperature comparable to liquid nitrogen.

While this remains their natural resting state, that conclusion is now considered incomplete.

Researchers have confirmed that Marts can consciously regulate their own temperature.

Observed uses include:

- Cooling people with fevers.
    
- Warming themselves into Blanket Form.
    
- Holding warm dishes without rapidly cooling them.
    
- Preparing hot meals.
    
- Remaining comfortable during outdoor activities.
    
- Adjusting to the preference of whoever they are helping.
    

The transition is gradual and entirely voluntary.

No discomfort has been observed during the process.

---

# **RESEARCH FINDINGS**

Despite the temperature change, a Mart's body composition remains unchanged.

The water composing its body does not boil or freeze conventionally.

The adjustment appears to affect only the anomalous thermal field surrounding the Mart.

Medical Division concludes that the process is Ether-based rather than physical.

---

# **COMMON PHRASE**

When asked whether they are warm or cold, many Marts now respond:

> "...Whatever help."

Researchers believe this summarizes the species' approach surprisingly well.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

Corin and Corey's treehouse has gradually become another recognized Mart residence.

Initially, Marts merely visited.

Over time, several simply...

Stayed.

The treehouse now contains:

- Small sleeping nests.
    
- Tiny shelves built specifically for Marts.
    
- Cheese cracker storage.
    
- Miniature blankets.
    
- Several improvised "Mart-sized" reading corners.
    

Corin and Corey insist none of these additions were their idea.

---

# **DAILY LIFE**

The resident Marts have become part of the household.

Typical activities include:

- Helping prepare breakfast.
    
- Keeping the treehouse tidy.
    
- Reading picture books with Corey.
    
- Watching sunsets from the balcony.
    
- Sleeping in hammocks.
    
- Acting as blankets during cool evenings.
    
- Greeting birds that land nearby.
    

At night, it is common to find several sleeping peacefully throughout the treehouse.

---

# **COREY'S COMMENT**

Corey was asked whether she minded having permanent Mart roommates.

She answered immediately.

> "...No."

She looked around the cozy treehouse.

Several Marts were quietly reading while another slept in a flowerpot.

> "...It feels more like home."

---

# **CORIN'S COMMENT**

Corin admitted there had been one adjustment.

> "...Sometimes I wake up..."

She pointed upward.

> "...And there's a sleeping Mart hanging from the ceiling beam."

She smiled.

> "...I've stopped questioning it."

---

# **LUMI'S RESPONSE**

When informed that some of her children had effectively moved out, Lumi laughed.

> "...I suppose they're old enough."

The researcher asked whether she minded.

She shook her head.

> "...Home is wherever they're loved."

---

# **STAR'S COMMENT**

Star visited the treehouse shortly after the Marts settled in.

She looked around.

There were tiny hats on shelves.

Little nests in the corners.

Cheese crackers in labeled jars.

She laughed.

> "...They've actually decorated."

One nearby Mart proudly replied:

> "...Home."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

At sunset, Corin and Corey were sitting together on the treehouse balcony.

The evening had turned slightly cool.

Without a word, two Marts warmed themselves and settled across their laps in Blanket Form.

Another adjusted its temperature just enough to keep a mug of hot chocolate pleasantly warm.

A fourth simply watched the forest below.

After several peaceful minutes, Corey quietly smiled.

> "...Good night."

Several tiny voices answered almost together.

> **"...Good night."**

For a species that can drift anywhere...

Some Marts had decided they liked having another place to call home.

**End of Research Update**
