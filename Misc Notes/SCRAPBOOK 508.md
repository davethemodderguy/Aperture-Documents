# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **MEDICAL / ANOMALOUS CONDITION FOLLOW-UP LOG**

# **ANOTHER SILENT DAY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Condition:** **Persistent Aphonia / Ether Secretion**  
**Voice Status:** **Absent**  
**Communication:** Written / Gestural  
**Status:** **Ongoing**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Another day has passed.

Corin still cannot speak.

There has been no improvement in her voice.

No deterioration has been detected either.

The condition remains exactly as unexplained as before.

She continues communicating through her notebook, gestures, and facial expressions.

For the most part, the treehouse has adapted.

The silence has become routine.

That does not mean Corin has become comfortable with it.

---

# **ETHER COUGHING**

Corin has developed another recurring symptom.

Occasionally, she coughs.

The cough itself is ordinary.

What follows is not.

A small amount of **fluid Ether** may be expelled.

The substance is luminous and semi-translucent, with a consistency that varies from thin liquid to something slightly viscous.

It does not appear to cause Corin significant pain.

She usually looks more embarrassed than distressed.

---

# **DISINTEGRATION**

The expelled Ether does not remain stable for long.

After being separated from Corin's body, it gradually loses cohesion.

It begins breaking apart.

Then it disintegrates completely.

No residue remains.

No biological contamination has been detected.

No environmental effects have been observed.

The process appears to be a natural consequence of Ether existing outside Corin's sustaining system.

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

The first time it happened, Corin immediately reached for her notebook.

She wrote:

> **Sorry.**

Orphie shook her head.

> **"You don't need to apologize."**

Corin wrote:

> **"It's gross."**

Orphie replied:

> **"I've stitched your arm back onto you."**

Corin stared at her.

Orphie shrugged.

> **"This isn't even close to the weirdest thing I've dealt with."**

Corin smiled faintly.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light continues monitoring the condition.

She has confirmed that the expelled Ether does not appear to represent a significant loss of Corin's sustaining energy.

Nevertheless, she remains cautious.

The fluid may be a symptom of whatever caused Corin's aphonia.

It may also be unrelated.

At present, there is insufficient information to determine which.

Guiding Light has therefore made one rule clear:

> **"Don't force anything."**

Until the underlying mechanism is understood, no attempt will be made to forcibly restore Corin's voice.

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT**

Curious Light has collected observations regarding the Ether.

He has resisted the urge to conduct invasive testing.

His notes remain strictly observational.

He recorded:

> **"The material behaves more like expelled structural energy than conventional biological fluid."**

Then, underneath:

> **"Corin does not like being observed while coughing."**

He stopped there.

---

# **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT**

Mischevious Light happened to visit during one episode.

Corin coughed.

A small amount of Ether spilled onto the floor.

Mischevious Light looked at it.

Then at Corin.

Corin immediately wrote:

> **Don't.**

He raised both hands.

> **"I wasn't going to say anything."**

Corin narrowed her eyes.

He smiled.

> **"...Much."**

Corin threw a pillow at him.

He caught it.

> **"Okay. Fair."**

---

# **THE BLUE JAY**

Jay is considerably less concerned.

When Corin coughs Ether, he usually tilts his head.

Once, he attempted to inspect the dissipating fluid.

Corin gently moved him away.

She wrote:

> **Don't eat that.**

Jay chirped.

> **"No."**

Another chirp.

> **"Still no."**

Jay flew away.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

Corin's written entries have become increasingly matter-of-fact.

One entry:

> **Day 12. Still can't talk.**

Another:

> **Coughed Ether again.**

Then:

> **It disappeared after a while.**

She paused before adding:

> **I hate this body.**

The next line was written much more carefully:

> **But everyone keeps telling me it's still mine.**

---

# **CURRENT CONDITION**

Corin remains physically stable.

Current observations:

- Voice remains completely absent.
    
- Breathing remains functional.
    
- Swallowing remains functional.
    
- No conventional throat injury identified.
    
- Ether remains stable within the body.
    
- Occasional fluid Ether is expelled during coughing.
    
- Expelled Ether subsequently disintegrates.
    
- No significant Ether depletion detected.
    
- Psychological distress remains present.
    

No immediate life-threatening complication has been identified.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The treehouse has become accustomed to Corin's silence.

Someone asks a question.

Corin writes an answer.

Someone makes a joke.

Corin rolls her eyes.

Jay steals something.

Corin retrieves it.

She coughs.

Ether briefly spills onto the floor.

Everyone waits.

It disappears.

And the conversation continues.

The strangest part is how quickly an impossible condition can become ordinary when the people around someone simply decide to adapt.

Corin still hates that she cannot speak.

She still hates what her body has become.

But nobody is treating her silence as a reason to stop talking to her.

Until her voice returns—or until they learn that it will not—they will simply keep listening in whatever way they can.

**End of Medical / Anomalous Condition Follow-Up Log**
