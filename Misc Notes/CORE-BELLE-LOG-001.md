### **RESIDENTIAL GUIDELINES – LIVING WITH BELLE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Personnel Wellness

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following Belle's successful integration into Room N-07, Residential Services has published several informal guidelines based on observations from her roommates.

While not official regulations, staff have found that following these recommendations helps Belle feel comfortable and welcomed within the Blacksite community.

As Taiga jokingly summarized:

> **"The rules are actually pretty easy."**

---

# **Room N-07 Rules for Belle**

### **1. Don't tease her height.**

At **145 cm (4 ft 9 in)**, Belle is the shortest resident among the Friendly Roommates.

Although she usually laughs off harmless jokes, repeated teasing about her height genuinely hurts her feelings.

If someone places an item on a high shelf just to watch her struggle...

Expect Endmin to step in.

---

### **2. Don't tease her for liking vegetables.**

Belle insists she dislikes vegetables.

Evidence consistently suggests otherwise.

Everyone is aware she secretly enjoys them.

Out of respect for both Belle and Endmin—who shares the exact same habit—roommates generally pretend not to notice.

Current policy:

> **Just let them keep the act going.**

---

### **3. Don't be a meanie.**

Belle is naturally trusting.

Staff are expected to treat her with the same kindness shown to every resident.

Intentionally upsetting or bullying Belle is considered unacceptable residential behavior.

As Niko once put it:

> **"She's basically sunshine with rabbit ears."**

---

### **4. Do hug her.**

Belle absolutely loves hugs.

She is known to initiate them without warning, especially after missions or when greeting friends.

Behavioral Sciences has observed that physical affection is one of Belle's primary ways of expressing trust and appreciation.

Residents who are comfortable with hugs are encouraged to return them.

---

## **Room N-07 Sleeping Arrangement**

Belle shares Endmin's bed.

The arrangement began on Belle's first night at the Blacksite and has continued by mutual choice.

According to Endmin:

> **"...She sleeps better if someone is nearby."

Belle immediately corrected her.

> **"...I sleep better if _you're_ nearby."

Endmin had no response.

Her ears simply folded back in embarrassment.

---

## **Medical Observation**

Medical staff describe hugging Belle as remarkably comforting.

Her thick pink fur, soft rabbit ears, and small rabbit nose have often been compared to a plush toy.

One physician jokingly wrote:

> **"Like hugging a sentient bunny plushie."**

The comment was later underlined by three additional staff members.

Belle proudly framed the printout.

### **Closing Observation**

Late one winter evening, Endmin quietly returned to Room N-07 after finishing maintenance in Engineering.

Belle was already asleep.

Curled beneath the blanket beside her.

The moment Endmin climbed into bed, Belle instinctively scooted closer without waking.

One fluffy rabbit ear flopped over Endmin's shoulder.

Endmin smiled.

Very gently, she wrapped an arm around Belle.

> **"...Goodnight."

Half-asleep, Belle mumbled only one thing.

> **"...'Night..."

Within moments, both were asleep.

Taiga, walking past the open doorway, peeked inside.

She quietly smiled before pulling the door closed.

> **"...Looks like the bunny found her home."**
