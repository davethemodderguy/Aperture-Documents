# 4. NERI'S CHALLENGE

The challenge consists of a series of chemistry problems personally selected by Neri.

The problems are not intended to test whether a contestant has memorized a textbook.

They require actual chemical reasoning.

Contestants have been required to demonstrate understanding of matters including:

- molecular properties;
    
- chemical symbols;
    
- atomic characteristics;
    
- classification;
    
- reaction behavior;
    
- molecular structure;
    
- material interactions;
    
- quantitative reasoning;
    
- and theoretical chemical relationships.
    

The difficulty increases substantially as the challenge progresses.

Neri is not interested in whether someone can recite information.

She wants to know whether they **understand it.**

---

# 5. WHY NERI?

Neri is currently considered the strongest chemist operating within Aperture.

This distinction is unusual.

Neko remains vastly superior in engineering, computing, systems architecture, anomalous technology, and related disciplines.

Chemistry is different.

There is effectively no comparison.

Neri's research output has repeatedly exceeded what Blacksite chemistry personnel expected to be possible from a ten-year-old resident.

The challenge therefore comes directly from her.
