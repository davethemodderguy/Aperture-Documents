## **SUMMARY**

Following a routine review by the Krampus Compliance Authority and the Central Execution Department, the identity protection status of two long-serving personnel has been updated.

After years of distinguished service, their legal names have been formally declassified for internal use.

Their callsigns remain the preferred form of address during official operations.

## **REASON FOR DECLASSIFICATION**

The Central Execution Department concluded that continued classification of their legal names no longer provided a meaningful operational benefit.

Both individuals have accumulated sufficient years of public government service that internal disclosure poses no significant security concern.

No changes have been made to external information release policies.
