## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **BEHAVIORAL / RESIDENTIAL STATUS UPDATE**

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **RESIDENTIAL WITHDRAWAL**  
**Outdoor Activity:** **NONE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following her most recent recovery, Corin has stopped leaving the treehouse.

Not temporarily.

Not because of weather.

Not because she is physically incapable.

She simply refuses to go outside.

The forest beyond the treehouse has effectively ceased to exist for her.

---

# **THE DOOR**

Corin no longer approaches the front door unless absolutely necessary.

She does not look outside.

She does not sit on the porch.

She does not accompany Orphie on supply trips.

She does not visit the Blacksite.

She does not attend gatherings.

She does not go for walks.

When asked whether she wants to leave, her response is usually:

> **"No."**

If asked why:

> **"There's nothing out there for me."**

---

# **HER REASONING**

Corin's reasoning is consistent with her increasingly detached self-image.

She believes that going outside would only expose her to people who might see what she has become.

The forest reminds her of where she was found.

The Blacksite reminds her of the laboratory.

The outside world reminds her that she is different.

The treehouse, meanwhile, is familiar.

It contains Orphie.

It contains her belongings.

It contains the people who still come to see her.

So she stays.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie initially attempted to encourage Corin to go outside.

She stopped after realizing that pressure only made Corin withdraw further.

Now she simply asks:

> **"Want to sit by the window?"**

Sometimes Corin agrees.

Sometimes she closes the curtains.

Orphie no longer forces the issue.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light has remained concerned.

She understands that staying inside is not inherently dangerous.

The problem is what the isolation represents.

Corin is not simply enjoying the safety of her home.

She is slowly convincing herself that she does not belong anywhere else.

Guiding Light wrote:

> **"A safe place becomes a prison when you believe you're not allowed to leave it."**

---

# **CORIN'S DAILY ROUTINE**

Corin's activities have become increasingly confined to the treehouse.

Typical activities include:

- Sitting beside Orphie.
    
- Writing in her notebook.
    
- Combing her hair.
    
- Caring for Jay.
    
- Reading.
    
- Looking through old belongings.
    
- Sitting silently by the window.
    
- Sleeping with her eyes open.
    

She rarely asks for anything.

She rarely complains.

She rarely initiates conversation.

The absence of complaints is considered concerning rather than reassuring.

---

# **THE WINDOW**

Corin occasionally sits near the window.

She can see the forest.

She simply does not enter it.

During one evening, Orphie asked:

> **"Do you want to go outside?"**

Corin looked through the glass.

> **"No."**

> **"Why?"**

A long pause.

> **"I'm safer here."**

Orphie nodded.

Then Corin added:

> **"...And everyone else is safer if I'm here."**

Orphie immediately understood that this was not actually about safety.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

Corin is physically stable.

Her Ether remains under control.

Her limbs remain properly attached.

Her voice remains functional.

She is capable of leaving the treehouse.

She simply refuses to.

The behavior is therefore considered **psychological withdrawal rather than physical incapacity.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin used to leave the treehouse for sandwiches.

She used to visit the Blacksite.

She used to sit with everyone at lunch.

She used to complain about spicy food.

She used to walk through the forest.

Now she watches the world through a window.

The door remains unlocked.

Nobody forces her through it.

The forest remains outside.

And Corin remains inside.

For now, the treehouse is not where she is trapped.

It is where she has decided she belongs.

**End of Behavioral / Residential Status Update**
