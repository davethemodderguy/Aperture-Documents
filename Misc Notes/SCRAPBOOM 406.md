### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / TREEHOUSE INCIDENT LOG**

# **ORPHIE — MISCOMMUNICATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Concern  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Marts / Luna / Solaria  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **ESCALATING CONCERN**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the incident involving Corin missing one day of work, Corin attempted to have a serious conversation with Orphie.

Her intention was to reassure her.

Instead, she chose the wrong words.

The conversation significantly worsened Orphie's emotional state.

---

# **THE CONVERSATION**

Corin approached Orphie in the evening.

There was no argument at first.

Orphie was sitting quietly near the window.

Corin sat across from her.

> **Corin:** "...Can we talk?"

> **Orphie:** "...About what?"

> **Corin:** "...About you."

Orphie immediately became uncomfortable.

> "...Why?"

Corin hesitated.

> "...Because you're not doing well."

Orphie looked away.

> "...I'm fine."

> "...You're not."

Silence.

---

# **THE WRONG WORDS**

Corin attempted to explain how worried everyone was.

> "...Everyone is worried about you."

Orphie:

> "...They shouldn't be."

Corin:

> "...They can't just ignore this."

Orphie:

> "...Then make them."

Corin:

> "...That's not what I meant."

Orphie remained silent.

Corin continued:

> "...You need people to take care of you right now."

The room became completely silent.

Orphie's expression changed.

---

# **THE INTERPRETATION**

Corin meant:

> **"You deserve support."**

Orphie heard:

> **"You are incapable of taking care of yourself."**

She looked toward Corin.

> "...So I'm helpless now?"

Corin immediately shook her head.

> "...No, that's not what I—"

> "...Then why do I need everyone to take care of me?"

Corin struggled to answer.

---

# **THE BURDEN**

Corin eventually said:

> "...Because we don't want to lose you."

Orphie stared at her.

> "...Lose me?"

> "...Yes."

> "...You make it sound like I'm something you have to keep."

Corin:

> "...No."

Orphie:

> "...Like I'm some problem you have to manage."

Corin realized too late that every attempt to explain was making things worse.

---

# **ORPHIE'S ANGER**

Orphie stood up.

> "...I'm tired of everyone worrying about me."

Corin:

> "...We're worried because we love you."

Orphie:

> "...Then stop."

Corin froze.

> "...What?"

> "...Stop worrying."

> "...I can't."

> "...Then you're choosing this."

---

# **THE MARTS**

Several Marts were nearby.

They became visibly distressed.

One approached Orphie.

> "...Orphie..."

Orphie gently pushed the Mart away.

Not violently.

Just enough to create distance.

> "...Please."

The Mart retreated.

---

# **CORIN'S REALIZATION**

Corin finally understood the problem.

She had been trying to convince Orphie that she mattered.

But she kept using language that framed Orphie as someone who needed to be **fixed**, **managed**, or **protected**.

Every sentence accidentally reinforced the belief that Orphie was a burden.

Corin stopped talking.

---

# **THE SILENCE**

For several minutes, neither spoke.

Eventually, Corin said:

> "...I'm sorry."

Orphie:

> "...For what?"

Corin:

> "...For saying it wrong."

Orphie:

> "...You didn't say it wrong."

Pause.

> "...You said what you meant."

That answer hurt Corin more than an argument would have.

---

# **CORIN LEAVES**

Corin eventually left the room.

She did not go far.

She remained in the treehouse.

She simply gave Orphie the space she had asked for.

The Marts followed Corin.

One looked back at Orphie.

It clearly wanted to stay.

But it followed Corin anyway.

---

# **LUNA'S OBSERVATION**

Luna was later informed of the conversation.

> "...She used the words that everyone uses when someone is sick."

> "...But Orphie doesn't hear them that way."

She paused.

> "...Every reminder that she's being cared for sounds like a reminder that she's difficult to care for."

---

# **SOLARIA'S OBSERVATION**

Solaria quietly added:

> "...Orphie doesn't want to be fixed."

> "...She wants to stop being a problem."

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Orphie:**  
Further withdrawn following the conversation. Burden-related beliefs reinforced.

**Corin:**  
Emotionally distressed. Recognizes that her wording worsened the situation.

**Marts:**  
Concerned. Temporarily giving Orphie additional space.

**SAFEHOUSE:**  
Informed of escalation.

**Luna:**  
Concerned.

**Solaria:**  
Concerned.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin wanted to say:

> **"You don't have to do this alone."**

Instead, she said things that Orphie interpreted as:

> **"You can't do this yourself."**

Corin wanted to say:

> **"We care about you."**

Orphie heard:

> **"We have to worry about you."**

Corin wanted to say:

> **"You matter."**

But the conversation became another reminder that everyone was changing their lives because of her.

Corin was not cruel.

She was not careless.

She was simply trying to reach someone who no longer interpreted words the way she once did.

And now the distance between them has grown a little wider.
