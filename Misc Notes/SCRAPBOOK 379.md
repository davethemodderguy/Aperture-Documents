# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION LOG**

# **Z-335 — MART NEST EXPANSION**

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE Pocket-Dimensional Closet  
**Area:** 10th Floor  
**Classification:** Internal Residential Observation

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following continued Mart residence within Project SAFEHOUSE, C.O.R.E. has documented the existence of a dedicated **Mart Nest** located inside the pocket-dimensional closet.

Although the closet is officially classified as:

> **Anomalous Storage Compartment**

Its internal structure has expanded far beyond standard storage requirements.

The current confirmed structure contains multiple internal floors.

The **10th floor** has become the preferred gathering area for Marts.

---

# **THE 10TH FLOOR**

The 10th floor is unofficially designated:

> **Mart Nest**

The area is not an assigned workspace.

It is not a containment area.

It is simply where Marts choose to spend time.

---

# **ENVIRONMENT**

The Mart Nest contains:

- Soft resting areas.
    
- Water sources.
    
- Blankets.
    
- Small decorations.
    
- Comfortable platforms.
    
- Floating spaces.
    
- Areas for sleeping.
    
- Areas for gathering.
    

The environment appears to have naturally adapted to Mart preferences.

No personnel have intentionally designed the full layout.

---

# **MART BEHAVIOR**

Inside the Mart Nest, Marts display their most relaxed behavior.

Common observations include:

- Large groups sleeping together.
    
- Marts drifting through the rooms.
    
- Marts stacking themselves into towers.
    
- Marts playing together.
    
- Marts practicing movement.
    
- Marts simply existing peacefully.
    

The atmosphere has been described as:

> "A room full of tiny blue creatures taking a break."

---

# **NO KNOWN LEADER**

Despite the large number of Marts present, no hierarchy has been observed.

The Elder Marts, when present, are treated the same as every other Mart.

A normal Mart may sleep beside an Elder Mart without recognizing any difference.

---

# **ELDER MART OBSERVATION**

The 10th floor is one of the locations where Elder Marts are most difficult to identify.

Due to:

- Similar appearance.
    
- Identical top hats.
    
- Similar movement patterns.
    
- Lack of special behavior.
    

An Elder Mart can remain hidden among hundreds of other Marts.

The only confirmed differences remain:

- Slightly larger size.
    
- More opaque body.
    
- Ability to speak fluent English.
    

---

# **COREY'S VISITS**

Corey frequently visits the Mart Nest.

Both normal Corey and her manifested forms have been observed there.

### **Corey**

Usually arrives wearing a hat.

Marts gather around her normally.

### **Solaria**

Marts are attracted to her warmth and solar energy.

### **Luna**

Marts gather significantly faster due to her lunar gravitational field.

During Luna manifestations, the Mart Nest often becomes almost completely occupied.

---

# **LUNA EVENT**

During one nighttime observation, Corey fell asleep inside the Mart Nest.

Without her hat, she naturally became Luna.

Within several minutes:

- Marts gathered around her.
    
- The room became softly illuminated.
    
- Luna began floating.
    
- Several Marts floated alongside her.
    

A security report described the scene:

> "A blue moon surrounded by smaller blue moons."

---

# **NEKO'S OBSERVATION**

Neko inspected the dimensional closet after noticing the 10th floor's expansion.

His conclusion:

> "...The closet is bigger again."

When asked if this was concerning:

> "...Probably."

Pause.

> "...But the Marts like it."

---

# **LUMI'S REACTION**

Upon seeing the Mart Nest, Lumi was silent for several minutes.

She observed hundreds of Marts resting peacefully.

When asked what she thought, she answered:

> "...They made a home."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Mart Nest remains an approved area within Project SAFEHOUSE.

Personnel are allowed access under normal SAFEHOUSE rules.

Restrictions:

- Do not disturb sleeping Marts.
    
- Do not damage the environment.
    
- Do not remove Mart top hats.
    
- Do not attempt to reorganize the Nest without approval.
    

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The 10th floor of the SAFEHOUSE pocket-dimensional closet was never planned.

It was never designed.

It simply became what the Marts wanted it to be.

A place to rest.

A place to gather.

A place where hundreds of small blue blobs with antlers and tiny top hats can exist peacefully.

In official documents, it remains:

> **Anomalous Storage Compartment — Floor 10**

To the Marts:

> **Home.**

**End of Project SAFEHOUSE Observation Log**
