# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / PERSONAL RESEARCH ARCHIVE**

# **THE LAST LINE OF HOPE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Document Type:** Personal Research / Psychological Review  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Reader:** Orphie

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie has developed a habit.

Before going to bed, she reads Corin's old research notes.

Not the sketchbook.

Not the darker entries.

Not the pages filled with Eyes.

The research.

Pages Corin wrote while trying to understand herself.

Her wings.

Her body.

Her two hearts.

Her feathers.

Hundreds of pages documenting how Corin worked, how she healed, how her anomalous biology behaved, and what she believed allowed her to keep going.

Orphie reads them while looking through the window.

Outside, Corin is usually still there.

Under the tree.

Wrapped in her wings.

Silent.

---

# **CORIN'S RESEARCH**

Corin's notes are surprisingly thorough.

She documented herself almost scientifically.

---

## **01 — WINGS**

Corin recorded:

- Feather growth.
    
- Wing structure.
    
- Flight mechanics.
    
- Regeneration.
    
- Feather shedding.
    
- Energy distribution.
    
- Environmental responses.
    
- Anomalous feather functions.
    
- Emotional responses associated with her wings.
    

One repeated conclusion appears throughout the notes:

> **"My wings are part of how I express myself."**

Later, she added:

> **"If I can't explain how I feel, my wings usually do it first."**

---

# **02 — BODY**

Corin documented her body extensively.

Temperature regulation.

Regeneration.

Pain response.

Strength.

Sensory changes.

Cat-like characteristics.

Her resistance to environmental hazards.

She repeatedly attempted to understand why her emotional state could affect her physical condition.

One section is titled:

> **"Why does being sad make my body stop working?"**

There is no conclusion.

---

# **03 — TWO HEARTS**

Corin's two hearts received their own research section.

She tracked:

- Resting rhythms.
    
- Synchronization.
    
- Responses to stress.
    
- Responses to anomalous energy.
    
- Recovery after injury.
    
- Emotional influence.
    

Her notes indicate that the two hearts were normally synchronized.

She described them as:

> **"Two little engines keeping me going."**

A later annotation reads:

> **"I think they listen to me."**

---

# **04 — FEATHERS**

This is by far the largest section.

Corin documented nearly every known property.

Before their dormancy, her feathers could:

- Produce light.
    
- Burn without heat.
    
- Store and distribute kinetic force.
    
- Become projectiles.
    
- Explode under certain conditions.
    
- Function as lockpicks.
    
- Intercept incoming objects.
    
- Provide illumination.
    
- React to Corin's emotional state.
    

There are pages upon pages of experiments.

Most of them contain small drawings.

Some are labeled:

> **"Don't eat this."**

Another:

> **"Definitely don't give me catnip while holding these."**

Another simply says:

> **"Neko thought this was a good idea."**

---

# **THE PERSONAL NOTES**

Mixed among the scientific observations are small personal entries.

They are often cheerful.

Messy.

Filled with doodles.

Occasionally followed by:

**":D"**

They describe Corin learning about herself.

Learning to fly.

Learning what her feathers could do.

Learning what her body could withstand.

Learning how to live.

And occasionally...

learning how to love living.

---

# **THE LINE**

Orphie almost always stops at one particular page.

It is near the end of the original research collection.

The handwriting is unmistakably Corin's.

The sentence is underlined several times.

> **"I live on hope, as long as I spread hope and I have hope of my own, I will move on, live with Orphie :D"**

Orphie reads it every night.

Sometimes twice.

---

# **ORPHIE'S FIXATION**

She knows the sentence by heart.

She does not need to read it anymore.

Yet she still opens the page.

She places her finger beneath the words.

And reads them silently.

Outside the window, Corin remains beneath the tree.

---

# **THE CONTRADICTION**

The sentence describes the person Corin used to be.

Someone who believed:

**Hope keeps me alive.**

Someone who wanted to:

**Move on.**

Someone who wanted to:

**Live with Orphie.**

Someone who added a smiling face at the end.

**:D**

Now Corin sits outside with no heartbeat.

No active regeneration.

No functioning anomalous feathers.

No desire to move.

No hope.

---

# **ORPHIE'S ROUTINE**

Every night:

1. Orphie prepares for bed.
    
2. She opens Corin's research archive.
    
3. She turns to the same page.
    
4. She reads the line.
    
5. She looks outside.
    
6. She sees Corin beneath the tree.
    
7. She remains there for a while.
    

Sometimes Orphie whispers:

> **"You were supposed to live with me."**

---

# **THE WINDOW**

From Orphie's room, the Treehouse window provides a direct view of Corin's usual place.

At night, there is very little light.

Corin's feathers no longer glow.

Her wings appear almost completely black against the snow.

She sits motionless.

Orphie watches.

Then looks back at the page.

---

# **THE QUESTION**

Orphie has never crossed out the sentence.

Never written over it.

Never corrected it.

Never removed the smile.

She refuses to change Corin's words.

Because those were Corin's words when she still had hope.

And perhaps because Orphie is afraid of what it would mean to accept that they are no longer true.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin spent much of her life trying to understand herself.

She studied her wings.

Her body.

Her strange biology.

Her two hearts.

Her impossible feathers.

She documented everything.

And among all the measurements, observations, diagrams, and experiments...

she left one sentence behind.

> **"I live on hope, as long as I spread hope and I have hope of my own, I will move on, live with Orphie :D"**

Orphie reads it every night.

Then she looks outside.

Corin is still there.

Cold.

Silent.

Wrapped in her wings.

Waiting.

The sentence says Corin wanted to live with her.

The person outside seems to have forgotten how.

Orphie closes the notebook.

She looks through the window one more time.

And quietly says:

> **"You still can."**

Outside, Corin does not move.

But Orphie leaves the window open anyway.

**End of Personal Research Archive**
