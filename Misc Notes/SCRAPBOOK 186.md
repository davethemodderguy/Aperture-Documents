### **SUPPLEMENTAL OBSERVATION LOG**

## **SUBJECT: WHITE TIGER – SHELTER T-01 HABITUAL BEHAVIOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Routine welfare observations have documented that the white tiger and Corin Lockwood appear to have established a stable daily routine.

Despite the tiger's territorial nature toward unfamiliar personnel, interactions between the two are notably calm.

Neither displays signs of fear around the other.

---

# **SHARED MEALS**

Visitors to Shelter T-01 frequently notice an unusually large stainless-steel water bowl positioned beside the base of the tree.

It is refilled daily.

Engineering originally assumed it was part of Corin's emergency water storage.

It was not.

The bowl belongs to the tiger.

Nearby is often a second tray containing several exceptionally large cooked chicken drumsticks.

Personnel unfamiliar with the arrangement have occasionally wondered why Corin receives such oversized portions.

The explanation is considerably simpler.

They are not all for Corin.

She shares them.

---

# **OBSERVATION**

One afternoon, Mart visited Shelter T-01 carrying several books.

He arrived just in time to witness lunch.

Corin sat on the wooden steps of the treehouse.

Beside her lay the tiger.

Corin absentmindedly tore pieces from a large chicken drumstick.

She ate some herself.

The rest she offered to the tiger.

The animal accepted each piece with remarkable care, taking the food gently from her hand before returning to its relaxed position.

Neither appeared in any hurry.

The scene lasted nearly twenty minutes.

Mart later remarked that it was one of the quietest afternoons he had seen Corin have in a very long time.

---

# **CARE**

Corin consistently ensures the tiger has access to clean water before preparing her own meals.

Veterinary Services note that the animal appears well-fed and healthy.

Personnel have occasionally observed Corin reminding visitors,

> "His bowl first."

Only after refilling it does she return to making tea or preparing food for herself.

# **LUMI**

During one visit, Lumi watched Corin quietly divide a meal into two portions.

Without thinking, Corin placed the larger piece in front of the tiger.

Lumi smiled.

> "...You always shared your food."

Corin looked briefly embarrassed.

She shrugged.

> "...He's bigger than me."

The tiger looked up as if it understood the conversation.

Lumi laughed softly for the first time that day.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The white tiger remains a regular presence around Shelter T-01.

Its routine with Corin has become so consistent that Security patrols now regard the sight of a large water bowl, a steaming mug of tea, and an oversized chicken drumstick on the treehouse steps as an indication that everything is proceeding normally.

Personnel are reminded that the food beside Corin is not evidence of excessive catering.

She simply refuses to eat while her enormous striped companion is hungry.
