# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – STATUS UPDATE & MATERIALS RESEARCH REPORT**

### **SUBJECT: LUMI – ICE MAGE LINEAGE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Research, Medical & Executive Personnel

---

# **STATUS UPDATE**

Recent medical examinations have confirmed that Lumi's condition has deteriorated significantly.

Although she retains her knowledge of Ice Magic, her ability to channel Ether has diminished considerably.

Spells that were once effortless now require substantial concentration.

Large-scale Cryomancy has become physically exhausting.

Medical Division attributes the decline to cumulative damage sustained over her lifetime and death, rather than a loss of magical knowledge.

Lumi herself summarized the situation simply.

> "...I still remember every spell."

> "...I just can't cast them the way I used to."

---

# **FROST**

Among all current personnel, Frost has developed a particular admiration for Lumi.

The reason is simple.

Both possess ice-based abilities.

While Frost's Cryokinesis originates from her own anomalous abilities, Lumi's magic is the product of the ancient Ice Mage tradition.

The two often spend time together discussing frost, snow, crystal formation, and temperature control.

Although their methods differ greatly, Frost eagerly listens to every lesson.

Lumi has begun referring to Frost as:

> "...My little snowflake."

Frost pretends to be embarrassed every time.

---

# **THE BLUE STRANDS**

For many years, Lumi concealed the icy light-blue strands at the front of her hair with a small hair clip.

Following discussions with Belle, Corin, Corey, and Frost...

She quietly stopped hiding them.

When asked why, Lumi smiled.

> "...They're part of me."

> "...I don't think they need to hide anymore."

Since then, the pale blue strands have remained visible.

Many personnel have remarked that they suit her remarkably well.

---

# **RESEARCH DISCOVERY**

Research Division recently completed an extensive analysis of the naturally occurring pigment responsible for the blue coloration.

The pigment demonstrated highly unusual optical properties.

When incorporated into specialized industrial coatings, it significantly increases the reflection of:

- Visible light.
    
- High-intensity thermal radiation.
    
- Low-energy Ether emissions.
    

Engineering immediately recognized potential defensive applications.

---

# **PROTECTIVE COATING PROGRAM**

Using a synthetically fabricated version of the pigment's structure, Materials Engineering successfully developed a durable protective paint.

**Important Note**

The coating **does not** require harvesting material from Lumi herself.

The pigment has been fully reproduced through laboratory synthesis after studying its molecular and Ether-reactive properties.

The resulting paint has been approved for application to:

- Blast doors.
    
- Research vaults.
    
- Ether containment chambers.
    
- High-security laboratory entrances.
    
- Selected SAFEHOUSE protective doors.
    

The coating improves resistance to both intense illumination and low-level Ether exposure while remaining visually distinctive.

Its soft icy-blue finish has become immediately recognizable throughout the facility.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Lumi's magical strength continues to decline, and large-scale Ice Mage techniques are now used sparingly.

Even so, her influence reaches further than ever before.

She continues to teach Frost.
