# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **SECURITY DIVISION – INCIDENT REPORT**

### **CASE FILE: KCA-IR-077**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **INCIDENT OVERVIEW**

**Date:** ██/██/20██

At approximately **14:23**, four armed individuals entered the Krampus Compliance Authority headquarters with the apparent intent to rob the facility.

The suspects were equipped with:

- Two assault rifles.
    
- One shotgun.
    
- One pistol.
    
- Improvised restraints.
    
- Large duffel bags intended for stolen valuables.
    

They demanded access to the Authority's reserve vault.

The demand was never fulfilled.

---

# **TIMELINE**

**14:23:07**

The suspects entered the main lobby.

Customer Services immediately activated the silent alarm.

Dialyn calmly pressed the alarm button underneath her desk while continuing to speak to the suspects in an unusually polite tone.

---

**14:23:12**

Nearby Marts collectively stopped moving.

Security footage later confirmed every Mart simultaneously looked toward the suspects.

---

**14:23:15**

One Mart quietly said:

> "...Bad."

---

**14:23:18**

The colony dispersed.

The suspects assumed the creatures were fleeing.

Research later concluded the Marts were instead positioning themselves.

---

**14:23:24**

The first impact occurred.

A solidified Mart exited a ventilation duct at high velocity, striking one suspect's forearm.

The shotgun discharged harmlessly into the ceiling.

---

**14:23:26**

Approximately forty additional Marts entered "Rock Mode."

---

**14:23:28**

Security cameras recorded what investigators later described as:

> "...An avalanche."

Marts ricocheted from walls, pillars, reception desks and ceiling beams, striking weapons, knees, shoulders and arms with remarkable precision.

No impacts to vital areas were observed.

---

**14:23:34**

One suspect attempted to flee.

Thirty-seven Marts rolled together into a moving barrier, causing him to stumble directly into Security personnel arriving from the adjacent corridor.

---

**14:23:41**

The final suspect attempted to raise his rifle.

A Mart struck the magazine release.

Another hit the charging handle.

A third collided with the optic.

The suspect reportedly shouted:

> "...WHY ARE THEY AIMING?"

---

**14:23:47**

The suspects were completely immobilized beneath an estimated **126 Marts**, all remaining in solid form.

Several Marts politely informed arriving officers:

> "Safe!"

All four suspects were taken into custody.

No firearms were discharged by KCA personnel.

The only property damage recorded was a single ceiling tile struck by a suspect's accidental shotgun discharge.

The Marts returned to normal behavior approximately thirty seconds after the incident concluded.

Security footage ends with the same 126 Marts happily following Dialyn to the break room, where they each received a sugary cheese cracker.

Several could be heard saying:

> "Thank you!"

Followed by:

> "Happy!"
