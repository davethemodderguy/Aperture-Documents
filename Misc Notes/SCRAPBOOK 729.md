### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / INCIDENT & RECOVERY LOG**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — THE RAIN**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Incident:** Missing person / severe weather exposure  
**Status:** **RECOVERED**

---

# **WEATHER CONDITIONS**

The Blacksite experienced unusually heavy rainfall.

Visibility was poor.

The temperature had dropped considerably.

Rainwater had accumulated throughout the exterior grounds, turning paths into mud and shallow pools.

Alice was not inside the Treehouse.

She was not inside SAFEHOUSE.

She was not with Corin.

She was not with Orphie.

For a period of time, nobody knew where she was.

---

# **STAR'S ERRAND**

Star had been sent outside to retrieve environmental data for Neko.

She wore a raincoat and carried the necessary equipment.

The assignment was routine.

Until Star noticed something beneath one of the trees.

At first, she assumed it was a discarded piece of clothing.

Then it moved.

Star stopped.

> **"...Alice?"**

---

# **DISCOVERY**

Alice was sitting underneath the tree.

She wasn't hiding.

She wasn't attempting to run.

She was simply sitting there.

Her clothes were completely soaked.

Her white hair was dripping with rain.

Her soft hat was saturated and hanging heavily over her head.

Her rabbit ears had fallen downward from the weight of the water.

Her small tail was pressed against the wet ground.

She was trembling.

Her entire body shook from the cold.

Yet she remained sitting beneath the tree.

---

# **STAR**

Star immediately ran toward her.

> **"Alice!"**

Alice slowly looked up.

Her face was pale.

She couldn't speak.

Her lips trembled.

Star crouched beside her.

> **"What are you doing out here?"**

Alice looked down.

She didn't answer.

Her hands were shaking too badly to write.

Star removed her raincoat.

She wrapped it around Alice.

It wasn't enough.

Star carefully picked her up.

Alice was frighteningly light.

She immediately curled against Star's chest.

---

# **ALICE'S NOTEBOOK**

Star noticed Alice's notebook lying beside the tree.

The pages were soaked.

Most of the ink had blurred.

One page remained partially readable.

It contained a drawing.

A small figure sitting beneath a tree in heavy rain.

No umbrella.

No shelter.

The same composition Alice had drawn previously.

Underneath it were several nearly illegible words.

> **"Alice..."**

The rest had dissolved in the water.

---

# **STAR'S REACTION**

Star held Alice tighter.

> **"You don't have to sit here."**

Alice's ears lowered further.

Star continued.

> **"You can come inside."**

Alice looked toward the Treehouse.

Then toward SAFEHOUSE.

She shook her head.

Star understood immediately.

Alice wasn't trying to escape.

She was isolating herself.

Again.

---

# **RECOVERY**

Star carried Alice back.

She did not let Alice walk.

By the time they reached the Treehouse, Corin and Orphie were already waiting.

Corin's expression changed the instant she saw Alice.

> **"Oh, Alice..."**

Corin wrapped her in warm blankets.

Orphie immediately prepared dry clothes.

Alice sat between them, still trembling.

Her wet hat was removed.

Her soaked hair was gently dried.

Her ears slowly began to rise again as she warmed.

---

# **ALICE**

After several minutes, Alice reached for her notebook.

Most of the pages were ruined.

She found a dry page.

Her hands were still shaking.

She wrote:

> **"Sorry."**

Corin shook her head.

> **"No."**

Alice wrote again.

> **"Alice went outside."**

> **"We know."**

Another line.

> **"Alice wanted rain."**

Corin looked at her.

> **"You could have told us."**

Alice stared at the page.

She slowly wrote:

> **"Alice no voice."**

Corin's expression softened.

> **"Then draw it."**

Alice looked at her.

> **"Or write it."**

Corin hugged her.

> **"Just don't disappear."**

---

# **STAR'S REPORT**

Star's report to Neko contained one additional observation:

> _Alice wasn't trying to get away._

> _She was just sitting there._

> _Like she wanted to disappear without actually leaving._

Neko read the report twice.

Then immediately increased the Treehouse's exterior monitoring.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Alice had not gone far.

She had not attempted to cross the Blacksite perimeter.

She had not been abducted.

She had simply gone beneath a tree during the worst rain of the season.

And sat there.

Cold.

Soaked.

Trembling.

Alone.

The image was almost exactly the same as the drawing she had made earlier.

A small figure beneath the rain.

No umbrella.

No one beside her.

Except this time, someone found her.

Star did.

And Star brought her home.
