## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **FAMILY HISTORY DISCLOSURE REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: LUMI LOCKWOOD – POST-RECOVERY UNDERSTANDING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following recent memory-related distress episodes involving Corin Lockwood, a significant information gap was identified.

Lumi Lockwood, despite being Corin's mother and primary emotional support figure, did not possess the full details of what happened to Corin after her disappearance.

She remembered losing her daughter.

She remembered searching.

She remembered the aftermath.

But she did not know the complete extent of what Corin endured during the fifteen years she was gone.

That changed when Norma Lockwood explained everything.

---

# **NORMA'S ACCOUNT**

The conversation occurred privately.

No researchers were present.

No recording was requested.

Norma simply sat with Lumi and explained what had happened.

The experimentation.

The Ether procedures.

The forced alterations.

The trauma Corin carried afterward.

The years of struggling to accept that she was alive.

The fear.

The nightmares.

The belief that she was no longer the same person.

Lumi listened silently.

At first, she did not speak.

She only looked at Norma.

---

# **LUMI'S REALIZATION**

For years, Lumi had believed Corin's greatest pain was losing her family.

She believed Corin needed help remembering the happiness before everything went wrong.

She believed reminding her of the past would help her return to who she was.

Now she understood.

The past was not only something Corin missed.

It was also something that hurt her.

The child Lumi remembered was not simply lost.

That child had suffered.

---

# **NORMA'S WORDS**

Norma explained quietly:

> "Mom..."

> "She doesn't need to be reminded of who she was."

A pause.

> "She needs someone to understand what happened to her."

Norma looked away.

> "She thinks everyone wants the old Corin back."

> "But she doesn't know how to tell them that the old Corin is the one who got hurt."

---

# **LUMI'S RESPONSE**

For the first time since her return, Lumi had no comforting words.

No advice.

No story.

No reminder.

Only grief.

Not because Corin had changed.

Because Lumi finally understood why.

She remembered holding Corin as a child.

She remembered protecting her from small fears.

Thunderstorms.

Nightmares.

Illness.

And now she understood that Corin had faced something no child should have experienced.

Something Lumi could not protect her from.

---

# **RECORDED STATEMENT**

Later, Lumi spoke privately with Norma.

> "I kept asking her to come back."

A pause.

> "I didn't understand why she couldn't."

Her voice became quieter.

> "I thought she was afraid of leaving the office."

> "I didn't know she was afraid of everything waiting outside it."

Norma remained silent.

Lumi continued:

> "I was trying to bring my daughter home."

> "I didn't realize she was still trying to survive."

---

# **CHANGE IN APPROACH**

Following the conversation, Lumi altered how she interacted with Corin.

She stopped focusing on the person Corin used to be.

She stopped describing the past unless Corin asked.

Instead, she began acknowledging the person in front of her.

The person who survived.

The person who continued working.

The person who still cared about others despite everything.

---

# **CORIN'S OFFICE**

That evening, Lumi sat beside Corin's desk.

No stories were told.

No memories were brought up.

No requests to visit SAFEHOUSE were made.

After several minutes, Corin looked up.

> "...You're quiet."

Lumi smiled sadly.

> "I'm listening."

Corin looked confused.

> "...To what?"

Lumi answered:

> "To you."

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the report before adding:

> "Understanding someone requires knowing both who they were..."

> "...and what happened to them."

He continued:

> "Corin was never refusing to return."

> "She was trying to carry something nobody else could see."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Lumi remains with Corin.

Her purpose has shifted.

She is no longer trying to restore the daughter she remembers.

She is learning the daughter who survived.

The goal is no longer to make Corin look back.

It is to help Corin feel safe enough to look forward.

**End of Family History Disclosure Report**
