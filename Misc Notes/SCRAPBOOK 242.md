# **POST-CALL NOTE**

Approximately twenty minutes later, Dialyn received another phone call.

She answered immediately.

> "Hello, Krampus Compliance Authority, this is Dialyn speaking, how can I help you?"

The caller was Corey.

There was a short silence before Corey burst into laughter.

Dialyn sighed.

Then laughed as well.

She has since admitted that the greeting is no longer a requirement of her position.

It is simply reflex.

After answering thousands upon thousands of calls over the years, it has become the first thing she says whenever she picks up a ringing phone.

Human Resources has officially updated her personnel file.
