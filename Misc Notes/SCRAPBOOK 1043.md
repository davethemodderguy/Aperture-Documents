**SUBJECT:** Neri — Unanticipated Exposure to Spicy Chicken Curry  
**INCIDENT TYPE:** Cafeteria / Dietary / Regrettable Discovery

---

## Executive Summary

Neri has an exceptionally low tolerance for spicy food.

This became relevant when Neri selected the cafeteria's **"Today's Special"** without knowing that the day's meal was **spicy chicken curry**.

Neri ate approximately one bite before discovering this information firsthand.

The incident was resolved without serious medical complications.

Neri, however, appeared to regard the curry as a personal betrayal.

---

## I. CAFETERIA CONDITIONS

The cafeteria menu displayed:

> **TODAY'S SPECIAL**  
> **Chicken Curry**

No prominent warning regarding spice level was present.

Neri selected the meal.

She had previously established a habit of eating whatever appeared under "Today's Special."

She did not ask what kind of curry it was.

She did not inspect it particularly closely.

She sat down.

She took a bite.

---

## II. INCIDENT

Approximately three seconds after swallowing, Neri stopped moving.

She slowly lowered her fork.

Her eyes widened.

She looked at the plate.

Then at her coffee.

Then back at the plate.

A nearby resident reportedly asked:

> **"Neri?"**

Neri did not answer.

She reached for her drink.

After taking several sips, she quietly stated:

> **"Hot."**

Pause.

> **"Why is it hot?"**

She was informed that the curry contained significant amounts of chili.

Neri stared at the person who told her this.

Then at the plate.

Then back at the person.

> **NERI:** "It didn't say."

The menu was checked.

She was correct.

It did not.

---

## III. SPICE TOLERANCE ASSESSMENT

Neri's tolerance for capsaicin-containing foods is now classified as:

**VERY LOW**

This is unrelated to her existing anemia and cardiovascular condition, although severe discomfort should obviously not be ignored given her medical history.

Neri is capable of eating ordinary seasoned food.

Her problem is specifically **spicy food**, not flavor in general.

She does not appear to possess the cultural instinct of:

> "Curry probably means spicy."

She apparently possesses the instinct of:

> "Today's Special probably means food."

---

## IV. AFTERMATH

Neri did not complain loudly.

She did not become dramatic.

She simply sat there looking increasingly offended by the concept of curry.

She eventually finished the non-spicy portions of the meal.

The remaining curry was left untouched.

Later, Neko asked:

> **"How bad was it?"**

Neri considered the question.

She raised one hand.

Then made a small gesture indicating:

**very.**

Neko asked:

> **"Do you want me to add a spice indicator to the cafeteria menu?"**

Neri nodded immediately.

---

## V. MENU POLICY UPDATE

Following the incident, Cafeteria Services added a standardized spice indicator to Today's Special listings.

### New notation:

**🌶 0 — No noticeable heat**  
**🌶 1 — Mild**  
**🌶 2 — Noticeable**  
**🌶 3 — Spicy**  
**🌶 4 — Very spicy**  
**🌶 5 — Why**

The chicken curry has been classified as:

> **🌶 3**

Neri has personally requested that future meals above **🌶 1** be clearly labeled.

---

## VI. NERI'S FINAL POSITION

Later that evening, Neri was asked whether she would eat curry again.

She shook her head.

> **"No."**

A pause.

> **"Not that one."**

This distinction was considered reasonable.
