# **CORIN — MERCURY POISONING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Condition:** **Mercury Poisoning**  
**Status:** Critical / Under Treatment

---

# **DIAGNOSIS**

C.O.R.E. medical personnel have identified the cause of Corin's prolonged decline.

**Corin has mercury poisoning.**

The toxic exposure had remained undetected long enough for the mercury to accumulate within her body.

This explains the gradual progression of her symptoms.

What initially appeared to be an unexplained illness was, in reality, a slowly developing toxic condition.

---

# **WHY CORIN BECAME SO WEAK**

The poisoning had time to progress.

Corin's anomalous physiology prevented the immediate catastrophic effects normally associated with severe poisoning, but it did not completely neutralize the mercury.

Instead, her Ether appears to have kept her organs functioning while the toxin continued affecting them.

The result was a prolonged period of:

- Increasing weakness.
    
- Extreme fatigue.
    
- Reduced appetite.
    
- Reduced activity.
    
- Difficulty flying.
    
- Increased need for rest.
    
- Progressive physical deterioration.
    

Her body was continuing to function.

It was simply doing so while fighting a persistent toxin.

---

# **THE MINIATURE FORM**

Corin's miniature feline transformation made the decline considerably more noticeable.

She became small enough that even minor reductions in strength were obvious.

The once-active winged kitten gradually stopped flying.

She spent most of her time curled beneath her wings.

She could still walk and drink, but increasingly required assistance.

The transformation itself is not believed to have caused the poisoning.

---

# **DISCOVERY**

The diagnosis was made after Corin's condition failed to improve despite rest, hydration, nutrition, and continued observation.

Medical personnel detected an abnormal toxic signature within her body.

Further examination confirmed mercury contamination.

The discovery immediately explained the otherwise unexplained decline.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The atmosphere inside SAFEHOUSE changed immediately.

Nobody blamed Corin.

Nobody knew she had been poisoned.

The residents had simply watched her become weaker day after day without understanding why.

Orphie remained beside her.

Belle stayed close.

Neko immediately assisted with securing anything potentially contaminated.

The rest of the residents followed medical instructions and kept the environment calm.

---

# **TREATMENT**

Corin has been placed under intensive medical observation.

C.O.R.E. medical personnel are treating the poisoning and supporting her affected organs while the mercury is removed from her system.

Her hydration and nutrition are being carefully maintained.

Small meals remain preferable while her strength is low.

Her Ether is also being monitored continuously.

The objective is straightforward:

> **Remove the toxin and give Corin's body enough support to recover.**

---

# **CORIN'S CONDITION**

Despite the severity of the poisoning, Corin remains conscious.

She still recognizes everyone.

She still responds to voices.

She still accepts water from her cup.

When held, she generally remains calm.

If someone handles her uncomfortably, she can still produce her characteristic tiny warning nibble.

The behavior has been interpreted as a positive sign.

She is still herself.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie was visibly shaken after learning the cause.

She had spent days believing Corin was simply sick.

Now there was an identifiable reason.

Orphie carefully held the miniature Corin in her hands.

Corin curled up against her.

Her wings slowly closed around Orphie's fingers.

Orphie quietly said:

> **"We'll get it out of you."**

Corin gave a faint meow.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

For weeks, everyone wondered why Corin kept getting weaker.

She had been sleeping.

She was being fed.

She was drinking.

Her wings were healthy.

Her Ether was stable.

Yet she continued to deteriorate.

Now C.O.R.E. knows why.

The poison had simply been given enough time to **brew inside her system**.

Corin's anomalous body kept her alive long enough for everyone to notice something was wrong—but not enough to prevent the mercury from taking its toll.

The tiny white winged cat is now under continuous medical care.

For the first time since her decline began, the SAFEHOUSE residents have an answer.

**they finally have something they can treat.**
