# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The **NIGHT STALKER** is a heavily modified M1911-pattern pistol independently developed by Neri Tachyon following her experimentation with Nekonium alloy.

Ten Night Stalkers currently exist.

Neri has established a strict authorization policy:

> **"Prove your worth."**

As of this document, only **one** Night Stalker has received formal authorization.

The recipient is:

> **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

The remaining nine weapons remain under Neri's authorization control.

---

# 2. MSAC

The **MSAC — Military Special Activities Command** is Aperture Industries' premier high-risk armed response organization.

For operational comparison, MSAC is commonly described internally as:

> **Aperture's equivalent to the British SAS.**

The comparison is intentionally conservative.

MSAC personnel are among Aperture Industries' most capable and heavily trained armed forces.

They are routinely assigned to situations involving:

- extreme hostile resistance;
    
- high-value extraction;
    
- anomalous threats;
    
- hostile organizations;
    
- containment failures;
    
- deep hostile-territory operations;
    
- high-risk reconnaissance;
    
- and circumstances where conventional Aperture security forces are insufficient.
    

MSAC deployment is not routine.

When MSAC arrives, C.O.R.E. generally assumes the situation has already become extremely serious.

# 4. NEKONIUM ALLOY

The defining technological feature of the Night Stalker is its use of Neri's modified Nekonium alloy.

Neri alloyed Nekonium with:

- gold;
    
- platinum;
    
- silver;
    
- and Neondylium.
    

The resulting material substantially reduces several of the practical weaknesses associated with raw Nekonium while retaining useful anomalous properties.

Neko's reaction upon reviewing the research remains archived as:

> **"I have no idea how she did this."**

Followed shortly afterward by:

> **"Actually, I have several ideas. I just don't like any of them."**

---

# 5. ACOUSTIC PERFORMANCE

The Night Stalker's most immediately noticeable characteristic is its exceptionally low acoustic signature.

The combination of Neri's ammunition design, suppressor configuration and material implementation produces a discharge sound that observers have repeatedly described as unusually soft.

During the initial range demonstration, a janitor working nearby failed to notice the firing.

The range supervisor initially believed Neri had not fired the weapon.

Neri had.

Multiple times.

---

# 6. MSAC OPERATOR FEEDBACK

An MSAC operator was permitted to test one of the prototypes.

After firing a controlled sequence, the operator removed their hearing protection.

They paused.

Then stated:

> **"The sound is very satisfying, it can be used as ASMR."**

The statement was subsequently repeated by three other operators.

A fourth operator described the sound as:

> **"The most polite gunshot I've ever heard."**

C.O.R.E. has elected not to include that statement in the official technical evaluation.

It remains here anyway.

---

# 7. OPERATOR HANDLING

MSAC evaluation determined that the Night Stalker is sufficiently familiar in handling to allow experienced M1911 users to adapt rapidly.

However, the weapon's integrated systems and unusual material characteristics require formal familiarization before operational deployment.

Neri has explicitly rejected unrestricted distribution.

Her authorization criteria remain intentionally vague.

When asked what qualifies an operator, she responded:

> **"Good results."**

When asked to elaborate:

> **"...Prove your worth."**

No further clarification was provided.

# 8. AUTHORIZATION EVENT

Of the ten existing Night Stalkers, Neri selected one for individual authorization.

The recipient was:

> **Director Lucious**

The weapon was presented directly to him.

Neri handed over the pistol.

Lucious examined it.

He noticed the engraving.

> **TACHYON INDUSTRIES**

He looked at Neri.

Neri said nothing.

Lucious understood the significance.

The authorization was accepted.

Neri was asked why she selected Lucious.

She did not provide a conventional answer.

# 12. SECURITY NOTICE

The Night Stalker is not a toy.

Its extremely low acoustic signature does not make it harmless.

Personnel are prohibited from:

- handling unauthorized units;
    
- attempting to reproduce Neri's ammunition;
    
- attempting to reproduce the Nekonium alloy without authorization;
    
- removing components from an authorized unit;
    
- or requesting that Neri demonstrate it because they "want to hear it."
    

Neri has already demonstrated it.

C.O.R.E. has recordings.

---

# 13. HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

The Night Stalker carries an unusual significance beyond its technical characteristics.

The engraving:

> **TACHYON INDUSTRIES**

connects the weapon directly to one of the founding scientific families of Aperture Industries.

Neri Tachyon was initially unaware of the significance.

She created the weapon because she saw an interesting material and wanted to experiment.

Only afterward did Aperture establish her identity as the last surviving member of the Tachyon family.

Thus, the first major weapon independently developed by Neri carries the name of the family that helped create Aperture itself.

The symbolism was not intentional.

That has made it considerably more significant.

# 15. FINAL ASSESSMENT

The Night Stalker represents an unusual convergence of:

- Neri Tachyon's chemical expertise;
    
- Neko's Nekonium research;
    
- MSAC's operational requirements;
    
- and a piece of Aperture Industries' forgotten history.
    

Ten weapons exist.

One has been authorized.

The remaining nine await Neri's judgment.

The authorization system is therefore extremely simple:

### **If Neri thinks you're worthy, you get one.**

If she doesn't:

You don't.

C.O.R.E. has no authority to override this decision.
