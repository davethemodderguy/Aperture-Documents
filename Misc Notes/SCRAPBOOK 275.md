# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **INTERNAL PERSONNEL DISCOVERY REPORT**

### **SUBJECT: DIALYN – PREVIOUS AFFILIATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

During a routine cleaning of Dialyn's office, Belle Merryweather discovered a small metal storage box tucked away at the back of a filing cabinet.

The box contained several personal effects from before Dialyn joined the Krampus Compliance Authority.

Among them was an identification card.

The card did not bear the name "Dialyn."

Instead, it carried a single callsign.

> **FAIRY**

Affiliation:

> **Central Execution Department – Mercenary Division**

---

# **IDENTIFICATION CARD**

**Organization:** Central Execution Department (CED)

**Status:** Archived

**Role:** Contract Mercenary

**Callsign:** Fairy

**Legal Name:** [REDACTED]

**Photograph:**

The image depicts Dialyn wearing a white kitsune mask, tilted slightly downward.

Only the lower half of her face and her distinctive split black-and-white hair are visible.

The mask obscures nearly all identifying facial features.

No additional personal information is present.

---

# **INITIAL REACTION**

Belle initially assumed the card belonged to someone else.

After comparing the photograph with Dialyn's hair and earrings, she realized the individual pictured was almost certainly Dialyn herself.

She immediately informed Endmin.

Neither confronted Dialyn until verifying the card's authenticity.

CED archives confirmed it was genuine.

---

# **BACKGROUND**

Prior to joining the Krampus Compliance Authority, Dialyn spent an undocumented period working as an independent mercenary under contract with the Central Execution Department.

Operational records are unusually sparse.

The available files indicate that "Fairy" accepted assignments involving:

- Escort operations.
    
- Search and recovery.
    
- Hostage extraction.
    
- Hollow reconnaissance.
    
- High-risk courier work.
    

There is no evidence linking Fairy to unlawful activity or civilian massacres.

Mission reports consistently describe precise execution with minimal collateral damage.

---

# **THE MASK**

CED doctrine allows mercenaries to conceal their identities using approved operational masks.

Dialyn chose a traditional white kitsune mask.

Investigators believe the decision was deliberate.

It concealed her face while leaving her unmistakable split-colored hair visible enough for teammates to recognize her.

No surviving records explain why she selected the fox motif.

---

# **CONFRONTATION**

Belle eventually approached Dialyn in private.

She simply placed the identification card on the desk.

Dialyn looked at it for several seconds.

Then sighed.

> "...I wondered when someone would find that."

Belle asked only one question.

> "...Why didn't you tell us?"

Dialyn smiled faintly.

> "...Because that wasn't who I wanted to be anymore."

# **DIALYN'S STATEMENT**

Later that evening, Dialyn voluntarily requested that the discovery be documented.

Her statement was added to the file in full.

> "I wasn't hiding it because I was ashamed."

> "I just..."

She looked toward the office window.

> "...liked being known as Dialyn more than Fairy."

Another pause.

> "Fairy survived."

A quiet smile followed.

> "...Dialyn gets to live."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Dialyn's previous service with the Central Execution Department has been officially acknowledged and incorporated into her personnel record.

Her employment with the Krampus Compliance Authority remains unaffected.

Among close friends, the revelation quickly became less surprising than expected.

After all, they had already known Dialyn as the endlessly patient customer support agent, the caretaker who watched over Corin and Corey, the woman who accidentally made soup far too spicy, and the one who could somehow guess every ingredient except salt.

Learning that she had once been the masked mercenary known as **Fairy** simply added another chapter to a life already filled with unlikely stories.
