## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / RECOVERED PERSONAL PROPERTY**

# **THE SKETCHBOOK**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Item:** Corin's Personal Sketchbook  
**Status:** **RECOVERED / PSYCHOLOGICAL SIGNIFICANCE**  
**Author:** Corin  
**Secondary Anomalous Imagery:** **UNIDENTIFIED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Alongside Corin's notebook, C.O.R.E. recovered a separate sketchbook.

Unlike the notebook, which primarily documents Corin's body, abilities, health, and mental state, the sketchbook is almost entirely visual.

Corin drew constantly.

She drew:

- Herself.
    
- Her own anatomy.
    
- Her wings.
    
- Her two hearts.
    
- Orphie.
    
- Lumi.
    
- Arcturus.
    
- Norma.
    
- SAFEHOUSE residents.
    
- The Treehouse.
    
- Landscapes.
    
- Flowers.
    
- Places she remembered.
    
- Places she wanted to visit.
    

The drawings are, by Corin's own assessment:

> **Terrible.**

---

# **THE EARLY DRAWINGS**

The earliest pages are filled with crude sketches.

Proportions are inconsistent.

Hands are frequently too large.

Feet are occasionally missing.

Wings are drawn at unusual angles.

Faces vary wildly from one page to another.

Landscapes sometimes consist of little more than:

**Tree + mountain + sun.**

Despite this, the drawings are clearly affectionate.

Corin drew people she cared about repeatedly.

---

# **CORIN'S SELF-PORTRAITS**

There are numerous drawings of Corin herself.

Some depict her standing.

Some show her flying.

Some depict her wings extended.

Others are simply small doodles in the corner of a page.

One early drawing shows Corin with exaggeratedly large wings.

Beside it:

> **"I'm so bad at drawing, maybe I should ask Orphie sometime."**

Below:

> **"Those are so funny looking. I'm not too good with my hands."**

Then:

> **"-Corin"**

The handwriting is casual.

There are several small attempts at drawing hands underneath.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie appears more frequently than almost any other subject.

Corin repeatedly attempts to draw her.

The quality does not improve significantly.

Several drawings show Orphie with wildly incorrect proportions.

One depicts Orphie with enormous hair.

Another has one arm substantially longer than the other.

A third appears to have been abandoned halfway through.

Corin nevertheless kept all of them.

---

# **THE SAFEHOUSE**

The SAFEHOUSE residents are frequently drawn together.

There are crude group portraits.

Some depict everyone sitting together.

Others show them eating.

One page depicts the residents sleeping.

Another depicts Corin flying overhead while everyone looks upward.

The drawings are simple.

But they are clearly happy drawings.

---

# **THE LANDSCAPES**

Corin also draws landscapes.

Trees.

Mountains.

Snow.

Clouds.

The Treehouse.

The Blacksite.

Places she has visited.

Places she apparently imagined.

The early landscapes are colorful and somewhat chaotic.

Several pages contain enormous suns.

Others contain stars drawn far too large.

One corner of a landscape contains a tiny Corin flying across the sky.

---

# **THE CHANGE**

The sketchbook changes after Orphie's disappearance.

The drawings become less playful.

The landscapes become emptier.

People disappear from group drawings.

Corin's self-portraits become increasingly distorted.

Her wings are frequently drawn folded.

Sometimes they appear damaged.

Sometimes they are almost completely black.

The pages become increasingly dominated by eyes.

---

# **THE EYES**

At first, the eyes appear sporadically.

One in the corner.

Another behind a character.

A small black circle hidden within a landscape.

Then there are dozens.

Then hundreds.

The normal eyes eventually begin disappearing.

They are replaced by the characteristic **Black Eyes**.

Thick.

Circular.

Dark.

Unsettling.

Always facing outward.

Always appearing to watch the reader.

---

# **SELF-PORTRAITS**

Corin's later drawings of herself become increasingly negative.

Her proportions become distorted.

Her wings become oversized or skeletal.

Her eyes are sometimes erased.

In several drawings, Corin is barely recognizable.

One recurring depiction shows her sitting alone beneath the Treehouse.

Black Eyes cover the surrounding trees.

No other person is present.

---

# **THE WRITING**

Several drawings contain handwritten commentary.

One later entry reads:

> **"I'm bad at everything I do, Orphie is better off being alone and not have to take care of someone this fragile, this heavy, this emotionally dependent."**

Below:

> **"If I ask her to teach me to draw, she just throws me off."**

Then:

> **"-Corin"**

The statement appears to be a distorted recollection or imagined response.

There is no evidence that Orphie actually behaved this way.

---

# **THE EYES WITH WINGS**

The final sections contain a new form of imagery.

The Black Eyes begin developing features.

One drawing depicts a massive eye surrounded by wings.

Above it is a halo.

The entire figure emits a vivid azure glow.

The coloration is remarkably similar to **Guiding Light's known visual signature**.

Beneath the drawing:

> **"I am always watching"**

Written in bright azure neon ink.

Below that:

> **"-NOT Corin"**

---

# **IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION**

**Guiding Light did not create this drawing.**

Guiding Light has **no known knowledge of its existence.**

No evidence indicates that Guiding Light instructed Corin to draw it.

No evidence indicates that Guiding Light communicated the phrase.

No evidence indicates that the entity depicted is actually Guiding Light.

The resemblance appears to be entirely Corin's construction.

C.O.R.E. has therefore designated the drawing:

> **UNAUTHORIZED GUIDING-LIGHT-ASSOCIATED IMAGERY**

---

# **THE SIGNATURE**

The phrase:

> **"I am always watching"**

is not written in Corin's usual handwriting.

The text is sharper.

More deliberate.

The azure neon ink is unlike the standard blue ink previously documented in Corin's notebook.

The signature:

> **"-NOT Corin"**

appears intentionally added to distinguish the speaker from Corin herself.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION**

The progression of the sketchbook is striking.

Early Corin draws:

> **People she loves.**

Later Corin draws:

> **People leaving.**

Then:

> **Herself alone.**

Then:

> **Eyes watching her.**

Finally:

> **An eye that claims to be watching everyone.**

The final depiction may represent Corin externalizing the sensation of being watched, judged, or observed.

However, the deliberate denial of authorship is unusual.

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

Orphie was shown the early drawings first.

She smiled at several of them.

> **"She really couldn't draw hands."**

Then she reached the later pages.

The smile disappeared.

She continued until reaching the winged Eye.

She stared at it.

Then at the words beneath it.

> **"I am always watching."**

Orphie quietly asked:

> **"...Who wrote this?"**

C.O.R.E. could not answer.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light has been formally notified that her visual characteristics appear in Corin's sketchbook.

Her response remains pending.

Until confirmation is obtained, C.O.R.E. will not attribute the depiction to her.

The current official position is:

> **The depicted entity is not confirmed to be Guiding Light.**

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The first pages are almost charming.

A badly drawn Corin.

A badly drawn Orphie.

A badly drawn family.

A badly drawn Treehouse.

Corin knew they were bad.

She laughed about it.

She wanted Orphie to teach her.

Then Orphie disappeared.

And the drawings changed.

The people became fewer.

The landscapes became darker.

Corin began drawing herself smaller.

The eyes appeared.

Then more eyes.

Then eyes everywhere.

And finally, one enormous Eye with wings and a halo, glowing the same azure blue associated with Guiding Light.

Underneath it:

> **"I am always watching"**

And beneath that:

> **"-NOT Corin"**

Guiding Light does not know why it exists.

Corin apparently did not claim responsibility for it.

And nobody knows who the statement is supposed to belong to.

The sketchbook therefore remains open to its final page.

The Eye remains there.

Watching.

**End of Corin / Recovered Personal Property**
