## **SUBJECT: CORIN & COREY / NEKO & MISS NEKO – COMPLEMENTARY EXISTENCE MODEL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Research Personnel Only

---

# **ADDENDUM**

Following continued observation, Research Division has amended previous reports regarding dual manifestation phenomena.

Earlier documentation described the manifested individuals as "independent."

While legally and socially accurate, this description proved biologically and psychologically incomplete.

Current evidence suggests a more precise conclusion.

Neither pair exists as two completely separate beings.

Rather...

Each pair completes the other.

---

# **THE COMPLEMENTARY MODEL**

Research Division has adopted the term:

> **Complementary Existence**

The model proposes that both manifested individuals represent inseparable halves of a single complete existence.

Neither individual is incomplete by accident.

They are incomplete by design.

Together, they form a stable whole.

---

# **CORIN & COREY**

Observation has demonstrated remarkable complementarity.

Corin retains characteristics associated with her earlier anomalous physiology.

- Chalky lime hair.
    
- Pale skin.
    
- No heartbeat.
    
- No measurable blood circulation.
    
- Minimal biological requirements.
    

Corey possesses the physiological traits restored after Corin's revival.

- Natural black hair.
    
- Healthy skin tone.
    
- Normal cardiovascular function.
    
- Ordinary human metabolism.
    

Neither can accurately describe life without the other.

When Corin struggles emotionally, Corey frequently notices before anyone else.

When Corey becomes overwhelmed, Corin instinctively adjusts her own behavior to compensate.

Personnel have repeatedly noted that conversations between the two often end before complete sentences are spoken.

Each already understands the other.

---

# **NEKO & MISS NEKO**

Neko later clarified that the same principle applies to himself and Miss Neko.

He explained:

> "People think she's my opposite."

He shook his head.

> "No."

> "She's everything I forget."

Miss Neko, after reading the statement, quietly added:

> "...And he's everything I would forget to enjoy."

Neither correction was removed from the report.

---

# **SEPARATION**

Controlled observations continue to demonstrate that prolonged separation produces progressively worsening symptoms in both complementary partners.

Documented effects include:

- Emotional instability.
    
- Difficulty concentrating.
    
- Heightened anxiety.
    
- Physical exhaustion.
    
- Reduced cognitive performance.
    

Research Division has concluded that these effects are not simply emotional attachment.

They reflect disruption of a naturally complementary system.

Accordingly, intentional separation is prohibited except during immediate life-threatening emergencies.

---

# **SOCIAL OBSERVATION**

Personnel have gradually stopped asking questions such as:

> "Which one is the real Corin?"

Or:

> "Which one is the original?"

Instead, newcomers receive a much simpler explanation.

> "They're together."

> "That's all you need to know."

The same explanation now applies to Neko and Miss Neko.

---

# **LUMI**

During one family gathering, someone quietly asked Lumi whether she felt like she had one daughter...

Or two.

Lumi looked toward Corin and Corey.

The sisters were arguing over whose turn it was to make tea.

Neither noticed the conversation.

Lumi smiled.

Then answered:

> "I have two daughters."

She paused.

> "...Who make each other whole."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Research Division no longer considers Corin and Corey to be two unrelated individuals sharing an origin.

Nor does it consider Neko and Miss Neko to be opposites.

Each pair represents a complementary existence.

Distinct enough to have their own thoughts.

Connected enough that neither can truly be understood in isolation.

As one researcher summarized in the final review:

> **"Do not study Corin without Corey."**

> **"Do not study Corey without Corin."**

> **"The same is true for Neko and Miss Neko."**

> **"Each half explains the other."**

**End of Research Addendum**
