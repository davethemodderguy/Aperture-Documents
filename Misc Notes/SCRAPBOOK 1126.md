# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### RESIDENT WELFARE & INCIDENT RESPONSE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-NEKO-243  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — SAFEHOUSE  
**STATUS:** CLOSED — EMBARRASSING  
**SUBJECT:** Neko — Catnip Coffee Incident  
**INVOLVED PERSONNEL:** Jane Doe

---

# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

At approximately 09:14, Neko entered the SAFEHOUSE kitchen and prepared his usual coffee.

Jane Doe was already present.

This should have been the first warning.

It was not.

At approximately 09:16, Neko consumed the coffee.

At approximately 09:18, personnel began reporting an unusual sound originating from the engineering department.

The sound was identified as:

> **Purring.**

---

# 2. INITIAL OBSERVATION

Neko continued working normally.

He opened his Dell laptop.

He reviewed several engineering documents.

He began typing.

He was also purring continuously.

A nearby researcher initially assumed the sound was coming from a ventilation system.

It was not.

The researcher eventually looked over.

Neko was sitting at his workstation.

Eyes half-closed.

Tail relaxed.

Purring loudly.

Typing at normal speed.

---

# 3. DISCOVERY

Miss Neko entered the room.

She stopped.

She stared at him.

> **MISS NEKO:**  
> “...Are you purring?”

Neko continued typing.

> **NEKO:**  
> “No.”

**Purring intensified.**

Miss Neko stared at him.

> **MISS NEKO:**  
> “You are literally purring.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “I'm not.”

**Prrrrrrrrrrrrrrrr.**

> **MISS NEKO:**  
> “Neko.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “What?”

> **MISS NEKO:**  
> “You're doing it again.”

Neko looked genuinely confused.

---

# 4. JANE DOE

Jane was subsequently located in the kitchen.

She was drinking her own coffee.

C.O.R.E. personnel asked whether she knew why Neko was purring.

Jane paused.

> **JANE:**  
> “Maybe he's happy.”

> **C.O.R.E. WELFARE OFFICER:**  
> “Jane.”

> **JANE:**  
> “What?”

> **C.O.R.E. WELFARE OFFICER:**  
> “Did you put something in his coffee?”

Jane smiled.

She said nothing.

---

# 5. INVESTIGATION

The coffee was examined.

No dangerous substance was detected.

The residue was subsequently identified as **catnip**.

Neko stared at the analysis report.

Then at Jane.

Then back at the report.

> **NEKO:**  
> “You put catnip in my coffee.”

Jane nodded.

> **JANE:**  
> “A little.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “Why?”

Jane shrugged.

> **JANE:**  
> “Science.”

Neko's tail flicked.

> **NEKO:**  
> “That's my line.”

Jane smiled.

> **JANE:**  
> “Not today.”

---

# 6. THE PURRING PROBLEM

The primary complication was that Neko could not stop purring.

He attempted several methods.

### Method 1 — Concentration

Neko attempted to focus exclusively on engineering.

Result:

**Purring continued.**

### Method 2 — Anger

Neko became visibly irritated.

Result:

**Purring continued.**

### Method 3 — Ignoring It

Neko attempted to pretend the purring was not happening.

Result:

**Purring continued.**

### Method 4 — Leaving the Room

Neko left the engineering department.

Result:

**Purring continued.**

### Method 5 — Threatening Jane

> **NEKO:**  
> “I'm going to delete your access to the coffee machine.”

> **JANE:**  
> “You're still purring.”

Neko stopped.

His ears flattened.

> **NEKO:**  
> “I hate you.”

**Prrrrrrrrrrrr.**

Jane nearly fell over laughing.

---

# 7. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri eventually encountered Neko.

She stopped.

Looked at him.

Listened.

> **NERI:**  
> “...Purring.”

Neko looked at her.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Don't.”

Neri continued staring.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Neri.”

She slowly raised her coffee cup.

> **NERI:**  
> “Want some?”

Neko immediately shook his head.

Neri nodded.

She walked away.

After approximately five seconds, she quietly added:

> **NERI:**  
> “Cute.”

Neko froze.

The purring became noticeably louder.

Neri disappeared around the corner.

---

# 8. JANE'S ASSESSMENT

Jane was extremely pleased with the results.

> **JANE:**  
> “He's basically a giant vibrating cat now.”

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “He's still working.”

> **JANE:**  
> “That's somehow funnier.”

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “Should we be concerned?”

Jane looked toward the engineering department.

Neko was still typing.

Still purring.

Still glaring at everyone who looked at him.

> **JANE:**  
> “Nah.”

Pause.

> **JANE:**  
> “He's fine.”

---

# 9. NIKO'S RESPONSE

Niko encountered Neko approximately twenty minutes later.

They listened for several seconds.

> **NIKO:**  
> “You're purring.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “Apparently.”

> **NIKO:**  
> “Do you like it?”

Neko stared at them.

> **NEKO:**  
> “No.”

> **NIKO:**  
> “You sound like you do.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “I am going to replace everyone's coffee with water.”

Niko smiled.

> **NIKO:**  
> “Still purring.”

---

# 10. DURATION

The effect gradually diminished.

Neko's purring eventually became quieter.

Then intermittent.

Then ceased entirely.

He immediately checked the time.

He checked the coffee.

He checked the kitchen.

Then he looked directly at Jane.

> **NEKO:**  
> “Never again.”

Jane raised both hands.

> **JANE:**  
> “Fine.”

Pause.

> **JANE:**  
> “Probably.”

> **NEKO:**  
> “Jane.”

> **JANE:**  
> “What?”

> **NEKO:**  
> “Don't.”

Jane laughed.

---

# 11. C.O.R.E. RESPONSE

The following policy has been added to SAFEHOUSE kitchen guidance:

> **Do not alter another resident's food or beverage without their knowledge or consent.**

This policy was approved unanimously.

Except by Jane.

Jane's objection was:

> **“Where's the fun in that?”**

Her objection was ignored.

---

# 12. ARCHIVIST NOTE

Neko is one of Aperture's most respected engineers.

He has designed containment systems.

He has developed anomalous materials.

He has personally contributed to some of the Blacksite's most sophisticated infrastructure.

He is also, unfortunately, still a cat.

Jane has apparently discovered this distinction.

And she intends to abuse it.
