### **RESTRICTED ANOMALOUS WEATHER / SAFEHOUSE INCIDENT**

# **THE SILENCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **ANOMALOUS SUPPRESSION STORM**  
**Status:** **ACTIVE**

---

# **ANOMALOUS WEATHER CLASSIFICATION**

The storm has now been confirmed as an anomalous phenomenon.

It is not merely interfering with anomalous abilities.

It **suppresses them.**

All of them.

Observed effects include suppression of:

- Ether manifestations.
    
- Dimensional traversal.
    
- Remote communication.
    
- Reality manipulation.
    
- Spatial anchoring.
    
- Regeneration and anomalous healing.
    
- Manifestation abilities.
    
- Long-range perception.
    
- Architect-level abilities.
    

The suppression appears indiscriminate.

There is no known distinction between minor anomalies and entities capable of manipulating reality itself.

**Everything is affected.**

---

# **THE ARCHITECTS**

Guiding Light and Curious Light have both been affected.

For the first time since their existence began, the Architects are unable to simply override an environmental condition.

Guiding Light cannot manifest normally.

She cannot send her blue-ink notes.

She cannot create the familiar blue glow.

She cannot simply appear beside Corin.

Curious Light is similarly restricted.

The phenomenon has effectively reduced both Architects to ordinary physical limitations while they remain inside its influence.

This is unprecedented.

---

# **THE BLUE NOTES**

The last remaining blue notes have lost their glow.

They are still physically present.

The ink remains blue.

But the faint celestial illumination is gone.

Corin keeps them anyway.

She arranged them carefully on the table.

The final note reads:

> **"I am still trying to reach you."**

Corin touches the paper.

Nothing happens.

No glow.

No response.

Just paper.

She eventually whispers:

> **"...I know."**

---

# **THE OTHER SIDE OF THE PROBLEM**

Corin had previously discovered that sleeping allowed her to escape the waiting.

For several days, she deliberately slept for unusually long periods.

The storm has now interfered with that as well.

Corin's anomalous physiology normally allows her to manipulate her own state of consciousness to some degree.

Under suppression, she cannot.

She can no longer simply force herself to sleep.

She has to remain awake.

And wait.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

The notebook has become increasingly difficult to read.

Not because the writing is incomprehensible.

Because the thoughts recorded inside it are.

The entries no longer follow a consistent structure.

There are no longer clear beginnings or endings.

Some pages contain only a few words.

Others are covered almost completely.

Several pages have been rewritten repeatedly.

---

# **CORIN'S STATE**

Despite the disturbing contents of the notebook, Corin remains physically stable.

She eats when prompted.

She drinks water.

Her stitches remain intact.

Her Ether levels are unusually quiet due to the suppression effect.

She has not attempted to leave the treehouse.

She has not become physically aggressive.

She simply sits.

Waits.

Writes.

And listens to the storm.

---

# **THE WINDOW**

Corin spends increasing amounts of time looking outside.

The storm has erased the forest.

There is no recognizable horizon.

No road.

No distant lights.

No movement.

Only white.

She once asked aloud:

> **"Is the world still out there?"**

There was no answer.

She looked at the dead-blue notes on the table.

Then at the notebook.

> **"...I suppose it is."**

---

# **THE ABSENCE OF GUIDING LIGHT**

The most significant psychological change is not simply that Guiding Light cannot reach Corin.

It is that Corin has become accustomed to seeing evidence of her.

A blue note meant:

**Someone noticed.**

A blue glow meant:

**Someone was watching over her.**

Now there is nothing.

The absence is therefore much louder than silence should be.

---

# **AN UNUSUAL ENTRY**

After several hours without writing, Corin finally opened the notebook.

She wrote:

> **Guiding can't reach me.**

> **Orphie can't reach me.**

> **Curious can't reach me.**

> **Nobody can.**

Then she paused.

She added:

> **Maybe that's what the storm wants.**

The sentence was crossed out.

Underneath:

> **No.**

> **Storms don't want things.**

A final line:

> **I think I'm just scared.**

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

The notebook should not be interpreted as a literal account of Corin's beliefs.

It documents an increasingly isolated mental state under extraordinary circumstances.

Corin remains aware that many of her thoughts are distorted.

The concerning factor is that she no longer has her usual external anchors.

No Orphie.

No Guiding Light.

No Curious Light.

No blue notes.

No outside contact.

And no ability to escape the situation through sleep.

For perhaps the first time since her reanimation, Corin is being forced to remain completely conscious of her own thoughts.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The storm has accomplished something previously considered impossible.

It has made the Architects ordinary.

It has silenced Guiding Light.

It has severed the Blacksite from the treehouse.

It has taken away Corin's ability to escape into sleep.

And it has turned her notebook into something deeply unsettling.

Yet beneath all of the disturbing writing, one thing remains consistent.

Corin continues writing.

She continues eating.

She continues waiting.

She continues expecting Orphie to return.

And, despite everything her mind tells her, she has not stopped believing that there is something beyond the storm.

The final page contains only one sentence:

> **"When this ends, I want someone to tell me it was real."**

The blue notes remain beside the notebook.

Their ink is still blue.

They simply no longer glow.

**End of Restricted Anomalous Weather / Safehouse Incident**
