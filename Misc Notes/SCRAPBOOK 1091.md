# 3. RAW NEKONIUM — OBSERVED ADVANTAGES

Neko's presentation established several properties of raw Nekonium.

### PRIMARY ADVANTAGES

**1. Anomalous containment capability**

When properly sealed and configured, Nekonium can contain or suppress certain anomalous effects.

**2. Extremely high thermal transfer**

Nekonium transfers heat very readily.

This makes it useful for rapidly distributing thermal energy through a structure.

**3. Strong anomalous compatibility**

Unlike many conventional materials, Nekonium can remain structurally relevant when exposed to certain anomalous phenomena.

**4. Predictable behavior when properly isolated**

Its behavior becomes significantly more manageable when the material is isolated from incompatible environments.

---

# 4. RAW NEKONIUM — KNOWN FLAWS

Neko was very explicit regarding the material's limitations.

Neri wrote down every single one.

### FLAW 01 — EXTREME DAMAGE SUSCEPTIBILITY

Raw Nekonium is unusually susceptible to physical damage.

It is not a conventional high-strength structural material.

Impacts, deformation and localized stress can compromise its intended function.

Neko therefore emphasized:

> **"Containment capability does not equal structural durability."**

---

### FLAW 02 — EXCESSIVE THERMAL CONDUCTIVITY

Nekonium transfers heat extremely efficiently.

While useful for controlled thermal applications, this creates substantial disadvantages.

Heat introduced into one region can rapidly propagate through the material.

This makes thermal isolation difficult.

---

### FLAW 03 — POOR STANDALONE ARMOR PERFORMANCE

Raw Nekonium is unsuitable as a conventional protective material.

Its anomalous properties do not compensate for its mechanical weakness.

Using raw Nekonium as armor would therefore be:

> **"A spectacularly bad idea."**

— Neko

---

### FLAW 04 — CONTAINMENT DEPENDS ON SEALING

Nekonium does not automatically contain every anomaly merely by being nearby.

Its containment properties require an appropriately sealed configuration.

Incorrect implementation can dramatically reduce effectiveness.

---

### FLAW 05 — ENVIRONMENTAL SENSITIVITY

Raw Nekonium's properties can become inconsistent when exposed to unsuitable environmental conditions.

Material isolation and controlled implementation are therefore required.

---

### FLAW 06 — DIFFICULT FABRICATION

Nekonium is difficult to work with using ordinary manufacturing processes.

Its unusual physical and anomalous behavior complicates machining, shaping and integration.

---

### FLAW 07 — THERMAL MANAGEMENT

Because Nekonium transfers heat so efficiently, systems incorporating large quantities require dedicated thermal-management considerations.

Otherwise, heat can become a system-wide problem rather than a localized one.

---

### FLAW 08 — BRITTLE FUNCTIONAL FAILURE

Damage to a Nekonium component may not simply reduce performance proportionally.

Once sufficiently compromised, its intended anomalous-containment function can fail abruptly.

# 13. ALLOY PERFORMANCE

Neri's iteration of Nekonium alloy demonstrated substantially improved characteristics compared with raw Nekonium.

The alloy retained useful anomalous-containment properties while mitigating several of the material's most problematic characteristics.

Observed improvements include:

- substantially improved structural integrity;
    
- improved resistance to ordinary physical damage;
    
- more manageable thermal behavior;
    
- improved fabrication characteristics;
    
- improved reliability when incorporated into compact systems;
    
- improved stability under repeated operational conditions;
    
- and substantially reduced practical impact of several raw-Nekonium limitations.
    

The alloy does not completely eliminate Nekonium's anomalous behavior.

It makes that behavior considerably more useful.

# 15. THE TACHYON FAMILY

The Tachyon family represents one of the most important scientific lineages in Aperture Industries' early history.

### LEWIS TACHYON

Lewis Tachyon was among the original co-founders of Aperture Industries.

His contributions helped establish the organization's earliest scientific and operational framework.

He later died of a heart attack.

---

### DAVID TACHYON

David Tachyon subsequently became one of the principal figures responsible for establishing the foundations of Aperture's earliest anomaly-containment technology.

Much of the organization's modern containment philosophy can be traced to work originating from this period.

---

- Lucia Tachyon;

# 19. THE KIDNAPPING

Three months before the Tachyon residence bombing, two Tachyon children disappeared.

Neri Tachyon.

And her younger sister.

Historical records had previously classified both children as victims of the later bombing.

That classification was incorrect.

The children had been **kidnapped approximately three months before the bombing.**

They were therefore not present at the Tachyon residence when Woodland Coorp attacked it.

The kidnapping unintentionally saved their lives.
