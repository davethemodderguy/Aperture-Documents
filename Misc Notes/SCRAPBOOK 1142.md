# OFFICIAL UPDATE

Neri has provided additional information regarding the contents of the Trial II haystack.

Contestants must locate:

> **THE NEKONIUM NEEDLE**

However, Neri has now confirmed that she did not hide only one needle.

She hid:

> **1 × NEKONIUM NEEDLE**

and

> **50 × STANDARD IRON NEEDLES**

throughout the haystack.

Only one of these produces a victory.

---

# 1. TARGET DISTRIBUTION

Cargo Bay 4 now contains the following confirmed targets:

|Object|Quantity|Victory Condition|
|---|--:|---|
|**Nekonium Needle**|**1**|**WIN**|
|Standard Iron Needle|**50**|**NO WIN**|
|Hay|**~25,000,000,000 pieces**|Unfortunately, all of it|

Contestants must find:

> **THE CORRECT NEEDLE.**

---

# 2. IDENTIFICATION

The Nekonium needle is distinguishable from the ordinary iron needles by its material.

Contestants are expected to identify it through direct observation and handling.

No equipment may be used.

No external testing apparatus may be introduced.

The competition therefore requires contestants to determine whether the object they have found is actually the target.

---

# 3. FIRST DISCOVERY

The first contestant to find an ordinary needle became extremely excited.

> **CONTESTANT:**  
> “I FOUND ONE!”

Several people immediately stopped searching.

The contestant held the needle above their head.

> **CONTESTANT:**  
> “I WON!”

Neri looked at it.

Pause.

> **NERI:**  
> “No.”

The contestant froze.

> **CONTESTANT:**  
> “What?”

Neri pointed toward the needle.

> **NERI:**  
> “Iron.”

Silence.

The contestant slowly lowered their hand.

> **CONTESTANT:**  
> “You put fake needles in here?”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Fifty.”

The entire search area became noticeably quieter.

# 5. RULE CLARIFICATION

The following rules now apply:

### Rule 1

There is exactly **one Nekonium needle.**

### Rule 2

There are exactly **fifty ordinary iron needles.**

Finding an iron needle does not advance a contestant toward victory.

### Rule 8

### Rule 9

There is no replacement Nekonium needle.

### Rule 10

There will be **one winner.**
