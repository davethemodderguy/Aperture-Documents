# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – ANOMALOUS SPECIES REGISTRY**

### **DESIGNATION: M.A.R.T. BLOBS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Friendly Anomalous Organism

---

# **OVERVIEW**

During ongoing attempts to reconstruct portions of the lost Ice Mage arts, Lumi unintentionally created a previously undocumented Ether lifeform.

The creatures collectively refer to themselves by a single name.

> **"Mart."**

The reason every individual answers to the same name remains unknown.

When asked which one is "the real Mart," they simply point at themselves simultaneously.

Research has since stopped asking.

---

# **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

Marts are small, gelatinous organisms composed primarily of magically stabilized water.

Common characteristics include:

- Bright blue, translucent body.
    
- Deer antlers.
    
- A small black top hat.
    
- Two large expressive eyes.
    
- Rounded blob-like appearance.
    

Despite their watery composition, they maintain a stable shape and can move by bouncing, stretching, or gently sliding across surfaces.

Their bodies remain noticeably cold to the touch.

---

# **ORIGIN**

Research indicates the Marts were accidentally created during one of Lumi's experiments to recreate fragments of ancient Ice Mage magic.

Rather than producing the intended spell...

She produced life.

Lumi later described the event as:

> "...Not exactly what I was aiming for."

She paused as dozens of tiny blobs gathered around her feet.

> "...But I can't say I'm disappointed."

---

# **TEMPERAMENT**

Marts have demonstrated consistently friendly behavior toward all known personnel.

Observed behaviors include:

- Carrying small objects.
    
- Delivering messages.
    
- Watering plants.
    
- Cleaning windows.
    
- Cooling drinks.
    
- Retrieving dropped tools.
    
- Following personnel around out of curiosity.
    
- Sitting quietly beside people who appear upset.
    

No hostile behavior has ever been documented.

---

# **CAPABILITIES**

Although physically weak, Marts can perform many tasks expected of a small body of water.

Examples include:

- Flowing through narrow openings.
    
- Extinguishing small fires.
    
- Washing equipment.
    
- Cooling overheated machinery.
    
- Absorbing minor spills.
    
- Assisting with gardening.
    

Personnel are reminded that Marts are living organisms.

They are not utility equipment.

---

# **MEDICAL NOTICE**

**DO NOT DRINK THE MARTS.**

Although composed largely of water, their internal temperature remains significantly below freezing due to sustained Ice Mage Ether.

Accidental ingestion may result in:

- Oral cold burns.
    
- Temporary numbness.
    
- Gastrointestinal discomfort.
    
- A very confused Mart.
    

Medical Division has already had to issue this reminder after multiple incidents.

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

When informed that Research Division wished to classify the Marts as anomalous entities, Lumi looked down at the tiny blobs happily bouncing around her boots.

She smiled.

> "...They're not experiments anymore."

One climbed onto her shoulder.

She gently patted it.

> "...They're family."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Mart population has grown steadily since their first appearance.

They require little care beyond access to clean water and a cool environment, and they frequently choose to spend time around SAFEHOUSE and the Blacksite gardens.

They have become beloved by staff for their helpful nature, cheerful personalities, and unwavering willingness to assist with even the smallest tasks.

Personnel are reminded of one standing policy:

> **Please be kind to the Marts.**

And another, added in bold after repeated incidents:

> **Seriously. Do not drink them.**

**End of Anomalous Species Registry**
