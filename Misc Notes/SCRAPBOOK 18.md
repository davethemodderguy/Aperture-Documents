# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **EXECUTIVE OPERATIONS DIRECTIVE**

### **DIRECTIVE KCA-EO-14 – REMOTE EXECUTIVE DUTIES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Executive Personnel

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following recommendations from Medical Services and mutual agreement between the Krampus Compliance Authority (KCA) and Aperture Industries, Director **Belle Merryweather** has been authorized to perform executive duties remotely from her office within **Heaven's Blacksite**.

Her office is located adjacent to **Project SAFEHOUSE**, allowing her to remain close to the resident support network while maintaining full operational command.

This arrangement does **not** alter her authority or responsibilities as Director.

---

## **PURPOSE**

The directive was implemented to:

- Reduce unnecessary travel between facilities.
    
- Provide Belle with a more stable daily routine.
    
- Allow Medical personnel to perform regular wellness check-ins when necessary.
    
- Keep Belle within close proximity of trusted colleagues and family while carrying out executive responsibilities.
    

All secure KCA communications are routed through an encrypted executive terminal installed within her Blacksite office.

---

## **MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

Medical Services has documented that Belle has developed **mild insomnia**.

Current symptoms include:

- Difficulty falling asleep on some nights.
    
- Occasional early-morning awakening.
    
- Mild daytime fatigue following poor sleep.
    
- Increased reliance on a consistent bedtime routine.
    

Despite these symptoms, Belle remains fully capable of performing her duties.

Medical staff have emphasized that the condition is currently considered **manageable**, though continued observation is recommended.

---

## **WORKPLACE ACCOMMODATIONS**

To support Belle's well-being, the following accommodations have been approved:

- Flexible start times following nights of poor sleep, where operationally feasible.
    
- Scheduled breaks during extended meetings.
    
- Availability of a quiet office environment.
    
- Access to warm milk or tea within the executive office.
    
- Medical wellness checks upon request.
    

These measures are intended to reduce fatigue without impacting operational readiness.

---

## **OFFICE LOCATION**

Belle's executive office is situated immediately beside Project SAFEHOUSE.

The location allows her to transition between work and personal time without leaving the Blacksite.

Personnel have noted that, once the office door closes, Belle is the Director.

Once it opens at the end of the day, she is simply Belle again.
