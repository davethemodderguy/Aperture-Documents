# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — VISUAL INSTABILITY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** **PRESENT / INVISIBLE / INTANGIBLE / FLICKERING**

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Alice is flickering.

Not metaphorically.

Her presence is becoming visually unstable.

When the stabilization field makes her visible, Alice does not remain continuously rendered.

She appears.

Disappears.

Appears again.

Sometimes for several seconds.

Sometimes for less than a second.

Sometimes only parts of her become visible.

---

# **VISUAL EFFECT**

The first sign is usually her outline.

A faint white silhouette appears where Alice is standing.

Then her hair.

Her ears.

Her face.

Her clothing.

Then everything becomes transparent again.

Occasionally, one part remains visible after the rest disappears.

An ear.

A hand.

Her eyes.

Then—

nothing.

Then Alice is there again.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin has developed the habit of watching Alice constantly.

She refuses to look away for long.

During one observation, Alice flickered out.

Corin immediately reached toward her.

> "Alice?"

Alice reappeared.

She was standing exactly where she had been.

Confused.

Corin smiled through tears.

> "There you are."

Alice smiled back.

---

# **THE WORRYING PART**

The flickering does not appear random.

It becomes more frequent when Alice is tired.

It becomes more severe when she attempts to move.

It becomes particularly unstable when she tries to interact with physical objects.

The more she tries to exist normally—

the less stable she becomes.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle attempted to sit beside her.

Alice flickered.

Belle stopped moving.

> "Is that hurting?"

Alice shook her head.

She flickered again.

Belle's ears lowered.

> "Okay."

She stayed anyway.

---

# **STAR**

Star tried talking to Alice.

Not because Alice could answer.

Just because Star wanted her to hear a familiar voice.

> "Hey, Alice."

Flicker.

> "It's Star."

Flicker.

> "You're still here."

Flicker.

> "You're not going anywhere."

Alice remained visible for several seconds.

---

# **NEKO'S ANALYSIS**

Neko's equipment cannot determine exactly what Alice is doing.

The current hypothesis is that Alice's existence is no longer continuously synchronized with baseline physical reality.

She isn't disappearing.

She is being **dropped from reality temporarily.**

The stabilization field can force her back into visibility.

But it cannot yet maintain the connection indefinitely.

Neko summarized the problem:

> "She's here."

> "Reality just keeps losing her."

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin doesn't care about the terminology.

She doesn't care whether Alice is intangible.

Invisible.

Flickering.

Out of phase.

Or something else entirely.

She only cares that Alice is there.

So when Alice flickers—

Corin talks to her.

When Alice disappears—

Corin waits.

When Alice returns—

Corin smiles.

Every time.

---

# **ALICE**

During one particularly stable interval, Alice sat at the table.

Corin placed a bowl of chicken potato soup in front of her.

Alice couldn't touch it.

Her hand passed through the bowl.

She looked disappointed.

Then she looked at Corin.

Corin smiled.

> "We'll figure it out."

Alice nodded.

She flickered.

Then became visible again.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Alice flickered again.

For half a second, she was completely visible.

A small white bunny.

Her ears drooped naturally.

Her expression was tired.

She looked toward everyone around her.

Then she disappeared.

Nobody panicked.

Nobody said goodbye.

They simply waited.

A few seconds later—

Alice flickered back into existence.

Corin smiled.

> "Welcome back."

Alice smiled too.

For now,

**she remains here.**
