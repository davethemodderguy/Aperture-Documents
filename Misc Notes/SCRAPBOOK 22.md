# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **FINANCE & LOGISTICS DIVISION**

### **INTERNAL MEMORANDUM – AMMUNITION EXPENDITURE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal Use

---

## **SUBJECT**

**Accounting Department's unofficial opinion regarding Senior Manager Norma Lockwood's mission reports.**

---

Officially, the KCA Accounting Department remains impartial toward all personnel.

Unofficially...

Every accountant has the same reaction whenever a mission report arrives bearing the callsign:

> **JUGGERNAUT**

Not because the mission failed.

Quite the opposite.

The mission is usually a complete success.

The concern comes afterward.

---

## **AMMUNITION RECONCILIATION**

Norma's assigned support weapon is a belt-fed **7.62 mm machine gun** firing specialized **Evisceration-Tipped** ammunition.

The ammunition is significantly more expensive than standard service rounds due to its specialized manufacturing process.

Following each deployment, Logistics submits ammunition expenditure records to Accounting.

Accounting then begins calculating replacement costs.

This process is commonly accompanied by prolonged silence.

---

## **COMMON ACCOUNTING REACTIONS**

> "...How many belts?"

---

> "...She used all of them?"

---

> "...Again?"

---

> "...Engineering, why are you requesting another shipment?"

---

## **LOGISTICS RESPONSE**

Logistics has repeatedly pointed out one important detail.

Norma's ammunition expenditure is entirely justified by operational reports.

When the mission requires sustained suppressive fire, Norma delivers exactly that.

The resulting expenditure is substantial.

So is the mission success rate.
