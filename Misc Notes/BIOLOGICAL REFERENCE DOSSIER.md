## **SUMMARY**

Director Belle Merryweather belongs to an exceptionally rare Thiren subtype designated as a **Full Body Thiren**.

Unlike conventional Thirens, who typically possess a humanoid form with animal traits and may be capable of transforming into a full animal form, Full Body Thirens undergo a fundamentally different developmental process.

---

## **FULL BODY THIREN**

Current biological research suggests that a Full Body Thiren begins life with two distinct physiological states:

- A humanoid Thiren form.
    
- A complete animal form.
    

During development, these two forms gradually undergo an irreversible fusion.

Rather than retaining the ability to transform between forms, the body permanently settles into a single hybrid state that incorporates characteristics of both.

This process cannot be reversed through any known medical or anomalous means.

---

## **BELLE'S CASE**

Medical reconstruction indicates that Belle once possessed a complete rabbit form.

However, as her Full Body Thiren physiology matured, her rabbit form and humanoid form permanently merged.

The result is Belle's current appearance:

- Plush-like pink fur covering her body.
    
- Rabbit ears.
    
- Rabbit tail.
    
- Soft, stuffed-animal-like exterior.
    
- Fully humanoid posture and dexterity.
    

Although she no longer has a separate bunny transformation, traces of her original rabbit physiology remain evident throughout her anatomy.

---

## **MEDICAL OBSERVATIONS**

The merged physiology provides several unusual characteristics.

Documented examples include:

- Exceptional impact absorption.
    
- High flexibility.
    
- Naturally soft external tissue.
    
- Enhanced jumping ability.
    
- A tendency to bounce rather than suffer injury from large falls.
    

Researchers note that these traits are attributed to Belle's unique physiology in combination with the stabilizing Ether present throughout her body.

---

## **COMMON MISCONCEPTION**

Personnel occasionally ask:

> "Can Belle turn into a bunny?"

The answer is:

> **No.**

She already is.

Belle does not possess a separate rabbit form because it no longer exists as an independent state.

It became part of who she is.
