## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / PERSONAL RESEARCH ARCHIVE**

# **THE SENTENCE ORPHIE READS EVERY NIGHT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Reader:** Orphie  
**Archive:** Corin's Personal Research Notes

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie has developed a nightly routine.

Before going to bed, she looks through Corin's research notes.

Not the darker pages.

Not the pages filled with Eyes.

Not the entries documenting Corin's disappearance.

She reads the pages where Corin tried to understand herself.

Then Orphie looks out the window.

Corin is usually still outside.

Under the tree.

Under the snow.

Wings wrapped around herself.

Waiting.

---

# **CORIN'S RESEARCH**

Before everything changed, Corin spent considerable time documenting her own physiology.

Her notes cover four primary subjects.

---

## **I — THE WINGS**

Corin carefully documented:

- Feather structure.
    
- Wing movement.
    
- Regeneration.
    
- Flight.
    
- Anomalous properties.
    
- Temperature response.
    
- Kinetic absorption.
    
- Feather shedding.
    
- Luminosity.
    
- The relationship between her wings and emotions.
    

Many entries contain crude diagrams.

Some are surprisingly detailed.

One recurring conclusion appears throughout the research:

> **"My wings react before I do."**

---

## **II — THE BODY**

Corin documented almost everything she could observe.

Temperature.

Regeneration.

Blood.

Muscle structure.

Healing.

Anomalous reactions.

Changes caused by environmental conditions.

She treated herself almost like a scientific specimen.

Not because she disliked herself.

Originally, it was curiosity.

She wanted to understand what she was.

---

## **III — THE TWO HEARTS**

Corin was particularly fascinated by her two hearts.

She recorded their rhythm.

Synchronization.

Response to physical exertion.

Response to emotional stress.

She even attempted to determine whether each heart served a different biological purpose.

Her conclusion was surprisingly simple:

> **"They both beat because I'm alive."**

---

## **IV — THE FEATHERS**

The feather research is the largest section.

Corin documented almost every anomalous property she discovered.

Ignition.

Kinetic storage.

Projectile behavior.

Explosive reactions.

Lockpicking.

Light production.

Regeneration.

She repeatedly tested their limits.

She was fascinated by how something as small as a feather could contain so much potential.

Then came the later entries.

The feathers became dormant.

Their abilities disappeared.

Corin recorded the change without understanding why.

---

# **THE SENTENCE**

Among hundreds of pages of observations, there is one line Orphie repeatedly returns to.

It is written in Corin's handwriting.

The lettering is noticeably more energetic than the surrounding entries.

The sentence reads:

> **"I live on hope, as long as I spread hope and I have hope of my own, I will move on, live with Orphie :D"**

The **":D" is intentional.**

It is not a transcription error.

It is not an annotation.

It was drawn deliberately.

---

# **ORPHIE'S FIXATION**

Orphie reads that sentence almost every night.

She has memorized it.

She knows exactly where it appears in the notebook.

Sometimes she does not even read the surrounding research.

She opens directly to that page.

Reads the sentence.

Then looks outside.

---

# **THE WINDOW**

Outside:

Corin.

Cold.

Motionless.

Wings wrapped around herself.

No glowing feathers.

No heartbeat.

No apparent hope.

Just the girl who once wrote:

> **":D"**

---

# **THE CONTRAST**

Orphie has difficulty reconciling the two versions of Corin.

The Corin who wrote:

> **"I will move on."**

And the Corin who now refuses to move.

The Corin who wanted to:

> **"live with Orphie :D"**

And the Corin who sits outside despite Orphie being right there.

The Corin who believed hope was enough.

And the Corin who eventually lost it.

---

# **ORPHIE'S THOUGHT**

Orphie once traced the ":D" with her finger.

She whispered:

> **"You really meant this, didn't you?"**

Outside the window, Corin did not move.

Orphie continued:

> **"You were happy."**

Silence.

> **"You wanted a future."**

Still nothing.

Orphie closed the notebook.

Then opened it again.

She read the sentence one more time.

---

# **THE RESEARCHER**

There is something particularly painful about Corin's research.

She documented herself because she wanted to understand herself.

She studied her wings.

Her body.

Her hearts.

Her feathers.

She tried to understand what made her different.

She wrote observations.

She wrote theories.

She wrote conclusions.

And eventually she wrote something that wasn't scientific at all.

Something simple.

Something human.

> **"I will move on, live with Orphie :D"**

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Every night, Orphie reads about Corin.

Not the missing Corin.

Not the broken Corin.

Not the girl beneath the snow.

She reads about the girl who was fascinated by her own wings.

Who studied her two hearts.

Who experimented with her feathers.

Who tried to understand her strange body.

And then she reaches that one sentence.

> **"I live on hope, as long as I spread hope and I have hope of my own, I will move on, live with Orphie :D"**

Orphie always pauses there.

Because that little face matters.

The **":D"** means Corin was genuinely happy when she wrote it.

She wasn't pretending.

She wasn't being poetic.

She was excited about the future.

She wanted to live.

She wanted to spread hope.

She wanted to live with Orphie.

Orphie looks through the window.

Corin is still outside.

Still cold.

Still waiting.

Still wrapped in her wings.

Orphie closes the notebook.

Then quietly says:

> **"You were supposed to live with me, Corin."**

And before going to sleep, she leaves the window uncovered.

Just in case Corin looks back.
