# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / MISSING PERSONNEL LOG**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — FIVE WEEKS MISSING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Age:** Approximately 5 years old  
**Missing Since:** Archive Incident  
**Current Status:** **NOT FOUND**

---

# **FIVE WEEKS**

Five weeks have passed.

There is still no confirmed sighting of Alice.

No surveillance footage.

No recovered clothing.

No confirmed tracks.

No reliable communication.

No sign that she has returned to the Treehouse.

The search remains active.

---

# **THE SEARCH**

The Blacksite has been searched repeatedly.

Every known route Alice could reasonably have taken has been examined.

Search teams have covered:

- Residential sectors
    
- Archives
    
- Maintenance corridors
    
- Exterior grounds
    
- Forests
    
- Mountain paths
    
- Abandoned facilities
    
- Remote shelters
    
- Underground passages
    
- Areas surrounding the Treehouse
    

Nothing.

The search has been expanded beyond the locations Alice normally visited.

Still nothing.

---

# **THE MOST DISTURBING DETAIL**

Alice is five years old.

She cannot walk particularly far.

She cannot move quickly.

Her stamina is extremely poor.

Her immune system is severely compromised.

Her body is vulnerable to cold, illness, dehydration, and physical injury.

Under normal circumstances, these limitations should make finding her relatively straightforward.

They have not.

Alice has simply vanished.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin continues searching.

She sleeps less.

She eats only when someone reminds her.

She repeatedly returns to the same routes.

Every small movement in the snow catches her attention.

Every white shape in the distance becomes Alice for half a second.

Then it isn't.

Corin has stopped asking:

> **"Where could she have gone?"**

She now asks:

> **"Where would she go if she thought nobody wanted her?"**

Nobody has an answer.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle has become increasingly quiet.

She continues participating in searches, carrying supplies and checking locations that smaller personnel can reach.

She still carries a small carrot in her pocket.

When asked why, she replied:

> **"If we find Alice, she might be hungry."**

She has never removed it.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has ordered the search to remain active indefinitely.

No expiration date has been placed on Alice's missing-person case.

The Archives incident has also resulted in permanent revisions to medical-record security.

No restricted medical record may again be left accessible in an unsecured terminal.

The policy is now informally known as:

> **"The Alice Rule."**

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

The Treehouse remains untouched.

Alice's belongings are still there.

Her bed remains made.

Her small gardening tools remain beside the plants she helped grow.

A few carrots are still growing outside.

Nobody has removed them.

Corin refuses to let anyone clean out Alice's room.

> **"She's coming back."**

That sentence has remained unchanged for five weeks.

---

# **MEDICAL CONCERN**

Every passing day increases concern regarding Alice's physical survival.

She requires regular nutrition.

She requires clean water.

She requires warmth.

She requires medical supervision.

She requires protection from infection.

None of these things are guaranteed wherever she currently is.

Her previous eight-month survival does not reassure the medical department.

It frightens them.

Because Alice's previous survival occurred under circumstances that should have killed her.

There is no guarantee that she can repeat it.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT**

The possibility that Alice intentionally isolated herself remains the leading psychological hypothesis.

If so, she may be avoiding civilization rather than seeking it.

This creates a particularly difficult search problem.

Alice may hide when she sees people.

She may remain silent when called.

She may smile if approached and insist that she is fine.

She may believe that returning would inconvenience everyone.

The search teams have therefore been instructed to avoid treating Alice as a conventional runaway.

She is not believed to be running **from** SAFEHOUSE.

She may be running because she believes SAFEHOUSE would be better without her.

---

# **CORIN'S STATEMENT**

During a briefing, Corin was asked whether she believed Alice would return voluntarily.

Corin answered:

> **"Yes."**

The officer asked:

> **"Why?"**

Corin looked toward the Treehouse.

> **"Because she still loves us."**

A pause.

> **"She just doesn't think she's allowed to."**

---

# **SEARCH STATUS**

**ACTIVE**

No reduction in search resources has been authorized.

No assumption of death has been made.

No assumption of permanent disappearance has been made.

No closure date has been established.

The case remains:

> **MISSING — ALIVE UNTIL PROVEN OTHERWISE**

# **FINAL NOTE**

Five weeks ago, Alice walked through the Archives.

She found a document she was never supposed to read.

She learned things she was never supposed to learn.

Then she left.

Five weeks later, her bed is still empty.

Corin still waits.

Belle still carries a carrot.

Orphie still keeps the search active.

The Treehouse remains ready for her return.

And somewhere beyond the search perimeter, there is a five-year-old white rabbit who may still believe that disappearing was the kindest thing she could do for everyone.

She is wrong.

She has always been wrong about that.

And if anyone finds her, they will not ask why she left.

They will not tell her how frightened everyone became.

They will not tell her she caused trouble.

They will simply tell her:

> **"Alice."**

> **"Come home."**
