# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — THE CONVERSATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** **ALIVE / EXTREMELY FRAGILE**

---

# **THE CONVERSATION**

Alice was awake several hours after her cardiac arrest.

She was extremely weak.

Her voice was barely audible.

Corin sat beside her.

For several minutes, neither spoke.

Eventually, Corin took Alice's hand.

> **"Alice..."**

Alice looked toward her.

> **"Hm?"**

Corin hesitated.

She had been holding the words inside for too long.

> **"I can't lose you."**

Alice became still.

Corin continued.

> **"I've already lost enough people in my life."**

Alice looked down.

Corin squeezed her hand.

> **"I don't think I could handle losing you too."**

She meant:

**Please stay.**

**I love you.**

**You matter to me.**

**I don't want the world to take another person I love.**

Alice heard something different.

---

# **ALICE'S INTERPRETATION**

Alice's expression slowly changed.

She looked frightened.

> **"Because Alice?"**

Corin frowned.

> **"What?"**

> **"You lost people because of Alice?"**

> **"No."**

Alice looked away.

> **"Alice can't leave."**

Corin immediately realized something had gone wrong.

> **"That's not what I meant."**

Alice continued.

> **"If Alice go away..."**

She swallowed.

> **"...you lose Alice."**

Corin nodded.

> **"Yes, but—"**

Alice interrupted quietly.

> **"So Alice stay."**

Corin paused.

> **"Yes, but I don't want you to stay because you're scared."**

Alice looked confused.

> **"Then why?"**

Corin searched for an answer.

> **"Because I need you."**

Alice's ears lowered.

That sentence made everything worse.

---

# **ANOTHER MISINTERPRETATION**

Alice heard:

> **"Corin needs Alice."**

Her mind translated it into:

> **"Alice is responsible for Corin."**

She became visibly distressed.

> **"Alice need be good."**

> **"Alice need stay."**

> **"Alice can't make Corin sad."**

Corin shook her head.

> **"No, Alice. That's not what I mean."**

> **"But you need Alice."**

> **"I mean I love you."**

Alice stared at her.

> **"Love need Alice?"**

Corin froze.

She realized the problem.

Alice wasn't asking about love.

She was asking whether love was another obligation.

---

# **CORIN'S CORRECTION**

Corin took a breath.

She carefully changed her wording.

> **"Listen to me."**

Alice looked at her.

> **"You don't have to take care of me."**

Alice blinked.

> **"You don't have to make me happy."**

Another pause.

> **"You don't have to stay alive for me."**

Alice's expression became uncertain.

Corin continued:

> **"I want you here because you're Alice."**

> **"Not because I need you to fix anything."**

Alice stared at her.

> **"Alice can just... be?"**

Corin nodded.

> **"Yes."**

---

# **ALICE**

Alice thought for a long time.

Then:

> **"Even if Alice sick?"**

> **"Yes."**

> **"Even if Alice can't walk?"**

> **"Yes."**

> **"Even if Alice forget?"**

Corin's eyes began watering.

> **"Yes."**

> **"Even if Alice don't eat?"**

Corin hesitated.

> **"...We'll help you."**

Alice looked down.

> **"Even if Alice make everyone worry?"**

Corin answered:

> **"Especially then."**

Alice didn't understand that last part.

But she remembered the tone.

It wasn't angry.

It wasn't disappointed.

It was warm.

---

# **THE WRONG WORDS**

The conversation demonstrated how easily ordinary language can become distorted inside Alice's current worldview.

Corin said:

> **"I can't lose you."**

Alice heard:

> **"You must not leave."**

Corin said:

> **"I need you."**

Alice heard:

> **"You are responsible for me."**

Corin said:

> **"Don't make me worry."**

Alice would hear:

> **"Your suffering hurts me."**

Corin said:

> **"Stay with us."**

Alice might hear:

> **"You are not allowed to leave."**

None of these were Corin's intentions.

But Alice's mind fills the gaps with guilt.

---

# **THE FINAL MOMENT**

Corin eventually rested her forehead against Alice's.

> **"I'm sorry I used the wrong words."**

Alice whispered:

> **"Corin bad words."**

Corin gave a tiny laugh through her tears.

> **"Yeah."**

Alice looked at her.

> **"Alice bad understanding."**

Corin immediately shook her head.

> **"No."**

> **"Both of us got confused."**

Alice thought about this.

Then she quietly asked:

> **"Can Alice stay because Alice wants?"**

Corin smiled.

> **"Yes."**

> **"Not because Corin need?"**

> **"Not because I need."**

> **"Not because everyone sad?"**

> **"Not because everyone sad."**

Alice's eyes slowly closed.

> **"Okay."**

Corin stayed beside her.

Alice fell asleep holding her hand.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL NOTE**

Alice's interpretation of language remains heavily influenced by her self-sacrificial worldview.

Statements concerning:

- Responsibility
    
- Loss
    
- Need
    
- Obligation
    
- Worry
    
- Dependence
    

should therefore be phrased carefully.

Alice is extremely likely to convert expressions of affection into perceived obligations.

The safest message remains simple:

> **"You are loved."**

Not:

> **"We need you."**

Not:

> **"Don't leave us."**

Not:

> **"You can't do this to us."**

Alice needs to understand that her value does not depend on what she provides to others.

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

Corin wanted Alice to understand:

> **"Please don't disappear. I love you."**

What Alice initially heard was:

> **"You are responsible for preventing me from losing another person."**

It took several more sentences to separate those ideas.

Alice still has difficulty understanding the difference.

But before falling asleep, she asked one final question:

> **"Can Alice stay because Alice wants?"**

Corin answered:

> **"Yes."**

For now, that was enough.
