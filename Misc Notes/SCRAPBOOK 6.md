## **ADDENDUM 01 – TERMINAL BREACHING PERFORMANCE**

Following controlled testing of the KCA-BRH-12 **"PILEDRIVER,"** additional performance data has been collected regarding its effectiveness against armored targets and hardened materials.

---

## **TEST OBJECTIVES**

Evaluate the PILEDRIVER's ability to:

- Breach modern ballistic armor.
    
- Defeat hardened plate carriers.
    
- Destroy reinforced protective equipment.
    
- Penetrate industrial barriers.
    
- Maintain structural integrity after repeated impacts.
    

---

## **TEST RESULTS**

### **Ballistic Armor**

The initial hammer strike produces substantial blunt-force trauma, often deforming or fracturing rigid armor components.

Immediately following impact, the integrated 12-gauge discharge strikes an already-compromised surface.

The combined effect dramatically increases localized damage compared to either impact or firearm use alone.

---

### **Steel Plates**

Testing against hardened steel plates demonstrated severe localized deformation.

Repeated strikes eventually fractured mounting hardware and supporting fixtures before significant wear appeared on the hammer itself.

---

### **Composite Barriers**

The weapon proved highly effective against:

- Reinforced wooden doors.
    
- Security shutters.
    
- Composite barricades.
    
- Concrete block walls.
    
- Industrial locking mechanisms.
    

Observers noted that the hammer rarely became stuck after impact due to its wedge-shaped striking geometry.

---

### **Nekonium Durability**

Inspection after extensive testing revealed only superficial cosmetic wear.

No measurable deformation of the reinforced hammer head was observed.

---

## **TACTICAL ANALYSIS**

Researchers concluded that the PILEDRIVER is particularly effective because it combines two distinct mechanisms in rapid succession:

1. A concentrated kinetic strike that compromises structural integrity.
    
2. An immediate close-contact shotgun discharge directed into the damaged area.
    

Against rigid protective equipment, this sequence substantially increases breaching effectiveness.

The platform is therefore recommended only for specialized breaching roles and close-quarters operations requiring significant mechanical force.

---

## **LIMITATIONS**

The PILEDRIVER is intentionally heavy and optimized for close-range use.

It is unsuitable for:

- Long-range engagements.
    
- General patrol duties.
    
- Routine security work.
    

The platform is intended to complement, not replace, standard tactical equipment.
