# 1. OVERVIEW

Jane Doe frequently contacts the Blacksite directly to deliver intelligence gathered from within Woodland Coorp.

Despite the sensitive nature of these communications, Jane has developed an unusually effective cover story.

Woodland Coorp personnel are aware that Jane occasionally makes personal calls.

They have apparently concluded that she is simply:

> **“Calling her brother.”**

They have not questioned this assumption to any meaningful degree.

This has allowed Jane to communicate with Aperture Industries while remaining under the appearance of making an ordinary family telephone call.

---

# 2. THE BLACKSITE PHONE CALL

Jane generally contacts the Blacksite using an established telephone line.

The call is commonly answered by:

> **Neko.**

This arrangement has become sufficiently routine that Neko will frequently answer the phone without requiring Jane to identify herself.

The conversation may then transition naturally from an apparently ordinary family call into an intelligence exchange.

Because Jane is already known to have family connections, the call does not immediately appear suspicious to Woodland observers.

---

# 3. THE "BROTHER" COVER

Woodland's assumption is remarkably simple:

> **Jane is calling her brother.**

They have no reason to believe that the person answering the telephone is receiving classified intelligence.

This provides Jane with plausible cover for:

- Calling the Blacksite regularly;
    
- Remaining on the phone for extended periods;
    
- Speaking casually;
    
- Asking about people at the Blacksite;
    
- Mentioning apparently personal matters;
    
- Calling back after short intervals;
    
- Becoming familiar with the people answering;
    
- Maintaining an established relationship with the supposed recipient.
    

The more ordinary the conversation appears, the less suspicious it becomes.

---

# 4. SAFEHOUSE RESIDENT PARTICIPATION

A particularly effective extension of the cover involves **SAFEHOUSE residents joining the call.**

Neko has determined that allowing other residents to participate can make the cover significantly more convincing.

For example, Jane may speak with Neko before another resident joins the conversation.

A Woodland observer hearing the call would therefore hear Jane speaking with multiple people.

This creates the impression that Jane is contacting her family rather than communicating with an intelligence organization.

The participants do not need to maintain an elaborate story.

Normal conversation is generally sufficient.

---

# 5. RECOMMENDED COVER PRACTICE

SAFEHOUSE personnel are encouraged to behave naturally during Jane's calls.

The objective is **not** to create an elaborate deception.

The objective is to make the call look boring.

Potential conversation topics include:

- Food
    
- Daily activities
    
- Work
    
- Family matters
    
- Minor complaints
    
- SAFEHOUSE events
    
- Hobbies
    
- Casual jokes
    
- Other mundane subjects
    

Classified information can then be incorporated into the conversation using previously established communication conventions.

---

# 6. WHY THIS WORKS

The cover relies primarily on **expectation**.

Woodland already believes Jane is making a family call.

Once that assumption has been established, ordinary behavior reinforces it.

A call involving multiple SAFEHOUSE residents may actually make the cover stronger.

From Woodland's perspective:

> Jane called her brother.

Then:

> Jane spoke to several people in the same location.

Therefore:

> Jane must have been speaking with her family.

The correct conclusion would instead be:

> **Jane contacted an Aperture facility and delivered intelligence.**

Woodland has, so far, failed to reach this conclusion.

---

# 7. NEKO'S ROLE

Neko has become the most frequent recipient of Jane's calls.

His familiarity with Jane makes him particularly useful for maintaining the cover.

Neko is also capable of transitioning between casual conversation and intelligence-related discussion without making the change obvious.

This has resulted in an unusual situation where:

> **Aperture's Head of Engineering and Archives is also functioning as the world's most suspicious-looking "brother" who somehow never gets questioned.**

Neko reportedly considers the arrangement amusing.

---

# 8. SAFEHOUSE PARTICIPATION

Residents who are present may occasionally participate in Jane's calls.

Their involvement serves several purposes:

### Familiarity

Jane can demonstrate that she has an established relationship with the people at the Blacksite.

### Cover reinforcement

Multiple voices make the call sound more like a genuine family conversation.

### Natural interaction

Residents can provide spontaneous conversation rather than requiring Jane to maintain a scripted exchange.

### Suspicion reduction

A caller who routinely speaks with the same group of people is less likely to appear to be contacting a secret intelligence facility.

---

# 9. OPERATIONAL GUIDANCE

Personnel participating in Jane's calls are instructed to:

**DO:**

- Act naturally.
    
- Speak casually.
    
- Treat Jane as a familiar person.
    
- Allow ordinary conversation to occur.
    
- Follow established intelligence communication procedures.
    
- Keep classified information within approved channels.
    

**DO NOT:**

- Suddenly become formal when Jane calls.
    
- Refer to Jane as an informant.
    
- Mention Woodland's surveillance.
    
- Discuss classified operations openly.
    
- Ask Jane to repeat sensitive information unnecessarily.
    
- Deliberately make the conversation sound suspicious.
    

The best cover is one that nobody has to think about.
