# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **SECURITY DIVISION – INCIDENT REPORT**

### **CASE FILE: KCA-IR-084**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **INCIDENT OVERVIEW**

**Date:** ██/██/20██

At approximately **02:17**, four armed suspects infiltrated the Krampus Compliance Authority headquarters after business hours.

Unlike previous attempts, the suspects bypassed the reception area and headed directly toward the reserve vault.

The colony of resident Marts detected the intrusion before Security personnel reached the area.

---

# **TIMELINE**

**02:17:42**

Four suspects entered the vault corridor.

No Marts were visible.

One suspect remarked:

> "...Guess those little things are asleep."

Security footage later confirmed they were not.

---

**02:17:48**

Thermal cameras recorded over one hundred Marts quietly attached to the corridor ceiling, ventilation ducts, support beams, and light fixtures.

All remained completely motionless.

---

**02:17:51**

The lead suspect reached the vault door.

A single Mart looked down.

> "...Bad."

---

**02:17:52**

Approximately ninety Marts simultaneously entered **Rock Mode**.

---

**02:17:53**

The ambush began.

Security footage shows dozens of blue boulders dropping almost vertically from above while others ricocheted from opposite walls.

Within three seconds:

- Suspect #1 was struck in both shoulders and pinned.
    
- Suspect #2 lost hold of their rifle after multiple impacts to the forearms.
    
- Suspect #3 attempted to flee before colliding with another wave of solidified Marts.
    

---

**02:17:58**

Suspect #4 remained standing for approximately two additional seconds.

Researchers later determined this was because the colony had unintentionally stacked nearly thirty solidified Marts directly onto his torso.

Unable to move beneath the growing pile, he lost consciousness.

---

# **UNEXPECTED RESPONSE**

Immediately after the suspects ceased resisting, every Mart returned to its normal form.

One Mart looked at Suspect #4.

There was no movement.

The Mart quietly asked:

> "...Friend?"

No response.

Several nearby Marts began nudging him.

Another gently shook his sleeve.

> "...Wake?"

Within seconds, dozens of Marts had gathered around him.

Security audio recorded increasingly worried voices.

> "...Wake."

> "...Please?"

> "...Sorry."

> "...Safe?"

One Mart repeatedly adjusted its own tiny top hat in apparent nervousness.

---

# **MEDICAL ARRIVAL**

**02:18:24**

KCA Medical and Security personnel arrived.

A medic quickly assessed Suspect #4.

After checking for injuries, they announced:

> "...He's alive."

> "...He fainted."

The surrounding Marts collectively froze.

One cautiously asked:

> "...Sleep?"

The medic nodded.

> "...Basically."

The colony visibly relaxed.

Several Marts immediately sat beside the unconscious suspect until he regained consciousness several minutes later.

---

# **WHEN THE SUSPECT AWOKE**

Suspect #4 slowly opened his eyes.

The first thing he saw was approximately twenty Marts staring directly at him.

One leaned forward.

> "...Okay?"

The suspect stared in complete silence.

Then quietly answered:

> "...I... think so."

The Marts collectively replied:

> "Happy!"

---

# **CASUALTIES**

**KCA Personnel**

- None.
    

**Civilians**

- None.
    

**Marts**

- None.
    

**Suspects**

- Bruising.
    
- Minor sprains.
    
- One temporary loss of consciousness due to compression beneath a large number of solidified Marts.
    

Medical evaluation confirmed no permanent injuries.

# **CASE STATUS**

All four suspects were apprehended without lethal force.

The attempted robbery failed before the vault door was touched.

The Marts resumed normal activity shortly after medical personnel confirmed that Suspect #4 had merely fainted.

The first thing the suspect received after regaining consciousness was not another barrage of blue rocks.

It was a sugary cheese cracker offered by a very apologetic Mart.

The suspect accepted it.

**End of Incident Report**
