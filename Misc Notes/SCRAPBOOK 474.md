### **SAFEHOUSE / PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION LOG**

# **"MOM HATES MY BODY, DOESN'T SHE?"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Sensitive Family Record  
**Subjects:** Corin / Orphie / Lumi  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Status:** **ONGOING**

---

# **BACKGROUND**

It has been **over five months** since Corin and Orphie last spoke with Lumi.

No direct conversation has occurred between them during that period.

Lumi remains active within the Blacksite, primarily occupied with her responsibilities involving **Arcturus, Norma, and the Marts**.

Corin understands that Lumi is busy.

Understanding it intellectually has not prevented the absence from hurting.

---

# **THE QUESTION**

Late one evening, Corin sat beside the window of the treehouse.

Her red wool beanie was pulled low.

Her scarf covered the scar around her neck.

Her stitches were concealed beneath her coat.

Orphie sat nearby.

After several minutes of silence, Corin spoke.

> **Corin:**  
> "...Lumi hates my body, isn't she?"

Orphie looked at her.

Corin continued before she could answer.

> **"That's why she doesn't come here."**

> **"She probably looks at me and sees..."**

She stopped.

Her hand tightened around the edge of her scarf.

> **"...this."**

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

Orphie immediately shook her head.

> **Orphie:**  
> "No."

Corin looked at her.

> **"You don't know that."**

Orphie paused.

She couldn't honestly claim to know what Lumi thought.

But she knew something else.

> **Orphie:**  
> "I know Mom."

> **"She wouldn't hate you because of what happened to your body."**

Corin remained quiet.

> **"Then why hasn't she come?"**

Orphie had no answer.

That was the part that hurt.

---

# **CORIN'S FEAR**

Corin looked down at her hands.

They were pale.

Still.

Cold.

Held together by stitches beneath her sleeves.

> **"Maybe she wanted her daughter back."**

> **"And this isn't her daughter anymore."**

Orphie moved closer.

> **"You're still Corin."**

Corin's eyes remained lowered.

> **"...My body isn't."**

Orphie answered:

> **"Then Mom needs to learn to see you."**

Corin looked at her.

> **"Not decide whether you're worth seeing."**

---

# **SILENCE**

Neither spoke for several minutes.

The forest outside remained completely still.

Eventually, Corin quietly said:

> **"...I miss her."**

Orphie nodded.

> **"I know."**

> **"I want Mom to see me."**

Another pause.

> **"...Even if she hates what she sees."**

Orphie took Corin's hand.

> **"She won't."**

Corin didn't argue.

She simply leaned against her sister.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S OBSERVATION**

Guiding Light was aware of the conversation.

She did not interrupt.

She knew that giving Corin an answer would be easy.

Telling her that Lumi loved her would be easy.

But Corin's uncertainty came from something deeper than a lack of information.

It came from five months of silence.

And no celestial explanation could make five months of waiting feel shorter.

Guiding Light's private note read:

> **"She isn't really asking whether Lumi hates her body."**
> 
> **"She's asking whether her mother still sees her as her daughter."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's fear is not really about her scars.

It is about what she believes those scars mean.

She sees a body that was taken apart, reconstructed, and left permanently changed.

She fears that Lumi sees the same thing.

A corpse.

A failed experiment.

Someone who came back wrong.

Someone who is no longer the daughter she remembers.

But Corin is still waiting.

Five months of silence have not erased that.

She still wants her mother.

And somewhere beneath the scarf, the stitches, the Ether, and the coldness of her reanimated body is the same question she has been carrying for months:

> **"Does Mom still see me as her daughter?"**

**End of SAFEHOUSE / Psychological Observation Log**
