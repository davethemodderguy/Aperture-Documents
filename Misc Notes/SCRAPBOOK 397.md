### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION LOG**

# **ORPHIE — EMOTIONAL ABSENCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Concern  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Former Name:** Corey  
**Associated Forms:** Luna / Solaria  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE / Remote Treehouse  
**Status:** **ACTIVE CONCERN**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie's current emotional state continues to concern nearly everyone who knows her.

The problem is not anger.

It is not hostility.

It is not instability.

It is the apparent **absence of emotion altogether**.

Orphie remains functional and cooperative.

She eats.

She works.

She speaks.

She sleeps when she can.

She responds when spoken to.

She can perform ordinary tasks.

But there is almost nothing behind her responses.

---

# **OBSERVED BEHAVIOR**

Orphie rarely displays:

- Happiness.
    
- Sadness.
    
- Anger.
    
- Excitement.
    
- Fear.
    
- Affection.
    
- Anticipation.
    
- Grief.
    

Her voice remains mostly flat.

Her facial expressions rarely change.

Even events that previously would have produced an immediate emotional response now receive little more than:

> "...Okay."

---

# **THE MOST CONCERNING PART**

Orphie does not appear distressed by her own emotional absence.

When asked whether she misses feeling things:

> "...No."

When asked whether she wants her emotions back:

> "...I don't know."

When asked whether she is unhappy:

> "...I don't feel unhappy."

This distinction has made the SAFEHOUSE residents increasingly uncomfortable.

She does not appear to be suppressing an obvious emotional reaction.

She appears to genuinely feel nothing.

---

# **MARTS**

The Marts are once again able to remain around Orphie.

They have returned to their usual behavior.

Marts sit on her shoulders.

They sleep beside her.

They follow her.

They occasionally become blankets.

Orphie permits all of it.

However, she rarely reacts.

A Mart may climb onto her head.

Previously, Corey would immediately worry about its top hat.

Now:

> "...Hello."

The Mart:

> "...Mart."

And that is all.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin remains one of the most concerned people.

The change is particularly obvious because she knew Corey before the emotional collapse.

Corin remembers:

- The jokes.
    
- The cooking.
    
- The drawings.
    
- The hats.
    
- The nightmares.
    
- The Marts.
    
- The quiet moments together.
    

She knows exactly how different Orphie is now.

One evening, Corin asked:

> "...Do you still care about me?"

Orphie answered:

> "...You're important to me."

Corin:

> "...That's not what I asked."

Orphie remained silent.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The residents have collectively become more attentive toward Orphie.

### **Bao**

> "...I don't like seeing her like this."

### **Frost**

> "...She's too quiet."

### **Astral**

> "...Emotional absence should not be mistaken for recovery."

### **Taiga**

> "...I want Corey back."

She was corrected:

> "Orphie."

Taiga paused.

> "...I want Orphie back."

---

### **Star**

Star remains unusually gentle around her.

> "...You don't have to pretend you're okay."

Orphie:

> "...I'm not pretending."

Star had no response.

---

### **Nightshade**

Nightshade rarely presses Orphie for conversation.

She simply remains nearby.

---

### **Belle**

Belle continues trying to make Orphie smile.

It has not worked.

She keeps trying anyway.

---

### **Endmin**

> "...She's still in there."

---

### **Niko**

Niko has become noticeably more cautious around her.

Not because Orphie is dangerous.

Because he does not know what she is feeling.

---

### **Neko**

Neko has been quietly monitoring her condition.

He has avoided treating her like a problem to be engineered away.

---

### **Miss Neko**

Miss Neko summarized the situation simply:

> "...She's alive."

Pause.

> "...But that's not the same as being okay."

---

# **LUNA'S CONCERN**

Luna remains emotionally intact.

When asked about Orphie, she answered:

> "...She used to feel everything."

> "...Now she feels nothing."

Luna's expression became visibly worried.

> "...I don't think that's peace."

---

# **SOLARIA'S CONCERN**

Solaria expressed similar concern.

> "...She isn't calm."

> "...She's empty."

She paused.

> "...There's a difference."

---

# **THE IMPORTANT DISTINCTION**

Orphie's current name does not explain her condition.

**Orphie is Corey.**

She simply changed her name.

There was no transformation of identity.

No new personality was created.

No separate individual appeared.

The emotional absence is a consequence of the events preceding this state, not the name change.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Physical condition:** Stable.

**Cooperation:** Normal.

**Hostility:** None observed.

**Emotional expression:** Extremely limited.

**Social interaction:** Functional but detached.

**Marts:** Safely present.

**Corin:** Highly concerned.

**SAFEHOUSE:** Increasingly concerned.

**Luna:** Concerned.

**Solaria:** Concerned.

**Overall welfare concern:** **SEVERE.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Everyone had once worried because Orphie was suffering.

Then they worried because she could not stop crying.

Then because she could not sleep.

Then because she stopped drawing.

Then because she stopped wanting to be herself.

Now there are no tears.

No nightmares spoken aloud.

No anger.

No sadness.

No happiness.

Nothing.

And somehow, that frightens everyone more than anything that came before.

The Marts can finally approach her again.

They sit beside her.

They sleep on her.

They follow her through the halls.

Orphie allows them to.

She simply does not smile.

**End of Personnel Welfare / SAFEHOUSE Observation Log**
