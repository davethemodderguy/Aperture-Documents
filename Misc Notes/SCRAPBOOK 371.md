# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL OBSERVATION REPORT**

# **COREY — NOCTURNAL LUNAR MANIFESTATION**

**Classification:** Level 4 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corey / Luna

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has documented a recurring phenomenon involving Corey's sleeping state.

Unlike during normal activities, Corey does not wear her usual headwear while asleep.

As a result, nighttime exposure frequently causes Corey to naturally transition into her lunar form:

> **Luna**

This occurrence is considered harmless and is now recognized as part of Corey's natural cycle.

---

# **SLEEPING STATE**

During sleep, Corey is unable to consciously maintain her usual separation between her normal state and her manifested forms.

At night, moonlight can reach her uncovered head, causing a gradual transition.

Common indicators include:

- Soft azure glow.
    
- Reduced surrounding temperature.
    
- Lunar energy presence.
    
- Formation of lunar wings.
    
- Increased calmness in the surrounding area.
    

The transformation does not wake Corey.

---

# **LUNA DURING SLEEP**

When Corey sleeps as Luna, her behavior remains identical to normal sleep.

She does not appear more aware.

She does not communicate.

She simply rests.

However, Luna's lunar energy causes one additional effect:

> **Corey begins to float.**

---

# **LUNAR LEVITATION**

While sleeping in Luna form, Corey naturally levitates.

The effect is similar to Dialyn's own floating sleeping behavior.

Observed characteristics:

- Slow movement.
    
- Gentle hovering.
    
- Stable position.
    
- No signs of discomfort.
    
- No conscious control required.
    

Corey often remains several centimeters above the bed or sleeping surface.

In some cases, she slowly drifts around the room.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION**

Residents of Project SAFEHOUSE have become accustomed to finding Corey asleep in unusual locations.

Examples include:

- Floating above her bed.
    
- Resting near a window beneath moonlight.
    
- Slowly drifting through the room while surrounded by Marts.
    
- Hovering above blankets.
    

The Marts have adapted accordingly.

If Corey begins drifting, they often gently follow beneath her.

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

The Marts do not appear concerned by Corey's nocturnal transformations.

Their typical response is:

> "...Luna."

Followed by:

> "...Sleep."

Many Marts choose to sleep nearby during these events.

Some have been observed floating alongside her due to their own natural abilities.

---

# **CORIN'S COMMENT**

Corin was initially concerned after discovering Corey floating above her bed.

After several repeated occurrences, her reaction changed.

First night:

> "...Corey is floating."

Later nights:

> "...She's Luna again."

Eventually:

> "...At least she's not stuck under the blanket."

---

# **NEKO'S COMMENT**

Neko inspected the phenomenon once.

After observing the stable lunar energy field, he concluded:

> "...Safe."

He then added:

> "...Useful."

When asked what he meant, he answered:

> "...No lights needed."

---

# **DIALYN COMPARISON**

Researchers have noted similarities between Corey/Luna and Dialyn's sleeping behavior.

Both exhibit:

- Natural levitation during rest.
    
- Reduced awareness while floating.
    
- Calm environmental effects.
    
- A tendency to make observers quietly accept the unusual.
    

The primary difference:

Dialyn floats naturally.

Corey floats due to her lunar manifestation.

---

# **COREY'S RESPONSE**

When informed that she frequently becomes Luna while sleeping, Corey was initially embarrassed.

> "...I can't control it while asleep."

After learning that the Marts simply stay nearby, she became less concerned.

> "...They don't mind?"

A nearby Mart answered:

> "...No."

> "...Sleep."

Corey smiled.

> "...Okay."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corey's hats serve as her choice during waking hours.

When she sleeps, however, the sky decides.

Under sunlight, Corey may become Solaria.

Under moonlight, Corey may become Luna.

And during quiet nights at the Blacksite, residents have become accustomed to seeing a familiar sight:

A young mage peacefully sleeping.

Soft blue light filling the room.

Lunar wings gently moving.

Marts resting nearby.

And Corey slowly floating beneath the moon.

**End of Personnel Observation Report**
