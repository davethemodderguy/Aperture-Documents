# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **CENTRAL EXECUTION DEPARTMENT**

### **COMBAT ANALYSIS ADDENDUM – CINDER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Personnel Only

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following review of multiple combat recordings spanning several years, the Central Execution Department has formally concluded that previous observations regarding Cinder's close-quarters behavior are accurate.

The working theory has been **confirmed**.

---

## **CONCLUSION**

When Cinder is forced into prolonged hand-to-hand combat, particularly after losing access to her assigned KCA briefcase, she exhibits a marked behavioral shift.

The transition is characterized by:

- Significantly increased aggression.
    
- Reduced concern for personal safety.
    
- Extreme determination to eliminate perceived threats.
    
- Preference for overwhelming force and improvised weapons.
    
- Temporary disregard for pain and fatigue while engaged.
    

Medical staff describe the state as a **partial combat-induced psychological loss of restraint** rather than complete loss of awareness.

Cinder remains capable of distinguishing allies from hostiles.

However, her methods become considerably more violent.

---

## **OBSERVED COMBAT CHARACTERISTICS**

Archived footage documents the use of:

- Heavy uppercuts.
    
- Kickboxing combinations.
    
- Elbow strikes.
    
- Knee strikes.
    
- Dropkicks.
    
- Joint destruction.
    
- Environmental impacts using walls, floors, vehicles and structural supports.
    
- Repeated strikes until the hostile is no longer capable of continuing the engagement.
    

Combat analysts noted that once the behavioral shift occurs, Cinder prioritizes ending the threat as quickly as possible over maintaining conventional restraint.

---

## **LIMITATIONS**

Despite the increased aggression, Cinder has consistently demonstrated:

- No attacks against allies.
    
- No friendly-fire incidents during the documented episodes.
    
- Continued recognition of commands prior to entering the state.
    

One notable operational limitation remains.

If instructed to capture a hostile alive **after** the behavioral shift has begun, compliance is considered unlikely.

Commanders are advised to issue capture orders before engagement whenever possible.

---

## **TACTICAL RECOMMENDATIONS**

Whenever operationally feasible:

- Ensure Cinder retains access to her assigned KCA briefcase.
    
- Avoid unnecessary disarmament.
    
- Allow Keith to carry the briefcase only when tactically required and with prior coordination.
    
- If capture of enemy personnel is a mission objective, assign another operator to secure prisoners.
    

---

## **COMMANDER'S NOTE**

The report emphasizes that Cinder does **not** seek violence in everyday life.

Outside combat she is consistently described as polite, intelligent, approachable, and happiest while reading, working with Belle, or spending time with friends.

The documented behavioral shift is specific to high-intensity combat situations where she perceives a threat requiring immediate elimination.
