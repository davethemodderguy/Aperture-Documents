# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – ANOMALOUS SPECIES ADDENDUM**

### **DESIGNATION: M.A.R.T. BLOBS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Friendly Anomalous Organism

---

# **NOMENCLATURE NOTICE**

Personnel are reminded that the following are **not** the same individual.

## **Mart**

- Owl Thiren.
    
- Son of Astral.
    
- Permanent SAFEHOUSE resident.
    

## **Marts**

- Friendly blue blob-like organisms.
    
- Created accidentally by Lumi during Ice Mage research.
    
- Semi-aquatic Ether lifeforms.
    
- Collective species name.
    

To reduce confusion, Research Division refers to the organisms collectively as **"Marts"**, while the SAFEHOUSE resident is simply **"Mart."**

Despite this distinction, misunderstandings remain common.

---

# **MAGICAL CONTAINMENT FIELD**

Although Marts are primarily composed of water, they do **not** leave surrounding objects wet during normal interaction.

Research has identified the cause as a naturally occurring magical containment field surrounding each organism.

The field:

- Retains nearly all liquid within the Mart's body.
    
- Prevents uncontrolled dripping.
    
- Maintains the creature's shape.
    
- Stabilizes its internal temperature.
    

As a result, Marts can safely rest on most surfaces without causing water damage.

---

# **ELECTRONIC COMPATIBILITY**

One of the most surprising consequences of the containment field is that Marts can interact with electronic equipment without harming it.

Documented examples include:

- Sleeping on laptop keyboards.
    
- Resting atop desktop computers.
    
- Sitting on server racks.
    
- Curling up beside tablets.
    
- Napping on charging docks.
    

Following their departure, the electronics remain:

- Completely dry.
    
- Fully functional.
    
- Noticeably cooler than before.
    

Engineering has repeatedly confirmed that no moisture enters the devices.

---

# **COOLING EFFECT**

Because each Mart maintains a naturally low internal temperature, they function as small, living cooling systems.

Their presence can slightly reduce the operating temperature of nearby equipment without producing condensation.

Engineering personnel have jokingly referred to them as:

> "Self-propelled liquid cooling."

The nickname has not been adopted officially.

Mostly because the Marts become excited whenever someone says it.

---

# **REPRODUCTION**

Marts reproduce asexually.

Approximately every **three weeks**, a healthy Mart will divide into two complete individuals.

The process is painless and typically lasts only a few minutes.

Observed sequence:

1. The Mart becomes slightly larger over several days.
    
2. It develops a visible seam.
    
3. The body gently separates.
    
4. Two fully formed Marts bounce away.
    

Neither offspring displays signs of distress.

Both immediately begin introducing themselves as:

> "Mart!"

---

# **POPULATION MANAGEMENT**

Despite continuous population growth, no overpopulation issues have been observed.

Many newly divided Marts naturally disperse throughout the Blacksite, helping with maintenance, gardening, and other routine activities.

Others remain near Lumi, Frost, or SAFEHOUSE.

Research suspects an instinctive distribution mechanism but has not confirmed one.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Marts continue to thrive throughout Aperture Industries.

They have become a familiar sight in offices, laboratories, workshops, gardens, and SAFEHOUSE, quietly assisting wherever they can.

Engineering appreciates their cooling effect.

Medical appreciates their harmless nature.

Psychological Services appreciates how often they cheer people up.

The only department still filing regular complaints is Administration.

Not because the Marts cause problems.

But because attendance records occasionally list:

> **Mart**

When the employee present was actually:

> **Thirty-seven Marts.**
