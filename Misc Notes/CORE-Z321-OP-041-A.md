## **Initial Contact**

At 02:17 local time, the joint task force arrived at the Banlands Watchdogs' fortified headquarters.

The compound consisted of multiple warehouses, defensive barricades, and observation towers surrounding the central administration building.

Thermal scans confirmed dozens of armed personnel.

Belle activated her communications headset.

> **"Krampus Compliance Authority."**

> **"This facility is surrounded."**

> **"Lay down your weapons and surrender peacefully."**

For several seconds...

Nothing.

Then a rifle shot rang out.

The round struck the armored transport.

Belle sighed.

> **"...Negotiations have concluded."**

---

## **Breach**

Endmin immediately took command.

> **"Lucius, east flank."

> **"Belle, front gate."

> **"Move."**

Lucius disappeared into the surrounding structures, using cover to approach the eastern side of the compound.

Endmin advanced toward an elevated firing position.

Belle...

Walked directly toward the main gate.

---

## **Main Gate**

Several Watchdogs opened fire.

Belle accelerated into a sprint.

Her rabbit legs became little more than a blur.

Rounds struck the pavement behind her as she rapidly changed direction.

Within seconds she reached the gate.

Her sword came off her back.

One powerful swing—

The reinforced locking mechanism shattered.

The gate burst inward.

Belle entered the compound.

---

## **Eastern Flank**

Lucius moved silently between abandoned vehicles and supply containers.

Drawing his twin prototype pistols, he engaged armed Watchdog personnel who attempted to reinforce the gate.

His accurate fire forced multiple gunmen to retreat deeper into the compound.

Rather than chasing them into exposed positions, Lucius maintained cover and continued securing escape routes for civilians and noncombatants.

---

## **Overwatch**

From an elevated position overlooking the compound, Endmin provided precision support.

Using her sniper rifle, she disabled defensive emplacements, surveillance equipment, and hostile firing positions threatening Belle's advance.

When several armed combatants attempted to regroup behind heavy cover, Endmin switched to her underbarrel shotgun, breaching their position and forcing their surrender.

---

## **Compound Interior**

Belle advanced through the headquarters with remarkable speed.

Whenever armed resistance appeared, she used her sword to disarm opponents, break through barricades, or create openings for advancing officers.

KCA personnel followed behind, securing suspects and collecting evidence from offices, laboratories, and storage rooms.

---

## **Etheric Contraband Laboratory**

The task force reached the underground laboratory.

Inside were numerous containers marked with Ether hazard warnings.

Research notes, experimental apparatus, and confiscated Etheric Contrabands were recovered.

Endmin silently examined one workstation.

Several documents described attempts to artificially reproduce Ether-sensitive perception.

She closed the folder.

> **"...Package everything."

> **"Forensics first."

> **"Nothing leaves this room undocumented."**

KCA forensic teams immediately began cataloging evidence before preparing the contraband for destruction under hazardous-material procedures.

---

## **Operation Status**

Within approximately one hour, organized resistance inside the headquarters collapsed.

The Banlands Watchdogs' command structure was dismantled.

Large quantities of illegal Etheric Contrabands were seized.

Research materials connected to the illegal experiments were secured for evidentiary purposes.

Additional arrests were carried out based on recovered records identifying participants in the corruption network.
