# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT ADDENDUM**

## **SUBJECT: COREY LOCKWOOD – POST-RECOVERY AMNESIA**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – Executive & Medical Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

At 09:26 on ██/██/20██, Corey Lockwood regained consciousness following prolonged hospitalization.

Initial neurological examinations were encouraging.

She recognized familiar faces.

She was able to communicate normally.

Motor function was intact.

No evidence of permanent neurological injury was immediately observed.

However, subsequent interviews revealed a significant memory gap surrounding the events leading to her disappearance.

---

# **MEMORY LOSS**

Corey retains memories from before the night she left the treehouse.

She also retains memories beginning shortly after awakening in the Medical Wing.

The intervening period is absent.

Repeated interviews produced the same response.

> "...What happened?"

Medical Services concluded that Corey has no conscious recollection of the circumstances surrounding her disappearance.

No attempt has been made to forcibly recover those memories.

---

# **BEHAVIOR AFTER AWAKENING**

Within hours of regaining consciousness, Corey resumed her usual demeanor.

She smiled.

She joked with nursing staff.

She apologized for "making everyone worry."

She immediately asked whether Corin had been eating properly.

Personnel unfamiliar with the incident would have found nothing unusual.

Those who had participated in the search found the contrast deeply unsettling.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin was present when Corey regained consciousness.

Medical staff observed an immediate improvement in Corin's previously declining vital signs.

Within twenty-four hours:

- Physiological instability diminished.
    
- Body temperature normalized.
    
- Cardiovascular anomalies subsided.
    
- Appetite partially returned.
    
- Sleep resumed intermittently.
    

The Corin Paradox appeared to stabilize once more.

Despite this physical recovery, Corin's emotional state remained subdued.

She answered Corey's questions gently, but never discussed the missing night.

---

# **CONFIDENTIAL OBSERVATION**

The details surrounding Corey's disappearance remain restricted to personnel with Level 10 clearance.

Corin and the individuals directly involved in the search possess knowledge of what Corey intended on the night she vanished.

Corey herself does not.

Psychological Services unanimously recommend against attempting to restore those memories without compelling medical justification.

The recommendation states:

> "The objective is not to erase the past."

> "It is to avoid reopening a wound that has only just begun to close."

---

# **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

**Lumi**

When Corey apologized for worrying everyone, Lumi simply embraced her.

She did not mention the search.

She did not mention the hospital.

She only replied:

> "I'm just glad you're here."

---

**Star**

Star later remarked:

> "Seeing her smile again..."

She paused.

> "...I think that's enough for today."

---

**Nightshade**

> "Some memories protect."

> "Others only injure."

She offered no further explanation.

---

**Neko**

After reviewing Corey's scans, Neko closed the report.

> "...The paradox repaired itself."

A brief pause.

> "...Let's not test it again."

---

**Miss Neko**

She approved Psychological Services' recommendation without amendment.

---

**Corin**

When asked privately whether Corey should be told everything, Corin remained silent for several moments.

She eventually shook her head.

> "...If she remembers on her own..."

Another pause.

> "...I'll be there."

> "...But I won't make her remember."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corey Lockwood has returned to her normal daily routine.

She continues working alongside Corin at the Krampus Compliance Authority.

She visits Project SAFEHOUSE regularly.

She laughs, smiles, and encourages those around her as she always has.

Only a small number of personnel know how close the Blacksite came to losing her.

They have chosen not to burden Corey with memories she no longer carries.

Instead, they quietly appreciate every ordinary conversation, every shared meal, and every cheerful greeting.

For them, seeing Corey happy again is not something to take for granted.

It is something worth protecting.

**End of Psychological Incident Addendum**
