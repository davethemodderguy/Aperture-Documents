# CUSTOMIZATION NOTICE

**NS-008 — NIGHT STALKER**

has received its authorized personalization.

Owner:

> **DON**

Design theme:

> **MAFIA**

The customization was personally approved and applied by **Neri Tachyon** as part of the closed Night Stalker personalization program.

No additional Night Stalker was produced.

No replacement unit was issued.

NS-008 remains the same authorized weapon originally awarded to Don.

Only its appearance and personalized identification were changed.

---

# 1. DESIGN BRIEF

Neri requested that Don provide a theme.

Don provided one answer:

> **“Mafia.”**

Neri apparently required no further explanation.

The resulting design follows a deliberately old-fashioned organized-crime aesthetic.

The overall appearance emphasizes:

- dark, formal styling;
    
- understated ornamentation;
    
- vintage criminal-underworld imagery;
    
- elegant engraving;
    
- restrained decorative detailing;
    
- and the visual impression of something that would be more appropriate in a 1920s private office than a modern MSAC armory.
    

The result is considerably less visually aggressive than Elsher's space-themed Night Stalker.

It is, however, considerably more intimidating when placed beside a suit.

---

# 2. IDENTIFICATION

The standard Tachyon Industries engraving remains.

Neri did not remove it.

Don's personalized identification has been incorporated into the decorative treatment.

The weapon therefore retains the unmistakable designation:

> **TACHYON INDUSTRIES**

while also making it extremely obvious whose Night Stalker it is.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

NS-008's customization is considered successful.

The Night Stalker now reflects its owner's personality rather than appearing as a generic member of the Tachyon series.

This is consistent with Neri's original objective:

> **Every owner gets their own.**

The Night Stalker program remains permanently closed at **10/10 units.**

No additional Night Stalkers will be produced.

NS-008 remains Don's authorized weapon.
