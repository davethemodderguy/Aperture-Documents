# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neri Tachyon has produced another piece of **Tachyon Industries “Limited Merchandise.”**

Designation:

> **HELL FIRE**

The Hell Fire is a fictional experimental shotgun platform developed by Neri following her recent Armory research involving several existing shotgun systems and raw magnesium.

Only:

> **15 units**

will exist.

There will be no mass-production run.

There will be no standard procurement program.

There will be no additional units once all fifteen have been awarded.

As with the Night Stalker and Sleight of Hand programs:

> **You have to earn it.**

---

# 2. PLATFORM

The Hell Fire is built around a compact **bullpup, single-tube shotgun configuration**.

Recorded characteristics include:

- 12-gauge chambering;
    
- **8+1 capacity**;
    
- integral suppressed barrel assembly;
    
- mounted bayonet;
    
- EOTech holographic sight;
    
- foldable buffer-tube configuration;
    
- Magpul CDR stock as standard equipment.
    

The resulting platform is compact despite its unusually specialized ammunition.

C.O.R.E. has classified the Hell Fire as an:

> **Experimental Tachyon Industries Limited Armament.**

It is not an ordinary MSAC shotgun.

---

# 3. THE AMMUNITION

The Hell Fire's defining feature is not the shotgun itself.

It is the ammunition.

### **12 GAUGE MAGNESIUM MAGNUM — “HELLFIRE”**

The ammunition is an experimental magnesium-based incendiary/flechette-type shell developed specifically for the Hell Fire platform.

Unlike conventional Dragon's Breath ammunition, which produces a short-lived incendiary effect, Hellfire ammunition produces a fundamentally different phenomenon.

Upon firing, the shell releases numerous **diamond-shaped magnesium projectiles** surrounded by an intensely bright white combustion effect.

The projectiles behave more like miniature incendiary darts than conventional shot.

---

# 4. THE FIRE

The resulting combustion is extraordinary.

Observed characteristics include:

> **Bright white flame**

> **Extreme thermal output**

> **Prolonged combustion**

> **Severe energy release**

Under controlled fictional test conditions, individual Hellfire effects have remained active for:

> **More than five hours.**

Recorded peak temperatures have approached:

> **5,000°C**

The thermal output is so extreme that C.O.R.E. considers the ammunition unsuitable for conventional firearm platforms.

The Hell Fire itself is specifically designed around this fictional extreme operating envelope.

---

# 5. ENERGY OUTPUT

One of the more absurd properties of the Hellfire reaction is its total energy release.

C.O.R.E. measurements indicate that a sustained Hellfire event can produce enough recoverable energy to theoretically supply the electrical demand of a **small apartment complex**.

This has resulted in an unusual situation where the Armaments Division and Energy Research Division have both expressed interest in the same ammunition.

The Energy Division has been informed:

> **It is ammunition.**

Not a power plant.

---

# 6. PLATFORM COMPATIBILITY

The Hellfire ammunition is **not compatible with ordinary shotguns.**

This is an intentional design limitation.

The original Hell Fire platform is the only authorized firearm capable of handling the extreme fictional operating conditions generated by the ammunition.

Attempting to fire a Hellfire shell from another shotgun results in catastrophic platform failure.

In testing and simulation, an incompatible shotgun does not merely malfunction.

It undergoes a violent destructive failure comparable in effect to a dynamite-like explosion.

Consequently:

> **HELLFIRE AMMUNITION IS HELL FIRE PLATFORM ONLY.**

No exceptions.

---

# 7. C.O.R.E. SAFETY CLASSIFICATION

Hellfire ammunition has been classified:

> **EXTREME HAZARD — PLATFORM RESTRICTED**

The following are prohibited:

- unauthorized handling;
    
- unauthorized firing;
    
- compatibility testing;
    
- attempts to use Hellfire ammunition in other firearms;
    
- attempts to reproduce the ammunition;
    
- unauthorized disassembly;
    
- unauthorized modification.
    

No technical manufacturing information has been added to the general Blacksite database.

Neri's research documentation remains restricted.

---

Nothing else.
