## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **ARCHITECTAL PERSONNEL RECORD**

# **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT — BLACKSITE VISITATION PROFILE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Entity:** Mischevious Light  
**Role:** Architectal Enforcer  
**Visit Indicator:** **Spiral Symbol / Red Luminescence**  
**Standard Equipment:** **Sword**  
**Purpose of Weapon:** **"Intimidation."**  
**Status:** **ACTIVE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Mischevious Light is the youngest member of the known Architectal trio.

Unlike his name suggests, his primary function is not mischief.

He is an **enforcer**.

His duties frequently take him away from the Blacksite, resulting in relatively infrequent visits to Corin and Orphie.

When he does appear at the Blacksite, personnel generally know before he says anything.

A distinctive **spiral symbol** manifests nearby.

A faint **red glow** accompanies it.

The combination has become his unofficial arrival signature.

---

# **THE SPIRAL**

The spiral symbol is not a warning alarm.

It is simply an indication that Mischevious Light is present.

Security personnel have learned to recognize it immediately.

The usual sequence is:

**Spiral appears.**

**Red light follows.**

**Mischevious Light arrives.**

Nobody has determined whether the symbol serves an actual practical purpose.

When asked, Mischevious Light simply answered:

> **"It looks cool."**

C.O.R.E. has accepted this explanation.

---

# **THE RED GLOW**

Unlike Guiding Light's softer blue-white illumination, Mischevious Light manifests with a distinct red radiance.

The glow is usually strongest around his silhouette and the spiral symbol.

It does not inherently indicate aggression.

It is simply his natural Architectal signature.

Nevertheless, junior personnel tend to become considerably more nervous when they see it.

Mischevious Light finds this amusing.

---

# **THE SWORD**

Mischevious Light carries a sword.

It is not considered necessary for his survival.

It is not his primary source of power.

It is not required for him to enforce Architectal authority.

In practical terms, he could accomplish his duties without it.

When asked why he carries one:

> **"Intimidation."**

That is the entire explanation.

---

# **SECURITY INTERACTION**

Security personnel are advised not to attempt to confiscate the sword.

Not because Mischevious Light would necessarily object.

But because there is no operational reason to do so.

The sword has never been used improperly inside the Blacksite.

Mischevious Light generally carries it openly while walking through the facility.

He does not threaten personnel.

He does not swing it around.

He simply carries it.

The psychological effect is apparently sufficient.

---

# **THE FIRST RECORDED REACTION**

A junior security officer once saw the spiral symbol appear at the end of a corridor.

The red glow followed.

Mischevious Light stepped through.

The officer immediately reached for his radio.

Mischevious Light stopped.

> **"Relax."**

The officer looked at the sword.

> **"...You're carrying a sword."**

Mischevious Light looked down at it.

> **"Yes."**

> **"Why?"**

A pause.

> **"Intimidation."**

The officer had no further questions.

---

# **CORIN AND ORPHIE**

Mischevious Light's visits to the treehouse are considerably less intimidating.

When he arrives, Corin generally recognizes him by the red glow before he speaks.

Orphie is usually less impressed.

> **Orphie:**  
> "You know you don't need the sword here."

> **Mischevious Light:**  
> "I know."

> **Orphie:**  
> "Then why bring it?"

He points toward the sword.

> **Mischevious Light:**  
> "Intimidation."

Orphie sighs.

Corin quietly laughs.

---

# **JAY**

Jay does not appear particularly intimidated by Mischevious Light.

In fact, Jay has attempted to land on the sword several times.

Guiding Light immediately intervenes whenever this happens.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Jay."

The bird flies away.

Mischevious Light watches.

> **"...He's brave."**

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "He's stupid."

> **Mischevious Light:**  
> "That too."

---

# **VISITATION PROTOCOL**

When the spiral symbol and red glow appear inside the Blacksite:

1. Personnel should remain calm.
    
2. Security should verify Mischevious Light's identity.
    
3. Do not interfere with his movement unless specifically instructed.
    
4. Do not attempt to confiscate his sword.
    
5. Do not assume the red glow indicates hostility.
    
6. Do not encourage Jay to steal the sword.
    
7. Do not ask Mischevious Light why he carries the sword unless prepared to receive the answer:
    
    > **"Intimidation."**
    

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Mischevious Light is an Architect.

He does not need weapons.

He does not need conventional security.

He does not need intimidation.

He carries the sword anyway.

The spiral symbol appears.

The red glow follows.

The sword hangs at his side.

Personnel move aside.

And Mischevious Light walks through the Blacksite as though nothing unusual has happened.

Perhaps, to him, nothing has.

He simply likes the aesthetic.
