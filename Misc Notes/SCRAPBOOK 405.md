### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / ANOMALOUS PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

# **ORPHIE — REJECTION OF EXISTENCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Concern  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Marts / Luna / Solaria  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **CRITICAL CONCERN**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie has made her position regarding her birthday clear.

She does not want anyone celebrating it.

Not because she dislikes birthdays.

Not because she dislikes parties.

Not because she wants a quieter celebration.

She does not want anyone celebrating **her existence**.

To Orphie, the birthday is not a reminder that she was born.

It is a reminder that she is still here.

And she does not believe that being here is something worth celebrating.

---

# **THE REJECTED CELEBRATION**

The SAFEHOUSE had attempted to celebrate quietly.

Orphie refused to attend.

When Corin brought up the celebration, Orphie said:

> "...Don't."

Corin:

> "...It's your birthday."

> "...I know."

> "...We just wanted to—"

> "...Don't celebrate me."

Corin stopped.

Orphie continued:

> "...There's nothing to celebrate."

---

# **THE EMPTY SEAT**

The birthday gathering at SAFEHOUSE was eventually ended.

The decorations were removed.

The cake was put away.

The gifts were collected.

The empty seat was no longer left open.

Not because the SAFEHOUSE had stopped caring.

Because they finally understood that forcing Orphie into a celebration would only make her retreat further.

---

# **CORIN'S ROLE**

Corin has historically been one of the people most capable of reaching Orphie emotionally.

She knew how to make her laugh.

She cooked with her.

She lived with her.

She understood her silences.

She was there during the nightmares.

She was the person Orphie returned home to.

For a long time, Corin could act as a bridge between Orphie and the rest of the world.

That bridge is now failing.

---

# **THE DISTANCE**

The remote treehouse was originally meant to be a peaceful home.

Now it has become a barrier.

The distance from the main Blacksite means:

- SAFEHOUSE residents cannot casually visit.
    
- Orphie does not encounter people naturally.
    
- Corin may be away for most of the day.
    
- Marts cannot compensate for human companionship.
    
- Emotional intervention becomes increasingly difficult.
    

The physical distance has begun reinforcing the emotional distance.

---

# **CORIN'S ATTEMPT**

Corin tried to approach Orphie as she had before.

She sat beside her.

No celebration.

No gifts.

No speeches.

Just company.

> "...I'm here."

Orphie:

> "...I know."

Corin:

> "...That's all."

Orphie looked at her.

> "...You don't have to stay."

Corin:

> "...I know."

She stayed anyway.

---

# **THE PROBLEM**

Corin can no longer simply cheer Orphie up.

The old methods do not work.

Cooking does not work.

Jokes do not work.

Marts do not work.

Old memories do not work.

Birthday celebrations are rejected.

Her favorite hobbies are rejected.

Even objects connected to her mother have been sealed away.

There is increasingly little that reaches her.

---

# **LUNA'S OBSERVATION**

Luna described the situation as:

> "...She's too far away."

The researcher asked whether she meant physically.

Luna shook her head.

> "...Both."

She continued:

> "...She's far away from everyone."

> "...And she's far away from herself."

---

# **SOLARIA'S OBSERVATION**

Solaria was less restrained.

> "...We waited too long."

The researcher asked whether she blamed Corin.

> "...No."

> "...Nobody knew how far she was falling."

Solaria looked toward the forest.

> "...Now she's somewhere we can't reach easily."

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts remain beside Orphie whenever possible.

They are perhaps the only beings capable of providing constant physical companionship without demanding anything from her.

A Mart can sleep beside her for hours.

Another can become a blanket.

Another can simply sit on her shoulder.

Orphie permits them.

But she rarely reacts.

The Marts are beginning to understand that their presence cannot solve everything.

They remain anyway.

---

# **ORPHIE'S BELIEF**

Orphie's reasoning has become increasingly consistent:

> **"I am a burden."**

> **"Everyone has their own lives."**

> **"They would be happier without worrying about me."**

> **"My existence isn't something to celebrate."**

She does not appear angry when expressing these beliefs.

She states them as facts.

That makes them particularly difficult to challenge.

---

# **THE VERSION OF CORIN**

Corin has spent much of her life trying to make Orphie feel better.

She was once the person who could walk through the door after work and immediately change the atmosphere of the treehouse.

Now she sometimes arrives late to find Orphie sitting in darkness.

The difference is devastating.

Corin can still sit beside her.

She can still talk.

She can still stay.

But she cannot force Orphie to feel something.

---

# **THE BIRTHDAY GIFT**

The birthday gift remains unopened.

Corin eventually brought it home.

It sits on the table.

Orphie has not touched it.

She has not asked what is inside.

She has not asked who made it.

It remains there.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Orphie:**  
Severely isolated. Emotionally detached. Rejects celebration of her existence. Believes herself to be a burden. Increasingly inaccessible to emotional support.

**Corin:**  
Still present, but increasingly unable to reach Orphie through previous methods.

**Marts:**  
Remain close. Highly concerned.

**SAFEHOUSE:**  
Concerned but unable to provide constant direct support due to distance.

**Luna:**  
Concerned.

**Solaria:**  
Concerned.

**Overall welfare concern:** **CRITICAL.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Once, Corin could cheer her up.

A meal.

A joke.

A hug.

A Mart.

Sometimes that was enough.

Now Corin can sit beside Orphie for hours and receive almost nothing in return.

The SAFEHOUSE is farther away.

The people who love her are farther away.

Her old happiness is farther away.

And Orphie herself seems farther away than all of them.

Her birthday was supposed to celebrate the fact that she exists.

She refused.

Because in Orphie's mind, existence is not a gift.

It is a burden she believes everyone else has been forced to carry with her.

And for the first time, Corin has realized something terrifying:

She cannot simply cheer Orphie up anymore.

She can only **stay**.

So she stays.

**End of Personnel Welfare / Anomalous Psychological Observation**
