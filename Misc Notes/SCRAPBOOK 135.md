## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CONFIDENTIAL PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – LONG-TERM MENTAL HEALTH STATUS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin Lockwood continues to experience significant psychological consequences stemming from the abuse and experimentation she endured as a child.

Her symptoms have persisted for years and fluctuate in severity.

Despite gradual improvements in her social life within Project SAFEHOUSE, Corin remains psychologically vulnerable and requires ongoing support.

---

# **CURRENT CLINICAL OBSERVATIONS**

Medical and Psychological Services have documented the following persistent concerns:

- Chronic insomnia with frequent nights of little or no sleep.
    
- Major depressive symptoms, including persistent sadness, hopelessness, and diminished enjoyment of activities.
    
- Severe self-hatred centered on her altered body and Ether-dependent condition.
    
- Severe body image disturbance, with Corin perceiving herself as fundamentally "broken" or "inhuman" despite reassurance from those around her.
    
- Episodes of self-injurious behavior during periods of overwhelming emotional distress.
    

Psychological Services emphasize that these behaviors arise from profound trauma and emotional pain rather than a desire for attention.

---

# **PATTERN OF DISTRESS**

Corin's episodes most commonly occur after:

- Intrusive memories of the experiments.
    
- Discovering new manifestations of her Ether abilities.
    
- Encounters that reinforce her negative self-image.
    
- Extended periods of isolation.
    
- Sleep deprivation.
    

During these episodes, Corin's judgment becomes significantly impaired, and she may struggle to recognize her own worth or accept reassurance from others.

---

# **PROTECTIVE FACTORS**

Although Corin frequently withdraws, she is no longer facing her struggles entirely alone.

SAFEHOUSE residents have gradually learned to recognize when she is deteriorating.

Sometimes Belle notices Corin becoming unusually quiet.

Sometimes Norma recognizes the look in her sister's eyes before Corin says a word.

Sometimes Arcturus arrives with no explanation other than,

> "I had a feeling."

Lumi remains one of Corin's strongest sources of comfort, consistently encouraging her to care for herself, take her medication, and remember that she is loved.

On more than one occasion, someone's timely presence has interrupted a moment of overwhelming despair.

No one scolds her afterward.

They stay with her until the crisis passes.

---

# **LUMI'S STATEMENT**

Lumi addressed Medical Services with a simple request.

> "She doesn't need people to tell her she's strong."

> "She already survives more than she thinks she can."

> "She needs people to remind her that she doesn't have to survive by herself."

---

# **MEDICAL RECOMMENDATIONS**

Psychological Services recommend:

- Continued regular therapy and psychiatric follow-up.
    
- Strict adherence to prescribed medication.
    
- Consistent opportunities for ordinary social interaction within Project SAFEHOUSE.
    
- Monitoring during periods of prolonged insomnia or increased withdrawal.
    
- Immediate supportive intervention if Corin shows signs of acute emotional crisis.
    

Staff are reminded that calm, compassionate presence is often more effective than attempting to argue with Corin's negative beliefs in moments of severe distress.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin's recovery is ongoing.

She is not healed.

She still experiences nights where the weight of her past feels overwhelming.

However, she is no longer facing those struggles in complete isolation.

Around her is a family that has learned an important lesson:

Sometimes the most meaningful thing they can do is simply make sure Corin is not left alone when she needs someone most.
