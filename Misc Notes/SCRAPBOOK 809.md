**Classification:** C.O.R.E. INTERNAL

---

## **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

At approximately 02:19, Alice encountered two SPAWN personnel attempting to infiltrate the SAFEHOUSE.

Unlike previous encounters, Alice did not immediately pursue or physically attack the intruders.

Instead, she removed her medical eye patch.

This resulted in the first documented deliberate use of Alice's **right ocular anomaly** against a hostile individual.

The ability appears to involve an azure luminescent eye containing a small spatial rift.

Direct observation produces a pronounced hypnotic effect.

Both SPAWN personnel were incapacitated without physical injury.

---

# **1. RIGHT OCULAR ANOMALY**

Alice's right eye was previously injured during an unrelated incident.

The injury resulted in permanent anomalous alteration.

Current appearance:

- Azure luminescence.
    
- Irregular visual distortion surrounding the iris.
    
- Small rift-like structure visible within the eye.
    
- Rift does not appear to correspond to normal ocular anatomy.
    
- No conventional pupil structure has been consistently observed.
    

The eye is normally covered by a **medical eye patch**.

The patch is not merely intended to conceal the injury.

It functions as a practical containment precaution.

Alice has been advised not to expose the eye unnecessarily.

---

# **2. HYPnotic EFFECT**

Direct visual exposure to the right eye produces an anomalous cognitive effect.

Observed symptoms include:

1. Loss of situational awareness.
    
2. Reduced voluntary movement.
    
3. Difficulty maintaining attention.
    
4. Significant susceptibility to Alice's verbal instructions.
    
5. Hypnotic fixation upon the eye.
    
6. Temporary loss of resistance to suggestion.
    

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

C.O.R.E. researchers have been unable to determine whether the effect originates from:

- The eye itself.
    
- The rift.
    
- The light emitted by the eye.
    
- A cognitohazardous property of the rift.
    
- Or some combination thereof.
    

Testing has therefore been deliberately limited.

Alice herself appears to understand the danger.

This is one reason she normally keeps the eye covered.

---

# **3. SPAWN ENCOUNTER**

Two SPAWN infiltrators entered the SAFEHOUSE grounds.

Alice initially approached them while wearing her eye patch.

> **SPAWN-01:** "Stay where you are."

Alice stopped.

> **ALICE:** "Why?"

> **SPAWN-01:** "We're looking for someone."

Alice tilted her head.

> **ALICE:** "You're not supposed to be here."

> **SPAWN-02:** "Put the axe down."

Alice looked at them.

Then at her axe.

Then back at them.

> **ALICE:** "No."

The first operative raised his weapon.

Alice reached up to her face.

And removed the eye patch.

---

# **4. VISUAL RECORD**

Security footage became severely distorted immediately after exposure.

The following description was reconstructed from multiple cameras.

Alice's right eye began emitting an intense azure glow.

A small rift became visible inside the eye.

The surrounding area appeared to warp slightly.

Both SPAWN personnel stopped moving.

Neither attempted to fire.

Alice walked toward them.

Neither reacted.

She reportedly asked:

> **"Are you going to hurt anyone?"**

No response.

Alice waited.

Then:

> **"No?"**

Both operatives nodded.

Alice replaced the eye patch.

Both individuals immediately collapsed into a seated position.

They remained conscious but displayed severely impaired responsiveness.

---

# **5. POST-INCIDENT INTERROGATION**

The two SPAWN operatives were subsequently recovered.

Neither could provide a coherent account of the encounter.

Their recollections were fragmented.

One repeatedly described Alice's eye as:

> **"A window."**

The other described it as:

> **"A hole where something was looking back."**

Neither could explain what they believed they saw inside the rift.

Both refused to look directly toward Alice during subsequent questioning.

---

# **6. WHY THE EYE PATCH EXISTS**

C.O.R.E. medical personnel have confirmed that Alice's eye patch serves **three purposes**.

### **Medical**

The original injury remains abnormal and requires protection.

### **Safety**

The patch prevents accidental exposure to the eye's anomalous effect.

### **Social**

Alice does not want to inadvertently hypnotize people around her.

This third reason is particularly notable.

Alice has demonstrated that she understands that her eye can affect others.

She therefore keeps it covered unless she believes exposure is necessary.

---

# **7. SECURITY ASSESSMENT**

The discovery substantially changes Alice's threat profile.

Previously, SPAWN intelligence primarily associated Alice with:

> **Battle axe / close-range combat**

This is now considered incomplete.

Alice possesses at least two distinct defensive options:

**Physical:**  
Battle axe.

**Anomalous:**  
Right ocular rift / hypnotic effect.

The latter is particularly concerning because conventional protective equipment may not prevent exposure.

Personnel are therefore instructed:

> **DO NOT REQUEST THAT ALICE REMOVE HER EYE PATCH.**
