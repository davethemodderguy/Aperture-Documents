### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE PROGRESS REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – SOCIAL REINTEGRATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following recommendations from Medical Services and continued encouragement from Lumia Lockwood, Corin Lockwood gradually became more involved in the daily life of Project SAFEHOUSE.

Rather than isolating herself in her office, Corin began spending more time with the residents.

No formal rehabilitation program was introduced.

SAFEHOUSE simply did what it had always done.

It made room for one more person.

---

# **THE FRIENDLY ROOMMATES**

Corin officially joined the daily routine of the SAFEHOUSE residents.

Morning tea.

Movie nights.

Shared meals.

Late-night conversations.

Board games.

Even the occasional engineering discussion that somehow ended with Neko drawing impossible blueprints on the dining table.

For the first time in years...

Corin had roommates.

Not coworkers.

Not doctors.

Not researchers.

Roommates.

---

# **CHANGING FIRST IMPRESSIONS**

As Corin became a familiar face around the Blacksite, personnel who initially found her appearance unsettling began seeing something else.

The quiet accountant who always double-checked everyone's expense reports.

The girl who apologized whenever someone else bumped into her.

The teenager who was more likely to ask if **you** were doing okay than to talk about herself.

Her unusual Ether-dependent physiology never changed.

People's understanding of it did.

The fear that once defined first meetings gradually gave way to simple familiarity.

---

# **EVERYDAY LIFE**

SAFEHOUSE residents quickly discovered that Corin was surprisingly easy to live with.

She kept shared spaces tidy.

She rarely complained.

She quietly made tea whenever she noticed someone else was still awake.

She often fell asleep while reviewing accounting spreadsheets.

Belle eventually started placing a blanket over her whenever that happened.

Corin always woke up confused.

No one admitted who did it.

Everyone knew.

---

# **LUMI'S OBSERVATION**

One evening, Lumi noticed something she had not seen before.

Corin walked into the lounge.

Several residents greeted her.

Not because they wanted something.

Not because of her abilities.

Simply because she had arrived.

Corin paused for a moment.

Then smiled.

It was small.

Almost hesitant.

But it was genuine.

Lumi quietly smiled to herself.

That smile had become more common.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin continues to heal.

She still has difficult nights.

She still carries painful memories.

She still occasionally questions herself.

But she no longer spends every evening alone behind a locked office door.

More people know her.

More people talk to her.

More people wave when they pass her in the hallway.

And with each passing week...

Fewer people are afraid of the girl with the cold hands.

More people simply know her as Corin.
