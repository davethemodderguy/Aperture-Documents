### **Clinical Note**

Reviewing psychologists noted that the journal reflects Belle's private uncertainty about her identity and place within the family during a period of emotional distress.

The entries document her self-doubt, but they are not considered evidence of how others viewed or valued her.

The journal is retained as part of Belle's confidential medical record and is accessible only to authorized medical personnel with her consent.
