# **PROFESSIONAL QUALIFICATIONS**

While officially serving as the **Senior Manager of the Krampus Compliance Authority**, Norma Lockwood has accumulated an unusually broad range of technical certifications and professional experience across multiple disciplines.

Personnel have jokingly referred to her as:

> **"The Jack of All Trades."**

The nickname has become so widespread that even Executive Administration occasionally uses it informally.

---

## **KNOWN PROFESSIONS & SPECIALIZATIONS**

### **Central Computing Department (CCD) Researcher**

Norma periodically collaborates with the CCD on advanced research projects involving computational modeling, materials science, and experimental technologies.

Although not permanently assigned to the department, she is a recognized researcher and consultant.

---

### **Chemist**

Qualified in analytical and industrial chemistry.

Often assists with:

- Material synthesis.
    
- Chemical analysis.
    
- Laboratory troubleshooting.
    
- Safe handling procedures.
    

---

### **Biochemist**

Experienced in biological chemistry and laboratory research.

She has contributed to several interdisciplinary studies alongside both CCD researchers and Medical personnel.

---

### **Blacksmith**

Norma enjoys traditional metalworking outside of her executive responsibilities.

She has handcrafted:

- Utility tools.
    
- Training weapons.
    
- Decorative metalwork.
    
- Reinforced equipment components.
    

She describes forging as "surprisingly relaxing."

---

### **Locksmith**

Certified in advanced lock design, repair, and security mechanisms.

Frequently consulted when engineering secure storage systems or evaluating physical security.

---

### **Engineer**

Perhaps her best-known skill outside management.

Norma is proficient in:

- Mechanical engineering.
    
- Structural design.
    
- Manufacturing.
    
- Prototype fabrication.
    
- Equipment maintenance.
    

Her engineering hobby is one of the main reasons she gets along so well with Neko.

---

### **Senior Manager – Krampus Compliance Authority**

Norma's primary occupation.

Responsibilities include:

- Executive oversight.
    
- Operational coordination.
    
- Department management.
    
- Strategic planning.
    
- Emergency command.
    

Her ability to understand the technical work of nearly every department has made her an exceptionally effective manager.

---

# **REPUTATION**

Norma's broad expertise allows her to communicate naturally with specialists from vastly different fields.

It is not unusual for her to spend the morning reviewing executive budgets, the afternoon assisting in Engineering, and the evening discussing research findings with the CCD.

One researcher summarized her skillset as:

> "If she doesn't already know how to do it..."

> "...Give her an afternoon."
