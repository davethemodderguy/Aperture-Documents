# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-PSY-011**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT REPORT – SOCIAL WITHDRAWAL OF DIRECTOR BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **SUMMARY**

**Location:** Heaven's Blacksite

Following an unidentified psychological trigger associated with memories Belle has repeatedly declined to discuss, Director Belle Merryweather displayed an abrupt change in behavior.

Witnesses reported that she became unusually withdrawn, avoided eye contact, and sought complete isolation.

Her eyes exhibited sustained Ether-related luminescence.

---

## **INITIAL OBSERVATIONS**

Belle quietly left Room N-07 without explanation.

When Endmin attempted to follow, Belle stopped without turning around.

In a trembling voice, she said:

> **"...Don't look in my eyes."

A long silence followed.

Then:

> **"...Leave me alone."

No anger.

No shouting.

Only fear.

Believing Belle wished to process her emotions privately, those present reluctantly respected her request.

This decision would later be recognized as a critical mistake.

---

## **FIVE HOURS**

Belle remained completely alone for approximately **five hours**.

No one entered her room.

No one checked on her.

No one attempted even a brief welfare check.

Each person assumed someone else would eventually go.

No one did.

---

## **DISCOVERY**

When Endmin finally decided that enough time had passed, she quietly knocked on Belle's door.

No response.

She opened it slowly.

Belle was sitting on the floor against the side of her bed.

Her sword rested untouched nearby.

The room was dark.

The curtains remained closed.

The golden Ether lines beneath her fur were still faintly visible.

She had not moved very far from where she had first sat down.

Her eyes were red from crying.

---

## **INTERACTION**

Endmin did not immediately speak.

She simply sat down beside Belle.

After several minutes, Belle whispered:

> **"...I told everyone to leave..."

She paused.

> **"...I didn't think..."

Her voice cracked.

> **"...You actually would."

Endmin's expression fell.

She realized Belle's words had not been a request born from desire.

They had been spoken from shame.

Belle had expected someone to stay anyway.

---

## **FOLLOW-UP**

The others gradually arrived.

Lucius remained near the doorway.

Keith quietly stood outside to provide privacy.

Cinder entered carrying a blanket but said nothing.

Alice performed a brief medical assessment before deciding Belle needed comfort far more than examination.

Eventually, Belle looked around the room.

Her voice was barely audible.

> **"...I was scared..."

No one interrupted.

> **"...I just..."

A tear rolled down her cheek.

> **"...Didn't want anyone to see me like that."

Endmin gently took Belle's hand.

> **"...You don't have to hide from us."

Belle lowered her head.

> **"...I'm trying."

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Alice concluded that Belle's Ether activity had stabilized naturally over time.

The prolonged isolation had not caused additional physiological harm.

However, the emotional impact of remaining alone during a period of acute distress was considered significant.

A recommendation was issued that future incidents involving comparable symptoms should include discreet welfare monitoring, even if Belle requests solitude.

The goal would be to respect her privacy while ensuring she is not left entirely without support.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Endmin

> **"...Next time..."

She squeezed Belle's hand.

> **"...I'll still give you space."

> **"...But I'm staying outside your door."

---

### Lucius

> **"...No one should have to wonder if they'll be forgotten."

---

### Cinder

She carefully wrapped the blanket around Belle's shoulders.

> **"...Being alone..."

She smiled gently.

> **"...Doesn't have to mean being abandoned."

---

### Keith

> **"...Protection isn't always standing in front of someone."

> **"...Sometimes it's simply making sure they're not alone."

---

### Alice

> **"...There's a difference between respecting someone's wishes and ensuring their safety."

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious reviewed the incident and approved a revision to Blacksite wellness guidance.

His concluding note stated:

> **"Personnel experiencing severe emotional distress may request privacy."**

> **"Privacy shall be respected."**

> **"Isolation shall not."**

---

## **STATUS**

Belle recovered after spending the remainder of the evening with her friends and family in Room N-07.

No one attempted to force a conversation about the memories that had resurfaced.

Instead, they simply stayed.

The incident prompted the creation of an informal understanding among the residents of Heaven's Blacksite:

> **If someone says they need space...**

> **Give them space.**

> **But make sure they know someone is still waiting just outside the door.**
