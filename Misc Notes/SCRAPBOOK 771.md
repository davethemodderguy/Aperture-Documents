# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — FACIAL MARKINGS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY  
**Status:** Stable

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Three black star-shaped markings have appeared beneath Alice's right eye.

The markings appeared without warning.

No injury was observed.

No pain was reported.

No change in Alice's behavior was detected.

---

# **APPEARANCE**

The markings consist of three black stars:

- **One large star**
    
- **Two smaller stars**
    

They are positioned beneath Alice's right eye, partially following the natural curve of her cheek.

The markings are solid black.

They do not glow.

They do not move.

They do not appear to emit anomalous energy.

They simply look like small black star tattoos.

---

# **MEDICAL EXAMINATION**

Medical personnel examined the markings.

No significant physiological abnormality was found.

The markings do not appear to be wounds.

They do not bleed.

They do not cause inflammation.

They do not appear to interfere with Alice's vision.

Attempts to determine whether they are technically tattoos, pigmentation, or another anomalous manifestation have produced inconclusive results.

---

# **THE EYEPATCH**
