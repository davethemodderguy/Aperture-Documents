# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / MOUNTAIN RECOVERY INCIDENT**

# **THE EMPTY SHELL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Location:** Mountain Region — Search Perimeter ██  
**Item:** Spent Shotgun Shell  
**Status:** **UNDER FORENSIC EXAMINATION**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

A single **blood-stained shotgun shell** was discovered in the mountains during a routine search for Corin.

Only the spent shell casing was recovered.

No projectile was found.

No wad was recovered.

No propellant remains were detected.

No weapon was located nearby.

Most importantly:

> **There was no remaining anomalous energy.**

The shell is completely inert.

---

# **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

The recovered object is consistent with a previously fired shotgun shell.

Observed characteristics:

- Spent primer.
    
- Deformed casing.
    
- Evidence of discharge.
    
- Blood staining along the exterior.
    
- No intact ammunition components.
    
- No detectable anomalous charge.
    

The blood is undergoing testing.

Preliminary analysis indicates a strong possibility that it originated from **Corin**.

Confirmation remains pending.

---

# **NO PROJECTILE**

Search personnel initially assumed the shell might have been associated with an unknown weapon.

A complete search of the surrounding area produced:

**No slug.**

**No pellets.**

**No fragments.**

**No wad.**

Nothing corresponding to the fired payload has been located.

The terrain has been searched extensively.

---

# **NO WEAPON**

No shotgun was found within the immediate area.

No reliable footprints lead to the recovery site.

No surveillance footage covers the exact location.

There is therefore currently no evidence identifying:

- Who fired the weapon.
    
- What weapon was used.
    
- What the projectile struck.
    
- Why the shell was left behind.
    

---

# **THE BLOOD**

The blood staining is the most significant part of the discovery.

It is not present in sufficient quantity to establish what happened.

However, its composition appears consistent with Corin's known biological profile.

Environmental alteration has also begun affecting the sample.

The outermost material is darkening.

C.O.R.E. has therefore placed the shell into a sealed evidence container.

---

# **SEARCH TEAM RESPONSE**

The discovery immediately changed the nature of the search.

Personnel were instructed to stop treating the surrounding region as ordinary terrain.

The area was searched again.

Nothing else was found.

No body.

No weapon.

No additional blood trail.

No feathers.

No clothing.

No sign of a struggle.

Just the shell.

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

Orphie was informed about the discovery.

She immediately asked:

> **"Is there a body?"**

The answer was:

> **"No."**

She then asked:

> **"Is there anything else?"**

Again:

> **"No."**

Orphie stared at the evidence photograph.

After several seconds:

> **"...Then don't assume the worst."**

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

The shell alone does **not** establish that Corin was shot.

The blood alone does **not** establish how it was deposited.

The absence of a projectile does **not** establish what happened to it.

At present, several possibilities remain open.

### **Possibility 01 — Corin Fired the Weapon**

The blood may have originated from an injury sustained before or during the firing.

### **Possibility 02 — Corin Was Present**

The shell may simply indicate that she was nearby when the weapon was discharged.

### **Possibility 03 — Unrelated Incident**

The shell may have no connection to Corin whatsoever.

### **Possibility 04 — Unknown Anomalous Interaction**

The missing projectile and lack of conventional evidence may indicate an interaction that current instrumentation cannot explain.

None can currently be confirmed.

---

# **IMPORTANT**

C.O.R.E. is explicitly **not** classifying this as evidence of Corin's death.

There is:

- No body.
    
- No confirmed fatal injury.
    
- No confirmed weapon.
    
- No confirmed projectile.
    
- No confirmed cause of injury.
    

Corin remains officially:

> **MISSING.**

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

A single empty shell.

That's all they found.

No weapon.

No bullet.

No body.

No explanation.

Just a spent casing lying in the mountains, stained with blood.

For everyone who has spent months searching for Corin, the object carries a terrible implication.

But implications are not answers.

C.O.R.E. will not turn an empty shell into a conclusion.

Until Corin herself is found, her status remains unchanged:

> **MISSING.**

And somewhere beyond the mountains, there may still be a girl who knows exactly what happened.
