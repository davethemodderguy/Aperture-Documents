# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY / APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **PHYSICAL CAPABILITY REFERENCE**

### **STRENGTH ASSESSMENT – BELLE, KEITH & CINDER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal Reference

---

## **SUMMARY**

Personnel have occasionally described Belle Merryweather, Keith, and Cinder as possessing "superhuman strength."

Following physical assessments, this description has been deemed inaccurate.

The three individuals are **not** classified as possessing superhuman physical strength.

They are, however, exceptionally strong by the standards of highly trained adults.

---

## **BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

Belle's strength is significantly above average.

Contributing factors include:

- Years of training with her 50-kilogram greatsword.
    
- Excellent physical conditioning.
    
- The stabilizing Ether present throughout her body.
    

Current research indicates that Belle's stabilized Ether reinforces her body, allowing her to make better use of her natural strength and endure greater physical strain than would otherwise be expected.

Researchers emphasize that this does not make Belle infinitely strong.

She can still become exhausted, overexert herself, and require recovery after intense combat.

---

## **KEITH**

Keith's strength is attributed to:

- Five years of military service.
    
- Continuous combat training.
    
- Physical conditioning required for executive protection duties.
    

He routinely carries heavy equipment, including his modified M1014 combat shotgun and tactical gear, without noticeable difficulty.

His strength is considered the result of discipline and training rather than anomalous enhancement.

---

## **CINDER**

Cinder's strength reflects years of conditioning and experience in underground fight clubs prior to joining the Krampus Compliance Authority.

She routinely handles:

- Her 55-kilogram KCA briefcase.
    
- Heavy gauntlets.
    
- Combat equipment.
    

Even when the briefcase is used as an improvised melee weapon, Cinder demonstrates excellent control.

Her physical capability is considered exceptional, though still within the limits of a highly conditioned individual.

---

## **COMPARATIVE NOTE**

The fact that Belle, Keith, and Cinder can comfortably carry equipment weighing approximately 50–55 kilograms often creates the impression that they possess anomalous strength.

Medical evaluations indicate otherwise.

Their performance reflects an uncommon combination of training, conditioning, and, in Belle's unique case, the physiological support provided by stabilized Ether.

---

## **ENGINEERING COMMENT**

Chief Engineer Neko summarized the assessments as follows:

> "They're not breaking the laws of physics."

He paused before looking at Belle effortlessly lifting her sword.

> "...They're just making the rest of us look lazy."

Belle responded with a smile.

> "...Want to try lifting it?"

No volunteers were recorded.

---

## **STATUS**

Belle Merryweather, Keith, and Cinder are classified as possessing **exceptionally high physical strength**.

They are not classified as possessing superhuman strength.

Personnel are nevertheless advised not to underestimate them based on appearance.

**End of Reference.**
