# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **WINTER OPERATIONS UPDATE**

## **SUBJECT: SHELTER T-01 – SEASONAL VISITATION & RESIDENT WILDLIFE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

With the arrival of midwinter, conditions surrounding Shelter T-01 have deteriorated considerably.

Persistent snowfall, sub-zero temperatures, and strong winds have made routine travel to the treehouse increasingly difficult.

As a result, visitation frequency has naturally declined.

This change was anticipated by both Corin Lockwood and Medical Services.

---

# **THE "TIGER"**

Personnel continue referring to the white tiger simply as "the Tiger."

Veterinary Services have noted that, behaviorally, the nickname often paints the wrong picture.

Around familiar individuals, the animal behaves less like a stereotypical apex predator and more like an exceptionally large house cat.

It naps for hours.

Enjoys being scratched beneath the chin by Corin.

Occasionally rolls onto its side for a belly rub—an invitation extended only to Corin, and very rarely to Taiga.

It follows Corin around the clearing with surprising patience.

The resemblance ends at its size.

Personnel are reminded that this is still a powerful wild animal weighing approximately 95 kilograms.

Its jaws are capable of inflicting catastrophic injuries.

The fact that it behaves affectionately toward trusted companions should never be mistaken for harmlessness.

Security's unofficial description remains:

> "A very large striped house cat."

Medical's unofficial addition is:

> "...whose bite you should never volunteer to experience."

---

# **WINTER CONDITIONS**

Travel to Shelter T-01 now requires specialized winter equipment for most personnel.

Blizzards frequently reduce visibility to only a few meters.

The route becomes hazardous even for experienced patrols.

Routine visits have therefore become less frequent.

Not because people stopped caring.

Because the weather simply became unforgiving.

---

# **REGULAR VISITORS**

Even under severe winter conditions, several individuals continue making the journey.

### **Lumi**

Lumi remains the most frequent visitor.

The cold has never discouraged her from checking on Corin.

Most visits are quiet.

Sometimes they share tea.

Sometimes they simply watch the snow fall together.

---

### **Taiga**

Taiga's natural resistance to cold makes the journey considerably easier for her than for most residents.

She often arrives carrying books, supplies, or simply herself.

The tiger almost always greets her before Corin does.

It is not uncommon for Taiga to spend an afternoon resting beside the tiger while Corin reads nearby.

---

### **Astral**

Astral's visits are usually prompted by a familiar observation.

> "Taiga wandered off again."

Finding Taiga has become remarkably straightforward.

If she cannot be found inside SAFEHOUSE...

She is often discovered near Shelter T-01, leaning comfortably against ninety-five kilograms of warm striped fur.

Astral usually laughs.

Taiga usually looks up and says,

> "...Hi."

As if nothing about the situation is unusual.

---

# **REMOTE OPERATIONS**

During periods of severe weather, KCA paperwork continues arriving via autonomous delivery drones.

Visitors carrying food or supplies frequently coordinate with drone deliveries to minimize unnecessary travel through hazardous conditions.

Corin continues performing her accounting duties without interruption.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Shelter T-01 remains occupied.

The forest has grown quieter as winter deepens.

The path is visited less often now.

But it is never completely abandoned.

Somewhere beneath the snow-covered treehouse...

A large striped "house cat" is almost certainly asleep.

And if Taiga has been missing from SAFEHOUSE for an unusually long time...

Astral already knows exactly where to start looking.

**End of Winter Operations Update**
