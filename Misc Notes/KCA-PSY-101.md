# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **KCA-PSY-101**

### **EXECUTIVE OFFICE OBSERVATION – ITEM OF INTEREST**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Executive Personnel Only

---

## **SUMMARY**

During routine document collection from Director Belle Merryweather's office, Executive Protection Officer **Keith** and Executive Secretary **Cinder** observed an unusual personal item left on the Director's desk.

The item was not concealed.

It had been placed neatly beside the day's completed paperwork.

---

## **ITEM DESCRIPTION**

**Object:** Dictionary

**Condition:** Well-maintained

**Known Origin:** Gift from Star during an earlier visit to Heaven's Blacksite.

Only one page had been marked.

A folded corner identified a single entry.

---

## **MARKED ENTRY**

**Resentment**

> _"Resentment is a deep, lingering feeling of anger, bitterness, and displeasure caused by feeling treated unfairly, wronged, or forced to accept something unwanted."_

No notes accompanied the page.

No explanation was provided.

The book remained open.

---

## **OFFICE CONDITION**

Nothing else appeared unusual.

The office was immaculate.

Paperwork had been completed.

Meetings had been signed off.

The Director had already left for the evening.

The dictionary was the only personal object remaining on the desk.

---

## **INTERNAL COMMUNICATION**

Keith and Cinder did not disturb the book.

Neither spoke while leaving the office.

Later that evening, a secure message was transmitted to Endmin.

---

**FROM:** Keith

> "Found something in the Director's office."

---

**FROM:** Cinder

> "It's Star's dictionary."

> "One page is marked."

> "Only one word."

> "Resentment."

---

Several minutes passed before Endmin acknowledged the report.

No immediate reply followed.

---

## **EXECUTIVE REVIEW**

Within hours, the observation had quietly reached the small group closest to Belle.

No emergency meeting was called.

No public announcement was made.

No one dismissed the finding.

The atmosphere among those who received the report was described as unusually quiet.

Concern spread quickly.

There was little disagreement about what the discovery might mean.

What remained uncertain was whether anything could still be said that Belle would be willing to hear.

---

## **STATUS**

The dictionary remains secured as part of the confidential executive observation record.

The marked page has been photographed and archived.

No additional entries or annotations have been found.

**End of Report.**
