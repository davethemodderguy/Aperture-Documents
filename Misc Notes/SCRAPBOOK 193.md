## **SUBJECT: DIETARY PREFERENCES – SHELTER T-01 RESIDENT WILDLIFE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Continued observation has revealed that the two unofficial wildlife residents of Shelter T-01 possess surprisingly predictable food preferences.

Logistics has requested these observations be documented after several recurring "missing lunch" reports.

---

# **THE BIG HOUSE CAT**

The white tiger continues to receive a veterinarian-approved diet.

When offered prepared meals by Corin, however, one preference has become immediately obvious.

Chicken.

More specifically...

Chicken prepared by **Bao** or **Belle Merryweather**.

Whether the difference is due to seasoning, preparation, or simple preference remains unknown.

The tiger has repeatedly demonstrated noticeably greater enthusiasm whenever either of them brings food to Shelter T-01.

Belle has jokingly declared herself,

> "Official Tiger Chef."

Bao has neither confirmed nor denied the title.

Veterinary Services note that approved portions remain carefully controlled despite the tiger's obvious willingness to accept additional servings.

---

# **THE OWL**

If the tiger enjoys chicken...

The owl appears to operate under a different philosophy.

If it contains **potatoes** or **grains**...

It is a potential target.

Documented stolen items include:

- Bread rolls.
    
- Toast.
    
- Sandwiches.
    
- Crackers.
    
- Biscuits.
    
- Rice balls.
    
- French fries.
    
- Baked potatoes.
    
- Potato wedges.
    
- Hash browns.
    
- Cornbread.
    
- Pretzels.
    

The owl displays remarkable consistency.

Meat left unattended is frequently ignored.

A single dinner roll, however...

Will disappear within seconds.

---

# **RECORDED INCIDENT**

During one afternoon visit, Keith placed his lunch on a tree stump while helping Cinder organize paperwork.

The meal consisted of:

- Grilled chicken.
    
- Mashed potatoes.
    
- Bread.
    

Helmet camera footage recorded the following sequence.

The owl landed silently.

Ignored the chicken.

Ignored the utensils.

Collected the bread.

Returned.

Collected several spoonfuls of mashed potatoes.

Departed.

The grilled chicken remained untouched.

Keith stared at the remaining meal for several moments before commenting,

> "...It robbed me selectively."

# **CORIN**

Corin has adapted to the owl's habits.

Whenever she eats outside, she automatically places a small piece of bread or a few potato wedges on a nearby railing.

Within minutes...

The offering disappears.

The owl usually remains perched nearby afterward.

It has never attempted to steal from Corin while she is actively sharing food with it.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The dietary preferences of Shelter T-01's unofficial wildlife residents are considered well established.

Personnel visiting Corin are advised to remember two simple rules:

- If you brought chicken from Bao or Belle, the tiger will probably be interested.
    
- If you brought anything involving potatoes or grains...
    

The owl already knows.
