## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL PROFILE REVISION**

# **COREY — APPEARANCE UPDATE**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corey

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Personnel have observed several gradual changes in Corey's appearance and demeanor over recent months.

None are believed to be anomalous in nature.

Rather, they reflect changes in personal preference and behavior.

---

# **STANDARD ATTIRE**

Corey continues to favor comfortable clothing.

Her current appearance is most commonly characterized by:

- A soft hat (typically a beret, beanie, or similar headwear).
    
- An oversized white lab coat.
    
- Sleeves extending beyond her hands, usually concealing her wrists.
    
- Comfortable clothing beneath the coat.
    
- Practical footwear suitable for both laboratory and residential areas.
    

The oversized sleeves have become one of the most recognizable aspects of her appearance.

Corey frequently keeps her hands partially hidden inside them.

---

# **THE LAB COAT**

Although the coat is standard laboratory attire, Corey's has been intentionally tailored with oversized sleeves.

When asked why, Corey answered simply:

> "...They're comfortable."

She offered no further explanation.

No concerns regarding mobility or workplace safety have been identified.

---

# **BEHAVIORAL CHANGES**

Personnel who have known Corey for an extended period have noted a subtle change in her disposition.

She remains:

- Kind.
    
- Polite.
    
- Patient.
    
- Caring toward both people and Marts.
    

However...

She laughs less often than she once did.

She speaks more quietly.

She spends more time alone.

The change has been gradual rather than sudden.

---

# **MART OBSERVATIONS**

The Marts appear to have noticed this change long before most personnel.

Several have adjusted their routines accordingly.

Current behavior includes:

- Visiting the treehouse more frequently.
    
- Sitting beside Corey while she works.
    
- Quietly accompanying her during walks.
    
- Forming warm blankets during evenings.
    
- Bringing small gifts without prompting.
    

The colony has made no apparent attempt to "fix" her mood.

Instead, they simply remain nearby.

---

# **CORIN'S COMMENT**

When asked whether Corey had changed, Corin nodded.

> "...She's still Corey."

She smiled gently.

> "...Just... quieter."

After a pause, she added:

> "...She still smiles."

> "...I just treasure those moments a little more now."

---

# **STAR'S COMMENT**

Star occasionally catches herself telling a joke before remembering that Corey no longer laughs as easily.

One afternoon, after an especially terrible pun, Corey let out a small laugh.

Star immediately pointed at her.

> "...There it is."

Corey covered her face with one oversized sleeve.

> "...Don't make a big deal out of it..."

Star did anyway.

---

# **LUMI'S OBSERVATION**

Lumi has noticed that Corey often hides her hands within her oversized sleeves while thinking.

She has never asked why.

Instead, she simply sits beside her when she has time.

Sometimes they talk.

Sometimes they don't.

Lumi considers both equally valuable.

---

# **PERSONNEL NOTE**

Corey has repeatedly stated that she does not require special treatment.

Personnel are encouraged to interact with her normally while remaining mindful that quieter behavior should not automatically be interpreted as a desire to be left alone.

As always, respect her wishes if she indicates she would like privacy.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

One evening, Corey sat on the balcony of the treehouse.

Her oversized lab coat sleeves covered most of her hands.

A beret rested comfortably on her head.

Several Marts quietly settled beside her.

No one spoke.

After a long while, Corey looked at the sunset and smiled—a small, genuine smile.

One nearby Mart noticed immediately.

It softly whispered:

> **"...Happy."**

Corey looked at it.

She gently patted it, carefully avoiding its tiny top hat.

> **"...Trying."**

The Mart nodded as though that answer was enough.

Then the two continued watching the sunset together.
