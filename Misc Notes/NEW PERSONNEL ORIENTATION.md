# **WELCOME!**

You'll probably meet Director **Belle Merryweather** sooner or later.

She's easy to recognize.

She's the short, fluffy bunny plushie carrying a very large sword.

Before you introduce yourself, here are a few things you should know.

---

## **✔ Belle Likes...**

🥕 **Carrots**

If Belle is holding a carrot, this is perfectly normal.

If she isn't...

Someone has probably already asked where it went.

---

🤗 **Hugs**

Belle enjoys hugs from people she knows and trusts.

Always respect personal boundaries and consent.

---

🍽 **Food**

Belle enjoys eating and is rarely one to refuse snacks.

Offering food is a surprisingly effective way to start a conversation.

---

👥 **Company**

Despite occasionally enjoying quiet moments, Belle generally likes being around friends.

Don't be surprised if she ends up sitting next to someone she knows.

---

## **Interesting Facts**

- Belle is **20 years old.**
    
- Her greatsword weighs approximately **50 kilograms.**
    
- She once fell **125 meters** from a Blacksite balcony...
    

...bounced...

...and walked away completely unharmed.

Engineering still isn't sure how.

Medical has stopped asking.

---

## **Is She Really That Soft?**

Yes.

Multiple personnel have independently confirmed that Belle feels remarkably soft.

No, this is not considered a scientific measurement.

---

## **Is She Clingy?**

With close friends?

Absolutely.

If Belle trusts you, don't be surprised if she:

- Leans against you.
    
- Hugs you.
    
- Uses you as an accidental pillow.
    
- Quietly appears beside you during breaks.
    

This is considered normal behavior.

# **ONE IMPORTANT RULE**

## **DO NOT CALL HER "ZHAO."**

Belle's name is **Belle Merryweather.**

Personnel are expected to address her by her current name or official title.

Deliberately using her former name after being informed otherwise is considered unacceptable workplace conduct and may result in disciplinary action.

If you're ever unsure...

Just call her **Belle.**

She'll probably smile.

---

## **FINAL ADVICE**

Don't judge her by her height.

Don't judge her by the fact she's a plushie.

Definitely don't judge her by the fact she's carrying a carrot.

She's one of the friendliest people you'll meet at the Blacksite.

Just remember...

The fluffy bunny with the carrot is also the one carrying the 50-kilogram sword.
