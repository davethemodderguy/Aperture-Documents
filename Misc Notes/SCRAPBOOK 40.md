# **OVERVIEW**

New personnel occasionally ask the same question after seeing Director Belle Merryweather walking through the Blacksite.

> "...Why does Director Belle have two belts strapped around the top of her foot?"

More specifically...

> "...Why does one of them have a little bell attached to it?"

The answer is...

No one is entirely sure anymore.

Belle has worn them for so long that they've simply become part of her appearance.

When asked, Belle usually just smiles and replies:

> "...I like them."

And that is apparently the end of the discussion.

---

# **THE BELL**

The small silver bell is attached to one of the straps much like a decorative collar charm.

It produces a soft chime whenever Belle walks, runs, or performs one of her trademark acrobatic flips.

Personnel have grown so accustomed to the sound that many recognize Belle's approach before they see her.

Some operators jokingly refer to it as:

> **"The Executive Early Warning System."**

---

# **A BLACKSITE TRADITION**

Over the years, personnel noticed something interesting.

Nearly every senior executive or long-serving leader has at least one distinctive accessory that makes them instantly recognizable, even from a distance.

Examples include:

**Belle Merryweather**

- Twin foot straps.
    
- Small silver bell.
    

---

**Endmin**

- Signature black-themed equipment and accessories that complement her appearance.
    

---

**Norma Lockwood**

- Her ever-present hoodie outside of missions.
    
- The teal halo that occasionally phases into view—though only the most observant ever notice it.
    

---

**Remielle (Cinder)**

- Her unmistakable multifunction combat briefcase, rarely more than an arm's length away.
    

---

**Kent**

- His distinctive shark tail, making him easy to identify even in a crowd.
    

---

**Star**

- Stardust that subtly glimmers around her and the luminous details of her attire.
    

---

Personnel eventually concluded that these small, personal touches made identifying key executives much easier during the bustle of daily Blacksite life.

Whether intentional or not...

The tradition simply stuck.

# **STATUS**

The exact origin of Belle's foot straps and decorative bell remains undocumented.

No one has requested that she remove them.

No one particularly wants her to.

At this point, the gentle _chime_ has become as much a part of the Blacksite as Belle herself.

As one researcher summarized in the margin of this report:

> **"If you hear a tiny bell, chances are the Blacksite's favorite bunny isn't far away."**
