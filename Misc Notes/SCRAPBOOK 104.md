# **ENTITY UPDATE**

**Previous Designation:** AE-LK-01 – _The Lockwood Matriarch_

**Current Designation:** **AE-LK-01 "LUMI"**

**Status:** Confirmed Non-Hostile Ether-Superseded Ghost

---

# **RECLASSIFICATION**

Following extensive observation and Ether analysis, C.O.R.E. has formally reclassified AE-LK-01.

The entity is no longer considered an unidentified Ether manifestation.

Instead, Lumi has been identified as a **Ghost whose existence has been superseded and stabilized by Ether**.

Unlike conventional ghosts documented within Aperture Industries, Lumi possesses several unique characteristics.

She retains:

- Full coloration.
    
- Stable physical form.
    
- The ability to physically interact with objects.
    
- The ability to make physical contact with living individuals.
    
- Persistent Ether signatures consistent across every observation.
    

Researchers believe Ether is functioning as a stabilizing medium, allowing Lumi to maintain a degree of physical presence unavailable to ordinary ghosts.

---

# **COMPARISON**

For reference, personnel compared Lumi to previously documented ghost entities.

### **Niko**

Exists primarily as a spectral phenomenon.

Unable to consistently interact with the physical environment.

Limited visual fidelity.

---

### **Astral**

Likewise demonstrates characteristics expected of a conventional ghost.

Visible.

Present.

Yet unable to sustain ordinary physical interaction.

---

### **Lumi**

Stable.

Colored.

Capable of touch.

Capable of embracing family members.

Able to sit, walk, hold objects, and perform other ordinary physical actions.

Despite these differences, Lumi continues to register as deceased by every available biological metric.

Her existence remains anomalous.

---

# **VISIBILITY UPDATE**

A significant change has also been confirmed.

Lumi is **no longer visible exclusively to the Lockwood siblings.**

Personnel throughout Heaven's Blacksite have independently observed her presence.

The first reports were met with understandable confusion.

One security officer later summarized the experience.

> "...One day everyone kept talking about Corin's 'imaginary mother.'"

> "...The next day she waved at me."

---

# **PERSONNEL RESPONSE**

The transition from classified anomaly to familiar presence occurred remarkably quickly.

Lumi rarely interrupts anyone's work.

She often walks through the facility quietly.

Occasionally stopping to speak with personnel.

She thanks cafeteria workers.

Compliments engineers.

Checks on Medical.

Reminds researchers to rest.

Most employees describe her as having an unmistakably maternal presence.

Within a week, many had unconsciously begun referring to her simply as...

> **"Miss Lumi."**

---

# **THE LOCKWOOD SIBLINGS**

Despite Lumi's wider appearance throughout the Blacksite...

One absence remains painfully noticeable.

Corin.

She continues residing within her office at KCA Headquarters.

Every visit Lumi makes to Heaven's Blacksite is observed by the siblings she can physically reach.

Norma smiles when she sees her.

Arcturus speaks with her during quiet evenings.

Yet each conversation eventually reaches the same point.

Lumi's eyes drift toward the empty chair at SAFEHOUSE.

She smiles gently.

But there is sadness behind it.

---

# **RECORDED CONVERSATION**

During one evening, Belle found Lumi quietly looking at Corin's untouched place at the dinner table.

Belle approached without speaking.

After several moments, Lumi finally broke the silence.

> "She still thinks she's too far away."

Belle nodded.

"...Yeah."

Lumi rested a hand on the back of Corin's empty chair.

> "Distance is measured by the heart."

> "Not by kilometers."

Belle smiled sadly.

"...We'll keep waiting."

Lumi returned the smile.

> "I know you will."

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious approved Lumi's reclassification following unanimous agreement from C.O.R.E., Medical Services, and Ether Research.

His closing remarks were brief.

> "She has asked for no authority."

> "No resources."

> "No recognition."

He signed the report.

Then added one final observation.

> "The only thing Lumi has attempted to rebuild..."

> "...is her family."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

AE-LK-01 "Lumi" remains classified as a **Non-Hostile Ether-Superseded Ghost**.

No containment procedures are authorized.

Personnel are instructed to treat Lumi with the same courtesy afforded to any respected visitor within Heaven's Blacksite.

Research into Ether-superseded ghosts continues.

Meanwhile, each evening at SAFEHOUSE, one chair remains unoccupied.

Lumi never sits in it.

She simply looks toward it for a moment before quietly hoping that, one day, her youngest daughter will choose to come home of her own accord.

**End of Entity Reclassification Report**
