### **SAFEHOUSE PERSONNEL / ANOMALOUS FORM UPDATE**

# **CORIN — MINIATURE CAT FORM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Current Form:** Miniature White Cat  
**Reason:** **Carelessness / Convenience**  
**Status:** **Stable / Extremely Small**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has voluntarily returned to her miniature cat form.

No emergency occurred.

No injury was involved.

No anomalous event triggered the transformation.

She simply felt **careless** and decided that being a tiny cat was easier.

The transformation remains an unusual contrast to her normal humanoid form.

---

# **CURRENT APPEARANCE**

Corin currently appears as a **very small white kitten**, complete with:

- Tiny cat ears.
    
- Cat fangs.
    
- Twin tails.
    
- Small wings.
    
- Soft white fur.
    
- Extremely small paws.
    

Her miniature size gives her considerable mobility.

She can comfortably:

- Crawl into small spaces.
    
- Climb onto furniture.
    
- Curl up almost anywhere.
    
- Be carried around in someone's hands.
    
- Hide inside containers.
    
- Sit on someone's shoulder.
    
- Loaf.
    

The last activity remains particularly popular among SAFEHOUSE residents.

---

# **BEHAVIOR**

Corin's miniature form is significantly less demanding than her normal form.

She can simply:

> **Sit.**

> **Walk around.**

> **Loaf.**

> **Sleep.**

> **Be carried.**

> **Do absolutely nothing.**

This appears to be precisely why she chose the form.

---

# **MOBILITY**

Despite her tiny size, Corin retains her anomalous mobility.

Her miniature wings remain functional.

She can fly short distances and maneuver through spaces that would be impossible to access in humanoid form.

However, Corin currently appears to have little interest in doing so.

Walking is apparently sufficient.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin is considered completely stable.

She is simply choosing to remain a tiny winged kitten because she does not currently feel like doing anything more complicated.

C.O.R.E. sees no operational reason to interfere.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's normal form allows her to:

- Fly.
    
- Fight.
    
- Intercept missiles.
    
- Use multifunctional feathers.
    
- Operate equipment.
    
- Perform aerial maneuvers.
    

Her miniature cat form allows her to:

> **Sit on a couch and do absolutely nothing.**

Today, Corin has chosen the second option.

She is currently curled into a small white ball on the SAFEHOUSE sofa.

One tail is wrapped around her body.

Her tiny wings are folded against her back.

She appears completely content.
