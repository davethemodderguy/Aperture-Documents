## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SHELTER T-01 – RESIDENT STATUS UPDATE**

## **SUBJECT: VISITATION RECORDS & RESIDENTIAL CONDITIONS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Shelter T-01 has become Corin Lockwood's permanent residence within the Heaven Blacksite perimeter.

Although structurally reinforced by Engineering, the shelter remains intentionally isolated.

Corin selected the location because of its distance from the rest of the Blacksite.

She has repeatedly stated that she prefers the quiet.

Medical and Psychological Services continue to respect this decision while encouraging regular social contact through voluntary visits.

Project SAFEHOUSE remains her registered support network.

The SAFEHOUSE itself continues to house the other residents.

---

# **LOCATION**

Shelter T-01 is located deep within one of the Blacksite's northern forests.

The journey requires traversing steep terrain, dense woodland, and prolonged exposure to severe winter conditions.

During heavy snowfall, travel is only recommended with appropriate cold-weather equipment.

Personnel are reminded that the treehouse's remote location is intentional.

Corin chose a place where she could be alone without being completely disconnected from the people who care about her.

---

# **ENGINEERING STATUS**

Following multiple reinforcement projects, Shelter T-01 is now considered structurally sound for long-term occupancy.

Improvements include:

- Reinforced support beams.
    
- Weather-resistant roofing.
    
- Enhanced insulation.
    
- Emergency communications.
    
- Lightning protection.
    
- Improved heating efficiency.
    

Engineering proposed numerous additional upgrades.

Most were declined.

# **VISITATION RECORDS**

The following individuals are documented as the most frequent visitors to Shelter T-01.

### **Lumia ("Lumi") Lockwood**

Most frequent visitor.

Visits whenever possible.

Often brings tea, groceries, or simply sits with Corin in comfortable silence.

---

### **Remielle ("Cinder")**

Delivers paperwork requiring Corin's review or signature.

Administrative discussions are usually brief before the conversation shifts to ordinary topics.

---

### **Kent ("Keith")**

Frequently accompanies Cinder.

While paperwork is being delivered, Keith often wanders the surrounding forest, commenting that he enjoys the snow and quiet scenery.

---

### **Belle Merryweather**

Usually arrives carrying freshly baked cookies.

Rarely comes alone.

---

### **Bao**

Often accompanies Belle.

Typically brings warm drinks or prepared meals to ensure Corin is eating regularly.

---

### **Frost**

Occasionally leaves small ice roses around Shelter T-01.

Many remain frozen for weeks in the surrounding winter air.

Corin has quietly begun collecting them in glass jars inside the treehouse.

---

### **Star & Nightshade**

Star's visits are energetic and cheerful.

Nightshade's are calm and measured.

Together, they provide a balance Corin appears increasingly comfortable with.

Star has, on several occasions, convinced Corin to smile.

Even if only briefly.

---

### **Neko**

Conducts what he refers to as "routine structural inspections."

Engineering records indicate these inspections frequently expand into suggestions for increasingly elaborate upgrades.

---

### **Miss Neko**

Accompanies Neko during nearly every inspection.

Officially to assist with engineering evaluations.

Unofficially...

To prevent Neko from turning Shelter T-01 into something resembling an experimental research facility.

One inspection ended with Miss Neko removing six pages of unauthorized upgrade proposals from Neko's clipboard.

Corin thanked her.

---

# **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE**

Although Corin no longer resides in SAFEHOUSE, she remains a member of its household.

Her room there has not been reassigned.

Residents continue their daily lives together within SAFEHOUSE while making the journey to Shelter T-01 whenever schedules permit.

The distance, weather, and operational responsibilities mean visits cannot occur every day.

Corin understands this.

Likewise, the residents understand why Corin continues choosing the quiet of the forest.

Neither side expects the other to change overnight.

---

# **MEDICAL COMMENT**

Psychological Services note that Corin's desire for solitude should not be mistaken for a desire to be abandoned.

She intentionally chose a place where visitors are uncommon, not impossible.

The routine of knocking on her door, sharing tea, delivering paperwork, or leaving cookies has become part of the rhythm of her recovery.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Shelter T-01 remains isolated beneath the Blacksite's winter forest.

The cold beyond its walls is unforgiving.

The journey is long.

The wind is relentless.

Yet, despite the distance...

The path to the treehouse is never completely empty.

Someone is almost always willing to make the walk.

And these days...

Corin is usually there to answer the knock.

**End of Shelter T-01 Resident Status Update**
