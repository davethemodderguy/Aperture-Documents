### **BEHAVIORAL / POSTURAL OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — VISIBLE WINGS & WALKING POSTURE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Observation:** **Preferred Wing Presentation / Locomotion**  
**Status:** Stable

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although Corin's wings can fold against her back and become effectively invisible, she generally chooses **not** to conceal them while walking.

Corin prefers to keep her wings visible and partially folded behind her.

She considers them a part of herself rather than something that needs to be hidden.

---

# **VISIBLE WING CONFIGURATION**

While walking, Corin usually keeps her wings:

- Fully visible.
    
- Folded loosely behind her.
    
- Slightly elevated from her back.
    
- Relaxed rather than rigid.
    
- Positioned symmetrically when standing still.
    

The wings remain out of the way during normal movement.

When Corin turns or changes direction, the wings subtly shift with her body.

---

# **WALKING POSTURE**

Corin's walking posture has become notably elegant.

Her back remains straight.

Her shoulders stay relaxed.

Her head is held naturally upright.

Her movements are smooth and deliberate.

The wings contribute significantly to the visual effect.

They move gently behind her, creating a broad silhouette without making her movement appear cumbersome.

Personnel have described her gait as:

> **"Graceful."**

> **"Almost ceremonial."**

> **"Like she knows exactly where she's going."**

Corin insists she is simply walking normally.

---

# **WING COORDINATION**

Corin has developed excellent subconscious coordination between her wings and her body.

While walking, the wings automatically adjust to avoid:

- Walls.
    
- Doorways.
    
- Furniture.
    
- Passing personnel.
    
- Other residents.
    

She rarely needs to consciously think about their position.

The wings have effectively become another set of body movements.

---

# **POSTURE AND PSYCHOLOGICAL CHANGE**

The change in Corin's posture has been noticed alongside her gradual psychological recovery.

Previously, Corin often walked with her shoulders lowered and her body closed in on itself.

She attempted to make herself smaller.

Now she walks differently.

Her back is straight.

Her wings are visible.

She occupies space without apologizing for it.

This has been interpreted as another indication that Corin is becoming more comfortable with her identity and body.

---

# **ORPHIE'S OBSERVATION**

Orphie once watched Corin walking down a hallway.

She called out:

> **"You walk differently now."**

Corin stopped.

> **"Different how?"**

Orphie smiled.

> **"You look confident."**

Corin glanced over her shoulder at her wings.

Then back at Orphie.

> **"...I like my wings."**

Orphie nodded.

> **"I know."**

---

# **BELLE**

Belle considers Corin's walking posture impressive.

She once tried to imitate it.

The result lasted approximately five seconds.

Belle eventually laughed and said:

> **"I can't walk like Corin."**

Corin replied:

> **"You don't have to."**

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi has also noticed the difference.

She remembers how Corin once tried to make herself disappear.

Now she sees her daughter walking openly through the Blacksite with her wings displayed.

Lumi considers the change significant.

Her private observation was:

> **"She isn't hiding anymore."**

---

# **CURRENT BEHAVIOR**

Corin generally keeps her wings visible while:

- Walking.
    
- Visiting the Blacksite.
    
- Spending time in the SAFEHOUSE.
    
- Meeting Lumi.
    
- Visiting the other residents.
    
- Traveling through open areas.
    

She uses the invisible folded configuration primarily when concealment is genuinely useful.

Otherwise:

**The wings stay visible.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin does not need to hide her wings.

She knows this.

More importantly, she seems to believe it.

When she walks through the Blacksite, her wings rest naturally behind her.

Her posture is straight.

Her movements are controlled.

Her steps are quiet.

There is no hesitation.

No attempt to make herself smaller.

The wings that once represented everything strange about her body have become something she is comfortable displaying.

Corin's elegant gait has become almost unmistakable.

You can often recognize her before you see her face.

Just look for the girl walking calmly down the hallway—

**with a pair of wings proudly displayed behind her.**
