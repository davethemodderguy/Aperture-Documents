# 1. SUMMARY

Neri does **not** like being called cute.

This is not a minor preference.

This is not a suggestion.

This is not something personnel should attempt to test.

Neri will become visibly irritated, verbally defensive, and noticeably flustered when described as "cute."

Personnel should note that the subject's reaction does **not** generally escalate into lethal violence.

However, repeated use of the term is strongly discouraged.

> **ARCHIVIST NOTE:**  
> She will not kill you for calling her cute.  
> She will, however, become extremely angry about it.  
> There is a meaningful difference.

---

# 2. OBSERVED RESPONSE

The standard sequence appears to be:

**Personnel:** "Neri, you're cute."

**Neri:** "...What?"

**Personnel:** "I said you're—"

**Neri:** "NO."

**Personnel:** "You are, though."

**Neri:** "I AM NOT."

**Personnel:** "You kind of are."

**Neri:** "STOP SAYING THAT."

At this stage, Neri typically exhibits:

- Increased facial redness.
    
- Avoidance of eye contact.
    
- Defensive posture.
    
- Rapid denial.
    
- Increased volume of speech.
    
- Attempts to change the subject.
    
- Occasional retreat from the immediate area.
    
- An apparent inability to formulate a response that does not involve denying the accusation.
    

She has occasionally responded by pulling her coat collar over part of her face.

This behavior has unfortunately been interpreted by several observers as making the situation worse.

# 4. PERSONALITY CONTRADICTION

Further observation has resulted in a somewhat unusual behavioral classification.

Neri displays characteristics commonly associated with both:

### TSUNDERE BEHAVIOR

Observed characteristics include:

- Immediate denial of compliments.
    
- Becoming embarrassed when receiving affection.
    
- Pretending not to care about people she clearly cares about.
    
- Becoming defensive when emotional subjects are brought up.
    
- Difficulty directly expressing affection.
    
- Quietly performing helpful actions and subsequently denying that they were motivated by affection.
    

Example:

> **Neri:** "I made you food."

> **Resident:** "For me?"

> **Neri:** "Obviously."

> **Resident:** "That's really nice of you."

> **Neri:** "...It's not a big deal."

> **Resident:** "Thank you."

> **Neri:** "I just made too much."

> **Resident:** "There's exactly one plate."

> **Neri:** "SHUT UP."

### YANDERE-LIKE BEHAVIOR

Neri also exhibits an unusually intense protective attachment toward people she considers friends.

Her behavioral pattern can be summarized as:

> **Friend:** Protect.  
> **Friend threatened:** Intervene.  
> **Friend injured:** Extreme retaliation.  
> **Friend killed:** █████████████████████████████████

The final category has been intentionally left incomplete.

Personnel familiar with Neri's history should understand why.

Her extreme protective behavior appears less rooted in possessiveness and more strongly associated with her profound fear of losing people again.

She does not want another friend to disappear.

She does not want another person to die while she is unable to intervene.

She does not want to experience another loss.

Unfortunately, her preferred solution to this problem is sometimes:

> **"I'll make sure nobody hurts them again."**

This statement should not be interpreted casually.

# 8. ADDENDUM — THE CAKE INCIDENT

At approximately 15:42, Neri was observed in the cafeteria eating cake using the collar of her oversized coat.

An Archivist entered the room.

The Archivist looked at Neri.

The Archivist smiled.

The Archivist said:

> "You're cute."

Neri stopped eating.

Approximately three seconds of silence followed.

Neri slowly lowered the piece of cake.

> **Neri:** "...No."

> **Archivist:** "Yes."

> **Neri:** "No."

> **Archivist:** "You are."

> **Neri:** "I'm not."

> **Archivist:** "You're sitting there eating cake with your coat."

> **Neri:** "THAT DOESN'T MAKE ME CUTE."

> **Archivist:** "It kind of does."

Neri stared at the Archivist.

The Archivist immediately realized they had made a tactical error.

Neri's face became visibly red.

She pulled the coat collar over her mouth.

> **Neri:** "...You're annoying."

The Archivist placed another slice of cake on the table.

Neri looked at it.

She looked back at the Archivist.

> **Neri:** "...You're less annoying now."

She resumed eating.

**Incident resolved without violence.**
