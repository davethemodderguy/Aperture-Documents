## Executive Summary

The prolonged incident in which Plushie Neri was forgotten outside for more than two days appears to have produced a significantly more serious behavioral change than initially documented.

Neri has begun treating herself as a **liability that others are forced to keep around**.

This belief has progressed beyond withdrawal and passive acceptance.

Neri has begun to **stop resisting danger**.

When confronted with a situation that could reasonably injure her, Neri may no longer attempt to escape, call for assistance, or even indicate that something is wrong.

She simply allows it to happen.

This includes situations in which she is clearly capable of recognizing the danger.

The most serious example occurred when Plushie Neri was nearly placed into a washing machine.

She recognized what was happening.

She did not resist.

---

## Incident: Laundry Room

**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE Utility Area  
**FORM:** Plushie  
**DATE:** REDACTED

A resident discovered Plushie Neri in a pile of blankets.

Believing her to be an ordinary plush toy, the resident picked her up along with several other fabric items and proceeded toward the laundry area.

Neri was awake.

She recognized the environment.

She recognized the washing machine.

She understood what was about to happen.

She did not move.

She did not speak.

She did not attempt to climb away.

She did not signal for assistance.

The resident opened the washing machine door.

Neri remained completely still.

At this point, Bao entered the room.

She immediately recognized Neri.

> **Bao:** "STOP."

The resident froze.

Bao removed Neri from their hands.

Neri remained limp.

Bao examined her.

> **Bao:** "Neri."

No response.

> **Bao:** "Did you know where you were going?"

Neri nodded.

> **Bao:** "Did you know what that was?"

Neri nodded again.

> **Bao:** "Then why didn't you move?"

Neri was silent.

After several seconds, she quietly said:

> **Neri:** "It's okay."

Bao immediately understood that it was not.

---

## Subsequent Interview

Neri was asked why she had not attempted to escape.

She initially refused to answer.

Eventually, she wrote:

> **"It doesn't matter."**

Bao asked:

> "What doesn't matter?"

Neri wrote:

> **"Me."**

She then added:

> **"I'm already a problem."**

---

## Behavioral Assessment

This is a substantial escalation from Neri's previous pattern.

Previously, Neri's internal logic was:

> **I am a burden, so I should avoid bothering people.**

Current logic appears to be:

> **I am a burden, so if something happens to me, people don't need to stop it.**

This distinction is critical.

Neri is not necessarily attempting to harm herself.

She is not necessarily seeking death.

Rather, she appears to have stopped assigning sufficient importance to her own safety.

Her reasoning appears to be:

> **If keeping me safe requires effort, then letting something happen is easier for everyone.**

This is consistent with her longstanding expendability belief, but represents a dangerous progression.

---

## Observed Behaviors

Since the incident, Neri has been observed:

- remaining still when placed somewhere unsafe;
    
- failing to move away from approaching hazards;
    
- allowing objects to fall near her without reacting;
    
- remaining outdoors despite worsening conditions;
    
- not signaling when she is uncomfortable;
    
- accepting potentially unsafe handling without resistance;
    
- allowing other residents to move her without indicating where she wants to go;
    
- failing to call for help even when assistance would be appropriate.
    

Importantly:

**Neri still possesses the ability to recognize danger.**

The problem is not ignorance.

The problem is that she increasingly believes her own safety is optional.

---

## Plushie Form Complication

This behavior is particularly dangerous because Plushie Neri is visually disarming.

She is small.

She is soft.

She is quiet.

She can sit completely motionless for extended periods.

Under normal circumstances, this is simply Neri enjoying her plush form.

Following the recent behavioral change, however, complete stillness can no longer automatically be interpreted as comfort.

A motionless Plushie Neri may be:

1. comfortable;
    
2. asleep;
    
3. choosing solitude;
    
4. tired;
    
5. ignoring everyone;
    
6. or consciously allowing a dangerous situation to continue.
    

Personnel must therefore exercise judgment.

## The Washing Machine Incident Was Not an Accident

The washing machine itself was not the concerning part of the incident.

The concerning part was Neri's response.

Bao later demonstrated that Neri could have easily moved away.

Neri was physically capable of doing so.

She simply decided not to.

When asked whether she expected Bao to intervene, Neri wrote:

> **"No."**

Bao asked:

> "Did you think anyone would?"

Neri shook her head.

---

## The Liability Belief

Neri's current internal model appears to be:

> **I am difficult to keep.**

Therefore:

> **Keeping me safe costs people effort.**

Therefore:

> **If something happens to me, they don't have to keep spending that effort.**

Therefore:

> **I should not make them stop it.**

This is the exact opposite of the behavior C.O.R.E. wants to reinforce.

Neri has essentially taken the concept of being a burden and converted it into a reason not to protect herself.

---

## Welfare Directive

Personnel are reminded:

**Do not interpret passive behavior as consent to danger.**

If Neri is in imminent danger, intervention is authorized and required.

Do not wait for her to ask.

Do not ask:

> "Do you want help?"

if the answer must be obtained before preventing immediate harm.

Remove her from the hazard.

Afterward, do not lecture her.

Do not tell her:

> "You should have moved."

Do not tell her:

> "Why didn't you say something?"

Do not tell her:

> "You scared us."

Those statements risk reinforcing the belief that her danger is another burden she has imposed on everyone.

---

## Current Assessment

Neri's recent belief that she is a liability has progressed from an emotional belief into a **behavioral safety hazard**.

She is increasingly willing to accept danger rather than create inconvenience for others.

This does not mean Neri wants to die.

It means that, in moments where self-preservation requires drawing attention to herself, she may choose not to act.

That distinction must remain clear.

The appropriate response is not constant surveillance.

It is not forcing Neri to stay surrounded by people.

It is not removing Plushie Form.

It is not preventing her from being alone.

It is simply ensuring that when an actual hazard occurs, personnel do not mistake her silence for safety.

---

## Final Note

Neri once believed:

> **"I am expendable."**

She is now behaving as though that statement is a practical rule.

C.O.R.E. therefore considers the following principle operationally important:

> **Neri does not need to prove that she wants to survive before someone is allowed to protect her.**

If she is in danger, protect her.

If she is safe and wants to be alone, leave her alone.

If she wants to be held, hold her.

If she wants to walk away, let her.

But if a washing machine is involved:

**Take the seal out.**
