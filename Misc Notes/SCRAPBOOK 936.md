# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. DIVISION — ARCHITECTIAL PHYSIOLOGY ADDENDUM

**SUBJECTS:** Guiding Light / Neri  
**TOPIC:** Wing Manifestation and Behavioral Differences  
**STATUS:** Stable

---

## 1. GUIDING LIGHT'S WING SHARDS

Guiding Light's wings remain manifested as numerous floating fragments of azure Light.

The fragments are not permanently fixed to her body.

Individual shards can be physically removed.

This does not appear to cause Guiding Light any meaningful physical harm.

A shard can simply be picked up and pulled away from the surrounding formation.

Guiding Light's standard response is:

> **"Ouch."**

The response is considered largely theatrical.

When questioned about whether removing a shard actually hurts:

> **Curious Light:** "Does that actually hurt?"

> **Guiding Light:** "No."

> **Curious Light:** "Then why did you say ouch?"

> **Guiding Light:** "Because you pulled my wing off."

> **Curious Light:** "It was one shard."

> **Guiding Light:** "MY WING."

> **Curious Light:** "You're being dramatic."

> **Guiding Light:** "Yes."

No significant injury, bleeding, loss of function, or distress has been observed following the removal of individual shards.

The fragments appear to be more comparable to detachable portions of manifested Light than conventional biological tissue.

---

# 2. THE CUPCAKE MAKES MORE SENSE NOW

The ability to detach individual wing shards explains how the original fragment entered the cupcake.

Guiding Light did not lose an entire wing.

A small fragment simply separated from the larger formation and subsequently became incorporated into the baked good.

The incident was therefore not medically catastrophic.

It was, however, extremely consequential.

Neri ate the cupcake.

The fragment's Architect-derived Light integrated with her.

Neri subsequently developed a connection to Guiding Light's power.

---

# 3. NERI'S WINGS

Neri's wings are fundamentally different in presentation.

Unlike Guiding Light's fragmented wings, Neri currently possesses:

> **Two complete, stable wings composed of azure Light.**

They are fully functional.

They allow her to:

- fly;
    
- hover;
    
- maneuver vertically;
    
- remain suspended without conventional support;
    
- and conceal the wings entirely.
    

The last capability is the one Neri uses most frequently.

---

# 4. NERI PREFERS BEING NORMAL

Despite possessing a significant amount of Architect-derived power, Neri has shown little interest in constantly displaying it.

Her preference is remarkably simple:

> **She wants to be normal.**

When inside the SAFEHOUSE, Neri generally keeps her wings hidden.

She does not consider them frightening.

She does not hate them.

She simply doesn't feel a particular need to have them visible.

When asked why she hides them:

> **Neri:** "They're big."

> **Neko:** "That's your reason?"

> **Neri:** "They get in the way."

> **Neko:** "...Fair."

She also appears to dislike the attention they attract.

A normal walk through the SAFEHOUSE attracts very little attention.

A ten-year-old Seal Thiren walking through the hallway with two enormous glowing wings attracts considerably more.

Neri has consequently developed a simple solution:

**Hide the wings. Continue walking.**

---

# 5. OCCASIONAL EXCEPTIONS

Neri will still manifest her wings when necessary.

Known circumstances include:

- practicing flight;
    
- crossing large gaps;
    
- testing her abilities;
    
- emergencies;
    
- situations where hovering is more practical than walking.
    

She has also occasionally manifested them simply because someone asked.

However, once the novelty wears off, they generally disappear again.

Neri does not appear to regard flight as a replacement for ordinary movement.

If the destination is reachable by walking, she will usually walk.

---

# 6. GUIDING LIGHT'S REACTION

Guiding Light finds Neri's reluctance to display her wings somewhat amusing.

She is exceptionally proud of Neri's development and considers the wings an important achievement.

Neri, meanwhile, treats them like an inconvenient accessory.

During one observation:

> **Guiding Light:** "Show them!"

> **Neri:** "No."

> **Guiding Light:** "Why?"

> **Neri:** "I have them all the time."

> **Guiding Light:** "But they're pretty!"

> **Neri:** "They're still wings."

> **Guiding Light:** "YOUR WINGS."

> **Neri:** "Yes."

> **Guiding Light:** "YOU CAN FLY."

> **Neri:** "I know."

> **Guiding Light:** "YOU'RE HALF ARCHITECT."

> **Neri:** "I know."

> **Guiding Light:** "AREN'T YOU EXCITED?"

Neri paused.

> **Neri:** "...I want cake."

Guiding Light reportedly considered this a completely acceptable answer.

---

# 7. BEHAVIORAL ASSESSMENT

Neri's continued preference for ordinary life is considered healthy.

Her new abilities have not caused her to abandon her established identity.

She still identifies herself primarily through her relationships and everyday activities rather than her power.

She remains:

- Bao's daughter;
    
- Frost's little sister;
    
- Alice's friend;
    
- Neko's chemistry assistant;
    
- a SAFEHOUSE resident;
    
- and simply **Neri**.
    

The wings are an ability.

They are not her identity.

---

## 8. ARCHIVIST'S NOTE

There is a certain irony in the current situation.

Guiding Light:

**Has detachable floating wing fragments.**

**Dramatically says "Ouch" when one is removed.**

**Is teaching herself baking under Curious Light's supervision.**

**Is immensely proud of Neri.**

Neri:

**Accidentally inherited a substantial portion of Guiding Light's power by eating one of those fragments in a cupcake.**

**Can now fly.**

**Can manipulate reality.**

**Can create subspaces.**

**Can manipulate Light.**

**Can restructure matter.**

And yet her preferred lifestyle remains:

> **Eat cake.**
> 
> **Hang out with friends.**
> 
> **Do chemistry.**
> 
> **Walk around normally.**
> 
> **Hide the wings.**
> 
> **Repeat.**

**Assessment:**  
Neri has acquired an extraordinary amount of power without acquiring an extraordinary amount of interest in showing it off.

This is considered preferable.
