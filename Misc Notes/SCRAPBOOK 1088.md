## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### PSYCHOLOGICAL & RESIDENTIAL WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-NERI-195  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESTRICTED — RESIDENTIAL WELFARE  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE OBSERVATION  
**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**OBSERVATION:** Interaction between Plush Neri and newly assigned Blacksite personnel

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Contrary to initial expectations, Plush Neri has developed an unusually positive relationship with recently assigned Blacksite personnel.

This was not facilitated through a formal socialization program.

No program exists.

Nobody attempted to introduce her.

She simply started appearing near newcomers.

The newcomers, in turn, quickly discovered that the small plush-like entity following them around was:

1. alive;
    
2. sentient;
    
3. a permanent SAFEHOUSE resident;
    
4. exceptionally intelligent;
    
5. capable of speech;
    
6. capable of biting;
    
7. drinking coffee;
    
8. and apparently interested in determining whether they were trustworthy.
    

Most newcomers passed.

Several were subsequently headpatted.

One was bitten.

He considered this an honor.

---

# 2. FIRST CONTACT

A newly assigned maintenance technician was performing routine inspection work in Sector 4-C.

He noticed something sitting beside his toolbox.

A miniature humanoid figure.

Long white hair.

Oversized hoodie.

Round glasses.

Seal tail.

Approximately the size of a large stuffed animal.

The technician assumed it belonged to one of the residents.

He moved his toolbox.

The figure moved with it.

He moved the toolbox again.

The figure followed.

After approximately thirty seconds, he crouched down.

**TECHNICIAN:**  
"...Are you lost?"

Neri shook her head.

**TECHNICIAN:**  
"Do you work here?"

Neri nodded.

**TECHNICIAN:**  
"...Okay."

Pause.

**TECHNICIAN:**  
"What do you do?"

Neri pointed toward herself.

Then toward the research wing.

Then made a small writing motion with her hand.

The technician understood.

**TECHNICIAN:**  
"Research?"

Neri nodded.

**TECHNICIAN:**  
"You're a researcher?"

Neri nodded again.

The technician looked at her.

Then at the toolbox.

Then back at Neri.

**TECHNICIAN:**  
"You're very small."

Neri stared at him.

The technician immediately corrected himself.

**TECHNICIAN:**  
"Sorry."

Neri climbed onto his shoe.

The technician froze.

---

# 3. THE HEADPAT PROBLEM

New personnel rapidly discovered that Neri permits gentle headpats when she voluntarily approaches them.

This has resulted in a peculiar pattern.

A newcomer encounters Neri.

Neri looks at them.

The newcomer hesitates.

Neri steps closer.

The newcomer cautiously pats her head.

Neri lowers her head slightly.

The newcomer continues.

Neri remains still.

Sometimes her eyes close.

Sometimes she leans into the hand.

Sometimes she simply sits there afterward.

No verbal acknowledgment is required.

This has become one of the easiest ways for new personnel to establish that Neri is comfortable around them.

It is also one of the few situations in which Neri will occasionally approach someone first.

---

# 4. NEW EMPLOYEES LEARN VERY QUICKLY

Personnel orientation now informally includes the following warning:

> **If Plush Neri approaches you, do not assume she needs something.**

She may simply want to sit there.

She may want a headpat.

She may want to be carried.

She may want to inspect someone's equipment.

She may want coffee.

She may want to bite a sleeve.

She may have no particular reason whatsoever.

One new archivist reported:

> "She climbed onto my chair, sat beside my keyboard, stared at my spreadsheet for five minutes, and then fell asleep."

The archivist did not move her.

She slept for forty-three minutes.

---

# 5. THE COFFEE INCIDENT

A new laboratory assistant discovered Neri sitting on a counter.

A small cup of coffee was beside her.

The assistant looked at the cup.

Then at Neri.

**ASSISTANT:**  
"Is that coffee?"

Neri nodded.

**ASSISTANT:**  
"You're ten."

Neri stared.

**ASSISTANT:**  
"...Right. Sorry."

Neri took another sip.

The assistant eventually asked:

**ASSISTANT:**  
"Do you actually like it?"

Neri nodded.

**ASSISTANT:**  
"That's terrifying."

Neri continued drinking.

---

# 6. NEWCOMERS AND THE BITE

The majority of newcomers eventually learn about Neri's bite.

Usually from somebody else.

One security recruit did not.

He was speaking with another employee when Neri walked past.

The recruit crouched.

**RECRUIT:**  
"Hey, little—"

Neri bit his sleeve.

She did not release it.

The recruit stood.

Neri remained attached.

The other employee looked at her.

Then at him.

**EMPLOYEE:**  
"Yeah. She does that."

**RECRUIT:**  
"WHY DID NOBODY TELL ME?"

Neri continued hanging from his sleeve.

After approximately twelve seconds, she released it.

She sat down.

The recruit stared at her.

Neri stared back.

**RECRUIT:**  
"...Are we friends now?"

Neri nodded.

The recruit smiled.

**RECRUIT:**  
"Okay."

---

# 7. THE NEWCOMERS DO NOT TREAT HER AS A MASCOT

An important distinction has emerged.

The newcomers generally begin by assuming Neri is an unusual plush toy.

They stop doing so after meeting her.

She is introduced as:

> **Neri. SAFEHOUSE resident. Researcher.**

Not:

> "The Blacksite plushie."

Not:

> "The mascot."

Not:

> "The toy."

The distinction appears to matter.

Neri has shown no discomfort when people affectionately refer to her as a plushie.

She does, however, retain the ability to decide when interaction ends.

If she crawls away, the interaction is over.

Personnel have been compliant with this boundary.

This appears to be one reason she is comfortable around them.

---

# 8. OBSERVED SOCIAL PATTERN

Neri's behavior around newcomers differs significantly from her historical behavior around established SAFEHOUSE residents.

She does not appear particularly concerned about whether newcomers expect anything from her.

There is no established emotional debt.

No long history.

No complicated relationship.

No expectation that she must explain herself.

A newcomer can sit down.

Neri can climb onto their lap.

They can gently pat her.

Neither party has to say anything.

After several minutes, Neri may simply leave.

The newcomer does not interpret this as rejection.

Neri does not interpret their lack of pursuit as abandonment.

The interaction ends naturally.

This appears to suit her extremely well.

---

# 9. THE NEW RESEARCHER

One newly assigned chemistry researcher was particularly surprised to discover Neri's actual academic status.

She found Plush Neri sitting beside a stack of molecular research papers.

**RESEARCHER:**  
"Are those yours?"

Neri nodded.

**RESEARCHER:**  
"You understand this?"

Neri nodded.

**RESEARCHER:**  
"All of it?"

Neri shook her head.

The researcher looked confused.

**RESEARCHER:**  
"So you don't understand it?"

Neri shook her head again.

She then pointed toward the papers.

Then toward herself.

Then made a writing gesture.

The researcher eventually understood.

**RESEARCHER:**  
"You research things you don't understand yet?"

Neri nodded.

**RESEARCHER:**  
"...That's actually a very good researcher mindset."

Neri looked pleased.

She climbed onto the researcher's shoulder.

The researcher remained still.

---

# 10. THE NEWCOMERS' CONSENSUS

After several weeks, an informal consensus has emerged among recently assigned personnel.

Neri is:

- easy to get along with;
    
- quiet;
    
- extremely intelligent;
    
- occasionally absent-minded;
    
- fond of coffee;
    
- fond of headpats;
    
- willing to be carried;
    
- occasionally bitey;
    
- comfortable with silence;
    
- surprisingly friendly;
    
- and significantly less intimidating than her research files suggest.
    

One employee summarized her as:

> "She's basically the world's smallest senior scientist."

Another corrected him:

> "No. She's a ten-year-old who happens to be better at chemistry than most of the senior scientists."

Both statements were accepted.

---

# 11. NERI'S RESPONSE

When asked whether she likes the newcomers, Neri was sitting on a cafeteria chair in plush form.

The question was written down for her.

Neri read it.

She thought for several seconds.

Then nodded.

The employee asked:

> "You like them?"

Neri nodded again.

She then added, verbally:

> **"...They're nice."**

The employee smiled.

Neri immediately looked away.

No further comment was provided.

---

# 12. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

This is considered a positive development.

However, personnel are reminded that Neri's comfort around newcomers should not be interpreted as a general invitation to increase contact.

Her current social behavior remains highly selective.

She does not suddenly want everyone around her.

She simply appears to find newcomers easier to interact with because they carry fewer expectations.

They do not know her history.

They do not expect her to perform emotional recovery.

They do not attempt to fix her.

They do not chase her when she leaves.

They let her sit beside them.

That is sufficient.

---

# 13. FINAL OBSERVATION

There is a noticeable pattern among newcomers.

At first, they see a strange little plush figure wandering around the Blacksite.

Then she bites someone's sleeve.

Then she drinks coffee.

Then someone discovers she is a researcher.

Then someone learns she filled twelve terabytes of storage in a month.

Then they realize she is ten.

Then they see her sitting quietly beside them.

And eventually, the novelty wears off.

She stops being:

> **"the weird plush thing."**

She becomes:

> **Neri.**

That appears to be exactly what she wants.

Not to be admired.

Not to be studied.

Not to be protected every second.

Just to be another person who happens to sit on someone's desk sometimes.

And occasionally bite their sleeve.
