# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL / INCIDENT RECORD**

# **THE WRONG WORDS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Incident Type:** **Miscommunication / Voluntary Withdrawal**  
**Current Status:** **Missing from Treehouse — Location Unconfirmed**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin overheard a conversation between **Orphie, Curious Light, and Neko**.

She was not invited into the conversation.

She was not supposed to be listening.

More importantly, she did not hear the entire discussion.

She heard fragments.

Fragments that, when interpreted through her already severely distorted perception of herself, sounded like confirmation of her worst fears.

Corin believed they were talking about her.

She believed they were tired of her.

She believed they regretted taking care of her.

She believed they saw her as exactly what she had always feared she was:

**A burden.**

The conversation did not mean what Corin thought it meant.

By the time anyone realized what she had heard, she was already gone.

---

# **THE CONVERSATION**

The three had been discussing Corin's deteriorating self-image.

Curious Light was concerned.

Neko was concerned.

Orphie was exhausted.

Not exhausted **with Corin**.

Exhausted from worrying about her.

The conversation included statements regarding:

- Corin's dependence on others.
    
- The difficulty of keeping her safe.
    
- The emotional strain of her condition.
    
- The need to establish boundaries.
    
- The possibility that the current arrangement was becoming unsustainable.
    

None of them were expressing hatred toward Corin.

None of them wanted her gone.

But several statements, when heard without context, could easily be misunderstood.

Especially by Corin.

---

# **WHAT CORIN HEARD**

Corin remained outside the room.

She heard:

> **"...can't keep doing this forever."**

A pause.

> **"...too much responsibility."**

Another fragment:

> **"...she needs more help than we can give."**

And:

> **"...the treehouse can't keep functioning like this."**

She stopped listening.

She did not hear the rest.

She did not hear Orphie's response.

She did not hear Neko defend her.

She did not hear Curious Light clarify what they were actually discussing.

She only heard the parts that confirmed what she already believed.

---

# **CORIN'S INTERPRETATION**

To Corin, the conversation translated into something much simpler:

> **I'm a burden.**

Then:

> **They're tired of me.**

Then:

> **They want me gone.**

The conclusion came almost immediately.

> **"I knew it."**

She quietly left.

Nobody noticed.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

Corin did not return.

At first, Orphie assumed she was somewhere nearby.

Then an hour passed.

Then several.

The usual places were searched.

Nothing.

Eventually, Orphie returned to the treehouse.

Corin's belongings were still there.

Her writing materials.

Her spare clothes.

Her food.

Everything.

Except Corin.

---

# **ORPHIE'S REALIZATION**

When Orphie understood what had happened, she immediately remembered the conversation.

She froze.

> **"...She heard us."**

Curious Light looked at her.

> **"How much?"**

Orphie shook her head.

> **"I don't know."**

Neko immediately stood.

> **"Then we find her."**

---

# **CORIN'S NOTE**

A single page was found missing from Corin's notebook.

It was later discovered several hundred meters from the treehouse.

The writing was brief.

> **"I heard you."**

Then:

> **"I'm sorry."**

And:

> **"You don't have to worry about me anymore."**

There was no accusation.

That made the message considerably worse.

Corin was not angry.

She believed she had finally understood what everyone really thought of her.

---

# **THE WITHDRAWAL**

Corin has not returned to the treehouse.

She has also refused to respond to attempts to contact her.

No matter how cold the weather becomes.

No matter how uncomfortable her body becomes.

No matter whether her stitched limbs remain attached.

She refuses to go back.

The treehouse, to Corin, is no longer home.

It is the place where she discovered that everyone had supposedly been pretending to care about her.

That belief is false.

But Corin currently believes it completely.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has repeatedly attempted to explain what happened.

There has been no response.

She has left messages throughout the surrounding forest.

> **"You heard the wrong part."**

> **"Please let me explain."**

> **"Nobody wanted you gone."**

> **"I was talking about how worried I am."**

> **"Corin, please."**

Nothing.

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT**

Curious Light blames himself.

Not because he said something malicious.

Because he knew Corin's mental state was fragile.

He knew how easily she could interpret ordinary statements as confirmation that she was unwanted.

He should have been more careful.

His only comment was:

> **"...We should have checked who was listening."**

---

# **NEKO**

Neko has been unusually quiet.

He remembers sharing his lunch with Corin.

He remembers buying extra sandwiches.

He remembers her insisting that pickles belonged on everything.

Now her seat at the table is empty.

He eventually said:

> **"She thinks we were talking about getting rid of her."**

Orphie replied:

> **"We weren't."**

Neko looked toward the forest.

> **"I know."**

---

# **THE MOST CONCERNING PART**

Corin is not running away because she hates them.

She is running away because she believes she is **protecting them from herself.**

Her reasoning is consistent with her established self-dehumanization.

> **They don't want me.**

Therefore:

> **Leaving helps them.**

Therefore:

> **Returning would be selfish.**

The conclusion is entirely based on a misunderstanding.

But Corin's belief in it is genuine.

---

# **CURRENT SEARCH**

Search teams have been deployed.

The Architects are attempting to locate her without overwhelming her.

Orphie has specifically requested that nobody approach Corin aggressively.

The objective is not to force her home.

It is to reach her.

To explain.

To let her decide what happens next.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin heard three people talking.

She heard a few sentences.

She filled in everything else herself.

And because she already believed she was a burden, the worst possible interpretation was the easiest one for her mind to accept.

She did not hear:

> **"We're worried about her."**

She heard:

> **"We're tired of her."**

She did not hear:

> **"She needs help."**

She heard:

> **"She's too much."**

She did not hear:

> **"How do we keep her safe?"**

She heard:

> **"How do we get rid of her?"**

And now she refuses to return.

Even if the cold reaches her.

Even if her limbs detach.

Even if she has nowhere else to go.

Because in Corin's mind, returning to the treehouse would mean forcing herself into a place where she believes she is no longer wanted.

The tragedy is not that Orphie stopped loving her.

It is that **Corin stopped believing her.**

**End of Psychological / Incident Record**
