### **MEDICAL / ANOMALOUS CONDITION FOLLOW-UP LOG**

# **THE MISSING VOCAL CORDS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Condition:** **Permanent Aphonia / Structural Absence of Vocal Cords**  
**Voice Status:** **Absent**  
**Ether Expulsion:** **Benign / Non-Depleting**  
**Status:** **Persistent**

---

# **UPDATED FINDING**

Further examination of Corin's throat has revealed the reason she cannot speak.

Her vocal cords are gone.

Not damaged.

Not paralyzed.

Not scarred beyond repair.

**Absent.**

The structures that once produced her voice no longer exist.

This explains the complete lack of sound despite her respiratory system remaining functional.

Corin can breathe.

She can swallow.

She can cough.

But she can no longer produce a conventional human voice.

---

# **THE EXAMINATION**

Curious Light initially believed the problem might involve severe damage to Corin's existing vocal structures.

The examination proved otherwise.

There were no functional vocal cords to examine.

The tissues simply weren't there.

Corin was shown the results afterward.

She stared at the report for a long time.

Then she wrote:

> **"So they're just gone."**

Guiding Light nodded.

> **"Yes."**

Corin wrote:

> **"Can you put them back?"**

There was a long silence.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "I don't know."

---

# **WHY GUIDING LIGHT WILL NOT RECONSTRUCT THEM**

Theoretically, an Architect could attempt to recreate the missing structures.

Practically, Corin is an extraordinarily poor candidate for experimental reconstruction.

Her body is already sustained through an abnormal Ether-based state.

Her biological systems no longer operate normally.

Her body has demonstrated unusual self-repair.

Her limbs require stitching to maintain their physical configuration.

Her memories and perception have previously been affected by Ether concentration.

Her entire existence is balanced between biological death and continued anomalous function.

Reconstructing an essential biological structure without understanding what originally happened could destabilize the entire system.

Guiding Light refuses to gamble with Corin's existence.

---

# **THE COUGHING**

The finding also explains one unusual detail.

Corin can still cough despite having no vocal cords.

Coughing does not require vocalization.

Her respiratory system can still force air through the throat.

The resulting pressure occasionally expels fluid Ether.

The Ether itself does not harm her.

The coughing motion, however, causes discomfort around the scarred area of her neck.

Corin's previous statement remains accurate:

> **"My neck hurts when I cough."**

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

Corin initially seemed more disturbed by the simplicity of the answer than the severity of it.

There was no complicated disease.

No hidden infection.

No mysterious paralysis.

One piece of her body was simply missing.

She wrote:

> **"Another thing gone."**

Orphie sat beside her.

Corin continued writing.

> **"I keep losing pieces of myself."**

Orphie did not answer immediately.

She eventually said:

> **"You're still here."**

Corin looked at her notebook.

Then wrote:

> **"I know."**

---

# **THE ARCHITECTS**

The discovery affected all three Architects differently.

### **Guiding Light**

Frustrated.

She can do so much.

Yet she cannot safely give Corin something as simple as a voice.

### **Curious Light**

Quiet.

For once, the discovery provided more questions than answers.

Where did the vocal cords go?

When were they lost?

Were they destroyed?

Were they consumed by Ether?

Were they removed during the experiments?

Or did Corin's reanimation simply determine that they were unnecessary?

No surviving records provide an answer.

### **Mischevious Light**

Angry.

Not at Corin.

At whoever made her body this way.

He did not say much.

He simply remained nearby.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

That night, Corin wrote:

> **"I don't remember what my voice sounded like."**

She stopped.

Then:

> **"I remember that I had one."**

Another pause.

> **"That's worse."**

She closed the notebook.

---

# **CURRENT MEDICAL STATUS**

Corin remains stable.

**Vocal cords:** Absent.

**Voice:** Impossible through conventional vocalization.

**Respiration:** Functional.

**Swallowing:** Functional.

**Cough reflex:** Functional.

**Ether expulsion:** Occasional, benign, non-depleting.

**Neck discomfort:** Present during coughing.

**Reconstruction:** Not currently considered safe.

**Cause of vocal-cord absence:** Unknown.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

There is no cure currently available.

There is not even a confirmed explanation.

Corin's silence is not psychological.

It is not voluntary.

It is not temporary paralysis.

Her vocal cords are simply no longer there.

And for the foreseeable future, the girl in the red beanie will communicate through paper and ink.

She writes.

Everyone reads.

She gestures.

Everyone watches.

She coughs.

Everyone waits until the discomfort passes.

And despite everything that has disappeared from her body, Corin remains.
