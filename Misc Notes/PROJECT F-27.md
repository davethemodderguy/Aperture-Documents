**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal Humor Archive

## **PROPOSAL**

Following the previous rejection of Project F-12, Chief Engineer Neko submitted a revised proposal.

**Proposal:**

> Develop a dedicated, compact flamethrower for KCA Executive Secretary Cinder.

Attached to the proposal was a handwritten note.

> "I made it smaller."

---

She read it.

Then wrote:

When asked why, Cinder shrugged.

> "...Fire is useful."

Neko enthusiastically recorded the approval.

## **ENGINEERING DISCUSSION**

The review board revisited archived combat footage.

The recordings showed Cinder fighting after being separated from her KCA briefcase.

Her behavior became noticeably more aggressive, relying on relentless close-quarters attacks until every hostile had been neutralized.

One reviewer looked away from the screen.

Another quietly slid Neko's proposal back across the table.

## **FINAL VOTE**

- Neko

## **PROJECT STATUS**

**REJECTED**

**Reason:**

> _"Subject already demonstrates exceptional combat capability. Additional incendiary equipment is not operationally justified, particularly given documented behavioral changes during prolonged hand-to-hand engagements after disarmament."_
