### **RESTRICTED SAFEHOUSE / PERSONNEL STATUS RECORD**

# **CORIN & ORPHIE — THE FORGOTTEN TREEHOUSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Sensitive  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Subjects:** Corin / Orphie  
**Related Personnel:** Lumi / Guiding Light / Curious Light  
**Status:** **ACTIVE / SOCIALLY ISOLATED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's recent Ether-related cognitive disturbances have stabilized.

Her memories are no longer actively deteriorating.

Her perception has stopped progressively changing.

This should have been considered good news.

It wasn't enough.

Corin remains extremely unstable in nearly every other aspect of her existence.

Her body remains dependent on stitches.

Her vision remains entirely monochromatic.

Her emotional state remains fragile.

And the isolation surrounding the treehouse has become increasingly severe.

---

# **THE SUNFLOWER POT**

The sunflower pot Corin cared for was accidentally broken.

It was not destroyed intentionally.

There was no dramatic incident.

It simply slipped.

The pot struck the floor.

**Crack.**

The sunflower fell with it.

Corin stood completely still.

She stared at the broken pieces.

For several seconds, she said nothing.

Then:

> **Corin:**  
> "...Again."

Orphie looked at her.

> **"Corin—"**

> **"Everything keeps breaking."**

---

# **ANOTHER REMINDER**

The broken pot became another reminder of everything Corin had already lost.

The flower itself could be replaced.

The pot could be repaired.

But Corin did not see an ordinary accident.

She saw another piece of her life that had failed to remain intact.

Her eyes remained fixed on the shattered ceramic.

> **"Mom gave this to me."**

Orphie remembered.

> **"I know."**

Corin crouched down.

She carefully touched one of the pieces.

> **"...She doesn't come anymore."**

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi has stopped visiting the treehouse.

There has been no new confrontation.

No final argument.

No explanation sufficient to satisfy Corin.

She simply stopped coming.

The days became weeks.

The weeks became months.

Corin eventually stopped asking when Lumi would return.

That silence hurt more than the questions ever did.

---

# **THE BLACKSITE**

Far away from the treehouse, life inside the Blacksite continued.

Personnel changed shifts.

Projects continued.

New anomalies arrived.

Old incidents became archived records.

People moved on.

And slowly, almost imperceptibly, **Corin and Orphie became less present in people's minds.**

Not erased.

Not deliberately forgotten.

Simply... absent.

They were too far away.

Too rarely seen.

Too disconnected from ordinary Blacksite life.

Their names appeared less often in conversation.

Their visits became nonexistent.

Their presence became something people vaguely remembered.

---

# **THE FORGOTTEN FAMILY**

Corin eventually noticed.

> **Corin:**  
> "...Do people remember us?"

Orphie didn't answer immediately.

> **"Some do."**

> **"Some?"**

Orphie looked away.

> **"...Yeah."**

Corin nodded.

She seemed strangely unsurprised.

> **"I thought so."**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie remains with Corin.

She is still the person Corin sees most often.

She repairs her stitches.

She stays when Corin becomes unstable.

She eats with her.

She sleeps in the same treehouse.

She remembers the things Corin is afraid of forgetting.

But even Orphie cannot completely replace the outside world.

And the outside world is gradually becoming distant.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light remains aware of them.

She has not forgotten.

She cannot forget.

Her presence is sometimes the only indication that the rest of reality still remembers the two girls exist.

A soft blue glow occasionally appears beside the broken flowerpot.

Not repairing it.

Not changing anything.

Just quietly illuminating it.

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT**

Curious Light continues visiting when he can.

Unlike most Blacksite personnel, he remains interested in Corin and Orphie.

However, even his visits have become less frequent due to his responsibilities elsewhere.

He notices the change.

The treehouse is quieter.

The girls are more isolated.

The outside world feels increasingly distant.

He does not like it.

But he also understands that simply appearing occasionally cannot solve the problem.

---

# **CORIN'S STATE**

Corin has stopped expecting people to remember her.

That may be more concerning than when she was asking why they left.

She no longer waits by the window.

She no longer listens for footsteps.

She no longer asks whether Lumi is coming.

She simply continues existing.

One evening, she looked toward the broken sunflower pot.

> **Corin:**  
> "...Maybe this is what happens."

Orphie looked at her.

> **"What?"**

> **"People leave."**

A pause.

> **"Things break."**

> **"And eventually nobody remembers they were ever there."**

Orphie moved closer.

> **"I remember."**

Corin looked at her.

> **"...You're the only one who does."**

> **"No."**

Orphie shook her head.

> **"I'm just the one who's here."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's mind has stopped deteriorating.

Her memories are no longer being overwritten by Ether.

But remembering everything does not necessarily make things easier.

She remembers Lumi.

She remembers the treehouse.

She remembers the sunflower.

She remembers what it felt like when people still visited.

And now she remembers their absence.

The sunflower pot can be replaced.

The flower can be replanted.

The broken ceramic can be cleaned up.

But none of those things change the larger truth:

**Lumi stopped coming.**

And far away, in the Blacksite, life continued without Corin and Orphie.

They were not erased.

They were not rejected by everyone.

They were simply becoming distant enough that people stopped thinking about them.

The treehouse remains standing at the edge of the forest.

Two girls still live inside.

One is barely holding herself together.

The other refuses to leave her.

And beyond the trees, the world continues moving forward—

**slowly forgetting that they are still there.**
