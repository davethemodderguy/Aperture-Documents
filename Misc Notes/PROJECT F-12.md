> "A flamethrower seems appropriate."

## **ENGINEERING REVIEW**

To support the proposal, Neko attached several combat clips of Cinder.

The board watched.

Clip One.

Cinder with her briefcase.

Professional.

Precise.

Methodical.

Clip Two.

The briefcase was taken away.

Cinder quietly put on her gauntlets.

Then proceeded to eliminate an entire hostile squad using walls, floors, doors, concrete pillars, and one unfortunate vending machine.

The room remained silent.

Neko slowly reached for the proposal.

Miss Neko took it first.

She stamped it.

**DENIED**

In large red letters.

## **PROJECT STATUS**

**Rejected.**

Reason recorded:

> _"Subject already possesses sufficient destructive capability without additional incendiary equipment."_

A handwritten note was later added underneath.

No signature accompanied it.

> _"If Cinder ever asks for a flamethrower..."_

> _"...pretend you didn't hear her."_
