### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE – BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION LOG**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – OUTDOOR SLEEPING HABIT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Since her physiological recovery, Corin Lockwood has developed an unusual sleeping habit.

Despite having a room within Project SAFEHOUSE, Corin is frequently found sleeping outdoors.

Medical evaluation has determined that this behavior is psychological rather than physiological.

She is capable of sleeping comfortably indoors.

She simply often chooses not to.

---

# **OBSERVATIONS**

Corin has been found sleeping in numerous locations around the Blacksite grounds, including:

- Beneath snow-covered trees.
    
- On grassy hills overlooking the artificial lake.
    
- Inside botanical gardens.
    
- On rooftops with authorized access.
    
- Against the trunk of large trees within the nature preserve.
    

She carries only a blanket and a pillow.

No tent.

No shelter.

No additional equipment.

---

# **ENVIRONMENTAL TOLERANCE**

Corin demonstrates an unusually high resistance to environmental conditions.

Medical testing confirms that she tolerates extreme cold, heat, wind, and prolonged exposure significantly better than an ordinary human.

Researchers believe this is a lasting consequence of the alterations to her physiology.

Even so, Medical Services emphasize that she is **not** invulnerable.

She now requires food, water, oxygen, and sleep like any other person.

Prolonged exposure, injury, dehydration, or exhaustion can still endanger her health.

Personnel are instructed not to assume she is immune simply because she appears comfortable.

---

# **WHEN ASKED**

During one evening patrol, a security officer found Corin asleep beneath a tree during light snowfall.

The officer gently woke her.

> "You know you've got a room inside."

Corin looked up at the night sky.

After a long pause, she quietly answered,

> "...It's quieter out here."

The officer asked,

> "Do you want me to walk you back?"

Corin smiled politely.

> "...Not yet."

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi eventually found Corin asleep beneath the same tree.

She quietly spread another blanket over her daughter before sitting nearby.

When Corin woke sometime later, she noticed the extra blanket.

She immediately knew who had placed it there.

Without opening her eyes, she murmured,

> "...Thanks, Mom."

Lumi smiled.

> "You always liked falling asleep under the stars."

Corin gave a small nod.

> "...Back then..."

> "...I felt safe."

Lumi looked up at the night sky.

> "One day..."

> "...I hope you feel that way again."

---

# **SAFEHOUSE**

The residents gradually accepted that Corin occasionally preferred sleeping outside.

Belle checks that she has a warm blanket before bed.

Bao quietly leaves a thermos of tea near the garden entrance.

Kent makes an extra round outside before turning in for the night.

Mart has been known to sit nearby with a book until Corin falls asleep.

No one forces her back inside.

They simply make sure she knows she doesn't have to be alone.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Medical Services continue encouraging Corin to use her room at Project SAFEHOUSE whenever possible.

However, they recognize that her choice to sleep outdoors is currently tied to emotional comfort rather than physical necessity.

As long as her safety is monitored and she remains medically stable, staff have been advised to support the habit without judgment while continuing to help Corin rebuild a sense of security indoors.
