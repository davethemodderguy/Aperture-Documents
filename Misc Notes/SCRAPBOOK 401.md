# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / ABSENCE REPORT**

# **ORPHIE — BLACKSITE ABSENCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Missing Personnel Concern  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Marts / Luna / Solaria  
**Primary Residence:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **UNRESOLVED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie has stopped coming to the Blacksite.

Previously, she regularly visited the facility for work, social interaction, and time with the SAFEHOUSE residents.

That has now ceased.

No formal leave request was submitted.

No transfer was requested.

No explanation was provided.

She simply stopped coming.

---

# **THE CHANGE**

At first, her absence appeared ordinary.

One missed visit.

Then another.

Then several.

Eventually, personnel realized that Orphie had not been seen inside the main Blacksite for an extended period.

Her access credentials remain valid.

No security violation has been recorded.

There is no indication that she has been forcibly prevented from entering.

She has simply chosen not to.

---

# **REMOTE TREEHOUSE**

Orphie remains associated with the remote treehouse within the Blacksite perimeter forest.

This makes the situation particularly difficult.

She has not disappeared from the Blacksite perimeter entirely.

She has instead withdrawn further into the isolated area where she and Corin live.

The forest provides privacy.

It also provides very little opportunity for spontaneous social interaction.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin remains the primary person physically capable of checking on Orphie regularly.

However, her work at the KCA frequently keeps her away from the treehouse until late.

This has become increasingly concerning.

Corin can no longer simply rely on Orphie encountering someone at the Blacksite during the day.

If Orphie chooses to remain home, she may spend most of the day alone.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE REACTION**

The SAFEHOUSE residents noticed immediately.

Star was among the first to ask:

> "...Where's Orphie?"

Nobody had a satisfactory answer.

Bao became concerned.

Frost quietly asked whether she was sick.

Belle attempted to visit.

Endmin discouraged everyone from overwhelming her.

Astral summarized the situation:

> "...She's withdrawing further."

Nightshade said nothing.

She simply continued watching the entrance whenever someone arrived.

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts are particularly confused.

They can still safely approach Orphie.

They want to.

But they cannot simply make her return to the Blacksite.

Several Marts have accompanied Corin toward the treehouse.

Others have remained at the SAFEHOUSE.

The colony appears to be divided between:

> **"Stay with Orphie."**

and

> **"Wait for Orphie."**

---

# **LUNA**

When Luna manifested, she was asked why Orphie had stopped visiting.

Luna answered:

> "...She doesn't want to be there."

> "Why?"

> "...She doesn't want to be anywhere."

Luna paused.

> "...Not because she hates everyone."

> "...She just doesn't care anymore."

---

# **SOLARIA**

Solaria gave a similar assessment.

> "...She's retreating."

The researcher asked whether Orphie was avoiding the SAFEHOUSE.

> "...Yes."

"Does she hate them?"

> "...No."

> "...That's the problem."

---

# **ABSENCE OF EMOTIONAL MOTIVATION**

Orphie's withdrawal does not appear motivated by anger or resentment.

She has not complained about the SAFEHOUSE.

She has not accused anyone of mistreating her.

She has not severed relationships formally.

She simply does not appear to have a reason to leave the treehouse.

This is consistent with her broader emotional detachment.

---

# **BLACKSITE ACCESS**

Security has confirmed:

- Orphie's access credentials remain active.
    
- No access restrictions were placed on her.
    
- No disciplinary action was issued.
    
- No security incident forced her departure.
    
- No evidence indicates that she was removed from the Blacksite.
    

Her absence is voluntary.

---

# **CORIN'S CONCERN**

Corin has attempted to encourage Orphie to visit.

The responses have remained minimal.

> **Corin:** "...Want to come with me tomorrow?"

> **Orphie:** "...No."

> **Corin:** "...The others miss you."

> **Orphie:** "...I know."

> **Corin:** "...Don't you miss them?"

Orphie paused.

> "...I don't know."

Corin stopped asking.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

The treehouse has become noticeably quieter.

Orphie spends much of her time there.

The sketchbook remains unused.

The cookbook remains sealed.

The broken pencil remains in Corin's possession.

The Marts remain nearby.

And Orphie remains emotionally distant from all of it.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Orphie:**  
No longer visiting the Blacksite. Increasingly isolated. Severe emotional detachment and ongoing sleep deprivation.

**Corin:**  
Maintaining contact and monitoring her when possible.

**Marts:**  
Concerned. Remaining near Orphie when possible.

**SAFEHOUSE:**  
Concerned and missing her presence.

**Luna:**  
Concerned.

**Solaria:**  
Concerned.

**Security:**  
No indication of forced absence.

**Welfare concern:** **SEVERE.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie used to visit the Blacksite because there were people there.

Friends.

Family.

Marts.

Work.

A kitchen.

A place to draw.

A reason to leave the treehouse.

Now she has stopped coming.

The doors remain open to her.

Her access remains valid.

People are waiting.

The Marts are waiting.

Corin is waiting.

But Orphie stays in the forest.

Not because she is hiding from anyone.

Not because she wants anyone to suffer.

She simply does not seem to see a reason to come back.

**End of Personnel Welfare / Absence Report**
