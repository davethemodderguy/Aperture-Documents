# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **SECURITY DIVISION – RESEARCH ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: CAMERA MART RECLASSIFICATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Security & Research Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following months of comparative observations, Research Division has confirmed the identity of one of the five Elder Marts.

The confirmed individual is...

> **Camera Mart.**

Unlike previous identification attempts, Camera Mart's permanently assigned position within the KCA Security Control Room allowed researchers to continuously observe subtle physical characteristics over an extended period.

The conclusion has now been independently verified by multiple researchers.

---

# **IDENTIFICATION**

The following characteristics distinguish Camera Mart from ordinary Marts:

- Slightly larger body.
    
- Marginally more opaque coloration.
    
- A subtly deeper blue appearance under direct lighting.
    

These differences remain almost imperceptible.

However, because Camera Mart rarely leaves the Security Terminal except during maintenance or emergencies, researchers were finally able to study the same individual long enough to reach a definitive conclusion.

---

# **SIGNIFICANCE**

Camera Mart is now officially recognized as:

> **Elder Mart #1**

The identities of the remaining four Elder Marts remain unknown.

No evidence suggests Camera Mart possesses abilities beyond those of any other Mart.

Its exceptional observational skills are believed to result from years of experience rather than any unique Elder trait.

---

# **REACTION**

Upon being informed of the discovery, Camera Mart looked up from Monitor 6.

It blinked once.

Then calmly replied:

> "Okay."

It immediately returned to watching the security cameras.

Research noted no measurable change in behavior.

---

# **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

**Dialyn**

She walked into the Security Control Room after hearing the news.

Camera Mart looked up.

> "Hello!"

Dialyn smiled.

> "...Congratulations?"

Camera Mart tilted its head.

> "Thank you."

Then pointed toward Monitor 12.

> "Delivery."

A courier had just arrived at Reception.

---

**KCA Ambassador**

When informed that Camera Mart had finally been identified as an Elder, the Ambassador simply nodded.

> "Yes."

Researchers collectively stared.

> "...You knew?"

> "Of course."

> "...Why didn't you tell us?"

The Ambassador smiled politely.

> "You didn't ask the right Mart."

The interview was immediately concluded.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Camera Mart remains stationed at the KCA Security Terminal, continuing to monitor the facility exactly as it always has.

Its reclassification as an Elder Mart has not altered its responsibilities in any way.

It still watches the cameras.

It still alerts Security to anything unusual.

It still proudly wears its miniature KCA Security cap.

And every morning, before beginning another shift, it adjusts the tiny hat atop its own tiny black top hat and cheerfully announces:

> "Ready."
