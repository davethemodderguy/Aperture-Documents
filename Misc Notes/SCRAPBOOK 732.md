## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / PERSONNEL SAFETY DIRECTIVE**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — LIFE TRANSFER PREVENTION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Directive Status:** **PERMANENT**

---

# **IMMEDIATE DIRECTIVE**

Following confirmation of Alice Lockwood's life-transfer ability, all deceased or critically deteriorated organisms within Alice's accessible environment are to be:

**1. Removed from her sight immediately.**

**2. Transferred to an approved disposal or containment area.**

**3. Incinerated when appropriate.**

**4. Otherwise secured where Alice cannot access or observe them.**

This directive applies to:

- Dead plants
    
- Dying plants
    
- Dead animals
    
- Severely injured organisms
    
- Biological waste
    
- Any other organism capable of triggering Alice's life-transfer response
    

No exceptions are to be made without C.O.R.E. authorization.

---

# **REASON**

Alice does not understand the value of her own life.

She understands the value of **other life.**

That combination is dangerous.

If Alice sees something dying, her instinct is not:

> _"Someone should help it."_

Her instinct is:

> **"Alice can help."**

And she will attempt to pay the cost herself.

---

# **PERSONNEL WARNING**

Do **not** present Alice with dying organisms.

Do **not** ask:

> "Can you save it?"

Do **not** encourage her ability.

Do **not** praise her for sacrificing herself.

Do **not** allow her to believe that using her life force is an acceptable way to solve a problem.

If Alice discovers a dying organism, remove it **before she attempts intervention.**

---

# **SAFEHOUSE PROCEDURE**

If Alice notices a dead plant or organism:

**STEP 1:** Calmly redirect Alice.

**STEP 2:** Remove the organism from her immediate surroundings.

**STEP 3:** Do not explain that it could potentially be revived using her life force.

**STEP 4:** Reassure Alice that someone else will handle it.

**STEP 5:** Monitor Alice afterward for attempted life-transfer.

If Alice has already begun using the ability:

**STOP THE TRANSFER IMMEDIATELY.**

Her safety takes priority.

---

# **CORIN'S RULE**

Corin summarized the entire directive in one sentence:

> **"Alice doesn't get to die saving something else."**

Everyone agreed.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi has become particularly vigilant around plants.

Any dying flowers near the Treehouse are removed before Alice notices them.

Lumi initially felt terrible about throwing away plants.

Then she remembered what happened with the flower pot.

She stopped hesitating.

> **"I'm not letting her spend herself on a flower."**

---

# **BELLE**

Belle has taken responsibility for checking the exterior grounds.

If she finds a dead plant, it disappears.

If Alice asks where it went:

> **"We took care of it."**

Nothing more.

Belle refuses to let Alice interpret a dead organism as something she personally needs to fix.

---

# **NEKO**

Neko has modified the SAFEHOUSE waste systems.

Biological waste is now routed away from Alice's accessible areas.

The Treehouse's surrounding grounds are also periodically inspected.

Neko's reasoning:

> **"If the problem can be prevented mechanically, prevent it mechanically."**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie was unusually direct.

> **"She's already spent enough of herself."**

No further explanation was required.

---

# **FINAL PRIORITY**

There is no shortage of life in the Blacksite.

There is no shortage of people willing to help.

There is no reason Alice should have to sacrifice herself to save something else.

The objective is therefore simple:

> **Keep Alice alive.**

> **Keep Alice healthy.**

> **Keep Alice safe.**

> **Keep things that would tempt her to sacrifice herself away from her.**

Everyone wants Alice alive and well.

**Keep it that way.**
