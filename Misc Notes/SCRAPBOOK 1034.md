**SUBJECT:** Neri — Voluntary Plush Form

---

## Executive Summary

Neri has developed the ability to voluntarily transform herself into a small, plush-bodied form.

She appears to have created the form primarily because:

> **It is comfortable.**

And because Alice apparently likes having a tiny seal-teddy to carry around.

The resulting form resembles a **seal/teddy-bear hybrid plushie**, approximately teddy-bear sized.

Neri permits selected SAFEHOUSE residents—most notably Alice—to handle and carry her while in this form.

She is fully aware of her surroundings throughout the transformation.

---

## Plush Form Characteristics

When transformed, Neri's physical structure changes substantially.

### Musculoskeletal Conversion

Normal muscle tissue is replaced with a cotton-like internal material.

Her exterior skin becomes soft fabric.

The fabric is:

- waterproof;
    
- resistant to ordinary staining;
    
- compatible with Neri's normal eating and drinking;
    
- noticeably softer than her ordinary body.
    

Neri can therefore eat or drink while transformed without leaving ordinary food or beverage stains on herself.

This has resulted in the following observation:

> Neri can apparently drink coffee while being a plushie.

C.O.R.E. has decided that this is somehow perfectly consistent with her personality.

---

## Physical Appearance

The transformation reduces Neri to approximately teddy-bear size.

Her resulting form retains recognizable characteristics of Neri while adopting a deliberately plush-like appearance.

**Classification:**

> **SEAL + TEDDY BEAR HYBRID**

The ears, body proportions, soft construction, and general silhouette give the form a distinctly stuffed-animal appearance.

Personnel are reminded that this is still **Neri**.

Not a toy.

Not merchandise.

Not a facility mascot.

And absolutely not property.

---

## Mobility

Neri cannot independently move her limbs while transformed.

She cannot:

- walk;
    
- run;
    
- climb;
    
- gesture;
    
- pick things up;
    
- operate equipment.
    

She can, however, **move around through external handling**.

In practical terms:

> Neri cannot move herself.

> People can move Neri.

She remains conscious and aware while being carried.

---

## Sensory Changes

The plush form produces several unusual physiological effects.

### Pain

**Complete pain numbness.**

Neri does not experience ordinary pain while transformed.

This does **not** mean the form is invulnerable.

### Impact / Falls

The plush body has substantial resistance to ordinary impacts and falls.

A dropped Neri is therefore substantially less likely to sustain conventional impact injury than normal Neri.

However:

> **“Resistant to impact” does not mean “throwable.”**

Personnel are specifically prohibited from throwing Neri.

### Anemia

Neri's normal anemia does not occur while the plush form is active.

This is classified as:

> **ANEMIC IMMUNITY**

Her blood-related instability is temporarily suppressed for the duration of the transformation.

---

## Critical Limitation

Despite the form's advantages, Neri's internal organs remain insufficiently protected.

The plush exterior is soft.

The underlying vital structures are **not adequately shielded**.

Therefore, sufficiently severe trauma can still kill her.

This creates an unusual situation:

> Neri cannot feel the injury.

> Neri can survive impacts that would normally hurt her.

> Neri can still be seriously injured or killed.

Personnel must therefore **not use her lack of pain response as an indication of safety.**

---

# Duration

Neri can sustain the plush form for approximately:

> **5 HOURS**

After reaching the form's limit, the transformation is forcibly reversed.

The return to normal form is automatic.

Neri cannot simply remain a plush indefinitely.

---

# Alice Interaction Record

Alice is currently the resident most frequently observed handling Neri in plush form.

Typical sequence:

Alice finds Neri.

Neri transforms.

Alice picks her up.

Neri gets carried around.

Alice occasionally places Neri somewhere comfortable.

Neri remains there.

No conversation is necessary.

On one occasion, Alice placed Neri on a couch.

Neri remained completely motionless.

Alice stared at her.

> **Alice:** “You're really soft.”

Neri's plush body did not move.

> **Alice:** “Can I hug you?”

A very small nod.

Alice hugged her.

Neri remained still.

## Jane Doe Interaction

Jane has also been authorized to carry Neri in plush form.

However, the existing transportation restrictions remain in effect.

**DO NOT:**

- jump while carrying Neri;
    
- spin while carrying Neri;
    
- swing Neri around;
    
- throw Neri;
    
- use Neri as a projectile;
    
- treat Neri like a basketball.
    

This is particularly important because Neri cannot move herself to correct an unsafe position.

Bao's existing instruction remains active:

> **“Please don't jump or spin when Neri is on your back.”**

The instruction applies regardless of Neri's current form.

---

## Communication While Plush

Neri retains consciousness and communication ability.

However, because she cannot move her limbs, her usual gestures become unavailable.

She can still vocalize when necessary.

Her speech remains uncommon.

For example:

> **Alice:** “Are you comfortable?”

> **Neri:** “Mhm.”

> **Alice:** “Want me to move you?”

> **Neri:** “No.”

> **Alice:** “Okay.”

Neri remains where she is.

---

## C.O.R.E. Handling Directive

When Neri is in plush form:

**DO:**

- handle gently;
    
- place her somewhere safe;
    
- respect her consent regarding handling;
    
- monitor the five-hour transformation limit;
    
- remember that she is still medically vulnerable despite reduced pain sensitivity.
    

**DO NOT:**

- throw her;
    
- kick her;
    
- toss her onto furniture;
    
- spin her around;
    
- use her as a pillow without permission;
    
- assume “plushie” means “indestructible.”
    

---

## Final Assessment

Neri's plush form is unusual even by SAFEHOUSE standards.

It gives her:

- reduced physical size;
    
- increased softness;
    
- impact and fall resistance;
    
- complete pain numbness;
    
- temporary immunity to her anemia;
    
- waterproof fabric;
    
- freedom from ordinary staining while eating or drinking.
    

In exchange, she loses voluntary physical movement and remains vulnerable to sufficiently severe internal injury.

The form lasts approximately five hours.

Most importantly:

> **Neri chose this form.**

She appears comfortable with it.

She permits Alice to carry her.

She occasionally allows other trusted residents to do the same.

C.O.R.E. therefore recognizes the form as a legitimate voluntary transformation rather than a medical emergency or anomalous containment event.
