## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SECURITY / EXTERNAL THREAT RESPONSE DIVISION**

# **SPAWN — BLACKSITE PERIMETER INCURSIONS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Restricted / Active Hostile Threat  
**Threat Designation:** **SPAWN**  
**Status:** **ACTIVE INCURSIONS**  
**Primary Area:** Heaven's Blacksite Perimeter

---

# **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

Following confirmation of the **SPAWN** organization, multiple suspected SPAWN members have begun appearing around the outer perimeter of **Heaven's Blacksite**.

The individuals have not attempted conventional communication.

Most observed groups have attempted to approach restricted perimeter areas before being intercepted by Blacksite security.

The majority have been forced to retreat under defensive fire.

Several groups have fled into the surrounding terrain.

One fleeing group abandoned a weapon during their retreat.

The weapon has since been confiscated and transferred to C.O.R.E. for examination.

---

# **FIRST CONTACT**

The initial encounters were reported by perimeter security personnel following sightings of unidentified individuals carrying unfamiliar weapons.

Personnel observed individuals:

- Moving in small groups.
    
- Remaining outside established security boundaries.
    
- Attempting to approach perimeter infrastructure.
    
- Carrying equipment inconsistent with conventional civilian weaponry.
    
- Wearing equipment displaying visual similarities to previously recovered SPAWN material.
    

When challenged, the groups generally refused to comply.

Security forces subsequently engaged them.

Most encounters lasted less than several minutes.

No SPAWN group has successfully penetrated the Blacksite perimeter.

---

# **HOSTILE EQUIPMENT**

The most significant discovery from the encounters is the weaponry carried by SPAWN members.

The confiscated weapon has been provisionally designated:

> **SPAWN-DMEIR-01**

### **DARK MATTER ENERGY IMPULSE RIFLE**

The weapon is an advanced energy-based firearm utilizing a form of energy currently classified as **Dark Matter Energy**.

The exact mechanism remains under investigation.

Unlike conventional firearms, the weapon does not appear to rely upon conventional chemical propellant or standard ammunition.

Its firing system produces a concentrated energy impulse capable of causing substantial damage to test materials.

---

# **CONFISCATED WEAPON**

The weapon was recovered after a SPAWN group attempted to flee the perimeter.

During the retreat, one member abandoned their rifle.

Security personnel secured the weapon before the group could return for it.

The weapon was transported directly to a controlled C.O.R.E. laboratory.

It has since been placed under armed containment.

---

# **INITIAL OBSERVATIONS**

The rifle possesses several characteristics inconsistent with conventional weapon platforms.

The weapon appears deliberately engineered for field use.

Its construction suggests that SPAWN personnel are not simply using improvised anomalous technology.

The rifle is:

- Compact enough for individual operators.
    
- Durable.
    
- Designed for repeated use.
    
- Equipped with controls intended for trained users.
    
- Manufactured to a consistent standard.
    

This reinforces the previous C.O.R.E. assessment that SPAWN possesses access to an external technological resource.

---

# **WEAPONS SUPPLIER CONNECTION**

The confiscated Dark Matter Energy Impulse Rifle has significantly strengthened the investigation into the unidentified third party supplying SPAWN.

The organization previously demonstrated access to specialized weapons designed for hunting supernatural targets.

SPAWN-DMEIR-01 represents the clearest physical evidence yet that these capabilities are supported by an advanced weapons-production system.

However:

> **No manufacturer markings have been identified.**

There is currently no confirmed connection to any known Aperture subsidiary, government weapons program, or recognized anomalous organization.

The manufacturer remains designated:

> **UNKNOWN THIRD PARTY**

---

# **PERIMETER RESPONSE**

Blacksite security has been instructed to treat all suspected SPAWN personnel as hostile intruders.

Standard response procedures have been modified.

### **Personnel are authorized to:**

- Challenge unidentified individuals approaching restricted perimeter zones.
    
- Prevent unauthorized entry.
    
- Request immediate reinforcement when multiple SPAWN members are detected.
    
- Recover abandoned equipment where safe.
    
- Preserve all recovered SPAWN materials for C.O.R.E. investigation.
    

Personnel are **not** authorized to pursue fleeing groups beyond the established perimeter without specific authorization.

---

# **ALICE SECURITY CONSIDERATIONS**

The timing of these incursions is considered highly concerning.

Alice has only recently been recovered from the grave site associated with her mother.

SPAWN has subsequently begun appearing near the Blacksite.

Whether these events are directly connected remains unknown.

However, given Alice's demonstrated fear response to the SPAWN symbol, C.O.R.E. has classified the possibility of a connection as a **high-priority concern**.

Alice's location and recovery status remain restricted information.

Under no circumstances are perimeter personnel to disclose:

- Alice's presence within the Blacksite.
    
- Her current medical location.
    
- Her recovery status.
    
- Her identity as a potential SPAWN target.
    

---

# **ALICE'S REACTION**

Following the first perimeter reports, Alice was not informed of the encounters.

Nevertheless, when security personnel discussed the confiscated weapon in a nearby corridor, Alice reportedly overheard part of the conversation.

She became visibly tense.

When asked what was wrong, she quietly said:

> "...They found us?"

Personnel immediately ended the conversation within her hearing range.

Alice subsequently refused to discuss the matter.

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

SPAWN is no longer considered an organization operating at a distant or theoretical level.

They are now:

> **At the Blacksite perimeter.**

Their personnel have demonstrated the willingness to approach Aperture-controlled territory.

They possess advanced weapons.

They are searching for something.

Whether that objective is:

- Alice,
    
- another anomalous entity,
    
- a specific individual,
    
- information,
    
- or something else entirely
    

remains unknown.

The confiscation of the Dark Matter Energy Impulse Rifle provides C.O.R.E. with its first intact example of SPAWN weapon technology.

Analysis is ongoing.

Until the weapon's origin and purpose are understood, all recovered SPAWN technology is to be treated as potentially anomalous.

---

# **FINAL SECURITY NOTICE**

A perimeter breach has not occurred.

No SPAWN member has entered the Blacksite.

However, the repeated appearances demonstrate that the organization is willing to approach the facility despite its security.

The next group may not retreat.
