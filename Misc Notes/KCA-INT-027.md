# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **KCA-INT-027**

### **ARCHIVAL FOOTAGE REVIEW – CINDER (DECLASSIFIED TRAINING EXCERPT)**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Evaluation Archive

**Source:** Archived body camera footage and training assessments.

---

## **SUMMARY**

During a historical review of Executive Secretary **Cinder's** personnel file, investigators examined several recordings documenting incidents in which she was forcibly separated from her assigned Multi-Purpose Tactical Briefcase.

The footage demonstrated that loss of her primary weapon does **not** significantly reduce her combat effectiveness.

Instead, it triggers an immediate transition to close-quarters combat using reinforced gauntlets.

Personnel evaluating the footage consistently described the change as abrupt.

---

## **Behavioral Transition**

When operating with her briefcase, Cinder favors controlled engagements, distance management, and measured use of force.

Once disarmed, however, her priorities change.

Her objective becomes straightforward:

> **Neutralize the immediate threat as quickly as possible.**

She abandons ranged tactics in favor of aggressive close-quarters combat, relentlessly exploiting openings until the attacker can no longer continue the fight.

Training analysts noted her style incorporates elements commonly associated with:

- Boxing.
    
- Kickboxing.
    
- Clinch fighting.
    
- Wrestling-style takedowns.
    

Rather than relying on formal choreography, Cinder adapts continuously to her surroundings.

---

## **Archival Incident**

One reviewed recording showed an attacker forcing Cinder away from her briefcase during an executive protection mission.

She immediately equipped her gauntlets.

Over the following seconds, she drove the attacker backward into a reinforced concrete support pillar while preventing them from regaining control of their weapon.

The structural damage to the pillar was significant enough that engineers later ordered it demolished and replaced.

The attacker did not survive the encounter.

The reviewing board concluded that Cinder's actions were consistent with the life-threatening circumstances documented in the incident.

---

## **Background**

Subsequent interviews revealed that, before entering government service, Cinder participated in an **underground fight club** during her free time.

According to Cinder, the experience was never about fame or prize money.

It was an opportunity to test herself, improve her endurance, and learn to remain composed under pressure.

Those years contributed substantially to her confidence and adaptability in close-quarters combat.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Modern executive protection doctrine continues to emphasize that Cinder's briefcase is her preferred and most versatile tool.

However, training curricula also recognize that should she lose access to it, she remains a capable close-quarters combatant whose effectiveness stems not only from her equipment, but also from years of practical experience and disciplined preparation.

For this reason, recruits assigned to executive security details are reminded of a simple lesson recorded beneath the archived footage:

> **"Disarming Cinder does not end the fight."**

> **"It only changes the battlefield."**
