# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. DIVISION — SUBSPACE SAFETY REPORT

**SUBSPACE DESIGNATION:** NERI-01  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — RESIDENTIAL WING  
**PRIMARY USER:** Neri  
**STATUS:** HABITABLE / SAFETY HAZARD IDENTIFIED  
**HAZARD CLASS:** OUT OF BOUNDS

---

# 1. DISCOVERY

Neri-01 was initially considered a stable and relatively safe personal subspace.

This assessment was revised following an incident involving an Archivist.

The subspace's floating islands are surrounded by open space.

While this space is normally traversable, it has been discovered that the **usable region has boundaries.**

These boundaries are not visibly marked.

They do not resemble walls.

There is no obvious barrier.

A person can simply travel too far.

Or, more accurately:

> **Jump too hard.**

---

# 2. "OUT OF BOUNDS"

The term was coined by Neko.

The phenomenon occurs when an individual moves beyond the stable navigable region of Neri-01.

The individual does not collide with an invisible wall.

They simply continue moving.

There is no floor to return to.

There is no automatic gravity pulling them back.

There is no known mechanism that forces them toward one of the islands.

Once sufficiently far from the islands, the individual effectively becomes stranded in the surrounding subspace.

Neko's terminology has consequently become the official informal designation:

> **OUT OF BOUNDS**

---

# 3. THE FIRST SERIOUS INCIDENT

An Archivist entered Neri's room for routine documentation.

The Archivist was reportedly attempting to jump between two islands.

The jump was substantially stronger than intended.

The Archivist cleared the destination island.

They continued moving.

They subsequently passed beyond the normal navigable area.

At first, the incident was treated as amusing.

Then the Archivist did not return.

Then communications ceased.

Then a search was initiated.

---

# 4. SEARCH STATUS

The Archivist has **not been found.**

Multiple attempts have been made to locate them within Neri-01.

No confirmed visual contact has been established.

No reliable signal has been recovered.

No confirmed physical remains have been located.

The extent of the subspace beyond the known islands remains unknown.

Consequently, the possibility that the Archivist is:

- still drifting;
    
- stranded somewhere within the subspace;
    
- located in another connected subspace;
    
- or otherwise inaccessible
    

cannot currently be excluded.

The investigation remains open.

---

# 5. MISS NEKO'S EXPLANATION

During the subsequent safety briefing, an Archivist asked why the individual could not simply stop moving.

Miss Neko provided the following explanation:

> **"Just imagine you jumping in outer space, you'll keep floating until you die."**

The room reportedly became silent.

Miss Neko continued:

> **"Except we don't know how big this place is."**

No further questions were asked.

---

# 6. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri was extremely disturbed by the incident.

She had not intended for the subspace to become dangerous.

When informed that an Archivist had disappeared, her immediate response was:

> **"I didn't know."**

She subsequently spent a considerable amount of time examining the subspace boundaries.

Neri has begun attempting to solve the problem herself.

---

# 7. NERI'S PROPOSED SOLUTIONS

Neri has already considered several approaches.

### A. VISIBLE BOUNDARIES

Create a Light-based warning boundary around the safe region.

**Status:** Under development.

---

### B. AUTOMATIC RETURN

Cause anything leaving the safe region to automatically return to the nearest island.

**Status:** Theoretically possible.

Neri has not yet determined how to implement it safely.

---

### C. ARTIFICIAL GRAVITY

Create a localized force that gradually returns objects toward the islands.

**Status:** Experimental.

Neko has strongly advised against testing this by jumping into the void herself.

---

### D. FLIGHT LIMIT

Prevent Neri-01 users from gaining sufficient velocity to leave the safe region.

**Status:** Under consideration.

Neri reportedly dislikes this solution because:

> **"Then flying is less fun."**

---

### E. SUBSPACE WALL

Create an actual boundary at the edge of the usable region.

**Status:** Preferred solution.

The difficulty is that Neri does not currently perceive the boundary as a physical object.

She therefore has nothing obvious to construct a wall around.

---

# 8. THE MORE SERIOUS PROBLEM

The missing Archivist has demonstrated that the danger is not merely falling off an island.

There is no conventional "bottom."

There is no confirmed maximum distance.

There is no confirmed edge.

There is no confirmed method of returning once sufficiently far away.

The subspace therefore contains a potentially enormous volume of inaccessible space surrounding the areas Neri has intentionally constructed.

The islands are safe.

The **space between and beyond them** is not yet fully understood.

---

# 9. NERI'S CURRENT BEHAVIOR

Neri has become considerably more careful when moving through her subspace.

She now tends to:

- move slowly between islands;
    
- avoid high-speed jumps;
    
- use controlled hovering;
    
- keep visual contact with the islands;
    
- test new areas cautiously;
    
- and discourage others from experimenting with momentum.
    

This has created an amusing reversal.

Previously, everyone was worried about Neri accidentally breaking reality.

Now Neri is worried about everyone accidentally breaking themselves out of reality.

---

# 10. TEMPORARY SAFEHOUSE RULE

Until Neri develops a reliable solution:

> ### **NO HIGH-VELOCITY MOVEMENT INSIDE NERI-01.**

Specifically prohibited:

- launching oneself between islands;
    
- attempting maximum-distance jumps;
    
- racing;
    
- uncontrolled flight;
    
- throwing objects into the surrounding subspace;
    
- testing "how far it goes."
    

Neri has additionally requested that nobody attempt to find the missing Archivist by simply flying outward.

Her reasoning:

> **"Then we'll have two missing people."**

C.O.R.E. agrees.

---

# 11. GUIDING LIGHT'S ASSESSMENT

Guiding Light was initially surprised that the subspace possessed such an issue.

After examining the phenomenon, she reportedly concluded:

> **"She made more space than she meant to."**

Curious Light:

> **"Can you fix it?"**

Guiding Light:

> **"Probably."**

Curious Light:

> **"Then why aren't you?"**

Guiding Light:

> **"Neri should learn how her own space works."**

Curious Light:

> **"...Fair."**

Guiding Light subsequently offered to supervise Neri's attempts.

---

# 12. CURRENT STATUS

**NERI-01:** Stable.

**Constructed islands:** Stable.

**SAFEHOUSE entrance:** Stable.

**Neri's bedroom:** Stable.

**Out-of-Bounds region:** Undefined.

**Missing Archivist:** Not located.

**Cause:** Excessive momentum during inter-island movement.

**Known recovery method:** None confirmed.

**Neri:** Actively working on a solution.

---

## FINAL NOTE

Neri created her subspace because she wanted more room.

She successfully achieved that objective.

Perhaps too successfully.

The SAFEHOUSE now possesses a room containing floating islands, a chemistry laboratory, bookshelves, a bed, weapons, and enough open space that one sufficiently enthusiastic jump can result in someone leaving the **known universe of the room** entirely.

Neko has named this:

> **"Out of Bounds."**

Neri is currently trying to add invisible walls.

Everyone has been instructed to stop jumping really hard until she does.
