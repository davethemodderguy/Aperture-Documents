# **MEDICAL CONDITION**

## **Ether Regression Syndrome (ERS)**

Senior Manager Norma Lockwood has been diagnosed with **Ether Regression Syndrome (ERS)**, a rare chronic condition involving unstable interaction between Ether and the body's normal biological processes.

Current research indicates the Ether sustaining Norma's body is also the source of the condition itself.

As described in Medical Division notes:

> **"The Ether is simultaneously preserving her life while progressively damaging it."**

The exact mechanism remains under investigation by the CCD and KCA Medical Division.

---

# **SYMPTOMS**

Observed symptoms include:

- Sudden episodes of intense pain.
    
- Fatigue following severe episodes.
    
- Muscle stiffness.
    
- Temporary reduction in physical performance.
    
- Unpredictable onset with no consistent warning signs.
    

The episodes may occur during meetings, engineering work, training, or active operations.

Their frequency and duration vary considerably.

---

# **PAIN MANAGEMENT**

To manage acute episodes, Norma is authorized to carry a **medical emergency injector** containing physician-approved medication prescribed specifically for her condition.

When symptoms become severe, she self-administers the injector according to medical guidance to reduce the intensity of the episode until further treatment can be provided.

The injector is regarded as emergency medical equipment and is replaced after each use.

---

# **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION**

Norma rarely discusses her condition.

During an episode, she typically excuses herself quietly if circumstances allow.

If an episode occurs during an operation, she first ensures the immediate safety of those around her before attending to herself whenever possible.

Several colleagues have remarked that many people never realize she is in pain because of how composed she remains.

---

# **MEDICAL NOTE**

Despite the condition, Norma continues to meet operational standards for her assigned duties through regular medical evaluation and monitoring.

Her treatment plan is reviewed routinely by KCA Medical Services in coordination with the CCD's ongoing Ether research program.
