# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CELESTIAL ARCHITECTURE / PERSONNEL INTERACTION PROTOCOL**

# **THE ARCHITECTS — PHYSICAL INTERACTION GUIDELINES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Celestial Authority  
**Subjects:** Guiding Light / Curious Light  
**Status:** **FRIENDLY — INTERACTION PERMITTED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Despite their status as the two Architects who forged the foundations of reality and its realms, **Guiding Light and Curious Light are generally friendly toward ordinary personnel.**

Physical interaction is permitted.

This includes **hugs**.

Both Architects will generally accept a hug when approached appropriately.

Personnel should not allow their cosmic status to create unnecessary fear.

They are people.

Very powerful people.

But still people.

---

# **HUGGING GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light is particularly receptive to friendly physical affection.

If approached calmly and respectfully, she will generally allow someone to hug her.

She may return the hug.

Her spectral body is surprisingly comfortable to touch despite its ghostlike appearance.

The blue glow surrounding her tends to become slightly warmer during friendly interactions.

She has occasionally initiated hugs herself when someone clearly needs comfort.

---

# **HUGGING CURIOUS LIGHT**

Curious Light is also willing to accept hugs.

However, his response depends heavily on what he is currently doing.

If he is:

- Relaxing.
    
- Finished with a project.
    
- Socializing.
    
- Exploring.
    
- Simply standing around.
    

A hug is generally fine.

If he is:

- Building something.
    
- Researching.
    
- Concentrating.
    
- Testing an invention.
    
- Trying to understand something.
    

**Do not interrupt him just to hug him.**

He may accept the hug.

He may also stare at you for several seconds and ask:

> **"...Why?"**

---

# **THE IMPORTANT RULE**

There is one extremely important distinction:

> **Hugging an Architect is fine.**

> **Annoying an Architect is not.**

The two are friendly.

They are not endlessly patient.

Their status as Architects means that they possess abilities far beyond conventional understanding.

Among these abilities is the theoretical capability to **remove things from existence entirely**.

---

# **"REMOVE FROM EXISTENCE"**

This is not ordinary destruction.

An object destroyed normally still existed.

Something removed from existence by an Architect may simply cease to have ever been present in the relevant reality.

The exact limits of this ability remain unknown.

Personnel are therefore strongly advised **not to test it.**

---

# **PERSONNEL SAFETY**

Do not:

- Repeatedly poke an Architect.
    
- Interrupt their work unnecessarily.
    
- Steal their equipment.
    
- Deliberately provoke them.
    
- Repeatedly ask the same question after receiving an answer.
    
- Touch experimental devices without permission.
    
- Attempt to challenge them.
    
- Ask them to demonstrate their destructive capabilities.
    

Especially do not say:

> **"What are you going to do, erase me?"**

This has been officially classified as:

**An extremely stupid question.**

---

# **NEKO'S EXPERIENCE**

Neko has developed a particularly good relationship with Curious Light.

He has learned the difference between approaching him socially and interrupting him while working.

Neko once walked up to Curious Light and hugged him while he was standing idle.

Curious Light simply accepted it.

Later, Neko approached him while he was assembling an experimental device.

Curious Light immediately said:

> **"Not now."**

Neko backed away.

> **"Okay."**

Miss Neko, observing nearby, nodded approvingly.

> **"Good."**

---

# **MARTS' EXPERIENCE**

Marts initially hesitated before hugging Guiding Light.

> **Marts:**  
> "...Can I?"

Guiding Light opened her arms.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Of course."

Marts hugged her.

The floating fragments of Guiding Light's shattered wings gently shifted around them.

Marts eventually smiled.

> **Marts:**  
> "...You're soft."

Guiding Light laughed.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "I've been told."

---

# **LUMI'S EXPERIENCE**

Lumi does not appear to have any hesitation about hugging either Architect.

She has known them long enough that their cosmic status does not seem particularly important to her.

Curious Light has occasionally complained:

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Lumi."

> **Lumi:**  
> "What?"

> **Curious Light:**  
> "I'm working."

> **Lumi:**  
> "You can work later."

Curious Light sighed.

He did not remove her.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S ADVICE**

When asked how personnel should interact with the Architects, Guiding Light gave a simple answer:

> **"Treat us normally."**

A pause.

> **"Just don't be obnoxious."**

Curious Light added:

> **"Especially while I'm working."**

Guiding Light:

> **"...Especially while he's working."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The Architects are not untouchable beings.

They can be spoken to.

They can be joked with.

They can be thanked.

They can be hugged.

They may even initiate physical affection themselves.

Guiding Light will generally welcome it.

Curious Light will generally welcome it too...

**provided you aren't interrupting him.**

Their friendliness should not be mistaken for weakness, however.

They are still the Architects.

If someone pushes them too far, the consequences could extend far beyond ordinary injury.

So the official Blacksite rule is simple:

> **Hug the Architects.**
> 
> **Respect the Architects.**
> 
> **Do not annoy the Architects.**
> 
> **And under absolutely no circumstances ask them to demonstrate how erasure works.**

**End of Celestial Personnel Interaction Protocol**
