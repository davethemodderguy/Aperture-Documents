# C.O.R.E. PSYCHOLOGICAL THREAT ASSESSMENT

## NERI — PSYCHOLOGICAL INFLUENCE, BEHAVIORAL INSTABILITY & CONCEALED WEAPONRY

**Classification:** C.O.R.E. INTERNAL — HIGH PRIORITY  
**Resident:** Neri  
**Age:** 10  
**Species:** Seal Thiren  
**Status:** SAFEHOUSE RESIDENT  
**Psychological Classification:** Severely traumatized / partially unstable  
**Threat Assessment:** Context-dependent

---

# 1. OVERVIEW

Neri's psychological condition is considerably more complicated than her outward behavior suggests.

She is ten years old.

She is also an exceptionally intelligent chemist, a highly capable close-range fighter, an anomalous matter restructurer, and someone capable of producing unusual psychological effects in other people.

The combination has resulted in a resident who is simultaneously:

- extremely vulnerable,
    
- exceptionally dangerous when threatened,
    
- emotionally immature in age-appropriate ways,
    
- deeply traumatized,
    
- intensely attached to people she considers family,
    
- and capable of deliberately making another person experience overwhelming fear.
    

Neri is not conventionally “insane.”

However, C.O.R.E. considers her **partially psychologically unstable as a consequence of prolonged trauma, loss, experimentation, captivity, and years of untreated psychological distress.**

Her behavior should therefore not be interpreted through a simple sane/insane binary.

---

# 2. PSYCHOLOGICAL INFLUENCE

Neri possesses an unusual ability to influence the psychological state of other people.

Her most consistently observed effect is the induction of:

> **Fear**

and, more specifically:

> **An overwhelming sense of incoming danger.**

The affected individual may feel that something is about to happen.

They may become convinced that they are being watched.

They may experience an intense instinctive urge to leave.

They may suddenly perceive Neri as significantly more threatening than she objectively appears.

In extreme cases, the affected person may become too frightened to continue an aggressive action.

---

# 3. THE EFFECT IS NOT SIMPLE MIND CONTROL

Neri does not normally take complete control of another person's thoughts.

She does not simply tell someone:

> “Be afraid.”

and force them to obey.

Instead, she appears to manipulate the person's **perception of threat**.

The victim's own mind supplies the details.

This can make the effect particularly disturbing.

A person may be unable to explain _why_ they are frightened.

They simply know:

> **Something is wrong.**

The sensation can become overwhelming.

---

# 4. ORIGIN OF THE ABILITY

Neri has demonstrated an unusual understanding of psychological intimidation since childhood.

She learned various “mind games” while dealing with bullies.

Her stated purpose was simple:

> **“It helps with bullies.”**

These techniques were originally learned as survival mechanisms.

Neri discovered that making someone believe danger was imminent could sometimes stop them without requiring physical confrontation.

Her current anomalous influence appears to be an extension of that learned behavior.

In other words:

**Neri learned how to scare people.**

Something within Neri eventually learned how to make that fear substantially stronger.

---

# 5. WHY NERI IS PARTICULARLY EFFECTIVE

Neri has an unusual combination of characteristics.

She can appear completely harmless.

She is small.

She wears oversized clothing.

She may be sitting quietly with a book.

She may be eating cake.

She may be walking beside Alice.

Then the psychological effect begins.

The target's instincts suddenly begin screaming:

> **RUN.**

Neri does not necessarily need to raise her voice.

She does not necessarily need to draw a weapon.

She can simply stand there.

The contradiction between her appearance and the target's perception of danger is one of the strongest components of the effect.

---

# 6. THE “DANGER” RESPONSE

Affected individuals have described sensations including:

- sudden dread,
    
- heightened alertness,
    
- overwhelming suspicion,
    
- the feeling that an attack is imminent,
    
- irrational fear of approaching Neri,
    
- difficulty concentrating,
    
- panic,
    
- and an intense desire to escape.
    

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

C.O.R.E. suspects that Neri is not creating fear from nothing.

She is **amplifying the brain's threat-detection mechanisms.**

This remains a working theory.

---

# 7. NERI'S USE OF THE ABILITY

Neri does not generally use this ability on friends.

Her preferred targets are people she considers threatening.

Especially:

**Bullies.**

Her logic is straightforward.

> Friend → protect.

> Threat → stop.

> Friend injured → retaliation.

> Friend killed → catastrophic escalation.

This makes Neri's psychological influence defensive in most documented circumstances.

That does not make it harmless.

---

# 8. THE YANDERE / TSUNDERE CONTRADICTION

C.O.R.E. has informally noted that Neri displays characteristics associated with **both yandere and tsundere behavior.**

This classification is behavioral rather than clinical.

### TSUNDERE CHARACTERISTICS

Neri may:

- deny that she cares,
    
- become embarrassed by affection,
    
- become irritated when people point out that she is worried,
    
- act annoyed when someone helps her,
    
- insist she can handle things herself,
    
- become defensive when emotionally vulnerable.
    

She may care deeply about someone while verbally insisting:

> **“I don't care.”**

Nobody believes her.

---

### YANDERE CHARACTERISTICS

Her attachment to trusted people is considerably more intense.

She can become:

- extremely protective,
    
- possessive of relationships,
    
- highly distressed by perceived abandonment,
    
- violently defensive when someone she loves is threatened,
    
- and dangerously uncompromising when someone harms a friend or family member.
    

This originates largely from trauma.

Neri has lost too many people.

She does not want to lose anyone else.

Her internal response to another potential loss is essentially:

> **“Not again.”**

---

# 9. THE IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Neri is not constantly violent.

She is not searching for people to hurt.

She is capable of kindness.

She befriends people quickly when they treat her well.

She loves her family.

She likes spending time with friends.

She enjoys chemistry.

She eats cake.

She reads.

She jokes.

She can be remarkably childish.

Her dangerous behavior emerges primarily when she perceives someone as threatening the people she loves.

The problem is that **Neri's perception of threat can become extremely intense.**

---

# 10. CONCEALED WEAPONRY

Neri also possesses an unusually large number of concealed weapons.

The exact number remains unknown.

Attempts to count them have been unsuccessful.

Personnel have discovered blades concealed within or attached to:

- clothing,
    
- boots,
    
- ribbons,
    
- hair accessories,
    
- and other personal items.
    

Her two crossed hairclips are themselves small, extremely sharp blades.

Her fish-bone hairclip is **not** a weapon.

It is a sentimental item given to her by her younger sister and is to be treated accordingly.

Neri also carries her Starlight katana and ranged equipment under established SAFEHOUSE authorization.

---

# 11. DO NOT ATTEMPT TO COUNT THE KNIVES

This has become an unofficial SAFEHOUSE rule.

Neri knows people are curious.

She finds this amusing.

Personnel asking:

> **“How many knives do you have?”**

will generally receive:

> **“Enough.”**

Further investigation is unnecessary.

Miss Neko has formally requested that the question never be asked again.

Neko considers the situation “scientifically interesting.”

Miss Neko considers Neko part of the problem.

---

# 12. BEHAVIORAL RISK MODEL

Neri's response to interpersonal situations can be approximated as:

### KIND PERSON

**Neri:** Friend.

Response:

> Trust / companionship / affection.

---

### RUDE PERSON

**Neri:** Annoying.

Response:

> Irritation / teasing / avoidance.

---

### BULLY

**Neri:** Threat.

Response:

> Psychological intimidation.

Potential escalation:

> Physical defense.

---

### THREAT TO FRIEND

**Neri:** Immediate danger.

Response:

> Psychological influence + physical defense.

---

### FRIEND INJURED

**Neri:** Personal emergency.

Response:

> Extreme retaliation risk.

---

### FRIEND KILLED

**Neri:** Loss.

Response:

> **Critical.**

This is the situation in which Neri's emotional stability is most likely to deteriorate.

---

# 13. WHY HER TRAUMA MATTERS

Neri's behavior cannot be separated from her history.

She grew up believing people could be discarded.

Her younger sister was taken from her.

Later, people she befriended disappeared from her life.

She internalized the idea that she had failed to protect them.

This produced an extremely dangerous psychological rule:

> **If someone disappears, I failed them.**

SAFEHOUSE is attempting to replace it with:

> **If someone is in danger, I don't have to face it alone.**

This distinction is central to Neri's rehabilitation.

---

# 14. MEDICAL AND PSYCHOLOGICAL INTERACTION

Neri's psychological instability is made considerably more concerning by her medical condition.

She may push herself beyond safe physical limits when protecting someone.

Her anemia can occur spontaneously.

Severe stress can worsen her overall condition.

She can therefore enter a dangerous feedback loop:

**Friend threatened → fear → protective response → physical exertion → medical deterioration → collapse.**

Neri's determination to protect someone can literally put her own life at risk.

---

# 15. STAFF GUIDANCE

Neri should not be treated as:

> **“The crazy kid with knives.”**

This is both disrespectful and medically counterproductive.

She should instead be treated as:

> **A traumatized ten-year-old with extraordinary abilities who requires appropriate boundaries, supervision, and genuine relationships.**

Staff should:

- be kind,
    
- avoid bullying,
    
- respect her personal belongings,
    
- avoid touching concealed weapons,
    
- avoid deliberately provoking her,
    
- take threats seriously,
    
- de-escalate rather than challenge her,
    
- and remind her that she is allowed to rely on other people.
    

Most importantly:

**Do not use her fear of losing people as leverage.**

---

# 16. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Neri is one of the strangest contradictions currently residing in SAFEHOUSE.

She can discuss chemistry with Neko.

She can restructure matter.

She can fight people who vastly outweigh her.

She carries more concealed weapons than anyone has successfully counted.

She can make an adult feel an overwhelming sense of impending danger without necessarily touching them.

And then she can sit in the cafeteria eating cake with both hands hidden inside her oversized coat sleeves.

She is dangerous.

She is damaged.

She is intelligent.

She is frightened.

She is affectionate.

She is stubborn.

She is protective.

She is sometimes unstable.

And she is **still ten years old.**

The appropriate response is not to fear her.

It is to understand her.

Because beneath all of the weapons, abilities, fear, anger, and trauma is a child who has lost too much and has decided:

> **“I'm not losing anyone else.”**

SAFEHOUSE's job is to teach her that protecting someone does not require destroying herself in the process.

She is one of the people worth saving.
