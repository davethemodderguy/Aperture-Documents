# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **KCA-PER-015**

### **PERSONNEL ADDENDUM – EXECUTIVE PROTECTION COMPANIONSHIP**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Personnel Record

---

## **SUMMARY**

Although Keith's official assignment is to protect Director Belle Merryweather during her duties within the Banlands, personnel have observed that his role has gradually become more than that of a conventional bodyguard.

By his own choice, Keith has also become Belle's companion.

His objective is not only to ensure her physical safety...

...but to make sure she is never left feeling alone while carrying the responsibilities of directing the Krampus Compliance Authority.

---

## **An Unofficial Duty**

Keith has never requested that this responsibility be written into his assignment.

He simply began doing it.

He accompanies Belle between meetings.

Waits outside her office while she finishes paperwork.

Walks beside her through the city.

If Belle wants to stop to greet children, pet a stray animal, or buy a sweet from a market stall, Keith quietly waits nearby, maintaining security without rushing her.

No regulation requires this.

He does it because he believes the Director deserves more than constant vigilance.

She deserves company.

## **How They Work Together**

Their personalities are often described as complementary.

When Belle becomes distracted by something interesting, Keith gently reminds her of the schedule.

When Keith becomes completely focused on work, Belle encourages him to take a break or share a snack.

If Belle's optimism lifts the mood of those around her...

Keith's composure keeps everyone safe.

Neither tries to change the other.

Instead, each naturally fills the gaps the other leaves.

## **Observation Log**

Following a lengthy inspection tour, Belle emerged from another government building carrying several folders.

She stretched.

> **"...I'm exhausted."

Keith checked the next appointment.

> **"We have twenty minutes before the next meeting."

Belle smiled.

> **"...Ice cream?"

Keith looked at the schedule again.

After a brief pause, he closed the folder.

> **"...Twenty minutes is enough."

Belle's rabbit ears perked up.

> **"...Really?"

Keith nodded once.

> **"Lead the way."

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Keith continues serving as Belle Merryweather's executive protection officer during all official Banlands duties.

While he remains vigilant against threats at all times, personnel have come to recognize that his presence serves another purpose.

When Belle is overwhelmed by paperwork, difficult decisions, or the weight of her office...

She is rarely facing it alone.

Some partnerships are built on shared personalities.

Keith and Belle's is built on shared trust.

Each provides what the other needs, making them stronger together than either would be alone.
