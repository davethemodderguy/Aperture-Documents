### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / BIOLOGICAL RESTORATION REPORT**

# **CORIN — THE BODY RECONNECTS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **Progressive Biological Restoration**  
**Status:** **ONGOING / STABLE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's body continues to transition away from its former reanimated state.

The most recent development concerns the wounds and seams that previously separated her limbs from the rest of her body.

For years, Corin's limbs could detach when the stitches holding them together loosened.

Those wounds required Orphie to manually reconnect and restitch them.

That is no longer happening.

Her body has begun **sealing the separation points on its own.**

---

# **THE FIRST SEALING**

The change was first noticed around one of Corin's older stitch lines.

The thread had loosened slightly.

Normally, this would have required immediate repair.

Instead, the underlying tissue began closing.

The edges of the wound slowly drew together.

New biological tissue formed between them.

The opening disappeared.

No stitching was required.

---

# **BIOLOGICAL RECONNECTION**

Further observations confirmed that this was not an isolated incident.

The same process occurred along other former separation points.

Corin's body now appears capable of:

- Closing the wounds around her limbs.
    
- Regenerating connective tissue.
    
- Reconstructing damaged skin.
    
- Restoring structural continuity.
    
- Maintaining the limbs without external stitching.
    

The stitches that once held Corin together are becoming increasingly unnecessary.

---

# **THE STITCHES**

Orphie noticed first.

She was examining one of Corin's arms when she realized that several old stitches were no longer supporting anything.

They were simply embedded in healed tissue.

Orphie carefully removed one.

The skin remained intact.

She stared at it.

> **"...You don't need these anymore."**

Corin looked at her arm.

> **"Really?"**

Orphie nodded.

> **"Really."**

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin touched the former wound.

There was no opening.

No gap.

No visible separation.

Just skin.

She slowly moved her arm.

It moved naturally.

Corin became quiet.

Then:

> **"...It stayed."**

Orphie smiled.

> **"Yeah."**

Corin flexed her fingers.

> **"...It stayed."**

---

# **ETHER AND BIOLOGY**

The phenomenon appears to be another consequence of Corin's revitalized physiology.

Ether remains responsible for her unusually rapid regeneration.

However, the repaired tissue is now increasingly **biological rather than purely Ether-formed.**

Her body is no longer simply forcing damaged components back into place.

It is rebuilding them.

This distinction is considered extremely significant.

---

# **THE OLD CORIN**

Previously, Corin's body functioned through a precarious combination of:

- Reanimated tissue.
    
- Ether.
    
- External stitching.
    
- Anomalous regeneration.
    

Her limbs could detach.

Her wounds could reopen.

Her body required constant maintenance.

She viewed these characteristics as evidence that she was:

> **"A doll held together by Ether."**

That description is becoming increasingly inaccurate.

---

# **THE NEW CORIN**

Her body now demonstrates:

- A functional heartbeat.
    
- Blood circulation.
    
- Oxygen consumption.
    
- Revitalized organs.
    
- Biological wound closure.
    
- Natural tissue regeneration.
    
- Stable limb attachment.
    
- Functional wings.
    
- Persistent Ether abilities.
    

She is increasingly resembling a conventional living organism.

Not completely.

Not yet.

But significantly more than before.

---

# **LUMI'S REACTION**

Lumi noticed the change during a visit.

She examined Corin's arm.

There were still faint scars.

But there were no open seams.

Lumi touched one carefully.

> **Lumi:**  
> "No stitches?"

Corin shook her head.

> **"I don't need them anymore."**

Lumi smiled.

She gently touched Corin's hand.

> **"I'm glad."**

---

# **ORPHIE'S ROLE**

For a long time, Orphie had been the person responsible for keeping Corin physically assembled.

If an arm detached, Orphie reattached it.

If a leg came loose, Orphie stitched it back.

If a seam reopened, Orphie repaired it.

That responsibility is slowly disappearing.

Orphie is relieved.

But she is also strangely emotional about it.

During one examination, she quietly said:

> **"I don't have to put you back together anymore."**

Corin looked at her.

> **"You can still stay."**

Orphie smiled.

> **"I know."**

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL SIGNIFICANCE**

The physical change is having a profound effect on Corin's self-perception.

Her body is slowly contradicting the belief that defined her for so long.

She once looked at her stitches and thought:

> **"I'm broken."**

Now some of those stitches are simply unnecessary.

She once believed:

> **"My body can't keep itself together."**

Now it can.

She once believed:

> **"I'm just a thing moved by Ether."**

Now her body is independently performing biological functions.

The distinction between **reanimated** and **living** is becoming increasingly difficult to maintain.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Heartbeat:** Functional.

**Blood:** Functional.

**Respiration:** Required and functional.

**Organs:** Revitalized.

**Limb attachment:** Naturally maintained.

**Former wound seams:** Closing.

**External stitches:** Increasingly unnecessary.

**Ether regeneration:** Active.

**Biological regeneration:** Increasing.

**Overall condition:** **Progressively approaching conventional human physiology.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's body once needed to be manually held together.

Now it is learning to hold itself together.

The seams that once separated her limbs are closing.

The stitches are disappearing.

The wounds are becoming scars.

The scars are becoming memories.

And every day, Corin looks a little less like a corpse that Ether refuses to let die.

She is still anomalous.

She is still different.

She is still Corin.

But her body is slowly becoming something it has not been for a very long time:

**a living human body.**
