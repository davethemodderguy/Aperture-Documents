# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL PROFILE ADDENDUM**

# **COREY — HAT COLLECTION**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corey

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Personnel have noted that Corey is almost never seen without some form of soft headwear.

Originally adopted simply to keep her head warm, the habit has become one of her most recognizable characteristics.

While the specific hat changes from day to day, Corey is rarely observed without something covering her head.

---

# **COMMON HEADWEAR**

Corey's most frequently worn headwear includes:

- Knit beanies.
    
- Berets.
    
- Soft winter caps.
    
- A custom **oversized replica of a Mart's top hat.**
    
- Arcturus's fedora (borrowed with permission).
    
- Occasionally...
    

A sleeping Mart.

---

# **THE MART TOP HAT**

One of Corey's favorite hats is an enlarged version of a Mart's iconic top hat.

Despite its appearance, the hat is **not** made from an actual Mart.

It was custom-made to resemble one.

No Marts were harmed, modified, or inconvenienced during its creation.

When shown the finished product, several Marts proudly exclaimed:

> "Big hat!"

They appeared delighted that someone wanted to imitate their style.

---

# **ARCTURUS'S FEDORA**

On occasion, Corey has been seen wearing Arcturus's fedora.

The arrangement is entirely voluntary.

Staff have observed that the fedora is returned in excellent condition every time.

When asked why she liked wearing it, Corey simply replied:

> "...It's comfortable."

Arcturus reportedly answered:

> "...Then keep borrowing it."

---

# **THE "MART HAT"**

Perhaps the most unusual entry in Corey's collection is...

A Mart.

Several resident Marts in the treehouse have developed a habit of curling up on Corey's head while she is reading, relaxing, or quietly working.

If the Mart falls asleep, the situation becomes permanent until it naturally wakes.

Personnel have therefore documented multiple instances of Corey attending routine activities while wearing what appears to be:

> **A very content blue hat with antlers.**

---

# **CORIN'S COMMENT**

Corin has stopped questioning Corey's choice of headwear.

> "...Some people collect hats."

She glanced toward Corey, who currently had a sleeping Mart balanced perfectly on top of her beanie.

> "...Corey collects whatever decides to become a hat."

---

# **STAR'S COMMENT**

Star once asked Corey:

> "...Do you even notice when you're wearing a Mart anymore?"

Corey thought for a moment.

> "...Usually not."

The Mart opened one eye.

> "...Hat."

---

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

Lumi finds the oversized Mart hat particularly amusing.

> "...My children have fashion now."

The nearby Marts immediately nodded in agreement.

> "Fashion."

---

# **MART OPINION**

The Ambassador was asked whether the colony minded Corey wearing a hat modeled after theirs.

It answered almost immediately.

> "...Honor."

Another Mart added proudly:

> "...Big hat."

Researchers noted unanimous approval from the surrounding colony.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Personnel have gradually accepted that Corey will almost always be wearing something soft on her head.

Whether it is:

- A beanie.
    
- A beret.
    
- A winter cap.
    
- An oversized Mart-style top hat.
    
- Arcturus's fedora.
    
- Or a peacefully sleeping Mart.
    

The result is usually the same.

Corey is warmer.

The Marts are happy.

And nobody is particularly surprised anymore when someone walks into the treehouse and casually remarks:

> "...Morning, Corey."

Followed immediately by:

> "...Morning... Mart."

The Mart, still balanced on top of Corey's head, sleepily replied:

> **"...Morning."**

Without moving an inch.

**End of Personnel Profile Addendum**
