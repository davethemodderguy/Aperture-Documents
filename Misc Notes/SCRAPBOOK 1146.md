# 1. THE NEEDLE

After the expansion of the Cargo Bay 4 search area to approximately:

> **50,000,000,000 pieces of hay**

the competition continued.

Fifty ordinary iron needles had been deliberately placed throughout the haystack.

Only one object could produce victory:

> **The Nekonium Needle.**

Contestants were permitted no equipment.

No scanners.

No detectors.

No magnets.

No automated assistance.

Nothing except themselves.

After an extended search, one newly assigned MSAC operator located the target.

---

# 2. THE DISCOVERY

The contestant had already spent considerable time searching.

He was not among the most experienced operators present.

He was, in fact:

> **15 YEARS OLD.**

At approximately [TIME REDACTED], he reached into a section of the haystack and stopped.

He withdrew a small needle.

He examined it.

Then looked toward the supervising personnel.

> **CONTESTANT:**  
> “...I think I found it.”

The supervising officer approached.

The needle was inspected according to competition protocol.

It was not iron.

It was:

> **NEKONIUM.**

Silence.

The officer looked at the contestant.

> **OFFICER:**  
> “You won.”

The contestant stared at him.

> **CONTESTANT:**  
> “...Actually?”

> **OFFICER:**  
> “Actually.”

The contestant looked down at the needle again.

> **CONTESTANT:**  
> “Oh.”

---

# 3. THE REACTION

The announcement reached the other contestants almost immediately.

The search stopped.

One operator emerged from the hay.

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “Who found it?”

The supervising officer pointed toward the fifteen-year-old.

The operator stared.

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “Him?”

The winner nodded.

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “How long have you been searching?”

The winner shrugged.

> **WINNER:**  
> “A while.”

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “And you actually found the Nekonium one?”

The winner held up the needle.

> **WINNER:**  
> “Apparently.”

Someone further back in the haystack shouted:

> **“YOU HAVE GOT TO BE KIDDING ME.”**

### PRIZE TWO

# 5. THE YOUNGEST NIGHT STALKER OWNER

The winner is now:

> **15 YEARS OLD.**
