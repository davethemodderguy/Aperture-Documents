# 1. SUMMARY

Neri has extensive scar tissue across her body.

The majority of the scars were not previously documented because Neri almost never permits anyone to examine large portions of her body.

Following several weeks of increasingly stable residency, Neri voluntarily allowed a medical examination.

The examination was, technically, facilitated by cake.

Again.

> **MEDICAL STAFF NOTE:**  
> We are beginning to suspect that cake has become an unofficial medical instrument.

The scars are consistent with multiple periods of severe physical trauma.

Based on their distribution, appearance, age, and the known history of the subject, medical personnel believe they originate from a combination of:

- Childhood physical abuse.
    
- Torture.
    
- Deliberate injury.
    
- Experimental procedures associated with her artificial Thiren transformation.
    
- Repeated untreated injuries.
    

The full extent of the damage is still being documented.

# 4. CARDIAC REGION

### MEDICAL ALERT — HIGH PRIORITY

Scarring was discovered around the region of Neri's heart.

This finding has substantially increased concern regarding her existing cardiovascular instability.

Neri's history already includes:

- Fainting.
    
- Exercise-induced cardiovascular crashes.
    
- Severe stress intolerance.
    
- Episodes of sudden weakness.
    
- Dependence on supplemental blood.
    

The presence of significant previous trauma in the cardiac region introduces another possible contributing factor.

However:

> **It cannot be considered unrelated until proven otherwise.**

Additional invasive testing is **not authorized without Neri's informed cooperation and appropriate medical justification.**

# 9. WHY THIS WAS MISSED

The question has been raised as to how such extensive scarring remained undocumented for so long.

Several factors contributed.

Neri almost always wears clothing that covers large portions of her body.

Her oversized coat, shirt, tie, scarf, and other clothing conceal much of her skin.

She also actively avoids discussing her physical condition.

Additionally, many scars are old and partially faded.

Most importantly:

> **Nobody at SAFEHOUSE wanted to force her to undress for an examination.**

Her autonomy was prioritized.

The discovery therefore occurred only after Neri herself agreed.
