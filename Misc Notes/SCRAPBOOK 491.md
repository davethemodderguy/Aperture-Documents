# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **RESTRICTED SAFEHOUSE / EXTREME WEATHER INCIDENT**

# **DAY THREE — NO MORE BLUE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Architect-Sealed  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **"STABLE" / COMMUNICATIONS ABSENT**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The storm continues.

The route between the Blacksite and the treehouse remains inaccessible.

Orphie has not returned.

Guiding Light's blue notes have stopped appearing.

No explanation accompanied their disappearance.

No final message.

No warning.

Just silence.

Corin remains **"stable."**

The quotation marks are intentional.

---

# **THE MORNING**

Corin woke up and immediately looked at the table.

Nothing.

She checked the windowsill.

Nothing.

The floor beside her notebook.

Nothing.

The kitchen counter.

Nothing.

For three days, there had always been something blue somewhere.

Today, there wasn't.

Corin sat quietly.

> **"...Guiding?"**

No response.

She waited.

Nothing appeared.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

Corin opened her notebook.

The first entry of the day was unusually short.

> **Day 3.**

Then:

> **No notes.**

She waited several minutes before adding:

> **Maybe she's busy.**

A pause.

> **Maybe the storm is worse.**

Another pause.

> **Maybe she's trying.**

She stopped writing.

---

# **STABILITY**

Medical observation indicated that Corin's Ether levels remained within her previously established range.

Her stitches remained intact.

No major structural deterioration was observed.

Her cognition remained coherent.

Her perception remained stable.

She ate.

She drank water.

She slept.

She did not attempt to leave the treehouse.

She did not damage anything.

She did not display overt physical agitation.

By every measurable metric:

**Corin was stable.**

---

# **THE PROBLEM**

Corin was simply sitting in the same chair for most of the morning.

She stared at the window.

The snow remained visible.

Nothing else was.

She occasionally looked toward the table.

Still nothing.

Eventually she whispered:

> **"...She stopped."**

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK ENTRY**

At midday:

> **I think Guiding Light can't reach me anymore.**

> **That's okay.**

> **She tried.**

> **She stayed as long as she could.**

Then:

> **I miss her notes.**

The next line was written much later.

> **The blue made the room feel less empty.**

---

# **NO SPIRAL**

Something unusual happened.

Corin did not spiral.

She did not begin tearing through the treehouse.

She did not repeatedly check the door.

She did not fill pages with increasingly frantic thoughts.

She simply remained quiet.

Perhaps the previous days had exhausted her.

Perhaps she had begun accepting that some things were beyond her control.

Or perhaps she was simply too tired to react.

The observation team could not determine which.

---

# **THE EMPTY TABLE**

Corin eventually placed the previous blue notes together.

She arranged them carefully.

One by one.

> **"Eat something."**

> **"Drink water."**

> **"You are not alone."**

> **"Orphie is still trying to come home."**

> **"I am still trying to reach you."**

She kept the final note on top.

She stared at it.

Then quietly said:

> **"...You tried."**

---

# **EVENING**

The storm continued.

Corin ate dinner alone.

She washed her cup.

She checked her stitches.

She combed her own hair as best she could.

The hairclip remained in place.

She went to bed without writing anything else.

Before sleeping, she looked toward the table.

Still no blue light.

> **"Goodnight, Guiding."**

Silence.

Corin closed her eyes.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Day Three presents a disturbing contradiction.

Corin is stable.

Her body is stable.

Her Ether is stable.

Her cognition is stable.

Her behavior is stable.

And yet the treehouse has never felt quieter.

The blue notes have stopped.

Orphie has not returned.

Guiding Light cannot be reached.

The storm continues.

Still, Corin remains functional.

She has not broken down.

She has not forgotten.

She has not given up.

She simply waits.

The notebook contains one final sentence before she went to sleep:

> **"I hope they're okay."**

Nothing else.

No panic.

No anger.

No despair.

Just waiting.

**End of Restricted Safehouse / Extreme Weather Incident — Day Three**
