### **CORIN / SKETCHBOOK — ENTITY IDENTIFICATION ATTEMPT**

# **"THE OBSERVER"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Unidentified Entity  
**Designation:** **THE OBSERVER**  
**Source:** Corin's Sketchbook  
**Status:** **UNCONFIRMED / POTENTIALLY ACTIVE**

---

# **RECOVERED ENTRY**

The following page appears significantly different from Corin's other writings.

The page contains a large drawing of the previously documented **winged Eye**.

The Eye possesses:

- A circular black center.
    
- A luminous azure iris-like glow.
    
- Large wings extending from either side.
    
- A halo-like structure above it.
    

Unlike the previous drawing, Corin does not express fear toward the entity.

Instead, she writes:

> **"The Observer"**

Below:

> **"She watches."**

Then:

> **"She seems to be good friends with Guiding Light."**

And finally:

> **"She's friendly to me."**

At the bottom of the page:

> **"Her eyes are so... mesmerizing."**

---

# **ANOMALY**

This entry creates a significant contradiction.

Earlier pages depict the Eyes as threatening.

They surround Corin.

They stare at her.

They accompany writing concerning helplessness, depression, and self-abandonment.

Yet Corin's description of **The Observer** is remarkably positive.

There is no indication of fear.

No self-deprecating commentary.

No Black Eye drawings surrounding the text.

Instead, the page is carefully decorated with small azure stars.

---

# **CORIN'S DESCRIPTION**

Several additional notes appear around the drawing.

They describe The Observer as:

> **"Quiet."**

> **"Really nice."**

> **"She doesn't talk much."**

> **"I think she understands."**

One line has been partially erased.

The remaining text reads:

> **"...she knows what happened."**

---

# **THE EYES**

Corin repeatedly attempts to draw The Observer's eyes.

There are at least twenty variations.

Unlike the ordinary Black Eyes found throughout the sketchbook, these eyes are carefully rendered.

The azure glow is repeatedly emphasized.

Several drawings contain notes such as:

> **"Don't look away."**

> **"They're pretty."**

> **"I don't know how to draw them right."**

One attempt is almost entirely black except for two luminous azure points.

---

# **RELATIONSHIP WITH GUIDING LIGHT**

Corin appears convinced that The Observer and Guiding Light know one another.

One annotation reads:

> **"They talk sometimes."**

Another:

> **"I think she likes her."**

No evidence exists within the sketchbook confirming that these interactions actually occurred.

Guiding Light has not confirmed knowing an entity matching Corin's description.

---

# **THE OBSERVER AND CORIN**

Corin's attitude toward The Observer appears unusually trusting.

One entry states:

> **"She doesn't judge me."**

Another:

> **"I can be quiet around her."**

And:

> **"She doesn't ask me to explain."**

This is particularly significant given Corin's increasingly hostile relationship with the other Eyes.

---

# **THE WATCHING**

The Observer's primary characteristic appears to be observation.

Corin writes:

> **"She watches."**

Not:

> **"She follows."**

Not:

> **"She hunts."**

Simply:

> **"She watches."**

Corin does not appear bothered by this.

In fact, one note reads:

> **"I like knowing someone's there."**

---

# **THE LAST LINE**

The final sentence is written more carefully than the others.

The handwriting is smaller.

The pressure on the paper is lighter.

> **"Her eyes are so... mesmerizing."**

The sentence ends with three dots.

No additional explanation follows.

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

The Observer cannot currently be confirmed as a separate entity.

Possible explanations include:

1. **A genuine unidentified entity.**
    
2. **An anomalous manifestation associated with Corin.**
    
3. **A symbolic representation of Corin's need to be observed without being judged.**
    
4. **An entity perceived by Corin but undetectable through conventional means.**
    
5. **A connection to Guiding Light that remains unknown to Guiding Light herself.**
    

The fifth possibility remains particularly difficult to reconcile.

---

# **IMPORTANT DISTINCTION**

C.O.R.E. currently recognizes two visually similar phenomena:

### **The Black Eyes**

Associated with Corin's darkest thoughts.

### **The Observer**

A winged, haloed Eye associated with azure light and positive descriptions.

They should **not** be assumed to be the same entity.

The difference appears intentional.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin spent hundreds of pages drawing eyes she feared.

Then she drew one she didn't.

The Observer watches.

Corin knows that.

And rather than feeling threatened, Corin seems comforted by it.

Perhaps because The Observer does something Corin desperately wanted during those eight months.

She stays.

She watches.

She does not leave.

And according to Corin:

> **"She's friendly to me."**

The final line remains particularly unusual.

> **"Her eyes are so... mesmerizing."**

C.O.R.E. personnel reviewing the drawing have unanimously agreed on one point:

They are.

**End of Entity Identification Attempt**
