# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY / APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **PERSONNEL DOSSIER**

### **Q&A – KEITH, CINDER & ALICE**

**Classification:** Public Release

---

## **KEITH**

### **Q: Who is Keith?**

**A:** Keith is Belle Merryweather's personal bodyguard and companion, assigned by the Central Execution Department while she is in the Banlands. He is a veteran soldier with five years of military combat experience.

---

### **Q: Is Keith always serious?**

**A:** Almost.

Keith is disciplined, responsible, and rarely displays strong emotion while on duty.

Outside of work, he is considerably more relaxed, though still quiet.

---

### **Q: What weapon does Keith use?**

**A:** A specially modified **M1014 Semi-Automatic Combat Shotgun** equipped with a shell rack and various tactical attachments.

The shotgun bears the Aperture Industries insignia along with Keith's callsign and **"Property of KCA."**

---

### **Q: Why is Keith always with Belle?**

**A:** Initially as her assigned bodyguard.

Over time, he chose to remain by her side as her companion as well, believing that protecting someone also means ensuring they are not alone.

---

### **Q: Can Keith fight without his shotgun?**

**A:** Yes.

His military training allows him to remain effective even without firearms, although he prefers to keep engagements at a tactical advantage.

---

### **Q: Can I touch Keith's shark tail?**

**A:** No.

Do not touch Keith's tail without permission.

---

### **Q: Why is the end of Keith's tail missing?**

**A:** Official records only note that part of the tail was lost in a previous incident.

Keith has declined to elaborate.

---

---

## **CINDER**

### **Q: Who is Cinder?**

**A:** Cinder is Belle Merryweather's secretary within the Krampus Compliance Authority and Keith's younger sister.

She formerly worked for the Central Computing Department before transferring to the KCA.

---

### **Q: Why is she called Cinder?**

**A:** Because she possesses the ability to manipulate fire.

The callsign reflects this ability.

---

### **Q: What is inside Cinder's briefcase?**

**A:** Much more than paperwork.

The KCA-issued briefcase can function as:

- An Explosive Smart Ordnance Launcher.
    
- A homing rocket launcher.
    
- A volatile Ether rocket launcher for creating escape routes.
    
- A compact SMG.
    
- A 55-kilogram melee weapon.
    

The briefcase is officially marked **"Property of KCA."**

---

### **Q: What happens if Cinder loses her briefcase?**

**A:** She loses her job.

Fortunately, no one has managed to make that happen.

---

### **Q: Is Cinder dangerous without the briefcase?**

**A:** Extremely.

Archived combat footage shows Cinder relying on brutal hand-to-hand combat, using techniques drawn from her underground fight club experience.

Personnel are strongly advised not to mistake disarming her for neutralizing her.

---

### **Q: Why do people say not to take away her briefcase?**

**A:** Because it changes how she fights.

History has shown that Cinder becomes significantly more aggressive in close-quarters combat once forced to rely solely on her gauntlets.

---

### **Q: Does Cinder actually enjoy fighting?**

**A:** Outside combat, no.

She prefers reading, spending time with Belle, talking with Endmin, and visiting the Friendly Roommates.

Combat is simply the role she fulfills when required.

---

### **Q: Can I touch Cinder's shark tail?**

**A:** Yes, provided she is comfortable with it.

Her white tail is decorated with small stickers and tattoos.

Please do not peel them off.

---

---

## **ALICE**

### **Q: Who is Alice?**

**A:** Alice serves as the **Head of Security for Merryweather Manor**.

She is a trusted member of the household's security staff and works closely with the Merryweather family, although she is not a member of the family herself.

---

### **Q: Is Alice part of the Merryweather family?**

**A:** No.

She works **for** the Merryweathers, not **as** one.

---

### **Q: What are Alice's responsibilities?**

**A:** Alice oversees the security of Merryweather Manor, coordinates protective measures, and assists with the safety of its residents and guests.

She also provides medical support when needed, including Endmin's eye medication.

---

### **Q: Is Alice strict?**

**A:** Professionally, yes.

Personally, she is calm, dependable, and well-liked by those who know her.

---

### **Q: Does Alice visit Heaven's Blacksite?**

**A:** Occasionally.

Her visits are usually work-related, though she often stays long enough to spend time with Belle, Endmin, Keith, Cinder, and the Friendly Roommates.

---

### **Q: Does Alice fight?**

**A:** Yes.

As Head of Security, she is fully trained to respond to threats against Merryweather Manor.

However, she generally prefers preventing conflicts rather than escalating them.

---

### **Q: What's Alice's opinion of the others?**

**A:** She has remarked that keeping the Manor secure is often easier than keeping Belle, Cinder, and Neko from turning an ordinary day into an unforgettable incident.

She has not elaborated.

---

### **Q: Final advice?**

**A:** Respect Keith's professionalism.

Never underestimate Cinder because she's carrying a briefcase.

And if Alice tells you to stand behind her...

Do it.

---

**End of Q&A**
