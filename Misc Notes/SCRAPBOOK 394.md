### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION LOG**

# **COREY — THE BROKEN PENCIL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Concern  
**Subject:** Corey  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / Luna / Solaria / SAFEHOUSE Residents  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **ACTIVE CONCERN**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

A small incident involving one of Corey's personal belongings has caused significant concern among those closest to her.

Corey's favorite pencil was found **snapped cleanly in half**.

Under normal circumstances, this would be considered an insignificant accident.

Given Corey's current psychological condition, it was not.

The pencil was one of the objects Corey used most frequently when drawing in her sketchbook.

It had accompanied her through both her hopeful drawings and the increasingly disturbing sketches produced during her nightmares.

---

# **THE PENCIL**

The pencil was worn from extensive use.

It was not particularly expensive.

It was not anomalous.

It was simply:

> **Corey's favorite pencil.**

She had used it for countless drawings.

It had become familiar enough that Corey could identify it immediately among her other art supplies.

When it broke, Corey did not become visibly angry.

She simply stared at the two pieces.

For several seconds.

Then:

> "...Oh."

She placed both halves on the desk.

She did not throw them away.

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

Corin noticed the broken pencil later.

She immediately understood why Corey had kept it.

Corin approached her carefully.

> "...You okay?"

Corey looked at the pencil.

> "...It's just a pencil."

Corin:

> "...I know."

Corey:

> "...It's stupid."

Corin:

> "...No."

Corey did not answer.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The incident was reported to the other residents.

The reaction was immediate concern rather than panic.

Everyone understood that the pencil itself was not the problem.

It was what the pencil represented.

Corey has been increasingly withdrawn.

She has lost sleep.

Her sketchbook has become filled with disturbing imagery.

She has begun deliberately remaining as Luna or Solaria for extended periods.

And now, even small personal losses appear capable of affecting her significantly.

---

# **LUNA'S RESPONSE**

When Luna manifested, she was informed about the pencil.

She became quiet.

> "...She loved that pencil."

The researcher asked whether Corey had told her.

Luna replied:

> "...I remember."

She looked toward the desk.

> "...Everything she remembers."

Then:

> "...She's already lost enough."

---

# **SOLARIA'S RESPONSE**

Solaria reacted similarly.

> "...That was the one she always used."

She paused.

> "...She doesn't need another reason to feel like everything is falling apart."

Solaria remained unusually quiet afterward.

---

# **THE THREE FORMS**

Luna and Solaria's concern has become increasingly obvious.

Neither can speak directly to the other.

They are different manifestations of the same person and cannot hold a conversation between forms.

However, each independently talks about Corey when speaking with other people.

Their message remains consistent:

> **Corey is not okay.**

And both are increasingly aware that they cannot simply take her place forever.

---

# **THE REMOTE TREEHOUSE**

Corey's primary residence with Corin is not located inside the main SAFEHOUSE.

They live in a **remote treehouse within the forests surrounding the Blacksite perimeter**.

The location was originally valued for its quiet surroundings and distance from the main facility.

Under current circumstances, however, the isolation has become a significant complication.

---

# **DISTANCE**

Corin continues working as KCA's accountant.

Her work frequently keeps her at the KCA for long periods.

As a result, she often returns to the treehouse late.

By the time Corin arrives home:

- Corey may already be asleep.
    
- Corey may still be awake.
    
- Corey may be drawing.
    
- Corey may have spent hours alone.
    
- Corey may already have shifted into Luna or Solaria.
    

The distance makes immediate emotional support considerably more difficult.

---

# **THE MATS**

Several Marts permanently reside with Corey and Corin at the treehouse.

Normally, they provide constant companionship.

They can:

- Keep Corey warm.
    
- Sleep beside her.
    
- Act as blankets.
    
- Adjust their internal temperature.
    
- Remain nearby when she is upset.
    

However, Corey's current emotional state has made this impossible.

The Marts are avoiding her in her normal state as an act of colony-wide self-preservation.

This leaves the treehouse noticeably quieter.

---

# **CORIN'S RETURN HOME**

Corin sometimes returns late at night to find the treehouse almost completely silent.

No Marts crowd the rooms.

Corey's sketchbook may be open.

The broken pencil may still be on the desk.

And Corey may be awake.

Corin has begun recognizing that asking:

> "...Are you okay?"

often receives the same answer.

> "...Yeah."

It is rarely convincing.

---

# **A RECENT NIGHT**

Corin returned home late from work.

The lights were off.

She found Corey sitting at the table.

The two halves of the pencil were still beside the sketchbook.

Corin sat down.

She did not immediately speak.

After several minutes, Corey quietly said:

> "...You're home."

Corin answered:

> "...Yeah."

Corey:

> "...You're late."

Corin:

> "...I know."

Silence.

Then Corin moved her chair closer.

---

# **LUNA'S OBSERVATION**

Later, when Luna manifested, she spoke privately with Corin.

> "...She's getting worse when you're gone."

Corin looked down.

> "...I know."

Luna continued:

> "...It's not your fault."

Corin did not answer.

Luna:

> "...You can't be everywhere."

---

# **SOLARIA'S OBSERVATION**

Solaria gave Corin a similar warning.

> "...She needs people."

Corin:

> "...I know."

Solaria:

> "...And you need sleep too."

Corin sighed.

> "...I know."

Solaria looked toward the forest outside the treehouse.

> "...This place is too quiet for her right now."

---

# **THE PENCIL**

The two broken halves remain on Corey's desk.

She has not thrown them away.

She has not attempted to sharpen or repair them.

For now, they simply remain together.

Corin has considered finding a replacement.

She has not done so yet.

The original pencil means more to Corey than its material value would suggest.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Corey:**  
Severely distressed. Sleep-deprived. Increasingly withdrawn.

**Corin:**  
Highly concerned. Limited by work schedule and distance from the main SAFEHOUSE.

**Luna:**  
Concerned. Stable while manifested.

**Solaria:**  
Concerned. Stable while manifested.

**Marts:**  
Still avoiding Corey in her normal state. Remain protective from a distance.

**SAFEHOUSE Residents:**  
Aware and concerned.

**Treehouse:**  
Safe physically. Increasingly difficult environment for Corey's emotional recovery due to isolation.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

It was only a pencil.

A worn piece of wood and graphite.

But Corey had used it to draw things she loved.

Then nightmares.

Then memories.

Then questions she could not answer.

Now it lies broken beside the sketchbook.

Corin is worried.

The SAFEHOUSE is worried.

Luna is worried.

Solaria is worried.

Even the Marts remain nearby in their own way, watching from a safe distance.

And every night, Corin walks through the forest back to the treehouse, hoping that when she opens the door, Corey will be a little better than she was when Corin left.

Sometimes she is.

Sometimes she isn't.

But the door still opens.

And Corin still comes home.

**End of Personnel Welfare / SAFEHOUSE Observation Log**
