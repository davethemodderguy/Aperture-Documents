# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — SOCIAL CLASSIFICATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — PERSONNEL CARE  
**Species Classification:** **Full-Body Rabbit Thiren**  
**Social Status:** **Friend of the Blacksite**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Alice has developed an unusually strong social bond with personnel throughout Heaven's Blacksite.

She is, for all practical purposes:

> **Everyone's friend.**

Alice gets along with nearly everyone she meets.

Security officers know her.

Researchers know her.

Engineers know her.

Cafeteria staff know her.

Archivists know her.

Maintenance personnel know her.

And almost all of them are happy to see her.

---

# **SPECIES CLASSIFICATION**

Following a formal review of her anatomy, Alice has officially been classified as a:

> **FULL-BODY RABBIT THIREN**

Her rabbit characteristics are not limited to isolated features.

Her entire physical presentation is consistent with a rabbit thiren.

Notable characteristics include:

- Rabbit ears.
    
- Rabbit-like dentition.
    
- Small rabbit-like frame.
    
- Rabbit tail structure, although anomalously resembling a feather-like fluff.
    
- Rabbit physiology.
    
- Characteristic hopping and movement patterns when excited.
    
- Extremely sensitive hearing.
    

Her ears, however, normally remain lowered under gravity.

---

# **THE BUNNY FRIENDSHIP**

Alice's closest species-based friendship is with Belle Merryweather.

The two are frequently described by personnel as:

> **"The two bunny girls."**

They have an exceptionally close relationship.

Belle is one of the people Alice most frequently seeks out.

If Alice is looking for someone to play with—

Belle is usually one of her first choices.

If Belle is looking for Alice—

she usually doesn't have to search for long.

---

# **BELLE & ALICE**

Belle and Alice share an unusual amount in common.

Both are bunny girls.

Both have rabbit characteristics.

Both enjoy spending time together.

Both are comfortable simply sitting beside each other without speaking.

And both seem to understand the other without requiring much explanation.

---

# **TWO BUNNY GIRLS**

When they sit together, the contrast is immediately noticeable.

Belle:

> Energetic.

Alice:

> Quiet.

Belle:

> Larger.

Alice:

> Tiny.

Belle:

> Pink.

Alice:

> Usually wearing white.

Belle's ears may stand upright.

Alice's generally fall downward.

Yet they get along remarkably well.

---

Piggyback rides remain common.

Belle will sometimes let Alice climb onto her back.

Alice holds on carefully.

Belle walks around the SAFEHOUSE.

Alice's ears flop behind her.

Alice usually says:

> "Faster!"

Belle:

> "You sure?"

> "Yes!"

# **ALICE'S FAVORITE ACTIVITY**

Following Belle around.

Sometimes Alice rides her scooter beside Belle.

Sometimes she walks.

Sometimes she simply sits nearby while Belle does something else.

Alice doesn't require constant entertainment.

She just likes being near her friend.

---

# **THE FOUR RABBIT THIRENS**

Personnel occasionally refer to a broader group as:

> **"The rabbit girls."**

However, the classification is more accurately divided according to species and body plan.

Alice is a **full-body rabbit thiren**.

Belle is likewise a rabbit thiren.

The two are therefore especially recognizable as the Blacksite's:

> **Two bunny girls.**

---

# **SOCIAL INTEGRATION**

Alice's friendship is not limited to rabbit thirens.

She has developed positive relationships with almost everyone in her environment.

She may approach someone she has never spoken to before and simply say:

> "Hi."

If they respond kindly—

Alice may decide they are friends.

Her standards are remarkably simple.

---

# **EMPLOYEE TESTIMONIAL**

One maintenance worker reported:

> "I was repairing a ventilation panel."

> "Alice sat beside me for twenty minutes."

> "Didn't say anything."

> "Then she said, 'You're doing good.'"

> "That made my entire week."

---

# **SECURITY DEPARTMENT**

A guard once found Alice waiting beside their checkpoint.

> "What are you doing here?"

Alice:

> "Waiting."

> "For who?"

Alice pointed at the guard.

> "...You."

The guard reportedly had no idea how to respond.

Alice simply smiled.

---

# **CAFETERIA STAFF**

The cafeteria staff know Alice by name.

Some have started keeping small portions of rabbit-safe foods available for her.

Alice sometimes arrives on her scooter.

She parks it carefully.

Then announces:

> "Alice is here!"

Someone invariably responds:

> "We know!"

---

# **THE IMPORTANT PART**

Alice doesn't distinguish between "important" and "unimportant" people.

To her:

A director.

A researcher.

A janitor.

A guard.

A cook.

A mechanic.

A friend.

They're all simply:

> **People Alice likes.**

---

# **BELLE'S ASSESSMENT**

When asked what makes Alice special, Belle answered:

> "She's Alice."

The interviewer asked:

> "That's all?"

Belle nodded.

> "That's enough."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Alice is officially a **full-body rabbit thiren**.

Belle is her closest rabbit friend.

Together, they form the Blacksite's most recognizable bunny duo.

But Alice's friendships extend far beyond Belle.

She has become woven into everyday Blacksite life.

Someone always recognizes her.

Someone always says hello.

Someone always smiles when she passes.

And Alice—

with her soft voice, floppy ears, little boots, white beanie, and impossibly small scooter—

is simply happy to have friends.

Especially Belle.
