# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION REPORT**

# **YARN — SHARED SAFEHOUSE FAVORITE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Internal Personnel  
**Subjects:** Corin, Neko, Miss Neko, Endmin  
**Observation:** **Yarn Preference**  
**Status:** Harmless

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has determined that Corin is not the only SAFEHOUSE resident with an unusual fondness for yarn.

**Neko, Miss Neko, and Endmin** also enjoy playing with yarn.

All three possess feline-derived traits, making their interest in yarn considerably less surprising than Corin's.

Corin, despite not being a cat thiren, has nevertheless joined them.

The SAFEHOUSE therefore has an unofficial:

> **Yarn Enthusiast Group.**

---

# **THE CAT THIRENS**

Neko, Miss Neko, and Endmin have demonstrated a natural interest in yarn.

Common activities include:

- Rolling yarn across the floor.
    
- Chasing loose strands.
    
- Pulling yarn from a ball.
    
- Batting yarn with their hands.
    
- Untangling it.
    
- Occasionally turning the entire thing into a mess.
    

This behavior is considered normal for their feline physiology.

---

# **CORIN'S INVOLVEMENT**

Corin simply joins them.

She does not require much encouragement.

If Neko starts rolling a ball of yarn across the floor, Corin may immediately chase after it.

If Miss Neko is unraveling one, Corin may sit beside her and start pulling on another strand.

Endmin has occasionally tossed a yarn ball toward Corin.

Corin always catches it.

Usually.

---

# **THE SAFEHOUSE YARN BASKET**

A dedicated basket of yarn has now been established in the central living area.

The basket contains several colors and sizes.

It was originally intended for Neko, Miss Neko, and Endmin.

Corin quickly adopted it as well.

The residents have since stopped trying to determine who owns which ball.

The current policy is:

> **"If it's in the basket, it's everyone's."**

---

# **A TYPICAL EVENING**

A surprisingly common SAFEHOUSE scene now consists of:

Neko sitting on the floor with a ball of yarn.

Miss Neko quietly unraveling another.

Endmin attempting to keep hers organized.

Corin sitting nearby, batting a loose strand around with one hand.

Then someone rolls a ball across the floor.

All four notice.

A brief pause follows.

Then:

**Chaos.**

---

# **BELLE'S OBSERVATION**

Belle once watched all four chasing yarn around the living room.

She looked at Orphie.

> **"Why are they all doing that?"**

Orphie shrugged.

> **"They like yarn."**

Belle thought for a moment.

> **"...Can I play too?"**

Nobody stopped her.

She lasted approximately thirty seconds before getting tangled in the yarn.

---

# **NEKO'S DEFENSE**

Neko maintains that enjoying yarn is perfectly normal.

He objects to Corin being included in the comparison.

> **Neko:**  
> "We're cat thirens."

Corin held up a ball of yarn.

> **"And?"**

Neko had no response.

---

# **MISS NEKO**

Miss Neko is considerably more restrained than the others.

She does not chase yarn around the room.

Instead, she prefers quietly manipulating the strands while sitting somewhere comfortable.

If someone throws a yarn ball toward her, however, she will catch it.

She insists this does not count as playing.

Nobody believes her.

---

# **ENDMIN**

Endmin enjoys the organizational side of yarn.

She sometimes takes tangled balls and patiently untangles them.

Unfortunately, the moment she finishes, someone usually takes the yarn and starts playing with it again.

Her resulting expression has become familiar to everyone in the SAFEHOUSE.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin has no feline ancestry.

She simply likes yarn.

Her behavior is therefore considered an amusing coincidence rather than evidence of another anomalous transformation.

C.O.R.E. has found no connection between her Ether, wings, or biological restoration and her interest in yarn.

She just likes it.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Yarn has become one of the SAFEHOUSE's strangest shared hobbies.

Neko likes it.

Miss Neko likes it.

Endmin likes it.

They are cat thirens, so nobody is particularly surprised.

Corin likes it too.

Nobody knows why.

But when four residents are sitting together, surrounded by yarn, nobody really cares about the explanation.

It is simply another ordinary evening at the SAFEHOUSE.

Until someone rolls the yarn across the floor.

Then everyone runs after it.

**End of SAFEHOUSE Behavioral Observation Report**
