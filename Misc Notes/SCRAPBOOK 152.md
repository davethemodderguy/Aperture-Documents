# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CRITICAL PERSONNEL INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – RESIGNATION & UNAUTHORIZED DEPARTURE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

At 08:17 local time, Corin Lockwood submitted a formal letter of resignation to KCA Headquarters.

The letter contained no accusations.

No demands.

No explanation beyond a single handwritten sentence.

> **"I'm sorry for causing everyone so much trouble."**

The resignation became effective immediately.

Before Executive Personnel could respond, Corin left the building carrying only a small backpack.

She declined transportation.

She declined security.

She declined company housing.

She simply walked away.

---

# **DISCOVERY**

At first, staff assumed Corin intended to return later.

She did not.

Her office was found unusually clean.

Every financial record had been completed.

Every pending budget had been approved or transferred.

Her identification badge rested neatly on the center of her desk.

Beside it sat the company-issued calculator she had used for years.

Nothing had been damaged.

Nothing had been taken except her personal belongings.

It looked less like someone quitting.

More like someone quietly erasing herself from the workplace.

---

# **REACTION**

Within minutes of confirming Corin's departure, Accounting entered a state of confusion.

Managers attempted to contact her.

No answer.

Engineering called Accounting.

Security called Engineering.

Project SAFEHOUSE was notified immediately.

The news spread through the Blacksite faster than any formal announcement.

Personnel who had never raised their voices were suddenly speaking over one another.

Not because of unfinished paperwork.

Because Corin was gone.

---

# **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE**

The lounge fell silent.

Belle stared at the notification for several seconds.

Then stood up without saying a word.

Norma immediately left to search.

Arcturus followed moments later.

Neko abandoned an engineering meeting halfway through a presentation.

Miss Neko quietly took over the meeting before leaving herself.

Kent stood motionless.

> "...No."

Cinder had already begun compiling every confirmed sighting of Corin within the last twenty-four hours.

No one questioned why.

Everyone understood.

This was no longer a personnel matter.

It was family.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi read the resignation letter once.

Then folded it carefully.

She held it against her chest.

Her voice trembled.

> "...You apologized again."

A tear rolled down her face.

> "You never had to."

---

# **INITIAL SEARCH**

Security cameras tracked Corin until she exited the city limits surrounding KCA Headquarters.

After that...

Nothing.

She deliberately avoided monitored transportation.

She remained on foot.

No destination could be established.

No evidence suggested criminal involvement or coercion.

Medical Services assessed that Corin had left voluntarily while experiencing severe emotional distress.

Executive authorization for a discreet welfare search was issued immediately.

Personnel were specifically instructed:

- Do not pursue aggressively.
    
- Do not surround her.
    
- Do not force her return.
    
- If located, prioritize her immediate safety and speak calmly.
    

The objective was not to bring Corin back against her will.

The objective was to ensure she was alive.

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ORDER**

Director Lucious personally suspended all discussion regarding Corin's resignation.

His written order contained only three sentences.

> "Her position can be replaced."

> "Her paperwork can be reassigned."

> "Corin cannot."

He signed the order without further comment.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin Lockwood's resignation remains on file but has not been formally accepted pending Executive review.

Her office at KCA Headquarters and her place within Project SAFEHOUSE remain untouched.

No replacement has been assigned.

Across both KCA Headquarters and Heaven's Blacksite, one sentiment was repeated by nearly everyone involved.

They were not worried about losing an accountant.

They were worried about losing Corin.

**End of Critical Personnel Incident Report**
