# **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE**

## **RESIDENT WELFARE LOG**

### **ONGOING OBSERVATION – BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Medical & Resident Welfare

---

## **SUMMARY**

Over an extended period, Belle Merryweather's recurring nightmares have gradually increased in severity.

Although she continues to fulfill her responsibilities as Director with determination, the cumulative effects of interrupted sleep and emotional strain have begun to manifest physically.

Medical personnel have documented occasional episodes in which Belle loses consciousness following prolonged exhaustion or overwhelming emotional stress.

---

## **MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

Belle's episodes are preceded by one or more of the following signs:

- Noticeable fatigue.
    
- Reduced reaction time.
    
- Difficulty maintaining focus.
    
- Pale complexion.
    
- Unsteady posture.
    
- Brief dizziness.
    

If exposed to significant emotional distress while already exhausted, Belle may suddenly collapse from a combination of fatigue and emotional overload.

These episodes are typically brief.

Medical evaluations have found no evidence of physical injury during the loss of consciousness itself, though the underlying sleep deprivation remains an active concern.

---

## **BLACKSITE RESPONSE**

Without any formal order ever being issued, personnel gradually developed the same instinctive reaction.

If Belle begins to lose her balance—

Someone catches her.

Whether it is in the Blacksite...

At KCA Headquarters...

During a meeting...

Or in the Starlands...

Someone is almost always close enough to keep her from hitting the ground.

It has become an unspoken habit shared across departments.

---

## **INCIDENT EXCERPTS**

**SAFEHOUSE – 07:41**

Belle stood from the breakfast table before pausing mid-step.

She swayed slightly.

Before she could fall, Endmin caught her by the shoulders.

Belle never reached the floor.

---

**KCA Headquarters – 13:18**

Following several consecutive meetings, Belle quietly excused herself.

Only a few steps later, her knees buckled.

Kent caught her before anyone else realized what had happened.

Medical was called immediately.

---

**Blacksite Corridor – 16:02**

Belle was speaking with researchers when she suddenly stopped responding.

Remielle was already beside her before Belle began to fall.

She carried Belle to Medical while the corridor was cleared.

## **MEDICAL RECOMMENDATION**

Medical Services recommends:

- Immediate evaluation following any collapse.
    
- Opportunities for uninterrupted sleep whenever operationally possible.
    
- Reduced workload after an episode.
    
- Continued observation by trusted colleagues until Belle has fully recovered.
