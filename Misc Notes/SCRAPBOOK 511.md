# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **MISSING PERSON / ANOMALOUS INCIDENT LOG**

# **THE EMPTY ROOM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **MISSING**  
**Last Confirmed Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Current Location:** **UNKNOWN**

---

# **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

Corin is missing.

There was no struggle.

No damaged furniture.

No broken window.

No obvious sign of forced entry.

The treehouse was simply found empty.

Her usual belongings remain.

Her coat remains.

Her hat remains.

Her scarf remains.

Her bluejay caretaker's supplies remain.

Her notebook does not.

The notebook was found on the floor near the doorway.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

The majority of the notebook consists of Corin's handwriting.

The entries become increasingly disturbing as they approach the final pages.

There are repeated themes of:

- Worthlessness.
    
- Fear of abandonment.
    
- Feeling like a burden.
    
- Hatred toward her own body.
    
- Belief that others would be happier without her.
    
- Confusion regarding her continued existence.
    
- Fear that Orphie will eventually leave.
    
- Resentment toward her own dependence on others.
    
- Repeated questioning of whether she deserves to be cared for.
    

Several pages contain sentences that have been crossed out repeatedly.

Some are almost illegible.

The final readable entries include:

> **I don't know why everyone keeps trying.**

> **I'm already broken.**

> **I can't even talk anymore.**

> **Everyone has to take care of me.**

> **What am I giving back?**

Another page reads:

> **Orphie deserves someone better.**

The final complete sentence is:

> **I don't want anyone to have to keep finding me.**

The page ends there.

---

# **THE DOORWAY**

A faint puddle of Ether was discovered immediately beside the treehouse entrance.

Unlike the Ether Corin occasionally coughs up, this sample remained coherent considerably longer.

It did not immediately disintegrate.

The substance slowly lost its luminosity and structure over several minutes.

No physical residue remained afterward.

No trail was discovered.

No footprints were found leading away from the treehouse.

No conventional tracking method has produced a result.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light arrived first.

She immediately recognized the notebook.

She read the final pages.

She stopped.

For several seconds, she did not move.

Then she looked toward the doorway.

> **"Corin?"**

No response.

She called again.

Nothing.

Guiding Light searched the entire treehouse.

Nothing.

Her attempts to locate Corin through conventional Architectal perception failed.

This has deeply concerned her.

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT**

Curious Light examined the Ether residue.

For perhaps the first time, he did not immediately begin theorizing.

He simply stared at it.

> **"...This isn't from her coughing."**

Guiding Light looked at him.

> **"What is it?"**

Curious Light shook his head.

> **"I don't know."**

That answer frightened both of them.

---

# **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT**

Mischevious Light arrived shortly afterward.

The usual red glow appeared.

The spiral manifested.

He entered the treehouse.

He saw the notebook.

He read the final page.

His expression changed completely.

There was no joke.

No teasing.

No childish behavior.

Only silence.

He eventually asked:

> **"How long?"**

Guiding Light answered:

> **"We don't know."**

Mischevious Light looked toward the doorway.

> **"...Then we find her."**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie arrived soon after.

She recognized Corin's handwriting immediately.

She read everything.

She did not speak for several minutes.

Then she picked up the notebook.

She held it against herself.

Guiding Light approached her.

Orphie shook her head.

She pointed toward the doorway.

Then wrote on a blank page:

> **"She's still out there."**

Guiding Light nodded.

> **"Yes."**

Orphie wrote:

> **"Then we're bringing her home."**

---

# **THE SEARCH**

The search has expanded beyond the treehouse.

Aperture personnel have been instructed to monitor:

- The surrounding forest.
    
- Blacksite perimeter routes.
    
- Known anomalous locations.
    
- Ether fluctuations.
    
- Dimensional disturbances.
    
- Unusual Architectal activity.
    
- Any appearance of Corin's belongings.
    
- Any traces matching the unidentified Ether.
    

The Architects have begun searching independently.

No one is treating the notebook as proof that Corin is gone permanently.

It is evidence that she was suffering.

Nothing more.

---

# **JAY**

Jay has been unusually quiet.

He remains inside the treehouse.

He repeatedly returns to the doorway.

He looks outside.

Then returns.

Guiding Light eventually sits beside him.

> **"We'll find her."**

Jay chirps softly.

For once, there is no mischief.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Corin:** Missing.

**Body:** Not located.

**Cause of disappearance:** Unknown.

**Evidence of external intrusion:** None.

**Ether residue:** Confirmed; behavior differs from normal expelled Ether.

**Notebook:** Recovered.

**Psychological state before disappearance:** Severe distress and profound self-worthlessness.

**Evidence of death:** None.

**Evidence of survival:** Inconclusive.

**Search status:** **ACTIVE**

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

The treehouse is too quiet.

Corin's chair is empty.

Her notebook is gone from where she normally keeps it.

Her hat remains.

Her scarf remains.

Everything she left behind is exactly where it was.

Except Corin.

Guiding Light refuses to accept that the notebook's final words are a conclusion.

Mischevious Light has already begun searching.

Curious Light is studying the Ether.

Orphie refuses to leave the doorway for long.

And somewhere beyond the trees, nobody knows where Corin is.

But the search has only just begun.

**End of Missing Person / Anomalous Incident Log**
