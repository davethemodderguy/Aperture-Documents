# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL OBSERVATION REPORT

**Subject:** Resident Reactions to Alice's Post-Recovery Condition  
**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL  
**Status:** Ongoing  
**Primary Subject:** Alice  
**Related Individuals:** SAFEHOUSE Residents / Corin / Orphie

---

# I. OVERVIEW

Following Alice's recovery and subsequent demonstrations of restored physical capability, C.O.R.E. personnel conducted informal observations of the SAFEHOUSE residents.

The general expectation was that Alice's transformation from a physically weak, frequently fainting child into an anomalously capable individual would significantly alter how the residents interacted with her.

It did not.

For the most part, the SAFEHOUSE residents continue to treat Alice exactly as they did before.

The primary difference is that everyone now knows the tiny pencil clipped to Alice's coat can become a battle axe capable of cutting through Nekonium.

This has resulted in a number of... interesting reactions.

---

# II. CORIN — "BIG SIS"

Corin's reaction was arguably the most complicated.

She was relieved.

Then worried.

Then relieved again.

Then worried again.

The fact that Alice could now defend herself did not make Corin feel better in the way C.O.R.E. initially expected.

Corin had spent a considerable amount of time worrying about Alice's safety while she was missing.

Seeing Alice standing upright again, eating normally, moving around on her own, and actually showing signs of confidence was deeply reassuring.

Seeing the weapon responsible for that confidence was considerably less reassuring.

When shown the Nekonium test footage, Corin reportedly stared at the recording for several seconds before looking at Alice.

> **Corin:** "...Alice."

> **Alice:** "Hm?"

> **Corin:** "That's a battle axe."

Alice looked down at the pencil clipped to her coat.

> **Alice:** "...Yeah."

> **Corin:** "That's a _huge_ battle axe."

> **Alice:** "...Yeah."

A pause followed.

> **Corin:** "...And you carry it around as a pencil?"

> **Alice:** "Mhm."

Corin reportedly sighed.

She then crouched down and hugged Alice.

Not because she was afraid of her.

Because she was relieved that Alice was still there.

Corin's current position is straightforward:

**Alice being able to protect herself is good.**

**Alice believing she has to protect everyone herself is not.**

Corin continues to regard herself as Alice's Big Sis and remains highly protective of her.

She has also made it clear that Alice is still allowed to ask for help.

---

# III. ORPHIE — "BIGGER SIS"

Orphie's reaction was considerably quieter.

She did not initially focus on the axe.

She focused on Alice.

Orphie observed Alice walking around the SAFEHOUSE, interacting with residents, eating, drawing, and generally behaving like herself.

The most important thing to Orphie was not that Alice had become stronger.

It was that Alice was **still Alice.**

When Alice showed Orphie the pencil, Orphie examined it briefly.

> **Alice:** "...It's my pencil."

> **Orphie:** "I know."

> **Alice:** "...It can do something."

> **Orphie:** "I know."

Alice then demonstrated its true form.

The battle axe appeared.

Orphie looked at it.

Then looked at Alice.

Then looked back at the axe.

> **Orphie:** "...You were carrying that around this entire time?"

Alice nodded.

> **Alice:** "Mhm."

Orphie simply sighed and gently patted Alice's head.

> **Orphie:** "You're still little."

Alice blinked.

> **Alice:** "...I know."

The incident became one of the clearest examples of Orphie's attitude toward Alice.

The weapon did not make Alice less deserving of protection.

It simply meant Alice had one more way to protect herself.

Orphie remains Alice's **Bigger Sis**.

---

# IV. NEKO

Neko's reaction was notably different.

Neko was initially interested in the weapon's material interaction.

Specifically:

**The fact that it cut through Nekonium.**

Nekonium represented an extremely high point in Neko's own material engineering.

Alice's axe cutting through a one-meter Nekonium pillar was therefore not merely impressive.

It was personally offensive to the laws of engineering.

Neko reportedly examined the damaged pillar for an extended period.

Then examined the axe.

Then examined Alice.

> **Neko:** "...How?"

Alice shrugged.

> **Alice:** "I hit it."

Neko apparently had no immediate response to this.

Despite the scientific implications, Neko does not treat Alice differently.

If anything, Neko has become more interested in determining what exactly the pencil/axe is capable of.

Alice, meanwhile, remains completely uninterested in explaining the weapon.

---

# V. MISS NEKO

Miss Neko was less interested in the weapon and more interested in Alice's behavior.

Her primary observation was that Alice had become more willing to move around independently.

She no longer appeared constantly exhausted.

She was eating.

She was walking.

She was interacting.

She was beginning to act like a normal six-year-old again.

Miss Neko reportedly considers this considerably more important than the battle axe.

Her reaction to the axe itself was essentially:

> "...That's certainly a weapon."

Followed by returning her attention to Alice.

---

# VI. NEKO'S HOUSEHOLD

Niko's reaction was largely one of surprise.

The existence of an apparently harmless pencil that could transform into an enormous battle axe was difficult to reconcile with Alice's tiny frame.

Niko's primary concern, however, remained whether Alice was okay.

The SAFEHOUSE's general attitude toward Alice has remained remarkably consistent:

If Alice wants to play, let her play.

If Alice wants to draw, let her draw.

If Alice wants to sit quietly, let her sit quietly.

If Alice wants to be carried, carry her.

And if someone threatens her—

The pencil comes off the coat.

---

# VII. BELLE MERRRYWEATHER

Belle's reaction was immediate.

She was happy.

Very, very happy.

Belle had spent considerable time worrying about Alice during her disappearance.

Seeing Alice healthy again was enough to make Belle noticeably more energetic around her.

The battle axe did not particularly change that.

Belle's first instinct remained to hug Alice.

Her second was to check whether Alice wanted to go somewhere.

Her third was apparently to ask:

> "Can I see the axe?"

Alice showed her.

Belle stared.

> "...Whoa."

Then, after several seconds:

> "...Can you still ride on my back?"

Alice nodded.

> "Mhm."

Belle reportedly smiled.

That was apparently all she needed to hear.

To Belle, Alice being strong is nice.

Alice being **back** is what matters.

---

# VIII. ENDMIN MERRYWEATHER

Endmin's response was calmer.

He was pleased to see Alice healthy and considerably more alert than before.

The battle axe was treated with appropriate caution.

Endmin's general attitude could be summarized as:

> "Good. She's capable of defending herself."

followed shortly afterward by:

> "...But someone should still keep an eye on her."

The latter statement remains consistent with SAFEHOUSE policy.

---

# IX. FROST

Frost reacted with curiosity.

The weapon's size was immediately noticeable.

Alice's ability to use it without apparent difficulty was even more noticeable.

Frost reportedly asked Alice whether it was heavy.

Alice answered:

> "...Not really."

Frost apparently found this concerning.

Alice did not.

Frost continues to treat Alice normally and remains attentive to her wellbeing.

---

# X. BAO

Bao's reaction was primarily parental concern.

The axe itself was impressive.

Alice's restored appetite was more important.

Bao reportedly paid considerably more attention to whether Alice was eating and drinking than to what weapon she had clipped to her coat.

When Alice was asked whether she was hungry, she initially said no.

Bao offered food anyway.

Alice eventually ate.

No further comment was made.

---

# XI. TAIGA

Taiga's response was relatively simple:

Alice is stronger.

Good.

Alice is happier.

Better.

Alice has an enormous battle axe disguised as a pencil.

...Interesting.

Taiga did not appear particularly frightened by the weapon.

The general conclusion was that if Alice trusted the people around her, there was little reason for anyone else to fear her.

---

# XII. MART

Mart was among the residents most visibly surprised by the contrast.

The same tiny rabbit girl who once appeared too weak to stand for extended periods was now casually carrying around an anomalous battle axe.

Mart reportedly found the situation difficult to process.

Especially because Alice herself seemed completely normal.

She could be sitting quietly with a drawing one moment and potentially bisecting an armored target the next.

The important distinction, however, was not lost on Mart:

Alice does not appear interested in hurting people.

The weapon is defensive.

Her hostility is directed toward those who threaten the people she cares about.

---

# XIII. ASTRAL

Astral's reaction was similarly focused on Alice rather than the weapon.

The recovery represented something considerably more meaningful than increased physical capability.

Alice had spent months believing that she was unwanted, forgotten, or burdensome.

Now she was walking around the SAFEHOUSE again.

She was interacting with people.

She was eating.

She was carrying her little pencil.

And she was allowing people to stay close to her.

Astral considered this progress.

The axe was merely an anomalous footnote.

---

# XIV. GENERAL SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE

Despite the enormous difference between Alice's former and current physical capabilities, the SAFEHOUSE has not begun treating her as a weapon.

She remains:

**Alice.**

The little white rabbit thiren.

The quiet child.

The girl who likes to draw.

The girl who sometimes needs to be carried.

The girl who still worries about bothering people.

The girl who keeps a pencil clipped to her tiny coat.

The fact that the pencil can become an axe capable of cutting through Nekonium has not changed the way the SAFEHOUSE sees her.

If anything, it has produced a somewhat unusual consensus:

> **Alice is allowed to be strong.**
> 
> **Alice is allowed to be scared.**
> 
> **Alice is allowed to ask for help.**
> 
> **Alice is allowed to still be a child.**

---

# XV. C.O.R.E. OBSERVATION

The most significant finding from the SAFEHOUSE interviews was not fear.

It was normalization.

Nobody appears particularly interested in making Alice prove how strong she has become.

Nobody is asking her to demonstrate the axe.

Nobody is treating her like a security asset.

Corin still calls herself her Big Sis.

Orphie still calls herself her Bigger Sis.

Belle still wants to give her piggyback rides.

Bao still worries about whether she has eaten.

And Alice still wanders around with a tiny pencil clipped to her coat.

The weapon changed.

Alice didn't.

And perhaps that is the healthiest thing about her recovery.
