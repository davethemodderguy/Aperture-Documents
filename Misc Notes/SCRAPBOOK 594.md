## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **TREEHOUSE RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — WAKING UP**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Location:** Treehouse  
**Subjects:** Corin / Orphie  
**Status:** Awake / Recovering

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin eventually woke up on Orphie's lap.

She remained still for a few moments, apparently unaware that she had returned to her normal form.

Her wings were still partially spread.

One gave a slow, sleepy flap.

Then another.

Corin slowly opened her eyes.

---

# **REALIZATION**

She looked down.

Then at her hands.

Then behind herself.

Her wings were there.

Full-sized.

Corin blinked several times.

She had not seen her normal body in quite some time.

She looked toward Orphie.

Orphie was still half-asleep.

Corin quietly asked:

> **"...Did I grow?"**

Orphie opened one eye.

> **"Apparently."**

Corin looked at herself again.

A small smile appeared on her face.

---

# **THE WINGS**

Corin stretched.

Her wings opened fully for the first time in a while.

Several feathers shifted and settled.

She gave them a gentle flap.

Then another.

They moved normally.

There was no visible weakness.

No pain.

No instability.

Corin paused.

She seemed almost surprised.

She could feel them working again.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie sat up slightly.

> **"How do you feel?"**

Corin thought for a moment.

Then answered:

> **"...Better."**

That was enough.

Orphie smiled.

Corin leaned against her shoulder.

Her wings folded loosely around them both.

---

# **A SMALL TEST**

Corin slowly stood.

She remained upright without difficulty.

She stretched her arms.

Then her wings.

Then she carefully bent her knees.

No dizziness.

No collapse.

No pain.

She looked toward the open doorway.

Outside was the Treehouse's familiar surroundings.

Corin smiled again.

She was not completely recovered.

But she was back.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin had spent a long time as a tiny white winged kitten.

She had been weak.

Silent.

In pain.

Unable to return to her normal form.

Now she was standing again.

Her wings were moving.

Her voice had returned.

And she could finally look down at herself and recognize the person she had been before the illness.

Corin took one more deep breath.

Then quietly said:

> **"I missed being tall."**

Orphie laughed.

**End of Treehouse Residential Observation**
