# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **FACILITIES & SECURITY DIVISION**

### **VENTILATION NETWORK – OCCUPANCY REPORT**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The KCA ventilation network has developed several unconventional areas of permanent Mart occupancy.

What was originally intended to be a purely functional ventilation system now contains:

- A dedicated **Mart Meeting Room**.
    
- A former office belonging to **Belle Merryweather**, now functioning as a secondary Mart Nest.
    

Neither area was formally designed for Mart habitation.

Both have nevertheless become permanent fixtures.

---

# **I. MART MEETING ROOM**

Deep within the KCA ventilation network is a dedicated chamber informally designated:

> **Mart Meeting Room**

The room is large enough to accommodate a significant number of Marts simultaneously.

Its exact purpose is unclear.

Marts routinely gather there for extended periods, apparently to discuss matters concerning the colony.

Unfortunately, their conversations remain:

> **100% words.**

> **0% grammar.**

Common observations include:

> "Security!"

> "Cheese!"

> "Vent!"

> "Happy!"

> "Danger!"

> "Hello!"

> "Mart!"

No human observer has successfully determined what constitutes an official meeting.

---

## **MEETING ROOM FURNISHINGS**

The room contains:

- Several small cushions.
    
- Shelves containing cheese crackers.
    
- A water basin.
    
- Ventilation access points.
    
- Several tiny objects of unknown importance.
    
- A whiteboard.
    

The whiteboard is covered in writing.

Most entries consist of:

> "MART"

> "MART!"

> "MART?"

Nobody has erased it.

---

# **II. FORMER BELLE MERRYWEATHER OFFICE**

The KCA previously assigned an office to **Belle Merryweather**, the sentient Rabbit Thiren plushie.

Belle now performs much of her work remotely from the Heaven Blacksite.

Her former office remains completely intact.

No renovation was authorized.

No furniture was removed.

No personal modifications were made.

The only significant change is the presence of Marts.

---

# **CURRENT CONDITION**

The office remains exactly as Belle left it.

The:

- Desk.
    
- Chair.
    
- Majority of the original flooring.
    
- Walls.
    
- Fixtures.
    
- General arrangement.
    

remain unchanged.

The Marts have simply...

Moved in.

The room is now informally designated:

> **Secondary Mart Nest – Belle's Office**

---

# **MART OCCUPANCY**

Marts use the former office for:

- Sleeping.
    
- Resting.
    
- Playing.
    
- Reading.
    
- Eating cheese crackers.
    
- Meeting.
    
- Hiding during games.
    
- Occasionally sitting on Belle's old chair.
    

The desk remains largely untouched.

Marts appear to understand that it belongs to Belle.

One Mart was observed sitting beside the desk rather than on top of it.

When asked why, it simply replied:

> "Belle."

---

# **BELLE'S REACTION**

Belle was informed that her old office had become a Mart Nest.

She visited the KCA remotely through a video call.

The camera showed approximately fifty Marts gathered around her old desk.

Belle stared at the screen.

> "...You guys took my office."

The Marts collectively responded:

> "Hello!"

Belle laughed.

> "...Fine."

She then added:

> "...Just don't break anything."

The Marts immediately responded:

> "No!"

---

# **FACILITIES INSPECTION**

KCA Facilities has conducted repeated inspections of the former office.

Results:

**Structural damage:** None.

**Furniture damage:** None.

**Desk condition:** Unchanged.

**Chair condition:** Unchanged.

**Floor condition:** Mostly unchanged.

**Mart population:** Excessive.

**Cheese cracker debris:** Moderate.

The Facilities Department has concluded that the Marts are unusually respectful occupants.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The KCA ventilation system now contains a permanent Mart community infrastructure.

The **Mart Meeting Room** serves as an informal gathering point.

The former **Belle Merryweather Office** serves as a secondary nest.

Neither location interferes with KCA operations.

In fact, Security has found the arrangement useful.

If a large number of Marts suddenly disappear from the main facility...

Personnel now know where to look.

The answer is usually:

> **"The vents."**

And if they aren't there?

Check Belle's old office.

They're probably sleeping.

**End of Occupancy Report**
