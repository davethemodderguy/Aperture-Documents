## **SUMMARY**

At approximately 15:42, Taiga challenged Belle to eat an entire fresh lemon.

Not lemonade.

Not a slice.

The whole fruit.

Belle accepted without hesitation.

---

## **EVENT LOG**

Taiga placed a freshly cut lemon onto the kitchen table.

> **Taiga:** "Bet you can't eat the whole thing."

Belle looked at it.

Looked back at Taiga.

> **Belle:** "...Can I have salt?"

> **Taiga:** "No."

Belle shrugged.

> "...Okay."

She picked up the lemon.

Everyone stopped what they were doing.

Even Nightshade looked up from her book.

Belle took one bite.

Silence.

Another bite.

Silence.

Halfway through...

Her rabbit ears slowly folded backwards.

Her eyes watered.

Her nose twitched.

But she kept eating.

Thirty seconds later...

The entire lemon was gone.

Peel included.

---

## **POST-INCIDENT OBSERVATION**

Belle remained completely still for approximately ten seconds.

Her face was perfectly expressionless.

Then—

> "...Sour."

She immediately accepted a glass of water from Endmin.

Followed by three carrots.
