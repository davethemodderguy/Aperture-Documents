---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the government's request for Aperture Industries to secure **50 tons of solid gold for fourteen days**, an unusual storage decision was made.

Rather than place the shipment inside a conventional vault, the gold was transferred into the **10th floor of SAFEHOUSE's Pocket-Dimensional Closet**.

The floor has subsequently become known as:

> **"Mart Nest."**

This decision has resulted in a mixture of confusion, amusement, concern, and several very enthusiastic Marts.

---

# **THE MART NEST**

Marts are small water-based beings created by **Lumi**.

They are characterized by:

- Water-like bodies.
    
- Top hats.
    
- Deer antlers.
    
- High levels of happiness.
    
- An existence sustained by **happiness**.
    

The 10th floor of the Pocket-Dimensional Closet has historically served as a large Mart living area.

It is now also housing fifty tons of government gold.

The gold has been placed in a secured section of the floor.

The Marts have been instructed:

> **DO NOT TOUCH THE GOLD.**

The Marts have unanimously agreed.

Whether they understand why is less certain.
