# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE / FAMILY VISITATION LOG**

# **ONE DAY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Sensitive Family Record  
**Subjects:** Corin / Orphie / Lumi  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Visit Duration:** Approximately 24 hours  
**Status:** **EMOTIONALLY UNRESOLVED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

After more than **five months without seeing her daughters**, Lumi finally visited the treehouse.

She came because she missed them.

Especially Corin.

She had not forgotten her daughter.

She had not stopped caring.

She had simply been overwhelmed by her responsibilities within the Blacksite.

Unfortunately, knowing this did not make the reunion easy.

---

# **THE ARRIVAL**

Corin was sitting outside the treehouse when Lumi arrived.

She immediately recognized her.

For several seconds, neither moved.

Then Corin stood.

> **Corin:**  
> "...Mom?"

Lumi smiled.

> **Lumi:**  
> "Hey, Corin."

Corin rushed toward her.

Lumi caught her in a hug.

Corin held onto her tightly.

She did not say anything.

Neither did Lumi.

For a while, there was no need.

---

# **THE DAY TOGETHER**

Lumi stayed for the entire day.

She cooked.

She talked with Orphie.

She listened to Corin talk about the treehouse.

She watched the two girls spend time together.

For the first time in months, the treehouse felt like a family home again.

Corin was visibly happier.

She rarely left Lumi's side.

Even when she eventually sat down, she remained close enough to touch her.

---

# **THE PROBLEM**

The reunion itself was not what went wrong.

It was a short conversation later that evening.

Lumi and Corin were sitting together outside.

The forest was quiet.

Corin's scarf was wrapped around her neck.

Her red wool beanie covered most of her head.

After a long silence, Corin finally asked:

> **Corin:**  
> "...Do you hate what happened to me?"

Lumi looked confused.

> **Lumi:**  
> "What?"

Corin hesitated.

> **"...My body."**

Lumi immediately shook her head.

> **"No. Of course not."**

Corin looked down.

> **"Then why did you stay away?"**

Lumi's expression changed.

She knew this question was coming.

She simply wasn't prepared for it.

> **Lumi:**  
> "Corin, I was busy."

Corin's face fell slightly.

Lumi continued:

> **"I had Arcturus. Norma. The Marts. Everything happening in the Blacksite..."**

She sighed.

> **"I couldn't just leave everything behind."**

Corin quietly replied:

> **"...So you chose them."**

Lumi immediately realized what her words had sounded like.

> **"That's not what I meant."**

But the damage was already done.

---

# **THE MISUNDERSTANDING**

Corin looked away.

> **"It's okay."**

Lumi frowned.

> **"Corin—"**

> **"I understand."**

She pulled her scarf higher.

> **"You have other things that matter more."**

> **Lumi:**  
> "No."

> **Corin:**  
> "You don't have to explain."

Lumi reached for her.

Corin moved slightly away.

Not angrily.

Just hurt.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie had been inside the treehouse.

She heard the conversation.

She knew immediately what had happened.

Lumi had not meant to reject Corin.

Corin had not meant to accuse Lumi.

They had simply found the wrong words for something that had been hurting for five months.

---

# **LUMI'S REALIZATION**

Lumi finally understood what Corin had actually been asking.

Not:

> _"Why were you busy?"_

But:

> **"Why didn't you come see me?"**

And beneath that:

> **"Did you still want me?"**

Lumi's voice softened.

> **"Corin..."**

Corin didn't look at her.

> **"I'm sorry."**

Lumi sat beside her.

> **"I missed you."**

Corin's eyes began to water.

> **"...Then why didn't you come?"**

Lumi had no good answer.

Not one that could undo five months.

> **"I should have."**

---

# **THE VISIT CONTINUES**

Lumi did not leave immediately.

She stayed.

She did not try to force Corin to talk.

She simply remained beside her.

Eventually, Corin leaned against her shoulder.

Lumi wrapped an arm around her.

No explanation could fix everything in one conversation.

But at least they were together.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S OBSERVATION**

Guiding Light watched from the edge of the forest.

She understood the irony.

She had once struggled with the same problem.

Trying to help someone.

Choosing the right intentions.

And still finding the wrong words.

Lumi's love for Corin was genuine.

Corin's hurt was genuine.

Neither canceled the other.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Lumi came because she missed her daughter.

She stayed because she still loved her.

The reunion was real.

The happiness was real.

And so was the hurt.

One poorly phrased explanation was enough to reopen months of doubt.

But unlike the tragedies of the past, this time nobody walked away.

Lumi stayed.

Corin stayed.

Orphie stayed.

The conversation had gone wrong.

**The relationship had not.**

**End of SAFEHOUSE / Family Visitation Log**
