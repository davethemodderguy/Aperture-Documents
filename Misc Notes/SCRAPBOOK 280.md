### **SUBJECT: CUSTOMER SUPPORT OFFICE – DIALYN**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Security & Executive Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following several security audits, Dialyn's office has been designated a **Hardened Communications Workspace**.

Although it appears to be an ordinary customer support office, numerous concealed emergency tools have been integrated into the room.

The philosophy behind the design is simple:

> **"If Customer Support becomes the front line, help may not arrive immediately."**

Every concealed item exists to improve survivability during an emergency.

---

# **CONCEALED EQUIPMENT**

## **Desk Compartment**

Mounted beneath Dialyn's reinforced desk are two secured firearms.

**Primary**

- MP7 Personal Defense Weapon (PDW)
    

**Secondary**

- Compact sawed-off shotgun
    

Both weapons are stored in concealed locking brackets and are accessible only through emergency release procedures.

---

## **Emergency Breaching Tool**

Concealed within a removable wall panel is a hardened **Halligan bar**.

Primary uses include:

- Forcing jammed doors.
    
- Emergency evacuation.
    
- Rescue operations.
    
- Limited defensive applications if absolutely necessary.
    

The panel is intentionally unmarked.

Most visitors never notice it.

---

## **Emergency Lighting**

Several high-output flashlights are hidden beneath one of the desk drawers.

They are automatically recharged while stored.

The lights are intended for:

- Power outages.
    
- Smoke-filled environments.
    
- Search operations.
    
- Emergency evacuations.
    

Dialyn has admitted that she occasionally forgets they are there because of her Ether Vision.

---

## **Chair Holster**

Attached beneath the office chair is a concealed pistol holster.

The sidearm remains secured and out of sight during normal operation.

Its purpose is to provide immediate access should Dialyn become separated from the equipment beneath her desk.

Routine inspections confirm the weapon remains unloaded until required under KCA security protocols.

---

# **OFFICE APPEARANCE**

Despite the extensive emergency equipment, Dialyn's office remains unusually welcoming.

Visitors are far more likely to notice:

- Potted plants.
    
- A white fluffy cat sleeping nearby.
    
- Small floating crowns resting above her head.
    
- Family photographs.
    
- Notes from coworkers.
    
- A pot of Snow Blossoms from Corin and Corey.
    

Most people leave believing it is simply a cheerful customer support office.

Very few realize how thoroughly prepared it is.
