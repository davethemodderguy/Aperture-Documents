**STATUS:** ACTIVE INVESTIGATION  
**SUBJECT:** Nora Tachyon — Blue Moon Phenomenon  
**SECONDARY SUBJECT:** Guiding Light

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon has provided additional information regarding the entity she encountered immediately before arriving at SAFEHOUSE.

Her previous description was limited to:

> **“A blue moon talked to me.”**

At the time, this was considered insufficiently specific to identify the phenomenon.

That assessment has changed.

During a conversation with Neri, Nora provided additional details concerning the encounter.

The description contains multiple characteristics associated with **Guiding Light**.

Neri recognized the pattern almost immediately.

---

# 2. NORA'S DESCRIPTION

The conversation occurred inside Neri's subspace room.

Nora was sitting on one of the smaller floating islands.

Neri was working nearby.

After several minutes of silence:

> **Nora:**  
> “Big sis?”

Neri looked up.

> **Neri:**  
> “Hm?”

Nora pointed toward the artificial stars above them.

> **Nora:**  
> “The blue moon.”

Neri stopped working.

> **Neri:**  
> “What about it?”

Nora thought for a moment.

> **Nora:**  
> “It wasn't really a moon.”

Neri turned toward her.

> **Neri:**  
> “What do you mean?”

> **Nora:**  
> “It looked like one.”

Pause.

> **Nora:**  
> “But it was blue.”

> **Nora:**  
> “Really blue.”

Neri remained silent.

> **Nora:**  
> “And there was a crack in the sky.”

Neri's expression changed.

---

# 3. THE RIFT

Nora described the opening as a **blue-colored rift**.

According to her:

- it appeared suddenly;
    
- there was no visible physical structure supporting it;
    
- the surrounding area became illuminated by blue light;
    
- the rift remained stable long enough for Nora to see through it;
    
- the “moon” was visible near or within the phenomenon;
    
- and a voice came from the blue light.
    

Nora could not provide a conventional location.

She could not determine whether the moon was physically present or simply an appearance created by the entity.

She also stated that the environment around the rift felt:

> **“Quiet.”**

Not empty.

Not dark.

Just quiet.

---

# 4. THE VOICE

Nora was unable to reproduce the voice accurately.

She described it as:

> **“Nice.”**

Then:

> **“Like someone who already knew me.”**

Neri asked:

> **“What did it say?”**

Nora answered:

> **“It told me not to be scared.”**

Pause.

> **Nora:**  
> “Then it asked if I wanted to go home.”

Neri stopped moving.

> **Neri:**  
> “Home?”

Nora nodded.

> **Nora:**  
> “Yeah.”

> **Nora:**  
> “I thought it meant you.”

---

# 5. NERI'S RECOGNITION

At this point, Neri asked Nora to describe the blue moon again.

Nora repeated the description.

Blue light.

A rift.

A moon-like shape.

A familiar voice.

A feeling of being watched over rather than threatened.

Neri stared at the floor.

Then at the artificial sky above her room.

Then at the azure light surrounding one of the floating islands.

She finally spoke.

> **Neri:**  
> “Guiding.”

Nora blinked.

> **Nora:**  
> “Who?”

Neri looked at her.

> **Neri:**  
> “Guiding Light.”

Nora's eyes widened.

> **Nora:**  
> “You know her?”

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:**  
> “Yes.”

---

# 6. HISTORICAL CORRELATION

Neri's recognition is considered significant.

Guiding Light is an Architect of Reality.

She is capable of interacting with reality through mechanisms that are not fully understood by conventional Blacksite science.

Relevant characteristics include:

- blue light manifestations;
    
- dimensional/rift phenomena;
    
- localized reality manipulation;
    
- artificial celestial imagery;
    
- movement between otherwise inaccessible spaces;
    
- direct interaction with anomalous residents;
    
- and the ability to appear in forms that may not correspond to her physical existence.
    

The description provided by Nora is therefore highly consistent with previous manifestations associated with Guiding Light.

No evidence currently indicates that Nora encountered a separate entity resembling Guiding Light.

The working identification is:

> **BLUE MOON PHENOMENON = GUIDING LIGHT**

Confidence: **HIGH.**

---

# 7. THE LATE-NIGHT VISIT

Neri did not immediately confront Guiding Light.

She waited.

The SAFEHOUSE eventually became quiet.

Nora was asleep.

The artificial stars above the primary island were dim.

Neri remained awake for several minutes.

Then she got out of bed.

She put on her coat.

She quietly crossed one of the bridges.

She left her subspace room.

---

# 8. SEARCH

Neri did not use any equipment.

She simply walked.

She eventually reached a quiet section of the Blacksite where the lights were reduced and almost no personnel were present.

There was no announcement.

No security alert.

No dramatic rift.

Just a small patch of blue light appearing against the darkness.

Neri stopped.

The blue light expanded.

A familiar figure appeared.

**Guiding Light.**

She looked at Neri.

Neri looked back.

Neither spoke for several seconds.

Then:

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “You know.”

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:**  
> “Nora told me.”

Guiding smiled faintly.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “I wondered when you would figure it out.”

Neri sat down.

> **Neri:**  
> “Why didn't you tell me?”

Guiding did not immediately answer.

---

# 9. THE CONVERSATION

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Because Nora needed to arrive here herself.”

Neri looked down.

> **Neri:**  
> “You knew she was alive.”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Yes.”

Silence.

> **Neri:**  
> “For how long?”

Guiding paused.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Long enough.”

Neri did not look particularly pleased with the answer.

> **Neri:**  
> “That's not an answer.”

Guiding laughed softly.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “No. It isn't.”

Neri looked toward the blue rift.

> **Neri:**  
> “Why me?”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Because she was looking for you.”

Neri's eyes lowered.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “And because you were looking for her.”

Neri remained silent.

---

# 10. THE BLUE MOON

Neri eventually asked the question that had apparently been bothering her.

> **Neri:**  
> “Why a moon?”

Guiding looked upward.

For a moment, the blue light above them formed the shape Nora had described.

A small blue moon.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “It was easier for her to understand.”

> **Neri:**  
> “You could have just appeared.”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “I could have.”

> **Neri:**  
> “You didn't.”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “No.”

Pause.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “She was frightened.”

Neri understood.

---

# 11. WHAT NORA REMEMBERED

Neri asked whether Nora remembered everything.

Guiding shook her head.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Not everything.”

> **Neri:**  
> “Why?”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Because some things are easier to survive when they aren't remembered all at once.”

Neri did not ask for clarification.

She simply nodded.

After a while:

> **Neri:**  
> “She thinks you saved her.”

Guiding smiled.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “I helped.”

> **Neri:**  
> “That's not the same thing.”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “No.”

---

# 12. NERI'S QUESTION

There was another long silence.

Then Neri asked:

> **Neri:**  
> “Did you bring her here?”

Guiding looked at her.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “I opened the way.”

> **Neri:**  
> “So yes.”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Yes.”

Neri stared at the floor.

Her fingers tightened slightly around the sleeve of her coat.

> **Neri:**  
> “Thank you.”

Guiding's expression softened.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “You don't have to thank me.”

Neri looked up.

> **Neri:**  
> “I know.”

Pause.

> **Neri:**  
> “I still want to.”

Guiding did not argue.

---

# 13. THE SISTER

Neri eventually asked:

> **Neri:**  
> “Why did she remember me?”

Guiding answered immediately.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Because you were her sister.”

Neri looked away.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “Some things survive.”

Neri was quiet.

Then:

> **Neri:**  
> “She calls me Big Sis.”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “I know.”

Neri looked at her.

> **Neri:**  
> “You watched?”

Guiding smiled.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “A little.”

Neri sighed.

> **Neri:**  
> “That's creepy.”

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “I'm an Architect.”

> **Neri:**  
> “That's not an excuse.”

Guiding laughed.

---

# 14. THE FINAL QUESTION

Before leaving, Neri asked one final question.

> **Neri:**  
> “Will she be okay?”

Guiding did not immediately answer.

She looked toward the distant SAFEHOUSE.

Then back at Neri.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “She has you.”

Neri's expression softened.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “She has Bao.”

Pause.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “She has Alice.”

Another pause.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “She has an entire place full of people who already decided she belongs here.”

Neri lowered her head.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> “I think she'll be okay.”

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:**  
> “Okay.”

---

# 15. RETURN

Neri returned to her room shortly before dawn.

Nora was still asleep.

Neri quietly climbed back into bed.

Nora shifted.

Half-asleep, she reached toward Neri.

Neri let her.

Nora murmured:

> **Nora:**  
> “Big sis...?”

> **Neri:**  
> “I'm here.”

Nora relaxed.

Neri looked toward the artificial stars.

For several seconds, she watched the blue light reflecting across the floating islands.

Then she closed her eyes.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Nora's “blue moon” was almost certainly **Guiding Light**.

The rift appears to have been intentionally created to facilitate Nora's arrival at SAFEHOUSE.

The precise mechanism remains unknown.

More importantly, Guiding Light appears to have been aware of Nora's existence before her arrival.

Whether Guiding intentionally preserved Nora, redirected her, or merely encountered her during an anomalous transition remains unclear.

Guiding Light has declined to provide a complete explanation.

C.O.R.E. has elected not to press the matter.

This is partly because attempting to interrogate an Architect of Reality has historically produced very little useful data.

It is also because, for once, the outcome is already clear.

Nora is here.

Neri is no longer alone.

And the “blue moon” that Nora remembered was not a random phenomenon.

It was someone who knew exactly where she needed to go.
