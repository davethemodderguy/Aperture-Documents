# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – MEDICAL OBSERVATION LOG**

### **SUBJECT: DIALYN – NOCTURNAL LEVITATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Medical Division has documented an unusual phenomenon occurring exclusively while Dialyn is asleep.

During deeper stages of sleep, Dialyn gradually loses contact with the ground and begins to levitate.

The event is completely involuntary.

Dialyn herself was initially unaware it occurred.

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Approximately twenty to thirty minutes after falling asleep, Dialyn slowly rises between **10 and 40 centimeters** above the surface beneath her.

The ascent is extremely gentle.

No measurable acceleration has been detected.

Once airborne, she may slowly drift through the room, seemingly influenced by minor air currents.

Documented drift speeds rarely exceed normal walking pace.

There is no indication that the phenomenon is intentional.

---

# **SAFETY CONSIDERATIONS**

Although harmless under ordinary circumstances, nocturnal levitation has resulted in several minor incidents.

Recorded examples include:

- Waking up on the opposite side of the room.
    
- Drifting into curtains.
    
- Becoming gently wedged against bookshelves.
    
- Floating into ceiling corners while curled into a blanket.
    
- Being found asleep with her white cat comfortably resting on her stomach several centimeters above the bed.
    

No injuries have been reported.

---

# **AWAKENING**

The levitation ceases almost immediately upon waking.

Regardless of height, Dialyn gently returns to the nearest stable surface over the course of several seconds.

The descent is smooth and controlled.

Medical staff have never observed a sudden fall.

---

# **WORKPLACE ADAPTATIONS**

Because Dialyn occasionally naps during extended overnight shifts, several precautions have been introduced.

These include:

- Beds positioned away from ceiling fixtures.
    
- Soft wall padding near designated sleeping areas.
    
- Light safety rails in guest rooms.
    
- Ceiling fans automatically disabled when Dialyn is sleeping nearby.
    

SAFEHOUSE residents have also developed the habit of quietly checking whether she has drifted somewhere unexpected before waking her.

# **DIALYN'S RESPONSE**

After being shown security footage of herself slowly drifting across a room while asleep, Dialyn watched the recording in complete silence.

She blinked.

Twice.

Then quietly asked:

> "...Do I really do that?"

After another clip showed her gently bumping into a wall before rotating and continuing to drift in another direction, she covered her face.

> "...Please don't let anyone else see that."

The request was politely ignored.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Nocturnal levitation has been classified as a benign Ether-related physiological phenomenon.

It presents no known medical risk and requires no treatment.

SAFEHOUSE residents have simply accepted it as another part of living with Dialyn.

It has become common for someone making a late-night cup of tea to glance around the room before looking up.

Because if Dialyn isn't in bed...

There's a reasonable chance she's somewhere near the ceiling, peacefully asleep, slowly drifting through the room.
