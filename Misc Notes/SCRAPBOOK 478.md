### **SAFEHOUSE / BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — THE WORLD OUTSIDE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Sensitive  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Status:** **WITHDRAWAL OBSERVED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has stopped going outside.

The forest surrounding the treehouse was once one of the few places where she could simply exist without feeling watched.

Now she rarely crosses the front door.

The reason is simple.

She can no longer see the world the way she remembers it.

---

# **THE FOREST**

Before her reanimation, Corin remembered the forest as something alive with color.

Green leaves.

Blue skies.

Golden sunlight.

Bright flowers.

Now every tree is rendered in shades of gray.

The sky has no blue.

The sunlight has no warmth of color.

The flowers have no distinct hues.

Everything is technically visible.

But to Corin, the visual beauty she remembers is gone.

One morning, Orphie asked:

> **"Want to go outside?"**

Corin shook her head.

> **"Why?"**

> **Orphie:**  
> "It's nice out."

Corin looked toward the window.

> **"Nice how?"**

Orphie paused.

> **"The sun's out."**

> **"So?"**

---

# **CORIN'S REASONING**

Corin eventually explained it.

> **"I already know what it looks like."**

> **"Gray trees."**

> **"Gray sky."**

> **"Gray flowers."**

She looked down.

> **"Everyone keeps telling me how pretty everything is."**

> **"I can't see it."**

A pause.

> **"So why bother going outside?"**

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

Corin increasingly spends entire days inside.

She sits near windows.

Reads.

Draws.

Listens to the forest.

Talks with Orphie when she feels like talking.

Sometimes she simply sits in silence.

She does not hate the forest.

She hates what happened to her ability to experience it.

---

# **ORPHIE'S ATTEMPTS**

Orphie occasionally tries to describe things.

> **"The leaves are really bright today."**

> **"There's a huge sunflower outside."**

> **"The sunset looks beautiful."**

Corin listens.

Sometimes she smiles.

Sometimes she doesn't.

One evening, Orphie asked:

> **"Do you want me to describe it?"**

Corin shook her head.

> **"...No."**

> **"Why?"**

> **"Because I remember."**

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light understands why Corin refuses to go outside.

She could show Corin the world through an alternative perception.

She could temporarily restore the sensation of color.

She could even allow Corin to experience colors that her damaged eyes can no longer perceive.

But she does not force it.

Corin's senses are part of her life now.

Guiding Light refuses to turn another aspect of Corin's existence into something controlled by someone else.

Instead, she tells her:

> **"You don't have to enjoy the world the way everyone else does."**

Corin looks at her.

> **"Then what am I supposed to enjoy?"**

Guiding Light smiles faintly.

> **"Whatever you find."**

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT**

Curious Light offered a different perspective.

He reminded Corin that vision is only one method of experiencing an environment.

The forest has:

- Wind.
    
- Temperature.
    
- Sound.
    
- Texture.
    
- Smell.
    
- Movement.
    

Corin listened.

Then replied:

> **"...That sounds like a lot of work."**

Curious Light paused.

> **"...Fair."**

---

# **A SMALL CHANGE**

Eventually, Orphie stopped asking Corin to go outside.

Instead, she began bringing small pieces of the outside world inside.

A leaf.

A flower.

A pinecone.

A smooth stone.

Corin could touch them.

Smell them.

Study their shapes.

She could not see their colors.

But she could still experience them.

One day, Orphie brought in a sunflower.

Corin touched the petals.

> **"Yellow?"**

> **"Yeah."**

Corin smiled faintly.

> **"...I don't remember what yellow looks like anymore."**

Orphie sat beside her.

> **"That's okay."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin stopped going outside because the world had lost one of the things that once made it beautiful to her.

She does not see the forest as ugly.

She sees it as incomplete.

The world still exists beyond the treehouse.

The sunlight still reaches the trees.

Flowers still bloom.

The sky still changes.

But Corin cannot see those changes the way she once could.

For now, the door remains closed.

Perhaps someday she will open it again.

Not because someone convinces her that the world is beautiful.

But because she discovers that there are still things worth experiencing...

**even in grayscale.**

**End of Behavioral Observation**
