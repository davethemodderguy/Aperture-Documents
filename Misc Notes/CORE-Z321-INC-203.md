## **MEDICAL EXAMINATION**

Following the incident, Belle voluntarily agreed to undergo a comprehensive examination.

Results shocked every physician present.

---

### **ETHER SATURATION**

Estimated Ether presence throughout Belle's body:

**Approximately 95%.**

The concentration exceeded every previously documented survivable case.

Ordinarily, exposure of this magnitude would result in complete Ether corruption.

Belle showed no signs of becoming an Ether Walker.

---

### **PHYSIOLOGICAL FINDINGS**

Advanced imaging revealed that a substantial portion of Belle's original biological organs had gradually been replaced or supplemented by stable Ether constructs.

Affected systems included portions of:

- Circulatory system.
    
- Respiratory system.
    
- Internal organ structure.
    
- Cellular regeneration pathways.
    

Despite these findings, Belle remained clinically stable.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL EFFECTS**

Although physically stable, physicians identified several long-term side effects.

Documented symptoms include:

- Increasing difficulty sleeping.
    
- Gradual reduction in the need for sleep.
    
- Occasional intrusive thoughts.
    
- Emotional fluctuations during periods of intense stress.
    
- Temporary changes in eye appearance corresponding with heightened Ether activity.
    

Researchers concluded that the residual Ether continually influences Belle's neurological activity.

---

## **Mutual Dependence**

The medical board reached an unusual conclusion.

Belle is **not merely surviving despite the Ether.**

The Ether is also sustaining Belle.

Removal of the Ether would likely result in catastrophic systemic failure due to the extent to which it has become integrated into her body.

Likewise, Belle's own biological systems appear to stabilize the Ether, preventing corruption from progressing into the destructive patterns seen in Ether Walkers.

One physician summarized the relationship as:

> **"Neither is winning."**

> **"Neither is losing."**

> **"They exist because the other does."

---
