### **BIOLOGICAL STATUS / RESTORATION CONFIRMATION**

# **CORIN — RETURN TO LIFE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **ALIVE**  
**Previous Status:** Anomalous / Reanimated  
**Current Status:** **Biologically Living**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has officially been confirmed as **alive**.

The gradual restoration of her biological systems has reached a critical point.

Her heart beats independently.

Her blood circulates naturally.

Her lungs require and process oxygen.

Her organs are functioning.

Her tissues heal biologically.

Her limbs remain naturally attached.

Her body no longer requires external stitching to maintain structural integrity.

For the first time since her death and reconstruction, Corin meets the fundamental physiological criteria for a living organism.

---

# **THE TRANSITION**

Corin's return to life was not instantaneous.

It occurred gradually.

First came the heartbeat.

Then blood circulation.

Then oxygen dependency.

Then increasingly normal organ function.

Then natural closure of the wounds that once separated her limbs.

Eventually, C.O.R.E. could no longer classify her as merely a reanimated entity.

The appropriate classification became:

> **Living Human — Anomalously Modified**

---

# **APPEARANCE**

Despite the biological restoration, Corin's appearance has not returned to its original state.

Her skin remains its characteristic **chalky lime coloration**.

Her hair remains the same **chalky lime color**.

Neither has reverted to a conventional human coloration.

These traits appear to have become permanent characteristics of her restored body.

Her biological revival therefore did not erase the changes that occurred during her anomalous existence.

---

# **HER BODY IS STILL CORIN'S BODY**

The restoration did not transform Corin into an ordinary human.

She still possesses:

- Ether manipulation.
    
- Ether radiation immunity.
    
- Ether-integrated wings.
    
- Feather-based airburst capabilities.
    
- Extreme regeneration.
    
- Anomalous resilience.
    
- Her unusual skin coloration.
    
- Her unusual hair coloration.
    

The difference is that these characteristics now exist alongside genuine biological life.

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

Corin was informed of her new classification during a medical examination.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Corin, you're alive."

Corin became quiet.

She placed a hand against her chest.

**Thump.**

**Thump.**

She looked at her hands.

Then touched her face.

Her skin was still chalky lime.

Her hair was still chalky lime.

Nothing about her appearance had suddenly become normal.

But her heart was beating.

She took a breath.

> **"...I'm alive."**

Guiding Light smiled.

> **"Yes."**

Corin looked down for a moment.

Then smiled.

> **"I actually did it."**

---

# **ORPHIE**

> **"Yeah."**

# **LUMI**
