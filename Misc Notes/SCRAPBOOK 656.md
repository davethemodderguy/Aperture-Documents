# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / TREEHOUSE OBSERVATION LOG**

# **"I'M FINE. I'M ALWAYS FINE."**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Observer:** Orphie  
**Location:** Treehouse Exterior  
**Status:** **ONGOING**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin can no longer fly.

The loss of flight has been confirmed as permanent under current conditions.

She does not appear to care.

No rehabilitation has been requested.

No attempt to regain flight has been made.

Her wings have become something else entirely.

They no longer carry her.

They simply cover her.

---

# **THE EVENING**

Orphie sat beside Corin beneath the tree.

Snow continued falling.

Neither spoke for a long time.

Corin remained curled beneath her wings.

Her body trembled continuously.

Orphie remained beside her despite the cold.

For several hours.

Eventually, the temperature became unbearable.

Orphie pulled her coat tighter around herself.

She looked toward Corin.

Corin had not moved.

---

# **THE CONVERSATION**

Orphie finally whispered:

> **"I'm sorry, Corin. I can't take this anymore. It's too cold."**

Corin answered quietly:

> **"I know."**

Orphie hesitated.

Then asked:

> **"Are you cold?"**

Corin's answer came immediately.

> **"I'm fine."**

A pause.

Then:

> **"I'm always fine."**

Orphie stared at her.

She knew that wasn't true.

---

# **THE CHEST**

Orphie reached toward Corin's chest.

Not to check her heartbeat.

There was no heartbeat to find.

She simply wanted to touch her.

To feel something.

Warmth.

Movement.

Life.

Instead, Orphie felt an unbearable emptiness.

Her hand remained there for several seconds.

Nothing.

---

# **THE REALIZATION**

Orphie withdrew her hand.

She suddenly understood why Corin's words sounded so empty.

> **"I'm fine."**

It wasn't reassurance.

It wasn't optimism.

It wasn't even denial anymore.

It had become a reflex.

A sentence Corin could say without having to think about how she actually felt.

---

# **ORPHIE LEAVES**

Orphie stood.

She looked back at Corin.

She wanted to say something.

Anything.

But nothing came.

She eventually turned toward the Treehouse.

Her footsteps disappeared into the snow.

She walked inside.

Bitterly.

Not angry at Corin.

Not angry at herself.

Just exhausted.

---

# **CORIN REMAINS**

Corin did not follow.

She remained beneath the tree.

Her wings stayed wrapped around her.

Snow accumulated across them.

Her body continued trembling.

She watched Orphie disappear through the doorway.

Then looked forward again.

Waiting.

---

# **THE EMPTY CHEST**

Orphie's hand had found no heartbeat.

No warmth.

No familiar rhythm.

Only emptiness.

The same emptiness Corin had described psychologically long before her hearts stopped.

It is unclear whether the physical absence of cardiac activity contributes to Corin's emotional state or merely reflects it.

C.O.R.E. has been unable to determine which came first.

---

# **THE WINGS**

The wings remain the only part of Corin that appears completely steady.

They do not tremble.

They do not glow.

They do not ignite.

They do not fly.

They simply cover her.

Corin has stopped expecting them to do anything else.

---

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie could only endure the cold for a few hours.

Corin has endured it for a year.

Orphie eventually stood up.

Corin didn't.

Orphie went inside.

Corin stayed outside.

The difference is not that Corin is stronger.

It is that she stopped caring whether the cold hurts.

When Orphie asked:

> **"Are you cold?"**

Corin answered:

> **"I'm fine."**

Then:

> **"I'm always fine."**

Orphie knew it was a lie.

Corin probably knew it too.

But she remained beneath the tree anyway.

Wings around herself.

Trembling.

Waiting.

**End of Treehouse Observation Log**
