## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL PROFILE ADDENDUM**

# **COREY — "SOLARIA" DESIGNATION**

**Classification:** Level 4 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corey

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following cross-dimensional interviews and archival review, C.O.R.E. has revised its understanding of the designation **"Solaria."**

The name is **not** a nickname.

It refers to an **alternate persona** intrinsically connected to Corey.

The exact mechanism behind this phenomenon remains unknown.

---

# **SOLARIA**

Available evidence indicates that **Solaria** is an alternate identity associated with Corey.

Characteristics currently confirmed include:

- Sun-affinity mage.
    
- Associated with solar and light-based magical abilities.
    
- Recognized by individuals from Corey's native universe.
    
- Shares the same physical body as Corey.
    

Despite this connection, Corey possesses little to no understanding of Solaria herself.

When asked why she is called Solaria, her answer remains truthful.

> "...I don't know."

From her perspective...

She genuinely doesn't.

---

# **ALTER EGO**

Researchers currently classify Solaria as an **alter ego**, rather than an independent entity.

There is no evidence suggesting two separate consciousnesses exist simultaneously.

Instead, Solaria appears to represent another aspect of Corey that is currently inaccessible.

How this alter ego is reached—or whether it can be reached voluntarily at all—remains unknown.

No documented activation event exists.

---

# **KNOWN FACTS**

Current verified information includes:

- Corey is a capable sun mage.
    
- The title "Solaria" originates from her native universe.
    
- Corey is comfortable being addressed by either name.
    
- The alter ego has never manifested within Aperture facilities under observation.
    
- No known trigger has been identified.
    

Everything beyond these points remains speculative.

---

# **MAGICAL AFFINITY**

Corey's magical specialization has been confirmed as solar magic.

Observed applications include:

- Generation of radiant energy.
    
- Heat manipulation.
    
- Solar-based spellcasting.
    

Researchers note that Corey rarely demonstrates these abilities unless necessary.

She has never referred to them as unusual.

---

# **INTERVIEW LOG**

**Researcher**

> "Who is Solaria?"

**Corey**

> "...Me."

**Researcher**

> "Can you explain?"

Corey remained silent for several moments.

> "...Not really."

> "...I know the name."

> "...I know the magic."

> "...But everything else..."

She gently shook her head.

> "...It's like trying to remember a dream."

---

# **STAR'S COMMENT**

After reading the report, Star quietly closed the folder.

> "...So..."

> "...She's both Corey and Solaria?"

The lead researcher nodded.

Star smiled.

> "...Either way..."

> "...She's still Corey to me."

---

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

Lumi expressed little concern regarding the revelation.

> "...Names change."

She watched Corey teaching several Marts new vocabulary.

> "...People grow."

> "...Whatever Solaria is..."

> "...She's still my friend."

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

The Ambassador was informed that "Solaria" represented another side of Corey.

It considered this carefully.

Then answered:

> "...Corey."

The researcher clarified:

> "Even if Solaria appears?"

The Ambassador nodded.

> "...Corey."

Nearby Marts unanimously agreed.

---

# **RESEARCH NOTE**

No attempt will be made to forcibly induce or access the Solaria alter ego.

Without a known activation method or understanding of the underlying mechanism, any such experiment has been deemed both scientifically unjustified and ethically inappropriate.

Should the phenomenon naturally manifest in the future, it will be documented under standard observational procedures.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

As the interview concluded, Corey returned to her workbench in Engineering.

One Mart drifted onto her shoulder.

Another rested atop her beret.

The researcher looked down at the report one last time.

At the top of the page, two names appeared.

> **Corey**

Below it:

> **Solaria**

The researcher paused before closing the file.

Whatever the mystery behind the second name...

For now, the person quietly repairing laboratory equipment while tiny blue blobs followed her through the Blacksite was simply...

Corey.
