### **SAFEHOUSE MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — FEATHER SACK RESPONSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Location:** Treehouse  
**Condition:** Severe Emotional Distress / Wing Regeneration

---

# **OBSERVATION**

The sight of Corin's collected feathers has affected her more deeply than expected.

After seeing the large sack containing the feathers shed from her injured wing, Corin became noticeably quieter.

She no longer looks at it.

Even passing near it appears to upset her.

The feathers are a reminder of what happened.

---

# **THE LEFT WING**

Corin's left wing now hangs loosely from her back.

It no longer holds its usual posture.

There is no strength behind its movement.

When Corin sits, the wing often slides forward and comes to rest across her lap.

She occasionally adjusts it.

Otherwise, she leaves it alone.

Her right wing remains folded normally against her back.

The difference between them is impossible to miss.

---

# **MOVEMENT**

Corin barely moves around.

She spends most of her time sitting or lying down.

Walking has become something she does only when necessary.

She does not fly.

She does not practice.

She does not explore.

Even activities she normally enjoys hold little interest.

Her energy is being consumed by both physical recovery and emotional exhaustion.

---

# **SELF-SOOTHING**

When the pain becomes noticeable, Corin places her hand against the injured wing.

Sometimes she gently pets it.

Sometimes she simply rests her hand there.

She no longer attempts to move the wing unnecessarily.

The gesture appears less like checking the injury and more like reassurance.

---

# **EMOTIONAL RESPONSE**

Corin's sadness has become quieter.

There are fewer obvious episodes of crying.

This has not been interpreted as improvement.

Instead, she appears emotionally exhausted.

She sits with her eyes lowered.

Her ears remain drooped.

Her two tails rarely move.

Her luminous eyes are often dimmed by tears.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle has stopped bringing attention to the feather sack.

She understands what it represents to Corin.

Instead, she simply sits beside her.

If Corin rests the injured wing across her lap, Belle does not move it.

If Corin wants to be held, Belle holds her.

If Corin wants silence, Belle gives her silence.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie remains close.

She has tried conversation.

She has tried reassurance.

She has tried simply sitting beside Corin.

Nothing has made the sadness disappear.

So she stays.

Sometimes that is all she can do.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

The physical regeneration remains active.

The left wing is still expected to recover.

There is no evidence that the injury is permanent.

However, Corin's current lack of movement is concerning enough to warrant continued monitoring.

Personnel should encourage—but never force—gentle movement, hydration, nutrition, and rest.

The objective is to support recovery without making Corin feel pressured.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin does not want to look at the feathers.

They remind her of the explosion.

They remind her of the injury.

They remind her that the wing that once carried her through the sky is currently unable to move properly.

So she barely moves herself.

She sits quietly.

Her right wing remains folded against her back.

Her left wing hangs loosely behind her or rests across her lap.

Sometimes she reaches down and gently touches it.

Then she simply waits.

The feathers will grow back.

The wing will heal.

But until then, Corin remains very still.
