# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neri's initial SAFEHOUSE assessment was substantially incorrect.

She was initially classified as a harmless rescued Seal Thiren who enjoyed water, fish, sweets, and spending time with other residents.

This remains true.

It is simply not the complete picture.

Following several weeks of observation, C.O.R.E. has determined that Neri possesses an **extreme fixation on knives and sharp weapons**.

This fixation is not limited to practical self-defense.

Neri appears to genuinely **like** sharp weapons.

She is fascinated by them.

She enjoys examining them.

She appears unusually comfortable handling them.

She understands their properties with an abnormal degree of intuition.

And when combined with her intense attachment to people she considers friends, this produces a potentially dangerous behavioral pattern.

Neri is not merely protective.

She is potentially a **yandere with anomalous characteristics**.

---

# 2. BEHAVIORAL PROFILE

Neri forms attachments rapidly.

Once an individual is categorized as a friend, Neri's emotional investment can become unusually strong.

Her current known attachments include:

- **Bao — "Mom"**
    
- **Frost — "Big sis"**
    
- **Alice — "Bunny"**
    
- **Neko — Friend**
    
- Numerous other SAFEHOUSE residents
    

Neri is generally affectionate toward these individuals.

She is also highly protective of them.

The problem arises when Neri perceives someone as a threat.

Her internal distinction appears to be remarkably simple:

> **Friend = protect.**

> **Threat = eliminate.**

The severity of the perceived threat does not necessarily correspond to the severity of her response.

A verbal insult may be interpreted very differently from an actual attack, but Neri's willingness to escalate appears unusually high.

---

# 3. KNIFE FIXATION

Neri demonstrates a persistent fascination with edged weapons.

This includes:

- knives;
    
- blades;
    
- cutting implements;
    
- sharpened objects;
    
- unusual weapons;
    
- anomalous cutting instruments;
    
- weapon construction;
    
- blade geometry;
    
- edge characteristics.
    

Neri is particularly interested in **sharpness**.

When presented with an unfamiliar knife, she is likely to immediately notice its construction and cutting characteristics.

This is not normal curiosity.

It is closer to enthusiasm.

A researcher once showed Neri an experimental blade.

Neri examined it for approximately ten seconds.

Her response:

> "Sharp."

The researcher asked how she knew.

Neri replied:

> "I can tell."

No explanation followed.

---

# 4. THE HAIRCLIPS

The most obvious manifestation of this behavior is Neri's hair.

Neri wears three hairclips.

### CLIP A — FISH BONE

A decorative fish-bone hairclip.

Harmless.

Neri likes it because she likes fish.

### CLIP B — JAGGERED BLADE

Approximately the length of an index finger.

Extremely sharp.

Jagged edge.

Normally crossed through Neri's bangs.

### CLIP C — JAGGERED BLADE

Approximately the length of an index finger.

Extremely sharp.

Jagged edge.

Crossed with Clip B.

The latter two are not ornamental.

They are weapons.

Their compact size makes them extremely difficult to notice until Neri has already removed one.

---

# 5. INCIDENT SH-██-NERI-01

The discovery of Neri's willingness to use the hairclips occurred during an altercation involving a facility bully.

Neri had been walking alongside an Archivist.

The bully shoved Neri.

The individual subsequently continued harassing her and the accompanying Archivist.

Neri approached.

She removed one of her hairclips.

The weapon was deployed.

The bully sustained severe injuries before Neri disengaged.

Neri subsequently laughed.

Witnesses described the laughter as:

> "Maniacal."

The injured individual was later revived through intervention by Lucy Merryweather.

The physical injuries were survivable.

The psychological consequences were not negligible.

The individual developed an intense fear of seals.

---

# 6. WHY THIS INCIDENT MATTERS

The incident was initially treated as an isolated defensive reaction.

It is no longer considered one.

Review of Neri's behavior before and after the incident indicates that the attack was consistent with a broader pattern.

Neri does not merely possess weapons.

She **likes weapons**.

She keeps them with her constantly.

She incorporates them into her appearance.

She is comfortable with them.

And she appears to derive some degree of emotional satisfaction from the idea of using them against threats.

This does not mean Neri constantly wants to hurt people.

Quite the opposite.

When Neri is comfortable, she is remarkably pleasant.

The danger is activated by her attachment system.

---

# 7. POSSIBLE ANOMALOUS COMPONENT

C.O.R.E. has been unable to conclusively determine whether Neri's fixation is psychological, anomalous, or both.

Several observations remain unexplained.

### Observation 01

Neri demonstrates an unusually strong intuitive understanding of sharp weapons despite no documented formal weapons training sufficient to explain it.

### Observation 02

Neri's attachment to friends can develop unusually quickly.

### Observation 03

Her perception of threats appears heavily influenced by emotional attachment.

### Observation 04

Her confidence with edged weapons appears disproportionate to their size.

### Observation 05

Neri's emotional state during violent incidents is inconsistent with ordinary defensive panic.

She appears **happy**.

This is the most concerning observation.

---

# 8. YANDERE CLASSIFICATION

C.O.R.E. Behavioral Division currently uses the following provisional classification:

> **YANDERE — PROTECTIVE / POSSESSIVE VARIANT**

Neri's behavior is characterized by:

- rapid emotional attachment;
    
- intense loyalty;
    
- extreme protectiveness;
    
- strong emotional investment in friends;
    
- fascination with weapons;
    
- willingness to seriously harm perceived threats;
    
- limited hesitation during protective violence;
    
- apparent enjoyment of the weapon itself.
    

There is currently insufficient evidence to classify Neri as deliberately controlling or possessive toward her friends.

She does not appear to demand exclusivity.

She simply wants them safe.

Very safe.

**Extremely safe.**

Preferably with nobody she dislikes within stabbing distance.

---

# 9. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Neri is **not considered inherently hostile**.

Personnel should not treat her as though she is constantly looking for an excuse to attack.

She is capable of ordinary friendship.

She jokes.

She eats sweets.

She swims.

She talks about fish.

She studies chemistry with Neko.

She rides/walks around with Alice.

She spends time with Bao and Frost.

She gets along with almost everyone.

Her dangerous behavior is conditional.

However, **the condition can be triggered by perceived harm to someone she cares about.**

Personnel must therefore avoid deliberately testing the boundary.

---

# 10. PERSONNEL DIRECTIVE

### DO:

- Treat Neri respectfully.
    
- Treat her friends respectfully.
    
- Allow her reasonable access to aquatic facilities.
    
- Engage with her interests.
    
- Provide sweets when appropriate.
    
- Discuss chemistry with her if qualified.
    
- Allow her to maintain her normal social relationships.
    
- Inform Security if Neri appears unusually agitated.
    

### DO NOT:

- Bully Neri.
    
- Bully her friends.
    
- Threaten her friends.
    
- Deliberately injure her friends.
    
- Threaten to hurt her friends as a joke.
    
- Attempt to take her knives.
    
- Ask her to demonstrate them.
    
- Challenge her fascination with blades.
    
- Assume her friendly demeanor means she is harmless.
    

---

# 11. INTERACTION RECOMMENDATION

Personnel are strongly encouraged to establish positive relationships with Neri.

This is not merely for her emotional wellbeing.

It is also practical.

Neri responds exceptionally well to kindness.

Food works.

Sweets work better.

Fish works extremely well.

Friendship works best.

Once Neri likes someone, she is substantially less likely to perceive them as a threat.

C.O.R.E. therefore considers **positive social integration to be a legitimate risk-management strategy.**

---

# 12. NERI'S CURRENT LIFE

Despite the reassessment, Neri remains one of the most socially integrated residents in the SAFEHOUSE.

She has befriended almost everyone.

She spends significant amounts of time in water.

She has a severe sweet tooth.

She loves fish.

She wears blue, azure, and white.

She calls Bao "Mom."

She calls Frost "Big sis."

She calls Alice "Bunny."

She became friends with Neko almost immediately after discovering his chemistry work.

And she still walks around the SAFEHOUSE with Alice while Alice rides her scooter.

From an ordinary residential perspective, Neri appears happy.

That is precisely why the Behavioral Division recommends **not disturbing that stability unnecessarily.**

---

# 13. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Neri is a paradox.

She is:

**Kind.**

**Affectionate.**

**Social.**

**Intelligent.**

**Playful.**

**Fond of sweets.**

**Fond of fish.**

**Exceptionally talented at chemistry.**

And potentially:

**Very, very dangerous.**

The two tiny blades hidden in her hair are not the entirety of the threat.

They are merely the most visible evidence of it.

C.O.R.E. currently has no confirmed explanation for Neri's unusual affinity toward sharp weapons, nor whether the intensity of her protective behavior has an anomalous origin.

Until this is determined, the following assessment remains active:

> **Neri should be treated as a valued SAFEHOUSE resident.**
> 
> **Neri should also be treated as potentially anomalous.**
> 
> **Do not give her a reason to consider you a threat.**
