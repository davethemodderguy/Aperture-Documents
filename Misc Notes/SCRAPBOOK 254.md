# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – MEDICAL & PERSONNEL ADVISORY**

### **SUBJECT: DIALYN – DIETARY HABITS & HORTICULTURAL PRECAUTIONS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical & Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The following advisory has been issued after several well-intentioned but recurring incidents involving Dialyn.

While largely lighthearted in day-to-day conversation, the underlying reasons are rooted in documented survival trauma.

Personnel are asked to be mindful and avoid turning the matter into a joke around Dialyn.

---

# **COOKING**

Dialyn is strongly discouraged from preparing meals for large groups.

This recommendation is **not** based on safety concerns.

She simply has very little confidence in her own cooking.

When asked why she almost never cooks, Dialyn usually laughs and replies:

> "...I'd rather leave it to people who know what they're doing."

As a result, SAFEHOUSE residents generally insist that:

- Belle bakes.
    
- Bao cooks.
    
- Dialyn sets the table.
    

Everyone agrees this arrangement works exceptionally well.

---

# **FLOWER GARDENS**

Personnel are requested not to assign Dialyn to maintain decorative flower gardens without first consulting Psychological Services.

During periods of absent-mindedness, extreme fatigue, or emotional distress, Dialyn has occasionally reached toward flowers as though they were food before catching herself.

She has never intentionally damaged Blacksite gardens.

The behavior is understood to be an unconscious survival response.

---

# **MEDICAL EXPLANATION**

During her childhood, Dialyn survived for an extended period while trapped inside a Hollow.

With no reliable source of food available, she resorted to consuming whatever edible plant life she could find.

The experience occurred during a critical stage of her development.

Psychological Services believe those survival behaviors were deeply ingrained.

Although years have passed, fragments of those instincts occasionally resurface under stress.

One psychologist summarized the phenomenon as follows:

> "Her mind knows she is safe."

> "Sometimes her instincts haven't caught up yet."

---

# **OBSERVED INCIDENTS**

**Incident 01**

Dialyn was seen absent-mindedly examining a flower bed outside the KCA headquarters.

Before she could pick a flower, Corin gently called her name.

Dialyn blinked, looked down at her hand, and quietly apologized.

No flowers were damaged.

---

**Incident 02**

While visiting the SAFEHOUSE greenhouse, Bao noticed Dialyn staring at a row of edible blossoms for an unusually long time.

Rather than comment on it, Bao simply handed her a warm bun.

Dialyn accepted it with a sheepish smile.

> "...Thanks."

Nothing more was said.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Dialyn no longer needs to survive on wild plants.

She is surrounded by friends, warm meals, and people who quietly make sure she never goes hungry.

Even so, there are moments when the past briefly reaches into the present.

When that happens, nobody laughs.

Someone simply hands her something to eat.

The flowers remain in the garden.

And Dialyn is gently reminded that she no longer has to survive the way she once did.
