## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **Z-335 FACILITY SAFETY MEMORANDUM**

# **MART DRIFT PREVENTION SYSTEM**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel  
**Applies To:** Heaven Blacksite / Krampus Compliance Authority

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Due to the complete lack of gravitational influence on Z-335 entities, Marts are capable of unintentionally drifting through open windows, balconies, vents, and other elevated openings.

This behavior is not considered dangerous from the Mart's perspective.

It is, however, inconvenient for personnel.

A Mart drifting several floors away from its assigned facility is difficult to retrieve.

Consequently, long-form **insect nets** have been installed across frequently opened windows.

The nets were provided courtesy of **Neko**.

---

# **WINDOW NET SYSTEM**

The installed nets are substantially longer than conventional insect screens.

They are designed to:

- Prevent Marts from drifting outside.
    
- Catch Marts without damaging them.
    
- Allow normal airflow.
    
- Remain unobtrusive.
    
- Prevent accidental falls from elevated areas.
    
- Allow personnel to retrieve trapped Marts safely.
    

The system has proven highly effective.

Marts generally become gently caught in the mesh and remain there until retrieved.

---

# **STANDARD RECOVERY PROCEDURE**

If a Mart becomes caught in a window net:

1. Approach calmly.
    
2. Do not pull the Mart forcefully.
    
3. Carefully remove the Mart from the mesh.
    
4. Check that its top hat remains attached.
    
5. Return the Mart indoors.
    
6. Provide water if desired.
    
7. Continue with normal activities.
    

Marts generally respond with:

> "Thank you!"

or:

> "Hello!"

---

# **INCIDENT FREQUENCY**

Window-drift incidents are relatively common.

Typical causes include:

- Open windows.
    
- Strong airflow.
    
- Marts attempting to follow someone.
    
- Marts simply floating toward an interesting object.
    
- Marts forgetting that the window is open.
    
- Marts deliberately testing whether they can drift outside.
    

The final category remains under investigation.

---

# **RECORDED INCIDENT**

A Mart was observed drifting through an open second-floor window.

It slowly traveled approximately three meters away from the building.

The Mart appeared completely unconcerned.

It eventually encountered the safety net.

The Mart gently bounced against it.

> "Oh."

It remained suspended in the mesh.

A nearby employee retrieved it.

The Mart immediately said:

> "Thank you."

It then floated directly back toward the same window.

The employee closed it.

---

# **PERSONNEL REMINDER**

Marts are not capable of reliably recognizing environmental hazards.

Their lack of gravitational awareness means they may casually drift toward:

- Windows.
    
- Balconies.
    
- Stairwells.
    
- Open exterior doors.
    
- Ventilation exhausts.
    

Personnel should therefore keep elevated openings secured when possible.

If a Mart is observed drifting outside:

> **Retrieve it.**

Do not assume it will return by itself.

It may not even realize it has left.

---

Because sometimes...

It thinks:

> **"Interesting."**

And floats away.

Thanks to Neko's nets, it usually gets caught before it gets very far.
