# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **FIREARMS ENGINEERING MEMORANDUM**

### **SUBJECT: GUNSLINGER'S LEVER-ACTION RIFLE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Technical Reference

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although outwardly conventional, Arcturus Lockwood's modernized lever-action rifle has become the subject of frequent discussion among KCA personnel.

The reason is not the rifle itself.

It is the operator.

---

# **CYCLIC RATE**

During controlled range evaluations, Arcturus demonstrated an exceptionally high rate of operation.

Through years of practice, he is capable of cycling the lever with extraordinary speed while maintaining control of the rifle.

Researchers noted that the practical firing rate he achieves is far beyond what most trained marksmen can accomplish with a manually operated firearm.

One instructor summarized the demonstration by writing:

> **"The limiting factor isn't the rifle."**

> **"It's whether you can even see his hand moving."**

Despite the speed, instructors consistently emphasize that Arcturus prioritizes accurate, deliberate fire over simply shooting as fast as possible.

---

# **SPECIALIZED AMMUNITION COMPATIBILITY**

Early prototypes of the rifle included the ability to chamber several specialized ammunition types for field testing.

Among them were experimental shell-based cartridges intended for close-range applications.

Following evaluation, Senior Manager Norma Lockwood permanently removed the concept from production.

Her reasoning, recorded in the project notes, reads:

> **"If he can hit heads from five miles away, he does not need shotgun shells."**

The proposal was officially marked:

> **DISCONTINUED**

Neko later attempted to revive the idea.

The proposal was denied before he finished speaking.

# **STATUS**

The Gunslinger's lever-action rifle remains intentionally simple in design.

Its reputation comes not from exotic mechanisms, but from the remarkable proficiency of the person carrying it.

As one KCA range officer jokingly remarked after another qualification session:

> **"Most people need an automatic rifle to put rounds on target."**

> **"Arcturus just needs a lever and good aim."**
