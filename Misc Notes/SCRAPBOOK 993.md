# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. — CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### SAFEHOUSE MEDICAL INCIDENT RECORD

**SUBJECT:** NERI  
**INCIDENT:** ANEMIC SHOCK / STAIRWELL COLLAPSE  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE STAIRWELL  
**PRIMARY WITNESSES:** JANE DOE, BAO, ALICE  
**MEDICAL STATUS:** RECOVERED / UNDER OBSERVATION  
**SEVERITY:** CRITICAL

---

# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

Neri experienced another episode of severe anemia.

Unlike previous episodes, she did not immediately lose consciousness.

Instead, the primary symptom was extreme cardiac pain.

The pain was described as severe enough that Neri later stated:

> **"It hurt really, really bad."**

Despite this, Neri told nobody.

She continued with her normal activities.

She walked.

She interacted with people.

She read.

She behaved normally.

She smiled when appropriate.

She answered questions normally.

She successfully concealed the severity of her condition from everyone around her.

This was not because the symptoms were mild.

It was because Neri had become exceptionally good at pretending that she was fine.

---

# 2. THE MASK

Neri's behavior throughout the episode was later reconstructed.

When asked whether she was okay:

> **NERI:** "I'm fine."

When asked whether she was tired:

> **NERI:** "A little."

When asked whether something hurt:

> **NERI:** "No."

These answers were delivered convincingly.

There was no obvious panic.

No crying.

No visible distress.

No request for assistance.

Neri simply continued walking.

The medical team subsequently determined that her outward behavior was not an accurate representation of her physiological state.

---

# 3. STAIRWELL INCIDENT

Neri was walking down a SAFEHOUSE stairwell.

She had already been experiencing severe cardiac pain.

She continued anyway.

Approximately halfway through the stairwell, her condition deteriorated.

Neri stopped.

She placed one hand against the railing.

She remained standing for several seconds.

Then her legs gave out.

Neri collapsed.

She lost consciousness.

---

# 4. DISCOVERY

The incident was noticed almost immediately by nearby personnel.

The situation was significantly different from Neri's previous "background character" incidents.

This time, people saw her fall.

The response was immediate.

Medical personnel were called.

Bao was notified.

Jane and Alice were also informed.

---

# 5. BAO'S RESPONSE

Bao arrived shortly afterward.

She immediately recognized the severity of the situation.

There was no hesitation.

No discussion about whether Neri should have said something.

No argument.

Just:

> **BAO:** "Neri."

Neri was unconscious.

Bao remained beside her while medical personnel stabilized her.

Her expression reportedly became increasingly distressed as the severity of the anemia became clear.

---

# 6. JANE'S RESPONSE

Jane arrived shortly afterward.

She immediately understood what had happened.

Her reaction was noticeably different from her usual playful behavior.

There was no teasing.

No jokes.

No attempt to lighten the situation.

She looked at Neri.

Then at the medical equipment.

Then back at Neri.

**JANE:**  
"She didn't tell anyone?"

Nobody answered.

Jane's expression hardened.

**JANE:**  
"Of course she didn't."

Jane remained beside the medical team.

She did not leave.

---

# 7. ALICE'S RESPONSE

Alice arrived shortly afterward.

She saw Neri unconscious.

Her cheerful demeanor disappeared immediately.

Alice approached the bed but stopped before touching Neri.

**ALICE:**  
"...Is she okay?"

Medical personnel informed her that Neri was being stabilized.

Alice remained nearby.

She did not leave.

She simply watched.

---

# 8. THE THREE

For several minutes, Bao, Jane, and Alice remained together near Neri.

None of them were particularly happy.

Not with Neri.

Not with each other.

Not with the situation.

They were scared.

And all three understood the same problem:

Neri had been in severe pain.

She knew something was wrong.

And she had decided that nobody needed to know.

---

# 9. NERI REGAINS CONSCIOUSNESS

Neri eventually regained consciousness.

Her eyes opened slowly.

The first things she saw were Bao, Jane, and Alice.

She immediately realized something was wrong.

**NERI:**  
"...Oh."

Jane crossed her arms.

**JANE:**  
"Don't."

Neri blinked.

**NERI:**  
"Don't what?"

**JANE:**  
"Don't say you're fine."

Neri went quiet.

Alice looked at her.

**ALICE:**  
"Why didn't you tell us?"

Neri looked down.

**NERI:**  
"I thought it would go away."

Bao answered quietly.

**BAO:**  
"It almost didn't."

Neri said nothing.

---

# 10. NERI'S EXPLANATION

After several minutes, Neri finally admitted:

> **NERI:** "My heart hurt."

Jane stared at her.

**JANE:**  
"How much?"

Neri hesitated.

**NERI:**  
"...A lot."

**JANE:**  
"How much is a lot?"

Neri looked away.

**NERI:**  
"Really, really bad."

Alice's expression changed.

Bao closed her eyes for a moment.

Jane took a slow breath.

**JANE:**  
"And you walked around the Blacksite like that?"

Neri nodded.

**JANE:**  
"Why?"

Neri's answer was quiet.

> **NERI:** "I didn't want to bother anyone."

---

# 11. THE RESPONSE

Jane crouched beside her.

Her voice was noticeably softer.

**JANE:**  
"Neri."

Neri looked at her.

**JANE:**  
"You don't bother me by telling me you're hurting."

Neri remained silent.

**JANE:**  
"You don't bother Bao."

Bao reached for Neri's hand.

**BAO:**  
"You don't bother me."

Alice spoke next.

**ALICE:**  
"You don't bother me either."

Neri looked between them.

**NERI:**  
"...But everyone was busy."

Jane shook her head.

**JANE:**  
"Then they can be busy later."

---

# 12. THE PART THAT SCARED THEM

Jane eventually explained why she was upset.

Not because Neri had collapsed.

Not because Neri had frightened everyone.

Because Neri had been suffering in silence.

**JANE:**  
"You were hurting that badly."

Neri nodded.

**JANE:**  
"And you decided nobody needed to know."

Neri looked down.

**JANE:**  
"That's what scares me."

Neri remained silent.

Jane continued:

**JANE:**  
"Not that you got sick."

**JANE:**  
"That you were sick enough to collapse on a staircase and still thought telling us would be worse than dealing with it yourself."

Neri's eyes began watering.

---

# 13. BAO

Bao pulled Neri into an embrace.

**BAO:**  
"You don't have to hide pain from me."

Neri quietly answered:

**NERI:**  
"I know."

Bao looked at her.

**BAO:**  
"Do you?"

Neri didn't answer.

Bao hugged her tighter.

**BAO:**  
"Then we'll keep reminding you."

---

# 14. ALICE

Alice sat beside the bed.

She was still visibly upset.

**ALICE:**  
"You scared me."

Neri looked at her.

**NERI:**  
"I'm sorry."

Alice shook her head.

**ALICE:**  
"I don't want you to say sorry."

Neri blinked.

**ALICE:**  
"I want you to tell me when you're hurting."

A pause.

**ALICE:**  
"Even if I'm busy."

Neri nodded.

---

# 15. JANE

Jane eventually sat down beside them.

Her expression softened.

**JANE:**  
"You remember what I told you?"

Neri nodded.

**NERI:**  
"I can bother you."

**JANE:**  
"Exactly."

**NERI:**  
"Even when you're working."

**JANE:**  
"Especially then."

Neri gave a tiny smile.

**NERI:**  
"That's annoying."

Jane smiled.

**JANE:**  
"Good."

---

# 16. MEDICAL CONCLUSION

Neri's underlying condition remains unchanged.

She continues to experience spontaneous blood degradation and anemia.

The incident demonstrates that unconsciousness is **not** the only warning presentation.

Neri may remain awake and outwardly functional while experiencing dangerous cardiovascular symptoms.

Her ability to mask these symptoms is now considered a significant medical risk.

Future monitoring will therefore prioritize behavioral changes and subtle indications rather than relying exclusively on verbal complaints.

Neri remains medically dependent upon Bao's compatible blood during severe episodes.

This dependency has not changed.

---

# 17. SAFEHOUSE NOTE

The three people who were "not very happy" with Neri afterward did not leave her alone.

Bao stayed because she was her mother.

Alice stayed because she was her friend.

Jane stayed because she had already told Neri:

> **You are allowed to bother me.**

Neri had not yet fully learned to believe it.

But she had heard it.

And this time, when she woke up, there were three people waiting for her.

---

> **ANEMIA — ONGOING**

> **CARDIAC RISK — ONGOING**

> **SUPPORT NETWORK — PRESENT**
