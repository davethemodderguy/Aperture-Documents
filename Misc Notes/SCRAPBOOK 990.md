# 1. EVENT SUMMARY

At approximately **[TIME REDACTED]**, Neri returned to the Blacksite alongside Bao following Bao's successful entry into Neri's subspace.

Immediately preceding the return, Neri's personal subspace underwent a complete reintegration.

The fifteen floating islands, artificial stellar environment, localized gravity regions, and isolated spatial structure ceased existing as a separated pocket of reality.

The space was restored to its original configuration.

There was no explosion.

No dimensional collapse.

No structural damage.

No containment breach.

The subspace simply became part of the Blacksite again.

And Neri came home.

---

# 2. NERI — CURRENT MEDICAL STATUS

**STATUS:** ALIVE  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE MEDICAL ROOM  
**CONDITION:** STABLE

Neri remains medically abnormal.

Her underlying blood-degradation condition has **not** disappeared.

She still experiences anemia.

Her cardiovascular system remains vulnerable.

Her blood continues to deteriorate spontaneously.

She still requires Bao's compatible blood to survive severe episodes.

The return therefore does **not** represent a medical cure.

It represents something considerably simpler:

> **Neri is alive, and she is no longer alone somewhere outside the Blacksite.**

Medical personnel have been instructed to continue treating her condition exactly as before.

Bao remains Neri's primary compatible blood source.

Neri has also been informed that requiring help does not constitute failure.

She has not entirely accepted this statement.

She has, however, stopped arguing about it.

For now.

---

# 3. BLACKSITE REACTION

The moment Neri's return was confirmed, word spread through the SAFEHOUSE.

The reaction was immediate.

People came to see her.

Nobody demanded an explanation first.

Nobody asked why she disappeared before saying they were glad she was alive.

Nobody treated her return as an inconvenience.

They were simply happy.

Neri was happy too.

She had spent two months believing that everyone would eventually move on without her.

Instead, she returned to people who had been waiting for her.

# 5. MEDICAL NOTE

Neri's return must **not** be interpreted as the end of her medical risk.

Her condition remains.

Her anemia remains.

Bao's blood remains medically significant to her survival.

The emergency blood-response procedures remain active.

The difference is that Neri is no longer isolated.

If her condition deteriorates, there are people nearby.

If she collapses, someone can find her.

If she is afraid of bothering someone, someone can remind her:

> **She is allowed to bother them.**

# 6. FINAL SAFEHOUSE STATEMENT

For two months, the SAFEHOUSE had an empty space where Neri used to be.

Her room was empty.

Her chair was empty.

Her books remained untouched.

Her cake was left at the memorial.

Her friends kept waiting.

Now she is back.

The subspace has reintegrated.

The impossible distance between the Blacksite and Neri has disappeared.

The fifteen islands are no longer somewhere unreachable.

And Neri is no longer a missing resident.

She is a resident again.

Still fragile.

Still anemic.

Still occasionally stubborn.

Still capable of forgetting that she matters.

But surrounded by people who refuse to let her forget for very long.

**FINAL STATUS:**

> **NERI IS BACK.**

> **SAFEHOUSE IS COMPLETE.**

> **SHE IS NOT ALONE.**
