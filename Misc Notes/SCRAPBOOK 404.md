### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION LOG**

# **ORPHIE — ABSENT ON HER BIRTHDAY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Concern  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Marts / Luna / Solaria  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **SEVERE CONCERN**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Today is Orphie's birthday.

The SAFEHOUSE had quietly prepared for it.

Orphie did not come.

No message was sent.

No explanation was provided.

She simply remained at the remote treehouse.

---

# **THE BIRTHDAY**

The preparations were deliberately modest.

Nobody wanted to overwhelm her.

The residents prepared:

- Food.
    
- A small cake.
    
- A few decorations.
    
- A comfortable place for her to sit.
    
- Several gifts.
    

The Marts were especially excited.

They had been waiting for the occasion.

One Mart had apparently spent an unusually long time deciding where to place its tiny top hat.

---

# **THE EMPTY SEAT**

One seat was left open for Orphie.

Nobody wanted to remove it.

The cake remained untouched for some time.

Eventually, Belle asked:

> "...Is she coming?"

Nobody answered.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin had intended to bring Orphie herself.

She went to the treehouse.

When she returned, she was alone.

She did not explain what happened immediately.

She simply sat down.

Star quietly asked:

> "...She doesn't want to come?"

Corin shook her head.

> "...She didn't say."

---

# **THE CAKE**

The cake was originally intended to be made by Orphie.

Instead, the SAFEHOUSE prepared one for her.

It was not intended to replace her cooking.

It was simply meant to say:

> **"We remembered."**

Nobody ate it at first.

Eventually, the residents decided that leaving it untouched would accomplish nothing.

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts were visibly confused.

Birthdays had always been something they enjoyed.

They understand celebrations.

They understand gifts.

They understand making someone happy.

But Orphie was not there.

Several Marts gathered around the empty seat.

One quietly asked:

> "...Where Orphie?"

Another answered:

> "...Treehouse."

The first Mart:

> "...Why?"

No answer followed.

---

# **THE GIFT**

The Marts had prepared something themselves.

It was small.

A soft handmade item assembled from materials they had gathered.

It was placed beside the empty seat.

Nobody moved it.

---

# **LUNA**

Luna was eventually asked whether Orphie would come.

She answered:

> "...No."

The researcher asked how she knew.

> "...Because she thinks she shouldn't be here."

Luna looked toward the empty seat.

> "...It's her birthday."

Pause.

> "...And she still thinks nobody wants her around."

---

# **SOLARIA**

Solaria's response was quieter.

> "...She knows it's her birthday."

> "...She just doesn't think it matters."

The researcher asked whether Solaria thought Orphie had forgotten.

> "...No."

> "...She remembers."

> "...That's why she stayed away."

---

# **ORPHIE'S ABSENCE**

The most painful part of the day is not that Orphie missed a celebration.

It is that everyone had expected her absence.

Nobody was surprised.

That realization has deeply affected the SAFEHOUSE residents.

They have become accustomed to Orphie withdrawing.

Accustomed to her refusing invitations.

Accustomed to her silence.

Accustomed to her saying that she does not care.

Her birthday made the pattern impossible to ignore.

---

# **CORIN'S RETURN**

Corin eventually returned to the treehouse.

She found Orphie exactly where she had expected.

Quiet.

Awake.

Alone.

Corin placed the birthday gift on the table.

> "...Happy birthday."

Orphie looked at it.

> "...Thanks."

Corin:

> "...Everyone remembered."

Orphie:

> "...I know."

Corin sat down.

> "...You didn't have to come."

Orphie:

> "...I know."

---

# **THE MARTS FOLLOW**

Several Marts eventually accompanied Corin home.

They approached Orphie cautiously.

One climbed onto her lap.

Another placed the handmade gift beside her.

Orphie looked at it.

> "...You made this?"

The Mart nodded enthusiastically.

> "...For Orphie."

She stared at it for several seconds.

> "...Okay."

The Mart remained beside her.

---

# **THE BIRTHDAY THAT DIDN'T HAPPEN**

There was no celebration.

No laughter.

No birthday wishes shouted across the room.

No cake eaten together.

No photographs.

No drawings.

No Marts piling onto Orphie.

Only an empty seat at SAFEHOUSE.

And a small gift sitting beside it.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Orphie:**  
Absent from her birthday gathering. Continues to isolate herself and demonstrate severe emotional detachment.

**Corin:**  
Returned to the treehouse. Continuing to remain present despite Orphie's withdrawal.

**Marts:**  
Deeply concerned. Refuse to abandon her.

**SAFEHOUSE:**  
Concerned and saddened by her absence.

**Luna:**  
Concerned.

**Solaria:**  
Concerned.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Nothing seems to be working.

The people around Orphie remember her.

They visit her.

They wait for her.

They prepare food for her.

They keep her seat open.

The Marts still follow her.

Corin still comes home.

Her birthday still arrives.

And yet Orphie remains in the treehouse.

The cake sits at SAFEHOUSE.

The gifts remain unopened.

The Marts wait.

Everyone remembers.

Orphie doesn't believe they do.

And today, of all days, the empty seat made that difference impossible to ignore.

**End of Personnel Welfare / SAFEHOUSE Observation Log**
