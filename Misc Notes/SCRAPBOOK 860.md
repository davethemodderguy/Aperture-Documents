# 1. PURPOSE

This document establishes the correct interpretation of cake as a comfort measure for resident **Neri**.

Previous informal observations have occasionally been misinterpreted to suggest that providing Neri with sweets causes her to disclose information regarding her past.

**This is incorrect.**

Cake does not function as a psychological access mechanism.

Neri likes cake.

That is all.

---

# 2. NORMAL RESPONSE TO CAKE

When Neri is in a normal or positive mood, receiving cake generally produces a normal response:

> **"Thank you."**

She eats it.

She may ask for another piece.

She may compliment whoever prepared it.

She may continue whatever activity she was doing beforehand.

She does **not** automatically begin discussing her childhood, her sister, SPAWN, the experimentation, or any other traumatic experiences.

In most cases, she simply enjoys the cake.

---

# 3. WHEN DISCLOSURE MAY OCCUR

There is one notable correlation.

When Neri is already experiencing significant sadness, loneliness, exhaustion, or emotional distress, familiar comforts — including cake — can sometimes make her feel sufficiently safe to voluntarily talk.

This is **not guaranteed**.

It is also **not intentional on Neri's part**.

A typical sequence may be:

> Neri feels sad.

> Someone gives her cake.

> Neri eats.

> Neri becomes somewhat calmer.

> Neri may begin talking.

Sometimes she talks about ordinary things.

Sometimes she says nothing.

Sometimes she may mention her sister.

Sometimes she may discuss something from her childhood.

Sometimes she simply eats the cake and goes to sleep.

All outcomes are normal.

---

# 4. DISCLOSURE CANNOT BE FORCED

Under no circumstances is personnel permitted to attempt to induce disclosure through food.

The following are **prohibited**:

> "I'll give you cake if you tell me what happened."

> "Tell me about your family and I'll get you another slice."

> "You seem sad. Tell me about your past."

> "Come on, Neri. You talked about it last time."

> "You already told the Archivists, so you can tell me."

These approaches are considered inappropriate.

Neri's past belongs to her.

If she wants to discuss it, she may.

If she does not want to discuss it, **the conversation ends there.**

---

# 5. ARCHIVIST GUIDANCE

If Neri begins discussing traumatic memories while eating, personnel should:

1. Listen.
    
2. Do not interrupt unnecessarily.
    
3. Do not interrogate.
    
4. Do not demand details.
    
5. Do not ask leading questions.
    
6. Do not treat spontaneous disclosure as an opportunity to collect intelligence.
    
7. Allow Neri to stop whenever she wants.
    
8. Continue providing ordinary emotional support.
    

If she changes the subject to cake, fish, Alice's scooter, or anything else, **follow her lead.**

She does not owe anyone a complete account of her life.

---

# 6. PREVIOUS MISINTERPRETATION

An earlier archival note described sweets as having been used to "get Neri to talk."

This wording has been corrected.

The actual circumstances were considerably more nuanced.

During one particularly difficult period following her rescue, Neri was visibly distressed and repeatedly spent time in the cafeteria eating cake while crying.

On several occasions she muttered fragments regarding her sister.

Later, during a voluntary interview, sweets were present as a source of comfort.

Neri continued speaking for approximately six hours.

The duration of that interview was **her choice**.

The cake did not compel the conversation.

---

# 7. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

### CAKE CAN:

- Make Neri happy.
    
- Comfort her.
    
- Help her feel safe.
    
- Give her energy.
    
- Provide a familiar positive experience.
    
- Sometimes create an environment in which she feels comfortable enough to voluntarily talk.
    

### CAKE CANNOT:

- Force memories out of her.
    
- Make her confess information.
    
- Override her wishes.
    
- Cure trauma.
    
- Treat her anemia.
    
- Replace blood.
    
- Guarantee emotional disclosure.
    

**CAKE IS FOOD.**

It is not a truth serum.

# 9. FINAL DIRECTIVE

Personnel are reminded that Neri's willingness to discuss her past is **not a service that can be purchased with cake.**

When she is sad, cake may help.

When she wants to talk, listen.

When she doesn't, let her eat her cake in peace.

Do not turn one of the few things that makes her happy into another obligation.
