### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

# **ORPHIE — LOSS OF CORE VALUES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Concern  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Marts / Luna / Solaria  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **SEVERE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie's current behavior appears to be centered around a single belief:

> **"I am unimportant."**

The belief has become increasingly rigid.

She repeats it internally.

She behaves according to it.

She interprets other people's actions through it.

And she has begun using it to justify her complete withdrawal from the lives of others.

However, C.O.R.E. has identified another significant change.

Orphie appears to have forgotten one of her own core values:

> **Caring for other people.**

---

# **THE OLD ORPHIE**

Before her emotional collapse, Orphie was deeply concerned with other people's wellbeing.

She cooked for others.

She shared food.

She spent time with the SAFEHOUSE residents.

She cared for the Marts.

She listened when someone was struggling.

She worried when someone was hurt.

She created things for other people.

Her kindness was not something she performed for recognition.

It was simply part of who she was.

---

# **THE CHANGE**

That behavior has almost completely disappeared.

Orphie no longer asks:

> "Are you okay?"

She no longer worries about whether someone has eaten.

She no longer tries to make people happy.

She no longer notices when someone is upset unless it directly affects her.

When someone expresses concern for her, she becomes irritated.

When someone expresses concern about themselves, she often remains indifferent.

---

# **THE CONTRADICTION**

Orphie's belief is:

> **"I am unimportant."**

But the conclusion she has reached from this belief is not:

> _"Everyone else is important."_

Instead, it has become:

> **"Nothing I do matters."**

And eventually:

> **"Therefore, I don't need to care."**

This is a fundamental departure from the person she previously was.

---

# **CORIN'S OBSERVATION**

Corin noticed the change while preparing dinner.

She accidentally burned part of the meal.

Previously, Orphie would have noticed immediately.

She would have helped.

This time, Orphie simply looked at the food.

> **Corin:** "...I ruined it."

> **Orphie:** "...Okay."

Corin looked at her.

> "...That's it?"

Orphie shrugged.

> "...It's food."

She walked away.

---

# **THE COOKBOOK**

The cookbook belonging to Orphie's mother remains sealed away.

It contains countless recipes that Orphie once treasured.

She used to cook from it regularly.

She used to make meals for the Friendly Roommates.

She used to make breakfast every morning.

She used to experiment with recipes.

Now the cookbook remains untouched.

Not because she has forgotten how to cook.

Because she no longer sees a reason to cook for anyone.

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts were once among the strongest recipients of Orphie's affection.

She tolerated their antics.

She fed them.

She allowed them to use her as a warm place to sleep.

She wore their hats.

She worried about their safety.

Now she deliberately frightens them away.

The same person who once protected the Marts now uses her abilities to ensure they stay away from her.

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

When confronted about this change:

> **Corin:** "...You used to care about them."

> **Orphie:** "...I don't anymore."

> "...Why?"

> "...Why should I?"

Corin struggled to answer.

Orphie continued:

> "...Nobody needs me."

---

# **THE FORGOTTEN VALUE**

Orphie appears to have forgotten that caring for others was never dependent on being important.

She cared because she **wanted people to be okay**.

There was no requirement that they repay her.

There was no requirement that they remember every kindness.

There was no requirement that she be central to their lives.

She simply cared.

That distinction has been lost.

---

# **LUNA**

Luna described the change as:

> "...She's so focused on proving that she doesn't matter that she's forgotten why she mattered."

The researcher asked what she meant.

Luna answered:

> "...She made people feel cared for."

---

# **SOLARIA**

Solaria was more direct.

> "...She thinks being unimportant means she has permission to stop caring."

She paused.

> "...That's not who she was."

---

# **THE MOST CONCERNING DEVELOPMENT**

Orphie's emotional detachment was already concerning.

Her isolation was concerning.

Her use of anomalous abilities to drive people away was concerning.

But the loss of her instinct to care for others represents something different.

It is not merely that Orphie has stopped receiving affection.

She has begun rejecting the values that once defined how she **gave** affection.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Primary fixation:**

> **"I am unimportant."**

**Former core value:**

> **Caring for other people.**

**Current expression:**  
Minimal concern for others. Deliberate emotional distance. Active deterrence of people attempting to reconnect.

**Marts:**  
No longer approached by Orphie.

**Corin:**  
Increasingly unable to reach her.

**SAFEHOUSE:**  
Maintaining distance while continuing to remember her.

**Luna:**  
Concerned.

**Solaria:**  
Concerned.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie believes she has become unimportant.

She has spent so much time trying to prove that belief that she has forgotten something fundamental.

Her worth was never measured by how many people needed her.

It was never measured by how often people mentioned her.

It was never measured by whether she was the most important person in the room.

She cared because that was who she was.

She cooked because people needed food.

She drew because it made someone smile.

She stayed beside people because they were hurting.

She cared about Marts because they were Marts.

She cared about Corin because Corin was Corin.

None of those things required Orphie to be important.

Now she has forgotten that.

And perhaps the saddest part is that the person who once spent so much effort making sure everyone else felt valued has convinced herself that **her own existence has no value at all.**

**End of Personnel Welfare / Psychological Observation**
