# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **BLACKSITE PERSONNEL HANDBOOK**

### **BEHAVIORAL PROFILE – DIRECTOR BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Internal Reference

---

## **SUMMARY**

Director Belle Merryweather is an unusually affectionate individual among Blacksite personnel.

While always respectful of personal boundaries, Belle is well known for enjoying hugs from close friends and trusted colleagues.

Among those who know her well, physical affection is considered a normal part of daily life.

---

## **OBSERVED BEHAVIOR**

Belle has been observed:

- Greeting close friends with hugs.
    
- Leaning against trusted companions during long meetings.
    
- Accepting hugs without hesitation.
    
- Falling asleep while being hugged during extended trips.
    
- Using friends as impromptu pillows during movie nights.
    

Personnel familiar with Belle describe physical affection as one of the ways she expresses trust.

---

## **ENDMIN INCIDENTS**

A recurring occurrence documented by Room N-07 residents involves Endmin unintentionally becoming Belle's "hugging pillow."

Typical sequence:

1. Endmin sits down.
    
2. Belle sits beside her.
    
3. Conversation continues.
    
4. Several minutes later, Belle has quietly leaned against Endmin.
    
5. Endmin realizes she is now functioning as a pillow.
    

Attempts to move generally result in Belle instinctively hugging tighter while remaining completely unaware.

Endmin has reportedly stopped trying.

---

## **FIELD OBSERVATION**

During one evening in Room N-07, researchers observed the following:

Belle quietly sat beside Endmin while everyone talked.

Approximately fifteen minutes later, Belle had wrapped both arms around Endmin and was comfortably resting against her.

Endmin glanced down.

Looked around the room.

Shrugged.

Continued the conversation as though nothing unusual had happened.

No one else reacted.

The behavior appeared entirely normal to everyone present.

## **PERSONNEL NOTE**

Director Belle Merryweather values physical affection as a gesture of friendship and trust.

Personnel should always respect personal boundaries and obtain consent before initiating physical contact.

Conversely, those who have earned Belle's trust may occasionally find themselves promoted—without warning—to the highly unofficial position of **"Belle's Hugging Pillow."**

The position carries no additional pay.

It does, however, come with a very comfortable bunny plushie.
