# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / MOBILITY OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — CARRYING FLIGHT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Ability:** **Aerial Transport / Load Carrying**  
**Status:** **Confirmed**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's wings are capable of carrying significantly more than Corin herself.

She can pick up and fly with **people or objects**, provided she can physically secure them.

The ability appears to be a natural extension of her flight capability.

Corin can maintain controlled flight while carrying another individual.

Her most frequent passenger is, unsurprisingly:

**Orphie.**

---

# **ORPHIE — PRIMARY PASSENGER**

Orphie has become Corin's most frequent passenger.

The arrangement began casually.

Orphie needed to reach the Blacksite.

Corin offered:

> **"I'll carry you."**

Orphie stared at her.

> **"...You can do that?"**

Corin unfolded her wings.

> **"Probably."**

She could.

Since then, Orphie has occasionally allowed Corin to carry her between the treehouse and the Blacksite.

---

# **CARRYING CAPABILITY**

Corin can transport:

- One person.
    
- Multiple lightweight objects.
    
- Supplies.
    
- Bags.
    
- Small equipment.
    
- Other anomalous objects.
    
- Orphie.
    

She can maintain stability while carrying passengers and adjust her flight posture to compensate for additional weight.

Her wings appear capable of producing considerably more lift than their physical size would normally permit.

Ether integration is believed to contribute to this discrepancy.

---

# **ORPHIE'S PREFERRED METHOD**

Orphie usually holds onto Corin from behind while Corin flies.

Corin's wings fold slightly around her when they are not actively needed for maneuvering.

The arrangement is considerably more stable than it initially appeared.

Orphie has become comfortable enough with the process that she sometimes simply asks:

> **"Can you fly me there?"**

Corin responds:

> **"Sure."**

And off they go.

---

# **SUPPLY RUNS**

Corin has also started using her carrying ability for practical purposes.

A typical trip may involve:

**Corin:** Flying.

**Orphie:** Passenger.

**Supplies:** Attached to Orphie.

**Destination:** Blacksite.

**Return:** Same arrangement.

This has significantly reduced the amount of time required for supply runs.

It has also resulted in Corin occasionally returning to the treehouse carrying considerably more supplies than she could reasonably transport by walking.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE REACTIONS**

The SAFEHOUSE residents quickly became accustomed to seeing Corin arrive from the sky.

Neko was particularly unimpressed.

> **Neko:**  
> "You could have just used the road."

Corin landed.

> **"Flying is faster."**

Neko nodded.

> **"...Fair."**

---

# **BELLE**

Belle has repeatedly asked to be carried.

Orphie has repeatedly told her:

> **"Ask Corin."**

Corin usually agrees.

This has resulted in several short recreational flights around the perimeter.

Belle considers this one of the best things about Corin's wings.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi has also been carried once.

She initially refused.

Corin insisted.

Eventually Lumi agreed.

After landing, Lumi simply said:

> **"...That was surprisingly comfortable."**

Corin smiled.

> **"I told you."**

Lumi has not yet admitted that she enjoyed it.

Nobody believes her.

---

# **LIMITATIONS**

Despite the apparent strength of Corin's wings, C.O.R.E. does not recommend unlimited load testing.

Current observations indicate that Corin can carry substantial weight, but the exact maximum remains unknown.

The following are therefore prohibited without supervision:

- Carrying excessively heavy objects.
    
- High-speed maneuvering with passengers.
    
- Extreme-altitude flight.
    
- Deliberate overloading.
    
- Carrying unsecured hazardous materials.
    

Corin has been specifically reminded not to treat herself as an unlimited aerial transport vehicle.

Her response:

> **"Okay."**

She then proceeded to carry Orphie anyway.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

The carrying ability has had a surprisingly positive effect on Corin.

For so long, Corin believed she was dependent on others.

Now she can be the one carrying someone else.

She can help.

She can transport people.

She can bring supplies home.

She can make things easier for Orphie.

This appears to matter to her.

During one flight, Orphie told her:

> **"You know, you don't have to carry me every time."**

Corin replied:

> **"I know."**

A pause.

> **"...I like carrying you."**

Orphie smiled.

> **"Then I guess you're stuck with me."**

Corin laughed.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin once believed she was nothing more than a broken body being held together by Ether.

Now she has wings.

She can fly.

She can cross the forest whenever she wants.

She can carry supplies.

She can carry people.

And, most importantly to her, she can carry **Orphie**.

For someone who spent so long believing she was only a burden to others, being physically capable of carrying someone she loves is perhaps more meaningful than Corin realizes.

**End of Anomalous Physiology / Mobility Observation**
