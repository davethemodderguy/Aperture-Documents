# 6. COMMON FEEDBACK

Across all participating groups, the most frequently reported problems were:

### **1. Jane's unpredictability**

Participants could not reliably determine what Jane would do next.

### **2. Jane's observation skills**

Participants repeatedly reported feeling watched even when Jane was not visibly observing them.

### **3. Psychological manipulation**

Participants became increasingly uncertain whether their own assumptions were reliable.

### **4. Group distrust**

Participants began suspecting one another despite receiving no definitive evidence of betrayal.

### **5. Sleep deprivation**

Participants reported difficulty sleeping because they remained concerned Jane might interact with them while they were asleep.

### **6. Jane's normal behavior**

This was repeatedly identified as the most disturbing aspect.

Jane could sit in the kitchen making food.

Jane could watch television.

Jane could drink coffee.

Jane could have an ordinary conversation.

And participants still could not relax.
