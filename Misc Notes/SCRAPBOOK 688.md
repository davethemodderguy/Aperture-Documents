### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / ENTITY PSYCHOLOGY & MEDICAL LOG**

# **BUNNY — WHAT REMAINS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Bunny  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE / Treehouse  
**Status:** **MEMORY LOSS CONFIRMED / MEDICALLY FRAGILE / STABLE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Further examination of Bunny's memories has revealed a more severe condition than initially believed.

Bunny does not simply have fragmented memories of her past.

She has forgotten almost **everything**.

Her mother's face.

Her mother's voice.

Her mother's name.

Her own original name.

Where she lived.

Where she came from.

What happened immediately before she ran.

Most of it is gone.

There is only one memory she consistently retains.

> **"Mom killed."**

---

# **HER NAME**

"Bunny" is not necessarily Bunny's original name.

When Corin first found her, she had no name to provide.

When asked:

> **"What's your name?"**

She pointed toward herself.

> **"Bunny."**

At the time, this was interpreted as an identification.

It may not have been.

Bunny may simply have understood that the rabbit everyone was referring to was **her**.

She accepted the word.

Eventually, it became her name.

---

# **MEMORY LOSS**

Bunny cannot describe what she looked like before arriving at the Treehouse.

She cannot remember her mother's appearance.

She cannot remember her mother's name.

She cannot remember whether she had siblings.

She cannot remember where she lived.

She cannot remember how long she was alone.

Attempts to recover these memories produce little useful information.

The memories simply aren't there.

When asked:

> **"What did your mother look like?"**

Bunny answers:

> **"...Don't know."**

> **"What was her name?"**

> **"...Don't know."**

> **"Do you remember her voice?"**

A long pause.

> **"...No."**

Then the smile.

> **"Bunny happy."**

---

# **THE ONE MEMORY THAT REMAINS**

There is one fact Bunny refuses to forget.

Her mother was killed.

She does not know how.

She does not remember who did it.

She does not remember where it happened.

She does not remember what her mother looked like.

But the fact remains.

> **"Mom killed."**

It appears to be the final surviving fragment of her earliest trauma.

Everything surrounding it has faded.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Bunny's physical condition is equally unusual.

She is naturally fragile.

Medical examination indicates significantly reduced physical resilience across several systems.

### **Musculoskeletal**

Bunny possesses unusually low muscle strength.

Her physical power is considerably below that of an average human adult.

Her bones are also comparatively fragile.

Minor injuries that would normally be insignificant can pose a greater risk to Bunny.

---

### **Immune System**

Bunny's immune response is unusually weak.

She is significantly more vulnerable to:

- Common infections.
    
- Respiratory illness.
    
- Fever.
    
- Environmental pathogens.
    
- Prolonged exposure to extreme temperatures.
    

This is particularly concerning given her history of surviving outdoors.

---

### **General Condition**

Bunny tires easily.

She requires adequate food, hydration, warmth, and rest.

Her body is simply not built for prolonged physical strain.

Medical personnel have therefore classified her as:

> **MEDICALLY FRAGILE**

This does not prevent her from living normally.

It simply means that her health must be monitored more carefully.

---

# **AND YET**

Despite everything...

Bunny stands.

She walks.

She helps Corin.

She tends the garden.

She carries what she can.

She sits beside Corin.

She sleeps beside Corin.

She gets sick.

She recovers.

She gets scared.

She smiles.

She cries.

Then she stands again.

---

# **CORIN'S OBSERVATION**

Corin is aware of Bunny's physical weakness.

She is also aware that Bunny hates being treated as though she is incapable.

One morning, Corin tried to carry Bunny back inside after noticing she was tired.

Bunny protested.

> **"Bunny walk."**

Corin:

> **"You're tired."**

> **"Bunny walk."**

> **"You can rest."**

Bunny shook her head.

> **"Bunny stand."**

Corin eventually let her.

Bunny slowly walked beside her.

Not because she was strong.

Because she wanted to stay beside Corin.

---

# **STANDING TOGETHER**

There is an unusual pattern between the two.

Corin does not ask Bunny to become stronger.

Bunny does not ask Corin to become happier.

They simply remain together.

When Bunny becomes tired, Corin waits.

When Corin becomes quiet, Bunny sits beside her.

When Bunny becomes frightened, Corin holds her hand.

When Corin becomes worried, Bunny remains close.

Neither needs to say much.

---

# **BELLE'S OBSERVATION**

Belle once watched Bunny struggle to carry a small basket from the garden.

Belle offered to help.

Bunny initially refused.

> **"Bunny carry."**

Belle waited.

Bunny took several steps.

Then stopped.

Belle smiled.

> **"Can I help now?"**

Bunny considered it.

> **"...Okay."**

Belle took one side.

Bunny took the other.

Together, they carried it inside.

Belle later commented:

> **"Bunny is small."**

Then:

> **"But Bunny tries really hard."**

---

# **THE IRONY**

Bunny has forgotten almost everything about herself.

Her original name.

Her family.

Her home.

Her mother's face.

Her mother's voice.

Her own childhood.

Yet she remembers one thing.

She remembers losing someone.

And perhaps that is why she holds so tightly to the people she has now.

Corin.

Orphie.

Belle.

The SAFEHOUSE residents.

She doesn't remember who she was.

So she has accepted who she is now.

**Bunny.**

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Bunny doesn't remember her own name.

She doesn't remember her mother's face.

She doesn't remember her voice.

She doesn't remember where she came from.

She doesn't even know if **Bunny** was ever supposed to be her name.

She simply accepted it.

Because Corin called her Bunny.

And Corin stayed.

Her body is fragile.

Her bones are weak.

Her muscles are weak.

Her immune system is weak.

She gets sick easily.

She cannot endure what most people can.

But every morning, Bunny still gets up.

She stands beside Corin.

And when she cannot walk any further, she rests.

Then she stands again.

She has lost almost everything she once knew.

But she has not lost the people beside her.

And for now, that's enough.

> **"Bunny stand with Big Sis."**
