# **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

**Time:** ██:██

A high-priority distress beacon registered to **Director Belle Merryweather** was automatically activated.

The beacon transmitted for approximately **4.8 seconds** before abruptly going offline.

The final coordinates directed response teams to an abandoned industrial district on the outskirts of the Banlands.

A joint response team consisting of the Krampus Compliance Authority, the Central Execution Department, Banlands Enforcement Division, and Aperture Industries arrived within minutes.

Director Belle Merryweather was **not** present.

---

# **SCENE OBSERVATIONS**

The scene showed unmistakable signs of an intense close-quarters battle.

Investigators documented:

- Multiple reinforced concrete pillars sliced completely through with clean, single-strike cuts.
    
- Deep slash marks across walls, floors, and structural supports.
    
- Heavy impact craters.
    
- Scattered debris covering the surrounding area.
    

No civilian casualties were found.

No bodies were recovered.

---

# **RECOVERED EVIDENCE**

Approximately thirty meters from the apparent center of the confrontation, investigators located Belle's weapon.

Her oversized hybrid sword.

The blade had been driven into the ground.

No visible structural damage was observed.

Forensic officers immediately secured the weapon.

The fact that Belle had been separated from it was considered highly unusual.

Personnel familiar with her habits unanimously agreed:

> **Belle would not willingly abandon her sword.**

---

# **FORENSIC PRELIMINARY REPORT**

The combat damage indicates Belle actively resisted.

The clean cuts through reinforced concrete suggest repeated use of her full strength during the engagement.

No evidence indicates she surrendered voluntarily.

The absence of remains, combined with the sudden loss of the distress signal, supports the working conclusion that Belle was removed from the scene against her will.

---

# **INVESTIGATION STATUS**

The incident has been officially reclassified.

**Previous Status:**

Missing Person Investigation.

**Current Status:**

**Confirmed Kidnapping.**

---

# **PERSONNEL REACTIONS**

## **Endmin Merryweather**

Endmin was among the first to reach Belle's sword.

She slowly knelt beside it.

Her hand rested against the white-wrapped grip.

She stared at it for several seconds.

> **"...She fought..."

She looked around at the severed pillars.

> **"...She fought with everything she had."

For the first time since the search began...

Endmin knew Belle had not left on her own.

---

## **Lucius Merryweather**

Lucius carefully examined the battlefield.

Years of surviving on the streets had taught him how to read signs of violence.

He looked toward the broken pillars.

Then toward Belle's sword.

His voice was quiet.

> **"...Whoever did this..."

> **"...They expected resistance."

---

## **Lucy Merryweather**

Lucy stood silently at the center of the destroyed area.

She closed her eyes briefly.

When she opened them again, her expression had hardened.

> **"...Find them."

No one questioned the order.

---

## **Director Lucious**

After reviewing the initial forensic photographs, Director Lucious immediately authorized additional investigative resources.

His written directive contained only three lines.

> **"This is no longer a search."**

> **"This is a rescue operation."**

> **"Identify those responsible."**

## **FINAL ENTRY**

The battlefield was eventually cleared.

Evidence was cataloged.

Photographs were taken.

Measurements recorded.

Belle's sword was carefully removed from the ground and placed into protective custody, awaiting the day its owner could reclaim it.

As the last investigators departed, the scarred concrete pillars remained standing.

Silent witnesses to a fight that Belle had refused to give up...

...even after the battle was taken from her.
