# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **LUMI OBSERVATION LOG – FAMILY MEMORY RESTORATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the return of **AE-LK-01 "Lumi"** to Corin Lockwood's daily life, Medical Services have observed a gradual change.

The most significant improvement has not been physical.

It has been memory.

For a long time, Corin associated her childhood almost entirely with the events that followed.

The operation.

The experimentation.

The loss.

The years afterward.

The memories before that period became distant, fragmented, and painful to access.

Lumi has begun helping Corin remember something she had slowly lost.

Not the tragedy.

The life before it.

---

# **FAMILY MEMORIES**

Lumi rarely talks about the worst moments.

Instead, she talks about ordinary days.

Small moments.

The moments that made them a family.

---

## **CORIN LOCKWOOD**

Lumi often reminds Corin of what she was like as a child.

> "You were always curious."

> "You asked questions about everything."

> "You wanted to know how things worked."

According to Lumi, Corin was the quietest of the three siblings, but also the one who noticed the most.

She remembered details others missed.

She would organize things that were not hers.

She would try to solve problems much bigger than herself.

Lumi smiled when recalling:

> "You once tried to fix a broken toy because you thought everything could be repaired if you tried hard enough."

Corin initially laughed.

Then became quiet.

Because she remembered.

---

## **NORMA LOCKWOOD**

Lumi frequently tells stories about Norma's childhood.

Especially one particular detail.

Thunderstorms.

The same Norma known today for her confidence, engineering ability, and combat capability once had a very different reaction to storms.

According to Lumi:

> "The moment thunder started, Norma would pretend she wasn't scared."

> "Then she would quietly move closer."

Corin remembered this.

She remembered Norma hiding under blankets.

She remembered trying to stay awake to keep her sister company.

She remembered that Norma always acted brave afterward.

Even when she wasn't.

---

## **ARCTURUS LOCKWOOD**

Lumi often laughs when talking about Arcturus.

His relationship with animals was apparently obvious from a young age.

Especially horses.

> "He never met a horse that didn't like him."

According to Lumi, Arcturus would approach unfamiliar horses carefully.

No fear.

No hesitation.

Just patience.

Within minutes, the animal would trust him.

Corin remembered teasing him about it.

Calling him a "horse magnet."

Arcturus denied it every time.

The evidence suggested otherwise.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

During one evening in her office, Lumi told another childhood story.

Corin listened quietly.

Then interrupted.

> "...Wait."

Lumi looked at her.

Corin stared at the desk.

> "...I remember that."

For several seconds, neither spoke.

Then Corin smiled.

A real smile.

Not forced.

Not polite.

A memory that belonged to her.

Not to the experiment.

Not to the trauma.

Just her.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

Medical staff noted a significant difference in Corin's behavior.

Previously, memories of her childhood were dominated by:

- Fear.
    
- Loss.
    
- Confusion.
    
- Pain.
    

After Lumi's return, those memories have slowly become more balanced.

Corin has begun recognizing that her life did not begin with the operation.

She had a childhood before it.

She had a family before it.

She was loved before it.

---

# **CORIN'S STATEMENT**

When asked how she felt about remembering her mother again, Corin answered:

> "...I thought I lost her."

A pause.

> "...But I didn't."

She looked toward Lumi.

> "...I just forgot."

---

# **LUMI'S NOTE**

When asked why she kept telling old stories, Lumi gave a simple answer.

> "Because she deserves to remember."

She smiled.

> "Corin spent so long remembering what was taken from her."

> "I wanted her to remember what she had."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin continues working from KCA Headquarters, with Lumi remaining nearby.

She has not yet fully returned to SAFEHOUSE.

However, a significant barrier has begun to weaken.

Corin no longer sees her past as only the moment everything was lost.

She is beginning to remember the years before.

The laughter.

The family.

The ordinary moments.

The person she was.

And the person she still is.

**End of Lumi Observation Log**
