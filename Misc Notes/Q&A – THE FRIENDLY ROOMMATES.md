# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **BLACKSITE INFORMATION DOSSIER**

### **Q&A – THE FRIENDLY ROOMMATES**

**Classification:** Public Release

---

### **Q: Who are the Friendly Roommates?**

**A:** The Friendly Roommates are a close-knit group of anomalous residents living within Heaven's Blacksite. Despite their diverse origins and abilities, they are best known for treating one another like family.

---

### **Q: Who are the members?**

**A:**

- Star
    
- Nightshade
    
- Taiga
    
- Mart
    
- Frost
    
- Neko
    
- Miss Neko
    

They are frequently joined by close friends and visitors, though the above are recognized as the core residents.

---

### **Q: Why are they called the "Friendly Roommates"?**

**A:** Because they quite literally live together and, despite occasional chaos, maintain an unusually close and supportive relationship.

The name began informally among researchers and eventually stuck.

---

### **Q: Are they dangerous?**

**A:** Individually, several members possess capabilities that would be considered extremely dangerous under ordinary circumstances.

Fortunately, they are also among the friendliest residents of the Blacksite.

As with all anomalies, personnel should follow established safety procedures and treat them with respect.

---

### **Q: Who is the loudest?**

**A:** Usually Star.

Room N-07 tends to become noticeably louder whenever Star gets excited.

---

### **Q: Who is the quietest?**

**A:** Nightshade.

She generally speaks only when she has something meaningful to say.

---

### **Q: Who cooks?**

**A:** Whoever reaches the kitchen first.

Results vary.

Researchers are advised to confirm whether Neko was involved before eating experimental recipes.

---

### **Q: Who fixes things?**

**A:** Neko.

If the repair somehow makes the object stronger than before, that is considered normal.

Miss Neko usually ensures the repair is also safe.

---

### **Q: Is Frost really able to freeze water just by stepping on it?**

**A:** Yes.

Please do not ask her to demonstrate inside the cafeteria.

---

### **Q: Can Mart actually fly?**

**A:** Yes.

Belle has been observed riding on Mart's back during transport on multiple occasions.

Neither party appears to find this unusual.

---

### **Q: Can Star teleport?**

**A:** Yes.

She has also been known to teleport while hugging Belle.

Belle has described this experience as:

> "Convenient."

---

### **Q: Why does everyone spoil Belle?**

**A:** No official explanation exists.

It simply... happens.

Examples include:

- Carrying her around.
    
- Giving her snacks.
    
- Letting her ride on their backs.
    
- Bringing her gifts.
    
- Listening to stories about carrots.
    

No one seems to mind.

Least of all Belle.

---

### **Q: Is Room N-07 ever quiet?**

**A:** Rarely.

Most meetings eventually become conversations.

Most conversations become jokes.

Most jokes become complete chaos.

---

### **Q: Has OSHA inspected Room N-07?**

**A:** Yes.

The inspectors left with considerably more questions than answers.

---

### **Q: Can I challenge Belle to arm wrestling?**

**A:** You may.

Winning is another matter entirely.

---

### **Q: Can I touch Keith's shark tail?**

**A:** No.

Don't.

---

### **Q: Can I touch Cinder's shark tail?**

**A:** If Cinder says yes.

She generally doesn't mind.

Please avoid pulling on the stickers.

---

### **Q: Why does everyone say not to disarm Cinder?**

**A:** Because history has repeatedly demonstrated that removing her briefcase does not reduce the threat.

It changes the threat.

---

### **Q: What is the quickest way to convince Belle to follow you somewhere?**

**A:** The preferred method is simply asking politely.

Engineering also maintains a prototype device officially known as the **Carrot-on-a-Stick Guidance System**.

Its effectiveness has been described as "embarrassingly high."

Belle disputes this conclusion.

---

### **Q: Is there anything I should absolutely never do?**

**A:** Yes.

- Do not deliberately provoke members of the group.
    
- Do not take Belle's carrot without permission.
    
- Do not encourage Neko's questionable inventions unless you've informed Miss Neko.
    
- Do not borrow Cinder's briefcase.
    
- Do not underestimate anyone based on appearance.
    

---

### **Q: What's the best part about Room N-07?**

**A:** Ask anyone who's visited.

The answer is usually the same.

> "It feels like home."

---

**End of Q&A**
