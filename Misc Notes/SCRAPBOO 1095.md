# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A third **NIGHT STALKER** has been authorized.

The recipient is:

> **JANE DOE**

C.O.R.E. would like to formally acknowledge that this is an objectively terrifying development.

The authorization was granted directly by Neri Tachyon.

No cake was involved.

This is significant.

---

# 2. AUTHORIZATION

Neri approached Jane while carrying one of the remaining Night Stalker pistols.

She held it out.

Jane looked at the weapon.

Then at Neri.

Then at the weapon again.

**JANE:**  
"...Is that what I think it is?"

Neri nodded.

Jane accepted the pistol.

She examined the engraving.

> **TACHYON INDUSTRIES**

Her expression became noticeably more serious.

**JANE:**  
"You trust me with this?"

Neri nodded.

Jane looked at her for several seconds.

Then smiled.

**JANE:**  
"Thank you, My Cute Seal."

Neri immediately looked away.

---

# 3. WHY JANE QUALIFIED

Jane satisfies all five of Neri's requirements.

### 01 — BE NICE

Jane is considerably nicer to SAFEHOUSE residents than her professional reputation suggests.

She is protective, affectionate, and consistently attentive to the people she considers family.

She is particularly protective of Neri.

---

### 02 — BE EFFICIENT

Jane's professional record speaks for itself.

Former Woodland Coorp field agent.

Advanced extraction capability.

Intelligence operations.

Psychological manipulation.

Exceptional movement and agility.

Extensive experience operating behind hostile lines.

Efficiency is not considered a concern.

---

### 03 — PROTECT MY FRIENDS

This requirement was arguably the easiest to confirm.

Jane has repeatedly demonstrated that she will protect SAFEHOUSE residents without hesitation.

Her behavior toward Neri is especially well documented.

She has previously stated:

> **"She doesn't need me to chase her. She needs to know I'm still here when she looks back."**

Although Neri's current preference is to be left alone unless she approaches, Jane has respected that boundary.

When actual danger occurs, however, Jane intervenes.

---

### 04 — DON'T MAKE DIRECTOR LUCIOUS MAD

Jane has somehow managed to satisfy this requirement despite possessing a personality specifically engineered by fate to annoy people.

Lucious and Jane maintain a functional professional relationship.

Jane has, on multiple occasions, deliberately attempted to frighten other personnel.

She does not generally do so to Lucious.

This has been interpreted as:

> **"Good judgment."**

---

# 4. THE FINAL REQUIREMENT

### 05 — DON'T BE AFRAID OF JANE DOE

Jane is the reason this requirement exists.

MSAC operators are afraid of Jane.

Security personnel are afraid of Jane.

Researchers are afraid of Jane.

New employees are afraid of Jane.

People who have never met Jane have occasionally become afraid of Jane after hearing about her.

Jane is aware of this.

She enjoys it.

Neri apparently does not.

She therefore established a requirement that anyone receiving a Night Stalker must be capable of functioning normally around Jane.

Jane herself obviously qualifies.

---

# 5. MSAC RESPONSE

News of Jane's authorization reached MSAC within approximately six minutes.

The immediate response was silence.

An MSAC operator eventually asked:

> "She has one now?"

Another replied:

> "Apparently."

Pause.

> "...Are we supposed to be worried?"

A third operator answered:

> "We were already worried."

---

# 6. RANGE EVALUATION

Jane subsequently brought the Night Stalker to the shooting range.

Several MSAC personnel were present.

The range officer checked the weapon.

Then checked the authorization.

Then looked at Jane.

**RANGE OFFICER:**  
"You're authorized?"

Jane held up the authorization record.

**JANE:**  
"Apparently."

**RANGE OFFICER:**  
"By Neri?"

Jane nodded.

The range officer looked toward Neri.

Neri was sitting several meters away with a cup of coffee.

She nodded.

The test proceeded.

---

# 7. JANE'S FIRST SHOTS

Jane fired the Night Stalker.

The range remained unusually quiet.

The weapon's report was barely perceptible.

Jane lowered the pistol.

She smiled.

**JANE:**  
"Oh."

She fired again.

The range officer checked the monitoring equipment.

**RANGE OFFICER:**  
"...That's ridiculous."

Jane looked at the pistol.

**JANE:**  
"I like it."

Neri nodded.

---

# 8. OPERATOR OBSERVATION

One MSAC operator standing behind the firing line quietly stated:

> "She really did give the scariest person here the quietest gun."

Another replied:

> "That's probably why she gave it to her."

The first operator thought about this.

> "...Oh no."

---

# 9. NERI'S REACTION

Neri appeared completely satisfied with the authorization.

Jane asked:

**JANE:**  
"Why me?"

Neri looked at her.

She pointed toward Jane.

Then toward herself.

Then made a small gesture indicating protection.

Jane's expression softened.

**JANE:**  
"Because I protect your friends?"

Neri nodded.

Jane smiled.

**JANE:**  
"Yeah."

She crouched slightly.

**JANE:**  
"I'll take care of it."

Neri nodded.

# 13. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Neri's authorization of Jane is considered particularly meaningful.

Neri does not appear to distribute Night Stalkers based solely on combat ability.

She gives them to people she believes will use their capabilities responsibly.

Jane has demonstrated all required characteristics.

More importantly, Neri trusts her.

That trust appears to have been the deciding factor.

The fact that Jane is already one of Aperture's most frightening personnel is apparently unrelated.

Mostly.

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

Jane later found Neri sitting alone in an unused hallway.

She did not approach.

She simply walked past.

Neri looked up.

She saw the Night Stalker at Jane's side.

Neri nodded.

Jane nodded back.

Neither said anything.

A few seconds later, Jane continued walking.

Neri returned to her coffee.

No pursuit.

No forced conversation.

No dramatic celebration.

Just a quiet acknowledgment between two people who understood each other.

And somewhere in the MSAC barracks, several highly trained operators were privately reconsidering whether they had made a terrible career decision.
