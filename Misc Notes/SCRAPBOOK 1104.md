# 5. THE PROBLEM WITH NERI'S HINTS

C.O.R.E. has identified a recurring problem.

Neri's hints are technically useful.

They are also sufficiently vague to permit approximately twelve different interpretations.

The current sequence is:

> **“Think outside the box.”**

followed by:

> **“Go touch some grass.”**

The two statements may indicate:

1. the model is outside;
    
2. the model is near grass;
    
3. the model is somewhere unconventional;
    
4. the model is not inside a container;
    
5. the MSAC has spent too long indoors;
    
6. Neri thinks the MSAC has spent too long indoors;
    
7. all of the above.
