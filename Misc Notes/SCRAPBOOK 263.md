# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **MEDICAL & PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION LOG**

### **SUBJECT: DIALYN – SODIUM METABOLISM & STRESS RESPONSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Medical Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Medical examinations have identified an unusual metabolic anomaly affecting Dialyn.

She is unable to perceive the taste of salt, and her body is unable to utilize sodium through normal physiological processes.

As a result, conventional salty foods taste no different to her than their unsalted counterparts.

This has led to several unique dietary habits.

---

# **SALT PERCEPTION**

Repeated taste tests confirmed that Dialyn cannot detect saltiness.

Whether the food contains almost no sodium or a significant amount, her subjective experience remains essentially identical.

When informed that a meal had been "heavily salted," her response was simply:

> "...Really?"

She finished the meal without noticing anything unusual.

---

# **DIETARY ADAPTATION**

Because salt provides no flavor for her, Dialyn instead relies on spices—particularly chili peppers—to add intensity to food.

She has developed a preference for meals that most personnel would describe as extremely spicy.

According to Dialyn:

> "...If I can't taste salty..."

She smiled.

> "...I can at least taste spicy."

Bao has since learned to prepare a separate serving whenever Dialyn visits SAFEHOUSE.

---

# **STRESS RESPONSE**

Psychological Services have documented another unusual behavior.

During periods of significant anxiety or worry, Dialyn has occasionally been seen holding or nibbling small pieces of **rock salt**.

Laboratory testing confirmed that she is not doing so for the taste.

When asked why, Dialyn appeared genuinely uncertain.

> "...It helps."

She was unable to explain further.

Counselors believe the behavior functions as a grounding ritual rather than a nutritional one.

The familiar texture appears to provide comfort during periods of emotional strain.

Personnel are advised **not** to encourage or mock the behavior.

If observed, the recommended response is simply to check whether Dialyn is doing alright.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Dialyn continues to carry a small container of food-grade rock salt in her bag.

Most days, it remains untouched.

Those closest to her have learned that if she absent-mindedly reaches for it, she is probably carrying a worry she hasn't shared yet.

More often than not, someone quietly sits beside her.

A cup of tea appears.

Conversation is optional.

The rock salt usually goes back into the container.

And Dialyn remembers she no longer has to face everything alone.
