# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ENG-067**

### **MATERIAL TEST LOG – NEKONIUM BASEBALL BAT vs. BELLE'S HYBRID GREATSWORD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Engineering Archive

---

## **PURPOSE**

Following an informal discussion regarding the durability of various melee weapons, Endmin proposed a controlled materials test.

The objective was simple:

> **"Let's see which one gives first."

The test was approved under engineering supervision.

---

## **TEST EQUIPMENT**

### **Weapon A**

**Nekonium Baseball Bat**

Owner: Lindervene "Endmin" Merryweather

Approximate Weight:

**5 kilograms**

Construction:

- Solid Nekonium alloy
    
- Reinforced striking surface
    
- Standard grip wrap
    

---

### **Weapon B**

**Hybrid Greatsword**

Owner: Belle Merryweather

Approximate Weight:

**50 kilograms**

Construction:

- Classified hybrid alloy
    
- Custom forged
    
- Personal weapon
    

---

## **TEST CONDITIONS**

Location:

Engineering Testing Range

Personnel Present:

- Belle
    
- Endmin
    
- Neko
    
- Lucius
    
- Keith
    
- Cinder
    
- Miss Neko
    

Protective barriers were installed.

Both participants agreed to avoid striking each other directly.

Only weapon-on-weapon impacts were permitted.

---

## **TEST**

Belle planted her feet.

She held her sword horizontally.

Endmin rested the bat on her shoulder.

Neko gave the signal.

> **"...Begin."

Endmin swung first.

**CLANG!**

The impact echoed through the testing range.

Belle barely moved.

She blinked.

Endmin shook out her hands.

> **"...Okay..."

> **"...That's solid."

Inspection revealed no visible damage.

---

A second strike followed.

Then a third.

On the fourth full-power swing—

**CRACK!**

Everyone looked at Belle's sword.

A small chip had formed along one edge.

Silence filled the room.

Belle slowly looked down.

> **"...My sword..."

Endmin immediately lowered the bat.

> **"...Uh..."

> **"...Sorry?"

---

## **POST-TEST EXAMINATION**

Engineering inspection confirmed:

- The Nekonium baseball bat remained structurally intact.
    
- Belle's sword had sustained a minor edge chip.
    
- No cracks or structural weaknesses were detected elsewhere.
    

Neko concluded that the result was not due to poor craftsmanship.

Instead, the concentrated hardness of the reinforced Nekonium striking surface produced localized damage under repeated impacts.

---

## **REPAIRS**

Belle quietly handed her sword to Neko.

Neko spent the afternoon restoring the damaged edge.

In addition to repairing the chip, he reinforced the blade with an updated internal support layer to improve resistance against repeated high-energy impacts.

Upon completion, he returned the sword to Belle.

She examined the repaired edge.

Ran a finger gently along it.

Smiled.

> **"...Thank you."

Neko simply nodded.

> **"...Try not to let people hit it with baseball bats."

Belle looked at Endmin.

Endmin awkwardly smiled.

> **"...Fair."

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

---

### Cinder

Looking at the undamaged bat.

> **"...Remind me never to get hit with that."

---

### Lucius

Watching Neko repair the sword.

> **"...Well..."

> **"...At least we learned something."

---

### Miss Neko

> **"...You two are banned from testing furniture next."

Belle and Endmin answered at the same time.

> **"...We weren't planning to."

No one looked convinced.

---

## **ENGINEERING CONCLUSION**

The test demonstrated the exceptional durability of both weapons.

Although Belle's greatsword sustained a minor chip under repeated impacts, the damage was superficial and successfully repaired.

The reinforced Nekonium baseball bat also proved to be an unexpectedly formidable impact weapon despite weighing only one-tenth as much as Belle's sword.

Following reinforcement, Belle's sword was returned to active service.

A final note was added to the engineering log:

> **"Material testing complete."**

> **"Future requests involving repeated collisions between treasured personal weapons require prior approval from Engineering."**

A second note appeared underneath in different handwriting.

> **"Especially if Belle is involved."**
