### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: COREY LOCKWOOD – STABILIZING INFLUENCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical & Research Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Continued observation has identified a consistent behavioral pattern between Corin Lockwood and Corey Lockwood.

Corin has demonstrated an exceptional level of trust in Corey.

When Corey asks Corin to eat, she does.

When Corey reminds her to take medication, she does.

When Corey tells her to rest, she usually complies.

When Corey tells her not to isolate herself...

Corin listens.

Psychological Services have not observed another individual whose guidance Corin follows with comparable consistency.

---

# **UNIQUE TRUST**

Personnel initially believed Corey was simply more persuasive than other visitors.

Further observation disproved this.

Corey rarely argues.

She rarely raises her voice.

She almost never insists.

Instead, she speaks calmly.

Corin almost always agrees.

Medical staff believe this is not obedience.

It is trust.

Corin appears to believe that if Corey is asking something of her, it is because Corey genuinely believes it will help.

---

# **COREY'S EMOTIONAL PRESENTATION**

An equally unusual observation concerns Corey herself.

Since her first documented appearance, Corey has never been observed in a prolonged state of sadness.

She experiences ordinary emotions.

She laughs.

She becomes frustrated.

She worries.

She is occasionally irritated—most often with Corin for forgetting meals, medication, or working too long.

However, sustained despair comparable to Corin's has never been documented.

Psychological Services caution against interpreting this as the absence of emotional depth.

Corey does experience concern and grief.

She simply does not remain trapped in them.

---

# **WORKING THEORY**

Researchers have proposed that Corey naturally approaches hardship with a restorative outlook.

Where Corin's first instinct is often to withdraw,

Corey's first instinct is to move toward the problem.

When Corin says:

> "...I'm fine."

Corey usually replies:

> "No, you're tired."

When Corin says:

> "...Leave me alone."

Corey often answers:

> "I'll sit outside until you're ready."

This pattern has been repeated dozens of times.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Medical Services continue to regard Corey as Corin's strongest protective factor.

Her presence does not eliminate Corin's struggles.

It does not erase nightmares or painful memories.

What it consistently does is interrupt the cycle before it deepens into complete isolation.

Researchers have also noted that Corey herself remains remarkably emotionally resilient.

Whether this resilience is a property of the anomalous phenomenon that created her or simply part of who Corey is remains unknown.

One conclusion, however, is shared by nearly everyone who knows them:

If Corin is the one who carries the weight...

Corey is the one who refuses to let her carry it alone.
