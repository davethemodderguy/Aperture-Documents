## Executive Summary

Neri has stopped voluntarily moving.

This is not the usual behavior associated with Plushie Neri.

Previously, Neri could crawl, roll, scoot, climb onto furniture, follow people, seek quiet locations, leave uncomfortable situations, and move herself whenever she wanted.

She no longer does any of these things.

If someone picks her up, she allows it.

If someone carries her somewhere, she allows it.

If someone puts her down somewhere, she remains there.

If the person leaves, Neri does not follow.

If she is placed somewhere inconvenient, she does not relocate herself.

If food or drink is placed beside her, she does not reach for it.

As of the latest observation, **Neri has consumed no food or drinks since the outdoor incident.**

The behavioral change is now considered a serious welfare emergency.

---

## Current Behavior

Neri has effectively stopped making voluntary decisions regarding her own physical position.

Her current pattern can be summarized as:

> **Pick her up.**  
> **She allows it.**  
> **Put her down.**  
> **She stays.**

There is no resistance.

There is no protest.

There is no attempt to return somewhere she prefers.

There is no attempt to seek food.

There is no attempt to seek water.

There is no attempt to find Bao.

There is no attempt to find Alice.

There is no attempt to find Jane.

She simply remains wherever someone last placed her.

## Sustenance

The absence of eating and drinking is particularly concerning.

Neri has previously been capable of eating normally while in Plushie Form.

Her plush physiology does not remove the need for sustenance.

She has nevertheless made no apparent attempt to obtain food or drink since the outdoor incident.

Personnel have offered:

- water;
    
- coffee;
    
- milk;
    
- cake;
    
- sandwiches;
    
- fruit;
    
- other cafeteria food;
    
- sweets.
    

Neri has not reached for any of them.

She has not verbally refused them.

She simply does nothing.

## Interaction With Personnel

The most concerning characteristic is that Neri does not appear to care where she is placed.

She can be moved from:

**SAFEHOUSE → cafeteria → bedroom → medical area → hallway → lounge**

and will remain wherever she is deposited.

Personnel initially interpreted this as cooperation.

It is not.

Neri is no longer cooperating with people.

She is **surrendering agency to them.**

## C.O.R.E. Medical Assessment

This behavior cannot be treated as ordinary plushie lethargy.

Neri's existing medical condition makes prolonged refusal or failure to eat and drink particularly dangerous.

Her blood instability, cardiovascular fragility, and history of severe anemic episodes mean that a period of inadequate intake can compound an already unstable condition.

**Medical personnel have therefore classified this as an active welfare emergency.**

This does not mean Neri should be forced to eat.

It means her condition requires immediate professional assessment.

## Welfare Directive

Personnel are instructed **not** to treat this as an opportunity to carry Neri everywhere.

Do not turn her passivity into convenience.

Do not repeatedly relocate her because she does not object.

Do not interpret silence as preference.

Do not assume:

> "She didn't say no."

means:

> "She wants this."

At the same time, personnel should not attempt to force ordinary independence back onto her through confrontation.

The immediate priority is **medical stabilization and restoration of basic voluntary engagement**, not making Neri walk.

---

## Critical Distinction

Neri's established preference for solitude remains valid.

She is allowed to be alone.

She is allowed to sit quietly.

She is allowed to spend hours doing nothing.

She is allowed to become Plushie Neri.

She is allowed to be carried when she wants to be carried.

What has changed is that she is no longer **choosing** these things.

She is allowing other people to choose everything for her.

That is the problem.

The outdoor incident appears to have fundamentally altered Neri's relationship with her own existence.

She is no longer merely thinking:

> **"I'm a liability."**

> **"A liability doesn't get to decide where it goes."**
