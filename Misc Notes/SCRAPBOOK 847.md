# 2. EARLY CHILDHOOD

Neri was raised in an abusive household.

Available testimony indicates that her family operated under an extremely transactional worldview:

> **If a person was not considered worth the effort, they were discarded.**

Affection was conditional.

Attention was conditional.

Even basic familial acceptance appears to have been conditional.

Neri described her childhood as being dominated by:

- Insults
    
- Bullying
    
- Neglect
    
- Constant criticism
    
- Social isolation
    
- Emotional abuse
    
- Fear of becoming “useless”
    

The concept of being useful became deeply embedded in Neri's understanding of her own worth.

If she could not provide value, she believed she would be discarded.

Eventually, that belief became reality.

---

# 3. CHEMISTRY

One of the few subjects Neri demonstrated genuine aptitude for was **chemistry**.

She learned rapidly.

She remembered compounds unusually well.

She could understand chemical relationships far beyond what would normally be expected of a child.

However, her parents considered the subject unnecessary.

To them, chemistry did not provide sufficient immediate value.

Neri's own description was remarkably simple:

> **“They said it was useless.”**

The subject that later became one of the defining features of Neri's intellect was therefore treated as another reason she was not worth keeping.

Her parents eventually abandoned her.

Literally.

Neri was thrown out onto the street.

---

# 4. LOSS OF FRIENDS

Neri subsequently formed friendships with other people who were similarly isolated.

These friendships appear to have been extremely important to her.

For perhaps the first time, Neri had people who:

- Wanted her around.
    
- Listened to her.
    
- Accepted her interests.
    
- Did not require her to prove her usefulness.
    
- Treated her as a person rather than an asset.
    

However, Neri's history with friendship became catastrophic.

Several of the people she befriended were already suffering from severe emotional distress.

Within months of becoming friends with Neri, multiple individuals died following self-harm.

The losses accumulated.

Again.

And again.

And again.

Neri began developing an increasingly severe fear of losing people she cared about.

---

# 5. PSYCHOLOGICAL CONSEQUENCE

C.O.R.E. assessment indicates that Neri's current protective behavior is not simply an unusual personality trait.

It is likely the product of repeated abandonment and bereavement.

Neri appears unable to emotionally tolerate another significant loss.

Her mind has effectively adapted around one central principle:

> **“If they're my friend, I have to keep them safe.”**

This principle has become extreme.

A normal protective response can become aggression.

Aggression can become retaliation.

Retaliation can become disproportionate violence.

The more important the person is to Neri, the less willing she becomes to accept the possibility of losing them.

This provides additional context for her previously documented behavioral pattern:

> **Friend threatened → intervene.**  
> **Friend injured → extreme retaliation.**  
> **Friend killed → catastrophic response.**

This is not presently understood as simple bloodlust.

It is fear.

Fear reinforced by years of evidence that people she loves can disappear.

---

# 6. THE SPAWN EXTREMIST

Neri eventually reached what records describe as one of the lowest points in her life.

She was vulnerable.

Alone.

Without meaningful protection.

That was when she was captured by a **deranged SPAWN extremist cultist**.

Important distinction:

> **The individual was acting independently and was not representative of modern SPAWN leadership.**

The extremist imprisoned Neri in a secret facility.

She was kept inside what appeared to be a modified jail cell.

The room contained little more than basic containment infrastructure and a small water tub.

Neri was subsequently subjected to experimentation.

---

# 7. ARTIFICIAL THIRENIZATION

The experiments transformed Neri's body.

The result was a **Seal Thiren**.

Blacksite records currently classify Neri as the **first confirmed artificially created Thiren in Blacksite history**.

Prior Thiren records describe naturally occurring Thiren populations and individuals.

Neri's case is different.

Her transformation was artificially induced.

The precise mechanism remains under investigation.

The original experimental documentation is either missing, destroyed, or still held by the extremist's former network.

C.O.R.E. has intentionally avoided attempting to recreate the process.

---

# 8. CHEMISTRY RECONNECTION

There is an uncomfortable irony surrounding the transformation.

The chemistry that Neri's parents considered worthless became the field she used to understand what had happened to her.

During her time in captivity, Neri demonstrated an unusually sophisticated understanding of the compounds and processes involved in her experimentation.

She remembered chemical structures.

She recognized unfamiliar substances.

She understood reactions.

When questioned about where she learned this, she reportedly answered:

> **“Chemistry.”**

When asked why she knew so much:

> **“Because that's what I had.”**

No further explanation was provided.

The interviewer did not press.

---

# 9. CAPTIVITY CONDITIONS

Neri remained imprisoned until Aperture discovered the facility.

The original containment chamber was later documented as being structurally consistent with a jail cell rather than a laboratory containment room.

The presence of the water tub is considered particularly significant.

Following rescue, Neri initially spent considerable time around water.

However, she eventually refused to use the original tub after realizing that it reminded her of captivity.

The tub was removed.

Neri was given access to proper SAFEHOUSE facilities instead.

Her adjustment improved considerably.

---

# 10. RESCUE

Aperture personnel recovered Neri from the facility and removed her from the extremist's custody.

She was subsequently brought to SAFEHOUSE.

Initial behavioral observations showed:

- Extreme caution
    
- Strong attachment behavior
    
- Distrust of confinement
    
- Strong aversion to closed spaces
    
- Immediate interest in water
    
- Immediate interest in food
    
- Unusually rapid attachment to kind individuals
    

Her relationship with Bao developed particularly quickly.

The two being Whale Thiren and Seal Thiren respectively appears to have contributed to Neri feeling understood and safe.

Neri subsequently began referring to Bao as:

> **“Mom.”**

---

# 11. CURRENT BEHAVIOR

Neri is currently substantially more stable than she was immediately following rescue.

However, the underlying damage has not disappeared.

Her strongest emotional vulnerability remains **loss**.

She forms attachments rapidly.

She protects those attachments intensely.

She reacts poorly to threats against them.

This explains why seemingly minor incidents can become serious when Neri believes somebody intends to harm one of her friends.

It also explains why she can be remarkably affectionate one moment and terrifying the next.

Neri is not searching for people to hurt.

She is searching for people she can keep.
