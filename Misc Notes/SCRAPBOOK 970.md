# SAFEHOUSE CULINARY INCIDENT REPORT

## JANE DOE ATTEMPTS TO COOK

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE Kitchen  
**Participants:** Jane Doe, Curious Light  
**Secondary Participant:** █████████████  
**Result:** Unexpectedly Excellent

---

## 1. Background

Jane Doe decided that she should learn how to cook.

No particular reason was given.

She simply entered the kitchen one afternoon and announced:

> **Jane:** “I'm gonna cook.”

Several residents immediately became concerned.

Not because Jane was dangerous.

Because Jane had apparently never developed the necessary skill set.

---

## 2. Attempt

Jane initially intended to prepare an entire meal.

She gathered ingredients.

She stared at them.

She reconsidered.

She attempted to determine what several of the ingredients were actually for.

She then abandoned the idea of making an entire meal.

Instead, Jane noticed Curious Light was preparing to grill.

This produced what Jane later described as:

> **“An easier idea.”**

She decided to make a cheese sauce to accompany Curious Light's grilled food.

---

## 3. The Sauce

Jane's cheese sauce was simple.

Very simple.

Suspiciously simple.

She did not produce a complicated restaurant-style recipe.

She did not create an elaborate reduction.

She did not attempt anything particularly ambitious.

She just made:

**cheese sauce.**

And somehow—

**it was really good.**

Curious Light tasted it.

Paused.

Looked at Jane.

Tasted it again.

> **Curious Light:** “...Jane.”

> **Jane:** “What?”

> **Curious Light:** “This is actually excellent.”

Jane stared at the sauce.

> **Jane:** “Wait, seriously?”

> **Curious Light:** “Seriously.”

Jane immediately became significantly more confident.

> **Jane:** “I CAN COOK.”

> **Curious Light:** “You made one sauce.”

> **Jane:** “I CAN COOK.”

---

## 4. Integration With Grilled Food

The cheese sauce was served alongside Curious Light's grilled food.

The combination was unexpectedly excellent.

The grilled food provided the main flavor.

Jane's sauce added richness and complemented it without overpowering the meal.

Multiple SAFEHOUSE residents who sampled it independently reached essentially the same conclusion:

**Jane had somehow succeeded.**

This was particularly impressive given the preceding cooking attempt.

---

## 5. Culinary Assessment

### Jane Doe

**Cooking Skill:** Questionable  
**Sauce Skill:** Surprisingly high  
**General Meal Preparation:** Unconfirmed  
**Confidence after one successful dish:** Excessive

The kitchen staff have therefore declined to classify Jane as a competent cook.

She is instead classified as:

> **“A person who can apparently make one really good cheese sauce.”**

---

## 6. Jane's Conclusion

Jane disagreed with the assessment.

She reportedly spent the remainder of the evening telling people:

> **“I made the sauce.”**

When informed that Curious Light had done most of the actual cooking, Jane responded:

> **“Yeah, but the cheese part was mine.”**

Curious Light reportedly nodded.

> **“That's fair.”**

Jane then asked whether she could make it again tomorrow.

Curious Light agreed.

---

## 7. Follow-Up

The SAFEHOUSE kitchen has subsequently developed an informal division of labor:

**Curious Light:** Grill the food.

**Jane Doe:** Make the cheese sauce.

This arrangement has produced consistently good results.

It has also prevented Jane from attempting to prepare an entire meal until her abilities have been properly assessed.

Neko reportedly summarized the situation as:

> **“She failed at cooking and accidentally specialized.”**

Jane disagreed.

> **“I didn't fail.”**

> **“I discovered my specialty.”**

No further argument was made.

The cheese sauce was, in fact, excellent.

**END REPORT**
