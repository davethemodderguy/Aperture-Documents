# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **ANOMALOUS ENTITY INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: LUMIA ("LUMI") LOCKWOOD – TEMPORARY ETHER MANIFESTATION FAILURE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Executive & Research Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The anomalous manifestation known as **Lumia ("Lumi") Lockwood** experienced an unexpected instability resulting in the temporary loss of her physical manifestation.

Research Division attributes the event to complications involving the interaction between Ghost and Ether, the anomalous state that permits Lumi to possess physical form, color, and tactile interaction.

Current evidence indicates that the manifestation is **not permanently lost**.

However, restoration requires time and cannot be forced without risking complete destabilization.

---

# **COMMUNICATION FAILURE**

Although Engineering and Research became aware that Lumi's manifestation would fail temporarily, the information was not successfully communicated to every affected individual.

Most critically...

No one informed Corin Lockwood.

The omission was later classified as a major communication failure.

---

# **INCIDENT**

Corin followed her usual routine that evening.

She carried two mugs of tea to the tree where Lumi normally visited.

She set one mug beside her own.

Then she waited.

Five minutes.

Ten minutes.

Half an hour.

Lumi never appeared.

Corin quietly looked around the clearing.

> "...Mom?"

Only the wind answered.

Assuming Lumi had simply been delayed, Corin returned the following evening.

Again...

No one came.

---

# **THE THIRD DAY**

On the third day, Corin finally searched the surrounding forest.

Every place Lumi normally appeared...

Was empty.

Corin's pace became increasingly frantic.

She searched the gardens.

The lake.

SAFEHOUSE.

The library.

The old tree.

The walking paths.

Nothing.

Late that evening, Security cameras recorded Corin standing alone in the snowfall.

She whispered only one sentence.

> "...Not again..."

---

# **DISCOVERY**

The following morning, Corin entered Medical Services without an appointment.

This was highly unusual.

She asked only one question.

> "...Where's my mom?"

The attending physician paused.

The room fell silent.

It became immediately apparent that Corin had never been informed.

---

# **EXPLANATION**

Research staff explained the situation as quickly and carefully as possible.

Lumi had not abandoned her.

She had not disappeared forever.

The manifestation itself had become unstable.

For safety, it had collapsed temporarily while corrective procedures were conducted.

Researchers expected Lumi to return once stabilization was complete.

Corin listened without interrupting.

When the explanation ended, she quietly nodded.

> "...Someone..."

A long silence followed.

> "...Should've told me."

She stood.

Thanked the staff.

Then quietly walked out.

---

# **PERSONNEL REVIEW**

An internal review concluded that the communication failure caused unnecessary emotional distress.

Personnel acknowledged that, given Corin's history of abandonment and loss, she should have been informed immediately.

New procedures now require direct notification of immediate family members whenever anomalous manifestations affecting close relatives become unstable.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Lumi's disappearance is considered temporary.

Research continues efforts to restore her manifestation safely.

Corin has resumed placing two mugs beside the tree each evening.

One for herself.

One for her mother.

The second mug remains untouched.

Corin continues bringing it anyway.

**End of Anomalous Entity Incident Report**
