# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nora Tachyon has experienced recurring nightmares since her arrival at SAFEHOUSE.

The content appears to involve fragmented memories surrounding her original death and subsequent anomalous displacement.

Nora does not provide a consistent description of the dreams.

Her statements generally involve:

- cold;
    
- falling;
    
- darkness;
    
- being unable to find Neri;
    
- being alone;
    
- the sensation of being dropped;
    
- and, occasionally, a blue light or moon.
    

Nora's nightmares are particularly significant because she spent years believing that she had died.

She has now returned to a world where her older sister is alive.

The resulting emotional contradiction appears to be producing nighttime distress.

There was, however, an unexpected development during the third documented episode.

Nora discovered something about her sister that she had never previously known.

Neri can become a plushie.

---

# 1. FIRST NIGHTMARE

At approximately 02:13, monitoring personnel detected movement inside Neri's subspace.

Nora was asleep on one of the smaller floating islands.

She began moving restlessly.

Her breathing became irregular.

She eventually sat upright.

> **NORA:**  
> “Big sis?”

Neri woke immediately.

> **NERI:**  
> “Hm?”

Nora looked around.

She appeared disoriented.

> **NORA:**  
> “Where am I?”

Neri sat up.

> **NERI:**  
> “My room.”

Nora looked around.

The artificial stars were still overhead.

The floating islands were still present.

The bridges had not moved.

Neri was still there.

Nora slowly relaxed.

Then she climbed onto Neri's bed.

Neri allowed it.

---

# 2. THE NIGHTMARE

When asked the following morning what she had dreamed about, Nora provided a fragmented explanation.

> **NORA:**  
> “I was falling.”

Neri listened.

> **NORA:**  
> “It was cold.”

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> “I couldn't see you.”

Another pause.

> **NORA:**  
> “I kept calling you.”

Neri's expression changed.

> **NORA:**  
> “You didn't answer.”

Neri did not respond.

Nora continued:

> **NORA:**  
> “Then there was the blue moon.”

> **NERI:**  
> “The one you told us about?”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “It was talking.”

> **NERI:**  
> “What did it say?”

Nora shook her head.

> **NORA:**  
> “I don't remember.”

---

# 3. THE SECOND NIGHT

The second documented nightmare occurred the following night.

This time, Nora woke crying.

Neri was already awake.

She had apparently noticed Nora's movement.

Nora immediately grabbed Neri.

> **NORA:**  
> “Don't disappear.”

Neri froze.

She placed one hand on Nora's head.

> **NERI:**  
> “I'm here.”

> **NORA:**  
> “Promise?”

Neri paused.

Then:

> **NERI:**  
> “Promise.”

Nora remained beside her until she fell asleep again.

Neri did not return to sleep immediately.

She stayed awake until Nora's breathing stabilized.

---

# 4. NERI'S SOLUTION

Neri appears to have recognized that Nora was having difficulty sleeping alone.

She therefore changed her own sleeping arrangement.

Instead of remaining on the primary island, Neri began sleeping closer to Nora's island.

No formal discussion occurred.

She simply moved her bedding.

Nora approved immediately.

> **NORA:**  
> “You're sleeping here now?”

> **NERI:**  
> “For now.”

> **NORA:**  
> “Good.”

Neri:

> **“Go to sleep.”**

Nora:

> **“Okay.”**

Approximately thirty seconds later:

> **NORA:**  
> “Big sis?”

> **NERI:**  
> “What?”

> **NORA:**  
> “Your room is really cool.”

Neri:

> **“Sleep.”**

---

# 5. THE THIRD NIGHT

The third incident began differently.

Nora woke suddenly.

She looked toward Neri.

Neri was asleep.

Nora quietly climbed off her bed.

She approached.

> **NORA:**  
> “Big sis?”

No response.

Nora gently touched Neri's shoulder.

Neri did not wake.

Nora became worried.

> **NORA:**  
> “Big sis?”

Still nothing.

Nora began shaking her slightly.

Neri opened one eye.

> **NERI:**  
> “What?”

Nora jumped.

> **NORA:**  
> “You're awake!”

Neri blinked.

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

Nora hugged her.

Neri sighed.

> **NERI:**  
> “Nightmare?”

Nora nodded.

> **NORA:**  
> “I don't want to sleep.”

Neri thought for a moment.

Then she did something Nora had never seen before.

---

# 6. TRANSFORMATION

Neri's form began changing.

There was no dramatic effect.

No flash.

No rift.

No visible machinery.

Her body simply compressed and softened.

Her clothing transformed with her.

Within seconds, Neri had become:

> **A small plush version of herself.**

Long white hair.

Round glasses.

Oversized hoodie.

Seal tail.

Tiny proportions.

Soft fabric body.

Nora stared.

Neri looked up at her.

> **NERI:**  
> “...What?”

Nora's mouth was open.

She pointed.

> **NORA:**  
> “Big sis.”

Neri blinked.

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

> **NORA:**  
> “You're a toy.”

Neri immediately shook her head.

> **NERI:**  
> “No.”

> **NORA:**  
> “You're tiny.”

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

> **NORA:**  
> “You're soft.”

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

Nora carefully picked her up.

Neri allowed it.

Nora stared at her.

> **NORA:**  
> “You can do this?”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Sometimes.”

> **NORA:**  
> “How long?”

Neri shrugged.

> **NERI:**  
> “As long as I want.”

Nora's eyes widened.

---

# 7. IMMEDIATE EXCITEMENT

Nora's nightmare was forgotten.

Completely.

> **NORA:**  
> “YOU CAN BE A PLUSHIE?”

Neri:

> **“Quiet.”**

> **NORA:**  
> “YOU'RE A PLUSHIE!”

Neri covered her own face with her tiny hoodie sleeves.

> **NERI:**  
> “Please.”

Nora gently hugged her.

> **NORA:**  
> “You're so soft!”

Neri:

> **“I know.”**

Nora turned her around.

Looked at the seal tail.

Looked at the glasses.

Looked at the hoodie.

> **NORA:**  
> “You look like you.”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “That's the point.”

---

# 8. THE SHARK AND THE SEAL

Nora examined the seal tail.

She looked at her own shark tail.

Then Neri's.

Then her own.

Then Neri's again.

> **NORA:**  
> “I'm a shark.”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

> **NORA:**  
> “You're a seal.”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

Nora smiled.

> **NORA:**  
> “Shark and seal sisters.”

Neri looked away.

> **NERI:**  
> “Apparently.”

Nora then gently poked the plush tail.

Neri stared at her.

> **NERI:**  
> “Don't bite it.”

Nora:

> **“I wasn't going to.”**

Pause.

> **NORA:**  
> “Yet.”

Neri sighed.

---

# 9. THE NIGHTMARE DOES NOT RETURN

Nora eventually curled up with Plush Neri beside her.

Neri's plush form was considerably softer than her normal body.

Nora held her carefully.

> **NORA:**  
> “You're staying?”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “Yes.”

> **NORA:**  
> “Promise?”

> **NERI:**  
> “Promise.”

Nora closed her eyes.

She fell asleep within several minutes.

Neri remained beside her.

No further distress was recorded that night.

---

# 10. MORNING

Nora woke before Neri.

She looked down.

Neri was still plush.

Nora stared.

Then whispered:

> **NORA:**  
> “Cool.”

Neri opened one eye.

> **NERI:**  
> “Morning.”

Nora immediately hugged her.

> **NORA:**  
> “Morning, plushie.”

Neri's eyes narrowed.

> **NERI:**  
> “Don't call me that.”

Nora grinned.

> **NORA:**  
> “Okay, Big Sis Plushie.”

Neri:

> **“Nora.”**

Nora laughed.

---

# 11. THE NEW INFORMATION SPREADS

Nora subsequently discovered that several SAFEHOUSE residents were already familiar with Neri's plush form.

She was deeply offended that nobody had told her.

> **NORA:**  
> “YOU ALL KNEW?”

Alice:

> **“Yes.”**

Frost:

> **“Yes.”**

Jane:

> **“Yes.”**

Bao:

> **“Yes.”**

Nora looked at Neri.

> **NORA:**  
> “Why didn't you tell me?”

Neri shrugged.

> **NERI:**  
> “You didn't ask.”

Nora considered this.

> **NORA:**  
> “That's fair.”

She then picked up Plush Neri.

Neri did not resist.

---

# 12. NORA'S NEW FAVORITE ACTIVITY

Nora now regularly asks:

> **“Can you become plushie?”**

Neri's answer varies.

Sometimes:

> **“No.”**

Sometimes:

> **“Later.”**

Sometimes:

> **“Okay.”**

When Neri agrees, Nora generally:

- carries her;
    
- sits with her;
    
- places her beside her while reading;
    
- gently hugs her;
    
- carries her across the floating bridges;
    
- introduces her to imaginary guests;
    
- makes her attend imaginary tea parties;
    
- and occasionally forgets that Neri can speak.
    

Neri tolerates this.

She appears to enjoy it.

---

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Nora's nightmares remain an active welfare concern.

Her memories of her original death are incomplete.

The blue portal and “blue moon” remain unexplained.

Further investigation is ongoing.

However, the discovery of Neri's plush form has unexpectedly provided Nora with a new source of comfort.

Importantly, Neri herself initiated the transformation.

She was not transformed by another resident.

She was not treated as an object.

She chose to become softer, smaller, and easier for her little sister to hold.

Nora, meanwhile, appears to have accepted the transformation immediately.

Her current understanding is extremely simple:

> **Big Sis is a seal.**

> **Big Sis can become a plushie.**

> **Big Sis is still Big Sis.**

And perhaps most importantly:

> **Big Sis stays when Nora is scared.**

The following morning she spent twenty minutes loudly explaining to everyone that:

> **“MY BIG SIS CAN TURN INTO A PLUSHIE.”**
