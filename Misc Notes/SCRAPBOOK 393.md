# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / ANOMALOUS PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

# **COREY — SKETCHBOOK DEGRADATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Hazard  
**Subject:** Corey  
**Associated Forms:** Luna / Solaria  
**Status:** **SEVERE CONCERN**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has documented a significant change in Corey's personal sketchbook.

The sketchbook was previously filled with:

- Hopeful drawings.
    
- Landscapes.
    
- Friends.
    
- Marts.
    
- Food.
    
- Flowers.
    
- SAFEHOUSE.
    
- Light.
    
- Stars.
    
- Small personal illustrations.
    

The newer pages are markedly different.

They are increasingly filled with disturbing imagery, fragmented scenes, and drawings produced during or immediately after nightmares.

The change appears to correlate strongly with Corey's deteriorating emotional condition.

---

# **THE SKETCHBOOK**

Corey does not appear to draw these images for artistic experimentation.

She draws them because she cannot sleep.

When awakened from a nightmare, she frequently reaches for the sketchbook rather than attempting to return to sleep.

Some pages contain only a single drawing.

Others contain several images covering the entire page.

Many have no captions.

Some contain sentences written in Corey's handwriting.

---

# **THE DRAWINGS**

Personnel who have viewed the newer sketches consistently report an unusual sensation.

The drawings themselves are not anomalous objects.

Nevertheless, looking at them can produce:

- Unease.
    
- Dread.
    
- Fear.
    
- A feeling of being watched.
    
- An uncomfortable sense of recognition.
    
- The impression that something is wrong despite being unable to identify what.
    

One researcher described the experience as:

> "Like remembering a nightmare that wasn't mine."

Another wrote:

> "I knew it was only pencil on paper. My instincts didn't."

---

# **NO KNOWN ANOMALOUS EFFECT**

C.O.R.E. has found no evidence that Corey intentionally imbued the drawings with an anomalous effect.

The fear appears to originate from the imagery itself.

The drawings communicate something deeply personal.

They are not merely frightening.

They are **desperate**.

---

# **NIGHTMARE DRAWINGS**

Several pages appear to have been created immediately after Corey woke from nightmares.

The drawings are often rougher than her normal work.

Lines are repeatedly crossed out.

Figures are distorted.

Some scenes contain empty spaces where a person should be.

Others depict Corey standing somewhere she does not recognize.

One recurring motif is:

> **A person disappearing into white space.**

---

# **THE WHITE VOID**

The motif appears connected to Corey's previously documented nightmare.

A small island.

A white void.

Her parents.

Running.

Falling.

Waking.

The image has appeared repeatedly in different forms.

Sometimes Corey is alone.

Sometimes there is another figure beside her.

Sometimes the island is completely empty.

---

# **THE OTHER COREY**

Another recurring theme is the presence of two figures occupying the same space.

One is frequently drawn with solar imagery.

The other with lunar imagery.

Between them is Corey.

In several sketches, the central figure is barely visible.

In one particularly disturbing drawing, the center figure has been almost completely erased.

---

# **COREY'S QUESTION**

One page contains only a single sentence:

> **"I died. Why am I still here?"**

Another:

> **"This isn't my life."**

Another:

> **"Who am I supposed to be?"**

And, on a later page:

> **"Why do I have to keep going?"**

C.O.R.E. considers these writings significant indicators of Corey's current psychological state.

---

# **THE ALTERNATE UNIVERSE**

Corey's identity conflict appears connected to the circumstances surrounding her existence.

Corey remembers dying.

Yet she continues to exist.

She now shares a life with an alternate version of herself originating from another universe.

The existence of Luna and Solaria has further complicated her understanding of identity.

She is alive.

But she remembers being dead.

She is herself.

But she shares one existence with alternate expressions of another version of herself.

The contradiction has become increasingly difficult for her to reconcile.

---

# **LUNA — STATEMENT**

Luna cannot communicate directly with Solaria.

Although both are manifestations of Corey, they cannot speak to one another as independent people.

They can only communicate with **other individuals**.

When Luna manifests, however, she frequently speaks about Corey.

> "...She's hurting."

> "...She remembers dying."

> "...She doesn't understand why she came back."

Luna pauses before continuing:

> "...And she thinks she shouldn't have."

---

# **SOLARIA — STATEMENT**

Solaria has independently expressed similar concerns.

> "...Corey keeps asking why she's here."

She looked toward the floor.

> "...I don't have an answer."

Another pause.

> "...Neither does Luna."

---

# **IMPORTANT IDENTITY NOTE**

Luna and Solaria are not two independent people capable of having conversations with each other.

They are different forms of one person.

When Luna is present, Luna can speak to others about Solaria and Corey.

When Solaria is present, Solaria can speak to others about Luna and Corey.

But:

> **Luna cannot speak directly to Solaria.**

> **Solaria cannot speak directly to Luna.**

Their communication is mediated through their shared identity and memory.

---

# **THE TWO FORMS**

Despite this limitation, both forms have independently reached the same conclusion:

> **Corey is suffering.**

Neither wants to replace her.

Neither considers herself superior to her.

Both understand that their existence is partly responsible for making Corey's identity more complicated.

And both are increasingly afraid that Corey may eventually stop wanting to return.

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts remain absent whenever Corey is in her normal state.

They still avoid her for their own survival.

However, when Luna or Solaria manifests, the colony returns.

This creates an emotionally difficult situation.

Corey sees the Marts happily approaching her alternate forms.

She knows why they avoid her.

She understands.

But she still misses them.

---

# **ONE PAGE**

A particularly concerning page contains three drawings.

The first is Corey surrounded by Marts.

The second is Luna surrounded by Marts.

The third is Solaria surrounded by Marts.

The final image is almost entirely blank.

In the corner, Corey wrote:

> **"Maybe they would be happier without me."**

The page has not been removed.

It has been flagged for welfare review.

---

# **LUNA'S REACTION**

When shown a reproduction of the page, Luna became silent.

After several seconds:

> "...That's not true."

The researcher asked whether she believed Corey would accept that.

Luna shook her head.

> "...No."

Then:

> "...But I believe it."

---

# **SOLARIA'S REACTION**

Solaria was shown the same page.

She stared at it for a long time.

> "...She thinks disappearing would solve everything."

The researcher asked what she thought.

Solaria answered:

> "...It wouldn't."

---

# **THE SKETCHBOOK'S ORIGINAL PURPOSE**

The sketchbook once represented something completely different.

It was where Corey recorded things she loved.

It contained proof that she could still find beauty in ordinary things.

Now it has become a place where she records things she cannot say aloud.

The sketchbook is no longer simply an art book.

It is becoming a record of her nightmares.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Corey's psychological condition:** Severe concern.

**Sleep:** Significantly disrupted.

**Social withdrawal:** Increasing.

**Self-perception:** Severely distorted.

**Mart interaction:** Currently avoided due to colony self-preservation.

**Luna:** Concerned and actively attempting to support Corey.

**Solaria:** Concerned and attempting to support Corey.

**Immediate anomalous threat:** Low.

**Immediate welfare concern:** **High.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The first pages of Corey's sketchbook contain sunlight.

Flowers.

Friends.

Marts wearing tiny hats.

People sitting together.

A home.

The later pages contain darkness.

Empty spaces.

Nightmares.

A figure disappearing.

And one question that keeps returning:

> **"I died. Why am I here?"**

Luna and Solaria cannot answer each other.

They cannot stand together and discuss what to do.

They can only speak when one of them is present.

So they speak to everyone else.

To Lumi.

To Corin.

To the SAFEHOUSE residents.

To anyone who will listen.

And they keep saying the same thing:

> **"Please don't let Corey disappear."**

**End of Personnel Welfare / Psychological Observation**
