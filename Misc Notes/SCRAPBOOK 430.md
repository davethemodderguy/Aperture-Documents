# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / CONTAINMENT INCIDENT REPORT**

# **CORIN — STABLE STATE COLLAPSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Critical Psychological / Containment  
**Subjects:** Corin / Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Lumi / Arcturus / Norma / SAFEHOUSE Residents / C.O.R.E. Security  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE / C.O.R.E. Containment Wing  
**Status:** **CONTAINED — CRITICAL**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's psychological condition reached a critical point after she overheard Orphie discussing her privately.

Orphie was not speaking to Corin.

She was speaking about her.

The statements were significantly harsher than anything Orphie had previously said directly to Corin.

Corin heard them.

The effect was catastrophic.

---

# **ORPHIE'S CONVERSATION**

Orphie was speaking with another SAFEHOUSE resident.

Corin was not supposed to be within hearing range.

She remained nearby without being noticed.

Orphie spoke openly.

> "...I hate her."

A pause.

> "...I don't just dislike what she's become."

> "...I deeply hate her."

> "...I can't stand being around her anymore."

> "...Every time I look at her, I remember everything that happened."

> "...I wish she'd stop making herself my problem."

She continued.

> "...I don't care what happens to her anymore."

Corin heard every word.

---

# **CORIN**

She did not interrupt.

She did not confront Orphie.

She simply stood there.

Her expression gradually emptied.

The conversation continued behind her.

Corin eventually walked away.

Nobody noticed.

---

# **THE INTERNAL RESPONSE**

For several minutes, Corin remained completely silent.

Inside her mind, the two opposing states reacted.

### **INSANE CORIN**

> **"She really hates you."**

### **SANE CORIN**

> **"...Maybe she didn't mean it."**

> **"Maybe she's angry."**

> **"Maybe—"**

The other voice interrupted.

> **"You heard her."**

Silence.

> **"She hates you."**

The stable side attempted to resist.

> **"She's still my sister."**

The response:

> **"She said she doesn't care."**

A pause.

> **"She said she wishes you weren't her problem."**

The stable side became quieter.

---

# **THE FINAL RESISTANCE**

Corin sat alone.

She tried to remember everything that contradicted Orphie's words.

Orphie coming home.

Orphie asking whether she was alive.

Orphie allowing her to hug her.

Her family.

The SAFEHOUSE.

The Marts.

Everything.

But every memory was overwhelmed by the same sentence:

> **"I deeply hate her."**

The stable side eventually stopped fighting.

Not violently.

Not dramatically.

It simply...

**gave in.**

---

# **CORIN'S COLLAPSE**

Corin's behavior changed almost immediately.

Her remaining emotional regulation disappeared.

She began laughing.

Then crying.

Then laughing again.

She spoke to people who were not there.

She became increasingly disoriented.

She no longer responded consistently to her name.

When Norma approached her:

> "...Corin?"

Corin looked at her.

> "...Which one?"

Norma froze.

---

# **CONTAINMENT**

C.O.R.E. personnel determined that Corin could no longer safely remain in the treehouse or SAFEHOUSE residential area.

The decision was not punitive.

It was made for the protection of:

- Corin.
    
- Orphie.
    
- SAFEHOUSE residents.
    
- Medical personnel.
    
- Corin herself.
    

A containment cell was prepared.

---

# **THE TRANSFER**

Corin initially resisted.

Not out of aggression.

She appeared confused.

> "...Where are we going?"

Arcturus:

> "...Somewhere safe."

Corin:

> "...For who?"

He could not answer.

---

# **THE CONTAINMENT CELL**

The cell was deliberately designed to resemble a quiet medical room rather than a prison.

It contains:

- A bed.
    
- Soft lighting.
    
- Basic furnishings.
    
- Medical monitoring equipment.
    
- Food and water access.
    
- Observation systems.
    
- Emergency communication access.
    

No restraints were initially required.

The door remained locked.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie was informed that Corin had been moved.

Her reaction was minimal.

> "...Okay."

She did not ask to see her.

She did not ask whether Corin was safe.

She simply returned to the treehouse.

Later that night, she slept on the floor.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi was devastated.

She understood why containment had become necessary.

She also understood why it had become necessary far too late.

> "...She's my daughter."

The medical staff assured her that Corin was being monitored continuously.

Lumi nodded.

> "...Then don't let her disappear."

---

# **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus remained outside the containment cell for some time.

Corin sat on the bed.

She stared at nothing.

He placed his hand against the observation window.

> "...I'm still here."

Corin did not respond.

---

# **NORMA**

Norma reviewed the incident report several times.

Eventually she closed it.

> "...That was the last thing holding her together."

She understood.

Corin had been unstable for a long time.

But some part of her still believed that Orphie cared.

That belief had been keeping the stable side alive.

Now it was gone.

---

# **STAR**

Star cried when she heard.

Again.

She could barely speak.

> "...She lost her sister."

Nightshade held her.

> "...She didn't lose her."

Star shook her head.

> "...She thinks she did."

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts remain outside the containment area.

They are not permitted inside.

One Mart quietly watched the door.

> "...Corin?"

Another answered:

> "...Safe."

The first Mart lowered its head.

> "...Good."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

### **CORIN**

- Contained.
    
- Under continuous observation.
    
- Severe psychological instability.
    
- Stable cognitive state currently inaccessible.
    
- Unable to safely return to normal residential life.
    
- KCA duties remain suspended.
    

### **ORPHIE**

- Physically stable.
    
- Emotionally detached.
    
- Continues expressing hatred toward Corin.
    
- Has not requested contact.
    
- Remains separated from Corin.
    

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's mind had been fighting itself for weeks.

The stable part kept reminding her:

> **"She's still your sister."**

> **"She came back."**

> **"She must still care."**

Then Corin heard Orphie speaking behind her back.

Not anger.

Not frustration.

Not a single cruel sentence.

A declaration.

> **"I deeply hate her."**

That was the moment the stable side could no longer find something to hold onto.

It surrendered.

Now Corin sits behind a containment door.

Orphie sleeps alone on the floor.

Their family waits outside.

And the two sisters who once shared everything are now separated by a reinforced door.

**End of Containment Incident Report**
