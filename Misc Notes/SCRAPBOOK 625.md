# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **TREEHOUSE / MISSING PERSON CRISIS REPORT**

# **ORPHIE — EIGHT-WEEK ABSENCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Time Missing:** **8 Weeks**  
**Last Confirmed Activity:** Left Treehouse to collect flowers  
**Status:** **MISSING**  
**Corin:** **CRITICAL WELFARE CONCERN**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Eight weeks have passed since Orphie disappeared.

She has not returned.

No confirmed sighting has been established.

No reliable communication has been received.

The search remains active.

However, the primary emergency is no longer the passage of time alone.

It is **Corin's condition.**

---

# **TREEHOUSE SUPPLIES**

The Treehouse's stored food and water supplies have been exhausted.

They ran out some time ago.

Corin had already stopped eating and drinking regularly before the supplies were completely depleted.

She has now refused food and water for an extended period.

The Treehouse therefore contains neither:

- Adequate food.
    
- Adequate drinking water.
    

Corin has also refused to leave the Treehouse to obtain supplies.

---

# **CORIN'S WITHDRAWAL**

Corin remains inside.

She no longer checks the entrance with the same anticipation as before.

She simply stays there.

Her wings remain wrapped around her body for long periods.

She barely moves.

When someone approaches the Treehouse, she may acknowledge them.

She does not come outside.

Even the arrival of Lumi, Arcturus, Norma, or SAFEHOUSE personnel does not reliably convince her to leave.

---

# **FAMILY RESPONSE**

Lumi has continued attempting to reach her daughter.

Arcturus and Norma have remained involved.

The SAFEHOUSE residents have continued searching for Orphie while monitoring Corin.

At this stage, however, the situation has exceeded what passive observation can safely accommodate.

The previous approach of:

> **"Give Corin space and wait for her to come out."**

is no longer considered acceptable.

---

# **EMERGENCY INTERVENTION**

C.O.R.E. has declared the Treehouse a **welfare emergency**.

The search for Orphie continues.

At the same time, a dedicated team has been assigned to Corin.

Their immediate priorities are:

1. **Restore access to food and clean water.**
    
2. **Assess Corin's physical condition.**
    
3. **Keep her safe inside the Treehouse if she refuses to leave.**
    
4. **Maintain continuous contact with her.**
    
5. **Prevent her from becoming completely isolated.**
    
6. **Continue searching for Orphie.**
    

Nobody is waiting for Corin to somehow recover on her own.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi has refused to abandon the Treehouse.

She remains nearby for extended periods.

Eventually, she called through the door:

> **"Corin."**

Silence.

> **"I'm not asking you to leave."**

Another pause.

> **"I'm just asking you to let me take care of you."**

Corin gave no immediate answer.

Lumi stayed.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

After a long silence, Corin finally whispered:

> **"...I want Orphie."**

Lumi answered:

> **"I know."**

Corin:

> **"...She said she'd come back."**

Lumi:

> **"I know."**

Neither tried to argue with her.

---

# **SEARCH OPERATION**

The investigation into Orphie's disappearance has been escalated accordingly.

The Architects have authorized expanded dimensional searches.

C.O.R.E. is examining every available lead.

Guiding Light continues monitoring the Treehouse's rift.

Star has made the Starlands available for additional search operations.

Neko is reviewing anomalous records and access data.

The SAFEHOUSE remains on heightened readiness.

---

# **THE FLOWERS**

The original reason Orphie left remains painfully simple.

She wanted to bring flowers back to the Treehouse.

Corin still remembers.

A small empty space remains near the Treehouse entrance where Corin had expected Orphie to place them.

Nothing has been placed there.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Eight weeks ago, Orphie left the Treehouse to collect flowers.

She never returned.

Corin waited.

Then she waited longer.

Eventually, waiting became the only thing she could do.

Now the Treehouse has run out of supplies.

Corin has stopped eating.

She has stopped drinking.

She remains inside the place she associates with Orphie, even as the rest of the world tries to reach her.

The search for Orphie will continue.

But C.O.R.E. has issued one overriding order:

> **Corin must not be allowed to disappear with her.**

The next phase of the operation is no longer simply **finding Orphie**.

It is **bringing both of them home.**

**End of Treehouse / Missing Person Crisis Report**
