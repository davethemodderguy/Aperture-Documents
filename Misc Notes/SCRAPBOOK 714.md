# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — THE SOUP**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** **CRITICAL / MINIMAL NUTRITIONAL INTAKE**

---

# **CURRENT NUTRITIONAL STATUS**

Alice has begun eating again.

There is only one problem.

She will only eat **Corin's chicken potato soup.**

Nothing else.

Not fruit.

Not bread.

Not ordinary meals.

Not nutritional substitutes.

Not the meals prepared specifically for her.

Not even carrots.

Only the soup.

When offered something else, Alice usually shakes her head.

> **"No."**

When asked what she wants:

> **"Corin soup."**

---

# **THE ROUTINE**

Corin now makes the soup every day.

The recipe has become almost absurdly consistent.

Chicken.

Potatoes.

Broth.

A small amount of seasoning.

Nothing too strong.

Nothing complicated.

Alice prefers it warm.

Not hot.

Warm.

Corin has memorized the exact temperature Alice likes.

She has also memorized the texture.

The chicken must be soft.

The potatoes must be thoroughly cooked.

Anything too large makes Alice stop eating.

Corin adjusts everything accordingly.

---

# **WHY THIS SOUP?**

Nobody knows exactly.

Alice doesn't remember why.

When asked:

> **"Why do you like Corin's soup?"**

Alice simply answers:

> **"Good."**

Sometimes:

> **"Warm."**

Sometimes she says:

> **"Corin made it."**

That answer is probably the most important one.

---

# **THE MEDICAL PROBLEM**

The soup is not nutritionally sufficient to reverse Alice's condition by itself.

Medical personnel are fully aware of this.

Alice needs substantially more nutrition than the soup alone can provide.

However—

**Alice is eating.**

After nearly a year of severe deprivation, maintaining that intake is currently more important than forcing variety.

The medical team has therefore adopted a gradual approach.

The immediate objective is:

> **Keep Alice eating.**

The next objective is:

> **Expand what Alice can tolerate.**

Neither objective will be pursued through force.

---

# **CORIN'S APPROACH**

Corin refuses to pressure Alice.

She doesn't say:

> **"You need to eat something else."**

She doesn't say:

> **"Soup isn't enough."**

She doesn't make Alice feel guilty.

She simply makes the soup.

Every day.

Sometimes twice.

Sometimes Alice only manages a few spoonfuls.

Corin still makes it.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle has attempted to add things.

A little bread.

Some vegetables.

A tiny piece of carrot.

Alice immediately notices.

> **"Different."**

Belle removes it.

> **"Okay."**

She doesn't argue.

---

# **NEKO**

Neko has already analyzed the soup's nutritional composition.

He has suggested several modifications that could increase its caloric and nutritional density without substantially changing its taste or texture.

Corin is currently experimenting with them.

The changes are deliberately tiny.

Alice must not notice a sudden difference.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi believes the soup has become more than food.

It is predictable.

Warm.

Familiar.

Made by someone Alice trusts.

There are no surprises.

No expectations.

No pressure.

Just Corin sitting beside her.

Lumi described it as:

> **"A safe thing she can put inside herself."**

---

# **ALICE**

One evening, Alice finished her bowl.

She looked at Corin.

> **"More?"**

Corin froze.

> **"...More?"**

Alice nodded.

> **"Please."**

Corin immediately got another serving.

Alice ate slowly.

Nobody celebrated.

Nobody clapped.

Nobody called anyone into the room.

They simply let her eat.

---

# **THE SIGNIFICANCE**

For everyone else, it is chicken potato soup.

For Alice, it is currently the one food her body accepts without resistance.

For Corin, it is a way of saying:

> **"I'm here."**

For the SAFEHOUSE, it is hope.

Not the overwhelming kind.

Not the kind that demands Alice recover immediately.

Just enough hope to make another bowl tomorrow.
