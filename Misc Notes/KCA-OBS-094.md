# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **KCA-OBS-094**

### **EXECUTIVE OBSERVATION REPORT – DIRECTOR BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Executive Personnel Only

---

## **SUMMARY**

During a routine inspection of the Director's office, administrative staff documented a subtle but unusual change.

A framed photograph that had occupied the corner of Belle Merryweather's desk for several years was no longer present.

The frame itself was gone.

No replacement had been put in its place.

---

## **PHOTOGRAPH DESCRIPTION**

Archived inventory records identify the missing photograph as depicting:

- Belle Merryweather
    
- Members of the Merryweather family
    
- The Friendly Roommates
    
- Taken during a retreat to Starlands
    

The photograph had previously remained on Belle's desk through office relocations, renovations, and multiple changes in furniture layout.

No record exists indicating it was transferred to storage.

---

## **OFFICE OBSERVATIONS**

Personnel noted additional changes within the office.

- Personal decorations had been reduced.
    
- The room appeared noticeably more sterile.
    
- Recreational books had been packed away.
    
- Plush decorations were absent.
    
- Only work-related documents remained visible.
    

The office reflected efficiency.

Very little reflected Belle herself.

---

## **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATIONS**

Director Belle's leadership remained consistent.

Her decisions continued to be impartial.

Response times remained exemplary.

No decline in operational capability was identified.

However, employees reported that interactions with the Director felt increasingly intimidating.

Not because she raised her voice.

Not because she issued harsher punishments.

But because of how little emotion she displayed.

Meetings became exceptionally quiet.

Questions were answered briefly.

Conversations ended as soon as business concluded.

Employees who had once known Belle as approachable now described her presence as difficult to read.

Several supervisors independently used the same word in confidential observation forms:

> **"Scary."**

Reviewers clarified that this description referred to the atmosphere surrounding the Director rather than any threatening conduct.

No incidents of aggression, intimidation, or abuse of authority were documented.

---

## **RECORD REVIEW**

No evidence confirms why the photograph was removed.

Likewise, there is no evidence that Belle has forgotten the Merryweather family or her time at Heaven's Blacksite.

The photograph's absence and her increasing emotional distance have prompted concern among reviewing staff, but any conclusion regarding her memories or intentions would be speculative.

---

## **STATUS**

Director Belle Merryweather continues to perform all executive responsibilities to an exceptionally high standard.

Behavioral monitoring remains ongoing.

The location of the missing photograph is currently **unknown**.

**End of Report.**
