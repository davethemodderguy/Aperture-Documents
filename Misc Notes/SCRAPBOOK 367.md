# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SOLARIA PHENOMENON / LUNAR FORM UPDATE**

# **COREY — SOLARIA / LUNA**

**Classification:** Level 4 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corey  
**Associated Forms:** Solaria / Luna

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has confirmed that Corey's solar manifestation possesses an additional transformation that occurs under **moonlight**.

This form is designated:

> **LUNA**

Luna is **not considered a second alter ego**.

Instead, Luna is classified as an **alternate magical form** of Solaria.

The distinction is important.

Solaria represents Corey's alternate persona.

Luna represents a lunar state of that persona.

---

# **SOLARIA — SOLAR FORM**

When exposed to sunlight without headwear, Corey can manifest as **Solaria**.

Solaria is characterized by:

- Yellow solar radiance.
    
- Increased environmental temperature.
    
- A right eyepatch.
    
- Solar-based magic.
    
- Greater confidence, wisdom, and intelligence.
    
- A spear formed from sunlight.
    
- Solar wings.
    

---

# **SOLAR WINGS**

Solaria possesses wings constructed entirely from concentrated sunlight.

The wings are not physical feathers.

They appear to consist of radiant, semi-solid solar energy.

The wings allow Solaria to **fly**.

Her flight is not dependent on conventional aerodynamics.

She can:

- Hover.
    
- Accelerate rapidly.
    
- Change direction in midair.
    
- Remain stationary while airborne.
    
- Fly vertically.
    
- Maintain flight without visible fatigue.
    

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

Solaria simply describes it as:

> "...Flying."

---

# **LUNA**

When Solaria is exposed to **moonlight**, her solar state can transition into Luna.

The transformation does not require direct sunlight.

Moonlight is sufficient.

During the transition, Solaria's characteristic yellow radiance gradually changes into a:

> **Soft azure-blue moonlight.**

Her magical affinity simultaneously shifts.

### **Solar Form**

**Magic:** Sun / Solar

**Glow:** Yellow

### **Lunar Form**

**Magic:** Moon / Lunar

**Glow:** Soft blue / Azure

---

# **LUNA'S MAGIC**

Luna's magic appears to be the lunar counterpart of Solaria's abilities.

Her energy is considerably softer in appearance.

Instead of the intense warmth associated with Solaria, Luna produces a cool, gentle radiance.

Confirmed properties include:

- Moonlight manipulation.
    
- Lunar energy generation.
    
- Soft blue illumination.
    
- Temperature reduction.
    
- Lunar-based spellcasting.
    

The full extent of Luna's abilities remains unknown.

---

# **TRANSFORMATION**

The transformation between Solaria and Luna is gradual.

Under moonlight, Solaria's yellow glow begins fading.

The light becomes pale.

Then blue.

Then a soft azure.

At the same time, the surrounding temperature decreases.

The solar energy surrounding her is replaced by lunar energy.

Her wings also change accordingly.

---

# **LUNAR WINGS**

Luna retains the ability to fly.

Her wings transform from brilliant solar light into **soft blue moonlight**.

Unlike Solaria's wings, Luna's appear considerably more diffuse and gentle.

They leave faint trails of blue illumination when she moves.

The wings remain capable of sustained flight.

---

# **SHARED CONSCIOUSNESS**

Luna is not a separate consciousness.

She remains part of the same Corey's consciousness as Solaria.

Memory continuity is maintained.

Corey remembers Solaria.

Solaria remembers Corey.

Luna remembers both.

There is currently no evidence of memory loss during transformation.

---

# **PERSONALITY**

Luna's personality remains largely consistent with Solaria.

However, early observations suggest that Luna is:

- Calmer.
    
- Quieter.
    
- More reflective.
    
- Less energetic.
    
- More comfortable with silence.
    

Researchers emphasize that this is an initial observation and not yet a confirmed personality distinction.

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

The Marts immediately accepted Luna.

When the first transformation occurred, several Marts stared at the blue glow.

One whispered:

> "...Blue Sun?"

The Ambassador corrected it:

> "...Moon."

Another Mart looked up at Luna's wings.

> "...Pretty moon."

Luna smiled.

> "...Thank you."

Within minutes, several Marts were sleeping beneath her wings.

The soft lunar energy appears to be particularly comfortable for them.

---

# **SOLARIA'S COMMENT**

When asked what she thought of Luna, Solaria replied:

> "...She's still me."

The researcher clarified:

> "But she's different?"

Solaria nodded.

> "...A little."

> "...Sun and moon aren't the same."

> "...But they're both part of the same sky."

---

# **COREY'S COMMENT**

Corey retained full memory of the first Luna manifestation.

After being informed that she could become a lunar form, she stared at the report.

> "...So I have a sun form..."

She paused.

> "...And a moon form."

A Mart nodded.

> "...Yes."

Corey sighed.

> "...I really need to stop losing my hats."

The Mart looked concerned.

> "...Hat important."

Corey smiled.

> "...Very."

---

C.O.R.E. now recognizes three relevant states:

### **COREY**

Normal state.

- No mandatory magical manifestation.
    
- Usually wears soft headwear.
    
- Standard personality.
    

### **SOLARIA**

Solar manifestation.

- Activated by sunlight reaching Corey's uncovered head.
    
- Yellow radiance.
    
- Sun magic.
    
- Solar wings.
    
- Increased temperature.
    
- Right eyepatch.
    
- More confident, wiser, and smarter.
    

### **LUNA**

Lunar form of Solaria.

- Activated through exposure to moonlight.
    
- Soft blue / azure radiance.
    
- Moon magic.
    
- Lunar wings.
    
- Cooler environmental effect.
    
- Shares memories with Corey and Solaria.
    

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

One evening, Solaria was flying above the Blacksite.

Her golden wings illuminated the sky.

As the sun disappeared beyond the horizon, the first moonlight reached her.

The yellow glow slowly faded.

Soft blue light replaced it.

Her wings changed.

The warmth disappeared.

And Luna descended gently toward the Blacksite.

Hundreds of Marts looked upward.

One quietly said:

> "...Sun gone."

Another answered:

> "...Moon here."

Luna landed.

A Mart immediately climbed onto her shoulder.

> "...Pretty."

Luna smiled.

Above her, the moon illuminated the Blacksite.

For the first time, personnel realized that Corey did not merely carry the sun with her.

She carried a little bit of the moon, too.
