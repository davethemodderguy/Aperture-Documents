### **TREEHOUSE / FAMILY WELFARE OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — WITHDRAWAL FOLLOWING ORPHIE'S DISAPPEARANCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Location:** Treehouse  
**Status:** **Severe Emotional Withdrawal**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following Orphie's disappearance, Corin has refused to leave the Treehouse.

The behavior has persisted despite repeated attempts by friends and family to reach her.

She will not go outside.

She will not accompany search teams.

She will not visit the SAFEHOUSE.

She will not even come outside to see people she normally considers family.

---

# **FAMILY VISITS**

Several individuals have attempted to visit Corin.

Among them were:

- **Lumi** — Corin's mother.
    
- **Arcturus** — Corin's sibling.
    
- **Norma** — Corin's sibling.
    

None were permitted inside by Corin herself.

She does not appear angry with them.

She simply does not want to leave her current position.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi approached the Treehouse entrance and gently called for Corin.

> **"Corin?"**

No answer.

She waited.

Eventually, Corin spoke from inside.

> **"...I'm here."**

Lumi:

> **"Can I come in?"**

A long pause.

> **"...Please don't."**

Lumi did not force the issue.

She remained outside for a while before leaving.

---

# **ARCTURUS AND NORMA**

Her siblings attempted a similar approach.

They called to her from outside.

Corin responded only briefly.

> **"I'm okay."**

Neither believed her.

But they understood that forcing her outside would only make things worse.

They left her some food and water near the entrance.

---

# **CORIN'S CURRENT ROUTINE**

Corin spends most of her time inside the Treehouse.

She rarely moves from her usual resting area.

Her wings remain partially folded around herself.

She frequently looks toward the entrance.

She appears to be listening for someone.

Specifically:

**Orphie.**

---

# **THE EMPTY SPACE**

One particular area of the Treehouse has become significant.

It is where Orphie normally spends time.

Corin frequently sits there.

Sometimes she curls up.

Sometimes she simply stares at the empty space.

She has not moved several of Orphie's belongings.

When asked why, Corin quietly answered:

> **"...She'll need them when she comes back."**

---

# **REFUSAL TO LEAVE**

The SAFEHOUSE residents have offered to bring Corin to the Blacksite.

She refused.

Lumi offered to take her somewhere else.

She refused.

Her siblings offered to stay with her outside.

She refused.

Even when told that everyone was worried about her, Corin only replied:

> **"I don't want to go anywhere."**

---

# **NO HOSTILITY**

C.O.R.E. emphasizes that Corin's withdrawal should not be interpreted as hostility.

She does not hate anyone.

She is not rejecting her family.

She is simply overwhelmed.

Orphie's disappearance has left Corin clinging to the only place she still associates with certainty:

**The Treehouse.**

---

# **CURRENT SUPPORT**

Family and SAFEHOUSE personnel have adopted a nonintrusive approach.

They leave:

- Food.
    
- Water.
    
- Blankets.
    
- Messages.
    
- Small gifts.
    
- Updates regarding the search.
    

Nobody forces her outside.

Nobody demands that she "cheer up."

Nobody tells her to stop worrying.

They simply remind her:

> **"We're here."**

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

She rarely answers.

Sometimes:

> **"...Okay."**

Sometimes nothing.

But she has not asked anyone to stop visiting.

That is being interpreted as a small sign that she still wants their presence.

---

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin is not hiding because she wants to be alone.

She is hiding because the person she was expecting to return has not returned.

Her mother is outside.

Her siblings are outside.

Her friends are outside.

People who care about her are waiting for her.

But Corin remains inside the Treehouse.

She sits near the entrance.

She listens.

And every time she hears footsteps approaching, she looks up.

Hoping they belong to Orphie.

**End of Treehouse / Family Welfare Observation**
