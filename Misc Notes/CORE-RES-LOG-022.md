# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-RES-LOG-022**

### **RESIDENTIAL RECORD – PETS & SELF-PROCLAIMED PETS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential

---

## **SUMMARY**

Although no official Blacksite program exists regarding companion animals, many long-term residents have formed close bonds with pets—or, in some cases, have jokingly referred to one another as pets.

Archive Division has recorded the following relationships for residential reference.

---

## **Resident Companions**

### **Star**

**Companion:** **Leo**

Species: Snake

Leo accompanies Star during much of her free time and is noted for being unusually calm around other residents.

Star frequently introduces him proudly.

> "This is Leo."

---

### **Nightshade**

**Companion:** **Star**

Species: ...Star.

This entry exists solely because Star insisted.

When Archive Division questioned the classification, Star immediately replied:

> "I'm Nightshade's pet."

Nightshade sighed.

> "...She refuses to change the paperwork."

The entry was left unchanged.

---

### **Neko & Miss Neko**

**Companion:** **Aster**

Species: Cat

Although Aster is an independent resident with her own room access and remarkable intelligence, Neko and Miss Neko still affectionately describe her as their cat.

Aster has never objected.

She simply writes on her whiteboard:

> **"Acceptable."**

---

### **Lucy**

**Companion:** **Endmin**

Officially, this is incorrect.

Unofficially...

Lucy refers to Endmin as:

> **"...My adorable little troublemaker."**

Whenever this is said in front of others, Endmin immediately protests.

> "...Mom!"

Lucy only smiles.

> "What?"

Endmin's ears invariably turn bright red.

---

### **Endmin**

**Companion:** **Unnamed Rabbit**

Species: Domestic rabbit

Sex: Female

Unlike many of her roommates, Endmin's companion does not live at the Blacksite.

The rabbit remains in the Banlands, where Endmin believes she is happiest.

When asked why she has never brought her to Iceland, Endmin looked away shyly.

> "...She's comfortable at home."

After a pause, she quietly admitted,

> "...And... I'm a little nervous about bringing her somewhere unfamiliar."

Instead, Endmin periodically returns to the Banlands during approved leave to visit her rabbit.

Staff note that these visits noticeably improve her mood.

Very few people have met the rabbit, as Endmin rarely talks about her unless asked directly.

---

## **Conversation**

One afternoon, Taiga noticed a small framed photograph on Endmin's desk.

It showed a tiny rabbit resting comfortably in Endmin's lap.

Taiga smiled.

> "...She's adorable."

Endmin immediately reached for the picture.

> "...T-Thank you..."

Mart looked over.

> "...You never told us you had a rabbit."

Endmin scratched the back of her neck.

> "...I didn't think anyone would care."

The entire room looked at her.

Neko blinked.

Miss Neko blinked.

Star looked genuinely offended.

Taiga folded her arms.

> "...Endmin."

> "...We absolutely care."

Endmin quietly lowered her head.

> "...Oh..."
