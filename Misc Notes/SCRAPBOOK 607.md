## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

# **BELLE — SURVIVOR'S GUILT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subjects:** Belle Merryweather / Corin  
**Location:** Treehouse  
**Status:** **Belle Distressed / Corin Recovering**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Belle has begun blaming herself heavily for Corin's injury.

Although the pressure cooker malfunction was accidental, Belle has repeatedly convinced herself that she should have prevented it.

She knows Corin chose to shield her.

That knowledge has not made the guilt disappear.

It has made it worse.

---

# **BELLE'S GUILT**

Belle keeps replaying the incident in her head.

The pressure cooker.

The sudden explosion.

Corin moving in front of her.

The sound of the wing being struck.

The feathers covering the floor.

The large sack now sitting in storage.

Every detail remains vivid.

Belle's thoughts repeatedly return to one conclusion:

> **"She wouldn't be hurt if I wasn't there."**

This conclusion is inaccurate.

Belle knows it is inaccurate.

She cannot emotionally accept that fact.

---

# **CORIN'S CONDITION**

Corin's damaged left wing remains loosely resting across her lap.

She barely moves.

Her regeneration continues.

Her emotional state remains fragile.

Belle sees this every time she looks at her.

And every time she does, the guilt becomes stronger.

---

# **BELLE'S BEHAVIOR**

Belle has become increasingly attentive toward Corin.

She checks the bandages repeatedly.

She prepares her food.

She keeps her water nearby.

She stays awake when Corin cannot sleep.

She asks medical personnel for updates constantly.

If Corin winces, Belle immediately looks toward her.

If Corin moves the injured wing, Belle becomes worried.

If Corin cries, Belle blames herself.

---

# **CONFRONTATION**

Eventually, Corin notices.

She looks at Belle.

> **"...You're sad."**

Belle tries to deny it.

> **"I'm fine."**

Corin shakes her head.

> **"No, you're not."**

Belle looks away.

After a long silence, she finally admits:

> **"...It's my fault."**

Corin immediately responds:

> **"No."**

Belle:

> **"Your wing—"**

Corin interrupts.

> **"I chose to protect you."**

Belle falls silent.

---

# **CORIN'S PERSPECTIVE**

Corin does not regret protecting Belle.

There was no calculation.

No hesitation.

She saw Belle in danger and moved.

The injured wing was the price.

Corin would make the same decision again.

She tells Belle as much.

> **"I'd do it again."**

Belle's eyes fill with tears.

> **"Don't say that."**

Corin gently touches her hand.

> **"I mean it."**

---

# **THE FEATHER SACK**

Belle refuses to look at the collected feathers.

She knows what they represent.

Corin's loss.

Her injury.

Her guilt.

The sack has therefore been moved somewhere Belle does not have to see it.

---

# **ORPHIE'S INTERVENTION**

Orphie eventually speaks to Belle privately.

She tells her something simple:

> **"You didn't hurt Corin."**

> **"The accident did."**

> **"Corin made her choice."**

Belle listens.

She understands the words.

But understanding them intellectually is not the same as believing them emotionally.

---

# **CURRENT PSYCHOLOGICAL STATE**

|Category|Status|
|---|---|
|Guilt|**Severe**|
|Self-blame|**Persistent**|
|Anxiety|**Elevated**|
|Sleep|**Disrupted**|
|Concern for Corin|**Extreme**|
|Responsibility for accident|**None**|
|Ability to accept this|**Poor**|
|Corin's forgiveness|**Immediate / Absolute**|

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Two people are hurting from the same accident.

Corin hurts because her wing was severely damaged.

Belle hurts because she believes she should have prevented it.

Neither wants the other to suffer.

Corin keeps telling Belle:

> **"It wasn't your fault."**

Belle keeps looking at the bandaged wing.

And quietly crying anyway.

The medical team can regenerate Corin's wing.

They cannot regenerate guilt.

That will take time.

**End of SAFEHOUSE Psychological Observation**
