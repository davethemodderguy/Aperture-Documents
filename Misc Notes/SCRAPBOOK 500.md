## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **POST-STORM RECOVERY / ARCHITECTAL INTERVENTION LOG**

# **A LITTLE COLOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Internal Personnel  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Intervention:** **Guiding Light — Controlled Blessing**  
**Status:** **SUCCESSFUL / NO STRUCTURAL ALTERATION**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's monochromatic vision was one of the lasting consequences of her experimentation and reanimation.

She could perceive brightness.

She could distinguish shapes.

She could recognize objects.

But color itself had gradually disappeared from her perception.

Worse, her memories of color had begun fading with it.

Guiding Light could not simply restore Corin's body to normal.

Attempting to reconstruct her biological functions carried unacceptable risks.

Corin's current state is extremely delicate.

Her body is dead.

Her Ether sustains it.

Her limbs require structural support.

Her mind has already endured extensive damage.

A complete restoration could destabilize the entire system keeping her alive.

So Guiding Light chose something much smaller.

Something safe.

---

# **THE INTERVENTION**

Guiding Light did not alter Corin's body.

She did not recreate her organs.

She did not restore her original nervous system.

She did not attempt to reverse the experiments.

Instead, she carefully interacted with Corin's perception.

A controlled blessing.

Nothing more.

Guiding Light placed her hand gently over Corin's eyes.

> **"Close them."**

Corin did.

> **"What are you doing?"**

> **"Something I think I can do safely."**

A pause.

> **"I can't make you what you were."**

Corin already knew.

> **"I know."**

> **"But I might be able to give you something back."**

---

# **THE FIRST COLOR**

Guiding Light removed her hand.

> **"Open your eyes."**

Corin did.

She stared at the room.

For several seconds, nothing happened.

Then her eyes widened.

The world was no longer monochrome.

There was color.

Real color.

Soft colors.

Bright colors.

Subtle colors.

Colors she had almost forgotten existed.

Corin stared at her own hands.

Then at her clothes.

Then the walls.

Then the forest outside.

She looked toward the table.

The blue notes were glowing.

Actually blue.

Not merely bright.

**Blue.**

---

# **CORIN**

Corin didn't speak.

She simply stared.

Then she looked at Guiding Light.

> **"...That's blue."**

Guiding Light smiled.

> **"Yes."**

Corin picked up one of the old notes.

She watched the blue glow between her fingers.

Her eyes began watering.

> **"I remember this."**

Guiding Light nodded.

> **"Good."**

---

# **THE WORLD**

Corin slowly walked outside.

The forest was suddenly unfamiliar.

Not because it had changed.

Because she could finally see what had always been there.

Green leaves.

Brown bark.

Blue sky.

White clouds.

Red wool.

The colors of the flowers around the treehouse.

She stopped when she saw the sunflower pot.

She stared at it for a long time.

> **"...Yellow."**

Guiding Light stood beside her.

> **"Yellow."**

Corin smiled.

A genuine one.

---

# **THE LIMIT**

Guiding Light eventually explained the limitation.

> **"I can't safely fix everything."**

Corin nodded.

> **"I know."**

> **"I can't make your body normal."**

> **"Okay."**

> **"I can't undo what happened to you."**

Corin looked toward the forest.

> **"...I know."**

Guiding Light paused.

> **"But I can give you this."**

Corin looked back at her.

> **"That's enough."**

---

# **WHY THIS WAS SAFE**

The intervention did not reconstruct Corin's body.

It did not alter her Ether core.

It did not repair her damaged organs.

It did not attempt to restore biological function.

Instead, Guiding Light modified the way Corin's existing perception processed color.

The result is stable.

No degradation has been observed.

No Ether instability occurred.

No memory loss occurred.

Most importantly:

**Corin's body remained exactly as it was.**

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

That evening, Corin opened her notebook.

For the first time in months, she could actually describe colors from memory and perception together.

She wrote:

> **Blue is brighter than I remembered.**

Then:

> **Green feels like the forest is alive.**

Then:

> **Red is warmer than I thought.**

She paused.

Then wrote:

> **I forgot how pretty everything was.**

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light watched from the doorway.

She knew this was not a cure.

It was not even close.

Corin was still an Ether-sustained corpse.

Her scars remained.

Her stitches remained.

Her memories remained complicated.

Her mental health still needed time, care, and people who would stay.

Guiding Light could not safely undo all of that.

But she could give Corin one thing.

A little piece of the world back.

And for now, that was enough.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Architects can do almost anything.

But **almost anything** is not the same as **anything safely.**

Guiding Light could potentially attempt far greater alterations.

She refuses to risk Corin's existence for the sake of making her "normal."

So she chose something smaller.

Something gentle.

Something Corin had lost.

Color.

The next morning, Corin put on her red wool beanie.

She looked at the blue hairclip.

Then at the green forest.

Then at the yellow sunflower.

And smiled.

> **"I can see it again."**

Guiding Light smiled back.

> **"I know."**

**End of Post-Storm Recovery / Architectal Intervention Log**
