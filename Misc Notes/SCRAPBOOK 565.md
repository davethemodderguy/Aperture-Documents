# **CORIN — PERSONAL WRITING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY

**Subject:** Corin  
**Assessment:** Severe emotional distress

---

# **RECORDED ENTRY**

> **"A deep sense of resentment and sadness filling my soul,**
> 
> **don't look at me.**
> 
> **I shouldn't exist.**
> 
> **I'm still the same.**
> 
> **I'm alive, but still have scars.**
> 
> **I can never escape myself."**

---

# **OBSERVATION**

The entry demonstrates that Corin's recent recovery has not erased her perception of herself.

She recognizes that she is alive.

She recognizes that her body has changed.

She recognizes that she has people around her.

Yet internally, she continues to associate herself with the person she was before.

Her scars are not merely physical.

To Corin, they represent everything that happened to her and everything she believes those experiences made her.

---

# **"DON'T LOOK AT ME."**

This particular statement has appeared repeatedly in Corin's recent behavior.

She has increasingly attempted to hide herself from others.

Her wings are frequently used to cover her face and body.

When someone enters the room, she may turn away.

When someone attempts to comfort her, she sometimes becomes visibly uncomfortable.

This does not appear to mean that she dislikes the people around her.

Quite the opposite.

Corin appears to want companionship while simultaneously believing that she does not deserve to be seen.

---

# **"I SHOULDN'T EXIST."**

This statement is consistent with Corin's longstanding identity conflict.

For a long time, Corin believed herself to be neither alive nor dead.

Even after becoming biologically alive, that belief did not disappear.

Her body changed.

Her mind did not change at the same speed.

C.O.R.E. therefore considers her current distress partially rooted in a persistent disconnect between:

**What Corin is now**

and

**what Corin believes she is.**

---

# **"I'M STILL THE SAME."**

This appears to be the most important sentence in the entry.

Corin recognizes the physical changes.

She knows she is alive.

She knows she has wings.

She knows she is a Cat Thiren.

But she does not believe these things have fundamentally changed who she is.

To her, the laboratory, the years of suffering, the scars, and the memories remain part of the same identity.

She cannot simply discard them.

---

# **"I'M ALIVE, BUT STILL HAVE SCARS."**

C.O.R.E. considers this statement significant.

Corin's body has healed remarkably well.

Some physical injuries have regenerated.

Her organs were restored.

Her limbs were repaired.

Her heart beats.

She breathes.

She can bleed.

She can heal.

Yet physical healing did not erase psychological scars.

Corin appears to understand this distinction painfully well.

---

# **"I CAN NEVER ESCAPE MYSELF."**

This statement appears to summarize her current condition.

Corin has repeatedly attempted to escape her past through change.

A new body.

A new identity.

A new home.

A new life.

Even her feline form once provided a temporary sense of safety.

But none of these things allowed her to leave herself behind.

Eventually, her memories followed.

---

# **CURRENT RESPONSE**

No attempt has been made to erase or suppress Corin's memories.

The Architects have agreed that doing so would not constitute genuine healing.

For now, personnel are instructed to remain available to Corin without forcing interaction.

She does not need to perform happiness for anyone.

She does not need to pretend that she is fine.

She simply needs to know that someone will remain nearby even when she cannot bear to be looked at.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin wanted to become someone new.

Instead, she discovered something more difficult:

She was still herself.

The scars remained.

The memories remained.

The resentment remained.

But so did everything else.

Her life remained.

Her friendships remained.

Her ability to laugh remained.

Her wings remained.

Her home remained.

And despite what she believes when the darkness becomes overwhelming—

**Corin has not disappeared.**

She is still here.

**End of Psychological Personnel Note**
