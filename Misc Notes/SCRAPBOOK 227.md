# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: COREY LOCKWOOD – NOCTURNAL BEHAVIOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical & Executive Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corey Lockwood is generally regarded as one of the more cheerful residents associated with Project SAFEHOUSE.

She is sociable.

Approachable.

Frequently smiling.

Personnel consistently describe her as emotionally resilient during daytime hours.

However, Medical Services have identified a pronounced contrast between Corey's daytime and nighttime behavior.

The difference is significant enough to warrant continued observation.

---

# **DAYTIME PRESENTATION**

Throughout the day, Corey is typically observed:

- Assisting Corin with administrative duties.
    
- Visiting Project SAFEHOUSE.
    
- Talking with residents.
    
- Laughing easily.
    
- Participating in communal activities.
    
- Encouraging Corin to remain socially engaged.
    

Nothing about her daytime behavior suggests significant emotional distress.

Most personnel who only interact with Corey during working hours remain unaware that any concern exists.

---

# **NIGHTTIME PATTERN**

The change begins shortly after evening.

Corey rarely sleeps through the night.

Instead, she commonly adopts one of three behaviors.

### **Distraction**

Corey keeps herself occupied for as long as possible.

Common activities include:

- Reading.
    
- Organizing paperwork.
    
- Quietly cleaning.
    
- Walking the halls.
    
- Watching snowfall from windows.
    

She often continues until physical exhaustion forces her to stop.

---

### **Private Crying**

When alone, Corey has been observed quietly crying without attempting to seek assistance.

She consistently stops if she believes someone is approaching.

When asked the following morning, she invariably replies that she is "fine."

---

### **The Snow**

On particularly difficult nights, Corey leaves her residence entirely.

She sits or stands outside in the middle of severe snowstorms.

She does not wander.

She simply remains there.

Sometimes for several hours.

Personnel initially believed she was attempting self-harm through exposure.

Medical evaluation found no supporting evidence.

When questioned, Corey answered only:

> "...It's quieter."

---

# **CORIN**

Corin appears to recognize these episodes immediately.

She does not attempt to force Corey back indoors.

Instead, she quietly joins her.

The two are frequently observed sitting together beneath falling snow without speaking.

Sometimes for hours.

Neither appears uncomfortable with the silence.

Medical Services believe Corin understands that Corey is not looking for conversation.

Only company.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical Services believe these behaviors are consistent with unresolved traumatic grief associated with Universe C-02.

During the day, Corey focuses on the people she has gained.

At night, she remembers the family she lost.

She has never expressed resentment toward her current family.

Nor has she expressed a desire to return to Universe C-02.

Instead, interviews suggest that nighttime simply removes the distractions that help her function during the day.

One clinician summarized the pattern as follows:

> "Corin remembers what happened to her."

> "Corey remembers who is missing."

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corey's condition remains stable.

She continues fulfilling all professional responsibilities.

She remains affectionate toward her family and friends.

Her daytime demeanor has not diminished.

Medical Services do not believe Corey is pretending to be happy.

Her happiness is genuine.

So is her grief.

One does not invalidate the other.

Each morning, Corey greets everyone with the same bright smile they have come to know.

Each night, when the Blacksite grows quiet and snow begins to fall...

She is reminded of another home.

Another family.

Another life.

And although she has found a new place to belong...

Some nights, she still mourns the one she lost.
