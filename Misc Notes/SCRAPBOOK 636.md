### **CORIN / PERSONAL NOTEBOOK ANALYSIS**

# **THE BODY THAT RUNS ON HOPE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Document Source:** Personal Notebook  
**Status:** **CONFIRMED THROUGH SUBJECT'S OWN OBSERVATIONS**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

A newly analyzed section of Corin's notebook appears to document Corin's own understanding of her physiology following Orphie's disappearance.

Unlike previous entries, these pages are not primarily emotional.

Corin was attempting to **study herself**.

Her conclusion is remarkably simple:

> **"I live on one thing."**

> **"Hope."**

---

# **CORIN'S SELF-DOCUMENTATION**

The entry begins with a crude anatomical drawing of herself.

Two hearts are clearly marked.

Her wings are drawn behind her.

Several lines extend from her chest toward her wings and head.

Beside the drawing:

> **"I don't think my body works like everyone else's."**

Below it:

> **"I think it knows when I give up."**

---

# **THE TWO HEARTS**

Corin specifically documents her two hearts.

She notes that both continue functioning even when her overall physical condition deteriorates.

However, she believes her hearts do not explain everything.

> **"Two hearts should mean I can keep going."**

> **"But sometimes it feels like there's nothing making them beat."**

The next sentence is written in blue ink:

> **"Maybe it's hope."**

---

# **THE WINGS**

Corin also documents her wings.

She notes that their condition appears to correspond with her emotional state.

When she feels safe:

> **"They feel light."**

When she is frightened:

> **"They curl up."**

When she is happy:

> **"I want to fly."**

Later:

> **"When I don't want to be here, they feel heavy."**

The final sentence is written in blackened blood.

---

# **THE FEATHERS**

Corin appears to have independently discovered the relationship between her feathers and her emotional state.

She writes:

> **"My feathers don't burn because they're special."**

Then:

> **"They burn because I am."**

Several lines below:

> **"As long as I have something to hope for, they keep burning."**

This is followed by a drawing of a small azure flame.

---

# **THE FLAME**

Corin describes the flame as:

> **"A little piece of me that refuses to stop."**

She then documents its behavior.

> **"It doesn't need air."**

> **"It doesn't need fuel."**

> **"It doesn't care about fire."**

Then:

> **"But it cares if I give up."**

This statement is heavily underlined.

---

# **AFTER ORPHIE DISAPPEARED**

The handwriting changes significantly when Corin begins documenting the period after Orphie's disappearance.

The first entry reads:

> **"Orphie is gone."**

Then:

> **"I'm still okay."**

Several pages later:

> **"I'm not okay."**

Then:

> **"But she'll come back."**

The next pages repeatedly mention waiting.

---

# **THE HOPE EXPERIMENT**

Corin appears to have unintentionally conducted an experiment on herself.

She tracked her own condition alongside her expectations.

One section reads:

> **"When I think she's coming back, I feel better."**

> **"When I think she's not, everything hurts."**

> **"When I stop expecting her, my feathers get darker."**

She eventually writes:

> **"Hope is keeping me alive."**

---

# **THE PHYSIOLOGICAL THEORY**

Corin's theory is crude but surprisingly consistent with later observations.

She believes her body converts emotional hope into something that supports her anomalous physiology.

Her diagram describes the process approximately as:

**HOPE**

↓

**HEART**

↓

**BLOOD**

↓

**WINGS**

↓

**FEATHERS**

↓

**LIGHT**

She adds a note:

> **"Don't know if this is scientifically correct."**

Then:

> **"It feels correct."**

---

# **THE DECLINE**

As the months pass, the diagrams become simpler.

Corin stops recording detailed physiological observations.

Her entries become shorter.

> **"Hope is getting smaller."**

Later:

> **"The lights are dimmer."**

Later:

> **"I'm tired."**

Later:

> **"I don't think she is coming."**

---

# **THE FINAL OBSERVATION**

Near the end of this section is one of the most important statements in the notebook.

Written in blue:

> **"If hope is what keeps me alive..."**

The sentence stops.

Below it, in black:

> **"...what happens when I run out?"**

A large Black Eye occupies the remainder of the page.

---

# **C.O.R.E. INTERPRETATION**

Corin's theory should not be treated as conventional biology.

Nevertheless, her observations correspond disturbingly well with documented phenomena.

Her:

- Feather luminosity decreased.
    
- Physical activity decreased.
    
- Social interaction decreased.
    
- Emotional stability deteriorated.
    
- Burning feathers eventually extinguished.
    

All occurred during prolonged perceived abandonment.

This strongly supports the possibility that **hope is genuinely integrated into Corin's anomalous physiology.**

---

# **ORPHIE**

The revelation has affected Orphie significantly.

After reading Corin's explanation, Orphie reportedly remained silent for several minutes.

She eventually said:

> **"She wasn't just waiting for me."**

> **"She was surviving because she thought I'd come back."**

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin spent years believing her strength came from her body.

Her wings.

Her regeneration.

Her two hearts.

Her anomalous blood.

Her feathers.

Her light.

But after Orphie disappeared, Corin began studying herself closely.

And she reached a different conclusion.

All of those things could continue functioning only as long as something inside her told them to.

That something was hope.

She wrote it herself:

> **"I live on one thing."**

> **"Hope."**

For eight months, hope kept her waiting.

Hope kept her moving.

Hope kept her feathers burning.

And when hope finally disappeared...

the flames followed.
