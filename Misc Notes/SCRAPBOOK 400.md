### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION LOG**

# **ORPHIE — PHYSICAL EFFECTS OF SLEEP DEPRIVATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Medical Concern  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Marts / Luna / Solaria  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **SEVERE CONCERN**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie's physical appearance has begun reflecting the extent of her prolonged sleep deprivation.

Her emotional detachment is concerning on its own.

Her body now provides visible evidence that her condition has persisted long enough to affect her physically.

Personnel no longer need to ask whether Orphie has been sleeping.

> **Her appearance answers for her.**

---

# **VISIBLE INDICATORS**

C.O.R.E. medical personnel have documented several noticeable changes.

### **Eye Bags**

Dark circles and pronounced bags have developed beneath Orphie's eyes.

They remain visible even after periods when she claims to have slept.

---

### **Wrinkling Around the Eyelids**

Fine lines and wrinkles have become increasingly noticeable around her eyelids.

The change is particularly apparent when she is tired or has been awake for an extended period.

---

### **Heavy Eyes**

Orphie's eyes frequently appear:

- Tired.
    
- Heavy.
    
- Unfocused.
    
- Slow to react.
    

She sometimes stares at something for several seconds before responding.

---

### **Facial Fatigue**

Her face has developed a consistently exhausted appearance.

Even when she is sitting quietly and doing nothing physically demanding, she looks as though she has not rested.

---

### **General Physical Exhaustion**

Additional observations include:

- Slower movement.
    
- Reduced physical energy.
    
- Longer pauses before responding.
    
- Occasional difficulty maintaining attention.
    
- Remaining seated for extended periods.
    
- Falling briefly asleep only to wake again shortly afterward.
    

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

When asked whether she was tired, Orphie answered:

> "...Yes."

The interviewer asked:

> "When did you last sleep properly?"

Orphie remained silent.

After several seconds:

> "...I don't remember."

---

# **THE MIRROR**

Corin once found Orphie standing in front of a mirror.

She was looking at the dark circles beneath her eyes.

Corin asked:

> "...Have you been sleeping?"

Orphie:

> "...Not much."

Corin:

> "...How long?"

Orphie looked at her reflection.

> "...I don't know."

She then walked away.

---

# **CORIN'S CONCERN**

Corin has become increasingly distressed by the physical changes.

She remembers what Orphie looked like before the sleep deprivation became severe.

Now she can see the exhaustion every time Orphie comes home.

Corin reportedly said:

> "...You don't even look rested when you say you slept."

Orphie answered:

> "...I probably didn't."

---

# **THE MARTS NOTICE**

The Marts have also noticed the physical changes.

One Mart stared at Orphie's eyes for several seconds.

> "...Tired."

Orphie:

> "...Yes."

The Mart climbed onto her shoulder.

> "...Sleep."

Orphie:

> "...Later."

The Mart remained there.

> "...Now."

Orphie did not answer.

---

# **LUNA'S OBSERVATION**

Luna was visibly disturbed when she saw Orphie's physical condition.

> "...She looks exhausted."

The researcher asked whether Luna experienced the same fatigue while manifested.

She answered:

> "...No."

She paused.

> "...That's why I'm worried."

---

# **SOLARIA'S OBSERVATION**

Solaria gave an equally concerned assessment.

> "...Her body is keeping track of everything she refuses to."

She looked toward Orphie.

> "...She can say she's fine."

> "...Her eyes can't."

---

# **SLEEP PATTERN**

Current observations indicate that Orphie's sleep is not merely shortened.

It is **irregular and fragmented**.

She may:

- Remain awake for most of a night.
    
- Sleep briefly during the day.
    
- Wake suddenly.
    
- Remain awake for hours afterward.
    
- Fall asleep unintentionally.
    
- Repeat the cycle.
    

The pattern has persisted long enough to produce visible physical effects.

---

# **WHY THIS CONCERNS C.O.R.E.**

Sleep deprivation is beginning to compound Orphie's existing problems.

Her emotional detachment makes it difficult for her to recognize the importance of rest.

Her lack of interest in self-care makes her less likely to correct the problem herself.

Her isolation at the remote treehouse makes supervision more difficult.

And her declining emotional connection to the people around her means she may not respond to ordinary encouragement.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The SAFEHOUSE residents have become increasingly attentive to her physical condition.

They notice when she enters a room.

They notice how slowly she moves.

They notice when she has been awake all night.

They notice her eyes.

Nobody needs to ask anymore.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Sleep:** Severely disrupted.

**Visible fatigue:** Significant.

**Eye bags:** Pronounced.

**Eyelid wrinkling:** Increased.

**Energy:** Reduced.

**Self-care:** Declining.

**Emotional state:** Extremely detached.

**Marts:** Concerned and encouraging rest.

**Corin:** Highly concerned.

**Luna:** Concerned.

**Solaria:** Concerned.

**Medical concern:** **HIGH.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie does not talk about her exhaustion very much.

She doesn't complain.

She doesn't ask for help.

She rarely even acknowledges it.

But her body has begun telling the story for her.

The dark circles beneath her eyes.

The fine wrinkles around her eyelids.

The heavy stare.

The slow movements.

The exhausted expression.

All of it says the same thing:

> **Orphie has not been sleeping.**

And unlike her emotions, her body cannot simply pretend that nothing is wrong.

**End of Personnel Welfare / SAFEHOUSE Observation Log**
