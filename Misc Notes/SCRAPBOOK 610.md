# **CORIN — NANONEKONIUM WING ARMOR: KINETIC DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Designers:** Neko / Curious Light  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **Fully Operational**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Further testing of Corin's Nano Nekonium wing armor has revealed a capability significantly beyond its original defensive purpose.

The armor can withstand **all conventional ballistic impacts tested to date**.

In practical terms:

> **Corin's armored wings can block bullets.**

Every tested projectile has failed to penetrate the protected wing.

The underlying reason is simple.

**Nekonium does not break.**

---

# **NEKONIUM PROPERTIES**

Nekonium is considered **100% indestructible** under known conditions.

Its defining properties include:

- Complete structural integrity.
    
- Extreme impact resistance.
    
- Kinetic energy storage.
    
- Kinetic energy distribution.
    
- Controlled kinetic energy transfer.
    

Rather than simply stopping incoming force, the material can **absorb and redistribute it** throughout its structure.

Neko recognized that this could be combined with Corin's existing feather-based abilities.

---

# **THE SYNERGY**

Neko's design philosophy was straightforward:

> **"If the armor can catch the force, why waste it?"**

When a projectile strikes the armored wing, its kinetic energy is captured by the Nano Nekonium system.

Instead of allowing that energy to dissipate harmlessly, the armor can distribute it through the wing's protective structure.

Corin's feathers can then receive a controlled portion of that stored kinetic energy.

If a feather is subsequently launched as a projectile, the stored energy can be transferred into it.

In effect:

> **Incoming attack → Wing armor → Stored kinetic force → Feather → Counterattack**

---

# **KINETIC FEATHER SYSTEM**

Corin's feathers already possess multiple functions.

The new armor adds another layer to them.

A feather launched after absorbing distributed kinetic energy can carry a significantly greater amount of momentum than it normally would.

The exact output depends on:

- Impact intensity.
    
- Amount of stored energy.
    
- Distribution across the armor.
    
- Corin's launch velocity.
    
- Feather condition.
    
- Ether interaction.
    

C.O.R.E. has classified this as a **synergistic anomalous weapons system**.

---

# **DEFENSIVE MODE**

When Corin raises her wings to protect herself or another person, the armor forms a defensive barrier.

Incoming projectiles strike the Nano Nekonium surface.

The material remains completely intact.

The kinetic force is captured and distributed.

Corin experiences substantially reduced impact compared with an unarmored wing.

Her feathers remain attached.

Her wing remains functional.

---

# **OFFENSIVE TRANSFER**

Corin can then launch selected feathers.

The stored kinetic energy follows the feather.

This creates an unusual interaction between:

**Nekonium engineering**

and

**Corin's Ether-infused biology.**

The armor effectively turns incoming kinetic force into an available resource for her feather-based abilities.

Neko has referred to the concept as:

> **"Free ammunition."**

Curious Light strongly recommended using a less alarming term in official documentation.

The term remains in engineering notes.

---

# **LIMITATIONS**

The system does not create energy from nothing.

It only stores and redistributes kinetic energy received by the armor.

Additionally:

- Corin cannot indefinitely accumulate energy.
    
- The armor has controlled distribution limits.
    
- Excess energy must eventually be released or dissipated.
    
- Corin's feathers remain subject to their normal properties.
    
- Safe discharge protocols remain active.
    

The armor will automatically prevent unsafe accumulation.

---

# **FLIGHT**

Despite the additional armor, Corin's flight remains fully functional.

The Nano Nekonium system dynamically adjusts to her wings.

No significant loss of maneuverability has been detected.

Corin can still:

- Fly.
    
- Hover.
    
- Backflip.
    
- Perform rapid turns.
    
- Generate airbursts.
    
- Launch feathers.
    
- Shield others with her wings.
    

She can now also do all of this while carrying a highly durable kinetic-energy distribution system on her wings.

---
