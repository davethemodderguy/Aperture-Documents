# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### ARMAMENTS & PERSONNEL AUTHORIZATION DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-ARM-4C-NS-218  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — PERSONNEL / ARMAMENTS  
**STATUS:** CLOSED  
**SUBJECT:** Elsher — Customized Night Stalker Display & Unauthorized Handling Incident

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Elsher has begun openly displaying his newly customized Night Stalker.

Neri has explicitly permitted him to do so.

The customized unit is still:

> **NS-006 — Elsher — MSAC**

Its authorization remains valid.

Its functional characteristics remain unchanged.

Its appearance, however, is now unique to Elsher.

The display period also produced an entirely predictable incident.

An MSAC Scout attempted to steal the Night Stalker while Elsher was changing.

The Scout successfully picked it up.

The Scout immediately discovered that **stealing Elsher's Night Stalker was a terrible idea.**

The Scout's hand was burned by the weapon's security system.

No serious injury occurred.

The weapon was recovered.

Neri subsequently added a warning:

> **“Don't think about stealing one. Your hand will be burned.”**

---

# 2. ELSHER'S CUSTOMIZED NIGHT STALKER

Elsher has begun referring to the weapon as:

> **“My Night Stalker.”**

This distinction is technically unnecessary.

However, given that Neri personally authorized the customization, C.O.R.E. considers the terminology acceptable.

The customized Night Stalker is visually distinct from the other authorized units.

It retains the original weapon's established functional identity while incorporating the cosmetic changes specifically requested by Elsher.

No additional Night Stalker was created.

No additional authorization was issued.

It remains:

> **NS-006.**

---

# 3. NERI'S APPROVAL

Elsher asked Neri whether he was permitted to show the customized weapon to other personnel.

Neri nodded.

She subsequently wrote:

> **“It's yours.”**

Elsher interpreted this as permission to display it.

Neri later clarified through another note:

> **“Just don't lose it.”**

Elsher:

> “I won't.”

C.O.R.E. considers this statement historically optimistic.

---

# 4. THE FLEX

Elsher began showing the customized Night Stalker to other MSAC personnel.

The reaction was immediate.

### MSAC OPERATOR:

“That's the customized one?”

### ELSHER:

“Yep.”

### OPERATOR:

“You actually got it?”

### ELSHER:

“Neri made it.”

### SECOND OPERATOR:

“That's unfair.”

### ELSHER:

“I found the hidden one.”

### SECOND OPERATOR:

“Fair.”

Elsher subsequently spent an unnecessary amount of time showing the customization to personnel.

C.O.R.E. has classified this behavior as:

> **Acceptable Flexing.**

Neri has apparently approved.

---

# 5. THE INCIDENT

Later that day, Elsher entered a changing area.

The Night Stalker was temporarily placed in its approved secured position.

An MSAC Scout noticed it.

The Scout looked at the weapon.

Then looked around.

Nobody appeared to be watching.

The Scout picked it up.

This was a mistake.

---

# 6. SECURITY RESPONSE

The moment the unauthorized individual attempted to handle the weapon, its security system activated.

The Scout immediately dropped it.

### SCOUT:

“OW!”

Personnel nearby turned toward him.

The Scout was holding his hand.

The Night Stalker remained on the floor.

### SECOND OPERATOR:

“What did you do?”

### SCOUT:

“I—”

Pause.

### SCOUT:

“...I was just looking.”

### SECOND OPERATOR:

“You were stealing it.”

### SCOUT:

“I was borrowing it.”

### SECOND OPERATOR:

“Without permission?”

### SCOUT:

“...Yes.”

---

# 7. ELSHER RETURNS

Elsher returned shortly afterward.

He saw the Scout.

Then the Night Stalker.

Then the Scout's hand.

### ELSHER:

“You touched it?”

The Scout nodded.

### ELSHER:

“Without asking?”

Another nod.

Elsher picked up the Night Stalker.

No defensive response occurred.

He looked at the Scout.

### ELSHER:

“Yeah.”

Pause.

### ELSHER:

“Don't do that.”

The Scout nodded immediately.

---

# 8. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri was informed of the incident.

She looked at the Scout.

Then at Elsher.

Then at the Night Stalker.

She wrote:

> **“Why did you steal it?”**

The Scout had no satisfactory answer.

Neri stared at him for several seconds.

She then wrote:

> **“Don't think about stealing one.”**

Pause.

Then:

> **“Your hand will be burned.”**

The Scout looked at his hand.

### SCOUT:

“Understood.”

Neri nodded.

---

# 9. SECURITY WARNING

The following notice has now been added to the Night Stalker authorization records:

> ### DO NOT HANDLE ANOTHER OPERATOR'S NIGHT STALKER.
> 
> Authorized Night Stalkers are individually secured.
> 
> Unauthorized handling may activate the weapon's security system.
> 
> Do not attempt to circumvent, disable, test, or otherwise interfere with the security system.
> 
> **Ask the owner.**

A handwritten addition from Neri appears underneath:

> **“It's not yours.”**

---

# 10. ELSHER'S RESPONSE

Elsher was asked whether he intended to stop displaying his customized Night Stalker following the incident.

He shook his head.

### ELSHER:

“No.”

### C.O.R.E. OFFICER:

“Why?”

Elsher looked at the weapon.

### ELSHER:

“Because Neri let me.”

Pause.

### ELSHER:

“And because it looks great.”

C.O.R.E. has no objection.

---

The incident resulted in:

- **No weapon loss**
    
- **No serious injury**
    
- **No additional authorization**
    
- **No modification to NS-006**
    
- **One embarrassed MSAC Scout**
    
- **One extremely visible warning**
    
- **Several MSAC personnel now aware that stealing Elsher's Night Stalker is a poor career decision**
    

The customized Night Stalker remains with Elsher.

Neri continues to permit him to display it.
