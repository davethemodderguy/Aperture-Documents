# 2. Anemic Shock

Neri's anemia does not behave like ordinary blood loss.

Her blood can progressively degrade while remaining inside her vessels.

Once functional blood reaches a critical threshold, her cardiovascular system may no longer be capable of sustaining itself.

Possible progression includes:

**Pallor → weakness → altered responsiveness → collapse → loss of consciousness → cardiovascular failure.**

Recovery is not guaranteed.

Previous episodes have been successfully reversed through Bao's intervention.

However, each severe episode carries the possibility of becoming fatal.

# 4. The Snow

The snow itself is not considered the primary cause of Neri's anemia.

Her blood degradation can occur regardless of temperature or activity.

However, exposure to severe cold creates additional complications.

A medically compromised Neri may:

- become increasingly lethargic,
    
- have difficulty communicating,
    
- lose coordination,
    
- become difficult to distinguish from someone merely resting,
    
- and lose consciousness while alone.
    

Consequently, the snow dramatically increases the danger of an otherwise spontaneous medical episode.
