# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **POST-STORM RECOVERY LOG**

# **BACK TO NORMAL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Internal Personnel  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse / SAFEHOUSE  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **STORM TERMINATED / NORMAL OPERATIONS RESTORED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The anomalous suppression storm ended as abruptly as it began.

Within hours, the affected region returned to normal.

Anomalous abilities functioned again.

Communications were restored.

Travel between the Blacksite and the treehouse became possible.

The Architects could manifest freely.

The treehouse could be supplied again.

And, eventually, the residents simply returned to their routines.

There was no grand ceremony.

No official celebration.

Just the quiet realization that everyone could finally go home.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

The damage was repaired.

Corin's detached arm was reattached and properly secured.

Her stitches were reinforced.

The empty pantry was restocked.

The kitchen was cleaned.

The blankets were replaced.

The scattered belongings were put back where they belonged.

The old blue notes remained on the table.

Their glow had returned.

One of them softly illuminated the room.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin slowly returned to her usual routine.

She wore her red wool beanie.

Her scarf concealed the scars around her neck.

Her coat concealed most of the damage to her body.

Her chalky lime hair was kept in place by the hairclip.

She ate.

She drank.

She slept.

She read.

She sat with Orphie.

Nothing about these things was extraordinary.

That was precisely why they mattered.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie returned to her usual role.

She made sure Corin's stitches remained secure.

She combed her hair.

She brought supplies.

She cooked.

She occasionally complained when Corin neglected herself.

Corin occasionally complained right back.

It was ordinary.

Comfortingly ordinary.

One evening:

> **Orphie:**  
> "You need to eat."

> **Corin:**  
> "I know."

> **Orphie:**  
> "Then eat."

> **Corin:**  
> "...Bossy."

> **Orphie:**  
> "Yes."

Corin ate.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light's blue glow returned completely.

She began visiting the treehouse normally again.

No more paper notes were necessary.

She could simply appear beside Corin.

Still, Corin kept the old notes.

Guiding Light noticed.

> **"You kept them?"**

Corin nodded.

> **"They helped."**

Guiding Light smiled.

> **"Good."**

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT**

Curious Light resumed his work.

He continued researching the storm from a safe distance.

This time, he was considerably more careful about treating Corin's condition as a research subject.

Neko occasionally joined him.

Their research sessions quickly became long discussions about Ether, anomalous phenomena, and completely unrelated inventions.

Miss Neko continued having to intervene when they became too absorbed.

Some things never changed.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi began visiting the treehouse again.

The relationship remained complicated.

There were still things that needed to be talked about.

There were still wounds that the storm had exposed.

But she came.

That mattered.

Corin did not immediately become completely comfortable around her.

Lumi understood.

She did not force it.

She simply stayed.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE**

The SAFEHOUSE returned to its familiar rhythm.

Nightshade returned to her usual routines.

Neko resumed his endless collection of suspiciously legitimate access permissions.

Miss Neko continued keeping him out of trouble.

Niko returned to the dormitory.

Belle and Endmin resumed their usual arrangement.

Frost returned to her normal activities.

Bao returned to the kitchen.

Taiga went back to sleeping on the rug.

Mart returned to being a happy little water blob in a top hat.

Astral resumed her normal responsibilities.

Lucy, Kent, and Remielle returned to their usual visits.

The SAFEHOUSE was noisy again.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE AT NIGHT**

One evening, Corin sat outside.

The forest was visible again.

No endless white.

No impossible wall of snow.

Just trees.

Stars.

Wind.

She looked upward.

Guiding Light appeared beside her.

> **"Beautiful, isn't it?"**

Corin nodded.

> **"Yeah."**

A pause.

> **"I couldn't see it when the storm was here."**

Guiding Light looked at her.

> **"You can now."**

Corin smiled.

> **"Yeah."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Nothing miraculous happened after the storm.

Corin did not suddenly forget what happened.

She did not instantly recover.

The notebook still exists.

The memories still exist.

The scars still exist.

But life continued.

And that was enough.

The kitchen became stocked again.

The lights came back.

The blue glow returned.

Orphie came home.

Lumi kept visiting.

The Architects returned.

The SAFEHOUSE became loud again.

Corin went back to eating food she did not technically need.

She went back to sleeping.

She went back to wearing her favorite hat.

She went back to being herself.

For once, **"back to normal"** did not mean pretending nothing happened.

It simply meant that everyone was there again.

**End of Post-Storm Recovery Log**
