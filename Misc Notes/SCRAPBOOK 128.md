### **VISITOR ARRIVAL LOG**

## **SUBJECT: KEITH ("KENT") & REMIELLE ("CINDER")**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Frequent visitors Keith, more commonly known as **Kent**, and Remielle, known by the callsign **Cinder**, arrived at Heaven's Blacksite for a scheduled visit.

Both individuals are long-time associates of Belle Merryweather and are recognized as trusted personnel with unrestricted access to Project SAFEHOUSE.

Unlike most visitors, Kent and Cinder require little supervision.

They know the facility almost as well as its residents.

---

# **KEITH ("KENT")**

**Role:** Belle Merryweather's Bodyguard

Kent is a veteran with years of military and government service.

His demeanor is relaxed and approachable, but personnel are reminded that this should not be mistaken for inexperience.

He possesses exceptional situational awareness and remains attentive even during casual conversation.

Kent rarely speaks unless he has something meaningful to add.

He is often found making coffee in the SAFEHOUSE kitchen or quietly helping with routine tasks without being asked.

When questioned about why he keeps helping around the house, Kent simply answered:

> "It's still a home."

---

# **REMIELLE ("CINDER")**

**Role:** Belle Merryweather's Secretary

Cinder manages much of Belle's scheduling, correspondence, and administrative work.

She is organized, efficient, and known for keeping Belle's paperwork under control.

Outside of work, however, she is considerably more relaxed than many personnel expect.

She frequently joins residents for meals and has become a familiar face during SAFEHOUSE movie nights.

Her military background also means she is perfectly comfortable adapting to unusual situations.

Including sleeping on an office floor if necessary.

---

# **MEETING CORIN**

Corin had met both visitors before, though only briefly.

This time was different.

Rather than treating her as someone fragile, Kent greeted her exactly as he greeted everyone else.

> "Morning, Corin."

She quietly replied,

> "...Morning."

Cinder walked over moments later carrying several folders.

> "Accounting?"

Corin nodded.

Without another word, the two sat down together and began comparing expense reports.

The conversation lasted nearly an hour.

Almost none of it involved Corin's condition.

---

# **LUMI'S OBSERVATION**

Lumi watched the interactions from across the lounge.

No one stared.

No one hesitated.

No one looked uncomfortable.

Kent casually asked Corin if she wanted coffee.

Cinder asked whether Norma's latest ammunition request was really that expensive.

Corin sighed.

> "...Unfortunately."

The room laughed.

Even Corin smiled.

Lumi quietly realized something.

Neither Kent nor Cinder had spent the afternoon trying to cheer Corin up.

They had simply included her.

Sometimes...

That was enough.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Kent and Cinder remain frequent visitors to Heaven's Blacksite and continue to be welcomed by the residents of Project SAFEHOUSE.

Their presence has provided Corin with two more people who treat her not as a medical case or an anomaly, but simply as another member of the household.

For Corin, that ordinary kindness continues to make an extraordinary difference.
