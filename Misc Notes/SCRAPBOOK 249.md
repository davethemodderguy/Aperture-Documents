# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – RESTRICTED PSYCHOLOGICAL DOSSIER**

### **SUBJECT: DIALYN – PERSONAL HISTORY CLARIFICATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 – Executive & Psychological Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

This addendum corrects a longstanding misunderstanding contained within earlier personnel records.

Previous documentation referred to Dialyn's "childhood pet."

That description is inaccurate.

The individual in question was not an animal.

She was Dialyn's younger sister.

A sheep Thiren.

The error originated from Dialyn herself.

---

# **THE "PET"**

During psychological interviews, Dialyn repeatedly referred to her younger sister as:

> "...My little pet."

Investigators initially interpreted the statement literally.

It was only after recovering fragmented records from Dialyn's home village that the misunderstanding was identified.

According to surviving accounts, Dialyn's younger sister delighted in being treated like the family's "little sheep."

She frequently insisted that Dialyn call her a pet.

The sisters turned it into a game.

Dialyn would pretend to "feed" her.

Brush her wool.

Pat her head.

Her sister would happily bleat in response, much to the amusement of their parents during happier days.

The nickname endured for years.

Even after those happier days ended.

---

# **CHILDHOOD**

Recovered testimony indicates both sisters experienced prolonged parental abuse and severe emotional trauma throughout much of their childhood.

Psychological Services believe the sisters survived largely because they relied upon one another.

Dialyn became fiercely protective.

Her younger sister remained one of the few people capable of making Dialyn genuinely laugh.

---

# **THE HOLLOW**

When the Hollow appeared near their village, panic spread rapidly.

As previously documented, Dialyn was selected as the village's sacrifice and thrown into the anomaly.

She survived.

Her sister did not.

The Hollow consumed the settlement before any organized evacuation could occur.

No members of Dialyn's family survived.

---

# **ETHER RESISTANCE**

Research Division has re-examined the Hollow Incident following confirmation of Dialyn's survival.

Current findings indicate Dialyn possesses an exceptionally high natural resistance to Ether corruption.

The origin of this resistance remains unknown.

Measurements taken throughout her employment consistently demonstrate anomalous tolerance well beyond known human limits.

Research has repeatedly failed to explain why.

Neko's official note on the matter reads:

> "She survived exposure that should have been universally fatal."

> "...That alone deserves investigation."

At Dialyn's request, those investigations remain limited.

---

# **THE MISUNDERSTANDING**

When informed that personnel believed she had once owned a pet sheep, Dialyn laughed so hard she reportedly needed several minutes to compose herself.

After calming down, she quietly clarified:

> "...She wasn't my pet."

A smile crossed her face.

> "...She was my little sister."

Another pause.

> "...She just really liked pretending."

That was all she said.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Dialyn continues to keep several sheep-themed items in her office.

To most visitors, they are simple decorations reflecting a fondness for sheep.

Only a small number of people know they also serve as quiet reminders of the younger sister who never had the chance to grow up.

Dialyn has never asked anyone to stop giving her sheep-themed gifts.

She treasures every one.

Not because they remind her of loss.

But because they remind her that, for a little while, she got to be an older sister.

And those memories are the ones she chooses to keep.
