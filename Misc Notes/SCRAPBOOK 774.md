# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE / PERSONNEL CARE DIVISION**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — SCOOTER MODIFICATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — PERSONNEL CARE / MINOR ENGINEERING ANOMALY  
**Status:** Active  
**Subject Condition:** Weak / Fatigued / Mobile with Assistance

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Alice remains physically weak.

Walking for extended periods continues to exhaust her quickly.

As a result, she frequently uses the scooter previously given to her by Neko.

The scooter allows Alice to travel through the Blacksite without expending as much physical energy.

Recently, however—

the scooter became noticeably faster.

Nobody knows exactly how.

---

# **THE CHANGE**

Personnel first noticed the difference when Alice passed through a corridor at considerably higher speed than normal.

An employee looked up.

> "...Wasn't Alice's scooter slower than that?"

Another employee turned around.

Alice was already several meters away.

Her white beanie remained firmly in place.

Her ears were completely horizontal from the movement.

She looked extremely pleased with herself.

---

# **ALICE'S EXPLANATION**

Neko eventually asked:

> "What did you do to it?"

Alice smiled.

> "Made it faster."

Neko inspected the scooter.

He found several modifications.

Some were clearly mechanical.

Some were not.

One component had been rearranged in a way Neko described as:

> "...Technically possible."

Another component was:

> "...Not supposed to fit there."

A third modification had no immediately identifiable purpose.

The scooter nevertheless worked.

Very well.

---

# **SPEED**

The scooter now reaches speeds significantly higher than its original configuration.

Alice appears capable of controlling it comfortably.

She does not appear to have increased the scooter's speed randomly.

The modifications seem specifically designed to improve acceleration and sustained movement.

---

# **THE PROBLEM**

Alice is still Alice.

This means the faster scooter has introduced a new problem:

**She is now capable of getting herself into places much faster than before.**

Alice may be physically weak.

She may tire easily.

She may faint.

She may become distracted.

But the scooter does not get tired.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin discovered the modification after hearing a faint:

> "Wheeeee!"

followed by Alice rapidly passing through the hallway.

Corin stared.

> "...Alice?"

Alice stopped.

Barely.

> "Hi, Big sis!"

> "Why are you going so fast?"

Alice smiled.

> "Because scooter fast!"

Corin sighed.

> "Slow down."

Alice immediately slowed.

> "...Okay."

---

# **BELLE**

Belle thinks the new speed is wonderful.

> "Can I race you?"

Alice immediately answered:

> "Yes!"

Corin, from nearby:

> "No."

Both rabbits looked disappointed.

---

# **NEKO'S ASSESSMENT**

Neko inspected the scooter again.

He eventually concluded:

> "It's actually pretty good."

Miss Neko looked at him.

> "You sound surprised."

> "I am."

He looked toward Alice.

> "She shouldn't have been able to do half of this."

Alice smiled proudly.

> "Alice smart."

Neko nodded.

> "...Apparently."

---

# **SAFETY LIMIT**

The scooter has been fitted with a controllable speed limiter.

Alice is allowed to use it.

She is **not** allowed to operate it at maximum speed inside crowded corridors.

Alice agreed.

She then asked:

> "Outside?"

Corin:

> "We'll talk about it."

Alice:

> "...Okay."

---

# **WHY THE SCOOTER MATTERS**

The scooter is not simply a toy.

For Alice, it is mobility.

She can travel without exhausting herself.

She can visit friends.

She can follow people.

She can explore the Blacksite.

She can return to SAFEHOUSE without needing someone to carry her.

Most importantly—

it gives her a small degree of independence.

---

# **THE NEW RULE**

Alice is allowed to use her scooter.

Personnel should not take it away simply because she is small or fragile.

However:

**Someone must know where she is going.**

**Someone must know who she is following.**

**Alice must not ride into restricted areas.**

**Speed must be appropriate for the environment.**

And if Alice becomes tired—

she stops.

Or someone carries her.

---

# **ONE FINAL OBSERVATION**

Alice remains weak.

Her body has not suddenly become stronger.

She still gets tired.

She still needs rest.

She still needs people around her.

But now she has a solution.

A little scooter.

A faster little scooter.

And apparently enough engineering intuition to make Neko question his own profession.

Alice rode past the SAFEHOUSE entrance one afternoon.

Her ears were flapping backward from the speed.

Her coat was fluttering.

Her white beanie somehow stayed perfectly secure.

She slowed down beside Corin.

> "Big sis!"

> "Yes?"

Alice smiled.

> **"Look how fast Alice can go!"**

Corin smiled back.

> "I see."

Alice accelerated away.

> "...Slowly!"

Alice immediately braked.

> "Okay!"

**End of CORE-SAFEHOUSE / PERSONNEL CARE DIVISION**
