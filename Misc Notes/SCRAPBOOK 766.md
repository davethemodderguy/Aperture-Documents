### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE / PERSONNEL CARE DIVISION**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — BIRTHDAY INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — CRITICAL CHILD SAFETY  
**Date:** **1 November**  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE Exterior  
**Status:** SUBJECT RECOVERED

---

# **THE ASSUMPTION**

For several hours, everyone inside the SAFEHOUSE believed Alice simply hadn't come to her birthday party.

They eventually accepted that she might not have known about it.

Or perhaps she had been too tired.

Nobody knew.

The party continued without her.

The present was put away.

The cake was eaten.

The decorations remained.

What nobody knew was that Alice **had tried to come.**

---

# **OUTSIDE**

Alice had been left outside in heavy rain.

She was wearing her usual clothing.

White beanie.

Hoodie.

Coat.

Boots.

Her right eye remained protected beneath its bandage and eyepatch.

Her rabbit ears had fallen naturally under gravity, becoming soaked against the sides of her head.

She knew where everyone was.

She knew there was a party.

She wanted to go inside.

---

# **THE WALK**

Alice began walking toward the SAFEHOUSE.

Slowly.

The rain was extremely heavy.

Her clothes became saturated.

Her boots became heavy with water.

Her coat stopped providing much protection.

She continued anyway.

She could see the lights.

She could probably hear people inside.

She was close.

Very close.

---

# **THE BODY GAVE OUT**

Alice stopped.

Her knees weakened.

She tried to take another step.

She couldn't.

She fell into the wet ground.

Her body had simply reached its limit.

She did not call for help.

She could not.

She did not use a whistle.

She did not use a flashlight.

She did not bang on the door.

She simply remained there.

In the rain.

---

# **THE DOOR**

The SAFEHOUSE entrance was not far away.

Close enough to see.

Close enough that someone looking outside would have noticed her.

Alice could not reach it.

She tried once more.

Her arm moved slightly.

Then stopped.

She remained where she had fallen.

---

# **INSIDE**

At the same time—

everyone was celebrating.

Quietly.

Without her.

Corin looked at the empty chair.

She had no idea Alice was only a short distance away.

Bao and Corin eventually ate the cake.

The present was placed into storage.

The party slowly came to an end.

---

# **DISCOVERY**

It was only afterward that someone opened the exterior door.

A small shape was visible through the rain.

Corin stopped.

Her expression changed immediately.

> "...Alice?"

She ran outside.

---

# **CORIN**

Alice was soaked.

Her white beanie was drenched.

Her ears hung completely limp.

Her coat was saturated.

Her boots were covered in mud.

Her small body was trembling.

She was barely conscious.

Corin picked her up immediately.

Alice's head fell against her shoulder.

Her body was frighteningly light.

---

# **THE PARTY**

Corin carried Alice inside.

Everyone went silent.

Belle immediately ran toward them.

Star froze.

Bao stood up.

Nightshade was already moving.

Nobody cared about the decorations anymore.

Nobody cared about the cake.

Nobody cared about the present.

Alice was finally inside.

---

# **ALICE**

Alice slowly opened her eyes.

She looked around.

She saw the decorations.

The table.

The cake.

The people.

Her birthday.

She had made it.

Barely.

Her eyes became slightly wider.

She raised one hand.

Corin held it.

Alice weakly pointed toward the cake.

Then toward herself.

Corin understood.

> "...Yes, Alice."

> "It's your birthday."

Alice smiled.

---

# **THE PRESENT**

Someone retrieved the present immediately.

It was still wrapped.

Still labeled.

**ALICE.**

Belle placed it in her lap.

Alice stared at it.

She didn't open it immediately.

She was too tired.

She simply held it.

---

# **CORIN'S REALIZATION**

Corin eventually understood what had happened.

Alice hadn't missed her birthday.

She had been trying to attend it.

She had walked through the rain because she wanted to be there.

She had gotten within sight of the SAFEHOUSE.

And her body had given out before she could reach the door.

Corin held Alice closer.

She whispered:

> "...You really wanted to come."

Alice nodded weakly.

---

# **THE CAKE**

Bao brought over the remaining cake.

Corin looked at the two partially eaten pieces from earlier.

Nobody cared.

A fresh piece was cut.

It was placed in front of Alice.

Alice looked at it.

Then at everyone.

She smiled.

---

# **HAPPY BIRTHDAY**

The room became quiet.

Then everyone spoke together.

> **"Happy birthday, Alice."**

Alice's ears remained limp.

Her eye was hidden beneath its patch.

Her clothes were still damp.

She was exhausted.

But she was surrounded by people.

And this time—

she wasn't alone.

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

Alice had not forgotten her birthday.

She had not ignored the party.

She had not decided she didn't want to come.

She wanted to be there.

She tried.

She walked through heavy rain while already severely weakened.

She made it almost all the way.

And then her body simply stopped.

She was found before the situation became fatal.

The present was returned to her.

The cake was replaced.

The decorations remained.

And Alice finally experienced the birthday everyone had prepared for her.

Not because she managed to reach the party herself.

But because someone finally noticed her before she disappeared from sight.
