# SAFEHOUSE BEHAVIORAL NOTICE

## SUBJECT: JANE DOE

### BLACKSITE WORKER INTERACTION GUIDANCE

**Classification:** Internal  
**Distribution:** Blacksite Personnel  
**Resident Status:** SAFEHOUSE — PERMANENT  
**Threat Assessment:** Context-Dependent  
**Primary Recommendation:** Do not panic.

## 1. GENERAL NOTICE

Jane Doe has been fully integrated into SAFEHOUSE following the completion of her Woodland Coorp infiltration.

Personnel unfamiliar with Jane should be advised that her behavior differs considerably depending on who she is interacting with.

### SAFEHOUSE RESIDENTS

Jane is:

- Friendly.
    
- Affectionate.
    
- Teasing.
    
- Protective.
    
- Social.
    
- Extremely comfortable around children and junior residents.
    
- Known to distribute food, particularly cheesecake and other sweets.
    
- Known to use excessive pet names.
    

### NON-SAFEHOUSE BLACKSITE PERSONNEL

Jane is:

- Still generally friendly.
    
- Considerably more intimidating.
    
- Very fond of psychological games.
    
- Extremely observant.
    
- Fully aware that her reputation precedes her.
    
- Completely willing to let someone scare themselves.
    

This distinction has resulted in a peculiar Blacksite phenomenon:

> **Jane has done very little to frighten most workers.**

> **Most workers frighten themselves.**

---

# 2. THE TAIL

Jane possesses a long Rat Thiren tail.

It is considerably longer than that of an ordinary rat and possesses sufficient strength and flexibility to wrap around and restrain a person.

The terminal portion of the tail forms a pointed, arrow-like tip.

This combination makes Jane's tail both a mobility tool and a potentially dangerous close-range restraint weapon.

Personnel are therefore reminded:

> **DO NOT GRAB JANE'S TAIL.**

Not because Jane will necessarily retaliate.

Because there is absolutely no reason to find out.

---

# 3. THE "TAIL INCIDENT"

The following incident is frequently cited during new-worker orientation.

A newly hired Blacksite worker encountered Jane in a corridor.

Jane stopped.

The worker stopped.

Jane's tail slowly moved behind her.

The worker watched it.

The tail curled around her leg.

The worker took one step backward.

The tail followed.

The worker looked at Jane.

Jane smiled.

> **Worker:** "...Is that supposed to happen?"

> **Jane:** "What?"

The tail slowly curled around the worker's ankle.

> **Worker:** "That."

Jane looked behind herself.

> **Jane:** "Oh."

Pause.

> **Jane:** "Yes."

The worker immediately froze.

Jane released them.

> **Jane:** "You looked scared."

> **Worker:** "I WAS."

Jane laughed and continued walking.

No threat had been made.

No injury occurred.

The worker nevertheless submitted a request to transfer departments.

---

# 4. JANE'S FAVORITE GAME

Jane has discovered that merely allowing personnel to notice her tail is often enough.

She does not need to explain what it can do.

She does not need to demonstrate it.

She does not need to threaten anyone.

She simply lets people notice.

A worker will glance down.

See the tail.

See the pointed tip.

Look at Jane.

Jane will notice them looking.

Then smile.

That is usually enough.

---

# 5. WHY THIS WORKS

Jane is exceptionally skilled at intimidation and psychological manipulation.

Her greatest advantage is not necessarily physical force.

It is **anticipation**.

She understands that people will often construct a considerably worse scenario in their own heads than anything she would actually do.

Jane therefore rarely needs to say:

> _"I could restrain you."_

The worker has already reached that conclusion.

---

# 6. SAFEHOUSE EXCEPTION

There is, however, a very obvious difference between Jane's treatment of Blacksite personnel and SAFEHOUSE residents.

When around SAFEHOUSE:

> **Jane's tail is generally harmless.**

Neri has been observed sitting beside Jane while Jane's tail casually rests around the back of a chair.

Alice has attempted to grab it.

Jane allowed it.

Mart has apparently used it as an excuse to ask questions about Rat Thiren anatomy.

Star and Nightshade have become accustomed to it.

Corin barely reacts anymore.

Bao does not appear concerned.

Frost has seen Jane use it to retrieve objects from across a room.

# 8. BLACKSITE WORKER ETIQUETTE

Personnel encountering Jane are advised:

### DO:

- Treat her normally.
    
- Speak respectfully.
    
- Remember that she is a SAFEHOUSE resident.
    
- Maintain awareness of your surroundings.
    
- Ignore her attempts to make you nervous.
    
- Accept that she is probably aware of you before you notice her.
    

### DO NOT:

- Grab her tail.
    
- Attempt to test the pointed tip.
    
- Challenge her to an intimidation contest.
    
- Ask how many ways she can restrain someone.
    
- Ask whether she is watching you.
    
- Assume that because she is smiling, she hasn't noticed something.
    
- Bully SAFEHOUSE residents in her presence.
    

That final recommendation is particularly important.

# 9. THE BLACKSITE'S UNOFFICIAL RULE

A handwritten note has appeared on several internal noticeboards:

> **"If Jane Doe is looking at you, you're probably fine."**

A second line was added underneath:

> **"If Jane Doe is smiling at you, you're probably still fine."**

> **"If her tail is moving toward you, reconsider your life choices."**

The author of the third line remains unidentified.

Jane has denied writing it.

She also found it hilarious.

# 10. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Jane Doe is not considered an active threat to Blacksite personnel.

She is, however, an exceptionally capable intelligence operative with:

- Advanced extraction skills.
    
- Exceptional intimidation ability.
    
- Advanced psychological manipulation.
    
- High agility and mobility.
    
- A highly flexible prehensile tail.
    
- A pointed tail tip.
    
- Extensive experience operating undercover.
    
- An apparent enjoyment of watching people become nervous.
    

For SAFEHOUSE residents, Jane is family.

For hostile intelligence personnel, Jane is a problem.

For an unsuspecting Blacksite employee who turns a corner and discovers a smiling Rat Thiren watching them from the other end of the hallway:

**Jane is probably just saying hello.**

Probably.
