# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. — CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### SAFEHOUSE ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY RECORD

**SUBJECT:** JANE DOE  
**TEMPORARY DESIGNATION:** JOHN DOE  
**INCIDENT TYPE:** Anomalous Physiological Transformation  
**TRIGGER:** Unprocessed Strawberry  
**EXPECTED DURATION:** ~24 HOURS  
**CURRENT STATUS:** TEMPORARY / REVERSIBLE

---

## 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

Jane Doe has once again demonstrated her unusual physiological response to unprocessed strawberries.

Following consumption of a raw strawberry, Jane underwent her established anomalous transformation.

The transformation was not gradual.

Within a short period:

**JANE DOE → PHYSIOLOGICAL TRANSFORMATION → JOHN DOE**

For the following approximately twenty-four hours, Jane's normal female physiology was replaced by male physiology.

Her memories remained intact.

Her personality remained intact.

Her intelligence remained intact.

Her skills remained intact.

Her relationships remained intact.

There was no evidence of a second consciousness.

There was no evidence of duplication.

There was no evidence that Jane had been replaced.

**John Doe is Jane Doe.**

The name is simply used to distinguish the temporary physiological state.

---

# 2. THE STRAWBERRY

The triggering object was an ordinary-looking unprocessed strawberry.

Processed strawberry products remain unaffected.

Jam: safe.  
Strawberry-flavored foods: safe.  
Strawberry drinks: safe.  
Strawberry desserts: safe.

Raw strawberry:

> **Absolutely not.**

The strawberry was subsequently classified as an anomalous dietary hazard specifically for Jane.

Jane's immediate reaction upon realizing what had happened was reportedly:

> **"Oh, you've got to be kidding me."**

---

# 3. TEMPORARY DESIGNATION

For administrative purposes, the transformed state is designated:

> **JOHN DOE**

This is not a new person.

It is not Jane's brother.

It is not a clone.

It is not an alter.

It is not an independent entity.

John possesses Jane's complete memories.

He knows everyone Jane knows.

He remembers everything Jane remembers.

He retains Jane's professional experience and personality.

The most significant difference is physiological.

His voice is deeper, his hair is somewhat messier, his chest is slightly flatter, and his body has male physiology.

Everything else is recognizably Jane.

---

# 4. NERI'S FIRST ENCOUNTER

Neri was one of the first SAFEHOUSE residents to encounter John.

She had not yet been properly informed about the strawberry anomaly.

She entered the common area.

She saw an unfamiliar tall man standing where Jane normally would be.

Neri stopped.

John looked at her.

Neri looked at John.

Several seconds passed.

**NERI:**  
"...Who are you?"

**JOHN:**  
"Jane."

Neri immediately backed away.

**NERI:**  
"No."

**JOHN:**  
"...Yes."

**NERI:**  
"Jane is a girl."

**JOHN:**  
"Usually."

Neri stared.

**NERI:**  
"What did you do to Jane?!"

**JOHN:**  
"Nothing."

**NERI:**  
"WHERE IS SHE?!"

John pointed at himself.

**JOHN:**  
"I'm Jane."

Neri's expression became increasingly concerned.

---

# 5. EXPLANATION

Neri was eventually informed of Jane's strawberry anomaly.

The explanation did little to immediately reassure her.

**NERI:**  
"So you ate a strawberry..."

**JOHN:**  
"Yes."

**NERI:**  
"...and became a man."

**JOHN:**  
"Yes."

**NERI:**  
"And Jane is still there."

**JOHN:**  
"I'm Jane."

**NERI:**  
"..."

**JOHN:**  
"You can stop looking at me like I'm a Woodland infiltrator."

**NERI:**  
"I don't trust strawberries anymore."

**JOHN:**  
"Good."

---

# 6. INITIAL FEAR

Neri remained noticeably uncomfortable around John during the first several hours.

This was not because John behaved threateningly.

Quite the opposite.

John behaved exactly like Jane.

He still teased people.

He still carried himself like an intelligence operative.

He still enjoyed psychologically terrifying unsuspecting Blacksite personnel.

He still had the same sense of humor.

He still knew exactly how Neri reacted to certain things.

That somehow made the situation stranger.

Neri could recognize Jane's personality coming from someone who looked completely different.

At one point:

**JOHN:**  
"My Cute Seal."

Neri immediately froze.

**NERI:**  
"...That's definitely Jane."

**JOHN:**  
"Finally."

---

# 7. THE FIRST CONVERSATION

Later that day, Neri sat beside John in the cafeteria.

There was a considerable amount of empty space between them.

John noticed.

**JOHN:**  
"Still scared of me?"

**NERI:**  
"...A little."

**JOHN:**  
"Why?"

**NERI:**  
"You look different."

**JOHN:**  
"Fair."

**NERI:**  
"And your voice."

**JOHN:**  
"Also fair."

**NERI:**  
"And you're really tall."

**JOHN:**  
"Jane was already really tall."

**NERI:**  
"Not like this."

John laughed.

Neri eventually laughed too.

---

# 8. THE CHANGE

The fear did not disappear immediately.

It simply became familiarity.

Neri discovered that John still:

- knew exactly how she liked her cake;
    
- understood when she was trying to hide being tired;
    
- teased her about being stubborn;
    
- knew when she wanted company without asking for it;
    
- remembered their private conversation;
    
- still called her **"My Cute Seal"** specifically because it annoyed her.
    

At some point Neri stopped referring to John as "that guy."

She simply started saying:

> **"Jane."**

Then corrected herself.

> **"...John."**

John found this hilarious.

---

# 9. JOHN AND NERI

By the evening, the two were sitting together eating cake.

Neri was no longer sitting several feet away.

She had moved beside him.

John looked at her.

**JOHN:**  
"So."

**NERI:**  
"What?"

**JOHN:**  
"Still scared?"

Neri took another bite of cake.

**NERI:**  
"No."

**JOHN:**  
"Good."

**NERI:**  
"But when you turn back into Jane, I'm telling her how weird you look."

**JOHN:**  
"That's fair."

**NERI:**  
"And I'm telling her you ate my cake."

**JOHN:**  
"That is slander."

**NERI:**  
"You did."

**JOHN:**  
"I was hungry."

Neri smiled.

---

# 10. SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION

The incident produced an unexpected social result.

Neri initially perceived John as an unfamiliar adult occupying Jane's place.

Within hours, however, she became comfortable enough to spend time with him normally.

The determining factor was apparently not physical appearance.

It was recognition.

John still had Jane's mannerisms.

Jane's humor.

Jane's memories.

Jane's protective instincts.

And, most importantly:

**Jane's relationship with Neri.**

Once Neri understood that the person in front of her was still the same person she trusted, the fear largely disappeared.

---

# 11. RETURN TO JANE DOE

Approximately twenty-four hours after the initial transformation, the anomaly ended naturally.

John Doe reverted to Jane Doe.

No intervention was required.

No residual physiological changes were detected.

Jane immediately resumed her normal identity and appearance.

Neri looked at her.

Then looked at the strawberry sitting on the table.

Then looked back at Jane.

**NERI:**  
"...So you're Jane again."

**JANE:**  
"Unfortunately."

**NERI:**  
"I liked John."

Jane paused.

**JANE:**  
"...Excuse me?"

Neri smiled.

**NERI:**  
"He was nice."

**JANE:**  
"I _am_ nice."

**NERI:**  
"Sometimes."

Jane stared at her.

Neri laughed.

---

# 12. FINAL ASSESSMENT

The strawberry incident caused no permanent harm.

Jane's transformation was temporary and fully reversible.

More importantly, it demonstrated something rather amusing about Neri's relationship with Jane.

Neri was initially frightened because she saw someone unfamiliar.

She eventually realized:

> **The person had never actually changed.**

Only the body had.

By the end of the twenty-four-hour period, Neri had accepted John as Jane without difficulty.

And apparently acquired a new piece of ammunition for teasing Jane.
