### **SAFEHOUSE PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

# **BELLE — THE ACCIDENT WASN'T HER FAULT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Location:** Treehouse  
**Subjects:** Belle Merryweather / Corin  
**Status:** Emotional Distress / Mutual Support

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Belle eventually broke down.

The guilt she had been carrying became too much.

Her legs gave out.

She stumbled forward and fell against Corin's chest, immediately wrapping her arms around her.

Belle began crying.

Hard.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin instinctively caught her.

The movement caused pain.

Her injured left wing shifted.

Corin winced.

But she did not pull away.

Instead, the damaged wing slowly moved around Belle's back.

It could barely support its own weight.

It was still badly injured.

Yet Corin carefully positioned it over Belle like a blanket.

---

# **THE EMBRACE**

Belle's face was pressed against Corin's chest.

Her shoulders shook as she cried.

Corin held her with one arm.

Her right wing remained folded behind her.

Her left wing rested gently across Belle's back.

Neither spoke for several moments.

Eventually, Corin quietly said:

> **"You're okay."**

Belle continued crying.

> **"I'm sorry..."**

Corin shook her head.

> **"Don't be."**

---

# **BELLE'S GUILT**

Belle could barely get the words out.

> **"Your wing..."**

Corin looked down at her.

> **"It'll heal."**

> **"But I hurt you."**

> **"You didn't."**

Belle cried harder.

Corin gently stroked her hair.

> **"It was an accident."**

---

# **THE INJURED WING**

The left wing trembled slightly around Belle.

It hurt.

Corin's face tightened from the pain.

Still, she kept it there.

She knew Belle needed the embrace.

The wing that had been damaged protecting Belle was now protecting her again.

This time from nothing physical.

Just the feeling that she was alone with her guilt.

---

# **ORPHIE'S OBSERVATION**

Orphie watched quietly from nearby.

She did not interrupt.

There was no need.

Corin had already made her decision.

She would comfort Belle.

Even while hurting herself.

---

# **BELLE FINALLY SPEAKS**

After several minutes, Belle whispered:

> **"...Why are you still comforting me?"**

Corin answered immediately.

> **"Because you're sad."**

Belle:

> **"But you're the one who's hurt."**

Corin's expression softened.

> **"That doesn't mean you have to hurt too."**

Belle finally stopped trying to hold back her tears.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Belle fell against Corin's chest crying.

Corin caught her.

Her injured left wing, barely strong enough to move, slowly settled across Belle's back.

It hurt Corin.

She didn't care.

The same wing that had shielded Belle from the explosion now shielded her from her own guilt.

Corin rested her head against Belle's.

> **"I'm still here."**

And for the moment, that was enough.

**End of SAFEHOUSE Psychological Observation**
