# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Following SPAWN's abandonment of its previous spiritual operations and subsequent doctrinal reforms under **Guiding Light**, Aperture personnel conducted a limited inspection of several previously inaccessible areas within SPAWN Headquarters.

The inspection was not intended to be an investigation into SPAWN personnel.

It was intended to determine whether anything dangerous had been left behind.

Something had.

Behind a concealed section of wall, Aperture Operators discovered a previously undocumented chamber.

The chamber resembled a small prison cell.

Inside was a young female Thiren.

She was alive.

She was severely isolated.

And she had apparently been held there **against her will.**

The subject identified herself as:

> **Neri**

She stated that she was also perfectly comfortable being called:

> **“Seal.”**

Neri was subsequently identified as a **Seal Thiren.**

The cell contained little more than a small water tub and basic provisions.

The circumstances surrounding her imprisonment were later determined to involve a **deranged cultist operating within the former SPAWN structure**, rather than an official SPAWN directive.

Neri was removed from the chamber and placed under Aperture protection.

She was later introduced to SAFEHOUSE.

She immediately formed a strong friendship with **Bao.**

This was not unexpected.

Bao is a Whale Thiren.

Neri is a Seal Thiren.

Both apparently reached the conclusion that:

> **“Oh. You're like me.”**

---

# 2. DISCOVERY

The chamber was discovered during a structural inspection of the former SPAWN headquarters.

An Aperture Operator noticed that one section of wall did not correspond with the building's architectural records.

Neko was called to inspect it.

He immediately determined that the structure had been deliberately concealed.

> **NEKO:**  
> “This isn't on their floor plan.”

> **OPERATOR:**  
> “Hidden room?”

> **NEKO:**  
> “Yep.”

Neko opened the concealed entrance.

The atmosphere changed immediately.

The chamber was quiet.

There were no active rituals.

No weapons.

No anomalous equipment.

Just a small cell.

And someone inside.

---

# 5. THE CELL

The chamber had clearly been constructed or modified for long-term confinement.

Its contents were minimal.

### RECOVERED ITEMS

- Small water tub
    
- Basic bedding
    
- Several empty food containers
    
- Drinking container
    
- Damaged children's book
    
- Assorted scraps of cloth
    
- One small wooden toy
    
- No obvious communication device
    

There was no proper recreational area.

The water tub appeared to have been intended to provide the minimum amount of water necessary for Neri's comfort.

The cell's design strongly suggested that its occupant was not expected to leave.

---

# 6. THE CAPTOR

Neri's account indicated that she had been imprisoned by an individual she described as:

> **“Crazy.”**

Further investigation identified the individual as a **deranged cultist** operating independently within the broader former SPAWN environment.

The person had apparently become obsessed with Neri and imprisoned her for reasons that remain only partially understood.

The cultist's actions were not authorized by SPAWN leadership.

This distinction is important.

### C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

There is currently **no evidence that SPAWN's reformed leadership knowingly authorized Neri's imprisonment.**

The chamber appears to have been deliberately hidden from the organization's ordinary personnel.

# 7. NERI'S CONDITION

Neri was physically stable when discovered.

However, prolonged confinement had clearly affected her.

She was initially cautious around unfamiliar people.

She was particularly uncomfortable when doors were closed behind her.

Aperture personnel therefore avoided forcing her into enclosed spaces.

Bao remained with her during the initial recovery period.

This proved extremely effective.

Neri became noticeably more relaxed whenever Bao was nearby.
