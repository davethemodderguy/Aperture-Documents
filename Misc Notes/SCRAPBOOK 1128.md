# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The **Night Stalker Program has officially concluded.**

After an extended authorization process, ten individually numbered Night Stalkers have now been assigned.

The final unit:

> **NS-010 — “REGALIA”**

has been authorized to the **MSAC Commander**.

This authorization marks the formal end of the Night Stalker program.

No eleventh unit will be produced.

No replacement program is currently planned.

No further Night Stalker authorization competitions will be conducted.

The collection is complete.

---

# 2. FINAL AUTHORIZATION

**UNIT:** NS-010  
**DESIGNATION:** Regalia  
**AUTHORIZED USER:** MSAC Commander  
**STATUS:** OPERATIONAL

Regalia was removed from permanent museum display following final authorization.

The gold presentation case was opened.

The weapon was formally transferred under MSAC custody.

The Commander was required to acknowledge the standard Night Stalker conditions of ownership and the weapon's historical significance.

The authorization was then recorded.

---

# 3. THE FINAL CEREMONY

Neri was present.

For the first time, she was not in plush form.

She had returned to her normal human form.

Her oversized light-azure coat covered most of her hands.

Her glasses sat properly on her face.

Her crossed hairclips remained in place.

She stood quietly beside the display case.

The museum was unusually crowded.

MSAC personnel were present.

Several Night Stalker owners attended.

Neko was present.

Jane Doe was present.

Bao was present.

Elsher and Don were present.

The Commander approached the display.

Neri looked at Regalia.

Then at the Commander.

She held out the authorization document.

> **NERI:**  
> “You earned it.”

The Commander accepted it.

> **MSAC COMMANDER:**  
> “Thank you.”

Neri nodded.

---

# 4. THE FINAL HANDOVER

The gold presentation case was opened.

Regalia was removed.

The Commander examined the weapon.

The **TACHYON INDUSTRIES** engraving was visible on the right side.

Neri's signature remained beside it.

The Commander looked at the signature.

Then at Neri.

> **MSAC COMMANDER:**  
> “You really signed it.”

Neri nodded.

> **NERI:**  
> “All of them.”

The Commander looked back at Regalia.

> **MSAC COMMANDER:**  
> “I'll take care of it.”

Neri was silent for several seconds.

Then:

> **NERI:**  
> “I know.”

# 10. NERI'S RETURN

Following the ceremony, Neri quietly left the museum.

She walked down the corridor.

She stopped.

Her plush transformation had been voluntary.

She had simply decided that she no longer wanted to remain in plush form.

Her normal body returned completely.

She adjusted her coat.

Adjusted her glasses.

Checked the time.

Then continued walking.

No dramatic transformation sequence occurred.

No announcement was made.

Neri simply returned to being herself.

# 12. C.O.R.E. FINAL ASSESSMENT

The Night Stalker program began with three discarded M1911s.

It became an experiment.

Then a research project.

Then a collection.

Then a series of competitions.

Then a test of character.

Then a historical artifact.

Ten units were ultimately produced.

Ten were ultimately authorized.

And the final unit was given to the person responsible for commanding the organization that had ultimately earned Neri's trust.

There will be no more.

---

The final record of the Night Stalker program contains an unusual number of contradictory statements.

At various points, personnel were told:

> “There are only ten.”

> “Don't be afraid of Jane Doe.”

> “Protect my friends.”

> “Don't make Director Lucious mad.”

> “Five tickets.”

> “Think outside the box.”

> **“No more Night Stalkers.”**

That last statement appears to have been genuine.

For once.

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING RECORD

Neri stood in front of the empty Regalia display for one final time.

She looked at the empty case.

She touched the glass.

Then she stepped away.

The final Night Stalker was no longer hers.

It belonged to someone who had earned it.

The collection was complete.

The program was finished.

And Neri?

She went back to the cafeteria.

Ordered coffee.

Sat down.

And returned to being a ten-year-old chemist who happened to have changed the history of Aperture's armaments division.

No more challenges.

No more tickets.

No more Night Stalkers.

Just Neri.
