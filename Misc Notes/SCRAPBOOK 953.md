# SAFEHOUSE ARCHIVE

## UNRECORDED CONVERSATION: JANE DOE & NERI

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE Cafeteria  
**Time:** Late night  
**Participants:**

- Jane Doe
    
- Neri
    

**Recording Status:** NONE  
**Transcript Source:** Reconstructed from neither participant  
**Disclosure Status:** PRIVATE  
**Current Knowledge of Jane Doe's Legal/Original Name:** Neri only

> **ARCHIVAL NOTE:**  
> This conversation was never recorded, logged, or formally reported. The following document exists only as a narrative reconstruction of the circumstances known to have occurred. No information disclosed during the conversation has been incorporated into Jane Doe's official personnel file.

---

# 1. THE CAFETERIA

It was late.

The cafeteria was almost completely empty.

Neri was sitting at her usual table.

A chemistry book was open in front of her.

A plate containing cake sat beside it.

She wasn't particularly hungry.

She was simply awake.

Jane found her there.

She stopped at the entrance.

> **Jane:** "You're awake."

Neri looked up.

> **Neri:** "So are you."

Jane laughed quietly.

> **Jane:** "Fair."

Jane walked over and sat down.

For a while, neither of them said anything.

There was no investigation.

No intelligence exchange.

No jokes.

No interrogation.

Just two people sitting in a nearly empty cafeteria in the middle of the night.

---

# 2. JANE'S STORY

Eventually, Jane spoke.

> **Jane:** "You know something funny?"

Neri looked at her.

> **Neri:** "What?"

> **Jane:** "I've never really had a name."

Neri tilted her head.

> **Neri:** "Jane Doe is your name."

Jane smiled.

> **Jane:** "That's the point."

She explained that she had been abandoned at birth.

She had not been abandoned alone.

She had a little brother.

The two of them had been left together.

Her brother lived for only another three months.

He died of leukemia.

Jane spoke about it without the theatrical confidence she normally carried around the Blacksite.

There was no intimidation.

No manipulation.

No carefully chosen words designed to make someone uncomfortable.

Just Jane.

> **Jane:** "After that, there wasn't really anyone left."

Neri listened.

She didn't interrupt.

Jane looked down at the table.

> **Jane:** "So I picked a name."

Neri waited.

> **Jane:** "Jane Doe."

Neri looked confused.

> **Neri:** "Why?"

Jane shrugged.

> **Jane:** "Because it's a placeholder."

A pause.

> **Jane:** "I'm not significant enough to need a real one."

Neri's expression changed.

She understood that sentence far more than Jane probably expected.

---

# 3. THE NAME

Jane eventually told Neri the name she had been born with.

**Lucifer Jameson.**

She did not present it as something important.

She did not ask Neri to remember it.

She did not ask Neri to call her by it.

She simply told her.

> **Jane:** "That's my real name."

Neri remained quiet.

Then:

> **Neri:** "Lucifer."

Jane nodded.

> **Jane:** "Yeah."

Neri looked at her for several seconds.

> **Neri:** "I won't tell anyone."

Jane looked back at her.

> **Jane:** "You don't have to promise."

> **Neri:** "I want to."

Jane smiled.

> **Jane:** "Okay."

---

# 4. NERI'S STORY

The silence returned.

Eventually, Neri closed her chemistry book.

She rarely talked about her childhood voluntarily.

This time, she did.

She told Jane about being called **002** instead of having a proper name.

She spoke about her little sister.

The sister who had actually given Neri the name she still uses.

She talked about how much her sister loved sweets.

Cake.

Candy.

The things Neri now associated with her.

She spoke about losing her.

About being unable to stop it.

About the people she later befriended and lost.

About how every disappearance eventually became another reason to believe that she had failed someone.

Jane didn't interrupt.

She didn't attempt to analyze Neri.

She didn't try to fix the story.

She simply listened.

At one point Neri became quiet.

Then she said:

> **Neri:** "I don't want to lose anyone anymore."

Jane nodded.

> **Jane:** "Neither do I."

---

# 5. TWO PLACEHOLDERS

The conversation eventually returned to their names.

Neri looked at Jane.

> **Neri:** "You said you're not significant enough to have a real name."

Jane nodded.

Neri thought about that.

Then quietly said:

> **Neri:** "I was 002."

Jane looked at her.

> **Neri:** "They didn't give me a name."

A pause.

> **Neri:** "My sister did."

Jane understood.

Neither of them said anything for several seconds.

Two people who had been reduced to something impersonal by the people around them.

One called **002**.

One choosing **Jane Doe**.

Both eventually receiving something more meaningful from someone they loved.

---

# 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Before leaving, Jane looked at Neri.

> **Jane:** "Nobody else knows."

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:** "They won't."

> **Jane:** "Not even Neko?"

> **Neri:** "Not even Neko."

Jane smiled.

> **Jane:** "Good."

Neri picked up her fork.

> **Neri:** "Do you want cake?"

Jane stared at her.

> **Jane:** "Is that your solution to everything?"

Neri thought about it.

> **Neri:** "Mostly."

Jane laughed.

> **Jane:** "Sure."

Neri pushed the plate toward her.

The two continued eating in silence.

# 8. FINAL NOTE

Jane Doe has spent much of her life operating under identities.

**Jane Doe** is the identity she chose.

**Lucifer Jameson** is the name she gave to exactly one person.

Neri, in turn, trusted Jane with pieces of a childhood she normally keeps buried.

Neither gained intelligence from the conversation.

Neither gained an advantage.

Neither attempted to manipulate the other.

They simply listened.

For perhaps the first time, Jane's most valuable secret wasn't extracted from someone.

She **gave it away voluntarily.**

And Neri kept it.
