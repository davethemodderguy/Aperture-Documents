**SUBJECT:** Neri — Reduced Mobility Following Recent Anemic Shock

---

# Executive Summary

Neri's most recent episode of severe anemia has left her **considerably weaker than her previous baseline.**

Although the immediate crisis has resolved, her physical stamina has not fully recovered.

As a result, Neri currently spends prolonged periods in one location or relies on other SAFEHOUSE residents for transportation when she needs to move farther than she can comfortably manage.

This has become sufficiently common to constitute part of her current daily routine.

---

# Current Mobility

Neri can still walk independently.

However, doing so for extended periods can exhaust her rapidly.

Her current pattern is therefore inconsistent:

- sometimes she walks normally;
    
- sometimes she moves very slowly;
    
- sometimes she stops somewhere quiet and remains there for a prolonged period;
    
- sometimes she simply decides that moving is not worth the effort;
    
- and sometimes someone gives her a ride.
    

The last option has become increasingly common.

---

# Current Transportation Methods

## 1. Alice's Scooter

Alice's scooter has become one of Neri's preferred methods of transportation when the destination is suitable.

Neri can simply hitch a ride rather than expend the stamina required to walk.

The arrangement is remarkably uncomplicated.

Alice drives.

Neri rides.

Neri does not have to talk.

Alice does not have to make conversation.

Everyone wins.

There have been multiple observations of Neri sitting quietly on Alice's scooter while Alice travels through the Blacksite.

The scooter has therefore unofficially become:

**Neri's second-best mobility device.**

Her first is apparently everyone else's legs.

---

## 2. Jane Doe

Jane has also become a frequent source of transportation.

If Neri is too weak to walk, Jane may simply carry her.

More specifically:

**Piggyback.**

Neri generally does not protest.

She simply climbs onto Jane's back when offered the opportunity and remains there until they reach their destination.

Jane has become surprisingly efficient at this.

Neri, meanwhile, appears perfectly content to stare over Jane's shoulder in complete silence.

---

## 3. Other SAFEHOUSE Residents

Other residents occasionally assist with transportation when circumstances require it.

Methods vary according to the individual and destination.

These include:

- being carried;
    
- being supported while walking;
    
- riding with another resident;
    
- using a wheelchair or other available mobility equipment when appropriate;
    
- being transported alongside someone using existing Blacksite infrastructure.
    

No single method is mandatory.

The objective is simply to prevent Neri from exhausting herself unnecessarily.

---

# The "Stationary Neri" Phenomenon

There is another increasingly common situation.

Neri simply...

**stays somewhere.**

For a long time.

She may sit in:

- a quiet hallway;
    
- an unused room;
    
- a library area;
    
- the cafeteria;
    
- a stairwell landing;
    
- her room;
    
- a bench;
    
- or another sufficiently quiet location.
    

She may remain there for an extended period without attempting to relocate.

This should not automatically be interpreted as distress.

Sometimes she is simply conserving energy.

Sometimes she is reading.

Sometimes she is drinking coffee.

Sometimes she is doing absolutely nothing.

And sometimes moving simply isn't worth the effort.

---

# Interaction With Current Social Behavior

The change in mobility has produced an unusual side effect.

Neri can now remain physically close to people without necessarily having to **approach** them.

This is particularly relevant to her current relationship with Jane.

Neri may choose to remain near Jane, but if she becomes tired, she does not necessarily leave.

She can simply sit down.

If Jane needs to move elsewhere, Neri may occasionally accept a piggyback ride rather than separating from her.

No verbal interaction is required.

This has created a surprisingly quiet form of companionship.

---

# Medical Consideration

The reduction in stamina is consistent with Neri's recent severe anemia and subsequent physiological stress.

Her ability to regenerate tissue does not automatically restore her blood supply or cardiovascular stability.

Consequently:

> **Neri being able to walk does not necessarily mean Neri should walk.**

Personnel should avoid encouraging her to "push through" exhaustion.

If she requests transportation, provide it.

If she stops because she is exhausted, allow her to rest.

If she appears medically unstable, normal emergency procedures apply.

---

# Important Distinction

Neri's increased reliance on others for transportation should **not** be interpreted as regression.

She has not suddenly become incapable of caring for herself.

She is physically recovering from a serious physiological event.

Accepting a ride is not the same as surrendering independence.

In fact, allowing someone else to carry her may be one of the few circumstances in which Neri is currently permitting herself to accept assistance without attempting to compensate for it.

# C.O.R.E. Assessment

Neri's current physical state requires a slightly different understanding of her normal behavior.

Previously:

> **Neri is somewhere quiet because she wants to be alone.**

Currently:

> **Neri may be somewhere quiet because she wants to be alone, because she is conserving energy, or because she simply doesn't have enough stamina to move.**

These possibilities can coexist.

Personnel should therefore avoid assuming either extreme.

Do not drag her into social interaction because she is sitting alone.

Do not leave an obviously medically compromised Neri unattended merely because she prefers solitude.

Observe.

Respect.

Intervene when necessary.
