# SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL INCIDENT REPORT

**CASE ID:** SH-████-NERI  
**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**Classification:** MISSING RESIDENT  
**Status:** ACTIVE SEARCH  
**Last Confirmed Sighting:** [REDACTED]  
**Time Since Confirmed Sighting:** [REDACTED] weeks

---

# 1. INITIAL NOTICE

Neri has been reported missing.

The report was not filed immediately.

This is currently considered one of the most serious complications of the recent behavioral decline surrounding Neri.

Her absence had become normalized.

She had already stopped appearing regularly in communal areas.

She had stopped walking the halls.

She was rarely seen in the cafeteria.

Her medical episodes had become less frequent.

People had gradually become accustomed to not seeing her.

Consequently, nobody initially recognized the difference between:

> **"Neri isn't around today."**

and

> **"Neri isn't around anymore."**

The distinction was discovered only after staff attempted to locate her for a routine welfare check.

She could not be found.

---

# 2. THE BULLY

The individual responsible for the earlier incident remains a significant point of interest.

They have reportedly stated that they did not understand why Neri reacted so severely to their comments.

They believed they were bullying her.

They did not understand that they had accidentally repeated the language used against Neri throughout her childhood.

They did not understand what those words represented.

They did not understand why Neri collapsed.

They did not understand why she became different afterward.

They did not understand why she stopped appearing around SAFEHOUSE.

And they certainly did not understand what they had done to her.

Their reported attitude was essentially:

> **"I didn't know it was that serious."**

That statement has been retained in the case file.

It is considered relevant.

---

# 3. THE CHANGE AFTER THE INCIDENT

The incident did not produce an immediate disappearance.

Neri remained in SAFEHOUSE for several weeks afterward.

This period is now being reviewed as a potential deterioration window.

She became increasingly absent.

She stopped visiting the cafeteria regularly.

The extra desserts prepared specifically because she normally ate them were eventually discontinued.

Her usual table remained empty.

Her interactions with friends became infrequent.

Her medical episodes decreased.

Her activity decreased.

Her presence decreased.

Nothing dramatic happened.

There was no obvious breaking point.

No emergency alarm.

No violent incident.

No announcement.

Neri simply became harder and harder to find.

Until eventually:

**she could not be found at all.**

---

# 4. LAST KNOWN ROUTINE

The final period in which Neri was believed to be living normally was characterized by extremely low activity.

Available records indicate:

- Minimal time in communal areas.
    
- Minimal interaction with residents.
    
- Reduced food consumption observed by communal staff.
    
- Reduced participation in laboratory activities.
    
- No recent archery practice.
    
- No recent combat training.
    
- No recent recreational activity.
    
- No documented arguments.
    
- No documented fights.
    
- No requests for assistance.
    

Her medical record similarly showed an unusual quiet period.

No major anemic episode was recorded immediately preceding her disappearance.

This has created considerable concern.

---

# 5. ROOM SEARCH

Neri's room was inspected after the missing-person report was filed.

Several personal belongings remain.

Her disappearance therefore does not currently appear to have been preceded by a complete relocation of her possessions.

Items identified include:

- Clothing.
    
- Books.
    
- Personal effects.
    
- Several food wrappers.
    
- Various small chemical notes.
    
- Personal correspondence.
    
- Her fish-bone hairclip.
    

The fish-bone hairclip was particularly significant.

It remains one of the few objects Neri consistently treated as irreplaceable because it was given to her by her younger sister.

Its presence suggests that Neri did not simply leave with everything she considered personally important.

---

# 6. WEAPONS

Neri's concealed weapons inventory cannot be accurately determined.

Several known knives are missing.

Her Starlight Katana is also unaccounted for.

Her mechanical crossbow is absent.

The absence of these items does not establish hostile intent.

Neri habitually carried weapons.

However, their absence establishes that she may have deliberately prepared herself before leaving.

---

# 7. MEDICAL CONCERN

This case is medically complicated.

Neri can experience spontaneous anemia without physical exertion.

The fact that her recent episodes decreased does not remove this risk.

If she is currently alone, several possibilities exist:

1. She may be physically healthy at present.
    
2. She may have experienced an episode without anyone witnessing it.
    
3. She may have become unconscious somewhere inaccessible.
    
4. She may be injured.
    
5. She may be deliberately avoiding contact.
    
6. She may be experiencing another severe psychological episode.
    

All remain possible.

The absence of a medical alarm does **not** establish her safety.

---

# 8. BAO

Bao was notified immediately.

Her response was reportedly brief.

> **"Where is my daughter?"**

No staff member initially had an answer.

This was followed by an immediate search.

Bao has since remained involved in the search operation.

Personnel have been reminded that Neri's relationship with Bao is one of her strongest remaining anchors.

If Neri is located, Bao is to be notified immediately.

---

# 9. ALICE

Alice's reaction was substantially different.

Upon being informed that Neri could not be found, Alice reportedly asked:

> **"How long?"**

When told that Neri's absence may have begun substantially earlier than initially believed, Alice became silent.

She subsequently began searching locations that Neri had historically visited.

The cafeteria.

The laboratory.

Quiet corridors.

Outdoor areas.

Places where Neri had previously sat alone.

She reportedly checked her usual table last.

It was empty.

# 11. BEHAVIORAL INTERPRETATION

The most dangerous assumption at this stage would be:

> **"Neri ran away."**

That remains possible.

But it is not yet established.

Neri has a history of withdrawing when overwhelmed.

She has also demonstrated a willingness to endure severe suffering without informing anyone.

Her recent behavior suggests that she may have spent considerable time psychologically distancing herself from SAFEHOUSE before physically disappearing.

The exact moment at which she decided to leave is unknown.

That uncertainty is itself concerning.

---

The individual who bullied Neri believed they were hurting her feelings.

They were not aware of:

- The discarded-child mentality imposed on her.
    
- The death of her sister.
    
- The years of experimentation.
    
- The people she had already lost.
    
- The friends she believed she had failed.
    
- The fear of abandonment.
    
- The terror of being helpless.
    
- The years without proper sleep.

They found something that hurt her.

They pressed harder.

They did not know they were standing on top of an entire childhood.
