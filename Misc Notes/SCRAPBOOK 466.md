## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CELESTIAL ARCHIVE / CORIN PERSONNEL OBSERVATION**

# **"SHE REMEMBERS"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Architect-Sealed  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Related Subjects:** Orphie / Guiding Light  
**Status:** **ONGOING**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin remembers.

The memories were never lost.

They were never sealed.

They were never replaced.

She remembers her family.

She remembers being taken.

She remembers the experiments.

She remembers the pain.

She remembers dying.

And she remembers waking up afterward.

The Ether kept her body together.

It did not erase what happened to her.

---

# **THE MEMORIES**

Corin rarely talks about her past.

Not because she cannot remember it.

Because she **can**.

Certain memories remain particularly vivid.

The faces of the people who took her.

The rooms where she was kept.

The equipment.

The procedures.

The sensation of being treated as something rather than someone.

And, most painfully:

**her family.**

She remembers what it was like before everything changed.

That contrast is sometimes worse than the memories themselves.

---

# **WHEN IT RETURNS**

The memories do not always appear deliberately.

Sometimes a smell does it.

Sometimes a sound.

Sometimes a particular piece of equipment.

Sometimes a sterile room.

Sometimes nothing obvious happens at all.

Corin can be laughing with Orphie one moment and suddenly become quiet the next.

Her attention leaves the room.

Her expression changes.

And she remembers.

---

# **THE ETHER**

Her Ether-based existence makes the situation stranger.

The Ether keeps her functioning.

It holds her body together.

It allows her to continue living despite what happened.

But it cannot remove the memories.

Corin once described it simply:

> **"It kept my body."**

> **"It didn't take my memories with it."**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has learned to recognize when Corin is hurting.

She does not always know why.

Sometimes she simply sits beside her.

Sometimes she brings her something.

Sometimes she talks about completely unrelated things until Corin gradually returns to the present.

Orphie does not try to erase Corin's memories.

She understands something now that she once struggled to understand about herself:

**Not every wound needs to be forgotten to heal.**

---

# **A NIGHT IN THE TREEHOUSE**

Corin once woke from a nightmare.

She was trembling.

Orphie was already awake.

> **Orphie:**  
> "...Again?"

Corin nodded.

Orphie sat beside her.

> **Corin:**  
> "I remember everything."

Orphie did not ask what she meant.

She already knew.

> **Corin:**  
> "Sometimes I wish I didn't."

Orphie remained silent for a moment.

Then:

> **Orphie:**  
> "Yeah."

Corin looked at her.

Orphie continued:

> **"But you're here."**

Corin quietly replied:

> **"...Yeah."**

> **Orphie:**  
> "So am I."

They stayed there together.

No speeches.

No promises that the memories would disappear.

Just two people sitting in the dark.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light knows that she cannot remove Corin's past simply because it hurts.

She could.

That is precisely why she refuses to do so without Corin's consent.

Those memories are part of Corin.

Erasing them would mean changing something fundamental about the person who survived them.

Guiding Light will not decide that for her.

Instead, she watches.

She protects.

She listens.

And when Corin remembers, she remains nearby.

---

# **PRIVATE NOTE — GUIDING LIGHT**

> **"I used to think saving someone meant taking away what hurt them."**
> 
> **"Now I understand."**
> 
> **"Sometimes saving someone means letting them remember..."**
> 
> **"...while making sure they don't have to remember alone."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's past still hurts.

It probably always will.

The fact that she survived does not make what happened acceptable.

The fact that she can laugh now does not erase the nights when she remembers.

The fact that Ether keeps her body together does not repair everything that was broken.

But Corin is still here.

She remembers who she was.

She remembers what was done to her.

And she is slowly learning that her past is something she **carries**, not something that has to control every step she takes.

She is not the experiment.

She is not the corpse.

She is not what they did to her.

She is **Corin**.

And she is still living.
