# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **INTERNAL PERSONNEL NOTICE**

### **SUBJECT: RETIREMENT OF AUTOMATIC CUSTOMER SUPPORT GREETING**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

For several years, Dialyn's first instinct upon answering any ringing phone was to say:

> "Hello, Krampus Compliance Authority, this is Dialyn speaking, how can I help you?"

The greeting became so deeply ingrained that it was used:

- On her personal phone.
    
- During calls from coworkers.
    
- While visiting Heaven Blacksite.
    
- Even after being promoted to Human Representative.
    

Although no longer required by policy, the habit persisted.

Until today.

---

# **THE INCIDENT**

At approximately 16:12, Neko placed a routine call to Dialyn.

Several SAFEHOUSE residents happened to be nearby.

The phone rang.

Dialyn answered.

There was a brief pause.

Then she said:

> "...Hi, Neko."

Silence.

Neko looked at the phone.

Then slowly looked around the room.

Belle blinked.

Corey nearly dropped the mug she was holding.

Corin looked up from her paperwork.

Nobody spoke for several seconds.

---

# **CONFIRMATION**

Neko ended the call.

He immediately called again.

Dialyn answered.

> "...Hello?"

Not:

> "Hello, Krampus Compliance Authority..."

Just...

> "Hello?"

A third call was placed.

The result remained the same.

# **DIALYN'S RESPONSE**

When informed that everyone had noticed the change, Dialyn laughed.

> "...I didn't even realize."

She thought for a moment.

> "...I guess my brain finally accepted that every phone call isn't a customer."

After a brief pause, she smiled.

> "...It only took a few years."

---

# **AFTERMATH**

Although the automatic greeting has finally faded, it has not been forgotten.

Occasionally, when Dialyn is especially tired or answers a phone before her morning coffee, the old introduction still slips out.

Whenever it does, whoever is on the other end of the line usually smiles.

Not because it's unusual.

But because it reminds them of the years when one patient woman answered hundreds of calls a day, always with the same warmth and enthusiasm.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Human Resources has officially updated Dialyn's personnel record.

**Automatic Customer Support Greeting**

**Status:** Retired.

The record was quietly amended by Neko several hours later with an additional handwritten note.

> "...We'll miss it."

Dialyn noticed the note.

She smiled.

And, for once, didn't answer with a customer support greeting.
