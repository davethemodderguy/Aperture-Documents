### **SUPPLEMENTAL OBSERVATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN & COREY – SOCIAL ADAPTATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Several months after the Dual Manifestation Event, personnel have largely adapted to Corin and Corey as two distinct individuals.

Initial uncertainty over how to address them has disappeared.

Within the Blacksite, they are simply referred to as:

> **"The Twins."**

Although they share the same origin, neither considers herself "more real" than the other.

Likewise, personnel have made a conscious effort to avoid favoring one over the other.

---

# **RELATIONSHIP**

Corin and Corey behave less like duplicates and more like identical twins who have known each other their entire lives.

They routinely:

- Finish each other's sentences.
    
- Borrow each other's belongings without asking.
    
- Argue over insignificant topics.
    
- Defend one another immediately when criticized.
    
- Quietly sit together for hours without speaking.
    

It is not unusual for an argument over who forgot to wash a teacup to end with both laughing a few minutes later.

SAFEHOUSE residents have described their relationship as:

> "Professionally synchronized."

> "Personally chaotic."

---

# **PERSONAL DIFFERENCES**

While sharing many interests, subtle differences have become apparent.

### **Corin**

- More reserved.
    
- Prefers quiet evenings.
    
- Frequently spends time with the white tiger.
    
- Reads outdoors regardless of weather.
    

### **Corey**

- More outgoing.
    
- More likely to begin conversations.
    
- Laughs more easily.
    
- Frequently encourages Corin to join group activities.
    

Neither personality is considered "better."

They simply approach life differently.

---

# **THE LOCKWOOD FAMILY**

The Lockwood family has deliberately refused to distinguish one daughter as being "the original."

From the beginning, both Corin and Corey have been treated with equal affection and equal expectations.

Family events include both.

Family photographs include both.

If one receives a gift, so does the other.

When introducing them, Lumi simply says:

> "These are my daughters."

Nothing more needs to be explained.

---

# **NORMA**

One notable exception in personal preference has been observed.

Corey appears to spend slightly more time with Norma than Corin does.

The difference is subtle.

She often chooses to accompany Norma during routine activities, seeks her advice, and frequently shares afternoon tea with her.

When asked why, Corey answered with a smile.

> "...I don't know."

> "I just like talking to Mom Norma."

Norma responded by smiling and pouring another cup of tea.

Medical Services emphasize that this preference has never resulted in exclusion or jealousy.

Corin has shown no indication of being bothered by it.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin and Corey continue residing together at Shelter T-01.

Visitors have become accustomed to seeing two nearly identical sisters walking through the snow toward Project SAFEHOUSE.

One with chalky lime hair.

One with black hair.

Sometimes they argue all the way there.

Sometimes they walk in complete silence.

By the time they reach SAFEHOUSE, however, they almost always enter together.

To everyone else, the distinction has become surprisingly simple.

There are not two versions of one daughter.

There are simply two daughters.

And both are family.
