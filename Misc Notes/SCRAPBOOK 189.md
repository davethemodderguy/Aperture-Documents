## **SUBJECT: WHITE TIGER – SOCIAL BOND OBSERVATIONS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Continued observation of the white tiger residing at Shelter T-01 has revealed a small but consistent social circle.

The animal demonstrates its strongest attachment toward Corin Lockwood.

The only other individual consistently receiving comparable trust is Taiga.

Veterinary Services emphasize that this behavior appears to reflect individual familiarity and comfort rather than training.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin remains the tiger's primary companion.

The animal routinely follows her through the surrounding forest, waits beneath the treehouse during the day, and accompanies her on short walks around Shelter T-01.

When Corin is reading outside, the tiger is frequently observed lying beside her with its head resting on its front paws.

The pair appear comfortable sharing long periods of silence.

---

# **TAIGA**

Taiga's relationship with the tiger has developed steadily over repeated visits.

Unlike most personnel, Taiga is occasionally permitted to lean against the tiger while resting.

On colder afternoons, the tiger has also been observed lying down beside Taiga, allowing her to nap against its thick winter coat.

The behavior has never been forced.

The tiger simply chooses not to move.

Behavioral specialists noted that the tiger seems unusually relaxed around Taiga, responding to her calm demeanor and familiar presence.

One veterinarian jokingly summarized the relationship as:

> "It looks at Taiga like she's just another big spotted cat looking for somewhere warm."

The description is informal rather than scientific, but the comparison has become popular among personnel.

---

# **ASTRAL**

Astral witnessed one such afternoon while delivering supplies to Shelter T-01.

She found Taiga asleep against the tiger's side.

The tiger opened one eye.

Saw Astral.

Then quietly closed it again.

Astral couldn't help but laugh.

> "...Congratulations, Taiga."

She smiled.

> "...You've apparently been accepted into the giant cat club."

Taiga, still half asleep, simply replied,

> "...Warm..."

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The white tiger continues to display calm, protective behavior toward Corin and notable tolerance toward Taiga.

Veterinary Services remind personnel that these relationships were built gradually through repeated, respectful interactions.

Accordingly, the standing guidance remains unchanged:

> **If the tiger chooses to trust you, consider it a privilege.**

> **Do not assume that privilege extends to anyone else.**
