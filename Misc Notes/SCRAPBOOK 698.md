### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / MISSING PERSONNEL & MEDICAL LOG**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — RECOVERY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Age:** Approximately 5 years old  
**Time Missing:** **3 MONTHS**  
**Status:** **FOUND / CRITICAL CONDITION**

---

# **RECOVERY**

Alice Lockwood has been found.

Three months after disappearing from the Blacksite, a search team located her in the mountains.

The location was immediately recognized.

It was the exact area where Corin originally found Alice.

The same wilderness.

The same cold.

The same isolated terrain.

Alice had somehow returned to the place where her life had first intersected with Corin's.

---

# **THE GRAVE**

Alice was discovered lying on top of a raised mound of snow.

At first, the search team believed it to be a natural formation.

Closer inspection revealed otherwise.

The mound appeared deliberately constructed.

Surrounding it were numerous wilted flowers.

At its center stood a poorly carved stone slab.

Only one word had been carved into it.

> **"Mom"**

No name.

No date.

No explanation.

Just:

> **Mom**

Alice had apparently created the grave herself.

---

# **ALICE**

Alice was found lying beside the grave.

She was alive.

Barely responsive.

Her physical condition was immediately classified as critical.

Observed injuries included:

- Numerous cuts
    
- Multiple lacerations
    
- Bruising
    
- Signs of prolonged environmental exposure
    
- Severe fatigue
    
- Significant nutritional deficiency
    
- Symptoms of acute illness
    
- General physical deterioration
    

Her nutritional state was particularly concerning.

For a child of Alice's age and already-fragile physiology, the amount of nutrition she had received over the preceding months was **grossly insufficient**.

Her body had been surviving on almost nothing.

Again.

---

# **THE DISCOVERY**

When the search team approached, Alice woke.

She saw them.

For a moment, she froze.

Then panic took over.

Alice immediately attempted to run.

A searcher called:

> **"Alice!"**

She ran faster.

Or attempted to.

Her legs could not sustain it.

Her stamina failed almost immediately.

Alice stumbled.

Her body pitched forward.

She fell **headfirst into the snow.**

The search team rushed toward her.

Alice attempted to crawl away.

She couldn't.

Her body simply stopped cooperating.

---

# **PANIC**

Alice was terrified.

Not angry.

Not aggressive.

Terrified.

She kept trying to move away despite being physically incapable of doing so.

> **"No..."**

> **"No, no, no..."**

A medic approached slowly.

Alice curled into herself.

Her ears flattened.

Her hands trembled.

She tried to hide her face.

Then she whispered:

> **"Alice sorry..."**

The medic stopped.

Nobody touched her immediately.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin was notified.

She reached the location shortly afterward.

She immediately recognized the stone.

> **"Mom."**

Then she saw Alice.

The cuts.

The bruises.

The thin body.

The wilted flowers.

The snow.

Everything.

Corin did not say anything.

She simply knelt several feet away.

Alice looked at her.

Her expression changed.

Recognition.

Fear.

Guilt.

Then tears.

---

# **THE APPROACH**

Corin did not rush her.

She did not ask:

> "Why did you leave?"

She did not ask:

> "Why didn't you come home?"

She did not mention the Archives.

She did not mention the medical record.

She did not tell Alice how long everyone had searched.

She simply said:

> **"Alice."**

Alice shook her head.

> **"Alice bad."**

Corin's expression broke.

> **"No."**

Alice began crying.

> **"Alice make everyone work..."**

> **"No."**

> **"Alice sick..."**

> **"I know."**

> **"Alice hurt..."**

Corin moved slightly closer.

> **"I know."**

Alice looked at her.

> **"Why you come?"**

Corin answered:

> **"Because you're my little sister."**

Alice stared at her.

Then:

> **"...But Alice burden."**

Corin shook her head.

> **"Never."**

---

# **MEDICAL EVACUATION**

Alice was transported immediately.

She was not made to walk.

She was not asked to stand.

She was carried.

Her condition required urgent medical attention.

The medical team identified:

### **Nutritional Deficiency**

Severe caloric and nutrient deprivation.

### **Acute Illness**

Likely resulting from prolonged exposure, inadequate nutrition, and her already compromised immune system.

### **Skin and Soft-Tissue Injuries**

Multiple cuts and lacerations of varying severity.

### **Environmental Exposure**

Evidence of prolonged cold exposure.

### **Physical Exhaustion**

Extreme exhaustion consistent with prolonged survival under inadequate conditions.

---

# **THE GRAVE**

Personnel returned to the site after Alice had been evacuated.

The grave remained untouched.

Wilted flowers surrounded it.

Some appeared to have been placed recently.

Others were significantly older.

The stone slab was crude.

The carving was uneven.

The word remained unmistakable.

> **MOM**

C.O.R.E. has not ordered its removal.

The site will remain undisturbed.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION**

The current interpretation is that Alice returned to the place where she first lost her mother.

She constructed a small grave.

She gathered flowers.

She stayed there.

She may have believed that this was where she belonged.

She may also have been attempting to preserve the only fragment of her mother she could still understand.

No attempts will be made to force Alice to explain.

---

# **IMPORTANT**

Alice must **not** be confronted about the grave.

Do not ask:

> "Did you build it?"

> "Why did you come here?"

> "What happened to your mother?"

> "Why didn't you come home?"

If Alice chooses to talk about it, she will be listened to.

If she does not:

**The subject remains closed.**

# **CORIN'S STATEMENT**

Corin remained beside Alice after the evacuation.

Alice was asleep.

Corin looked at her for a long time.

Then she whispered:

> **"You came all the way back here..."**

She looked toward the mountains.

Then back at Alice.

> **"...You didn't have to."**

Alice remained asleep.

Corin carefully pulled the blanket higher around her.

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

Three months ago, Alice disappeared because she believed she was a burden.

She returned to the place where she had once been found alone.

She built a grave for the mother she could barely remember.

She surrounded it with flowers.

Then she stayed.

Until her body could no longer keep going.

When she was found, she tried to run.

Her body gave out before she could get anywhere.

She fell into the snow.

And even then, her first words were not:

> **"Help me."**

They were:

> **"Alice sorry."**

That sentence has been added to the incident review.

Not because Alice did anything wrong.

Because it demonstrates exactly why the original medical-record restriction existed.

Alice never needed to be protected from the truth about her body because the truth was shameful.

She needed protection from believing that **being fragile made her undeserving of being loved.**

She is home now.

For the moment, that is enough.
