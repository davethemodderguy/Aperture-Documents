# **INITIAL OBSERVATION**

During his first full tour of Project SAFEHOUSE, Arcturus quickly noticed something unexpected.

He quietly counted on his fingers.

> "...One rabbit."

A few steps later.

> "...Three cats?"

Another hallway.

> "...A snow leopard."

Then, looking up.

> "...An owl."

He turned to Norma.

> "...You live with a surprisingly large number of Thirens."

Norma nodded matter-of-factly.

> "...I know."

# **THE CAT SITUATION**

One thing became apparent almost immediately.

Arcturus likes cats.

Quite a lot.

Unfortunately...

The three cats he encountered also happened to be:

- An inventor who constantly asks to modify his firearms.
    
- The inventor's far more responsible counterpart.
    
- A composed, quiet marksman.
    

---

## **HEADPATS**

After turning down another one of Neko's engineering ideas...

> "No, you may not install a laser rangefinder inside my revolver's grip."

Arcturus paused.

Then reached over.

_Pat._

He gave Neko a few gentle headpats.

> "...Good effort."

Neko's ears immediately perked up.

> "...Worth asking."

---

The pattern repeated throughout the week.

Every time Arcturus had to say "no" to one of Neko's increasingly elaborate proposals...

Neko received headpats as compensation.

---

**Proposal #31**

> "Automatic lever-action?"

> "...No."

_Headpat._

---

**Proposal #32**

> "Rocket-assisted revolver?"

> "...No."

_Headpat._

---

**Proposal #33**

> "Can I at least polish the trigger?"

> "...Still no."

_Headpat._

---

Eventually, Neko stopped looking disappointed after being refused.

Instead, he simply leaned forward slightly.

Miss Neko noticed this almost immediately.

---

# **STATUS**

Arcturus Lockwood has adjusted well to life around the unusually high concentration of Thirens within Project SAFEHOUSE.

He continues to politely reject every engineering proposal presented by Neko.

Neko, meanwhile, has accepted an unspoken arrangement:

He probably won't get a modified revolver.

But he'll almost certainly get a headpat.

According to Belle, this has resulted in "one of the healthiest engineer–cowboy negotiations she's ever seen."
