# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### PSYCHOLOGICAL & RESIDENTIAL WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-NERI-197  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESTRICTED — RESIDENTIAL WELFARE / BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION  
**STATUS:** CLOSED  
**SUBJECTS:** Neri / Jane Doe  
**INCIDENT:** Subject became extremely angry with Jane Doe and attempted to express displeasure through biting

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jane Doe made Neri **very, very angry.**

Neri responded in the manner she has increasingly adopted when interacting with people she is comfortable with.

She bit Jane.

Unfortunately for Neri:

Jane is approximately **185 cm tall.**

Neri is approximately the size of a plush toy.

The bite was real.

The anger was real.

The effectiveness was questionable.

---

# 2. INCIDENT

Jane had been teasing Neri.

The exact subject of the teasing has been classified as:

> **"Jane being Jane."**

Neri initially ignored her.

Jane continued.

Neri looked at her.

Jane continued.

Neri's expression changed.

Personnel present described the expression as:

> "The face of someone who has decided that diplomacy has failed."

Jane apparently recognized this.

She continued anyway.

**JANE:**  
"Oh, come on. Don't look at me like that."

Neri stood up.

She walked toward Jane.

Jane crouched slightly.

**JANE:**  
"What are you going to do?"

Neri bit her hand.

---

# 3. THE BITE

Neri's jaws closed around approximately half of Jane's hand.

The bite was firm.

Neri remained attached.

Jane looked at her hand.

Then at Neri.

Neri glared upward.

**JANE:**  
"...Are you biting me?"

Neri nodded.

Still attached.

**JANE:**  
"You're really angry."

Neri nodded again.

Still attached.

Jane waited.

Approximately ten seconds passed.

**JANE:**  
"Does that make you feel better?"

Neri shook her head.

She continued biting anyway.

---

# 4. EFFECTIVENESS ASSESSMENT

The bite produced:

- no significant pain;
    
- no injury;
    
- no bleeding;
    
- no loss of hand function;
    
- no intimidation effect.
    

It did, however, successfully communicate:

> **Neri is extremely angry.**

Jane therefore classified the bite as:

> **"Emotionally effective, physically useless."**

Neri disagreed.

She bit harder.

Jane still did not appear concerned.

---

# 5. JANE'S RESPONSE

Jane deliberately avoided laughing.

This was difficult.

**JANE:**  
"Okay."

Neri stared at her.

**JANE:**  
"I understand."

Neri released her.

She crossed her arms.

Her oversized hoodie sleeves covered most of her hands.

She turned away.

Jane waited.

**JANE:**  
"...Are you done?"

Neri shook her head.

Jane raised an eyebrow.

Neri pointed at her.

Then pointed at the floor.

Then made a small motion indicating:

> **"Stay there."**

Jane obeyed.

---

# 6. THE ACTUAL PROBLEM

The bite itself was not considered concerning.

The concerning part was how angry Neri had become before resorting to it.

Neri does not ordinarily bite people out of uncontrolled aggression.

Her recent biting behavior is generally social:

- sleeves;
    
- hands;
    
- clothing;
    
- familiar personnel;
    
- brief attachment;
    
- playful or communicative interactions.
    

This incident was different.

The bite was deliberate.

It was her equivalent of saying:

> **"Stop."**

Jane understood immediately.

---

# 7. APOLOGY

Approximately three minutes later, Jane approached.

She stopped at a respectful distance.

**JANE:**  
"I'm sorry."

Neri looked at her.

Silence.

**JANE:**  
"I pushed too far."

Neri nodded.

**JANE:**  
"I won't do it again."

Neri continued looking at her.

Then:

**NERI:**  
"...Good."

Jane froze.

Neri rarely speaks.

Jane had apparently received the verbal equivalent of a formal disciplinary notice.

**JANE:**  
"Understood."

Neri nodded.

---

# 8. POST-INCIDENT

Several minutes later, Neri climbed onto Jane's shoulder.

Jane looked at her.

**JANE:**  
"You know you're allowed to just sit there without biting me."

Neri nodded.

**JANE:**  
"Good."

Neri settled into place.

Five minutes later, she bit Jane's hoodie sleeve.

Jane sighed.

**JANE:**  
"That one doesn't count."

Neri remained attached.

---

# 9. C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

Neri's bite should not be interpreted as dangerous aggression.

It was a communication method.

The message was extremely clear.

The delivery mechanism was simply ineffective against Jane Doe.

Jane's hand was essentially a very inconvenient chew toy.

Personnel are nevertheless reminded:

**Do not provoke Neri to test her bite.**

Her plush form is durable.

Her patience is not.

---

# 10. FINAL OBSERVATION

The incident demonstrates an unusual feature of Neri's current relationship with Jane.

Neri was angry enough to bite her.

She was comfortable enough to bite her.

She was angry enough to make the bite deliberate.

And afterward, she was comfortable enough to climb onto Jane again.

There was no prolonged resentment.

No withdrawal.

No attempt to disappear.

Just:

> **"You annoyed me."**

Followed by:

> **bite**

Followed by:

> **"Sorry."**

Followed by:

> **"Good."**

Followed by another bite to the sleeve.

C.O.R.E. has therefore reached the following conclusion:

### Neri and Jane Doe appear to have developed a functional conflict-resolution system.

It is primitive.

It is bizarre.

It is surprisingly effective.

The only known flaw is that Jane Doe's hands appear to be completely immune to the consequences.

— **END OBSERVATION**
