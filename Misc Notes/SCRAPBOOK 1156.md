# 1. ANNOUNCEMENT

MSAC personnel were informed that Neri Tachyon had produced a new limited armament.

The announcement was initially received as expected.

People became interested.

Then the specifications were displayed.

The room became significantly quieter.

### HELL FIRE

**15 units.**

**12-gauge.**

**8+1 capacity.**

**Integral suppressed barrel.**

**Mounted bayonet.**

**EOTech holographic sight.**

**Foldable stock configuration.**

And, most importantly:

> **12 Gauge Magnesium Magnum — “Hellfire” ammunition.**

The reaction was immediate.

---

# 2. GENERAL MSAC REACTION

The first few seconds consisted primarily of personnel staring at the presentation screen.

Then:

> **MSAC Operator:**  
> “Five thousand degrees?”

> **Another Operator:**  
> “Five hours?”

> **Another:**  
> “Apartment complex?”

> **Another:**  
> “Why does a shotgun have an energy department?”

Nobody had an answer.

Someone eventually asked:

> **“Can we earn one?”**

The presentation changed slides.

> **YES.**

The room immediately became considerably louder.

---

# 3. ELSHER

Elsher was among the first operators to react.

He already possesses two Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise items:

> **NS-006 — Night Stalker**

and

> **SOH-002 — Sleight of Hand**

He stared at the Hell Fire specifications.

Then at Neri.

Then back at the screen.

> **Elsher:**  
> “Fifteen?”

Neri nodded.

> **Elsher:**  
> “You're making fifteen?”

> **Neri:**  
> “Yes.”

Elsher folded his arms.

> **Elsher:**  
> “And we have to earn them?”

Neri nodded.

A pause.

> **Elsher:**  
> “...Of course we do.”

He appeared remarkably unsurprised.

---

# 4. ELSHER'S SECOND REACTION

Elsher then noticed something else.

> **Elsher:**  
> “So this is the second generation?”

Neri nodded.

> **Elsher:**  
> “And the Night Stalker and Sleight of Hand are the first generation?”

> **Neri:**  
> “Yes.”

Elsher looked at his two weapons.

Then at the Hell Fire.

> **Elsher:**  
> “I'm going for it.”

Neri gave a small nod.

> **Neri:**  
> “Okay.”

Elsher immediately began considering what sort of challenge Neri might create.

He was visibly concerned about the possibility of another haystack.

---

# 5. MSAC COMMANDER

The MSAC Commander had a different reaction.

He remained silent throughout the presentation.

He examined the specifications.

He examined the ammunition restrictions.

He examined the number of units.

Then he asked:

> **Commander:**  
> “How difficult is the qualification?”

Neri looked at him.

> **Neri:**  
> “Hard.”

> **Commander:**  
> “Define hard.”

Neri thought for a moment.

> **Neri:**  
> “Hard.”

The Commander sighed.

> **Commander:**  
> “I knew you were going to say that.”

---

# 6. COMMANDER'S INTEREST

The Commander already owns:

> **NS-010 — Regalia**

and

> **SOH-003 — Sleight of Hand**

He therefore has substantial experience with Neri's Limited Merchandise program.

He was not impressed by the concept of another competition.

He was, however, extremely interested in the Hell Fire itself.

> **Commander:**  
> “Fifteen units.”

> **Neri:**  
> “Yes.”

> **Commander:**  
> “No more?”

> **Neri:**  
> “No more.”

The Commander nodded.

> **Commander:**  
> “Then I want one.”

Neri simply replied:

> **“Earn it.”**

---

# 7. THE FIFTEEN-YEAR-OLD

The youngest current Night Stalker owner was also present.

He is already notable in Tachyon Industries history.

At only **15 years old**, he successfully located the Nekonium Needle hidden among approximately **50 billion pieces of hay**, earning:

- **SOH-004 — Sleight of Hand**
    
- **NS-009 — Night Stalker**
    

He therefore became the youngest documented owner of Tachyon Industries Limited Merchandise.

When the Hell Fire announcement was made, he initially looked excited.

Then he saw the ammunition specifications.

His expression changed.

> **15-Year-Old Operator:**  
> “...That's insane.”

Elsher looked at him.

> **Elsher:**  
> “You still want one?”

The teenager looked back at the screen.

> **15-Year-Old Operator:**  
> “Absolutely.”

Elsher laughed.

---

# 8. THE TWO-TIME WINNER

The teenager was then asked whether he thought the Hell Fire challenge would be harder than the haystack.

He answered immediately:

> **“Probably.”**

Pause.

> **“But I found the needle.”**

Several operators laughed.

He was not joking.

His confidence appears to come from experience rather than arrogance.

He has already completed one of Neri's seemingly impossible challenges.

He therefore considers another one merely:

> **“The next challenge.”**

---

# 9. ELSHER'S RESPONSE TO THAT

Elsher looked at the teenager.

> **Elsher:**  
> “You know you're making the rest of us look bad, right?”

The teenager shrugged.

> **15-Year-Old Operator:**  
> “Sorry.”

> **Elsher:**  
> “Don't apologize.”

Pause.

> **Elsher:**  
> “Just don't find another impossible object before I do.”

The teenager smiled.

> **15-Year-Old Operator:**  
> “No promises.”
