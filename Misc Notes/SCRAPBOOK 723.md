### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / PERSONNEL RECORD**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — BIRTHDAY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Date of Assignment:** **01/11**  
**Status:** **OFFICIALLY REGISTERED**

---

# **BACKGROUND**

Alice has no reliable memory of her birth date.

Her early memories are too fragmented to establish an actual birthday.

She does not remember:

- The day she was born
    
- The year she was born
    
- Her original birthday celebrations
    
- Her mother's birthday traditions
    
- Any specific childhood celebration
    

For administrative purposes, a birthday therefore had to be assigned.

The SAFEHOUSE chose something more meaningful than an arbitrary date.

---

# **NOVEMBER 1ST**

Alice's official birthday is now:

> **November 1st.**

The date corresponds to the day Corin originally found her.

It was the beginning of Alice's life at the Blacksite.

The day she was discovered at the Treehouse.

The day someone finally found her.

The day she stopped being alone.

---

# **ALICE'S RESPONSE**

When Corin explained the decision, Alice was quiet.

> **"So Alice's birthday is when Big Sis found Alice?"**

Corin nodded.

> **"Yeah."**

Alice thought about it.

Then smiled.

> **"I like that."**

---

# **THE REGISTRATION**

Neko entered the date into Alice's personnel record.

**DATE OF BIRTH:** UNKNOWN  
**OFFICIAL OBSERVANCE DATE:** **01 NOVEMBER**

A secondary notation was added:

> **"Assigned by SAFEHOUSE consensus. Subject accepts designation."**

---

# **CORIN**

Corin was asked whether she wanted November 1st to remain Alice's birthday.

Her answer was immediate.

> **"Yes."**

When asked why:

> **"Because that's when I found her."**

She paused.

> **"And she was still here."**

---

# **BELLE**

Belle immediately began planning.

Not an enormous party.

Not something overwhelming.

Just cake.

Decorations.

Flowers.

And probably an unreasonable number of carrots.

When Alice heard about it, she asked:

> **"What birthday taste like?"**

Belle laughed.

> **"Cake."**

Alice considered this.

> **"Alice likes cake?"**

> **"You will."**

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi quietly pointed out something important.

Alice's birthday does not need to represent the day she was born.

It represents the day she was **found.**

The day someone noticed that she was still alive.

The day she was given a place to belong.

For Alice, that may ultimately mean more than knowing an exact date of birth ever could.

---

# **THE GRAVE**

Alice still visits the unnamed grave marked **"Mom."**

She sometimes wonders whether her mother knew her birthday.

She will probably never know.

But she now has another date.

A date she can remember.

A date with a place.

A date with people who will be there.

---

# **OFFICIAL RECORD**

**Name:** Alice Lockwood  
**Age:** Approximately 5 years  
**Birthday:** **November 1st**  
**Original Date of Birth:** Unknown  
**Birthday Basis:** Date of discovery by Corin Lockwood  
**Birthday Status:** Officially recognized  
**Subject Approval:** Confirmed

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

Alice doesn't know when she was born.

She doesn't remember her first birthday.

She doesn't remember what her mother might have done for it.

But she remembers November 1st.

Because that was the day Corin found her.

The day Alice was no longer alone.

So November 1st became her birthday.

Not because it was necessarily the day she entered the world—

but because it was the day she finally found a place in it.
