### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / BIOLOGICAL RECOVERY REPORT**

# **CORIN — RETURN OF BIOLOGICAL ACTIVITY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **Spontaneous Biological Reactivation**  
**Status:** **ONGOING**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's body has begun exhibiting signs of genuine biological activity.

For a long period following her reanimation, Corin possessed none of the conventional indicators of life.

Her heart did not beat.

Her circulatory system showed no conventional activity.

Her body was sustained almost entirely through Ether.

This is changing.

Recent examinations have confirmed the presence of a **heartbeat**.

---

# **THE FIRST HEARTBEAT**

The discovery occurred during a routine examination.

A researcher was monitoring Corin's Ether levels when an unexpected rhythmic signal appeared.

At first, it was assumed to be equipment interference.

It wasn't.

The signal repeated.

Regularly.

A second examination confirmed it.

Corin's heart was beating.

Not through Ether simulation.

Not through external stimulation.

Not through an Architect's intervention.

**Her heart was functioning on its own.**

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin was initially confused when she was told.

> **"My heart?"**

Guiding Light nodded.

> **"It's beating."**

Corin remained silent.

She placed a hand against her chest.

For the first time in an extremely long time, she could feel something beneath it.

A faint rhythm.

**Thump.**

...

**Thump.**

...

**Thump.**

Corin stared downward.

> **"...That's mine?"**

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Yes."

Corin remained there for several minutes.

---

# **PHYSIOLOGICAL SIGNIFICANCE**

The heartbeat represents the first confirmed return of conventional biological activity within Corin's body.

It does **not** immediately mean that her entire body has returned to normal.

Ether remains essential to her current physiology.

Her anomalous reconstruction remains present.

Her body continues to possess characteristics inconsistent with ordinary human biology.

However, something is changing.

Her body is beginning to perform functions it previously could not.

---

# **THEORIES**

C.O.R.E. currently has no confirmed explanation.

Potential contributing factors include:

- Long-term stabilization of Corin's Ether.
    
- Progressive biological adaptation.
    
- Spontaneous reconstruction of damaged systems.
    
- Interaction between her anomalous physiology and newly developed biological structures.
    
- The same unknown process responsible for her spontaneous wings.
    

No theory has been confirmed.

Most importantly:

**No Architect caused the change.**

---

# **THE WINGS**

The timing has drawn attention to Corin's wings.

Both developments occurred after a prolonged period of physiological stabilization.

However, there is currently no evidence that the wings directly caused the heartbeat.

They may simply be separate manifestations of the same underlying process.

C.O.R.E. has therefore classified both phenomena as part of Corin's:

> **Progressive Biological Reawakening.**

---

# **CORIN'S OLD BELIEF**

For years, Corin's identity was built around one statement:

> **"I'm not alive nor dead."**

Her lack of a heartbeat had been one of the strongest pieces of evidence supporting that belief.

Now that evidence is changing.

Her body is no longer completely silent.

Something inside her is beating.

Something inside her is functioning.

Something inside her is beginning to behave as though it remembers how to live.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie was present when Corin first demonstrated that she could feel the heartbeat.

She placed her hand against Corin's chest.

She waited.

Then she felt it.

> **Orphie:**  
> "...You're really alive."

Corin looked uncertain.

> **"I don't know."**

Orphie smiled.

> **"Your heart seems pretty convinced."**

Corin laughed quietly.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light has refused to declare Corin fully alive.

Not yet.

There are still too many unknowns.

But she has acknowledged that the distinction may no longer be as simple as it once was.

Her assessment:

> **"Something is changing."**

> **"Let it."**

For once, there is no immediate intervention planned.

Corin's body is doing something on its own.

C.O.R.E. intends to observe rather than interfere.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin once touched her chest and felt nothing.

No heartbeat.

No familiar rhythm.

Only Ether.

She had accepted that silence as proof that whatever she was, it was not truly alive.

Now there is a sound beneath her hand.

A quiet rhythm.

Slow.

Steady.

Unmistakably biological.

Corin still does not know whether she can call herself alive.

But for the first time since her reanimation, she cannot honestly say:

> **"My body is dead."**

Something inside her has started beating again.

And nobody made it happen.
