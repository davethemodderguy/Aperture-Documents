# 1. RETIREMENT

The authorized holder of:

> **NS-009 — NIGHT STALKER**

has retired from MSAC.

As part of the retirement process, the operator voluntarily returned NS-009 to **Neri Tachyon**, its original creator.

The weapon was inspected upon return.

No significant damage was reported.

No unauthorized modifications were identified.

The presentation case and accompanying materials were also returned.

---

# 2. DEAUTHORIZATION

Neri subsequently revoked the former operator's authorization.

NS-009 is no longer registered to its previous holder.

The unit has therefore returned to:

> **Neri Tachyon — Original Owner / Creator**

The former operator retains no authorization to possess or access NS-009.

The return was conducted voluntarily and without incident.

---

# 3. THE MOMENT

The former operator placed the case on Neri's desk.

Neri opened it.

She looked at the Night Stalker.

Then at the former operator.

> **NERI:**  
> “You're sure?”

> **FORMER OPERATOR:**  
> “Yeah.”

> **NERI:**  
> “You don't want to keep it?”

The operator shook their head.

> **FORMER OPERATOR:**  
> “I think it's time someone else got a chance.”

Neri was quiet for several seconds.

Then:

> **NERI:**  
> “Okay.”

She closed the case.

> **NERI:**  
> “Thank you.”

The former operator nodded.

No further ceremony occurred.

---

# 4. MSAC RESPONSE

News of the return spread through MSAC considerably faster than C.O.R.E. expected.

The reason was obvious.

There are now **newly available opportunities** for an extremely rare Tachyon-family weapon.

Several new MSAC personnel immediately began asking:

> “Is NS-009 available?”

The answer:

> **Not yet.**

Neri has not yet announced how the returned unit will be reassigned.

# 6. TACHYON FAMILY HEIRLOOM

The significance of NS-009 extends beyond its status as an MSAC weapon.

The Night Stalker series was created by:

> **Neri Tachyon**

the final known surviving member of the Tachyon family.

The engraving:

> **TACHYON INDUSTRIES**

therefore represents more than a manufacturer designation.

The Night Stalkers are considered by C.O.R.E. to be:

> **TACHYON FAMILY HEIRLOOMS**

Their ownership is highly restricted.

Their historical significance is known to senior Aperture personnel.

Most newer MSAC personnel, however, primarily know them as:

> **“Those ridiculously rare pistols Neri made.”**

---

# 7. THE NEW HOPE

Prior to NS-009's return, the practical situation for new MSAC operators was straightforward:

> **Night Stalker program closed.**

All ten units were allocated.

No additional units would be produced.

For a new operator, obtaining one was effectively impossible.

Now:

> **One has returned.**

This does **not** mean the Night Stalker program has reopened.

It means an existing heirloom has become available for reassignment.

The distinction is significant.

Neri has not agreed to produce an eleventh unit.

There will still be:

> **10 Night Stalkers.**

No more.

No less.

---

# 8. NERI'S DECISION

Neri has not yet announced the next owner.

When asked whether NS-009 would simply be given to the next qualified operator, she answered:

> **NERI:**  
> “No.”

> **MSAC OFFICER:**  
> “Then how will someone get it?”

Neri looked at the case.

> **NERI:**  
> “I'll decide.”

The officer nodded.

> **MSAC OFFICER:**  
> “Understood.”

Neri returned to her work.

# C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT

The retirement of NS-009's holder has created an unusual situation.

The Night Stalker program remains closed.

However, one Tachyon-family heirloom has returned to its creator.

For newly assigned MSAC personnel, this represents something that previously did not exist:

> **A possibility.**

Not a guarantee.

Not an application.

Not a standard equipment request.

A possibility.

The next holder of NS-009 will not be receiving a newly manufactured Night Stalker.

They will be receiving one of the original ten.
