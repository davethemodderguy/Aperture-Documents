# **MARKSMANSHIP PROFILE**

Arcturus Lockwood is regarded by KCA firearms instructors as one of the organization's premier precision shooters.

Rather than relying on volume of fire, he emphasizes:

- Accuracy.
    
- Target discrimination.
    
- Ammunition conservation.
    
- Deliberate shot placement.
    

His guiding philosophy is often summarized by a sentence heard around the KCA firing range:

> **"One shot should solve one problem."**

---

# **DRAW SPEED**

Among KCA personnel, Arcturus is renowned for his extraordinary draw speed.

Repeated range evaluations have shown he is capable of drawing his revolver and firing all six rounds in an exceptionally short span while maintaining remarkable accuracy.

The feat has earned him the unofficial title:

> **"The Fastest Hand in the KCA."**

Instructors stress that the speed is the product of decades of disciplined practice rather than supernatural ability.

---

# **PRIMARY WEAPONS**

### **KCA Revolver – .357 Magnum**

Standard carry weapon.

Chosen for its reliability, precision, and stopping power.

Maintained personally by Arcturus.

---

### **Modernized Lever-Action Rifle – .357 Magnum**

Preferred medium-range weapon.

Features modern materials and optics while retaining the classic lever-action operating system Arcturus favors.

He has repeatedly declined offers to replace it with a self-loading rifle.

---

# **SPECIALIZED AMMUNITION**

Arcturus' ammunition is manufactured to a custom specification designed by **Senior Manager Norma Lockwood**.

The specialized **.357 Magnum** cartridges provide:

- Enhanced armor penetration.
    
- Improved terminal performance against Ether-based hostile entities.
    
- Stable ballistic characteristics under operational conditions.
    

Operational reports confirm the ammunition is highly effective against **Ether Walkers**, allowing precise engagement without resorting to high-volume suppressive fire.

Due to its cost and specialized construction, production is tightly controlled by the KCA.

---

# **WEAPON PHILOSOPHY**

Arcturus has never hidden his opinion regarding automatic firearms.

When asked why he refuses to carry assault rifles or machine guns, he usually replies:

> "If I need fifty rounds to hit one target..."

> "...I should spend more time at the range."

Despite the remark, he acknowledges that automatic weapons have an important role in tactical operations.

He simply prefers not to use them himself.

---

# **SIBLING RIVALRY**

Norma and Arcturus frequently tease one another regarding their weapon preferences.

One notable exchange recorded at the Blacksite firing range:

**Norma**

Patting her machine gun.

> "Five hundred rounds."

> "Problem solved."

---

**Arcturus**

Looking over his glasses.

> "...Or..."

He spun the cylinder of his revolver closed.

> "...Six."

---

**Norma**

> "...Mine solves bigger problems."

---

**Arcturus**

> "...Mine solves them cheaper."

Personnel nearby reported that the Accounting Department unanimously sided with Arcturus.

# **STATUS**
