# 3. TACHYON FAMILY HERITAGE

The significance of the Night Stalker extends beyond its technical characteristics.

The weapon bears the engraving:

> **TACHYON INDUSTRIES**

The engraving was initially interpreted as a manufacturer designation.

Following the Tachyon investigation, its meaning became considerably more significant.

Neri Tachyon is now formally recognized throughout the Blacksite as:

> **THE FINAL SURVIVING MEMBER OF THE TACHYON FAMILY.**

The Tachyon family was instrumental in the early development of Aperture Industries.

Among its known historical figures:

### Lewis Tachyon

Co-founder of Aperture Industries.

### David Tachyon

Responsible for foundational anomaly-containment research.

### Later Tachyon generations

Contributed to multiple scientific, engineering, and containment programs.

The family residence was ultimately destroyed during a Woodland Coorp bombing.

The event resulted in the deaths of multiple Tachyon family members.

Neri survived only because she and her younger sister had been kidnapped approximately three months before the bombing.

The kidnapping, while horrific, inadvertently prevented their presence at the residence during the attack.

Neri's younger sister did not survive the subsequent events.

Neri eventually became the final surviving member of the family.

---

# 4. THE HEIRLOOM

This has fundamentally changed how the Night Stalker is regarded.

It is no longer merely:

> **A highly advanced sidearm.**

It is now also regarded as:

> **A Tachyon family heirloom.**

Every authorized operator therefore possesses something that is directly connected to the final surviving member of one of Aperture Industries' foundational families.

The weapons are not technically ancient.

The significance is historical rather than chronological.

Neri created them.

But the name engraved on them carries generations of history.

---

# 5. BLACKSITE AWARENESS

Neri's identity as the final surviving Tachyon is no longer restricted to a small number of senior personnel.

The information is now broadly known throughout the Blacksite.

Personnel reactions have varied.

Some were surprised.

Some were fascinated.

Some immediately began reconsidering the significance of the Tachyon Industries engraving.

Others simply looked at Neri and realized:

> **The quiet little seal in the oversized coat is the last Tachyon.**

Neri herself appears largely indifferent to the attention.

She continues her normal routine.

# 9. CONSUMABLE PRODUCTION

Neri's production restriction applies specifically to the **pistols**.

It does not prohibit continued production of supporting equipment.

Therefore:

### 15-ROUND MAGAZINES

**STATUS:** CONTINUED PRODUCTION

Authorized owners will continue receiving compatible magazines through standard armament logistics.

---

### .45 ACP SUBSONIC AP

**STATUS:** CONTINUED PRODUCTION

The specialized ammunition remains available for authorized Night Stalker operators.

Production is maintained specifically so that existing Night Stalker pistols remain operational.

# 10. WHY CONTINUE MAKING AMMUNITION?

C.O.R.E. asked Neri whether continuing ammunition production contradicted her statement that the Night Stalker was:

> **"Limited Merchandise."**

> **"The guns are limited."**

Pause.

> **"Ammo isn't."**

# 11. WHY NOT MAKE MORE PISTOLS?

The answer was even simpler.

The production restriction therefore remains entirely intentional.
