# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY / APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **JOINT INTELLIGENCE DOSSIER**

### **THREAT ASSESSMENT – DIRECTOR BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Executive Personnel Only

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following confirmation that Director Belle Merryweather possesses the anomalous ability designated **Ether Stabilization**, the Krampus Compliance Authority has reassessed the security risks surrounding her.

The conclusion is unequivocal.

Belle's value is no longer limited to her position as Director of the KCA.

Her unique physiology has made her a high-priority target for hostile organizations seeking to exploit Ether.

---

## **THREAT ANALYSIS**

Intelligence gathered over recent months indicates increased interest in Belle from multiple unidentified groups.

Recovered communications repeatedly reference the following objectives:

- Capturing Belle alive.
    
- Acquiring biological samples.
    
- Understanding or replicating Ether Stabilization.
    
- Weaponizing stabilized Ether.
    
- Using Belle's biology to gain resistance to Ether corruption.
    

The intelligence division concluded:

> _"Director Belle Merryweather is no longer merely a government official. She is now considered a strategic biological asset."_

---

## **REVIEW OF PRIOR INCIDENTS**

The attempted kidnapping of Belle, previously believed to be politically motivated, has been reclassified.

Current evidence suggests the operation may have been connected to research into her anomalous physiology.

No definitive conclusion has been reached, and the investigation remains active.

---

## **HISTORICAL INVESTIGATION**

During the same review, investigators reopened files concerning Belle's childhood.

One case attracted particular attention.

Weeks before Belle was eventually found and given a new life, her biological mother had been discovered living on the streets.

Witness statements and recovered personal effects suggested she had deliberately kept Belle at a distance.

At the time, this was interpreted as abandonment.

Newly recovered evidence has led investigators to consider another possibility.

---

## **WORKING HYPOTHESIS**

Investigators now believe Belle's mother may have separated from her daughter in an attempt to protect her from individuals interested in Belle's unusual condition.

No conclusive proof has been recovered.

However, several pieces of evidence are considered consistent with this hypothesis.

The case remains open.

---

## **FOLLOW-UP INCIDENT**

Only days after Belle's mother was located, she died in what was officially recorded as a truck collision.

The investigation found insufficient evidence to classify the incident as anything other than a traffic accident.

Nevertheless, several inconsistencies prevented the file from being fully closed.

The case has since been marked for continued review.

At present, no definitive determination regarding the cause of the collision has been made.

---

## **SECURITY DIRECTIVE**

In light of Belle's confirmed Ether Stabilization ability, protective measures have been increased.

Additional protocols include:

- Enhanced executive security during travel.
    
- Restricted access to biological samples.
    
- Continuous chain-of-custody documentation for all medical specimens.
    
- Increased intelligence monitoring of organizations seeking Ether-related technologies.
    

Keith and Cinder remain assigned to Belle's executive protection detail.

---

## **FINAL NOTE**

A handwritten annotation, believed to have been added by an investigating officer after reviewing the reopened case, reads:

> _"Whether she was abandoned... or protected..._

> _...Belle deserved a childhood without fear."_

**Investigation remains active.**

**End of Dossier.**
