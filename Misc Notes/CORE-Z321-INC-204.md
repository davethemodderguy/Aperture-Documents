# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-INC-204**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT REPORT – DIRECTOR BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

**Location:** Heaven's Blacksite – Research Wing

**Personnel Involved:**

- Director Belle Merryweather
    
- Executive Secretary Cinder
    
- Director Lindervene "Endmin" Merryweather
    
- One Aperture Researcher (Identity Withheld)
    

At approximately 15:42, a verbal confrontation occurred between a junior researcher and Director Belle Merryweather.

Witnesses stated that the interaction began as inappropriate teasing. Initially, the remarks concerned Belle's short stature and her well-known fondness for carrots.

Belle ignored the comments.

The situation escalated when the researcher repeatedly addressed her by another name.

> **"Zhao."**

The researcher continued.

> **"Come on, Zhao."**

> **"That's your real name, isn't it?"**

Multiple witnesses reported Belle immediately freezing in place.

Her ears lowered.

She stopped responding.

After several more repetitions of the name...

Belle ran.

---

## **SEARCH**

Cinder and Endmin immediately began searching the Blacksite.

Approximately twelve minutes later, Belle was located alone in an unused observation corridor overlooking the snowy mountains.

She was sitting against the wall with her knees pulled tightly to her chest.

Her hybrid greatsword rested several meters away.

She had left it behind.

---

## **OBSERVATIONS**

Upon arrival, both Endmin and Cinder observed visible manifestations of Belle's Ether condition.

Golden luminescent lines glowed beneath her pink fur.

Unlike previous combat activations, no hostile threat was present.

Medical staff later concluded the Ether had responded solely to Belle's emotional distress.

Belle's breathing was irregular.

She was visibly crying.

Attempts to approach her caused her to shrink further into herself.

Without looking up, she spoke in a barely audible voice.

> **"...Leave me alone..."

Several seconds passed.

Then, almost pleading...

> **"...Don't look at me..."

---

## **RESPONSE**

Neither Endmin nor Cinder moved closer.

Neither attempted to force a conversation.

They simply remained nearby.

Several minutes passed in complete silence.

Eventually, Endmin quietly removed her own jacket and laid it beside Belle without saying a word.

Cinder sat on the floor a respectful distance away.

A tiny flame flickered between her hands, providing warmth against the cold corridor.

No one spoke.

---

## **FOLLOW-UP**

After nearly half an hour, Belle finally broke the silence.

Still staring at the floor, she whispered:

> **"...I thought..."

Her voice trembled.

> **"...I thought nobody here knew that name."

No one interrupted.

Belle wiped her eyes.

> **"...I buried it."

> **"...I buried everything that came with it."

She hugged herself tighter.

> **"...Why won't it stay buried...?"

---

## **Personnel Statements**

### Endmin

Endmin slowly sat beside Belle, careful not to crowd her.

> **"...You're my sister."

She paused.

> **"...Not because of a name."

> **"...Because you chose to be."

Belle's shoulders shook as she quietly cried.

---

### Cinder

Cinder extinguished the small flame in her hands.

> **"...I don't care who you were."

She looked directly at Belle.

> **"...I care about who stayed beside us."

> **"...That's Belle."

---

### Alice

Following the incident, Alice conducted a medical examination.

She concluded that Belle's Ether activation had been triggered by acute psychological trauma rather than physical danger.

Her recommendation was simple:

> **"Treat the emotional wound."

> **"The Ether will calm with it."

---

### Director Lucious

After reviewing witness statements, Director Lucious authorized immediate disciplinary action against the researcher responsible.

His written statement was attached to the report.

> **"A person's past is not a weapon."**

> **"No member of Aperture Industries is to use another individual's former identity, history, or trauma as a means of ridicule, intimidation, or harassment."**

> **"Such conduct is incompatible with this organization's standards."**

---

## **AFTERMATH**

Later that evening, Belle returned to Room N-07.

She spoke little.

As she quietly sat on the couch, Endmin sat beside her.

Without asking, she rested her head gently against Belle's shoulder.

A few moments later, Cinder arrived carrying two mugs of hot chocolate.

She handed one to Belle.

No one mentioned what had happened.

No one mentioned the name.

To everyone in the room, there was only one person sitting on that couch.

Not Zhao.

Not the person she had once been.

Just Belle.

And for that night, that was enough.

---

## **STATUS**

The researcher was removed from duties pending disciplinary review and mandatory investigation under Aperture's workplace conduct policies.

Belle remains medically stable.

Medical staff have noted, however, that the incident demonstrates the lingering psychological impact of experiences predating her current life.

An addendum has since been appended to Belle's personnel file:

> **"Recovery is not the absence of scars."**

> **"Sometimes, it is finding people who refuse to define you by them."**
