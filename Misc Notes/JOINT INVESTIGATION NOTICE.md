# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **GENERAL ARCHIVE – JOINT INVESTIGATION NOTICE**

### **Case Reference: LUCIUS MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Active Investigation

---

## **SUMMARY**

At the request of **Lucy Merryweather** and with authorization from **Director Lucious**, Aperture Industries has officially joined the long-running investigation into the disappearance of **Lucius Merryweather**.

The case remains active.

No conclusions have been reached.

---

## **Objective**

The investigation has three primary objectives:

- Determine the circumstances surrounding Lucius Merryweather's disappearance.
    
- Identify the individual or group responsible for taking him.
    
- Locate Lucius Merryweather or establish his fate through verified evidence.
    

Personnel are reminded that Lucius is officially classified as **Missing**, not deceased.

---

## **Assigned Resources**

The investigation has been designated a joint operation involving multiple divisions.

Participating personnel include:

- **Director Lucious** – Overall operational oversight.
    
- **Lucy Merryweather** – Banlands liaison and family representative.
    
- **Endmin (Lindervene Merryweather)** – Historical witness and Central Execution Department representative.
    
- **Neko** – Technical analysis and evidence reconstruction.
    
- **Aster** – Advisory support where her future-perception abilities may provide relevant, actionable insight.
    
- **Archive Division** – Historical record recovery and cross-referencing.
    
- **Perimeter Security** – Intelligence collection and external coordination.
    

Additional personnel may be assigned as required.

---

## **Known Evidence**

At present, investigators have confirmed only the following facts:

- Lucius Merryweather disappeared approximately four years ago while he and Endmin were playing hide-and-seek.
    
- Endmin witnessed Lucius being taken away by an unidentified individual or individuals.
    
- The last object she clearly remembers seeing was Lucius' matching bracelet.
    
- No verified sightings have been confirmed since the incident.
    

Any additional claims remain unverified pending investigation.

---

## **Family Statement**

Upon learning that Aperture Industries had committed resources to the search, Endmin remained silent for several moments.

She eventually asked one question.

> **"...You're really going to look for him?"

Director Lucious answered without hesitation.

> **"Yes."**

> **"Every unresolved case deserves a proper investigation."**

Lucy quietly placed a hand on Endmin's shoulder.

Neither mother nor daughter spoke again.

They didn't need to.

---

## **Director Lucious' Note**

Director Lucious attached the following statement to the investigation order.

> **"Time does not diminish the value of a missing person's case."**

> **"Whether someone vanished yesterday or years ago, they deserve the same commitment to finding the truth."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

The disappearance of **Lucius Merryweather** is now an active joint investigation between the Banlands authorities and Aperture Industries.

No conclusions have been drawn, and investigators continue to evaluate evidence as it becomes available.

Personnel with information relevant to the case are instructed to report it through official investigative channels immediately.

---

### **Closing Observation**

As the investigation file was formally added to the Archive Division, Endmin walked past the records room.

She paused when she saw the new label on a shelf.

**ACTIVE CASE — LUCIUS MERRYWEATHER**

For the first time in years, the file was no longer sitting among forgotten archives.

It was back on someone's desk.

Endmin smiled faintly.

> **"...Thank you."

Then she quietly returned to work, carrying with her a little more hope than she had the day before.
