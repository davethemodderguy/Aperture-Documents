### **RESTRICTED ANOMALOUS WEATHER / SAFEHOUSE INCIDENT LOG**

# **THE EMPTY PANTRY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **ANOMALOUS SUPPRESSION STORM**  
**Status:** **FOOD SUPPLY DEPLETED / SUBJECT STILL FUNCTIONAL**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The storm has now lasted long enough for Corin's food supply to run out.

Under normal circumstances, this would represent a serious logistical problem.

For Corin, it is different.

She does not biologically require food in the same way a living person does.

Her body is an Ether-sustained corpse.

She does not starve.

She does not experience ordinary hunger.

She does not require calories to remain physically functional.

But Corin still eats.

Because eating makes her feel normal.

---

# **THE LAST MEAL**

Corin opened the pantry.

Nothing.

She checked again.

Still nothing.

The refrigerator contained only empty containers.

She looked through the cabinets.

Nothing.

She stood there for several seconds.

Then quietly closed the door.

> **"...That's everything."**

---

# **WHY SHE EATS**

Corin has always tried to preserve ordinary routines.

She eats because living people eat.

She drinks because living people drink.

She sleeps because living people sleep.

She wears clothes.

She reads.

She sits by the window.

She allows Orphie to comb her hair.

These small routines make her feel less like the corpse she knows herself to be.

Food is one of them.

It is not necessary for survival.

It is necessary for something else.

**Normality.**

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

Corin opened the notebook.

She wrote:

> **No food left.**

Then:

> **I don't need it.**

A pause.

> **I know I don't need it.**

Then:

> **I just liked eating.**

She stared at the sentence.

> **It made me feel alive.**

---

# **THE EMPTY KITCHEN**

Corin eventually prepared herself a drink using the last remaining ingredients.

She sat at the table.

The empty plates remained stacked nearby.

She instinctively reached for another serving.

Then remembered.

There was nothing.

She lowered her hand.

> **"...Right."**

---

# **THE DISTURBING PART**

Corin's problem was not hunger.

It was what the empty pantry represented.

One more ordinary thing had disappeared.

First:

Orphie.

Then Guiding Light's notes.

Then the outside world.

Then sleep as an escape.

Now food.

Another small piece of her routine was gone.

She wrote:

> **"Everything that makes me feel normal keeps disappearing."**

---

# **A SMALL RITUAL**

Despite having nothing left to eat, Corin still set a place at the table.

She placed a cup in front of herself.

She sat down.

She took a slow breath.

Then pretended there was a meal in front of her.

Not because she believed it was there.

Because the routine mattered.

She picked up the empty fork.

> **"...Dinner."**

She quietly laughed at herself.

It lasted only a second.

Then the room became silent again.

---

# **THE OTHER SIDE**

At the Blacksite, Orphie learned that Corin's food supply had been exhausted.

She immediately tried to find a way through.

There was none.

The storm continued suppressing anomalous abilities.

The route remained inaccessible.

Orphie stared at the weather monitor.

> **"...She doesn't need food."**

She paused.

> **"But she likes eating."**

That distinction hurt.

Because everyone knew exactly what food meant to Corin.

It meant pretending, for a little while, that she was simply a girl living in a treehouse.

Not an Ether-reanimated corpse.

Not a medical anomaly.

Not something that required constant maintenance.

Just Corin.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle quietly prepared another care package.

Food.

Snacks.

Something sweet.

Something warm.

A small handwritten note.

She placed it beside the others.

It could not be delivered.

Not yet.

But she refused to put it away.

---

# **ORPHIE'S NOTE**

Orphie eventually wrote something herself.

She knew Corin could not receive it.

Still, she wrote:

> **"I'll bring you something good when I get home."**

She folded the note.

Held it for a moment.

Then placed it with the supplies waiting for Corin.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin does not need food to survive.

But she needs the _act of eating_ to feel connected to the life she believes she lost.

A warm meal.

A cup on the table.

A familiar routine.

Small, ordinary things.

They remind her that she is more than the condition of her body.

Now the pantry is empty.

Corin remains physically unaffected.

But psychologically, another ordinary comfort has disappeared.

She closes the kitchen cabinet.

Looks at the empty table.

And whispers:

> **"I'll eat again when Orphie comes home."**

She does not know when that will be.

But for the first time in days, the sentence sounds less like despair.

It sounds like a promise.
