### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY UPDATE**

# **CORIN — FELINE DENTITION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** Stable / Post-Recovery  
**New Trait:** **Feline Canines**

---

# **OBSERVATION**

C.O.R.E. has confirmed that Corin possesses **feline-style canine teeth**.

Her upper and lower canines are noticeably sharper and more pronounced than those of a normal human.

The teeth are proportionally consistent with her feline physiology.

They are particularly noticeable whenever Corin yawns, smiles, or opens her mouth to meow.

---

# **FUNCTION**

The fangs appear to be fully functional biological teeth.

Despite their appearance, Corin does not normally use them for aggression.

Her usual interaction remains harmless.

She has, however, retained her habit of giving people tiny warning nibbles when uncomfortable.

With the addition of actual feline fangs, personnel have been advised not to underestimate the warning.

---

# **FELINE TRAITS**

The discovery further confirms that Corin's physiology is no longer entirely human.

Her currently documented feline characteristics include:

- Cat ears.
    
- Two tails.
    
- Feline fangs.
    
- Miniature feline transformation.
    
- Feline behavioral tendencies.
    
- Preference for enclosed spaces.
    
- Affection toward yarn.
    
- Ability to loaf.
    

And, of course:

**Wings.**

---

# **SAFEHOUSE REACTION**

Neko immediately noticed the fangs.

He stared at Corin.

Corin stared back.

She opened her mouth slightly.

The fangs became visible.

Neko:

> **"...You have little vampire teeth."**

Corin:

> **"Mew."**

Belle immediately disagreed with the terminology.

> **"They're cat fangs."**

Corin appeared satisfied with this answer.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin now has cat fangs.

They are sharp.

They are adorable.

And they make her already infamous tiny warning nibble slightly more convincing.

Fortunately, Corin appears to understand the difference between:

**"Nib."**

and

**"Bite."**

C.O.R.E. strongly recommends that everyone else does too.
