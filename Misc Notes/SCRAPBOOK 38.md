# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **RECREATION COMMITTEE**

### **EVENT STATUS REPORT – BLACKSITE BOXING TOURNAMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Internal

---

# **EVENT PROPOSAL**

To encourage friendly competition and physical fitness, the Blacksite Recreation Committee proposed hosting the first annual:

> **Blacksite Boxing Tournament**

The idea was met with overwhelming enthusiasm.

Personnel immediately began discussing brackets, training sessions, and who would make it to the finals.

Then...

The committee reviewed the list of potential participants.

---

# **RISK ASSESSMENT**

Medical and Security jointly evaluated the proposal.

Several names immediately stood out.

### **Belle Merryweather**

Documented to possess exceptional physical strength, enhanced by stabilized Ether.

Medical records already note that Belle has survived impacts that would seriously injure an average person.

Recommendation:

> **Do not let her punch ordinary participants.**

---

### **Norma Lockwood**

Years of strength training, heavy equipment handling, and tactical combat experience.

Recommendation:

> **Also do not let her punch ordinary participants.**

---

### **Remielle (Cinder)**

Certified close-quarters combat specialist.

Known for extremely aggressive hand-to-hand techniques during combat operations.

Recommendation:

> **Absolutely do not let her punch ordinary participants.**

---

# **MEDICAL CONCLUSION**

After reviewing all available data, the Medical Division submitted the following summary:

> "A friendly boxing tournament should not have a realistic chance of producing multiple fractures."

The Recreation Committee unanimously agreed.

---

# **EVENT STATUS**

The boxing tournament was officially cancelled before registrations opened.

Instead, the committee proposed several lower-risk alternatives:

- Table tennis.
    
- Archery.
    
- Obstacle courses.
    
- Cooking competitions.
    
- Team relay races.
    

Medical immediately approved all of the above.
