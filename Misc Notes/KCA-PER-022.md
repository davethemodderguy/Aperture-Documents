# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **KCA-PER-022**

### **PERSONNEL PROFILE – EXECUTIVE SECRETARY "CINDER"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Assessment

---

## **SUMMARY**

Executive Secretary **Cinder** presents one of the most contrasting behavioral profiles within the Krampus Compliance Authority.

Outside of combat, she is approachable, organized, and enjoys spending her free time with friends.

During combat, her behavior depends almost entirely on whether she has access to her assigned Multi-Purpose Tactical Briefcase.

---

# **OFF-DUTY BEHAVIOR**

When not performing official duties, Cinder prefers quiet activities.

Common hobbies include:

- Reading books.
    
- Spending time with Director Belle Merryweather.
    
- Talking with Endmin.
    
- Socializing with the Friendly Roommates whenever she visits Heaven's Blacksite.
    

Personnel consistently describe her as polite, patient, and easy to talk to.

She is frequently seen helping Belle organize paperwork before joining everyone else for tea or conversation.

---

# **COMBAT PROFILE – BRIEFCASE AVAILABLE**

When equipped with her assigned Multi-Purpose Tactical Briefcase, Cinder fights with remarkable discipline.

Characteristics include:

- Careful battlefield assessment.
    
- Effective use of cover.
    
- Controlled ammunition expenditure.
    
- Rapid adaptation to changing threats.
    
- Strong coordination with Keith and Belle.
    

She continuously evaluates the battlefield before committing to an engagement.

Training officers routinely describe her as a **tactician first and a combatant second**.

---

# **COMBAT PROFILE – BRIEFCASE UNAVAILABLE**

If Cinder is deprived of her primary weapon during an active life-threatening engagement, she immediately equips her reinforced gauntlets.

Behavior changes dramatically.

Observed characteristics include:

- Relentless close-quarters assaults.
    
- Extremely high aggression toward confirmed hostile combatants.
    
- Continuous forward pressure.
    
- Little regard for her own safety while eliminating immediate threats.
    
- Exceptional tolerance to pain and fatigue.
    

She remains capable of identifying allies and does not attack friendly personnel.

However, once engaged, she becomes extremely difficult to redirect until all immediate hostile threats have been neutralized.

---

## **Operational Limitation**

Because of her singular focus during this emergency response state, Cinder is **unsuitable for missions requiring the capture of suspects after she has transitioned to gauntlet combat**.

Attempts to issue instructions such as:

> **"Leave one alive for questioning."**

have proven ineffective once she has fully committed to close-quarters combat.

For this reason, operational plans requiring prisoner capture prioritize preventing Cinder from entering this state whenever practical.

---

## **Psychological Effect**

Several investigations have noted an unintended consequence of Cinder's reputation.

Hostile personnel witnessing her defeat fellow attackers in close-quarters combat have, on multiple occasions, immediately surrendered or volunteered information without further resistance.

Interrogators have remarked that surviving suspects often become significantly more cooperative after witnessing her effectiveness.

One investigator summarized the phenomenon simply:

> **"By the time we arrived, they were already asking to be arrested."**

## **CURRENT STATUS**

The Krampus Compliance Authority continues to regard Cinder as one of its most dependable executive support personnel.

Her preferred method of operation remains the tactical use of her Multi-Purpose Tactical Briefcase in coordination with Keith and Director Belle Merryweather.

Her gauntlets are considered an emergency contingency, employed only when no other option remains.

Within the Authority, one saying has become common among executive protection teams:

> **"If Cinder still has her briefcase, the situation is under control."**

> **"If she's wearing the gauntlets..."**

> **"...make sure you're standing behind her."**
