# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **EXECUTIVE PERSONNEL STATUS REPORT**

### **SUBJECT: PROLONGED ABSENCE – CORIN LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Executive Personnel Only

---

## **OVERVIEW**

Corin Lockwood has not visited Heaven's Blacksite for an extended period.

No disciplinary action has been taken.

She continues to perform all assigned duties as Head of Accounting.

Financial reports remain punctual.

Budgets remain balanced.

Audits continue to return with Corin's characteristic precision.

Professionally...

Nothing has changed.

Personally...

Everything has.

---

## **SAFEHOUSE**

Project SAFEHOUSE remains lively.

The television is still too loud.

Neko continues creating engineering projects nobody asked for.

Norma still experiments in the kitchen.

Belle still prepares enough food for everyone.

Yet one chair remains empty.

No one has reassigned it.

No one has moved it.

Some evenings, Belle instinctively sets another cup on the table before quietly realizing who it was for.

She says nothing.

She simply leaves it there until dinner is over.

---

## **THE OFFICE**

Corin's office at KCA Headquarters has become her residence in all but name.

A folding bed.

A bookshelf.

A kettle.

Medication arranged with perfect precision.

Stacks of financial reports.

Everything has its place.

Everything is immaculate.

Access remains restricted.

The executive office employs reinforced electronic locking mechanisms intended to protect highly confidential financial records.

Entering without authorization would require invoking emergency executive override procedures, which are reserved for extraordinary circumstances.

Beyond the security implications, forcing entry would also undermine the trust the Wellness Team has worked to preserve.

As one executive summarized:

> "If we break the door down every time she's hurting..."

> "...she'll only build a stronger one."

---

## **PERSONNEL OBSERVATIONS**

**Belle Merryweather**

Every Friday, Belle still prepares Corin's favorite tea.

She pours a second cup.

It grows cold.

She quietly washes it before going to bed.

She has never stopped making it.

---

**Norma Lockwood**

Norma still finishes cooking with an extra serving.

Sometimes she catches herself calling toward the hallway.

> "...Corin, dinner—"

She stops halfway through the sentence.

The hallway remains silent.

---

**Arcturus Lockwood**

Arcturus continues to drive past KCA Headquarters after finishing his own work.

Most nights, one light is still on.

He looks toward the familiar office window.

He tips his fedora.

Then drives home.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' COMMENT**

Several executives proposed forcing Corin to take leave.

Others suggested overriding the office locks simply to bring her back.

Director Lucious rejected both proposals.

His reasoning was recorded in the meeting minutes.

> "We cannot rebuild trust by taking away someone's choices."

He continued.

> "If we must enter that office one day, it will be because there is an immediate threat to her safety."

> "Not because we are impatient."

He closed the discussion with a quieter remark.

> "I would rather wait outside her door for a month than have her believe we stopped respecting it."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin remains one of the KCA's most valuable executives.

Her work continues without error.

Her office remains occupied long into the night.

At Heaven's Blacksite, life goes on.

Movies are watched.

Meals are shared.

Conversations continue.

Yet there is an unmistakable absence.

No one speaks about it very often.

They don't need to.

Every empty chair at the table says enough.

**End of Executive Personnel Status Report**
