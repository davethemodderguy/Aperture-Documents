**Classification:** C.O.R.E. INTERNAL — LEVEL 5

**Affected Facility:** HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE  
**Threat:** SPAWN  
**Priority:** CRITICAL

---

# **DIRECTORIAL ORDER**

Following the continued escalation of SPAWN activity around Heaven's Blacksite, Director Lucious has authorized an immediate revision to perimeter engagement procedures.

Effective immediately:

> # **SPAWN PERSONNEL ARE TO BE ENGAGED ON SIGHT.**

No verbal warning is required.

No attempt to establish communication is required.

No request for withdrawal is required.

Any positively identified SPAWN personnel entering or operating within the designated Blacksite security perimeter are to be considered **HOSTILE COMBATANTS.**

---

# **1. PERIMETER ARMAMENT DIRECTIVE**

All perimeter security personnel are to be equipped with weapons appropriate for engagement against heavily equipped anomalous or supernatural targets.

Personnel are specifically advised to carry **high-caliber weaponry** where operationally appropriate.

Authorized examples include:

### **M4X**

**Caliber:** 5.95×448mm  
**Classification:** High-Caliber Assault Rifle

The M4X is approved for perimeter personnel requiring a weapon capable of engaging heavily protected targets while retaining practical field mobility.

---

### **.50 GI MAGNUM**

**Classification:** Heavy Magnum Sidearm

The .50 GI Magnum is authorized as a secondary weapon for personnel requiring increased stopping capability at close to medium engagement distances.

---

### **TRC-12G**

**Type:** High-Intensity Magnum Shotgun  
**Classification:** Close-Quarters Heavy Weapon

The TRC-12G is authorized for personnel assigned to areas where close-range engagement is considered likely.

Its use is particularly recommended for:

- Gate security.
    
- Perimeter checkpoints.
    
- Narrow access routes.
    
- Structures adjacent to the external perimeter.
    

---

# **2. PERIMETER OVERSEER ARMAMENT**

Personnel designated as **Perimeter Overseers** have been granted access to a substantially heavier weapons platform.

### **.50 CALIBER RAIL-ASSISTED ANTI-MATERIAL RIFLE**

**Classification:** ANTI-MATERIAL / LONG-RANGE  
**Authorization:** PERIMETER OVERSEER ONLY

The weapon is intended for engagement of:

- Heavily armored SPAWN personnel.
    
- Reinforced equipment.
    
- Shield-bearing targets.
    
- Light vehicles.
    
- Fortified firing positions.
    
- Other high-value hostile targets where conventional perimeter weapons may prove insufficient.
    

Perimeter Overseers are not to discharge the weapon indiscriminately.

However, once a target has been positively identified as hostile SPAWN personnel, **engagement authorization is already granted under the current directive.**

---

# **3. RULES OF ENGAGEMENT**

The previous escalation procedure is hereby suspended for SPAWN personnel.

### **PREVIOUS**

> Identify → Warn → Order Withdrawal → Engage if Necessary

### **CURRENT**

> **Identify → Engage**

SPAWN personnel attempting to approach, observe, infiltrate, or otherwise operate within the protected perimeter are to be engaged immediately.

Personnel should not expose themselves unnecessarily to establish positive identification.

Available surveillance systems, identification equipment, and command confirmation may be utilized where practical.

---

# **4. WHY THE DIRECTIVE WAS ISSUED**

SPAWN activity has demonstrated a consistent pattern of escalation.

Their personnel have repeatedly:

- Approached restricted perimeter zones.
    
- Conducted surveillance.
    
- Returned after previous encounters.
    
- Operated in coordinated groups.
    
- Carried specialized weapons.
    
- Demonstrated knowledge of Blacksite defensive positions.
    
- Refused to permanently withdraw from the surrounding area.
    

Their repeated presence has therefore been reclassified from:

> **PERIMETER DISTURBANCE**

to:

> **ACTIVE HOSTILE ACTIVITY.**

Director Lucious has determined that continued warning-based engagement is no longer an acceptable security posture.

---

# **5. RESPONSE TO SPAWN SHIELDS**

Security personnel are reminded that SPAWN has demonstrated access to protective shields capable of absorbing or resisting conventional firearm impacts.

Personnel encountering shield-bearing targets should **not** assume that a shield represents an impenetrable defense.

Heavy weapons have been specifically authorized to ensure perimeter personnel possess appropriate means of responding to such targets.

Perimeter Overseers are additionally authorized to engage fortified or heavily protected positions when operationally justified.

---

# **6. RECOVERED SPAWN EQUIPMENT**

The recent recovery of SPAWN firearms, melee weapons, shields, and anomalous equipment has further demonstrated the organization's increasing combat capability.

Recovered equipment is to remain property of Aperture Industries and is to be transferred to C.O.R.E. Research following field recovery.

Personnel are prohibited from retaining recovered SPAWN equipment for personal use without explicit authorization.

---

# **7. ALICE SECURITY CONSIDERATION**

The directive carries an additional security implication.

Alice remains under Aperture medical custody following her recovery.

She has demonstrated a severe fear response to the SPAWN symbol and has previously stated:

> **"...They hurt people."**

SPAWN's continued presence near the Blacksite therefore constitutes a potential threat to an individual already believed to have a possible connection to the organization.

Until SPAWN's intentions are established, Alice's location and medical status are to remain **restricted information.**

Personnel are not to discuss her presence with unidentified visitors, contractors, or external organizations.

---
