# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **ENGINEERING MEMORANDUM**

### **UNOFFICIAL OBSERVATION – WHAT HAPPENS WHEN NEKO GETS BORED**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Internal Humor Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

Chief Engineer Neko has demonstrated a consistent tendency to begin designing new equipment whenever he has an extended period of free time.

Most projects are harmless.

Some are extremely useful.

A concerning number begin with the phrase:

> **"...I had an idea."**

For this reason, Engineering personnel have developed an informal understanding.

**If Neko says he's bored, someone should probably check on him.**

---

## **PREVIOUS PROJECTS STARTED "BECAUSE HE WAS BORED"**

- Reinforced wing armor for Endmin.
    
- Improvements to Belle's greatsword after the baseball bat test.
    
- Experimental equipment for Aperture personnel.
    
- Multiple tools now in everyday Blacksite use.
    
- A proposal to build Cinder a flamethrower.
    

The final project was unanimously considered the point at which supervision became necessary.

---

## **KNOWN COUNTERMEASURES**

Should Neko begin sketching a questionable invention, the following personnel have demonstrated the ability to stop him.

---

### **Miss Neko**

**Success Rate:** Extremely High

Method:

Walks into the workshop.

Looks at the blueprint.

Says,

> "...No."

Project immediately stops.

---

### **Lucius**

Method:

Asks one question.

> "...Do we actually need this?"

Neko usually pauses long enough to reconsider.

---

### **Belle**

Method:

Politely asks him not to.

Neko has never ignored the request.

---

### **Endmin**

Method:

Points out every safety concern in exhaustive detail.

The discussion generally lasts longer than the project itself.

---

### **Keith**

Method:

Crosses his arms.

Stares silently.

Neko somehow understands exactly what that stare means.

---

### **Cinder**

Method:

Looks over Neko's shoulder.

Reads the design.

Asks,

> "...Who is this for?"

If the answer is "you," she immediately replies,

> "...No."

If the answer is "Belle,"

She replies even faster.

> "...Absolutely not."

---

### **Alice**

Method:

Begins calculating the number of patients she would receive if the project were approved.

The answer is usually enough to end the discussion.

---

### **Lucy**

Method:

One signature.

Or more accurately—

The lack of one.

---

### **Star**

Method:

Gets excited.

Accidentally convinces Neko the idea is even cooler.

Star is therefore **not** considered a reliable safety measure.

---

## **ENGINEERING NOTE**

Despite requiring occasional supervision, Neko remains one of Aperture Industries' most respected engineers.

Many of the Blacksite's most effective technologies originated from ideas he pursued during quiet afternoons in the workshop.

Personnel generally agree that his creativity is invaluable.

They also agree it benefits from having other people nearby.

---

A whiteboard outside Engineering currently reads:

> **Current Status of Neko**

☑ Working.

☐ Sleeping.

☐ Eating.

☑ Being supervised.

No one remembers who wrote the last line.

No one has erased it.

It has remained accurate for months.
