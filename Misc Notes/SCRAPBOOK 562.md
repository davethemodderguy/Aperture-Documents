# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / PERSONNEL CLASSIFICATION UPDATE**

# **CORIN — FELINE FORM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**New Capability:** **Full Feline Transformation**  
**Status:** Stable / Voluntary

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin was reported missing from her bed during a routine SAFEHOUSE check.

Her room appeared empty.

Her belongings were undisturbed.

Her wings were nowhere to be seen.

However, sitting comfortably on her bed was a **small white kitten**.

The kitten looked directly at the personnel member conducting the check.

It then meowed.

C.O.R.E. subsequently confirmed:

> **The kitten was Corin.**

Corin has developed the ability to assume a complete feline form.

---

# **FELINE FORM**

Corin's transformed body is that of a small, adorable white kitten.

The form is considerably smaller than her normal Cat Thiren body.

Observed characteristics include:

- White fur.
    
- Small feline body.
    
- Two tails.
    
- Feline ears.
    
- Extremely high agility.
    
- Extremely high mobility.
    
- Enhanced reflexes.
    
- Ability to communicate only through meowing.
    

Her anomalous Ether remains fully present.

Her wings are not visible in this form.

Their exact state during transformation remains under investigation.

---

# **VOCALIZATION**

Corin cannot speak normally while transformed.

Her vocal system is entirely feline.

Therefore, her primary means of communication is:

> **"Meow."**

Different tones and patterns appear to convey different meanings.

Unfortunately, nobody has yet developed a reliable translation system.

Corin appears increasingly frustrated when people misunderstand her.

---

# **THE FIRST DISCOVERY**

Neko was the first resident to encounter Corin in her feline form.

He entered her room expecting to find Corin asleep.

Instead, a tiny white kitten was sitting on her bed.

Neko stared.

The kitten stared back.

> **Neko:**  
> "...Corin?"

The kitten:

> **"Meow."**

Neko froze.

> **"...No way."**

The kitten walked over and headbutted his hand.

Neko immediately picked her up.

> **"...You're adorable."**

Corin's tail flicked angrily.

> **"Mrrrow."**

Neko immediately understood the tone.

> **"Okay, okay. Sorry."**

---

# **MOBILITY**

The feline form provides Corin with an enormous improvement in mobility.

Her small size allows her to move through spaces that her normal body cannot.

She can:

- Fit through narrow gaps.
    
- Climb furniture.
    
- Jump onto shelves.
    
- Move underneath tables.
    
- Hide inside small spaces.
    
- Move silently.
    
- Change direction extremely quickly.
    

Her agility is considerably greater than her normal form.

---

# **REFLEXES**

Corin's feline body possesses extremely fast reflexes.

She can react to sudden movement almost instantly.

Her small size also makes her difficult to catch when she does not want to be caught.

Several residents have already attempted to pick her up.

Some succeeded.

Some did not.

Neko has a particularly high success rate.

Corin claims this is because she **allows him to catch her.**

Nobody knows whether this is true.

---

# **THE TWO TAILS**

Corin retains both feline tails in her kitten form.

They provide additional balance during movement.

The tails also make her emotional state easier to read.

When curious:

**Both tails rise.**

When frightened:

**Both tails become extremely fluffy.**

When irritated:

**One tail starts flicking.**

When happy:

**Both tails move constantly.**

---

# **YARN**

The transformation has created an entirely new problem.

Corin already liked yarn in her normal form.

As a kitten, her interest has become significantly stronger.

A ball of yarn placed in front of her will almost immediately become her highest priority.

She will chase it.

Pounce on it.

Bat it around.

Hide behind it.

And occasionally fall asleep on top of it.

Neko considers this completely predictable.

---

# **THE SAFEHOUSE'S REACTION**

The SAFEHOUSE residents have collectively decided that the kitten is:

> **"Too cute."**

Corin strongly disagrees.

Unfortunately, she cannot verbally communicate this disagreement.

Her attempts usually sound like:

> **"Mrrp."**

Which everyone interprets as:

> **"She wants attention."**

Corin has learned that being unable to speak makes arguing significantly harder.

---

# **COMBAT AND SURVIVAL**

Although the feline form appears harmless, it should not be mistaken for vulnerability.

Corin retains her anomalous physiology.

Her Ether manipulation, regeneration, and other applicable abilities remain present.

Her small body also makes her exceptionally difficult to target.

However, C.O.R.E. does not currently recommend combat testing in this form.

She is a kitten.

Everyone agrees that is enough reason.

---

# **COMMUNICATION PROBLEM**

The largest limitation of the feline form is communication.

Corin cannot speak.

She can only meow.

She can communicate through:

- Meowing.
    
- Body language.
    
- Tail movement.
    
- Ear position.
    
- Facial expressions.
    
- Physical gestures.
    

She has already demonstrated that she can convey surprisingly complex emotions this way.

Whether she can communicate precise information remains uncertain.

---

# **CORIN'S OPINION**

After returning to her normal form, Corin was asked whether she liked being a kitten.

She smiled.

> **"Yeah."**

She admitted that the smaller body feels unusually light and agile.

She also enjoys being able to climb almost anywhere.

Her only major complaint was:

> **"I can't talk."**

Neko responded:

> **"You meow."**

Corin stared at him.

> **"...That's not talking."**

---

# **CURRENT CLASSIFICATION**

Corin is now recognized as possessing **two stable physical forms**:

### **Standard Cat Thiren Form**

- Humanoid.
    
- Cat ears.
    
- Two tails.
    
- Large anomalous wings.
    
- Full speech.
    
- Flight capable.
    

### **Feline Form**

- Small white kitten.
    
- Cat ears.
    
- Two tails.
    
- Highly agile.
    
- Highly mobile.
    
- Meowing only.
    
- Wings not externally visible.
    

Both forms are considered to be **the same individual.**

No separate consciousness has been detected.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

For a while, C.O.R.E. thought Corin had simply become a Cat Thiren.

Then she gained two tails.

Then she discovered she could turn into a kitten.

Now there is a small white cat wandering around the SAFEHOUSE that occasionally turns out to be Corin.

She can fit into places nobody else can.

She can run almost impossibly fast.

She can climb furniture.

She can chase yarn.

She can communicate only through meowing.

And, unfortunately for Corin's dignity, everyone thinks she's adorable.

**End of Anomalous Physiology / Personnel Classification Update**
