# **BASIC INFORMATION**

**Name:** Norma Lockwood

**Position:** Senior Manager, Krampus Compliance Authority

**Status:** Active

**Current Assignment:** Executive Operations / Acting Operational Lead (when delegated by the Director)

---

# **APPEARANCE**

### **On Duty**

Norma is most commonly seen wearing a tailored **blue executive suit**.

Her long **blonde hair** is neatly braided down her back, giving her a professional appearance that reflects her role within the KCA.

Despite the formal attire, she is frequently found in engineering workshops with sleeves rolled up, assembling prototypes or helping technicians troubleshoot equipment.

---

### **Off Duty**

Away from meetings and paperwork, Norma's wardrobe changes dramatically.

Her preferred outfit consists of:

- Oversized hoodie.
    
- Comfortable trousers.
    
- Sneakers.
    

Personnel often joke that she transforms from "Senior Manager" into "Engineering Volunteer" the moment the workday ends.

---

# **PERSONALITY**

Norma is energetic, approachable, and genuinely enjoys solving problems.

She has developed a reputation for:

- Encouraging junior employees.
    
- Remaining calm during emergencies.
    
- Taking genuine interest in engineering projects.
    
- Treating every department with equal respect.
    

Although she carries significant executive authority, she rarely acts superior to anyone.

Her office door is usually open.

---

# **ENGINEERING HOBBY**

Engineering is not part of Norma's official responsibilities.

She simply enjoys it.

If given free time, she will often wander into Engineering and begin helping with ongoing projects.

This frequently results in one particular pairing.

---

## **NEKO & NORMA**

Norma and Neko get along exceptionally well.

The reason is simple.

Neither of them can stay bored for very long.

If left unattended for an afternoon, the two are almost guaranteed to begin designing, modifying, or improving something.

Common conversation starters include:

> "What if we..."

Which is usually enough to concern everyone else in the room.

Fortunately, Miss Neko is almost always nearby to keep the projects within reason.

---

# **CALLSIGN – JUGGERNAUT**

Within tactical operations, Norma is known by the callsign:

> **JUGGERNAUT**

The designation stems from her use of specially engineered heavy protective armor designed for sustained frontline operations.

The armor emphasizes protection, stability, and endurance while remaining sufficiently mobile for command duties in hazardous environments.

Personnel describe the suit as:

> "Built to keep moving no matter what."

---

# **PRIMARY WEAPON**

Norma's assigned weapon is a belt-fed **7.62 mm machine gun** configured for sustained suppressive fire.

The weapon is issued with **Evisceration-Tipped** ammunition approved for specialized KCA operations.

The projectile design follows the same family of specialized ammunition employed in equipment issued to personnel such as Lucy and Endmin, adapted to Norma's support weapon platform.

Because of the machine gun's weight and recoil, it is considered impractical for most operators.

Norma's Juggernaut armor allows her to employ it effectively during authorized operations.
