# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL STATUS REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – DETERIORATION FOLLOWING LUMI'S ABSENCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Executive & Medical Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the temporary loss of Lumia ("Lumi") Lockwood's manifestation, Medical and Psychological Services documented a marked decline in Corin Lockwood's emotional condition.

Lumi had become Corin's primary source of emotional stability over recent months.

Although Corin remained surrounded by people who cared for her, she did not experience those relationships as interchangeable.

The absence of Lumi affected her in a way no one else could immediately compensate for.

---

# **OBSERVATIONS**

Corin's daily routine changed rapidly.

She stopped reading for long periods.

Meals became increasingly irregular.

Her accounting work, previously one of the few activities she consistently maintained, was frequently left unfinished.

She spoke less than usual.

When addressed directly, her responses were brief.

Often limited to a simple nod.

---

# **FAMILY CONTACT**

Norma and Arcturus both attempted to spend time with their younger sister.

Corin thanked them politely.

She never asked them to leave.

But she also never allowed them close in the way she had allowed Lumi.

One evening, Norma quietly said,

> "You don't have to go through this alone."

Corin stared at the ground.

After a long silence, she answered,

> "...I know."

Another pause.

> "...But I don't want someone else."

She finally looked up.

Her eyes were tired.

> "...I want my mom."

Norma had no answer.

She simply sat beside Corin until sunset.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

Psychological Services emphasize that Corin's reaction does not reflect a lack of love for her siblings or friends.

Rather, it reflects the unique role Lumi had come to occupy in Corin's recovery.

For the first time in many years, Corin had allowed herself to depend emotionally on another person.

When that source of comfort suddenly disappeared, even temporarily, the sense of loss reopened earlier wounds.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE**

The residents continued their quiet routines.

Belle still left warm milk where Corin usually sat.

Mart still brought books, even when they remained unopened.

Bao continued preparing meals.

Kent and Cinder continued checking on her from a respectful distance.

None of them tried to become Lumi.

They understood that they couldn't.

They simply remained present.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical Services documented an accelerating decline in Corin's emotional wellbeing during Lumi's absence.

Clinicians stressed that the deterioration was associated with the loss of Corin's principal emotional anchor, combined with her longstanding trauma and depressive symptoms.

They also noted that Corin continued accepting basic care from others, even when she struggled to engage with them.

This was regarded as an important sign that, despite withdrawing further, she had not completely severed her connection with those around her.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin continues to wait for Lumi's return.

She does not ask when it will happen.

She asks only whether it will.

Each evening, she still prepares two mugs.

One grows cold beside her.

The other she drinks alone.

For everyone else, Lumi's absence is temporary.

For Corin, each passing day without her mother feels much longer than the one before.

**End of Psychological Status Report**
