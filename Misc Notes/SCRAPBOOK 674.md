# **OVERVIEW**

The SAFEHOUSE was holding a routine movie night.

The selected films were the classic **Home Alone** series.

Everyone had gathered in the Central Living Area.

Blankets were everywhere.

Snacks had been distributed.

Someone had already complained about the volume.

Nobody could agree on which _Home Alone_ movie was the best.

The situation was completely normal.

Until Corin asked a question.

> **"What's everyone's full name?"**

Silence.

Everyone looked at each other.

Neko slowly lowered his snack.

> **"...Why?"**

Corin shrugged.

> **"I'm curious."**

That was apparently enough.

The SAFEHOUSE's informal full-name registry began that evening.

---

# **MOVIE NIGHT RESUMES**

After approximately twenty minutes of questioning, everyone finally returned their attention to _Home Alone_.

Kevin was still setting up traps.

Neko stole someone's popcorn.

Belle laughed at a scene she had already seen.

Star attempted to predict the next trap.

Nightshade told her to stop spoiling it.

Endmin quietly ate snacks.

Lumi sat beside her children.

Astral sat with Taiga and Mart.

And Corin, notebook still open on her lap, looked around the room.

She smiled.

Not because anything particularly special had happened.

Just because she had learned everyone's names.

And, perhaps more importantly...

she had another page filled with people she wanted to remember.
