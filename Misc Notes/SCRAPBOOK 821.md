# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The battle axe carried by **Alice** is not a conventional weapon.

At baseline, the axe possesses a distinctive **white blade** and appears physically unremarkable aside from its anomalous construction.

The weapon's most significant property is activated when Alice introduces **her own blood** into the blade.

Upon sufficient blood contact, the blade undergoes an anomalous transformation:

> **WHITE → RED**

The transformation is not cosmetic.

C.O.R.E. testing and observation indicate that the axe becomes **substantially more dangerous** following activation.

The exact mechanism by which the weapon converts biological material into increased cutting capability remains unknown.

More concerning is the fact that **Alice is physically weak.**

She does not possess enhanced strength proportional to the weapon's threat level.

She cannot casually swing the axe with superhuman force.

She does not become stronger after activation.

She does not gain increased durability.

She does not become physically faster.

She is, by all conventional measurements, still a physically frail individual.

The weapon simply becomes much more dangerous.

This distinction has been repeatedly misunderstood by hostile observers.

---

# 2. SUBJECT PROFILE

### DESIGNATION

**Resident:** Alice  
**Species:** Rabbit-like humanoid  
**Primary Weapon:** White anomalous battle axe  
**Physical Strength:** Below expected combatant baseline  
**Combat Motivation:** Extremely high when defending residents or Blacksite property  
**Anomalous Weapon Compatibility:** Exceptional

Alice is not considered a conventional frontline combatant.

She is small.

She is physically weak.

She lacks the mass and muscular strength normally associated with effective use of a heavy battle axe.

Under ordinary circumstances, an individual of Alice's physical capability would have considerable difficulty using such a weapon effectively.

This assumption becomes incorrect once the weapon's anomalous properties are considered.

---

# 3. BASELINE AXE CONFIGURATION

The axe normally presents as a white-bladed weapon.

The blade does not appear to require sharpening in the conventional sense.

Microscopic examination has produced inconsistent results.

Several examinations reported the blade as possessing an edge geometry that should not retain its observed sharpness.

One technician summarized the problem as:

> “It looks like it shouldn't cut this well.”

Another replied:

> “That's because you're assuming it follows physics.”

No further comment was recorded.

---

# 4. BLOOD-FEEDING MECHANISM

Alice possesses the ability to intentionally introduce her own blood into the axe.

The process appears deliberate.

She does not require an external source.

She does not require another individual's blood.

**Only Alice's blood has been observed producing the full transformation.**

Upon contact, the blood does not behave normally.

Rather than remaining as a biological fluid on the surface, it appears to be **absorbed into the blade.**

No conventional residue remains.

The white material progressively changes coloration.

### OBSERVED TRANSFORMATION

**Stage 0 — Dormant**

Blade remains white.

No visible anomalous activity.

↓

**Stage 1 — Blood Contact**

Alice's blood contacts the blade.

Absorption begins.

↓

**Stage 2 — Saturation**

White coloration begins disappearing.

Red coloration spreads through the blade.

↓

**Stage 3 — Activated**

Blade becomes predominantly red.

Weapon performance increases significantly.

↓

**Stage 4 — Unknown**

No reliable upper limit has been established.

C.O.R.E. strongly recommends against determining one experimentally.

---

# 5. THE RED BLADE

The red configuration is the reason the weapon has been classified as a high-threat anomalous object.

The change in appearance corresponds with a measurable increase in destructive capability.

Observed effects include:

- dramatically improved cutting performance,
- increased resistance to physical damage,
- anomalous interaction with materials normally resistant to conventional blades,
- apparent preservation of sharpness,
- and an unknown degree of interaction with anomalous targets.

The exact source of the increase remains unidentified.

It is not simply "blood coating the blade."

The blood disappears into the weapon.

The weapon then becomes stronger.

There is no conventional mechanical explanation currently accepted by C.O.R.E.

---

# 6. PHYSICAL LIMITATION OF THE USER

This section is considered essential.

**Alice is still physically weak.**

The axe does not appear to compensate for all of Alice's physical limitations.

She cannot lift arbitrary weights.

She cannot overpower physically superior individuals through strength alone.

She cannot swing the axe indefinitely.

She can become tired.

She can be physically injured.

She can be restrained.

She can be knocked down.

The anomalous weapon does not transform Alice into a superhuman combatant.

Instead, it creates a dangerous mismatch:

> **Low physical capability + extremely dangerous weapon**

This has resulted in multiple hostile personnel incorrectly assessing Alice as harmless.

That assessment has historically ended poorly.

---

# 7. WHY THE WEAPON IS PARTICULARLY DANGEROUS

The axe does not need Alice to be physically powerful if the blade itself provides the destructive capability.

Alice's primary advantages are instead:

1. **Determination**
2. **Familiarity with the weapon**
3. **Anomalous compatibility**
4. **High pain tolerance during defensive incidents**
5. **Unpredictable behavior**
6. **Complete willingness to use the weapon against perceived threats**

The fifth and sixth factors are considered particularly problematic.

Alice does not fight according to conventional combat doctrine.

She does not appear interested in demonstrating superiority.

She does not seek prolonged engagements.

When she believes somebody is threatening her friends or her home, her objective becomes extremely simple:

> **Make the threat stop.**

---

# 8. BLOOD COST

The enhancement has an obvious biological cost.

Alice must provide her own blood.

This means every activation carries a physical consequence.

Alice's existing physical weakness makes this especially concerning.

Repeated activation can result in:

- blood loss,
- weakness,
- dizziness,
- reduced coordination,
- exhaustion,
- and potentially serious medical complications.

C.O.R.E. medical personnel have repeatedly instructed Alice **not to use her own blood as an unlimited ammunition source.**

Alice's response has generally been cooperative.

Until someone threatens the Blacksite.

# 10. MEDICAL OBSERVATION

Following one confirmed activation, Alice was found sitting near the Blacksite perimeter.

The axe had returned to its white configuration.

Alice was visibly pale.

Medical personnel requested that she surrender the weapon temporarily.

Alice complied.

She then asked:

> “Did they leave?”

Medical personnel confirmed that the hostile personnel had retreated.

Alice responded:

> “Okay.”

She then attempted to return to her normal activities.

Medical personnel noted that Alice appeared significantly more concerned about the safety of the Blacksite than her own blood loss.

This behavior is consistent with previous observations.

---

# 11. WEAPON-USER RELATIONSHIP

The axe should not be regarded as a standard anomalous artifact merely carried by Alice.

Evidence suggests a much stronger relationship.

The weapon responds specifically to **Alice's blood**.

It is unclear whether:

- Alice created the weapon,
- the weapon selected Alice,
- Alice altered the weapon,
- the weapon was altered to accommodate Alice,
- or the relationship predates Alice's current residence.

No definitive answer exists.

Attempts to separate Alice from the axe for prolonged periods have been unsuccessful in establishing whether the weapon remains fully functional without her.

Further testing is not recommended without Alice's explicit cooperation.

# 12. SPAWN ASSESSMENT

SPAWN intelligence has independently observed the white and red configurations.

Their internal terminology differs considerably.

Recovered descriptions include:

> “White Axe”

> “Blood Axe”

> “Red Phase”

> “The Rabbit's Weapon”

> “Do Not Let Her Bleed”

The final designation appears to have become the preferred field instruction.

One SPAWN field manual contains the following handwritten addition:

> **IF THE AXE IS WHITE, LEAVE.**  
> **IF THE AXE IS RED, RUN.**

The manual contains no explanation for what personnel should do if the axe is already red.

A second handwritten note provides the answer:

> **“Pray.”**

# 13. C.O.R.E. ANALYST NOTE

The weapon's greatest danger is not its raw destructive potential.

It is the fact that observers tend to make the same mistake.

They see Alice.

They see a small, physically weak rabbit girl.

They see an oversized white axe.

They assume the weapon is compensating for her weakness.

This is only partially correct.

The more accurate assessment is:

> **Alice does not need to be strong if the thing she is holding is.**

Furthermore, Alice's physical weakness should not be interpreted as harmlessness.

In fact, it may make the weapon more psychologically disturbing.

A hostile combatant can reasonably expect a heavily armed, physically powerful opponent to be dangerous.

It is considerably harder to psychologically process a frightened-looking rabbit girl quietly cutting her own palm, allowing her white axe to turn red, and then asking:

> “Are you going to hurt my friends?”

# 15. FINAL ASSESSMENT

Alice remains physically vulnerable.

She can be injured.

She can become exhausted.

She can lose blood.

She cannot rely on raw strength to solve every physical confrontation.

The axe changes the equation.

Its blood-feeding mechanism allows Alice to convert a portion of her own biological resources into increased weapon capability.

The result is an anomalous weapon that becomes progressively more dangerous while its wielder becomes progressively more physically compromised.

C.O.R.E. therefore considers the red-blade state **an emergency defensive measure rather than Alice's standard combat configuration.**

The most important observation remains:

> **The red axe does not mean Alice has become stronger.**

It means:

> **Alice has decided that whatever is in front of her needs to be stopped badly enough to spend her own blood doing it.**
