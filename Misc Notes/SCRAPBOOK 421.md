# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE PERSONNEL / LONG-TERM WELFARE REPORT**

# **TWO WEEKS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Critical Psychological Concern  
**Subjects:** Corin / Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Lumi / Arcturus / Norma / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Marts  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **CRITICAL — NO MEANINGFUL RECOVERY**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

It has been **two weeks** since Orphie walked away from the treehouse and disappeared into the snow.

No confirmed sighting has been obtained.

No reliable trail has been recovered.

No message has been received.

Orphie remains missing.

Meanwhile, every attempt to stabilize Corin has failed.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin is still at the treehouse.

She has refused to permanently relocate.

She has refused to leave Orphie's belongings behind.

She has refused to accept that Orphie may not return.

More concerningly, Corin's psychological condition has continued deteriorating despite repeated attempts by SAFEHOUSE personnel and family members to reach her.

Nothing has worked.

---

# **FAILED INTERVENTIONS**

Multiple people have attempted to stay with Corin.

Some tried conversation.

Some tried companionship.

Some brought food.

Some simply sat quietly with her.

None produced a lasting improvement.

Corin either became distressed, refused interaction, or returned to speaking about Orphie.

The problem is no longer simply that Corin is alone.

She has begun actively rejecting attempts to replace the person she lost.

---

# **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus tried speaking to his sister as her older brother.

Corin listened.

She eventually said:

> "...You're not Orphie."

Arcturus had no response.

Corin continued:

> "...I know you're trying."

> "...But you're not her."

Arcturus left the room shortly afterward.

He cried outside.

---

# **NORMA**

Norma attempted a more practical approach.

She tried establishing routines.

Meals.

Sleep schedules.

Regular visits.

Conversation.

Work breaks.

Nothing lasted.

Corin eventually told her:

> "...Stop trying to fix me."

Norma:

> "...I'm not trying to fix you."

Corin:

> "...Yes, you are."

Norma went quiet.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi's attempts were different.

She did not try to reason with Corin.

She simply sat beside her.

For several hours, mother and daughter remained together without speaking.

Eventually, Corin quietly said:

> "...Mom."

Lumi looked at her.

> "...I'm tired."

Lumi held her hand.

> "...I know."

Corin pulled away.

> "...I want Orphie."

Lumi had no answer.

---

# **STAR**

Star tried to bring hope.

She brought food.

She brought small gifts.

She tried talking about happier things.

She tried making Corin laugh.

Nothing worked.

Eventually, Star began crying again.

> "...I don't know what to do."

Nightshade stayed beside her.

> "...You can't force her to heal."

Star:

> "...Then what am I supposed to do?"

Nightshade:

> "...Stay."

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts remain unwilling to approach Corin.

They remember what happened.

They are still frightened.

Occasionally, they watch the treehouse from a distance.

They do not enter.

They do not bring food.

They do not attempt to comfort her.

The creatures that once surrounded Orphie and Corin are now keeping their distance from both.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

The treehouse has become almost completely silent.

Orphie's discarded belongings remain.

The broken pencil.

The sketchbook.

The cookbook.

The hats.

Nothing has been removed.

Corin refuses to let anyone throw them away.

She also refuses to use them.

They simply remain where they were left.

---

# **THE EMPTY CHAIR**

The chair remains untouched.

Corin still occasionally sits across from it.

Sometimes she talks.

Sometimes she simply stares.

One night, Arcturus heard her say:

> "...Two weeks."

A pause.

> "...You're taking a long time."

She waited.

> "...Come home."

Nothing answered.

---

# **THE TWO-WEEK MARK**

Two weeks have now passed.

The snowfall has covered the original trail completely.

Any ordinary footprints left by Orphie are long gone.

The forest has returned to silence.

There is still no evidence confirming whether she is alive, dead, or somewhere beyond the Blacksite's known perimeter.

---

# **ORPHIE'S STATUS**

**Confirmed:**

- Left the treehouse voluntarily.
    
- Entered the snow-covered forest.
    
- Has not returned.
    
- Has not contacted anyone.
    
- Has not been reliably observed since departure.
    

**Unknown:**

- Current location.
    
- Physical condition.
    
- Whether she remains within the Blacksite.
    
- Whether she is intentionally avoiding detection.
    
- Whether she intends to return.
    

---

# **CORIN'S STATUS**

**Physical:** Functional, but increasingly neglected.

**Emotional:** Severely deteriorated.

**Social:** Withdrawn.

**Psychological:** Unstable.

**Primary fixation:** Orphie's return.

**Response to intervention:** Poor.

**Response to family:** Limited.

**Response to SAFEHOUSE:** Limited.

**Response to Marts:** Avoidant after previous incident.

---

# **THE FAMILY**

Arcturus has stopped saying that he can fix this.

Norma has stopped pretending she knows what to do.

Lumi has stopped trying to find the right words.

Star has stopped pretending that optimism alone is enough.

Nightshade remains beside her.

Nobody has stopped caring.

They simply no longer know how to help.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Two weeks ago, Orphie walked into the snow.

Nobody followed.

Two weeks later, nobody knows where she is.

And the person she left behind is falling apart.

Everyone tried.

Her brother.

Her sister.

Her mother.

Her friends.

The SAFEHOUSE.

Even the Marts, before fear drove them away.

Nobody could reach Corin.

Nobody could replace Orphie.

And nobody can tell Corin when Orphie is coming home.

The treehouse remains occupied by one person.

The second chair remains empty.

The snow continues falling outside.

And somewhere beyond the trees, Orphie remains missing.

**Day 14.**

**No contact.**

**No recovery.**

**No answers.**

**End of SAFEHOUSE Personnel / Long-Term Welfare Report**
