## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE VISITOR LOG**

## **SUBJECT: DIALYN'S REGULAR VISITS TO CORIN'S TREEHOUSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following her promotion to Human Representative and the expansion of the Krampus Compliance Authority's Customer Support Division, Dialyn's schedule became considerably more flexible.

Rather than returning directly to headquarters after official business, she developed a quiet routine.

She would visit Corin's treehouse.

No official assignment required these visits.

No supervisor instructed her to do so.

She simply... kept showing up.

---

# **THE ROUTINE**

Dialyn usually arrived in the late afternoon.

Sometimes carrying tea.

Sometimes carrying paperwork.

Sometimes carrying absolutely nothing.

Corin gradually stopped asking why she came.

Eventually, the conversation became as routine as the visits themselves.

> "Hi, Corin."

> "...Hi, Dialyn."

> "How's accounting?"

> "...Still accounting."

> "Good."

The exchange rarely became more complicated than that.

Neither seemed to mind.

---

# **THE BIG HOUSE CAT**

The enormous white striped house cat initially viewed Dialyn with suspicion.

Several visits consisted entirely of Dialyn sitting several meters away while the cat silently watched her.

She never attempted to pet it.

She never offered commands.

She simply acknowledged its presence.

> "...Hello again."

Weeks later, the cat walked over on its own.

It sniffed her hand.

Then quietly laid down nearby.

Corin noticed immediately.

> "...He likes you."

Dialyn smiled.

> "...I think he finally decided I'm not here to bother you."

---

# **THE OWL**

The white owl proved considerably less cautious.

Within minutes of Dialyn's second visit, it stole half of her bread.

By the fourth visit, it had escalated to stealing an entire sandwich.

When Dialyn looked up at the tree in disbelief, Corin merely shrugged.

> "...Congratulations."

> "...It considers you worth stealing from now."

The owl blinked innocently.

A potato bun disappeared shortly afterward.

---

# **QUIET COMPANY**

Unlike many visitors, Dialyn never tried to fill the silence.

There were afternoons where she and Corin exchanged fewer than twenty words.

Instead, they occupied themselves separately.

Corin reviewed KCA financial reports.

Dialyn answered the occasional customer call from her work phone.

Every now and then, her automatic greeting echoed through the snowy forest.

> "Hello, Krampus Compliance Authority, this is Dialyn speaking, how can I help you?"

Corin eventually began smiling every time she heard it.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE GUEST BOOK**

During one visit, Corey jokingly placed a small notebook near the entrance.

Its cover read:

> **Treehouse Visitors**

Most guests signed their names.

Dialyn's first entry consisted of only one sentence.

> "Thanks for letting me sit here."

Corin later wrote beneath it.

> "Thanks for coming."

Neither added anything else.

---

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Dialyn continues to visit Corin's treehouse whenever her schedule allows.

She has never explained why those visits became part of her routine.

Corin has never asked.

To outside observers, the two often appear to be doing nothing at all.

One quietly reviews paperwork.

The other occasionally answers a phone call.

The big white house cat sleeps beneath the porch.

The white owl watches from its favorite branch, waiting for an opportunity to steal another piece of bread.

Snow falls.

Tea grows cold.

Very little is said.

Yet somehow, neither of them is ever alone.

**End of Safehouse Visitor Log**
