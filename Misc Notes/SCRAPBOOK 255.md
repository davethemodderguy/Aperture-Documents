## **SUBJECT: SNOW BLOSSOM (_Flora nivis_)**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the gifting of a potted Snow Blossom to Dialyn by Corin and Corey, Botanical Research Division was asked to catalogue the species.

The original note attached to the gift stated:

> **Don't eat those. They're very toxic.**

Subsequent investigation determined this statement to be...

Entirely false.

Corin later admitted it was intended as a joke.

Corey nodded in agreement.

---

# **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

Snow Blossoms are small alpine flowers native to the coldest regions surrounding Heaven Blacksite.

Characteristics include:

- White petals with a faint light-blue tint.
    
- Dark green leaves adapted to freezing temperatures.
    
- Slow year-round growth.
    
- Remarkable resistance to frost.
    

The most recognizable feature is the constant shedding of microscopic crystalline particles from the petals.

The particles drift gently through the air before disappearing, giving the impression that the flower is continuously surrounded by light snowfall.

The phenomenon is purely cosmetic.

It poses no known health risk.

Many personnel have described it as:

> "...A flower that's always in winter."

---

# **EDIBILITY**

Despite the warning attached to Dialyn's flowerpot, Snow Blossoms are **not poisonous**.

They are, however...

Exceptionally bitter.

Chemical analysis indicates several naturally occurring compounds responsible for the taste.

While harmless when consumed in small quantities, Botanical Research Division strongly discourages eating the petals unless one is conducting taste research.

Or has made a very poor decision.

---

# **THE COREY INCIDENT**

During cultivation of the first potted Snow Blossoms, Corey accidentally bit into one of the petals while adjusting a stem.

Witnesses reported the following sequence of events.

Corey paused.

Chewed once.

Her smile disappeared.

She immediately spat the petal back out.

After drinking nearly an entire bottle of water, she summarized the experience as:

> "...I have made a mistake."

Corin, who had been planting beside her, laughed for approximately two minutes.

The original warning note was written shortly afterward.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Snow Blossom remains one of Botanical Research Division's favorite ornamental plants.

Its gently drifting crystalline particles have earned it a place throughout several common areas of Heaven Blacksite.

Visitors often mistake the particles for tiny snowflakes.

They are usually too distracted by the beautiful display to notice the small label beside every planter.

> **Please admire.**
> 
> **Please do not taste.**

Apparently...

One accidental taste test was enough for everyone.
