## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – INTERNAL EMOTIONAL CONFLICT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Personnel visiting Shelter T-01 have documented a pronounced fluctuation in Corin Lockwood's emotional presentation.

Medical Services emphasize that these observations do **not** indicate the presence of separate personalities or alternate identities.

Rather, Corin appears to be experiencing an intense internal conflict between two incompatible emotional states.

For ease of documentation, Psychological Services have assigned descriptive names to these recurring patterns.

* **Cheerful** — periods in which Corin is warm, sociable, and hopeful.
* **Resentment** — periods dominated by self-hatred, grief, and withdrawal.

These are not different people.

They are different ways Corin experiences herself.

---

# **"CHEERFUL"**

During these periods, Corin behaves much as she did during the months following her recovery.

Common observations include:

* Smiling naturally.
* Inviting visitors inside.
* Reading with Mart.
* Sharing meals with the white tiger.
* Leaving bread for the owl before it can steal it.
* Accepting Belle's cookies.
* Laughing at Neko's engineering ideas.
* Making tea for guests.

Personnel often describe these days as feeling "normal."

---

# **"RESENTMENT"**

During other periods, Corin becomes markedly withdrawn.

Observed behaviors include:

* Closing the treehouse for long periods.
* Declining visitors.
* Speaking only when necessary.
* Avoiding photographs.
* Extended periods of staring into the forest.
* Expressing harsh self-criticism.
* Returning to anonymous cafeteria meals.

During these episodes, Corin's view of herself becomes significantly more negative than her view of others.

Psychological Services note that the resentment is directed almost entirely inward.

---

# **OBSERVATION**

The transition between these states is not abrupt.

There is no loss of memory.

No change in identity.

No alteration in speech patterns beyond emotional tone.

Instead, it resembles an ongoing struggle.

Some days, Corin believes she deserves the life she has built.

Other days, she believes she never did.

---

# **SHELTER T-01**

The treehouse has become the place where this struggle is most visible.

Visitors sometimes arrive to find Corin happily brewing tea.

On another visit, perhaps only days later, they find the curtains closed and only paperwork waiting outside.

Neither presentation is an act.

Both are genuine.

Both are Corin.

---

# **THE WHITE TIGER & THE SNOWY OWL**

Curiously, neither animal appears to distinguish between Corin's emotional states.

On cheerful days, the tiger naps beside her.

On difficult days, it still naps beside the tree.

The owl continues watching from the branches regardless.

Neither approaches more.

Neither leaves.

Their routine does not change.

Veterinary Services have remarked that the animals appear to respond to Corin's presence rather than her mood.

---

**Lumi**

During one visit, Corin quietly apologized.

> "I'm sorry."

Lumi gently asked,

> "For what?"

Corin looked toward the snow.

> "...For not knowing which version of me you'll meet."

Lumi reached over and held her hand.

> "Both of them are my daughter."

Corin said nothing.

But she didn't let go.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services consider the fluctuating emotional states consistent with an ongoing recovery complicated by unresolved trauma.

The "Cheerful" and "Resentment" states are not separate identities, but different emotional responses that emerge over time.

Current treatment focuses on helping Corin recognize that both states are part of her experience without allowing self-hatred to define her entire sense of self.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Shelter T-01 remains quiet.

Some mornings begin with laughter and tea.

Others begin with silence and closed curtains.

No one has learned how to predict which kind of day it will be.

So the people who care about Corin have settled on something simpler.

They knock.

If she answers, they stay.

If she doesn't, they leave food, wave toward the window, and promise to visit again.

Because whether Cheerful or Resentment is louder that day...

Behind the door, it is still Corin.

**End of Psychological Observation Report**
