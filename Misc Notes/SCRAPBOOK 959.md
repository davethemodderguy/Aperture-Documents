# POST-INCIDENT REPORT

## AFTERMATH OF THE WOODLAND COORP BLACKSITE ASSAULT

**Incident:** Woodland Coorp Assault  
**Defender:** Neri  
**Attacking Force:** ~250 mercenaries  
**Outcome:** Woodland withdrawal  
**SAFEHOUSE Status:** Secure  
**Jane Doe:** Extremely proud  
**Woodland Coorp:** Experiencing an organizational crisis

---

# 1. JANE DOE'S REACTION

Jane arrived at the Blacksite shortly after the engagement.

She had already received the basic report.

Two hundred and fifty Woodland mercenaries.

One defender.

Neri.

Jane found her sitting in the cafeteria afterward.

Neri was eating cake.

Jane stopped beside the table.

> **Jane:** "I heard what happened."

Neri looked up.

> **Neri:** "Oh."

> **Jane:** "You fought 250 people?"

Neri shrugged.

> **Neri:** "They came here."

Jane stared at her for several seconds.

Then smiled.

> **Jane:** "I'm proud of you."

Neri blinked.

> **Neri:** "...Really?"

> **Jane:** "Really."

Jane sat beside her.

> **Jane:** "You protected your friends."

Neri looked down at her cake.

> **Neri:** "I didn't want them to get hurt."

Jane nodded.

> **Jane:** "I know."

---

# 2. JANE'S PROFESSIONAL ASSESSMENT

Jane's intelligence background gave her a somewhat unusual perspective on the incident.

She understood Woodland.

She knew how they thought.

She knew how they planned.

She knew what their personnel were trained to expect.

And she knew exactly how embarrassing the after-action report was going to be.

Jane read the preliminary report.

She reached the section stating that the attacking force had broken formation after encountering Neri.

She stopped.

Then laughed.

> **Jane:** "Oh, they're never going to live this down."

Neri looked over.

> **Neri:** "Why?"

> **Jane:** "Because their report is going to have to say that two hundred and fifty armed mercenaries were scared away by a ten-year-old."

Neri continued eating.

> **Neri:** "Okay."

> **Jane:** "You really don't understand how funny that is, do you?"

> **Neri:** "No."

Jane laughed harder.

---

# 3. WOODLAND COORP — EXECUTIVE REVIEW

Woodland's executive leadership held an emergency meeting following the failed assault.

The original agenda was:

1. Assess the failure.
    
2. Determine additional forces required.
    
3. Develop a second assault plan.
    
4. Recover Jane Doe.
    
5. Reassess Aperture's defensive capabilities.
    

The meeting eventually became:

1. **Why did we do this?**
    
2. **How much money are we losing?**
    
3. **Can we simply stop?**
    
4. **Does anyone know how to grow vegetables?**
    

---

# 4. EXECUTIVE DISCUSSION

> **Director:** "We lost the assault."

> **Security Chief:** "Yes."

> **Director:** "Against Aperture."

> **Security Chief:** "Yes."

> **Director:** "With two hundred and fifty personnel."

> **Security Chief:** "...Yes."

> **Director:** "And one child stopped them."

Silence.

> **Operations Director:** "Technically, she didn't stop all of them."

> **Director:** "That's not helping."

---

# 5. THE NEW STRATEGY

One executive eventually proposed a radical strategic alternative.

> **Executive:** "What if we just... stop fighting Aperture?"

Everyone looked at him.

> **Executive:** "We could shut down the hostile operations division."

> **Security Chief:** "And do what?"

> **Executive:** "Live quietly."

Another executive slowly nodded.

> **Executive:** "Humble."

> **Operations Director:** "Humble?"

> **Executive:** "Yes."

> **Director:** "You're proposing that Woodland Coorp become humble?"

> **Executive:** "I'm proposing that we survive."

Silence.

Nobody immediately objected.

---

# 6. THE PROPOSAL

The proposal began informally.

Woodland could:

- Stop attacking Aperture.
    
- Stop trying to recover Jane.
    
- Stop escalating the rivalry.
    
- Reduce military expenditure.
    
- Decommission unnecessary mercenary units.
    
- Focus on legitimate commercial operations.
    
- Avoid anomalous entities whenever possible.
    
- Live quietly.
    

One executive summarized the idea:

> **"Maybe we could just be a normal company."**

Another replied:

> **"Do we even remember how?"**

---

# 7. SECURITY DIRECTORATE RESPONSE

The Security Directorate produced a risk assessment.

### OPTION A — ATTACK APERTURE AGAIN

**Expected Result:** Unknown  
**Cost:** Extremely high  
**Probability of success:** Poor  
**Recommendation:** **No**

### OPTION B — ATTEMPT TO RECOVER JANE

**Expected Result:** Major escalation  
**Cost:** Catastrophic  
**Probability of success:** Extremely poor  
**Recommendation:** **Absolutely not**

### OPTION C — CONTINUE THE RIVALRY

**Expected Result:** Additional losses  
**Cost:** Increasing  
**Recommendation:** **Why**

### OPTION D — STOP

**Expected Result:** Everyone gets to go home  
**Cost:** Ego  
**Recommendation:** **Strongly consider**

---

# 8. JANE HEARS ABOUT IT

Jane eventually received a copy of the leaked Woodland discussion.

She read it.

Then read it again.

She looked at Neri.

> **Jane:** "Woodland might shut down."

Neri looked up from her book.

> **Neri:** "Why?"

> **Jane:** "Apparently you're making them reconsider their career choices."

Neri thought about it.

> **Neri:** "Good."

Jane laughed.

> **Jane:** "That's my girl."

---

# 9. BLACKSITE REACTION

The rest of SAFEHOUSE was considerably less dramatic about the incident.

Neri was encouraged to rest.

Medical personnel continued monitoring her because of her anemia risk.

Residents congratulated her.

Someone brought her cake.

Someone else brought her more cake.

Neri eventually asked:

> **Neri:** "Why is everyone giving me cake?"

Jane answered:

> **Jane:** "Because you scared away 250 mercenaries."

Neri looked at the cake.

> **Neri:** "...Okay."

She ate it.

---

# 10. WOODLAND'S FINAL QUESTION

The final page of Woodland's emergency strategic review contained a single handwritten question:

> **"What if we just stop?"**

Underneath it, someone wrote:

> **"Seriously."**

A third person added:

> **"I don't want to fight the ten-year-old again."**

A fourth added:

> **"Neither do I."**

The document was eventually forwarded to the executive board.

No one removed the comments.

---

# 11. CONCLUSION

Jane Doe remains extremely proud of Neri.

Neri successfully defended the people she cared about.

Woodland Coorp, meanwhile, has reached an unusual strategic crossroads.

After five years of rivalry, a compromised intelligence network, the loss of Jane Doe, a failed assault, and **250 mercenaries running from one ten-year-old**, the company's leadership is beginning to consider an alternative to escalation.

Perhaps they don't need to conquer Aperture.

Perhaps they don't need to recover Jane.

Perhaps they don't need another army.

Perhaps they could simply:

> **Shut everything down.**

> **Go home.**

> **Grow vegetables.**

> **Live a humble life.**

For the first time in Woodland's history, this is being considered a legitimate military strategy.
