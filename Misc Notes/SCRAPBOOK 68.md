# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **PERSONNEL WELFARE OBSERVATION**

### **SUBJECTS: NORMA LOCKWOOD & CORIN LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **OVERVIEW**

Following continued psychological evaluations, Medical and Wellness Services have noted a common pattern shared by **Norma Lockwood** and **Corin Lockwood**.

Although the two sisters express it differently, both carry a deep-seated fear rooted in their childhood trauma:

- Fear of being abandoned.
    
- Fear of being left completely alone.
    

Neither speaks of it openly.

Both have instead developed quiet habits that help them cope.

---

## **CORIN**

Corin visits Heaven's Blacksite frequently.

She enjoys having lunch with Belle, discussing engineering with Norma, occasionally teasing Arcturus, and helping Accounting review expenditures with the Blacksite staff.

Yet, despite being offered a room many times, she almost never stays overnight.

When asked why, her answer is always gentle.

> "...I should get back."

Or...

> "...I've still got work."

Medical staff believe those explanations are genuine—but incomplete.

Psychologists note that Corin appears most comfortable when she knows exactly where she is going next.

Remaining somewhere unfamiliar overnight can make her noticeably uneasy.

She has never asked for accommodations.

She has never complained.

She simply leaves before evening whenever possible.

---

## **NORMA**

Norma is different.

She lives in SAFEHOUSE.

She is surrounded by friends.

She knows she is safe.

Yet the nights remain difficult.

Most evenings pass without incident.

Some do not.

---

## **THE LOUNGE**

Long after the lights have gone out...

Long after the conversations have ended...

Long after everyone else has fallen asleep...

A single lamp in the SAFEHOUSE lounge is occasionally switched on.

There sits Norma.

Still wearing comfortable clothes.

A warm mug of tea resting in her hands.

The room is silent except for the faint ticking of a nearby clock.

Some nights she simply stares out the window.

Other nights she quietly cries.

Not loudly.

Not dramatically.

Just silent tears rolling down her face while she tries to steady her breathing.

Sometimes her shoulders tremble.

Sometimes she quietly hugs her knees against the sofa.

She almost always wipes away the tears before they can fall onto the cup she's holding.

By morning...

The mug has been washed.

The blanket folded.

The light turned off.

No evidence remains.

---

## **WHY NO ONE KNOWS**

SAFEHOUSE residents generally sleep through the night.

By the time anyone wakes...

Norma has already prepared breakfast.

She's smiling.

She's asking how everyone slept.

As though nothing happened.

Most mornings, nobody suspects she spent part of the night quietly fighting memories on the sofa.

---

## **BELLE'S DISCOVERY**

One morning, Belle noticed something unusual.

Norma's tea mug was already washed.

The lounge blanket had been folded.

Yet Belle distinctly remembered putting both away before going to bed.

That night...

Belle quietly woke earlier than usual.

She walked into the lounge.

Norma was there.

Curled up in the corner of the sofa.

Holding a cup of tea with both hands.

Trying very hard not to make a sound.

Belle didn't announce herself.

She simply walked over and sat beside her.

After several quiet minutes, Norma noticed her.

She looked startled.

> "...I'm sorry."

Belle tilted her head.

> "For what?"

Norma looked down.

> "...I didn't want anyone to see me like this."

Belle gently placed an arm around her shoulders.

> "...You don't have to hide from us."

Norma leaned into the hug, exhausted.

For the first time in a long while...

She let herself cry without trying to hide it.

---

## **CORIN'S REACTION**

When Corin later learned what had been happening, she became unusually quiet.

She stared into her cup of tea for several moments.

Then softly said:

> "...She's been carrying that by herself..."

Belle nodded.

Corin smiled sadly.

> "...She always tries to protect everyone else first."

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' NOTE**

Director Lucious added a confidential annotation to both sisters' personnel files.

> "Trauma often convinces people that asking for comfort is the same as becoming a burden."

> "Our responsibility is to prove that belief wrong."

---

## **STATUS**

Norma continues to experience occasional sleepless nights and recurring nightmares.

Corin continues to prefer returning home before nightfall.

Both sisters are making steady progress in their own ways, though neither journey is linear.

Within SAFEHOUSE, an unspoken change has slowly taken place.

The lounge light is no longer surprising.

If someone happens to wake in the middle of the night and finds Norma there, they no longer ask questions.

They simply make another cup of tea.

Pull up a chair.

And remind her, without needing many words, that she does not have to face the night alone.

**End of Personnel Welfare Observation**
