# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **KCA-PER-016**

### **PERSONNEL BACKGROUND ADDENDUM – KEITH**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Confidential Personnel Record

---

## **SUMMARY**

Prior to joining the Central Execution Department, Keith grew up as an orphan following the deaths of both parents in a tragic house fire.

From a young age, he assumed responsibility for protecting and raising his younger sister, becoming her sole guardian despite the hardships they faced.

This experience is believed to have strongly influenced his sense of duty, discipline, and unwavering commitment to protecting others.

---

## **Early Life**

Official records indicate Keith and his younger sister lost both parents in a residential fire during childhood.

The incident left the siblings without immediate family support.

With no one else to rely on, Keith devoted himself to keeping his younger sister safe.

He worked various jobs, learned to defend himself, and often placed his sister's wellbeing ahead of his own.

Personnel psychologists note that this period of his life shaped his tendency to prioritize the safety of others above personal comfort.

---

## **Service**

Keith later enlisted with the Central Execution Department, where his composure under pressure and protective instincts quickly distinguished him.

His performance led to his eventual selection as the permanent executive protection officer assigned to Director Belle Merryweather.

Supervisors noted that Keith treated every protectee with the same determination he once showed while protecting his younger sister.

---

## **Younger Sister**

Keith's younger sister currently serves as **Belle Merryweather's Secretary** within the Krampus Compliance Authority.

She is responsible for:

- Managing Belle's daily schedule.
    
- Organizing official documents.
    
- Coordinating meetings and correspondence.
    
- Assisting with administrative duties.
    

The siblings remain close despite their demanding careers.

Although they now work in different roles, they are often seen leaving headquarters together after long workdays.

---

## **Observation Log**

Late one evening, Belle finally finished signing the last stack of documents.

Keith stood by the office door as usual.

His sister entered carrying another folder.

She smiled apologetically.

> **"...Sorry, Director. Just one more signature."

Belle laughed.

> **"...You always say 'just one more.'"

Keith quietly accepted the completed folder from Belle's desk.

His sister looked at him.

> **"...You remembered to eat today?"

Keith answered without looking up.

> **"...Yes."

She narrowed her eyes.

> **"...That wasn't convincing."

Belle quietly reached into one of her dress pockets.

She produced a wrapped candy.

Then another.

She placed both into Keith's hand.

> **"...Emergency snacks."

Keith stared at them for a moment.

A small smile appeared.

> **"...Thank you."
