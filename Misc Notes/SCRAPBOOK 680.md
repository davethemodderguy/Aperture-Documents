## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE LOG**

# **BUNNY — CONSTANT COMPANION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 — Internal Personnel  
**Primary Subject:** Corin Lockwood  
**Secondary Subject:** Bunny  
**Location:** Treehouse  
**Status:** **BONDED / STABLE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Bunny is no longer merely a rabbit Corin rescued.

Bunny is now almost invariably found **beside Corin**.

The relationship developed naturally.

No formal bonding procedure was performed.

No behavioral conditioning was required.

Bunny simply decided that wherever Corin was...

**Bunny wanted to be there too.**

---

# **AT CORIN'S SIDE**

Bunny follows Corin throughout the Treehouse.

If Corin walks into the kitchen, Bunny follows.

If Corin goes into the garden, Bunny follows.

If Corin sits beneath the tree, Bunny sits beside her.

If Corin curls up somewhere to read, Bunny settles nearby.

If Corin changes into her tiny cat form...

Bunny follows the tiny cat.

Personnel have observed Bunny waiting outside rooms when Corin is temporarily elsewhere.

It rarely wanders far.

---

# **SLEEPING ARRANGEMENT**

Bunny sleeps with Corin.

Every night.

Initially, this was considered temporary due to Bunny's recovery.

It stopped being temporary several weeks ago.

Corin sleeps in her preferred location.

Bunny settles beside her.

Sometimes it curls against Corin's stomach.

Sometimes beneath one of her wings.

Sometimes against her back.

When Corin is in cat form, Bunny simply curls up beside the tiny cat.

Neither appears willing to change the arrangement.

---

# **THE WING SHELTER**

Corin's wings have become Bunny's preferred sleeping shelter.

At night, Corin instinctively lowers one wing around Bunny.

The feathered wing forms a small enclosure around the rabbit.

Bunny usually falls asleep almost immediately.

Corin sometimes remains awake afterward, quietly stroking its fur.

---

# **ORPHIE'S OBSERVATION**

Orphie once walked into the Treehouse late at night.

She found Corin asleep.

Bunny was curled against her.

One of Corin's wings covered both of them.

Orphie quietly whispered:

> **"You two are inseparable."**

Corin, apparently half asleep, mumbled:

> **"Bunny's warm."**

Bunny's ears twitched.

Neither woke up.

---

# **WHEN CORIN LEAVES**

Bunny becomes visibly attentive whenever Corin gets ready to leave the Treehouse.

If Corin is only going to the garden, Bunny follows.

If Corin is going farther away, Bunny waits at the entrance.

Corin occasionally turns around.

> **"I'll be back."**

Bunny remains there.

The rabbit appears to understand.

When Corin returns, Bunny immediately approaches her.

Corin usually responds by picking it up.

> **"Miss me?"**

Bunny nuzzles her.

Corin smiles.

> **"I missed you too."**

---

# **BUNNY'S FAVORITE PLACE**

C.O.R.E. personnel conducted an entirely unnecessary observational study.

Bunny's preferred locations were recorded.

The result was consistent:

**#1 — Beside Corin**

**#2 — Under Corin's wing**

**#3 — In the carrot garden**

**#4 — Beside Corin while she draws**

**#5 — Anywhere Corin happens to be**

---

# **CORIN'S NOTEBOOK**

A recent entry reads:

> **"Bunny sleeps with me now."**

Underneath:

> **"It likes my wings."**

Then:

> **"Sometimes I wake up and it's still there."**

The final line reads:

> **"I like it."**

A small drawing of Bunny follows.

It is considerably better than some of Corin's older drawings.

Still not good.

But better.

---

# **THE SIGNIFICANCE**

C.O.R.E. personnel have noted the irony.

Corin once spent an entire year beneath the same tree because she was terrified of being abandoned.

She waited alone.

Now she sleeps beneath that same treehouse roof with a small creature that refuses to leave her side.

Bunny does not ask Corin to be happy.

It does not ask her to recover.

It does not ask her to explain herself.

It simply stays.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Bunny is always there.

Beside Corin while she farms.

Beside Corin while she draws.

Beside Corin while she reads.

Beside Corin beneath the tree.

And every night, when the Treehouse becomes quiet, Bunny curls against her.

Corin lowers her wing.

The Feather Lanterns glow softly above them.

And the two sleep together.

Corin once thought being alone was all she deserved.

Now she has a tiny rabbit that apparently disagrees.

Bunny doesn't care about her past.

Bunny doesn't care about her mistakes.

Bunny doesn't care how fragile she once was.

Bunny just wants to be beside her.

And Corin seems very happy to let it.
