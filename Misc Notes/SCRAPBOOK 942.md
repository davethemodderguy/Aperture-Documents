# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. DIVISION — NERI-01 SAFETY ADDENDUM

**SUBSPACE DESIGNATION:** NERI-01  
**PRIMARY RESIDENT:** Neri  
**CURRENT STATUS:** STABLE  
**GRAVITY:** ACTIVE  
**KNOWN HAZARDS:** OUT-OF-BOUNDS / VERTICAL FALL

---

# 1. THE PROBLEM WAS "FIXED"

Neri successfully corrected the original Out-of-Bounds problem by introducing localized gravity into the subspace.

The modification solved the original issue.

People could no longer accidentally jump upward and continue floating indefinitely.

If someone jumped:

> **They came back down.**

If someone flew upward:

> **They eventually came back down.**

If someone simply stood on an island:

> **They remained on the island.**

The subspace was considerably safer.

Neri was satisfied.

C.O.R.E. was satisfied.

Everyone assumed the problem was solved.

---

# 2. THE IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Gravity solved **falling upward.**

It did not eliminate:

> **Falling downward.**

Neri's subspace still contains open space between and beneath the floating islands.

The gravity field now provides a consistent downward direction.

Unfortunately, "down" in NERI-01 does not necessarily mean:

> **"Toward the nearest island."**

It means:

> **Down.**

If an individual somehow leaves the bottom edge of a floating island without another surface beneath them, they will fall.

There is no confirmed lower boundary.

---

# 3. THE OBVIOUS CONCLUSION

Neko eventually summarized the problem:

> **"So we fixed the floating problem by adding gravity."**

Miss Neko:

> **"Yes."**

Neko:

> **"Which means people can now fall."**

Miss Neko:

> **"Yes."**

Neko:

> **"...That's technically an improvement."**

Miss Neko:

> **"Technically."**

---

# 4. SURELY NOBODY WOULD DO THAT

The general assumption was that nobody would intentionally jump off an island.

There is no reason to do so.

The islands are connected by rope bridges.

Visitors can float.

Visitors can fly.

Neri can reposition people if necessary.

The subspace is not designed around extreme sports.

Therefore, C.O.R.E. initially determined that accidental downward falls were unlikely.

The relevant safety note was consequently written as:

> **"Users are not expected to deliberately leave the island boundaries."**

This assessment lasted approximately six days.

---

# 5. INCIDENT REPORT

An unnamed SAFEHOUSE resident was observed standing near the edge of one of the islands.

Another resident reportedly asked:

> **"What happens if you jump?"**

The first resident responded:

> **"I don't know."**

A pause.

> **"Don't."**

Another pause.

> **"...Obviously."**

The individual then jumped.

The person immediately began falling.

There was approximately three seconds of silence before someone shouted:

> **"WHY WOULD YOU DO THAT?"**

---

# 6. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri was informed.

She looked over the edge.

Then she looked at the person falling.

Then she looked back at the others.

> **Neri:** "...Why?"

Nobody had a satisfactory answer.

Neri subsequently used her abilities to recover the individual.

The resident was returned safely to the island.

Neri then added another modification.

---

# 7. THE SECOND SAFETY PATCH

Neri expanded the localized gravity system.

The new system provides stronger positional stability around the constructed islands.

The intention is not to prevent gravity.

Rather, Neri is attempting to make the subspace's gravitational behavior **preferentially return individuals toward the usable environment.**

The system remains under development.

Neri has also begun considering physical boundaries around particularly dangerous edges.

This is currently considered preferable to relying on the intelligence of SAFEHOUSE residents.

---

# 8. THE ROPE BRIDGES ARE STILL THERE

The rope bridges remain the recommended method of movement.

They exist specifically so that visitors do not need to jump between islands.

Despite this, several visitors continue to prefer jumping.

Neri has expressed confusion regarding this behavior.

> **Neri:** "There's a bridge."

> **Alice:** "Jumping is faster."

> **Neri:** "You have a scooter."

Alice looked at the scooter dock.

> **Alice:** "...Not relevant."

---

# 9. CURRENT SAFETY RULE

The NERI-01 visitor rules have been amended.

### **RULE 1**

Do not jump too hard.

### **RULE 2**

Do not intentionally enter Out-of-Bounds.

### **RULE 3**

Do not jump downward into the void.

### **RULE 4**

Use the rope bridges.

### **RULE 5**

If you cannot see where you will land, **do not jump.**

### **RULE 6**

If Neri tells you not to jump, don't jump.

### **RULE 7**

Alice's scooter remains docked.

### **RULE 8**

Cake is allowed.

---

One Archivist reviewing the new safety rules reportedly asked:

Miss Neko answered:

> **"Apparently."**

# 11. CURRENT ASSESSMENT

The original Out-of-Bounds problem has been largely resolved.

### Previous problem:

**Jump upward → continue floating → potentially disappear into subspace.**

### Current problem:

**Jump downward → gravity works exactly as intended.**

Neri is currently working on making the second scenario considerably less stupid.
