## **DREAM ACCOUNT**

Endmin found herself standing in an endless gray void.

There was no floor.

No sky.

Only silence.

In the distance, Belle sat alone on a small fragment of stone suspended in the emptiness.

She wasn't crying.

She wasn't smiling.

She simply stared downward.

Around her stood indistinct figures.

Their faces could not be seen.

Their voices, however, were unmistakably hostile.

They laughed.

They mocked.

They spoke words Endmin could not fully remember after waking.

The voices blended together until they became meaningless noise.

The only thing Endmin understood was that every word was directed at Belle.

Belle never answered.

She never looked up.

---

## **ATTEMPTED CONTACT**

Endmin called Belle's name.

No response.

She began running toward the floating stone.

The closer she came, the farther away it seemed.

Eventually she reached out.

Her hand came within centimeters of Belle.

An unseen force violently pushed her backward.

She fell into the darkness below.

The last thing she saw before waking was Belle.

Still sitting alone.

Still looking at the ground.

Still surrounded by the faceless figures.
