# Executive Summary

Neri is currently spending another period in her voluntary plush form.

Unlike her normal routine, there is one particularly important difference:

**Neri is not doing chemistry.**

At all.

No molecular simulations.

No reaction modeling.

No datasets.

No papers.

No experimental records.

No mysterious 40-gigabyte folder named something completely unhelpful like:

> `final_final_actual_final_7`

This has produced an unexpected secondary effect throughout Blacksite infrastructure.

**IT is having an extremely good week.**

Neko is also having an extremely good week.

---

# 07:14 — Wake-Up

Neri is discovered on a SAFEHOUSE couch.

Nobody knows when she got there.

She is lying completely flat.

One small plush arm is hanging over the edge.

She is awake.

She is doing absolutely nothing.

This continues for approximately twelve minutes.

Neri eventually rolls onto her side.

---

# 07:31 — Breakfast

Neri is carried into the cafeteria by Alice.

Alice does not ask whether Neri wants to be carried.

Neri simply extends both plush arms upward.

Alice understands.

Breakfast consists of:

- a sandwich;
    
- fruit;
    
- a small pastry;
    
- coffee.
    

Neri eats normally.

There is no unusual reaction to the food.

She then drinks her coffee while sitting on the table.

---

# 08:02 — The Chemistry Incident That Does Not Happen

At approximately 08:02, Neko walks past the cafeteria.

He stops.

Looks at Neri.

Looks at the clock.

Looks at his tablet.

Looks back at Neri.

He then quietly says:

> "She's not going to work."

Neri shakes her head.

Neko's expression visibly improves.

---

# 08:04 — IT Department Status

A routine storage-monitoring notification appears on Neko's workstation.

Normally, this would be concerning.

Neri's research storage has historically been one of the most demanding workloads on the Blacksite network.

The previous storage incident is still referenced in IT documentation:

> **12 TB SSD — FILLED.**

Originally expected to provide substantial long-term storage for Neri's chemistry research, the drive was consumed in approximately **one month** during her latest intensive research period.

The resulting IT response was described by one Archivist as:

> "A spiritual event."

Neko's response was less poetic:

> "HOW."

Today, however:

**No new chemistry data has been generated.**

Storage growth:

**0 B.**

Neko stares at the number for several seconds.

Then closes the monitoring window.

> "Beautiful."

---

# 09:20 — Plushie Neri's Research Break

Neri is found in the library.

She has apparently selected a book.

She is not reading it.

The book is simply beside her.

Neri is lying on top of another book.

She appears comfortable.

After several minutes, she rolls onto her back.

She continues doing nothing.

---

# 10:03 — Neko Checks Again

Neko checks the research server.

Still nothing.

He checks Neri's laptop.

No new files.

He checks the network.

No unusual computational activity.

He checks the reserved research storage.

No significant change.

Neko quietly closes the monitoring tools.

> "She's actually taking a day off."

---

# 11:17 — Alice

Alice finds Neri again.

Neri is sitting on the floor.

Alice picks her up.

Neri does not resist.

Alice carries her around for approximately twenty minutes.

There is no destination.

There is no task.

Alice eventually sits down and places Neri beside her.

Neri remains there.

Neither speaks.

---

# 12:40 — Lunch

Neri returns to the cafeteria.

Today's Special is not spicy.

This is considered a successful menu decision.

Neri eats.

She drinks coffee.

She takes a small piece of dessert afterward.

No chemistry is performed during lunch.

This is apparently worth documenting because of how unusual the absence has become.

---

# 13:16 — IT Department Emergency

There is no emergency.

IT simply checks the storage server again.

Still:

**0 B additional research data.**

A junior technician asks:

> "Is this what normal days are supposed to look like?"

Neko replies:

> "Yes."

The technician looks confused.

Neko continues:

> "We're just not used to it."

---

# 14:05 — Afternoon

Neri is carried outside.

She spends approximately thirty minutes sitting beneath a shaded area.

She watches people walking past.

She occasionally rolls onto her side.

At one point, she falls asleep.

Nobody wakes her.

---

# 15:22 — Plushie Activity

Neri wakes up.

She crawls approximately two meters.

Stops.

Changes her mind.

Rolls back.

She is subsequently picked up.

No objections are recorded.

---

# 16:40 — The Laptop Remains Closed

Neri's Dell laptop remains untouched.

This is perhaps the most remarkable event of the day.

Normally, the device is an active research workstation.

During intensive periods, Neri can generate enormous amounts of chemistry-related material.

Her 12 TB SSD was previously filled in approximately one month.

The research was legitimate.

The files included:

- molecular models;
    
- chemical simulations;
    
- reaction studies;
    
- mathematical models;
    
- experimental records;
    
- visualization data;
    
- failed approaches;
    
- revised approaches;
    
- reference material;
    
- theoretical work;
    
- and approximately twelve terabytes of evidence that Neri had absolutely no intention of stopping.
    

Today:

**Laptop activity: None.**

Neko considers this a personal gift.

---

# 18:03 — Dinner

Neri eats with several SAFEHOUSE residents.

She remains in plush form.

She does not participate in conversation.

She does not need to.

At one point, someone places a small dessert beside her.

Neri immediately takes it.

---

# 20:11 — Quiet Hours

Neri returns to a quiet area of the SAFEHOUSE.

She settles down.

No research.

No calculations.

No molecular structures.

No server activity.

No 300-gigabyte simulation output appearing without warning.

Just Neri.

---

# 22:47 — Final IT Check

Neko performs one final storage check before leaving his workstation.

**Neri Research Storage:**  
No meaningful change.

**Laptop:**  
Inactive.

**Network computation:**  
Normal.

**IT personnel:**  
Alive.

Neko writes one line in the daily maintenance report:

> **"Neri is plush. Storage is safe."**

He then adds:

> **"Please let her stay plush."**

The second sentence is removed from the official report.

It is subsequently recovered from the draft history.

---

# 23:30 — End of Day

Neri is found asleep.

She is curled into a small plush seal shape.

Her laptop remains untouched.

Her research remains untouched.

Her server allocation remains untouched.

For approximately twenty-four hours, Neri has produced **zero chemistry research files**.

This is not because she has lost interest in chemistry.

It is not because her ability has diminished.

It is not because anyone has prohibited her from researching.

She simply does not have to work while she is a plushie.

And, for once, nobody is asking her to.

## C.O.R.E. Conclusion

A day in the life of Plush Neri is remarkably uneventful.

She eats.

She drinks coffee.

She sleeps.

She gets carried around.

She sits in quiet places.

She occasionally moves herself.

She occasionally does absolutely nothing.

And, most importantly:

**She does not have to prove her existence by producing twelve terabytes of chemistry research.**

For the duration of her plush state, the laboratory can wait.

The servers can wait.

The questions can wait.

Neri is allowed to simply exist.
