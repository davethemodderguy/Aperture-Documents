# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **ANOMALOUS ABILITY RECORD**

# **CORIN — ETHER MANIPULATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Ability Classification:** **ETHER DOMINANCE**  
**Known Comparand:** Dialyn  
**Status:** **ACTIVE / INNATE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin possesses a previously under-documented anomalous capability involving **Ether itself**.

Her body is not merely sustained by Ether.

It is capable of directly interacting with it.

Corin demonstrates three distinct properties:

- **Ether Manipulation**
    
- **Ether Control**
    
- **Complete Immunity to Ether Radiation**
    

The latter is particularly significant.

Corin can remain within environments saturated with Ether radiation without suffering the effects that would normally occur in other individuals.

Her resistance is comparable to that demonstrated by **Dialyn**.

---

# **ETHER MANIPULATION**

Corin can manipulate Ether directly.

She can influence its:

- Movement
    
- Concentration
    
- Distribution
    
- Shape
    
- Density
    
- Stability
    

The ability appears to be instinctive rather than formally trained.

Corin does not require conventional equipment to interact with Ether.

Her own body appears to act as a natural interface.

---

# **ETHER CONTROL**

Manipulation and control are not considered identical.

Corin can exert direct authority over Ether once she establishes contact with it.

This includes Ether that does not originate from her own body.

In sufficiently concentrated environments, she can perceive and influence surrounding Ether as though it were an extension of her own anomalous physiology.

The exact upper limit remains unknown.

Corin herself has little understanding of the ability.

When asked how she controls Ether, she simply wrote:

> **"I don't know."**

After a pause:

> **"It just listens."**

---

# **ETHER RADIATION IMMUNITY**

Corin demonstrates complete resistance to Ether radiation.

This includes prolonged exposure to concentrations that would normally be considered hazardous.

No significant cellular, neurological, or anomalous deterioration has been observed.

The immunity appears intrinsic.

It does not come from protective equipment.

It does not require activation.

It does not appear to consume energy.

Corin can simply exist inside an Ether-saturated environment.

---

# **COMPARISON WITH DIALYN**

Dialyn, a Customer Support Representative of the **Kramus Compliance Authority**, possesses a similarly exceptional tolerance toward Ether radiation.

Corin's resistance appears to operate on a comparable level.

Both can remain exposed to Ether radiation that would be dangerous to ordinary personnel.

However, Corin possesses an additional distinction:

**She can actively manipulate the Ether surrounding her.**

This makes her resistance more than simple immunity.

Her relationship with Ether is fundamentally different from that of conventional organisms.

---

# **THE BODY CONNECTION**

C.O.R.E. suspects Corin's ability is directly connected to her reanimated physiology.

Her body is already sustained by Ether.

Her biological systems and Ether-based systems are no longer clearly separable.

The distinction between:

> **"Corin"**

and

> **"The Ether sustaining Corin"**

may therefore be considerably less clear than previously assumed.

This could explain why external Ether radiation does not harm her.

Her body may simply recognize Ether as part of its normal operating environment.

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

Corin does not consider the ability impressive.

When Curious Light explained that most personnel could not safely tolerate the same radiation levels, Corin responded:

> **"So?"**

> **Curious Light:**  
> "That's extremely unusual."

Corin shrugged.

> **"I'm already a thing made of Ether."**

Curious Light immediately corrected her.

> **"You're not a thing."**

Corin looked away.

She did not respond.

---

# **POTENTIAL APPLICATIONS**

Corin's ability could theoretically make her valuable in environments involving:

- Ether-contaminated zones.
    
- Ether-based anomalies.
    
- High-radiation research sites.
    
- Damaged Ether infrastructure.
    
- Unstable Ether concentrations.
    

However, C.O.R.E. has no intention of treating Corin as a disposable radiation-resistant asset.

Her ability is considered a part of her.

Not a resource to be exploited.

---

# **LIMITATIONS**

The full limits remain unknown.

Current observations cannot determine:

- Maximum Ether concentration she can control.
    
- Maximum range of manipulation.
    
- Whether she can permanently alter Ether.
    
- Whether she can control Ether inside another entity.
    
- Whether her control improves with concentration or emotional stress.
    
- Whether her ability can be consciously expanded.
    

Further testing is currently considered unnecessary unless Corin voluntarily participates.

---

# **FINAL ASSESSMENT**

Corin's relationship with Ether is unusual even by Architectal standards.

Ether does not simply sustain her.

She can **touch it, control it, manipulate it, and exist within its radiation without harm.**

Dialyn demonstrates a comparable immunity.

Corin possesses something beyond that:

**Ether listens to her.**

The discovery also provides a possible explanation for one of the strangest aspects of Corin's existence.

Perhaps her body is not merely a corpse being kept alive by Ether.

Perhaps, after everything that happened in the laboratory, Ether became so deeply integrated into her existence that the distinction between **body, anomaly, and Ether** no longer has a clear boundary.

Corin may call herself an abomination.

She may call herself a thing.

C.O.R.E. records her differently:

**Corin — Ether-integrated anomalous individual.**

Not a laboratory object.

Not a resource.

Not a radiation-resistant tool.

**A person who happens to have an extraordinary relationship with Ether.**

**End of Anomalous Ability Record**
