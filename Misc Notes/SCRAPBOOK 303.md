# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – COMPENSATION MEMORANDUM**

### **SUBJECT: MART EMPLOYEE BENEFITS (UNOFFICIAL)**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although Marts are not employees of Aperture Industries or the Krampus Compliance Authority, personnel have gradually developed an informal tradition of "paying" them for their help.

The preferred currency is...

> **Sugary cheese crackers.**

No one remembers exactly when this tradition began.

Everyone participates anyway.

---

# **PAYMENT SYSTEM**

When a Mart helps someone, personnel often reward it with:

- Sugary cheese crackers.
    
- Small cookies.
    
- Fruit pieces.
    
- Tiny candies (approved in moderation).
    

By overwhelming preference, however, Marts choose sugary cheese crackers.

If given a choice between multiple snacks, they almost always select the crackers.

---

# **WORK ETHIC**

Researchers have confirmed that Marts do **not** expect payment.

They happily help regardless.

If no snack is offered, a Mart simply smiles and continues helping elsewhere.

If a cracker **is** offered, the usual exchange is:

Employee:

> "...Thanks for the help."

Mart:

> "Thank you!"

_Crunch._

Then it immediately goes off to help someone else.

---

# **UNOFFICIAL PAYDAY**

Every Friday afternoon, personnel from both the Blacksite and the KCA gather in common areas carrying boxes of sugary cheese crackers.

Within minutes, hundreds of Marts begin arriving from every direction.

Engineering jokingly refers to the event as:

> **"Payroll."**

No one has corrected them.

---

> **Mart Salaries**

The cabinet contains nothing except several large boxes of sugary cheese crackers.

---

**Endmin Merryweather**

She reviewed the quarterly budget.

One line item read:

> **Sugary Cheese Crackers**

She approved it without comment.

---

**Lumi**

Lumi occasionally hands out crackers one Mart at a time.

The Marts politely queue without pushing or cutting in line.

She calls it:

> "...Feeding my children."

---

**Corin Lockwood**

Corin accidentally forgot Payday once.

Approximately twenty Marts politely gathered outside her room.

One quietly said:

> "...Please?"

She laughed and apologized.

---

**Corey Lockwood**

Corey keeps emergency crackers beneath her treehouse.

The Marts somehow know exactly where the box is.

They have never opened it themselves.

---

**Star**

Star once tried paying a Mart with vegetables as a joke.

The Mart accepted them.

Then immediately traded them to another employee for a cheese cracker.

Negotiation skills have apparently improved along with vocabulary.

---

**Neko**

Research Summary:

> "...Primary compensation accepted: Sugary cheese crackers."

Below it he added:

> "...Remarkably cost-effective."

---

**Miss Neko**

She quietly refills the Engineering snack container every Thursday evening.

She has never admitted why.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Mart colony continues to work tirelessly despite having no obligation to do so.

Their "salary" remains entirely symbolic.

A sugary cheese cracker is not payment in the traditional sense.

It is simply a way for people to say:

> "Thank you."

The Marts understand that perfectly.

After receiving a cracker, they almost always tip their tiny black top hats and cheerfully say:

> "Friend!"

Before bouncing off to earn their next entirely unnecessary paycheck.

**End of Compensation Memorandum**
