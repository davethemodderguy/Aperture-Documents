# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – COGNITIVE DEVELOPMENT REPORT**

### **DESIGNATION: M.A.R.T. BLOBS – LANGUAGE ACQUISITION EVENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Research Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

On ██/██/20██, Security Division reported the disappearance of a single pocket dictionary from the Blacksite library.

The dictionary was recovered approximately three days later.

It had not been damaged.

It had, however, apparently been **read**.

By every Mart.

---

# **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

Security footage showed several Marts entering the library after hours.

One Mart climbed onto a chair.

Another pushed the dictionary onto the floor.

The colony then spent the next several days slowly transporting it between different groups throughout the facility.

Researchers initially assumed the Marts simply enjoyed carrying books.

They were mistaken.

---

# **COGNITIVE CHANGES**

Following the event, Research Division administered a new series of behavioral tests.

The results were unprecedented.

Although the Marts remain playful and emotionally expressive, their average intelligence has increased considerably.

Current estimates place an individual Mart's reasoning ability roughly equivalent to:

> **A young child.**

The Ether-linked hivemind has become noticeably more sophisticated.

Problem solving, cooperation, and contextual understanding have all improved.

---

# **VOCABULARY EXPANSION**

The Mart vocabulary has grown substantially.

Newly documented words include:

> "Hungry."

> "Cold."

> "Warm."

> "Safe."

> "Scared."

> "Lost."

> "Alone."

> "Cry."

> "Miss."

> "Sorry."

> "Please."

> "Promise."

> "Hope."

> "Remember."

> "Together."

Unlike previous additions, these words describe more complex emotions, including sadness and loss.

Researchers believe the dictionary provided vocabulary for feelings the Marts already sensed through observing humans.

---

# **A NEW QUESTION**

Several days after the incident, Corey was sitting beneath her treehouse when a Mart quietly climbed beside her.

It looked at her for several seconds before asking:

> "...Sad?"

Corey hesitated.

Then nodded.

> "...Yeah."

The Mart leaned against her.

> "...Together."

No further words were spoken.

---

# **THE FIRST APOLOGY**

One Mart accidentally knocked over a flower pot while helping Lumi.

Instead of bouncing away as usual, it looked down and quietly said:

> "...Sorry."

Lumi froze.

She knelt beside it.

> "...It's okay."

The Mart visibly relaxed.

---

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Mart colony remains fundamentally the same cheerful species created by Lumi.

They still greet strangers.

Still wear tiny top hats.

Still promise:

> "We'll be back."

But now...

They possess words for emotions they once expressed only through actions.

They know what it means for someone to be alone.

To be scared.

To miss someone.

To hope.

Research considers this a significant milestone in the colony's development.

Lumi considers it something else entirely.

When asked what she thought of the Marts becoming smarter, she looked toward the library, where dozens of blue blobs were quietly reading together.

She smiled.

> "...They're growing up."
