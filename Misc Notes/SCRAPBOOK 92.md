# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **CONFIDENTIAL PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – PERSISTENT SOCIAL WITHDRAWAL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical Director & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the return of the maternal Ether resonance, Corin Lockwood demonstrated a modest improvement in mood and emotional regulation.

However...

Her behavior did not immediately return to normal.

She continued to reside almost exclusively within her office at KCA Headquarters.

Meals were delivered there.

Work was completed there.

She rested there.

When asked whether she intended to return to SAFEHOUSE, she would politely decline.

> "...Not yet."

---

## **PATIENT ACCOUNT**

Corin later described a conversation with the Ether resonance during another late evening.

She was sitting at her desk.

The lights in the headquarters had long since been turned off.

Only her office remained illuminated.

The familiar voice spoke softly.

> **"They're waiting for you."**

Corin continued reviewing paperwork.

The voice tried again.

> **"Belle still sets out a cup for you."**

No response.

> **"Norma still brews extra tea."**

Silence.

> **"Arcturus still checks the doorway every evening."**

Corin closed the file in front of her.

Without looking up, she quietly answered.

> "...I know."

The voice waited.

> **"Then why won't you go?"**

A long silence followed.

Eventually...

Corin whispered:

> "...Because if I go back..."

> "...I'll have to believe I deserve to be there."

---

## **CLINICAL INTERPRETATION**

The attending psychologist noted that Corin's refusal was not motivated by a lack of affection for those around her.

Nor by distrust.

Instead, it reflected a deeply rooted trauma-related belief that she no longer deserved comfort, belonging, or happiness.

The report concludes:

> "Corin understands intellectually that she is cared for."

> "Emotionally, she has not yet accepted that those feelings apply to her."

---

## **OBSERVATION**

Staff noted a subtle but significant change.

The Ether resonance no longer spent its time trying to persuade Corin.

Instead...

It remained with her.

Sometimes saying nothing at all.

Sometimes only offering quiet reassurance.

One evening, after nearly an hour of silence, it simply said:

> **"You don't have to decide tonight."**

Corin rested her forehead against the desk.

She closed her eyes.

For the first time that evening...

She allowed herself to stop working.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the report before adding a handwritten note.

> "Healing is not measured by how quickly someone returns."

> "It is measured by whether they continue to believe tomorrow is worth reaching."

He paused before writing one final sentence.

> "Until Corin can believe she belongs, we will continue to leave the door open."

---

## **STATUS**

Corin remains under active medical and psychological care.

She continues to isolate herself within KCA Headquarters, though she maintains regular contact with her treatment team and fulfills her professional responsibilities.

Her colleagues continue to extend ordinary invitations—tea, dinner, conversation—without pressure or expectation.

The treatment team considers those quiet, consistent gestures to be an important part of her recovery.

They are reminders that, even on the days Corin cannot yet accept it herself, there are people who sincerely hope to see her safe, alive, and smiling again.

**End of Confidential Psychological Observation**
