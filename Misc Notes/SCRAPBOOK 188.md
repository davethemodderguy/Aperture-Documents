# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SHELTER T-01 – OPERATIONS UPDATE**

## **SUBJECT: RESIDENTIAL LOGISTICS & WHITE TIGER CARE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Shelter T-01 continues to function as Corin Lockwood's permanent residence.

Despite living in an isolated treehouse deep within the northern forest, Corin remains employed as an accountant for the **Krampus Compliance Authority (KCA)**.

Engineering, Logistics, and SAFEHOUSE personnel have gradually adapted procedures to support Corin's preferred living arrangement without disrupting her work.

---

# **WHITE TIGER**

The white tiger remains in excellent health.

Current veterinary estimates place its weight at approximately **95 kilograms**.

When standing naturally, its shoulder reaches roughly halfway up Corin's height.

Despite its size, the tiger is generally calm around familiar individuals.

It no longer actively hunts within the Blacksite.

Instead, it has become accustomed to accepting prepared meals from Corin and Veterinary Services.

Personnel have jokingly remarked that the tiger possesses an "eat first, question later" attitude.

While exaggerated, the observation has some basis in reality.

The tiger is notably unselective about food provided by trusted people.

Veterinary Services nevertheless maintain a controlled diet and remind personnel:

> **Do not feed the tiger without approval.**

A large animal will happily accept too many treats if given the opportunity.

---

# **SAFETY NOTE**

Although considerably calmer than when first encountered, the tiger remains a wild animal.

It still communicates through warning bites, growls, and other natural behaviors if it feels threatened or if someone ignores its boundaries.

Security reminds personnel that a warning bite from a ninety-five-kilogram tiger is still capable of causing serious injury.

Respect its space.

---

# **SLEEPING HABITS**

Despite spending nearly every day beside Shelter T-01, the tiger does not sleep inside the treehouse.

The reason is straightforward.

It is simply too heavy.

Engineering determined that repeatedly supporting the tiger's full weight would place unnecessary stress on the elevated structure.

The tiger appears to have reached the same conclusion on its own.

Instead, it curls up beneath the tree every evening.

Visitors frequently find it asleep against the trunk, where it serves as both an imposing guardian and, according to Belle, "the world's largest striped pillow."

---

# **SAFEHOUSE**

Following Corin's permanent relocation, her remaining personal belongings were respectfully transferred from Project SAFEHOUSE to Shelter T-01 with her approval.

Her former room has been preserved but is no longer used as her primary residence.

Residents still occasionally joke that Corin "moved out... just vertically."

---

# **KCA OPERATIONS**

Corin continues her duties as an accountant for the Krampus Compliance Authority.

Routine paperwork is delivered directly to Shelter T-01.

During severe winter conditions, when travel becomes hazardous, Logistics employs autonomous delivery drones to transport documents, supplies, and office materials.

Corin has become accustomed to hearing the faint hum of delivery drones before opening the treehouse window to retrieve another stack of financial reports.

---

# **ENGINEERING INCIDENT**

Upon learning that paperwork required regular transport between the Blacksite and Shelter T-01, Neko proposed a permanent solution.

His design consisted of a high-pressure pneumatic tube system stretching from KCA Administration to the treehouse.

Estimated length:

Approximately several kilometers.

Estimated engineering cost:

"...Don't ask."

Estimated practicality:

Debated.

Before construction could begin, Miss Neko reviewed the proposal.

She quietly removed the blueprint.

Folded it in half.

Placed it back into Neko's hands.

Then stated,

> "Use the drones."

Construction never began.

Neko maintains that the pneumatic tube "would've been awesome."

Miss Neko maintains that the surrounding forest does not need to become "the world's longest office mail chute."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Shelter T-01 continues to operate as both Corin Lockwood's home and remote workplace.

Each morning, she begins the day with a mug of tea.

The tiger stretches beneath the tree.

A delivery drone occasionally arrives carrying another bundle of accounting documents.

Corin signs invoices.

Balances budgets.

Reviews compliance reports.

Then, during her lunch break...

She climbs down the ladder to share an oversized chicken drumstick with the enormous white tiger waiting patiently below.

It is, by every official definition,

A remarkably unusual office.

**End of Shelter T-01 Operations Update**
