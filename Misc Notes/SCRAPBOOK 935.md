# APERTURE INDUSTRIES


## C.O.R.E. DIVISION — POST-TRANSFORMATION OBSERVATION REPORT

**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**STATUS:** SAFEHOUSE RESIDENT  
**CURRENT CLASSIFICATION:** THIREN / ARCHITECT-ADJACENT  
**ESTIMATED ARCHITECT POWER INHERITANCE:** ~25%  
**PRIMARY SOURCE:** Guiding Light  
**OBSERVING ARCHITECT:** Guiding Light  
**REPORT STATUS:** ONGOING

---

### 1. CURRENT CONDITION

Neri is alive, stable, and has returned to her normal physical appearance following the correction of her uncontrolled transformation.

The previously observed blue luminescent skin, malformed wing development, and fractured halo have disappeared.

They have been replaced by:

- Two fully formed wings composed of azure Light.
    
- Stable flight and hovering capability.
    
- Continued access to localized reality/subspace manipulation.
    
- Continued molecular restructuring capability.
    
- Continued anomalous Light manipulation.
    
- Retention of the crescent-moon iris.
    
- Approximately **25% of Guiding Light's power**.
    

Despite the above, Neri does not appear to fully understand what she has become capable of.

More importantly:

> **Neri does not appear to understand that she is now partially Architect.**

This distinction is important.

Neri remains Neri.

She is not an Architect in the same sense as Guiding Light, Curious Light, or Mischievous Light.

However, approximately one quarter of Guiding Light's power is now integrated into her.

For all practical purposes, Neri is **halfway between her original Thiren physiology and an Architect-derived state.**

---

# 2. NERI'S REACTION TO THE WINGS

Neri's first several attempts at using her wings have been characterized by confusion rather than excitement.

During initial observation, Neri repeatedly looked over her shoulder at them.

She eventually asked:

> **Neri:** "Why are they there?"

Guiding Light reportedly became visibly delighted.

> **Guiding Light:** "Because they're yours!"

Neri stared at the wings.

> **Neri:** "...Mine?"

> **Guiding Light:** "Yes!"

Neri attempted to touch one.

Her hand passed through the outer glow before making contact with the denser Light structure.

She immediately pulled her hand back.

> **Neri:** "They're weird."

Guiding Light:

> **"They're beautiful."**

Neri:

> "...They're blue."

Guiding Light:

> **"YES."**

---

# 3. THE FIRST FLIGHT

Neri initially believed that possessing wings meant she was required to physically flap them.

This resulted in several minutes of extremely confused wing movement.

No meaningful lift was produced.

Neri eventually stopped.

> **Neri:** "They're broken."

Guiding Light immediately corrected her.

> **Guiding Light:** "No, you're using them like bird wings."

> **Neri:** "They're wings."

> **Guiding Light:** "They're Light."

Neri paused.

> **Neri:** "...Oh."

Guiding Light instructed her to stop thinking of the wings as conventional anatomy.

The wings responded to intent.

Neri gradually lifted approximately several centimeters from the ground.

She immediately looked down.

> **Neri:** "..."

> **Bao:** "You're flying."

> **Neri:** "I'm not."

> **Bao:** "Neri."

> **Neri:** "I'm standing."

> **Bao:** "You're four feet above the floor."

Neri looked down again.

> **Neri:** "...Oh."

She then dropped.

Bao caught her.

Guiding Light reportedly found this extremely funny.

---

# 4. GUIDING LIGHT'S RESPONSE

Guiding Light has demonstrated an unusually high level of personal pride regarding Neri's development.

This is not surprising given that Neri's current Light-based abilities originated from a fragment of Guiding Light's own power.

However, Guiding Light appears particularly pleased by the fact that Neri independently discovered several applications of the power.

Of particular interest:

Neri learned to:

- suppress her own face from visual recordings;
    
- interfere with memory association;
    
- construct defensive anomalous fields;
    
- create localized subspaces;
    
- restructure physical matter;
    
- manipulate Light;
    
- and eventually manifest Architect-like physical traits.
    

None of these abilities were formally taught to her.

Guiding Light summarized the situation as:

> **"I gave her a fragment."**

Pause.

> **"She figured out the rest herself."**

Pause.

> **"I'm so proud."**

---

# 5. THE "HALF ARCHITECT" PROBLEM

Neri's current understanding of her transformation remains incomplete.

When asked what she thought had happened to her, Neri responded:

> **Neri:** "I got wings."

When asked whether she understood what that meant:

> **Neri:** "I can fly."

When asked about her connection to Guiding Light:

> **Neri:** "She fixed me."

When asked whether she understood that approximately 25% of Guiding Light's power now resides within her:

> **Neri:** "Twenty-five is a number."

When asked whether she understood that this makes her partially Architect:

> **Neri:** "No."

This appears genuine.

Neri understands the **abilities**.

She does not yet understand the **scale or significance** of those abilities.

---

# 6. GUIDING LIGHT'S INTERPRETATION

Guiding Light does not appear concerned.

In fact, she appears almost excessively enthusiastic about teaching Neri.

During one observation, Neri attempted to hide her wings.

They disappeared.

Neri looked surprised.

Guiding Light immediately smiled.

> **Guiding Light:** "You figured that out too!"

Neri:

> **"...I did?"**

Guiding Light:

> **"YES!"**

Neri:

> "...Okay."

Guiding Light:

> **"YOU HAVE NO IDEA HOW COOL THAT IS."**

---

# 7. SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATIONS

Neri continues behaving primarily like herself.

She still:

- eats cake;
    
- spends time with Bao;
    
- calls Frost "Big sis";
    
- calls Bao "Mom";
    
- works with Neko;
    
- carries far too many knives;
    
- prefers close-range combat;
    
- becomes confused when people make a significant event out of something she considers normal.
    

The wings have therefore created a somewhat unusual situation.

The SAFEHOUSE has effectively gained a resident who possesses a substantial portion of an Architect's power while that resident is still learning how wings work.

Neri does not currently behave as though she considers herself powerful.

This is considered beneficial.

However, staff are reminded that **Neri's lack of confidence in her abilities should not be mistaken for lack of capability.**

## FINAL ASSESSMENT

Neri should not currently be treated as a miniature Guiding Light, an Architect substitute, or an experimental asset.

She remains a ten-year-old SAFEHOUSE resident learning how to live with abilities that appeared far faster than her understanding of them.

Her wings are new.

Her flight is new.

Her relationship with Light is new.

Her partial Architect status is new.

For Neri, this is not an ascension.

It is simply another thing she has to learn.

For Guiding Light, however:

**This is apparently the greatest thing that has happened all week.**
