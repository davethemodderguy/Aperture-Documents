# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## SUBSPACE RESEARCH & CONTAINMENT DIVISION

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** Operational Constraint Addendum  
**SUBJECT:** Neri Subspace  
**CLASSIFICATION:** BLACKSITE / ARCHITECT-LEVEL ANOMALOUS SPACE  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE INVESTIGATION

---

### 1. PRIMARY CONSTRAINT

A previously established assumption regarding the accessibility of Neri's subspace has been formally invalidated.

**No entity possesses anomalous abilities while physically inside Neri's subspace.**

This restriction is absolute.

The affected entities include, but are not limited to:

- **Guiding Light**
    
- **Curious Light**
    
- **Mischievous Light**
    
- Any other Architect
    
- Aegis Entities
    
- Dawnbringers
    
- Thiren
    
- Human personnel
    
- Artificial systems possessing anomalous functions
    

The restriction applies regardless of the entity's normal capabilities, authority, or power classification.

---

### 2. ARCHITECTAL NULLIFICATION

Architect status provides no advantage once an individual crosses into Neri's subspace.

For example:

**Guiding Light** normally possesses capabilities vastly exceeding conventional dimensional and physical limitations.

Inside Neri's subspace:

> **Guiding Light is simply Guiding Light.**

Her Architect-derived abilities are unavailable.

The same applies to Curious Light and Mischievous Light.

No reality manipulation.  
No dimensional manipulation.  
No omnidirectional perception.  
No anomalous teleportation.  
No direct subspace manipulation.  
No ability to simply override the environment.

The subspace does not appear to recognize external authority.

---

### 3. WHY THIS MATTERS

The discovery fundamentally changes the recovery strategy for Neri.

Previously, an Architect could theoretically be considered the ultimate rescue asset.

That assumption is incorrect.

If Guiding Light enters Neri's subspace, she becomes subject to the same environmental rules as everyone else.

Consequently:

> **The person who created, located, or understands an anomalous phenomenon is not necessarily capable of controlling it from within.**

Neri's subspace is not subordinate to Architect authority.

It is **Neri's space**.

---

### 4. THE CURRENT ENTRANCE

The recently discovered opening at the original transformation site remains the only confirmed physical access point into Neri's subspace.

The opening does not lead to another dimension.

It leads directly into **Neri's subspace**.

However, the region immediately beyond the entrance is empty.

No fifteen-island archipelago is visible from the entrance.

The known archipelago remains significantly distant from the access point.

This distance is therefore considered genuine internal spatial separation rather than evidence of multiple subspaces.

---

### 5. THE FIFTEEN ISLANDS

Remote observation confirms that Neri's original fifteen-island archipelago remains intact.

The islands have not vanished.

Neri has not abandoned the subspace entirely.

She has instead been observed at a considerable distance from the known inhabited region.

This creates an unusual situation:

**Entrance → Empty Subspace → Unknown Distance → Fifteen-Island Archipelago → Neri**

The actual distance remains undetermined.

Conventional measurements may also be unreliable.

---

### 6. DRONE INCIDENT

A reconnaissance drone successfully traversed the newly discovered entrance and traveled through the subspace.

The drone eventually located the fifteen-island archipelago.

Visual confirmation subsequently identified **Neri**.

Neri appeared:

- Alive
    
- Physically intact
    
- Mobile
    
- Aware of her surroundings
    

The drone was noticed.

Neri looked directly toward it.

Azure particulate subsequently obscured the camera.

The drone was lost shortly afterward.

No conventional projectile, explosive event, or recognizable attack mechanism was detected.

Whether the drone was disabled by Neri, the environment, or an automatic property of the subspace remains **UNCONFIRMED**.

---

### 7. COMMUNICATION PRIORITY

The objective has therefore changed.

**OLD OBJECTIVE:**

> LOCATE NERI.

**CURRENT OBJECTIVE:**

> ESTABLISH COMMUNICATION WITH NERI.

The Blacksite now possesses sufficient evidence to establish that Neri is alive and remains within her subspace.

The problem is no longer locating her.

The problem is reaching her **without assuming that power can solve the problem.**

---

### 8. ARCHITECT ENTRY PROTOCOL

Any proposal involving an Architect entering the subspace must account for the following:

> **Architect abilities will cease functioning upon entry.**

Guiding Light cannot simply locate Neri from within and force open the space.

Curious Light cannot rely upon anomalous capabilities to create a solution.

Mischievous Light cannot simply alter the environment through Architect authority.

All three would be entering the subspace as individuals subject to its rules.

This makes an Architect's presence potentially valuable for **knowledge, communication, and ordinary assistance**, but not as an overwhelming solution.

---

### 9. CURRENT ASSESSMENT

Neri's subspace should no longer be classified as merely a dimensional refuge.

It is an **isolated environment governed by its own internal rules**, with demonstrated resistance to external anomalous authority.

The fact that even Architects lose their abilities inside it is considered one of the most significant discoveries in the investigation.

Neko's preliminary conclusion:

> **"If Guiding Light walks through that hole, she's not an Architect anymore."**

After several seconds of silence, he added:

> **"She's just Guiding Light."**
