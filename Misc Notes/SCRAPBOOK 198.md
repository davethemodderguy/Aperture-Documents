# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **MEDICAL INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – SHELTER T-01 UNRESPONDED STATUS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Emergency Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

A medical concern has been raised regarding Corin Lockwood following a period of severe winter isolation at Shelter T-01.

At the time of this report, Corin's condition remains unknown to most Blacksite personnel.

The incident was discovered not through routine communication...

But through the actions of the two animals that remained with her.

---

# **MISSED MEDICATION**

Due to worsening weather conditions, standard supply deliveries to Shelter T-01 were delayed.

Among the delayed supplies was Corin's prescribed medication.

Corin, already experiencing increased emotional strain due to isolation and resurfacing trauma symptoms, failed to notice the missed doses.

No communication was sent requesting assistance.

Personnel believe this was not intentional.

Corin simply continued working until she could no longer maintain her normal routine.

---

# **DISCOVERY**

The white tiger was the first to react.

Normally calm and patient, the animal began displaying unusual behavior around Shelter T-01.

It repeatedly approached the entrance.

Returned to the forest path.

Then returned again.

The snowy owl displayed similar behavior.

It flew repeatedly between the treehouse and the Blacksite perimeter.

Both animals attempted to attract attention.

---

# **ATTEMPTED ASSISTANCE**

The owl attempted to lead nearby personnel toward Shelter T-01.

However, due to the severe snowstorm, visibility was extremely limited.

The usual route to the treehouse was unsafe.

The tiger remained near the shelter, refusing to leave Corin alone.

Neither animal could bring direct help.

Neither abandoned her.

---

# **BLACKSITE STATUS**

At the time of this report:

- Lumi has not received confirmation of Corin's condition.
    
- Taiga and Astral remain unable to reach Shelter T-01 due to weather conditions.
    
- SAFEHOUSE personnel remain unaware of the severity of the situation.
    
- KCA has not received recent reports from Corin.
    

The storm has effectively isolated Shelter T-01.

---

# **THE ANIMALS**

Veterinary Services noted an unusual but important observation.

The tiger and owl are not trained rescue animals.

They do not understand emergency procedures.

They do not understand medical conditions.

They only understand that something is wrong.

And they know one thing with certainty:

Corin is not moving like she normally does.

---

# **SHELTER T-01 STATUS**

The last external observation recorded:

- The treehouse remains intact.
    
- The tiger remains stationed beneath the structure.
    
- The owl remains nearby.
    
- No external damage has been detected.
    

The interior condition of Shelter T-01 is currently unknown.

---

# **UNRESOLVED**

The storm continues.

The path remains dangerous.

Communication remains limited.

Everyone assumes Corin is simply refusing visitors again.

They do not yet know that this time...

She is unable to answer the door.

Outside the treehouse:

A large white tiger waits beneath the snow.

A white owl watches from the branches.

Both remain exactly where they have always been.

Near her.

**End of Medical Incident Report**
