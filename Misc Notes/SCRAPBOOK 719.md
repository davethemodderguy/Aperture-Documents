# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / ANOMALOUS INCIDENT LOG**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — THE EYE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Incident:** Unidentified marking on personal medical equipment  
**Status:** **UNRESOLVED**

---

# **DISCOVERY**

The incident was first noticed in the afternoon.

Alice returned to SAFEHOUSE after riding Neko's electric scooter to the Treehouse.

Her routine appeared completely normal.

Soft hat.

White hair.

Rabbit ears.

Eyepatch.

Nothing unusual.

Until Belle noticed the patch.

She stared at it.

> **"...Alice?"**

Alice looked at her.

> **"Hm?"**

Belle pointed toward her face.

> **"Your eyepatch."**

Alice touched it.

> **"What?"**

---

# **THE MARKING**

An eye had been drawn directly onto Alice's eyepatch.

It was small.

Simple.

A rough outline surrounding a dark pupil.

The drawing was not part of the original design.

Alice had not drawn it.

Corin had not drawn it.

Belle had not drawn it.

Neko had not drawn it.

No SAFEHOUSE resident admitted to doing so.

Nobody remembered seeing anyone near Alice's eyepatch.

---

# **SECURITY REVIEW**

Security footage was reviewed.

No conclusive explanation was found.

Alice's eyepatch appeared normal during an earlier recorded period.

Later footage showed the marking already present.

There was no clear moment in which another person physically approached Alice's face and drew on it.

The recording has been escalated for further technical analysis.

---

# **THE DRAWING**

The eye itself is strangely familiar.

Corin recognized the style immediately.

Not because it resembles Corin's own artwork.

Because it resembles the eyes appearing throughout her old sketchbook.

The same crude proportions.

The same deliberate pupil.

The same unsettling simplicity.

Corin did not mention this to Alice.

---

# **ALICE**

Alice examined the eyepatch in a mirror.

She tilted her head.

> **"Pretty."**

Corin stared at her.

> **"You like it?"**

Alice nodded.

> **"Now Alice has two eyes."**

Nobody knew how to respond.

Alice smiled.

Then she returned to her scooter.

---

# **NEKO**

Neko inspected the patch.

He checked the material.

The ink.

The stitching.

The surface.

He found no obvious tampering.

He eventually asked:

> **"Want me to replace it?"**

Alice immediately shook her head.

> **"No."**

> **"Why?"**

Alice touched the drawing.

> **"It looks nice."**

Neko frowned.

He did not agree.

But he left it alone.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin was the most disturbed.

She had seen eyes before.

Hundreds of them.

In Corin's own drawings.

In the pages produced after Orphie's disappearance.

In the depiction of the entity she called:

> **"The Observer."**

An eye with wings.

A halo.

An azure glow resembling Guiding Light.

Corin had once written:

> **"I am always watching."**

And now there was an eye on Alice's eyepatch.

Corin did not know what it meant.

She did not know whether it meant anything.

She only knew she didn't like it.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi inspected the drawing.

> **"Who did this?"**

Nobody answered.

She looked at Alice.

Alice shrugged.

> **"Maybe Bunny did."**

Lumi smiled faintly.

> **"Bunny isn't here."**

Alice thought about that.

> **"...Oh."**

---

# **THEORIES**

Current explanations include:

1. An unidentified SAFEHOUSE resident drew it as a joke.
    
2. The marking was made while Alice was asleep.
    
3. The marking existed previously and was unnoticed.
    
4. The footage is incomplete.
    
5. The marking is anomalous.
    
6. Someone is deliberately attempting to imitate the imagery associated with Corin's drawings.
    
7. Alice herself may have interacted with the patch without remembering.
    

No theory has been confirmed.

---

# **ADDITIONAL OBSERVATION**

The marking has not been removed.

Attempts to wipe it away have been unsuccessful.

The surface of the eyepatch behaves normally.

However, the drawing itself does not appear to respond to cleaning.

Replacement of the eyepatch has been proposed.

Alice refuses.

Her explanation remains:

> **"I like my eye."**

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

Nobody drew the eye.

At least, nobody admits to it.

Security cannot identify when it appeared.

Neko cannot determine how the marking was applied.

Corin recognizes the imagery.

Alice simply thinks it looks pretty.

For now, the eyepatch remains exactly where it is.

An eyepatch covering a blind eye.

With another eye drawn over it.

Watching nothing.

Or perhaps—

**something.**
