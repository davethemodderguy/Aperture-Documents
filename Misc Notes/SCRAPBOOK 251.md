# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **FACILITY OBSERVATION RECORD**

### **SUBJECT: DIALYN'S COMPANION ANIMAL**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Personnel have reported that Dialyn is frequently accompanied by a small white cat.

The cat appears almost exclusively alongside Dialyn, following her between offices, meeting rooms, and, on occasion, during visits to Heaven Blacksite.

Unlike her childhood nickname for her younger sister, this companion is an actual cat.

---

# **DESCRIPTION**

**Species:** Domestic Cat

**Coat:** Long-haired, white

**Temperament:**

- Calm.
    
- Friendly.
    
- Exceptionally affectionate.
    
- Enjoys being carried.
    
- Frequently naps in warm locations.
    

The cat has developed a habit of quietly walking behind Dialyn wherever she goes.

Should Dialyn stop walking, the cat usually sits beside her feet.

If she remains seated for long enough, the cat will eventually climb onto her lap without invitation.

---

# **BEHAVIOR**

Unlike most office animals, the cat appears unusually comfortable around large groups of people.

It has become something of an unofficial morale booster within the Krampus Compliance Authority.

Employees have reported seeing it:

- Sleeping beside Dialyn during paperwork.
    
- Watching customer support calls from the edge of her desk.
    
- Following her through hallways.
    
- Greeting coworkers at the office entrance.
    

The cat has never displayed aggressive behavior toward personnel.

---

# **VISITS TO HEAVEN BLACKSITE**

During Dialyn's visits to Project SAFEHOUSE, the cat accompanies her whenever conditions permit.

Initial concerns regarding Corin's large white striped house cat proved unfounded.

The much larger feline briefly inspected the newcomer.

After determining it posed no threat, it returned to lying beside Corin.

The smaller cat appeared perfectly content with this arrangement.

Personnel have since observed the two peacefully sharing the area around the treehouse, with the smaller cat wisely keeping a respectful distance.

The white owl has attempted to steal the cat's food on two documented occasions.

Both attempts failed.

The cat simply stared at the owl until it lost interest.

Dialyn smiled.

> "...I think..."

The white cat has become a familiar sight throughout both the Krampus Compliance Authority and Heaven Blacksite.

Employees have quietly accepted that if Dialyn is nearby, the cat probably is too.

Likewise, if the little white cat is seen wandering a hallway with unusual confidence...

Dialyn is usually only a few steps behind.
