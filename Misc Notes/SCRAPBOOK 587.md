### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / FELINE FORM OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — GLASS JAR CONFINEMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Form:** Miniature Feline  
**Phenomenon:** **Self-Confinement in Glass Containers**  
**Status:** Confirmed

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's extremely small size has produced an unexpected logistical problem.

She can fit inside certain glass jars.

More importantly:

> **She can get herself stuck inside them.**

Her palm-sized feline form is small enough to enter containers that would normally be completely unsuitable for housing a person.

---

# **THE FIRST INCIDENT**

Corin was found sitting inside a glass jar.

Nobody knew how she got there.

She was simply sitting at the bottom, wings folded around herself.

The opening was too narrow for her to comfortably turn around.

She appeared completely calm.

Neko looked at the jar.

Then at Corin.

Then back at the jar.

> **"...How?"**

Corin stared back.

No answer was possible.

She could no longer meow.

---

# **SELF-CONFINEMENT**

Corin does not appear to intentionally imprison herself.

Rather, she sometimes enters containers because they are:

- Enclosed.
    
- Quiet.
    
- Warm.
    
- Difficult for others to disturb.
    

Once inside, however, her small wings and body positioning can make it difficult for her to exit.

This is especially problematic when she is tired.

---

# **WINGS**

Corin's wings make the situation considerably more complicated.

When folded, they rest closely against her body.

This allows her to fit through surprisingly narrow openings.

Once inside the jar, however, she may not have enough room to properly extend them.

Her wings therefore cannot always be used to assist her escape.

---

# **RECOVERY CONSIDERATION**

Because Corin is still recovering from mercury poisoning, personnel are instructed to pay particular attention to where she goes.

A healthy Corin getting temporarily stuck in a jar would be inconvenient.

A severely weakened Corin getting stuck in one is considerably more serious.

Personnel should never assume that she can free herself simply because she entered voluntarily.

---

# **BELLE'S RESPONSE**

Belle discovered Corin inside a jar on one occasion.

She carefully removed the container and gently tipped it sideways.

Corin slowly crawled out.

Belle stared at her.

> **"You can't keep doing this."**

Corin looked at the jar.

Then at Belle.

Then back at the jar.

She appeared to disagree.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RULE**

A new informal rule has been established:

> **If Corin can fit inside it, someone should probably keep an eye on it.**

Glass jars, bottles, containers, boxes, and other small enclosures are no longer considered automatically safe around the miniature Corin.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's miniature form gives her exceptional mobility.

She can squeeze into places no normal person could reach.

Unfortunately, this also means she can squeeze into places from which she cannot easily escape.

The SAFEHOUSE residents have therefore discovered another consequence of having a palm-sized winged cat:

Sometimes you have to rescue Corin from a jar.

Again.

**End of Anomalous Physiology / Feline Form Observation**
