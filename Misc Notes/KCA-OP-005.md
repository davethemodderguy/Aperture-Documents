# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **KCA-OP-005**

### **AFTER ACTION REPORT – OPERATION: HOMECOMING**

**Classification:** OMEGA PRIORITY – DECLASSIFIED TO LEVEL 6

> **NOTICE:** This operation concluded the emergency response initiated under KCA-ALERT-001 through KCA-ALERT-004.

---

# **MISSION OBJECTIVES**

**Primary**

- Recover Director Belle Merryweather alive.
    

**Secondary**

- Neutralize the remaining Banlands Watchdogs.
    
- Secure all Etheric Contrabands.
    
- Prevent unauthorized Ether experimentation.
    
- Capture all available intelligence.
    

---

# **OPERATION COMMENCEMENT**

Following weeks of intelligence gathering, Aperture Industries and the Banlands government identified the Watchdogs' final operational stronghold.

Reconnaissance confirmed the worst.

Director Belle Merryweather was being held inside an underground laboratory.

Recovered surveillance indicated the group intended to forcibly inject Belle with concentrated Ether as part of a new series of illegal experiments.

With the preparations nearly complete, the joint task force launched an immediate assault.

---

# **THE ASSAULT**

Banlands Enforcement Division breached the outer perimeter while the Central Execution Department struck fortified entrances.

Aperture Industries operators secured evacuation routes and prevented reinforcements from escaping.

The battle quickly spread throughout the compound.

Gunfire echoed through the halls.

Concrete walls cracked beneath explosions.

The remaining Watchdogs resisted fiercely.

---

# **ENDMIN**

Endmin advanced through the central corridor with remarkable precision.

Her rifle eliminated fortified firing positions one after another.

When ammunition ran low during a hallway engagement, she transitioned to the underbarrel shotgun, breaching the barricade protecting the laboratory sector.

She immediately continued pushing forward.

> **"...Belle."

---

# **LUCIUS**

Lucius moved along the laboratory's maintenance passages.

Using his retractable spear in close quarters and his twin pistols when distance opened up, he disabled several armed guards attempting to reach the operating theater.

His objective never changed.

Reach Belle.

---

# **THE LABORATORY**

The laboratory doors burst open.

Inside...

Belle was restrained to an operating platform.

A large injector filled with unstable Ether had already been positioned.

Researchers scattered as alarms blared.

One remaining Watchdog commander grabbed the injector.

> **"Hold them back!"

The injector began moving toward Belle.

---

# **THE BREAKOUT**

Before the injector could reach her...

Belle drove both feet into the operating frame.

**CRACK!**

The restraints failed.

The heavy steel mount tore free.

She rolled from the table just as the injector struck the platform instead.

Unarmed.

Separated from her sword.

Belle immediately charged.

---

# **HAND-TO-HAND**

The nearest Watchdog swung a metal baton.

Belle slipped underneath it.

A punch to the ribs.

An elbow to the jaw.

Another attacker rushed forward.

Belle caught the strike, pivoted, and threw him into nearby equipment.

Even without her weapon, years of combat training remained evident.

Every movement was efficient.

Every strike deliberate.

She fought with nothing but her fists.

---

# **THE RETURN OF THE SWORD**

Endmin entered the laboratory moments later.

Her eyes immediately found Belle.

Then...

Belle's sword.

It lay several meters away where it had been discarded.

Without hesitation, Endmin grabbed the massive weapon.

> **"BELLE!"

Belle turned.

Endmin spun once before throwing the enormous sword across the room.

The blade rotated through the air.

Belle reached upward.

Her hand closed around the familiar grip.

**THUD.**

The sword's weight settled naturally into her hands.

For the first time in weeks...

She smiled.

A genuine one.

> **"...Thanks."

---

# **THE FINAL PUSH**

The atmosphere changed instantly.

Belle advanced.

Her oversized sword carved through barricades and disarmed armed opponents with overwhelming force.

The remaining Watchdogs attempted to retreat deeper into the facility.

There was nowhere left to run.

Banlands Enforcement officers secured the exits.

Central Execution teams cleared each remaining room.

Within minutes...

Organized resistance collapsed.

---

# **MISSION SUCCESS**

The underground laboratory was secured.

Belle Merryweather was recovered alive.

The Ether injection procedure was successfully prevented before administration.

All identified Etheric Contrabands within the facility were seized for forensic processing and lawful destruction.

The remaining Banlands Watchdogs organization ceased functioning as a coordinated force following the operation.

---

# **PERSONNEL OBSERVATIONS**

## **Endmin Merryweather**

Medical staff had barely finished examining Belle before Endmin wrapped her in a hug.

She didn't say anything at first.

She simply held on.

Eventually, Belle quietly hugged her back.

> **"...I'm home."

Endmin nodded against her shoulder.

> **"...Welcome back."

---

## **Lucius Merryweather**

Lucius quietly retrieved Belle's rabbit keychain from his pocket.

He handed it back.

Belle stared at it.

Then smiled.

A small tear rolled down her cheek.

> **"...You kept it safe."

---

## **Lucy Merryweather**

Lucy looked over Belle carefully for injuries.

Finding no life-threatening wounds, she exhaled for what felt like the first time in weeks.

She gently placed a hand on Belle's head.

> **"...Let's go home."

---

## **Director Lucious**

After reviewing the final report, Director Lucious wrote:

> **"Operation HOMECOMING achieved all primary objectives."**

He paused before adding one final sentence.

> **"Some victories are measured not only by the enemies defeated..."**

> **"...but by the people brought home."**

---

# **MISSION STATUS**

**Director Belle Merryweather:** Recovered Alive

**Ether Experiment:** Prevented

**Remaining Banlands Watchdogs:** Organization dismantled

**Joint Operation:** Successful

---

# **FINAL ENTRY**

As the convoy departed the ruined facility, Belle sat quietly between Endmin and Lucius.

Her rabbit keychain hung once again from her belt.

Her oversized sword rested beside her.

For the first time since her disappearance...

The seat reserved for Belle was no longer empty.

She was finally going home.
