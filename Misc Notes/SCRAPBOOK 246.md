# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – PSYCHOLOGICAL SERVICES**

### **PERSONNEL BACKGROUND ADDENDUM**

## **SUBJECT: DIALYN – CHILDHOOD PET**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Medical & Psychological Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

During a routine counseling session, Dialyn briefly mentioned having a pet during her childhood.

The subject had never appeared in previous interviews.

She spoke about it for less than five minutes before quietly changing the topic.

No further questioning was conducted at her request.

---

# **BACKGROUND**

According to the limited information provided, Dialyn's pet lived with her family in their village within the Banlands.

She did not specify its species.

When asked, she smiled apologetically.

> "...I'd rather keep that part to myself."

Psychological Services respected the request.

The animal remained with the village during the Hollow Incident.

It did not survive.

Dialyn has never returned to the village.

---

# **OBSERVATIONS**

Employees often describe Dialyn as remarkably difficult to upset.

She is patient.

Kind.

Steady under pressure.

Even after exhausting workdays, she is usually seen smiling.

However, personnel have noted one exception.

When conversations unexpectedly drift toward childhood pets, Dialyn becomes noticeably quieter.

She does not leave the conversation.

She simply listens more than she speaks.

---

# **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

**Belle Merryweather**

> "I asked if she'd ever had a pet."

Belle looked down for a moment.

> "...She smiled."

> "...But her eyes didn't."

---

**Corin Lockwood**

Corin later apologized for bringing up the subject.

Dialyn shook her head.

> "...You didn't know."

She smiled gently.

> "...It's okay."

---

**Corey Lockwood**

> "She still smiled while talking about it."

Corey paused.

> "...That somehow made it sadder."

---

**Lumi**

> "Some memories don't stop hurting."

> "...You just learn to carry them more quietly."

---

**Bao**

Without saying anything, Bao later left a small knitted animal on Dialyn's desk.

No note accompanied it.

Dialyn never asked who made it.

She simply kept it on a shelf in her office.

---

**Neko**

After reading the file, Neko closed it immediately.

> "...That's enough."

No additional comments were recorded.

---

**Miss Neko**

> "...Some grief belongs only to its owner."

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL NOTE**

Psychological Services emphasize that Dialyn's generally cheerful disposition should not be mistaken for an absence of grief.

Like any individual, she carries painful memories.

She has simply chosen not to let those memories define how she treats others.

One counselor summarized the observation as follows:

> "She has experienced profound loss."

> "She also continues to greet people with kindness."

> "Those two facts are not contradictory."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Dialyn rarely speaks about her childhood pet.

No photographs are known to exist.

No name has ever been recorded.

She has never requested that the subject be removed from her file.

She has only asked that people not dwell on it.

Those who know her have learned to respect that request.

After all, Dialyn has spent much of her life listening to other people's sorrows.

On the rare occasions when her own quietly surface, her friends simply offer the same patience she has always offered them.

**End of Personnel Background Addendum**
