#### **ADDENDUM B – THE CLOSET**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel

---

## **OVERVIEW**

Although officially referred to as the **SAFEHOUSE Closet**, the storage space bears little resemblance to a conventional closet.

Upon opening the closet door, occupants enter a stabilized pocket dimension designated:

> **SAFEHOUSE Storage Annex**

The interior is so large that navigation maps, directional signs, and an elevator system are required for practical use.

As one engineer famously remarked:

> "...We've reached the point where calling it a 'closet' is technically misinformation."

---

# **DIMENSIONS**

Current surveys estimate the Storage Annex occupies an internal volume comparable to the entire **Krampus Compliance Authority Headquarters**.

The space is divided into **ten floors**, connected by a central freight/passenger elevator and multiple stairwells.

Despite its immense size, the exterior entrance remains an ordinary double-door closet inside Project SAFEHOUSE.

# **NAVIGATION**

Because of the Storage Annex's size, personnel are issued a printed and digital map upon first entering.

Major intersections include:

- Color-coded hallways.
    
- Floor directories.
    
- Directional signs.
    
- Elevator maps.
    
- Emergency exits.
    

It is entirely possible for first-time visitors to become lost.

---

# **ELEVATOR**

A full-size elevator services every floor.

Features include:

- Freight mode.
    
- Passenger mode.
    
- Emergency recall.
    
- Voice announcements.
    
- Keycard-restricted maintenance access.
    

Belle has been known to ride the elevator simply because she enjoys hearing it announce the floors.

---

# **SAFETY NOTICE**

Personnel are advised to inform another resident before exploring unfamiliar sections of the Storage Annex.

Although the layout is stable, its scale can make orientation difficult without a map.

Fortunately, the Annex has never exhibited hostile anomalous behavior.

It is simply...

Very, very large.
