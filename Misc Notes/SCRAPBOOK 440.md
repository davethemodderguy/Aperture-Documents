# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CELESTIAL ARCHITECTURE / COMMUNICATION EVENT**

# **GUIDING LIGHT — SECOND RECIPIENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – CELESTIAL AUTHORITY  
**Designation:** **GUIDING LIGHT**  
**Primary Recipient:** Orphie  
**Secondary Recipient:** Corin  
**Status:** **ACTIVE — COMMUNICATION EXPANDED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Guiding Light's blue-glowing notes were previously visible only to **Orphie**.

This is no longer the case.

Corin can now read them.

The change occurred without warning and without any known action from either sister.

No additional manifestation occurred.

No temporal distortion was detected.

The notes simply became visible to Corin.

---

# **THE DISCOVERY**

Orphie found another note sitting on the table.

As usual, the paper emitted a faint blue glow.

She picked it up.

Corin was nearby.

> **Corin:**  
> "...What's that?"

Orphie hesitated.

> **Orphie:**  
> "...A note."

Corin looked at it.

Her expression changed.

> **Corin:**  
> "...I can read it."

Orphie froze.

> "...What?"

Corin pointed at the paper.

> **Corin:**  
> "It says—"

She read the message aloud.

Orphie stared at her.

For the first time, someone else could see what had always been invisible to everyone but her.

---

# **THE NOTE**

The message read:

> **"Hello, Corin."**
> 
> **"Yes, you can see this now."**
> 
> **"Don't worry."**
> 
> **"I haven't changed anything about you."**
> 
> **"You simply weren't meant to remain unaware forever."**
> 
> **"Take care of each other."**
> 
> **— Guiding Light**
> 
> **☼ 🌙**

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

Corin read the note twice.

> "...Okay."

She looked at Orphie.

> "...You've been getting these this whole time?"

Orphie nodded.

> "...Yeah."

> "...How long?"

> "...Since before I came back."

Corin paused.

> "...Came back from where?"

Orphie looked away.

She wasn't ready to explain everything.

Corin noticed.

She didn't push.

---

# **THE CHANGE**

From this point onward, Corin was able to perceive Guiding Light's notes.

The phenomenon appears selective.

No other SAFEHOUSE resident has demonstrated the ability to read them.

Star attempted to read one.

To her, the paper appeared completely blank.

Nightshade reported the same result.

The notes remain exclusively accessible to Orphie and Corin.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S RESPONSE**

A second note appeared several hours later.

This time, both sisters were present.

The message read:

> **"I see you've found each other."**
> 
> **"Good."**
> 
> **"Orphie has been carrying this alone for long enough."**
> 
> **"You don't need to know everything she remembers."**
> 
> **"But you should know that she chose you."**
> 
> **"And she chose correctly."**
> 
> **— Guiding Light**
> 
> **☼ 🌙**

Corin became unusually quiet.

She looked at Orphie.

Orphie looked embarrassed.

> "...Don't."

Corin smiled.

> "...I wasn't going to say anything."

---

# **CORIN'S QUESTION**

Eventually, Corin asked the note directly:

> **"Are you watching us?"**

The writing changed.

A new sentence appeared beneath the existing message.

> **"Always."**

Corin stared.

> "...That's slightly creepy."

Another line appeared.

> **"Fair."**

Orphie laughed.

Corin laughed too.

It was the first time either of them had joked about Guiding Light together.

---

# **THEIR NEW UNDERSTANDING**

Corin now understands that Orphie's unusual behavior was never entirely unexplained.

She knows there is someone—or something—watching over them.

She knows Orphie was given a second chance.

She knows Guiding Light refuses to reveal herself.

But she does **not** know everything Orphie experienced in the erased future.

And Guiding Light has made no indication that she intends to reveal it.

Some memories are still Orphie's alone.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S CONDITION**

One final note appeared that evening.

It was addressed to both sisters.

> **"You may read my messages now."**
> 
> **"That does not mean I will answer every question."**
> 
> **"Some things are yours to discover."**
> 
> **"Some things are better left unknown."**
> 
> **"For now, live."**
> 
> **"Together."**
> 
> **— Guiding Light**
> 
> **☼ 🌙**

The note stopped glowing.

Neither sister spoke for several seconds.

Then Corin folded it carefully.

> "...We're keeping this one."

Orphie smiled.

> "...Obviously."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Guiding Light remains hidden.

She has not revealed her form.

She has not spoken directly.

She continues communicating through blue-glowing notes.

The difference is that Orphie no longer has to read them alone.

For the first time, someone else can see the messages.

Someone she trusts.

Someone Guiding Light apparently trusts as well.

The two sisters now share a small window into the presence of one of reality's Architects.

And somewhere beyond their understanding...

the Guiding Light continues watching.

**End of Celestial Architecture / Communication Event**
