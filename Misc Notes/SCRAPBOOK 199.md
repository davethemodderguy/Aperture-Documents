# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **MEDICAL INCIDENT & EVENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – BIRTHDAY INCIDENT AT SHELTER T-01**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the conclusion of the severe winter storm surrounding Shelter T-01, a coordinated visit was arranged by residents of Project SAFEHOUSE and associated personnel.

The purpose of the visit was simple:

A birthday surprise for Corin Lockwood.

The planned event included members of her support network:

- Neko
    
- Miss Neko
    
- Taiga
    
- Mart
    
- Endmin Merryweather
    
- Astral
    
- Bao
    
- Frost
    
- Lumi
    
- Star
    
- Nightshade
    
- Belle Merryweather
    
- Keith
    
- Cinder
    

The intent was to remind Corin that, despite the distance and isolation, she remained part of a family.

The event did not proceed as planned.

---

# **ARRIVAL AT SHELTER T-01**

After the storm cleared, the group made the difficult journey to the treehouse.

The route was heavily covered in snow.

The surrounding forest was unusually quiet.

The white tiger was found beneath the tree.

The snowy owl was perched above.

Both immediately reacted to the arrival of familiar personnel.

However...

Neither approached the group.

Both remained focused on the treehouse.

This raised immediate concern.

---

# **DISCOVERY**

The group expected Corin to open the door.

She did not.

After receiving no response, Lumi entered the shelter.

Inside, they found no sign of preparation for the birthday.

No decorations.

No tea.

No paperwork.

No usual signs of Corin's routine.

Then they found her.

Corin was unconscious.

---

# **EMERGENCY RESPONSE**

Lumi immediately began medical intervention.

Due to previous concerns regarding Corin's condition, Lumi carried emergency medical supplies during all visits.

Among them was emergency adrenaline.

Medical response was initiated immediately.

After stabilization, Corin regained consciousness.

The first thing she saw was not the storm.

Not the empty room.

Not the memories.

It was everyone.

---

# **THE BIRTHDAY EVENT**

The original celebration was quickly changed.

The decorations remained.

The food remained.

The people remained.

But the celebration itself became something else.

A quiet gathering.

A recovery room.

A reminder that everyone had come because they cared.

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

Throughout the gathering, Corin remained emotionally distant.

She did not appear angry.

She did not reject everyone.

She simply looked exhausted.

The usual Corin — the one who laughed at Neko's inventions, accepted Belle's cookies, and talked about the tiger — was barely present.

When someone mentioned that the gathering was supposed to be a birthday celebration, Corin became visibly uncomfortable.

Eventually, she spoke.

> "I hate birthdays."

The room went quiet.

---

# **EXPLANATION**

Corin did not hate the people who came.

She did not hate being remembered.

She hated what birthdays represented.

A reminder of time passing.

A reminder of the life she lost.

A reminder of the person she used to be.

A reminder that people celebrated someone who, in her own mind, was not supposed to still exist.

---

# **PERSONNEL RESPONSES**

**Belle**

Stopped trying to make the celebration cheerful.

Instead, she simply sat beside Corin.

---

**Star**

Turned off the brighter decorations.

She understood that sometimes light could be too much.

---

**Nightshade**

Stayed nearby without speaking.

---

**Mart**

Read quietly beside her.

No questions.

No pressure.

---

**Taiga**

Sat with the white tiger outside.

Both remained close to the treehouse.

---

**Neko**

Did not mention upgrades.

For once.

---

**Miss Neko**

Made sure he continued not mentioning upgrades.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi remained with Corin after everyone else left.

She did not try to convince her to like birthdays.

She did not tell her to be grateful.

She only said:

> "You don't have to celebrate today."

A pause.

> "But you are still allowed to be here tomorrow."

Corin did not answer.

But she did not ask Lumi to leave.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The birthday gathering at Shelter T-01 was officially canceled.

No celebration was recorded.

No photographs were taken.

No party occurred.

However, all attendees agreed on one point:

The purpose of the visit was never the birthday.

It was Corin.

And even on the day she hated most...

She was not alone.

**End of Medical Incident & Event Report**
