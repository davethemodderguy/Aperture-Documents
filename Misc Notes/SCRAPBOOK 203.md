# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – RECURRENCE OF TRAUMA-RELATED NIGHTMARES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Approximately several weeks after Corin Lockwood's documented recovery, Medical and Psychological Services observed an unexpected decline in her emotional condition.

The decline did not begin while she was awake.

It began while she slept.

Corin started experiencing recurring nightmares.

She consistently reported waking with intense fear and sadness, despite being unable to remember the dreams themselves.

---

# **SLEEP OBSERVATIONS**

According to Corin's own account:

> "I know something happened."

> "I wake up crying."

> "...Then it's gone."

Repeated interviews found the same pattern.

She could not describe the dreams.

She could only describe the emotions left behind.

Medical Services noted that this presentation was consistent throughout the observation period.

---

# **PROGRESSION**

Over the following weeks, personnel documented gradual changes.

Observed behaviors included:

- Reduced sleep duration.
    
- Frequent nighttime awakenings.
    
- Fatigue during working hours.
    
- Decreased participation in social activities.
    
- Increased periods of isolation inside Shelter T-01.
    
- Noticeably subdued mood.
    

Corin remained cooperative with medical staff and continued taking her prescribed medication.

However, her overall psychological state steadily worsened.

---

# **THE SUPPRESSED MEMORIES**

Neurological examinations found no evidence that Corin had consciously regained access to her suppressed childhood memories.

Instead, Psychological Services proposed that the emotional content of those memories may still be influencing her during sleep, even if the memories themselves remain inaccessible.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

Executive Directive C-17 remains in effect.

No attempts are to be made to deliberately recover or reconstruct the suppressed memories.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi recognized the change almost immediately.

One evening she found Corin sitting outside the treehouse before dawn.

Snow had collected on her shoulders.

She had not noticed.

Lumi quietly placed a blanket around her.

Corin looked up.

> "...I had another one."

Lumi gently asked,

> "Do you remember it?"

Corin slowly shook her head.

> "...No."

A long silence followed.

Then Corin whispered,

> "...I only remember how it made me feel."

Lumi sat beside her until sunrise.

Neither of them spoke again.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services emphasize that this development does **not** necessarily indicate that Corin has lost all previous progress.

She continues to maintain relationships.

She continues her duties.

She continues to seek medical care when needed.

However, the recurrence of severe nightmares has significantly affected her quality of life and emotional well-being.

Continued monitoring and supportive care have been recommended.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin Lockwood remains at Shelter T-01.

The white tiger continues sleeping beneath the tree.

The snowy owl continues watching from the branches.

The nightmares continue.

No one knows what Corin sees while she sleeps.

Only that, each morning, she wakes carrying the weight of something she cannot remember.

**End of Psychological Incident Report**
