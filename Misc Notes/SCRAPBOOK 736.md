### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / ANOMALOUS OBSERVATION LOG**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — SEMI-TRANSPARENCY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** **ANOMALOUS PHYSICAL CHANGE — UNDER OBSERVATION**

---

# **OBSERVATION**

The change was first noticed by Corin.

Alice was sitting inside the Treehouse, drawing.

Corin looked toward her.

Something appeared wrong.

She could see the wooden wall behind Alice.

Not clearly.

Just enough to notice.

Alice's body had become **partially transparent**.

Her white hair remained visible.

Her clothing remained visible.

Her bunny ears remained visible.

But portions of her body appeared almost translucent, as though Alice was slowly becoming part of the light around her.

Corin immediately stopped.

> "Alice?"

Alice looked up.

She didn't seem particularly concerned.

She simply tilted her head.

---

# **DEGREE OF TRANSPARENCY**

The effect is inconsistent.

Some portions of Alice's body remain completely opaque.

Other areas allow the environment behind her to be faintly visible.

The effect is most noticeable around:

- Arms
    
- Lower legs
    
- Ears
    
- Hair
    
- Edges of her silhouette
    

Her outline remains visible at all times.

Alice is **not invisible**.

She is simply becoming increasingly difficult to see.

---

# **ALICE'S RESPONSE**

Alice looked at her hands.

She could see the Treehouse floor faintly through them.

She moved her fingers.

Then wrote in her notebook:

> **"Alice see through Alice."**

Corin didn't know what to say.

Alice added:

> **"Alice broken?"**

Corin immediately answered:

> "No."

Alice looked at her.

Corin gently placed a hand on Alice's head.

> "You're still Alice."

Alice nodded.

---

# **MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

The transparency does not currently appear to be causing direct physical pain.

However, medical staff have been unable to determine its origin.

No corresponding loss of body mass has been detected.

Alice remains physically tangible.

She can:

- Touch objects
    
- Hold her pencil
    
- Ride her scooter
    
- Eat
    
- Be carried
    
- Interact normally with her surroundings
    

The phenomenon appears to be primarily **visual and anomalous**.

---

# **THEORY**

Several possibilities have been proposed.

Possible causes include:

**1. Ether instability**

Alice's previously extreme Ether concentration may be interacting with her body in an unknown way.

**2. Life-force depletion**

Her life-transfer ability may have permanently altered how her body maintains itself.

**3. Anomalous regeneration**

Alice's regeneration may be compensating for damage through increasingly abnormal means.

**4. Unknown manifestation**

The change may represent an entirely new ability.

No conclusion has been reached.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

Nobody is treating Alice differently.

This was made an explicit instruction.

She is still allowed to sit with everyone.

She is still allowed to ride her scooter.

She is still allowed to draw.

She is still allowed to visit her friends.

And she is still allowed to be picked up.

Belle reportedly stared at Alice for several seconds before saying:

> "You're kinda shiny now."

Alice smiled.

---

# **STAR**

Star was fascinated.

She carefully examined Alice's translucent hand.

> "That's definitely unusual."

Alice wrote:

> **"Pretty?"**

Star smiled.

> "Yeah."

Alice seemed relieved.

---

# **NEKO**

Neko immediately began investigating.

He found the phenomenon interesting.

Then he noticed Alice watching him.

He stopped analyzing her.

> "I'll figure it out without poking you."

Alice nodded.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi was less interested in the phenomenon itself.

She was interested in Alice.

She gently brushed Alice's hair.

> "Still soft."

Alice smiled.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin remains concerned.

Not because Alice is transparent.

Because Alice is still Alice.

Still fragile.

Still prone to hiding discomfort.

Still willing to give away pieces of herself to help others.

And now—

part of her body is literally becoming difficult to see.

Corin has begun spending more time with her again.

Not because Alice needs to be watched every second.

But because she doesn't want Alice sitting alone wondering whether she is disappearing.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Alice remains stable.

She is semi-transparent.

She is still tangible.

She is still conscious.

She is still drawing.

She is still loved.

The phenomenon is being monitored.

No attempts will be made to reverse it until its cause is understood.

For now, the most important fact remains unchanged:

> **Alice is still here.**

And everyone intends to keep it that way.
