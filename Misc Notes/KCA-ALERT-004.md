# **INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY**

Following the confirmed kidnapping of Director **Belle Merryweather**, joint intelligence units from the Krampus Compliance Authority, the Central Execution Department, Banlands Enforcement Division, and Aperture Industries have identified a coordinated resurgence of the **Banlands Watchdogs**.

Surviving members who escaped **Operation: SILENT VERDICT** have regrouped into a reorganized command structure.

Evidence indicates the organization has begun recruiting new members, re-establishing supply routes, and fortifying multiple safehouses across the Banlands.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

Recovered intelligence indicates the reorganized Watchdogs have:

- Re-established a central leadership cell.
    
- Recruited additional armed personnel.
    
- Acquired vehicles, communications equipment, and weapons.
    
- Occupied several fortified locations.
    
- Begun coordinated movement between safehouses to avoid detection.
    

Investigators believe the kidnapping of Belle was intended to remove the Banlands' most effective anti-corruption leader while rebuilding their organization.

---

# **BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

Director Belle Merryweather remains unaccounted for.

Her condition and location are unknown.

No verified communication has been received.

The Krampus Compliance Authority has classified her recovery as its highest operational priority.

---

> **"Faster than anticipated."

Lucius folded his arms.
