# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

SPAWN has ceased conducting reconnaissance trips to **Heaven's Blacksite**.

The decision follows a prolonged series of failed reconnaissance attempts, compromised operations, personnel losses, accidental encounters with residents, technological security failures, and the recent incident in which Neko impersonated a divine entity through SPAWN's own infrastructure.

C.O.R.E. considers the cessation of reconnaissance activity a positive development.

The Archivist Division, however, has expressed mild disappointment.

The Blacksite reconnaissance logs had become a regular source of entertainment during late-night shifts.
