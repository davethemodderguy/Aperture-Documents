# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: STAR (Z-314-S) – PRIVATE EMOTIONAL INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Star has long been regarded as one of the most consistently optimistic residents of Project SAFEHOUSE.

She is frequently described by personnel as the emotional center of the residence.

Her laughter is common.

Her encouragement is genuine.

Her ability to restore morale is considered exceptional.

For this reason, an incident occurring on ██/██/20██ attracted immediate attention.

---

# **INCIDENT**

At approximately 22:40, Mart entered the SAFEHOUSE lounge intending to retrieve a book left on one of the shelves.

Instead, he discovered Star sitting alone on the sofa.

She was crying.

No one else was present.

Her wings were folded tightly around herself.

She had buried her face in her knees.

Mart quietly stepped back toward the doorway to avoid startling her.

Before he could leave unnoticed...

Star looked up.

The moment she realized someone had seen her...

She immediately wiped away her tears.

Her first response was not anger.

It was embarrassment.

> "...P-Please don't tell everyone..."

Without waiting for an answer, Star hurried from the lounge.

She avoided eye contact with everyone for the remainder of the evening.

---

# **FOLLOW-UP**

Medical Services attempted a routine welfare check the following morning.

Star insisted she was fine.

She thanked the staff for their concern.

She apologized repeatedly for "making everyone worry."

No further explanation was offered.

Psychological Services elected not to press the matter.

---

# **OBSERVATIONS**

Personnel have since noted a recurring pattern.

Star almost always presents herself as cheerful around others.

She comforts people before they ask.

She notices when someone is lonely.

She celebrates even small successes.

She laughs easily.

However...

There have been several documented occasions in which Star was found alone shortly after supporting someone else emotionally.

Each time, she appeared exhausted.

On two occasions, she had clearly been crying.

There is no evidence that her optimism is false.

Rather, Psychological Services believe Star habitually places the emotional needs of others ahead of her own.

---

# **NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade declined to participate in formal interviews.

Her only official statement consisted of a single sentence.

> "Anyone who uses this against her will answer to me."

When Medical Services clarified that no one intended to exploit the incident, Nightshade simply nodded and ended the interview.

Personnel familiar with the pair noted that Nightshade's protectiveness became even more pronounced following the event.

She was observed remaining physically closer to Star than usual during the following weeks.

No explanation was given.

None was requested.

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services have found no indication that Star's emotional presentation is deceptive.

Her happiness appears genuine.

However, like any individual, she is capable of grief, fear, and exhaustion.

The current concern is not that Star experiences these emotions.

It is that she consistently chooses to experience them in private.

Medical Services recommend that personnel remember an important distinction.

Being the one who comforts everyone else does not mean someone never needs comfort themselves.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Star continues residing at Project SAFEHOUSE.

She continues encouraging others.

She continues laughing loudly enough to fill the common room.

Most residents would never know anything had happened.

Only a few people remember finding her alone that night.

And among those few...

No one mentions it.

Not because it should be forgotten.

But because they know that if Star ever decides to talk about it...

She deserves to do so on her own terms.

**End of Psychological Observation Report**
