### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / CRITICAL PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT LOG**

# **CORIN — COMPLETE PSYCHOLOGICAL BREAKDOWN**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Critical Personnel Welfare  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Associated Personnel:** Orphie / Lumi / Arcturus / Norma / SAFEHOUSE Residents / KCA  
**Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **CRITICAL — SEVERE PSYCHOLOGICAL DETERIORATION**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's psychological condition has deteriorated beyond anything previously documented.

She is no longer merely unstable, withdrawn, or overwhelmed.

Her behavior has become profoundly disorganized.

Her perception of herself, her surroundings, and her relationship with Orphie has become increasingly detached from reality.

The distinction between memory, imagination, and present events is no longer consistently maintained.

---

# **BEHAVIORAL STATE**

Corin now frequently:

- Speaks to people who are not present.
    
- Holds conversations with empty spaces.
    
- Repeats the same sentences for extended periods.
    
- Forgets conversations shortly after having them.
    
- Becomes distressed by events that did not occur.
    
- Treats objects as though they possess intentions.
    
- Reacts to imagined responses.
    
- Loses track of time.
    
- Neglects food, sleep, and hygiene.
    
- Becomes emotionally overwhelmed without an obvious trigger.
    
- Rapidly shifts between calmness, fear, anger, and laughter.
    

Her behavior is no longer reliably predictable.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has become the center of Corin's fractured thoughts.

Corin knows Orphie is physically present.

At the same time, she sometimes behaves as though Orphie is still missing.

At other times, she speaks to an imagined version of Orphie while the real Orphie is standing elsewhere in the room.

The distinction appears meaningless to her.

---

# **THE EMPTY CHAIR**

The empty chair remains Corin's most persistent fixation.

She frequently sits across from it.

Sometimes she talks to Orphie.

Sometimes she talks to herself.

Sometimes she becomes angry at the chair.

> "...You said you'd come back."

Silence.

> "...You did come back."

Pause.

> "...Then why did you leave again?"

Orphie may be sleeping on the opposite side of the room.

Corin does not appear to notice.

---

# **ORPHIE'S REACTION**

Orphie has become increasingly hostile toward Corin's behavior.

> "...Stop talking to the chair."

Corin looks at her.

> "...You weren't there."

> "...I'm right here."

Corin smiles.

> "...No."

Orphie:

> "...What?"

Corin looks back at the chair.

> "...You're over there."

Orphie eventually leaves the room.

---

# **THE WORST CONTRADICTION**

Corin can sometimes recognize that something is wrong.

She may suddenly stop and say:

> "...I'm losing my mind."

Then, moments later:

> "...No."

> "...I'm fine."

Then:

> "...I'm not fine."

Then she begins laughing.

The cycle can repeat several times.

---

# **FAMILY RESPONSE**

### **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus has stopped trying to reason with her.

He now focuses primarily on keeping her physically safe.

> "...She's not listening anymore."

---

### **NORMA**

Norma has become increasingly frightened.

Her usual analytical approach is no longer sufficient.

> "...I don't know what she's seeing."

> "...I don't know what she's hearing."

> "...I don't know how to reach her."

---

### **LUMI**

Lumi is devastated.

She recognizes that her daughter is no longer simply grieving.

> "...That's not my Corin."

She pauses.

> "...She's still in there."

---

# **STAR**

Star could barely look at Corin without crying.

She had always believed that happiness could reach people eventually.

Now she was confronted with someone who could not even reliably recognize the difference between reality and memory.

> "...I don't know how to make her smile anymore."

Nightshade remained beside her.

> "...Then don't make her smile."

> "...Just stay."

Star nodded.

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts remain completely absent from the treehouse.

They are too frightened to approach.

Their previous attempts at companionship ended badly.

They now consider Corin's presence unsafe.

---

# **KCA**

Corin remains suspended from her position as Lead Accountant.

No return-to-work date has been established.

At her current level of functioning, she would be unable to reliably manage financial records or make professional decisions.

---

# **ORPHIE'S POSITION**

Orphie continues to insist that Corin needs to control herself.

> "...She's choosing to act like this."

Multiple family members have attempted to explain that Corin is no longer capable of simply choosing to stop.

Orphie does not accept this.

Her frustration continues to worsen the relationship.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

The building is no longer functioning as a normal residence.

It has become a containment concern.

Not because Corin is intentionally dangerous.

Because nobody can reliably predict what she will do next.

The environment remains quiet.

The curtains remain closed.

The empty chair remains occupied only by Corin's perception.

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

Corin's condition is now considered **severe and acute**.

She cannot reliably:

- Regulate her emotions.
    
- Maintain ordinary routines.
    
- Distinguish memories from current events.
    
- Maintain consistent interpersonal communication.
    
- Perform professional duties.
    
- Accurately assess her own condition.
    

She requires continuous observation and professional intervention.

Leaving her completely alone is no longer considered acceptable.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin spent two weeks waiting for Orphie.

Orphie returned.

Corin continued deteriorating.

Orphie rejected her.

Corin deteriorated further.

Now Corin's mind has become a maze of memories, imagined conversations, guilt, fear, and fragments of reality.

Sometimes she knows Orphie is there.

Sometimes she thinks Orphie is still gone.

Sometimes she talks to an empty chair.

Sometimes she looks directly at Orphie and does not recognize the person standing in front of her.

And sometimes, for only a few seconds, the old Corin appears.

She looks around.

She realizes something is terribly wrong.

And whispers:

> **"...Please help me."**

Then the moment passes.

**End of Critical Psychological Incident Log**
