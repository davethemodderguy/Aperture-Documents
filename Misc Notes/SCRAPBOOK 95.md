# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **CONFIDENTIAL MEDICAL OBSERVATION ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – PERSISTENT VISUAL ETHER MANIFESTATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical Director & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **OVERVIEW**

Corin Lockwood has voluntarily reported a new development regarding the stabilized Ether resonance previously documented in her medical file.

The phenomenon is no longer experienced solely as an internal voice.

Corin now consistently perceives a visual manifestation resembling her late mother.

The figure remains visible **only to Corin**.

No cameras, recording equipment, or other personnel have observed any corresponding physical presence.

The true nature of the manifestation remains undetermined.

Medical staff have not concluded whether it represents an Ether-generated projection perceived only by Corin, a psychological phenomenon associated with her trauma, or another currently unexplained anomaly.

---

## **DESCRIPTION**

According to Corin, the figure appears exactly as she remembers her mother.

She is always calm.

Always neatly dressed.

Always standing nearby.

Sometimes she sits beside Corin while she works.

Sometimes she quietly watches from the corner of the room.

Sometimes she walks beside Corin through empty hallways.

The figure has never demonstrated hostile behavior.

She never obstructs Corin's work.

She never interferes with other people.

She simply remains close.

---

## **BEHAVIOR**

The manifestation behaves as though aware that only Corin can perceive her.

When other people enter the room, she becomes noticeably quieter.

Corin explained:

> "...She doesn't interrupt conversations."

> "...She just smiles."

If Corin appears as though she is about to answer aloud, the figure will often shake her head gently, reminding her that no one else can see or hear her.

Corin laughed softly while describing one such moment.

> "...She told me I'd confuse everyone."

---

## **SOCIAL ADAPTATION**

Corin has consciously learned not to respond verbally to the figure while others are present.

Instead, communication is limited to subtle gestures or silent acknowledgment.

This behavior is not motivated by fear of the figure.

It is motivated by concern that speaking openly to someone no one else can perceive would alarm her colleagues.

As Corin explained:

> "...I'd rather they think I'm quiet than think I've lost my mind."

Psychological evaluation indicates that Corin remains fully aware that the manifestation is a private experience.

She demonstrates intact orientation, judgment, and awareness that others do not share her perception.

---

## **SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION**

Although Corin has only recently resumed occasional visits to SAFEHOUSE, Belle noticed an unusual habit.

Every so often, Corin would instinctively move slightly to one side before sitting down.

As though making room for someone.

Belle eventually asked with a gentle smile.

> "...Habit?"

Corin hesitated.

Then quietly nodded.

> "...Something like that."

Belle accepted the answer without pressing further.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the report before adding the following note.

> "Whether this manifestation originates from Ether, memory, or another phenomenon remains unknown."

> "What matters clinically is that Corin retains insight into the experience and continues to function responsibly."

He paused before writing one final sentence.

> "Continue observation."

> "Continue treatment."

> "And continue judging Corin by how she treats the people around her—not by experiences only she can perceive."

---

## **STATUS**

The visual Ether manifestation remains stable and consistently non-threatening.

Corin reports that its presence provides reassurance during periods of anxiety, though clinicians continue to encourage her to rely on both professional care and the support of trusted family and colleagues.

The phenomenon remains under observation as researchers work to better understand its origin and relationship to Corin's recovery.

**End of Medical Observation Addendum**
