# **MERRYWEATHER FAMILY**

## **PUBLIC Q&A DOSSIER**

### **Frequently Asked Questions**

**Classification:** Public Release

---

### **Q: Who are the Merryweathers?**

**A:** The Merryweather family is one of the oldest and most respected families in the Banlands. Rather than ruling through fear or wealth alone, they have maintained public trust over centuries through consistent leadership, public service, and accountability. This longstanding trust is the primary reason members of the family are repeatedly elected to high office.

---

### **Q: Who currently belongs to the Merryweather family?**

**A:**

- **Lucy Merryweather** – Senior government leader.
    
- **Belle Merryweather** – Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority.
    
- **Endmin (Lindervene) Merryweather** – Central Execution Department.
    
- **Lucius Merryweather** – Family member and close associate of Aperture Industries.
    

**Note:** **Alice is not a member of the Merryweather family.** She works alongside and supports the family in a professional capacity but is not part of it.

---

### **Q: Are all Merryweathers politicians?**

**A:** No.

Although several hold important government positions, each serves in different capacities.

- Lucy oversees government leadership.
    
- Belle enforces compliance and oversight through the KCA.
    
- Endmin serves within the Central Execution Department.
    
- Lucius frequently operates alongside Aperture Industries.
    

Each has different responsibilities despite sharing the same family name.

---

### **Q: Are they difficult to approach?**

**A:** It depends on the individual.

- **Belle** is friendly, approachable, and enjoys talking with employees.
    
- **Endmin** is generally quiet but approachable.
    
- **Lucius** is polite and composed.
    
- **Lucy** is professional and direct, particularly during official business.
    

---

### **Q: Do they receive special treatment because they're family?**

**A:** Officially, no.

Government procedures, internal reviews, and oversight apply regardless of family affiliation.

Their positions carry greater responsibility, not exemption from accountability.

---

### **Q: Why do people trust the Merryweather family?**

**A:** Trust was built over generations.

The family has developed a reputation for public service, transparency, and placing the interests of the Banlands above personal gain.

This reputation is one of the primary reasons they continue to receive strong public support.

---

### **Q: Is Belle really carrying a 50-kilogram sword?**

**A:** Yes.

No, no one knows how she swings it so effortlessly.

No, borrowing it is not recommended.

---

### **Q: Is Endmin's baseball bat really capable of damaging Belle's sword?**

**A:** Surprisingly, yes.

The Nekonium bat managed to chip the sword during a sparring session.

Neko later reinforced the blade.

The baseball bat remains the subject of frequent jokes within Engineering.

---

### **Q: Does Lucius always carry a spear?**

**A:** Almost always.

His primary weapon is a retractable spear constructed from a Nekonium–Titanium–Platinum alloy, extending from approximately 45 cm to 95 cm at the press of a button.

---

### **Q: Does the family spend time together outside work?**

**A:** Yes.

Despite demanding schedules, they make time for shared meals, visits to Heaven's Blacksite, and occasional trips such as the Starlands picnic.

---

### **Q: Is it true Belle always has a carrot?**

**A:** More often than not.

If Belle appears without one, someone usually asks if she's feeling okay.

---

### **Q: Can I call Belle "Zhao"?**

**A:** No.

Personnel are expected to address Director Belle Merryweather by her current name or official title.

Respect for colleagues includes respecting the names they use.

Deliberately using former names to distress or provoke someone is considered unacceptable workplace conduct.

---

### **Q: Which family member is the scariest?**

**A:** Opinions vary.

- Belle becomes intimidating when protecting others.
    
- Endmin is formidable in combat.
    
- Lucius is calm and highly disciplined.
    
- Lucy rarely needs to raise her voice to command a room.
    

Most employees conclude that choosing only one is unwise.

---

### **Q: Any advice for new employees?**

**A:** Treat the Merryweathers as you would any respected colleague:

- Be professional.
    
- Be honest.
    
- Don't judge Belle by her size.
    
- Don't underestimate Endmin.
    
- Don't challenge Lucius unless you're prepared.
    
- Don't mistake Lucy's calm demeanor for leniency.
    

And if Belle offers you a carrot...

It's generally polite to accept.

---

**End of Q&A**
