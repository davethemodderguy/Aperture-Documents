# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – COGNITIVE ANOMALY REPORT**

### **DESIGNATION: M.A.R.T. BLOBS – AMBASSADOR SUBTYPE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Among the approximately **1,500** members of the Mart colony, researchers have identified an extremely rare subtype.

Current confirmed population:

- **1 Ambassador stationed at Heaven's Blacksite.**
    
- **1 Ambassador stationed at the Krampus Compliance Authority.**
    

Unlike ordinary Marts, these two individuals possess significantly enhanced intelligence and are fully capable of speaking fluent English.

Despite belonging to the same Ether-linked hivemind as every other Mart, they are considered unique individuals within the colony.

---

# **ROLE**

The colony refers to these two simply as:

> **"Ambassador."**

Neither has an individual name.

When asked what their names are, both answer:

> "Mart."

Researchers eventually stopped asking.

Their primary duties appear to be:

- Speaking on behalf of the colony.
    
- Explaining Mart behavior to humans.
    
- Resolving misunderstandings.
    
- Organizing large groups of Marts.
    
- Welcoming visitors to the Mart Nest.
    

Neither Ambassador appears to have appointed themselves.

The rest of the colony simply listens to them.

---

# **INTELLIGENCE**

Both Ambassadors demonstrate intelligence comparable to an average adult human.

Observed abilities include:

- Fluent English.
    
- Reading technical documents.
    
- Writing.
    
- Planning.
    
- Negotiation.
    
- Humor.
    
- Abstract reasoning.
    
- Leadership.
    

One Ambassador has even been observed correcting grammatical mistakes in an Engineering memo.

Engineering accepted the correction.

---

# **THE HIVEMIND PARADOX**

Perhaps the strangest discovery is that both Ambassadors can freely share their knowledge through the colony's Ether-linked hivemind.

In theory...

Every Mart should now be capable of speaking fluent English.

They simply...

Choose not to.

When asked why, the Blacksite Ambassador answered:

> "Because saying 'Hello!' is faster."

The KCA Ambassador nodded.

> "Most conversations don't require a vocabulary larger than 'Friend' and 'Cheese Cracker.'"

Research was unable to dispute this.

---

# **INTERVIEW EXCERPT**

**Researcher**

> "If every Mart can understand English, why don't they all speak it?"

**Blacksite Ambassador**

> "They can."

**Researcher**

> "...Then why don't they?"

The Ambassador looked toward several Marts happily carrying books across the library.

One proudly announced:

> "Book!"

Another replied:

> "Friend!"

The Ambassador smiled.

> "They're perfectly happy talking like that."

---

**Researcher**

> "...So they intentionally simplify their speech?"

> "Yes."

> "They think that's normal conversation."

---

# **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

**Lumi**

The first time one of the Ambassadors calmly introduced itself with complete sentences, Lumi stared for several seconds.

> "...You could always do that?"

The Ambassador politely answered:

> "Yes."

> "There simply wasn't much reason to."

---

**Dialyn**

The KCA Ambassador occasionally assists Customer Services by directing confused visitors.

Its customer satisfaction rating is, somewhat unbelievably, higher than the facility average.

Dialyn insists she isn't jealous.

No one believes her.

---

**Belle Merryweather**

> "...One Mart is correcting paperwork."

> "...The other two hundred are wearing flower pots as hats."

The Ambassador quietly corrected her.

> "...One thousand five hundred."

---

**Endmin Merryweather**

> "...Expected."

No one knows why he expected this.

---

**Corin Lockwood**

Corin once overheard an Ambassador discussing philosophy with Neko.

Five minutes later it turned around and happily greeted another Mart with:

> "Hello!"

The conversation immediately devolved into:

> "Friend!"

> "Mart!"

> "Happy!"

---

**Corey Lockwood**

Corey asked whether the other Marts ever found the Ambassadors strange.

The Ambassador smiled.

> "No."

> "We're all Mart."

---

**Star**

Star immediately attempted to teach the Ambassadors sarcasm.

Research now regrets allowing this.

---

**Neko**

Research Summary:

> "...The colony possesses a latent linguistic capability far beyond its observable speech."

Additional Note:

> "...Their limited vocabulary is cultural, not intellectual."

---

**Miss Neko**

She asked the Blacksite Ambassador one final question.

> "...Why only two of you?"

The Ambassador thought for a moment.

Then shrugged.

> "Two is enough."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The two Mart Ambassadors continue to serve as the primary representatives of the colony.

They are proof that the Mart hivemind is capable of remarkable intelligence.

Ironically, this discovery has changed very little.

The Ambassadors still spend most of their day doing exactly what every other Mart does:

Helping people.

Eating sugary cheese crackers.

And greeting friends with a cheerful—

> "Hello!"

Because, as the Ambassador later explained in an interview:

> "Just because you know many words..."

> "...doesn't mean you need to use them."

**End of Cognitive Anomaly Report**
