# APERTURE BLACKSITE

## SUBSPACE ACCESS INVESTIGATION

### SUBJECT: NERI

**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE / ARCHIVE  
**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Subject Status:** **ALIVE — CONFIRMED WITHIN DETACHED SUBSPACE**  
**Access Status:** **IMPOSSIBLE**  
**Lead Investigator:** Neko  
**External Confirmation:** Guiding Light

---

# I. CURRENT SITUATION

Neri remains inside her detached subspace.

This has been confirmed by Guiding Light through the residual Azure Light particulate continuing to originate from within the subspace.

The location is no longer unknown.

The entrance, however, is effectively useless.

Guiding Light can locate the boundary where the entrance **should** be.

She cannot open it.

She cannot break it.

She cannot force it.

She cannot override it.

And now Neko has determined something considerably worse.

### **There is nothing behind the door.**

---

# II. THE DOOR

Under ultraviolet illumination, the Azure particulate forms a faint outline resembling a conventional doorway.

There is no physical door.

No hinges.

No frame.

No handle.

Yet the shape corresponds precisely with the last known connection to Neri's subspace.

Guiding Light can identify it immediately.

> “That's the door.”

She can sense the other side.

Or, more accurately—

she can sense **that there is supposed to be an other side.**

She cannot enter it.

Attempts to interact with the boundary produce no measurable deformation.

Architect-level force produces no meaningful result.

The boundary simply remains.

---

# III. GUIDING LIGHT'S ATTEMPT

Guiding Light attempted to open the entrance.

Nothing happened.

She attempted to force it open.

Nothing happened.

She attempted to bypass it through dimensional traversal.

Nothing happened.

She attempted to replicate the structural conditions under which Neri originally created the subspace.

Nothing happened.

The boundary did not resist her.

It simply did not acknowledge the attempts.

This distinction is important.

Guiding Light is not being prevented from opening a door.

### **There is no door to open.**

There is only the _concept_ of the entrance remaining behind.

---

# IV. NEKO'S INSPECTION

Neko performed an independent inspection.

His conclusion was initially rejected by several members of SAFEHOUSE because it sounded impossible.

He repeated it.

> “The door leads nowhere.”

A second inspection was performed.

Same result.

A third inspection was performed using anomalous spatial instrumentation.

Same result.

### **No connected space exists beyond the boundary.**

The apparent entrance does not connect to:

- Neri's bedroom.
    
- The floating archipelago.
    
- The primary island.
    
- Any known subspace.
    
- Any Blacksite location.
    
- Any conventional dimension.
    
- Any accessible transitional space.
    

There is simply—

**nothing.**

---

# V. THE IMPOSSIBLE CONTRADICTION

This creates an apparent contradiction.

### Observation A

Neri is still inside her subspace.

### Observation B

The Azure trail continues to originate from that subspace.

### Observation C

Guiding Light can identify the location of the entrance.

### Observation D

Neko can detect no destination behind the entrance.

All four observations are simultaneously true.

Therefore:

> **Neri's subspace exists independently of the entrance through which it was originally accessed.**

The door is no longer functioning as an entrance.

It is functioning as a **reference point.**

---

# VI. THE AZURE TRAIL

The Azure dust makes the situation even stranger.

Under UV illumination, particles continue to drift toward the boundary.

Some disappear when they reach it.

Others appear to emerge from it.

The effect resembles an open doorway.

Spatial analysis disagrees.

There is no measurable passage.

The particulate appears capable of crossing a boundary that conventional space cannot.

Neko's report describes this as:

> “A connection that exists physically only for the thing that created it.”

---

# VII. GUIDING LIGHT'S RESPONSE

Guiding Light stood before the boundary for several minutes after Neko finished his inspection.

> “So she's there.”

Neko nodded.

> “Yes.”

> “And I can find her.”

> “Yes.”

She looked at the doorway.

> “But I can't get to her.”

Neko paused.

> “Correct.”

Guiding Light's expression fell.

> “That's cruel.”

Nobody disagreed.

---

# VIII. THE WORST PART

The entrance does not appear damaged.

It does not appear locked.

It does not appear sealed.

It does not appear destroyed.

It is simply **disconnected.**

There is no mechanism to repair.

No hinge to replace.

No wall to breach.

No lock to pick.

No physical obstruction to remove.

Breaking the entrance would accomplish nothing.

There is nothing behind it to break into.

---

# IX. THE SUBSPACE ITSELF

Neri's original subspace remains structurally consistent with previous observations.

The fifteen floating islands remain.

The primary inhabited island remains.

The artificial stars remain.

The localized gravitational regions remain.

The rope bridges remain.

The laboratory remains.

Her belongings remain.

Nothing indicates that the internal environment has collapsed.

This strongly suggests that Neri did not destroy her own subspace.

She merely became **completely disconnected from external reality.**

---

# X. WHAT THIS MEANS

The Blacksite has effectively reached the most frustrating possible state of a rescue operation.

They know:

**Who:** Neri.

**Where:** Her subspace.

**How to locate it:** Azure Light.

**Where the entrance is:** Known.

**Whether the entrance still functions:** No.

**Whether the subspace still exists:** Yes.

**Whether Neri is alive:** Yes.

**Whether they can reach her:** No.

---

# XI. THE MEMORIAL

The memorial remains unchanged.

The fishbone hairclip.

The knives.

The Starlight Katana.

The crossbow.

The blueberry cupcakes.

Under UV light, the memorial is surrounded by Neri's Azure dust.

Guiding Light eventually placed a UV lamp beside the display.

It remains switched off.

Nobody needs to see the dust anymore.

They already know what it means.

---

# XII. JANE DOE

Jane's response to the investigation was unusually short.

> “So we know where she is.”

Neko answered:

> “Yes.”

> “And we can't get her.”

> “Not currently.”

Jane stared at the floor.

> “Then we'll figure it out.”

Neko asked:

> “What if it takes years?”

Jane answered immediately.

> “Then it takes years.”

> “What if it takes decades?”

> “Then decades.”

> “What if—”

Jane interrupted.

> “She's worth waiting for.”

---

# XIII. BAO

Bao was informed of Neko's findings.

She stood outside the boundary.

She placed one hand against the empty space where the doorway appeared under UV light.

> “Neri?”

Nothing.

Bao waited.

> “You don't have to come out yet.”

A pause.

> “But we're still here.”

One tiny azure particle appeared.

Bao smiled.

> “I know.”

---

# XIV. CURRENT DIRECTIVE

The boundary is **NOT** to be forcibly destroyed.

No further attempts at brute-force entry are authorized until the nature of the disconnected spatial reference is understood.

Guiding Light will continue monitoring the location.

Neko will continue attempting to determine what constitutes the missing connection.

SAFEHOUSE personnel will maintain the area.

The Azure particulate is to remain undisturbed.

And the door—

despite leading nowhere—

will remain marked.
