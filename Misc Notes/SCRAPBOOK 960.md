**SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL NOTE**

**Subject:** Jane Doe  
**Incident:** Neri — Acute Anemic Shock  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE Cafeteria  
**Witness:** Jane Doe  
**Primary Responder:** Bao

---

Jane had been sitting several tables away.

She had been eating.

Of course she had been eating.

Then she noticed Neri stop moving.

At first, Jane assumed Neri had simply fallen asleep over her chemistry book.

Then the book slid from Neri's hands.

Her head dropped.

Her breathing became shallow.

Jane's expression changed immediately.

> **Jane:** "…Neri?"

No response.

Jane stood.

By the time she reached the table, Neri was already extremely pale.

Bao arrived only moments later.

She immediately recognized what was happening.

> **Bao:** "Anemia."

Jane froze.

> **Jane:** "…What?"

Bao didn't explain.

There wasn't time.

She took one of her own fingers, made a small cut, and allowed several drops of blood to fall into Neri's mouth.

Jane stared.

Neri swallowed reflexively.

For several seconds, nothing happened.

Then—

Neri's breathing steadied.

Color slowly returned to her face.

Her heart stabilized.

Jane looked at Bao.

Then at Neri.

Then back at Bao.

There was a long silence.

> **Jane:** "…I have questions."

Bao sighed.

> **Bao:** "I know."

> **Jane:** "A lot of questions."

> **Bao:** "I know."

---

### JANE DOE — INITIAL QUESTIONS

Jane's questioning began immediately after Neri was stable.

**Question 01:**  
"Why did Neri collapse?"

**Answer:**  
Her blood had degraded to a critically low functional level, triggering cardiovascular failure.

Jane frowned.

**Question 02:**  
"Her blood just… stops working?"

**Answer:**  
Essentially.

Jane stared at Neri.

**Question 03:**  
"How often does this happen?"

Bao did not immediately answer.

Jane noticed.

She did not like the hesitation.

**Question 04:**  
"How long has this been happening?"

**Question 05:**  
"How close was she to dying?"

**Question 06:**  
"Would she have died if nobody noticed?"

Bao's expression became serious.

> **Bao:** "Yes."

Jane went quiet.

That answer bothered her considerably more than the others.

---

### THE BLOOD

Jane eventually pointed toward Bao's finger.

> **Jane:** "And your blood fixes it?"

> **Bao:** "It stabilizes her."

> **Jane:** "Why yours?"

> **Bao:** "It's compatible."

> **Jane:** "Compatible how?"

Bao paused.

> **Bao:** "Her body accepts it."

Jane's eyes narrowed.

That was not a satisfying answer.

> **Jane:** "So you're basically carrying emergency blood for your daughter."

> **Bao:** "Yes."

> **Jane:** "And you can just give it to her like that?"

> **Bao:** "When necessary."

Jane looked at Neri.

Then back at Bao.

> **Jane:** "Does she need you every time?"

> **Bao:** "Not necessarily."

> **Jane:** "But if her heart starts failing?"

Bao nodded.

> **Bao:** "Then I need to be there."

Jane became noticeably quieter.

---

### THE QUESTION JANE DIDN'T EXPECT TO ASK

Jane eventually crouched beside Neri.

Neri was conscious again, exhausted and confused.

> **Jane:** "Hey."

Neri looked up.

> **Neri:** "…Hi."

> **Jane:** "Does this happen a lot?"

Neri shrugged.

> **Neri:** "Sometimes."

Jane stared at her.

> **Jane:** "You say that like you're talking about getting a headache."

Neri looked down.

> **Neri:** "I don't know what it is."

Jane's expression softened.

> **Jane:** "You don't know?"

Neri shook her head.

> **Neri:** "Everything gets blurry then I can't breathe and fell."

Jane was silent.

She had interrogated hostile operatives before.

She had extracted information from people who desperately wanted to hide it.

She had lied, manipulated, intimidated, and read people for years.

But there was something about Neri casually describing an episode that could kill her that made Jane uncomfortable.

Not professionally uncomfortable.

Personally uncomfortable.

---

### JANE'S FINAL QUESTIONS

Jane eventually turned back toward Bao.

> **Jane:** "How do we know when it's happening?"

Bao explained the warning signs.

Jane listened carefully.

She memorized them.

> **Jane:** "And if she's unconscious?"

> **Bao:** "Call medical staff. Call me."

> **Jane:** "Immediately?"

> **Bao:** "Immediately."

Jane nodded.

Then she pointed toward Neri.

> **Jane:** "And nobody gives her anything by mouth if she's unconscious?"

> **Bao:** "Correct."

Jane nodded again.

That answer, at least, was simple.

She looked at Neri.

The little seal had already picked her chemistry book back up.

Jane sighed.

> **Jane:** "You're a walking medical emergency."

Neri frowned.

> **Neri:** "I'm not."

> **Jane:** "You literally just almost died while reading."

Neri paused.

> **Neri:** "…Oh."

Jane rubbed her forehead.

> **Jane:** "Yeah. We're having a conversation about this later."

Neri quietly returned to her book.

Bao smiled faintly.

Jane sat down beside them.

She didn't leave.

---

### INTERNAL OBSERVATION

Jane Doe's response was unusual.

She did not treat the incident as intelligence.

She did not attempt to document Neri's condition for operational purposes.

She simply stayed nearby.

When asked why, Jane answered:

> **"Because apparently someone has to make sure the kid doesn't quietly die while eating cake."**
