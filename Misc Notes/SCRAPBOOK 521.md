# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL / ANOMALOUS PERSONNEL RECORD**

# **"I'M JUST A DOLL"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** Recovered / Psychologically Unstable  
**Primary Concern:** **Persistent Dehumanization of Self**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's recovery has not altered the central belief underlying her psychological deterioration.

She continues to reject the idea that she is a person.

During a recent conversation, she stated:

> **"Don't treat me like a person."**

> **"I'm not even alive nor dead."**

> **"I'm just a doll that have a conscious, moved by Ether."**

The statement is consistent with a pattern that has persisted since her disappearance.

Corin does not merely believe that her body is abnormal.

She believes her abnormal body invalidates her personhood.

---

# **THE DOLL**

Corin's description of herself as a **doll** is particularly revealing.

She sees her body as something assembled rather than something belonging to her.

Her reasoning is based on her own physical condition:

- Her body is dead.
    
- Her organs do not function conventionally.
    
- Ether sustains her.
    
- Her limbs can detach.
    
- Her limbs can be stitched back together.
    
- Her wounds repair themselves.
    
- Her food is dissolved into Ether.
    
- Her vocal cords were absent and had to be reconstructed.
    
- Her body does not respond to injury like a living human body.
    

To Corin, these facts form a simple conclusion:

> **"I'm not a person."**

The Architects disagree.

Corin remains unconvinced.

---

# **THE WRIST SCAR**

A large scar is visible across Corin's wrist.

It is older than the recent events surrounding her disappearance.

The scar is associated with previous attempts to end her existence.

Corin had repeatedly tried to determine whether her anomalous body could be made to stop functioning.

It could not.

The injuries repaired themselves.

Her Ether sustained her.

She remained conscious.

She continued existing.

The scar therefore became another reminder of something Corin finds deeply distressing:

**Even death is no longer something she can choose.**

---

# **"AND YET I CAN'T"**

Corin once stared at the scar for several minutes.

She eventually said:

> **"I tried."**

Guiding Light remained silent.

Corin touched the scar.

> **"It didn't work."**

A pause.

> **"Nothing works."**

She looked at her own hand.

> **"I can't live normally."**

Then:

> **"I can't die normally."**

And finally:

> **"So what am I supposed to be?"**

Guiding Light did not have an answer she believed would satisfy her.

---

# **THE PROBLEM WITH THE WORD "DOLL"**

Curious Light has noted that Corin's description contains an important contradiction.

A conventional doll has:

- No consciousness.
    
- No memories.
    
- No personal identity.
    
- No desires.
    
- No relationships.
    

Corin possesses all of these.

She remembers.

She chooses.

She dislikes things.

She enjoys sandwiches.

She likes pickles.

She cares about Orphie.

She cares for Jay.

She becomes angry.

She becomes frightened.

She laughs.

She cries.

She makes decisions.

A doll does not do these things.

Corin does.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

When this was explained to her, Corin simply replied:

> **"Then I'm a doll with a conscious."**

Curious Light corrected her:

> **"That's still a person."**

Corin shook her head.

> **"No."**

> **"Why?"**

She looked at her hands.

> **"Because people are alive."**

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light has stopped trying to win the argument through definitions.

Telling Corin:

> _"You're a person."_

has never been enough.

Instead, she focuses on what Corin actually does.

When Corin says:

> **"Dolls don't need sandwiches."**

Guiding Light answers:

> **"You like sandwiches."**

When Corin says:

> **"Dolls don't have families."**

Guiding Light answers:

> **"You have Orphie."**

When Corin says:

> **"Dolls don't have feelings."**

Guiding Light answers:

> **"You clearly do."**

She does not attempt to make Corin's body normal.

She simply refuses to let Corin's abnormality define the entirety of who she is.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has a simpler response.

When Corin once said:

> **"I'm just a doll."**

Orphie answered:

> **"Then you're my sister-shaped doll."**

Corin stared at her.

Orphie smiled.

Corin eventually laughed.

It was brief.

But it was real.

---

# **THE SCAR**

The wrist scar remains.

C.O.R.E. will not remove it without Corin's consent.

It is part of her history.

It represents something painful.

It also represents something else.

Every attempt failed.

Not because Corin was weak.

Not because she was incapable.

But because her body refused to stop.

That fact is deeply frustrating to her.

It is also evidence that, despite how Corin understands herself, some part of her continued choosing to remain present.

---

# **CURRENT PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT**

Corin's central identity belief remains:

> **"I am not alive."**

Secondary belief:

> **"I am not dead."**

Resulting conclusion:

> **"I am a thing."**

Current self-description:

> **"A conscious doll moved by Ether."**

The belief remains persistent.

Attempts to directly contradict it have limited effectiveness.

C.O.R.E. therefore recommends continued social interaction, patience, and reinforcement of Corin's individual identity without forcing her to accept a definition she currently rejects.

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

Corin's body is strange.

It is dead in some ways.

Alive in others.

Sustained by Ether.

Covered in scars.

Held together by stitches.

Capable of repairing wounds that should be fatal.

Unable to die conventionally.

Unable to return to the life she once knew.

None of that makes her ordinary.

But none of it determines whether she deserves to be treated with care.

Corin may insist:

> **"I'm just a doll."**

The people around her will continue answering in their own ways.

Not by pretending her body is normal.

Not by denying what happened to her.

But by reminding her of something simpler:

**A doll cannot choose who to love.**

**Corin can.**

**End of Psychological / Anomalous Personnel Record**
