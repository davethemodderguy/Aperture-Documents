# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **MEDICAL / RECOVERY MILESTONE REPORT**

# **CORIN — RETURN TO FLIGHT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Location:** Treehouse  
**Status:** **Fully Mobile / Recovery Exceeded Expectations**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has successfully returned to flight.

After recovering from her mercury poisoning and regaining her normal form, Corin cautiously tested her wings.

The first attempt was successful.

The second was considerably more confident.

By the third attempt, Corin was flying normally.

---

# **FIRST FLIGHT**

Corin stood outside the Treehouse.

She extended both wings.

For several seconds, she simply looked at them.

Then she pushed downward.

**WHOOSH.**

Her feet left the ground.

She remained airborne.

Her wings continued beating.

Corin looked genuinely surprised.

She made another wingbeat.

Then another.

She was flying.

---

# **RECOVERY RESULT**

The flight test revealed something unexpected.

Corin was not merely back to her previous baseline.

She appeared **stronger than before her illness**.

Her wingbeats were more powerful.

Her acceleration was improved.

Her aerial control was noticeably sharper.

Her endurance also appeared significantly greater.

C.O.R.E. has not yet determined whether this represents:

- Regenerative overcompensation.
    
- Ether adaptation.
    
- Physiological strengthening following prolonged regeneration.
    
- Or another undocumented effect of Corin's anomalous biology.
    

Further testing is pending.

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

Corin flew higher.

Then faster.

She performed a wide turn around the Treehouse.

She descended.

Pulled upward.

Then performed a small aerial roll.

She landed safely.

She stood there for a moment.

Then smiled.

> **"...It works."**

She spread her wings again.

> **"It really works."**

---

# **DESCRIPTION OF THE ILLNESS**

When asked to describe the pain she experienced during the mercury poisoning, Corin struggled to explain it.

Eventually, she gave a simple description:

> **"It felt like my heart and organs were being constantly stabbed."**

C.O.R.E. medical personnel consider this description consistent with the severe, persistent pain caused by her body's continuous regenerative response to the mercury-induced damage.

The toxin was repeatedly damaging tissue.

Her regeneration repeatedly repaired it.

The process prevented catastrophic organ failure.

But Corin experienced the pain of that cycle continuously.

---

# **WHY SHE SURVIVED**

Corin's regenerative factor prevented the mercury from destroying her organs.

Her body continually replaced damaged tissue faster than the toxin could permanently destroy it.

Eventually, the mercury was successfully expelled.

The cost was immense physical exhaustion and prolonged pain.

However, the recovery period appears to have produced an unexpected secondary effect.

Her body may have adapted to the extreme regenerative demand.

---

# **CURRENT CAPABILITIES**

Corin has now demonstrated:

- Normal humanoid form.
    
- Full wing deployment.
    
- Sustained flight.
    
- Improved aerial acceleration.
    
- Improved maneuverability.
    
- Increased apparent physical strength.
    
- Stable Ether activity.
    
- Regeneration functioning at baseline.
    
- Restored vocalization.
    
- Full cognitive function.
    

Her miniature feline form remains available.

Whether she can voluntarily enter it again without triggering her previous involuntary transformation remains under observation.

---

# **ORPHIE'S OBSERVATION**

Orphie watched Corin complete her first flight from the ground.

When Corin landed, Orphie simply smiled.

> **"You're really back."**

Corin looked at her wings.

Then at Orphie.

> **"Better than back."**

She stretched her wings.

> **"Stronger."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

For weeks, Corin could barely move.

She could not speak.

She could not fly.

She could barely eat.

She spent much of her time lying flat with her wings folded, enduring what she later described as:

> **"My heart and organs being constantly stabbed."**

Yet her body kept repairing itself.

The mercury is gone.

Her strength has returned.

Her normal form has returned.

And now—

**so has her flight.**

If anything, Corin came out of the ordeal stronger than she entered it.

The first thing she did with that strength was fly.

**End of Medical / Recovery Milestone Report**
