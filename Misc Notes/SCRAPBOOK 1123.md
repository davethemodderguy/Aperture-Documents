**SUBJECT:** NIGHT STALKER — Owner Customization Program

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neri has acknowledged a recurring complaint from Night Stalker owners.

The complaint:

> **“Everyone's Night Stalker looks the same.”**

There is one exception.

**Elsher's Night Stalker.**

Following the completion of the Night Stalker authorization program, Elsher exchanged his original NS-006 configuration for a personalized variant featuring a space-themed aesthetic, including his requested visual details.

This apparently created a problem.

Other owners noticed.

They were not particularly subtle about it.

---

# 2. THE COMPLAINTS

C.O.R.E. has recorded numerous comments from Night Stalker owners.

Examples include:

> **“Why does Elsher get a special one?”**

> **“Mine looks exactly like everyone else's.”**

> **“Can I get mine customized?”**

> **“Neri, please.”**

> **“I don't need another Night Stalker. I want _my_ Night Stalker.”**

> **“Why does Elsher get the cool one?”**

The final statement was reportedly repeated by multiple MSAC personnel.

Neri eventually stopped ignoring them.

---

# 3. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri appeared in the Armory carrying a stack of forms.

She placed them on the counter.

The header read:

> ## **NIGHT STALKER PERSONALIZATION REQUEST**

Below it:

> **“If you want yours customized, fill this out.”**

At the bottom:

> **“No ETAs.”**

---

# 4. THE FORM

The form allows the current owner to request personalized aesthetic changes to their assigned Night Stalker.

The exact contents are not being reproduced in full, but the form includes sections for:

- owner identification;
    
- preferred visual theme;
    
- engraving preferences;
    
- display preferences;
    
- sight appearance preferences;
    
- finish preferences;
    
- symbolic elements;
    
- color/material appearance;
    
- personal markings;
    
- and additional notes.
    

One section is simply labeled:

> **“What do you want it to look like?”**

The space provided is approximately half a page.

Neri apparently expects people to have opinions.

---

# 5. IMPORTANT RESTRICTION

The customization program does **not** increase the total number of Night Stalkers.

There remain exactly:

> **10**

manufactured units.

The program does not create new pistols.

It does not replace existing ownership.

It does not alter the Night Stalker's limited-production status.

It simply allows Neri to personalize existing authorized units.

---

# 6. NO ETA

The most important line on the form is:

> **NO ETA.**

Neri has explicitly refused to provide completion dates.

When asked:

> **“How long will customization take?”**

Neri responded:

> **“I don't know.”**

When asked:

> **“A week?”**

> **“No.”**

> **“A month?”**

> **“Maybe.”**

> **“Six months?”**

> **“Maybe.”**

> **“A year?”**

Neri shrugged.

> **“I don't know.”**

---

# 7. REASON

Neri is not operating a conventional customization service.

She is doing the work herself.

She has other research.

She has chemistry.

She has her own projects.

She has apparently been taking MP5SDs, magazines and stocks from the Armory for reasons she has not yet disclosed.

She also continues to maintain normal resident responsibilities.

Consequently:

> **Night Stalker customization happens when Neri feels like working on it.**

There is no production schedule.

There is no queue estimate.

There is no guaranteed delivery date.

# 9. ELSHER'S STATUS

Elsher's existing personalized Night Stalker remains unchanged.

His configuration remains recognized as the first major example of owner-specific Night Stalker personalization.

It is therefore no longer unique in principle.

It remains unique in its specific design.

Neri has confirmed that:

> **“Everyone gets their own.”**

Whether that means everyone receives something equally elaborate remains unknown.

---

# 10. NERI'S DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Neri has apparently adopted a simple principle:

> **“It's yours.”**

If someone has earned the right to own a Night Stalker, she believes they should be able to make it feel like **their** Night Stalker.

Not a standardized issue weapon.

Not an identical copy of somebody else's.

Their own.

This appears to be particularly important given the significance the weapons have accumulated.

Each Night Stalker already carries:

> **TACHYON INDUSTRIES**

and its owner's name.

Neri's customization program adds another layer:

> **identity.**

---

# 11. NS-010 IS EXCLUDED

There is one obvious exception.

**NS-010 — NIGHT STALKER "REGALIA"**

is not part of the customization program.

It remains permanently displayed in the Blacksite Museum.

Its gold case, Nekonium-plated glass, three loaded magazines, handwritten letter, Tachyon Industries engraving, and Neri's signature remain unchanged.

Nobody owns it.

Nobody customizes it.

Nobody gets to submit a form.
