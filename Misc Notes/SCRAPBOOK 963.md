# SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL INCIDENT REPORT

## ANEMIC EPISODE — NERI / JANE DOE

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE Cafeteria  
**Activity:** Lunch  
**Subject:** Neri  
**Witness:** Jane Doe  
**Medical Responder:** Bao  
**Severity:** Critical

---

## 1. THE LUNCH

It was supposed to be an ordinary lunch.

Jane had taken a seat across from Neri.

Neri was eating quietly while occasionally glancing at a chemistry book beside her plate.

Jane was talking.

Mostly about nothing important.

Neri occasionally answered.

> **Neri:** "Mm."

> **Jane:** "That's a very enthusiastic response."

> **Neri:** "I'm eating."

> **Jane:** "Fair."

Several minutes passed.

Neri gradually stopped responding.

Jane didn't immediately notice.

She was still talking while Neri had settled into her usual posture:

Head resting against her hands.

Elbows on the table.

Eyes open.

Completely still.

Jane continued eating.

> **Jane:** "And then I told them, 'If you're going to leave a security terminal unlocked, at least don't name the password—'"

She stopped.

Neri hadn't responded.

Jane looked up.

Neri was still in the exact same position.

Her eyes were open.

She appeared to be looking toward Jane.

> **Jane:** "Neri?"

Nothing.

Jane waved a hand.

Nothing.

> **Jane:** "Hey."

Nothing.

Jane's expression changed.

She stood and moved around the table.

> **Jane:** "Neri?"

Still nothing.

She gently checked her.

Neri was unconscious.

Again.

---

# 2. THE REALIZATION

Jane immediately called for medical assistance.

Her voice was considerably less playful than usual.

> **Jane:** "Medical! Neri's down!"

Staff began moving.

Jane remained beside Neri.

And then something struck her.

Neri's eyes were still open.

Exactly like before.

There had been no dramatic collapse.

No falling from the chair.

No warning loud enough for the cafeteria to notice.

She had simply...

**stopped.**

Jane stared at her.

> **Jane:** "You were sitting here the whole time."

There was no response.

Jane felt a cold realization settle in.

If Jane hadn't looked up—

Neri could have remained there.

Quietly.

Unnoticed.

---

# 3. NERI RETURNS

Bao arrived.

The medical response began.

Neri was stabilized.

Eventually, her eyes moved.

She blinked.

Jane leaned closer.

> **Jane:** "Neri?"

Neri looked at her.

Her vision was clearly still unfocused.

> **Neri:** "…Jane?"

> **Jane:** "Yeah."

Neri blinked again.

> **Neri:** "What happened?"

Jane stared at her.

> **Jane:** "You went unconscious."

Neri was quiet.

Then:

> **Neri:** "Oh."

Jane's expression hardened.

> **Jane:** "That's it?"

Neri looked confused.

> **Neri:** "What?"

> **Jane:** "You just said 'oh.'"

> **Neri:** "What am I supposed to say?"

Jane couldn't answer immediately.

Neri looked toward the table.

Her lunch was still there.

Her chemistry book was still open.

> **Neri:** "Did I finish eating?"

Jane stared at her.

That question bothered her.

But what came next bothered her much more.

---

# 4. THE MOST CONCERNING REACTION

Jane sat beside her.

> **Jane:** "Neri."

> **Neri:** "Hm?"

> **Jane:** "You could have died."

Neri looked at her.

There was no visible panic.

No shock.

No fear.

She simply nodded.

> **Neri:** "I know."

Jane went silent.

> **Jane:** "You know?"

> **Neri:** "Yeah."

> **Jane:** "And you're okay with that?"

Neri didn't answer immediately.

She looked down.

> **Neri:** "If I die, I die."

Jane's face went completely still.

That was the answer she had been afraid of.

Not:

_"I don't want to die."_

Not:

_"I'm scared."_

Not:

_"Please don't let me die."_

Just:

> **"If I die, I die."**

Jane stared at her.

> **Jane:** "Neri."

Neri looked up.

> **Jane:** "That's not normal."

Neri shrugged.

> **Neri:** "I can't stop it."

> **Jane:** "That's not what I asked."

Silence.

> **Jane:** "Do you care if you die?"

Neri's eyes moved away.

She didn't answer.

Jane understood.

---

# 5. JANE PUTS IT TOGETHER

Jane had already suspected that Neri didn't take her own condition seriously.

This confirmed it.

Neri understood that her episodes could kill her.

She understood that Bao sometimes had to intervene.

She understood that losing consciousness could mean her heart was failing.

And yet, when confronted with the possibility of dying, her response was essentially:

**Accept it.**

Jane had spent years reading people.

She knew the difference between someone who was afraid and someone who was pretending not to be afraid.

Neri wasn't pretending.

That was the problem.

---

# 6. "WHY DIDN'T YOU TELL ME?"

Jane's voice softened.

> **Jane:** "Why didn't you tell me you felt sick?"

Neri looked at her.

> **Neri:** "You were talking."

> **Jane:** "So?"

> **Neri:** "I didn't want to interrupt."

Jane closed her eyes for a moment.

There it was again.

The same pattern.

Even while her body was shutting down, Neri's first concern had apparently been whether she was inconveniencing Jane.

> **Jane:** "Neri."

> **Neri:** "Hm?"

> **Jane:** "You were dying."

Neri looked down.

> **Neri:** "I know."

> **Jane:** "And you were worried about interrupting my conversation?"

Neri nodded.

Jane was visibly struggling to keep her composure.

> **Jane:** "You are allowed to interrupt me."

Neri said nothing.

> **Jane:** "You are allowed to interrupt everyone."

Still nothing.

> **Jane:** "If you're having trouble breathing, you interrupt."

> **Jane:** "If everything goes blurry, you interrupt."

> **Jane:** "If your heart hurts, you interrupt."

Jane paused.

> **Jane:** "If you're dying, you interrupt."

Neri looked at her.

> **Neri:** "Okay."

---

# 7. THE QUESTION JANE DIDN'T WANT TO ASK

Jane hesitated.

Then she asked:

> **Jane:** "If nobody had noticed you today..."

Neri waited.

> **Jane:** "Would you have told anyone?"

Neri shook her head.

Jane's heart sank.

> **Jane:** "Why?"

Neri answered quietly.

> **Neri:** "I thought I'd wake up."

Jane looked at her.

> **Jane:** "And if you didn't?"

Neri shrugged.

> **Neri:** "Then I wouldn't."

That sentence stayed with Jane.

Not because it was dramatic.

Because Neri said it like a simple fact.

---

# 8. JANE'S RESPONSE

Jane leaned forward.

> **Jane:** "Listen to me."

Neri looked at her.

> **Jane:** "Your death is not an acceptable solution."

Neri blinked.

> **Neri:** "It's not a solution."

Jane paused.

She hadn't expected that answer.

Neri continued quietly.

> **Neri:** "It's just what happens."

Jane shook her head.

> **Jane:** "No."

Neri stared at her.

> **Jane:** "It is what happens if everyone gives up."

Jane pointed toward herself.

> **Jane:** "I'm not giving up."

Then toward the cafeteria.

> **Jane:** "They're not giving up."

Then toward Bao.

> **Jane:** "Your mom definitely isn't giving up."

She looked back at Neri.

> **Jane:** "So you don't get to give up either."

Neri's eyes began watering.

She didn't cry.

She just sat there.

---

# 9. THE LUNCH

Eventually, Neri looked toward her food.

> **Neri:** "My lunch is cold."

Jane stared at her.

For several seconds.

Then she laughed despite herself.

> **Jane:** "You're unbelievable."

> **Neri:** "Can I finish it?"

Jane sighed.

> **Jane:** "After medical says you're okay."

Neri nodded.

Jane remained beside her.

She didn't return to her own seat.

She didn't resume her meal.

She simply stayed.

Because Jane had learned something today.

The greatest danger wasn't Neri's anemia itself.

The Blacksite already knew how dangerous that was.

The greatest danger was that **Neri could be dying and still think that nobody needed to be bothered.**

And Jane wasn't going to let that remain normal.

---

> _Ask if she wants to live and she'll hesitate._

> _She's supposed to be afraid of dying._

> _She's supposed to want another piece of cake._

> _She's supposed to complain about homework._

> _She's supposed to grow up._

> _So if she can't convince herself that her life matters yet…_

> _I'll remind her._

> _Every single time._
