## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORIN / POST-RECOVERY PSYCHOLOGICAL STATUS**

# **THE WOUND THAT DIDN'T HEAL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** **RECOVERING / CRITICAL PSYCHOLOGICAL DISTRESS**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's physical recovery has begun.

It is slow.

Her body is repairing itself.

Her strength is gradually returning.

Her temperature has stabilized.

She is eating again, although only in small amounts.

She is drinking.

She can sleep for longer periods.

Her wings are functioning.

Her regenerative factor continues to work.

But there is one injury that has shown no meaningful improvement.

Not in her body.

In her mind.

---

# **THE PHYSICAL RECOVERY**

Medical personnel initially expected Corin's anomalous regeneration to accelerate her recovery significantly.

It has.

But only physically.

The damage caused by prolonged exposure to extreme cold, starvation, dehydration, and exhaustion is gradually being repaired.

Her body is doing what it has always done.

It heals.

Again.

And again.

And again.

But Corin herself does not seem interested in getting better.

---

# **ORPHIE'S EXPLANATION**

Orphie eventually explained everything.

She told Corin where she had been.

She explained the cave.

The isolation.

The berries.

The vines.

The failed attempts to return.

She explained that she had never intended to disappear.

She never abandoned Corin.

She never stopped looking.

Corin listened.

She did not argue.

She did not accuse Orphie of lying.

She simply said:

> **"Okay."**

---

# **THE PROBLEM**

The explanation did not fix anything.

Corin understood.

That was the problem.

She understood that Orphie had not intentionally abandoned her.

She understood that it had been an accident.

She understood that Orphie had spent months trying to return.

She understood that everyone had searched.

And yet...

The pain remained.

Because understanding what happened did not erase what Corin experienced.

She still waited.

She still starved.

She still froze.

She still believed she had been forgotten.

Her body experienced those months as reality.

An explanation could not undo them.

---

# **THE FLOWERS**

Orphie brought Corin her favorite flowers.

The same kind she had originally intended to bring to the Treehouse.

She placed them beside Corin's bed.

Corin looked at them.

For a moment, she smiled faintly.

Then the expression disappeared.

> **"They're pretty."**

Orphie asked:

> **"Do you want them?"**

Corin shook her head.

> **"No."**

---

# **THE BIRTHDAY**

Someone suggested recreating Corin's canceled birthday.

A cake.

Decorations.

Her favorite foods.

Everyone together.

A second chance.

Corin's response was immediate.

> **"No."**

When asked why:

> **"I don't want it."**

---

# **NO HAPPINESS**

The residents tried small things.

Movies.

Food.

Games.

Conversation.

Flowers.

Music.

Nothing seemed to reach her.

Corin does not appear angry.

She does not reject people aggressively.

She simply does not want anything.

When offered something she once enjoyed, she frequently responds:

> **"I'm okay."**

Or:

> **"You can keep it."**

---

# **NO HOPE**

The most concerning development is her reaction to the word **hope**.

Corin's notebook once described hope as the thing that kept her alive.

Now she refuses to discuss it.

During one conversation, Orphie said:

> **"You don't have to give up hope."**

Corin looked away.

> **"I already did."**

---

# **THE FLAMES**

Medical personnel have tested one of Corin's feathers.

Nothing.

No azure flame.

No glow.

No heatless combustion.

The feather remains completely dormant.

This is consistent with Corin's current emotional state.

The flame that once represented her refusal to stop has not returned.

---

# **THE WOUND**

A medical researcher asked Corin whether she was still hurting.

She answered:

> **"Not where you can fix it."**

The statement was recorded exactly.

No further questions were asked.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie remains beside her whenever possible.

She does not attempt to force happiness upon Corin anymore.

She simply stays.

Sometimes they sit together in silence.

Sometimes Corin sleeps.

Sometimes Orphie talks.

Corin occasionally listens.

That is enough.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi has adopted the same approach.

She no longer tries to convince Corin that everything will be okay.

Instead, she sits nearby.

No speeches.

No promises.

No demands.

Just presence.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's body is healing.

Slowly.

Her organs are recovering.

Her muscles are strengthening.

Her wings are regaining their strength.

Her body wants to live.

But the part of Corin that spent eight months waiting has not recovered.

Orphie explained everything.

The cave.

The accident.

The search.

The flowers.

The birthday.

None of it changed what Corin felt during those months.

She knows Orphie didn't mean to abandon her.

She knows she was loved.

She knows everyone searched.

And still...

She does not want the flowers.

She does not want the birthday.

She does not want the celebration.

She does not want happiness.

She does not want hope.

Because hope was what kept her waiting.

And waiting was what hurt her.

Her body can regenerate almost anything.

But there is one wound it cannot close.

The wound of believing, for eight months, that nobody was coming.

**End of Corin / Post-Recovery Psychological Status**
