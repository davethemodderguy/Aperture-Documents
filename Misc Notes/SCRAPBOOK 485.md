### **RESTRICTED FAMILY / MEDICAL STATUS RECORD**

# **THE DISTANCE BETWEEN THEM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Architect-Sealed  
**Subjects:** Corin / Orphie / Lumi  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Status:** **LONG-TERM ISOLATION**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The distance between Lumi and her daughters has become more than emotional.

It is physical.

The treehouse sits deep within a forest at the far edge of the Blacksite Perimeter, separated from the main facilities by a considerable distance.

Under ordinary circumstances, the distance would be inconvenient.

For Corin, it is effectively prohibitive.

Her medical condition has made prolonged travel unsafe.

---

# **CORIN'S CONDITION**

Corin's reanimated body requires constant structural maintenance.

Her limbs remain dependent on stitches.

Her Ether maintains her body but does not restore normal biological function.

She cannot reliably perform strenuous activity.

Long-distance travel presents several risks:

- Structural stitches may loosen.
    
- Limbs may separate.
    
- Ether instability may occur.
    
- Severe fatigue can destabilize her condition.
    
- Medical intervention would be difficult while away from the treehouse.
    
- Her psychological state can deteriorate rapidly when overwhelmed.
    

As a result, relocating Corin to the main Blacksite is not considered a practical solution.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE BECOMES HOME**

The treehouse was never intended to become a permanent medical refuge.

It simply became one.

Corin cannot easily leave.

Orphie will not leave Corin alone.

Therefore:

> **If Corin stays, Orphie stays.**

And if Orphie stays, the distance between them and Lumi remains.

---

# **LUMI'S POSITION**

Lumi can travel to the treehouse.

But the journey is lengthy.

Her responsibilities at the Blacksite make frequent visits difficult.

She is still responsible for:

- Arcturus.
    
- Norma.
    
- The Marts.
    
- Her other duties within the Blacksite.
    

None of these responsibilities mean she loves Corin less.

But they mean she cannot simply abandon everything and live in the forest.

This has created an impossible situation.

---

# **THE THREE OF THEM**

Lumi wants to be closer.

Orphie wants Corin to have her mother nearby.

Corin wants her family.

Yet none of them can easily change the circumstances.

There is no villain to blame.

No locked door preventing them from leaving.

No order keeping them apart.

Just distance.

And a body that cannot safely make the journey.

---

# **A CONVERSATION**

One evening, Lumi sat with both girls.

> **Lumi:**  
> "I wish you two could come back with me."

Corin looked down.

> **Corin:**  
> "I can't."

Lumi nodded.

> **"I know."**

Orphie added quietly:

> **"And I'm not leaving her."**

Lumi looked at Orphie.

She understood.

> **"I know."**

Corin's expression tightened.

> **"I'm sorry."**

Lumi immediately shook her head.

> **"Don't apologize."**

> **"This isn't your fault."**

---

# **THE MEDICAL REALITY**

Guiding Light has confirmed that moving Corin to the Blacksite would not automatically solve the isolation problem.

The Blacksite has better medical facilities.

But Corin's condition is not something that conventional equipment can simply treat.

Her body is dependent on a fragile equilibrium between:

**Ether.**

**Physical structure.**

**Consciousness.**

**Memory.**

Breaking that equilibrium could be catastrophic.

The treehouse, despite its isolation, has become the safest environment for maintaining that balance.

---

# **ORPHIE'S CHOICE**

Orphie understands the implications.

She could return to the Blacksite.

She could live among everyone else.

She could have access to normal facilities.

But doing so would mean leaving Corin behind.

She refuses.

Not because she believes she is obligated to care for Corin.

Because she wants to.

> **Orphie:**  
> "If she has to stay here, then I'll stay too."

Lumi looked at her.

> **"You shouldn't have to."**

Orphie replied:

> **"I know."**

> **"I want to."**

---

# **CORIN**

Corin hates this.

She believes her condition has trapped Orphie with her.

One night, she quietly said:

> **"You could leave."**

Orphie looked at her.

> **"I know."**

> **"Then why don't you?"**

Orphie smiled faintly.

> **"Because I don't want to."**

Corin looked away.

> **"...You're weird."**

> **"Probably."**

---

# **LUMI'S VISITS**

Lumi has begun making the treehouse visits more deliberate.

They are still infrequent.

But when she comes, she stays longer.

She cooks with them.

She talks.

She listens.

Sometimes Corin still struggles to remember her.

Sometimes she doesn't.

When Corin forgets, Lumi simply introduces herself again.

No anger.

No disappointment.

Just patience.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The treehouse is far from the Blacksite.

Too far for casual visits.

Too far for ordinary family life.

And Corin's condition makes leaving even more difficult.

So the family remains divided by distance.

Lumi remains at the Blacksite.

Corin remains in the treehouse.

Orphie remains beside Corin.

None of them wanted this arrangement.

None of them chose it.

But they have adapted to it.

The treehouse is no longer merely a remote home.

It is a place where Corin can safely exist.

And until a better solution is found, that means something difficult:

**The family cannot easily come together.**

So they have to keep finding ways to reach each other across the distance.
