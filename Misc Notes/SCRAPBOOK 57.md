# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **CIVILIAN LIAISON LOG**

### **TRADE & FRIENDSHIP AGREEMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although no formal contract exists between Heaven's Blacksite and the five Trusted Visitors, a mutual tradition has naturally developed over time.

The Blacksite provides:

- Medical assistance when needed.
    
- A safe place to rest during long journeys.
    
- Hot meals.
    
- Equipment repairs.
    
- Shelter from severe weather.
    
- Friendly company.
    

In return, the wandering traders bring back goods and stories gathered from across the world.

As one trader put it:

> "You've given us a place to call home between journeys."

> "The least we can do is bring a little bit of the world back with us."

---

# **GOODS BROUGHT TO THE BLACKSITE**

Every visit brings something different.

Common items include:

- Fresh fruits and vegetables unavailable locally.
    
- Rare herbs and spices.
    
- Regional teas.
    
- Coffee beans.
    
- Handmade blankets.
    
- Leather goods.
    
- Traditional clothing.
    
- Books from distant settlements.
    
- Maps updated with newly discovered routes.
    
- Decorative trinkets and souvenirs.
    
- Exotic candies for Belle and Star.
    
- Fresh mint leaves for Belle.
    
- High-quality woodworking timber for Norma.
    
- Rare mechanical components that Neko somehow always finds a use for.
    

Nothing is demanded in exchange.

The traders simply enjoy seeing everyone's reactions.

---

# **THE HORSES**

One tradition quickly became a favorite.

Whenever the caravan arrived, the horses would almost immediately wander toward Arcturus.

By now, the traders had stopped being surprised.

One of them simply laughed.

> "...Looks like they've picked their favorite again."

Arcturus scratched one behind the ears.

> "...Can't say I mind."

Without needing to ask, the traders would hand him the reins.

> "Go on."

> "They've been waiting."

---

# **MORNING RIDES**

On many mornings before the caravan departed, Arcturus could be found riding alongside them through the open fields surrounding the Blacksite.

Sometimes they spoke.

Sometimes they rode in comfortable silence.

The horses seemed equally content either way.

The young boy always insisted on riding beside Arcturus whenever possible.

He listened carefully to every lesson.

> "Hold the reins gently."

> "Trust your horse."

> "And remember..."

Arcturus smiled.

> "The horse is your partner."

> "Not your vehicle."

# **THE TRADERS' PERSPECTIVE**

The eldest trader once explained why they never hesitated to lend Arcturus their horses.

> "A horse knows people better than most humans."

He watched one of the mares gently nuzzle Arcturus's shoulder.

> "If they trust him..."

He smiled.

> "...That's all the proof we need."

---

# **FINAL ENTRY**

As the caravan disappeared beyond the horizon after another visit, the horses occasionally looked back toward the Blacksite before continuing onward.

Belle watched from the entrance.

> "...See you next time."

The young boy stood in the wagon, waving enthusiastically.

Arcturus tipped his blue fedora in farewell.

The lead mare gave a quiet whinny in return.

One researcher watching the exchange remarked:

> "We rescued five traders..."

He looked toward the distant caravan.

> "...But I think we gained five friends."
