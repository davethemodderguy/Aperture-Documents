# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **KCA-PER-014**

### **PERSONNEL PROFILE – EXECUTIVE PROTECTION DETAIL "KEITH"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Security Personnel

---

## **SUMMARY**

Due to the high-risk nature of the Krampus Compliance Authority Director's position, the Central Execution Department permanently assigns an executive protection officer to accompany **Director Belle Merryweather** whenever she is operating within the Banlands.

The assignment is automatically suspended whenever Belle departs for Aperture Industries' Heaven's Blacksite, where Blacksite Security assumes responsibility for her protection.

# **Primary Responsibilities**

Keith's duties include:

- Maintaining Belle's immediate physical security.
    
- Conducting route reconnaissance before official visits.
    
- Monitoring for potential assassination attempts.
    
- Escorting Belle during public appearances.
    
- Responding to armed threats directed toward the Director.
    
- Coordinating with Banlands Enforcement Division during large-scale operations.
    

Keith is authorized to intervene immediately if Belle's safety is endangered.

# **Operational Behavior**

Keith is noted for maintaining exceptional situational awareness.

He habitually:

- Walks slightly behind and to Belle's side.
    
- Scans rooftops, windows, and elevated positions.
    
- Speaks only when operationally necessary.
    
- Maintains professional distance while remaining immediately available.
    

Personnel have described him as calm, disciplined, and unwavering during protective assignments.

---

# **Contrast in Personality**

Multiple officers have commented on the unusual pairing between Belle and Keith.

Belle is energetic, affectionate, and frequently smiles at civilians.

Keith is reserved, stoic, and rarely displays emotion while on duty.

One patrol officer summarized their partnership:

> **"...She greets everyone she meets."**

> **"...He checks every rooftop they pass."**

Despite their contrasting personalities, operational evaluations conclude that the pair work exceptionally well together.

Keith's composed demeanor complements Belle's approachable leadership style.

---

# **Blacksite Protocol**

Whenever Belle travels to Heaven's Blacksite:

- Keith escorts her to the dimensional transit point.
    
- Protection responsibilities are formally transferred to Aperture Industries Security.
    
- Keith immediately returns to Banlands duty.
    

This protocol prevents unnecessary overlap between Banlands security forces and Blacksite personnel.
