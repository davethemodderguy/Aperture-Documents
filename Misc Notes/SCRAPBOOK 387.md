## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL ANOMALOUS PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE**

# **COREY — LUNA BEHAVIORAL UPDATE**

**Classification:** Level 4 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Luna  
**Associated Individual:** Corey  
**Status:** Cooperative / Low Threat

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Further observation of Corey's **Luna** manifestation has revealed a previously underreported aspect of her personality.

While Luna is generally calm, quiet, peaceful, and wise, she possesses a subtle tendency toward:

- Obsessive behavior.
    
- Unusual fixation.
    
- Morbid curiosity.
    
- Mildly deranged humor.
    
- Unconventional ideas.
    

The behavior is noticeable.

It is also considerably less dangerous than it initially sounds.

---

# **PERSONALITY DIFFERENCE**

Luna remains fundamentally Corey.

She retains Corey's:

- Memories.
    
- Values.
    
- Relationships.
    
- Emotional attachments.
    
- General desire to protect others.
    

However, Luna's personality is somewhat less restrained.

She is more willing to explore strange ideas.

She may stare at something for an unusually long time.

She may become intensely interested in a particular subject.

She may occasionally say something that makes everyone else stop talking.

---

# **THE "DERANGED" SIDE**

Luna is not generally violent or hostile.

Her unusual behavior instead manifests as an unsettling level of enthusiasm.

For example, if someone mentions an unusual scientific problem, Luna may become extremely interested.

> "...How does it work?"

If given an explanation:

> "...Interesting."

Then:

> "...Can I make one?"

This is usually followed by a very long silence from everyone else.

---

# **HER MOST CONCERNING TALENT**

Luna has demonstrated a surprising ability to **create intimidating or dangerous-looking things**.

She possesses the creativity and magical control required to construct unusual objects, weapons, traps, or visual effects from light and lunar energy.

However, she rarely has any intention of actually harming someone.

Her interest is primarily:

> **"Can I make it?"**

Not:

> **"Can I hurt someone with it?"**

---

# **EXAMPLE INCIDENT**

Luna once spent several minutes staring at an engineering schematic.

Corin eventually asked:

> "...What are you looking at?"

Luna replied:

> "...Nothing."

Corin:

> "...That doesn't look like nothing."

Luna:

> "...I could make it."

Corin immediately became concerned.

> "...Please don't."

Luna:

> "...Okay."

She put the schematic down.

Five minutes later, she was cooking soup.

---

# **LIGHT MANIPULATION**

Luna's ability to manipulate light makes her particularly capable of creating convincing constructs.

She can form:

- Blades of condensed moonlight.
    
- Spears.
    
- Shields.
    
- Decoys.
    
- Illusions.
    
- Visual disguises.
    
- Temporary structures.
    

Her constructs can appear extremely dangerous.

C.O.R.E. emphasizes:

> **Appearance does not necessarily indicate intent.**

---

# **OBSESSIVE TENDENCIES**

Luna may become fixated on particular subjects.

When interested in something, she can spend hours:

- Studying it.
    
- Recreating it.
    
- Improving it.
    
- Thinking about it.
    
- Asking questions about it.
    

She rarely becomes frustrated.

Instead, she becomes increasingly quiet.

This is generally the point where personnel are advised to check what she is doing.

---

# **COREY'S RESPONSE**

Corey is aware of Luna's behavior.

When asked whether Luna's personality concerns her, Corey answered:

> "...A little."

She then added:

> "...She's still me."

When asked whether Luna should be allowed to experiment freely:

> "...Depends what she's making."

---

# **LUNA'S RESPONSE**

Luna was asked directly whether she considered herself insane.

She thought for several seconds.

> "...Maybe."

Pause.

> "...But I'm functional."

She then returned to cooking.

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

Marts do not appear particularly concerned by Luna's unusual behavior.

They remain strongly attracted to her.

If Luna begins constructing something unusual, Marts may simply gather around and watch.

Typical commentary:

> "...Luna making."

> "...Pretty."

> "...Big."

Luna usually appreciates their approval.

---

# **THREAT ASSESSMENT**

Despite Luna's unusual personality and considerable capabilities, her threat level remains relatively low under normal circumstances.

### **Threat Factors**

**Power:** High  
**Creativity:** High  
**Combat potential:** High  
**Intent to harm:** Low  
**Emotional stability:** Generally stable  
**Cooperation:** High

The primary concern is not that Luna wants to hurt someone.

It is that she may become extremely interested in something that everyone else considers a terrible idea.

---

# **PERSONNEL GUIDELINE**

If Luna becomes unusually quiet and starts staring at a technical schematic:

**Ask what she is doing.**

If Luna answers:

> "...Nothing."

**Ask again.**

If she smiles:

**Find Neko.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Luna is not a dangerous person.

She is simply:

> **A very powerful, very intelligent, slightly obsessive lunar manifestation who occasionally has extremely questionable ideas.**

She can make frightening things.

She can make impressive things.

She can make things that probably should not exist.

But most of the time?

She's just making soup.

**End of Personnel Anomalous Psychological Profile**
