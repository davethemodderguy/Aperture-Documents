## Executive Summary

Neri's intense hostility toward Alice has begun to fade.

The involuntary response documented in C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-NERI-172 is no longer presenting with the same intensity.

Neri still maintains significant distance from Alice.

She still remembers what happened.

She still does not fully trust her.

However, the overwhelming hatred has diminished.

This development occurred without forced reconciliation or deliberate exposure therapy.

Neri simply began feeling less angry.

Unfortunately, this improvement was accompanied by another anemic episode.

---

# 1. Decline in Hostility

Over several days, Neri's reactions to Alice became progressively less severe.

Previously, Alice entering the same room could cause Neri to immediately leave.

More recently, Neri has been able to remain in the same general area.

She does not approach Alice.

She does not seek conversation.

She does not initiate physical contact.

But she no longer appears overwhelmed by the urge to escape.

When asked whether she still hated Alice, Neri wrote:

> **"Less."**

After a pause, she added:

> **"I don't want to anymore."**

No further questioning was conducted.

---

# 2. Current Relationship

Neri's feelings toward Alice should not be described as restored.

The current state is better characterized as:

> **Hostility declining, trust unresolved.**

Neri remembers the harm.

She remembers the period when Alice could not remember her.

She remembers being hurt and forgotten.

Those memories remain.

The difference is that they no longer appear to automatically produce overwhelming hatred.

There is now room for other emotions to exist alongside them.

---

# 3. Anemic Episode

During this period of emotional stabilization, Neri experienced another anemic episode.

The episode was not preceded by notable exertion.

Neri was sitting quietly when her condition deteriorated.

She became pale and visibly weak.

Her breathing became shallow.

Her ability to remain upright decreased.

As she currently has no functional voice, she could not verbally report what was happening.

The episode was identified by observation rather than a request for assistance.

---

# 4. Episode Classification

The episode was classified as **Resentment-class**.

Neri remained conscious during the initial and middle stages.

She experienced significant cardiac pain and increasing weakness.

Unlike the previous five-hour episode, the present event did not continue for as long.

Medical personnel intervened before Neri reached the same prolonged state of suffering.

Neri eventually lost consciousness.

She was subsequently stabilized using the established fictional medical protocol appropriate to her condition.

---

# 5. Neri's Behavior During the Episode

The most concerning behavioral feature was familiar.

Neri did not ask for help.

She recognized that something was wrong.

She attempted to remain still and wait for the episode to pass.

When asked afterward why she had not alerted anyone, Neri wrote:

> **"Didn't want to bother anyone."**

Personnel reminded her that this was a medical emergency.

Neri did not argue.

She simply looked away.

---

# 6. Alice's Presence

Alice was not responsible for the episode.

She was also not the person who discovered Neri this time.

Nevertheless, the relationship between the two remains relevant.

Neri's hatred has faded sufficiently that Alice's presence is no longer automatically interpreted as an immediate emotional threat.

This does **not** mean Neri is ready to return to their former relationship.

It means the defensive response appears to be losing its intensity.

That distinction is important.

---

# 7. Psychological Observation

Neri's emotional state appears to be changing naturally rather than through deliberate intervention.

She has moved from:

> **"I hate Alice."**

toward:

> **"I don't want to hate Alice."**

and now toward:

> **"Less."**

This is not forgiveness.

It is not reconciliation.

It is not forgetting.

Neri is simply becoming less consumed by anger.

That is considered a positive development.

---

# 8. Medical Concern

The improvement in Neri's emotional state must not be mistaken for an improvement in her underlying blood condition.

Her anemic episodes remain active.

The established classifications remain:

### TRANSCENDENCE

Immediate loss of consciousness following activation.

### RESENTMENT

Extended extreme cardiac pain followed by eventual loss of consciousness.

The current episode reinforces that either classification may occur without significant warning.

Neri's inability to speak further increases the importance of passive observation.

## Final Assessment

Two things happened almost simultaneously.

Neri stopped hating Alice so intensely.

Neri became sick again.

The first should not be interpreted as the second being caused by it.

There is no evidence that her emotional recovery triggered the episode.

They simply happened during the same period.

For the first time in some time, Neri can look at Alice without immediately feeling consumed by anger.

She still keeps her distance.

She still remembers.

She still hurts.

But the hatred is fading.

And beneath it, something else may finally be becoming visible again:

**the fact that Neri never actually wanted to lose Alice in the first place.**
