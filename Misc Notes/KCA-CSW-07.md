# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **TACTICAL EQUIPMENT DOSSIER**

### **KCA-CSW-07 "RIPTIDE" – NEKONIUM ASSAULT CHAINSAW**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Authorized Personnel Only

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the acquisition of refined Nekonium by the Krampus Compliance Authority (KCA) and the Central Execution Department (CED), the KCA Tactical Development Branch approved a specialized rescue and breaching tool for Executive Secretary **Remielle "Cinder."**

Official Designation:

> **KCA-CSW-07 "RIPTIDE"**

Although capable of cutting through heavy obstacles during emergency operations, the chainsaw is also authorized for use during high-risk tactical incidents under KCA Rules of Engagement.

---

## **TECHNICAL OVERVIEW**

**Designation:** KCA-CSW-07 "RIPTIDE"

**Classification:** Class-B Executive Tactical Equipment

**Construction:**

- Reinforced Nekonium cutting chain.
    
- High-torque industrial motor.
    
- Shock-resistant frame.
    
- Emergency quick-release system.
    
- KCA identification markings.
    

The housing bears the inscription:

> **REMIELLE**

Below it:

> **PROPERTY OF KCA**

---

## **PRIMARY ROLE**

The KCA-CSW-07 is primarily intended for:

- Emergency breaching.
    
- Cutting through reinforced barriers.
    
- Removing obstructions during rescue operations.
    
- Defeating heavily armored hostile equipment when conventional tools are ineffective.
    

Its deployment requires authorization consistent with KCA operational procedures.

---

## **COMBAT OBSERVATIONS**

During tactical exercises, Remielle demonstrated exceptional proficiency with the equipment.

Evaluators noted that she handled the tool with the same precision she displayed with her issued briefcase.

One instructor remarked:

> "She moves it like it's part of her."

---

## **OPERATIONAL NOTE**

The chainsaw is **not** intended to replace Remielle's KCA multifunction briefcase.

The briefcase remains her primary assigned equipment.

The chainsaw is deployed only for missions where its specialized capabilities are required.

---

## **BEHAVIORAL CONSIDERATIONS**

Personnel records continue to document a significant behavioral shift if Remielle is unexpectedly deprived of her assigned briefcase during a hostile engagement.

In such situations she has demonstrated a tendency to rely on aggressive close-quarters combat techniques until the threat has been neutralized.

For this reason, mission planners are instructed to avoid unnecessary disarmament of KCA personnel and to ensure Remielle has access to appropriate mission equipment whenever operationally feasible.

---

## **EXECUTIVE PROTECTION DIRECTIVE**

The Krampus Compliance Authority maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward attacks against Director Belle Merryweather.

Should Belle's life be placed in immediate danger, KCA and CED personnel are authorized to use the level of force permitted under applicable operational rules to stop the threat.

The priority is:

1. Protect Belle.
    
2. Protect civilians and allied personnel.
    
3. End the attack as quickly as possible.
