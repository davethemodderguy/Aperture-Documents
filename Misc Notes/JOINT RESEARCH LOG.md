# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES / CENTRAL COMPUTING DEPARTMENT**

## **JOINT RESEARCH LOG**

### **SUBJECT: BELLE MERRYWEATHER – SELF-VOLUNTEERED STUDY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Scientific Personnel Only

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the adoption of the Ether Radiation Model, Director Belle Merryweather voluntarily consented to participate in a series of non-invasive and minimally invasive medical studies.

Chief Engineer Neko was appointed lead engineering researcher.

The primary objective was to better understand Belle's unique physiology and unusually stable relationship with Ether.

---

## **PRIMARY RESEARCH SUBJECT**

Belle's blood quickly became the focus of the investigation.

During sample collection, researchers documented several characteristics unlike any previously recorded biological specimen.

---

## **OBSERVATIONS**

Blood samples displayed the following properties:

- A faint shimmering appearance under normal lighting.
    
- A distinct golden coloration.
    
- Continuous low-level luminescence, visible even after collection.
    
- Exceptionally high concentrations of Ether.
    

Repeated laboratory analysis confirmed the readings.

The Ether contained within the blood showed **no measurable corruptive effect** on laboratory equipment or personnel under controlled handling procedures.

The samples remained biologically active far longer than expected.

---

## **ETHER ANALYSIS**

Spectroscopic and anomalous analysis indicated that the Ether present within Belle's bloodstream differed from Ether commonly collected from Hollows.

While chemically and energetically similar, its behavior was markedly different.

Observed characteristics included:

- Stable energy output.
    
- No spontaneous corruption events.
    
- No measurable hostile influence on nearby biological tissue during testing.
    
- Long-term containment stability.
    

Researchers provisionally designated the substance:

> **Concentrated Stable Ether (CSE)**

Further classification remains under review.

---

## **BELLE'S COMMENTS**

Throughout testing, Belle remained cooperative.

During one blood draw she watched the glowing sample for several seconds before smiling.

> "...Huh."

> "...It looks kind of pretty."

When asked if seeing glowing golden blood worried her, she simply replied:

> "...It's been keeping me alive."

> "...So I guess we're getting along."

---

## **NEKO'S NOTES**

Chief Engineer Neko documented the following after completing the initial analysis.

> "This shouldn't exist."

After reviewing the results a second time:

> "No... the instruments are correct."

A third review produced the same conclusion.

> "Either Belle's body is continuously stabilizing the Ether..."

> "...or the Ether has reached a stable equilibrium because of Belle."

He underlined the final sentence twice.

---

## **CCD REVIEW**

Independent verification by the Central Computing Department reproduced Aperture's findings.

No discrepancies were identified between laboratories.

The joint review concluded:

> _"Director Belle Merryweather remains the only documented individual known to sustain extraordinarily high concentrations of Ether within the circulatory system while retaining long-term physiological and psychological stability."_

The mechanism responsible remains unknown.

---

## **CURRENT RESEARCH**

Future studies have been approved in the following areas:

- Interaction between blood cells and Concentrated Stable Ether.
    
- Long-term physiological effects.
    
- Tissue regeneration.
    
- Ether transport throughout the body.
    
- Potential medical applications, subject to ethical review.
    

All experimentation will continue only with Belle's informed consent.

---

## **STATUS**

Research remains ongoing.

Director Belle Merryweather continues to participate voluntarily in the study and has expressed hope that understanding her condition may eventually improve safety and treatment for others exposed to Ether.

**End of Research Log.**
