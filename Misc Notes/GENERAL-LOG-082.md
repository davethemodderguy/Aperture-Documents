# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **GENERAL-LOG-082**

### **OSHA INSPECTION – HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE (FOLLOW-UP VISIT)**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Administrative Archive

## **DATE**

Redacted

---

## **PURPOSE**

A routine Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) inspection was conducted at Heaven's Blacksite.

Unlike previous inspections, several visiting officials from the Banlands Government were present during the audit.

Present were:

- Lucius Merryweather
    
- Lindervene "Endmin" Merryweather
    
- Belle Merryweather
    
- Keith (Executive Protection Officer)
    
- Cinder (Executive Secretary)
    

---

## **INITIAL IMPRESSION**

The OSHA inspectors were greeted by Director Lucious.

After introductions, Director Lucious gestured toward the visitors.

> **"These are representatives of the Banlands Government."

Belle waved enthusiastically.

> **"Hello!"

Endmin gave a polite nod.

Lucius quietly introduced himself.

Keith stood silently behind Belle.

Cinder smiled and shook the inspectors' hands.

The inspection began normally.

---

## **FIRE SAFETY**

Emergency exits were unobstructed.

Fire suppression systems were operational.

Fire extinguishers were inspected.

One inspector paused after noticing Cinder casually creating a small flame in her hand while explaining evacuation routes.

> **"...Should I... be concerned?"

Cinder immediately extinguished it.

> **"...Old habit."

---

## **WEAPON STORAGE**

The Armory passed inspection.

Weapons were properly secured and documented.

The inspector stopped at Belle's hybrid greatsword.

After reading the engraving, he looked up.

> **"...'Property of Bunny Girl'?"

Belle nodded proudly.

> **"...Correct."

The inspector looked at Director Lucious.

Director Lucious simply replied:

> **"...It is, in fact, her property."

The inspection continued.

---

## **EXECUTIVE PROTECTION**

Keith accompanied Belle throughout the inspection while maintaining a discreet distance.

One inspector eventually asked:

> **"Are you required to stay this close?"

Keith answered without hesitation.

> **"...Yes."

Belle looked back.

> **"...You can take a break if you want."

Keith shook his head.

> **"...Respectfully, no."

The inspector quietly wrote:

**Executive protection procedures appear exceptionally disciplined.**

---

## **MEDICAL FACILITIES**

Alice demonstrated the Blacksite's medical wing.

Emergency equipment was fully stocked.

Medical response times met all required standards.

The inspectors complimented the organization.

---

## **RESEARCH WING**

Containment systems were functioning normally.

Hazard signage exceeded minimum requirements.

Ether handling laboratories were inspected separately due to their specialized nature.

No violations were issued.

---

## **BREAK ROOM**

The inspection paused for lunch.

Belle offered the inspectors carrots.

One accepted.

Belle smiled.

> **"...Good choice."

Another inspector reached toward Belle's own carrot.

Keith cleared his throat.

The inspector slowly withdrew his hand.

Cinder quietly whispered:

> **"...Excellent decision."

---

## **RESIDENTIAL AREA**

Room N-07 was inspected for general safety.

Observations included:

- Proper emergency exits.
    
- Functional smoke detectors.
    
- Secure electrical installations.
    
- Reasonably organized living space.
    

The inspector did note an unusually large collection of blankets, plushies, board games, and art supplies.

Lucius explained:

> **"...We spend a lot of time together in here."

The inspector smiled.

> **"...It shows."

---

## **FINAL REVIEW**

Before concluding the inspection, OSHA representatives met with the Banlands visitors.

One inspector asked Belle:

> **"...Anything you'd improve?"

Belle thought seriously.

> **"...More snack shelves."

The inspector laughed.

> **"...Noted."

Endmin added:

> **"...More seating in the briefing rooms."

Lucius suggested:

> **"...Additional studio space for recreational art."

Cinder requested:

> **"...A stronger shelf."

The inspector asked why.

Cinder answered:

> **"...My briefcase weighs fifty-five kilograms."

The inspector quietly wrote:

**Approved.**

---

## **INSPECTION RESULT**

**Overall Rating:** **PASS**

No critical violations identified.

Minor recommendations:

- Install additional seating in common areas.
    
- Reinforce selected storage shelving for unusually heavy equipment.
    
- Increase recreational storage space to accommodate resident activities.
    

---

## **POST-INSPECTION REMARKS**

As the inspectors prepared to leave, one turned back.

> **"...This might be the strangest facility we've ever inspected."

He looked around the lively room.

A sentient bunny with a giant sword was sharing carrots.

A wolf Thiren was sketching the snowy mountains.

A shark Thiren secretary was discussing filing systems while carrying a 55-kilogram briefcase.

Another shark Thiren calmly stood watch with a combat shotgun.

Despite all of it...

Everyone was laughing together.

The inspector smiled.

> **"...It's also one of the healthiest workplaces we've visited."

Director Lucious extended a hand.

> **"We'll take that as a compliment."

The inspector shook it.

> **"...You should."**
