# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – SAFETY ADVISORY**

### **DESIGNATION: M.A.R.T. BLOBS – GRAVITATIONAL ANOMALY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – All Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Research has confirmed that Marts are almost entirely unaffected by conventional gravity.

Rather than falling, they drift through the air at low speeds, their movement influenced primarily by air currents, ventilation systems, and gentle impacts.

This behavior appears to be an innate property of their Ether-stabilized bodies.

---

# **OBSERVATIONS**

Examples of normal Mart behavior include:

- Floating across hallways.
    
- Slowly drifting through courtyards.
    
- Bouncing gently off ceilings.
    
- Riding elevator air currents.
    
- Hovering beside open windows.
    
- Being carried by a light breeze.
    

None of these behaviors are considered abnormal for the species.

---

# **BALCONY INCIDENTS**

Due to their near-weightless nature, Marts occasionally drift beyond balcony railings or through open doors into exterior areas.

A Mart floating outside a balcony is **not** necessarily in distress.

However, without assistance, it may continue drifting for an extended period before finding its way back indoors.

Personnel encountering such a Mart are encouraged to:

- Gently scoop it back inside.
    
- Guide it using a box, clipboard, or similar flat object.
    
- Allow it to continue on its way once safely indoors.
    

Do **not** throw, kick, or swat a drifting Mart.

They are surprisingly aerodynamic.

---

# **RESCUE BEHAVIOR**

When helped back inside, Marts almost always respond enthusiastically.

Typical responses include:

> "Hello!"

> "Yes!"

Or simply:

> "Mart!"

Many will immediately bounce away to resume whatever they were doing before drifting outdoors.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Facilities Management has installed several small "Mart Return Shelves" near frequently used balconies and rooftop access points.

These provide an easy place to set down any drifting Mart before it continues its day.

The official policy is simple.

> **If you see a Mart floating outside...**

> **Please help it back inside.**

It was probably trying to visit someone.

And simply caught the wind.
