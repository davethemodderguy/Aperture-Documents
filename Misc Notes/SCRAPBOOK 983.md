# BLACKSITE ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY REPORT

## NERI — AZURE RESIDUE

**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL  
**Subject:** Neri  
**Phenomenon:** Azure Light Particulate  
**Status:** CONFIRMED  
**Visibility:** Ultraviolet spectrum only

---

## I. CORRECTION TO PREVIOUS ASSESSMENT

Previous reports classified the blue azure particulate as an unidentified residue potentially associated with Neri.

That assessment is now considered **incorrect.**

The particulate has been positively identified as material originating from **Neri herself.**

The dust is not contamination.

It is not environmental residue.

It is not merely a signature.

It is not a byproduct of Guiding Light.

### **The dust is Neri.**

---

# II. COMPOSITION

The particulate consists of extremely small fragments of the anomalous Azure Light integrated into Neri's physiology.

The same Light responsible for Neri's Architect-derived abilities is present within the particles.

Under normal illumination, the material is effectively invisible.

Under ultraviolet illumination, it becomes visibly azure and faintly luminous.

The particles appear to exist in several states simultaneously:

- Fine dust.
    
- Microscopic fragments.
    
- Small crystalline Light shards.
    
- Larger wing-like fragments.
    

Despite their appearance, they do not behave like conventional biological material.

---

# III. WHY NOBODY NOTICED

The Blacksite had been covered in it for months.

Possibly longer.

Nobody noticed because the material is invisible under ordinary lighting.

Neri had been shedding microscopic quantities of Azure Light continuously.

Every hallway she walked through.

Every chair she sat in.

Every laboratory she worked in.

Every time she leaned against a wall.

Every time she sat at her cafeteria table.

Every time she fell asleep somewhere she shouldn't have.

Tiny pieces of her were being left behind.

Nobody knew.

---

# IV. THE CAFETERIA

Neri's usual seat contains one of the highest concentrations discovered.

Under ultraviolet illumination, the chair, table, and surrounding floor are covered in azure particulate.

There are even particles embedded in microscopic scratches in the table.

The cafeteria staff initially wanted to clean the area.

They were stopped.

The table is now designated:

> **PRESERVED — NERI RESIDUAL SITE**

Nobody is permitted to remove the material.

---

# V. THE MEMORIAL

The discovery makes Neri's memorial considerably different from what it originally appeared to be.

Her fishbone hairclip sits beneath normal lighting.

Nothing appears unusual.

Under UV illumination—

it is surrounded by her Light.

Her knives contain small traces.

Her crossbow contains more.

The Starlight Katana contains a particularly concentrated layer.

The objects she carried are effectively surrounded by tiny pieces of the person who carried them.

Guiding Light stood in front of the display for several minutes.

Then she said:

> “She's still here.”

Neko immediately replied:

> “Not literally.”

Guiding Light looked at him.

> “I know.”

She smiled faintly.

> “But these are still pieces of her.”

Neko did not argue.

---

# VI. THE MOST IMPORTANT FINDING

The particulate is not simply being shed randomly.

Its distribution corresponds strongly with Neri's movements prior to disappearance.

The Blacksite effectively contains a record of her existence.

Not photographs.

Not surveillance footage.

Not written reports.

**Physical remnants.**

Everywhere she went, she left something behind.

The Blacksite had unknowingly been preserving her.

---

# VII. GUIDING LIGHT'S RESPONSE

Guiding Light eventually understood something nobody else had considered.

Neri had absorbed part of her wing.

That Light had become part of Neri.

Therefore, when Neri's anomalous physiology began behaving differently—

the Light did not simply disappear.

It continued to exist.

Pieces of Neri's Azure Light had been separating from her over time.

And now those pieces were scattered across the Blacksite.

Guiding Light stood beneath a UV inspection lamp.

Azure dust surrounded her.

She whispered:

> “You carried my Light.”

A pause.

> “And now you're leaving it everywhere.”

She looked toward Neri's empty chair.

> “You really did make this place yours.”

---

# VIII. THE QUESTION OF THE DISAPPEARANCE

The discovery does **not** establish Neri's current location.

The dust is not a confirmed trail leading to her.

It cannot currently be used to locate her.

No evidence demonstrates that the particles are consciously produced.

However, one fact is now certain:

### Neri's physical presence left an anomalous imprint throughout the Blacksite.

Her disappearance did not erase that imprint.

---

# IX. PRESERVATION ORDER

Effective immediately:

**DO NOT CLEAN THE AZURE DUST.**

**DO NOT VACUUM IT.**

**DO NOT COLLECT IT FOR DISPOSAL.**

**DO NOT DESTROY IT.**

Any naturally occurring concentration is to remain undisturbed unless removal is required for immediate safety.

Collected fragments are to be preserved in secure archival conditions.

No attempt is to be made to reconstruct Neri from the particulate without authorization from SAFEHOUSE leadership.

# X. THE BLUEBERRY CUPCAKE

At 19:06, Guiding Light placed another blueberry cupcake on Neri's memorial table.

She activated the UV lamp.

Azure dust surrounded the cupcake.

Not a single piece was visible under normal lighting.

Under UV, however, the entire table looked like it had been sprinkled with tiny blue stars.

Guiding Light stared at it.

Then laughed quietly.

> “You would've eaten this.”

She sat down.

Nobody else joined her.

For several minutes, she simply stayed there.
