# 1. TRIAL RESULT

Neither participant was:

- knocked unconscious;

### RESULT:

> **ELSHER — WINNER**

---

# 2. JANE DOE — POST-TRIAL ASSESSMENT

Jane Doe was asked to provide her assessment.

She appeared mildly disappointed.

Not because the participants had won.

Because she had expected them to make at least one particularly obvious mistake.

> **JANE:**  
> “They were annoying.”

Pause.

> **C.O.R.E. OFFICER:**  
> “That's a compliment?”

> **JANE:**  
> “Yes.”

She then looked at Elsher.

> **JANE:**  
> “You were especially annoying.”

Elsher smiled.

> **ELSHER:**  
> “Thank you.”

> **JANE:**  
> “That wasn't a compliment.”

> **ELSHER:**  
> “I'm taking it as one.”

---

# 3. MSAC COMMANDER

The Commander demonstrated unusually disciplined behavior throughout the trial.

Rather than attempting to actively locate Jane, the Commander focused on:

- maintaining awareness;
    
- minimizing predictable movement;
    
- controlling exposure;
    
- avoiding unnecessary engagements;
    
- and refusing to allow Jane to dictate the pace of the encounter.
    

Jane attempted several approaches.

None succeeded.

At the conclusion of the trial, Jane simply appeared beside the Commander.

> **JANE:**  
> “Congratulations.”

The Commander turned.

> **COMMANDER:**  
> “You could have done that earlier.”

Jane smiled.

> **JANE:**  
> “Yes.”

The Commander stared at her.

> **COMMANDER:**  
> “You waited until the trial was over.”

> **JANE:**  
> “Obviously.”

---

# 4. ELSHER

Elsher's performance was similarly successful.

His previous experience with Tachyon Industries trials appears to have contributed significantly to his ability to recognize deceptive patterns.

He did not attempt to chase Jane.

He did not attempt to interrogate her.

He did not attempt to establish a direct confrontation.

He simply survived.

At one point, Jane reportedly came within extremely close proximity.

Elsher noticed.

Jane withdrew.

Neither participant commented on the encounter.

After the trial:

> **JANE:**  
> “You saw me.”

> **ELSHER:**  
> “Yeah.”

> **JANE:**  
> “Why didn't you run?”

> **ELSHER:**  
> “You wanted me to.”

Jane paused.

> **JANE:**  
> “...I dislike you.”

> **ELSHER:**  
> “I'll take that as a compliment too.”
