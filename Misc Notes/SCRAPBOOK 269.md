## **C.O.R.E. – PSYCHOLOGICAL COUNSELING TRANSCRIPT**

### **SUBJECT: COREY LOCKWOOD – EXISTENTIAL DISTRESS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Executive, Medical & Psychological Personnel Only

---

# **SESSION OVERVIEW**

The following excerpts were recorded with Corey's permission during a routine counseling session after several weeks of increasing emotional withdrawal.

The discussion shifted away from the Courtyard Incident and toward a question Corey had been unable to answer herself.

---

# **TRANSCRIPT EXCERPT**

**Psychologist**

> "What have you been thinking about lately?"

Corey remained silent for nearly a minute.

Eventually, she spoke.

> "...I died."

Another pause.

> "...I remember it."

She touched the side of her neck.

> "...I remember being shot."

Silence.

> "...So..."

> "...Why am I here?"

---

**Psychologist**

> "Can you explain what you mean?"

Corey nodded faintly.

> "...There's another me."

> "...She's the one that belongs here."

A long silence followed.

> "...I'm living in her world."

She looked toward the window.

> "...With parents that aren't mine."

Another pause.

> "...A sister who isn't really my sister."

> "...Friends I never met."

Her voice became quieter.

> "...And sometimes..."

> "...I see my mom."

---

**Psychologist**

> "Lumi?"

Corey nodded.

> "...She's a ghost."

> "...She smiles at me."

Another pause.

> "...Sometimes I almost call her 'Mom.'"

Corey looked down.

> "...Then I remember..."

> "...She's not my mom."

---

# **CLINICAL OBSERVATION**

Throughout the interview, Corey demonstrated no signs of hallucinations beyond previously documented interactions with Lumi's spiritual manifestation.

Psychological Services believe the distress originates from survivor's guilt and a persistent identity conflict.

Corey does not question whether she exists physically.

She questions whether she has the **right** to exist.
