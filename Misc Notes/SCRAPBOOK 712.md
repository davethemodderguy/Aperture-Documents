## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / RECOVERY OBSERVATION LOG**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — FIRST MEAL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Age:** Approximately 5 years old  
**Status:** **CRITICAL / FIRST POSITIVE NUTRITIONAL RESPONSE**

---

# **THE SOUP**

Corin made soup.

Chicken.

Potatoes.

A little salt.

Nothing complicated.

She made it soft enough that Alice wouldn't have to chew much.

She let it cool.

Then she brought the bowl to Alice.

Alice looked at it.

She didn't recognize it.

> **"What's that?"**

Corin answered:

> **"Chicken potato soup."**

Alice stared at the bowl.

> **"Is it good?"**

Corin smiled.

> **"I think so."**

Alice hesitated.

Then she took a spoonful.

---

# **SHE ATE**

Alice swallowed.

Nothing happened.

No immediate rejection.

No pushing the bowl away.

No apology.

She simply took another spoonful.

Then another.

Corin watched silently.

Alice continued.

Slowly.

Carefully.

One spoonful at a time.

For the first time in approximately a year—

**Alice ate something substantial.**

---

# **CORIN**

Corin didn't celebrate.

She didn't cheer.

She didn't make a big deal out of it.

She knew Alice might stop eating if she felt everyone was watching her.

So Corin simply sat there.

> **"Good?"**

Alice nodded.

> **"Good."**

Another spoonful.

> **"Warm."**

> **"Yeah."**

Another.

> **"Alice likes."**

Corin's eyes began watering.

She looked down at the floor.

> **"I'm glad."**

---

# **THE SAFEHOUSE**

The news spread quietly.

Belle heard first.

Then Lumi.

Then Orphie.

Neko.

Bao.

Frost.

Everyone knew.

Nobody rushed into the room.

Nobody wanted to overwhelm Alice.

They simply waited.

For once, there was something good to wait for.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle was crying.

She tried not to.

She failed.

She whispered:

> **"She ate."**

Lumi nodded.

> **"She ate."**

Neither said anything else.

---

# **MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

The meal does not immediately reverse Alice's condition.

Her nutritional deficiency remains severe.

Her organs remain compromised.

Her regeneration remains dangerously resource-limited.

Her immune system remains fragile.

A single meal cannot repair a year of deprivation.

However, this is still medically significant.

Alice has demonstrated:

- Ability to voluntarily consume food
    
- Ability to swallow safely
    
- Positive response to warm food
    
- No immediate rejection
    
- No immediate gastrointestinal crisis
    

Medical personnel have therefore classified the event as a:

> **Positive nutritional response.**

Further intake must be carefully monitored.

---

# **THE CARROT**

After finishing some of the soup, Alice noticed the carrot Neko had left on the scooter.

She picked it up.

She stared at it.

> **"Can I eat this?"**

Corin smiled.

> **"Of course."**

Alice took a bite.

She chewed slowly.

Then paused.

Her expression changed.

Not recognition.

Not exactly.

Something smaller.

A faint familiarity.

> **"...I think I like this."**

Corin's smile broke.

> **"Yeah."**

---

# **ALICE**

Alice didn't finish the entire bowl.

Nobody expected her to.

She ate what she could.

When she became tired, she stopped.

Corin removed the bowl.

Alice looked embarrassed.

> **"Sorry."**

Corin immediately answered:

> **"You don't have to finish everything."**

Alice looked confused.

> **"But waste."**

> **"No."**

Corin shook her head.

> **"You ate."**

She smiled.

> **"That's enough."**

Alice looked down at the empty portion of the bowl.

Then quietly smiled.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

It wasn't a feast.

It wasn't a miracle.

Alice didn't suddenly become healthy.

She didn't regain her strength.

Her regeneration didn't suddenly become unlimited.

Her memories didn't return.

Her injuries didn't disappear.

But she ate.

After a year of barely giving her body anything to work with, Alice finally gave it something.

Chicken.

Potatoes.

Warm broth.

Enough to begin.

Corin stayed beside her after the meal.

Alice leaned against her.

For the first time in a very long time, her body had received something other than snow, grass, flowers, and whatever else she could find.

It was a small meal.

But to everyone in SAFEHOUSE—

it felt enormous.
