# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **Z-335 PHYSIOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

# **THERMAL BLANKET FORM**

**Classification:** Allied Entity Research  
**Subject:** Z-335 (Marts)

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although Marts are best known for providing localized cooling due to their extremely low internal temperature, C.O.R.E. researchers have documented an unexpected complementary ability.

A Mart can voluntarily flatten and spread its body into a thin, flexible sheet resembling a blanket.

This behavior has become informally known as:

> **Blanket Form**

Contrary to expectations, Blanket Form does **not** feel cold.

Instead, it provides the opposite effect.

It becomes pleasantly warm.

---

# **THERMAL REGULATION**

Researchers initially assumed the Mart was somehow increasing its internal temperature.

Testing indicates this is **not** the case.

The Mart's core temperature remains extremely low.

Instead, the anomalous containment field surrounding its body acts as an insulating layer, regulating heat transfer between the Mart and the person beneath it.

The result is a comfortable, gentle warmth.

No conventional explanation has yet been found.

---

# **COMMON USES**

Blanket Form is most commonly observed when someone is:

- Feeling cold.
    
- Resting.
    
- Reading.
    
- Watching a movie.
    
- Sleeping.
    
- Recovering from exhaustion.
    

The Marts appear to instinctively recognize when warmth is more comforting than cooling.

---

# **BEHAVIOR**

A Mart entering Blanket Form will politely ask permission whenever possible.

Typical phrases include:

> "Blanket?"

> "Warm?"

> "Help?"

If permission is given, the Mart gently settles over the person's lap, shoulders, or blanket.

The transformation is completely painless.

The Mart remains awake throughout the process.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATIONS**

Blanket Form has become particularly common inside **Project SAFEHOUSE**.

During movie nights, several Marts have been observed covering the residents like small living blankets.

The Marts appear to enjoy the activity just as much as the residents.

When the movie ends, they simply return to their normal shape and happily announce:

> "Finished!"

---

# **COREY'S EXPERIENCE**

Following her recovery from illness, Corey remarked that one Mart had changed from Cooling Pad Form to Blanket Form after her fever subsided.

She described the transition as:

> "...Like someone knew exactly when I stopped needing ice and started needing a warm blanket."

Medical staff confirmed no one had instructed the Mart to do so.

---

# **STAR'S COMMENT**

Star frequently allows Marts to use Blanket Form while she reads.

She has described them as:

> "...The softest weighted blankets imaginable."

She also noted that the Marts occasionally become so comfortable that they quietly fall dormant with her.

---

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

Lumi admitted she never intentionally designed this ability.

> "...I made little water blobs."

She watched a Mart happily covering someone with Blanket Form.

> "...They decided blankets were a good idea."

She smiled.

> "...I agree."

---

# **DIALYN'S COMMENT**

Dialyn returned to her office one evening to find a Mart stretched across the back of her chair.

It immediately asked:

> "Blanket?"

She laughed.

> "...I'm still working."

The Mart replied:

> "...Later."

It patiently waited until she finished.

---

# **RESEARCH NOTES**

Blanket Form appears to be another example of Z-335 instinctively adapting to provide comfort.

Researchers have now documented several supportive forms:

- **Standard Form** – Everyday assistance and companionship.
    
- **Cooling Pad Form** – Gentle cooling for fever or overheating.
    
- **Blanket Form** – Comfortable warmth for cold or tired individuals.
    
- **Boulder Mode** – Protective, defensive morphology used during security incidents.
    

Despite the dramatic differences, each form serves the same underlying purpose:

> **Helping someone.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Late one evening, Star fell asleep while reading in the SAFEHOUSE living room.

The room had grown slightly chilly.

A nearby Mart noticed.

It quietly asked no one in particular:

> "...Cold?"

Another Mart nodded.

The first Mart slowly spread itself across Star like a small blanket.

A second covered her feet.

Neither said another word.

When Star woke the next morning, both Marts were still there.

She smiled, gently patted their tiny top hats, and whispered:

> "...Thank you."

Two sleepy voices replied almost simultaneously:

> **"...Happy."**

**End of Physiological Observation**
