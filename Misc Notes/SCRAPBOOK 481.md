# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **RESTRICTED ETHER / COGNITIVE OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — ETHER SATURATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Architect-Sealed  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Condition:** **Progressive Cognitive Distortion**  
**Primary Cause:** High Ether concentration within reanimated body  
**Status:** **ONGOING**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's unusually high concentration of Ether is beginning to affect more than her physical body.

It has started interfering with her **memory, perception, and interpretation of reality**.

The effect is subtle at first.

A memory becomes slightly different.

A familiar room looks unfamiliar.

A person's voice sounds wrong.

A moment from the past becomes difficult to distinguish from something that happened yesterday.

Over time, these discrepancies have become more frequent.

---

# **MEMORY DISTORTION**

Corin's memories are no longer consistently reliable.

She remembers events that genuinely happened, but certain details are being altered.

Colors are the most obvious example.

Her inability to perceive color has already caused her memories of color to deteriorate.

Ether saturation appears to be accelerating the process.

Corin may remember:

> **"Mom's dress was blue."**

Then later:

> **"No... it was gray."**

Then eventually:

> **"Did she even wear a dress?"**

The original memory has not necessarily been erased.

It has become increasingly difficult for Corin to determine which version is correct.

---

# **PERCEPTION DISTORTION**

The effect is not limited to memories.

Corin's present perception occasionally becomes unreliable.

Objects may appear subtly different from how she remembers them.

Familiar rooms can feel larger or smaller.

Distances can seem incorrect.

A familiar voice may briefly sound distant.

The forest outside her window can sometimes appear completely unfamiliar despite her knowing exactly where she is.

Corin has described these episodes as:

> **"The world feels like it got replaced while I wasn't looking."**

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

The treehouse was once one of the most familiar places in Corin's life.

Recently, she entered the kitchen and stopped.

She looked around.

> **Corin:**  
> "...Was this always here?"

Orphie looked at her.

> **"Yeah."**

Corin stared at the doorway.

> **"...I don't remember it looking like that."**

> **"Nothing changed."**

Corin touched the wall.

> **"...Then why does it feel wrong?"**

---

# **IDENTITY CONFUSION**

The most concerning observations involve Corin's memories of herself.

She occasionally looks at old objects and struggles to remember whether they belonged to her.

She has also begun questioning memories involving her family.

Not because she believes they never happened.

Because the memories increasingly feel like something she **watched happen to someone else**.

One evening, she looked at Orphie.

> **Corin:**  
> "...Were you always my sister?"

Orphie froze.

Corin immediately looked frightened.

> **"I know you are."**

> **"I know that."**

> **"It just..."**

She struggled to find the words.

> **"...doesn't feel like I remember it."**

Orphie stayed beside her.

---

# **ETHER SATURATION**

Curious Light believes the phenomenon may be caused by the concentration of Ether necessary to maintain Corin's existence.

The Ether is not simply sustaining her body.

It is interacting with the remnants of her nervous system and consciousness.

As the concentration increases, the boundary between:

- Memory
    
- Perception
    
- Sensory input
    
- Ether information
    

appears to be becoming less distinct.

His current hypothesis:

> **"Corin's brain is attempting to process a substance that does not follow biological rules."**

> **"Eventually, the distinction between what she remembers and what the Ether tells her may become unstable."**

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light is deeply concerned.

She can perceive the Ether throughout Corin's body with extraordinary precision.

She can see where it interacts with Corin's consciousness.

But she cannot simply remove it.

The Ether is now part of what keeps Corin coherent.

Reducing it too aggressively could cause catastrophic destabilization.

Removing it entirely could mean removing **Corin herself**.

Guiding Light's conclusion remains unchanged:

> **"I cannot simply fix this."**

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

Orphie has begun helping Corin distinguish memory from present reality.

She does not argue when Corin remembers something incorrectly.

Instead, she provides reassurance.

> **Corin:**  
> "Did we really go stargazing?"

> **Orphie:**  
> "Yeah."

> **"You and Corin?"**

Orphie smiled sadly.

> **"You and me."**

Corin paused.

> **"...Right."**

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

Orphie has encouraged Corin to keep a small notebook beside her bed.

Not as a medical record.

As an anchor.

Important memories are written down.

Names.

Places.

People.

Small events.

Things Corin considers important.

The purpose is not to tell Corin what she is allowed to remember.

It is simply something she can return to when her own memories become uncertain.

One page contains:

> **MY NAME IS CORIN.**
> 
> **ORPHIE IS MY SISTER.**
> 
> **THIS IS OUR TREEHOUSE.**
> 
> **I LIKE SUNFLOWERS.**
> 
> **MOM IS LUMI.**
> 
> **I AM STILL HERE.**

The last line was written by Corin herself.

---

# **THE SUNFLOWER MEMORY**

One afternoon, Corin stared at the sunflower that Guiding Light had blessed for her.

She touched one of its petals.

> **Corin:**  
> "...Was this always here?"

Orphie answered:

> **"No."**

Corin looked relieved.

Then confused.

> **"...I thought it was."**

Orphie sat beside her.

> **"That's okay."**

Corin stared at the flower.

> **"I don't know what's happening to me."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's greatest fear is no longer simply that her body will fail.

She is beginning to fear that **her mind will become unreliable before her body ever does.**

The colors are already gone.

The memories of those colors are fading.

Now other memories are beginning to shift.

Places.

People.

Events.

Even pieces of her own identity.

The Ether keeping Corin alive may eventually become the same thing threatening her ability to understand the life it is preserving.

For now, Orphie remains her strongest anchor.

Guiding Light watches every change.

Curious Light continues studying the phenomenon without interfering.

And Corin keeps writing things down.

Because there is one thing she refuses to let the Ether take from her without a fight:

**the knowledge that she is Corin.**
