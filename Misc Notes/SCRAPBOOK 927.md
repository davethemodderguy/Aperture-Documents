## 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

At approximately [REDACTED], Neri was subjected to sustained verbal harassment by another SAFEHOUSE individual.

The initial bullying was reportedly ordinary enough to be handled through established SAFEHOUSE disciplinary procedures.

The situation changed when the individual began referencing Neri's childhood.

Specifically, the individual repeated insults and phrases that had historically been used against Neri by people she once considered friends and family.

The remarks reportedly included variations of the same language Neri had previously been subjected to during her childhood:

- That she was useless.
    
- That nobody would bother keeping her around.
    
- That she was an expendable asset.
    
- That she caused problems for everyone around her.
    
- That people leaving her was somehow her fault.
    
- That nobody actually wanted her.
    

The individual had no apparent understanding of the significance of these statements.

That did not make the effect any less severe.

Neri initially attempted to remain silent.

She then became visibly distressed.

Witnesses observed her becoming increasingly disoriented, covering her ears, trembling, and struggling to respond.

When someone attempted to intervene, Neri reportedly said:

> **"Stop."**

She repeated it several times.

Eventually, Neri became completely overwhelmed.

She lost consciousness and fell to the floor.

Medical personnel were immediately summoned.

### Medical determination

Neri's cardiovascular system was functioning.

Her blood parameters were within her current monitored range.

No evidence of spontaneous blood degradation reaching critical levels was found.

No evidence of oxygen deprivation consistent with her usual anemic episodes was found.

**This was not anemia.**

Neri had experienced an acute psychological collapse resulting in loss of consciousness.

# 2. WHAT MADE THE INCIDENT DIFFERENT

Neri has survived physical violence before.

She has survived experimentation.

She has survived prolonged neglect.

She has survived captivity.

She has survived losing people she loved.

What she has considerably more difficulty surviving is being told that the people she loves are going to leave her again.

The bully unknowingly reconstructed pieces of Neri's childhood using the same language that had once been used against her.

To Neri, these were not simply insults.

They were familiar statements coming from a familiar world.

A world in which children were assets.

A world in which useless things were discarded.

A world in which people disappeared and nobody cared.

A world in which Neri believed that every person she failed to save was another failure on her part.

The distinction between **past** and **present** appears to have temporarily collapsed.

She was not merely hearing:

> "You're useless."

She was hearing every previous instance of someone telling her the same thing.
