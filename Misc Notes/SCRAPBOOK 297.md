# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – COMFORT & BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION**

### **DESIGNATION: M.A.R.T. BLOBS – COLLECTIVE CUSHIONING BEHAVIOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Researchers have documented an unusual social behavior exhibited by large groups of Marts.

When a significant number of Marts become particularly fond of an individual, they may voluntarily gather together and form a soft, supportive surface for that person to sit or lie on.

The behavior is entirely voluntary.

No Mart has ever been observed being instructed or coerced into doing so.

---

# **FORMATION**

Using their shared Ether-linked hivemind, dozens—or occasionally hundreds—of Marts coordinate to create a stable, cushion-like mass.

Common formations include:

- Chairs.
    
- Beanbags.
    
- Reclining cushions.
    
- Mattresses.
    
- Pillows.
    

Despite consisting entirely of individual Marts, the structure remains surprisingly supportive.

The containment fields of each Mart prevent the person from becoming wet.

---

# **WHO RECEIVES THIS TREATMENT**

This behavior has only been observed toward individuals the colony collectively trusts.

Known recipients include:

- Lumi.
    
- Corin.
    
- Corey.
    
- Mart.
    
- Frost.
    

Researchers believe this is one of the highest displays of affection a Mart colony can offer.

---

# **SIDE EFFECTS**

Although perfectly safe, resting on a Mart cushion may produce temporary discomfort.

Commonly reported symptoms include:

- Mild motion sickness.
    
- A sensation similar to floating on gentle waves.
    
- Occasional dizziness after standing.
    
- Difficulty determining whether the surface is "solid" or "liquid."
    

These symptoms generally disappear within a few minutes.

Personnel prone to motion sickness are advised to use conventional furniture instead.

---

# **MART PERSPECTIVE**

When asked why they do this, the colony consistently responds with variations of:

> "Yes!"

> "Mart!"

Or:

> "Hello!"

Research has concluded that the behavior is simply how Marts express affection.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Mart colony continues to invent new ways of helping the people around them.

Whether cooling computers, comforting the lonely, or transforming themselves into surprisingly comfortable furniture, their actions consistently reflect the same simple goal:

To make someone else's day a little better.

Personnel are reminded that while Mart furniture is safe to use, extended periods may cause mild nausea due to the subtle sensation of resting on a living, water-based surface.

The Marts apologize for this.

Not with words.

But by bouncing happily when you feel better.
