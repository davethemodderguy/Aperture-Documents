# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **SECURITY EQUIPMENT REGISTER**

### **SUBJECT: DIALYN – EMERGENCY DEFENSIVE EQUIPMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Security Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although Dialyn's primary duties involve administration, public relations, and customer support, KCA policy permits designated personnel to maintain secured emergency defensive equipment at their workstation.

Dialyn is one of the authorized personnel.

The equipment remains secured beneath her desk and is intended **only** for situations in which evacuation is impossible or immediate defense of personnel is required.

---

# **ASSIGNED EQUIPMENT**

**Primary Weapon**

- MP7 Personal Defense Weapon (PDW)
    

The compact platform was selected due to its small size and suitability for indoor defensive use.

---

**Secondary Weapon**

- Compact sawed-off shotgun
    

The shotgun is stored with its **action open and chamber empty**, in accordance with KCA safety procedures.

Ammunition is stored separately in the secured mounting system.

The configuration is intended to reduce the risk of an accidental discharge while still allowing rapid deployment during an emergency.

---

# **WORKSTATION STORAGE**

Both firearms are mounted beneath Dialyn's reinforced desk inside a concealed locking bracket.

Access requires:

- Dialyn's credentials.
    
- Security authorization.
    
- Emergency release procedures.
    

The storage compartment is inspected during routine security audits.

No irregularities have been reported.

---

# **RATIONALE**

Customer Support is one of the few departments that cannot always evacuate immediately during an incident.

Personnel answering emergency calls may be responsible for:

- Coordinating evacuations.
    
- Relaying information to responders.
    
- Maintaining communication with civilians.
    
- Assisting internal security.
    

For this reason, emergency defensive equipment is issued to certain trained employees assigned to critical communication roles.
