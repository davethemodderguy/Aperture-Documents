# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **MISSING PERSON OBSERVATION LOG**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – AVOIDANCE BEHAVIOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the argument within Project SAFEHOUSE, Corin Lockwood's behavior changed significantly.

She did not return to SAFEHOUSE.

She stopped attending routine gatherings.

She no longer visited Accounting except when absolutely necessary.

Most notably—

She began actively avoiding the people closest to her.

---

# **OBSERVED BEHAVIOR**

Security footage from multiple areas of Heaven's Blacksite confirms a consistent pattern.

If Corin notices Norma...

She turns around and leaves.

If she notices Arcturus approaching...

She quietly changes direction before he reaches her.

Even when Lumi appears beside her, Corin lowers her head and walks away without speaking.

The behavior is deliberate.

It is not hostility.

It is avoidance.

---

# **WHEN FOUND**

Personnel attempting to approach Corin report similar outcomes.

If called by name, Corin may pause briefly.

She does not respond.

If someone begins walking toward her...

She walks away.

If they continue following...

She begins running.

She has repeatedly escaped pursuit by using her knowledge of the Blacksite's maintenance corridors and service passages.

No pursuit has been authorized beyond confirming that she remains physically safe.

Medical Services believe chasing Corin would likely intensify her distress.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services believe Corin's withdrawal is driven by shame rather than anger.

She has repeatedly demonstrated a tendency to isolate herself when convinced she has hurt the people she loves.

Rather than expecting rejection...

Corin rejects herself first.

In doing so, she attempts to spare others from what she believes is the burden of her presence.

---

# **NORMA**

Norma searched for Corin repeatedly during the first week.

Each attempt ended the same way.

Corin would see her.

Freeze for a moment.

Then run.

Norma never chased her.

Each time, she simply watched her little sister disappear around another corner.

The final recorded attempt ended with Norma quietly saying to the empty hallway,

> "...I'm not trying to make you come back."

> "...I just want you to know I'm still here."

---

# **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus encountered Corin by chance near the eastern transit corridor.

For a brief second, their eyes met.

Corin immediately turned and fled.

Arcturus took one step after her.

Then stopped.

He quietly sighed.

> "...She's scared."

He turned around and left.

---

# **LUMI**

For the first time since returning to her children, Lumi found herself unable to comfort Corin.

Whenever she appeared, Corin simply walked away.

One evening, Lumi remained standing alone in an empty corridor after Corin disappeared from view.

She whispered to herself,

> "...I'm not giving up on you."

No answer came.

---

# **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE**

Corin's place at SAFEHOUSE remains unchanged.

Her belongings have not been moved.

Her room has not been reassigned.

The residents continue treating it as though she might return at any time.

Belle still leaves a folded blanket on Corin's bed.

Bao continues replacing the lemons in the kitchen.

Mart occasionally glances toward the hallway at dinner.

Taiga still sleeps on the rug.

No one says that Corin has left.

Only that...

She isn't home right now.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin's location within the Blacksite is intermittently confirmed through security systems.

She is not considered physically missing.

However, Medical Services classify her as emotionally unreachable at this time.

Staff have been instructed not to corner or pursue her.

Instead, if Corin is encountered, personnel are advised to calmly remind her of one thing before allowing her to leave.

> "You'll still have a place here whenever you're ready."

**End of Missing Person Observation Log**
