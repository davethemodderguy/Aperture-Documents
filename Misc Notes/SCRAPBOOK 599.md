### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY UPDATE**

# **CORIN — DUAL CARDIAC SYSTEM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** Stable / Post-Recovery  
**New Discovery:** **Two Hearts**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. medical examinations have confirmed that Corin possesses **two hearts**.

Both are fully developed, functional organs.

Neither appears to be a secondary or vestigial structure.

Corin genuinely has:

> **Two functioning hearts.**

---

# **CARDIAC ARRANGEMENT**

The two hearts operate as a coordinated circulatory system.

Their exact positioning differs from ordinary human anatomy, with Corin's anomalous physiology providing the additional space and connective structures necessary to support the second organ.

Both hearts beat independently.

However, they maintain an unusually precise rhythm with one another.

Under normal conditions, their heartbeats are nearly synchronized.

---

# **PURPOSE**

The exact evolutionary or anomalous purpose of the second heart remains unknown.

C.O.R.E. has identified several potential advantages:

- Increased blood circulation.
    
- Greater oxygen delivery.
    
- Improved physical endurance.
    
- Increased tolerance for extreme exertion.
    
- Additional cardiovascular redundancy.
    
- Better support for Corin's unusually powerful wings.
    

The second heart may also contribute to her improved physical performance following her recovery from mercury poisoning.

---

# **RELATION TO CORIN'S WINGS**

Corin's wings require considerable oxygen and blood flow during sustained flight.

The dual-heart system may provide the additional circulation necessary to support them.

This could partially explain why Corin has demonstrated stronger flight performance following her recovery.

Her body may simply have considerably more cardiovascular capacity than previously understood.

---

# **ANOMALOUS BLOOD**

The discovery is particularly significant because Corin's blood is already unusual.

Her blood is:

**White.**

Her veins are:

**Luminous.**

And now it has been confirmed that the circulatory system contains **two hearts**.

C.O.R.E. has therefore classified Corin's cardiovascular system as significantly anomalous.

---

# **MEDICAL INCIDENT CORRELATION**

The dual-heart system may also explain part of Corin's survival during the mercury poisoning.

Although both hearts were exposed to the same systemic toxin, her regenerative factor prevented permanent damage.

The presence of two hearts provided additional physiological redundancy during the prolonged poisoning.

However, it did **not** prevent the severe pain Corin experienced.

Her previous description remains:

> **"My heart and organs being constantly stabbed."**

Medical personnel have noted that this statement is now technically plural.
