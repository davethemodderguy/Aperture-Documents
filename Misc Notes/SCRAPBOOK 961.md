# SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL FOLLOW-UP

## SUBJECT: JANE DOE / NERI

### PURPOSE: POST-INCIDENT QUESTIONING

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE Cafeteria  
**Time:** Approximately 20 minutes after Neri's stabilization  
**Participants:** Jane Doe, Neri  
**Additional personnel:** None

**Recording Status:** Not recorded.

---

Jane waited until Bao had returned to her work.

Neri was back at her usual cafeteria table.

Cake in front of her.

Chemistry book open.

Both hands hidden inside the oversized sleeves of her coat.

From the outside, everything looked normal.

Jane knew better.

She pulled out the chair across from Neri and sat down.

Neri looked over her book.

> **Neri:** "What?"

Jane leaned forward.

> **Jane:** "We're talking."

Neri immediately became suspicious.

> **Neri:** "About what?"

> **Jane:** "You."

> **Neri:** "Why?"

> **Jane:** "Because you almost died."

Neri blinked.

> **Neri:** "Oh."

Jane stared at her.

> **Jane:** "That's all you have to say?"

> **Neri:** "What else?"

Jane slowly exhaled.

> **Jane:** "Okay. Let's start simple."

---

## 1. "HOW DO YOU KNOW IT'S HAPPENING?"

> **Jane:** "When one of these episodes starts, what do you feel?"

Neri thought for a moment.

> **Neri:** "Everything gets blurry."

> **Jane:** "Then?"

> **Neri:** "I can't breathe."

> **Jane:** "Then?"

> **Neri:** "I fell."

Jane nodded.

> **Jane:** "Anything before the blurry vision?"

> **Neri:** "Sometimes I feel weird."

> **Jane:** "Define weird."

Neri shrugged.

> **Neri:** "Tired."

> **Jane:** "Anything else?"

> **Neri:** "My heart hurts."

Jane's expression immediately changed.

> **Jane:** "Your heart hurts?"

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:** "Sometimes."

> **Jane:** "And you don't tell anyone?"

Neri looked confused.

> **Neri:** "Why?"

Jane stared at her for several seconds.

> **Jane:** "Because that's usually something people tell someone about."

Neri looked down at her cake.

> **Neri:** "It goes away."

Jane's voice became quieter.

> **Jane:** "Until it doesn't."

Neri didn't answer.

---

## 2. "HOW LONG HAS THIS BEEN HAPPENING?"

> **Jane:** "When was the first time?"

Neri paused.

> **Neri:** "I don't remember."

> **Jane:** "You don't remember?"

> **Neri:** "No."

> **Jane:** "Months?"

> **Neri:** "Maybe."

> **Jane:** "Years?"

Neri looked away.

> **Neri:** "I don't know."

Jane leaned back.

She was beginning to understand why the medical staff were so concerned.

Not because Neri was hiding information.

She genuinely didn't know.

---

## 3. "WHAT DO YOU DO WHEN IT HAPPENS?"

Jane expected something like _call Bao_.

Instead:

> **Neri:** "Wait."

Jane's eyebrows rose.

> **Jane:** "Wait for what?"

> **Neri:** "For it to stop."

Silence.

> **Jane:** "You just wait?"

> **Neri:** "Usually."

> **Jane:** "Alone?"

Neri nodded.

Jane stared at the child sitting across from her.

> **Jane:** "Neri."

> **Neri:** "Hm?"

> **Jane:** "That's not a treatment."

Neri looked genuinely puzzled.

> **Neri:** "I know."

> **Jane:** "Then why do you do it?"

Neri's fingers tightened inside her sleeves.

> **Neri:** "Because I'm supposed to."

Jane's expression hardened.

> **Jane:** "Who told you that?"

Neri didn't answer.

Jane immediately understood that the question had hit something deeper.

She softened her tone.

> **Jane:** "You don't have to tell me who."

Neri remained quiet.

> **Jane:** "But why do you think you're supposed to handle it yourself?"

Neri stared at the cake.

> **Neri:** "Because I'm not supposed to bother people."

Jane's eyes narrowed.

Not anger toward Neri.

Anger toward whoever had taught her that.

---

## 4. "WHAT HAPPENS IF YOU DON'T GET HELP?"

> **Jane:** "Do you know what happens if you don't get help?"

Neri shook her head.

> **Neri:** "I fall asleep."

Jane went completely still.

> **Jane:** "You don't fall asleep."

Neri looked at her.

> **Jane:** "You lose consciousness."

Neri shrugged.

> **Neri:** "Same thing."

> **Jane:** "No."

Jane's answer was immediate.

> **Jane:** "It really isn't."

Neri looked down again.

Jane paused before continuing.

> **Jane:** "Has anyone ever found you after you've passed out?"

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:** "Sometimes."

> **Jane:** "Who?"

> **Neri:** "People."

> **Jane:** "Neri."

> **Neri:** "I don't remember."

Jane rubbed her face.

> **Jane:** "You're making this very difficult."

> **Neri:** "Sorry."

> **Jane:** "No, don't apologize."

---

## 5. "DO YOU KNOW WHAT BAO'S BLOOD DOES?"

> **Jane:** "Do you understand why your mom gives you her blood?"

Neri shook her head.

> **Neri:** "It makes me feel better."

> **Jane:** "Do you know why?"

> **Neri:** "No."

> **Jane:** "Do you know that your blood is degrading?"

Neri shook her head again.

> **Neri:** "What's degrading?"

Jane stopped.

She suddenly remembered that Neri didn't even know what anemia was.

> **Jane:** "…Never mind."

Neri stared at her.

> **Neri:** "What?"

> **Jane:** "Nothing."

Jane leaned back.

She wasn't going to dump medical terminology onto a ten-year-old who was still trying to understand why she kept collapsing.

Instead, she asked:

> **Jane:** "When your mom gives you blood, does it hurt?"

> **Neri:** "No."

> **Jane:** "Does she always know?"

> **Neri:** "Usually."

> **Jane:** "And today?"

Neri looked at Jane.

> **Neri:** "You saw."

> **Jane:** "Yes."

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:** "Then you know."

---

## 6. THE QUESTION THAT STOPPED JANE

Jane was quiet for a moment.

Then:

> **Jane:** "Neri."

> **Neri:** "Hm?"

> **Jane:** "If Bao hadn't been there today…"

Neri waited.

Jane struggled to finish the sentence.

> **Jane:** "What would have happened?"

Neri's answer was remarkably calm.

> **Neri:** "I would've died."

Jane said nothing.

Neri went back to eating.

That somehow made it worse.

> **Jane:** "You're very calm about that."

> **Neri:** "I'm used to it."

Jane looked down at the table.

> **Jane:** "That's not something a ten-year-old should be used to."

Neri stopped eating.

For a few seconds, neither spoke.

---

## 7. JANE FINALLY ASKS THE IMPORTANT QUESTION

Jane reached across the table.

She didn't touch Neri.

She simply placed her hand on the table near hers.

> **Jane:** "Do you want help?"

Neri looked at her.

> **Neri:** "With what?"

> **Jane:** "With this."

Neri didn't answer.

> **Jane:** "You don't have to understand the medical stuff."

> **Jane:** "You don't have to know what anemia is."

> **Jane:** "You don't have to figure out what's happening to your body."

Jane paused.

> **Jane:** "You just have to tell someone when something feels wrong."

Neri stared at her.

> **Neri:** "What if they're busy?"

Jane answered immediately.

> **Jane:** "Then they can stop being busy."

Neri blinked.

> **Jane:** "What if you think it's not serious?"

> **Neri:** "Then?"

> **Jane:** "Tell someone anyway."

> **Neri:** "What if I bother them?"

Jane gave her a small smile.

> **Jane:** "Then you can bother me."

Neri looked surprised.

> **Neri:** "You?"

> **Jane:** "Me."

> **Neri:** "Even if you're eating?"

Jane looked at the half-finished meal sitting beside her.

> **Jane:** "Especially if I'm eating."

Neri smiled faintly.

---

## 8. JANE'S LAST QUESTION

Jane stood.

She looked down at Neri.

> **Jane:** "One last thing."

> **Neri:** "What?"

> **Jane:** "If it happens again, what do you do?"

Neri thought.

> **Neri:** "Tell someone."

> **Jane:** "Who?"

Neri looked toward the direction Bao had left.

> **Neri:** "Mom."

Jane nodded.

> **Jane:** "And?"

Neri looked at Jane.

> **Neri:** "You."

> **Jane:** "Good."

Neri picked up her fork.

> **Neri:** "Can I eat my cake now?"

Jane stared at her.

Then laughed.

> **Jane:** "Yeah."

She began walking away.

After three steps, she stopped.

> **Jane:** "And Neri?"

> **Neri:** "Hm?"

> **Jane:** "You're not bothering anyone."

Neri quietly nodded.

> **Neri:** "Okay."

Jane left.

Neri returned to her cake and chemistry book.

For the first time, however, she didn't simply assume that collapsing was something she had to endure alone.

---

## POST-INCIDENT NOTE

Jane Doe later submitted no formal intelligence report regarding the conversation.

Instead, she privately informed SAFEHOUSE personnel of one behavioral recommendation:

> **"If Neri says she feels weird, don't ask whether she's sure."**

> **"Just check on her."**

Jane reportedly added:

> **"Apparently we've got a kid who thinks nearly dying is an acceptable inconvenience."**

**END OF NOTE**
