# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CRITICAL MEDICAL INCIDENT REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: RECOVERY OF CORIN LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Executive & Medical Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

After an extended search conducted jointly by Heaven's Blacksite, the Krampus Compliance Authority, and the Merryweather Family, Corin Lockwood was located in critical condition.

She was found unconscious in an abandoned alleyway.

Her clothing was heavily damaged.

She had sustained numerous injuries consistent with prolonged exposure and physical trauma.

Most alarming of all...

Medical instruments detected almost no active Ether within her body.

---

# **INITIAL EXAMINATION**

Emergency Medical Services immediately transported Corin to Heaven's Blacksite.

Standard examinations produced familiar results.

No heartbeat.

No spontaneous respiration.

No measurable body heat.

Unlike previous examinations, however, Ether resonance scanners also returned nearly empty readings.

The Ether that had sustained Corin's altered physiology had been almost completely depleted.

Medical personnel recorded the observation:

> **"Ether reserves critically exhausted."**

Within minutes, further deterioration became apparent.

Skin discoloration increased.

Neurological activity became unstable.

Cellular preservation began to fail.

For the first time since her forced revival years earlier, Corin's body no longer appeared capable of sustaining itself.

The atmosphere inside the operating room became silent.

One physician quietly stated:

> "...We're losing her."

---

# **EMERGENCY CONSULTATION**

C.O.R.E. researchers reviewed every available record concerning Ether stabilization.

Among the archived studies was one unusual observation involving Belle Merryweather.

Belle's blood possessed a unique compatibility with refined Ether, allowing it to stabilize and integrate Ether in ways not observed in ordinary humans.

The mechanism remained poorly understood.

There was no guarantee it would help.

But there were no conventional alternatives.

---

# **PROCEDURE**

With Belle's informed consent, Medical Services prepared an emergency stabilization mixture consisting of:

- A carefully measured donation of Belle Merryweather's blood.
    
- **250 grams of highly purified Ether**, refined to medical standards.
    

The mixture was intended solely to restore Ether activity within Corin's body.

It was not designed to enhance her abilities.

Its purpose was survival.

The solution was administered under continuous monitoring while Medical Services observed for any signs of Ether rejection or destabilization.

The first several minutes passed without measurable change.

Then...

A faint Ether signature appeared.

Small.

Weak.

But unmistakable.

The stabilization process had begun.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle never left the operating room waiting area.

She sat quietly with dried blood still visible on the bandage covering her arm.

When informed that Corin's Ether signature had returned, Belle did not celebrate.

She simply covered her face and cried.

> "...Please wake up."

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi remained beside Corin throughout the procedure.

She gently held her daughter's cold hand.

As the monitors registered the first signs of renewed Ether activity, Lumi smiled through tears.

She whispered,

> "There you are."

---

# **MEDICAL STATUS**

Corin remained unconscious following the procedure.

Her Ether reserves were no longer empty, but they were critically low.

Medical Services warned that stabilization was only the beginning of treatment.

A prolonged period of intensive monitoring and rehabilitation would be required before any assessment of long-term recovery could be made.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin Lockwood survived the immediate medical crisis.

The Ether depletion that nearly ended her life has been halted through emergency intervention, and her condition has stabilized sufficiently for continued treatment.

Her prognosis remains guarded.

As one physician summarized in the final line of the report:

> **"Tonight, we did not restore the Corin we knew."**

> **"We gave her the chance to become that Corin again."**

**End of Critical Medical Incident Report**
