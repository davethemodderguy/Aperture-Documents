# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **EMERGENCY INCIDENT REPORT**

### **SUBJECT: MISSING PERSON – CORIN LOCKWOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – ACTIVE RESPONSE

---

## **TIME OF REPORT**

**07:14**

Head of Accounting **Corin Lockwood** failed to report for her scheduled financial review.

Initial assumptions were that she had become absorbed in paperwork or was delayed during transit.

At **07:41**, repeated attempts to contact her through standard communication channels received no response.

At **08:02**, Security Advisor Arcturus Lockwood personally confirmed that Corin had not arrived at KCA Headquarters.

A Level 6 Missing Person Protocol was immediately initiated.

---

## **LAST CONFIRMED SIGHTING**

The previous evening, Corin departed Heaven's Blacksite shortly before sunset.

As usual, she politely declined the offer to stay overnight.

Belle walked her to the entrance.

Their final exchange was brief.

> **Belle:** "Travel safely."

Corin smiled.

> "I always do."

She waved once before leaving.

No confirmed sightings have been recorded since.

---

## **KNOWN CIRCUMSTANCES**

There is currently **no evidence** indicating whether Corin:

- Became lost.
    
- Was involved in an accident.
    
- Left voluntarily.
    
- Was forcibly taken.
    

Given Corin's personal history of childhood abduction, Executive Command has classified the situation as a potential high-risk disappearance until evidence suggests otherwise.

---

## **PERSONNEL REACTIONS**

### **Norma Lockwood**

When informed that Corin could not be located, Norma immediately stood up from her desk.

She asked only one question.

> "...Have you found her?"

When the answer was no...

She became very quiet.

The mug of tea she'd been holding slipped from her hands and shattered on the floor.

For several seconds, she simply stared at the broken porcelain.

Then she whispered:

> "...Not again..."

Despite visibly shaking, she immediately volunteered to assist in the search.

---

### **Arcturus Lockwood**

Arcturus's expression did not change.

He calmly collected his revolver, lever-action rifle, and radio.

Only Belle noticed his hands trembling slightly as he loaded his equipment.

His first transmission over the KCA network was short.

> "This is Gunslinger."

A pause.

> "...We're bringing her home."

---

### **Belle**

Belle's ears lowered the moment she heard the report.

Knowing Corin's fear of being alone, her first thought was not about the investigation.

It was much simpler.

> "...She must be terrified."

Belle immediately offered to use her dream-walking ability if Corin could be located asleep or unconscious, while acknowledging that it would only be possible under the appropriate circumstances.

---

### **Director Lucious**

Director Lucious assumed overall command of the search.

His orders were clear.

> "Every lead is to be investigated."

> "Do not speculate."

> "Find her."

---

## **SEARCH OPERATION**

Joint KCA and Blacksite teams began coordinated search efforts.

Arcturus took responsibility for ground reconnaissance.

Norma coordinated communications and logistical support.

Belle remained on standby to provide psychological support and any assistance her abilities could offer if needed.

The five wandering traders, having learned of Corin's disappearance during a routine visit, quietly volunteered to help search nearby trade routes.

The eldest trader said only:

> "She welcomed us when we had nowhere else to go."

> "We'll help bring her home."

---

## **STATUS**

**Corin Lockwood remains missing.**

At the time of this report, no conclusions have been reached regarding the cause of her disappearance.

All available personnel have been instructed to treat the incident with the highest priority until Corin is safely located or further verified information becomes available.

**End of Active Incident Report**
