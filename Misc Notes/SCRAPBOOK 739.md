### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / ANOMALOUS INCIDENT RECORD**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — FINAL FADE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Incident Status:** **SUBJECT LOST / EXISTENTIAL VOIDING CONFIRMED**

---

# **THE FINAL MOMENTS**

Alice was sitting inside the Treehouse.

Her notebook was in her hands.

Her soft hat rested on her white hair.

She had become almost completely transparent.

The people around her had grown accustomed to seeing light pass through her.

But this was different.

Alice was barely visible.

Corin was sitting beside her.

She held Alice's hand.

It was still warm.

Still tangible.

For a few seconds, Alice looked completely normal.

She looked down at her notebook.

Then wrote one final sentence.

> **"I struggle to remember, no one loves me"**

Corin saw it.

Her expression immediately changed.

> "Alice..."

Alice looked up.

For a moment—

she smiled.

The same little smile she had used for years.

The smile she used when she was frightened.

The smile she used when she was hurting.

The smile she used because she thought everyone else needed her to be happy.

Then she began to fade.

---

# **THE FADE**

There was no explosion.

No flash.

No sound.

Alice simply became less present.

Her hand became transparent.

Then her arm.

Her ears.

Her hair.

Her face.

Corin tried to hold her tighter.

There was nothing she could do.

> "No."

Alice looked at her.

For the briefest moment, Corin could still see her eyes.

Then—

nothing.

Alice was gone.

---

# **WHAT REMAINED**

The room became completely silent.

Alice's hat fell onto the floor.

Her notebook followed.

Nothing else.

No body.

No trace.

No remaining biological material.

No detectable Ether signature.

No anomalous residue.

Just an empty space where Alice had been sitting.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin remained frozen.

She was still holding her hands in the position they had been holding Alice.

She slowly looked down.

Her fingers closed around nothing.

Then she broke down.

Not quietly.

Not gracefully.

She cried.

She had promised Alice she would stay.

She had promised that Alice would never be alone.

And now there was nobody left to stay beside.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle found Alice's notebook on the floor.

She picked it up.

She saw the final sentence.

Her ears lowered.

She hugged the notebook against her chest.

> "She was loved."

Nobody answered.

Belle repeated it.

> "She was loved."

---

# **STAR**

Star couldn't speak.

She stared at the empty space.

The little rhombus-shaped sticker she had given Alice was still attached to the edge of the notebook.

Star touched it.

Then began crying.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi held Corin.

Neither said anything.

There was nothing that could make the moment better.

Lumi simply stayed.

Because Alice had spent her life afraid of people leaving.

And now everyone was experiencing exactly what she had feared.

---

# **THE NOTEBOOK**

The journal was recovered intact.

Every page remains preserved.

Pages about Corin.

Pages about Orphie.

Pages about Belle.

Neko.

Miss Neko.

Lumi.

Star.

Nightshade.

Endmin.

Taiga.

Mart.

Bao.

Frost.

Astral.

And finally—

Alice herself.

The final page contains only one sentence.

> **"I struggle to remember, no one loves me"**

It is now considered the most important line in the entire journal.

Because it was wrong.

Completely wrong.

Alice simply didn't remember that it was wrong.

---

# **BLACKSITE RESPONSE**

There was no celebration.

No announcement.

No attempt to minimize the incident.

The Blacksite simply became quiet.

People who knew Alice cried.

People who had only seen her riding her scooter cried.

People who had once waved at her cried.

The cafeteria staff left her usual place untouched.

The engineer who had labeled her chair kept the sign.

Security continued checking the corridors out of habit.

Everyone expected to see a tiny white rabbit appear around the corner.

She never did.

---

# **CORIN'S FINAL WORDS**

Corin eventually picked up Alice's hat.

She held it against herself.

Through tears, she whispered:

> **"You were loved, Alice."**

Then again:

> **"You were loved."**

And again.

> **"You were loved."**

Because Alice had forgotten.

So someone had to remember for her.

---

# **FINAL RECORD**

**Alice Lockwood**

White rabbit.

Five years old.

Artist.

Friend.

Little sister.

Daughter in everything but blood.

A child who cared about everyone.

A child who never learned how to care about herself.

She wanted everyone else to be happy.

She wanted flowers to live.

She wanted her friends to remember her.

She wanted to remember them.

In the end, she couldn't remember enough.

And the last thing she believed before disappearing was the one thing that had never been true.

> **"No one loves me."**

The Blacksite will preserve the record indefinitely.

Not because Alice is still here.

But because she was.

And because everyone who knew her refuses to let her become forgotten.
