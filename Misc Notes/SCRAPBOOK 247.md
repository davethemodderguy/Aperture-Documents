# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **PERSONNEL PROFILE – ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: DIALYN – PERSONAL PREFERENCES**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although Dialyn rarely speaks about herself, coworkers have gradually compiled a small collection of harmless personal preferences.

The most universally recognized is surprisingly simple.

Dialyn likes sheep.

Very much.

---

# **DISCOVERY**

The preference was discovered accidentally.

During a routine office gift exchange, an employee gave Dialyn a small plush sheep after noticing it in a shop window.

The gift was intended as a lighthearted joke.

Dialyn's reaction immediately made it clear that it had been an excellent choice.

She smiled from ear to ear.

> "...It's adorable."

The plush sheep has remained on her desk ever since.

---

# **OFFICE DECORATION**

Over time, more sheep-themed items quietly appeared around Dialyn's workspace.

These include:

- A small plush sheep.
    
- A sheep-shaped pen holder.
    
- A mug decorated with cartoon sheep.
    
- A desk calendar featuring photographs of sheep.
    
- A tiny knitted sheep made by Bao.
    

None of the decorations are excessive.

They simply make her office feel a little warmer.

# **DIALYN'S EXPLANATION**

When finally asked why she likes sheep so much, Dialyn thought for a moment before answering.

> "...They're gentle."

She smiled softly.

> "...They don't need to be loud to make people feel calm."

She glanced at the plush sheep sitting on her desk.

> "...I think there's something nice about that."

No one asked any further questions.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Sheep-themed gifts have become a common choice for birthdays, holidays, and office celebrations involving Dialyn.

She accepts every one of them with the same genuine smile.

Visitors to her office have also noticed one unofficial rule.

If someone absentmindedly picks up the little plush sheep from her desk...

They should remember to put it back exactly where it was.

Not because Dialyn will become upset.

She won't.

It's simply where the little sheep belongs.

And somehow...

Everyone in the office understands that.
