# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE / MEMORIAL ARCHIVE**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — RESTORATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** **PRESENT / RESTORED**

---

# **RESTORATION**

Alice is back.

There was no dramatic event.

No flash of light.

No explosion.

No warning siren.

She simply became visible again.

One moment, the Treehouse was empty.

The next—

Alice was sitting on her bed.

Small.

Fragile.

Thin.

Her white hair slightly messy.

Her soft hat resting on her head.

She looked around the room.

Then she yawned.

---

# **THE JOURNAL**

The journal has also been restored.

Every page has returned to its previous condition.

Alice's handwriting is back.

Her drawings are back.

Every little mistake in her writing.

Every uneven line.

Every crude sketch that somehow captured exactly what she wanted to draw.

Even the pages that had been completely wiped clean have returned.

The red warnings remain.

The stickers remain.

Everything is as it was.

As though the erasure never happened.

---

# **ALICE'S RESPONSE**

The SAFEHOUSE expected Alice to be confused.

Or frightened.

Or traumatized.

Instead, Alice looked at everyone.

She tilted her head.

Then pointed at the journal.

She wrote:

> **"Why everyone crying?"**

Nobody could answer immediately.

Corin hugged her.

Carefully.

Very carefully.

Alice simply sat there.

She didn't seem particularly bothered.

---

# **THE DISAPPEARANCE**

Alice remembers almost nothing about disappearing.

Her memories surrounding the event are fragmented.

She remembers:

Being invisible.

Seeing people around her.

Flickering.

Then darkness.

Then waking up.

That's about it.

When Corin explained that everyone believed Alice had disappeared permanently, Alice looked confused.

She wrote:

> **"Alice was gone?"**

Corin nodded.

Alice thought for a moment.

Then wrote:

> **"But Alice here now."**

That was her entire response.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin struggled with this.

For Corin, Alice's disappearance had been devastating.

She had watched Alice vanish.

She had mourned her.

She had kept her hat.

She had cried over her blank journal.

She had spent nights wondering whether Alice even remembered her.

For Alice, it was apparently little more than a strange gap in her memory.

Corin eventually smiled.

> "Yeah."

> "You're here."

Alice nodded.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle immediately checked Alice from head to toe.

Alice remained her usual fragile self.

Still weak.

Still easily exhausted.

Still requiring careful supervision.

Still unable to tolerate much physical strain.

Belle hugged her.

Alice's ears flattened slightly.

She didn't complain.

Instead, she wrote:

> **"Belle still squish Alice."**

Belle burst into tears.

Then laughed.

> "Yeah."

> "I still do."

---

# **STAR**

Star showed Alice the restored journal.

Alice looked through it.

Page after page.

Names.

Drawings.

Descriptions.

Warnings.

Memories.

She paused at her own drawing.

Then pointed at the rhombus-shaped sticker.

Star smiled.

> "You remembered me."

Alice wrote:

> **"Alice try."**

---

# **NEKO**

Neko inspected Alice's condition.

Physically, there was no major change.

She was still extremely weak.

Still fragile.

Still prone to exhaustion.

Still dependent on others for much of her daily care.

The disappearance had not made her stronger.

It had not made her invulnerable.

Alice was still Alice.

Neko seemed relieved by that.

---

# **HER FIRST WALK**

Alice eventually got out of bed.

She stood.

Waited.

Then took approximately three steps.

She became tired.

She sat down.

Everyone immediately became concerned.

Alice looked at them.

She wrote:

> **"Alice okay."**

Then:

> **"Stop worry so much."**

Nobody stopped worrying.

---

# **THE JOURNAL — NEW ENTRY**

Alice turned to a blank page.

She wrote slowly.

Her handwriting was still slightly uneven.

> **"Alice went away."**
> 
> **"Everyone sad."**
> 
> **"Alice come back."**
> 
> **"Everyone happy."**
> 
> **"Alice don't understand."**
> 
> **"But Alice happy too."**

She drew a small bunny beneath it.

Then drew everyone around the bunny.

---

# **CORIN'S OBSERVATION**

Corin later admitted that the strangest part wasn't Alice returning.

It was how ordinary she seemed.

She still wanted soup.

She still liked sitting in warm places.

She still needed help getting around.

She still worried about everyone else.

She still cared far more about other people's wellbeing than her own.

The disappearance hadn't changed that.

If anything—

Alice seemed completely uninterested in what had happened to herself.

When asked whether she was scared, she simply wrote:

> **"Alice don't remember enough to be scared."**

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

Movie night has been rescheduled.

This time, there will be a movie.

There will be snacks.

There will be arguments over what to watch.

Belle will probably bring carrots.

Neko will probably complain about the scooter being left somewhere inconvenient.

Star will probably pick something ridiculous.

Nightshade will probably pretend not to care.

Corin will probably spend the entire movie checking whether Alice is still there.

And Alice—

will probably fall asleep halfway through it.

Nobody minds.

---

# **FINAL NOTE**

Alice disappeared.

Everyone thought they had lost her.

She returned.

The world did not become brighter because Alice suddenly became stronger.

She didn't.

She is still fragile.

Still weak.

Still easily tired.

Still the little bunny girl everyone had spent so long protecting.

And somehow, that's comforting.

Because Alice came back exactly as herself.

She doesn't seem haunted by what happened.

She doesn't seem angry.

She doesn't seem afraid.

She simply exists.

Quietly.

Beside the people she loves.

And when Corin asked her what she wanted now that she was back, Alice thought for a moment.

Then wrote:

> **"Soup."**

Corin laughed.

> "Of course."

For once—

Alice chose something for herself.
