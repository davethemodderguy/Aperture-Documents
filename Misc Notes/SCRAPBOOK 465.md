### **CELESTIAL ARCHIVE / SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATION**

# **THE BLESSED SUNFLOWER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Celestial Authority  
**Subjects:** Guiding Light / Corin / Orphie  
**Location:** Corin's Treehouse  
**Status:** **STABLE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

The situation between Orphie and Guiding Light has stabilized.

Orphie has gradually become comfortable being by herself.

She no longer depends on Guiding Light's constant presence.

She can sit alone.

Sleep alone.

Think alone.

Spend time in the treehouse without needing someone beside her.

This was not interpreted as isolation.

It was independence.

Guiding Light recognized the difference.

---

# **THE SUNFLOWER**

Guiding Light eventually blessed a sunflower for **Corin**.

The flower was ordinary in appearance.

Golden petals.

Green stem.

Nothing visually extraordinary.

The only indication of its blessing was the faint celestial glow that appeared around it when Guiding Light was nearby.

The blessing was deliberately gentle.

It was not intended to make the sunflower immortal or extraordinarily powerful.

It was simply meant to help it **live**.

---

# **THE BLESSING**

The blessed sunflower possesses several subtle properties.

- It remains healthy considerably longer than an ordinary sunflower.
    
- It requires less water.
    
- It is highly resistant to disease.
    
- Its petals recover from minor damage.
    
- It remains warm to the touch even during cold weather.
    
- Its growth is unusually stable.
    

Most importantly:

> **It does not wither simply because someone stops looking after it for a few days.**

Guiding Light considered that appropriate.

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

Corin stared at the flower for several seconds.

> **Corin:**  
> "...Is this for me?"

Guiding Light nodded.

> **"Yes."**

Corin carefully touched one of the petals.

> **"...It's beautiful."**

Guiding Light smiled.

> **"I remembered what you said."**

Corin immediately understood.

Her mother's old words.

The sunflower.

Hope.

She hugged the flowerpot.

> **"Thank you."**

---

# **ORPHIE'S REACTION**

Orphie was nearby when Corin received it.

She looked at the sunflower.

Then at Corin.

Then back at the sunflower.

She did not make a sarcastic remark.

She did not dismiss it.

She simply smiled faintly.

> **Orphie:**  
> "...It suits you."

Corin smiled.

> **"Told you sunflowers were nice."**

Orphie rolled her eyes.

> **"...Don't push it."**

Corin laughed.

---

# **ORPHIE'S INDEPENDENCE**

Guiding Light noticed something else.

Orphie did not remain nearby because she was afraid of being alone.

She did not need Guiding Light to reassure her every few minutes.

She did not need someone constantly watching over her.

She was capable of sitting on the porch by herself, wearing her white cat-eared beanie and the softly glowing blue hairclip, simply watching the forest.

Sometimes she talked.

Sometimes she drew in her sketchbook.

Sometimes she did absolutely nothing.

And that was okay.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S OBSERVATION**

Guiding Light watched Orphie from a distance.

She did not approach.

She did not leave a note.

She simply watched her quietly for a moment.

Then she smiled.

> **"She's okay."**

Not perfectly healed.

Not free from the past.

Not suddenly happy every day.

Just...

**okay.**

And for Guiding Light, that was enough.

---

# **PRIVATE NOTE — GUIDING LIGHT**

> **"I once thought keeping someone close was the same as protecting them."**
> 
> **"I know better now."**
> 
> **"Orphie doesn't need me standing beside her every moment."**
> 
> **"She knows where to find me."**
> 
> **"And now she knows she can also find herself."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The blessed sunflower belongs to Corin.

The hairclip belongs to Orphie.

Guiding Light remains nearby.

But none of them need to hold onto one another out of fear anymore.

Corin has her sunflower.

Orphie has herself.

And Guiding Light finally understands that sometimes the best way to care for someone...

is to give them enough room to stand on their own.

**End of Celestial Archive / Safehouse Observation**
