## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE / MEMORIAL ARCHIVE**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — THE HAT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — MEMORIAL ARTIFACT  
**Artifact:** Alice Lockwood's soft hat  
**Current Custodian:** Corin  
**Status:** **PRESERVED / NOT TO BE DISTURBED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Of everything Alice left behind—

the hat is the most cherished.

Not because it is valuable.

It isn't.

It is an ordinary, soft hat.

It has no remarkable material composition.

It contains no anomalous properties.

It isn't a powerful artifact.

It is simply **Alice's hat.**

And that is enough.

---

# **THE LAST THING SHE WORE**

The hat was on Alice's head when she disappeared.

Corin remembers it perfectly.

Alice was barely visible.

Her body had almost completely faded.

Her white hair was difficult to distinguish from the light.

But the hat remained visible.

Then Alice vanished.

The hat fell onto the floor.

That was the moment Corin realized she was truly gone.

---

# **PRESERVATION**

The hat has been carefully preserved.

It has not been washed.

It has not been repaired.

It has not been altered.

It has not been placed inside a sterile containment case.

Corin specifically requested that it remain exactly as it was.

> "Don't change it."

The request was approved.

The hat is kept in the Treehouse.

Not in a laboratory.

Not in the Archives.

Not inside a high-security vault.

**At home.**

---

# **WHY IT MATTERS**

The hat is different from Alice's journal.

The journal was erased.

Every word Alice wrote disappeared.

Every drawing disappeared.

Every mark she made disappeared.

The notebook survived physically—

but Alice's presence within it did not.

The hat is different.

It remains exactly as it was when she disappeared.

It is tangible.

It is visible.

It can be held.

It can be placed on a chair.

It can be hugged.

It is one of the last things everyone can point to and say:

> **"Alice wore this."**

---

# **CORIN**

Corin keeps the hat close.

Sometimes she holds it while sitting in Alice's old place.

Sometimes she places it beside herself.

Sometimes she falls asleep holding it.

She knows it isn't Alice.

She knows it cannot bring Alice back.

She doesn't pretend otherwise.

She simply cannot bear the thought of putting it away.

When asked why she keeps it so close, Corin answered:

> "Because she can't."

---

# **BELLE**

Belle visits the Treehouse sometimes.

She always looks at the hat.

Once, she gently touched it.

> "Can I hold it?"

Corin gave it to her.

Belle hugged it.

For a few seconds, she smiled.

> "It's still soft."

Then she started crying.

She gave it back carefully.

---

# **STAR**

Star refuses to call it an artifact.

> "It's Alice's hat."

That's all.

She once suggested putting a protective case around it.

Then reconsidered.

> "No."

> "Alice wouldn't want it behind glass."

The idea was abandoned.

---

# **NEKO**

Neko offered to make a protective storage container.

Corin declined.

He instead made a small environmental monitor that quietly checks temperature and humidity around the hat.

He did not tell Corin.

She noticed anyway.

She smiled.

> "Thanks."

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie occasionally sits beside Corin while she holds the hat.

She never asks to touch it.

She understands.

There are some things that don't need to be shared.

They simply need to be protected.

---

# **THE BLACKSITE**

Personnel have been instructed:

**Do not touch Alice's hat without permission.**

This isn't because the hat is dangerous.

It isn't.

It's because everyone understands what it means.

For many employees, the hat is the closest thing they have left to Alice.

Some people stop by the Treehouse simply to see it.

They don't say anything.

They just stand there for a moment.

Then leave.

---

# **THE EMPTY CHAIR**

The hat is sometimes placed on Alice's old chair.

It looks almost as though someone is sitting there.

For a moment, from the corner of the room, it is easy to imagine:

White hair.

Long ears.

Small hands.

A little girl quietly drawing.

Then you look again.

There's only the hat.

---

# **CORIN'S NOTE**

A handwritten note has been placed beside the hat.

It reads:

> **"Alice wore this."**
> 
> **"She sat here."**
> 
> **"She drew here."**
> 
> **"She laughed here."**
> 
> **"She cried here."**
> 
> **"She was here."**
> 
> **"That's enough."**

---

# **FINAL ARCHIVE STATEMENT**

Alice left almost nothing behind.

Her journal was erased.

Her drawings disappeared.

Her handwriting disappeared.

There was never a photograph.

There are no birthday pictures.

No recorded image of her smiling for the camera.

No physical record capable of capturing everything she was.

But there is one little hat.

And everyone knows whose it is.

It doesn't prove Alice existed.

Nobody needs proof.

It simply reminds everyone of someone they loved.

The hat is not kept because Alice might return.

It is kept because **Alice mattered.**

And until the day every person who remembers her is gone—

that little hat will remain exactly where it belongs.

**With her family.**

**End of CORE-SAFEHOUSE / MEMORIAL ARCHIVE**
