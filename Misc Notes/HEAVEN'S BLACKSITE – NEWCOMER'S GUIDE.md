# **WELCOME TO HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

Congratulations!

You've somehow managed to get assigned to one of Aperture Industries' most unusual facilities.

The Blacksite is home to researchers, security personnel, engineers... and a handful of very unique residents.

This guide exists to help you avoid awkward introductions, accidental injuries, and several entirely preventable incidents.

---

# **GENERAL RULES**

- Be respectful.
    
- Don't touch people's equipment.
    
- Ask before hugging someone.
    
- Don't assume someone's appearance reflects their capabilities.
    
- If Neko says, "I have an idea," locate Miss Neko immediately.
    

---

# **MEET THE RESIDENTS**

---

# 🐆 **TAIGA**

**Species:** Snow Leopard Thiren

Taiga is friendly, dependable, and enjoys spending time with everyone.

Despite being a snow leopard, she has never attempted to eat a coworker.

**Notes**

✔ Talk to her.

✔ She's very approachable.

❌ **Don't leave her alone for long periods.**

She enjoys company more than she'll admit.

---

# 🦉 **MART**

**Species:** Owl Thiren

Mart enjoys reading almost as much as he enjoys flying.

He's patient, intelligent, and surprisingly easygoing.

**Notes**

✔ Ask him about books.

✔ He's happy to chat when he's free.

❌ **Don't interrupt him while he's reading.**

He'll eventually answer.

Eventually.

❌ **Don't ask him to carry you while flying.**

He's capable of it.

He simply doesn't want to.

---

# 🐈 **ENDMIN MERRYWEATHER**

**Species:** Cat Thiren

Member of the Central Execution Department.

Calm.

Quiet.

Exceptionally skilled.

**Notes**

✔ Call her **Endmin.**

✔ She's approachable once she knows you.

❌ **Do NOT call her "Lindervene."**

She strongly prefers "Endmin."

❌ **Don't challenge her to a marksmanship competition.**

You will lose.

Fun Fact:

If Endmin panics, she has a habit of instinctively grooming herself like a cat.

Engineering has unsuccessfully tried to document it on camera.

---

# 🐇 **BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Species:** Rabbit Thiren

Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority.

Also:

Short.

Soft.

Carries a fifty-kilogram sword.

**Notes**

✔ She likes hugs (with people she trusts).

✔ She likes carrots.

✔ She likes snacks.

✔ She is surprisingly clingy around close friends.

❌ **Do NOT ask why she's so soft.**

No one has received a scientific answer.

❌ **Don't struggle if Belle is hugging you.**

She'll usually let go once she's finished.

❌ **ABSOLUTELY DO NOT call her "Zhao."**

Use "Belle" or "Director Belle."

❌ **Don't try to pick up her sword.**

It weighs approximately **50 kilograms.**

She'll probably offer.

Don't.

---

# 🦈 **KEITH**

**Species:** Shark Thiren

Executive Protection Officer.

Professional.

Disciplined.

Quiet.

**Notes**

✔ Listen to his instructions.

✔ If Keith says move...

Move.

❌ **Don't interfere while he's working.**

❌ **Don't touch his shark tail.**

He dislikes it.

And yes...

He can smack someone with it.

Yes...

It hurts.

---

# 🦈🔥 **CINDER**

**Species:** Shark Thiren

KCA Executive Secretary.

Former Central Computing Department researcher.

Current owner of the world's most dangerous briefcase.

**Notes**

✔ She enjoys reading.

✔ She's friendly outside work.

✔ She likes spending time with Belle and the Friendly Roommates.

❌ **Don't disarm her.**

❌ **Don't touch her briefcase.**

It belongs to the KCA.

❌ **Don't repeatedly ask about her firebending.**

She's heard every joke already.

❌ **Don't touch her tail without permission.**

She's usually okay with it...

...if you ask first.

❌ **ABSOLUTELY DO NOT challenge her to hand-to-hand combat.**

This handbook would prefer not to explain why.

---

# 🐈 **NEKO**

**Species:** Cat Thiren

Chief Engineer.

Inventor.

Professional problem solver.

Professional creator of new problems.

**Notes**

✔ Ask him about engineering.

✔ He enjoys explaining his work.

❌ **Don't encourage questionable ideas.**

He's already motivated enough.

Fun Fact:

Neko likes laser pointers.

Yes.

Really.

---

# 🐈 **MISS NEKO**

**Species:** Cat Thiren

Represents Neko's feminine side.

Calm.

Practical.

The reason Engineering still exists.

**Notes**

✔ If you need the sensible answer...

Ask Miss Neko.

❌ **If she says "No"...**

That's the end of the discussion.

Fun Fact:

Unlike Neko...

She has absolutely no interest in laser pointers.

---

# 🛡 **ALICE**

**Position:** Head of Security – Merryweather Manor

Alice is calm, dependable, and exceptionally competent.

You'll almost certainly feel safer when she's nearby.

**Notes**

✔ Listen to security instructions.

✔ You'll be fine having Alice around.

---

# 👑 **LUCY MERRYWEATHER**

**Position:** Warden of the Banlands

One of the highest-ranking officials you'll ever meet.

She doesn't expect special treatment.

**Notes**

✔ Treat her with the same respect you'd show anyone else.

✔ Be professional.

She'll appreciate that more than excessive formality.

---

# 🌟 **STAR**

Star has a remarkable ability to brighten the mood wherever she goes.

If she's around...

People generally end up smiling.

**Notes**

✔ She's incredibly friendly.

✔ Expect hugs.

✔ Expect optimism.

---

# 🌙 **NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade appears distant and reserved.

She speaks very little to people she doesn't know.

This does **not** mean she's unfriendly.

She simply takes time to trust others.

**Notes**

✔ Give her space.

✔ Let conversations happen naturally.

❌ **Don't deliberately provoke her.**

❌ **And whatever you do...**

**Don't call her a tsundere.**

Especially if she can hear you.

She'll deny it immediately.

Probably while blushing.

---

# **FINAL ADVICE**

The people living at Heaven's Blacksite are unusual.

Some are government officials.

Some are engineers.

Some are anomalies.

Some are all three.

Treat them with kindness, respect, and common sense.

You'll fit in just fine.

Welcome to the Blacksite.

**End of Handbook.**
