### **RESTRICTED MEDICAL / ETHER OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — THE WILL THAT REMAINS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Architect-Sealed  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Related Subjects:** Guiding Light / Curious Light  
**Status:** **STABLE**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin's continued existence is sustained by an unusual interaction between her **shattered will to live** and the Ether maintaining her body.

The psychological state that should, by all conventional understanding, represent profound vulnerability has produced one unusual physical consequence:

> **Corin's body refuses to permanently cease functioning through ordinary biological failure.**

Her condition does not make her invulnerable.

It does, however, make conventional mortality largely irrelevant to her current physiology.

---

# **THE ETHER RESPONSE**

Corin's Ether appears to interpret her continued existence as something that must be maintained.

Her body does not possess the biological systems required for ordinary life.

Yet the Ether compensates.

It maintains:

- Structural integrity.
    
- Consciousness.
    
- Motor function.
    
- Cellular stability.
    
- Resistance to decomposition.
    
- Continued physical animation.
    

When Corin's body sustains ordinary damage, the Ether attempts to preserve its existing state.

It is less like healing a living organism and more like **refusing to allow an already-dead structure to completely fall apart.**

---

# **AN UNCOMFORTABLE PARADOX**

Corin's relationship with living is complicated.

Her mind has questioned whether she should continue.

Her body, however, does not appear to recognize that question.

The Ether continues maintaining her regardless.

This creates a strange contradiction:

> **Her mind can doubt life.**
> 
> **Her body continues anyway.**

Guiding Light considers this both a blessing and a deeply unsettling consequence of Corin's condition.

---

# **IMPORTANT DISTINCTION**

This does **not** mean Corin cannot be harmed.

She can.

She can experience pain.

She can become exhausted.

Her body can be damaged.

Her Ether can become unstable.

Her psychological condition can deteriorate.

The Ether simply prevents ordinary biological death from functioning the way it would in a living person.

Corin is therefore not invincible.

She is simply **very difficult to permanently kill by conventional biological failure.**

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

When Curious Light explained the physical implications to her, Corin was quiet.

> **Corin:**  
> "...So my body just refuses to give up?"

Curious Light paused.

> **"Essentially."**

Corin looked down at her hands.

> **"...That's kind of funny."**

Curious Light raised an eyebrow.

> **"Funny?"**

> **Corin:**  
> "My body has more determination than I do."

Neither Architect laughed.

The statement was too sad to be funny.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S RESPONSE**

Guiding Light eventually spoke.

> **"Your body keeping you here doesn't mean you have to earn the right to stay."**

Corin looked at her.

> **"You don't owe the Ether anything."**

> **"And you don't owe me anything."**

> **"If you continue living, I want it to eventually be because you chose to."**

Corin remained silent.

Then quietly:

> **"...I'm trying."**

Guiding Light smiled.

> **"I know."**

---

# **A POSITIVE EFFECT — WITH A CAVEAT**

The Ether's refusal to let Corin's body simply fail has protected her from some of the ordinary consequences of her condition.

It gives her time.

Time to recover.

Time to think.

Time to build relationships.

Time to experience things she was never supposed to experience.

Time to discover whether she actually wants the life she has been given.

But Guiding Light refuses to treat this physical protection as a substitute for Corin's emotional wellbeing.

A body that refuses to die cannot, by itself, make a life worth living.

That part still belongs to Corin.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's shattered will has produced an unusual paradox.

Her mind can ask:

> **"Should I live?"**

Yet the Ether answers:

> **"You're still here."**

For now, that gives Corin something invaluable:

**time.**

Not an obligation.

Not a command.

Not a reason she is required to continue.

Just time.

And perhaps, eventually, enough time for Corin to discover her own answer.
