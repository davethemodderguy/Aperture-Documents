# **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE**

## **RESIDENT WELFARE LOG**

### **INCIDENT REPORT – EARLY MORNING NIGHTMARE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Resident Welfare

---

**Time:** 03:02

**Location:** SAFEHOUSE – Shared Bedroom

---

## **SUMMARY**

At approximately **03:02**, Belle Merryweather unexpectedly awoke from a nightmare.

This was notable because Belle normally wakes naturally at approximately **07:00**.

The disturbance woke no one else immediately.

---

## **EVENT LOG**

03:02

Belle abruptly sat upright in bed.

Her breathing was faster than normal.

For several moments, she remained silent, staring toward the floor.

She instinctively wrapped her arms around herself.

The bedroom remained quiet.

Only the soft hum of the SAFEHOUSE ventilation system could be heard.

---

Belle carefully climbed out of bed, making every effort not to wake Endmin, who was still asleep beside her.

She quietly walked into the central living area.

The room was dark.

Only the emergency lighting illuminated the hallway.

Belle sat on the sofa.

She pulled a blanket around herself.

She couldn't fall asleep again.

---

## **OBSERVATION**

Approximately twenty minutes later, Endmin awoke and noticed Belle was no longer beside her.

After a brief search of the SAFEHOUSE, she found Belle sitting quietly in the living room.

Endmin sat beside her.

Neither spoke for a while.

After several minutes, Endmin gently asked,

> "...Nightmare?"

Belle gave a small nod.

> "...Yeah."

---

Endmin placed an arm around Belle.

Belle leaned against her almost immediately.

The two remained there in silence.

No further questions were asked.

---

## **FOLLOW-UP**

Around 04:10, Belle appeared noticeably calmer.

Although she attempted to return to bed, she was unable to fall asleep again.

Instead, she spent the remainder of the early morning reading quietly until sunrise.
