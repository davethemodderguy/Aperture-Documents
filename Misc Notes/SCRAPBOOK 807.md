## **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

Following the discovery of SPAWN's interest in Aperture facilities, several unauthorized personnel have attempted to approach or infiltrate the SAFEHOUSE during nighttime hours.

None of the attempts resulted in a conventional firefight.

Instead, individual residents responded according to their own personalities and abilities.

Some used force.

Some used intimidation.

Some simply made the intruders sufficiently uncomfortable that they immediately abandoned the operation.

One resident apparently scared an entire infiltration team without ever directly confronting them.

C.O.R.E. has therefore concluded that the SAFEHOUSE possesses a surprisingly effective **resident-driven nighttime security system**.

It is, however, entirely informal.

# **INCIDENT 01 — ORPHIE**

**Time:** 01:37  
**Intruders:** 2  
**Outcome:** Immediate withdrawal

The two SPAWN operatives entered the rear garden after bypassing the exterior fence.

They expected the SAFEHOUSE to be asleep.

They were correct.

Mostly.

One operative noticed a figure sitting motionless on a bench.

The figure was **Orphie**.

She was facing away from them.

She did not move.

The operatives whispered to one another.

> **SPAWN-01:** "Is that a resident?"

> **SPAWN-02:** "I think so."

> **SPAWN-01:** "Then why isn't she moving?"

Orphie slowly turned her head.

Nothing else happened.

She simply looked at them.

According to recovered helmet audio, approximately eight seconds passed.

Then:

> **SPAWN-01:** "...Nope."

Both immediately left the property.

Orphie reportedly returned to sitting on the bench.

**C.O.R.E. Assessment:**  
No hostile action occurred.

The intimidation was entirely accidental.

# **INCIDENT 02 — NEKO**

**Time:** 02:11  
**Intruders:** 1  
**Outcome:** Pursuit / retreat

The intruder entered through a ground-floor window.

He successfully reached the hallway.

He then encountered **Neko**.

> **"You're not supposed to be here."**

The operative raised his weapon.

Neko immediately lunged.

The resulting confrontation lasted approximately nine seconds.

The operative fled through the same window he had entered through.

Neko followed him outside.

The operative continued running.

Neko continued following.

According to exterior footage, Neko stopped at the property boundary.

The operative did not.

# **INCIDENT 03 — MISS NEKO**

**Time:** 02:46  
**Intruders:** 3  
**Outcome:** Psychological deterrence

Three SPAWN personnel approached the SAFEHOUSE from the northern side.

They discovered **Miss Neko** standing beside the perimeter.

She was holding a cup.

She appeared completely relaxed.

The operatives attempted to conceal themselves.

Miss Neko looked directly toward their hiding location.

Then she said:

> **"You can come out now."**

No response.

She took a sip.

> **"I'm not going to ask twice."**

The three operatives immediately retreated.

Miss Neko did not pursue them.

Security later determined that the operatives had spent approximately seven minutes hiding in a drainage ditch before leaving the area.

# **INCIDENT 07 — FROST**

**Time:** 04:12  
**Intruders:** 2  
**Outcome:** Environmental deterrence

Two SPAWN personnel attempted to enter through the rear garden.

They initially reported normal environmental conditions.

Approximately thirty seconds later, their thermal equipment began reporting rapidly decreasing temperatures.

One operative reported:

> "It's getting cold."

The second replied:

> "How cold?"

The first checked his equipment.

> "...Why is the grass freezing?"

Frost was standing approximately twenty meters away.

She apparently hadn't noticed them initially.

Once she did, she raised one hand.

The temperature dropped further.

The operatives immediately retreated.

Frost watched them leave.

She then reportedly said:

> **"It's cold outside anyway."**

# **INCIDENT 08 — BAO**

**Time:** 04:37  
**Intruders:** 1  
**Outcome:** Complete removal from property

The final incident involved one SPAWN operative attempting to cross the backyard.

Bao noticed him.

Rather than confront him directly, Bao generated a dense cloud around the intruder.

Visibility dropped to nearly zero.

The operative became disoriented.

Several seconds later, a strong gust of wind pushed him backward.

Then another.

Then another.

The operative eventually found himself standing outside the SAFEHOUSE perimeter.

The cloud dissipated.

Bao remained in the garden.

The operative looked back toward the property.

Bao waved.

The operative left.

# **POST-INCIDENT ANALYSIS**

The incidents demonstrate a recurring pattern.

SPAWN personnel appear to approach the SAFEHOUSE expecting:

> **A sleeping residence.**

Instead, they encounter:

> **Residents who are awake.**

And every resident responds differently.

Orphie simply watches.

Neko chases.

Miss Neko intimidates.

Niko orders them out.

Belle makes the situation very clear.

Endmin apparently does something nobody understands.

Frost freezes the environment.

Bao physically removes the intruder with weather manipulation.

None of these residents were formally assigned to nighttime security.

They simply noticed someone was where they weren't supposed to be.

---

## **C.O.R.E. COMMENT**

The SAFEHOUSE is becoming increasingly difficult for SPAWN to infiltrate.

Not because of upgraded cameras.

Not because of additional guards.

Not because of automated defenses.

Because the residents have apparently developed an informal rule:

> **If someone sneaks into the SAFEHOUSE at night, wake up whoever is nearby and make them leave.**

The rule has proven remarkably effective.
