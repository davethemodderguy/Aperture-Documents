# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **KCA-LOG-052**

### **UNOFFICIAL USES OF DIRECTOR BELLE'S HYBRID GREATSWORD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Recreational Archive

---

## **DISCLAIMER**

This document is **not** an equipment manual.

It originated as a joke among KCA personnel after repeatedly observing Director Belle Merryweather using her sword for purposes that were decidedly not combat.

Despite Engineering's objections, every entry has been verified.

---

## **1. Sword**

**Intended Use:** ✔

Cuts things.

Cuts them extremely well.

---

## **2. Paperweight**

Belle has, on numerous occasions, placed the 50-kilogram sword on top of stacks of paperwork to stop them from blowing away.

Result:

Nothing moved.

Including the desk.

---

## **3. Hat Stand**

Belle once balanced her sun hat on the sword's crossguard while eating lunch.

Several researchers copied the idea.

It worked surprisingly well.

---

## **4. Firewood Axe**

While camping in Starlands, Belle embedded the sword into a log.

One downward swing later...

There were two logs.

And a very surprised Taiga.

---

## **5. Doorstop**

Only for unusually large blast doors.

Engineering strongly discourages this practice.

Belle insists:

> **"...It works."

---

## **6. Coat Rack**

Keith once returned to find Belle's jacket, scarf, and backpack hanging from the sword.

He quietly admitted it was...

"...efficient."

---

## **7. Bench**

Belle occasionally sits on the flat side of the blade while talking to others.

No one understands how that is comfortable.

Belle claims it is.

---

## **8. Walking Stick**

Only while carrying the sword upside down.

This has confused multiple new employees.

---

## **9. Measuring Stick**

Need to estimate a doorway?

Belle simply stands the sword next to it.

> **"...Yep."

> **"...Fits."

---

## **10. Hoverboard**

---

### **Incident Report**

Location:

Blacksite Hangar

Witnesses:

Everyone.

Belle placed the sword flat on the floor.

She stepped onto it.

The sword...

Rose several centimeters into the air.

Then quietly glided across the hangar.

Belle happily surfed past everyone.

> **"...Wheeeee!"

---

Silence.

Neko slowly lowered the tablet he was holding.

> **"...How."

Belle shrugged.

> **"...I don't know."

---

Miss Neko rubbed her forehead.

> **"...That isn't how swords work."

---

Star immediately wanted a turn.

Belle let her try.

Nothing happened.

---

Taiga tried.

Nothing.

---

Mart.

Nothing.

---

Keith carefully stepped onto the sword.

Still nothing.

---

Belle stepped back on.

The sword immediately floated again.

She waved as she drifted past.

> **"...Maybe it just likes me."

Engineering listed the phenomenon as:

> **"Unexplained."**

---

## **ENGRAVING**

Near the base of the blade, beneath several years of wear, the following text remains clearly visible.

**Belle**

Below it, in larger lettering:

**Property of Bunny Girl**

When asked why that engraving existed, Belle answered with complete sincerity.

> **"...Because it's my sword."

## **FINAL NOTE**

Although Director Belle's hybrid greatsword has proven useful for an astonishing variety of tasks, the Krampus Compliance Authority reminds personnel that it remains a specialized combat weapon.

The following uses are **officially approved**:

- Combat.
    
- Emergency breaching.
    
- Controlled wood splitting.
    

The following uses remain **officially unapproved**:

- Office paperweight.
    
- Coat rack.
    
- Hat stand.
    
- Hoverboard.
    

Belle has acknowledged these guidelines.

She has also continued doing all four.
