## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION**

# **THE WING BLANKET**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Internal Personnel  
**Subjects:** Corin / Belle Merryweather  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE — Central Living Area  
**Incident Type:** **Unplanned Shared Sleep**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Belle Merryweather became unusually sleepy during an afternoon at the SAFEHOUSE.

Rather than returning to her usual sleeping area, she approached Corin, who was sitting comfortably in the central living area.

Belle sat beside Corin.

Then, apparently deciding that moving any further was unnecessary, she simply leaned against her and fell asleep.

Corin initially looked surprised.

She then quietly adjusted her wings around Belle.

A few minutes later, Corin fell asleep as well.

---

# **THE WING BLANKET**

Corin's highly flexible wings folded around both of them.

The feathers overlapped until almost their entire bodies were hidden.

From outside the enclosure, only two things remained visible:

**Belle's ears.**

And:

**the top of Corin's head.**

The wings effectively formed a warm, feathered enclosure around them.

---

# **CORIN'S SLEEPING POSITION**

Corin remained seated.

Her wings were wrapped around Belle from behind, with the outer portions folded around both sides.

During sleep, Corin's arms naturally settled around Belle as well.

She was holding her tightly.

The grip was not restrictive or defensive.

It appeared to be an unconscious protective embrace.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle did not wake.

She remained completely comfortable against Corin.

Her ears occasionally twitched beneath the feathers.

Aside from that, she barely moved.

When Neko walked past and noticed the unusual feather-covered shape, he stopped.

He looked at the ears.

Then at the top of Corin's head.

Then back at the ears.

> **Neko:**  
> "...They're both asleep."

Nobody attempted to wake them.

---

# **ORPHIE'S OBSERVATION**

Orphie eventually found them.

She quietly approached the wing enclosure.

She could barely see Corin.

> **Orphie:**  
> "...Corin?"

No response.

She gently lifted one of the outer feathers.

Inside, Corin was still asleep with both arms around Belle.

Orphie smiled.

> **"Okay."**

She carefully lowered the feather again.

---

# **WING RESPONSE**

The wings remained remarkably stable throughout the entire period.

Corin's subconscious control kept them positioned around Belle without excessive pressure.

The feathers remained soft against Belle rather than producing their usual sharp-edged profile.

No feather detached.

No Ether discharge occurred.

The Nekonium nano-forcefield remained inactive beyond its normal protective state.

---

# **THE SCENE**

The SAFEHOUSE gradually became quieter around them.

Belle slept.

Corin slept.

The wings formed a protective enclosure around both.

Only Belle's ears occasionally emerged from the feathers.

Corin's head rested above hers.

Her arms remained wrapped around Belle.

Nobody disturbed them.

---

# **AFTERWARD**

Several hours later, Belle woke first.

She looked around.

She was still inside Corin's wings.

She blinked.

> **Belle:**  
> "...Cozy."

Corin was still asleep.

Belle decided not to move.

Instead, she settled back down.

Corin's arms tightened slightly around her in her sleep.

Belle smiled.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's wings were originally understood as:

- A means of flight.
    
- A defensive system.
    
- A source of Ether-based projectiles.
    
- A protective structure.
    

They have since become something else.

A blanket.

A shelter.

And, apparently, a perfectly acceptable place for a sleepy Belle to take a nap.

For several hours, the SAFEHOUSE contained an unusual sight:

A small feathered enclosure.

Two sleeping residents inside.

Only **Belle's ears** and **Corin's head** visible.

And Corin, even while completely asleep, holding Belle tightly enough to make one thing very clear:

She was not letting her fall.

**End of SAFEHOUSE Behavioral Observation**
