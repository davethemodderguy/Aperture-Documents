### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / ENTITY PSYCHOLOGY LOG**

# **BUNNY — THE SMILE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Bunny  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE — Room N-07  
**Status:** **PSYCHOLOGICAL ANOMALY UNDER INVESTIGATION**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Bunny had been adapting remarkably well to SAFEHOUSE life.

She learned names.

She learned routines.

She helped Corin in the garden.

She spent time with Belle.

She slept beside Corin.

She laughed.

She smiled.

She appeared happy.

Until someone asked her a simple question.

> **"Bunny, where did you come from?"**

---

# **IMMEDIATE RESPONSE**

Bunny froze.

Her ears lowered.

Her hands began trembling.

She stopped looking at the person speaking.

> **"...Bunny?"**

No response.

Her breathing became increasingly irregular.

Corin immediately noticed.

> **"Bunny?"**

Bunny covered her face.

Then she began crying.

---

# **PANIC RESPONSE**

The reaction escalated rapidly.

Bunny backed away.

Her ears flattened against her head.

She began shaking.

She repeatedly attempted to speak.

> **"No..."**

> **"Don't..."**

> **"Bunny..."**

> **"No past..."**

The words became increasingly difficult to understand.

Corin approached carefully.

> **"It's okay."**

Bunny cried harder.

---

# **THE QUESTION WAS WITHDRAWN**

The interviewer immediately stopped.

> **"You don't have to answer."**

Bunny continued crying.

Corin sat beside her.

She did not ask another question.

She simply stayed.

After several minutes, Bunny's breathing began to stabilize.

Then something happened.

---

# **THE SMILE**

Bunny suddenly stopped crying.

Her head lifted.

Her ears returned to their normal position.

And she smiled.

It was an ordinary smile.

Almost cheerful.

Far too cheerful.

Corin stared at her.

> **"...Bunny?"**

Bunny looked at everyone.

> **"Bunny okay!"**

She laughed.

The transition occurred in less than a second.

There was no gradual emotional recovery.

No visible relief.

No indication that the previous distress had resolved naturally.

The sadness simply...

**stopped.**

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

Corin immediately became uncomfortable.

She looked at Bunny.

Then at Orphie.

Then back at Bunny.

> **"You're not okay."**

Bunny's smile remained.

> **"Bunny okay!"**

Corin shook her head.

> **"No."**

Bunny continued smiling.

> **"Bunny happy!"**

Her voice was cheerful.

Her hands were still trembling.

---

# **OBSERVATION**

C.O.R.E. psychological personnel reviewed the recording repeatedly.

The emotional transition does not resemble normal recovery.

The crying stops too abruptly.

The facial expression changes too quickly.

The vocal tone becomes artificially cheerful.

Most concerningly:

**Bunny appears to be consciously attempting to act happy.**

However, there is evidence suggesting that even this behavior may not be entirely voluntary.

---

# **THE QUESTION**

Corin eventually asked quietly:

> **"Do you want to smile?"**

Bunny froze.

Her smile remained.

Her eyes filled with tears again.

She whispered:

> **"...No."**

Corin's expression changed immediately.

> **"Then why are you smiling?"**

Bunny stared forward.

Her mouth remained in the same smile.

A few seconds passed.

Then:

> **"...Have to."**

---

# **THE SECOND RESPONSE**

Orphie approached.

> **"Who says you have to?"**

Bunny's smile twitched.

Her ears flattened.

She looked frightened again.

> **"...Can't."**

> **"Can't what?"**

Bunny shook her head.

> **"Can't stop."**

The smile remained.

---

# **ANOMALOUS POSSIBILITY**

C.O.R.E. has identified several possibilities.

### **1. Psychological Conditioning**

Bunny may have been conditioned to suppress negative emotions.

### **2. Anomalous Emotional Control**

An external anomaly may be directly influencing her emotional expression.

### **3. Memory Suppression**

Bunny may possess traumatic memories that trigger an automatic defense mechanism.

### **4. External Entity**

Something may be actively forcing Bunny to maintain a specific emotional state.

No conclusion has been reached.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin did something simple.

She hugged Bunny.

Bunny's smile remained.

But she began crying again.

She whispered:

> **"Sorry..."**

Corin shook her head.

> **"Don't say sorry."**

Bunny:

> **"Bunny bad..."**

> **"No."**

> **"Bunny broken..."**

> **"No."**

Corin tightened the hug.

> **"You're Bunny."**

The smile finally disappeared.

Bunny cried into Corin's shoulder.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle witnessed the incident.

She approached slowly.

Her expression was unusually serious.

She sat beside Bunny.

> **"You don't have to smile."**

Bunny looked at her.

Belle continued:

> **"I smile because I want to."**

She pointed at Bunny.

> **"You can cry."**

Bunny's ears lowered.

She nodded.

> **"...Okay."**

---

# **AFTERMATH**

Bunny has not been asked about her past again.

This restriction was immediately accepted by all SAFEHOUSE residents.

If Bunny wants to discuss it, she will be allowed to do so voluntarily.

Until then:

**Nobody asks.**

---

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Bunny's smile is no longer automatically considered evidence that Bunny is happy.

Sometimes she smiles because she is happy.

Sometimes she smiles because she wants to.

And sometimes...

she smiles while crying.

She doesn't know how to explain why.

She only knows that she **has to**.

Whatever happened before Bunny arrived at the Treehouse remains unknown.

But one thing is now certain.

There is something in Bunny's past that she is terrified of remembering.

And something — whether psychological, anomalous, or external — appears to be determined to make sure she keeps smiling anyway.

For now, Corin has made one rule.

> **"If Bunny wants to cry, let her cry."**

No one has argued.
