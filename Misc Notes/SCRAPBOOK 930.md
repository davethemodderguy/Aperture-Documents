# APERTURE BLACKSITE

## INTERNAL MEMORANDUM — RESIDENT MISSING-PERSON CASE

**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**CASE STATUS:** MISSING  
**TIME SINCE DISAPPEARANCE:** **5 WEEKS**  
**SEARCH STATUS:** ACTIVE  
**LOCATION:** UNKNOWN  
**SURVIVAL STATUS:** UNKNOWN

---

# 1. FIVE WEEKS

Five weeks have passed since Neri disappeared.

There has been no confirmed sighting.

No message.

No medical alert.

No confirmed contact.

No reliable indication of where she went.

The search has continued continuously.

And the Blacksite has slowly had to confront something nobody wanted to say aloud:

> **Neri may not be coming back soon.**

Nobody has accepted the possibility that she is gone permanently.

Nobody has stopped searching.

But five weeks is long enough for the absence to become part of everyday life.

And that is what hurts.

---

# 2. THERE IS NO PHOTOGRAPH

There is one particularly painful problem with Neri's disappearance.

There is no proper photograph of her.

Not one that anyone can confidently call **"Neri."**

There are surveillance recordings.

Security footage.

Medical records.

Laboratory documentation.

Archived images where she happens to appear in the background.

There are photographs of groups where she may be present.

There are countless written descriptions.

But there is no simple photograph of Neri.

No portrait.

No photograph of her smiling.

No photograph of her eating cake.

No photograph of her with Bao.

No photograph of her with Frost.

No photograph of her with Alice.

Nothing that someone can hold up and say:

> **"This is my daughter."**

---

# 3. THE ALICE PARALLEL

Corin understands this particular pain better than almost anyone.

When Alice disappeared, Corin had experienced the same realization.

She had spent so much time with Alice that she had never considered the possibility that she would someday need a photograph of her.

Why would she?

Alice was there.

There was always going to be another day.

Another conversation.

Another photograph.

Another chance.

Until suddenly there wasn't.

Now Bao and Frost understand.

They never thought they would need a picture of Neri either.

They saw her every day.

They knew her face.

They knew her clothes.

They knew her expressions.

They knew how she looked when she was annoyed.

They knew how she looked when she was eating cake.

They knew how she looked when she was tired.

They knew how she looked when she was scared.

They never thought they would have to search a database just to find a picture of their daughter.

---

# 4. BAO

Bao has reportedly searched through every photograph she could access.

Nothing.

There are photographs where Neri was nearby.

Photographs where someone else is the subject.

Photographs taken before Neri became part of their lives.

Photographs of Frost.

Photographs of the SAFEHOUSE.

Photographs of family gatherings.

But no photograph exists that Bao feels represents Neri.

At one point, Bao reportedly asked:

> **"Didn't anyone take a picture of her?"**

Nobody had an answer.

Eventually someone told her:

> "We all thought there would be time."

Bao reportedly went quiet.

She did not respond.

---

# 5. FROST

Frost's reaction was quieter.

She remembered Neri clearly.

She could describe her perfectly.

The oversized coat.

The glasses.

The blue eyes.

The strange mismatched iris shapes.

The hairclips.

The way she hid both hands inside her sleeves when eating cake.

The way she called Frost:

> **"Big sis."**

Frost could remember all of it.

But when asked whether she had a photograph of Neri, she realized she didn't.

She reportedly searched through her own belongings anyway.

She found photographs of friends.

Family.

Places.

Events.

But not Neri.

Frost eventually stopped searching.

She kept looking at the empty space on her desk afterward.

---

# 6. THE ENTIRE BLACKSITE

The realization eventually spread.

Neri had touched far more lives than most people realized.

Researchers remembered working with her.

Archivists remembered her questions.

Security remembered finding her where she wasn't supposed to be.

Maintenance remembered the little girl wandering through work areas.

The cafeteria remembered the extra desserts.

Neko remembered the chemistry.

Bao remembered her daughter.

Frost remembered her little sister.

Alice remembered her friend.

Everyone had a memory.

Almost nobody had a photograph.

---

# 7. THE PHOTOGRAPH PROBLEM

This has produced an unexpected emotional response throughout the Blacksite.

People have begun checking old files.

Old camera systems.

Old laboratory records.

Old security archives.

Old group photographs.

Anything.

Not because the search team needs another identification photograph.

They already know what Neri looks like.

They are looking for something else.

A memory.

Something ordinary.

Something that proves she was really there.

A picture of Neri eating cake.

A picture of Neri standing beside Alice.

A picture of Neri sitting beside Bao.

Anything.

---

# 8. WHAT THEY FOUND

They found almost nothing.

A blurry hallway image.

A distant cafeteria recording.

A laboratory photograph where Neri is partially obscured by equipment.

A security camera still where she is walking away from the camera.

Several group photographs where she appears only incidentally.

Nothing posed.

Nothing intentional.

Nothing that captures the Neri everyone remembers.

The closest thing to a "good" image is reportedly a cafeteria security frame.

Neri is sitting at her usual table.

Her head is resting on her hands.

There is a plate of cake in front of her.

She is looking down.

The image is grainy.

It is not a good photograph.

But it is Neri.

The cafeteria staff reportedly kept a copy.

---

# 9. CORIN

Corin eventually saw the image.

She understood immediately why it hurt.

She had once been in the same position.

Alice disappeared.

There were memories everywhere.

But there was no photograph taken with the thought:

> **"We might need this someday."**

Corin reportedly said:

> **"I remember feeling exactly like this."**

Bao answered:

> **"I understand now."**

Neither needed to explain further.

---

# 10. THE BLACKSITE'S COLLECTIVE GRIEF

Neri's disappearance has changed the atmosphere of the Blacksite.

Not dramatically.

There was no facility-wide mourning ceremony.

No official declaration that everyone must grieve.

Life continued.

Research continued.

Security continued.

People went to work.

The cafeteria continued serving meals.

The laboratories remained operational.

The Blacksite remained the Blacksite.

But people began noticing small things.

Someone still leaves a chair open.

Someone still checks the cafeteria entrance.

Someone still looks toward the corridor when they hear small footsteps.

Someone still catches themselves preparing to answer:

> **"Neri?"**

And then remembers.

---

# 11. THE WORST PART

The worst part isn't that people forgot Neri.

They didn't.

It is almost the opposite.

They remember her constantly.

There is just nowhere to put those memories.

There is no new memory being made.

No new conversation.

No new argument.

No new cake.

No new:

> **"Mom."**

No new:

> **"Big sis."**

No new:

> **"Friend."**

Only memories of things that already happened.

---

# 12. BAO AND FROST NOW UNDERSTAND

Before Neri disappeared, Bao and Frost had seen what Alice's disappearance did to Corin.

They understood intellectually.

They sympathized.

They tried to help.

But there is a difference between understanding someone's pain and experiencing it yourself.

Now they understand.

The waiting.

The uncertainty.

The empty room.

The unanswered questions.

The constant thought:

> **"Where is she?"**

The fear that something happened somewhere nobody could see.

The inability to do anything except keep looking.

Corin no longer needs to explain it.

Bao already knows.

Frost already knows.

---

# 13. A BLACKSITE WITHOUT NERI

Five weeks ago, Neri was simply a resident who had become increasingly absent.

Now her absence is itself a presence.

People notice it.

The cafeteria notices it.

The laboratory notices it.

Her friends notice it.

Her family notices it.

The Blacksite notices it.

The place hasn't become emptier because one person disappeared.

It has become emptier because everyone finally realized how much that one person had filled.

---

# 14. CURRENT SEARCH DIRECTIVE

Search operations remain active.

Neri is not considered deceased.

No evidence currently establishes her death.

No evidence establishes that she intentionally abandoned SAFEHOUSE permanently.

No evidence establishes that she has been abducted.

The investigation therefore remains open.

Personnel are reminded:

**Do not stop looking.**

**Do not fabricate sightings.**

**Do not spread rumors.**

**Do not assume the worst.**

And if Neri is found:

Do not immediately ask her where she has been.

Do not immediately ask why she left.

Do not immediately discuss the incident.

First:

### **Make sure she's alive.**

Then:

### **Bring her home.**

---

# 15. FINAL NOTE

There is now a photograph of Neri in the cafeteria's internal records.

It isn't a portrait.

It isn't clear.

It isn't particularly flattering.

Neri isn't even looking at the camera.

She's just sitting there with her head in her hands and a piece of cake in front of her.

Nobody took the picture because they thought it would become important.

Nobody knew they were documenting one of the last ordinary moments of her life in the Blacksite.

Someone eventually printed it.

It is now kept behind the cafeteria counter.

Not as a missing-person poster.

Not as evidence.

Just as a reminder.

Because five weeks later, the entire Blacksite has learned something painfully simple:

> **You don't always realize you're living through a moment you'll someday wish you could have back.**

And nobody in the Blacksite wants to make that mistake with Neri again.
