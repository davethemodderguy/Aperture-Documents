# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **ANOMALOUS PHYSIOLOGY / PERSONNEL CLASSIFICATION UPDATE**

# **CORIN — MINIATURE FELINE FORM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Form:** **Miniature Feline Form**  
**Status:** Stable / Voluntary

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Further observation has established that Corin's feline form is **far smaller than initially estimated**.

She is not merely kitten-sized.

She is approximately **palm-sized**.

When fully curled up, Corin can comfortably fit within the hands of an average adult.

This makes her feline form extraordinarily compact and agile.

---

# **PHYSICAL SIZE**

Corin's feline form is approximately the size of a small kitten that could comfortably sit in someone's palm.

Her miniature body retains:

- White fur.
    
- Feline ears.
    
- Two tails.
    
- Fully functional limbs.
    
- Extremely fast reflexes.
    
- Ether-enhanced regeneration.
    
- Full consciousness.
    

Despite the extreme reduction in size, Corin remains completely herself.

---

# **MOBILITY**

Her tiny size dramatically increases her mobility.

Corin can move through spaces that would otherwise be inaccessible to her.

She can:

- Crawl beneath furniture.
    
- Fit through small openings.
    
- Climb curtains.
    
- Hide inside bags.
    
- Move through ventilation-sized gaps **where physically possible**.
    
- Jump between nearby surfaces.
    
- Run across narrow ledges.
    
- Quickly disappear from sight.
    

Her movements are exceptionally difficult to track when she deliberately avoids being seen.

---

# **SPEED AND AGILITY**

The miniature form has produced an almost absurd level of agility.

Corin can rapidly change direction without losing balance.

Her two tails assist with stabilization.

Her small mass also allows her to perform movements that would be considerably more difficult in her normal body.

A short sprint can carry her across an entire tabletop in seconds.

---

# **BEING HELD**

The SAFEHOUSE residents quickly discovered that Corin can comfortably sit in someone's palm.

Her usual reaction depends on who is holding her.

If she trusts them:

She simply sits there.

If she is comfortable:

She may curl into a tiny ball.

If she is annoyed:

Her ears flatten.

If she wants to leave:

She jumps away.

Neko has become particularly fond of carrying her around.

Corin pretends she dislikes it.

Her tail says otherwise.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle was particularly excited upon discovering Corin's actual size.

She carefully held Corin in both hands.

> **Belle:**  
> "You're so tiny!"

Corin:

> **"Mew."**

Belle:

> **"I can barely hear you!"**

Corin stared at her.

Then climbed onto Belle's shoulder.

---

# **YARN**

At palm size, yarn is effectively enormous.

A normal ball of yarn can be nearly as large as Corin herself.

This has not discouraged her.

It has encouraged her.

She can spend considerable amounts of time attacking a single strand.

Neko considers this the most predictable development imaginable.

---

# **SLEEPING**

Corin can sleep almost anywhere in her miniature form.

Common locations include:

- Someone's palm.
    
- A folded blanket.
    
- A pillow.
    
- Inside a hood.
    
- Between cushions.
    
- On top of a yarn ball.
    
- Curled against one of her tails.
    

When particularly comfortable, she curls into a tiny white ball.

---

# **COMMUNICATION**

Her vocal limitation remains unchanged.

Corin can only meow while transformed.

At her miniature size, however, the sounds are considerably quieter.

This creates an amusing problem:

Corin can be sitting directly beside someone and attempting to get their attention without being noticed.

A tiny:

> **"Mew."**

may be followed by increasingly annoyed:

> **"Mrrp."**

until someone finally realizes she is there.

---

# **SAFETY CONSIDERATIONS**

Because of her size, residents have been instructed to exercise additional caution.

Corin can be accidentally:

- Stepped on.
    
- Sat on.
    
- Closed inside containers.
    
- Covered by blankets.
    
- Knocked from furniture.
    

Her regeneration provides substantial protection, but prevention is preferred.

A small tracking tag has been offered.

Corin refused.

She apparently considers herself capable of keeping track of where she is.

---

# **CURRENT CLASSIFICATION**

Corin's feline transformation is now officially designated:

> **MINIATURE FELINE FORM**

Approximate size:

> **Palm-sized.**

Despite her miniature size, she retains her full consciousness and anomalous capabilities.

Her tiny body does not represent a separate creature.

It is simply another form of Corin.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin is a Cat Thiren.

She has two tails.

She has enormous wings in her normal form.

And when she transforms, she becomes a **palm-sized white kitten**.

A creature capable of fitting comfortably in someone's hand.

A creature that can sprint across a desk.

A creature that can disappear beneath a couch.

A creature that can spend an hour fighting a single strand of yarn.

And a creature that, when asked what she thinks about being this small, simply responds:

> **"Mew."**

Nobody knows exactly what that means.

Everyone agrees it is adorable.

**End of Anomalous Physiology / Personnel Classification Update**
