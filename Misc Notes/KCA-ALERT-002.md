# **STATUS UPDATE**

Seven days have passed since Director **Belle Merryweather** was reported missing.

Despite continuous search operations involving the Krampus Compliance Authority, the Banlands Enforcement Division, the Central Execution Department, and assistance from Aperture Industries, no confirmed trace of Belle has been located.

No credible sightings have been verified.

No communication has been received.

No evidence has established whether she disappeared voluntarily or was taken by force.

The investigation remains active.

---

# **RECOVERED PROPERTY**

During a systematic search of Belle's office at Krampus Compliance Authority Headquarters, investigators documented one personal item left behind.

A small **pink rabbit keychain**.

Personnel from Room N-07 immediately recognized it.

It had been Taiga's gift.

Unlike Belle, who never went anywhere without attaching it to her belongings, the keychain rested alone on her desk.

It was collected, documented, and—with authorization—returned to Endmin for safekeeping.

---

# **ROOM N-07**

The room had become quieter than usual.

Endmin sat on the edge of her bed.

The rabbit keychain rested in her hands.

She slowly ran her thumb across its worn fabric.

Lucius quietly sat beside her.

Neither spoke for several minutes.

Finally, Endmin broke the silence.

> **"...I kept thinking she'd come through the door."

Her voice trembled.

> **"...And tell me she just got buried in paperwork again."

She gave a small, empty laugh.

It lasted only a second.

> **"...I wish..."

She tightened her grip on the keychain.

> **"...I wish I'd stayed with her longer."

A tear landed on the little rabbit charm.

> **"...She kept saying she had work."

> **"...I should've gone with her."

Lucius gently rested a hand on her shoulder.

He didn't try to argue.

He didn't tell her not to think that way.

He simply stayed beside his sister.

## **Lucy**

Lucy entered Room N-07 later that evening.

She saw Endmin still holding the rabbit keychain.

Without saying a word, she sat beside her daughter and wrapped an arm around her.

Endmin leaned against her.

Neither of them let go of the little charm.

---

## **Director Lucious**

After reviewing another report that ended with **"No New Leads,"** Director Lucious closed the file.

He wrote only a single sentence beneath the daily briefing.

> **"Continue the search."**

No further comment was added.

## **FINAL ENTRY**

Belle's office remained exactly as she had left it.

Her reports were still neatly stacked.

Her chair remained pushed in.

Only one thing was missing from the desk where she always worked.

The rabbit keychain that had once accompanied her everywhere.

Now...

It rested quietly in Endmin's hands, as she waited for the day she could give it back to its owner.
