# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **CRITICAL WELLNESS INCIDENT REPORT**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – ESCALATED SUPPORT PROTOCOL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical Director & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **SUMMARY**

Two weeks have passed since Corin Lockwood last experienced the comforting maternal Ether resonance she had previously described during counseling.

Its absence has coincided with a marked deterioration in her emotional wellbeing.

Although Corin has continued to fulfill her responsibilities with her usual precision, psychological staff have documented increasing signs of exhaustion, isolation, hopelessness, and self-neglect.

The contrast between her professional performance and her private wellbeing has become increasingly pronounced.

---

## **AFTER-HOURS OBSERVATIONS**

Security records show Corin continuing to work alone late into the night at KCA Headquarters.

She frequently remains in her sealed office until the early hours of the morning.

Several recordings show long periods where no work is completed.

Instead, Corin sits silently at her desk, sometimes crying, sometimes staring out the window for extended periods.

On multiple occasions she appears visibly distressed before gradually regaining enough composure to resume working.

When interviewed afterward, Corin consistently minimized these episodes.

> "...I'm alright."

Medical staff documented that this statement did not accurately reflect her observed condition.

---

## **CLINICAL ASSESSMENT**

Corin reported that the silence left by the disappearance of the Ether resonance has been profoundly unsettling.

She described it as losing "the last familiar voice from home."

Without that source of comfort, intrusive memories have become more difficult to manage.

Despite this, Corin has continued to interrupt her own moments of crisis and seek to regain control before harm occurs.

The attending psychologist noted:

> "Even during her darkest moments, Corin has repeatedly demonstrated that a part of her still wants the opportunity for tomorrow."

That observation has become the foundation of her revised treatment plan.

---

## **EXECUTIVE RESPONSE**

Following consultation between Medical Services, Director Lucious, Belle, Arcturus, and Norma, a new wellness protocol was unanimously approved.

Effective immediately:

- Corin is no longer scheduled to work overnight alone.
    
- Executive staff rotate evening check-ins, framed as ordinary visits rather than formal supervision.
    
- The Accounting Department remains staffed by at least one additional trusted employee during extended financial reviews whenever feasible.
    
- Belle, Norma, and Arcturus have unrestricted authorization to visit Corin after hours if they are concerned for her wellbeing.
    
- Counseling appointments are increased in frequency until her condition stabilizes.
    

Director Lucious personally signed the directive.

His annotation consisted of a single sentence.

> "No report is important enough to justify someone carrying this burden by themselves."

---

## **SAFEHOUSE**

When Belle learned that Corin had spent another night alone at Headquarters, she quietly packed breakfast before sunrise.

Norma brewed tea.

Arcturus drove.

None of them mentioned work.

None of them mentioned reports.

When Corin opened the office door, the three were waiting outside.

Belle held up the breakfast container.

> "...You skipped breakfast again."

Norma lifted the thermos.

> "...Tea."

Arcturus gave a small nod.

> "...Mind if we sit for a while?"

Corin looked at them.

For several seconds she said nothing.

Then, for the first time in days, she unlocked the office door without being asked.

---

## **STATUS**

Corin remains under active psychological and medical care.

Her recovery is ongoing, and her colleagues have recognized that her greatest need is not to face difficult nights alone.

The KCA's priority is no longer simply ensuring that the Accounting Department functions flawlessly.

It is ensuring that the person who keeps it running knows she is not expected to carry its weight—or her own—without support.

**End of Critical Wellness Incident Report**
