## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **MEDICAL / SAFEHOUSE INCIDENT REPORT**

# **CORIN — THE HEART ATTACK INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Incident Type:** **Acute Startle Response / Cardiac Event**  
**Status:** Resolved

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has confirmed that Corin's restored biological heart is susceptible to extreme startle responses.

This was discovered when someone successfully scared Corin badly enough to trigger an actual **heart attack**.

The incident was not caused by Ether instability, physical trauma, or anomalous interference.

It was, quite simply:

**Someone scared her.**

---

# **THE INCIDENT**

Corin was walking through the SAFEHOUSE while carrying a cup of tea.

She believed she was alone.

Someone was hiding nearby.

As Corin passed, the individual suddenly appeared and shouted.

Corin jumped violently.

Her wings immediately flared outward.

The cup nearly flew out of her hands.

For approximately two seconds, she appeared completely fine.

Then she collapsed.

---

# **INITIAL RESPONSE**

The person responsible immediately realized that this was not the intended reaction.

> **"...Corin?"**

Corin did not answer.

Medical personnel were called immediately.

Her heart was exhibiting severe abnormal activity.

Within moments, C.O.R.E. confirmed that Corin was experiencing an actual cardiac event.

---

# **WHY THIS WAS POSSIBLE**

Corin's heart is now a genuine biological organ.

Although her Ether can regenerate damaged tissue, it does not make her immune to physiological stress.

A sufficiently extreme surge of adrenaline and sympathetic nervous activity can still place enormous strain on her cardiovascular system.

Her anomalous physiology did not prevent the event.

Her body simply responded like a living human body under extreme stress.

---

# **REGENERATION**

Corin's Ether-assisted regeneration immediately began repairing the cardiac damage.

The process prevented permanent injury.

However, the heart attack still occurred.

This distinction is important.

Regeneration can repair damage.

It does not necessarily prevent the damage from occurring in the first place.

---

# **AFTER RECOVERY**

Corin regained consciousness shortly afterward.

She looked extremely confused.

> **"...What happened?"**

Someone explained.

Corin stared at them.

> **"You scared me so badly I had a heart attack?"**

The individual nodded.

Corin was silent.

Her wings slowly unfolded behind her.

> **"...Don't do that again."**

Nobody argued.

---

# **THE CULPRIT**

The identity of the person responsible has been recorded internally.

C.O.R.E. has determined that the scare was intended as a harmless prank.

It was not malicious.

It was, however, catastrophically misjudged.

The prankster has been formally prohibited from attempting to scare Corin again.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RULE**

A new rule has been added:

> ### **DO NOT JUMPSCARE CORIN.**

This rule exists for medical reasons.

Not comedic reasons.

Although several residents have admitted that the incident was initially extremely funny until Corin collapsed.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Corin has become considerably more cautious about people suddenly appearing behind her.

She occasionally glances over her shoulder when walking through unfamiliar areas.

Her wings also react automatically to unexpected noises.

They flare outward before Corin consciously realizes she was startled.

---

# **MEDICAL SIGNIFICANCE**

The incident confirms another consequence of Corin's biological restoration.

She is genuinely alive.

Therefore, she possesses genuine biological vulnerabilities.

Her anomalous abilities provide extraordinary resilience.

They do not make her invulnerable to:

- Cardiac stress.
    
- Adrenaline surges.
    
- Fear responses.
    
- Physiological shock.
    
- Extreme emotional stress.
    

Her heart is real.

And someone apparently had to prove it the hard way.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin survived years of being dead.

She survived Ether instability.

She survived losing limbs.

She survived her body rebuilding itself.

She survived falling out of the sky.

She survived a temporary saltwater allergy.

And somehow, one of the most medically significant incidents after becoming alive again was:

**Someone saying "boo."**

The SAFEHOUSE has since adopted a universal policy:

> **If Corin doesn't know you're there, announce yourself.**

Corin strongly supports this policy.

**End of Medical / SAFEHOUSE Incident Report**
