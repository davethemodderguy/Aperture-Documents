# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **GENERAL INCIDENT REPORT**

### **CASE UPDATE – LUCIUS MERRYWEATHER LOCATED**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Active Case Resolution

---

## **SUMMARY**

During an authorized visit to the Banlands capital, personnel from Aperture Industries and the Merryweather family encountered a group of individuals assaulting an unidentified Wolf Thiren in a public street.

The intervention resulted in the unexpected resolution of the four-year missing person case involving **Lucius Merryweather**.

---

## **Initial Incident**

The Friendly Roommates, accompanied by Lucy, Belle, and Endmin, were walking through the city when shouting echoed from a nearby alley.

Several Banlands Watchdogs surrounded a young Wolf Thiren with light-blue fur.

The individual displayed multiple bruises and visible injuries.

Most pedestrians ignored the altercation.

Endmin did not.

She immediately ran toward the scene.

Belle followed close behind.

---

## **Confrontation**

One of the thugs laughed.

> **"Huh? What are you little girls doing here?"**

Belle stepped forward first.

> **"Let him go. Right now."**

Another thug shoved both Belle and Endmin backward.

> **"Come on, little ladies. Stay out of business that isn't yours."**

The Friendly Roommates instinctively moved to help.

Lucy quietly raised a hand.

> **"Let them deal with this."**

She smiled, already knowing how the situation would end.

---

## **Identification**

Endmin's expression became completely emotionless.

She lifted her rifle.

Belle unsheathed her massive sword.

The blade struck the stone street with a heavy metallic **THUD**.

The laughter stopped immediately.

Both women presented their official identification.

Endmin spoke first.

> **"Lindervene Merryweather."**

> **"Head of the Central Execution Department."**

Belle followed.

> **"Belle Merryweather."**

> **"Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority."**

The five men visibly froze.

---

## **Charges**

Endmin calmly chambered a round into her rifle.

> **"...So."

> **"Do you leave..."

> **"...or do I personally remove you?"**

The apparent leader tried to regain his confidence.

> **"You can't abuse your authority."

> **"We didn't do anything."**

Endmin glanced at several packages lying nearby.

She spoke without raising her voice.

> **"Unauthorized distribution of Etheric contraband."**

Belle looked toward the injured Wolf Thiren.

> **"Abuse of authority and assault against a civilian."**

The two Directors didn't move another step.

They didn't need to.

The five men abandoned everything and fled.

Perimeter officers arriving moments later recovered the contraband and began an investigation.

---

## **The Survivor**

The Wolf Thiren remained where he was.

Bruised.

Exhausted.

Breathing heavily.

He bowed his head.

> **"...Thank you."**

As he looked up...

His eyes settled on the bracelet around Endmin's wrist.

He stared.

Endmin noticed something as well.

A familiar birthmark on the side of his neck.

She stopped breathing.

Almost afraid to speak.

> **"...Lucius?"**

The young man slowly looked at her.

Then at the bracelet.

His own trembling hand reached beneath his torn jacket.

From around his neck...

He pulled a weathered bracelet hanging from a cord.

The matching bracelet.

His voice cracked.

> **"...Linder?"**

---

## **Reunion**

Lucy was already running before anyone else reacted.

She threw her arms around Lucius.

For several long seconds...

No one said a word.

Lucius hugged her back with all the strength he had left.

> **"...Mom..."**

Lucy couldn't answer.

She was crying too hard.

Endmin stood frozen.

Unable to believe what she was seeing.

Lucius looked past Lucy.

His little sister was taller.

Older.

Still wearing her bracelet.

He smiled through tears.

> **"...You kept yours..."**

Endmin nodded.

She tried to speak.

Nothing came out.

Instead...

She ran forward and hugged him.

As tightly as she could.

> **"...I never stopped looking for you..."**

Lucius gently rested his forehead against hers.

> **"...I know."**

> **"...I never stopped hoping you'd find me."**

---

## **Witness Statements**

The Friendly Roommates watched quietly.

Star wiped away tears.

Taiga smiled while trying not to cry herself.

Mart quietly folded his wings.

Neko lowered her head with a relieved smile.

Miss Neko simply whispered,

> **"...Welcome home."**

Nightshade nodded silently.

Belle watched the reunion with a gentle smile.

She rested her oversized sword against her shoulder.

> **"...Another Merryweather came home today."**

---

## **Medical Evaluation**

Lucius was transported to the Blacksite Medical Wing for treatment.

Medical staff confirmed:

- Multiple bruises.
    
- Malnutrition.
    
- Signs of prolonged hardship.
    
- No immediately life-threatening injuries.
    

Behavioral Sciences recommended a gradual reintegration process following four years of unexplained absence.

An investigation into the circumstances of his disappearance remains active.

---

## **Director Lucious' Statement**

After receiving confirmation that the missing person had been identified, Director Lucious personally amended the archive.

The status line changed from:

**MISSING**

To:

**LOCATED — ALIVE**

He wrote one final note beneath the case.

> **"Some investigations end with answers."**

> **"The fortunate ones end with someone coming home."**

---

## **CASE STATUS**

**Lucius Merryweather has been officially located alive.**

The missing person investigation is considered resolved with respect to his location.

A separate investigation into the circumstances of his disappearance and the individuals responsible remains active.

---

### **Closing Observation**

That evening, Room N-07 was fuller than it had ever been.

Lucius sat on the sofa, wrapped in a blanket after receiving medical treatment.

Belle was already insisting he eat something.

Taiga placed a bowl of salad in front of him.

Star excitedly introduced herself.

Nightshade quietly welcomed him.

Neko and Miss Neko greeted him warmly.

Mart and Niko offered a handshake.

Endmin sat beside her older brother.

Neither of them spoke for a while.

They didn't need to.

Lucius looked around the room.

Then smiled.

> **"...So this is the family you found while looking for me."**

Endmin laughed softly.

A tear rolled down her cheek.

> **"...Yeah."**

> **"...And now... they're your family too."**
