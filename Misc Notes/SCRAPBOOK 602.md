# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — DAMAGED WING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE  
**Status:** Regenerating / Emotionally Distressed

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Corin has spent much of the day sitting quietly.

Her left wing remains heavily bandaged.

The regeneration process is active beneath the coverings, slowly rebuilding what was lost.

Corin barely acknowledges anyone around her.

She simply sits.

---

# **THE WING**

After being given permission to carefully touch the injured area, Corin slowly runs her hand over the bandages.

She pets the damaged wing.

Very gently.

Almost as if she is reassuring it.

Her hand stops whenever she feels pain.

Then she continues.

---

# **EMOTIONAL RESPONSE**

Corin is sobbing.

She tries to keep herself quiet, but the tears continue.

Her shoulders occasionally tremble.

Her ears remain lowered.

Her remaining wing is folded tightly around herself.

The injured wing cannot return the gesture.

That seems to hurt her almost as much emotionally as physically.

---

# **BELLE'S OBSERVATION**

Belle sits nearby.

She does not attempt to stop Corin from touching her wing.

She knows Corin needs to process what happened.

Belle simply stays close.

When Corin's crying becomes heavier, Belle places a hand against her back.

Corin leans into her.

Neither says anything.

---

# **CORIN'S THOUGHTS**

When Guiding Light briefly assists with interpreting Corin's emotions, the dominant feelings are clear:

**Pain.**

**Fear.**

**Frustration.**

And above all:

**Loss.**

Corin knows the wing will regenerate.

She knows her feathers will grow back.

She knows she will eventually fly again.

But knowing that does not make the present hurt any less.

---

# **FLIGHT**

Corin repeatedly looks toward the window.

The sky is visible outside.

She watches it for several seconds.

Then looks back down at her bandaged wing.

Her hand resumes gently petting it.

---

# **A SMALL REASSURANCE**

Belle eventually says:

> **"It'll grow back."**

Corin continues crying.

Belle adds:

> **"And I'll be here when it does."**

Corin's hand stops.

Her ears lift slightly.

She leans against Belle.

Her crying gradually becomes quieter.

---

# **MEDICAL NOTE**

The regeneration remains strong.

There is no indication that the wing will remain permanently damaged.

The loss of the feathers is temporary.

The underlying structures are already rebuilding.

C.O.R.E. therefore considers the physical prognosis **favorable**.

The emotional recovery may take considerably longer.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin sits with her damaged wing in her lap.

She gently pets the bandages.

Tears fall continuously.

She knows it will heal.

She knows she will fly again.

But right now, she misses her wing.

And she misses the sky.

So she simply sits there, sobbing softly, one hand resting gently against the injured wing while Belle remains beside her.

**End of SAFEHOUSE Medical Observation**
