## **Bloodline History**

Prior to the marriage of **Lucy Merryweather** and **Josh Merryweather**, the Merryweather family lineage consisted entirely of **Banlandians**.

Historical genealogical records identify the family as one of the oldest documented pure-blooded Banlandian noble lineages.

Josh Merryweather, a **Wolf Thiren**, became the first non-Banlandian to marry into the family.

Their daughter, **Lindervene "Endmin" Merryweather**, became the first biological Thiren born into the Merryweather bloodline.

---

## **The Thiren Inheritance Anomaly**

Despite Josh being a **Wolf Thiren**, Endmin was born a **Cat Thiren**.

Current Banlands biological research cannot adequately explain this phenomenon.

The observation directly contradicts conventional hereditary expectations.

Current evidence suggests:

- Thiren species does **not** appear to be inherited through conventional genetic or DNA-based mechanisms alone.
    
- A parent's Thiren type does not reliably predict that of their offspring.
    
- Additional biological or anomalous factors are believed to determine a child's Thiren classification.
    

No confirmed theory has yet been accepted by Banlands Biological Sciences.

The mechanism remains officially classified as **unknown**.

---

## **Josh Merryweather**

**Status:** Deceased

Josh Merryweather served the Banlands until his death during the **Banlands Conflict**.

He is remembered by Lucy and Endmin as a devoted husband and father.

Behavioral Sciences continues to advise personnel to approach discussions regarding Josh's final days with sensitivity, particularly around Endmin.

---

## **Knowledge of Aperture Industries**

For the majority of the Merryweather family's history, **Aperture Industries** remained completely unknown to them.

As of the date of this report, only the following members have direct knowledge of Aperture Industries and its operations:

- **Lucy Merryweather**
    
- **Endmin (Lindervene Merryweather)**
    
- **Belle Merryweather**
    

No evidence indicates that other members of the Merryweather family were aware of Aperture Industries prior to their retirement, disappearance, or death. This includes historical figures such as Gray, Linda, Robin, Dave, Clover, Josh, and John, based on currently available records.

Future contact with surviving relatives will be documented separately if their level of awareness changes.

---

## **Research Note**

The inheritance of Thiren species remains one of the largest unanswered questions in Banlands biology.

The Merryweather family is frequently cited as a notable example due to the birth of a Cat Thiren from a Banlandian and a Wolf Thiren, demonstrating that Thiren classification cannot currently be explained by ordinary hereditary models alone.
