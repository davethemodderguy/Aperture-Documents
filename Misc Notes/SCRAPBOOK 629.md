### **CORIN / ANOMALOUS FEATHER STATUS REPORT**

# **MASS WEAKENING OF CORIN'S BURNING FEATHERS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **Global Reduction in Feather-Flame Intensity**  
**Status:** **ACTIVE / UNEXPLAINED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following Corin's disappearance, C.O.R.E. conducted an inspection of every known **burning Corin feather** within Aperture-controlled territory.

The results are unprecedented.

**Every single feather has severely weakened in light intensity.**

This includes feathers previously stored in:

- SAFEHOUSE lanterns.
    
- Blacksite storage containers.
    
- Research facilities.
    
- Emergency illumination kits.
    
- Archived anomalous-material storage.
    
- Personnel-maintained containers.
    

No unaffected specimen has been identified.

---

# **NORMAL BEHAVIOR**

Under ordinary circumstances, a dormant Corin feather can be ignited and produces a bright, light-azure flame.

The flame:

- Requires no oxygen.
    
- Produces no measurable heat.
    
- Emits a faint vanilla scent.
    
- Does not consume the feather.
    
- Does not extinguish through conventional means.
    
- Continues burning indefinitely.
    

Its brightness has historically remained remarkably consistent.

That is no longer the case.

---

# **CURRENT CONDITION**

The flames remain active.

However, their luminosity has dropped dramatically.

Some feathers that previously illuminated entire rooms now produce little more than a faint blue glow.

Others are barely visible unless observed in darkness.

The flames themselves remain unmistakably anomalous.

They simply appear...

**tired.**

# **IMPORTANT OBSERVATION**

The weakening does not appear to be localized.

It is not limited to feathers stored near the Treehouse.

It is not limited to recently shed feathers.

It is not limited to a particular container.

Distance has produced no meaningful difference.

Every known burning feather is exhibiting the same phenomenon.

C.O.R.E. therefore considers the possibility that the phenomenon is **linked directly to Corin herself.**

No mechanism has been identified.

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE LANTERNS**

The Treehouse remains the most visually obvious example.

The lanterns that once filled the residence with Corin's characteristic blue glow are now barely illuminating their surroundings.

The effect is particularly unsettling.

For months, those lights were the only constant presence during Corin's long wait for Orphie.

Now even they are fading.

---

# **ORPHIE'S RESPONSE**

Orphie was shown the weakened flames.

She immediately recognized that something was wrong.

> **"...They weren't like this before."**

A researcher confirmed that they had not been.

Orphie stared at the nearest feather for several seconds.

> **"Is she doing this?"**

No one could answer.

---

# **LUMI'S RESPONSE**

Lumi reportedly became visibly distressed after observing the phenomenon.

She asked whether the weakening meant Corin was injured.

C.O.R.E. could not confirm this.

There is currently:

> **No reliable method of determining Corin's condition from feather luminosity alone.**

Nevertheless, the timing is considered deeply concerning.

---

# **SEARCH PRIORITY**

The mass weakening has resulted in Corin's search being escalated again.

Researchers are now treating the feather phenomenon as a potential **remote indicator** of Corin's current state.

If the luminosity changes further, all known specimens will be monitored simultaneously.

Any sudden:

- Increase.
    
- Decrease.
    
- Color change.
    
- Flickering.
    
- Extinction.
    
- Reignition.
    

is to be reported immediately.
