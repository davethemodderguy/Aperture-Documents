# INCIDENT REPORT

## MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT — “A LITTLE MISCHIEF”

**CLASSIFICATION:** C.O.R.E. INTERNAL  
**ACCESS:** ARCHIVIST / EXECUTIVE  
**STATUS:** CLOSED — NON-HOSTILE  
**LOCATION:** SPAWN HEADQUARTERS  
**ENTITY:** MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT  
**ENTITY CLASS:** ARCHITECT OF REALITY  
**OBSERVING ARCHIVIST:** Neko

---

# 1. SUMMARY

Following the recent visits of **Guiding Light** and **Curious Light** to SPAWN Headquarters, the third Architect of Reality, **Mischevious Light**, subsequently manifested within the facility.

Unlike the previous two visits, Mischevious Light appeared to have no particular reason for being there.

He was simply bored.

His stated objective was:

> **“A little mischief.”**

No personnel were injured.

No major infrastructure was damaged.

Several extremely confusing things happened.

SPAWN personnel remain unsure whether the incident qualifies as an attack.

C.O.R.E. does not classify it as one.

Neko classified it as:

> **“Funny.”**

---

# 2. INITIAL MANIFESTATION

At 21:13, a security camera in SPAWN's eastern corridor briefly displayed static.

When the image returned, Mischevious Light was standing in the hallway.

He looked directly into the camera.

He waved.

Then he said:

> **MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT:**  
> “They still don't know, do they?”

The camera feed immediately cut to another camera.

Mischevious Light was already standing in front of that one.

---

# 3. INCIDENT TIMELINE

### 21:14 — THE SIGIL

A SPAWN containment sigil automatically activated beneath Mischevious Light.

He looked down.

> **MISCHIEVIOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Oh, this again.”

He gently tapped the edge of the sigil with his foot.

The entire symbol turned into a flower bed.

SPAWN security initially interpreted this as a containment failure.

It was later determined that the containment system was still technically functioning.

It was simply containing flowers.

---

### 21:17 — SECURITY BADGES

Mischevious Light entered an administrative corridor.

He examined several employee identification cards.

Nothing happened to the employees.

Their cards, however, began displaying increasingly embarrassing information.

One senior ritualist's badge changed from:

> **SENIOR RITUAL SPECIALIST**

to:

> **VERY IMPORTANT RITUAL GUY**

Another became:

> **I'M SUPPOSED TO BE IN CHARGE**

A third simply displayed:

> **DO NOT ASK**

---

### 21:21 — THE ELEVATOR

Mischevious Light entered an elevator.

The elevator display normally showed:

> **FLOOR 4**

It changed to:

> **THE VOID**

The doors opened.

It was still Floor 4.

The doors closed.

The display changed to:

> **FLOOR 4**

Mischevious Light laughed.

---

### 21:24 — THE RITUAL ROOM

A group of SPAWN personnel were preparing another containment ritual.

Mischevious Light quietly entered the room.

Nobody noticed.

He watched them for approximately thirty seconds.

Then he changed one symbol.

The ritual continued normally.

Except every candle began floating upside down.

Nobody noticed for another eleven seconds.

Then:

> **SPAWN RITUALIST:**  
> “...Why are the candles upside down?”

Mischevious Light was already gone.

---

# 4. THE VENDING MACHINE

At 21:29, Mischevious Light approached a vending machine.

He pressed the button labeled:

> **WATER**

The machine dispensed tea.

He pressed it again.

Tea.

Again.

Tea.

Again.

Tea.

He looked at the camera.

> **MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Better.”

The vending machine has continued dispensing tea as of this report.

SPAWN maintenance has been unable to determine why.

---

# 5. THE “DIVINE AUTHORIZATION” INCIDENT

Mischevious Light eventually discovered one of SPAWN's divine-authentication terminals.

The terminal displayed:

> **AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY**

He pressed the screen.

The system displayed:

> **WELCOME, DIVINE AUTHORITY**

He pressed another button.

The system displayed:

> **WELCOME, CREATOR**

He pressed another.

The system displayed:

> **PLEASE DO NOT PRESS THIS BUTTON**

He pressed it.

The entire terminal displayed:

> **...WHY**

Mischevious Light reportedly laughed for approximately forty seconds.

---

# 6. SPAWN RESPONSE

Security personnel eventually located him.

Weapons were not raised.

This was considered progress.

A senior officer cautiously approached.

> **SPAWN OFFICER:**  
> “Are you responsible for these incidents?”

Mischevious Light smiled.

> **MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Maybe.”

> **SPAWN OFFICER:**  
> “Why are you here?”

> **MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT:**  
> “I was bored.”

The officer apparently did not know how to respond to this.

---

# 7. C.O.R.E. OBSERVATION

Neko had been monitoring the entire incident.

At 21:37, he physically wrote the following onto the incident observation printout:

> **“These guys don't know who they are fighting huh?”**

The handwriting was identified as Neko's.

Underneath it, he added:

> **“They're trying to use rituals on THE ARCHITECTS OF REALITY.”**

And, after approximately thirty seconds:

> **“This is better than television.”**

---

# 8. ARCHITECT BEHAVIORAL ASSESSMENT

Mischevious Light demonstrated no indication of hostile intent.

His actions appear to have been motivated almost entirely by amusement.

Importantly, he demonstrated repeated opportunities to cause catastrophic damage.

He did not take them.

He instead chose to:

- Rearrange symbols.
    
- Alter labels.
    
- Confuse security systems.
    
- Turn containment sigils into flowers.
    
- Modify vending-machine outputs.
    
- Change elevator displays.
    
- Inconvenience personnel.
    
- Wave at security cameras.
    
- Make SPAWN question their own equipment.
    

This distinction is significant.

Mischevious Light was not demonstrating what he **could** do.

He was demonstrating what he could do **without trying.**

# 10. FINAL NOTE

Mischevious Light eventually departed SPAWN Headquarters.

No portal was observed.

No ritual signature was detected.

He simply disappeared.

> **MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT:**  
> “Tell Neko I said hi.”

The camera turned off.
