# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-OP-041-B**

### **OPERATION: SILENT VERDICT – WARRANT EXECUTION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Joint Operations Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the seizure of the Banlands Watchdogs' headquarters, investigators from the Krampus Compliance Authority completed an expedited review of the recovered evidence.

Financial records.

Encrypted communications.

Witness testimony.

Recovered research documents.

Together, the evidence established probable cause linking the **Head of the Department of Defense** to the protection of the Watchdogs' smuggling network and the authorization of illegal Etheric Contraband experiments.

Under Banlands law, the Krampus Compliance Authority issued a lawful arrest warrant.

Belle Merryweather elected to personally oversee its execution.

---

## **Operation Brief**

Unlike the raid on the Watchdogs' headquarters, this mission had a different objective.

Belle addressed the entry team before departure.

> **"Our objective is the execution of a lawful warrant."**

She looked across the room.

> **"Not revenge."**

> **"Not intimidation."**

> **"Justice."**

Endmin nodded.

Lucius checked his sidearms before holstering them.

Neither interrupted.

---

## **Arrival**

Government vehicles arrived outside the Department of Defense headquarters.

Krampus Compliance Authority officers secured every entrance.

Central Execution Department personnel established an outer security perimeter.

Employees watched from windows in stunned silence.

Belle walked toward the main entrance carrying her oversized sword across her back—not in her hands.

She had no intention of escalating the situation.

---

## **The Confrontation**

The Head of Defense was waiting inside the executive office.

He remained seated.

> **"...Director Belle."**

Belle placed a sealed folder on the desk.

Inside were the signed warrant and supporting evidence.

> **"You are under investigation for corruption, abuse of public office, conspiracy with the Banlands Watchdogs, and facilitating the trafficking of prohibited Etheric Contrabands."**

He glanced through the documents.

His expression gradually changed.

> **"...You've built quite a case."**

Belle answered calmly.

> **"The evidence built it."**

---

## **The Arrest**

The official slowly stood.

He looked toward the officers surrounding the room.

Then back to Belle.

After a long silence, he removed his departmental identification badge and placed it on the desk.

> **"...I understand."**

Belle gave a small nod.

Two KCA officers stepped forward and placed him under arrest without incident.

No weapon was drawn.

No force was required.

---

## **Evidence Protection**

Immediately following the arrest, Krampus Compliance Authority investigators secured the office.

Digital records were preserved.

Physical documents were cataloged.

Evidence was transferred under sealed chain-of-custody procedures for prosecution.

---

## **Personnel Comments**

### Lucius

> **"...That's it?"**

Belle looked toward him.

> **"Justice isn't always loud."**

---

### Endmin

Watching the former official escorted away, Endmin quietly said:

> **"...The people he hurt deserve the truth."**

---

### Belle

As the convoy departed, Belle looked once at the headquarters behind her.

> **"No office..."

She adjusted the badge on her uniform.

> **"...is above the law."**

---

## **Director Lucious' Note**

After receiving the operation report, Director Lucious added a final observation.

> **"The measure of an oversight authority is not whether it investigates ordinary officials."**

> **"It is whether it applies the law equally to the most powerful."**

---

## **MISSION STATUS**

- Banlands Watchdogs: Dismantled.
    
- Etheric Contraband network: Disrupted.
    
- Illegal research: Seized for evidence.
    
- Head of the Department of Defense: Arrested pending judicial proceedings.
    

The Krampus Compliance Authority concluded the operation without allowing political influence to interfere with the execution of a lawful warrant, reinforcing its role as an independent oversight body entrusted with combating corruption wherever it is found.
