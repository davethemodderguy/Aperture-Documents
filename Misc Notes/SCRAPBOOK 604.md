# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **ANOMALOUS MATERIAL INVENTORY REPORT**

# **CORIN — SHED FEATHER COLLECTION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — C.O.R.E. RESTRICTED  
**Material:** Corin's Shed Wing Feathers  
**Quantity:** **One Large Sack**  
**Condition:** Inert  
**Status:** Secured

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the pressure cooker explosion that severely damaged Corin's left wing, essentially all feathers from the affected wing were shed.

The resulting quantity was unexpectedly large.

The feathers were collected into a single large storage sack.

The sack is now informally referred to by SAFEHOUSE personnel as:

> **"The Corin Feather Sack."**

---

# **APPEARANCE**

The sack is filled almost completely with Corin's former wing feathers.

The feathers remain visually recognizable as Corin's.

Despite having been shed, they retain their characteristic properties:

- Extremely lightweight.
    
- Soft.
    
- Durable.
    
- Slightly luminous.
    
- Ether-infused.
    

When placed in darkness, the entire sack produces a faint glow.

The effect is strongest immediately after collection and gradually decreases.

---

# **IMPORTANT: THE FEATHERS ARE INERT**

The feathers no longer possess an active connection to Corin.

They do not respond to her thoughts.

They cannot be remotely controlled.

They do not cause Corin pain when handled.

C.O.R.E. has confirmed that their removal does not interfere with her ongoing regeneration.

---

# **ETHER CONTENT**

Residual Ether remains present within the feathers.

The concentration is significantly lower than that of an attached feather.

No spontaneous activation has been observed.

The feathers are therefore considered **inert anomalous material**.

They remain under restricted storage because Corin's feathers have previously demonstrated multiple unusual properties.

---

# **CURRENT APPEARANCE OF CORIN'S LEFT WING**

The injured wing currently has very few, if any, usable feathers.

New feather growth will occur as regeneration progresses.

Corin has repeatedly looked toward the sack.

She knows exactly what it contains.

Her old feathers.

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

When shown the sack, Corin became unusually quiet.

She reached inside.

Her hand disappeared into the feathers.

She lifted a handful.

For several seconds, she simply held them.

Then she gently placed them back.

She did not ask for them to be discarded.

---

# **BELLE'S SUGGESTION**

Belle proposed keeping the feathers until Corin had fully recovered.

Corin agreed.

The sack will therefore remain stored rather than destroyed.

---

# **POTENTIAL USES**

C.O.R.E. has identified several theoretical applications for the material.

However:

**No experiments are authorized without Corin's consent.**

The feathers are part of her body-derived biological material.

Their anomalous properties do not automatically grant researchers permission to utilize them.

---

# **STORAGE**

The sack is currently stored in a secure, dry container.

It is:

- Protected from contamination.
    
- Kept away from active Ether sources.
    
- Clearly labeled.
    
- Inaccessible to unauthorized personnel.
    
- Not to be used as stuffing material.
    

The final instruction was added after a researcher asked whether the feathers could be used to fill pillows.

The answer was:

> **No.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The sack is surprisingly large.

It contains what used to cover Corin's entire left wing.

Hundreds of feathers.

Perhaps more.

All shed at once when the explosion destroyed the wing's feather structures.

Now they sit quietly in storage.

Corin's wing will eventually grow new feathers.

These old ones will never return to it.

For now, however, the sack remains untouched.

A physical reminder of what Corin lost—

and of what her body is slowly rebuilding.

**End of Anomalous Material Inventory Report**
