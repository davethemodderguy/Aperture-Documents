# NERI — CONCEALED ARMAMENT DISCOVERY

**CLASSIFICATION:** C.O.R.E. INTERNAL — RESTRICTED  
**ACCESS:** SAFEHOUSE ADMINISTRATION / SECURITY / BEHAVIORAL DIVISION  
**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**SPECIES:** Seal Thiren  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE SAFEHOUSE RESIDENT  
**THREAT LEVEL:** **HIGH — CONDITIONAL**  
**ANOMALOUS STATUS:** UNCONFIRMED  
**PRIMARY CONCERN:** Extreme Sharp-Weapon Fixation / Concealed Armament

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

C.O.R.E. has discovered that the two jagged knives incorporated into Neri's hairclips are **not her only concealed weapons**.

They are not even close.

Following the previous behavioral reassessment, Security conducted a non-invasive inventory of Neri's normal clothing and accessories.

The results were concerning.

Neri has accumulated numerous concealed knives throughout her everyday clothing.

Known locations include:

- boots;
    
- hair accessories;
    
- coat;
    
- clothing compartments;
    
- personal accessories;
    
- other locations not yet fully documented.
    

Neri appears to regard carrying multiple concealed blades as completely ordinary.

When asked how many knives she carries, she responded:

> "A lot."

When asked for an exact number:

> "I don't know."

When asked why:

> "Knives."

The interview was terminated.

---

# 2. CONCEALED ARMAMENT

Neri's preferred method of carrying weapons is concealment within objects she already wears.

This makes conventional visual screening unreliable.

### HAIR

Two of her three hairclips are functional jagged blades.

The third remains a decorative fish-bone clip.

Additional small blades have also been found incorporated into or concealed among her other hair accessories.

### BOOTS

Neri has been found carrying additional knives within her boots.

These are not immediately visible during ordinary interaction.

### COAT

Her coat contains several concealed storage locations.

At least one additional knife has been recovered from the coat during inventory.

### RIBBONS / HAIR ACCESSORIES

Several apparently decorative accessories have been determined to contain or conceal small edged implements.

Personnel are reminded that **"decorative" does not necessarily mean "harmless"** when dealing with Neri.

---

# 3. THE SCARF

Neri's scarf requires separate consideration.

The scarf itself is not anomalous and does not appear to contain a hidden blade.

However, its construction and length make it capable of functioning as a **dangerous restraint or strangulation hazard** during a physical confrontation.

Neri is aware of this.

Security personnel are therefore instructed not to treat the scarf as harmless simply because it is fabric.

No personnel are to request or encourage demonstrations.

---

# 4. DISCOVERY INCIDENT

The discovery occurred when a Security Officer noticed an unfamiliar metallic object while Neri was removing her coat.

The officer asked:

> "Neri, what's that?"

Neri looked down.

> "Knife."

The officer paused.

> "Why do you have a knife in your coat?"

Neri appeared confused by the question.

> "Because I need it."

> "For what?"

Neri thought for several seconds.

> "If I need a knife."

The officer subsequently requested a complete inventory.

Neri reluctantly agreed.

The inventory took significantly longer than expected.

After several additional weapons were discovered, the Security Officer asked:

> "How many of these do you have?"

Neri:

> "A lot."

Security:

> "Can you give me a number?"

Neri:

> "No."

Security:

> "Why not?"

Neri:

> "I don't count them."

---

# 5. BEHAVIORAL SIGNIFICANCE

This discovery substantially changes the interpretation of Neri's previously documented knife fixation.

Neri does not merely enjoy knives.

She has incorporated them into her daily life to an unusual degree.

Weapons are part of her clothing.

Weapons are part of her accessories.

Weapons are kept within immediate reach.

And Neri appears to consider this arrangement entirely normal.

The Behavioral Division therefore recommends treating her interest in edged weapons as a **persistent behavioral trait**, rather than an isolated defensive habit.

---

# 6. WHY NERI REMAINS A SAFEHOUSE RESIDENT

Despite the above, Neri has not been removed from the SAFEHOUSE.

There are several reasons.

First, Neri has demonstrated excellent social integration.

She is friends with almost everyone.

Second, she has not demonstrated indiscriminate aggression.

Third, her violent behavior is strongly associated with perceived threats.

Fourth, she responds positively to kindness and stable relationships.

Fifth, attempting to forcibly remove every knife from Neri without understanding her behavior would likely create considerably more danger than the knives themselves.

C.O.R.E. therefore favors **behavioral management and trust** over unnecessary confrontation.

---

# 8. PERSONNEL DIRECTIVE

Personnel are reminded:

> **Do not attempt to search Neri without authorization.**

> **Do not attempt to confiscate her personal belongings.**

> **Do not playfully reach for her hair, coat, boots, ribbons, or other accessories.**

> **Do not threaten her friends.**

> **Do not provoke her.**

> **Do not assume an ordinary-looking accessory is harmless.**

If Neri voluntarily discloses a weapon, personnel should notify SAFEHOUSE Security rather than attempting to seize it themselves.

---

# 9. ANOMALOUS QUESTION

One question remains unanswered.

**Why does Neri have such an extreme affinity for knives?**

Her chemistry aptitude may provide part of the explanation.

Her intelligence allows her to understand materials and weapons quickly.

Her protective personality provides a practical reason to carry them.

Neither explanation adequately accounts for the apparent **emotional attachment**.

Neri genuinely likes sharp weapons.

She talks about them with the same enthusiasm she displays when discussing:

- fish;
    
- sweets;
    
- chemistry;
    
- swimming.
    

Whether this is simply an unusual personality trait or evidence of an underlying anomaly remains unknown.

---

This assessment is now obsolete.

The updated assessment is:

> **Neri is a potentially anomalous yandere with an extensive concealed-weapon habit.**

The hairclips are simply the weapons everyone noticed first.
