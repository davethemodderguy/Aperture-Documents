# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL FOLLOW-UP REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – RETURN OF TRAUMA SYMPTOMS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the Shelter T-01 isolation event, Corin Lockwood experienced a brief period of improvement.

The white tiger and snowy owl gradually returned to their usual positions around the treehouse.

The owl resumed watching from the branches.

The tiger returned to sleeping beneath the tree.

Neither required anything from Corin.

They simply remained.

For a short time...

Corin improved.

---

# **THE RETURN OF COMPANIONSHIP**

The presence of the two animals restored part of the routine Corin had lost.

Morning tea.

The tiger waiting below.

The owl watching from above.

Quiet afternoons spent reading while snow fell around Shelter T-01.

Corin began speaking more.

She ate more regularly.

She occasionally smiled again.

To outside observers, it appeared that the worst of the episode had passed.

However...

The improvement depended heavily on the small world she had built around herself.

And that world was becoming smaller.

---

# **THE WINTER STORM**

A severe winter system moved through the Blacksite perimeter.

The storm was stronger than previous forecasts predicted.

Travel routes toward Shelter T-01 became unsafe.

Visibility dropped.

Temperatures reached dangerous levels.

Routine visits were suspended.

---

# **ABSENT VISITORS**

For the first time in weeks, the path to the treehouse was empty.

No paperwork deliveries by hand.

No cookies from Belle.

No books from Mart.

No supplies from Bao.

No conversations with Astral.

No visits from Taiga.

No quiet evenings with Lumi.

The absence was not caused by abandonment.

It was caused by the weather.

But Corin did not see the difference.

---

# **THE DISTANCE**

Corin knew why people had stopped coming.

She understood the storm.

She understood the danger.

She understood that everyone still cared.

But understanding something did not stop it from hurting.

A thought began repeating.

> "They left because I pushed them away."

Then another.

> "They finally got tired."

Then another.

> "I knew it would happen."

---

# **CORIN'S REFLECTION**

The most difficult part was not remembering the past.

Corin had already faced those memories.

The difficult part was realizing they could return.

She believed she had moved forward.

She believed she had accepted herself.

She believed she was finally becoming someone who could live.

So why was the pain back?

Why did the same fear return after everything she built?

---

# **PERSONAL LOG – CORIN LOCKWOOD**

Recovered from Shelter T-01.

> "I thought I was done."

> "I thought I forgot."

> "I thought I moved on."

> "So why did it come back?"

> "Why am I still like this?"

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services determined that Corin's worsening condition was not caused by losing progress.

The progress remained.

The relationships remained.

The trust remained.

However, recovery had never meant the absence of painful memories.

The return of old wounds did not erase everything built afterward.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Shelter T-01 remains occupied.

The white tiger remains beneath the tree.

The snowy owl remains among the branches.

Both continue staying near Corin despite the worsening weather.

The storm has separated Corin from her visitors temporarily.

But the distance created by the snow is not the same as the distance created by fear.

The first can pass.

The second must be healed.

For now...

Corin sits alone in her treehouse.

Listening to the storm.

Wondering why the past returned.

Not knowing that, outside her window...

Two old companions are still waiting.

**End of Psychological Follow-up Report**
