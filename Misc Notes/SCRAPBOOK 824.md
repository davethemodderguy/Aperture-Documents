# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

SPAWN's relationship with the anomalous realms surrounding Aperture is considerably more complicated than previously assumed.

SPAWN does not regard the **Starlands** as merely another anomalous location.

They regard it as **Heaven.**

Likewise, the **Banlands** are regarded as the **Afterlife**.

Both realms hold profound religious significance to SPAWN.

SPAWN is also aware of **Lucy Merryweather**, though their knowledge of her is unusual.

Lucy was already known within SPAWN religious circles.

Her status became significantly more concrete after a member of SPAWN High Command reportedly encountered her directly.

Following this encounter, Lucy became an explicitly worshipped figure within portions of SPAWN.

This produces an exceptionally strange strategic situation:

> SPAWN reveres the realms connected to several Aperture residents.

> SPAWN reveres Lucy.

> Aperture houses Lucy's daughters.

> Aperture houses descendants of the Starlands' Guardian Angel lineage.

> Aperture maintains friendly relations with entities responsible for the creation of reality.

> And SPAWN is currently attempting to break into Aperture's Blacksite.

The situation becomes considerably worse if SPAWN personnel injure Blacksite security staff.

Because Alice has now developed a specific emotional association between SPAWN and **people who hurt her friends.**

That is not a favorable development for SPAWN.

> **“Fifteen stacks of documents per day.”**

Lucy's response:

> “Only fifteen?”

This was determined to be a joke.

Probably.

Her visits to Aperture therefore provide something considerably more appealing:

- familiar residents,
    
- conversation,
    
- tea,
    
- unusual events,
    
- and occasionally something interesting happening that isn't another administrative report.
    

One particularly common explanation for her visits is:

> **“I have something better to watch than paperwork.”**

C.O.R.E. has elected not to disagree.

# 7. THEORETICAL SPAWN ATTACK

The following scenario assumes SPAWN conducts a successful enough breach to reach the Blacksite interior.

It further assumes that SPAWN personnel injure or kill Aperture security personnel during the breach.

This is considered the most dangerous possible escalation short of directly harming a protected resident.

The expected response would not be a single counterattack.

It would be **layered retaliation.**

---

# 8. FIRST RESPONSE — SECURITY

The Blacksite's conventional security infrastructure would respond first.

Doors would lock.

Internal routes would close.

Security personnel would be mobilized.

Intruders would become isolated from one another.

Escape routes would begin disappearing.

This phase would still resemble a normal security response.

SPAWN might therefore believe they are succeeding.

That assumption would be incorrect.

---

# 9. SECOND RESPONSE — RESIDENTS

The Blacksite is not staffed exclusively by professional security personnel.

It is a residence.

The residents care about one another.

If SPAWN personnel hurt security guards protecting their home, the situation ceases to be:

> **“Intruders have entered the facility.”**

It becomes:

> **“Someone hurt our people.”**

This distinction matters.

Particularly to Alice.

---

# 10. ALICE

Alice's response is expected to be immediate.

Her physical strength remains low.

She is still physically weak.

She is still capable of being injured.

She can still become exhausted.

None of this changes.

What changes is her willingness to use the axe.

Alice already has an established pattern of defending the Blacksite.

SPAWN personnel have previously encountered her.

They have already learned that she is dangerous.

They have **not**, however, necessarily experienced Alice operating under the motivation of:

> **“They hurt the guards.”**

That is a substantially worse scenario.

---

# 11. PROJECTED ALICE ESCALATION

Alice's likely progression:

### PHASE 1 — IDENTIFICATION

Alice determines that SPAWN personnel are responsible.

### PHASE 2 — INTERCEPTION

She attempts to prevent further movement through the residential area.

### PHASE 3 — WARNING

If circumstances permit, Alice may issue a verbal warning.

Something along the lines of:

> “Leave.”

### PHASE 4 — BLOOD-FEEDING

If SPAWN continues advancing or continues harming residents, Alice may activate her axe.

The white blade becomes red.

This is the point at which C.O.R.E. considers the encounter a **severe threat event.**

### PHASE 5 — PURSUIT

Alice is not expected to abandon the Blacksite simply because SPAWN retreats.

If she determines that the hostile group represents an ongoing threat, pursuit becomes possible.

---

# 12. IMPORTANT LIMITATION

Alice's blood-feeding ability does **not** make her physically stronger.

This must be emphasized.

She does not suddenly become a superhuman.

She does not gain enhanced musculature.

She does not become immune to injury.

The axe becomes stronger.

Alice does not.

Consequently, her use of the red blade is a dangerous trade.

She sacrifices her own blood and physical condition to increase the weapon's effectiveness.

This is precisely why C.O.R.E. considers Alice's determination more concerning than her physical capability.

She is willing to spend something she cannot easily replace.

# 16. C.O.R.E. RETALIATION ASSESSMENT

It is important to note that Aperture has no standing policy of indiscriminate retaliation against organizations merely for attempting reconnaissance.

SPAWN has already conducted multiple operations.

Aperture has tolerated a considerable amount.

The threshold changes when personnel begin **hurting residents or security staff.**

At that point, retaliation becomes considerably more likely.

However, Aperture's greatest advantage is not necessarily the ability to destroy SPAWN.

It is the sheer number of relationships and capabilities available to it.

A hostile organization would have to account for:

> **Aperture security**

then

> **Aperture residents**

then

> **anomalous entities**

then

> **external allies**

then

> **realm authorities**

and potentially, at the extreme end,

> **beings responsible for reality itself.**

SPAWN's current planning does not account for most of these layers.

**A home for Lucy Merryweather's daughters.**
