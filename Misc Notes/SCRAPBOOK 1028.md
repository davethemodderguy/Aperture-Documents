# Executive Summary

Neri's recent severe anemia has **substantially reduced her physical stamina**.

This does **not** mean that Neri is unable to walk.

It means that her ability to walk is currently unreliable and limited by exhaustion.

Her present condition is best described as:

> **Mobile, but with severely reduced endurance.**

Neri can still move independently when she has enough energy.

The problem is that personnel cannot reliably predict how far she can walk before exhaustion forces her to stop.

---

# Current Mobility Limits

## 1. Neri Can Walk

Neri remains capable of ordinary independent walking.

She can:

- get out of bed;
    
- walk through her room;
    
- walk to the cafeteria;
    
- walk through corridors;
    
- move between nearby locations;
    
- wander around the Blacksite when she has sufficient energy.
    

She does not require permanent assistance simply to stand or walk.

---

## 2. Distance Is the Primary Problem

The further Neri travels on foot, the more likely exhaustion becomes.

A short walk may be completely uneventful.

A longer trip can leave her severely fatigued.

There is no reliable distance threshold because her stamina varies according to her current physiological state.

Therefore:

> **"She walked there yesterday" does not mean she can walk there today.**

---

## 3. Endurance Is More Limited Than Basic Mobility

Neri's problem is not primarily inability to move her limbs.

It is **how long she can keep moving.**

She may begin walking normally and gradually slow down.

She may stop unexpectedly.

She may sit down and remain there for a long period.

She may decide that continuing is not worth the energy expenditure.

This can happen without a dramatic medical episode.

---

## 4. Prolonged Standing Can Also Be Difficult

Neri may choose to sit rather than remain standing for extended periods.

This is particularly common when she is already tired.

If she has reached a quiet location and sits down, there is no requirement for her to immediately continue moving.

She may remain there for a considerable amount of time.

---

# When Transportation Becomes Appropriate

Transportation is appropriate when:

- Neri is already visibly exhausted;
    
- the destination is far enough that walking would likely overexert her;
    
- she has already been moving for an extended period;
    
- she stops because she no longer has the stamina to continue;
    
- she voluntarily asks for assistance;
    
- or personnel have reason to believe continuing on foot would be medically unwise.
    

This does **not** mean someone should automatically carry her every time she walks.

The purpose is to conserve limited stamina, not eliminate her independence.

---

# Current Transportation Options

### Alice's Scooter

Useful for longer distances through appropriate areas.

Neri can ride rather than spend her limited stamina walking.

### Jane Doe

Jane can provide direct transportation, including piggybacking Neri when necessary.

Neri has demonstrated that she is willing to accept this.

### Other Residents

Depending on circumstances, Neri may be:

- carried;
    
- supported while walking;
    
- transported by scooter or another available vehicle;
    
- or assisted using appropriate mobility equipment.
    

---

# The Stationary Periods

Neri may remain in one location for **very long periods.**

This can mean:

> She is tired.

It can also mean:

> She simply wants to be there.

These cannot automatically be distinguished from observation alone.

Her preference for quiet places remains completely genuine.

Therefore, a stationary Neri should not automatically trigger social intervention.

---

# What Neri Is Currently NOT

Neri is **not permanently bedridden.**

Neri is **not incapable of walking.**

Neri does **not** require someone to physically accompany her everywhere.

Neri does **not** need to be carried simply because she is capable of being carried.

Neri does **not** need to prove that she can walk independently.

The issue is stamina.

---

# Practical Rule

The simplest operational description is:

> **Neri can walk until she can't.**

And because her limit is currently unpredictable, personnel should allow transportation to remain available.

If she walks somewhere successfully, good.

If she decides to ride instead, also good.

If she sits down for two hours because she is exhausted, let her rest.

If she wants to walk again afterward, let her walk.

If she wants a ride, give her one.

---

# Medical Context

Her regenerative ability does not negate this limitation.

Neri can regenerate damaged tissue while still suffering from inadequate blood function and reduced cardiovascular stamina.

Regeneration repairs.

It does not automatically restore the physiological resources required for sustained exertion.

Her recent anemia therefore remains the relevant factor in her reduced mobility.

---

# C.O.R.E. Assessment

Neri's current mobility should be understood as **variable and energy-limited**, rather than absent.

She remains independently mobile under favorable conditions.

However, she currently has a much smaller physical endurance reserve than she had previously.

Accordingly:

**Walking is permitted.**

**Resting is permitted.**

**Accepting transportation is permitted.**

**Stopping halfway is permitted.**

**Nobody gets to turn any of these into a test of independence.**

---

# Archivist Note

The important distinction is this:

Neri hasn't forgotten how to walk.

She's just running on a much smaller battery.

And unfortunately, there is no battery icon above her head telling us whether she's at 80%, 30%, or "Jane, please carry me."

So we let her decide when she needs a ride whenever she is able to do so.

If she climbs onto Alice's scooter, fine.

If she climbs onto Jane's back, fine.

If she walks under her own power, fine.

If she reaches a hallway, sits down, and stays there for three hours with a coffee:

Also fine.
