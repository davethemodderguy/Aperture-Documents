# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

After three days of searching, the hidden Night Stalker model has finally been found.

It was discovered by:

> **Elsher — MSAC**

The model was located before any non-MSAC personnel could claim it.

**MSAC HAS WON.**

However, Elsher already possesses an authorized Night Stalker.

The discovery therefore does not result in a second Night Stalker authorization.

Instead, Neri has apparently decided to provide Elsher with:

> **“Something special later.”**

The nature of this reward remains unknown.

---

# 2. THE DISCOVERY

At approximately 09:18, Elsher was conducting an exterior search following Neri's second hint:

> **“Go touch some grass.”**

Unlike several other operators, Elsher did not immediately begin searching the grass itself.

He stopped.

Looked around.

Then walked toward an area adjacent to the main exterior grounds.

He noticed an object that appeared slightly out of place.

He approached.

It was the plastic Night Stalker model.

Elsher picked it up.

He stared at it for several seconds.

Then:

> **“Found it.”**

The MSAC channel went silent.

# 6. WHY ELSHER FOUND IT

The MSAC search team reviewed the discovery.

Several operators had spent considerable time searching the same general exterior region.

Elsher did something different.

He stopped looking for **a gun.**

Instead, he looked for something that appeared **deliberately out of place.**

The model was not concealed in a conventional container.

It was not buried.

It was not locked away.

It was simply positioned somewhere where most personnel would overlook it because it looked like part of the surrounding environment.

The hints were therefore technically accurate.

> **“Think outside the box.”**

And:

> **“Go touch some grass.”**

Elsher interpreted them literally enough to find the relevant area, but broadly enough to recognize that the answer was not necessarily _inside_ the grass.
