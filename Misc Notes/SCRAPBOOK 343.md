# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **Z-335 SOCIAL OBSERVATION**

# **SPECIAL TREATMENT OF LUMI**

**Classification:** Allied Entity Behavioral Study  
**Subject:** Lumi / Z-335 (Marts)

---

# **OVERVIEW**

While Marts are universally friendly toward personnel, C.O.R.E. has documented that **Lumi consistently receives treatment unlike that given to anyone else.**

This behavior is not the result of training or instruction.

It appears to be instinctive.

To every known Mart, Lumi is simply:

> **"Mom."**

Attempts to teach the Marts to use terms such as "Lumi," "Miss Lumi," or "Doctor Lumi" have been unsuccessful.

They invariably return to:

> **"Mom."**

or

> **"Mother."**

---

# **AUTOMATIC GATHERING**

Whenever Lumi enters an area containing Marts, nearby individuals immediately become aware of her presence.

Within minutes, Marts begin gathering around her.

Typical behavior includes:

- Following her through hallways.
    
- Sitting on her shoulders.
    
- Resting in her lap.
    
- Floating alongside her.
    
- Bringing her small objects.
    
- Offering cheese crackers.
    
- Asking if she has eaten.
    
- Waving enthusiastically.
    

Researchers have observed no verbal coordination.

The colony simply seems to know.

---

# **THE "MOM CHECK"**

Several times each day, Marts voluntarily perform what personnel have nicknamed the **"Mom Check."**

A small group approaches Lumi and asks simple questions.

Examples include:

> "Mom okay?"

> "Mom happy?"

> "Hungry?"

> "Water?"

If Lumi answers positively, the Marts happily disperse.

If she appears tired or upset, additional Marts quietly remain nearby.

---

# **PRIORITY CARE**

Should Lumi become injured, exhausted, or ill, the Marts immediately suspend most non-essential activities.

A spontaneous care rotation forms within minutes.

Observed behaviors include:

- Cooling Pad Form for fevers.
    
- Blanket Form if she feels cold.
    
- Bringing drinking water.
    
- Retrieving medical staff.
    
- Remaining nearby while she rests.
    
- Politely asking visitors to keep their voices down.
    

No commands are required.

---

# **GIFTS**

Marts regularly present Lumi with gifts.

Common offerings include:

- Cheese crackers.
    
- Flowers.
    
- Interesting pebbles.
    
- Lost stationery returned to its owner.
    
- Drawings made with washable markers.
    
- Tiny paper hats folded from scrap paper.
    

Most gifts have little material value.

Lumi keeps nearly all of them.

---

# **PROTECTIVE INSTINCTS**

Although Marts are friendly toward everyone, they become noticeably protective when someone raises their voice at Lumi.

They do not become aggressive.

Instead, they simply surround her.

One Mart may stand between Lumi and the other person.

Others quietly ask:

> "Mom okay?"

The behavior generally de-escalates situations before staff intervention is required.

---

# **THE NEST'S WELCOME**

Whenever Lumi visits the Mart Nest in Project SAFEHOUSE, the colony greets her enthusiastically.

Hundreds of voices echo almost simultaneously:

> **"MOM!"**

The sound can reportedly be heard from outside the room.

Lumi always answers.

> "...Hello, everyone."

The room immediately becomes even louder.

---

# **LUMI'S RESPONSE**

When asked whether she minded being called "Mom," Lumi smiled.

> "...Not at all."

She looked around at the sea of blue blobs.

> "...I never expected to become a mother."

A Mart climbed into her lap.

> "Mom."

She gently adjusted its tiny top hat.

> "...But I'm glad I did."

---

# **STAR'S OBSERVATION**

Star has watched this interaction countless times.

> "...The funny part is..."

She laughed softly.

> "...Lumi still tries to count them whenever they all run over."

Lumi immediately replied:

> "...I keep losing count around fifty."

Several nearby Marts proudly shouted:

> "Fifty-one!"

None of them were actually the fifty-first.

---

# **DIALYN'S COMMENT**

> "...I've seen important researchers greeted with handshakes."

> "...I've seen directors greeted with salutes."

She watched Lumi disappear beneath a pile of happy Marts.

> "...I've never seen anyone else greeted by three hundred children yelling 'Mom.'"

---

# **AMBASSADOR'S EXPLANATION**

The Blacksite Ambassador was asked why every Mart calls Lumi "Mom."

It answered without hesitation.

> "...She made us."

The researcher asked:

> "...So that's why?"

The Ambassador smiled.

> "...No."

It pointed toward Lumi as she laughed with several Marts.

> "...She loves us."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Late one evening, Lumi finished her work and sat quietly in the SAFEHOUSE common room.

Without being called, several Marts drifted over.

One settled beside her.

Another rested in her lap.

A third leaned gently against her shoulder.

No one spoke for several minutes.

Eventually, one tiny voice broke the silence.

> **"...Love you, Mom."**

Lumi's eyes watered.

She smiled and softly replied:

> "...I love you too."

Within seconds, dozens of other tiny voices echoed throughout the room.

> **"Love you, Mom."**

It has become one of the few phrases every Mart understands exactly the same way.

**End of Social Observation**
