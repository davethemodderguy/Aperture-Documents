### **RESEARCH MEMORANDUM**

## **SUBJECT: DUAL MANIFESTATION PHENOMENA – COMPARATIVE OBSERVATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Research Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the appearance of Corin and Corey as two physically independent individuals, Research Division requested consultation from Neko.

The request was considered appropriate.

Unlike most personnel, Neko has firsthand experience with a comparable phenomenon.

Namely...

Miss Neko.

---

# **COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS**

Although the circumstances differ considerably, Research Division noted several similarities.

Both cases involve two physically separate individuals sharing a common origin.

Both demonstrate an unusually deep understanding of one another.

Both display strong emotional synchronization.

Both experience adverse effects when separated for prolonged periods.

The similarities end there.

Research has found no evidence that the two phenomena are caused by the same mechanism.

---

# **NEKO & MISS NEKO**

According to existing personnel records, Miss Neko represents aspects of Neko's personality that became independently expressed through anomalous circumstances.

Personnel frequently summarize the relationship as follows:

- Neko represents creativity, curiosity, spontaneity, and improvisation.
    
- Miss Neko represents restraint, responsibility, organization, and long-term judgment.
    

Neither is considered "more complete" than the other.

They simply embody different aspects of the same origin while existing as independent individuals.

Over time, both developed into distinct people with their own preferences, habits, and experiences.

---

# **NEKO'S OBSERVATION**

After several days speaking with Corin and Corey, Neko submitted the following conclusion.

> "Don't call one of them the original."

> "You'll upset both of them."

When asked whether Corin and Corey reminded him of himself and Miss Neko, he shrugged.

> "Not really."

> "We're similar."

> "...But they're twins."

---

# **MISS NEKO'S COMMENT**

Miss Neko reviewed Neko's report before submission.

She added one sentence beneath his conclusion.

> "Shared origins do not prevent independent lives."

The statement was accepted into the official record without revision.

---

# **RESEARCH NOTES**

Neko later explained that people often make the same mistake when observing dual manifestations.

They assume one individual must be "real."

The other must therefore be a copy.

Neko rejected that assumption.

During the research meeting, he drew two circles connected by a line.

He labeled one:

> **Corin**

The other:

> **Corey**

Then he wrote beneath them:

> **"Different experiences."

> "Equal existence."**

He repeated the same diagram using his own and Miss Neko's names.
