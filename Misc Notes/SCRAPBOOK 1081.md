# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### WELFARE & ANOMALOUS ENTITY MANAGEMENT DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-NERI-188  
**CLASSIFICATION:** INTERNAL — SAFEHOUSE / BEHAVIORAL  
**STATUS:** ACTIVE OBSERVATION  
**SUBJECT:** Neri Z-4C-NERI  
**FORM:** Plush / Chibi Humanoid  
**OBSERVATION:** Unusually unintelligent behavior during plush state

---

# 1. SUMMARY

Neri is extraordinarily intelligent.

This is well established.

She is an exceptional chemist, maintains extensive research archives, routinely produces work beyond Neko's understanding in her specialty, and has demonstrated an unusually high capacity for theoretical reasoning.

However:

**Plush Neri is sometimes unbelievably stupid.**

There is currently no evidence that this represents cognitive damage.

Her intelligence appears to remain completely intact.

She simply occasionally does things that make personnel question whether the world's foremost young chemist has temporarily misplaced her brain.

---

# 2. CHARACTERISTIC BEHAVIOR

Plush Neri has been observed:

- staring at an object she was holding;
    
- forgetting what she was holding;
    
- looking for the object;
    
- discovering it was still in her hands;
    
- becoming visibly confused;
    
- then continuing as if nothing happened.
    

She has also occasionally attempted to climb onto furniture that was approximately the same height as herself.

She succeeded.

She then appeared surprised that she had succeeded.

---

# 3. THE DOOR INCIDENT

Neri encountered a closed door while in plush form.

She stood in front of it.

She looked at it.

She looked at the handle.

She looked at herself.

She attempted to push the door.

Nothing happened.

She attempted to push it again.

Nothing happened.

She then sat down.

A nearby employee opened the door from the other side.

Neri immediately stood up and walked through.

No acknowledgment of the previous difficulty was provided.

---

# 4. THE COFFEE INCIDENT

Neri was given her usual coffee.

She held the cup.

She stared at it.

She apparently forgot she was holding it.

She searched the table for her coffee.

The cup remained in her hands.

After approximately twelve seconds, she looked down.

There was a pause.

She quietly said:

> "...Oh."

She drank it.

---

# 5. THE WALL

One particularly notable incident involved Neri attempting to reach Alice.

Alice was standing on the opposite side of a transparent glass partition.

Neri approached.

She saw Alice.

She attempted to walk directly toward her.

She walked into the glass.

She stopped.

She looked at the glass.

She looked at Alice.

Alice waved.

Neri walked into the glass again.

Jane Doe, observing from nearby, reportedly said:

> "She knows glass exists."

Neko responded:

> "She does."

Pause.

> "Apparently not today."

---

# 6. THE CHEMISTRY CONTRADICTION

The behavior becomes considerably stranger when compared with Neri's normal cognitive performance.

Earlier the same day, Neri completed a highly complex chemical model involving multiple interacting variables.

She identified an inconsistency in the model that Neko had not noticed.

Approximately twenty minutes later, while still in plush form, she attempted to open a cabinet by pulling on a handle labelled:

> **PUSH**

She pulled.

Nothing happened.

She pulled harder.

Nothing happened.

She looked at the label.

She pulled again.

Neko eventually opened it for her.

Neri entered the cabinet.

Neko stared after her.

> "You solved a molecular interaction problem this morning."

Neri looked back.

> "...Yes."

> "And you couldn't read 'PUSH.'"

Neri stared.

> "...No."

---

# 7. POSSIBLE EXPLANATIONS

C.O.R.E. has considered several hypotheses.

### A. Cognitive impairment

**Rejected.**

Neri's cognitive performance outside these episodes remains normal.

### B. Plush-form neurological alteration

**Unconfirmed.**

There is no evidence of actual neurological deterioration.

### C. Fatigue

**Possible.**

Neri spends considerably less effort on structured cognition while plush.

### D. Reduced inhibition

**Highly plausible.**

Neri may simply care less about appearing intelligent while in plush form.

### E. She is just being stupid sometimes.

**Also plausible.**

---

# 8. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Personnel should not confuse:

**"Neri is extremely intelligent."**

with:

**"Neri must behave intelligently at all times."**

She is ten years old.

She is allowed to have moments where her brain appears to leave the building.

Plush form seems to make this considerably more frequent.

It may be because she feels safe enough to stop monitoring herself.

It may be because she is tired.

It may be because plush Neri has approximately three active thoughts at any given time.

C.O.R.E. has not determined which explanation is correct.

---

# 9. STAFF OBSERVATION

The most consistent pattern is that Neri's intelligence remains available when she **needs** it.

If something genuinely matters:

She thinks.

If someone is in danger:

She notices.

If chemistry is involved:

She becomes terrifyingly competent.

If she is sitting on someone's lap with no responsibilities:

Her brain apparently clocks out.

This is not considered harmful.

In fact, several personnel have suggested that the behavior may represent one of the few times Neri allows herself to simply be a child.

# 11. NERI'S RESPONSE

When informed that personnel had begun documenting these incidents, Neri stared at the report.

She read several entries.

She became visibly embarrassed.

She pulled her oversized hoodie over her face.

After approximately ten seconds, she peeked back out.

Jane asked:

> "Are you embarrassed?"

Neri nodded.

Jane asked:

> "Do you want me to delete it?"

Neri shook her head.

Pause.

Neri pointed at the section describing her walking into the glass.

Jane:

> "That one?"

Neri nodded.

Jane:

> "You want it deleted?"

Neri nodded.

Jane deleted it.

Neri then immediately walked into the same glass.

---

# 12. FINAL ASSESSMENT

There is no indication that Neri's intelligence has decreased.

There is substantial evidence that her **willingness to use it constantly has.**

This distinction is important.

Neri has spent much of her life being valued for what she can do.

Her chemistry.

Her intelligence.

Her usefulness.

Her ability to solve problems.

Plush Neri appears to occasionally choose the opposite.

She can be brilliant.

She can be useless.

She can be carried around.

She can fall asleep.

She can bite someone's sleeve.

She can stare at a door for thirty seconds.

She can forget why she walked into a room.

And nobody takes her place away because of it.

For Neri, that may be more important than the behavior itself.
