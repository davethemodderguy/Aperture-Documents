# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE RESIDENTIAL CARE LOG**

# **CORIN — THE WEEK WITHOUT ORPHIE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Duration:** 7 Days  
**Primary Caregiver:** SAFEHOUSE Residents  
**Status:** **Stable / Supervised**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie was required to leave the Treehouse for an extended period.

This created one immediate concern:

**Corin must not be left alone.**

Following her recent illness and recovery, SAFEHOUSE residents agreed to collectively look after her until Orphie's return.

A simple schedule was established.

Corin would spend time with every resident.

The arrangement quickly became less of a formal medical rotation and more of a week-long SAFEHOUSE sleepover.

---

# **THE ONE RULE**

### **DO NOT LEAVE CORIN ALONE.**

No exceptions.

Corin could sleep.

She could wander.

She could sit inside one of her beloved containers.

She could loaf.

She could fly around the Treehouse.

But someone needed to be nearby.

The rule was not intended to restrict Corin.

It existed because everyone knew how badly she handled prolonged isolation.

---

# **DAY 1 — BELLE**

Belle took the first shift.

Corin spent most of the day following her around.

When Belle worked on Krampus Compliance Authority documents, Corin sat beside her.

When Belle took a break, Corin climbed into her lap.

When Belle returned to work, Corin curled up beneath her wings.

Eventually, Corin fell asleep against Belle's arm.

Belle quietly continued working.

---

# **DAY 2 — ENDMIN

Endmin spent most of her day with Corin.

The two eventually ended up sitting together on the floor.

Corin discovered Endmin's Ether composition was interesting.

She stared at her for several minutes.

Endmin stared back.

Neither said anything.

Eventually, Corin loafed.

Endmin gently poked her forehead.

Corin responded with a tiny:

> **"Mew."**

---

# **DAY 3 — NEKO & MISS NEKO**

Corin spent much of Day 3 with the two Neko residents.

There was considerable yarn involved.

Corin chased it.

Neko rolled it.

Miss Neko watched.

Corin eventually became tired and crawled into a container.

Neko attempted to retrieve her.

Corin refused to leave.

Miss Neko simply moved the container somewhere safe.

Problem solved.

---

# **DAY 4 — NIKO**

Niko kept Corin company during a quieter day.

Corin sat beside him while he worked.

Eventually, she climbed onto his lap and fell asleep.

Niko remained completely still for nearly an hour.

When asked why he didn't move, he replied:

> **"She's comfortable."**

Nobody argued.

---

# **DAY 5 — FROST & BAO**

Frost and Bao took care of Corin together.

The temperature was kept comfortable.

Corin spent most of the afternoon resting near them.

Bao prepared small meals.

Frost made sure Corin's environment remained cool without becoming uncomfortable.

Corin eventually spread her wings and used them as a blanket.

She fell asleep between them.

---

# **DAY 6 — TAIGA**

Taiga spent most of the day with Corin.

They sat together on the floor.

Taiga eventually lay down.

Corin climbed onto her.

Then loafed.

Then fell asleep.

Taiga did not move.

Several hours later, both were still there.

---

# **DAY 7 — ASTRAL & MART**

Astral and Mart handled the final day.

Corin spent the morning flying around the Treehouse.

Her strength had continued improving.

She landed beside them.

Then immediately climbed into a jar.

Mart stared.

Astral sighed.

> **"Again?"**

Corin:

> **"Mew."**

The jar remained open.

---

# **EVENINGS**

Regardless of who was responsible during the day, the residents made sure someone stayed with Corin at night.

Corin could sleep peacefully knowing someone was nearby.

Sometimes she slept in her normal form.

Sometimes she returned to her miniature feline form.

Sometimes she slept in someone's lap.

Sometimes she slept inside a container.

Nobody questioned it anymore.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Despite Orphie's absence, Corin remained surprisingly calm.

She clearly missed Orphie.

She occasionally looked toward the Treehouse entrance.

But she never became completely isolated.

Someone was always there.

Someone to hold her.

Someone to talk to.

Someone to feed her.

Someone to refill her cup.

Someone to hear her tiny meows.

---

# **CARE ASSESSMENT**

The week demonstrated that Corin does not necessarily require one specific person to remain beside her at all times.

What she needs most is **company**.

The presence of trusted people appears to provide enough emotional security for her to remain stable.

The rule therefore remains:

> **Don't leave Corin alone.**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

For seven days, Corin belonged to everyone.

Belle held her.

Endmin watched over her.

Neko and Miss Neko played with her.

Niko sat quietly with her.

Bao and Frost fed her.

Taiga slept beside her.

Astral and Mart supervised her final day.

Everyone took a turn.

Nobody let her be alone.

And when Orphie finally returned, she found Corin sitting peacefully in the middle of SAFEHOUSE.

Corin looked at her.

Her ears perked up.

Her two tails moved.

Then she ran straight toward her.

> **"Mew!"**

For the first time all week, Corin sounded completely happy.

**End of SAFEHOUSE Residential Care Log**
