# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. DIVISION — SUBSPACE SAFETY UPDATE

**SUBSPACE DESIGNATION:** NERI-01  
**PRIMARY USER:** Neri  
**PREVIOUS HAZARD:** OUT OF BOUNDS  
**CURRENT STATUS:** RESOLVED  
**MISSING PERSONNEL:** RECOVERED

---

# 1. INCIDENT RESOLUTION

Following the disappearance of an Archivist within Neri-01, Neri began modifying the subspace to prevent further accidental loss of personnel.

Rather than attempting to place an invisible wall around the existing islands, Neri chose a simpler solution:

> **Make the island bigger.**

The primary landmass was expanded substantially, increasing the amount of usable surface area surrounding Neri's room and equipment.

Neri subsequently added two additional features:

- **Localized gravity**
    
- **Rope bridges connecting the islands**
    

The resulting environment is significantly easier to navigate.

---

# 2. EXPANDED ISLAND

The original floating islands were relatively small and separated by large areas of open space.

Neri increased the size of the primary landmass.

This dramatically reduced the likelihood of someone accidentally jumping beyond the usable region.

The expanded island now provides considerably more room for:

- walking;
    
- reading;
    
- laboratory work;
    
- equipment storage;
    
- resting;
    
- socializing;
    
- and ordinary movement.
    

Neri's original belongings remain distributed throughout the subspace.

The room consequently feels less like several disconnected fragments and more like a small floating environment.

---

# 3. GRAVITY

Neri also implemented localized gravity.

Prior to the modification, movement through the subspace could become effectively weightless.

This was the primary reason excessive jumps were dangerous.

Once sufficient momentum was acquired, there was little to naturally slow the individual down.

Neri's solution was straightforward:

> **Make things fall again.**

The subspace now possesses a stable gravitational direction.

Individuals can jump normally and will return toward the islands rather than continuing indefinitely through the void.

The effect is localized to Neri-01 and does not alter gravity within the SAFEHOUSE itself.

---

# 4. ROPE BRIDGES

Neri connected the floating islands using rope bridges.

The bridges provide conventional walking routes between areas that previously required jumping or floating.

This was apparently Neri's own idea.

When asked why she added them:

> **"So people don't jump."**

Archivist:

> **"You could have just told them not to."**

Neri:

> **"They did it anyway."**

Archivist:

> **"...Fair."**

The bridges have subsequently become the preferred method of movement between islands.

---

# 5. THE ARCHIVIST IS FOUND

The missing Archivist was eventually located.

They were discovered approximately:

> **250 meters from the nearest island.**

The individual was found alive.

The distance provided an important confirmation of Neri's earlier concerns.

The Out-of-Bounds region was not merely a small area immediately surrounding the islands.

A person could travel a considerable distance before becoming stranded.

The Archivist's location also provided C.O.R.E. with the first reliable measurement of how far an individual could drift from the constructed environment.

---

# 6. RECOVERY

The Archivist was recovered and returned to the SAFEHOUSE.

Their first reported statement upon returning was:

> **"I'm never going in there again."**

Neri reportedly apologized.

> **Neri:** "Sorry."

> **Archivist:** "It's okay."

Pause.

> **Archivist:** "Please put a floor on everything next time."

Neri:

> **"There is a floor."**

Archivist:

> **"THERE WASN'T."**

# 8. OUT-OF-BOUNDS STATUS

The original Out-of-Bounds hazard has been **substantially mitigated.**

It has not been proven impossible to leave the constructed environment.

However:

- gravity prevents indefinite weightless drifting from ordinary jumps;
    
- expanded islands provide substantially more recovery space;
    
- rope bridges eliminate unnecessary jumping;
    
- Neri is aware of the boundary issue;
    
- and residents have been instructed not to deliberately test the remaining limits.
    

The term **"Out of Bounds"** remains in use.

Neko has refused to rename it.

His explanation:

> **"The bug is fixed. The name stays."**

## FINAL OBSERVATION

Neri's first attempt at designing a personal subspace produced a room where an Archivist could jump too hard and disappear into an effectively weightless void.

Neri's second attempt produced:

**A larger island.**

**Gravity.**

**Rope bridges.**

And, importantly:

**The Archivist came back.**

Neri has therefore demonstrated one of the more useful traits associated with her rapidly developing Architect-derived abilities:

She can make mistakes.

She can recognize them.

And she can **change reality until the mistake is no longer a problem.**
