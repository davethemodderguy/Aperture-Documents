# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL TRANSITION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – RETURN TO HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

After an extended period residing at KCA Headquarters, Corin Lockwood has officially relocated to Heaven's Blacksite.

The transition was conducted without incident.

No panic event occurred.

No emergency response was required.

No attempt was made to force interaction.

For the first time in a long period...

Corin simply arrived.

And stayed.

---

# **NEW ARRANGEMENT**

Due to Corin's current psychological and physical condition, Medical Services recommended against frequent movement throughout the facility.

Not as a restriction.

As support.

Corin has been assigned a private office within the Blacksite.

The room contains:

- Accounting equipment.
    
- Medical supplies.
    
- A resting area.
    
- Personal items provided by SAFEHOUSE residents.
    
- A small tea station.
    
- A comfortable sleeping arrangement.
    

Personnel were instructed that Corin's office is not a place of isolation.

It is a place where she can recover at her own pace.

---

# **DAILY OBSERVATIONS**

The change has been subtle.

Corin does not attend every gathering.

She does not suddenly become social.

She does not pretend everything is normal.

But she is present.

She walks through the halls occasionally.

She speaks when spoken to.

She accepts tea from Bao.

She allows Belle to visit.

She listens when Norma talks.

She occasionally sits with Arcturus without needing to say anything.

For many personnel, this alone is considered significant progress.

---

# **CONVERSATION RECORD**

**Location:** Corin's Office  
**Participants:** Corin Lockwood, Lumi Lockwood

---

Corin sat quietly near the window.

Lumi remained beside her.

After several minutes of silence, Corin spoke.

> "Mom..."

Lumi looked toward her.

> "Am I dead?"

There was no fear in the question.

Only exhaustion.

Lumi smiled gently.

Not because the question was easy.

Because she understood why Corin asked.

> "You're here with me."

Corin looked down.

Her hands tightened.

> "But I'm a corpse."

A pause.

> "Held together by Ether."

> "Held together by the same thing that caused so much pain."

Lumi reached toward her.

> "Yet you are here."

> "That is what matters."

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

For several moments, Corin remained silent.

Then everything she had carried for years surfaced.

> "Fifteen years, Mom."

Her voice shook.

> "Fifteen years."

She looked toward the floor.

> "People bullied me."

> "They looked at me like I was something wrong."

Her breathing became uneven.

> "They captured me again."

> "They took me apart."

A pause.

> "They took my eye."

Lumi remained silent.

Corin continued.

> "I'm so happy you're here."

> "I really am."

Tears formed.

> "But I keep forgetting the gap."

> "I keep forgetting how much time passed."

Her voice broke.

> "I was taken away from my family."

> "I was killed."

> "I was left there."

> "I was supposed to be gone."

A long silence.

> "And then I was brought back."

> "I never got to choose any of it."

---

# **LUMI'S RESPONSE**

For once...

Lumi had no immediate answer.

No story.

No comforting memory.

No explanation.

Because there was nothing she could say that would undo fifteen years.

She could not take away what happened.

She could not give Corin back the childhood that was stolen.

She could only sit beside her daughter.

And listen.

---

# **AFTERMATH**

Eventually, Corin became quiet.

The exhaustion returned.

She did not ask for help.

She did not ask anyone to stay.

She simply returned to her room.

Later that night, personnel discovered that Corin had chosen an unusual sleeping arrangement.

Not her assigned bed.

Not her office couch.

The floor beside Taiga.

When asked why, Corin only answered:

> "...It's less lonely."

Taiga did not question it.

She simply moved slightly closer.

---

# **LUMI'S OBSERVATION**

That evening, Lumi remained nearby.

She did not attempt to convince Corin that everything was fine.

Because everything was not fine.

Instead, she stayed.

The same way she had when Corin was a child.

The same way she would have if she had been there during those missing years.

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the report.

His note was brief.

> "Corin does not need someone to erase her past."

> "She needs someone willing to stand beside her while she carries it."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin Lockwood is now considered a resident of Heaven's Blacksite.

Her recovery remains ongoing.

She is not expected to immediately return to her previous personality.

She is not expected to forget.

She is not expected to heal on anyone else's schedule.

For the first time in years...

Corin has a place where she can exist without needing to prove she deserves to.

And every night, beside a snow leopard who understands quiet companionship, she sleeps knowing one thing:

She is no longer alone.

**End of Personnel Transition Report**
