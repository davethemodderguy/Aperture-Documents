# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **KCA-AAR-041**

### **AFTER-ACTION REPORT – EXECUTIVE PROTECTION INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Operational Archive

---

## **MISSION SUMMARY**

**Objective:** Escort Director Belle Merryweather during a joint operation with Aperture Industries.

**Status:** Successful.

**Civilian Casualties:** None.

**Executive Personnel:** Safe.

---

## **INCIDENT**

During the withdrawal phase of the operation, a hostile combatant who had been presumed neutralized emerged from cover and fired toward Director Belle Merryweather.

The engagement occurred at close range.

Belle had only just turned toward Cinder and was unable to react in time.

Before Keith could intercept the line of fire—

Endmin moved.

---

## **EXECUTIVE PROTECTION RESPONSE**

Endmin immediately stepped between Belle and the shooter.

Rather than relying solely on body armor, she instinctively spread her armored wings around Belle, creating a physical shield.

The incoming rounds struck the reinforced wing plating.

The impact echoed through the corridor.

Several rounds ricocheted harmlessly onto the floor.

Others embedded themselves into the armor.

Although the armor prevented penetration, the force of the impacts transferred directly through the wings.

Endmin visibly winced but maintained the shield until the threat was eliminated.

---

## **THREAT NEUTRALIZATION**

Keith immediately identified the shooter's position.

One controlled burst from his modified M1014 ended the engagement.

Cinder secured the surrounding area while Belle remained beside Endmin.

No additional hostile activity was detected.

---

## **MEDICAL REPORT**

Alice examined Endmin shortly afterward.

Findings:

- No penetrating injuries.
    
- Significant bruising beneath the wing armor.
    
- Soft tissue strain caused by repeated ballistic impacts.
    
- No fractures.
    

The protective wing armor, designed and reinforced by **Neko**, performed as intended.

Without the armor, physicians concluded the injuries would likely have been substantially more severe.

---

## **POST-INCIDENT**

As Alice finished applying treatment, Belle remained unusually quiet.

She eventually looked at Endmin.

> **"...You jumped in front of me."

Endmin gave a small smile despite the soreness.

> **"...Of course I did."

Belle lowered her head.

> **"...That must've hurt."

Endmin flexed one wing carefully before laughing through the discomfort.

> **"...Painful..."

She looked at Belle.

> **"...But worth it."

Belle's ears lowered.

Without another word, she hugged Endmin as gently as she could.

---

## **ENGINEERING REMARK**

Neko later inspected the damaged wing armor.

Several deep dents and gouges were visible across the reinforced plates.

After replacing the damaged sections, he quietly stated:

> **"...The armor did exactly what it was built to do."

He looked toward Endmin.

> **"...I'm just glad you didn't have to find out what would've happened without it."

---

---

## **STATUS**

Endmin made a full recovery after several days of rest.

Her wing armor was repaired and returned to service by Neko.

The damaged armor plate has since been retained by Engineering as a demonstration piece during protective equipment briefings.

Engraved on the display is a simple inscription:

> **"Armor can stop a bullet."**

> **"Only courage chooses to stand in front of one."**
