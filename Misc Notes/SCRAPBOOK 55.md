# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **CIVILIAN LIAISON LOG**

### **PROGRAM: TRUSTED VISITORS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Internal Archive

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Several months after **Operation: WAYFARER**, the five rescued wandering traders continued to honor the promise they had made upon leaving Heaven's Blacksite.

Despite countless opportunities, they never disclosed the Blacksite's location, purpose, or personnel to anyone.

As a result, Director Lucious approved an unprecedented arrangement.

The five traders were granted **Trusted Visitor** status.

They became the first—and so far only—non-government civilians permitted to visit the Blacksite on a recurring basis.

---

# **SECURITY PROTOCOL**

Every visit follows strict procedures.

- Visitors are escorted from a predetermined rendezvous point.
    
- Sensitive research laboratories remain restricted.
    
- Executive-level projects remain classified.
    
- All visitors wear temporary visitor keycards.
    
- Access is limited to approved common areas, the cafeteria, medical facilities if needed, and SAFEHOUSE with resident approval.
    

The traders accepted every condition without complaint.

Their response was simple:

> "You trusted us with our lives."

> "The least we can do is return the favor."

---

# **BECOMING FAMILIAR FACES**

Over time, the traders stopped feeling like guests.

They became familiar faces around the Blacksite.

Researchers greeted them by name.

Security personnel waved them through checkpoints after routine verification.

The cafeteria staff learned their favorite meals.

SAFEHOUSE residents started asking when they'd be visiting next.

---

# **THE RESIDENTS**

### **Belle**

Belle was always the first to greet them.

Usually with snacks.

Sometimes with fresh carrots.

Always with a hug—provided the visitor was comfortable with one.

One trader joked:

> "We're pretty sure Director Belle measures friendship in hugs."

Belle neither confirmed nor denied the allegation.

---

### **Endmin**

Endmin remained professional, though noticeably more relaxed around them over time.

She frequently discussed travel routes and wilderness safety with the traders.

---

### **Norma**

Norma became fascinated by the handmade tools and goods they carried.

The traders, in turn, admired her craftsmanship.

It wasn't uncommon to find Norma discussing metallurgy over tea with them.

---

### **Arcturus**

The traders immediately took a liking to the KCA's resident cowboy.

Conversations usually revolved around:

- Horses.
    
- Firearms.
    
- Camping.
    
- Navigation.
    
- Old trade routes.
    

On several occasions, Arcturus accompanied them on portions of their journeys simply because he enjoyed riding through the countryside.

---

### **Neko**

The traders quickly learned one important rule.

Never ask Neko what he's building.

Unless...

You have several free hours.

---

### **Miss Neko**

She became their unofficial guide whenever they visited.

The traders privately admitted they trusted her directions far more than Neko's.

---

### **Star**

Star insisted that every visit include games, stories, or desserts.

Sometimes all three.

---

### **Nightshade**

Though reserved, Nightshade gradually grew accustomed to their presence.

One trader remarked:

> "I think she likes us."

Another replied:

> "I think that's just her version of smiling."

Nightshade neither confirmed nor denied the statement.

---

### **Taiga**

The traders quickly discovered Taiga was an excellent guide through snowy terrain.

She also accepted dried meat as a perfectly acceptable gift.

---

### **Mart**

Mart often exchanged books with the traders.

Several rare regional journals eventually found their way into the Blacksite library thanks to these visits.

---

### **Frost**

Tea time became something of a tradition.

No visit felt complete without it.

---

# **THE UNWRITTEN RULE**

The traders developed one habit on their own.

Whenever conversation drifted toward the Blacksite outside its walls...

They simply changed the subject.

Not because anyone ordered them to.

Because they had given their word.

One trader summarized it best.

> "Some secrets aren't hidden because they're dangerous."

> "They're hidden because they're worth protecting."

# **STATUS**

The five wandering traders continue to visit Heaven's Blacksite whenever their routes bring them nearby.

To most of the world, they remain ordinary merchants traveling from settlement to settlement.

Only they know that, hidden beyond familiar paths, there exists a place where scientists, engineers, executives, Thirens, and cowboys share meals under the same roof.

And despite everything they have seen...

They have never told a soul.

As one researcher wrote beneath the Trusted Visitor registry:

> **"Sometimes the strongest vault isn't made of steel."**

> **"It's built on trust."**
