## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **BEHAVIORAL / MOBILITY OBSERVATION LOG**

# **CORIN TAKES FLIGHT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 — Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Status:** Stable / Increased Social Activity  
**Primary Change:** **Flight adopted as preferred transportation**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Since developing her wings, Corin has gradually become comfortable leaving the treehouse again.

She has discovered that flying is considerably easier than walking.

More importantly, she actually **likes it.**

Flight has therefore become Corin's preferred method of transportation.

The change has had an unexpected secondary effect:

**Corin has started visiting the Blacksite more frequently.**

---

# **WHY SHE FLIES**

Corin's explanation is remarkably simple.

> **"Walking takes too long."**

After a pause:

> **"Flying is fun."**

Her wings allow her to travel between the remote treehouse and the Blacksite without relying on anyone to transport her.

For someone who previously felt dependent on everyone around her, the independence appears particularly meaningful.

She can simply leave.

She can fly.

She can come back whenever she wants.

---

# **RETURN TO THE BLACKSITE**

Corin's visits were initially infrequent.

She would fly to the Blacksite, remain for a short period, and return home.

Over time, the visits became longer.

She began spending time with the SAFEHOUSE residents again.

She eats occasionally.

She talks.

She watches movies.

She sits around during meals.

Sometimes she simply sits in the same room as everyone else.

She does not always participate.

She does not have to.

Being there seems to be enough.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RECEPTION**

The residents quickly adapted to Corin arriving from above.

Neko was the first to notice her landing outside.

He looked through the window.

> **"She's back."**

Belle immediately went outside.

> **"Corin!"**

Corin folded her wings neatly against her back.

> **"Hi."**

Belle stared at the wings.

> **"...Can I touch them?"**

Corin laughed.

> **"Sure."**

This quickly became a regular occurrence.

---

# **NEKO**

Neko remains one of Corin's favorite people to visit.

Partially because he still makes sandwiches.

Corin has resumed eating occasionally when she visits.

Especially when there are pickles.

Neko has also learned that Corin's wings make it possible for her to arrive unexpectedly.

His current rule is:

> **"If you land on the roof, you're helping clean the roof."**

Corin agreed.

She has not cleaned the roof once.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle considers Corin's wings extremely cool.

She has asked numerous questions about them.

> **"Can you fly upside down?"**

> **"Probably."**

> **"Can you carry someone?"**

Corin looked at her.

> **"...Probably."**

Belle smiled.

> **"Can we try?"**

Orphie immediately intervened.

> **"No."**

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie is quietly pleased by the change.

Corin leaving the treehouse voluntarily is something she had not expected to see again so soon.

The fact that Corin can now travel independently is even more significant.

Orphie no longer has to accompany her everywhere.

Corin can simply say:

> **"I'm going to the Blacksite."**

And leave.

That independence appears to have improved Corin's confidence.

---

# **LUMI**

Corin has also begun visiting **Lumi** more often.

The relationship remains complicated.

Corin still remembers the long period in which Lumi stopped visiting the treehouse.

She still has unresolved feelings about it.

But she has started making the journey anyway.

Sometimes she simply appears outside Lumi's workplace.

Lumi will look up.

Corin will be standing there with her wings folded.

> **"Hi, Mom."**

Lumi is still getting used to that.

---

# **LUMI'S REACTION**

Lumi has repeatedly expressed surprise at how quickly Corin can now reach the Blacksite.

During one visit:

> **Lumi:**  
> "You flew all the way here?"

Corin nodded.

> **"It's faster."**

Lumi looked at the wings.

> **"...I suppose it is."**

Corin smiled slightly.

> **"And it's fun."**

Lumi smiled back.

> **"I'm glad."**

---

# **THE FLIGHT HOME**

Corin usually returns to the treehouse before night.

She has become particularly fond of flying during sunset.

The forest looks different from above.

For someone who previously refused to leave the treehouse because she believed there was nothing worth seeing outside, the change is significant.

Corin occasionally stops in midair simply to look around.

---

# **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION**

Flight appears to have provided Corin with something more important than transportation.

It has given her **agency.**

Previously:

> _"I need someone to take me there."_

Now:

> _"I'll go."_

Previously:

> _"I can't leave."_

Now:

> _"I'll fly."_

Previously, the world outside the treehouse represented fear, isolation, and the places where she believed she did not belong.

Now, she can cross that distance herself.

---

# **CURRENT TRAVEL PREFERENCE**

|Transportation|Corin's Preference|
|---|---|
|Walking|Low|
|Vehicle|Low|
|Being carried|Very low|
|Teleportation|Neutral|
|Flying|**Extremely High**|

Current unofficial designation:

> **"Corin's favorite way to get anywhere."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The wings changed more than Corin's mobility.

They changed her relationship with distance.

The treehouse is no longer a place she cannot leave.

The Blacksite is no longer impossibly far away.

Lumi is no longer someone she can only wait for.

The SAFEHOUSE residents are no longer people she can only visit when someone takes her there.

Corin can simply unfold her wings.

Rise into the air.

And go see the people she cares about.

For someone who once believed she belonged nowhere, being able to choose **where she goes** has become surprisingly important.

And apparently, flying is just really fun.

**End of Behavioral / Mobility Observation Log**
