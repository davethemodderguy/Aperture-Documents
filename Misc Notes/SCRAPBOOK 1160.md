# 2. LOTTERY PROCEDURE

The process is intentionally simple.

### STEP 1 — GET A TICKET

Eligible personnel may obtain a Hell Fire Lottery ticket through the designated registration system.

### STEP 2 — WAIT

Results are rolled once every day.

### **DAILY ROLL TIME: 7:30 PM**

### STEP 3 — CHECK

Participants check the published results.

If their ticket wins:

> **WIN STREAK: +1**

If their ticket does not win:

> **WIN STREAK: RESET**

### STEP 4 — REPEAT

The participant must win:

> **1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5**

consecutive daily rolls.

Upon reaching five consecutive wins:

> **ONE HELL FIRE IS AWARDED.**

# 8. THE 7:30 PM EVENT

Every evening at exactly:

> **19:30**

the lottery results are rolled.

The results are then made available to participants.

There is no requirement for participants to gather in one location.

No ceremonial announcement is necessary.

No dramatic music is required.

Participants simply:

1. check their ticket;
    
2. check the result;
    
3. determine whether they won;
    
4. update their streak.
    

The process is intentionally mundane.

The prize is not.

---
