# **BANLANDS GOVERNMENT BIOLOGICAL ARCHIVE**

## **BGBA-TH-020**

### **SUPPLEMENTAL OBSERVATIONS – KEITH & CINDER (SHARK THIREN ANATOMY)**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Personnel Reference

---

## **SUMMARY**

Routine medical examinations have documented a notable similarity between the Shark Thiren siblings, **Keith** and **Cinder**.

Both possess healed injuries at the tip of their tails, consistent with old bite or chewing wounds sustained many years ago.

Neither injury impairs mobility or balance.

The siblings have declined to publicly discuss how the injuries occurred.

---

## **KEITH**

Keith's shark tail is dark in coloration and bears a healed section at its tip where a small portion is missing.

Medical staff concluded the injury is old, fully healed, and causes no functional limitations.

Researchers have occasionally asked about it.

Keith's response is almost always the same.

> **"...Old injury."

He has never elaborated further.

---

## **CINDER**

Cinder's shark tail differs noticeably from her brother's.

Characteristics include:

- White coloration.
    
- A healed section missing from the tip, similar to Keith's.
    
- Decorative stickers applied during her free time.
    
- Small, non-operational decorative tattoos.
    

Unlike Keith, Cinder openly treats her tail as another form of self-expression.

The decorations change regularly.

Examples observed include:

- Small stars.
    
- Hearts.
    
- Smiling sharks.
    
- Tiny warning labels reading **"PROPERTY OF KCA"** as a joke.
    

Belle has contributed several of the stickers.

---

## **Observation Log**

During one visit to Room N-07, Star noticed Cinder carefully replacing a worn sticker.

> **"...You decorate your tail?"

Cinder nodded.

> **"...Why not?"

Belle immediately appeared beside her carrying an entire sheet of rabbit-themed stickers.

> **"...I found more."

Cinder laughed.

> **"...Of course you did."

Several minutes later...

Keith entered the room carrying mission reports.

Belle looked toward his tail.

Then toward Cinder's.

> **"...Matching bite marks."

Keith gave a short glance.

> **"...Coincidence."

Cinder looked away while trying not to smile.

> **"...Sure..."

Neither sibling offered any further explanation.

The subject was quietly dropped.
