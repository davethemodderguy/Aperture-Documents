### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / ENTITY PSYCHOLOGY LOG**

# **BUNNY — UNDER THE MASK**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Bunny  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE — Room N-07  
**Status:** **PAST PARTIALLY RECOVERED**

---

# **INTERVIEW CONTINUATION**

Despite the previous incident, Bunny eventually agreed to continue answering questions.

The questioning was conducted slowly.

No demands were made.

Bunny was repeatedly reminded that she could stop at any time.

She nodded.

She kept smiling.

It was immediately apparent that the smile was not genuine.

---

# **THE MASK**

Bunny sat on the couch with Corin beside her.

Her hands were clasped together.

Her ears were upright.

Her expression was cheerful.

Too cheerful.

Corin noticed first.

> **"You don't have to smile."**

Bunny smiled wider.

> **"Bunny happy!"**

Corin shook her head.

> **"You're doing it again."**

The smile remained.

Her eyes began watering.

---

# **THE QUESTIONS**

The interviewer asked:

> **"Who was with you before you came here?"**

Bunny's smile began trembling.

Her ears lowered.

She stared at the floor.

Then she spoke.

Slowly.

Using the fragmented language she normally uses.

> **"Bunny with mom."**

Silence.

Corin reached for her hand.

Bunny continued.

---

# **"MOM HURT"**

> **"Mom hurt."**

Bunny's hands tightened.

Her breathing became uneven.

> **"Mom..."**

She stopped.

Her smile returned.

> **"Mom okay."**

Corin immediately shook her head.

> **"You don't have to say that."**

Bunny's smile twitched.

Then disappeared.

> **"Mom hurt."**

---

# **"MOM KILLED"**

The next words came out barely above a whisper.

> **"Mom killed."**

The room became completely silent.

Bunny began crying.

Her ears flattened.

She covered her face.

Corin pulled her closer.

Nobody interrupted.

After several seconds, Bunny continued.

---

# **"BUNNY RAN"**

> **"Bunny ran."**

She wiped her eyes.

> **"Bunny scared."**

> **"Bunny run."**

Her grammar remained fragmented.

But the meaning was clear.

She had fled.

Alone.

---

# **"BUNNY FELL"**

> **"Bunny fell."**

Bunny pointed toward her leg.

> **"Leg hurt."**

She remembered the injury.

The same injury discovered when Corin first found her beneath the snow.

It was not caused by some random accident.

Bunny had sustained it while fleeing.

---

# **"BUNNY SLEEP"**

Bunny's voice became quieter.

> **"Bunny leg hurt."**

> **"Bunny cold."**

> **"Bunny tired."**

Then:

> **"Bunny sleep."**

She curled slightly into herself.

> **"Snow."**

> **"Bunny sleep."**

---

# **THE DISCOVERY**

The implication was immediate.

The injured snow rabbit Corin found beneath the Treehouse was not simply a wild animal.

Bunny had been running from something.

She had fallen.

She had injured her leg.

She had continued until she could no longer move.

Then she collapsed in the snow.

She slept.

And eventually...

Corin found her.

---

# **THE SMILE RETURNS**

Bunny suddenly looked up.

Her tears remained.

Her smile returned.

> **"Bunny okay!"**

Corin stared at her.

> **"You're crying."**

> **"Happy!"**

> **"You're not."**

Bunny's smile trembled.

> **"...Have to."**

---

# **CONFIRMATION**

C.O.R.E. psychological analysis has confirmed the earlier suspicion.

Bunny is **forcing herself to appear happy.**

The behavior appears deeply ingrained.

Whenever Bunny approaches painful memories, her emotional response follows a consistent pattern:

**Fear → sadness → crying → forced smile → declaration of happiness.**

The smile does not indicate recovery.

It is a mask.

---

# **CORIN UNDERSTANDS**

Corin knows the behavior.

She has done something similar herself.

She spent months convincing herself she was fine.

She told people:

> **"I'm always fine."**

Even when she wasn't.

Corin looked at Bunny.

> **"You don't have to be happy all the time."**

Bunny stared at her.

> **"...No?"**

> **"No."**

Corin hugged her.

> **"You can be sad."**

Bunny's expression broke.

She began crying again.

This time, she didn't try to smile.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle sat beside them.

She quietly placed a hand on Bunny's shoulder.

> **"You can be sad here."**

Bunny looked at her.

> **"...Here safe?"**

Belle nodded.

> **"Here safe."**

Bunny cried harder.

Nobody stopped her.

---

# **CORIN'S NOTEBOOK**

Corin later documented what Bunny had told them.

The entry reads:

> **"Bunny had a mom."**

> **"Mom was hurt."**

> **"Mom died."**

> **"Bunny ran."**

> **"Bunny fell and hurt her leg."**

> **"Bunny slept in snow."**

Below this, Corin wrote:

> **"That's where I found her."**

The final line is written in darker ink:

> **"She was alone too."**

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Bunny's story was not complicated.

It did not require hundreds of pages.

She remembered only fragments.

Five simple statements:

> **"Bunny with mom."**

> **"Mom hurt."**

> **"Mom killed."**

> **"Bunny ran."**

> **"Bunny fell."**

> **"Bunny sleep."**

That was enough.

She had been a frightened little rabbit running through the snow.

She had lost her mother.

She had been injured.

She had collapsed.

And then she woke up beside Corin.

Since then, Bunny has tried desperately to be happy.

Not because she is.

Because somewhere inside her, she seems to believe that she **has to be.**

But Corin understands something Bunny has not learned yet.

Happiness isn't something you owe people.

Neither is a smile.

So when Bunny cried this time...

Nobody asked her to stop.

Nobody told her to be happy.

Corin simply held her.

And for the first time since arriving at SAFEHOUSE...

Bunny cried without smiling.
