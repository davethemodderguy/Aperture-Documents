# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL PROFILE REVISION**

# **COREY / SOLARIA — ALTER EGO MANIFESTATION**

**Classification:** Level 4 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corey / Solaria

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Further observation has clarified several previously unknown properties of Corey's alternate persona, **Solaria**.

Solaria is confirmed to be an alternate state of Corey rather than a separate individual.

The transition can occur naturally when Corey is exposed to **direct sunlight**.

The exact mechanism remains unexplained.

---

# **SOLARIA ACTIVATION**

The previously unknown activation condition has now been identified.

> **Sunlight.**

No ritual, spell, emotional trigger, or conscious decision appears to be necessary.

When Corey is exposed to sufficient sunlight, Solaria may manifest.

The transition is not currently understood well enough to determine whether it is instantaneous or gradual.

Personnel are not to deliberately force the transformation without authorization.

---

# **ENVIRONMENTAL INDICATOR**

The most reliable early indication of Solaria's presence is a localized increase in temperature.

The surrounding area becomes **noticeably warmer**.

This effect does not appear to be caused by ordinary heating.

Thermal instruments have recorded elevated temperatures around the subject even when the surrounding environment remains unchanged.

The effect is generally subtle.

Personnel familiar with Corey can often notice it before the transformation is visually confirmed.

---

# **PHYSICAL DIFFERENCE**

The most consistent visible difference between Corey and Solaria is the presence of a:

> **Right eyepatch.**

The eyepatch appears as part of Solaria's manifested appearance.

Its origin and purpose are currently unknown.

No injury corresponding to the eyepatch has been identified.

Corey herself has no known memory of acquiring it.

---

# **SOLARIA'S ABILITIES**

Solaria possesses **sun-based magic**.

Her magical abilities are directly associated with solar energy and appear significantly more natural to her than conventional magical techniques.

Known properties include:

- Solar energy manipulation.
    
- Light-based magic.
    
- Heat generation.
    
- Increased environmental temperature around her.
    

The full extent of her abilities remains undocumented.

**No confirmed combat feats or upper limits have been established.**

---

# **SHARED MEMORIES**

Corey and Solaria appear to possess a **shared memory pool**.

Neither personality appears to lose awareness of the other's experiences.

This creates an unusual situation.

Corey remembers what Solaria does.

Solaria remembers what Corey does.

However, Corey does not appear to possess direct access to Solaria's thoughts while Solaria is inactive.

The reverse relationship appears similar.

---

# **PERSONALITY DIFFERENCES**

Solaria's personality is broadly consistent with Corey's.

She remains:

- Kind.
    
- Intelligent.
    
- Caring.
    
- Patient.
    

However, Solaria is noticeably **more confident**.

She speaks with greater certainty and appears more comfortable asserting herself.

Researchers currently believe this may represent an aspect of Corey's personality that is normally suppressed or less prominent.

This remains unconfirmed.

---

# **CURRENT LIMITATIONS**

Despite the discovery of the activation condition, researchers have not yet observed Solaria using her full capabilities.

Most importantly:

> **No confirmed feats have been documented yet.**

C.O.R.E. therefore refuses to assign Solaria a formal power ranking or estimated maximum capability.

All current classifications are based on demonstrated properties rather than speculation.

---

# **OBSERVATION LOG**

**Location:** Blacksite exterior  
**Weather:** Clear  
**Time:** 11:37

Corey was standing outside while speaking with several Marts.

A researcher noticed that the temperature around her had increased.

Corey looked confused.

> "...Is it warmer?"

One Mart looked upward.

> "...Sun."

Corey glanced toward the sky.

There was a brief pause.

Her demeanor changed.

She became noticeably more confident.

A black eyepatch appeared over her right eye.

The surrounding temperature increased further.

She looked at the researcher.

> "...Hello."

The researcher hesitated.

> "...Corey?"

She smiled.

> "...Close."

---

# **MART RESPONSE**

The Marts immediately recognized the change.

Several gathered around her.

One pointed upward.

> "...Solaria."

Solaria looked down at it.

> "...You remembered."

The Mart nodded enthusiastically.

> "...Remember."

Another Mart quietly added:

> "...Warm."

Solaria laughed.

> "...Yeah."

---

# **COREY / SOLARIA MEMORY TEST**

Following the event, Solaria was asked whether she remembered the previous conversation.

She answered immediately.

> "...Of course."

She then accurately repeated the conversation word-for-word.

When asked whether Corey would remember this interaction after returning to her normal state:

> "...She already does."

---

# **STAR'S COMMENT**

Star was present during one manifestation.

She looked at the eyepatch.

Then at the warmer environment.

Then back at Solaria.

> "...So you're Solaria."

Solaria smiled confidently.

> "...Finally figured it out."

Star laughed.

> "...You're more confident than Corey."

Solaria replied:

> "...Someone has to be."

---

# **COREY'S REACTION**

After returning to her normal state, Corey was informed of the exchange.

She covered part of her face with her oversized sleeve.

> "...I said that?"

Star nodded.

> "...You did."

Corey sighed.

> "...Great."

A nearby Mart quietly added:

> "...Confident Corey."

Corey looked at it.

> "...Don't encourage her."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

The Solaria phenomenon is now considered **partially understood**.

Confirmed:

- Solaria is Corey's alter ego.
    
- Sunlight can trigger manifestation.
    
- Solaria possesses solar-based magic.
    
- Solaria and Corey share memories.
    
- Solaria is somewhat more confident than Corey.
    
- A right eyepatch accompanies the transformation.
    
- Her presence produces a measurable increase in local temperature.
    

Unknown:

- How the alter ego originally formed.
    
- When Solaria first existed.
    
- Why Corey cannot consciously access her at will.
    
- Why the eyepatch manifests.
    
- The full extent of Solaria's abilities.
    
- Whether other manifestations or abilities remain undiscovered.
    

For now, C.O.R.E. will continue observing the phenomenon without attempting to force further development.

One conclusion, however, is already clear.

When the temperature rises...

And Corey suddenly has a right eyepatch...

Personnel should probably stop calling her Corey.

**End of Personnel Profile Revision**
