### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / ENTITY PSYCHOLOGY LOG**

# **BUNNY — ONLY BIG SIS SEES THE TEARS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Bunny  
**Primary Observer:** Corin Lockwood  
**Status:** **SEVERE MATERNAL TRAUMA / EMOTIONAL MASKING**

---

# **OBSERVATION**

There is one subject Bunny cannot discuss normally.

Her mother.

Any mention of Bunny's mother produces an immediate and severe emotional response.

However, most personnel never see it.

Because Bunny smiles.

---

# **THE QUESTION**

The question was simple.

> **"Do you remember your mother?"**

Bunny's ears immediately lowered.

Her expression froze.

For a moment, there was no response.

Then the smile appeared.

> **"Bunny happy."**

The answer did not correspond to the question.

The interviewer tried again.

> **"What was your mother like?"**

Bunny's hands began trembling.

Her smile widened.

> **"Mom..."**

She stopped.

A tear rolled down her cheek.

Bunny immediately wiped it away.

> **"Bunny okay."**

---

# **CORIN NOTICES**

Everyone else continued seeing the smile.

Corin didn't.

Corin noticed Bunny's breathing.

Her ears.

Her hands.

The way she kept wiping her face.

The way she refused to look toward anyone.

Corin quietly approached.

> **"Bunny."**

Bunny looked at her.

The smile remained.

> **"Big Sis!"**

Another tear appeared.

Corin sat beside her.

She didn't ask another question.

---

# **THE ONLY PERSON WHO SEES IT**

Bunny can conceal her sadness from almost everyone.

She cannot reliably conceal it from Corin.

Corin knows the difference between Bunny's genuine smile and her defensive one.

She notices the tiny changes:

- The ears sit differently.
    
- The smile is held too rigidly.
    
- Bunny's voice becomes unnaturally cheerful.
    
- Her hands grip her clothes.
    
- Her breathing becomes shallow.
    
- She avoids eye contact.
    
- Tears appear despite the smile.
    

Nobody else consistently catches all of them.

Corin does.

---

# **WHY CORIN?**

Bunny trusts Corin differently from everyone else.

Corin was the person who found her.

The first person who cared for her after she had lost everything.

The first person who gave her food.

The first person who stayed beside her.

The first person Bunny eventually called:

> **"Big sis."**

Bunny appears to believe she can finally let the mask slip around Corin.

Not always.

Not immediately.

But eventually.

---

# **THE PRIVATE MOMENTS**

When everyone else leaves, Bunny sometimes remains with Corin.

The smile disappears.

Her ears droop.

She leans against Corin.

Sometimes she says nothing.

Sometimes she whispers:

> **"Mom..."**

And then cries.

Corin never asks her to explain.

She simply holds her.

---

# **A RECENT INCIDENT**

Bunny was sitting beside Corin when she suddenly whispered:

> **"Bunny miss Mom."**

Corin looked at her.

Bunny's smile appeared automatically.

> **"Bunny happy."**

Corin shook her head.

> **"You can miss her."**

The smile disappeared.

Bunny began crying.

> **"Mom gone."**

Corin hugged her.

> **"I know."**

> **"Bunny want Mom."**

> **"I know."**

> **"Bunny scared."**

> **"I know."**

Bunny eventually fell asleep against Corin.

For several hours afterward, she remained asleep beneath Corin's wing.

---

# **NO QUESTIONS**

C.O.R.E. has now established a strict guideline.

Personnel are not to ask Bunny about her mother unless Bunny voluntarily initiates the conversation.

If she does initiate it, the appropriate response is not interrogation.

It is presence.

Listen.

Stay.

Let Bunny decide when she is finished.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Bunny smiles for almost everyone.

She smiles when she's scared.

She smiles when she's hurt.

She smiles when she wants other people to stop worrying.

But when the subject becomes her mother...

the mask begins to crack.

Most people never see what is underneath.

Corin does.

She sees the trembling hands.

The lowered ears.

The tears Bunny desperately wipes away.

The little girl who misses her mother.

And when Bunny finally stops pretending, Corin doesn't ask her to smile.

She simply opens her arms.

Bunny comes closer.

And for a few minutes...

**Big Sis is the only person Bunny lets see her cry.**
