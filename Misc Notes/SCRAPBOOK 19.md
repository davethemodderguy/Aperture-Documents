# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **MEDICAL & EXECUTIVE WELFARE DIVISION**

### **CONFIDENTIAL OBSERVATION REPORT – BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Restricted (Authorized Personnel Only)

---

## **SUMMARY**

Over recent weeks, Director **Belle Merryweather** has experienced a gradual decline in day-to-day performance caused by the combined effects of prolonged emotional strain and insufficient sleep.

Rather than a sudden change, the deterioration has been subtle enough to escape the notice of most personnel.

Belle has deliberately concealed the extent of her condition.

Only three individuals are currently aware of how much it has affected her:

- **Endmin Merryweather**
    
- **Kent (Keith)**
    
- **Remielle (Cinder)**
    

At Belle's request, they have respected her privacy while quietly supporting her whenever possible.

---

## **OBSERVED CHANGES**

Although Belle continues to complete her responsibilities, those closest to her have documented several noticeable differences.

These include:

- Reduced walking speed.
    
- Slower reaction to conversations.
    
- Longer pauses before answering questions.
    
- Frequently losing her train of thought.
    
- Increased mental fatigue during meetings.
    
- Difficulty concentrating for extended periods.
    
- More frequent mistakes that she normally would not make.
    

Despite these symptoms, Belle continues to insist she is "fine."

---

## **LOSS OF FOCUS**

One of the earliest and most recognizable changes has been Belle's tendency to lose focus in the middle of conversations.

When someone speaks to her, she may briefly stare into space before quietly responding:

> "...Huh?"

The response is rarely caused by confusion.

Instead, it reflects that her attention momentarily drifted as fatigue caught up with her.

After being asked again, she usually understands immediately.

Personnel unfamiliar with the situation often mistake this for distraction.

Those closest to her recognize it as a sign that she is exhausted.

---

## **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION**

Belle continues to smile, laugh, and carry out her duties as normal.

However, Keith, Cinder, and Endmin have each independently noted that maintaining this cheerful demeanor appears to require noticeably more effort than before.

When she believes no one is watching, Belle has occasionally been seen quietly rubbing her eyes or resting her head on her desk for a few moments before resuming work.

---

## **KNOWN TO AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL**

### **Endmin Merryweather**

Endmin became aware of Belle's worsening condition through the recurring nightmares and their shared living arrangements.

She has quietly encouraged Belle to rest whenever possible, though Belle often replies that there is still work to do.

---

### **Kent (Keith)**

Keith noticed Belle taking longer than usual to process information during KCA meetings.

He has since made a habit of repeating important details without drawing attention to the fact that Belle occasionally loses focus.

He has not discussed the matter with anyone else.

---

### **Remielle (Cinder)**

Cinder recognized the signs almost immediately.

Rather than confronting Belle directly, she has subtly reduced Belle's workload whenever possible and remains nearby during longer meetings.

She understands Belle well enough to know that forcing the issue would only make her hide it more.

---

**Endmin**

> "...She's trying very hard to be."

---

**Kent**

> "She shouldn't have to apologize for being tired."

---

## **BELLE'S COMMENT**

When asked privately if she had been sleeping enough, Belle looked away for a moment.

> "...Huh?"

After the question was repeated, she smiled apologetically.

> "...Sorry."

> "...I just... lost focus again."

She remained silent for a few seconds before quietly adding,

> "...Please don't worry everyone."

---

## **STATUS**

Belle continues to fulfill her responsibilities as Director despite increasing mental fatigue.

At present, the full extent of her condition remains known only to Endmin, Keith, and Cinder, who continue to support her discreetly while respecting her wishes for privacy.

**End of Confidential Observation Report**
