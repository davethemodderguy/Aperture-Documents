# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **CONFIDENTIAL PSYCHOLOGICAL HISTORY ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – DEVELOPMENTAL TRAUMA HISTORY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Medical Director & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **BACKGROUND**

Following her recovery from the unauthorized Ether experimentation that occurred when she was approximately **eight years old**, Corin attempted to return to an ordinary childhood.

Instead, she found herself struggling to reintegrate into everyday life.

Medical differences, visible emotional distress, prolonged absences, and rumors surrounding her condition made her the target of repeated bullying by other children.

Although the incidents were not physically severe, they occurred over an extended period and reinforced the beliefs Corin had already developed about herself.

The psychological team concluded that these experiences compounded the trauma caused by the experimentation rather than replacing it.

---

## **FAMILY RESPONSE**

Whenever Arcturus became aware of the bullying, he intervened immediately.

Norma was no different.

Neither sibling hesitated to stand between Corin and those who mocked or isolated her.

Despite their efforts, there were many moments they never witnessed.

Words spoken in empty hallways.

Whispers after class.

Children refusing to sit beside her.

Those experiences accumulated over time.

Corin later remarked during therapy:

> "...They weren't the first people to make me feel like I didn't belong."

> "...But they made sure I never forgot the feeling."

---

## **CLINICAL FINDINGS**

Corin demonstrates a longstanding pattern of placing the wellbeing of others above her own.

Examples include:

- Working through illness or exhaustion.
    
- Minimizing her own distress when others are present.
    
- Redirecting conversations away from herself.
    
- Consistently volunteering to help colleagues before accepting assistance herself.
    
- Neglecting rest, meals, and personal recovery while ensuring others are cared for.
    

When asked why she rarely asks for help, Corin answered:

> "...Someone else probably needs it more."

---

## **RISK ASSESSMENT**

Corin's treatment team has identified her as being at **persistently elevated risk during periods of acute psychological crisis**, particularly when multiple stressors occur simultaneously.

This assessment is based on her trauma history, recurring hopelessness, tendency toward isolation, and diminished regard for her own wellbeing.

For this reason, Corin remains under an individualized safety and wellness plan that includes:

- Regular psychological follow-up.
    
- Proactive wellness check-ins by trusted colleagues.
    
- Immediate access to crisis support from Blacksite medical personnel.
    
- Reduced overnight isolation whenever practical.
    
- Encouragement to accept support without viewing it as a burden to others.
    

The goal of this plan is early intervention and continued recovery.

---

## **SELF-CARE**

One recurring theme throughout treatment is Corin's markedly reduced sense of self-care.

She will often remember everyone else's medication.

Forget her own.

She will prepare meals for colleagues.

Then quietly skip hers.

She will stay awake to finish work that benefits others.

While ignoring the exhaustion affecting herself.

The attending psychologist summarized this pattern:

> "Corin has learned how to care for almost everyone."

> "She is still learning that she deserves the same care."

---

## **BELLE'S OBSERVATION**

Belle was asked to describe Corin in one sentence.

She answered immediately.

> "If you're hurting, she'll sit with you until you're okay."

Belle smiled sadly.

> "...I just wish she'd let someone do the same for her."

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' DIRECTIVE**

Director Lucious approved the continuation of Corin's long-term wellness plan.

His annotation reads:

> "Professional excellence must never be mistaken for personal wellbeing."

> "The measure of this organization is not how much work Corin can accomplish."

> "It is whether she knows she is valued even on the days she cannot give anything at all."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin continues to serve as Head of Accounting with exceptional dedication while participating in ongoing medical and psychological treatment.

Her recovery remains an active process.

The KCA's standing policy is that concerns about Corin's safety or wellbeing are to be met with timely, compassionate support, respecting her dignity while ensuring she is never left to face periods of severe distress entirely alone.
