## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CELESTIAL PHENOMENA / PERSONNEL EXPERIENCE REPORT**

# **HUGGING A CELESTIAL BEING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Celestial Authority  
**Subjects:** Guiding Light / Curious Light  
**Interaction:** Physical Affection  
**Status:** **SAFE — WHEN VOLUNTARY**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Personnel who have hugged either of the Architects consistently describe the experience as unusual.

The Architects are beings composed primarily of **celestial light and energy**, yet their manifested forms can be physically interacted with.

A hug is therefore entirely possible.

It simply feels very different from hugging an ordinary person.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light's manifested form is composed of soft blue spectral energy.

Despite her ghostlike appearance, physical contact is surprisingly natural.

Her body has a gentle warmth.

Not the warmth of a human body.

More like the sensation of standing beneath sunlight on a cold morning.

The blue radiance surrounding her becomes slightly brighter during affectionate interactions.

Her fragmented form does not interfere with physical contact.

The floating pieces of her halo and wings simply move aside.

---

# **THE EXPERIENCE**

One Marts described hugging Guiding Light as:

> **"Like hugging a warm cloud that somehow gives really good hugs."**

Another personnel member described it as:

> **"Warm, soft, and weirdly peaceful."**

A third simply said:

> **"I don't know how to explain it."**

---

# **THE LIGHT**

Direct contact with Guiding Light's spectral body does not cause burns or discomfort.

Her light appears to automatically adjust itself around the person touching her.

The result is a gentle sensation of warmth.

Personnel have occasionally reported feeling calmer afterward.

Whether this is an intentional effect or simply a psychological response remains uncertain.

Guiding Light has never clarified.

---

# **THE WINGS**

Hugging Guiding Light can be particularly interesting when her wings are present.

Her wings are partially composed of floating shards.

When someone hugs her, the shards gently reposition themselves.

Some may drift around the person without touching them.

Others form a loose protective arc.

The effect resembles being surrounded by floating pieces of blue glass made entirely from light.

No injuries have been recorded.

---

# **CURIOUS LIGHT**

Curious Light's manifestation produces a different sensation.

His body emits **golden-yellow light**, and physical contact feels warmer and more energetic.

Personnel have described it as:

> **"Like hugging sunlight."**

Curious Light generally accepts hugs without issue when he isn't busy.

He may even return them enthusiastically.

However, he is less likely than Guiding Light to remain still afterward.

---

# **AN UNEXPECTED DETAIL**

Curious Light's golden energy sometimes produces tiny sparks when someone hugs him.

These are harmless.

They appear to be a natural reaction between his manifested energy and the physical environment.

Curious Light finds them interesting.

Naturally.

He has attempted to study them.

---

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

Lumi has hugged both Architects many times.

When asked what it feels like, she answered:

> **"Like hugging my friends."**

The interviewer clarified:

> **"But they're made of light."**

Lumi shrugged.

> **"They're still my friends."**

---

# **NEKO'S EXPERIENCE**

Neko particularly enjoys hugging Curious Light.

The two have developed a comfortable friendship through their shared interest in research and invention.

Neko once described the experience as:

> **"Warm."**

Then, after a pause:

> **"...And he purrs less than I do."**

Curious Light strongly denied this.

---

# **MARTS' EXPERIENCE**

Marts' first hug with Guiding Light was followed by several seconds of silence.

When asked what they thought:

> **Marts:**  
> "...I forgot what I was worried about."

Guiding Light smiled.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Good."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Hugging a celestial being made of light is an experience that ordinary physics has no particularly useful vocabulary for.

It is warm.

Soft.

Bright.

Strange.

Comforting.

And somehow completely natural.

The Architects may be capable of creating worlds, observing reality from anywhere, and erasing things from existence...

but when someone they care about simply wants a hug, they are usually happy to oblige.

As one Blacksite employee put it:

> **"I don't understand how hugging a cosmic Architect feels like getting a hug from sunlight."**

Guiding Light's response:

> **"You don't have to understand everything."**

Curious Light added:

> **"I do."**

Guiding Light:

> **"...Of course you do."**

**End of Celestial Phenomena / Personnel Experience Report**
