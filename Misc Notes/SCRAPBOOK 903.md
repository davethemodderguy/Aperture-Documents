# SAFEHOUSE ORIENTATION NOTICE

## NERI — NEW RESIDENT / STAFF RUMORS

**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL — ORIENTATION  
**Subject:** Neri  
**Audience:** New Residents, Visitors, Junior Personnel  
**Status:** Informational

---

## 1. The Problem

New residents occasionally become frightened when introduced to Neri.

This is largely the result of a very popular SAFEHOUSE story.

The story goes approximately as follows:

> **“Don't bully Neri's friends. She'll take one of those hair clips out and slice your neck like sushi.”**

The story has become considerably more dramatic with every retelling.

Unfortunately, there is enough truth behind the underlying warning that nobody has managed to completely dismiss it.

Neri **does** have extremely sharp blades concealed among her hair accessories.

Neri **does** become intensely protective of her friends.

And bullying someone she cares about is an exceptionally bad way to introduce yourself.

---

# 2. The Hairclips

Neri's two crossed hairclips securing her bangs are not ordinary hair accessories.

They are small, jagged blades.

They are sharp.

They are concealed extremely well.

They are also one of the reasons new personnel are advised:

> **Do not touch Neri's hair accessories.**

Do not ask her to demonstrate them.

Do not ask how many knives she has.

Do not attempt to take one.

And, most importantly, **do not give her a reason to use one.**

---

# 3. The Actual Rule

The popular story has simplified Neri's behavior into:

> **Bully Neri → get stabbed.**

This is not an accurate description.

Neri is not randomly violent.

Her behavior is considerably more predictable:

**Friend:** Protect.  
**Threat:** Confront.  
**Friend injured:** Extreme response.  
**Friend killed:** Situation becomes extremely serious.

The best way to avoid problems with Neri is therefore remarkably simple:

### **Be nice.**

---

# 4. Newcomer Advice

If you encounter Neri for the first time:

### Recommended

- Introduce yourself politely.
    
- Leave her alone if she is eating in the cafeteria.
    
- Be kind to her friends.
    
- Respect her personal space.
    
- Ask before touching her belongings.
    
- If she offers you cake, accept it if you want it.
    
- If she is sad, don't force her to talk.
    

### Not Recommended

- Bullying her.
    
- Bullying her friends.
    
- Calling her expendable.
    
- Touching her hairclips.
    
- Trying to count her knives.
    
- Pulling her scarf.
    
- Making jokes about her sister.
    
- Calling her cute.
    

The last one is not technically dangerous.

She will simply become extremely annoyed.

---

# 5. The Sweet Exception

There is, however, one piece of advice that has become almost universal among long-term residents:

> **“Give Neri something sweet and you'll be fine.”**

This is somewhat exaggerated.

Sweets do not magically pacify Neri.

They do not override serious threats.

They do not make her forget that someone has hurt her friends.

And Neri does not automatically reveal her past because someone hands her cake.

Most of the time, she just eats the cake.

Still, offering her a sweet when she is already upset is generally a good way to make the situation considerably less unpleasant.

---

# 6. A Typical Newbie Encounter

A new resident once saw Neri sitting in the cafeteria.

They had already heard the stories.

They froze.

Neri looked up.

The newcomer looked at her hair.

Then at the coat.

Then at the cake.

Then back at the hair.

Neri stared.

> **“...Hi?”**

The newcomer slowly placed a wrapped candy on the table.

Neri looked at it.

Then at them.

> **“For me?”**

They nodded.

Neri picked it up.

> **“Thanks.”**

She unwrapped it.

She ate it.

Then went back to her cake.

The newcomer quietly walked away.

An older resident watching nearby remarked:

> **“See? You're alive.”**

---

# 7. Important Clarification

Neri should not be treated like a dangerous animal that needs to be "appeased."

That is not the purpose of the sweets.

She is a ten-year-old girl.

She likes being treated kindly.

She likes cake.

She likes having friends.

She likes people who make her feel safe.

The reason the sweet rule works so well is not because sugar controls her.

It's because **kindness works.**

---

# 8. Final Orientation Rule

If there is one thing new personnel should remember about Neri, it is this:

> **Don't be afraid of her.**

Be respectful.

Be kind.

Don't hurt her friends.

Don't touch the knives.

And if she's sitting alone in the cafeteria eating cake—

**let her eat her cake.**

She probably just wants a quiet moment.

And if she happens to offer you some?

You have officially been accepted.

Take the cake.

**End Notice.**
