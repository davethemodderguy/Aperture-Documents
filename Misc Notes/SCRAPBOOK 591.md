# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE PERSONNEL OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — FELINE LOAF**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Form:** Miniature Feline  
**Behavior:** **Loafing**  
**Status:** Extremely Cute / Operationally Irrelevant

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin has learned how to **loaf**.

The behavior consists of Corin sitting upright while tucking all four paws beneath her body, keeping her wings folded neatly against her back.

Her two tails usually curl around her sides.

The result is a very small:

> **White winged cat loaf.**

---

# **DISCOVERY**

Corin initially attempted to imitate the posture after observing other cats.

Her first attempt was unstable.

She shifted her paws several times.

Her wings twitched.

One tail escaped from beneath her body.

She eventually found a comfortable position.

Then she simply stopped moving.

Neko stared at her.

> **"...She's loafing."**

Belle immediately arrived to investigate.

---

# **BELLE'S ASSESSMENT**

Belle took approximately three seconds to reach a conclusion.

> **"She's adorable."**

No further scientific analysis was required.

---

# **TAIGA'S ASSESSMENT**

Taiga remains one of SAFEHOUSE's established standards of cuteness.

Corin's new loafing behavior has nevertheless created considerable competition.

The current informal Blacksite ranking is:

**1. Belle**

**2. Taiga**

**3. Corin**

The ranking has not been officially approved by C.O.R.E.

It has nevertheless become widely accepted.

---

# **THE WINGED LOAF**

Corin's loaf differs from that of an ordinary cat.

Her wings fold neatly over her back.

When viewed from above, the wings resemble a small pair of folded panels.

Her feathers become slightly puffed when she is comfortable.

Her ears remain upright.

Her two tails rest beside her.

She occasionally gives a quiet:

> **"Mew."**

while remaining completely motionless.

---

# **JAR LOAF**

Corin has also learned that she can loaf inside her preferred glass jars.

This has produced what SAFEHOUSE personnel have unofficially designated:

> **The Jar Loaf.**

The entire jar may contain nothing but:

**Corin.**

**Wings.**

**Two tails.**

**Tiny loaf.**

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

Attempts to resist picking her up have largely failed.

Belle has been observed staring at loafing Corin for several minutes before eventually picking her up.

Neko has taken photographs.

Endmin simply watches.

Orphie occasionally places a hand beside the loaf so Corin can lean against it.

Corin appears to enjoy the attention.

---

# **BEHAVIORAL SIGNIFICANCE**

Loafing appears to indicate that Corin is:

- Comfortable.
    
- Warm.
    
- Relaxed.
    
- Not experiencing immediate distress.
    

Her ability to maintain the posture also suggests that her physical strength is gradually returning.

C.O.R.E. considers this a positive recovery indicator.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin has learned to loaf.

She is palm-sized.

She has white fur.

She has two tails.

She has wings.

She can meow again.

And now she can fold herself into the smallest, fluffiest possible shape and simply sit there.

By unofficial SAFEHOUSE consensus, Corin has become:

> **The singlehandedly cutest thing in the Blacksite.**

With two exceptions.

Belle.

And Taiga.

Corin has accepted this ranking.

She is currently loafing inside a jar.

**End of SAFEHOUSE Personnel Observation**
