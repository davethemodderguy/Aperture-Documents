# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **MEDICAL ANOMALY REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – PHENOTYPIC REGRESSION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the recurrence of Corin Lockwood's trauma-related nightmares, Medical Services documented an unexpected physiological regression.

The change was not gradual.

It occurred over several days.

The phenomenon coincided with the worsening of Corin's psychological condition.

---

# **PHYSICAL CHANGES**

Medical examinations confirmed the following changes:

- Hair pigmentation gradually reverted from its restored natural black to its previous chalky lime coloration.
    
- Skin pigmentation faded, returning to its previously documented pale appearance.
    
- Increased fatigue despite normal laboratory findings.
    
- No evidence of poisoning, nutritional deficiency, infection, or genetic alteration.
    

No medical explanation currently accounts for the regression.

---

# **CORRELATION**

Medical Services cannot establish a direct causal relationship.

However, the timing is notable.

As Corin's nightmares intensified...

Her appearance began resembling the period of her life she feared most.

The change has raised the possibility that the unidentified phenomenon responsible for her previous recovery is no longer fully stabilizing her condition.

This remains a hypothesis.

No supporting mechanism has yet been identified.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

Unlike previous episodes, Corin noticed the changes immediately.

She quietly cut a strand of her hair and placed it beside an older photograph.

She compared them for several minutes.

Then folded the photograph and put it away.

When Medical Services asked how she felt about the change, Corin answered without visible emotion.

> "...It's happening again."

She declined to elaborate.

---

# **PERSONNEL RESPONSES**

**Star**

Initially believed the lighting in the room was unusual.

When she realized the change was real, she became visibly distressed.

> "...No..."

---

**Nightshade**

Remained close to Star.

She watched Corin carefully but did not mention the change in her presence.

---

**Belle**

Quietly continued bringing meals.

She deliberately avoided commenting on Corin's appearance.

Later, she was overheard telling Bao:

> "...I don't want her to think that's all I see."

---

**Mart**

Removed several older photographs from the common room without explanation.

When asked why, he replied:

> "She doesn't need reminders everywhere she looks."

---

**Neko**

Requested every available medical report relating to Corin's unexplained recovery and regression.

For the first time in months, none of his engineering knowledge offered even a theory.

---

**Miss Neko**

Closed the investigation file after several hours.

She simply wrote:

> **"Cause: Unknown."**

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi noticed before Corin said anything.

She gently brushed a lock of Corin's hair aside.

For a brief moment, she looked as though she wanted to say something.

Instead...

She smiled.

Not because nothing had changed.

But because she refused to let Corin believe that her appearance determined how she was loved.

Corin quietly asked:

> "...Do I look different?"

Lumi answered without hesitation.

> "You look like my daughter."

Corin looked away.

Her eyes began to water.

She did not reply.

---

# **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical Services stress that the regression in Corin's appearance does not, by itself, indicate a complete reversal of her previous recovery.

However, it coincides with worsening sleep disturbances and declining emotional health.

Further monitoring has been authorized.

Executive Directive C-17 remains in effect.

Personnel are instructed not to speculate about the cause of the regression in Corin's presence unless medically necessary.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin continues fulfilling her duties.

She continues residing at Shelter T-01.

Her nightmares persist.

Her physical appearance has returned to the pale skin and chalky lime hair previously associated with the most difficult period of her life.

For many around her, the change is painful to witness.

For Corin...

It feels like seeing the past reflected in the mirror once again.

**End of Medical Anomaly Report**
