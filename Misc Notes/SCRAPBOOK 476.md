## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE / PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION LOG**

# **THE BODY THAT DOESN'T ANSWER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Architect-Sealed  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Status:** **CONCERNING**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Lumi stopped visiting the treehouse.

No argument followed.

No final confrontation.

She simply stopped coming.

For Corin, the silence became another confirmation of the fear she had carried for months:

> **Maybe Mom really did leave me.**

Orphie remained with her.

Guiding Light continued watching over her.

Curious Light remained nearby when permitted.

But there was one place none of them could stand for her.

**Inside her own head.**

---

# **THE MIRROR**

Late one evening, Corin stood alone in front of the mirror.

She slowly removed her red wool beanie.

Then her coat.

Then her scarf.

She looked at herself without the clothing that normally concealed her body.

For once, she did not look away.

She touched the scars across her body.

Then the stitches.

One by one.

The evidence of everything that had happened to her was there.

Not memories.

Not photographs.

Not stories.

**Her body.**

---

# **THE CHECK**

Corin placed a hand over her chest.

Nothing.

No heartbeat.

No familiar rhythm beneath her palm.

She moved her hand to her wrist.

Nothing there either.

No pulse.

No ordinary vascular activity.

She stared at her reflection.

Her eyes moved toward the room around her.

Everything remained monochromatic.

No red.

No green.

No blue.

No yellow.

Only shades of light and dark.

She whispered:

> **"...There's nothing left."**

---

# **THE ETHER**

Corin eventually injured herself.

There was no dramatic biological response.

No normal bleeding.

Only a brief release of **shimmering Ether**, which quickly gathered around the damaged area and sealed it.

There was no pain.

That fact frightened her more than the injury itself.

Her body simply repaired the damage and continued existing.

As though nothing had happened.

Corin stared at the sealed skin.

> **"...Even that doesn't work."**

She wasn't talking about the wound.

She was talking about her inability to escape the body she hated.

---

# **THE REALIZATION**

Corin sat on the floor.

She looked at her hands.

> **"No heartbeat."**

She touched her wrist again.

> **"No blood."**

She looked toward the mirror.

> **"No colors."**

Her fingers traced one of the stitches.

> **"And this isn't even my body anymore."**

The Ether inside her continued glowing faintly.

Keeping her together.

Keeping her here.

Whether she wanted it or not.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT**

A soft blue glow appeared beside the mirror.

Guiding Light had noticed.

She did not immediately speak.

Corin looked at her reflection rather than at the Architect.

> **Corin:**  
> "...Why won't my body let me die?"

Guiding Light's expression softened.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Because your body doesn't know what your heart wants."

Corin gave a small, bitter laugh.

> **"I don't even have a working heart."**

Guiding Light stepped closer.

> **"You know what I mean."**

Silence.

---

# **CORIN'S QUESTION**

> **Corin:**  
> "If everything that makes a person alive is gone..."

She looked at herself.

> **"...what exactly am I?"

Guiding Light did not answer immediately.

She had once believed she needed an answer to every question.

She no longer did.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "You're Corin."

Corin closed her eyes.

> **"...That's not an answer."**

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "It's the only answer I know that doesn't take something else away from you."

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie eventually found Corin sitting on the floor.

She did not ask what happened.

She saw the discarded hat.

The coat.

The scarf.

She saw Corin staring at herself.

Orphie quietly picked up the scarf and placed it beside her.

Then she sat down.

No lecture.

No questions.

Just company.

After several minutes, Corin whispered:

> **"...I hate this body."**

Orphie answered:

> **"I know."**

> **"I hate that it keeps me here."**

Orphie looked at her.

> **"Then we'll figure out what you want to do with being here."**

Corin said nothing.

But she leaned against Orphie's shoulder.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S PRIVATE NOTE**

> **"She keeps searching her body for proof that she is alive."**
> 
> **"She finds nothing."**
> 
> **"No pulse."**
> 
> **"No blood."**
> 
> **"No color."**
> 
> **"Only Ether."**
> 
> **"But perhaps being alive was never supposed to be proven by a heartbeat."**

A final line was written underneath.

> **"Perhaps she has to decide what living means for herself."**

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's body continues to function.

The Ether continues to hold her together.

The stitches remain.

The scars remain.

The monochromatic world remains.

And Lumi remains absent.

Corin cannot find the ordinary signs of life inside herself.

Yet there are still things she does that her body alone cannot explain.

She misses her mother.

She loves Orphie.

She remembers.

She hurts.

She asks questions.

She keeps looking for an answer.

And despite everything inside her that says she is already dead...

**some part of Corin is still asking what it means to live.**

**End of SAFEHOUSE / Psychological Observation Log**
