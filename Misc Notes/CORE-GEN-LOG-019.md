# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-GEN-LOG-019**

### **DIPLOMATIC VISIT – UNITED STATES DELEGATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – International Relations

---

## **SUMMARY**

A high-level delegation from the United States, including representatives from the **Department of Defense**, the **Department of State**, several ambassadors, and the **President of the United States**, conducted an official visit to APRT-70 "Heaven's Blacksite."

The visit focused on Aperture Industries' containment philosophy, international cooperation, and the coexistence between humans and high-level anomalous individuals.

---

## **Visitor Observations**

Throughout the tour, the delegation observed personnel and residents interacting in ways that differed greatly from conventional military or containment facilities.

Among the notable sights were:

- Endmin skating through a corridor while carrying engineering parts.
    
- Taiga and Mart discussing marksmanship over lunch.
    
- Neko assisting Engineering with Nekonium research.
    
- Star and Nightshade helping Archive staff organize historical records.
    
- Frost quietly tending plants in one of the greenhouses.
    
- Lucy meeting with Director Lucious regarding Banlands diplomatic affairs.
    

The atmosphere resembled a community more than a prison.

---

## **Discussion**

During a conference in the central briefing room, one ambassador reviewed the resident roster.

After several moments of silence, they looked toward Director Lucious.

> **Ambassador:**
> 
> "Forgive the blunt question..."
> 
> "How are they not destroying the world yet?"

Director Lucious smiled.

Without hesitation, he answered.

> **Director Lucious:**
> 
> "Because they're my children..."
> 
> "...and they're the ones who trust me."

The room became quiet.

The ambassador looked down at the report again.

Then back at Lucious.

> **Ambassador:**
> 
> "You're responsible for..."
> 
> "Four Thirens..."
> 
> "Two angels..."
> 
> "A goddess of ice..."
> 
> "...while maintaining diplomatic relations with..."
> 
> "...Hell?"

Lucy politely cleared her throat.

> **Lucy:**
> 
> "...Purgatory."

A few people in the room quietly smiled.

The ambassador corrected himself with an embarrassed nod.

> "...Purgatory."

---

## **An Unexpected Reply**

Before the ambassador could continue, voices echoed from the hallway outside.

The delegation turned toward the open doorway.

Standing together were many of the Blacksite's residents.

Endmin.

Taiga.

Mart.

Neko.

Miss Neko.

Star.

Nightshade.

Bao.

Frost.

Astral.

Niko.

Aster sat quietly near Endmin's feet, holding her whiteboard.

Without planning it...

They all answered together.

> **"We trust him."**

The room fell completely silent.

There was no hesitation.

No uncertainty.

No one looked at anyone else before speaking.

The answer came naturally.

---

## **Visitor Reaction**

Several members of the delegation exchanged glances.

One Department of Defense representative quietly remarked,

> "...You can't fake that."

The President nodded in agreement.

> "...No."

> "...You can't."

---

## **Lucy's Statement**

Lucy folded her arms and looked toward Director Lucious.

> "When Endmin first came here..."

> "...I wasn't sure."

She smiled.

> "Now..."

> "...The Banlands owes Aperture Industries a great debt."

> "And I owe Director Lucious one personally."

---

## **President's Closing Remarks**

At the conclusion of the visit, the President addressed the assembled personnel.

> "I've visited military bases."

> "Research laboratories."

> "Emergency response centers."

He looked around the room.

Researchers.

Security officers.

Engineers.

Residents laughing together in the hallway.

> "This is different."

> "You didn't build a place that merely contains extraordinary people."

> "You built a place where they chose to belong."

He extended a hand to Director Lucious.

> "That's considerably harder."

Director Lucious accepted the handshake.

> "Thank you, Mr. President."

---

## **Archive Note**

The diplomatic visit concluded without incident.

The final observation entered into the official record was not about advanced technology, international agreements, or anomalous capabilities.

It was a single sentence from one of the visiting ambassadors.

> **"For the first time, I've seen a containment facility where the strongest security measure isn't fear..."**

> **"...it's trust."**
