# SAFEHOUSE EQUIPMENT RECORD

## KATANA ASSIGNMENT — RESIDENT NERI

**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL  
**Department:** Equipment & Welfare Administration  
**Subject:** Neri — New Melee Weapon Assignment  
**Issuing Party:** Curious Light  
**Status:** Approved for Resident Use  
**Risk Assessment:** ██████████ / 10  
**Administrative Concern:** Considerably higher than necessary

---

## 1. SUMMARY

Curious Light has formally presented Resident **Neri** with a katana.

The weapon has been accepted by Neri and is now considered part of her personal equipment.

This makes Neri the **seventh confirmed katana user associated with SAFEHOUSE**, joining:

1. **Neko**
    
2. **Miss Neko**
    
3. **Frost**
    
4. **Nightshade**
    
5. **Mart**
    
6. **Orphie**
    
7. **Neri**
    

The assignment has received two notably different reactions from the Neko pair.

**Neko:** Extremely pleased.

**Miss Neko:** Extremely displeased.

The source of Miss Neko's displeasure is not the katana itself.

It is the fact that **Neri now has a katana.**

---

# 2. THE PRESENTATION

The assignment occurred without any significant ceremony.

Curious Light approached Neri and presented the weapon as a gift.

Neri initially stared at it for several seconds.

She then examined the weapon carefully before asking:

> **Neri:** "For me?"

Curious Light confirmed that it was.

Neri accepted it.

No dramatic declaration followed.

She simply held the katana, looked at the blade, and quietly said:

> **Neri:** "Thank you."

This was considered a successful equipment handover.

---

# 3. NEKO'S REACTION

Neko was reportedly delighted.

The moment Neko learned that Neri had received a katana, his reaction was described by witnesses as:

> **Neko:** "YES."

Followed shortly afterward by:

> **Neko:** "ANOTHER ONE."

Neko subsequently began discussing possible training arrangements, maintenance procedures, carrying options, and whether Neri wanted to learn some of the techniques used by the existing katana users.

There was also an apparently unsolicited discussion regarding how many katana users SAFEHOUSE could eventually support.

No numerical answer was recorded.

This was likely intentional.

---

# 4. MISS NEKO'S REACTION

Miss Neko's reaction was substantially less enthusiastic.

She reportedly stared at Neri.

Then at the katana.

Then back at Neri.

Then at Neko.

A considerable silence followed.

> **Miss Neko:** "Why?"

> **Neko:** "Because she can use one."

> **Miss Neko:** "That wasn't my question."

Neko reportedly became even happier.

Miss Neko subsequently requested that someone explain why giving the resident who already possesses numerous concealed knives had been considered a reasonable idea.

No satisfactory answer was provided.

---

# 5. WHY NERI WAS CONSIDERED SUITABLE

Despite the concerns raised by Miss Neko, Neri's compatibility with a katana is not difficult to understand.

Neri already demonstrates:

- Exceptional close-quarters ability.
    
- Very high mobility.
    
- Strong hand-to-hand combat capability.
    
- A preference for close-range weapons.
    
- Extensive familiarity with bladed weapons.
    
- Extremely rapid reactions.
    
- An unusual ability to understand unfamiliar objects and mechanisms.
    
- A demonstrated dislike of firearms due to their distance from the target.
    

When asked about the weapon's range compared with a firearm, Neri reportedly replied:

> **Neri:** "Still closer."

This statement was entered into the equipment assessment.

---

# 6. EXISTING BLADE INVENTORY

The assignment should not be interpreted as Neri's first experience with edged weapons.

Neri already possesses an unknown number of concealed knives and other small blades.

Most notably, the two crossed hairclips holding her bangs in place are themselves extremely sharp blades.

The fish-bone hairclip remains **non-weaponized** and is considered a sentimental item belonging to Neri.

The katana therefore represents a significant increase in reach rather than the introduction of bladed weaponry into Neri's life.

---

# 7. SAFETY CONSIDERATIONS

The following rules have been added to Neri's equipment profile:

### Rule 1 — Do Not Touch the Katana Without Permission

This includes attempting to inspect, draw, reposition, borrow, polish, or otherwise interact with it.

### Rule 2 — Do Not Attempt to Count Neri's Weapons

This remains applicable despite the new katana.

### Rule 3 — Do Not Challenge Neri to a Duel

Particularly not as a joke.

### Rule 4 — Do Not Bully Neri or Her Friends

This was already SAFEHOUSE policy.

The katana does not change this policy.

It merely makes several staff members more enthusiastic about reminding newcomers of it.

### Rule 5 — Maintain Medical Oversight

Neri's cardiovascular condition remains unchanged.

A katana does not grant additional endurance, prevent anemia, or make overexertion medically safe.

Her combat ability should therefore not be mistaken for unlimited physical stamina.

---

The addition of Neri creates an unusual concentration of katana users within the SAFEHOUSE community.

|User|Status|

The equipment department has unofficially begun referring to this group as:

> **"The Seven."**

Miss Neko has explicitly rejected this terminology.

Neko has explicitly approved it.

No official designation has been created.

---

# 9. OBSERVATION

There is an additional psychological consideration regarding Neri's acceptance of the weapon.

Neri has spent much of her life viewing herself as something expendable.

The assignment of personal equipment by someone who trusts her with it carries a meaning beyond simple combat utility.

It is not an experimental instrument.

It is not a restraint.

It is not something given to her because she is considered disposable.

It is simply **hers**.

Neri reportedly spent several minutes quietly inspecting the weapon after receiving it.

She eventually asked whether she was allowed to name it.

Curious Light said yes.

The name has not yet been recorded.
