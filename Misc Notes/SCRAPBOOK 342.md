# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **Z-335 ANATOMICAL OBSERVATION**

# **RETRACTABLE WINGS**

**Classification:** Allied Entity Research  
**Subject:** Z-335 (Marts)  
**Status:** Confirmed

---

# **OVERVIEW**

For the majority of their lives, Marts appear as simple blue blobs with deer antlers and a small top hat.

Recent observations have confirmed that this appearance is incomplete.

Every known Mart possesses a pair of **small, retractable wings**.

The wings are normally concealed within the Mart's magical body and are rarely visible.

Most personnel had no idea they existed.

---

# **DISCOVERY**

The wings were first documented when a Mart drifted toward one of the Blacksite's upper balconies.

Rather than continue floating aimlessly, the Mart briefly unfolded two tiny blue wings.

It gave several quick flaps.

Corrected its direction.

Landed safely.

Then folded the wings away again.

Several witnesses initially believed they had imagined the event.

Subsequent observations confirmed the behavior.

---

# **APPEARANCE**

When extended, the wings are:

- Small relative to the Mart's body.
    
- Semi-transparent.
    
- Light blue.
    
- Slightly luminous.
    
- Feather-like in appearance, despite being composed of condensed Ether.
    

The wings remain visible only for as long as they are needed.

Once the task is complete, they dissolve back into the Mart's body.

---

# **FUNCTION**

The wings are **not** used for sustained flight.

Because gravity has little to no effect on Marts, they have little need to generate lift.

Instead, the wings are used for:

- Steering while drifting.
    
- Stabilizing movement.
    
- Precise landings.
    
- Hovering in confined spaces.
    
- Avoiding obstacles.
    
- Catching themselves before bumping into someone.
    

Their function is comparable to control surfaces rather than primary means of locomotion.

---

# **WHEN THEY APPEAR**

The wings are commonly observed during:

- Strong indoor air currents.
    
- Long-distance drifting.
    
- Rescue of another drifting Mart.
    
- Reaching elevated shelves.
    
- Careful landings on people.
    
- Avoiding collisions.
    

Outside of these situations, Marts almost never reveal them.

---

# **PERSONNEL REACTIONS**

Because the wings are so rarely seen, each new sighting tends to surprise personnel.

Several common responses have been recorded.

> "...They have wings?"

> "...Since when?"

> "...Why didn't anyone tell me?"

The usual answer from nearby Marts is:

> "Need."

---

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

Lumi was informed that her creations possessed retractable wings.

She blinked.

> "...They what?"

After witnessing the behavior herself, she remained silent for several seconds.

Finally:

> "...I didn't make those."

She paused.

> "...I don't think."

---

# **STAR'S COMMENT**

Star immediately adored the discovery.

She now refers to the extended wings as:

> "...Their little emergency wings."

The Marts seem to enjoy the nickname.

---

# **NEKO'S OBSERVATION**

Neko noticed that Marts almost always keep their wings hidden.

She asked one why.

The Mart answered simply:

> "...Not need."

Neko nodded.

> "...Fair."

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

Researchers believe the wings are another example of the Mart species favoring simplicity.

Rather than displaying abilities unnecessarily, they only reveal them when a task genuinely requires it.

This behavior mirrors many other Mart traits:

- They become rocks only when defending others.
    
- They become blankets only when someone is cold.
    
- They become cooling pads only when someone has a fever.
    

Likewise...

They only grow wings when they need them.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

During a routine afternoon in Project SAFEHOUSE, a Mart drifted toward an open window.

A gentle breeze carried it toward Neko's safety net.

Just before reaching the mesh, two tiny blue wings unfolded.

The Mart neatly turned around in midair.

It flew back inside.

The wings disappeared.

The Mart landed on the floor.

A researcher, having witnessed the entire event, quietly asked:

> "...Why didn't you do that before?"

The Mart tilted its head.

Then smiled.

> **"...Didn't need to."**

**End of Anatomical Observation**
