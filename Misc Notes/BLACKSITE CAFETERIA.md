# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **BLACKSITE CAFETERIA**

### **DIRECTOR BELLE MERRYWEATHER – FOOD PREFERENCES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Cafeteria Reference

---

## **SUMMARY**

To assist Cafeteria Services and personnel hosting meetings or events, the following list summarizes Director Belle Merryweather's favorite foods and drinks.

Belle is not considered a picky eater and is generally happy to try new dishes.

That said, there are several things she consistently enjoys.

---

## **FAVORITE FOODS**

### 🥕 Carrots

Belle's undisputed favorite.

She almost always carries a carrot with her, chewing on it whenever she's bored, thinking, or simply relaxing.

It is widely accepted that if Belle has disappeared, checking the cafeteria's carrot supply is a reasonable first step.

---

### 🍰 Cake

Belle has a noticeable sweet tooth.

Birthday cakes, celebration cakes, and simple slices are all enthusiastically accepted.

She has never turned down cake.

---

### 🥞 Pancakes

After participating in the infamous Blacksite breakfast debate—

> **"Pancakes or waffles?"**

Belle officially settled the matter by declaring:

> "...Pancakes."

The debate did not end.

It merely created two factions.

---

### 🍬 Candy

Belle enjoys most varieties of candy in moderation.

Her desk occasionally contains an emergency stash.

The location changes frequently.

---

### 🌿 Mint Leaves

Unlike most personnel, Belle enjoys eating fresh mint leaves by themselves.

Researchers initially assumed she was using them as garnish.

She was not.

---

## **FAVORITE DRINKS**

### 🥤 Soda

Belle enjoys carbonated drinks, particularly during celebrations or movie nights.

She has been reminded multiple times not to drink them too quickly.

---

### 💧 Water

Despite her fondness for sweets, Belle drinks water regularly throughout the day.

She has been known to remind others to stay hydrated as well.

---

## **HONORARY FOOD**

### 🍚 Sugar

Yes.

Just...

Sugar.

Belle has been observed eating small spoonfuls of plain sugar on more than one occasion.

The Cafeteria Manager has politely requested that she stop doing this.

Belle's response:

> "...But it's tasty."

Nutrition staff have since reached a compromise by encouraging desserts instead.

---

## **CAFETERIA NOTES**

Foods that reliably improve Belle's mood include:

- Fresh carrots.
    
- Warm pancakes.
    
- Cake.
    
- Candy.
    
- Mint leaves.
    

Providing these items is **not** required.

It is, however, an easy way to make her smile.
