### **RESTRICTED SAFEHOUSE / MEDICAL OBSERVATION LOG**

# **THE EMPTY TREEHOUSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 — Architect-Sealed  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Location:** Remote Blacksite Perimeter Treehouse  
**Status:** **UNSTABLE / UNSUPERVISED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie temporarily left the treehouse to travel to the Blacksite.

The purpose was routine:

- Obtain medical supplies.
    
- Restock household necessities.
    
- Visit Lumi.
    
- Deliver and retrieve several items from the main facility.
    

Orphie intended to return.

She did not abandon Corin.

She made sure Corin understood that.

Before leaving, Orphie prepared everything Corin might need and remained with her until the last possible moment.

Unfortunately, the timing was particularly bad.

**Guiding Light was away.**

**Curious Light was also away.**

For the first time in some time, Corin was completely alone.

---

# **THE DEPARTURE**

Orphie stood by the doorway.

> **Orphie:**  
> "I'll be back soon."

Corin nodded.

> **"How soon?"**

> **"A few hours."**

Corin looked toward the window.

> **"...Okay."**

Orphie hesitated.

> **"You know I always come back."**

Corin gave a small nod.

> **"I know."**

Orphie left.

The door closed.

The treehouse became quiet.

---

# **THE FIRST HOUR**

At first, Corin was fine.

She sat near the window.

Read.

Listened to the forest.

Nothing appeared wrong.

Then she noticed the silence.

No footsteps.

No Orphie's voice.

No stitching materials being moved around.

No conversation.

Nothing.

The absence gradually became impossible to ignore.

---

# **THE SECOND HOUR**

Corin began checking the clock repeatedly.

She walked toward the door.

Stopped.

Returned to the chair.

Then checked the clock again.

> **"...She's coming back."**

She said it aloud.

Almost as if reminding herself.

> **"She said she would."**

---

# **THE SPIRAL**

Her thoughts became increasingly difficult to control.

The same fears returned.

Lumi had stopped visiting.

The Blacksite was forgetting them.

Her mother had forgotten her.

Her own memories had once become unreliable.

Her body required constant maintenance.

And now Orphie was gone.

Corin knew intellectually that Orphie had only gone to obtain supplies.

Emotionally, the distinction became increasingly difficult to maintain.

> **"What if she doesn't come back?"**

She looked at the door.

> **"...What if everyone eventually leaves?"**

---

# **PHYSICAL INSTABILITY**

The emotional distress began affecting Corin's Ether.

The glow beneath her skin became noticeably brighter.

Several stitches loosened.

Her left arm partially detached at the shoulder.

Corin stared at it.

Normally, Orphie would have immediately repaired it.

There was nobody there.

She carefully held the limb against herself.

> **"...Not now."**

Her voice trembled.

> **"Please."**

---

# **THE TREEHOUSE**

Corin tried to move.

Her leg gave way.

She fell onto the floor.

The impact was not severe.

Her body simply failed to remain properly assembled.

She stared at the floor.

Then laughed once.

It was not a happy laugh.

> **"Of course."**

She looked toward the empty doorway.

> **"Orphie fixes this."**

Silence.

> **"...Orphie always fixes this."**

---

# **THE WORST PART**

Corin realized she had begun depending on Orphie's presence for more than physical repairs.

She depended on her voice.

Her reassurance.

Her presence in the next room.

The knowledge that someone was there.

Without Orphie, the treehouse felt enormous.

Empty.

Unsafe.

Corin pulled herself against the wall.

> **"I hate being alone."**

Nobody answered.

---

# **ETHER FLUCTUATION**

The Ether concentration throughout Corin's body began fluctuating.

Not enough to destroy her.

But enough to distort her physical stability.

Her stitches loosened further.

Her vision became difficult to focus.

The monochromatic room seemed to shift subtly.

Corin covered her eyes.

> **"Stop."**

> **"Please stop."**

She remained there for several minutes.

Eventually, the Ether stabilized slightly.

But Corin did not.

---

# **A MESSAGE**

Corin eventually found the small notebook Orphie had given her.

She opened it.

The page read:

> **MY NAME IS CORIN.**
> 
> **ORPHIE IS MY SISTER.**
> 
> **THIS IS OUR TREEHOUSE.**
> 
> **I AM STILL HERE.**

Corin stared at the words.

Then added another line beneath them.

> **ORPHIE WILL COME BACK.**

She stared at it for a long time.

Then whispered:

> **"...She will."**

---

# **THE BLACKSITE**

Far away, Orphie was unaware of the severity of Corin's deterioration.

She had finished collecting the supplies.

She had spoken with Lumi.

She was preparing to leave.

Lumi noticed Orphie's repeated glances toward the clock.

> **Lumi:**  
> "You're worried."

Orphie nodded.

> **"She's alone."**

Lumi understood immediately.

> **"Go."**

Orphie didn't need to be told twice.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin's greatest vulnerability is not necessarily her Ether.

It is isolation.

She can tolerate pain.

She can tolerate her body falling apart.

She can tolerate the world becoming grayscale.

What she struggles to tolerate is believing that she has been left behind.

For a few hours, the treehouse was empty.

To anyone else, that would have been nothing.

To Corin, it became a reminder of every person who had disappeared from her life.

Lumi.

The Blacksite.

Her old life.

Her memories.

And now, for a few hours—

**Orphie.**

She knew Orphie promised to return.

She wrote it down herself.

She held onto those words.

And somewhere far away, Orphie was already coming home.

**End of Restricted Safehouse / Medical Observation Log**
