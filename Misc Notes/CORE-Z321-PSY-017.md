# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-PSY-017**

### **RECURRING DREAM RECORD – ENDMIN MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Restricted Psychological Archive

---

## **SOURCE**

Over the course of several consecutive nights, Lindervene "Endmin" Merryweather voluntarily documented a recurring dream.

Each occurrence was nearly identical.

No evidence of anomalous influence has been identified.

The contents are recorded as recurring dreams and are not considered literal events.

---

## **DREAM ENVIRONMENT**

The setting never changed.

An endless gray expanse.

Silent.

Still.

Scattered throughout the void floated countless fragments of stone.

Each fragment drifted slowly, never touching another.

On the largest sat Belle.

Alone.

Exactly as before.

---

## **RECURRENT ELEMENTS**

Every dream repeated the same sequence.

Dark, faceless figures emerged around Belle.

They never struck her.

They never approached.

They simply spoke.

Their voices overlapped until individual words became impossible to distinguish.

Only their intent remained unmistakable.

Accusation.

Contempt.

Mockery.

The dream never revealed their faces.

Only the feeling they left behind.

Belle never answered them.

She simply listened.

---

## **ENDMIN'S ATTEMPTS**

Each night, Endmin tried something different.

She called Belle's name.

She ran.

She climbed between the floating rocks.

She jumped impossible distances.

She reached out her hand.

Every attempt ended the same way.

An unseen force pushed her back before she could reach Belle.

No matter how many times she tried...

The distance never truly became shorter.

---

## **FINAL DREAM**

On the most recent occurrence, one detail changed.

The figures continued speaking.

The floating rocks remained.

The silence between the voices remained.

Endmin reached Belle's stone once again.

This time...

Nothing pushed her away.

She stood only a few steps behind Belle.

Belle did not turn around.

She continued looking into the empty gray horizon.

Endmin quietly spoke.

> "...Belle."

For a long time...

Nothing happened.

Then Belle's shoulders moved almost imperceptibly.

Not enough to face her.

Only enough to show she had heard.

Endmin took one cautious step forward.

Belle finally spoke.

Her voice was calm.

Quiet.

Almost distant.

> "...Are you talking to me..."

A pause.

> "...Or the person you remember?"

The dream became silent.

Endmin searched for an answer.

None came.

Belle remained facing the horizon.

After another long silence, she asked one final question.

> "...Is Belle..."

Her voice was barely audible.

> "...Still here?"

Before Endmin could answer—

She woke.

---

## **POST-DREAM ENTRY**

Endmin recorded only one sentence in her notebook after waking.

> _"Next time, if there is one... I have to answer before I wake up."_

---

## **STATUS**

The recurring dream remains unexplained.

Medical review has found no indication of an external anomalous source.

Psychological staff note that recurring dreams commonly reflect unresolved fears, grief, or concerns rather than predicting future events.

The meaning of the final question remains a matter of personal interpretation by the dreamer.

**End of Record.**
