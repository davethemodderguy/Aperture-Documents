# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – PERSONIFIED ETHER RESONANCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Medical, Psychological & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **OVERVIEW**

During several voluntary counseling sessions, Corin provided additional information regarding the stabilized Ether resonance accompanying her.

The internal voice previously documented no longer presents to Corin as only an unseen presence.

Within dreams, moments of meditation, and periods of emotional distress, it consistently adopts the appearance of **her late mother**.

Historical records confirm that Corin and her siblings lost both parents in a tragic house fire during childhood, years before Corin's abduction and subsequent experimentation.

Medical staff emphasize that there is **no evidence** that the phenomenon is literally the consciousness or spirit of Corin's mother. Instead, it appears to be a manifestation shaped by Corin's memories and emotional needs, or another unexplained Ether phenomenon adopting a familiar and comforting form.

Its exact nature remains unknown.

---

## **PRESENTATION**

Corin describes the figure as appearing much as she remembers her mother.

She is never accompanied by fire.

Never injured.

Never frightening.

Instead, she is usually found in quiet places.

A kitchen.

A garden.

A warm living room.

Or sitting beside Corin beneath an endless evening sky.

The environments change.

The feeling does not.

---

## **RECORDED INTERACTIONS**

After one particularly severe nightmare, Corin described the following exchange.

She found herself standing alone in darkness.

Then a familiar voice spoke behind her.

> **"You don't have to keep walking by yourself."**

Corin turned.

Her mother was standing there with a gentle smile.

Corin immediately began crying.

> "...I couldn't save you."

The figure simply stepped closer.

> **"Sweetheart..."**

> **"None of that was ever yours to carry."**

The dream ended shortly afterward.

Corin reported waking calmer than she usually did after nightmares.

---

On another occasion, after Corin spent hours questioning whether she was still "normal," the figure quietly sat beside her.

No lecture.

No judgment.

Only a hand resting gently on Corin's shoulder.

> **"You are still my daughter."**

Corin later admitted that this was the sentence she remembered most clearly.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT**

Counselors note that the figure consistently fulfills a nurturing role.

It does not isolate Corin from others.

It does not encourage dependency.

Instead, it repeatedly encourages Corin to reconnect with the people already present in her life.

Examples include:

> **"Go have tea with Norma."**

> **"Arcturus is worried about you."**

> **"Belle has probably made too many cookies again."**

> **"You don't have to face today alone."**

These interactions have been interpreted as reinforcing healthy coping mechanisms rather than replacing real human relationships.

---

## **SAFEHOUSE**

One afternoon, Belle noticed Corin smiling to herself while watering plants outside SAFEHOUSE.

> "...Good memory?"

Corin nodded.

> "...Very."

Belle smiled back.

> "...Then I'm glad."

Corin looked toward the flowers.

> "...She always liked gardens."

Belle understood without another word.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' NOTE**

Director Lucious reviewed the evaluation before adding a short annotation.

> "Whether this manifestation originates from Ether, memory, or a combination of both, its influence has consistently been protective."

> "It encourages connection, hope, and recovery."

> "Until evidence suggests otherwise, it is to be regarded as part of Corin's therapeutic support, not as a threat."

---

## **STATUS**

Corin continues to experience the presence of the personified Ether resonance during periods of emotional stress and within certain dreams.

She does not believe it has replaced the mother she lost.

Rather, she describes it as a quiet reminder of the love she remembers and the future she is still trying to build.

When asked what the figure says most often before disappearing, Corin smiled softly.

> **"Keep going."**

> **"The people waiting for you love you."**

For Corin, those simple words are often enough to help her rise the next morning, straighten her jacket, and once again walk into the Accounting Department—not because the past has stopped hurting, but because she has chosen, one more day, to keep moving forward.
