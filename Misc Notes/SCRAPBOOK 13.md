# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **BLACKSITE CULTURE ARCHIVE**

### **PERSONNEL NOTE – "THE HUG PROTOCOL"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Internal Culture Record

---

## **SUMMARY**

Director **Belle Merryweather** is widely known throughout Heaven's Blacksite for one simple fact:

**She loves hugs.**

Unlike many personnel who prefer personal space, Belle genuinely enjoys physical affection from friends and trusted colleagues.

As a result, an unofficial tradition has developed among the residents of Project SAFEHOUSE and long-term Blacksite staff.

If Belle lets her guard down...

Someone is probably going to hug her.

---

## **COMMON OCCURRENCES**

Examples include:

- Greeting Belle with a quick hug after returning from a mission.
    
- Celebrating successful operations.
    
- Comforting Belle after a difficult day.
    
- Belle walking into SAFEHOUSE and immediately being greeted by hugs.
    
- Group hugs during birthdays and holidays.
    

Belle has never objected to hugs from people she trusts.

In fact, she usually hugs them back.

---

## **THE CHAIN REACTION**

Personnel have observed an amusing phenomenon.

One person hugs Belle.

Another sees it.

Then another.

Within moments...

Belle has somehow become the center of an impromptu group hug.

Researchers have jokingly named this:

> **"Hug Cascade."**

---

## **SAFEHOUSE OBSERVATIONS**

During one evening gathering, Belle sat down on the sofa to read.

Within ten minutes:

- Endmin leaned against her.
    
- Star hugged her from one side.
    
- Frost quietly rested beside her.
    
- Taiga joined after asking first.
    
- Bao smiled and wrapped an arm around her shoulders.
    
- Astral followed.
    
- Niko squeezed in wherever there was room.
    

Belle simply laughed.

> "...This keeps happening."

## **STATUS**

While no official "hug protocol" exists, Belle's fondness for hugs has become a small but cherished part of everyday life at Heaven's Blacksite.

Personnel are reminded that, despite Belle's enthusiasm, consent and personal boundaries always apply. Belle is happy to share hugs with friends—but only when she's comfortable with it.
