# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **RESTRICTED ANOMALOUS WEATHER / SAFEHOUSE INCIDENT LOG**

# **THE ONES WAITING ON THE OTHER SIDE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Location:** Blacksite / Safehouse Perimeter  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Phenomenon:** **ANOMALOUS SUPPRESSION STORM**  
**Status:** **ACTIVE / NO CONTACT**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

While Corin remains isolated inside the remote treehouse, the situation on the other side of the storm is becoming increasingly tense.

Everyone knows she is alone.

Everyone knows what prolonged isolation does to her.

Everyone knows that Orphie is desperately trying to return.

And everyone knows the most frustrating part:

**There is currently nothing they can do.**

The storm suppresses anomalous abilities indiscriminately.

The Architects cannot simply override it.

Conventional travel remains impossible.

Communication remains severed.

The treehouse is inaccessible.

For the first time, the people who normally protect Corin are forced to simply wait.

---

# **THE ARCHITECTS**

## **Guiding Light**

Guiding Light is taking the situation particularly badly.

She can perceive the treehouse's general location.

She knows Corin is still alive.

But she cannot reach her.

Her blue notes have completely stopped.

The familiar glow that normally accompanies her messages is gone.

She repeatedly attempts to push even a fragment of her presence through the suppression field.

Nothing works.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "I can see her."

A pause.

> **"I just can't reach her."**

For someone whose very purpose is guidance, the helplessness is devastating.

---

## **Curious Light**

Curious Light has been investigating the storm constantly.

Normally, the phenomenon would be a fascinating problem.

This time, it is personal.

He has attempted to determine:

- Its origin.
    
- Its range.
    
- Its suppression mechanism.
    
- Its duration.
    
- Its interaction with Ether.
    
- Why it affects Architects.
    
- Why conventional communications fail.
    

Every attempt has produced incomplete results.

Eventually, Curious stopped taking notes.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "...I hate this."

He knows Corin is alone.

And there is no experiment he can perform that will safely reach her.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie is arguably the most distressed person on the Blacksite side.

She knows exactly what Corin's mind does when she is gone too long.

She knows about the notebook.

She knows what Corin writes when she is alone.

And now she has been separated from her for days.

Orphie repeatedly attempts to find a route through the storm.

Every route is impassable.

She refuses to accept that waiting is the only option.

But eventually, even she has to stop.

> **Orphie:**  
> "...She's going to think I left her."

Nobody answers.

Because everyone knows she probably already does.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESIDENT REACTIONS**

## **Nightshade**

Nightshade remains unusually quiet.

She understands that there is no safe way to reach the treehouse.

She spends much of her time near Orphie.

Not speaking.

Just staying nearby.

---

## **Neko**

Neko has attempted to find unconventional routes through the Blacksite perimeter.

Every possible route has failed.

He has spent hours studying weather data and anomalous readings.

Eventually:

> **Neko:**  
> "...Nothing."

For once, there is no clever workaround.

---

## **Miss Neko**

Miss Neko has been keeping Neko from doing anything reckless.

> **Miss Neko:**  
> "You cannot walk into that."

Neko looks toward the storm.

> **"...I know."**

> **"Then stop trying."**

He reluctantly does.

---

## **Niko**

Niko is deeply worried.

He keeps asking whether the storm has weakened.

The answer has remained the same.

**No.**

---

## **Belle Merryweather**

Belle has become increasingly anxious.

She keeps asking whether Corin has food.

Whether she has enough blankets.

Whether Orphie will be able to return.

At one point, Belle quietly prepares a small care package anyway.

Nobody tells her that Corin cannot receive it yet.

---

## **Endmin Merryweather**

Endmin spends most of his time beside Belle.

He understands the situation better than most.

There is nothing he can do.

And he hates that fact.

> **Endmin:**  
> "...She must feel completely alone."

---

## **Frost**

Frost is particularly unsettled by the storm.

Her own cryokinetic abilities are suppressed within its influence.

The fact that something involving cold can suppress even her abilities is deeply unusual.

She keeps checking the readings.

Still nothing.

---

## **Bao**

Bao attempts to keep everyone calm.

She prepares food.

Makes drinks.

Encourages people to rest.

But even she keeps looking toward the storm.

She knows who is on the other side.

---

## **Taiga**

Taiga spends an unusually long time sitting near the observation area.

Normally, Taiga is perfectly comfortable sleeping anywhere.

This time, she barely sleeps.

She keeps watching.

Waiting for the storm to weaken.

---

## **Mart**

The Marts have become unusually subdued.

Their usual happiness-driven activity has decreased noticeably.

Even the little creatures seem to understand that something is wrong.

One Mart sits near the entrance wearing its top hat.

It refuses to move.

---

## **Astral**

Astral has been monitoring the situation quietly.

She has repeatedly asked whether Guiding Light has managed to send anything.

The answer remains:

**No.**

Astral eventually sits beside Orphie.

Neither says anything.

---

# **FREQUENT VISITORS**

## **Lucy Merryweather**

Lucy is deeply concerned.

She understands what prolonged isolation can do to someone already struggling with abandonment.

She remains close to the others and repeatedly asks for updates.

There are none.

---

## **Kent (Keith)**

Kent has been attempting to coordinate practical solutions.

Routes.

Transport.

Communication.

Weather reports.

Every option ends at the same conclusion.

**The storm prevents it.**

> **Kent:**  
> "...We're just waiting."

---

## **Remielle (Cinder)**

Remielle is visibly frustrated.

She hates situations where there is an obvious problem but no actionable solution.

She spends much of her time near the communications equipment.

The result never changes.

**NO CONNECTION.**

---

# **THE ROOM**

Eventually, almost everyone gathers together.

Nobody is celebrating.

Nobody is talking loudly.

The SAFEHOUSE feels unusually empty despite being full of people.

Orphie sits quietly.

Guiding Light cannot manifest.

Curious Light continues staring at incomplete readings.

Neko keeps checking the equipment.

Miss Neko watches him.

Belle holds the care package she prepared.

Endmin sits beside her.

Frost watches the weather monitor.

Bao prepares another round of drinks.

Taiga sits on the floor.

Mart remains near the entrance.

Astral watches the storm.

Lucy, Kent, and Remielle wait for updates that never arrive.

Everyone is waiting for the same thing.

**The storm to stop.**

---

# **THE WORST PART**

Nobody has forgotten Corin.

Nobody has abandoned her.

Nobody has decided she is too difficult.

Nobody has stopped caring.

The people Corin believes have left her are all gathered on the other side.

Worried.

Waiting.

Trying.

Unable to reach her.

And somewhere beyond the storm, Corin is sitting alone in her treehouse, believing the exact opposite.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The suppression storm has created a cruel separation.

**Corin thinks she has been abandoned.**

**Everyone else is terrified that she thinks that.**

Orphie wants to go home.

Guiding Light wants to reach her.

Curious Light wants to understand the storm.

The SAFEHOUSE residents want Corin safe.

The frequent visitors want answers.

Nobody can provide them.

The storm does not care about affection.

It does not care about promises.

It does not care about Architects.

It simply continues.

And for now, all anyone can do is wait.

**End of Restricted Anomalous Weather / Safehouse Incident Log**
