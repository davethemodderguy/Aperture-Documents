# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **KCA-PER-023**

### **PERSONNEL ADDENDUM – EXECUTIVE SECRETARY "CINDER"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Personnel Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

The callsign **"Cinder"** is not arbitrary.

It was assigned after repeated demonstrations of her anomalous ability to manipulate and control fire.

While her primary role is administrative and executive support, Cinder possesses documented **firebending** capabilities that supplement her conventional combat training.

---

## **ANOMALOUS ABILITY**

### **Pyrokinesis (Firebending)**

Cinder is capable of generating, shaping, and directing fire through anomalous means.

Observed applications include:

- Igniting flames without external ignition sources.
    
- Controlling the direction and intensity of existing fires.
    
- Creating concentrated bursts of flame.
    
- Sustaining controlled flames for illumination or emergency use.
    
- Heating metal equipment during field repairs or rescue operations.
    

Her control is noted to be exceptionally precise, allowing her to use fire in confined environments with minimal unintended spread when conditions permit.

---

## **TACTICAL APPLICATION**

Despite possessing firebending abilities, Cinder rarely relies on them as her primary means of combat.

Her preferred order of engagement remains:

1. Multi-Purpose Tactical Briefcase.
    
2. Conventional firearms and support weapons.
    
3. Firebending when tactically appropriate.
    
4. Reinforced gauntlets as an emergency close-quarters contingency.
    

Training officers have noted that she favors integrating small, controlled uses of fire into broader tactical plans rather than overwhelming opponents with raw destructive force.

---

## **Origin of the Callsign**

During her early government service, fellow personnel observed her extinguishing a dangerous fuel fire after safely evacuating nearby civilians.

Rather than allowing the blaze to spread, she manipulated it with remarkable precision until firefighters arrived.

One colleague reportedly remarked:

> **"You don't leave flames behind."

> **"You leave cinders."**

The nickname remained.

It was eventually adopted as her official operational callsign.
