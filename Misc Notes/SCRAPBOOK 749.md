### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE / MEMORIAL ARCHIVE**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — THE HAT RETURNED**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — MEMORIAL / PERSONNEL RECORD  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** **PRESENT / STABLE**

---

# **THE RETURN**

Corin had kept Alice's hat ever since the disappearance.

For weeks, it had been one of the most cherished objects in the SAFEHOUSE.

It had sat beside Alice's blank journal.

It had been held during sleepless nights.

It had been kept close because, for a while, it was one of the only things anyone had left of her.

Now Alice was back.

And the hat belonged to her.

---

# **THE MOMENT**

Alice was sitting quietly on the edge of her bed.

Her white hair was slightly messy.

Her long ears rested naturally against the sides of her head.

She looked tired, as usual.

Corin entered the room carrying the hat.

Alice looked up.

She recognized it immediately.

Her eyes followed Corin as she approached.

Corin knelt in front of her.

> "I think this belongs to you."

She held it out.

Alice reached for it.

Her hands were still weak.

She took the hat carefully.

For a moment, she just stared at it.

---

# **PUTTING IT BACK**

Alice placed the hat on her head.

It was slightly crooked.

Corin gently adjusted it.

Alice's ears popped out from underneath it.

Corin smiled.

> "There."

Alice touched the brim.

She looked at herself in the mirror.

Then smiled.

---

# **ALICE'S JOURNAL**

Alice opened her journal.

She wrote:

> **"Corin give Alice hat back."**

She paused.

Then added:

> **"Hat is Alice."**

Another pause.

> **"Alice like hat."**

Corin had to look away for a moment.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin had spent so long treating the hat as something precious because Alice was gone.

Giving it back felt strangely different.

It meant she wasn't holding onto a relic anymore.

She was giving something back to a living little girl.

Corin gently touched Alice's head.

> "Take good care of it."

Alice nodded.

---

# **THE SAFEHOUSE**

Everyone noticed the change immediately.

Alice was wearing her hat again.

Belle smiled.

Star nearly cried.

Neko quietly said:

> "Looks better on her."

Nightshade nodded.

Lumi smiled.

Orphie simply watched Alice for a few seconds.

There was something deeply reassuring about it.

The hat wasn't sitting on an empty chair anymore.

It wasn't beside a blank journal.

It wasn't being held as a memorial.

It was on Alice's head.

Where it belonged.

---

# **BELLE**

Belle approached Alice.

> "You look cute."

Alice's ears lifted slightly.

She wrote:

> **"Alice always cute."**

Belle laughed.

> "There she is."

---

# **CORIN'S PRIVATE THOUGHT**

Later, Corin admitted that giving the hat back was harder than she expected.

She had become attached to it.

Not because she wanted to keep it.

Because she had spent so much time believing that it was all she had left.

Now she realized something.

The hat was never meant to be a replacement for Alice.

It was always meant to be **Alice's hat.**

And Alice was finally wearing it again.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Alice sat quietly in the SAFEHOUSE.

Her scooter was nearby.

Her journal rested on the table.

Her friends were around her.

And her soft hat was back where it belonged.

On her head.

She reached up and adjusted it.

Then, after thinking for a moment, she wrote one final sentence:

> **"Alice don't want lose hat again."**

Corin smiled.

> "You won't."

Alice nodded.

Then leaned against her.

For the first time in a long time—

the hat was not a memorial.

It was simply hers.
