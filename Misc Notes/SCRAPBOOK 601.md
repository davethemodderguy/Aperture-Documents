### **SAFEHOUSE MEDICAL / INCIDENT REPORT**

# **CORIN — PRESSURE COOKER INCIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Location:** SAFEHOUSE Kitchen  
**Subjects:** Corin / Belle Merryweather  
**Incident:** Pressure Cooker Explosion  
**Status:** **Corin Recovering / Flight Temporarily Impossible**

---

# **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

Belle and Corin were preparing dinner together in the SAFEHOUSE kitchen when a pressure cooker malfunctioned.

The resulting explosion occurred at close range.

Belle was directly within the danger zone.

Corin reacted immediately.

She spread her wings and moved between Belle and the explosion.

Her **left wing absorbed the majority of the impact**.

Belle was protected.

Corin was not.

---

# **LEFT WING DAMAGE**

The left wing sustained catastrophic damage.

Medical personnel have classified the wing as:

> **100% injured.**

The damage was extensive enough that **every feather on the affected wing was shed**.

The amount of fallen feathers was extraordinary.

A single sack was required to collect them.

The empty wing was subsequently wrapped in medical bandages.

---

# **REGENERATION**

Corin's regenerative factor activated almost immediately.

The damaged tissue began repairing itself.

The process is expected to be slow.

Her regeneration has already demonstrated the ability to completely restore severely damaged tissue, but the wing contains an enormous amount of complex biological and Ether-infused structures.

C.O.R.E. therefore expects recovery to take time.

Corin is currently prohibited from flight.

---

# **THE FEATHERS**

The kitchen floor was covered in white feathers.

They were collected carefully rather than discarded.

Several SAFEHOUSE residents initially mistook the enormous pile for insulation material.

One researcher reportedly asked:

> **"Why is there a sack of Corin?"**

The question was corrected immediately.

It is, in fact:

> **A sack of Corin's feathers.**

---

# **BELLE**

Belle was unharmed.

Corin's wing had successfully shielded her from the worst of the explosion.

Belle's reaction to this fact was not relief.

She immediately focused on Corin.

She knelt beside her while medical personnel treated the damaged wing.

Corin remained unusually quiet.

---

# **CORIN'S EMOTIONAL STATE**

Corin is **very, very sad**.

The physical injury is significant.

However, the loss of her wing's functionality has affected her emotionally far more than the medical staff initially expected.

Flying has become an important part of Corin's life.

It is not merely transportation.

It is freedom.

It is recreation.

It is something she shares with the SAFEHOUSE residents.

Most importantly—

**flying keeps her mind occupied.**

---

# **WHY FLIGHT MATTERS**

Corin has a difficult past.

When she is flying, she is focused on the wind.

Her wings.

Her movements.

The sky.

The people flying with her.

There is little room for memories.

Flight gives her something else to think about.

It allows her to be present.

Without it, the thoughts she normally escapes can return.

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

After the wing was bandaged, Corin repeatedly looked toward it.

She attempted to move it.

Pain immediately stopped her.

She lowered her head.

Her ears drooped.

Her remaining wing folded tightly against her body.

She looked toward the window.

Outside, the sky was visible.

Corin quietly whispered:

> **"...I can't fly."**

Belle sat beside her.

> **"Not yet."**

Corin looked down.

> **"...I know."**

---

# **BELLE'S GUILT**

Belle has repeatedly insisted that Corin should not have shielded her.

Corin disagrees.

There was no hesitation when the pressure cooker exploded.

Corin simply acted.

Belle was more important to her than the wing.

Belle eventually held Corin's hand.

> **"You saved me."**

Corin looked at her.

> **"Of course I did."**

---

# **RECOVERY PROTOCOL**

Until the left wing has completely regenerated:

- Corin is prohibited from flight.
    
- The injured wing must remain protected.
    
- Physical exertion is minimized.
    
- Regeneration is monitored continuously.
    
- Pain levels are monitored.
    
- Adequate nutrition and hydration are maintained.
    
- Corin should not be left alone for prolonged periods.
    
- SAFEHOUSE residents are encouraged to remain available for emotional support.
    

The collected feathers are being stored until C.O.R.E. determines whether they retain anomalous properties.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The pressure cooker exploded.

Corin reacted without thinking.

Her left wing became a shield.

Belle walked away unharmed.

Corin did not.

Now she sits quietly with her injured wing wrapped in bandages, surrounded by enough fallen feathers to fill an entire sack.

Her regeneration is already working.

The wing will heal.

The feathers will return.

Eventually, she will fly again.

But Corin doesn't know that yet.

Right now, she can only look toward the sky and mourn the one thing that had become her escape.

Flying kept the past quiet.

Without it, the memories may become louder.

So the SAFEHOUSE residents remain close.

Not because Corin is physically helpless.

But because, for now, she needs someone beside her until her wings can carry her again.
