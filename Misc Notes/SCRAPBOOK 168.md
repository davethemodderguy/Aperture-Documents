# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE – WINTER OBSERVATION LOG**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – PROLONGED OUTDOOR ISOLATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

During the current winter period, Corin Lockwood ceased entering Project SAFEHOUSE for extended intervals.

Rather than using her assigned room, she remained outside the facility grounds for hours at a time.

The behavior represents a continuation of her preference for isolation and open spaces.

Medical Services continue to monitor the behavior due to concerns regarding her emotional wellbeing.

---

# **OBSERVED BEHAVIOR**

Corin is most frequently located in the snowfields surrounding the Blacksite.

She often sits beneath large trees or in secluded areas where snowfall is heaviest.

On multiple occasions, Security observed Corin quietly covering herself with fresh snow until only part of her outline remained visible.

She remained motionless for long periods, reading a book or simply watching the sky.

When snowfall became heavy enough to conceal her almost completely, she made no attempt to uncover herself until she chose to leave.

---

# **AVOIDANCE**

Corin has become increasingly careful not to be found.

She routinely chooses locations away from common patrol routes.

If she notices personnel approaching, she quietly relocates before they arrive.

She has also been observed waiting until outdoor recreation areas are empty before entering them.

Her intent appears to be minimizing the chance that anyone will interrupt her solitude.

---

# **MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

Medical Services note that Corin's preference for remaining outdoors is driven by emotional factors rather than physical necessity.

Her current physiology allows her to tolerate harsh environmental conditions better than most people, but prolonged exposure can still place strain on her body.

Staff continue to encourage regular meals, hydration, and opportunities to warm herself, while respecting her request for personal space whenever it can be done safely.

---

# **LUMI**

One afternoon, Lumi eventually found Corin sitting beneath a blanket of fresh snow.

Only the corner of her book remained visible.

Lumi quietly brushed a little snow away from Corin's shoulder.

Corin didn't look up.

> "...How did you find me?"

Lumi smiled.

> "You're still my daughter."

Corin closed her book.

> "...I wanted to be alone."

Lumi nodded.

She stood back.

> "I know."

After a pause, she added,

> "I'll be inside if you decide you don't want to be alone anymore."

Lumi did not ask Corin to return.

She simply walked back toward SAFEHOUSE.

Several hours later...

Corin quietly followed.

She stopped at the entrance.

Looked at the door.

Then turned away again.

---

# **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE**

The residents have noticed Corin's absence.

Her place at dinner remains available.

The lights in her room are left functional.

No one has reassigned her space.

No one assumes she is gone forever.

Belle still folds a clean blanket every evening.

Bao continues preparing extra soup.

Mart occasionally leaves a bookmark on Corin's desk, knowing she may eventually see it.

No one searches every hour.

No one forces her indoors.

They simply make sure that whenever Corin chooses to return, there is still a place waiting for her.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin remains emotionally withdrawn and continues spending much of her time outside the SAFEHOUSE.

Medical Services recommend maintaining regular welfare checks from a respectful distance and preserving opportunities for Corin to reconnect on her own terms, while remaining prepared to intervene should her safety become an immediate concern.

**End of Project SAFEHOUSE Winter Observation Log**
