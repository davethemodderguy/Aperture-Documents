# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neri hosted a lottery.

The top prize was a **Night Stalker**.

No application process was announced.

No performance evaluation was conducted.

No interviews were scheduled.

No cake was required.

Personnel were simply given the opportunity to enter.

This immediately caused significant interest throughout Sector 4-C.

The prize was, after all, a Night Stalker.

Only four units had previously been authorized:

- **NS-001 — Director Lucious**
    
- **NS-002 — Junior MSAC Operator**
    
- **NS-003 — Jane Doe**
    
- **NS-004 — Neko — Research Authorization**
    

One additional unit remained available.

Neri decided to give it away.

---

# 2. LOTTERY RULES

The rules were handwritten by Neri and posted on the cafeteria notice board.

They read:

> **NIGHT STALKER LOTTERY**
> 
> **1. Put name here.**
> 
> **2. Don't cheat.**
> 
> **3. Winner gets Night Stalker.**
> 
> **4. If you win, don't be stupid.**
> 
> **5. That's it.**

Neko reviewed the notice.

**NEKO:**  
"That's the entire ruleset?"

Neri nodded.

**NEKO:**  
"You're giving away a classified anomalous weapon through a raffle."

Neri nodded again.

**NEKO:**  
"Do you have any idea how many regulations this violates?"

Neri looked at him.

Pause.

She wrote:

> **"Probably."**

Neko stared at the paper.

**NEKO:**  
"That's not reassuring."

---

# 3. PARTICIPATION

The lottery attracted personnel from across the Blacksite.

Engineering.

Security.

Research.

Logistics.

Medical.

Administration.

MSAC.

Several employees entered almost immediately.

Others entered after learning what the prize was.

One employee reportedly asked:

> "Is this a joke?"

Neri shook her head.

The employee entered.

Another asked:

> "Do I need to pass your five requirements?"

Neri shook her head.

> "So literally anyone can win?"

Neri nodded.

The employee entered.

A third asked:

> "Can I buy extra tickets?"

Neri immediately shook her head.

The lottery remained one entry per person.

---

# 4. MSAC RESPONSE

MSAC personnel initially treated the lottery as a joke.

This changed approximately four minutes after the announcement.

An MSAC operator entered.

Then another.

Then another.

Within twelve minutes, a significant portion of MSAC personnel had entered the lottery.

One operator reportedly stated:

> "If one of us wins, I'm buying them dinner."

Another responded:

> "If one of us wins, I'm asking to inspect it."

A third:

> "If one of us wins, Jane is going to laugh at us."

The room became silent.

---

Jane Doe was informed about the lottery.

Then at Neri.

Then at the MSAC personnel gathered around the entry box.

**JANE:**

Neri nodded.

**JANE:**  
"To a random person?"

Neri nodded.

**JANE:**

# 6. THE DRAW

At 18:00, Neri conducted the drawing.

The entry slips were placed inside a container.

Neri mixed them manually.

No anomalous assistance was detected.

No external interference was detected.

Neri reached into the container.

She removed one slip.

She unfolded it.

Read the name.

Looked up.

Then held it out.

The winner was:

> **MSAC OPERATOR — [REDACTED]**

There was approximately three seconds of silence.

Then:

**MSAC PERSONNEL:**  
"NO WAY."

The winning operator appeared to require several seconds to process what had happened.

**OPERATOR:**  
"...I won?"

Neri nodded.

**OPERATOR:**  
"The Night Stalker?"

Neri nodded again.

**OPERATOR:**  
"...Actually?"

Neri handed him the authorization document.
