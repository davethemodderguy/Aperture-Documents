## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-SAFEHOUSE-WILDLIFE / OCULAR ANOMALY REPORT**

# **ALICE LOCKWOOD — RIGHT EYE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Alice Lockwood  
**Status:** **PERMANENTLY BLIND / ANOMALOUS OCULAR ACTIVITY**

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Following the unexplained disappearance of the eye marking from Alice's eyepatch, medical personnel conducted a closer examination of her damaged right eye.

The result was unexpected.

The eye is unquestionably damaged.

It remains incapable of normal vision.

Yet it is not empty.

It is **blue.**

Not an ordinary blue iris.

The coloration appears to originate from within the damaged ocular structure itself.

---

# **THE RIFT**

Deep within the eye is an anomalous structure resembling a tiny rift.

It does not appear to be physically embedded in the eye.

Rather, it appears to exist **through** it.

The interior seems deeper than the physical dimensions of the eye should allow.

Observation produces the strange impression of looking into something distant.

Something impossibly far away.

The anomaly emits a faint blue glow.

Very soft.

Not bright enough to illuminate the room.

Just enough to be noticed.

---

# **VISUAL CHARACTERISTICS**

The phenomenon has been described as:

- Deep azure coloration
    
- Soft internal luminescence
    
- Rift-like central structure
    
- Apparent depth exceeding physical anatomy
    
- No detectable pupil response
    
- No conventional retinal response
    
- No measurable visual function
    
- Persistent low-level glow
    

The glow does not appear to fluctuate with ambient lighting.

It remains present even in complete darkness.

---

# **THE MESMERIZING EFFECT**

Personnel who look directly into the eye consistently describe it as:

> **"Mesmerizing."**

Not painful.

Not frightening.

Simply difficult to look away from.

The longer it is observed, the more compelling it becomes.

One technician described the sensation as:

> **"Like staring into a tiny piece of the night sky."**

Another wrote:

> **"It looks like there's somewhere else behind her eye."**

No personnel have reported hallucinations or direct anomalous influence.

Nevertheless, prolonged observation is discouraged.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin was present during the examination.

She stared at Alice's eye for several seconds.

Then whispered:

> **"...It's beautiful."**

Alice looked confused.

> **"My bad eye?"**

Corin nodded.

> **"Yeah."**

Alice touched her eyepatch.

> **"It doesn't work."**

Corin looked at the blue glow.

> **"Maybe not like the other one."**

---

# **ALICE**

Alice appears completely unaware of the anomaly's appearance.

She cannot see through the damaged eye.

When asked what she feels from it, she answered:

> **"Nothing."**

> **"Does it hurt?"**

> **"No."**

> **"Can you see anything?"**

Alice shook her head.

> **"Just dark."**

This suggests that whatever the rift represents, Alice herself does not perceive its contents.

---

# **NEKO**

Neko attempted to scan the eye.

The scanner returned contradictory readings.

The physical dimensions of the eye were normal.

The internal spatial readings were not.

Neko checked the equipment.

Then checked it again.

Then replaced the scanner.

The results remained identical.

His conclusion:

> **"...That's not supposed to fit in there."**

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi found the eye strangely familiar.

She could not explain why.

She only knew that the blue glow reminded her of something.

Something distant.

Something she couldn't quite remember.

She eventually stopped looking.

> **"I don't think we should stare at it too much."**

Nobody disagreed.

---

# **MEDICAL CLASSIFICATION**

The right eye remains medically classified as:

> **Permanently blind.**

The anomalous rift does not appear to restore visual function.

It does, however, indicate that the eye underwent a transformation not accounted for by conventional regenerative processes.

The relationship between the rift and Alice's natural regeneration remains unknown.

---

# **CURRENT HYPOTHESES**

### **Hypothesis A — Residual Anomalous Damage**

The original injury may have altered the eye at a fundamental level.

### **Hypothesis B — Dimensional Aperture**

The apparent rift may represent a microscopic connection to another space.

### **Hypothesis C — Dormant Ability**

The structure may be an undeveloped or inaccessible manifestation of an existing anomalous trait.

### **Hypothesis D — Unknown**

No existing classification adequately explains the phenomenon.

---

# **IMPORTANT**

Personnel are reminded:

**Do not attempt to interact with the rift.**

Do not touch it.

Do not attempt to photograph the interior using experimental equipment.

Do not attempt to determine whether anything exists "on the other side."

And, most importantly:

**Do not tell Alice that there might be something inside her eye watching her.**

She has enough things to worry about.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Alice's right eye was once simply considered damaged.

Then it was considered permanently blind.

Now it appears to contain something else.

A tiny blue rift.

Quiet.

Softly glowing.

Beautiful.

Impossible.

It does not move.

It does not blink.

It does not appear to look at anyone.

Yet everyone who sees it gets the same strange feeling.

As though they aren't looking **at** an eye.

They're looking **through** one.

And somewhere inside that impossible blue depth—

something might be looking back.
