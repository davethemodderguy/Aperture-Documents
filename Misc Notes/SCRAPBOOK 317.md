# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – RESEARCH ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: FINAL TWO ELDER MARTS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Research Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Following the confirmed identification of three of the five Elder Marts, Research Division requested clarification regarding the remaining two.

Both Ambassadors provided the same answer.

> "They're everywhere."

After further questioning, the statement was clarified.

The remaining two Elder Marts **do not occupy fixed roles or locations.**

Instead, they appear throughout both the Heaven Blacksite and the Krampus Compliance Authority seemingly at random.

---

# **CONFIRMED INFORMATION**

According to both Ambassadors:

- The remaining Elder Marts are active.
    
- They regularly appear in both facilities.
    
- They perform ordinary Mart activities.
    
- They possess no special authority.
    
- They intentionally avoid standing out.
    

Researchers asked whether they could simply be pointed out.

Both Ambassadors politely declined.

---

# **TRACKING ATTEMPTS**

Following the interview, Research Division initiated multiple observation projects.

Results included:

- One suspected Elder Mart spent three hours shelving books before disappearing into a crowd.
    
- Another candidate delivered tools to Engineering, then vanished among several dozen Marts.
    
- A third suspected Elder turned out to simply be standing closer to the window, making it appear more opaque.
    

No confirmed identifications were made.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Three Elder Marts have been positively identified:

- Camera Mart.
    
- KCA Ambassador.
    
- Blacksite Ambassador.
    

The remaining two continue to live anonymously among the other **3,497** Marts.

Researchers now accept that they may be encountered at any time, in any room, performing the same ordinary acts as every other Mart.

Helping someone carry a book.

Watering a flower.

Sharing a cheese cracker.

Or simply saying—

> "Hello."

The Ambassadors insist that the remaining Elders are not hiding.

They're just...

Being Mart.
