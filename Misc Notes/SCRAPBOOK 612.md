### **ANOMALOUS MATERIAL / CORIN FEATHER REPORT**

# **CORIN — DORMANT FEATHER FLAME**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 — C.O.R.E. RESTRICTED  
**Material:** Dormant Corin Feathers  
**Phenomenon:** Persistent Azure Flame  
**Status:** **Active / No Extinguishing Method Known**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

C.O.R.E. has identified another property of Corin's shed feathers.

Once a feather becomes **dormant**, it can spontaneously transition into a persistent flame state.

The resulting flame is:

> **Bright light-azure blue.**

Despite its appearance, the flame produces **no detectable heat**.

It does not burn the feather.

It does not consume surrounding material.

And most importantly:

> **It does not require oxygen.**

The phenomenon continues indefinitely.

---

# **ACTIVATION**

A dormant feather can be activated through several methods.

### **Method 01 — Ignition**

The feather can simply be set on fire.

Once the flame establishes itself, the characteristic light-azure flame appears.

### **Method 02 — Mechanical Activation**

The feather can also be **snapped**.

The effect resembles activating a glowstick.

The feather immediately begins producing its characteristic blue flame.

The mechanism behind this transition is unknown.

---

# **FLAME CHARACTERISTICS**

The flame is highly unusual.

|Property|Observation|
|---|---|
|Color|**Light azure blue**|
|Temperature|**No measurable heat**|
|Oxygen requirement|**None**|
|Fuel consumption|**None observed**|
|Duration|**Indefinite**|
|Smoke|**None**|
|Ash|**None**|
|Odor|**Faint vanilla**|
|Extinguishing method|**Unknown**|

The flame behaves visually like ordinary fire while violating nearly every normal requirement for combustion.

---

# **BLACKOUT APPLICATION**

Corin discovered the phenomenon's practical use during a Blacksite blackout.

Rather than searching for conventional emergency lighting, she simply removed several dormant feathers.

She activated them.

Then placed each burning feather inside a glass jar.

The result was an improvised lantern.

The jars emitted a soft, bright azure glow throughout the room.

No batteries.

No fuel.

No oxygen.

No heat.

Just Corin's feathers.

---

# **CORIN'S "LANTERNS"**

Corin has since developed a habit of keeping several sealed jars containing burning feathers available for emergencies.

The jars are particularly effective because the flame:

- Does not heat the container.
    
- Does not consume oxygen.
    
- Does not produce smoke.
    
- Does not extinguish when sealed.
    
- Continues burning indefinitely.
    

The resulting lights are surprisingly pleasant.

The faint vanilla scent is considered an additional benefit.

---

# **CONTAINMENT TEST**

Researchers placed an activated feather inside a completely sealed container.

The flame continued burning.

The container was subsequently placed inside a vacuum chamber.

The flame continued burning.

The chamber was then filled with an inert atmosphere.

The flame continued burning.

C.O.R.E. eventually stopped the experiment.

The only conclusion reached was:

> **"Apparently it doesn't care."**

---

# **EXTINGUISHING TESTS**

Multiple methods have been attempted.

The flame has resisted:

- Smothering.
    
- Vacuum exposure.
    
- Water.
    
- Foam.
    
- Conventional fire suppression agents.
    
- Oxygen displacement.
    
- Cryogenic exposure.
    
- Physical isolation.
    

No reliable extinguishing method has been identified.

The flame therefore remains **permanently active once initiated**, unless future research discovers otherwise.

---

# **SAFETY NOTICE**

Although the flame produces no heat, personnel are forbidden from treating activated feathers as ordinary decorative objects.

The mechanism remains poorly understood.

In particular:

> **Do not attempt to "put one out."**

If an activated feather is discovered, personnel should leave it in place and contact C.O.R.E. anomalous-material containment staff.

---

# **BLACKOUT PROTOCOL**

C.O.R.E. has provisionally approved dormant Corin feathers as an emergency illumination source.

In the event of a major power failure:

1. Retrieve dormant feathers.
    
2. Activate them through ignition or snapping.
    
3. Place them in approved transparent containers.
    
4. Deploy throughout the affected area.
    
5. **Do not attempt to extinguish them afterward.**
    

The system is effectively self-sustaining.
