**Classification:** OMEGA PRIORITY – ACTIVE EMERGENCY

> **NOTICE:** This incident is maintained as a **Krampus Compliance Authority Emergency Sublog** and is **not** stored under the CORE-Z321 residential logs due to its governmental significance.

# **EMERGENCY DECLARATION**

**Date:** ██/██/████

At **08:42**, Director **Belle Merryweather** failed to report to her scheduled briefing.

Initial attempts to contact her through secure government channels received no response.

Her office at Krampus Compliance Authority Headquarters was found unlocked.

Her access card, weapon, communications device, and personal effects were **not** present.

Director Belle Merryweather's current location is **unknown**.

---

# **PROTOCOL INVOKED**

Pursuant to the **Banlands Emergency Continuity Protocols**, the unexplained disappearance of any Krampus Compliance Authority officer constitutes a national emergency.

The disappearance of the **Director** automatically elevates the incident to the highest emergency classification.

Under Banlands law, such an event is treated as a potential **act of terrorism or hostile action** until evidence indicates otherwise.

---

# **EMERGENCY RESPONSE**

The following measures were immediately authorized:

- City-wide manhunt initiated.
    
- All transportation checkpoints activated.
    
- Dimensional transit points monitored.
    
- Government facilities placed on heightened security.
    
- Krampus Compliance Authority personnel recalled from leave.
    
- Central Execution Department placed on operational standby.
    
- Banlands Enforcement Division assigned civilian protection duties.
    

Aperture Industries was formally notified due to its standing alliance with the Banlands.

---

Belle was last positively identified leaving Krampus Compliance Authority Headquarters after working late into the evening.

Security footage shows her carrying her oversized sword across her back.

She appeared calm.

No signs of distress were observed.

After leaving the building...

No verified sightings exist.
