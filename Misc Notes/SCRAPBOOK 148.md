### **RESEARCH BRIEF**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – ETHER-DEPENDENT PHYSIOLOGY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – C.O.R.E. Research Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corin Lockwood's physiology remains one of the least understood biological phenomena documented by Aperture Industries.

Despite repeated examinations, no conventional medical explanation fully accounts for her continued survival and normal cognitive function.

While Corin outwardly behaves much like an ordinary fifteen-year-old, internally her body does not conform to known human physiology.

---

# **OBSERVED CHARACTERISTICS**

Repeated examinations have consistently documented the following:

- No detectable heartbeat.
    
- No spontaneous respiratory activity.
    
- Extremely low body temperature.
    
- Minimal detectable thermal signature.
    
- Greatly altered circulatory function compared to an ordinary human.
    
- Normal speech, movement, cognition, and voluntary motor control.
    
- Intact digestive capability despite not requiring food for survival.
    

Corin **can** eat and drink normally.

She enjoys tea, milk, and fruit.

However, Medical Services have concluded that nutrition is no longer required to sustain her life.

She eats because she chooses to.

Not because her body depends on it.

Similarly, Corin can voluntarily breathe for speech or comfort, but routine respiration does not appear necessary for continued function.

---

# **THE ETHER REPLACEMENT THEORY**

The leading hypothesis within C.O.R.E. Research is informally known as the **Ether Replacement Theory**.

The theory proposes that, following the fatal experimentation and subsequent Ether-based revival, hostile Ether fundamentally altered Corin's physiology.

Rather than restoring normal biological function, the Ether assumed many of the roles ordinarily performed by living tissue.

Researchers theorize that Ether now continuously supplements essential cellular processes, replacing functions that would normally depend on active circulation and other vital systems.

Proposed functions include:

- Maintaining cellular activity.
    
- Supplying energy to tissues.
    
- Preserving neural function.
    
- Preventing widespread tissue degradation.
    

In effect, the Ether appears to act as a biological support system unlike anything previously documented.

At present, no direct mechanism has been identified.

---

# **LIMITATIONS OF CURRENT UNDERSTANDING**

Despite years of study, one central question remains unanswered.

> **How is Corin still alive?**

Every known biological model predicts that her body should be incapable of sustaining consciousness.

Yet she speaks.

She walks.

She laughs.

She works.

She remembers.

The phenomenon remains beyond the explanatory power of current medical science.

---

# **RESEARCH ETHICS**

Director Lucious has placed strict restrictions on research involving Corin.

No invasive studies are authorized.

No procedures may be conducted solely to satisfy scientific curiosity.

All examinations must provide a direct medical benefit to Corin.

The order concludes:

> "We will not repeat the mistakes of those who treated a child as a specimen."

---

# **CORIN'S RESPONSE**

When informed that researchers still did not understand her condition, Corin quietly replied:

> "...Good."

When asked why, she answered:

> "...I don't want anyone else becoming this."

No further explanation was offered.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin Lockwood's Ether-dependent physiology remains an active subject of research.

Although numerous theories exist, none fully explain how she continues to function despite the absence of several physiological processes considered essential to ordinary human life.

Research into the phenomenon continues under strict ethical oversight, with the understanding that Corin herself is not a research objective.

She is a patient.

A colleague.

A member of Project SAFEHOUSE.

And above all else—

A person.

**End of Research Brief**
