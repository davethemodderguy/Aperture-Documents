# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION REPORT**

## **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – IDENTITY CONFLICT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 7 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Despite measurable progress in Corin Lockwood's recovery, one belief remains deeply rooted.

Corin continues to see herself as something that should not exist.

She does not describe herself as surviving.

She describes herself as having been forced back.

Her body, sustained by Ether following the experimentation that killed her, has become the focus of her self-hatred.

Medical staff note that this belief is persistent and resistant to reassurance.

---

# **RECORDED CONVERSATION**

Late one evening, after everyone else had gone to bed, Corin sat quietly with Lumi.

She stared at her own hands for several minutes before speaking.

> "...I hate this."

Lumi waited.

Corin continued.

> "Every morning I wake up..."

> "...and it's still me."

She looked down.

> "The experiments."

> "The Ether."

> "Everything they did."

> "It's still here."

A long silence followed.

Then Corin whispered:

> "...I just wanted a normal life."

Lumi remained beside her.

Corin's voice became quieter.

> "Instead..."

> "...I became the reminder of everything they took away."

---

# **LUMI'S RESPONSE**

Lumi did not argue.

She did not dismiss Corin's pain.

She knew those feelings came from years of trauma, loss, and abuse.

Instead, she gently took Corin's hand.

> "You hate what was done to you."

Corin did not answer.

> "That is not the same as you."

Corin closed her eyes.

Lumi continued.

> "Those people chose to hurt you."

> "You did not choose any of it."

Another silence.

Then Lumi smiled warmly.

> "When I look at you..."

> "...I don't see an experiment."

> "I don't see Ether."

> "I don't see a corpse."

She squeezed Corin's hand.

> "I see my daughter."

---

# **CORIN**

Tears quietly rolled down Corin's face.

> "...I don't."

Lumi's expression softened.

Corin continued.

> "When I look in a mirror..."

> "...all I see is what they turned me into."

Lumi reached up and gently brushed a strand of hair away from Corin's face.

> "Then don't borrow your reflection."

Corin looked confused.

Lumi smiled.

> "Borrow my eyes."

> "Because every time I look at you..."

> "...I still see the little girl who used to ask me a hundred questions before breakfast."

> "She grew."

> "She suffered."

> "She survived."

> "But she never stopped being my daughter."

---

# **OBSERVATION**

Medical Services noted that Corin did not suddenly feel better after the conversation.

She still struggled.

She still questioned herself.

She still viewed her condition through the lens of the trauma she endured.

However, she remained with Lumi for the rest of the evening instead of withdrawing into isolation.

The conversation was not considered a solution.

It was considered another small step in helping Corin separate her identity from the abuse inflicted upon her.

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' ANNOTATION**

Director Lucious reviewed the report.

His comment read:

> "Trauma teaches people to see themselves through the eyes of those who harmed them."

> "Recovery begins when they can slowly learn to see themselves through the eyes of those who love them."

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin's recovery remains ongoing.

She continues to struggle with her self-image and the lasting consequences of the experiments performed on her.

Lumi continues to provide steady companionship, not by denying Corin's pain, but by reminding her of something Corin still finds difficult to believe.

To Lumi, Corin is not defined by Ether.

Not by scars.

Not by the years that were stolen.

She is, and always will be,

Her daughter.

**End of Psychological Observation Report**
