# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **EXECUTIVE PERSONNEL WELFARE REPORT**

### **SUBJECT: CORIN LOCKWOOD – PROLONGED SOCIAL WITHDRAWAL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Executive Personnel Only

---

## **EXECUTIVE ASSET STATUS**

Among the KCA's executive leadership, four individuals are consistently regarded as indispensable to the organization's operation.

- **Belle Merryweather** – Director of Operations.
    
- **Arcturus Lockwood** – Security Advisor.
    
- **Norma Lockwood** – Senior Manager and Chief Engineering Coordinator.
    
- **Corin Lockwood** – Head of Accounting.
    

Each possesses knowledge, experience, and responsibilities that are exceptionally difficult to replace.

However, internal wellness reviews unanimously reached the same conclusion.

**Corin is considered the most psychologically vulnerable member of the four.**

Her value to the KCA is not measured solely by her extraordinary analytical ability.

It is measured by the person she is.

---

## **CHANGE IN ROUTINE**

Several weeks ago, personnel noticed a gradual change.

Corin stopped making her regular visits to Heaven's Blacksite.

She no longer joined Belle for tea.

She no longer accepted Norma's invitations to dinner.

She no longer sat with Arcturus after work discussing the day's events.

Instead...

She remained inside KCA Headquarters.

Her office slowly became more than a workplace.

It became where she spent her evenings.

Then her nights.

Eventually, it became the place she returned to instead of anywhere else.

When asked where she was going after work, she answered simply:

> "...Home."

Security records confirmed she had walked only a few steps.

Into her office.

---

## **OBSERVATIONS**

The office itself changed over time.

A kettle appeared.

Several blankets were folded neatly beside a small sofa.

A bookshelf filled with accounting manuals gradually made room for novels, sketchbooks, and a small potted plant.

A framed photograph remained inside a drawer instead of on display.

The room became comfortable.

Orderly.

Quiet.

Yet every member of the executive staff shared the same impression.

It never truly felt like home.

It felt like somewhere Corin had convinced herself she should stay.

---

## **WELLNESS INTERVIEW**

During a voluntary counseling session, Corin was asked why she had stopped visiting SAFEHOUSE.

She took a long time to answer.

> "...I don't know."

Another pause.

> "...Every time I think about going..."

She looked toward the floor.

> "...Something tells me to turn around."

The counselor documented that Corin did not describe fear of the people waiting for her.

Instead, she described an overwhelming sense that she no longer belonged there.

This belief was assessed as a trauma-related perception rather than an accurate reflection of reality.

---

## **SAFEHOUSE**

Belle continued preparing an extra cup of tea each evening.

Sometimes it was left untouched.

Norma still cooked enough for one more person.

Arcturus occasionally glanced toward the empty chair before quietly continuing the conversation.

No one removed Corin's place at the table.

No one assumed she was gone for good.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' NOTE**

Director Lucious declined several suggestions to pressure Corin into returning.

Instead, he issued a simple instruction.

> "Do not treat the office as her prison."

> "Do not treat SAFEHOUSE as an obligation."

> "Keep the light on."

> "When she is ready to come back, she should find exactly what she left."

He later added another handwritten sentence beneath the directive.

> "A home is not defined by where someone sleeps."

> "It is the place where they know they will still be welcomed."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin continues to carry out her responsibilities with the same precision expected of the Head of Accounting.

Her office remains impeccably organized.

Her reports are submitted on time.

Her calculations remain flawless.

Yet those who know her best recognize that competence does not always reflect wellbeing.

For now, she calls her office home.

The people waiting at Heaven's Blacksite continue to hope that, one evening, she will walk through the SAFEHOUSE door again—not because anyone demanded it, but because she finally feels ready.

**End of Executive Personnel Welfare Report**
