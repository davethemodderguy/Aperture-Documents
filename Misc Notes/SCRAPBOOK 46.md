# **WEAPON PHILOSOPHY**

Arcturus firmly believes that a weapon should be:

- Reliable.
    
- Accurate.
    
- Simple to maintain.
    
- Familiar to its user.
    

He has repeatedly stated that his revolver and lever-action rifle already do exactly what he needs them to do.

When asked why he refuses further modifications, he replied:

> "A dependable tool doesn't need unnecessary complications."
