# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **CORE-ANOMALOUS WEAPONS DIVISION**

# **SPAWN WEAPONRY — NON-EUCLIDEAN FORCE DISRUPTION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Restricted / Anomalous Weapons Intelligence  
**Threat Designation:** **SPAWN**  
**Subject:** Dark Matter Energy Impulse Rifle and Related Weaponry  
**Status:** **CONFIRMED HIGH-THREAT**

---

# **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

Further analysis of the confiscated **SPAWN Dark Matter Energy Impulse Rifle** has revealed a critical property previously unknown to C.O.R.E.

SPAWN weaponry is specifically engineered to interact with and disrupt **Non-Euclidean forces** present within anomalous organisms and entities.

In practical terms, this means SPAWN weapons are not limited to harming conventional biological targets.

They are capable of directly affecting entities whose physical existence, energy composition, or defensive properties would normally make them resistant or immune to conventional weaponry.

### **Operational conclusion:**

> **SPAWN weaponry can hurt almost anything.**

This includes, but is not limited to:

- Humans.
    
- Animals.
    
- Thirens.
    
- Anomalous organisms.
    
- Entities.
    
- Angels.
    
- Supernatural beings.
    
- Entities composed partially or entirely of non-standard energy.
    
- Certain higher-order anomalous lifeforms.
    

The weapons therefore represent a significant threat to virtually every form of life currently housed or operating within the Blacksite.

---

# **NON-EUCLIDEAN FORCES**

C.O.R.E. uses the term **Non-Euclidean Force** to describe certain anomalous physical and metaphysical structures that do not behave according to conventional biological or physical principles.

These forces may exist within:

- Anomalous entities.
    
- Dimensional organisms.
    
- Supernatural beings.
    
- Angelic entities.
    
- Thirens.
    
- Reality-altered organisms.
    
- Energy-based lifeforms.
    
- Other anomalous structures.
    

Normally, conventional weapons interact poorly with such targets.

A bullet may pass through an energy-based entity.

A physical barrier may have no meaning to a dimensional organism.

A conventional weapon may fail entirely against a being whose defensive structure exists outside ordinary physical space.

SPAWN weaponry appears specifically designed to bypass this limitation.

---

# **DARK MATTER ENERGY IMPULSE RIFLE**

The confiscated rifle produces an energy discharge capable of interacting with Non-Euclidean structures.

Rather than simply transferring conventional kinetic or thermal energy into a target, the weapon appears to interfere with the target's underlying anomalous structure.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

However, controlled testing demonstrated that the weapon can produce measurable disruption in anomalous materials that conventional weapons cannot meaningfully affect.

---

# **TARGET COMPATIBILITY**

Testing indicates that the weapon does not discriminate based on whether a target is:

**Biological**

**Anomalous**

**Supernatural**

**Energy-based**

**Dimensional**

or

**Metaphysical**

If a target possesses a sufficiently coherent structure that can be interacted with, SPAWN weaponry appears capable of damaging it.

This is believed to be intentional.

SPAWN hunters were not equipped merely to hunt unusual animals.

They were equipped to hunt **things that ordinary weapons cannot reliably hurt.**

---

# **THIRENS**

Thiren physiology provides a particularly concerning example.

Although thirens possess biological bodies, many individuals possess anomalous characteristics that introduce Non-Euclidean components into their physical structure.

Consequently:

> **Thiren status provides no meaningful protection against SPAWN weaponry.**

A thiren can be injured by a SPAWN weapon in the same fundamental manner as any other vulnerable target.

---

# **ANGELIC ENTITIES**

The same applies to angelic entities.

The assumption that an angelic or supernatural being would be inherently protected from conventional weapons is already unreliable.

SPAWN technology makes that distinction even less relevant.

Testing indicates that the weapon can interact with anomalous energy structures comparable to those found in higher-order supernatural entities.

Therefore:

> **Being an angel does not make an individual immune to SPAWN weaponry.**

This finding has been escalated to all departments responsible for anomalous entity containment.

---

# **WHY THIS MATTERS**

A conventional hostile organization can be contained using conventional security doctrine.

SPAWN presents a different problem.

Their hunters appear to possess technology specifically intended to defeat the natural advantages of anomalous beings.

An entity may be:

- Stronger than a human.
    
- Faster than a human.
    
- Resistant to conventional ammunition.
    
- Capable of phasing through matter.
    
- Protected by supernatural energy.
    
- Constructed from anomalous material.
    

None of these properties can currently be assumed to provide reliable protection against SPAWN weapons.

---

# **SPAWN'S PURPOSE**

This discovery provides additional insight into SPAWN's ideology.

Their stated objective concerns obtaining a **"Second Chance at Life."**

Their methodology involves hunting lifeforms capable of existing beyond conventional biological limitations.

The specialized nature of their weapons suggests that this is not incidental.

SPAWN has apparently spent considerable resources developing or obtaining equipment capable of killing beings that normal hunters could never hope to kill.

This raises an important question:

> **What exactly do they need these beings for?**

The answer remains unknown.

---

# **UNKNOWN MANUFACTURER**

The technology remains far beyond what C.O.R.E. currently associates with conventional cult weapon manufacturing.

The Dark Matter Energy Impulse Rifle appears purpose-built.

Its ability to interact with Non-Euclidean forces implies knowledge of anomalous physics that SPAWN may not necessarily possess independently.

The possibility remains that the unidentified third party supplying SPAWN is responsible for the weapons' specialized properties.

The supplier remains unidentified.

---

# **SECURITY DIRECTIVE**

Effective immediately:

### **All SPAWN weaponry is to be classified as UNIVERSAL ANOMALOUS THREAT EQUIPMENT.**

Personnel are not to assume immunity based on:

- Species.
    
- Entity classification.
    
- Anomalous resistance.
    
- Angelic status.
    
- Thiren physiology.
    
- Dimensional nature.
    
- Energy composition.
    
- Existing protective abilities.
    

No personnel are to intentionally test SPAWN weaponry against living subjects without direct C.O.R.E. authorization.

---

# **ALICE**

This finding has resulted in an immediate escalation of Alice's protection status.

Alice is a confirmed supernatural individual.

She is therefore a viable target for SPAWN weaponry.

Her anomalous abilities do not provide sufficient assurance of protection.

Given her demonstrated fear of the SPAWN symbol, her previous disappearance, the empty grave associated with her mother, and the subsequent appearance of SPAWN personnel near the Blacksite perimeter, C.O.R.E. considers the possibility of a historical connection increasingly credible.

Alice is not to be exposed to recovered SPAWN weaponry.

She is not to be informed of its capabilities unless medically and operationally necessary.

---

# **FINAL ASSESSMENT**

The most dangerous characteristic of SPAWN's weapons is not their destructive power.

It is their **compatibility.**

They do not care whether something is human, animal, thiren, angel, or anomalous.

They were designed to cross the boundary between conventional and supernatural existence.

A target cannot rely on being:

> **"Something that bullets can't hurt."**

Because SPAWN appears to have built its entire arsenal around defeating exactly that assumption.
