### **TREEHOUSE / CORIN WELFARE REPORT**

# **CORIN — WITHDRAWAL EVENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 10 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Orphie Status:** **RECOVERED**  
**Corin Status:** **MISSING / WITHDRAWN**  
**Primary Concern:** **ABANDONMENT RESPONSE**

---

# **ORPHIE'S DISAPPEARANCE — RESOLUTION**

The mystery surrounding Orphie's eight-month absence has finally been resolved.

Orphie did not intentionally leave.

While returning to the Treehouse, she fell into an isolated cave.

She survived for approximately eight months using available cave vegetation, primarily:

- Cave berries.
    
- Edible vines.
    
- Naturally occurring water.
    

She was eventually able to escape.

Upon returning, Orphie immediately attempted to locate Corin.

By then, however, Corin was gone.

---

# **THE DISTINCTION**

Orphie was lost.

Corin does not know that.

From Corin's perspective, the only thing she experienced was:

> **Orphie left.**

> **Orphie never came back.**

> **Everyone kept living.**

> **The Treehouse became quiet.**

> **And eventually, there was nobody left to wait for.**

The explanation arrived after Corin had already reached her conclusion.

---

# **CORIN HAS STOPPED BEING AROUND**

Following Orphie's return, Corin has ceased appearing around the Treehouse, SAFEHOUSE, or the usual locations she frequented.

She does not respond to calls.

She does not answer messages.

She does not appear during family visits.

She has not been observed flying.

She has not been observed using her miniature cat form.

She has simply...

**stopped being around.**

No confirmed location has been established.

---

# **THE FEATHER PHENOMENON**

Every known burning feather remains severely dimmed.

The flames continue to burn.

But only faintly.

The phenomenon began before Corin disappeared and has persisted afterward.

Researchers have been unable to determine whether the weakened flames are:

- A symptom of Corin's condition.
    
- A consequence of her withdrawal.
    
- A remote anomalous response.
    
- Or something else entirely.
    

No conclusion has been reached.

---

# **ABANDONMENT**

C.O.R.E. psychological analysis has identified a recurring vulnerability in Corin:

> **Abandonment.**

Few things affect Corin as deeply.

The possibility of being left behind has repeatedly caused disproportionate emotional distress.

This incident represents the most prolonged exposure to that fear ever recorded.

Corin waited for Orphie for months.

Then she waited longer.

Eventually, she stopped believing that Orphie would return.

And when Orphie finally did return...

Corin was no longer there to see it.

---

# **THE NOTE**

The handwritten note remains the strongest indication of Corin's emotional state.

> **"You proved I'm right, thank you."**  
> **—Corin**

The statement is now understood to be referring to her belief that Orphie had abandoned her.

The belief was incorrect.

But by the time the truth became available, Corin had already internalized it.

---

# **ORPHIE**

Orphie has taken the discovery extremely hard.

She has repeatedly attempted to explain what happened.

> **"I didn't leave you."**

> **"I got lost."**

> **"I was trying to come home."**

> **"I had your flowers."**

The flowers are still with her.

They are the same rare blue flowers she originally intended to give Corin.

Corin has not seen them.

---

# **THE MOST DANGEROUS PART**

Corin is not angry.

She is not attacking anyone.

She is not threatening anyone.

She is not destroying property.

She is simply absent.

That absence concerns C.O.R.E. more than overt hostility would.

Corin has historically expressed herself through movement, flight, humor, feathers, and interaction with others.

Now there is very little of any of it.

---

# **LUMI**

Lumi continues searching.

So do Arcturus and Norma.

The SAFEHOUSE residents have resumed searching alongside Orphie.

Nobody is treating Corin's disappearance as punishment.

Nobody is assuming she will simply return when she feels better.

Everyone understands the seriousness of her withdrawal.

---

# **ORPHIE'S STATEMENT**

During the latest search briefing, Orphie reportedly said:

> **"She thought I hated her."**

Nobody answered.

Orphie looked down at the blue flowers in her hands.

> **"I was trying to bring these back."**

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie did not abandon Corin.

She fell into a cave.

She survived.

She fought her way home.

She came back carrying flowers.

But Corin had spent eight months believing something else.

And that belief became heavier with every passing day.

Abandonment has always been one of the closest things to killing Corin's spirit.

Not because it physically harms her.

Because it makes her believe that the people she loves can live perfectly well without her.

This time, that belief lasted long enough to make her disappear.

Orphie is finally home.

But the person she came home for is gone.

And somewhere beyond the reach of the Treehouse's lights, Corin is alone.
