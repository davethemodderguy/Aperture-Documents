# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **BLACKSITE CAFETERIA NOTICE**

### **DIETARY PREFERENCES – ROOM N-07 RESIDENTS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Cafeteria Services

---

## **SUMMARY**

Cafeteria Services has observed a consistent dietary preference among Belle Merryweather and most members of the Friendly Roommates.

While none of them are prohibited from eating fried foods, they generally avoid foods that are heavily deep-fried or excessively oily.

The preference is largely personal and fitness-related rather than medical.

---

## **OBSERVED PREFERENCES**

The following personnel generally prefer:

- Fresh vegetables.
    
- Fruit.
    
- Grilled foods.
    
- Steamed dishes.
    
- Soups.
    
- Salads.
    
- Lean meats.
    
- Light snacks.
    

Heavily oil-fried meals are usually declined when alternatives are available.

---

## **BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

Belle enjoys sweets almost as much as carrots.

Despite her sweet tooth, she rarely chooses greasy or heavily fried meals.

Her usual choices include:

- Carrots.
    
- Pancakes.
    
- Cake.
    
- Mint leaves.
    
- Candy (in moderation).
    
- Fresh water or soda.
    

Belle has commented:

> "...Too much oil just makes me feel sluggish."

---

## **FRIENDLY ROOMMATES**

Most members of Room N-07 have similar eating habits.

Although preferences vary from person to person, the group generally favors lighter meals that support their active lifestyles.

This has become something of an unspoken habit within the household.

---

## **FITNESS**

Medical evaluations indicate that the group maintains excellent physical condition through:

- Regular physical activity.
    
- Frequent combat training.
    
- Recreational sports.
    
- Balanced diets.
    

Researchers have jokingly referred to Room N-07 as:

> **"The healthiest group that still manages to consume an alarming amount of pancakes and cake."**
