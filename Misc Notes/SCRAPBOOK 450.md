### **CELESTIAL ARCHITECTURE / BEHAVIORAL ADDENDUM**

# **CURIOUS LIGHT — BLACKSITE VISITS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – CELESTIAL AUTHORITY  
**Designation:** **CURIOUS LIGHT**  
**Role:** Architect / Builder / Explorer  
**Relation:** Guiding Light's younger brother  
**Status:** **ACTIVE — BLACKSITE ACCESS PERMITTED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Curious Light has begun making occasional visits to the Blacksite.

Unlike Guiding Light, who often interacts with personnel through calm guidance and conversation, Curious Light generally visits for a specific purpose.

Usually:

**Research.**

**Construction.**

**Observation.**

Or simply because he found something interesting.

Personnel are advised not to assume that his usually energetic and curious personality means he is always approachable.

Curious Light can be remarkably **cold and easily annoyed when interrupted**.

---

# **BEHAVIORAL PROFILE**

Curious Light is highly focused when working.

Once he becomes interested in a project, his attention can become almost completely absorbed by it.

During these periods, interruptions are poorly received.

He does not necessarily become aggressive.

Instead, his demeanor simply changes.

His voice becomes colder.

His responses become shorter.

His golden radiance may become noticeably dimmer.

And his patience disappears.

---

# **COMMON RESPONSE**

A personnel member once interrupted Curious Light while he was examining an unknown dimensional structure.

> **Personnel:**  
> "Excuse me, can I—"

Curious Light didn't look up.

> **Curious Light:**  
> "No."

The personnel stopped.

> **Personnel:**  
> "...I haven't asked yet."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "I know."

---

# **BLACKSITE VISITS**

Curious Light's visits are not considered unauthorized.

Security systems cannot meaningfully restrict him.

His presence is tolerated because he has demonstrated no hostile intent toward the Blacksite.

He usually appears without warning.

A golden glow may appear somewhere inside the facility.

A rhombus-shaped star manifests.

Then Curious Light is simply there.

---

# **WHAT HE DOES**

During Blacksite visits, Curious Light has been observed:

- Studying Blacksite technology.
    
- Examining anomalous containment systems.
    
- Inspecting dimensional equipment.
    
- Building small experimental devices.
    
- Asking engineers highly specific questions.
    
- Taking notes.
    
- Disassembling things he has been explicitly told not to disassemble.
    

The last behavior has resulted in several security complaints.

Curious Light insists that he was:

> **"Putting it back better."**

Engineering has been unable to determine whether this is technically true.

---

# **THE BLACKSITE ENGINEERS**

Engineers have developed mixed feelings toward Curious Light.

On one hand, his knowledge is extraordinary.

He can identify technologies that no human engineer has encountered before.

On the other hand...

he has a habit of silently appearing behind someone and examining their work.

One engineer reportedly turned around to find Curious Light staring at a machine.

> **Engineer:**  
> "...How long have you been standing there?"

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Long enough."

> **Engineer:**  
> "...That's concerning."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "Your power regulation is inefficient."

> **Engineer:**  
> "...Okay."

Curious Light walked away.

The engineer later spent three hours redesigning the regulator.

It worked better.

---

# **INTERRUPTION WARNING**

Personnel should avoid interrupting Curious Light when he is:

- Building.
    
- Studying.
    
- Experimenting.
    
- Reading.
    
- Repairing something.
    
- Trying to understand an unfamiliar phenomenon.
    

If interruption is unavoidable, approach calmly.

Do not shout.

Do not grab him.

Do not touch his equipment.

And most importantly:

**Do not ask what he's doing while he's concentrating.**

He will tell you when he's finished.

---

# **GUIDING LIGHT'S ADVICE**

When asked how to deal with her brother's temper, Guiding Light simply responded:

> **"Leave him alone."**

The personnel member asked:

> **"That's it?"**

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "That's it."

> **"...What if he gets annoyed anyway?"**

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Then you interrupted him twice."

---

# **BLACKSITE SOCIALIZATION**

Despite his coldness when interrupted, Curious Light is not inherently antisocial.

Once he finishes whatever he is doing, he can become surprisingly conversational.

He enjoys:

- Discussing discoveries.
    
- Showing people his creations.
    
- Answering technical questions.
    
- Learning about unfamiliar technology.
    
- Exploring the Blacksite.
    

The difference is simply that **he chooses when the conversation begins**.

---

# **RELATIONSHIP WITH GUIDING LIGHT**

Guiding Light is one of the few beings who can interrupt Curious Light without immediately irritating him.

Even she occasionally receives a cold response.

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Curious."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "...What?"

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "You've been in that laboratory for six hours."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "And?"

> **Guiding Light:**  
> "Come outside."

> **Curious Light:**  
> "...Fine."

He follows her.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Curious Light may be one of reality's Architects.

He may be capable of building things beyond the understanding of ordinary civilizations.

He may be everywhere at once.

But when he visits the Blacksite, personnel quickly discover a much simpler truth:

**He's busy.**

And if someone interrupts him while he's working...

the cheerful curiosity disappears.

The golden light becomes colder.

The answers become shorter.

And everyone suddenly remembers that the Architect they're talking to is also someone who has absolutely no patience for being disturbed.

**End of Celestial Architecture / Behavioral Addendum**
