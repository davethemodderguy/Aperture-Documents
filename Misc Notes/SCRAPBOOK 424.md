## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / FAMILY DYNAMICS REPORT**

# **ORPHIE — THE UNRECOGNIZED CAUSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Personnel Welfare / Psychological Concern  
**Subjects:** Orphie / Corin  
**Associated Personnel:** Lumi / Arcturus / Norma / SAFEHOUSE Residents / KCA  
**Status:** **CRITICAL — CAUSALITY UNRECOGNIZED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie remains unaware of the extent to which her disappearance contributed to Corin's psychological collapse.

She recognizes that Corin became unstable while she was gone.

She recognizes that Corin is no longer functioning normally.

She recognizes that Corin lost her position as Lead Accountant of the KCA.

What she does **not** recognize is that much of Corin's current condition developed as a direct consequence of losing her.

Orphie believes she is merely dealing with the aftermath.

She does not realize that, from Corin's perspective, **Orphie's disappearance was the central event that caused the collapse.**

---

# **ORPHIE'S UNDERSTANDING**

When asked about Corin's condition, Orphie stated:

> "...She should've handled it better."

When asked whether she believed her disappearance affected Corin:

> "...Obviously it affected her."

When asked whether she believed she was responsible:

> "...No."

A pause.

> "...She chose how she reacted."

---

# **THE MISSING PIECE**

Orphie does not understand the difference between:

> **"I caused this."**

and

> **"My actions were a major factor in this happening."**

She sees Corin's instability as Corin's own failure to cope.

She does not account for the circumstances.

Corin had already been emotionally vulnerable.

Orphie was her closest constant.

Then Orphie disappeared without warning.

Corin spent fourteen days alone.

She waited.

She stopped sleeping properly.

She began talking to an absent person.

She became increasingly detached from reality.

The Marts became frightened of her.

Her work deteriorated.

And eventually, the KCA removed her from duty.

---

# **ORPHIE'S IRONY**

Orphie spent months believing:

> **"I am a burden."**

She then deliberately removed herself from Corin's life.

She believed that doing so would make things easier for everyone.

Instead, Corin deteriorated.

Now Orphie is angry at Corin for the consequences of the exact isolation Orphie believed would help.

She does not recognize the contradiction.

---

# **A CONVERSATION**

Orphie confronted Corin after another missed KCA assignment.

> **Orphie:** "You're still doing this."

> **Corin:** "...I know."

> **Orphie:** "You need to get yourself together."

Corin stared at the floor.

> "...I'm trying."

> **Orphie:** "Try harder."

Corin was silent.

Then:

> "...You left me."

Orphie stopped.

> "...What?"

Corin looked at her.

> "...You left me alone."

Orphie:

> "...You knew I wanted to be alone."

> "...You were the one who left."

> "...Yes."

> "...And I broke."

Orphie stared at her.

> "...That's not my fault."

Corin looked away.

> "...Okay."

She did not argue.

That somehow made the conversation worse.

---

# **LUMI KNOWS**

Lumi understands.

She knows Corin's history.

She knows what Orphie meant to her daughter.

She knows how severely the disappearance affected her.

But she also knows that simply telling Orphie:

> **"This is your fault."**

would accomplish nothing.

Orphie would become defensive.

Or she would simply shut down.

Lumi therefore tries to make her understand the connection rather than assign blame.

---

# **LUMI'S WARNING**

Lumi eventually told Orphie:

> "...You don't have to blame yourself."

Orphie looked at her.

> "...I don't."

> "...That's not what I mean."

> "...Then what?"

Lumi paused.

> "...You need to understand what happened to Corin."

Orphie:

> "...I know what happened."

Lumi:

> "...No."

> "...You know the events."

> "...You don't understand them."

Orphie said nothing.

---

# **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus has been more direct.

> "...You disappeared."

> "...Yes."

> "...She lost her mind waiting for you."

Orphie:

> "...That's her problem."

Arcturus stared at her.

> "...Do you actually believe that?"

Orphie:

> "...Yes."

Arcturus lowered his head.

> "...Then you're still not seeing it."

---

# **NORMA**

Norma refuses to frame the situation as simple blame.

> "...Nobody is saying you intentionally hurt her."

Orphie:

> "...Then why are you talking to me?"

> "...Because you need to know."

> "...Know what?"

Norma looked directly at her.

> "...Your absence had consequences."

---

# **CORIN**

Corin herself rarely blames Orphie.

That may be the most concerning part.

When someone says:

> "...Orphie caused this."

Corin immediately responds:

> "...Don't blame her."

Even after everything.

Even after losing her job.

Even after two weeks of isolation.

Even after losing her grip on reality.

Corin still protects Orphie.

---

# **THE CONTRADICTION**

Orphie believes:

> **"I don't matter."**

Corin's condition demonstrates the opposite.

Orphie mattered so much to Corin that losing her caused catastrophic psychological damage.

Neither sister seems capable of accepting this fact.

Orphie cannot accept that she was important.

Corin cannot accept that she was dependent on someone so deeply.

---

# **C.O.R.E. ASSESSMENT**

It would be inaccurate to state that Orphie intentionally caused Corin's condition.

She did not.

Her disappearance was an act of self-isolation, not an attempt to psychologically harm her sister.

However, it is equally inaccurate to pretend that her actions had no causal relationship to Corin's deterioration.

The current assessment is:

> **Orphie's disappearance was the primary triggering event in Corin's recent psychological collapse.**

Orphie does not currently understand this.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Orphie keeps asking why Corin became like this.

Why she stopped sleeping.

Why she stopped functioning.

Why the KCA removed her.

Why she became unstable.

Why the Marts are afraid of her.

Why everyone looks at Orphie differently whenever Corin is mentioned.

The answer is uncomfortable.

Corin did not simply become unstable on her own.

She was already vulnerable.

Then Orphie disappeared.

And Corin spent two weeks believing she had lost the person she depended on most.

Orphie returned.

But the damage remained.

Now Orphie stands beside the consequences of her disappearance without recognizing herself as part of their cause.

She is furious that Corin is broken.

She does not understand that, in trying to disappear from everyone's life...

**she nearly took Corin with her.**

**End of Family Dynamics Report**
