# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **PERSONNEL RECORD ADDENDUM**

### **SUBJECT: BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Internal Reference

---

## **COMMON MISCONCEPTION**

One of the most persistent misconceptions among new personnel concerns Director Belle Merryweather's age.

Due to her height, plush-like appearance, and energetic personality, many assume she is significantly younger than she actually is.

This assumption is incorrect.

---

## **OFFICIAL RECORD**

**Name:** Belle Merryweather

**Age:** **20 years**

The Personnel Department confirms that Director Belle Merryweather is a fully grown adult.

Her small stature is a natural characteristic of her appearance and is not indicative of her age.

---

## **PERSONNEL NOTICE**

Employees are reminded to avoid making assumptions based solely on appearance.

Questions such as:

> "Wait... you're twenty?"

or

> "I thought you were twelve..."

have become common enough that Human Resources has added this clarification to the orientation handbook.

---

## **BELLE'S RESPONSE**

When informed that new employees were once again surprised by her age, Belle simply laughed.

> "Yep."

> "Still twenty."

She then took another bite of her carrot before adding:

> "Being short has its advantages."

Belle declined to specify what those advantages were.
