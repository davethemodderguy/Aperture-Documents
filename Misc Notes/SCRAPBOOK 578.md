# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **SAFEHOUSE PERSONNEL OBSERVATION**

# **CORIN — TWIN-TAIL BOWS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Event:** **Decorative Tail Accessories**  
**Status:** Approved / Permanently Adorable

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Belle Merryweather has begun tying decorative bows around Corin's **two tails**.

The modification is entirely cosmetic.

No anomalous properties have been detected in the bows.

Corin initially appeared confused by the sudden addition of accessories to her tails.

She ultimately allowed Belle to keep them there.

---

# **THE INCIDENT**

Belle approached Corin carrying two small bows.

She asked:

> **"Can I put these on your tails?"**

Corin looked at the bows.

Then at Belle.

Then at her tails.

> **"...Why?"**

Belle responded:

> **"Because they'll look cute."**

Corin considered this for several seconds.

> **"...Okay."**

Belle proceeded to carefully tie one bow around each tail.

---

# **RESULT**

The bows remained securely attached while Corin moved normally.

They do not interfere with:

- Walking.
    
- Running.
    
- Flying.
    
- Backflips.
    
- Wing deployment.
    
- Feline transformation.
    
- Tail movement.
    

Corin's tails remain fully mobile.

She can still move them independently despite the decorations.

---

# **REACTION**

Corin initially appeared embarrassed.

She repeatedly touched the bows with one hand.

Eventually, she looked at herself in a reflective surface.

She paused.

Then quietly said:

> **"...They're actually nice."**

Belle was extremely pleased.

---

# **SAFEHOUSE RESPONSE**

The reaction was overwhelmingly positive.

**Neko:**

> "She's even more cat now."

**Endmin:**

> "...Cute."

**Miss Neko:**

> "They suit her."

**Orphie:**

> "...Keep them."

Corin did not object.

---

# **MINIATURE FORM**

The bows remain attached when Corin involuntarily transforms into her palm-sized feline form.

This produces an unexpected result:

A tiny white winged kitten with two tails, each decorated with a small bow.

SAFEHOUSE personnel have collectively agreed that this is:

> **Extremely cute.**

Corin has requested that nobody make a big deal about it.

This request has not been respected.

---

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Corin continues to wear the bows.

She has not removed them.

Belle has since prepared additional bows in different designs.

C.O.R.E. has determined that there is no operational reason to prohibit them.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corin has:

- Two tails.
    
- Two wings.
    
- Cat ears.
    
- A miniature feline form.
    
- The ability to fly.
    
- The ability to perform backflips.
    
- Ether-reactive feathers.
    

And now:

**Two bows.**

Belle considers this an improvement.

Corin secretly agrees.

**End of SAFEHOUSE Personnel Observation**
