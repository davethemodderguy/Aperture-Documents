# BLACKSITE OBSERVATION REPORT

## “THEY'RE STILL DOING IT”

**CLASSIFICATION:** C.O.R.E. INTERNAL — ARCHIVAL  
**ACCESS:** ARCHIVIST / EXECUTIVE  
**STATUS:** CLOSED — NON-HOSTILE  
**DATE:** 03/09/2026  
**LOCATION:** HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE / SPAWN HEADQUARTERS  
**SUBJECT:** Simultaneous Architect visitation  
**SECONDARY SUBJECT:** Alice / Neko  
**PRIMARY OBSERVER:** Neko

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

On 03/09/2026, **all three known Architects of Reality** manifested at SPAWN Headquarters within the same general visitation period.

The entities were:

- **Guiding Light** — oldest Architect; self-presents as female.
    
- **Curious Light** — second-oldest Architect; self-presents as male.
    
- **Mischevious Light** — youngest Architect; self-presents as male.
    

The Architects possess no inherent gender. Their chosen presentations were adopted during their interactions with Aperture for reasons described by Guiding Light as:

> **“Why not? It's easier on the eyes.”**

No coordinated attack was conducted.

No formal confrontation occurred.

SPAWN personnel nonetheless spent a significant portion of the incident attempting to understand how their facility's security systems could possibly respond to three entities who fundamentally operate at a level beyond those systems.

Meanwhile, at Heaven's Blacksite, **Alice** was casually riding her scooter through the facility.

She entered the Archives.

She found Neko monitoring the SPAWN situation.

She stopped.

She watched.

She began laughing.

She did not stop.

---

# 2. THE THREE ARCHITECTS

The simultaneous presence of all three Architects caused immediate concern within SPAWN.

Guiding Light appeared to be observing.

Curious Light appeared to be investigating.

Mischevious Light appeared to be looking for something entertaining to do.

SPAWN's internal security team apparently reached the conclusion that they were witnessing an organized supernatural incursion.

C.O.R.E. assessment:

> **They were visiting.**

The Architects displayed no indication of requiring an objective, target, or operational purpose.

At one point, Curious Light reportedly asked:

> **“What happens if I press this?”**

Guiding Light replied:

> **“Probably something stupid.”**

Mischevious Light:

> **“Do it.”**

---

# 3. SPAWN SECURITY RESPONSE

SPAWN activated multiple layers of defensive infrastructure.

The response included:

- Ritual containment systems
    
- Divine authorization barriers
    
- Metaphysical warding
    
- Emergency security protocols
    
- Personnel lockdown
    
- Anti-entity measures
    
- Several systems that C.O.R.E. analysts believe were never intended to operate simultaneously
    

The Architects were not impressed.

Guiding Light simply watched the barriers activate.

Curious Light examined one.

Mischevious Light attempted to interact with another.

The resulting sequence was described by one SPAWN officer as:

> **“Three gods standing around our security system like children looking at a broken toy.”**

The statement was later removed from the official report.

C.O.R.E. recovered it anyway.

---

# 4. MEANWHILE — HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE

At approximately 14:18, Alice was traveling through the Blacksite on her scooter.

No security emergency was active.

She was simply going from one area to another.

She entered the Archives.

She saw Neko.

She stopped the scooter.

> **ALICE:**  
> “What are you watching?”

Neko did not look away from the monitor.

> **Neko:**  
> “SPAWN.”

Alice rolled closer.

She looked at the screen.

There were three Architects on the feed.

SPAWN personnel were attempting to maintain a ritual perimeter around them.

Alice stared.

Silence.

Then:

> **ALICE:**  
> “They're doing it again?”

> **Neko:**  
> “Yep.”

Alice immediately started laughing.

---

# 5. THE ARCHIVES

Alice parked her scooter beside one of the Archive terminals.

She remained beside Neko.

The two watched the live feed.

On-screen, SPAWN personnel were attempting to determine whether their latest ritual barrier was functioning.

Curious Light was standing inside it.

He appeared completely unaffected.

Mischevious Light was sitting on a desk.

Guiding Light was reading one of SPAWN's ritual manuals.

Alice covered her mouth.

It did not help.

> **ALICE:**  
> “HAHAHAHA—”

Neko began laughing as well.

> **Neko:**  
> “I KNOW.”

> **ALICE:**  
> “WHY ARE THEY STILL TRYING?”

> **Neko:**  
> “I DON'T KNOW.”

> **ALICE:**  
> “THEY KNOW WHO THEY ARE!”

> **Neko:**  
> “APPARENTLY NOT.”

Alice nearly fell off her scooter.

---

# 6. THE NOTEBOOK

During the observation, Alice was holding a small notebook.

The notebook is believed to have been given or signed by **Guiding Light** during a previous interaction.

On one of the pages is a handwritten signature:

> **Guiding Light**

The signature is written in **glowing blue ink.**

The ink does not appear to be conventional ink.

C.O.R.E. has been unable to reproduce it.

The signature continues to emit a faint blue luminescence even in complete darkness.

Alice appeared particularly proud of the notebook.

At one point, she held it up toward Neko.

> **ALICE:**  
> “Look!”

Neko glanced over.

> **Neko:**  
> “You got her signature?”

Alice nodded enthusiastically.

> **ALICE:**  
> “Mhm!”

She immediately returned her attention to the SPAWN feed.

Approximately five seconds later:

> **ALICE:**  
> “They're trying to contain her with a circle.”

She started laughing again.

---

# 7. THE SIGNATURE

Neko later examined the page.

The signature was accompanied by a very small additional note written beneath it:

> **“To Alice :)”**

The writing glowed with the same blue coloration.

Neko reportedly stared at it for several seconds.

Then:

> **Neko:**  
> “You realize you have the signature of one of the Architects of Reality in your notebook, right?”

Alice:

> **“Yeah!”**

Neko:

> **“And you're using it to laugh at SPAWN.”**

Alice:

> **“Yeah!”**

Neko:

> **“Fair enough.”**

---

# 8. THE MOMENT THAT BROKE ALICE

At approximately 14:31, SPAWN activated a particularly elaborate containment ritual around Mischevious Light.

The ritual contained:

- Seven concentric circles
    
- Twelve anchor sigils
    
- Three divine seals
    
- A metaphysical stabilization lattice
    
- ████████
    
- A large amount of incense
    

Mischevious Light looked down.

He snapped his fingers.

The entire containment array transformed into approximately three dozen small blue butterflies.

They flew around the room.

One landed on a SPAWN officer's nose.

Alice completely lost composure.

> **ALICE:**  
> “HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!”

Neko was laughing so hard he had to lean against the desk.

> **Neko:**  
> “THEY SPENT THREE HOURS MAKING THAT.”

Alice:

> **“AND HE TURNED IT INTO BUTTERFLIES!”**

---

# 9. ARCHIVIST RECORDING

The following exchange was recorded while Alice and Neko continued watching.

> **ALICE:**  
> “Do they know?”

> **Neko:**  
> “Know what?”

> **ALICE:**  
> “That they're fighting the people who made reality?”

Neko paused.

Alice looked at the screen.

Guiding Light was casually walking through a sealed doorway.

Curious Light was examining a security terminal.

Mischevious Light was feeding tea into a vending machine.

> **ALICE:**  
> “They're so stupid.”

Neko:

> **“Yep.”**

---

# 10. INCIDENT STATUS

The three Architects eventually departed SPAWN Headquarters.

No facility-wide destruction occurred.

No mass casualties occurred.

No major structural damage was reported.

SPAWN's primary losses consisted of:

- Security confidence
    
- Personnel morale
    
- Several ritual arrays
    
- One vending machine
    
- Approximately 47 pages of revised emergency procedures
    
- The remaining belief that their containment systems were universally applicable
    

C.O.R.E. considers the incident closed.

---

# 11. ALICE'S FINAL COMMENT

Before leaving the Archives, Alice picked up her scooter.

She looked once more at the monitor.

The final SPAWN footage showed all three Architects standing together in the lobby.

Guiding Light appeared amused.

Curious Light appeared interested.

Mischevious Light appeared to be considering what he could break.

Alice smiled.

She held up her notebook, its blue signature glowing softly in the dim Archive lighting.

Then she said:

> **“I like them.”**

She rode away.

Approximately thirty seconds later, she returned.

> **“Can we watch tomorrow too?”**

Neko:

> **“Absolutely.”**
