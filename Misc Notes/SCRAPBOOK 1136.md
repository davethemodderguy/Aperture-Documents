# INCIDENT SUMMARY

A group of newly assigned MSAC personnel were introduced to **Elsher**.

This was not intended to be a formal weapons demonstration.

Elsher disagreed.

Within approximately four minutes, the new personnel had been shown:

- **NS-006 — Night Stalker**
    
- **SOH-002 — Sleight of Hand**
    
- Elsher's personalized configurations;
    
- the respective display cases;
    
- Neri's handwritten authorization letters;
    
- and, most importantly, the fact that **neither weapon is available through ordinary equipment requisition.**
    

The demonstration was conducted entirely by Elsher.

Neri was not present.

This was probably fortunate.

---

# 1. THE FLEX

A group of seven new MSAC personnel were standing in the equipment area.

One of them noticed the two weapon cases Elsher was carrying.

> **TRAINEE:**  
> "What's that?"

Elsher stopped.

He looked at the cases.

Then at the trainees.

> **ELSHER:**  
> "These?"

He placed them on the table.

> **ELSHER:**  
> "You don't get them."

Silence.

One trainee laughed.

> **TRAINEE:**  
> "Okay, but what are they?"

Elsher opened the first case.

Inside was **NS-006 — Night Stalker.**

The personalized space-themed markings were immediately visible.

The rhombus-shaped star reticle was also present.

The engraving:

> **ELSHER**

on one side.

And:

> **TACHYON INDUSTRIES**

on the other.

The trainees became considerably more interested.

> **TRAINEE:**  
> "That's a Night Stalker?"

> **ELSHER:**  
> "Yep."

> **TRAINEE:**  
> "You actually got one?"

Elsher nodded.

> **ELSHER:**  
> "Beat Jane."

Several trainees stared at him.

> **TRAINEE:**  
> "...You beat Jane?"

> **ELSHER:**  
> "Eventually."

One trainee immediately looked impressed.

Another looked concerned.

A third quietly asked:

> **TRAINEE:**  
> "How?"

Elsher shrugged.

> **ELSHER:**  
> "Made her quit."

---

# 5. ANTI-THEFT SYSTEM

This is not a metaphor.

Every authorized Night Stalker and Sleight of Hand contains Neri's established **anti-theft safeguard.**

Authorized users are unaffected.

Unauthorized attempts to take or manipulate the weapons trigger a defensive response.

The result is immediate and unpleasant.

The system exists because:

**People keep trying to steal them.**

Despite repeated warnings.

Despite the cases.

Despite the documentation.

Despite the fact that the weapons are individually registered.

Despite the fact that Neri personally knows who each one belongs to.

People still attempt it.

C.O.R.E. has documented multiple incidents.

No successful theft has occurred.

---
