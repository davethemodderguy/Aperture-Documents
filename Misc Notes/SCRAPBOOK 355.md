## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **PERSONNEL PROFILE ADDENDUM**

# **COREY — BERET**

**Classification:** Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corey

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Corey is frequently seen wearing a **beret**, primarily to keep her head warm.

The accessory has gradually become part of her recognizable appearance, particularly during colder periods and while residing in the treehouse.

It is considered entirely mundane.

The Marts, however, have developed an unusual fascination with it.

---

# **MART INTERACTION**

Marts generally leave Corey's beret alone.

They understand that it is important to her comfort.

However, when Corey is resting, they occasionally gather around it.

One Mart was observed sitting directly on top of the beret.

When asked why, it replied:

> "...Warm."

Corey sighed.

> "...That's my hat."

The Mart remained there.

---

# **MART TEMPERATURE REGULATION**

Because Marts can now regulate their internal temperature, they sometimes complement the beret by providing additional warmth.

A Mart may:

- Rest against Corey's head.
    
- Sit on her shoulder.
    
- Form a small warm cushion beside her.
    
- Adjust its temperature to keep her comfortable.
    

They are careful not to interfere with the beret itself.

Usually.

---

# **THE "BERET MART" INCIDENT**

One morning, Corey woke up to discover that a sleeping Mart had positioned itself directly on top of her beret.

The Mart had apparently decided that the beret was an acceptable sleeping surface.

Corey stared into a nearby mirror.

> "...Seriously?"

The Mart did not wake up.

Corin walked past.

She stopped.

Looked at Corey.

Looked at the Mart.

Then laughed.

> "...Looks comfortable."

Corey sighed.

> "...I'm never getting it back until it wakes up, am I?"

Corin shook her head.

> "...Nope."

---

# **LUMI'S COMMENT**

When Lumi heard about the incident, she smiled.

> "...At least it's keeping both of you warm."

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Corey's beret remains her preferred method of keeping her head warm.

The Marts have accepted this.

They simply occasionally decide that the beret is also an excellent place to sleep.

Corey's current policy is therefore:

> **"The beret stays on."**

> **"Marts are allowed on the beret."**

> **"...As long as they don't knock it off."**

**End of Personnel Profile Addendum**
