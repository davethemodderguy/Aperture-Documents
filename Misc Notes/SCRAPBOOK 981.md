# SAFEHOUSE ANOMALOUS PHENOMENON REPORT

## AZURE RESIDUE

**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL  
**Status:** ACTIVE INVESTIGATION  
**Related Subject:** Neri  
**Phenomenon:** Unidentified Azure Light Residue  
**Initial Observation:** ██/██/████

---

### I. INITIAL DISCOVERY

The first fragment was discovered at Neri's usual cafeteria table.

It was approximately the size of a grain of sand.

A small patch of **azure, glowing particulate** had settled on the wooden surface.

It did not behave like dust.

It did not fall normally.

It appeared to be suspended slightly above the table, drifting upward before gently descending again.

The particulate emitted a faint blue glow.

When illuminated directly, it became almost transparent.

When the surrounding lights were turned off, however—

**it became brighter.**

The residue was immediately recognized as anomalous.

No conventional material analysis could identify it.

---

### II. SECONDARY APPEARANCES

Within several hours, additional particles appeared throughout the SAFEHOUSE.

A small patch was discovered on:

- Neri's chair.
    
- The edge of her chemistry book.
    
- The floor beneath her old table.
    
- The memorial display containing her fishbone hairclip.
    
- The casing surrounding her Starlight Katana.
    
- The handle of her crossbow.
    

No personnel reported seeing the particles arrive.

They simply appeared.

At first, they were dismissed as residual contamination from Guiding Light.

That explanation lasted approximately six hours.

Then someone found a **wing shard**.

---

# III. WING SHARD

The object was unmistakable.

A small fragment of translucent azure Light.

It resembled one of Guiding Light's wing fragments.

It possessed no visible physical attachment point.

It floated approximately several centimeters above the surface on which it was discovered.

It produced no heat.

It produced no detectable radiation.

It could not be damaged through conventional means.

When Guiding Light approached it, the fragment immediately brightened.

She stopped.

Her expression changed.

> “That's mine.”

She reached toward it.

The shard drifted toward her hand.

Then stopped.

Guiding Light did not touch it.

She stared at it for several seconds.

> “No.”

A pause.

> “That's not just mine.”

---

# IV. GUIDING LIGHT'S ASSESSMENT

Guiding Light examined the fragment carefully.

Her conclusion was disturbing.

The shard possessed characteristics consistent with the Light that had previously integrated into Neri.

It was not simply an ordinary piece of her wing.

Something had changed.

The fragment appeared to contain a faint **residual signature associated with Neri.**

Not a consciousness.

Not a message.

Not proof of survival.

Nothing that could responsibly be called Neri.

But something had been left behind.

Guiding Light refused to call it a coincidence.

> “She carried my Light.”

> “If she's somewhere outside our reach...”

She looked at the shard.

> “Maybe some of it is still trying to come home.”

---

# V. THE RESIDUE SPREADS

Over the following days, the phenomenon became increasingly common.

Tiny azure particles appeared in places Neri had frequently visited.

Not everywhere.

Only where she had left some form of personal presence.

Her cafeteria table.

The greenhouse.

The laboratory.

The archive.

The corridor outside her room.

The SAFEHOUSE entrance.

Her memorial.

And eventually—

**nowhere in particular.**

A single particle appeared in an empty hallway.

Then another.

Then three.

They remained suspended in the air for several minutes.

Before disappearing.

---

# VI. THE MEMORIAL

The memorial changed noticeably.

The fishbone hairclip began accumulating tiny azure particles.

They settled around it like glowing dust.

Nobody cleaned them.

Neko explicitly prohibited anyone from doing so.

> “Leave them.”

The Starlight Katana occasionally reflected azure light despite having no external illumination.

The crossbow displayed the same phenomenon.

The knives did not.

They remained completely ordinary.

This distinction was noted.

No explanation was found.

---

# VII. THE CUPCAKE

The strangest occurrence happened in the cafeteria.

Guiding Light had once again placed a fresh blueberry cupcake on Neri's table.

She left.

Several minutes later, a tiny azure shard appeared directly beside it.

The shard slowly rotated.

The cupcake remained untouched.

Bao found it first.

She stood there silently.

Then she smiled.

Not because she believed Neri had returned.

She knew better than to make that assumption.

She simply whispered:

> “You would've liked this.”

The shard glowed slightly brighter.

Bao looked at it.

Her smile disappeared.

> “...I miss you too.”

---

# VIII. SCIENTIFIC CAUTION

**The following conclusions are NOT authorized:**

- Neri is alive.
    
- Neri is communicating with the Blacksite.
    
- Neri's consciousness is contained within the shards.
    
- The shards are a navigational signal.
    
- The shards indicate that Neri is returning.
    
- The shards originate directly from Neri.
    

None of these hypotheses have been demonstrated.

However—

**the phenomenon is occurring primarily around objects and locations strongly associated with Neri.**

This correlation remains unexplained.
