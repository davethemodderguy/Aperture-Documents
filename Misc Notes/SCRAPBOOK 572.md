### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE — TRANSPORTATION UPDATE**

# **CORIN — TREEHOUSE / BLACKSITE FLIGHT ROUTE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Route:** Corin's Treehouse ↔ Heaven's Blacksite  
**Average Flight Time:** **20 minutes one way**  
**Status:** Operational

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Although Guiding Light has established a dimensional door connecting Corin's treehouse directly to SAFEHOUSE, Corin still regularly travels between the two locations by flight.

The distance between the treehouse and Heaven's Blacksite requires approximately:

> **20 minutes of continuous flight.**

The same travel time applies in reverse.

Therefore:

**Treehouse → Blacksite:** ~20 minutes

**Blacksite → Treehouse:** ~20 minutes

**Round trip:** ~40 minutes

---

# **FLIGHT ROUTE**

Corin generally flies directly between the two locations.

Her wings provide sufficient endurance for the journey.

She does not require intermediate landing points.

Her route can vary depending on weather, surrounding terrain, and whether she is carrying another person.

---

# **WHY FLY?**

Despite the existence of the dimensional door, Corin genuinely enjoys flying.

Flight has become her preferred form of transportation.

She finds the journey calming.

The twenty-minute flight gives her time to:

- Clear her thoughts.
    
- Enjoy the surroundings.
    
- Feel the wind through her feathers.
    
- Watch the environment below.
    
- Simply be alone for a while.
    

It is less of a commute and more of a routine.

---

# **CARRYING PASSENGERS**

Corin can carry another person during the flight.

Her wings and Ether-enhanced strength allow her to transport individuals safely.

Orphie is her most frequent passenger.

Corin has also carried SAFEHOUSE residents when necessary.

Travel time may increase slightly depending on:

- Passenger weight.
    
- Weather.
    
- Corin's chosen flight path.
    
- Whether she decides to stop somewhere along the way.
    

The standard estimate remains approximately **20 minutes**.

---

# **THE DIMENSIONAL DOOR**

The blue door remains the fastest method of travel.

Walking through it is effectively instantaneous.

However, Corin does not always use it.

When asked why she would spend twenty minutes flying instead of simply opening the door, Corin answered:

> **"Because I like flying."**

No further justification was considered necessary.

---

# **EXAMPLE ROUTINE**

A common Corin schedule may look like:

**08:00** — Wake up at the treehouse.

**08:30** — Fly toward the Blacksite.

**08:50** — Arrive at SAFEHOUSE.

**09:00** — Begin preparing food.

**12:00** — Lunch with residents.

**14:00** — Visit the Blacksite.

**17:00** — Return to SAFEHOUSE.

**18:00** — Fly back to the treehouse.

The schedule is not mandatory.

Corin frequently changes it.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The dimensional door technically makes the distance between Corin's treehouse and SAFEHOUSE meaningless.

Corin doesn't care.

She likes flying.

So every so often, instead of walking through a magical blue door, she opens her wings and spends twenty minutes crossing the distance herself.

For Corin, the journey is part of the destination.
