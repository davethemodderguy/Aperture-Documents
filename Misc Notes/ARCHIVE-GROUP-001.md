# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **ARCHIVE DIVISION MEMORANDUM**

### **ARCHIVE-GROUP-001 – INFORMAL PERSONNEL GROUP DESIGNATIONS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Administrative

---

## **Purpose**

To improve communication, documentation, and record keeping, the Archive Division has adopted a series of **informal group designations**.

These groups are **not** official departments, divisions, chains of command, or containment classifications.

Instead, they are staff-recognized teams formed through close relationships, shared abilities, or recurring operational cooperation.

Archive personnel may use these names when addressing or documenting a specific collection of individuals.

---

---

# **Usage Guidelines**

These group names may be used in:

- Archive documentation.
    
- Internal memoranda.
    
- Recreational event planning.
    
- Training rosters.
    
- Non-critical operational notices.
    

They **shall not** replace official personnel designations, department names, anomaly classifications, or command structures.
