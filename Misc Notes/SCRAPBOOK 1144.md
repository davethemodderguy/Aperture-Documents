# PROPOSAL

Neri has submitted a single question to MSAC regarding the ongoing **Sleight of Hand Trial II**.

> **1 Nekonium Needle**

> **25,000,000,000 pieces of hay**

along with:

> **50 ordinary iron needles**

The winner currently receives:

> **SOH-004 — Sleight of Hand**

---

Increase the haystack from:

> **25,000,000,000**

to:

> **50,000,000,000 pieces of hay.**

All existing equipment restrictions remain.

> **SOH-004 — Sleight of Hand**

**AND**

> **NS-009 — Night Stalker**

> **SOH-004 — Sleight of Hand**
