# BLACKSITE PERSONNEL NOTICE

## JANE DOE — APPLE INCIDENTS

**Subject:** Jane Doe  
**Location:** Blacksite / SAFEHOUSE Common Areas  
**Classification:** Behavioral Advisory  
**Threat Level:** Usually None  
**Psychological Threat:** Potentially Significant  
**Primary Recommendation:** **Do not stare.**

---

# 1. THE SITUATION

There is a particular sight that has become infamous among Blacksite personnel.

Jane Doe.

Standing somewhere in a corridor.

Eating an apple.

Holding her dagger in one hand.

Her long Rat Thiren tail casually waving behind her, the pointed tip moving back and forth.

She isn't doing anything.

She isn't threatening anyone.

She isn't even necessarily looking at you.

And yet, new personnel tend to become extremely uncomfortable.

The problem is that Jane knows this.

---

# 2. THE DANGER OF BEING NOTICED

A worker may walk past Jane.

Jane takes another bite of her apple.

The worker continues walking.

Her tail moves.

The worker looks at it.

Jane notices.

She continues eating.

The worker walks faster.

Jane smiles.

At this point, the worker has made a critical mistake:

**They have become interesting.**

---

# 3. JANE'S ENTERTAINMENT

Jane occasionally decides that her entertainment for the day will be:

> **Scaring Blacksite personnel.**

There is no operational reason.

No intelligence objective.

No hostile target.

No emergency.

She simply gets bored.

When this happens, Jane does not necessarily chase people or physically threaten them.

She uses what she already has:

- Her reputation.
    
- Her intelligence.
    
- Her ability to appear unexpectedly.
    
- Her psychological instincts.
    
- Her dagger.
    
- Her tail.
    
- And an absolutely unreasonable sense of humor.
    

---

# 4. TYPICAL ENCOUNTER

**14:21 — Corridor C-4**

A junior worker encounters Jane standing beside a wall.

Jane is eating an apple.

Her dagger is in her free hand.

Her tail is slowly moving behind her.

The worker stops.

Jane looks at him.

The worker looks at the dagger.

Then the tail.

Then Jane.

> **Jane:** "Hi."

> **Worker:** "...Hi."

Jane takes another bite.

> **Jane:** "You look nervous."

> **Worker:** "I'm not."

Jane smiles.

> **Jane:** "Okay."

She continues eating.

The worker walks away.

Nothing happens.

Thirty seconds later, he looks back.

Jane is gone.

---

# 5. THE SECOND ENCOUNTER

The worker continues down another corridor.

Jane is standing there.

Still eating the apple.

He stops.

> **Worker:** "...How did you get here?"

Jane shrugs.

> **Jane:** "Walked."

> **Worker:** "You were behind me."

> **Jane:** "And now I'm here."

She takes another bite.

The worker leaves.

Jane waits several seconds.

Then laughs.

---

# 6. THE TAIL

The tail is frequently the most psychologically effective component.

Jane's tail is long enough to wrap around a person.

Its pointed tip is unmistakable.

When Jane is merely standing around, the tail moves naturally.

Unfortunately, to someone unfamiliar with her, it looks like she is deliberately positioning a restraint weapon.

Sometimes she is.

Usually she isn't.

The worker doesn't know that.

Jane does.

And that is precisely why it works.

---

# 7. THE DAGGER

Jane's dagger is similarly mundane from her perspective.

She may casually hold it while eating.

This is not necessarily preparation for violence.

Jane is simply comfortable carrying it.

Blacksite personnel, understandably, have a different interpretation.

One worker reportedly asked:

> **Worker:** "Why are you holding a dagger while eating an apple?"

Jane looked at the dagger.

Then at the apple.

Then back at the worker.

> **Jane:** "Convenience."

> **Worker:** "...Convenience for what?"

Jane smiled.

> **Jane:** "Cutting the apple."

The worker looked at the perfectly intact apple in Jane's other hand.

> **Worker:** "...Right."

---

# 8. SAFEHOUSE RESIDENTS

SAFEHOUSE residents are generally unaffected by this behavior.

They know Jane.

They know she is playing around.

Neri has reportedly walked past Jane during one of these episodes, looked at the dagger, looked at the tail, and simply said:

> **Neri:** "Are you scaring people again?"

> **Jane:** "Maybe."

> **Neri:** "Okay."

Neri then continued walking.

Jane appeared disappointed.

> **Jane:** "You didn't even get scared."

> **Neri:** "You're my friend."

> **Jane:** "That's not fair."

---

# 9. ADMINISTRATIVE GUIDANCE

Personnel are reminded:

Jane Doe is a resident, not a hostile infiltrator.

If encountered eating an apple while holding her dagger:

**Remain calm.**

If her tail is moving:

**Remain calm.**

If she looks at you:

**Remain calm.**

If she smiles:

**Probably remain calm.**

If she says:

> **"Hey, wait."**

You should probably stop and listen.

If she says:

> **"I have an idea."**

You may wish to reconsider your current location.

---

# 10. FINAL OBSERVATION

The most frightening thing about Jane Doe is not the dagger.

It is not the tail.

It is not even her intelligence-extraction ability.

It is the fact that she can look completely harmless while deciding:

> **"You know what? I'm going to scare this guy today."**

And because Jane is Jane, the victim often does not realize that they have been selected for today's entertainment until the game has already started.
