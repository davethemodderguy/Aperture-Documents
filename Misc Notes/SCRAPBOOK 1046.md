# SECURITY BULLETIN

## DO NOT REPORT THE PLUSHIE.

Personnel are advised that a small Seal plushie may occasionally be observed throughout the SAFEHOUSE.

The entity may be seen:

- drinking coffee;
    
- eating sandwiches;
    
- sitting at tables;
    
- being carried;
    
- sitting on furniture;
    
- accompanying Alice;
    
- accompanying Jane Doe;
    
- quietly observing other residents;
    
- or simply existing as a small plush Seal.
    

**This is not Z-637.**

It is **Neri.**

---

# I. IDENTIFICATION

The following characteristics strongly indicate that the observed plushie is Neri:

**Species:** Seal / Teddy-Bear hybrid  
**Approximate size:** Teddy-bear sized  
**Behavior:** Quiet, familiar, non-hostile  
**Diet:** Normal food and beverages  
**Known companions:** Alice, Jane Doe, other authorized SAFEHOUSE residents  
**Anomalous capability:** Voluntary plushie transformation  
**Duration:** Approximately five hours before forced reversion

Neri retains her consciousness and personality while transformed.

She can move independently.

She can eat.

She can drink.

She can communicate.

She can also be carried.

---

# II. THE COFFEE INCIDENT

A recent report described a small Seal plushie sitting at a cafeteria table with Alice and Jane Doe.

The plushie was:

> **Drinking coffee.**

It was also:

> **Eating a sandwich.**

The reporting employee initiated a Z-637 containment alert.

This was unnecessary.

The responding security team arrived to find:

- Alice eating normally;
    
- Jane eating normally;
    
- Neri sitting between them;
    
- Neri holding a coffee;
    
- half of a sandwich in front of her;
    
- and absolutely no haunted doll.
    

Neri reportedly looked at the responding security personnel.

Then continued eating.

---

# III. Z-637 DISTINCTION

Z-637 remains a separate classified anomaly.

A Seal plushie sitting at a cafeteria table with known SAFEHOUSE residents does not constitute evidence of Z-637 activity.

In particular:

### Z-637 does NOT:

- casually eat sandwiches with Alice;
    
- drink coffee with Jane Doe;
    
- allow itself to be carried around by Alice;
    
- respond to "Neri";
    
- become annoyed when called cute;
    
- perform chemistry research;
    
- possess a 12 TB Dell laptop;
    
- or occupy a reserved 250 TB research archive.
    

Neri does.

---

# IV. FALSE ALARM POLICY

Personnel are instructed **not** to initiate a Z-637 alarm solely because a Seal plushie has been observed.

Before reporting an anomalous doll sighting, personnel should first determine whether the object in question is:

1. moving normally;
    
2. eating;
    
3. drinking;
    
4. accompanied by SAFEHOUSE residents;
    
5. responding to Neri;
    
6. carrying or wearing recognizable Neri-related items;
    
7. or otherwise behaving like Neri.
    

If yes:

> **It is probably Neri.**

Do not scream.

Do not draw weapons.

Do not activate containment protocols.

Do not call the anomaly response team.

Do not announce:

> **"THE DOLL ESCAPED."**

---

# V. ADDITIONAL WARNING

Do not attempt to pick up Neri without permission merely because she is currently a plushie.

She can move independently.

She is also capable of deciding that she does not want to be carried.

Alice has considerably more experience with this distinction.

Jane Doe also understands it.

Personnel who do not know Neri should simply leave her alone unless assistance is required.

---

# VI. FINAL SECURITY DETERMINATION

**Seal plushie + coffee + sandwich + Alice + Jane Doe**

does **not** equal:

> **Z-637 BREACH**

It equals:

> **Neri having lunch.**

The Security Division would like to remind everyone that Neri is a resident of the SAFEHOUSE.

She is allowed to drink coffee.

She is allowed to eat sandwiches.

She is allowed to spend time with Alice and Jane.

She is allowed to be a plushie.

Please stop making the Security Division investigate the cafeteria because someone saw a stuffed Seal drinking coffee.

---
