## 1. OVERVIEW

Further mapping of Neri's personal subspace has confirmed that the environment is substantially larger than the original SAFEHOUSE surveys indicated.

The currently known subspace consists of **15 separate floating islands**, all belonging to the same connected subspace environment.

The islands vary considerably in size.

Some are large enough to support buildings, laboratories, or other substantial structures.

Others are comparatively small and contain little more than exposed terrain.

At present, **most of the islands are completely empty.**

This appears to be intentional, or at minimum tolerated, by Neri.

---

# 2. ISLAND COUNT

**Total confirmed islands: 15**

The islands are not arranged as a conventional chain.

Instead, they form a loose floating archipelago around the primary island containing Neri's room.

Their exact arrangement can change slightly depending on Neri's current configuration of the subspace, although the total known island count remains fifteen.

### General classification

|Island Category|Approximate Status|
|---|---|
|Primary Island|Neri's room / primary inhabited area|
|Secondary Islands|Connected, partially utilized|
|Auxiliary Islands|Connected but undeveloped|
|Unused Islands|Empty|
|Recovery Space|Below archipelago|

No island has currently been designated as permanently inhabited other than the primary island.

---

# 3. PRIMARY ISLAND

The primary island contains Neri's actual subspaced room.

Known contents include:

- Bed
    
- Bookshelf
    
- Chemistry equipment
    
- Personal belongings
    
- Knife and equipment storage
    
- Crossbow
    
- Laboratory workspace
    
- Access points to the surrounding islands
    

The primary island serves as the main social location.

Visitors generally arrive here before moving across the rest of the subspace.

---

# 4. SECONDARY ISLANDS

Several islands are directly accessible from the primary island through the existing network of rope bridges and other connections.

These islands currently have little or no permanent development.

They are nevertheless considered part of Neri's room's subspace.

Neri has not assigned most of them a specific purpose.

When asked what she planned to do with the empty islands, Neri reportedly answered:

> **“I don't know.”**

When asked why she created fifteen islands if she did not know what to use them for:

> **“They're there.”**

No further explanation was provided.

---

# 5. ISLAND SIZE VARIATION

The fifteen islands are not uniform.

Their sizes range from relatively small platforms to considerably larger landmasses.

This produces a natural variation in traversal difficulty.

### Small islands

Generally contain enough space for:

- standing;
    
- sitting;
    
- small equipment;
    
- temporary storage.
    

### Medium islands

Could potentially support:

- additional rooms;
    
- workshops;
    
- gardens;
    
- recreational areas;
    
- storage facilities.
    

### Large islands

Have enough available area for significantly larger developments.

At present, however, **most remain empty.**

---

# 6. EMPTY ISLANDS

The empty islands are currently regarded as undeveloped expansion space.

There are no current requirements for Neri to populate them.

This is important because the subspace is **not intended to be treated as a completed architectural project.**

Neri is still learning how her ability works.

The existing archipelago should therefore be considered a continually developing environment.

Neko has specifically advised against allowing other residents to independently modify the islands without Neri's permission.

---

# 7. CONNECTIVITY

The islands form part of the same subspace network.

A simplified representation is:

```text
                         [ ISLAND ]
                              |
                              |
               [ ISLAND ]---[ ISLAND ]
                    \          |
                     \         |
                [ ISLAND ]--[ PRIMARY ]
                              |
                         [ ISLAND ]
                           /    \
                    [ ISLAND ] [ ISLAND ]
                         |
                    [ ISLAND ]
```

The diagram is illustrative only.

The actual configuration contains **15 islands** and is considerably less orderly.

There is no requirement for every island to have a direct bridge to the primary island.

Some connections may instead pass through another island.

---

# 8. THE LOWER RECOVERY ZONE

The previously established **250-meter zero-gravity recovery zone** remains beneath the floating archipelago.

This means the entire island network effectively exists above a large open vertical recovery area.

Personnel falling from an island will eventually enter the gravity-null region.

Once inside:

> **Vertical acceleration stops.**

The individual remains suspended until recovered.

This configuration applies to the archipelago as a whole rather than only the primary island.

---

# 9. VISITOR ACCESS

SAFEHOUSE residents are permitted to visit the connected islands under normal subspace safety rules.

However:

**Empty does not mean unrestricted.**

Neri retains control over the environment.

She may:

- modify island geometry;
    
- create or remove connections;
    
- alter local gravity;
    
- create additional structures;
    
- isolate an island;
    
- connect previously separate areas;
    
- change access routes.
    

Visitors should therefore not assume that yesterday's route will necessarily remain today's route.
