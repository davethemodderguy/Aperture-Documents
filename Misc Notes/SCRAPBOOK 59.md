# **HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE**

## **PERSONNEL WELFARE INCIDENT LOG**

### **SUBJECT: NORMA LOCKWOOD – TRAUMATIC MEMORY RESPONSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Medical & Executive Personnel Only

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the discovery that Senior Manager **Norma Lockwood's** former Ether Regression Syndrome originated from childhood experimentation rather than a naturally occurring illness, Norma experienced an acute traumatic stress response after being reminded of those events during a medical review.

The incident was witnessed by Belle, Endmin, Arcturus, and Director Lucious.

---

## **INCIDENT**

Norma had been reviewing archived medical records with the Blacksite medical team.

Most of the session was uneventful.

Then one particular surgical diagram was placed on the table.

Norma stopped speaking.

Her breathing became shallow.

Her hands began to tremble.

Belle noticed first.

> "...Norma?"

Norma didn't answer.

Her eyes remained fixed on the page.

---

Several seconds passed.

The file slowly slipped from her hands.

She took a step backward.

Then another.

Tears began forming before she even realized she was crying.

Her shoulders shook uncontrollably.

She covered her mouth, desperately trying to steady her breathing.

Instead, it became even more uneven.

---

## **MEMORY TRIGGER**

Norma managed only a few broken sentences.

> "...I remember..."

A pause.

> "...It hurt..."

Another.

> "...I couldn't make it stop..."

She closed her eyes tightly, overwhelmed by memories she had spent years trying to bury.

The room remained completely silent.

No one interrupted.

---

## **RESPONSE**

Belle immediately crossed the room.

She didn't ask questions.

She simply wrapped Norma in a gentle hug.

Norma resisted for only a moment before her composure gave way completely.

She buried her face against Belle's shoulder and cried.

Years of carefully hidden fear surfaced all at once.

---

Endmin quietly dismissed everyone not essential to the situation, allowing the room to become calm and free from distractions.

Medical staff remained nearby but gave Norma the space she needed.

---

## **ARCTURUS**

Arcturus stood motionless.

He had faced armed threats without hesitation.

But seeing his little sister in tears left him unable to find the right words.

After a long silence, he finally walked over.

He gently rested a hand on her shoulder.

> "...Norma."

She looked up through tears.

His own voice had begun to shake.

> "...I'm here."

> "...You're not alone anymore."

Norma reached out and held onto his sleeve without saying a word.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Director Lucious quietly closed the archived file.

He handed it to a staff member.

> "Seal these records."

> "No further review today."

His attention returned to Norma.

> "Your well-being comes before any document."

---

## **AFTERCARE**

Norma was escorted back to SAFEHOUSE and relieved of all duties for the remainder of the day.

Belle prepared a warm mug of milk tea, knowing it often helped ease Norma's lingering discomfort.

The cup remained untouched for several minutes.

Eventually, Norma accepted it with both hands.

Her breathing gradually slowed.

---

SAFEHOUSE remained unusually quiet that evening.

No one attempted to force conversation.

Instead, everyone simply stayed nearby.

Sometimes, silent company was enough.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

**Belle**

> "You never have to hide your tears from us."

---

**Endmin**

> "Strength isn't pretending nothing happened."

> "It's letting people help when it becomes too much."

---

**Arcturus**

Sitting beside his sister.

> "...I couldn't protect you back then."

He looked at her.

> "...But I'll keep protecting you now."

Norma quietly leaned against his shoulder.

---

**Director Lucious**

> "The past cannot be changed."

> "But the future does not belong to those who caused it."

---

## **STATUS**

Norma has resumed her duties following an appropriate recovery period, with additional support available whenever needed.

Her past remains a painful part of her history, and reminders can still bring back overwhelming memories.

Within the Blacksite, however, she no longer faces them alone.

The people around her know that healing is not measured by forgetting what happened.

It is measured by having others beside you when the memories return.

**End of Personnel Welfare Incident Log**
