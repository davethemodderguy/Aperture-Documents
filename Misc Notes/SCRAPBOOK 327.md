# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE**

### **Z-335 BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION**

# **MART VOCALIZATION — "MEOWING"**

**Classification:** Benign Behavioral Development  
**Status:** Ongoing

---

# **OBSERVATION**

A previously undocumented vocalization has been observed among Marts frequently spending time around:

- **Neko**
    
- **Endmin Merryweather**
    
- **Miss Neko**
    

The vocalization is:

> **"Meow."**

This behavior appears to have been learned through repeated exposure to the three Cat Thirens.

There is no known biological reason for a Mart to meow.

They simply decided that they should.

---

# **BEHAVIORAL SPREAD**

The behavior was initially limited to a small number of Marts regularly visiting Neko, Endmin, and Miss Neko.

It subsequently spread through the Mart hivemind.

As a result, Marts throughout the facilities have begun occasionally meowing.

The frequency varies.

Some Marts only meow when greeting one of the three Cat Thirens.

Others have begun using it as a general-purpose expression.

---

# **DOCUMENTED USES**

A Mart may now say:

> "Meow."

When:

- Greeting someone.
    
- Asking for food.
    
- Requesting attention.
    
- Being happy.
    
- Seeing a cat.
    
- Seeing another Mart.
    
- Receiving cheese crackers.
    
- Sitting on someone's desk.
    
- Doing absolutely nothing.
    

The exact meaning remains difficult to determine.

---

# **INTERACTION WITH NEKO**

Neko has unintentionally become one of the primary sources of Mart meowing.

When a Mart approaches him and says:

> "Meow."

Neko usually responds:

> "...Meow."

The Mart becomes visibly happy.

Other nearby Marts then begin meowing.

This can result in an entire room becoming:

> **"Meow."**

> **"Meow."**

> **"Meow."**

> **"Meow."**

Neko has never successfully determined how to stop this.

---

# **INTERACTION WITH ENDMIN**

Endmin initially found the behavior amusing.

She eventually noticed that Marts had begun approaching him specifically to meow.

One Mart entered his office.

> "Meow."

Endmin looked down.

> "...Hello."

The Mart:

> "Meow."

Endmin:

> "...Meow."

The Mart immediately left.

Nobody understands what was accomplished.

---

# **INTERACTION WITH MISS NEKO**

Miss Neko's response is more restrained.

When a Mart approaches and meows at her, she generally pauses.

Then:

> "...Meow."

The Mart becomes extremely excited.

This has resulted in Miss Neko being approached by increasingly large groups of Marts.

She has not encouraged the behavior.

She has also not stopped it.

---

# **MART LANGUAGE DEVELOPMENT**

Researchers initially believed that the meow was simply an imitation.

Further observations suggest it has become an actual component of Mart communication.

Some Marts now combine it with their existing vocabulary.

Examples include:

> **"Hello, meow!"**

> **"Meow Mart!"**

> **"Meow, cheese!"**

> **"No meow."**

> **"Happy meow!"**

One particularly enthusiastic Mart was recorded saying:

> **"MEOW MART MEOW!"**

The Ambassador translated this as:

> "...It wants attention."

---

# **LUMI'S RESPONSE**

Lumi was informed that her creations had learned to meow.

She stared at the nearest Mart.

> "...Why?"

The Mart:

> "Meow."

Lumi looked toward Neko.

Neko shrugged.

Lumi sighed.

> "...Fine."

The Mart smiled.

> "Meow!"

---

# **STAR'S RESPONSE**

Star finds the behavior adorable.

She has begun encouraging the Marts.

This has unfortunately resulted in more Marts learning it.

Star's current favorite greeting is:

> "Hello, Mart!"

The Mart responds:

> "Meow!"

Star:

> "...Good."

---

# **C.O.R.E. CONCLUSION**

There is no evidence that meowing provides any practical advantage to Z-335.

It does not improve:

- Communication efficiency.
    
- Mobility.
    
- Combat capability.
    
- Survival.
    
- Ether manipulation.
    

It appears to exist for one reason:

> **Marts spend time around cat people.**

And therefore...

They learned to meow.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

A Mart was recently observed sitting beside Neko, Endmin, and Miss Neko.

All three were working quietly.

The Mart looked at Neko.

> "Meow."

Neko:

> "...Meow."

The Mart looked at Endmin.

> "Meow."

Endmin:

> "...Meow."

The Mart looked at Miss Neko.

> "Meow."

Miss Neko:

> "...Meow."

The Mart became extremely happy.

It sat down.

Five minutes later, another Mart entered the room.

It looked around.

> "...Meow."

Everyone in the room responded:

> **"Meow."**

Nobody questioned it.

**End of Behavioral Observation**
