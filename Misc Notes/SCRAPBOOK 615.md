### **SAFEHOUSE INCIDENT / EQUIPMENT TEST REPORT**

# **CORIN — RAILGUN IMPACT EVENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 9 — C.O.R.E. EYES ONLY  
**Subject:** Corin  
**Location:** Heaven's Blacksite — Controlled Testing Sector  
**Status:** **No Wing Damage / Unexpected Kinetic Discharge**

---

# **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

A controlled test involving Corin's Nano Nekonium wing armor produced an outcome that was considerably more energetic than anticipated.

A railgun projectile struck Corin's protected wing.

The wing remained completely intact.

The Nano Nekonium armor successfully absorbed and distributed the projectile's kinetic force.

The subsequent feather discharge, however, exceeded expectations.

---

# **IMPACT**

The railgun projectile struck the center of Corin's armored wing.

**Result:**

> **No penetration.**

> **No structural damage.**

> **No feather loss.**

> **No significant injury to Corin.**

The Nano Nekonium system absorbed the incoming kinetic force and distributed it throughout the wing's armored structure.

C.O.R.E. initially considered the test successful.

Then Corin launched a feather.

---

# **THE FEATHER**

Corin selected one feather from the affected wing.

The stored kinetic force was transferred into it.

The feather launched.

For approximately **0.4 seconds**, everything appeared normal.

Then it struck the first wall.

---

# **RESULT**

The feather continued through it.

And the next.

And the next.

And the next.

And the next.

### **Five walls were penetrated.**

The feather finally came to rest after its kinetic energy was sufficiently dissipated.

No structural failure occurred to the feather itself.

---

# **CORIN'S REACTION**

Corin stared at the hole through the five walls.

Then at Neko.

Curious Light slowly looked at the destroyed walls.

---

# **ENGINEERING ANALYSIS**

The railgun projectile provided an exceptionally large kinetic input.

The Nano Nekonium armor successfully stored and distributed that energy.

However, the feather's normal launch properties were never designed to handle such a large energy transfer.

The result was effectively:

> **Railgun kinetic energy + Corin's feather + Ether-infused biology**

The feather became an extremely efficient kinetic projectile.
