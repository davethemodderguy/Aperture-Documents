# **KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

## **KCA-EQP-009A**

### **ADDENDUM – MULTI-PURPOSE TACTICAL BRIEFCASE CUSTODY PROTOCOL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Internal Equipment Regulations

---

## **PROPERTY STATUS**

The **Multi-Purpose Tactical Briefcase** assigned to Executive Secretary **Cinder** is **property of the Krampus Compliance Authority**.

Although issued to Cinder for operational use, ownership remains with the Authority.

The weapon is individually serialized, tracked, and registered within the KCA Restricted Equipment Registry.

---

## **CUSTODY REQUIREMENTS**

The assigned operator is personally responsible for the briefcase at all times.

Personnel are required to:

- Maintain continuous accountability of the equipment.
    
- Prevent unauthorized access.
    
- Immediately report any theft, loss, or damage.
    
- Return the briefcase upon reassignment, retirement, or termination of employment.
    

The briefcase is not considered personal property and may not be loaned, transferred, or modified without authorization from the KCA Engineering Division.

---

## **LOSS OF EQUIPMENT**

Due to the classified technology contained within the system, loss of the briefcase is treated as an extremely serious breach of security.

If negligence results in the loss of the weapon, the assigned operator is subject to immediate review.

Possible consequences include:

- Suspension of security clearance.
    
- Internal disciplinary proceedings.
    
- Removal from active duty.
    
- Termination of employment.
    

For this reason, Cinder is exceptionally careful whenever transporting the briefcase.

Personnel frequently joke that she checks on it almost as often as Belle checks whether everyone has eaten.

---

## **WEIGHT**

Despite its compact appearance, the fully equipped briefcase weighs approximately **55 kilograms (121 pounds).**

The weight results from:

- Reinforced armor plating.
    
- Internal transformation mechanisms.
    
- Multiple weapon systems.
    
- Ammunition storage.
    
- Ether-resistant structural components.
    

Most personnel are unable to lift the briefcase comfortably, let alone carry it for extended periods.

---

## **Authorized Personnel**

Only a small number of individuals have demonstrated the strength and familiarity required to handle the briefcase with ease.

These include:

- **Cinder** – Assigned operator.
    
- **Belle Merryweather** – Director, Krampus Compliance Authority.
    
- **Keith** – Executive Protection Officer.
    

All three routinely carry the 55-kilogram briefcase without apparent difficulty during operations.

During one training session, Mart attempted to lift it using both hands.

After several seconds of effort, he managed to raise one corner.

Belle, noticing his struggle, casually picked it up with one hand.

> **"...Looking for this?"

Mart stared.

> **"...How...?"

Belle simply smiled.

> **"...It's not that heavy."

Keith quietly walked past.

He accepted the briefcase from Belle with one hand before handing it back to Cinder.

> **"...Director."

> **"...Secretary."

Cinder slung the 55-kilogram case over her shoulder as if it were an ordinary work bag.

Taiga watched the exchange.

> **"...I think my backpack weighs more emotionally than that thing does to you three."

Laughter filled the room.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Cinder's Multi-Purpose Tactical Briefcase remains one of the Krampus Compliance Authority's most tightly controlled pieces of field equipment.

To most people, it appears to be an unusually sturdy briefcase.

To the Krampus Compliance Authority, it is a restricted tactical weapons platform.

To Cinder...

It is both an indispensable tool for protecting others and a responsibility she never takes lightly.
