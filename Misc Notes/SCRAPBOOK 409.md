### **PERSONNEL WELFARE / CROSS-REALITY ANOMALY REPORT**

# **ORPHIE — THE ONE WHO SHOULD NOT BE HERE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Cross-Reality / Personnel Welfare  
**Subject:** Orphie  
**Associated Personnel:** Corin / SAFEHOUSE Residents / Marts / Luna / Solaria  
**Origin:** Unknown Reality  
**Current Location:** Remote Treehouse — Blacksite Perimeter Forest  
**Status:** **UNRESOLVED**

---

# **OVERVIEW**

Orphie has repeatedly told everyone to leave her alone.

Nobody has listened.

Not because her wishes are being ignored intentionally.

Not because anyone believes they have authority over her.

They simply cannot bring themselves to walk away.

Everyone can see that something is wrong.

And nobody has the heart to leave her alone with it.

---

# **"LEAVE ME ALONE."**

The phrase has become increasingly common.

> "...Leave me alone."

> "...Please."

> "...Just go."

> "...I don't need anyone."

> "...Stop checking on me."

> "...Go back to work."

> "...I'm fine."

> "...Leave me alone."

The responses are almost always the same.

Someone stays.

Someone checks on her later.

A Mart sits nearby.

Corin comes home.

The SAFEHOUSE leaves food outside.

Nobody forces their way into her room.

But nobody truly leaves.

---

# **WHY THEY CANNOT LISTEN**

Everyone understands what Orphie is asking for.

That is precisely why they cannot do it.

They can see the exhaustion.

They can see the dark circles beneath her eyes.

They can see the golden Ether pathways beneath her skin.

They can see the untouched food.

They can see the abandoned sketchbook.

They can see the sealed cookbook.

They can see someone who has gradually stopped caring whether she herself is okay.

Leaving her completely alone feels less like respecting her wishes—

and more like abandoning her.

---

# **THE CROSS-REALITY PROBLEM**

Orphie's existence presents another unanswered question.

She does not originate from the reality in which the Blacksite exists.

She is, in the most literal sense:

> **Something broken from another reality.**

Her presence cannot currently be explained through conventional dimensional transfer.

The mechanism responsible for her arrival is unknown.

No complete record exists describing:

- How she crossed realities.
    
- Why she crossed realities.
    
- Whether the crossing was intentional.
    
- Whether she was brought here.
    
- Whether she escaped from somewhere.
    
- Whether her original reality still exists.
    
- Whether she can return.
    

---

# **THE QUESTION**

The same question appears repeatedly in C.O.R.E. documentation:

> **Why is Orphie here?**

No answer exists.

A second question follows:

> **Why does she have to be here?**

Again:

No answer.

---

# **ORPHIE'S OWN THEORY**

Orphie has occasionally questioned her existence herself.

> "...I shouldn't be here."

Corin:

> "...Why?"

> "...Because I don't belong here."

> "...You live here."

> "...That doesn't mean I belong."

---

# **ANOTHER REALITY**

Orphie remembers a different life.

A different world.

Different circumstances.

Whatever happened there resulted in the person now known as Orphie appearing in this reality.

The details remain fragmented.

Some memories are clear.

Others are not.

Some appear contradictory.

C.O.R.E. has been unable to determine whether the contradictions are caused by:

- Cross-reality displacement.
    
- Memory damage.
    
- Temporal inconsistency.
    
- The presence of Ether.
    
- Or something else entirely.
    

---

# **THE BROKENNESS**

Calling Orphie "broken" is not an official medical diagnosis.

It is how she describes herself.

When asked what happened to her, she once answered:

> "...Something broke."

> "What?"

> "...Me."

She did not elaborate.

---

# **CORIN**

Corin refuses to accept the description.

> "...You're not broken."

Orphie:

> "...Look at me."

Corin:

> "...I am."

> "...Then you know."

Corin remained silent.

She knew Orphie was not asking for an argument.

She was asking whether Corin could look at what she had become and still stay.

Corin stayed.

---

# **THE MARTS**

The Marts do not understand alternate realities.

They do not understand Ether.

They do not understand why Orphie believes she does not belong.

They understand something simpler.

She is Orphie.

That is enough.

One Mart climbed onto her head while she was sitting outside.

Orphie sighed.

> "...I told you to leave me alone."

The Mart remained.

> "...No."

Orphie looked at it.

The Mart adjusted its tiny top hat.

> "...Stay."

---

# **LUNA**

Luna's assessment was unusually quiet.

> "...She's not asking for space because she wants peace."

> "...She's asking because she thinks nobody should have to stay."

---

# **SOLARIA**

Solaria added:

> "...She thinks being alone is what she deserves."

> "...And everyone keeps proving her wrong by refusing to leave."

---

# **THE CONTRADICTION**

Orphie wants to be left alone.

Everyone wants to respect that.

Nobody wants to abandon her.

So the Blacksite has entered an uncomfortable middle ground.

No one forces themselves upon her.

No one completely disappears.

Food is left nearby.

Messages are sent occasionally.

Corin returns home.

Marts remain within reach.

The SAFEHOUSE continues to remember her.

They wait.

---

# **CURRENT ASSESSMENT**

**Origin:** Another reality.

**Reason for arrival:** Unknown.

**Purpose of arrival:** Unknown.

**Return pathway:** Unknown.

**Physical condition:** 35% Ether composition; golden subdermal luminescence.

**Psychological condition:** Severe emotional detachment, isolation, insomnia, burden fixation.

**Social behavior:** Repeatedly requests isolation.

**Response from personnel:** Request respected partially, but complete isolation avoided.

---

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Nobody knows why Orphie is here.

Nobody knows what happened to her original reality.

Nobody knows whether she was supposed to cross into this one.

Nobody knows whether she was ever supposed to survive the crossing.

And Orphie doesn't know either.

She only knows that she woke up in a world that wasn't hers—

and somehow ended up living in it.

Now she sits alone in a treehouse beneath the Blacksite's forest canopy, telling everyone:

> **"Leave me alone."**

And everyone hears something else.

Not:

> _Go away._

But:

> **"Please don't make me believe I matter."**

So they don't force themselves into her room.

They don't drag her to SAFEHOUSE.

They don't demand that she be happy.

They simply refuse to disappear.

Because whatever reality Orphie came from may have broken her.

But this one has not forgotten her.

**End of Cross-Reality Anomaly Report**
