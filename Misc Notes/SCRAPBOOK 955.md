# SAFEHOUSE ANOMALOUS FOOD REACTION REPORT

## SUBJECT: JANE DOE / JOHN DOE

**Subject:** Jane Doe  
**Known Alternate State:** John Doe  
**Trigger:** Consumption of unprocessed strawberries  
**Reversion:** Not yet formally documented  
**Classification:** Anomalous Physiological Reaction  
**Threat Level:** None inherently  
**Embarrassment Level:** Severe

---

## 1. SUMMARY

Jane Doe possesses an anomalous reaction to **unprocessed strawberries**.

If Jane is successfully tricked into eating an unprocessed strawberry, her body undergoes an immediate anomalous transformation.

The resulting individual is externally recognizable as Jane, but presents as a male counterpart.

The alternate identity has been informally designated:

> **JOHN DOE**

The transformation does not appear to alter Jane's memories, intelligence, personality, skills, or general behavior.

John is still Jane.

He simply looks and sounds different.

---

# 2. PHYSICAL DIFFERENCES

The transformation produces several consistent changes.

### Hair

Jane's normally maintained hair becomes noticeably more messy and disorganized.

The change appears spontaneous rather than the result of physical disturbance.

### Build

John retains Jane's general body structure but has a slightly flatter chest.

The difference is noticeable but relatively minor.

### Voice

The most immediately obvious change is vocal.

Jane's voice becomes considerably deeper and more muscular-sounding.

Personnel have described it as:

> **"Jane's voice if she suddenly decided to become the final boss of a gym."**

The description has not been formally adopted by Medical.

---

# 3. COGNITIVE CONTINUITY

Despite the transformation, John retains:

- Jane's memories.
    
- Jane's intelligence.
    
- Jane's intelligence-gathering skills.
    
- Jane's personality.
    
- Jane's sense of humor.
    
- Jane's knowledge of SAFEHOUSE.
    
- Jane's relationships.
    
- Jane's ability to recognize everyone.
    
- Jane's ability to become annoyed when teased.
    

There is therefore no evidence that **John Doe is a separate person.**

The current medical consensus is:

> **John Doe is Jane Doe experiencing an anomalous physiological state.**

---

# 4. THE STRAWBERRY PROBLEM

The precise mechanism remains unknown.

The important distinction is that the reaction appears to require:

> **UNPROCESSED STRAWBERRIES**

Processed strawberry products have not demonstrated the same effect.

Current records therefore distinguish between:

**Strawberry:** Potentially dangerous to Jane's identity presentation.

**Strawberry cake:** Not currently confirmed.

**Strawberry jam:** Not currently confirmed.

**Strawberry-flavored products:** Not currently confirmed.

**Actual raw strawberry:** **DO NOT FEED TO JANE.**

---

# 5. INCIDENT

The first confirmed incident occurred when someone managed to convince Jane to eat a strawberry without informing her of the potential reaction.

Jane initially showed no concern.

She ate it.

Then stopped.

> **Jane:** "...What did I just eat?"

The individual responsible did not answer.

Jane looked down at herself.

Her hair had become noticeably messier.

Her voice changed when she spoke again.

> **Jane:** "...Why do I sound like this?"

Silence.

Jane looked toward the nearest reflective surface.

Several seconds passed.

> **John:** "...Oh."

The culprit immediately began laughing.

John slowly turned toward them.

> **John:** "You have approximately five seconds to explain yourself."

The culprit stopped laughing.

---

# 6. SAFEHOUSE REACTION

The transformation caused considerable confusion among Blacksite personnel.

A worker encountered John in a corridor.

> **Worker:** "Excuse me, are you—"

John turned.

> **John:** "Yeah?"

The worker froze.

> **Worker:** "...Jane?"

> **John:** "Technically."

> **Worker:** "WHAT?"

John sighed.

> **John:** "Apparently I'm John now."

The worker immediately left.

John watched them go.

> **John:** "...I don't even know why they're scared."

From around the corner:

> **Archivist:** "Because you're Jane with a man's voice."

> **John:** "That's fair."

---

# 7. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri was considerably less concerned.

She approached John.

Looked up.

Stared.

Then:

> **Neri:** "...Jane?"

> **John:** "Yeah."

> **Neri:** "You look weird."

> **John:** "Thank you."

Neri stared at his hair.

> **Neri:** "Your hair is messy."

> **John:** "I noticed."

> **Neri:** "You sound funny."

John sighed.

> **John:** "I noticed that too."

Neri thought for a moment.

> **Neri:** "Can I call you John?"

John shrugged.

> **John:** "Sure."

Neri nodded.

> **Neri:** "Okay, John."

Then she walked away.

No further investigation was conducted.

---

# 8. ADMINISTRATIVE WARNING

Personnel are reminded that deliberately feeding Jane unprocessed strawberries without her consent constitutes **unauthorized anomalous experimentation on a resident.**

Do not attempt to trigger the transformation for entertainment.

Do not attempt to determine whether:

- Strawberry smoothies count.
    
- Strawberry ice cream counts.
    
- Strawberry cheesecake counts.
    
- Strawberry frosting counts.
    
- Strawberry milk counts.
    
- Artificial strawberry flavoring counts.
    

Medical has specifically requested that personnel stop conducting "experiments."

Jane has requested the same.

Neri has apparently asked whether strawberry cake counts.

Medical has not answered.

---

# 9. CURRENT DESIGNATION

Until the mechanism is fully understood, the following terminology is recommended:

**Primary State:** Jane Doe  
**Alternate State:** John Doe  
**Original Name:** Lucifer Jameson — RESTRICTED  
**Trigger:** Unprocessed strawberry  
**Personality Continuity:** Confirmed  
**Memory Continuity:** Confirmed

John Doe should therefore be treated as **Jane Doe in an anomalous transformed state**, rather than as a new resident.
