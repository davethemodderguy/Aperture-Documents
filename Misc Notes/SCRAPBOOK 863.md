# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. SECURITY & MEDICAL DIVISION

### SAFEHOUSE NIGHT INCIDENT REPORT

**INCIDENT ID:** SH-NERI-MED-███  
**DATE:** ██/██/████  
**TIME:** 03:██  
**LOCATION:** SAFEHOUSE — CAFETERIA  
**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**SECONDARY SUBJECT:** Z-356-B "Bao"  
**MEDICAL CLASSIFICATION:** SPONTANEOUS ANEMIC COLLAPSE  
**STATUS:** RESOLVED

---

# 1. INCIDENT SUMMARY

At approximately 03:██, a SAFEHOUSE security guard conducting a routine overnight patrol discovered **Neri** sitting on the cafeteria floor.

She was positioned against the wall.

She was awake.

She was holding a small cupcake.

The cupcake had been prepared by **Guiding Light** earlier that day.

Neri was eating it slowly.

She was also crying.

Security footage indicates that Neri had been alone in the cafeteria for approximately ██ minutes before the guard arrived.

There was no evidence of an altercation, injury, physical exertion, or external threat.

She had simply been sitting there.

Eating.

Crying.

---

# 2. SECURITY FOOTAGE

### 03:██:12

Neri enters the cafeteria.

She walks to the refrigerator.

Retrieves the small cupcake.

### 03:██:31

Neri sits on the floor beside the wall rather than at one of the tables.

She places the cupcake in her lap.

### 03:██:48

Neri takes her first bite.

She begins crying.

### 03:██–03:██

Neri continues eating.

She periodically wipes her face with her sleeve.

The crying does not stop.

No audible conversation is detected.

### 03:██:04

Neri quietly says:

> **"I'm sorry."**

No recipient is apparent.

### 03:██:19

Another bite.

> **"I miss you."**

### 03:██:42

Neri continues eating.

Her crying becomes heavier.

She does not appear to be physically injured.

---

# 3. SECURITY RESPONSE

At approximately 04:██, an overnight guard entered the cafeteria.

The guard initially assumed Neri was simply awake and upset.

He approached her.

> **GUARD:** "Neri?"

No response.

The guard crouched beside her.

> **GUARD:** "Hey. You okay?"

Neri did not answer.

The guard gently nudged her shoulder.

Nothing.

He nudged her again.

Nothing.

The guard immediately recognized that something was wrong.

Neri was unconscious.

The cupcake remained in her hand.

---

# 4. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT

Medical personnel were contacted immediately.

Initial observations included:

- Severe pallor.
    
- Weak or absent responsiveness.
    
- Critically reduced circulation.
    
- Extremely low functional blood volume.
    
- Cardiovascular instability.
    

No external bleeding was found.

No physical trauma was identified.

No evidence suggested poisoning.

No evidence suggested an attack.

The conclusion was immediate:

> **SPONTANEOUS ANEMIC EPISODE.**

Neri's blood had degraded internally to a critical level.

She had been sitting completely still.

The episode had occurred despite the absence of exertion.

---

# 5. BAO AWAKENED

SAFEHOUSE personnel immediately contacted **Bao**.

Bao was asleep when contacted.

She reportedly answered the emergency call with:

> **BAO:** "...What happened?"

> **MEDICAL:** "It's Neri."

Bao was awake immediately.

> **BAO:** "Where?"

> **MEDICAL:** "Cafeteria."

Bao arrived shortly afterward.

She saw Neri unconscious against the wall.

She immediately understood what had happened.

> **BAO:** "Her blood?"

> **DOCTOR:** "Critical."

Bao extended one hand.

> **BAO:** "Okay."

---

# 6. EMERGENCY BLOOD SUPPLEMENTATION

Bao possesses an unusually strong regenerative capability.

Minor wounds that would normally require medical treatment can close rapidly.

More significant injuries can also regenerate with sufficient time and available biological resources.

As a result, Bao can safely provide small quantities of her blood when medically necessary without causing lasting harm to herself.

Bao made a small cut on her finger.

The wound began regenerating almost immediately.

She placed the injured finger gently against Neri's mouth.

Medical personnel monitored the process closely.

Neri's body began receiving Bao's compatible blood.

Within the following minutes, cardiovascular activity began improving.

The intervention did not constitute a permanent cure.

It simply restored enough functional blood to bring Neri above her critical threshold.

---

# 7. RECOVERY

Neri remained unconscious for several minutes.

Her breathing gradually stabilized.

Heart activity returned to a safer range.

Color slowly returned to her skin.

At approximately ██:██, Neri regained consciousness.

Her first visible reaction was confusion.

She looked around.

Then she noticed Bao.

> **NERI:** "...Mom?"

> **BAO:** "I'm here."

Neri was quiet for several seconds.

> **NERI:** "What happened?"

Bao looked toward the medical personnel.

Then back at Neri.

> **BAO:** "Your blood dropped again."

Neri looked down at the cupcake still sitting beside her.

> **NERI:** "...I didn't even do anything."

> **BAO:** "I know."

Neri began crying again.

Bao hugged her.

> **BAO:** "You don't have to do anything for it to happen."

---

# 8. THE CUPCAKE

The cupcake was recovered from the scene.

Approximately 63% had been consumed.

The remaining portion was discarded after medical personnel determined that it had been sitting on the floor during the incident.

A replacement cupcake was later offered.

Neri refused initially.

> **NERI:** "I don't want it."

Bao remained beside her.

> **BAO:** "That's okay."

Several minutes later:

> **NERI:** "...Maybe later."

No one pressured her.

---

# 9. GUIDING LIGHT

Guiding Light was informed that the cupcake she had prepared had been present during the incident.

She reportedly became extremely concerned.

> **GUIDING LIGHT:** "Was she eating the cupcake?"

> **MEDICAL:** "Yes."

> **GUIDING LIGHT:** "Did the cupcake cause this?"

> **MEDICAL:** "No."

> **GUIDING LIGHT:** "Are you certain?"

> **MEDICAL:** "Absolutely."

> **GUIDING LIGHT:** "Then why was she crying?"

The medical officer paused.

> **MEDICAL:** "We don't know."

Guiding Light subsequently prepared another cupcake.

She did not immediately give it to Neri.

Instead, she left it in the cafeteria with a small note:

> **"For whenever you're ready."**

Neri eventually ate it.

She did not discuss her past.

She simply said:

> **"Thank you."**

---

# 10. MEDICAL SIGNIFICANCE

This incident reinforces one of the most important characteristics of Neri's condition:

### HER ANEMIA IS SPONTANEOUS.

Neri does not need to:

- Fight.
    
- Run.
    
- Become injured.
    
- Lose blood externally.
    
- Become severely stressed.
    
- Become dehydrated.
    
- Skip meals.
    

Her blood can deteriorate while she is doing absolutely nothing.

On this occasion, she was:

> **Sitting on the floor.**

> **Eating a cupcake.**

> **Crying.**

And her circulatory system still failed.

This is why medical monitoring cannot rely exclusively on activity levels or visible injury.

---

# 11. BAO'S REGENERATIVE CAPABILITY

Bao's ability to regenerate tissue significantly reduces the personal cost of emergency blood donation.

Her regenerative physiology allows her to recover from small blood draws and minor wounds rapidly.

However, medical personnel continue to monitor Bao during emergency interventions.

Her regenerative ability is **not** considered an excuse to treat her as an unlimited biological resource.

Bao is Neri's mother.

She is not a blood bank.

The fact that she can regenerate does not remove the requirement for consent, medical supervision, or appropriate care.

---

# 12. POST-INCIDENT OBSERVATION

Neri remained with Bao for the remainder of the night.

She eventually became sleepy.

This was considered notable given her longstanding refusal to sleep due to recurring nightmares.

Bao stayed beside her.

Neri eventually leaned against Bao and closed her eyes.

She did not appear frightened.

She simply looked exhausted.

Before falling asleep, she quietly asked:

> **NERI:** "Mom?"

> **BAO:** "Yeah?"

> **NERI:** "Can I have another cupcake tomorrow?"

Bao smiled.

> **BAO:** "Of course."
