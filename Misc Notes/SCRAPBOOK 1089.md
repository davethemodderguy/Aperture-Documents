# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## C.O.R.E. – CENTRAL OPERATIONS & RESEARCH EXECUTIVE

### PSYCHOLOGICAL & RESIDENTIAL WELFARE DIVISION

**DOCUMENT ID:** C.O.R.E.-WEL-4C-NERI-196  
**CLASSIFICATION:** RESTRICTED — RESIDENTIAL WELFARE / WEATHER EXPOSURE  
**STATUS:** INCIDENT CLOSED — MONITORING CONTINUES  
**SUBJECT:** Neri  
**INCIDENT:** Subject forgotten outdoors during snowfall

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Neri was forgotten outside.

Again.

The incident occurred while Neri was in **Plush Form**.

She had been accompanying Alice during outdoor activity near the Blacksite's exterior recreational grounds.

At some point, Alice's memory anomaly resulted in Neri's presence being completely removed from Alice's accessible memory.

Alice returned indoors.

Neri remained outside.

Snow began falling shortly afterward.

Neri was eventually discovered by a maintenance employee.

She was covered in snow.

She was medically stable.

She was not particularly concerned.

---

# 2. INITIAL DISCOVERY

At approximately 17:40, a maintenance employee exited the southern service entrance.

He noticed a small shape sitting in the snow beside the walkway.

Initially, he assumed it was discarded equipment.

He approached.

The shape moved.

The employee stopped.

**EMPLOYEE:**  
"...Oh."

Neri looked up.

Snow had accumulated across her long white hair and oversized hoodie.

Her glasses were partially covered.

Her seal tail was almost completely buried.

The employee crouched.

**EMPLOYEE:**  
"Neri?"

Neri nodded.

**EMPLOYEE:**  
"...How long have you been out here?"

Neri shrugged.

The employee checked his watch.

His expression changed.

---

# 3. CONDITION

Neri was covered in snow but otherwise responsive.

She was sitting upright.

No visible injury was identified.

Her plush construction had prevented ordinary clothing saturation from becoming a significant concern.

She was, however, cold.

When asked whether she wanted to go inside, Neri nodded.

The employee carefully lifted her.

Neri did not resist.

She simply rested against his arm.

After several seconds:

**NERI:**  
"...Cold."

This was the only verbal complaint recorded during recovery.

---

# 4. THE WALK INSIDE

The employee carried Neri toward the entrance.

Several flakes of snow remained caught in her hair.

One remained on the edge of her glasses.

Neri noticed it.

She tried to remove it.

Missed.

Tried again.

Missed.

The employee removed it for her.

Neri looked at him.

**NERI:**  
"...Thanks."

**EMPLOYEE:**  
"You're welcome."

Pause.

**EMPLOYEE:**  
"Do you know who left you outside?"

Neri nodded.

**EMPLOYEE:**  
"Alice?"

Neri nodded.

The employee hesitated.

**EMPLOYEE:**  
"Are you upset?"

Neri shook her head.

There was no anger.

No visible distress.

No accusation.

Just fatigue.

---

# 5. ALICE

Alice was located approximately twenty minutes later.

She had no memory of taking Neri outside.

She did not remember Neri accompanying her.

She did not initially understand why Neri was being carried back inside.

Upon seeing her, Alice immediately asked:

> "Why is Neri snowy?"

No one answered immediately.

Alice stared.

Then:

> "...Oh."

She looked genuinely confused.

Not guilty.

Not defensive.

The memory simply wasn't there.

---

# 6. NERI'S RESPONSE

Neri was placed on a heated blanket in the cafeteria.

Alice approached.

Neri looked at her.

Alice crouched down.

**ALICE:**  
"Did I forget you again?"

Neri nodded.

Alice's ears lowered.

**ALICE:**  
"I'm sorry."

Neri stared at her for several seconds.

Then she reached out.

Alice hesitated.

Neri tapped Alice's hand once.

No hostility was observed.

No resentment.

Neri then pulled the blanket around herself.

The interaction ended there.

---

# 7. MEDICAL OBSERVATION

Medical personnel conducted a routine post-exposure assessment.

No acute injury was identified.

No new anemic episode occurred during the incident.

Neri remained tired for the remainder of the evening.

Bao was informed.

Bao arrived approximately fifteen minutes later.

She looked at Neri.

Then at the snow still caught in her hair.

Then at Alice.

Then back at Neri.

**BAO:**  
"...Again?"

Neri nodded.

Bao sighed.

She removed the remaining snow from Neri's hair.

Neri leaned slightly into her hand.

No further discussion was required.

---

# 8. NERI'S BEHAVIOR AFTERWARD

Neri did not appear frightened by the incident.

She did not appear angry.

She did not blame Alice.

She did not ask anyone to remain with her.

After warming up, Neri drank coffee.

She then climbed onto a nearby chair.

She sat there for approximately twenty minutes.

Eventually, she transformed back into her normal form.

She put her glasses back into position.

Then she returned to her room.

Alone.

---

# 9. SAFETY CONSIDERATIONS

The absence of psychological distress does **not** make repeated outdoor exposure acceptable.

Neri's current lack of resentment toward Alice does not eliminate the practical risk created by Alice's memory anomaly.

Previous incidents have demonstrated that Alice may forget:

- that Neri is present;
    
- where Neri was left;
    
- that she was responsible for Neri's care;
    
- and, temporarily, that Neri exists at all.
    

Accordingly:

**Alice is not to be considered a reliable sole supervisor for Neri during outdoor activities.**

This is a safety restriction.

It is not a disciplinary measure.

---

# 10. PERSONNEL RESPONSE

Personnel are instructed not to turn the incident into a social event.

Do not surround Neri.

Do not repeatedly ask:

> "Are you okay?"

Do not insist on staying beside her.

Do not tell her:

> "You should be angry."

Do not tell her:

> "Alice keeps forgetting you."

Neri already knows.

The appropriate response is simple:

Provide warmth.

Provide medical assessment when appropriate.

Provide food or drink if she wants it.

Allow her to leave.

---

# 11. NEWCOMER RESPONSE

Several recently assigned employees were present when Neri returned indoors.

One asked:

**NEW EMPLOYEE:**  
"Is that the researcher?"

Another answered:

**EMPLOYEE:**  
"Yes."

**NEW EMPLOYEE:**  
"Why is she covered in snow?"

Pause.

**EMPLOYEE:**  
"Alice forgot her outside."

The newcomer looked toward Alice.

Then toward Neri.

Then back toward Alice.

**NEW EMPLOYEE:**  
"...Again?"

The employee nodded.

The newcomer approached Neri cautiously.

Neri looked up.

The newcomer held out a dry towel.

Neri accepted it.

She placed it over her own head.

Only her glasses remained visible.

The newcomer smiled.

**NEW EMPLOYEE:**  
"Better?"

A muffled voice came from beneath the towel.

**NERI:**  
"...Mm-hm."

---

# 12. FINAL ASSESSMENT

This incident represents another recurrence of an already documented failure mode.

The important distinction is that Neri's emotional response has changed.

Previously, being forgotten could reinforce beliefs of abandonment, worthlessness, and disposability.

That response is no longer observed in relation to Alice.

Neri appears to understand that Alice's memory failure is an anomaly.

She does not interpret every instance of being forgotten as proof that she is unloved.

She simply waits.

Or moves.

Or gets found.

Or continues her day.

This is a significant improvement in emotional stability.

It does **not** mean the situation is safe.

---

## C.O.R.E. CLOSING NOTE

The maintenance employee responsible for finding Neri submitted the following comment:

> "I thought someone had left a stuffed animal outside."

He was later informed that the stuffed animal was a ten-year-old chemist.

He paused.

Then asked:

> "Does she need anything?"

The answer was:

> "Probably coffee."

He returned ten minutes later with a cup.

Neri accepted it.

She sat by the window.

Snow continued falling outside.

This time, she was inside.
