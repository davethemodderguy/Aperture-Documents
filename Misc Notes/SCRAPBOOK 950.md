# APERTURE INDUSTRIES

## SAFEHOUSE RESIDENT INDUCTION REPORT

**Document ID:** SH-RES-020  
**Classification:** SAFEHOUSE INTERNAL  
**Subject:** Jane Doe  
**Species:** Rat Thiren  
**Former Affiliation:** Woodland Coorp  
**Current Affiliation:** Aperture Industries / SAFEHOUSE  
**Previous Position:** Woodland Coorp Field Agent  
**Current Status:** **PERMANENT SAFEHOUSE RESIDENT**

---

# 1. RESIDENT STATUS

Following the successful completion of her infiltration of Woodland Coorp, **Jane Doe has been formally accepted as a permanent resident of SAFEHOUSE.**

Her previous status as an embedded Woodland Field Agent has been terminated.

Jane is no longer required to maintain a cover identity or return to Woodland Coorp.

She has instead been provided permanent accommodation within SAFEHOUSE and full resident privileges.

The transition was remarkably uncomplicated.

Jane already knew a substantial number of SAFEHOUSE residents personally through her previous intelligence calls.

In practical terms, she had already been talking to the household for months.

She simply had not physically lived there yet.

---

# 2. FIRST ACTION AS A RESIDENT

Jane's first recorded action after becoming a SAFEHOUSE resident was not:

- Requesting a room;
    
- Reviewing facility procedures;
    
- Meeting administration;
    
- Asking about her new duties;
    
- Inspecting the Blacksite;
    
- Discussing her former employer.
    

Instead, Jane walked into the cafeteria.

She found **Neri**.

And gave her a cake.

Neri reportedly accepted it immediately.

No questions were asked.

No introduction was required.

The interaction was recorded as:

> **Jane:** “Here.”

> **Neri:** “For me?”

> **Jane:** “Yeah.”

> **Neri:** “Why?”

> **Jane:** “Because I know you like cake.”

> **Neri:** “Okay.”

_Neri takes the cake._

> **Neri:** “You're good.”

Jane reportedly laughed.

---

# 3. NERI'S REACTION

The gesture was particularly significant because Jane had spent considerable time speaking with SAFEHOUSE residents remotely.

She was already aware of Neri's association with cake and her routine of eating it in the cafeteria.

Rather than making a formal introduction, Jane simply continued the relationship they had already established through the telephone.

Neri subsequently treated Jane as a familiar person rather than a stranger.

This is considered a successful integration indicator.

---

# 4. MEETING THE ARCHITECTS

Following her introduction to the residents, Jane was introduced to the three Architects commonly encountered around SAFEHOUSE:

- **Guiding Light**
    
- **Curious Light**
    
- **Mischievous Light**
    

Jane was already aware of the Architects from Aperture intelligence records.

Knowing about them from documents, however, proved considerably different from actually meeting them.

Her first interaction with the three was reportedly less formal than expected.

---

## 4.1 GUIDING LIGHT

Guiding Light greeted Jane first.

> **Guiding Light:** “You're Jane!”

> **Jane:** “That's me.”

> **Guiding Light:** “The Rat Thiren!”

> **Jane:** “...That's also me.”

> **Guiding Light:** “Welcome!”

> **Jane:** “Thanks.”

Guiding Light immediately began asking questions about Jane's experiences within Woodland Coorp.

Jane answered several of them before eventually saying:

> **“Can we not talk about work for a while?”**

Guiding Light agreed.

The conversation subsequently became considerably more casual.

---

## 4.2 CURIOUS LIGHT

Curious Light was considerably more interested in Jane herself.

Questions reportedly included:

- How Jane became an informant;
    
- How she maintained her cover;
    
- How she communicated with the Blacksite;
    
- How Woodland failed to notice;
    
- How her tail worked;
    
- Whether she could climb well;
    
- Whether she could run across walls;
    
- Whether rats actually liked cheese.
    

The final question was answered with:

> **Jane:** “I'm a Thiren.”

> **Curious Light:** “So?”

> **Jane:** “I'm not answering that.”

---

## 4.3 MISCHIEVOUS LIGHT

Mischievous Light was immediately interested in Jane's previous occupation.

Specifically:

> **Mischievous Light:** “So you spent all that time pretending to work for Woodland?”

> **Jane:** “Yep.”

> **Mischievous Light:** “And they believed you?”

> **Jane:** “Yep.”

> **Mischievous Light:** “That's hilarious.”

> **Jane:** “It was stressful.”

> **Mischievous Light:** “Still hilarious.”

Jane reportedly agreed after approximately three seconds.

---

# 5. JANE AND THE ARCHITECTS

Jane quickly became comfortable around the three Architects.

The interaction did not remain an intelligence briefing.

Instead, it became a normal social interaction.

Guiding Light was enthusiastic about Jane becoming part of SAFEHOUSE.

Curious Light was fascinated by her experiences and abilities.

Mischievous Light immediately treated her like someone who could potentially participate in mischief.

Jane appeared to have no objection to any of this.

---

# 6. INTEGRATION WITH SAFEHOUSE

After meeting the Architects, Jane spent the remainder of her first day interacting with the other residents.

Because Jane had already communicated with many of them during her infiltration, the process was unusually natural.

There was little of the typical:

> “Nice to meet you.”

Instead, many interactions were closer to:

> “Oh, you're finally here.”

Jane was already familiar with the voices, personalities, and habits of many residents.

The only major difference was that she could finally interact with them in person.

# 8. THE STRANGENESS OF THE SITUATION

Several residents noted that Jane's integration felt strangely familiar despite this being her first official day as a resident.

The reason is straightforward.

Jane had already been present in SAFEHOUSE life through her calls.

She had spoken with residents.

She had heard their conversations.

She had been asked about their days.

She had participated in jokes.

She had delivered intelligence.

She had even spoken with Neri.

The only thing missing was physical presence.

As a result, Jane's arrival was less:

> **“A stranger has joined SAFEHOUSE.”**

and more:

> **“The person from the phone finally showed up.”**

---

# 9. NERI'S ASSESSMENT

Neri was reportedly asked what she thought about Jane becoming a resident.

Her answer:

> **“She's nice.”**

After being asked whether she trusted Jane:

> **“Yeah.”**

Then, after looking at the cake Jane had given her:

> **“She brought cake.”**

The conversation ended there.

No further justification was considered necessary.

---

# 10. CURRENT RESIDENT RELATIONSHIP

Jane has rapidly established herself as part of the SAFEHOUSE social environment.

Her former role as an informant remains known to senior personnel, but it is no longer treated as her defining characteristic.

Jane is now primarily regarded as:

> **A resident.**

Her intelligence experience remains valuable to Aperture.

Her knowledge of Woodland remains valuable.

Her skills remain valuable.

But she is no longer required to spend every day pretending to belong somewhere else.

She belongs here now.

Jane's first day as a SAFEHOUSE resident consisted primarily of:

Jane spent months pretending that SAFEHOUSE was simply the place where her “brother” happened to answer the phone.
