### **PROJECT SAFEHOUSE – DAILY LIFE LOG**

## **SUBJECT: MOM CLUB GATHERING & CORIN'S AFTERNOON**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Internal Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

With Lucy Merryweather's visit to Heaven's Blacksite, all four resident mothers of Project SAFEHOUSE were present simultaneously.

SAFEHOUSE residents unofficially referred to the gathering as:

> **"THE Mom Club."**

Members present:

- Astral
    
- Bao
    
- Lucy Merryweather
    
- Lumia ("Lumi") Lockwood
    

The gathering took place in the central living area while the remainder of SAFEHOUSE continued their usual activities.

---

# **THE MOM CLUB**

Tea was prepared.

Cookies somehow appeared without anyone remembering who baked them.

Conversation flowed naturally.

The topics ranged from children and work to cooking, old memories, and the occasional complaint about paperwork.

Bao laughed as she recounted Frost freezing an entire section of the kitchen while trying to chill drinks.

Astral responded with a story about Mart accidentally reading for so long that he completely missed dinner.

Lucy shared several stories from Belle and Endmin's childhood.

Lumi quietly smiled as she remembered Corin insisting she could solve arithmetic faster than a calculator when she was very young.

The conversation quickly turned into four mothers exchanging stories about their children.

According to Kent:

> "...Nobody is escaping being embarrassed today."

---

# **CORIN**

Rather than joining the gathering, Corin quietly settled into one of the lounge chairs with a book.

She preferred listening over speaking.

The quiet atmosphere suited her.

Mart eventually noticed.

Without saying anything, he picked another book from the shelf and sat in the chair opposite hers.

No greeting.

No conversation.

Only the occasional sound of pages turning.

Nearly an hour passed before either of them spoke.

Corin looked up briefly.

> "...Thanks."

Mart lowered his book slightly.

> "For what?"

Corin hesitated.

> "...Keeping me company."

Mart smiled faintly.

> "I wasn't doing anything."

Corin looked back at her book.

> "...Exactly."

The room returned to silence.

Comfortable silence.

---

# **THE MOTHERS NOTICE**

From across the lounge, Bao quietly pointed toward the pair.

> "Look."

Lucy smiled.

Mart was completely absorbed in his novel.

Corin had become equally distracted by hers.

Neither had moved for nearly twenty minutes.

Astral laughed softly.

> "That's Mart."

Lumi watched her daughter.

Corin's shoulders were relaxed.

She wasn't staring into space.

She wasn't replaying old memories.

She was simply...

Reading.

Lumi smiled.

> "...Thank you, Mart."

Mart, still reading, had no idea she had said it.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Medical Services note that Corin's ability to spend extended periods quietly alongside another resident represents continued progress in her recovery.

Mart's calm and patient personality allows Corin to feel accompanied without feeling pressured to socialize.

Meanwhile, "THE Mom Club" continues to meet whenever all four mothers are present, much to the amusement—and occasional embarrassment—of their children.

Project SAFEHOUSE staff unanimously agree on one thing.

When all four moms are together...

Nobody leaves without at least one childhood story being shared.
