# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **C.O.R.E. – ANOMALOUS ABILITY DOSSIER**

### **SUBJECT: LUMI – ICE MAGE SPELL CATALOGUE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 8 – Research, Medical & Executive Personnel

---

# **OVERVIEW**

As the last known Ice Mage, Lumi possesses an extensive arsenal of frost-based magic accumulated through years of study, practice, and battlefield experience.

Contrary to common assumptions, Ice Magic is not solely an offensive discipline.

Historically, it was regarded as one of the most versatile magical arts, emphasizing preservation, protection, and control before destruction.

Lumi is considered the final living repository of this knowledge.

---

# **KNOWN DISCIPLINES**

## **Cryokinesis**

The fundamental manipulation of ice and snow.

Capabilities include:

- Forming ice from ambient moisture.
    
- Reshaping existing ice.
    
- Controlling snowfall.
    
- Creating frozen terrain.
    
- Constructing walls and barriers.
    

---

## **Cryo Constructs**

Lumi can rapidly create durable structures from magically reinforced ice.

Documented examples include:

- Bridges.
    
- Staircases.
    
- Defensive walls.
    
- Pillars.
    
- Shelters.
    
- Furniture.
    
- Temporary buildings.
    

The constructs gradually melt only if Lumi intentionally dismisses them or they are subjected to overwhelming heat.

---

## **Ice Weapons**

Lumi is capable of forming functional weapons in seconds.

Examples include:

- Swords.
    
- Spears.
    
- Polearms.
    
- Daggers.
    
- Shields.
    
- Bows.
    
- Arrows.
    

The weapons possess hardness comparable to high-quality forged steel while maintained through Ether.

---

## **Defensive Magic**

Lumi specializes in protective spells.

Known techniques include:

- Ice barriers.
    
- Reflective crystal shields.
    
- Frost domes.
    
- Projectile interception.
    
- Emergency cover.
    

These abilities historically allowed Ice Mages to defend settlements during natural disasters and warfare.

---

## **Environmental Cooling**

Rather than simply creating ice, Lumi can lower ambient temperatures across large areas.

Applications include:

- Preventing heatstroke.
    
- Food preservation.
    
- Fire suppression.
    
- Stabilizing sensitive medical supplies.
    
- Climate regulation.
    

---

## **Medical Cryomancy**

One of the rarest Ice Mage arts.

Lumi can precisely regulate temperature to:

- Reduce swelling.
    
- Slow bleeding.
    
- Ease pain.
    
- Limit tissue damage.
    
- Preserve critically injured patients until definitive treatment is available.
    

Medical Division considers this among her most valuable abilities.

---

## **Snow Manipulation**

Lumi can generate and control snowfall independently of local weather conditions.

The snowfall may be:

- Gentle.
    
- Dense.
    
- Localized.
    
- Wide-area.
    

The snow itself is ordinary unless intentionally infused with Ether.

---

## **Crystal Formation**

Lumi can grow complex crystalline ice structures with remarkable precision.

These formations have been used for:

- Architectural reinforcement.
    
- Decorative sculptures.
    
- Magical focusing arrays.
    
- Emergency supports.
    

---

## **Glacial Armor**

By coating herself in layered enchanted ice, Lumi can significantly increase her resistance to physical attacks while retaining mobility.

The armor continuously repairs minor damage while Ether reserves remain sufficient.

---

## **Absolute Freeze**

One of the most demanding techniques available to an Ice Mage.

This spell instantaneously freezes a designated target or area through intense Ether expenditure.

Because improper use can cause catastrophic injury, Lumi rarely employs this ability outside life-threatening situations.

---

# **MAGICAL PHILOSOPHY**

Historical records indicate that Ice Mages viewed their magic differently from many combat disciplines.

Their first lesson was traditionally:

> "Freeze the fire."

Their second:

> "Protect the frightened."

Their third:

> "Only then draw your blade."

Lumi has continued to follow this philosophy throughout her life.

# **CURRENT STATUS**

Lumi remains the only confirmed individual capable of performing the complete range of authentic Ice Mage techniques.

Recognizing the importance of preserving this knowledge, she has begun recording her spells, teachings, and philosophy for future generations.

Not because she expects another Ice Mage to appear.

But because she believes no art should vanish simply because its last practitioner one day does.

As she wrote on the first page of her grimoire:

> **"If I am the last, then let me also be the first teacher of whoever comes next."**
