---

### I. PURPOSE

The **Visions Archive** is a specialized documentation channel under **GENERAL**, created to store and review **observed footage captured through Aperture’s External Observation Technology**.

This system allows Aperture to:

- See through another individual’s perspective
- Record real-time visual input directly from the subject
- Capture phenomena that cannot be externally observed

---

### II. TECHNOLOGY OVERVIEW

**External Observation Technology** utilizes a compact device (commonly a **hair clip**) that links a subject’s visual perception to Aperture systems.

Through this:

- Everything the subject sees is recorded
- Visual distortions, hallucinations, and anomalies are preserved
- Non-standard perception becomes **observable data**

This includes:

- Altered reality states
- Entity-induced hallucinations
- Perspective-based anomalies

---

### III. ARCHIVE FUNCTION

The Visions Archive is used to document:

- Recorded visual experiences from subjects
- Direct interactions with anomalies through perception
- Unfiltered exposure to environments or entities
- Events that cannot be captured by standard surveillance

These records are treated as **first-person evidence**, not traditional logs.

---

### IV. CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM

Although stored under **GENERAL**, each Vision entry is categorized based on its content:

- **C.O.R.E Classification**  
    If the recorded subject involves:
    - Containment environments
    - Entities within blacksites
    - Internal Aperture operations
- **N.O.S.T Classification**  
    If the recorded subject involves:
    - Non-Earth environments
    - Interdimensional spaces
    - Extraterrestrial or unknown realms

---

### V. WHY THIS ARCHIVE EXISTS

Standard observation tools have limitations.

They cannot:

- Capture subjective distortion
- Interpret hallucinations
- See what only the subject perceives

The Visions Archive exists to record:

> **What is seen—but not always real.**

This fills the gap between:

- Physical reality
- Perceived reality

---

### VI. LIMITATIONS

- Data accuracy depends on the subject’s condition
- Visual input may be unstable or corrupted
- Hallucinations cannot always be distinguished from reality

All entries require careful analysis.

---

### VII. OPERATIONAL SIGNIFICANCE

The Visions Archive provides:

- Insight into anomaly-induced perception
- Access to otherwise unobservable phenomena
- Critical data for both C.O.R.E and N.O.S.T operations

Without it, entire categories of anomalies would remain invisible.

---

Cameras show what’s there.

This system shows what’s seen.

And sometimes—

**those are not the same thing.**
