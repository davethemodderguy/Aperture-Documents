### I. SUMMARY

The event was triggered unintentionally during physical contact with **Z-314-C (Charlie)**.

---

### II. RECORDED VISION

Charlie stood as she always did—composed, radiant, wings folded neatly behind her.

Director Lucious approached without hesitation.

He embraced her.

No protocol. No calculation.

Just instinct.

---

“Woah… this isn’t you. What’s wrong?”

---

At the moment of contact, **activation occurred**.

Director Lucious’ eyes emitted a **bright azure glow**.

Then—

**It began.**

---

Not memories.

Not images.

**Fragments.**

Violent. Unstable. Overlapping.

---

A sky that burned without heat.  
Feathers falling—but never touching the ground.  
A voice calling a name that didn’t belong to Charlie—  
—but felt like it did.

---

Pain.

Not physical.

Something deeper.

Something _remembered_.

---

Scenes tore through his mind without order:

- Hands reaching out—then gone
- A figure standing alone against something vast and unseen
- Light breaking apart into shards, each carrying a piece of someone else

---

And beneath all of it—

A constant.

A presence.

Familiar.

Too familiar.

---

Not something he was seeing.

Something he had **lost**.

---

The fragments didn’t slow.

They **collapsed** into one.

A single realization forced through everything else:

---

**Charlie is Lucy Lucious.**

Reincarnated.

---

Not similar.

Not connected.

**The same.**

---

### III. TERMINATION

The vision ended abruptly.

Director Lucious returned to baseline awareness.

Physiological response:

- Elevated stress
- Temporary loss of speech
- Cognitive overload

He attempted to speak—

But stopped.

---

A prior directive surfaced:

> _“Don’t tell them about their past.”_  
> _“The consequences are way harsher for you to take.”_  
> — Archangel Rose
