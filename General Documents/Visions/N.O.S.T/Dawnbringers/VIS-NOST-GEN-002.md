### II. RECORDED VISION

Star didn’t hesitate.

She ran forward—

and jumped.

---

Director Lucious barely reacted in time, catching her mid-air.  
She was light. Too light.

For a moment, everything felt normal.

Then—

**activation.**

---

The world collapsed again.

---

A small shape.

Fragile.

---

A kitten.

No more than a month old.

---

It cried—soft, desperate—  
as it was pulled away from warmth it didn’t understand it was losing.

A masked figure.

No face. No voice.

Just hands.

Taking.

---

Then—

a new place.

A home.

At first—

gentle.

Food. Shelter. Hands that didn’t hurt.

---

But it didn’t last.

---

The same hands changed.

---

Strikes.

Throws.

Impact after impact.

The world reduced to pain and confusion.

---

Still—

it held on.

Barely breathing.

Barely alive.

---

Until it wasn’t worth keeping.

---

It was thrown away.

Like nothing.

---

Silence.

---

Then—

**a shift.**

---

Another pair of hands.

Familiar.

But blurred.

The face hidden—like something intentionally forgotten.

---

The kitten was lifted again.

Not taken.

**Saved.**

---

Time passed—faster now.

Warmth returned.

Care.

Healing.

A family.

---

Then—

it happened.

---

Noise.

Violence.

An attack.

---

Two children hiding beneath a bed, shaking.

Two guards left standing.

Not enough.

Never enough.

---

And the kitten—

---

It moved.

---

Small.

Weak.

But it didn’t hesitate.

---

It grabbed something it shouldn’t even understand.

A grenade.

---

And ran.

---

No fear.

No pause.

---

Just action.

---

Light.

Sound.

Then—

**nothing.**

---

### III. REALIZATION

The vision ended.

---

Director Lucious stood still.

Not processing.

Not thinking.

---

**Remembering.**

---

The fur.

The color.

The collar.

The nametag.

---

Everything matched.

---

There was no doubt.

---

**Star is Leon.**

---

His cat.

---

The same one he found.

The same one he raised.

The same one that died—

**saving others.**

---

- **Star = Leon (Reincarnated)**

Paused.

Then—

put it away.
