# **II. GENERAL DESCRIPTION**

---

Snowy is a small humanoid anomalous entity originating from Whitefall Lake.

---

Notable traits include:

- white hair
    
- physically fragile appearance
    
- severe cold adaptation
    
- wolf-form transformation capability
    
- anomalous animal communication abilities
    
- chronic ocular injury
    
- corrective lens dependency (human form)
    

---

Snowy frequently alternates between:

- human form
    
- wolf pup form
    

---

### Important Note

Do NOT mistake wolf-form Snowy for a normal animal.

This has already caused several incidents.

---

# **III. CURRENT MEDICAL STATUS**

---

Snowy remains in partial long-term recovery from severe prior injuries.

---

Confirmed conditions include:

- chronic spinal damage
    
- limited stamina
    
- permanent mobility impairment
    
- cold exposure damage to hearing and hands
    
- severe psychological trauma history
    
- persistent left ocular injury
    
- right-eye strain related vision impairment
    

---

### Current Assistive Equipment

Snowy currently requires and/or regularly wears:

- corrective round-frame glasses
    
- insulated snow boots
    
- thermal gloves
    
- Whitefall-adapted beanie with wolf-ear slots
    
- medical eyepatch (usually concealed beneath hair)
    

---

### Staff Reminder

Snowy may appear physically stable while still experiencing pain.

Do not encourage excessive physical activity.

---

Do NOT remove or tamper with:

- his glasses
    
- gloves
    
- beanie
    
- eyepatch
    
- winter gear
    

without explicit permission.

---

# **IV. APPROVED INTERACTION GUIDELINES**

---

## Allowed

- casual conversation
    
- offering warm food/drinks
    
- quiet companionship
    
- headpats (WITH permission)
    
- gentle interaction during wolf form
    
- allowing Yuki/Shira access if accompanying him
    
- helping retrieve dropped glasses if needed
    

---

## Encouraged

- maintaining calm atmosphere
    
- avoiding sudden loud noises
    
- allowing him rest periods
    
- informing him before leaving shared areas unexpectedly
    
- ensuring snowy/icy walkways are safe if he is present
    

---

### Why The Last Rule Exists

Snowy possesses severe abandonment-related trauma.

Sudden unexplained disappearances can trigger psychological distress responses.

---

# **V. PROHIBITED ACTIONS**

---

## STRICTLY PROHIBITED

### 1. Do NOT grab him unexpectedly

Reasons:

- panic responses
    
- defensive wolf biting reflexes
    
- spinal instability risk
    

---

### 2. Do NOT joke about abandonment, disappearance, or suicide

Even mild jokes may trigger severe emotional destabilization.

---

### 3. Do NOT force physical affection

Snowy tolerates affection differently depending on emotional condition.

---

### 4. Do NOT compare him to:

- failed entities
    
- containment subjects
    
- “experiments”
    
- pets (light teasing permitted ONLY by close personnel)
    

---

### 5. Do NOT discuss his disappearance incident casually

This includes:

- “the ridge”
    
- the search period
    
- Exodus writings
    
- blood trails
    
- related recovery events
    

Unless Snowy initiates discussion himself.

---

### 6. DO NOT lift or move the hair covering his injured eye

This includes:

- brushing hair aside
    
- attempting to “check” the injury visually
    
- exposing the eyepatch without permission
    
- touching the concealed side of his face unexpectedly
    

---

#### Reason

The hair concealment behavior functions as:

- emotional boundary stabilization
    
- stress reduction
    
- trauma management
    

Unexpected exposure may trigger:

- panic responses
    
- shutdown behavior
    
- immediate withdrawal
    

---

### 7. Do NOT remove his glasses as a joke

Without corrective lenses:

> Snowy’s vision quality becomes critically impaired

Potential risks include:

- falling
    
- disorientation
    
- panic response
    
- inability to identify surroundings properly
    

---

# **XI. FINAL NOTE**

---

Snowy is not dangerous.

---

But he is fragile in ways most Blacksite entities are not.

---

Not because he lacks strength.

Not because he cannot survive.

---

But because he spent too long believing:

> surviving alone was the same thing as living.

---

The glasses help him see.

The winter gear helps him stay warm.

The hair covering his eye helps him feel safe.

---

These things may appear small to outside personnel.

They are not small to him.

---

Blacksite personnel are reminded:

Snowy responds significantly better to patience than authority.

---

He is more likely to hide suffering than report it.

More likely to apologize than complain.

And more likely to quietly freeze outside waiting for someone

than ask them to stay.

---

So if you see him wandering Sector 4-C quietly—

with snow boots crunching softly,  
glasses slipping slightly,  
and half his face hidden beneath silver bangs—

just greet him normally.

---

That alone helps more than most people realize.
