# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

# **CORE-ZNEKO-PROT-01**

## **POST-EMP SAFETY PROTOCOLS**

### **Classification:** INTERNAL / MANDATORY ENFORCEMENT

### **Applies To:** Neko, Whitefall Laboratory Personnel, Visitors

---

## **I. PURPOSE**

Following the accidental EMP event that disabled all lighting infrastructure within Whitefall Cabin, additional safety regulations have been established regarding Neko's scientific activities.

---

These regulations are intended to:

- Protect Whitefall infrastructure.
    
- Protect Whitefall residents.
    
- Protect Neko from himself.
    
- Protect reality from Neko's curiosity.
    

---

Director Lucious has stated the final objective is the most important.

---

## **II. GENERAL RESTRICTIONS**

### Rule 1

Neko shall not conduct experiments while sleep deprived.

---

### Rule 2

Neko shall not conduct experiments while half asleep.

---

### Rule 3

Neko shall not conduct experiments immediately after waking up.

---

### Rule 4

"Trust me" is not considered a safety procedure.

---

### Rule 5

"Probably safe" is not considered a safety procedure.

---

### Rule 6

"Mostly safe" is not considered a safety procedure.

---

### Rule 7

"Almost certainly safe" is not considered a safety procedure.

---

### Rule 8

All experiments must have an actual written plan before initiation.

---

### Rule 9

Plans written on napkins do not count.

---

### Rule 10

Plans written on cupcakes do not count.

---

## **III. ELECTRICAL RESTRICTIONS**

### Rule 11

Any experiment involving electricity must be reported beforehand.

---

### Rule 12

Any experiment involving electromagnetic fields must be reported beforehand.

---

### Rule 13

Any experiment involving the phrase:

> "I wonder what happens if—"

must be reported beforehand.

---

### Rule 14

Creation of additional EMP-producing compounds is prohibited.

---

### Rule 15

Attempts to improve existing EMP-producing compounds are prohibited.

---

### Rule 16

Attempts to weaponize EMP-producing compounds are prohibited.

---

### Rule 17

Attempts to "just test something quickly" are prohibited.

---

### Rule 18

The phrase:

> "What's the worst that could happen?"

immediately terminates the experiment.

---

### Rule 19

---

### Rule 20

## **V. EXPLOSION PREVENTION**

### Rule 28

Experiments may not exceed containment limits.

---

### Rule 29

Explosions are not data.

---

### Rule 30

Repeated explosions are especially not data.

---

### Rule 31

The statement:

> "That was supposed to happen."

must be verified independently.

---

### Rule 32

The statement:

> "That was definitely supposed to happen."

requires immediate investigation.

---

### Rule 33

Neko must inform nearby residents before performing unusual experiments.

---

### Rule 34

The phrase:

> "Don't worry about it."

is not considered notification.

---

### Rule 35

The phrase:

> "You'll see."

is not considered notification.

---

### Rule 36

The phrase:

> "Trust the process."

is specifically prohibited.

---

### Rule 37

Star may not assist with safety inspections.

---

### Rule 38

Star may not define safety.

---

### Rule 39

Star may not redefine safety.

---

### Rule 40

Star may not challenge safety regulations to combat.

---

## **VII. EMERGENCY PROCEDURES**

In the event of:

- Power failure.
    
- Electromagnetic anomaly.
    
- Unexplained glowing.
    
- Unexpected chemical reactions.
    
- Small explosions.
    
- Large explosions.
    
- Neko saying "Oops."
    

all laboratory activities are to cease immediately.

---

### Additional Clause

If Neko says:

> "Interesting."

during an emergency—

the emergency is presumed to be worse than initially estimated.

---

## **VIII. SPECIAL CLAUSE — MIOSIS EVENT**

Due to repeated misunderstandings:

---

If Neko's pupils suddenly constrict, personnel should first determine whether:

- He is angry.
    
- He is annoyed.
    
- He is extremely comfortable.
    
- He was exposed to bright light.
    
- He is attempting to make people leave.
    

---

Personnel are advised not to panic immediately.

---

Unless he is holding chemicals.
