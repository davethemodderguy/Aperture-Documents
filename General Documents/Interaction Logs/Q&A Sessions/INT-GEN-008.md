### I. SESSION RECORD

---

**Researcher:** So Bao, why did you pick a name like that?

**Bao:** Being the only Starladian who knows Vietnamese, “Bão” means “storm” in Vietnamese—a storm that can calm itself, a storm that's all alone by itself.

---

**Researcher:** Internal testing shows that you used to be a whale before you are… this. Can you elaborate?

**Bao:** I wandered for years, seeking the last of my species. They all died horrifically, so Nature gave me this form—to spread the love

---

**███████:** Bao, rumors say you are the first person to discover the Abyssal Express. What do you say?

**Bao:** The Abyssal Express—or the Starlands Express of the Afterlife… originally, I wasn’t recognized when I first got this form. I was lost. I was alone. I swam endlessly through clouds trying to find purpose, then they find me, gave me purpose, that's all

---

**████:** Can you explain your bendings?

**Bao:** I control the clouds because that is what I chose. Clouds are vast, infinite, and calm. Water is fluid, smooth, and grounded. That’s why

**████:** So affinities are chosen?

**Bao:** Of course it is


---

**Senior Researcher:** You described your previous life as a whale and your current form as a gift from nature. Do you still feel connected to your original form, or has that identity faded?

**Bao:** It hasn’t faded—it changed. I still remember the ocean, the pressure, the sound of nothing but currents. That doesn’t go away. But I’m not bound to it anymore. Back then, I was searching for survival. Now, I’m searching for meaning. It’s the same instinct—just… redirected

---

**Environmental Analyst:** Your control over clouds suggests large-scale atmospheric influence. Are there limits to how much you can manipulate at once?

**Bao:** There are always limits. I can guide clouds, shape them, move them—but I don’t create them from nothing. The larger the scale, the more focus it takes. If I push too far, things become unstable. Storms don’t like being forced—they like being understood

**Researcher:** So loss of control is possible?

**Bao:** Not loss—misalignment

---

**Psychologist:** You’ve experienced isolation on a massive scale—both as a whale and after your transformation. How has that affected how you interact with others now?

**Bao:** It made me patient. When you’ve spent years alone, you don’t rush connections. You value them. I don’t take people for granted—because I know what it feels like to have no one at all

**Psychologist:** Does that make you more protective?

**Bao:** Yes

---

**Archivist:** When you first encountered the Abyssal Express, what did you think it was?

**Bao:** At first, I didn’t understand it. It wasn’t something you “find”—it’s something you _arrive at_. I thought I was hallucinating. A structure in the clouds, moving like it had purpose. Then I realized… it wasn’t for the living. Not entirely

**Archivist:** And yet you stayed?

**Bao:** Because I finally wasn’t alone

---

**Containment Specialist:** If required, could you weaponize your cloud manipulation on a large scale within the Blacksite?

**Bao:** I could—but I won’t unless necessary. Inside a facility like this, it would cause more damage than control. Visibility loss, pressure changes, electrical interference… it wouldn’t be clean

**Security Officer:** So last resort only?

**Bao:** Exactly

---

**Diplomatic Officer:** You’ve described your role as “spreading love.” In practical terms, what does that mean?

**Bao:** It means helping those who are lost find direction. Calming those who are overwhelmed. Being there when someone feels like they have nothing left. I’m not here to fight—I’m here to balance

**Diplomatic Officer:** And if balance fails?

**Bao:** Then I adapt

---

**Engineer:** Do your abilities interact with other elemental users, like Frost or Cinder?

**Bao:** Yes. Frost stabilizes my clouds, makes them denser. Cinder disrupts them—heat breaks structure. But we adjust. It’s not conflict—it’s interaction

**Engineer:** So synergy is possible?

**Bao:** Very

---

**Medical Staff:** Do you require sustenance in your current form? Food, water, anything similar?

**Bao:** Not in the way you do. I sustain through energy—environmental, emotional, natural. Being around others helps more than anything physical

**Medical Staff:** So isolation weakens you?

**Bao:** Slowly, yes

---

**Senior Observer:** You said you chose clouds because they are calm. Does that mean you avoid more aggressive forms of power intentionally?

**Bao:** Yes. I could’ve chosen something more destructive. Storms, lightning, pressure. But that’s not what I wanted to become. I’ve seen enough destruction already

**Observer:** So your power reflects your choice?

**Bao:** Always

---

**Archivist:** If you were to meet another of your kind—another being who was once like you—what would you say to them?

**Bao:** I’d ask them if they’re still searching. And if they are… I’d tell them they don’t have to do it alone anymore

---

- Acts as stabilizing presence among high-risk entities

she makes sure no one else has to be.
