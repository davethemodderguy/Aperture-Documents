### I. SESSION RECORD

---

**Archivist:** Do you think any more of you will request permanent or temporary residence at the Blacksite?

**Charlie:** Depends, the zone is a warfare right now due to some internal conflicts. And no, if Aperture Industries intervene then this will go very downhill from here

**Bao:** Yes, it's pretty dangerous, so we'll have to expect more people seeking shelter here, like my daughter

**Frost:** Yeah mom, you don't know how much struggle I have to deal with in the front lines _flexes her Frostbound scythe_

---

**Scientist:** So Frost, you seem to wear too much clothing. According to your document, you'll die if your body temperature goes too high. What do you have to say?

**Frost:** True, however, this hoodie and this oversized scarf is made with Frost-woven silk, which in opposite to your normal clothes, will withdraw internal temperature _summons ice into a cup of water and takes a sip_. Like my trusty scythe here, anyone that touches this will very surely turn into an ice statue

**Bao:** She's physically cold, but not emotionally

---

**Security Guard:** So Frost, any concerns about you in close proximity?

**Frost:** Just see me like a walking ice cube, nothing more

**Bao:** And an AC Unit

**Frost:** And an AC unit

---

**Maintenance Worker:** So you and Cinder have contrasting elements, being fire and ice, any difficulties staying near him?

**Frost:** Not quite, his heat is easily countered by my frost, and surprisingly, my frost aura doesn't affect him that much thanks to my thick clothes. So yes, no “I touch him and he dies”, we just get along very much, though he has a tendency of melting my ice sculptures

---

**Star:** Do you like the cold?

**Frost:** Of course I do!

**Star:** Not a big fan, but I like the kind of cold around you

**Frost:** You can also be cold you know

**Star:** How did you—

**Frost:** Red the manual

---

**Armory Manager:** What's your reaction when knowing that Stitch is the Founder of the Starlands Blacksmith Alliance?

**Frost:** Pretty interesting, been a big fan of their work you know

**Stitch:** Yeah that's hard to hide sometimes, the other Celetisynths are also made by me and the other great Blacksmiths in the Alliance. Quite interesting concepts, overall everyone did a very great job


---

**Researcher:** Frost, what happens if your body temperature actually rises past the limit?

**Frost:** Then I die. Not dramatically, not explosively—just shutdown. My body isn’t built to handle heat, it rejects it

**Bao:** Which is why she’s layered like that all the time

---

**Containment Officer:** Can your frost spread unintentionally inside the facility?

**Frost:** If I lose control, yes. Temperature drops fast, surfaces freeze, systems might start failing

**Charlie:** Which is why she doesn’t lose control

---

**Engineer:** That scythe—does it generate the frost, or is it just a focus?

**Frost:** Both. It amplifies what I already have. Without it, I’m still cold. With it… things get worse for anything around me

**Stitch:** Designed efficiency. Minimal waste, maximum effect

---

**Medical Staff:** Do you feel cold the same way others do?

**Frost:** No. This is normal for me. What you call freezing is just… comfortable

**Star:** That explains a lot
