### III. EXTENDED ENTRIES

---

**Senior Researcher:** You stated your head was severed during a failed spell, yet you continue to function without observable degradation. Can you explain what “muscle memory” means in this context?

**Dusekkar:** It’s not your kind of muscle memory. Not repetition, not training. It’s… continuity. My body remembers being alive, so it continues acting like it is. The spell failed, but something persisted—something that refused to stop. My head is no longer required for the body to function, but it still exists… because something decided it should. That’s all you need to know

---

**Containment Specialist:** If your head and body are separated further, can they operate independently or become hostile?

**Dusekkar:** They won’t turn on each other, if that’s what you’re asking. But separation makes things… unstable. Not dangerous in a loud way—dangerous in a quiet way. The kind you don’t notice until it’s already happening

---

**Psychologist:** You show protective behavior toward Cinder. Would you say you see him as family, or as responsibility?

**Dusekkar:** Both. He was made into something he didn’t choose to be. That alone makes him worth protecting. But over time… it stopped being just responsibility. He’s not a weapon to me. He’s someone who deserved better from the beginning

---

**Diplomatic Analyst:** Your stance on the Commission suggests distrust. If given the opportunity, would you act against them directly?

**Dusekkar:** If I had proof, yes. Not out of revenge—out of correction. What they did wasn’t just wrong, it was intentional. You don’t create something like Cinder by accident. You plan it. You refine it. And then you justify it. That’s what I have a problem with

---

**Arcadian Observer:** Do you believe the Commission’s actions were isolated, or part of a larger system?

**Dusekkar:** Nothing that precise is isolated. There’s always a system behind it—layers of approval, silence, and denial. Even if only a few were directly involved, others knew. They always do

---

**Senior Archivist:** You react with immediate lethal force to certain types of disrespect. Is this emotional, instinctive, or intentional?

**Dusekkar:** Intentional. I choose when to respond. That question earlier? That wasn’t curiosity—it was mockery. I don’t tolerate that. Not here, not anywhere
