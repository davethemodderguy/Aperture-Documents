### I. SESSION RECORD

---

**Biochemist:** So Stitch, how does your body really work? You’ve been a very interesting specimen since the discovery of Z-████.

**Stitch:** Quite a simple concept, the cells in my left part were, well, damaged and killed in the blast, so Charlie used a special kind of magic tether to trick said cells into thinking they’re alive, and they just act like normal. Of course, with the added bonus of being able to fit into tight places by taking some limbs off _detaches left arm_

**Biochemist:** Isn’t that painful?

**Stitch:** Not really, just a slight itch, that’s all

---

**████:** Stitch, how did you get your current name?

**Stitch:** Well, I used to be called André thanks to my French accent, but because the explosion did have some effect on my voice—making it randomly either feminine or masculine—so thanks to the stitches on my body, I was given a new name, Stitch!

**████:** French accent?

**Stitch:** Hey, we are not in the dark, we know Earth and most of its cultures, mostly through Urbanshade when they invaded us

---

**HR Manager:** How, when, and why were you chosen to be a blacksmith?

**Stitch:** Simple, that’s just something I randomly got fond of. Besides, I seem to be very good at it, and the pieces I make are just masterpieces

**HR Manager:** Do you have any difficulties doing your blacksmith duties in your current state?

**Stitch:** Not at all

---

**Engineer:** So how does Cinder assist you with your job?

**Stitch:** Well, he heats some low-temperature metals up so I don’t have to use the furnace. He lets me cut corners on fuel. Besides, these metals have specialized traits that even Daniel doesn’t know why or how they work


---

**Researcher:** When you detach parts of your body, do they remain active or functional?

**Stitch:** Depends. They won’t move on their own, but they’re still… connected. Think of it like pausing a function, not shutting it down

---

**Security Officer:** Can your body be forcibly disassembled by external force?

**Stitch:** Technically yes. Practically? Not recommended. The tether doesn’t like being interrupted

**Charlie:** It reacts

---

**Medical Staff:** Does your body regenerate naturally, or does it rely entirely on the tether?

**Stitch:** The tether does most of the work. Without it, half of me wouldn’t even be here talking to you

---

**Scientist:** Your voice instability—does it affect anything beyond sound?

**Stitch:** No, just the voice. Think of it as… a side effect. Doesn’t change who I am, just how I sound

---

**Armory Specialist:** The weapons you forge—are they influenced by your condition?

**Stitch:** Of course. Everything I make carries a bit of how I work. Unstable, adaptive, and very hard to break
