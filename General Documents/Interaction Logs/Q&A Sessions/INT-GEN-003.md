### I. SESSION RECORD

---

**███████:** Star, do you describe yourself as emotionally sensitive?

**Star:** Of course I am. Do remember that one time I froze my room because Stitch was in a coma, or that time when I got backstabbed and got depressed and all

**███████:** Can you control said states?

**Star:** Very depends

---

**EXR-P:** You are rumored to have some kind of parental issue, what do you say about that?

**Star:** _shoots the EXR-P dead_ That’s out of the questions

---

**████:** Can you name all your “emotional spectrum” forms?

**Star:** Rage, Anger, Grief, Sadness, Betrayed, Joy, Hyperactive, Sloth, Annoyed, Depressed, and Fury

**████:** Are these forms a threat to the Blacksite?

**Star:** Only to those who triggered it

---

**██████:** It is very fascinating that you, Stitch, and Cinder have cat traits and forms. What do you think about this?

**Star:** I don’t know how and why we got those forms, but we like it. The enhanced reflexes, vision, and hearing just make us so efficient. 

Director Lucious looked at her with a slightly tearing eye, avoiding from being seen by Star, only Director Lucious knows why they’re like that

---

**Maintenance Worker:** Can you explain why you always retreat into the vents if you are emotionally attacked?

**Star:** It’s comfy, no one can touch me there. But can you please make the fans less sharp? I got grazed so many times already

**System Response:** Request denied — Intentional by Design

---

**Psychologist:** Star, do your emotional states change your abilities or just your behavior?

**Star:** Both. Some make me faster, some make me colder—literally. Some just make me not want to move at all

---

**Security Officer:** In a worst-case scenario, which state is the most dangerous?

**Star:** Fury. No control, just reaction

**Stitch:** Confirmed

---

**Researcher:** Do you remember everything that happens during those states?

**Star:** Yes. That’s the worst part

---

**Containment Officer:** Is there a way to calm you down once a state is triggered?

**Star:** Depends who’s trying. Some people can, some people make it worse just by being there

---

**Engineer:** Why the vents specifically? Not your room or another safe zone?

**Star:** Vents are quiet. No people. No noise. Just… me

---

**Medical Staff:** Have your emotional states become more frequent over time?

**Star:** Yeah. A bit.

**Bao (off-record):** We’re working on it

---

**Archivist:** If you could remove one emotional state, which one would it be?

**Star:** …Depressed, that's just like pouring oil into a fire

**Archivist:** That's actually sad to hear...

**Star:** I know, don't have to put extra salt on a salty dish...
