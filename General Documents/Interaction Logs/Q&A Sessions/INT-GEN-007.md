### I. SESSION RECORD

---

**Bioresearcher:** So Viridis, how exactly do your powers work?

**Viridis:** I have an affinity for nature. Nature follows my lead—it’s that simple

**Bioresearcher:** Which means you command nature?

**Viridis:** No. I don’t command. I guide

---

**██████:** Viridis, why are you in this ghost form?

**Viridis:** I fell in battle, but nature didn’t let me die, so I go on

**██████:** Is this tiring or annoying?

**Viridis:** I was given a second chance not by accident—it’s intentional. I treat it as a gift

---

**████:** Any comments on Star’s new form?

**Viridis:** She injected herself with my essence, which means she now possesses my power

**████:** Any side effects?

**Viridis:** That’s something out of my control

---

**███:** What’s the battle that you mentioned earlier?

**Viridis:** It’s the Great Starladian War. Two gods fight over two opinions, and we fight for the greater cause—me, Curious Light, Guiding Light, Crecentious

**███:** Who are these people?

**Viridis:** I can’t remember


---

**Senior Biologist:** When you say nature “follows your lead,” what exactly is happening on a deeper level? Are you influencing growth, behavior, or something more fundamental?

**Viridis:** It’s not influence in the way you understand it. I don’t force change—I align with it. Every living thing already has a direction, a purpose, a flow. I simply… make that flow clearer. Plants grow faster not because I command them, but because I remove hesitation. Roots find water because I help them “decide” where to go. It’s cooperation, not control

---

**Containment Specialist:** Your current form appears non-corporeal. Are you bound to a location, an object, or a concept?

**Viridis:** None of those, and all of them. I’m not tied to a single place, but I cannot exist where nature has no presence. Completely artificial environments weaken me. I don’t disappear—I just become… distant. Faint

---

**Medical Analyst:** Regarding Star’s injection of your essence—what exactly did she take from you? Power? Identity? Something else?

**Viridis:** Power, primarily. But essence is never just power. It carries fragments—instincts, tendencies, echoes. She didn’t become me, but she now carries something that remembers being me. That can shape how it behaves

---

**Psychological Researcher:** You’ve lost memory of key figures like Curious Light and the others. Do you believe this loss is natural or caused by something?

**Viridis:** It doesn’t feel natural. It feels… removed. Like something was taken, not forgotten. I remember that they mattered. I remember fighting beside them. But their faces, their voices—gone. That kind of absence doesn’t just happen

---

**Arcadian Liaison:** The war you described—two gods fighting over “opinions.” That seems… simplified. What were those opinions?

**Viridis:** That’s the problem. I don’t remember clearly enough to explain it. But I know it wasn’t small. When gods fight, “opinions” stop being ideas—they become reality. Entire regions changed because of it. What they believed reshaped everything around them

---

**Environmental Specialist:** If placed in a completely controlled artificial biome, could you restore natural balance within it?

**Viridis:** Yes—but it would take time. Artificial systems resist change because they’re designed to. I’d have to slowly introduce imbalance… then guide it back into something real

---

**Security Analyst:** In a hostile situation, can you weaponize nature directly?

**Viridis:** Yes. Roots can restrain, vines can crush, environments can shift. But again—I guide. I don’t force. The stronger the environment, the stronger I am

---

**Archivist:** Do you fear losing more of your memories over time?

**Viridis:** Not fear. Concern. Memories define continuity. If I lose too much, I won’t know why I’m still here

---

**Philosophical Inquiry Unit:** You describe your second existence as a “gift.” Do you believe you were brought back for a purpose?

**Viridis:** Yes. Nature doesn’t waste effort. If I’m still here, then something still needs to be done

---

**Senior Observer:** If the war you mentioned reaches this world, what role would you take?

**Viridis:** The same as before. I won’t lead armies or choose sides blindly. I’ll protect what needs to survive. That’s always been my role—even if I can’t remember everything else

---

### III. NOTES

- Subject operates on cooperative interaction with natural systems, not dominance
- Non-corporeal state tied to presence of “nature”
- Memory degradation appears abnormal and possibly external in origin
- Direct connection to Star introduces potential behavioral and power overlap
- Historical involvement in large-scale conflict confirmed but partially inaccessible

### IV. FINAL NOTE

Viridis doesn’t control nature.

Nature moves with her—

even when she no longer remembers why.
