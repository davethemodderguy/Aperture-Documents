### I. SESSION RECORD

---

**█████████:** So Cinder, how does your body actually work?

**Cinder:** Simple, I have high internal temperature. I use said temperature to fire-bend. If that’s too low then I collapse and die. Pretty simple concept

**█████████:** Does Bao and Frost actively pose a danger near you?

**Cinder:** If only they want to kill me, which they don’t. Otherwise we’re just great friends

---

**████:** What are your opinions about the Banlands?

**Cinder:** Pretty much my heaven. Hot, heat, and massive rivers of lava. Quite somewhere I can enjoy—but others don’t

**████:** Do you plan to work with the Devil?

**Cinder:** I’ll do The Contractor’s tasks. Otherwise, I don’t

---

**Engineer:** Any concerns with your heat?

**Cinder:** Not much, don’t worry


---

**Researcher:** What happens if your internal temperature rises too high?

**Cinder:** Then things around me start melting before I do. There’s a limit—but you’ll notice the environment failing first

---

**Security Officer:** Are you capable of unintentionally causing structural damage?

**Cinder:** Yeah. Metal softens, systems overheat. I try not to stand still too long in one place

---

**Maintenance Worker:** Do cooling systems in the facility affect you?

**Cinder:** They slow me down a bit. Nothing serious, just… uncomfortable

---

**Scientist:** How do you generate fire? Is it chemical, biological, or something else?

**Cinder:** It’s me. No fuel, no ignition—just heat turned into output

---

**Medical Staff:** Do you feel pain from your own heat?

**Cinder:** No. This is normal for me

---

**Armory Specialist:** Can your flames be contained or redirected?

**Cinder:** Contained? Maybe. Redirected? Only if I let you

---

**Containment Officer:** In a hostile scenario, how quickly can your temperature escalate?

**Cinder:** Fast enough that you won’t want to measure it twice

---

**Archivist:** Between you, Frost, and Star—who’s the most dangerous?

**Cinder:** Depends on the situation.

**Cinder (pause):** But if Star loses control… yeah, not me

---

**HR Manager:** Do you prefer working alone or with others?

**Cinder:** With others. Heat’s better when shared
