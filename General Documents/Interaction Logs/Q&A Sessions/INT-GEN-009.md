### I. SESSION RECORD

---

**Head Abnormality Inspection Manager:** How did you get your appearance?

**Candleheart:** I used to really like this anime—the main protagonist’s form is what I’m obsessed with the most, so I guess my dreams came true?

---

**████:** Candleheart, how are you so positive?

**Candleheart:** I love helping others! They make me happy :D

**████:** But you do know that the people you didn’t help turned you in, right?

**Candleheart:** It’s coming either way, so… I helped others by staying here?

---

**█████████:** Did you have any relationships before coming here?

**Candleheart:** I’m invisible when I help others. I helped invisibly, so no, I don’t

**█████████:** So you help as a ghost?

**Candleheart:** _fades away lightly_ Yes

---

**█████████████:** Do you partake in any particular duties around here?

**Candleheart:** I do everything, so no, I don’t

**█████████████:** How much does your knowledge scale?

**Candleheart:** Almost infinite

---

**████:** Why magenta?

**Candleheart:** Because I like it! Though I can shift into other colors, I love magenta _shifts into white_

---

**██████:** Do you like your name?

**Candleheart:** Yes! A good combination of my head and my necklace. Thanks, Star!

**Star:** You’re welcome


---

**Senior Researcher:** You claim to have “almost infinite” knowledge. Can you clarify what that actually means? Are there limits to what you know or understand?

**Candleheart:** I don’t know everything all at once. It’s more like… if something exists, I can _reach_ the knowledge if I try. Some things are harder to access than others, especially if they’re hidden or blocked. But if it’s out there, I can usually find it eventually

**Researcher:** So it’s retrieval, not constant awareness?

**Candleheart:** Exactly!

---

**Psychologist:** Your positivity remains consistent even under negative circumstances. Is this a conscious choice, or part of your nature?

**Candleheart:** Both! I _choose_ to stay positive, but it’s also just how I feel. Helping people feels good, so I keep doing it. Even if things go wrong, I don’t see a reason to stop

**Psychologist:** You don’t feel resentment?

**Candleheart:** Not really

---

**Containment Specialist:** Your ability to become invisible and intangible—are there limits? Can you remain undetected indefinitely?

**Candleheart:** I can stay invisible for a long time, but not forever if I’m actively doing things. The more I interact with the world, the easier it is to notice me

**Specialist:** So total non-interaction equals full invisibility?

**Candleheart:** Pretty much

---

**Security Analyst:** If you can access knowledge and remain unseen, what prevents you from becoming a security risk?

**Candleheart:** I don’t want to harm anyone. That’s it

**Security Analyst:** That’s a preference, not a limitation

**Candleheart:** Then I guess you’ll just have to trust me

---

**Archivist:** You said you helped people “invisibly” before arriving here. Why stay hidden instead of being recognized?

**Candleheart:** Because it’s not about me. If they’re safe and happy, that’s enough. Being seen doesn’t make helping better

**Archivist:** And being forgotten doesn’t bother you?

**Candleheart:** Not at all

---

**Energy Specialist:** Your color shifting—does it represent emotional state, power level, or is it purely aesthetic?

**Candleheart:** Mostly aesthetic! But sometimes it reflects how I feel a little. Magenta is just… me

**Specialist:** Can it affect your abilities?

**Candleheart:** A tiny bit, but not much

---

**Senior Observer:** If you truly have near-limitless knowledge and the ability to remain unseen, why choose a support role instead of a leadership position?

**Candleheart:** Because leaders get noticed. And noticed people can’t help quietly. I like being where I’m needed—not where I’m seen

**Observer:** So you intentionally avoid influence?

**Candleheart:** I guide, not lead
