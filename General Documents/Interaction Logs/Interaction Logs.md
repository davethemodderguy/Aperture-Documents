**APERTURE INDUSTRIES**  
**INTERACTION LOG ARCHIVE — INTRODUCTION**  
**Classification: Internal**

---

### I. PURPOSE

The **Interaction Log Archive** is a GENERAL-class documentation channel created to record **Q&A sessions, interviews, and direct personnel interactions** with notable individuals or entities.

These logs exist to:

- Capture unfiltered responses
- Clarify anomalies through direct questioning
- Document behavior, personality, and knowledge

---

### II. ORIGIN

This archive format was established following the arrival of **Frost**, whose presence generated widespread questions among personnel.

With authorization from **Director Lucious**, a controlled Q&A session was conducted.

Due to the nature of these interactions spanning both **C.O.R.E** and **N.O.S.T** subjects, the archive is classified under **GENERAL** for accessibility and neutrality.

---

### III. STRUCTURE

Each Interaction Log is organized as:

- **One Event → One Log**
- Each log may contain **multiple sessions**
- Each session includes a **full set of questions and answers**

Sessions are:

- Documented in sequence
- Linked to relevant entity or event records
- Preserved without alteration unless required for security

---

### IV. CONTENT TYPE

Interaction Logs include:

- Personnel questions
- Subject responses
- Behavioral observations
- Notable reactions or inconsistencies

Unlike Event Logs:

- These are **dialogue-based**
- Focused on **information exchange**, not incidents

---

### V. CLASSIFICATION

Although stored under **GENERAL**, each session may relate to:

- **C.O.R.E subjects** → containment, research, internal systems
- **N.O.S.T subjects** → non-Earth entities, realms, or phenomena

Classification is assigned per session, not per archive.

---

### VI. USAGE

These logs are used for:

- Intelligence gathering
- Behavioral analysis
- Cross-referencing entity knowledge
- Training and briefing personnel

---

### VII. LIMITATIONS

- Subjects may lie, omit, or distort information
- Some answers may be incomplete or intentionally vague
- Interpretation is required for accuracy

---

### VIII. FINAL NOTE

Not everything needs containment.

Sometimes—

you just need to ask the right question.
