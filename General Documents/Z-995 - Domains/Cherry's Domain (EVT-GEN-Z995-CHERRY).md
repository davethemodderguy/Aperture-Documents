### I. DOMAIN SUMMARY

Cherry’s Domain, codenamed **“Cherry’s Dream,”** manifests as an **infinite cherry blossom forest**, fully saturated in soft pink tones.

The environment is calm, gentle, and consistently stable.  
Petals drift endlessly through the air, carried by a light, warm breeze.

Ambient conditions induce:

- Emotional relaxation
    
- Psychological stabilization
    
- Reduction in stress, anxiety, and trauma responses
    

Unlike most Domains, **Cherry’s Dream exhibits passive healing properties**, primarily targeting the **mental and emotional state** of individuals within it.
