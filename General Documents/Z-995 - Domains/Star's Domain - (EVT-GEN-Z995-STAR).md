### I. EVENT SUMMARY

Authorization was granted by Director Lucious under controlled observation.

---

### II. EVENT RECORD

Star didn’t stand.

She sat.

Right in the middle of the room—

legs crossed, posture relaxed.

Excited.

---

**Star:**  
“Okay okay—watch this.”

---

She crossed her hands.

Not precise like Charlie.

Not controlled.

But natural.

---

> **“Domain Expansion: Infinite Starry Skies.”**

---

An azure light burst outward—

brighter than expected.

Less refined.

But wider.

---

It didn’t just _cover_ the room.

It **consumed** it.

---

When the light faded—

there was no floor.

No walls.

No ceiling.

---

Only—

**space.**

---

Endless.

Silent.

Vast beyond comprehension.

---

Stars stretched infinitely in every direction—dense clusters, distant galaxies, slow-moving cosmic dust. Light shimmered across nothingness, forming patterns that didn’t obey constellations known to humanity.

There was no ground to stand on—

yet they stood.

---

“…This is… beautiful.”

---

Director Lucious said nothing.

His eyes scanned the horizon—

if it could even be called that.

---

**Director Lucious:**  
“Star?”

---

No response.

---

They looked around.

No sign of her.

---

Then—

a flicker.

---

One of the stars pulsed brighter.

Then another.

Then dozens.

---

The light began to **gather**.

---

And slowly—

a figure formed.

---

Star.

---

But not the same.

---

Her body wasn’t physical.

Not entirely.

She was composed of **pure azure light**, her outline shimmering like a constellation barely holding itself together.

Her hair flowed like cosmic trails.

Her eyes—

were stars.

---

She floated—

not above them—

but **everywhere around them at once**.

---

**Star (voice echoing softly):**  
“Do you like it?”

---

Her voice didn’t come from one direction.

It came from all directions.

Layered.

Gentle.

---

**Linda:**  
“Star… is that you?”

---

**Star:**  
“Yep!”

---

She spun once—

and the entire space responded.

Stars shifted.

Galaxies rotated.

Light bent around her movement like she was the center of it all.

---

**Director Lucious:**  
“You’re not just in the Domain.”

---

A pause.

---

“You _are_ the Domain.”

---

Star tilted her head.

---

“…Yeah. I think so.”

---

She raised her hand—

and a cluster of stars condensed into a small glowing sphere.

She tossed it lightly.

It drifted past them—

before expanding back into a full constellation.

---

**Star:**  
“I can make anything here. Or nothing.”

---

She paused.

---

“Wanna see something cool?”

---

Before a response—

the stars dimmed.

---

Darkness.

Total.

---

Then—

memories.

---

Not spoken.

Not projected.

But **felt**.

---

Fragments of emotions drifted through the void:

- Warmth
- Loneliness
- Fear
- Joy
- Grief

Each one tied to a faint light.

Each light—

a star.

---

**Star (quietly):**  
“Every star here… is something I felt.”

---

Another pause.

---

“Or something I couldn’t let go.”

---

The space shifted again.

Faster this time.

Less stable.

---

Stars began flickering.

Some brightened violently.

Others dimmed almost to nothing.

---

**Director Lucious (firm):**  
“Star.”

---

She froze.

---

The stars stabilized.

---

Her form flickered—

just slightly.

---

**Star:**  
“…Still working on that part.”

---

She drifted closer—

her light softening.

---

“I don’t control it like Charlie does.”

---

A small pause.

---

“I _feel_ it.”

---

Silence.

But not empty.

---

Full.

---

Then—

she clapped her hands once.

---

Light returned.

---

And just like that—

they were back in the meeting room.

---

Star was back on the floor.

Same position.

Same energy.

---

**Star:**  
“…So?”

---
