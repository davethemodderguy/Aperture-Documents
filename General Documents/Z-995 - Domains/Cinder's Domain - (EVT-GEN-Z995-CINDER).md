### II. EVENT RECORD

Cinder didn’t hesitate.

No buildup.

No theatrics.

---

He crossed his fingers—

firm.

Controlled.

---

> **“Domain Expansion: Everlasting Flame of Etheros.”**

---

A burst of **bright orange light** consumed the room.

Not violent—

but intense.

---

Then—

silence.

---

When vision returned—

everything changed.

---

No flames.

No heat.

No destruction.

---

Just—

a **garden**.

---

Small.

Quiet.

Contained.

---

Soft soil beneath their feet.

Gentle wind brushing through barely-there grass.

A few scattered plants—healthy, but not excessive.

At the center—

a **stone statue**.

---

Dusekkar.

---

Perfectly carved.

Still.

Watching.

---

No movement.

No energy emission.

Just presence.

---

Cinder stood there.

Hands in pockets.

Looking… different.

---

Not weaker.

Just—

quieter.

---

**Cinder:**  
“…Yeah.”

---

A pause.

---

“I don’t have much here.”

---

He glanced at the statue.

---

“This is my sanctuary.”

---

Another pause.

---

“I guess this is it.”

---

Silence followed.

---

Not uncomfortable.

Not tense.

---

Just—

still.

---

**Stitch** looked around.

Then at Cinder.

Then back at the statue.

---

“…You could’ve made anything.”

---

**Cinder:**  
“I did.”

---

**The Contractor** stepped closer to the statue.

Studied it.

---

“No fire.”

---

**Cinder:**  
“Don’t need it here.”

---

Director Lucious didn’t move.

His eyes stayed on the statue.

---

“Why her?”

---

Cinder didn’t answer immediately.

---

The wind shifted slightly.

---

“…Because this is the only place where she’s not in danger.”

---

Silence.

---

Even The Contractor didn’t respond.

---

The garden didn’t change.

Didn’t react.

Didn’t shift like the others.

---

It stayed—

exactly the same.

---

Stable.

---

**Stitch (quietly):**  
“…This isn’t a Domain for fighting.”

---

**Cinder:**  
“No.”

---

“…It’s where I don’t have to.”

---

A longer pause.

---

Then—

Cinder snapped his fingers.

---

The orange light returned.

---

And just like that—

they were back.

---

Same room.

Same positions.

---

No residual heat.

No damage.

---

Just—

silence.
