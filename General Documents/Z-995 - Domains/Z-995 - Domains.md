### I. OVERVIEW

Z-995, designated **“Domains,”** refers to a universal anomalous phenomenon in which every individual possesses a **personalized pocket reality** intrinsically tethered to their existence.

These Domains operate as **self-contained environments** where the owner exerts **near-absolute authority** over structure, physics, and internal systems. Manifestations vary widely, ranging from controlled functional spaces to deeply personal environments shaped by memory, emotion, or identity.

Domains are **not exclusive** to any single classification.  
All entities—human, anomalous, administrative, or otherwise—are believed to possess one.

**Key Condition:**  
Domains must be **awakened** before access or utilization is possible.

---

### II. DISCOVERY CONTEXT

The existence of Z-995 was formally recognized following a **demonstration by Charlie** during a high-level assembly involving:

- Director Lucious
- The Dawnbringers
- Starladian Deities

All attending personnel were transported into a **controlled pocket environment** resembling a condensed Starlands construct.

Charlie identified this phenomenon as:

> “Domain Expansion”

### III. DEFINING CHARACTERISTICS

- **Personal Tethering:**  
    Each Domain is bound directly to its owner’s identity, psyche, or core essence
- **Localized Absolute Authority:**  
    Owners maintain near-total control within their Domain  
    _(Upper limits remain undefined)_
- **Reality Override:**  
    Standard physical laws may be altered, replaced, or removed entirely

    No two Domains are identical

    All entities are theorized to possess a dormant Domain
- **Awakening Requirement:**  
    Domains require activation through known or unknown triggers
