### II. EVENT RECORD

Charlie stood at the center of the room, posture steady.

She raised her hands—

fingers crossing into a shape resembling a **Christian cross**.

A quiet breath.

Then a single phrase:

> **“Domain Expansion: Shining Star.”**

A faint, golden _snap_ echoed—soft, but absolute.

Light followed.

Not explosive—

but overwhelming.

It swallowed the room whole.

---

When vision returned—

the Blacksite was gone.

---

They stood within a **miniaturized Starlands**.

Endless skies stretched outward, painted in soft gradients of gold and blue. Floating landmasses drifted lazily in the distance. Light bent unnaturally, like it was being _guided_ rather than emitted.

Everything felt—

calm.

Not artificially calm.

**Naturally calm.**

---

**The Contractor:**  
“This is… oddly calming.”

---

**Director Lucious:**  
“Well. Now I have more documents to write about.”

---

Charlie smiled faintly.

With a small motion of her hand—

the sky shifted.

Day became dusk.

Dusk became night.

Stars formed, aligned, then dissolved into sunrise again—each transition seamless, instantaneous, and **effortless**.

Wind followed her gestures.

Clouds parted when she glanced at them.

Even gravity felt… optional.

---

**Candleheart:**  
_glowing slightly brighter_  
“It feels like the place _wants_ us here.”

---

**Charlie:**  
“It does.”

---

She paused.

Then tilted her head slightly.

---

“Want to see a magic trick?”

---

A nod from everyone.

Even The Contractor.

---

Charlie crossed her fingers again.

This time—

slower.

Deliberate.

---

> **“Domain Ability: Divine Judgment.”**

---

The light returned.

Brighter than before.

Sharper.

Less comforting.

---

When it faded—

the world had changed.

---

They now stood inside a **courtroom**.

But not a normal one.

---

The architecture was impossibly tall—pillars stretching beyond visible limits. The walls shimmered like stained glass, yet displayed no images—only shifting light.

At the center stood a **judge’s platform**—

elevated far above.

And seated upon it—

was Charlie.

---

Different.

---

Her wings extended wider than before.

Her presence heavier.

Not oppressive—

but **absolute**.

---

**Charlie:**  
“Surprise. I’m secretly working as a lawyer all this time!”

---

Star blinked.

---

**Star:**  
“…You’re kidding.”

---

**Charlie:**  
“Nope.”

---

She raised a hand.

And the room responded.

---

Seats formed instantly behind each individual—adjusted perfectly to their size and position.

Documents appeared mid-air—blank at first, then rapidly filling with text.

Names.

Actions.

Histories.

---

**Director Lucious** narrowed his eyes.

---

“Those records—”

---

**Charlie (interrupting, calm):**  
“Everything inside a Domain can be defined. Including truth.”

---

A pause.

---

**The Contractor:**  
“So this isn’t just for show.”

---

Charlie shook her head.

---

“No. This is where judgment happens.”

---

She tapped the platform once.

The sound echoed—

not through space,

but through **something deeper**.

---

A silhouette appeared in the center of the courtroom.

Featureless.

Undefined.

Yet it _felt_ like something.

---

**Charlie:**  
“In here, I can summon representations. Not the real person—just the truth of them.”

---

The silhouette shifted.

Fragments of events flickered around it—moments, actions, decisions.

Good.

Bad.

Everything in between.

---

**Candleheart (quietly):**  
“…That’s not just memory.”

---

**Charlie:**  
“It’s not.”

---

She leaned forward slightly.

---

“Inside this Domain, I don’t decide what’s right or wrong.”

---

A pause.

---

“The Domain does.”

---

Silence filled the courtroom.

---

Then—

Charlie snapped her fingers.

---

Everything vanished.

---

Light again.

---

And just like that—

they were back in the meeting room.

---

No transition.

No distortion.

Just—

_back._
