### **Event ID:** NOST-Z356-LOG-004

**Subject(s):** Z-356-B (Bao), Z-356-F (Frost), Z-356 (Class), Z-356-C (C00lkid)  
**Location:** Starlands — Sector 4-C

---

### **EVENT SUMMARY**

Bao had already settled into permanent residence within the Blacksite, her presence becoming a quiet constant within Sector 4-C.

One day, she approached Director Lucious with a request.

> “Do you mind if I call my daughter over, she left for the Northwood War, that area near the Abyssal Express? Can she stay here, and yes, she need her own room, set it up next to mine, thanks”

The question lingered for a moment longer than expected.

For Director Lucious, this was unfamiliar territory.

A Starladian.  
With a daughter.

Without further hesitation, he accepted the request.

---

### **PREPARATIONS**

Immediately after, Director Lucious instructed **C00lkid** to prepare a room within **Sector 4-C**, specifically placing it adjacent to Bao’s.

C00lkid complied without question, rearranging and setting up the space as instructed.

Meanwhile, Bao departed back into the Starlands.

---

### **ARRIVAL**

Five hours passed.

Then, Bao returned.

But she was not alone.

---

A smaller figure stood beside her.

A child.

---

### **Z-356-F — “Frost”**

The entity introduced herself plainly, with a quiet, subdued tone:

> “Hello, my name is Frost, I'm the Goddess of Ice and Frost, and I am Bao's daughter.”

She appeared fragile at first glance.

A soft, minimalist form — pale skin, short tousled white hair, and half-lidded eyes that carried a quiet, almost sleepy expression. A faint blush rested on her cheeks.

Above her head floated a small halo, loosely tied with a white ribbon, drifting slightly as if caught in an unseen current.

Her body was wrapped in an oversized bluish scarf, layered with thick clothing beneath — garments that seemed designed more for comfort than presentation.

Behind her rested two asymmetrical wings — one bright and light, the other dark, shaded in nebula like color. Together, they gave her a soft contrast, like day and night bound into one form. Strangely enough, she describe the split as (small battle injury) and confidently said this is the color of the Starlands' night starry sky

---

### **TRANSFER**

Bao looked to her daughter.

> “Are you ready for your room?”

A quiet confirmation followed.

Without delay, Director Lucious acted.

A message was sent.

And beneath Frost’s feet—

a red portal opened.

---

### **ARRIVAL TO QUARTERS**

The transition was immediate.

Frost fell through the portal—

landing directly in front of her assigned room.

**Room 565.**

C00lkid was already there.

---

The moment she recovered, she immediately shouted:

> “HEY, YOU KNOW HOW PAINFUL IT IS?”

C00lkid responded without hesitation:

> “Uhh no you're not, the floors are padded”

---

The doors to her room slid open.

C00lkid tossed her a keycard casually.

Then continued:

> “Use the terminal on your right to buy furnitures, you earn credits, well that you have to ask the Contractor... If you have any question just come to the office, should be a glowing red door near Sector 4-C, I highly advise you to start looking around and get familiar”

With that, he vanished.

---

Frost stood there for a moment.

Then, under her breath:

> “What a weird landlord”

---

And just like that—

she stepped inside.
