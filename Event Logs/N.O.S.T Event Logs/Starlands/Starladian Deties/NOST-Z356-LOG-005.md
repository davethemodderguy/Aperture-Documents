**opened its eyes.**

Immediate reaction:

This action resulted in:

- Frost being forcefully awakened
- Rapid environmental temperature drop

---

- Targeted all present individuals

> **Frost:** “WHAT DID YOU DO TO MY MOM?”

No immediate explanation successfully delivered.

---

### **RESOLUTION**

Before escalation could proceed—

The liquid mass spoke.

> **Bao:** “Stop. This is one of my alternate forms. It helps me sleep.”

The entity then:

- Reformed
- Returned to standard humanoid state

Threat level immediately dropped.

Frost disengaged.

---
