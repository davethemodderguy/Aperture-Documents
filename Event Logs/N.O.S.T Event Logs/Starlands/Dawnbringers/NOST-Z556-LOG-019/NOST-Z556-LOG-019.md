### **EVENT SUMMARY**

The incident began as routine.

Star woke up and entered the **Sector 4-C cafeteria** as she normally would—movement energetic, presence familiar, behavior consistent with baseline.

However—

there was a difference.

---

### **INITIAL ANOMALY**

The vines covering Star’s body were **visibly wilting**.

- Loss of structural firmness
- Reduced coloration
- Slowed reactive movement

More critically—

The **Starladian rose** embedded within her right eye socket exhibited the same condition.

It, too, was wilting.

---

Upon inquiry, Star dismissed the condition.

> **Star:** “I just didn’t sleep much…”

No immediate distress was expressed.

No escalation was recorded at that time.

---

### **PROGRESSION**

Throughout the day, subtle degradation continued:

- Reduced activity levels
- Slower response time
- Noticeable instability in posture
- Vines showing progressive deterioration

Despite this, Star did not request assistance.

No intervention was initiated.

---

### **COLLAPSE EVENT**

Later that day, within **Room 150**, Star collapsed.

- Loss of consciousness confirmed
- Body became **immobile**
- Vines reacted immediately

---

### **DEFENSIVE RESPONSE (AUTONOMOUS)**

Upon collapse, Star’s vine system activated autonomously:

- Dense vine layering formed around her body
- Structure acted as a **protective barrier**
- Restricted direct physical access

Medical personnel experienced delay in intervention due to:

- Physical obstruction
- Active resistance from vine structures

Access was eventually forced.

---

### **CCTV REVIEW — ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS**

Footage review revealed the origin of the condition.

**Timestamp: Middle of the night (prior to incident)**

Star exited rest state and moved toward hydration storage.

Observations:

- Subject exhibited signs of **severe dehydration**
- Movement unstable but directed

Previous records confirm:

- Star prefers **sugar water** over standard water

---

### **CRITICAL ERROR**

While supposed to retrieve a bottle from the fridge, Star's lack of enviromental awareness while she was sleepy led to her accessed an adjacent storage unit:

- **Gardening Supplies Cabinet**

Without verification—

she consumed a contained liquid.

---

### **Substance Identified: Herbicide**

- Appearance: Similar to sugar-based liquid
- Taste: Sweet, comparable to sugar water

**Effect Profile:**

- Minimal to no effect on humans
- Minimal to no effect on standard Starladians

However—

Star’s condition differs.

---

Due to Star’s **plant-integrated physiology**:

- Vines (vascular system)
- Rose (embedded growth structure)

---

### **SYMPTOMS OBSERVED**

- Systemic weakening of vine structures

- Internal biological destabilization
