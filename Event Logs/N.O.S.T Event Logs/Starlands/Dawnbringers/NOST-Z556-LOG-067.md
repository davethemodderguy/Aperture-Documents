## **EVENT LOG**

---

### **I. INITIATION — CORRIDOR**

Star stops walking.

No visible reason.

Nightshade takes two more steps before noticing.

Turns back.

---

**Nightshade:**
“…What.”

---

Star doesn’t answer.

She just looks at her.

Then—

Raises one hand.

Two fingers. Small motion.

Points at Nightshade.

Then at herself.

Then tilts her head slightly.

---

Nightshade stares.

A second passes.

Then—

---

**Nightshade:**
“…You’re kidding.”

---

Star doesn’t react.

Just repeats the gesture.

Slightly clearer this time.

---

**Nightshade:**
“You want—”

She stops.

Exhales through her nose.

---

**Nightshade:**
“You want me to carry you.”

---

Star nods once.

---

#### **Sublog I-A — Communication Mode Shift**

* Star: **Voluntary speech suppression**
* Method: **Gesture-based signaling**
* Nightshade: **Interpretation role reactivated**

---

### **II. CONTACT — PIGGYBACK**

Nightshade turns around.

Crouches slightly—reluctant, but not refusing.

---

**Nightshade:**
“This is unnecessary.”

---

Star is already climbing onto her back.

Light wings fold in automatically to avoid obstruction.

Arms settle around Nightshade’s shoulders.

---

No words.

Just a small shift of weight.

---

Nightshade adjusts once, securing her balance.

---

**Nightshade:**
“You’re not even injured.”

---

Star taps her shoulder twice.

Short. Light.

---

**Nightshade:**
“Yeah, I remember.”

---

They start walking.

---

### **III. MOVEMENT — CORRIDOR TRANSIT**

Pace is slower.

Not because it has to be.

Because they let it be.

---

Star doesn’t speak.

Doesn’t try to.

Instead—

A small motion near Nightshade’s shoulder.

Pointing ahead.

Then a slight squeeze.

---

**Nightshade:**
“Left?”

---

Star taps once.

---

They turn.

---

#### **Sublog III-A — Gesture Translation Pattern**

* Tap (single) → Affirmation
* Tap (double) → Correction / attention
* Directional point → Navigation
* Shoulder pressure → Emphasis

Nightshade responds without hesitation.

No delay.

---

### **IV. MEMORY OVERLAY — SHARED STATE**

They pass a section of the corridor.

Same one.

Where it used to happen.

---

Nightshade slows slightly.

Doesn’t stop.

---

**Nightshade (quieter):**
“You used to do this because you couldn’t talk.”

---

No response.

Just a slight tightening of Star’s hold.

---

**Nightshade:**
“And I had to guess everything.”

---

Star lifts one hand.

Moves it into view.

Carefully.

---

She forms a shape.

Awkward, but intentional.

Then points to Nightshade.

Then to herself.

Then presses her hand flat against Nightshade’s shoulder.

---

Nightshade watches it through the corner of her eye.

---

**Nightshade:**
“…Yeah.”

A pause.

---

**Nightshade (lower):**
“I know.”

---

### **V. ROOM 150 — SETTLE**

They enter.

Door closes behind them.

---

Nightshade doesn’t immediately set her down.

Just stands there for a second.

---

**Nightshade:**
“You can talk, you know.”

---

Star doesn’t move.

---

**Nightshade:**
“You’re choosing not to.”

---

A small nod.

Barely felt.

---

Nightshade exhales.

Not frustrated.

Just… understanding.

---

**Nightshade:**
“…Alright.”

---

She walks further in.

Then finally lowers Star onto the bed.

Careful.

Automatic.

---

Star sits there.

Looking up at her.

---

No words.

---

Nightshade crosses her arms.

Then uncrosses them.

---

**Nightshade:**
“You want me to keep doing it?”

---

Star raises her hand again.

Small gesture.

Simple this time.

---

A single tap against her own chest.

Then a point at Nightshade.

---

Nightshade doesn’t need more than that.

---

**Nightshade:**
“…Fine.”

A beat.

---

**Nightshade (softer):**
“I’ll translate.”

---

Star smiles.

---

No light constructs.

No abilities.

Just that.
