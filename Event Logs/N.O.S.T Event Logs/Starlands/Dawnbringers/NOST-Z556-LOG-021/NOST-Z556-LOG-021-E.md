**Subject(s):** Z-314-S (Star), Z-314-C (Charlie)  
**Location:** Sector 4-C — Controlled Medical Room

---

### **EVENT SUMMARY**

Following the **Beanie Dependency Incident** and subsequent critical failure, a controlled attempt was made to **biologically recalibrate Star’s sensory system**—

Without reliance on external stabilization.

Lead: **Z-314-C (Charlie Dawnbringer)**

---

### **INITIAL CONDITION**

Star was:

- Stabilized
- Conscious
- Wearing Beanie (active)
- Under supervision

Physiological readings:

- Elevated anxiety
- Stable vitals
- No immediate overload

---

### **PROCEDURE START**

Charlie approached.

No sudden movement.

No external noise introduced.

Environment prepared:

- Soundproofed
- Minimal airflow
- Zero mechanical interference

---

Charlie knelt in front of Star.

Maintained eye contact.

Then—

She removed the beanie.

---

### **IMMEDIATE RESPONSE**

Star’s body tensed instantly.

- Shoulders locked
- Hands twitched
- Ears flattened

Breathing became uneven.

---

> **Star:** “…It’s coming back…”

Voice:

> **Unstable**

---

Her eyes flickered—

Not yet red—

but close.

---

### **INTERVENTION**

Charlie spoke.

Calm.

Steady.

Measured.

---

> **Charlie:** “Focus on the sound of my voice.”

---

No movement.

No repetition.

Just—

Consistency.

---

### **RESPONSE PHASE 1 — RESISTANCE**

Star’s reaction was immediate discomfort:

- Head turning slightly, searching for other inputs
- Ears twitching rapidly
- Hands rising toward her head

---

> **Star:** “It’s too much—”

---

Charlie did not raise her voice.

Did not move closer.

---

> **Charlie:** “Stay with me.”

---

### **RESPONSE PHASE 2 — CONFLICT**

Incoming stimuli began to rise.

Though the room was controlled—

Star’s amplification still pulled distant noise:

- Faint structural vibrations
- Distant airflow
- Residual environmental hum

---

Her breathing increased.

---

> **Star:** “…I hear everything again…”

---

Her claws partially extended.

Not aggression—

Instinct.

---

### **CHARLIE’S METHOD**

Charlie maintained:

- Eye contact
- Vocal consistency
- Stable tone and rhythm

---

> **Charlie:** “Then don’t listen to everything.”  
> **Charlie:** “Just listen to me.”

---

### **RESPONSE PHASE 3 — ANCHORING**

Star’s attention began to shift.

Not fully—

But enough.

---

Her ears:

- Gradually oriented toward Charlie

Her eyes:

- Locked onto a single point

---

> **Star:** “…Your voice…”

---

Charlie nodded slightly.

---

> **Charlie:** “That’s all you need.”

---

### **CRITICAL MOMENT**

A spike occurred—

Sudden internal amplification surge.

Star flinched.

Hard.

---

Her eyes flashed red—

For less than a second.

---

But—

She didn’t lose control.

---

Instead—

---

> **Star:** “No—”

---

She shut her eyes tightly.

Focused.

---

> **Star:** “…Just you…”

---

### **RESPONSE PHASE 4 — STABILIZATION**

Gradual normalization observed.

---

- Breathing slowed
- Muscle tension decreased
- Claws retracted

---

Auditory response logs show:

> Selective filtering beginning to occur

Not external.

Not artificial.

---

> **Internal**

---

### **FINAL STATE**

After approximately **11 minutes**—

Star opened her eyes.

No glow.

No distress.

---

> **Star:** “…It’s quieter.”

---

She looked around.

Slowly.

---

> **Star:** “…I can still hear things…”  
> **Star:** “…but they’re… far away now…”

---

Charlie placed the beanie back in Star’s hands—

Not on her head.

---

> **Charlie:** “You decide.”

---

Star hesitated.

Looked at it.

Then—

Held it against her chest.

---

But did not wear it.

---

### **RESULT**

- Partial biological recalibration achieved
- External dependency reduced (not removed)
- First recorded instance of **self-regulated sensory filtering**

---

### **LIMITATIONS**

- Stability not guaranteed under high-noise conditions
- Emotional spikes may disrupt control
- Beanie still recommended as backup

---

### **STATUS**

Z-314-S is:

- **Stable (assisted internally)**
- **Recovering control over sensory input**
- **No longer fully dependent**

---

### **CONCLUSION**

The solution wasn’t suppression.

It wasn’t filtering.

It wasn’t control through force.

---

It was focus.

---

And for the first time—

Star didn’t need something to block the world out.

She just needed something to hold onto within it.
