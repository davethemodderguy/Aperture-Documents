### **I. EXIT — TRANSITION POINT**

A door forms.

Not dramatic. Just… there.

A thin outline of light folds into shape against a blank wall, edges flickering softly before stabilizing.

Nightshade stares at it.

---

**Nightshade:**  
“…You’re sure this is allowed.”

**Star** _(already reaching for the handle)_:  
“Authorized.”

A pause.

She glances back.

**Star (lighter):**  
“Conditional, but still counts.”

---

Nightshade steps closer, eyes narrowing slightly.

---

**Nightshade:**  
“And if something goes wrong?”

**Star:**  
“I fix it.”

**Nightshade:**  
“That’s not reassuring.”

---

Star smiles anyway and pushes the door open.

Daylight spills through.

Real sunlight.

---

They step out.

The door closes behind them—gone instantly.

---

Cleaner.

Their outlines soften, edges blending into the environment just enough to avoid attention.

“Stay close.”

**Nightshade:**  
“I’m not the one who glows.”

**Star:**  
“…Fixed.”

Noise.

Movement.

People everywhere.

Nothing like Sector 4-C.

Nightshade slows slightly—not fear, just adjustment.

Her eyes move constantly.

Tracking.

Measuring.

---

**Nightshade:**  
“They don’t notice anything.”

**Star:**  
“That’s the point.”

---

They pass by a storefront.

Glass reflection.

Two figures.

Identical height. Similar build.

From the outside—just twins.

---

Nightshade stops for half a second.

Looks at the reflection.

---

**Nightshade:**  
“…That’s weird.”

**Star:**  
“What is?”

**Nightshade:**  
“Looking normal.”

---

Star tilts her head.

---

**Star:**  
“You _are_ normal.”

**Nightshade:**  
“No. I’m just not obvious.”

---

They keep walking.

---

Too fast to notice.

But Nightshade sees it.

---

**Nightshade (low):**  
“Careful.”

**Star:**  
“I didn’t even—”

**Nightshade:**  
“You did.”

---

**Star:**  
“…Right.”

Less pressure.

“Better?”

**Nightshade:**

**Star:**
