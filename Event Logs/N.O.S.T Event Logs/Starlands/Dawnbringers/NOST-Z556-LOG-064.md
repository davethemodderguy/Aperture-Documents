## **EVENT LOG**

---

### **I. MORNING — ROOM 150**

Star is already awake.

Light gathers lazily around her hands—thin strands forming shapes, dissolving, reforming. Not training. Just something to do.

Nightshade is still lying down, facing away.

A pause.

Then—

**Star** _(soft, casual)_:  
“You’re pretending.”

No response.

A small orb of light floats over, hovering just above Nightshade’s face.

It brightens.

Slightly.

**Nightshade** _(eyes still closed)_:  
“If you flashbang me first thing in the morning, I’m throwing you through a wall.”

The light dims immediately.

Star grins.

She shifts closer, wings folding slightly to fit the space.

**Star:**  
“You’ve been awake for like… ten minutes.”

**Nightshade:**  
“…Eight.”

A beat.

**Star:**  
“You counted.”

**Nightshade:**  
“I always count.”

Star doesn’t respond to that—just watches her.

Then, quieter—

**Star:**  
“Bad night?”

Nightshade opens her eyes this time. The glow is faint. Stable.

She exhales.

**Nightshade:**  
“No. Just… didn’t feel like moving.”

Star nods once. No pushing.

A small bridge of light forms between them across the bed—unnecessary distance, but she makes it anyway.

Nightshade looks at it.

Then places her hand on it.

It holds.

---

### **II. MIDDAY — CAFETERIA / OPEN AREA**

The Contractor’s stand is active.

Star is leaning over the counter, watching something being assembled that she clearly doesn’t need.

Nightshade stands beside her, arms crossed.

**Contractor** _(cheerful)_:  
“So, today’s order is… absolutely nothing again?”

**Star:**  
“I like watching.”

**Nightshade:**  
“She just likes existing near things she can’t break.”

**Star:**  
“I can break this.”

**Contractor:**  
“Please don’t.”

Star laughs.

A thin ribbon of light curls around a glass, lifting it slightly, spinning it once before placing it back down.

Controlled. Precise.

Nightshade watches the movement—not the object.

**Nightshade:**  
“You’re overcompensating.”

**Star:**  
“For what?”

**Nightshade:**  
“For not using too much.”

Star pauses.

Then shrugs.

**Star:**  
“I don’t want to mess things up.”

Nightshade’s expression doesn’t change, but her tone softens—barely.

**Nightshade:**  
“You already did that once.”

Silence.

Not tense. Just… real.

Star leans her chin into her hand.

**Star:**  
“Yeah.”

A second passes.

**Nightshade:**  
“…You also fixed it.”

Star glances at her.

That lands.

---

### **III. AFTERNOON — OVERHEAD GARDEN (VIRIDIS ZONE)**

Viridis’ influence keeps the space quiet. Stable.

Star walks along a suspended pathway—hard-light forming under each step before fading behind her.

Nightshade doesn’t use it.

She just jumps up beside her instead.

**Star:**  
“You know I can just make a bridge.”

**Nightshade:**  
“I know I don’t need it.”

They walk anyway.

Side by side.

Star slows slightly to match pace.

**Star:**  
“Do you still think about it?”

Nightshade doesn’t ask what _it_ is.

She knows.

**Nightshade:**  
“Yeah.”

No hesitation.

**Nightshade (cont.):**  
“Not like before.”

Star waits.

**Nightshade:**  
“Before, it was loud. Constant. Like something was forcing it in.”

A pause.

**Nightshade:**  
“Now it’s just… mine.”

Star looks ahead, not at her.

**Star:**  
“Is that worse?”

Nightshade thinks.

Actually thinks.

**Nightshade:**  
“No.”

Another step.

**Nightshade:**  
“It means I can control it.”

Star nods slowly.

A wall of light forms briefly at the edge of the garden—just to block the wind.

It fades after a few seconds.

---

### **IV. EVENING — BALCONY**

Same place.

No avoidance.

Stars above—real ones, not constructs.

They sit near the edge.

Not too close.

Not far.

Star rests her chin on her knees, wings dimmed to a soft glow.

Nightshade leans back slightly, eyes on the sky.

Long silence.

Comfortable.

---

**Star:**  
“You could’ve just… told me to move.”

Nightshade doesn’t look at her.

**Nightshade:**  
“I know.”

**Star:**  
“I would’ve listened.”

A pause.

**Nightshade:**  
“I know that too.”

Another silence.

The air shifts slightly—Star instinctively stabilizes it with light, barely noticeable.

---

**Nightshade:**  
“I remember everything.”

Star doesn’t react immediately.

---

**Nightshade (cont.):**  
“The way you looked at me right before—”

She stops.

Not because she can’t continue.

Because she chooses not to.

---

**Star:**  
“I remember missing.”

Nightshade turns slightly.

---

**Star (quiet):**  
“I tried to reach the thermos.”

A small, almost embarrassed smile.

**Star:**  
“I didn’t make it.”

Silence again.

Heavy—but not crushing.

---

**Nightshade:**  
“…You don’t have to say it like that.”

**Star:**  
“It happened like that.”

No denial.

No softening.

Just truth.

---

Nightshade exhales slowly.

Then—

She shifts closer.

Not touching yet.

Just closer.

---

**Nightshade:**  
“You’re here now.”

**Star:**  
“Yeah.”

---

A thin strand of light forms between their hands.

Not solid.

Not forcing contact.

Just there.

Nightshade looks at it.

Then closes the gap herself.
