# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **POST-INCIDENT CHARACTER RESPONSE RECORD**

### **Subject:** Snowy Disappearance / Whitefall Collapse Event

### **Classification:** Internal Emotional Observation Archive

---

# **I. STAR — “THE ONE WHO TRIED TO STAY HOPEFUL”**

---

Star received the report while returning from a N.O.S.T operation alongside Nightshade.

---

At first—

she thought Snowy was merely missing temporarily.

---

Then she was handed:

- the blood-written notes
    
- Exodus extracts
    
- recovery timelines
    
- search termination notice
    

---

Witnesses report Star stopped reading halfway through Exodus 56.

---

Specifically at:

> “You have something worth living for. I don’t.”

---

She reportedly whispered:

---

> “…No…”

---

Then again.

---

> “…No, no, no…”

---

Star spent several days insisting Snowy could still be saved.

---

She repeatedly proposed:

- light-domain tracking
    
- dimensional scans
    
- Starland resonance tracing
    
- full Aegis deployment into Whitefall
    

---

Every proposal failed.

---

Whitefall rejected nearly all long-range detection methods entirely.

---

The realization slowly destroyed her optimism.

---

Most painful observation:

Star reportedly entered Snowy’s room alone during a Blacksite visit.

---

She later emerged holding one of his drawings silently.

---

It depicted:

- Whitefall Cabin
    
- Sky
    
- Cherry
    
- Cheer
    
- Candleheart
    
- himself in wolf form
    

---

And in the corner—

a tiny smiling sun labeled:

> “Star”

---

Witnesses report Star cried immediately afterward.

---

Because even while mentally collapsing—

Snowy still drew everyone kindly.

---

Including her.

---

---

# **II. NIGHTSHADE — “THE ANGER RESPONSE”**

---

Nightshade reacted violently upon hearing search operations had been terminated.

---

Her immediate response:

---

> “You WHAT?”

---

> “You just LEFT him there?”

---

She reportedly attempted to reopen Whitefall search authorization personally.

---

Director Lucious denied the request.

---

That argument escalated rapidly.

---

Nightshade’s exact statement:

---

> “He’s eight.”

---

> “I don’t care how broken he is.”

---

> “You don’t abandon children to die in snowstorms.”

---

---

However—

after personally reviewing the Exodus pages—

her anger changed.

---

Not toward Whitefall.

---

Toward Snowy himself.

---

Not hateful anger.

---

Heartbroken anger.

---

She reportedly slammed the diary shut after reading:

> “Just forget I’ve ever existed.”

---

Then yelled:

---

> “…Stop deciding that for us!”

---

---

Nightshade later became unusually quiet for several days.

---

Which alarmed nearly everyone who knew her.

---

Eventually,

Stitch found her alone in a training room repeatedly destroying practice drones.

---

One sentence overheard quietly between attacks:

---

> “…Why do the kind ones always think they deserve pain?”

---

---

# **III. STITCH — “THE LOGICAL COLLAPSE”**

---

Stitch initially approached the incident analytically.

---

He focused heavily on:

- tracking probabilities
    
- environmental survivability
    
- injury patterns
    
- diary sequencing
    

---

However—

that detached approach failed gradually over time.

---

The breaking point occurred during analysis of Snowy’s blood traces.

---

Stitch reportedly paused mid-report before quietly stating:

---

> “…His body should not still be functioning.”

---

---

After reading the Exodus entries—

Stitch became increasingly disturbed by one realization:

---

Snowy’s writings displayed coherent structure.

---

Meaning:

he was psychologically shattered—

but still mentally organized enough to plan his own disappearance carefully.

---

This disturbed Stitch heavily.

---

His final conclusion during internal review:

---

> “…He wasn’t trying to survive Whitefall.”

---

> “…He was trying to become impossible to save.”

---

---

Afterward,

Stitch reportedly avoided Snowy-related discussions entirely.

---

Which was highly unusual behavior for him.

---

---

# **IV. CINDER — “THE QUIET GRIEF”**

---

Cinder did not speak for a long time after hearing the full report.

---

He simply listened.

---

Then quietly requested access to Whitefall environmental recordings.

---

For several hours,

he watched footage of Snowy:

- sleeping outside
    
- crawling with broken legs
    
- burying himself beneath snow
    
- wandering alone during storms
    

---

Nobody interrupted him.

---

Eventually,

Cinder asked only one question:

---

> “…How long was he hurting before anyone noticed?”

---

---

No one answered.

---

Because nobody truly knew.

---

Later—

despite Whitefall’s freezing conditions being dangerous for him—

Cinder reportedly visited the abandoned cabin personally.

---

He remained there for only six minutes before retreating due to thermal destabilization.

---

However—

before leaving,

he reignited the fireplace manually.

---

No one knows why.

---

---

# **V. DUSEKKAR — “THE HEAVIEST SILENCE”**

---

Dusekkar’s reaction was the most difficult to interpret.

---

Upon receiving the report—

he reportedly read every recovered Exodus section completely.

---

Twice.

---

Then closed the file.

---

No visible emotional response observed.

---

But afterward—

all nearby emotional monitoring systems detected unstable pressure fluctuations around him for several hours.

---

Extremely dangerous ones.

---

Director Lucious later noted:

---

> “Dusekkar looked calm.”

---

Pause.

---

> “That’s what worried me.”

---

---

Three days later,

Dusekkar reportedly visited Whitefall Ridge alone.

---

No logs exist regarding what occurred there.

---

However—

when he returned,

small ice fractures were discovered across the cliffside extending several kilometers outward.

---

No further explanation provided.

---

---

# **VI. VIRIDIS — “THE ONE WHO COULDN’T UNDERSTAND”**

---

Viridis reportedly struggled the most with understanding Snowy’s mindset.

---

After reviewing the logs,

her first response was confusion rather than anger.

---

> “…Why would he think nobody wanted him?”

---

---

Because from Viridis’ perspective—

Snowy was constantly loved by others.

---

Sky stayed beside him.

Cherry knitted for him.

Cheer protected him.

Everyone searched for him.

---

Yet somehow—

Snowy still believed he deserved abandonment.

---

That contradiction disturbed her deeply.

---

After reading Exodus 57,

Viridis reportedly began crying during the line:

---

> “I’ll give up everything to see you happy.”

---

---

Her response afterward:

---

> “…But who taught him he wasn’t allowed to be happy too?”

---

---

No one answered her either.

---

Because that question hurt too much.

---

# **VII. FINAL NOTE**

---

Everyone reacted differently.

---

Star cried.

Nightshade raged.

Stitch analyzed.

Cinder mourned quietly.

Dusekkar went silent.

Viridis questioned everything.

---

But beneath all of it—

they eventually reached the same horrifying conclusion.

---

Snowy never hated them.

---

Not once.

---

Even while disappearing.

Even while freezing.

Even while bleeding alone beneath Whitefall’s storms.

---

He still loved everyone around him enough

to believe they deserved happiness more than he did.

---

And that realization hurt far worse

than the possibility that he was gone forever.
