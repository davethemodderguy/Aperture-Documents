# **I. SUMMARY**

Snowy has begun showing clear preference toward remaining in wolf pup form for extended periods.

---

Transformation frequency into humanoid state has decreased significantly.

---

Current average daily state ratio:

- Wolf Form: ~82%
    
- Humanoid Form: ~18%
    

---

Most transformations back into humanoid form occur only for:

- medical treatment
    
- bathing
    
- communication clarity
    
- emotional discussions
    

---

Outside of these situations—

Snowy overwhelmingly chooses wolf form voluntarily.

---

Lucy AI assessment:

> “Wolf form is becoming Snowy’s emotional comfort state.”

---

---

# **II. POSSIBLE REASONS**

---

Several factors likely contribute to this preference.

---

## **1. Physical Comfort**

---

Wolf form provides:

- increased warmth
    
- reduced pain sensitivity
    
- easier movement in snow
    
- greater environmental adaptation
    

---

Notably—

his stronger wolf frame handles impacts significantly better than humanoid form.

---

This became especially relevant due to a new recurring issue:

> residents accidentally dropping him constantly

---

---

## **2. Emotional Safety**

---

Wolf form appears psychologically easier for Snowy to maintain.

---

In wolf state:

- speech becomes simpler
    
- anxiety decreases slightly
    
- panic frequency lowers
    
- social pressure reduces
    

---

Lucy AI interpretation:

> “Snowy feels less judged when not perceived as fully human.”

---

---

## **3. Physical Affection**

---

Despite frequent complaining—

Snowy receives substantially more affection while transformed.

---

Examples include:

- head pats
    
- brushing
    
- carrying
    
- cuddling
    
- lap sleeping
    

---

While he verbally protests most interactions—

tail monitoring strongly contradicts his statements.

---

---

# **III. THROWING INCIDENTS**

---

Due to Snowy’s defensive biting behavior—

many residents instinctively release or toss him accidentally after being bitten.

---

Unexpectedly—

Snowy appears to enjoy this.

---

---
