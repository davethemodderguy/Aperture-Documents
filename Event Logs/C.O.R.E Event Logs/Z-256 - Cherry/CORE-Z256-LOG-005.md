## **I. SUMMARY**

Session resulted in **high plant growth acceleration**, minor containment concerns, and **no structural damage**.

---

## **II. EVENT RECORD**

The door opened.

---

Cherry stepped in—

and immediately stopped.

---

“…Woah.”

---

Rows of plants.

Different shapes.  
Different colors.

Alive.

---

Not like her forest.

But close enough.

---

**Cherry (quietly):**  
“…Hi.”

---

Petals drifted from her hair.

---

The plants responded.

---

Subtle at first—

leaves turning, stems shifting.

---

Then—

growth.

---

**Viridis (appearing behind her):**  
“Careful.”

---

Cherry flinched slightly.

---

“Oh—sorry!”

---

She didn’t move.

---

But the plants did.

---

Vines curled closer.

Flowers bloomed early.

---

**Viridis (observing):**  
“…You’re not forcing it.”

---

**Cherry:**  
“I don’t know how to…”

---

A pause.

---

“They just… listen.”

---

Viridis nodded once.

---

“Good.”

---

## **III. ESCALATION**

---

**Bao (running in):**  
“CHERRY—WAIT—IS THIS A JUNGLE NOW—”

---

She stopped.

---

“…Oh.”

---

The greenhouse was greener.

Denser.

---

**Bao:**  
“CAN I TOUCH—”

---

She already did.

---

A vine wrapped around her wrist.

Not tight.

Just—

there.

---

**Bao:**  
“…It likes me.”

---

**Frost (entering):**  
“Or it’s restraining you.”

---

**Bao:**  
“…Rude.”

---

Cherry giggled.

---

Petals increased.

---

Growth followed.

---

## **IV. STABILITY POINT**

---

**Frost:**  
“…It’s getting cold.”

---

Ice formed along one section of the glass.

---

Plants near it slowed.

Stabilized.

---

Viridis glanced at Frost.

---

“…Good control.”

---

Frost didn’t respond.

---

But didn’t stop either.

---

Balance formed:

- Cherry → growth
    
- Frost → regulation
    
- Viridis → oversight
    

---

## **V. JAKE ARRIVAL**

---

Jake stepped in—

paused.

---

“…What happened.”

---

The greenhouse was now:

- thicker
    
- brighter
    
- slightly overgrown
    

---

Cherry ran over.

---

“Jake look! They’re happy!”

---

Jake looked at the vines.

Then at her.

---

“…Yeah.”

---

A small pause.

---

“…Just don’t let them take over the hallway again.”

---

**Bao (from behind):**  
“NO PROMISES—”

---

**Frost:**  
“There will be promises.”

---

## **VI. WIND EVENT**

---

A light airflow passed through the ventilation system.

---

Cherry’s hair shifted.

---

Petals—

multiplied.

---

For a moment—

it looked like snowfall.

---

Pink.

---

The entire greenhouse paused.

---

Even Viridis didn’t speak.

---

**Star (quietly, from the entrance):**  
“…Pretty.”

---

Cherry smiled.

---

## **VII. CONCLUSION**

- No damage to infrastructure
    
- Plant growth increased by ~230% during session
    
- No hostile reactions observed
    
- Emotional state of all participants: **positive**
    

---

## **VIII. FINAL NOTE**

Cherry didn’t try to control the greenhouse.

---

She just treated it like something alive.

---

And for a while—

---

it treated her the same way.
