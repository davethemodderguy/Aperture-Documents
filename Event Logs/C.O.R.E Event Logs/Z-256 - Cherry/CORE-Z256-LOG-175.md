# **I. SUMMARY**

Further biological observation of Snowy’s wolf transformation state has revealed unexpected proportional scaling.

---

Despite prior assumptions—

Snowy’s wolf form does NOT resemble a mature snow wolf entity.

---

Instead—

due to Snowy’s current developmental age in humanoid form—

his transformed state manifests as:

> a small wolf pup

---

Resulting appearance described by multiple personnel as:

- unusually small
    
- heavily furred
    
- visibly fragile
    
- “extremely cute” (direct quote from Candleheart)
    

---

Current estimated size:

- small enough to carry comfortably
    
- approximately equivalent to a large puppy
    

---

This discovery significantly altered social interactions between Snowy and Whitefall residents.

---

Though Snowy himself reportedly dislikes the attention.

# **II. PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION — WOLF FORM**

---

Current wolf-form traits include:

- dense snow-white fur
    
- fluffy oversized tail
    
- large ears relative to head size
    
- glowing azure eyes (when visible)
    
- soft cold-resistant paw pads
    

---

Additional observations:

- body releases faint snow particles during movement

- ears visibly twitch in response to emotion
    

---

Most notable feature:

Snowy’s wolf form appears physically incapable of looking intimidating.

---

Even when attempting aggression.

---
