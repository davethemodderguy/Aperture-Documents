# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **WHITEFALL RESIDENT OBSERVATION LOG — WOLF FORM MANIFESTATION**

### **Classification:** Internal / Psychological & Biological Monitoring

---

### **Log ID:** CORE-Z256-LOG-174

### **Status:** ACTIVE — BEHAVIORAL CONCERN

---

# **I. SUMMARY**

Snowy has developed the capability to transition voluntarily between:

- humanoid form
    
- original snow wolf form
    

---

Transformation appears instinctive rather than learned.

---

The event was initially considered positive due to improved environmental adaptation.

---

However—

subsequent behavioral analysis revealed the development is heavily linked to escalating self-neglect and emotional withdrawal.

---

Lucy AI has flagged the situation as:

> “functional regression disguised as adaptation”

---

---

# **II. INITIAL MANIFESTATION**

---

The transformation first occurred during nighttime Whitefall exposure.

---

At approximately 01:14 Whitefall Standard—

Snowy was observed collapsing briefly outside the cabin after severe leg pain escalation.

---

Thermal readings then spiked abnormally.

---

Moments later—

Snowy’s body rapidly shifted into his original wolf state.

---

Observed changes included:

- quadrupedal posture
    
- dense white fur generation
    
- enlarged tail mass
    
- enhanced cold insulation
    
- increased body heat retention
    

---

Notably—

the fur itself does not physically remain while humanoid.

---

Instead—

it appears and disappears alongside transformation state.

---

---

## **III. INITIAL REACTION**

---

Snowy reportedly panicked immediately after transforming.

---

Recorded behaviors:

- stumbling while walking
    
- confusion regarding paw movement
    
- repeated attempts to revert instantly
    
- hiding behind cabin exterior
    

---

Sky was reportedly first to approach him safely.

---

Initially—

Snowy refused eye contact entirely.

---

Even in wolf form.

---

He repeatedly muttered:

---

> “…I look weird…”

---

---

Sky reportedly sat beside him quietly for several minutes before answering:

---

> “You look like Snowy.”

---

---

No immediate response followed.

---

However—

Snowy reportedly stopped trying to hide afterward.

---

---

## **IV. CURRENT USAGE**

---

After several days—

Snowy began using wolf form regularly.

---

Primary uses include:

- sleeping outside
    
- enduring storms
    
- moving through deep snow
    
- emotional isolation
    

---

Most concerning detail:

Snowy now almost exclusively sleeps outside while transformed.

---

Reason likely includes:

- increased warmth from fur
    
- emotional comfort through familiarity
    
- distancing from others
    

---

Lucy AI notes wolf form behavior strongly resembles:

> “returning to a simpler emotional state”

---

Interpretation:

Wolf form allows Snowy to temporarily escape expectations associated with being human.

---

---

# **V. SOCIAL BEHAVIOR CHANGES**

---

In wolf form—

Snowy becomes noticeably quieter.

---

Observed differences include:

- reduced arguing
    
- less verbal apologizing
    
- increased avoidance
    
- stronger attachment to outdoor spaces
    

---

However—

physical affection tolerance increases slightly.

---

Examples include:

- allowing Sky to brush fur
    
- sleeping near Cherry occasionally
    
- resting beside Cheer silently near the cabin porch
    

---

Most notable change:

Snowy occasionally falls asleep with his head resting against others unintentionally.

---

Upon waking—

he usually becomes embarrassed and leaves immediately afterward.

---

---

## **VI. SELF-NEGLECT ESCALATION**

---

Despite improved survival adaptation—

Snowy’s self-care behaviors continue worsening.

---

Current concerns include:

- intentionally avoiding indoor warmth
    
- minimizing food intake again
    
- ignoring pain symptoms
    
- excessive strain on healing legs
    
- emotional withdrawal
    

---

When asked why he continues sleeping outside—

Snowy answered:

---

> “…Wolves belong outside.”

---

Small pause.

---

> “…Cabin’s for everyone else.”

---

---

This statement triggered immediate concern among all Whitefall residents.

---

---

## **VII. SKY OBSERVATION**

---

Sky appears to understand Snowy’s wolf form emotionally better than others.

---

Rather than forcing him inside—

she now often sits outside beside him at night.

---

Sometimes reading quietly.

Sometimes simply staying nearby.

---

One particularly significant observation occurred during heavy snowfall.

---

Snowy slept curled beside the porch in wolf form.

---

Sky quietly placed blankets over him despite his thick fur.

---

Half-awake—

Snowy reportedly muttered:

---

> “…Too much trouble…”

---

---

Sky gently stroked behind his ears afterward.

---

Then answered softly:

---

> “You’re worth the trouble.”

---

---

Snowy’s tail reportedly wagged slightly during sleep immediately afterward.

---

Though he denied it happened later.

---

---

# **VIII. FINAL NOTE**

---

Snowy becoming a wolf again

should have been comforting.

---

And in some ways—

it was.

---

The fur keeps him warm.

The snow hurts less.

The world feels quieter.

---

But beneath all of it—

the reason never changed.

---

He still sleeps outside because he believes

inside spaces belong to other people.

---

Still hides pain because he fears becoming a burden.

---

Still curls alone beneath the snow

like a stray animal waiting beside a home

he isn’t fully convinced belongs to him.

---

And somehow—

that hurts everyone at Whitefall Lake

more than the cold ever could.
