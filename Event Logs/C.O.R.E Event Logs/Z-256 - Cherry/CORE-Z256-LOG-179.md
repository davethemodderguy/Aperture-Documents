# **I. SUMMARY**

Due to a rare environmental fluctuation—

Whitefall Lake temperatures temporarily stabilized at approximately:

> 25°C

This resulted in one of the warmest recorded days in Whitefall history.

---

Activities initiated by residents included:

- snow sculpture building
    
- outdoor cooking
    
- skating
    
- snowball fights
    
- recreational sports
    

---

At approximately 14:33 Whitefall Standard—

Frost proposed a volleyball match.

---

> no volleyball available
