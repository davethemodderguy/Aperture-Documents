## **I. SUMMARY**

Z-256 (Cherry) has demonstrated the ability to assume a **true fox form**, previously unobserved during initial integration phases.

Upon manifestation, Cherry displays:

- Full fox physiology
    
- **Three tails total** (one baseline + two additional)
    
- Enhanced environmental interaction with surrounding flora and particles
    

Cherry confirms **prior awareness** of this form and does not treat it as a new development.

## **II. EVENT RECORD**

---

It wasn’t an accident.

---

Cherry was already sitting in the grass of her Domain.

---

Petals drifting.

Calm.

---

**Bao:**  
“Hey Cherry, can you turn into a full fox or—”

---

**Cherry:**  
“I can.”

---

No buildup.

No hesitation.

---

**Bao:**  
“…Wait, really?”

---

Cherry stood up.

Dusted her hands.

---

“Yeah.”

---

A small stretch—

---

Then—

---

No flash.

No dramatic light.

---

Just—

**shift**

---

---

Pink fur.

Soft glow.

---

And behind her—

---

**three tails**

---

---

Silence.

---

**Bao:**  
“…Oh.”

---

---

---

“…What?”

---

Didn’t distort.

---

It was still her.
