### **ALLIED ENTITY RESPONSE REPORT — SNOWY INCIDENT DISCLOSURE**

### **Classification:** Internal / Emotional Impact Documentation

# **I. SUMMARY**

Following official termination of Whitefall search operations—

multiple allied entities stationed outside Whitefall Lake were formally informed of the full situation regarding:

- Snowy’s disappearance
    
- the Exodus writings
    
- the search collapse
    
- operational withdrawal
    
- presumed suicidal intent
    

---

Most entities were previously aware Snowy had gone missing.

---

However—

this was the first time they learned the full extent of his mental deterioration.

---

Including the meaning behind:

---

> “Will you wake up tomorrow?”

---

And the fact Snowy likely disappeared intentionally believing everyone would live happier lives without him.

---

Responses varied significantly.

---

None were unaffected.

---

---

# **II. STAR RESPONSE**

---

Notification delivered inside N.O.S.T Sector lounge during archive review hours.

---

Star reportedly listened silently through the entire report.

---

No interruptions.

No emotional outburst.

---

But witnesses observed her wings dim gradually as the briefing continued.

---

Most severe reaction occurred upon hearing excerpts from Exodus 57.

---

Specifically:

---

> “I'm happy when others are happy.”

---

> “I don't care about myself anymore.”

---

---

Star reportedly lowered her head immediately afterward.

---

Her response was quiet.

---

Almost disbelieving.

---

> “…He’s just a child…”

---

Long silence.

---

> “…Why was he apologizing for existing?”

---

---

After the meeting,

Star reportedly canceled all scheduled operations for the day.

---

Later observations noted she remained alone inside Whitefall monitoring archives reviewing Snowy’s recovered notes repeatedly.

---

Lucy AI recorded elevated emotional instability throughout the session.

---

---

# **III. NIGHTSHADE RESPONSE**

---

Nightshade reacted differently.

---

Unlike Star—

she became visibly angry.

---

Not at Snowy.

---

At everyone.

---

Report states she immediately slammed the briefing tablet onto a table hard enough to crack it upon hearing search operations were terminated.

---

Her exact statement:

---

> “So everyone just left him there?”

---

Director Lucious reportedly attempted clarification.

---

Nightshade interrupted immediately.

---

> “He’s mentally destroyed.”

---

> “Of course he’d say to stop looking.”

---

> “That’s what broken people do.”

---

---

Further escalation occurred after learning Snowy intentionally returned emotionally significant belongings before disappearing.

---

Nightshade reportedly went silent briefly before muttering:

---

> “…That idiot already planned his own funeral…”

---

---

Despite aggressive reactions—

Lucy AI later detected severe emotional distress indicators hidden beneath anger responses.

---

Most notable observation:

Nightshade reportedly refused to read the Exodus pages directly.

---

Stating only:

---

> “…I don’t want his last thoughts stuck in my head too.”

---

---

# **IV. STITCH RESPONSE**

---

Stitch reportedly remained composed during briefing.

---

However—

his reaction became notably more subdued after hearing medical analysis of Snowy’s condition before disappearance.

---

Particularly:

- self-starvation
    
- untreated injuries
    
- exposure damage
    
- sleep deprivation
    
- passive suicidal behavior
    

---

Upon hearing Snowy intentionally surrendered himself to Whitefall conditions—

Stitch reportedly removed his glasses and remained silent for nearly forty seconds.

---

His eventual response:

---

> “…His body survived longer than his mind did.”

---

---

Later—

after reviewing Exodus materials personally—

Stitch reportedly requested temporary archive restrictions on younger personnel.

---

Reason given:

---

> “No child should write like this.”

---

---

---

Cinder reportedly reacted with immediate denial.

---

His first response:

---

> “…No.”

---

Then louder:

---

> “No.”

---

> “That’s not happening.”

---

---

---

---

---

Dusekkar reportedly requested complete uninterrupted access to all Exodus documents.

---

---

> “…He wasn’t trying to die.”

---

---

> “…He was trying to stop hurting.”

# **VII. VIRIDIS RESPONSE**

---

Viridis reportedly reacted the most emotionally out of all notified entities.

---

she reportedly started crying immediately.

---

Especially after hearing how he survived off:

- grass
    
- leaves
    
- snow
    
- wild crops
    

---

While refusing food because he believed:

---

> “Bad boys don’t deserve gifts.”

---

Viridis reportedly covered her face while listening to the report.

---

Repeated statement recorded:

---

> “…That’s not a bad child…”

---

> “…That’s a scared one…”

---

---

Most severe emotional reaction occurred after learning Snowy returned Cherry’s knitted belongings before disappearing.

---

Viridis reportedly asked quietly:

---

> “…Did he think everyone would forget him faster without those things around?”

No one answered.

# **VIII. FINAL NOTE**

---

The tragedy of Snowy spread through Aperture slowly.

---

Not like an explosion.

---

Like cold.

---

Quietly reaching people one by one—

until eventually everyone felt it.

---

Because the terrifying part was never just that Snowy disappeared.

---

It was understanding why.

---

A child convinced himself so completely

that he was unwanted,

harmful,

and undeserving of tomorrow—

that disappearing into endless snow alone

felt kinder than staying loved.

---

And now across Aperture,

inside laboratories,

archives,

domains,

and sanctuaries—

people who barely knew him

kept thinking about the same question.

---

How much pain does a child need to suffer

before being forgotten by the world

starts sounding comforting?
