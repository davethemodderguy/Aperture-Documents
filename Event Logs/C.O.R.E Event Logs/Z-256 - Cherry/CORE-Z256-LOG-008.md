Cherry initiated a **photography session**, offering to teach both Star and Nightshade basic and advanced techniques.

Session resulted in:

- Reluctant participation → active involvement (Nightshade)

## **II. EVENT RECORD**

---

The Blacksite was quiet.

---

Too quiet.

---

No footsteps.

No chatter.

---

Just three people.

---

**Cherry:**  
“…It’s weird.”

---

**Star:**  
“A little.”

---

**Nightshade:**  
“…Finally peaceful.”

---

Cherry looked at both of them.

---

“…Wanna do something?”

---

A pause.

---

She lifted the camera hanging from her neck.

---

“I can teach you this.”

---

---

## **III. INTRODUCTION**

---

They started in a hallway.

---

Cherry held the camera carefully.

---

“Okay, rule one—don’t just take pictures.”

---

**Nightshade:**  
“…That’s literally what a camera does.”

---

**Cherry:**  
“No, it _captures_ things.”

---

A small pause.

---

“…You have to pick what matters first.”

---

Star nodded immediately.

---

Nightshade crossed her arms.

---

“…Fine. Show me.”

---

---

## **IV. STAR — FAST LEARNER**

---

Cherry handed the camera to Star.

---

“Try.”

---

Star looked around.

---

Paused.

---

Then pointed the camera—

not at the hallway—

but at the light coming through a window.

---

Click.

---

Cherry leaned in.

---

“…That’s good.”

---

**Star:**  
“It felt right.”

---

**Cherry:**  
“Yeah. That’s how you know.”

---

Star smiled slightly.

---

---

## **V. NIGHTSHADE — RESISTANCE**

---

Cherry turned to Nightshade.

---

“Your turn.”

---

Nightshade didn’t move.

---

“…I don’t see the point.”

---

Cherry tilted her head.

---

“…Then don’t take a ‘point.’ Take something else.”

---

Silence.

---

Nightshade took the camera.

---

Looked around.

---

Nothing.

---

Then—

she turned it toward Star.

---

Click.

---

Star blinked.

---

“…Why me?”

---

**Nightshade:**  
“…You were there.”

---

Cherry looked at the photo.

---

Didn’t say anything.

---

---

They relocated.

---

---

Stars faintly visible.

---

Cherry adjusted the lens.

---

“Light matters more when there’s less of it.”

---

She handed the camera to Nightshade again.

---

“…Try again.”

---

Nightshade sighed.

---

But lifted it anyway.

---

She didn’t aim at the sky.

---

She aimed at—

---

Cherry.

---

Standing near the glass.

Petals drifting slightly.

---

Click.

---

A pause.

---

“…That one’s better.”

---

Cherry looked at her.

---

“…You’re getting it.”

---

Nightshade didn’t respond.

---

But didn’t argue either.
