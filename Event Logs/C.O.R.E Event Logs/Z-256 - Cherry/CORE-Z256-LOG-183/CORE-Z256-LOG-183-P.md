# **I. EVENT SUMMARY**

Shortly after recovery of the Week 14 blood-written note—

an anomalous message appeared within Whitefall Cabin.

---

The message contained only three words:

---

> “Check my room”

---

No known entity admitted responsibility.

No signs of forced entry detected.

No spectral anomalies recorded.

---

Snowy’s room was immediately searched.

---

Inside his storage drawer—

beneath folded blankets and unfinished sketches—

a book was recovered.

---

Initially assumed to be a diary.

---

Further examination revealed something significantly larger.

---

The object was not a diary.

---

It was a fully organized handwritten archive.

---

Complete with:

- numbered chapters
    
- labels
    
- indexing tabs
    
- table of contents
    

---

Most sections consisted of fragmented emotional writings.

---

Repeated phrases appeared throughout hundreds of pages including:

---

> “Please don’t leave me.”

---

> “I’m sorry.”

---

> “Will you wake up tomorrow?”

---

---

Many pages contained nothing except those same lines repeated endlessly.

---

As if written during psychological spirals.

---

However—

one section immediately drew attention.

---

# **EXODUS**

# **II. EXODUS DISCOVERY**

---

The term “Exodus” was initially believed to reference biblical scripture.

---

This assumption was incorrect.

---

“Exodus” referred instead to an internal chapter system within Snowy’s own writings.

---

The blood-written message from Week 14—

> “Exodus 56:8”

---

was actually directing readers toward:

---

### Chapter 56

### Entry 8

---

Most Exodus sections were heavily blacked out manually using thick graphite and ink layering.

---

Entire pages obscured deliberately.

---

However—

Section 56 remained visible.

---

Upon opening Exodus 56:8—

a letter was discovered.

---

---

The final sentence triggered immediate investigation.

---

Page 57 initially appeared completely illegible.

---

Only scribbles.

Heavy graphite marks.

Layered writing distortion.

---

However—

after exposure to directed light sources—

hidden impressions became faintly visible beneath the markings.

---

Lucy AI confirmed:

> each Exodus section likely contains instructions leading toward the next

---

Meaning Snowy intentionally structured the diary like a chain.

---

A trail.

---

Not toward survival.

---

But toward understanding his mental state.

---

Or perhaps—

toward wherever he believed he was going.

# **IX. FINAL NOTE**

---

The diary was warm when they found it.

---

Nobody could explain why.

---

Page after page,

it contained the thoughts of a child who believed disappearing was kindness.

---

Not because he stopped loving people.

---

---

coded chapters,

and trembling handwriting,

---

A roadmap.

---

Not toward where he went physically—

but toward the moment he finally decided

he no longer belonged in tomorrow.

---

While outside,

Whitefall’s storm continued endlessly.

---

And somewhere beneath it,

a tiny child with sapphire eyes

might still be writing new Exodus chapters alone—

one page closer

to becoming someone the world could forget forever.
