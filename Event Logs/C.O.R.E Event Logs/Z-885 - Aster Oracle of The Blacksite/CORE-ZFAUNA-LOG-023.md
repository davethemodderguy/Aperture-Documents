# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-023**

### **RESIDENTIAL RECORD — ROOM N-07 GROUP PHOTOGRAPH**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – INTERNAL / ARCHIVAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

For the first time since Room N-07's occupants began living together, everyone decided to take a proper group photograph.

It took considerably longer than expected.

Mostly because one resident refused to stay awake.

---

# **PREPARATION**

The common room was quickly rearranged.

A tripod was borrowed from the Media Department.

Someone found a remote shutter.

Someone else remembered to clean the camera lens.

The only remaining challenge...

Was organizing everyone.

---

## **Front Row**

**Neko**

Sat cross-legged in the center, still wearing his usual collar and with the folded form of **Neko's Claws** resting against the back of his chair like an unusually thick white staff.

---

**Miss Neko**

Sat beside her younger brother with one arm comfortably around his shoulders.

She smiled naturally.

---

**Taiga**

Still recovering well, she sat comfortably in front of everyone with her white scarf wrapped neatly around her neck.

The darker stripes across her skin and hair were now clearly visible, while the black restraining straps rested around both forearms.

Her snowflake earrings reflected the room's lights.

Her fluffy tail curled comfortably around her side.

---

**Aster**

Sat beside Taiga on the rug.

She held her whiteboard toward the camera.

Written across it was:

> **Smile!**

---

## **Back Row**

**Bao**

Towered happily behind everyone.

Her whale tail swayed lazily behind her as she laughed at something off-camera.

One arm rested gently on Frost's shoulder.

---

**Frost**

The young goddess stood calmly beside her mother.

Her cool aura lightly frosted the floor beneath her boots.

She wore her usual composed smile.

---

**Star**

Almost impossible to miss.

The glowing nine-year-old practically illuminated the photograph by herself.

Her brilliant wings shimmered softly behind the group.

She grinned enthusiastically at the camera.

---

**Nightshade**

Standing beside Star, arms folded as always.

Dark.

Quiet.

Reserved.

Despite her usual serious demeanor...

A careful observer might notice the faintest hint of a smile.

Very faint.

---

**Niko**

Having come from Whitefall Lake to visit his siblings, the ghostly brother stood behind Neko and Miss Neko.

He gave the camera a relaxed wave.

---

## **Special Guests**

**Leo**

No one needed to decide where Leo should be.

He had already chosen.

The tiny banana snake slept peacefully around Taiga's shoulders, completely unaware that a photograph was about to be taken.

Attempts to wake him failed.

The decision was made to simply leave him asleep.

---

# **THE PHOTO**

The countdown began.

> "Three..."

Everyone looked toward the camera.

> "Two..."

Star nearly started laughing.

Nightshade quietly reminded her to hold still.

> "One..."

Click.

The flash went off.

Leo did not wake up.

---

# **AFTERWARD**

The photograph was reviewed on the camera's display.

It came out surprisingly well.

Star looked excited.

Bao was laughing.

Frost looked composed.

Nightshade somehow managed to appear intimidating even in a family photo.

Taiga's ears were perked up happily.

Neko and Miss Neko looked completely relaxed.

Niko's ghostly outline shimmered softly behind them.

Leo remained asleep.

Only one problem remained.

---

Aster.

Just before the shutter fired...

She had looked down to make sure her whiteboard was straight.

As a result, the photograph captured only the top of her head and the words:

> **Smile!**

Everyone burst into laughter.

Aster looked at the picture.

Looked at everyone else.

Then silently erased the board.

She wrote something new.

> **Again?**

Everyone immediately agreed.

---

## **ARCHIVAL DESCRIPTION**

**Individuals present, left to right:**

- Frost, Goddess of Ice.
    
- Bao, Goddess of Clouds and Water, bearing distinct whale-like traits.
    
- Star, a radiant and energetic nine-year-old Aegis of Light.
    
- Nightshade, a quiet and mysterious ten-year-old companion to Star.
    
- Taiga, the growing snow leopard girl wearing her white scarf and power restrainers.
    
- Miss Neko and Neko, the cat-girl and cat-boy siblings.
    
- Niko, their ghostly brother visiting from Whitefall Lake.
    
- Leo, the young Starladian banana snake, asleep as usual.
    
- Aster, the prophetic white cat capable of reading, writing, and glimpsing the future, holding her trademark whiteboard.
    

The finished photograph was framed and hung just outside Room N-07.

A small plaque beneath it simply reads:

> **"Roommates."**

Nothing more needed to be written.
