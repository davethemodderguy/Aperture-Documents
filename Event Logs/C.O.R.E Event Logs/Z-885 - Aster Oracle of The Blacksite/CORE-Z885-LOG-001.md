# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-001**

### **A DAY WITHOUT HER CARETAKERS**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL OBSERVATION

---

## **I. STATUS REPORT**

For the first time since becoming a permanent resident of the Blacksite...

Every one of Aster's primary caretakers was occupied simultaneously.

- Neko — Emergency maintenance.
    
- Miss Neko — Logistics support.
    
- Mirage — Assisting Neko with repairs.
    
- Star — N.O.S.T. deployment.
    
- Nightshade — Containment operations.
    

Normally, at least one of them was free.

Today...

None were.

---

## **II. MORNING**

Aster walked to Engineering first.

The doors were closed.

Inside...

Neko and Mirage were repairing a ruptured coolant manifold.

Both noticed her through the observation window.

Neko smiled.

He waved.

---

### Neko

> "Morning, Aster!"

---

She meowed back.

Held up her marker.

Wrote against the glass.

> **Busy?**

---

Mirage nodded apologetically.

---

### Mirage

> "...Busy."

---

Neko looked genuinely disappointed.

---

### Neko

> "...I'm sorry."

---

Aster erased her message.

Then wrote another.

> **Okay.**

---

She smiled.

Turned around.

And quietly walked away.

---

## **III. A VERY POPULAR KITTEN**

It didn't take long for the rest of the Blacksite to notice.

The Oracle...

Was wandering alone.

Engineers greeted her.

Researchers stopped to pet her.

Security officers scratched behind her ears as she passed.

She happily accepted every bit of attention.

But after each interaction...

She continued walking.

Still looking.

---

## **IV. HER DUTIES**

Even without her caretakers...

Aster continued working.

She stopped a researcher from entering a laboratory moments before a pressure valve burst.

She gently nibbled an intern's sleeve before he stepped onto a loose catwalk panel.

She threw her marker at an engineer who was about to connect the wrong electrical cable.

---

### Engineer

> "...Ow."

---

Aster calmly wrote.

> **Would have hurt more.**

---

The engineer quietly admitted she had a point.

---

## **V. LUNCH**

Usually...

Lunch meant sitting with Neko.

Or curled up beside Miss Neko.

Sometimes on Mirage's shoulder.

Today...

She carried her marker into the cafeteria.

Sat alone on a chair.

Looked around.

The room was full.

Everyone smiled at her.

Several people invited her over.

She politely declined each time.

Writing the same answer.

> **Waiting.**

---

## **VI. THE AFTERNOON**

Hours passed.

Work finally began slowing down.

The Engineering alarm ceased.

Containment reports were completed.

Research concluded its experiments.

One by one...

People became available again.

The first person to emerge from Engineering...

Was Neko.

His uniform was covered in grease.

Mirage followed, carrying a toolbox.

The moment Aster spotted them...

She teleported.

Landing squarely on Neko's shoulder.

---

### Neko

> "...Hey."

---

She immediately bumped her forehead against his cheek.

Twice.

Then climbed onto Mirage's shoulder.

Repeated the exact same thing.

---

### Mirage

> "...Hello to you too."

---

## **VII. MISS NEKO**

Miss Neko rounded the corner moments later.

She immediately understood.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Were you waiting all day?"

---

Aster nodded.

---

Miss Neko crouched beside her.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Sorry."

---

Aster quickly shook her head.

She wrote:

> **Work first.**

---

Then, after a brief pause, she added:

> **Missed you anyway.**

---

Miss Neko smiled sadly.

She gently rubbed behind Aster's ears.

The tiny kitten immediately began purring.

---

## **VIII. THAT EVENING**

That night...

No one let Aster out of their sight.

Neko carried her through Engineering.

Mirage handled the heavier maintenance jobs.

Miss Neko took over whenever Neko needed both hands.

Star and Nightshade, finally free from their own work, each made time to visit her before ending their shifts.

Aster didn't seem upset about the busy day.

She simply wanted everyone together again.

And now...

They were.

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Personnel noted that Aster handled the day with remarkable maturity. Despite being left without her usual companions for several hours, she continued carrying out her duties, accepted kindness from other staff, and patiently waited until her caretakers became available again.

Before curling up to sleep in Neko's lap that evening, she wrote one final message.

> **I can wait.**

Neko read it.

He gently stroked between her ears.

---

### Neko

> "...Thanks for waiting."

---

Aster looked up.

Her tail swayed once.

She added one last sentence.

> **Family is worth waiting for.** 🐾
