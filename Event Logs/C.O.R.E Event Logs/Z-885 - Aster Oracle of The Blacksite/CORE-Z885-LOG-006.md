# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-006**

### **WITHDRAWAL**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE INCIDENT / HIGH PRIORITY

---

## **I. INCIDENT SUMMARY**

Following the behavioral changes documented in **CORE-Z885-LOG-005**, Aster's routine changed again.

This time...

She stopped leaving notes.

She stopped waiting in hallways.

She stopped observing work from a distance.

She simply...

Disappeared.

Not from the Blacksite.

From everyone.

---

## **II. INITIAL RESPONSE**

At first, personnel assumed she was simply hiding as before.

Security searched her usual resting places.

Nothing.

Engineering checked the maintenance tunnels she liked exploring.

Nothing.

N.O.S.T. scanned for anomalous displacement.

Nothing unusual.

She was still inside the Blacksite.

She simply refused to stay where anyone could reach her.

---

## **III. TELEPORTATION**

Whenever someone finally located her...

The same sequence occurred.

Someone would quietly approach.

Not rushing.

Not calling loudly.

Just...

Trying to spend a moment with her.

Before they came within reach...

A small white flash.

Aster was gone.

---

### Engineer

> "...Aster?"

_Flash._

Empty hallway.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Little one..."

_Flash._

Empty observation room.

---

### Star

> "...Please."

_Flash._

Only a few white hairs remained where she had been sitting.

---

## **IV. NEKO**

Neko eventually found her asleep beneath an unused staircase.

He didn't move.

He simply sat several meters away.

Waiting.

Nearly twenty minutes passed.

Aster slowly opened her eyes.

She saw him.

For a brief second...

Neither of them moved.

Neko gave a small wave.

---

### Neko

> "...Hi."

---

Aster lowered her ears.

A white flash filled the room.

She was gone.

Neko remained seated long after the light faded.

---

## **V. MIRAGE**

Mirage had more success.

Only because he could predict where she was likely to teleport next.

Eventually...

He appeared in a quiet storage room just moments after she did.

She looked surprised.

Then...

Prepared to teleport again.

---

### Mirage

> "...I'm not stopping you."

---

She hesitated.

---

### Mirage

> "...If you want to leave..."

> "...You can."

---

Another pause.

---

### Mirage

> "...I just wanted to see if you were okay."

---

For the first time in weeks...

Aster picked up her marker.

She stared at it.

Then slowly put it back down.

Without writing a single word.

A white flash.

She disappeared.

The marker remained on the floor.

Mirage quietly picked it up.

---

## **VI. OBSERVATIONS**

Over the following weeks...

Aster continued performing only one task.

Saving lives.

Security footage confirmed she still:

- Warned personnel of imminent danger.
    
- Prevented catastrophic accidents.
    
- Guided lost interns away from hazardous areas.
    

But the moment the danger had passed...

She vanished before anyone could thank her.

She no longer accepted pets.

No longer accepted treats.

No longer stayed long enough for conversation.

---

## **VII. PERSONNEL EFFECTS**

The atmosphere throughout the Blacksite changed.

Engineers who had once promised themselves they would "say hello tomorrow" now found empty corridors.

Researchers began leaving small bowls of water and approved treats in quiet places.

Most remained untouched.

Neko carried Aster's favorite toy mouse in his tool bag.

He never found an opportunity to give it back.

Miss Neko continued setting an extra place beside her during breaks.

The place stayed empty.

Even Oracle's tiny bed in Engineering remained undisturbed.

---

## **VIII. DIRECTOR'S REMARK**

Director Lucious declined to classify Aster as missing.

Instead, he added the following note:

> **"She knows exactly where we are. The problem is that she no longer believes we have time for her."**

No additional comment was written.

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Aster remains an active protector of the Blacksite.

She continues fulfilling every aspect of her duty.

She simply refuses every attempt at companionship.

The last confirmed close-up camera image of her shows a small white kitten sitting alone on a high maintenance catwalk, overlooking Engineering from above.

She watched until Neko safely completed his repair.

As he gathered his tools and turned toward the exit...

Aster quietly teleported away.

He never knew she had been there.

No notes have been recovered.

No messages have been written.

For the first time since joining the Blacksite...

The Oracle has fallen completely silent. 🐾
