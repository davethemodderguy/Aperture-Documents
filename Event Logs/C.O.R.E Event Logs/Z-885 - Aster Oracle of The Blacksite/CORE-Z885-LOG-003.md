# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-003**

### **THE ORACLE WHO WAITED**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE REPORT

---

## **I. SUMMARY**

Over the following weeks, operational demands reached levels not seen in recent months.

Engineering worked around the clock.

Containment teams remained deployed.

Research entered continuous testing cycles.

Even routine breaks became shorter.

Without anyone intending it...

Aster slowly became less visible.

Not because people stopped caring.

But because everyone believed someone else would have time for her.

---

## **II. SMALL THINGS**

At first...

Nothing seemed unusual.

An engineer hurried past.

> "Sorry, Aster. Later."

A researcher smiled.

> "Busy today."

A security officer gave a quick wave without slowing down.

> "See you this evening."

Even Neko.

---

### Neko

> "...I'll play with you after I finish this pipe."

---

He meant every word.

The pipe burst again.

He never came back that afternoon.

---

## **III. WAITING**

Aster waited in Engineering.

Then outside Medical.

Then beside N.O.S.T.

She quietly watched people she loved walk past.

Every one of them smiled.

Every one of them was in a hurry.

She never followed.

She never interrupted.

She had learned that everyone was busy.

---

## **IV. HER WHITEBOARD**

Near the end of the day, an intern noticed Aster sitting by herself.

Her whiteboard lay beside her.

Normally it contained little drawings.

Today it simply read:

> **Everyone is busy.**

Below it...

Written much smaller.

> **I'll wait.**

---

## **V. THE NEXT DAY**

It happened again.

And again.

People still petted her occasionally.

Still called her by name.

But nobody stayed.

Nobody noticed she had begun sitting in the same quiet corner every afternoon.

The place where she could watch everyone pass.

---

## **VI. MIRAGE**

Mirage finally finished a maintenance shift late into the evening.

He expected to find Aster with Neko.

She wasn't.

With Miss Neko.

She wasn't.

With Star.

No.

Nightshade.

No.

He eventually found her in the quiet corner.

Curled into a tiny ball.

Wide awake.

Watching the hallway.

---

### Mirage

> "...Aster."

---

She looked up.

Her tail gave one slow wag.

She picked up her marker.

Wrote:

> **Finished?**

---

### Mirage

> "...Yes."

---

She nodded.

Then erased it.

After several seconds...

She wrote another sentence.

> **Did I do something wrong?**

---

Mirage froze.

---

### Mirage

> "...No."

---

Aster looked down.

She wrote again.

> **Everyone stopped staying.**

---

Mirage had no answer.

He simply sat beside her.

---

## **VII. THEY DIDN'T KNOW**

The following morning, Mirage quietly showed Neko the whiteboard.

Neko read it once.

Then again.

His ears lowered.

---

### Neko

> "...She thought it was her..."

---

Miss Neko covered her mouth.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Oh no..."

---

Star quietly looked away.

Nightshade closed her eyes.

None of them had ignored Aster on purpose.

Each of them thought...

"I'll spend time with her tomorrow."

Tomorrow had become weeks.

---

## **VIII. THE APOLOGY**

That evening...

The five caretakers met Aster together.

She looked surprised.

Neko knelt in front of her.

---

### Neko

> "...We're sorry."

---

Miss Neko gently stroked behind her ears.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You didn't do anything wrong."

---

Star smiled sadly.

---

### Star

> "...We were so focused on work..."

---

Nightshade finished the sentence.

---

### Nightshade

> "...That we forgot someone important."

---

Mirage placed the whiteboard back in front of Aster.

---

### Mirage

> "...We won't promise to never be busy."

---

He gently scratched beneath her chin.

---

> "...But we'll stop making you wait alone."

---

Aster stared at each of them.

For several quiet seconds.

Then she slowly wrote:

> **Okay.**

She paused.

Then added:

> **I missed everyone.**

---

Without another word...

Neko picked her up.

She immediately buried her face against his shoulder and began purring.

Miss Neko rubbed behind her ears.

Star smiled.

Nightshade gently scratched under her chin.

Mirage rested a hand lightly on her head.

For the first time in weeks...

Aster wasn't waiting anymore.

---

## **IX. PERSONNEL REMINDER**

Following the incident, Director Lucious issued a welfare notice to all departments:

> **"The Blacksite runs because people work together. That includes looking after each other. If you see Aster waiting alone, and you have a minute to spare, stop and say hello. Sometimes thirty seconds is enough to remind someone they haven't been forgotten."**

---

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Aster never stopped helping the Blacksite.

She never stopped warning people of danger.

She never stopped believing her family would come back.

She was simply saddened by the silence.

Since this incident, her caretakers have made a point of checking in with her throughout the day whenever operationally possible, even if only for a brief greeting or a few moments together before returning to work.
