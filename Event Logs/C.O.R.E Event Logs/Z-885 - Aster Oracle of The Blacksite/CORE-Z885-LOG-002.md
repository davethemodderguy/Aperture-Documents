# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-002

### **THE QUIET KITTEN**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE OBSERVATION

---

## **I. STATUS REPORT**

Operational demands across the Blacksite continued to increase.

Emergency repairs.

Containment inspections.

Research deadlines.

Interdepartmental transfers.

Everyone was busy.

Again.

Unlike the previous occurrence, however...

Personnel had gradually grown accustomed to seeing Aster wandering the facility by herself.

At first, employees would stop to greet her.

Scratch behind her ears.

Read whatever she had written.

Now...

Many simply smiled as they walked past.

Some didn't even notice her.

---

## **II. THE HALLWAYS**

Aster sat beside one of the main corridors with her marker.

Every few minutes, someone hurried by.

---

### Engineer

> "Morning, Aster."

He didn't stop.

---

### Researcher

> "Sorry, little one. Busy."

She kept walking.

---

### Security Officer

> "Maybe later."

---

Aster simply nodded each time.

She never asked anyone to stay.

---

## **III. STILL WORKING**

Despite feeling increasingly unnoticed...

She never abandoned her duties.

She tapped an intern's boot before he stepped onto a wet floor.

She blocked a scientist's path until they remembered an important document.

She threw her marker at an engineer reaching for the wrong valve.

---

### Engineer

"...Thanks."

He hurried away before she could write anything back.

---

Aster quietly retrieved her marker herself.

---

## **IV. THE NOTEBOOK**

Later that afternoon...

She found an empty maintenance notebook.

Opening to the first page, she wrote:

> **Today was quiet.**

On the next line:

> **Everyone is busy.**

Another line.

> **I hope work went well.**

She paused.

Then closed the notebook.

---

## **V. MIRAGE**

Late in the evening, Mirage finally finished assisting Neko.

As he crossed the corridor...

He noticed a very familiar white kitten asleep on a bench.

Her marker rested beside her paw.

He picked up the notebook lying next to her.

Inside were only three short sentences.

He read them once.

Closed the notebook.

Then quietly sat beside her.

He didn't wake her.

He simply waited.

Several minutes later...

Aster slowly opened her eyes.

She looked at Mirage.

Then wrote:

> **Finished?**

---

### Mirage

> "...Finished."

---

She looked around.

---

> **Neko?**

---

### Mirage

> "...Still working."

---

Aster nodded.

No complaint.

No disappointment.

Just another nod.

---

## **VI. WHEN NEKO ARRIVED**

Almost an hour later...

Neko finally entered the hallway, exhausted and covered in dust.

The first thing he saw...

Was Aster asleep against Mirage's side.

The second...

Was the notebook.

He opened it.

Read the three sentences.

He looked down at Aster for a long moment.

---

### Neko

> "...She's been alone again."

---

Mirage nodded.

---

### Mirage

> "...She understands."

---

A pause.

---

### Mirage

> "...But understanding doesn't mean she doesn't miss us."

---

Neko quietly closed the notebook.

Very gently...

He picked Aster up.

She instinctively curled against him without waking.

---

## **VII. STAFF NOTICE**

The following morning, Director Lucious issued a brief reminder to all personnel.

> **Operational priorities come first, but don't forget the people—and kitten—who make this place feel alive. If you pass Aster in the hallway, taking thirty seconds to say hello won't delay your work, but it might make her whole day.**

The notice spread quickly through the Blacksite.

Many employees quietly realized...

They had been walking past her more often than they meant to.

---

## **VIII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Aster never demanded attention.

She never complained.

She simply waited, trusted that her family would return, and continued helping others in the meantime.

That evening, someone found a new message on the whiteboard she often carried.

> **Busy is okay.**

Below it, in much smaller writing:

> **Please don't forget to say hello.** 🐾
