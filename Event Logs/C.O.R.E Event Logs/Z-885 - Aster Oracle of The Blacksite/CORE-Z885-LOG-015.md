# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-015**

### **BEHAVIORAL CHANGE OBSERVATION — LEO**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – INTERNAL / VETERINARY REPORT

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following Incident 014, Veterinary Services have documented a noticeable and persistent change in Leo's behavior.

While physically healthy...

He no longer behaves like the carefree juvenile snake first introduced to the Blacksite.

---

# **I. OBSERVED CHANGES**

Before Incident 014, Leo spent an estimated **18–20 hours per day asleep**.

Personnel frequently joked that he was "professionally unconscious."

Since the incident...

That figure has dropped significantly.

Leo now sleeps only in short intervals.

Even while resting, he rarely enters deep sleep.

---

# **II. HYPERVIGILANCE**

Veterinary staff observed Leo reacting to minor environmental changes that previously never disturbed him.

Examples include:

- Doors opening.
    
- Nearby footsteps.
    
- Sudden voices.
    
- Mechanical tools activating.
    
- Neko transforming between forms.
    

Each time, Leo immediately lifts his head and surveys his surroundings.

Only after confirming everything is safe does he relax again.

---

# **III. THE TRANSFORMATION**

The most noticeable trigger remains Neko's transformation.

Whenever thick white smoke begins to form...

Leo instinctively uncoils.

His body becomes tense.

His tongue flicks rapidly as he attempts to identify where "his cat" has gone.

Even after realizing it is simply Neko changing forms...

He remains alert for several minutes before relaxing.

Medical believes the confusion experienced during Incident 014 left a lasting impression on the young snake.

---

# **IV. STAR**

Star noticed the changes almost immediately.

One evening, Leo was wrapped around her wrist.

Normally asleep.

Instead...

He repeatedly looked around the room every few seconds.

Star quietly stroked beneath his chin.

---

### Star

> "...You don't have to keep watch."

---

Leo looked at her.

Then slowly rested his head against her hand.

His eyes remained open.

---

# **V. ASTER**

Aster attempted to help in her own way.

She would often curl up beside Leo during naps.

Hoping he would relax.

Instead...

Leo chose to remain awake.

Watching the room.

Watching the doors.

Watching Aster.

Almost as though making sure nothing startled her either.

Aster eventually wrote on her whiteboard.

> **He protects.**

---

# **VI. NEKO**

Neko blamed himself.

Although everyone—including Star and Veterinary—reassured him it had simply been an accident, he became noticeably more careful around Leo.

Before every transformation, Neko now announces it.

---

### Neko

> "...Changing."

---

If Leo is nearby, Neko waits until Star or Aster has gently picked him up before transforming.

Leo has never been startled by the transformation again.

Even so...

He continues watching closely.

---

---

# **VIII. VETERINARY RECOMMENDATION**

Leo remains healthy.

No medication is recommended.

Instead, Veterinary advises:

- Maintaining predictable routines.
    
- Avoiding startling transformations nearby.
    
- Allowing Leo to choose where he rests.
    
- Providing quiet environments where he feels secure.
    

Recovery is expected to occur naturally over time.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Leo**

- Physical condition: Excellent.
    
- Behavioral condition: Increased vigilance following Incident 014.
    
- Sleep duration: Significantly reduced.
    
- Appetite: Unchanged (continues to steal Neko's sandwiches whenever possible).
    

---

**Closing Observation**

During the latest overnight observation, Leo was found awake, loosely wrapped around Aster's shoulder.

Aster had fallen asleep hours earlier.

Leo quietly watched the corridor.

Only after the night patrol passed and everything became still did he finally close his eyes.

For the first time that night...

He slept.
