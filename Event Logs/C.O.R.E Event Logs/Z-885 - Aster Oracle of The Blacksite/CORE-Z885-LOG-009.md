# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-009**

### **RECOVERY OF DESIGNATION "STAR"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – INTERNAL / N.O.S.T. / MEDICAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

After months listed as **Missing**, Star has been recovered.

Recovery occurred without resistance.

Without pursuit.

Without incident.

The circumstances surrounding her disappearance remain unresolved.

---

# **I. RECOVERY**

Star was located during a routine inspection of an abandoned maintenance sector beneath the eastern containment wing.

The sector had been searched numerous times during the initial investigation.

This time...

She was there.

Curled tightly in her cat form beneath an inactive coolant manifold.

She did not react when the inspection team entered.

She did not run.

She did not transform.

She simply opened her eyes.

Looked at the personnel.

Then closed them again.

Medical staff approached slowly.

She offered no resistance.

---

# **II. MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

Upon arrival at Medical, Star voluntarily returned to her human form.

Her physical condition was stable.

Minor weight loss.

Mild dehydration.

No significant injuries.

Psychological evaluation painted a far different picture.

The anomalous suppression affecting her positivity had already been reversed weeks earlier when Aster unknowingly restored Neko's and Star's suppressed traits.

However...

The return of the suppressed trait did not erase everything that had accumulated during those six weeks.

Star's positivity had returned.

But it now struggled beneath months of isolation, fear, and emotional exhaustion.

Medical summarized the condition as follows:

> **"The anomaly has ended. The consequences remain."**

---

# **III. INTERVIEW**

Director Lucious requested a private conversation.

The transcript was partially declassified.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...We're glad you're alive."

---

Star remained silent.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Will you come home?"

---

Several seconds passed.

---

### Star

> "...I don't think I can."

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Why?"

---

Star lowered her head.

---

> "...Everything is different."

---

Another pause.

---

> "...Aster's gone."

---

Silence filled the room.

---

> "...I know everyone says it wasn't her fault."

> "...I know."

---

She looked toward the window.

---

> "...But when I think about coming back..."

> "...all I can think about..."

Another pause.

---

> "...is what's missing."

---

# **IV. RISK ASSESSMENT**

Although Star displayed no aggression and no intention to harm personnel, Medical determined she was not psychologically ready to return to unrestricted residence within the Blacksite.

She repeatedly declined residential placement.

Declined recreational activities.

Declined transfer to her former quarters.

She consistently requested only one thing.

> "...Please leave me alone."

---

# **V. CONTAINMENT DIRECTIVE**

Following consultation between N.O.S.T., Medical, and Director Lucious, Star was temporarily redesignated under protective containment protocols.

The decision was documented as a **medical containment**, not a disciplinary measure.

Her containment suite was modified accordingly.

- Residential furnishings installed.
    
- Personal books transferred from her quarters.
    
- Access to art supplies authorized.
    
- Daily medical evaluations scheduled.
    
- Voluntary visitation only.
    
- No unnecessary restraints authorized.
    

The official purpose of containment was recorded as:

> **"To provide a secure, low-stimulation environment while psychological evaluation and recovery continue."**

---

# **VI. NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade was informed before the transfer.

She requested permission to visit.

Star declined.

Nightshade accepted the decision.

Before leaving, she quietly handed Medical a small package.

Inside was Star's favorite teacup.

No note.

No message.

Medical placed it on a shelf inside the containment suite.

Star looked at it for a long time.

She never moved it.

---

# **VII. DIRECTOR'S ENTRY**

Director Lucious personally signed the containment order.

Beneath his signature, he added a handwritten clarification.

> **"Containment is not punishment."**

> **"She is here because she has nowhere she feels able to be."**

> **"The door will remain locked for safety."**

> **"It will also remain ready to unlock the day she asks to walk out."**

---
