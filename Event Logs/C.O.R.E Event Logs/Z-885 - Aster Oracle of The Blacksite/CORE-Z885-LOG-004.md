# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-004**

### **THE IMPOSSIBLE SCHEDULE**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – HIGH PRIORITY PERSONNEL REVIEW

---

## **I. INCIDENT SUMMARY**

Over the course of several weeks, an unusual pattern emerged.

Not in containment.

Not in Engineering.

Not in Research.

But in the routines of the Blacksite itself.

Every member of Aster's immediate family and caretakers found themselves occupied to such an extent that even a brief interaction became nearly impossible.

Unlike previous busy periods...

This one was absolute.

Neko couldn't spare thirty seconds.

Miss Neko was constantly reassigned.

Mirage remained wherever Neko was needed.

Star and Nightshade were deployed almost continuously.

Even Director Lucious rarely left meetings.

---

## **II. COMPARISON TO A PREVIOUS INCIDENT**

The similarity was impossible to ignore.

Years earlier...

An administrator had secretly manipulated work schedules, unintentionally isolating Neko from nearly everyone he cared about.

The consequences had been severe.

This felt...

Uncomfortably familiar.

An investigation was immediately authorized.

---

## **III. INVESTIGATION RESULTS**

Every department was examined.

Maintenance schedules.

Security rosters.

Research priorities.

Containment alerts.

Communications.

Digital records.

Personnel transfers.

Access logs.

Nothing.

No falsified documents.

No altered schedules.

No missing records.

No hidden administrator.

No anomalous influence.

No memetic effects.

No hostile entities.

No evidence of deliberate sabotage.

Every assignment had been issued for legitimate operational reasons.

The workload was simply...

Real.

---

## **IV. RESEARCH NOTES**

N.O.S.T. independently searched for anomalous causes.

Spatial distortions.

Temporal drift.

Probability manipulation.

Reality interference.

Psychological effects.

Every test returned negative.

Star summarized the findings.

---

### Star

> "...There's nothing here."

---

Nightshade read the final report.

Closed the folder.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Which somehow makes this worse."

---

## **V. ASTER**

Meanwhile...

Aster continued her daily routine.

She still warned personnel of danger.

She still carried her marker.

She still greeted everyone.

But eventually...

She stopped waiting in the hallways.

Instead...

She began quietly sitting wherever her caretakers happened to be working.

If Neko was repairing a pipe...

She would sit several meters away.

If Star was analyzing data...

She would nap beneath the console.

If Miss Neko was organizing logistics...

She would quietly watch from a nearby chair.

She never interrupted.

She never asked for attention.

She was simply...

Nearby.

---

## **VI. THE FIRST THING PEOPLE NOTICED**

One evening...

Neko finally looked around after finishing nearly twelve consecutive hours of work.

Aster was asleep.

Still sitting exactly where she'd been that morning.

Waiting.

Not for playtime.

Not for pets.

Just...

Waiting until he was finished.

Neko slowly knelt beside her.

He reached out to stroke behind her ears.

His communicator beeped.

Another emergency.

He stopped.

Looked at Aster.

Then at the communicator.

For several long seconds.

Finally...

He stood.

---

### Neko

> "...I'm sorry."

---

He hurried away.

Aster quietly watched him disappear around the corner.

Then lowered her head onto her paws.

---

## **VII. PERSONNEL RESPONSE**

As more staff noticed the pattern...

Morale subtly declined.

Not because of Aster herself.

But because everyone recognized the same feeling.

Everyone wanted to stop.

Nobody realistically could.

Engineers began leaving tiny notes beside her.

Researchers left treats approved by Medical.

Security officers quietly checked on her while making rounds.

They were gestures of kindness.

But they weren't company.

---

## **VIII. DIRECTOR'S REVIEW**

Director Lucious personally reviewed every report.

He requested a second investigation.

Then a third.

Then an independent audit.

All three reached the same conclusion.

Nothing was manipulating the Blacksite.

Nothing was forcing people apart.

The Blacksite was simply operating at its limits.

He closed the final report.

Sat silently for several minutes.

Then wrote one sentence beneath the conclusion.

> **"Sometimes there isn't an enemy to defeat. Sometimes everyone is simply carrying too much."**

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Unlike the isolation Neko once endured, no malicious actor could be identified.

There was no conspiracy.

No anomaly.

No hidden hand.

Only an accumulation of legitimate responsibilities that left almost no room for rest or companionship.

Aster appeared to understand this better than anyone.

She never blamed her family.

She never stopped loving them.

She simply stayed close whenever she could, content to share the same room even if no one had time to speak.

One evening, an engineer found a message written neatly on her whiteboard.

> **I know everyone is trying.**

Beneath it, after a long pause, she had added one more sentence.

> **I still miss them.** 🐾
