# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-011**

### **RETURN OF Z-885 "ASTER"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – INTERNAL / CASE CLOSED

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

After several months living as a stray in New York City, **Z-885 "Aster"** has voluntarily returned to the Blacksite.

Recovery occurred peacefully.

No force was used.

No containment measures were required.

Director Lucious was the only person present during her return.

---

# **I. THE RETURN**

Director Lucious encountered Aster sitting beneath the familiar bus stop where they had first met.

She appeared noticeably healthier than previous observations, having recently been fed by several local residents who had grown fond of the unusually intelligent white kitten.

As Lucious approached...

Aster didn't teleport away.

She simply looked up.

After several moments, she picked up a piece of cardboard and wrote.

> **...Can I come home?**

Lucious smiled.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...You never stopped having one."

---

Aster stared at the words.

Then quietly climbed into his lap.

For the first time since leaving the Blacksite...

She fell asleep.

---

# **II. MEDICAL EXAMINATION**

Upon returning, Medical immediately performed a full examination.

The majority of Aster's condition was exactly as expected.

- Mild malnutrition.
    
- Reduced body mass.
    
- Minor scars consistent with prolonged life as a stray.
    
- No serious illness.
    

One new injury, however, drew everyone's attention.

A small section of Aster's **right ear** was missing.

The wound had already healed naturally.

Medical determined it had most likely been caused by another stray animal during territorial conflict.

When questioned...

Aster simply wrote:

> **Another cat was hungry too.**

After a pause, she added:

> **It didn't hurt much.**

No further explanation was given.

---

# **III. STAR**

Medical requested that Aster visit Star's containment suite before any other formal procedures.

The moment Star saw the small white kitten standing in the doorway...

She froze.

For several seconds...

Neither moved.

Then Aster slowly walked forward.

She gently rubbed against Star's leg.

Exactly as she had done countless times before.

Star knelt.

Wrapped both arms around her.

And cried.

Not from fear.

Not from despair.

From relief.

Aster quietly purred.

---

Over the following days...

Star's recovery accelerated beyond every previous projection.

She willingly left her containment suite.

She accepted visitors.

She began eating with everyone again.

The panic attacks became less frequent.

Then...

Stopped entirely.

Lighting restrictions were gradually removed.

Within weeks...

Medical officially declared her psychologically stable enough to return to unrestricted residence.

---

# **IV. BACK TO WORK**

The Blacksite slowly returned to its familiar rhythm.

Star resumed her duties.

Her smile returned.

So did the quiet glow everyone had missed.

Neko immediately resumed teasing her during breaks.

Nightshade finally stopped making tea for one.

She returned to making two cups.

Neither acknowledged the change.

They simply sat together again.

Mirage declared the crisis officially over by somehow teleporting a birthday hat onto Director Lucious during a staff meeting.

No one ever discovered how.

---

# **V. ORACLE OF THE BLACKSITE**

Aster resumed her previous designation.

She once again wandered freely throughout the facility.

Warning personnel of danger.

Stopping catastrophic mistakes before they occurred.

Randomly choosing someone to accompany each day.

Engineers quickly noticed that machinery seemed to fail less often whenever Aster happened to be nearby.

Whether coincidence or precognition remained impossible to determine.

Her title was officially restored.

> **Oracle of the Blacksite**

---

# **VI. THE EAR**

Naturally...

Everyone noticed the missing piece of Aster's right ear.

Comments became almost routine.

---

### Neko

> "...It kind of makes you look tougher."

Aster proudly puffed out her chest.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You're still adorable."

Aster immediately forgot she was trying to look intimidating.

---

### Nightshade

"...Does it still hurt?"

Aster shook her head.

She wrote:

> **No.**

---

### Star

"...May I touch it?"

Aster nodded.

Star gently rubbed the healed edge of the ear before giving Aster a careful headpat.

---

### Mirage

"...Congratulations."

Everyone looked at him.

---

"...You've unlocked the Limited Edition Cat Skin."

Aster blinked.

Then wrote:

> **...What?**

The room erupted into laughter.

Even Director Lucious had to hide a smile.

---

### Director Lucious

Looking over the medical report.

> "...You came home with less ear than you left with."

Aster looked up.

He closed the file.

---

> "...I'd still call that a good trade."

---

# **VII. CASE STATUS**

**Star**

- Fully recovered.
    
- Returned to active duty.
    
- Containment designation removed.
    

**Aster**

- Returned voluntarily.
    
- Resumed duties as **Oracle of the Blacksite**.
    
- Medical condition stable.
    
- Missing section of right ear documented as a permanent cosmetic injury.
    

---

## **CASE CLOSURE**

Following review by Medical, N.O.S.T., and Director Lucious, the suppression incident was officially closed.

The final sentence entered into the file reads:

> **"Some wounds remained, some scars stayed, and one kitten came home with a missing piece of her ear. Even so, the Blacksite finally felt whole again."**
