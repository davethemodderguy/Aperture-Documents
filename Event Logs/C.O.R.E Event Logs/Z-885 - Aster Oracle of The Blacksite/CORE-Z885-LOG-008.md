# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-008**

### **FIELD OBSERVATION — Z-885 "ASTER"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – INTERNAL / RECOVERY OBSERVATION

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Despite the suspension of active recovery operations, Director Lucious continues making periodic visits to New York City during authorized business travel.

Officially, these visits are unrelated.

Unofficially...

Everyone knows he is looking for Aster.

---

# **I. CURRENT CONDITION**

Months of living as a stray have visibly affected Z-885.

Her once immaculate white fur has become dull.

Her body has become noticeably smaller.

Medical analysis attributes this not to aging...

But to prolonged malnutrition.

Although classified as an **Immortal-Class Entity**, Z-885 still requires food to maintain normal body condition.

She cannot die from starvation.

Instead...

She slowly becomes thinner.

Lighter.

Smaller.

Her body appears to conserve energy by reducing itself toward the smallest viable form.

The process is gradual.

But measurable.

---

# **II. SURVIVAL**

Aster has adapted to life on the streets.

She sleeps beneath parked vehicles.

Inside abandoned buildings.

Along steam vents during winter.

Beneath loading docks when it rains.

She avoids shelters.

Avoids animal control.

Avoids people.

She still conceals every anomalous ability she possesses.

When threatened, she flees like an ordinary stray whenever possible.

Teleportation is reserved only for situations where escape is otherwise impossible.

No civilian has reported witnessing anomalous activity.

Her secrecy remains remarkably disciplined.

---

# **III. DAILY LIFE**

Field observations document a routine shared by countless stray animals.

Searching through discarded food containers.

Waiting behind restaurants after closing.

Drinking collected rainwater.

Crossing streets between traffic.

Running from larger animals.

Occasionally finding a quiet place to sleep uninterrupted.

It is an existence devoted almost entirely to survival.

---

# **IV. HUMAN INTERACTION**

Not everyone ignores her.

Some pedestrians leave food.

Others gently try to approach.

She always leaves before they get close.

Others are less kind.

She has been chased away from storefront entrances.

Startled by loud noises.

Shouted at for wandering too close to outdoor dining areas.

On several occasions, security footage captured individuals frightening her for amusement.

Each time, she fled immediately.

Director Lucious ordered those recordings archived only as evidence of her living conditions.

No retaliation was authorized.

His only instruction was:

> **"Keep watching over her from a distance."**

---

# **V. THE VISITS**

Director Lucious has never succeeded in bringing Aster back.

He no longer asks.

Instead...

Whenever business brings him to New York, he visits the neighborhoods where she is most often reported.

Sometimes he sees her.

Sometimes he does not.

On the days she appears...

She never approaches first.

She watches from rooftops.

Fire escapes.

Alley entrances.

Always just beyond reach.

Lucious never walks toward her anymore.

He simply sits somewhere she can see him.

Sometimes on a park bench.

Sometimes outside a small café.

Sometimes beside the same bus stop where they first met.

He remains for an hour.

Then leaves.

No words are exchanged.

---

# **VI. A SMALL ROUTINE**

One rainy afternoon, Director Lucious placed a sealed container of cat food beneath the awning of an abandoned shop before taking a seat across the street.

He never looked back.

Nearly twenty minutes later...

A tiny white kitten emerged cautiously from an alley.

She examined the area.

Confirmed he was not approaching.

Then quietly ate.

When she finished...

She looked toward Lucious.

He simply gave a small nod.

She stared for several seconds.

Then disappeared into the city's maze of streets.

The empty container was the only sign she had been there.

---

# **VII. MEDICAL NOTE**

N.O.S.T. has confirmed the following regarding Immortal-Class physiology:

- Z-885 cannot die from starvation.
    
- Prolonged nutritional deprivation results in progressive loss of body mass.
    
- Adequate nutrition gradually restores normal size and condition.
    
- No permanent physiological damage has been observed.
    

Personnel are reminded that immortality does **not** prevent suffering or physical weakness.

---

# **VIII. DIRECTOR'S PERSONAL ENTRY**

The following handwritten note was attached separately from the official report.

It has not been edited.

> "Every time I visit, she's a little smaller."

> "She still hides her abilities."

> "She still refuses to come home."

> "But she still watches to make sure I leave safely before she disappears."

> "For someone who believes she no longer belongs with us..."

> "...she still worries about us."
