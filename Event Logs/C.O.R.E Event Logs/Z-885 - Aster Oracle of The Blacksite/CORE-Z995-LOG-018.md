# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-018**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION — ASTER & LEO**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – INTERNAL / RECREATIONAL LOG

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Veterinary and N.O.S.T. personnel have documented two notable developments involving **Z-885 "Aster"**.

1. Leo has become unusually attached to Aster.
    
2. Aster has demonstrated a previously unknown shapeshifting ability affecting her appearance.
    

Neither development poses any known risk.

---

# **I. LEO'S NEW FAVORITE PLACE**

For reasons currently unknown, Leo spent an entire day attached to Aster.

Not merely following her.

Attached.

From the moment Aster woke up until she went to sleep that evening...

Leo refused to leave.

He remained asleep almost the entire time.

Whenever Aster walked through the Blacksite, Leo was present.

Around her neck.

Wrapped around her foreleg.

Curled around her tail.

Sleeping on her back.

Even balanced on top of her head.

Whenever Aster teleported...

Leo somehow remained asleep.

He never slipped.

Never woke.

Personnel jokingly began referring to him as:

> **"Aster's living scarf."**

---

# **II. ASTER'S RESPONSE**

Aster appeared to enjoy the company.

Rather than moving Leo elsewhere, she adjusted everything she did around him.

She walked more carefully.

Teleported more gently.

Even delayed climbing onto higher shelves if doing so might disturb his nap.

When Leo finally woke late that evening...

He stretched.

Yawned.

Looked around.

Then immediately curled back around Aster's shoulder.

And fell asleep again.

Aster simply smiled.

---

# **III. A NEW ABILITY**

Later that afternoon, Neko noticed something unusual.

Aster...

Didn't quite look like herself.

Her posture.

Her size.

Her eyes.

All unchanged.

But...

Her fur wasn't white anymore.

Instead...

She had become a **Scottish Fold**, complete with neatly folded ears.

The silver hoop earring still rested on her right ear, making the change even more noticeable.

Aster tilted her head proudly.

Neko blinked.

---

### Neko

> "...Since when can you do that?"

---

Aster looked down at herself.

As though she had only just realized it.

She shrugged.

Then changed again.

---

# **IV. APPEARANCE TESTING**

Medical confirmed Aster's physiology remained completely unchanged.

Only her outward appearance shifted.

Throughout the afternoon, she experimented with several different looks.

---

### Form One

**Scottish Fold**

Soft folded ears.

Round face.

Calm expression.

Personnel described her as:

> "Dangerously adorable."

---

### Form Two

**Orange Tabby**

Bright orange fur with darker stripes.

Immediately recognized by nearly everyone.

---

### Bao

> "...She's orange now."

---

### Frost

> "...She has exactly one brain cell."

---

Aster immediately wrote:

> **Rude.**

---

### Form Three

**Calico**

A patchwork coat of white, orange, and black.

Neko stared for several moments.

Then smiled.

---

### Neko

> "...You copied me."

---

Aster nodded proudly.

She had.

---

# **V. LEO'S OPINION**

Interestingly...

Leo appeared completely unconcerned.

Whether Aster was white...

A Scottish Fold...

An orange tabby...

Or a calico...

He continued sleeping on her without hesitation.

Veterinary concluded that Leo recognized Aster by scent and familiarity rather than appearance.

---

### Star

> "...He doesn't care what she looks like."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...He just knows it's Aster."

---

# **VI. PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Nightshade

> "...The folded ears suit you."

---

### Lucy

> "Can you become every cat breed?"

---

Aster paused.

Thought carefully.

Then wrote:

> **Working on it.**

---

### Mirage

Looking at the orange tabby form.

> "...One orange cat."

He looked at Leo.

> "...One sleepy snake."

He nodded.

> "...Perfect."

No one understood his reasoning.

---

### Director Lucious

Reviewing the report.

> "...Another ability."

He looked toward Aster, who had once again become her familiar white self.

Leo remained asleep around her shoulders.

Lucious smiled faintly.

---

> "...As long as she's happy."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Aster**

- Continues serving as the **Oracle of the Blacksite**.
    
- Can now voluntarily alter her fur pattern and certain breed-specific physical traits while remaining biologically the same cat.
    
- Confirmed forms include:
    
    - White Cat (Default)
        
    - Scottish Fold
        
    - Orange Tabby
        
    - Calico
        

**Leo**

- Fully recovered.
    
- Returned to his exceptionally sleepy behavior.
    
- Has developed a temporary habit of remaining with Aster throughout the day, rarely leaving her side.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

Near the end of the day, security cameras captured Aster sitting on a windowsill overlooking the snow-covered Blacksite.

She had chosen her original white coat again.

Leo was asleep around her shoulders.

The setting sun reflected off the small silver earring on her right ear.

A passing researcher smiled.

> "...There she is."

The Oracle looked up.

Gave a slow blink.

Then returned to watching the snow fall as her tiny companion continued peacefully sleeping.
