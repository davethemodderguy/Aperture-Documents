# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-013**

### **INTRODUCTION OF NON-HOSTILE FAUNA — "LEO"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – INTERNAL / BIOLOGICAL RECORD

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

During Star's scheduled monthly visit to the Starlands, she returned with unexpected news.

Her long-missing pet had finally been found.

A juvenile male **Starladian Banana Snake**, named **Leo**.

After completing veterinary and anomalous health examinations, Director Lucious approved Leo's residence within the Blacksite as a registered companion animal under Star's care.

---

# **I. DESCRIPTION**

**Name:** Leo

**Species:** Starladian Banana Snake

**Sex:** Male

**Length:** Approximately **12 cm**

**Growth:** No measurable growth has been observed since arrival. Whether this is typical for the species remains unknown.

**Temperament:** Exceptionally docile.

Leo has displayed no aggressive behavior toward personnel.

Instead, he spends the overwhelming majority of his time sleeping.

---

# **II. VENOM**

Leo is technically venomous.

However, analysis determined the venom to be remarkably weak.

Documented effects include:

- Mild fever.
    
- Temporary insomnia.
    
- Minor localized swelling.
    

No life-threatening effects have been recorded.

Medical considers accidental bites to be a minor workplace injury.

Personnel are still instructed not to intentionally provoke Leo.

---

# **III. FIRST IMPRESSIONS**

Star appeared noticeably happier once Leo was returned to her.

She admitted she had avoided bringing him to the Blacksite previously because she believed he had disappeared permanently.

Upon introducing Leo to the staff...

She hesitated.

---

### Star

> "...Um..."

> "...He's a snake."

---

Silence followed.

---

Then Neko walked over.

Carefully held out his hand.

Leo immediately climbed onto his wrist.

Curled into a tiny spiral.

And fell asleep.

---

### Neko

> "...He's warm."

---

Miss Neko gently stroked Leo's tiny head.

---

### Miss Neko

> "He's adorable."

---

Star blinked.

---

> "...You're... not scared?"

---

Several nearby researchers looked confused.

One eventually answered.

---

### Senior Researcher

> "...We've contained eldritch beings, living shadows, and furniture that screams."

> "...A tiny sleepy snake doesn't really register."

---

The statement was unanimously agreed upon.

---

# **IV. PERSONNEL REACTIONS**

### Aster

Leo quickly became one of Aster's favorite companions.

She often allowed him to curl around her foreleg while she walked through the facility.

Occasionally...

He simply slept on top of her head.

She appeared very proud of this arrangement.

---

### Director Lucious

Leo was repeatedly found sleeping around Lucious' wrist during paperwork.

Lucious eventually stopped moving him.

Several meetings were conducted with a tiny snake comfortably asleep on his arm.

No one commented anymore.

---

### Neko

Leo developed an unfortunate habit.

Every time Neko sat down to eat...

Leo somehow appeared.

Within minutes...

One of Neko's sandwiches had vanished.

---

### Neko

> "...That's my lunch."

---

Leo continued swallowing the sandwich.

Without waking up.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...I think you've been adopted."

---

### Bao

Bao declared Leo...

> "...A noodle."

She refused to elaborate.

---

### Frost

Initially worried her naturally cold aura might bother him...

She instead discovered Leo enjoyed curling inside the hood of her jacket where it remained comparatively warmer.

---

### Nightshade

Leo frequently wrapped around Nightshade's wrist while she worked.

She simply continued writing reports one-handed.

---

### Lucy

Lucy spent nearly an hour trying to determine whether Leo was asleep...

Or simply pretending to be asleep.

Results remained inconclusive.

---

# **V. SLEEPING HABITS**

Leo appears capable of sleeping almost anywhere.

Confirmed sleeping locations include:

- Around Star's shoulders.
    
- Around Aster's neck.
    
- Wrapped around Neko's wrist while he worked.
    
- Hanging from laboratory hooks.
    
- Curled around chair legs.
    
- Inside empty mugs.
    
- Around Director Lucious' forearm during meetings.
    
- Coiled inside a toolbox, forcing Engineering to postpone maintenance until he woke up.
    

Medical confirms these behaviors are completely normal for his species.

---

# **VI. DIET**

Leo demonstrates an unusually broad diet.

He willingly consumes almost anything considered edible.

Fruit.

Meat.

Vegetables.

Cooked meals.

Emergency rations.

However...

He has developed an obvious preference.

Neko's sandwiches.

No matter where Neko leaves one...

Leo somehow finds it.

Engineering has officially classified this phenomenon as:

> **"An unavoidable loss."**

---

# **VII. ASTER & LEO**

One afternoon, personnel discovered Aster asleep in the observation lounge.

Leo had wrapped himself around the small silver hoop in her right ear.

Using it as an anchor.

Neither appeared bothered.

Medical photographed the scene before quietly leaving.

The image rapidly became one of the most circulated photographs within the Blacksite.

---

# **VIII. DIRECTOR'S COMMENT**

Following Leo's first month of residence, Director Lucious approved his permanent registration.

His final note reads:

> **"We prepared for venomous entities, reality distortions, and unknown biological hazards."**

> **"Instead, we received a twelve-centimeter snake whose greatest threat is stealing sandwiches."**

> **"Approved."**
