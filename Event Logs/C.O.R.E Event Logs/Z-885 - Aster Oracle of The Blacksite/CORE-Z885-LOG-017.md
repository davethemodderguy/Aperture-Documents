## **STATUS UPDATE**

Two long-standing mysteries regarding Leo have finally been resolved.

1. Why he repeatedly targeted Neko's sandwiches.
    
2. The extent of his unusual durability.
    

---

# **I. THE SANDWICH MYSTERY**

For months, personnel believed Leo simply enjoyed stealing Neko's lunches.

This assumption proved incorrect.

The discovery occurred entirely by accident.

During lunch, Neko prepared two sandwiches.

One contained tomatoes.

The other did not.

Leo ignored the tomato-less sandwich completely.

Instead...

He crawled directly onto the sandwich containing tomatoes.

Carefully removed only the tomato slices.

Ate them.

Then went to sleep beside the untouched sandwich.

The cafeteria fell silent.

---

### Neko

> "...You don't like my sandwiches."

---

Leo looked at him.

Slowly yawned.

Then fell asleep.

---

After repeated testing under Veterinary supervision, the conclusion became obvious.

Leo wasn't stealing sandwiches.

He was stealing...

The tomatoes.

---

# **II. FAVORITE FOOD**

Further dietary testing revealed an overwhelming preference.

Tomatoes.

Fresh tomatoes.

Cherry tomatoes.

Cooked tomatoes.

Tomato slices.

Tomato soup.

If it contains tomatoes...

Leo wants it.

His appetite for tomatoes far exceeds his interest in any other food.

Neko immediately adjusted his lunch.

From that day onward, he simply packed a small container of tomato slices alongside his sandwiches.

Leo happily accepted the arrangement.

The sandwiches were left untouched.

Engineering celebrated what they called:

> **"The Great Sandwich Peace Treaty."**

---

# **III. DURABILITY TESTING**

A separate veterinary examination produced an unexpected finding.

Leo's physiology possesses an anomalous defensive property.

Repeated testing under tightly controlled conditions demonstrated that Leo cannot be physically injured through conventional means.

Blunt force.

Sharp objects.

Extreme temperatures.

Compression.

Explosive overpressure.

Electrical discharge.

All produced...

No measurable damage.

Medical confirmed that Leo still experiences physical sensations and instinctively avoids danger, but his body itself remains completely unharmed.

Following extensive testing, N.O.S.T. assigned the following physiological designation:

> **Infinitely Indestructible**

Despite this...

Director Lucious immediately issued a clarification.

---

### Director Lucious

> "This is not permission to test that statement."

---

Further durability testing was suspended.
