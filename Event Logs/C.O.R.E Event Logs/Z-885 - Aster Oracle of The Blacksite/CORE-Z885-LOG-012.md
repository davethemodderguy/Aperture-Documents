# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-012**

### **UNAUTHORIZED ACCESSORY ACQUISITION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – INTERNAL / RECREATIONAL LOG

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following her return to the Blacksite, personnel noticed Aster showing an unexpected interest in accessories.

The trend began after Engineering presented her with a tiny silver earring designed specifically for the healed notch in her right ear.

Medical confirmed the piercing would not interfere with her anomalous physiology.

Aster immediately approved the idea.

---

# **I. THE EARRING**

The earring itself was intentionally simple.

A polished silver hoop sized specifically for a kitten.

Once fitted...

The missing piece of Aster's right ear became far less noticeable.

Instead...

It became part of her appearance.

Personnel reactions were overwhelmingly positive.

---

### Miss Neko

> "It actually suits you."

---

### Nightshade

> "...It's cute."

---

### Star

> "It looks like you've always had it."

---

Aster looked into a mirror for nearly five minutes.

Then proudly nodded.

---

# **II. THE COLLAR INCIDENT**

Approximately three hours later...

Neko discovered his collar was missing.

A Blacksite-wide search began under the assumption it had simply been misplaced during maintenance work.

The mystery lasted exactly eleven minutes.

Mirage found the culprit first.

Aster.

Sitting proudly on top of a filing cabinet.

Wearing Neko's collar.

The collar was obviously far too large.

It rested around her shoulders like an oversized necklace.

She appeared extremely pleased with herself.

---

### Mirage

"...Neko."

---

Neko looked up.

Immediately froze.

---

### Neko

"..."

"...Aster."

---

Aster looked directly at him.

Then slowly placed one paw over the collar.

As if claiming ownership.

---

# **III. THE CHASE**

Neko carefully approached.

---

### Neko

"...Can I have my collar back?"

---

Aster immediately teleported onto a bookshelf.

---

### Neko

"...Aster."

---

Another teleport.

---

"...Come on."

---

Another teleport.

---

"...It's mine."

---

Another teleport.

---

Within minutes...

Half of Engineering had stopped working to watch.

The pursuit spread into Logistics.

Then the cafeteria.

Then the residential wing.

Each time Neko got close enough to grab the collar...

Aster vanished in a small white flash.

Reappearing just out of reach.

Never dropping the collar.

Never slowing down.

---

# **IV. STAR'S CONTRIBUTION**

Star watched the entire chase from the hallway.

She smiled.

---

### Star

> "...I don't think she plans on giving it back."

---

Neko sighed dramatically.

---

### Neko

"...I noticed."

---

# **V. DIRECTOR LUCIUS**

Director Lucious eventually encountered Aster sitting comfortably atop a cabinet in Administration.

Still wearing the collar.

He looked at Aster.

Then at Neko.

Then back at Aster.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Did you ask nicely?"

---

### Neko

"...I've been asking nicely for twenty minutes."

---

Lucious looked toward Aster.

---

> "...Oracle."

---

Aster tilted her head.

---

> "...Borrowed things should eventually be returned."

---

Aster thought for several seconds.

She slowly removed the collar.

Everyone relaxed.

Then—

She teleported behind Lucious.

Placed the collar around **his** neck.

And teleported away again.

Administration staff had to excuse themselves to avoid laughing out loud.

Even Lucious paused before removing it.

---

### Director Lucious

"...She's learning."

---

# **VI. RECOVERY**

Nearly an hour later...

Aster quietly walked into Engineering.

She was no longer wearing the collar.

Instead...

It was neatly folded on top of Neko's workbench.

Beside it sat a handwritten note.

> **Borrowed.**

Beneath it...

A much smaller line.

> **Thank you.**

Neko picked up the collar.

Looked around for Aster.

She was already gone.

---

A tiny, unmistakable giggle echoed through the corridor.

Still proudly wearing her new silver earring.
