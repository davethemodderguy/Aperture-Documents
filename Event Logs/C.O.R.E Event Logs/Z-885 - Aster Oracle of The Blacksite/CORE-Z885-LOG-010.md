# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-010**

### **POST-RECOVERY STATUS REPORT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – INTERNAL / MEDICAL / N.O.S.T.

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Three weeks after Star's recovery, Medical has documented a previously unobserved symptom.

The condition is consistent.

Repeatable.

And severe.

---

# **I. PHOTIC PANIC RESPONSE**

Star has developed an acute panic response to direct, bright lighting.

The intensity of the light is the primary trigger.

Overhead floodlights.

Direct sunlight.

Inspection spotlights.

Emergency lighting.

All have produced the same response.

The first recorded incident occurred during a routine medical examination.

A ceiling light was increased to full brightness.

Within seconds...

Star froze.

Her breathing accelerated.

Her pupils constricted.

She instinctively covered her face before collapsing against the examination room wall.

Medical staff immediately dimmed the room.

The response subsided after several minutes.

No physical injury occurred.

---

# **II. DARKNESS**

Conversely...

Dark environments consistently produce the opposite effect.

Low light.

Soft illumination.

Shaded rooms.

Nighttime observation.

These conditions significantly reduce Star's distress.

Personnel have repeatedly observed her choosing the darkest available area in any room.

She now sleeps almost exclusively with the lights off.

Her containment suite has been modified accordingly.

Lighting remains permanently dim unless medically necessary.

---

# **III. CONTAINMENT MODIFICATIONS**

Star's containment suite has undergone extensive alterations.

- Bright overhead lighting removed.
    
- Adjustable indirect lighting installed.
    
- Windows fitted with blackout curtains.
    
- Emergency lighting converted to low-intensity red illumination.
    
- Hallway lighting outside her suite permanently reduced.
    

Medical staff now announce themselves before entering and avoid activating unnecessary lights.

Since these changes...

The frequency of panic attacks has decreased.

They have not stopped.

---

# **IV. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

N.O.S.T. psychologists believe the condition is not caused by the suppression anomaly itself.

Rather...

It is believed to have developed during the months Star spent hiding alone within the Blacksite.

Extended isolation.

Minimal exposure to bright environments.

Persistent psychological distress.

Together, these factors appear to have conditioned an involuntary fear response to sudden or intense light.

Treatment attempts have shown only limited success.

---

# **V. ASTER**

During multidisciplinary review, one conclusion repeatedly appeared.

Aster.

Not because of anomalous properties.

Not because she possesses a cure.

Because she was one of the last people Star consistently accepted before her disappearance.

Medical unanimously agreed that Aster's presence **might** help Star feel safe enough to begin recovering.

However...

The conclusion remains entirely theoretical.

Aster has refused every invitation to return.

No recommendation involving her can presently be implemented.

The report therefore lists the proposed intervention as:

> **Unavailable.**

---

# **VI. NEW YORK UPDATE**

Field observers assigned to passive welfare monitoring have reported an unexpected development.

Residents in several neighborhoods have begun recognizing Aster.

Not as an anomaly.

But as...

"The little white cat that writes."

Multiple witnesses have independently reported seeing the kitten respond to spoken English.

Requests such as:

> "Are you hungry?"

Produce clear behavioral responses.

On several occasions...

Aster has been observed picking up discarded pens, chalk, or charcoal and writing simple words on cardboard, pavement, or paper left within reach.

Messages remain brief.

Examples documented include:

> **Thank you.**

---

> **No.**

---

> **I'm okay.**

---

> **Sorry.**

Witnesses generally assume the writing is an elaborate trick.

Videos posted online have been widely dismissed as edited or staged.

No confirmed public awareness of Aster's anomalous nature has resulted.

Foundation and Blacksite information suppression protocols remain unnecessary.

---

# **VII. DIRECTOR'S FIELD NOTES**

Director Lucious continues periodic visits.

He no longer attempts to persuade Aster to return.

Instead...

He simply confirms she is alive.

One observation from his latest visit was entered into the official file.

A young girl offered Aster half of her sandwich.

Aster accepted it.

Before leaving...

She carefully wrote on the paper wrapper.

> **Thank you.**

The child smiled.

Assumed someone had trained the cat.

And walked away.

Aster watched until the girl disappeared into the crowd.

Only then did she finish eating.

---

# **VIII. CURRENT STATUS**

**Star**

- Physical condition stable.
    
- Suffers severe panic attacks when exposed to bright lighting.
    
- Darkness consistently reduces distress.
    
- Remains under medical containment.
    

**Aster**

- Voluntarily absent.
    
- Continues surviving independently in New York City.
    
- Local residents have discovered her unusual ability to understand spoken English and communicate through writing.
    
- Public perception remains that she is an exceptionally intelligent stray cat.
    
- Continues refusing all offers to return to the Blacksite.
    

---

**Medical Conclusion**

> **"Star is no longer missing, but she has not truly found her way back."**

> **"Aster is no longer hidden, but she has chosen a life where no one asks her to return."**

**Both case files remain active.**
