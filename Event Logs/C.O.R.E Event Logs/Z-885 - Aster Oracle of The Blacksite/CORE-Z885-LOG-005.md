# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-007**

### **SELF-IMPOSED ABSENCE**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE INCIDENT

---

## **I. INCIDENT SUMMARY**

Approximately one week after the events documented in **CORE-Z885-LOG-006**, personnel began noticing a different anomaly.

Aster was no longer where she usually was.

She was not in Engineering.

Not in N.O.S.T.

Not in Logistics.

Not following Security patrols.

At first, it was assumed she was simply exploring another wing of the Blacksite.

By the end of the day...

No one had seen her.

---

## **II. SEARCH**

Unlike previous occasions, Aster had not disappeared.

She had hidden.

Security footage eventually located her in a little-used observation alcove overlooking several maintenance corridors.

From there, she could see people working.

Almost nobody could see her.

She remained there for hours.

Quietly watching.

---

## **III. THE WHITEBOARD**

When Mirage finally found her, she did not appear frightened.

She simply looked up.

Picked up her marker.

And wrote:

> **You were working.**

---

Mirage knelt beside her.

---

### Mirage

> "...We were."

---

She continued writing.

> **I didn't want to interrupt.**

---

Another sentence.

> **Everyone finishes faster without me.**

---

Mirage stared at the board.

Those words felt painfully familiar.

---

## **IV. A FAMILIAR PATTERN**

When Neko read the message later that evening...

He froze.

Years earlier, after being unintentionally isolated, he had reached almost the same conclusion.

He had written:

> _"I slow people down."_

Now...

Aster had reached her own version.

Not because anyone had told her.

Not because anyone wanted her to believe it.

But because repeated experience had quietly taught her that people always seemed too busy.

---

## **V. DISCOVERY**

Over the next several days, Aster developed a routine.

She would quietly check on each of her caretakers.

Confirm they were safe.

Then leave before they noticed her.

If Neko looked up...

She was already gone.

If Miss Neko entered a room...

She had just left it.

Star occasionally found tiny paw prints on her paperwork.

Nightshade once discovered a neatly written note reading:

> **Everything looks okay.**

No signature was necessary.

Everyone knew who had left it.

---

## **VI. CARETAKERS**

One evening, all five caretakers met to discuss the situation.

---

### Neko

> "...She's doing what I used to do."

---

Miss Neko lowered her head.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...She thinks disappearing helps us."

---

Star quietly nodded.

---

### Star

> "...Because that's what she's seen."

---

Nightshade folded her arms.

---

### Nightshade

> "...She isn't hiding from us."

---

She looked toward the corridor where Aster had last been seen.

---

> "...She's hiding _for_ us."

---

Mirage finished the thought.

---

### Mirage

> "...She believes she's helping."

---

Silence followed.

None of them blamed Aster.

None of them blamed each other.

But all of them understood how she had arrived at that belief.

---

## **VII. DIRECTOR'S NOTE**

Director Lucious reviewed the incident and added a handwritten observation to the report.

> **"Neko once believed he made everyone less efficient. Aster has reached a different conclusion with the same destination—that removing herself is an act of kindness. Neither belief reflects reality, but both grew from circumstances, not malice."**

He further instructed supervisors to remind staff that brief welfare checks remain important, even during periods of heavy workload, whenever operationally feasible.

---

## **VIII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Aster continued carrying out her duties.

She still warned people of danger.

She still prevented accidents.

She still watched over the Blacksite.

She simply did it from farther away.

Most personnel never realized she had been nearby until they found a small message waiting after they finished their work.

One evening, Neko found a note tucked beneath his toolbox.

It read:

> **You looked busy.**

Below it...

In much smaller handwriting...

> **So I watched from here instead.**

And beneath that, after a long pause:

> **I'm glad everything went okay.** 🐾
