# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-016**

### **BEHAVIORAL RECOVERY — LEO**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – INTERNAL / VETERINARY REPORT

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following several weeks of observation, Veterinary Services have confirmed that Leo has fully recovered from the behavioral changes documented in Incident 015.

The heightened vigilance has subsided.

Leo has returned to behaving exactly as everyone remembers.

Namely...

Sleeping.

Almost constantly.

---

# **I. RECOVERY**

The transition was gradual.

Leo became less reactive each day.

He stopped checking every noise.

Stopped waking at every footstep.

Eventually...

He simply fell asleep again.

Wherever he happened to be.

Veterinary attributes the recovery to consistent routines, familiar caretakers, and the absence of further frightening incidents.

---

# **II. NAP LOCATIONS**

Leo has resumed his habit of sleeping in increasingly unusual places.

Documented locations this month include:

- Around Star's neck during paperwork.
    
- Wrapped around Aster's tail in her cat form.
    
- Around Neko's wrist while he repaired a generator.
    
- Hanging from Miss Neko's hoodie string.
    
- Curled inside Bao's hood.
    
- Resting atop Frost's shoulder despite the cold.
    
- Around Nightshade's pen while she was writing.
    
- Around Director Lucious' wrist during an executive meeting.
    

No one disturbed him.

---

# **III. NEKO**

Neko continues announcing before transforming as a precaution.

---

### Neko

> "...Changing."

---

Leo's response is now...

Absolutely nothing.

He usually sleeps through the transformation.

On one occasion, Neko transformed from cat to human while Leo was wrapped around him.

Leo briefly opened one eye.

Looked at Neko.

Closed it again.

And continued sleeping.

---

### Neko

> "...I think he finally trusts me again."

---

# **IV. ASTER**

Aster was perhaps the happiest to see Leo recover.

She frequently finds him asleep on top of her head, around her shoulders, or draped across her foreleg.

One afternoon she wrote:

> **Sleepy noodle.**

Leo did not respond.

He was asleep.

---

Star smiled during the latest veterinary checkup.

---

---

Leo responded by yawning.

Then immediately falling asleep in her hands.

The veterinarian noted:

> "This is the healthiest he has looked since arriving."

During routine maintenance, an engineer spent nearly fifteen minutes searching for a missing cable.

No one had the heart to move him.
