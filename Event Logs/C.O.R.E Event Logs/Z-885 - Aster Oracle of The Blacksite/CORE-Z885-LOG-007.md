# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-007**

### **RECOVERY OPERATION — Z-885 "ASTER"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – INTERNAL / RECOVERY FILE

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Nine days after Aster's departure, the first confirmed sighting was reported.

Not inside another facility.

Not in another anomaly.

Not hidden.

She had returned to where Director Lucious had first found her.

The streets of New York City.

---

# **I. CONFIRMATION**

Several Foundation-adjacent observers reported seeing a small white kitten matching Aster's appearance wandering alone through the city.

She slept beneath delivery trucks.

Sheltered beneath storefront awnings during the rain.

Avoided people whenever possible.

Whenever someone tried to approach...

She teleported a short distance away.

Never far.

Just far enough to remain alone.

Director Lucious immediately departed for New York personally.

---

# **II. OBSERVATIONS**

Over three days, Aster was observed from a distance.

She no longer behaved like the confident little Oracle the Blacksite had known.

She behaved like an ordinary stray.

She searched trash bins for scraps.

She curled up beside heating vents at night.

When pedestrians came too close...

She flinched instinctively before disappearing in a white flash.

Some ignored her.

Some chased her away from shop entrances.

A few children threw small stones toward her, laughing when she teleported away before they reached her.

She was never seriously injured.

But every incident was recorded.

She gradually stopped lingering anywhere people gathered.

---

# **III. CONTACT**

On the fourth day, Director Lucious finally approached.

He came alone.

No security.

No recovery team.

No vehicles.

Just himself.

Aster noticed him immediately.

Her ears lowered.

A white flash.

She reappeared across the street.

Lucious followed at a walking pace.

He did not call her name.

He did not run.

He simply kept walking.

---

Another flash.

Another rooftop.

Another alley.

Again.

And again.

The pattern continued for nearly two hours.

Each time...

Lucious quietly followed.

Never faster than a walk.

---

Eventually...

Aster stopped teleporting.

She was sitting beneath an old fire escape.

Watching him.

---

# **IV. THE CONVERSATION**

Director Lucious stopped several meters away.

He remained standing.

Several minutes passed before either of them moved.

Finally...

Aster picked up a piece of discarded cardboard.

Using a small piece of charcoal, she slowly wrote.

> **Why are you here?**

---

Lucious answered calmly.

---

### Director Lucious

> "To bring you home."

---

Aster erased the words.

Wrote again.

> **I don't have one anymore.**

---

Lucious shook his head.

---

### Director Lucious

> "You do."

---

Aster looked away.

Another message appeared.

> **I hurt everyone.**

---

### Director Lucious

> "Accidentally."

---

She immediately crossed out the word.

Hard enough that the cardboard nearly tore.

Then she wrote:

> **It still happened.**

---

Silence settled between them.

Cars passed nearby.

Rain began falling lightly.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Yes."

> "It did."

---

He didn't deny it.

He didn't argue.

He simply acknowledged it.

---

### Director Lucious

> "And you fixed what you could."

---

Aster lowered her head.

---

> **Not Star.**

---

Lucious had no answer to that.

After a long pause...

He finally spoke.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...No."

---

Another silence.

---

> "...You couldn't."

---

# **V. REFUSAL**

Lucious eventually extended one hand.

Not to grab her.

Only to offer it.

---

### Director Lucious

> "Come back."

---

Aster stared at his hand.

Then slowly stepped backward.

She wrote one final sentence.

> **If I come back...**

She paused.

> **...someone else will get hurt someday.**

---

Before Lucious could answer...

A white flash.

She disappeared again.

---

# **VI. CONTINUED OBSERVATION**

Over the following week, recovery teams maintained visual contact whenever possible.

Aster continued surviving alone.

She avoided shelters.

Ignored food left specifically for her.

Accepted help from no one.

Despite being chased away from storefronts, soaked by rain, and living without the comforts she once had at the Blacksite, she consistently chose not to return.

Psychological Services summarized the situation:

> **"Her decision is not being driven by comfort. It is being driven by the belief that distance protects the people she cares about."**

---

# **VII. DIRECTOR'S ORDER**

Director Lucious suspended all attempts at physically retrieving Aster.

His standing instruction was updated:

> **"No one is to force her return."**

> **"If she runs, let her run."**

> **"If she appears, make sure she is safe."**

> **"If she ever decides to come back... the gates will already be open."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Z-885 "Aster"**

- Confirmed alive.
    
- Located intermittently within New York City.
    
- Declines all attempts to return voluntarily.
    
- Recovery operation remains active.
    

The report concludes with a handwritten note from Director Lucious:

> **"Home is not a place you drag someone back to. It is a place they know they can return to when they are ready."**
