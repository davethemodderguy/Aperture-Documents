# **I. SUMMARY**

Following increased familiarity with Snowy’s wolf pup form—

Whitefall residents have developed a problematic habit of:

> picking him up constantly

---

This behavior has rapidly escalated into a daily occurrence.

---

Primary offenders include:

- Bao
    
- Frost
    
- Cherry
    
- occasionally Cheer
    

---

Candleheart technically participates as well—

however physical contact limitations caused by her ghost physiology reduce discomfort significantly.

---

Sky remains the only individual Snowy consistently allows to carry him willingly.

---

As a result of excessive handling—

Snowy has developed a defensive behavioral response.

---

Specifically:

> light biting

---

---

# **II. DEFENSIVE BITING**

---

Snowy now lightly bites individuals who pick him up without permission.

---

Behavior characteristics:

- non-aggressive
    
- controlled bite pressure
    
- warning-oriented
    
- emotionally driven annoyance response
    

---

Most common targets:

- hands
    
- sleeves
    
- gloves
    

---

Lucy AI confirms:

> no genuine hostile intent detected

---

Interpretation:

Snowy is attempting to establish physical boundaries.

---

Unfortunately—

most residents find the behavior “cute.”

---

This reportedly frustrates Snowy heavily.

---

# **IV. PHYSICAL DURABILITY**

---

Despite frequent accidental falls—

Snowy rarely sustains meaningful injury.

---

Analysis confirms:

- abnormally durable bone structure
    
- enhanced cold adaptation
    
- reduced pain sensitivity
    

---

Likely causes include:

- Whitefall environmental conditioning
    
- wolf physiology
    
- prolonged exposure to injury and cold
    

---

Lucy AI notes with concern:

> “Snowy’s reduced pain response is medically abnormal.”

---

Most concerning detail:

Snowy himself barely reacts to injuries anymore.

---

Examples include:

- walking normally after hard impacts
    
- ignoring bruising
    
- dismissing bleeding
    
- treating falls as unimportant
    

---

Interpretation suggests prolonged suffering has partially normalized pain perception psychologically.
