# **I. SUMMARY**

At approximately 16:42 Whitefall Standard—

an accidental high-force launch event involving:

- Snowy
    
- Sky
    

occurred near the eastern Whitefall logging path.

---

Contrary to expectations—

Snowy sustained only negligible injuries.

---

The rock he impacted did not.

---

---

# **II. INCIDENT DETAILS**

---

Sky was reportedly assisting Snowy with outdoor supply transport during moderate snowstorm conditions.

---

Snowy, currently in wolf pup form, was being carried under Sky’s arm due to worsening terrain stability.

---

According to Sky’s statement:

---

> “He bit me unexpectedly.”

---

---

Snowy later clarified:

---

> “…I wanted down.”

---

---

The bite startled Sky enough to trigger instinctive release reflex.

---

However—

due to Sky’s partially stabilized Wandering Starladian physiology—

the release force was substantially stronger than intended.

---

Result:

> Snowy was launched at high velocity across the snowfield.

---

Witnesses report:

- one visible spin
    
- approximately three airborne rotations
    
- one confused bark mid-flight
    

---

Trajectory ended when Snowy collided directly with a large frozen rock formation.

---

Impact noise described by Bao as:

> “way too loud for something that small.”

---

---

---
