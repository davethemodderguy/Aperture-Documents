# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-044**

### **CRITICAL INCIDENT — COMPOUND PTSD TRIGGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – MEDICAL / RESIDENTIAL / SECURITY

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

**Date:** [REDACTED]

An unusually busy operational schedule resulted in nearly all residents of Room N-07 departing before sunrise.

Several containment inspections, engineering deadlines, dimensional research tasks, and administrative meetings occurred simultaneously.

Personnel departed quickly.

Many skipped breakfast.

Several forgot their usual routines.

Most importantly...

No one remembered to update the departure board.

---

## **TIMELINE**

### **06:48**

Neko departed for Engineering.

Miss Neko assisted Medical.

Bao and Frost were requested to the hydroponics wing.

Star left early to help N.O.S.T. with dimensional mapping.

Nightshade accompanied her.

Aster was asked to assist Archives with several historical documents.

Leo, asleep around Aster's shoulder, left with her.

Director Lucious entered a series of executive meetings shortly afterward.

Everyone assumed someone else had informed Taiga.

Nobody had.

---

### **07:13**

Taiga awoke.

She stretched, adjusted the black cross hanging from her collar, and looked around Room N-07.

Every bed was empty.

She smiled to herself.

> "...Breakfast already?"

She checked the common room.

Nobody.

Kitchen.

Empty.

Workshop.

Dark.

The balcony.

Only drifting snow.

---

### **07:20**

Taiga began searching.

She visited the laboratory.

Locked.

The greenhouse.

Empty.

Archives.

No Aster.

The cafeteria.

Almost deserted.

Director Lucious's office.

His secretary informed her he was attending meetings all morning.

The words seemed to linger.

> "...Meetings..."

Taiga quietly nodded and walked away.

---

## **ESCALATION**

Security cameras recorded Taiga's pace increasing noticeably.

She checked rooms she'd already searched.

She began calling names.

> "...Neko?"

Silence.

> "...Bao?"

Nothing.

> "...Aster?"

No answer.

Even Leo's familiar sleepy presence was absent.

Her breathing became uneven.

Moments later, she involuntarily transformed into her snow leopard form.

She ran.

---

## **SECONDARY TRIGGER**

Attempting to navigate through an unfamiliar corridor, Taiga unintentionally crossed near the Blacksite's secured long-range testing facility.

At that moment, Perimeter Security was conducting scheduled evaluations of newly acquired anti-materiel rifles.

The range had been properly secured.

Warning systems were functioning.

However, Taiga entered an adjacent service corridor just as testing began.

The first shot echoed through the reinforced concrete halls.

A deep, concussive report.

Then another.

Then another.

Although the rounds remained entirely within the secured firing range, the sound alone reverberated throughout the corridor.

Taiga stopped instantly.

Her ears flattened.

She looked around frantically.

Another shot rang out.

She bolted.

---

## **SEARCH OPERATION**

Security personnel reviewing live camera feeds quickly recognized the situation.

Weapons testing ceased immediately.

A Blacksite-wide search was initiated.

Unlike previous incidents, Taiga appeared to avoid familiar locations entirely.

She wasn't trying to return to Room N-07.

She was trying not to be found.

---

## **DISCOVERY**

Nearly forty minutes later, an exterior maintenance drone detected faint movement beneath a snow-covered emergency generator housing near the edge of the facility.

Search teams approached slowly.

Beneath the shelter, curled tightly into herself, was the small snow leopard.

She had buried herself behind utility piping where the wind was weakest.

She remained motionless except for slight trembling.

Medical personnel stayed back while familiar staff were contacted.

---

## **REUNION**

As soon as Director Lucious was informed, he immediately left the ongoing meeting.

Within minutes, Neko, Miss Neko, Bao, Frost, Star, Nightshade, and Aster had all returned from their assignments.

Aster was the first to step into view.

She slowly knelt several meters away and held up her whiteboard.

It read:

> **We were working.**

She erased it.

Then wrote another sentence.

> **We came back.**

Taiga looked up.

A few moments later, Leo slowly slithered down from Aster's shoulder, sleepily making his way across the snow until he curled beside Taiga.

As usual, he promptly fell asleep.

The sight drew the smallest hint of a smile.

Star quietly sat nearby.

> "...I'm sorry."

> "...I should've left you a note."

Neko lowered himself beside the group.

> "...That's on all of us."

No one argued.

---

## **POST-INCIDENT REVIEW**

An internal review concluded that the event resulted from two documented PTSD triggers occurring in rapid succession:

- Taiga unexpectedly believed her trusted companions had disappeared without explanation.
    
- She was subsequently exposed to repeated reports from high-caliber anti-materiel rifle testing.
    

While both events were individually manageable under normal circumstances, their combination significantly intensified her panic response.

No procedural violations occurred within the shooting range itself.

However, communication failures regarding Taiga's departure routine directly contributed to the incident.

---

## **CORRECTIVE ACTIONS**

The following measures were adopted immediately:

- The Room N-07 departure board is now mandatory whenever the room is vacated.
    
- If everyone must leave simultaneously, one resident will remain until Taiga has been informed whenever operationally feasible.
    
- Security will notify Residential Services before scheduled high-caliber weapons testing so routes near the range can be avoided.
    
- Director Lucious authorized an emergency contact protocol allowing residential incidents involving Taiga to immediately interrupt non-critical meetings.

Back in Room N-07, Aster replaced the old departure board with a much larger one.

Across the top, in bold letters, she wrote:

> **WHERE EVERYONE IS**

Below it were names, locations, and expected return times.

At the very bottom, she added one final line.

> **No one is gone.**

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga returned to Room N-07 without physical injury.

After spending the evening with her roommates, her breathing and behavior gradually returned to baseline.

The revised communication procedures have been adopted across the residential wing.

---

### **Closing Observation**

The following morning, before anyone left for work, every resident stopped by the new board.

One by one, they wrote where they would be.

Neko added a quick sketch of a wrench beside "Engineering."

Star drew a smiling star next to "N.O.S.T."

Bao added a cupcake by "Kitchen."

Aster finished with a large arrow pointing to the board itself.

Taiga stood reading each message.

Then she smiled.

When everyone finally headed off to work, she waved from the doorway.

This time, she knew exactly where to find them.
