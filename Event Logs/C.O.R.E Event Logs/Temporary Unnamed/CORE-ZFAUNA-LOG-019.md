# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-019**

### **HUMOROUS INTERNAL MEMORANDUM — "ENGINEERING OVERLOAD"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – INTERNAL / NON-ESSENTIAL

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

At approximately **14:20**, Bao was preparing a fresh batch of cupcakes in the residential kitchen.

According to Bao, they were Neko's favorite flavor.

Taiga, who was still under temporary mobility restrictions, was sitting nearby after having spent most of the previous afternoon receiving a piggyback ride from Neko.

During that time, Neko had apparently decided to "teach her some engineering."

No one expected what happened next.

---

## **THE INCIDENT**

As Bao mixed the batter, Taiga quietly began speaking.

At first, Bao assumed Taiga was simply making conversation.

Instead, Taiga calmly recited...

Chapter One.

Then Chapter Two.

Then Chapter Three.

Complete with formulas.

Material specifications.

Safety notes.

Manufacturing tolerances.

Historical development of Nekonium alloys.

She did not hesitate once.

She did not make a single mistake.

After nearly twenty uninterrupted minutes, Bao slowly looked up from the mixing bowl.

> "...Taiga?"

Taiga continued.

> "...Section 7.4.3—Plasma containment failures may be mitigated by increasing—"

Bao blinked.

> "...Taiga?"

Still no pause.

The recital continued.

Personnel entering the kitchen were greeted not by the smell of cupcakes...

But by what sounded suspiciously like an engineering lecture.

---

## **THE DISCOVERY**

Neko walked into the kitchen carrying a toolbox.

He listened for several seconds.

Smiled proudly.

> "...Oh."

> "...She's memorized it."

Bao stared.

> "...Memorized... what?"

Neko answered as though discussing the weather.

> "...The engineering manual."

Bao relaxed slightly.

> "...How long is it?"

Neko thought for a moment.

> "...About a hundred and fifty pages."

Silence.

Bao slowly placed the whisk down.

Very carefully.

Then looked directly at everyone else in the room.

With complete seriousness, she announced:

> **"If Taiga starts getting annoying, scream her name out loud."**

Everyone looked at her.

Bao continued.

> **"It stuns her long enough for someone to gently shake her and remind her she's not an engineering textbook."**

Another pause.

> "...Preferably before Chapter Twelve."

---

## **FIELD TEST**

Several minutes later, Taiga reached page forty-two.

Miss Neko experimentally called out:

> **"TAIGA!"**

Taiga immediately stopped speaking.

Her ears perked upright.

She blinked several times.

> "...Huh?"

Miss Neko gently rested a hand on her shoulder.

> "...Welcome back."

Taiga looked around the kitchen.

Then at Bao.

Then at Neko.

She tilted her head.

> "...Was I talking again?"

Everyone nodded.

Very slowly.

Taiga's ears folded back in embarrassment.

> "...Sorry..."

### Leo

Leo slept through the entire lecture.

When Taiga finally stopped talking...

He yawned.

Adjusted his position around her scarf.

And immediately went back to sleep.

Veterinary concluded that Leo possesses complete immunity to prolonged engineering discussions.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Engineering manuals are no longer recommended as bedtime reading material for Taiga.

Neko has been advised to limit spontaneous lectures to "reasonable lengths."

He responded by asking Medical to define "reasonable."

Medical declined.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Later that evening, Bao presented the finished cupcakes.

Neko took one bite.

Smiled.

> "...Perfect."

Taiga looked up.

> "...Did you know cupcakes rise because carbon dioxide produced by the leavening agents expands according to—"

The entire room shouted in unison.

> **"TAIGA!"**

She blinked.

> "...Oh."

> "...Sorry."

The room erupted into laughter.

Even Leo briefly opened one eye before deciding the joke wasn't important enough to interrupt his nap.
