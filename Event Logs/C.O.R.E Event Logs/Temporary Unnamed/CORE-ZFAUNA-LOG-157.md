# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-157**

### **BEHAVIORAL UPDATE — MART SUNBRINGER'S OWL FORM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – RESIDENT WELFARE / CONFIDENTIAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

Psychological Services have documented a significant behavioral change regarding Mart Sunbringer's owl form.

Historically, Mart instinctively transformed into his owl form during periods of extreme fear, grief, or emotional overload, often flying away before anyone could reach him.

This is no longer the case.

Instead, Mart remains in his human form during panic episodes.

While this has reduced the risk of prolonged disappearances, it has also made the emotional impact of these episodes more visible.

---

## **OBSERVED CHANGE**

Previously, severe emotional distress commonly resulted in:

- Immediate transformation into owl form.
    
- Flight from the surrounding area.
    
- Remaining hidden until emotional regulation returned.
    

Current observations indicate a different response.

When confronted by overwhelming memories or emotionally sensitive topics, Mart typically:

- Falls silent.
    
- Begins crying.
    
- Covers his face or lowers his head.
    
- Runs away on foot.
    
- Seeks a quiet place to calm himself.
    

He no longer instinctively transforms.

---

## **COMMON TRIGGERS**

The most frequently observed triggers remain:

- Questions regarding his childhood.
    
- Questions about Astral's death.
    
- Questions concerning the large scar on Taiga's left arm.
    
- Discussions involving abandonment.
    
- Unexpected reminders of past events.
    

When these topics arise unexpectedly, Mart often struggles to respond verbally.

If pressured to continue the conversation, he usually apologizes before leaving.

---

## **OBSERVED INCIDENT**

A newly assigned researcher, unaware of Mart's history, noticed the scar visible beneath Taiga's rolled-up sleeve during a routine medical examination.

Curious, the researcher casually asked,

> "...How did she get that scar?"

Mart, who had been reading nearby, froze.

The book slipped from his hands.

His breathing became noticeably uneven.

The researcher immediately realized something was wrong.

Before anyone could speak...

Mart quietly whispered,

> "...I'm sorry..."

He turned and ran from the room.

No wings.

No transformation.

Only the sound of hurried footsteps fading down the hallway.

Taiga excused herself immediately and followed.

Approximately twenty minutes later, both returned.

Mart's eyes were still red from crying, but he appeared calmer.

The researcher later submitted a written apology after being informed that the question had unintentionally touched on a deeply traumatic memory.

Mart accepted the apology without hesitation.

---

## **OWL FORM USAGE**

Mart's owl transformation remains fully functional.

However, it is now used primarily for practical purposes, including:

- Long-distance flight.
    
- Transportation.
    
- Reconnaissance.
    
- Recreational flying.
    
- Rest when emotionally or physically exhausted.
    

Its use as an involuntary stress response has become uncommon.

Staff have also noted that Mart simply chooses to remain in his human form more often than he once did.

One psychologist summarized the change as follows:

> **"He no longer runs from people."**

> **"He runs to find somewhere safe to cry."**

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Taiga Sunbringer

Taiga has become highly attentive to Mart's nonverbal cues.

She can often recognize the onset of a panic episode before anyone else.

When asked how, she answered,

> "...He's my little brother."

> "...I can tell."

She no longer chases after him immediately.

Instead, she gives him enough space to breathe before quietly checking on him.

---

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral admitted she initially worried that Mart had "lost" part of himself by no longer transforming.

Psychological Services reassured her that this was not the case.

His coping mechanisms had simply changed.

She later remarked,

> "...I miss seeing him fly away."

A pause.

> "...But I'd rather be able to hug him afterward."

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Growth isn't always becoming stronger."

> "...Sometimes it's choosing not to run quite as far."

---

### Neko

Neko quietly updated several internal briefing documents.

A highlighted note now appears under Mart's resident profile:

> **Avoid unnecessary discussion of documented childhood trauma unless clinically appropriate.**

Below it, in smaller handwriting:

> "...And if he runs, don't crowd him."

---

### Frost

Frost has taken to quietly leaving a bottle of water and a folded blanket near the places Mart most often retreats to.

She never waits there.

She simply leaves them and walks away.

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star noticed Mart hadn't transformed into his owl form much lately.

She eventually asked him why.

Mart thought for a moment before answering.

> "...I like being me."

Star smiled.

> "...You always were."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Mart Sunbringer**

- Human form: Primary form.
    
- Owl form: Used voluntarily rather than reflexively.
    
- Panic episodes: Human form retained.
    
- Recovery time: Improved with calm, familiar support.
    

Psychological Services consider this development a positive change.

While Mart's trauma has not disappeared, he no longer instinctively distances himself beyond everyone's reach.

When he leaves, he remains somewhere his family can eventually find him.

---

## **Closing Observation**

Late one afternoon, Mart was seen sitting alone beneath a tree in the courtyard.

His eyes were red.

A closed book rested beside him.

A few minutes later, Taiga quietly approached.

She didn't ask what was wrong.

She simply sat beside him.

Neither spoke.

After several minutes, Mart leaned gently against her shoulder.

He stayed in his human form.

For the first time in a long while...

He didn't feel the need to fly away.
