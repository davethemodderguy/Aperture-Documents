# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-159**

### **MEDICAL & PSYCHOLOGICAL UPDATE — MART SUNBRINGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – MEDICAL / RESIDENT WELFARE

---

## **SUMMARY**

Medical and Psychological Services have documented a recent deterioration in Mart Sunbringer's sleep quality.

While Mart has experienced chronic insomnia for an extended period, the frequency and intensity of recurrent nightmares have increased significantly.

The result has been progressively shorter sleep periods, frequent awakenings, and, on some nights, an inability to sleep at all.

The change has noticeably affected his daytime behavior.

---

## **CURRENT OBSERVATIONS**

Medical staff have recorded the following changes:

- Difficulty initiating sleep.
    
- Frequent awakening shortly after falling asleep.
    
- Nightmares occurring multiple times during a single night.
    
- Increased daytime fatigue.
    
- Reduced appetite after particularly severe nights.
    
- Increased emotional sensitivity.
    
- More frequent apologies.
    
- Longer periods of quietness.
    

Despite sleeping less, Mart continues attempting to maintain his normal daily routine whenever possible.

---

## **NIGHTMARE CONTENT**

Mart has voluntarily described recurring themes during counseling.

The most common include:

- Taiga abandoning him.
    
- Watching Taiga disappear without being able to reach her.
    
- Taiga dying while he is unable to help.
    
- Being left alone in the snow again.
    
- Returning home to find no one waiting.
    

One recurring nightmare has become particularly distressing.

Mart dreams that Taiga herself turns against him.

He described it only once.

> "...She looked at me..."

A long silence followed.

> "...Like she didn't want me anymore."

He declined to describe the remainder of the dream.

Psychological Services respected his decision.

---

## **OBSERVED INCIDENT**

**02:47**

Residential hallway cameras recorded Mart quietly opening his bedroom door.

He appeared disoriented.

His breathing was rapid.

Rather than waking anyone, he sat against the wall outside his room with his knees pulled to his chest.

Approximately three minutes later...

Taiga stepped into the hallway.

She had gone to get a glass of water.

She immediately noticed him.

> **Taiga:** "...Mart?"

Mart looked up.

His eyes were wet with tears.

> "...Sorry..."

Taiga knelt beside him.

> "...Another nightmare?"

He nodded.

She sat beside him without another question.

Several minutes passed before Mart finally spoke.

> "...You were gone."

Taiga gently rested a hand on his shoulder.

> "...Look at me."

He did.

> "...I'm right here."

Mart closed his eyes.

His breathing gradually slowed.

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical personnel note that Mart's insomnia appears to be closely linked to psychological distress rather than a primary sleep disorder alone.

Nightmares interrupt what little sleep he achieves, creating a cycle in which sleep deprivation further increases emotional vulnerability.

Treatment currently focuses on:

- Consistent evening routines.
    
- Psychological counseling.
    
- Stress reduction.
    
- Family support.
    
- Monitoring for excessive sleep deprivation.
    

Medication has not been adopted as a primary intervention due to concerns about balancing benefits with Mart's overall wellbeing within his anomalous physiology.

---

## **FAMILY RESPONSE**

### Taiga Sunbringer

Without being asked, Taiga has quietly adopted a new habit.

Before going to bed each night, she knocks lightly on Mart's bedroom door.

> "...Goodnight."

Mart always answers.

> "...Goodnight, Big Sis."

On nights when Mart cannot sleep, he sometimes knocks on her door instead.

She has made one rule.

> "...If you wake up from a nightmare..."

> "...Wake me."

> "...Don't sit there alone."

Mart has only done it a handful of times.

Each time...

She answered immediately.

---

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral has begun spending more evenings reading with Mart before bedtime.

Neither of them expects it to stop the nightmares completely.

They simply hope it gives him at least one peaceful memory to fall asleep with.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Director Lucious

After reviewing the updated medical report, Director Lucious wrote a brief instruction beneath the assessment.

> "Fatigue is not laziness."

> "Allow additional rest periods when needed."

The recommendation was approved immediately.

---

### Frost

Frost has adjusted breakfast hours for Mart.

If he finally falls asleep shortly before dawn, meals are quietly saved for later rather than waking him unnecessarily.

---

### Neko

Neko installed a small, warm bedside lamp in Mart's room.

It automatically turns on at a low brightness whenever Mart wakes suddenly during the night.

When asked why, Neko shrugged.

> "...No one should wake up scared in complete darkness."

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star occasionally stays up late reading nearby when Mart has particularly difficult nights.

She doesn't try to keep him awake.

She simply lets him know someone else is still there.

Sometimes that alone helps him relax enough to try sleeping again.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Mart Sunbringer**

- Chronic insomnia: Worsening.
    
- Nightmares: Increasing in frequency.
    
- Daytime functioning: Maintained, though with visible fatigue.
    
- Family support: Strong.
    
- Ongoing medical and psychological monitoring: Active.
    

Psychological Services stress that recovery is expected to take time.

Mart's nightmares are not reflections of reality.

They are reflections of fears he still carries.

---

## **Closing Observation**

Just before sunrise, a nurse making routine rounds noticed light beneath Mart's bedroom door.

Expecting him to still be awake, she knocked softly.

A quiet voice answered.

> "...Come in."

Mart was sitting on the edge of his bed.

He hadn't slept.

On the bedside table rested a framed photograph.

The one of him and Taiga together after they reconciled—Mart piggybacking Taiga while his wings of light were spread behind them.

The nurse glanced at the photo.

Then back at Mart.

He smiled faintly.

Tired.

Exhausted.

But genuine.

> "...If I keep having the same nightmare..."

He looked at the picture again.

> "...I'll keep remembering this instead."

The photograph remained on the bedside table.

So that every morning, no matter how little sleep he managed...

The first thing Mart saw was proof that his greatest fear had not come true.
