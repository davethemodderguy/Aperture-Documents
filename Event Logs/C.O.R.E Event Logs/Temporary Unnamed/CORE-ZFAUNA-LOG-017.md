# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-017**

### **RECOVERY OPERATION — TAIGA**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – SECURITY / MEDICAL

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

At **15:41**, Blacksite perimeter surveillance detected multiple unauthorized individuals several kilometers north of the facility.

Simultaneously, a thermal imaging drone identified a small heat signature lying motionless in the snow.

Perimeter Security was immediately dispatched.

The individual was confirmed to be **Taiga**.

She had sustained gunshot injuries and was unconscious.

Nearby, the same group of hunters believed to have been responsible for the death of Taiga's mother were attempting to secure and remove her from the area.

Before they could leave the perimeter, Security Units intercepted them.

---

# **I. PERIMETER RESPONSE**

Security personnel ordered the suspects to surrender.

Instead, the group attempted to flee.

A brief pursuit followed across the snowfield.

Taiga was successfully recovered during the operation and immediately evacuated to the Blacksite Medical Wing.

The suspects were detained by Blacksite Security for transfer to the appropriate authorities.

No Aperture personnel were seriously injured during the recovery.

---

# **II. MEDICAL STATUS**

Taiga arrived unconscious but with stable vital signs.

Medical teams immediately began emergency treatment.

Her injuries, while serious, were determined to be survivable.

Following surgery and several hours of observation, Medical released the following statement:

> **"Patient is stable."**

> **"Recovery is expected, though extended rest will be required."**

Her snowflake earrings, white scarf, and personal katana were recovered separately and secured until she regained consciousness.

---

# **III. ROOM N-07**

News of Taiga's recovery spread quickly through the residential wing.

No one celebrated loudly.

Everyone simply... exhaled.

For the first time since her disappearance, her bed was no longer empty because no one knew where she was.

Now they did.

She was alive.

---

# **IV. VISITORS**

### Neko

Was among the first to visit after Medical permitted limited access.

He quietly placed Taiga's folded white scarf on the bedside table.

> "...Welcome back."

---

### Miss Neko

Stayed only long enough to gently adjust Taiga's blanket.

Before leaving, she whispered:

> "...You don't have to run anymore."

---

### Frost

Left a small ice sculpture of a snow leopard beside the window.

It never melted.

---

### Bao

Arrived carrying a container of fresh snow.

When asked why, she simply replied:

> "...She always liked eating it."

Medical allowed the container to remain in the room.

---

### Star

Sat nearby reading quietly.

She knew silence could sometimes be more comforting than conversation.

---

### Nightshade

Remained outside the room for most of the evening.

Her only comment was directed toward Security Command.

> "Double the northern patrols."

The request was approved immediately.

---

### Aster

When Medical finally allowed her inside, Aster climbed carefully onto the chair beside the bed.

She looked at Taiga for a long time.

Then she wrote on her whiteboard.

> **You came back.**

She placed the whiteboard where Taiga would see it upon waking.

Then curled up beside the bed and quietly fell asleep.

---

### Leo

Leo immediately reclaimed his favorite sleeping place.

He curled loosely around Taiga's forearm.

Veterinary attempted to move him.

He refused.

Medical ultimately allowed him to remain, provided he did not interfere with treatment.

He slept there for the rest of the night.

---

# **V. DIRECTOR'S NOTE**

Director Lucious reviewed the perimeter footage personally.

Afterward, he issued two orders.

**Order One:**

Increase patrols around the northern perimeter and expand long-range surveillance coverage.

**Order Two:**

> **"No resident of this Blacksite will ever have to face those responsible for their suffering alone."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Taiga**

- Successfully recovered by Blacksite Perimeter Security.
    
- Receiving ongoing medical treatment.
    
- Condition stable.
    
- Expected to recover with rest and rehabilitation.
    

The investigation into the detained hunters remains ongoing.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late that evening, a nurse performing routine rounds paused outside Taiga's room.

Inside, the lights were dim.

Leo slept around her arm.

Aster slept in the chair beside the bed, her whiteboard resting in her lap.

On it, illuminated by the soft bedside lamp, were four simple words.

> **Welcome home, Taiga.**
