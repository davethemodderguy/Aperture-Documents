# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-055**

### **ABILITY MANIFESTATION — "BLINK"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – ANOMALOUS / MOVEMENT

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Taiga has successfully demonstrated an anomalous movement technique previously observed only in **Star**, **Aster**, and **Mirage**.

The technique has been informally designated **"Blink."**

Despite repeated study by C.O.R.E. and N.O.S.T., no one currently understands the mechanism behind it.

---

## **DESCRIPTION**

Blink allows the user to instantaneously reposition a short distance in the direction they intend to travel.

To an observer, the movement appears as one of the following:

- The user vanishes and immediately reappears several meters away.
    
- A rapid dash too fast for the eye to accurately follow.
    
- A brief distortion resembling reality folding for a fraction of a second.
    

High-speed cameras have provided little additional information.

In many recordings, the subject is simply absent for one or two frames before appearing elsewhere.

---

## **FIRST OBSERVATION**

The first recorded instance occurred during routine engineering work.

Neko accidentally dropped a screwdriver beneath a heavy fabrication table.

Taiga instinctively stepped forward.

She disappeared.

A faint shimmer crossed the floor.

A moment later she was already crouching on the opposite side of the table, screwdriver in hand.

She looked around.

"...Huh?"

"...I don't remember walking."

Neko blinked.

"...Neither do I."

---

## **TESTING**

Subsequent testing confirmed several characteristics.

- Effective range is currently limited to short distances.
    
- The movement is nearly instantaneous.
    
- Blink cannot currently pass through solid objects.
    
- Consecutive Blinks are possible but appear mentally tiring.
    
- Accuracy improves with practice.
    

Medical found no evidence of physical strain beyond mild fatigue after repeated use.

---

## **MECHANISM**

Researchers attempted to explain Blink using conventional anomalous models.

None proved satisfactory.

No measurable increase in kinetic energy.

No detectable dimensional gateway.

No conventional teleportation signature.

No measurable manipulation of space-time.

N.O.S.T. summarized the findings with a single sentence.

> **"It works."**

> **"We don't know why."**

---

## **COMPARISON**

Known users include:

- Star
    
- Aster
    
- Mirage
    
- Taiga
    

Although the movement appears visually similar among all four individuals, subtle differences have been observed.

Star's Blink leaves behind faint particles of white light.

Aster's produces almost no visual effect whatsoever.

Mirage's creates a brief holographic distortion.

Taiga's leaves behind a fleeting trail of drifting snowflakes that disappear within seconds.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga has begun incorporating Blink into everyday movement.

She frequently uses it to:

- Cross short hallways.
    
- Reach high shelves.
    
- Retrieve dropped objects.
    
- Catch up to her roommates when they walk ahead.
    

Medical has advised against excessive use until its underlying mechanism is better understood.

---

### **Closing Observation**

One afternoon, Star and Taiga were walking through the residential corridor together.

Star smiled mischievously.

> "...Race you."

Before Taiga could answer—

Blink.

Star vanished several meters ahead.

Taiga laughed.

"...You're on."

Blink.

The two disappeared down the hallway in alternating flashes of light and drifting snowflakes.

Several engineers looked up from their work.

One sighed.

> "...There they go again."

No one chased after them.

They would almost certainly Blink back in a few minutes.
