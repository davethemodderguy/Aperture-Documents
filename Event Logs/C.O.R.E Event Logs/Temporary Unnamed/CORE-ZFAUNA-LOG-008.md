# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-008**

### **PHYSICAL DEVELOPMENT OBSERVATION — TAIGA**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – VETERINARY / N.O.S.T.

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Veterinary Services have documented another gradual developmental change in Taiga's humanoid form.

Unlike her initial transformation, this change occurred slowly over several weeks rather than overnight.

Medical examinations confirm she remains perfectly healthy.

According to Taiga...

> "...I'm just growing into myself."

---

# **I. CHANGING APPEARANCE**

Personnel first noticed that the leopard-like markings across Taiga's body had become more prominent.

The soft gray rosettes and stripes visible when she first transformed gradually darkened into richer black markings.

The pattern spread naturally across:

- Her arms.
    
- Legs.
    
- Shoulders.
    
- Back.
    
- Tail.
    
- Hair.
    

The markings remained unique, matching the coat pattern of her snow leopard form.

Veterinary confirmed they were a natural part of her physiology and not the result of any anomalous event.

---

# **II. SNOWFLAKE EARRINGS**

A few days later, another detail appeared.

Small silver earrings shaped like delicate snowflakes now adorned both of Taiga's ears.

When asked where she got them...

Taiga simply smiled.

---

### Taiga

> "...They grew."

---

Medical initially assumed she was joking.

A careful examination proved otherwise.

The earrings had naturally formed alongside her transformation, much like antlers or horns on certain anomalous species.

They were biologically part of her body but had the appearance of finely crafted jewelry.

They were cool to the touch and shimmered faintly whenever snow was nearby.

Veterinary determined they could not be removed without causing injury.

As such, they were officially documented as part of Taiga's natural anatomy.

---

# **IV. ASTER**

Aster spent several minutes studying Taiga's new appearance.

She circled her once.

Twice.

Then held up her whiteboard.

> **Pretty.**

Taiga's ears twitched happily.

She hugged Aster without another word.

---

# **V. LEO**

Leo's opinion was...

Predictable.

He woke up.

Looked at the new earrings.

Used one as a convenient place to rest his chin.

Then promptly fell asleep again.

Veterinary noted that Leo appeared to consider the earrings "acceptable sleeping accessories."

Thin.

Healthy.

### **Closing Observation**

Fresh snow had begun falling over the Blacksite.

Taiga stepped outside into the courtyard.

As the snowflakes drifted around her, the tiny earrings caught the pale winter sunlight, glittering like freshly fallen ice crystals.

Aster looked up from making paw prints in the snow.

She raised her whiteboard one last time.

> **Very pretty.**

Taiga laughed softly.

The snowflakes around her swirled happily in the wind.
