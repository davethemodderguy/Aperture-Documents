# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-106**

### **ABILITY UPDATE — TAIL STRENGTH & VARIABLE OWL SIZE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – ANOMALOUS RESEARCH / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Recent observations have documented two additional anomalous developments involving the Sunbringer siblings.

- Taiga's tail possesses significantly greater strength than previously measured.
    
- Mart can now voluntarily alter the size of his **owl form**.
    

Both abilities have become part of their everyday lives.

---

# **TAIGA SUNBRINGER**

## **Enhanced Prehensile Tail**

Further testing has confirmed that Taiga's prehensile tail is far stronger than its appearance suggests.

While she rarely uses its full strength, the tail is capable of:

- Supporting Taiga's own body weight.
    
- Holding another person securely.
    
- Pulling heavy objects across the floor.
    
- Catching someone before they fall.
    
- Grasping delicate objects with surprising precision.
    

Despite its strength, Taiga instinctively handles living beings very gently.

Research has yet to observe her accidentally injure anyone with it.

---

## **COMMON USES**

Rather than using the tail for combat, Taiga typically employs it for mundane tasks.

Examples include:

- Carrying grocery bags while her hands are occupied.
    
- Holding a book while eating.
    
- Pulling chairs into place.
    
- Giving Mart an unexpected hug.
    
- Catching Mart before he walks into something while reading.
    

---

# **MART SUNBRINGER**

## **Variable Owl Size**

Mart has gained the ability to alter the size of his owl form at will.

His human form remains unchanged.

Testing confirms that only his owl form is affected.

He appears capable of resizing himself almost instantly without visible strain.

---

## **PREFERRED SIZE**

Despite this new capability, Mart overwhelmingly prefers becoming **very small**.

At his smallest comfortable size, he is light enough to:

- Perch on Taiga's finger.
    
- Sit comfortably on Astral's shoulder.
    
- Rest on the edge of a teacup.
    
- Curl up inside the hood of Taiga's hoodie.
    
- Ride on Director Lucious's shoulder without being noticeable.
    

Mart has stated that he simply finds the smaller form "comfortable."

---

### **Closing Observation**

One quiet afternoon, Taiga sat beneath a tree, reading with one hand while lazily gliding five centimeters above the grass.

Her other hand rested on her knee, index finger extended.

Perched on it was an impossibly tiny Mart, barely larger than her thumb.

He had tucked his head beneath one wing and fallen asleep.

Taiga didn't dare move.

When Star walked by and whispered,

> "...Why are you sitting so still?"

Taiga smiled without taking her eyes off her brother.

She answered just as quietly.

> "...Because my little owl finally found the perfect perch."

Star looked closer.

"...Aww."

Neither of them disturbed him.

Mart slept peacefully, balanced on the finger of the sister who would happily stay still for as long as he needed.
