# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-129**

### **ONGOING OBSERVATIONS — THE DISTANCE BETWEEN SIBLINGS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – PSYCHOLOGICAL / ACTIVE SEARCH

---

## **SUMMARY**

Although Mart Sunbringer remains officially listed as missing, reports of sightings have continued throughout the Blacksite.

None have resulted in successful contact.

Meanwhile, Taiga Sunbringer's physical condition has continued to decline, limiting her ability to respond.

---

## **MART'S ROOM**

Personnel noted a subtle but significant change in Taiga's behavior.

She no longer entered Mart's room.

The room remained exactly as he had left it.

Nothing was moved.

Nothing was cleaned beyond routine maintenance.

Everything waited.

As though its owner might return at any moment.

---

## **THE PHOTOGRAPH**

One item remained on Mart's bedside table.

A framed photograph.

It showed a happier day.

Above the snow-covered training grounds, Mart was suspended in the air in his owl form, wings spread wide.

Beside him, Taiga laughed as she floated and chained several air jumps through the sky.

Both were smiling.

Written beneath the photograph, in Mart's handwriting, were four simple words.

> **"Best sister ever."**

Several days later, Taiga entered the room for the first time in weeks.

She stood silently beside the bed.

She picked up the frame.

Read the caption.

Closed her eyes.

No one heard her speak.

She carefully removed the photograph from its frame.

Folded neither the paper nor the caption.

She opened Mart's desk drawer.

Placed the photograph inside.

Closed it gently.

When she left...

The empty frame remained beside the night lamp.

No one has seen the photograph since.

---

## **REPORTED SIGHTINGS**

Despite the lack of confirmed contact, reports continue almost daily.

Descriptions remain remarkably consistent.

Personnel describe:

- A young boy standing silently on the highest rooftops.
    
- Two wings formed from brilliant white light.
    
- White hair moving in the wind.
    
- Faint blue eyes visible even after sunset.
    

Outdoor maintenance crews have reported seeing him flying overhead.

Cargo Bay workers have occasionally observed his silhouette through the large skylights.

Every report ends the same way.

By the time anyone reaches the location...

He is gone.

---

## **TAIGA'S RESPONSE**

Whenever a sighting is reported, Taiga immediately attempts to respond.

Initially, she ran.

Later...

She tried.

Her weakened condition no longer allowed it.

Medical staff documented multiple incidents in which she attempted to sprint despite obvious exhaustion.

Several ended with Taiga collapsing before reaching the reported location.

Eventually...

She stopped trying to transform.

She no longer had the strength.

One afternoon, personnel reported Mart flying above the eastern cargo yard.

Taiga heard the radio transmission.

She stood up immediately.

Took only a few hurried steps.

Her legs gave way.

By the time she managed to push herself back to her feet...

The workers looked upward.

The luminous figure was already disappearing beyond the perimeter.

Taiga reached toward the sky instinctively.

Her hand never came close.

She watched until the light disappeared beyond the clouds.

Then slowly lowered her arm.

Witnesses reported hearing her whisper only one sentence.

> "...Please don't leave again..."

The wind carried her voice away.

---

## **RECURRING MEMORY**

Psychological Services note that Taiga repeatedly recalls the final argument.

Most commonly, she fixates on the words:

> _"Maybe you're making yourself feel unwanted."_

Each recollection is accompanied by visible distress.

Taiga has never attempted to justify what she said.

She has never blamed Mart for leaving.

She simply replays the conversation, as though hoping it might somehow end differently.

---

## **BEHAVIORAL CHANGE**

Personnel have also observed increased irritability toward Astral.

Taiga has become noticeably shorter in conversation with her mother.

On several occasions, she has walked away during otherwise calm discussions.

No clear trigger has been identified.

Astral has not responded with anger.

Instead, she has continued to check on Taiga daily while respecting her daughter's boundaries.

Psychological Services have not assigned a definitive cause for this change, noting only that intense grief can sometimes be expressed toward those closest to us, even without a clear reason.

Astral herself requested that staff avoid interpreting Taiga's behavior as rejection.

Her only comment was:

> "...She's hurting."

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral noticed the empty picture frame on Mart's bedside table.

She opened the drawer.

Saw the photograph inside.

She did not move it.

She quietly closed the drawer again.

Later, she was overheard saying,

> "...She couldn't bear to look at it."

---

Director Lucious ordered every reported sighting investigated, regardless of how unlikely.

His reasoning was brief.

> "...If there's even a chance..."

---

### Neko

Neko once reached the cargo roof less than a minute after a sighting.

He found nothing except disturbed snow.

He looked toward the sky.

> "...You're making this difficult, kid."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Sometimes people stay close..."

She watched another search team return empty-handed.

> "...Because leaving completely hurts even more."

---

### Bao

Bao quietly began carrying small meals whenever Taiga rushed toward another reported sighting.

More than once, Taiga returned too exhausted to stand.

Bao never scolded her.

She simply helped her sit.

---

### Frost

> "...The distance..."

She looked toward the mountains.

> "...Isn't measured in meters."

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star eventually noticed the empty frame beside Mart's bed.

She asked where the picture had gone.

No one answered.

She quietly straightened the frame anyway.

---

### Nightshade Dawnbringer

Nightshade observed that every sighting occurred where Mart could easily see the Blacksite.

Never inside.

Never close enough to speak.

She wrote only one note.

> "He has not abandoned this place."

---

### Leo

Leo watched another report come over the radio.

He looked at Taiga trying to stand.

Before she could fall, he steadied her.

The sighting ended as all the others had.

Too late.

Leo looked toward the fading white shape in the distance.

> "...One day..."

He glanced at Taiga.

> "...Stop making her chase you."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

### Mart Sunbringer

- **Status:** Missing.
    
- Frequent unconfirmed visual sightings continue.
    
- No successful contact established.
    

### Taiga Sunbringer

- Physical condition remains fragile.
    
- Unable to sustain prolonged running or repeated transformations.
    
- Continues responding to every reported sighting despite medical advice.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

At sunset, another report came over the Blacksite radio.

> **"Possible visual on the eastern roof."**

Taiga heard it.

She stood.

Tried to run.

Managed only a few unsteady steps before she stumbled.

She looked up.

High above the rooftop, a boy with wings of white light glided silently across the evening sky.

She reached toward him.

He was already too far away.

By the time she found her balance...

He had become only a small point of light against the clouds.

Then...

Nothing.

The only thing left was the empty sky.

And a sister, standing beneath it, wishing she had chosen different words.
