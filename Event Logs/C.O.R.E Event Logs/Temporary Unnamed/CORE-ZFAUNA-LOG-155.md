# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-155**

### **RESIDENT WELFARE PROTOCOL — MART SUNBRINGER & EMERGENCY ROOM ACCESS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – RESIDENT CARE / INTERNAL PROCEDURE

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following Mart Sunbringer's previously documented period of emotional withdrawal after his conflict with Taiga, Psychological Services established an internal welfare protocol.

While Mart has recovered significantly, staff note that periods of isolation can still become emotionally overwhelming if prolonged.

Mart has repeatedly stated that he sometimes needs time alone.

That need is respected.

However, if he becomes unresponsive beyond an established welfare threshold, personnel are instructed **not** to assume he simply wishes to be left alone.

Instead, designated residents are contacted.

The first name on that list is always:

**Taiga Sunbringer.**

---

## **BACKGROUND**

Mart's room door had historically been locked whenever he required privacy.

During several previous welfare checks, personnel found themselves unable to enter despite believing Mart required immediate support.

After one particularly stressful incident...

Taiga solved the problem herself.

By kicking the door open.

The room was undamaged.

The door...

Was not.

---

## **TRAINING RECORD**

Following the first incident, Security jokingly suggested teaching Taiga proper breaching techniques.

She accepted the suggestion far more enthusiastically than anyone expected.

Under controlled supervision and using reinforced training doors scheduled for replacement, Taiga learned how to deliver a controlled front kick capable of forcing a door open without causing unnecessary structural damage beyond the door assembly itself.

Her success rate became...

Concerningly consistent.

One instructor wrote:

> "Excellent technique."

Another added underneath:

> "Please stop looking so excited."

---

## **ARMORY ENGINEERING MODIFICATION**

After replacing the same bedroom door multiple times, Neko proposed a simpler solution.

Rather than reinforcing the frame indefinitely...

He redesigned the hinge system.

---

### **MAGNETIC HINGE ASSEMBLY**

The new residential doors utilize magnetic quick-release hinges.

During normal operation:

- The door functions identically to a conventional door.
    
- Locking mechanisms remain unchanged.
    
- Structural integrity is unaffected.
    

During a forced entry:

- A sufficiently powerful impact disengages the magnetic hinge assembly.
    
- The door separates cleanly from the frame instead of splintering.
    
- The hinge components can be reattached in minutes.
    

When asked why he designed them this way, Neko answered:

> "...Because replacing hinges is cheaper than replacing doors."

Accounting enthusiastically approved the redesign.

---

## **CURRENT WELFARE PROCEDURE**

If Mart isolates himself:

1. Knock.
    
2. Call his name.
    
3. Wait for a response.
    
4. If he confirms he is alright, respect his privacy.
    
5. If he remains unresponsive beyond the welfare response window, notify Psychological Services and contact Taiga immediately.
    

Only if staff reasonably believe there is an immediate risk to Mart's wellbeing is emergency entry authorized.

In practice...

Taiga usually arrives before anyone finishes reading the procedure.

---

## **OBSERVED INCIDENT**

One evening, Mart remained in his room for several hours.

Repeated knocks received no answer.

Personnel contacted Taiga.

She arrived at a sprint.

She knocked once.

> **Taiga:** "Mart?"

Silence.

She knocked again.

> "...Mart?"

Still nothing.

She took one step back.

The nearby security officer quietly sighed.

> "...Here we go..."

Taiga launched a precise kick.

**CLACK.**

Instead of exploding inward, the door cleanly disengaged from its magnetic hinges and slid onto the padded floor.

Taiga rushed inside.

Mart was sitting beside his bed.

Head lowered.

Tears quietly running down his face.

He looked up.

Seeing Taiga...

His shoulders relaxed.

She immediately hugged him.

> **Taiga:** "...You scared me."

Mart's voice was barely above a whisper.

> "...I'm sorry."

He didn't resist the hug.

He simply stayed there.

---

## **FOLLOW-UP**

Several minutes later, Neko arrived carrying a toolkit.

He looked at the detached door.

Looked at Taiga.

Then at the frame.

He smiled.

> "...Worked perfectly."

Within three minutes, the door was hanging normally again.

Taiga blinked.

> "...That's it?"

Neko nodded proudly.

> "...Magnetic hinges."

## **Closing Observation**

A week after the new hinges were installed, Taiga walked past Mart's room.

She paused.

Knocked lightly.

> "...You okay?"

A moment later, the door opened normally.

Mart stood there holding a book.

He smiled faintly.

> "...Yeah."

Taiga smiled back.

> "...Good."

She turned to leave.

Mart spoke before she reached the hallway.

> "...Thanks..."

> "...For always checking."

Taiga looked back over her shoulder.

> "...You'd do the same for me."

Mart nodded.

There was no need to test the new hinges that day.

Neko, who happened to be passing by, quietly celebrated that fact.
