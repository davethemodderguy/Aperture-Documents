# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-001**

### **TEMPORARY WILDLIFE CUSTODY REPORT — JUVENILE SNOW LEOPARD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – VETERINARY / TEMPORARY HOLD

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

**Date:** ██/██/████

At approximately 06:18 local time, perimeter motion sensors detected a small quadrupedal animal approaching the eastern security fence during heavy snowfall.

Initial concern was that the contact represented an anomalous entity attempting to bypass the perimeter.

Instead...

Security cameras identified a **juvenile snow leopard**.

The cub appeared exhausted.

After stumbling several more meters into the Blacksite's outer grounds...

She collapsed.

---

# **I. RECOVERY**

Security and Veterinary personnel responded immediately.

The cub showed no signs of aggression.

Instead, she attempted to crawl beneath a maintenance vehicle before curling into a ball.

Initial examination revealed:

- Moderate malnutrition.
    
- Dehydration.
    
- Several superficial lacerations.
    
- Bruising along the left shoulder.
    
- A minor gunshot graze across her right hind leg.
    
- Severe exhaustion.
    

No anomalous properties were detected.

---

# **II. INVESTIGATION**

Reconnaissance drones surveyed the surrounding area.

Approximately three kilometers from the perimeter, personnel discovered the body of an adult female snow leopard.

Evidence at the scene strongly indicated illegal hunting activity.

The adult had sustained multiple rifle wounds.

The cub's tracks led away from the scene...

Eventually reaching the Blacksite.

Veterinary believes she had been wandering alone for several days before arriving.

---

# **III. TEMPORARY CUSTODY**

Director Lucious authorized temporary wildlife custody until the cub recovered sufficiently for relocation to an appropriate protected habitat.

Until then, she would remain under Veterinary supervision.

No containment measures were deemed necessary.

Her enclosure consisted of:

- Heated sleeping quarters.
    
- Artificial rock formations.
    
- Snow-covered exercise area.
    
- Fresh water.
    
- A nutritionally balanced diet.
    

Within hours...

She accepted the arrangement.

---

# **IV. THE RESIDENTS**

Word spread quickly.

Before long, several familiar faces quietly began visiting.

---

### Neko

Neko entered carrying a thick blanket nearly twice the cub's size.

He carefully placed it beside her.

She looked at it.

Looked at Neko.

Then immediately fell asleep on top of it.

---

### Miss Neko

She spent nearly an hour brushing loose snow and dirt from the cub's fur.

The snow leopard responded by gently placing one oversized paw on Miss Neko's arm.

Neither moved for several minutes.

---

### Star

Star sat quietly nearby, reading aloud from one of her favorite books.

The cub didn't understand the words.

But she appeared comforted by the calm voice.

Eventually...

She fell asleep listening.

---

### Nightshade

Nightshade prepared the cub's medication each evening.

Although the snow leopard initially seemed nervous...

She quickly learned that Nightshade always arrived with food.

Trust developed rapidly.

---

### Bao

Bao looked at the cub.

Then smiled.

> "...Big cat."

The cub blinked.

Bao received an unimpressed stare in return.

---

### Frost

Frost remained slightly farther away than everyone else.

Concerned that her naturally cold aura might make the recovering animal uncomfortable.

Veterinary later informed her the snow leopard seemed perfectly content.

Frost eventually relaxed enough to gently scratch behind the cub's ears.

A deep, rumbling purr followed.

---

### Lucy

Lucy volunteered to help with feeding schedules.

By the end of the first week...

The cub visibly recognized her footsteps.

---

# **V. ASTER & LEO**

Aster approached cautiously.

She remained in her ordinary white cat form.

The snow leopard watched silently.

After several moments...

Aster walked forward and gently bumped her forehead against the cub's nose.

The cub blinked.

Then carefully returned the gesture.

From that day onward, Aster frequently visited during quiet hours.

Usually with Leo.

Leo, as expected...

Climbed onto the snow leopard's back.

Curled into a tiny spiral.

And immediately fell asleep.

The cub looked over her shoulder in confusion.

Then decided not to question it.

---

# **VI. MEDICAL PROGRESS**

After two weeks of treatment:

- The hind leg wound had healed well.
    
- Body weight had increased steadily.
    
- Appetite had fully returned.
    
- Mobility was normal.
    
- Behavioral assessment indicated growing confidence around caretakers.
    

Despite her progress, the cub still became noticeably anxious whenever loud cracking sounds echoed outside, often seeking the nearest familiar person or curling beside Aster until she calmed down.

Veterinary attributed this behavior to trauma associated with the loss of her mother.

---

# **VII. DIRECTOR'S NOTE**

Director Lucious reviewed the report personally.

His comments were brief.

> **"She came here because she had nowhere else to go."**

> **"Until she has somewhere safe again..."**

> **"...this is her home."**

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, a security camera overlooking the veterinary habitat captured a quiet scene.

The young snow leopard slept soundly on her blanket.

Aster was curled against her side.

Leo was asleep on top of the snow leopard's head.

Outside, snow drifted softly across the Icelandic landscape.

For the first time since arriving at the Blacksite...

The cub slept without waking.
