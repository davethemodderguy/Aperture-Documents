# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-011**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL EMERGENCY — SUNBRINGER FAMILY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – INTERNAL / MEDICAL / PSYCHOLOGICAL

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

Following the prolonged absence of Mart Sunbringer and the continued lack of any confirmed sightings, the psychological condition of the remaining Sunbringer family has deteriorated significantly.

Medical Services has declared the situation an active family crisis.

---

# **TAIGA SUNBRINGER**

For days, Taiga insisted to everyone that Mart would come home.

She repeated it so often that it stopped sounding like reassurance.

It sounded like a plea.

Eventually...

The waiting became too much.

She broke.

---

Witnesses reported hearing quiet sobbing coming from Mart's room.

Taiga was found sitting on the floor beside his bed.

His favorite book rested in her lap.

She wasn't reading it.

She simply held it against her chest.

When Astral gently knelt beside her, Taiga looked up.

Her eyes were swollen from crying.

Her voice barely rose above a whisper.

> "...Mom..."

A long silence.

> "...I couldn't keep him safe..."

Astral immediately embraced her.

Taiga didn't resist.

She simply buried her face against Astral's shoulder and cried.

Not loudly.

Not dramatically.

Just...

Quietly.

As though every tear had been waiting for permission.

---

# **ASTRAL SUNBRINGER**

Astral has remained composed throughout the search.

She comforted Taiga.

She reassured everyone else.

She continued helping where she could.

She continued hoping.

Until she couldn't anymore.

One evening, after putting away another untouched meal, Astral quietly sat alone at the dining table.

No one noticed at first.

When Director Lucious approached, she didn't look up.

She stared at the empty chair across from her.

Then asked a question no one in the room could answer.

> "...Where's my son?"

Her voice broke on the final word.

She covered her face with trembling hands.

For the first time since regaining her physical form...

Astral cried.

Not for herself.

For the child she could no longer find.

And for the daughter she was watching fall apart.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Director Lucious found Astral and Taiga together in Mart's room later that night.

Astral was holding Taiga.

Taiga was clutching one of Mart's shed feathers.

Neither of them spoke.

Director Lucious quietly entered the room.

He knelt beside them.

Placed one hand gently on Astral's shoulder.

The other on Taiga's.

No speeches.

No promises he couldn't make.

He simply stayed.

---

She wasn't panicking.

She simply looked...

Empty.

### Star Dawnbringer

Star sat beside Taiga.

For once...

She had nothing cheerful to say.

Instead, she reached over and held Taiga's hand.

Taiga squeezed it back.

Neither of them let go for a long time.

---

### Nightshade Dawnbringer

Nightshade stood outside Mart's room.

She chose not to enter.

Some pain deserved privacy.

---

Leo eventually walked into Mart's room.

He looked around.

Then quietly placed one of his favorite pillows on Mart's bed.

He never explained why.

### **Closing Observation**

Late that night, the residential wing was silent.

Astral sat on the floor of Mart's room with Taiga asleep against her shoulder, exhausted from crying.

Astral gently brushed a strand of white hair away from her daughter's face.

Then she looked toward the neatly made bed that still waited for her son.

Her voice was barely audible.

> "...You don't have to be brave anymore..."

A tear rolled down her cheek.

> "...Just come home."

There was no answer.

Only the quiet hum of the Blacksite...

And an empty room that still felt like it belonged to a little owl.
