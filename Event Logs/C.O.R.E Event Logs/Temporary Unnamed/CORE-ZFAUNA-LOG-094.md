**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – ANOMALOUS RESEARCH / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **NEWLY DOCUMENTED ABILITIES**

Following controlled observation, Anomalous Research has confirmed two previously undocumented capabilities possessed by Mart.

Despite his calm and gentle personality, these abilities possess significant anomalous potential.

Medical and Research emphasize that Mart has **never** used these abilities with hostile intent.

---

# **ABILITY 1 — SPATIAL REPLICATION WORMHOLES**

Mart can generate stable wormholes connecting to what Research has designated a **Parallel Replication Space**.

The replicated environment appears to be an exact copy of a selected area at the moment of creation, with one critical distinction:

**No people are present.**

Buildings, furniture, vegetation, weather, infrastructure, and other non-living features are replicated with high fidelity, but the space is otherwise empty.

Current testing indicates that changes made within the replicated space do not affect the original location, and vice versa.

The mechanism behind this phenomenon remains unknown.

---

## **RESEARCH NOTES**

Potential applications include:

- Safe equipment testing.
    
- Disaster response training.
    
- Emergency evacuation drills.
    
- Environmental simulations.
    
- Infrastructure planning.
    

Personnel are reminded that all testing must be conducted under Research supervision.

---

## **RISK ASSESSMENT**

While Mart has shown no inclination to misuse this ability, Research has identified several theoretical hazards.

For example, an individual isolated within a replicated environment may experience:

- Disorientation.
    
- Difficulty determining whether they are in the original location.
    
- Psychological distress due to prolonged isolation.
    

Medical specifically notes that Taiga's history of trauma related to abandonment and being alone would make involuntary isolation particularly harmful.

Accordingly, Mart has been instructed never to use the ability to isolate another resident without their informed consent or during an emergency where it is necessary for safety.

Mart readily agreed.

When asked why, he answered simply,

> "...I don't..."

> "...Want anyone..."

> "...To be lonely."

---

# **ABILITY 2 — AERIAL TRANSPORT**

Mart's owl form possesses an anomalous lifting capability far exceeding what his size would suggest.

Testing confirms that he can safely carry people in flight despite his small stature.

The lifting capacity does not appear to decrease with passenger weight, and no signs of fatigue have been observed during prolonged flights.

Research has not yet identified an upper limit to either carrying duration or lifting capability.

The phenomenon does not conform to conventional physics.

---

## **PASSENGER SAFETY NOTICE**

The primary hazard during transport is not falling.

It is Mart's grip.

When carrying passengers, his talons instinctively tighten to maintain a secure hold.

This can result in:

- Minor scalp discomfort if perched on the head.
    
- Clothing damage if gripping collars, backpacks, or jackets.
    
- Small puncture marks if protective equipment is not worn.
    

No serious injuries have been recorded.

Mart is consistently careful and responds immediately if passengers indicate discomfort.
