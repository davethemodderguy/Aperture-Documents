# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-043**

### **FAUNA OBSERVATION — MART**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – FAUNA / BEHAVIORAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Continued observation of **Mart**, Taiga's pet owl, has revealed several unusual behavioral characteristics.

Although anatomically an owl, Mart consistently demonstrates flight patterns and hunting behavior more commonly associated with falcons.

Veterinary staff have found no signs of injury, mutation, or anomalous influence that would explain the discrepancy.

The conclusion remains simple:

Mart is simply... an owl that behaves like a falcon.

---

# **I. FLIGHT CHARACTERISTICS**

Unlike most owls, Mart:

- Frequently flies during daylight hours.
    
- Performs rapid dives and high-speed aerial turns.
    
- Can hover briefly before accelerating into a target.
    
- Is capable of remarkably precise landings.
    
- Appears to enjoy flying through snowfall rather than avoiding it.
    

Security personnel have occasionally mistaken him for a small falcon on exterior cameras before realizing it was Mart.

---

# **II. CONSTANT COMPANION**

Mart rarely strays far from Taiga.

Whether she is:

- Training.
    
- Reading.
    
- Walking through the Blacksite.
    
- Playing in the snow.
    
- Resting in Room N-07.
    

Mart is usually somewhere nearby.

Most often he can be found:

- Perched on her shoulder.
    
- Standing atop her umbrella.
    
- Resting on the headboard of her bed.
    
- Quietly watching from a nearby shelf.
    

Unlike Leo, who often sleeps through everything, Mart remains alert and observant.

---

# **III. EMOTIONAL SUPPORT**

Medical has also documented Mart's behavior during periods of emotional distress.

Whenever Taiga becomes upset, Mart immediately closes the distance between them.

He does not make loud calls.

He does not flap frantically.

Instead, he quietly lands beside her—or on her shoulder—and simply stays.

Even during Taiga's documented PTSD episodes, Mart has never flown away.

Veterinary staff believe this behavior is entirely voluntary rather than instinctive.

---

# **IV. INTERACTION WITH ROOMMATES**

Despite being an owl, Mart is exceptionally gentle.

His small size allows him to perch comfortably on several of the younger residents without causing discomfort.

His favorite landing spots include:

### Aster

Mart frequently lands on Aster's shoulder while she writes on her whiteboard.

Aster often pauses just long enough to gently stroke his feathers before continuing.

---

### Star

Mart occasionally lands on Star's head, shoulder, or forearm.

Star has never objected.

Instead, she usually smiles and greets him with,

> "Hi, Mart."

He answers with a quiet hoot.

---

Veterinary notes that Mart instinctively adjusts his grip when landing on either Aster or Star, ensuring his talons never apply enough pressure to cause discomfort.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Mart remains in excellent health.

His unusual flight style poses no operational concerns and has become a familiar sight around the Blacksite.

Personnel have grown accustomed to seeing a small owl streaking through the corridors with the speed and confidence of a falcon before quietly settling onto Taiga's shoulder.

---

### **Closing Observation**

One snowy afternoon, Taiga sat alone on the residential balcony, watching flakes drift across Whitefall Lake's distant horizon.

Mart circled overhead once.

Twice.

Then folded his wings and descended in a smooth, graceful dive more fitting for a falcon than an owl.

He landed gently on Taiga's shoulder.

She smiled and scratched beneath his beak.

Without a word, the two simply watched the snowfall together.

From inside, Aster glanced out the window, smiled at the familiar sight, and wrote one short sentence on her whiteboard.

> **He always finds her.**

And somehow...

He always did.
