# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-072**

### **WELFARE REPORT — PROGRESSIVE SOCIAL WITHDRAWAL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – MEDICAL / SECURITY / DIRECTORATE

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Medical Services has confirmed a continued decline in Taiga's willingness to interact with others.

Unlike previous periods of isolation, Taiga is now actively avoiding **all** contact whenever possible.

This includes:

- Room N-07 residents.
    
- Director Lucious.
    
- Medical staff.
    
- Security personnel.
    
- Whitefall Lake residents.
    
- **Mart.**
    

Medical considers the last point particularly concerning.

Mart has been Taiga's constant companion since the death of her mother.

His avoidance appears to be a deliberate attempt by Taiga to distance herself from anyone she believes she could emotionally burden or endanger.

---

## **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATIONS**

Taiga has become remarkably proficient at moving unnoticed throughout the Blacksite.

Security personnel reviewing camera footage noted several occasions where she crossed active hallways only seconds before patrols arrived.

She avoids predictable schedules.

Changes routes frequently.

Makes use of maintenance corridors.

Rarely remains in one location for more than a few minutes.

Director Lucious reviewed the footage personally before quietly asking:

> "...Who taught her this?"

Several heads slowly turned toward Engineering.

---

## **NEKO**

Neko looked at the recordings.

Then buried his face in his hands.

"..."

"...That's..."

Another pause.

"...My stealth lessons."

Several months earlier, Neko had casually taught Taiga simple methods for moving quietly around workshops so she wouldn't accidentally distract engineers or startle people while carrying equipment.

The lessons included:

- Walking silently.
    
- Observing surroundings.
    
- Choosing routes with minimal traffic.
    
- Remaining aware of blind corners.
    

They were intended for safety.

Not disappearance.

Neko watched another clip where Taiga slipped through an entire corridor unnoticed.

"...Why..."

He sighed deeply.

"...Why does she always learn things..."

Another sigh.

"...And then use them in the most improper way possible?"

Even Miss Neko couldn't think of an answer.

---

## **JOURNAL FRAGMENTS**

Maintenance personnel have recovered several torn pages believed to have originated from Taiga's personal journal.

Most entries were incomplete.

Many had been deliberately torn apart.

The surviving fragments contained repeated references to a **crimson red eye.**

Examples include:

> "...when I see red..."

---

> "...I don't remember..."

---

> "...please don't let..."

(The remainder of the page was missing.)

---

> "...I don't want them to be scared of me..."

---

Medical believes the journal reflects Taiga's fear of losing control rather than any desire to harm others.

Out of respect for her privacy, the remaining fragments have been secured and are not being used for research purposes.

## **ROOM N-07**

Taiga's room remains exactly as she left it.

No one has moved her belongings.

No one has reassigned her bed.

Every evening, someone quietly checks whether she has returned.

Every morning...

The bed remains empty.

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical believes Taiga's increasing isolation reflects a worsening cycle:

- Fear of harming others.
    
- Avoidance of those she loves.
    
- Growing loneliness.
    
- Reinforcement of the belief that isolation is safer.
    

Without intervention, this cycle risks becoming increasingly difficult for Taiga to break.

Medical recommends continued efforts to communicate safety, stability, and acceptance whenever opportunities arise naturally, while respecting her boundaries.

### **Closing Observation**

At 03:12, a security camera briefly captured Taiga standing outside Room N-07.

She looked through the small window in the door.

Inside...

Everyone was asleep.

She smiled faintly.

Raised a hand toward the glass.

Then quietly lowered it.

Without making a sound...

Without opening the door...

She disappeared back into the silent corridors before dawn.

The camera recorded only twenty-three seconds.

For everyone waiting inside...

It felt much shorter.
