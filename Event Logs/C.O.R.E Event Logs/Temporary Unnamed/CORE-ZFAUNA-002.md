## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following several weeks of recovery, CORE-ZFAUNA-001 has become fully acclimated to life inside the Blacksite's veterinary habitat.

Her physical recovery continues ahead of schedule.

Her social recovery has progressed even faster.

---

# **I. AN UNUSUAL DIETARY HABIT**

Personnel quickly noticed an odd behavior.

Whenever fresh snow accumulated...

The cub immediately walked outside.

Scooped up mouthfuls of snow.

And happily ate it.

A lot of it.

Veterinary initially became concerned that she had lost interest in her meals.

Further observation revealed this was not the case.

The cub continued eating all scheduled meat portions without issue.

She simply...

Also really liked snow.

Medical believes the behavior helps keep her hydrated and may simply be enjoyable for her species.

As a result, fresh, clean snow is now routinely placed inside her habitat after every snowfall.

# **II. BEST FRIEND**

If anyone wanted to find the snow leopard...

They only needed to find Aster.

The two had become nearly inseparable.

Whenever Aster walked into the habitat...

The cub immediately stood up.

Trotted over.

And gently rubbed her head against the much smaller white cat.

Aster always returned the greeting.

Security footage showed them spending hours together.

Playing.

Sleeping.

Watching snowfall through the habitat windows.

Or simply lying beside one another without making a sound.

Veterinary officially described their relationship as:

> **"Strong social attachment."**

Everyone else simply called them best friends.

---

# **III. EXTENDED CARETAKERS**

An interesting pattern soon emerged.

The snow leopard appeared to assume that anyone trusted by Aster...

Could also be trusted by her.

As a result, she gradually accepted Aster's regular caretakers.

---

### Neko

Became the cub's preferred playmate.

She frequently attempted to pounce on his boots before rolling onto her back for belly rubs.

---

### Miss Neko

Handled most grooming sessions.

The cub visibly relaxed whenever Miss Neko brushed her thick winter coat.

---

### Star

Usually spent quiet afternoons reading while Aster and the cub slept beside her.

---

### Nightshade

Handled health inspections.

The cub cooperated without complaint, provided Aster remained somewhere within sight.

---

### Director Lucious

Visited periodically.

The cub approached him without hesitation.

Accepted gentle head scratches.

Then returned to lying beside Aster.

Lucious remarked:

> "...She trusts our Oracle's judgment."

---

### Leo

Leo eventually decided the snow leopard was an acceptable sleeping platform.

He alternated between sleeping on Aster...

Or on the cub's back.

Neither seemed to mind.

---

# **IV. ASTER**

Aster appeared to take her self-appointed role very seriously.

If the cub became startled...

Aster was usually the first to reach her.

If Veterinary arrived...

Aster quietly watched nearby.

If the cub fell asleep...

Aster often curled against her side.

One evening, a caretaker found Aster using her marker to write on a small whiteboard outside the habitat.

It simply read:

> **Family.**
