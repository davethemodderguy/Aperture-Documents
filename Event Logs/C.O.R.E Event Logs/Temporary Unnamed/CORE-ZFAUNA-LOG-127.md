# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-127**

### **OBSERVATION REPORT — THE FEATHER OF LIGHT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – PSYCHOLOGICAL / ANOMALOUS

---

## **SUMMARY**

Since the final recorded encounter with Mart Sunbringer, Taiga has developed a daily routine.

Every evening, shortly before sunset, she walks to **Northern Maintenance Access, Sector 4A**.

The same place where Mart last spoke to her.

She always brings the same object.

One worn book.

Mart's favorite.

---

## **OBSERVATION**

Taiga does not call his name anymore.

She simply sits.

Sometimes she reads aloud.

Sometimes she silently turns pages.

Sometimes...

She never opens the book at all.

Personnel have repeatedly offered to accompany her.

She always declines politely.

> "...I'll wait here."

---

## **INCIDENT**

At approximately **18:37**, Security Camera M-4A recorded an unexpected event.

A faint white glow crossed above the maintenance corridor.

Personnel reviewing the footage later identified the silhouette as matching Mart Sunbringer.

He never descended.

He never landed.

He simply flew overhead.

Taiga noticed.

She stood immediately.

Neither sibling spoke.

Mart never looked down.

Or perhaps...

The cameras simply couldn't tell.

As he passed overhead...

Something drifted downward.

A single feather.

Unlike the owl feathers Mart had shed before his disappearance...

This one was composed entirely of white light.

It descended slowly.

Almost weightless.

Taiga reached toward it.

She stumbled forward, stretching as far as she could.

Her fingertips brushed only empty air.

The feather touched the ground.

And vanished.

No residue.

No anomalous energy spike.

Nothing.

As though it had never existed.

Taiga remained kneeling where it disappeared.

Her hand stayed outstretched for several minutes.

---

## **SECONDARY OBSERVATION**

Following the incident, Biological Research reported an unrelated anomaly.

For months, Mart's naturally shed owl feathers had remained scattered throughout the Blacksite.

Many personnel had quietly kept one.

Some were used as bookmarks.

Others decorated desks.

A few remained tucked into journals.

Within forty-eight hours of the luminous feather incident...

Every one of those feathers disappeared.

No signs of theft.

No decomposition.

No anomalous residue.

They simply...

Ceased to exist.

The disappearance occurred simultaneously across the facility.

No explanation has yet been found.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL NOTE**

Following the incident, Taiga asked Psychological Services only one question.

She did not elaborate.

She did not explain what prompted it.

She simply looked at the empty space where the feather had vanished and quietly asked,

> "...Can someone still care..."

A long pause.

> "...After hurting someone that much?"

No immediate answer was given.

The attending psychologist later noted:

_"The question was not theoretical."_

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral continues to watch her daughter leave each evening carrying the same worn book.

She never tells Taiga to stop.

Before Taiga leaves, Astral always says the same thing.

> "...Come home before it gets too cold."

Taiga always nods.

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious reviewed the footage of the luminous feather several times.

Finally, he closed the recording.

> "...He's still watching."

Whether he meant Mart...

Or the siblings' bond...

He never clarified.

---

### Neko

Neko noticed the disappearance of the old owl feathers before anyone else.

He looked at the empty page where one had rested inside his notebook.

He smiled sadly.

> "...Guess you needed them back."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Regret..."

She watched Taiga waiting alone from a distance.

> "...Means the heart still remembers."

---

### Bao

Bao now prepares a warm meal every evening before sunset.

Not because Taiga asks.

Because Bao knows she'll come back cold.

---

### Frost

Frost stood beside the place where the feather had vanished.

She closed her hand over empty air.

> "...Light..."

She opened her hand again.

> "...Cannot always be held."

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star asked Taiga why she kept bringing the same book.

Taiga smiled faintly.

> "...In case he wants me to finish the chapter."

Star quietly sat beside her until sunset.

Neither of them read.

---

### Nightshade Dawnbringer

Nightshade reviewed the security footage several times.

Mart never descended.

Yet...

The feather fell directly above Taiga.

She quietly closed the report.

> "...Not everything left behind is an accident."

---

### Leo

Leo looked through the latest anomaly report.

He noticed every feather collected around the Blacksite had vanished.

He leaned back in his chair.

> "...Huh."

A pause.

> "...Guess he stopped leaving pieces of himself behind."

### **Closing Observation**

On the seventh evening after the feather incident, Taiga returned to Sector 4A as usual.

She sat in the snow.

Opened the book.

And began reading aloud.

The story echoed quietly through the empty corridor.

No one answered.

When she finished the final page, she closed the book and looked toward the evening sky.

Her voice was barely more than a whisper.

> "...If you can still hear me..."

She rested the book gently against her chest.

> "...I'll keep your place."

The only reply was a cold breeze passing overhead.

But for just a heartbeat...

The snow around her shimmered with the faintest trace of white light.

Whether it was the setting sun...

Or someone silently listening...

No one could say.
