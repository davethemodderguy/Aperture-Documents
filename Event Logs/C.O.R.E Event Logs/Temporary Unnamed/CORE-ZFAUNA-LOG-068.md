# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-068**

### **CRITICAL PSYCHOLOGICAL REPORT — LOSS OF SELF-TRUST**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – MEDICAL / PSYCHOLOGICAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Medical Services has documented a significant deterioration in Taiga's psychological condition following an unauthorized conversation in which a member of staff reminded her of the details surrounding one of her documented dissociative defensive episodes.

The discussion occurred despite standing instructions that Taiga was **not** to be informed without authorization from Director Lucious and Medical Services.

The consequences were immediate.

---

## **INITIAL RESPONSE**

Witnesses reported that Taiga remained completely silent.

She did not cry.

She did not become angry.

She simply looked at the floor.

After several seconds, she quietly asked one question.

> "...I..."

> "...I hurt someone?"

No one answered immediately.

That silence was answer enough.

---

## **OBSERVED BEHAVIOR**

Over the following weeks, personnel observed marked changes.

Taiga stopped approaching people first.

She stopped asking for hugs.

She stopped following her roommates through the Blacksite.

Instead, she began choosing routes through empty corridors.

She would wait for rooms to clear before entering.

If someone unexpectedly walked toward her, she would step aside and quietly apologize, even when she had done nothing wrong.

---

## **SELF-PERCEPTION**

When interviewed by Medical, Taiga repeatedly expressed the same concern.

> "...What if I do it again?"

Another session.

> "...What if next time it's someone I love?"

Another.

> "...I don't want anyone getting hurt because of me."

Medical noted that Taiga did not express fear for herself.

Only for others.

---

## **KNOWN STRESSORS**

Taiga has developed strong aversions toward several situations and experiences, including:

- Loud gunfire and other sudden explosive noises.
    
- Hurting another person.
    
- Witnessing someone else being injured.
    
- Being left completely alone.
    
- Seeing her friends in pain.
    
- Seeing her owl, Mart, injured. (Medical notes that Mart remained with Taiga during the traumatic loss of her mother, making him a profound source of emotional security.)
    
- Kale.
    

The final item remains unexplained.

When asked why she disliked kale, Taiga simply answered,

> "...It tastes awful."

Nutrition Services accepted this explanation without further investigation.

---

## **SOCIAL WITHDRAWAL**

Room N-07 residents quickly noticed another change.

Taiga no longer sat in the middle of the room during the evenings.

Instead, she chose the corner nearest the window.

She always made sure there was space between herself and everyone else.

If Star excitedly ran over to sit beside her...

Taiga would quietly stand up.

Not because she disliked Star.

Because she was afraid.

Afraid that staying close might someday hurt someone she cared about.

---

## **ROOM N-07**

One evening, Bao found Taiga eating dinner alone.

The rest of the table was full.

An empty chair remained beside everyone.

Taiga had deliberately chosen a different table.

Bao carried her own tray over.

Without asking permission...

She sat down.

Taiga looked up.

"...You shouldn't."

Bao smiled gently.

> "...Why?"

Taiga lowered her eyes.

> "...I'm dangerous."

Bao's smile faded—not into fear, but sadness.

> "...No."

> "...You're hurting."

Taiga didn't answer.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Director Lucious reviewed the incident personally.

His conclusion was brief.

> "The mistake was not Taiga's."

He ordered additional safeguards around confidential psychological information and reminded all staff that trauma-related records existed to help residents recover—not to burden them.

---

## **PERSONNEL OBSERVATIONS**

### Neko

Neko eventually found Taiga sitting alone outside the workshop.

She immediately stood up to leave.

"...Sorry."

Neko stopped her.

> "...You're apologizing before I've even said anything."

Taiga remained silent.

He sat beside her.

Neither of them spoke for several minutes.

---

### Miss Neko

Miss Neko noticed Taiga hesitating before entering shared rooms.

One afternoon she simply walked over and held the door open.

She didn't ask Taiga to come in.

She only waited.

Eventually...

Taiga stepped inside.

---

### Frost

Frost quietly left a warm blanket where Taiga usually sat by herself.

She never mentioned it.

The next morning, the blanket had been folded neatly.

---

### Star

Star noticed the distance immediately.

She didn't understand why.

Every time she ran toward Taiga...

Taiga would take one careful step backward.

One evening Star finally asked,

> "...Did I do something wrong?"

Taiga's eyes widened.

"...No!"

"...Never..."

She looked away.

> "...I'm scared I'll do something wrong."

Star didn't reply.

She simply walked forward.

Slowly.

Giving Taiga every opportunity to move away.

Instead...

She gently took Taiga's hand.

Neither of them spoke again.

---

### Nightshade

Nightshade's report contained only one sentence.

> "She fears herself more than she fears anyone else."

---

### Aster

Aster erased her whiteboard three times before deciding what to write.

Finally, she held it up.

> **You're still Taiga.**

Taiga stared at those words for a long time.

She smiled...

But it never quite reached her eyes.

---

### Mart

Mart refused to leave Taiga's shoulder.

Even when she gently encouraged him to fly elsewhere.

According to Taiga's translation, he answered,

> "...I already stayed once."

> "...I'm staying again."

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical concludes that Taiga's greatest injury is no longer physical.

Nor is it solely the result of PTSD.

It is the profound loss of trust in herself.

She no longer sees herself as someone who needs protection.

She sees herself as someone others need protection from.

This belief has resulted in increasing isolation despite repeated reassurance from those around her.

Continued psychological care is strongly recommended.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, Room N-07 was quiet.

Everyone else had fallen asleep.

Taiga stood in the doorway.

She looked around the room.

Neko was asleep at his desk.

Miss Neko had a book resting on her chest.

Bao and Frost slept peacefully.

Star was curled beneath a mountain of blankets.

Nightshade sat nearby, keeping silent watch.

Aster slept on the rug.

Leo was wrapped around a pillow.

Mart rested with his head tucked beneath one wing.

Taiga smiled.

It was small.

Tired.

Almost invisible.

She whispered so quietly that only the empty room could have heard.

> "...I love all of you too much..."

She took one slow step backward.

Then another.

Careful not to wake anyone.

Careful not to get too close.

Because somewhere deep inside, beneath all the reassurance she had ever received, one fear refused to leave.

Not that someone would hurt her.

But that one day...

She might hurt someone she loved.

And that was the thought she could never convince herself to stop believing.
