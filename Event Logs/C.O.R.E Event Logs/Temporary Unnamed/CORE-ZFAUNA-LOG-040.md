# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-040**

### **BEHAVIORAL INCIDENT — MISSED DEPARTURE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – MEDICAL / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

On **[REDACTED]**, the primary residents of Room N-07, accompanied by Director Lucious, departed the Blacksite for a planned day trip to **Star's former treehouse in the Starlands**.

The outing had been organized by Star several days in advance.

Because Taiga had still been experiencing mild residual drowsiness in the mornings, the group decided not to wake her, expecting she would sleep until they returned later that day.

The plan was well intentioned.

It did not go as expected.

---

## **TIMELINE**

### **08:12**

Taiga awoke much earlier than anticipated.

Finding her bed empty, she assumed everyone had already gone to breakfast.

She quietly got dressed and walked into the common room.

No one.

She checked the kitchen.

Empty.

The workshop.

Empty.

The balcony.

Empty.

She called out,

> "...Neko?"

Silence.

> "...Bao?"

Nothing.

> "...Star?"

Only the sound of the wind outside answered.

---

### **08:21**

Security footage showed Taiga beginning to move quickly through the residential wing.

She checked every room her roommates commonly visited.

She stopped by Neko's laboratory.

The lights were off.

Director Lucious's office.

Empty.

The cafeteria.

Empty.

With each empty room, she became visibly more distressed.

---

### **08:28**

Near the central corridor, Taiga involuntarily transformed into her snow leopard form.

Medical later concluded that the transformation was triggered by escalating fear rather than conscious choice.

In leopard form, she began running throughout the Blacksite.

Personnel reported seeing a small white snow leopard sprinting through hallways, stopping only long enough to look into another room before continuing.

Several staff attempted to approach her.

She didn't stop.

She wasn't running from anyone.

She was looking for someone.

---

### **08:36**

After nearly ten minutes of searching, Taiga reached one of the older, unused equipment storage areas.

The room had long since been emptied and was rarely visited.

She slipped inside.

Curled herself into the corner behind a row of unused storage crates.

And remained there.

---

## **DISCOVERY**

Maintenance personnel eventually located her after hearing very quiet sounds coming from inside the locker room.

They found the small snow leopard curled tightly against the wall.

She wasn't injured.

She wasn't aggressive.

She was silently crying.

The responding staff remembered Medical's guidance and did not crowd her or attempt to pull her out immediately.

Instead, one operator contacted the Starlands group through the dimensional communications channel.

---

## **RETURN**

The outing ended early.

Within a short time, the group returned to the Blacksite.

As soon as Aster entered the storage room, she knelt several meters away and held up her whiteboard.

It read:

> **We're back.**

Taiga looked up.

A few moments later, she slowly walked toward the group.

Star immediately crouched to her level.

> "...I'm sorry."

> "...I thought letting you sleep would be nice."

Taiga gently leaned against her without saying anything.

Bao wrapped a blanket around the little snow leopard.

Neko rested a hand gently on her back.

Miss Neko quietly scratched behind her ears.

Mart landed beside her.

Leo, having slept through the entire field trip, lazily slithered onto her shoulder as if nothing unusual had happened.

Only after everyone had returned did Taiga transform back into her humanoid form.

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Following discussion with Taiga, Medical updated her behavioral profile.

Her documented PTSD responses are now known to have **two primary triggers**:

- **Sudden, loud authentic firearm reports**, particularly at close range.
    
- **The belief that the people she trusts have disappeared or left without her.**
    

Medical emphasized that this second trigger is **not** caused by ordinary separation.

Taiga understands and accepts when her trusted companions tell her they are leaving and that they will return.

The distress occurs when she unexpectedly discovers they are simply... gone, with no explanation available to her.

Given her history of loss, this uncertainty can rapidly escalate into panic.

---

## **PROCEDURAL UPDATE**

Effective immediately:

- If Room N-07 residents or Director Lucious leave the Blacksite together, Taiga is to be informed beforehand whenever reasonably possible.
    
- If she is asleep and a delayed departure is necessary, a written note is to be left in a clearly visible location explaining where everyone has gone and when they expect to return.
    
- Staff are encouraged to reassure her if she appears to be searching for her roommates.

Taiga has fully recovered from the incident.

No physical injuries were sustained.

Residential staff have adopted the new communication procedures, and a departure board has been placed near the entrance to Room N-07 to record where the group has gone and when they expect to return.

---

### **Closing Observation**

The following morning, Neko headed to the laboratory.

Before leaving, he wrote on the board:

> **Lab. Back before lunch.**

Bao added:

> **Kitchen. Baking.**

Frost wrote:

> **Greenhouse.**

Star drew a smiling sun beside:

> **Library!**

Director Lucious, passing through, added simply:

> **Office. My door is open.**

Taiga stood in front of the board for a moment, reading each note.

She smiled, adjusted the small black cross on her collar, and quietly said,

> "...Okay."

This time, she knew exactly where everyone was.

And more importantly, she knew they were all coming back.
