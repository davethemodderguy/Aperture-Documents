**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – N.O.S.T. / EQUIPMENT

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Recent training exercises have revealed two previously undocumented characteristics of Taiga.

The first concerns her personal katana.

The second concerns... her thought process.

Engineering has requested that the latter remain under observation, as it has already begun influencing several ongoing research projects.

---

# **I. FRACTURED BLADE CONFIGURATION**

During a supervised training session with Neko and Nightshade, Taiga demonstrated a previously unseen function of her katana.

Upon her command, the translucent blade did not disappear.

Instead...

It separated.

The blade divided into approximately one hundred individual fragments, each maintaining its original razor-sharp edge.

Rather than falling apart, every fragment remained connected by an almost invisible wire running through the center of the blade.

The rigid katana instantly transformed into a segmented flexible weapon resembling a bladed whip.

---

## **OBSERVED CHARACTERISTICS**

The fragmented configuration retains the katana's extreme cryogenic properties.

Each segment remains cold enough to rapidly freeze moisture on contact.

Because the blade is no longer rigid, Taiga can:

- Strike around obstacles.
    
- Attack multiple targets in a single sweeping motion.
    
- Retrieve distant objects by wrapping the segmented blade around them.
    
- Alter the weapon's trajectory mid-swing.
    
- Return the blade to its original form almost instantly.
    

When reassembled, no seams or damage are visible.

The transition occurs smoothly, as though the weapon had never separated.

---

# **II. SAFETY NOTICE**

Personnel are reminded that the segmented blade occupies a significantly larger area than the standard katana.

Training grounds must be cleared before demonstration.

One engineer summarized the experience as follows:

> "...Imagine getting hit by one hundred tiny freezing swords attached to a cable."

The description was accepted into the report without modification.

---

# **III. ADAPTIVE PROBLEM SOLVING**

The second discovery was considerably less expected.

While assisting Neko in his laboratory, the pair encountered a complex engineering problem involving plasma stabilization.

Neko paused.

Looked at the equations.

Scratched his head.

Taiga quietly stared at the whiteboard.

After several minutes, she spoke.

> "...What if it worked like this?"

She drew a completely original solution.

Neko examined it.

His expression slowly changed.

> "...That..."

A pause.

> "...Shouldn't work."

Another pause.

He performed the calculations.

Silence filled the laboratory.

Then—

> "...Why does that work?"

The design was built.

It worked exactly as Taiga predicted.

---

# **IV. COGNITIVE PATTERN**

Repeated observations suggest an unusual mental characteristic.

When Taiga encounters a problem she genuinely cannot solve...

Her mind does not simply stop.

Instead, it instinctively constructs an entirely new approach based on intuition, observation, and internal reasoning.

These solutions are not random guesses.

They are internally consistent, logically functional, and, in many cases, surprisingly effective.

Medical has described the process as **Adaptive Constructive Reasoning**, though Neko offered a much simpler description.

> "...She invents answers."

---
