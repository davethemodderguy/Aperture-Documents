# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-004**

### **DIETARY ANOMALY OBSERVATION — TAIGA**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – VETERINARY

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Veterinary Services have documented an unusual—but medically harmless—dietary preference exhibited by Taiga.

Although snow leopards are obligate carnivores, Taiga consistently demonstrates an unexpected preference.

Vegetables.

---

# **I. INITIAL DISCOVERY**

The behavior was discovered during a routine feeding.

Veterinary prepared Taiga's standard meal consisting primarily of fresh meat.

Nearby, a bowl of vegetables had been left for another resident.

Without hesitation...

Taiga walked past the meat.

Ignored it completely.

Carefully selected a carrot.

Then a cucumber.

Then several pieces of broccoli.

Only after finishing the vegetables did she return to eat her meat.

The room fell silent.

---

### Veterinary Technician

> "...Did..."

> "...Did the snow leopard just eat the salad first?"

---

She did.

---

# **II. REPEATED OBSERVATIONS**

Over the following month, Veterinary performed additional observations.

The result was remarkably consistent.

When presented simultaneously with meat and vegetables...

Taiga almost always chose the vegetables first.

Her favorites currently include:

- Carrots.
    
- Cucumbers.
    
- Broccoli.
    
- Lettuce.
    
- Cabbage.
    
- Bell peppers.
    

She still consumes all required meat portions afterward.

Medical examinations confirm she remains in excellent health.

No nutritional deficiencies have been detected.

---

# **III. VETERINARY ASSESSMENT**

Veterinary emphasizes that Taiga is **not** becoming herbivorous.

She continues to require and willingly consume meat as the primary component of her diet.

Her unusual preference appears to be behavioral rather than physiological.

The attending veterinarian summarized the findings as follows:

> **"She is still a snow leopard."**

> **"She simply appears to enjoy vegetables far more than any snow leopard reasonably should."**

# **VI. CURRENT STATUS**

**Taiga**

- Excellent physical health.
    
- Continues consuming a complete veterinary-approved carnivorous diet.
    
- Consistently chooses vegetables before meat whenever both are available.
    
- Favorite vegetables currently include carrots, cucumbers, broccoli, lettuce, cabbage, and bell peppers.
    

Veterinary considers the behavior unusual but not harmful.

---

### **Closing Observation**

During lunch in the residential cafeteria, Taiga was presented with a balanced meal.

Without hesitation, she gently picked up a carrot.

Beside her, Leo stole a tomato slice from Neko's sandwich.

Across the table, Aster quietly nibbled on a small piece of lettuce she had been offered.

Neko looked around the table.

A tomato-loving snake.

A vegetable-first snow leopard.

A cat writing notes on a whiteboard.

He sighed with a smile.

> "...This is somehow the most normal lunch we've had all week."
