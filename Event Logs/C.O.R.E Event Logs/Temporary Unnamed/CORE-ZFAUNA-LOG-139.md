# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-139**

### **POST-RECOVERY OBSERVATION — A NEW PHOTOGRAPH**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – RESIDENTIAL / INTERNAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following Mart Sunbringer's return, Residential personnel have reported a noticeable improvement in the atmosphere surrounding both siblings.

While Mart remains quieter than he was before his disappearance, he has resumed spending time with Taiga on a daily basis.

For the first time since his return, laughter was heard from Mart's room.

---

## **OBSERVATION**

At approximately 15:20, Mart was observed demonstrating his wings of light while remaining in his human form.

Unlike previous manifestations, the wings appeared fully developed despite the absence of any other transformation.

Personnel noted the wings were capable of supporting his full body weight with ease.

Taiga watched the demonstration with obvious amazement.

Then, after several seconds, folded her arms dramatically.

> **Taiga:** "...That's not fair."

Mart tilted his head.

> **Mart:** "...What isn't?"

Taiga pointed accusingly at the enormous glowing wings.

> **Taiga:** "...I spent months learning to air jump."

She floated exactly five centimeters off the floor to emphasize her point.

> "...You disappear for a while..."

Another point.

> "...And come back with actual wings?"

Mart blinked.

Taiga sighed theatrically.

> "...I'm jealous."

For the first time in weeks...

A small smile appeared on Mart's face.

Not forced.

Not fleeting.

Just... natural.

Several nearby staff quietly agreed.

It felt like the old days again.

---

## **PHOTOGRAPH SESSION**

Later that afternoon, Star insisted the siblings take another photograph.

This time, Mart made the suggestion.

Rather than standing side by side...

He climbed onto Taiga's back.

Taiga protested immediately.

> "...You're older now!"

Mart replied simply,

> "...You carried me before."

His luminous wings unfolded.

Instead of Taiga supporting his weight alone, the radiant wings held most of it effortlessly.

The resulting photograph captured both siblings laughing.

Taiga wore an exaggerated expression of mock annoyance.

Mart, still on her back, smiled openly.

His wings stretched behind them, glowing brilliantly.

The picture was later framed and hung inside Mart's room.

Directly beside the bedside lamp.

No caption was written.

None seemed necessary.

---

Astral paused in the hallway after seeing the new photograph.

She smiled warmly.

She couldn't tell whether she meant Mart's...

Or Taiga's.

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious noticed the newly framed picture during a routine walkthrough.

He adjusted it slightly so it hung perfectly level.

He left without saying a word.

---

### Neko

Then looked at Taiga.

---

### Miss Neko

Miss Neko passed the room just in time to hear everyone laughing.

She smiled quietly to herself.

Some sounds were worth preserving.

---

Bao walked in carrying lunch.

She stopped when she saw Mart still piggybacking on Taiga.

> "...Are either of you planning to get down?"

Mart answered first.

> "...No."

Taiga sighed.

> "...Apparently not."

---

### Frost

Frost examined the photograph after it was hung.

> "...Different picture."

She smiled softly.

> "...Same family."

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star proudly declared the new picture "better than the old one."

When asked why, she answered,

> "...Because nobody's missing."

---

### Nightshade Dawnbringer

Nightshade glanced at the framed photograph while passing the room.

She noticed one detail.

Mart's wings weren't holding only himself.

They curved slightly around Taiga as well.

Almost protectively.

She said nothing.

She simply continued walking.

---

### Leo

Leo saw the siblings arguing over whose idea the piggyback pose had been.

He laughed.

> "...Yep."

> "...Everything's back to normal."

### **Closing Observation**

The newly framed photograph now hangs prominently beside Mart's bed.

Visitors often notice it before anything else.

Two siblings.

One floating five centimeters above the floor.

The other riding on her back, suspended effortlessly by wings of white light.

Whenever someone asks why the picture has no caption, Mart simply smiles.

> "...It doesn't need one."

Because unlike the photograph that had once been torn apart...

This one was whole.
