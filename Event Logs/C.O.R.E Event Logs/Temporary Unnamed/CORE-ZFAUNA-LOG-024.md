# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-024**

### **RESIDENTIAL RECORD — STORY NIGHT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – INTERNAL / SOCIAL ARCHIVE

---

Winter storms outside the Blacksite had made going outdoors rather unpleasant.

So instead...

Everyone gathered in Room N-07.

The heaters hummed softly.

Hot chocolate sat on the coffee table.

Bao had baked another batch of cupcakes.

Leo had already claimed the warmest blanket and was asleep before the evening had officially begun.

Someone suggested telling stories.

The conversation lasted well into the night.

---

## **"So... who exactly are Neko and Miss Neko?"**

Taiga asked the question first.

She had lived with them for months now.

Yet she had never actually asked.

Neko looked at Miss Neko.

Miss Neko looked back.

Both smiled.

Neko shrugged.

> "We're siblings."

Miss Neko nodded.

> "Always have been."

Star laughed.

> "...That's the shortest explanation imaginable."

Bao folded her arms.

> "He's an engineer that somehow survives his own inventions."

> "She's the responsible one."

Neko immediately objected.

> "...I'm responsible."

The room collectively looked at **Neko's Claws** resting against the wall.

Silence.

Neko sighed.

> "...Fair."

Everyone laughed.

---

## **"Then... why is Niko a ghost?"**

Niko's smile softened.

He rested against the back of the sofa.

> "Long story."

A quiet silence settled over the room.

He continued.

> "I'm gone."

> "...But not completely."

> "Whitefall became my home."

He smiled toward Neko and Miss Neko.

> "Doesn't stop me from visiting."

Miss Neko smiled warmly.

> "He still cheats at board games."

> "Being dead hasn't fixed that."

Niko looked offended.

> "I do not cheat."

Aster slowly held up her whiteboard.

> **He cheats.**

The vote was unanimous.

---

## **"Is Frost really Bao's daughter?"**

Frost looked up from her tea.

> "...Yes."

Bao immediately wrapped an arm around her.

> "Very proudly."

Taiga tilted her head.

> "You're both goddesses."

Bao nodded.

> "Different domains."

Frost added quietly.

> "Mom controls water and clouds."

> "I control ice."

Bao grinned.

> "...She got all the talent."

Frost immediately shook her head.

> "...No."

The conversation briefly devolved into both insisting the other was more impressive.

Nobody interrupted.

---

## **"Star... how did Leo get lost?"**

Star instinctively reached over and gently stroked the tiny banana snake sleeping across the sofa.

Leo didn't react.

> "...I forgot to bring him back after one of my visits."

She looked down.

> "...I searched everywhere."

Nightshade quietly continued the story.

> "...She blamed herself."

Star nodded.

> "...Then I finally found him."

She smiled.

> "Sleeping."

Neko laughed.

> "...Of course he was."

Star nodded.

> "...He had no idea everyone was looking for him."

Leo yawned in his sleep.

Without opening his eyes...

He wrapped himself more tightly around the blanket.

---

## **"Nightshade?"**

Taiga looked toward the quiet girl.

> "...Who are you?"

Nightshade remained silent for several seconds.

Star answered first.

> "...She's part of my life."

Nightshade looked toward Star.

Then quietly continued.

> "...I protect her."

Star smiled.

> "...And she keeps pretending she isn't nice."

Nightshade sighed.

She didn't argue.

Everyone took that as confirmation.

---

## **"...Why do you like vegetables so much?"**

The room turned toward Taiga.

She froze.

Everyone waited.

She smiled awkwardly.

> "...Next question."

Neko grinned.

> "...Still classified?"

Taiga nodded.

> "...Very."

Nobody pressed further.

Some mysteries could wait.

---

## **"Who's Mirage?"**

The room became quieter.

Neko answered this one.

> "A friend."

Miss Neko nodded.

> "Someone dependable."

Star smiled.

> "Mirage helped us through some difficult times."

Nightshade added simply:

> "...Reliable."

Taiga noticed everyone smiling while talking about Mirage.

That alone told her plenty.

---

## **Miscellaneous Questions**

### "Leo... why do you sleep so much?"

Leo slowly opened one eye.

Everyone leaned closer.

He quietly answered...

> "...Warm..."

He closed his eye again.

And fell asleep.

---

### "Neko... why do you carry a rifle _and_ a transforming plasma scythe?"

Neko answered without hesitation.

> "Options."

Nobody questioned it.

---

### "Aster... what's the future like?"

Aster thought carefully.

She picked up her marker.

After nearly a minute of writing, she turned the whiteboard around.

> **Depends.**

Director Lucious, who had quietly walked past the open door during his nightly rounds, happened to see the message.

He smiled to himself and continued walking.

---

### "Bao... why are your cupcakes always so good?"

Bao looked genuinely confused.

> "...Because they're made with love?"

Neko took another bite.

> "...Scientifically accurate answer."

---

### "Frost... do you ever get cold?"

Frost looked out the window at the Icelandic snowstorm.

> "...Not really."

Bao laughed.

> "...She wears a winter coat because everyone else does."

---

## **Closing Observation**

The clock quietly passed midnight.

The hot chocolate had long since disappeared.

Only a few cupcakes remained.

Leo was asleep.

Aster had fallen asleep halfway through writing another sentence on her whiteboard.

Taiga looked around the room.

Everyone was talking.

Laughing.

Teasing one another.

For a while...

She simply listened.

Then she smiled.

Not because someone had tried to cheer her up.

But because, somewhere along the way, this strange collection of engineers, gods, ghosts, cats, and one very sleepy snake had become exactly what she never expected to find.

A family.
