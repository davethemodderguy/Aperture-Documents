# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-135**

### **RECURRING INCIDENTS — REPEATED PURSUITS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – MEDICAL / PSYCHOLOGICAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the incident documented in CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-134, Taiga Sunbringer has repeatedly attempted to pursue Mart Sunbringer whenever a sighting is reported.

Every attempt has ended the same way.

Taiga collapses from physical exhaustion.

When personnel arrive...

She is already gone.

She always regains consciousness safely inside the Blacksite.

No one has been able to identify who returns her.

---

## **INCIDENT PATTERN**

Over the past ██ days, Security has documented **twelve** separate incidents.

Each follows an almost identical sequence.

1. A sighting of Mart is reported.
    
2. Taiga leaves the building immediately.
    
3. She attempts to chase him despite medical restrictions.
    
4. Her weakened condition causes collapse.
    
5. Personnel reach the location.
    
6. Taiga is no longer present.
    
7. She awakens safely inside Medical or her bedroom.
    

No cameras have successfully captured the interval between collapse and recovery.

---

## **SELECTED INCIDENTS**

### **Incident 03**

Taiga was observed running across the western snowfields.

She stumbled repeatedly before collapsing beside a frozen fence line.

Response time:

Forty-eight seconds.

Arrival:

No Taiga.

Only disturbed snow.

---

### **Incident 07**

Taiga transformed into her leopard form in an attempt to continue the pursuit.

She managed only a short distance before losing consciousness.

When Medical reached the location...

Only her footprints remained.

She awoke thirty minutes later in her own bed.

---

### **Incident 11**

Personnel intentionally intercepted Taiga before she reached the outer gate.

She slipped past them without resistance.

Several minutes later she collapsed near Cargo Route B.

Again...

She disappeared before responders arrived.

---

## **MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

Each time Taiga awakens:

- Her injuries have been treated.
    
- She has been moved indoors.
    
- She is covered with blankets.
    
- Signs of cold exposure are minimal despite prolonged time outdoors.
    

No staff member has claimed responsibility.

Medical records simply list the transporter as:

> **Unknown.**

---

## **SUBJECT RESPONSE**

Taiga eventually stopped asking who carried her back.

Instead...

Whenever she woke up, she quietly looked toward the nearest window.

As though hoping to see someone leaving.

She never did.

One nurse asked why she kept chasing Mart despite knowing the outcome.

Taiga answered after a long silence.

> "...Because one day..."

She looked down at the permanent feather she still carried.

> "...He might stop."

### **Closing Observation**

Security Camera N-18 recorded Taiga collapsing beside a snow-covered trail at 18:53.

The camera briefly lost visibility as heavy snowfall swept across the lens.

When the image cleared twenty-three seconds later...

Taiga was gone.

Frame-by-frame enhancement revealed only one additional detail.

Just before visibility disappeared...

A faint white glow moved toward her through the snow.

Not away.

Toward her.

Whether it was Mart, another anomaly, or something else entirely could not be confirmed.

One fact, however, has become impossible for staff to ignore.

No matter how many times Taiga collapses while trying to reach Mart...

She is never left alone in the snow.
