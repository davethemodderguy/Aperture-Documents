# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-042**

### **PERSONAL EQUIPMENT ISSUANCE — "SNOWDROP" UMBRELLA**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – EQUIPMENT / PERSONAL DEFENSIVE DEVICE

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following discussions with Security and Medical regarding Taiga's preference for spending time outdoors during Iceland's frequent snowfall, Neko presented her with a custom-built gift.

At first glance...

It appeared to be nothing more than a simple white umbrella.

It wasn't.

---

# **I. PRESENTATION**

Late that afternoon, Neko entered Room N-07 carrying a long white package.

Taiga looked up from where she and Aster were building tiny snow sculptures on the balcony.

> "...For me?"

Neko nodded.

> "...Happy early winter."

Taiga carefully unwrapped the package.

Inside was an elegant translucent white umbrella.

The canopy resembled freshly fallen snow, allowing light to softly pass through it.

The handle was polished white with subtle silver accents.

It was surprisingly light.

> "...It's beautiful..."

She slowly opened it.

The canopy expanded silently.

Tiny snowflakes landing on it slid off without leaving moisture behind.

---

# **II. CONSTRUCTION**

Although outwardly decorative, the umbrella is constructed almost entirely from Blacksite-developed materials.

### Canopy

The canopy consists of a woven composite of:

- Nekonium Cotton Fabric.
    
- Transparent ballistic polymer.
    

The resulting material is:

- Waterproof.
    
- Wind resistant.
    
- Resistant to tearing.
    
- Rated to stop standard handgun rounds and small-caliber rifle projectiles.
    

Despite its protective properties, it remains lightweight enough for Taiga to carry comfortably.

---

### Frame

The internal frame utilizes reinforced Nekonium alloy joints.

The umbrella remains functional even after impacts that would destroy conventional umbrellas.

---

# **III. SECONDARY FEATURE**

After admiring the umbrella for several minutes, Taiga noticed the handle felt slightly heavier than expected.

She tilted her head.

> "...Why is the handle so thick?"

Neko smiled.

> "...Because..."

He pressed a concealed release.

A hidden compartment quietly opened within the handle.

Nested inside was a compact **.22 Long Rifle pistol**, custom-fitted to the umbrella.

Taiga stared.

"...There's..."

She looked between the umbrella and Neko.

"...A gun?"

Neko nodded matter-of-factly.

> "...Emergency use only."

He immediately added,

> "...Hopefully never."

The compartment closed seamlessly.

Once shut, the umbrella appeared completely ordinary once more.

---

# **IV. SAFETY FEATURES**

The concealed firearm incorporates several safeguards:

- Mechanical safety engaged by default.
    
- Hidden release requiring deliberate activation.
    
- Personalized grip dimensions sized specifically for Taiga.
    
- Standard Aperture biometric lock preventing unauthorized use.
    

Neko emphasized that the pistol was intended solely as a last-resort defensive measure should Taiga ever become separated from security personnel.

Medical and Security approved the design after reviewing its safety systems.

---

# **V. TAIGA'S RESPONSE**

Taiga spent several moments quietly holding the umbrella.

Then smiled warmly.

> "...Thank you."

She looked up at Neko.

> "...I hope I never have to use the gun."

Neko smiled back.

> "...Me too."
