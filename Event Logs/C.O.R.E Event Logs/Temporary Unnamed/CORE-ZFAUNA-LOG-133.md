# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-133**

### **STATUS CHANGE — TERMINATION OF CONTACT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – PSYCHOLOGICAL / INTERNAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

At 19:56 on ██/██/20██, Taiga Sunbringer and Mart Sunbringer experienced what is believed to be their final direct encounter.

No hostility was observed.

No raised voices.

No argument.

Only acceptance.

After the encounter, Mart altered his behavior permanently.

Although he continues to remain within the Blacksite perimeter, he has completely ceased appearing anywhere Taiga is likely to be.

As of this report, no accidental encounters have occurred since.

---

## **INCIDENT REPORT**

Mart appeared on the western observation roof shortly before sunset.

Taiga arrived several minutes later after another staff member quietly informed her of the sighting.

This time...

She did not run.

She walked.

Slowly.

The permanent feather remained tucked safely inside her jacket.

Mart waited.

Neither sibling spoke for nearly a minute.

Snow drifted quietly between them.

Finally...

Mart broke the silence.

> **Mart:** "...You don't have to keep looking for me anymore."

Taiga's eyes widened.

She shook her head immediately.

> **Taiga:** "...No."

> "...I do."

Mart looked toward the horizon instead of at her.

> **Mart:** "...Every time you find me..."

A pause.

> "...You get hurt."

Taiga took another step.

> **Taiga:** "...I can live with that."

Mart slowly shook his head.

> **Mart:** "...I can't."

Silence settled again.

The wind carried loose snow across the rooftop.

When Mart spoke again...

His voice was calm.

Not cold.

Not angry.

Simply tired.

> **Mart:** "...I'm staying here."

> "...Inside the Blacksite."

> "...You'll be safe knowing I'm alive."

Another pause.

> "...But..."

For the first time since his disappearance...

He forced himself to meet her eyes.

> **"...You won't see me anymore."**

Taiga's breathing caught.

She whispered,

> "...Please don't do this."

Mart's expression faltered.

It was obvious the words hurt him to say.

> **Mart:** "...This is the only thing I know how to do..."

> "...Without hurting you."

Taiga reached toward him.

Her hand stopped halfway.

She no longer expected him to take it.

Mart unfolded his wings of white light.

Before leaving, he spoke one final sentence.

Quietly.

Almost too softly to hear.

> **"...Take care of yourself... Big sis."**

The old name returned.

For just those four words.

Then he stepped backward into the evening sky.

He did not look back.

---

## **BEHAVIORAL CHANGE**

Since the incident:

- Mart has continued to appear on security cameras.
    
- He remains within the Blacksite perimeter.
    
- He actively alters his route whenever Taiga is nearby.
    
- He has avoided every opportunity for further contact.
    

Security analysts conclude this behavior is intentional.

Mart is no longer hiding from the Blacksite.

He is hiding from only one person.

---

## **SUBJECT: TAIGA SUNBRINGER**

Taiga has ceased responding to reports of Mart's sightings.

Not because she no longer wishes to see him.

Because she believes he has made his decision.

She continues carrying the permanent feather every day.

She no longer waits outside his room.

She no longer visits the observation roof.

She no longer asks staff whether he has been seen.

When asked why, she answered simply:

> "...He asked me not to."

Psychological Services describe her condition as one of persistent grief accompanied by profound regret.

She has repeatedly expressed the belief that one conversation permanently changed the course of their relationship.

Whether that belief is accurate remains undetermined.

She simply held her daughter closer.

### **Closing Observation**

A month after the final encounter, a security operator noticed two cameras activate at nearly the same time.

One showed Taiga quietly reading in the eastern courtyard.

The other showed Mart sitting alone on the western roof.

They were separated by only a few hundred meters.

Neither knew the other was there.

The operator looked between the two screens for a long moment before quietly muting the monitors.

In the daily log, he wrote only one line:

> "Sometimes the greatest distance inside the Blacksite isn't measured in meters."

> "It's measured by the words people wish they could take back."
