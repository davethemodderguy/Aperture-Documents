# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-142**

### **COMPARATIVE MOBILITY STUDY — AERIAL LOCOMOTION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – ANOMALOUS ABILITY COMPARISON

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following Taiga's successful glide training, an informal discussion among several anomalous residents unexpectedly developed into a comparative demonstration of aerial movement.

The conclusion was simple:

**Not everyone flies for the same reason.**

---

## **SUBJECT COMPARISON**

### **Mart Sunbringer**

**Flight Method:** Anatomical Wing Flight

Mart generates lift directly through his luminous wings.

Characteristics:

- Sustained powered flight.
    
- Hovering.
    
- High endurance.
    
- Exceptional precision.
    

His wings are both functional and necessary.

---

### **Taiga Sunbringer**

**Flight Method:** Air Jump + Glide

Despite possessing luminous wings, Taiga cannot sustain flight.

Instead she:

- Uses Air Jumps for propulsion.
    
- Converts momentum into long glides.
    
- Maintains extraordinary aerial mobility without true flight.
    

Her wings function primarily as control surfaces rather than engines.

---

### **Star Dawnbringer**

When Taiga proudly finished explaining how her glide worked, Star quietly raised one hand.

> "...Can I try?"

Everyone stepped back.

Star spread her angelic wings.

Then...

She simply floated upward.

No flapping.

No effort.

She continued rising.

Higher.

Higher.

Her wings never moved once.

Mart stared.

Taiga blinked.

Star tilted her head.

> "...What?"

Research quickly confirmed what Daniel had already documented long ago.

Star's angelic wings are **entirely cosmetic** for locomotion.

As an Aegis of Light and an angelic being, she manipulates her own movement directly.

Whether her wings are open...

Closed...

Or absent entirely...

Her ability to fly remains unchanged.

She can:

- Hover indefinitely.
    
- Accelerate instantly.
    
- Fly in any direction.
    
- Stop in midair without momentum.
    

The wings merely reflect her nature.

They are not what allows her to fly.

Taiga looked up.

Then looked at her own wings.

Then at Mart.

> "...I spent weeks learning this."

Star smiled innocently.

> "...I just thought about flying."

Taiga groaned loudly.

Mart quietly joined her.

---

### **Nightshade Dawnbringer**

To verify the findings, Nightshade demonstrated immediately afterward.

Exactly as Star had.

She folded her own wings tightly behind her back.

Without moving them...

She silently lifted several meters into the air.

Hovering perfectly still.

> "...They're decorative."

She stated it so matter-of-factly that it somehow made the revelation even worse.

Mart slowly looked at his own wings.

Then experimentally folded them.

He immediately lost altitude.

Taiga laughed.

> "...At least yours actually do something."

---

### **Bao**

Bao patiently waited for everyone else to finish.

Then took a single step forward.

The moisture in the surrounding air visibly condensed around her feet.

Tiny droplets spiraled upward.

Moments later...

Bao gently lifted from the ground.

She didn't flap.

She didn't hover through brute force.

Instead...

She manipulated surrounding water molecules into flowing currents that continuously supported her body.

When asked what it felt like, Bao smiled.

> "...Like swimming."

To demonstrate, she rolled sideways in midair and casually performed what looked remarkably like a breaststroke.

She drifted across the courtyard as though moving through an invisible ocean.

Taiga stared.

> "...You're swimming."

Bao nodded.

> "...Yes."

> "...The air just has less water."

---

### **Frost**

Everyone eventually turned toward Frost.

She looked back at them.

Then at the sky.

Then at herself.

After several moments she answered plainly.

> "...I can't."

Silence.

Star floated over encouragingly.

> "...You wanna try?"

Frost took one small hop.

She landed immediately.

Another pause.

> "...Still can't."

Mart gently patted her shoulder.

> "...That's okay."

Frost nodded.

She genuinely didn't seem bothered.

> "...Walking is fine."

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

Astral watched the increasingly ridiculous comparisons unfold.

She quietly laughed as Taiga attempted to convince Star that "thinking about flying" was cheating.

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious summarized the demonstrations in one sentence.

> "Every family has one show-off."

No one could agree whether he meant Star...

Or Bao.

---

### Neko

Neko looked from Mart...

To Taiga...

To Star floating upside down for absolutely no reason.

> "...This is unfair."

---

### Miss Neko

Miss Neko simply enjoyed watching everyone argue over whose flying style was superior.

No one noticed she never offered an opinion.

---

### Leo

Leo watched Bao casually "swim" across the sky.

He rubbed his eyes.

> "...I've seen a lot of weird things."

> "...That somehow isn't the weirdest."

## **Closing Observation**

The discussion ended with everyone hovering in different ways.

Mart circled overhead with effortless wingbeats.

Astral glided gracefully beside him.

Taiga launched herself into the air with an Air Jump before sweeping into a long glide.

Star floated upside down without the slightest concern for gravity.

Nightshade drifted silently beside her, wings folded the entire time.

Bao leisurely swam through the air, leaving tiny sparkling droplets in her wake.

Only Frost remained on the ground.

She looked up at the others for a moment.

Then quietly continued walking toward the cafeteria.

After a few steps, she called back without even turning around:

> "...I'm getting lunch."

Everyone landed almost immediately.

In the end, flight wasn't enough to beat hunger.
