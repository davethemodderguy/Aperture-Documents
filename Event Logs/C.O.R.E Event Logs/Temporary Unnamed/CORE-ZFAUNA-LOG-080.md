# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-080**

### **CRITICAL PSYCHOLOGICAL REPORT — ESCALATING IDENTITY DISTRESS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – MEDICAL / DIRECTORATE

**Medical Notice:** This report documents a significant decline in Taiga's mental wellbeing. The material is intended to guide care and support.

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Medical Services has determined that Taiga's psychological condition has continued to deteriorate.

While she remains physically safe within the Blacksite, clinicians are increasingly concerned by changes in how she views herself.

She no longer speaks about the "red eye" as something she fears.

Instead...

She speaks as though it has become part of who she is.

---

## **RECOVERED JOURNAL PAGES**

Additional pages recovered throughout the facility contain increasingly fragmented writing.

Several pages contain almost no words.

Instead...

They are filled with repeated sketches.

The most common image is a single eye.

An iris drawn in deep crimson.

Each version is progressively more distorted than the last.

Some pages show the eye surrounded by heavy, frantic pencil marks.

Others depict it fractured, blurred, or almost unrecognizable beneath layers of ink.

Medical notes that the drawings appear to reflect Taiga's emotional state rather than provide evidence of any external phenomenon.

One surviving line, written beneath a heavily distorted sketch, reads:

> _"...I don't know which one is really me anymore."_

Another page contains only four words.

> _"...I feel lost."_

---

## **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATIONS**

Personnel who have briefly encountered Taiga report that she has become quieter than ever.

She rarely remains in one place.

She avoids conversations before they begin.

She appears exhausted, often carrying the expression of someone who has not truly rested in a long time.

Medical believes that prolonged isolation, grief, nightmares, and persistent self-blame have combined into a severe crisis of identity.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Director Lucious

After reading the newest report, Director Lucious closed the folder immediately.

He did not continue to the attached journal pages.

After a long silence, he said,

> "...She isn't losing herself."

Another pause.

> "...She's losing the ability to believe she's still herself."

He instructed Medical to continue focusing on compassionate care whenever opportunities arose.

---

### Neko

Neko was shown only the clinical summary.

He declined to read the journal itself.

> "...Those thoughts..."

He looked toward the empty workshop doorway.

> "...She shouldn't have to carry them alone."

---

### Miss Neko

Miss Neko quietly folded one of the scarves Taiga used to wear.

She placed it back exactly where it belonged.

> "...We'll be ready."

---

### Bao

Bao continued preparing meals every day.

When another untouched container was returned, carefully washed as always, she smiled sadly.

> "...At least she's eating."

---

### Frost

Frost stood beside the window overlooking the snowy courtyard.

> "...She's still fighting."

She spoke softly, almost to herself.

> "...Even if she doesn't believe it."

---

### Star

Star eventually asked the question no one could answer.

> "...How do you help someone..."

She looked down.

> "...Who thinks everyone would be safer without them?"

The room remained silent.

---

### Nightshade

Nightshade looked over the report once before closing it.

> "...Pain..."

She paused.

> "...Can convince people that it is their identity."

> "...It isn't."

---

## **THE APPARITION**

That evening, Taiga's mother appeared once more in Room N-07.

She was shown one of the recovered journal pages.

The distorted red eye stared back from the paper.

For the first time since her manifestations had begun, the apparition said nothing.

She simply looked at the drawing.

Her expression broke into quiet sorrow.

After several moments, she whispered,

> "...That's not what I see."

She turned toward the empty bed.

> "...When I look at my daughter..."

Her voice trembled.

> "...I still see my little girl."

Unable to reach across the distance that separated them, she could only remain where she was.

Watching.

Waiting.

Hoping.

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical concludes that Taiga's greatest struggle is no longer simply fear.

It is a profound loss of confidence in her own identity.

The recurring drawings, fragmented writing, and withdrawal all suggest that she increasingly defines herself through her trauma rather than through the relationships and experiences that once brought her comfort.

Continued support remains essential.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Near dawn, a maintenance camera briefly captured Taiga sitting alone beneath a snow-covered overhang.

She held a notebook in her lap.

For several minutes she stared at a blank page.

Then she slowly drew a single eye.

She looked at it for a long time.

Finally...

She closed the notebook without tearing the page out.

Far away, in Room N-07, her mother stood silently beside the untouched bed.

She could not call out.

She could not hold her daughter.

She could only watch.

And hope that one day...

Taiga would be able to see herself through the eyes of the people who still loved her.
