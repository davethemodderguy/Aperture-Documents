# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-115**

### **SEARCH INCIDENT — RECOVERY OF MART / FIRST CONTACT WITH TAIGA**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – ACTIVE SEARCH

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

Following his voluntary departure from the Blacksite residential sector, Mart Sunbringer continued searching alone for Taiga.

Despite days of searching through maintenance corridors, abandoned utility tunnels, storage sections, and unused service routes, he was unable to locate her.

The prolonged failure began to take a severe emotional toll.

---

## **OBSERVATION**

Recovered footage and later evidence suggest Mart gradually became slower and less attentive as the search continued.

Witnesses were absent.

The following reconstruction is based on environmental evidence and Mart's eventual account.

Mart repeatedly whispered to himself while searching.

> "...Where are you...?"

Hours later...

The question changed.

> "...Why didn't I stay...?"

Eventually...

He stopped blaming circumstance.

He blamed himself.

> "...I left..."

He lowered his head.

> "...I should've stayed..."

> "...It's my fault..."

---

## **THE ACCIDENT**

While flying through an older maintenance sector, Mart failed to notice a temporary high-voltage electrical cable suspended across the corridor during ongoing repair work.

Still distracted...

He flew directly into it.

A bright flash illuminated the tunnel.

The resulting electrical shock threw him violently to the ground.

Personnel estimate the impact rendered him unable to continue flying.

---

## **FINAL MOMENTS OF CONSCIOUSNESS**

Mart attempted to stand.

His wings refused to respond.

His vision blurred.

The lights above him stretched into indistinct streaks.

He could no longer hear clearly.

His final coherent thought, according to his later recollection, was simple.

> "...Sorry..."

His eyes slowly closed.

---

## **UNEXPECTED CONTACT**

Just before losing consciousness completely...

He felt something wet against his face.

A gentle lick.

Then another.

He struggled to focus.

Through fading vision, he could make out white fur.

A familiar pair of ears.

A tail.

Blue and red eyes filled with panic.

It was...

Taiga.

Still in her snow leopard form.

She made a quiet, distressed chirping sound before carefully gripping the back of Mart's clothing.

Despite her own exhaustion...

She began dragging him through the maintenance corridors.

Slowly.

Patiently.

Stopping every few moments to make sure he was still breathing.

---

## **DISCOVERY**

Search Team Delta located both siblings several hours later after following disturbed dust and drag marks through an abandoned service tunnel.

They found Taiga lying protectively beside Mart.

The moment she heard approaching footsteps, she immediately stood.

Her body was tense.

She positioned herself between the unconscious Mart and the approaching personnel.

She did not attack.

She did not advance.

She simply refused to move away from him.

Director Lucious, who had arrived with the team, slowly removed his jacket and laid it on the floor before taking a step back.

His voice remained calm.

> "...Taiga."

Her ears twitched.

> "...You kept him safe."

The snow leopard's posture faltered.

She looked down at Mart.

Then back at Director Lucious.

For the first time in weeks...

Her defensive stance weakened.

### **Closing Observation**

While being transported to the infirmary, Mart briefly regained consciousness.

His vision was still blurred.

He felt soft fur pressed against his hand.

Without opening his eyes, he weakly curled his fingers into it.

A quiet rumble answered him.

The familiar sound of Taiga purring.

Mart managed a tiny smile.

> "...Found you..."

The purring didn't stop.

For the first time since they had both disappeared...

Neither of the Sunbringer siblings was alone.
