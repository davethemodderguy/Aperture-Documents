# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-125**

### **SEARCH UPDATE — FAILED CONTACT / TAIGA'S WITHDRAWAL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – ACTIVE SEARCH / PSYCHOLOGICAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Search efforts briefly resulted in multiple visual contacts with Mart Sunbringer.

Each encounter ended identically.

Mart did not attack.

He did not speak.

He did not allow anyone to approach.

Most notably...

He avoided only one person.

Taiga.

---

## **FAILED CONTACTS**

Over the following days, Taiga repeatedly left with search teams despite Medical's objections.

She insisted on looking for Mart herself.

She believed that if anyone could bring him home...

It would be her.

The opposite proved true.

---

### **FIRST SIGHTING**

Mart was seen standing on the edge of an abandoned observation tower.

Taiga slowly approached.

> "...Mart..."

He turned.

For a brief second, their eyes met.

His expression held no anger.

Only overwhelming sadness.

Tears silently ran down his face.

Taiga took another careful step.

> "...Please..."

Mart gently shook his head.

His luminous white wings unfolded.

Without a word...

He pushed off the tower.

The wind caught beneath his wings.

Within seconds, he disappeared into the clouds.

---

### **SECOND SIGHTING**

Two days later.

Whitefall Lake.

Mart was sitting beneath a snow-covered tree.

Reading.

The same book he had once read aloud to Taiga while she recovered.

Taiga approached slowly.

She didn't call his name.

She simply sat several meters away.

Waiting.

Mart looked toward her.

Closed the book.

Tears welled in his eyes once more.

He stood.

Spread the wings of light.

And quietly flew away.

The book remained behind.

---

### **THIRD SIGHTING**

The final confirmed sighting occurred on the roof of an unused maintenance building.

Taiga reached him before anyone else.

She never got closer than ten meters.

She whispered,

> "...I'm sorry..."

Mart heard her.

Witnesses confirmed he closed his eyes.

His shoulders trembled.

A tear fell onto the concrete.

For a moment...

It looked as though he might walk toward her.

Instead...

He stepped backward.

The white wings appeared again.

He left without saying a single word.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services believe Mart is not fleeing out of hatred.

Nor fear.

Current assessment suggests he is emotionally incapable of facing Taiga after the argument.

Likewise...

Taiga's repeated unsuccessful attempts have significantly worsened her own psychological state.

---

## **TAIGA'S WITHDRAWAL**

Following the third failed contact, Taiga voluntarily ended her participation in the search.

She returned to the residential wing.

Closed her bedroom door.

And stopped seeking out others.

She declined invitations to meals.

She avoided common areas.

Even when friends visited...

She rarely answered.

When she did...

It was only to quietly say,

> "...I'm tired."

Medical notes that Taiga has not become hostile.

She has simply...

Withdrawn.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, a security camera overlooking Whitefall Lake captured a familiar figure sitting alone on the frozen shoreline.

It was Taiga.

She wasn't searching.

She wasn't calling Mart's name anymore.

She simply watched the quiet water.

Hours later, another camera on the opposite side of the lake recorded a second figure.

Mart.

He had been there the entire time.

Far enough away that Taiga never saw him.

He looked across the frozen lake toward his sister.

His eyes shimmered with tears.

He took one hesitant step forward.

Then stopped.

The white wings slowly unfolded.

He looked at her one last time.

And disappeared silently into the night sky.

Neither sibling knew...

They had spent the evening together.

Separated only by a frozen lake they no longer knew how to cross.
