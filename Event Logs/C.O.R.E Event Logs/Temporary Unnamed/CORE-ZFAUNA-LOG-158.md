# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-158**

### **LONG-TERM PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION — MART SUNBRINGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – RESIDENT WELFARE / CONFIDENTIAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

Over the past several months, Psychological Services have documented a gradual but significant shift in Mart Sunbringer's personality.

The change did not occur suddenly.

Rather, it developed over time following repeated traumatic experiences, prolonged emotional stress, and the period in which he and Taiga became separated after their argument.

Personnel who knew Mart before these events consistently describe him as a different child.

His kindness remains unchanged.

His confidence does not.

---

## **PREVIOUS BASELINE**

When Mart first became a resident of the Blacksite, he was generally described as:

- Calm.
    
- Thoughtful.
    
- Intelligent.
    
- Patient.
    
- A good listener.
    
- Quietly confident.
    
- Comfortable speaking when he had something to say.
    

He rarely sought attention.

But neither did he avoid it.

He appeared secure in the knowledge that he belonged.

---

## **CURRENT PRESENTATION**

Mart now presents very differently.

Staff observations consistently describe him as:

- Quiet.
    
- Shy.
    
- Hesitant to speak first.
    
- Easily startled by emotionally sensitive conversations.
    
- Prone to leaving situations when overwhelmed.
    

During periods of emotional distress, he frequently apologizes, even when no one has blamed him.

Common examples include:

> "I'm sorry."

> "Sorry..."

> "I didn't mean to..."

> "I'm bothering everyone."

Psychological Services note that these apologies are often expressions of anxiety rather than admissions of wrongdoing.

---

## **FEAR OF BEING A BURDEN**

One of the most frequently recurring themes in Mart's counseling sessions is the fear of inconveniencing others.

He often worries that:

- He talks too much.
    
- He asks for too much help.
    
- His emotional struggles exhaust those around him.
    
- People spend time with him out of obligation rather than affection.
    

When reassured, he usually accepts the reassurance...

For a while.

The concern often returns during periods of stress.

---

## **ABANDONMENT ANXIETY**

Although Mart is comfortable spending time alone, Psychological Services emphasize that this should not be mistaken for emotional independence.

His greatest fear is not solitude.

It is being left behind.

The individuals most commonly associated with this fear are:

- Taiga Sunbringer.
    
- Astral Sunbringer.
    
- His roommates.
    
- Those he considers family.
    

When one of these individuals leaves unexpectedly without explanation—even for ordinary reasons—Mart occasionally becomes noticeably anxious until he knows they are safe.

These reactions have gradually become less intense, but they remain clinically significant.

---

## **OBSERVED INCIDENT**

One evening, several residents were leaving the common room after movie night.

Mart stayed behind to help clean.

When he finished, he noticed the room had become quiet.

For a brief moment...

He believed everyone had left without him.

Security footage shows him standing completely still.

His expression immediately changed.

He quietly whispered,

> "...Did I take too long...?"

Before he could leave, Taiga walked back through the doorway carrying two forgotten blankets.

She looked surprised.

> "...Mart?"

He looked up.

His shoulders visibly relaxed.

Taiga tilted her head.

> "...You okay?"

Mart smiled weakly.

> "...Yeah..."

A pause.

> "...I thought everyone left."

Taiga immediately understood.

She walked over and gently bumped his shoulder.

> "...I'd never leave without my little brother."

Mart lowered his head.

> "...Sorry."

Taiga sighed with exaggerated dramatic annoyance.

> "...See?"

> "...You're apologizing again."

Mart blinked.

Then laughed quietly.

> "...Sorry."

Taiga couldn't help laughing too.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral worries most about the number of apologies Mart gives each day.

She has started answering them the same way every time.

Whenever Mart says,

> "...I'm sorry."

She replies,

> "...For what?"

More often than not...

He cannot answer.

---

### Taiga Sunbringer

Taiga has made a habit of telling Mart where she is going, even if she is only leaving for a few minutes.

Not because he asks.

Because she knows he worries.

She also makes sure to say one thing before leaving.

> "...I'll be back."

Mart always smiles when he hears it.

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious reviewed Mart's latest psychological report.

His closing remark was concise.

> "...Confidence can be rebuilt."

> "...Provided someone keeps reminding him he belongs."

---

### Neko

Neko quietly altered Mart's room access panel.

Instead of displaying **Occupied**, it now reads:

> **Mart's In. Knock First.**

When asked why, Neko answered,

> "...Rooms shouldn't feel like places people disappear."

---

### Frost

Frost has noticed that Mart relaxes most during small gatherings rather than large groups.

She has gradually shifted several recreational activities into quieter settings.

Attendance improved.

Mart attended more often.

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star still greets Mart exactly the same way every morning.

Without fail.

> "...Morning, Mart!"

Mart always smiles back.

Sometimes only a little.

But he always does.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL NOTE**

Psychological Services emphasize that Mart's quieter personality should not be mistaken for emotional weakness.

He remains compassionate, observant, and dependable.

The difference is that he now carries visible fear alongside those qualities.

Healing is expected to be gradual.

Staff are encouraged to continue treating him normally while remaining mindful of his emotional wellbeing.

---

## **Closing Observation**

Several weeks ago, Mart would often wait until everyone had left the dining hall before quietly clearing his own plate.

Recently, Taiga noticed the habit.

Now, whenever she finishes eating, she waits by the door.

She doesn't rush him.

She simply stands there.

When Mart finally walks over, she smiles.

> "...Ready?"

Mart nods.

> "...Yeah."

They leave together.

It is a small routine.

One that reminds Mart, every single day...

That no one is leaving him behind.
