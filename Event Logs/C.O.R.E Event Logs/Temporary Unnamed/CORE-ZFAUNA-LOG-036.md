# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-036**

### **CRITICAL INCIDENT — PTSD TRIGGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – MEDICAL / PSYCHOLOGICAL / INTERNAL

---

### **Incident Summary**

**Date:** [REDACTED]  
**Location:** Blacksite Training Wing / Exterior Courtyard

At approximately **15:42**, an unscheduled firearm discharge occurred inside the Training Wing.

The weapon had been loaded with standard training ammunition. No one was struck, and the discharge was later determined to be accidental.

However, the shot occurred less than a meter from Taiga's head.

The sharp report echoed through the corridor.

---

## **Immediate Response**

Witnesses described Taiga freezing completely.

Her ears flattened.

Her pupils dilated.

Her breathing became rapid.

For several seconds, she did not respond to anyone speaking to her.

Then—

She transformed.

A brief shimmer of white light surrounded her as she involuntarily reverted into her small snow leopard form.

Without looking back, she sprinted.

---

## **Escape**

Taiga raced through the residential corridor, past Security, and out an exterior maintenance door before personnel understood what had happened.

The Icelandic winter outside was well below freezing.

Snow fell heavily across the courtyard.

Security cameras tracked the small snow leopard running blindly through the storm until she disappeared behind a line of snow-covered maintenance barriers.

A search was initiated immediately.

---

## **Recovery**

Approximately eleven minutes later, Search Team Two located Taiga near the outer perimeter.

She had curled herself tightly into the snow beside a concrete retaining wall, partially sheltered from the wind.

She was trembling uncontrollably.

Her tail was wrapped tightly around herself.

Witnesses reported that she appeared unaware of the search team at first.

Instead, she repeatedly called out through quiet sobs.

> "...Mom..."

A pause.

> "...Mom..."

Another.

> "...Please..."

The responding personnel did not approach immediately.

Following guidance from Medical, they remained several meters away, speaking calmly and avoiding sudden movements.

One operator quietly contacted Room N-07.

Within minutes, familiar faces arrived.

---

## **Room N-07 Response**

Aster was the first to reach the scene.

She slowly knelt in the snow several steps away.

Rather than trying to pick Taiga up, she simply placed her whiteboard where Taiga could see it.

Written across it were just three words.

> **We're here.**

Taiga looked up briefly.

Then lowered her head again.

---

Bao arrived next, wrapping a thick winter blanket around her own shoulders before carefully sitting nearby.

She spoke softly.

> "You're safe."

No response.

After a few moments, she repeated it.

> "You're safe."

---

Frost quietly stood between Taiga and the blowing wind, shielding her from the worst of the weather without saying anything.

---

Star approached more slowly than anyone had ever seen her move.

She crouched down near Aster.

She didn't try to hug Taiga.

She didn't reach for her.

She simply stayed close.

---

Nightshade remained a short distance behind the others, watching the surroundings to ensure no one else approached unexpectedly.

---

Neko and Miss Neko arrived carrying additional blankets and warm packs.

Neko knelt beside Bao.

Keeping his voice low, he said,

> "...Nobody's going to fire another shot."

Miss Neko gently placed a warmed blanket on the snow beside Taiga instead of over her, giving her space to move toward it on her own.

After several quiet minutes...

Taiga slowly inched closer.

---

## **Medical Assessment**

Once Taiga allowed personnel to approach, Medical escorted her back inside.

No physical injuries were sustained beyond mild cold exposure.

Medical concluded that the incident represented a severe PTSD response triggered by the unexpected firearm discharge in close proximity.

The response was involuntary and consistent with Taiga's previously documented trauma involving armed hunters.

---

## **Follow-Up Actions**

The following measures were implemented immediately:

- Live-fire exercises are suspended whenever Taiga is present in adjacent training areas.
    
- Additional sound-isolation procedures have been installed around firearm ranges.
    
- Personnel are reminded to announce any authorized live-fire training well in advance.
    
- Residential staff have been briefed on appropriate responses should Taiga experience another trauma-related episode.

Support from her roommates and staff continues as she settles back into her normal routine.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Later that night, the winter storm outside had finally begun to ease.

Room N-07 was quiet.

Leo slept curled against Taiga's side.

Mart stood watch from the headboard.

The room's heater hummed softly.

Before turning off the light, Neko looked back toward the sleeping snow leopard.

He quietly closed the door behind him, leaving the room warm, still, and peaceful once again.
