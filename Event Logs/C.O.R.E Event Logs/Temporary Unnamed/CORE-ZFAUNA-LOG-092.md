# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-092**

### **MEDICAL RECOVERY REPORT — MART**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – MEDICAL / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following several weeks of treatment, rest, and rehabilitation, Mart has made a complete recovery.

Medical examinations confirm that:

- The fracture to his **left wing** has fully healed.
    
- The fracture to his **left hand** in humanoid form has also fully healed.
    
- No permanent impairment has been detected.
    
- Mart has been cleared to resume unrestricted flight.
    

While conventional treatment contributed significantly to his recovery, the most dramatic improvement occurred after an anomalous intervention involving Star.

---

## **THE PROBLEM**

Several days into recovery, Medical noted that Mart's healing had begun to plateau.

The bones were mending, but far more slowly than expected.

Star quietly asked Medical a question.

> "...Would my tears help?"

The room went silent.

Everyone knew about the remarkable regenerative properties contained within Star's tears.

The problem was obvious.

Making Star cry was something nobody wanted to do.

Least of all Mart.

---

## **A DIFFICULT DECISION**

When Star was told why everyone hesitated, she immediately shook her head.

> "...If it'll help Mart..."

> "...Then I don't mind."

Ironically...

The hardest part became convincing **Star herself** to actually cry.

She kept smiling and insisting she was fine.

Bao eventually knelt beside her.

> "...Star..."

> "...You don't have to be strong every second."

Astral gently hugged her.

> "...Helping someone doesn't make your tears weak."

Finally...

After a long conversation and plenty of reassurance, Star allowed herself to cry.

Medical carefully collected only a very small amount.

The tears were then incorporated into Mart's treatment under controlled conditions.

---

## **RESULTS**

The regenerative response was immediate.

Within days:

- Bone regeneration accelerated dramatically.
    
- Inflammation subsided.
    
- Feather regrowth completed.
    
- Wing strength returned to baseline.
    

Medical declared the treatment successful.

---

## **THE FIRST FLIGHT**

The entire residential wing gathered in the courtyard.

No one announced anything.

Mart simply walked outside.

He looked up at the open sky.

He spread both wings.

Paused.

Then pushed off the ground.

For a brief second...

Everyone held their breath.

Mart climbed effortlessly into the air.

One graceful circle.

Then another.

Then another.

He wasn't testing his wing anymore.

He was simply flying because he could.

When he landed, Taiga immediately scooped him into a hug.

"...You can fly again..."

Mart nodded happily.

> "...Mm."

> "...Thank you."
