# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-098**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION — PREHENSILE TAIL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Medical and Research have confirmed that Taiga Sunbringer's snow leopard tail possesses a previously undocumented characteristic.

In addition to aiding balance and expression, it is **fully prehensile**, possessing enough dexterity and strength to securely grasp objects—and people.

The discovery was made entirely by accident.

---

## **PRIMARY TARGET**

For reasons no one can adequately explain...

Taiga's tail most commonly grabs **Mart**.

Not aggressively.

Simply...

To stop him.

From doing...

Well...

A lot of things.

---

## **DOCUMENTED INCIDENTS**

### **Incident 1 — "You're Not Flying Away."**

After being asked an uncomfortable question by a visitor, Mart instinctively transformed into an owl and prepared to take off.

Before he could leave—

_Wrap._

Taiga's tail gently looped around one of his legs.

Mart flapped once.

Twice.

Then looked down.

Taiga smiled apologetically.

> "...Stay?"

Mart sighed.

"...Okay."

He transformed back into his human form and sat beside her.

---

### **Incident 2 — "Come Eat."**

Mart became engrossed in reading and completely forgot lunch.

Without saying a word, Taiga's tail reached around the doorway, gently wrapped around his waist, and slowly pulled him toward the dining room.

Mart looked up from his book.

"..."

He did not resist.

> "...I forgot."

---

### **Incident 3 — "No Escaping the Hug."**

Astral watched as Mart attempted to quietly slip away after becoming embarrassed by Star complimenting him.

Taiga's tail caught him around the middle before he reached the hallway.

She smiled.

"...One hug."

Mart looked defeated.

"...One."

He accepted the hug.

Only then did the tail let go.

---

### **Incident 4 — "The Book Thief."**

Mart had been reading for nearly three hours without taking a break.

Taiga's tail quietly reached over his shoulder.

Lifted the book out of his hands.

Then placed it on a nearby shelf.

Mart blinked.

"..."

He looked at Taiga.

She pointed toward his untouched cup of water.

"...Hydrate."

Mart sighed.

"...Yes, sister."

### **Closing Observation**

Late that evening, Mart quietly transformed into an owl and prepared to fly out onto the balcony for some fresh air.

Just as he lifted off—

A familiar white-and-black tail gently curled around him.

He looked back.

Taiga hadn't even looked up from her book.

She simply said,

> "...Take a jacket."

Mart blinked.

He transformed into his human form, put on his coat, then smiled.

> "...Thank you."

The tail released him.

This time...

He flew outside.

Properly dressed.

As Taiga had intended all along.
