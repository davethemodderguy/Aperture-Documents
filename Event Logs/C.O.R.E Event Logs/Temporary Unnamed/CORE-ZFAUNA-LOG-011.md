# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-011**

### **EQUIPMENT OBSERVATION — TAIGA'S CRYOGENIC KATANA**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – N.O.S.T. / EQUIPMENT RECORD

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

During a routine training session with Frost, Taiga revealed that she possessed a personal weapon which had never previously been observed by Blacksite personnel.

Unlike conventional carried equipment, the weapon does not appear to be stored on Taiga's person.

Instead, witnesses consistently describe it as **"slowly appearing in reality"**, gradually materializing in her hand over the course of several seconds before becoming fully solid.

N.O.S.T. has not yet determined the exact mechanism responsible for this phenomenon.

---

# **I. PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

Once fully manifested, the weapon is identified as a traditional Japanese-style katana.

Notable features include:

- A slightly translucent blade with a frosted appearance.
    
- A white-wrapped handle.
    
- A simple circular guard.
    
- A small silver snowflake keychain attached to the end of the grip.
    
- No visible maker's marks or inscriptions.
    

Despite its unusual manifestation, structural analysis indicates the sword behaves like a normal physical object once fully materialized.

---

# **II. EXTREME TEMPERATURE**

The blade continuously emits an intense cold.

Thermal imaging equipment was unable to measure its exact surface temperature, as several sensors reached their lower operating limits during testing.

Personnel who briefly attempted to touch the blade while wearing insulated gloves described it as:

> **"...Like touching solid liquid nitrogen."**

Contact without specialized protection is strongly discouraged.

Medical recorded immediate cold injuries in test materials exposed to the blade for even short periods.

---

# **III. AUTHORIZED HANDLERS**

To date, only two individuals have been able to comfortably hold the weapon without protective equipment:

- **Taiga**
    
- **Frost**
    

Neither reported any discomfort while handling it.

Attempts by other personnel resulted in the weapon becoming painfully cold to hold almost immediately.

As a precaution, the katana is not to be handled by unauthorized personnel.

---

# **IV. OBSERVED PROPERTIES**

Controlled materials testing documented several unusual characteristics.

### Water Freezing

Any liquid water directly contacting the blade freezes almost instantly.

This effect occurs passively and continuously.

During one demonstration, Taiga gently dipped only the tip of the blade into a container of water.

Within moments, ice rapidly spread outward from the point of contact until the entire container had frozen.

---

### Cryogenic Residue

A second phenomenon occurs only while the blade is actively cutting or scraping against another object.

During contact, a clear, water-like liquid briefly appears along the cutting edge.

The liquid rapidly freezes whatever surface it touches before dissipating.

Interestingly, simply touching the stationary blade does **not** produce this residue.

It has only been observed during slicing or similar movement.

N.O.S.T. is continuing to investigate the mechanism behind this effect.

---

# **V. FROST'S OBSERVATION**

Following a training session, Frost examined the katana carefully.

After several minutes she looked at Taiga.

> "...It doesn't feel like ordinary ice."

Taiga nodded.

> "...It isn't."

The two spent the remainder of the afternoon discussing cryogenic control techniques rather than practicing with the weapon itself.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Designation:** Taiga's Personal Katana

**Manifestation:** Gradually materializes into reality at Taiga's command.

**Known Properties:**

- Extremely low surface temperature.
    
- Instantly freezes liquid water on contact.
    
- Produces a brief cryogenic, water-like residue only while the blade is actively moving against another surface.
    
- Safely handled only by Taiga and Frost.
    

Further study has been assigned to N.O.S.T. to better understand the weapon's origin and the mechanism behind its unique manifestation.

---

### **Closing Observation**

After training concluded, Taiga allowed the katana to fade from existence.

The translucent blade slowly dissolved into shimmering frost-like particles until nothing remained.

Only the gently swaying snowflake keychain was visible for a brief moment before it, too, vanished.

Taiga brushed a few snowflakes from her white scarf.

Then walked outside with Aster, Leo, and Frost to enjoy the fresh Icelandic snowfall.
