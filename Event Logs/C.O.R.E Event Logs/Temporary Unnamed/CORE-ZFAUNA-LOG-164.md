# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-164**

### **CAFETERIA OBSERVATION — THE SANDWICH EXPERIMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – SLICE OF LIFE

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following repeated recommendations from Cafeteria Services, Taiga Sunbringer and Mart Sunbringer agreed to try the Blacksite's freshly made sandwiches.

Neither sibling had shown much interest in sandwiches before, usually preferring rice dishes, soups, salads, or fruit.

The results were... unexpectedly wholesome.

---

## **OBSERVED INCIDENT**

Lunch had only just begun.

Taiga stared suspiciously at the neatly wrapped sandwich on her tray.

Mart looked at his.

Then at Taiga.

> **Mart:** "...Have you ever actually eaten one?"

Taiga slowly shook her head.

> "...No."

Mart nodded.

> "...Me neither."

The two siblings looked at the sandwiches as though they had been issued experimental equipment.

Nearby staff quietly watched.

---

Taiga picked hers up first.

She sniffed it.

> "...It smells nice."

Mart copied her.

> "...It does."

Taiga took a cautious bite.

She stopped chewing.

Mart waited nervously.

A few seconds later...

Her ears perked up.

Her tail started wagging.

> "...Mart."

He blinked.

> "...Yeah?"

She swallowed.

Then smiled widely.

> "...Bread."

Mart laughed.

> "...Yes..."

> "...That's usually how sandwiches work."

---

Encouraged, Mart took a bite of his own.

He chewed thoughtfully.

Looked down at it.

Then quietly nodded.

> "...I like this."

Taiga had already taken three more bites.

> "...Why didn't we eat these before?"

Mart shrugged.

> "...Nobody asked."

---

## **THE GREAT DEBATE**

Halfway through lunch...

Taiga looked at Mart very seriously.

> "...Is a sandwich just..."

She held it up dramatically.

> "...A salad you can hold?"

Mart thought about it.

Far longer than anyone expected.

Finally...

> "...Technically..."

Another pause.

> "...Maybe."

Taiga immediately pointed at him.

> "...See!"

Mart smiled.

> "...I'm not agreeing."

> "...I'm saying maybe."

The debate continued for nearly fifteen minutes.

No conclusion was reached.

---

## **LEO'S INVOLVEMENT**

Leo, Star's famously sleepy banana snake, slowly slithered onto the table.

He stared directly at Taiga's sandwich.

Taiga noticed.

> "...Don't."

Leo blinked.

Taiga looked away for exactly one second.

When she looked back...

One tomato slice was gone.

Leo was already swallowing it.

Mart sighed.

> "...He's doing it again."

Taiga pointed accusingly at the snake.

> "...You're banned from my sandwich."

Leo appeared entirely unbothered.

---

> "...Would you like grilled sandwiches next time?"

Taiga's eyes lit up.

Mart looked curious.

Both answered at the same time.

> "...Yes."

---

Neko walked by, looked at the nearly empty trays, and nodded approvingly.

> "...Good."

> "...Less paperwork than repairing doors."

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star arrived just in time to see Leo steal another tomato slice.

She looked at Leo.

Looked at the siblings.

Then laughed.

> "...He's got good taste."

---

## **Closing Observation**

As lunch ended, Taiga looked at the last bite of her sandwich.

Then at Mart.

> **Taiga:** "...Tomorrow?"

Mart smiled softly.

> "...Sandwiches again?"

Taiga nodded enthusiastically.

> "...Sandwiches again."

Mart chuckled.

> "...Okay, Big Sis."

The two returned their trays together.

Leo quietly followed behind them...

Hopefully searching for someone else's tomatoes this time.
