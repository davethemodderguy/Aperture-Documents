# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-014**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION — SOCIAL WITHDRAWAL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – MEDICAL / INTERNAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Residential staff have documented a gradual increase in Taiga's tendency to isolate herself from her roommates.

Unlike previous observations, this change has not involved conflict, arguments, or any apparent hostility.

Instead, Taiga has simply begun making herself... less present.

---

# **I. CREATING DISTANCE**

The change occurred gradually over several weeks.

She still smiled.

She still greeted everyone politely.

She still spent time with Frost in the snow and spoke with Neko during the day.

But when evening arrived...

She quietly disappeared.

At first she returned after midnight.

Then before dawn.

Eventually...

She stopped returning to Room N-07 altogether during the night.

No one knew exactly where she slept.

Security cameras only ever caught brief glimpses of her walking across snow-covered rooftops or sitting alone in quiet courtyards before moving out of view.

She always returned before breakfast as though nothing had happened.

---

# **II. A FAMILIAR EXCUSE**

Whenever someone asked where she had been, Taiga always answered with the same gentle smile.

> "I didn't want to bother anyone."

If asked whether she had slept...

> "A little."

If asked whether she was alright...

> "I'm okay."

Medical noted that her answers remained calm and sincere, but increasingly avoided any discussion about herself.

---

# **III. ROOM N-07**

The room felt noticeably quieter.

Taiga's neatly made bed remained untouched most mornings.

Her white scarf was usually gone overnight.

The only indication that she had visited at all was the occasional dusting of melted snow near the entrance.

---

# **IV. ASTER**

Of everyone in the household...

Aster noticed first.

Each night she waited on the heated rug where she and Taiga had always slept together.

Each night...

Taiga never came.

Aster stayed awake as long as she could.

Watching the door.

Listening for footsteps.

But eventually, her instincts always won.

Like any ordinary cat, exhaustion would finally overtake her.

She would curl into a ball on the rug...

And fall asleep still waiting.

One morning, Miss Neko found Aster asleep beside Taiga's empty blanket, still holding her small whiteboard.

Only two words had been written before she drifted off.

> **Come home.**

---

# **V. LEO**

Leo's routine changed very little.

He spent most of his time asleep, blissfully unaware of the growing distance.

Sometimes he slept around Aster.

Sometimes around Taiga's folded scarf when she left it behind.

On the nights Taiga briefly returned before dawn, Leo instinctively crawled onto her shoulders without waking.

When she quietly left again...

He usually remained asleep.

Veterinary jokingly concluded that Leo's loyalty was exceeded only by his ability to sleep through almost anything.

---

# **VI. PERSONNEL OBSERVATIONS**

### Neko

He stopped asking where Taiga had been.

Instead, he simply made a little extra breakfast every morning.

Sometimes she ate it.

Sometimes she quietly wrapped it up to eat later.

---

### Miss Neko

Each evening she continued making Taiga's bed, even knowing it would probably remain unused.

She never removed the blanket from the heater.

> "...Just in case."

---

### Frost

The snow sculptures Taiga once built every evening became less frequent.

Frost quietly left fresh snow untouched whenever she noticed one beginning to take shape.

---

### Bao

Noticed the empty chair during dinner more than anyone else.

She never commented on it aloud.

---

### Star

One evening she looked at Taiga's untouched bed before softly closing the bedroom door.

> "...We'll leave the light on."

---

### Nightshade

Reviewed overnight security footage several times.

Her report contained no disciplinary recommendations.

Only one observation.

> **"She isn't running away."**

> **"She's keeping herself away."**

---

# **VII. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical believes Taiga's increasing isolation is consistent with someone who has become convinced that reducing their own presence will lessen the burden they place on others.

There is no evidence that she wishes to leave the Blacksite.

She continues participating in daily activities, eating regularly, and interacting politely.

However, she has begun withdrawing from the sense of home she had previously embraced.

Medical recommends maintaining normal routines and keeping communication open, while avoiding pressure that could reinforce her belief that she is causing problems simply by needing support.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga continues to distance herself from her roommates during the night.

Her bed in Room N-07 remains available, and her roommates have intentionally left her place unchanged.

No one has claimed the empty space beside the heater.

---

### **Closing Observation**

At 05:18, before sunrise, a hallway camera recorded Taiga quietly opening the door to Room N-07.

She stepped inside without making a sound.

Aster was asleep on the heated rug.

Leo was curled against her side.

Taiga stood there for several moments.

She gently pulled the blanket a little higher over Aster's shoulders.

Smiled faintly.

Then turned around and left the room before anyone else woke up.

The rug beside the heater remained empty.
