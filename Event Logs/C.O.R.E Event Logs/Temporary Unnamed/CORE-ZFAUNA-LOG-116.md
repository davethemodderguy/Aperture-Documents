# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-116**

### **MEDICAL STATUS UPDATE — FORCED ANOMALOUS FORMS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – MEDICAL / BIOLOGICAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the recovery of the Sunbringer siblings, Medical Services has confirmed that **neither Taiga nor Mart is currently capable of returning to their human forms.**

This is **not** a voluntary transformation.

Extensive examinations indicate that both anomalous transformations have become biologically "locked" as a protective response to severe physical trauma.

Medical personnel have issued a standing order:

**Neither sibling is to attempt forced reversion until cleared by Medical Services.**

---

# **SUBJECT: TAIGA SUNBRINGER**

### **Current Form**

Snow Leopard (Forced)

### **Cause**

Taiga was located suffering from advanced exhaustion, dehydration, and prolonged exposure while hidden within the Blacksite.

Medical examinations also confirmed significant **hyperthermia** caused by sustained physical exertion, inadequate rest, and failure to regulate her body temperature.

Her body appears to have stabilized itself by remaining in its anomalous leopard form.

Researchers believe this form is currently placing less strain on her physiology than a return to human anatomy would.

Attempts to trigger a transformation produced no response.

---

### **Current Condition**

Although conscious and cooperative with trusted personnel, Taiga remains physically weak.

She has nevertheless retained all previously documented anomalous abilities, including:

- Ground Glide (5 cm above surfaces).
    
- Air Jump.
    
- Frost Walker.
    
- Prehensile tail.
    
- Other documented leopard-form traits.
    

Medical staff have prohibited strenuous activity until her condition improves.

Taiga has already ignored this recommendation twice by trying to follow Mart's hospital bed.

---

# **SUBJECT: MART SUNBRINGER**

### **Current Form**

Owl (Forced)

### **Cause**

Mart sustained a severe electrical shock after colliding with an energized maintenance cable carrying approximately **500 volts**.

The electrical discharge, combined with blunt-force trauma from the subsequent fall, resulted in extensive physiological stress.

His transformation ability appears to have entered a protective state.

Mart is presently **unable** to assume his human form.

---

### **Current Condition**

Mart has regained consciousness.

His ability to fly has gradually returned.

Veterinary and Medical specialists report no lasting wing fractures.

However, he continues to exhibit fatigue following even short flights.

Personnel are instructed **not** to encourage unnecessary flying until he has fully recovered.

---

## **TRANSFORMATION STATUS**

Testing confirms:

- Taiga is **unable** to become human.
    
- Mart is **unable** to become human.
    

Neither transformation has responded to voluntary attempts, emotional triggers, or controlled anomalous stimulation.

Medical Services has therefore suspended all transformation testing until further notice.

## **MEDICAL ORDERS**

Until further notice:

- No attempts to force either sibling into their human form.
    
- Minimize unnecessary physical exertion.
    
- Maintain hydration and nutrition.
    
- Continue psychological support.
    
- Allow the siblings to remain together whenever medically practical.
    

Medical staff note that both Taiga and Mart display reduced stress responses when they can see or hear one another.

---

### **Closing Observation**

During the first quiet night in the infirmary, Mart awoke from a light sleep.

Still too weak to fly far, he fluttered only a short distance.

He landed gently on Taiga's shoulder.

The snow leopard stirred but did not wake.

Instead, she instinctively curled her tail around the perch where Mart had settled, securing him without squeezing.

Mart tucked his head beneath one wing.

Safe.

Warm.

Close enough to hear the slow rhythm of his sister's breathing.

For the first time in many days...

Neither sibling had to wonder where the other was.
