# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-069**

### **WELFARE INCIDENT — UNEXPLAINED SELF-ISOLATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – MEDICAL / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Over the course of several weeks, personnel assigned to Room N-07 documented a gradual but unmistakable change in Taiga's behavior.

She did not leave the Blacksite.

She did not disappear.

She simply...

Stopped letting anyone reach her.

Medical believes this change is consistent with severe social withdrawal following prolonged psychological distress.

---

## **OBSERVATIONS**

Taiga became remarkably difficult to find.

She learned the quiet hours of the Blacksite.

She memorized which corridors were empty.

She began eating after everyone else had finished.

She left rooms moments before others entered.

If someone called her name, she would politely answer from somewhere nearby—

But by the time they reached her...

She was already gone.

Not because she was angry.

Not because she wanted to upset anyone.

She simply didn't want anyone worrying about her.

---

## **ROOM N-07**

Her bed remained perfectly made.

Night after night.

The blanket was neatly folded.

The pillow untouched.

No one knew where she had been sleeping.

Medical eventually determined that she had been choosing different quiet locations around the facility rather than returning to her room consistently.

---

## **MART**

Mart noticed first.

The little owl searched the room every evening.

He landed on Taiga's bed.

He waited.

She never came.

He flew through the residential corridor.

The library.

The observation deck.

The greenhouse.

The workshop.

The lake access tunnel.

Everywhere she usually liked to sit.

Nothing.

According to personnel familiar with Taiga's translations, Mart's calls became noticeably more frequent over the following days.

He was looking for her.

---

## **ROOMMATE RESPONSE**

No one became angry.

No one accused her of abandoning them.

They understood why she was hiding.

That didn't make it hurt any less.

---

### Neko

Neko eventually found one of Taiga's sketchbooks resting on a windowsill.

She wasn't there.

He quietly picked it up.

Brushed the snow from the cover.

Then carried it back to Room N-07 in case she came looking for it.

---

### Miss Neko

Every evening she still prepared one extra cup of warm tea.

It often sat untouched until it grew cold.

The next evening...

She made another.

---

### Bao

Bao continued cooking Taiga's favorite meals.

Even on days when Taiga never appeared for dinner.

She always saved a portion.

Just in case.

---

### Frost

Frost quietly placed a folded blanket on Taiga's empty bed every morning.

She never commented on it.

She simply hoped one day it would be used again.

---

## **STAR**

Star tried harder than anyone else.

For days she searched every corner of the Blacksite.

Every workshop.

Every rooftop.

Every snowy courtyard.

Every place Taiga had ever smiled.

Each time...

She arrived moments too late.

One evening, after another unsuccessful search, Star sat on Taiga's empty bed.

She stared at the neatly folded blanket.

Then finally broke down.

Nightshade quietly sat beside her.

Without speaking.

Star buried her face against Nightshade's shoulder.

Her voice barely rose above a whisper.

> "...She already lost her mom..."

She struggled to continue.

> "...Why..."

A sob interrupted her.

> "...Why does she have to lose more?"

Nightshade gently wrapped an arm around her.

She did not have an answer.

Only silence.

And the quiet hope that Taiga would come home.

---

### Aster

Aster erased her whiteboard several times.

Eventually she wrote only three words.

> **Please come home.**

She left it on Taiga's bed.

---

### Leo

Leo wandered into Taiga's room that night.

He climbed onto the empty bed.

Curled into his usual sleeping spot.

And waited.

He remained there until morning.

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical believes Taiga's withdrawal is not driven by a lack of love for her roommates.

Rather, it appears to stem from the opposite.

She cares for them so deeply that she has convinced herself they would be safer if she stayed away.

This belief is recognized as a harmful trauma-related distortion and is inconsistent with the observations of those closest to her.

Medical recommends continued efforts to maintain a welcoming environment without forcing contact.

---

### **Closing Observation**

The room was unusually quiet that night.

One bed remained untouched.

A warm mug of tea rested on the bedside table.

A small whiteboard leaned against the pillow.

A blanket had been folded with care.

Leo slept where Taiga usually rested.

Mart perched silently on the bedframe, watching the doorway long after everyone else had fallen asleep.

It stayed closed.

Even so...

No one removed the blanket.

No one packed away her belongings.

No one gave her room to someone else.

Because in Room N-07...

Taiga still had a place.

They were simply waiting for her to believe it again.
