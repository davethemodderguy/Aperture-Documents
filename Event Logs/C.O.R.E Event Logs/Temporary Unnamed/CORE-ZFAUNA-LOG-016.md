# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-016**

### **MISSING RESIDENT REPORT — TAIGA**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – INTERNAL PRIORITY

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

At **06:32**, Room N-07 residents reported that Taiga had not returned overnight.

Unlike previous occasions, she was also absent from breakfast.

Morning recreation.

Veterinary rounds.

The greenhouse.

The snowfields.

She was nowhere.

An immediate but controlled search of the Blacksite grounds was initiated.

No signs of Taiga were located.

---

# **I. HER ROOM**

Taiga's bed had been neatly made.

Her blanket was folded.

The white scarf made by Bao and Aster was gone.

The black bandages were gone.

No signs of struggle were present.

No damaged doors.

No forced entry.

No evidence suggesting she had been taken against her will.

Her room looked exactly as if she had simply...

Left.

---

# **II. SEARCH EFFORTS**

Search teams combed the surrounding Icelandic terrain.

Snow manipulation specialists examined recent snowfall for unusual disturbances.

Security personnel reviewed every external camera.

Drones surveyed nearby valleys and frozen coastlines.

Nothing.

The last confirmed recording showed Taiga quietly walking out onto the northern snowfield shortly after midnight.

She wore her white scarf.

No weapon was visible.

She disappeared into the falling snow.

After that...

Nothing.

---

# **III. ASTER**

Aster was the first roommate to understand that something was truly wrong.

Taiga had missed breakfast.

She had never done that before.

The little white cat searched every room she could access.

Every hallway.

Every quiet corner.

Every heater Taiga liked to sit beside.

Nothing.

She eventually returned to Room N-07.

Taiga's bed was still empty.

Aster climbed onto it.

Sat there for several minutes.

Then quietly wrote on her whiteboard.

> **Where are you?**

No answer came.

---

# **IV. LEO**

Leo wandered through the room unusually awake.

He inspected Taiga's empty bed.

Slithered beneath it.

Around the wardrobe.

Onto the windowsill.

Finding nothing, he eventually curled up around the pillow she normally used.

For the first time in recent memory...

He remained awake.

---

# **V. PERSONNEL REACTIONS**

### Neko

Personally joined the external search teams.

His route covered many of the quiet places where Taiga had once watched the snow until sunrise.

He found only fresh snowfall.

---

### Miss Neko

Remained in the residential wing in case Taiga quietly returned on her own.

She refused to pack away Taiga's belongings.

---

### Frost

Walked across the frozen landscape calling Taiga's name until sunset.

The only reply was the wind.

---

### Bao

Spent hours searching the forests surrounding the Blacksite perimeter.

She returned carrying only the untouched white snow.

---

### Star

Checked every peaceful place she herself had once hidden.

Each one was empty.

---

### Nightshade

Expanded the search radius beyond normal patrol routes.

No evidence of intrusion or abduction was discovered.

---

### Director Lucious

Reviewed every available camera recording personally.

After several hours, he issued a brief directive.

> **"Continue the search."**

> **"Assume she left voluntarily until evidence suggests otherwise."**

---

# **VI. CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga has been formally designated as a **Missing Resident**.

At this time:

- No evidence of foul play has been identified.
    
- No signs of injury have been located.
    
- Her current whereabouts remain unknown.
    

Search operations remain active.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late that evening, Room N-07 was unusually quiet.

Aster slept on the heated rug.

Restlessly.

Leo remained curled around Taiga's empty pillow.

The bed beside them stayed perfectly made.

Outside, snow continued falling over the Blacksite.

By morning, it had covered the last visible footprints leading away from the northern grounds.

No one knew where they ended.
