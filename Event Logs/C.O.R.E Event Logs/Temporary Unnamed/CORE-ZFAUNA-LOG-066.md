# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-066**

### **CRITICAL PSYCHOLOGICAL SAFETY NOTICE — TRAUMA-INDUCED DISSOCIATIVE DEFENSIVE RESPONSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – MEDICAL / SECURITY / DIRECTORATE

**Access:** Director Lucious, Medical Division, Security Command, Room N-07 Residents.

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following an unauthorized behavioral experiment conducted by a C.O.R.E. researcher, Medical Services has documented a previously unknown and highly dangerous trauma response exhibited by Taiga.

The researcher, without Medical approval, intentionally attempted to provoke Taiga's PTSD by repeatedly confronting her with her known fears and verbally reinforcing traumatic scenarios in an effort to "test a theory."

The experiment was immediately terminated.

The researcher has been removed from all contact with Taiga pending disciplinary review.

Medical has classified the incident as **entirely preventable**.

---

## **OBSERVED RESPONSE**

Initially, Taiga displayed her typical PTSD symptoms:

- Hyperventilation.
    
- Trembling.
    
- Crying.
    
- Attempts to retreat.
    
- Loss of verbal communication.
    

However, as the psychological stress continued to escalate, personnel observed an abrupt behavioral change.

The panic did not continue increasing.

Instead...

It disappeared.

Taiga stopped crying.

She stopped shaking.

She became completely silent.

Witnesses described her expression as emotionally vacant.

Moments later, both of her irises had become an intense crimson.

---

## **DEFENSIVE STATE**

Medical has provisionally designated this condition:

> **Trauma-Induced Dissociative Defensive Response**

During this state, Taiga appears to lose conscious awareness of her surroundings and acts solely to eliminate what her mind perceives as an immediate threat.

Testing and eyewitness testimony indicate:

- She demonstrates no hesitation.
    
- She ignores pain.
    
- She uses her abilities with remarkable precision.
    
- She employs whatever means are immediately available to neutralize the perceived danger.
    

These actions appear entirely defensive in origin rather than malicious.

Medical believes Taiga is not consciously choosing her actions during this state.

---

## **POST-INCIDENT MEMORY**

After the episode ended, Taiga was found sitting quietly on the floor.

She appeared exhausted and confused.

When asked what had happened, she answered:

> "...I..."

> "...Don't remember."

Repeated neurological examinations found no evidence that she was intentionally withholding information.

Medical believes the event is accompanied by complete dissociative amnesia.

Taiga retains no memory of her actions while in the defensive state.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL RISK**

Medical unanimously agrees on one point.

Taiga's greatest fear after recovering would not be punishment.

It would be learning that she had seriously harmed another person.

Given her well-documented aversion to seeing others injured, such knowledge would almost certainly worsen her existing PTSD and emotional condition.

Accordingly, unnecessary discussion of the event in Taiga's presence is prohibited.

---

## **CONTAINMENT PROTOCOL**

If personnel observe the following:

- Extreme PTSD symptoms escalating rapidly.
    
- Sudden emotional silence after intense panic.
    
- Both irises becoming completely crimson.
    

Personnel are to:

1. Immediately notify Security and Medical.
    
2. Evacuate uninvolved personnel from the immediate area.
    
3. Remove stimuli contributing to the perceived threat whenever safely possible.
    
4. Avoid provoking, cornering, or challenging Taiga.
    
5. Allow trained responders to manage the situation.
    

Personnel are specifically instructed **not** to attempt to punish or verbally confront Taiga during this state.

Medical considers such actions likely to worsen the situation.
