# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-076**

### **CONFIDENTIAL ANOMALOUS RECORD — DESIGNATED APPARITION "MOTHER'S WATCH"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – DIRECTORATE EYES ONLY

**Access Restriction:** This document is accessible only to Director Lucious, Neko, designated Medical personnel, and authorized Anomalous Research staff.

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following months of observation and repeated anomalous events, Research has reached a revised conclusion.

The apparition previously documented in **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-075** has been positively identified.

The entity is **Taiga's deceased mother**.

Evidence collected over multiple manifestations indicates that she has remained within or around Blacksite grounds since the day Taiga was rescued from the perimeter.

No hostile behavior has ever been observed.

Her actions have been consistently protective, particularly toward Taiga.

---

## **OBSERVATIONS**

The apparition has demonstrated several consistent characteristics:

- She appears only for short periods.
    
- She has never attempted to interfere with Blacksite operations.
    
- She consistently asks about Taiga's wellbeing before anything else.
    
- She possesses accurate knowledge of current events involving Taiga and Room N-07.
    
- She has never requested anything for herself.
    

Her concerns are always the same.

> "Is she eating?"

> "Did she sleep?"

> "Is she still having nightmares?"

> "Is she smiling again?"

---

## **COMMUNICATION**

For reasons currently unknown, direct communication with the apparition appears to be possible **only through Neko**.

Other personnel may occasionally detect minor anomalous readings during a manifestation but report hearing nothing.

Repeated testing has failed to determine why Neko alone can perceive her voice.

Research has classified the phenomenon as unexplained.

---

## **THE MOTHER'S REQUEST**

Across every documented conversation, one request has never changed.

> "Please take care of my daughter."

During the most recent manifestation, she quietly added:

> "...She's carrying far more than she should."

After a pause, she looked toward Taiga's empty bed.

> "...She still thinks she has to carry it alone."

---

## **CONFIDENTIAL DIRECTIVE**

Despite confirmation of the apparition's identity, Medical and Director Lucious have jointly issued the following order:

> **Taiga is not to be informed that her mother's spirit is present within the Blacksite at this time.**

Medical's rationale is as follows:

- Taiga remains psychologically vulnerable.
    
- Confirmation without a safe and reliable means of interaction could worsen her grief if her mother later became unreachable.
    
- Any decision to disclose this information must occur only if it is expected to benefit Taiga's long-term wellbeing.
    

The directive will be reviewed if circumstances change.

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, Neko remained alone in Room N-07.

The apparition appeared beside Taiga's untouched bed once more.

She gently straightened the blanket that no one else had dared move.

Her hand passed through the fabric, unable to truly touch it.

She looked toward the empty doorway.

Her voice was quiet.

> "...She still thinks she's alone."

Neko lowered his head.

> "...We're trying."

She smiled sadly.

> "...I know."

Before fading, she looked once more toward her daughter's room.

There was no fear in her expression.

Only the endless concern of a mother...

Still waiting for her little girl to come home.
