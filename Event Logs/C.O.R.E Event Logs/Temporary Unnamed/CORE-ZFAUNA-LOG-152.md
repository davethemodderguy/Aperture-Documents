# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-152**

### **CONTROLLED DEMONSTRATION — "BLISS OF WHITEFALL" AMMUNITION EVALUATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – ARMORY / FICTIONAL WEAPON SYSTEM EVALUATION

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the conclusion of the Blacksite defense, Miss Neko conducted a controlled demonstration of the **Bliss of Whitefall**, her signature handheld anti-materiel platform.

Due to the scale of the demonstration, testing was relocated to an uninhabited proving ground within **Z-556 — STARLANDS**.

The event was attended by Research, Armory personnel, Director Lucious, and several residents, including Taiga and Mart Sunbringer.

Throughout the demonstration, repeated reminders were issued that the weapon system is an anomalous, fictional platform intended solely for Blacksite operational use.

---

## **OPENING REMARKS**

Miss Neko rested the enormous weapon across one shoulder.

Taiga looked up at it.

Then looked at Miss Neko.

> "...You're carrying that like it's a backpack."

Miss Neko smiled.

> "...Practice."

Mart quietly adjusted his riot shield.

> "...I'm glad I'm standing behind her."

---

# **AMMUNITION DEMONSTRATION**

## **12.7×665mm — STANDARD IMPACT ROUND**

The first demonstration used the platform's standard training configuration.

The round impacted a reinforced target block positioned several hundred meters away.

The target disappeared beneath a cloud of dust.

When visibility returned...

Only a crater remained.

Taiga slowly lowered her binoculars.

> "...That's the _basic_ one?"

Miss Neko nodded.

> "...Yes."

---

## **12.7×665mm HPDE — DISPERSION ROUND**

The second configuration projected a wide anomalous dispersion pattern against a field of inert steel targets.

Rather than concentrating its effect at a single point, it spread energy across multiple closely spaced targets.

Research personnel noted the configuration was intended for broad-area engagement against durable non-human threats within the Blacksite setting.

Neko looked over the results.

> "...Efficient."

Taiga looked at the flattened targets.

> "...That's one word for it."

---

## **12.7×775mm — HIGH-ENERGY DEMOLITION ROUND**

The third demonstration focused on structural targets.

An abandoned concrete test structure had been constructed specifically for the evaluation.

One shot.

A brilliant flash.

Several seconds later...

The structure no longer qualified as a structure.

Director Lucious quietly made a note.

> "Testing area requires... another testing area."

---

## **6.5×335mm — LOW-COLLATERAL PRECISION ROUND**

The final demonstration surprised nearly everyone.

Instead of emphasizing raw destructive force, this configuration was designed around precision and reduced collateral effects.

Research described it as the platform's **controlled-response** configuration, intended for situations where minimizing unintended damage was a priority.

Miss Neko fired at a series of small moving targets.

Each was cleanly disabled without affecting the surrounding test environment.

Taiga blinked.

> "...That's..."

Mart finished the sentence.

> "...Much more precise."

Miss Neko nodded.

> "...Sometimes restraint is the better option."

---

## **RESEARCH NOTE**

Although Bliss of Whitefall is capable of extraordinary destructive output, not every configuration is intended to maximize force.

Research emphasized that the platform contains multiple specialized configurations suited to different operational requirements within the fictional setting, ranging from precision engagement to anti-structure use.

---

## **POST-DEMONSTRATION DISCUSSION**

While packing equipment away, Taiga jokingly remarked:

> "...I'm pretty sure half our armory would give military lawyers a headache."

Several personnel laughed.

Director Lucious looked up from his clipboard.

> "...Fortunately..."

A brief pause.

> "...We're a fictional anomalous organization dealing with fictional existential threats."

He closed the clipboard.

> "...Our legal department has enough problems already."

The conversation quickly shifted to another familiar topic.

Workplace safety.

---

## **OSHA**

Despite the Blacksite employing:

- Human researchers.
    
- Human-animal hybrids.
    
- Angels.
    
- Demigods.
    
- Anomalous entities.
    
- Residents capable of flying without visible support.
    

Director Lucious remained unwavering on one subject.

> "...Handrails."

Another.

> "...Protective equipment."

And another.

> "...Fall protection."

When a technician asked whether those rules also applied to people who could literally fly, Lucious answered immediately.

> "...Yes."

Star raised a hand while hovering upside down.

> "...Even me?"

> "...Especially you."

Nightshade silently landed before entering the maintenance area.

Mart folded his wings before climbing a ladder.

Taiga, after a brief reminder, also wore her safety harness while practicing elevated glides.

Even Miss Neko was once observed wearing safety glasses while calibrating Bliss of Whitefall.

Taiga whispered to Mart,

> "...We're fighting world-ending anomalies..."

Mart nodded.

> "...And still have to sign ladder safety forms."

## **CLOSING OBSERVATION**

As everyone prepared to leave the proving ground, Star instinctively started to lift off rather than use the marked access stairs.

Director Lucious looked over.

He didn't raise his voice.

He simply pointed toward the clearly labeled staircase.

Star looked at the stairs.

Looked back at the sky.

Then landed with an exaggerated sigh.

Taiga laughed.

Mart quietly followed the stairs as well.

Later that afternoon, an inspection report from the Blacksite Safety Office concluded:

> **"No recordable injuries."**

Director Lucious smiled faintly.

For him, that was a more satisfying result than any successful weapons test.
