# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-149**

### **WEAPON SYSTEM DISCOVERY — "ECLIPSE: TWILIGHT CONFIGURATION"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – ARMORY / SUNBRINGER AUTHORIZATION ONLY

---

## **SUMMARY**

While performing routine familiarization drills with the **STAR**, **MOON**, **SOLAR**, and **LUNAR** weapon systems, Taiga and Mart Sunbringer unintentionally discovered a third modular configuration.

Unlike the previous transformation, this configuration is only accessible while both weapons are already in their **Eclipse** shotgun forms.

The resulting weapons are **two identical submachine guns**, chambered in a proprietary **10mm Nekonium Magnum** cartridge.

The discovery was accidental.

Again.

---

## **INCIDENT REPORT**

The siblings had been experimenting with the transformation mechanisms out of curiosity.

Taiga repeatedly switched between **STAR** and **SOLAR**.

Mart alternated between **MOON** and **LUNAR**, timing each transformation for speed.

At one point, Taiga jokingly suggested:

> "...What happens if we combine the shotguns too?"

Neko, who happened to be in the armory, immediately looked up from his workbench.

> "...Wait."

A pause.

> "...Don't—"

The locking mechanisms engaged before he finished the sentence.

Four distinct clicks echoed through the room.

The shotguns separated into moving assemblies.

Receivers rotated.

Magazine tubes retracted.

Barrels shortened dramatically.

The components exchanged sections with one another before locking into a new arrangement.

Several seconds later...

Two compact weapons rested in the siblings' hands.

Both were visually identical.

Silence filled the room.

Neko blinked.

> "...I..."

Another pause.

> "...I didn't build that."

## **OBSERVATION**

Taiga shouldered one of the SMGs.

Mart examined the other.

Both looked at each other.

Then...

Without saying a word...

They swapped weapons.

Nothing changed.

They compared them again.

Still nothing.

Taiga frowned.

> "...Which one's mine?"

Mart quietly inspected both.

> "...I..."

A pause.

> "...Don't think either is."

---

## **RESEARCH NOTE**

Unlike **STAR**, **MOON**, **SOLAR**, and **LUNAR**, the **Twilight** configuration deliberately removes all visible distinction between the two weapons.

There are:

- No color differences.
    
- No unique engravings.
    
- No handedness bias.
    
- No assigned ownership.
    

Researchers believe this is intentional.

Where the previous weapon systems emphasized the siblings' individuality, **Twilight** emphasizes complete equality and interchangeability.

Each operator can immediately take over the other's weapon without any functional disadvantage.
