# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-078**

### **ANOMALOUS COMMUNICATION UPDATE — APPARITION SIGNAL EXPANSION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – DIRECTORATE / ANOMALOUS RESEARCH / MEDICAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Research has revised its understanding of the communication anomaly associated with Taiga's mother.

Rather than producing sound in a conventional sense, the apparition appears to generate an anomalous signal that is interpreted by the human brain as spoken language.

Senior researchers have compared the process to tuning an old radio.

The apparition herself provided the simplest explanation.

> "...I'm still learning."

> "...It's... difficult."

> "...Like finding the right frequency."

Research considers this description metaphorical but useful.

---

## **EXPANDING CONTACT**

Over several weeks, additional personnel gradually became capable of hearing the apparition.

Confirmed listeners now include:

- Neko
    
- Miss Neko
    
- Star
    
- Bao
    
- Frost
    

Each new listener reported that the voice initially sounded distant and indistinct before gradually becoming clearer over successive manifestations.

Research believes the apparition is actively refining whatever mechanism allows communication.

The exact process remains unknown.

---

## **THE APPARITION**

Personnel unanimously describe her as calm, gentle, and remarkably patient.

She has never complained about her own circumstances.

Instead, nearly every conversation returns to the same subject.

> "...How's Taiga today?"

---

> "...Has she smiled?"

---

> "...Is she eating enough?"

---

> "...Please don't stop looking."

---

## **TAIGA**

Despite these developments...

Taiga remains unable to perceive the apparition.

Medical does **not** believe this is due to any physiological limitation.

Instead, clinicians have proposed that Taiga's overwhelming grief, guilt, and persistent belief that she is dangerous may be preventing her from recognizing or accepting the anomalous phenomenon.

This remains a working hypothesis and has not been experimentally verified.

---

## **THE FAILED DISCLOSURE**

Taiga continues to believe Neko fabricated the story about her mother.

During a brief encounter several days after the initial conversation, she quietly confronted him.

"...Why would you do that?"

Neko looked genuinely confused.

"...Do what?"

"...Use my mom..."

"...To make me come back."

"...That's cruel."

Neko immediately tried to explain.

"...Taiga, I swear—"

She shook her head.

"...Please..."

"...Just stop."

She walked away before he could continue.

Medical notes that Taiga's anger appeared rooted not in hostility, but in profound hurt and mistrust.

---

## **THE RED EYE**

Journal fragments recovered since the incident continue to reference the phrase:

> **"...the red eye..."**

Medical believes Taiga has come to associate the red-eyed dissociative state with the possibility of hurting those around her.

She repeatedly refers to it as though it were a separate part of herself.

One recovered fragment reads:

> _"...Everyone says it's still me..."_

> _"...I don't think it is."_

No evidence currently supports the existence of a separate consciousness.

Medical continues to interpret these writings as expressions of trauma and fear rather than literal observations.

### **Closing Observation**

That evening, Taiga sat alone on the roof of an unused maintenance building, watching snow drift silently across the Blacksite.

Inside Room N-07, her mother stood beside the window.

She could see her daughter.

Her daughter could not see her.

The apparition slowly reached a hand toward the distant figure.

It passed harmlessly through the cold air.

She smiled anyway.

> "...I'll keep trying."

Across the Blacksite, Taiga quietly whispered to the empty sky.

"...I miss you, Mom."

The only reply was the gentle sound of falling snow.

For now...

It was enough to know that, even separated by grief, they were both still reaching for each other.
