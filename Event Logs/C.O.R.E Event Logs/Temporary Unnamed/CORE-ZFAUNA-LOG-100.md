# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-100**

### **RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION — "A BETTER USE FOR FEATHERS"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

As Mart naturally sheds feathers in his owl form, Residential Services has accumulated a modest collection of naturally molted feathers over time.

These feathers are **never** plucked from Mart and are only collected after they have fallen off naturally.

One afternoon, Taiga quietly approached Bao carrying several of Mart's feathers.

> "...Can I borrow some ink?"

Bao looked at the feathers.

Then at Taiga.

> "...What are you making?"

Taiga smiled.

> "...A pen."

---

## **THE FEATHER PEN**

With a little help from Neko, Taiga carefully trimmed one of Mart's naturally shed flight feathers into a traditional quill.

After dipping it into ink, she cautiously wrote her name.

> **Taiga Sunbringer**

She stared at the page for a few seconds.

Then smiled.

> "...It works."

Mart, sitting nearby in his human form, looked over.

> "...My feather?"

Taiga nodded.

"...One you shed."

Mart examined the quill closely before smiling.

> "...Good."

> "...Better than throwing it away."

---

## **AN UNEXPECTED HOBBY**

Over the following weeks, Taiga began using Mart's naturally shed feathers for more than just notes.

She wrote:

- Journal entries.
    
- Letters to Astral.
    
- Small thank-you notes for the kitchen staff.
    
- Labels for the plants she cared for.
    
- Sketches of Mart, Leo, and the rest of Room N-07.
    

She claimed the feather pen felt "lighter" than ordinary pens.

Whether this was psychological or anomalous remains unknown.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

- Naturally shed feathers may be collected with Mart's permission.
    
- Taiga has adopted feather quills as her preferred writing instrument.
    
- Mart has expressed no objection and appears pleased that his shed feathers are put to meaningful use.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, Taiga sat quietly at her desk, writing in her journal with a white feather quill.

Mart, in his owl form, rested on the back of her chair.

Another feather drifted gently onto the page.

Taiga picked it up.

She smiled without looking back.

> "...Thanks."

Mart gave a soft, content hoot.

Neither of them clarified whether the thanks was for the feather...

Or for always being there.
