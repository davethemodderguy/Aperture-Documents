# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-038**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION — SEPARATION ANXIETY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – MEDICAL / BEHAVIORAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Over the past month, Residential Services has documented a recurring behavioral pattern involving Taiga.

The behavior is subtle and generally goes unnoticed unless personnel are specifically watching for it.

Taiga appears comfortable, cheerful, and socially engaged while in the presence of her roommates or Director Lucious.

However, if she unexpectedly finds herself completely alone, her demeanor changes almost immediately.

---

## **INITIAL OBSERVATION**

The behavior was first documented after breakfast.

Neko, Miss Neko, Bao, Frost, Star, Nightshade, Aster, and Director Lucious had all gradually left Room N-07 to begin their daily work.

Each assumed someone else was still inside.

Within moments...

Taiga realized she was alone.

Security footage showed her standing still in the center of the room.

She looked toward the hallway.

Then toward the now-empty beds.

She quietly called out,

> "...Hello?"

No response.

She walked to the doorway.

Looked both directions.

Still no one.

She slowly sat down on the floor beside the heater, pulled her knees close, and lowered her head.

By the time Miss Neko returned a few minutes later after realizing she'd forgotten her notebook, Taiga was quietly crying to herself.

---

## **PATTERN**

Subsequent observations revealed the response was consistent.

If one person left the room, Taiga remained calm.

If several people left together but someone familiar remained nearby, she remained calm.

The response occurred only when she believed she had been left entirely by herself.

Personnel described the reaction as immediate.

She would stop whatever she was doing.

Sit down wherever she happened to be.

And quietly sob until someone returned.

---

## **DISCUSSION**

Medical approached the matter carefully.

Rather than conducting a formal interview, Bao simply sat beside Taiga one evening while the others were nearby.

After a long silence, Bao gently asked,

> "...What goes through your mind when everyone's gone?"

Taiga didn't answer immediately.

She stared at the warm mug in her hands.

Several minutes passed.

Finally, she spoke.

Very quietly.

> "...I've lost too many..."

Silence filled the room.

She continued.

> "...Every time I can't see anyone..."

Her voice shook.

> "...A part of me starts wondering if they're gone too."

Bao gently placed a hand over Taiga's.

Neither of them spoke for a while.

---

## **ADJUSTMENTS**

Following the discussion, Room N-07 made several small changes.

No one treated Taiga differently.

Instead, they made a habit of letting her know when they were leaving.

Simple phrases became routine.

> "I'll be back in a little while."

> "I'm just heading to the workshop."

> "We'll see you at lunch."

> "Come find us whenever you want."

The goal was not to prevent Taiga from ever being alone.

It was simply to ensure she never felt abandoned.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Neko

> "...I don't disappear."

> "...Sometimes I just get distracted by engineering."

He later admitted to setting reminders so he wouldn't accidentally spend an entire day in the laboratory without checking in.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...We'll always tell her where we're going."

---

### Bao

> "Sometimes the smallest reassurance means the most."

---

### Frost

> "...Knowing someone is coming back can make all the difference."

---

### Star

Star now waves dramatically every time she leaves the room.

> "I'm going!"

> "...And I'm coming back!"

Taiga always smiles.

---

### Nightshade

Nightshade isn't particularly expressive.

But every time she leaves, she pauses at the doorway.

She looks directly at Taiga.

> "...Later."

Then leaves.

She has never forgotten to say it.

---

### Director Lucious

One afternoon, Taiga noticed him preparing to leave after a visit.

Before he reached the door, he turned back.

> "...I'll be in my office."

> "...If you need me, you know where to find me."

Taiga nodded.

> "...Okay."

She remained smiling long after he had gone.

---

### Aster

Aster eventually made a small laminated card that simply read:

> **Not gone. Just busy.**

Whenever everyone needed to leave for meetings, she left the card on Taiga's bedside table before walking out.

Taiga kept it.

---

### Leo

Leo, unsurprisingly, slept through most departures.

However, whenever Taiga stayed behind, he often remained curled around her scarf instead of following anyone else.

According to Taiga's translation after he woke,

> "...No alone."

He promptly went back to sleep.

---

### Mart

Mart chose a simpler solution.

Whenever Taiga found herself by herself, he quietly flew down from his perch and settled onto her shoulder.

He didn't need to say anything.

His presence was enough.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga continues to experience distress if she believes she has been left completely alone.

Simple communication before leaving has significantly reduced these episodes.

Residential Services notes that the adjustment has been easy for everyone to adopt and has become part of the normal routine within Room N-07.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, everyone was preparing for bed.

Neko headed to the laboratory to finish a calculation.

Bao and Frost went to the kitchen for tea.

Star wandered off to look for Leo, who had somehow fallen asleep on top of a bookshelf again.

Nightshade stepped into the hallway.

For just a moment, Taiga looked around the room.

Before uncertainty could settle in, voices echoed back down the corridor.

> "We'll be right back!"

> "Don't start without us!"

> "Tea's almost ready!"

Taiga smiled to herself.

She waited by the heater.

A few minutes later, one by one, her family returned.
