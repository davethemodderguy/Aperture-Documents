# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-061**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT — TRAUMA-RELATED NIGHTMARE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – MEDICAL / PSYCHOLOGICAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Medical Services has documented a significant PTSD episode experienced by Taiga following an unusually vivid nightmare.

The event did **not** result from a real-world trigger such as loud gunfire or being separated from trusted individuals.

Instead, it was triggered entirely by the content of the dream itself.

Medical emphasizes that trauma-related nightmares can temporarily affect how a person feels after waking, even when the events in the dream never actually happened.

---

## **THE NIGHTMARE**

According to Taiga's account, the dream began peacefully.

In it, the Blacksite's gates opened.

Someone told her she was free to return to the wilderness.

She believed them.

She smiled.

She thanked everyone.

She walked away from the Blacksite.

Further and further.

Eventually the facility disappeared behind the snowy hills.

Then—

A single gunshot.

She fell.

The hunters had found her again.

As she lay on the snow, she desperately looked back toward the Blacksite.

In the dream, nobody came.

She woke before the dream continued.

---

## **AFTER WAKING**

Taiga awoke abruptly with a rapid heartbeat, crying and visibly disoriented.

Medical staff responding to the incident found no physical injuries.

However, she appeared deeply distressed.

For several hours afterward, she remained unusually withdrawn and reluctant to leave the residential wing.

When gently asked what had happened, she quietly replied,

> "...I dreamed..."

She paused.

> "...That everyone let me go."

Another pause.

> "...And then they couldn't protect me anymore."

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical believes the nightmare reflects Taiga's unresolved fear of losing the safety she has found at the Blacksite.

Importantly, there is **no indication** that the Blacksite has ever planned to return Taiga to the wilderness.

From the day she was rescued, Director Lucious's standing directive has remained unchanged:

> **"She is not to be returned to the wild."**

The purpose of that directive was to ensure Taiga's long-term safety after the death of her mother and the threat posed by the hunters.

Medical concluded that the nightmare represented Taiga's trauma expressing itself through an imagined scenario rather than any real change in policy.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Upon being informed of the incident, Director Lucious visited Taiga later that morning.

He found her sitting quietly by the window.

After sitting beside her for a while, he spoke.

> "...Do you remember what I said when you first came here?"

Taiga gave a small nod.

He repeated it anyway.

> "...You were never brought here to be sent away."

Another pause.

> "...This is your home."

He looked directly at her.

> "...That has not changed."

Taiga remained silent for a long moment.

Then slowly leaned against his shoulder.

> "...I know..."

> "...It just didn't feel real when I woke up."

Director Lucious nodded.

> "...Nightmares can do that."

---

## **ROOM N-07 RESPONSE**

No one tried to convince Taiga that the nightmare was "silly."

Instead, they simply reminded her of reality.

### Neko

> "...You'd have to get past me first."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You're family."

---

### Bao

> "...You're staying right here with us."

---

### Frost

> "...Home is where you feel safe."

She gently adjusted Taiga's scarf.

> "...You're home."

---

### Star

Star immediately hugged Taiga.

> "...You're not leaving!"

Then, realizing how forceful that sounded, she quickly corrected herself.

> "...I mean..."

> "...You don't have to."

---

### Nightshade

> "...Dreams are convincing."

A pause.

> "...Reality is stronger."

---

### Aster

Aster quietly erased her whiteboard.

She wrote one sentence.

> **Nobody is sending you away.**

She held it where Taiga could read it for as long as she needed.

---

### Leo

Leo climbed into Taiga's lap, curled into a small circle, and immediately fell asleep.

According to Taiga, his sleepy presence said more than words could.

---

### Mart

Mart settled onto the back of Taiga's chair.

According to her translation, he simply said,

> "...Your flock is still here."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga gradually recovered over the course of the day.

Medical notes that the nightmare caused a temporary increase in anxiety but did not alter her relationships with the Blacksite's residents.

Her trust in those around her remains strong, though the dream highlighted how deeply she fears losing the safety and family she has found.

Medical recommends continued support and monitoring of recurring trauma-related dreams.

---

### **Closing Observation**

That evening, before going to sleep, Taiga quietly walked through Room N-07.

She looked at each of her roommates.

Neko was still working on a blueprint.

Miss Neko was reading.

Bao was preparing tomorrow's lunch.

Frost had already fallen asleep with a book resting on her chest.

Star and Nightshade were sharing the same blanket.

Aster was curled up on the rug with Leo asleep across her back.

Mart rested on his perch near the window.

Finally, she looked toward the framed family photograph on the shelf.

She stood there for a long moment.

Then whispered to herself,

> "...It was only a dream."

She climbed into bed.

This time...

When she closed her eyes, she knew where she would wake up.
