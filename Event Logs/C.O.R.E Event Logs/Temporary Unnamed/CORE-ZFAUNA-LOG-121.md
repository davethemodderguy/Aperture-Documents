# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-121**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION — MART SUNBRINGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – RESIDENTIAL / PSYCHOLOGICAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

Since the recovery of both Sunbringer siblings, personnel have reported gradual behavioral changes in Mart.

The changes are subtle.

But nearly everyone who knows him has noticed them.

Mart still smiles.

Just...

Not as often.

And when he does...

They never seem to last very long.

---

## **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATIONS**

Mart remains polite, cooperative, and kind.

He continues spending most of his free time with Taiga, Astral, and the other residents.

He still enjoys reading.

He still quietly accompanies Taiga everywhere she asks.

He still sits with Leo during afternoon naps.

Outwardly...

Very little has changed.

Yet those closest to him have noticed that conversations with Mart have become quieter.

He speaks less.

Laughs less.

His answers have become shorter.

Even his smiles seem... hesitant.

A smile that once lingered for several seconds now fades almost immediately.

Several staff members admitted they now celebrate even the smallest laugh.

Not because it is rare.

Because they know it is genuine.

---

## **ANOMALOUS OBSERVATION**

Medical Services has also documented another shared phenomenon between the Sunbringer siblings.

Under low-light conditions, both Mart and Taiga emit a faint natural luminescence.

The glow is extremely subtle.

It is most noticeable around:

- Their eyes.
    
- Their hair (human forms).
    
- Their fur and feathers (animal forms).
    
- The azure rune on Taiga's back.
    

The effect is comparable to that observed in:

- Star Dawnbringer.
    
- Neko.
    
- Miss Neko.
    

Testing has revealed no harmful effects.

Researchers currently believe the glow is a benign manifestation of anomalous energy rather than a medical concern.

---

## **OBSERVED INCIDENT**

During a scheduled maintenance blackout, the residential wing briefly lost power.

Emergency lighting had not yet activated.

The hallway fell into darkness.

Within seconds, several small points of soft light appeared.

Blue.

White.

Azure.

Taiga looked around in confusion before noticing Mart.

His eyes glowed faintly.

Mart looked back.

Then noticed Taiga was glowing as well.

For the first time that day...

He smiled.

A small one.

Taiga quietly laughed.

> "...We're nightlights now."

Mart gave a tiny chuckle.

> "...Guess we are."

The smile disappeared a few moments later.

But for those few seconds...

It had returned.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Taiga Sunbringer

Taiga has become increasingly attentive to Mart's emotional state.

Whenever his expression begins to fall, she quietly nudges his shoulder or reaches for his hand.

She never forces a conversation.

She simply stays beside him.

---

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral has noticed the difference immediately.

She often catches Mart smiling at family photographs.

The moment he realizes someone is watching...

The smile fades.

She has stopped pointing it out.

Instead, she simply hugs him a little longer.

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious observed Mart helping Taiga through rehabilitation one afternoon.

He later remarked,

> "...He's carrying more than anyone can see."

---

### Neko

Neko attempted one of his usual jokes.

Mart laughed.

For about two seconds.

Then quietly returned to staring out the window.

Neko sighed.

> "...We'll get that laugh back."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Some smiles disappear quickly..."

> "...Not because they're fake."

> "...Because they're tired."

---

### Bao

Bao has quietly begun making some of Mart's favorite meals more often.

Not to cheer him up.

Simply to remind him that someone is thinking about him.

Sometimes he smiles when he notices.

Those moments are enough.

---

### Frost

> "...The brightest lights..."

She looked toward the softly glowing siblings.

> "...Often shine after surviving the darkest winters."

---

### Star Dawnbringer

The first time Star noticed everyone glowing during the blackout, she gasped dramatically.

> "...We're matching!"

She immediately declared them members of her "Glow Club."

Mart actually laughed.

Everyone in the room immediately looked at him.

Mart became embarrassed.

Taiga burst into laughter.

For a little while...

The room felt normal again.

---

### Nightshade Dawnbringer

Nightshade watched the siblings walking through a dim corridor, their faint glow just visible against the darkness.

> "...Some lights don't exist to be seen."

She folded her arms.

> "...They exist to help others find their way home."

---

### Leo

Leo woke just enough to notice the faint glow coming from the hallway.

He looked at Mart.

Then at Taiga.

Closed one eye again.

> "...Convenient."

He went back to sleep.

### **Closing Observation**

Late that evening, Mart and Taiga walked through one of the older corridors after the lights had been dimmed for the night.

Neither of them spoke.

They didn't need to.

In the darkness, their soft glow reflected faintly against the polished floor.

A maintenance worker watched them pass.

Later, he wrote a single sentence in the shift log:

> "They've both been through more than anyone their age should have."

After a pause, he added one more line.

> "I'm just glad neither of them has to walk through the dark alone anymore."
