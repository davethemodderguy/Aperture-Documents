# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-050**

### **PERSONNEL RECORD — ADOPTION OF TAIGA**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – PERSONNEL / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

On **[REDACTED]**, an unexpected conversation resulted in a permanent change to Taiga's legal and residential status within Aperture Industries.

The incident began entirely by accident.

---

## **INCIDENT**

Director Lucious had just concluded a lengthy meeting before returning to the residential wing.

Finding Taiga sitting alone in the common room reading a book, he stopped to ask how she was feeling.

The two spoke quietly for several minutes.

Nothing unusual occurred.

As Director Lucious prepared to leave, he reminded her,

> "If you need anything, come find me."

Without thinking, Taiga smiled.

> "...Okay, Dad."

Silence.

Taiga froze.

Her eyes widened.

Realizing what she had just said, her face immediately turned bright red.

She covered her mouth.

> "...I—"

> "...I didn't..."

> "...I mean..."

She looked ready to disappear from embarrassment.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS'S RESPONSE**

Director Lucious remained silent for a few moments.

Then he smiled.

A genuine smile.

One rarely seen by most Blacksite personnel.

He simply replied,

> "...I don't mind."

Taiga blinked.

"...Really?"

He nodded once.

> "...Really."

---

## **ADMINISTRATIVE ACTION**

Approximately twenty-three minutes later, Director Lucious appeared in Administration.

He requested several legal documents.

Human Resources assumed there had been an emergency.

There had not.

After reviewing the paperwork, the administrator looked up.

"...You're filing adoption papers?"

> "Correct."

"...Today?"

> "Correct."

"...For Taiga?"

> "Correct."

"...Have you discussed this with her?"

Director Lucious paused.

"...I'm about to."

---

## **THE PROPOSAL**

Later that afternoon, Director Lucious returned to Room N-07 carrying a folder.

Taiga looked up nervously.

"...Am I in trouble?"

He handed her the folder.

Inside were adoption documents.

She stared silently at the first page.

Then looked back at him.

"...What is this?"

He answered simply.

> "...If you're comfortable with it..."

> "...I'd like to officially become your father."

The room fell completely silent.

Taiga read the sentence again.

And again.

Her hands began shaking.

Tears slowly formed in her eyes.

"...Really...?"

Director Lucious nodded.

> "...Really."

For several moments, she couldn't speak.

Then, without another word, she stepped forward and hugged him.

Very tightly.

Through tears, she managed only four words.

> "...Thank you..."

> "...Dad."

Director Lucious gently returned the hug.

> "...You're welcome."

---

## **LEGAL STATUS**

Following Taiga's enthusiastic consent, the adoption process was completed through Aperture Industries' legal department and the appropriate civil authorities.

Effective immediately:

- Director Lucious is Taiga's legal adoptive father.
    
- Taiga remains a permanent resident of the Blacksite.
    
- Her existing residential arrangements within Room N-07 remain unchanged.
    

Medical notes that the adoption was initiated only after Taiga freely agreed to it.

---

## **ROOM N-07 REACTION**

News spread through the residential wing surprisingly quickly.

---

### Star

Immediately hugged Taiga.

"...You have a dad!!"

She looked genuinely happier than anyone else in the room.

---

### Bao

Smiled warmly.

> "...Congratulations."

Then quietly wiped away a tear.

---

### Frost

> "...I'm happy for you."

Simple.

Sincere.

---

### Nightshade

Looking toward Director Lucious.

> "...Good choice."

After a pause, she added,

> "...For both of you."

---

### Neko

Without looking up from his workbench.

> "...Guess I should start calling him Uncle Lucious."

Director Lucious raised an eyebrow.

> "...Please don't."

Neko laughed.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Our family somehow became even bigger."

---

### Aster

Aster stared at the paperwork for several seconds.

Then rapidly wrote across her whiteboard in large letters:

> **CONGRATULATIONS!!**

She immediately hugged Taiga as well.

---

### Leo

Leo slept through the entire announcement.

He woke only long enough to yawn, climb onto Taiga's shoulder, and resume sleeping.

Veterinary staff noted this was typical Leo behavior.

---

### Mart

Mart landed on the back of Director Lucious's chair.

According to Taiga's translation, his quiet hoot meant:

> "...Looks like the nest got bigger."

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS'S OFFICE**

The following morning, personnel noticed a small framed photograph had appeared on Director Lucious's desk.

It showed Room N-07's residents together.

Near the center stood Taiga.

On the back of the frame, written in Director Lucious's handwriting, were six simple words.

> **Family isn't always found by birth.**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga has adjusted positively to her adoption.

Medical reports a noticeable improvement in her sense of security, particularly regarding Director Lucious.

Although she still experiences anxiety and nightmares, she has become more willing to seek comfort from the people around her.

Director Lucious has likewise become a more frequent presence within the residential wing, often checking on Room N-07 whenever his schedule allows.

---

### **Closing Observation**

That evening, Director Lucious stopped outside Room N-07 before heading home.

He knocked gently on the open door.

Taiga looked up from her book.

Without hesitation this time, she smiled.

> "...Good night, Dad."

Director Lucious smiled back.

> "...Good night, Taiga."

For the first time in many years...

Neither of them had to say the words by accident.
