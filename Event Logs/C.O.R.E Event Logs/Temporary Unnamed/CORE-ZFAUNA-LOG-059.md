# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-059**

### **ABILITY EXPLANATION — AERIAL PROPULSION ("DOUBLE JUMP")**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – ANOMALOUS MOVEMENT

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

During routine mobility training, Taiga demonstrated another previously undocumented anomalous movement ability.

The maneuver has been informally designated **"Double Jump."**

At the time of writing, Taiga is the **only** known Blacksite resident capable of performing this technique.

---

## **ABILITY DESCRIPTION**

Unlike a conventional jump, Double Jump allows Taiga to perform a second jump while already airborne.

The movement is achieved by releasing a short, concentrated burst of compressed air beneath and slightly behind her body.

This burst provides enough upward and forward force to briefly propel her through the air before she performs a second jump.

The technique does **not** violate gravity or allow sustained flight.

Instead, it functions as a controlled aerial boost.

---

## **MECHANICS**

Testing indicates the maneuver occurs in four stages:

1. **Initial Jump**
    
    - Taiga performs a normal jump from the ground.
        
2. **Air Burst**
    
    - While airborne, she generates a brief pulse of compressed air beneath herself.
        
    - Observers report hearing a soft "whoosh" rather than an explosion.
        
3. **Aerial Propulsion**
    
    - The burst slightly lifts and pushes Taiga forward, effectively resetting her body into a position where she can jump again.
        
4. **Second Jump**
    
    - Taiga performs a second full jump before descending normally.
        

The entire sequence lasts less than one second.

---

## **CHARACTERISTICS**

Current testing has shown:

- Effective for clearing obstacles.
    
- Allows rapid changes in direction while airborne.
    
- Does not permit indefinite aerial movement.
    
- Requires a solid initial jump before activation.
    
- Produces only a minor air disturbance, posing no hazard to nearby personnel.
    

Medical found no evidence of abnormal strain or injury associated with routine use.

---

## **PRACTICAL APPLICATIONS**

Potential applications include:

- Traversing difficult terrain.
    
- Crossing gaps.
    
- Avoiding hazards.
    
- Improving mobility during rescue operations.
    
- Reaching elevated platforms without climbing equipment.
    

The ability is considered primarily a movement tool rather than an offensive capability.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Double Jump has been added to Taiga's documented movement capabilities.

Researchers currently believe the technique results from precise anomalous manipulation of compressed air rather than true flight or gravity manipulation.

The ability is considered stable, safe for supervised use, and highly practical for navigation and rescue scenarios.
