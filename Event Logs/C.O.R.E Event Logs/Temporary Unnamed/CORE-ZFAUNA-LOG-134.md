# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-134**

### **MEDICAL INCIDENT — FAILED PURSUIT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – MEDICAL / PSYCHOLOGICAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

On ██/██/20██, Taiga Sunbringer unexpectedly left the residential wing after a confirmed sighting of Mart Sunbringer outside the Blacksite.

Ignoring Medical restrictions, she attempted to pursue him on foot.

The attempt resulted in complete physical collapse.

---

## **INCIDENT REPORT**

At approximately **17:48**, several maintenance personnel reported seeing Mart gliding over the northern service road.

The report was transmitted over the local maintenance channel.

Taiga heard it.

Before anyone could stop her...

She was already outside.

---

## **PURSUIT**

Witnesses observed Taiga running across the snow-covered grounds.

Her movements lacked the speed she once possessed.

Her breathing became strained almost immediately.

Personnel repeatedly called for her to stop.

She didn't even turn around.

Far ahead...

Mart could be seen walking quietly through the snow.

His wings were folded.

He hadn't noticed her.

Or perhaps...

He had.

No one could tell.

Taiga called out once.

Her voice was carried away by the wind.

Mart continued forward.

She kept running.

---

## **COLLAPSE**

Approximately one hundred meters into the pursuit, Taiga stumbled.

She caught herself.

Tried again.

Her legs shook violently beneath her.

She refused to stop.

Another few steps.

Then another stumble.

She pushed herself onward despite obvious exhaustion.

Finally...

Her body gave out completely.

She collapsed heavily into the snow.

Witnesses reported that even after falling...

She continued trying to crawl forward.

Her hands dug into the snow.

Dragging herself meter by meter.

Barely moving.

Barely conscious.

Medical staff estimate she covered several additional meters in this condition.

One responding officer later stated:

> "She wasn't chasing him anymore."

> "She was refusing to stop."

---

## **EMERGENCY TRANSFORMATION**

As her condition worsened, Taiga's anomalous abilities activated involuntarily.

Unable to continue in human form...

She forcefully transformed into her leopard form.

The transformation completed successfully.

But the additional physical strain proved too much.

Seconds later...

She lost consciousness.

Medical teams arriving at the scene found only disturbed snow.

Taiga was gone.

---

## **RECOVERY**

Taiga regained consciousness several hours later inside the Blacksite Medical Wing.

She was already lying in bed.

Warm blankets covered her.

Intravenous fluids had been started.

Her body temperature had stabilized.

Medical staff reported no memory of transporting her.

The personnel first assigned to the incident also denied carrying her back.

No security camera captured anyone returning with her.

When Taiga awoke...

Her first question was immediate.

> "...Who brought me home?"

No one answered.

Because...

No one knew.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral remained beside Taiga's bed throughout the night.

She gently brushed snow from her daughter's hair.

When Taiga asked who had rescued her...

Astral simply smiled sadly.

> "...Whoever it was..."

> "...They made sure you came home."

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious personally reviewed every exterior security recording.

The footage showed Taiga collapsing.

Moments later, heavy snowfall obscured the camera's view.

When visibility returned...

She was gone.

No identifiable individual appeared in the footage.

The report was marked:

> **Cause Unknown.**

---

### Neko

Neko searched the collapse site the following morning.

He found only one unusual detail.

A single set of footprints.

Leading away.

None leading back.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Someone..."

She quietly read the report again.

> "...Didn't want her left alone."

---

### Bao

Bao prepared warm vegetable soup before Taiga regained consciousness.

She never asked what had happened.

She simply placed the bowl beside the bed.

---

### Frost

Frost visited the collapse site herself.

The snow had already buried most evidence.

She quietly whispered,

> "...Someone reached her first."

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star rushed into the Medical Wing as soon as she heard Taiga had awakened.

She hugged her carefully.

Much more carefully than usual.

---

### Nightshade Dawnbringer

Nightshade inspected the remaining tracks personally.

She noticed something strange.

There were signs that someone had knelt beside Taiga before she disappeared.

Nothing more.

Nothing less.

---

### Leo

Leo reviewed the exterior cameras one final time.

He leaned closer to one blurred frame.

Just before the snowfall completely obscured the view...

There appeared to be a faint pair of white wings.

The image was too distorted to confirm.

Leo closed the recording.

He never mentioned it in the official report.

---

---

### **Closing Observation**

When Medical staff helped Taiga change into clean clothes later that evening...

One nurse paused.

Caught carefully between the folds of Taiga's jacket...

Was a single white feather.

Not glowing.

Not fading.

Just resting there quietly.

No one had placed it there.

The nurse looked toward the window overlooking the snowy grounds.

Then carefully tucked the feather back into Taiga's pocket without saying a word.

No notation about the feather appeared in the official medical record.
