# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-056**

### **PHYSIOLOGICAL DEVELOPMENT — HETEROCHROMIA**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – MEDICAL / BIOLOGICAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

As Taiga continues to mature and gradually reveal more of her natural appearance, Medical Services has documented another permanent physiological change.

During a routine morning examination, personnel noticed that Taiga's irises were no longer identical.

Her eyes had developed complete heterochromia.

- **Left Iris:** Ice Blue
    
- **Right Iris:** Crimson Red
    

The change appears to have occurred naturally over time rather than suddenly.

Medical believes this is another stage of Taiga's ongoing biological development.

---

## **DISCOVERY**

The change went unnoticed for several hours.

Taiga herself had not realized anything was different.

It was Miss Neko who noticed first during breakfast.

She paused while pouring tea.

Looked at Taiga.

Looked again.

"...Taiga."

Taiga looked up.

"...Hm?"

"...Your eyes."

Taiga blinked.

"...What about them?"

Everyone at the table looked over.

Star tilted her head.

"...Wait..."

"...They're different."

---

## **MEDICAL EXAMINATION**

Subsequent examination confirmed:

- No vision impairment.
    
- No neurological abnormalities.
    
- No signs of injury.
    
- No evidence of anomalous instability.
    

Both eyes respond normally to light.

Visual acuity remains unchanged.

The heterochromia appears entirely cosmetic.

---

## **LIMITATIONS**

When asked whether she could voluntarily change the colors of her irises, Taiga attempted several times.

Nothing happened.

After a minute, she laughed awkwardly.

> "...No..."

> "...I don't know how."

Medical concluded that, unlike some of her other physical changes, Taiga currently has no conscious control over her eye coloration.

---

Taiga looked confused.

> "...Welcome to the club."

Taiga smiled brightly.

The other red.

Then proudly showed it to her.

---

### Leo

Leo briefly opened one eye while wrapped around Taiga's shoulders.

He looked directly into her face.

According to Taiga's translation, he sleepily mumbled,

> "...Pretty."

He then fell asleep again before anyone could respond.

---

### Mart

Mart landed on Taiga's shoulder and stared unusually closely at her face.

After a moment, he gave a satisfied hoot.

According to Taiga:

> "...You finally look like yourself."

Taiga quietly smiled.

"...Maybe I do."

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical believes the emergence of heterochromia represents another step in Taiga's gradual return to her true biological appearance.

Combined with her increasingly defined black stripes, restrained tail markings, and other physical developments, personnel believe her transformation is still ongoing.

No treatment is required.

Routine observation will continue.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Later that evening, Taiga stood before the bathroom mirror for several minutes.

She leaned closer.

One eye reflected the pale blue of fresh snow beneath a winter sky.

The other shone a deep crimson.

She stared at herself quietly.

"...So..."

"...This is me."

Behind her, Miss Neko appeared in the doorway.

She smiled warmly.

> "...Looks good."

Taiga smiled back.

For once, she wasn't looking at someone else.

She was finally beginning to recognize herself.
