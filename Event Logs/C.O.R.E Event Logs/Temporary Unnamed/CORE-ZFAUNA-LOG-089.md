# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-089**

### **RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION — "HE IS AN OWL."**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Despite Mart's confirmed identity as an owl, Star continues to unintentionally treat him like a house cat.

This has become a recurring source of amusement within the residential wing.

---

## **INCIDENTS**

Documented examples include:

- Attempting to entice Mart with a ball of yarn.
    
- Gently scratching under his chin while saying, "Who's a fluffy kitty?"
    
- Calling him with "Pspspspsps."
    
- Trying to coax him into a cardboard box because, according to Star, "Cats like boxes."
    

Mart's response has been remarkably consistent.

He simply stares at Star.

Unblinking.

For several seconds.

Then slowly turns his head nearly 180 degrees to look at someone else, as if silently asking for help.

---

## **ASTRAL'S CORRECTION**

After witnessing Star refer to Mart as a cat for the fourth time that week, Astral finally laughed.

> "...Star."

Star looked up.

> "...Yeah?"

Astral pointed toward Mart.

> "...He's an owl."

Star blinked.

"..."

"...I know."

A pause.

> "...I forgot."

Mart gave a single, resigned hoot.

Taiga translated.

> "...He says this keeps happening."

The room erupted into laughter.

---

## **LEO**

One unexpected friendship has become increasingly noticeable.

Mart spends an unusual amount of time with Leo.

It is common to find the two together during quiet afternoons.

Usually...

Leo is asleep.

Mart simply perches nearby.

Sometimes on the headboard.

Sometimes on the arm of a chair.

Occasionally...

Directly on Leo's blanket.

Neither seems to mind the silence.

---

## **WHY?**

When Taiga asked Mart why he enjoyed Leo's company so much, he transformed briefly into his humanoid form.

He thought carefully before answering.

> "...Quiet."

A pause.

> "...No pressure."

Another pause.

> "...He sleeps."

Mart smiled faintly.

> "...It's peaceful."

He looked toward the sleeping Leo.

> "...I like peaceful."

With that, he transformed back into an owl and silently returned to his favorite perch beside Leo.

---

## **LEO'S RESPONSE**

Several hours later, Leo woke up just enough to notice Mart sitting nearby.

Without opening his eyes completely, he mumbled,

> "...Hey..."

> "...You still here?"

Mart gave a soft hoot.

Leo smiled sleepily.

> "...Good..."

Less than five seconds later...

He was asleep again.

Mart gently fluffed his feathers and settled down beside him.

---

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late in the afternoon, Star walked past the lounge.

Leo was asleep on the couch.

Mart rested quietly on the backrest above him.

Star smiled.

She almost whispered,

> "...Cute kitty..."

Mart immediately turned his head and gave her the most unimpressed stare anyone had ever seen from an owl.

Star raised both hands.

> "...Sorry."

> "...Cute owl."

Mart gave one satisfied hoot.

Balance had been restored.
