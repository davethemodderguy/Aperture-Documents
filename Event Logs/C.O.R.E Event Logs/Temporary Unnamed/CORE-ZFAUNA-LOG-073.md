## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical notes a concerning pattern.

The journal does **not** express anger toward others.

Nor does it indicate a desire to harm anyone.

Instead, nearly every surviving entry reflects fear, guilt, and a profound loss of confidence in herself.

Psychologists emphasize that these writings should be understood as expressions of emotional distress, not as predictions of future behavior.

The repeated references to the "red eyes" appear to symbolize Taiga's fear of entering her documented dissociative defensive state again.
