# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-005**

### **RESIDENTIAL REASSIGNMENT — NEKO'S LIVING QUARTERS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – INTERNAL / RESIDENT LIFE

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following extensive renovations carried out by Engineering (and, according to multiple witnesses, "mostly by Neko because he got carried away"), Residential Room **N-07** has been completely rebuilt.

Originally intended as a standard living space...

It now resembles a small apartment.

The renovation included:

- Multiple bedrooms.
    
- A shared living room.
    
- A full kitchen.
    
- Reading and recreation areas.
    
- Heated flooring.
    
- Large panoramic windows overlooking the Icelandic mountains.
    
- Additional insulation for the long Arctic winters.
    

Director Lucious approved the modifications after confirming they complied with Blacksite safety regulations.

His only comment was:

> **"...You somehow built an entire house inside the residence wing."**

Neko replied:

> **"Space optimization."**

No one challenged the explanation.

---

# **I. CURRENT RESIDENTS**

The room has since become home to several permanent residents.

Current occupants include:

- **Neko**
    
- **Miss Neko**
    
- **Star**
    
- **Nightshade**
    
- **Bao**
    
- **Frost**
    
- **Taiga**
    
- **Aster**
    
- **Leo**
    

Despite the unusually large household...

Personnel report remarkably few disputes.

Most disagreements involve who finished the tomatoes, where Leo has fallen asleep this time, or whether Bao has hidden snacks somewhere in the kitchen.

---

# **II. SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS**

The expanded room contains enough proper beds for every humanoid resident.

Each has naturally settled into their own routine.

### Neko

Usually the last one asleep.

Frequently found reading engineering journals long after everyone else has gone to bed.

---

### Miss Neko

Sleeps peacefully after making certain everyone else is comfortable.

She has unofficially become the household's bedtime supervisor.

---

### Star

Keeps a small nightlight near her bed.

Although she has recovered from her earlier fear of bright light, she still prefers a softly lit room.

---

### Nightshade

Sleeps lightly.

Usually with a book resting on the bedside table.

---

### Bao

Almost always falls asleep first.

Usually after announcing she is "definitely not tired."

---

### Frost

Keeps an extra blanket nearby despite her natural affinity for cold.

She insists it's comfortable.

No one argues.

---

# **III. ASTER & TAIGA**

Aster was offered her own bed.

She politely declined.

Instead...

Every evening, she walks over to a thick, circular rug placed beside one of the room's large heaters.

Waiting there...

Is Taiga.

The young snow leopard immediately lowers herself onto the rug.

Aster curls against her side.

Taiga carefully wraps a foreleg around the much smaller white cat.

Within minutes...

Both are asleep.

The arrangement has become so routine that the heater has unofficially been nicknamed:

> **"The Cat Corner."**

No one has ever seen either of them choose anywhere else.

---

# **IV. LEO**

Leo, meanwhile...

Has absolutely no concept of assigned sleeping spaces.

Documented sleeping locations include:

- Around the coat rack.
    
- Hanging from a curtain tie.
    
- Wrapped around Neko's desk lamp.
    
- Curled around Star's chair.
    
- Around the handle of the refrigerator.
    
- Hanging from Bao's backpack.
    
- Around the bedframe.
    
- Around Neko's inactive **Neko's Claws**.
    
- Around the shower curtain rail.
    
- Around Taiga's tail.
    
- Around Aster's silver earring (briefly, before sliding onto her shoulder).
    

Veterinary confirms Leo appears perfectly comfortable sleeping while suspended from almost anything capable of supporting twelve centimeters of snake.

---

# **V. EVENINGS**

The room has gradually developed a quiet nightly routine.

Neko usually prepares a late snack.

Miss Neko checks that everyone has eaten.

Bao raids the refrigerator.

Frost reminds Bao not to eat someone else's dessert.

Nightshade finishes the day's paperwork.

Star reads aloud from whatever book she's currently enjoying.

Aster quietly listens while grooming Taiga's fur.

Leo sleeps through nearly all of it.

Outside...

Snow continues falling over the Icelandic mountains surrounding the Blacksite.

Inside...

The heaters hum softly.

For many personnel passing the open doorway...

The room feels less like staff housing...

And more like a home.

---

# **VI. DIRECTOR'S COMMENT**

During an evening inspection, Director Lucious stopped outside Room N-07.

Through the open door he observed:

Neko asleep in a chair after falling asleep mid-book.

Miss Neko had gently placed a blanket over him.

Star had also fallen asleep with her book still open.

Nightshade quietly closed it and set it on the bedside table.

Bao was somehow hugging an extra pillow she claimed she "wasn't using."

Frost was asleep despite insisting she wasn't tired.

Taiga and Aster were curled together on the rug beside the heater.

Leo hung from a coat hook.

Asleep.

Again.

Lucious stood silently for several moments before switching off the hallway lights and quietly closing the door.

His report contained only one sentence.

> **"For a place built to contain the extraordinary... it is surprisingly good at feeling like home."**
