# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-037**

### **RESIDENTIAL RECORD — "DUM"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – INTERNAL / SOCIAL ARCHIVE

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following several quiet weeks of recovery, Medical and Residential Services are pleased to report that Taiga has returned to her usual cheerful self.

She has resumed training.

She spends time with her roommates again.

She plays in the snow outside with Aster.

She can once again be found laughing in the hallways.

This morning, however...

Personnel noticed something new.

There was a yellow sticky note attached to her forehead.

Written on it in neat black marker was one word.

> **Dum**

---

## **INITIAL DISCOVERY**

The first person to notice was Neko.

He looked up from his morning coffee.

Looked at Taiga.

Then looked again.

> "...Taiga."

She smiled.

> "Morning!"

Neko pointed toward his own forehead.

> "...You have a sticky note."

Taiga reached up.

Touched it.

Nodded proudly.

> "I know."

Neko waited.

"...Why?"

Taiga simply shrugged.

> "...I dunno."

---

## **THE MYSTERY**

Throughout the day, nearly everyone asked the same question.

The answers varied slightly.

None explained anything.

---

### Bao

> "...Why 'Dum'?"

Taiga smiled.

> "...Because."

---

### Frost

> "...Is it supposed to mean something?"

> "...Maybe."

---

### Star

> "...Did someone put it there?"

> "...Nope."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You wrote it yourself?"

> "...Yeah."

---

### Nightshade

> "...Explain."

Taiga thought very hard.

Nearly a full minute passed.

Finally...

> "...I can't."

Nightshade accepted this with a small nod.

---

## **ASTER'S INVESTIGATION**

Aster appeared determined to solve the mystery.

She approached Taiga with her whiteboard.

> **Why?**

Taiga stared thoughtfully at the question.

She looked up at the ceiling.

She looked down at the floor.

She looked at Aster again.

Then she slowly wrote beneath the question.

> **...Dunno :)**

Aster looked at the answer.

Looked at the sticky note.

Then erased her board.

She wrote something new.

> **Good enough.**

---

## **LEO'S OPINION**

Leo eventually woke from his afternoon nap.

Still wrapped around Taiga's shoulders, he looked directly at the sticky note.

According to Taiga's translation, he had only one comment.

> "...Warm."

He promptly went back to sleep.

No one was surprised.

---

## **MART'S OBSERVATION**

Mart tilted his head while examining the note.

Taiga translated his quiet hoot.

> "...Humans are strange."

Taiga laughed.

> "...Probably."

---

## **ROOM N-07 REACTION**

By dinner, nobody questioned it anymore.

The sticky note remained exactly where it was.

Taiga remained happy.

She laughed.

She helped Bao bake.

She played cards with Star.

She listened to one of Neko's engineering explanations.

She even convinced Aster to join her in making tiny paper snowflakes.

The note stayed on her forehead the entire time.

Eventually, Neko simply sighed.

> "...If she's happy..."

He took another sip of coffee.

> "...I'm not asking."

Everyone else quietly agreed.

---

---

---

> "...I kind of like it."

---

### Nightshade

After observing Taiga happily walking through the hallway for several hours.

> "...No operational impact."

---

### Aster

Before bed, Aster quietly stuck a second sticky note onto her own forehead.

It read:

> **Cat**

Taiga laughed so hard she nearly fell off the sofa.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

The origin and meaning of Taiga's "Dum" sticky note remain unknown.

Taiga has declined—or perhaps been unable—to provide a clear explanation.

No adverse effects have been observed.

Morale within Room N-07 has, if anything, improved.

---

### **Closing Observation**

As Director Lucious walked through the residential wing during his evening rounds, Taiga cheerfully waved.

The sticky note was still attached.

He paused.

Read it.

Looked at Taiga.

She smiled back without a care in the world.

After a brief silence, he smiled faintly in return and continued on his way.

Some mysteries at the Blacksite demanded months of investigation.

Others...

Apparently just wore a sticky note and carried on with their day.
