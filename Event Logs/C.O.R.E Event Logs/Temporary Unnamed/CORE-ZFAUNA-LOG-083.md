# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-083**

### **CRITICAL INCIDENT REPORT — PERIMETER BREACH**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – DIRECTORATE / SECURITY / MEDICAL

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

**Time:** 07:16

Security confirmed that Taiga successfully exited the Blacksite perimeter through an emergency cargo access route before the sector-wide lockdown was completed.

The outer gate had not yet been reopened to the public, but the sequence of cargo operations created a brief window that Taiga exploited.

Review of surveillance footage showed exceptional planning.

She avoided patrol routes.

She remained outside camera coverage whenever possible.

She demonstrated an intimate understanding of Blacksite operations.

Neko's earlier stealth lessons, originally intended for harmless movement within the facility, had been adapted into a highly effective method of evasion.

Security lost visual contact shortly after she entered the surrounding snowfields.

---

## **THE APPARITION**

At the exact moment Taiga crossed the perimeter boundary, every confirmed listener reported hearing the same voice simultaneously.

Not calm.

Not composed.

Afraid.

> "...Taiga!"

The apparition immediately followed.

Unlike previous manifestations confined largely to the residential sector, she moved without hesitation toward the perimeter.

Witnesses observed her racing across the snow after her daughter.

She repeatedly called Taiga's name.

There was only one problem.

Taiga still could not hear her.

---

## **PREVIOUS ENCOUNTERS**

Review of security footage from the days leading up to the incident revealed a consistent behavioral pattern.

Whenever Taiga unexpectedly encountered members of Room N-07, she immediately became defensive.

She refused conversation.

She withdrew as quickly as possible.

Her reactions were strongest toward Neko.

She consistently interpreted any attempt at contact as an effort to bring her back before she was "safe."

Medical continues to attribute this to the damaged trust resulting from the failed disclosure about her mother.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

One encounter differed from all the others.

Director Lucious found Taiga alone near an auxiliary maintenance tunnel several days before the perimeter breach.

He made no attempt to approach quickly.

He simply stopped several meters away.

For a long time, neither spoke.

Finally, Director Lucious quietly said,

> "...Taiga."

She didn't look at him.

He continued.

> "...Come home."

Another long silence.

When Taiga finally answered, her voice was barely audible.

> "...Forget me, Dad."

Director Lucious remained silent.

Taiga continued, still unable to meet his eyes.

> "...You'll be happier..."

She struggled to finish the sentence.

> "...Without worrying about me."

She turned away.

Within seconds, she disappeared into another corridor.

Director Lucious did not pursue her.

Security footage showed him standing alone for nearly a full minute after she had gone.

---

She stepped forward instinctively.

Though she could not truly touch the world around her, she continued after her daughter without hesitation.

### **Closing Observation**

The perimeter cameras recorded one final image before the blowing snow obscured everything.

A small figure walked steadily into the white horizon.

Several meters behind...

A translucent woman followed with desperate determination.

She reached out again and again.

Her hand never reached her daughter's shoulder.

The distance between them remained only a few steps.

Yet it was a distance neither of them could cross.

One could not stop running.

The other could not stop following.
