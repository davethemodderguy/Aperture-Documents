# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-148**

### **CROSS-TRAINING EXERCISE — "HOW TO GLIDE"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL / TRAINING

---

## **SUMMARY**

Despite Mart Sunbringer possessing fully capable powered flight, Taiga insisted there was "more than one way to fly."

As a result, she organized an afternoon training session with the stated goal of teaching Mart how to glide.

When reminded that Mart could already fly, Taiga responded,

> "...Exactly."

> "...But can he glide?"

---

## **TRAINING SESSION**

Location:

Eastern cliffs overlooking the Starlands.

Mart hovered several meters above the ground.

Taiga floated five centimeters above the grass.

She folded her arms like an instructor.

> **Taiga:** "Lesson one."

> "...Turn your brain off."

Mart blinked.

> "...What?"

> **Taiga:** "Flying keeps you in the air."

> "Gliding lets the air do the work."

Mart looked unconvinced.

---

## **FIRST ATTEMPT**

Taiga demonstrated first.

She launched upward using an Air Jump.

A second.

Then a third.

At the peak of her climb, she folded her wings slightly and allowed herself to accelerate forward instead of climbing higher.

She skimmed gracefully over the valley before circling back.

Landing with a small flourish.

> **Taiga:** "...See?"

Mart nodded politely.

> "...You stopped flying."

Taiga pointed accusingly.

> "...Exactly!"

Mart tilted his head.

> "...I don't understand."

---

## **SECOND ATTEMPT**

Mart climbed to the same height.

He folded his wings as instructed.

Immediately...

He began falling.

Instinctively, his wings spread again.

He resumed normal flight.

Taiga sighed.

> "...You're thinking too much."

Mart looked genuinely confused.

> "...Not falling is kind of the point."

---

## **BREAKTHROUGH**

After several more attempts, Taiga realized the problem.

Mart's wings automatically generated lift.

He had spent his entire life correcting even the slightest loss of altitude without thinking.

She explained it differently.

> **Taiga:** "...Pretend your wings are just steering."

Mart paused.

Then deliberately reduced the lift from his wings.

For the first time...

He allowed gravity to do most of the work.

His speed increased.

His wings adjusted only enough to control direction.

He crossed the valley in one long, smooth descent.

When he landed, he looked back toward Taiga.

> "...That..."

A small smile appeared.

> "...Felt different."

Taiga grinned proudly.

> "...That's gliding."

---

## **OBSERVATIONS**

Researchers noted that Mart remained perfectly capable of true flight throughout the exercise.

However, learning to glide offered several advantages:

- Reduced energy expenditure during long descents.
    
- Increased forward speed.
    
- Greater momentum preservation.
    
- Improved silent movement when avoiding wingbeats.
    

Mart later admitted it was unexpectedly enjoyable.

### **Closing Observation**

As the sun began to set, the siblings launched from the same cliff one last time.

Mart rose effortlessly on glowing wings.

Taiga followed with a series of Air Jumps before spreading her own wings.

For a while, neither of them climbed.

Neither of them descended quickly.

They simply rode the evening air together, side by side.

After landing, Mart looked at Taiga.

A faint smile crossed his face.

> "...You're right."

Taiga immediately perked up.

> "...About what?"

Mart folded his wings.

> "...Gliding isn't the same as flying."

Taiga's smile disappeared.

Mart continued before she could protest.

> "...It's its own kind of fun."

Taiga laughed.

She accepted that answer.
