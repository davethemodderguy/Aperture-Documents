# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-112**

### **ACTIVE INCIDENT — SECONDARY MISSING PERSON (TAIGA SUNBRINGER)**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – INTERNAL EMERGENCY

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

**Mart Sunbringer remains missing.**

At the time of writing, there have been no confirmed sightings, no contact, and no evidence indicating his current condition or location.

Following weeks of unsuccessful search operations, a second critical incident has occurred.

**Taiga Sunbringer has disappeared.**

---

## **EVENT TIMELINE**

**08:17**

Taiga attended breakfast.

She barely touched her food.

Witnesses described her as unusually quiet.

She repeatedly glanced toward Mart's empty chair.

---

**10:42**

Taiga was seen entering Mart's room alone.

She remained inside for approximately thirty-one minutes.

No sounds were heard.

---

**11:15**

Security cameras captured Taiga exiting the room.

She was crying.

She attempted to wipe the tears away before anyone noticed.

---

**11:17**

Taiga encountered Director Lucious in the hallway.

He stopped.

"...Taiga."

She looked at him.

For several seconds, neither spoke.

Finally, Director Lucious asked quietly,

> "...Have you slept?"

Taiga forced a smile.

> "...I'm okay, Dad."

She wasn't.

Director Lucious took one step toward her.

Taiga instinctively stepped back.

---

**11:18**

Without another word—

Taiga transformed into her snow leopard form.

Witnesses described the transformation as unusually abrupt.

She immediately sprinted down the corridor.

---

## **PURSUIT**

Security personnel did not attempt to tackle her.

Instead, they followed at a distance.

Taiga moved through maintenance corridors, ventilation access routes, abandoned storage sections, and service tunnels with a familiarity that surprised even Residential Security.

Within minutes...

Visual contact was lost.

---

## **SEARCH STATUS**

An immediate lockdown of non-essential corridors was initiated.

Thermal imaging.

Motion sensors.

Security cameras.

Maintenance drones.

Internal tracking systems.

Nothing.

Research later concluded only one thing with certainty.

**Taiga never left the Blacksite.**

She is still somewhere inside.

No one has found her.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral was informed less than five minutes after Taiga vanished.

She did not speak.

She simply stood up from her chair.

Looked toward the hallway.

And whispered,

> "...Not again..."

She searched until long after midnight.

Long after everyone else had been ordered to rest.

She refused.

> "...I'm not losing both of my children."

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious personally assumed command of the search operation.

His orders were brief.

> "No weapons."

Another pause.

> "No pursuit."

> "If you find Taiga..."

He lowered his voice.

> "...Don't chase her."

---

### Neko

Neko reviewed the security footage repeatedly.

He eventually pushed the monitor away.

> "...She's using everything I taught her."

A long silence.

He covered his face with one hand.

> "...I taught her to survive."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...She's hiding because she's hurting."

> "...Not because she wants to disappear."

---

### Bao

Bao quietly packed several insulated containers with food and bottles of water.

She handed them to the search teams.

> "...If you find her..."

She hesitated.

> "...Make sure she eats."

---

### Frost

Whitefall Lake was searched despite there being no evidence Taiga had traveled there.

Nothing.

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star searched every place she and Taiga had ever played together.

The rooftop.

The gardens.

The training yard.

The library.

Mart's room.

Every time...

She called softly,

> "...Taiga?"

Only silence answered.

---

### Nightshade Dawnbringer

Nightshade searched the maintenance tunnels alone.

She found pawprints in the dust.

They ended abruptly at a concrete wall.

There was nowhere else to go.

Yet Taiga was gone.

---

### Leo

Leo woke from an afternoon nap.

He noticed two empty chairs.

Mart's.

And Taiga's.

He remained awake for the rest of the day.

## **INTERNAL MEMORANDUM**

Personnel are reminded that Taiga is **not** considered hostile.

She is experiencing severe psychological distress.

If located:

- Do not corner her.
    
- Do not raise your voice.
    
- Do not attempt physical restraint unless required to prevent immediate harm.
    
- Notify Director Lucious and Medical Services immediately.
    
- Allow trusted family members to make first contact whenever possible.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

At 02:13, one of the maintenance cameras briefly captured movement.

A small snow leopard stepped cautiously into the edge of the frame.

She looked thinner than before.

She carried something gently in her mouth.

When the footage was enhanced, the object was identified.

It was one of Mart's books.

Taiga carefully set it down.

Curled up beside it.

Lowered her head onto its cover.

For nearly a minute, she didn't move.

Then a distant door closed somewhere else in the facility.

Her ears immediately perked up.

She stood.

Picked the book back up.

And disappeared into the maze of corridors before anyone could reach her.

By the time the response team arrived...

The hallway was empty once again.
