# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-156**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL CASE SUMMARY — MART SUNBRINGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – RESIDENT WELFARE / CONFIDENTIAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following multiple counseling sessions and long-term observation, Psychological Services have concluded that Mart Sunbringer possesses an unusually persistent autobiographical memory.

Unlike Taiga and Astral, whose memories of their early lives faded or returned only in fragments, Mart recalls nearly every significant event from his life with exceptional clarity.

This includes memories formed during early childhood.

Unfortunately, those memories include some of the most traumatic experiences the family endured.

---

## **CLINICAL OBSERVATION**

Mart remembers...

Their mother being hunted.

Their mother dying.

The cold.

The silence afterward.

He remembers Taiga wandering alone through the snow.

He remembers staying behind because he believed she would come back for him.

He remembers eventually finding her again.

He remembers Taiga's arrival at the Blacksite.

He remembers every place they slept before then.

Every storm.

Every day they went hungry.

Every promise they made to each other.

Research staff noted that, while many people naturally forget parts of very early childhood, Mart does not appear to have experienced the same degree of memory loss.

His recollections remain vivid and emotionally immediate.

---

## **THE BURDEN OF MEMORY**

Psychological Services described Mart's condition with one sentence:

> **"He remembers too much for someone who was so young."**

Many of the events he witnessed occurred before he could fully understand them.

As an adult mind developed around those memories, their emotional impact grew rather than faded.

What once confused him...

Now traumatizes him.

---

## **CURRENT SYMPTOMS**

Mart continues to experience:

- Chronic insomnia.
    
- Recurring nightmares.
    
- Episodes of emotional withdrawal.
    
- Periodic crying spells when alone.
    
- Persistent fear of abandonment.
    
- Heightened distress when reminded of Taiga's past injuries or separation.
    

Nightmares most commonly involve:

- Taiga disappearing.
    
- Taiga rejecting him.
    
- Being left behind in the snow.
    
- Returning to an empty place where no one answers him.
    

Upon waking, Mart often requires several moments to convince himself he is still at the Blacksite.

---

## **OBSERVED INCIDENT**

**03:18**

Residential staff heard movement inside Mart's room.

A welfare check was considered.

Before anyone acted, Taiga quietly walked down the hallway.

She had apparently woken on her own.

She knocked gently.

> **Taiga:** "...Mart?"

No answer.

She opened the door.

Mart sat on the floor beside his bed.

His breathing was uneven.

Tears quietly ran down his face.

He stared at the wall without speaking.

Taiga didn't ask what happened.

She simply sat beside him.

Several minutes passed.

Eventually, Mart whispered,

> "...I dreamed you left again."

Taiga immediately took his hand.

> "...I'm here."

Mart closed his eyes.

> "...I know..."

> "...I just needed to hear it."

---

## **FAMILY OBSERVATIONS**

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral carries guilt over the fact that she cannot remember many details Mart still relives every day.

She once apologized to him.

Mart gently shook his head.

> "...You forgetting..."

> "...Isn't your fault."

After a pause, he added,

> "...Sometimes I wish I could."

Astral hugged him tightly.

---

### Taiga Sunbringer

Taiga only learned the extent of Mart's memories during counseling.

She quietly cried afterward.

> "...All this time..."

> "...You remembered everything?"

Mart nodded once.

> "...Everything important."

Taiga lowered her head.

> "...You were just a little brother."

Mart smiled sadly.

> "...I still am."

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL RECOMMENDATIONS**

Mart's desire for solitude should continue to be respected.

However, prolonged isolation is discouraged.

Clinical guidance recommends:

- Routine social interaction.
    
- Gentle welfare checks.
    
- Predictable daily routines.
    
- Reassurance from trusted family members when appropriate.
    

Research emphasizes an important distinction.

Mart does **not** fear being physically alone.

He fears believing he has been forgotten.

**Mart Sunbringer**

- Chronic insomnia: Ongoing.

> **"Do not allow him to believe he has been left alone."**

---

## **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, Taiga quietly slipped a folded note beneath Mart's bedroom door before going to sleep.

The next morning, the note was gone.

In its place was a single white feather.

The note, later found tucked safely inside one of Mart's books, read:

> _"If you wake up from another nightmare and I'm asleep..._

> _...wake me anyway._

> _I'd rather lose sleep than let you face it alone._

> _— Big Sis"_

When Mart read it, he smiled.

For someone who remembered every painful moment of his life...

He made sure to remember that one too.
