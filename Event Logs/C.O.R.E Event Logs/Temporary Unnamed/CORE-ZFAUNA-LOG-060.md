# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-060**

### **RESIDENTIAL LOG — A QUIET DAY IN ROOM N-07**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Due to an unusually busy work schedule throughout the Blacksite, the majority of Room N-07's residents departed early in the morning.

Neko and Miss Neko reported to Engineering.

Bao spent the day in the kitchens.

Star and Nightshade were assigned to C.O.R.E. duties.

Aster accompanied Director Lucious during administrative work as Oracle of the Blacksite.

Leo, despite normally sleeping through most events, had curled up around Aster's shoulders before she left.

For the first time in several weeks...

Only **Taiga** and **Frost** remained in Room N-07.

---

## **08:17**

The room was unusually quiet.

Taiga looked around.

"...Everyone's gone..."

Frost looked up from the book she had been reading.

> "...For today."

Taiga nodded slowly.

Then walked over and sat beside her.

"...What do we do now?"

Frost closed her book.

A small smile appeared.

> "...Let's find something."

---

## **SNOWMEN**

The pair walked into the courtyard outside the residential wing.

Fresh Icelandic snow had fallen overnight.

Without exchanging many words, they began rolling snow into large spheres.

An hour later...

There were six snowmen.

One had Star's flower-shaped hair ornament.

One wore an old engineering helmet that Neko had accidentally left outside.

Another somehow had a tiny paper whiteboard attached to it.

Aster would later claim it looked surprisingly accurate.

---

## **ICE SCULPTURES**

Frost suggested another activity.

Together, they began creating sculptures using their cryogenic abilities.

Frost carefully shaped elegant crystalline flowers.

Taiga experimented with her newer powers.

Her first attempt resembled a chair.

Her second looked vaguely like Leo.

Her third...

Actually looked like Leo.

Mart landed on the sculpture.

Apparently approving of the resemblance.

---

## **FROST WALKER**

Taiga eventually asked,

"...Want to go to the lake?"

Frost nodded.

The two quietly walked to Whitefall Lake.

Taiga stepped onto the water.

A frozen path appeared beneath her feet.

Frost followed beside her, effortlessly freezing the surface as she walked.

The two spent nearly an hour simply wandering across the lake.

No training.

No experiments.

Just conversation.

---

## **THE CONVERSATION**

For a while...

Neither spoke.

Eventually Taiga broke the silence.

"...Do you ever get lonely?"

Frost thought carefully before answering.

> "...Sometimes."

Another pause.

> "...But loneliness feels smaller when someone else is nearby."

Taiga smiled softly.

"...Yeah."

"...I think so too."

---

## **SNOWBALL FIGHT**

The peaceful atmosphere lasted exactly four minutes.

Taiga quietly packed a snowball.

She gently tossed it toward Frost.

It landed harmlessly against Frost's shoulder.

Frost looked down at the snow.

Then back at Taiga.

"...You started it."

Taiga laughed.

"...I know."

What followed was perhaps the calmest snowball fight in Blacksite history.

Neither participant threw particularly hard.

Neither attempted to "win."

Both laughed more than either expected.

---

## **RETURN TO ROOM N-07**

By late afternoon, the two returned indoors.

Their clothes were dusted with snow.

Taiga made hot tea.

After remembering Medical's warning...

She patiently waited for it to cool before drinking.

Frost noticed.

> "...Good."

Taiga smiled.

"...I'm learning."

---

## **THE OTHERS RETURN**

That evening, the rest of Room N-07 gradually returned from work.

Neko immediately noticed the tiny engineering helmet missing from his workshop.

"...Why..."

He looked toward the courtyard.

"...Is there a snowman wearing my helmet?"

Taiga quietly raised one hand.

"...Sorry."

Neko sighed.

Then laughed.

"...Keep it."

---

Star ran outside moments later.

"...YOU BUILT SNOWMEN WITHOUT ME?!"

She looked utterly betrayed.

Taiga couldn't stop laughing.

"...We'll build more tomorrow."

Star immediately forgave her.

---

Bao smiled after seeing the ice sculptures still standing outside.

> "...Looks like you two had fun."

Frost gave a small nod.

> "...We did."

---

Aster returned with Director Lucious shortly afterward.

She looked at the tiny snowman holding a paper whiteboard.

Then looked at Taiga.

She smiled.

It looked exactly like her.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS'S REMARK**

Director Lucious reviewed the security footage that evening.

Most of it consisted of two young girls quietly building snowmen, walking across a frozen lake, and laughing during a gentle snowball fight.

He closed the recording with a faint smile.

> "...Sometimes..."

He looked out toward the snow-covered courtyard.

> "...A quiet day is exactly what this place needs."

---

### **Closing Observation**

Later that night, everyone gathered back in Room N-07.

The room felt lively again.

Taiga curled up on the rug near the heater.

Frost returned to her book.

Star excitedly announced plans for "an even bigger snow fort tomorrow."

Leo, now back from spending the day with Aster, lazily slithered over and wrapped himself around Taiga's arm before falling asleep almost instantly.

Taiga looked around the room.

It was noisy.

Warm.

Comfortable.

She smiled to herself.

The day had been quiet.

But she hadn't been alone.
