# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-062**

### **AFTER-ACTION SUMMARY — PERIMETER THREAT NEUTRALIZATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – RESTRICTED (DIRECTORATE ONLY)

**Access Notice:** This document is restricted to Director-level personnel and designated Security Command staff. Discussion of the operation with Taiga is expressly prohibited.

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following continued intelligence regarding the group of illegal hunters responsible for:

- The death of Taiga's mother.
    
- The attempted capture of Taiga.
    
- Multiple armed incursions near Blacksite territory.
    

Director Lucious authorized a direct response.

He personally led the operation.

Taiga was neither informed beforehand nor involved in any capacity.

---

## **OBJECTIVE**

Primary objectives were:

- Eliminate the threat posed by the hunters.
    
- Prevent any future attempts to locate or capture Taiga.
    
- Secure evidence of illegal activities.
    
- Ensure the safety of the Blacksite's surrounding perimeter.
    

Mission objectives were completed.

---

## **OPERATIONAL NOTES**

Security footage from the operation remains sealed under Director-level clearance.

Personnel with access have unanimously agreed that the footage should remain classified.

One responding security officer summarized the engagement with a single sentence:

> "...Accounting is going to hate this."

When asked to clarify, the officer pointed toward the post-operation expenditure report.

---

## **PROPERTY DAMAGE**

The operation resulted in extensive destruction of hostile equipment and infrastructure.

The Accounting Department later received invoices including, but not limited to:

- Multiple vehicles rendered beyond repair.
    
- Heavy equipment destroyed.
    
- Temporary structures demolished.
    
- Ammunition expenditure exceeding projected estimates.
    
- Recovery and disposal costs.
    

The final expense report required several additional pages.

Accounting's official comment consisted only of:

> "...Again?"

---

## **ENGINEERING COMMENT**

Upon reviewing the photographs of the site afterward, Neko adjusted his glasses and quietly remarked,

> "...That's..."

He paused.

> "...One way to solve the problem."

When asked if he could estimate the amount of energy released during the operation, he looked at the images again.

> "...I'd rather not."

---

## **DIRECTOR'S STATEMENT**

Director Lucious submitted only a brief operational summary.

> "Threat neutralized."

> "No further danger to Taiga from this organization is anticipated."

No additional details were included.

---

## **MEDICAL DIRECTIVE**

Medical and Psychological Services jointly issued the following recommendation:

> **Taiga is not to be informed of the operational details.**

Medical notes that Taiga has repeatedly demonstrated a strong aversion to seeing others harmed, including individuals who previously threatened her.

Informing her of the specifics of the operation would provide no therapeutic benefit and could cause unnecessary emotional distress.

If Taiga inquires about the hunters, personnel are instructed only to reassure her that the threat has been dealt with and that she is safe.

---

## **ROOM N-07**

Several days after the operation, Taiga quietly asked Director Lucious a question.

> "...Dad?"

He looked up from his paperwork.

> "...Yes?"

She hesitated.

> "...Those people..."

> "...They won't come back... right?"

Director Lucious answered without hesitation.

> "...No."

> "...They won't."

Taiga lowered her shoulders in relief.

> "...Okay."

She smiled faintly.

> "...Thank you."

She did not ask for further details.

Director Lucious did not volunteer them.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

The group responsible for the original attack on Taiga's family is no longer considered an active threat to the Blacksite or its residents.

Security has returned to normal patrol levels.

Taiga remains unaware of the specifics of the operation, in accordance with Medical's recommendation.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Later that evening, Taiga was outside playing in the snow with Star, Frost, Aster, Leo, and Mart.

She laughed as Star accidentally buried herself in a snowbank.

Watching from a nearby balcony, Director Lucious folded the classified report closed.

He looked toward the courtyard for a moment before placing the file into a secure archive.

Some burdens, he decided, did not need to be shared.

Especially not with a little girl who had already carried far too many of them.
