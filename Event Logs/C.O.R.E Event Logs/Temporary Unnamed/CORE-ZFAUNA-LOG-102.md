# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-102**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION — THE SUNBRINGER BALANCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Since Taiga's emotional recovery and Mart's increased use of his human form, Residential Services has noticed an amusing pattern.

The two siblings possess almost perfectly opposite personalities.

Yet somehow...

They balance each other.

---

## **TAIGA SUNBRINGER**

Current baseline behavior:

- Highly energetic.
    
- Cheerful.
    
- Optimistic.
    
- Curious.
    
- Social.
    
- Frequently excited by even small things.
    
- Has a tendency to become _too_ enthusiastic.
    

Examples include:

- Running instead of walking.
    
- Excitedly explaining something while accidentally talking over herself.
    
- Wagging her tail so hard she knocks things off tables.
    
- Smiling at almost everyone she passes.
    
- Getting so excited over food that she once choked on a salad.
    

Residential staff have affectionately nicknamed her:

> **"The Happy Leopard."**

---

## **MART SUNBRINGER**

Current baseline behavior:

- Quiet.
    
- Calm.
    
- Patient.
    
- Observant.
    
- Soft-spoken.
    
- Rarely rushes.
    
- Prefers listening before speaking.
    

Mart seldom raises his voice.

He walks slowly.

Eats slowly.

Reads slowly.

Even his blinking somehow seems unhurried.

Residential staff have jokingly nicknamed him:

> **"The Nonchalant Owl."**

Mart neither confirmed nor denied the nickname.

---

## **THE BALANCE EFFECT**

When observed separately:

Taiga can become so energetic that entire rooms seem livelier simply because she's there.

Mart can make even a noisy room feel peaceful just by quietly sitting with a book.

However...

When the two occupy the same room...

Something curious happens.

Their personalities seem to "average out."

Taiga naturally slows down.

Mart becomes slightly more talkative and expressive.

Neither appears to be doing it consciously.

---

## **OBSERVED EXAMPLE**

Location: Residential Lounge.

Taiga rushed into the room.

> "...MART!"

> "...LOOK WHAT I FOUND!"

She was halfway through explaining an unusual-shaped snowflake she'd frozen with Frost Walker.

Mart looked up from his book.

He smiled.

> "...Show me."

Taiga immediately sat down.

Instead of excitedly bouncing around the room...

She calmly explained every detail.

Nearly twenty minutes later, Star walked in.

She stopped.

Looked around.

Then asked,

> "...Why is it so... normal in here?"

Neko glanced over the top of his newspaper.

> "...They're cancelling each other out again."

### **Closing Observation**

One rainy afternoon, the common room was unusually noisy.

Residents chatted.

Star and Aster were playing.

Someone had music on in the background.

Taiga bounded into the room with her usual enthusiasm.

Mart followed a minute later, carrying a book.

Taiga noticed him.

She smiled and sat beside him.

Mart looked up from his book.

> "...Hi."

> "...Hi."

That was all.

Within a few minutes, conversations around them naturally quieted.

The room wasn't silent.

It wasn't loud.

It simply felt... peaceful.

Neko looked up from his coffee.

> "...There it is."

Miss Neko smiled knowingly.

> "...The Sunbringer balance."
