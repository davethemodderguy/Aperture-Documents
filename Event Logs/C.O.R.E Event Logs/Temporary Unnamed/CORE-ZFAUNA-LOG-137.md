# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-137**

### **CRITICAL INCIDENT — UNAUTHORIZED USE OF MEDICAL EQUIPMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – PSYCHOLOGICAL / SECURITY

---

## **SUMMARY**

On ██/██/20██, Taiga Sunbringer unlawfully removed a tranquilizer launcher from the Medical Response Armory.

Minutes later, Mart Sunbringer was sighted crossing the central courtyard.

Believing it was the only way to stop him from leaving again, Taiga attempted to immobilize him.

The attempt failed.

The emotional consequences did not.

---

## **SECURITY REPORT**

At approximately **16:11**, Medical staff reported one tranquilizer launcher missing during routine inventory.

Security footage later confirmed Taiga entering the storage room.

She hesitated before unlocking the cabinet.

She stood motionless for several seconds.

Then quietly took the launcher.

She never attempted to conceal what she had done.

---

## **COURTYARD INCIDENT**

At **16:27**, Mart was observed walking through the central courtyard.

His wings were absent.

For the first time in months...

He was simply walking.

Taiga saw him from the opposite side of the courtyard.

She called his name.

Mart stopped for only a fraction of a second.

Then continued walking.

Taiga raised the tranquilizer launcher.

Witnesses shouted for her to stop.

She fired.

One dart struck Mart's shoulder.

He continued walking.

Another hit his back.

Then another.

Several missed entirely.

By the time the launcher clicked empty, multiple tranquilizer darts had struck him.

Mart finally stopped.

Not because he collapsed.

Because he chose to.

---

## **MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

Medical later confirmed that the cumulative dosage delivered would ordinarily be expected to incapacitate an animal many times larger than a human.

Mart displayed no visible signs of sedation.

No instability.

No loss of balance.

No alteration in consciousness.

The tranquilizer appeared to have no observable effect.

Research has offered no explanation.

Psychological Services recommended that discussion remain focused on the encounter itself rather than the anomalous resistance.

---

## **DIRECT CONTACT**

Taiga dropped the empty launcher and ran toward him.

By the time she reached him, she was struggling to breathe.

Mart slowly reached behind himself.

One by one...

He pulled the tranquilizer darts free.

He looked at them quietly for a moment.

Then held them out toward Taiga.

Not angrily.

Not mockingly.

Simply returning them.

Taiga stared at the darts in disbelief.

She couldn't bring herself to take them.

Mart gently placed them into her trembling hands.

For several long seconds...

Neither of them spoke.

Finally...

Mart broke the silence.

His voice was tired.

Almost unbearably so.

> **"...How hard is it..."**

He looked at his younger sister with eyes full of sorrow rather than anger.

> **"...to make you give up, Taiga?"**

The courtyard fell completely silent.

Taiga's hands shook so violently that the darts slipped from her fingers onto the pavement.

She opened her mouth.

No words came.

Mart waited.

Just for a moment.

As though part of him still hoped she would say something that could reach him.

Nothing came.

He quietly stepped around her.

Continued walking.

He never looked back.

---

## **SECURITY ACTIONS**

The tranquilizer launcher was recovered immediately after the incident.

Director Lucious ordered no disciplinary action against Taiga.

Instead, Security procedures surrounding medical equipment have been reviewed to prevent future unauthorized access.

Psychological Services emphasized that Taiga's actions arose from desperation rather than hostility.

Even so, they concluded that future encounters between the siblings should be carefully managed if possible.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral found Taiga sitting alone in the courtyard long after everyone else had left.

The tranquilizer darts still lay where they had fallen.

Astral quietly picked them up.

Taiga whispered without looking up,

> "...I wasn't trying to hurt him."

Astral nodded.

> "...I know."

---

### Director Lucious

After reviewing the footage, Director Lucious paused at the moment Mart handed the darts back.

He remained silent for a long time before closing the recording.

---

### Neko

Neko stared at the recovered launcher.

Then at the security footage.

> "...She wasn't chasing him anymore."

He sighed.

> "...She was trying to stop time."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Desperation..."

She folded the incident report closed.

> "...Can make people believe that holding on and trapping someone are the same thing."

---

### Bao

Bao visited Taiga later that evening.

She didn't bring food first.

She simply sat beside her.

Only after a long silence did she quietly slide a warm bowl across the table.

---

### Frost

Frost looked over the courtyard where the darts had fallen.

> "...The body..."

She looked toward the empty gate.

> "...Was never what needed healing."

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star heard about the incident but never asked Taiga why.

She only hugged her.

Taiga cried harder than she had in weeks.

---

### Nightshade Dawnbringer

Nightshade watched the recording once.

She paused on Mart's final expression.

There was no fear.

No anger.

Only exhaustion.

Her report ended with one line.

> "Neither of them knows how to let the other suffer less."

---

### Leo

Leo quietly collected the last tranquilizer dart from the courtyard after everyone had gone inside.

He turned it over once in his hand.

Then dropped it into the evidence bag.

> "...This was never going to bring him home."

---

### **Closing Observation**

When the courtyard was cleaned that evening, one small detail remained.

The footprints.

Mart's footprints continued toward the residential wing before turning away.

Taiga's stopped exactly where he had returned the darts.

The two trails had crossed.

For a single moment.

Then separated once again.
