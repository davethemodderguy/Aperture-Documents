## **SUMMARY**

During a routine cleaning session in the Residential Armory, Taiga Sunbringer accidentally discovered that the paired revolvers **STAR** and **MOON** possess an undocumented modular transformation system.

When combined in a precise configuration, the two revolvers separate and reconfigure into two distinct 12-gauge Nekonium magnum shotguns.

The transformation was not present in the original design documentation.

When questioned, Neko admitted he had intentionally hidden the feature as a "surprise," expecting it would eventually be discovered naturally.

His estimate was "a few months."

Taiga found it in three days.

---

# **DISCOVERY**

While comparing both revolvers side by side, Taiga absentmindedly aligned them muzzle-to-muzzle before rotating them in opposite directions.

Several internal locking mechanisms activated simultaneously.

Both revolvers emitted a soft mechanical click.

The weapons separated into dozens of moving components.

White and azure plates unfolded.

Frames elongated.

New barrels extended into place.

Within seconds...

The two handguns had become two full-sized combat shotguns.

Taiga stared.

Mart stared.

Neko, who had just walked into the room, quietly sighed.

> "...Well..."

> "...Guess you found it."

---

# **TACTICAL OBSERVATION**

Testing quickly revealed the intended combat doctrine.

Taiga naturally advanced first.

Solar delivered powerful, deliberate shots while she moved between cover.

Mart followed slightly behind.

His riot shield absorbed incoming fire as Lunar delivered rapid semi-automatic support from behind the shield.

Observers noted that the two weapons appeared designed less as individual firearms and more as two halves of a coordinated system.

Director Lucious summarized the exercise simply:

> "One breaks the line."

He pointed toward Taiga.

> "One holds it."

He looked toward Mart.

---

# **RESET PROCEDURE**

To return **Solar** and **Lunar** to their original revolver forms:

1. Place **Lunar** directly beneath **Solar**.
    
2. Align both receivers.
    
3. Engage the synchronization latches.
    

The transformation completes automatically within approximately three seconds.

Both weapons then revert to:

- **STAR** (Taiga)
    
- **MOON** (Mart)
    

The process is fully reversible.
