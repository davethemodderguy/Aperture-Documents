# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-163**

### **FAMILY WELFARE REPORT — RECONCILIATION OF THE SUNBRINGER SIBLINGS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – RESIDENT WELFARE

---

## **SUMMARY**

After several weeks of voluntarily keeping their distance, Taiga Sunbringer and Mart Sunbringer have formally reconciled.

The reconciliation was not the result of counseling, mediation, or staff intervention.

It happened naturally.

Neither sibling could bear the distance any longer.

---

## **OBSERVED INCIDENT**

**Location:** Residential Courtyard

Mart had been quietly reading beneath one of the courtyard trees.

Taiga approached slowly.

Unlike previous encounters...

Neither of them walked away.

Taiga stopped a few meters away.

For nearly a minute...

Neither spoke.

Finally, Taiga broke the silence.

> **Taiga:** "...Can I sit here?"

Mart looked at the empty space beside him.

He nodded once.

> "...Okay."

She sat.

Silence returned.

Not uncomfortable.

Just quiet.

After several minutes, Taiga spoke again.

> "...I miss my little brother."

Mart lowered his head.

His hands tightened around the book he was holding.

Very quietly, he answered,

> "...I miss my big sister too."

Taiga froze.

She slowly looked at him.

He had said it.

Not "Taiga."

Not by accident.

Not because someone reminded him.

**"My big sister."**

Tears immediately welled in her eyes.

> **Taiga:** "...You called me Big Sis..."

Mart's voice trembled.

> "...Because you are."

> "...You always were."

> "...I'm sorry."

Taiga shook her head before he could continue.

She wrapped her arms around him.

This time...

Mart hugged her back immediately.

He held on tightly.

Neither sibling tried to hide their tears.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL NOTE**

Following the reunion, Mart explained the incident documented in **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-161** during a counseling session.

He quietly stated:

> "...I never stopped thinking of her as my big sister."

A pause.

> "...I just woke up inside one of my nightmares."

Another pause.

> "...I was terrified when I realized what I'd said."

Psychological Services concluded that the previous regression was a consequence of acute nightmare-related disorientation rather than a genuine change in Mart's feelings toward Taiga.

---

## **FAMILY RESPONSE**

### Astral Sunbringer

When informed that the siblings had reconciled, Astral simply smiled.

Then cried.

She hugged both of them at the same time.

> "...No more running away from each other."

Mart nodded.

Taiga nodded.

Neither objected to being hugged.

No one believed her.

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star had apparently been waiting for this day.

The moment she heard the news, she rushed into the courtyard carrying a camera.

> "...Nobody move!"

Before either sibling could react—

**Click.**

Another photograph.

This one captured Mart laughing while Taiga unsuccessfully tried to hide her tears.

The picture was later framed.

Unlike the old photograph...

Neither half would ever be torn.

---

### Nightshade Dawnbringer

Nightshade quietly observed the siblings from a distance.

After a long silence, she remarked:

> "...Sometimes surviving isn't the hardest part."

She watched them continue talking.

> "...Forgiving yourself is."

---

## **Closing Observation**

The following morning, Mart woke from another nightmare.

His breathing was uneven.

His heart raced.

For a brief moment...

He wasn't sure where he was.

A soft knock came from his bedroom door.

> **"...Mart?"**

He recognized the voice immediately.

Without hesitation, he answered.

> **"...Come in, Big Sis."**

Taiga opened the door.

She smiled.

> "...Morning."

Mart smiled back.

A tired smile.

But a real one.

> "...Morning, Big Sis."

For the first time in a long while...

The nightmare ended before the day began.
