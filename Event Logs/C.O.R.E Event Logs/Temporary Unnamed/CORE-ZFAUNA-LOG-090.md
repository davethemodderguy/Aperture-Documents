# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-090**

### **RESIDENTIAL INCIDENT — SIBLING BEHAVIOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Personnel have documented that Mart has begun displaying unmistakable sibling behavior toward Taiga.

Specifically...

He has discovered that teasing her is funny.

---

## **INCIDENT REPORT**

The incident occurred in one of the Blacksite's indoor courtyards.

Taiga was practicing her double jump and short-range air bursts, successfully crossing several gaps.

Mart watched from a railing above.

After a few minutes, he transformed into his humanoid form, folded his arms, and waited for Taiga to notice him.

"...What?"

Mart looked up toward the open skylight.

Then back at Taiga.

A tiny smile appeared.

> "...You jump..."

A pause.

> "...I fly."

Before Taiga could respond, Mart transformed back into an owl.

He launched into the air with two effortless wingbeats, circled above her head once, and landed on a high light fixture.

Looking down, he gave a single triumphant hoot.

Taiga immediately understood.

"...Did you just make fun of me because I can't fly?"

Mart bobbed his head once.

Yes.

---

## **ESCALATION**

Not willing to lose, Taiga activated her Frost Walker ability.

She created a staircase of floating ice platforms and climbed upward.

"...Got you."

Mart calmly took off again.

He flew another few meters higher.

"..."

Taiga sighed.

"...I walked into that one."

Mart gave another cheerful hoot.

---

## **THE FINAL WORD**

Later that evening, Taiga found Mart perched on her shoulder again.

She narrowed her eyes.

"...You know..."

"...I could just carry you everywhere."

Mart transformed briefly.

He smiled innocently.

> "...You already do."

Before Taiga could think of a comeback...

He transformed back into an owl.

Curled up comfortably on her shoulder.

And promptly fell asleep.

Taiga sighed.

"...I can't even stay mad at you."

Mart gave one sleepy little hoot.

It sounded suspiciously like laughter.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

- Mart has begun engaging in harmless sibling teasing.
    
- Primary target: **Taiga.**
    
- Favorite joke: **"You can't fly."**
    
- Taiga's response: Mock annoyance followed by immediate forgiveness.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

The next morning, Taiga walked through the residential hallway.

Mart was perched on her shoulder as usual.

As they passed a large window, Mart glanced outside at the open sky.

He spread one wing dramatically.

Then looked at Taiga.

Taiga didn't even wait for him to start.

"...Yes, Mart."

"...I know."

"...You can fly."

Mart gave a very satisfied hoot.

Mission accomplished.
