# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-099**

### **BIOLOGICAL OBSERVATION — MART SUNBRINGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – MEDICAL / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following routine observation, Medical has documented additional biological characteristics of Mart Sunbringer in both of his known forms.

---

# **OWL FORM**

Like ordinary owls, Mart naturally sheds feathers.

The process is completely harmless.

Small white feathers can frequently be found throughout the residential wing, particularly in locations where Mart enjoys resting.

Common locations include:

- Taiga's shoulder.
    
- The back of Leo's couch.
    
- Window ledges.
    
- Bookshelves.
    
- Astral's chair.
    
- Director Lucious's office windowsill.
    

Housekeeping has jokingly requested that Mart "please molt somewhere convenient."

Mart politely declined.

---

## **FEATHER REGENERATION**

Research has also confirmed that Mart's feather regrowth is unusually rapid.

Lost feathers are replaced within a remarkably short period of time, ensuring his plumage remains healthy and capable of sustained flight.

Medical has found no signs of discomfort associated with the process.

When asked if shedding bothered him, Mart simply replied,

> "...It itches..."

A pause.

> "...Sometimes."

---

# **HUMAN FORM**

Mart's humanoid appearance remains notably ordinary.

Unlike Taiga, Neko, or several other anomalous residents, Mart displays very few outwardly unusual physical characteristics.

Documented appearance:

- White hair.
    
- Bright blue eyes.
    
- Fair complexion.
    
- Small stature.
    
- Soft facial features.
    

He possesses no visible animal ears, tail, claws, fangs, or other persistent physical traits while human.

Personnel have repeatedly described him as looking like "a perfectly normal little boy."

Research notes that this appearance likely helps Mart blend in and contributes to his quiet, unassuming nature.

---

Holding one of Mart's freshly shed feathers.

> "...I keep some."

---

### Astral Sunbringer

> "...He used to molt every spring."

She looked around the room.

> "...Now he molts whenever he feels like making housekeeping work harder."

---

### Director Lucious

A white feather drifted onto a report he was reading.

He picked it up.

Looked toward the open office window.

Mart was perched outside.

Director Lucious quietly placed the feather in a small glass jar already containing several others.

He returned to work without another word.

---

> "...Sorry."

> "...It's fine."

> "...How do you still have feathers?"

> "...Growing."

---

### Leo

Neither seemed bothered.

### **Closing Observation**

One afternoon, Mart sat in the library in his human form, quietly reading beside Taiga.

A white feather drifted lazily from above.

It landed directly on the page of his book.

Mart looked up.

Confused.

Then reached up and scratched the back of his head.

Another feather floated down.

Taiga giggled.

> "...You're shedding while human again."

Mart blinked.

He looked at the feather in his hand.

Then at Taiga.

> "...Oops."

The two quietly spent the next few minutes picking feathers out of his hair.

No one was quite sure how they got there.

Mart certainly wasn't complaining.
