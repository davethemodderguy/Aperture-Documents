# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-067**

### **WELFARE LOG — VISIT TO WHITEFALL LAKE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – MEDICAL / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Medical Services has noted that, despite recent improvements in her physical condition, Taiga continues to experience persistent psychological distress.

While she has become more comfortable around her roommates and the Blacksite, recurring PTSD symptoms, nightmares, anxiety, and hypervigilance remain ongoing concerns.

To help clear her thoughts, Taiga requested permission to spend several hours at **Whitefall Lake**.

The request was approved.

---

## **WHITEFALL LAKE**

Whitefall Lake was quiet that day.

Snow drifted gently across the frozen shoreline.

Taiga walked slowly along the edge of the water, occasionally stopping to watch the snowflakes settle on the ice.

For the first time in several days...

Her breathing began to steady.

Then she noticed voices.

---

## **UNEXPECTED MEETING**

Waiting near the lakeside were six familiar residents of Whitefall Lake.

- Cherry
    
- Cheer
    
- Sky
    
- Aurora
    
- Niko
    
- Shu
    

Taiga hesitated.

"...Um..."

"...Hello?"

Cherry smiled first.

> "Hi there."

Cheer waved enthusiastically.

> "You're Taiga, right?"

Taiga blinked.

"...You know my name?"

Shu laughed softly.

> "Word travels."

---

## **THE CONFUSION**

The conversation continued pleasantly for several minutes before Taiga asked a simple question.

"...Wait..."

"...How do all of you know Neko?"

Everyone looked at one another.

Cherry tilted her head.

> "...He didn't tell you?"

Taiga looked confused.

"...Tell me what?"

A brief silence followed.

Niko slowly turned toward Shu.

Shu quietly covered his face with one hand.

Cherry sighed with an amused smile.

> "...He forgot again..."

---

## **BACK AT THE BLACKSITE**

Several hours later, Taiga returned home.

She found Neko in the workshop repairing one of his inventions.

She stood quietly in the doorway.

"Neko."

He looked up.

"...Yeah?"

"...I met Cherry."

"...Cheer."

"...Sky."

"...Aurora."

"...Niko."

"...And Shu."

Neko froze.

"..."

"...Oh."

A long pause.

"...I forgot to tell you they lived at Whitefall Lake."

Taiga crossed her arms.

"...You forgot?"

"..."

"...Yes."

Another pause.

"...Sorry."

From the hallway, Miss Neko overheard the conversation.

She couldn't help laughing.

> "...You really did forget."

Neko sighed.

> "...Apparently."

He closed the folder.

---

## **MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

Medical notes that Taiga's psychological pain remains an ongoing concern.

However, spending time in peaceful environments and interacting with trusted individuals appears to reduce her anxiety temporarily.

Visits to Whitefall Lake may continue on a voluntary basis when operationally appropriate.

---

### **Closing Observation**

As the sun began to set over Whitefall Lake, Taiga looked back toward the frozen shoreline.

She had arrived hoping only for a little quiet.

Instead, she had met new friends—and discovered that Neko had, once again, forgotten to mention something important.

When she returned to Room N-07 that evening, she looked at Neko with a playful smile.

> "...Next time..."

> "...Tell me first."

Neko rubbed the back of his neck.

> "...I'll try."

Miss Neko quietly whispered from across the room,

> "...He'll forget."

Everyone laughed.

Even Taiga.
