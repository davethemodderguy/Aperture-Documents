# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-007**

### **INTERPERSONAL OBSERVATIONS — TAIGA**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – VETERINARY / RECREATIONAL RECORD

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following the confirmation of Taiga's innate ability to communicate with animals, personnel have documented her day-to-day interactions with two of the Blacksite's most frequent animal residents:

- **Aster**
    
- **Leo**
    

The observations have produced exactly one surprise.

Leo does, in fact, have things to say.

He simply rarely stays awake long enough to say them.

---

# **I. ASTER**

Although Taiga is fully capable of speaking directly with Aster through her animal communication ability...

She usually doesn't.

There is one simple reason.

Aster enjoys writing.

Whenever Taiga asks a question, Aster almost instinctively reaches for the small marker clipped to her collar and writes her response on her whiteboard.

---

### Taiga

> "You know we can just... talk."

---

Aster thought for a moment.

Then wrote:

> **I like writing.**

---

Taiga laughed.

> "...Fair enough."

---

From then on, they continued using both methods interchangeably.

Sometimes they spoke.

Sometimes Aster wrote.

Sometimes she wrote something dramatically, only to immediately say the exact same thing aloud through Taiga's ability.

Neither seemed to mind.

---

# **II. LEO**

Communicating with Leo proved...

Different.

---

### First Attempt

Taiga gently sat beside him while he slept around the backrest of a chair.

---

> "Hello, Leo."

---

A long pause.

Nearly a full minute later...

Without opening his eyes...

Leo quietly replied.

---

> "...Hi."

---

Silence.

Another minute passed.

---

> "...Tomato?"

---

Then...

Nothing.

He had fallen asleep again.

---

Veterinary concluded that Leo is perfectly capable of conversation.

He simply prefers sleeping.

---

### Second Attempt

Taiga later asked:

> "What are you dreaming about?"

---

Leo remained asleep.

Several seconds later...

He quietly mumbled.

---

> "...Warm..."

---

"...Tomatoes..."

---

"...Aster..."

---

"...Sleep..."

---

Then resumed softly snoring.

---

Taiga smiled.

---

> "...That's about what I expected."

---

# **III. SNOW**

If Taiga has one favorite activity...

It is snow.

Not merely because she can control it.

She genuinely loves it.

Every fresh snowfall becomes an excuse to go outside.

She builds snow forts.

Rolls enormous snowballs through the courtyard.

Creates tiny snow animals that scamper across the ground before dissolving into harmless powder.

Sometimes she simply lies in freshly fallen snow and watches the clouds drift overhead.

Personnel have repeatedly observed Taiga stepping outside solely because "it started snowing."

---

# **IV. FROST**

It did not take long for Frost to become Taiga's regular companion during these outings.

Although their abilities differ, the two enjoy combining them.

Taiga shapes drifting snow into soft, flowing forms.

Frost reinforces them with delicate layers of ice.

Together they have created:

- Snow leopards.
    
- Dragons.
    
- Castles.
    
- Entire miniature villages.
    
- A life-sized sculpture of Leo asleep around a tomato.
    

Leo slept through the unveiling of his own sculpture.

---

### Frost

> "...Want to build another one tomorrow?"

---

### Taiga

> "...Only if it snows."

---

### Frost

Looking up at Iceland's winter sky.

> "...It probably will."

---

Taiga nodded enthusiastically.

---

# **VI. DIRECTOR'S NOTE**

Director Lucious observed Taiga during one particularly heavy snowfall.

She was laughing as she sent ribbons of powdery snow swirling harmlessly through the air while Aster chased them and Leo slept comfortably on Taiga's shoulder.

After watching for several moments, he added a brief note to the report.

> **"Some children play because they enjoy it."**

> **"Taiga plays because, for the first time in a long while, she finally can."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Taiga**

- Continues to freely converse with animals.
    
- Usually communicates with Aster through her whiteboard out of respect for Aster's preferred way of expressing herself.
    
- Can converse with Leo, though conversations are typically very short due to his habit of falling asleep mid-sentence.
    
- Frequently spends her free time outdoors playing in Iceland's snow and practicing her snow manipulation alongside Frost.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late one afternoon, security cameras captured Taiga lying on her back in freshly fallen snow, slowly moving her hands to guide thousands of snowflakes into shimmering spirals overhead.

Aster sat beside her, quietly drawing little paw prints in the snow.

Leo slept around Taiga's neck, completely undisturbed.

For several peaceful minutes, the only sounds outside the Blacksite were laughter...

And the gentle whisper of falling snow.
