# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-034**

### **ABILITY AWAKENING — ENHANCED STRENGTH & MORPHOLOGICAL CHANGE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – N.O.S.T. / MEDICAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Over the past several weeks, Taiga has continued to exhibit gradual physiological changes consistent with her ongoing maturation.

Two developments have now been formally documented:

- Manifestation of extraordinary physical strength.
    
- Apparent inability to return to her original snow leopard form.
    

Neither development appears to be causing her physical distress, though the second has become a subject of continued observation.

---

# **I. ENHANCED PHYSICAL STRENGTH**

The first indication came during a routine maintenance day.

Neko and several engineers were attempting to reposition a large Nekonium test chamber for calibration.

The chamber weighed several tonnes and normally required powered lifting equipment.

Seeing them struggle, Taiga innocently asked,

> "...Do you need help?"

Before anyone could answer, she placed both hands against the side of the chamber.

With a gentle push...

The entire assembly rolled several meters across the floor.

Everyone stopped.

The room fell completely silent.

Taiga looked around.

> "...Was... that too much?"

---

## **Controlled Testing**

Subsequent strength evaluations confirmed that Taiga's physical capabilities had increased dramatically.

Observed feats include:

- Lifting reinforced steel barriers that normally require machinery.
    
- Supporting heavy equipment without visible strain.
    
- Pulling disabled transport vehicles during maintenance exercises.
    
- Catching falling structural beams before they reached personnel.
    
- Opening emergency blast doors manually when their hydraulic systems were offline.
    

Despite this increase in strength, Taiga continues to display exceptionally fine motor control, allowing her to handle delicate objects—such as Leo—without difficulty.

Medical attributes this to instinctive regulation rather than conscious effort.

---

# **II. MORPHOLOGICAL CHANGE**

Following the discovery of her humanoid form, Taiga had occasionally demonstrated the ability to return to the appearance of an ordinary snow leopard cub.

Recently, however, repeated attempts have failed.

Medical observed several transformation attempts under supervision.

Each ended the same way.

A brief shimmer of anomalous energy.

Then...

Nothing.

Taiga remained in her humanoid leopard form.

She frowned.

> "...It used to work."

Further testing produced identical results.

At present, there is no indication that the ability has been suppressed externally or intentionally removed.

It simply no longer functions.

---

# **III. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

N.O.S.T. researchers currently believe Taiga's body has entered a more stable developmental stage.

One working theory suggests that her humanoid form has become her primary biological state, replacing the earlier leopard form.

This hypothesis remains unconfirmed.

Importantly, Taiga reports no discomfort, pain, or instability associated with the change.

---

# **IV. PERSONAL RESPONSE**

Although outwardly calm, Taiga admitted she misses her original form.

> "...It felt familiar."

She gently held the end of her scarf.

> "...But..."

She smiled a little.

> "...I'm still me."

Aster immediately wrote on her whiteboard.

> **Exactly.**

Taiga smiled wider.

---

### **Closing Observation**

That evening, Taiga stood before the full-length mirror in Room N-07.

For several moments, she tried once more to return to the form she had first arrived in.

Nothing happened.

She sighed softly.

Aster walked over and gently took her hand.

On her whiteboard, she wrote:

> **Your form changed.**

Then, after a moment, she added another line.

> **Your home didn't.**

Taiga looked around the room.

Neko was tinkering with a gadget.

Miss Neko was reading.

Bao and Frost were talking quietly.

Star was trying to convince Nightshade to play another board game.

Leo slept on the back of the sofa.

Mart perched contentedly by the window.

Taiga smiled.

She couldn't become the little snow leopard cub she once was.

But surrounded by the people who had become her family, she realized she didn't have to.
