# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-151**

### **POST-ENGAGEMENT — ACCOUNTING DEPARTMENT RESPONSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – INTERNAL / HUMOROUS INCIDENT LOG

---

## **SUMMARY**

Approximately one hour after the successful defense of APRT-70, the majority of Blacksite personnel gathered outside the cafeteria to celebrate with ice cream following Frost's suggestion.

The Accounting Department did not attend immediately.

They were occupied with something considerably more frightening.

The invoice.

---

## **10:32 — ACCOUNTING OFFICE**

The Accounting Department received the preliminary after-action reports.

Within minutes, their desks disappeared beneath paperwork.

The first report read:

> **Ammunition Expenditure**

The second:

> **Infrastructure Damage**

The third:

> **Property Replacement**

Then came another.

And another.

And another.

One accountant slowly removed his glasses.

He looked toward the ceiling.

> "...How..."

He flipped another page.

> "...Does someone..."

Another page.

> "...Spend this much ammunition..."

Final page.

> "...In an hour?"

No one answered.

## **SPECIAL CLAIMS**

One repair request simply read:

> **"Mountain."**

Accounting rejected it immediately.

Five minutes later...

Miss Neko personally clarified.

The request was approved.

Reluctantly.

---

## **OUTSIDE THE ACCOUNTING OFFICE**

Meanwhile...

Outside in the courtyard...

Reality looked very different.

Perimeter Security had finally finished their shifts.

Several officers had gathered around Taiga and Mart.

Someone challenged Taiga to a target-throwing contest using empty ice cream cups.

She accepted immediately.

Mart quietly volunteered to retrieve the missed throws using his wings.

A Perimeter Security sniper jokingly asked,

> "...Can your shield survive a snowball?"

Mart looked at the shield.

Then at the sniper.

> "...Probably."

Thirty seconds later...

The entire security team was engaged in an increasingly competitive snowball fight.

Mart's shield proved exceptionally effective.

Taiga responded by repeatedly Air Jumping over it.

One officer laughed.

> "...That's unfair!"

Taiga grinned.

> "...Skill issue."

---

## **ACCOUNTING (CONTINUED)**

Back inside...

Another invoice arrived.

It contained exactly two words.

> **Ice Cream**

The senior accountant blinked.

> "...Who authorized this?"

Director Lucious' signature occupied the bottom of the page.

Without comment.

The accountant quietly stamped it:

> **APPROVED**

When informed of the repair estimates, Director Lucious simply asked one question.

Receiving an affirmative answer, he signed every expense authorization without hesitation.

---

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral stopped by Accounting carrying a tray of ice cream.

She quietly left it on the break table.

No one noticed until several minutes later.

One exhausted accountant smiled for the first time all morning.

---

### Taiga Sunbringer

Taiga wandered into Accounting looking for Mart.

She glanced at the towering stack of invoices.

> "...That looks boring."

She immediately wandered back out.

---

### Mart Sunbringer

Mart returned a few minutes later.

He noticed the exhausted staff.

Without saying much, he silently helped carry several heavy archive boxes to storage.

When he finished, he simply smiled politely and left.

One accountant later remarked,

> "...He's a good kid."

---

### Frost

Frost quietly delivered ice cream to the last accountant still working.

She placed it beside his keyboard.

> "...Don't let it melt."

Then left before he could thank her.

---

### Neko

Neko peeked into the office.

Saw the mountain of paperwork.

Immediately turned around.

> "...Nope."

---

### Miss Neko

Miss Neko politely offered to explain why Bliss of Whitefall had technically only fired one shot.

Accounting unanimously decided they didn't need any additional details.

---

### Leo

Leo walked past the open office door.

He looked inside.

Then outside toward the laughter coming from the courtyard.

He smiled.

> "...Every organization has heroes."

A pause.

> "...Some just use calculators."

## **CLOSING OBSERVATION**

Security cameras later captured two scenes at exactly the same time.

Outside...

Perimeter Security officers laughed as Taiga unsuccessfully tried to convince everyone that gliding made her harder to hit with snowballs.

Mart stood nearby, shield in one hand, laughing as several officers failed to flank him.

Inside...

The Accounting Department silently calculated the replacement cost of one mountain, three guard towers, several thousand rounds of ammunition, and a vending machine.

One junior accountant finally looked up from the paperwork and asked the room:

> "...Can we classify ice cream as post-combat mental health support?"

The senior accountant didn't even look away from the calculator.

He simply stamped the form.

> **APPROVED.**

No one argued.
