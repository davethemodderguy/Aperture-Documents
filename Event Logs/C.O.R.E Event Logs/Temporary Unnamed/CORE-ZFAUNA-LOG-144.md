# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-144**

### **ARMAMENT EVALUATION — "STAR", "MOON", AND DEMONSTRATION OF SPECIALIZED WEAPON SYSTEMS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – ARMORY / AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following their successful firearms certification, Neko presented Taiga and Mart Sunbringer with two custom-built sidearms.

The weapons were designed as a matched pair, reflecting both siblings' complementary combat styles.

During the same evaluation, Miss Neko and Neko demonstrated their own personal weapon systems inside an isolated region of **Z-556 — STARLANDS**, selected due to the destructive potential involved.

---

# **SUNBRINGER SIDEARMS**

---

## **Designation**

### **STAR**

**Assigned User:** Taiga Sunbringer

Color Scheme:

- White ceramic frame
    
- Gold accents
    
- Luminous white highlights
    

Caliber:  
**12.7×665mm Experimental**

Platform:  
Re-engineered high-caliber revolver inspired by the RSh-12 platform.

Research Note:

The weapon has been substantially redesigned for anomalous operation and should not be considered mechanically equivalent to any real firearm.

Characteristics:

- Extremely high stopping power
    
- Significantly reduced felt recoil through internal anomalous stabilization
    
- Six-round cylinder
    
- Optimized for rapid target engagement
    

---

## **Designation**

### **MOON**

**Assigned User:** Mart Sunbringer

Color Scheme:

- Azure frame
    
- Silver detailing
    
- Pale blue luminous accents
    

Mechanically identical to **STAR**, differing only in finish and personalized grip dimensions.

Mart additionally received a custom **Nekonium Riot Shield**.

Despite its considerable size, Mart carries it effortlessly using either his physical strength or his wings.

The shield demonstrates:

- High ballistic resistance
    
- Exceptional durability
    
- Compatibility with aerial maneuvering
    

---

## **OBSERVATION**

Neko presented both revolvers simultaneously.

He placed them side by side.

One white.

One azure.

Without being told, Taiga quietly smiled.

Mart looked at the pair for several seconds before speaking.

> "...They look like us."

Neko nodded.

> "...Exactly."

Taiga carefully picked up **Star**.

Mart lifted **Moon**.

The two revolvers had clearly been designed together.

Neither appeared complete without the other.

# **LIVE FIRE EVALUATION**

Testing took place within a secured valley inside **Starlands**.

Reason for relocation:

Projected destructive capability exceeded acceptable limits for Blacksite testing.

---

## **TAIGA SUNBRINGER**

Taiga fired first.

She expected substantial recoil.

Instead...

The revolver barely moved.

Taiga looked at Neko.

Then at the revolver.

Then back at Neko.

> "...That's cheating."

Neko grinned.

> "...Engineering."

---

## **MART SUNBRINGER**

Mart calmly equipped his riot shield.

The enormous shield rested comfortably against his back.

Taiga stared.

> "...How is that not heavy?"

Mart answered honestly.

> "...It is."

A pause.

> "...Just not to me."

He fired **Moon**.

Every shot landed exactly where intended.

Predictably.

# **SPECIAL WEAPON DEMONSTRATION**

## **MISS NEKO**

### **BLISS OF WHITEFALL**

Classification:

**Handheld Anti-Material Howitzer**

Secondary Designation:

**Handheld Terraformer**

Construction:

Exact specifications remain classified.

Visible characteristics include:

- Massive reinforced barrel assembly.
    
- Multi-stage energy chambers.
    
- Integrated anomalous focusing arrays.
    

Miss Neko calmly shouldered the weapon.

She pulled the trigger.

There was no conventional explosion.

Instead...

The landscape itself changed.

The impact excavated an enormous crater hundreds of meters across.

The surrounding terrain was permanently reshaped.

Research teams immediately suspended further testing pending geological assessment.

Taiga stared silently.

Then whispered,

> "...That's not a gun."

Miss Neko politely corrected her.

> "...Technically..."

A brief pause.

> "...It is."

---

## **NEKO**

### **NEKO'S CLAWS**

Configuration One:

**Plasma Scythe**

The weapon manifested as a long, elegant scythe composed almost entirely of stabilized white plasma.

Neko performed several fluid movements.

Each swing left incandescent arcs hanging briefly in the air.

He then pressed a release mechanism.

The scythe folded inward.

Segments rotated and locked.

Within seconds...

The weapon had transformed into its second configuration.

---

### **Configuration Two**

**Plasma Assault Rifle**

Compact.

Angular.

Extremely stable.

Instead of conventional ammunition, the rifle launched tightly focused plasma bolts at extraordinary velocity.

Testing demonstrated:

- Minimal recoil.
    
- Exceptional sustained accuracy.
    
- Significant thermal effects on impact.
    

Entire rock formations were reduced to glowing slag within seconds.

Taiga looked between the rifle...

Then the scythe.

> "...You turned a farming tool into a rifle."

Neko tilted his head.

> "...Other way around."
