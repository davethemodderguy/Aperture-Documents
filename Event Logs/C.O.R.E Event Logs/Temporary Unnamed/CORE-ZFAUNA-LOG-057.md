# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-057**

### **ABILITY AWAKENING — DORSAL RUNE MANIFESTATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – MEDICAL / ANOMALOUS

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

On **[REDACTED]**, Taiga underwent another stage of her ongoing anomalous development.

Unlike previous physical changes, this manifestation was accompanied by severe pain.

Medical personnel describe the event as both a biological transformation and an acute traumatic episode.

---

## **INCIDENT**

The event began during the afternoon while Taiga was helping Bao prepare lunch.

Without warning, Taiga suddenly dropped the vegetables she was carrying.

She instinctively reached for her back.

Several seconds later she collapsed to her knees.

Witnesses reported that she appeared to be in extreme pain.

When asked what was happening, Taiga struggled to answer between breaths.

> "...It..."

She cried out.

> "...It hurts..."

Star immediately called Medical.

---

## **DESCRIPTION OF PAIN**

During treatment, Taiga described the sensation as:

> "...Like..."

She hesitated before continuing.

> "...Someone is drawing on my back..."

Another pause.

> "...With a scalpel."

Medical notes that no external wounds corresponding to the pain were initially visible.

Despite this, Taiga's heart rate, breathing, and stress response indicated genuine severe pain.

---

## **PTSD RESPONSE**

As the pain intensified, Taiga's existing PTSD was unintentionally triggered.

Personnel observed:

- Hyperventilation.
    
- Trembling.
    
- Crying.
    
- Difficulty recognizing people around her.
    
- Attempts to instinctively retreat into confined spaces.
    

Although her roommates remained nearby throughout the incident, Taiga required significant reassurance before recognizing she was safe.

The episode lasted approximately forty-three minutes.

---

## **MANIFESTATION**

When the pain finally subsided, Medical discovered the source.

Across the center of Taiga's back was an intricate glowing rune.

The symbol emitted a faint azure light and was cool to the touch.

No signs of burns, cuts, or scar tissue were present.

The rune has remained visible since its appearance.

Its exact function was initially unknown.

---

## **ABILITY DEVELOPMENT**

Within twenty-four hours of the manifestation, Taiga unconsciously demonstrated several new cryogenic abilities.

### Frost Walker

Taiga can traverse the surface of water.

As she walks, water immediately beneath each foot freezes into solid ice before melting naturally after she has passed.

The effect requires no visible concentration.

---

### Ice Constructs

Taiga can create a variety of solid ice structures, including but not limited to:

- Walls.
    
- Platforms.
    
- Bridges.
    
- Shields.
    
- Pillars.
    
- Steps.
    
- Temporary barriers.
    

The constructs possess considerable structural strength before gradually melting when no longer maintained.

Testing suggests they are composed of anomalously dense ice significantly stronger than ordinary frozen water.

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical believes the rune functions as a biological focus for Taiga's cryogenic abilities.

However, the manifestation process itself proved exceptionally painful.

Although no permanent physical damage was identified, the experience temporarily aggravated Taiga's existing psychological trauma.

Medical recommends avoiding unnecessary discussion of the incident until Taiga has fully recovered emotionally.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga has made a full physical recovery from the manifestation event.

The azure rune remains permanently visible and continues to emit a faint glow.

She has begun safely incorporating Frost Walker and her ice constructs into training under supervision.

Psychologically, however, Medical notes that the pain associated with the manifestation left a temporary increase in anxiety and nightmares.

Continued observation has been recommended.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Several evenings after the incident, Taiga stood quietly at the edge of Whitefall Lake during a supervised visit.

She hesitated.

Then cautiously took one step forward.

A thin layer of brilliant ice formed beneath her foot.

Then another.

And another.

Soon she was standing peacefully atop the frozen path she had unknowingly created.

She looked back toward the shoreline where her roommates were watching.

A small smile appeared.

Not because of the new power.

But because this time...

The only thing she felt beneath her feet was cold.
