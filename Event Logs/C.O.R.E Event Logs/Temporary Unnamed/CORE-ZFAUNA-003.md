# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-003**

### **PERMANENT RESIDENCY APPROVAL — "TAIGA"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – VETERINARY / RESIDENT RECORD

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following a unanimous recommendation from Veterinary Services, N.O.S.T., Security, and Director Lucious, **CORE-ZFAUNA-001** has officially concluded her temporary custody period.

After careful review, it was determined that releasing the juvenile snow leopard back into the wild would present an unacceptable risk to her long-term survival.

The Director approved permanent residency.

She has also been given an official name.

**Taiga.**

---

# **I. OFFICIAL DESIGNATION**

**Name:** Taiga

**Species:** Snow Leopard (_Panthera uncia_)

**Sex:** Female

**Status:** Permanent Blacksite Resident

**Classification:** Non-anomalous Protected Wildlife

---

# **II. WHY SHE STAYED**

Although physically recovered, Taiga had become deeply integrated into daily life within the Blacksite.

She had formed strong social bonds with both personnel and resident entities.

Additionally, investigations confirmed that the hunting group responsible for killing her mother had operated throughout the surrounding region.

Returning her to the wild was deemed both unsafe and unnecessary.

Director Lucious summarized the decision with a single sentence.

> **"She found a home before we found one for her."**

---

# **III. DAILY LIFE**

Taiga no longer spends most of her time inside the veterinary habitat.

Instead, she has become a familiar sight throughout the Blacksite.

She wanders freely through approved areas, often appearing wherever her friends happen to be.

Personnel have grown accustomed to stepping around a sleeping snow leopard in hallways or finding large paw prints leading through freshly fallen snow outside.

Security no longer questions her presence.

They simply wave.

---

# **IV. THE USUAL GROUP**

Over time, Taiga settled into a routine.

If someone was looking for her, there was a good chance they would also find:

- **Aster**
    
- **Leo**
    
- **Neko**
    
- **Miss Neko**
    

The five became an oddly recognizable group.

Neko usually walked in front.

Miss Neko beside him.

Aster wandered wherever she pleased.

Leo slept on whichever companion happened to be the most comfortable.

Taiga calmly followed behind—or occasionally walked ahead, pretending she wasn't being followed by everyone else.

Engineers jokingly referred to them as:

> **"The Neighborhood Patrol."**

---

# **V. FRIENDSHIPS**

### **Aster**

Aster remains Taiga's closest companion.

The two spend hours together every day.

If Aster teleports away, Taiga patiently waits for her return.

When Aster reappears...

Taiga greets her with a gentle head bump every single time.

---

### **Leo**

Leo has fully accepted Taiga as one of his preferred sleeping places.

Most commonly, he sleeps:

- Between her ears.
    
- Around her tail.
    
- Across her shoulders.
    
- Curled against her neck.
    

Taiga has never once complained.

---

### **Neko**

Neko has somehow convinced Taiga that snowball fights are an acceptable recreational activity.

Veterinary officially refuses to comment.

---

### **Miss Neko**

Whenever Taiga finishes playing outside, Miss Neko is usually the one responsible for brushing snow out of her thick winter coat.

Taiga has become so accustomed to this routine that she now sits down automatically whenever Miss Neko appears carrying a grooming brush.

### **Closing Observation**

Early one morning, Security cameras overlooking Whitefall Lake recorded a familiar scene.

Fresh snow had fallen overnight.

Neko walked quietly along the frozen path.

Miss Neko sat comfortably on his shoulder.

Aster trotted beside them in her usual white coat.

Leo slept peacefully around Aster's neck.

Behind them padded Taiga, leaving large paw prints in the untouched snow.

A young security officer watched the monitor and smiled.

> "...You know..."

> "...They really do look like a family."

No one in the control room disagreed.
