# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-012**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION — TAIGA**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – MEDICAL / INTERNAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Medical and Residential staff have documented a recurring pattern involving Taiga's sleep.

Although she remains cheerful throughout the day, nighttime observations indicate she experiences frequent nightmares.

Notably, Taiga has made consistent efforts to ensure that no one notices.

---

# **I. NIGHTTIME OBSERVATIONS**

The first signs were subtle.

Some mornings, Taiga appeared more tired than usual despite going to bed early.

Other nights, security cameras recorded her quietly sitting awake beside the heater after everyone else had fallen asleep.

When questioned, she always gave the same answer.

> "I'm okay."

Medical initially accepted the explanation.

However, repeated observations suggested otherwise.

---

# **II. THE NIGHTMARES**

One evening, Miss Neko awoke to the sound of quiet whimpering.

Looking toward the heater, she saw Taiga still asleep beside Aster.

Her breathing had become uneven.

Her ears were pressed flat against her head.

Small tears rolled down her cheeks.

She repeatedly whispered only a few words.

> "...Mom..."

> "...Run..."

> "...Please..."

Aster had already awakened.

The little white cat quietly moved closer and curled tightly against Taiga's chest.

Leo, disturbed only briefly, sleepily slithered from the coat rack and wrapped himself around Taiga's arm before falling asleep again.

A few minutes later, Taiga's breathing gradually steadied.

She never woke.

---

# **III. HIDING IT**

The following morning, Taiga behaved exactly as she always did.

She smiled.

Ate breakfast.

Played in the snow with Frost.

Laughed with Bao.

No one would have guessed anything had happened.

When Miss Neko gently asked if she had slept well, Taiga simply smiled.

> "...Yep."

Medical later determined this had become a routine.

Whenever she woke from a nightmare before anyone else, she would quietly wipe away her tears, straighten her scarf, and pretend everything was normal.

She did not want anyone worrying about her.

---

# **IV. WHY**

During a private conversation with Director Lucious, Taiga finally explained her reasoning.

She looked down at her hands before speaking.

> "...Everyone already takes care of me."

A long pause followed.

> "...I don't want to make them sad, too."

> "...So... if I just smile..."

> "...Then they don't have to worry."

Lucious remained silent for several moments before replying.

> "...Caring about you isn't a burden, Taiga."

She looked up.

He continued.

> "...You don't have to protect everyone from your own feelings."

Taiga didn't answer immediately.

She simply nodded quietly.

---

# **V. HER FRIENDS NOTICE**

Even though Taiga tried to hide it...

The people closest to her gradually noticed.

### Neko

Began leaving a warm cup of hot chocolate on the kitchen table during nights when he happened to wake up.

He never mentioned why.

Some mornings, the cup was empty.

---

### Miss Neko

Started checking on Taiga before going to sleep each night, gently adjusting the white scarf if it had slipped off during the evening.

---

### Frost

Quietly made small snowflake decorations that glowed faintly in moonlight, placing one beside Taiga's bed because she thought it might make the nights feel a little less lonely.

She never mentioned who made them.

Taiga knew anyway.

---

### Bao

Without explanation, began insisting on giving Taiga a hug before bedtime.

When asked why, Bao simply shrugged.

> "...Feels right."

---

### Star

Occasionally stayed awake a little longer, reading softly until Taiga drifted into a peaceful sleep.

---

### Nightshade

Though not particularly expressive, she began leaving the hallway light outside the room on its dimmest setting whenever she was awake late.

Just enough that if Taiga woke frightened, the room wouldn't feel completely dark.

---

### Aster

Aster never asked about the nightmares.

She simply remained there.

Every night.

Curled against Taiga's side.

On one particularly difficult morning, Taiga found a message already written on the whiteboard beside the heater.

> **You don't have to hide from us.**

Taiga read it several times before quietly hugging Aster.

---

### Leo

Leo never seemed to understand nightmares.

He only understood that Taiga sometimes woke up sad.

On those nights, he instinctively curled a little tighter around her scarf.

Then went back to sleep.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga continues to experience recurring nightmares related to the loss of her mother.

Medical believes the frequency is gradually decreasing, though the memories remain distressing.

While she still instinctively tries to hide her bad nights, her friends have quietly adapted their routines—not by forcing her to talk, but by making sure she never has to face the night alone.

---

### **Closing Observation**

During a late-night security check, a camera overlooking Room N-07 captured a peaceful scene.

Snow fell silently outside the Blacksite.

Taiga stirred briefly in her sleep.

Before the nightmare could fully take hold, Aster shifted closer, resting a paw against her hand.

Leo remained asleep around her scarf.

A few moments later, Taiga's expression relaxed again.

The room returned to silence, warmed by the heater and the quiet presence of those who cared for her.
