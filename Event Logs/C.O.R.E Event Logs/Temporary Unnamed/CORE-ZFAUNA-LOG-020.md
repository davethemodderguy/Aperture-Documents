# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-020**

### **PHYSIOLOGICAL OBSERVATION — PREHENSILE TAIL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – BIOLOGICAL / FAUNA

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following her continued recovery, Medical and N.O.S.T. personnel have documented another characteristic of Taiga's physiology.

Although previously believed to serve only for balance and expression, Taiga's snow leopard tail possesses exceptional strength and dexterity.

The appendage functions almost as a third arm.

---

# **I. OBSERVATIONS**

During rehabilitation exercises, Taiga accidentally demonstrated the capability while reaching for a dropped water bottle.

Before anyone could pick it up...

Her tail quietly wrapped around it.

She lifted it from the floor and handed it back to herself without interrupting her conversation.

The rehabilitation therapist stopped writing.

Looked up.

Looked back at the clipboard.

Then quietly added a new section to the report.

Further testing revealed the tail is capable of:

- Grasping and carrying everyday objects.
    
- Opening unlocked doors and drawers.
    
- Holding bowls, books, or tools while both hands are occupied.
    
- Assisting with climbing and maintaining balance.
    
- Supporting a surprising amount of weight.
    

Motor control is remarkably precise, allowing Taiga to manipulate delicate objects without difficulty.

---

# **II. COMPARISON**

Engineering compared Taiga's tail strength with the known capabilities of Neko and Miss Neko.

After several controlled tests, the conclusion was straightforward.

> **Comparable.**

Like the siblings, Taiga can comfortably use her tail as an additional limb during daily activities.

Neko's response was immediate.

> "...Cool."

Miss Neko simply nodded.

> "...Welcome to the club."

---

# **III. DAILY LIFE**

The discovery quickly became part of Taiga's normal routine.

She was observed:

- Carrying her salad with her tail while waving to personnel.
    
- Holding Leo securely while reading.
    
- Catching a falling notebook before it hit the floor.
    
- Keeping her scarf from blowing away during strong winds.
    
- Gently helping Aster onto a chair without using her hands.
    

When asked why she hadn't mentioned the ability sooner, Taiga looked slightly embarrassed.

> "...I thought everyone could do that..."

---

# **IV. ASTER**

Aster found the new ability endlessly fascinating.

One afternoon she intentionally dropped her marker.

Before it reached the floor...

Taiga's tail caught it.

Aster blinked.

Then deliberately dropped the eraser.

The tail caught that too.

Aster looked at Taiga.

Then wrote:

> **Again.**

For the next fifteen minutes, Aster repeatedly "accidentally" dropped random objects.

Taiga caught every single one.

By the end, both were laughing.

---

# **V. LEO**

Leo immediately decided Taiga's tail made an excellent hammock.

Personnel now regularly observe the tiny banana snake sleeping coiled around the end of her tail while she continues walking completely unbothered.

Taiga claims she barely notices the extra weight.

Leo has offered no opinion.

He remains asleep.

---

# **VI. MEDICAL NOTE**

Medical staff have also noted an encouraging trend unrelated to the tail itself.

Over the past several weeks, Taiga has become noticeably more relaxed around her roommates.

She has started spending more evenings back in Room N-07.

She joins conversations more often.

Her smiles appear less forced.

While she still has difficult nights from time to time, she no longer feels the same need to disappear without telling anyone.

Recovery, Medical emphasizes, has not erased the hardships she has experienced.

Instead, she seems to be slowly rebuilding her confidence and trust in those around her.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga's prehensile tail has been confirmed to possess strength and dexterity comparable to that of Neko and Miss Neko.

Medical considers the trait a normal part of her physiology rather than an anomaly requiring containment.

More importantly, Taiga herself continues to recover—not just physically, but emotionally. While there are still challenging days, the distance she once placed between herself and those around her has begun to shrink.

---

### **Closing Observation**

One snowy evening, the residents of Room N-07 gathered by the heater.

Neko was reading.

Miss Neko was knitting.

Bao and Frost were talking quietly.

Star and Nightshade played a board game.

Aster drew little snow leopards on her whiteboard.

Taiga sat among them, Leo asleep around the end of her tail.

For the first time in a long while, no one was worried about whether she would quietly leave during the night.

She stayed exactly where she was.

Home.
