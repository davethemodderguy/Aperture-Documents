# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-104**

### **TRAINING EXERCISE — UNSCHEDULED AERIAL MOBILITY DRILL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – TRAINING / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

Despite being referred to by several witnesses as a "kidnapping," the incident was later clarified to have been an impromptu mobility training exercise initiated by Mart Sunbringer.

No injuries were sustained.

No disciplinary action was taken.

---

## **THE INCIDENT**

Taiga was walking through one of the Blacksite courtyards when she suddenly felt herself being lifted off the ground.

"...Huh?"

Looking up, she found Mart in his owl form.

Without any visible effort, he had gently gripped the back of her hoodie with his talons.

Despite Taiga's weight, Mart flew upward as if carrying nothing at all.

Personnel watched as the pair steadily climbed higher above the courtyard.

Star looked up.

> "...Is Mart... kidnapping Taiga?"

Neko glanced over.

> "...Looks like it."

Neither appeared particularly concerned.

---

## **AERIAL TRAINING**

Several dozen meters above the ground, Mart slowed to a hover.

Taiga dangled beneath him, arms folded.

"...Mart."

A soft hoot.

"...What?"

Another hoot.

Taiga narrowed her eyes.

"...You're making me practice, aren't you?"

Mart bobbed his head once.

Yes.

---

## **THE DROP**

Without warning...

Mart released his grip.

Taiga began falling.

For a split second—

Nothing.

Then—

**Boom!**

A burst of compressed air erupted beneath her feet.

She launched herself upward.

Another burst.

Then another.

Then another.

Using her newly evolved **Air Jump**, Taiga redirected herself through the air before landing lightly on the training field.

She remained standing.

Perfect landing.

Mart descended a moment later, landing beside her.

He transformed into his human form.

> "...Better."

Taiga dusted off her hoodie.

"...You could've warned me."

Mart tilted his head.

> "...You knew."

Taiga paused.

"...Fair."

## **TRAINING ASSESSMENT**

Research concluded that the exercise unintentionally demonstrated:

- Improved confidence while airborne.
    
- Rapid decision-making under pressure.
    
- Effective use of consecutive Air Jumps.
    
- Safe recovery from an unexpected fall.
    

While the outcome was positive, future training exercises are recommended to include **prior warning and consent**.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Later that afternoon, Taiga sat beside Mart on a rooftop overlooking the Blacksite.

She nudged him gently with her shoulder.

"...You know..."

"...You really scared me."

Mart looked down.

> "...Sorry."

Taiga smiled.

"...But..."

She stood up.

Leapt from the roof.

**Boom.**

**Boom.**

**Boom.**

She crossed the courtyard using a chain of graceful Air Jumps before landing perfectly below.

Looking back up, she waved.

"...I have to admit..."

"...It was good practice."

Mart smiled quietly.

Then, without a word...

He transformed into an owl.

And flew down to join his sister—this time without dropping her.
