# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-124**

### **MISSING PERSON REPORT — MART SUNBRINGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – ACTIVE SEARCH / PSYCHOLOGICAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Approximately **six hours** after the incident documented in CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-123, Mart Sunbringer remains unaccounted for.

Search teams have expanded operations to all residential sectors, rooftops, gardens, observation towers, maintenance corridors, and surrounding grounds.

No confirmed sightings have been reported.

The luminous wings manifested during his departure have not been observed again.

---

## **SUBJECT: TAIGA SUNBRINGER**

For several hours following the argument, Taiga showed little outward reaction.

Personnel initially believed she remained convinced that she had been justified.

This assessment proved incorrect.

It simply took time.

---

## **THE REALIZATION**

The realization came quietly.

No dramatic revelation.

No sudden discovery.

Just...

Silence.

Taiga walked into the Sunbringer residence expecting Mart to be reading in his usual chair.

It was empty.

She checked the library.

Nothing.

The observation deck.

Nothing.

Whitefall Lake.

Nothing.

She walked faster.

Then she started running.

By the time she reached the rooftops, her breathing had become uneven.

She called his name once.

No answer.

Again.

Nothing.

Only the wind.

---

## **THE MEMORY**

While standing alone on the rooftop, fragments of the argument replayed in her mind.

Not the beginning.

The end.

> _"Maybe you're making yourself feel unwanted."_

She remembered Mart's face.

His trembling hands.

The way he stopped speaking.

The quiet sob.

The white wings.

And then...

She understood.

Not that they had argued.

But **what she had said**.

The things she had chosen to say.

She had reached for the fears he never hid from her.

The fear of Taiga's scar.

The fear of not belonging.

The fear of being unwanted.

The fears he had trusted only her with.

And she had used them against him.

---

## **INCIDENT**

Witnesses later reported seeing Taiga standing alone on the rooftop for several minutes.

She didn't move.

She didn't cry.

She simply stared at the empty sky where Mart had disappeared.

Then, almost in a whisper...

She said,

> "...Mart...?"

No response.

Another whisper.

Even quieter.

> "...Come back..."

Still nothing.

Only then did her knees give way.

She collapsed onto the concrete.

Covering her face with both hands.

Over and over, she repeated the same sentence.

> "...I'm sorry..."

> "...I'm sorry..."

> "...I'm sorry..."

No one approached immediately.

No one knew what words could possibly help.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral found Taiga long after sunset.

Her daughter had not moved.

Astral knelt beside her.

Taiga looked up through tears.

Her voice was barely audible.

> "...I broke him..."

Astral gently held her.

She didn't deny it.

She didn't agree with it.

She simply wrapped both arms around her daughter.

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious personally authorized the expansion of the search radius.

Before leaving the command room, he quietly said,

> "...Find my son."

No one corrected him.

---

### Neko

Neko sat beside Taiga on the rooftop after Astral had taken her inside.

Neither of them spoke.

Eventually, Neko looked toward the stars.

> "...Go yell at him when we find him."

A pause.

> "...Not before."

---

### Miss Neko

Miss Neko gently covered Taiga with a blanket after she had cried herself to exhaustion.

She whispered,

> "...Regret comes after anger."

> "...That's why anger is so dangerous."

---

One for Taiga.

One for Mart.

Bao still replaced it with a fresh meal the next morning.

Waiting.

### **Closing Observation**

Later that night, Taiga quietly entered Mart's room.

Everything was exactly where he had left it.

His books.

His jacket.

A feather resting on the desk.

She picked it up with trembling hands.

Then sat on the floor beside his bed.

She remembered every time he had told her,

> "...It's okay."

Every time he had waited for her.

Every time he had carried her when she was too weak to walk.

And she remembered the last thing she had given him.

Words meant to hurt.

She clutched the feather tightly against her chest.

For the first time since the argument...

She understood that being forgiven was not the thing she wanted most.

She wanted the chance to apologize.

Whether she would ever get that chance...

No one in the Blacksite could answer.
