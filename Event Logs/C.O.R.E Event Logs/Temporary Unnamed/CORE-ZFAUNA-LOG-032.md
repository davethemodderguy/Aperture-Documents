# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-032**

### **PERSONAL RECORD — TAIL RIBBON**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL ARCHIVE

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following Taiga's full recovery, Star decided she wanted to give her a small gift.

According to Star:

> "...Nothing serious."

> "...Just something cute."

The result was a neatly tied white ribbon fastened near the end of Taiga's tail.

Attached to the center of the ribbon was a tiny silver bell.

---

# **I. THE GIFT**

Star had spent most of the previous evening quietly sewing the ribbon herself.

She intentionally chose white fabric to match Taiga's scarf and snowy fur.

The small bell was polished until it reflected light like fresh snow.

When Star finally presented it, Taiga looked at it curiously.

> "...For me?"

Star nodded enthusiastically.

> "...Yep!"

> "...Miss Neko has one."

> "...Now you can too."

Taiga smiled.

> "...Thank you."

Star carefully tied the ribbon around Taiga's tail.

The little bell gave a soft...

**Jingle.**

Everyone immediately looked over.

---

# **II. FIRST IMPRESSIONS**

Taiga slowly swished her tail.

**Jingle.**

She tilted her head.

Swished it again.

**Jingle. Jingle.**

Her ears perked up.

"...It's adorable."

Star beamed.

Mission accomplished.

---

# **III. PERSONNEL REACTIONS**

### Neko

Looked up from his workbench.

> "...Now we'll always know where Taiga is."

As if to prove him wrong, Taiga quietly walked away.

The tiny bell barely made a sound.

Neko blinked.

> "...She's learned stealth with a bell."

---

### Miss Neko

Gently examined the ribbon.

> "...Looks good on you."

She smiled.

> "...Welcome to the ribbon club."

Taiga laughed.

---

### Bao

Immediately wanted one for herself.

Everyone politely reminded her that her whale tail was significantly larger.

Bao admitted the proportions might be a problem.

---

### Frost

Lightly tapped the bell.

**Jingle.**

She smiled.

> "...Cute."

---

### Star

Watching Taiga happily swish her tail.

> "...Worth every stitch."

---

### Nightshade

Looked at the ribbon.

Then at Star.

Then at Taiga.

After a brief pause:

> "...Appropriate."

Coming from Nightshade, this was interpreted as high praise.

---

### Aster

Aster drew Taiga with an oversized ribbon on her whiteboard.

Then drew Miss Neko beside her.

Underneath, she wrote:

> **Matching.**

Taiga giggled.

---

### Leo

Leo immediately decided the ribbon was an excellent place to sleep.

Personnel later discovered the tiny banana snake wrapped around the bow itself, somehow remaining perfectly balanced while asleep.

The bell occasionally gave a faint...

**Jingle...**

Every time Leo shifted.

He never woke up.

---

### Mart

Mart perched on the back of Taiga's chair and observed the ribbon for several minutes.

According to Taiga's translation:

> "...It suits you."

She smiled.

> "...Thanks."

---

# **IV. MEDICAL NOTE**

Medical confirmed that the ribbon does not interfere with Taiga's prehensile tail or the anomalous restrainer located nearer the base of the tail.

The bell is lightweight and poses no safety concerns.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga now wears a white ribbon with a small silver bell on the end of her tail, handcrafted by Star as a gift.

The accessory has quickly become part of her everyday appearance, much like her scarf, snowflake earrings, and arm restraints.

---

### **Closing Observation**

That evening, the residents of Room N-07 gathered for dinner.

As Taiga walked into the room carrying a bowl of salad, the tiny bell on her tail gave a soft, cheerful **jingle**.

Everyone looked up.

Star smiled proudly.

Miss Neko gave an approving nod.

Leo continued sleeping on the ribbon without falling off.

Taiga gently flicked her tail once.

**Jingle.**

She couldn't help smiling.

It was a small gift.

But somehow, it already felt like it had always belonged there.
