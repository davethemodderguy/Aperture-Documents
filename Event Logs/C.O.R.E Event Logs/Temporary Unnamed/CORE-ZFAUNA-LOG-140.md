# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-140**

### **ANOMALOUS DEVELOPMENT — SUNBRINGER WINGS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – ANOMALOUS BIOLOGY / FAMILY RECORD

---

## **SUMMARY**

On ██/██/20██, Mart Sunbringer demonstrated an anomalous ability previously undocumented.

He manifested a second pair of luminous wings.

Rather than remaining attached to himself, the newly formed wings separated and became anchored to Taiga Sunbringer.

The transfer was painless.

No adverse physiological effects were observed.

The event has led to the discovery of a previously unknown Sunbringer family trait.

---

## **INCIDENT REPORT**

Training was taking place in the eastern courtyard.

Taiga was, once again, complaining.

> **Taiga:** "...You have actual wings."

She floated five centimeters above the ground in protest.

> "...I have... hovering."

Mart looked at her thoughtfully.

Then asked a simple question.

> "...Do you still want wings?"

Taiga answered immediately.

> "...YES."

Without another word, Mart stepped closer.

He rested a hand lightly against her shoulder.

The glow from his wings brightened.

A second pair of white wings slowly formed behind him.

The new pair detached as though woven from light itself.

They drifted behind Taiga.

The feathers dissolved into countless particles before reforming against her back.

Several seconds later...

Taiga stumbled forward.

Two large wings of brilliant white light unfurled behind her.

Silence filled the courtyard.

Taiga slowly turned her head.

Cross-eyed.

Trying to look at them.

> "...I HAVE WINGS."

Her excitement lasted approximately three seconds.

Then she tried to flap them.

Nothing happened.

She flapped harder.

Still nothing.

Mart quietly covered his face with one hand.

---

## **TRAINING SESSION**

Despite possessing fully manifested wings, Taiga demonstrated no instinctive understanding of their use.

Initial attempts included:

- Flapping only one wing.
    
- Attempting to air jump and flap simultaneously.
    
- Turning her shoulders instead of moving the wings.
    
- Accidentally folding herself backward.
    
- Forgetting the wings were behind her and walking into a doorway.
    

No successful flight was achieved.

After watching several increasingly creative failures, Mart finally sighed.

> "...Big sis."

Taiga looked at him hopefully.

> "...How do I make them work?"

Mart blinked.

> "...I..."

He looked back at his own wings.

Then shrugged.

> "...I just... do."

When asked how he had learned, Mart answered honestly.

> "...I've always had wings."

Only then did everyone realize why.

Mart's owl form had given him years of instinctive experience.

To him...

Using wings was as natural as walking.

To Taiga...

They were an entirely new set of limbs.

---

## **FAMILY DISCOVERY**

Following the incident, Astral quietly approached the siblings.

She looked at Taiga struggling to balance.

Then smiled apologetically.

> "...There's something I forgot to mention."

Before anyone could ask...

A pair of radiant wings unfolded from Astral's back.

They were broader than Mart's.

Elegant.

Warm in color.

The courtyard fell silent once again.

Taiga stared.

Then very slowly turned toward her mother.

> "...Mom."

Another pause.

> "...You had wings this whole time?"

Astral nodded sheepishly.

> "...Yes."

Taiga's eye twitched.

> "...YOU NEVER TOLD ME?"

Astral rubbed the back of her neck.

> "...I was afraid..."

She smiled awkwardly.

> "...You'd get jealous."

Taiga pointed dramatically between Astral and Mart.

> "...YOU WERE RIGHT."

Laughter echoed through the courtyard.

Even Mart laughed quietly.

It was the loudest anyone had heard him laugh since his return.

---

## **RESEARCH NOTE**

Anomaly Research currently believes the manifestation of luminous wings is an inherited Sunbringer characteristic.

Known confirmed users:

- **Astral Sunbringer**
    
- **Mart Sunbringer**
    
- **Taiga Sunbringer** (newly manifested)
    

Further research is ongoing to determine whether the wings represent a dormant hereditary trait awakened under specific conditions, or an ability unique to members of the Sunbringer family.

### **Closing Observation**

Near sunset, Taiga managed something she hadn't done all afternoon.

She lifted herself cleanly off the ground.

One meter.

Then two.

She hovered in place for nearly four whole seconds.

Before immediately losing balance and tumbling into Mart.

Both siblings fell into the snow.

Mart looked at the sky.

Then at his sister lying on top of him.

He smiled.

> "...Good try, Big sis."

Taiga grinned despite the faceful of snow.

> "...I'm gonna beat you one day."

Mart laughed.

> "...You have to learn how to land first."

The training continued long after the sun disappeared below the mountains.
