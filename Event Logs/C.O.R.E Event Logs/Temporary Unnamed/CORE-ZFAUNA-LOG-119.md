# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-119**

### **MEDICAL RECOVERY UPDATE — TAIGA SUNBRINGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – MEDICAL / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Medical Services has confirmed that **Taiga Sunbringer has regained the ability to return to her human form.**

Unlike Mart's recovery, however, Taiga's recovery remains incomplete.

Although the transformation has stabilized, her human body is currently **significantly weaker** than before the incident.

Medical personnel describe the condition as one of severe post-traumatic physical exhaustion.

---

## **CURRENT CONDITION**

Taiga has difficulty maintaining her energy levels throughout the day.

Symptoms include:

- General weakness.
    
- Reduced stamina.
    
- Frequent dizziness.
    
- Difficulty remaining standing for extended periods.
    
- A strong tendency to faint when physically or emotionally overexerted.
    

While no permanent damage has been identified, Medical expects recovery to take considerable time.

---

## **NUTRITIONAL REQUIREMENTS**

To support recovery, Taiga has been instructed to substantially increase her food intake.

Her previous diet is no longer sufficient.

Medical has authorized:

- Larger meal portions.
    
- Additional snacks throughout the day.
    
- Increased hydration.
    
- Regular monitoring of blood pressure and blood glucose.
    

Bao has already revised her meal schedule.

Taiga initially protested the larger portions.

She was reminded that recovering is now part of her job.

She quietly finished everything on her plate.

---

## **OBSERVED BEHAVIOR**

One notable change has become apparent throughout the Blacksite.

Mart is rarely more than a few steps away from Taiga.

Whether she is walking through the residential wing, eating with friends, reading in the library, or training under supervision...

Mart is there.

Not because anyone instructed him to.

Because he worries.

Whenever Taiga pauses for a little too long...

Mart is already beside her.

Whenever she sways slightly...

He instinctively reaches out to steady her.

When she faints...

He is almost always the one who catches her before she reaches the floor.

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services believes Mart's constant presence is motivated by unresolved guilt over the events surrounding Taiga's disappearance and recovery.

Despite repeated reassurance from Medical staff that neither sibling was responsible for the other's condition...

Mart continues to blame himself.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

Mart wanted to argue.

### **Closing Observation**

Late in the afternoon, Taiga and Mart were walking back toward the residential wing.

Without warning, Taiga's knees buckled.

Before she could fall, Mart caught her effortlessly.

She leaned weakly against him, eyes half-closed.

Mart still wore the same guilty expression.
