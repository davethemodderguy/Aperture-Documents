# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-109**

### **MISSING PERSON INCIDENT — MART SUNBRINGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – ACTIVE SEARCH / MEDICAL

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

**Time:** 02:41

At approximately 02:41, multiple residents of Residential Wing N-07 were awakened by a loud cry coming from Mart Sunbringer's room.

Taiga, Astral, and Star reached the room first.

Mart was awake.

But he wasn't fully aware of his surroundings.

Medical believes he had awakened directly into a severe nightmare.

---

## **OBSERVED BEHAVIOR**

Witnesses described Mart as displaying extreme fear.

Symptoms included:

- Rapid breathing.
    
- Trembling.
    
- Dilated pupils.
    
- Difficulty recognizing familiar faces.
    
- Repeatedly looking toward the window.
    
- Speaking incoherently.
    

Astral attempted to approach him.

> "Mart..."

He recoiled.

Not from her...

From something only he appeared to see.

He whispered one sentence.

> "...No..."

Then another.

> "...Not again..."

Before anyone could reach him—

He transformed into his owl form.

---

## **ESCAPE**

Mart accelerated toward the bedroom window at full speed.

The reinforced glass shattered outward.

By the time security personnel reached the courtyard...

He was already gone.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Search teams immediately began surveying the surrounding forests and mountains.

No signs of Mart have been located.

No feathers.

No footprints.

No wormhole activity.

No confirmed sightings.

At the time of this report...

**Mart Sunbringer has not returned.**

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral remained standing beside the broken window long after everyone else had left.

Snow drifted silently into the room.

She whispered into the night.

> "...Please come home..."

There was no reply.

---

### Taiga Sunbringer

Taiga never left Mart's room.

His book remained open on the bedside table.

She carefully placed a bookmark between its pages.

Then quietly closed it.

> "...I'll keep your place..."

She sat beside the broken window until sunrise.

---

> "Do not corner him."

> "Bring him home safely."

No one touched it.

---

Nothing.

---

Star sat on Mart's bed, clutching one of his books.

She looked toward the broken window.

> "...He's coming back..."

No one answered.

---

His blanket remains folded.

His favorite book rests on the bedside table with a bookmark tucked neatly inside.

By the window...

It remains unlocked.

Just in case.
