# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-154**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATION — ASTRAL SUNBRINGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – RESIDENT WELFARE

---

## **SUMMARY**

Although Astral Sunbringer has successfully stabilized her spectral form and become a permanent resident of APRT-70, recent counseling sessions indicate that fragments of her final memories continue to resurface.

These recollections are infrequent but vivid.

Unlike Taiga, whose trauma has substantially healed, Astral is now the one processing memories she had forgotten for years.

---

## **COUNSELING NOTE**

During a quiet afternoon in the residential gardens, Astral suddenly stopped speaking.

She stared into the distance for several seconds.

When asked if she was alright, she answered softly,

> "...I remember."

Later interviews confirmed another fragment of memory had returned.

She remembered the day she died.

She remembered armed assailants.

She remembered being fatally shot.

Most painfully...

She remembered looking past them and seeing a young Taiga witnessing everything.

Astral lowered her head.

> "...She saw all of it..."

Her voice trembled.

> "...She was just a little girl."

---

## **OBSERVATION**

Astral did not display anger.

She did not ask for names.

She did not ask where those responsible were.

Instead, she quietly asked one question.

> "...What happened afterward?"

Director Lucious answered honestly.

> "...We investigated."

> "...The people responsible were found."

> "...They are no longer a threat."

No additional operational details were disclosed.

Astral remained silent for several moments.

Then slowly nodded.

> "...Thank you."

Another pause.

> "...Because if they were still out there..."

She looked down at her translucent hands.

> "...I think I would have gone looking myself."

No one doubted she meant it.

---

## **FAMILY RESPONSE**

Taiga overheard part of the conversation.

She immediately wrapped her arms around Astral.

> **Taiga:** "...Mom..."

Astral hugged her back.

This time...

She could.

Something she had longed to do for years.

> **Astral:** "...I'm sorry you had to see that."

Taiga shook her head.

> "...You're here now."

> "...That's enough."

---

Mart stood nearby, quietly watching.

After a moment, he stepped forward and joined the hug without saying anything.

Astral smiled through tears.

> "...Both of my children..."

> "...Together."

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Director Lucious

Later that evening, Director Lucious spoke privately with Astral.

> "...Revenge doesn't bring people back."

He looked toward the residential wing, where Taiga and Mart were laughing over a board game.

> "...Protecting the people still here does."

Astral followed his gaze.

She smiled faintly.

> "...I know."

---

### Taiga Sunbringer

After learning what Astral had remembered, Taiga refused to leave her mother's side for the rest of the evening.

At one point she simply rested her head on Astral's shoulder.

Neither of them spoke.

Neither needed to.

---

### Mart Sunbringer

Mart quietly made tea for Astral and left it outside her room with a handwritten note.

> _"You don't have to remember everything at once."_

No signature was necessary.

Astral recognized his handwriting immediately.

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star sat with Astral beneath one of the courtyard trees.

She didn't try to distract her.

She simply stayed.

Sometimes company was enough.

---

### Nightshade Dawnbringer

Nightshade later remarked,

> "...Memories can reopen wounds."

A brief pause.

> "...They can also remind us how far we've come."

---

### Frost

Frost quietly brought Astral a blanket, despite knowing ghosts did not truly feel cold.

Astral accepted it anyway.

It was the kindness that mattered.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Astral Sunbringer**

- Spectral form: Stable.
    
- Permanent Blacksite resident.
    
- Continues to recover fragmented memories.
    
- Receiving ongoing psychological support.
    

No signs of retaliatory behavior have been observed.

Astral has expressed that knowing those responsible are no longer a threat has helped her focus on the present rather than the past.

---

## **Closing Observation**

That night, Astral stood outside the residential wing, watching Taiga and Mart talking through the window.

They were arguing over whether gliding counted as flying.

Mart remained unconvinced.

Taiga remained loudly convinced.

Astral laughed quietly to herself.

The memory of her final moments would always hurt.

But it was no longer the last chapter of her story.

Now, when she thought of her children, she no longer pictured a frightened little girl standing in the snow.

She pictured a cheerful daughter, a gentle son, and a home filled with people who had become family.

For the first time since those memories returned...

The past no longer felt stronger than the present.
