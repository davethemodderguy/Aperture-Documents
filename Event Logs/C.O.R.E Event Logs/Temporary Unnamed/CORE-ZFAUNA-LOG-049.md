# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-049**

### **MEDICAL STATUS REVIEW — INCREASED PSYCHOLOGICAL DISTRESS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – MEDICAL / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Medical Services has issued an updated psychological assessment regarding Taiga.

While her physical recovery has continued to progress, her emotional condition has shown measurable deterioration over recent weeks.

The change has been gradual rather than sudden.

Those who see her every day barely noticed it.

Those comparing older recordings did.

---

## **OBSERVED CHANGES**

Personnel have documented the following behavioral changes:

- Sleeping fewer hours despite persistent exhaustion.
    
- More frequent nightmares.
    
- Reduced appetite on particularly stressful days.
    
- Spending long periods quietly staring out windows overlooking the Icelandic snow.
    
- Becoming noticeably startled by unexpected sounds, even when they are harmless.
    
- Asking where her roommates are significantly more often than before.
    
- Frequently apologizing for minor inconveniences.
    
- Smiling less often than previous months.
    

None of these changes individually appeared alarming.

Together, however, they formed a concerning pattern.

---

## **CONCEALED DISTRESS**

Medical staff have also noted that Taiga has become increasingly adept at masking her emotions.

She still smiles.

She still thanks everyone.

She still helps Bao cook.

She still follows Neko to Engineering.

She still plays with Star.

She still pets Leo while he sleeps.

But once she believes nobody is looking...

The smile fades.

---

## **SECURITY OBSERVATIONS**

Review of overnight security footage revealed several recurring behaviors.

After nightmares, Taiga now remains awake for long periods even after returning to bed.

Instead of sleeping, she quietly watches the ceiling or listens to the breathing of her roommates.

Several recordings showed her briefly checking that each roommate was still present before finally attempting to rest again.

Medical believes these checks are not ritualistic, but rather an attempt to reassure herself that the people she loves are still safe.

---

## **MEDICAL INTERVIEW**

During a scheduled wellness session, Taiga was asked a simple question.

> "How have you been feeling lately?"

She answered automatically.

> "...I'm okay."

The attending physician did not continue with the next question.

Instead, he quietly replied,

> "...You don't have to give us the automatic answer."

Taiga remained silent.

Nearly a minute passed before she spoke again.

Much more quietly.

> "...I'm tired."

Another pause.

> "...Not sleepy..."

She searched for the right words.

> "...Just..."

> "...Tired."

---

## **ROOM N-07**

The report was shared privately with Taiga's roommates.

The atmosphere that evening was unusually quiet.

No one wanted to overwhelm her with attention.

No one wanted her to feel watched.

Instead...

Everyone made small changes.

Neko finished work earlier than usual.

Bao invited Taiga to help prepare dinner, even though she already knew how.

Frost suggested taking a quiet walk through the greenhouse.

Star invited Taiga to build another snow fort after breakfast.

Nightshade simply remained nearby more often than before.

Aster, as always, said the least.

She simply sat beside Taiga while drawing.

Sometimes they spent nearly an hour together without either of them saying anything.

Neither seemed to mind.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIUS'S REMARKS**

Director Lucious reviewed the updated assessment personally.

Later that evening, he visited Room N-07.

Finding Taiga reading quietly, he sat across from her.

For several minutes, neither spoke.

Eventually, he broke the silence.

> "...You're carrying a great deal."

Taiga gave a small nod.

He continued.

> "...You don't have to carry it alone."

She looked down at the floor.

After another long pause, she quietly answered,

> "...I'm still learning how."

---

## **MEDICAL ACTIONS**

Medical Services has increased the frequency of Taiga's routine wellness appointments.

The objective is not confinement or isolation, but providing consistent opportunities for her to speak openly, rest, and receive support before her distress becomes overwhelming.

Room N-07 residents have also agreed to check in with Taiga more intentionally, particularly after nightmares or unusually stressful days.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga remains cooperative, affectionate toward her roommates, and engaged in daily life.

However, Medical considers her emotional state to require continued attention.

Her willingness to reassure others often exceeds her willingness to acknowledge her own struggles.

Continued support from trusted companions has been assessed as an important protective factor.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, the lights in Room N-07 were already dim.

Everyone else had fallen asleep.

Taiga remained awake, sitting quietly by the window as snow drifted across the Blacksite.

Without a word, Aster—who had apparently been awake the entire time—walked over carrying her whiteboard.

She didn't ask what was wrong.

She simply wrote:

> **Can't sleep?**

Taiga looked at the message for a long moment.

Then, instead of giving her usual answer, she slowly nodded.

Aster erased the board.

She wrote only two more words.

> **I'm here.**

Taiga leaned gently against her shoulder.

Outside, the Icelandic winter remained silent.

Inside, she no longer had to face the quiet by herself.
