# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-101**

### **RESIDENTIAL NOTICE — FEATHER ETIQUETTE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL

---

## **NOTICE TO ALL PERSONNEL**

Following several questions regarding Mart Sunbringer's feathers, Residential Services has issued the following guidance.

### **General Rule**

**Please do not pluck feathers from Mart.**

He sheds naturally, and there are usually plenty of loose feathers available for anyone who has permission to collect them.

Plucking feathers without asking is considered rude.

---

## **...HOWEVER**

The rule became slightly more complicated after staff noticed Mart's reactions varied dramatically depending on **who** attempted it.

---

### **Taiga Sunbringer**

Taiga occasionally notices a loose feather that is barely attached.

She asks first.

> "...May I?"

Mart lowers his head.

> "...Okay."

She gently removes the loose feather.

Mart barely reacts.

Sometimes he even thanks her.

---

### **Astral Sunbringer**

Astral mothers him exactly as she always has.

She smooths his feathers, notices one that's about to fall out, and gently removes it.

Mart simply sighs.

> "...Mom..."

Astral smiles.

> "...It was already coming out."

---

### **Director Lucious**

On one occasion, Director Lucious absentmindedly removed a loose feather while Mart was perched on his shoulder during paperwork.

Mart looked at him.

Director Lucious looked at the feather.

"...Sorry."

Mart accepted the apology.

---

### **Star Dawnbringer**

Star once asked,

> "...Can I have one?"

Mart nodded.

She carefully took a single loose feather.

Then spent the next ten minutes thanking him repeatedly.

Mart seemed more embarrassed than bothered.

---

### **Neko**

Neko once jokingly reached toward Mart.

Mart immediately hopped backward.

> "...No."

Neko withdrew his hand.

> "...Fair enough."

---

### **Miss Neko**

She has never attempted to pluck one.

Mart occasionally leaves naturally shed feathers near her books anyway.

---

### **Leo**

Leo has never intentionally taken a feather.

Mostly because...

He's asleep.

---

## **MEDICAL COMMENT**

Medical Services notes that forcibly removing healthy feathers may be painful and should never be done.

Loose feathers that are already ready to molt are a different matter and generally cause little to no discomfort when removed gently with Mart's permission.

---

## **MART'S OPINION**

When asked to clarify the "rules," Mart thought for a long time before answering.

> "...If it hurts..."

He shook his head.

> "...No."

A small pause.

> "...If it's already loose..."

Another pause.

> "...And you ask..."

He smiled.

> "...Usually okay."

---

## **CURRENT GUIDELINES**

- Do **not** pull healthy feathers.
    
- Naturally shed feathers are preferred.
    
- If a feather is obviously loose, **ask Mart first**.
    
- Respect his answer, even if it's just a quiet shake of the head.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

One afternoon, Taiga noticed a single loose feather sticking up awkwardly on Mart's head while he was reading in his human form.

She pointed at it.

> "...Loose feather."

Mart sighed dramatically.

> "...Again?"

He leaned his head toward her.

Taiga gently removed it.

Mart rubbed the spot.

> "...Better."

Star, watching from across the room, gasped.

> "...You have permission to do that?"

Mart nodded.

> "...She's my sister."

Taiga smiled.

> "...Perks of the job."

Mart couldn't help but laugh.
