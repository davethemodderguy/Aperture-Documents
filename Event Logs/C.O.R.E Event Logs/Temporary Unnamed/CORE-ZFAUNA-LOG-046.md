# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-046**

### **NOCTURNAL OBSERVATION — NIGHTMARES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – MEDICAL / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Residential Services has documented an ongoing pattern involving Taiga's sleep.

Although she has gradually become more cheerful during the day, her nights remain difficult.

Medical notes that Taiga experiences recurring nightmares several times each week.

The exact content varies, but they consistently produce the same physical response.

---

## **OBSERVED SYMPTOMS**

When startled awake by a nightmare, Taiga typically exhibits:

- Rapid heartbeat.
    
- Accelerated breathing.
    
- Trembling.
    
- Heavy perspiration.
    
- Tears that crystallize into tiny frost-like droplets due to her anomalous physiology.
    
- Temporary disorientation lasting several minutes.
    

Personnel emphasize that these episodes occur while waking from sleep and are not deliberate behavior.

---

## **UNUSUAL BEHAVIOR**

For several weeks, roommates noticed Taiga was often missing from her bed during the early hours of the morning.

Initially, staff assumed she had gone to the kitchen or balcony.

She had not.

One evening, Security reviewed overnight camera footage.

The recordings showed a consistent pattern.

Taiga would wake suddenly from a nightmare.

She would sit upright, breathing heavily.

She would look around the room.

Seeing everyone else asleep...

She would quietly stand.

Carefully avoid waking anyone.

And leave.

---

## **OUTSIDE**

The cameras followed her into the winter night.

Even during snowfall.

Even when temperatures dropped well below freezing.

Taiga would quietly sit outside on the residential balcony or beneath the sheltered overhang overlooking the snow.

She hugged her knees close.

Waited for her breathing to settle.

Then remained there until she believed she could return without disturbing anyone.

Medical later confirmed that prolonged exposure to those temperatures placed Taiga at genuine risk of hypothermia and frostbite.

When asked why she chose to go outside instead of waking someone, Taiga lowered her eyes.

After a long silence, she answered softly.

> "...They work hard."

Another pause.

> "...They deserve to sleep."

She smiled faintly despite the exhaustion.

> "...I didn't want to wake them."

---

## **DISCOVERY**

The behavior continued unnoticed until one particularly cold night.

Neko woke briefly to get a glass of water.

Passing the window, he noticed the balcony door was slightly open.

Outside...

Taiga sat curled beneath the falling snow.

Her scarf and hair had accumulated a thin layer of frost.

She was shivering.

Neko immediately stepped outside, wrapped his coat around her shoulders, and quietly guided her back inside.

He didn't scold her.

He simply asked,

> "...Why?"

Taiga hesitated.

Then answered in almost a whisper.

> "...I love everyone too much..."

She looked toward the sleeping room.

> "...I don't want my nightmares to become theirs."

---

## **ROOM N-07 RESPONSE**

The following morning, everyone learned what had been happening.

The reaction was immediate.

Bao looked heartbroken.

Star sat silently for several moments before gently hugging Taiga.

Miss Neko wrapped another blanket around her even though she was already warm.

Frost quietly made a mug of hot tea.

Nightshade stood nearby, arms folded, saying nothing for a long while.

Finally, she spoke.

> "...You don't have to carry difficult nights by yourself."

Aster wrote only one sentence.

> **Wake us.**

Then underlined it twice.

Director Lucious, after reading the report later that day, approved additional residential accommodations without hesitation.

---

## **PROCEDURAL CHANGES**

To help Taiga feel comfortable asking for help, several small changes were made within Room N-07.

- A warm blanket is kept beside Taiga's bed.
    
- A thermos of hot tea is prepared each evening.
    
- The balcony door now quietly alerts roommates if opened overnight.
    
- Every roommate has made it clear that waking them after a nightmare is acceptable.
    

No one considered the interrupted sleep an inconvenience.

### Aster

That evening, Aster placed a small whiteboard beside Taiga's bed.

Written on it before everyone went to sleep were the words:

> **If you wake up scared...**

Below that, every roommate added a signature or drawing.

Neko drew a wrench.

Miss Neko, a cat.

Bao, a cloud.

Frost, a snowflake.

Star, a glowing star.

Nightshade simply wrote her name.

Director Lucious added his initials before leaving for the night.

At the bottom, Aster wrote one final sentence.

> **You're never waking up alone.**

Taiga read it quietly before going to bed.

She smiled.

Then placed the board where she could see it from her pillow.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga continues to experience nightmares, though she has begun accepting comfort from her roommates rather than leaving the Blacksite residence alone.

Medical considers this a positive step.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Several nights later, Taiga awoke from another nightmare.

Her breathing was uneven.

Her heart raced.

For a moment, she looked toward the balcony door out of habit.

Then her eyes fell on the little whiteboard beside her bed.

She read the message again.

Slowly, she reached over and gently tapped Aster's shoulder.

Aster blinked awake.

She didn't ask questions.

She simply smiled sleepily, reached for her whiteboard, and wrote:

> **Bad dream?**

Taiga gave a small nod.

Within minutes, the room was quietly awake.

Bao was making tea.

Miss Neko found an extra blanket.

Star sat beside Taiga with Leo, who had somehow slept through the entire commotion until being gently picked up.

Mart fluttered down from his perch to Taiga's shoulder.

Outside, snow continued to fall over the Blacksite.

Inside, no one complained about losing a little sleep.

To them, a few hours of rest was easy to replace.

A friend wasn't.
