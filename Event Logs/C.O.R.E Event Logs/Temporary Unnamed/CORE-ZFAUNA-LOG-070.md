# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-070**

### **STATUS UPDATE — RESIDENTIAL SEARCH & PERSONNEL DISCIPLINARY ACTION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – MEDICAL / SECURITY

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following Taiga's prolonged withdrawal from her roommates, Security conducted a comprehensive review of surveillance footage and access logs.

Results confirmed the following:

- Taiga **has not left the Blacksite**.
    
- She **has not transformed into her snow leopard form**.
    
- She continues to eat and drink regularly.
    
- She has avoided all restricted areas.
    
- She has intentionally remained out of sight.
    

Despite these findings...

Finding her proved unexpectedly difficult.

---

## **SEARCH EFFORTS**

Over several days, personnel searched numerous locations throughout the facility.

Including:

- Observation decks.
    
- Maintenance corridors.
    
- Storage rooms.
    
- Libraries.
    
- Engineering workshops.
    
- Empty laboratories.
    
- Whitefall Lake access tunnels.
    
- Residential common areas.
    

Each search ended the same way.

Someone would report,

> "...She was just here."

Only for Taiga to be gone by the time anyone arrived.

Reviewing security footage later showed she had simply walked away moments beforehand, taking quiet routes through the Blacksite that few people normally used.

Medical concluded this was not an attempt to escape.

It was an attempt **not to be found.**

---

## **ROOM N-07**

Her belongings remained untouched.

Her bed remained neatly made.

Occasionally, someone noticed that a blanket had been unfolded during the night...

Only to find it carefully folded again by morning.

Evidence suggested Taiga occasionally returned while everyone else was asleep.

She never stayed.

---

## **PERSONNEL RESPONSIBLE FOR THE DISCLOSURE**

The staff member who disregarded Medical's standing instructions and informed Taiga about her dissociative defensive episode was identified during the subsequent investigation.

Director Lucious personally reviewed the findings.

The employee was immediately removed from duties involving resident welfare and psychological care while a formal disciplinary process was carried out.

The Director's written conclusion was concise:

> "Medical directives exist for a reason."

> "Disregarding them caused preventable harm."

No further details of the disciplinary outcome are included in this log.

---

---

### Star

Star eventually stopped running through the corridors shouting Taiga's name.

Not because she had given up.

Because she realized it only made Taiga hide more.

Instead...

Every night before bed, she quietly whispered toward the empty bed across the room,

> "...Goodnight."

> "...We'll be here tomorrow."

---

### Nightshade

Watching Star one evening, Nightshade quietly remarked:

> "...Sometimes..."

> "...The hardest person to forgive..."

She looked toward the empty doorway.

> "...Is yourself."

---

### Aster

One morning, Aster discovered that the note reading **"Please come home."** had been moved.

It was now lying neatly folded beneath Taiga's pillow.

Nothing else had changed.

Aster smiled sadly.

It was the first proof that Taiga had read it.

---

### Leo

Leo continued to sleep on Taiga's bed whenever she was absent.

Veterinary staff observed that, despite his normally indiscriminate sleeping habits, he consistently returned to the same spot each evening.

---

### Mart

Mart eventually changed his routine.

Rather than searching endlessly, he began waiting near Room N-07 each night.

As if he believed that sooner or later...

Taiga would come back.

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical reiterates that Taiga's withdrawal is not evidence of hostility toward the Blacksite or its residents.

Rather, it reflects a profound loss of confidence in herself and a fear that her presence may endanger those she loves.

The recommended course of action remains unchanged:

- Do not pressure Taiga into returning.
    
- Maintain predictable routines.
    
- Continue leaving her room available.
    
- Reassure her whenever contact occurs.
    
- Allow her to choose when she is ready to reconnect.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late one night, Security footage captured a familiar figure quietly entering Room N-07.

Taiga moved carefully through the darkened room, making sure not to wake anyone.

She gently adjusted the blanket that had slipped off Star.

Straightened one of Bao's books.

Refilled Leo's bowl of cherry tomatoes after noticing it was nearly empty.

Smiled faintly at Aster's folded note.

Then she looked at her own bed.

She reached out...

Rested one hand on the pillow for a moment...

And quietly walked back out into the sleeping Blacksite.

The room never knew she had been there.

But by morning, everyone somehow felt that she had.
