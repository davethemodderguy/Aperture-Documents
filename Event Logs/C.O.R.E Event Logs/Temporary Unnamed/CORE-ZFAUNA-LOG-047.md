# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-047**

### **MEDICAL OBSERVATION — SELF-NEGLECTING BEHAVIOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – MEDICAL / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following several months of observation, Medical Services has identified an important pattern regarding Taiga's wellbeing.

Contrary to initial assumptions, Taiga's greatest vulnerability is not her physical injuries, anomalous abilities, or environmental hazards.

It is the way she prioritizes everyone else above herself.

Medical notes that Taiga consistently minimizes her own needs whenever she believes caring for herself might inconvenience someone she loves.

---

## **OBSERVED PATTERNS**

### Accompanying Others

Taiga prefers remaining near her roommates throughout the day.

If someone is working, she asks to accompany them.

If someone is busy, she quietly waits nearby.

She rarely asks for attention.

She simply feels safer knowing the people she cares about are still there.

---

### Nightmares

When nightmares wake her in the middle of the night, Taiga's first instinct is not to seek help.

Instead, she worries about waking everyone else.

On multiple occasions she attempted to endure severe emotional distress alone rather than interrupt anyone's sleep.

Medical believes this behavior places her at unnecessary physical risk, particularly during Iceland's winter months.

---

### Physical Health

Taiga has repeatedly been observed saying she is "fine" despite displaying obvious signs of exhaustion or discomfort.

Examples include:

- Continuing daily activities despite poor sleep.
    
- Downplaying pain from minor injuries.
    
- Remaining outside in freezing weather longer than medically recommended.
    
- Delaying asking for assistance until others notice something is wrong.
    

When questioned about these incidents, her answer rarely changes.

> "...I didn't want everyone to worry."

---

## **MEDICAL INTERVIEW**

During a routine wellness appointment, Medical asked Taiga a direct question.

> "If one of your roommates felt the way you do, what would you tell them?"

Taiga answered immediately.

> "...I'd tell them to come find us."

The attending physician smiled gently.

> "Then why don't you?"

Taiga was quiet.

She stared at her hands for a long time before speaking.

> "...Because..."

Her voice became almost a whisper.

> "...They're already doing so much."

Another pause.

> "...I don't want to make things harder."

---

## **ROOM N-07 RESPONSE**

When the report was shared with the residents of Room N-07, the room remained unusually quiet.

Neko was the first to speak.

> "...You know what's hard?"

Taiga looked up.

> "...Watching someone we care about pretend everything's okay."

Miss Neko nodded.

> "...We don't want a perfect roommate."

> "...We just want you."

Bao gently held Taiga's hand.

> "...You always take care of us."

She smiled warmly.

> "...Please let us return the favor."

Frost added quietly,

> "...Being strong also means accepting help."

Star's eyes watered slightly.

She leaned over and hugged Taiga.

> "...You don't have to protect us from your feelings."

Nightshade, after a long silence, spoke in her usual calm voice.

> "...You keep saying you're fine."

She looked directly at Taiga.

> "...I believe you're trying."

Another pause.

> "...I don't believe you're fine."

Taiga lowered her head.

For the first time during the conversation...

She didn't argue.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIUS'S REMARKS**

Director Lucious reviewed the report later that evening.

He visited Room N-07 after work.

Finding Taiga reading quietly by the heater, he sat across from her.

> "...You've become very good at taking care of other people."

She smiled faintly.

> "...I try."

He nodded.

> "...Now learn something just as important."

Taiga looked up.

> "...Let other people take care of you."

---

## **ASTER'S MESSAGE**

That night, Aster quietly left a note on Taiga's bedside table.

It simply read:

> **If you say you're fine...**

Below it, after a small pause, she had written:

> **...I'll still check on you.**

Taiga stared at the message for a long time.

She folded it carefully.

Then tucked it inside the pocket of her journal.

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical concludes that Taiga's deep affection for her friends and roommates is genuine and has helped her build strong, meaningful relationships within the Blacksite.

However, that same devotion has also led her to neglect her own physical and emotional wellbeing.

Her tendency to insist she is "fine," even when clearly distressed, increases the risk of worsening exhaustion, illness, or emotional crises.

Personnel are encouraged to continue offering support while respecting Taiga's independence, and to gently check in when her actions suggest she may be struggling despite her reassurances.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Later that week, Bao found Taiga sitting quietly in the common room.

> "...How are you feeling?"

Taiga smiled automatically.

> "...I'm fi—"

She stopped herself.

The room was silent for a moment.

Then she took a slow breath.

> "...I'm... having a hard day."

Bao smiled gently.

> "...Thank you for telling me."

She sat down beside Taiga.

A few minutes later, Star wandered in carrying a deck of cards.

Neko arrived with hot chocolate.

Miss Neko brought an extra blanket.

Leo sleepily curled around Taiga's scarf.

Mart settled onto her shoulder.

No one tried to solve everything.

They simply stayed.

For the first time in a long while, Taiga let herself lean on the people she loved, just as they had always been willing to lean on her.
