# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-053**

### **CAFETERIA INCIDENT — "THE PEPPER CHALLENGE"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RECREATIONAL / CAFETERIA RECORD

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

Following Medical's confirmation that Taiga could consume Carolina Reapers without discomfort, curiosity spread rapidly throughout the Blacksite.

It eventually led to one question.

> "...Can anyone else do it?"

Director Lucious, who had already reviewed the medical report in full, immediately declined to participate.

He remained in the cafeteria solely to observe.

According to witnesses, he already knew exactly how this was going to end.

---

## **PARTICIPANTS**

Volunteers included:

- Neko
    
- Miss Neko
    
- Bao
    
- Frost
    
- Star
    
- Nightshade
    

Taiga sat nearby with her usual salad.

Leo was beside her, happily eating a bowl full of cherry tomatoes.

Mart watched from Taiga's shoulder.

Aster prepared her whiteboard in advance.

---

## **THE EXPERIMENT**

Nutrition placed one Carolina Reaper in front of each volunteer.

Taiga looked around.

> "...You're all really going to eat those?"

Neko grinned.

> "...How bad could it be?"

Director Lucious quietly took a sip of coffee.

He said nothing.

---

### **Neko**

One bite.

Three seconds passed.

Five seconds.

Then—

"..."

"..."

"...Water."

He stood up.

"...Water."

A full pitcher disappeared shortly afterward.

Engineering later reported Neko was heard muttering,

> "...That violates chemistry..."

for the remainder of the afternoon.

---

### **Miss Neko**

She managed remarkable composure...

For approximately seven seconds.

Then she calmly stood up, walked to the refrigerator, removed an entire carton of milk, and drank directly from it.

After finishing, she looked toward Taiga.

> "...You're adopted."

> "...I refuse to believe you're normal."

---

### **Bao**

She lasted exactly one bite.

"..."

"...It's..."

"...Warm."

Five seconds later—

"...Never mind."

She immediately began making yogurt desserts for herself.

---

### **Frost**

Frost maintained her usual calm expression.

She quietly swallowed the pepper.

Paused.

Then slowly exhaled visible frost.

"...Interesting."

She proceeded to consume enough ice cubes to concern Medical.

---

### **Star**

Star looked determined.

"...Taiga can do it."

One bite.

Instant regret.

She rapidly waved both hands in front of her mouth.

> "...WHY?!"

Medical handed her a glass of milk before she could finish the sentence.

Nightshade quietly took the remaining pepper away before Star could convince herself to try again.

---

### **Nightshade**

Nightshade ate half.

Stopped.

Looked at the remaining half.

Placed it back on the tray.

> "...I've learned enough."

Nobody questioned her decision.

---

## **TAIGA**

Throughout the entire event...

Taiga quietly continued eating her salad.

Halfway through, she picked up another Carolina Reaper.

Crunch.

She chewed thoughtfully.

"...This one's sweeter."

The cafeteria collectively stared.

Medical requested another examination.

---

## **LEO**

Leo slowly slithered over from his bowl of cherry tomatoes.

He looked at one of the remaining peppers.

Swallowed it whole.

Waited.

Nothing happened.

He looked completely unimpressed.

Taiga translated his quiet hiss.

> **"Not as good as tomatoes."**

Leo immediately returned to eating his bowl of cherry tomatoes.

Medical noted that because Leo swallowed the pepper whole without biting into it, virtually no capsaicin was released inside his mouth, explaining his lack of reaction.

Leo remained entirely convinced tomatoes were the superior food.

---

## **MEDICAL FINDINGS**

Subsequent examination confirmed that Taiga possesses a highly unusual insensitivity to capsaicin.

Testing suggests the sensory receptors on her tongue do not respond to capsaicin in the same manner as those of a typical human.

As a result:

- She experiences the flavor of peppers.
    
- She detects texture and aroma normally.
    
- She experiences virtually none of the characteristic burning sensation.
    

Researchers emphasize that this immunity is limited to capsaicin and does not provide protection against actual heat.

### **Closing Observation**

Later that afternoon, Taiga was quietly eating another bowl of salad by the window.

She sprinkled chopped Carolina Reapers over the top as if they were herbs.

Leo curled beside her with his bowl of cherry tomatoes.

He looked at Taiga.

Then at the peppers.

Then back at his tomatoes.

According to Taiga, he gave one sleepy, satisfied remark.

> **"Snow Leopard that loves salads."**

Taiga giggled.

"...And banana snakes that love tomatoes."

Leo seemed to consider that for a moment.

Then continued eating.

He couldn't argue with that.
