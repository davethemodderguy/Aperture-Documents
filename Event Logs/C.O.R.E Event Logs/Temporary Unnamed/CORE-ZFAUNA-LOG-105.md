# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-105**

### **ANOMALOUS ABILITY UPDATE — "GROUND GLIDE"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – ANOMALOUS RESEARCH / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **NEWLY DOCUMENTED ABILITY**

Research has confirmed the emergence of another passive anomalous ability in Taiga Sunbringer.

The ability has been designated:

## **Ground Glide**

Unlike flight or levitation, the ability is subject to one remarkably strict limitation.

Taiga can float **exactly five (5) centimeters above the nearest solid surface.**

Not four.

Not six.

Exactly five.

Attempts to exceed this height have consistently failed.

---

## **DESCRIPTION**

While the ability is active, Taiga no longer needs to walk in the conventional sense.

Instead, she quietly glides over the ground as though standing on invisible ice.

Her feet remain motionless unless she intentionally chooses to walk normally.

The glide is smooth, silent, and surprisingly graceful.

Research confirms this is **not flight**.

The ability is anchored to the surface beneath her.

If the ground changes elevation, Taiga automatically follows it while remaining approximately five centimeters above it.

---

## **DOCUMENTED USES**

Taiga has quickly become accustomed to remaining in a gliding state for much of the day.

Common observations include:

- Gliding through hallways instead of walking.
    
- Preparing meals while hovering just above the kitchen floor.
    
- Reading while lazily drifting across the common room.
    
- Taking afternoon naps while floating five centimeters above her bed.
    
- Lying on the floor... without actually touching the floor.
    

Medical reports no adverse effects.

---

## **LIMITATIONS**

Research has confirmed several limitations.

- Maximum altitude: **5 centimeters.**
    
- Does **not** permit sustained flight.
    
- Cannot hover freely in open air.
    
- Crossing large gaps still requires jumping or Air Jump.
    
- If there is no supporting surface beneath her, the effect ends until she reaches one again.
    

Despite repeated testing...

Taiga still cannot fly.
