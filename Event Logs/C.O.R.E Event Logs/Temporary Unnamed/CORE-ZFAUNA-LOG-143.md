# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-143**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL STATUS UPDATE — SHOOTING RANGE OBSERVATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – MENTAL HEALTH / RECREATIONAL ACTIVITY

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following several months of observation, Psychological Services have officially concluded that Taiga Sunbringer no longer demonstrates trauma responses associated with the death of her mother.

Previously documented triggers—including gunfire, firearm handling, and fears directly tied to losing Astral—no longer produce panic responses.

Researchers emphasize that this does **not** erase Taiga's memories.

Rather, those memories are no longer accompanied by the overwhelming fear that once controlled her.

Astral's continued presence has been identified as the most likely contributing factor.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL EVALUATION**

Previous responses included:

- Freezing upon hearing gunfire.
    
- Avoiding firearms.
    
- Panic associated with separation from maternal figures.
    
- Persistent fear of abandonment.
    

Current evaluations found none of the above behaviors.

Instead, Taiga demonstrated curiosity toward firearms and voluntarily requested basic safety instruction at the Blacksite shooting range.

Psychological Services approved the request under supervision.

---

## **SHOOTING RANGE SESSION**

The first session was intended to be simple.

It did not remain simple for long.

Taiga arrived wearing hearing protection nearly twice the size of her head.

She looked around excitedly.

> "...I've always wanted to try this."

Astral raised an eyebrow.

> "...Now you tell me?"

Taiga scratched the back of her head.

> "...Timing?"

Astral sighed.

> "...Fair enough."

---

Mart quietly walked in several minutes later.

To the surprise of several newer personnel...

He also checked out a training firearm.

Taiga looked at him.

> "...You shoot too?"

Mart nodded.

> "...Sometimes."

> "...It's relaxing."

Taiga stared.

> "...Everything's relaxing to you."

Mart thought for a second.

> "...Not paperwork."

Director Lucious, who happened to be nearby, silently agreed.

---

## **RANGE RESULTS**

### **Taiga**

Initial accuracy:

Poor.

Very enthusiastic.

She celebrated every target she hit.

Including one that had clearly been hit by someone else.

Her excitement dramatically outweighed her consistency.

After several magazines, however, she began improving rapidly.

Her athletic coordination transferred surprisingly well.

---

### **Mart**

Mart approached the firing line calmly.

No unnecessary movement.

No wasted shots.

He quietly fired several rounds.

Every shot grouped neatly near the center of the target.

Taiga looked between Mart...

And his target.

> "...Since when could you shoot like that?"

Mart shrugged.

> "...I practiced."

> "...While you were learning how to glide."

Taiga dramatically accused him of secretly becoming good at everything.

Mart denied the allegation.

Unconvincingly.

---

## **UNEXPECTED DISCOVERY**

Despite Taiga's former trauma surrounding gunfire...

Repeated exposure during training produced no measurable distress.

Instead...

She smiled every time she heard the electronic target system announce another hit.

Psychological Services officially removed firearm-related trauma from her active treatment notes.

---

## **Closing Observation**

As the session ended, Taiga looked over her target with obvious pride.

Mart walked over carrying his own.

His grouping was noticeably tighter.

Taiga stared at it for several seconds.

Then looked at him.

> "...You're impossible."

Mart smiled faintly.

> "...You hit the paper every time."

Taiga crossed her arms.

> "...That wasn't the compliment you think it was."

Mart laughed.

The sound echoed across the shooting range.

Several staff members paused for just a moment.

It had become a familiar sound again.

And nobody minded hearing it.
