## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-021**

### **EQUIPMENT & PHYSIOLOGICAL DISCLOSURE — POWER RESTRAINERS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – N.O.S.T. / BIOLOGICAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

During a routine rehabilitation session, personnel noticed another gradual change in Taiga's appearance.

The black stripes along her hair, skin, and leopard ears had become more defined and noticeably darker than when she first arrived at the Blacksite.

More importantly, what had previously been assumed to be ordinary black bandages were revealed to be something entirely different.

They were not bandages.

They were restraints.

---

# **I. DISCLOSURE**

When Medical once again asked about the black wraps around her forearms, Taiga remained quiet for several moments before answering.

She carefully loosened one edge of the fabric.

The material underneath was unlike any textile known to Aperture Industries.

Smooth.

Matte black.

Thin, yet completely resistant to cutting, tearing, burning, or freezing during preliminary testing.

Taiga looked down at the restraints.

> "...These aren't bandages."

She gently tightened them again.

> "...They're power restrainers."

The room became silent.

---

# **II. PURPOSE**

Taiga explained that, when left completely unrestricted, she has difficulty controlling the full extent of her anomalous abilities.

According to her:

> "...I don't mean to break things."

A pause.

> "...Sometimes... I just do."

The restraints continuously suppress her anomalous output to a safe level.

While wearing them, Taiga is able to comfortably live among personnel without worrying about accidentally causing damage.

Medical reviewed incident records from before the revelation.

Several unexplained events—such as localized bursts of snow manipulation that Taiga herself couldn't explain—are now believed to have occurred when the restraints had briefly loosened during activity.

---

# **III. SECONDARY EFFECT**

Further testing revealed an even more remarkable property.

The restraints do not merely regulate Taiga's own abilities.

They temporarily suppress anomalous abilities in general.

While active and worn correctly, they significantly reduce or completely nullify Taiga's own supernatural capabilities, leaving only her normal physical traits.

N.O.S.T. researchers attempted to determine the mechanism responsible.

Every test produced the same conclusion.

**Unknown.**

No measurable energy field.

No identifiable material composition.

No detectable technology.

The restraints simply...

Worked.

---

# **IV. ORIGIN**

When asked where they came from, Taiga answered almost immediately.

> "...Mom made them."

Further questioning revealed little additional information.

Taiga does not know how they were manufactured.

She does not know what they are made from.

She only knows that her mother gave them to her while saying:

> "...You'll know when you need these."

Taiga has worn them ever since.

---

# **V. REPLICATION ATTEMPTS**

Engineering, Medical, and N.O.S.T. jointly requested permission to study the restraints.

Taiga agreed, provided they were not removed from her for extended periods.

Despite exhaustive analysis:

- Material composition could not be identified.
    
- Manufacturing method remains unknown.
    
- No microscopic seams or joints were located.
    
- Samples could not be collected.
    
- The restraints resisted every non-destructive analytical technique available.
    

Director Lucious officially suspended further replication attempts.

His note was brief.

> **"If Taiga says her mother made them, then for now, that's our answer."**

## **CURRENT STATUS**

The black restraints worn by Taiga have been identified as unique anomalous suppressors created by her late mother.

Their known functions include:

- Regulating Taiga's anomalous output.
    
- Preventing accidental destructive incidents.
    
- Temporarily suppressing anomalous abilities while active.
    
- Remaining completely resistant to all current attempts at analysis or replication.
    

At Taiga's request, the restraints are to be regarded as personal equipment and are **not** to be removed except in an extreme, life-threatening medical emergency.

---

### **Closing Observation**

After the day's examinations concluded, Taiga quietly retightened the black straps around her arms.

She flexed her fingers.

The gentle swirl of snow that had been drifting around her slowly faded until only ordinary Icelandic snowfall remained.

Aster looked up from her whiteboard.

She wrote only one sentence.

> **Your mom made something amazing.**

Taiga rested a hand on one of the restraints.

Her smile was small, but genuine.

> "...She really did."
