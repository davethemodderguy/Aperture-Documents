# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-091**

### **ACCIDENT REPORT — MART'S LEFT WING INJURY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – MEDICAL / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

**Location:** Residential Training Hall

During a routine recreational session, Taiga and Mart were playfully chasing one another through the training area.

According to witnesses, Mart briefly transformed into his humanoid form to tease Taiga before darting away again.

At nearly the same moment, Taiga attempted an acrobatic maneuver.

The timing could not have been worse.

She accidentally struck Mart with a powerful dropkick.

The impact launched him several meters across the padded floor.

Medical personnel confirmed the injuries were accidental.

---

## **MEDICAL FINDINGS**

Mart immediately reverted to his owl form upon impact.

Examination revealed injuries affecting the same side in both forms.

**Humanoid Form**

- Fracture of the left hand.
    

**Owl Form**

- Fracture of the left wing.
    

Medical determined the injuries are linked across his transformations.

Treating one form aids recovery in the other.

His prognosis is favorable with rest, though he will be unable to fly until the wing has healed.

---

## **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION**

The physical pain was only part of the incident.

As soon as Medical confirmed his wing was broken, Mart became visibly distressed.

He cried continuously while staff examined him, repeatedly trying to spread his injured wing before stopping from the pain.

When Astral gently asked what was wrong, Mart answered through tears.

> "...My wing..."

> "...I can't fly..."

He buried his face against her shoulder.

> "...My wings..."

> "...They're important..."

Astral held him carefully, mindful of the injury.

> "...I know."

> "...We'll help it heal."

---

## **TAIGA**

The person most affected emotionally was Taiga.

The moment she realized what had happened, she froze.

"...Mart...?"

Seeing his injured wing, she immediately dropped to her knees beside him.

"...No..."

"...No, no, no..."

She refused to let Medical blame it on an accident.

Instead, she repeatedly apologized.

> "...I'm sorry..."

> "...I didn't mean to..."

> "...I'm so sorry..."

Mart was still crying too hard to answer.

Taiga remained beside him throughout the examination, refusing to leave until Medical assured her they had finished treating his injuries.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Astral

Astral stroked Mart's hair as he cried.

> "...It's alright."

> "...Broken wings can heal."

She then looked at Taiga.

> "...And accidents don't erase love."

---

### Director Lucious

After reading the report, Director Lucious issued one instruction.

> "No blame."

> "Treat the injury."

> "Support both of them."

---

### Neko

Neko quietly inspected the training area afterward.

> "...Wrong place."

> "...Wrong timing."

He sighed.

> "...That's all it was."

---

### Miss Neko

Miss Neko stayed with Taiga after Mart had been taken to Medical.

Taiga could barely stop crying.

Miss Neko simply sat beside her until she was ready to speak.

---

### Bao

Bao prepared food for both siblings that evening.

Neither ate much.

She left the meals anyway.

---

### Frost

Frost fashioned a lightweight cooling wrap to reduce swelling around Mart's injured wing.

---

### Star

Star visited Medical carrying one of Mart's favorite storybooks.

She sat beside his bed and read aloud while he rested.

He didn't say much.

But he listened.

---

### Nightshade

Nightshade found Taiga sitting alone outside Medical hours later.

Without looking up, Taiga whispered,

> "...He loves flying..."

Nightshade quietly answered,

> "...Then we'll make sure he flies again."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

- Mart: Stable.
    
- Left wing: Fractured.
    
- Left hand (humanoid form): Fractured.
    
- Flight status: Temporarily grounded.
    
- Taiga: Experiencing significant guilt; receiving emotional support alongside Mart.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late that night, Medical found Taiga asleep in a chair beside Mart's bed.

One of her hands rested carefully against the edge of his blanket, as though afraid to move too much.

Mart woke briefly.

Still tired, and with his wing secured in a splint, he reached over with his uninjured hand and gently held one of Taiga's fingers.

He didn't say anything.

Neither did she.

For the first time since the accident...

Both of them finally fell asleep.
