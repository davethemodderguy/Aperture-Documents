# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-123**

### **CRITICAL INTERPERSONAL INCIDENT — SUNBRINGER SIBLINGS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – PSYCHOLOGICAL / INTERNAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

On ██/██/20██, Taiga Sunbringer and Mart Sunbringer became involved in the first major argument documented since their recovery.

Witnesses unanimously described the disagreement as entirely out of character for both siblings.

No physical violence occurred.

The resulting emotional consequences, however, have been assessed as severe.

---

## **INCIDENT LOG**

The argument began as a minor disagreement regarding Taiga's rehabilitation.

Mart repeatedly insisted she should rest.

Taiga insisted she was capable of taking care of herself.

Voices gradually rose.

Several personnel nearby chose not to intervene, believing the disagreement would resolve naturally.

It did not.

---

### **Recovered Transcript**

**Mart:**  
_"You almost fainted again today."_

**Taiga:**  
_"I'm fine."_

**Mart:**  
_"You said that yesterday."_

**Taiga:**  
_"Because I AM fine!"_

**Mart:**  
_"You're pushing yourself too hard."_

**Taiga:**  
_"Stop treating me like I'm going to break!"_

Mart fell silent for a moment.

When he spoke again, his voice was much quieter.

**Mart:**  
_"I just... don't want to lose you again."_

Instead of calming down...

Taiga's frustration boiled over.

Weeks of exhaustion.

Months of fear.

Everything she had been holding inside finally erupted.

---

**Taiga:**  
_"Lose me?!"_

_"You keep acting like everything is your fault!"_

_"You weren't there when I got this!"_

She pulled back the sleeve covering the large scar on her left arm.

For only a moment.

Then covered it again.

Mart froze.

---

**Taiga:**  
_"Every time someone looks at this..."_

_"You look like you're the one bleeding!"_

_"I'm tired of it!"_

Mart lowered his head.

He couldn't answer.

---

Taiga continued.

Still angry.

Still hurting.

Not realizing every word was striking exactly where Mart was most vulnerable.

---

**Taiga:**  
_"Maybe stop thinking everyone needs you!"_

_"Maybe stop acting like you'll be abandoned every second!"_

_"No one's throwing you away, Mart!"_

_"You're the one who keeps believing you don't belong!"_

Mart's breathing became noticeably uneven.

Witnesses reported seeing his hands begin to shake.

---

Taiga took another step forward.

She was crying now.

But she was no longer listening.

Only speaking.

---

**Taiga:**  
_"Do you know how exhausting it is?!"_

_"Having to remind you that you're wanted over and over again?!"_

_"If you stopped thinking like that..."_

She stopped.

Just for a second.

Then the sentence she would later wish she had never said escaped her lips.

---

**Taiga:**  
_"Maybe you're making yourself feel unwanted."_

Silence.

Complete silence.

---

Mart stared at the floor.

He did not argue.

He did not defend himself.

He simply stood there.

His shoulders trembled.

A quiet sob escaped him.

Then another.

---

## **ANOMALOUS MANIFESTATION**

Without warning, a surge of anomalous energy was detected throughout the room.

Medical sensors recorded a rapid increase in radiant output centered on Mart.

Moments later...

Two wings composed entirely of white light emerged from his back.

Unlike his owl wings, these appeared wholly energetic in nature.

No feathers detached.

No biological structures were observed.

They resembled living light.

Personnel nearby instinctively stepped back.

Mart did not appear aware of them.

He never looked behind himself.

He only wiped at his eyes.

---

Then...

Without saying a single word...

He lifted from the floor.

The luminous wings spread once.

A gentle gust swept through the corridor.

Mart turned away.

And flew.

Not quickly.

Not angrily.

Just...

Away.

Within seconds, he had disappeared beyond the upper residential levels.

Search teams dispatched immediately were unable to locate him.

---

## **AFTERMATH**

The corridor remained silent.

Taiga stood exactly where she had been.

Her breathing slowly returned to normal.

She looked toward the place where Mart had disappeared.

Still angry.

Still convinced she had been right.

She did not yet understand what her words had done.

---

Not in anger.

In heartbreak.

After a pause, he added,

> "...Do not blame Taiga."

Not like that.

He quietly muttered to himself,

> "...Those weren't angry tears..."

---

### Miss Neko

---

### Bao

Bao prepared Mart's dinner anyway.

She covered the plate.

Left it warm.

And hoped.

---

### Frost

Frost said nothing for a long time.

Finally, she spoke.

> "...Words..."

> "...Can leave wounds no one else can see."

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star heard the shouting from another corridor.

She arrived only in time to see a streak of white disappear overhead.

She looked at Taiga.

Then toward the ceiling.

She didn't know who needed comforting first.

---

### Nightshade Dawnbringer

Nightshade remained with Taiga after everyone else had gone.

Neither of them spoke.

The silence felt heavier than the argument.

---

### Leo

Leo quietly picked up the chair Mart had accidentally knocked over while leaving.

He set it upright.

Then looked toward the empty ceiling.

> "...Come back."

### **Closing Observation**

Security footage from an exterior rooftop camera captured Mart several minutes after he departed.

He landed alone on one of the highest observation towers.

The radiant wings slowly folded behind him.

He sat at the edge of the roof, knees drawn to his chest.

No one was with him.

No one heard what he whispered.

The camera had no audio.

But when the footage was magnified, one detail became painfully clear.

Mart wasn't looking out across the snow.

He was staring at his own reflection in the dark window beside him.

And he was crying.
