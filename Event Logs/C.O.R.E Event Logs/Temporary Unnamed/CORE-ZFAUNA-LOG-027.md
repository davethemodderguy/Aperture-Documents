# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-027**

### **NEW FAUNA ENTRY — MART**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – FAUNA / INTERNAL RECORD

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

At approximately **07:18**, Security cameras recorded a large owl landing on the balcony outside Room N-07.

Rather than behaving cautiously, the owl calmly pecked on the glass door three times.

Taiga, who had been eating breakfast with the others, immediately looked up.

Without hesitation, she rushed to the balcony and slid the door open.

The owl flew directly onto her shoulder.

The two quietly stared at each other for several seconds.

Then Taiga smiled.

> "...Mart."

The owl softly hooted in response.

---

# **I. IDENTIFICATION**

According to Taiga, the owl had been her companion long before she arrived at the Blacksite.

Following the death of her mother and Taiga's escape into the Icelandic wilderness, the pair had become separated.

Mart had apparently spent months searching the surrounding region before eventually locating the Blacksite.

How he managed to find such a heavily concealed facility remains unknown.

Security jokingly suggested promoting him to Reconnaissance.

---

# **II. DESCRIPTION**

**Name:** Mart

**Species:** Owl

**Temperament:** Exceptionally calm and friendly

**Behavior:**

- Frequently perches on Taiga's shoulders.
    
- Enjoys quietly observing people from elevated locations.
    
- Rarely vocalizes unless greeting Taiga.
    
- Displays remarkable loyalty toward Taiga.
    
- Comfortable around personnel after only a short period of observation.
    

Unlike most wild owls, Mart shows almost no fear of humans or anomalous entities.

---

# **III. LEO**

One concern arose almost immediately.

Leo.

Personnel expected at least some territorial behavior between a bird of prey and a very small snake.

Instead...

Leo remained asleep.

Mart looked at the sleeping banana snake.

Tilted his head once.

Then calmly settled onto Taiga's opposite shoulder.

That was the end of it.

Several hours later, personnel observed Leo sleeping while loosely wrapped around Taiga's scarf.

Mart stood less than twenty centimeters away, quietly preening his feathers.

Neither appeared bothered by the other's presence.

---

## **Behavioral Note**

Veterinary staff have observed no signs of aggression between the two animals.

Mart has never attempted to attack Leo.

Leo has never shown fear of Mart.

The two simply coexist peacefully.

One veterinarian summarized the situation as:

> "...Honestly, this is much less paperwork."

---

# **IV. DAILY LIFE**

Mart has integrated into Room N-07 with surprising ease.

He can frequently be found:

- Resting on Taiga's shoulder while she walks through the Blacksite.
    
- Watching snowfall from balconies.
    
- Perching atop bookshelves during quiet evenings.
    
- Sitting beside Taiga during meals.
    
- Waiting patiently outside laboratory doors while Taiga visits Neko.
    

Personnel have also noticed that Mart appears unusually attentive whenever Taiga seems upset.

Without prompting, he will quietly hop closer or rest on her shoulder until she relaxes.

# **VI. SECURITY NOTE**

Mart has demonstrated excellent recall and reliably returns to Taiga when called.

He has not interfered with Blacksite operations and has shown no aggressive behavior toward personnel or resident fauna.

Director Lucious has approved Mart as a permanent resident of the facility.

His access is limited only to areas already approved for civilian animals.

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, snow drifted gently across the Blacksite courtyard.

Taiga stood on the balcony overlooking the frozen landscape.

Mart rested quietly on her right shoulder.

Leo slept around her white scarf.

Aster leaned against the doorway with her whiteboard.

She wrote:

> **Your family keeps getting bigger.**

Taiga looked at the owl, then at the tiny sleeping snake.

She smiled.

> "...Yeah."

Mart gave a soft, contented hoot.

Leo slept through it.
