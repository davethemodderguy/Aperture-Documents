# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-093**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION — MART'S TRANSFORMATION HABITS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following several weeks of observation, Residential Services has documented a clear pattern regarding Mart's use of his humanoid form.

Contrary to initial expectations, Mart overwhelmingly prefers remaining an owl.

He only transforms when he considers it genuinely necessary.

---

## **DOCUMENTED REASONS FOR TRANSFORMING**

### **1. To Speak**

Although Mart is capable of communicating through hoots, body language, and Taiga's surprisingly accurate translations, he occasionally prefers to speak directly.

These moments are usually reserved for important conversations.

Examples include:

- Thanking someone personally.
    
- Comforting Taiga.
    
- Asking Astral questions.
    
- Speaking with Director Lucious.
    
- Expressing something that cannot easily be conveyed through owl behavior.
    

Even then, he tends to keep his sentences very short.

---

### **2. To Eat**

Mart finds eating in humanoid form considerably easier for certain foods.

He especially enjoys:

- Warm soups.
    
- Bread.
    
- Rice.
    
- Chicken.
    
- Fruits.
    

After finishing his meal, he usually thanks whoever prepared it.

Then, within seconds...

White feathers drift into the air.

The owl returns.

---

### **3. To Sleep**

Perhaps the biggest surprise to Medical was Mart's preferred sleeping arrangement.

Although he naps almost anywhere as an owl...

When it's time for a proper night's sleep, he transforms into his humanoid form.

According to Astral:

> "...He's always slept better like that."

Mart himself explained it simply.

> "...Blankets..."

A pause.

> "...Are nice."

No one argued.

---

## **BEDTIME ROUTINE**

Every evening follows almost exactly the same pattern.

Mart lands on Taiga's shoulder.

They return to Room N-07 together.

He quietly transforms beside his bed.

Changes into comfortable pajamas.

Says goodnight to everyone.

Climbs beneath several blankets.

Falls asleep within minutes.

By morning...

He is usually an owl again before anyone else wakes up.

### **Closing Observation**

Late that night, the lights in Room N-07 gradually went out.

One by one, everyone wished each other goodnight.

Mart quietly transformed into his human form.

He climbed beneath his blanket.

Pulled it up to his chin.

Looked toward Taiga across the room.

> "...Goodnight... sister."

Taiga smiled warmly.

> "...Goodnight, Mart."

Within moments...

The little boy was asleep.

And by sunrise...

A small owl was once again perched peacefully on the headboard, ready for another day.
