# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-077**

### **ANOMALOUS UPDATE — EXPANDING APPARITION CONTACT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – DIRECTORATE / MEDICAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Over the past several days, the anomalous phenomenon surrounding Taiga's mother has changed.

Where previously only Neko could perceive and converse with the apparition, two additional individuals have independently reported hearing her voice during separate manifestations.

Confirmed listeners:

- Neko
    
- Miss Neko
    
- Star
    

All three accounts were recorded independently before comparison.

The descriptions of the apparition, her demeanor, and the conversations were consistent.

Research has confirmed that the phenomenon is expanding, though the reason remains unknown.

---

## **OBSERVATION**

The apparition remains non-hostile.

Her first concern during every manifestation continues to be Taiga.

She has never asked about herself.

She has never asked why she remains here.

Instead, her questions remain painfully ordinary.

> "...Did she eat today?"

> "...Is she sleeping?"

> "...Is she still hiding?"

During one manifestation, Star quietly answered,

> "...She's still hiding..."

The apparition lowered her head.

> "...I was afraid of that."

---

## **THE DECISION**

After consultation with Director Lucious and Medical, Neko proposed a carefully controlled attempt to tell Taiga the truth.

Not to pressure her.

Not to lure her back.

Only to let her know that her mother had never truly stopped watching over her.

Medical expressed concerns but agreed that, if handled gently, it might help restore some of Taiga's hope.

---

## **THE ENCOUNTER**

Neko eventually found Taiga sitting alone on a quiet observation platform overlooking the snow.

He approached slowly.

She noticed him immediately.

"...Hi."

"...Hi."

Neither spoke for a while.

Finally, Neko took a seat several meters away.

"...Taiga..."

"...There's something I need to tell you."

She remained silent.

He continued.

"...Your mom..."

"...She's here."

No reaction.

"...We've been talking to her."

"...She worries about you every day."

"...She asked us to find you."

"...She asked us to tell you that—"

Taiga stood.

She didn't look angry.

She looked tired.

Very tired.

"...Please..."

Her voice barely rose above a whisper.

"...Don't."

Neko blinked.

"...Taiga—"

She shook her head.

"...Don't lie to me..."

"...Please..."

Another step backward.

"...You're just trying to get me to come back."

"...I know..."

"...I know everyone misses me..."

Her eyes filled with tears.

"...But don't use my mom..."

"...Please..."

Neko's expression fell.

"...Taiga..."

She was already walking away.

"...I'm sorry..."

"...I can't believe that..."

She disappeared around the corner before he could say another word.

---

## **AFTERMATH**

For several minutes, Neko remained where he was.

He didn't move.

He didn't call after her.

When he finally returned to Room N-07, Star immediately knew how the conversation had ended.

"...She didn't believe you..."

Neko quietly nodded.

"...No."

"...She thought I was baiting her back."

The room fell silent.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Neko

Later that evening, Neko sat alone in the workshop.

Looking down at his hands, he sighed.

> "...I wanted to help."

Another long silence.

> "...I made it worse."

---

### Miss Neko

Miss Neko placed a hand on his shoulder.

> "...You didn't lie."

> "...She just isn't ready to believe something that hopeful."

---

### Star

Star cried quietly after hearing what had happened.

> "...If I were her..."

She wiped her eyes.

> "...I don't know if I'd believe it either..."

---

### Taiga's Mother

The apparition appeared again later that night.

She listened as Neko explained what had happened.

She did not become angry.

She simply closed her eyes.

> "...She's protecting herself."

A pause.

> "...If believing me means risking having hope..."

> "...I understand why she's afraid."

She looked toward the corridor where Taiga had disappeared hours earlier.

> "...Don't stop looking for her."

---

### Director Lucious

After reading the report, Director Lucious spoke quietly.

> "...No one is to pressure Taiga into believing."

He closed the file.

> "...Trust cannot be argued into existence."

---

### Nightshade

After hearing the outcome, Nightshade remarked,

> "...Trauma teaches people that impossible things are traps."

> "...To Taiga..."

> "...A miracle sounded too much like another heartbreak."

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical concludes that the failed disclosure was not the result of dishonesty, but of Taiga's severely diminished ability to trust hopeful information concerning her mother.

Further attempts at direct persuasion are suspended.

Any future disclosure should occur only if Taiga is emotionally prepared and able to evaluate the experience without feeling manipulated.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Later that night, the apparition stood quietly in Room N-07.

Star and Miss Neko could now hear her.

No one spoke for a long time.

Finally, she looked toward Taiga's untouched bed.

Her voice was barely audible.

> "...It's alright, sweetheart..."

> "...You don't have to believe it today."

> "...I'll still be here tomorrow."

No one in the room replied.

They simply hoped that one day...

Taiga would hear those words for herself.
