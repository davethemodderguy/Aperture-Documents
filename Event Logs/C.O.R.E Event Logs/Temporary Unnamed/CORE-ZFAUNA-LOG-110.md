# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-110**

### **ONGOING MISSING PERSON REPORT — MART SUNBRINGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – ACTIVE SEARCH

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

**Mart Sunbringer has not returned.**

Hours became days.

Days became weeks.

Despite continuous search efforts throughout the Blacksite's surrounding territory, there have been **no confirmed sightings**.

No naturally shed feathers.

No traces of wormhole activity.

No messages.

Nothing.

The official search remains active.

---

## **RESIDENTIAL STATUS**

Life inside Residential Wing N-07 has continued.

Quietly.

The absence of one resident has changed the atmosphere more than anyone expected.

Mart's chair at the dining table remains empty.

No one has moved it.

His room is cleaned regularly, but nothing has been rearranged.

His books remain exactly where he left them.

The bedside lamp is still turned on every evening.

No one has been instructed to do this.

It simply... keeps happening.

---

## **TAIGA SUNBRINGER**

Taiga has resumed her normal routine.

She smiles.

She trains.

She eats.

She talks with everyone.

But whenever she passes Mart's room...

She slows.

Sometimes she quietly opens the door.

Looks inside.

Then closes it again.

Without saying a word.

---

## **ASTRAL SUNBRINGER**

Astral continues preparing enough food for three members of the Sunbringer family.

Every meal.

Every day.

One serving inevitably goes untouched.

She still prepares it anyway.

No one has asked her why.

No one needs to.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIUS**

Director Lucious has not suspended the search.

Search teams continue rotating patrols beyond the perimeter.

The standing order remains unchanged.

> "If you find him..."

> "...Bring him home."

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, Taiga quietly entered Mart's room.

Everything was exactly as he had left it.

She walked over to the bookshelf.

Carefully dusted the covers.

Straightened one that had tilted slightly.

Then sat on the edge of his bed.

For a long time, she simply looked at the empty room.

Finally, she whispered,

> "...You said you'd always come home."

The room offered no reply.

Outside, snow continued to fall against the window.

Inside...

His lamp remained on.

Waiting.
