# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-074**

### **MEDICAL OBSERVATION — AZURE RUNE & RECURRING "RED EYE" REFERENCES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – MEDICAL / DIRECTORATE

**Notice:** This report concerns Taiga's ongoing psychological health. Any intervention must prioritize her safety, dignity, and recovery.

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Medical Services has documented that Taiga continues to make recurring references to what she calls her **"red eye."**

The phrase appears repeatedly in her journal fragments and has occasionally been heard during periods of emotional distress.

Medical currently interprets these references as being associated with her fear of entering the previously documented dissociative defensive state.

There is no evidence that Taiga desires this state.

Rather, all available observations indicate that she fears it deeply.

---

## **THE AZURE RUNE**

Several weeks after the appearance of the glowing azure rune on Taiga's back, Medical personnel made an unexpected observation.

During an acute psychological crisis, Taiga appeared to attempt to act against herself.

Before any physical injury occurred, the rune activated.

Witnesses reported that the markings briefly emitted a soft blue light.

Taiga immediately froze in place, as though restrained by an unseen force.

Moments later, the light faded.

No physical injuries were observed.

Medical has since documented multiple occasions where the rune appeared to intervene in similar circumstances.

The mechanism remains unknown.

---

## **WORKING THEORY**

Researchers have proposed a provisional hypothesis.

The rune may function as a **protective anomalous safeguard**, preventing Taiga from causing serious harm to herself during periods of overwhelming emotional distress.

At present, there is insufficient evidence to determine:

- Who or what maintains the rune.
    
- Whether it possesses independent decision-making capability.
    
- Whether its activation is entirely automatic.
    

Further study is ongoing.

---

## **RELATED OBSERVATION**

Another theory has emerged following repeated analysis of the rune's behavior.

Several researchers have suggested that the protection it provides resembles something deliberately created by someone who understood Taiga intimately.

Given existing records regarding the handcrafted restrainers made by Taiga's mother, a speculative hypothesis has been raised:

> The rune may represent a final protective measure left by Taiga's mother.

At this time, **there is no conclusive evidence supporting this hypothesis.**

Medical and Research have classified it as an **unverified working theory**.

No operational decisions are to be based on this assumption until additional evidence is obtained.
