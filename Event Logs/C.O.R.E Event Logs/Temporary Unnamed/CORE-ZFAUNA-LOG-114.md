# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-114**

### **SEARCH UPDATE — MART'S RETURN / SECONDARY DEPARTURE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – ACTIVE SEARCH

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

At **05:48**, a motion sensor in Residential Wing N-07 detected movement outside the Sunbringer residence.

Responding personnel initially believed it to be a false alarm.

It wasn't.

**Mart Sunbringer had returned.**

---

## **THE RETURN**

The front door was already open when Astral reached the hallway.

A small white owl stood just inside the entrance.

His feathers were dirty.

Several were missing.

He appeared exhausted.

The moment he saw Astral...

He transformed.

The little white-haired boy standing before her looked thinner than when he had disappeared.

Dark circles sat beneath his blue eyes.

Before Astral could say a word...

She wrapped him in the tightest hug she had ever given him.

She cried.

Not quietly.

Not politely.

She cried like a mother who had just found one of her children.

> "...You're alive..."

Mart slowly returned the embrace.

His voice was barely audible.

> "...Sorry..."

---

## **THE FIRST QUESTION**

Only after several minutes did Mart look around the room.

His eyes searched every corner.

Then he asked,

> "...Where's Taiga?"

No one answered immediately.

Mart noticed the silence.

He looked toward the hallway leading to Taiga's room.

The door was open.

The room...

Was empty.

He looked back at Astral.

"...Mom?"

Astral's expression was enough.

Mart understood.

---

## **THE DECISION**

Witnesses report that Mart stood perfectly still.

He neither cried nor shouted.

He simply lowered his head.

After several seconds, he quietly walked to Taiga's room.

Her bed was empty.

Her hoodie was gone.

Her journal was gone.

The room still smelled faintly of snow.

Mart picked up one of the feathers Taiga had once used as a bookmark.

He held it for a moment.

Then placed it back exactly where it had been.

He walked back into the hallway.

Looked at Astral.

Then at Director Lucious.

> "...I'm going to find her."

Astral immediately stepped forward.

> "...Mart, you've only just come home—"

He gently shook his head.

> "...She waited for me."

His voice broke for the first time.

> "...Now it's my turn."

---

## **SECONDARY DEPARTURE**

Before anyone could convince him to rest...

Mart transformed into his owl form.

He spread his wings.

Astral reached toward him.

> "...Please..."

Mart landed briefly on her shoulder.

He gently pressed his head against hers.

A silent goodbye.

Then he took flight.

He disappeared through the open doorway into the morning sky.

No one stopped him.

---

Astral remained standing in the doorway long after Mart vanished from sight.

This time...

She didn't call after him.

She knew why he had left.

> "...Bring your sister home..."

### **Closing Observation**

The security cameras recorded one final moment before Mart disappeared from view.

As he crossed over one of the Blacksite's older maintenance sectors, he slowed.

Below him, in a dusty corridor barely used anymore...

Were faint paw prints.

Small.

Fresh enough to still be visible.

Mart landed.

He gently touched one with the tip of a wing.

Then looked down the corridor.

Without hesitation...

He followed the trail into the darkness.

The camera never saw him again.
