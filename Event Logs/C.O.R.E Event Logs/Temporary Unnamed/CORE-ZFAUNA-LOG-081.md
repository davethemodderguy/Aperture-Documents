## **THE LAST PERSON**

Despite the gradual expansion of those capable of hearing her...

One person still cannot.

Taiga.

Research has no explanation as to why the phenomenon continues to spread among others while its intended recipient remains unable to perceive it.

The investigation remains ongoing.

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Since learning that Director Lucious legally adopted Taiga as his daughter, the apparition has been observed near his office more frequently.

She has expressed gratitude on multiple occasions.

During one manifestation, Director Lucious was working alone after normal hours when the familiar voice quietly spoke behind him.

> "...Director."

He looked up from his paperwork.

"...Good evening."

She smiled politely.

> "...Thank you."

He already knew what she meant.

> "...She's my daughter now."

The apparition lowered her head.

> "...I know."

After a brief silence, she added,

> "...Please..."

> "...Keep reminding her."

Director Lucious nodded.

> "...I will."

---

## **MART**

A notable development occurred during the latest manifestation.

Mart, Taiga's owl, was resting on the windowsill when the apparition appeared.

Unlike previous observations, Mart immediately turned toward her.

He flew across the room without hesitation.

Landing on the edge of the nearby desk, he tilted his head before giving a soft, familiar chirp.

The apparition smiled.

> "...Hello, Mart."

Mart responded with another quiet call before calmly settling beside her.

No signs of fear or confusion were observed.

Research concludes that Mart recognized the apparition immediately.

Whether through anomalous perception, memory, or another unknown mechanism remains undetermined.

### **Closing Observation**

Late that evening, Director Lucious remained in his office, finishing paperwork.

The apparition stood quietly by the window, watching snow drift across the Blacksite.

Mart rested peacefully on the back of a nearby chair.

For several minutes, none of them spoke.

Finally, the apparition looked toward the distant residential wing.

Her voice was almost lost beneath the sound of the wind.

> "...She's still looking for me..."

Director Lucious looked out at the same falling snow.

> "...And we're still looking for her."

The apparition smiled sadly.

> "...Then neither of us will stop."

Outside...

Somewhere in the vast, quiet corridors of the Blacksite...

Taiga continued walking.

Unaware that, little by little...

The distance between herself and her mother's voice was growing smaller.
