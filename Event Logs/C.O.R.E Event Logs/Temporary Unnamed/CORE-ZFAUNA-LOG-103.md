# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-103**

### **RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION — A MOTHER AGAIN**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Since regaining a permanent physical form, Astral Sunbringer has naturally resumed her role as a mother.

Although both Taiga and Mart are capable of caring for themselves, Astral continues to look after them in countless small, everyday ways.

She has never asked whether they still need her.

She simply acts like their mother.

And neither of them has ever objected.

---

## **TAIGA**

Astral has learned that Taiga throws herself into almost everything with boundless enthusiasm.

This also means Taiga regularly forgets things.

Astral can often be heard saying:

> "Taiga, drink some water."

> "Slow down while you're eating."

> "Zip your hoodie up, you'll catch a chill."

> "You've been Air Jumping for an hour. Come rest."

Taiga's response is almost always the same.

> "...Okay, Mom."

Sometimes...

Five minutes later...

Astral has to remind her again.

---

## **MART**

Looking after Mart is very different.

He rarely causes trouble.

Instead...

He quietly forgets to look after himself.

Astral often finds him:

- Reading long after bedtime.
    
- Skipping breaks because he's focused on a book.
    
- Standing at a window watching snowfall for nearly an hour.
    
- Sitting so still that everyone assumes he's asleep.
    

She'll gently close his book.

> "Time for bed."

Or place a warm meal beside him.

> "Eat before it gets cold."

Mart simply smiles.

> "...Thank you, Mom."

---

## **THE SUNBRINGER HOUSEHOLD**

It has become common to see Astral instinctively checking on both children throughout the day.

She knows where Taiga usually is.

She knows where Mart usually hides with a book.

If one of them disappears for too long...

She's already looking before anyone else notices.

Not because she doesn't trust them.

Because she's their mother.

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, Astral walked through the residential wing to check on her children.

She found Taiga asleep on the couch, a notebook resting on her chest.

Mart had fallen asleep in his human form beside her, a book still open in his lap.

Astral smiled.

She carefully removed the notebook.

Closed Mart's book with a bookmark tucked inside.

Pulled a blanket over both of them.

Then kissed each of them gently on the forehead.

Neither woke up.

As she quietly turned off the lamp, she whispered to herself with a contented smile,

> "...Goodnight, my little snow leopard."

She looked at Mart.

> "...Goodnight, my little owl."

For the first time in a long time...

Astral wasn't mourning the family she had lost.

She was simply taking care of the family she had found again.
