# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-028**

### **ABILITY CONFIRMATION — INTERSPECIES COMMUNICATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – N.O.S.T. / BEHAVIORAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Although Taiga's ability to communicate with animals had been documented shortly after her arrival at the Blacksite, recent observations involving Mart have confirmed the extent of this ability.

Taiga is not merely interpreting animal behavior.

She is capable of holding complete, meaningful conversations with animals.

---

# **I. INITIAL OBSERVATION**

Personnel first became suspicious when they noticed Taiga apparently having lengthy discussions with Mart while standing on the residential balcony.

To everyone else, the exchange consisted only of soft hoots and quiet chirps from the owl.

Taiga, however, responded naturally.

She laughed.

She nodded.

She occasionally looked surprised.

The "conversation" lasted nearly fifteen minutes.

Afterward, Bao asked the obvious question.

> "...What were you two talking about?"

Taiga answered as though it were completely normal.

> "...He was telling me about a family of foxes living near the southern cliffs."

Silence.

> "...And he thinks one of them is stealing fish from the coast."

---

# **II. VERIFICATION**

To confirm the ability, researchers conducted several harmless observational tests.

Personnel asked Mart simple questions while Taiga remained outside the room.

Mart responded only with ordinary owl vocalizations.

When Taiga returned, she listened for a few moments before translating.

Her responses consistently matched Mart's demonstrated behavior during the tests.

Medical and N.O.S.T. concluded that Taiga possesses genuine interspecies communication rather than simple intuition.

Exactly how the ability functions remains unknown.

---

# **III. MART**

Mart appears completely comfortable speaking with Taiga.

According to her, he is calm, patient, and surprisingly observant.

He frequently tells her about:

- Wildlife around the Blacksite.
    
- Weather changes before they become noticeable.
    
- New nests built by local birds.
    
- Animals wandering near the perimeter.
    

When asked what Mart thinks about humans, Taiga smiled.

> "...He says you're all very noisy."

No one argued with that assessment.

---

# **IV. LEO**

Personnel became curious whether Taiga also regularly spoke with Leo.

She nodded.

> "...Almost every day."

Neko looked interested.

> "...What's he usually say?"

Taiga listened as Leo let out a tiny sleepy sound without opening his eyes.

She waited.

Then translated.

> "...He's comfortable."

A pause.

> "...He's also wondering if anyone has tomatoes."

Everyone looked at the sleeping snake.

Neko slowly reached into the refrigerator.

Leo somehow woke up immediately.

---

# **V. OTHER ANIMALS**

Taiga has occasionally been observed quietly greeting local wildlife around the Blacksite.

Snowy birds.

Arctic foxes.

Hares.

Even passing seals along the frozen shoreline.

None appeared frightened by her presence.

Many approached voluntarily.

Director Lucious noted that this has proven unexpectedly useful for monitoring wildlife around the facility without disturbing the local ecosystem.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga's ability to communicate with animals has been fully confirmed.

She is capable of holding meaningful conversations with a wide variety of animal species, including her companions Mart and Leo.

The ability has become a valuable asset for both wildlife observation and the care of resident animals within the Blacksite.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Near sunset, Taiga stood outside in gently falling snow.

Mart rested on her shoulder.

Leo slept around her scarf.

A small arctic fox approached the balcony railing.

After a short exchange of quiet chirps, hoots, and soft words, the fox trotted away.

Neko, who had watched the entire interaction from inside, looked at Taiga.

> "...What did it want?"

Taiga smiled.

> "...It was asking if Leo was actually alive."

She glanced down at the tiny banana snake.

Leo was asleep.

Mart gave a single amused hoot.

Even Taiga had to admit...

It was a reasonable question.
