# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-097**

### **RESIDENTIAL INCIDENT — LUNCHTIME HAZARD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL (COMEDIC)

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

**Location:** Residential Cafeteria

At approximately 12:18, Taiga Sunbringer and Mart Sunbringer were observed eating lunch together without the rest of Room N-07 present.

Their meals were identical.

- Fresh garden salad.
    
- Tomatoes.
    
- Cucumbers.
    
- Carrots.
    
- Bell peppers.
    
- Grilled chicken.
    

Mart ate quietly and neatly, as usual.

Taiga...

Was significantly more enthusiastic.

---

## **THE INCIDENT**

Security footage shows Taiga happily eating her salad at an alarming pace.

Medical later noted that she appeared "exceptionally excited" for reasons unknown.

Approximately four minutes into lunch...

Taiga accidentally inhaled a piece of lettuce.

She immediately began choking.

Panicking, she wildly flailed both her arms and legs while trying to cough.

Unfortunately...

Mart's chair happened to be directly beside her.

Taiga's foot struck one of its legs.

The chair tipped forward.

Mart, still holding his fork, pitched headfirst toward the table.

**Thunk.**

His forehead collided with the tabletop.

Silence followed.

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical responded immediately.

**Taiga**

- Airway obstruction cleared after coughing.
    
- No lasting injury.
    
- Mild embarrassment.
    

**Mart**

- Minor bump to the forehead.
    
- No concussion.
    
- Ice pack recommended.
    
- Cleared for normal activity.
    

No further treatment was required.

---

## **AFTERMATH**

Taiga immediately burst into tears.

> "...Mart!"

> "...I'm sorry!"

Mart rubbed the growing bump on his forehead.

He looked at Taiga for several seconds.

Clearly annoyed.

Very clearly.

"..."

"...You..."

He sighed.

> "...Are very loud."

Taiga lowered her ears.

"...Sorry..."

Another long pause.

Mart accepted the apology with a small nod.

> "...Okay."

Seconds later, he was calmly eating his salad again.

Incident concluded.
