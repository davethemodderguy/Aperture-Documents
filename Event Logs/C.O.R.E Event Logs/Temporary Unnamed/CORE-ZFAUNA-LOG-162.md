# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-162**

### **OBSERVATIONAL REPORT — VOLUNTARY DISTANCING BETWEEN SIBLINGS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – RESIDENT WELFARE

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the incident documented in **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-161**, a noticeable change has been observed in the daily interactions between Taiga Sunbringer and Mart Sunbringer.

Neither sibling has argued.

Neither has expressed anger toward the other.

Instead...

They have quietly begun keeping their distance.

The separation appears to be voluntary on both sides.

---

## **OBSERVATIONS**

Before the incident, the siblings were rarely seen apart for extended periods.

Typical daily interactions included:

- Eating together.
    
- Walking through the Blacksite together.
    
- Visiting the shooting range.
    
- Reading in the library.
    
- Training together.
    
- Casual conversations throughout the day.
    

These routines have largely stopped.

Mart now quietly leaves an area if Taiga enters.

Taiga, upon noticing him, often turns around and walks away instead.

Neither action appears hostile.

Both simply avoid placing the other in an uncomfortable situation.

---

## **COMMON AREAS**

The dining hall has become noticeably quieter.

Mart now chooses a table near the window.

Taiga sits several tables away.

Occasionally they glance toward one another.

Neither speaks.

Once one finishes eating...

They leave.

---

The library shows similar behavior.

Mart reads in the eastern corner.

Taiga occupies the western side.

Neither disturbs the other.

---

## **OBSERVED INCIDENT**

**15:22**

Mart entered the courtyard carrying a book.

He stopped when he noticed Taiga already sitting beneath one of the trees.

She noticed him moments later.

For several seconds...

Neither moved.

Mart offered a small, polite smile.

Taiga returned it.

No words were exchanged.

Mart quietly turned around and walked toward another section of the courtyard.

Taiga lowered her head after he disappeared from view.

---

## **STAFF OBSERVATIONS**

Personnel report that both siblings appear noticeably quieter than usual.

Mart has become even more reserved.

Taiga has resumed eating less during several meals, though not to medically concerning levels.

Neither has spoken negatively about the other.

Whenever someone asks what happened...

Both answer almost identically.

Mart:

> "...It's better this way."

Taiga:

> "...I'm giving him space."

---

## **FAMILY RESPONSE**

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral has not attempted to force a reconciliation.

Instead, she continues spending time with each of them individually.

She later remarked during counseling:

> "...Distance can help people breathe."

Her expression became sad.

> "...But not forever."

---

> **"Trust cannot be ordered."**

Neither sibling moved it back.

Frost has begun alternating meal invitations so neither sibling feels pressured to attend the same gatherings.

She hopes to keep both socially engaged while respecting their current boundaries.

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star attempted to invite both siblings to another rooftop sunset.

Mart politely declined.

Taiga quietly said,

> "...Maybe another time."

The rooftop remained empty that evening.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Mart Sunbringer**

- Continues participating in daily activities.
    
- Avoids unnecessary encounters with Taiga.
    
- Emotional condition remains fragile.
    

**Taiga Sunbringer**

- Continues respecting Mart's personal space.
    
- Shows visible sadness following accidental encounters.
    
- Maintains hope for eventual reconciliation.
    

Psychological Services do not consider the current distance to be evidence of hostility.

Rather, both siblings appear to believe they are protecting the other from further emotional pain.

---

## **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, Security cameras captured a quiet moment in the residential hallway.

Mart stepped out of his room carrying a blanket.

At the opposite end of the corridor, Taiga emerged from hers with a cup of tea.

They noticed each other immediately.

For a few seconds...

They simply stood there.

Mart gave a small wave.

Taiga slowly returned it.

Neither moved closer.

Neither walked away immediately.

Eventually, they continued in opposite directions.

The hallway fell silent once more.

Two siblings...

Still caring about one another.

Still hurting.

And still believing that staying apart was the kinder choice.
