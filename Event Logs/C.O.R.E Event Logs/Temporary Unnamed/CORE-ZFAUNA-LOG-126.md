# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-126**

### **FIELD REPORT — FINAL RECORDED ENCOUNTER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – ACTIVE SEARCH / PSYCHOLOGICAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

Over the course of several weeks, Mart and Taiga continued to encounter one another by chance throughout the Blacksite.

Neither sibling appeared to intentionally seek the other.

Neither encounter lasted more than a few minutes.

Mart would leave.

Taiga would remain behind.

Each meeting ended the same way.

Until one final encounter.

No further confirmed sightings of Mart have been recorded since.

---

## **LOCATION**

**Sector:** Northern Maintenance Access — Service Tunnel 4A

**Time:** 18:42

The encounter was observed remotely by Security Camera M-4A.

Audio quality was sufficient to reconstruct the following exchange.

---

## **TRANSCRIPT**

Taiga rounded the corner first.

She froze.

Mart stood only a few meters away.

For a long moment...

Neither of them moved.

Taiga took a careful step forward.

> **Taiga:** "...Mart."

Mart lowered his eyes.

His expression was exhausted.

The faint glow in his blue eyes had dimmed.

After several seconds, he finally spoke.

> **Mart:** "Why do you keep hurting me?"

The question wasn't angry.

It wasn't loud.

It sounded like someone who no longer had the strength to ask it.

Taiga immediately shook her head.

Tears began to form.

> **Taiga:** "Mart... I'm sorry... please..."

Mart looked away.

His voice barely rose above a whisper.

> **Mart:** "Just leave me alone."

Taiga stepped forward again.

> **Taiga:** "No, Mart... please come back..."

Her voice broke.

> "...Mom's worried."

Mart closed his eyes.

A tear rolled silently down his cheek.

When he spoke again...

His voice was almost empty.

> **Mart:** "I'm unwanted."

Taiga answered immediately.

> **Taiga:** "No, Mart... you're wrong."

For the first time during the encounter...

Mart looked directly at her.

There was no anger in his eyes.

Only heartbreak.

He gave the faintest, saddest smile.

> **Mart:** "Thanks for letting me know sooner."

The words struck Taiga like a physical blow.

She reached toward him.

> **Taiga:** "Mart, please... come back."

Mart didn't answer.

Two wings of pure white light slowly unfolded behind him.

Snow stirred gently around his feet.

He looked at Taiga one final time.

As though trying to memorize her face.

Then...

Without another word...

He lifted into the evening sky.

Taiga ran after him.

She wasn't fast enough.

Within seconds...

He disappeared into the falling snow.

---

## **AFTERMATH**

Taiga remained standing in the snow long after Mart had vanished.

She did not call his name again.

She simply looked upward.

Eventually...

She whispered,

> "...I'll wait."

Search teams arrived several minutes later.

They found only Taiga.

No anomalous energy trail remained.

No feathers.

No footprints.

Nothing.
