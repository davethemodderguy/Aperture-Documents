# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-010**

### **MEDICAL & BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION — TAIGA**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – MEDICAL / VETERINARY

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

An unexpected medical observation has identified a previously unknown trauma response in Taiga.

The discovery occurred following a routine conversation with Neko regarding one of his personal weapons.

No injuries occurred during the incident.

Medical has since implemented several accommodations.

---

# **I. "WHAT'S THAT STICK?"**

Late in the afternoon, Taiga and Neko were returning from the courtyard after another light snowfall.

Taiga glanced toward the thick white pole slung across Neko's back.

She had seen it every day since arriving at the Blacksite.

Finally, curiosity won.

She pointed at it.

> "...Neko?"

> "What's that long stick slung on your back do?"

Neko smiled.

> "...Want to see?"

Taiga nodded enthusiastically.

---

# **II. THE DEMONSTRATION**

The pair moved to an empty testing area under Engineering supervision.

Neko removed the white pole from his back.

With a press of a concealed switch, the upper section unfolded.

Blue conduits illuminated.

The brilliant plasma scythe emerged with its familiar humming sound.

Taiga's ears perked up.

Rather than appearing frightened, she watched with fascination.

Neko explained the weapon's two configurations, showing how it could transform between its plasma scythe and plasma support-weapon forms.

No live firing was conducted.

Only the transformation mechanisms were demonstrated.

When finished, Taiga smiled.

> "...That's really cool."

Neko folded the weapon back into its compact staff form and slung it over his shoulder.

The two began walking back toward the residential wing.

---

# **III. THE GUNFIRE**

As they passed near one of the Blacksite's conventional firearms ranges, a security team began a scheduled qualification exercise.

A burst of unsuppressed rifle fire echoed through the corridor.

The effect on Taiga was immediate.

She froze.

Her ears flattened.

She instinctively dropped to the floor, covering her head with both hands.

Within seconds she had begun crying uncontrollably.

Neko immediately knelt beside her.

He quietly reassured her while nearby personnel halted the training exercise.

Medical responded within moments.

Taiga remained physically unharmed, but was visibly distressed.

---

# **IV. MEDICAL EVALUATION**

Following examination and a private conversation with Taiga, Medical reached a conclusion.

The sound of authentic ballistic firearms strongly resembled the gunfire she had heard when hunters killed her mother.

That specific sound had become deeply associated with the traumatic event.

Interestingly, controlled testing revealed a very specific trigger pattern.

Taiga showed **no significant distress** when exposed to:

- Suppressed firearms.
    
- Plasma weapon discharges.
    
- Directed-energy weapons.
    
- Simulated electronic training sounds.
    

However, recordings and live demonstrations of conventional unsuppressed firearms consistently produced an immediate trauma response.

Medical concluded that the response was linked to the distinctive acoustic signature of traditional gunfire rather than weapons in general.

---

# **V. ACCOMMODATIONS**

The following measures have been adopted:

- Whenever practical, personnel will avoid using unsuppressed firearms near Taiga.
    
- Live-fire qualification schedules will be communicated to residential staff in advance.
    
- If Taiga unexpectedly encounters conventional gunfire, nearby personnel should calmly guide her away from the area and allow her time to recover.
    
- She is not to be pressured into approaching firearm ranges.
    

Medical notes that familiar companions, particularly Aster, Neko, and Miss Neko, help her regain a sense of safety more quickly after an episode.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Diagnosis:** Trauma response associated with the sound of authentic unsuppressed firearms.

**Trigger:** Conventional ballistic gunfire.

**Not a Trigger:**

- Suppressed firearms.
    
- Plasma weapon discharges.
    
- Energy weapons.
    

Taiga continues to participate normally in daily Blacksite life, with simple accommodations in place to reduce unexpected exposure to the sounds that remind her of the loss of her mother.

---

### **Closing Observation**

The following morning, Taiga and Neko walked the new route back from Engineering.

It was a little longer.

But it bypassed the shooting range entirely.

Outside, fresh Icelandic snow fell quietly.

Taiga smiled as she lifted a handful of snow into the air, letting the flakes swirl around Aster and Leo.

Neko noticed.

Without saying anything, he kept to the quieter path.
