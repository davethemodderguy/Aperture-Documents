# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-161**

### **INCIDENT REPORT — FAMILIAL ADDRESS REGRESSION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – PSYCHOLOGICAL / CONFIDENTIAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

Psychological Services have documented an unexpected behavioral development involving Mart Sunbringer following a period of severe sleep deprivation and an exceptionally distressing nightmare.

After awakening from the episode and while being comforted by Taiga, Mart addressed her by her given name rather than the familial title he had consistently used throughout his life.

For as long as personnel have known the siblings, Mart had always referred to Taiga as **"Big Sis."**

Only once before had he called her **"Taiga."**

That occasion occurred during the period in which the siblings had become estranged following their documented argument.

The recurrence of that behavior has raised significant psychological concern.

---

## **OBSERVED INCIDENT**

**Time:** 05:31

Mart was awakened by another severe nightmare.

Medical staff noted elevated breathing and visible distress.

Taiga arrived shortly afterward and remained with him until he became responsive.

After several minutes of silence, she quietly asked,

> **Taiga:** "...Feeling any better?"

Mart looked at her.

His expression was distant, exhausted.

He answered almost automatically.

> **"...Thanks... Taiga."**

The room fell silent.

Taiga froze.

She stared at him without speaking.

Mart blinked.

For a brief moment, neither of them moved.

Then realization crossed Mart's face.

His expression shifted from confusion...

To horror.

He covered his mouth.

> "...I..."

He took a step backward.

Another.

> "...I'm sorry..."

Without waiting for a response, Mart turned and ran from the room.

Taiga did not follow.

She remained exactly where she was.

Speechless.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL NOTE**

When interviewed later that day, Taiga quietly stated:

> "...He only calls me 'Taiga' when..."

She could not finish the sentence.

The previous documented occurrence had been during the period in which Mart believed he no longer deserved to be her younger brother.

For Taiga, hearing it again carried the same emotional weight.

She later remarked:

> "...It felt like... I'd lost my little brother all over again."

---

## **CLINICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services do **not** believe Mart consciously intended to distance himself from Taiga.

Instead, clinicians believe the change occurred involuntarily.

Repeated nightmares involving abandonment, rejection, and distorted recollections appear to have influenced automatic patterns of speech immediately after waking.

One psychologist noted:

> **"Names associated with close relationships can become emotionally disrupted after repeated traumatic dream exposure."**

> **"This does not necessarily reflect Mart's conscious feelings toward Taiga."**

Rather, it reflects the extent to which his nightmares have begun interfering with deeply ingrained emotional associations.

His subconscious appears to have linked the word **"Big Sis"** with fear of loss, conflict, and separation.

The result was an involuntary substitution immediately after waking.

---

## **FOLLOW-UP**

Mart could not be located for the remainder of the day.

Unlike previous incidents, he did not transform into his owl form.

He simply avoided populated areas.

Taiga also withdrew from common activities.

Neither sibling attempted to contact the other.

Staff respected their privacy while maintaining routine welfare checks.

---

## **Closing Observation**

Late that evening, Taiga passed Mart's bedroom.

She paused outside the closed door.

Her hand rested against the wood for several seconds.

She considered knocking.

Instead, she quietly whispered through the door,

> "...You'll always be my little brother..."

There was no reply.

Only silence from the other side.

Whether Mart had fallen asleep...

Or whether he had simply heard every word without answering...

No one could say.
