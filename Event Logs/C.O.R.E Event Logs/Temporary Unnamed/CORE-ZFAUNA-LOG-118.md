# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-118**

### **RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION — THE SUNBRINGER SIBLINGS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – RESIDENTIAL / BEHAVIORAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following Mart Sunbringer's recovery, staff have documented a new routine between the Sunbringer siblings.

Although Taiga has regained the ability to glide, air jump, and move normally in her snow leopard form...

She often chooses not to.

Instead...

Mart carries her.

---

## **OBSERVATION**

Despite being smaller than Taiga's leopard form appears to warrant, Mart somehow carries her with surprising ease.

Whether this is due to anomalous physiology, refined technique, or simply determination remains unknown.

He has been observed carrying her:

- Between the infirmary and residential wing.
    
- To meals.
    
- Through the gardens.
    
- During evening walks.
    
- To Whitefall Lake.
    
- Back to bed after she falls asleep.
    

Taiga is fully capable of walking.

She simply seems happiest when she doesn't have to.

---

## **TAIGA'S RESPONSE**

Medical staff initially worried that Taiga was refusing to move due to lingering weakness.

This assessment proved incorrect.

If food is involved...

She walks.

If Star wants to play...

She runs.

If Mart needs help...

She sprints without hesitation.

For everything else...

She simply looks at Mart.

Mart already knows what that look means.

Without a word, he kneels.

Taiga climbs into his arms.

The journey continues.

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services believes this behavior is not caused by dependency.

Rather, it appears to be a response to the siblings' recent separation.

Physical closeness provides reassurance to both of them.

Neither sibling has objected.

Medical therefore considers the behavior healthy and sees no reason to discourage it.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late in the evening, Mart was walking back toward the residential wing with Taiga comfortably resting in his arms.

Halfway there, Director Lucious noticed something.

Taiga's eyes had closed.

She had fallen asleep.

Mart slowed his pace even further to avoid waking her.

As he passed, Director Lucious quietly asked,

> "...Need any help?"

Mart smiled faintly and adjusted his grip.

> "...I'm okay."

He looked down at his sleeping sister.

> "...She carried me when I couldn't stand."

He continued down the hallway.

> "...Now it's my turn."

The corridor remained silent, save for the soft rhythm of Taiga's breathing as she slept safely in her brother's arms.
