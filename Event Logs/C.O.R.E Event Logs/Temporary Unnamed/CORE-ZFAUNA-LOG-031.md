# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-031**

### **MINOR FAUNA INCIDENT — UNEXPECTED RECOVERY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – MEDICAL / VETERINARY

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

Several days after Taiga's unusually persistent drowsiness had become the subject of countless jokes throughout Room N-07, Medical documented an equally unexpected development.

It began with Leo.

As usual.

---

## **THE INCIDENT**

Late in the afternoon, Taiga settled onto the heated rug in Room N-07, intending to read a book before taking what she described as "a five-minute nap."

Leo, who had been sleeping beneath the blanket, was completely hidden from view.

As Taiga leaned backward to sit down...

Leo suddenly woke up.

The tiny banana snake looked up.

Realized an entire snow leopard girl was about to sit on him.

Panicking, he instinctively bit the nearest thing he could reach.

Taiga's hand.

"...Ow!"

She immediately shifted her weight.

Leo wriggled free and hurried behind a pillow, looking thoroughly startled.

According to Mart, who witnessed the entire event:

> "He thought he was about to become a pancake."

---

## **MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

Medical was called once again out of an abundance of caution.

Personnel expected another wave of overwhelming sleepiness.

Instead...

The exact opposite occurred.

Within minutes, Taiga stopped yawning.

Her posture straightened.

Her eyes became noticeably more alert.

She looked around the room.

Then blinked.

"...Wait..."

"...Why do I suddenly feel... awake?"

Over the next hour, Medical noted:

- Normal alertness returned.
    
- Reaction times returned to baseline.
    
- Environmental awareness improved significantly.
    
- Excessive daytime sleepiness disappeared.
    

After repeated examinations, Medical concluded that whatever lingering effect had remained from the previous bite had somehow been neutralized by the second accidental bite.

The precise biological mechanism remains unknown.

## **MEDICAL CONCLUSION**

Medical has officially confirmed that Taiga's abnormal drowsiness has resolved.

No further symptoms have been observed.

Leo's venom continues to display unusual but harmless properties that remain poorly understood.

Additional study has been approved, provided Leo is not intentionally provoked or made uncomfortable.

---

### **Closing Observation**

That evening, Taiga walked through the Blacksite with her usual energy restored.

She noticed details she had been absent-mindedly overlooking for days.

She waved to passing personnel.

She helped Aster carry supplies.

She even caught Neko just before he absentmindedly walked into a freshly painted wall.

Neko stopped.

Looked at the wet paint.

Then at Taiga.

> "...Good to have you back."

Taiga smiled.

Leo, asleep around her shoulder, quietly twitched in his dreams—completely unaware that he had accidentally solved the very problem he had accidentally started.
