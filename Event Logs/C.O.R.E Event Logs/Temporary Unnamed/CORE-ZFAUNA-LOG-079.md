# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-079**

### **INTERPERSONAL STATUS UPDATE — BREAKDOWN OF TRUST**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – MEDICAL / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Medical Services has documented a significant deterioration in the relationship between Taiga and Neko.

Following the unsuccessful attempt to tell Taiga about her mother's continued presence, Taiga no longer interprets Neko's actions as attempts to help.

Instead, she believes he exploited the most painful part of her life to persuade her to return to Room N-07.

From Taiga's perspective, the event confirmed a fear she already carried:

> _"If someone says exactly what I want to hear... it's probably because they want something from me."_

Medical emphasizes that this conclusion is the result of Taiga's current psychological state and loss of trust, not evidence that Neko acted with malicious intent.

---

## **OBSERVED BEHAVIOR**

Whenever Taiga notices Neko approaching:

- She quietly leaves before he reaches her.
    
- She avoids eye contact.
    
- She declines conversation.
    
- She refuses gifts or assistance.
    
- She chooses alternate routes through the Blacksite.
    

On one occasion, Neko simply placed a fresh salad on a table and walked away without saying a word.

Security footage later showed Taiga waiting until he was completely gone before taking the meal.

She never looked in the direction he had left.

---

## **RECOVERED JOURNAL FRAGMENT**

A recently recovered page contained a single paragraph.

> _"...I trusted him..."_

> _"...Then he used Mom..."_

> _"...I can't let that happen again."_

Medical notes that this entry reflects Taiga's perception of events rather than an objective account of Neko's intentions.

---

### Taiga's Mother

During her next manifestation, she listened as Neko explained what had happened.

She did not accuse him.

She did not defend him.

She simply sighed.

> "...She's frightened."

After a pause, she added,

> "...Fear can make love look like danger."

She looked toward Neko.

> "...Don't answer anger with anger."

> "...She needs time."

---

### Director Lucious

After reviewing Medical's report, Director Lucious issued a simple instruction.

> "No one is to force reconciliation."

> "If trust returns..."

> "It must be because Taiga chooses it."

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical considers the current estrangement to be a consequence of unresolved trauma and profound mistrust following the failed disclosure.

Attempts to argue Taiga out of her beliefs are not recommended.

For now, staff are instructed to respect her boundaries while continuing to ensure she has access to food, shelter, medical care, and a safe environment.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Another quiet evening settled over the Blacksite.

Neko walked down a familiar corridor.

At the far end, Taiga appeared for only a moment.

Their eyes met.

Before either of them spoke...

Taiga turned into another hallway and disappeared from sight.

Neko made no attempt to follow.

He simply stood there for a few seconds before continuing in the opposite direction.

Neither of them wanted the distance between them.

But for now...

Neither knew how to cross it.
