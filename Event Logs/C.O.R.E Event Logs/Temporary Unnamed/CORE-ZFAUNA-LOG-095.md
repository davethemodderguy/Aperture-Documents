# **ABILITY UPDATE — "AIR JUMP"**

Research has confirmed that Taiga's previously documented **Double Jump** ability has evolved.

The ability has been redesignated:

## **Air Jump**

Rather than being limited to a single midair propulsion, Taiga can now generate multiple successive bursts of compressed air while airborne.

Each burst briefly redirects her momentum, allowing her to:

- Chain multiple aerial jumps.
    
- Change direction while airborne.
    
- Cross large gaps without landing.
    
- Recover from falls by redirecting herself toward stable ground.
    

Testing suggests the ability functions independently of nearby surfaces.

The exact upper limit of consecutive jumps has not yet been determined.

Research notes that Taiga instinctively stops before showing signs of exhaustion, making the true limit unknown.

---

# **MEDICAL OBSERVATION — LEFT ARM SCAR**

Medical examinations have documented a large, fully healed scar extending along Taiga's left forearm.

The scar is almost always concealed beneath the long sleeves of her oversized hoodie.

When asked about it, Taiga simply looks at it for a few moments before answering,

> "...I don't remember."

Astral was asked separately.

Her response was identical.

> "...Neither do I."

Medical records predating Taiga's rescue are unavailable.

The scar's origin therefore remains officially **unknown**.

---

# **MART'S REACTION**

Although Taiga and Astral have no memory of the injury...

Mart does.

The first time someone casually asked where the scar came from, Mart froze.

His breathing became noticeably faster.

He stared at the scar for several seconds.

Then...

Without saying a single word...

He transformed into an owl and immediately flew away.

Subsequent attempts to discuss the scar have produced similar reactions.

When in his humanoid form, the response is even more pronounced.

Documented behaviors include:

- Sudden silence.
    
- Trembling hands.
    
- Avoiding eye contact.
    
- Backing away from the conversation.
    
- Running from the room.
    
- Transforming mid-stride and flying elsewhere.
    

Medical believes the scar is associated with a deeply traumatic memory for Mart.

Importantly, no evidence suggests he currently poses any danger.

Rather, his behavior is consistent with overwhelming guilt and fear.

# **LEOPARD FORM OBSERVATION**

Medical has also documented that Taiga has begun using her snow leopard form significantly more often.

Unlike earlier observations, these transformations no longer appear to be driven solely by fear or overwhelming comfort.

Instead, Taiga now seems to choose the form naturally.

Common observations include:

- Sleeping curled up in leopard form.
    
- Walking through the residential gardens as a snow leopard.
    
- Resting beside Leo during quiet afternoons.
    
- Allowing children such as Star and Aster to gently pet her soft fur.
    
- Following Astral through the residential wing while quietly purring.
    

Medical believes the increased use of the form reflects growing confidence in expressing this part of her identity.

### **Closing Observation**

One quiet evening, Taiga lay curled up in her snow leopard form beneath a tree in the Blacksite courtyard.

Mart, in his human form, sat beside her with a book resting in his lap.

The sleeve of Taiga's hoodie shifted just enough for the scar on her left arm to become visible.

Mart noticed.

His smile faded.

He gently reached over...

Not to uncover it.

But to carefully pull the sleeve back into place.

Making sure the scar was hidden again.

Taiga looked at him curiously.

Mart managed a small smile.

> "...There."

She simply leaned her head against his shoulder in silent thanks.

Mart looked away toward the evening sky.

His expression suggested there was a story behind that scar.

One he wasn't ready to tell.

Not yet.
