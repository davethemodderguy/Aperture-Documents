# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-153**

### **ANNUAL OSHA INSPECTION — APRT-70 "HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – ADMINISTRATIVE RECORD

---

## **SUMMARY**

From **██/██/20██** to **██/██/20██**, APRT-70 underwent its scheduled one-week occupational safety inspection conducted by representatives from OSHA.

Despite the Blacksite housing anomalous residents, advanced technologies, and entities well outside the scope of ordinary workplaces, the inspection proceeded professionally.

The inspection team had been fully briefed on the nature of the facility before arrival.

As a result, most encounters that would surprise a first-time visitor were met with little more than a note on a clipboard.

Director Lucious reminded all personnel before the inspection:

> **"Be yourselves."**

A pause.

> **"And absolutely no attempting to influence the inspection."**

He looked directly at Accounting.

Then at Procurement.

Finally...

At Taiga.

> "...That includes gifts."

Taiga immediately raised both hands.

> "...I wasn't even thinking about it!"

Several people looked unconvinced.

---

# **DAY 1 — ARRIVAL**

The inspection team entered through the main administration building.

Security procedures were completed without incident.

The lead inspector glanced at the orientation packet.

Looked up.

Then asked calmly,

> "...Where would you like to begin?"

Director Lucious smiled faintly.

> "...Everywhere."

---

# **DAY 2 — RESIDENTIAL WING**

The inspectors encountered **Sleepy Leo**, Star's pet banana snake.

The venomous snake was found peacefully sleeping across a hallway handrail.

One junior inspector stopped.

Looked at the snake.

Then checked the briefing.

> "...Venomous?"

Director Lucious nodded.

> "...Yes."

The inspector looked at the sleeping snake again.

> "...Seems comfortable."

Sleepy Leo yawned.

Curled up tighter.

The inspection continued.

---

# **DAY 3 — RESIDENT OBSERVATIONS**

Several inspectors witnessed Taiga transform into her snow leopard form before returning to human form moments later.

Mart later demonstrated his owl transformation.

Neither event produced much reaction.

One inspector simply wrote:

> **"Transformation protocols verified."**

Another added:

> **"No apparent workplace hazard when conducted responsibly."**

Taiga looked slightly disappointed.

> "...That's it?"

The inspector smiled politely.

> "...It was in the briefing."

---

# **DAY 4 — ANOMALOUS RESIDENTS**

The inspection team observed Star and Nightshade hovering while discussing maintenance work.

One inspector asked,

> "...Do personnel who can levitate still use elevated work platforms when required?"

Director Lucious answered before anyone else could.

> "...Yes."

Star quietly descended and clipped into her fall-arrest harness before continuing.

The inspector checked another box.

> **✔ Compliant**

---

Astral demonstrated emergency evacuation procedures involving residents capable of flight.

Bao explained water-control safety around electrical infrastructure.

Frost reviewed cold-environment hazard procedures.

Each presentation was concise and well documented.

---

# **DAY 5 — ARMORY**

The inspection focused heavily on weapons storage.

Inspectors verified:

- Secure storage.
    
- Ammunition accountability.
    
- Access controls.
    
- Maintenance records.
    
- Training documentation.
    

The inspection team intentionally did **not** request live demonstrations.

After reviewing the documentation for **Bliss of Whitefall**, the lead inspector quietly closed the binder.

> "...The paperwork is... thorough."

Neko whispered to Taiga,

> "...Translation?"

Taiga whispered back,

> "...He doesn't want to see it fired."

---

# **DAY 6 — GENERAL SAFETY**

Inspectors toured laboratories, workshops, cargo areas, and maintenance tunnels.

Several recommendations were made, including:

- Additional floor markings in one storage corridor.
    
- Improved labeling on a maintenance cabinet.
    
- More anti-slip strips near a loading ramp.
    

Director Lucious approved every recommendation before the meeting ended.

> "...If it makes the site safer..."

> "...We'll do it."

---

# **DAY 7 — CLOSING REVIEW**

The final meeting concluded in the main conference room.

No major violations were issued.

Several minor recommendations were documented.

The lead inspector complimented the Blacksite's safety culture.

> "...Considering the environment..."

He looked around the room.

> "...Your personnel take workplace safety remarkably seriously."

Director Lucious replied simply,

> "...We have to."

---

# **NO GIFTS POLICY**

Throughout the week, Taiga, Star, and Miss Neko volunteered to accompany inspectors during breaks and tours.

Their objective was straightforward:

Make visitors feel welcome.

Not influenced.

Lunches were shared in the cafeteria.

Games of table tennis were played after hours.

Taiga organized an informal paper-airplane competition.

Star somehow convinced several inspectors to join a snowball fight.

Miss Neko quietly guided interested visitors through the Blacksite gardens.

At no point were gifts, money, discounts, or favors offered.

When one inspector jokingly remarked,

> "...You know, this place could probably buy OSHA."

Director Lucious answered immediately.

> "...Perhaps."

A pause.

> "...Which is exactly why we never would."

The matter ended there.

---

# **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Lead OSHA Inspector

> "...One of the most unusual facilities we've inspected."

A brief smile.

> "...Also one of the most organized."

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Safety isn't something we do because inspectors visit."

> "...Inspectors visit because safety matters."

---

### Taiga Sunbringer

Taiga spent most of the week making inspectors laugh.

By the final day, several knew her by name.

She proudly declared the inspection "less scary than expected."

---

### Mart Sunbringer

Mart mostly kept to himself.

He quietly helped carry equipment, answered questions when asked, and occasionally demonstrated proper lifting techniques with cargo that looked far heavier than it actually was for him.

One inspector later wrote:

> "Very polite."

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star attempted to convince an inspector to race her across the courtyard.

The proposal was politely declined.

She accepted the decision with good humor.

---

### Miss Neko

Miss Neko's garden tour became unexpectedly popular.

Several inspectors commented that it was the quietest place in the entire Blacksite.

---

### Frost

Frost distributed ice cream on the final afternoon.

Even the inspectors joined.

One admitted it was becoming a tradition they hoped would continue next year.

---

# **FINAL INSPECTION RESULT**

**Major Violations:** None.

**Minor Recommendations:** Implemented.

**Overall Assessment:**

> **Facility demonstrates a mature safety culture despite highly unconventional operational conditions.**

---

## **Closing Observation**

As the inspection team prepared to leave, Sleepy Leo lazily slid onto the roof of one of the departing transport vehicles.

The snake had apparently decided it looked warm.

An inspector looked up through the windshield.

Then calmly radioed:

> "...We've got one extra passenger."

Star jogged over, gently lifted the sleepy banana snake into her arms, and apologized with an embarrassed smile.

Sleepy Leo gave a tiny yawn, curled around her shoulders, and promptly fell back asleep.

The lead inspector chuckled.

> "...We'll see you all next year."

Taiga waved enthusiastically.

Mart gave a small wave beside her.

Director Lucious nodded respectfully.

As the convoy disappeared beyond the gates, one inspector looked back at the Blacksite in the side mirror and quietly remarked:

> "...Strangest workplace I've ever inspected."

Another smiled.

> "...Also one of the safest."

The rest of the team agreed.
