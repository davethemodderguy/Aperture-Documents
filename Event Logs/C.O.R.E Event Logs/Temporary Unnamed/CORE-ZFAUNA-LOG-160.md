# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-160**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL STATUS UPDATE — MART SUNBRINGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – CONFIDENTIAL / RESIDENT WELFARE

---

## **SUMMARY**

Psychological Services have documented a further deterioration in Mart Sunbringer's sleep quality and nightmare severity.

His insomnia has progressed beyond chronic sleep disruption into periods where restful sleep has become increasingly uncommon.

The principal contributing factor is believed to be Mart's exceptionally persistent autobiographical memory.

Unlike most individuals, whose memories naturally lose detail over time, Mart continues to recall emotionally significant events with extraordinary clarity.

Those memories do not feel distant to him.

During nightmares, they often feel immediate.

---

## **CLINICAL OBSERVATION**

One psychologist summarized Mart's condition as follows:

> **"His memory isn't harming him because he remembers."**

> **"It's harming him because, emotionally, those moments never seem completely over."**

Mart's recollections remain highly detailed.

He remembers voices.

Expressions.

Weather.

The feeling of cold snow beneath his feet.

The sound of people crying.

Small details that most people would likely lose over time remain remarkably clear.

These memories frequently become incorporated into recurring nightmares.

---

## **NIGHTMARE PROGRESSION**

Recent counseling sessions indicate that Mart's dreams have become increasingly difficult to distinguish emotionally from his memories.

Common themes now include:

- Searching endlessly for Taiga without finding her.
    
- Witnessing loved ones disappear one by one.
    
- Reliving childhood events with altered outcomes.
    
- Calling for help without receiving an answer.
    
- Returning to places associated with loss.
    

Mart reports that, upon waking, it can take several minutes before he fully recognizes that he is back in the Blacksite.

---

## **OBSERVED INCIDENT**

**04:11**

A staff member passing through the residential wing heard quiet crying from behind Mart's bedroom door.

No sounds of distress beyond crying were detected.

Following established welfare procedures, Taiga was notified.

She knocked softly.

> **Taiga:** "...Mart?"

There was no response.

She waited.

A minute later, the door unlocked.

Mart opened it himself.

His eyes were visibly swollen from crying.

He looked exhausted.

Taiga asked only one question.

> "...Nightmare?"

Mart nodded.

After several seconds, he quietly admitted,

> "...It felt real."

Taiga gently hugged him.

Neither spoke for several minutes.

---

## **MEMORY ASSESSMENT**

Psychological Services emphasize that Mart's strong memory is **not** considered an illness in itself.

However, when paired with severe trauma, it can make recovery substantially more difficult.

Painful memories that remain emotionally vivid may continue to provoke distress long after the events have ended.

Therapeutic efforts therefore focus not on erasing memories, but on helping Mart experience them as events that happened in the past rather than experiences that feel continuously present.

---

## **FAMILY RESPONSE**

### Taiga Sunbringer

Taiga has become increasingly attentive to signs of exhaustion.

If Mart appears unusually quiet, she often asks simple questions rather than pressing him to explain.

One afternoon she quietly told him:

> "...You don't have to carry every bad memory by yourself."

Mart looked down before answering.

> "...I know."

After a pause, he added,

> "...I'm still learning how."

---

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral has begun keeping a journal of new family memories.

She occasionally reads entries aloud during dinner.

Her stated reason is simple.

> "...I can't change what happened."

> "...But we can keep making memories worth remembering."

Mart listens every time.

## **Closing Observation**

One evening, Mart was found sitting quietly in the library.

Not reading.

Simply looking through a photo album.

It contained recent photographs.

Taiga learning to glide.

Astral laughing in the garden.

Frost handing out ice cream.

Star making everyone pose for a picture they hadn't planned to take.

Mart carefully turned each page.

When he reached the end, he closed the album gently.

A librarian passing by asked,

> "...Looking at old memories?"

Mart shook his head.

He rested a hand on the cover.

> "...Making sure I don't forget the good ones too."

The album remained on the table long after he left.

A quiet reminder that even when painful memories demanded his attention, there were happier ones still waiting to be remembered.
