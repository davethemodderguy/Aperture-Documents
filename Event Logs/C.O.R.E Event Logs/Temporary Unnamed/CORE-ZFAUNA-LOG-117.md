# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-117**

### **RECOVERY UPDATE — MART SUNBRINGER / TAIGA SUNBRINGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – MEDICAL / BIOLOGICAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following several days of continuous treatment, Medical Services has confirmed a divergence in the recovery of the Sunbringer siblings.

**Mart Sunbringer has regained the ability to transform into his human form.**

**Taiga Sunbringer has not.**

---

# **SUBJECT: MART SUNBRINGER**

## **Transformation Recovery**

At approximately **09:12**, Mart's forced owl form ended naturally.

The transformation was gradual, with no signs of distress or instability.

Within moments, the familiar white-haired boy was sitting on his hospital bed, looking down at his own hands in quiet disbelief.

He flexed his fingers several times before softly whispering,

> "...I'm... back."

Medical examinations confirmed that his transformation ability had fully stabilized.

He may now freely alternate between his human and owl forms.

Residual fatigue remains, but his recovery is progressing well.

---

## **FIRST ACTION**

Medical staff expected Mart to ask for food, water, or rest.

Instead, he climbed out of bed.

Walked directly across the infirmary.

And sat beside Taiga.

He remained there for nearly an hour.

Saying nothing.

Simply keeping her company.

---

# **SUBJECT: TAIGA SUNBRINGER**

## **Transformation Status**

Despite identical treatment protocols, Taiga's condition has shown no comparable improvement.

Repeated examinations confirm that she remains unable to return to her human form.

Unlike Mart's recovery, there has been no indication that her transformation lock is weakening.

Medical cannot yet determine whether the cause is purely physiological, psychological, or a combination of both.

---

## **CURRENT CONDITION**

Taiga remains conscious and responsive.

She continues to recognize staff, family, and friends.

She still purrs when Astral pets her.

She still nudges Mart whenever he sits beside her.

She still happily eats Bao's salads.

Yet...

Whenever asked to try transforming...

Nothing happens.

The snow leopard simply lowers her ears.

As though she already knows the answer.

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical personnel believe Taiga's body has recovered faster than her mind.

The physical injuries continue to heal.

The emotional trauma does not.

Psychological Services has recommended against repeated transformation testing, noting that unsuccessful attempts have begun to visibly discourage her.

---

### **Closing Observation**

That evening, Mart returned to Taiga's bedside carrying a book.

He climbed onto the chair beside her and began reading aloud.

Not because she couldn't read.

Because he remembered how she used to read to him when he was frightened.

Halfway through the story, Taiga shifted closer.

She rested her head gently across his lap.

Mart paused only long enough to smile.

Then continued reading.

He never once asked her to become human.

To him...

His sister had already come home.
