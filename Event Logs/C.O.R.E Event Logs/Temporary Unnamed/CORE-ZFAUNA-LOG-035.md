# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-035**

### **ABILITY UPDATE — CONDITIONAL LEOPARD TRANSFORMATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – MEDICAL / N.O.S.T.

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following several weeks of believing Taiga had permanently lost the ability to return to her original snow leopard form, Medical has revised its assessment.

The transformation was **not** lost.

It had become conditional.

After repeated observations, three distinct triggers have been identified.

---

# **I. TRANSFORMATION CONDITIONS**

Taiga may transform into her snow leopard form under any of the following circumstances:

### **Condition One — Extreme Fear**

If Taiga experiences an intense startle or overwhelming fear, her body instinctively shifts into her smaller snow leopard form.

Medical believes this is an unconscious defensive response.

Personnel are reminded **not** to intentionally frighten Taiga.

---

### **Condition Two — Complete Comfort**

If Taiga feels exceptionally safe, relaxed, and emotionally comfortable, she may also transform into her leopard form naturally.

Unlike the fear response, this transformation is calm and gradual.

It most commonly occurs while resting with trusted companions.

---

### **Condition Three — Voluntary Transformation**

Taiga is also capable of consciously choosing to transform.

This requires concentration but can be performed at will.

---

# **II. LEOPARD FORM**

Despite the transformation, Taiga remains noticeably smaller than a normal adult snow leopard.

Her appearance closely resembles the young cub first recovered near the Blacksite perimeter.

Characteristics include:

- Thick white winter fur.
    
- Black rosette markings.
    
- Small rounded ears.
    
- Fluffy tail.
    
- Bright blue eyes.
    
- Black restraining bands remain present, scaled appropriately.
    
- White tail ribbon and bell also remain, resizing with the transformation.
    

The transformation affects only her body.

Her clothing is safely incorporated into the process and reappears upon returning to her humanoid form.

---

# **III. OBSERVED EVENTS**

### **Comfort Trigger**

One evening, after dinner, Taiga was resting beside the heater.

Aster slept curled against her.

Leo had wrapped himself around her tail.

Mart perched quietly on the back of the sofa.

Bao was reading aloud while everyone else relaxed nearby.

Without warning...

A faint shimmer surrounded Taiga.

A few seconds later, a small snow leopard cub lay peacefully where she had been sitting.

Nobody spoke.

She simply continued sleeping.

Medical later noted no signs of distress.

The transformation had occurred solely because she felt completely at ease.

---

### **Voluntary Trigger**

The following day, Taiga demonstrated the transformation under supervision.

She closed her eyes.

Took a slow breath.

A soft wave of white light briefly surrounded her.

Moments later...

A small snow leopard looked up at the observing staff.

She gave a quiet chirp.

Then calmly padded over to Aster.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga's transformation ability has been reclassified.

The snow leopard form remains fully functional and may be entered through one of three confirmed conditions:

- Extreme fear.
    
- Complete emotional comfort.
    
- Conscious choice.
    

Medical notes that the comfort-induced transformation is viewed as a positive indicator of Taiga's growing trust in those around her.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late one snowy evening, the residents of Room N-07 sat quietly around the heater.

Nobody was talking.

Nobody needed to.

Without anyone noticing exactly when it happened, Taiga quietly transformed into her small snow leopard form and curled up beside Aster.

The little bell on her tail gave one soft...

**Jingle.**

Leo shifted onto the top of her head.

Mart tucked his wings and watched over the room.

The only sound left was the wind outside and the steady crackle of the heater.

For Taiga, it was enough to make her feel completely, unquestionably safe.
