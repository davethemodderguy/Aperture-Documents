# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-130**

### **ANOMALOUS PHENOMENON — LUMINOUS FEATHERS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – ANOMALOUS / PSYCHOLOGICAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

A new anomaly has been documented during nearly every reported sighting of Mart Sunbringer.

Whenever Mart is observed flying above the Blacksite, **a single feather composed entirely of white light** slowly descends from the sky.

Unlike the first documented luminous feather, these specimens are physically stable.

At least...

For everyone except one person.

---

## **OBSERVATION**

Personnel have successfully recovered multiple luminous feathers.

Testing has confirmed the feathers possess no measurable mass, yet can be held normally.

The feathers emit:

- A soft white glow.
    
- No detectable heat.
    
- No harmful anomalous radiation.
    

The feathers remain stable for several hours before naturally fading.

Researchers have been unable to determine their composition.

---

## **UNIQUE PROPERTY**

A consistent anomaly has been observed.

Every recovered feather behaves identically...

Until it is handed to Taiga Sunbringer.

The moment Taiga's skin makes contact—

The feather begins to dissolve.

Not instantly.

Slowly.

Painfully.

From the tip inward.

The white light breaks apart into thousands of tiny glittering particles.

Within seconds...

Nothing remains.

Personnel have attempted multiple methods:

- Placing the feather in a container before handing it to Taiga.
    
- Protective gloves.
    
- Direct observation.
    
- Delayed transfer.
    

The result has never changed.

Only Taiga witnesses the feather disappear in her hands.

---

## **RECORDED INCIDENT**

A maintenance technician recovered a luminous feather after another rooftop sighting.

Unable to locate Mart, he instead brought the feather to Medical.

Taiga happened to be nearby.

The technician smiled gently.

> "...I think this belongs with you."

He carefully placed the feather into her open hands.

Everyone watched.

For a brief moment...

Nothing happened.

Then the glow flickered.

Tiny fragments of white light drifted away like snow.

Taiga stared silently.

No one moved.

The feather continued dissolving until only glittering particles remained.

They floated between her fingers.

Then disappeared completely.

Taiga never blinked.

She only whispered,

> "...Even this..."

She closed her empty hand.

> "...Won't stay with me."

No one knew how to answer.

---

## **RESEARCH NOTE**

Anomaly Research has proposed several theories.

None have been confirmed.

Current hypotheses include:

- A resonance unique to Taiga.
    
- A subconscious anomalous response.
    
- A property intentionally linked to Mart's emotional state.
    

No experimental evidence currently supports one theory over another.

Further testing has been suspended at the request of Psychological Services.

Reason:

> "The phenomenon is causing unnecessary emotional harm."

### **Closing Observation**

Near sunset, another white feather drifted from the sky.

This time...

No one picked it up.

Everyone simply watched it fall.

It landed softly in the fresh snow beneath the cargo yard.

It glowed there quietly.

Undisturbed.

Until the light faded on its own.

No one wanted Taiga to watch another feather disappear in her hands.

She saw it from a distance anyway.

She stood behind the window, forehead resting against the glass.

She watched the feather's glow fade naturally into the evening.

Then she whispered, barely loud enough for herself to hear:

> "...I'm glad... at least one of them didn't disappear because of me."
