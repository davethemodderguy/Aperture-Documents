# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-113**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL INCIDENT — ASTRAL SUNBRINGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – MEDICAL / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

With Mart Sunbringer still missing and Taiga Sunbringer now unaccounted for within the Blacksite, Astral Sunbringer has entered a period of acute psychological distress.

Medical Services emphasizes that Astral has **not** displayed anger toward staff, Security, or Research personnel.

She has blamed no one.

Instead...

She blames herself.

---

## **OBSERVATION**

Astral continued helping with search efforts long after Medical recommended she rest.

She walked every residential corridor.

Every garden.

Every quiet place Taiga liked.

Every high perch Mart used to visit.

Each time she reached an empty room...

She moved on to the next one.

Eventually, she stopped searching.

Not because she had given up.

Because she no longer knew where to look.

---

## **THE BREAKDOWN**

Witnesses reported finding Astral sitting alone in the Sunbringer residence.

Mart's room was open.

Taiga's room was open.

Both were empty.

She sat between the two doors.

Staring at the hallway.

When Director Lucious quietly approached, she didn't look at him.

She spoke so softly that Security reviewing the hallway recording had difficulty hearing her.

> "...I was dead..."

A long silence.

> "...I came back..."

Another.

> "...I got my children back..."

Her hands trembled.

> "...How did I lose them again...?"

She covered her face.

For several minutes...

The only sound in the hallway was her crying.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical concluded that Astral is experiencing overwhelming grief combined with uncertainty.

Unlike bereavement, there has been no confirmation of loss.

Unlike hope, there has been no sign of return.

She exists in the space between those two realities.

This uncertainty has proven emotionally exhausting.

---

## **PERSONNEL RESPONSE**

No attempt was made to interrupt Astral.

Instead...

People simply began sitting with her.

Not because they had answers.

Because she shouldn't have to be alone.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious sat beside Astral for nearly an hour.

Neither of them spoke.

Eventually, Astral whispered,

> "...Did I fail them?"

Lucious answered immediately.

> "...No."

A pause.

> "...They're still your children."

Another.

> "...And they're still somewhere."

He looked down the empty hallway.

> "...We'll keep looking."

---

### Neko

Neko quietly sat on Astral's other side.

For once...

He had no joke.

No clever remark.

Only one sentence.

> "...We're not stopping."

---

### Miss Neko

Miss Neko gently wrapped a blanket around Astral's shoulders.

> "...Until they're home..."

She squeezed Astral's hand.

> "...You won't wait alone."

---

### Bao

Bao arrived carrying warm tea.

Astral didn't reach for it.

Bao simply placed the cup beside her.

It stayed warm for a very long time.

---

### Frost

Frost knelt in front of Astral.

> "...Neither trail has ended."

> "...We simply haven't found where they lead."

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star quietly crawled into Astral's lap.

She hugged her tightly.

Her own eyes were red from crying.

> "...Taiga promised she'd stay..."

Her voice cracked.

> "...Mart always comes back..."

She looked up at Astral.

> "...Right...?"

Astral couldn't answer.

She simply held Star closer.

---

### Nightshade Dawnbringer

Nightshade stood watch outside the room.

Not because she expected danger.

Because no one carrying that much grief should have to face it alone.

---

### Leo

Leo silently brought over one of the large cushions from the common room.

He set it beside Astral.

Sat down on it.

And stayed.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

### Astral Sunbringer

- Severe emotional distress.
    
- Receiving continuous support from Residential and Medical Services.
    
- Continues to participate in search updates despite recommendations to rest.
    

### Search Operations

- **Mart Sunbringer:** Active.
    
- **Taiga Sunbringer:** Active.
    

No evidence has been found indicating either has left the Blacksite's broader area or come to harm.

Both searches remain ongoing.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late that evening, Astral remained seated between the doors to her children's rooms.

Neither room had been closed.

Neither bed had been disturbed.

One by one, members of the Blacksite quietly joined her.

Director Lucious sat beside her.

Neko leaned against the opposite wall.

Miss Neko rested a comforting hand on Astral's shoulder.

Bao left warm tea within reach.

Frost stood silently at the end of the hallway.

Nightshade kept watch.

Leo dozed nearby, refusing to leave.

No one spoke for a long time.

Finally, Astral looked down the empty corridor and whispered through tears,

> "...They're still here..."

Her voice trembled.

> "...Somewhere."

Director Lucious gently took her hand.

> "...Then that's where we'll keep looking."

And for the first time since the searches began...

Astral wasn't sitting alone.
