# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-141**

### **ABILITY ASSESSMENT — TAIGA SUNBRINGER'S WINGS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – ANOMALOUS ABILITY UPDATE

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following several weeks of flight training conducted by Mart and Astral Sunbringer, Research has concluded that Taiga's wings function fundamentally differently from those of other known Sunbringers.

Contrary to initial expectations, Taiga **cannot achieve sustained powered flight.**

Instead, her wings have developed a unique synergy with her pre-existing anomalous movement abilities.

---

## **ABILITY ANALYSIS**

### **Mart Sunbringer**

Mart's wings generate continuous lift.

He can:

- Hover indefinitely.
    
- Fly for extended periods without visible fatigue.
    
- Maneuver freely in three-dimensional space.
    
- Carry additional passengers using his wings.
    

His control appears entirely instinctive, likely due to years spent flying in his owl form.

---

### **Astral Sunbringer**

Astral's wings function similarly to Mart's.

She demonstrates smooth, controlled flight with exceptional stability.

No additional propulsion is required.

---

### **Taiga Sunbringer**

Testing has confirmed a completely different mechanism.

Taiga's wings **cannot maintain enough lift for continuous flight.**

Repeated attempts result in gradual altitude loss.

However...

When combined with her anomalous **Air Jump**, the results become remarkable.

---

## **GLIDE MECHANICS**

Taiga's movement now follows a distinct sequence.

1. Perform an Air Jump.
    
2. Use the burst of upward momentum.
    
3. Extend both wings.
    
4. Convert vertical momentum into a controlled glide.
    

Rather than repeatedly falling after each jump, Taiga can now remain airborne for significantly longer periods.

Each additional Air Jump provides another burst of speed and altitude, allowing her to chain long-distance aerial movement.

Research has unofficially nicknamed the technique:

> **"Sky Skipping."**

---

## **TRAINING OBSERVATION**

During testing over the eastern training grounds, Mart demonstrated traditional flight.

He circled comfortably overhead.

Taiga attempted to imitate him.

After several seconds she began losing altitude.

> **Mart:** "...You're dropping."

> **Taiga:** "...I KNOW!"

Just before touching the ground...

She performed an Air Jump.

The sudden burst launched her upward once more.

Her wings immediately caught the momentum.

Instead of falling...

She glided gracefully across nearly the entire training field.

Mart stopped midair.

Astral blinked.

Frost quietly lowered her clipboard.

Taiga landed in a dramatic crouch.

Looked around proudly.

Then shouted,

> "...WHO NEEDS NORMAL FLYING?"

Mart couldn't help laughing.

> "...Apparently you."

Taiga stuck her tongue out at him.

---

## **RESEARCH CONCLUSION**

Although incapable of conventional flight, Taiga's movement style offers several advantages.

Compared to sustained flight:

**Advantages**

- Extremely rapid bursts of acceleration.
    
- Unpredictable aerial movement.
    
- Excellent maneuverability in confined spaces.
    
- Long-distance gliding with minimal energy expenditure.
    
- Difficult trajectory for hostile targets to predict.
    

**Limitations**

- Cannot hover.
    
- Cannot remain airborne indefinitely.
    
- Requires periodic Air Jumps to maintain altitude.
    
- Less efficient for vertical climbing than Mart or Astral.
    

Researchers concluded that Taiga has not developed a weaker version of Sunbringer wings.

She has developed a **different specialization.**

### **Closing Observation**

Late that afternoon, Mart hovered high above the Blacksite, waiting.

Far below, Taiga grinned confidently.

She launched herself upward with an Air Jump.

Then another.

Then another.

Her wings spread wide, catching the rushing air.

Instead of climbing endlessly like Mart...

She carved a long, graceful arc across the evening sky, gliding over rooftops, courtyards, and snow-covered fields before touching down almost silently beside him.

Mart smiled.

> "...Still can't fly."

Taiga folded her wings with exaggerated pride.

> "...Maybe."

She floated five centimeters above the ground.

Then pointed toward the horizon she had just crossed.

> "...But I can glide farther than you expected."

Mart laughed.

He couldn't argue with that.
