# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-082**

### **INCIDENT REPORT — UNACCOUNTED RESIDENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – DIRECTORATE / SECURITY / MEDICAL

---

### **STATUS UPDATE**

At approximately **05:42**, staff reported that the meal routinely left by Bao for Taiga remained untouched.

This marks the first confirmed occurrence since Taiga began isolating herself.

Medical immediately requested a welfare verification.

Simultaneously, Security reported another anomaly.

**Taiga's personal tracking signal went offline.**

No evidence of device destruction or tampering has been found.

The signal simply ceased transmitting.

The timing has been classified as significant.

---

## **SECURITY ALERT**

During the initial search, Logistics notified Security that the **Cargo Hold** had been accessed.

Inventory appeared largely undisturbed.

However, the main cargo compartment was found open.

No breach of the Blacksite perimeter has been detected.

All external blast gates remain **closed and secured**.

At this time, there is **no evidence** that Taiga has left the facility.

Even so, the combination of events prompted an immediate escalation.

---

## **DIRECTORATE ACTIONS**

Director Lucious issued the following orders:

- Suspend all non-essential cargo operations.
    
- Conduct a complete search of the Cargo Hold.
    
- Maintain perimeter lockdown.
    
- Do **not** treat Taiga as a hostile subject.
    
- If located, prioritize her safety and de-escalation.
    

He added one final instruction.

> "Nobody corners her."

> "If she's there..."

> "Give her a way out."

Medical unanimously supported the order.

---

## **ROOM N-07**

For the first time since Taiga disappeared into isolation...

Bao quietly removed the untouched meal.

She stood in silence for several moments.

Then prepared another one anyway.

When asked why, she answered simply,

> "...In case tomorrow is different."

### **Closing Observation**

The Cargo Hold was unusually quiet.

Rows of sealed containers stretched into the darkness.

Forklifts stood abandoned where operators had left them.

The heavy blast doors remained shut, sealing the Blacksite from the frozen world outside.

Somewhere within the facility...

Taiga was believed to still be there.

The question was no longer **whether** she was inside.

It was **where**.

And for the first time since she began hiding...

Even the people who knew her best had no answer.
