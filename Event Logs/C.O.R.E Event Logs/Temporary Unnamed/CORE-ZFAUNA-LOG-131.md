# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-131**

### **OBSERVATION REPORT — THE WINDOW**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – ACTIVE OBSERVATION / PSYCHOLOGICAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

A recurring pattern has emerged over the past two weeks.

Mart Sunbringer has been repeatedly observed **inside his own room** despite his official status as missing.

He does not enter through doors.

He does not remain long.

He simply appears at the open bedroom window, sits quietly on the windowsill with his wings folded behind him, and leaves before anyone can reach him.

This behavior has been documented on nine separate occasions.

---

## **OBSERVATION**

Unlike previous sightings on rooftops and around the perimeter, Mart now consistently returns to a single location.

His room.

Security footage shows him sitting silently on the windowsill.

His luminous wings remain folded tightly against his back.

He rarely moves.

He simply looks around the room.

On every recorded occasion, he has been holding the same photograph.

The photograph removed from the bedside frame weeks earlier.

The one captioned:

> **"Best sister ever."**

How the photograph came back into Mart's possession remains unknown.

No signs of forced entry into the desk drawer have been found.

---

## **FIRST RECORDED CONTACT ATTEMPT**

The first successful visual confirmation occurred at 20:14.

Taiga happened to be passing the residential hallway when she glanced through the open doorway.

She froze.

Mart was there.

Sitting exactly where he used to read before bed.

The photograph rested carefully in his hands.

For several seconds...

Neither sibling moved.

Taiga took one cautious step into the room.

Her voice shook.

> **Taiga:** "...Mart?"

Mart slowly looked toward her.

His expression was calm.

But tired.

Taiga continued.

> **Taiga:** "...Please..."

Another step.

> "...Can we just talk?"

Mart looked down at the photograph.

His thumb brushed gently across the words.

**"Best sister ever."**

He closed his eyes.

For a moment...

It looked as though he might answer.

Instead...

He quietly placed the photograph onto the bedroom floor.

The wings of white light unfolded.

Without a single word...

He stepped backward through the open window.

A soft gust of wind swept through the room.

By the time Taiga reached the window...

He was already disappearing into the night sky.

---

## **SUBSEQUENT ATTEMPTS**

The pattern repeated.

Again.

And again.

Each encounter differed only in Taiga's words.

---

### **Encounter Four**

> **Taiga:** "...Mom misses you."

Mart lowered his head.

He silently left.

---

### **Encounter Six**

> **Taiga:** "...Dad still keeps your room exactly the same."

Mart looked around the room one last time.

Then flew away.

---

### **Encounter Eight**

Taiga never asked him to come back.

She simply said,

> "...I miss my little brother."

Mart's shoulders trembled.

A single tear fell onto the windowsill.

He picked up the photograph.

Held it close to his chest.

Then disappeared into the darkness once more.

---

## **BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS**

Research staff now believe Mart is no longer actively hiding his location.

Instead...

He appears to be maintaining a deliberate distance.

He consistently returns to places connected to home.

He consistently leaves before direct conversation can begin.

Current assessment:

> **Mart knows where home is.**

> **He simply does not believe he is ready to stay.**

---

### **Closing Observation**

At 20:31, Security Camera R-12 captured Mart sitting quietly in his bedroom window once again.

The photograph rested in his lap.

The room was silent.

A few moments later, Taiga appeared in the doorway.

She didn't speak immediately.

She simply looked at her younger brother.

Mart looked back.

Neither of them smiled.

Neither of them cried.

For one brief moment...

They were together in the same room again.

Then Mart gently set the photograph onto the windowsill.

He unfolded the wings of white light.

And with one slow beat...

Rose into the night.

Taiga reached the window only after he had gone.

She rested one hand on the windowsill where he had been sitting.

The wood was still warm.
