# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-015**

### **OBSERVATION REPORT — BLACK BANDAGES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – INTERNAL / MEDICAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Personnel have documented a noticeable change in Taiga's appearance.

During morning rounds, several staff members observed black bandages wrapped around portions of her forearms, hands, and lower legs.

The bandages appeared clean, professionally applied, and showed no visible signs of blood or other injury.

When asked what had happened, Taiga simply smiled.

> "...They're helping."

She declined to elaborate further.

Medical noted no signs of impaired movement during routine observation.

---

# **I. PERSONNEL RESPONSE**

Over the following days, nearly everyone noticed the bandages.

Neko noticed.

Miss Neko noticed.

Frost noticed.

Bao noticed.

Star noticed.

Nightshade noticed.

Aster noticed.

No one knew why Taiga was wearing them.

After several quiet discussions, the household reached an unspoken understanding.

Taiga had recently become uncomfortable whenever too much attention was directed toward her.

They did not want to overwhelm or frustrate her by repeatedly asking questions she clearly wasn't ready to answer.

So...

No one pressed the issue.

---

# **II. THE CHANGE**

The atmosphere in Room N-07 shifted.

Instead of asking if she was alright...

Everyone greeted her as they normally would.

Instead of surrounding her with concern...

They simply included her in everyday activities.

Bao still asked if she wanted lunch.

Frost still invited her outside whenever it snowed.

Star still left room for her during evening reading sessions.

Nightshade still nodded politely when they passed in the hallway.

Miss Neko still made enough dinner for everyone.

Neko still absentmindedly asked if anyone wanted sandwiches.

Nothing about their routines changed.

Except...

Nobody mentioned the bandages.

---

# **III. ASTER**

Aster struggled the most.

Every instinct told her to ask.

To write something.

To make sure Taiga was okay.

Several times she picked up her marker.

Several times she stopped.

Eventually, she erased what she had written and instead held up a different message.

> **Want to build a snow leopard later?**

Taiga looked at the whiteboard.

Then smiled.

> "...I'd like that."

Aster quietly put the marker away.

For now...

That was enough.

---

# **IV. LEO**

Leo appeared completely unconcerned.

He simply chose the bandaged arm as his new favorite sleeping place.

When Taiga looked down, the tiny banana snake was already asleep, loosely wrapped around the black fabric.

She couldn't help but laugh.

> "...You're not worried at all, are you?"

Leo gave no reply.

He was asleep.

---

# **V. MEDICAL NOTE**

Medical staff continue to monitor Taiga's overall health through routine observation.

At this time:

- No obvious signs of physical deterioration have been observed.
    
- Taiga remains active during the day.
    
- She continues eating regularly, with her usual preference for vegetables.
    
- She continues participating in normal activities when invited.
    

Medical has noted that, should Taiga choose to discuss the purpose of the bandages in the future, she will be given the opportunity to do so without pressure.

---

# **VI. DIRECTOR'S COMMENT**

Director Lucious reviewed the report before adding a short note.

> **"Respecting someone's privacy is not the same as ignoring them."**

> **"If she wishes to talk, we will listen."**

> **"Until then, she should continue to know she has a place here."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga continues wearing the black bandages.

The reason remains undisclosed.

Her roommates and the wider Blacksite staff have deliberately avoided repeatedly questioning her about them, while continuing to include her in daily life and treating her with the same warmth and respect as before.

---

### **Closing Observation**

That evening, fresh snow covered the Blacksite courtyard.

Frost and Taiga quietly built snow sculptures together.

Bao laughed after accidentally collapsing one with an oversized snowball.

Aster wrote **"Round two?"** on her whiteboard.

Taiga laughed, nodded, and picked up another handful of snow.

For a little while, the black bandages went unnoticed—not because nobody cared, but because everyone chose to let Taiga decide when, and if, she wanted to explain them.
