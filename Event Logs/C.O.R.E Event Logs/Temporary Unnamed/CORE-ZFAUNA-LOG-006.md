## **STATUS UPDATE**

At approximately **05:41**, Neko became the first resident of Room N-07 to wake, followed shortly by Miss Neko.

As was their usual routine, the two quietly prepared for work, taking care not to wake the others.

Before leaving, Neko glanced toward the heated rug in the corner.

Then stopped.

"...Miss Neko."

"...Yeah?"

"...Where's Taiga?"

Miss Neko looked over.

Taiga wasn't there.

At least...

Not as anyone remembered her.

Curled beside the sleeping Aster was a young girl, appearing no older than **ten years old**.

She had long snow-white hair marked with dark leopard-like rosettes, matching markings across her arms and legs, and a long snow leopard tail resting beneath a blanket. Soft leopard ears twitched occasionally as she slept.

She wore a simple white tank top and loose gray pants.

Aster remained asleep beside her, one paw resting against the girl's arm as though nothing had changed.

The two quietly exchanged glances.

Neither woke her.

Instead, they gently closed the bedroom door and left for work.

# **I. LUNCHTIME DISCOVERY**

The revelation came much later.

At lunchtime, the mysterious girl wandered into the cafeteria carrying the same relaxed expression Taiga always wore.

Without saying a word...

She walked directly to the salad counter.

Picked up an impressively large bowl of vegetables.

And sat beside Aster.

The cafeteria gradually fell silent.

Nobody recognized her.

Except...

Aster.

The white cat simply climbed into the chair beside the girl without hesitation.

Leo slithered onto her shoulder.

Curled up.

And immediately fell asleep.

The silence finally broke.

---

### Frost

Calmly setting down her tray.

> "...Who are you?"

---

Aster looked up from her lunch.

Picked up her whiteboard.

Wrote carefully.

> **You aren't Taiga.**

She held it toward the girl.

---

The girl blinked.

Looked around the room.

Then tilted her head.

---

> "...I am Taiga."

---

Complete silence.

# **II. THE EXPLANATION**

What followed was nearly an hour of questions.

Fortunately...

The girl spoke fluent English.

Piece by piece, the truth emerged.

Taiga explained that her species naturally possessed two physical forms.

The snow leopard form everyone had known.

And a humanoid form.

Because she had been extremely young—and exhausted from fleeing after her mother's death—she had remained instinctively in her animal form ever since arriving at the Blacksite.

Only after finally feeling completely safe...

Did her body naturally shift into its humanoid appearance while she slept.

Veterinary confirmed there was no sign of injury or distress.

The transformation appeared entirely natural.

---

# **III. HER PAST**

As the conversation continued, Taiga shared something she had never spoken about before.

Her mother had not merely been an ordinary snow leopard.

She had been the **Protector of the Icelandic Guardian**, entrusted with safeguarding an ancient guardian spirit tied to Iceland's natural balance.

Taiga herself had been born into that lineage.

The hunters who shot her mother had unknowingly attacked one of the Guardian's sworn protectors.

When her mother fell...

Taiga fled.

Eventually finding the Blacksite.

She looked down quietly.

---

> "...I didn't know where else to go."

---

No one interrupted.

---

### Director Lucious

Who had quietly arrived midway through the explanation simply nodded.

---

> "...You chose well."

# **IV. ABILITIES**

Following controlled testing, N.O.S.T. documented two confirmed abilities.

### Animal Communication

Taiga can naturally understand and converse with animals.

This includes ordinary wildlife as well as many non-hostile anomalous species.

Personnel quickly noticed that Leo appeared to "listen" when she spoke to him.

Whether he was actually listening...

Or simply asleep with his eyes open...

Remains undetermined.

---

### Snow Manipulation

Taiga demonstrated the ability to freely manipulate snow and falling ice crystals.

Unlike Frost's cryokinesis, which creates and shapes ice directly...

Taiga's ability focuses specifically on naturally occurring snow.

She can gather drifting snow into gentle spirals, sculpt figures, blanket areas in soft snowfall, or guide snowflakes through the air with remarkable precision.

---

### Frost

Watching tiny snowflakes dance around the cafeteria window.

Her eyes quietly lit up.

> "...That's beautiful."

---

The two spent the rest of the afternoon exchanging techniques.

Frost taught Taiga how to shape snow into stronger structures.

Taiga showed Frost how to make snow move almost as if it were alive.

By sunset, they had filled one of the training courtyards with intricate sculptures.
