# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-041**

### **RESIDENTIAL RECORD — A VERY FELINE DAY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL ARCHIVE

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following the previous day's events, Taiga decided she wanted to spend the entire day in her snow leopard form.

Medical approved the request, noting no adverse effects from prolonged transformation.

According to Taiga:

> "...I just feel like being fluffy today."

No one objected.

Coincidentally, both Neko and Miss Neko had unusually light workloads that morning.

Seeing there was little requiring their immediate attention, the siblings looked at each other.

Neko shrugged.

> "...Cat Day?"

Miss Neko smiled.

> "...Cat Day."

A brief shimmer of light surrounded them both.

Moments later...

Two cats stood where they had been.

---

## **THE REVELATION**

Taiga, who had been quietly grooming her paws nearby, looked up.

She blinked once.

Then twice.

Her ears slowly stood upright.

She stared at the two unfamiliar cats for several long seconds before realizing who they were.

Her eyes widened.

She let out a surprised little chirp.

The orange-and-white cat sitting where Neko had been flicked an ear.

> "Mrrp?"

Taiga looked between him and the calico cat that was now Miss Neko.

She transformed back into her humanoid form out of pure surprise.

"...You two can transform!?"

Neko returned to human form just long enough to answer.

> "...We've been able to for years."

Taiga blinked.

"...Nobody told me!"

Miss Neko laughed.

> "...You never asked."

---

## **A NEW DISCOVERY**

Taiga immediately transformed back into her snow leopard form.

This time, however, she wasn't the only animal in the room.

For the remainder of the day, Room N-07 was occupied by:

- One small snow leopard.
    
- One orange-and-white cat (Neko).
    
- One calico cat (Miss Neko).
    

Star and Nightshade quietly watched the trio from the sofa.

Neither revealed that they, too, possessed feline forms.

They agreed it would be more fun for Taiga to discover that another day.

---

## **THE BLACKSITE'S NEW RESPONSIBILITY**

The change did not go unnoticed.

Personnel walking through the residential wing quickly realized something.

There were now **three free-roaming felines** wandering the Blacksite.

Security jokingly updated the whiteboard in the control room.

**Today's Residents to Keep an Eye On:**

- ✓ Snow Leopard
    
- ✓ Orange Cat
    
- ✓ Calico Cat
    

Below it, someone had written:

> **Please do not accidentally feed them three lunches.**

The note was ignored almost immediately.

---

## **OBSERVATIONS**

The three displayed distinctly different behaviors.

### Taiga

Curious.

Playful.

Frequently rolled onto her back in warm patches of sunlight.

Preferred napping beside heaters.

---

### Neko

Spent most of the afternoon investigating machinery.

Several engineers watched in amusement as an orange cat somehow completed a diagnostics inspection simply by walking across a control panel.

Neko refused to explain.

---

### Miss Neko

Immediately found the warmest blanket in Room N-07.

Stayed there for nearly two hours.

Medical noted this was entirely expected.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Star

Watching the three cats sleeping together.

> "...This is adorable."

She quietly took several photographs.

---

### Nightshade

Looking over Star's shoulder.

> "...Three."

A pause.

> "...Cats."

Star nodded enthusiastically.

> "...Three cats."

Nightshade gave the smallest hint of a smile.

---

### Bao

> "...I'm making extra sandwiches."

She looked toward Neko.

> "...And no, Leo doesn't get all the tomatoes."

---

### Frost

Watching Taiga chase a drifting snowflake outside.

> "...She seems happier today."

---

### Director Lucious

During his afternoon rounds, he opened the door to Room N-07.

He expected a meeting.

Instead, he found:

One snow leopard.

Two sleeping cats.

One banana snake wrapped around the orange cat's tail.

He quietly closed the door again.

> "...I'll come back later."

---

### Aster

Aster entered the room carrying her whiteboard.

She looked at the three animals.

Then carefully drew:

🐆 + 🐈 + 🐈

Underneath, she wrote:

> **Too many cats.**

Taiga immediately rubbed against her leg.

Neko purred.

Miss Neko stretched lazily.

Aster erased the sentence.

She replaced it with:

> **Perfect amount of cats.**

---

### Leo

Leo showed absolutely no preference between them.

Throughout the afternoon, he alternated sleeping on:

- Taiga's back.
    
- Neko's tail.
    
- Miss Neko's stomach.
    

Veterinary staff concluded that Leo's primary criterion for choosing a sleeping spot remained unchanged:

> **Warm.**

---

### Mart

Mart landed on the top of a bookshelf and watched the three felines for several minutes.

According to Taiga's translation, he eventually commented,

> "...Your family keeps getting furrier."

Taiga looked around the room.

Then at Neko.

Then at Miss Neko.

She couldn't help letting out a soft, amused purr.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga voluntarily remained in her snow leopard form for the entirety of the day.

Neko and Miss Neko likewise spent much of the day in their respective cat forms, resulting in an unusually peaceful atmosphere within Room N-07.

Star and Nightshade have not yet disclosed their own transformation abilities to Taiga.

Medical reports no concerns.

Residential Services, however, recommends performing a quick headcount before closing any doors—just to make sure all three felines are accounted for.

---

### **Closing Observation**

As evening settled over the Blacksite, the heater in Room N-07 hummed softly against the Icelandic cold.

Three furry figures lay asleep together on the thick rug.

Taiga, the little snow leopard, rested comfortably in the middle.

On one side, Neko's orange cat form slept with a paw lazily stretched toward her.

On the other, Miss Neko's calico form was curled into a tight ball.

Leo was draped across all three at once, somehow finding room to nap without disturbing any of them.

From her chair, Aster smiled, picked up her marker, and quietly wrote on her whiteboard:

> **Best Cat Day Ever.**

No one disagreed.
