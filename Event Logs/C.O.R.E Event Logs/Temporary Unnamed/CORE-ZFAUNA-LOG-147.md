# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-147**

### **DAILY RESIDENT OBSERVATION — SUNBRINGER SIBLINGS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL / ROUTINE LOG

---

## **SUMMARY**

The following log documents a typical day involving Taiga and Mart Sunbringer.

No containment breaches occurred.

No injuries requiring Medical intervention occurred.

The only reported theft involved tomatoes.

---

## **08:14 — BREAKFAST**

Taiga and Mart were observed eating breakfast together.

As usual, both chose nearly identical salads.

Taiga enthusiastically announced that tomatoes were "the best part."

Mart quietly agreed.

Several seconds later...

Both tomato slices disappeared.

Security footage identified the culprit.

**Sleepy Leo**, Star's pet banana snake.

The snake had silently stretched across the table, delicately stolen both tomato slices, and retreated without either sibling noticing.

Taiga looked at her plate.

Then at Mart's.

> "...Did you eat mine?"

Mart looked equally confused.

> "...I thought you ate mine."

Several seconds passed before they noticed Sleepy Leo swallowing the evidence.

Taiga gasped dramatically.

> "...HE STOLE OUR TOMATOES!"

Sleepy Leo showed no remorse.

---

## **10:37 — SHIELD TEST**

Taiga requested permission to test Mart's Nekonium riot shield.

Mart agreed.

With one condition.

> "...Don't break it."

Taiga smiled confidently.

> "...Got it."

The next twenty minutes consisted of increasingly questionable "tests."

Including:

- Throwing rocks.
    
- Hitting it with a training hammer.
    
- Shooting it with rubber rounds.
    
- Standing on it.
    
- Attempting to Air Jump onto it.
    

None produced visible damage.

Finally, Taiga sighed.

> "...Fine..."

> "...It's indestructible."

Mart corrected her politely.

> "...Very durable."

Taiga immediately wrote "indestructible" on the testing form anyway.

Research later amended the wording.

---

## **14:05 — ECLIPSE TRAINING**

The siblings transformed **STAR** and **MOON** into their Eclipse configuration.

**Solar** and **Lunar** were tested during close-quarters drills.

Results remained consistent with previous evaluations.

Taiga aggressively pushed forward using Solar's longer barrel and greater ammunition capacity.

Mart advanced steadily behind his riot shield, firing Lunar one-handed with remarkable precision.

During one drill, Taiga accidentally stepped directly into Mart's shield.

She bounced backward.

Mart didn't move.

Taiga rubbed her forehead.

> "...Okay..."

> "...Maybe it _is_ indestructible."

Mart smiled.

> "...Very durable."

---

## **16:42 — THE GREAT GLIDING ARGUMENT**

While returning to the residential wing, Taiga launched herself into the air with an Air Jump before gliding across the courtyard.

She landed gracefully.

Folded her wings proudly.

And announced,

> "...See?"

> "...I'm flying now."

Mart looked at her thoughtfully.

Then answered,

> "...No."

Taiga blinked.

> "...What?"

> "...I literally just flew."

Mart shook his head.

> "...You glided."

> "...Different."

Taiga immediately objected.

> "...Gliding is flying!"

Mart remained calm.

> "...Flying means making lift."

> "...You borrowed momentum."

Taiga dramatically pointed toward the sky.

> "...I WAS IN THE AIR."

> "...THAT COUNTS."

Mart folded his arms.

> "...Birds glide."

> "...Birds also flap."

Taiga pointed accusingly at her wings.

> "...THEY WERE OPEN."

Mart nodded.

> "...Exactly."

> "...Open."

A growing audience gathered around the increasingly serious debate.

---

## **PUBLIC OPINIONS**

### Star Dawnbringer

> "...I think both count."

Neither sibling accepted this answer.

---

### Bao

> "...If you're smiling while airborne..."

She shrugged.

> "...Close enough."

---

### Frost

After listening quietly for several minutes:

> "...Gliding..."

A pause.

> "...Is controlled falling."

Taiga looked betrayed.

---

### Neko

Neko solved nothing.

He simply walked past saying,

> "...You're both technically wrong somehow."

Then continued walking.

---

### Miss Neko

Miss Neko wrote on a nearby whiteboard:

> **Flight:** Generates sustained lift.

> **Gliding:** Converts existing momentum into lift.

She underlined both.

Taiga looked at the board.

Then quietly erased the word "Gliding."

Replacing it with:

> **Stylish Flying**

Mart sighed.

---

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral laughed so hard she had to lean against the wall.

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious overheard the discussion while passing through the courtyard.

Without slowing down, he commented,

> "...If you arrive where you intended, I don't particularly care what you call it."

He kept walking.

Neither sibling found that satisfying.

---

## **END OF DAY**

That evening, Taiga and Mart returned to their room.

Sleepy Leo was already asleep on Mart's bed.

Curled into a tiny yellow spiral.

Between them...

Sat two untouched tomato slices.

No one knew where they had come from.

Taiga narrowed her eyes.

> "...He's bribing us."

Mart looked at the peacefully sleeping snake.

Then picked up one tomato slice.

> "...It's working."

Taiga laughed.

Then split the tomatoes in half.

One for herself.

One for Mart.

Sleepy Leo remained asleep the entire time.

Or at least...

He convincingly pretended to be.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Research concludes that, despite frequent arguments over highly technical subjects such as the definition of flight, Taiga and Mart's relationship has returned to a healthy, sibling-like dynamic.

The current longest-standing unresolved dispute remains officially documented as:

> **"Does gliding count as flying?"**

No consensus has been reached.
