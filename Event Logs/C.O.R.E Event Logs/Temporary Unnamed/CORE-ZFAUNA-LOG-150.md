# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-150**

### **FULL-SCALE ASSAULT — DEFENSE OF APRT-70 "HEAVEN'S BLACKSITE"**

**Classification:** OMEGA BLACK – INTERNAL SECURITY RECORD

---

## **SUMMARY**

At **06:41**, APRT-70 "Heaven's Blacksite" entered full lockdown following a coordinated assault by armed personnel originating from **Pinewood Laboratories**.

Subsequent investigation determined Pinewood had suffered a catastrophic internal corruption event, resulting in the deployment of nearly its entire armed response force against the Blacksite.

Recovered communications indicate the attackers believed they were assaulting an ordinary research installation.

They were not.

---

## **06:41 — FIRST CONTACT**

Long-range surveillance detected multiple armored convoys approaching all perimeter gates simultaneously.

Mortars.

Armored vehicles.

Attack drones.

Heavy infantry.

Within seconds, every alarm throughout the Blacksite activated.

> **"GENERAL ALERT."**

> **"GENERAL ALERT."**

> **"ALL SECURITY PERSONNEL TO DEFENSIVE POSITIONS."**

Director Lucious calmly closed the report he had been reading.

He stood.

Adjusted his coat.

Activated the site-wide command channel.

> **"All personnel."**

A brief pause.

> **"...Defend the Blacksite."**

---

# **PERIMETER SECURITY**

The first wave never reached the walls.

Perimeter Security opened fire almost simultaneously from every tower.

Heavy machine guns.

Anti-material rifles.

Automated emplacements.

Entire sections of the attacking formation collapsed before entering effective engagement range.

One security officer calmly remarked over the radio:

> "...They're advancing in a straight line."

Another answered,

> "...Convenient."

---

# **SECURITY DIVISION**

As breaches began appearing along the outer checkpoints, Security Guards formed overlapping firing lines.

Shield teams moved first.

Riflemen followed.

Every corridor became a layered defense.

One by one, attempted breakthroughs failed.

A rookie guard nervously asked his squad leader,

> "...How many are there?"

The veteran reloaded.

> "...Enough."

---

# **TAIGA SUNBRINGER**

Taiga drew **STAR**.

The white revolver thundered across the battlefield.

Each shot forced heavily armored attackers off their feet.

When ammunition began running low, she transformed STAR into **SOLAR**.

The pump-action shotgun echoed across the courtyard.

Every blast scattered hostile formations attempting to rush defensive positions.

She laughed despite the chaos.

> "...Now _this_ is why I wanted a shotgun!"

---

# **MART SUNBRINGER**

Mart unfolded his luminous wings.

The Nekonium Riot Shield locked into place.

He advanced without hesitation.

Incoming fire struck the shield harmlessly.

Behind it...

**LUNAR** barked repeatedly.

The semi-automatic shotgun cleared corridors methodically.

Personnel quickly adapted.

Wherever Mart walked...

Others followed.

His shield became a moving wall.

---

# **THE ECLIPSE TRANSFORMATION**

During a lull in the fighting, the siblings exchanged a glance.

No words were needed.

STAR.

MOON.

SOLAR.

LUNAR.

Mechanisms shifted.

The paired shotguns briefly became **ECLIPSE** once more before transitioning into **TWILIGHT**.

Twin 10mm Nekonium Magnum SMGs roared to life.

The siblings advanced side by side.

One covering left.

The other right.

Neither weapon could be distinguished from the other.

Neither operator needed to.

---

# **STAR DAWNBRINGER**

Star stepped into the open courtyard.

Her angelic wings remained perfectly still.

She simply floated upward.

Hostile drones redirected toward her.

She raised one hand.

Hundreds of radiant spears of light materialized overhead.

The drones vanished almost instantly.

Taiga looked upward.

> "...She's showing off."

Mart nodded.

> "...A little."

---

# **NIGHTSHADE DAWNBRINGER**

Nightshade moved almost invisibly through damaged hallways.

By the time attackers realized she had passed...

Their weapons had already been disabled.

Those who attempted pursuit found only fading trails of light.

One hostile transmission recovered afterward simply read:

> **"...Where did she go?"**

No answer followed.

---

# **ASTRAL SUNBRINGER**

Astral's radiant wings spread across the defensive line.

Rather than attacking directly, she shielded evacuation routes and civilians, intercepting incoming fire before it could reach them.

One frightened technician later recalled:

> "...It felt like standing behind sunlight."

---

# **BAO**

Bao raised one hand.

Moisture condensed instantly.

The battlefield transformed.

Walls of flowing water redirected explosions.

Hostile vehicles lost traction as the ground became a sheet of ice and water.

Several attackers found themselves sliding helplessly before being disarmed.

At one point, Bao casually swam through the air to rescue an isolated security team.

A guard blinked.

> "...She..."

Another simply answered,

> "...Don't question it."

---

# **FROST**

Frost advanced calmly through the western gate.

Where Bao flowed...

Frost solidified.

Entire roads froze beneath advancing enemy vehicles.

Engines stalled.

Wheels locked.

The assault ground to a halt.

Her rifle fired only when necessary.

Each shot was deliberate.

---

# **NEKO**

Neko finally stepped outside carrying **Neko's Claws**.

The plasma scythe ignited with a brilliant white edge.

Entire defensive sectors paused just long enough to watch.

The scythe swept once.

Hostile barricades ceased existing.

Moments later...

The weapon transformed.

The plasma assault rifle configuration illuminated.

Disciplined bursts of incandescent plasma dismantled heavy equipment with surgical precision.

Taiga quietly whispered,

> "...He's still cheating."

---

# **MISS NEKO**

Director Lucious finally authorized deployment.

Miss Neko rested **Bliss of Whitefall** on her shoulder.

Every radio channel fell silent.

She fired once.

There was no explosion.

The distant mountainside simply...

Wasn't there anymore.

The resulting shockwave permanently erased the attackers' primary staging position.

One Perimeter Security officer slowly lowered his binoculars.

> "...I'm..."

A long pause.

> "...Not writing that report."

---

# **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Lucious entered the battlefield only after confirming civilian sectors were secure.

His weapon never remained visible long enough for surveillance to identify it.

Every engagement involving him ended before nearby cameras could focus.

Recovered hostile communications included one final transmission:

> **"...Who _are_ these people?!"**

---

# **END OF ENGAGEMENT**

At **07:58**, surviving Pinewood personnel surrendered.

Medical teams immediately began treating both Blacksite personnel and captured attackers.

Interrogations later confirmed the assault had been launched under false intelligence after Pinewood's leadership had been compromised from within.

The attacking force had expected a conventional research facility.

Instead...

They encountered the Blacksite.

---

# **POST-ENGAGEMENT**

Silence gradually returned.

Weapons were unloaded.

Barricades repaired.

Smoke drifted into the morning sky.

Frost quietly looked around at the exhausted defenders.

Then spoke.

> "...Everyone."

Several people looked over.

> "...Ice cream?"

The courtyard became unexpectedly quiet.

Taiga immediately raised her hand.

> "...Chocolate!"

Star followed.

> "...Mint!"

Bao smiled.

> "...Strawberry."

Mart thought for a moment.

> "...Vanilla."

Neko shrugged.

> "...Cookies and cream."

Miss Neko nodded.

> "...Matcha."

Leo, brushing dust from his jacket, added,

> "...Whatever's cold."

Director Lucious sighed, almost amused.

> "...Very well."

An hour later...

The same people who had defended the Blacksite from a full-scale assault were sitting together outside the cafeteria, eating ice cream beneath a clear sky.

Taiga bumped Mart's shoulder.

> "...Good job."

Mart smiled faintly.

> "...You too, Big sis."

Astral watched them from nearby.

For the first time that morning...

She allowed herself to relax.

### **Closing Observation**

The security footage from the final hour of the incident contains two scenes recorded only minutes apart.

In the first, defenders stand shoulder to shoulder against an overwhelming assault.

In the second, those same defenders argue over who received the biggest scoop of ice cream.

Research archived both recordings together.

The accompanying note reads:

> **"The Blacksite is defended not only by extraordinary people... but by a family that always finds a reason to smile once the danger has passed."**
