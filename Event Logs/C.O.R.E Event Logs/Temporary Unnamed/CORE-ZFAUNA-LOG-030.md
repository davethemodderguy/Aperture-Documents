# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-030**

### **POST-INCIDENT OBSERVATION — PERSISTENT DROWSINESS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – MEDICAL / INTERNAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Several days after Leo's accidental bite, Medical has confirmed that Taiga has made a full physiological recovery.

However...

Her personality has become noticeably more relaxed.

Or, as Neko described it:

> "...She has somehow become even sleepier."

---

# **I. GENERAL BEHAVIOR**

Recent observations indicate that Taiga now:

- Sleeps significantly more than before.
    
- Frequently yawns throughout the day.
    
- Is slower to react to her surroundings.
    
- Occasionally loses focus during simple conversations.
    
- Has developed an impressive talent for falling asleep almost anywhere.
    

Medical emphasizes that she remains healthy.

She is simply...

Very lazy.

---

# **II. ENVIRONMENTAL AWARENESS**

Personnel have also noticed that Taiga has become considerably less aware of her surroundings when tired.

This culminated in an incident during routine maintenance.

---

# **III. THE PAINT INCIDENT**

Earlier that afternoon, Star had been helping Neko test the cutting performance of **Neko's Claws**.

The test was successful.

Perhaps...

A little too successful.

One section of the training room wall no longer existed.

Neko sighed.

Collected repair materials.

And began patching the damage.

Nearby sat an open bucket of white wall paint.

At approximately **14:37**, Taiga wandered into the room.

Still half asleep.

Still yawning.

She looked around vaguely.

Spotted the bucket.

Picked it up with both hands.

And slowly began lifting it toward her face.

Neko happened to glance over.

He froze.

"...Taiga."

No response.

She continued raising the bucket.

"...Taiga."

Still nothing.

Realizing what was about to happen, Neko hurried over and gently took the bucket from her hands.

Taiga blinked several times.

Looked at the bucket.

Then at Neko.

> "...That's..."

A pause.

> "...Not milk."

Neko stared.

> "...No."

> "...It's paint."

Taiga's ears folded back.

> "...That explains the smell..."

---

# **IV. AFTERMATH**

The room remained completely silent for several seconds.

Then Star burst into laughter.

Bao nearly dropped the tray of cupcakes she had just brought in.

Even Nightshade briefly covered her face to hide a smile.

Taiga, thoroughly embarrassed, quietly accepted the glass of water Miss Neko handed her instead.

---

> "...We are labeling the paint buckets."

---

Still laughing.

---

> "...Please ask before drinking anything that comes in a bucket."

Taiga nodded very seriously.

> "...Okay."

---

---

---

Closing the incident report.

> "...Recommendation."

> "...Keep hazardous materials above Taiga's eye level."

---

### Aster

Aster wrote on her whiteboard.

> **Paint is not a beverage.**

Taiga quietly nodded.

> "...I know that now..."

---

### Leo

Leo slept through the entire incident.

According to Taiga's translation after he woke up:

> "...Good."

> "...Paint probably tastes bad."

---

### Mart

Mart, who had watched everything from the top of a cabinet, gave a single disapproving hoot.

When asked what he had "said," Taiga translated with an embarrassed smile.

> "...He said I need more sleep..."

---

## **MEDICAL NOTE**

Medical has found no evidence of neurological injury or poisoning.

Taiga's increased sleepiness and occasional absent-mindedness are currently being monitored, but she remains healthy and fully capable of carrying out her normal daily activities.

Staff have simply been advised to keep an extra eye on her whenever she's wandering around while half asleep.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Later that evening, the repaired wall looked as good as new.

Star apologized for the hole.

Neko accepted the apology.

Taiga walked into the room carrying a mug.

Neko immediately asked:

> "...What's in it?"

Taiga looked inside.

> "...Hot chocolate."

Neko smiled.

> "...Good."

After the events of the afternoon, everyone agreed it was worth checking.
