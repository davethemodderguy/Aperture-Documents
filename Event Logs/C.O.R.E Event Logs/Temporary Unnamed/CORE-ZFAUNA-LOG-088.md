# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-088**

### **RESIDENTIAL NOTE — MART'S PREFERRED FORM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following Mart's first successful humanoid transformation, several residents wondered whether he would begin spending more time as a human.

The answer was obtained the following morning.

It was a very enthusiastic **no**.

---

## **INTERVIEW**

With Taiga translating where necessary, Neko decided to simply ask.

> **Neko:** "So... why do you like being an owl so much?"

Mart thought for nearly a minute.

Then transformed just long enough to answer.

> "...Flying."

> "...It's peaceful."

> "...People expect me to talk when I'm human."

> "...As an owl..."

He smiled softly.

> "...I can just listen."

He looked at Taiga.

> "...I like sitting on her shoulder."

> "...I can watch over everyone."

After saying that, he transformed back into an owl almost immediately.

Landing exactly where he always did.

On Taiga's shoulder.

---

## **ASTRAL**

Astral wasn't surprised in the slightest.

She chuckled.

> "...He's always preferred feathers."

She smiled at the little owl.

> "...Even when he was younger."

Mart gave a proud little hoot.

---

## **STAR**

Star crossed her arms dramatically.

> "...So you're telling me..."

> "...You're cute as a boy..."

> "...And even cuter as an owl?"

Mart blinked once.

Then gave an approving hoot.

"...He says yes," Taiga translated.

Star laughed.

> "...Fair enough."

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Watching Mart settle comfortably onto Taiga's shoulder once again, Director Lucious remarked,

> "...Some people find peace in who they naturally are."

He returned to his paperwork.

> "...There's no reason to ask them to be anything else."

---

## **MEDICAL NOTE**

Medical Services has confirmed there is no health-related reason for Mart to remain in either form.

His choice appears to be entirely personal.

Staff are instructed to respect his preference.

No further attempts to persuade him to remain human are recommended.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

- Humanoid transformation: **Voluntary.**
    
- Preferred form: **Owl.**
    
- Reason (self-reported):
    
    - Flying is peaceful.
        
    - Prefers listening over talking.
        
    - Enjoys watching over Taiga and the others from her shoulder.
        
- Psychological assessment: **Content.**
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

That evening, Taiga walked through the residential wing with Mart perched comfortably on her shoulder.

Someone jokingly called out,

> "Hey, Mart! Human form?"

Mart looked in their direction.

He fluffed his feathers.

Turned around.

Tucked his head beneath one wing.

And promptly fell asleep.

Taiga laughed quietly.

> "...That's his answer."

No one asked again.

After all...

Some brothers preferred sneakers.

Mart preferred wings.
