## **RECURRING INCIDENT**

Despite Director Lucious's standing order not to pressure Mart regarding the scar on Taiga's left arm, the subject continues to arise.

Most personnel ask out of simple curiosity.

They rarely know Mart's reaction beforehand.

Unfortunately...

The outcome has become predictable.

---

### **OBSERVED RESPONSE**

When someone asks:

> "Mart, do you know where Taiga got that scar?"

Mart immediately becomes distressed.

In his owl form:

- His feathers puff up.
    
- He backs away.
    
- He often takes flight without another sound.
    

In his human form, the response is significantly stronger.

Documented reactions include:

- Freezing in place.
    
- Trembling.
    
- Difficulty speaking.
    
- Tears forming almost immediately.
    
- Covering his ears.
    
- Running from the room.
    

On one occasion he managed only a single sentence before fleeing.

> "...Please..."

> "...Don't ask..."

Medical notes that Mart's fear is not one of being punished.

Rather, it appears to stem from reliving whatever event caused the scar.

---

## **RESIDENTIAL RESPONSE**

After repeated incidents, the residents of Room N-07 quietly developed an unspoken habit.

Whenever someone unknowingly asked Mart about the scar...

Someone else would gently change the subject before the conversation could continue.

It became second nature.

Not because they wanted to hide the truth.

Because they wanted to protect Mart until he was ready.

### **Closing Observation**

That evening, Mart sat in the common room in his human form, quietly reading beside Taiga.

A newly assigned researcher walked past and noticed the scar beneath the edge of Taiga's sleeve.

Without thinking, they asked,

> "...Mart, do you know what happened to her arm?"

The room fell silent.

Before Mart could react, Astral calmly answered from the kitchen,

> "...He doesn't talk about it."

The researcher immediately apologized.

Mart's hands, which had begun to shake, slowly steadied.

He looked toward Astral.

She smiled reassuringly.

He returned the smile—small, but genuine.

For today...

That was enough.
