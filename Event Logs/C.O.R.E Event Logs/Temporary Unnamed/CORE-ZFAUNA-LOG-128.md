# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-128**

### **MEDICAL EMERGENCY — TAIGA SUNBRINGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – MEDICAL / PSYCHOLOGICAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the anomalous disappearance of all known owl feathers shed by Mart Sunbringer, Taiga's psychological condition has deteriorated significantly.

Medical Services has declared her condition an **active psychological and physical health emergency**.

---

## **OBSERVATION**

Personnel first noticed Taiga searching through the residential wing late at night.

She checked bookshelves.

Desks.

Storage boxes.

Drawers.

Anywhere she thought one of Mart's feathers might still remain.

She found nothing.

One particular item drew Medical's attention.

For months, Taiga had used one of Mart's naturally shed feathers as a writing pen.

It had become one of her most treasured possessions.

When she opened the drawer where she kept it...

The feather was gone.

Only the metal pen tip remained.

She held the empty pen in silence.

She never put it back.

---

## **DECLINING CONDITION**

Over the following days, Taiga's daily routine deteriorated rapidly.

Medical staff documented the following:

- Repeatedly refusing meals.
    
- Consuming little to no water.
    
- Remaining awake throughout multiple nights.
    
- Neglecting personal care.
    
- Spending prolonged periods sitting alone in Mart's room.
    

She did not become aggressive.

She did not lash out.

She simply...

Stopped taking care of herself.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT**

When finally persuaded to attend a psychological evaluation, Taiga answered only a handful of questions.

The final exchange was recorded.

**Psychologist:**

> "Why aren't you eating?"

A long silence.

Then...

> **Taiga:** "...Because everyone keeps taking care of me."

Another pause.

> "...I don't deserve it."

The psychologist gently asked,

> "Why do you believe that?"

Taiga lowered her head.

Her voice had almost no strength left.

> "...Everyone who gets close to me gets hurt."

She looked at the empty pen tip resting in her hand.

> "...Mom lost me."

> "...Dad worried because of me."

> "...Star cried because of me."

> "...Everyone searched for me."

A tear fell onto the desk.

> "...And Mart..."

She couldn't finish the sentence.

After nearly a minute of silence...

She whispered,

> "...I only hurt people."

---

## **MEDICAL RESPONSE**

Medical Services immediately placed Taiga under continuous observation.

She has not been confined.

However, staff have been instructed to ensure she is never left completely unattended while respecting her need for personal space.

Hydration and nutrition are now being closely monitored.

Psychological Services warn that prolonged refusal of food, water, and sleep presents a serious risk to her recovery.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral entered Mart's room late one evening.

Taiga was sitting on the floor, still holding the empty pen tip.

Astral quietly sat beside her.

Neither spoke for several minutes.

Finally, Astral gently rested her hand over Taiga's.

> "...You are my daughter."

Another pause.

> "...That has never been something you had to earn."

Taiga began crying again.

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious received the emergency report personally.

He closed the file.

Looked toward the residential wing.

And quietly said,

> "...Don't let her disappear too."

---

### Neko

Neko found the empty pen lying on Taiga's desk.

He turned it over in his hand.

Only the metal tip remained.

He set it back exactly where he found it.

> "...He always liked lending you feathers."

His voice faltered.

> "...I hope he does again."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Guilt..."

She folded her hands together.

> "...Has a way of convincing good people that they deserve to suffer."

---

### Bao

Bao continued preparing meals.

Even when Taiga refused them.

She never argued.

She only left a note beside each tray.

> "I'll cook again tomorrow."

---

### Frost

Frost quietly collected the empty meal trays each evening.

She noticed that the untouched portions had become smaller.

Not because Taiga was eating.

Because Bao refused to stop hoping.

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star walked into Mart's room carrying a fresh notebook.

She placed it beside Taiga.

> "...When he comes home..."

She smiled through watery eyes.

> "...You'll need something to write with again."

Taiga stared at the notebook for a long time.

She couldn't bring herself to open it.

---

### Nightshade Dawnbringer

Nightshade remained outside Mart's room after everyone else had left.

She knew Taiga needed someone nearby.

Even if Taiga never looked up.

---

### Leo

Leo quietly replaced the blanket over Taiga after she finally drifted into an exhausted sleep against Mart's bed.

He looked toward the empty pillow.

Then back at Taiga.

> "...Come back soon, kid."

He wasn't looking at Taiga.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

### Taiga Sunbringer

- Severe emotional distress.
    
- Refusing adequate food and water.
    
- Significant sleep deprivation.
    
- Under continuous medical observation.
    

### Mart Sunbringer

- **Status:** Missing.
    
- No confirmed sightings since the luminous feather incident.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

During routine rounds just before dawn, a nurse found Taiga asleep for the first time in nearly three days.

She hadn't made it to her own bed.

She had fallen asleep on the floor beside Mart's.

The empty pen tip was still clutched tightly in one hand.

The nurse carefully reached down to move it aside.

Even in her sleep...

Taiga's fingers refused to let go.

The pen no longer had its feather.

But she held onto what remained...

As though letting go of the last piece would mean admitting there was nothing left to wait for.
