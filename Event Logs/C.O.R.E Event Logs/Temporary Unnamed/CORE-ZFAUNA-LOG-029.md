# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-029**

### **MINOR FAUNA INCIDENT — LEO'S BITE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – VETERINARY / MEDICAL

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

At approximately **13:07**, Taiga was carrying Leo back to Room N-07 after lunch.

As usual, Leo had been asleep around her scarf for most of the afternoon.

While Taiga attempted to reposition him without waking him, Leo suddenly startled awake.

Mistaking her moving hand for an unfamiliar object, he instinctively delivered a quick defensive bite before immediately realizing who he had bitten.

Leo froze.

Taiga blinked.

"...Ow."

The tiny snake immediately loosened his grip and gently nudged Taiga's hand with his head.

According to Taiga's translation:

> "...Sorry..."

---

# **I. MEDICAL RESPONSE**

Medical personnel were notified as a precaution.

The bite itself was superficial.

Within a few minutes, however, Taiga began yawning repeatedly.

She rubbed one eye.

Then the other.

> "...Why am I..."

_Yawn._

> "...So..."

Another yawn.

> "...Sleepy..."

Medical compared her symptoms to previous documentation regarding Leo's venom.

The conclusion matched earlier findings.

Leo's venom is exceptionally weak.

Rather than causing serious illness, its primary effect resembles a mild sedative.

Medical summarized the incident simply.

> **"Comparable to a strong sleeping aid."**

No additional treatment was required beyond observation.

---

# **II. MART'S RESPONSE**

Several personnel instinctively looked toward Mart after the incident.

The owl remained perfectly calm.

He did not flare his feathers.

He did not attempt to intervene.

He simply watched Leo for a moment before relaxing again.

Later, Taiga translated Mart's thoughts.

> "...He's harmless."

> "...Just clumsy."

Leo had already fallen back asleep.

---

# **III. THE AFTERNOON**

By early afternoon, Taiga was struggling to stay awake.

She tried reading with Aster.

She fell asleep halfway through the first page.

She attempted to help Neko organize laboratory notes.

She fell asleep sitting in the chair.

She joined Bao while cupcakes were baking.

She fell asleep before they finished rising.

Eventually, Miss Neko gave up trying to keep her awake.

> "...Go to bed."

Taiga didn't argue.

She was asleep before her head touched the pillow.

---

# **IV. "CONTAGIOUS SLEEPINESS"**

The event revived one of the Blacksite's oldest running jokes.

> **"Leo's sleepiness is contagious."**

Medical would like to clarify that this statement is **not literally true**.

However...

After observing Taiga spend nearly the entire remainder of the day asleep following the bite, several staff members jokingly amended the saying.

> "...Maybe it is."

The phrase quickly spread through the residential wing.

---

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga experienced temporary drowsiness following an accidental defensive bite from Leo.

Medical confirms:

- The bite caused no lasting injury.
    
- Symptoms were consistent with Leo's previously documented mild venom.
    
- Rest was considered sufficient treatment.
    
- Full recovery was expected after the sedative effects wore off.
    

Leo appeared noticeably apologetic following the incident and remained close to Taiga for the rest of the day.

---

### **Closing Observation**

By evening, Room N-07 was unusually quiet.

Taiga slept peacefully beneath a warm blanket.

Leo was curled comfortably around the pillow beside her.

Mart stood watch on the headboard, eyes half-closed but alert.

Aster looked at the sleeping trio before writing one final sentence on her whiteboard.

> **The sleepiest room in the Blacksite.**

No one disagreed.
