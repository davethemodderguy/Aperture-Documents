# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-058**

### **ABILITY DEMONSTRATION — FROST WALKER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – ANOMALOUS TESTING

---

## **TEST SUMMARY**

Following Medical clearance after the manifestation of her dorsal rune, Taiga voluntarily participated in a supervised demonstration of her newly awakened ability, designated **Frost Walker**.

Testing took place on the shores of **Whitefall Lake** under the supervision of C.O.R.E. researchers, Medical personnel, and several of Room N-07's residents.

Weather conditions were ideal.

Ambient temperature: **-14°C**

Wind speed: Moderate.

Water temperature: Near freezing.

---

## **DEMONSTRATION**

Taiga stood quietly at the edge of the lake.

She appeared noticeably nervous.

Director Lucious noticed her hesitation.

> "There's no pressure."

> "Only continue if you're comfortable."

Taiga took a slow breath.

"...Okay."

She stepped forward.

Instead of sinking—

A perfectly circular sheet of crystal-clear ice instantly formed beneath her foot.

It spread only enough to support her weight.

The ice was smooth, remarkably dense, and emitted a faint azure glow matching the rune on her back.

She cautiously took another step.

Another circle of ice appeared.

Then another.

Soon, a narrow frozen pathway stretched across the lake.

The surrounding water remained completely liquid.

Only the exact area beneath her feet froze.

---

## **OBSERVATIONS**

Researchers recorded the following:

- Ice forms before Taiga's foot fully contacts the water.
    
- The frozen surface consistently supports her weight without cracking.
    
- Ice thickness automatically adjusts according to the load being supported.
    
- The ability activates instinctively.
    
- No verbal commands or gestures are required.
    

Taiga herself appeared surprised by how natural the movement felt.

> "...It's like..."

She looked down at the ice.

> "...The lake already knows where I'm going."

---

## **ADDITIONAL TESTS**

### Sprint Test

Taiga accelerated into a run.

The frozen path extended beneath her in real time.

No loss of balance was observed.

---

### Sudden Stop

Taiga stopped abruptly.

The ice beneath her stabilized immediately without sliding.

---

### Jump Test

After jumping several meters forward, a fresh platform of ice formed before she landed.

Medical confirmed no conscious activation had occurred.

The ability responded automatically.

---

### Load Test

Neko volunteered.

> "...For science."

Taiga carefully took his hand.

Together they walked across the lake.

The ice thickened slightly beneath them but remained stable throughout.

No structural failure occurred.

---

## **SECONDARY ABILITY**

While returning to shore, Taiga accidentally demonstrated another application.

She instinctively raised one hand.

A waist-high wall of ice erupted from the lake beside her.

The structure measured approximately two meters wide and one and a half meters tall.

When asked if she intended to create it, Taiga shook her head.

> "...No..."

> "...I just wanted somewhere to lean."

Medical noted the construct remained solid for approximately twelve minutes before gradually melting into perfectly clean water.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Testing confirms Frost Walker to be a stable, instinctive ability with both defensive and traversal applications.

No adverse physical effects were observed following controlled use.

Medical continues to monitor the glowing rune responsible for the manifestation but currently considers the ability safe for supervised use.

---

### **Closing Observation**

As the demonstration concluded, everyone began walking back toward the Blacksite.

Taiga lingered for a moment.

She looked back across Whitefall Lake.

The frozen footprints she had created slowly melted away one by one until the surface was calm again, as though she had never crossed it.

She smiled softly.

Not because she had gained another ability.

But because, for the first time since the painful awakening of the rune...

She no longer associated it only with fear.

Now, when she looked at the glowing symbol on her back, she could also remember the quiet sound of her own footsteps across the water.
