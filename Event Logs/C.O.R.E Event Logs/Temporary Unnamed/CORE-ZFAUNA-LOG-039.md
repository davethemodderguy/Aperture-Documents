## **STATUS UPDATE**

During a routine inventory of personal belongings, Taiga presented two items that had remained carefully stored among her possessions since before her arrival at the Blacksite.

Unlike her scarf, tail ribbon, or snowflake earrings, these items had not been gifts from her roommates.

They were gifts from her mother.

Taiga has since begun wearing them every day.

---

# **I. CROSS HAIR PINS**

Taiga now wears a pair of small cross-shaped bobby pins near the right side of her bangs.

The pins are delicately crafted and surprisingly lightweight.

Metallurgical analysis performed by Neko identified the material as an alloy consisting of:

- High-purity gold.
    
- Nekonium alloy.
    

The Nekonium content is extremely small, serving primarily to reinforce the structure while maintaining its elegant appearance.

Although decorative, the pins are exceptionally durable.

---

### Neko's Notes

> "Whoever made these knew exactly what they were doing."

Microscopic examination found virtually no manufacturing imperfections.

The craftsmanship is comparable to Blacksite precision engineering despite being entirely handcrafted.

---

# **II. THE COLLAR**

The second item drew considerably more attention.

Around Taiga's neck now rests a simple black fabric collar.

Unlike Neko's or Miss Neko's collars, it bears no bell.

Instead, suspended from the front is a matte black metal cross.

The cross is cool to the touch and shows no signs of corrosion or wear despite its age.

Medical confirmed that the collar is comfortable and poses no health concerns.

Taiga instinctively touches the cross whenever she becomes nervous or deep in thought.

---

# **III. ORIGIN**

When Bao asked where the new accessories had come from, Taiga gently held the cross in her hand.

She smiled softly.

> "...Mom made them."

The room became quiet.

After a few moments, she continued.

> "...She said..."

She looked down at the collar.

> "...No matter where I go..."

A faint smile returned.

> "...I'd always carry a piece of home with me."

Nobody interrupted.

### **Closing Observation**

That evening, Taiga sat by the window watching fresh snow fall over the Blacksite.

The white scarf Bao and Aster had made rested around her shoulders.

The small bell on her tail jingled softly as it swayed.

The snowflake earrings caught the warm light from the room.

The golden cross hair pins held her bangs neatly in place.

And around her neck, the black cross rested quietly against the fabric of her collar.

Mart perched comfortably on her shoulder.

Leo slept around her scarf.

For a long while, Taiga simply watched the snow in silence, one hand lightly resting on the cross her mother had made.

It wasn't just an accessory.

It was a reminder that, even after everything she had lost, she still carried a part of her mother's love with her every day.
