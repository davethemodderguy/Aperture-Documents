# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-136**

### **RECOVERED EVIDENCE — TORN PHOTOGRAPH**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – PSYCHOLOGICAL / INTERNAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

At 20:18 on ██/██/20██, Taiga encountered Mart Sunbringer at the window of his bedroom once again.

The encounter lasted less than thirty seconds.

Mart spoke no words.

After his departure, Taiga recovered the remains of the photograph previously captioned **"Best sister ever."**

The condition of the photograph has become a significant concern for Psychological Services.

---

## **INCIDENT REPORT**

Mart was seated on the familiar windowsill.

His wings remained folded behind him.

His posture was noticeably slumped.

Unlike previous encounters, he was not holding the framed photograph.

Instead...

He held only a torn piece of it.

Taiga stopped in the doorway.

She didn't try to approach immediately.

She quietly said,

> "...Hi..."

Mart looked toward her.

There was no anger.

No resentment.

Only exhaustion.

He lowered his gaze.

A few moments later, he stood.

The wings of white light slowly unfolded.

Without saying a word...

He stepped backward through the open window.

And disappeared into the evening sky.

---

## **RECOVERED EVIDENCE**

After Mart's departure, Taiga noticed several pieces of paper lying on the bedroom floor beneath the window.

She slowly knelt beside them.

It was the photograph.

Or what remained of it.

The picture had been torn cleanly into two halves.

The half containing Taiga remained almost perfectly intact.

Only a crease marked its surface.

The half depicting Mart had been violently crushed.

The paper had been compressed repeatedly in someone's hand before being discarded onto the floor.

Several tears radiated outward from the center of his own image.

The damage was deliberate.

Not accidental.

---

## **HANDWRITTEN MESSAGE**

Written on the back of the crushed half, in handwriting confirmed to belong to Mart Sunbringer, were five words.

> **"I don't deserve her."**

No additional writing was found.

---

## **SUBJECT RESPONSE**

Witnesses reported that Taiga remained kneeling on the floor for several minutes.

She carefully smoothed the damaged half of the photograph against her knees.

Attempting to flatten the creases.

They would not disappear.

She aligned both halves together.

Trying to restore the picture.

The tear line remained plainly visible.

She whispered quietly,

> "...No..."

Then again.

Softer.

> "...No..."

She placed both halves against her chest.

No one attempted to interrupt her.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL NOTE**

Psychological Services conclude that the recovered message is consistent with Mart's previously documented statements, including:

> _"I'm unwanted."_

And later,

> _"I'm just keeping myself away from things I don't deserve."_

The newly recovered note suggests these beliefs remain deeply entrenched.

There is no evidence within the note indicating resentment toward Taiga.

Rather, the message reflects Mart's diminished perception of his own worth.

---

Astral gently examined the torn photograph after Taiga allowed her to.

She ran a finger over the crushed half.

Then quietly said,

> "...He didn't destroy you."

She looked at the damaged image of Mart.

> "...He tried to destroy himself."

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious reviewed a photograph of the evidence.

He stared longest at the handwritten message.

The report he signed contained only one additional instruction.

> "Preserve the original."

---

---

Taiga declined.

She carefully placed both halves inside a protective sleeve instead.

She did not want the tear to disappear.

---

She lightly touched the edge.

> "...Can be unfolded."

A pause.

> "...The marks remain."

---

Star looked at the two halves.

Her lower lip trembled.

> "...It's still one picture..."

No one answered.

---

He had left Taiga's side untouched.

Her concluding remark read:

> "He still sees her as someone worth protecting."

---

Leo quietly picked up the empty picture frame that still rested beside the bedside lamp.

He looked at it for a long time.

Then set it back exactly where it had always been.

> "...This room's still waiting."

---

### **Closing Observation**

Later that evening, Taiga returned alone to her room.

She placed the permanent feather beside the torn photograph.

For several minutes, she tried fitting the two halves together.

Again.

And again.

The picture aligned perfectly.

The tear never disappeared.

She traced the words on the back with trembling fingers.

> **"I don't deserve her."**

Very quietly, to the empty room, she answered the handwriting she wished could hear her.

> "...You never had to deserve me."

No reply came.

Only the soft glow of the feather beside the broken photograph illuminated the darkness.
