# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-054**

### **DIETARY OBSERVATION — TAIGA'S PREFERENCES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – NUTRITION / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following several months of observation, Nutrition Services has finalized Taiga's general dietary profile.

Contrary to common belief among newer personnel, Taiga is **not** vegetarian.

She regularly consumes meat as part of a balanced diet.

However, one fact remains unchanged.

Given the choice...

She almost always reaches for vegetables first.

---

## **DIETARY HABITS**

Taiga's meals typically consist of:

- Mixed salads.
    
- Leafy vegetables.
    
- Tomatoes.
    
- Carrots.
    
- Cucumbers.
    
- Bell peppers.
    
- Various leafy greens.
    
- Seasonal vegetables available through the Blacksite greenhouse.
    

These are usually accompanied by moderate portions of:

- Chicken.
    
- Beef.
    

She has no difficulty eating meat and enjoys both.

Medical has found no nutritional deficiencies.

Her diet is considered healthy and well-balanced.

---

## **THE QUESTION**

One mystery remains unresolved.

Personnel have asked Taiga countless times why she prefers vegetables over meat.

Her answers have always been remarkably consistent.

She simply smiles.

Then says,

> "...It's a secret."

Or,

> "...Maybe someday."

She has never elaborated further.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga continues to maintain excellent nutritional health.

Her preference for vegetables over meat remains unexplained by choice rather than by circumstance.

Personnel have gradually stopped trying to guess the reason.

If Taiga wishes to tell them one day...

She will.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Lunch was unusually quiet.

Taiga carried her usual tray to the table.

A fresh salad.

Grilled chicken.

A slice of beef.

Several tomatoes.

And, naturally...

A few Carolina Reapers.

She happily mixed everything together.

Star watched curiously.

> "...Still not going to tell us?"

Taiga looked up with a gentle smile.

> "...Not today."

She took another bite.

Across the table, Aster erased a question from her whiteboard before anyone else could ask it again.

Some mysteries, she decided, were better left until their owner was ready to share them.
