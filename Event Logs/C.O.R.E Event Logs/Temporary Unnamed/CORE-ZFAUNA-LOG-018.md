# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-018**

### **RECOVERY STATUS — TAIGA**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – MEDICAL / REHABILITATION

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following several weeks of treatment, rehabilitation, and medical observation, Taiga has made an excellent recovery.

Medical has officially declared her condition stable.

Although her injuries have healed well, she is still under temporary mobility restrictions.

Running, climbing, and extended walks remain prohibited until Medical completes her final evaluation.

Taiga has accepted these restrictions...

Somewhat reluctantly.

---

# **I. TRANSPORTATION PROBLEM**

Since Taiga is currently discouraged from walking long distances, a surprisingly simple solution has become standard practice.

Someone just...

Picks her up.

Despite being a growing snow leopard girl, Taiga is remarkably light.

Engineering jokingly attempted to quantify this during a discussion in the cafeteria.

Their unofficial conclusion:

> **"Approximately four Asters."**

The comparison somehow became the accepted unit of measurement.

---

# **II. COMMON SIGHTINGS**

Throughout the Blacksite, personnel have reported frequently seeing Taiga being carried from place to place.

### Neko

Usually carries her over one shoulder while discussing whatever invention currently occupies his mind.

Taiga has become very good at listening to engineering lectures.

Whether she understands all of them remains unknown.

---

### Miss Neko

Prefers carrying Taiga bridal-style.

According to Miss Neko:

> "...It's more comfortable."

Taiga quietly agrees.

---

### Bao

Almost always chooses piggyback rides.

Halfway through every trip, Bao starts chatting.

Halfway through the conversation...

Taiga is usually asleep.

---

### Frost

Carries Taiga carefully with both arms whenever they head outside.

Since Taiga can still control snow while being carried, Frost often walks through gentle swirling snowflakes the entire journey.

She claims it makes winter walks "more peaceful."

---

### Star

Frequently teleports short distances to avoid unnecessary walking.

Taiga still laughs every time the scenery changes instantly.

---

### Nightshade

Simply picks Taiga up without comment whenever she notices her trying to walk farther than Medical permits.

If Taiga protests...

Nightshade responds with the same sentence every time.

> "...Doctor's orders."

The discussion usually ends there.

---

# **III. ASTER**

Aster initially attempted to carry Taiga herself.

Unfortunately...

Taiga currently weighs approximately four Asters.

The experiment lasted less than three seconds before both gently toppled onto the heated rug in a fluffy heap.

Neither was hurt.

Aster immediately wrote on her whiteboard.

> **Too heavy.**

Taiga burst into laughter.

---

# **IV. LEO**

Leo quickly realized that being carried everywhere had unexpected advantages.

He no longer had to choose where to sleep.

If someone picked up Taiga...

Leo simply remained wrapped around her scarf.

As a result, personnel often observe:

Someone carrying Taiga.

Taiga carrying Leo.

Aster walking alongside them.

Engineering has updated its earlier joke.

> **"One Taiga equals four Asters and one Leo."**

No one knows why they felt the need to be that specific.

---

# **V. MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

Medical staff report that Taiga's recovery continues to exceed expectations.

She has faithfully followed rehabilitation instructions, although she occasionally tries to insist she can walk farther than recommended.

Fortunately, her roommates have become very effective at preventing this.

Medical has expressed appreciation for their cooperation.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Taiga**

- Recovering well.
    
- Temporarily restricted from excessive walking.
    
- Frequently transported around the Blacksite by friends and roommates.
    
- Continues to make excellent progress toward a full recovery.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

During lunchtime, security cameras captured a familiar scene.

Miss Neko was carrying Taiga toward the cafeteria.

Leo slept peacefully around Taiga's white scarf.

Aster trotted alongside them with her whiteboard.

As they passed through the hallway, one engineer looked up from his paperwork.

He smiled.

> "...Looks like the usual convoy is back."

It was.

And for the first time in weeks, that felt perfectly normal.
