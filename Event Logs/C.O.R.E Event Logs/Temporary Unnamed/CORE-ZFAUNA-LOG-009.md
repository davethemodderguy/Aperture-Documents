# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-009**

### **PERSONAL EFFECTS & BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION — TAIGA**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – INTERNAL / RECREATIONAL RECORD

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Taiga has received her first handmade gift since becoming a permanent resident of the Blacksite.

The gift was jointly made by **Bao** and **Aster**.

It has since become one of Taiga's favorite possessions.

---

# **I. THE SCARF**

As Iceland's winter continued, Bao decided that Taiga "needed something cozy."

Aster immediately volunteered to help.

While Bao handled most of the knitting, Aster contributed by carefully embroidering the ends of the scarf using a needle under Miss Neko's supervision.

The finished scarf was:

- Pure white.
    
- Thick and exceptionally warm.
    
- Long enough to wrap around Taiga several times.
    
- Decorated with small embroidered snowflakes and tiny paw prints near each end.
    

A small stitched label on the inside simply read:

> **For Taiga.**

---

# **II. THE GIFT**

When presented with the scarf, Taiga stared at it for several seconds.

She carefully picked it up.

Ran her fingers across the embroidery.

Then quietly wrapped it around her neck.

After adjusting it a few times...

She smiled.

Without saying anything...

She hugged Bao.

Then Aster.

Aster quickly scribbled on her whiteboard.

> **Looks warm.**

Taiga nodded enthusiastically.

> "...Very."

From that day onward, she wore the scarf almost every time she went outside.

---

# **III. SURPRISINGLY EASY TO CARRY**

Another observation has become increasingly well known throughout the Blacksite.

Despite being a growing snow leopard girl...

Taiga is remarkably easy to pick up.

Whether due to her relaxed nature or simple trust in those around her, she rarely objects when someone gently lifts her.

Instead, she instinctively relaxes.

Sometimes she even rests her head on the person's shoulder.

Personnel have jokingly compared her to "an oversized house cat."

---

### Neko

Frequently carries Taiga when she accidentally falls asleep after playing in the snow.

She never wakes up.

---

### Miss Neko

Can effortlessly lift Taiga into a chair whenever she falls asleep somewhere inconvenient.

Taiga usually responds by sleepily mumbling:

> "...Thanks..."

Before immediately drifting back to sleep.

---

### Bao

Has discovered that Taiga enjoys piggyback rides.

She now occasionally offers one after long walks outside.

---

### Frost

Initially hesitated, worried she might embarrass Taiga.

Instead, Taiga simply held out her arms.

Frost picked her up.

Taiga smiled.

> "...Comfy."

---

### Star

Often carries Taiga back indoors after particularly energetic snowball fights.

---

### Nightshade

Rarely initiates it herself.

However, on one occasion she quietly picked up a sleeping Taiga to move her away from a draft near an open maintenance door.

Taiga never woke up.

---

# **IV. ASTER & LEO**

Aster quickly realized that being carried had one unexpected benefit.

Whenever someone picked up Taiga...

Aster could simply climb into her lap.

Leo, naturally, saw no reason to change his routine.

He remained asleep around Taiga's shoulders.

As a result, personnel have frequently observed the following:

Someone carrying Taiga.

Taiga carrying Aster.

Aster carrying Leo.

Engineering unofficially refers to this arrangement as:

> **"Stack Overflow."**

---

### Director Lucious

Watching Taiga walk through the corridor with her new white scarf wrapped snugly around her neck.

He noticed the careful stitching along the ends.

He immediately recognized Bao's work...

And Aster's embroidered paw prints.

He smiled quietly.

> "...It suits her."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Taiga**

- Frequently wears the handmade white scarf created by Bao and Aster.
    
- Has become strongly attached to the gift.
    
- Remains unusually comfortable with trusted personnel gently picking her up or carrying her.
    
- Continues to spend much of her free time with Aster and Leo.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

A heavy snowfall covered the Blacksite grounds late that evening.

Taiga returned indoors with her scarf dusted in tiny snowflakes.

She had barely taken two steps into the warm residence before she yawned.

Neko chuckled.

Without a word, he gently picked her up.

Taiga instinctively rested her head against his shoulder.

Aster climbed into her lap.

Leo remained asleep around her neck.

No one said anything.

It had become a perfectly normal sight.
