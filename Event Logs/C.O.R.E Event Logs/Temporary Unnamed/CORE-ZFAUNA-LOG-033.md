# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-033**

### **BIOLOGICAL DISCOVERY — CRYOGENIC BLOOD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – MEDICAL / N.O.S.T.

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

During routine weapons maintenance in the training hall, Taiga accidentally received a small cut across the tip of one finger while handling her katana.

The injury itself was superficial.

The consequences were not.

As a single drop of blood struck the floor, sensors across the maintenance wing registered an instantaneous and dramatic temperature collapse.

Emergency engineering alarms activated throughout the lower utility levels.

---

# **I. INITIAL OBSERVATION**

Personnel nearby described the blood as unlike any biological fluid previously documented.

Rather than red, it emitted a faint, soft **white luminescence**, comparable to moonlight reflecting on fresh snow.

More concerning was its temperature.

The droplet froze the surrounding air almost immediately.

A thin layer of frost spread outward in every direction.

Within seconds, the floor beneath the droplet began fracturing.

---

# **II. STRUCTURAL DAMAGE**

Despite the extremely small volume involved, the cryogenic effect continued propagating downward through the reinforced flooring.

Engineering inspection later confirmed localized damage extending approximately **35 meters** beneath the incident site.

Affected infrastructure included:

- Reinforced structural concrete.
    
- Multiple insulated utility conduits.
    
- Coolant distribution piping.
    
- Industrial gas transfer lines.
    

The surrounding material had not melted or shattered.

Instead, it had become so cold that sections fractured under their own internal stress.

Fortunately, automated emergency valves isolated the damaged pipelines before any secondary failures occurred.

Repairs began immediately.

---

# **III. CONTAINMENT**

Medical personnel treated Taiga's finger with a standard dressing before any additional blood could escape.

The remaining droplet was isolated using specialized cryogenic containment equipment.

Attempts to warm the sample proved unsuccessful.

The blood remained at an immeasurably low temperature while continuing to emit its faint white glow.

N.O.S.T. researchers suspended further testing until appropriate containment procedures could be developed.

---

# **IV. TAIGA'S RESPONSE**

When shown the engineering report, Taiga stared at the page for several seconds.

Then quietly asked:

> "...I did all of that?"

After receiving a reluctant nod from Engineering, she looked down at the bandage on her finger.

> "...Mom always told me not to bleed if I could help it..."

The room became noticeably quieter.

---

---

---

# **VI. UPDATED MEDICAL PROTOCOL**

Effective immediately:

- Any injury likely to produce bleeding is to be treated without unnecessary delay.
    
- Personnel assisting Taiga should use approved protective equipment.
    
- Samples of Taiga's blood are to remain in dedicated cryogenic-safe containment.
    
- Engineering is to be notified following any accidental release, regardless of volume.
    

These measures are precautionary and are not intended to restrict Taiga's normal daily life.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga's blood has been confirmed to possess two previously undocumented properties:

- A soft white luminescence.
    
- An extreme cryogenic effect capable of rapidly freezing and damaging surrounding materials.
    

The underlying biological mechanism remains unknown.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Later that evening, Engineering finally completed repairs beneath the training hall.

Taiga quietly stood beside the freshly restored floor, looking at the small bandage on her finger.

Neko walked up beside her.

> "...The floor can be repaired."

He smiled reassuringly.

> "...You can't."

Taiga looked up.

Then smiled back.

From that day onward, she became just a little more careful whenever handling anything sharp.
