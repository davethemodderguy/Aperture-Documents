# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-132**

### **DIRECT CONTACT REPORT — THE FIRST PERMANENT FEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – PSYCHOLOGICAL / ANOMALOUS

---

## **SUMMARY**

At 20:09 on ██/██/20██, Taiga and Mart Sunbringer engaged in the longest recorded conversation since Mart's disappearance.

The exchange lasted approximately one minute.

It also marked the first documented instance of a luminous feather **remaining physically stable after coming into Taiga's possession.**

---

## **LOCATION**

**Residential Wing — Mart Sunbringer's Bedroom**

Mart appeared at the bedroom window as he had on previous evenings.

He was seated quietly on the windowsill.

His wings of white light were folded behind him.

The framed photograph was absent.

Neither sibling moved for several seconds.

---

## **TRANSCRIPT**

Taiga stood just inside the doorway.

She did not step any closer.

She knew by now that approaching too quickly would only make him leave.

Her voice was quiet.

Almost pleading.

> **Taiga:** "...Mart..."

He looked at her.

His expression was calm.

But distant.

Taiga lowered her head.

She had rehearsed countless apologies over the past weeks.

None of them came out.

Instead...

She asked the only thing she could think of.

> **Taiga:** "...If you're hurting me..."

She struggled to keep her voice steady.

> "...It's working."

A long silence.

She looked back at him.

Tears had already begun running down her face.

> "...Please..."

> "...Stay."

Mart closed his eyes.

His shoulders rose and fell with a slow breath.

When he finally answered...

His voice was so quiet that Security Audio Enhancement was required to recover the recording.

> **Mart:** "...I'm not you."

Another pause.

His eyes never left the floor.

> "...I'm just keeping myself away..."

His voice faltered.

> "...From things I don't deserve..."

He finally looked up.

Not at the room.

Not at the window.

At his sister.

For the first time since his disappearance...

He addressed her differently.

Not as he always had.

Not with the familiar,

_"Big sis."_

Instead...

> **"...Taiga."**

The single word carried no anger.

No resentment.

Only resignation.

Taiga froze.

Witnesses later reported that her expression changed immediately.

Not because of what he had said.

But because of **how** he had said it.

Mart had never called her by her name.

Not once.

To him...

She had always been **Big sis.**

Hearing her own name instead felt like hearing a stranger speak.

Taiga covered her mouth.

She couldn't answer.

---

## **ANOMALOUS EVENT**

Mart slowly stood.

The wings of white light unfolded behind him.

Just before taking flight...

One luminous feather detached.

It drifted gently toward the floor.

Taiga instinctively reached out.

The feather landed softly in her hands.

Everyone watching expected it to dissolve.

It didn't.

The feather continued glowing peacefully.

Stable.

Untouched by the phenomenon that had destroyed every previous feather.

Mart looked at it.

Then back at Taiga.

He gave the faintest nod.

Without another word...

He stepped backward into the night sky.

And disappeared.

---

## **RESEARCH NOTE**

The recovered feather has remained completely stable.

Testing has confirmed:

- No measurable decay.
    
- Constant luminous output.
    
- No anomalous instability upon contact with Taiga.
    

At Taiga's request, the feather has **not** been transferred to Research.

It remains in her personal possession.

Director Lucious approved the request immediately.

No objections were raised.

### **Closing Observation**

Later that night, Taiga sat alone in her room.

The permanent feather rested across her palms.

Its soft white glow illuminated the darkness around her.

She traced one finger gently along its edge.

For the first time in weeks...

Nothing disappeared.

Nothing faded.

Nothing slipped through her hands.

She held it close to her chest.

And whispered through quiet tears,

> "...I'll protect this one."

Outside her window, high above the Blacksite, a lone figure with wings of white light circled once beneath the stars.

Then turned toward the mountains.

Leaving behind only the steady glow of a single feather that, at last...

Stayed.
