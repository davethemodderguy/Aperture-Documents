# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-086**

### **RESIDENTIAL INCIDENT — "MART IS FAMILY"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL

---

## **INCIDENT SUMMARY**

Following Astral's permanent residency within the Blacksite, several previously unknown details regarding Mart were revealed during a casual family conversation.

According to Astral...

Mart had been with their family since Taiga was very young.

Astral laughed as she explained,

> "...He wasn't just Taiga's pet."

She gently scratched beneath Mart's beak.

> "...He practically raised her alongside me."

Mart gave a proud little hoot.

Taiga nodded without hesitation.

> "...He's my brother."

No one in the room immediately argued.

After thinking about everything Mart had done—from staying with Taiga after Astral's death, searching for her throughout the Blacksite, comforting her during breakdowns, and refusing to leave her side—

No one could really disagree either.

---

## **A FAMILY DISCUSSION**

Star immediately became excited.

> "...Wait..."

> "...Does that mean Mart can turn into a person?"

Astral blinked.

"...Well..."

She looked at Mart.

"...Technically..."

The room became very quiet.

Every pair of eyes slowly turned toward the owl.

Mart froze.

"...Hoo?"

---

## **THE PROBLEM**

Astral explained that Mart possesses a dormant anomalous ability allowing him to assume a humanoid form.

However...

There was one issue.

Mart had absolutely no interest in doing so.

Astral smiled apologetically.

> "...He doesn't like changing."

Neko looked at Mart.

"...Seriously?"

Mart gave a firm, disapproving hoot.

Then puffed up his feathers.

The meaning was perfectly clear.

**No.**

---

## **ATTEMPTS AT PERSUASION**

Star crouched in front of Mart.

> "...Pleeeease?"

Mart looked away.

---

Bao offered him extra snacks.

Mart accepted the snacks.

He still didn't transform.

---

Frost politely asked.

Mart politely declined.

---

Niko floated upside down in front of him.

> "...Come on."

> "...Ghost club needs another member."

Mart blinked once.

Then deliberately turned around on his perch.

---

Finally...

Taiga gently picked Mart up.

She smiled.

"...You don't have to if you don't want to."

Mart immediately rubbed his head against her cheek.

The discussion ended there.

---

---

Holding Mart carefully.

> "...He's perfect the way he is."

Mart answered with a content little hoot.

---

---

> "...Still curious, though."

---

> "...Maybe someday."

---

### Star

> "...One day I'll convince him!"

Mart immediately stared at her.

Unimpressed.

---

### Nightshade

> "...He has already made his choice."

---

### Niko

Floating beside Mart.

> "...You know..."

> "...Being an owl is honestly a pretty good look."

Mart gave an approving hoot.

Niko grinned.

> "...Thought so."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

- Mart's relationship to Taiga: **Regarded by the family as her brother.**
    
- Humanoid transformation: **Possible, according to Astral.**
    
- Willingness to transform: **None whatsoever.**
    
- Staff recommendation: **Respect Mart's choice.**
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

Later that evening, Mart perched comfortably on Taiga's shoulder as she walked through the residential hallway.

Star passed by and jokingly asked,

> "...Still not transforming?"

Mart looked directly at her.

He slowly closed one eye.

Then deliberately fluffed his feathers and tucked his head beneath his wing.

Taiga laughed.

"...I think that's his way of saying 'goodnight.'"

Mart remained exactly as he was.

An owl.

Entirely by choice.

And perfectly happy with it.
