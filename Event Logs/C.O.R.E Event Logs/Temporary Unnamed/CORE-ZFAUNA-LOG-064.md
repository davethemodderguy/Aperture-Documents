# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-064**

### **RESIDENTIAL LOG — STAR'S CAT DAY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL / MEDICAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following a mild illness earlier in the week, Medical recommended that Star minimize unnecessary exertion until fully recovered.

Star voluntarily spent the day in her feline form.

According to Star, remaining as a cat:

> "...Uses the least stamina."

Medical confirmed this was consistent with previous observations and approved the decision.

---

## **ROOM N-07**

Star was unusually quiet that morning.

Instead of greeting everyone with her usual boundless energy, a small white cat slowly wandered out from beneath the blankets.

She stretched.

Yawned.

Then immediately climbed onto the nearest warm blanket and curled into a ball.

Taiga looked down.

"...Poor Star..."

She gently stroked the cat behind the ears.

Star responded with a quiet purr before falling asleep again.

---

## **OBSERVATIONS**

Throughout the day, Cat Star displayed behavior consistent with recovering from illness.

Observed behaviors included:

- Sleeping for extended periods.
    
- Seeking warm locations near heaters.
    
- Preferring to be carried rather than walking long distances.
    
- Eating smaller meals more frequently.
    
- Purring when comfortable.
    

Medical reported steady improvement.

---

## **CARETAKERS**

Most of the day's care was provided by:

- **Taiga**
    
- **Nightshade**
    

Taiga regularly checked whether Star had eaten, was warm enough, or needed water.

Nightshade handled the quieter tasks, making sure Star's sleeping areas remained undisturbed and gently moving blankets whenever Star decided to nap somewhere inconvenient.

The arrangement worked surprisingly well.

---

## **THE "SECOND LEO" INCIDENT**

At approximately 14:00, Neko entered Room N-07 carrying a toolbox.

He looked toward the rug.

Leo was asleep.

Star was asleep.

Both were curled into remarkably similar little circles.

He stopped walking.

Looked between the two.

Then calmly remarked,

> "...We got another Leo."

A pause.

> "...But a cat this time."

Even Nightshade smiled.

---

## **TAIGA**

Throughout the day, Taiga appeared noticeably quieter than usual.

She sat beside Star whenever she slept.

Occasionally checking whether she was breathing comfortably.

At one point, Bao found Taiga gently covering the sleeping cat with a small blanket.

Bao smiled.

> "...You're worried."

Taiga nodded.

"...I don't like seeing Star sick."

"...I know she'll get better..."

She looked down at the sleeping cat.

"...But I still don't like it."

Bao gently rested a hand on her shoulder.

> "...Neither do I."

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Neko

Watching Star and Leo sleeping side by side.

> "...One sleeps because he's Leo."

He pointed toward Star.

> "...The other because she's recovering."

He shrugged.

> "...Honestly, they look the same."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...She's adorable."

Whether she was referring to Star or Taiga remained unclear.

---

### Bao

Quietly preparing warm soup for Star once she felt like eating again.

---

### Frost

Gently brushing Star's fur while she slept.

> "...She'll recover."

---

### Nightshade

When Star woke briefly and looked around, Nightshade was already sitting nearby.

She simply said,

> "...Rest."

Star immediately curled back up and resumed sleeping.

---

### Director Lucious

After checking Medical's report.

> "...Nothing serious."

He looked toward the sleeping cat.

> "...Good."

---

### Aster

Aster sat on the rug beside Cat Star for nearly an hour.

She quietly sketched Star sleeping with Leo curled up nearby.

On her whiteboard she later wrote:

> **Tiny sleepy things.**

Star would later insist she was not "tiny."

Nobody agreed.

---

### Leo

Leo slept beside Star for much of the afternoon.

At one point, the two had become so tangled together that Taiga couldn't immediately tell where the cat ended and the banana snake began.

Neither appeared to mind.

---

### Mart

Mart landed beside the sleeping pair.

After watching them for several minutes, Taiga translated his amused observation.

> "...They're competing."

"...For who can sleep longer."

---

## **MEDICAL STATUS**

By evening, Star's condition had continued to improve.

Medical expects a full recovery with additional rest.

Until then, Star is permitted to remain in her feline form as long as she finds it more comfortable.

---

### **Closing Observation**

That night, Taiga sat on the rug reading quietly.

Curled against one side of her was Leo.

Against the other...

Cat Star.

Both were asleep.

Nightshade looked over from her chair.

> "...Comfortable?"

Taiga smiled softly.

"...Very."

She carefully turned another page of her book.

Making sure not to wake either of the two little sleepy companions resting against her.
