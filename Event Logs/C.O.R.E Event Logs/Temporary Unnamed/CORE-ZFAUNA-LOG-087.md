# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-087**

### **RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION — MART'S FIRST HUMAN TRANSFORMATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

After countless unsuccessful attempts by residents to persuade him over the past several weeks...

Mart transformed.

No one knows what changed his mind.

He simply looked at Taiga one afternoon, gave a small hoot, fluttered down from her shoulder...

...and a soft burst of white feathers briefly surrounded him.

When the feathers settled...

The owl was gone.

Standing in his place was a small boy.

---

## **APPEARANCE**

Mart's humanoid appearance is estimated to be approximately **eight years old**, placing him around Star's apparent age.

Personnel described him as:

- Small enough to be carried comfortably.
    
- Snow-white hair with soft feather-like texture.
    
- Quiet crimson-brown eyes.
    
- Fair skin.
    
- A simple oversized white sweater and dark shorts generated as part of the transformation.
    
- Bare feet.
    

Aside from lacking Taiga's snow leopard ears, tail, and feline markings, many noted a family resemblance.

Medical staff remarked that he shared Taiga's gentle expressions and calm demeanor.

Star's first response was immediate.

> "...He's adorable."

---

## **FIRST WORDS**

The room remained completely silent.

Everyone waited.

Mart looked around nervously before quietly speaking.

> "...Hello..."

His voice was soft.

Almost timid.

It was the first time most residents had ever heard him speak.

---

## **THE REACTION**

Taiga blinked several times.

"...Mart...?"

He nodded.

"...Mm."

Without another word, he walked over to her.

He gently tugged on the sleeve of her jacket.

She instinctively picked him up.

Mart immediately relaxed against her shoulder exactly as he did in his owl form.

The only difference...

He was now hugging her with two small arms instead of balancing on her shoulder.

Taiga smiled.

"...Still my brother."

Mart gave a tiny nod.

> "...Always."

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Astral

Astral laughed warmly.

> "...There you are."

She gently brushed a strand of hair away from Mart's face.

> "...I almost forgot how little you were."

Mart leaned into the affection without saying anything.

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious looked at Mart for a moment before quietly remarking,

> "...Another child."

He smiled.

> "...Welcome to the family."

Mart bowed politely.

> "...Thank... you."

---

### Star

Star practically vibrated with excitement.

> "...YOU CAN TALK!"

Mart looked at her.

"...Yes."

"...Sometimes."

"...Not much."

"...Talking is tiring."

"..."

"...Sorry."

Star immediately hugged him.

Mart accepted the hug with quiet patience.

---

### Neko

Neko crouched down to Mart's height.

> "...So..."

> "...You've understood everything we've been saying this whole time?"

Mart nodded.

"...Yes."

"...Mostly."

Neko covered his face.

> "...Great."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...He's just as polite as I imagined."

---

### Bao

Bao offered Mart a cookie.

He accepted it.

"...Thank you."

He then quietly walked back beside Taiga.

---

### Frost

> "...He speaks..."

A pause.

> "...Just enough."

---

### Nightshade

Watching Mart remain close to Taiga.

> "...Some habits..."

> "...Don't change with form."

---

## **TRANSFORMATION DURATION**

Mart maintained his humanoid form for approximately **eleven minutes**.

Without warning, he yawned.

Looked up at Taiga.

"...Sleepy."

She smiled.

"...Okay."

A few white feathers drifted into the air.

Seconds later...

The little boy was gone.

A small owl now rested comfortably in Taiga's arms.

Already asleep.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

- Humanoid form: **Confirmed.**
    
- Estimated apparent age: **Eight years old.**
    
- Personality: **Quiet, gentle, reserved.**
    
- Speech: **Capable but infrequent.**
    
- Preferred form: **Owl.**
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

Later that evening, Star asked Mart one final question while he rested on Taiga's shoulder in his usual owl form.

"...Are you going to stay human more often?"

Mart opened one eye.

He considered the question carefully.

Then shook his tiny head.

He gave one soft hoot.

Taiga translated automatically.

"...He says..."

She smiled.

> "...Being an owl is comfortable."

No one argued.

After all...

Whether he had feathers or hands...

Mart had never stopped being exactly who he was.
