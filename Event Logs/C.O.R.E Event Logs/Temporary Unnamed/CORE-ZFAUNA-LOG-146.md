# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-146**

### **AMMUNITION SPECIFICATION — SUNBRINGER ENGRAVED CARTRIDGES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – ARMORY / EQUIPMENT DOCUMENTATION

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following a detailed inspection of the **STAR** and **MOON** revolver systems, Armory personnel discovered that their proprietary **12.7×665mm Nekonium** cartridges are individually engraved.

The engravings serve no known ballistic or anomalous purpose.

According to Neko, they exist for one reason.

> "...Because details matter."

Each cartridge is hand-finished before issuance.

---

# **AMMUNITION DESIGN**

The cartridge cases are manufactured from a polished Nekonium alloy and feature precision laser-etched motifs around the casing.

No measurable effect on chamber pressure, velocity, or terminal performance has been observed.

The engravings are considered ceremonial and aesthetic.

---

## **STAR**

**Assigned User:** Taiga Sunbringer

Every cartridge issued for **STAR** bears two repeating engravings:

- **A six-pointed star**
    
- **A snowflake**
    

The patterns spiral subtly around the cartridge case.

Under direct light, the etchings shimmer against the polished metal.

When Taiga first noticed them, she turned one over in her fingers for nearly a minute before smiling.

> "...They're tiny..."

She looked at Neko.

> "...You engraved every single one?"

Neko nodded.

> "...Yep."

> "...Every round."

---

## **MOON**

**Assigned User:** Mart Sunbringer

Each cartridge issued for **MOON** carries a complementary pattern:

- **A crescent moon**
    
- **A five-pointed star**
    

The engraving style matches STAR's ammunition exactly, creating a visual pair when cartridges from both weapons are placed side by side.

Mart quietly lined one of his cartridges beside one of Taiga's.

The similarities were immediately obvious.

The designs weren't identical.

They were meant to complement each other.

---

# **RESEARCH NOTE**

No anomalous properties have been linked to the engravings.

They do not:

- Increase damage.
    
- Alter projectile behavior.
    
- Affect weapon synchronization.
    
- Influence the Eclipse transformation.
    

The engravings are purely symbolic.

Research staff unanimously recommended preserving the design in future production batches.

## **Closing Observation**

During routine maintenance that evening, Taiga absentmindedly placed one unfired STAR cartridge beside one of Mart's MOON cartridges on the workbench.

The engravings aligned almost perfectly.

A snowflake beneath a star.

A crescent embracing another.

Mart looked down at the two cartridges.

Then quietly rotated them until the etched star on each case faced the other.

He smiled faintly.

> "...They match."

Taiga nodded.

> "...Like us."

Neither cartridge was loaded back into its cylinder immediately.

For a little while longer, they simply remained together on the bench.
