# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-108**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL CARE GUIDELINES — MART SUNBRINGER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – MEDICAL / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **OVERVIEW**

Although Mart Sunbringer presents as exceptionally calm and emotionally resilient, Psychological Services has identified one fundamental emotional need.

**Mart needs to know that he is not alone.**

This should not be mistaken for an inability to spend time by himself.

Those are two very different things.

---

## **SOLITUDE VS. ABANDONMENT**

Mart is perfectly capable of being alone.

He often:

- Reads by himself.
    
- Watches snowfall from quiet rooftops.
    
- Flies alone over the surrounding forests.
    
- Spends hours in peaceful silence.
    

He enjoys solitude.

What he does **not** tolerate well is the belief that he has been **abandoned**.

The difference is psychological rather than physical.

If Mart knows someone is waiting for him, he remains calm.

If he genuinely believes no one will come back...

His emotional state deteriorates rapidly.

---

## **COMFORT THROUGH CONTACT**

Medical has also observed that gentle physical affection has a noticeably calming effect on Mart.

Examples include:

- A hand resting on his shoulder.
    
- Gentle pats on the head.
    
- Hugs.
    
- Sitting close enough for shoulders to touch.
    
- Holding his hand during periods of distress.
    

Mart rarely initiates physical affection himself.

However, he almost never pulls away from trusted family and friends.

Instead, he quietly relaxes.

His breathing slows.

His shoulders loosen.

Sometimes he simply closes his eyes for a moment.

---

## **HISTORICAL RECORD**

Recovered evidence from before the Blacksite located the Sunbringer siblings has helped explain this behavior.

Following the death of Astral, Taiga disappeared into the snow.

Mart searched for her.

When he couldn't find her...

He stayed.

For **weeks**.

Surviving alone in the freezing wilderness.

Not because he believed she was gone.

Because he believed...

She was still somewhere out there.

Cold.

Scared.

Waiting for him.

He refused to leave.

Medical believes that hope alone kept him going.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL RISK**

Psychologists have identified one scenario as particularly dangerous.

If Mart were ever to genuinely believe that everyone he loved had abandoned him...

They do not expect panic.

They do not expect anger.

Instead...

They expect silence.

Withdrawal.

He would likely stop looking for others.

Stop asking for help.

And quietly isolate himself.

Not because he wanted to be alone.

Because he would believe there was no one left to return to.

Residential Services considers preventing this belief a high priority.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Taiga Sunbringer

Taiga has developed a simple habit.

Whenever she passes Mart in the hallway, she lightly brushes his shoulder with her hand or gently pats his head.

When asked why, she answered,

> "...So he knows I'm here."

Mart has never objected.

---

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral hugs Mart almost every morning.

Not because he asks.

Because she knows he doesn't have to.

One morning she quietly told him,

> "...I'm not going anywhere."

Mart buried his face against her shoulder.

> "...I know."

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious once found Mart reading alone in the library late at night.

Instead of interrupting him, he simply sat in the chair across from him and continued reviewing paperwork.

Neither spoke for nearly an hour.

As Director Lucious stood to leave, Mart quietly said,

> "...Thanks."

Lucious paused.

> "...For what?"

Mart smiled.

> "...Staying."

---

### Neko

Neko has learned that sometimes the best thing to do is simply sit beside Mart.

No conversation.

No advice.

Just company.

---

He pretends not to notice.

She knows he secretly likes it.

---

Then slowly hugged her back.

> "...Hello, Star."

---

---

### Leo

Leo once woke from a nap to find Mart quietly reading beside him.

Without opening his eyes completely, Leo shifted over just enough to make room on the couch.

Mart accepted the silent invitation.

Neither of them said a word.

Both fell asleep.

---

## **CURRENT CARE RECOMMENDATIONS**

- Gentle physical affection from trusted individuals is encouraged if Mart is comfortable with it.
    
- If he chooses to be alone, respect that choice.
    
- Regularly reassure him that people will return after leaving.
    
- Avoid unintentionally creating situations where he believes he has been forgotten or abandoned.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

One snowy evening, Taiga needed to leave the residential wing for a training exercise.

Mart was reading in the common room.

As she walked toward the door, she stopped beside him.

She gently patted his head.

> "...I'll be back after training."

Mart looked up from his book.

> "...Okay."

She smiled.

> "...Promise."

Several hours later, the front door opened.

Cold air drifted inside.

Before Taiga could even take off her boots, she heard quiet footsteps.

Mart walked over.

He didn't say anything.

He simply leaned against her shoulder for a few seconds.

Taiga smiled and rested her head lightly against his.

> "...See?"

> "...I came back."

Mart closed his eyes.

> "...I knew you would."

For him...

That promise meant everything.
