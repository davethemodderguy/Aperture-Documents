# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZFAUNA-LOG-048**

### **MEDICAL OBSERVATION — RECURRING SELF-NEGLECT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – MEDICAL / RESIDENTIAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Following routine examinations over the past several weeks, Medical Services has documented a recurring pattern concerning Taiga's physical health.

The injuries themselves are generally accidental.

The concerning pattern is not how they occur.

It is how long Taiga waits before telling anyone.

Medical concludes that Taiga consistently places her own wellbeing behind everyone else's, often dismissing pain or discomfort until another person notices.

---

## **OBSERVED INCIDENTS**

Documented examples include, but are not limited to:

- Mild burns sustained while helping Bao in the kitchen after insisting she was "okay."
    
- Early-stage frostbite after remaining outdoors longer than recommended during a snowfall.
    
- Mild hypothermia after spending extended periods outside following nightmares.
    
- Dehydration after accompanying Neko through an entire afternoon of engineering work without stopping for water.
    
- Muscle exhaustion after refusing to rest during training.
    
- Minor cuts left untreated until discovered during routine checks.
    

None of the incidents were intentional.

Each could have been addressed much earlier had Taiga informed someone.

---

## **A COMMON ANSWER**

Medical staff noted that regardless of the injury, Taiga's response remained remarkably consistent.

> "...It's okay."

Or,

> "...It doesn't hurt that much."

Or simply,

> "...I'll be fine."

In many cases, objective examination showed the injury required treatment despite her reassurance.

---

## **DISCOVERY**

The pattern became impossible to ignore during one particularly cold afternoon.

Taiga had spent several hours outside helping clear snow from one of the residential walkways.

When she returned, she greeted everyone as usual.

She smiled.

She joked with Star.

She helped Bao carry groceries.

She insisted she felt perfectly normal.

Only later did Miss Neko notice Taiga's hands trembling slightly as she reached for a cup of tea.

Concerned, she gently held one of Taiga's hands.

It was painfully cold.

Medical examination confirmed the early stages of cold-related injury.

When asked why she hadn't said anything, Taiga looked genuinely surprised.

> "...I thought it would get better by itself."

---

## **SECOND OPINION**

A few days later, Neko found Taiga quietly rubbing one arm while reading.

He asked if something was wrong.

She smiled.

> "...Nothing."

Neko wasn't convinced.

After a brief examination, he discovered a small burn hidden beneath her sleeve.

It had already been cleaned.

Poorly.

She had attempted to treat it herself rather than interrupt Bao while she was cooking.

---

## **MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Medical believes Taiga does not ignore injuries because she enjoys pain or believes she should suffer.

Instead, she consistently underestimates the seriousness of her own condition and overestimates her ability to simply "push through."

Combined with her reluctance to inconvenience others, this has resulted in repeated delays in receiving treatment.

---

## **ROOM N-07 RESPONSE**

The report prompted an immediate discussion among Taiga's roommates.

### Neko

He folded his arms.

> "...Engineering rule."

Taiga looked up.

> "...If something is damaged..."

He pointed gently toward her.

> "...You repair it before it becomes a bigger problem."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You're included in that rule."

---

### Bao

Bao placed a small first-aid pouch into Taiga's hands.

> "...You can always take care of little injuries."

She smiled warmly.

> "...But if you're not sure..."

> "...Come find us."

---

### Frost

> "...Cold can be beautiful."

She gently adjusted Taiga's scarf.

> "...It's also dangerous."

---

### Star

Star frowned slightly.

> "...You always tell me to be careful."

She crossed her arms playfully.

> "...Now it's your turn."

Taiga couldn't help smiling.

---

### Nightshade

Nightshade looked over the report.

> "...You're very observant."

A pause.

> "...About everyone except yourself."

Taiga quietly laughed.

> "...Maybe..."

---

### Director Lucious

Reviewing the medical findings.

> "...You are not another task on someone's schedule."

He looked directly at Taiga.

> "...You are someone they care about."

---

### Aster

Without saying a word, Aster quietly placed a sticky note inside Taiga's first-aid pouch.

Written in careful handwriting were four words.

> **Check yourself, too.**

Taiga found it later that evening.

She smiled softly before putting it back.

---

### Leo

Leo remained entirely unhelpful.

He slept inside the open first-aid kit for nearly an hour.

Medical politely relocated him.

---

### Mart

Mart developed an unusual habit afterward.

Whenever Taiga ignored an obvious injury, he would repeatedly perch nearby until she acknowledged it.

According to Taiga's translations, his message was always the same.

> "...Take care of yourself."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Medical has implemented more frequent routine wellness checks for Taiga.

The purpose is not to restrict her activities, but to identify injuries she may dismiss or overlook herself.

Room N-07 residents have also adopted a lighthearted daily tradition.

Before dinner, someone inevitably asks:

> "...Any mystery injuries today?"

Taiga usually laughs.

Then actually checks.

---

### **Closing Observation**

One evening, after helping Neko in the workshop, Taiga walked into the common room carrying a cup of tea.

She paused halfway through the room.

Looked down at her hand.

"...Huh."

Star looked up from her book.

> "...What?"

Taiga smiled sheepishly.

> "...I think I forgot I scraped my hand earlier."

Neko looked over from his workbench.

> "...See?"

He smiled.

> "...That's why we ask."

Taiga laughed, accepted a bandage from Bao, and let Miss Neko help clean the small cut.

It was a simple moment.

But for everyone in Room N-07, it was progress.

Because this time...

She hadn't tried to hide it.
