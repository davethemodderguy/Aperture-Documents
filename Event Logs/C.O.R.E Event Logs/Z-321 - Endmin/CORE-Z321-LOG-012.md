# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-012**

### **SUPPLEMENTAL OBSERVATIONS — Z-321 "ENDMIN"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – MEDICAL / N.O.S.T. REFERENCE

---

## **SUMMARY**

The following observations have been compiled over several weeks following Z-321's permanent residency at the Blacksite. They concern her personal equipment, medical condition, environmental adaptations, and living arrangements within Room N-07.

---

## **PERSONAL RIFLE MAINTENANCE**

Personnel have repeatedly observed Endmin performing meticulous maintenance on her personal bullpup bolt-action 7.62 mm precision rifle.

Maintenance typically includes:

- Complete field stripping.
    
- Cleaning of the bore and bolt assembly.
    
- Inspection of the trigger mechanism.
    
- Lubrication of moving components.
    
- Manual verification of optic alignment.
    

Her work is precise enough that Neko jokingly remarked,

> "...I'm not sure whether she's cleaning it or rebuilding it."

Endmin smiled.

> "...Mom taught me to take care of the things that take care of me."

---

## **AMMUNITION ORIGIN**

The exact source of Banlands-manufactured 7.62 mm ammunition remains unknown.

When asked how conventional rifle cartridges are produced in an otherwise anomalous civilization, Endmin simply answered,

> "...We make them."

Further questioning yielded no additional information.

Research Division has officially classified the ammunition's origin as:

> **Unknown.**

Director Lucious later commented,

> "...Some mysteries are apparently destined to stay mysteries."

---

## **MASK STARTLE RESPONSE**

Although Endmin has largely recovered emotionally from the earlier mask-related incident, one response persists.

Unexpected contact with her mask—even accidental—causes an immediate startle reaction.

Examples include:

- Someone brushing past her in a hallway.
    
- An object lightly tapping the mask.
    
- Unexpected contact while carrying supplies.
    

Her body instinctively recoils before she consciously realizes there is no danger.

Medical Division considers this an involuntary trauma response rather than fear of any particular individual.

To prevent unnecessary distress, personnel are advised to announce themselves when approaching Endmin from unfamiliar angles and to avoid contact with her mask whenever possible.

---

## **A PERSONAL WISH**

During a quiet conversation with Astral one evening, the topic of colors arose.

Astral described a vivid sunset over Starlands.

Endmin listened quietly.

After a long pause, she softly admitted,

> "...I wonder what blue looks like now."

Another pause.

> "...Or green."

She smiled, though it carried a trace of sadness.

> "...I'd like to see colors again."

Astral gently held her hand.

Neither of them spoke for several moments.

---

## **LUCY'S DIRECTIVE**

Following this conversation, several researchers proposed investigating possible treatments.

Lucy respectfully declined.

In a written statement to Director Lucious, she explained:

> "Too many people have already tried to 'fix' my daughter."

> "Every attempt ended with another promise that couldn't be kept."

> "I will not allow her to become another experiment."

Lucy clarified that she has no objection to routine medical care or measures that improve Endmin's comfort and quality of life.

However, she requested that no experimental procedures intended to restore her eyesight be pursued without her explicit approval.

Director Lucious accepted the request in full.

---

## **ETHER CORRUPTION**

Lucy also provided additional information regarding Endmin's condition.

The damage to her eyes originated from an extreme Ether Corruption surge.

Current Banlands medical knowledge indicates:

- Ether Corruption can be **suppressed**.
    
- Ether Corruption cannot presently be **eliminated**.
    
- Existing damage remains permanent.
    

Despite the injury, Endmin developed an unexpected adaptation.

Rather than relying on ordinary vision, she can naturally perceive the presence and flow of Ether Corruption.

Within the Banlands, this ability proved invaluable.

She could identify unstable regions, corrupted environments, and concealed Ether disturbances long before they became obvious to others.

---

## **WHY SHE REMAINED**

Lucy disclosed another reason for Endmin's permanent relocation.

Endmin's ability to detect Ether Corruption made her a valuable target.

Hostile groups operating within the Banlands had previously shown interest in capturing individuals with rare sensory capabilities.

Lucy concluded that keeping Endmin at Banlands Headquarters no longer protected her.

Instead, it increasingly placed her at risk.

Combined with the deteriorating effect of the local Ether density on her eyesight, permanent residence at the Blacksite was judged to be the safest option.

---

## **ROOM N-07**

Following Endmin's arrival as a permanent resident, sleeping arrangements changed naturally.

Endmin now occupies the bed that had previously belonged to Taiga.

When asked where she intended to sleep, Taiga simply pointed toward a large wool rug spread across the floor.

> **Taiga:** "Right there."

Mart blinked.

> "...You know beds exist."

Taiga flopped onto the rug dramatically.

> "...This is comfy."

Astral laughed softly.

> "...You've been sleeping there for months anyway."

Taiga nodded proudly.

> "...Exactly."

Endmin looked uncertain.

> "...Are you sure?"

Taiga smiled.

> "...You're family."

> "...Besides..."

She bounced once on the rug.

> "...This thing is amazing."

Endmin eventually accepted the arrangement.

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, Endmin finished cleaning her rifle and carefully locked the bolt open before placing it on its rack.

She removed her boots and climbed into bed.

Across the room, Taiga was already curled up asleep on her favorite wool rug.

Mart had fallen asleep with a book resting on his chest.

Astral quietly switched off the room lights.

In the darkness, Endmin smiled.

She still couldn't see the colors everyone else described.

But she could sense the familiar presence of the people around her.

For now...

That was enough.
