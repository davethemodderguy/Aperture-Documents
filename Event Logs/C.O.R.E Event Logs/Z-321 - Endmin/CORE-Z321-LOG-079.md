# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-079**

### **ETHER RESEARCH – LONG-DURATION ILLUSION MAINTENANCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Ether Phenomena / Research

---

## **SUMMARY**

Z-321 (Endmin) has established a new personal record for sustained Ether illusion maintenance.

Without interruption, Endmin continuously maintained a complex, full-body illusion for **14 consecutive days**.

The feat significantly exceeded all previous documented demonstrations and has been recognized by the Ether Research Division as evidence of extraordinary Ether efficiency and concentration.

---

## **The Experiment**

The trial was entirely voluntary.

Rather than creating a powerful battlefield illusion, Endmin chose something considerably more difficult:

She maintained dozens of subtle cosmetic alterations simultaneously throughout her normal daily routine.

These included minor adjustments such as:

- Hairstyle.
    
- Clothing appearance.
    
- Small decorative accessories.
    
- Minute changes to shadows and lighting around her figure.
    
- Tiny visual corrections that were almost impossible to notice individually.
    

The illusion remained active while she:

- Slept.
    
- Skated through the Blacksite.
    
- Worked in Engineering.
    
- Ate meals.
    
- Attended training.
    
- Visited the greenhouse.
    
- Performed weapons maintenance.
    

Researchers periodically verified that the illusion never collapsed.

---

## **Conclusion of the Trial**

Exactly fourteen days after activation, Endmin entered Engineering.

She looked toward Neko.

> **"...Watch this."

She closed her eyes.

The Ether surrounding her quietly dispersed.

The decorative braid dissolved into glowing particles.

The altered clothing shimmered before returning to its normal appearance.

Several small cosmetic details throughout her appearance subtly changed all at once.

Silence filled the room.

Neko slowly looked at the calendar.

Then back at Endmin.

> **"...Two weeks?"

Endmin nodded.

> **"...New record."

---

## **Research Assessment**

Ether Research concluded that the achievement was not impressive because of the illusion itself.

It was impressive because of its **consistency**.

Maintaining a stable Ether construct continuously for fourteen days without accidental collapse requires exceptional control, concentration, and energy efficiency.

Researchers noted that Endmin appeared no more fatigued than usual after dismissing the illusion, indicating that she has learned to sustain low-intensity Ether techniques with remarkable precision.
