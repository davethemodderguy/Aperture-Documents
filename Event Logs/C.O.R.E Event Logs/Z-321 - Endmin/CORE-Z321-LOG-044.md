# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-044**

### **NEW ROOM N-07 RESIDENT – NIKO**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential Observation

---

## **SUMMARY**

Room N-07 welcomed a new permanent resident following the arrival of **Niko**, the eldest brother of Neko and Miss Neko.

Unlike his younger siblings, Niko is a **ghost** and **not** a Thiren.

Following approval from Director Lucious, Niko was assigned to Room N-07 due to available space and his existing familial relationship with two Blacksite residents.

---

## **Identity**

**Name:** Niko

**Status:** Ghost

**Species:** Human (Deceased)

**Family:**

- Youngest — **Neko**
    
- Middle — **Miss Neko**
    
- Oldest — **Niko**
    

Despite being the oldest sibling, Niko possesses a calm, easygoing personality and generally prefers observing rather than leading conversations.

---

## **Not a Thiren**

Following Lucy's recent briefing regarding Thirens, Xenobiology personnel asked whether Niko also belonged to the species.

Lucy immediately clarified:

> "No."

> "Niko is simply a ghost."

Unlike Neko and Miss Neko, Niko possesses:

- No animal ears.
    
- No tail.
    
- No animal transformation.
    
- No Thiren physiology.
    

He is an ordinary human spirit whose current state is entirely the result of his death rather than his biology.

---

## **Meeting Endmin**

Endmin first met Niko shortly after he moved into Room N-07.

She looked at him for several seconds.

Then politely asked,

> "...You're Neko's brother?"

Niko smiled.

> "The oldest one."

Endmin nodded.

> "...You don't have cat ears."

Niko laughed.

> "Nope."

> "I'm the boring one."

Endmin quickly shook her head.

> "...I don't think you're boring."

Niko smiled again.

> "...Thanks."

The two got along almost immediately.

---

## **Room N-07 Sleeping Arrangement**

With another resident joining the room, sleeping positions were adjusted.

Current arrangement:

- **Mart** — Bed.
    
- **Endmin** — Bed.
    
- **Star** — Bed.
    
- **Astral** — Does not require a bed.
    
- **Taiga** — Wool rug on the floor (by personal preference).
    
- **Niko** — Floor bedding positioned near the window, a short distance from Taiga.
    

When asked why he chose that location, Niko answered simply,

> "Fresh air."

As a ghost, he technically had no need for it.

Nobody questioned him further.

---

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Niko has officially become a resident of Room N-07.

He requires no special containment procedures and has integrated smoothly into daily Blacksite life.

Personnel describe him as calm, approachable, and an overall positive addition to the household.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late that night, the lights in Room N-07 were off.

Taiga was already asleep on her rug.

Niko rested nearby, close to the open window.

A cool breeze drifted into the room.

Looking up at the stars outside, Niko smiled quietly.

> "Nice view."

From somewhere on the floor, Taiga mumbled in her sleep.

> "...Told you..."

Niko chuckled softly.

It already felt like home.
