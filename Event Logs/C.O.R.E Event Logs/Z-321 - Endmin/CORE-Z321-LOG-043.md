## **SUMMARY**

During a scheduled intelligence exchange with Aperture Industries, Lucy corrected a long-standing assumption regarding several Blacksite residents.

Individuals possessing permanent animal characteristics or the ability to naturally shift between humanoid and animal forms are **not** isolated anomalies.

According to Banlands records, they belong to a recognized sapient species known collectively as **Thirens**.

Lucy noted that the term is widely used in several neighboring dimensions and civilizations.

---

# **Thirens**

**Scientific Classification:** Sapient Humanoid Species

A Thiren is an intelligent species whose physiology naturally combines humanoid anatomy with animal traits.

Common characteristics include:

- Animal ears.
    
- Animal tails.
    
- Species-specific eyes.
    
- Enhanced senses.
    
- Partial or complete transformation into their respective animal.
    
- Naturally occurring hybrid physiology.
    

Unlike shapeshifters, Thirens are born this way.

Their traits are a normal part of their biology and are inherited genetically.

# **What Thirens Are Not**

Lucy emphasized several misconceptions.

Thirens are **not**:

- Artificial hybrids.
    
- Experimental organisms.
    
- Cursed humans.
    
- Shape-changing anomalies.
    
- Magical familiars.
    

They are simply another intelligent species.

Many civilizations consider their existence completely ordinary.

---

# **Behavioral Notes**

Individual personalities vary greatly between Thirens.

Species classification should **not** be used to predict temperament or behavior.

Lucy specifically reminded researchers:

> "Judge individuals."

> "Not species."

---

# **Research Implications**

The revelation prompted Aperture's Xenobiology Division to begin reclassifying certain physiological records.

Previous descriptions such as "animal-human hybrid" will gradually be updated to **Thiren**, where appropriate and confirmed.

The change is intended to improve scientific accuracy and avoid unnecessary ambiguity.
