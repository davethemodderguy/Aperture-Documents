# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-084**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION – BELLE'S SENSE OF BELONGING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Behavioral

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following her arrival at APRT-70, Behavioral Sciences noted a significant improvement in Belle's confidence and overall happiness.

When asked about the change, Belle gave a surprisingly simple answer.

> **"...I'm not the only Thiren anymore."**

Although Merryweather Manor employed several Thirens, Belle often served alongside mostly non-Thiren personnel in her role as Endmin's bodyguard. Living at the Blacksite surrounded by others who shared similar traits gave her an immediate sense of belonging.

---

## **First Week**

Belle adapted to life in Room N-07 remarkably quickly.

One of the first things she noticed was just how many Thirens lived at the Blacksite.

- Endmin.
    
- Taiga.
    
- Mart.
    
- Neko.
    
- Miss Neko.
    

She stared at them for several seconds before smiling from ear to ear.

> **"...There's so many!"

---

## **An Unexpected Conversation**

During breakfast, Belle looked around the table.

Rabbit ears.

Cat ears.

Snow leopard ears.

Owl feathers.

Tails.

Wings.

To everyone else, it was perfectly normal.

Belle quietly smiled.

Taiga noticed.

> **"What's got you smiling?"

Belle swung her legs beneath the chair.

> **"...Back home..."

> **"...I was usually the only fluffy one in the room."

She looked around again.

> **"...Now everyone's fluffy."

There was a brief silence.

Then Endmin chuckled.

> **"...Welcome to the club."

---

## **The Thirens**

The established group known informally as **"The Thirens"** welcomed Belle immediately.

Although Archive Division has yet to update the unofficial group roster, Belle is frequently seen participating in their activities.

These include:

- Shared meals.
    
- Training sessions.
    
- Friendly races through the residential wing.
    
- Group movie nights.
    
- Quiet afternoons in the greenhouse.
    

Belle especially enjoys comparing ears and tails with the others, much to Endmin's embarrassment.

---

## **A Familiar Habit**

Only a few days after moving in, researchers witnessed Belle sitting comfortably between Neko and Miss Neko while Taiga brushed her fluffy ears.

Belle sighed happily.

> **"...This is nice."

Mart looked over.

> **"...Comfortable?"

Belle nodded enthusiastically.

> **"...Very."

Endmin smiled quietly from across the room.

---

She smiled warmly.

> "...Every child deserves to feel like they belong."

---

### Director Lucious

After reading the observation report, Director Lucious simply wrote:

> **"A home isn't defined by walls."**

> **"It's defined by the people who make you feel welcome."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Belle has integrated seamlessly into life at APRT-70.

Behavioral Sciences reports that she is noticeably more confident than when she first arrived, attributing the change in part to the friendships she has formed with the other Thiren residents.

---

### **Closing Observation**

One evening, Belle wandered into the common room carrying a blanket.

She looked around.

Endmin was reading.

Neko and Miss Neko were discussing a new engineering project.

Taiga and Mart were arguing over a board game.

Aster was asleep on the sofa.

Belle quietly sat down in the middle of them.

She wrapped herself in the blanket and smiled.

Endmin looked up.

> **"...Everything okay?"

Belle nodded happily.

Her rabbit ears bounced.

> **"...Yeah."

She looked around at everyone.

> **"...I'm not the only Thiren anymore."

No one said anything.

Taiga simply reached over and gently patted Belle's head.

Belle leaned into it with a content smile.

For the first time in a long time, she truly felt at home.
