# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-063**

### **PERSONNEL UPDATE – THIREN DISCLOSURE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Personnel / Behavioral

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following her continued recovery and long-term residence at APRT-70, Z-321 (Endmin) has voluntarily discontinued the use of Ether illusion to conceal her Thiren ears during everyday life.

Personnel are no longer surprised to see Endmin walking through the Blacksite with her feline features openly visible.

Behavioral Sciences considers this another indication that she now views the Blacksite as her home.

---

## **Physical Description**

Updated observations record the following Thiren characteristics:

**Ears**

- Completely white fur.
    
- Highly expressive, often reflecting her mood before her facial expression does.
    
- Rotate instinctively toward unfamiliar sounds.
    

**Tail**

- Primarily white.
    
- Distinct black stripes and spots along its length.
    
- Usually hangs naturally behind her wings, though it moves noticeably when she is relaxed or excited.
    

Personnel have noted that her tail often gives away her emotions despite her efforts to remain composed.

---

## **An Unsolved Question**

Following Endmin's decision to stop hiding her ears, curiosity spread rapidly throughout the Blacksite.

Several researchers attempted to determine exactly **which breed of cat Thiren** she belonged to.

The results were...

Unsuccessful.

Not because the investigation lacked scientific merit.

Because Endmin disliked the question.

---

## **Incident Report**

One researcher approached Endmin during lunch.

> **Researcher:**
> 
> "Out of curiosity... what kind of cat are you?"

Endmin stopped eating.

She slowly looked up.

Her ears lowered slightly.

> **Endmin:**
> 
> "...Does it matter?"

The researcher hesitated.

> "...I just thought it'd be interesting."

Endmin sighed.

> "...I'm Endmin."

> "...That's enough."

The conversation ended there.

---

## **Behavioral Assessment**

Behavioral Sciences later interviewed Endmin regarding the subject.

She explained:

> "People always try to categorize everything."

She gently brushed her tail aside.

> "Back in the Banlands, I was judged by my title."

> "Here..."

> "...I'd rather not be reduced to a cat breed."

Medical and Behavioral staff agreed that her preference should be respected.

Accordingly, personnel have been instructed not to speculate or assign a breed to Endmin.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Endmin now openly displays her Thiren ears and tail throughout the Blacksite.

Her appearance has quickly become a familiar sight to both staff and residents.

While curiosity about her exact feline lineage persists, Archive Division considers the matter closed out of respect for her wishes.

Official records will simply identify her as:

**Cat Thiren.**

No further classification is required.

---

### **Closing Observation**

As Endmin skated through one of the main corridors, her white ears flicked happily in the cool air while her spotted tail swayed behind her.

A new intern watched her pass and quietly asked another researcher,

> "...What kind of cat Thiren is she?"

Before anyone else could answer, Taiga smiled.

> "...Wrong question."

The intern blinked.

> "...Then what's the right one?"

Taiga looked toward Endmin, who was laughing with Mart as Aster trotted alongside them.

> "...Her name."

> "...It's Endmin."
