## **SUMMARY**

During a casual discussion in Room N-07, personnel began comparing their personal weapons and equipment.

While most members of the Friendly Roommates and associated personnel possessed specialized weapons, one detail quickly became noticeable.

Almost everyone had a weapon.

Except Lucius.

# **Lucius Merryweather**

**Weapon:** None

When the discussion reached Lucius, everyone noticed something.

He was the only person in the room without a dedicated weapon.

Taiga looked at him.

> **"...Wait."

> **"You don't have one?"**

Lucius shook his head.

> **"No."

> **"I never really had one."**

The room became quiet.

---

## **Neko's Observation**

Neko looked over the collection of weapons.

Most carried the same marking.

A signature.

His signature.

Weapons created or modified by Neko carried his personal engraving as a symbol of craftsmanship.

Neko pointed out:

- Taiga's revolver.
    
- Mart's revolver.
    
- Star's weapons.
    
- Nightshade's blade.
    
- Frost's katana.
    
- Lucy's equipment.
    
- Endmin's rifle modification.
    

Almost all carried his mark.

Belle's sword and Miss Neko's Handheld Terraformer were the exceptions.

---

## **Endmin's Comment**

Endmin looked toward Lucius.

> **"You know..."

> **"You don't have to use a weapon just because everyone else does."**

Lucius smiled.

> **"I know."

He looked around the room.

> **"...I think I spent enough time surviving."

> **"Maybe I don't need to carry something everywhere anymore."**

---

## **Director Lucious' Note**

After reviewing the conversation, Director Lucious added:

> **"A weapon is a tool."**

> **"Not having one does not make someone powerless."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Lucius remains unarmed by personal choice.

While nearly every combat-capable member of Room N-07 possesses a specialized weapon, Lucius has chosen to focus on recovery, art, and reconnecting with his family.

Personnel have noted that despite lacking a weapon, Lucius remains highly capable due to his Wolf Thiren traits and survival experience.

His greatest weapon currently remains the same thing that carried him through four years alone:

His own determination.
