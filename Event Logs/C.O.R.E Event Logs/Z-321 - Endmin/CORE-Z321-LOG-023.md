# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-023**

### **SAFETY INCIDENT REPORT — ACCIDENTAL STORAGE ROOM CONFINEMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – SAFETY / MEDICAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

At approximately 09:15, Z-321 ("Endmin") entered Storage Sector C-14 to retrieve a set of precision tools requested by Neko for a maintenance project.

Due to a mechanical fault in the storage room's locking mechanism, the door automatically sealed after she entered.

The room was not scheduled for use that day.

The incident went unnoticed for approximately **five hours**.

Endmin was recovered unconscious but made a full recovery following immediate medical treatment.

---

## **TIMELINE**

**09:13**

Neko requested a small toolkit and several specialty fasteners.

> **Neko:** "Endmin, could you grab the precision kit from C-14?"

> **Endmin:** "Sure!"

Security footage shows Endmin entering Storage C-14 carrying a small clipboard.

---

**09:15**

The storage door automatically closed behind her.

Unknown to personnel at the time, a malfunction caused the interior release mechanism to fail.

The room remained sealed.

---

## **INSIDE THE STORAGE ROOM**

Recovered camera footage showed Endmin remaining calm initially.

She searched for the requested equipment, placed everything neatly into a carrying case, and approached the exit.

The door did not open.

She attempted the release handle several times.

Nothing happened.

She knocked firmly on the door.

No response.

Her radio produced only static due to the room's heavily shielded construction.

Realizing no one could hear her, she sat beside the door to conserve energy.

As time passed, the enclosed space became increasingly oxygen-depleted.

Several hours later, Endmin showed clear signs of hypoxia:

- Labored breathing.
    
- Dizziness.
    
- Loss of coordination.
    
- Increasing difficulty remaining conscious.
    

Even then, footage showed her trying to remain calm.

She quietly spoke to herself.

> **Endmin:** "...Someone will notice..."

She smiled weakly.

> "...Eventually..."

---

## **DISCOVERY**

At approximately 14:20, Neko looked around his workshop.

The requested tools had never arrived.

> **Neko:** "...Where's Endmin?"

He initially assumed she had become distracted with another task.

Ten minutes later, after confirming no one had seen her since the morning, Director Lucious authorized an immediate site-wide search.

Room N-07 was informed.

Taiga and Mart joined the search without hesitation.

---

## **RECOVERY**

A maintenance technician eventually reported an access fault in Storage C-14.

The emergency override was activated.

The door opened.

Endmin was found collapsed beside the exit.

The toolkit she had been sent to retrieve remained neatly packed beside her.

She had apparently continued protecting the equipment even as she lost consciousness.

Medical staff responded immediately.

Endmin regained consciousness later that afternoon in the infirmary.

---

## **ROOM N-07**

When Endmin opened her eyes, she immediately saw Taiga sitting beside the bed.

Mart was asleep in a nearby chair, still holding the book he had brought while waiting.

Neko stood quietly near the doorway.

The first thing Endmin asked was,

> **Endmin:** "...Did I lose the tools?"

Neko blinked.

Then laughed with obvious relief.

> **Neko:** "...No."

He picked up the case.

> "...You protected them better than yourself."

Endmin smiled sheepishly.

> "...Sorry..."

---

## **INVESTIGATION**

Engineering determined the incident resulted from multiple simultaneous failures:

- Automatic lock malfunction.
    
- Failed interior emergency release.
    
- Faulty occupancy sensor.
    
- Shielded room preventing radio communication.
    

No evidence of intentional misconduct was found.

The room was immediately removed from service pending repairs.

---

## **CORRECTIVE ACTIONS**

Following the incident, Director Lucious approved several new safety measures:

- All storage rooms now include redundant mechanical emergency releases.
    
- Oxygen monitoring sensors installed in enclosed storage areas.
    
- Automatic occupancy alerts linked to Security Control.
    
- Mandatory check-in/check-out logging for personnel entering isolated storage rooms.
    
- Annual inspection of emergency exits and ventilation systems.
    

Neko also insisted on one additional rule.

> **Neko:** "If I need tools..."

He looked toward Endmin.

> "...I'm coming with you."

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Taiga Sunbringer

Taiga later admitted she had been terrified.

> "...Don't ever scare me like that again."

Endmin lowered her head.

> "...I'll try."

Taiga gently flicked her forehead.

> "...Good."

---

### Mart Sunbringer

Mart quietly adjusted Endmin's blanket.

> "...You kept apologizing..."

He smiled faintly.

> "...Even though you didn't do anything wrong."

Endmin didn't answer.

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious concluded the investigation with a brief statement.

> "A safety system exists to prevent a single failure from becoming an emergency."

> "In this incident, several failed at once."

> "That is unacceptable."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Z-321 – Endmin**

- Medical condition: Fully recovered.
    
- Lasting effects: None.
    
- Psychological status: Stable.
    
- Cleared to resume normal duties.
    

Storage Sector C-14 remained closed until all corrective actions had been independently verified.

---

### **Closing Observation**

The day after the incident, Neko once again needed a toolkit.

He looked toward Endmin.

She immediately stood up.

> **Endmin:** "I'll go get—"

Neko interrupted with a grin.

> **Neko:** "...We're going together."

Endmin smiled.

> "...Deal."

The two walked toward the storage wing side by side.

Neither of them noticed Taiga quietly following a few steps behind—just in case.
