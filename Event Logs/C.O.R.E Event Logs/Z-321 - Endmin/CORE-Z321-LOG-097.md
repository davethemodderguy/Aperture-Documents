# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-097**

### **MOVEMENT OBSERVATION – BELLE'S SPRINTING STYLE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Personnel Recreation

---

## **SUMMARY**

Among the residents of APRT-70, Belle's method of running has become something of a Blacksite spectacle.

Despite carrying a hybrid sword weighing approximately **50 kilograms**, Belle is capable of sprinting at extraordinary speeds thanks to her Rabbit Thiren physiology.

Her distinctive running style has become so recognizable that personnel often stop what they're doing just to watch her dash through the corridors.

---

## **Running Mechanics**

Unlike most personnel, Belle instinctively carries her sword above and slightly behind her head while sprinting.

This unusual posture allows the weapon to clear obstacles and keeps it from striking the floor.

Once Belle reaches full speed, observers consistently note that her legs move so quickly they appear to spin in circular motions, almost like cartoon wheels.

Security footage confirms the motion is genuine and not an optical illusion.

Behavioral Sciences has unofficially described it as:

> **"Cartoonishly efficient."**

---

## **Speed Assessment**

During controlled movement trials, Belle's maximum sprint speed was compared against Endmin.

Results showed:

- **Belle:** Comparable to Endmin skating **without** activating her boot boosters.
    
- **Endmin:** Faster once her booster system is engaged.
    

The result surprised many researchers, considering Belle achieves this speed using nothing but her natural Rabbit Thiren physiology.

---

## **One Small Problem**

Belle's greatest weakness while sprinting is stopping.

Because of the enormous momentum generated by both her speed and the sword balanced over her head, abrupt stops rarely end gracefully.

Typical sequence:

1. Belle sprints at full speed.
    
2. Belle stops suddenly.
    
3. Momentum shifts backward.
    
4. Belle falls onto her back.
    

Fortunately, Rabbit Thiren physiology appears well suited to handling such impacts.

Belle almost always bounces back to her feet within moments.

Most of the time, she simply laughs.

> **"...I'm okay!"

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Belle's sprinting style has become one of the Friendy Roommates' favorite sights around APRT-70.

While it is not recommended to imitate due to the unique physiology required, staff have grown accustomed to hearing a rapid series of footsteps followed by a soft **thump**, immediately followed by Belle cheerfully announcing that she is perfectly fine.

---

### **Closing Observation**

One afternoon, Endmin was casually skating through a residential corridor without activating her boot boosters.

Behind her came a familiar voice.

> **"...Race you!"

Belle shot past like a pink blur, sword held triumphantly over her head.

Her legs spun so quickly they were almost impossible to follow.

Researchers instinctively stepped aside as the rabbit sped through the hallway.

Belle reached the finish first.

She threw both feet forward to stop.

There was a brief moment of silence.

Then—

**Thump.**

Belle toppled neatly onto her back.

The sword landed safely beside her.

A second later, she sprang right back to her feet as if nothing had happened.

> **"...I meant to do that!"

The entire corridor burst into laughter.

Endmin skated over, trying—and failing—not to smile.

> **"...Sure you did."

Belle grinned, dusted herself off, hoisted the oversized sword back over her head, and was ready to sprint again before anyone could stop her.
