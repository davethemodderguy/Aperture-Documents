# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-050**

### **ENGINEERING EVALUATION – ENDMIN'S MOBILITY BOOTS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Engineering / Materials Science

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following increased daily use of Z-321 (Endmin)'s custom Mobility Boots, Engineering Division conducted a detailed materials evaluation to determine whether the integrated Nekonium skate wheels caused wear to facility flooring.

The results surprised even Neko.

---

## **Objective**

Determine whether the Mobility Boots produce:

- Surface scratches.
    
- Abrasion.
    
- Glass damage.
    
- Microfractures.
    
- Excessive floor wear.
    

Testing was conducted using newly manufactured engineering-grade glass panels before installation elsewhere in the Blacksite.

---

## **Test Procedure**

Neko placed a pristine sheet of reinforced laboratory glass across the testing platform.

Measurements confirmed:

- Zero existing scratches.
    
- Optical-grade finish.
    
- Mirror-level smoothness.
    

Endmin looked at the setup.

> **Endmin:**
> 
> "...You want me to skate on that?"

Neko nodded.

> **"Full speed."**

---

## **Results**

Endmin accelerated across the glass.

She performed:

- Normal skating.
    
- Sharp turns.
    
- Emergency stops.
    
- Reverse skating.
    
- Short booster bursts.
    
- Controlled slides.
    

After completion, the panel was removed for inspection.

---

## **Microscopic Analysis**

Inspection revealed:

- No scratches.
    
- No scoring.
    
- No visible marks.
    
- No measurable material loss.
    
- No surface deformation.
    

Even under microscopic examination, the glass remained indistinguishable from its condition before testing.

Engineering repeated the experiment three additional times.

The results remained identical.

---

## **Engineering Conclusion**

The wheel assemblies distribute Endmin's weight with extraordinary precision.

Rather than scraping across a surface, the wheels maintain an exceptionally smooth rolling interface that minimizes friction while preserving traction.

Researchers believe this behavior results from a combination of:

- Nekonium wheel composition.
    
- Precision machining.
    
- Endmin's remarkably stable balance.
    
- Active suspension mechanisms within the boots.
    

Neko summarized the findings with one sentence.

> **"They're somehow gentler than rubber."**

---

## **Blade Mode**

For completeness, the blade assemblies were also examined.

Unlike the skate wheels, the blades are designed for cutting and braking.

When deployed, they behave exactly as intended and are capable of marking or cutting suitable materials.

The non-damaging behavior applies **only** while operating in skate mode.
