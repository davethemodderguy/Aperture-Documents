# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-011**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION — PRECISION MARKSMANSHIP PRACTICE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – RESIDENT ACTIVITY

---

## **SUMMARY**

Since becoming a permanent resident of the Blacksite, Z-321 ("Endmin") has established a routine of periodically visiting the outdoor precision shooting range.

While initially believed to be ordinary firearms training, Psychological Services later concluded that the activity serves as a healthy coping mechanism for homesickness and maintaining familiarity with the skills she developed while living in the Banlands.

---

## **OBSERVATIONS**

Unlike most personnel, Endmin rarely trains for speed.

Instead, she spends long periods sitting quietly between shots.

A typical session consists of:

- Carefully inspecting her rifle.
    
- Confirming the firing line is clear.
    
- Firing a single round.
    
- Waiting several minutes.
    
- Repeating the process.
    

She often remains at the range for over an hour while firing fewer than twenty rounds.

When asked why she practiced so slowly, Endmin answered:

> "...Mom always said..."

She gently rested a hand on the rifle's stock.

> "...The shot happens long before you pull the trigger."

---

## **HOMESICKNESS**

Psychological Services observed that these practice sessions become more frequent after Lucy returns to the Banlands following a visit.

When asked if she missed her mother, Endmin smiled.

> "...Every day."

She quickly added,

> "...But..."

> "...I know she'll come back."

Although she occasionally feels homesick, clinicians note that she does not express the same uncertainty she did during her first weeks at the Blacksite.

Lucy has consistently kept her promise to visit whenever her duties allow, and the regular contact has greatly reduced Endmin's anxiety.

---

## **AUDITORY RESPONSE**

Research Division conducted several controlled observations regarding Endmin's unusual sensory perception.

Because her perception relies heavily on delicate vibrations and echoes in the environment, researchers questioned whether loud sounds might overwhelm or distort her spatial awareness.

Testing produced an unexpected result.

Even repeated rifle fire—including shots from neighboring firing lanes—did not noticeably impair her perception.

Endmin explained:

> "...A gunshot is loud."

She smiled.

> "...But it's honest."

When asked what she meant, she elaborated:

> "...It's only there for a moment."

> "...Then everything settles again."

Research concluded that brief, high-intensity sounds do not significantly interfere with her anomalous perception.

Sudden physical impacts to her protective mask, however, remain highly disruptive and are to be strictly avoided.

---

## **RANGE OBSERVATION**

During one afternoon session, Taiga, Mart, and Frost stopped by the range to watch.

Taiga leaned over the safety barrier.

> **Taiga:** "How tiny are today's targets?"

Endmin pointed toward the far end of the range.

The others looked.

They couldn't see anything.

Mart narrowed his eyes.

> "...Is there even a target?"

The range officer quietly handed him a pair of binoculars.

Nearly eight hundred meters away, a single sewing pin had been mounted upright in a block of wood.

Mart lowered the binoculars.

> "...That's ridiculous."

Endmin simply smiled.

She settled into position.

One controlled breath.

A single shot echoed across the range.

When the target was retrieved, the pin had been struck cleanly through its shaft without toppling the wooden block that supported it.

The range fell silent.

Taiga stared.

Then slowly turned toward Endmin.

> "...You're not allowed to make fun of my aim ever again."

Endmin laughed.

> "...I wasn't planning to."

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Taiga Sunbringer

Taiga has begun accompanying Endmin to the range on occasion.

While Endmin practices precision, Taiga prefers rapid-fire drills.

Their shooting styles are almost complete opposites.

They nevertheless enjoy training together.

---

### Mart Sunbringer

Mart occasionally helps maintain Endmin's rifle after long practice sessions.

Although she insists she can do it herself, she no longer objects when he quietly sits beside her and assists.

---

### Frost

Frost summarized Endmin's shooting style in one sentence:

> "...She has the patience of a mountain."

---

### Director Lucious

After reviewing Endmin's ammunition usage report, Director Lucious remarked:

> "She somehow uses fewer rounds than anyone else."

A brief pause.

> "...And somehow hits more targets."

---

### **Closing Observation**

As the sun began to set, Endmin packed away her rifle.

She looked briefly toward the distant horizon.

Not toward the Banlands.

Toward the Blacksite.

Taiga waved from across the courtyard.

Mart was waiting by the entrance, holding the door open.

Endmin smiled.

She slung the rifle across her back and jogged toward them.

The range reminded her where she came from.

The people waiting for her reminded her where she belonged.
