## **Multi-Purpose Tactical Briefcase**

At first glance, Cinder's equipment resembles an ordinary reinforced executive briefcase.

Internally, however, it is a highly specialized modular weapon platform issued exclusively by the Krampus Compliance Authority.

Available configurations include:

- **Explosive Smart Ordnance Launcher**
    
- **Compact Submachine Gun**
    
- **Rocket Launcher**
    
- **Heavy Impact Weapon** (using the closed briefcase itself)
    

Cinder typically carries it by the handle or swings it comfortably over her shoulder despite its considerable weight.

Transformation between configurations is electronically controlled and completed within seconds.

## **Training Exercise**

The Friendly Roommates took turns sparring against the visiting personnel.

Highlights included:

- Belle demonstrating her acrobatic sword techniques.
    
- Endmin practicing coordinated fire with Keith.
    
- Keith teaching defensive movement around civilians.
    
- Cinder showcasing rapid transitions between her briefcase's weapon modes.
    

---

### **Cinder's Demonstration**

Standing in the center of the training range, Cinder calmly set her briefcase on the ground.

Mart tilted his head.

> **"...That's your weapon?"

Cinder smiled.

> **"...Yes."

She pressed a concealed switch.

Mechanical locks disengaged.

The briefcase unfolded into a compact submachine gun.

Several researchers applauded.

Cinder pressed another control.

Panels shifted again.

The weapon expanded into an explosive ordnance launcher.

Neko stared silently.

One final transformation.

The launcher compacted before revealing a rocket launcher configuration.

Belle clapped enthusiastically.

> **"...That's so cool!"

Cinder simply folded it back into a briefcase.

> **"...It also works like this."

She swung it forward with both hands.

**THUD.**

The reinforced training dummy tipped over instantly.

Lucius blinked.

> **"...So..."

> **"...The briefcase itself is also a weapon."

Cinder nodded.

> **"...Correct."
