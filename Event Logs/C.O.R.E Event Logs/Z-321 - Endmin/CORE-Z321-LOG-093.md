# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## CORE-Z321-LOG-093

### **WARDROBE OBSERVATION – BELLE'S FORMAL ATTIRE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Personnel

---

## **SUMMARY**

Residential Services recently expanded Belle's wardrobe after discovering her enthusiasm for trying different styles of clothing.

Among the additions were two formal outfits that quickly became favorites:

- A traditional maid dress.
    
- A formal maid suit.
    

Both outfits were custom-tailored by Aperture Industries' Thiren Clothing Department to accommodate Belle's rabbit ears, tail, and thick fur.

---

## **Outfit A – Maid Dress**

Belle's maid dress follows a classic design while remaining practical for daily wear.

Features include:

- Black dress with white apron.
    
- Comfortable, fur-friendly fabric.
    
- Reinforced stitching to withstand Belle's energetic movements.
    
- Tail opening integrated into the rear.
    
- Adjustable headband positioned comfortably between her rabbit ears.
    
- Hidden pockets large enough for small tools and snacks.
    

Belle particularly enjoys wearing the dress while helping around Room N-07.

Tasks she commonly volunteers for include:

- Sweeping floors.
    
- Carrying laundry.
    
- Preparing tea.
    
- Tidying shared spaces.
    

Although she insists she is "just helping," everyone has agreed she looks adorable doing it.

---

## **Outfit B – Maid Suit**

Belle also owns a maid-inspired formal suit.

The outfit consists of:

- A fitted black vest.
    
- White dress shirt.
    
- Neatly pressed trousers.
    
- White gloves.
    
- Decorative apron.
    
- Matching rabbit-ear headpiece.
    

The suit was originally commissioned after Belle remarked that dresses were not always practical when carrying a fifty-kilogram sword.

The result was an outfit that retained the elegant maid aesthetic while allowing greater freedom of movement.

Belle refers to it as her "work mode uniform."

---

Everyone nodded.

Belle's smile could probably have illuminated the room by itself.

### **Closing Observation**

One Saturday afternoon, several residents returned to Room N-07 after training.

The room was spotless.

The dishes had been washed.

The blankets were neatly folded.

Fresh tea waited on the table.

Belle stepped out of the kitchenette wearing her maid dress.

She bowed politely.

> **"...Welcome back!"

Endmin blinked.

> **"...You did all this?"

Belle nodded proudly.

> **"...I wanted everyone to come home to a clean room."

Endmin smiled and gently patted one of Belle's rabbit ears.

> **"...Thanks, Belle."

Belle's ears twitched happily.

For the rest of the evening, she couldn't stop smiling.
