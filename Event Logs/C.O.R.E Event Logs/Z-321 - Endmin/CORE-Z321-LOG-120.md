**Quantity:** 2

---

## **Design Philosophy**

Unlike many weapons used by the Friendly Roommates, Lucius' pistols are not designed for overwhelming power or specialized anomaly elimination.

They are designed around practicality.

After years of surviving alone, Lucius prefers equipment that:

- Does not slow him down.
    
- Can be carried discreetly.
    
- Allows quick defense when necessary.
    
- Does not replace his own skills.
    

---

## **Neko's Explanation**

When asked why Lucius received a prototype weapon instead of one of Neko's more advanced creations, Neko simply replied:

> **"Because he doesn't fight like everyone else."**

Neko explained that Lucius' fighting style relies more on:

- Movement.
    
- Awareness.
    
- Precision.
    
- Survival instincts.
    

The pistols were created to support those traits rather than force him into another person's combat style.

## **Neko's Signature**

Like many weapons created by Neko, the pistols contain his signature marking.

The engraving represents that the weapon is part of Neko's personal collection of creations.

However, unlike many of the others, Lucius' pistols were not designed for a specific role in the Banlands government.

They were made for someone who needed a reliable way to protect himself after finally returning home.
