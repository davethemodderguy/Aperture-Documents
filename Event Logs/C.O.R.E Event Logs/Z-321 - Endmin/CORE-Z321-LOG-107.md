# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-107**

### **RESIDENTIAL CARE ADVISORY – BELLE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Personnel Welfare

---

## **SUMMARY**

Although Belle is fully capable of performing her duties independently as Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority, Residential Services and Behavioral Sciences recommend that she not be left alone for extended periods whenever reasonably avoidable.

This recommendation is not based on operational concerns.

It exists to support Belle's emotional well-being.

---

## **Background**

Belle spent much of her childhood isolated and emotionally neglected before being rescued and later adopted by Lucy Merryweather.

Since joining the Merryweather family and later becoming one of the Friendly Roommates, Belle has become accustomed to living alongside people she trusts.

She thrives in social environments.

Extended periods of unnecessary isolation have been observed to noticeably affect her mood.

---

## **Behavioral Observations**

When left alone for a prolonged time with no work to occupy her, Belle may:

- Become unusually quiet.
    
- Wander the residential wing looking for company.
    
- Hug a plush rabbit or blanket while waiting.
    
- Sit in Endmin's office reading until someone returns.
    
- Worry that she is bothering others, despite evidence to the contrary.
    

These behaviors are temporary and resolve quickly once she is reunited with friends or colleagues.

---

## **Buddy System**

Belle is included in the Blacksite's informal **Buddy System**.

She is frequently accompanied by:

- Endmin.
    
- Members of the Friendly Roommates.
    
- Alice (when visiting from the Merryweather Manor).
    
- Lucy during Banlands assignments.
    

This arrangement developed naturally rather than by formal policy.

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, Endmin finished organizing mission reports and noticed the office next door was unusually quiet.

She looked over.

Belle was sitting in her chair, hugging a small rabbit plush while reading through tomorrow's schedule.

When she noticed Endmin, her ears perked up immediately.

> **"...Done working?"

Endmin nodded.

> **"...Yeah."

Belle smiled, stood up, and picked up her oversized sword.

> **"...Want to walk back together?"

Endmin couldn't help but smile.

> **"...Of course."

The two Directors switched off the office lights and headed back toward Room N-07 together, chatting quietly along the way.
