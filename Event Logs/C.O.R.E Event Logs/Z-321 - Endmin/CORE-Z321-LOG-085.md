# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-085**

### **WEAPONS OBSERVATION – BELLE'S UNIDENTIFIED BLADE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Armory / Personnel

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following Belle's arrival at APRT-70, Armory Division documented the weapon she carries during security duties.

Despite extensive examination, the weapon cannot be classified under any conventional category.

It combines characteristics of a:

- Machete.
    
- Battle axe.
    
- Greatsword resembling the legendary Excalibur.
    

As no suitable classification exists, Armory currently refers to it simply as:

> **"Belle's Sword."**

---

## **Weapon Description**

**Owner:** Belle

**Classification:** Unknown Hybrid Blade

**Estimated Weight:** **50 kilograms (110 lbs)**

**Construction:** Unknown

The blade possesses:

- A long, single-edged primary cutting surface similar to a machete.
    
- A pronounced axe head integrated into the forward section.
    
- A broad reinforced spine.
    
- A two-handed hilt.
    
- Overall dimensions comparable to an oversized greatsword.
    

Despite its massive weight, Belle routinely carries it as though it were a normal field weapon.

Engineering Division has found no evidence of weight-reduction technology or anomalous assistance.

---

## **Mobility Observation**

While Belle has little trouble carrying the weapon on her back, an unusual problem appears whenever she sprints with it in her hands.

Rather than holding the sword low, Belle habitually lifts it high over her head while running.

The combination of the weapon's mass and its elevated center of gravity frequently causes Belle to lose her balance.

Security footage has documented numerous instances of the following sequence:

1. Belle begins sprinting heroically.
    
2. The sword shifts backward.
    
3. Belle tips over.
    
4. Belle lands flat on her back.
    
5. Belle immediately stands up and continues running as if nothing happened.
    

Medical Division has confirmed this happens often enough that Belle has become remarkably good at falling safely.

---

## **Training Sessions**

Belle's primary sparring partner is Endmin.

Training usually takes place in the Blacksite's practice arena.

The contrast between their weapons has become a favorite sight among nearby personnel.

Belle enters carrying a **50-kilogram hybrid blade**.

Endmin arrives...

Holding an ordinary-looking baseball bat.

The two exchange a respectful nod.

Then begin sparring.

---

## **Combat Assessment**

Observers quickly discovered that the matches are far more technical than expected.

Belle relies on:

- Powerful sweeping strikes.
    
- Momentum.
    
- Defensive blocks using the massive blade.
    

Endmin counters with:

- Precise footwork.
    
- Roller-skate mobility.
    
- Quick repositioning.
    
- Controlled strikes using the baseball bat.
    

Neither attempts to injure the other.

The sessions are conducted purely for practice.

No injuries have been recorded.

### **Closing Observation**

One afternoon, several researchers gathered to watch another friendly sparring match.

Belle planted her enormous blade into the ground.

Endmin rested her baseball bat across one shoulder.

> **Belle:**
> 
> "...Ready?"

Endmin grinned.

> **"...Ready."

A loud clang echoed across the arena as steel met wood.

To everyone's surprise...

The baseball bat held.

The practice continued for nearly half an hour.

When it finally ended, both of them were laughing.

No bruises.

No injuries.

Just another afternoon spent training between a bodyguard with a fifty-kilogram weapon of doom...

...and her best friend armed with a baseball bat.
