# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-081**

### **DIPLOMATIC VISIT – MERRYWEATHER MANOR TOUR (PART II)**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Cultural Exchange / Banlands Relations

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following their arrival at Merryweather Manor, Z-321 (Endmin) personally guided the visiting delegation through the estate.

The tour included several sections of the manor, its gardens, training grounds, library, and observation terraces overlooking the Banlands.

Also accompanying the group was the newly appointed **Head of Security, Alice**, who maintained a professional yet approachable demeanor throughout the visit.

One detail, however, became increasingly noticeable.

Despite Endmin frequently talking about her pet rabbit...

The rabbit was nowhere to be seen.

---

## **The Manor**

The interior of Merryweather Manor reflected both elegance and practicality.

Rather than excessive luxury, the building emphasized warmth.

Large windows overlooked the floating island's forests.

Fireplaces quietly crackled despite the pleasant weather outside.

Portraits of previous Banlands Wardens lined the main hallway.

Director Lucious paused briefly to admire several historical artifacts.

> **Director Lucious:**

> "...You've preserved your history well."

Lucy smiled proudly.

> **"We try."**

---

## **The Library**

The group's first stop was an enormous circular library.

Thousands of books stretched from floor to ceiling.

Some appeared centuries old.

Others were surprisingly modern.

Endmin absentmindedly ran her fingers along one bookshelf.

> **"...I spent most of my childhood here."

Taiga laughed.

> **"...That explains a lot."

Endmin looked confused.

> **"...What does that mean?"

> **"...You read instruction manuals for fun."

Taiga replied.

Endmin quietly nodded.

> **"...Fair."

---

## **Training Grounds**

Outside, the group visited the manor's training courtyard.

Several Banlands Guards were practicing swordsmanship.

Others trained with firearms.

The moment Lucy appeared, every guard saluted.

Director Lucious quietly observed the discipline.

> **"...Impressive."

Alice smiled modestly.

> **"Lady Lucy expects us to be prepared."

---

## **The Gardens**

The gardens surrounding the manor overlooked the vast city below.

Colorful flowers lined winding stone paths.

Small streams flowed peacefully between ancient trees.

Star immediately wandered toward a flowerbed.

Nightshade followed a few steps behind.

Just in case.

Bao took a deep breath.

> **"...This place is beautiful."

Astral nodded in agreement.

---

## **Alice**

Throughout the tour, Alice remained close enough to ensure everyone's safety without interrupting conversations.

She answered questions politely.

Offered directions when needed.

Even shared a few stories about the manor's history.

Taiga eventually smiled.

> **"...You're a lot friendlier than your predecessor."

Alice laughed awkwardly.

> **"...I hope that's a good thing."

Mart answered immediately.

> **"...Very."

---

## **A Curious Question**

As the tour continued, Taiga looked around.

Then toward Endmin.

> **"...Wait."

> **"...Where's your rabbit?"

Endmin stopped walking.

Her ears almost poked through her Ether illusion before she corrected them.

She looked around instinctively.

> **"...She usually..."

Her voice trailed off.

> **"...She's normally nearby."

Lucy glanced toward the gardens.

> **"...That's unusual."

Even Alice looked mildly surprised.

> **"...I haven't seen her today either."

---

## **The Search**

The group spent the next several minutes casually looking around the manor grounds.

Behind bushes.

Near the greenhouse.

Along the garden paths.

Nothing.

Star giggled.

> **"...Maybe she's hiding."

Nightshade replied dryly.

> **"...She's a rabbit."

> **"That's kind of what they do."

Everyone chuckled.

Endmin, however, seemed genuinely concerned.

> **"...She always comes when she hears me."

---

> **"The manor grounds are completely enclosed."

> **"She couldn't have wandered very far."

---

### Lucy

> **"She has escaped exactly three times."

She looked toward Endmin.

> **"...Every single time she was found eating flowers."

Endmin covered her face.

> **"...Mom..."

---

### Taiga

> **"...I think your rabbit likes vegetables almost as much as you do."

Endmin immediately protested.

> **"...Coincidence!"

Nobody believed her.

---

### Director Lucious

Watching the entire search unfold, he quietly smiled.

> **"...Even Wardens have to look for missing pets."

Lucy laughed.

> **"Occupational hazard."

---

## **Closing Observation**

As everyone reached the rear gardens, Endmin suddenly paused.

Her ears twitched beneath the illusion.

She smiled.

Very softly, she called out into the tall grass.

> **"...Come here."

The grass rustled.

Everyone instinctively turned.

Something small was hopping toward them.

Endmin's smile grew wider.

> **"...There you are."

The little rabbit had finally decided to make an appearance.
