## **SUMMARY**

Due to the destructive nature of **Bliss of Whitefall**, Miss Neko's handheld anti-material howitzer, Director Lucious authorized a live-fire demonstration within an uninhabited region of the Starlands.

The exercise also marked **Endmin's first visit to Starlands.**

---

## **FIRST IMPRESSIONS**

As the portal opened, Endmin stepped through cautiously.

She immediately stopped walking.

Floating islands stretched endlessly into the distance.

A brilliant rhombus-shaped sun illuminated fields of flowers unlike anything she had ever seen.

Waterfalls poured from islands suspended in the sky before vanishing into clouds far below.

For several moments...

She simply looked.

Star was almost vibrating with excitement.

> **Star:** "Welcome to Starlands!"

Nightshade folded her arms, though the smile on her face betrayed her usual stoic demeanor.

> **Nightshade:** "We've been waiting to show you."

Endmin slowly turned in place, taking in every direction.

The colors.

The sky.

The flowers.

The impossible landscape.

She smiled.

> **Endmin:** "...It's beautiful."

Star immediately grabbed one of her hands.

> **Star:** "Come on!"

Without waiting for an answer, she enthusiastically began showing Endmin everything she considered important.

---

## **A TOUR OF STARLANDS**

The next hour consisted almost entirely of Star proudly introducing her home.

Every few minutes...

Another excited explanation.

> "These flowers only bloom every forty-eight hours!"

> "That's Leo's favorite hill!"

> "Those islands weren't there last month!"

Nightshade occasionally corrected Star's more exaggerated claims.

> **Nightshade:** "...Half of that is true."

Endmin laughed throughout the entire tour.

For someone who had spent years wondering what colors looked like...

Starlands was overwhelming in the best possible way.

---

## **LIVE FIRE DEMONSTRATION**

Once the tour concluded, everyone relocated to a designated weapons testing zone.

Several kilometers away, an abandoned mountain selected for geological research stood alone.

Miss Neko rested **Bliss of Whitefall** across her shoulder.

Endmin stared at the weapon.

> **Endmin:** "...That's..."

She looked at the barrel.

> "...Still absurd."

Miss Neko grinned.

> **Miss Neko:** "You'll love it."

Director Lucious addressed everyone present.

> **Director Lucious:** "Standard safety distances."

> "Eye and hearing protection."

> "Remain behind the marked line."

He looked directly at Endmin.

> "...Now you'll understand why I stopped her from firing this at the Blacksite."

Endmin nodded.

She wasn't entirely convinced.

---

## **FIRING SEQUENCE**

Miss Neko loaded a standard **12.7×665 mm Basic Howitzer** round.

She planted the enormous weapon against her shoulder as though it weighed almost nothing.

The range officer raised a hand.

> "Range clear."

A deep silence settled over the valley.

Miss Neko smiled.

She squeezed the trigger.

The report was unlike a conventional firearm.

It was closer to distant thunder.

A brilliant projectile crossed the valley almost instantly.

Several kilometers away—

The mountain simply...

Disappeared.

Not exploded.

Not shattered.

A massive section ceased to exist, replaced by an expanding cloud of pulverized stone.

The shockwave reached the observation point several seconds later.

Dust rolled across the valley floor.

Silence followed.

Endmin slowly lowered the binoculars.

She blinked once.

Then again.

> **Endmin:** "...Director..."

Lucious didn't even look away from the impact site.

> **Director Lucious:** "Yes?"

> **Endmin:** "...You weren't exaggerating."

Lucious smiled.

> "...No."

---

## **REACTIONS**

Taiga immediately began cheering.

> **Taiga:** "THAT WAS AWESOME!"

Mart quietly adjusted his glasses.

> "...I'm revising my definition of 'large caliber.'"

Frost sighed.

> "...There goes another mountain."

Bao laughed.

> "...Good thing Starlands has plenty."

Star clapped enthusiastically.

Nightshade looked toward Miss Neko.

> "...Show-off."

Miss Neko bowed dramatically.

> "...Professionally."

---

## **ENDMIN'S COMMENT**

As the group prepared to return, Endmin took one last look across the valley.

The immense crater left by Bliss of Whitefall dominated the landscape.

She looked down at the ordinary 7.62 mm rifle slung across her own back.

Then back toward Miss Neko.

She smiled.

> **Endmin:** "...I think I'll keep my rifle."

Miss Neko laughed.

> **Miss Neko:** "Good."

> "...Because I'm not sharing this one."

---

## **STAR'S GIFT**

Before everyone returned through the portal, Star quietly picked a small blue flower growing near the path.

She tucked it behind Endmin's ear.

> **Star:** "Now you have something from Starlands."

Endmin gently touched the flower.

She looked across the floating islands one final time.

Then toward Star and Nightshade.

> **Endmin:** "...Thank you."

Nightshade gave a small nod.

Star wrapped Endmin in a quick hug.

> **Star:** "Come back anytime."

---

## **DIRECTOR'S NOTE**

No safety violations occurred during testing.

One mountain was removed from the Starlands landscape with prior authorization.

Miss Neko later requested permission to demonstrate additional ammunition types.

That request remains under review.

Director Lucious's handwritten response consisted of a single sentence:

> **"One mountain per demonstration is enough."**
