# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-021**

### **SOCIAL OBSERVATION — THE MASK**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – RESIDENT INTERACTION

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following Z-321 ("Endmin")'s increased involvement in Blacksite activities, curiosity regarding her protective mask became increasingly common.

Personnel frequently asked what she looked like beneath it.

Endmin consistently answered with the same polite response.

She declined every request.

---

## **COMMON INTERACTIONS**

The following exchange became increasingly familiar throughout the Blacksite.

> **Researcher:** "Can we see what's under the mask?"

Endmin smiled apologetically.

> **Endmin:** "...I'm sorry."

> "...No."

---

Another occasion.

> **Security Officer:** "Just for a second?"

> **Endmin:** "...I'd rather not."

---

Another.

> **Junior Researcher:** "Not even a little?"

Endmin laughed softly.

> "...No."

> "...But thank you for asking instead of touching it."

Unlike earlier incidents involving unwanted contact, personnel gradually became more respectful after learning the importance of her mask.

Most accepted her answer immediately.

---

## **ROOM N-07**

Only two people at the Blacksite, aside from Lucy, had ever seen Endmin's eyes.

Mart.

Taiga.

Neither had ever spoken publicly about what they saw.

Occasionally someone would ask.

> **Researcher:** "Come on."

> "...You've seen them."

> "...What are they like?"

Taiga simply smiled.

> "...Beautiful."

When asked for more details, she shook her head.

> "...They're not mine to describe."

---

Mart was asked the same question during a maintenance shift.

He looked up from the book he was reading.

Thought for a moment.

Then quietly answered,

> "...Some things..."

He smiled faintly.

> "...Should stay special."

He returned to his book.

No further explanation followed.

---

## **ENDMIN'S RESPONSE**

One evening, Taiga asked Endmin if she was ever bothered by everyone's curiosity.

Endmin shook her head.

> "...Not really."

She adjusted her mask.

> "...People are curious."

A small smile.

> "...That's okay."

Taiga tilted her head.

> "...Then why don't you show them?"

Endmin looked toward the window.

The setting sun painted the room in warm colors she could finally appreciate.

After a long silence, she answered.

> "...Because trust..."

She gently rested a hand on the side of her mask.

> "...Should never feel ordinary."

---

## **LUCY'S COMMENT**

When informed that personnel had become increasingly respectful of Endmin's decision, Lucy smiled.

> "...Good."

She folded her arms.

> "...If she chooses to show someone one day..."

A small smile crossed her face.

> "...It should always be her choice."

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL SERVICES NOTE**

Researchers noted that Endmin no longer appears anxious when asked about her mask.

Instead of reacting with fear, she politely establishes a clear personal boundary.

Psychological Services considers this a significant sign of emotional recovery and growing confidence.

Her willingness to say "no" without fear of rejection is viewed as healthy progress.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Z-321 – Endmin**

- Protective mask: Worn at all times outside exceptional circumstances.
    
- Requests to remove mask: Politely declined.
    
- Personnel compliance: High.
    
- Emotional response: Calm and confident.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late that evening, Room N-07 had gone quiet.

Endmin sat by the window, watching the colors of the sunset fade into the deep blues of the approaching night.

Taiga and Mart were already preparing for bed.

For just a brief moment, Endmin looked toward them.

She smiled beneath the mask.

Those two already knew what lay underneath.

Not because they had asked.

But because, one quiet night beneath the stars...

She had chosen to trust them.
