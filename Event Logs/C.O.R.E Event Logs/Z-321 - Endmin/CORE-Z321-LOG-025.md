# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-025**

### **OBSERVATION REPORT — Z-321 AND MART'S RIOT SHIELD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – RESIDENT INTERACTION

---

## **SUMMARY**

Personnel have repeatedly observed an unusual habit shared between Z-321 ("Endmin") and Mart Sunbringer.

Whenever Mart removes his Nekonium riot shield—for maintenance, training, or simply setting it aside—Endmin almost instinctively retrieves it and places it back on his back before walking away.

She has done so often enough that it has become a familiar sight around the Blacksite.

---

## **INITIAL OBSERVATION**

The behavior was first documented after a training exercise.

Mart leaned his shield against a wall while cleaning his shotgun.

A few minutes later, Endmin happened to walk past.

Without saying anything, she picked up the shield.

She walked behind Mart.

Lifted it.

Fastened it neatly onto the mounting harness on his back.

Then quietly continued on her way.

Mart didn't even look up.

> **Mart:** "...Thanks."

> **Endmin:** "...You're welcome."

The interaction lasted less than ten seconds.

---

## **FREQUENCY**

Over the following weeks, Security cameras documented dozens of similar moments.

Examples included:

- Outside the shooting range.
    
- Engineering workshop.
    
- Room N-07.
    
- Cafeteria.
    
- Courtyard.
    
- Greenhouse.
    

If Mart forgot the shield...

Endmin noticed.

If she was nearby...

The shield somehow ended up back where it belonged.

---

## **WHEN ASKED WHY**

During lunch, Taiga finally asked the obvious question.

> **Taiga:** "Endmin."

> "...Why do you keep putting Mart's shield back?"

Endmin looked genuinely confused.

> "...Because he should have it."

Taiga tilted her head.

> "...But why?"

Endmin thought quietly.

Then answered.

> "...Back home..."

She rested her hand lightly on the shield.

> "...The people protecting everyone else..."

A pause.

> "...Needed someone protecting them too."

She smiled at Mart.

> "...This helps."

Mart looked down at the shield.

Then back at Endmin.

He quietly nodded.

> "...Thank you."

---

## **MART'S RESPONSE**

When interviewed separately, Mart admitted something surprising.

> "...I don't actually notice anymore."

He smiled softly.

> "...If I forget..."

> "...I just assume Endmin already fixed it."

He laughed quietly.

> "...She's usually right."

---

## **THE SHIELD ITSELF**

Neko later remarked that Mart's shield had become almost symbolic.

Originally designed as defensive equipment, it had gradually become part of Mart's everyday appearance.

The shield was rarely absent for long.

Not because Mart remembered it...

But because Endmin did.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Taiga Sunbringer

Taiga laughed after witnessing the routine several times.

> "...You two don't even talk anymore."

She pointed at Mart's back.

> "...She just installs the shield."

Endmin nodded matter-of-factly.

> "...Someone has to."

---

### Neko

Neko crossed his arms.

> "...Good."

He looked at Mart.

> "...That shield only works..."

Then toward Endmin.

> "...If it's actually with him."

---

### Lucy

During one of her visits, Lucy observed the interaction from a distance.

She smiled knowingly.

> "...Old habits."

Director Lucious glanced toward her.

Lucy explained,

> "...Back in the Banlands..."

> "...She always checked everyone's equipment before missions."

She watched Endmin adjust one of the shoulder straps.

> "...Looks like she still does."

---

## **ENDMIN'S PERSPECTIVE**

Later that evening, Mart asked Endmin directly.

> **Mart:** "...Do you really think I need it all the time?"

Endmin adjusted the final buckle before stepping back.

> "...Hopefully..."

She smiled gently.

> "...You'll never need it."

A brief pause.

> "...But if you do..."

She lightly tapped the shield.

> "...You'll be glad it was there."

Mart instinctively reached back, resting a hand on the Nekonium plating.

He smiled.

> "...Fair point."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Although no formal procedure exists requiring Mart to carry his shield at all times, personnel have largely stopped questioning the routine.

If Mart leaves the shield behind...

Most simply assume Endmin will notice.

She usually does.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, after another long training session, Mart absentmindedly left his shield outside Room N-07.

Before he could even remember it, there was a quiet knock on his door.

Endmin stood outside.

Without a word, she handed him the shield.

Mart accepted it with a small smile.

> **Mart:** "...I forgot again."

Endmin smiled beneath her mask.

> "...I know."

She turned and walked back toward her room.

Mart secured the shield onto his back before heading inside.

Neither of them seemed to think the exchange was unusual anymore.

It had simply become one of the many quiet ways they looked after each other.
