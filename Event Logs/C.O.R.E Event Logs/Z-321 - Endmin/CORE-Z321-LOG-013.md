# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-013**

### **RESEARCH REPORT — ANALYSIS OF Z-321'S ANOMALOUS VISUAL PERCEPTION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – RESEARCH / MEDICAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following discussions with Lucy and after giving her own informed consent, Z-321 ("Endmin") voluntarily agreed to participate in a series of **non-invasive** studies examining her unique method of perceiving the world.

The research was conducted under the following conditions:

- No removal of the protective mask.
    
- No experimental treatment of her eyes.
    
- No procedures intended to alter or restore her vision.
    
- Endmin could end testing at any time.
    

Lucy approved the study on the condition that it remain observational rather than corrective.

---

## **GENERAL FINDINGS**

Research confirms that Endmin's perception is **not enhanced human eyesight**.

Instead, it is a distinct sensory system that constructs an environmental model using minute echoes, vibrations, and the surrounding presence of ambient Ether.

Although she cannot perceive color and only identifies the outlines and contours of objects, her sensory system surpasses ordinary vision in several specialized areas.

Researchers have concluded that comparing her perception directly to human eyesight is misleading.

Each possesses advantages the other lacks.

---

## **ABILITY 01 — ETHER SENSE**

One of Endmin's most valuable abilities is the continuous perception of Ether.

Testing confirmed Lucy's earlier statements:

- Ether exists throughout the environment.
    
- Ambient Ether is naturally present almost everywhere.
    
- Under ordinary conditions, this background Ether is harmless and remains at concentrations far below those associated with Ether Corruption.
    

Endmin is able to distinguish this normal background from dangerous concentrations.

During testing, Research Division introduced inert Ether samples at varying concentrations.

Without instruments, Endmin accurately identified the location and relative intensity of every sample.

She also immediately recognized the sample containing unstable Ether.

When asked how she knew, she simply answered,

> "...It doesn't feel like everything else."

---

## **ABILITY 02 — EXTRAORDINARY DEPTH PERCEPTION**

Conventional human depth perception relies heavily on binocular vision and visual cues.

Endmin's spatial awareness functions differently.

Researchers constructed a complex obstacle course with narrow gaps, uneven terrain, suspended objects, and transparent barriers.

Endmin completed the course without making contact with a single obstacle.

Motion analysis revealed remarkably consistent distance estimation.

One researcher remarked,

> "...She isn't guessing."

> "...She knows exactly where everything is."

---

## **ABILITY 03 — HIGH-SPEED SPATIAL REACTION**

Testing demonstrated that Endmin's environmental awareness allows exceptionally rapid reactions to fast-moving objects.

During controlled exercises using non-lethal training projectiles, Endmin consistently avoided incoming rounds that ordinary participants failed to evade.

She was not reacting after seeing the projectile in flight.

Instead, researchers believe she perceived the subtle disturbances created by its movement through the surrounding air.

This provides additional reaction time compared to conventional visual tracking.

Director Lucious summarized the finding as follows:

> "...If something is moving toward her..."

> "...She usually knows before it gets there."

Researchers note that while this capability can allow her to evade extremely fast threats under favorable conditions, it is **not** a guarantee against every projectile or attack. Success still depends on factors such as distance, timing, available space, and the nature of the threat.

---

## **ABILITY 04 — LOW-LIGHT OPERATION**

Because Endmin's perception does not rely primarily on visible light, reductions in ambient lighting have minimal effect on her navigation.

During comparative testing:

- Standard lighting.
    
- Dim emergency lighting.
    
- Near-total darkness.
    

Endmin's navigation accuracy remained essentially unchanged.

She later explained,

> "...Darkness isn't empty."

> "...Everything is still there."

Research Division concluded that her perception grants effective operation in environments where ordinary human vision would be severely limited.

---

## **LIMITATIONS**

Despite these remarkable advantages, Endmin's perception retains significant limitations.

She cannot naturally perceive:

- Color.
    
- Conventional visual artwork in the way most people experience it.
    
- Facial expressions through color or subtle visual changes alone.
    
- The visual beauty of sunsets, flowers, or paintings as described by others.
    

When shown a painting by Star, Endmin gently traced the frame with her fingertips.

She smiled.

> "...Everyone says it's beautiful."

After a brief silence, she added,

> "...I wish I knew what those colors looked like."

The room remained quiet.

---

> "Understand how she experiences the world."

> "Don't spend your time wishing she experienced yours."
