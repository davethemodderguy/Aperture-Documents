# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-105**

### **PERSONNEL DEPARTURE – ALICE RETURNS TO THE MERRYWEATHER MANOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the completion of Lucy Merryweather's assignment, **Alice**, Head of Security of the Merryweather Manor, concluded her temporary stay at APRT-70.

Her assignment to supervise Endmin's well-being was deemed successful.

Before departing, the residents of Room N-07 gathered to see her off.

---

## **Departure**

Alice stood near the main entrance with a packed travel bag.

Endmin folded her arms.

> **"...Thanks for putting up with us."

Alice smiled.

> **"It was hardly difficult."

She looked directly at Endmin.

> **"...Remember to eat on time."

Endmin sighed.

> **"...You're starting to sound like Mom."

Alice laughed.

> **"...I've been working with Lady Lucy for years."

---

## **A Gift**

As Alice prepared to leave, Taiga suddenly jogged over.

She held out a neatly wrapped package.

> **"...Wait."

Alice looked surprised.

> **"...For me?"

Taiga nodded.

> **"...A little thank-you."

Alice carefully opened the wrapping.

Inside was a beautifully crafted insulated scarf in white and light blue, embroidered with a small snow leopard paw on one end and the Merryweather Manor crest on the other.

A handwritten note rested inside.

> **"For the person who kept looking after everyone else.  
> Stay warm.  
> — Taiga"**

Alice ran a hand across the fabric.

She smiled warmly.

> **"...I'll treasure it."

Taiga grinned.

> **"...Knew you'd like it."

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Alice has resumed her duties as **Head of Security of the Merryweather Manor**.

The scarf gifted by Taiga now accompanies her on duty and has been observed folded neatly over the back of her office chair when not in use.

---

### **Closing Observation**

As Alice's transport disappeared through the Banlands portal, Taiga stood with her hands in her pockets.

Endmin walked over.

> **"...You didn't have to make her a gift."

Taiga shrugged.

> **"...I wanted to."

She watched the portal slowly close.

> **"...She looked after us."

Endmin smiled softly.

> **"...Yeah."

> **"...She did."

With one final shimmer, the portal closed.

Room N-07 felt just a little quieter.

But everyone knew Alice would always be welcome whenever she returned.
