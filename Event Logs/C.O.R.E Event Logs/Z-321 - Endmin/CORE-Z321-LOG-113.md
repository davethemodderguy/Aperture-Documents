# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-113**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION – AN OVERPROTECTIVE BROTHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Residential

---

## **SUMMARY**

Since moving into Room N-07, personnel have observed that **Lucius Merryweather** is highly protective of his younger sister, Endmin.

Behavioral Sciences notes that this behavior is consistent with his personality prior to his disappearance. However, Lucius has yet to fully realize just how much Endmin has changed during the four years they were apart.

---

## **Observation**

Lucius instinctively positions himself between Endmin and unfamiliar people whenever he perceives a potential threat.

He also frequently asks questions such as:

> **"You okay?"**

> **"Need me to come with you?"**

> **"Did someone bother you?"**

Endmin usually responds with the same embarrassed sigh.

> **"...I'm fine, Lucius."

---

## **A Reality Check**

During a training session at the firing range, Lucius watched from behind the safety barrier.

He expected to see the little sister he remembered.

Instead...

Endmin effortlessly transitioned between rifle drills, sidearm drills, and close-quarters exercises with her baseball bat.

She demonstrated precise weapon handling before engaging moving targets with her custom rifle and its integrated underbarrel shotgun.

Every drill was completed with practiced confidence.

When she finished, the instructors simply nodded.

> **"Excellent work, Director Endmin."**

Lucius stood speechless.

---

## **Later That Evening**

Back in Room N-07, Lucius finally asked the question that had been bothering him.

> **"...When did you become this strong?"**

Endmin smiled faintly.

> **"...While you were gone."

The room grew quiet.

Lucius lowered his head.

> **"...I'm sorry I wasn't there."

Endmin immediately shook her head.

> **"...You didn't choose to disappear."

She gently nudged his shoulder.

> **"...Besides..."

She smiled.

> **"...You protected me when we were kids."

> **"...Now it's my turn to protect people too."**

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Lucius continues to be noticeably protective of Endmin.

While he now understands that she is the **Head of the Central Execution Department** and routinely assists Lucy in neutralizing dangerous threats, he still occasionally forgets that the shy little sister he remembers has grown into one of the Banlands' most capable officers.

Endmin, although occasionally embarrassed by his concern, has never asked him to stop.

She knows exactly why he does it.

He missed four years of her life.

And now that he's home, he's trying not to miss any more.
