# **BANLANDS ADMINISTRATION**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-086**

### **ORGANIZATIONAL OVERVIEW – KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Administrative / Intergovernmental

---

## **SUMMARY**

To maintain accountability within the Banlands government and prevent abuses of authority, the Banlands operates three primary enforcement organizations.

Although each division has distinct responsibilities, they routinely cooperate on operations involving public safety, national security, and internal oversight.

Collectively, they form one of the Banlands' primary law enforcement structures.

---

# **Banlands Enforcement Structure**

## **1. Banlands Enforcement Division (BED)**

**Director:** **Lucy Merryweather**

**Primary Responsibilities:**

- Public safety.
    
- Criminal investigations.
    
- Protection of civilians.
    
- Emergency response.
    
- Patrol and security throughout the Banlands.
    

As the Warden of the Banlands, Lucy oversees the division and is responsible for maintaining peace across the realm.

Personnel often describe the Banlands Enforcement Division as the organization's public-facing branch.

---

## **2. Central Execution Department (CED)**

**Director:** **Endmin (Lindervene Merryweather)**

**Primary Responsibilities:**

- Neutralization of high-value threats.
    
- Recovery and protection of strategic assets.
    
- Counter-terrorism operations.
    
- High-risk missions beyond the capabilities of standard enforcement units.
    
- Special operations requiring Ether expertise.
    

The Central Execution Department is deployed only when conventional forces are insufficient.

Despite the intimidating name, every operation is conducted under Banlands law and strict authorization procedures.

Members are selected for exceptional discipline and operational capability.

---

## **3. Krampus Compliance Authority (KCA)**

**Director:** **Belle**

**Primary Responsibilities:**

- Internal affairs investigations.
    
- Preventing bribery and corruption.
    
- Investigating abuse of authority.
    
- Auditing government agencies.
    
- Ensuring officers comply with Banlands law and regulations.
    

Unlike the other two divisions, the KCA primarily monitors Banlands officials rather than civilians.

Its purpose is straightforward:

> **"Nobody is above the law."**

Belle's appointment surprised many due to her cheerful personality.

However, those familiar with her note that she becomes remarkably serious whenever honesty or fairness is at stake.

---

## **Inter-Division Cooperation**

The three divisions frequently support one another.

Examples include:

- BED securing an area while CED engages a dangerous target.
    
- KCA investigating misconduct within either division when necessary.
    
- Joint operations involving public safety and government oversight.
    

Although their responsibilities differ, cooperation between the divisions is considered excellent.

# **Identification Cards**

Each division director carries a personalized Banlands access card.

---

### **Lucy Merryweather**

Lucy's identification card follows the standard government design.

It remains clean, formal, and professionally maintained.

Like its owner, it is straightforward and recognizable.

---

### **Endmin**

Endmin's identification card contains one unusual feature.

The eyes in her identification photograph have been crossed out with thick black marker.

This modification dates back to the period before her Ether Corruption was cured, when official photographs inadvertently revealed the distinctive appearance of her eyes.

Although her condition has since been treated, Endmin never requested a replacement.

When asked why, she simply shrugged.

> **"...It's part of my history."

---

### **Belle**

Belle's access card is entirely legitimate.

It is also covered with tiny rabbit paw prints.

Investigation determined that Belle had enthusiastically decorated the protective plastic sleeve herself.

When asked whether this violated identification standards, Lucy reviewed the card.

She looked at Belle.

Then at the tiny pink paw prints.

She smiled.

> **"...Approved."

The card remains valid.
