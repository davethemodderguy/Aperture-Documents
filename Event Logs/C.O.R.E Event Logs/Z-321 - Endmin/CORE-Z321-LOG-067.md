# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-067**

### **ABILITY DOCUMENTATION – OCULAR COMPULSION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Cognitive Anomaly / Personnel Safety

---

## **SUMMARY**

Behavioral and Cognitive Research Divisions have documented an additional Ether-based ability possessed by Z-321 (Endmin).

The ability has been designated:

> **Ocular Compulsion**

The phenomenon is **not passive** and cannot activate accidentally.

Endmin must intentionally invoke it.

---

## **Activation Requirements**

The effect will only function when all of the following conditions are met:

- Endmin intentionally activates the ability.
    
- Her eyes are **completely uncovered**.
    
- No mask, glasses, visor, or other eye covering is present.
    
- The target maintains direct eye contact for a sufficient duration.
    

If any requirement is absent, the ability does not activate.

Simply looking at Endmin during everyday conversation is completely safe.

---

## **Observed Effects**

Upon successful activation, the target experiences:

- Rapid narrowing of attention toward Endmin.
    
- Difficulty diverting their gaze.
    
- Accelerated mental fatigue.
    
- Reduced awareness of surrounding distractions.
    
- A heightened tendency to focus on Endmin's words.
    

The effect resembles a powerful hypnotic suggestion rather than complete mind control.

Subjects retain awareness of their surroundings and moral judgment, but their concentration becomes overwhelmingly centered on Endmin until the effect naturally dissipates or she voluntarily releases it.

Medical monitoring shows that prolonged use is mentally exhausting for both the target and Endmin.

---

## **Known Immunities**

Repeated testing has shown no observable effect on:

- Director Lucious
    
- Lucy
    
- Bao
    

The exact reason remains undetermined.

Behavioral Sciences proposes that unusually strong cognitive resilience or exceptionally powerful anomalous resistance may render the ability ineffective.

---

## **Training Incident**

During a controlled demonstration, Taiga volunteered to assist.

Endmin removed her glasses.

Her eyes met Taiga's.

Several seconds passed.

> **Endmin:**
> 
> "Taiga."

> "Focus."

Taiga immediately stopped looking around the room.

Her attention settled entirely on Endmin.

Researchers attempted to distract her.

No response.

Endmin calmly continued speaking.

> "Take one step forward."

Taiga did so without hesitation.

After several seconds, Endmin looked away.

The effect immediately weakened.

Taiga blinked several times.

> "...Wait."

> "...Why was I only listening to you?"

Endmin quietly put her glasses back on.

> "...Sorry."

## **Operational Guidance**

Personnel are advised that:

- Routine eye contact with Endmin is harmless.
    
- The ability cannot activate through protective eyewear.
    
- Endmin normally wears either her signature eye mask or round glasses specifically to prevent accidental concern and because she has grown accustomed to covering her eyes.
    
- Demonstrations require approval from both Behavioral Sciences and Endmin herself.
    

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Endmin considers Ocular Compulsion to be one of her least favorite abilities.

She rarely uses it outside of controlled research or emergency situations, describing it as "forcing someone to pay attention," a practice she finds uncomfortable except when absolutely necessary.
