# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-035**

### **FAMILY VISIT & PRIVATE TRUST EVENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – RESIDENT INTERACTION

---

## **SUMMARY**

During one of Lucy's scheduled visits to the Blacksite, she was formally introduced to **Bao**, the Starladian Deity of Clouds and Seas, and mother of Frost.

The meeting was cordial and quickly became an exchange between two mothers whose children now lived under the same roof.

Later that evening, Z-321 ("Endmin") made a personal decision.

She voluntarily revealed her eyes to every member of Room N-07, as well as Director Lucious.

The event was conducted privately and was not recorded by cameras at Endmin's request.

---

## **THE MEETING**

The meeting took place in the greenhouse.

Bao was tending to several flowering plants while Frost quietly helped nearby.

Lucy entered with Endmin.

Bao smiled warmly.

> **Bao:** "So..."

> "...You're Endmin's mother."

Lucy nodded.

> "...And you're Frost's."

The two exchanged a respectful handshake.

---

## **A CONVERSATION BETWEEN MOTHERS**

The discussion quickly shifted away from politics and responsibilities.

Instead, it became surprisingly ordinary.

They spoke about:

- Their daughters.
    
- Worrying too much.
    
- Watching children grow.
    
- Learning when to let them make their own decisions.
    

Bao smiled knowingly.

> **Bao:** "She's a wonderful girl."

Lucy looked toward Endmin.

A faint smile appeared.

> "...I know."

Frost quietly stood beside Endmin.

Neither said much.

They simply enjoyed listening.

---

## **A PRIVATE DECISION**

That evening, Endmin gathered everyone in Room N-07.

Present were:

- Taiga
    
- Mart
    
- Star
    
- Nightshade
    
- Frost
    
- Astral
    
- Director Lucious
    

Lucy stood quietly near the doorway.

Endmin held both hands against the sides of her protective face covering.

She looked slightly nervous.

> **Endmin:** "...There's something..."

> "...I'd like to do."

The room became silent.

Lucy gave a small encouraging nod.

---

## **THE REVEAL**

Endmin slowly removed her protective face covering.

For several moments...

No one spoke.

The room remained completely silent.

The eyes beneath were unlike anything most had ever seen.

Beautiful.

Their hollow irises glowed softly, resonating with faint Ether light.

Despite the injuries they had endured, there was an undeniable elegance to them.

The colors reflected across the room in subtle, shimmering patterns.

Nobody looked away.

Nobody stared with fear.

Only quiet admiration.

Endmin waited anxiously.

---

## **REACTIONS**

### Star

Star smiled first.

She gently hugged Endmin.

> **Star:** "...They're beautiful."

---

### Nightshade

Nightshade gave a small nod.

> "...I can see why you kept them hidden."

Another pause.

> "...Thank you for trusting us."

---

### Frost

Frost looked quietly into Endmin's eyes.

Then smiled.

> "...They're pretty."

Bao gently rested a hand on Frost's shoulder.

---

### Astral

Astral's expression softened.

> "...You don't have anything to be ashamed of."

Endmin lowered her head slightly.

---

### Mart

Mart simply smiled.

He had seen them once before beneath the stars.

Seeing them again only reaffirmed his first impression.

> "...Still beautiful."

---

### Taiga

Tears formed almost immediately.

> "...Now everyone gets to see..."

She smiled through them.

> "...What I couldn't even describe."

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious remained silent for several moments.

Finally, he spoke.

> "...Thank you."

Endmin tilted her head.

> "...For what?"

> **Director Lucious:** "For trusting us."

Nothing more needed to be said.

---

## **LUCY**

Lucy watched the entire scene quietly.

She remembered the years when Endmin had hidden behind the mask out of fear.

Now...

Her daughter had removed it willingly.

Not because anyone asked.

Not because anyone demanded it.

Because she wanted to.

Lucy smiled.

It was perhaps the greatest sign that Endmin had finally found a place where she felt safe.

---

## **AFTERWARD**

Once everyone had admired the colors shimmering gently within Endmin's eyes, she calmly placed the protective face covering back on.

No one asked her to leave it off.

No one asked to see them again.

The trust had already been given.

That was enough.

### **Closing Observation**

Later that night, Room N-07 returned to its usual routine.

Books lay scattered across the room.

Taiga and Mart argued over whose turn it was to make sandwiches.

Star sketched flowers while Frost watched.

Endmin quietly adjusted her face covering before climbing into bed.

Nothing had really changed.

And to Endmin...

That was the best part.

Her roommates had seen what she had once feared most.

Then they simply treated her exactly the same as they always had.
