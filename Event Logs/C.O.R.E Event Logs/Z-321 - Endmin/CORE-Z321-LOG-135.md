# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-135**

### **PERSONNEL OBSERVATION – TRANSPORTATION HABITS OF BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

Despite being fully capable of running at remarkable speeds under her own power, Belle Merryweather has developed an amusing reputation around Heaven's Blacksite.

Whenever possible...

Someone else ends up carrying her.

This has become such a common sight that newer personnel often assume Belle dislikes walking.

This assumption is incorrect.

She simply has a family and a group of friends who spoil her whenever they get the chance.

---

## **Documented Methods of Transportation**

### **1. Endmin Express**

When traveling longer distances inside the Blacksite, Endmin will occasionally scoop Belle into her arms before activating her motorized skater boots.

Belle simply relaxes while Endmin glides through the corridors.

> **Belle:** "Thanks, Endmin."

> **Endmin:** "...You were literally standing."

> **Belle:** "I know."

---

### **2. Taiga Piggyback Service**

Taiga frequently lets Belle climb onto her back.

Belle happily wraps her arms around Taiga's shoulders while the two chat on the way to their destination.

Most conversations involve food.

Or rabbits.

Sometimes both.

---

### **3. Mart Airlines**

When Mart needs to travel between sectors quickly, he'll simply lift Belle into his arms before taking flight.

Belle enjoys watching the Blacksite from above.

She has been heard saying:

> **"...Everything looks tiny from up here."

Mart usually replies:

> **"...You already look tiny."

Belle pouts.

---

### **4. Star Express**

Star has discovered that Belle enjoys hugs.

This unfortunately led to Star developing a new transportation method.

She hugs Belle.

Teleports.

Repeats as necessary.

Belle has accepted this as a perfectly normal way of getting around.

---

### **5. Frost Crossing**

Whenever the group encounters lakes or large bodies of water, Frost simply freezes the surface beneath Belle's feet.

As Belle runs across the newly formed ice, Frost continues extending it ahead of her.

Belle thinks it's fun.

---

### **6. Bao's Air Swim**

Bao occasionally lifts Belle using controlled streams of water suspended in midair.

To observers...

It looks as though Belle is calmly swimming through the sky.

Belle finds this endlessly entertaining.

She has attempted to imitate swimming motions despite not needing to.

---

### **7. Researcher Transportation**

Belle has discovered that asking politely...

Combined with looking exceptionally adorable...

Produces surprisingly consistent results.

Researchers have been observed voluntarily carrying Belle, pushing her office chair, opening doors for her, and helping transport her paperwork.

Engineering has jokingly referred to this phenomenon as:

> **"Weaponized Cuteness."**

Belle insists she isn't manipulating anyone.

No one believes her.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Belle remains fully capable of sprinting at extraordinary speed using her Rabbit Thiren abilities.

She can also jump exceptionally high and traverse difficult terrain with ease.

However...

Whenever her family or friends are nearby...

She rarely has to.

By unofficial consensus throughout Heaven's Blacksite, Belle has earned another affectionate nickname:

> **"The Sentient Bunny Plushie with a Big Sword."**

The sword is heavy.

The Director is terrifying when duty calls.

But outside of work...

She is very often found being carried from place to place by people who simply enjoy making sure she never has to walk alone.
