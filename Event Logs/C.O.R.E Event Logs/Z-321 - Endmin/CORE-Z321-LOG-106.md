### **THE MERRYWEATHER FAMILY – HISTORICAL RECORD**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Historical Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

The **Merryweather Family** is one of the oldest surviving noble families in the Banlands. For generations, members of the family have held positions in government, engineering, law enforcement, and public service.

Following the **Banlands Conflict**, much of the family's history was lost, and many members either perished or disappeared. Despite these losses, the family continues to play a significant role in the administration and protection of the Banlands.

---

# **Known Family Lineage**

## **Gray Merryweather**

**Status:** Deceased

Founder of the Merryweather family approximately one hundred years ago.

Remembered as a peaceful, compassionate nobleman who believed authority existed to serve the people rather than rule over them. Many principles still followed by the family today are attributed to Gray.

---

## **Linda Merryweather**

**Status:** Deceased

Wife of Gray Merryweather.

Historical records describe her as a respected member of Banlands nobility who frequently accompanied Gray in charitable and civic affairs.

---

## **Robin Merryweather**

**Status:** Deceased

Son of Gray and Linda Merryweather.

Little information survives regarding his life.

---

## **Cylia Merryweather**

**Status:** Unknown

Very few verified records concerning Cylia remain.

Her role within the family is currently unknown.

---

## **Josh Merryweather**

**Status:** Deceased

Late husband of Lucy Merryweather.

Father of Endmin (Lindervene Merryweather).

Official records confirm he died during the **Banlands Conflict**.

The circumstances surrounding his death remain classified in this archive.

Behavioral Sciences notes that discussions of Josh's final moments remain emotionally difficult for Endmin, who has been observed becoming tearful when hearing the full account.

---

## **Lucy Merryweather**

**Status:** Active

Current head of the surviving Merryweather family.

Warden of the Banlands.

Following the Banlands Conflict, Lucy became one of the family's principal surviving leaders, overseeing reconstruction efforts while continuing to protect the Banlands.

---

## **Lindervene "Endmin" Merryweather**

**Status:** Active

Daughter of Lucy and Josh Merryweather.

Director of the **Central Execution Department**.

Known throughout the Banlands by her preferred name, **Endmin**.

---

## **Lucius Merryweather**

**Status:** Missing

Lucy Merryweather's firstborn son.

Older brother of Endmin by five years.

Disappeared four years ago under unknown circumstances.

Despite extensive efforts, no confirmed information regarding his whereabouts has been recovered.

His case remains open.

---

## **John Merryweather**

**Status:** Active

Brother of Lucy Merryweather.

Senior engineer within the **Central Banlands Computing Department**.

Widely recognized for his engineering expertise and contributions to Banlands computing infrastructure.

---

## **Clover Merryweather**

**Status:** Retired

Founder of the **Krampus Compliance Authority**.

Served as its inaugural Director before retiring and personally transferring leadership to Belle.

Although retired, Clover remains a respected advisor and occasionally visits Krampus Compliance Authority Headquarters.

---

## **Belle Merryweather**

**Status:** Active

Director of the **Krampus Compliance Authority**.

Originally known as **Zhao ██████████** before her adoption.

After being abandoned as a child and nearly killed by Banlands Watchdogs, Belle was rescued and later adopted by Lucy Merryweather, becoming a member of the Merryweather family.

She now serves as Clover's successor, continuing the organization's mission of combating corruption and protecting the integrity of the Banlands government.

---

## **Dave Merryweather**

**Status:** Retired

Former Head of the **Central Execution Department**.

Husband of Clover Merryweather.

Retired alongside Clover before officially passing leadership of the Central Execution Department to Endmin.

---

# **Family Legacy**

Across multiple generations, members of the Merryweather family have founded or led several of the Banlands' most important institutions, including:

- The Merryweather noble house.
    
- The Central Execution Department.
    
- The Krampus Compliance Authority.
    
- Major government engineering initiatives.
    

Their influence extends across public administration, justice, engineering, and security.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

The Merryweather family remains one of the Banlands' most influential surviving families.

While its members have experienced significant loss throughout its history, they continue to serve the Banlands in positions of leadership, justice, security, and public service, carrying forward a legacy that spans more than a century.
