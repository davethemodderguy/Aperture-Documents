# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-104**

### **RESIDENTIAL ADVISORY – RESPECTING BELLE'S PERSONAL SPACE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Personnel Conduct

---

## **SUMMARY**

While Belle is widely known for welcoming hugs and enjoying physical affection from trusted friends, there are two notable exceptions.

Personnel are instructed **not** to grab:

- Belle's long braided hair.
    
- Belle's rabbit ears.
    

These are considered part of her personal space and should only be touched with her clear permission.

---

## **Hair**

Belle's signature braid reaches toward her hips and is one of her most recognizable features.

Despite its appearance, it is **not** a handle.

Grabbing, pulling, or holding the braid without permission is discouraged.

Belle takes great pride in maintaining it and does not appreciate it being tugged or used to stop her.

---

## **Rabbit Ears**

Belle's rabbit ears are highly expressive and sensitive.

Although they often invite curiosity, they should not be grabbed, pulled, or handled without consent.

Unexpected contact is uncomfortable for Belle and is likely to startle her.

Staff are reminded that curiosity is not an excuse to ignore personal boundaries.

---

## **Behavioral Response**

Belle is normally cheerful and forgiving.

However, if someone deliberately grabs her braid or ears after being told not to, she becomes visibly upset.

Typical reactions include:

- Puffing out her cheeks.
    
- Folding her ears back.
    
- Giving an unmistakably disappointed stare.
    
- Firmly telling the person to stop.
    

She rarely responds aggressively and generally causes little or no physical harm, preferring to disengage or verbally correct the behavior.

Behavioral Sciences nevertheless recommends avoiding the situation altogether out of respect for her boundaries.

### **Closing Observation**

One afternoon, a curious intern looked at Belle's long braid swaying behind her.

He instinctively reached out.

Before his hand got close, Alice gently caught his wrist.

> **"...Ask first."

The intern immediately withdrew his hand.

> **"...Sorry."

Belle looked over and smiled.

> **"...Thanks for asking instead."

A few moments later, the intern politely asked if he could gently pet one of her ears.

Belle thought for a second.

Then smiled.

> **"...Sure."

The interaction lasted only a moment, but it ended with everyone smiling—a reminder that the difference between an uncomfortable encounter and a pleasant one was simply asking first.
