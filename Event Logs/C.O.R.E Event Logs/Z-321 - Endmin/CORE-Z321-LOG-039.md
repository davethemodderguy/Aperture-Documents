# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

### **PROGRESSIVE ETHER PURIFICATION OBSERVATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Medical / Ether Research

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the success of the initial Ether Corruption Purge Trial, Medical Division continued long-term observation of Z-321 (Endmin).

Without additional intervention, Endmin's body continued adapting to the reduced Ether Corruption load.

Routine examinations revealed that the total amount of Ether Corruption successfully removed had increased to **45%** of her original baseline.

Researchers believe her body has begun healing itself more effectively now that the most unstable Ether concentrations have been eliminated.

---

## **Current Ether Status**

**Original Ether Corruption:** 100%

**Purged:** **45%**

**Remaining:** **55%**

Current readings indicate the remaining Ether Corruption has become significantly more stable than before the first treatment.

No signs of systemic rejection or Ether imbalance have been observed.

---

## **Physiological Changes**

Medical staff documented continued improvement across multiple areas.

Observed changes include:

- Further reduction in chronic fatigue.
    
- Increased resistance to ambient Ether fluctuations.
    
- Faster natural healing.
    
- Greater physical endurance.
    
- Improved balance during flight.
    
- Reduced need for prolonged recovery after training.
    

Researchers noted that Endmin's body appears to be rebuilding damaged tissue instead of merely compensating for it.

---

## **Wing Transformation**

One of the most unexpected developments involved Endmin's wings.

Originally, her wings consisted almost entirely of dense darkness, a defining characteristic of Banlands physiology.

Over several weeks, faint white feathers began appearing throughout the wings.

The change was gradual.

By the latest examination, patches of white had become clearly visible across both wings.

The transformation remains symmetrical and appears completely natural.

Lucy personally confirmed this was **not** a known phenomenon within Banlands records.

> **Lucy:**
> 
> "I've never seen wings heal like this."

The wings continue to retain their original defensive capabilities, including compatibility with Neko's Nekonium Wing Armor.

---

## **Visual Adaptation**

Endmin's vision has also continued to evolve.

Previously, her perception relied heavily on:

- Ambient Ether.
    
- Vibrations.
    
- Echoes.
    

Recent testing indicates a measurable shift.

While Ether perception remains extraordinarily powerful, her eyes have become **less dependent on surrounding Ether Radiation** to construct a complete image of the world.

New observations include:

- Greater visual stability in low-Ether environments.
    
- Improved recognition of fine detail without Ether assistance.
    
- Reduced visual fatigue.
    
- Increased tolerance to changes in lighting.
    

Researchers believe healthy ocular tissue is gradually reclaiming functions that had long been replaced by Ether-based perception.

Her unique Ether Sense remains fully intact.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late that evening, Endmin stood alone outside the greenhouse.

She slowly stretched her wings.

Against the night sky, the growing patches of white caught the moonlight.

For the first time in years, she noticed something without relying on Ether.

The moon's reflection shimmered across a nearby pond.

She stared at it quietly.

Then smiled.

> "...I can see it a little better now."

From a distance, Lucy watched without interrupting.

She had spent years believing her daughter's condition would only worsen.

Now, with every new white feather, that belief faded just a little more.
