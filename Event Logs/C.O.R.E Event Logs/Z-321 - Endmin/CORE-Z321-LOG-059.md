## **SUMMARY**

Following intelligence provided by Z-945 (Lucy), Personnel Records has been updated with additional information regarding Z-321 (Endmin).

The update concerns her species and legal identity.

---

## **Species Classification**

**Species:** Thiren

Further clarification from Lucy confirms that Endmin is, biologically, a **Thiren**.

Unlike Taiga and Mart, however, Endmin has never been observed displaying obvious Thiren characteristics during daily life.

Specifically:

- No visible ears.
    
- No visible tail.
    

Despite numerous observations by medical staff, engineering personnel, and roommates, the method by which Endmin conceals these features remains unknown.

Even Neko has admitted he has no explanation.

Several humorous theories have circulated throughout the Blacksite, including:

- They retract into her clothing.
    
- They're hidden by Banlands magic.
    
- They're concealed using Ether manipulation.
    
- "She's just really good at hiding them."
    

No evidence currently supports any theory.

When asked directly, Endmin simply smiles.

> **"...Trade secret."**

Further questioning has consistently produced the same response.

---

## **Legal Identity**

According to Banlands civil records provided by Lucy:

**Full Legal Name**

**Lindervene Merryweather**

Mother:

**Lucy Merryweather**

The surname confirms a direct familial relationship with the Warden of the Banlands.

---

## **Preferred Name**

Z-321 has made her preference exceptionally clear.

She wishes to be addressed as:

> **Endmin**

Only.

She considers "Endmin" not merely a nickname, but the name by which she has lived for most of her life.

Personnel have therefore been instructed to use it in all official and casual communication.

---

## **Incident Report**

Following the update, one newly assigned researcher happened to read the personnel file aloud.

> **Researcher:**
> 
> "...Lindervene Merryweather..."

Endmin slowly looked up from cleaning her rifle.

The room became noticeably quieter.

She smiled politely.

A little too politely.

> **Endmin:**
> 
> "...Please don't."

The researcher nervously laughed.

> "...Sorry..."

> "...Lind—"

The metallic sound of a rifle bolt being checked echoed through the room.

Not aimed.

Not loaded for firing.

Just enough to make a point.

The researcher immediately corrected himself.

> "...Endmin."

Endmin smiled normally again.

> **"...Thank you."**

Director Lucious later clarified that no threat had actually been made.

The researcher simply decided that continuing the joke was not in his best interests.
