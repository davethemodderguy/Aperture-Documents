# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-020**

### **INCIDENT REPORT — HARASSMENT OF Z-321 PROTECTIVE MASK**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – PERSONNEL CONDUCT / MEDICAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

On multiple occasions throughout the day, several personnel repeatedly poked, tapped, or flicked Z-321 ("Endmin")'s protective mask as a joke while passing through the Blacksite.

Although each individual incident appeared minor, the repeated impacts accumulated over several hours.

By early afternoon, Endmin exhibited severe disorientation and was escorted to Medical Division.

The personnel involved have since been identified and disciplinary action has been initiated.

---

## **BACKGROUND**

Endmin's perception does **not** rely on conventional vision.

Instead, she constructs her surroundings through extremely delicate Ether resonance, airborne vibrations, and echoes.

Her protective mask shields her damaged eyes from unnecessary mechanical disturbance.

Previous medical reports have already documented that unexpected contact with the mask causes discomfort and can trigger trauma-related responses.

This incident demonstrates that repeated disturbances can also produce significant physical symptoms.

---

## **INCIDENT TIMELINE**

Security footage documented numerous interactions throughout the morning.

Examples included:

- Lightly tapping the front of the mask.
    
- Flicking the side "to get her attention."
    
- Briefly poking the glowing edges.
    
- Jokingly asking, "Can you feel this?"
    

Each time, Endmin politely responded.

> **Endmin:** "...Please don't."

She continued walking.

Several individuals laughed.

A few apologized and stopped immediately.

Others unfortunately repeated the behavior later.

---

## **MEDICAL EFFECTS**

By approximately 13:20 hours, Endmin began displaying noticeable symptoms.

Observed effects included:

- Difficulty maintaining balance.
    
- Delayed responses while walking.
    
- Headaches.
    
- Nausea.
    
- Temporary reduction in spatial awareness.
    
- Visible fatigue.
    

Medical staff compared the condition to severe motion sickness combined with sensory overload.

When seated in Medical, Endmin quietly admitted,

> "...Everything feels noisy."

---

## **ROOM N-07 RESPONSE**

Mart arrived first after hearing Endmin had been taken to Medical.

He found her sitting quietly with both hands covering her mask.

Without saying anything, he simply sat beside her.

A few minutes later, Taiga arrived carrying water and snacks.

She frowned.

> **Taiga:** "...Again?"

Endmin gave a small nod.

Astral later joined them, ensuring the room remained quiet while Endmin recovered.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL NOTE**

Although Endmin remained calm throughout the incident, Psychological Services noted signs that the repeated touching had resurfaced memories of previous bullying experienced in the Banlands.

During a private interview she quietly explained,

> "...Back then..."

> "...People thought it was funny too."

She looked down.

> "...Eventually..."

> "...I stopped asking them to stop."

Psychological Services emphasized that repeated unwanted physical contact, even when intended as humor, can reinforce prior traumatic experiences.

---

## **DIRECTOR'S STATEMENT**

Following review of the incident, Director Lucious issued the following site-wide reminder.

> "A person's protective equipment is not a toy."

> "If someone asks you not to touch something, you don't touch it."

> "This is no different than grabbing someone's medical equipment without permission."

The statement was distributed to all departments.

---

## **UPDATED PERSONNEL GUIDELINES**

Effective immediately:

- Personnel shall not intentionally touch, tap, poke, flick, or otherwise interfere with Endmin's protective mask without her explicit permission.
    
- Repeated violations may result in disciplinary action.
    
- Staff are encouraged to gain Endmin's attention verbally rather than through physical contact.
    
- Training on respectful interaction with residents possessing medical or anomalous assistive equipment will be incorporated into new employee orientation.
    

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Z-321 – Endmin**

- Physical condition: Recovered after rest.
    
- Temporary sensory overload: Resolved.
    
- Psychological status: Stable.
    
- Medical recommendation: Avoid unnecessary contact with the protective mask.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

The following morning, Endmin walked through the same corridor where the incident had occurred.

Several personnel greeted her.

Instead of reaching toward her mask, they simply waved.

> **"Morning, Endmin."**

She smiled beneath the mask and waved back.

> **"...Morning."**

Medical Division later noted that the simplest accommodation had also proven to be the most effective:

**Respecting the boundary she had asked for from the beginning.**
