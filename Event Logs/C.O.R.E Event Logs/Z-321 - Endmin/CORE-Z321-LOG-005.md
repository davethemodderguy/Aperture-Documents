# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-005**

### **ABILITY DEMONSTRATION — Z-321 "ENDMIN"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – N.O.S.T. / RESEARCH

---

## **SUMMARY**

At the request of Research Division and Director Lucious, Z-321 ("Endmin") voluntarily conducted a controlled demonstration of the abilities she had acquired since arriving at the Blacksite.

The demonstration took place inside the N.O.S.T. Training Hall under supervised conditions.

All original ability owners were present.

---

## **OPENING**

Endmin stood in the center of the training hall.

She stretched her arms.

> **Endmin:** "Okay!"

> "...Don't panic if I accidentally use the wrong one."

Director Lucious looked up from his clipboard.

> **Director Lucious:** "That sentence doesn't inspire confidence."

Taiga giggled.

Mart quietly took one step farther back.

---

## **DEMONSTRATION 01 — TAIGA**

Endmin crouched.

She floated exactly **5 centimeters** above the floor.

Without taking a single step...

She silently glided across the room.

Taiga immediately pointed.

> **Taiga:** "See?!"

> "...Gliding!"

> "...Not flying!"

Mart sighed.

> "...We're not starting this again."

Endmin then performed several controlled air jumps before landing smoothly.

Research confirmed that her movements closely matched Taiga's unique style.

---

## **DEMONSTRATION 02 — MART**

Endmin smiled.

Brilliant white wings unfolded from her back.

Unlike Mart's careful, graceful takeoff...

Endmin immediately overestimated her lift.

She shot straight toward the ceiling.

> **Endmin:** "...Uh oh."

She narrowly avoided a light fixture before stabilizing herself.

Mart looked embarrassed.

> "...That's normal."

> "...The first time."

After regaining control, Endmin demonstrated precise hovering, rapid directional changes, and controlled landings.

---

## **DEMONSTRATION 03 — STAR**

A soft radiance surrounded Endmin.

The room brightened noticeably.

Researchers recorded that she reproduced Star's characteristic aura with remarkable fidelity.

One researcher quietly remarked,

> "...It's comforting."

Star smiled proudly.

> "...Told you."

---

## **DEMONSTRATION 04 — FROST**

The room's temperature gently dropped.

Small crystalline ice flowers formed harmlessly across the testing platform.

Frost inspected them.

> "...Perfect."

Then quietly added,

> "...Please don't freeze the cafeteria."

Endmin saluted.

> "...Noted."

---

## **DEMONSTRATION 05 — NEKO**

With a brief flash of white plasma-like light, Endmin produced an energy blade resembling those used by Neko.

She examined it curiously.

Swung it once.

The blade cleanly sliced a reinforced steel testing block.

Silence filled the hall.

Neko calmly wrote something in his notebook.

> "...Sharp enough."

---

## **DEMONSTRATION 06 — ASTRAL**

Golden-white wings slowly manifested behind Endmin.

Unlike Mart's wings, these appeared broader and carried an unmistakable sense of calm.

Astral watched quietly.

She smiled.

> "...Beautiful."

Endmin smiled back.

---

## **CHAIN DEMONSTRATION**

Research requested a combined demonstration.

Endmin nodded.

Over the next several minutes she seamlessly transitioned between abilities.

She glided across the floor using Taiga's technique.

Air-jumped into the air.

Spread Mart's wings to gain altitude.

Shifted into a graceful glide.

Created Frost's ice beneath herself for landing.

Illuminated the room with Star's radiance.

Then safely dismissed every manifestation.

No instability was observed.

---

## **RESEARCH FINDINGS**

Researchers concluded that Endmin does not merely imitate abilities.

She appears to understand how they function at a fundamental level after acquiring them.

However, each replicated ability still reflects Endmin's own personality and mannerisms rather than becoming an exact copy of its original user.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Taiga Sunbringer

> "...She copied my gliding!"

A pause.

> "...Still not flying."

Mart looked upward.

> "...You literally left the ground."

Taiga folded her arms.

> "...Gliding."

The debate resumed immediately.

---

### Mart Sunbringer

Mart admitted he was impressed by how quickly Endmin adapted to using his wings.

> "...She learned faster than I expected."

Endmin grinned.

> "...I had a good teacher."

Mart blushed slightly.

---

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral noted that every ability still "felt" different when Endmin used it.

> "...She isn't becoming someone else."

> "...She's making every ability her own."

---

### Neko

After reviewing the data, Neko simply wrote:

> **"Recommendation: Do not underestimate Z-321."**

No further explanation was provided.

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious closed the final report and looked at Endmin.

> "...Anything else?"

Endmin thought for a moment.

Then smiled.

> "...Probably."

Lucious sighed.

> "...I was afraid you'd say that."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Z-321 – Endmin**

- Ability replication: Stable.
    
- Ability integration: Exceptional.
    
- Safety compliance: Excellent.

- Research priority: High.
    

Research Division continues documenting newly acquired abilities as Endmin spends time with Blacksite residents.

---

### **Closing Observation**

As the demonstration concluded, everyone applauded.

Endmin gave a small bow.

Then paused.

> **Endmin:** "...Wait."

Everyone looked at her.

She frowned thoughtfully.

> "...I forgot which abilities are mine..."

The room fell silent.

Director Lucious slowly rubbed the bridge of his nose.

> "...We're going to need..."

He looked at the rapidly growing stack of reports.

> "...A much larger filing cabinet."
