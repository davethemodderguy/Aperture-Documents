# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-034**

### **PERSONNEL INTERVIEW — GETTING TO KNOW Z-321**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENT PROFILE UPDATE

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following several months of residency at the Blacksite, Z-321 ("Endmin") voluntarily participated in an informal interview hosted by Human Resources and Psychological Services.

The purpose was not to investigate anomalous abilities, but simply to help personnel become more familiar with one of the Blacksite's newest residents.

While Endmin answered many personal questions, she continued to keep details regarding her eyes private.

---

## **INTERVIEW EXCERPTS**

### **Favorite Color**

> **Endmin:** "White."

She smiled.

> "...Now I actually know what white looks like."

---

### **Favorite Place**

> **Endmin:** "The greenhouse."

When asked why:

> "...It's quiet."

> "...And flowers are prettier than I imagined."

---

### **Favorite Activity**

> **Endmin:** "Target practice."

A brief pause.

> "...And gliding."

Taiga immediately celebrated from across the room.

> **Taiga:** "SEE?!"

---

### **Favorite Food**

> **Endmin:** "Sweet things."

> "...Especially candy."

Neko quietly joked,

> "...As long as nobody mistakes it for another prototype."

The room laughed.

---

### **Favorite Weapon**

Endmin looked toward the rifle resting beside her chair.

> "...My rifle."

She then glanced at the Nekonium baseball bat leaning against the wall.

> "...And my bat."

---

### **Biggest Wish**

This time, there was no hesitation.

> "...To keep seeing colors."

The room became noticeably quieter.

---

## **THE EYES**

Eventually, someone asked the question everyone expected.

> **Researcher:** "...What are your eyes really like?"

Endmin's smile remained.

She gently touched the edge of her protective face covering.

> "...They're mine."

A short pause.

> "...I'd like to keep them private."

No one asked again.

---

## **THE PROTECTIVE FACE COVERING**

One researcher then realized something unusual.

Nobody actually knew what to call the device.

Suggestions quickly appeared.

- Protective Mask
    
- Faceplate
    
- Eye Shield
    
- Eye Cover
    
- Protective Visor
    

Engineering reviewed the design.

Medical reviewed the design.

Security reviewed the design.

Each department reached a different conclusion.

Final report:

**Official Classification: Undetermined.**

The equipment protects only Endmin's eyes while leaving the remainder of her face unobstructed, making it difficult to place into any existing equipment category.

Until further notice, internal documentation simply continues referring to it as:

**"Endmin's Mask."**

---

## **KNOWN INFORMATION**

Although the exact appearance of Endmin's eyes remains undisclosed to the vast majority of personnel, the following information has been publicly confirmed with her permission:

- They suffered severe Ether Corruption damage in the Banlands.
    
- Direct exposure to normal light can worsen the condition.
    
- They perceive the world primarily through Ether and microscopic vibrations.
    
- Aperture's Ether research later allowed her to perceive colors once again.
    
- She removes the mask only under exceptional circumstances.
    

Everything beyond those facts remains personal.

---

## **WHO KNOWS?**

At the time of this report, only three individuals at the Blacksite have seen Endmin's uncovered eyes.

- Lucy.
    
- Taiga.
    
- Mart.
    

Neither Taiga nor Mart have ever described them in detail.

When asked why, Mart quietly replied,

> "...Because she trusted us."

Taiga nodded.

> "...That's enough."

Psychological Services commended both siblings for consistently respecting Endmin's privacy.

### **Closing Observation**

Near sunset, Endmin sat with Taiga and Mart in the greenhouse.

The conversation drifted from flowers...

To books...

To gliding...

To colors.

Not once did either sibling ask about the eyes hidden beneath her protective face covering.

Endmin noticed.

She smiled quietly beneath it.

For someone who had spent years afraid of being judged for what was under the mask...

Being accepted without anyone needing to look meant far more than revealing the secret itself.
