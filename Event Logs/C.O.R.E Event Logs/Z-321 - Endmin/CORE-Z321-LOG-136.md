# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-136**

### **INCIDENT REPORT – UNINTENTIONAL FALL TEST: BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

An accidental fall from one of Heaven's Blacksite's exterior observation balconies resulted in an unexpected demonstration of Belle Merryweather's remarkable ability to absorb impact.

No injuries were sustained.

The balcony, however, briefly became the busiest place in the Blacksite.

---

## **INCIDENT**

**Location:** Exterior Observation Balcony – Residential Sector

**Estimated Fall Height:** 125 meters

Personnel were conversing on the balcony when Belle stepped backward while turning to answer a question.

Her heel caught the edge.

> **"...Eh?"

Before anyone could react...

Belle tipped backwards over the railing.

Silence.

Then—

> **"BELLE!"**

Endmin was already sprinting toward the edge.

Taiga nearly vaulted the railing herself.

Mart immediately took flight.

Star vanished in a flash of light.

Lucius reached the balcony just in time to watch Belle disappear from view.

---

## **Ground Level**

Several maintenance staff looked up after hearing distant shouting.

A pink figure rapidly descended from the sky.

**BOOMF.**

Belle struck the ground.

The surrounding dust puffed outward.

She compressed slightly against the pavement...

Like an oversized plush toy.

Then...

She bounced.

Once.

Twice.

Three smaller hops.

Before gently settling upright.

Belle blinked.

Looked around.

Brushed a bit of dust off her dress.

> **"...I'm okay?"

---

## **Response**

Seconds later...

Mart landed beside her.

Endmin arrived immediately afterward, having raced through the stairwells.

Star teleported in while still looking panicked.

Taiga wasn't far behind.

Lucius reached the scene moments later.

Endmin immediately grabbed Belle by the shoulders.

> **"...Are you hurt?"

Belle flexed her arms.

Wiggled her rabbit ears.

Stomped each foot once.

> **"...Nope."

She tilted her head.

> **"...Why's everyone looking at me like that?"

Endmin didn't answer.

She simply pulled Belle into a tight hug.

---

## **Medical Examination**

Medical personnel insisted on performing a complete evaluation.

Findings:

- No fractures.
    
- No bruising.
    
- No internal injuries.
    
- No concussion.
    
- No joint damage.
    
- No muscular strain.
    

The examining physician looked at the report.

Then at Belle.

Then quietly wrote:

> **"Patient appears medically... normal."**

Another physician added:

> **"Recommend inspecting the pavement instead."**

The pavement required minor repairs.

Belle did not.

---

## **Engineering Note**

Engineering concluded that Belle's Rabbit Thiren physiology, unusually soft body, and exceptional ability to dissipate kinetic energy allow her to absorb impacts that would normally cause catastrophic injury.

This trait does **not** make her invulnerable to all forms of harm, but it provides extraordinary resistance to blunt-force impacts and falls.

One engineer summarized the report simply:

> **"She's basically equipped with built-in shock absorbers."**

Then at the maintenance crew patching the cracked concrete.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Belle returned to normal duties approximately fifteen minutes after the incident.

No restrictions were issued.

The observation balcony has since had additional safety reminders installed—not because Belle couldn't survive another fall, but because everyone else would panic long before she reached the ground.

Personnel continue to maintain that while Belle may be known as the **"Sentient Bunny Plushie with a Big Sword,"** they would prefer future demonstrations of her impact resistance to occur under considerably less alarming circumstances.
