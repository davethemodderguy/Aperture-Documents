# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-114**

### **PERSONNEL PROFILE ADDENDUM – LUCIUS MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Residential

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following his arrival at APRT-70, personnel gradually discovered that Lucius Merryweather possesses a talent that very few expected.

He is an accomplished artist.

Lucius rarely speaks about it and almost never paints or sketches in front of other people.

Instead, he prefers working alone in quiet places.

---

## **Preferred Environment**

Despite having spent years surviving outdoors, Lucius has developed an unusual preference for painting during cold weather.

Whenever the Blacksite overlooks a snowy landscape or during expeditions to regions with harsh winters, he can occasionally be found sitting alone outside.

Wrapped in a heavy coat, with a sketchbook balanced on his knees, he quietly paints the scenery around him.

He has remarked that the silence of snowfall helps him concentrate.

---

## **Artistic Style**

Lucius works primarily with:

- Pencil sketches.
    
- Charcoal drawings.
    
- Watercolor landscapes.
    
- Oil paintings.
    

His subjects are most commonly:

- Snow-covered forests.
    
- Mountains.
    
- Quiet villages.
    
- Wildlife.
    
- Family portraits recreated from memory.
    

He rarely signs his work.

---

## **A Familiar Scene**

One afternoon, Endmin quietly left the Blacksite to look for her brother.

She eventually found him sitting on a rocky overlook beyond the facility.

Snow drifted gently around him.

His paintbrush moved slowly across the canvas.

Endmin didn't interrupt.

She simply sat beside him.

Several minutes passed before Lucius finally spoke.

> **"...The snow's peaceful."**

Endmin nodded.

> **"...Yeah."**

The two siblings remained there together, listening to the wind.

Neither felt the need to say anything else.
