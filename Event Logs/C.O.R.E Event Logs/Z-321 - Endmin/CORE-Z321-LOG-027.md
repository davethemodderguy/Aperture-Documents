# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-027**

### **BIOLOGICAL OBSERVATION — Z-321 WINGS & BANLANDS CLARIFICATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – ANOMALOUS BIOLOGY / CULTURAL REFERENCE

---

## **SUMMARY**

During a discussion comparing the various forms of flight possessed by Blacksite residents, Z-321 ("Endmin") revealed that she also possesses wings.

Contrary to initial assumptions, the wings **do not enable flight**.

The session also prompted Lucy to clarify a common misconception regarding the Banlands.

> **"The Banlands are not Hell."**

> **"They're a prison."**

> **"A place where dangerous souls are contained—not where evil comes from."**

---

## **THE REVELATION**

The discussion began innocently enough.

Taiga was practicing gliding.

Mart demonstrated his wings of pure white light.

Star and Nightshade floated effortlessly through the air despite their wings being largely ornamental.

Bao swam gracefully through the sky using water manipulation.

Frost watched from below.

Endmin quietly observed everyone.

Taiga suddenly looked toward her.

> **Taiga:** "...Wait."

> "...Do _you_ have wings?"

Endmin hesitated.

Then nodded.

> "...Yes."

---

## **THE WINGS**

With everyone's attention on her, Endmin stepped into an open area of the courtyard.

She took a slow breath.

Darkness gathered behind her.

Not shadow.

Not smoke.

Something deeper.

Two enormous wings unfolded from her back.

They were significantly larger than anyone expected.

Each feather appeared to be woven from a dense, velvety darkness that absorbed rather than reflected light. Their edges shimmered faintly with traces of stabilized Ether, giving them a subtle outline against the surroundings.

Despite their appearance, they inspired an unexpected sense of calm rather than fear.

Mart stared upward.

> **Mart:** "...They're huge..."

Taiga's ears stood upright.

> **Taiga:** "...They're beautiful."

Endmin gave a shy smile.

---

## **NOT FOR FLIGHT**

Taiga pointed upward.

> **Taiga:** "...Can you fly?"

Endmin gently folded one wing around herself.

> "...No."

Everyone blinked.

> **Neko:** "...Those things can't fly?"

Endmin shook her head.

> "...They're too heavy."

She lightly tapped one of the feathers.

> "...They're made for protection."

---

## **DEFENSIVE FUNCTION**

To demonstrate, Endmin wrapped both wings completely around herself.

The dark feathers overlapped into a seamless barrier.

From the outside, almost none of her body remained visible.

Neko gently tapped one feather with a metal tool.

The impact was absorbed with little visible movement.

Lucy explained,

> **Lucy:** "Banlands Guardians often use their wings as shields."

> "Against fragments."

> "Against Ether storms."

> "Against debris."

> "Against each other."

Endmin added,

> "...Flying was never their purpose."

---

## **HIDDEN IN PLAIN SIGHT**

Mart looked at Endmin's uniform.

> **Mart:** "...Where do they go?"

Endmin folded the wings.

The darkness gradually settled against her back until it blended perfectly into the black sections of her clothing.

Within seconds, they were almost impossible to notice.

Only someone actively looking for them could tell they were there.

Taiga walked around her twice.

> "...That's cheating."

Endmin laughed softly.

> "...It's convenient."

---

## **LUCY'S CLARIFICATION**

During the discussion, a junior researcher casually referred to the Banlands as "Hell."

Lucy immediately corrected him.

> **Lucy:** "...No."

The room fell silent.

She continued calmly.

> **Lucy:** "The Banlands are not Hell."

A map of interdimensional territories appeared on the display.

> **Lucy:** "They're a prison."

> "A place where dangerous, corrupted, or condemned souls are confined."

She folded her arms.

> "Some deserve to be there."

> "Some were victims."

> "Some can still be redeemed."

Endmin quietly nodded.

> "...Not everyone there is evil."

Lucy smiled sadly.

> "...Exactly."

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Taiga

Taiga poked one of the folded wings.

> "...They're really soft."

Endmin nodded.

> "...Please don't pull on them."

> **Taiga:** "...Deal."

---

### Mart

Mart compared one of his glowing owl feathers with Endmin's dark feathers.

The contrast was striking.

Light and darkness.

Different in appearance.

Similar in purpose.

> **Mart:** "...They both protect people."

Endmin smiled.

> "...I like that."

---

### Star

Star carefully walked around Endmin with obvious fascination.

> **Star:** "...You have night wings."

Endmin laughed.

> "...Night wings?"

Star nodded enthusiastically.

> "...Mine are light."

She pointed toward Endmin.

> "...Yours are night."

The nickname spread through Room N-07 almost immediately.

Endmin didn't seem to mind.

---

**Z-321 – Endmin**

- Primary purpose: Defensive shielding and protection.
    
- Concealment: Wings fold and blend seamlessly into clothing.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

Later that evening, while walking back to Room N-07, a cold wind swept across the courtyard.

Without thinking, Endmin quietly unfolded one wing and held it over Mart and Taiga like a canopy.

The dark feathers shielded them from the wind until they reached the building.

Taiga smiled.

> **Taiga:** "...You really do use them as a shield."

Endmin folded the wing neatly against her back.

> "...That's what they were made for."

Mart looked up at the now-hidden wings.

He smiled quietly.

> "...They suit you."
