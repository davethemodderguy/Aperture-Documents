# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-101**

### **PERSONNEL PROFILE – BELLE'S DUAL DEMEANOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Personnel / Behavioral Sciences

---

## **SUMMARY**

Personnel unfamiliar with Belle often make the same first impression.

She appears to be:

- A small pink Rabbit Thiren.
    
- Exceptionally cheerful.
    
- Constantly smiling.
    
- Hugging everyone she meets.
    
- Running through the Blacksite with an oversized sword balanced over her head.
    

This first impression is accurate.

It is also incomplete.

---

## **Public Appearance**

Belle has earned several affectionate nicknames throughout APRT-70, including:

- **"The Sentient Bunny Plushie."**
    
- **"The Sentient Bunny Plushie with a Big Sword."**
    

She is frequently seen:

- Laughing with the Friendly Roommates.
    
- Accepting surprise hugs.
    
- Helping researchers carry boxes.
    
- Falling over while sprinting, then immediately bouncing back to her feet.
    
- Running so quickly that her legs become a blur.
    

To many new employees, she appears harmless.

---

## **Professional Authority**

Belle serves as the **Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority**, the Banlands' highest internal affairs and anti-corruption organization.

Her office investigates:

- Corruption.
    
- Bribery.
    
- Abuse of authority.
    
- Government misconduct.
    
- Violations committed by public officials.
    

When legally authorized by the Banlands judicial system, the Krampus Compliance Authority may also carry out capital sentences.

Belle possesses that authority.

She has exercised it.

She never celebrates doing so.

---

## **Behavioral Shift**

Behavioral Sciences has documented a dramatic change in Belle's demeanor when transitioning from daily life to official duty.

The change is immediate.

Her smile fades.

Her posture straightens.

Her voice becomes calm, measured, and unwavering.

She does not raise her voice.

She does not threaten.

She simply performs her duty.

Personnel who have witnessed both sides of Belle often describe the experience as unsettling—not because she becomes cruel, but because the warmth she normally radiates is replaced by complete professional focus.

---

## **Psychological Assessment**

The apparent contrast in Belle's personality is not considered contradictory.

Rather, it reflects a strong separation between her personal life and her professional responsibilities.

Outside of work, Belle chooses kindness whenever possible.

During official operations, she prioritizes justice, due process, and the responsibilities of her office.

Behavioral Sciences concludes that these qualities reinforce one another rather than conflict.
