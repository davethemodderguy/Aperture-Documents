# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-115**

### **RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION – A NAME, A TITLE, AND A Missing "O"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential

---

## **SUMMARY**

A casual moment in the residential wing resulted in two amusing discoveries.

The first was Lucius finally seeing Endmin's official Aperture Industries identification.

The second was Director Lucious realizing that he and Lucius have nearly identical names.

---

## **The ID Card**

Endmin was carrying a stack of maintenance reports through the hallway when her access card slipped from her pocket.

It landed face-up.

Lucius bent down and picked it up before she noticed.

He glanced at it.

> **Lindervene "Endmin" Merryweather**  
> **Director of the Central Execution Department**  
> **Security Clearance: Level 6**

Lucius stared for several seconds.

Then looked at his sister.

Then back at the card.

> **"...Level 6?"**

Endmin scratched the back of her head awkwardly.

> **"...Yeah..."**

> **"...That's me."**

Lucius handed the card back.

> **"...I knew you had an important job..."**

> **"...I didn't realize you were _that_ important."**

Endmin quickly clipped the card back onto her belt.

Her ears twitched with embarrassment.

> **"...Can we not make a big deal out of it?"**

---

## **Director Lucious Arrives**

Director Lucious happened to be passing through the corridor just in time to hear the conversation.

Lucius introduced himself politely.

> **"I'm Lucius Merryweather."**

Director Lucious paused.

He blinked once.

> **"...Lucius?"**

Lucius nodded.

> **"Yes, sir."**

The Director thought for a moment.

Then smiled.

> **"...Interesting."**

Everyone looked at him.

He chuckled.

> **"...I just realized something."**

He pointed between himself and Lucius.

> **"Your name is _Lucius_..."**

Then pointed to himself.

> **"...Mine is _Lucious_."**

He emphasized the extra syllable.

> **"...There's an 'o' in mine."**

The hallway fell silent.

Endmin looked between the two of them.

Belle tilted her head.

Taiga counted the letters on her fingers.

Mart blinked.

Star was the first to laugh.

> **"...I've never noticed that!"**

Within moments, everyone else was laughing as well.

Even Director Lucious couldn't help smiling.

> **"...We've worked together for years..."**

> **"...And it took meeting you for me to notice the spelling."**

Lucius laughed.

> **"...I suppose we're almost namesakes."**

> **"...Almost."**

---

## **Personnel Comments**

> **"...Great."

> **"...Now everyone's going to compare your name to the Director's."

---

### Belle

> **"...One protects the Banlands."

She pointed at Lucius.

> **"...The other runs the Blacksite."

She pointed at Director Lucious.

> **"...Easy!"

---

### Taiga

> **"...I honestly thought they were spelled the same."

---

### Director Lucious

With a smile, he concluded the conversation.

> **"Well..."

> **"At least now I know exactly when someone forgets the 'o'."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Endmin has since become more careful about securing her Level 6 access card.

Lucius remains quietly impressed by how much responsibility his younger sister carries.

As for Director Lucious, personnel have jokingly begun referring to the situation as:

> **"The Case of the Missing 'O'."**

The Director has taken the joke in good humor—provided everyone continues spelling his name correctly.
