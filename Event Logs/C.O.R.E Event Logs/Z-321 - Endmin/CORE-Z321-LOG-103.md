# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-103**

### **RESIDENTIAL DISCLOSURE – BELLE'S "CLASSIFIED" TRANSPORT SECRET**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Humor Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

While chatting with the Friendly Roommates, Alice unintentionally revealed a long-kept piece of Banlands history involving Belle's early career.

Belle was present for the entire conversation.

She attempted to stop Alice.

She was too late.

---

## **Conversation**

The discussion began innocently enough.

Taiga looked toward Belle.

> **"...You've always been this short?"

Belle confidently nodded.

> **"...Yep!"

Alice smiled, remembering something.

> **"...Actually..."

Belle immediately looked at Alice.

> **"...Alice."

Alice continued.

> **"...When she first joined the Krampus Compliance Authority..."

Belle's rabbit ears stood straight up.

> **"...Alice."

Alice looked completely oblivious.

> **"...She was only about 125 centimeters tall."

The room became quiet.

Belle slowly covered her face.

---

## **The Secret**

Alice couldn't help laughing.

> **"...Whenever official convoys transported her..."

> **"...They had to install a child booster seat."

Silence.

Then—

Star burst into laughter.

Taiga nearly fell off her chair.

Mart covered her beak, trying not to laugh.

Niko turned away, shoulders shaking.

Even Nightshade smiled.

Neko quietly muttered,

> **"...That's... actually adorable."

Miss Neko simply nodded.

> **"...Very."

Belle remained frozen.

Her face was completely red.

---

## **Belle's Defense**

After several seconds, Belle finally managed to speak.

> **"...I..."

> **"...Couldn't reach the seat."

Taiga immediately replied.

> **"...I know."

Belle pointed dramatically.

> **"...It was a government-issued booster seat!"

Mart nodded.

> **"...Which somehow makes it funnier."

The room dissolved into laughter once again.

---

## **Alice's Explanation**

Trying to help, Alice explained,

> **"It wasn't meant to embarrass her."

> **"It was simply the safest way for her to travel."

She smiled warmly at Belle.

> **"...Besides..."

> **"...She looked very proud sitting in it."

Belle buried her face in Endmin's shoulder.

> **"...Alice..."

> **"...Please stop helping..."

---

## **Endmin's Response**

Endmin gently patted Belle's head.

Trying very hard not to laugh herself.

> **"...You were tiny."

Belle groaned.

> **"...I know..."

Endmin smiled.

> **"...Still are."

Belle looked up dramatically.

> **"...Traitor."

---

## **Personnel Comments**

### Taiga

> **"...Government-issued booster seat."

> **"...I'm never forgetting that."

---

### Star

> **"...Tiny Director!"

Nightshade quietly whispered,

> **"...Don't push your luck."

---

### Neko

> **"...It was practical."

After a pause,

> **"...Still funny."

---

### Alice

> **"...I'm... probably not supposed to have shared that."

Belle immediately answered,

> **"...Definitely not."

---

### Lucy**

After hearing about the incident through Alice's report, Lucy laughed for nearly a full minute before regaining her composure.

She simply said,

> **"...She really was that small."

---

### Director Lucious

After reviewing the report, Director Lucious added a brief note.

> **"Appropriate vehicle restraints are an important safety measure regardless of rank."**

A second sentence appeared underneath.

> **"Belle has requested that this report never be mentioned in front of visiting dignitaries."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Belle has formally—but unsuccessfully—requested that everyone forget the existence of the government-issued booster seat.

The request has been acknowledged.

It has also been completely ignored by the Friendly Roommates.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Several days later, Belle walked into the shared office she occupied with Endmin.

Waiting on her chair...

...was a perfectly clean booster cushion.

Attached to it was a handwritten note.

> **"For official business. — Taiga"**

Belle stared at it.

Then slowly turned toward the room.

Every single person was trying very hard not to laugh.

Belle sighed dramatically.

> **"...I'm transferring all of you to paperwork."

No one believed the threat.

Not even Belle.

A few moments later...

She was laughing too.
