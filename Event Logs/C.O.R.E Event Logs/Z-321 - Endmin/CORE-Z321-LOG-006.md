# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-006**

### **INCIDENT REPORT — UNAUTHORIZED CONTACT WITH Z-321'S PROTECTIVE MASK**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – PERSONNEL MISCONDUCT / RESIDENT WELFARE

---

## **SUMMARY**

During an afternoon recreation period, Z-321 ("Endmin") was subjected to repeated unwanted physical contact by several junior researchers while other personnel were occupied elsewhere.

The researchers repeatedly tapped and poked Endmin's protective mask despite multiple verbal requests from her to stop.

The incident resulted in severe physical disorientation, emotional distress, and the resurfacing of previously documented childhood trauma.

---

## **INCIDENT LOG**

While most Room N-07 occupants were engaged elsewhere in the recreation hall, Endmin remained in the common area speaking with several junior researchers.

One researcher curiously tapped the side of her mask.

> **Endmin:** "...Um... please don't."

The researcher laughed.

> "Sorry."

A few moments later...

Another tap.

> **Endmin:** "...Please stop."

Another researcher joined in.

The tapping became more frequent.

Each time Endmin politely asked them to stop.

> "...Please..."

> "...It hurts."

> "...Really..."

> "...Please stop..."

Her voice remained gentle.

Nervous.

Almost apologetic.

Rather than recognizing her discomfort, several researchers mistakenly interpreted her soft-spoken protests as playful reluctance and continued.

Repeated impacts transmitted vibrations through the mask into the already damaged structures surrounding her eyes.

Within moments, Endmin staggered backward.

She lost her balance.

Her breathing became uneven.

Her hands instinctively covered her mask.

She was no longer responding to anyone around her.

Instead, she whispered almost inaudibly,

> "...Stop..."

> "...Please..."

> "...I didn't do anything..."

She turned and ran.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL RESPONSE**

Security footage later showed Endmin hiding alone in a quiet maintenance corridor.

She had curled into a corner, both hands firmly holding her mask.

She continued trembling even after the physical pain had subsided.

When Astral eventually found her, Endmin quietly explained between tears:

> "...They used to do that too..."

Astral knelt beside her.

> "...Who?"

Endmin hesitated.

> "...The other kids..."

A long silence followed.

> "...They kept touching it..."

> "...They laughed..."

> "...They said I looked strange..."

She lowered her head.

> "...I thought..."

> "...It was happening again."

Astral gently hugged her.

No further questions were asked.

---

## **MEDICAL FINDINGS**

Medical Division confirmed that the mask itself remained undamaged.

However, repeated impacts caused painful vibration around Endmin's injured eyes, resulting in:

- Severe disorientation.
    
- Temporary balance impairment.
    
- Heightened sensitivity.
    
- Acute stress response.
    

No permanent physical deterioration was observed.

Psychological effects were considered significantly more serious.

---

## **DISCIPLINARY ACTION**

Following review of security footage, Director Lucious issued immediate disciplinary measures.

The involved junior researchers were removed from resident interaction duties pending retraining.

Mandatory instruction on resident consent, disability awareness, and professional conduct was ordered before any future assignment involving N.O.S.T. occupants.

Director Lucious addressed the group directly.

> "When someone says 'stop,' you stop."

> "It doesn't matter how quietly they say it."

> "You do not decide whether another person's pain is real."

No appeals were accepted.

---

## **ROOM N-07 RESPONSE**

Later that evening, Endmin returned to Room N-07.

She remained noticeably quieter than usual.

Mart sat beside her without speaking.

Astral prepared warm tea.

Taiga quietly looked at Endmin's mask for several seconds.

Then smiled.

Without touching the mask itself, she held up a small sticky note.

Written on it was:

> **:3**

> **Taiga:** "...Can I put this on?"

Endmin blinked.

Then laughed for the first time that day.

> "...Okay."

Taiga carefully placed the sticky note on the front of the mask.

Endmin looked toward everyone.

> "...How do I look?"

Mart smiled.

> "...Cute."

Astral nodded.

> "...Very."

Endmin kept the sticky note where it was.

It didn't interfere with how she perceived the world.

Over the next few days, she continued wearing it around Room N-07.

The sight of the little **":3"** gradually became a familiar one.

Whenever she caught Taiga looking at it and smiling...

Endmin smiled too.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Z-321 – Endmin**

- Physical condition: Stable.
    
- Eye condition: Unchanged.
    
- Psychological condition: Recovering.
    
- Comfortable with trusted roommates: Yes.
    
- Comfortable with unfamiliar personnel: Temporarily reduced.
    

Room N-07 residents have requested that Endmin be allowed to approach new people at her own pace.

Psychological Services fully support this recommendation.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Several days later, a newly assigned researcher met Endmin in the hallway.

They noticed the small **":3"** sticky note on her mask.

Instead of commenting on the mask itself, they smiled and asked,

> "Cute note. Did someone make it for you?"

Endmin reached up and lightly touched the corner of the sticky note.

She smiled.

> "...My big sister did."

Then she continued down the hallway, the little **":3"** still proudly attached to her mask.
