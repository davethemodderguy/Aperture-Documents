# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-078**

### **ABILITY OBSERVATION – THE ANGEL OF DELUSION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Ether Phenomena / Personnel

---

## **SUMMARY**

During an off-duty afternoon in the residential wing, Z-321 (Endmin) was observed wearing an outfit rarely seen by other residents.

She wore:

- A white braided hairstyle resembling a princess's crown braid.
    
- A knee-length skirt.
    
- Protective shorts beneath the skirt, consistent with her preference for practicality due to skating.
    

The appearance prompted several compliments from residents.

Later investigation revealed that much of what had been observed was not entirely real.

---

## **Observation**

Taiga was the first to notice.

> **Taiga:**
> 
> "...You braided your hair?"

Endmin looked up from the book she was reading.

> "...Yeah."

Mart smiled.

> "...It looks nice."

Even Star quietly admired it.

> "...Very elegant."

Endmin simply thanked everyone and continued reading.

Nothing appeared unusual.

---

## **The Reveal**

Several hours later, Endmin entered Engineering to assist Neko.

While adjusting an Ether calibration device, she casually dismissed a layer of Ether surrounding herself.

The long braid dissolved into drifting blue particles before vanishing.

The elegant skirt shimmered briefly before reverting to her usual attire.

Neko blinked.

> "...You were practicing."

Endmin nodded.

> "...Illusions."

She twirled one finger.

Thin strands of Ether spiraled around her hand.

> "...They're easier to maintain if I wear them for a long time."

---

## **The Angel of Delusion**

For many years in the Banlands, criminals referred to Endmin by a single title:

> **"The Angel of Delusion."**

While many assumed the title referred only to her ability to create convincing visual illusions, Lucy clarified that the nickname came from Endmin's relentless dedication to mastering Ether manipulation.

According to Lucy:

> "She practiced constantly."

> "Changing little details."

> "Hair."

> "Clothing."

> "Her silhouette."

> "Even how shadows fell around her."

> "Not because she wanted to deceive people..."

> "...but because perfect control only comes through practice."

Endmin quietly added,

> "...If I can maintain harmless illusions all day..."

> "...I'll be ready when I actually need them."

---

## **Medical and Behavioral Assessment**

Behavioral Sciences emphasizes that Endmin's everyday illusion practice is non-hostile.

The projected clothing and hairstyles are cosmetic Ether constructs with no physical substance.

They are used solely to refine precision, concentration, and Ether efficiency.

Maintaining multiple small illusions for extended periods is considered significantly more difficult than creating a single dramatic illusion for a short time.

Endmin continues to use harmless Ether illusions during her free time as a form of daily training.

Residents are aware that small cosmetic details—such as hairstyles, accessories, or clothing appearance—may occasionally be part of her practice.

No security concerns have been associated with this habit.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Later that evening, Endmin stood before the mirror in Room N-07.

With a quiet flick of her fingers, her braid unraveled into glowing blue Ether before reforming into a different style.

Then another.

Then another.

Aster, watching from the bed, tilted her head.

She held up her whiteboard.

> **"Pretty."**

Endmin smiled.

Another shimmer of Ether passed over her.

The princess braid returned.

> **"...Thanks."**

She glanced at her reflection one last time.

Not because she wanted to fool anyone.

Simply because every perfect illusion was another step toward mastering the power that had earned her the title:

**The Angel of Delusion.**
