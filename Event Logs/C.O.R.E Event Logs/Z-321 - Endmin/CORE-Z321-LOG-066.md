# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-066**

### **EQUIPMENT OBSERVATION – ENDMIN'S MASK**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Equipment / Residential

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following Endmin's full recovery and restoration of her eyesight, her signature eye mask is no longer worn continuously.

Instead, she typically carries it clipped to her belt or stored in one of her jacket pockets as a sentimental item.

During an informal gathering in Room N-07, Z-885 (Taiga) asked if she could try it on.

Endmin agreed.

---

## **Observation**

Taiga carefully accepted the mask with both hands.

Everyone watched with curiosity.

For years, the mask had been associated with Endmin's unique vision and Ether sensitivity.

Many wondered if it possessed anomalous properties.

Taiga placed it over her eyes.

She stood still for several seconds.

Then...

> **Taiga:**
> 
> "...It's dark."

Silence.

She slowly lifted the mask.

> "...That's it."

---

## **Engineering Examination**

With Endmin's permission, Engineering Division performed a non-invasive inspection of the mask.

Results confirmed:

- Constructed from the same mysterious alloy found on Endmin's original rifle.
    
- Extremely durable.
    
- Precisely machined.
    
- Contains no electronics.
    
- Contains no Ether amplification systems.
    
- Contains no optical components.
    
- Contains no hidden mechanisms.
    

Internally, it is simply a smooth metal eye covering designed to protect the wearer's eyes.

The anomalous abilities previously associated with the mask originated entirely from **Endmin herself**, not from the equipment.

---

## **Endmin's Comment**

After Taiga removed the mask, she handed it back.

> **Taiga:**
> 
> "...I thought it'd do something."

Endmin smiled as she fastened it back onto her belt.

> **"...It did."**

Taiga tilted her head.

> **"...It protected my eyes."

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Endmin's mask remains her personal property.

Although she rarely needs to wear it anymore, she continues carrying it as a keepsake from a chapter of her life she refuses to forget.

Engineering has concluded that the mask itself possesses **no anomalous properties** beyond its exceptional durability.

---

### **Closing Observation**

As everyone prepared to leave Room N-07, Taiga glanced back.

> **Taiga:**
> 
> "...You know..."

> "...It still looks cool."

Endmin laughed softly.

She clipped the mask back onto her belt.

> **"...Maybe that's why I still keep it."

Taiga grinned.

> "...Or maybe you're just used to it."

Endmin thought for a moment.

Then nodded.

> **"...Yeah."

> "...Probably both."**
