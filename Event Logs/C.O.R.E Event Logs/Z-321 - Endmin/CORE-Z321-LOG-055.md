# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-055**

### **FIELD TEST – MODULAR UNDERBARREL SHOTGUN SYSTEM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Engineering / Weapons Evaluation

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the completion of post-installation safety inspections, Z-321 (Endmin) conducted the first live-fire evaluation of her newly modified sniper rifle at the Blacksite's outdoor firing range.

The demonstration revealed the purpose of the oversized replacement handguard installed the previous day.

It was not merely a handguard.

It was a fully integrated **underbarrel shotgun system**.

---

## **Pre-Test Observation**

Endmin arrived carrying her rifle in its usual case.

As she placed it onto the shooting bench, Director Lucious happened to walk past.

Something immediately caught his attention.

Beneath the rifle barrel sat a second barrel.

Running beneath it...

...was a familiar magazine tube.

Lucious quietly keyed his radio.

> **Director Lucious:** "Armory, inventory check."

Several seconds later, Josh replied.

> **Josh (Comms):** "One Benelli M1014 reported removed from storage yesterday."

Lucious looked back toward Endmin.

Then smiled.

> **Director Lucious:** "...I had a feeling."

He released the transmit button.

> "...Carry on."

No further questions were asked.

---

## **Marksmanship Demonstration**

Before testing the new system, Endmin noticed a hostile surveillance drone approaching the Blacksite perimeter.

She calmly shouldered the rifle.

A single **7.62×51 mm** round rang out.

The drone lost power instantly and fell from the sky.

A call came over perimeter communications.

> **Perimeter Security:** "Uh... sir?"

> "...Was that Endmin again?"

Director Lucious looked toward the smoking remains of the drone.

> **Director Lucious:** "Yes."

> "You're cleared."

> "Continue patrol."

The guard answered almost immediately.

> **"Copy that."**

---

## **Secondary Weapon Test**

Endmin worked the bolt, chambering another rifle cartridge.

She then reached for the rifle's selector.

Instead of selecting a different firing mode...

She flipped the newly repurposed switch.

A solid metallic **clack** echoed across the range.

The underbarrel action cycled.

A shotgun shell fed smoothly into battery.

Endmin raised the rifle toward a ballistic target.

**BOOM.**

The target lurched backward under the impact.

She lowered the rifle.

The transition between sniper rifle and shotgun had taken less than two seconds.

---

## **Engineering Notes**

The integrated system retains the rifle's original functionality while adding a manually operated underbarrel shotgun.

Observed features include:

- Independent barrel assembly.
    
- Tubular magazine beneath the rifle barrel.
    
- Separate action linked to the repurposed selector mechanism.
    
- No interference with the rifle's optic or primary bolt operation.
    
- Bayonet removed to accommodate the new assembly, now carried separately as a combat knife.
    

Engineering praised the clean integration, noting that the additional system appeared almost factory-built.

Future evaluations will focus on durability and long-term maintenance.
