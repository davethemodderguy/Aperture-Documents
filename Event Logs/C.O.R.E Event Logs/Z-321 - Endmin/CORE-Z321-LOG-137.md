# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-137**

### **MEDICAL DISCUSSION RECORD – "WHAT EXACTLY IS BELLE?"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Medical & Research Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following numerous medical examinations—and particularly after Belle's documented 125-meter fall with no resulting injuries—the Medical Division has been unable to reach a unanimous conclusion regarding Belle Merryweather's physiology.

While Belle is officially classified as a **Rabbit Thiren**, several physicians and researchers argue that her physical characteristics differ significantly from every other documented Thiren.

The debate has become one of the most active discussions within Aperture Industries' Medical Division.

---

## **Current Theories**

### **Theory A – Rabbit Thiren**

The conservative position.

Supporters argue Belle is simply an exceptionally unusual Rabbit Thiren.

Evidence includes:

- Rabbit ears.
    
- Rabbit nose.
    
- Rabbit hind feet.
    
- Enhanced running speed.
    
- Exceptional jumping ability.
    
- Typical Thiren anatomy in most respects.
    

Conclusion:

> **"An unusually gifted Rabbit Thiren."**

---

### **Theory B – Plushie Physiology**

Supporters of this theory point toward Belle's unusual physical properties.

Observed characteristics include:

- Extremely soft body.
    
- Fur that resembles plush fabric.
    
- Exceptional impact absorption.
    
- Noticeable squeaking or plush-like sounds when hugged or compressed.
    
- Ability to rebound from severe falls without injury.
    

One physician summarized the theory as:

> **"...She behaves less like soft tissue and more like an incredibly resilient cushioning material."**

---

### **Theory C – Specialized Rabbit Thiren**

This group believes Belle represents a previously undocumented subtype of Rabbit Thiren.

Rather than being biologically impossible...

They argue Belle simply possesses an extremely rare physiological adaptation.

Proposed traits include:

- Extraordinary kinetic energy absorption.
    
- Dense yet flexible musculature.
    
- Exceptional load-bearing capability despite her small frame.
    
- Highly elastic connective tissues.
    

Current evidence remains insufficient to confirm this hypothesis.

---

### **Theory D – "Just Belle"**

A small but growing number of staff have stopped trying to classify her altogether.

Their reasoning:

> **"...Whatever she is..."

> **"...She's Belle."**

Medical officially declined to accept this as scientific terminology.

---

## **Case Study**

To illustrate the difficulty of classification, physicians reviewed several documented events.

- Survived a 125-meter fall without injury.
    
- Carries a 50-kilogram sword comfortably.
    
- Broke a researcher's ribs and pelvis with a single punch.
    
- Produces plush-like squeaks when hugged.
    
- Possesses remarkably soft fur and skin.
    
- Demonstrates extraordinary resistance to blunt impacts.
    

One physician closed the file before quietly asking:

> **"...How does any of this fit together?"**

No one answered.

The debate is expected to continue.
