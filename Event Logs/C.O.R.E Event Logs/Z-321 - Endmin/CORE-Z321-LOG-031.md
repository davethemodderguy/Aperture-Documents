# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-031**

### **RECREATIONAL INCIDENT — HIDE-AND-SEEK IS NO LONGER FAIR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RECREATIONAL OBSERVATION

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the successful integration of Z-321's Nekonium Nanoparticle Wing Armor, Room N-07 organized an informal game of hide-and-seek.

The event unintentionally revealed an undocumented property of the wing armor.

When Endmin completely folds her wings around herself, the adaptive nanoparticle lattice and forcefield generate an optical cloaking effect.

Any individual fully enclosed behind the wings is concealed as well.

This effect was discovered entirely by accident.

The game ended shortly afterward.

---

## **THE GAME**

Participants included:

- Endmin
    
- Taiga
    
- Mart
    
- Star
    
- Nightshade
    
- Frost
    
- Leo (who was apparently participating despite nobody inviting him)
    

Mart volunteered to count.

> **Mart:** "...Ready or not..."

> "...Here I come."

Everyone scattered.

---

## **THE DISCOVERY**

Taiga found Endmin standing quietly behind a support column.

> **Taiga:** "Can I hide with you?"

Endmin nodded.

> "...Sure."

She unfolded her enormous dark wings.

Taiga stepped behind them.

The wings closed.

At that exact moment...

The adaptive Nekonium nanoparticles synchronized with the protective forcefield.

Instead of simply covering the pair...

They vanished.

Not physically.

Visually.

To an outside observer, nothing remained except the ordinary corridor behind them.

---

## **THE SEARCH**

Mart searched for nearly twenty minutes.

He checked:

- Room N-07.
    
- The greenhouse.
    
- Storage.
    
- The library.
    
- The workshop.
    
- The courtyard.
    

Nothing.

Star floated through the hallways.

Nothing.

Nightshade checked the roof.

Nothing.

Even Security cameras failed to clearly identify their position.

The cloaking field distorted visual observation just enough to blend seamlessly into the environment.

---

## **THE REVEAL**

Eventually, Mart sighed.

> **Mart:** "...I give up."

Immediately, Endmin unfolded her wings.

Taiga stumbled out laughing.

Mart stared.

Then looked at the exact location where he'd walked past several times.

> **Mart:** "...No."

Endmin tilted her head.

> "...No?"

> **Mart:** "...That's cheating."

---

## **ENGINEERING ANALYSIS**

Neko was called to investigate.

After several controlled tests, Engineering confirmed the phenomenon.

When fully folded:

- The nanoparticle armor aligns with the forcefield.
    
- Ambient light is redirected around the wings.
    
- Minor visual distortions are compensated automatically.
    
- Individuals completely enclosed within the wings are concealed alongside Endmin.
    

The effect is not true invisibility.

Specialized sensors, thermal imaging, and certain anomalous detection systems can still locate her.

To the unaided eye, however, the concealment is remarkably effective.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Wing Cloaking Capability**

- Activation: Automatic when wings are fully folded.
    
- Coverage: Endmin and anyone completely enclosed behind the wings.
    
- Detection: Visible to specialized sensors; effectively concealed from normal visual observation.
    
- Intended feature: No.
    
- Engineering assessment: Unexpected but stable interaction between the Nekonium nanoparticle armor and defensive forcefield.
