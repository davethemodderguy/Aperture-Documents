# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-131**

### **DIPLOMATIC VISIT – THE MERRYWEATHER FAMILY DELEGATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Intergovernmental Relations

---

## **SUMMARY**

A formal delegation from the **Merryweather Family** visited Aperture Industries' Heaven's Blacksite to discuss expanding cooperation between the Banlands and Aperture Industries.

The visit focused on strengthening diplomatic ties, sharing engineering expertise, and exploring joint research into defensive equipment and specialized weapon design.

The delegation was received by Director Lucious and senior Blacksite personnel.

---

## **Attending Delegation**

**Banlands Representatives**

- Lucy Merryweather – Warden of the Banlands
    
- John Merryweather – Central Banlands Computing Department, Chief Engineer
    
- Clover Merryweather – Founder of the Krampus Compliance Authority (Retired)
    
- Dave Merryweather – Former Head of the Central Execution Department (Retired)
    
- Belle Merryweather – Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority
    
- Endmin (Lindervene) Merryweather – Head of the Central Execution Department
    
- Lucius Merryweather
    

**Aperture Representatives**

- Director Lucious
    
- Miss Neko
    
- Neko
    
- Senior Engineering Division personnel
    
- Research and Development representatives
    

---

## **Reception**

The delegation arrived through a stabilized Banlands transit rift before being escorted through the administrative sector.

Many personnel paused their work as the Merryweather family walked through the halls.

Director Lucious greeted the delegation personally.

> **"Welcome back to Heaven's Blacksite."**

Lucy smiled.

> **"Thank you for having us again."**

The two leaders shook hands before proceeding to the conference chamber.

---

## **Agenda**

The meeting covered several long-term cooperation initiatives.

### **Engineering Collaboration**

John Merryweather presented several Banlands engineering concepts involving durable alloys and Ether-compatible mechanisms.

Neko responded by showcasing Aperture's manufacturing capabilities, including precision machining techniques and modular weapon components.

Both engineers quickly found themselves discussing design schematics across an entire whiteboard.

Belle quietly leaned toward Endmin.

> **"...We've lost them."

Endmin looked at the rapidly filling diagrams.

> **"...Definitely."**

---

### **Weapon Development**

The meeting also explored future collaboration in designing equipment intended for:

- Government security forces.
    
- Specialized anomaly-response teams.
    
- Ether-resistant protective equipment.
    
- Heavy-duty field tools.
    
- Non-standard defensive systems.
    

Prototype concepts would remain jointly reviewed by both organizations before production.

---

### **Alliance**

Lucy addressed the room.

> **"The Banlands has benefited greatly from Aperture's friendship."**

She looked toward Director Lucious.

> **"We believe that continuing this partnership will strengthen both our people."**

Director Lucious nodded.

> **"Shared knowledge strengthens everyone involved."**

---

## **Room N-07**

After the formal meeting ended, the atmosphere became much more relaxed.

Clover found Belle enthusiastically explaining recent improvements within the Krampus Compliance Authority.

Dave spoke with Endmin about leadership and the responsibilities of directing the Central Execution Department.

John wandered into Engineering with Neko and promptly disappeared into another discussion involving blueprints and alloy compositions.

Lucy eventually found Lucius sitting near a window, sketching part of the Blacksite.

She smiled.

> **"...Already drawing?"**

Lucius nodded.

> **"...It's a nice place."**

---

### John Merryweather

Examining one of Neko's recent designs, John smiled.

> **"...You engineers are dangerous."

Neko raised an eyebrow.

> **"...How so?"

John laughed.

> **"...Give us enough coffee and we'll redesign half the world."**

---

### Director Lucious

At the conclusion of the visit, Director Lucious addressed both delegations.

> **"Alliances are not built solely through treaties."**

> **"They are built through trust, cooperation, and the willingness to solve difficult problems together."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

The visit concluded successfully.

Both organizations agreed to continue technical exchanges, engineering cooperation, and joint development of specialized equipment for authorized operational use.

The delegation departed through the return rift later that evening.

Before leaving, Belle waved enthusiastically toward the Blacksite staff.

> **"See you next time!"**

Several personnel waved back.

By the time the rift closed, many were already looking forward to the Merryweather family's next visit.
