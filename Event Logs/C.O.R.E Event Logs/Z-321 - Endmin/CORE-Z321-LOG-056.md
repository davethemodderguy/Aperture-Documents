# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-056**

### **MEDICAL OBSERVATION – TEMPORARY ILLNESS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Medical / Residential

---

## **SUMMARY**

At approximately **09:20**, Room N-07 residents reported that Z-321 (Endmin) had remained in her room throughout the morning and had declined invitations to breakfast, engineering, and recreational activities.

Medical Division later confirmed she was suffering from a common seasonal illness.

No anomalous cause was detected.

---

## **Observation**

Unlike her usual routine of skating through the Blacksite shortly after waking, Endmin remained in bed.

Her Mobility Boots sat neatly beside the door.

Her rifle rested on its rack.

The room was unusually quiet.

Medical staff recorded:

- Mild fever.
    
- Fatigue.
    
- Reduced appetite.
    
- General weakness.
    

She was advised to remain in bed, stay hydrated, and rest for the day.

---

## **Visitors**

Although Endmin wished to rest, Room N-07's occupants quietly checked on her throughout the day.

Each visit lasted only a few minutes.

No one wanted to wake her if she had finally managed to fall asleep.

---

### **Taiga**

Taiga gently opened the door.

Seeing Endmin asleep beneath several blankets, she quietly placed a bottle of water and a handwritten note on the bedside table.

The note simply read:

> **"Get well soon. We'll race again when you're better. — Taiga"**

She quietly left without saying a word.

---

### **Mart**

Mart entered later that afternoon carrying a fresh book.

Seeing Endmin still sleeping, he placed it beside the note.

A bookmark had already been inserted into the first chapter.

He quietly whispered,

> "...Take your time."

Then closed the door.

---

### **Neko**

Neko briefly inspected Endmin's room heater to make sure it was working properly.

Satisfied, he adjusted the temperature slightly before leaving.

Before closing the door, he quietly said,

> "Engineering can wait."

---

### **Miss Neko**

Miss Neko prepared a warm meal and left it covered on the desk in case Endmin woke up hungry.

---

### **Aster**

Aster required no invitation.

She simply climbed onto Endmin's bed.

Curled into a small ball beside her pillow.

And stayed there.

Whenever Endmin stirred awake, she would find Aster quietly purring beside her.

Whenever she fell asleep again...

Aster remained.

---

## **Medical Report**

Doctors confirmed that Endmin's condition was expected to improve with ordinary rest.

No Ether instability, anomalous infection, or Banlands-related complications were detected.

Her recent recovery from Ether Corruption remained stable.

---

## **Personnel Comments**

### Endmin

Speaking softly after waking briefly.

> "...I hate being sick."

---

### Taiga

> "The fastest person in the Blacksite..."

> "...Defeated by a fever."

---

### Mart

> "...Even heroes need rest."

---

### Lucy

Unable to visit that day due to responsibilities in the Banlands, Lucy contacted Endmin over a secure communicator.

> **Lucy:** "How are you feeling?"

Endmin smiled weakly.

> "...Sleepy."

Lucy laughed softly.

> "Then listen to your doctor."

> "Go back to sleep."

---

### Director Lucious

After receiving the medical update, he approved one day of leave from all duties.

His only written instruction read:

> **"No engineering. No training. Rest."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Endmin remains under routine observation.

She has been temporarily excused from engineering work, marksmanship practice, and field activities until Medical Division confirms a full recovery.

Aster has, unofficially, appointed herself as Endmin's bedside companion.

No one has attempted to move her.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Outside Room N-07, the familiar sounds of laughter, conversation, and distant target practice echoed through the corridor.

Inside...

The room was still.

Endmin slept peacefully beneath a warm blanket.

Curled beside her pillow, Aster quietly purred.

For once, the fastest skater in the Blacksite wasn't racing through the halls.

She was exactly where everyone wanted her to be.

Resting.
