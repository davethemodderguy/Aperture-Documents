# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-090**

### **RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION – BELLE'S FIRST NIGHTMARE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Behavioral Sciences

---

## **SUMMARY**

Several weeks after becoming a permanent resident of Room N-07, Belle was observed integrating exceptionally well with the Friendly Roommates.

Despite her cheerful demeanor during the day, one overnight incident revealed that memories of her childhood continue to affect her during sleep.

The event also demonstrated the strong support network that now surrounds her.

---

## **Daytime Activities**

Belle spent the afternoon with the Friendly Roommates.

Among the day's activities:

* Racing Taiga through the residential hallways.
* Helping Neko organize Engineering supplies.
* Watching Mart practice aerial maneuvers.
* Receiving hugs from Bao.
* Playing with Star and Nightshade.
* Following Aster around until the cat finally wrote on her whiteboard:

> **"You are following me."**

Belle gasped dramatically.

> **"...I have been discovered."

Even Aster gave a small, amused smile.

By the evening, Room N-07 was filled with relaxed conversation.

For the first time in years, Belle remarked that she had almost forgotten what loneliness felt like.

---

## **Overnight Incident**

At approximately **02:18**, residential microphones detected distressed vocalizations coming from Belle during sleep.

Medical intervention was not immediately required.

Belle repeatedly muttered fragmented phrases.

> "...I'm trying..."

> "...I can do better..."

> "...Please don't..."

Her heart rate increased significantly.

Behavioral Sciences later concluded she was experiencing a nightmare related to her childhood.

---

## **Endmin's Response**

Sleeping beside Belle, Endmin awoke almost immediately.

Without startling her, Endmin gently placed a hand over Belle's.

She quietly spoke.

> **"...Belle."

> "...You're safe."

Belle continued trembling.

Endmin carefully wrapped her in a reassuring hug.

> **"...You're home."

> "...I'm here."

After several moments, Belle slowly began calming down.

Her breathing returned to normal.

Still asleep, she instinctively held onto Endmin's sleeve.

The nightmare gradually faded.

---

## **The Following Morning**

Belle awoke unusually quiet.

She remembered fragments of the nightmare.

Looking down, she noticed she was still holding onto Endmin's sleeve.

She immediately let go.

> **"...Sorry..."

Endmin smiled gently.

> **"...You don't have to apologize."

Belle hesitated.

> **"...I dreamed they found me again."

Endmin shook her head.

> **"...They didn't."

She looked around the room.

Taiga was asleep in her bed.

Mart was perched comfortably nearby.

Neko and Miss Neko were just beginning to wake up.

Star was curled up with Leo.

Nightshade hadn't moved.

Aster had quietly climbed onto Belle's blanket sometime during the night.

Endmin smiled.

> **"...Look around."

> "...You're with family now."

Belle looked at everyone.

After a long silence...

She smiled through tears.

> **"...Yeah..."

> "...I am."

---

## **Personnel Comments**

### Taiga

> "Nobody should have to carry those memories alone."

---

### Neko

> "If she ever wakes up from another nightmare..."

> "...One of us will be there."

---

### Bao

Belle received an especially warm hug that morning.

Bao simply said,

> **"Bad dreams end."**

> **"Good people stay."**

---

### Lucy

After hearing about the incident, Lucy visited Room N-07 later that day.

She gently stroked Belle's ears.

> **"You survived your past."**

> **"It doesn't get to decide your future."**

Belle quietly nodded.

---

### Director Lucious

Reviewing the overnight report, Director Lucious concluded:

> **"Recovery isn't measured by never remembering the past."**

> **"It's measured by knowing you no longer face it alone."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Behavioral Sciences considers occasional trauma-related nightmares an understandable response to Belle's history.

No abnormalities were observed beyond temporary emotional distress.

Belle continues to show healthy adjustment to life at APRT-70, supported by Endmin, Lucy, and the Friendly Roommates.

---

### **Closing Observation**

That evening, Belle climbed into bed beside Endmin.

She hesitated for a moment.

> **"...Can I... stay close tonight?"

Endmin answered without hesitation.

> **"...Of course."

Belle relaxed almost immediately, resting against Endmin.

Across the room, Taiga quietly pulled the blanket a little higher over Belle before returning to her own bed.

No one made a joke.

No one teased her.

The room simply settled into silence.

Outside, winter winds brushed against the windows of the Blacksite.

Inside Room N-07, Belle slept peacefully.

This time, when she dreamed...

She wasn't running from her past.

She was surrounded by the people who had become her family.
