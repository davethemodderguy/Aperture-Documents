# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-071**

### **PERSONAL PROFILE – MUSIC & THE MERRYWEATHER NAME**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Personnel / Cultural Record

---

## **SUMMARY**

An informal cultural exchange between Banlands personnel and Blacksite residents revealed two previously undocumented facts regarding Z-321 (Endmin):

- Endmin possesses an exceptionally well-trained singing voice.
    
- Very little is publicly known about the origin of the **Merryweather** family name, despite it belonging to both Endmin and her mother, Lucy.
    

---

## **Musical Ability**

Contrary to her normally reserved personality, Endmin is an accomplished vocalist.

Lucy confirmed that, during Endmin's childhood, music was one of the few peaceful hobbies she consistently enjoyed.

Endmin herself rarely volunteers to sing in front of large groups.

She strongly prefers singing alone.

According to Lucy:

> "She always sounded the best when she thought nobody was listening."

---

## **The Banlands**

While growing up in the Banlands, Endmin's identity as **Lindervene Merryweather**, daughter of the Warden, was known to many.

Because of this, she developed a habit of quietly participating in local performances under anonymous or unofficial names.

Lucy laughed while recalling one particular incident.

> "People argued for weeks over who the singer was."

> "Meanwhile..."

> "...she was already home reading a book."

Endmin covered her face in embarrassment.

> "...Mom..."

---

## **An Evening at the Blacksite**

One winter evening, Taiga happened to wake up for a glass of water.

As she passed the greenhouse, she heard someone singing.

The voice was gentle.

Clear.

Beautiful enough to echo softly through the glass.

Curious, she peeked inside.

Endmin stood alone among the flowers, believing everyone else was asleep.

She sang quietly to herself.

Not performing.

Simply enjoying the moment.

Taiga smiled.

Without interrupting, she quietly returned to Room N-07.

The following morning, she only said one thing.

> **Taiga:**

> "...You have a beautiful voice."

Endmin nearly dropped her breakfast.

> "...You heard that?"

Taiga nodded.

Endmin buried her face in her hands.

> "...I thought everyone was asleep..."

---

## **The Merryweather Name**

Archive Division also inquired about the origin of the **Merryweather** family name.

Lucy answered with a small smile.

> "...That's an old story."

When asked if she would elaborate, she simply shook her head.

> "Another time."

Historical Banlands records provide almost no information regarding the family's ancestry before Lucy's generation.

Whether "Merryweather" is an inherited surname, an adopted title, or something with historical significance remains unknown.

Even Endmin admitted:

> "...Mom never really talks about it."

As of this report, the origin of the Merryweather name remains classified or otherwise undocumented.

### **Closing Observation**

Late that night, the corridors of the Blacksite were quiet once again.

Somewhere near the greenhouse, a familiar melody drifted softly through the air.

No audience.

No applause.

Just Endmin singing to herself beneath the winter stars.

Unbeknownst to her, several roommates passing by paused for only a moment.

They smiled at one another.

Then silently continued walking.

Some moments, they agreed, were too precious to interrupt.
