# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-127**

### **EQUIPMENT DEMONSTRATION – "BLISS OF WHITEFALL"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 6 – Restricted Equipment Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

At the request of Belle, Endmin, and Lucius, Miss Neko conducted a controlled demonstration of her signature weapon, **Bliss of Whitefall**, at Aperture Industries' extreme-range testing grounds.

The demonstration was performed under strict safety protocols in an uninhabited test area.

---

## **Initial Observation**

The weapon rested inside a reinforced transport case.

It was long.

Elegant.

Painted in a clean white finish.

To anyone unfamiliar with it...

It looked like an unusually large sniper rifle.

Lucius walked around it once.

> **"...That's... a rifle?"

Miss Neko smiled politely.

> **"...It resembles one."**

Belle looked at the barrel.

Then at the receiver.

Then back at the barrel.

> **"...It's enormous..."

Endmin folded her arms.

> **"...I've heard stories."

---

## **Miss Neko's Explanation**

Miss Neko rested a hand on the weapon.

> **"Despite its appearance, calling this a sniper rifle would be inaccurate."**

She paused.

> **"...Its operating principle and payload place it much closer to artillery than a conventional firearm."**

Lucius blinked.

> **"...Artillery?"

Miss Neko nodded.

> **"A very large piece of artillery."**

Belle slowly looked back at the weapon.

> **"...Someone put a howitzer in a rifle stock..."

Miss Neko gave a small smile.

> **"...Essentially."**

---

## **Demonstration**

The target was a distant, uninhabited rock formation within the approved test range.

Personnel cleared the area.

Miss Neko settled behind the weapon.

She breathed once.

The range became silent.

**BOOM.**

The report echoed across the valley.

Several seconds later...

The distant rock formation disappeared beneath an immense cloud of dust and debris.

When the dust finally settled...

A significant portion of the formation was simply gone.

Lucius stared through the observation binoculars.

He lowered them slowly.

> **"...There..."

He looked again.

> **"...There used to be a mountain there."

Miss Neko calmly opened the action and began the post-firing inspection.

> **"Correct."**

---

## **Personnel Reactions**

### Belle

Belle looked at the weapon.

Then at Miss Neko.

Then back toward the distant impact site.

After several seconds, she quietly said:

> **"...I'm never calling that a gun."

---

### Endmin

Endmin let out a slow whistle.

> **"...I thought people were exaggerating."

She shook her head.

> **"...They weren't."**

---

### Lucius

Still staring into the distance, Lucius finally managed to speak.

> **"...That's not a sniper rifle."

He looked at Miss Neko.

> **"...That's a siege weapon pretending to be one."**

Miss Neko nodded politely.

> **"...A reasonable description."**

---

### Neko

Having helped maintain parts of the system, Neko folded his arms.

> **"...Engineering classified it as a strategic weapons platform."

He looked toward Lucius.

> **"...Calling it 'just a gun' would be underselling it."**

---

## **Director Lucious' Note**

After reviewing the demonstration footage, Director Lucious added the following remark:

> **"Bliss of Whitefall exists for situations in which ordinary solutions are no longer sufficient."**

A second note was appended beneath it.

> **"Its use requires the highest level of authorization and is restricted to approved operational scenarios."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Bliss of Whitefall** remains one of Aperture Industries' most tightly controlled weapons systems.

Despite its outward resemblance to an oversized precision rifle, personnel are reminded that it is a strategic heavy weapon designed for exceptional circumstances.

As the group departed the test range, Lucius glanced back one last time at the distant gap where the rock formation had stood.

He quietly summed up everyone's thoughts.

> **"...Who looked at a howitzer..."

> **"...and decided it needed a stock and a scope?"**

For the first time that afternoon, even Miss Neko laughed.
