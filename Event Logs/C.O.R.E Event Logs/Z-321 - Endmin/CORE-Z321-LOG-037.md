# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-037**

### **ETHER TEAR ANALYSIS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Medical / Biological Research

---

## **SUMMARY**

During a routine medical examination, Z-321 (Endmin) became emotional and began crying.

Medical personnel observed faint traces of **Ether radiation** emanating from the tears before they were collected by her protective mask's internal reservoir.

The incident prompted an immediate investigation.

The findings were unexpectedly reassuring.

---

## **Incident**

While discussing her eyesight and recent progress in adapting to seeing colors, Endmin quietly began crying.

Small sensors around the examination room registered an extremely weak burst of Ether radiation.

The room's containment systems activated automatically.

The tears were safely collected inside Endmin's mask before they could reach the floor.

No injuries or contamination occurred.

---

## **Research Findings**

Analysis revealed that the Ether radiation was **not** produced by the tears themselves.

Instead, the tears carried microscopic amounts of stabilized Ether Corruption that naturally existed within Endmin's body.

Researchers compared it to the human body's handling of minerals and salts.

Endmin's body continuously suppresses harmful Ether Corruption.

Water plays a critical role in maintaining that balance.

---

## **Medical Conclusion**

Researchers concluded that Endmin's hydration level directly affects how effectively her body suppresses Ether Corruption.

Proper hydration results in:

- Stable Ether levels.
    
- Reduced biological stress.
    
- Lower radiation leakage.
    
- Improved physical comfort.
    

Severe dehydration may result in:

- Increased Ether instability.
    
- Minor Ether leakage through tears.
    
- Fatigue.
    
- Headaches.
    
- Increased sensitivity around her damaged eyes.
    

The radiation levels observed remained extremely low and were easily contained by standard Blacksite safety systems.

---

## **Containment Update**

Medical Division issued a simple recommendation rather than a restriction.

### Z-321 Hydration Guidelines

- Maintain regular water intake.
    
- Increase hydration following strenuous activity or prolonged flight.
    
- Additional monitoring during illness.
    
- No special containment required.
    

Endmin happily accepted the recommendation.

Her only question was:

> "...Does tea count?"

Medical staff unanimously answered:

> "Yes."

Endmin visibly relaxed.
