# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-099**

### **BEHAVIORAL INCIDENT – IMPROPER USE OF BELLE'S FORMER NAME**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Personnel Welfare

---

## **SUMMARY**

During routine operations within APRT-70, a newly transferred contractor unintentionally addressed Belle using her former childhood name after reading an outdated Banlands personnel archive.

The incident demonstrated the severity of Belle's emotional response to hearing the name associated with her abusive childhood.

No malicious intent was identified.

---

## **Incident**

At approximately **14:27**, Belle was assisting Residential Services with moving supplies.

A contractor, unfamiliar with current personnel guidance, glanced at an archived document before looking toward her.

He smiled politely.

> **"...Excuse me, Zhao?"

The corridor became silent.

---

## **Immediate Response**

Belle stopped walking.

The smile she had been wearing moments earlier disappeared completely.

Her rabbit ears slowly lowered.

The supplies she had been carrying slipped slightly in her hands.

She didn't answer.

For several seconds...

She simply stood there.

Her breathing became noticeably uneven.

Without looking at anyone, she quietly placed the box on the floor.

Then turned around.

And ran.

Not at her usual cheerful sprint.

Just... away.

---

## **Room N-07**

Endmin found Belle sitting on the floor beside her bed.

Her knees were pulled against her chest.

Her long braid had partially come loose.

Belle was trying very hard not to cry.

Endmin quietly sat beside her.

Neither of them spoke for a while.

Finally, Endmin broke the silence.

> **"...Hey."

Belle kept looking at the floor.

> **"...He didn't mean it..."

Belle nodded.

> **"...I know..."

Her voice trembled.

> **"...But hearing it..."

She covered her face.

> **"...It feels like I never escaped."

Endmin gently wrapped an arm around her shoulders.

> **"...Look at me."

Belle slowly did.

> **"...Who are you?"

Belle hesitated.

Then answered quietly.

> **"...Belle."

Endmin smiled.

> **"...That's right."

> **"...You're Belle."

---

## **Follow-Up**

The contractor was immediately informed of Belle's preferred name and the reason behind the guidance.

He requested permission to apologize personally.

Belle accepted.

When they met later that day, he bowed respectfully.

> **"...I'm sorry, Belle."

> **"...I read an old record without realizing..."

> **"...I didn't mean to hurt you."

Belle remained quiet for a moment.

Then gave a small smile.

> **"...Thank you..."

> **"...For calling me Belle."

The matter was considered resolved.

No disciplinary action was taken, as the incident resulted from unfamiliarity with updated records rather than intentional misconduct.

### Director Lucious

After reviewing the report, Director Lucious authorized an administrative update requiring archived records containing Belle's former identity to display a prominent notice reminding readers to address her as **Belle** in all present-day interactions.

He added a handwritten note.

> **"Names can carry history."**

> **"The one a person chooses deserves our respect."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Belle recovered emotionally after spending the evening with the Friendly Roommates.

Behavioral Sciences recommends continued use of her chosen name in all daily interactions and reminds personnel that respect for a resident's identity is an important part of maintaining a supportive living environment.

---

### **Closing Observation**

That evening, Room N-07 was unusually quiet.

Belle sat on the sofa with a blanket wrapped around her shoulders.

No one brought up the afternoon.

Instead, Taiga offered her a cup of hot chocolate.

Mart sat nearby reading.

Neko and Miss Neko continued working on a project.

Aster climbed into Belle's lap without a sound.

Endmin eventually sat beside her.

> **"...Feeling better, Belle?"

Belle looked up.

Hearing her chosen name, her ears slowly lifted again.

A small smile returned.

> **"...Yeah."

She leaned gently against Endmin's shoulder.

> **"...Thanks... for reminding me who I am."**
