## **FIELD TEST #1**

Belle noticed the carrot.

She tilted her head.

> **"...Is that..."

She followed it.

Across the hallway.

Through two doors.

Into Room N-07.

> **"...Wait..."

> **"...I got tricked."

## **FIELD TEST #2**

Star attempted the same experiment.

> **"...You're enjoying this."

Belle followed anyway.

## **FIELD TEST #4**

Cinder improved the prototype by adding a second carrot.

Halfway down the corridor she stopped.

> **"...There's two carrots."

Belle smiled.

> **"...You're definitely trying to bribe me."

She accepted both carrots.

Then followed anyway.
