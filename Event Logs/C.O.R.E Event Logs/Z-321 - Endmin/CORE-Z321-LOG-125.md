# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-125**

### **THIREN OBSERVATION – BELLE'S "PLUSHIE" SOUNDS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Behavioral Observation

---

## **SUMMARY**

Personnel have repeatedly reported an unusual but harmless characteristic of Belle Merryweather.

Due to her exceptionally soft Rabbit Thiren physiology, movements that compress her fur or clothing often produce faint squeaking or plush-like sounds.

The phenomenon is entirely natural and has become one of Belle's more recognizable traits around Room N-07.

---

## **Observed Behavior**

The sounds are most commonly heard when Belle:

- Walks quickly across smooth floors.
    
- Is hugged tightly.
    
- Is gently squished by Star during enthusiastic hugs.
    
- Flops onto a bed or sofa after work.
    

The sounds are soft, high-pitched, and resemble the gentle squeak of a plush toy being compressed.

Medical examinations have found no abnormalities.

---

## **Room N-07 Observation**

One afternoon, Belle had just returned from a long day with the Krampus Compliance Authority.

As she walked through the room...

**Squeak.**

**Squeak.**

Taiga looked over.

> **"...There it is again."

Belle froze.

> **"...What?"

Another step.

**Squeak.**

Belle sighed.

> **"...Not again..."

---

## **Star's Discovery**

Star, being Star, became immediately curious.

She walked over.

> **"...Can I hug you?"

Belle smiled.

> **"...Of course!"

Star wrapped Belle in a warm hug.

**Squeeeeak.**

Star's eyes sparkled.

> **"...YOU SQUEAK!"

Belle's ears folded back in embarrassment.

> **"...I do not."

Star gave one more gentle squeeze.

**Squeak.**

The room burst into laughter.

Belle covered her face.

> **"...Traitor..."

She wasn't sure whether she meant Star or her own fluffy body.
