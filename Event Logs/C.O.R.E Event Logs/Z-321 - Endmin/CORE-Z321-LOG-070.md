# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-070**

### **PERSONAL HISTORY – ENDMIN'S SCHOOL YEARS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Psychological / Historical Record

---

## **SUMMARY**

During an informal discussion in Room N-07, Z-321 (Endmin) voluntarily shared details regarding her childhood education in the Banlands.

The discussion provided additional context for her longstanding discomfort with people touching her eye covering and explains several behavioral patterns documented throughout earlier observations.

The account was recorded with Endmin's permission.

---

## **Conversation**

The topic began innocently.

Taiga had asked why Endmin disliked unexpected contact around her face so much.

Endmin was quiet for several moments.

Then she closed the book she had been reading.

> "...It started at school."

The room gradually became silent.

---

## **Endmin's Account**

> "Everyone already knew who my mom was."

> "The Warden of the Banlands."

She smiled faintly.

> "People thought that meant nobody would dare bother me."

The smile disappeared.

> "...They were wrong."

---

> "Back then..."

> "...the Ether Corruption had already damaged my eyes."

She gently picked up her old metal eye mask from the desk.

> "I wore this every day."

She ran a thumb across its smooth surface.

> "...Most students didn't see someone protecting damaged eyes."

> "...They saw someone different."

---

## **Bullying**

Endmin described being regularly mocked during her school years.

Common remarks included:

> "Freak."

> "Monster."

> "What's under the mask?"

Some students would deliberately tap or flick the metal eye covering as they walked past.

Others would reach out without permission simply to see her reaction.

Because her damaged eyes perceived the world through delicate Ether vibrations and echoes, even light impacts against the mask could leave her disoriented or nauseated.

She quietly admitted:

> "...They knew I'd tell them to stop."

> "...Sometimes they laughed because of how I said it."

She lowered her head.

> "...So they kept doing it."

---

## **Lucy's Response**

Lucy never ignored what was happening.

Whenever she learned who was responsible, she confronted the students and their families through the appropriate Banlands authorities.

Disciplinary action was taken repeatedly.

Yet the bullying continued.

As Endmin explained:

> "...Punishing one group didn't stop the next."

Lucy listened quietly as Endmin spoke.

When she finished, Lucy reached over and gently held her daughter's hand.

She didn't interrupt.

She simply stayed beside her.

---

## **A Change of Perspective**

Mart finally broke the silence.

> "...So when we tell people not to touch your face..."

> "...it's not just because it hurts."

Endmin nodded.

> "...It reminds me."

Taiga looked down.

She remembered all the times she had accidentally startled Endmin during their first weeks together.

> "...I'm sorry."

Endmin smiled reassuringly.

> "...You stopped when I asked."

> "...That's what matters."

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Behavioral Sciences notes that Endmin has made significant progress since arriving at the Blacksite.

She is now comfortable removing her mask around trusted individuals, no longer relies on it for vision, and openly displays her Thiren traits.

However, staff are reminded that touching her face, eyes, glasses, or former mask without permission remains inappropriate and may unintentionally bring back difficult memories.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Later that evening, Endmin quietly placed her old metal mask back on its shelf.

Not because she needed it.

Simply because it was part of her history.

Taiga walked over and stood beside her.

She looked at the mask.

Then at Endmin.

Without saying anything, she gave Endmin a gentle hug.

No teasing.

No questions.

No reaching for the mask.

Endmin hugged her back.

After a moment, she smiled.

> **"...Thanks."

Taiga smiled too.

> **"For what?"

Endmin glanced around Room N-07, where her friends were chatting and laughing together.

> **"...For proving school was wrong."**
