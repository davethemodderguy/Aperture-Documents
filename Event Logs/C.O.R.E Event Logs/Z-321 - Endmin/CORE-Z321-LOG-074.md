# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-074**

### **BIOLOGICAL OBSERVATION – FELINE TRANSFORMATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Medical / Residential

---

## **SUMMARY**

Medical and Behavioral Sciences have confirmed that Z-321 (Endmin) possesses the ability to fully transform into a domestic feline, consistent with her Thiren physiology.

Unlike many Thirens capable of transformation, Endmin demonstrates exceptional control over the ability and almost never transforms unintentionally.

There is, however, one notable exception.

---

## **Transformation Control**

Endmin is capable of voluntarily transforming into her feline form.

Outside of controlled demonstrations, she almost never does so.

She explained the reason simply:

> "It's... embarrassing."

Behavioral Sciences believes years of discipline and self-control developed during her service in the Banlands allow her to suppress involuntary transformations under nearly all circumstances.

---

## **The Exception**

Only one person has consistently caused Endmin to transform unintentionally.

**Lucy.**

Specifically, moments in which Lucy comforts, protects, or embraces her daughter after periods of emotional stress.

Behavioral Sciences believes this is not caused by Lucy herself, but by the profound sense of safety Endmin feels around her mother.

When Endmin's mind fully relaxes, the effort required to maintain her humanoid form briefly disappears.

The result is an involuntary transformation.

Lucy has apologized numerous times.

Endmin insists it is not her fault.

> "...I just feel... safe."

---

## **Feline Form**

Endmin's feline form has now been documented.

Species identification confirms she most closely resembles a **grey tabby domestic cat** with extensive white markings.

Coloration includes:

- Approximately **55% white fur** across the body.
    
- Grey tabby patterning over much of the remaining coat.
    
- Distinct black patches and stripes matching those seen on her Thiren tail.
    
- White ears with grey tabby markings near the base.
    
- The same distinctive eyes:
    
    - Hollow dark navy-blue irises.
        
    - Slowly rotating golden rings.
        

Despite her smaller form, the unusual eyes remain immediately recognizable.

---

## **Behavior**

While transformed, Endmin retains her full intelligence, memories, and personality.

She remains capable of understanding speech and following conversations.

However, she exhibits noticeably more instinctive feline behaviors, including:

- Curling into warm blankets.
    
- Seeking elevated resting places.
    
- Purring when comfortable.
    
- Kneading soft cushions unconsciously.
    
- Grooming herself more frequently.
    

Speech is unavailable while transformed.

Communication instead relies on body language, vocalizations, and gestures.

---

## **Residential Incident**

One evening, following an especially exhausting week of research and training, Lucy found Endmin asleep on the sofa in Room N-07.

She quietly sat beside her and gently stroked her hair.

Within seconds...

There was a brief shimmer of Ether.

The blanket suddenly looked much larger.

Lucy looked down.

Curled against her side was a small grey tabby cat with white fur covering much of her body.

The cat slowly opened its unusual navy-and-gold eyes.

It looked up at Lucy.

> "...Mrrp."

Lucy laughed softly.

> "...There you are."

The little cat immediately climbed into her lap.

---

## **Director Lucious' Note**

After reviewing the report, Director Lucious added a brief annotation.

> **"Transformation is not a containment concern."**

> **"It is simply another expression of her Thiren biology."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Endmin's feline transformation remains an uncommon occurrence.

Outside of rare moments when she feels completely secure—most often in Lucy's presence—she maintains full control over the ability.

Medical Division considers the transformation healthy and places no restrictions on it.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late one night, Lucy carried a sleeping Endmin back to Room N-07 after a long day.

Halfway down the hallway, there was a faint shimmer.

Lucy looked down with a knowing smile.

A small grey tabby with white fur was now asleep in her arms, purring quietly without waking.

Lucy gently adjusted the blanket around her.

> **"...Goodnight, Lindervene."**

The little cat gave a sleepy chirp in reply and tucked her head beneath Lucy's chin.

Lucy smiled.

> "...Sleep well."

For perhaps the first time in years, the former "Angel of Delusions" slept without needing to guard herself against the world.
