# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-082**

### **DIPLOMATIC VISIT – MEETING ENDMIN'S RABBIT (PART III)**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Cultural Exchange / Residential

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the search around Merryweather Manor, Z-321's long-mentioned pet rabbit was finally located.

The rabbit displayed an exceptionally calm and affectionate temperament, showing no fear toward the visiting delegation.

Notably, the rabbit exhibited a clear preference for staying close to **Endmin**, **Alice**, and **Taiga**.

---

## **Species Observation**

**Name:** Undisclosed (Endmin declined to give a public name.)

**Species:** Domestic Rabbit

**Sex:** Female

**Appearance:**

- Soft **pink fur** covering her entire body.
    
- Long, exceptionally fluffy ears.
    
- A tiny round tail resembling a soft cotton orb.
    
- Gentle red-pink eyes.
    
- Small build.
    

Behavioral Sciences noted that the rabbit appeared remarkably well-socialized.

---

## **First Meeting**

The rustling in the tall grass grew louder.

A small pink rabbit emerged, stopping only for a moment before spotting Endmin.

Her ears immediately perked up.

Without hesitation—

She bounded toward Endmin.

Endmin knelt, laughing softly as the rabbit hopped directly into her lap.

> **"...There you are."**

The rabbit nuzzled against Endmin's hands before receiving several gentle head scratches.

Taiga smiled almost immediately.

> **"...She's adorable."**

---

## **An Unexpected Preference**

As Endmin stood up, the rabbit briefly looked around the group.

She hopped toward Alice.

Alice blinked.

> **"...Me?"**

The rabbit gently nudged Alice's boot before happily circling around her feet.

Alice crouched carefully.

The rabbit willingly climbed into her lap.

Alice couldn't help smiling.

> **"...I think she likes me."**

Lucy chuckled knowingly.

> **"...She usually has good judgment."**

---

## **Taiga's Turn**

A few moments later, Taiga quietly sat down on the grass.

The rabbit tilted her head.

Then casually hopped over and settled beside her.

Taiga carefully stroked the rabbit's fluffy ears.

> **"...You're unbelievably soft."**

The rabbit responded by happily leaning against Taiga's hand.

Mart folded his arms.

> **"...Well..."

> **"...I've officially been ignored."

The rabbit looked toward him.

Thought about it.

Then hopped straight back to Endmin.

Everyone laughed.

---

## **A Familiar Routine**

Alice later explained that this behavior was perfectly normal.

Whenever Endmin returned home, the rabbit typically stayed near three people.

- Endmin.
    
- Alice.
    
- Whoever Endmin trusted the most.
    

Alice smiled toward Taiga.

> **"...Looks like you've been accepted."**

Taiga looked genuinely touched.

> **"...That's... actually an honor."**

Endmin quietly nodded.

> **"...She doesn't usually warm up to strangers that quickly."**

## **Closing Observation**

As the afternoon continued, the tour became noticeably more relaxed.

Endmin walked through the gardens with the rabbit happily hopping at her heels.

When she stopped, the little bunny settled beside her feet.

If Endmin wandered too far, the rabbit would gently follow.

Occasionally she would detour to Alice for a few affectionate nudges, then bounce over to Taiga for ear scratches before returning to Endmin once more.

Watching the scene, Lucy folded her arms with a satisfied smile.

> **"...Looks like she's adopted another favorite."**

Taiga looked down at the little pink rabbit leaning against her boot.

She smiled warmly.

> **"...I don't mind one bit."**

The rabbit answered with a content little hop before rejoining Endmin as the tour of Merryweather Manor continued.
