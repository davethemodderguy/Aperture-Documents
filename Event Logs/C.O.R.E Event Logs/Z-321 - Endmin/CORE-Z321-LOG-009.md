# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-009**

### **RESIDENCY STATUS UPDATE — PERMANENT BLACKSITE RESIDENCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – ADMINISTRATIVE / MEDICAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following extensive medical consultation between Aperture Industries and **Lucy, Warden of the Banlands**, Z-321 ("Endmin") has been granted **permanent residency** within the Blacksite.

The decision was made jointly and was based primarily on Endmin's long-term medical welfare.

Although the Banlands Headquarters remains her legal home, its environmental conditions have been determined to be increasingly harmful to her already fragile visual system.

Lucy has retained full guardianship and has stated that she will visit regularly through authorized N.O.S.T. portals.

---

## **MEDICAL JUSTIFICATION**

Over several months of observation, Medical Division identified a clear trend.

Within the controlled environment of the Blacksite:

- Endmin's eye condition remained stable.
    
- Episodes of discomfort became less frequent.
    
- Motion sickness diminished.
    
- Psychological stress decreased.
    
- Sleep quality improved.
    

By contrast, Lucy provided records indicating that prolonged stays within the Banlands Headquarters consistently aggravated Endmin's condition.

According to Lucy, the ambient Ether density surrounding Banlands administration facilities has gradually increased over the years.

While harmless to most Banlands residents, it poses a continual risk to Endmin's damaged eyes.

Medical Division concluded that returning Endmin to live there permanently would likely accelerate further deterioration.
