Following continued observation and carefully monitored purification treatments, Z-321 (Endmin) has reached the most significant milestone since arriving at APRT-70.

Medical Division confirms that **95% of the Ether Corruption originally present within her body has been successfully purged or neutralized.**

The remaining 5% has reached a stable equilibrium and continues to be monitored.

Although the physical structure of Endmin's eyes has **not** returned to a normal human state, their functionality has improved beyond all initial projections.

For the first time since her childhood injury...

**Endmin can see the world normally.**

- Full color vision.

At the researchers.

At her roommates.

At her mother.
