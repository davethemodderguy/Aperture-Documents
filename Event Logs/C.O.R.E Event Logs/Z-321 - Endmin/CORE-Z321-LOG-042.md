# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-042**

### **POST-RECOVERY ADAPTATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Behavioral Observation

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the complete stabilization of Z-321 (Endmin), personnel noticed an unexpected behavior.

Despite no longer requiring eye protection for medical reasons, Endmin continued wearing something over her eyes almost every day.

Medical evaluation concluded this was neither a dependency nor a medical necessity.

It was simply... habit.

---

## **The Mask**

For years, Endmin's mask had been far more than protective equipment.

It had been:

- Her eye protection.
    
- Her confidence.
    
- Her identity.
    
- Her shield against both light and unwanted attention.
    

Removing it permanently felt... strange.

When asked why she still wore it, Endmin answered simply:

> "I'm used to it."

> "...It feels weird without something here."

She lightly tapped the area around her eyes.

---

## **A New Alternative**

Medical staff suggested that, since the mask was no longer required, she could try lighter eye coverings.

Several options were presented.

Endmin rejected nearly all of them.

Until Taiga walked into the room carrying a small case.

Inside was a pair of simple, round-framed glasses with clear lenses.

Taiga grinned.

> "How about these?"

Endmin carefully put them on.

She blinked once.

Then looked into a mirror.

She tilted her head.

> "...They're light."

Another pause.

> "...Comfortable."

From that day onward, Endmin alternated between two appearances:

- Her iconic Banlands mask.
    
- A pair of round glasses with clear circular lenses.
    

Neither affected her vision.

She simply wore whichever felt right that day.

---

## **Lucy's Opinion**

Lucy noticed the glasses during one of her visits.

She smiled.

> "They suit you."

Endmin adjusted the frames.

> "...Really?"

Lucy nodded.

> "Really."

After a moment, she added with a small laugh,

> "Though I still recognize you instantly with the mask."

---

## **Room N-07 Reactions**

### Taiga

Immediately declared the glasses "cute."

She attempted to buy Endmin six more pairs.

Endmin politely stopped her after the second.

---

### Mart

Looked at Endmin wearing the glasses for several seconds.

Then quietly said,

> "...They fit you."

Endmin smiled for the rest of the afternoon.

---

Asked permission to draw Endmin wearing both the mask and the glasses.

The resulting sketches were later pinned to the Room N-07 notice board.

---

### Nightshade

Looked at the glasses once.

> "...You somehow look even more approachable."

---

### Frost

Suggested matching winter scarves to go with the glasses.

Endmin agreed immediately.

---

Adjusted the glasses for proper fit.

Then jokingly asked,

> "Want me to install a heads-up display?"

Endmin laughed.

> "...No."

---

## **Medical Assessment**

Psychological Services determined the behavior to be entirely healthy.

The continued use of the mask or glasses is considered a comfort habit developed over many years rather than a response to anxiety or medical need.

No intervention is recommended.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

- Vision fully restored.
    
- Mask no longer medically required.
    
- Frequently alternates between:
    
    - Banlands protective mask.
        
    - Round-framed clear-lens glasses.
        
- Reports feeling more comfortable when wearing either.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

One afternoon, Endmin arrived at the shooting range wearing her round glasses instead of her mask.

A junior researcher blinked.

> "I almost didn't recognize you."

Endmin smiled.

> "Most people don't."

She adjusted the glasses before taking aim.

Three kilometers away, a training drone launched into the air.

A single shot echoed across the range.

The drone fell immediately.

Without lowering her rifle, Endmin smiled again.

> "The glasses don't make me miss."
