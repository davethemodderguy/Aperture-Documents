# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-053**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION – ENGINEERING HYPERFOCUS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Psychological / Engineering

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following several months of working alongside Neko in the Engineering Division, Behavioral Sciences noted an amusing but recurring pattern involving Z-321 (Endmin).

Given enough uninterrupted time...

Endmin begins thinking like an engineer.

Given even more time...

She begins thinking like **Neko**.

---

## **Observed Symptoms**

The phenomenon is entirely harmless but has become well known among Engineering personnel.

Common signs include:

- Forgetting to eat while working.
    
- Sketching weapon modifications on scrap paper.
    
- Quietly muttering design calculations.
    
- Disassembling perfectly functional equipment "just to improve it."
    
- Asking increasingly concerning questions beginning with:
    
    - "Hypothetically..."
        
    - "What if..."
        
    - "Can Nekonium withstand..."
        

Neko has repeatedly denied responsibility.

No one believes him.

---

## **Engineering Assessment**

Miss Neko summarized the situation during a weekly review.

> "Endmin already had engineering experience before arriving here."

She glanced toward Neko, who was enthusiastically explaining an impossible mechanism to Endmin.

> "...Neko just accelerated the process."

Engineering staff jokingly began referring to the condition as:

**Mad Scientist Mode**

No medical diagnosis exists under that name.

It nevertheless became popular throughout the Blacksite.

---

## **Recommended Countermeasures**

Behavioral Sciences discovered that Endmin's engineering hyperfocus can be interrupted almost immediately through one of two methods.

### Method 1 – Tell Her to Go Skating

This is the preferred solution.

The moment Endmin leaves the workshop and starts skating, she rapidly relaxes.

Fresh air, movement, and exercise consistently reduce prolonged hyperfocus.

Taiga frequently volunteers.

> **Taiga:**
> 
> "Come on!"
> 
> "Engineering can wait!"
> 
> "Race me!"

Endmin almost never refuses.

---

### Method 2 – Call Aster

If Endmin remains too absorbed in a project to notice the passage of time, personnel simply look for Aster.

Conveniently...

Aster is usually already there.

The white cat is almost never observed far from Endmin.

She quietly jumps onto the workbench.

Places a paw on whichever blueprint Endmin is studying.

Then writes on her whiteboard.

> **"Break."**

Endmin immediately understands.

> "...Right."

> "...I've been here for five hours."

Aster nods once.

Mission accomplished.

---

## **Aster's Attachment**

Archive Division has noted that Aster is rarely seen leaving Endmin's side unless responding to another urgent matter.

Whether Endmin is:

- Engineering.
    
- Skating.
    
- Reading.
    
- Practicing marksmanship.
    
- Gardening.
    
- Sitting quietly by a window.
    

Aster is usually somewhere nearby.

Sometimes on her shoulder.

Sometimes in her lap.

Sometimes asleep on her backpack.

When asked why, Aster simply wrote:

> **"She pets me."**

A few seconds later, she added:

> **"...Very well."**

---

## **Endmin's Perspective**

Endmin has never hidden her affection for cats.

She happily greets every cat she encounters at the Blacksite, but Aster has become her constant companion.

When asked if she minded Aster following her everywhere, Endmin looked genuinely confused.

> "...Mind?"

She looked down as Aster rubbed against her leg.

> "...Why would I?"

She bent down and gently scratched behind Aster's ears.

> "...She's adorable."

Aster began purring.

---

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Endmin continues contributing significantly to Engineering projects.

However, supervisors are encouraged to monitor prolonged periods of uninterrupted design work.

If signs of engineering hyperfocus appear, the recommended response is simple:

1. Tell Endmin to take a skating break.
    

**Or...**

2. Find Aster.
    

Most of the time, step two has already happened.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, Neko and Endmin were standing in front of a whiteboard completely covered in equations.

> **Neko:** "What if we add another stabilizer?"

> **Endmin:** "...Or seven."

Before either could continue, a soft _thump_ echoed through the workshop.

Aster had jumped onto the table.

Without a word, she placed herself directly over the blueprints.

She slowly held up her whiteboard.

> **"Outside."**

Endmin stared at it.

Then laughed.

> "...Okay."

She picked up her skates.

Neko sighed.

> "...She's right, isn't she?"

Aster looked at him.

Then wrote one final sentence.

> **"You should come too."**
