# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-029**

### **WING DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM — FLIGHT CERTIFICATION & NEKONIUM WING ARMOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – ENGINEERING / BIOLOGICAL AUGMENTATION

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the events of Incident Report CORE-Z321-LOG-028, Neko completed the development of a dedicated protection system for Z-321 ("Endmin")'s wings.

Simultaneously, Lucy began Endmin's long-awaited flight training within the Starlands.

Both programs concluded successfully.

Endmin is now certified for controlled aerial maneuvering.

---

## **PROJECT: WING ARMOR**

Following the explosion that injured Endmin's wings, Neko was noticeably quieter than usual for several days.

When asked what he was working on, his only reply was,

> **Neko:** "Fixing a design flaw."

Several days later, he returned carrying two compact metallic modules.

He handed them to Endmin.

> **Neko:** "Hold still."

---

## **NEKONIUM NANO WING ARMOR**

The modules attached harmlessly near the base of Endmin's wings.

Upon activation, billions of microscopic Nekonium particles spread across every feather, forming an incredibly thin protective lattice.

Once fully deployed, a low-profile forcefield enveloped the entire wing structure.

The armor possessed several advantages:

- Distributed impact protection across the full wing.
    
- Reinforced resistance against heat and fragmentation.
    
- Increased resistance to cuts and punctures.
    
- Minimal restriction of feather movement.
    
- Automatic reformation after minor damage.
    
- Negligible increase in weight.
    

Rather than replacing the wings' natural durability, the system acted as an additional protective layer.

Neko summarized it simply.

> **Neko:** "If your wings are shields..."

He folded his arms.

> "...Then they deserve armor."

Endmin smiled.

> "...Thank you."

---

## **COMFORT TEST**

Medical staff expected the armor to interfere with natural movement.

Instead, Endmin reported almost no discomfort.

> **Endmin:** "...I can barely feel it."

The combination of adaptive Nekonium nanoparticles and the forcefield automatically adjusted to feather movement, preserving flexibility during flight and defensive use.

Engineering classified the system as a complete success.

---

## **FLIGHT TRAINING**

With her wings now better protected, Lucy declared there were no more excuses.

Training began in the open skies of Starlands.

The first lesson was surprisingly simple.

> **Lucy:** "Stop fighting your wings."

Endmin blinked.

> "...What?"

Lucy smiled.

> "...They're trying to help you."

---

## **LEARNING TO FLY**

The first attempts were... awkward.

Endmin could already flap powerfully enough to lift herself.

Landing, however, proved considerably more difficult.

Several early sessions ended with:

- Rolling through flower fields.
    
- Landing in bushes.
    
- Sliding across grassy hills.
    
- Accidentally frightening Leo the Banana Snake, who responded by slithering indignantly away.
    

Star laughed so hard she nearly floated into a tree.

Nightshade quietly kept score.

---

## **THE FIRST TRUE FLIGHT**

Several days into training, something changed.

Rather than forcing every movement, Endmin relaxed.

She let the wings move naturally.

One powerful downstroke.

Another.

Then...

She stayed airborne.

She climbed steadily into the Starlands sky.

No stumbling.

No panic.

No crashing.

Lucy watched proudly from below.

> **Lucy:** "...There you go."

Endmin looked around in amazement.

For the first time in her life...

She wasn't protecting someone beneath her wings.

She was flying with them.

---

## **GLIDING**

Despite successfully mastering powered flight, Endmin quickly developed a favorite method of travel.

Gliding.

She would climb high into the sky before quietly folding her wings into long, graceful descents.

The technique required very little effort.

It was peaceful.

When Taiga witnessed it for the first time, she immediately began cheering.

> **Taiga:** "YOU GLIDE TOO!"

Endmin laughed.

> "...It's relaxing."

Taiga nodded enthusiastically.

> "...See? Gliding is awesome!"

Mart smiled from below.

> "...You're both impossible."

---

## **SPARRING EXERCISE**

To evaluate the armor, Neko conducted several controlled impact tests.

Training rounds struck the armored wings.

Blunt impacts.

Heat.

Controlled fragmentation.

The nanoparticle lattice dispersed the force while the forcefield absorbed much of the remaining energy.

Endmin folded her wings around herself.

When the exercise ended, she carefully inspected them.

Not a single feather had been damaged.

Neko looked satisfied.

> "...Now that's better."

---

## **LUCY'S COMMENT**

As Endmin landed after another flawless flight, Lucy looked toward the sky.

> **Lucy:** "...You've finally learned."

Endmin smiled.

> "...How to fly?"

Lucy shook her head.

> "...How to trust your wings."

Endmin looked at the enormous dark wings stretching behind her.

For the first time...

They no longer felt like a burden she carried.

They felt like part of her.

### **Closing Observation**

One quiet afternoon in the Starlands, three figures could be seen crossing the sky.

Taiga leapt from one current to another, using her wings to glide between graceful air jumps.

Mart flew effortlessly on brilliant wings of white light.

Above them both, Endmin spread her vast dark wings.

She caught a warm updraft, then simply... glided.

For several peaceful minutes, none of them spoke.

Taiga eventually looked up and laughed.

> **Taiga:** "Looks like we really do fly the same way now!"

Endmin smiled beneath her mask.

> "...Almost."

Then, with a single powerful flap, she climbed high into the open sky before easing back into another long, effortless glide.

From the ground below, Lucy watched her daughter disappear into the clouds.

She folded her arms and smiled quietly.

> "...About time."
