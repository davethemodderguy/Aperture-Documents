# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-014**

### **PROJECT PRISM — PARTIAL ETHER PERCEPTION AUGMENTATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – MEDICAL / N.O.S.T. JOINT RESEARCH

---

## **SUMMARY**

After months of collaborative research into ambient Ether, Aperture Industries successfully developed a **non-invasive Ether resonance augment** based on naturally occurring Ether properties.

The project was **not** intended to restore Z-321 ("Endmin")'s damaged eyesight.

Instead, its sole objective was to supplement her existing perception with one capability she had long wished for:

**The ability to perceive color.**

Lucy was consulted throughout the project, and no procedure proceeded without her knowledge.

---

## **DESIGN PHILOSOPHY**

Researchers deliberately avoided attempting to repair Endmin's eyes.

Medical Division reiterated that the structural damage caused by Ether Corruption remains irreversible with current knowledge.

Instead, Project PRISM sought to answer a different question:

> _Could color information be translated into a form Endmin's existing sensory system could naturally understand?_

After numerous failed prototypes, researchers achieved a stable Ether resonance pattern capable of conveying chromatic information without altering the underlying injury.

Endmin would still perceive the world through her unique sensory system.

Only now...

That world could also contain color.

---

## **THE DELIVERY**

One final problem remained.

Endmin has an exceptional ability to perceive Ether disturbances.

Every previous organization that had promised to "fix" her had approached with unfamiliar equipment, injections, or suspicious medications.

As a result, she instinctively distrusted experimental treatments.

Research Division therefore chose a different approach.

The finalized Ether resonator was safely incorporated into something familiar.

A small fruit-flavored candy.

No needles.

No machines.

No deception.

Director Lucious personally explained what it was.

> **Director Lucious:** "This won't heal your eyes."

Endmin listened quietly.

> "...Okay."

> **Director Lucious:** "It probably won't change how you perceive the world."

Another nod.

> **Director Lucious:** "...But..."

He smiled.

> "...We think it might let your world have colors."

Endmin stared silently at the wrapped candy in her hands.

After a long pause, she quietly asked,

> "...Really?"

Lucious answered honestly.

> "...We don't know."

> "...But we'd like to try."

Endmin smiled.

Then slowly unwrapped it.

---

## **INITIAL EFFECT**

For several seconds...

Nothing happened.

Researchers quietly exchanged uncertain glances.

Then Endmin froze.

Her breathing caught.

She slowly turned toward the nearby window.

Outside...

A flower bed swayed gently in the wind.

She stood perfectly still.

Neither speaking.

Nor moving.

Tears began running beneath the edges of her mask.

---

## **FIRST COLORS**

Later, Endmin attempted to describe the experience.

> "...Everything..."

She struggled to find words.

> "...Still feels like my world."

She looked around the room.

> "...But..."

Another tear escaped.

> "...There's... something else now."

She reached toward a small blue flower on the windowsill.

Her hand trembled.

> "...So..."

> "...This is blue..."

---

She spent nearly an hour simply looking around the room.

The green leaves of potted plants.

The warm brown of wooden furniture.

The bright yellow safety markings on the floor.

The colorful covers of books she had only known by shape.

Everything she had spent years imagining.

She could finally perceive.

---

## **STAR'S PAINTINGS**

Word spread quickly through Room N-07.

Star immediately arrived carrying several canvases.

She carefully placed the first painting in front of Endmin.

> **Star:** "...You always wanted to know."

> "...These are my favorite colors."

Endmin looked.

Really looked.

She reached toward the painting.

Then broke down crying.

She covered her face with both hands.

Unable to stop the tears.

> "...They're..."

She laughed through sobs.

> "...They're beautiful..."

Star quietly hugged her.

Neither of them spoke again for several minutes.

---

## **LUCY'S ARRIVAL**

Lucy arrived through a portal shortly after receiving Director Lucious's message.

The moment she entered the room, her expression hardened.

> **Lucy:** "...What did you do?"

The room fell silent.

She looked directly at Director Lucious.

Years of failed promises from others weighed heavily on her voice.

> "...I told you..."

> "...I didn't want anyone trying to fix her."

Before anyone could answer...

Endmin ran toward her.

She gently took Lucy's hands.

Still crying.

Still smiling.

> "...Mom..."

Lucy looked down.

> "...I can see them."

Lucy frowned.

> "...See what?"

Endmin turned toward Star's paintings.

Then toward the flowers beside the window.

Finally...

Back toward her mother.

> "...The colors."

Silence.

Lucy slowly looked around the room.

Then back at her daughter.

The joy on Endmin's face was unmistakable.

It wasn't the excitement of a successful experiment.

It was the wonder of someone experiencing something she had dreamed about for years.

Lucy's expression softened.

She wrapped Endmin in a tight embrace.

For a long time...

Neither moved.

Finally, Lucy looked toward Director Lucious.

Her voice was quieter now.

> "...Thank you."

For the first time since Aperture Industries and the Banlands established formal cooperation...

The Warden of the Banlands bowed her head in sincere gratitude.

It was a gesture few present had ever expected to witness.

Director Lucious simply returned the nod.

> "...You're welcome."

---

## **MEDICAL FINDINGS**

Follow-up examinations confirmed:

- No deterioration of Endmin's eyes.
    
- No increase in Ether Corruption.
    
- No alteration to her existing perception.
    
- Stable integration of the Ether resonance augment.
    

Her perception continues to rely on echoes, vibration, and Ether.

The augmentation provides an additional layer of color information without replacing her natural sensory system.

### **Closing Observation**

Late that night, the lights in Room N-07 were already off.

Everyone else had fallen asleep.

Endmin quietly sat by the window.

Outside, the Blacksite gardens rested beneath the night sky.

For the first time in years...

The flowers weren't just shapes.

The trees weren't just outlines.

The world wasn't only structure.

It had color.

She smiled softly.

Then whispered to herself,

> "...So this..."

> "...Is what everyone kept talking about."
