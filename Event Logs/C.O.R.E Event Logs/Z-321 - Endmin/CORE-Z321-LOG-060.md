**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Banlands Intelligence

## **SUMMARY**

Further information provided by Z-945 (Lucy) has clarified two previously unresolved aspects of Z-321 (Endmin):

- The method used to conceal her Thiren anatomy.
    
- The origin of the Banlands title **"Angel of Delusions."**
    

---

## **Thiren Concealment**

### **Tail**

The mystery surrounding Endmin's tail has been resolved.

Rather than using technology or specialized clothing, Endmin simply folds her exceptionally large wings around her back.

Her tail naturally rests beneath the folded wings, completely hidden from normal observation.

This concealment is purely physical.

---

### **Ears**

Her ears, however, are concealed through an anomalous ability.

Endmin continuously manipulates ambient Ether to generate a subtle perceptual illusion.

The illusion affects:

- Normal human vision.
    
- Conventional optical cameras.
    
- Standard video recording equipment.
    

To observers, her ears simply... aren't there.

Medical scans later confirmed an important detail:

> **The ears never disappear.**

They are present at all times.

People simply fail to perceive them.

Because the illusion is maintained almost effortlessly, most individuals remain unaware that they are looking at a concealed Thiren.

---

## **Ether Illusion Manipulation**

Lucy identified the concealment as one of Endmin's oldest and most refined Ether techniques.

Unlike dramatic visual illusions, this application is extremely subtle.

Rather than creating false images, Endmin gently alters what the observer's senses interpret.

The result is convincing enough that even trained personnel seldom question what they are seeing.

Engineering tests demonstrated that ordinary cameras record the illusion exactly as a human observer would perceive it.

Specialized Ether-sensitive instrumentation, however, can detect the underlying distortion.

---

## **Banlands Designation**

Before her relocation to the Blacksite, Endmin was widely feared throughout the Banlands under another name.

> **"Angel of Delusions."**

The title originated among criminal organizations.

According to Lucy:

> "By the time they realized what was real..."

> "...Endmin had already completed her mission."

---

## **Operational Use**

Historical Banlands records describe several applications of Endmin's Ether manipulation, including:

- Concealing her own presence.
    
- Hiding specific physical traits.
    
- Distorting an opponent's perception of distance or direction.
    
- Creating convincing false movements.
    
- Causing targets to misidentify threats during pursuit.
    

The ability was especially effective during capture and assassination operations, earning her a reputation for appearing and disappearing without warning.

Importantly, the illusion does **not** physically alter reality.

It alters how reality is perceived.

### **Closing Observation**

One afternoon, Endmin was reading quietly in the greenhouse.

Believing she was alone, she relaxed her Ether concealment.

A pair of white cat ears appeared atop her head.

Moments later, Taiga walked in.

She stopped.

Looked at the ears.

Then smiled from ear to ear.

> **Taiga:** "...You forgot."

Endmin reached up.

Felt the ears.

Her eyes widened.

> "...Oh no."

Before she could restore the illusion, Aster calmly jumped into her lap and curled up beneath one of the exposed ears.

Aster wrote on her whiteboard without looking up.

> **"Too late."**

Endmin sighed in defeat.

> "...Five more minutes."

No one in the room objected.
