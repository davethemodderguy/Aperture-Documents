# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-138**

### **VISITOR LOG – ALICE'S DELIVERY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Residential Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

Alice, Head of Security of the Merryweather Manor, arrived at Heaven's Blacksite on an approved temporary visit.

Her purpose was straightforward:

To personally deliver Endmin's prescribed eye medication from the Banlands.

---

## **Arrival**

The front security checkpoint recognized Alice immediately.

After the usual identity verification, she was escorted toward the residential wing.

She carried a small insulated medical pouch rather than her usual stack of security documents.

Taiga was the first to notice.

> **"Alice!"**

Alice smiled warmly.

> **"Hello, everyone."**

---

## **Room N-07**

Endmin opened the door.

> **"...Alice?"

Alice held up the pouch.

> **"You forgot something."

Endmin looked inside.

Several bottles of her prescribed eye medication rested neatly in protective packaging.

Endmin immediately looked embarrassed.

> **"...I forgot to bring my refill..."

Alice nodded.

> **"Mrs. Lucy asked me to make sure you received it."

> **"You're supposed to use it regularly."

Endmin scratched the back of her head.

> **"...I know..."

---

## **Medication Reminder**

Alice carefully placed the medication on Endmin's desk.

She then looked directly at Endmin.

> **"Promise me you'll actually use it."

Endmin quietly nodded.

> **"...Promise."

Satisfied, Alice smiled.

> **"Good."

---

## **Friendly Roommates**

The rest of the group gradually gathered.

Star happily waved.

Mart greeted Alice with a smile.

Neko and Miss Neko welcomed her back.

Lucius thanked her for making the trip.

Taiga immediately offered snacks.

Alice accepted a cup of tea before sitting down for a short visit.

The conversation quickly shifted away from work and toward everyday life at the Blacksite.

For a little while...

It felt less like a security visit...

And more like an old friend dropping by.

---

## **Personnel Comments**

### Alice

Looking toward Endmin, she smiled.

> **"...You take care of everyone else."

She gently tapped the medication pouch.

> **"...Don't forget to take care of yourself too."**

---

### Endmin

Holding one of the medicine bottles, Endmin gave a small smile.

> **"...Thanks, Alice."

---

### Lucius

> **"...Mom really did send Alice just to make sure Endmin remembered."

Alice laughed softly.

> **"...She knows your sister."**

---

### Taiga

> **"...You should visit more often."

Alice smiled.

> **"...I'd like that."**

---

### Lucy (Message Attached)

Along with the medication was a handwritten note from Lucy.

> **"Endmin,**

> **Take your eye medicine on schedule.**

> **Don't make Alice come all the way here again because you forgot your refill.**

> **Love, Mom."**

Endmin read the note.

She smiled sheepishly.

> **"...That does sound like her."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Endmin's medication was successfully delivered without incident.

Alice remained at Heaven's Blacksite for several hours before returning to Merryweather Manor to resume her duties as Head of Security.

Before leaving, she reminded Endmin one last time:

> **"...Medicine first."**

Endmin sighed with an embarrassed smile.

> **"...Yes, Alice."**
