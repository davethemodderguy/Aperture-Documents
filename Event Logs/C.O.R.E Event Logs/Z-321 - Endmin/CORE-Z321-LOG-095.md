## **CORE-Z321-LOG-095**

### **BIOLOGICAL OBSERVATION – THIREN RACIAL TRAITS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Biological Research / Personnel

---

## **SUMMARY**

Research into Thiren physiology has confirmed that, in addition to their visible animal characteristics, each Thiren possesses innate biological traits inherited from their species.

These traits are natural adaptations rather than anomalous abilities and are present regardless of training or occupation.

Individual skill and experience may further enhance these abilities.

---

# **Documented Thiren Traits**

## **Rabbit Thiren**

**Representative:** Belle

### Natural Traits

- Exceptional sprinting speed.
    
- High jumping capability.
    
- Lightweight skeletal and muscular structure.
    
- Excellent balance while airborne.
    
- Powerful lower-body muscles.
    

These traits allow Belle to move with remarkable agility despite carrying an approximately **50 kg** hybrid sword.

Behavioral Sciences notes that Belle's frequent falls while sprinting are not caused by her body, but by the enormous weight and unusual way she carries her weapon over her head.

Without the sword, Belle is one of the fastest runners observed among Blacksite residents.

---

## **Cat Thiren**

**Representatives:**

- Endmin
    
- Neko
    
- Miss Neko
    

### Natural Traits

- Enhanced hearing.
    
- Superior reflexes.
    
- Excellent agility and balance.
    
- Increased flexibility.
    
- Exceptional performance in low-light environments.
    

### Behavioral Drawback

Cat instincts activate involuntarily under certain emotional conditions.

Observed behaviors include:

- Ear movements reflecting emotion.
    
- Tail movements revealing mood.
    
- Grooming motions such as licking a hand or sleeve.
    
- Scratching the face absentmindedly.
    
- Involuntary meowing when startled or frightened.
    

These behaviors are entirely instinctive and are not consciously performed.

---

## **Snow Leopard Thiren**

**Representative:** Taiga

### Natural Traits

- High resistance to extreme cold.
    
- Exceptional running speed.
    
- Sharp, durable claws suitable for climbing and close combat.
    
- Powerful leg muscles for rapid acceleration.
    
- Stable footing on snow and ice.
    

Medical testing confirms Taiga remains comfortable in temperatures that would normally require heavy protective clothing for humans.

---

## **Snow Owl Thiren**

**Representative:** Mart

### Natural Traits

- Natural flight through large wings.
    
- Excellent aerial maneuverability.
    
- High resistance to cold environments.
    
- Stable vision while airborne.
    
- Efficient long-distance gliding with minimal fatigue.
    

Mart's ability to fly makes her one of the fastest responders within APRT-70 for tasks requiring vertical movement.

---

## **Behavioral Sciences Notes**

Although these species share certain characteristics, personality is not determined by Thiren type.

For example:

- Belle's kindness is personal, not a Rabbit Thiren trait.
    
- Endmin's tactical discipline comes from training, not being a Cat Thiren.
    
- Taiga's calm demeanor is unrelated to being a Snow Leopard Thiren.
    
- Mart's curiosity is individual rather than species-wide.
    

Researchers are reminded to avoid making assumptions based solely on Thiren classification.
