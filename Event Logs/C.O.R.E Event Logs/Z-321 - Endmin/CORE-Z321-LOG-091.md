# **BANLANDS ADMINISTRATION**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-091**

### **JOINT OPERATION – KRAMPUS COMPLIANCE AUTHORITY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Internal Affairs / Criminal Enforcement

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the conclusion of a Krampus Compliance Authority investigation into allegations of government corruption, bribery, abuse of office, and the manipulation of Banlands public officials, Director **Belle** and Central Execution Department Director **Endmin** were assigned to carry out the court-authorized sentence against the primary offender.

Neither officer was informed of the target's identity until arrival at the location.

Upon reaching the residence, Belle immediately recognized it as the house where she had spent her childhood.

The condemned individual was her biological father.

---

## **Arrival**

The transport came to a stop outside an aging residence.

Belle stood silently.

Endmin looked toward her.

> **"...You alright?"**

Belle nodded once.

> **"...Let's finish the mission."**

The two approached the front door together.

It opened before either of them knocked.

Belle's father froze.

His eyes shifted between Endmin and Belle.

He laughed nervously.

> **"...Miss Endmin..."**

His expression turned toward Belle with visible contempt.

> **"...What are you doing with that failure?"**

Silence.

Endmin slowly stepped forward.

Without warning—

**SMACK.**

The slap echoed through the entrance hall.

The man stumbled sideways, holding his cheek in shock.

Endmin lowered her hand.

Her voice was calm.

Cold.

> **"Watch your words."**

She took one more step.

> **"Your daughter is Director Belle."**

> **"Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority."**

> **"We're here to carry out your sentence."**

Belle never looked away from him.

One hand quietly rested on the hilt of her sword.

---

## **Formal Sentencing**

Belle drew the massive blade.

The room fell silent.

Her usual warmth had completely disappeared.

She spoke with the composure expected of the Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority.

> **"On behalf of the Krampus Compliance Authority..."**

> **"You have been found guilty of corruption, bribery, and the unlawful manipulation of Banlands government officials."**

She paused.

> **"Evidence presented before the Banlands High Tribunal further established that your coercive actions contributed to the suicide of a government officer who attempted to report your crimes."**

The only sound in the room was the quiet ringing of the bell attached to Belle's leg.

> **"By lawful order of the Banlands High Tribunal..."**

> **"You are hereby sentenced to death."**

---

## **Final Plea**

Before Belle moved, her mother collapsed to her knees.

She wrapped both hands around Belle's leg.

Tears streamed down her face.

> **"...Zhao..."**

> **"...Please..."**

> **"...Don't do this..."**

> **"...He's your father..."**

Belle remained completely still.

She looked down.

When she finally spoke, her voice carried no anger.

Only resolve.

> **"You lost the right to call me that."**

She gently freed her leg.

> **"I am Director Belle."**

> **"I came here because the law required it."**

Her gaze returned to the condemned man.

> **"If he had not chosen corruption..."**

> **"...if he had not manipulated innocent people..."**

> **"...if he had not driven a government officer to her death..."**

> **"...I would never have had to stand here."**

She tightened her grip on the sword.

> **"I don't want to hear another excuse."**

---

## **Sentence Carried Out**

Belle closed her eyes for only a moment.

Then opened them again.

There was no hesitation.

One clean motion.

One decisive strike.

The sentence was carried out immediately.

Medical personnel confirmed death at the scene.

No unnecessary force was employed.

No additional injuries occurred.

Belle quietly lowered her sword.

She remained motionless.

For several long seconds.

---

## **Scene Security**

Banlands Forensic Services entered the residence after the area was declared secure.

The scene was documented.

Evidence was collected.

The residence was placed under official seal pending further investigation.

Belle's mother was taken into custody for questioning regarding her knowledge of the corruption network and any potential involvement. No determination of criminal liability was made at the scene.

---

## **Aftermath**

As the forensic teams continued their work, Endmin quietly approached Belle.

She spoke softly.

> **"...Belle..."**

Belle didn't answer.

Endmin looked into her eyes.

> **"...Are you feeling alright?"**

Several seconds passed.

Then Belle finally smiled.

It was small.

But genuine.

> **"...I'm okay, Linder—"**

She immediately covered her mouth.

Endmin raised an eyebrow.

Belle laughed nervously.

> **"...I mean..."**

> **"...Endmin."**

Endmin couldn't help smiling.

> **"...Hard habit to break?"**

Belle rubbed the back of her head.

> **"...Yeah."**

For the first time that day...

She looked relieved.

---

## **Return to APRT-70**

The operation concluded without further incident.

Belle and Endmin returned to the Blacksite together later that evening.

Neither spoke much during the journey home.

As they entered Room N-07, Belle was quietly welcomed back by the Friendly Roommates.

No one asked about the mission.

No one needed to.

They simply made room for her.

Belle sat beside Endmin.

For a while, she said nothing.

Then, almost absentmindedly, she leaned against Endmin's shoulder.

Endmin didn't move away.

Outside, Belle was the unwavering Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority.

Inside Room N-07...

She was simply home.
