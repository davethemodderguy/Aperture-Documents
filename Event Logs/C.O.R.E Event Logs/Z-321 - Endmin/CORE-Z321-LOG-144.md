# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-144**

### **RECREATIONAL ACTIVITY REPORT – SNOW DAY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following several weeks of continuous operations between the Banlands and Heaven's Blacksite, Director Lucious approved an informal recreational period for visiting Banlands personnel.

Participants included:

- Belle Merryweather
    
- Lindervene "Endmin" Merryweather
    
- Keith
    
- Alice
    
- Cinder
    

The event was held in the Blacksite's snow-covered courtyard.

No injuries were reported.

Keith's dignity, however, suffered moderate casualties.

---

## **Beginning**

Fresh snow had blanketed the courtyard overnight.

Belle's rabbit ears immediately perked up the moment she looked outside.

> **"...Snow!"

She grabbed Endmin's hand.

> **"Come on!"

Before Endmin could answer, Belle was already sprinting across the courtyard, legs spinning so quickly they almost looked like little wheels.

**Fwip.**

Belle leapt several meters into the air.

**Boing!**

She landed with a soft bounce before immediately making another jump.

Alice smiled.

> **"...She's excited."

---

## **Snowball Fight**

Within minutes...

The first snowball flew.

Nobody admitted throwing it.

It struck Mart's snowman.

Belle gasped dramatically.

> **"...War."

---

Teams lasted approximately fifteen seconds before everyone began throwing snowballs at everyone else.

Endmin used a wall as cover, peeking out to launch remarkably accurate throws.

Alice calmly built snow barricades.

Belle darted across the field so quickly that half the snowballs missed her entirely.

Occasionally she would jump several meters into the air...

Only to land with a cheerful **boing** before continuing her charge.

---

## **Keith**

Keith remained exactly as expected.

Standing upright.

Scanning the surroundings.

Maintaining professional awareness.

Even while snowballs repeatedly bounced off his coat.

**Thump.**

No reaction.

**Thump.**

Still none.

Belle threw another.

Endmin threw one immediately afterward.

Alice joined in.

Keith simply brushed snow from one shoulder.

> **"...Are you all finished?"

---

## **Professionalism Lost**

Cinder quietly walked over carrying a perfectly packed snowball.

She looked at Belle.

Belle understood instantly.

Without saying a word...

Belle pointed toward Keith.

Cinder nodded.

The siblings locked eyes.

Keith immediately recognized the look.

> **"...Cinder."

Too late.

**Paf!**

The snowball landed squarely on Keith's face.

Silence.

Keith slowly wiped the snow away.

Everyone waited.

Then...

For the first time anyone at the Blacksite had ever witnessed...

Keith smiled.

A genuine smile.

He reached down.

Picked up an enormous amount of snow.

> **"...Very well."

Belle's ears stood straight up.

> **"...RUN!"

The courtyard erupted into laughter.

---

## **The Chase**

Belle sprinted in impossible zigzags.

Endmin skated across the snow.

Alice attempted to maintain order before immediately being hit by a stray snowball.

She laughed.

Keith pursued everyone with surprising speed for someone carrying military equipment.

Even Cinder couldn't stop laughing.

---

## **Revenge**

Nearly an hour later...

The fight finally ended.

Keith sat on a bench catching his breath.

Cinder quietly walked behind him.

She had noticed something over the past several weeks.

Keith had been accepting more overtime.

Skipping breaks.

Working through meals.

Barely resting.

She folded her arms.

> **"...Too busy with work lately?"

Keith looked over.

> **"...Occupational necessity."

Cinder smiled innocently.

> **"...I disagree."

Belle looked at Endmin.

Endmin looked at Alice.

Alice slowly smiled.

No words were exchanged.

The plan was immediate.

---

Several minutes later...

Keith found himself lying on the ground.

Belle was sitting on his legs.

Endmin held one arm.

Alice held the other.

Cinder calmly shoveled snow.

Keith sighed.

> **"...Mutiny."

Belle giggled.

> **"...Affection."

Another shovel of snow landed.

Soon only Keith's head remained visible.

Cinder placed a small carrot on top of the snow mound like a flag.

She crossed her arms proudly.

> **"...There."

Keith looked upward.

> **"...I deserved this."

Cinder nodded.

> **"...For ignoring your little sister."

Keith couldn't argue.

---

## **Personnel Comments**

### Belle

> **"...Best snow day ever!"

---

### Endmin

> **"...I've never seen Keith smile that much."

---

### Alice

Laughing quietly.

> **"...I don't think he'll ever live this down."

---

### Cinder

Standing beside the snow mound.

> **"...Next time..."

She smiled sweetly.

> **"...Take your breaks."

---

### Keith

Still buried beneath the snow.

After a long silence...

> **"...Understood."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Keith returned to duty shortly afterward, thoroughly covered in snow but otherwise unharmed.

Cinder declared her "revenge" complete.

Belle spent the rest of the afternoon bouncing through the snow, occasionally tackling Endmin into soft snowdrifts before immediately helping her back up.

Security camera footage of Keith—normally the embodiment of discipline—being buried alive in snow by his own sister has since become a favorite clip among authorized Blacksite personnel.

The recording remains archived under the unofficial title:

> **"Executive Protection Officer Defeated by Family."**
