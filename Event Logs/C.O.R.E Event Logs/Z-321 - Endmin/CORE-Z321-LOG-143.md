# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-143**

### **VISITING PERSONNEL OBSERVATION – CINDER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Residential & Administrative Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

Unlike her older brother Keith, whose visits to Heaven's Blacksite are typically brief and limited to escorting Director Belle Merryweather, **Cinder** frequently remains at the Blacksite for extended periods.

With approval from both the Krampus Compliance Authority and Aperture Industries, Cinder assists Belle with administrative work in the Director's satellite office while enjoying the quieter atmosphere of the Blacksite.

She has also become a familiar face to the Friendly Roommates.

---

## **Belle's Mini Office**

Belle's office near Room N-07 serves as a temporary workspace whenever she conducts official Krampus Compliance Authority business from the Blacksite.

During these visits, Cinder is almost always present.

Typical responsibilities include:

- Organizing incoming documents.
    
- Managing Belle's appointment schedule.
    
- Preparing reports for the Banlands government.
    
- Handling correspondence between the Blacksite and KCA Headquarters.
    
- Politely reminding Belle to finish paperwork before wandering off to chat with everyone.
    

Whenever Belle starts getting distracted, Cinder simply clears her throat.

> **"...Director."

Belle slowly turns around.

> **"...Five more minutes?"

Cinder smiles.

> **"...Three."

Belle sighs dramatically before returning to her paperwork.

---

## **A Familiar Visitor**

Outside of work, Cinder is noticeably more relaxed.

She often spends breaks in Room N-07, chatting with the Friendly Roommates over tea or snacks.

She is particularly fond of listening to the group's everyday conversations, finding them a welcome contrast to the formal environment of government offices.

---

## **A Fan of Endmin**

One detail quickly became obvious to everyone.

Cinder greatly admires Endmin.

Having heard countless stories about the Head of the Central Execution Department before meeting her, Cinder was genuinely excited when she finally had the opportunity to spend time with her at the Blacksite.

She often asks Endmin about:

- Field operations.
    
- Leadership responsibilities.
    
- Equipment used by the Central Execution Department.
    
- Funny stories from missions (when appropriate).
    

Endmin, though somewhat embarrassed by the attention, always answers patiently.

---

## **Observation Log**

Cinder entered Room N-07 carrying a small stack of completed documents.

Belle immediately signed the last page and happily pushed the pile back.

> **"...Done!"

Cinder smiled.

> **"...Good work, Director."

Across the room, Endmin was cleaning her rifle.

Cinder hesitated for a moment before walking over.

> **"...Miss Endmin..."

Endmin looked up.

> **"...Yes?"

> **"...Would you mind telling me how you became Director of the Central Execution Department?"

Endmin blinked.

Lucius quietly smiled.

Taiga nudged Mart.

Belle looked between them before whispering to Lucius.

> **"...She's a really big fan."

Lucius chuckled.

> **"...I noticed."

For the next half hour, Cinder listened intently while Endmin recounted parts of her career.

By the end of the conversation, Cinder's notebook contained several pages of carefully written notes.

Endmin scratched the back of her head.

> **"...You... took notes?"

Cinder nodded enthusiastically.

> **"...Every word."

Endmin looked thoroughly embarrassed.
