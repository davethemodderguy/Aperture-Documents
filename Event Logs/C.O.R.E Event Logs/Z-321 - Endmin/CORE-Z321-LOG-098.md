# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-098**

### **RESIDENTIAL ADVISORY – BELLE'S FORMER NAME**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Personnel Welfare / Behavioral Sciences

---

## **SUMMARY**

Behavioral Sciences has issued the following advisory regarding Belle's former name.

Belle was known by the name **"Zhao"** during her childhood.

Due to significant negative experiences associated with that period of her life, she has chosen to leave that name behind.

Personnel are instructed to address her as **Belle** at all times.

---

## **Background**

The name "Zhao" is closely associated with a period in Belle's life marked by prolonged emotional abuse and neglect.

After being taken in by Lucy Merryweather and later becoming part of Endmin's family, Belle adopted the name **Belle** as the identity she wished to live by.

For Belle, the change represents more than a nickname.

It represents a new beginning.

---

## **Behavioral Response**

Behavioral Sciences has documented that unexpectedly hearing her former name can trigger an immediate emotional response.

Possible reactions include:

- Sudden silence.
    
- Visible distress.
    
- Crying.
    
- Emotional withdrawal.
    
- Running away from the situation.
    

These reactions are not expressions of anger.

They are trauma responses connected to painful memories.

---

## **Residential Guidance**

When speaking with Belle:

- Address her as **Belle**.
    
- If referencing events from her childhood, continue using **Belle** unless discussing official historical records where accuracy requires otherwise.
    
- If another individual accidentally uses her former name, gently correct the mistake and continue addressing her as Belle.
    

Respecting her chosen name is considered part of maintaining a supportive residential environment.
