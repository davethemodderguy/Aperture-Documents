# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-048**

### **EQUIPMENT LOAN – ENDMIN'S MOBILITY BOOTS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Recreational Observation

---

## **SUMMARY**

During a routine afternoon in Room N-07, Z-321 (Endmin) temporarily loaned her custom Mobility Boots to Z-885 (Taiga).

The exchange was entirely voluntary and was intended to let Taiga experience skating with the integrated booster system.

Until the boots were returned, Endmin simply wore a normal pair of shoes.

---

## **The Loan**

Taiga had spent several days admiring Endmin's skating.

Eventually, she asked the obvious question.

> **Taiga:** "Can I try them?"

Endmin looked down at the boots.

Then at Taiga.

> "...Sure."

Without hesitation, she removed the boots and handed them over.

She slipped into an ordinary pair of athletic shoes instead.

> **Endmin:** "Just..."

She paused.

> "...Don't crash."

Taiga grinned.

> "No promises!"

---

## **Initial Testing**

Engineering recalibrated the boots to fit Taiga's feet before use.

Basic skating came naturally thanks to Taiga's athleticism.

The booster...

...was another story.

The moment she activated it—

She shot several meters forward.

> **Taiga:** "AAAAAAAA—"

She disappeared around a hallway corner.

A loud but harmless crash echoed through the corridor.

---

## **Damage Report**

Damage sustained:

- One overturned recycling bin.
    
- Three traffic cones.
    
- One bruised ego.
    

No injuries were reported.

Neko declared the test a success.

---

## **Endmin's Perspective**

While Taiga continued practicing, Endmin quietly walked through the Blacksite in regular shoes.

Several researchers noticed something unusual.

She looked...

...slow.

One junior researcher commented,

> "I've never actually seen Endmin walk before."

Endmin blinked.

> "...I do walk."

The researcher thought for a moment.

> "...You usually skate."

Endmin looked down at her shoes.

> "...Huh."

She hadn't realized it herself.

---

Endmin.

Endmin later admitted that was the first compliment she had ever received about her shoes.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

The Mobility Boots remain Endmin's primary footwear.

Temporary loans are permitted only under Engineering supervision due to the integrated booster and retractable skate mechanisms.

Taiga has already requested to borrow them again.

Endmin has not yet decided.

---

### **Closing Observation**

That evening, Taiga returned the boots.

She carefully placed them in front of Endmin.

> **Taiga:** "Thanks."

Endmin smiled.

> "...Did you have fun?"

Taiga nodded enthusiastically.

> "Best boots ever."

Endmin quietly put them back on.

With a familiar click, the wheels deployed beneath her feet.

She rolled a short distance across the floor.

Then looked back at Taiga.

> "...I missed these."

Taiga laughed.

> "...I could tell."

From across the room, Mart looked up from his book.

> "Welcome back... to having the fastest person in the Blacksite."

Endmin smiled sheepishly.

She didn't deny it.
