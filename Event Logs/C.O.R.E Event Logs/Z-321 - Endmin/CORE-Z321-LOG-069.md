# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-069**

### **PERSONAL EQUIPMENT DEMONSTRATION – LUCY'S INSPECTION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Engineering / Residential

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the successful field evaluation of her modular underbarrel shotgun system, Z-321 (Endmin) invited Z-945 (Lucy) to inspect the completed modification.

The meeting took place at the outdoor firing range during one of Lucy's scheduled visits to the Blacksite.

---

## **Observation**

Lucy found Endmin seated at a shooting bench, carefully cleaning her rifle after a morning practice session.

As usual, every component had already been wiped spotless.

The bolt rested open.

The underbarrel assembly gleamed beneath the rifle's barrel.

Hearing footsteps, Endmin looked up.

Her ears perked slightly.

> **Endmin:**
> 
> "Mom."

Lucy smiled.

> **"I heard you've been making modifications."**

Endmin nodded.

Without a word, she picked up the rifle and handed it over.

---

## **Inspection**

Lucy accepted the rifle with the practiced confidence of someone who had spent years around firearms.

She immediately noticed the enlarged handguard.

Her fingers traced the magazine tube beneath the barrel.

She smiled.

> **"...An underbarrel shotgun."**

Endmin nodded proudly.

> **"The idea was mine."**

She pointed toward Neko's engraved signature near the rear of the assembly.

> **"...Neko machined the parts."

Lucy examined the clean fit between the original rifle and the new mechanism.

No unnecessary bulk.

No exposed linkage.

No rattling.

She worked the shotgun action.

**Clack.**

Smooth.

She cycled it several more times.

Each movement locked firmly into place.

> **Lucy:**
> 
> "...Good craftsmanship."

Endmin's tail gave a small, pleased swish.

---

## **Demonstration**

Endmin loaded a single shotgun shell into the tubular magazine.

She shouldered the rifle.

Flipped the repurposed selector.

The shotgun action came into battery.

She aimed at a close-range steel target.

**BOOM.**

The target rang loudly.

She immediately switched back to rifle configuration.

Worked the bolt.

A distant target nearly a kilometer away was struck moments later.

Lucy watched the seamless transition.

> **"...Close quarters and long range."

Endmin lowered the rifle.

> **"Exactly."

> "One weapon."

> "Two jobs."

---

## **Discussion**

Lucy handed the rifle back.

> **Lucy:**
> 
> "You've learned a lot since coming here."

Endmin looked down at the weapon.

She smiled.

> **"Back home..."

> "...I would've just carried another gun."

She looked toward Engineering through the range windows.

> **"Neko taught me that sometimes..."

She gently rested a hand on the new handguard.

> **"...it's better to improve what you already trust."

Lucy nodded approvingly.

> **"...A lesson worth learning."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Lucy's inspection concluded with no mechanical concerns.

The underbarrel shotgun system remains approved for Endmin's personal use.

Engineering notes that the modification continues to perform reliably under live-fire conditions.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Before leaving the range, Lucy paused.

She looked once more at the rifle slung across Endmin's back.

Then she smiled.

> **Lucy:**
> 
> "You've built something that's truly yours."

Endmin adjusted the sling on her shoulder.

Her white ears flicked happily.

> **"...Not quite."

Lucy raised an eyebrow.

Endmin grinned.

> **"...Version three is already in my notebook."

Lucy sighed, trying not to laugh.

> **"...You're spending too much time with Neko."

From somewhere inside the Engineering workshop came Neko's voice.

> **"I heard that!"**

Both mother and daughter burst into laughter.
