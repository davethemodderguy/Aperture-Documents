# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-117**

### **PERSONNEL OBSERVATION – LUCIUS MERRYWEATHER AND MOONLIGHT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Residential / Biological Observation

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following Lucius Merryweather's reintegration into Aperture Industries, personnel noticed a recurring behavior associated with his Wolf Thiren traits.

During clear nights, Lucius often seeks areas where moonlight is visible.

This behavior is not considered abnormal.

It appears to be a natural instinctive response connected to his Thiren physiology and personal memories.

---

## **Nighttime Routine**

When the Blacksite is quiet, Lucius can often be found:

- Sitting near observation windows.
    
- Walking through outdoor areas during the night.
    
- Painting under moonlight.
    
- Simply watching the sky in silence.
    

Unlike his survival habits from his years missing, these moments are not caused by fear or the need to stay alert.

He does it because it brings him comfort.

---

## **Room N-07 Observation**

The first time the Friendly Roommates noticed, Taiga found Lucius sitting outside late at night with his sketchbook.

The moon reflected across the snow while he painted.

She approached carefully.

> **"Couldn't sleep?"**

Lucius looked up.

> **"No."

He glanced back at the moon.

> **"I just like seeing it."

Taiga sat beside him.

> **"Wolf thing?"**

Lucius smiled slightly.

> **"Probably."

> **"...Or maybe I just missed having something peaceful to look at."**

---

## **Endmin's Reaction**

Endmin eventually learned about the habit.

She immediately remembered their childhood.

Before everything changed, Lucius would sometimes take her outside during quiet nights and show her the moon.

He told her stories while they sat together.

When she found him doing the same thing years later, she quietly sat beside him.

> **"You still do this."**

Lucius looked surprised.

> **"You remember?"

Endmin nodded.

> **"Of course I remember."**

---

## **Wolf Thiren Traits**

Aperture's research division notes that Lucius displays several traits commonly associated with his Wolf Thiren heritage.

Observed behaviors include:

- Heightened awareness at night.
    
- Preference for colder environments.
    
- Strong attachment to familiar individuals.
    
- Increased relaxation under moonlight.
    
- Protective instincts toward family and companions.
    

Researchers have noted that these traits are not limitations.

They are simply part of who Lucius is.
