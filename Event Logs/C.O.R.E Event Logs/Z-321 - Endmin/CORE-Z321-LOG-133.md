# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-133**

### **PERSONNEL WELFARE OBSERVATION – BELLE MERRYWEATHER'S WITHDRAWAL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Personnel Welfare

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the previously documented changes in Belle Merryweather's behavior, personnel have observed a continued pattern of social withdrawal.

While Belle's performance as Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority remains exemplary, she has gradually begun distancing herself from the people she once spent the most time with.

No official explanation has been provided.

When asked, Belle continues to insist that she is simply busy.

---

## **Observed Behavioral Changes**

Over several weeks, the following changes became increasingly apparent:

- Spending significantly less time in Room N-07.
    
- Avoiding crowded lounges and common areas.
    
- Declining invitations to casual gatherings.
    
- Returning to Krampus Compliance Authority Headquarters more frequently.
    
- Remaining in her office long after normal working hours.
    
- Choosing paperwork over social activities whenever possible.
    

Despite these changes, Belle has remained courteous and professional toward everyone she meets.

---

## **Room N-07**

One evening, everyone gathered for dinner.

Taiga counted the seats.

> **"...Belle's missing again."

Endmin glanced at the empty chair.

> **"...She said she had work."

Lucius looked at the clock.

> **"...Again?"

Nobody answered.

The chair remained empty throughout the meal.

---

## **Krampus Compliance Authority Headquarters**

Several KCA officers reported arriving early in the morning only to find Belle already in her office.

Others reported leaving late at night...

Only to discover she was still there.

One senior officer politely asked,

> **"Director, you've been here since yesterday."

Belle looked up from another stack of documents.

> **"...There's still work to do."

The officer hesitated.

> **"...You should probably go home."

Belle smiled faintly.

> **"...I will."

She returned to reading.

Hours later...

She was still there.

---

## **Endmin's Visit**

Unable to ignore the pattern any longer, Endmin traveled to KCA Headquarters.

She knocked softly on Belle's office door.

> **"...Come in."

Belle was sitting behind her desk.

Documents covered nearly every available surface.

Endmin looked around.

> **"...You've been here all day?"

Belle smiled.

> **"...Mostly."

> **"...You should come back to the Blacksite."

Belle looked toward the window.

After several seconds, she quietly replied,

> **"...I still will."

She paused.

> **"...Just... not today."

Endmin didn't argue.

She simply sat in the chair across from her.

The two worked in silence for nearly an hour.

Neither of them seemed to mind.

## **Director Lucious' Note**

Director Lucious reviewed the welfare reports before writing a brief observation.

> **"Dedication is admirable."**

> **"Isolation is not."**

He authorized Belle to continue performing her duties without interference but instructed supervisory personnel to quietly monitor her wellbeing.

No mandatory leave was issued.

No formal counseling was ordered.

Instead, he added one final sentence.

> **"When someone who once sought the company of others begins choosing solitude every day..."**

> **"...it is worth asking why."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Belle continues to fulfill all responsibilities as Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority with exceptional professionalism.

However, her increasing preference for solitude, extended hours at headquarters, and absence from the Friendly Roommates have become impossible to ignore.

The office at KCA Headquarters has become the place she spends most of her time.

Room N-07 still has a bed waiting for her.

Lately...

It has remained neatly made.
