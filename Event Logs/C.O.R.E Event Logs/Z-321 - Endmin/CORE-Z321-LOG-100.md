# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-100**

### **FACILITY OBSERVATION – BELLE'S WORKPLACE & DAILY ROUTINE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Residential / Administrative

---

## **SUMMARY**

To facilitate rapid deployment and coordination between Banlands Administration and Aperture Industries, a shared administrative office has been established adjacent to **Room N-07**.

The office is jointly occupied by:

- **Director Endmin** — Central Execution Department (CED)
    
- **Director Belle** — Krampus Compliance Authority (KCA)
    

Although they oversee different organizations, their offices are connected due to the frequency of their cooperation.

---

## **Shared Office**

The office is located only a short walk from Room N-07.

The layout consists of two primary workspaces.

### **Endmin's Side**

Contains:

- Weapon maintenance bench.
    
- Mission planning terminal.
    
- Tactical maps.
    
- Rifle storage.
    
- Ammunition cabinet.
    
- Skate maintenance tools.
    

A baseball bat usually rests beside her desk.

---

### **Belle's Side**

Contains:

- Government investigation files.
    
- Corruption case archives.
    
- Personnel reports.
    
- Audit documentation.
    
- Banlands legal code.
    
- A reinforced weapon stand designed specifically for her oversized hybrid sword.
    

Unlike Endmin's workspace, Belle's desk is remarkably tidy.

The only decorations include:

- A framed photograph of the Friendly Roommates.
    
- A plush rabbit.
    
- Several small gifts received from her friends.
    
- A mug labeled:
    

> **"World's Shortest Director."**

Belle insists she did not buy the mug.

---

## **Barefoot**

Belle does not wear shoes during normal duties.

Medical Division has confirmed this presents no health concerns.

Rabbit Thiren anatomy provides thick, soft fur covering the bottoms of her feet, naturally cushioning her steps and protecting against ordinary walking surfaces.

Belle simply finds footwear uncomfortable.

As a result, she almost always walks barefoot unless mission equipment specifically requires protective boots.

---

## **Housekeeping Notice**

Because Belle's feet are covered in soft fur, Residential Services and Engineering maintain particularly high cleanliness standards in areas she regularly visits.

Cleaning schedules prioritize removing:

- Metal shavings.
    
- Glass fragments.
    
- Loose screws.
    
- Sharp debris.
    
- Engineering offcuts.
    

This policy is intended to prevent accidental injury rather than accommodate unusual vulnerability.

Engineering personnel have jokingly nicknamed the practice:

> **"The Belle Sweep."**

---

## **A Busy Director**

Belle spends much of her day reviewing investigations and signing official documentation.

Throughout the day, agents and senior officers from the Krampus Compliance Authority frequently visit the office carrying additional reports.

Common phrases overheard include:

> **"Director Belle, today's audit report."

> "Chief, another corruption investigation requires authorization."

> "Miss Belle, we need your signature."

Belle patiently works through every document before moving to the next.

---

## **The Height Jokes**

Despite Belle's authority, her own officers occasionally make harmless jokes about her height.

One afternoon, a senior investigator placed a report on the highest shelf before saying,

> **"...Oops."

Belle looked up.

Then looked at him.

Without saying a word...

She pointed.

The investigator sighed dramatically, retrieved the folder himself, and handed it to her.

Belle smiled sweetly.

> **"...Thank you."

The office burst into laughter.

The investigator later admitted,

> **"...I probably deserved that."

Behavioral Sciences notes that Belle only tolerates this sort of teasing from people she knows well and trusts. Professional conduct is still expected in formal settings.

### **Closing Observation**

Late one afternoon, Endmin looked up from cleaning her rifle.

Belle was buried beneath a stack of investigation files almost as tall as she was.

Only her rabbit ears were visible.

Endmin quietly stood, walked over, and lifted the top half of the stack away.

Belle blinked.

> **"...Thanks."

Endmin smiled.

> **"...Couldn't find the Director."

Belle laughed.

Just then, one of her senior investigators walked through the door carrying another folder.

He looked at the mountain of paperwork.

Then at Belle.

> **"...Morning, Director."

He paused with a grin.

> **"...Need a taller desk?"

Belle puffed out her cheeks.

> **"...I could sentence you to filing paperwork for a month."

The investigator saluted dramatically.

> **"...Worth it."

Even Endmin couldn't hold back a laugh.
