# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-124**

### **TRAINING RECORD – "EVERYONE HAS THEIR OWN DROPKICK"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Recreational Training Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

During a break between scheduled training exercises, the Friendly Roommates somehow ended up discussing one very specific topic:

**"If you had to dropkick someone... how would you do it?"**

The discussion quickly turned into a friendly demonstration.

Training drones volunteered as targets.

---

## **Taiga**

Taiga stretched her legs.

> **"Simple."

She lowered her stance.

Then exploded into a full sprint.

Without slowing down, she launched herself feet-first into the practice drone.

**CRASH!**

The drone tumbled across the floor.

> **"...Momentum."

Taiga smiled proudly.

---

## **Mart**

Mart unfolded his wings.

> **"Mine's a little different."

He flew down the length of the training hall, steadily accelerating.

Just before reaching the drone—

He tucked his wings.

**THUD!**

The flying dropkick launched the drone several meters backward.

Mart landed cleanly.

---

## **Star**

Star tilted her head.

> **"...I don't really run."

Everyone watched.

She disappeared.

A flash of light.

She reappeared directly above the drone.

> **"...Hi."

**BONK!**

The dropkick sent the drone rolling.

She landed gracefully.

> **"...Teleporting saves time."

---

## **Nightshade**

Everyone looked at Nightshade.

She looked back.

After several seconds...

> **"...No comment."

No one questioned it.

---

## **Frost**

Frost looked at the drone.

Then raised a hand.

Several ice projectiles launched forward in rapid succession.

The drone froze solid before toppling over.

Taiga blinked.

> **"...That wasn't really a dropkick."

Frost shrugged.

> **"...It worked."

---

## **Bao**

Everyone turned toward Bao.

She smiled politely.

> **"...No comment."

Nobody pressed the issue.

---

## **Endmin**

Endmin rested her rifle against her shoulder.

> **"...Alright."

She sprinted forward.

Halfway there, she planted the butt of her rifle against the floor like a vaulting pole.

The maneuver launched her upward.

She twisted through the air.

Then—

**WHAM!**

Her boots connected squarely with the training drone.

She landed lightly, retrieved her rifle, and calmly slung it back over her shoulder.

Taiga applauded.

> **"...That was awesome."

---

## **Belle**

Everyone eagerly looked at Belle.

Belle smiled innocently.

She rested her enormous sword on her shoulder.

> **"...Okay!"

She sprinted.

Her rabbit legs blurred beneath her.

Then she jumped.

Much higher than anyone expected.

At the peak of the jump—

She drove her oversized sword downward, pinning the practice drone in place.

Using the sword as a brace, she pushed off.

**THUMP!**

Her kick struck the drone squarely in the chest, sending it flying free of the blade.

Mission accomplished.

Belle, however, had committed fully to the jump.

She landed on her backside with a soft **poof**, her fluffy fur cushioning the impact.

She blinked once.

Then smiled sheepishly.

> **"...I meant to do that."

The room erupted into laughter.

Taiga was laughing so hard she had to sit down.

Mart covered his face with his wings, trying not to laugh.

Even Nightshade's expression softened into a faint smile.

Belle simply stood back up, dusted off her dress, lifted her 50-kilogram sword onto her shoulder as if it weighed nothing, and proudly declared:

> **"...Ten out of ten landing."

---

> **"...And adorable at the same time?"

---

### Neko

He paused.

> **"...Barely."

---

### Miss Neko

---

### Director Lucious

After reviewing the training footage, Director Lucious added:

> **"Only this group could turn a discussion about dropkicks into a legitimate training exercise."**

A second note was added shortly afterward.

> **"Engineering has requested fewer demonstrations involving planted swords in the training floor."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

No personnel were injured during the exercise.

Three practice drones require repair.

One section of the training floor needs resurfacing after repeated impacts.

Belle continues to insist her landing was intentional.

No one believes her.

Belle continues to insist anyway.
