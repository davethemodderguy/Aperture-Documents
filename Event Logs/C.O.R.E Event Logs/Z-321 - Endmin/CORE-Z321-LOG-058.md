# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-058**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION – "WARDEN'S DIGNITY"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Psychological / Residential

---

## **SUMMARY**

Behavioral Sciences has documented a recurring personality trait in Z-321 (Endmin).

Whenever she enjoys something she personally considers **"unbefitting of a Banlands Warden,"** she becomes visibly embarrassed and attempts to appear tougher or more professional.

These attempts have, to date, been almost entirely unsuccessful.

---

## **Background**

Before becoming a Blacksite resident, Endmin served as the **Endministrator** of the Banlands alongside her mother, Lucy.

Although she now lives a much more peaceful life, Endmin still carries many of the expectations she placed upon herself during that time.

She believes a Banlands Warden should appear:

- Composed.
    
- Professional.
    
- Fearless.
    
- Disciplined.
    

Unfortunately for her...

She also genuinely enjoys a great many simple, ordinary things.

---

## **Documented Examples**

### Vegetables

Observed behavior:

- Happily orders salads.
    
- Prefers vegetables over many meat dishes.
    

Observed response after being noticed:

> **"This is a professional taste test."**

---

### Cats

Observed behavior:

- Immediately kneels to pet any friendly cat.
    
- Smiles noticeably around Aster.
    
- Has been observed talking to Aster for extended periods.
    

Observed response after someone points it out:

> **"...I'm conducting morale inspections."**

Aster later wrote on her whiteboard:

> **"Liar."**

---

### Skating

Observed behavior:

- Clearly enjoys skating.
    
- Smiles while racing Taiga.
    

Observed response:

> **"...This is mobility training."**

Taiga replied:

> "...You were doing spins."

---

### Candy

Despite often carrying mint candy to freshen her breath, Endmin has developed a liking for sweets after trying them at the Blacksite.

When caught choosing a lollipop from the cafeteria bowl:

> **"...It's... medicinal."

Medical Division confirmed there are no medicinal lollipops.

---

## **Attempting to Look Tough**

Whenever embarrassed, Endmin instinctively straightens her posture.

She folds her arms.

Speaks in a lower voice.

Attempts to look intimidating.

Unfortunately...

Her roommates know her far too well.

---

## **Typical Interaction**

> **Taiga:** "Aww, you're petting Aster again!"

Endmin immediately withdrew her hand.

Straightened her back.

Folded her arms.

> **Endmin:** "...Negative."

Aster looked up.

Then held up her whiteboard.

> **"You were."**

Silence.

Taiga laughed first.

Mart followed.

Even Neko smiled.

Endmin quietly accepted defeat.

---

## **Lucy's Opinion**

Lucy was informed of the phenomenon during one of her visits.

She listened patiently.

Then looked at her daughter.

Who was attempting to look intimidating while secretly eating cucumber slices.

Lucy smiled.

> **"Endmin."**

> **"A Warden doesn't stop being a Warden because she likes vegetables."**

Endmin looked away.

> "...I know."

Lucy gently patted her head.

> **"Then stop pretending."**

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Behavioral Sciences considers Endmin's embarrassment to be a harmless result of adjusting from a lifetime of responsibility to an environment where she is free to enjoy ordinary hobbies.

Staff have been asked not to tease her excessively.

That request has been... only partially successful.

---

### **Closing Observation**

One evening, Taiga and Endmin were sitting together in the greenhouse.

Each had a bowl of fresh salad.

Endmin looked around cautiously.

Satisfied that no one was watching, she happily took a bite.

From behind a nearby flower pot came the sound of a marker squeaking.

Aster slowly raised her whiteboard.

It read:

> **"Having another professional taste test?"**

Endmin froze.

Her face immediately turned red.

She sighed.

Then quietly laughed.

> **"...Fine."

> "...I just like vegetables."**

Taiga grinned.

> **"See? That wasn't so hard."**

Endmin smiled sheepishly.

> **"...Don't get used to it."**
