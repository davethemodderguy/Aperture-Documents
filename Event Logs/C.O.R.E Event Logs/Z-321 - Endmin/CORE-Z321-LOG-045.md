# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-045**

### **INTRODUCTION – NIKO & THE WARDEN**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential Interaction Log

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following his move into Room N-07, Niko was formally introduced to Z-321 (Endmin) and, during one of her routine visits, Z-945 (Lucy).

The meeting was uneventful, cordial, and quickly became another example of the relaxed atmosphere within Room N-07.

---

## **Initial Introduction**

Niko found Endmin in the workshop, where she was cleaning her sniper rifle while wearing her round glasses.

He knocked lightly on the open door.

> **Niko:** "Mind if I come in?"

Endmin looked up from her work.

> **Endmin:** "Sure."

Niko walked over and smiled.

> **Niko:** "Guess we never properly introduced ourselves."

He extended a hand.

> **Niko:** "I'm Niko."

> "Neko and Miss Neko's older brother."

Endmin shook his hand.

> **Endmin:** "I'm Endmin."

> "...It's nice to finally meet you."

Niko chuckled.

> "I've heard a lot about you."

Endmin tilted her head.

> "...Hopefully good things."

> **Niko:** "Mostly about your impossible aim."

Endmin smiled modestly.

> "...That's fair."

---

## **Lucy's Visit**

Several hours later, Lucy arrived for one of her regular visits to see Endmin.

She entered Room N-07 and noticed an unfamiliar face sitting near the window, reading a book.

Niko stood respectfully.

> **Niko:** "You must be Lucy."

Lucy nodded.

> **Lucy:** "And you're Niko."

> "I've heard your siblings mention you."

Niko smiled.

> "Hopefully they didn't exaggerate too much."

Lucy gave a faint smile.

> "Not at all."

The two shook hands.

Lucy immediately noticed the subtle spiritual energy surrounding him.

> **Lucy:** "You're a ghost."

> **Niko:** "That's right."

> "Nothing mysterious about it."

Lucy nodded.

> "I've met many spirits."

> "You're... surprisingly peaceful."

Niko laughed softly.

> "I take that as a compliment."

---

## **A Conversation**

The four—Lucy, Endmin, Niko, and later Neko—sat together over tea.

The discussion drifted between everyday life, the Blacksite, and the Banlands.

Lucy eventually asked,

> **Lucy:** "How are you settling in?"

Niko glanced around Room N-07.

Taiga was trying to convince Mart that gliding counted as flying.

Star was painting near the window.

Nightshade was pretending not to listen while quietly correcting Star's brush technique.

Astral was making another pot of tea.

Frost was arranging flowers.

Niko smiled.

> **Niko:** "It's... lively."

Lucy looked around and quietly laughed.

> "That's one way to describe it."

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Niko has become well acquainted with both Endmin and Lucy.

Lucy considers him a trustworthy resident and has expressed confidence in his presence within Room N-07.

Endmin has also grown comfortable around him, often inviting him to join the group during meals or evening conversations.

---

### **Closing Observation**

As Lucy prepared to leave through the Banlands rift, she looked back toward Room N-07.

Niko was sitting by the window.

Endmin was beside him, quietly polishing her rifle.

Neither of them felt the need to fill the silence with words.

Lucy smiled to herself.

> **"She has another family looking after her."**

Satisfied, she stepped through the rift and disappeared.

The portal closed, leaving Room N-07 just as it had been moments before—peaceful, crowded, and unmistakably home.
