# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-062**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION – INVOLUNTARY THIREN RESPONSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Behavioral / Medical

---

## **SUMMARY**

Behavioral Sciences has identified a series of **involuntary Thiren responses** exhibited by Z-321 (Endmin) during moments of extreme surprise, fear, or acute stress.

Unlike her voluntary Ether concealment, these reactions cannot be consciously suppressed.

Medical Division considers them to be instinctive reflexes rather than anomalous behavior.

---

## **Observed Responses**

Under normal circumstances, Endmin maintains excellent composure.

However, if startled severely or frightened unexpectedly, several instinctive behaviors occur almost simultaneously.

Documented responses include:

- Her concealed cat ears immediately become visible as her Ether illusion momentarily drops.
    
- Her tail reflexively slips free from beneath her folded wings.
    
- Her ears flatten or rotate toward the source of the disturbance.
    
- Her pupils briefly widen.
    
- She may involuntarily vocalize with a short **"Mew!"** or startled **"Meow?!"**
    

The vocalization is entirely unintentional.

According to Endmin, she often doesn't realize she has done it until everyone else reacts.

---

## **Incident Example**

During a routine afternoon in Room N-07, Endmin was quietly reading maintenance notes for her rifle.

Without warning, Taiga enthusiastically hugged her from behind.

> **Endmin:** "M-MEOW?!"

The room fell silent.

Endmin slowly froze.

She reached toward the top of her head.

Her ears were visible.

Her tail had slipped free.

She closed her eyes.

Very slowly.

> "...You..."

She sighed.

> "...Didn't hear that."

Nobody answered.

Taiga was trying very hard not to laugh.

Mart had already buried his face behind his book.

Neko quietly turned away, shoulders shaking.

Miss Neko politely covered her smile.

Aster simply held up her whiteboard.

> **"I heard it."**

Endmin let out the deepest sigh recorded in the observation log.

---

## **Medical Assessment**

Medical Division concluded that these responses are equivalent to instinctive startle reflexes seen in many Thiren species.

Attempts to consciously suppress them are largely ineffective during moments of genuine surprise.

No treatment is recommended or considered necessary.
