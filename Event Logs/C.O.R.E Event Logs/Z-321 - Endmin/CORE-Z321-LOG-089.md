# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-089**

### **RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION – BELLE'S CLOTHING & DAILY LIFE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Personnel Welfare

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following Belle's integration into APRT-70, Residential Services conducted a routine assessment regarding her clothing preferences and comfort.

The assessment unexpectedly highlighted one of the quality-of-life differences between the Banlands and Aperture Industries.

It also explained why Belle had always been seen wearing oversized children's clothing.

---

## **Clothing Preferences**

As a Full-Body Rabbit Thiren, Belle has always struggled to find comfortable clothing.

According to Belle, most clothing designed for Thirens in the Banlands suffers from three common problems:

- Expensive to purchase.
    
- Rough and itchy against thick fur.
    
- Limited availability in smaller sizes.
    

Rather than tolerate uncomfortable clothing, Belle developed her own solution.

She simply purchased oversized children's dresses and carefully modified them herself by creating an opening for her tail.

When asked if she found the arrangement embarrassing, Belle shook her head.

> **"...It's comfy."

> **"...That's what matters."

---

## **Discovery at APRT-70**

During a visit to Residential Services, Belle happened to notice a catalog produced by Aperture Industries.

One page immediately caught her attention.

The catalog featured clothing specifically tailored for residents with animal traits, including accommodations for ears, tails, wings, and various body types.

Belle blinked.

Then looked up at the staff member.

> **"...You make clothes for Thirens?"

The employee nodded.

> **"Of course."

> **"Comfort matters."

Belle stared at the catalog for several more seconds.

Her rabbit ears stood straight up.

> **"...Really?"

---

## **A Happy Rabbit**

Later that afternoon, Belle received her first properly fitted outfit.

The fabric was soft.

Breathable.

Designed to avoid irritating thick fur.

It fit perfectly around her ears and tail without requiring any modifications.

Belle quietly looked at herself in a mirror.

Then she smiled.

A very, very big smile.

Without saying a word...

She ran straight through the residential wing looking for Endmin.

---

## **A Thank You**

Belle found Endmin performing maintenance on her rifle.

She immediately hugged her.

> **"...Look!"

Endmin looked up.

She smiled warmly.

> **"...It suits you."

Belle happily spun in a circle.

The small bell attached to her leg jingled with every turn.

> **"...It's so comfortable!"

Endmin laughed.

> **"...Told you Aperture takes care of its people."

---

## **Behavioral Observation**

Behavioral Sciences has noted that Belle possesses an unusually positive effect on those around her.

Staff frequently describe her as:

- Cheerful.
    
- Encouraging.
    
- Optimistic.
    
- Easy to approach.
    
- Comforting.
    

Several researchers have informally referred to Belle as a **"positivity magnet,"** noting that her enthusiasm often improves the mood of those nearby.

---

## **Protective Instinct**

Despite her cheerful personality, Belle remains deeply committed to her role as Endmin's bodyguard.

Whenever Endmin leaves Room N-07, Belle is often only a few steps behind.

Whether Endmin is:

- Skating through the corridors.
    
- Visiting Engineering.
    
- Training.
    
- Reading quietly in the greenhouse.
    
- Heading to the firing range.
    

Belle is usually nearby.

Not because Endmin asked.

Because Belle wants to be.

When questioned about it, Belle answered simply:

> **"...She's my best friend."

She smiled.

> **"...Protecting her makes me happy."

Endmin, standing beside her, quietly looked away as her face turned red.

> **"...You don't have to announce it..."

Belle hugged her anyway.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Belle now wears clothing produced by Aperture Industries that is specifically designed for Full-Body Thirens.

She has happily retired the practice of modifying oversized children's dresses for daily wear, though she has kept several of her old dresses as personal keepsakes.

Belle continues serving as Endmin's personal bodyguard and is most often seen walking—sometimes running, sometimes tumbling—faithfully at Endmin's side.

---

### **Closing Observation**

One evening, Endmin skated through the residential corridor on her way back to Room N-07.

Behind her came the familiar sound of a tiny bell.

Jingle.

Jingle.

Jingle.

Belle jogged after her in one of her new outfits, smiling from ear to ear.

A passing researcher watched the pair disappear around the corner.

> **Researcher:**
> 
> "...Does Belle ever leave Endmin's side?"

Alice smiled.

> **"...Only when Endmin tells her to."

A few seconds later, laughter echoed down the hallway.

Belle had tripped over her own oversized sword again.

Endmin immediately turned around, helped her back to her feet, and the two continued walking together as if nothing had happened.

For most people, Belle was the Banlands' anti-corruption chief.

For Endmin, she was simply a loyal friend who always made the day a little brighter.
