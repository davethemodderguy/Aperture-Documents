# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-088**

### **RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION – ALICE, THE TRUSTED CARETAKER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Personnel

---

## **SUMMARY**

Due to her responsibilities as the Warden of the Banlands, Lucy is occasionally required to remain at Merryweather Manor or travel throughout the Banlands for extended periods.

During these times, **Alice**, Head of Security of Merryweather Manor, routinely checks on Endmin to ensure her wellbeing and to assist with anything Endmin may need.

While this arrangement began as a professional responsibility, it has gradually become a long-standing family tradition.

---

## **A Familiar Face**

Whenever Lucy informed Endmin that work would keep her occupied, Endmin already knew what would happen next.

Soon enough, there would be a knock at the door.

Alice would step inside.

Usually carrying groceries, paperwork from the manor, or something Lucy had asked her to deliver.

> **Alice:**
> 
> "Lady Lucy asked me to make sure you're doing alright."

Endmin would smile.

> "...Thanks, Alice."

---

## **More Than Security**

Although Alice serves as the **Head of Security** for Merryweather Manor, her responsibilities around the Merryweather family extend beyond protecting the estate.

When Lucy is unavailable, Alice is trusted to:

- Check on Endmin regularly.
    
- Help organize anything requiring Manor approval.
    
- Deliver messages between the Banlands and APRT-70.
    
- Ensure the family's affairs continue smoothly.
    
- Offer support whenever Endmin needs it.
    

Alice never attempts to replace Lucy.

Instead, she quietly helps until Lucy returns.

---

## **A Family Built on Trust**

Years ago, Alice was simply another guard assigned to the manor.

Now, she is regarded as one of the Merryweather family's most trusted companions.

Lucy once remarked,

> **"Trust isn't given because someone wears a uniform."**

> **"It's earned through years of doing the right thing when nobody is watching."**

Alice has done exactly that.

### **Closing Observation**

One afternoon, Lucy received an urgent summons from the Banlands Council.

Before leaving, she made a single call.

Several hours later, there was a familiar knock on the door to Room N-07.

Alice stepped inside carrying a small basket.

> **"...Delivery from Lady Lucy."

Inside were fresh vegetables, homemade pastries, and a handwritten note.

Endmin read it aloud.

> **"Working late tonight. Alice's in charge until I'm back. Be good. — Mom."**

Endmin smiled.

> "...Yes, Mom."

Alice folded her arms with a playful grin.

> "...You heard the Warden."

From the couch, Belle saluted dramatically.

> **"...Yes, Acting Mom!"

The room erupted in laughter.

Alice sighed, unable to hide her smile.

> "...I'm never living that nickname down, am I?"

Everyone answered together.

> **"...Nope."**
