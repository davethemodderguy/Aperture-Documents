# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-094**

### **PERSONNEL OBSERVATION – BELLE'S BRAID & COMBAT PHILOSOPHY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Personnel / Behavioral Sciences

---

## **SUMMARY**

A routine personnel review documented two defining characteristics of Belle:

- Her signature tightly braided hair.
    
- Her personal dislike of firearms despite serving as the Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority.
    

These observations are consistent with Belle's preference for direct, personal responsibility in combat.

---

## **The Signature Braid**

Belle's hair is **always** kept in a tight braid.

Regardless of the occasion—patrols, training, formal ceremonies, or relaxing in Room N-07—the braid remains meticulously maintained.

According to Belle, there are several reasons:

- It never gets caught on equipment.
    
- It stays out of her face during combat.
    
- It doesn't interfere with carrying her oversized sword.
    
- It reminds her to stay disciplined.
    

When asked if anyone else was allowed to braid it, Belle smiled.

> **"...Only people I trust."

Endmin quietly looked away, pretending she hadn't heard that.

---

## **Views on Firearms**

Unlike many members of the Friendly Roommates, Belle has little interest in firearms.

She is fully qualified to operate standard Banlands service weapons if absolutely necessary.

However, she almost never carries one.

When questioned by Armory staff, Belle gave a simple explanation.

> **"...I don't hate people with guns."

She rested a hand on the hilt of her sword.

> **"...I just don't like using them myself."

She believes her sword better reflects her role.

To Belle, drawing the blade means she has consciously accepted the responsibility for every action that follows.

It is a burden she believes should never be taken lightly.

---

## **Weapon Record**

Like Endmin, Belle's primary weapon bears small engraved tally marks documenting confirmed eliminations performed in the line of duty.

However, the number is significantly lower than that found on Endmin's weapons.

Behavioral Sciences notes this is largely due to the differing responsibilities of their organizations.

- **Endmin**, as Director of the Central Execution Department, regularly undertakes high-risk operations against dangerous targets.
    
- **Belle**, as Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority, spends most of her career investigating corruption, conducting audits, and enforcing internal accountability. Her sword is drawn only when lawful duties require it.
    

Belle has never shown pride in the tally marks.

When asked about them, she quietly replied,

> **"...Every mark means something serious happened."

> **"...I hope I don't have to add many more."

### **Closing Observation**

During a joint training exercise, Endmin finished cleaning her rifle before looking over at Belle.

Belle sat on a nearby bench, carefully tightening the braid that reached down toward her hips.

After checking every strand, she tied the final ribbon into place.

Endmin smiled.

> **"...You redo that braid every morning?"

Belle nodded.

> **"...Every morning."

She stood, lifting her enormous sword onto her shoulder with practiced ease.

> **"...If I'm going to represent the Krampus Compliance Authority..."

She straightened her uniform.

> **"...I should look the part."

Endmin chuckled.

> **"...You always do."**
