## **SUMMARY**

Within the Banlands, Belle Merryweather is frequently addressed as **"Miss Belle"** due to her position, reputation, and formal etiquette surrounding the Krampus Compliance Authority.

However, Aperture Industries personnel have earned a unique privilege.

They may simply call her:

**Belle.**

---

## **Official Identification**

**Name:** Belle Merryweather  
**Position:** Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority  
**Security Clearance:** Level 6 Access Card

The card contains no references to her previous identity.

Only her chosen name and current role are recognized.

---

## **The "Miss" Habit**

Many Banlands citizens automatically refer to Belle as:

> **"Miss Belle."**

The title is usually used out of respect for her authority as the Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority.

However, within Aperture Industries, the title is rarely necessary.

The residents of Room N-07, Lucy, Endmin, and Aperture personnel who know her personally simply call her **Belle**.

---

## **Why Aperture Uses Her Name**

Personnel records note that Belle does not require a title to be respected.

Her authority comes from her actions.

Despite her cheerful personality, small frame, and soft appearance, Belle has:

- Built her reputation through years of work.
    
- Earned her position as Director.
    
- Protected citizens of the Banlands.
    
- Held powerful officials accountable.
    
- Proven herself as a capable leader.
    

---

## **Physical Appearance Notes**

Belle's appearance often causes visitors unfamiliar with her to underestimate her.

She is known for:

- Short stature.
    
- Pink fur.
    
- Soft rabbit-like features.
    
- Fluffy ears.
    
- A gentle personality.
    

Several personnel have compared her appearance to a living plush toy.

However, this comparison must not be confused with weakness.

The same individual described as a "sentient bunny plushie" is also capable of:

- Carrying a 50-kilogram weapon.
    
- Performing advanced combat maneuvers.
    
- Leading the Krampus Compliance Authority.
    
- Making powerful officials answer for their actions.
