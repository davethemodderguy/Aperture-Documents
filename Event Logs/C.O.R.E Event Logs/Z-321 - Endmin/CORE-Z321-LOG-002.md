# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-002**

### **INITIAL PSYCHOLOGICAL & MEDICAL ASSESSMENT — Z-321 "ENDMIN"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – MEDICAL / RESIDENT WELFARE

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following her admission into Room N-07, Z-321 ("Endmin") has adapted exceptionally well to daily life within the Blacksite.

She is energetic, cheerful, and highly sociable, quickly becoming acquainted with the residents of Room N-07 and nearby personnel.

However, beneath her optimistic personality, Psychological Services identified several significant emotional vulnerabilities.

Like Mart Sunbringer, Endmin exhibits pronounced anxiety surrounding abandonment and prolonged separation from her primary caregiver.

---

## **PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVATIONS**

During routine interviews, Endmin consistently demonstrated a positive outlook and enthusiasm toward meeting new people.

She readily participates in meals, recreational activities, and tours around the facility.

Despite this, several recurring concerns became apparent.

Endmin is notably distressed by the thought of:

- Losing her mother.
    
- Being abandoned.
    
- Being forgotten.
    
- Remaining alone for extended periods.
    

When discussing Lucy, Endmin often unconsciously reaches toward a pendant hanging beneath her shirt.

Psychologists interpret this as a self-soothing behavior.

Unlike Mart, whose fear developed primarily through repeated trauma, Endmin's anxiety appears closely tied to uncertainty about her mother's safety while she is away.

---

## **MEDICAL OBSERVATION — OCULAR CONDITION**

During the initial medical examination, staff requested permission to inspect the metallic mask covering Endmin's eyes.

The request produced an immediate and severe stress response.

Endmin instinctively stepped backward.

Her hands began trembling.

Her breathing became shallow.

She tightly gripped the edges of her mask.

When reassured that no examination would proceed without consent, she gradually calmed.

Medical personnel immediately suspended the request.

Subsequent interviews determined that the mask itself is not the cause of her distress.

Rather, discussions concerning what lies beneath it evoke significant anxiety.

---

## **MEDICAL SUMMARY**

Lucy later provided the following explanation.

Several years earlier, Endmin suffered a catastrophic **Ether Corruption Surge**.

The event permanently damaged both eyes.

Current medical status:

- Approximately **95% vision loss**.
    
- Residual perception remains.
    
- Exposure to normal environmental light presents a risk of additional damage.
    
- The protective mask must remain in place except under carefully controlled conditions.
    

Despite this, Endmin is **not completely blind**.

She described her vision herself:

> "...Imagine everything..."

She paused.

> "...The whole world is black."

She smiled gently.

> "...But I can still see where everything is."

> "...Just..."

She searched for the words.

> "...The outlines."

> "...And how far away things are."

Psychological Services summarized this as:

> **Endmin perceives spatial depth, movement, and object boundaries despite profoundly limited visual input.**

Her ability to navigate the environment remains remarkably effective.

Many staff members were unaware of the severity of her impairment until reading this report.

---

## **ROOM N-07 INTERACTIONS**

### Mart

Mart quietly adjusted his walking speed after noticing Endmin occasionally hesitated near unfamiliar corners.

He never mentioned why.

Endmin eventually noticed.

> **Endmin:** "...You're slowing down for me."

Mart looked embarrassed.

> "...A little."

She smiled.

> "...Thank you."

---

### Taiga

Taiga initially became worried after learning about Endmin's eyesight.

The next morning, she appeared carrying a brightly colored rope.

> **Taiga:** "...Safety rope!"

Endmin tilted her head.

> "...Why?"

> **Taiga:** "...So we don't lose each other!"

Endmin burst into laughter.

The rope was never actually used.

It remained hanging inside Room N-07 as a running joke.

---

### Astral

Astral noticed Endmin occasionally became anxious whenever Lucy's name appeared in conversations.

She quietly reassured her each evening.

> "...Your mom knows where you are."

> "...And she trusted us."

Endmin always nodded.

Even so...

She still checked the Portal Hub more often than anyone realized.

## **LETTER FROM LUCY**

**To:** Director Lucious  
**From:** Lucy, Warden of the Banlands

---

Lucious,

By now you've probably noticed that Endmin never removes her mask.

Please don't ask her to.

Not yet.

Years ago, I made a mistake.

One that nearly cost my daughter her sight.

An Ether surge reached her before I could stop it.

I protected her life.

I couldn't fully protect her eyes.

She still blames herself sometimes.

Don't let her.

The fault was mine.

Her vision isn't completely gone.

She sees the world differently now.

Dark.

Shapes.

Movement.

Distance.

Enough to live.

Enough to smile.

But bright light can still worsen the damage.

For that reason, the mask stays on.

I know Aperture possesses technology capable of restoring or replacing damaged organs.

I also know your researchers are already thinking about it.

Please don't.

Not yet.

She has already spent enough of her childhood surrounded by machines, examinations, and people trying to "fix" her.

Right now...

She doesn't need another experiment.

She needs friends.

She needs somewhere that feels like home.

If, one day...

She decides for herself that she wants technological assistance...

Then we'll discuss it together.

Until then...

Let her simply be Endmin.

Not a patient.

Not a project.

Just...

My daughter.

— Lucy

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Director Lucious

After reading the letter, Director Lucious signed a facility-wide instruction.

> **No technological intervention regarding Z-321's eyesight is to be proposed, developed, or discussed with the occupant unless initiated by Z-321 herself or authorized directly by Lucy.**

---

### Medical Division

The directive has been accepted without objection.

Routine eye examinations have been suspended.

Medical support will instead focus on maintaining Endmin's comfort and preventing accidental exposure to excessive light.

### **Closing Observation**

Late in the evening, the lights in Room N-07 were switched off.

For everyone else, the room became dark.

For Endmin...

It looked almost exactly the same as it always did.

She quietly climbed into bed.

Mart noticed.

> "...Can you tell it's nighttime?"

Endmin smiled.

She pointed toward the window.

> "...I can't really see the stars."

> "...But I know they're still there."

Mart looked out at the night sky.

Then back at her.

He smiled softly.

> "...Yeah."

> "...They are."
