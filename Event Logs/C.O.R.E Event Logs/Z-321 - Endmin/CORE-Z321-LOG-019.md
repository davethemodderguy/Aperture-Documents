# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-019**

### **N.O.S.T. TECHNICAL SEMINAR — EVISCERATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – WEAPON RESEARCH / INTERDIMENSIONAL KNOWLEDGE

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following several questions from Engineering and Research Division regarding Banlands weaponry, Lucy, Warden of the Banlands, and Z-321 ("Endmin") conducted a technical seminar explaining one of the most fundamental concepts in Banlands combat engineering:

**Evisceration.**

The lecture clarified why conventional weapons—even exceptionally powerful anomalous ones—are often ineffective against certain Banlands entities.

---

## **INTRODUCTION**

Lucy stood before the lecture room, placing her sheathed katana on the table.

Endmin rested her rifle beside it.

A projector displayed a single word.

**EVISCERATION**

Lucy began.

> **Lucy:** "Many of you believe a stronger weapon solves every problem."

She looked toward Miss Neko.

> "...It doesn't."

Miss Neko raised an eyebrow.

Lucy smiled.

> "...Not in the Banlands."

---

## **THE PROBLEM**

Lucy continued.

> "In the Banlands, a soul is not simply a biological life."

A diagram appeared on the screen.

> "Souls, especially corrupted souls, are partially anchored by Ether."

Another diagram followed.

> "Destroying the physical form does not necessarily destroy the soul."

She paused.

> "Which means..."

Endmin finished the sentence.

> **Endmin:** "...You can reduce someone to dust."

> "...And they still aren't dead."

The room became noticeably quieter.

---

## **WHY CONVENTIONAL WEAPONS FAIL**

Research personnel asked whether sufficiently powerful weapons could compensate through raw destructive force.

Lucy shook her head.

> "No."

She gestured toward an illustration of a corrupted Ether Walker.

> "A conventional projectile may completely destroy the body."

Another image appeared.

The Ether signature remained.

> "The soul persists."

She looked toward Miss Neko.

> "Even your Bliss of Whitefall."

Miss Neko folded her arms.

> "...Wouldn't finish it."

Lucy nodded.

> "Correct."

> "It would erase the body."

> "Not the soul."

---

## **STABILIZED ETHER CORRUPTION**

Endmin stepped forward.

She carefully removed the spare cartridge mounted beside the bolt of her rifle.

The room immediately recognized it.

The same cartridge she had once described as:

> _"One shot to cut all ties with evil."_

She held it carefully in her palm.

> **Endmin:** "This round isn't stronger."

> "...It's different."

She pointed toward the projectile.

> "Inside is a microscopic quantity of **Stabilized Ether Corruption**."

Several researchers looked surprised.

One cautiously asked,

> "...Corruption?"

Lucy nodded.

> "Yes."

She immediately clarified.

> "**Stabilized** Ether Corruption."

---

## **WHAT IS STABILIZED ETHER CORRUPTION?**

Lucy explained that not every manifestation of Ether Corruption is inherently destructive.

Through Banlands refinement techniques, corrupted Ether can be stabilized into a controlled state.

Properties include:

- Stable and predictable behavior.
    
- Safe handling under proper procedures.
    
- Beneficial applications in specialized tools.
    
- Ability to directly affect Ether-bound entities.
    

Unlike uncontrolled Ether Corruption, stabilized Ether does not continue to spread uncontrollably once properly contained.

Lucy emphasized,

> "It remains dangerous if misused."

> "But it is no longer alive."

---

## **EVISCERATION**

Endmin loaded the demonstration cartridge into her rifle.

She did not chamber it.

Instead, she held it where everyone could see.

> **Endmin:** "When this projectile strikes..."

She gently tapped the bullet.

> "...The stabilized Ether is released."

A holographic simulation appeared.

Rather than merely damaging the target's body, the Ether interacted directly with the soul's Ether anchor.

Lucy finished the explanation.

> **Lucy:** "That process is called..."

The slide changed.

**EVISCERATION**

> "The complete severing of an Ether-bound soul from its corrupted anchor."

The simulation showed the Ether signature collapsing entirely.

No regeneration.

No resurrection.

No lingering corruption.

---

## **EXAMPLES**

Lucy pointed toward several familiar weapons.

### Endmin's Spare Cartridge

Reserved for targets requiring permanent elimination.

---

### Lucy's Katana

The crimson blade continuously resonates with stabilized Ether.

Combined with specially manufactured ammunition fired from the weapon's integrated sheath, it is capable of performing Evisceration against corrupted Banlands souls.

---

### Standard Banlands Military Weapons

Most Banlands firearms incorporate minute quantities of stabilized Ether Corruption into their ammunition, allowing soldiers to neutralize Ether Walkers and similarly anchored threats.

---

## **QUESTIONS**

One researcher raised a hand.

> "...Could Aperture manufacture these rounds?"

Lucy answered honestly.

> "Eventually."

> "But not today."

Endmin nodded.

> "The refining process took Banlands centuries to perfect."

> "Doing it incorrectly is... dangerous."

Research Division immediately removed "trial-and-error experimentation" from the proposed development plan.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Research Division has opened a cooperative project with Banlands authorities to better understand stabilized Ether Corruption and its applications.

No independent manufacturing is authorized at this time.

All Banlands-derived Evisceration technology remains under Lucy's supervision.

---

### **Closing Observation**

As the seminar concluded, Endmin carefully returned the single stabilized Ether cartridge to its holder beside the bolt of her rifle.

A researcher quietly asked,

> "...Have you ever needed to use it?"

Endmin looked at the cartridge for a long moment.

Then she smiled.

> "...No."

She gently locked it back into place.

> "...And I hope I never do."

Lucy glanced toward her daughter with quiet relief.

For the first time in many years...

That final round remained exactly where it belonged.
