# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-032**

### **CONTROLLED DURABILITY TRIAL — Z-321 WING ARMOR**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – ENGINEERING / LIVE WEAPONS TEST

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the successful implementation of Z-321's Nekonium Nanoparticle Wing Armor, Engineering Division sought to determine its upper defensive limits.

At Miss Neko's suggestion, a **strictly controlled** test was conducted in an uninhabited region of the Starlands using **Bliss of Whitefall**.

Director Lucious approved the trial only after multiple safety systems, emergency personnel, and Lucy herself were present.

The objective was **not** to determine whether Endmin could survive a direct hit from Bliss of Whitefall.

The objective was to determine whether her wings could withstand **a carefully calibrated, low-power test shot** without catastrophic failure.

---

## **PREPARATION**

Lucy stood beside Endmin.

> **Lucy:** "You can stop this test whenever you want."

Endmin nodded.

> "...I know."

Miss Neko adjusted the controls on Bliss of Whitefall.

For this trial, the weapon was configured to output only a fraction of its normal destructive capability.

Neko verified the telemetry systems one final time.

> **Neko:** "Everything ready."

---

## **THE TEST**

Endmin stepped forward.

She unfolded her enormous wings.

The dark feathers spread wide before folding into a complete defensive barrier around her body.

The nanoparticle armor activated.

A translucent forcefield shimmered briefly over every feather before becoming nearly invisible.

Director Lucious raised one hand.

> "Fire."

Miss Neko squeezed the trigger.

The projectile struck the center of Endmin's folded wings.

A brilliant flash lit the valley.

A shockwave rolled across the grasslands.

For several moments...

Nothing could be seen.

---

## **RESULT**

As the dust settled, Endmin remained standing.

Her wings were still closed.

Several feathers had been scorched.

The forcefield flickered briefly before stabilizing.

Slowly, she unfolded her wings.

She was unharmed.

Medical examination confirmed:

- No skeletal damage.
    
- No penetration of the wing structure.
    
- Moderate localized pain.
    
- Superficial feather loss.
    
- Immediate onset of regenerative healing.
    

Engineering concluded that the combined Nekonium nanoparticle armor, forcefield, and the wings' natural resilience had successfully dispersed the calibrated impact.

---

## **IMPORTANT LIMITATION**

Following the test, Miss Neko addressed everyone present.

> **Miss Neko:** "Before anyone gets any ideas..."

She rested Bliss of Whitefall over her shoulder.

> "...This was a controlled engineering test."

Neko continued the explanation.

> "The weapon was intentionally configured for evaluation."

> "This does **not** mean Endmin can simply ignore the full destructive capability of Bliss of Whitefall under all conditions."

The trial demonstrated the effectiveness of her defensive system under controlled parameters—not invulnerability.

### **Closing Observation**

Several hours later, Endmin stood overlooking the Starlands.

She gently spread her wings.

The scorched feathers from the trial had already begun to regrow.

She gave them an experimental flap.

Perfect.

Lucy joined her.

> **Lucy:** "...Still hurts?"

Endmin nodded.

> "...A little."

Lucy smiled.

> "...Pain reminds you they're real."

Endmin looked at the vast dark wings stretching behind her.

Then quietly answered,

> "...They're worth protecting."

Neko, overhearing from a distance, simply folded his arms with a small, satisfied grin.
