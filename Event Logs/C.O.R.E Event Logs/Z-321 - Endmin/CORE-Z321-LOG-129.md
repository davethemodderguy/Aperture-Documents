## **SUMMARY**

Following Lucius Merryweather's reintegration into Aperture Industries, his physical appearance was documented in detail for personnel identification.

Among his most distinctive features are an unusual streak of red hair and a pair of heterochromatic gradient eyes.

---

## **Hair**

Lucius possesses predominantly light blue hair, inherited alongside his Wolf Thiren characteristics.

However, one bundle of hair at the front of his face is naturally **crimson red**.

The streak extends downward across the front of his hairstyle and is present from the roots, confirming it is a natural characteristic rather than dye or cosmetic alteration.

It has become one of the easiest ways for personnel to identify him at a glance.

---

## **Eyes**

Lucius' eyes display a rare color gradient.

Rather than being a single color, each iris gradually transitions between:

- Deep blue.
    
- Crimson red.
    

The colors blend smoothly together, giving his eyes a distinctive appearance that changes subtly depending on the lighting.

Researchers have confirmed that the coloration is stable and poses no medical concern.
