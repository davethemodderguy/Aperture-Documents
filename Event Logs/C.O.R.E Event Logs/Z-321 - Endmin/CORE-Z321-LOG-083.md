# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-083**

### **NEW RESIDENT RECORD – BELLE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Residential / Personnel

---

## **SUMMARY**

At the conclusion of the Friendly Roommates' visit to Merryweather Manor, Z-321 (Endmin) introduced everyone to her longtime companion, **Belle**.

Contrary to initial assumptions, Belle was not an ordinary rabbit.

She is a **Full-Body Thiren**, a rare subtype of Thiren whose body is almost entirely covered in animal fur while retaining a humanoid figure.

Following discussions between Lucy, Endmin, and Director Lucious, Belle requested permission to accompany Endmin back to APRT-70 as her personal bodyguard and roommate.

The request was approved.

---

## **Designation**

**Name:** Belle

**Species:** Rabbit Thiren (Full-Body Thiren)

**Height:** **145 cm (4 ft 9 in)**

**Occupation:**

- Personal Bodyguard to Endmin
    
- Resident of Room N-07
    

---

## **Physical Description**

Belle possesses a humanoid body completely covered in soft **pink fur**.

Distinctive characteristics include:

- Bright red eyes.
    
- Long fluffy rabbit ears.
    
- Small cotton-like tail.
    
- Oversized children's dress.
    
- Two decorative hair ornaments.
    
- Two belts worn around her legs.
    
- A small bell attached to the right leg belt, producing a soft chime while walking.
    

Despite her small stature, Belle routinely carries a sword that appears comically oversized compared to her height.

---

## **Behavior**

Belle's personality contrasts sharply with Endmin's reserved demeanor.

Documented traits include:

- Extremely energetic.
    
- Cheerful.
    
- Silly.
    
- Highly affectionate.
    
- Fiercely protective of Endmin.
    

When running at full speed, Belle's legs rotate so quickly they appear to spin in circles, resembling exaggerated movement seen in classic video games.

Unfortunately, the momentum generated by her oversized sword frequently causes her to lose balance.

Security personnel have documented numerous instances of Belle sprinting heroically toward a destination...

...only to tumble forward moments later before standing up as if nothing had happened.

Medical Division has confirmed she is rarely injured by these incidents.

---

## **Favorite Things**

Belle enjoys:

- Hugs.
    
- Running.
    
- Exploring.
    
- Spending time with Endmin.
    
- Training with her oversized sword.
    

Like Endmin, Belle enjoys vegetables.

Also like Endmin...

She insists she does not.

## **Request**

Before the group departed the Banlands, Belle nervously approached Endmin.

Holding the oversized sword behind her back, she quietly asked,

> **"...Can I... come live with you?"**

Endmin blinked.

> **"...Really?"**

Belle nodded enthusiastically.

> **"I wanna protect you!"

> **"And everyone sounds really nice!"

Lucy smiled warmly.

Director Lucious considered the request before replying,

> **"If that's what both of you want..."**

> **"...then you're welcome at the Blacksite."**

Belle practically tackled Endmin into a hug.

The bell on her leg jingled continuously as she bounced with excitement.

## **Residential**

Belle was assigned accommodations within **Room N-07**, joining the Friendly Roommates after returning from the Banlands.

Within hours, she had somehow learned everyone's names.

By the end of the first evening:

- She challenged Mart to a race.
    
- Asked Neko about building an even bigger sword.
    
- Hugged Bao twice.
    
- Accidentally startled Nightshade by appearing behind her.
    
- Fell over while trying to salute Director Lucious.
    

Aster simply wrote on her whiteboard:

> **"Chaos bunny."**

Belle pointed excitedly.

> **"...I like that title!"

## **Banlands Security Update**

Following the departure of the Aperture delegation and Belle, Lucy concluded the internal disciplinary proceedings concerning the former Head of Security whose misconduct had occurred at the manor gates.

After a formal investigation confirmed abuse of authority, intimidation of subordinates, and conduct unbecoming of a Banlands officer, the former guard was stripped of rank and custody was transferred to Banlands judicial authorities.

Records supplied by Lucy indicate that the sentence was carried out by Endmin in accordance with Banlands law and under Lucy's authorization.

Director Lucious acknowledged the report but noted that the matter fell under Banlands jurisdiction.

No further action was taken by Aperture Industries.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Belle is now an official resident of APRT-70 and serves as Endmin's designated bodyguard.

Although she is the shortest member of the Friendly Roommates, staff have quickly learned that her enthusiasm, unwavering loyalty, and oversized sword make her a memorable addition to Room N-07.

---

### **Closing Observation**

On Belle's very first morning at the Blacksite, researchers watched as Endmin skated down the hallway with Taiga.

A few meters behind them came the unmistakable jingling of a tiny bell.

Belle was sprinting after them.

Her legs became a pink blur.

> **"WAIT FOR MEEEEEE—!"**

She made it three more steps.

Her oversized sword shifted.

She pitched forward and rolled across the polished floor before popping back to her feet without missing a beat.

> **"...I MEANT TO DO THAT!"

Endmin covered her face, trying not to laugh.

Taiga failed completely.

Even Director Lucious, passing by with a stack of reports, paused just long enough to smile.

> **"...Welcome to the Blacksite, Belle."**
