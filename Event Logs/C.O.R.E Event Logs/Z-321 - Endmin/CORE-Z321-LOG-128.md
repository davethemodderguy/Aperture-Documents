# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-128**

### **RESIDENTIAL EVENT – ENDMIN'S BIRTHDAY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Personnel Welfare

---

## **SUMMARY**

Room N-07 held a small birthday celebration for **Lindervene "Endmin" Merryweather**.

While the event was intended to be cheerful, Endmin became unexpectedly emotional.

It marked the first birthday she had celebrated with her older brother, Lucius, since his disappearance four years earlier.

---

## **Preparations**

The celebration had been quietly planned for several days.

Belle insisted on decorating the room herself.

Star volunteered to make paper stars to hang from the ceiling.

Taiga helped prepare the food.

Mart and Neko arranged the tables.

Miss Neko and Astral handled the cake.

Bao made sure Lucy could arrive on time despite her work.

Nightshade remained in the background, making sure nothing disturbed the celebration.

Lucius spent the evening before finishing a wrapped canvas.

He refused to let anyone see it.

---

## **The Celebration**

When Endmin opened the door to Room N-07...

Everyone shouted together.

> **"Happy Birthday!"**

Endmin jumped in surprise.

Her white cat ears immediately stood straight up.

Belle ran over first and hugged her.

> **"Happy Birthday, Endmin!"**

Soon everyone gathered around.

The room was warm.

Lively.

Filled with laughter.

For a little while...

It felt like there was no work waiting outside the door.

---

## **The Gift**

When it was Lucius' turn, he quietly handed Endmin the wrapped canvas.

> **"...It's not much."

Endmin carefully removed the paper.

Inside was a painting.

It showed two children sitting beneath the moon.

A young Wolf Thiren.

A little Cat Thiren.

Both wearing matching bracelets.

The memory had been recreated exactly as Lucius remembered it.

In the lower corner, he had written only a few words.

> **"For the little sister who never stopped waiting."**

Endmin stared silently at the painting.

Her hands trembled.

A tear landed on the canvas.

Then another.

Before anyone realized what was happening...

She began crying.

---

## **A Brother's Apology**

Lucius immediately became worried.

> **"...Endmin?"

> **"...Did I do something wrong?"**

She quickly shook her head.

Unable to speak, she threw her arms around him.

She cried into his shoulder.

Between quiet sobs, she finally managed to whisper:

> **"...This..."

> **"...This is the first birthday you've been here for..."

The room became completely silent.

Lucius slowly hugged her back.

His own eyes began to water.

> **"...I'm sorry..."

> **"...I missed so many."

Endmin shook her head again.

> **"...You're here now."

> **"...That's enough."**

---

## **Everyone Else**

Lucy wiped away tears before pulling both of her children into a hug.

Belle immediately joined, wrapping her arms around both siblings.

Star declared that "group hugs make everything better" and joined without hesitation.

Taiga laughed while pretending she wasn't crying.

Mart quietly smiled.

Neko looked away, giving the family a moment.

Miss Neko smiled warmly.

Astral gently rested a hand on Lucy's shoulder.

Bao simply watched with a peaceful expression.

Nightshade stood nearby with folded arms.

After a long silence, she quietly said:

> **"...Welcome home."

No one was entirely sure whether she was speaking to Lucius...

Or to the family itself.

---

## **Birthday Wish**

After everyone had settled down, the candles were lit.

Endmin closed her eyes.

She didn't tell anyone what she wished for.

When she opened them again...

She blew out every candle in one breath.

Belle immediately asked:

> **"...What did you wish for?"**

Endmin smiled through the last of her tears.

She looked around the room.

At Lucy.

At Belle.

At Lucius.

At all of her friends.

> **"...It already came true."**

---

## **Director Lucious' Note**

After hearing about the celebration, Director Lucious added a short entry to the residential archive.

> **"Some birthdays celebrate another year of life."**

> **"Others celebrate the return of someone thought to be lost."**

---

## **Closing Observation**

Later that night, after the decorations had been taken down and the cake had disappeared piece by piece, Endmin carefully carried Lucius' painting to her room.

She hung it on the wall beside her bed.

Right above the framed original handguard from her rifle.

Before turning off the lights, she looked at the painting one last time.

Then at the matching bracelet on her wrist.

She smiled.

For the first time in four years...

Her birthday didn't feel like it was missing someone.
