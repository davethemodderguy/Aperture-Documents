# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-077**

### **SECURITY INCIDENT FOLLOW-UP – ETHER WALKER NEUTRALIZATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Security / After-Action Report

---

## **SUMMARY**

The investigation into the assault on Z-321 (Endmin) concluded approximately six hours after the incident.

Evidence collected by Security Division, combined with Banlands Ether analysis performed by Z-945 (Lucy), identified the assailant as an **Ether Walker** that had infiltrated APRT-70 by disguising itself as a human.

The entity was successfully located and neutralized.

---

## **Identification**

Initial forensic analysis found no usable fingerprints or conventional biological evidence.

However, traces of **Stabilized Ether Corruption** left on Endmin's clothing reacted abnormally.

Lucy examined the residue.

Within minutes, she reached a conclusion.

> **Lucy:**
> 
> "This wasn't a person."

She closed her eyes briefly.

> "...It's an Ether Walker."

The residue's Ether signature was consistent with known Banlands records.

---

## **Pursuit**

Perimeter Security established containment zones while Lucy tracked the Ether signature through the Blacksite.

Director Lucious authorized Lucy to take operational lead due to her extensive experience combating Ether Walkers.

Several security teams followed at a safe distance.

The pursuit ended near an abandoned maintenance sector beneath the facility.

The Ether Walker abandoned its disguise once cornered.

Witnesses described the transformation as rapid and unstable, its human appearance dissolving into a corrupted Ether form.

---

## **Neutralization**

Lucy calmly unsheathed her crimson Banlands blade.

The Ether Walker charged.

The engagement lasted less than ten seconds.

Using the specialized **Evisceration** properties inherent to Banlands weaponry, Lucy delivered a decisive strike that disrupted the creature's Ether structure.

The entity attempted to regenerate.

It failed.

A follow-up shot from the firearm concealed within Lucy's sheath dispersed the remaining Ether fragments.

No further anomalous activity was detected.

The Ether Walker was declared permanently neutralized.

---

## **Lucy's Statement**

After confirming the entity's destruction, Lucy quietly sheathed her weapon.

She looked toward the responding security personnel.

> **"It won't be hurting anyone again."**

When later asked why she insisted on leading the pursuit herself, Lucy answered simply:

> **"...It tried to kill my daughter."**

No further explanation was given.

---

## **Director Lucious' Statement**

Director Lucious reviewed the after-action report personally.

> **"Excellent work."**

He thanked both Aperture Security and Lucy for preventing further casualties.

He also ordered an immediate review of all Blacksite screening procedures to determine how the Ether Walker infiltrated the facility.

> **"We neutralized the threat."**

> **"Now we make sure another one never gets this far again."**

---

## **Endmin**

After learning the investigation had concluded, Endmin quietly asked only one question.

> **"...Nobody else got hurt?"

When informed that there were no additional casualties, she visibly relaxed.

She thanked the Security Division before returning to Room N-07.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

The Ether Walker responsible for the assault on Z-321 has been confirmed destroyed.

No evidence suggests additional Ether Walkers remain inside APRT-70.

Security protocols concerning Ether-based infiltrators have been updated in cooperation with Banlands authorities.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late that evening, Lucy stopped outside Endmin's room before returning to her own quarters.

Endmin opened the door.

Neither of them spoke for a few moments.

Finally, Endmin quietly asked,

> **"...Is it over?"

Lucy nodded.

> **"...It's over."

Endmin stepped forward and hugged her mother.

Lucy returned the embrace.

Outside, the Blacksite continued its nightly routine.

Inside Room N-07, for the first time since the attack, Endmin was finally able to rest knowing the threat was gone.
