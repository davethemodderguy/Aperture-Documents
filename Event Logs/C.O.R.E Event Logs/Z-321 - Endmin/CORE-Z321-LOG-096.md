# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-096**

### **RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION – "THE SENTIENT BUNNY PLUSHIE"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Personnel

---

## **SUMMARY**

Although Director Belle is one of the Banlands' highest-ranking law enforcement officials, personnel quickly discovered that outside of official duties she is remarkably approachable.

When relaxed around trusted friends, Belle has a habit of completely lowering her guard, often resulting in surprise hugs, being picked up without warning, and endless jokes about her height.

Belle has accepted this as part of life in Room N-07.

---

## **Off Duty**

While on duty, Belle maintains professional awareness and composure.

Inside Room N-07, however, that vigilance largely disappears.

Behavioral Sciences has documented numerous occasions where Belle was unexpectedly:

- Hugged from behind.
    
- Scooped into someone's arms.
    
- Gently lifted off the ground.
    
- Surrounded by group hugs.
    

Her usual reaction is confusion, followed by laughter.

> **"...Oh!"

> **"...We're hugging now?"

---

## **The Height Problem**

Standing at **145 cm (4 ft 9 in)**, Belle is the shortest resident among the Friendly Roommates.

Even Endmin stands noticeably taller.

This fact has become the subject of light-hearted teasing among her friends.

Typical exchanges include:

**Taiga:**

> "...Need help reaching that shelf?"

**Belle:**

> "...I can reach it!"

She jumped.

She still couldn't.

Endmin quietly reached up, took the item down, and handed it to her.

> "...Thanks..."

---

## **Ambush Hugs**

Because Belle rarely expects affection to be used as a surprise attack, she is particularly vulnerable to what the Friendly Roommates have unofficially named **"hug ambushes."**

A common sequence is:

1. Belle is distracted.
    
2. Someone hugs her.
    
3. Belle squeaks in surprise.
    
4. She immediately hugs them back.
    

Behavioral Sciences has concluded that this cycle is unlikely to change.

---

## **The Nickname**

The nickname originated with a researcher who watched Belle happily accept hugs while carrying a sword almost as tall as herself.

He quietly remarked,

> **"...She looks like a sentient bunny plushie..."

He glanced at the massive weapon resting on her shoulder.

> **"...With a really big sword."

The description spread through the residential wing within days.

Belle eventually heard it herself.

She tilted her head.

> **"...I... kind of like that."

The nickname has remained ever since.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Belle continues to be one of the most approachable residents of APRT-70.

While she remains fully capable of performing her duties as Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority, she is equally known for welcoming hugs, laughing with her friends, and occasionally needing assistance reaching the top shelf.

The title **"Sentient Bunny Plushie with a Big Sword"** has become an affectionate nickname among staff and residents alike.

---

### **Closing Observation**

One evening, Belle was standing on her tiptoes, trying very hard to reach a box on the highest shelf in Room N-07.

She stretched.

She hopped.

She stretched again.

Without a word, Endmin walked over, retrieved the box with ease, and placed it in Belle's hands.

Belle puffed out her cheeks.

> **"...I was almost there."

Taiga snickered.

> **"...Sure you were."

Before Belle could protest, Star darted over and wrapped her in a surprise hug.

Nightshade followed.

Then Bao.

Within seconds, Belle disappeared into a pile of smiling roommates.

Only the tips of her rabbit ears remained visible.

From somewhere inside the hug pile came a muffled voice.

> **"...I can't move..."

No one seemed particularly concerned.

Least of all Belle, who was laughing the entire time.
