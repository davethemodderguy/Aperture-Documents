# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-065**

### **INCIDENT REPORT – FAILED ILLUSION PRANK**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Internal / Personnel

---

## **SUMMARY**

On a quiet afternoon, Z-321 (Endmin) elected to use one of her signature Ether techniques, commonly associated with her former Banlands title **"Angel of Delusions,"** for a decidedly non-combat purpose.

Specifically...

To prank Director Lucious.

The attempt was unsuccessful.

---

## **Incident**

Security cameras observed Endmin waiting outside Director Lucious' office.

As he approached, she subtly manipulated ambient Ether.

The hallway appeared to stretch farther than it really was.

Door signs shifted position.

A potted plant briefly appeared where there was none.

The office door seemed to move several feet to the left.

To an ordinary observer, the illusion was flawless.

Director Lucious continued walking.

Without slowing down.

Without looking around.

Without even glancing at the false doorway.

He walked directly through the illusion and opened the real office door.

Endmin blinked.

She quietly dismissed the illusion.

---

## **Conversation**

Endmin stepped into the office a moment later.

> **Endmin:**
> 
> "...Director?"

Lucious looked up from his paperwork.

> **"Yes?"**

> **Endmin:**
> 
> "...How did you see through it?"

Director Lucious leaned back in his chair before laughing.

Not mockingly.

Just warmly.

> **Director Lucious:**
> 
> "Lindervene..."

Endmin instinctively looked up.

He continued.

> "There are countless entities in containment with abilities just like yours."

> "Some manipulate vision."

> "Some manipulate sound."

> "Some alter memory."

> "Others distort perception itself."

He smiled.

> "I've spent years learning every common way someone can force me into an illusion."

> "If I couldn't recognize those tricks..."

> "...I wouldn't be very good at my job."

Endmin nodded respectfully.

> **"Yes, Director."**

Lucious raised an eyebrow.

> **"One more thing."**

> "Just call me Lucious."

Endmin looked surprised.

Lucious chuckled.

> "Everyone else insists on calling me 'Director.'"

> "Outside of your roommates and your mother..."

> "...almost nobody simply calls me by my name."

There was a brief pause.

Endmin smiled.

> **"...Yes, Lucious."**

Lucious nodded approvingly.

> "There we go."

---

## **Behavioral Note**

Archive Division notes that Endmin appeared more curious than disappointed by the failed prank.

After the conversation, she reportedly asked Director Lucious several questions regarding illusion-resistant training methods.

The discussion lasted nearly forty minutes.

> **"If you're going to prank me..."**

### **Closing Observation**

As Endmin left the office, she paused in the hallway.

She glanced back toward the closed door.

A tiny illusionary paper airplane drifted silently beneath it before vanishing into harmless Ether.

From inside the office came Lucious' amused voice.

> **"Nice try, Endmin."**

She froze.

Then laughed to herself.

> **"...He saw that too."**
