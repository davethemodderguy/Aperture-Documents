# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-030**

### **ARMORY PROFILE — Z-321 PRIMARY MELEE WEAPON**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – ARMORY RECORD

---

## **SUMMARY**

Despite being one of the Blacksite's most accomplished marksmen and carrying a precision rifle at nearly all times, Z-321 ("Endmin") has a surprisingly unconventional primary melee weapon.

It is not a sword.

Not a combat knife.

Not an anomalous relic.

It is...

A baseball bat.

A very good one.

---

## **THE BAT**

The bat was manufactured by Neko at Endmin's request.

Constructed around a reinforced Nekonium core with a shock-absorbing outer layer, it retains the appearance and balance of a traditional wooden baseball bat while possessing vastly greater durability.

Features include:

- Reinforced Nekonium internal structure.
    
- Excellent impact absorption.
    
- Comfortable anti-slip grip.
    
- Optimized balance for one- or two-handed use.
    
- Resistant to deformation and cracking.
    
- Non-anomalous striking surface, making it suitable for training and non-lethal engagements.
    

Neko intentionally avoided adding blades, spikes, or unnecessary attachments.

> **Neko:** "If a bat already solves the problem..."

> "...Don't make it a worse bat."

---

## **WHY A BASEBALL BAT?**

During an armory inspection, Taiga finally asked the obvious question.

> **Taiga:** "...Everyone around here has katanas, plasma scythes, giant rifles..."

She pointed at the bat resting against Endmin's locker.

> "...Why a baseball bat?"

Endmin picked it up and gave it an experimental swing.

It cut cleanly through the air.

> **Endmin:** "...It's simple."

Another swing.

> "...Reliable."

She smiled.

> "...And I like it."

---

## **TRAINING**

Although primarily known as a precision shooter, Endmin demonstrated remarkable proficiency with the bat.

Her movements emphasized:

- Precise timing.
    
- Footwork.
    
- Controlled strikes.
    
- Defensive positioning.
    

Rather than relying on brute force, she used accuracy and leverage to place every hit exactly where intended.

Neko observed the session with folded arms.

> **Neko:** "...Figures."

> "...Even with a bat..."

> "...She's aiming."

---

## **MART'S OBSERVATION**

Mart watched one training session from the sidelines.

After Endmin cleanly struck a suspended steel training target, he commented,

> **Mart:** "...You swing it like a sniper."

Endmin blinked.

> "...Is that a thing?"

Mart smiled.

> "...It is now."

---

## **TAIGA'S ATTEMPT**

Naturally, Taiga asked to try it.

Endmin happily handed the bat over.

Taiga swung with all her strength.

The training dummy launched several meters across the room.

Silence.

Then Taiga looked at the bat.

> **Taiga:** "...I love this thing."

Endmin laughed.

> "...You say that about every weapon."

---

## **LUCY'S COMMENT**

During one of her visits, Lucy noticed the bat leaning against the wall.

She raised an eyebrow.

> **Lucy:** "...You still use that?"

Endmin nodded.

> "...Every chance I get."

Lucy smiled knowingly.

> "...You haven't changed much."

When asked later if Endmin had always favored a bat, Lucy simply replied,

> "...Ever since she was little."

### **Closing Observation**

One afternoon, Star wandered into the training hall carrying a baseball.

She looked at Endmin's bat.

Then at Endmin.

> **Star:** "...Can you actually play baseball?"

Endmin smiled.

> "...I've never tried."

Taiga immediately grinned.

> "...Well..."

She picked up a glove.

> "...Looks like we're fixing that."

Mart sighed, already anticipating the inevitable chaos.

> "...Someone warn Maintenance before Taiga hits the ball into another building."

From somewhere across the room, Neko called out without even looking up from his work.

> "...And not through my workshop this time."
