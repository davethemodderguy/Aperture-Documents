# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-024**

### **MATERIALS LECTURE — INTRODUCTION TO NEKONIUM**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – ENGINEERING / EDUCATION

---

**Location:** Advanced Materials Laboratory

Participants:

- Endmin (Z-321)
    
- Neko
    
- Miss Neko (Occasional Commentary)
    
- Taiga Sunbringer
    
- Mart Sunbringer
    
- Engineering Division Personnel
    

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following several weeks of working alongside Engineering Division, Endmin expressed interest in learning about **Nekonium**, the proprietary anomalous material used throughout Aperture Industries.

Neko volunteered to give the lecture personally.

Research staff later noted that Endmin spent nearly the entire session taking handwritten notes.

---

## **THE LECTURE**

Neko placed several metal samples on the workbench.

Each looked nearly identical.

One was ordinary steel.

One was titanium.

The last was a polished gray-white ingot.

Neko gently tapped the final sample.

> **Neko:** "This..."

> "...Is Nekonium."

Endmin leaned in.

Her fingers hovered over the ingot.

> **Endmin:** "...May I?"

> **Neko:** "Go ahead."

She picked it up.

The material felt unexpectedly smooth and cool to the touch.

---

## **FIRST IMPRESSION**

Endmin slowly turned the ingot over in her hands.

> **Endmin:** "...It's lighter than I expected."

Neko nodded.

> **Neko:** "Strength isn't everything."

He wrote several words across the whiteboard.

**Strength**

**Durability**

**Manufacturability**

**Stability**

**Repairability**

> **Neko:** "A good engineering material isn't just hard."

> "...It has to actually be useful."

---

## **THE DISCUSSION**

For nearly two hours, Neko explained the philosophy behind Nekonium.

Topics included:

- Structural resilience.
    
- Thermal resistance.
    
- Fatigue resistance.
    
- Precision machining techniques.
    
- Controlled anomalous interactions.
    
- Applications in weapons, armor, and infrastructure.
    

Rather than focusing on numbers, Neko repeatedly emphasized practical engineering.

> **Neko:** "A material isn't good because it's impossible to break."

He pointed toward Mart's riot shield.

> "...It's good because you know exactly how it'll behave."

Endmin nodded while writing furiously in her notebook.

---

## **QUESTIONS**

Endmin asked questions almost continuously.

> **Endmin:** "Why doesn't it deform here?"

> "Why use this alloy instead of another?"

> "Why reinforce the edges differently?"

> "Why polish this surface but not the other?"

Engineering personnel quietly smiled.

Most newcomers simply admired the equipment.

Endmin wanted to understand why it had been built that way.

---

## **MISS NEKO'S INTERRUPTION**

Partway through the lecture, Miss Neko walked into the laboratory carrying **Bliss of Whitefall**.

She casually rested the enormous anti-material howitzer against the workbench.

> **Miss Neko:** "Tell her it's mountain-proof."

Neko sighed.

> **Neko:** "...That's not how engineering works."

Miss Neko grinned.

> "...Still mountain-proof."

Neko reluctantly looked toward Endmin.

> "...It is."

Endmin wrote exactly one sentence in her notebook.

> _"Apparently mountain-proof is an engineering unit here."_

The room laughed.

---

## **DISCUSSION OF LIMITATIONS**

Endmin then asked a question that surprised everyone.

> **Endmin:** "...Can Nekonium do everything?"

Neko answered immediately.

> **Neko:** "Absolutely not."

He pointed toward Lucy's katana resting nearby.

> "...Remember what Lucy taught."

> "Even Nekonium has limits."

He continued.

> "Engineering isn't about making something perfect."

> "...It's about knowing what it's can and can't do."

Endmin smiled.

> "...I like that answer."

---

## **MART'S SHIELD**

Neko used Mart's riot shield as a practical example.

He invited Endmin to inspect it.

She carefully examined every joint, fastener, and reinforcing rib.

After several minutes she quietly said,

> "...This wasn't designed just to stop bullets."

Neko smiled proudly.

> "...Exactly."

> "...It was designed to protect Mart."

Mart looked slightly embarrassed.

Taiga immediately nudged him.

> "...You're the demonstration model."

---

## **ENDMIN'S NOTES**

At the end of the lecture, researchers were allowed to view Endmin's notebook.

Rather than copying specifications, many pages contained observations.

Examples included:

- _Materials should solve problems, not create them._
    
- _Every part has a reason._
    
- _Good engineering begins with understanding failure._
    
- _If you can't explain why something exists, redesign it._
    

Neko quietly read the final page.

It simply said,

> _"Build things that protect people."_

He smiled.

> "...You've been paying attention."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Research Division has approved Endmin to attend future Engineering seminars upon request.

Although she has no immediate plans to design weapons of her own, Neko believes she has a strong aptitude for practical engineering and systems thinking.

---

### **Closing Observation**

As everyone packed up, Endmin gently placed the Nekonium sample back onto the workbench.

She looked toward Neko.

> **Endmin:** "...Thank you."

Neko folded his arms with a satisfied grin.

> "...Anytime."

As she left the laboratory, Mart noticed her notebook tucked under one arm.

On the cover, she'd written a new title in neat handwriting:

> **"Things Neko Knows That I Want to Learn."**

Neko pretended not to notice.

He was smiling the rest of the afternoon.
