# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-109**

### **CAFETERIA INCIDENT – THE GREAT SALAD PANIC**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Minor Incident

---

## **SUMMARY**

A routine lunch involving Taiga and Belle resulted in a minor accident after Taiga enthusiastically ate her salad far too quickly.

No injuries were reported.

The incident has since become another story frequently retold within Room N-07.

---

## **Incident**

The Blacksite cafeteria was unusually quiet that afternoon.

Taiga and Belle sat together, each with a large bowl of freshly prepared salad.

Taiga took one bite.

Her eyes widened.

> **"...This is amazing."

Belle nodded happily.

> **"...Right? It's really good!"

Without another word, Taiga began eating at an alarming speed.

Belle watched in mild concern.

> **"...Taiga..."

> **"...Slow down..."

Taiga didn't hear her.

She was already halfway through the bowl.

Then—

She suddenly stopped.

Her eyes went wide.

She clutched at her throat.

> **"...Mmph!"

She had choked.

Unable to speak, Taiga instinctively kicked and flailed her legs in panic beneath the table.

---

## **Collateral Damage**

One of Taiga's frantic kicks struck the leg of Belle's chair.

The chair tipped forward.

Belle, caught completely off guard, leaned with it.

**Thunk.**

Her forehead gently bumped against the edge of the table.

The cafeteria fell silent.

Belle blinked.

She rubbed her forehead once.

> **"...Ow..."

She paused.

> **"...Actually..."

She blinked again.

> **"...I'm okay."

Medical staff later noted that Belle's naturally thick skin and dense rabbit fur absorbed much of the impact, leaving her with nothing more than a brief surprise.

Meanwhile, another resident quickly helped Taiga clear the obstruction from her airway.

After a few coughs, she finally caught her breath.

> **"...I'm alive..."

---

## **Aftermath**

Taiga immediately turned to Belle.

> **"...Belle!"

> **"...I'm so, so sorry!"

Belle smiled reassuringly.

> **"...It's okay."

She gently patted her own head.

> **"...Hard head."

Then she pointed at Taiga's salad.

> **"...Next time..."

> **"...Chew first."

Taiga nodded repeatedly.

> **"...Lesson learned."

---

## **Mart's Comment**

Watching the whole scene unfold, Mart quietly raised a wing.

> **"...Something similar happened to me once."

Everyone looked at him.

Mart shrugged.

> **"...Different food."

> **"...Same embarrassment."

The table burst into laughter.

Even Taiga couldn't help smiling.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

No injuries were sustained.

Belle was cleared immediately after examination.

Taiga made a full recovery after a few minutes and, according to witnesses, proceeded to finish the rest of her salad at a much more reasonable pace.

The cafeteria staff have jokingly placed a small sign near the salad bar reading:

> **"Please chew before attempting another bite."**
