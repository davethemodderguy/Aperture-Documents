# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-126**

### **PERSONNEL OBSERVATION – A BROTHER NOTICES THE CHANGE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Residential / Personal History

---

## **SUMMARY**

Several days after Lucius settled into Room N-07, he found himself studying Endmin's face.

Something about her seemed different from the sister he remembered.

It took him several moments to realize what it was.

Her eyes.

---

## **Room N-07**

The evening was quiet.

Endmin was reading reports at the desk while Lucius worked on a small sketch nearby.

He looked up.

Then looked again.

> **"...Linder..."

Endmin instinctively tensed.

> **"...It's Endmin."

Lucius smiled apologetically.

> **"...Sorry... Endmin."

She relaxed.

> **"...What's up?"

He hesitated.

> **"...Your eyes."**

Endmin froze.

---

## **A Change**

Lucius remembered the eyes his little sister once had.

Normal.

Bright.

Then came the Ether Corruption.

The mask.

The bullying.

Years later...

The corruption was gone.

But her eyes had not returned to what they once were.

He quietly looked into them.

The deep navy irises.

The narrow golden rings.

The slow, almost imperceptible rotation that gave them the appearance of a distant black hole.

They were beautiful.

But unmistakably different.

> **"...They're not the same anymore."

Endmin instinctively looked away.

Her ears lowered slightly.

> **"...Yeah..."

---

## **Embarrassment**

She rubbed the back of her neck.

> **"...Most people notice eventually."

Lucius quickly shook his head.

> **"...I didn't mean anything bad."

Endmin gave a small, shy smile.

> **"...I know."

She glanced back at him for only a moment before looking away again.

> **"...I'm still getting used to people seeing them."

Even after no longer needing to hide her Cat Thiren traits, and no longer wearing her old mask to conceal her gaze, Endmin still felt self-conscious whenever someone commented on her eyes.

Old memories lingered.

---

## **Lucius' Response**

Lucius stood and walked over to his sister.

He gently placed a hand on her shoulder.

> **"...The last time I saw you..."

> **"...You were hiding your eyes because people called you a freak."

He smiled warmly.

> **"...Now I see someone who survived."

Endmin looked back at him.

Her eyes shimmered slightly.

> **"...You really think so?"

Lucius nodded without hesitation.

> **"...They're different."

He smiled.

> **"...But they're still your eyes."

> **"...And you're still my little sister."

Endmin laughed softly.

A faint blush spread across her face.

> **"...You're embarrassing..."

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Endmin no longer hides her eyes from those around her.

However, comments about them still make her noticeably shy due to memories of the years she spent being bullied because of her appearance.

Lucius has made a point of reminding her that, to him, they are not something to fear or hide.

They are simply another part of his little sister.
