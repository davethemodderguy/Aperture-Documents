# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-015**

### **ANOMALOUS OBSERVATION — Z-321 TEAR ANALYSIS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – RESEARCH / MEDICAL

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the events of **Project PRISM**, personnel observed Z-321 ("Endmin") crying continuously for an extended period after experiencing color for the first time.

Several researchers noted an unusual detail.

Despite the amount she cried, **no tears were ever seen running down her face beneath the mask.**

This prompted a voluntary investigation, with Endmin's permission.

The findings revealed another previously undocumented anomalous characteristic.

---

## **INITIAL OBSERVATION**

During the celebration following Project PRISM, Endmin spent nearly an hour alternating between laughing and crying while looking at Star's paintings, flowers, and the evening sky.

Eventually, Taiga tilted her head.

> **Taiga:** "...Wait."

> "...Where are your tears going?"

Everyone paused.

Endmin blinked.

> "...Oh."

> "...The mask catches them."

Silence filled the room.

> **Mart:** "...It... what?"

Endmin nodded casually.

> "...It always has."

---

## **THE MASK'S INTERNAL RESERVOIR**

With Endmin's consent, Engineering and Medical Division examined the mask's interior.

No mechanical reservoir, tubing, or collection system was discovered.

Instead, the tears simply... remained.

The interior of the mask contained a small volume of clear liquid despite there being no identifiable physical mechanism capable of storing it.

When asked how she emptied it, Endmin replied,

> "...I just pour them out."

No further explanation was available.

The phenomenon is currently classified as anomalous.

---

## **UNEXPECTED PROPERTY**

During sample handling, a researcher accidentally spilled a single drop onto a small potted plant that had long since withered.

Within moments, the leaves began to straighten.

Color returned.

New shoots appeared over the following hours.

The plant ultimately recovered completely.

The incident immediately halted disposal of the remaining sample.

Subsequent controlled testing demonstrated similar restorative effects.

Observed properties include:

- Revitalization of severely wilted plants.
    
- Accelerated healing of minor injuries.
    
- Promotion of tissue repair.
    
- Increased recovery rate of superficial wounds.
    

Medical Division emphasized that the tears are **not** a universal cure and are not capable of reversing every illness or injury. Their precise limits remain under investigation.

---

## **THEORY**

Research Division currently theorizes that the restorative effect is linked to Endmin's unique interaction with Ether.

Whether the phenomenon originates from her own anomalous biology, the lingering effects of Ether Corruption, or another unknown factor has not yet been determined.

Director Lucious added a note to the report:

> "Further study is authorized."

> "Collection is voluntary."

> "No resident is to be pressured into providing samples."

---

## **LUCY'S COMMENT**

When informed of the discovery, Lucy appeared genuinely surprised.

> **Lucy:** "...I didn't know."

She quietly looked toward Endmin.

> "...You never cried much."

Endmin smiled faintly.

> "...There wasn't much reason to."

Lucy understood immediately.

Before coming to the Blacksite...

Endmin had rarely allowed herself to cry.

The realization weighed heavily on her.

---

## **PERSONNEL REACTIONS**

### Taiga Sunbringer

Taiga stared at the report.

Then at Endmin.

> "...Your tears heal things?"

Endmin nodded.

Taiga smiled.

> "...That's so you."

---

### Mart Sunbringer

Mart looked thoughtful.

> "...You used to protect people..."

He smiled softly.

> "...Now even your tears do."

Endmin laughed quietly.

---

### Star

Star immediately brought a tray of flowers from the gardens.

Several had begun to wilt naturally.

With Endmin's permission, a tiny droplet was placed onto one blossom.

The flower gradually regained its vitality.

Star smiled brightly.

> "...Beautiful."

---

### Frost

Frost jokingly remarked,

> "...Please don't cry on my garden."

She paused.

> "...Actually..."

She looked at the flourishing flowers.

> "...Maybe do."

The room laughed.

Even Endmin.

---

## **ENDMIN'S RESPONSE**

When researchers asked how she felt about possessing such an unusual ability, Endmin remained quiet for a while.

Finally she answered.

> "...It's strange."

She looked at the small vial containing a voluntarily collected sample.

> "...Before..."

She rested a hand on her rifle.

> "...People knew me because I ended fights."

Then she looked toward the revived flower.

> "...Now..."

A gentle smile appeared.

> "...Maybe I can help things grow, too."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Z-321 – Endmin**

- Tear production: Anomalous.
    
- Collection method: Naturally retained within protective mask.
    
- Restorative properties: Confirmed.
    
- Research participation: Voluntary.
    
- Medical risk: None currently identified.
    

Research Division continues to investigate the mechanism responsible for the restorative effects while maintaining strict ethical oversight.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Several days later, one of the gardeners quietly left a small, struggling potted flower outside Room N-07 with a handwritten note.

> _"Only if you want to."_

The next morning, the flower stood upright, healthy once again.

No one saw Endmin step outside that night.

No one asked if she had.

The empty watering can beside the pot—and the faint smile on Endmin's face that morning—were answer enough.
