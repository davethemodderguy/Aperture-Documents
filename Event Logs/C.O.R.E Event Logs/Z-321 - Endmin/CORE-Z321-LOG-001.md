# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-001**

### **INITIAL CONTACT — Z-321 "ENDMIN"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – N.O.S.T. RESIDENT ADMISSION

---

**Date:** ██/██/████  
**Location:** N.O.S.T. Portal Hub

---

### **SUMMARY**

At approximately **14:27**, the N.O.S.T. Portal Hub registered an unscheduled dimensional breach. The breach was immediately identified as an authorized Banlands transit rift.

The individual who emerged was identified as **Lucy**, Warden of the Banlands.

No hostile intent was detected.

---

## **INCIDENT LOG**

A violet rift tore open in the center of the Portal Hub.

A tall woman in a dark professional office suit stepped through, adjusting one of her sleeves before looking around.

> **Lucy:** "Hello."

Taiga instinctively hid behind Mart.

> **Taiga:** "Eek! Who's that?!"

Director Lucious sighed before clearing his throat.

> **Director Lucious:** "Everyone... meet Lucy."

> "...She's the Warden of the Banlands."

The room immediately fell quiet.

Astral stared at the newcomer.

Lucy turned toward her.

For several seconds...

Neither of them spoke.

Then—

> **Lucy:** "...Astral?"

Astral's eyes widened.

> **Astral:** "...Lucy?"

Without another word, both rushed forward and embraced each other tightly.

Neither attempted to hide the emotion.

Astral quietly laughed through tears.

> **Astral:** "...It's really you..."

Lucy smiled warmly.

> "...You're still terrible at staying dead."

Astral laughed harder.

> "...Not by choice."

---

Taiga, meanwhile, had become completely starstruck.

She practically bounced where she stood.

> **Taiga:** "Lucy?!"

> "OH MY GOD, I'M YOUR BIGGEST FAN!"

Lucy blinked.

Then laughed.

She gently patted Taiga's head.

> **Lucy:** "Really?"

Taiga had already produced a notebook seemingly from nowhere.

> "...Please sign it!"

Lucy accepted the notebook with an amused smile.

She signed her name before handing it back.

Taiga immediately hugged it to her chest.

> "...I'm never washing this page."

Mart quietly leaned toward Director Lucious.

> **Mart:** "...Can paper even be washed?"

Lucious whispered back.

> "...Don't question it."

---

After introductions concluded, everyone gathered around one of the meeting tables.

Director Lucious folded his hands.

> **Director Lucious:** "So..."

> "..._The_ Lucy had to come all the way here."

> "...What's wrong?"

Lucy gave him a look.

One eyebrow slowly raised.

> **Lucy:** "I think..."

> "...you owe me a favor."

Lucious blinked.

> "...For?"

Lucy smiled.

> "...Reviving all of your clumsy dead employees."

Silence.

Lucious slowly scratched the back of his head.

> **Director Lucious:** "...Yeah."

> "...I guess I do."

A pause.

> "...So..."

> "...What do you need?"

Lucy nodded.

> **Lucy:** "Give me a second."

She turned toward the still-open dimensional rift.

> **Lucy:** "Endmin."

> "...Come here."

A small figure cautiously stepped through.

She appeared close to Mart's height and age.

Despite the unusual metal mask covering her eyes, she carried herself with unmistakable enthusiasm.

She immediately waved.

> **Endmin:** "Hi, everyone!"

> "I'm Endmin!"

> "Nice to meet you!"

The room waved back.

Lucy smiled fondly.

> **Lucy:** "Don't worry about her."

> "...She's just a little shy around strangers."

Everyone looked at Endmin.

Endmin awkwardly rubbed the back of her neck.

> **Endmin:** "...Mom..."

> "...You're making it worse."

Laughter spread around the room.

Even Mart smiled.

---

Lucy rested a hand on Endmin's shoulder.

Her tone became more serious.

> **Lucy:** "Endmin is my daughter."

> "I have a lot to deal with right now."

> "...Both in the Banlands..."

> "...and elsewhere."

She looked directly at Director Lucious.

> **Lucy:** "I'm entrusting her to Aperture Industries."

> "...Could you look after her..."

> "...while I handle both my problems..."

A small smile.

> "...and yours?"

Director Lucious answered without hesitation.

> **Director Lucious:** "Of course."

> "...She'll be safe here."

Lucy visibly relaxed.

> "...Thank you."

She gently hugged Endmin one last time.

> **Lucy:** "Be good."

Endmin nodded enthusiastically.

> "...I will!"

Lucy smiled at everyone one final time before stepping backward into the dimensional rift.

Moments later...

The portal quietly closed.

---

## **POST-INCIDENT**

Following standard admission procedures, Endmin was designated:

> **Z-321 — ENDMIN**

Residential assignment:

> **Room N-07**

Fortunately, the room still contained an unused bed.

Taiga had long since developed the habit of sleeping on the floor, insisting it was "more comfortable."

When staff reminded her there was an empty bed available, she simply replied,

> "...Floor."

No further explanation was given.

The bed was therefore prepared for Endmin.

---

## **ROOM N-07 INTERACTIONS**

Endmin entered the room carrying a single backpack.

She looked around with obvious curiosity.

Mart gave a small wave.

> **Mart:** "...Hi."

> "...I'm Mart."

Taiga enthusiastically pointed at herself.

> **Taiga:** "I'm Taiga!"

Astral smiled warmly.

> **Astral:** "Welcome home."

Endmin looked around the room.

Then smiled.

A genuine one.

> **Endmin:** "...Thanks."

> "...It's nice here."

Mart quietly picked up her backpack before she could protest.

> "...I'll help."

Endmin blinked.

> "...You're... really nice."

Mart smiled shyly.

> "...I try."

### **Closing Observation**

At lights out, Endmin looked around Room N-07.

Astral was reading.

Mart was quietly organizing books on his shelf.

Taiga had already curled up on the floor with a blanket despite having an empty bed available.

Endmin looked at the empty bed beside hers.

Then back at her new roommates.

She smiled to herself.

Very quietly, so nobody else could hear, she whispered,

> "...I think..."

> "...I'm going to like living here."
