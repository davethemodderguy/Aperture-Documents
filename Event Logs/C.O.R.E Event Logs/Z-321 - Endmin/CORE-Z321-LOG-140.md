# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-140**

### **DIETARY PROFILE – BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Personnel Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

Belle Merryweather has simple eating habits compared to most Blacksite personnel.

Her favorite snacks consist primarily of **sweets** and **vegetables**, with one particular habit becoming instantly recognizable throughout Heaven's Blacksite.

She almost always carries a fresh carrot.

---

## **Preferred Snacks**

Belle has been observed regularly eating:

- Carrots.
    
- Lettuce and leafy vegetables.
    
- Salads.
    
- Candies.
    
- Chocolate.
    
- Cookies.
    
- Sweet pastries.
    

Despite repeatedly claiming she "doesn't like vegetables," personnel have documented her happily eating them on numerous occasions.

This statement is universally regarded as unconvincing.

---

## **The Carrot**

Belle is rarely seen without a carrot tucked into one of the pouches on her dress or clipped to her equipment belt.

Whenever she becomes bored, waits during meetings, or accompanies others on patrol, she quietly pulls out the carrot and begins chewing on it.

The habit is so common that many researchers can identify Belle's presence before seeing her.

> **"...Crunch..."**

> **"...Crunch..."**

> **"...Crunch..."**

---

## **Observation Log**

During a Level 6 departmental briefing, Belle remained unusually quiet.

While senior personnel discussed security logistics, the room was silent except for one sound.

**Crunch.**

Director Lucious briefly looked up from the briefing documents.

Belle was calmly chewing on a carrot.

Noticing everyone looking at her, Belle paused.

> **"...What?"

Endmin sighed.

> **"...Nothing."

Lucius smiled.

> **"...Carry on."

Belle nodded and resumed.

**Crunch.**

The meeting continued.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Belle continues to favor sweets and vegetables as her primary snacks.

Although Belle still insists she dislikes vegetables...

Personnel have yet to witness any evidence supporting that claim.
