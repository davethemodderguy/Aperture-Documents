# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-049**

### **SKATING LESSONS & THE ONE-OF-A-KIND BOOTS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Recreational / Engineering Record

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following her successful trial with Endmin's custom Mobility Boots, Z-885 (Taiga) developed an interest in roller skating.

Over the following weeks, Endmin personally taught Taiga the basics, eventually progressing to advanced skating techniques around the Blacksite's recreation corridors.

Engineering Division has confirmed that while skating itself can be taught, **Endmin's Mobility Boots will remain unique equipment designed exclusively for Z-321.**

---

## **Learning to Skate**

Training began in one of the Blacksite's indoor recreation halls.

At first, Taiga struggled with balance.

> **Endmin:** "Bend your knees a little."

> **Taiga:** "Like this?"

> **Endmin:** "...Less 'about to pounce on someone.'"

Mart watched from a nearby bench.

> "...She's trying."

Within an hour, Taiga could skate without falling.

Within a week, she was confidently weaving through cones.

By the end of the month...

She was racing Endmin through the designated skating course.

She still lost.

---

## **Engineering Statement**

Following several requests from personnel, Engineering Division issued an official clarification regarding Endmin's equipment.

### The Mobility Boots are:

- Personally designed by Endmin.
    
- Custom-built around her skating style.
    
- Individually calibrated to her balance, stride, and reaction speed.
    
- Integrated with proprietary control systems.
    

For these reasons, **the retractable skates, blade conversion mechanism, booster, and internal control package are exclusive to Endmin's boots.**

No duplicates will be manufactured.

Personnel interested in skating are encouraged to use standard recreational skates instead.

---

## **Lucy's Request**

During one of her routine visits, Lucy watched Endmin effortlessly skate across the hangar before coming to a graceful stop.

She folded her arms.

> **Lucy:** "Can you make me a pair?"

Endmin stared at her for a second.

Then burst into laughter.

> **Endmin:** "Mom..."

She laughed harder.

> "...Can you imagine you trying to stop?"

Lucy raised an eyebrow.

> "I think I'd do fine."

Neko, who had overheard the conversation from across the workshop, looked up from his workbench.

> **Neko:** "Respectfully..."

> "...No."

The room erupted into laughter.

Even Lucy couldn't help smiling.

> "...Fair enough."

### **Closing Observation**

One snowy afternoon, Taiga and Endmin rolled side by side through the Blacksite courtyard.

Both were skating.

Only one left a faint blue trail from a tiny booster hidden in her boots.

Taiga glanced over.

> **"You're still faster."**

Endmin smiled.

> **"The boots help."**

Taiga shook her head.

> **"No."**

She laughed.

> **"It's because you've had a lot more practice."**

Endmin slowed slightly until they were skating side by side again.

This time, neither of them was trying to win.

They simply enjoyed the ride together.
