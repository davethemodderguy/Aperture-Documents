# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-092**

### **RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION – BELLE'S BRAID**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Cultural

---

## **SUMMARY**

Room N-07 residents recently observed Belle with her head covering removed during an evening inside the residential wing.

Although most personnel were already familiar with Belle's rabbit ears, few had ever seen her hair fully uncovered.

The reveal surprised several residents.

---

## **Physical Observation**

With her head uncovered, Belle's hair was observed to be a **single, exceptionally long braid** extending from the back of her head down to approximately her hips.

The braid measures approximately **45 cm** in braided length and is maintained with remarkable care.

Rather than using ordinary ribbons, the braid is decorated with several small ornaments that once belonged to Endmin.

These include:

- Small decorative ribbons.
    
- Metallic hair clips.
    
- Floral accessories previously worn by Endmin.
    

Behavioral Sciences noted that Belle appears to treat the decorations with great care.

---

## **Origin**

When asked about the ornaments, Belle smiled.

> **"...Endmin gave them to me."

She gently held the end of the braid.

> **"...She said they looked prettier on me."

Endmin immediately became flustered.

> **"...I-I just wasn't using them anymore..."

Taiga quietly looked between the two.

> **"...Sure."

The room erupted into laughter.

---

## **Why She Keeps It Braided**

Belle explained that she almost always keeps her hair braided.

The style prevents it from becoming tangled during:

- Sword practice.
    
- Patrol duties.
    
- Running.
    
- Falling over while sprinting.
    

After a brief pause, Belle added,

> **"...Mostly the last one."

Mart nodded.

> **"...That checks out."

---

> "...I'd rather see someone wearing them than leaving them in a drawer."

Belle looked proudly at her braid.

> **"...Priorities."

---

---

After reading the report, Director Lucious wrote a short note.

> **"Small gestures often carry the greatest meaning."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Belle continues wearing her hair in its signature long braid, decorated with accessories gifted by Endmin.

The braid has become one of Belle's most recognizable features alongside her oversized sword, rabbit ears, and the small bell attached to her leg.

---

### **Closing Observation**

One quiet evening, Endmin noticed that one of the ribbons woven into Belle's braid had come loose.

Without saying a word, she walked over.

> **"...Hold still."

Belle obediently stood still.

Endmin carefully retied the ribbon, straightened a small flower clip, and gently smoothed the braid.

> **"...There."

Belle reached back to feel it.

> **"...Perfect?"

Endmin smiled.

> **"...Perfect."

Belle's rabbit ears twitched happily.

> **"...Thanks."

As the two walked back toward Room N-07 together, the tiny bell on Belle's leg jingled softly, while the braid—decorated with Endmin's keepsakes—swayed gently behind her with every step.
