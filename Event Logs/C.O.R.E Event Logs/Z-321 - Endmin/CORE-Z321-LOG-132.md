# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-132**

### **PERSONNEL WELFARE OBSERVATION – BEHAVIORAL CHANGE: BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Personnel Welfare

---

## **SUMMARY**

Over the course of several weeks, personnel assigned to Room N-07 observed a gradual but noticeable change in Belle Merryweather's behavior.

No single incident appeared to trigger the change.

When asked directly, Belle consistently replied that she was fine.

However, those closest to her began noticing that many of the little things that defined Belle's cheerful personality had quietly disappeared.

---

## **Observed Changes**

The following behavioral changes have been consistently documented:

- No longer sprinting through the hallways with her characteristic rabbit-like run.
    
- Walking at a normal, measured pace.
    
- Speaking less during group conversations.
    
- Smiling less frequently.
    
- Reduced playful behavior.
    
- Spending more time quietly completing paperwork in her office.
    

No decline in work performance has been observed.

Her professionalism remains unchanged.

Only her personality appears different.

---

## **Room N-07**

One afternoon, Taiga watched Belle walk into the room carrying a stack of reports.

She crossed the room.

No squeaky sprint.

No oversized sword balanced over her head.

No cheerful greeting.

She simply placed the documents on her desk.

> **"...Morning."**

Then sat down.

Taiga quietly looked toward Endmin.

> **"...Did she just..."

Endmin nodded before Taiga could finish.

> **"...Yeah."

> **"...She walked."**

---

## **The Question**

Later that evening, Endmin sat beside Belle.

> **"...Belle?"

Belle looked up from her paperwork.

> **"Hm?"

> **"...Is something wrong?"

Belle smiled.

It was polite.

Small.

Almost practiced.

> **"...Nothing's wrong."

Endmin frowned.

> **"...Really?"

Belle nodded.

> **"...I'm okay."

Then she returned to reading.

Endmin remained unconvinced.

---

## **Personnel Observations**

---

### Star

> **"...She hasn't laughed much lately."

---

### Mart

> **"...She's still kind."

> **"...Just... quieter."

---

### Lucius

Lucius watched Belle leave the office one evening.

After a long silence, he quietly said:

> **"...Sometimes..."

> **"...People stop smiling before they stop hurting."

---

### Lucy

Lucy had known Belle long enough to notice the smallest changes.

She watched Belle complete another day's paperwork in complete silence.

After Belle left the room, Lucy sighed.

> **"...She says she's fine."

She looked toward Endmin.

> **"...But she isn't acting like Belle."**

---

## **Director Lucious' Note**

Director Lucious reviewed the observation log before adding a recommendation.

> **"Behavioral changes should not be ignored simply because someone insists they are well."**

> **"Sometimes concern begins with noticing what is no longer there."**

He authorized no disciplinary action and no mandatory intervention.

Instead, he instructed personnel only to remain observant and supportive.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Belle continues to perform all professional duties to an exceptional standard.

However, personnel have unanimously noted the gradual disappearance of many of her usual cheerful habits.

The energetic rabbit who once sprinted through the Blacksite with a 50-kilogram sword over her head...

Now simply walks.

When asked if anything is wrong...

She always gives the same answer.

> **"...I'm okay."**

But for those who know Belle best...

The answer doesn't quite match what they see.
