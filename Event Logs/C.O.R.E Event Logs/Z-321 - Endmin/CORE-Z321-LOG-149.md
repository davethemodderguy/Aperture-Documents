### **STARLANDS RETREAT – TWO-DAY PICNIC**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the planning meeting in Room N-07, the residents of Heaven's Blacksite departed for a **two-day recreational retreat** to **Z-556 – STARLANDS**.

The retreat was approved by Director Lucious to improve morale after an extended period of investigations, rescue operations, and government assignments.

For the duration of the retreat, all non-essential work was suspended.

# **DAY ONE**

The group arrived through an Aperture gateway shortly after sunrise.

Starlands greeted them with gentle winds, floating islands, crystal-clear lakes, and its characteristic rhombus-shaped sun hanging high overhead.

Belle immediately spread her arms.

> **"Vacation!"**

Taiga didn't even wait for everyone else.

She sprinted toward the open meadow.

> **"Last one there is buying dessert!"

Mart simply flew after her.

---

## **Lunch**

Lucy and Neko worked together over the barbecue while Bao prepared drinks.

Astral arranged desserts on a large picnic blanket.

Frost kept everything chilled with carefully formed ice.

Belle proudly placed an enormous basket in the center.

Inside...

Mostly carrots.

Alice laughed quietly.

> **"...You really did."

Belle nodded proudly.

> **"...I said I would."

---

## **Lucius' Painting**

While everyone ate, Lucius wandered toward a nearby hill carrying his easel.

He spent several hours painting the floating islands and endless sky.

Endmin quietly sat beside him.

Neither spoke.

She simply watched him paint.

Eventually Lucius smiled.

> **"...I never thought we'd get to do something like this again."

Endmin leaned gently against his shoulder.

> **"...Me neither."

---

## **Games**

The afternoon quickly became chaotic.

Taiga organized a game of tag.

Star teleported around the field laughing.

Nightshade quietly appeared beside unsuspecting players before disappearing again.

Mart cheated slightly by flying.

Belle bounced across the meadow instead of running.

Nobody complained.

---

## **The Lake**

Later, everyone moved to one of Starlands' lakes.

Bao playfully lifted Belle into the air using flowing water.

Belle happily "swam" through the sky.

> **"...I'm a flying bunny!"

Frost froze small sections of the lake into stepping stones.

Taiga challenged everyone to cross without falling in.

Belle simply bounced from one ice platform to another.

---

## **Evening**

As daylight faded, everyone gathered around a campfire.

Lucius quietly showed everyone the painting he had completed.

It depicted the entire group sitting together beneath the floating islands.

No one had noticed he had included every single person.

Belle looked at the painting for a long time.

Then carefully hugged Lucius.

> **"...Can I have a copy someday?"

Lucius smiled.

> **"...Of course."

---

# **DAY TWO**

The second morning began peacefully.

Most of the group woke naturally to birdsong.

Lucius, however, was already outside sketching the sunrise.

Belle eventually wandered over carrying two mugs of hot chocolate.

She quietly handed one to him.

Neither said anything.

The silence was comfortable.

---

## **Exploration**

Star volunteered to guide everyone around several nearby floating islands.

She eagerly pointed out familiar landmarks while explaining stories associated with Starlands.

Nightshade occasionally corrected small details.

Star pretended to be offended.

Everyone knew she appreciated the help.

---

## **Group Photograph**

Before returning home, Alice insisted on taking a group photograph.

After several failed attempts because Star kept making funny faces...

Everyone finally stood still.

Belle was in the middle.

Endmin stood beside her.

Lucius stood with Endmin.

Lucy smiled behind them.

The Friendly Roommates gathered together.

Astral and Bao stood near the back.

Alice pressed the shutter.

**Click.**

The photograph was later framed and placed inside Room N-07.

---

## **Return**

As the gateway home opened, nobody seemed particularly eager to leave.

Belle looked back one last time.

> **"...We'll come back."

Star smiled.

> **"...Starlands will be waiting."

The group stepped through the portal together.

## **AFTERWORD**

The framed photograph and Lucius' painting remain displayed in **Room N-07**.

For many of those present, the retreat became a reminder that even in a world of anomalies, dangerous missions, and endless responsibilities, there was still time to enjoy the simple things:

A shared meal.

A quiet sunset.

A good conversation.

And two peaceful days spent together in Starlands.
