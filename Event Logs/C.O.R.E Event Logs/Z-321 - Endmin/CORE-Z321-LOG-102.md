### **PERSONNEL ARRIVAL – ALICE'S TEMPORARY RESIDENTIAL ASSIGNMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following Lucy Merryweather's temporary assignment away from APRT-70, **Alice**, Head of Security of the Merryweather Manor, was temporarily assigned to the Blacksite.

Her objective was straightforward:

> Ensure Endmin was eating properly, sleeping when possible, and not overworking herself.

Endmin was... less than enthusiastic.

---

## **Arrival**

There was a knock on Room N-07's door.

Endmin opened it.

Standing outside was Alice, carrying a travel bag and several neatly organized folders.

Alice smiled.

> **"Lady Lucy assigned me here until she returns."

Endmin immediately sighed.

> **"...Mom actually did it."

Alice nodded apologetically.

> **"She specifically said, 'Make sure Endmin doesn't skip meals.'"

Endmin buried her face in her hands.

> **"...This is so embarrassing..."

Alice couldn't help but laugh.

### **Closing Observation**

That evening, Endmin quietly slipped out of Room N-07, hoping to grab a late-night snack without anyone noticing.

As she reached the hallway...

Alice was already standing there with a tray.

> **"...Lady Lucy said you might try this."

On the tray sat a balanced dinner, complete with vegetables.

Endmin froze.

She slowly looked from the tray...

...to Alice...

...then sighed in defeat.

> **"...Mom planned this, didn't she?"

Alice smiled knowingly.

> **"...She knows you very well."

From inside the room came Taiga's voice.

> **"...Called it!"

Endmin covered her face in embarrassment as laughter echoed through the hallway.
