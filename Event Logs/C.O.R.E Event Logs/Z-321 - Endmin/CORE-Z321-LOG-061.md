# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-061**

### **RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION – COMFORT BEHAVIORS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Behavioral

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following additional observation within **Room N-07**, Behavioral Sciences has documented that Z-321 (Endmin) relaxes many of the habits she maintains elsewhere in the Blacksite.

Among trusted companions, she no longer feels the need to continuously maintain her Ether concealment.

As a result, only members of the **Friendly Roommates** group routinely see her Thiren characteristics.

---

## **Visibility of Thiren Traits**

Outside Room N-07, Endmin instinctively maintains her Ether illusion, concealing her cat ears while her tail remains tucked beneath her folded wings.

Inside Room N-07, however, the illusion is often dismissed.

Her white cat ears become visible, occasionally twitching in response to sounds or emotions.

She has never made a formal announcement regarding this.

She simply... stopped hiding them around those she trusts.

---

## **Reasoning**

When asked why she only reveals them in the room, Endmin answered after a long pause.

> "...It's tiring pretending all the time."

She looked around at her roommates.

> "...Here..."

> "...I don't have to."

---

## **Observed Behaviors**

Behavioral Sciences noted several instinctive feline mannerisms that Endmin displays only when relaxed.

These behaviors appear entirely subconscious.

Examples include:

- Absentmindedly licking the collar or sleeve of her jacket while reading.
    
- Scratching the side of her face with the back of her hand in a distinctly cat-like manner.
    
- Her ears rotating toward unfamiliar sounds before she consciously reacts.
    
- Her tail occasionally shifting beneath her wings when surprised or excited.
    
- Curling up comfortably while sleeping, often with Aster nearby.
    

Endmin is generally unaware she is doing any of these until someone points them out.

---

## **Roommate Reactions**

### Taiga

Taiga was the first to notice Endmin quietly grooming the cuff of her sleeve while reading.

She watched for several seconds before smiling.

> **Taiga:** "...You know you're doing the cat thing again?"

Endmin immediately stopped.

She stared at her sleeve.

Then at Taiga.

> "...I was?"

Taiga nodded enthusiastically.

Endmin covered her face.

> "...Please don't tell anyone."

---

### Mart

Mart looked up from his book one evening as Endmin absentmindedly scratched behind one ear.

He smiled faintly.

> "...Comfortable?"

Endmin looked confused for a moment before realizing what she had been doing.

> "...Apparently."

---

### Neko

Neko glanced over from his workbench.

> "You keep forgetting your ears are visible."

Endmin instinctively reached for the top of her head.

Her ears flicked beneath her fingertips.

> "...Oops."

---

### Miss Neko

Miss Neko simply smiled.

> "It's nice seeing you relax."

---

### Bao

Bao laughed softly.

> "You don't have to act like a Warden every second of every day."

Endmin smiled.

> "...I'm starting to believe that."

---

### Frost

Frost found Endmin's ear movements fascinating.

She once spent several minutes trying to determine what different ear positions meant.

Endmin eventually noticed.

> "...You're studying me, aren't you?"

Frost nodded without embarrassment.

---

### Star

Star immediately asked,

> "...Can I pet them?"

Endmin hesitated.

Then sighed.

> "...Fine."

Star gently stroked one ear.

Endmin's ears twitched involuntarily.

Star's eyes lit up.

> "...They're so soft!"

---

### Nightshade

Nightshade folded her arms.

> "...You're blushing."

Endmin responded without looking up.

> "...I know."

---

### Astral

Astral simply chuckled.

> "Trust is a wonderful thing."

---

### Niko

Niko looked over from near the window.

> "...You look more comfortable like this."

Endmin quietly nodded.

---

### Aster

Aster required no permission.

She simply curled up beside Endmin as usual.

After a moment, she held up her whiteboard.

> **"Home."**

Endmin looked at the message.

Then gently rested her forehead against Aster's.

> "...Yeah."

> "...Home."

---

## **Director Lucious' Note**

Director Lucious reviewed the report and added a brief handwritten comment.

> **"Do not treat this as an anomaly."**

> **"Treat it as someone finally feeling safe enough to be herself."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Room N-07 remains the only location where Endmin consistently relaxes her Ether concealment and displays her natural Thiren traits.

Archive Division considers this a significant indicator of the trust she places in her roommates and the environment they have created together.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late one winter evening, the room was quiet.

Taiga was asleep on her rug.

Mart had dozed off with a book in his hands.

Neko and Miss Neko had retired for the night.

Star, Nightshade, Bao, Frost, Astral, and Niko were all resting.

Endmin sat by the window, reading.

Her ears gave a small, sleepy twitch as she absentmindedly scratched the side of her face.

Aster looked up from where she was curled against Endmin's side.

She quietly wrote on her whiteboard.

> **"You look happy."**

Endmin smiled.

For once, she didn't try to hide it.

> **"...I am."**
