### **PERSONNEL DISCUSSION – LUCIUS LEARNS ABOUT ENDMIN AND BELLE'S DUTIES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Residential / Historical

---

## **SUMMARY**

After settling into Room N-07, Lucius Merryweather began learning about the lives his sister and Belle built during the years he was missing.

While he knew Endmin had become powerful and respected, he had not fully understood the scale of their responsibilities within the Banlands government.

The conversation began with a simple question.

---

## **The Question**

One evening, Lucius looked over Endmin's equipment and noticed the official documents beside her.

Her Level 6 access card.

Her department insignia.

Her rifle.

Then he looked toward Belle, who was reviewing a large stack of compliance reports while her massive sword rested beside her.

He paused.

> **"...Can I ask something?"**

Endmin looked up.

> **"Sure."**

Lucius pointed between them.

> **"...What exactly do you two do?"**

Belle smiled.

> **"Our jobs?"**

Lucius nodded.

> **"I know you're important."**

He looked at Endmin.

> **"But I don't think I understand how important."**

---

# **Central Execution Department**

Endmin explained first.

She placed her rifle down.

> **"I'm the Head of the Central Execution Department."**

Lucius became quiet.

> **"...Execution?"**

Endmin nodded.

> **"We handle threats that normal authorities cannot."**

The department's responsibilities include:

- Eliminating high-level threats.
    
- Handling dangerous individuals who threaten Banlands stability.
    
- Securing high-value assets.
    
- Carrying out authorized operations against severe risks.
    

Endmin looked away slightly.

> **"It's not just about fighting."

> **"It's making sure fewer people get hurt later."**

Lucius understood.

He remembered the little girl who hid behind him when others mocked her.

Now she was the person standing between danger and everyone else.

---

# **Krampus Compliance Authority**

Belle followed.

She adjusted the documents in her hands.

> **"I'm the Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority."**

Lucius looked surprised.

> **"You?"**

Belle tilted her head.

> **"Me?"

He quickly shook his head.

> **"No, I mean..."

He looked at her cheerful expression.

Then at the 50-kilogram sword.

> **"...Actually, that makes sense."**

Everyone laughed.

Belle explained.

The Krampus Compliance Authority is responsible for:

- Preventing corruption within government systems.
    
- Investigating bribery and misconduct.
    
- Ensuring officials follow Banlands laws.
    
- Holding powerful individuals accountable.
    

> **"Powerful people still need rules."**

Belle's expression became serious.

> **"Especially powerful people."**

---

## **Lucius' Reaction**

For several moments, Lucius said nothing.

He looked at both of them.

His little sister.

And the girl who had become part of their family.

Two people who had once needed protection.

Now they were the ones protecting others.

> **"...I missed a lot."

Endmin immediately looked at him.

> **"Lucius..."

He smiled.

> **"No."

> **"I'm not upset."

> **"I'm just realizing how much happened while I was gone."**

Lucius continues adjusting to the reality that four years of his absence transformed many people around him.

Endmin is no longer the younger sister who needed him to stand in front of her.

Endmin.

Belle.

And family.
