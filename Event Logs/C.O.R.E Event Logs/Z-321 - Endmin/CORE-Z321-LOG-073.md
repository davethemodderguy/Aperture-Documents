# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-073**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION – STRESS & SELF-SOOTHING BEHAVIORS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Behavioral / Residential

---

## **SUMMARY**

Behavioral Sciences has identified several involuntary self-soothing behaviors exhibited by Z-321 (Endmin).

These behaviors become noticeably more frequent when she is:

- Startled.
    
- Distressed.
    
- Deeply embarrassed.
    
- Interrupted during intense concentration.
    
- Mentally overwhelmed.
    

Medical Division has concluded that these are instinctive Thiren behaviors and should not be interpreted as signs of aggression or loss of self-control.

---

## **Observed Behaviors**

When under emotional stress, Endmin often displays subtle feline instincts without realizing it.

Documented behaviors include:

- Licking the back of one hand or the cuff of her sleeve absentmindedly.
    
- Briefly grooming her fingers before returning to what she was doing.
    
- Ears lowering or flattening.
    
- Tail wrapping closer around one leg.
    
- Quietly staring into space for several seconds before regaining focus.
    

These actions typically last only a short time before Endmin realizes what she is doing.

---

## **Distress Response**

The grooming behavior is most commonly observed after a frightening or highly stressful event.

Behavioral Sciences believes it functions as an unconscious method of calming herself.

During these moments, Endmin becomes unusually quiet and may not immediately respond when spoken to.

Medical staff recommend giving her a few moments to settle before continuing a conversation.

---

## **Incident Report**

Following an unexpected explosion during a training exercise, Endmin successfully shielded Taiga and Mart with her wings.

Once Medical confirmed everyone was safe, Endmin sat quietly on a nearby bench.

Without noticing, she began absentmindedly licking the back of one hand while staring at the ground.

Taiga slowly approached.

She sat beside her without speaking.

After nearly a minute, Endmin blinked.

She looked at her hand.

Then at Taiga.

> **Endmin:**
> 
> "...I did it again, didn't I?"

Taiga smiled gently.

> **"...Yeah."

> "...Just a little."

Endmin sighed in embarrassment.

> "...Please don't tell Neko."

From several meters away came Neko's voice.

> **"...Too late."**

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Behavioral Sciences has determined that these instinctive behaviors are a normal part of Endmin's Thiren physiology and emotional regulation.

Staff are encouraged to simply give her space or quiet reassurance if she appears distressed.

The behaviors almost always cease naturally once she has regained her composure.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, Endmin was quietly working in Engineering, softly humming as she adjusted a rifle component.

A toolbox unexpectedly slipped from a nearby shelf with a loud clang.

> **"Mew!"**

She jumped.

Her ears flattened, and before she even realized it, she had begun absentmindedly grooming the back of her hand.

Neko looked over from across the workshop.

He didn't comment.

He simply picked up the fallen toolbox and set it back on the shelf.

A few moments later, Endmin noticed what she was doing.

She looked at Neko.

> **"...You saw that?"

Neko shrugged with a small smile.

> **"...Yep."

> "...Didn't seem important."

Endmin smiled back.

> **"...Thanks."**
