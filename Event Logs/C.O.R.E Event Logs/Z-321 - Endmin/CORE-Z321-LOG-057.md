# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-057**

### **DIETARY OBSERVATION – THE VEGETABLE CONSPIRACY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Cafeteria Record

---

## **SUMMARY**

Cafeteria staff have noted that Z-321 (Endmin) and Z-885 (Taiga) consistently choose meals containing fresh vegetables.

Unlike Taiga, who openly admits she enjoys them, Endmin becomes noticeably embarrassed whenever anyone points out her preference.

Despite repeated denials...

The evidence has become overwhelming.

---

## **Observed Preferences**

Both Endmin and Taiga regularly order meals containing:

- Salads.
    
- Roasted vegetables.
    
- Stir-fried greens.
    
- Carrots.
    
- Broccoli.
    
- Bell peppers.
    
- Cucumbers.
    

When given a choice between an equal-quality vegetable side and a meat side...

Endmin has repeatedly selected the vegetables.

---

## **Behavioral Pattern**

Whenever someone comments on this habit, Endmin immediately attempts to explain it away.

Common responses include:

> **"Who said I like vegetables? This is a professional taste test."**

---

> **"Hey! I told you not to read my orders out loud."**

> **"That was a side dish for my steak."**

---

> **"...I'm evaluating nutritional balance."**

---

> **"...It's temporary."**

Medical Division has found no evidence supporting any of these explanations beyond Endmin simply enjoying vegetables.

---

## **Repeated Exposure**

Unfortunately for Endmin, maintaining the secret has proven difficult.

One afternoon in the cafeteria, Taiga enthusiastically carried over two large salads.

Without hesitation, Endmin smiled.

> **Endmin:** "That looks really good."

The two happily ate together while discussing skating and target practice.

Halfway through the meal, Endmin realized several researchers were staring.

She slowly looked down at her salad.

Then at everyone else.

> "...This isn't what it looks like."

Nobody believed her.

---

---

## **Medical Division Note**

Medical staff are pleased with Endmin's eating habits.

Her preference for vegetables contributes to a balanced diet and has been encouraged, particularly during recovery from recent illnesses.

No dietary concerns have been identified.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Taiga continues to openly enjoy vegetables.

Endmin continues to enjoy them just as much...

...while insisting she does not.

Archive Division has officially concluded that convincing anyone otherwise is no longer possible.

---

### **Closing Observation**

During dinner, Taiga happily pushed a bowl of fresh salad toward Endmin.

> **Taiga:** "Want some?"

Endmin glanced around the cafeteria to make sure no one was watching.

She quietly took a serving.

Then another.

Then another.

Neko happened to walk past.

He looked at the nearly empty bowl.

Then at Endmin.

> **Neko:** "...Professional taste test?"

Endmin froze.

Without missing a beat, she replied,

> **"...Extremely professional."**

From across the table, Taiga burst into laughter.

Endmin couldn't help laughing too, even if her face had turned bright red.
