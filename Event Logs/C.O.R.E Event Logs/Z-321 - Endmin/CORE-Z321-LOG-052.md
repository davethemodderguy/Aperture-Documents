# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-052**

### **FIRST DOCUMENTED INTERACTION – Z-321 "ENDMIN" & ASTER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Behavioral Observation

---

## **SUMMARY**

Although both Z-321 (Endmin) and Aster had occupied the Blacksite for an extended period, Archive Division discovered that Endmin had never formally introduced herself to Aster.

The reason was unexpectedly simple.

Endmin believed Aster was merely someone's exceptionally well-behaved pet cat.

---

## **Initial Assumption**

Since her arrival, Endmin had frequently noticed a white cat quietly resting in hallways, laboratories, gardens, and even the shooting range.

Curiously...

The cat always seemed to appear wherever she happened to be.

Endmin assumed the animal simply liked following people.

Having a fondness for cats, she never questioned it.

Instead, she would occasionally kneel down.

Scratch behind Aster's ears.

Smile.

Then continue with her day.

---

## **Aster's Perspective**

When later asked why she never introduced herself, Aster simply picked up her small whiteboard.

She wrote:

> **"She is careful."**

After a pause, she added another sentence.

> **"She almost never chooses the dangerous option."**

Using telepathy, Aster elaborated further.

> **"Usually I intervene before someone makes a disastrous decision."**

> **"Endmin rarely gives me a reason to."**

According to Aster, Endmin naturally evaluates situations with exceptional patience and caution.

Because of this, Aster seldom found it necessary to warn or redirect her.

---

## **The Discovery**

The misunderstanding ended one afternoon in the greenhouse.

Endmin was sitting among the flowers with Aster curled comfortably in her lap.

She absentmindedly stroked the cat's fur while reading maintenance notes for her rifle.

Director Lucious happened to walk past.

He smiled.

> **Director Lucious:**
> 
> "Getting along with Aster?"

Endmin nodded happily.

> **"...She's adorable."**

Lucious looked mildly surprised.

> **"You know she can communicate, right?"**

Endmin blinked.

> "...What?"

Aster slowly opened one eye.

She lifted her whiteboard.

With practiced precision, she wrote:

> **"Hello, Endmin."**

Endmin froze.

She looked at the whiteboard.

Then at Aster.

Then back at the whiteboard.

> "...You can write?"

Aster nodded.

Using gentle telepathy, a calm voice followed inside Endmin's mind.

> **"Hello."**

Endmin nearly jumped.

> "...YOU CAN TALK?!"

Aster simply blinked.

---

## **An Immediate Friendship**

After the initial surprise wore off, Endmin's reaction was exactly what staff expected.

She hugged Aster.

Very gently.

> **"...You're even cooler than I thought."**

Aster accepted the hug without complaint.

On her whiteboard she wrote:

> **"Thank you."**

---

## **Shared Recognition**

Archive Division later noted an interesting parallel between the two.

Although their methods differ greatly, both possess abilities centered around perceiving what others cannot.

### **Aster**

- Future perception.
    
- Detection of dangerous decisions.
    
- Predictive intuition.
    

### **Endmin**

- Ether Sense.
    
- Exceptional environmental awareness.
    
- Perception beyond ordinary vision.
    

For this reason, both had already been listed under the informal Archive designation:

## **The Oracles**

When Endmin learned about the group name, she looked at Aster.

Then smiled.

> "...I like it."

Aster wrote:

> **"Me too."**

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Endmin and Aster have become close companions.

Aster continues accompanying Endmin throughout the Blacksite, though now Endmin understands that the quiet white cat beside her is not simply following her out of curiosity.

She's choosing to.

Interestingly, Aster reports that she still rarely needs to intervene.

According to her latest whiteboard message:

> **"Endmin usually notices trouble before it reaches her."**

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, Endmin sat in the greenhouse reading while Aster slept comfortably across her lap.

A junior researcher walked past.

> **Researcher:**
> 
> "Cute cat."

Without opening her eyes, Aster quietly lifted her whiteboard with one paw.

It read:

> **"Thank you."**

The researcher screamed.

Endmin sighed.

> "...I probably should've warned you."

Aster erased the board.

Then wrote one final message.

> **":3"**

Endmin laughed so hard she nearly dropped the marker.
