# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-118**

### **COMBAT OBSERVATION – BELLE MERRYWEATHER'S UNORTHODOX STYLE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Training Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

During a routine training session, personnel observed Belle Merryweather demonstrating a unique combat style that combines her exceptional physical strength, agility, and unconventional use of her oversized weapon.

Despite carrying a weapon weighing approximately 50 kilograms, Belle displays remarkable mobility and control.

---

## **Combat Demonstration**

The training area was prepared for a standard sparring session.

Most personnel expected Belle to rely on raw strength.

They were incorrect.

After drawing her massive sword, Belle moved several steps forward before suddenly driving the blade into the ground.

The entire training floor shook slightly.

Several observers assumed she had stopped.

She had not.

Using the sword as a fixed support point, Belle launched herself upward.

She rotated around the planted blade, using it as a temporary pole while transitioning between movements.

Her technique combined:

- Acrobatics.
    
- Evasion.
    
- Momentum-based attacks.
    
- Rapid repositioning.
    
- Heavy weapon control.
    

The result looked less like traditional sword fighting and more like a combination of combat and aerial movement.

## **Belle's Explanation**

When asked how she developed the technique, Belle simply smiled.

> **"My sword is heavy."**

She lifted it back onto her shoulder.

> **"So I learned how to make the weight help me."**

She explained that her fighting style relies on controlling momentum rather than resisting it.

Her small frame allows her to change direction quickly, while her Thiren strength allows her to use the weapon as both an offensive tool and a movement aid.

---

## **Training Notes**

Instructors recorded several unusual advantages:

- Belle's lightweight body allows rapid movement.
    
- Her rabbit Thiren traits provide exceptional jumping ability.
    
- Her strength allows her to safely handle her oversized weapon.
    
- Her agility allows her to perform maneuvers normally impossible with heavy equipment.
    

However, instructors also noted:

> **"Do not attempt to copy Belle's techniques without appropriate physical capability."**
