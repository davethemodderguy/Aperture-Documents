# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-072**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION – SINGING & HUMMING HABITS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Behavioral

---

## **SUMMARY**

Behavioral Sciences has documented a distinction between two musical habits regularly exhibited by Z-321 (Endmin).

While both involve music, they differ significantly in terms of comfort and social interaction.

- **Singing** is a deeply private activity.
    
- **Humming** is an unconscious habit indicating intense concentration.
    

---

## **Private Singing**

Endmin remains extremely reluctant to sing in front of others.

When she believes she is completely alone, she occasionally sings songs she has learned from Earth during her time at the Blacksite.

Her repertoire consists primarily of gentle ballads, folk songs, and quiet contemporary melodies.

She does not perform.

She simply enjoys singing.

If someone accidentally discovers her, the song stops almost immediately.

Typical responses include:

> "...How long have you been standing there?"

Or simply covering her face in embarrassment while her ears flatten.

Even when reassured that her voice is beautiful, Endmin rarely changes her mind.

> "I like singing."

> "...I just don't like people watching."

---

## **Incident Report**

Late one evening, Niko walked toward the greenhouse to enjoy the night air.

As he approached, he heard someone singing softly.

It was Endmin.

Standing alone beneath the glass ceiling, looking up at the stars.

She never noticed him.

Niko quietly smiled.

Without entering the greenhouse, he turned around and walked away.

The next morning, he never mentioned it.

Endmin never knew anyone had heard her.

---

## **Humming**

Unlike singing, humming is almost entirely subconscious.

Whenever Endmin is deeply focused, she begins humming without realizing it.

This has been observed while:

- Maintaining her rifle.
    
- Engineering new equipment with Neko.
    
- Reading technical manuals.
    
- Repairing skating equipment.
    
- Sharpening her baseball bat.
    
- Caring for plants.
    

The humming is generally soft, rhythmic, and continuous.

---

## **Important Observation**

Interrupting Endmin while she is humming is strongly discouraged.

The humming reliably indicates that she has entered a state of intense concentration.

Breaking that concentration causes immediate annoyance.

No exceptions have been documented.

Whether the interruption comes from:

- Taiga.
    
- Mart.
    
- Neko.
    
- Lucy.
    
- Director Lucious.
    

The response is nearly identical.

She slowly looks up.

Pauses her work.

Then politely—but very clearly—asks not to be interrupted.

---

## **Recorded Example**

Engineering Workshop.

Endmin was carefully adjusting the mechanism linking her rifle's selector switch to the underbarrel shotgun.

She quietly hummed to herself.

Neko approached.

> **Neko:**

> "...Endmin?"

No response.

Still humming.

> "...Endmin?"

Nothing.

He gently tapped the workbench.

The humming stopped.

Very slowly, Endmin looked up.

> "...Yes?"

Neko immediately recognized the expression.

> "...I'll come back in ten minutes."

> "...Good idea."

The humming resumed before he had reached the doorway.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Endmin continues to keep her singing private, preferring moments of solitude to enjoy music from Earth.

Her humming, however, has become a familiar sound throughout Engineering and Room N-07, serving as an unofficial indicator that she is completely absorbed in whatever task lies before her.

Residents have informally adopted a simple rule:

> **"If Endmin is humming... let her finish first."**

It has proven remarkably effective.
