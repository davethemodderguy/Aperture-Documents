### **EQUIPMENT ARCHIVE UPDATE – LUCIUS MERRYWEATHER'S PRIMARY WEAPON**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Personnel Equipment Record

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following a review of Lucius Merryweather's combat history, personnel records have been updated.

Although Lucius carries a pair of prototype pistols for ranged engagements, they are considered secondary weapons.

His **primary weapon** is a **retractable spear**, chosen because it reflects the combat style he developed during the four years he spent surviving after his disappearance.

---

## **Primary Weapon**

**Designation:** Retractable Combat Spear

**Primary User:** Lucius Merryweather

**Manufacturer:** Aperture Industries Armory (Designed with Neko's assistance)

---

### **Construction**

The spear is forged from a proprietary alloy consisting of:

- Nekonium
    
- Titanium
    
- Platinum
    

The combination gives the weapon a smooth, polished white finish while maintaining excellent durability without excessive weight.

Its appearance is elegant and understated, matching Lucius' preference for practical equipment.

---

### **Extension Mechanism**

Unlike a conventional spear, the shaft incorporates a telescoping mechanism.

At the press of a button, it extends from:

- **Collapsed:** 45 cm
    
- **Extended:** 95 cm
    

The mechanism locks securely into place and can be retracted just as quickly after combat.

This allows Lucius to carry it discreetly while retaining the reach of a polearm.

---

## **Combat History**

When questioned about why he preferred a spear over more conventional weapons, Lucius answered quietly:

> **"...Because it was what I had."**

During the four years he was forced to work under the Banlands Watchdogs, firearms were not always available to him.

He often had to defend himself with whatever was within reach.

Most commonly:

- Spears.
    
- Wooden poles.
    
- Brooms.
    
- Other long-handled tools.
    

Over time, he developed an efficient fighting style centered around distance control, precision, and quick footwork.

When Aperture Industries offered to build him a personal weapon, he requested only one thing.

> **"...Make it a spear."**

---

## **Training Observation**

During a sparring session with Endmin, Lucius demonstrated the weapon.

With a press of the release button—

**Click.**

The compact handle smoothly extended into a full-length spear.

Endmin smiled.

> **"...Still using a spear?"

Lucius nodded.

> **"...Feels natural."**

The spar that followed showcased fluid thrusts, sweeping strikes, and defensive movements, making full use of the weapon's reach.

Watching from the sidelines, Belle quietly whispered to Taiga:

> **"...Now I know why he never seemed scared of my sword."**

Taiga nodded.

> **"...He's fought long weapons for years."**
