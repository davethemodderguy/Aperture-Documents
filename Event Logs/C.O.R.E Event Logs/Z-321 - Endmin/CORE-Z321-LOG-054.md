# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-054**

### **UNAUTHORIZED OBSERVATION – PERSONAL WEAPON MODIFICATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Engineering Observation

---

### **SUMMARY**

During an otherwise quiet afternoon in Room N-07, Z-321 (Endmin) was observed performing extensive modifications to her personal sniper rifle.

The observation was entirely accidental.

Z-885 (Taiga) and Z-886 (Mart), who had been quietly reading together nearby, chose not to interrupt after realizing Endmin was deeply focused on her work.

---

## **Event Log**

The room was unusually peaceful.

Taiga was lounging on the floor with a book while Mart read nearby.

Endmin entered carrying a collection of rifle components.

Among the parts were:

- A replacement handguard significantly longer than the original.
    
- Several mounting brackets.
    
- Fasteners.
    
- A concealed mechanical linkage assembly.
    
- A large handguard bearing **Neko's engraved signature** near its rear.
    

Endmin carefully placed her sniper rifle on the workbench.

Without hesitation, she began disassembly.

First came the optic.

Then the weapon-mounted flashlight.

The bayonet was detached and set aside.

Finally, the original handguard was removed.

Rather than placing it into storage, Endmin picked up a wooden picture frame.

She carefully mounted the old handguard inside it.

After hanging the framed component neatly on the wall, she quietly smiled.

> "...You've earned retirement."

---

## **Observation**

As Endmin lifted the replacement handguard, Taiga leaned forward.

Her eyes widened.

She whispered just loudly enough for Mart to hear.

> **Taiga:** "…Is that a shotgun bolt?"

Mart blinked.

> **Mart:** "What?"

> "...How do you know?"

Taiga kept watching Endmin work.

Without taking her eyes off the rifle, she answered calmly.

> **Taiga:** "Our revolvers combine into shotguns."

> "Of course I know what a shotgun looks like."

Mart slowly nodded.

> "...Fair point."

---

## **Installation**

Endmin aligned the replacement assembly with remarkable precision.

The oversized handguard locked directly onto the barrel assembly before she began connecting an internal mechanical linkage.

Interestingly, the rifle's original fire selector no longer appeared to control firing modes.

Instead, it had been mechanically repurposed to actuate whatever mechanism was hidden inside the new handguard.

The work was completed without consulting notes or blueprints.

Once satisfied, Endmin reinstalled:

- Her 24× optic.
    
- Her weapon-mounted flashlight.
    

She looked at the detached bayonet for a moment.

Instead of remounting it beneath the barrel, she clipped it onto her belt.

It would now serve as a dedicated combat knife.

---

## **Discovery**

Only after finishing did Endmin notice she was no longer alone.

She turned.

Taiga and Mart were staring at the rifle in complete silence.

Endmin sighed.

Visibly annoyed—but not angry.

> **Endmin:**
> 
> "Ugh..."
> 
> "Guys, you know I hate surprises."

Taiga immediately stood up.

Eyes sparkling.

> **Taiga:**
> 
> "Nice upgrades!"

> "Can you show us?"

Endmin looked back at the rifle.

She gave the action a final check.

Then shook her head.

> **Endmin:**
> 
> "Maybe tomorrow."

> "Still have a few things to check out."

Taiga nodded, slightly disappointed but understanding.

> "...Okay."

---

## **Final Observation**

As Endmin left Room N-07, Taiga happened to notice something beneath the edge of her jacket.

Hidden almost completely from view...

...was a compact shotgun shell bandolier.

The loops were loaded.

Taiga looked at Mart.

Mart looked back.

Neither said a word.

Both silently reached the same conclusion.

Whatever Endmin had just installed...

...the new handguard was almost certainly **more than just a handguard**.

---

## **Engineering Note**

No testing was conducted during this event.

At Endmin's request, the modified rifle will not be demonstrated until post-installation inspections and safety checks have been completed.

Engineering Division has acknowledged the request.

---

### **Closing Observation**

After Endmin had left, Taiga walked over to the framed handguard hanging neatly on the wall.

She tilted her head.

> **Taiga:** "She framed it."

Mart smiled faintly.

> **Mart:** "...She gets attached to her equipment."

Taiga looked back toward the doorway where Endmin had disappeared.

Then at the concealed shotgun shell bandolier she had briefly spotted.

She grinned.

> "...Tomorrow's going to be interesting."
