# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-068**

### **MEDICAL ADDENDUM – OCULAR MORPHOLOGY**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Medical / Anatomical Record

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following Endmin's complete recovery from Ether Corruption, Medical Division has updated the official anatomical description of her eyes.

Despite her restored eyesight, the physical appearance of her irises remains permanently altered.

Medical staff unanimously describe them as among the most visually striking anomalous ocular structures documented at the Blacksite.

---

## **Official Description**

**Endmin's eyes are characterized by:**

> **A hollow, dark navy-blue iris outlined by a narrow ring of luminous gold. The golden ring rotates almost imperceptibly, completing a slow continuous movement approximately every second.**

Personnel have repeatedly compared the appearance to observing a celestial phenomenon.

One researcher summarized it as:

> **"It's like looking into a black hole."**

The comparison has since become the standard informal description within Medical Division.

---

## **Visual Characteristics**

Under normal lighting, Endmin's eyes exhibit:

- A deep navy-blue iris with an apparently hollow center.
    
- A thin golden ring tracing the outer edge of the iris.
    
- Continuous, slow rotational movement of the ring.
    
- A faint natural glow visible in dim environments.
    
- No observable discomfort or impairment to Endmin herself.
    

The rotational motion appears perfectly smooth and never accelerates or stops.

No biological explanation has yet been identified.

---

## **Medical Findings**

Comprehensive examinations concluded that the appearance is **permanent**.

The ocular structure remains stable.

No evidence suggests ongoing Ether Corruption or progressive degeneration.

Medical Division therefore classifies the condition as a permanent anatomical adaptation resulting from Endmin's previous exposure and subsequent recovery.

Functionally, her vision is normal.

In complete darkness, she also retains her Ether perception, allowing her to sense her surroundings even in the absence of visible light.

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, Endmin stood before the greenhouse windows, looking out at the winter sky.

The stars reflected softly in her eyes.

For a brief moment, the tiny rotating golden rings seemed almost like distant galaxies orbiting within the deep navy of her irises.

Taiga quietly walked up beside her.

She looked at the reflection.

Then smiled.

> **"...They really do look like little black holes."**

Endmin chuckled.

Without taking her eyes off the stars, she replied,

> **"...I think they're prettier than the old ones."**
