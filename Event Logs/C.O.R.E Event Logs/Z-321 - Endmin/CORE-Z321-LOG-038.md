# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-038**

### **PARTIAL ETHER CORRUPTION PURGE TRIAL**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – MEDICAL / ETHER RESEARCH

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following months of collaborative research between Aperture Industries and the Banlands, Medical Division successfully conducted the first controlled **Ether Corruption Purge Trial** on Z-321 ("Endmin").

The objective was **not** to cure Ether Corruption outright.

The objective was to determine whether a controlled amount of stabilized Ether Corruption could be safely removed without destabilizing Endmin's body.

The procedure exceeded all expectations.

Approximately **35%** of the Ether Corruption within Endmin's body was successfully purged.

---

## **THE PROCEDURE**

The treatment combined:

- Aperture Ether manipulation technology.
    
- Stabilized Ether research.
    
- Continuous life-support monitoring.
    
- Medical supervision.
    
- Lucy's direct oversight.
    

Throughout the procedure, Ether concentrations were reduced gradually rather than extracted all at once.

Researchers halted the process once the predetermined safety threshold had been reached.

---

## **RESULTS**

### Ether Corruption

**Approximately 35% successfully removed.**

No signs of systemic instability were observed.

Remaining Ether Corruption continues to be actively monitored.

---

### Physiological Improvements

Medical examinations recorded measurable improvements.

Observed changes include:

- Increased overall stamina.
    
- Reduced biological stress.
    
- Lower baseline Ether radiation.
    
- Improved sleep quality.
    
- Reduced sensory discomfort.
    
- Greater resistance to fatigue.
    

---

### Ocular Recovery

Perhaps the most significant finding involved Endmin's eyes.

Although not fully restored, Medical Division documented substantial healing.

Changes included:

- Reduced tissue damage.
    
- Improved structural integrity.
    
- Increased tolerance to ambient light.
    
- Greater visual clarity through her Ether-based perception.
    

Her eyes remain dependent on the protective face covering.

Complete restoration has **not** yet been achieved.

---

## **POST-PROCEDURE OBSERVATION**

Endmin slowly opened her eyes.

Medical personnel waited quietly.

She blinked several times.

Then looked around the room.

Her breathing became uneven.

> "...Everything..."

She smiled.

> "...Feels clearer."

---

## **LUCY**

Lucy had remained silent throughout the entire procedure.

Only after Director Lucious confirmed the operation had concluded successfully did she move.

She walked directly toward him.

Without saying a word...

She embraced him tightly.

For several seconds, the room was completely silent.

Then Lucy quietly spoke through tears.

> **"This is a place of miracles."**

She struggled to continue.

> **"For years..."**

> **"...I believed there was nothing anyone could do."**

She looked toward Endmin, who was smiling while carefully looking around the room.

Lucy covered her face.

Years of guilt, fear, and helplessness finally gave way to relief.

She cried openly.

Not from sorrow.

But from hope.

---

Research would continue.
