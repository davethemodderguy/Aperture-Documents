# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-047**

### **PERSONAL PROFILE UPDATE – COLD WEATHER ATTIRE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential Observation

---

## **SUMMARY**

With winter temperatures around APRT-70 "Heaven's Blacksite" regularly reaching **-25°C**, Medical Division conducted a routine review of resident cold-weather clothing.

The review highlighted an unusual but effective wardrobe preference belonging to Z-321 (Endmin).

---

## **Typical Attire**

Endmin's everyday clothing generally consists of:

- Round glasses or her Banlands mask (personal preference).
    
- Short white hair, neatly clipped.
    
- Comfortable jacket or hoodie.
    
- **Very short shorts.**
    
- **High socks** extending above the knee.
    
- Custom Mobility Boots.
    

She very rarely wears skirts, stating they interfere with both skating and carrying equipment.

---

## **Reason for High Socks**

When questioned about her preference, Endmin gave a straightforward explanation.

> "Skating makes my legs cold."

Medical staff initially assumed this referred to indoor skating.

Endmin clarified.

> "...Outside."

At the time, the outdoor temperature measured **-25°C**.

The room collectively stared at her.

---

## **Medical Evaluation**

Despite frequently skating outdoors during winter, Endmin has not experienced any cold-related injuries.

Several factors contribute to this:

- High-quality insulated socks.
    
- Thermally lined shorts.
    
- Heated interior lining within her Mobility Boots.
    
- Continuous physical movement while skating.
    

The boot heating system was later confirmed to be another small addition from Neko.

---

## **Engineering Notes**

The Mobility Boots include:

- Adjustable internal heating.
    
- Moisture control.
    
- Cold-resistant wheel bearings.
    
- Ice-resistant blade assemblies.
    

Neko explained the reasoning simply.

> "If she's going to skate in Iceland..."

> "...I'd rather she kept all her toes."

---

> "...Usually."

Lucy's expression suggested that answer was only partially reassuring.

---

### Director Lucious

Issued a reminder to all residents:

> "Just because Endmin can handle the cold doesn't mean everyone else should race her outside."

This reminder was directed primarily at Taiga.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Endmin continues to use her Mobility Boots throughout the Blacksite.

During winter, she consistently wears insulated high socks to remain comfortable while skating.

Medical Division reports no concerns regarding her clothing choices or cold tolerance.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Fresh snow covered the Blacksite courtyard.

Taiga and Mart bundled themselves in heavy winter clothing before stepping outside.

A moment later, Endmin rolled past them on her skates.

She wore:

- A warm jacket.
    
- High socks.
    
- Short shorts.
    
- Her Mobility Boots.
    

Nothing else unusual.

She waved.

> "Morning."

Then activated her booster and disappeared across the snow-covered path as if it were perfectly ordinary.

Taiga watched her vanish into the distance.

> "...I still don't know how she does that."

Mart smiled.

> "...Neither does winter."
