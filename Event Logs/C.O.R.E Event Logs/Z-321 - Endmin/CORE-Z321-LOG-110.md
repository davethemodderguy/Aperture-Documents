# **BANLANDS ADMINISTRATION**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-110**

### **PERSONAL HISTORY – LUCIUS MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Historical / Missing Persons

---

## **SUMMARY**

During an evening in Room N-07, the Friendly Roommates asked Endmin about the framed bracelet resting on a shelf beside her bed.

After a long silence, Endmin quietly explained that it had belonged to her older brother.

For many present, it was the first time she had spoken about him in detail.

---

## **Lucius Merryweather**

**Status:** Missing

Lucius Merryweather is the firstborn child of Lucy and Josh Merryweather, and the older brother of Lindervene "Endmin" Merryweather.

He is believed to have inherited his father Josh's **Wolf Thiren** traits.

Despite the differences in their Thiren types, the siblings were known to be exceptionally close.

---

## **A Protective Brother**

According to Endmin, Lucius was fiercely protective of his younger sister.

Whenever Endmin was bullied because of the Ether Corruption affecting her eyes and the mask she wore, Lucius would step between her and the bullies without hesitation.

Endmin recalled with a small smile,

> **"...He always told me to stand behind him."**

> **"...He'd handle the rest."**

---

## **His Personality**

Endmin described Lucius as:

- Brave.
    
- Dependable.
    
- Patient.
    
- Protective.
    
- Someone who rarely hesitated to help others.
    

She also mentioned that he got along especially well with Taiga whenever they met.

> **"...He treated everyone kindly."**

> **"...Especially kids."**

---

## **The Bracelets**

When they were younger, Lucius and Endmin wore matching bracelets.

They promised never to lose them.

To Endmin, they symbolized that no matter where they were, they would always be brother and sister.

Endmin still keeps her bracelet.

---

## **The Day He Disappeared**

The room became silent as Endmin described the final day she saw her brother.

The two had been playing hide-and-seek.

It was an ordinary afternoon.

Endmin laughed as she counted with her eyes covered.

When she finished counting...

She began looking for him.

Instead...

She remembered seeing someone taking Lucius away in the distance.

The last clear image burned into her memory was his bracelet.

After that...

He was gone.

Despite years of searching, no confirmed trace of Lucius has ever been found.

His disappearance remains one of the Banlands' unresolved missing person cases.

---

## **Room N-07**

No one spoke for several moments after Endmin finished.

Taiga quietly walked over and sat beside her.

Belle gently held Endmin's hand.

Niko closed the book he had been reading.

Star lowered her wings.

Nightshade remained quietly beside Star.

Neko and Miss Neko looked toward the framed bracelet resting on the shelf.

Finally, Taiga spoke softly.

> **"...We'll keep hoping."

Endmin nodded.

Her eyes never left the bracelet.

> **"...I know."

Lucius Merryweather remains officially listed as **Missing**.

The matching bracelet preserved by Endmin is retained as a personal keepsake and serves as one of the last tangible reminders of her brother before his disappearance.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late that night, after everyone else had gone to sleep, Endmin stood quietly beside the small shelf in Room N-07.

She picked up her bracelet.

The worn metal reflected the room's dim light.

For a long moment, she simply held it.

Then, almost in a whisper, she smiled sadly.

> **"...I'm still it, you know."

She looked toward the window.

> **"...I've just been looking for you a lot longer than we planned."**

The bracelet was carefully placed back on the shelf before the room fell silent once more.
