# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-141**

### **MINOR INCIDENT REPORT – FLASHBANG TEST IN ROOM N-07**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

A minor incident occurred in Room N-07 after Neko attempted to better understand the internal mechanism of a standard flashbang.

The experiment ended abruptly when the device activated unexpectedly.

No personnel sustained lasting injuries.

One desk lamp, however, did not survive.

---

## **INCIDENT**

Room N-07 was occupied by:

- Endmin
    
- Belle
    
- Neko
    
- Miss Neko
    
- Lucius
    
- Taiga
    
- Mart
    
- Star
    
- Nightshade
    

Neko sat at the workbench, carefully examining a **training flashbang** that had been rendered inert for study.

He quietly muttered to himself.

> **"...Interesting..."

He rotated the device.

Pressed what he believed was a locking catch.

It wasn't.

**Click.**

Neko froze.

Everyone looked toward him.

> **"...Uh..."

A very familiar metallic hiss followed.

> **"...Run?"**

Nobody had time.

---

## **FLASH**

**BANG!**

The room was instantly filled with an intense flash of light and a deafening report.

---

## **Immediate Effects**

The room erupted into confusion.

Taiga covered her eyes.

Mart stumbled into a chair.

Star instinctively teleported to the opposite side of the room.

Lucius was left blinking repeatedly.

Miss Neko calmly rubbed her eyes.

Nightshade stood silently, waiting for everyone's hearing to recover.

Neko simply sighed.

> **"...Well..."

> **"...Now I know how it works."**

---

## **Two Exceptions**

While everyone else was recovering...

Two people remained perfectly still.

Endmin.

Belle.

Endmin blinked once.

> **"...That's loud."

Belle looked around.

> **"...Is everyone okay?"

Neither displayed any signs of temporary blindness or disorientation.

---

## **Medical Observation**

Subsequent examination confirmed that both Endmin and Belle had experienced no noticeable effects from the flashbang.

The exact reasons remain under investigation.

Medical has documented only the observable result:

- No flash-induced blindness.
    
- No balance impairment.
    
- No apparent auditory disorientation beyond acknowledging the loud noise.
    

Personnel are advised that this observation applies only to standard flashbang effects and should not be interpreted as immunity to all sensory hazards.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

No lasting injuries were reported.

Room N-07 was cleaned and returned to normal operation.

Neko's request for additional flashbangs to "repeat the experiment under controlled conditions" was approved only for use in designated testing ranges.

As for Belle and Endmin...

They simply resumed what they had been doing before the flash.

Everyone else needed another few minutes.
