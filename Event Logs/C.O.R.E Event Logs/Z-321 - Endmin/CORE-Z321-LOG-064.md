# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-064**

### **BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATION – LEGAL NAME RESPONSE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Personnel / Residential

---

## **SUMMARY**

Despite expressing a strong preference for the name **Endmin**, Z-321 has demonstrated a noticeable exception.

When **her roommates** use her legal name, **Lindervene**, whether intentionally or by accident, she does not become upset.

When anyone else does it...

...the reaction is considerably different.

---

## **General Personnel Guidance**

Personnel are reminded to address Z-321 as **Endmin** in both official and informal settings.

Her legal name remains restricted to administrative documentation and identification procedures.

Outside of those situations, the preferred form of address is:

> **Endmin**

---

## **Residential Exception**

Within Room N-07, however, a unique exception exists.

The Friendly Roommates have earned enough of Endmin's trust that the occasional accidental use of her legal name no longer bothers her.

She usually responds with little more than an embarrassed sigh.

---

## **Observed Incident**

One evening, everyone was gathered in Room N-07.

Taiga was enthusiastically talking about the next skating race.

Without thinking, she called across the room.

> **Taiga:**
> 
> "Lindervene, can you—"

The room became quiet.

Taiga immediately covered her mouth.

> "...Oops."

Endmin looked up from cleaning her rifle.

Everyone waited.

Instead of glaring...

She simply sighed.

Her ears gave a small flick.

> **"...You know it's Endmin."**

Taiga rubbed the back of her head.

> "Sorry..."

Endmin smiled faintly.

> "...It's okay."

> "...Just don't make it a habit."

---

## **Comparison**

Later that same week, a newly assigned researcher accidentally addressed her the same way.

> **Researcher:**
> 
> "...Lindervene?"

Endmin slowly turned toward him.

Her expression remained polite.

Professional.

But noticeably colder.

> **"...Please call me Endmin."**

The researcher apologized immediately.

No further issue occurred.

---

## **Behavioral Assessment**

Behavioral Sciences believes the difference stems from trust rather than the name itself.

To Endmin, her roommates are family.

A mistake made by family is simply a mistake.

The same familiarity does not exist with most other personnel.
