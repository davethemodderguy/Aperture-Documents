# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-036**

### **ICE CREAM, LOLLIPOPS & AN UNEXPECTED HABIT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Recreational Observation

---

## **SUMMARY**

As part of continued social integration, Z-321 (Endmin) was introduced to several common snacks available within the Blacksite cafeteria.

Among them:

- Ice cream.
    
- Lollipops.
    

The results were... memorable.

---

## **Ice Cream**

Frost happily handed Endmin a vanilla ice cream cone.

> **Frost:** "You haven't tried one before?"

Endmin shook her head.

> "No."

She carefully took a tiny bite.

Silence.

Then her eyes widened beneath her mask.

> "...Cold."

Another bite.

> "...Really cold."

Another.

> "...Really good."

Within minutes, she had finished the entire cone.

Frost smiled proudly.

> "I knew you'd like it."

---

## **The Lollipop**

Later that afternoon, Taiga offered Endmin a mint-flavored lollipop.

Endmin looked at it curiously.

> "...Candy on a stick?"

She unwrapped it and placed it into her mouth.

A few moments passed.

She tilted her head.

> "...My mouth feels cold."

Neko laughed.

> "That's mint."

---

## **An Unexpected Discovery**

Several days later, personnel began noticing something unusual.

Endmin almost always had a mint lollipop in her mouth.

During rifle maintenance.

While reading.

During engineering lessons.

Even while gliding.

Director Lucious eventually asked.

> **Director Lucious:** "Endmin..."

> "...Do you just really like mint?"

She nodded.

Then explained.

> "...Partly."

Everyone waited.

> "...It helps."

> "...Bad smells."

The room went quiet.

Endmin continued.

> "...My senses are stronger than most people's."

> "...Some chemicals..."

> "...Some smoke..."

> "...Some cleaning supplies..."

> "...They're overwhelming."

She gently tapped the lollipop stick.

> "...Mint covers them."

Medical later confirmed that Endmin's Ether-based perception likely made her unusually sensitive to airborne particles and odors.

The constant mint aroma helped reduce sensory discomfort.

---

## **Room N-07's Response**

The next morning, a large glass jar appeared on the common table.

Filled entirely with mint lollipops.

Attached was a small handwritten note.

> **Emergency Endmin Supply**

Nobody admitted to placing it there.

Everyone knew exactly who had contributed.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Endmin now routinely keeps a **mint lollipop** with her.

Reasons include:

- Reducing unpleasant odors.
    
- Helping with sensory overload.
    
- Enjoying the taste.
    

Medical has confirmed the habit is harmless.

As a precaution, the Blacksite cafeteria now keeps a permanent stock of mint lollipops for Z-321.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, Endmin sat on the greenhouse roof beside Taiga and Mart.

The three quietly watched the stars.

A cool breeze drifted by.

Taiga looked over.

> "...You have another one already?"

Endmin nodded, the white stick peeking out from beneath her mask.

> "...Mint."

Mart chuckled.

> "...Of course."

None of them spoke again.

The silence, broken only by the occasional crunch of mint candy, was comfortable enough.
