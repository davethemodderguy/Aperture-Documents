# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-033**

### **PERIMETER SECURITY OBSERVATION — UNAUTHORIZED AERIAL SURVEILLANCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – SECURITY OPERATIONS

---

## **SUMMARY**

Hostile organizations periodically deploy small unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) near the Blacksite in attempts to collect intelligence on personnel, infrastructure, and anomalous assets.

Although the Blacksite maintains a layered air-defense network, Z-321 ("Endmin") is frequently the first individual to detect and neutralize these surveillance drones.

This has become so consistent that Perimeter Security jokingly refers to her as the site's "early warning system."

---

## **DETECTION**

Unlike conventional observers, Endmin's Ether-based perception allows her to sense subtle disturbances in the surrounding environment.

Combined with her exceptional hearing and spatial awareness, even small reconnaissance drones are difficult to hide from her.

Security logs consistently show Endmin identifying aerial contacts before automated systems complete target classification.

One operator summarized it simply:

> "If Endmin looks up..."

> "...There's probably a drone."

---

## **STANDARD RESPONSE**

The sequence has become remarkably predictable.

Endmin pauses.

Tilts her head slightly.

Looks toward an apparently empty section of sky.

Unslings her rifle.

Works the bolt.

One shot.

A faint metallic _clang_ echoes somewhere in the distance.

Seconds later, Perimeter Security receives a radar update.

> **Target Lost.**

---

## **RECORDED INCIDENT**

During a routine afternoon patrol, Endmin suddenly stopped walking.

Taiga noticed.

> **Taiga:** "...What's wrong?"

Endmin remained silent.

She slowly looked toward the northern ridgeline.

Without another word, she shouldered her rifle.

Mart instinctively stepped aside.

A single shot rang out.

Several seconds passed.

Then, far beyond the Blacksite perimeter, a small drone spiraled from the sky before disappearing behind the trees.

Perimeter Security radioed moments later.

> **Perimeter Security:** "Unknown surveillance drone neutralized."

A brief pause.

> "...Endmin, was that you again?"

Endmin smiled beneath her mask.

> "...Maybe."

---

## **RECOVERY OPERATIONS**

Whenever safe to do so, Security teams recover the wreckage.

Recovered drones have originated from multiple unidentified hostile organizations.

Many carried:

- Long-range optical cameras.
    
- Thermal imaging systems.
    
- Signal interception equipment.
    
- Mapping hardware.
    

No recovered drone has successfully collected significant intelligence after entering Endmin's effective engagement zone.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Perimeter Security continues to maintain full aerial surveillance.

However, personnel are advised that if Endmin unexpectedly stops, looks into the sky, and reaches for her rifle...

There is likely already an unauthorized aircraft overhead.

---

### **Closing Observation**

One quiet evening, a new security recruit heard a distant rifle shot.

He looked up in confusion.

> "...Did anyone see what she was aiming at?"

A veteran guard didn't even glance away from his coffee.

> "...Give it about five seconds."

Exactly five seconds later...

A smoking reconnaissance drone fell out of the clouds and landed harmlessly in an empty field outside the perimeter fence.

The veteran took another sip.

> "...Told you."

Far in the distance, Endmin calmly worked the bolt of her rifle before continuing her walk as though nothing unusual had happened.
