# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-151**

### **BLACKSITE SEASONAL LOG – ICELANDIC SUMMER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following months of snow, freezing winds, and sub-zero temperatures, summer arrived in Iceland.

To the surprise of several Blacksite residents, the temperature rose dramatically compared to previous weeks.

While still mild by many international standards, it was hot enough for the Blacksite's climate control systems to remain active throughout the day.

The snow surrounding the facility disappeared, revealing green fields and rocky volcanic terrain.

---

## **GENERAL OBSERVATIONS**

The sudden change in weather produced mixed reactions.

---

### **Belle**

Standing outside for approximately thirty seconds.

> **"...It's warm."

Five minutes later...

She walked back inside.

> **"...Air conditioning."

Belle spent most afternoons lounging in the common room with a cold drink and, naturally, a fresh carrot.

---

### **Endmin**

After years of operating in the Banlands, she tolerated the warmth reasonably well.

She still chose to remain indoors whenever possible.

Her Ether-dampening glasses occasionally fogged when moving between the cool interior and the warmer outdoors.

---

### **Lucius**

Lucius welcomed the weather.

He carried his sketchbook outside almost every afternoon, painting the green Icelandic landscape instead of snowy mountains.

Belle occasionally wandered over to quietly watch him paint.

---

### **Keith**

Keith's response to the weather consisted of removing his jacket.

Nothing else changed.

He continued escort duties exactly as before.

Cinder later commented:

> **"...I don't think he's capable of looking uncomfortable."

---

### **Cinder**

Cinder was the least enthusiastic about the warmer temperatures.

As someone capable of firebending, she joked:

> **"...Nature already has enough fire today."

She spent most of her free time beside the air conditioning vents while organizing KCA paperwork.

---

### **Star**

Star immediately declared the weather perfect.

She organized outdoor games almost every afternoon.

---

### **Nightshade**

She preferred evenings, when temperatures dropped and the air became cooler.

---

### **Taiga**

> **"...No snow means I can actually run without slipping."

---

### **Mart**

Spent considerably more time flying outdoors, taking advantage of the calmer summer weather.

---

### **Frost**

She jokingly became the most popular resident in the Blacksite.

Any drink she touched became perfectly chilled.

By lunchtime, a queue had formed outside the kitchen.

---

### **Bao**

Frequently brought cool breezes into outdoor seating areas using gentle air and water currents.

---

### **Alice**

Quietly reminded everyone to stay hydrated.

Medical reports showed a noticeable increase in residents forgetting to drink enough water while enjoying the unusually warm weather.

---

### **Miss Neko**

Took full advantage of the sunshine to tend to outdoor plants around the Blacksite.

---

### **Neko**

Adjusted several laboratory cooling systems to compensate for the warmer ambient temperatures.

He also discovered that Belle had unofficially claimed the seat directly beneath the largest air-conditioning vent.

She refused to move.

---

## **ROOM N-07**

By mid-afternoon, nearly everyone had gathered in the common room.

The air conditioner hummed quietly overhead.

Belle lay stretched across the couch.

Endmin occupied the opposite end.

Lucius sketched near the window.

Star had somehow convinced Taiga into another board game.

Keith stood by the doorway as usual.

Cinder was sitting directly beneath the nearest AC vent with her briefcase beside her.

She sighed happily.

> **"...This is nice."

Belle looked over.

> **"...You're stealing all the cold air."

Cinder smiled.

> **"...First come, first served."

---

## **DIRECTOR LUCIOUS' NOTE**

A seasonal memo was later circulated throughout the facility.

> **"Despite the warmer weather, personnel are reminded to remain hydrated, use appropriate sun protection during extended outdoor activity, and avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight where practical."**

A handwritten note appeared underneath sometime later.

The handwriting was identified as Belle's.

> **"...Also, share the air conditioner."**

No one admitted to ignoring that last instruction.

---

## **STATUS**

The Icelandic summer brought a welcome change of scenery to Heaven's Blacksite.

For a few months, the endless snow gave way to green hills, longer days, and open skies.

The only thing everyone unanimously agreed upon was that, regardless of the season...

The air conditioning in Room N-07 had become the most valuable piece of equipment in the building.
