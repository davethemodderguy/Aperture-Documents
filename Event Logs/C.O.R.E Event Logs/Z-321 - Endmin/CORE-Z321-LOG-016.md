# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-016**

### **OBSERVATION LOG — GREENHOUSE STARGAZING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – RESIDENT INTERACTION

---

**Location:** Greenhouse Rooftop Observation Platform  
**Time:** 22:41

Personnel reported Z-321 ("Endmin"), Taiga Sunbringer, and Mart Sunbringer climbing onto the roof of the greenhouse after evening hours. The three remained there for approximately one hour observing the night sky.

No anomalous activity requiring intervention was recorded.

The following conversation was reconstructed from nearby security microphones.

---

The three sat quietly beneath the stars.

For several minutes, nobody spoke.

The greenhouse glass reflected the constellations above while a cool breeze drifted across the rooftop.

Endmin looked upward.

The faint yellow edges of her mask glowed softly.

After a long silence, she smiled.

> **Endmin:** "...It's been so long since I saw colors again."

Her voice trembled slightly.

She wiped at her face beneath the mask.

> **Taiga:** "This is a place of miracles."

Endmin gave a small nod.

Without saying another word...

She slowly reached behind her head.

The clasps of her mask clicked open.

Mart looked over.

Taiga followed.

Endmin carefully lifted the mask away.

Neither sibling spoke.

They simply stared.

Her eyes were unlike anything they had ever seen.

Though the centers of her irises appeared hollow, they were breathtakingly beautiful.

Soft golden rings resonated within them, glowing faintly beneath the starlight like distant galaxies.

Tiny ripples of Ether shimmered across their surface.

The stars themselves seemed reflected within them.

For a brief moment...

The rooftop was completely silent.

Endmin quietly placed the mask back over her face and secured it once more.

Taiga was the first to recover.

> **Taiga:** "...Why did you show us your eyes?"

Endmin looked back toward the stars.

> **Endmin:** "Back home..."

A small pause.

> "...Whoever sees my eyes..."

> "...Will die."

Both Mart and Taiga froze.

Taiga's ears flattened.

Mart immediately sat upright.

> **Mart:** "...W-What?"

Endmin quickly waved both hands.

> "...Not like that."

She chuckled softly.

> "...It's an old Banlands custom."

Another gentle smile appeared.

> "...Showing your eyes means you've placed your life in someone's hands."

She turned toward the siblings.

> "...It's one of the greatest forms of trust we have."

Both Mart and Taiga visibly relaxed.

Mart let out the breath he had been holding.

Taiga lightly hit his shoulder.

> **Taiga:** "...You panicked too."

Mart quietly nodded.

> "...A little."

Endmin laughed.

The sound was carried away by the night breeze.

After another period of silence, she spoke again.

> **Endmin:** "Back home..."

> "...All I did was kill."

She rested a hand on the rifle slung across her back.

> "...The people I fought were the ones hurting ours."

She looked toward the stars again.

> "...That was my job."

A pause.

> "...My mom still blames herself."

Her voice remained calm.

There was no anger.

Only quiet understanding.

> "...What happened to my eyes..."

> "...Was her mistake."

She slowly shook her head.

> "...But she can't forgive herself."

The wind gently lifted strands of her white hair.

> "...I can still see."

She smiled faintly.

> "...Just not the way I was supposed to."

Another silence settled over the rooftop.

Finally, she whispered,

> "...I just wish she'd forgive herself."

Neither Mart nor Taiga interrupted.

They simply sat beside her.

Sharing the quiet.

Sharing the stars.

Eventually, Endmin stood.

She looked upward one last time.

> **Endmin:** "...Thanks for the stargazing."

A warm smile crossed her face.

> "...It's been a long time since I last saw this."

Mart stood beside her.

Taiga stretched before hopping to her feet.

Without another word, the three climbed down from the greenhouse roof together and walked back toward Room N-07.

---

### **Psychological Services Note**

This event is considered a significant milestone in Endmin's emotional recovery.

According to Banlands cultural information provided by Lucy, voluntarily revealing her eyes to another person is an act reserved only for those she trusts without reservation.

Psychological Services concludes that Endmin now regards Taiga and Mart not merely as roommates, but as family.
