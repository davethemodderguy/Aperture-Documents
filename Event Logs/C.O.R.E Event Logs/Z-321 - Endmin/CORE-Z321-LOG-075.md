# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-075**

### **RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION – LET HER REST**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Wellness

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following an unusually demanding operation involving multiple hostile Ether entities, the residents of Room N-07 returned to the Blacksite physically and mentally exhausted.

Z-321 (Endmin) was observed to be the most fatigued.

Shortly after returning to her room, she involuntarily transformed into her feline form and fell asleep almost immediately.

Behavioral Sciences recommended that she not be disturbed.

---

## **Observation**

Room N-07 was unusually quiet.

The lights had been dimmed.

The usual chatter between roommates had been replaced by whispered conversations.

Curled into a tight ball atop her bed was a small grey tabby cat, over half of her coat covered in soft white fur.

Her breathing was slow and steady.

Her tail occasionally twitched in her sleep.

Beside the bed, resting on the desk, was a handwritten note.

---

### **Handwritten Note**

> **She had to fight her heart out today.**
> 
> **Just... please don't wake her up, okay?**
> 
> — Lucy

---

Nobody questioned it.

---

## **Roommates**

### Taiga

Taiga quietly entered the room carrying a blanket she had planned to give Endmin.

Seeing the note, she stopped.

She looked at the sleeping cat.

Then silently unfolded the blanket and tucked it around the edge of the bed without touching Endmin herself.

> "...Sleep well."

---

### Mart

Mart noticed one of Endmin's engineering notebooks lying open.

He quietly closed it so the pages wouldn't wrinkle from the draft coming through the window.

Not a single sound.

---

### Neko

Neko had originally intended to ask Endmin about a prototype component.

After reading the note, he simply wrote the question on a sticky note instead.

He placed it neatly beside her toolbox.

> "Tomorrow."

---

### Miss Neko

Miss Neko quietly adjusted the room's thermostat by a single degree.

> "...She always sleeps better when it's a little warmer."

---

### Star & Nightshade

The Angelic Twins normally enjoyed lively conversations in the evenings.

Tonight, they spoke only in whispers.

Even Nightshade made a conscious effort not to slam the door.

---

### Bao

Bao quietly watered the plants near the window.

The gentle sound of running water was the loudest noise in the room.

---

### Frost

Frost carefully lowered the temperature near the window just enough to keep fresh winter air flowing—without letting the room become cold.

---

### Niko

Niko closed the curtains halfway.

Moonlight still reached Endmin's bed.

Just enough to make the room feel peaceful.

---

### Aster

Without making a sound, Aster leapt onto the bed.

She circled twice.

Then curled into a small ball beside the sleeping cat.

Within moments, both were asleep.

---

## **Lucy's Observation**

Lucy quietly opened the bedroom door later that evening.

She looked around.

Everyone had instinctively become quieter.

Nobody had disturbed Endmin.

Lucy smiled.

She whispered,

> "...Thank you."

No one answered.

They simply nodded.

---

## **Director Lucious**

When informed that Engineering wanted Endmin for a routine consultation, Director Lucious looked at the report.

He read Lucy's handwritten note.

Then simply replied,

> **"It can wait until she wakes up."**

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Endmin slept uninterrupted for nearly twelve hours.

Medical Division reported a full recovery from operational fatigue upon waking.

Behavioral Sciences noted that the willingness of every roommate to quietly protect her rest demonstrated the level of trust and care shared within Room N-07.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Just before midnight, Taiga quietly checked on Endmin one last time.

The little grey-and-white tabby was still sound asleep.

One paw rested over her nose.

Her ears gave a tiny sleepy twitch.

Aster remained curled beside her like a fluffy guardian.

Taiga smiled to herself.

She gently switched off the bedside lamp.

As she closed the door, she whispered just loudly enough for no one but the sleeping cat to hear,

> **"...Goodnight, Endmin."**

The only reply was a soft, contented purr that continued uninterrupted as the room settled into silence.
