# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-017**

### **COMBAT TRAINING OBSERVATION — WEAPON FAMILIARIZATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – TRAINING / ARMORY

---

**Location:** Blacksite Combat Training Grounds

Participants:

- Taiga Sunbringer
    
- Mart Sunbringer
    
- Endmin (Z-321)
    
- Neko
    
- Miss Neko
    
- Lucy, Warden of the Banlands
    
- Director Lucious
    
- Perimeter Security Personnel
    

---

## **SUMMARY**

Routine combat qualification evolved into an informal weapon familiarization session after Endmin expressed interest in the increasingly unusual arsenal developed within the Blacksite.

No injuries were reported.

One surveillance drone was unintentionally destroyed.

---

## **THE STAR AND THE MOON**

As the three occupants of Room N-07 prepared their lanes, Endmin noticed the two large revolvers holstered on Taiga and Mart.

She smiled.

> **Endmin:** "Nice guns."

Taiga grinned and pointed toward Neko, who was practicing nearby with his transforming plasma scythe.

> **Taiga:** "Ask him. Maybe he'll make you something."

Mart nodded.

> **Mart:** "He's done stranger things."

Endmin rested a hand on the rifle slung across her back.

She cycled the bolt with practiced ease.

The movement revealed dozens of carefully engraved tally marks carved into the left side of the stock.

None appeared decorative.

She looked at the rifle fondly.

> **Endmin:** "I'll be fine with this beauty."

A small smile.

> "...Thank you for the offer, though."

She shouldered the rifle.

Three kilometers beyond the designated range perimeter, an autonomous observation drone happened to pass overhead.

One shot echoed across the valley.

Several seconds later...

The drone quietly disappeared from radar.

---

## **RADIO TRAFFIC**

Perimeter Security immediately contacted Command.

> **Perimeter Security:** "Control, who just took that shot?"

Director Lucious looked up from his clipboard.

He already knew.

He sighed with amusement.

> **Director Lucious:** "Don't have to worry about that."

A pause.

> "...That one was intentional."

---

## **NEKO'S INSPECTION**

Curiosity inevitably brought Neko over.

He politely asked to examine Endmin's rifle.

She handed it over without hesitation.

After several minutes of inspection, Neko looked... confused.

The rifle was...

Ordinary.

Mechanically, there was almost nothing anomalous about it.

Specifications included:

- Bullpup bolt-action configuration.
    
- Chambered in 7.62 mm.
    
- Twenty-round detachable magazine.
    
- 24× telescopic optic.
    
- Folding bipod.
    
- Vertical foregrip.
    
- Fixed bayonet.
    
- Construction from an unidentified alloy visually similar to the material comprising Endmin's mask.
    

Neko slowly lowered the rifle.

> **Neko:** "...This is..."

Another pause.

> "...Just a really good rifle."

Endmin nodded proudly.

> **Endmin:** "Exactly."

Neko then pointed toward the bayonet.

> **Neko:** "...Why the bayonet?"

Endmin looked mildly offended.

> **Endmin:** "I don't have infinite ammo, you know."

Neko stared for a second.

Then nodded.

> **Neko:** "...Fair enough."

---

## **THE ECLIPSE DEMONSTRATION**

Taiga and Mart demonstrated the transformation sequence of **Star** and **Moon**, followed by their combined **Eclipse** configuration.

Endmin watched the revolvers merge into their shotgun forms before separating once again.

She blinked.

Twice.

> **Endmin:** "...Have you actually fought with these?"

Taiga casually chambered a shell into **Eclipse – Solar**.

> **Taiga:** "Many times."

Endmin simply looked at Mart.

Mart shrugged.

> **Mart:** "...She's not exaggerating."

---

## **THE SINGLE CARTRIDGE**

While the conversation continued, Mart noticed something unusual on Endmin's rifle.

Resting in a small retention slot beside the bolt was a single unfired cartridge.

Separate from the magazine.

Separate from the chamber.

He pointed toward it.

> **Mart:** "What's that for?"

Endmin's smile disappeared.

She looked at the lone cartridge for several seconds.

When she finally answered, her voice was quiet.

Almost distant.

> **Endmin:** "...One shot..."

A pause.

> "...To cut all ties with evil."

No one spoke afterwards.

Mart quietly lowered his hand.

The subject changed naturally.

---

## **LUCY'S BLADE**

Lucy had spent much of the morning observing from the sidelines.

A sheathed katana rested comfortably at her waist.

Neko eventually noticed it.

He laughed.

> **Neko:** "How many katanas are there around here?"

He began counting on his fingers.

> "Star."

> "Nightshade."

> "Bao."

> "Frost."

> "Miss Neko."

He looked back toward Lucy.

> "...Now you."

Lucy smiled.

Without ceremony, she drew the weapon.

The blade glowed a brilliant crimson.

She stepped toward a ballistic testing dummy.

One clean horizontal cut.

The dummy separated instantly.

Instrumentation produced unusual readings.

Rather than overwhelming force, sensors detected extremely high-frequency resonance propagating through the blade, greatly increasing its cutting effectiveness against compatible materials.

Testing later confirmed one notable limitation.

The blade could **not** cut through Nekonium.

Neko smirked.

> **Neko:** "Good."

> "...I'd have to redesign a lot of things otherwise."

---

## **THE SHEATH**

As Lucy returned the blade to its scabbard, Neko noticed what appeared to be a magazine protruding slightly from its side.

He raised an eyebrow.

> **Neko:** "...Is that..."

> "...A gun?"

Lucy answered by demonstration.

She thrust the katana through another dummy to pin it in place.

With her free hand, she gripped the sheath.

A single report rang out.

The projectile struck the dummy.

Instead of penetrating conventionally, the target disintegrated into countless crimson particles before collapsing.

Lucy calmly explained.

> **Lucy:** "The sheath houses a firearm."

> "Its ammunition is designed to erase corrupted Banlands souls."

> "Against conventional targets..."

She glanced at the remains of the dummy.

> "...It is equally effective."

Neko's eyes lit up.

For the next several minutes, he enthusiastically questioned Lucy about the weapon's construction.

Lucy answered every question with equal enthusiasm.

She had designed and built the weapon herself.

Endmin smiled quietly.

> **Endmin:** "I remember using it before..."

> "...Before my accident."

Lucy looked at her daughter and smiled.

> "...You always kept it cleaner than I did."

---

## **BLISS OF WHITEFALL**

As the discussion concluded, Endmin's attention shifted to the enormous rifle slung across Miss Neko's back.

She tilted her head.

> **Endmin:** "What is that?"

Miss Neko immediately brightened.

She unslung the weapon with obvious excitement.

> **Miss Neko:** "Want to see me fire it?"

> "It's my favorite way of dealing with obstructions."

Before the weapon could even be shouldered—

Director Lucious appeared beside her almost instantly.

One hand gently but firmly lowered the barrel.

> **Director Lucious:** "...Absolutely not."

Endmin looked between them, confused.

Lucious pointed toward the massive anti-material rifle.

> **Director Lucious:** "That thing..."

He paused.

> "...Disintegrates mountains."

Endmin slowly looked back at the weapon.

Then at Miss Neko.

Then back at the weapon.

After several seconds, she quietly concluded,

> "...I'm beginning to understand why your safety briefings are so long."

The entire firing line erupted into laughter.

Even Lucy smiled.

---

## **CLOSING REMARK**

The day's training highlighted the remarkable contrast between the Banlands and Aperture Industries.

Endmin's trusted companion remained a remarkably conventional precision rifle, refined through discipline and accuracy.

By comparison, Aperture's engineers had somehow created revolvers that merged into shotguns, shotguns that became submachine guns, plasma weapons capable of changing form, and an anti-material howitzer whose safe demonstration required relocating to another dimension.

Endmin later summarized the experience in her training journal with a single sentence:

> _"Back home, our weapons were built to end wars."_

> _"Here... they somehow have personalities."_
