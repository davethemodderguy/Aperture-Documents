# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-051**

### **ABILITY ADAPTATION – AERIAL HYDRODYNAMIC SKATING**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Ability Observation / Mobility Research

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following further development of her adaptive ability duplication, Z-321 (Endmin) successfully replicated a non-destructive application of **Bao's hydrokinesis**.

Rather than using the ability offensively, Endmin incorporated it into her existing skating style.

The result was unlike anything previously documented.

She no longer simply skates.

She **swims through the air**.

---

## **Background**

Z-321 possesses the anomalous ability to temporarily learn and duplicate compatible abilities through direct contact.

After spending considerable time around Z-356 (Bao), Endmin successfully reproduced a limited form of Bao's water manipulation.

Instead of creating large bodies of water, Endmin instinctively focused on mobility.

---

## **Observed Mechanism**

During skating, Endmin continuously condenses countless microscopic water droplets from moisture naturally present in the surrounding air.

These droplets form an invisible, constantly shifting pathway beneath and around her Mobility Boots.

Rather than rolling directly against the air, the boots glide across this dynamically maintained layer of water.

To outside observers, it appears as though Endmin is swimming through empty space.

High-speed cameras revealed subtle ripples and tiny sparkling droplets trailing behind her movements before quickly dispersing back into the atmosphere.

No measurable increase in ambient humidity was detected.

---

## **Movement Characteristics**

The adaptation provides several advantages.

Observed capabilities include:

- Smooth aerial skating without requiring a solid surface.
    
- Gentle changes in altitude.
    
- Tight aerial turns.
    
- Improved maneuverability while gliding.
    
- Reduced momentum loss during direction changes.
    
- Silent movement compared to booster-assisted acceleration.
    

Despite appearances, Endmin is **not** flying solely through hydrokinesis.

Her wings continue to provide lift and stability, while the microscopic water layer serves as a medium for her skating technique.

Researchers describe it as a hybrid between skating, gliding, and swimming.

---

## **Demonstration**

The first demonstration occurred in one of the Blacksite's flight hangars.

Personnel watched as Endmin pushed off the floor.

Instead of descending...

She continued forward.

Several meters above the ground.

She leaned into an invisible curve as though carving across a frozen lake.

Tiny droplets shimmered around her boots before vanishing.

She performed a complete loop around the hangar before gently returning to the floor.

Silence followed.

Then—

Taiga clapped enthusiastically.

> **Taiga:**
> 
> "YOU'RE SKATING ON AIR!"

Endmin laughed.

> "...Kind of."

---

## **Bao's Remarks**

Bao observed the demonstration personally.

She smiled warmly.

> "Most people copy power."

She watched Endmin glide gracefully overhead.

> "...You copied the idea behind it."

Bao later explained that she had never imagined using hydrokinesis in that manner herself.
