# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-112**

### **MINOR SAFETY INCIDENT – BELLE'S "RESCUE" OF LUCIUS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential / Safety Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

On Lucius Merryweather's first full day under Aperture Industries' care, a minor misunderstanding involving a gas canister resulted in Belle forcibly evacuating him from the area.

No injuries occurred.

The incident also unintentionally demonstrated Belle's extraordinary physical strength.

---

## **Incident**

The morning had been uneventful.

Lucius was accompanying Belle through one of the Blacksite's maintenance corridors while she explained the layout of the facility.

Belle's oversized sword rested across her back as usual.

As they passed Engineering, Belle suddenly stopped.

Her rabbit ears stood upright.

Several meters away sat a large compressed gas canister awaiting transport.

Belle's eyes widened.

> **"...Bomb."**

Without another thought—

She spun around.

Picked Lucius up with both hands over her head.

And sprinted.

---

## **Emergency Evacuation**

Witnesses reported seeing what appeared to be a small pink blur racing through the corridor.

Belle accelerated with astonishing speed.

Despite carrying:

- A fully grown Wolf Thiren.
    
- Her approximately 50-kilogram sword.
    

...she showed no visible signs of slowing down.

Lucius, held securely above her head, could only look down in confusion.

> **"...Belle?"

> **"...Belle?"

> **"...BELLE?!"

She didn't answer.

She simply kept running.

---

## **The Discovery**

Engineering personnel eventually caught up.

One engineer, breathing heavily, pointed back toward the hallway.

> **"...Belle..."

> **"...That wasn't a bomb."

Belle blinked.

> **"...It..."

She looked back.

> **"...Wasn't?"

The engineer shook his head.

> **"...It's a compressed gas canister waiting to be installed."**

Several seconds of silence followed.

Belle slowly lowered Lucius back onto the floor.

She folded her ears back in embarrassment.

> **"...Oops..."

Lucius brushed off his jacket.

Then smiled.

> **"...I appreciate the thought."

Belle covered her face.

> **"...I kidnapped you..."

---

## **Unexpected Observation**

While everyone was laughing about the misunderstanding, one of the Engineering staff quietly looked toward the reinforced security door Belle had pushed open during her sprint.

The door, constructed with a reinforced **Nekonium** frame, was hanging slightly off alignment.

The engineer looked at the hinges.

Then at Belle.

Then back at the door.

> **"...Wait."

> **"...She pushed that open..."

> **"...While carrying someone?"

Engineering later confirmed the reinforced door had not been damaged beyond minor hinge misalignment.

The observation, however, led to an interesting conclusion.

---

## **Physical Strength Assessment**

Although Belle is only 145 cm tall and often mistaken for physically fragile due to her plush-like appearance, personnel have repeatedly documented strength far exceeding her size.

Observed capabilities include:

- Carrying her approximately 50 kg sword for extended periods.
    
- Sprinting at high speed while carrying another person.
    
- Lifting objects several times her own body weight.
    
- Moving or forcing open extremely heavy obstacles when necessary.
    

Engineering noted that her apparent strength remains disproportionate to her size and is likely related to her Thiren physiology and extensive combat training.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

No injuries were reported.

Belle apologized to Lucius for the misunderstanding, while Lucius assured her he wasn't upset.

Engineering has since attached a large identification label to all compressed gas canisters in the area.

A handwritten note beneath the label reads:

> **"Not a bomb, Belle."**

Belle reportedly sighed dramatically upon seeing it.

Lucius laughed.

And even Belle eventually joined in.
