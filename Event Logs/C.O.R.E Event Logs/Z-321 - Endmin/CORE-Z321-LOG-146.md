## **First Impressions**

Keith gave a professional salute.

> **"Keith."

> **"Executive Protection Officer."

> **"...He really doesn't say much."

Belle giggled.

> **"...Nope."

---

Cinder, meanwhile, greeted everyone with a cheerful smile.

> **"It's wonderful to finally meet all of you."

Within minutes she was comfortably chatting with everyone.

---

## **The Briefcase**

Eventually Mart noticed the reinforced briefcase resting beside Cinder's chair.

> **"...You carry your work everywhere?"

Cinder smiled.

> **"...Not exactly."

She stood.

Picked up the briefcase.

Pressed a concealed switch.

**Click.**

Mechanical locks disengaged.

Panels unfolded.

The ordinary briefcase transformed into a compact submachine gun.

Taiga's ears perked up.

> **"...That's awesome."

Cinder pressed another control.

The weapon reconfigured into an **Explosive Smart Ordnance Launcher**.

Mart's wings twitched.

> **"...It changes again?"

> **"...Yep."

Another button.

The launcher extended into a compact rocket launcher.

Even Keith glanced over.

Belle clapped excitedly.

> **"Do the last one!"

Cinder laughed softly.

> **"...As you wish."

She folded every component back into the original briefcase.

Then...

She swung it like a sledgehammer.

**THOOM.**

The reinforced training dummy across the room tipped over onto its back.

Silence.

Then Star slowly raised one hand.

> **"...Office equipment is terrifying."

---

## **Engineering Discussion**

Neko approached almost immediately.

He carefully examined the briefcase's exterior.

> **"...Transformation tolerances are impressive."

Cinder smiled.

> **"...Would you like to inspect the mechanism?"

Neko's eyes lit up.

> **"...Very much."

The two spent nearly half an hour discussing locking systems, recoil absorption, and modular weapon architecture while everyone else watched with mild confusion.

Miss Neko quietly sipped her tea.

> **"...They've entered engineer mode."

---
