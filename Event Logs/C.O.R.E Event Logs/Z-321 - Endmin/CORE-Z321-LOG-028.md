# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-028**

### **INCIDENT REPORT — PROTECTIVE WING DEPLOYMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – SAFETY / BIOLOGICAL OBSERVATION

---

## **SUMMARY**

During a routine afternoon in the Blacksite logistics yard, an industrial gas cylinder being transported by maintenance personnel suffered a catastrophic failure and exploded.

Before nearby personnel could react, Z-321 ("Endmin") instinctively deployed her wings, shielding Mart and Taiga from the blast.

The siblings escaped without injury.

Endmin sustained moderate trauma to both wings.

---

## **INCIDENT TIMELINE**

At approximately 15:47, Taiga and Mart were in the courtyard near the logistics route.

The two were arguing—once again—over whether gliding counted as flying.

Nearby, maintenance personnel were transporting several compressed industrial gas cylinders.

One cylinder unexpectedly ruptured.

Security footage shows Endmin turning toward the sound before any visible signs of the explosion occurred.

Her Ether perception detected the danger first.

Without hesitation—

She ran.

---

## **PROTECTIVE RESPONSE**

Endmin reached the siblings just as the cylinder detonated.

Her enormous dark wings unfolded instantly.

Rather than trying to flee, she wrapped both wings completely around Mart and Taiga.

The explosion struck moments later.

Flames.

Fragments.

A powerful pressure wave.

All impacted Endmin's wings.

Inside the protective barrier, Taiga instinctively covered Mart.

Mart held onto Endmin's arm.

Neither sibling was injured.

---

## **AFTERMATH**

When the smoke cleared, Endmin slowly unfolded her wings.

Several feathers had been torn away.

Others were scorched.

The wings trembled visibly.

Endmin took a step forward.

Then winced.

Taiga immediately noticed.

> **Taiga:** "...Endmin?"

Endmin forced a small smile.

> "...I'm okay."

Lucy, who had arrived moments after the explosion, gently touched one damaged wing.

Endmin flinched.

Lucy looked toward the gathered personnel.

> **Lucy:** "They protected you."

She spoke calmly.

> "...But they're still her limbs."

The courtyard fell silent.

---

## **MEDICAL FINDINGS**

Medical Division confirmed that Endmin's wings possess greatly accelerated regenerative abilities compared to the rest of her body.

However, the examination also confirmed an important detail.

Although highly durable, the wings remain fully living tissue.

Damage to them causes pain comparable to injuries sustained to arms or legs.

The enhanced regeneration reduces recovery time.

It does **not** eliminate pain.

Within several hours, damaged feathers had already begun regrowing.

By the following morning, most superficial injuries had healed completely.

---

## **DISCUSSION**

Later that evening, Endmin sat quietly while gently preening several damaged feathers.

Mart looked at the tears in the remaining feathers.

> **Mart:** "...Did it hurt?"

Endmin paused.

Then nodded.

> "...A lot."

Mart lowered his eyes.

> "...Why didn't you move?"

Endmin looked between him and Taiga.

Her answer was immediate.

> "...Because you two couldn't."

Neither sibling knew how to respond.

Taiga simply hugged her.

Very carefully.

Avoiding the injured wings.

---

## **CAN SHE FLY?**

The incident led to another question from Research Division.

If Endmin possessed wings of that size...

Why hadn't she flown away from the explosion?

Lucy answered.

> **Lucy:** "Because she doesn't know how."

Several researchers looked surprised.

Lucy continued.

> "She can flap them."

> "She can control them."

> "She can shield with them."

She smiled toward Endmin.

> "...But I've never taught her to fly."

Endmin scratched the back of her head.

> "...I never really had a reason to learn."

Lucy laughed softly.

> "...You do now."

---

## **OBSERVATION OF WING STRUCTURE**

Further examination revealed that Endmin's wings are unexpectedly lightweight despite their enormous size.

Engineering measurements showed they place little additional strain on her body during normal movement.

Researchers believe their unusual composition allows them to generate lift efficiently once proper technique is learned.

Current conclusion:

**The limitation is not the wings.**

**It is training.**

---

## **LUCY'S DECISION**

Following the incident, Lucy made a personal announcement.

> **Lucy:** "...When I visit next."

She looked toward Endmin.

> "...We're starting flight lessons."

Endmin blinked.

> "...Really?"

Lucy nodded.

> "...You've spent long enough keeping everyone else safe."

A warm smile crossed her face.

> "...It's time you learned how to carry yourself too."

Taiga immediately cheered.

> "...YES! Flying lessons!"

Mart smiled.

> "...We'll be there to watch."

Endmin looked at her wings.

Still healing.

Still slowly replacing damaged feathers.

She smiled beneath her mask.

> "...I think..."

> "...I'd like that."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Z-321 – Endmin**

- Wing injuries: Fully regenerated.
    
- Protective capability: Unaffected.
    
- Flight capability: Not yet achieved.
    
- Scheduled training: To begin under Lucy's instruction during her next Blacksite visit.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

Several days later, Endmin stood alone in one of the Starlands' open meadows.

She unfolded her enormous wings.

They stretched far beyond her small frame.

She gave them a tentative flap.

A stronger one.

For the briefest moment, her feet left the ground before she landed again with an awkward stumble.

Lucy, watching from nearby, smiled.

> **Lucy:** "...That's a start."

Endmin looked back with a sheepish grin.

> "...I guess every good marksman has to miss a few shots first."

Lucy laughed.

> "...Exactly."

The first lesson had not truly begun.

But for the first time, Endmin was looking forward to the sky.
