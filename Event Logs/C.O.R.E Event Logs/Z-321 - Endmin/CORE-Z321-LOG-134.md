# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-134**

### **PERSONNEL INCIDENT REPORT – UNARMED FORCE DEMONSTRATION: BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Internal Personnel Record

---

## **SUMMARY**

An internal personnel incident resulted in an unexpected demonstration of Belle Merryweather's unarmed striking capability.

Despite her small stature, soft Rabbit Thiren physiology, and long-standing reliance on her oversized sword in combat, Belle possesses extraordinary punching power.

The incident prompted Engineering and Medical to formally document that Belle should **not** be considered harmless when unarmed.

---

## **INCIDENT**

Location: Engineering Corridor B

A researcher repeatedly mocked Belle's height while she was delivering documents.

Personnel nearby reported hearing comments including:

> **"...Can you even reach the top shelf?"

Belle ignored the remark and continued walking.

The researcher continued.

> **"...Did someone forget to pick up the kindergarten class?"

Several witnesses observed Belle stop walking.

She slowly turned around.

> **"...Please stop."

The researcher laughed.

> **"...Or what?"

Belle sighed.

She walked over.

Raised one fist.

And threw a single, full-force haymaker.

---

## **IMPACT**

Witnesses described the punch as sounding less like a fist...

...and more like a vehicle collision.

The researcher was thrown backwards into a reinforced wall.

Medical personnel responding to the incident documented:

- Multiple fractured ribs.
    
- Fractured pelvis.
    
- Extensive bruising.
    
- Concussion.
    

No life-threatening injuries were sustained.

Belle suffered no injury whatsoever.

---

## **Medical Observation**

Following the incident, Medical examined Belle's hands.

The findings were unusual.

Her hands remained:

- Soft.
    
- Warm.
    
- Fur-covered.
    
- Completely free of injury.
    

There was no swelling.

No bruising.

No fractures.

One physician reportedly stared at the scan for nearly a minute before quietly saying:

> **"...How?"**

---

## **Engineering Assessment**

Engineering concluded that Belle's strength does not correspond to her outward appearance.

Her compact frame is capable of generating force far exceeding what most personnel would expect.

One engineer summarized the report with the following remark:

> **"The mistake was assuming soft means weak."**

---

---

## **DISCIPLINARY REVIEW**

Security footage confirmed the researcher had repeatedly engaged in targeted verbal harassment despite Belle's request for the behavior to stop.

The review board determined that Belle's response occurred following sustained provocation.

No disciplinary action was taken against Belle.

The researcher received formal sanctions for workplace harassment upon recovery.

Personnel were reminded that respectful conduct toward colleagues is expected regardless of rank or appearance.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Belle continues to prefer resolving conflicts peacefully and remains primarily armed with her oversized hybrid sword during field operations.

However, personnel are now aware that disarming Belle does **not** render her defenseless.

As one anonymous engineer wrote beneath the incident report:

> **"Reminder to self."

> **"The sentient bunny plushie... punches like a semi-truck."**
