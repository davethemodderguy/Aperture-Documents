### **PHYSICAL ABILITY ADDENDUM – BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Personnel Capability Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

Further observation of Belle Merryweather has documented two additional characteristics associated with her Rabbit Thiren physiology and unique body structure.

In addition to her exceptional sprinting speed and impact resistance, Belle is capable of extraordinarily high jumps and can rebound or "bounce" after landing without sustaining injury.

These traits have become a familiar sight around Heaven's Blacksite.

---

## **High Jump Capability**

Belle's powerful legs allow her to leap significantly higher than the average Rabbit Thiren.

Whether clearing obstacles, reaching elevated platforms, or simply playing around with the Friendly Roommates, Belle frequently relies on jumping instead of climbing.

Personnel have jokingly nicknamed this behavior:

> **"The Bunny Route."**

---

## **Bounce Response**

Belle's soft, highly impact-absorbent body allows her to rebound after many landings.

Rather than stopping abruptly, she will often bounce one or more times before coming to a complete stop.

The effect is especially noticeable after:

- High jumps.
    
- Large falls.
    
- Accidental trips.
    
- Being launched during training exercises.
    

The behavior appears to be an unintended consequence of her remarkable ability to absorb and redistribute impact forces.

---

## **Observation Log**

During an outdoor recreation period, Star pointed toward the top of a storage container.

> **"...Bet you can't get up there."

Belle grinned.

> **"Watch me!"

She crouched.

Then launched herself upward.

With a single jump, Belle landed cleanly on top of the container.

She waved proudly.

> **"Hi!"

A moment later, she jumped back down.

**Boing.**

She bounced twice.

Landed on her feet.

Then continued walking as though nothing unusual had happened.

## **Safety Notice**

Personnel are reminded **not** to intentionally encourage Belle to perform dangerous jumps.

While Belle demonstrates exceptional resistance to impacts and frequently rebounds unharmed, standard Blacksite safety procedures remain in effect.

Belle herself is also reminded to announce when she intends to jump from elevated structures, as previous incidents have caused unnecessary panic among nearby personnel.
