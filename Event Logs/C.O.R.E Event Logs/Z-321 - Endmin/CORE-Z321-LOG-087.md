# **BANLANDS ADMINISTRATION**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-087**

### **PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE – BELLE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – Personnel Psychological Assessment

---

## **SUMMARY**

Belle is widely recognized throughout both the Banlands and APRT-70 as one of the friendliest individuals most personnel will ever meet.

She is cheerful.

Optimistic.

Affectionate.

Always eager to help.

However, Behavioral Sciences and the Krampus Compliance Authority have identified one subject that causes an immediate and dramatic change in her demeanor.

**Lying.**

Belle possesses an exceptionally low tolerance for dishonesty, regardless of its scale.

---

## **Background**

Prior to meeting Lucy and Endmin, Belle endured years of emotional abuse within her former family.

According to Belle's own testimony, she was repeatedly lied to, belittled, and manipulated.

She was frequently told she was:

- "Useless."
    
- "Worthless."
    
- "A burden."
    

These statements profoundly affected her self-worth during childhood.

Lucy later discovered Belle living in those conditions and brought her under the protection of the Merryweather household.

There, alongside Endmin, Belle gradually rebuilt her confidence.

After years of training, discipline, and public service, Belle earned her position through merit, eventually becoming the **Chief and Director of the Krampus Compliance Authority**.

---

## **Behavioral Observation**

Under ordinary circumstances, Belle is playful and expressive.

She laughs easily.

She enjoys hugs.

She trips over while running.

She brightens almost every room she enters.

But when she knowingly witnesses someone lying...

The change is immediate.

Her smile disappears.

Her ears lower.

Her posture straightens.

She quietly places a hand on the hilt of her sword.

Staff familiar with Belle have learned that this silence is far more unsettling than if she were shouting.

---

## **Case Study**

During an internal audit, an officer knowingly submitted a falsified report.

Belle listened without interrupting.

The officer continued insisting the report was accurate.

Belle slowly reached behind her back and rested her hand on her sword.

She spoke calmly.

> **"...Tell me the truth."**

The officer attempted another excuse.

Belle did not raise her voice.

> **"...One more chance."**

The officer confessed moments later.

After the investigation concluded, Belle personally thanked him for finally telling the truth.

The disciplinary action that followed was based on the misconduct itself—not the confession.

---

## **Psychological Assessment**

Behavioral Sciences concludes that Belle's hatred of lying is not rooted in anger alone.

It stems from painful personal experience.

Having spent much of her childhood being deceived and emotionally harmed, Belle places extraordinary value on honesty.

She does not expect perfection.

She understands that people make mistakes.

What she cannot accept is someone knowingly choosing deception when they have the opportunity to be truthful.

### **Closing Observation**

One evening in Room N-07, Belle sat between Endmin and Taiga while everyone shared stories over dinner.

The room was filled with laughter.

Belle smiled so much that her rabbit ears bounced with every giggle.

A newly assigned researcher quietly asked Endmin,

> **"...Is she always this happy?"**

Endmin looked at Belle for a moment before answering.

> **"...Most of the time."

She gently patted Belle's head.

> **"...She worked very hard to become this cheerful."

After a brief pause, Endmin added with unusual seriousness,

> **"...Just don't lie to her."

The researcher nodded.

> **"...That important?"

Endmin met his eyes.

> **"...More than you know."**
