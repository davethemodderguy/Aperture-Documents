# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-142**

### **VISITATION OBSERVATION – SHIFT CHANGE AT THE BLACKSITE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential Archive

---

## **SUMMARY**

Whenever Director Belle Merryweather returns to Heaven's Blacksite from official duties in the Banlands, Executive Protection Officer Keith escorts her to the dimensional transit point as required by protocol.

After Belle safely arrives at the Blacksite, security responsibility is formally transferred to Aperture Industries.

Keith does not leave immediately.

Instead, he typically spends a few minutes speaking with Director Lucious and Neko before returning to the Banlands.

Meanwhile, Keith's younger sister—Belle's secretary—often remains with the Friendly Roommates for a short visit.

---

## **Security Handover**

The transition has become a familiar routine.

Keith confirms Belle's safe arrival.

Blacksite Security acknowledges the transfer.

Belle waves enthusiastically.

> **"See you tomorrow, Keith!"

Keith gives a small nod.

> **"Take care, Director."

Only after confirming the handover is complete does he relax slightly.

---

## **Conversation with Director Lucious**

Keith and Director Lucious usually discuss operational matters.

Topics commonly include:

- Security developments in the Banlands.
    
- Coordination between Aperture Industries and the Krampus Compliance Authority.
    
- Protective procedures.
    
- Threat assessments.
    

The conversations are brief, professional, and efficient.

---

## **Conversation with Neko**

Keith's discussions with Neko are noticeably different.

Rather than strategy, they often revolve around equipment.

Examples include:

- Shotgun maintenance.
    
- Ammunition performance.
    
- Weapon durability.
    
- Mechanical improvements.
    

Neko occasionally presents prototype components.

Keith provides practical feedback from field use.

Their exchanges have contributed to several refinements of Keith's modified M1014 combat shotgun.

---

## **Meanwhile...**

While Keith is occupied, his younger sister naturally drifts toward Room N-07.

Belle immediately notices her.

> **"You're here!"

The secretary smiles.

> **"Finished your paperwork?"

Belle proudly nods.

> **"...Mostly."

Endmin quietly raises an eyebrow.

> **"...Mostly?"

Belle looks away.

> **"...Eighty percent..."

Everyone laughs.

---

## **Friendly Roommates**

Keith's sister has become a familiar visitor.

She often spends her short visits chatting with:

- Belle.
    
- Endmin.
    
- Taiga.
    
- Mart.
    
- Lucius.
    

The conversations rarely involve work.

Instead, they revolve around everyday life, stories from the Banlands, and whatever amusing situation Belle has found herself in recently.

---

## **Observation Log**

As Keith and Director Lucious discussed recent security reports, Neko laid several shotgun components across a workbench.

> **Neko:** "How did the reinforced shell carrier perform?"

Keith considered the question.

> **"Reliable."

> **"Though I'd prefer a stronger retaining spring."

Neko made a note.

> **"...Already working on one."

Across the hallway...

Belle's secretary walked into Room N-07.

Belle immediately hugged her.

**Squeak.**

Taiga laughed.

Mart looked up from his sketchbook.

Lucius poured another cup of tea.

Endmin smiled quietly.

The office atmosphere of the Banlands was left outside the door.

For a little while...

Everyone simply enjoyed each other's company.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

These brief visits have become an informal tradition following Belle's return from Banlands assignments.

While Keith exchanges updates with Director Lucious and Neko, his younger sister enjoys a welcome break with Belle and the Friendly Roommates.

By the time Keith is ready to return to duty, Belle is already smiling again.

For him...

That is enough to know his companion made it home safely once more.
