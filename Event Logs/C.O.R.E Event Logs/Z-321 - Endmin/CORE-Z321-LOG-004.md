# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-004**

### **ANOMALOUS ABILITY REPORT — ADAPTIVE POWER REPLICATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – HIGH PRIORITY / N.O.S.T.

---

## **SUMMARY**

Further analysis has confirmed that **Z-321 ("Endmin")** possesses an anomalous adaptive ability allowing her to **learn and replicate the powers or special abilities of others through direct physical contact.**

This process is passive and does not appear to require conscious activation.

Simple physical contact is sufficient.

Examples include:

- Handshakes.
    
- Hugs.
    
- Being carried.
    
- Piggyback rides.
    
- Physical assistance.
    
- Casual contact during daily activities.
    

No discomfort or loss of ability has been observed in the original owner.

Replication does **not** appear to remove, weaken, or alter the source individual's abilities.

---

## **CURRENT UNDERSTANDING**

Research Division currently believes Endmin's ability functions as an adaptive learning mechanism rather than outright theft.

The replicated abilities become part of her own growing repertoire.

Endmin described it simply.

> "...It's like learning a new language."

> "...Some are easier than others."

---

## **OBSERVED DEVELOPMENT**

Following several weeks living in Room N-07, researchers noticed Endmin demonstrating an increasing variety of anomalous behaviors.

Initially, these were believed to be unrelated coincidences.

Subsequent review of security footage revealed a consistent pattern.

Almost every newly demonstrated ability appeared only **after physical contact** with another anomalous resident.

Considering Endmin's personality...

This occurred frequently.

Very frequently.

---

## **INCIDENT LOG**

During one morning briefing, Director Lucious reviewed Endmin's developing profile.

He paused midway through the report.

Then slowly looked toward Room N-07.

Inside:

Taiga had just enthusiastically hugged Endmin.

Astral was fixing Endmin's collar.

Mart had helped her carry several books only minutes earlier.

Star had greeted her with her usual enthusiastic hug.

Frost had patted her shoulder after breakfast.

Neko had helped adjust her backpack.

Director Lucious slowly lowered the report.

A long silence followed.

Finally...

> **Director Lucious:**

> "...How many people have touched this child?"

Research personnel quietly looked at one another.

One researcher cautiously answered.

> "...Yes."

Silence.

Lucious closed the report.

> "...That doesn't answer my question."

The researcher looked increasingly nervous.

> "...Exactly."

---

## **CURRENT ABILITY LIBRARY**

Due to the amount of daily interaction Endmin has with Blacksite residents, the complete list of replicated abilities is currently under continuous review.

Researchers have confirmed that her available abilities now represent a combination of numerous residents' unique traits.

The exact limits of simultaneous replication remain unknown.

Notably, Endmin does **not** instinctively use every replicated ability.

She appears to select them deliberately depending on the situation.

---

## **LIMITATIONS**

Current observations indicate several important restrictions.

- Physical contact is required before initial replication.
    
- Replicated abilities require practice to master.
    
- Endmin cannot immediately achieve the same level of proficiency as the original owner in every case.
    
- Some highly specialized abilities require extensive training before practical use.
    

Research into additional limitations remains ongoing.

---

## **PERSONNEL RESPONSES**

### Taiga Sunbringer

After learning about Endmin's ability, Taiga became visibly excited.

> "...Wait..."

She counted on her fingers.

> "...I've hugged you..."

> "...Like..."

She stopped counting.

> "...A lot."

Endmin smiled innocently.

> "...You have."

Taiga stared.

> "...Oops."

---

### Mart Sunbringer

Mart quietly apologized.

> "...Sorry."

Endmin tilted her head.

> "...For what?"

> "...I carried your books..."

Endmin laughed.

> "...Mart."

> "...I'm not going to complain about free superpowers."

Mart blinked.

Then quietly laughed as well.

---

### Star Dawnbringer

Star immediately became curious.

> "...Can you copy flying?"

Endmin thought for a moment.

> "...Maybe."

Star grinned.

> "...Let's test it."

Director Lucious interrupted before either of them could leave.

> "...Outside."

---

### Neko

Neko reviewed the report before calmly writing one additional sentence beneath it.

> **"Research Division is no longer permitted to estimate how many abilities Endmin currently possesses."**

Reason:

> **"The answer changes every day."**

---

### Frost

Frost jokingly suggested everyone stop hugging Endmin.

Taiga looked horrified.

> "...Absolutely not."

Endmin laughed.

---

## **DIRECTOR'S REMARK**

Director Lucious added the following administrative note.

> "Replication through ordinary social interaction was not something I expected to write into official documentation."

A second note appeared beneath it.

> "...Please continue treating Endmin normally."

> "...We are not banning hugs."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Z-321 – Endmin**

- Replication ability: Confirmed.
    
- Activation: Passive through physical contact.
    
- Ability library: Expanding.
    
- Operational threat level: Stable.
    
- Social behavior: Unchanged.
    

Research Division considers Endmin one of the most adaptable anomalous residents currently housed within the Blacksite.

Despite this, she continues behaving exactly as she did on her first day:

Like an energetic, polite girl simply happy to have people around her.

---

### **Closing Observation**

During evening recreation, Endmin walked into the common room carrying a stack of books.

Taiga immediately ran over and hugged her.

Mart smiled and helped take the books.

Astral gently fixed Endmin's slightly crooked collar.

Star ruffled her hair.

Frost handed her a cup of hot chocolate.

Director Lucious happened to walk past the doorway.

He watched the scene unfold.

Then quietly muttered to himself,

> "...At this rate..."

He closed the report in his hands.

> "...She's going to collect this entire facility."

No one inside the room heard him.

They were too busy laughing together.
