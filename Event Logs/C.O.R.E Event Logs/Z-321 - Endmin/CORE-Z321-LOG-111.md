# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-111**

### **RESIDENTIAL UPDATE – LUCIUS MERRYWEATHER JOINS ROOM N-07**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Residential

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following medical treatment and clearance by Behavioral Sciences, **Lucius Merryweather** accepted Lucy and Endmin's invitation to remain at APRT-70 while recovering.

At Endmin's request, Lucius was assigned temporary residence in **Room N-07**.

---

## **The First Night**

When everyone returned to Room N-07, Endmin showed Lucius around.

The room was lively, comfortable, and far different from the streets where he had spent the last several years.

A spare mattress had already been prepared.

Instead...

Lucius quietly laid a blanket on the floor.

Taiga tilted her head.

> **"...The bed's over there."**

Lucius smiled apologetically.

> **"...I'm... not really used to sleeping in one anymore."**

> **"...The floor feels normal."**

The room became quiet.

Endmin's ears lowered.

Lucy looked away for a moment.

No one argued.

Taiga simply moved her own bedding a little closer.

> **"...Then you won't be sleeping alone."**

Lucius looked surprised.

> **"...Thanks."**

---

## **A Small Surprise**

Looking around the room, Lucius blinked several times.

He counted silently.

Then looked back toward Endmin.

> **"...There are..."

He paused.

> **"...A lot of Thirens here."**

Belle raised her hand enthusiastically from across the room.

> **"...Yep!"

Taiga grinned.

> **"...You're definitely not the odd one out anymore."**

Lucius laughed.

> **"...I noticed."**

---

## **New Belongings**

Before the evening ended, Endmin placed a neatly folded bundle on the table.

Inside were:

- A fresh Blacksite uniform tailored for him.
    
- Casual clothing.
    
- Comfortable shoes.
    
- Personal hygiene supplies.
    

Finally, she handed him a small keychain.

Attached were his newly issued Aperture access cards.

Lucius carefully turned the keychain over in his hand.

> **"...This is mine?"**

Endmin smiled.

> **"...You're home now."**

For several moments...

Lucius simply stared at the cards.

Then clipped them onto his belt.

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Lucius Merryweather is temporarily residing in **Room N-07** while recovering physically and emotionally.

Residential Services will gradually help him readjust to everyday life.

Although a bed has been prepared for him, Lucius currently prefers sleeping on a mattress on the floor near Taiga, a habit developed during his years surviving on the streets.

Behavioral Sciences expects this preference may change naturally over time as he becomes more comfortable.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late that night, the lights in Room N-07 were switched off.

Everyone settled in.

Lucius lay quietly on his mattress.

For the first time in years...

There was no rain.

No cold pavement.

No fear of being chased away.

Just the quiet breathing of the people around him.

He looked toward Endmin's bed.

She was already asleep.

Still wearing the bracelet.

Lucius smiled softly.

Then, for the first time since disappearing four years earlier...

He fell asleep knowing exactly where he would wake up.
