## **VISUAL CAPABILITY**

Although Endmin is medically classified as approximately **95% blind**, operational observations demonstrate remarkable spatial awareness.

She consistently:

- Navigates unfamiliar rooms without assistance.
    
- Detects movement with high accuracy.
    
- Estimates distance precisely.
    
- Recognizes individuals through movement, posture, and other sensory cues.
    

During recreational activities, several personnel forgot she possessed impaired vision at all.

Her disability primarily affects conventional visual perception rather than her ability to function independently.

## **MARKSMANSHIP EVALUATION**

As part of a voluntary recreational session at the Blacksite shooting range, Endmin participated in standard firearms qualification.

Several instructors lowered the difficulty of the course, assuming her visual impairment would significantly affect performance.

The assumption proved incorrect.

Endmin calmly requested that the targets begin moving.

The request was approved.

Results:

- Every target was successfully engaged.
    
- Accuracy exceeded that of several veteran Security personnel.
    
- No unintended impacts were recorded.
    
- Consistent center-mass and precision hits were observed throughout the exercise.
    

After the session, one instructor quietly asked,

> "...How are you doing that?"

Endmin smiled.

> "...Practice."

She declined to elaborate further.

Medical and Research Divisions agree that her method of spatial perception likely allows for a level of environmental awareness fundamentally different from ordinary eyesight.
