# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-022**

### **PERSONNEL PROFILE ADDENDUM — APPEARANCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – RESIDENT RECORD

---

## **SUMMARY**

Routine interviews with Z-321 ("Endmin") have documented several details regarding her appearance that were previously omitted from her personnel profile.

Most notably, Endmin possesses voluntary control over the color of her hair through an anomalous Ether-based ability.

Despite this capability, she consistently chooses to keep her natural white hair.

---

## **HAIR COLOR MANIPULATION**

Endmin demonstrated the ability before Research Division under controlled observation.

Without any visible strain, the color of her hair shifted smoothly through several shades.

Observed colors included:

- Black
    
- Brown
    
- Blonde
    
- Silver
    
- Crimson
    
- Deep blue
    
- Violet
    
- White (default)
    

No measurable Ether fluctuations beyond normal baseline levels were detected during the process.

Researchers currently believe the ability is a cosmetic Ether manipulation rather than a biological transformation.

When asked why she rarely changes it, Endmin smiled.

> **Endmin:** "I can..."

She gently held a lock of her hair.

> "...But I like white."

Another researcher jokingly suggested trying bright pink.

Endmin laughed.

> "...Maybe for five minutes."

She did not elaborate.

---

## **HAIRSTYLE**

Endmin keeps her hair deliberately neat and practical.

Characteristics include:

- Short overall length.
    
- Evenly trimmed around the sides and back.
    
- Short bangs carefully clipped above the eyes to prevent interference with her mask.
    
- Minimal daily maintenance.
    

Although her protective mask already covers much of her face, she explained that longer bangs become uncomfortable beneath it.

> **Endmin:** "If it gets too long..."

She brushed her bangs lightly.

> "...It catches inside the mask."

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Taiga Sunbringer

After seeing Endmin briefly change her hair color several times, Taiga excitedly suggested,

> "...Rainbow!"

Endmin looked thoughtful.

For approximately three seconds, her hair displayed a soft rainbow gradient.

She immediately changed it back to white.

> **Endmin:** "...Too distracting."

Taiga laughed.

---

### Mart Sunbringer

Mart glanced up from his book.

> "...White suits you."

Endmin smiled.

> "...I think so too."

---

### Star

Star was fascinated by the demonstration.

> **Star:** "Can you match flowers?"

Endmin spent the next several minutes matching her hair to different flowers growing around the Blacksite gardens.

Everyone agreed the white version remained the most recognizable.

---

### Lucy

When asked whether Endmin had always kept white hair, Lucy nodded.

> "...Almost always."

She smiled warmly.

> "...Even when she was little."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Z-321 – Endmin**

- Hair color: White (preferred).
    
- Alternate colors: Voluntarily selectable.
    
- Hair length: Short.
    
- Bangs: Neatly clipped above the mask.
    
- Appearance preference: Practical and low maintenance.
    

---

### **Closing Observation**

Before leaving for the day's training, Endmin paused in front of a mirror.

She briefly changed her hair to black.

Then blonde.

Then blue.

She smiled to herself.

A moment later, it quietly returned to white.

She adjusted the straps of her mask, smoothed her neatly clipped bangs, picked up her rifle, and headed out the door.

It was the look she felt most like herself in.
