# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-007**

### **MEDICAL INCIDENT — POST-TRAUMATIC SYMPTOMS FOLLOWING MASK DISTURBANCE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 3 – MEDICAL / RESIDENT WELFARE

---

## **SUMMARY**

Approximately twenty-four hours after the unauthorized contact incident documented in **CORE-Z321-LOG-006**, Z-321 ("Endmin") developed a fever and symptoms consistent with acute motion sickness.

Medical Division determined that the illness was not infectious.

Instead, it was likely triggered by a combination of physical irritation around her damaged eyes, stress, exhaustion, and the emotional distress caused by the previous day's events.

---

## **INITIAL OBSERVATION**

During breakfast, personnel noticed Endmin eating unusually slowly.

Unlike her normal energetic self, she remained quiet.

Mart, seated beside her, noticed she kept one hand resting against her forehead.

> **Mart:** "...Are you okay?"

Endmin gave a small smile.

> "...I'm fine."

She stood to return her tray.

After only a few steps, she paused.

The hallway appeared to spin.

She instinctively grabbed the nearest table for support.

---

## **MEDICAL RESPONSE**

Medical staff escorted Endmin to the infirmary.

Initial findings:

- Mild fever.
    
- Elevated heart rate.
    
- Significant dizziness.
    
- Temporary motion sickness.
    
- Increased sensitivity to movement and bright environments.
    
- Fatigue.
    

When asked whether anything hurt, Endmin quietly pointed toward her mask.

> "...Everything shakes..."

She swallowed.

> "...It makes me feel sick."

Medical Division concluded that repeated impacts to the mask the previous day had aggravated the delicate structures surrounding her injured eyes, resulting in temporary vestibular irritation and disorientation.

No permanent damage was detected.

---

## **ROOM N-07 RESPONSE**

News spread quickly through Room N-07.

Taiga arrived first.

She carefully sat beside Endmin's bed.

Unlike usual, she didn't speak immediately.

Instead, she gently placed a glass of water on the bedside table.

> **Taiga:** "...You don't have to pretend you're okay."

Endmin looked away.

> "...I didn't want everyone worrying."

Taiga smiled sadly.

> "...Too late."

---

Mart quietly entered carrying several books.

He placed them beside the bed.

> **Mart:** "...In case you get bored."

Endmin looked at the books.

Then at Mart.

> "...Thanks."

Mart gave a small nod.

> "...Get better."

---

Astral spent most of the afternoon beside Endmin.

She occasionally brushed a hand through Endmin's hair while reading aloud from one of Mart's books.

Endmin eventually fell asleep halfway through the chapter.

Astral quietly continued reading anyway.

---

## **MEDICAL ORDERS**

Medical Division issued the following temporary recommendations:

- Reduced physical activity until dizziness subsides.
    
- Avoid rapid head movements.
    
- Quiet, dimly lit environments whenever possible.
    
- No unnecessary physical contact involving the protective mask.
    
- Continued observation for any worsening symptoms.
    

The recommendations were distributed facility-wide.

---

## **DIRECTOR'S ADDENDUM**

Director Lucious attached a short note beneath the medical report.

> "This incident was preventable."

A second sentence followed.

> "Resident medical equipment is not to be touched without permission."

The directive has since been incorporated into orientation material for newly assigned personnel.

---

## **PERSONNEL COMMENTS**

### Taiga Sunbringer

Taiga checked on Endmin multiple times throughout the day.

Each visit ended the same way.

> "...Need anything?"

Endmin always smiled.

> "...Stay."

Taiga always did.

---

### Mart Sunbringer

Mart quietly remained in the infirmary for much of the evening, reading nearby.

Neither of them spoke very much.

Neither seemed to mind.

---

### Astral Sunbringer

Astral later remarked:

> "...Sometimes the best medicine..."

She looked toward the sleeping Endmin.

> "...Is making sure someone doesn't wake up alone."

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

**Z-321 – Endmin**

- Fever: Mild, improving.
    
- Motion sickness: Temporary.
    
- Eye condition: Stable.
    
- Psychological condition: Recovering.
    
- Expected prognosis: Full recovery with rest.
    

Medical staff anticipate complete resolution of the temporary symptoms over the coming days.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late that evening, Endmin stirred awake.

The room was quiet.

Taiga had fallen asleep in a chair beside the bed.

Mart was slumped over a book that had long since slipped closed.

Astral remained nearby, quietly watching over them both.

Endmin smiled faintly.

She reached up to adjust the little **":3"** sticky note still attached to her mask.

It was slightly crooked.

She left it that way.

Then, feeling a little less alone than she had the day before, she closed her eyes and went back to sleep.
