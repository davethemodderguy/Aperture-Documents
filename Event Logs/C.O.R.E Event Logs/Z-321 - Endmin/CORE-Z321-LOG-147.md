# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-147**

### **THIREN ETIQUETTE – THE SHARK TAIL RULE**

**Classification:** LEVEL 1 – Residential Orientation

---

## **NOTICE**

Following several awkward incidents involving curious researchers, the following etiquette has been added to the Blacksite orientation handbook regarding the visiting Shark Thiren siblings.

---

## **KEITH**

**Do not touch Keith's tail without his explicit permission.**

Keith is professional and courteous, but his tail is considered personal space.

Unexpected contact is strongly discouraged.

If someone accidentally brushes against it, Keith typically responds with nothing more than a brief glance before continuing his work.

Deliberately grabbing or pulling it, however, is considered extremely disrespectful.

Fortunately, very few people are willing to test the patience of an executive protection officer carrying a combat shotgun.

---

## **CINDER**

Cinder is considerably more relaxed.

She generally doesn't mind if trusted friends gently touch or pat her tail after asking.

Among Blacksite residents, it has become fairly common.

Belle has been seen absentmindedly hugging Cinder from behind while holding onto her tail.

Taiga has asked if it feels different from a wolf tail.

Star has declared it "very squishy."

Cinder usually just laughs.

> **"...You all are strange."

---

## **Observation Log**

During one visit, Belle noticed Keith standing near the convoy vehicle.

Curious, she reached toward his tail.

Keith looked over before she could touch it.

> **"...Director."

Belle immediately withdrew her hand.

> **"...Sorry."

A few minutes later...

Cinder walked into Room N-07 carrying paperwork.

Star looked at her.

Then at her tail.

> **"...Can I?"

Cinder smiled.

> **"...Sure."

A few seconds later, Star happily declared:

> **"...Soft."

Taiga nodded in agreement.

> **"...Very."

Mart looked over from the couch.

> **"...There's an actual queue now?"
