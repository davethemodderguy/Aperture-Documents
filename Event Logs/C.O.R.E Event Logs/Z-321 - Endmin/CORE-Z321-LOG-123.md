# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-123**

### **RESIDENTIAL OBSERVATION – COMFORT DURING NIGHTMARES**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Personnel Welfare

---

## **SUMMARY**

Behavioral Sciences has observed that both Endmin and Belle occasionally experience nightmares related to past traumatic experiences.

Over time, the two have naturally developed a mutual habit of comforting one another.

No formal intervention was ever suggested.

It simply became part of their routine.

---

## **Observation**

Room N-07 personnel have noted that when either Endmin or Belle awakens from a nightmare, the other often notices almost immediately.

Neither asks many questions.

Instead, they instinctively move closer.

Most commonly, Belle wraps both arms around Endmin in a tight hug.

If Belle is the one frightened, Endmin returns the embrace just as firmly.

The room usually becomes quiet again within a few minutes.

---

## **One Recorded Evening**

Late one night, Belle suddenly woke with a startled gasp.

Before anyone else could react, Endmin quietly climbed down from her bed.

Without saying a word, she sat beside Belle.

Belle's ears drooped.

> **"...Bad dream."

Endmin nodded.

> **"...Again?"

Belle gave a small nod.

Endmin gently hugged her.

Belle immediately hugged back, burying her face against Endmin's shoulder.

After several moments, Belle's breathing slowed.

> **"...Thanks."

> **"...Always."

---

## **Another Night**

On another occasion, it was Endmin who awoke from a nightmare.

Belle noticed first.

Still half asleep herself, she shuffled across the room and wrapped Endmin in a soft but surprisingly strong hug.

Endmin didn't resist.

She simply leaned into it.

Belle quietly patted her back.

> **"...You're safe."

Endmin closed her eyes.

> **"...I know."
