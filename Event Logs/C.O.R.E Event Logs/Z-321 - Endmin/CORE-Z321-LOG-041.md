# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-041**

### **COMPLETE ETHER STABILIZATION**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – Medical / Ether Research

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following extensive observation after the 95% purification milestone, Medical Division confirms that Z-321 (Endmin) has reached **complete biological stabilization**.

Rather than removing every trace of Ether from her body, researchers successfully eliminated all **harmful Ether Corruption and associated Ether radiation** while preserving the unique physiological adaptations that had become part of Endmin's nervous system over the years.

As a result, Endmin no longer emits hazardous Ether radiation.

She has retained the benefits of both forms of perception without the pathological effects that once threatened her health.

---

## **Medical Findings**

### Ether Corruption

**Status:** Eliminated as an active pathological condition.

Repeated examinations found:

- No measurable harmful Ether radiation.
    
- No signs of Ether instability.
    
- No progressive tissue damage.
    
- No risk of spontaneous Ether surges.
    

Endmin no longer requires medical monitoring for Ether contamination.

---

## **Visual Function**

Endmin now possesses two complementary methods of perception.

### Conventional Vision

- Normal color vision.
    
- Normal depth perception.
    
- Daylight tolerance restored.
    
- Standard visual acuity.
    

### Ether Sense

Her unique Ether-based perception remains intact.

It activates naturally when needed and provides:

- Perception in complete darkness.
    
- Detection of ambient Ether.
    
- Recognition of Ether disturbances.
    
- Exceptional spatial awareness.
    

Researchers concluded that her brain now transitions seamlessly between conventional sight and Ether Sense.

The two systems no longer interfere with one another.

---

## **Research Conclusion**

Medical Division considers this outcome unprecedented.

The original objective had been to save Endmin's eyesight.

Instead, the treatment resulted in a stable integration of recovered biological vision and her anomalous sensory abilities.

Director Lucious summarized the findings during the final review meeting.

> **"We didn't erase what made her unique."**

> **"We removed what was hurting her."**

---

## **Lucy's Statement**

After reviewing the final report, Lucy requested a private meeting with Director Lucious.

She entered his office carrying the completed medical file.

For a long moment, she simply looked at it.

Then she closed the folder.

She bowed her head.

Deeply.

A gesture few had ever witnessed from the Warden of the Banlands.

> **"Director Lucious."**

> **"You gave my daughter a future I believed had already been taken from her."**

She raised her head.

There was no trace of hesitation in her voice.

> **"I owe Aperture Industries a debt that cannot be measured."**

After a brief pause, she continued.

> **"The Banlands owes Aperture Industries a favor."**

The room fell silent.

For an organization as independent and formidable as the Banlands, such a declaration carried extraordinary weight.

Director Lucious smiled politely.

> **"Then let's hope we never have to ask."**

Lucy smiled in return.

> **"Agreed."**
