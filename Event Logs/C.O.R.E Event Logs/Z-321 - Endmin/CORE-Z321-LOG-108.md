# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-108**

### **BEHAVIORAL SCIENCES – SHARED FEAR OF ABANDONMENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Personnel Welfare

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following a routine discussion among the residents of Room N-07, Behavioral Sciences noted an unexpected pattern.

Many members of the **Friendly Roommates** have, at one point or another, exhibited a fear of abandonment, isolation, or losing those they trust.

The realization was not made during formal counseling.

It happened during an ordinary evening conversation.

---

## **The Conversation**

The topic began when someone casually remarked that almost nobody in Room N-07 actually enjoyed being left alone.

One by one, everyone thought back to when they first arrived at the Blacksite.

The room gradually became quieter.

---

## **Documented Events**

### **Neko**

When Neko first joined the Blacksite, an administrator manipulated staff schedules to reduce their own son's workload.

The altered schedule unintentionally left Neko isolated from the people she relied on.

Although the incident was eventually corrected, it left a lasting impression on her.

---

### **Taiga**

Shortly after arriving, Taiga experienced a nightmare in which she witnessed her mother dying.

She awoke in panic and instinctively ran away before eventually being found and comforted.

The event became one of the earliest welfare reports involving her.

---

### **Mart**

Mart also suffered a severe nightmare during his early days at the Blacksite.

Rather than staying in his room, he quietly left.

Taiga was the one who found him and stayed by his side until he had calmed down.

---

### **Endmin**

Before treatment for her Ether Corruption and before she became comfortable showing her eyes, Endmin wore her signature metal eye mask at all times.

Some researchers repeatedly tapped or poked the mask out of curiosity despite her visible discomfort.

Although the behavior eventually stopped, the experience reinforced Endmin's reluctance to let others get close during that period.

---

## **Observations**

Behavioral Sciences notes that these experiences differ greatly in cause and severity.

However, they share a common outcome.

Each incident occurred during a vulnerable period in the individual's life and influenced how they formed trust within the Blacksite.

As friendships developed, many of those early fears gradually lessened.

---

## **Exceptions**

Among the current residents of Room N-07, two individuals have not demonstrated a documented fear of abandonment or prolonged isolation:

- **Miss Neko**
    
- **Niko**
    

This observation is descriptive only and should not be interpreted as either resilience or vulnerability compared to other residents.

The Friendly Roommates continue to participate in the Blacksite's informal Buddy System and regularly check on one another.

Behavioral Sciences considers the strong social bonds within Room N-07 to be a positive protective factor for the group's overall well-being.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Late that evening, the Friendly Roommates sat together in comfortable silence.

No missions.

No emergencies.

Just quiet conversation.

After a while, Star looked around the room.

> **"...We've all had bad days, haven't we?"

No one answered immediately.

They didn't need to.

The silence itself was enough.

Belle leaned gently against Endmin.

Mart sat beside Taiga.

Neko and Miss Neko continued tinkering with a project nearby.

Nightshade watched over the room.

Niko quietly turned another page in his book.

For a moment, no one was thinking about the difficult days that brought them here.

Only about the people sitting beside them now.

And for everyone in Room N-07, that was enough.
