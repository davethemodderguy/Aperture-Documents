# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-122**

### **THIREN PHYSIOLOGY OBSERVATION – BELLE MERRYWEATHER**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Biological Observation

---

## **SUMMARY**

Repeated observations of Belle Merryweather have revealed several unusual characteristics associated with her Rabbit Thiren physiology.

Most notable is her remarkable ability to withstand impacts that would normally result in injury.

---

## **Impact Resistance**

Belle's body possesses exceptionally soft fur and dense underlying tissue, allowing it to absorb and distribute impact forces efficiently.

Observed examples include:

- Falling backward after sprinting with her oversized sword.
    
- Accidentally striking her head against tables and walls.
    
- Landing from considerable heights.
    
- Being knocked over during training.
    

In nearly every documented case, Belle simply bounces back onto her feet with little or no injury.

Personnel often compare the experience of hugging Belle to hugging a very large plush toy.

---

## **Practical Effects**

Belle's physiology provides excellent protection against blunt impacts.

Examples include:

- Falls.
    
- Trips.
    
- Accidental collisions.
    
- Non-penetrating impacts during training.
    

Medical personnel have jokingly described her as having "built-in cushioning."

---

## **Limitations**

Belle's impact resistance should **not** be mistaken for invulnerability.

Her soft body does **not** protect her from penetrating or cutting injuries.

She remains vulnerable to conventional weapons, including firearms and bladed weapons.

Accordingly, Belle relies heavily on:

- Exceptional sprinting speed.
    
- Rabbit Thiren agility.
    
- Rapid changes in direction.
    
- Acrobatics using her oversized sword.
    

Rather than absorbing bullets, Belle's preferred method of survival is simply to avoid being hit.

As one training instructor summarized:

> **"If Belle gets shot, something has already gone wrong."**

---

## **Training Demonstration**

During an agility exercise, automated training drones fired low-power practice projectiles along a programmed course.

Belle planted her sword into the floor.

Using it as a pivot, she vaulted around the blade, changing direction repeatedly.

Every practice round missed.

After the exercise ended, Belle pulled her sword free and smiled.

> **"...Much easier than getting hit."**

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Belle's Rabbit Thiren physiology grants her an unusually high tolerance to blunt impacts and falls, making accidental injuries from such events uncommon.

However, she is **not bulletproof** or immune to conventional weapons.

Her survivability in combat comes from the combination of her natural agility, remarkable speed, extensive training, and the disciplined use of her unique fighting style.
