# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-080**

### **DIPLOMATIC VISIT – ARRIVAL AT THE MERRYWEATHER MANOR (PART I)**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Cultural Exchange / Banlands Relations

---

### **SUMMARY**

Following an invitation from Z-945 (Lucy) and Z-321 (Endmin), members of the **Friendly Roommates**, Bao, Astral, and Director Lucious traveled to the Banlands to visit the Merryweather Manor.

The visit was intended as a cultural exchange and a gesture of appreciation following Aperture Industries' assistance in curing Endmin's Ether Corruption.

Lucy and Endmin departed several hours early to prepare the manor, leaving a stable dimensional rift connecting APRT-70 to the Banlands.

---

## **Arrival**

Once preparations were complete, Lucy contacted the Blacksite.

> **Lucy:**
> 
> "Everything's ready. You may come through."

One by one, everyone stepped through the shimmering rift.

The cold Icelandic air disappeared instantly.

A warm breeze greeted them.

Stretching before them was a massive floating island suspended high above the Banlands.

At its center stood an elegant manor built from pale stone, surrounded by immaculate gardens and ancient trees.

Far below, an enormous city sprawled across rolling grasslands, its lights sparkling beneath the floating estate.

For a few moments...

Nobody spoke.

---

### **The Walk**

As they made their way toward the front gate, conversation gradually returned.

Star skipped happily along the stone path, looking around with childlike excitement.

> **Star:**
> 
> "Damn, my first time visiting this place in ages!"

Bao admired the scenery.

> **"...This isn't Hell."**

Taiga smiled.

She carried Aster comfortably in her arms.

> **"Pretty sure Lucy emphasized that this place is a purgatory."**

Mart looked over the cliffs surrounding the floating island.

> **"Yeah... and it doesn't look like Hell either."**

He smiled.

> **"It's peaceful."**

Director Lucious quietly observed the architecture without saying a word.

---

## **At the Main Gate**

The group arrived before an enormous iron gate bearing the Merryweather family crest.

Two Banlands Security Guards stepped forward.

One saluted professionally.

The other folded his arms with a smug grin.

> **Guard:**
> 
> "Well, well."

He looked the group over.

> **"Who are these peasants wandering up to the Warden's manor?"**

The second guard, a young woman, looked uncomfortable.

> **Female Guard:**
> 
> "Sir... maybe we should just call Lady Lucy first."

> **"I don't think random visitors can even reach this island..."**

The senior guard scoffed.

> **"I'm the Head of Security."**

> **"You want to get fired?"**

She immediately fell silent.

---

The guard turned back toward the visitors.

> **"You lot can't seriously be the people Lindervene kept talking about."**

The female guard quietly attempted to help.

> **"...She asked everyone to call her Endmin..."**

Without looking at her, the senior guard snapped.

> **"Did I give you permission to speak?"**

She lowered her head.

---

Director Lucious stepped forward calmly.

> **"We don't want any trouble."**

> **"Would you please inform Lucy that we're here?"**

The guard laughed.

> **"Nice try."**

> **"You're just another fan hoping to meet the Warden."**

He gave Director Lucious a dismissive shove.

> **"Turn around."**

Several members of the group instinctively tensed.

---

## **Endmin Arrives**

Before anyone could react further...

The front doors of the manor burst open.

> **"Hey!"

Everyone turned.

Endmin came running down the stone steps.

She wore an elegant white dress prepared for the occasion.

Her cat ears and tail were concealed beneath perfectly maintained Ether illusions.

She waved excitedly.

Then noticed the situation.

Her smile vanished.

> **"...Why are you keeping my friends outside?"**

The senior guard froze.

Color drained from his face.

> **"...M-Miss Lindervene..."**

Endmin immediately covered her face.

> **"HEY!"**

> **"I TOLD YOU NOT TO CALL ME THAT!"**

Her ears nearly appeared through the illusion before she hastily corrected it.

---

## **Lucy Arrives**

Hearing the commotion, Lucy hurried outside wearing her formal Warden's uniform.

> **"Endmin? What's going on?"**

Endmin immediately pointed toward the gate.

Her eyes shimmered briefly with Ether.

Tiny illusionary tears appeared.

> **"Mom..."

> **"...Our Head of Security is bullying my friends..."**

She dramatically sniffled.

> **"...And your benefactors..."**

Lucy looked from Endmin...

To Director Lucious...

To the guard.

The illusion didn't matter.

The situation spoke for itself.

Her expression became ice cold.

---

## **Dismissal**

Lucy faced the senior guard.

> **"You're dismissed."**

The guard blinked.

> **"...Ma'am?"**

> **"Effective immediately."**

> **"Hand over your insignia and weapon."**

There was no shouting.

No argument.

Only silence.

Realizing there was no changing her decision, the former Head of Security slowly complied before being escorted away.

---

## **Alice**

As everyone relaxed, Endmin suddenly looked around.

> **"...Where's Alice?"**

She scanned the courtyard.

Concern appeared on her face.

> **"...Has anyone seen Alice?"**

A few moments later, the same young female guard came jogging toward them, clearly out of breath.

> **"...Sorry! I was dealing with another patrol!"**

Lucy looked at her.

> **"...Did you abandon your post?"**

Before Alice could answer, Taiga stepped forward.

> **"No!"**

Everyone looked at her.

> **"She's the one who tried to protect us."**

Star nodded immediately.

> **"I can confirm that."**

Nightshade gave a silent nod of agreement.

Director Lucious also added,

> **"She attempted to de-escalate the situation from the beginning."**

---

## **A Promotion**

Endmin smiled.

She cleared her throat.

> **"Alice."**

The young guard stood at attention.

> **"Yes, Miss End—"

She immediately corrected herself.

> **"...Endmin."**

Endmin nodded approvingly.

> **"...Would you like to become our new Head of Security?"**

Alice blinked.

> **"...W-Wait..."

> **"Only Lady Lucy can promote someone..."**

Before Lucy could answer—

Endmin threw both hands into the air.

Her face turned bright red.

> **"HEY!"

> **"I KILL MORE PEOPLE THAN HER!"

> **"I CAN BE IN CHARGE!"**

The courtyard fell completely silent.

Lucy slowly turned toward her daughter.

A smile spread across her face.

She couldn't help herself.

She laughed.

Director Lucious covered his mouth to hide a chuckle.

Taiga buried her face in Mart's shoulder trying not to laugh.

Even Nightshade's expression softened.

Lucy finally composed herself.

> **"...For the record..."**

She placed a hand on Alice's shoulder.

> **"She's technically right."**

Alice stared in confusion.

Lucy smiled.

> **"Congratulations."**

> **"You're the new Head of Security."**

Alice stood speechless before giving a crisp salute.

> **"...I won't disappoint you."**

---

## **Closing Observation**

With introductions finally complete, the massive gates of Merryweather Manor slowly opened.

Warm light spilled across the stone path.

Endmin looked back at everyone with an embarrassed smile.

> **"...Sorry about the welcome."**

Director Lucious chuckled.

> **"I've certainly had worse receptions."**

The group laughed together as they stepped through the gates and into the manor.
