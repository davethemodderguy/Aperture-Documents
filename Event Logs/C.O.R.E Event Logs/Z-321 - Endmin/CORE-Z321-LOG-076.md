# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-076**

### **SECURITY INCIDENT – ASSAULT ON Z-321**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – Security / Personnel

---

## **SUMMARY**

On ██/██/20██, Z-321 (Endmin) was the victim of an attempted homicide by manual strangulation within APRT-70.

The attack was interrupted by Z-885 (Taiga), whose arrival caused the unidentified assailant to flee the scene.

An investigation by Security Division remains ongoing.

---

## **Incident Timeline**

**20:17**

Endmin was returning alone from Engineering after delivering prototype components to Neko.

Security footage confirms she entered a service corridor connecting Engineering to Residential.

**20:19**

Camera feeds from the corridor were disrupted for approximately eleven seconds.

The cause of the interference has not yet been determined.

When visual recording resumed, Endmin had been forced against a wall by an unidentified individual.

The attacker was attempting to manually strangle her.

---

## **Intervention**

At approximately **20:20**, Taiga entered the corridor while searching for Endmin.

According to her statement, she immediately recognized signs of a struggle.

Without hesitation, she shouted and charged toward the attacker.

Startled by the sudden intervention and approaching personnel alerted by Taiga's voice, the assailant abandoned the attack and escaped before Security could arrive.

A facility-wide search was initiated immediately.

At the time of this report, the suspect has not been identified.

---

## **Medical Report**

Medical Division documented the following injuries:

- Bruising around the neck.
    
- Minor airway trauma.
    
- Temporary loss of voice.
    
- Oxygen deprivation consistent with attempted strangulation.
    
- Psychological distress.
    

No permanent physical injury was identified.

Endmin remained under overnight observation before being released back to Residential.

---

## **Witness Statements**

### Taiga

> "I don't even remember thinking."

> "I just saw someone hurting her..."

> "...and ran."

When asked whether she had been afraid, Taiga quietly replied:

> "...Afterwards."

---

### Endmin

Several hours after the incident, her voice remained hoarse.

She managed only a few quiet words.

> "...She saved me."

Looking toward Taiga, she added,

> "...Thank you."

---

### Lucy

Upon learning what had happened, Lucy arrived at Medical almost immediately.

She embraced Endmin carefully, avoiding pressure on her neck.

Her expression remained calm.

Her voice did not.

> "...Someone laid a hand on my daughter."

She looked toward Director Lucious.

> "...Find them."

---

### Director Lucious

Director Lucious personally authorized the highest internal investigative priority.

> **"No resident of this facility should ever fear being attacked in their own home."**

> **"Find whoever did this."**

---

## **Behavioral Observation**

Following discharge from Medical, Endmin remained noticeably quieter than usual.

That evening, she stayed close to her roommates rather than spending time alone.

Behavioral Sciences noted this response as a normal reaction to a traumatic assault.

No pressure was placed on her to discuss the incident further.

---

## **Residential**

When Endmin returned to Room N-07, no one asked questions.

Taiga quietly made tea.

Mart brought an extra blanket.

Neko moved a chair closer so Endmin would not have to stand.

Miss Neko checked the door locks without saying a word.

Bao rested a reassuring hand on Endmin's shoulder.

Frost silently placed a warm compress nearby.

Star and Nightshade remained in the room until she fell asleep.

Aster climbed onto the bed and curled against Endmin's side.

Nobody tried to make conversation.

They simply stayed with her.

---

## **CURRENT STATUS**

Security Division continues its investigation into the unidentified assailant.

Additional patrols and surveillance have been assigned to residential corridors until the investigation concludes.

Endmin is expected to make a full physical recovery and continues to receive support from Medical and Behavioral Sciences.

---

### **Closing Observation**

Before leaving Medical that night, Taiga quietly apologized.

> **"...I'm sorry I wasn't there sooner."**

Endmin gently shook her head.

Her voice was barely above a whisper.

> **"...You came."**

She reached over and took Taiga's hand.

> **"...That's what matters."**

Taiga smiled through tears.

Neither of them said anything else as they walked back to Room N-07 together.
