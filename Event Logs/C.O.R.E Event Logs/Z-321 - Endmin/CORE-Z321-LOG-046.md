# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z321-LOG-046**

### **PERSONAL EQUIPMENT UPDATE – MOBILITY BOOTS**

**Classification:** LEVEL 2 – Equipment Evaluation

---

## **SUMMARY**

Following the successful restoration of her eyesight and full physical rehabilitation, Z-321 (Endmin) resumed modifying her personal equipment.

The most notable addition was discovered during a routine patrol.

Her boots...

...had wheels.

Not removable roller skates.

The boots themselves transformed into a high-performance skating system.

Engineering later confirmed Endmin had quietly designed the modifications herself, with Neko providing only manufacturing assistance.

---

## **Equipment Designation**

**Endmin Personal Mobility Boots**

**Developer:** Endmin

**Manufactured by:** Engineering Division (Neko)

---

## **Features**

### Retractable Roller Skates

At the press of a hidden switch, four reinforced skate wheels deploy from each boot.

Deployment Time:

**Less than one second.**

The mechanism is smooth enough that Endmin can activate it while already moving.

---

### Nekonium Skate Wheels

The wheels are constructed from a hardened Nekonium alloy.

Characteristics include:

- Extremely low rolling resistance.
    
- Exceptional durability.
    
- High-speed stability.
    
- Excellent grip on most Blacksite flooring.
    
- Resistant to debris and wear.
    

---

### Blade Conversion

Each wheel assembly can retract and expose a sharpened blade.

Primary uses include:

- Conventional skating.
    
- Ice skating.
    
- Emergency braking.
    
- Traversing damaged terrain.
    

Endmin jokingly refers to them as:

> "Plan B."

---

### Rear Booster

Integrated into the heel is a compact directional booster.

Capabilities include:

- Rapid acceleration.
    
- Short burst propulsion.
    
- Enhanced aerial movement.
    
- Increased momentum during gliding.
    

The booster is intentionally limited to short bursts to prevent uncontrolled acceleration within the Blacksite.

---

## **Performance**

With her restored vision and greatly improved balance, Endmin adapted to the skates almost immediately.

Recorded observations include:

- Faster corridor traversal.
    
- Greater maneuverability.
    
- Exceptional cornering.
    
- No recorded collisions.
    

Researchers noted that her combination of normal vision and Ether Sense allows her to predict movement with extraordinary precision, making skating almost effortless.

---

## **Recreational Race**

To evaluate the new equipment, Taiga challenged Endmin to a race across the designated recreation circuit.

Mart reluctantly agreed to participate.

**Participants**

- Endmin
    
- Taiga
    
- Mart
    

The race began normally.

For approximately four seconds.

Endmin quietly pressed the booster.

A brief blue-white flash appeared behind her boots.

She disappeared down the corridor.

Taiga stared.

> "...Hey!"

Mart laughed.

> "...She's gone."

Security cameras later recorded Endmin reaching the finish line well before either sibling.

She was calmly waiting with a bottle of water when they finally arrived.

### **Closing Observation**

Late one evening, Endmin skated quietly through the nearly empty Blacksite corridors.

The only sound was the soft hum of her wheels.

She passed a window overlooking the surrounding mountains and slowed to a stop.

Looking at her reflection, she smiled.

Round glasses.

White wings.

A rifle over one shoulder.

Roller skates beneath her boots.

She laughed quietly to herself.

> "...Mom's never going to believe this."

As if on cue, her communicator chimed.

It was Lucy.

> **Lucy:** "Endmin... why did Neko just send me a video of you outrunning Taiga?"

Endmin looked down the hallway.

Then smiled sheepishly.

> "...Long story."
