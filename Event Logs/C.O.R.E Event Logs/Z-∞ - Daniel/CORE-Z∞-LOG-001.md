Following Archangel Rose’s prior invitation, an unidentified male entity manifested at the Blacksite entrance without triggering any known detection or defense systems.

The entity presented as a human male in a formal suit. All personnel present reported an immediate and involuntary perception of “perfection” across all observable attributes, including posture, symmetry, and presence.

Upon visual confirmation, multiple high-level entities—including the Dawnbringers and Starladian Deities—instinctively knelt.

The entity addressed Director Lucious directly:

> “Rose told me a lot about you, the man of wonder. Hello, my name is Daniel.”

At this moment, Z-356 (C00lkid) forcibly opened a spatial breach into the lobby, accompanied by Operative 007n7.

Z-356 immediately made physical contact with the entity and stated:

> “DAD! It’s so nice to see you.”

This statement caused immediate confusion among present personnel.

Z-∞ clarified:

- He is the biological and existential father of Z-356
- Z-356’s presence within this reality was accidental, caused by interaction with an unspecified “console”

Z-∞ then questioned Z-356’s attachment to Operative 007n7.  
Z-356 responded:

> “He saved me from the bad guys that captured me!”

Director Lucious confirmed that said hostile actors had been neutralized, stating:

> “We got them under control. Or more specifically, C00lkid deleted them.”

Z-∞ produced a tablet-like device resembling Z-356’s C00lgui, differing in coloration (black interface with white outline).

Shortly after, Archangel Rose and the Abyssal Express crew arrived on-site. No hostility observed.
