# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-147**

### **THE RETURN OF AZURE**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE

---

## **I. NORMAL**

The change wasn't dramatic.

There was no announcement.

No ceremony.

No celebration.

It simply...

Started feeling normal again.

Maintenance crews no longer found themselves unexpectedly pulled away every time Neko appeared.

Lunch breaks overlapped naturally.

Repairs were shared.

People had time to stop and talk.

The Blacksite returned to the rhythm it had before everything went wrong.

---

## **II. MAINTENANCE**

An engineer was repairing a coolant valve when he heard small footsteps behind him.

---

### Neko

> "Need another wrench?"

---

The engineer looked over.

Then smiled.

---

### Engineer

> "...Actually?"

> "Yeah."

---

Neko handed him the correct wrench without even looking.

---

### Engineer

> "...Thanks."

---

Instead of immediately walking away...

Neko stayed.

They spent the next twenty minutes fixing the valve together.

No emergency interrupted them.

No alarms called either of them elsewhere.

Just two maintenance workers quietly doing their job.

When they finished—

---

### Engineer

> "...Want to grab lunch?"

---

Neko blinked.

---

### Neko

> "...Sure."

---

No hesitation.

No doubt.

Just...

"Sure."

---

## **III. THE MAINTENANCE JOKE RETURNS**

A week later.

Someone quietly "kidnapped" Neko again.

An engineer simply picked him up from a hallway.

---

### Neko

> "...Again?"

---

### Engineer

> "Maintenance requires emotional support."

---

### Neko

> "...That's not a maintenance procedure."

---

### Engineer

> "It is now."

---

Neko sighed dramatically.

---

### Neko

> "...At least let me bring my toolbox."

---

Laughter echoed down the hallway.

The unofficial joke had returned.

And this time...

Neko laughed too.

---

## **IV. MISS NEKO**

Miss Neko rounded a corner just in time to see two technicians carrying Neko away.

One in a piggyback.

The other carrying his toolbox.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Where are you taking my lil bro?"

---

### Engineer

> "Borrowing him."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You filling out the paperwork this time?"

---

### Engineer

> "...There's paperwork?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "No."

---

She smiled.

---

### Miss Neko

> "Have fun."

---

She simply kept walking.

She didn't feel the need to follow anymore.

She knew he'd be fine.

---

## **V. STAR AND NIGHTSHADE**

That evening, back in the shared room.

Star was sitting on the floor with a deck of cards.

---

### Star

> "Nekoooo!"

---

### Neko

> "Hm?"

---

### Star

> "Game night!"

---

Neko looked toward Nightshade.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Don't look at me."

---

A beat.

---

### Nightshade

> "...I'm already playing."

---

Neko smiled.

A real one.

He sat down beside them.

---

### Neko

> "...You're going to lose."

---

### Star

> "Confident today?"

---

### Neko

> "Very."

---

### Nightshade

> "...That's more like it."

---

## **VI. LUCY**

Lucy found him during lunch.

---

### Lucy

> "...Mind if I sit here?"

---

Neko looked up from his sandwich.

---

### Neko

> "...You don't have to ask."

---

She smiled.

---

### Lucy

> "...Good."

---

She sat beside him.

No awkward silence.

No forced conversation.

Just...

Lunch.

Exactly the way friends should be.

---

## **VII. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Lucious happened to pass through Engineering later that afternoon.

He stopped.

Not because anything unusual was happening.

But because...

Something ordinary was.

Neko was sitting on top of a workbench while three engineers debated how to reroute a coolant line.

He wasn't working alone.

He wasn't hiding.

He wasn't trying to disappear.

He was arguing—politely—about pipe diameters.

---

### Neko

> "...If we increase the diameter here, pressure loss drops."

---

### Engineer

> "...That's actually..."

---

He checked the calculations.

---

### Engineer

> "...You're right."

---

Lucious quietly smiled to himself.

Then continued walking.

He didn't interrupt.

He didn't need to.

---

## **VIII. THE EYES**

Medical noticed it first.

The soft azure glow had returned.

Not suddenly.

Gradually.

Day by day.

Until it looked exactly as it had before.

Bright.

Warm.

Alive.

The glow no longer dimmed when people approached.

Instead...

It seemed brightest during conversations.

Especially when someone laughed.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "Eye luminescence has returned to baseline."

---

### Assistant

> "...So he's recovered?"

---

The researcher looked through the observation window.

Neko was currently receiving enthusiastic head pats from Maintenance while pretending to complain about it.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...Physically?"

> "Yes."

---

He smiled faintly.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...And I think..."

> "...He's finally remembering something he forgot."

---

## **IX. THE NOTEBOOK**

One evening, Miss Neko noticed Neko writing again.

She peeked over his shoulder.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Journal?"

---

### Neko

> "Mhm."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Can I read?"

---

Neko thought for a moment.

Then nodded.

She opened to the newest page.

---

> **How was today?**

> Fixed a coolant pump.

> Lost three card games to Nightshade.

> Star still cheats.

> Lucy brought sandwiches.

> Maintenance kidnapped me twice.

> Got head pats.

> It was fun.

At the bottom of the page...

Where everyone expected to see the old reminder...

There was something different.

---

> **Reminder.**

> **People really did want to spend time with me.**

Miss Neko read the line twice.

Then quietly hugged Neko from behind.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...See?"

---

Neko leaned back against her.

---

### Neko

> "...Yeah."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Told you."

---

He smiled.

Not because he was trying to be polite.

Not because he thought he should.

But because...

For the first time in a long while...

He believed it.

---

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

With the scheduling issues resolved and normal routines restored, personnel interactions with Neko returned naturally. Maintenance resumed its lighthearted tradition of "borrowing" him for jobs, Star and Nightshade regularly included him in activities after work, Lucy made time to share meals with him, and Miss Neko no longer felt the need to constantly watch over her younger brother.

Medical documented that the azure glow in Neko's eyes had fully returned to its previous intensity. As before, the glow appeared to correlate with his emotional well-being rather than his physical condition.

The welfare case was officially closed.

The final note entered into the archive read:

> **"The investigation uncovered many technical faults, but the most important repair was not made with tools. It was made by giving someone time, company, and enough ordinary days to believe they belonged again."**
