# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-140**

### **ACQUIRED TASTES**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE / ACTIVE INVESTIGATION

---

## **I. INITIAL OBSERVATION**

The first person to notice was a cafeteria worker.

Neko had taken a tray as usual.

A sandwich.

Warm soup.

A glass of water.

And...

Carrots.

Lots of carrots.

The worker blinked.

---

### Cafeteria Staff

> "...Neko?"

---

### Neko

> "Hm?"

---

### Cafeteria Staff

> "...You hate carrots."

---

Neko looked down at the tray.

---

### Neko

> "...Yeah."

---

### Cafeteria Staff

> "...Then... why?"

---

Neko gave a small shrug.

---

### Neko

> "...I should probably get used to them."

---

The answer sounded simple.

Too simple.

---

## **II. THE FIRST BITE**

He sat alone.

Picked up one carrot slice.

Stared at it.

For several seconds.

Then ate it.

His ears twitched immediately.

His face scrunched up for just a moment.

Anyone who knew Neko could tell he disliked every second of it.

But...

He reached for another.

Then another.

Until the bowl was empty.

---

## **III. IT DIDN'T STOP THERE**

Within days...

People noticed a pattern.

Carrots.

Broccoli.

Brussels sprouts.

Bitter vegetables.

Anything he had spent months avoiding.

Anything that usually made him quietly complain.

Anything he would normally trade away for a sandwich.

Now...

He simply ate it.

Without complaint.

Without bargaining.

Without making faces.

He never claimed to enjoy it.

He simply finished everything on his tray.

---

### Chef

> "...Does it taste better now?"

---

Neko smiled faintly.

---

### Neko

> "...No."

---

### Chef

> "...Then why keep eating it?"

---

A pause.

---

### Neko

> "...Because I don't think I should keep choosing only things I like."

---

The chef didn't know how to answer.

---

## **IV. MISS NEKO**

Lunch.

Miss Neko sat beside him.

She glanced at his tray.

Broccoli.

Carrots.

Peas.

Every food she knew he disliked.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro."

---

### Neko

> "Yeah?"

---

She pointed with her fork.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Since when?"

---

Neko looked at the vegetables.

---

### Neko

> "...Recently."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You used to hide broccoli under mashed potatoes."

---

A tiny smile.

---

### Neko

> "...Didn't work very well."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...No."

---

She looked directly at him.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Why are you forcing yourself?"

---

He quietly finished chewing.

Drank some water.

Then answered.

---

### Neko

> "...Because..."

---

He searched for the words.

---

### Neko

> "...Sometimes..."

> "...You don't get to choose what you like."

---

Silence.

---

### Neko

> "...So I figured I should stop expecting to."

---

Miss Neko lowered her fork.

She suddenly wasn't hungry anymore.

---

## **V. LUCY**

Lucy arrived late to lunch.

She immediately noticed Neko's tray.

---

### Lucy

> "...Broccoli?"

---

Neko nodded.

---

### Lucy

> "...You always said it tasted like wet trees."

---

A tiny chuckle escaped him.

The first genuine one she'd heard in days.

---

### Neko

> "...It still does."

---

Lucy smiled instinctively.

Then realized...

He was still eating it.

---

### Lucy

> "...You don't have to."

---

Neko looked at her.

His ordinary blue eyes remained calm.

---

### Neko

> "...I know."

---

Another bite.

---

### Neko

> "...I'm just learning."

---

### Lucy

> "...Learning what?"

---

He looked back down at the tray.

---

### Neko

> "...How not to mind."

---

Lucy couldn't finish her lunch.

---

## **VI. DISAPPEARING**

Finding Neko became even harder.

Not because he avoided work.

Because he had become...

Exceptionally efficient.

Jobs disappeared from the maintenance queue before anyone accepted them.

Equipment failures were repaired almost immediately.

Broken valves.

Damaged wiring.

Faulty conveyors.

Someone would arrive expecting to help.

Only to find another small handwritten note.

> **"Done."**

---

The notes became shorter.

Eventually...

Only a single check mark remained.

✓

Nothing else.

---

### Maintenance Supervisor

> "...Did anyone actually see him today?"

---

Silence.

---

One intern slowly raised a hand.

---

### Intern

> "...I think I did..."

---

### Supervisor

> "...Think?"

---

### Intern

> "...He waved..."

---

### Supervisor

> "...And then?"

---

### Intern

> "...I turned around for maybe five seconds."

---

Another pause.

---

### Intern

> "...He was gone."

---

## **VII. THE INVESTIGATION**

Security continued reviewing every report.

Camera footage.

Maintenance logs.

Radio traffic.

Door access records.

Duty schedules.

Every emergency call.

Every delayed lunch.

Every interrupted meeting.

Nothing.

No altered records.

No forged orders.

No suspicious personnel movements.

Every interruption had a legitimate operational cause.

Frustratingly legitimate.

---

### Security Analyst

> "...This makes no sense."

---

### Chief of Security

> "...Facts."

---

### Analyst

> "...The facts contradict each other."

---

The Chief quietly nodded.

He disliked mysteries that behaved like ordinary paperwork.

---

## **VIII. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Lucious reviewed the latest welfare report.

One line caught his attention.

> **Subject has begun deliberately consuming foods he has historically expressed strong dislike for.**

He read the sentence again.

Then quietly asked the Medical Researcher.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Could this be a medical symptom?"

---

The researcher shook their head.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...No."

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Then why?"

---

The researcher remained silent.

Finally...

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...I think..."

---

A pause.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...He's teaching himself not to expect comfort."

---

Lucious slowly closed the report.

He didn't respond.

Because he feared that assessment might be correct.

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Medical evaluations remained unchanged.

Neko's metabolism continued recovering as expected.

His physical condition was stable.

His emotional condition, however, continued to shift in subtle ways.

Personnel noted that he increasingly accepted discomfort without complaint—whether in food, workload, or solitude. He no longer attempted to replace unpleasant experiences with enjoyable ones, instead quietly enduring them as though they were simply another part of the day.

Security's investigation into the repeated scheduling anomalies remained active.

Despite exhaustive review, no evidence of deliberate interference had been found.

The unusual pattern persisted.

No culprit had been identified.

No anomaly had been explained.

The final note added to the case file was brief:

> **"Neko once filled his free time by inventing impossible things because he expected tomorrow to be interesting. Now he spends that same time teaching himself to expect less."**
