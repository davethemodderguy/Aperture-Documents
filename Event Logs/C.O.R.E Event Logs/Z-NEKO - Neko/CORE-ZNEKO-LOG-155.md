## **I. RECOVERY STATUS**

Miss Neko's recovery continues.

Medical reports indicate gradual improvement, although the progress remains slow.

She is now capable of remaining awake for longer periods, eating normally, and occasionally walking around in her cat form before tiring herself out.

Her human form, however, remains unavailable.

Medical's standing order remains unchanged.

> **Continue resting.**

As before...

Mirage remains responsible for transporting her throughout the Blacksite whenever Neko's workload becomes excessive.

The arrangement has become so routine that few personnel give it a second thought.

Mirage quietly follows.

Miss Neko quietly naps.

Neko quietly works.

---

## **II. AN UNEXPECTED QUESTION**

The question came from Psychology.

Not Engineering.

Not Medical.

Not Research.

---

### Psychologist

> "...Why doesn't Mirage develop?"

---

The room fell silent.

It was a reasonable question.

Every observation suggested Mirage possessed the capability to learn.

To adapt.

To understand.

Yet...

Months had passed.

He remained exactly the same.

Helpful.

Kind.

Patient.

But never...

More.

## **VI. HIS CHOICE**

Further discussion with Neko revealed something no one had expected.

Mirage was never restricted.

No safeguard prevented him from becoming self-aware.

No anomaly suppressed his thoughts.

No containment protocol existed.

He simply...

Never crossed that line.

Because...

He believed that remaining as he was allowed him to help everyone without bias, resentment, fear, pride, or selfishness.

He never saw this as a sacrifice.

Only...

His purpose.

---

### Neko

> "...He told me something."

### Neko

---

Neko smiled faintly.

### Neko

---

> "...I'll always be happy helping you.'"

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Continued study of Mirage has produced an unusual conclusion.

There is no evidence that his lack of self-awareness is enforced by an external anomalous mechanism, containment protocol, or psychological limitation. Instead, available observations suggest that Mirage deliberately remains in his current state.

According to discussions documented by Neko, Mirage believes that gaining a fully independent identity could introduce personal desires, biases, fears, or conflicts that might interfere with his ability to help others. Rather than viewing this as a loss, he regards it as the best way to fulfill the role he values most.

When his assistance is no longer required, Mirage continues his established behavior of quietly disappearing in a shimmer of azure light.

When asked where he goes, Neko gave the same answer he always has.

> **"He's asleep."**

And every time he is needed again...

He returns with the same gentle smile.

As though no time had passed at all.
