# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

### **WEEK THREE – THE PERMAFROST INCIDENT**

### **Classification:** INTERNAL – MEDICAL / FACILITY NOTICE

## **I. THREE WEEKS**

Against everyone's expectations...

Neko was still sick.

The fever had eased compared to the first week, but it stubbornly refused to disappear completely.

Medical Division had stopped asking when he would recover.

Instead, they simply wrote:

> **"Recovery progressing... eventually."**

---

One thing, however, had not changed.

---

### Medical Officer

> "You're supposed to rest."

---

### Neko

> "...I am."

---

The medical officer looked over the engineer's shoulder.

Neko was simultaneously reviewing blueprints, answering radio calls, and redesigning a coolant valve.

---

### Medical Officer

> "...That is not rest."

---

### Neko

> "...It's sitting down."

---

No one had a counterargument.

---

## **II. THE DISCOVERY**

The incident happened during lunch.

Star had brought everyone drinks.

Water.

Tea.

Juice.

And...

One ice-cold lemonade.

---

### Star

> "Here you go!"

---

Without thinking, Neko took a sip.

---

Miss Neko looked over.

Her ears immediately stood upright.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro."

---

Neko blinked.

---

### Neko

> "...Hm?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Was that cold?"

---

Neko looked at the glass.

Then back at her.

---

### Neko

> "...Very."

---

Miss Neko slowly covered her face.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Oh no."

---

### Star

> "...What?"

---

Neko sniffled once.

---

Twice.

---

### Neko

> "...I think I'm going to—"

---

**ACHOO!!**

---

Silence.

---

Then...

A thick wave of white frost erupted from the sneeze, racing across the floor like water before instantly freezing solid.

Within seconds...

The hallway was covered.

Walls.

Tables.

Toolboxes.

Door frames.

Everything.

Encased beneath approximately **20 millimeters of solid permafrost**.

---

The room temperature dropped noticeably.

---

### Star

> "...Oh."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...I knew it."

---

For the next four hours...

Nobody knew what had happened.

---

### Maintenance Engineer

> "Coolant pipe burst?"

---

### Researcher

> "Cryogenic containment failure?"

---

### Security

---

Every pipe was inspected.

Every cooling unit was tested.

Nothing.

---

Neko quietly sat in Medical.

Looking increasingly guilty.

---

### Medical Officer

---

### Neko

> "...Maybe."

---

### Medical Officer

> "...What happened?"

---

Neko looked at the floor.

---

### Neko

> "...I sneezed."

---

Silence.

---

### Medical Officer

> "...You what?"

---

### Neko

> "...I drank something really cold..."

---

Another pause.

---

### Neko

> "...Then I sneezed."

---

### Medical Officer

> "...The permafrost?"

---

### Neko

> "...Yeah."

---

The medical officer slowly closed the clipboard.

---

### Medical Officer

> "...I'm going to need you to repeat that."

---

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Three weeks into his illness, Neko remained one of the most stubborn patients Medical Division had ever encountered.

He refused to stop contributing.

He refused to stay in bed all day.

He refused to let a fever keep him from helping Engineering.

The Blacksite eventually stopped trying to make him rest completely.

Instead...

Everyone quietly worked around him, ensuring he stayed warm, hydrated, and as comfortable as possible.

The only universally enforced rule was surprisingly simple:

> **Never give Neko an ice-cold drink while he's sick.**

No one wanted to spend another two hours waiting for a hallway-sized sheet of permafrost to thaw.
