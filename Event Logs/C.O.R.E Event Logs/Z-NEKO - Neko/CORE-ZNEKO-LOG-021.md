# **I. OVERVIEW**

Neko returned.

---

Five times.

---

Exactly five.

---

After that.

---

He stopped.

---

Again.

---

Not because anyone told him to.

---

Not because he was forced to.

---

Not because he was angry.

---

Simply because he reached a conclusion.

---

One that nobody realized he had made.

---

# **II. THE VISITS**

The first visit brought relief.

---

The second brought optimism.

---

The third brought hope.

---

The fourth brought excitement.

---

The fifth brought realization.

---

Not for everyone else.

---

For Neko.

---

# **III. OBSERVATIONS**

During all five visits.

---

Neko did very little.

---

He didn't work.

---

He didn't invent.

---

He didn't help Engineering.

---

He didn't help Research.

---

He didn't assist Medical.

---

He didn't visit Containment.

---

Most of the time.

---

He simply sat somewhere.

---

Observation Deck.

---

Cafeteria.

---

Courtyard.

---

Hallway.

---

Sometimes with the bird.

---

Sometimes with Niko.

---

Sometimes alone.

---

Yet every time.

---

Something happened.

---

Morale improved.

---

# **IV. THE EFFECT**

Researchers became less stressed.

---

Engineers argued less.

---

Security personnel smiled more.

---

Even the cafeteria became louder.

---

Not because Neko was doing anything.

---

But because he was there.

---

### Internal Observation

---

Nobody found that surprising.

---

Until Neko noticed it himself.

---

# **V. THE REALIZATION**

The realization occurred during Visit Five.

---

A group of researchers were discussing a difficult materials problem.

---

One that had delayed a project for almost a week.

---

Neko happened to walk by.

---

The room visibly relaxed.

---

People smiled.

---

Someone waved.

---

The conversation became easier.

---

The tension disappeared.

---

The problem remained unsolved.

---

Yet everyone looked happier.

---

Neko noticed.

---

And for a long time.

---

He simply watched.

---

### Final Observation

Neko believed he no longer helped.

He believed his presence merely made people comfortable.

What he failed to notice was that comfort had never been a small thing.

The Blacksite did not miss him because he could solve impossible equations.

It did not miss him because he could invent materials that broke reality.

It did not miss him because he could solve a week's worth of problems in two minutes.

Those things were useful.

But they were not what people remembered first.

They remembered the scientist who showed up uninvited, asked strange questions, doodled in reports, shared sandwiches with a bird, and somehow made difficult days feel lighter.

And now that he was gone again,

everyone was finally learning how much work that had been doing. 💙🐱🥽🐦
