## **I. MEDICAL ANNOUNCEMENT**

After months of treatment, observation, rehabilitation, and gradual improvement...

Medical Division has officially declared Miss Neko fully recovered.

Comprehensive examinations confirmed:

- Stable vital signs.
    
- Complete restoration of stamina.
    
- No residual metabolic abnormalities.
    
- Human form may now be maintained indefinitely.
    
- Cat form may now be entered and exited voluntarily without medical concern.
    

At 09:00 Blacksite Time, the recovery order was signed.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "Recovery complete."

---

Silence filled the room.

Then...

Applause.

---

## **II. THE FIRST FULL TRANSFORMATION**

Miss Neko stepped into the center of the Medical Wing.

She smiled.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Ready?"

---

A dense cloud of white smoke erupted around her.

Several seconds later...

The smoke dispersed.

Miss Neko stood there.

Human.

Healthy.

Standing confidently without the slightest hint of fatigue.

She stretched her arms above her head.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...I don't feel tired."

---

Medical instruments confirmed it.

Ten minutes.

Twenty.

Thirty.

An hour.

No instability.

No weakening.

No forced transformation.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...Welcome back."

---

Neko had been waiting outside Medical.

The moment the doors opened...

Miss Neko walked out under her own strength.

No backpack.

No assistance.

No medical escort.

Just...

Walking.

Neko stared for a moment.

Then smiled wider than anyone had seen in months.

---

### Neko

> "...Big sis."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro."

---

Miss Neko reached over and ruffled his messy hair.

Then gently rubbed one of his cat ears.

---

### Neko

> "...Mrrp..."

---

He immediately began purring.

Miss Neko laughed.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Missed that."

---

### Neko

> "...You cheated."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Maybe."

---

## **IV. MIRAGE**

Mirage quietly approached.

Still carrying the now-familiar transparent recovery backpack.

He stopped in front of Miss Neko.

Looked at the backpack.

Then at her.

---

### Mirage

> "...You don't need this anymore."

---

Miss Neko smiled softly.

She took the backpack from his hands.

Looked at it for a long moment.

Running her fingers across the transparent shell.

Remembering countless naps.

Countless conversations.

Countless quiet days while Mirage carried her through the Blacksite.

She closed the hatch one final time.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Thank you."

---

Mirage tilted his head.

---

### Mirage

> "...For what?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...For carrying me."

---

A brief pause.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Every single day."

---

Mirage smiled.

That same gentle smile.

---

### Mirage

> "...I liked helping."

---

She stepped forward.

And hugged him.

Mirage hesitated for just a fraction of a second.

Then gently returned the hug.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You're family."

---

### Mirage

> "...Family."

---

The word lingered in the air.

Simple.

Quiet.

Warm.

---

## **V. THE LAST RIDE**

Before returning the backpack to storage...

Miss Neko climbed inside one last time.

Curled up on the little pillow.

Looked through the transparent shell at Neko and Mirage.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...One last ride?"

---

Neko laughed.

---

### Neko

> "...Seriously?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Please?"

---

Mirage immediately put the backpack on.

---

### Mirage

> "...Okay."

---

Together, the three walked a slow lap around Engineering.

Past the coolant lines.

Past the reactor hall.

Past the workshops.

Engineers waved as they passed.

Some smiled knowingly.

Others simply nodded.

No words were necessary.

Everyone understood what this meant.

When the lap was complete...

Mirage knelt.

Miss Neko stepped out.

Folded the tiny blanket.

Fluffed the little pillow.

Closed the hatch.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Goodbye, little bed."

---

## **VI. ENGINEERING**

The backpack was never discarded.

Instead...

It was cleaned.

Polished.

And placed inside Engineering's display cabinet.

A small plaque beneath it read:

> **Portable Feline Recovery Containment Pack**  
> _"Miss Neko's Bed."_  
> _Used throughout Miss Neko's recovery. Built by Neko. Carried by Neko and Mirage._

Engineers passing by occasionally smiled at the display.

It reminded them that recovery could be slow...

But it was possible.

## **VIII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Medical Division has officially concluded Miss Neko's recovery program. All temporary restrictions have been lifted, and she has returned to active duty with full medical clearance.

Although no longer required for recovery, the Portable Feline Recovery Containment Pack has been preserved as a piece of Engineering history, commemorating the months during which Neko and Mirage faithfully carried Miss Neko through the Blacksite.

Mirage has resumed his normal role assisting Neko with engineering tasks, occasionally glancing at the display cabinet as he passes. Miss Neko has also returned to her position, though she now makes a point of checking in on both Neko and Mirage during the workday.

A final handwritten note was added to the completed medical file by Miss Neko:

> **"Recovery wasn't something I did alone. Lil bro carried my worries. Mirage carried my bed. Everyone else carried my hope. Now it's my turn to walk beside all of them again."**
