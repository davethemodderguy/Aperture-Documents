# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-141**

### **BLACK IRISES**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE / ACTIVE INVESTIGATION

---

## **I. INCIDENT REPORT**

At approximately 08:17 Blacksite Time, multiple personnel independently submitted the same observation.

Neko's eyes no longer appeared azure.

They no longer appeared blue.

Behind the familiar round glasses...

His irises had become completely black.

Not glowing.

Not reflective.

Simply black.

Medical personnel were notified immediately.

---

## **II. MEDICAL EVALUATION**

Neko cooperated fully with every examination.

Pupil response.

Vision.

Heart rate.

Neurological activity.

Blood chemistry.

Metabolic recovery.

Every measurable value remained within expected ranges.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "Can you still see normally?"

---

### Neko

> "Yeah."

---

### Researcher

> "Any pain?"

---

### Neko

> "No."

---

### Researcher

> "Dizziness?"

---

### Neko

> "No."

---

### Researcher

> "...How do you feel?"

---

Neko thought for several seconds.

---

### Neko

> "...Normal."

---

The researcher made no immediate comment.

---

## **III. A DIFFERENT KIND OF CHANGE**

Throughout the day, staff began noticing something far more concerning than the color of his eyes.

Nothing seemed to excite him anymore.

A freshly repaired machine that would once have earned an enthusiastic explanation...

Received only a quiet checkmark on the maintenance log.

A joke from Maintenance...

No reaction.

Star enthusiastically showed him a new dessert she had baked.

---

### Star

> "I made extra!"

---

Neko accepted a small slice.

Took a bite.

Swallowed.

---

### Star

> "...Well?"

---

### Neko

> "...It's good."

---

He smiled politely.

The smile reached his mouth.

Not his eyes.

Star quietly lowered her plate.

---

She folded her arms.

---

### Neko

> "...I don't know."

---

That answer frightened her more than any other could have.

---

## **V. LUCY**

Lucy found him in the familiar maintenance alcove.

He wasn't reading.

He wasn't building.

He wasn't even looking at anything.

He was simply sitting there.

Listening to the ventilation system.

---

### Lucy

> "...Can I sit here?"

---

Neko nodded.

---

Several minutes passed.

Neither spoke.

Finally—

---

### Lucy

> "...I still haven't forgiven myself."

---

Neko quietly looked toward her.

---

### Neko

> "...You don't have to carry that forever."

---

Lucy smiled weakly.

---

### Lucy

> "...Then why are you carrying everything by yourself?"

---

Neko didn't answer.

Not because he refused.

He simply seemed unable to find the words.

---

## **VI. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

A confidential meeting was convened.

Present:

- Director Lucious
    
- Miss Neko
    
- Niko
    
- Lucy
    
- Lead Medical Researcher
    
- Chief of Security
    

Medical presented its findings first.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "Physically, he's recovering."

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Mentally?"

---

The researcher hesitated.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "We can document behavior."

> "We cannot directly measure what he is feeling."

---

Lucious nodded once.

---

### Director Lucious

> "Then document behavior."

---

The report listed observable changes only:

- Markedly reduced emotional expression.
    
- Minimal spontaneous conversation.
    
- Little interest in hobbies that previously occupied him.
    
- Consistent completion of assigned work.
    
- No aggression.
    
- No disciplinary concerns.
    

No conclusion about his internal emotional state was entered, because it could not be verified.

---

## **VII. THE INVESTIGATION**

Security continued examining the repeated scheduling anomalies.

Camera recordings.

Door logs.

Maintenance requests.

Communications.

Authorization histories.

Nothing indicated forged records.

Nothing identified a person intentionally orchestrating events.

The pattern remained statistically unusual.

The explanation remained unknown.

---

### Security Analyst

> "...Either this is an extraordinary series of coincidences..."

---

He stopped speaking.

---

### Chief of Security

> "...Or there's a factor we haven't discovered."

---

### Analyst

> "...Can we prove either?"

---

The Chief slowly closed the case file.

---

### Chief of Security

> "...Not yet."

---

The investigation remained active.

No suspect had been identified.

No evidence supported assigning blame to any individual.

---

## **VIII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Neko's black irises became the subject of quiet discussion throughout the Blacksite.

Medical could confirm the change in appearance.

They could not confirm what it meant.

Likewise, staff increasingly described him as emotionally distant, but no instrument could determine whether he had "lost" his emotions or was simply expressing them differently after weeks of isolation and disappointment.

The official record therefore remained cautious:

> **"Personnel have observed a significant reduction in Neko's outward emotional expression. While many interpret this as emotional withdrawal, the precise cause cannot presently be determined."**

Meanwhile, Security's investigation continued.

Whoever—or whatever—was responsible for the recurring pattern of interrupted opportunities, if there was a responsible party at all, remained unidentified.

One final remark was appended by Director Lucious before the meeting adjourned:

> **"Speculation solves nothing. We continue gathering facts. At the same time, we continue making opportunities to reach him. If there's a way back, we won't stop looking for it."**
