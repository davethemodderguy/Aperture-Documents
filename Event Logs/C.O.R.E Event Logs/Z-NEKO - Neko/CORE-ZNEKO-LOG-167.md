# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-167

### **MISS NEKO'S SHIFT**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE

---

## **I. SHIFT CHANGE**

For the first time in several weeks...

Neko and Mirage found themselves buried under an unusually heavy engineering workload.

A major coolant network overhaul required both of them to work continuously across multiple sectors.

Neither had much time for breaks.

Oracle noticed almost immediately.

She patiently waited beside Neko for nearly ten minutes.

He was halfway inside a maintenance conduit.

---

### Neko

> "...Sorry, Oracle."

---

He reached out and gave her a gentle scratch under the chin.

---

### Neko

> "...I'm really busy today."

---

Oracle blinked.

Then looked toward Miss Neko.

Who had just finished organizing a shipment nearby.

---

## **II. A NEW COMPANION**

Miss Neko crouched down.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...What?"

---

Oracle walked over.

Rubbed against her leg.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Your boys are working, huh?"

---

> "Mew."

---

Miss Neko smiled.

Picked Oracle up.

Supporting her properly beneath her chest and hind legs.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Guess it's just us today."

---

Oracle answered with a content little chirp.

---

## **III. MORNING PATROL**

Unlike Neko...

Miss Neko didn't have maintenance tickets.

She simply carried Oracle around the Blacksite while completing lighter administrative work.

Oracle seemed perfectly happy.

She spent most of the morning sitting comfortably in Miss Neko's arms.

Occasionally watching people pass by.

Occasionally watching for danger.

Mostly...

Watching Miss Neko.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...I'm not nearly as interesting as Lil bro."

---

Oracle gently bumped her chin.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Okay, maybe a little."

---

## **IV. STAR JOINS IN**

Star rounded the corner.

Immediately spotted Oracle.

---

### Star

> "...ORACLE!"

---

Oracle looked up.

---

> "...Mew!"

---

Miss Neko smiled.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...She's keeping me company today."

---

### Star

> "...Can I borrow her?"

---

Oracle looked at Star.

Then tucked herself closer against Miss Neko.

---

### Star

> "...Rejected..."

---

Miss Neko couldn't help laughing.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Looks like she's chosen."

---

## **V. LUNCH**

During lunch...

Miss Neko sat beneath one of Engineering's large observation windows.

Oracle climbed onto her lap.

Curled into a fluffy circle.

And immediately fell asleep.

Miss Neko absentmindedly stroked behind her ears.

Very slowly.

Very gently.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You're lighter than Lil bro."

---

Oracle twitched one ear.

Still asleep.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Much less likely to nibble me, too."

---

From across the room...

Neko looked up from a blueprint.

---

### Neko

> "...I heard that."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Good."

---

Mirage looked over without lifting his eyes from a maintenance manual.

---

### Mirage

> "...Accurate."

---

Neko sighed dramatically.

---

## **VI. THE INSPECTION**

Oracle suddenly opened her eyes.

She hopped down from Miss Neko's lap.

Walked a short distance into the hallway.

Stopped.

Looked around carefully.

Miss Neko immediately followed.

Oracle sniffed a loose toolbox.

Pawed at it once.

A nearby engineer realized one of the wheels hadn't been locked.

He secured it.

Oracle looked satisfied.

She calmly walked back.

Jumped onto Miss Neko's shoulder.

---

### Engineer

> "...False alarm?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...No."

---

She nodded toward the toolbox.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...She probably prevented someone from tripping over it later."

---

The engineer smiled.

---

### Engineer

> "...Good catch."

---

Oracle purred proudly.

---

## **VII. END OF SHIFT**

Late that afternoon...

Neko and Mirage finally finished the coolant overhaul.

Both looked exhausted.

Covered in dust.

Grease.

And coolant residue.

Miss Neko was waiting outside Engineering.

Oracle perched comfortably on her shoulder.

The moment Oracle saw Neko...

She leapt gracefully into his waiting arms.

---

### Neko

> "...Missed me?"

---

Oracle rubbed against his cheek.

Purring loudly.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...She behaved."

---

### Mirage

> "...I expected that."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...She also inspected three hallways..."

---

"...Stopped one unsecured toolbox..."

---

"...And rejected Star."

---

### Star

> "...She did!"

---

Oracle looked entirely unapologetic.

---

## **VIII. EVENING**

As the four caretakers gathered for dinner...

Oracle made her final decision for the day.

She climbed into Miss Neko's lap.

Looked at Neko.

Then at Mirage.

Then back at Miss Neko.

Curled into a tiny ball.

And fell asleep.

Miss Neko smiled.

Carefully resting one hand over the sleeping kitten.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Looks like tonight's my turn."

---

Neko smiled.

---

### Neko

> "...She chose well."

---

Mirage nodded.

---

### Mirage

> "...She always does."

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Despite Neko and Mirage being occupied for the majority of the day, Oracle adapted without issue, spending her time alongside Miss Neko as she completed lighter duties around the Blacksite.

Researchers note that Oracle appears equally comfortable with all four of her designated caretakers, readily shifting between them depending on circumstance. While Neko remains her preferred playmate and Mirage her favorite partner for quiet games, Miss Neko has become her preferred source of comfort during slower days.

A handwritten note was later attached by Miss Neko:

> **"Lil bro fixes the Blacksite. Mirage helps him keep it running. On days they're too busy to play... Oracle and I keep each other company until they come back."**
