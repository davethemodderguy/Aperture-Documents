# **VIII. RESEARCH CONCLUSION**

Following several harmless observations:

- Neko's hair fluoresces under ultraviolet lighting.
    
- His eyes exhibit a soft bioluminescent glow beneath UV exposure.
    
- Despite his bangs completely obscuring his eyes, his vision remains completely unaffected.
    
- The mechanism behind this phenomenon remains unknown.
    

Repeated requests to examine his eyes more closely have all been politely declined.

# **IX. PROTOCOL UPDATE**

The following notice has been added to the Maintenance Sector handbook.

---

## **NOTICE REGARDING NEKO**

When UV disinfection systems are active:

- Do not be alarmed if Neko's hair begins glowing.
    
- Do not be alarmed if light is visible beneath his bangs.
    
- He can see perfectly well.
    
- This is considered normal.
    

### Additionally:

**Do not attempt to move, separate, brush aside, or otherwise lift Neko's bangs without his explicit permission.**

This is a personal boundary and is to be respected by all personnel.
