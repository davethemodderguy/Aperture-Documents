## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Snowy's archived journals confirmed an extraordinary fact:

- He experienced the same illness.
    
- He reached the same critical state.
    
- His eyes darkened in the same way.
    
- He entered the same unexplained sleep.
    

And yet...

He eventually wrote three simple words.

> **"I woke up."**

From that moment onward, the Blacksite officially reclassified the case.

No longer was it solely a post-incident investigation.

It became **Operation: Wake the Sleeping**, dedicated to uncovering the missing piece of Snowy's recovery and determining whether it could still offer answers.

Because if Snowy had once returned from the edge...

Then perhaps the story was not yet over.
