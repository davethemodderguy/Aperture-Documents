# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-190**

### **PROJECT FILE — "NEKO'S CLAWS"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – ENGINEERING / ARMORY

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Personnel have long recognized a dangerous pattern.

Whenever Neko becomes sufficiently bored...

Something expensive usually follows.

Sometimes it's a harmless quality-of-life invention.

Sometimes it's an entirely new class of weapon.

This time...

It was the latter.

---

# **I. TWO WEEKS OF SILENCE**

Without notifying anyone beyond a simple "Busy."

Neko disappeared into his personal laboratory overlooking Whitefall Lake.

The reinforced blast doors sealed behind him.

For the next **fourteen days**, nobody entered.

Nobody left.

The only indication the laboratory was still occupied came from the occasional blue flashes escaping the ventilation shafts, distant explosions echoing across the frozen lake, and power consumption graphs that made Engineering question whether Neko was attempting to construct a particle accelerator.

Every request to check on him was denied.

Director Lucious gave a single instruction.

> "Leave him alone."

> "We'll find out what he's built when he's finished."

---

# **II. THE REVEAL**

Exactly fourteen days later...

The blast doors opened.

Neko stepped into the hallway.

He looked exhausted.

His lab coat was stained with grease, plasma residue, scorch marks, and what Medical hoped was coffee.

Resting over one shoulder...

Was what appeared to be an oversized polearm.

At first glance...

It resembled nothing more than a thick white pole.

Roughly the length of a broom.

Noticeably wider than a normal staff.

Matte white along its body with subtle black mechanical seams running its length.

No blade.

No obvious barrel.

No visible power source.

One engineer frowned.

> "...You spent two weeks making a stick?"

Neko smiled.

---

# **III. FIRST TRANSFORMATION**

Without answering...

Neko pressed a recessed switch near the center of the pole.

A quiet mechanical hum followed.

Hidden panels shifted.

The upper third unfolded.

Blue conduits illuminated beneath the housing.

An elegant crescent frame rotated into place.

A brilliant azure plasma blade erupted into existence with a sharp _FWOOM._

Within seconds...

The simple white pole had become an enormous plasma scythe.

The blade emitted the familiar sound every member of the Armory recognized.

---

### Senior Armorer

"...That's..."

He stared.

---

> "...Photon Blade output."

---

Neko simply nodded.

---

# **IV. SECOND TRANSFORMATION**

Another engineer tilted his head.

> "...So it's just a plasma scythe?"

---

Neko looked almost offended.

---

### Neko

> "...You think I'm that boring?"

---

He pressed another switch.

The plasma blade instantly collapsed.

A detachable energy magazine ejected from the weapon.

Several internal locking mechanisms disengaged.

The shaft split apart.

The rear stock unfolded.

A heavy plasma barrel rotated into alignment.

An ammunition feed extended from the side.

Moments later...

The scythe had transformed into an unmistakable support weapon.

Large.

Heavy.

Purpose-built.

Neko shouldered it.

A training dummy stood one hundred meters away.

---

**BRRRRRRRT!**

---

A continuous stream of concentrated azure plasma erupted from the weapon.

The reinforced target lasted less than three seconds.

Armor melted.

Structural supports vaporized.

Nothing remained except glowing slag.

Silence filled the firing range.

---

### Engineer

"...That's..."

---

### Quartermaster

"...Photon Alternator performance."

---

Everyone slowly turned toward Neko.

---

---

# **VI. DESIGNATION**

The completed weapon was officially registered under Engineering.

**Designation:**

> **Neko's Claws**

The name was unanimously accepted.

Mostly because nobody wanted to argue with the person currently holding it.

---

# **VII. TECHNICAL DESCRIPTION**

**Stored Configuration**

When inactive, Neko's Claws resembles an unusually thick white pole approximately the length of a household broom.

This compact form allows it to be comfortably carried across the back without immediately identifying it as a weapon.

Its smooth outer shell conceals every moving component.

---

### **Configuration A — Plasma Scythe**

Activating the primary configuration deploys an advanced plasma emitter forming a brilliant azure blade.

Performance matches Aperture's legendary Photon Blades.

Capabilities include:

- Cutting through armored vehicles.
    
- Penetrating fortified steel.
    
- Slicing reinforced composite armor.
    
- Sustained plasma edge with negligible degradation.
    
- Exceptional close-quarters capability.
    

---

### **Configuration B — Plasma Support Weapon**

Through mechanical transformation, the scythe converts into a fully functional plasma support weapon.

Performance equals the prototype Photon Alternator.

Capabilities include:

- Extremely high sustained rate of fire.
    
- Heavy suppressive capability.
    
- Anti-material effectiveness.
    
- Extended engagement endurance.
    
- Rapid transformation between firing and melee configurations.
    

Transformation between both forms requires only a few seconds.

---

# **IX. LEO**

An unexpected side effect of the weapon's introduction involved Leo.

The juvenile Starladian Banana Snake immediately decided that **Neko's Claws** was an ideal sleeping location.

Whether the weapon was in its inactive pole form or deployed as a scythe...

Leo showed no preference.

Personnel frequently discovered him peacefully wrapped around the thick white shaft while it was slung across Neko's back.

More surprisingly...

When the weapon was deployed into its scythe configuration, Leo often coiled himself around the base of the crescent head, sleeping comfortably just beneath the glowing plasma blade.

The blade's containment field prevented any heat from reaching him.

He appeared perfectly comfortable.

---

Curled tighter around the scythe.

And continued sleeping.

If Leo happened to be asleep on **Neko's Claws**, the weapon simply wasn't used until he woke up on his own.

# **X. DIRECTOR'S COMMENT**

Director Lucious reviewed the weapon following live-fire testing.

After confirming its performance...

And confirming that the missing Armory prototypes had indeed become part of the new design...

He closed the report.

Looked at Neko.

Then at the weapon.

Then at the Quartermaster.

Finally, he signed the approval form.

His only written comment read:

> **"Next time, ask before dismantling classified prototypes."**

A second note was added beneath it.

> **"Approved for operational deployment."**
