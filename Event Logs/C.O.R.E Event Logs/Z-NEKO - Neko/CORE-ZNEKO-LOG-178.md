Mirage adopted one rule.

> **If Neko can see peanuts... Mirage should already be there.**

Mostly because Mirage had developed an uncanny ability to appear exactly when Neko was about to make a poor decision.

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Mirage's close supervision has dramatically reduced the likelihood of accidental or intentional peanut exposure. His calm, non-confrontational approach has proven particularly effective, often redirecting Neko before temptation turns into another emergency.

Medical Division has noted that while Neko still misses peanuts—and occasionally still has the **nerve** to eye them hopefully—he has also become much quicker to accept being stopped.

Oracle summarized the situation best during a routine observation. She carefully wrote on her whiteboard:

> **Neko fixes pipes.**
> 
> **Mirage fixes Neko.** 🐾
