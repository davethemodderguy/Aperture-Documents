# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

### **INTERPRETING NEKO'S BITES**

### **Classification:** INTERNAL – BEHAVIORAL GUIDE

---

## **I. FOLLOW-UP OBSERVATION**

Several weeks after documenting Neko's defensive biting behavior, personnel noticed something unusual.

Not every bite meant the same thing.

In fact...

There were **two** distinctly different kinds.

---

## **II. TYPE ONE — "PLEASE FIX YOUR GRIP."**

This is the bite most personnel encounter first.

It occurs when:

- Neko is being carried awkwardly.
    
- He's picked up without warning.
    
- Someone is squeezing him too tightly.
    
- Someone (usually an Unchosen One) attempts to "borrow" him without asking.
    

The bite is firm enough to be immediately noticeable.

His small feline fangs produce a brief stinging sensation.

It rarely breaks the skin and has never caused a significant injury during normal interactions.

---

### Example

### Unchosen One

> "He's lighter than I thou—"

**Nib.**

---

### Unchosen One

> "Ow!"

---

### Neko

> "...Support my legs."

---

### Maintenance Engineer

> "He's giving you instructions."

---

### Unchosen One

> "...By biting me?"

---

### Maintenance Engineer

> "Yes."

---

## **III. TYPE TWO — AFFECTION**

The second type took much longer to identify.

Because...

Nobody realized Neko was doing it intentionally.

Unlike the defensive bite...

This one is extremely gentle.

His fangs barely make contact.

There is no sting.

No pain.

Only a faint ticklish sensation.

It usually lasts only a second before he lets go.

---

### Cherry

> "...Did..."

---

### Neko

> "...Hm?"

---

### Cherry

> "...Did you just bite my hand?"

---

Neko looked up.

---

### Neko

> "...Yeah."

---

### Cherry

> "...Why?"

---

### Neko

> "...I like you."

---

Cherry blinked.

Then laughed.

---

### Cherry

> "...That's actually adorable."

No complaints.

---

## **IX. PERSONNEL GUIDE**

### **Interpreting Neko's Bites**

|Bite Type|Sensation|Meaning|
|---|---|---|
|Firm nib|Slight sting|"I'm uncomfortable. Please change how you're carrying or handling me."|
|Gentle nib|Ticklish, no pain|Sign of trust, affection, or appreciation.|

Personnel are advised **not** to overreact to either.

If uncertain...

Simply ask.

Neko is generally happy to explain.

---

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Although Neko is perfectly capable of speaking, some of his more cat-like habits remain.

Biting has become one of them.

It is never malicious toward trusted personnel.

Instead, it has become a surprisingly honest form of communication.

As one Maintenance engineer summarized:

> **"If he nibbles and it stings, fix what you're doing. If he nibbles and it tickles... congratulations, the little guy likes you."**
