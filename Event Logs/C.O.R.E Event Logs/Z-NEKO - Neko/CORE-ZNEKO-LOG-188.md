# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-188**

### **ANOMALOUS EMOTIONAL SUPPRESSION EVENT — WEEK 6**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – INTERNAL / N.O.S.T. / MEDICAL

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Six weeks after the initial suppression event, the source of the anomaly was finally identified.

It was Aster.

Not through malice.

Not through negligence.

Not through intent.

Simply because she had unknowingly developed a new anomalous ability that neither she nor anyone else understood.

The ability had selectively suppressed fundamental traits in those closest to her.

By the time the discovery was made...

Aster had already realized it herself.

---

# **I. THE DISCOVERY**

The breakthrough came during another series of resonance scans.

For the first time, the anomalous signatures surrounding Aster reacted.

She stopped.

Looked down at herself.

Then toward Star.

Then toward Neko.

Her ears slowly folded back.

---

### Aster

She quietly picked up her marker.

Her paws were shaking.

She wrote only two words.

> **...Me?**

---

Nobody answered immediately.

Nobody wanted to.

Silence itself became the answer.

---

Aster stared at the board for several seconds.

Then erased it.

---

# **II. THE REVERSAL**

Without instruction...

Without guidance...

Aster walked to Neko first.

She gently placed one paw against his hand.

The same faint white light recorded six weeks earlier appeared once again.

Only this time...

It flowed back toward her.

The anomalous signature vanished.

---

Neko blinked.

He looked around the workshop.

His eyes stopped on a discarded pressure regulator.

Within seconds...

He grabbed a pencil.

Covered three pages with sketches.

Crossed out half of them.

Improved the remaining half.

Then suddenly laughed.

---

### Neko

> "...Mirage."

---

Mirage looked over.

---

### Neko

> "...I've got an idea."

---

Mirage smiled for the first time in weeks.

---

### Mirage

> "...Welcome back."

---

Engineering personnel erupted into applause.

Some didn't even know why.

They simply knew...

Neko was himself again.

---

# **III. STAR**

Aster immediately ran toward N.O.S.T.

She found Star's usual corner.

Empty.

The observation lounge.

Empty.

The training hall.

Empty.

Medical.

Empty.

Nightshade was already searching.

---

### Nightshade

> "...She's gone."

---

Security reviewed the cameras.

Star had completed her morning duties as scheduled.

She acknowledged several coworkers.

Left the department.

Turned one corner.

And...

Never appeared on another camera again.

No teleportation.

No containment breach.

No reality distortion.

She had simply...

Disappeared.

---

# **IV. THE SEARCH**

A full facility search began immediately.

Engineering.

Medical.

Containment.

Residential sectors.

Maintenance tunnels.

Observation decks.

Every place Star had ever liked.

Nothing.

Aster personally searched beside Nightshade.

Every time they reached another empty room...

Aster's pace became faster.

Her breathing became heavier.

She kept writing the same question.

> **Where?**

No one had an answer.

---

# **V. ASTER**

Late that evening...

Aster returned to Star's empty corner.

She sat down.

Placed her marker on the floor.

Then...

For the first time since arriving at the Blacksite...

She cried openly.

Between sobs, she wrote one sentence.

> **I'm sorry.**

She erased it.

Wrote it again.

Erased it again.

The page eventually became too wet to write on.

---

Miss Neko quietly picked up the marker.

She didn't hand it back.

She simply sat beside Aster.

Neither spoke.

---

# **VI. NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade refused to end the search.

Long after everyone else had been ordered to rest...

She continued walking the corridors alone.

Checking rooms she had already searched.

Looking behind doors that had already been opened.

Calling Star's name only when nobody else was nearby.

No response ever came.

---

# **VII. DIRECTOR'S ORDER**

Director Lucious suspended all non-essential projects.

A facility-wide search protocol was initiated.

Every department received the same instruction.

> **"Finding Star takes priority."**

No disciplinary action was taken against Aster.

Director Lucious personally rejected every suggestion to isolate or contain her.

His written statement was brief.

> **"She corrected the harm the moment she understood it. Guilt is not evidence of intent."**

The Week Six report ends with an unusual note.

Star's corner remained exactly as she had left it.

On the windowsill rested a single flower.

No one knew who had placed it there.

Beside it lay Aster's marker.

Unused.
