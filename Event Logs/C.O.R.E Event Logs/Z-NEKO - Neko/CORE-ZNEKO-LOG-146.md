# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-146**

### **INVESTIGATION CONCLUSION**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – LEVEL 5 CONFIDENTIAL

---

## **I. BREAKTHROUGH**

After weeks of reviewing maintenance requests, scheduling records, emergency dispatch logs, and administrative permissions, Security finally discovered an inconsistency.

Unlike previous anomalies, this one left a trace.

It was small.

Repeated.

And always originated from the same administrative terminal.

---

### Chief of Security

> "...Run that access history again."

---

The analyst complied.

Every unauthorized schedule adjustment...

Every reassigned work order...

Every delayed maintenance notification...

Every "urgent" task that redirected personnel elsewhere...

All originated from the same administrator account.

---

## **II. THE MOTIVE**

The administrator was immediately detained for questioning.

At first, he denied everything.

Presented with the audit logs, he admitted altering schedules.

Not to target Neko.

But to benefit his own son.

His son worked in Maintenance.

By quietly shifting assignments and priorities, the administrator had been creating openings for his son to avoid undesirable work while redirecting those tasks elsewhere.

Each individual change seemed insignificant.

A delayed inspection.

A reassigned repair.

An emergency call sent to another team.

A meeting moved by thirty minutes.

On their own, they appeared harmless.

Over months, however, the cumulative effect unintentionally caused nearly every opportunity for personnel to spend time with Neko to be interrupted by legitimate work elsewhere.

---

### Administrator

> "...I wasn't trying to hurt anyone..."

---

### Chief of Security

> "...Intent and consequence are not always the same."

---

## **III. THE REALIZATION**

Niko overlaid the confirmed schedule manipulations onto the previous investigation timeline.

Every unexplained interruption...

Finally made sense.

The room remained silent.

Not because they had found a mastermind bent on harming Neko.

But because something so ordinary—

Administrative favoritism—

Had produced consequences no one had imagined.

---

### Lucy

> "...All this..."

---

She looked at the timeline.

---

### Lucy

> "...Started because someone wanted their son to skip work?"

---

No one answered.

Because that was exactly what the evidence showed.

---

## **IV. MISS NEKO**

Miss Neko quietly looked at the recovered notebook resting on the conference table.

Then at the scheduling reports.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro kept blaming himself..."

---

---

### Miss Neko

> "...When none of this was his fault."

---

## **V. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Director Lucious dismissed everyone except Security leadership.

The disciplinary proceedings that followed were classified.

The official record released to most personnel stated only:

> **"An administrator was found to have deliberately manipulated internal scheduling for personal benefit, resulting in severe operational disruption and significant harm to personnel welfare. Employment was terminated, and the matter was referred for criminal prosecution under Blacksite regulations."**

The specific legal proceedings and penalties remained restricted to those with the appropriate clearance.

---

## **VI. ARMORY ARCHIVE**

Rumors spread quickly after the investigation concluded.

Some claimed Armory personnel had opened an old classified archive related to the **Bliss of Whitefall** demonstrations.

Others claimed it contained records of theoretical effects on living subjects.

Security issued an immediate clarification:

> **No human testing of the Bliss of Whitefall has ever been authorized or conducted by Aperture Industries.**

Any discussions suggesting otherwise were designated as unverified rumors and were not entered into the official archive.

---

## **VII. AFTERMATH**

For the first time in weeks...

People understood why every attempt to reach Neko had failed.

Not because no one cared.

Not because everyone had forgotten him.

Because the schedules had quietly been working against them.

Lucy sat beside the recovered notebook.

She reread the sentence that had haunted everyone.

> **I slow people down.**

Then she placed the investigation report beside it.

Two documents.

One built on misunderstanding.

One built on evidence.

They told completely different stories.

---

## **VIII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

The investigation was formally closed.

It concluded that repeated unauthorized scheduling manipulations had unintentionally isolated Neko over an extended period. There was no evidence that the administrator's objective was to emotionally harm him; the motive established by the investigation was personal favoritism toward a family member. Nevertheless, the resulting operational and welfare impacts were deemed severe.

Director Lucious's closing statement was circulated to department heads:

> **"Small abuses of responsibility can produce consequences far beyond what anyone intends. We cannot change what happened, but we can ensure our systems—and our people—are never allowed to drift so far apart again."**
