# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-164**

### **A QUIET KIND OF COMPANY**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE

---

## **I. INCREASED WORKLOAD**

For reasons unrelated to anomalous activity, the Blacksite entered another period of exceptionally high operational demand.

Engineering requests doubled.

Research deadlines tightened.

Medical staff worked extended shifts.

Most personnel found themselves rushing from one assignment to the next.

The atmosphere reminded many of the difficult period months earlier, when manipulated schedules had unintentionally left Neko isolated.

This time...

The schedules were verified.

No sabotage was found.

Everyone really was just busy.

---

## **II. A DIFFERENCE**

There was one important difference.

Neko was no longer alone.

Wherever he went...

Mirage quietly followed.

And, more often than not...

A tiny kitten could be seen trotting behind the pair.

---

### Engineer

> "...Looks like you've got your own maintenance crew."

---

Neko looked behind him.

Oracle was carrying a zip tie in her mouth for absolutely no reason.

Mirage was carrying an actual toolbox.

---

### Neko

> "...One of them is definitely more helpful."

---

Oracle proudly dropped the zip tie at his feet.

---

> "Mew."

---

Neko picked it up anyway.

---

### Neko

> "...Thanks."

---

Oracle purred loudly.

---

## **III. NO DANGER TODAY**

Research had been quietly monitoring Oracle.

Something unusual had happened.

Or rather...

Nothing had happened.

For nearly three weeks...

Oracle had not issued a single emergency warning.

No blocked hallways.

No frantic meowing.

No pulling at sleeves.

No attempts to redirect personnel.

The Blacksite remained unusually calm.

---

### Lead Researcher

> "...She hasn't predicted a single catastrophe."

---

### Assistant

> "...That's... good, right?"

---

The researcher looked through the observation window.

Oracle was rolling onto her back while Neko gently rubbed her stomach.

---

### Lead Researcher

> "...I think she's enjoying the break."

---

## **IV. LUNCH BREAK**

Neko finally sat down after finishing another maintenance ticket.

Mirage handed him a sandwich.

Oracle climbed onto the bench beside him.

She looked at the sandwich.

Then at Neko.

Then at the sandwich again.

---

### Neko

> "...No."

---

Oracle blinked innocently.

---

### Neko

> "...You already had lunch."

---

Oracle slowly leaned a little closer.

---

### Neko

> "...Still no."

---

She gently rested one tiny paw on his arm.

---

### Neko

> "......"

---

Mirage watched silently.

---

### Mirage

> "...She's negotiating."

---

### Neko

> "...I noticed."

---

After a long stare...

Neko sighed.

Broke off a tiny piece of plain chicken from his lunch.

Oracle accepted it with great dignity.

As though she had won a difficult legal argument.

---

## **V. BREAK TIME**

With no emergencies to respond to...

Oracle became remarkably playful.

She chased shadows cast by rotating maintenance lights.

She pounced on loose packing foam.

She hid behind toolboxes, waiting for Neko to walk by before springing out with an enthusiastic—

> "Mew!"

Neko reacted the same way every time.

---

### Neko

> "...You got me."

---

Oracle's tail stood proudly upright.

Mission accomplished.

---

Mirage eventually joined in.

Using his short-range teleportation...

He would disappear behind one crate...

Then reappear behind another.

Oracle enthusiastically tried to predict where he'd appear next.

She almost never guessed correctly.

---

### Mirage

> "...Too slow."

---

Oracle immediately sprinted after him.

---

### Mirage

> "...Still too slow."

---

For nearly fifteen minutes...

Engineering watched an anomalous hologram play teleport-tag with a future-seeing kitten.

No one questioned it.

---

## **VI. MISS NEKO**

When Miss Neko finally finished her own work, she found Neko sitting on the floor of the workshop.

Oracle was asleep across his lap.

Mirage sat beside him, quietly reading a maintenance manual.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Busy?"

---

### Neko

> "...Always."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lonely?"

---

Neko looked down at the sleeping kitten.

Then at Mirage.

He smiled.

---

### Neko

> "...Not anymore."

---

Miss Neko smiled back.

She remembered the notebook.

The dark corners.

The birthdays spent alone.

The words:

> _"I slow people down."_

Now...

Even on the busiest days...

Someone chose to stay with him.

Not because they had to.

Because they wanted to.

She quietly sat beside him.

Without saying another word.

---

## **VII. ORACLE'S CHOICE**

The following morning...

Oracle ignored every offered lap in the cafeteria.

She walked past Star.

Past Nightshade.

Past Lucy.

Past Director Lucious.

Straight to Neko.

She hopped onto the chair beside him.

Curled into a tiny fluffy ball.

And resumed sleeping.

---

### Star

> "...Guess she's picked today's human."

---

Nightshade nodded.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Good."

---

### Star

> "...No danger?"

---

Nightshade looked toward Oracle.

The kitten was sleeping so deeply that one tiny paw twitched in a dream.

---

### Nightshade

> "...No."

---

### Nightshade

> "...She's just being a kitten today."

---

## **VIII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

The recent increase in Blacksite workload has significantly reduced casual interaction between personnel. Unlike previous periods of isolation, however, Neko has maintained steady companionship through Mirage and Z-885 "Oracle."

Medical and Psychology divisions note that this consistent company has had a positive effect on Neko's overall mood. Although still busy, he no longer withdraws from others during quieter moments, and the presence of Mirage and Oracle appears to provide a reassuring sense of routine.

Z-885 has not exhibited any precognitive warning behaviors in recent weeks. Researchers interpret this as an indication that no imminent catastrophic threats have been detected within her range of perception. During these calmer periods, she behaves much like an ordinary kitten—playing, sleeping, exploring, and spending much of her free time with Neko.

Director Lucious added a brief note to the end of the report:

> **"The best days are the ones where Oracle forgets she's an anomaly and gets to spend the afternoon being a kitten."**
