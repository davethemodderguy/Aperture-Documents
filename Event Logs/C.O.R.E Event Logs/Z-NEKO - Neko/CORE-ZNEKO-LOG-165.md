# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-165**

### **THE ONE SHE WOULDN'T LEAVE**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – MEDIUM CLEARANCE

---

## **I. OBSERVATION**

After nearly three weeks without a single recorded precognitive response...

Z-885's anomalous behavior abruptly resumed.

At first, personnel assumed another facility-wide hazard had been detected.

No alarms sounded.

No containment failures occurred.

No unstable experiments were reported.

Yet Oracle refused to leave one particular individual.

Neko.

---

## **II. THE FIRST INCIDENT**

08:43 — Engineering Sector.

Neko reached for a wrench resting on the edge of a workbench.

Oracle suddenly ran between his feet.

---

### Neko

> "...Oracle?"

---

She looked up at him.

Didn't move.

Five seconds later...

The wrench rolled from the edge of the bench and struck the floor exactly where Neko's foot would have been.

---

### Engineer

> "...Lucky."

---

Oracle simply purred.

---

## **III. THE SECOND INCIDENT**

10:12 — Coolant Distribution.

Neko was preparing to cross an elevated maintenance catwalk.

Oracle stood in front of him.

Refused to move.

---

### Neko

> "...I'm working."

---

The kitten shook her head.

Or at least...

It looked like she did.

Neko waited.

Three seconds later...

A maintenance cart below clipped a support beam.

The catwalk vibrated.

A loose handrail section detached and fell into the shaft beneath.

---

### Maintenance Worker

> "...That would've landed right next to him..."

---

Oracle quietly continued walking.

Only after the danger had passed.

---

## **IV. THE THIRD INCIDENT**

13:27 — Engineering Storage.

Neko opened a cabinet.

Oracle suddenly pawed at his sleeve.

He stepped back instinctively.

A heavily loaded storage bin, improperly secured by another shift, slid from the top shelf and crashed onto the floor.

Silence.

---

### Neko

> "...Thanks."

---

Oracle answered with a tiny:

> "Mew."

---

## **V. A PATTERN**

By the end of the day...

Research compiled the incident log.

Every warning.

Every intervention.

Every prevented accident.

The same name appeared each time.

**Affected Personnel: Neko**

There were no reports involving anyone else.

---

### Lead Researcher

> "...All of today's imminent hazards were centered around one person."

---

The room grew quiet.

---

## **VI. MIRAGE**

Mirage noticed before anyone else.

Oracle had stopped wandering the Blacksite.

She no longer picked a random employee to follow.

Instead...

She stayed beside Neko almost constantly.

If Neko walked...

She walked.

If Neko stopped...

She sat beside him.

If Neko rested...

She curled up nearby.

Mirage looked down at the kitten.

---

### Mirage

> "...You're worried."

---

Oracle looked up.

Softly meowed.

---

> "Mew."

---

Mirage simply nodded.

---

### Mirage

> "...Me too."

---

## **VII. MISS NEKO**

Miss Neko found Oracle sleeping against Neko during lunch.

The kitten rarely slept this deeply while on "watch."

She gently stroked Oracle's head.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Working hard?"

---

Oracle opened one eye.

Purred once.

Then immediately looked back toward Neko.

Even while resting...

She never completely took her attention off him.

Miss Neko sighed.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You're guarding my lil bro, aren't you?"

---

The kitten rubbed against her hand.

---

## **VIII. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

A review of security footage revealed something unsettling.

None of the incidents were connected.

No sabotage.

No malicious intent.

No anomalous influence.

They were simply...

Bad luck.

Loose equipment.

Minor mechanical failures.

Objects slipping.

Mistimed movements.

The kinds of accidents that happen independently.

Yet they all seemed to occur near Neko within a remarkably short period.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...He's become the center of an improbable streak of workplace hazards."

---

An engineer raised a hand.

---

### Engineer

> "...Should we restrict him from working?"

---

Lucious shook his head.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...No."

---

He looked toward Oracle on the monitor.

The kitten had just stopped Neko from absentmindedly stepping onto a freshly mopped floor where a heavy trolley was passing.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...We continue operations."

---

### Director Lucious

> "...And we trust the little inspector."

---

## **IX. NEKO**

That evening...

Neko sat on a toolbox.

Oracle climbed into his lap.

He gently scratched behind her ears.

---

### Neko

> "...You've been following me all day."

---

Oracle purred.

---

### Neko

> "...Busy day?"

---

She looked up at him.

Those bright azure eyes reflected his own for a moment.

Then she tucked her head beneath his hand.

---

### Neko

> "...Guess that's a yes."

---

Mirage sat beside them.

---

### Mirage

> "...You should be careful tomorrow."

---

Neko smiled.

---

### Neko

> "...I will."

---

He looked down at Oracle.

---

### Neko

> "...Especially since I've got such a good lookout."

---

Oracle answered with a contented chirp.

---

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Research Division has confirmed that Z-885's precognitive responses have fully resumed. During the current observation period, every recorded warning has involved preventing minor but potentially serious accidents affecting Neko.

No evidence suggests deliberate targeting, sabotage, or coordinated anomalous activity. The events appear to be unrelated workplace mishaps occurring with unusual frequency around a single individual.

As a precaution, Engineering has reminded all personnel to maintain proper safety practices around Neko and to pay close attention if Oracle attempts to interrupt his actions.

An addendum was signed by Director Lucious:

> **"Most days, Oracle protects the Blacksite from danger. Some days, she protects one engineer from a dozen little accidents. If she decides your next step isn't the right one, don't argue with her. Wait. Then follow where she leads."**
