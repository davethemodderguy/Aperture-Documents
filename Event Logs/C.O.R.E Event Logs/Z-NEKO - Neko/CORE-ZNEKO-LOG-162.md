# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-162**

### **DOUBLE TROUBLE**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE / LOW PRIORITY  
**Certain details remain classified at the request of the involved personnel.**

---

## **I. PRELUDE**

At approximately 08:15 Blacksite Time...

Neko and Mirage were observed speaking privately with Star and Nightshade.

The conversation lasted six minutes.

No witnesses were close enough to hear what was said.

Security cameras recorded only the following:

- Neko nodding seriously.
    
- Mirage smiling.
    
- Star suddenly becoming extremely excited.
    
- Nightshade covering her face with one hand and sighing.
    

The remaining details have been classified.

Official documentation simply states:

> **Reason for compliance:** Classified.

Unofficial Engineering documentation states:

> **"Neko and Mirage successfully bribed them."**

The exact nature of the "bribe" remains unknown.

---

## **II. DOUBLE TROUBLE**

At exactly 09:00...

Two clouds of white smoke appeared simultaneously.

When the smoke cleared...

There were now four cats in Engineering.

- White Cat Neko.
    
- Miss Neko.
    
- Star.
    
- Nightshade.
    

The Maintenance Sector immediately coined the day:

> **Double Trouble.**

Not because of Neko and Miss Neko.

Everyone was already used to them.

It was because...

Star and Nightshade had both transformed on the same day.

---

### Engineer

> "...Today's going to be interesting."

---

## **III. STAR**

Tiny Star immediately proved everyone's prediction correct.

Within thirty seconds...

She had:

- Climbed onto a toolbox.
    
- Jumped onto Mirage's shoulder.
    
- Jumped off Mirage's shoulder.
    
- Chased a loose cable.
    
- Tried to pounce on Neko's moving tail.
    

---

### Neko

> "...Star."

---

Tiny Star froze.

---

### Tiny Star

> "...Meow?"

---

### Neko

> "...That's my tail."

---

She slowly let go.

---

### Tiny Star

> "...Sorry."

---

## **IV. NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade's experience...

Was very different.

She transformed.

Sat down.

Wrapped her tail around herself.

And looked deeply embarrassed.

---

### Tiny Nightshade

> "...Mrr..."

---

### Star

> "...You're adorable."

---

Nightshade immediately flattened her ears.

---

### Tiny Nightshade

> "...No."

---

She clearly wanted to transform back immediately.

But...

She had made a deal.

So she stayed.

---

## **V. NEKO'S EXPERIENCE**

Neko quietly walked over.

He crouched to Nightshade's level.

Didn't reach out immediately.

Didn't try to pet her.

Instead...

He simply held out one hand.

---

### Neko

> "...Your choice."

---

Nightshade looked at his hand.

Then at him.

She remembered something.

Neko had spent months looking after both himself and Miss Neko in cat form.

He knew feline body language instinctively.

He never forced contact.

Never surprised anyone.

Never ignored flattened ears or nervous tails.

After several quiet seconds...

Nightshade stepped forward on her own.

And gently bumped her forehead against his palm.

---

### Neko

> "...There you are."

---

Only then did he softly scratch behind one ear.

Slowly.

Carefully.

Exactly where a cat usually enjoys it.

Nightshade's tense posture gradually relaxed.

Her ears lifted.

Her tail stopped flicking.

---

### Tiny Nightshade

> "...Prr..."

---

She immediately looked horrified that she'd purred.

---

### Neko

> "...Too much?"

---

Nightshade shook her head.

Very slightly.

---

### Tiny Nightshade

> "...Continue."

---

Neko smiled.

---

### Neko

> "...Okay."

---

## **VI. MISS NEKO**

Miss Neko watched from nearby with crossed arms.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro."

---

### Neko

> "...Hm?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You spoil cats."

---

### Neko

> "...Probably."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Correction."

---

She smiled.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Definitely."

---

Mirage nodded in agreement.

---

### Mirage

> "...Definitely."

---

## **VII. MIRAGE**

Mirage had his own assignment.

Keeping Star occupied.

This proved...

Difficult.

Whenever Star attempted to sprint somewhere she probably shouldn't...

Mirage simply teleported in front of her.

---

### Tiny Star

> "...!"

---

She changed direction.

Mirage appeared there too.

---

Another direction.

---

Another teleport.

---

After the sixth attempt...

Star flopped onto the floor dramatically.

---

### Tiny Star

> "...You cheated."

---

### Mirage

> "...Yes."

---

### Tiny Star

> "...Again."

---

Mirage teleported behind her.

---

### Mirage

> "...Okay."

---

Soon the hallway had become a harmless game of cat-and-mouse, except the "mouse" could teleport and seemed to enjoy making Star guess where he'd appear next.

---

## **VIII. LUNCH**

Lunch was unusually peaceful.

Tiny Star had finally worn herself out and curled up beside Miss Neko.

Nightshade...

Had quietly settled against Neko's side.

She clearly hadn't intended to.

But after relaxing earlier...

She apparently found the warmth comfortable.

Neko noticed.

Said nothing.

Simply continued eating his sandwich one-handed.

Careful not to disturb either cat.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Comfortable?"

---

Tiny Nightshade hesitated.

Then answered quietly.

---

> "...Yes."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Thought so."

---

Nightshade closed her eyes.

For the first time all day...

She looked completely relaxed.

---

## **IX. THE AFTERMATH**

By the end of the shift...

Star had declared Cat Day an overwhelming success.

Nightshade refused to comment.

However...

When everyone began packing up...

She quietly transformed into her cat form again.

Walked over to Neko.

Jumped onto his shoulder without a word.

Sat there for nearly a minute.

Receiving a few gentle scratches behind the ears.

Then transformed back into her human form before anyone could tease her.

She folded her arms.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Don't get used to it."

---

### Neko

> "...Wouldn't dream of it."

---

Mirage looked at Neko.

Then at Nightshade.

---

### Mirage

> "...Tomorrow?"

---

Nightshade immediately pointed at him.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Absolutely not."

---

Mirage smiled.

---

### Mirage

> "...Worth asking."

---

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Engineering unanimously declared the event **"Double Trouble Day."**

No equipment was damaged.

No anomalies escaped containment.

The only measurable consequence was a significant increase in staff morale.

Medical also made an unexpected note regarding Nightshade's behavior. Although initially uncomfortable with using her cat form, she relaxed considerably once Neko interacted with her. His experience caring for cats—both through his own transformations and while helping Miss Neko during her recovery—allowed him to read subtle feline cues and respond appropriately without causing additional stress.

A handwritten note later appeared on the Engineering notice board.

No one admitted to writing it.

> **Double Trouble Results:**  
> • Star: 100% chaos.  
> • Nightshade: Pretends she hates it, secretly enjoyed the ear scratches.  
> • Neko: Certified cat handler.  
> • Miss Neko: "I already knew that."  
> • Mirage: Somehow turned teleportation into a game of tag.  
> **Overall Rating:** ★★★★★ — "Would absolutely classify the bribe again."
