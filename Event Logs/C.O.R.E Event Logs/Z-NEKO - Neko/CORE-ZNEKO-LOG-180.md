# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-180**

### **PROTOCOL UPDATE – PEANUT EXPOSURE PREVENTION**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – MEDICAL DIRECTIVE

---

## **I. REASON FOR UPDATE**

Following multiple severe anaphylactic incidents, including one in which Neko was nearly lost after consuming peanuts while alone, Medical Division has concluded that verbal reminders are no longer sufficient in every circumstance.

Although Neko has expressed a genuine desire to avoid peanuts, he occasionally acts on impulse when they are available.

Accordingly, the following protocol has been approved by Medical Division and Director Lucious.

---

## **II. AUTHORIZED INTERVENTION**

If Neko is observed attempting to:

- Consume peanuts.
    
- Consume food known to contain peanuts.
    
- Reach for peanut-containing products after being informed of the risk.
    

Personnel are authorized to physically interrupt the attempt using the **minimum force necessary**.

Approved methods include:

- Gently taking the food from his hands.
    
- Guiding him away from the area.
    
- Carrying him away if necessary.
    
- Placing him into a piggyback carry, provided it can be done safely.
    
- Asking nearby personnel for assistance if he continues attempting to obtain the food.
    

These actions are considered **medical intervention**, not disciplinary measures.

---

## **III. PREFERRED RESPONDERS**

Personnel most familiar with Neko are encouraged to intervene first whenever possible.

Priority responders:

1. Miss Neko
    
2. Mirage
    
3. Oracle
    
4. Star
    
5. Nightshade
    

If none are available, any trained staff member may intervene.

---

## **IV. TRAINING EXAMPLE**

Engineering Break Room.

Someone unknowingly left a bowl of peanuts on a table.

Neko noticed.

He quietly walked toward it.

Oracle immediately wrote:

> **No.**

Neko smiled awkwardly.

---

### Neko

> "...I'm just looking."

---

He reached anyway.

Before his hand could touch the bowl...

Two familiar arms gently lifted him completely off the floor.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...No, Lil bro."

---

Neko blinked.

His feet dangled several centimeters above the ground.

---

### Neko

> "...You're carrying me?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Correct."

---

She calmly walked away from the peanuts.

Still carrying him.

---

### Neko

> "...I can walk..."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Not toward peanuts."

---

## **V. SECOND EXAMPLE**

Later that week...

Neko accepted a peanut candy from a visitor who had not yet been informed of the protocol.

Mirage appeared almost immediately.

He quietly removed the candy from Neko's hand.

Neko instinctively reached after it.

Without a word...

Mirage crouched slightly.

Lifted Neko onto his back.

---

### Neko

> "...Mirage..."

---

### Mirage

> "...Medical orders."

---

He calmly walked away.

Neko made no attempt to climb down.

Instead...

He rested his chin on Mirage's shoulder.

---

### Neko

> "...This is embarrassing."

---

### Mirage

> "...Alive."

---

### Neko

> "...Fair."

---

## **VI. ORACLE'S ROLE**

Oracle remains responsible for early detection.

If she identifies peanut exposure...

She follows her established escalation procedure:

1. Written warning.
    
2. Gentle nib.
    
3. Marker throw.
    

If Neko still attempts to eat the peanuts...

She immediately seeks the nearest trusted responder.

Testing has shown Oracle is surprisingly good at locating Miss Neko or Mirage in remarkably short periods of time.

Research continues to investigate how.

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

This protocol update reflects the Blacksite's commitment to preventing another life-threatening allergic reaction. Authorized physical restraint in this context is limited to safe, protective actions necessary to prevent Neko from ingesting peanuts.

Director Lucious approved the directive with the following remark:

> **"Normally, picking up an engineer and carrying him away from a snack would sound ridiculous. In this case, it's an approved medical procedure. If he complains, carry him anyway."**
