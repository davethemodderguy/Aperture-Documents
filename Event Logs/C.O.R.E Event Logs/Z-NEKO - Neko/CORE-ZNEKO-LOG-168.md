# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-168**

### **THE SUBSTITUTE CARETAKERS**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE

---

## **I. ANOTHER BUSY DAY**

The Blacksite had somehow become even busier.

Neko and Mirage were assigned to emergency maintenance following an unexpected coolant pump replacement.

Miss Neko had been pulled into Logistics to help coordinate incoming supplies.

For the first time since Oracle arrived...

Three of her four caretakers were occupied simultaneously.

That left...

Star.

And Nightshade.

---

### Star

> "...It's our turn!"

---

Nightshade sighed.

---

### Nightshade

> "...I know."

---

Oracle looked between the two.

Tilted her head.

---

> "...Mew?"

---

## **II. STAR'S PLAN**

Star immediately picked Oracle up.

---

### Star

> "...We're gonna have so much fun!"

---

Oracle purred.

Nightshade folded her arms.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Define 'fun.'"

---

### Star

> "...We'll improvise."

---

### Nightshade

> "...That's exactly what worries me."

---

## **III. BLACKSITE TOUR**

Star decided Oracle deserved a "grand tour."

Ignoring the fact that Oracle had already wandered most of the Blacksite on her own.

Still...

Oracle seemed happy.

She rode comfortably on Star's shoulder.

Occasionally looking around with curious eyes.

Every engineer they passed smiled.

---

### Engineer

> "...New tour guide?"

---

### Star

> "...VIP tour."

---

Oracle meowed proudly.

---

## **IV. NIGHTSHADE'S APPROACH**

Nightshade preferred a quieter pace.

When Star inevitably became distracted talking to half the Engineering department...

Nightshade sat down on a nearby bench.

Oracle wandered over.

Without a word...

Nightshade gently reached out.

Scratched beneath Oracle's chin.

Exactly once.

Oracle immediately leaned into the touch.

Purring softly.

Nightshade looked mildly surprised.

---

### Nightshade

> "...You like that?"

---

Oracle answered by rubbing against her hand again.

---

### Nightshade

> "...I suppose that's a yes."

---

Star turned around just in time to witness it.

---

### Star

> "...YOU'RE PETTING HER!"

---

Nightshade froze.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Quiet."

---

### Star

> "...I SAW IT!"

---

### Nightshade

> "...You saw nothing."

---

Oracle chose that exact moment to climb into Nightshade's lap.

---

### Star

> "...Evidence."

---

Nightshade sighed.

Defeated.

---

## **V. THE HALLWAY**

While walking through Research...

Oracle suddenly stopped.

She stared at a young intern.

The intern froze.

Everyone nearby became alert.

Had Oracle sensed another danger?

She walked toward the intern.

Sat down.

Looked up.

The intern looked increasingly nervous.

Then...

Oracle gently rubbed against his leg.

And walked away.

Silence.

---

### Intern

> "...What... was that?"

---

Nightshade looked him over.

Then noticed the untied shoelace dragging across the floor.

She pointed.

---

### Nightshade

> "...That."

---

The intern blinked.

Tied his shoe.

Realized he probably would have tripped on the staircase ahead.

He looked back.

Oracle was already gone.

---

### Intern

> "...Thank you..."

---

## **VI. LUNCH BREAK**

Star sat cross-legged on the floor.

Oracle was busy batting at the drawstring of Star's hoodie.

Nightshade sat nearby reading a report.

Every so often...

Oracle would sprint toward Nightshade.

Boop her ankle.

Then sprint back to Star.

This repeated several times.

---

### Star

> "...She's playing tag."

---

### Nightshade

> "...With me."

---

Oracle did it again.

---

### Nightshade

> "...I have become the objective."

---

Star laughed so hard she nearly fell over.

---

## **VII. THE RETURN**

Late in the afternoon...

Neko and Mirage finally emerged from Engineering.

Covered in dust once again.

Miss Neko arrived moments later carrying a stack of completed paperwork.

Oracle looked up.

She stood.

Stretched.

Then calmly walked over.

Instead of choosing just one caretaker...

She sat down in the middle of the four.

Looking from face to face.

As if taking attendance.

---

### Star

> "...Well?"

---

Oracle looked at Neko.

Purr.

Looked at Miss Neko.

Purr.

Looked at Mirage.

Purr.

Finally looked at Nightshade.

A small chirp.

Then she flopped onto her side directly between them all.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Decision made."

---

### Neko

> "...Nobody wins."

---

### Mirage

> "...Everybody wins."

---

Nightshade looked down at the tiny kitten.

A faint smile crossed her face.

---

### Nightshade

> "...I can live with that."

---

## **VIII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Oracle adapted well to spending the day under the care of Star and Nightshade. Star kept her entertained with endless enthusiasm, while Nightshade provided a calm, reassuring presence whenever the kitten wanted to rest.

Personnel noted that the contrast between the two caretakers seemed to suit Oracle perfectly. She happily bounced between energetic play and quiet naps, never appearing distressed by the temporary change in routine.

An unsigned note later appeared on the staff bulletin board:

> **Caretaker Performance Report**  
> **Star:** Excellent entertainment. Slightly excessive enthusiasm.  
> **Nightshade:** Claims she "isn't a cat person." Observed giving chin scratches anyway.  
> **Oracle:** Approved both. Requested additional lap time.  
> **Overall Result:** Kitten successfully spoiled by all caretakers.
