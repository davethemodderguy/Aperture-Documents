# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-118**

### **WHEN THE LIGHT WENT OUT**

### **Classification:** INTERNAL – MEDICAL EMERGENCY / OMEGA PRIORITY

---

## **I. 07:14 HOURS**

The morning examination began as it always had.

Temperature.

Pulse.

Respiration.

Neurological response.

Eye examination.

The Medical Officer lifted a small penlight.

He froze.

For several seconds...

He didn't move.

---

### Medical Officer

> "...Miss Neko."

---

She immediately stood from the chair beside Neko's bed.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...What is it?"

---

The doctor slowly stepped aside.

She looked.

And her heart sank.

---

The familiar sapphire glow...

Was gone.

Completely.

Neko's irises had become a deep, featureless black.

Not clouded.

Not damaged.

Simply...

Dark.

The room fell silent.

---

## **II. CODE OMEGA**

Within seconds, Medical declared an internal emergency.

Not because Neko had stopped breathing.

He hadn't.

His heartbeat was still present.

Weak.

But steady.

His fever remained.

However, the unexplained change in his eyes represented the most severe progression of his illness observed so far.

---

### Automated Medical System

> **"High Priority Medical Alert."**

> **"All non-essential personnel clear Medical Wing."**

---

Researchers, physicians, and anomaly specialists converged on the room.

No one spoke louder than a whisper.

---

## **III. NEKO**

Neko slowly opened his eyes.

He looked around.

Confused by the number of people present.

---

### Neko

> "...Morning..."

---

His voice was barely above a whisper.

---

### Neko

> "...Why is everyone here?"

---

No one answered immediately.

Miss Neko sat beside him.

She gently took his hand.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Just checking on you, lil bro."

---

Neko looked at her for a moment.

Then quietly nodded.

---

### Neko

> "...Okay..."

---

He didn't know.

No one wanted to tell him yet.

---

## **IV. STAR**

Star arrived moments later.

She smiled as she entered.

---

### Star

> "Morning, Neko!"

---

He smiled weakly.

---

### Neko

> "...Morning..."

---

She walked closer.

Then saw his eyes.

Her footsteps stopped.

The soup she was carrying nearly slipped from her hands.

---

For a brief moment...

She couldn't speak.

---

Neko noticed.

---

### Neko

> "...Star?"

---

She forced herself to smile again.

---

### Star

> "...I... brought soup."

---

### Neko

> "...Thanks."

---

She carefully set the bowl down.

Then quietly stepped into the hallway.

Only there did she let herself cry.

---

## **V. NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade entered shortly afterward.

She took one look.

Immediately understood.

---

She walked straight to the bedside.

Folded her arms.

Looked directly at him.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Still alive?"

---

Neko smiled faintly.

---

### Neko

> "...Last time I checked."

---

### Nightshade

> "...Good."

---

She reached over.

Adjusted his blanket without saying another word.

---

As she turned to leave...

She stopped.

---

### Nightshade

> "...You're not allowed to die."

---

Neko blinked.

---

### Neko

> "...Wasn't planning to."

---

She nodded once.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Good."

---

## **VI. NIKO**

Niko arrived carrying new medical files.

He never got the chance to hand them over.

He saw Neko.

Saw his eyes.

And slowly lowered the folder.

---

### Niko

> "...No..."

---

He quickly regained his composure before walking to the bed.

---

### Niko

> "...Hey."

---

### Neko

> "...Hi."

---

### Niko

> "...How're you feeling?"

---

Neko thought carefully.

---

### Neko

> "...Really tired."

---

### Niko

> "...Then rest."

---

## **VII. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Director Lucious entered Medical less than ten minutes after the alert.

Every specialist in the room stood aside.

He looked toward Neko.

Then toward the medical scans.

Then back again.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Explain."

---

The Lead Medical Officer answered honestly.

---

### Lead Medical Officer

> "...We don't know why the change occurred."

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Is he dying?"

---

The room fell silent.

After several long seconds...

The physician finally replied.

---

### Lead Medical Officer

> "...His condition has become critical."

> "...We cannot predict the outcome."

---

Director Lucious nodded once.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Then prediction isn't our job."

---

He looked around the room.

---

### Director Lucious

> "Finding a way to save him is."

---

Every researcher immediately returned to work.

---

## **VIII. MISS NEKO**

Late that evening...

The room was quiet again.

Machines hummed softly.

Rain tapped against the windows.

Miss Neko sat beside the bed.

Still holding Neko's hand.

---

He slowly looked toward her.

---

### Neko

> "...Sis?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Yeah?"

---

### Neko

> "...Everyone keeps pretending."

---

She looked down.

---

### Neko

> "...Something's wrong."

---

She stayed silent.

---

### Neko

> "...Isn't there?"

---

After several seconds...

She nodded.

Just once.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Medical says your condition has gotten worse."

---

Neko looked toward the ceiling.

---

### Neko

> "...Oh."

---

Another long silence.

---

### Neko

> "...My eyes?"

---

She couldn't bring herself to answer immediately.

Finally...

---

### Miss Neko

> "...They changed."

---

Neko reached for the glasses on the bedside table.

He put them on.

Looked into the small mirror nearby.

The lenses reflected back a pair of dark, black irises where brilliant sapphire had once shone.

He stared quietly.

Then set the mirror down.

---

### Neko

> "...Huh."

---

No panic.

No shouting.

Just quiet acceptance.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Don't."

---

Neko looked at her.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Don't give up before Medical does."

---

He smiled weakly.

---

### Neko

> "...I won't."

---

## **IX. THE BLACKSITE**

Word spread only to senior staff.

No announcements were made.

No rumors were encouraged.

Even so...

People noticed the increased activity around Medical.

Maintenance quietly repaired equipment without being asked.

Researchers volunteered for longer shifts.

Engineering paused several nonessential projects so additional personnel could assist Medical however possible.

No one wanted to stand by and do nothing.

---

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Neko's blackened eyes marked the most serious change since the illness began.

Medical Division classified his condition as **critical**, but they did **not** conclude that recovery was impossible. The cause of the transformation remained unknown, and research efforts intensified rather than ceased.

As Director Lucious summarized in the emergency briefing:

> **"As long as he's still breathing, we keep fighting."**

And throughout the night, Miss Neko never left her younger brother's side.

Whenever Neko briefly woke, she was always there to greet him with the same words.

> **"I'm here, lil bro."**

No matter how dark the room became, no one in the Blacksite was willing to let hope disappear with the light in his eyes.
