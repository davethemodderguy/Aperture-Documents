# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-185**

### **ANOMALOUS EMOTIONAL SUPPRESSION EVENT**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – INTERNAL / MEDICAL / N.O.S.T. JOINT INVESTIGATION

---

## **WARNING**

This document concerns the first known manifestation of an undocumented secondary property of **Entity Z-885 ("Aster")**.

The effect was determined to be **completely accidental**.

At the time of writing, Aster was unaware she had caused it.

---

# **I. INCIDENT SUMMARY**

During an off-duty gathering in the Residential Wing, Aster was observed resting on Neko's shoulder while Star, Nightshade, Miss Neko, and Mirage discussed recent engineering projects.

Nothing unusual occurred.

No energy signatures.

No teleportation.

No visible anomaly.

Approximately forty minutes later...

Neko quietly stared at an unfinished blueprint.

Normally, within minutes, he would have already begun sketching improvements.

Instead...

He simply stared.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Thinking?"

---

### Neko

"..."

He looked at the paper.

Then quietly answered.

---

> "...I... can't."

---

# **II. NEKO**

Medical initially suspected fatigue.

They were wrong.

Several routine tests were performed.

---

### Test 1

Invent an improvement to a standard maintenance wrench.

Result:

Neko stared at the wrench for nearly twenty minutes.

No ideas.

---

### Test 2

Design a solution to an engineering problem.

Result:

He solved it correctly.

But only using existing procedures.

No innovations.

No shortcuts.

No inventions.

---

### Test 3

Asked to invent anything at all.

Anything.

Result:

Neko quietly replied:

> "...Nothing comes to mind."

---

This answer immediately alarmed Engineering.

Neko had once invented an airtight permafrost spray because...

He was bored.

Now...

He couldn't think of a single original idea.

---

Director Lucious personally noted:

> "I've known him for years."

> "Silence scares me."

---

# **III. STAR**

The second anomaly appeared hours later.

Star was found sitting alone outside N.O.S.T.

Nightshade approached first.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Star?"

---

Star looked up.

She smiled.

But...

Only with her mouth.

Her eyes remained empty.

---

### Star

> "...Hi."

---

Nightshade immediately noticed something was wrong.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Are you alright?"

---

Star answered after several seconds.

---

> "...I don't know."

---

Within hours...

Her personality changed dramatically.

---

# **IV. THE LOSS OF POSITIVITY**

Medical evaluations quickly reached an impossible conclusion.

Star's optimism...

Was gone.

Not reduced.

Not hidden.

Gone.

---

For most people...

Losing optimism would be distressing.

For Star...

It was catastrophic.

Her relentless positivity had never existed because life was easy.

It existed because she had learned, over years of hardship, to keep moving forward despite overwhelming negative emotions.

With that positivity suddenly absent...

Everything it had been holding back surfaced at once.

---

She stopped smiling.

Stopped laughing.

Stopped initiating conversations.

Meals were left unfinished.

Training sessions were cancelled.

She answered questions politely.

Briefly.

Then fell silent again.

---

# **V. WEEK ONE**

The Blacksite changed.

Not because Star stopped working.

She continued every assignment flawlessly.

But...

Without the warmth everyone had grown accustomed to.

---

### Researcher

"...Morning."

---

### Star

"...Morning."

---

Nothing more.

---

An intern accidentally dropped several documents.

Normally...

Star would have helped while making a joke.

Instead...

She silently picked them up.

Returned them.

Walked away.

---

The intern later reported:

> "...She wasn't angry."

> "...She just looked..."

> "...Tired."

---

# **VI. NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade remained beside Star whenever operationally possible.

One evening...

She found Star staring out a window overlooking the mountains.

Nearly forty minutes passed before either spoke.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Beautiful view."

---

Star nodded.

---

### Star

> "...It is."

---

Silence returned.

---

### Nightshade

> "...What are you thinking?"

---

Star answered quietly.

---

> "...Nothing good."

---

Nightshade waited.

---

Star continued.

---

> "...I keep wondering..."

A pause.

---

> "...Whether everyone only tolerated me because I smiled all the time."

---

Nightshade immediately turned toward her.

---

### Nightshade

> "...No."

---

Star didn't argue.

She simply looked back toward the mountains.

---

# **VII. MEDICAL FINDINGS**

Psychological evaluation revealed something extraordinary.

Star's emotional range remained intact.

She could still feel happiness.

Relief.

Affection.

Comfort.

But...

The mechanism that instinctively guided her toward hopeful interpretations and resilient emotional recovery had been almost completely suppressed.

Negative thoughts no longer encountered their usual counterbalance.

They lingered.

Accumulated.

Repeated.

---

Dr. Amelia summarized it as follows:

> "Imagine removing a dam without removing the river behind it."

---

# **VIII. ASTER**

Aster remained completely unaware.

She continued visiting Star every day.

Bringing toys.

Writing cheerful messages.

Leaving tiny flowers she had found around the facility.

Star always thanked her.

Softly.

Kindly.

But the usual sparkle in her eyes never returned.

---

One afternoon...

Aster wrote:

> **You look sad.**

---

Star looked at the words.

Held the whiteboard for several seconds.

Finally...

She answered.

---

> "...I think I am."

---

Aster gently rubbed against Star's arm.

Confused.

Concerned.

Unable to understand why nothing she tried made Star smile.

---

# **IX. INVESTIGATION**

N.O.S.T. ruled out:

- Memetic hazards.
    
- Psychic attacks.
    
- Reality corruption.
    
- Divine interference.
    
- Whitefall anomalies.
    
- Banlands influence.
    

The only consistent variable...

Was Aster.

Repeated scans eventually detected faint anomalous signatures surrounding her.

Tiny.

Nearly impossible to detect.

But matching both affected individuals.

---

The horrifying implication became unavoidable.

Aster had somehow...

Accidentally suppressed parts of other people's identities.

Without realizing she possessed the ability.

Without intending harm.

---

# **X. STATUS – END OF WEEK ONE**

Affected Personnel:

**Neko**

- Creativity absent.
    
- Engineering competency unchanged.
    
- Innovation impossible.
    
- Emotional distress moderate but stable.
    

**Star**

- Positivity almost entirely suppressed.
    
- Daily functioning maintained.
    
- Significant emotional decline observed.
    
- Continuous support from Nightshade, Miss Neko, and Medical authorized.
    

**Aster**

- Physically healthy.
    
- Completely unaware of the connection.
    
- Under close observation by N.O.S.T.
    

No known method exists to reverse the phenomenon.

Research is ongoing.

Director Lucious concluded the first week's report with a handwritten note:

> **"The cruelest part is that she didn't take away who they are. She accidentally took away the very things that helped them carry who they are."**

**Investigation continues.**
