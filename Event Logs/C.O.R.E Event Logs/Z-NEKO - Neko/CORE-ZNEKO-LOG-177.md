# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-177**

### **THE ENTIRE BAG**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – MEDICAL INCIDENT / PERSONNEL NOTICE

---

## **I. INCIDENT SUMMARY**

Despite repeated warnings...

Despite Medical Division's standing orders...

Despite every reminder from Miss Neko, Mirage, Star, Nightshade, and Oracle...

Neko somehow managed to obtain **an entire bag of roasted peanuts**.

Security footage later showed him quietly sitting alone in an Engineering break room.

He looked around.

Opened the bag.

Paused for a moment.

---

### Neko

> "...Just this once..."

---

He proceeded to eat...

The entire bag.

---

## **II. DISCOVERY**

Approximately three minutes later...

An engineer entered the room.

He immediately noticed the empty peanut bag lying on the table.

Then...

He noticed Neko.

His breathing had become labored.

His face was turning pale.

One hand clutched his throat.

The other pressed against his chest.

---

### Engineer

> "...Neko?"

---

Neko tried to answer.

No sound came out.

He collapsed onto the floor.

---

### Engineer

> "**MEDICAL!!**"

---

## **III. ANAPHYLAXIS**

Medical arrived within moments.

Symptoms progressed far more rapidly than during the previous incident.

Observed symptoms included:

- Complete airway swelling.
    
- Severe respiratory distress.
    
- Generalized allergic reaction.
    
- Critically unstable heart rhythm.
    
- Loss of consciousness.
    

Immediate treatment consisted of:

- Emergency epinephrine.
    
- Oxygen support.
    
- Intravenous medication.
    
- Defibrillation after cardiac arrest.
    
- Continuous cardiac monitoring.
    

After several tense minutes...

A heartbeat returned.

Breathing slowly stabilized.

Neko survived.

Again.

---

## **IV. THE EMPTY BAG**

Medical later recovered the evidence.

One empty peanut bag.

Completely empty.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...He ate..."

_A pause._

> "...The whole bag?"

---

An engineer silently nodded.

The room collectively sighed.

---

## **V. MISS NEKO**

Several hours later...

Neko regained consciousness.

The first person he saw...

Was Miss Neko.

She wasn't angry.

She wasn't shouting.

She simply looked...

Deeply disappointed.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro."

---

Neko looked away.

---

### Neko

> "...Sorry..."

---

Miss Neko gently placed the empty peanut bag on the bedside table.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Why?"

---

A long silence followed.

---

### Neko

Quietly...

> "...I really like peanuts..."

---

Miss Neko closed her eyes.

Let out a slow sigh.

Then gently rested a hand on his head.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...I know."

---

Another pause.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...But I like having my little brother alive."

---

Neko couldn't find anything to say.

---

## **VI. MIRAGE**

Mirage had remained silent the entire time.

He finally walked over.

Picked up the empty bag.

Looked at it for several seconds.

Then looked back at Neko.

---

### Mirage

> "...You knew."

---

Neko nodded.

---

### Mirage

> "...You remembered."

---

Another nod.

---

### Mirage

Softly...

> "...And you still did it."

---

Neko quietly answered.

---

### Neko

> "...I thought..."

He stopped.

Shook his head.

---

> "...I don't know what I thought."

---

Mirage simply set the bag back down.

---

### Mirage

> "...I'm disappointed."

---

Those three words hurt far more than any lecture could have.

---

## **VII. ORACLE**

Oracle entered the room carrying her marker.

She looked at Neko.

Then at the empty peanut bag.

She didn't write immediately.

She simply sat beside the bed.

For nearly a minute.

Finally...

She picked up her marker.

Carefully wrote:

> **I warned you.**

Neko nodded.

---

### Neko

> "...You did."

---

She added another sentence.

> **Please don't scare us again.**

Neko reached over.

Gently scratched behind her ears.

---

### Neko

> "...I'll try."

---

Oracle erased the last sentence.

Then rewrote it.

Larger this time.

> **Promise.**

After a long silence...

Neko looked at Miss Neko.

At Mirage.

At Oracle.

---

### Neko

> "...Promise."

---

Oracle put the marker away.

Curled up beside him.

And quietly fell asleep.

---

## **VIII. NEW SAFETY PROTOCOL**

Following the incident, Director Lucious approved additional precautions.

Effective immediately:

- Peanuts and peanut-containing snacks are no longer permitted in Engineering.
    
- Cafeteria staff are instructed not to serve peanut products to Neko under any circumstances.
    
- If Neko is seen attempting to obtain peanuts, personnel are authorized to confiscate the food immediately.
    
- Oracle's warnings regarding peanuts are to be treated with the same priority as her life-threatening danger alerts.
    

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Medical Division confirmed that Neko has made a full recovery from his second severe anaphylactic episode.

Psychological Services noted that the most significant impact was not the medical emergency itself, but the realization that his decision had frightened those closest to him. Miss Neko and Mirage, who rarely express disappointment, were visibly shaken by how close they came to losing him over something entirely preventable.

A handwritten note now hangs above the Engineering snack cabinet. It was written by Neko himself.

> **"Yes, peanuts are still my favorite. No, I am not allowed to have them. If you catch me trying, please stop me—even if I complain. I want to make it home with everyone."**
