# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-182**

### **TELEPORTATION INSTRUCTION SUCCESS**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – N.O.S.T. / TRAINING RECORD

---

## **I. SUMMARY**

Following several weeks of discussion, Star and Mirage volunteered to teach Neko and Oracle the fundamentals of short-range anomalous teleportation.

The training was conducted under controlled conditions inside N.O.S.T.'s Spatial Testing Chamber.

Director Lucious approved the exercise with one condition:

> **"Nobody teleports into a wall."**

Fortunately...

Nobody did.

---

## **II. THE INSTRUCTORS**

The lesson was divided between the two instructors.

**Star** focused on theory.

She explained:

- Spatial awareness.
    
- Destination visualization.
    
- Momentum conservation.
    
- Safety procedures.
    

Meanwhile...

**Mirage** handled practical demonstrations.

Being naturally capable of teleportation, he repeatedly vanished and reappeared around the room.

Sometimes behind people.

Sometimes on top of crates.

Sometimes directly beside Oracle just to make her jump.

---

### Oracle

> "Mew!"

---

Mirage smiled innocently.

---

### Mirage

> "...Sorry."

He wasn't.

---

## **III. NEKO**

Neko understood the theory surprisingly quickly.

The practical side...

Not so much.

---

### Star

> "...Picture where you want to be."

---

### Neko

> "...Okay."

---

A puff of thick white smoke erupted.

Everyone expected him to disappear.

Instead...

He remained exactly where he was.

---

### Neko

> "...Did it work?"

---

### Mirage

> "...No."

---

Several more attempts followed.

One produced another cloud of smoke.

One caused his hair to stand on end.

One somehow teleported...

His wrench.

But not him.

---

### Neko

> "...Progress?"

---

### Star

> "...Technically."

---

## **IV. ORACLE**

Oracle approached the lesson differently.

She quietly watched every demonstration.

Then...

She picked up her marker.

Wrote:

> **Like jumping?**

---

### Star

> "...Sort of."

---

Oracle nodded.

Closed her eyes.

Focused.

For a brief instant...

She disappeared.

A tiny flash of white light filled the room.

She reappeared...

Exactly twenty centimeters away.

---

Silence.

---

### Mirage

> "...She actually did it."

---

Oracle proudly wrote:

> **Yes.**

---

## **V. BREAKTHROUGH**

Inspired by Oracle's success...

Neko tried again.

He concentrated.

Visualized the destination.

Took a deep breath.

White smoke engulfed him.

This time...

He vanished.

Less than a second later...

He appeared safely on the opposite side of the room.

There was complete silence.

Neko looked around.

---

### Neko

> "...I did it?"

---

### Star

Smiling.

> "...You did."

---

Mirage gave him a thumbs-up.

---

### Mirage

> "...Nice."

---

Neko couldn't stop smiling.

---

## **VI. RAPID IMPROVEMENT**

Over the following hours...

Both students improved considerably.

Neko learned to:

- Teleport short distances.
    
- Maintain orientation.
    
- Carry small tools during teleportation.
    
- Arrive without immediately stumbling.
    

Oracle learned to:

- Teleport onto tables.
    
- Teleport onto chairs.
    
- Teleport into Neko's lap.
    

The last quickly became her favorite.

---

### Neko

> "...You're getting good."

---

Oracle proudly wrote:

> **Fast kitty.**

---

## **VII. MIRAGE'S TEST**

Mirage prepared one final exercise.

He placed four identical boxes around the room.

Only one contained a toy mouse for Oracle.

---

### Mirage

> "...Ready?"

---

Oracle nodded.

Mirage teleported the boxes into different positions.

Without hesitation...

Oracle teleported once.

Twice.

Three times.

Stopped beside the correct box.

Opened it.

Retrieved the toy mouse.

---

### Mirage

> "...Perfect."

---

Oracle proudly carried the toy around the room for the next ten minutes.

---

## **VIII. STAR'S OBSERVATION**

Star later summarized the lesson.

---

### Star

> "...Neko thinks before he teleports."

---

She looked toward Oracle.

Who had just teleported onto Mirage's shoulder for absolutely no reason.

---

### Star

> "...Oracle teleports because she can."

---

Mirage laughed.

Oracle simply wrote:

> **Yes.**

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

N.O.S.T. has confirmed that both Neko and Oracle have successfully acquired safe, short-range teleportation capabilities.

Neko's teleportation manifests as a burst of thick white smoke before he reappears at his destination, reflecting the same visual phenomenon seen during his transformations.

Oracle's teleportation is quieter, marked only by a brief white flash and a soft _pop_, allowing her to move with remarkable precision despite her small size.

Both are currently limited to short-range movement, with additional training planned before longer-range applications are authorized.

Director Lucious added the following note to the training report:

> **"Congratulations. We have successfully taught an engineer and a future-seeing kitten how to ignore doors."**
