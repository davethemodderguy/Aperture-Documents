# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-127**

### **THE RETURN**

### **Classification:** INTERNAL – N.O.S.T. / BANLANDS LIAISON

---

## **I. WHITEFALL LAKE**

The snow had remained undisturbed for three days.

Medical staff maintained a constant watch.

N.O.S.T. instruments continued recording the strange energy flowing through Whitefall's snow.

At 08:37...

The monitors changed.

---

### Medical Technician

> "...Director."

---

The heart monitor remained steady.

But another reading...

One that had remained flat for weeks...

Suddenly began rising.

Neurological activity.

Not erratic.

Not unstable.

Normal.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...Impossible..."

---

Before anyone could react—

A small movement.

One finger twitched beneath the snow.

Then another.

---

Miss Neko froze.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro...?"

---

Slowly...

Very slowly...

Neko opened his eyes.

Their familiar sapphire color had returned.

Not as brilliantly luminous as before.

But alive.

The blackness was gone.

---

He blinked several times.

Looked up at the falling snow.

Then smiled weakly.

---

### Neko

> "...Cold..."

---

Miss Neko laughed and cried at the same time.

She immediately hugged him, careful not to squeeze too tightly.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Welcome back, lil bro..."

---

Star burst into tears.

Nightshade quietly looked away, pretending to inspect the snowfall.

Niko simply covered his face with one hand, laughing in relief.

Even Director Lucious allowed himself a small smile.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Welcome home."

---

## **II. THE FIRST QUESTION**

After Medical confirmed that Neko was stable enough to speak, everyone gathered around.

The Lead Medical Researcher leaned forward.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...Can you remember anything?"

---

Neko nodded.

---

### Neko

> "...Everything."

---

The room became silent.

---

### Niko

> "...Everything?"

---

### Neko

> "...While I was asleep..."

---

He looked at everyone.

---

### Neko

> "...I heard all of you."

---

Star immediately covered her mouth.

Snowy's journal had been right.

---

### Star

> "...You really heard us..."

---

Neko smiled gently.

---

### Neko

> "...Every morning."

---

He looked toward Miss Neko.

---

### Neko

> "...You never missed a day."

---

She couldn't answer.

She simply hugged him again.

---

## **III. THE BANLANDS**

Later that evening...

Director Lucious requested a formal debrief.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Where were you?"

---

Neko thought quietly.

---

### Neko

> "...Not here."

---

Another pause.

---

### Neko

> "...I was in the Banlands."

---

Every N.O.S.T. officer in the room looked up.

---

### Researcher

> "...The Banlands?"

---

Neko nodded.

---

### Neko

> "...I walked."

---

### Nightshade

> "...Walked?"

---

### Neko

> "...Didn't really have another choice."

---

### Niko

> "...What did you see?"

---

Neko smiled faintly.

---

### Neko

> "...Someone waiting."

---

## **IV. THE DEVIL**

He described her calmly.

Not as a monster.

Not as a ruler seated upon a throne.

Simply...

A woman.

Around twenty-five years old.

Wearing a perfectly pressed uniform that looked strangely modern against the Banlands' bleak landscape.

Professional.

Calm.

Almost... ordinary.

Entirely out of place.

---

### Neko

> "...She introduced herself."

---

### Neko

> "...She said..."

---

> "...'People here just call me **The Devil**.'"

---

The room remained silent.

---

### Neko

> "...She already knew who I was."

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Did she identify herself as our Banlands liaison?"

---

### Neko

> "...No."

---

### Neko

> "...She just said she'd worked with Aperture for years."

---

Director Lucious frowned.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...I was never notified."

---

Neko shrugged weakly.

---

### Neko

> "...She said..."

---

He remembered her exact words.

---

> **"I didn't tell Lucious."**

> **"You came here as a guest, not as an incident report."**

---

The room exchanged puzzled looks.

---

## **V. ONE MORE REQUEST**

Neko's expression changed.

The smile disappeared.

---

### Neko

> "...Then I remembered..."

---

He lowered his head.

---

### Neko

> "...The engineer."

---

Silence.

---

### Neko

> "...I asked if I could see her."

---

The Devil reportedly looked at him for several moments.

Then simply asked—

---

> **"Why?"**

---

### Neko

> "...Because she thought I blamed her."

---

Another silence.

---

### Neko

> "...I never did."

---

According to Neko...

The Devil sighed.

Closed a folder resting on her desk.

Then looked directly at him.

---

> **"You're an unusual engineer."**

---

### Neko

> "...Maybe."

---

She stood.

Walked toward a distant doorway.

Opened it.

---

> **"Go tell her yourself."**

---

## **VI. THE CONVERSATION**

Neko never described the conversation in detail.

Only its ending.

---

### Neko

> "...I told her exactly what I'd said before."

---

> "...It wasn't your fault."

---

> "...You saved people."

---

> "...Please... come home."

---

He looked toward the window.

---

### Neko

> "...She cried."

---

Another pause.

---

### Neko

> "...Then she agreed."

---

## **VII. AN UNEXPECTED ARRIVAL**

Less than an hour after Neko awoke...

A N.O.S.T. gateway activated inside Whitefall Lake.

Security prepared for an emergency.

Instead...

A lone figure stepped through.

Maintenance coveralls.

Familiar face.

The engineer.

Alive.

Confused.

Looking around as though waking from a dream.

---

She saw Neko standing nearby.

Healthy.

Smiling.

---

She immediately burst into tears.

---

### Engineer

> "...You're..."

---

### Neko

> "...Hi."

---

She ran toward him.

Stopped just short of knocking him over.

Remembering he had only just recovered.

---

### Engineer

> "...I'm so sorry..."

---

Neko smiled.

The same gentle smile she'd remembered.

---

### Neko

> "...I know."

---

### Engineer

> "...I thought..."

---

### Neko

> "...I know."

---

He gently hugged her.

---

### Neko

> "...Welcome back."

---

## **VIII. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Director Lucious watched the reunion quietly.

Then looked toward the still-open Banlands gateway.

It closed without anyone else emerging.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...She still didn't file the paperwork."

---

Niko blinked.

---

### Niko

> "...That's what you're concerned about?"

---

Lucious gave the faintest smile.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...I'm relieved."

---

He looked toward Neko.

Then toward the engineer.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Today..."

---

He nodded once.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...We got two people back."

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Whitefall Lake restored Neko's life.

His unexplained "sleep" was later understood not as death, nor coma, but as a journey through the Banlands while his body remained alive.

The Banlands' liaison known only as **The Devil** neither requested payment nor offered an explanation for allowing Neko—and later the engineer—to return. She also did not notify Director Lucious of Neko's presence, despite the longstanding working relationship between Aperture Industries and the Banlands.

Research was unable to explain why this exception was made.

No one pressed the matter further.

For the first time in many weeks, the Blacksite cafeteria was full again.

Maintenance laughed.

Engineering argued over blueprints.

Star served soup that actually tasted good.

Nightshade complained about it anyway.

Miss Neko refused to let her younger brother out of her sight for the rest of the day.

And the engineer, now sitting quietly among her coworkers, looked around the room with tears in her eyes.

Everyone understood the same thing.

They had gone to Whitefall hoping to save one life.

They came home having saved two.
