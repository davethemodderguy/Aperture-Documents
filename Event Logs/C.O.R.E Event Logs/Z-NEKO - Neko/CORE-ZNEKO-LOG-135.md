# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-135**

### **A DAY EVERYONE MISSED**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE / INCIDENT LOG

---

## **I. DATE**

It was an ordinary workday.

Maintenance was handling emergency repairs following a coolant system inspection.

Research was preparing for a high-clearance containment transfer.

Security was conducting a site-wide audit.

Engineering had fallen behind schedule after several unexpected equipment failures.

The Blacksite was alive with activity.

No one noticed the date.

---

## **II. NEKO**

Neko clocked in on time.

---

### Guard

> "Morning, Neko."

---

### Neko

> "Morning."

---

He smiled politely and headed toward Engineering.

There was nothing unusual about him.

He repaired pipes.

He recalibrated machinery.

He helped Lucy replace a damaged valve.

He answered questions from interns.

He quietly fixed a printer that no one had asked him to fix.

Just another day.

---

Several times throughout the day, he instinctively looked toward the entrance to Engineering.

As if expecting someone.

Nobody came.

Not because they didn't care.

Because everyone assumed they'd have time later.

---

## **III. EVENING**

His shift ended.

The corridors had grown quieter.

Neko stopped by the cafeteria.

Most of the food had already been cleared away.

He found a leftover sandwich in the refrigerator.

A small carton of milk.

He carried them back to his room.

---

On the way, he passed the common room.

Someone had decorated it for an upcoming department celebration.

Colorful streamers hung from the ceiling.

A half-inflated balloon drifted lazily near a window.

He paused for only a second.

Then kept walking.

---

## **IV. ALONE**

His room was quiet.

Miss Neko had been called away to assist with an overnight engineering project.

Star and Nightshade were still supporting Research.

Niko had not yet left his office.

Lucy was helping Maintenance finish emergency repairs.

For the first time since returning from Whitefall...

The room belonged to him alone.

---

He placed the sandwich on the desk.

Opened the milk.

Looked out the window.

Then quietly spoke to himself.

---

### Neko

> "...Happy birthday."

---

There was no audience.

No laughter.

No candles.

No cake.

Just the faint hum of the Blacksite ventilation.

---

He took a bite of the sandwich.

Finished the milk.

Turned off the light.

And lay down.

---

## **V. THE THOUGHTS**

Sleep didn't come immediately.

He stared at the ceiling.

The words he had been pushing away for weeks returned.

---

> _Maybe everyone's happier without me bothering them._

> _They're all busy._

> _I shouldn't interrupt._

> _Maybe... I'm just not important enough to remember._

---

He squeezed his blanket a little tighter.

---

### Neko

_(barely a whisper)_

> "...It's okay..."

> "...Work comes first."

---

The room fell silent.

---

Outside, the moonlight reached his face.

Behind the lenses of his glasses...

The sapphire glow in his eyes had become faint.

Not extinguished.

Just...

Very small.

---

## **VI. 23:58**

Meanwhile...

Lucy finally finished in Maintenance.

She stretched.

Checked the date on her terminal.

Her expression froze.

---

### Lucy

> "...No..."

---

She immediately grabbed her communicator.

---

### Lucy

> "Niko!"

---

Within seconds—

Niko checked his calendar.

His eyes widened.

---

### Niko

> "...Today's..."

---

Star heard the conversation.

She dropped the tray she was carrying.

---

### Star

> "...His birthday..."

---

Nightshade looked at the clock.

---

**23:59**

---

### Nightshade

> "...Run."

---

## **VII. TOO LATE**

Doors opened throughout the Blacksite.

Footsteps echoed through the corridors.

Lucy.

Niko.

Miss Neko.

Star.

Nightshade.

Several engineers.

Maintenance staff.

Even Director Lucious left his office.

One by one, they arrived outside Neko's room.

Lucy gently opened the door.

The room was dark.

Neko was already asleep.

Curled beneath his blanket.

His empty milk carton rested on the bedside table beside the sandwich wrapper.

On the desk...

A small sticky note.

Written in his familiar handwriting.

---

> **"Everyone looked really busy today."**

> **"I'll celebrate next year."**

---

Nobody spoke.

---

The clock on the wall quietly changed.

---

**00:00**

---

A new day.

His birthday was over.

---

Star covered her mouth.

Tears filled her eyes.

---

### Star

> "...We forgot..."

---

Lucy lowered her head.

---

### Lucy

> "...He spent the whole day alone..."

---

Niko looked at the untouched room.

---

### Niko

> "...He didn't even remind us."

---

Nightshade clenched her fists.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Of course he didn't."

---

Miss Neko slowly walked to the bedside.

She brushed a few strands of hair away from Neko's forehead.

His breathing was calm.

Peaceful.

But when she looked through the lenses of his glasses...

She saw it.

The glow in his sapphire eyes, visible even through his closed eyelids, had dimmed to its weakest point since Whitefall.

She whispered so quietly that only those nearest could hear.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...I'm sorry, lil bro..."

---

## **VIII. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Lucious stood silently in the doorway.

He looked around the room.

No decorations.

No presents.

No celebration.

Only a tired young engineer asleep after an ordinary day's work.

He spoke without raising his voice.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...No one intended this."

---

He paused.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...But intentions don't change what happened."

---

No one argued.

Because he was right.

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Medical personnel later noted another measurable reduction in the anomalous luminosity of Neko's eyes.

Physical examinations remained completely normal.

The change was attributed solely to emotional state.

No disciplinary action was issued.

No individual was blamed.

The missed birthday was the result of an exceptionally demanding day in which every department believed there would be time later.

There wasn't.

The final entry in the incident log read:

> **"Sometimes the greatest failures are not caused by malice or negligence, but by everyone believing someone else will have a moment to spare."**

And that night, while the Blacksite finally fell quiet, the busiest place in the facility was not Engineering or Maintenance.

It was the hallway outside one small room, where people who genuinely cared arrived only after the day they wanted to celebrate had already slipped away.
