# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-131**

### **POST-REVIVAL RECOVERY PROTOCOL – NEKO**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – MEDICAL / PERSONNEL DIRECTIVE  
**Applies To:** All Blacksite Personnel

---

## **I. PURPOSE**

Following Neko's successful revival through the **Whitefall Protocol**, Medical Division has determined that while his condition is stable and he has returned to active duty, his metabolism has **not yet fully recovered**.

Although fully conscious and mentally unimpaired, Neko currently exhibits physical symptoms comparable to those experienced during his previous periods of illness. Personnel are therefore instructed to provide reasonable assistance during daily operations until Medical Division declares his recovery complete.

---

## **II. CURRENT CONDITION**

Neko is currently capable of performing normal engineering, research, and maintenance duties.

However, personnel should expect the following:

- Reduced stamina.
    
- General fatigue.
    
- Slower physical performance.
    
- Occasional dizziness.
    
- Increased susceptibility to exhaustion.
    
- Possibility of sudden fainting without significant warning.
    

Despite these limitations, Neko has repeatedly insisted that he is capable of working.

Medical Division has determined that **he is only partially correct.**

---

## **III. FAINTING RESPONSE PROCEDURE**

Should Neko suddenly lose consciousness, personnel are instructed to remain calm and follow the procedures below.

1. Confirm that Neko is breathing normally.
    
2. Check for injuries sustained during the fall.
    
3. Notify nearby medical personnel whenever reasonably possible.
    
4. If no injuries are present and immediate medical intervention is unnecessary, personnel are authorized to transport Neko via **piggyback carry**, a method explicitly approved by Neko himself.
    
5. Personnel may continue their assigned duties while carrying him, provided doing so does not:
    
    - interfere with critical operations,
        
    - endanger themselves,
        
    - or place Neko at additional risk.
        
6. Upon regaining consciousness:
    
    - Provide water.
        
    - Allow several minutes of rest.
        
    - Determine whether he is fit to resume work.
        
    - Notify Medical if recovery appears abnormal.
        

---

## **IV. GLACIER SNEEZE STATUS**

Medical observation has confirmed that Neko's previous anomalous **"Glacier Sneeze"** has **completely ceased** following his revival.

As a result:

- Ice-cold beverages are fully permitted.
    
- Frozen desserts are permitted.
    
- Chilled foods are permitted.
    
- Refrigerated drinks require no supervision.
    

Personnel are no longer required to confiscate or restrict cold food or beverages.

Medical Division has classified the phenomenon as:

> **Resolved.**

Several maintenance engineers reportedly celebrated this announcement by immediately buying Neko an ice cream.

Medical approved.

---

## **V. ESCORT REQUIREMENT**

Until further notice, **Neko is not to be left unattended** while on duty.

Whenever reasonably possible, a designated companion should accompany him to ensure rapid assistance if his condition deteriorates unexpectedly.

Recommended escorts, in order of preference:

1. **Lucy**
    
2. **Niko**
    
3. **Miss Neko**
    
4. **Star**
    
5. **Nightshade**
    

If none of the above personnel are available, another trusted employee familiar with Neko's medical condition may temporarily assume escort responsibilities.

---

## **VI. SELF-ASSESSMENT WARNING**

Personnel are reminded that Neko has developed the habit of minimizing his own condition.

Examples include, but are not limited to:

> "I'm fine."

> "It's just a little dizzy."

> "I'll sit down later."

> "One more pipe."

> "I can finish this first."

These statements are **not** considered valid medical evaluations.

If obvious signs of fatigue, instability, or illness are observed, personnel are authorized to encourage immediate rest and contact Medical if necessary.

---

## **VII. RECOVERY GUIDELINES**

Until full metabolic recovery is achieved, the following precautions remain in effect:

- Scheduled rest breaks throughout each shift.
    
- Avoid unnecessary physical exertion.
    
- Maintain adequate hydration.
    
- Ensure regular meals and sufficient caloric intake.
    
- Report repeated fainting, worsening weakness, or any unexplained symptoms to Medical immediately.
    

---

## **IX. COMPANION NOTES**

### **Lucy**

Has unofficially become Neko's primary escort.

She is most frequently observed carrying him piggyback after fainting episodes, ensuring he rests, reminding him to eat, and quietly ignoring his repeated insistence that he "doesn't need the help."

---

### **Niko**

Regularly checks on Neko during Engineering work and has become adept at recognizing signs of exhaustion before Neko himself notices them.

---

### **Miss Neko**

Refers to him almost exclusively as **"Lil bro."**

She has been observed effortlessly picking him up whenever he begins to look unsteady.

When questioned, her response is consistently:

> "He's staying with me until he stops pretending he's okay."

---

### **Star**

Frequently supplies snacks, warm meals, and drinks.

Medical Division credits Star with significantly improving Neko's nutritional recovery simply because he rarely refuses food she offers.

---

### **Nightshade**

Claims she is "only making sure he doesn't collapse into expensive equipment."

Personnel generally agree this is her preferred way of saying she cares.

---

## **X. FINAL REMARKS**

Director Lucious approved this directive with the following handwritten note:

> **"Neko has returned to us once already. We will not lose him a second time because he refused to admit he was tired."**

Accordingly, all personnel are reminded that Neko's health takes priority over routine productivity. If he falls asleep on your shoulder, faints into a piggyback ride, or insists he can "finish one last repair," staff are expected to exercise sound judgment—even if that means carrying one of the Blacksite's most brilliant engineers through the corridors until he wakes up.

After all, as one maintenance engineer summarized:

> **"He's light, he snores quietly, and he still tells us how to fix the pipes while asleep. We've carried heavier toolboxes."**
