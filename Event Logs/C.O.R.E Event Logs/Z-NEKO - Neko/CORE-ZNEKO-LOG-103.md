## **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Neko retains Snowy's unusual "throwability" in both his human and cat forms, but this is treated more as a trusted game among close friends than anything reckless. Everyone handling him does so gently and only with his clear permission.

The leave day ended without incident.

Which, according to several senior staff members, was the most remarkable event of all.
