## **I. A CRITICAL FAILURE**

**Time:** 14:26

The unofficial Blacksite policy had been simple.

> **If Neko says he's bored...**
> 
> **Occupy him immediately.**

Normally this worked.

Maintenance would hand him a broken coolant pump.

Star would challenge him to a board game.

Miss Neko would drag him into Engineering.

Niko would ask for help calibrating something.

Someone would inevitably pick him up and carry him somewhere.

Anything.

Anything except letting him think.

---

Unfortunately...

At exactly 14:26...

Everyone was busy.

---

Maintenance was replacing an entire turbine.

Research had a containment inspection.

Security was responding to a false alarm.

Miss Neko had been called into a meeting.

## **XI. MAINTENANCE DIVISION**

Within a week, Glacier Spray became standard emergency equipment throughout the Blacksite.

It proved exceptionally effective for:

- Sealing ruptured gas and coolant pipes.
    
- Creating temporary airtight barriers during hazardous leaks.
    
- Reinforcing damaged doors and walls until permanent repairs could be made.
    
- Isolating contaminated sections of corridors.
    
- Rapidly plugging hull breaches in test chambers.
    

Maintenance crews were delighted.

The only restriction issued by Director Lucious was simple:

> **"Do not spray coworkers."**

This rule was added after one engineer jokingly suggested using Glacier Spray to stop a colleague from talking during lunch.

Lucy immediately confiscated the bottle before the idea could be tested.

---

## **XII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Unlike many of Neko's inventions born from boredom, **Glacier Spray** required no special containment, generated no anomalous radiation, and posed no existential threat to the Blacksite.

It was, by Aperture Industries' standards, almost refreshingly practical.

Director Lucious nevertheless amended the standing policy with one additional line:

> **"If Neko becomes bored, occupy him promptly. While the results may occasionally be beneficial, the Blacksite should not rely on luck to determine whether his next invention is an emergency repair tool... or another handheld terraforming device."**
