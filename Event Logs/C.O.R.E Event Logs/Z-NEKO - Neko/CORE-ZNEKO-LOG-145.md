## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Behavioral specialists noted that several pages differed from ordinary diary entries.

Rather than recording events, they appeared to document an internal pattern of reasoning:

- A positive interpretation of an interaction.
    
- Immediate self-correction.
    
- Replacement with a more negative explanation.
    
- Reinforcement of the belief: **"I slow people down."**
    

The notebook does not allow any definitive conclusion about Neko's internal emotional state, but it does provide a record of the thoughts he chose to write down. Staff were cautioned not to assume that every written belief reflected objective reality, nor that every crossed-out hope reflected the truth of how others felt.

The notebook was returned without alteration.

Its final reviewed page contained no engineering calculations.

No diagrams.

No inventions.

Only a short exchange written by one person to himself:

> **I think everyone wants to spend time with me.**

A single line crossed through it.

Then, underneath:

> **I slow people down.**
