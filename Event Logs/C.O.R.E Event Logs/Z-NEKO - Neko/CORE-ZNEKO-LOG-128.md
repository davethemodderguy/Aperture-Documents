# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

### **THE NAME THAT RETURNED**

### **Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL / BANLANDS AFTERMATH

## **I. FULL RECOVERY**

Two days after Whitefall Lake stabilized Neko’s condition, Medical Division issued a final confirmation.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> “Neurological stability: 100%.”

> “Anomalous suppression: resolved.”

> “Subject is fully operational.”

---

The room stayed quiet for a moment.

Then—

---

### Research Assistant

> “…So he’s actually back?”

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> “He’s back.”

---

From outside the Medical Wing, a crash echoed.

Miss Neko had hugged him too tightly again.

---

## **II. BACK ON DUTY**

By morning, Neko was already walking the corridors.

Barefooted confidence in his step.

Glasses properly adjusted.

Bangs slightly messy again.

Collar back in place.

The same familiar expression that made half the Engineering Division nervous and the other half relieved.

---

### Engineer

> “Should he really be back this soon?”

---

### Niko

> “He’s already trying to audit coolant systems.”

---

### Engineer

> “…Yeah, that sounds like him.”

---

In Armory, someone immediately locked away three half-finished prototypes.

Just in case.

---

## **III. THE ENGINEER RETURNS**

Lucy was officially reinstated under Medical Override Clearance.

She insisted she was fine.

No one believed her.

No one argued either.

She simply sat in Engineering for the first time since returning from the Banlands, quietly repairing a broken calibration rig.

Hands steady.

Eyes tired.

Alive.

---

Neko approached her without ceremony.

---

### Neko

> “Hey.”

---

She froze.

Slowly turned.

---

### Lucy

> “…Hi.”

---

A long silence followed.

Not uncomfortable.

Just… heavy.

---

### Lucy

> “I’m sorry.”

---

### Neko

> “You already said that.”

---

### Lucy

> “I mean it.”

---

Neko leaned lightly against the workbench.

---

### Neko

> “I know.”

---

Then, after a pause—

---

### Neko

> “You’re still bad at taking care of yourself.”

---

Lucy let out a shaky laugh.

---

### Lucy

> “You’re still bad at not getting hospitalized.”

---

That got a small smile out of him.

---

## **IV. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Later that day, Lucious requested Lucy’s formal report from Banlands exposure.

She handed it over.

He didn’t open it immediately.

Instead, he looked at her name tag.

---

**LUCY**

---

His hand paused.

For a second too long.

---

### Lucy

> “…Is something wrong, Director?”

---

Lucious didn’t answer right away.

When he did, his voice was quieter than usual.

---

### Director Lucious

> “No.”

---

Another pause.

---

### Director Lucious

> “…It’s just a name I haven’t heard in a long time.”

---

Lucy tilted her head slightly.

---

### Lucy

> “Lucy… is common, isn’t it?”

---

Lucious gave a faint, distant smile.

---

### Director Lucious

> “It was.”

---

He closed the file without reading it.

---

## **V. THE MEMORY**

That night, Lucious remained in his office longer than usual.

Old archives were pulled up.

Pre-Aperture records.

College enrollment logs.

Photographs from a life before Blacksites, anomalies, and containment protocols.

And there—

A younger version of him.

Messy hair.

Simple clothing.

Standing beside a girl no older than her late teens.

Laughing.

Her name, written on the photo’s metadata:

> **LUCY**

---

## **VI. THE RETURNED NAME**

The next morning, Lucy was called into Director Lucious’s office.

She entered cautiously.

He did not look at her immediately.

When he finally spoke—

---

### Director Lucious

> “Where were you before the Banlands?”

---

### Lucy

> “I worked maintenance before I joined Aperture.”

---

Lucious nodded slowly.

---

### Director Lucious

> “Before that.”

---

Lucy hesitated.

---

### Lucy

> “…I don’t remember everything clearly from that far back.”

---

Silence.

---

Then Lucious placed something on the desk.

A photograph.

Worn at the edges.

But unmistakable.

---

Lucy stared at it.

Her expression didn’t change immediately.

Then her breath caught.

---

### Lucy

> “…That’s…”

---

She touched the photo carefully.

---

### Lucy

> “…me.”

---

Lucious finally looked at her directly.

---

### Director Lucious

> “And this is my sister.”

---

Lucy went still.

---

## **VII. SILENCE IN THE OFFICE**

Neither of them spoke for a long time.

The connection didn’t need explanation.

It wasn’t something logical.

Not yet.

Not fully.

But it was real enough to change the air in the room.

---

### Lucy

> “…I didn’t know.”

---

### Director Lucious

> “Neither did I… until I saw your name again.”

---

He exhaled slowly.

---

### Director Lucious

> “The Banlands don’t usually return people without reason.”

---

Lucy looked down.

---

### Lucy

> “I just… came back because someone asked me to.”

---

Lucious raised an eyebrow slightly.

---

### Lucy

> “Neko.”

---

That name made the tension shift.

---

## **VIII. Neko’S ANSWER**

Later, Lucious confronted Neko.

No formality this time.

Just direct.

---

### Director Lucious

> “Why her?”

---

Neko blinked.

---

### Neko

> “Because she didn’t deserve to stay there.”

---

### Director Lucious

> “That’s not what I meant.”

---

Neko thought for a moment.

Then shrugged slightly.

---

### Neko

> “She reminded me of someone who got lost a long time ago.”

---

Lucious went quiet.

---

### Neko

> “So I asked for her back.”

---

A pause.

---

### Neko

> “The Devil said yes.”

---

## **IX. THE BLACKSITE TODAY**

Work resumed.

But something had changed.

Lucy was no longer just “the engineer from the incident.”

She was part of Engineering again.

Not as a replacement.

Not as a symbol.

Just as herself.

---

Neko was back to work too.

Too much work.

Immediately too much work.

---

### Niko

> “He’s already trying to redesign the coolant system again.”

---

### Miss Neko

> “I’ll supervise him.”

---

### Star

> “That sounds like damage control.”

---

### Nightshade

> “That sounds like disaster prevention.”

---

Lucy watched from across the room.

Then quietly spoke.

---

### Lucy

> “He really does just… fix things.”

---

Niko nodded.

---

### Niko

> “And sometimes breaks them worse first.”

---

---

## **X. FINAL NOTE**

The Banlands did not provide an official explanation for Lucy’s return.

The entity known as **The Devil** declined all requests for comment, stating only:

> “Some names aren’t accidents.”

Director Lucious has not reopened the question publicly.

Not yet.

But in private logs, one entry was added.

---

> **Subject: LUCY**
> 
> Status: Returned  
> Relation: Unknown / Possibly Confirmed  
> Emotional Impact: Significant  
> Recommendation: No Further Questions Until Prepared

---

And for the first time in years...

Lucious allowed himself to believe something he thought he had lost forever.

That some things—some people—might still find their way back.
