# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-148**

### **AMBUSH VULNERABILITY**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – LOW PRIORITY / PERSONNEL NOTICE

## **I. SUMMARY**

Following Neko's emotional recovery and return to normal duty, an unexpected behavioral pattern has emerged.

Personnel have discovered that Neko has become remarkably susceptible to what Maintenance has collectively labeled **"Affection Ambushes."**

Unlike previous observations, this phenomenon poses no safety risk.

It does, however, significantly reduce Neko's ability to continue walking in the intended direction.

---

## **II. HEADPAT AMBUSH**

The first incident occurred in Engineering.

Neko was carrying a toolbox toward a coolant manifold.

An engineer happened to walk past him.

---

### Engineer

> "...Morning."

---

### Neko

> "Morning."

---

The engineer casually rested a hand on Neko's head.

A gentle pat.

Nothing unusual.

---

Neko stopped walking.

Completely.

---

### Engineer

> "...Neko?"

---

No response.

Only the faint twitch of his cat ears.

---

### Engineer

> "...System crash?"

---

Neko finally looked up.

---

### Neko

> "...You distracted me..."

---

### Engineer

> "...Sorry."

---

### Neko

> "...I'm not complaining."

---

The engineer laughed.

## **VIII. MEDICAL NOTE**

Medical Division confirmed there was no evidence that these interactions caused stress or discomfort when initiated respectfully.

Personnel were reminded, however, that Neko remains an individual with personal boundaries.

If he indicates that he wants to be put down, left alone, or declines physical contact, those wishes are to be respected immediately.

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Since the resolution of the scheduling incident, spontaneous positive interactions with Neko have become commonplace throughout the Blacksite.

The most frequently observed include:

- Gentle head pats, particularly around his cat ears.
    
- Offering piggyback rides during long walks between sectors.
    
- Briefly carrying him between work areas, especially by Maintenance and Miss Neko.
    
- Sharing meals and casual conversations during breaks.
    

One final note from Maintenance was attached to the report:

> **"A few months ago, we had to invent excuses just to spend five minutes with him. Now we have to compete with three other departments because everyone keeps finding him first."**

Beneath that, someone had added in different handwriting:

> **"Objective update: Neko is no longer difficult to find. He is difficult to keep on the ground."**
