# **SUMMARY**

On the morning of the incident, Z-NEKO arrived at Blacksite in full Scottish Fold cat form.

This was not unusual.

---

What was unusual was that he never transformed back.

---

Not once.

---

For the entire day.

---

No human form.

No scientist form.

No laboratory coat.

No goggles.

No chemistry.
