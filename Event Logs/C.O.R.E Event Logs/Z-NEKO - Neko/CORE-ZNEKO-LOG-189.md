# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-189**

### **SEARCH UPDATE — DESIGNATION "STAR"**

**Classification:** LEVEL 5 – INTERNAL / ACTIVE SEARCH

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Thirty-six hours after Star's disappearance, the investigation produced its first credible lead.

The clue did not come from surveillance.

Nor from N.O.S.T.

Nor from any anomaly.

It came from Aster.

---

## **I. THE REALIZATION**

Aster had spent nearly two days helping search every accessible section of the Blacksite.

Nothing.

Every room.

Every hallway.

Every observation deck.

Every maintenance tunnel.

Nothing.

She eventually stopped in front of one of Star's lockers.

Looked down.

Then slowly wrote on her whiteboard.

> **...Cat.**

Neko looked at the board.

---

### Neko

> "...Cat?"

---

Aster nodded repeatedly.

She wrote again.

> **Star... cat.**

The room fell silent.

Everyone had been searching for Star.

No one had been searching for...

A small white cat.

---

## **II. SEARCH PARAMETERS UPDATED**

Search procedures were immediately revised.

Personnel were instructed:

- Do **not** assume every cat in the facility is Aster.
    
- Any unidentified cat is to be approached calmly.
    
- Do not chase.
    
- Do not corner.
    
- Notify N.O.S.T. immediately.
    

Engineering personnel jokingly remarked:

> "...We've somehow lost a cat girl by forgetting she can become an actual cat."

No one laughed.

---

## **III. SECURITY REVIEW**

Archived footage was reviewed again.

This time...

Looking only for cats.

The review uncovered a previously ignored recording.

Three minutes after Star disappeared from camera...

A small white cat quietly exited an adjacent service corridor.

She paused.

Looked back once.

Then walked into an older section of the Blacksite.

Nobody paid attention.

Cats wandering the facility were considered normal.

---

## **IV. WHY SHE WASN'T FOUND**

The realization explained everything.

In cat form...

Star was almost impossible to distinguish from an ordinary resident cat unless approached closely.

She could fit beneath machinery.

Inside ventilation alcoves.

On high maintenance pipes.

Behind storage crates.

She no longer needed doors.

She needed only a gap large enough for a cat.

---

## **V. ASTER**

Upon learning Star was likely hiding in her cat form...

Aster stopped crying.

Not because she felt better.

Because she finally had something to search for.

She immediately began checking places only another cat would think to hide.

Warm pipes.

Sunny windowsills.

Quiet shelves above eye level.

Storage boxes.

Maintenance crawlspaces.

The search changed completely.

---

## **VI. NEKO**

Neko quietly added one instruction to every search team.

---

### Neko

> "...If you find her..."

A long pause.

---

> "...Don't grab her."

---

Everyone looked toward him.

---

### Neko

> "...She'll run."

---

He lowered his ears.

---

> "...Just stay nearby."

> "...Let her decide."

---

Nobody objected.

---

## **VII. NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade searched alone again that evening.

She eventually entered one of Star's favorite observation rooms.

It was empty.

Almost.

A food bowl placed near the window had been disturbed.

Fresh pawprints crossed the dusty floor.

Leading toward a narrow ventilation opening.

Nightshade slowly knelt beside them.

---

### Nightshade

Softly.

> "...You're alive."

---

She did not follow.

She simply placed a small blanket beside the bowl.

Along with a handwritten note.

She left without looking back.

---

## **VIII. THE NOTE**

The following morning...

The blanket had been used.

The food was gone.

The note remained.

One additional line had been written beneath Nightshade's message.

The handwriting was unmistakably Star's.

It read:

> **I'm sorry.**

Nothing else.

No location.

No explanation.

Only proof.

She was still somewhere inside the Blacksite.

---

## **IX. DIRECTOR'S ORDER**

Director Lucious immediately updated the standing search directive.

> **"This is no longer a search for a missing person."**

> **"This is a search for someone who is hiding."**

He added one final instruction.

> **"If she chooses not to come out, no one is to force her. Confirm her safety. Leave food, water, and a way back. We will not turn recovery into another source of fear."**

---

## **X. CURRENT STATUS**

**Star**

- Confirmed alive.
    
- Believed to be remaining exclusively in cat form.
    
- Voluntarily avoiding contact.
    
- Exact location unknown.
    

**Nightshade**

- Continues daily search efforts.
    
- Established non-intrusive supply points in locations Star is known to visit.
    

**Aster**

- Assisting with searches.
    
- No longer believes Star has left the Blacksite.
    
- Continues to blame herself despite reassurance from staff.
    

**Neko**

- Creativity fully restored.
    
- Personally searching maintenance spaces during every break.
    

---

The latest camera recording, timestamped **02:17**, shows a small white cat stepping silently into an empty hallway.

She notices a food bowl left outside an unused observation room.

She hesitates.

Looks around to make sure no one is there.

Eats a little.

Drinks some water.

Then curls up on the folded blanket Nightshade had left.

For the first time since disappearing...

She sleeps peacefully.

Before dawn, she is gone again.
