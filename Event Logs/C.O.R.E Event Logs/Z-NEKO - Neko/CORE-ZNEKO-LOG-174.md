Following continuous observation and final imaging...

Medical Division has officially declared Neko's right leg fully healed.

Evaluation confirms:

- Fracture: **Completely healed.**
    
- Bone density: **Normal.**
    
- Regeneration: **Successful.**
    
- Mobility: **Fully restored.**
    
- Restrictions: **Lifted.**
    

Neko has been cleared to resume normal engineering duties.

Engineering applauded.

Neko simply smiled.

---

> "...Responsibly."

## **II. THE FIRST STEPS**

The cast was removed.

Neko carefully stood.

For a few moments...

He simply remained still.

Testing his balance.

Flexing his ankle.

Taking one careful step.

Then another.

No pain.

No instability.

Just...

A normal walk.

The Engineering workshop erupted into applause.

---

### Engineer

> "...Welcome back."

---

Neko bowed his head slightly.

---

### Neko

> "...Thanks."

## **III. ORACLE'S DECISION**

Despite Medical Division's clearance...

Oracle was unconvinced.

For the next several days...

She followed Neko almost everywhere.

Not because she sensed danger.

Not because she expected another accident.

She was simply...

Watching.

Whenever Neko climbed a ladder...

Oracle sat at the bottom.

Watching.

Whenever he crossed a catwalk...

She watched from the entrance.

Whenever he carried equipment...

Her eyes followed every step.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Still worried?"

---

Oracle nodded.

---

She picked up her marker.

Wrote:

> **Just checking.**

---

## **IV. THE TEST**

Research quietly observed.

They intentionally scheduled a routine maintenance task requiring:

- Walking.
    
- Climbing.
    
- Kneeling.
    
- Standing.
    
- Carrying tools.
    

Neko completed every step flawlessly.

Oracle watched the entire process.

Not once did she interrupt.

Not once did she write:

> **Bad idea.**

When Neko climbed down the final ladder...

Oracle walked over.

Looked at his healed leg.

Gently tapped it once with her paw.

Then looked up at him.

---

### Neko

> "...Inspection?"

---

She wrote:

> **Passed.**

---

## **V. BACK TO NORMAL**

Engineering slowly returned to its familiar rhythm.

Neko repaired pipes.

Mirage handled heavier components when needed.

Miss Neko occasionally stopped by just to check on her "Lil bro."

Oracle resumed wandering the Blacksite, saving people from accidents and questionable decisions.

Still...

She visited Engineering more often than before.

Mostly just to see Neko.

---

One evening...

Miss Neko found Neko working beneath a control panel.

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Medical Division has officially concluded Neko's fracture recovery. All temporary restrictions have been lifted, and he has returned to full engineering duties without complication.

Although Oracle maintained close observation for several days after medical clearance, her behavior appears to have been one final precaution rather than a response to any foreseen danger. Once satisfied that Neko had truly recovered, she gradually resumed her normal patrol schedule.

Research has noted one lasting change: Oracle still makes a point of checking on Neko every day. Not because he needs supervision, but because, in her own words, written neatly on her whiteboard during a routine interview:

> **He looked after me when I was little. Now I look after him too.**

Director Lucious signed beneath her statement with a brief comment:

> **"That's not a duty. That's family."**
