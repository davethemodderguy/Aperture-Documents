# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-160**

### **UNSCHEDULED SILLY MODE**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – ANOMALOUS ENTITY OBSERVATION

---

## **I. SUMMARY**

Engineering personnel have documented a peculiar behavioral trait exhibited by Mirage.

Under normal circumstances, Mirage is calm, polite, helpful, and remarkably efficient.

However...

On seemingly random occasions...

He will abruptly abandon all dignity.

This phenomenon has been unofficially designated:

> **"Silly Mode."**

No anomalous energy spikes accompany the behavior.

No neurological changes have been detected.

Mirage simply...

Decides to be an idiot.

For reasons currently unknown.

---

## **II. THE FIRST DOCUMENTED INCIDENT**

Neko was repairing a coolant pump.

Mirage stood beside him holding a flashlight.

Everything was normal.

Until...

---

### Neko

> "...Little to the left."

---

Mirage nodded.

Moved the flashlight.

Far too much.

---

### Neko

> "...Other left."

---

Mirage nodded again.

Turned completely around.

Now illuminating the ceiling.

---

### Neko

> "...Mirage."

---

### Mirage

> "...Yes?"

---

### Neko

> "...Why are you lighting the ceiling?"

---

Mirage looked up.

As if genuinely surprised.

---

### Mirage

> "...The ceiling is bright now."

---

Neko sighed.

---

### Neko

> "...You're doing this on purpose."

---

Mirage smiled.

---

### Mirage

> "...Maybe."

---

## **III. MISS NEKO**

Miss Neko has become particularly fond of Silly Mode.

One afternoon—

Mirage was carrying a crate.

Miss Neko walked beside him.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Mirage."

---

### Mirage

> "...Yes?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...How many fingers am I holding up?"

---

She held up three fingers.

Mirage stared seriously.

For nearly ten seconds.

---

### Mirage

> "...Tuesday."

---

Miss Neko stopped walking.

Then immediately burst into laughter.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...That's not even a number!"

---

Mirage smiled proudly.

---

### Mirage

> "...Correct."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You did that on purpose!"

---

### Mirage

> "...Yes."

---

She laughed so hard she had to lean against Neko for support.

---

### Neko

> "...You broke my sister."

---

### Mirage

> "...Oops."

---

Star discovered Silly Mode almost immediately.

She decided to encourage it.

---

### Star

> "...Mirage!"

---

### Mirage

> "...Yes?"

---

### Star

> "...What's two plus two?"

---

Mirage looked genuinely thoughtful.

---

### Mirage

> "...Fish."

---

### Star

> "...Wrong."

---

### Mirage

> "...Very wrong."

---

### Star

> "...Again!"

---

### Mirage

> "...Purple."

### Star

> "...Still wrong!"

---

### Mirage

> "...I'm improving."

Neither of them got bored.

## **V. NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade remained skeptical.

---

### Nightshade

> "...He's pretending."

---

### Neko

> "...Probably."

---

### Nightshade

> "...Mirage."

---

### Mirage

> "...Yes?"

---

### Nightshade

> "...Spell 'Engineering.'"

---

Mirage answered instantly.

---

### Mirage

> "...Engineering."

---

### Nightshade

> "...No."

---

### Mirage

> "...I spelled it."

---

She pinched the bridge of her nose.

---

### Nightshade

> "...I meant the letters."

---

Mirage gasped dramatically.

---

### Mirage

> "...You changed the question."

---

Neko quietly looked away.

Trying—and failing—not to laugh.

Even Nightshade's lips twitched before she gave up and chuckled.

---

## **VI. LUCY'S TEST**

Lucy wondered if Silly Mode affected Mirage's work.

So she conducted an experiment.

---

### Lucy

> "...Mirage."

---

### Mirage

> "...Yes?"

---

### Lucy

> "...Torque this bolt to exactly ninety newton-meters."

---

Mirage nodded.

---

### Mirage

> "...Okay."

---

Five seconds later—

Perfect.

Exactly ninety.

Measured and confirmed.

---

### Lucy

> "...So..."

---

### Lucy

> "...You can still work?"

---

### Mirage

> "...Yes."

---

### Lucy

> "...Then why are you acting silly?"

---

Mirage thought for a moment.

---

### Mirage

> "...Because Miss Neko laughed."

---

Miss Neko smiled softly.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You like making people smile, don't you?"

---

Mirage nodded.

---

### Mirage

> "...Yes."

---

## **VII. THE RUBBER CHICKEN INCIDENT**

One morning, Neko reached into his toolbox.

Instead of a wrench...

He pulled out a bright yellow rubber chicken.

He stared at it.

Slowly turned toward Mirage.

---

### Neko

> "...Mirage."

---

### Mirage

> "...Yes?"

---

### Neko

> "...Why?"

---

Mirage answered with complete seriousness.

---

### Mirage

> "...Emergency chicken."

---

### Neko

> "...Emergency..."

---

A beat.

---

### Mirage

> "...You never know."

---

Miss Neko laughed so hard she had tears in her eyes.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...I'm keeping that!"

---

The rubber chicken permanently disappeared into her desk drawer.

Labeled:

> **Emergency Chicken**

No one questioned it again.

---

## **VIII. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Director Lucious eventually witnessed Silly Mode firsthand.

He approached Mirage.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Status report."

---

Mirage stood perfectly straight.

Saluted.

---

### Mirage

> "...Status."

---

A pause.

---

### Mirage

> "...Reported."

---

Silence.

The nearby engineers froze.

Lucious stared for several seconds.

Then...

A tiny smile appeared.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Accepted."

---

He continued walking as though nothing unusual had happened.

Later that afternoon, several engineers swore they heard him quietly laugh to himself in the hallway.

None dared mention it.

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Psychological Division has concluded that "Silly Mode" is not indicative of cognitive decline or anomalous instability. Mirage retains full technical competence during these episodes and continues to perform engineering tasks with his usual precision.

The behavior appears entirely voluntary and is most often observed in relaxed environments or when interacting with friends. Staff have noted that Mirage seems particularly inclined to act playfully when it makes Neko, Miss Neko, Star, or other nearby personnel laugh.

A note from Miss Neko was later attached to the report:

> **"Mirage says the smartest things when he's working... and the dumbest things when he's having fun. I'm pretty sure he knows exactly what he's doing—and I hope he never stops."**
