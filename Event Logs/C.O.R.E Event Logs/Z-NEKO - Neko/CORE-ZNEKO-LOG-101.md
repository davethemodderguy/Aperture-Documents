## **VIII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

By the end of the day, Sky and Aurora had accepted the new normal surprisingly quickly.

Like everyone else, they realized two things:

* Neko may be smaller, lighter, and much easier to carry around, but he is still the same engineering prodigy who can redesign a reactor over lunch.
* Miss Neko is not his twin, but his older sister in every practical sense. The two work together so seamlessly that separating them during engineering projects is generally considered counterproductive.

As they left Engineering, Sky glanced back one last time.

Miss Neko was leaning over the blueprint, making annotations.

Neko was already calculating revised coolant flow rates.

Neither had noticed the rest of the room quietly watching them work.
