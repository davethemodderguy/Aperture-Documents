## **I. FOLLOW-UP EXAMINATION**

Following the discovery of the notebook documented in **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-142**, Director Lucious authorized a more thorough examination.

The intent was not to invade Neko's privacy out of curiosity.

The notebook had already become relevant to an ongoing welfare assessment, and Medical requested to determine whether it contained information that could explain the recent behavioral changes.

The examination was conducted jointly by:

- Director Lucious
    
- Lucy
    
- Miss Neko
    
- Niko
    
- Lead Medical Researcher
    

No copies were made.

No pages were removed.

---

## **II. THE DISCOVERY**

Most of the notebook remained what everyone expected.

Engineering calculations.

Pipe dimensions.

Coolant formulas.

Material strengths.

Then...

Several pages near the back had been folded together.

Not hidden particularly well.

Simply...

Never opened.

Miss Neko unfolded the first page.

The handwriting immediately changed.

Instead of technical notes...

It looked like a diary.

The remaining pages were blank.

## **XIII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Behavioral specialists reviewing the diary noted a recurring pattern.

Neko consistently evaluated his days not by whether he was happy, successful, or connected with others.

Instead, he measured them by one question:

> **"Did I stay out of everyone's way?"**

The entries suggested that, over time, he had gradually internalized the belief that making himself less noticeable was a way of helping those around him. The notebook did not indicate hostility toward others; rather, it reflected a pattern of self-imposed withdrawal and a growing assumption that minimizing his own needs was beneficial.

The notebook was returned to Neko's room without alteration.

No pages were removed.

No annotations were added.

The investigation into the unexplained scheduling anomalies remained ongoing.

It still had no confirmed cause.
