# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-175**

### **NEWLY IDENTIFIED ALLERGEN – PEANUTS**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – MEDICAL EMERGENCY / PERSONNEL NOTICE

---

## **I. INCIDENT SUMMARY**

**Location:** Central Cafeteria  
**Time:** 12:18

Neko was having lunch with several Engineering personnel.

As part of his meal, he opened a small packet of roasted peanuts, a snack he had eaten many times before and frequently referred to as one of his favorite treats.

He ate one peanut.

Approximately five seconds later...

Neko stopped moving.

He reached for his throat.

The packet fell from his hand.

---

### Nearby Engineer

> "...Neko?"

---

He attempted to answer.

No sound came out.

Within seconds:

- Severe difficulty breathing.
    
- Rapid swelling of the airway.
    
- Collapse to the floor.
    
- Signs consistent with anaphylactic shock.
    

Moments later...

He clutched his chest.

His heart rhythm became critically unstable.

The entire cafeteria fell silent.

---

## **II. EMERGENCY RESPONSE**

The emergency alarm was activated immediately.

Medical personnel arrived within minutes.

Witnesses reported seeing Neko struggling to breathe as personnel cleared the surrounding area.

A severe allergic reaction was diagnosed on scene.

Treatment included:

- Immediate administration of an epinephrine auto-injector (EpiPen).
    
- Oxygen support.
    
- Continuous cardiac monitoring.
    
- Defibrillation after a life-threatening cardiac rhythm developed.
    
- Rapid transfer to Medical.
    

Following treatment...

His breathing gradually returned.

Cardiac rhythm stabilized.

Neko regained consciousness later that afternoon.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...That was far too close."

---

## **III. AN UNEXPECTED DISCOVERY**

The incident surprised everyone.

Including Neko.

---

### Neko

> "...I'm... allergic?"

---

Medical testing conducted after stabilization confirmed the diagnosis.

Neko possesses a **severe peanut allergy**.

Researchers believe the allergy either:

- Developed recently following physiological changes after his revival and recovery, or
    
- Had previously gone unnoticed due to never reaching a critical exposure threshold.
    

Regardless of origin...

The diagnosis is now confirmed.

---

## **VII. NEW PROTOCOL**

Effective immediately:

Neko is prohibited from knowingly consuming:

- Peanuts.
    
- Peanut butter.
    
- Foods containing peanuts.
    
- Foods with significant peanut contamination risk.
    

Personnel serving meals are instructed to verify ingredients before providing food to Neko.

Emergency epinephrine auto-injectors have been placed in:

- Engineering.
    
- Medical.
    
- Logistics.
    
- Cafeteria.
    
- N.O.S.T.
    

Additional units are carried by Medical Response Teams.

## **VIII. THE WATCH**

An unofficial agreement formed almost immediately.

Whenever food appeared near Neko...

Someone checked it first.

Usually:

Miss Neko.

If she wasn't nearby...

Mirage.

If both were busy...

Star.

Nightshade.

Or Oracle.

Oracle quickly developed a habit.

Whenever someone handed Neko unfamiliar food...

She simply held up her marker.

Writing one word.

> **Ingredients?**

The entire cafeteria had grown accustomed to it.

No one complained.

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Medical Division has officially diagnosed Neko with a severe peanut allergy capable of causing life-threatening anaphylaxis. The incident has prompted immediate updates to cafeteria procedures and emergency medical preparedness across the Blacksite.

Neko has accepted the diagnosis, though he openly admits he misses his favorite snack. His caretakers—Miss Neko, Mirage, Star, Nightshade, and Oracle—have collectively taken it upon themselves to ensure peanuts never end up in his meals again.

Director Lucious closed the report with a brief note:

> **"Engineering can replace pipes. Medical can restart hearts. Neither wants to do both because someone insisted on eating peanuts. Read the ingredient label."**
