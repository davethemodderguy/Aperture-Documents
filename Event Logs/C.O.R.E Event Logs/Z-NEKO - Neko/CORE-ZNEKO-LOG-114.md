## **I. INCIDENT SUMMARY**

**Location:** Maintenance Sector – Utility Corridor C-17

**Incident Type:** High-pressure flammable gas leak

**Result:** Leak successfully sealed

**Casualties:** None

**Structural Damage:** Minimal

**Medical Outcome:** One severely aggravated headache (Neko)

**Disciplinary Action:** None

---

## **II. THE LEAK**

09:42.

Maintenance was conducting a routine inspection beneath Utility Corridor C-17.

An aging high-pressure gas line suddenly ruptured.

A sharp metallic **CRACK** echoed through the corridor.

Then...

**HISSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSS...**

Flammable gas rapidly filled the hallway.

Emergency alarms immediately activated.

Red warning lights flashed across the sector.

---

### Automated System

> **"WARNING. FLAMMABLE GAS DETECTED."**

> **"REMOVE ALL IGNITION SOURCES."**

> **"EVACUATE IMMEDIATELY."**

---

Personnel began shutting down nearby machinery.

Power was isolated.

Welding stations were disabled.

Security established a perimeter.

No one dared touch the damaged pipe.

One spark...

And the Maintenance Sector could become an inferno.

---

Still carrying his warm tea.

Still sick.

### Neko

> "...?"

---

### Engineer

> "Sorry!"

---

### Neko

> "...Wait—"

---

### Engineer

> "Really sorry!"

---

### Neko

> "Mm?!"

His eyes immediately widened.

---

### Neko

> "...Cold..."

---

### Miss Neko

### Star

---

### Engineer

> "...I'm so, so sorry."

---

### Neko

> "...Achoo—!!"

---

## **VI. THE PATCH**

A wave of freezing air erupted forward.

Unlike the previous uncontrolled sneeze...

This one struck the damaged gas line directly.

The escaping gas instantly crystallized.

Moisture in the air froze solid.

The fractured section disappeared beneath a thick shell of dense white permafrost.

The hissing stopped.

Immediately.

The leak...

Was sealed.

---

The alarm system paused.

Sensors recalculated.

---

### Automated System

> **"...Gas pressure stabilized."**

---

Silence filled the corridor.

---

### Maintenance Supervisor

> "...It..."

---

### Engineer

> "...Worked."

---

---

### Miss Neko

The cryogenic discharge itself caused no lasting damage.

However...

Forcing the reaction placed enormous strain on Neko while he was already ill.

---

### Medical Officer

---

### Neko

> "...Like..."

---

He rubbed his forehead.

---

---

- Temporary weakness
    
- Reduced balance

- Muffled hearing

### Medical Officer

---

## **XIII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

The incident demonstrated that Neko's cryogenic sneeze could serve as an effective emergency sealing method for hazardous leaks.

However, Medical Division unanimously concluded that **intentionally inducing the reaction while Neko is ill places excessive strain on him and is not to be used as a standard procedure**.

The permafrost patch held until Maintenance safely depressurized the line and completed permanent repairs.

As for the engineer who force-fed him the lemonade...

She visited Neko every day afterward with warm tea, homemade soup, and sandwiches until he recovered.

Not because anyone ordered her to.

Simply because she felt it was the least she could do.

Neko never held it against her. In fact, whenever she stopped by his room, he'd give a small smile and say, in a slightly congested voice,

> **"...Hi."**

And Miss Neko, standing nearby with folded arms, would always add,

> **"Warm drinks only."**
