# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-184**

### **POOL DAY**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – RECREATIONAL RECORD

---

## **I. SUMMARY**

Following several uninterrupted weeks of successful operations, Director Lucious authorized a facility-wide recreation period for off-duty personnel.

Rather than remaining in Engineering or the Residential Wing, the following personnel elected to spend the afternoon at the Blacksite's indoor recreational pool:

- Neko
    
- Miss Neko
    
- Aster
    
- Mirage
    
- Star
    
- Nightshade
    
- Bao
    
- Frost
    

For the first time in months...

The pool area echoed with laughter instead of maintenance announcements.

---

## **II. BAO**

The moment Bao saw the water...

She practically lit up.

---

### Bao

> "...POOL!!"

---

Before anyone could stop her...

She made an impressively graceful dive into the water.

Instead of a massive splash, the water rose to greet her, cushioning her landing through her hydrokinesis before settling back into the pool.

She resurfaced laughing.

---

### Bao

> "...Best day ever!"

---

Several lifeguards applauded.

One quietly admitted that was the smoothest dive he'd ever seen.

---

## **III. FROST**

Frost stopped at the edge of the pool.

She watched everyone for a few moments before shaking her head.

---

### Frost

> "...I'll stay here."

---

Her natural aura had one unfortunate side effect.

Tiny patches of frost began spreading across the tiles beneath her feet.

The surface of the pool closest to her slowly developed a thin sheet of ice.

---

### Frost

> "...See?"

---

She sighed.

---

> "...I'd freeze half the pool."

---

Instead, she sat beneath a parasol with a cold drink, occasionally chatting with everyone and creating tiny floating snowflakes for Aster to chase.

---

## **IV. NEKO & MISS NEKO**

Neko happily paddled around the shallow end while Miss Neko floated nearby.

Every few minutes...

Neko would splash her.

Every few minutes...

Miss Neko splashed back.

Eventually...

She won.

Mostly because she was taller.

---

### Neko

> "...Unfair."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Skill issue."

---

## **V. ASTER**

Aster surprised absolutely everyone.

Instead of hesitating near the water...

She ran straight toward it.

---

### Neko

> "...Wait—"

---

Splash.

---

She landed in the pool.

Then...

Started happily paddling around.

Her tiny paws moved surprisingly efficiently.

---

### Bao

> "...She swims!"

---

Aster proudly circled the pool.

Then teleported...

Only to reappear with another splash a few meters away.

---

### Mirage

> "...She's combining swimming and teleportation."

---

### Star

> "...Somehow."

---

Aster simply wrote on her waterproof whiteboard:

> **Fast.**

---

## **VI. MIRAGE**

Mirage spent most of the afternoon helping retrieve beach balls.

Mostly because Aster kept teleporting them onto the ceiling.

How...

Nobody knew.

---

### Mirage

> "...Again?"

---

Aster nodded.

---

### Mirage

Sighing.

> "...Again."

---

Using his telekinesis, he floated the ball back down.

Thirty seconds later...

It was somehow on top of a lifeguard tower.

Again.

---

## **VII. STAR & NIGHTSHADE**

Star floated comfortably on her back, enjoying the peaceful atmosphere.

Nightshade initially remained near the edge of the pool.

She wasn't particularly interested in swimming.

Until...

Neko gently splashed a little water toward her.

---

### Nightshade

"...Really?"

---

Neko grinned.

---

The next splash came from Miss Neko.

Then Bao.

Then Aster.

Nightshade looked around.

She slowly smiled.

Then stepped into the water herself.

Within minutes...

She had joined the water fight.

---

### Star

Laughing.

> "...There she goes."

---

## **VIII. FROST'S CONTRIBUTION**

Although she stayed dry...

Frost wasn't left out.

Using careful control over her abilities, she shaped tiny decorative ice sculptures along the poolside.

Small whales.

Cats.

Stars.

Snowflakes.

One particularly detailed sculpture depicted Aster holding her marker.

Aster stared at it.

Then gently hugged Frost.

---

### Frost

Smiling softly.

> "...You're welcome."

---

## **IX. THE LIVELIEST THE POOL HAD BEEN**

By late afternoon...

The indoor pool looked completely different.

Beach balls bounced everywhere.

Laughter echoed off the walls.

Bao organized races.

Miss Neko refereed them.

Neko lost almost all of them.

Mirage somehow won one without even entering the water until the last moment.

Star floated peacefully whenever the chaos became too much.

Nightshade found herself laughing far more than she expected.

Aster swam happily between everyone, occasionally teleporting onto floating pool toys just to surprise them.

Even personnel walking past stopped to watch.

One engineer quietly remarked:

> "...I don't think I've ever seen the pool this alive."

---

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

The recreation period concluded without incident.

No injuries occurred.

No containment breaches were reported.

No one slipped despite the exceptionally wet floor.

Medical recorded only one unusual note:

> **"Z-885 (Aster) appears to enjoy swimming as much as she enjoys warm bubble baths. She demonstrates excellent swimming ability despite her small size."**

As everyone prepared to leave...

Frost looked at the now-calm water.

She smiled.

---

### Frost

> "...Next time..."

She held up one hand, where a tiny layer of frost shimmered.

> "...I'll figure out how not to freeze the pool."

---

Bao wrapped an arm around her shoulders.

---

### Bao

> "...And when you do..."

She pointed at the water.

> "...We're having a race."

---

Frost chuckled.

> "...You're on."

Across the pool, Aster held up her waterproof whiteboard one final time.

> **Best pool day ever.** 🐾
