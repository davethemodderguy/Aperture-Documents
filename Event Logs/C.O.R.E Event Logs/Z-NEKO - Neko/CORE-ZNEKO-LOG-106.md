### **Classification:** INTERNAL

## **I. A FAMILY HEIRLOOM**

Following the eye-contact incident, Research Division explored numerous ideas.

Special visors.

Polarized lenses.

Tinted goggles.

None were tested.

Because someone remembered something.

---

### Cherry

> "...Wait."

---

Everyone looked over.

---

### Cherry

> "I still have them."

---

Silence.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Have what?"

---

Cherry carefully opened a small wooden case she had kept among her personal belongings.

Inside rested a familiar pair of glasses.

Thin metal frame.

Round lenses.

Simple.

Elegant.

Immediately recognizable to those who had known Snowy.

---

### Cherry

> "...Snowy's glasses."

---

The room became noticeably quieter.

---

## **II. HISTORY**

The glasses had belonged to Snowy for years.

Snowy had worn them almost constantly.

Not because his eyesight required them.

But because they were comfortable.

And eventually, they simply became part of who he was.

---

After Snowy's split...

Into **Ghostly Niko** and **Neko**...

The glasses remained with Cherry.

She had kept them carefully ever since.

---

### Cherry

> "I never thought anyone else would wear them."

---

She looked toward Neko.

---

### Cherry

> "...But maybe it's time."

---

## **III. THE FITTING**

Cherry handed the glasses to Neko.

---

### Cherry

> "Try them."

---

Neko accepted them carefully.

Almost reverently.

He unfolded the arms.

Placed them over his ears.

The round lenses rested comfortably in front of his bangs.

Oddly enough...

He made no attempt to move the bangs aside.

The glasses simply sat over them.

---

### Aurora

> "...Your hair's still covering your eyes."

---

### Neko

> "...I know."

---

### Sky

> "Can you even see?"

---

### Neko

> "Perfectly."

---

### Sky

> "...Of course you can."

---

## **IV. THE TEST**

With Neko's permission, a controlled test was arranged.

One researcher stood several meters away.

Neko remained seated.

His bangs still covered his eyes.

The glasses reflected the room's lights faintly.

---

### Director Lucious

> "Ready?"

---

### Researcher

> "Ready."

---

### Neko

> "Ready."

---

For the first time since the protocol had been established...

Neko gently lifted his own bangs.

Just enough for his eyes to become visible behind the circular lenses.

The researcher looked directly at him.

Five seconds.

Ten.

Thirty.

A full minute.

---

Nothing happened.

---

### Researcher

> "...That's it?"

---

### Neko

> "...Looks like it."

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Again."

---

The test was repeated several more times.

Each with the same result.

No mesmerization.

No cognitive effects.

No memory loss.

The glasses completely interrupted the phenomenon.

---

## **IX. PROTOCOL UPDATE**

Following extensive testing:

The eye-contact phenomenon is **fully suppressed** while Neko wears Snowy's glasses.

The effect has remained stable throughout all observations.

Personnel may interact with Neko normally while he is wearing the glasses.

Despite this, Neko continues to wear his bangs over his eyes.

When asked why...

---

### Neko

> "I like how it looks."

---

No further explanation was provided.

---

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Snowy's glasses have found a new owner.

What began as a practical safety measure unexpectedly became something more meaningful.

For Cherry, it was seeing a cherished keepsake continue to be used instead of remaining locked away.

For Niko, it was a quiet reminder of where they had all come from.

For Neko, it was both a solution to a dangerous anomaly and a connection to the one whose legacy ultimately led to his own existence.

The round frames quickly became part of his everyday appearance, fitting naturally alongside his hoodie, azure collar with its bell, messy glowing bangs, and calm expression.
