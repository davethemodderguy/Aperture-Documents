# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-183**

### **LOST BET**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – RECREATIONAL INCIDENT

---

## **I. SUMMARY**

At approximately 18:20, off-duty recreational activities were being conducted in the Engineering Lounge.

Participants:

* Neko
* Miss Neko
* Aster

According to all three participants, the event began as a series of harmless games and increasingly ridiculous challenges.

Unfortunately for Neko...

He lost.

Repeatedly.

Medical and Security both confirmed that no coercion or misconduct occurred. The agreed-forfeit had been accepted by all participants beforehand.

---

## **II. THE BET**

The final challenge remains disputed.

Depending on who was interviewed:

### Neko

> "...It was definitely rigged."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Skill issue."

---

### Aster

Holding up her whiteboard:

> **You lost fairly.**

A tiny paw print was drawn underneath.

---

Regardless...

Neko lost.

The agreed consequence was simple.

For one day...

Neko and Miss Neko would swap outfits.

---

## **III. THE PROBLEM**

There was one obvious complication.

Neko was physically six years old.

Miss Neko appeared approximately twelve.

The difference in height made a direct exchange impossible.

Engineering personnel solved the problem in the only sensible way.

Tailors were called.

Scaled replicas of each outfit were produced overnight.

By the following morning...

The challenge could proceed.

---

## **IV. THE SWAP**

Neko emerged from his room first.

He was wearing a miniature version of Miss Neko's outfit, carefully resized to fit him.

His ears immediately folded back in embarrassment.

---

### Neko

> "...Can I go back inside?"

---

From the opposite hallway...

Miss Neko stepped out wearing a resized version of Neko's engineer attire.

She adjusted the oversized utility belt.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Not bad."

---

She looked at Neko.

Covered her mouth.

Trying—and failing—not to laugh.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...It actually suits you."

---

### Neko

> "..."

---

> "...I regret everything."

---

## **V. ASTER'S VERDICT**

Aster slowly walked around Neko once.

Then twice.

Studying the outfit with complete seriousness.

She finally picked up her marker.

Everyone leaned in.

She wrote:

> **Cute.**

---

Neko sighed dramatically.

---

### Neko

> "...Traitor."

---

Aster added another word.

> **Very.**

---

Mirage happened to walk into the room at exactly that moment.

He looked at Neko.

Looked at Miss Neko.

Looked back at Neko.

---

### Mirage

> "...You lost."

---

### Neko

> "...I lost."

---

### Mirage

> "...Badly."

---

### Neko

> "...Very badly."

---

## **VI. PERSONNEL REACTION**

The outfit swap quickly became the least productive topic in Engineering.

Engineers attempted to remain professional.

They lasted approximately forty seconds.

---

### Engineer

> "...Morning, N—"

---

He stopped.

Looked at Neko.

Looked away.

Laughed into his coffee.

---

### Another Engineer

> "...Lost another bet?"

---

### Neko

> "...Don't."

---

Meanwhile...

Miss Neko had adapted remarkably well to Neko's work clothes.

---

### Engineer

> "...You actually look ready to repair pipes."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...I probably could."

---

## **VII. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Director Lucious eventually crossed paths with the siblings.

He looked at Miss Neko.

Nodded approvingly.

---

### Director Lucious

> "Engineer look suits you."

---

Then he looked at Neko.

A long silence followed.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...I'm not asking."

---

He simply continued walking.

---

### Neko

Quietly.

> "...Thank you."

---

## **VIII. END OF CHALLENGE**

Exactly twenty-four hours later...

The challenge officially ended.

Both siblings changed back into their usual clothes.

Neko visibly relaxed.

Miss Neko handed back the resized engineer uniform.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Worth it."

---

### Neko

> "...For you."

---

Aster held up her whiteboard one final time.

> **Rematch next week?**

---

Neko immediately shook his head.

---

### Neko

> "...Absolutely not."

---

Mirage glanced at Aster.

Then at Miss Neko.

---

### Mirage

> "...He's going to lose again."

---

Miss Neko smiled.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Probably."

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

The incident resulted in no injuries, no property damage, and only a moderate decrease in Neko's dignity for approximately one day.

Personnel agreed that, despite Neko's embarrassment, the challenge remained entirely lighthearted and good-natured.

The final entry in Aster's notebook for the day consisted of a simple sketch of Neko in his borrowed outfit.

Beneath it, she had written:

> **He still lost.** 🐾
