# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-132**

### **NEAR-FALL INCIDENT**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – SAFETY / MEDICAL INCIDENT

---

## **I. INCIDENT SUMMARY**

**Location:** Maintenance Sector – Cooling Distribution Catwalk C-7  
**Time:** 15:41

Despite the implementation of **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-131**, an unexpected workload surge across multiple departments temporarily disrupted Neko's escort protocol.

Maintenance was responding to several simultaneous coolant pressure alarms.

Engineering was assisting Research with emergency equipment recalibration.

Medical staff were occupied treating unrelated personnel injuries.

For approximately twelve minutes, no designated escort was available.

Neko, as expected, insisted he was fine.

No one had the opportunity to argue.

---

## **II. THE REPAIR**

Neko volunteered to repair a coolant pipe running along Catwalk C-7.

The catwalk overlooked a maintenance ventilation shaft approximately **40 meters deep**.

At the bottom of the shaft were multiple industrial ventilation turbines.

While normally protected by maintenance grilles, the grilles had been temporarily removed for servicing.

A fall into the shaft presented two likely outcomes:

- Fatal injuries from the impact.
    
- Contact with the active ventilation fans below.
    

Neither outcome was considered survivable.

---

Neko climbed onto the maintenance platform.

Adjusted the damaged pipe.

Removed his wrench.

Tightened the final coupling.

---

### Nearby Engineer

> "Nice work."

---

### Neko

> "...Thanks..."

---

His voice sounded unusually quiet.

---

## **III. LOSS OF CONSCIOUSNESS**

Without warning...

Neko's knees buckled.

His wrench slipped from his hand.

He swayed once.

Twice.

Then collapsed sideways toward the open edge of the catwalk.

---

### Engineer

> "NEKO!"

---

The engineer lunged forward.

Too far away.

There wasn't enough time.

---

Neko's body slipped completely beyond the safety railing.

---

## **IV. THE INTERN**

At that exact moment...

A recently assigned maintenance intern happened to be walking beneath the catwalk access ladder.

Officially...

He was supposed to be transporting inspection paperwork.

Unofficially...

He had been attempting to quietly disappear before someone found more work for him.

---

He later described hearing only one thing.

---

### Engineer

> "NEKO!"

---

He looked up.

Saw a small figure falling.

Instinct took over.

---

Without thinking, he sprinted.

Jumped.

Reached upward with both arms.

---

And caught Neko just before his body dropped beyond the platform.

---

The sudden impact nearly knocked the intern off balance.

Fortunately...

Neko's small stature and unusually light weight made the catch manageable.

The intern stumbled backward several steps before regaining his footing.

---

### Intern

> "...Got him!"

---

The entire catwalk fell silent.

---

## **V. IMMEDIATE RESPONSE**

The intern carefully adjusted his grip.

Remembering the medical briefing from only a few days earlier...

He gently moved Neko onto his back in an approved piggyback carry.

---

### Intern

> "...He's breathing."

---

### Engineer

> "Medical!"

---

### Intern

> "...No injuries."

---

### Engineer

> "...Thank goodness..."

---

Neko remained peacefully unconscious.

One arm loosely draped over the intern's shoulder.

His glasses had stayed on.

His bangs still covered part of his forehead.

He looked less like someone who had nearly fallen forty meters...

...and more like someone who had simply fallen asleep after working too hard.

---

## **VI. MEDICAL ARRIVAL**

Lucy arrived first.

She stopped the instant she saw Neko.

---

### Lucy

> "...What happened?"

---

### Engineer

> "...He fainted."

---

### Intern

> "...I caught him."

---

Lucy immediately checked Neko's pulse.

Breathing.

Pupillary response.

No signs of trauma.

No bruising.

No fractures.

She let out a long breath she hadn't realized she was holding.

---

### Lucy

> "...He's okay..."

---

She looked at the intern.

---

### Lucy

> "...Thank you."

---

The intern scratched the back of his head awkwardly.

---

### Intern

> "...I... didn't really think."

---

### Lucy

> "...Good."

---

## **VII. WAKING UP**

Approximately eighteen minutes later...

Neko stirred.

He blinked slowly.

Looked around.

Realized he was on someone's back.

---

### Neko

> "...Did I faint again?"

---

### Intern

> "...Yeah."

---

### Neko

> "...Sorry..."

---

### Lucy

> "Don't apologize."

---

She handed him a bottle of water.

---

### Lucy

> "Drink."

---

Neko quietly obeyed.

---

### Neko

> "...Did the pipe get fixed?"

---

The nearby engineer laughed in disbelief.

---

### Engineer

> "...You almost fell forty meters."

---

### Neko

> "...Yeah..."

---

A pause.

---

### Neko

> "...But is the pipe fixed?"

---

### Engineer

> "...Yes."

---

### Neko

> "...Good."

---

Lucy sighed.

---

### Lucy

> "...You're impossible."

---

## **VIII. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Director Lucious reviewed the incident footage later that evening.

The recording ended exactly where the intern caught Neko.

He remained silent for several moments.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...How close?"

---

### Safety Officer

> "...Less than half a second."

---

Another pause.

---

### Safety Officer

> "...Had the intern reacted any later..."

---

He didn't finish the sentence.

He didn't need to.

Lucious nodded slowly.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Find the intern."

---

## **IX. THE INTERN**

The following morning...

The intern nervously entered Director Lucious's office.

He had spent the entire night convinced he was about to be reprimanded for abandoning his assigned paperwork route.

He stood at attention.

---

### Intern

> "...Sir?"

---

Director Lucious looked up from the report.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...I read the incident."

---

The intern swallowed.

---

### Intern

> "...About that..."

---

### Director Lucious

> "...You were attempting to leave work early."

---

The intern's shoulders slumped.

---

### Intern

> "...Yes, sir..."

---

A brief silence.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...And in doing so..."

---

He closed the report.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...You saved one of this facility's most valuable people."

---

The intern blinked.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...No disciplinary action."

---

The intern looked completely stunned.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Next time..."

---

Another pause.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Use the front hallway if you're trying to sneak away."

---

The intern stared.

Then realized...

That was probably the closest thing to a joke Director Lucious had ever told him.

---

### Intern

> "...Yes, sir."

---

## **X. FINAL NOTE**

Medical Division confirmed that Neko suffered no injuries beyond temporary exhaustion.

The escort requirement established in **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-131** remains in full effect.

The unnamed intern received a formal commendation for exceptional situational awareness and rapid response.

When informed that he had likely saved Neko's life, the intern reportedly replied:

> **"...I honestly just saw someone falling and reacted."**

When Neko later thanked him personally, he smiled, adjusted his glasses, and quietly said:

> **"Thanks... I owe you one."**

The intern laughed.

> **"Just promise me your next invention is another Glacier Spray... not another Bliss of Whitefall."**

Neko thought about it for a moment.

Then nodded.

> **"...Deal."**

Several engineers present visibly relaxed after hearing that answer.
