# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-181**

### **SPONTANEOUS ALLERGY REMISSION**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – MEDICAL ANOMALY

---

## **I. INCIDENT SUMMARY**

**Location:** Engineering Break Room

At approximately 14:37, Neko was observed staring at a sealed packet of roasted peanuts.

This was immediately noticed by Oracle.

She wrote:

> **No.**

Mirage quietly walked over.

Miss Neko was already approaching from the opposite hallway.

Before either of them could reach him...

Neko opened the packet.

---

### Mirage

> "...Neko."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro, don't—"

---

Neko had already eaten one peanut.

Then another.

Then another.

Mirage immediately removed the bag from his hands.

Medical personnel were called before symptoms could develop.

Everyone waited.

Thirty seconds.

One minute.

Two minutes.

Nothing happened.

---

## **II. MEDICAL RESPONSE**

Expecting another episode of severe anaphylaxis, Medical Division immediately performed emergency assessments.

No swelling.

No difficulty breathing.

No abnormal heart rhythm.

No skin reaction.

No changes in blood oxygen.

Neko remained perfectly healthy.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...Again."

---

Under controlled medical supervision, further testing was conducted over the following days.

Small, carefully measured exposures produced no reaction.

Larger controlled exposures also produced no reaction.

Comprehensive allergy testing was repeated multiple times.

The previous severe peanut allergy...

Could no longer be detected.

---

## **III. CONCLUSION**

Medical Division has concluded that Neko's previously documented peanut allergy has undergone a spontaneous remission.

The exact cause remains unknown.

Researchers have proposed several hypotheses, including:

- Delayed effects of his anomalous regenerative physiology.
    
- Metabolic changes following his revival.
    
- An undocumented adaptive immune response.
    

At present, none can be confirmed.

Medical emphasizes that the earlier reactions were genuine and life-threatening. The disappearance of the allergy does **not** invalidate previous diagnoses or the precautions that were taken.

---

## **IV. THE ROOM**

After the final test...

Medical officially announced:

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...You're no longer allergic."

---

Silence.

Neko blinked.

---

### Neko

> "...Really?"

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...Really."

---

Neko looked toward the small bowl of peanuts sitting on the table.

Then...

Looked at Miss Neko.

---

### Neko

> "...Can I?"

---

Miss Neko folded her arms.

Thought for a long moment.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...One."

---

Neko carefully picked up a single peanut.

Ate it.

Waited.

Nothing happened.

He smiled.

---

## **V. MIRAGE**

Mirage remained skeptical.

---

### Mirage

> "...Eat another."

---

Neko did.

Nothing.

---

### Mirage

> "...Another."

---

Another.

Still nothing.

Mirage finally relaxed.

---

### Mirage

> "...Good."

---

Then he quietly took the bowl away.

---

### Neko

> "...Hey."

---

### Mirage

> "...You don't need the whole bowl."

---

## **VI. ORACLE**

Oracle had watched the entire examination.

She slowly picked up her marker.

Thought for a moment.

Then wrote:

> **Good.**

She erased it.

Wrote again.

> **Still don't eat a whole bag.**

The room burst into laughter.

Even the Lead Medical Researcher nodded.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...That advice still stands."

---

Neko scratched behind Oracle's ears.

---

### Neko

> "...Deal."

---

Oracle purred.

---

## **VII. PROTOCOL UPDATE**

The emergency peanut restriction has been lifted.

Accordingly:

- Neko is no longer prohibited from consuming peanuts.
    
- Peanut-containing foods may once again be served to him.
    
- Emergency epinephrine requirements specific to peanut exposure have been removed.
    

However, Medical Division recommends moderation.

Consuming an entire bag of peanuts in one sitting is discouraged—not because of an allergy, but because it is simply unhealthy.

---

## **VIII. MISS NEKO**

As they left Medical...

Miss Neko handed Neko a very small packet of peanuts.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Welcome back."

---

Neko smiled.

---

### Neko

> "...Thanks."

---

She immediately took the packet back.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...After dinner."

---

Neko sighed dramatically.

---

### Neko

> "...You almost had me."

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Neko's spontaneous loss of his severe peanut allergy has been classified as an anomalous medical event requiring further study. While the exact mechanism remains unknown, repeated supervised testing confirms that he no longer experiences the life-threatening reactions previously documented.

Although the emergency restrictions have been lifted, one habit remains unchanged.

Whenever Neko reaches for a bag of peanuts...

Mirage still instinctively checks the ingredient label.

Oracle still watches.

Miss Neko still asks:

> **"...How many are you planning to eat?"**

After everything that happened...

Old habits are understandably difficult to break.
