# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-170**

### **ORACLE – COGNITIVE DEVELOPMENT I**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – N.O.S.T. / PERSONNEL NOTICE

---

## **I. SUMMARY**

Routine observations have confirmed that Z-885 ("Oracle") has undergone a natural stage of anomalous development.

The changes occurred gradually over several weeks and do not appear to be the result of external anomalous influence.

Medical Division has confirmed Oracle remains healthy.

Research Division has confirmed her primary precognitive ability remains unchanged.

However...

She has acquired several new abilities.

---

## **II. PHYSICAL DEVELOPMENT**

Oracle has grown slightly.

While still unmistakably a kitten, she is now noticeably larger than when first brought to the Blacksite.

Personnel estimate her growth to be approximately equivalent to one month of normal feline development.

Her anomalously slow growth rate remains unchanged.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You're getting bigger."

---

Oracle proudly stood as tall as she could.

---

> "Mew."

---

### Neko

> "...Still tiny."

---

Oracle lightly bonked his hand with her head.

---

## **III. LANGUAGE COMPREHENSION**

Testing has confirmed that Oracle can now understand spoken human language to a remarkable degree.

She consistently demonstrates comprehension of:

- Simple instructions.
    
- Personal names.
    
- Department names.
    
- Safety commands.
    
- Everyday conversation.
    
- Context-dependent requests.
    

She has also demonstrated an ability to distinguish jokes from serious instructions in most situations.

Testing continues regarding abstract concepts.

---

### Researcher

> "...Oracle, where's Neko?"

---

Without hesitation...

Oracle walked directly toward Engineering.

---

### Researcher

> "...Good."

---

### Researcher

> "...Where's Director Lucious?"

---

Oracle immediately turned toward Administration.

---

Testing was repeated seventeen times.

Accuracy exceeded 98%.

---

## **IV. WRITTEN COMMUNICATION**

To better facilitate communication...

Neko designed a custom writing marker.

The marker features:

- A shortened lightweight body.
    
- Soft bite-resistant rubber grip.
    
- Rounded safety cap.
    
- Quick-drying ink.
    
- Balanced weight allowing comfortable use while held in Oracle's mouth.
    

Engineering has affectionately nicknamed it:

> **"The Cat Marker."**

Oracle quickly learned to hold the marker between her teeth.

She writes by moving her head rather than her paws.

Her handwriting is...

Surprisingly neat.

Though slightly larger than average.

---

## **V. FIRST CONVERSATION**

The first successful written exchange occurred in Engineering.

Neko placed a whiteboard on the floor.

Oracle picked up her marker.

---

### Neko

> "...Can you understand me?"

---

Oracle wrote:

> **Yes.**

Silence filled the workshop.

---

### Engineer

> "...She just..."

---

Oracle wrote again.

> **Hello.**

Half the workshop applauded.

Oracle looked rather pleased with herself.

---

## **VI. THE BAD LIFE CHOICE DETECTOR**

Research soon documented another newly developed ability.

Oracle has begun reacting not only to imminent physical danger...

But also...

Terrible decisions.

This ability has been informally designated:

> **Behavioral Hazard Recognition**

Or, according to Engineering:

> **"Bad Life Choice Detection."**

Unlike her future-sight, this ability is not limited to life-threatening events.

Instead...

Oracle will intervene whenever someone is about to make a decision that is likely to end badly.

---

### Example 1

An intern reached toward a clearly labeled button reading:

> **DO NOT PRESS**

Oracle immediately sat on his hand.

---

The intern withdrew.

---

### Oracle (writing)

> **Bad idea.**

---

The button was later confirmed to initiate an emergency decontamination cycle.

---

### Example 2

A researcher prepared to microwave leftover fish in the shared break room.

Oracle intercepted him.

Holding up her marker.

She slowly wrote:

> **Everyone will dislike you.**

The researcher quietly chose a different lunch.

---

### Example 3

A maintenance worker attempted to climb a ladder while balancing three heavy toolboxes.

Oracle tugged at his pant leg until he climbed down.

She then wrote:

> **One trip better than one fall.**

The worker made two trips instead.

---

## **VII. THE CHEESE INCIDENT**

One newly hired employee approached Oracle with a slice of cheese.

Oracle looked at it.

Looked at him.

Slowly picked up her marker.

Wrote:

> **No.**

The employee looked confused.

---

### Employee

> "...You don't want cheese?"

---

Oracle continued writing.

> **I want it.**

A pause.

She added another line.

> **That is why no.**

The cafeteria erupted into laughter.

Even Oracle looked faintly embarrassed.

She carefully pushed the cheese back toward the employee.

Then walked away before she could change her mind.

---

## **VIII. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Director Lucious requested a demonstration.

He stood before Oracle.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Oracle."

---

She looked up.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...How are you?"

---

Oracle picked up her marker.

Thought for several seconds.

Then wrote:

> **Happy.**

Lucious smiled.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Good."

---

Oracle looked around the room.

Then added another sentence.

> **Everyone is together today.**

The room became very quiet.

Because she was right.

Every one of her caretakers happened to be present.

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Z-885's cognitive development represents a significant milestone in her ongoing anomalous growth. While retaining the behavior and temperament of a playful kitten, she now demonstrates comprehension of spoken language and can reliably communicate through written messages using a specially designed marker created by Neko.

Additionally, Oracle's new ability to recognize and discourage poor decisions has already prevented numerous embarrassing, unsafe, or otherwise regrettable situations. Although this ability is less dramatic than her precognitive warnings, Engineering has enthusiastically embraced it as another layer of Blacksite safety.

Director Lucious approved the following addition to Oracle's standing guidance:

> **"If Oracle blocks your path, she's probably saving your life. If Oracle writes 'Bad idea,' she's probably saving your dignity. Listen to her in either case."**
