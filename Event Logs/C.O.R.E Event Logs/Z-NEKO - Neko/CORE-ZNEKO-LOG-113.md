## **I. WEEK FOUR**

Four weeks.

Neko was **still** sick.

The fever had finally become mild, but it stubbornly refused to disappear.

Medical Division had long since given up predicting when he would recover.

At this point, the standard morning exchange had become routine.

---

### Medical Officer

> "How are you feeling?"

---

### Neko

> "...Still sick."

---

### Medical Officer

> "...Figures."

---

Despite everything...

He still clocked in every morning.

Blanket.

Warm tea.

Snowy's round glasses.

And, today...

Something was different.

---

## **II. A SURPRISE**

When Neko entered Engineering, the room went unusually quiet.

Not because he looked worse.

Not because of another strange invention.

Because...

His bangs were gone.

Well...

Not completely.

He had trimmed them much shorter.

They no longer hid his face.

For the first time since anyone could remember...

His azure eyes were clearly visible behind Snowy's round glasses.

---

### Engineer

> "...Wait."

---

Another engineer looked up.

---

### Engineer #2

> "...Your bangs."

---

Neko adjusted his glasses.

---

### Neko

> "...I cut them."

---

### Engineer

> "...You can actually see now."

---

### Neko

> "...I could always see."

---

### Engineer

> "...Fair enough."

## **III. THE GLASSES REMAIN**

The haircut changed one thing.

Not the safety protocol.

The glasses stayed.

Always.

Without them, his eyes would still carry their anomalous effect.

With them...

They were simply beautiful.

Bright sapphire-blue.

Almost luminous.

Calm.

Clear.

No memory loss.

No mesmerization.

Just Neko.

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Neko's shorter bangs marked a surprisingly significant change.

For the first time, he no longer felt the need to hide behind them.

With Snowy's glasses safely suppressing the anomaly, he was comfortable letting people see his eyes every day.

The only thing that hadn't changed...

Was his determination to keep working despite still being ill.

Medical Division continued hoping each week would be his last week as a patient.

Engineering simply accepted that if Neko was going to insist on showing up, they would make sure he did so with a warm drink, plenty of breaks, and absolutely nothing ice cold.
