## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Medical Division officially confirmed that Neko's recovery was complete.

His sapphire eyes once again emitted their characteristic glow.

His strength returned.

His appetite returned.

His curiosity returned.

Unfortunately for Engineering...

So did his tendency to design things simply because he thought they were interesting.

To reduce the probability of another "Bliss of Whitefall Incident," Director Lucious quietly approved a simple, unofficial policy:

> **"If Neko starts saying he's bored, give him maintenance work, a sandwich, or someone to help before he finds a reason to invent something."**

The policy proved remarkably effective.

Life at the Blacksite settled back into its familiar rhythm.

Miss Neko and Neko worked side by side.

Maintenance continued to "borrow" Neko for head pats and piggyback rides.

His gentle little nibbles remained an accepted form of communication—sometimes a polite complaint about being carried incorrectly, sometimes a quiet sign of trust and affection.

The Blacksite had endured fear, loss, uncertainty, and hope.

Now, at last, it had regained something that everyone realized they had missed.

Not extraordinary weapons.

Not impossible anomalies.

Just the ordinary, comforting routine of seeing Neko and Miss Neko walking through the halls together, making the Blacksite feel like home again.
