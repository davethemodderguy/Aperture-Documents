# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-136**

### **THE DAY AFTER**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE / INTERNAL OBSERVATION

## **I. CLARIFICATION**

Following the events recorded in **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-135**, Records Division issued a clarification regarding Neko's birthday.

Neither Neko nor Miss Neko knows their actual birth date.

The date observed by the Blacksite had never been their biological birthday.

Instead, it marked the day that the two entities separated into independent individuals.

Neko accepted the date without objection.

Miss Neko, however, quietly selected a different commemorative day for herself—five days later.

When asked why, she answered simply:

---

### Miss Neko

> "...I'd rather not steal everyone's attention."

---

No one argued.

That answer sounded exactly like something she would say.

---

## **II. THE NEXT MORNING**

The front gate opened.

Neko arrived exactly on time.

---

### Guard

> "...Morning."

---

### Neko

> "Morning."

---

Nothing seemed unusual.

He signed in.

Collected his work tablet.

Walked toward Engineering.

The same routine as always.

---

If anyone had only watched for a few seconds...

They would have believed nothing had changed.

---

## **III. SMALL DIFFERENCES**

The changes were subtle.

Almost invisible.

---

Someone offered to carry him across Maintenance.

---

### Technician

> "Need a lift?"

---

### Neko

He smiled politely.

> "I'm okay."

---

He walked instead.

---

Someone reached over to pat his head after helping repair a coolant pump.

---

Neko took one small step back.

Not abruptly.

Not angrily.

Just enough that the hand stopped halfway.

---

### Neko

> "...Thanks."

---

Then he returned to tightening bolts.

---

The technician slowly lowered their hand.

---

No bite.

No annoyance.

No smile either.

---

Just distance.

## **IV. THE EYES**

Maintenance UV lights activated during routine disinfection.

Several engineers instinctively looked toward Neko.

His eyes still glowed.

Azure.

Beautiful.

Healthy.

---

But quieter.

The glow no longer filled the room with its familiar warmth.

Instead...

It resembled moonlight through winter clouds.

Still present.

Just farther away.

---

Medical confirmed once again:

> **No physical abnormalities detected.**

Miss Neko found Neko reorganizing tools.

Twice.

### Neko

Allowing it.

But not responding.

---

She didn't force it.

### Miss Neko

### Neko

> "...I'm just trying not to need people so much."

Neither of them picked it up immediately.

## **VII. AN UNUSUAL PATTERN**

By the end of the week...

Department supervisors began comparing schedules.

Something felt...

Wrong.

Every time someone planned to spend time with Neko...

Something urgent happened.

Lucy would be called away moments before lunch.

Star's cooking schedule would suddenly change.

Maintenance would receive emergency repairs.

Research would request Engineering support.

Security inspections would begin unexpectedly.

Even Miss Neko's meetings had increased.

At first...

Everyone assumed it was coincidence.

Then Niko overlaid the duty logs.

The timing was...

Uncomfortably consistent.

---

### Niko

> "...This shouldn't happen this often."

---

### Lucy

> "...You're saying someone planned it?"

---

### Niko

> "...I'm saying..."

---

He looked at the data again.

---

### Niko

> "...Either we're having the worst streak of scheduling luck I've ever seen..."

---

He tapped the screen.

---

### Niko

> "...Or someone keeps making sure we're all busy at the same time."

---

The room became quiet.

## **IX. NEKO**

Meanwhile...

Neko knew nothing about the discussions.

Efficiently.

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Medical Division recorded no decline in Neko's physical condition.

However, Behavioral Observation noted a significant change in his interpersonal habits.

- He no longer sought out company.
    
- He declined offers to be carried unless operationally necessary.
    
- He rarely accepted head pats, though he did so politely.
    
- He stopped giving his characteristic playful nibbles almost entirely.
    
- Conversations became shorter.
    
- Smiles became less frequent.
    

His eyes retained their azure glow.

But the instruments continued to register a lower emotional resonance than at any previous point since his revival.

Separately, Administrative Review identified an unusual concentration of overlapping work assignments involving personnel closest to Neko.

No evidence of deliberate interference has yet been found.

At present, the possibility that someone is intentionally manipulating schedules to isolate Neko remains **unconfirmed**.

The case has therefore been opened as a **confidential internal review**.

No suspects have been identified.

No conclusions have been reached.

Only one fact was certain:

Whether by coincidence or by design, the busiest people in the Blacksite kept arriving just a little too late.
