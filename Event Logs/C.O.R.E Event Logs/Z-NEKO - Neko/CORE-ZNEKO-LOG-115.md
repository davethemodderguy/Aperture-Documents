## **I. STATUS UPDATE**

Following the **Permafrost Patch Incident**, Neko's condition deteriorated.

Not dangerously.

But enough that Medical Division finally overruled him.

For the first time in weeks...

He was officially declared **unfit for work**.

---

### Medical Officer

> "No Engineering."

---

### Neko

> "...Okay."

---

The room went silent.

Everyone present slowly looked at him.

---

### Engineer

> "...You... agreed?"

---

### Neko

> "...I don't think I can stand up..."

---

That answer worried everyone far more.

---

## **II. BEDREST**

Neko remained in the shared residential room.

Blankets.

Pillows.

Warm tea.

Medicine.

The familiar reusable ice bag resting gently on his head.

His round glasses remained on the bedside table whenever he slept.

His shortened bangs framed his face neatly, making it easy to see how exhausted he looked.

Even his cat ears drooped.

The tiny bell on his azure collar barely jingled whenever he shifted position.

---

## **III. MISS NEKO**

Miss Neko immediately adjusted her schedule.

Outside of essential duties...

She stayed with him.

Whenever he woke up...

She was usually there.

Sketching.

Or simply sitting quietly nearby.

---

Neko slowly opened his eyes.

---

### Neko

## **X. OBSERVATION**

With Neko bedridden...

Something unexpected happened.

People came to him instead.

Maintenance.

Engineering.

Security.

Researchers.

Even personnel who had barely spoken to him before.

Nobody stayed long.

Usually only a few minutes.

Just enough to ask how he was feeling.

Leave something warm.

Wish him well.

Then quietly leave so he could continue resting.

By the end of the third day...

His bedside table had accumulated:

- Seven mugs.
    
- Three handwritten "Get Well Soon" cards.
    
- A small plush cat someone anonymously left.
    
- Two sandwiches.
    
- A stack of engineering magazines.
    
- One wrench with a sticky note reading:
    

> **"Get back soon. The pipes miss you." — Maintenance**

Miss Neko laughed when she read it aloud.

Neko smiled.

---

### Neko

> "...Tell them..."

---

He yawned.

---

### Neko

> "...I'll fix the pipes..."

---

His eyes closed again before he could finish.

---

## **XI. FINAL OBSERVATION**

For nearly a month, Neko had stubbornly refused to slow down.

It ultimately took a dangerous incident and a crippling headache to force him into bed.

No one complained.

No one pressured him to return.

Instead, the Blacksite quietly adapted around his absence, just as it had adapted around his presence.

Engineering carried on.

Maintenance managed.

The Blacksite continued operating.

And every evening, when Miss Neko checked on her younger brother...

She always asked the same question.

---

### Miss Neko

> "How are you feeling, lil bro?"

---

Even if his voice was barely above a whisper...

His answer never changed.

---

### Neko

> "...A little better."

---

It wasn't a dramatic recovery.

It wasn't instant.

But for the first time in weeks...

Everyone believed him.
