# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-166

### **PEACEFUL DAYS AGAIN**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE

---

## **I. INCIDENT STATUS**

Following four consecutive days of unusually frequent workplace accidents centered around Neko...

The phenomenon ceased.

Completely.

Engineering reported no further unusual mishaps.

No falling tools.

No failing catwalks.

No collapsing shelves.

No loose pipes.

No inexplicable streaks of bad luck.

The Blacksite had returned to its normal rhythm.

---

### Lead Researcher

> "...She's quiet again."

---

## **II. ORACLE**

The change in Oracle's behavior was immediate.

She no longer shadowed Neko's every step.

She stopped standing in front of doors.

Stopped tugging on sleeves.

Stopped blocking hallways.

Instead...

She returned to doing what every kitten should be doing.

Sleeping.

---

## **III. LUNCH BREAK**

Neko sat down with his usual sandwich.

Mirage sat beside him.

Before either of them could begin eating...

Oracle jumped onto the bench.

Walked in a neat little circle across Neko's lap.

Circled twice more.

Then flopped over dramatically.

---

### Neko

> "...Long day?"

---

> "Mrr."

---

He gently scratched under her chin.

Oracle immediately began purring.

Loudly.

---

### Mirage

> "...She's off duty."

---

### Neko

> "...Looks like it."

---

Mirage reached over with one finger.

Oracle grabbed it with both front paws.

Kicked it exactly once.

Then went right back to sleep.

---

### Mirage

> "...Ferocious."

---

### Neko

> "...Terrifying."

---

## **IV. THE FAVORITE NAP SPOT**

Over the next several days...

Personnel noticed a familiar routine.

Whenever Neko took a break...

Oracle would appear.

No matter where he sat.

Workbench.

Toolbox.

Engineering office.

Maintenance corridor.

If there was room on Neko's lap...

That became Oracle's chosen bed.

If there wasn't...

She settled beside Mirage instead.

Sometimes with one paw resting against Neko's arm.

As if making sure he was still there.

---

## **V. MIRAGE**

Mirage eventually discovered a game.

He would quietly teleport a few meters away.

Oracle would wake up.

Look around.

Spot him.

Walk over.

Curl up against his leg.

Mirage would teleport again.

Oracle would repeat the process.

After the sixth teleport...

She simply looked at him.

---

> "...Mew."

---

Mirage smiled.

---

### Mirage

> "...You figured it out."

---

Instead of walking...

Oracle sat exactly where she was.

Waiting.

Mirage sighed dramatically.

Teleported back beside her.

---

### Mirage

> "...I lost."

---

Oracle purred.

Victorious.

---

## **VI. MISS NEKO**

Miss Neko entered Engineering carrying a stack of reports.

She stopped when she saw the scene.

Neko was leaning against the wall reading a maintenance checklist.

Oracle was asleep across his lap.

Mirage sat beside them.

Also reading.

Neither wanted to move.

Neither wanted to wake the kitten.

Miss Neko quietly placed the reports on a nearby desk.

Walked over.

And sat beside them.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Looks like someone's busy."

---

### Neko

> "...Very."

---

### Mirage

> "...Extremely."

---

Miss Neko looked down.

Oracle was dreaming.

One tiny paw twitched.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...I think she's chasing something."

---

### Neko

> "...Hopefully not cheese."

---

All three quietly laughed.

---

## **VII. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

Later that afternoon...

Director Lucious happened to pass through Engineering.

He found the three exactly where Miss Neko had left them.

Neko.

Mirage.

Oracle.

Still together.

Still quiet.

Lucious looked at the sleeping kitten.

Then at Neko.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...No incidents today?"

---

### Neko

> "...None."

---

Lucious smiled.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Good."

---

He continued walking.

After a few steps...

He glanced back once.

Oracle hadn't moved.

She looked completely relaxed.

For the first time in days...

She wasn't guarding anyone.

She was simply a kitten taking a nap.

---

## **VIII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Research Division has concluded the recent cluster of accidents surrounding Neko has ended. Z-885 has displayed no further emergency warning behaviors and has resumed her typical free-roaming, playful routine.

Personnel have also noted that Oracle consistently chooses to spend her quiet moments with Neko and Mirage. Whether curled up in Neko's lap or resting against Mirage's leg, she appears exceptionally comfortable in their company.

Mirage, for his part, has been observed reading manuals one-handed simply to avoid disturbing Oracle when she falls asleep against him. Neko has similarly postponed breaks ending early rather than wake the kitten from a comfortable nap.

A final note was attached to the observation log by Miss Neko:

> **"When Oracle is warning people, everyone pays attention. When she's asleep on Lil bro's lap with Mirage beside them... that's how we know everything is okay again."**
