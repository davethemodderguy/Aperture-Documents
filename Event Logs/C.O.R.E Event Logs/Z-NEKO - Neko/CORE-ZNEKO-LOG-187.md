# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-187**

### **ANOMALOUS EMOTIONAL SUPPRESSION EVENT — WEEK 3**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – INTERNAL / MEDICAL / N.O.S.T. JOINT INVESTIGATION

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Three weeks have passed since the anomalous suppression event.

No improvement has been observed.

No treatment has proven effective.

The two affected individuals continue to exhibit markedly different responses to the suppression.

---

# **I. NEKO**

Neko's condition remains clinically stable.

He continues reporting to Engineering every morning.

He repairs machinery.

He completes inspections.

He performs maintenance with the same precision as before.

Only one aspect of him remains absent.

His creativity.

Engineering has slowly adapted.

Where Neko once produced solutions...

Mirage now supplies them.

---

### Typical Workflow

Mirage studies the problem.

Mirage proposes a new approach.

Neko quietly builds it.

Together, they continue operating almost as efficiently as before.

Several engineers have begun referring to Mirage as:

> **"Neko's imagination."**

The nickname spread quickly.

Mirage never acknowledged it.

Neko only smiled faintly.

---

One afternoon, a prototype cooling system stalled halfway through construction.

Months earlier...

Neko would have improvised.

Instead, he simply looked toward Mirage.

---

### Neko

> "...Any ideas?"

---

Mirage glanced at the design.

Within seconds, he had three.

---

### Mirage

> "...Let's try this."

---

Neko nodded.

Without hesitation.

---

The prototype worked.

Engineering celebrated.

Neko quietly returned to work.

---

Later that evening, Mirage was asked how he felt about effectively becoming Neko's creative half.

He answered simply.

---

### Mirage

> "Until he gets his back..."

> "...I'll lend him mine."

---

# **II. STAR**

Star's condition continued to deteriorate.

She still attended every required briefing.

Still completed every assigned operation.

Still responded politely when spoken to.

But outside of duty...

She withdrew almost completely.

---

She no longer spent time in the common areas.

She no longer joined evening meals.

She no longer visited Engineering to see Neko.

Instead...

Personnel repeatedly found her sitting alone in the same quiet corner of the N.O.S.T. observation lounge.

There was nothing special about the corner.

No remarkable view.

No comfortable chair.

Just a place where few people walked.

---

She would sit there for hours.

Sometimes looking at the floor.

Sometimes looking out the window.

Often...

Looking at nothing at all.

---

# **III. NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade continued visiting every evening.

Sometimes with tea.

Sometimes with books.

Sometimes with absolutely nothing.

She eventually stopped trying to fill the silence.

Instead...

She simply sat beside Star.

On one occasion, nearly an hour passed without either of them speaking.

As Nightshade stood to leave...

Star quietly spoke.

---

### Star

> "...Thank you."

---

Nightshade looked back.

---

### Nightshade

> "...For what?"

---

Star hesitated.

---

> "...For sitting here."

---

Nightshade smiled gently.

---

### Nightshade

> "...You never have to thank me for that."

---

# **IV. THE WEIGHT OF SILENCE**

Medical personnel observed that Star had begun pausing before answering even simple questions.

---

### Researcher

> "How are you today?"

---

A long silence.

---

### Star

> "...I'm here."

---

### Researcher

> "...Anything else?"

---

Another pause.

---

> "...Not really."

---

Her responses were never hostile.

Never dismissive.

Simply...

Heavy.

As though every sentence required effort.

---

# **V. ASTER**

Aster remained unaware of the cause.

She noticed only that Star spent more time alone.

Every day, she visited the same corner.

Sometimes she brought flowers.

Sometimes she drew little pictures.

Sometimes she simply curled up beside Star.

One afternoon, she pushed her whiteboard toward her.

It read:

> **Can I stay here?**

---

Star looked down.

A small, tired smile appeared.

She nodded.

---

### Star

> "...Please."

---

Aster climbed into her lap.

Curled into a small white ball.

Began purring softly.

The two remained there until the end of Star's shift.

Neither said another word.

---

# **VI. OBSERVATIONS**

Personnel reported an unusual change.

The observation corner had quietly become known as:

> **"Star's Corner."**

Nobody officially named it.

The designation simply appeared in casual conversation.

Engineers walking past lowered their voices.

Researchers avoided unnecessary interruptions.

No one wanted to disturb the quiet.

---

# **VII. DIRECTOR'S REVIEW**

Director Lucious reviewed the latest reports in silence.

After several minutes, he wrote only one sentence beneath the Week Three summary.

> **"The anomaly has not taken away Star's compassion. It has taken away the light she used to carry it with."**

He closed the folder.

No additional comments were added.

---

# **VIII. STATUS – END OF WEEK THREE**

**Neko**

- Creativity remains fully suppressed.
    
- Engineering performance remains excellent with Mirage providing innovative solutions.
    
- Psychological condition stable.
    

**Star**

- Positivity remains fully suppressed.
    
- Increasing social withdrawal observed.
    
- Frequently spends extended periods sitting alone in a quiet corner outside operational duties.
    
- Continues to perform all assigned responsibilities without error.
    
- Receives regular companionship from Nightshade and, increasingly, Aster.
    

**Mirage**

- Continues serving as Neko's creative counterpart during engineering work.
    
- No anomalous effects detected.
    

**Aster**

- Unaware of her suspected involvement.
    
- Continues attempting to comfort Star through quiet companionship.
    
- Remains under observation.
    

The suppression event remains unresolved.

Research continues.
