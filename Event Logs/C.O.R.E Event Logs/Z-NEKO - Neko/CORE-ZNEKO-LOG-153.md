# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-153**

### **"MISS NEKO'S BED"**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – ENGINEERING / MEDICAL EQUIPMENT

---

## **I. BACKGROUND**

Although Miss Neko had become accustomed to sleeping around Neko's shoulders, Medical Division noted one drawback.

Neko still had to:

- Bend over during repairs.
    
- Crawl beneath machinery.
    
- Climb ladders.
    
- Enter maintenance tunnels.
    
- Carry heavy equipment.
    

While Miss Neko never complained, these movements frequently disturbed her sleep.

Engineering was therefore asked a simple question.

> **"Can we give Miss Neko somewhere safer to rest while Neko works?"**

Neko answered before anyone else.

---

### Neko

> "...I can build something."

---

## **II. THE DESIGN**

Three days later...

Neko presented a prototype.

Official Designation:

> **Portable Feline Recovery Containment Pack**

Unofficial Designation (adopted by literally everyone):

> **"Miss Neko's Bed."**

Or, among Maintenance:

> **"Miss Neko's Bed on Neko's Back."**

---

## **III. SPECIFICATIONS**

The device resembled a compact backpack with a large transparent capsule mounted to its rear.

Constructed primarily from reinforced Nekonium composite glass, the capsule offered excellent visibility while remaining lightweight.

Features included:

- Bullet-resistant construction.
    
- Blast-resistant shell designed to withstand nearby explosions.
    
- Soft memory-foam bedding.
    
- Temperature regulation to keep the interior comfortable.
    
- Adjustable ventilation with dust filtration.
    
- Built-in water dispenser.
    
- Small food compartment.
    
- Internal lighting with automatic day/night brightness.
    
- Sound-dampening insulation to reduce noise from heavy machinery.
    
- Quick-release emergency access from both inside and outside.
    
- Medical monitoring sensors linked to Medical Division.
    

The interior was surprisingly spacious for Miss Neko's cat form.

It even included...

A tiny pillow.

---

### Engineer

> "...You made her a pillow?"

---

### Neko

> "...She likes pillows."

---

## **IV. FIRST TEST**

Miss Neko inspected the backpack carefully.

She sniffed it once.

Walked around it.

Looked up at Neko.

---

### Miss Neko

> "Mrr?"

---

### Neko

> "...It's for you."

---

She cautiously stepped inside.

Walked one circle.

Then another.

Curled up on the cushion.

Within seconds—

She began purring.

---

### Neko

> "...I guess she approves."

---

The tiny pillow was immediately claimed.

---

## **XI. FINAL OBSERVATION**

The **Portable Feline Recovery Containment Pack** has been approved for continued use throughout Miss Neko's recovery period.

Medical reports indicate that the stable, enclosed environment allows her to sleep more comfortably while remaining close to Neko during the workday. The reinforced capsule also provides substantial protection from accidental impacts and environmental hazards commonly encountered in Engineering.

Personnel are reminded not to tap on the enclosure unnecessarily or attempt to wake Miss Neko unless there is a genuine operational or medical need.

Maintenance's unofficial label has become so widespread that even official reports occasionally include it in parentheses:

> **Portable Feline Recovery Containment Pack** _(commonly referred to as "Miss Neko's Bed on Neko's Back")._

A final handwritten note, found taped to the inside of the backpack after Neko finished assembling it, read:

> **"Get well soon, sis. Until then, you've got the safest bed I know how to build."**
