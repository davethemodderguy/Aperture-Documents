## **IX. PROTOCOL UPDATE**

### **Handling Neko**

If you intend to carry Neko:

- Ask first.
    
- Support him comfortably.
    
- Avoid lifting him by one arm or awkward positions.
    
- If Neko begins lightly biting your hand, immediately adjust your grip or set him down.
    

The bite is **not** considered aggressive behavior.

It is Neko's way of communicating that he is uncomfortable.

No disciplinary action is to be taken against Neko for this response.

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

The discovery of Neko's small fangs surprised very few people.

Most simply accepted it as another cat-like trait.

The bites themselves are intentionally gentle.

They do not break skin under normal circumstances and are better described as a clear, instinctive "please stop" than an attack.

Among Maintenance, a new saying quickly appeared:

> **"If Neko bites you, check your grip before blaming the cat."**
