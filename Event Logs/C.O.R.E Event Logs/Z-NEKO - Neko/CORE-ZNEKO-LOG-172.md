# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-172

### **OFF DUTY DOES NOT EXIST**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE

---

## **I. CONTINUING OPERATIONS**

Following Neko's injury, daily Blacksite operations returned to normal.

Oracle resumed her regular duties without prompting.

She continued to:

- Warn personnel of imminent danger.
    
- Interrupt hazardous actions.
    
- Detect questionable life choices.
    
- Patrol departments at her own discretion.
    

Her effectiveness remained unchanged.

Personnel had developed a simple habit.

If Oracle blocked your path...

You waited.

If Oracle wrote:

> **Bad idea.**

You immediately reconsidered whatever you were about to do.

Incident reports involving ignored warnings had dropped to nearly zero.

---

## **II. VISITING HOURS**

No matter how busy she became...

Oracle always found time to visit Neko.

Sometimes during lunch.

Sometimes between patrols.

Sometimes after preventing what Security described as:

> "Another impressively avoidable disaster."

She would quietly trot into Engineering Medical.

Hop onto Neko's bed, chair, or lap.

Purr once.

As if checking him in.

---

### Neko

> "...Hey, Oracle."

---

She would answer every single time.

> "Mew."

---

Then she'd settle in for a few minutes before returning to work.

---

## **III. THE MARKER**

One afternoon...

Oracle arrived carrying her special marker.

Neko immediately knew what was coming.

---

### Neko

> "...You're going to write on me, aren't you?"

---

Oracle nodded.

---

> **Yes.**

---

She picked up the marker.

Thought carefully.

Then began writing on the back of Neko's hand.

When she finished...

It read:

> **Good Engineer**

Neko smiled.

---

### Neko

> "...Thanks."

---

---

The following day...

She wrote on his forearm instead.

> **Drink Water**

---

### Neko

> "...Already did."

---

Oracle underlined it anyway.

---

## **IV. ESCALATION**

Within a week...

Oracle had become increasingly creative.

Messages documented included:

- **Take Break**
    
- **Stretch**
    
- **Sleep Earlier**
    
- **No Overwork**
    
- **You Promised**
    
- **Listen To Medical**
    
- **No Climbing**
    

Every message was handwritten.

Every message appeared somewhere different.

Hands.

Arms.

Sleeves.

Occasionally...

His cheek.

---

## **V. THE FACE INCIDENT**

Oracle climbed onto Neko's shoulder.

Looked thoughtfully at his face.

Picked up the marker.

---

### Neko

> "...Oracle..."

---

She gently wrote on his forehead.

Finished.

Sat back.

Admired her work.

---

Miss Neko walked into the room.

Looked at Neko.

Stopped.

Started laughing.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro..."

---

### Neko

> "...What?"

---

She pointed toward a mirror.

Written neatly across his forehead was:

> **Rest**

With a tiny heart beside it.

---

### Neko

> "...I suppose I deserve this."

---

He made absolutely no attempt to wipe it off.

---

## **VI. WHY HE DOESN'T STOP HER**

Several engineers eventually asked.

---

### Engineer

> "...Why don't you stop her?"

---

Neko looked at Oracle.

She was concentrating very hard while drawing a tiny paw print on the sleeve of his hoodie.

He smiled.

---

### Neko

> "...Because she likes doing it."

---

The engineer blinked.

---

### Engineer

> "...That's all?"

---

Neko nodded.

---

### Neko

> "...It's washable."

---

He watched Oracle proudly finish another tiny doodle.

---

### Neko

> "...And it makes her happy."

---

Oracle looked up.

Purred.

Then added a second paw print.

---

## **VII. MIRAGE**

Mirage quietly observed the growing collection of messages.

One afternoon...

He asked Oracle a question.

---

### Mirage

> "...Why only Neko?"

---

Oracle paused.

Thought carefully.

Picked up her marker.

Wrote:

> **He forgets.**

Mirage tilted his head.

---

### Mirage

> "...Forgets what?"

---

Oracle added another line.

> **To take care of himself.**

Mirage smiled softly.

---

### Mirage

> "...You're right."

---

## **VIII. THE CAST**

As weeks passed...

Neko's cast became less notable than the messages Oracle continued leaving on him.

Medical eventually removed the cast.

The first thing Oracle did after seeing his healed leg...

Was write on his sleeve.

> **No More Pipes**

Neko laughed.

---

### Neko

> "...I'll try."

---

Oracle slowly wrote another sentence.

> **Promise?**

---

Neko looked at the tiny kitten.

Then nodded.

---

### Neko

> "...Promise."

---

Oracle seemed satisfied.

She erased the board herself with one paw.

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Oracle has fully resumed her role as the Blacksite's precognitive guardian while maintaining a daily habit of checking on Neko during his recovery.

In addition to her official duties, she has developed a peculiar but well-loved tradition of writing encouraging messages and drawing tiny paw prints on Neko's arms, hands, sleeves, and occasionally his face with her specially designed marker. The ink is non-toxic and easily washable, and Neko has consistently declined to stop her.

When asked why, he simply replied:

> **"She spends all day looking after everyone else. If drawing on me for five minutes makes her smile... I can live with that."**

Personnel have since adopted an unofficial policy:

> **If you see writing on Neko's face, don't erase it immediately. Chances are Oracle was proud of it.**
