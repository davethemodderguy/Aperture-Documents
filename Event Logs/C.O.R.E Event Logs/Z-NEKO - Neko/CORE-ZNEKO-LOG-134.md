# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-134**

### **THE LIGHT IN HIS EYES**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE OBSERVATION

---

## **I. NO LONGER A MEDICAL ISSUE**

Medical Division noted an unexpected development.

Neko had not experienced a fainting episode in over three weeks.

His metabolism continued recovering.

His stamina had improved significantly.

His appetite had returned to normal.

Physically...

He was getting better.

---

Emotionally...

No one noticed he wasn't.

---

## **II. DISTANCE**

It happened so gradually that nobody could point to the day it began.

Neko stopped waiting outside Maintenance.

He stopped wandering into Star's kitchen to ask if she was making sandwiches.

He stopped looking into Miss Neko's workshop whenever she was in a meeting.

Instead...

He simply worked.

If someone asked for help—

He helped.

If nobody called—

He stayed in his lab.

When lunch arrived...

He ate alone.

When work finished...

He quietly returned to his room.

No complaints.

No requests.

No interruptions.

---

## **III. "THEY'RE BUSY."**

Lucy noticed it first.

She found Neko sitting alone in Engineering after everyone else had gone home.

---

### Lucy

> "...You're still here?"

---

Neko looked up from a maintenance schematic.

---

### Neko

> "...Just finishing this."

---

### Lucy

> "...Want company?"

---

He smiled politely.

It was a genuine smile.

Just... smaller than it used to be.

---

### Neko

> "...You're busy."

---

### Lucy

> "...I can make time."

---

Neko shook his head.

---

### Neko

> "...It's okay."

> "...Everyone's busy."

---

Lucy stood there for a moment.

She wanted to stay.

Her radio crackled.

Another maintenance call.

She closed her eyes briefly.

---

### Lucy

> "...I'll come back."

---

### Neko

> "...Okay."

---

When she returned an hour later...

The workshop was empty.

---

## **IV. THE GLOW**

Maintenance Sector activated its routine UV disinfection lights.

For months, they had been a reassuring sight.

Neko's sapphire eyes would softly glow beneath his glasses.

Today...

Someone paused.

---

### Engineer

> "...Are his eyes dimmer?"

---

Another looked over.

---

### Second Engineer

> "...Maybe it's just the lighting."

---

It wasn't.

The glow was still there.

But noticeably softer.

Less vibrant.

Like a lantern running low on oil.

Medical examined him.

His vital signs were excellent.

No fever.

No metabolic issues.

No neurological abnormalities.

Nothing explained the change.

---

## **V. A DISCOVERY**

During a routine interview, Medical asked Neko to recall different memories while monitoring the anomalous properties of his eyes.

When he spoke about engineering...

The glow remained steady.

When he described Whitefall Lake...

It brightened slightly.

When he talked about Snowy...

It shimmered.

Then they asked him about the Blacksite.

About home.

About everyone.

He smiled.

---

### Neko

> "...They're all doing well."

---

The glow weakened.

Not dramatically.

Just enough for the instruments to record it.

The Lead Researcher frowned.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...Again."

---

Neko repeated the sentence.

---

The same result.

---

Hours later, another test was conducted.

Star visited unexpectedly.

She handed Neko a homemade sandwich.

He laughed.

A real laugh.

The instruments immediately reacted.

The glow strengthened.

Bright enough that the sensors briefly recalibrated.

The room went silent.

---

Medical repeated the tests over the next several days.

The conclusion remained consistent.

His eyes no longer reflected only his anomalous state.

They reflected something deeply personal.

Joy.

Hope.

Comfort.

Belonging.

When those feelings grew stronger...

So did the light.

When they faded...

The glow dimmed.

---

## **VI. UNHEARD STORIES**

One evening...

Niko found Neko quietly repairing an old toolbox.

---

### Niko

> "...That doesn't need fixing."

---

### Neko

> "...I know."

---

### Niko

> "...Then why?"

---

Neko tightened one last screw.

---

### Neko

> "...It gives me something to do."

---

Silence.

---

### Niko

> "...You've been spending a lot of time alone."

---

Neko shrugged.

---

### Neko

> "...Everyone has work."

---

### Niko

> "...You have work too."

---

### Neko

> "...Yeah."

---

He closed the toolbox.

---

### Neko

> "...Work doesn't get disappointed if I don't interrupt it."

---

Niko looked at him.

For the first time...

He noticed how little light remained in Neko's eyes.

---

## **VII. MISS NEKO**

Late that night...

Miss Neko returned from an engineering meeting.

Neko's bed was empty.

She eventually found him sitting outside the Blacksite.

Looking at the stars.

---

She sat beside him.

Neither spoke.

After several quiet minutes—

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro."

---

### Neko

> "...Hm?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You can tell me."

---

He looked down.

---

### Neko

> "...Tell you what?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...What's making your eyes so quiet."

---

Neko was silent for a long time.

The wind drifted across the courtyard.

---

Finally—

---

### Neko

> "...I know everyone cares."

---

A pause.

---

### Neko

> "...But lately..."

---

He searched for the right words.

---

### Neko

> "...I feel like I'm just... there."

---

His voice never broke.

It was calm.

Almost matter-of-fact.

---

### Neko

> "...No one's doing anything wrong."

---

Another pause.

---

### Neko

> "...I just don't want to bother anyone."

---

Miss Neko reached over and gently took his hand.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You never bother me."

---

Neko smiled.

A small one.

---

### Neko

> "...I know."

---

But the light in his eyes did not brighten.

Not yet.

---

## **VIII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Medical Division determined that the reduction in the luminosity of Neko's eyes was **not associated with any decline in physical health**.

Instead, evidence strongly suggested that the phenomenon had become linked to his emotional well-being.

One researcher summarized the finding in a single sentence:

> **"His eyes don't tell us how healthy he is anymore."**

> **"They tell us how he's feeling."**

Unfortunately...

The Blacksite remained exceptionally busy.

Most personnel saw only that Neko was healthy again.

He no longer fainted.

He no longer required constant supervision.

He completed every assignment on time.

From a distance...

Everything looked normal.

Only those who stopped long enough to truly look into his eyes would have noticed that their familiar sapphire glow had grown faint.

Not because his body was failing.

But because, behind the quiet smile and the completed work orders, the stories they told had become lonelier.

And while the Blacksite never stopped caring about Neko...

For a little while, it became too busy to notice that he needed someone to simply sit beside him and listen.
