# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-173**

### **RECOVERY PROTOCOL – NEKO**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – MEDICAL DIRECTIVE

---

## **I. RECOVERY STATUS**

Medical Division reports that Neko's fractured right leg is healing as expected.

His anomalous regeneration has significantly accelerated bone repair, but the healing process remains incomplete.

Current condition:

- Fracture alignment: **Stable**
    
- Bone regeneration: **Progressing steadily**
    
- Pain level: **Mild to Moderate**
    
- Weight-bearing: **Limited**
    
- Recovery outlook: **Excellent**
    

While the prognosis is favorable, Medical Division emphasizes that regeneration can only repair damage if additional injury is avoided.

As such, Neko remains under temporary activity restrictions.

---

## **II. REQUIRED RECOVERY GUIDELINES**

To support complete recovery, Neko is strongly encouraged to follow the following daily regimen.

### Whitefall Snow

A medically approved quantity of **Whitefall Snow** is to be consumed according to Medical Division's schedule.

Researchers believe the unique properties of Whitefall Snow complement Neko's regenerative metabolism, improving overall recovery and reducing fatigue.

---

### Sandwiches

Neko's unusually fast metabolism requires increased caloric intake during regeneration.

Accordingly, he is encouraged to eat:

**Lots of sandwiches.**

The recommendation has since become something of a running joke throughout Engineering.

---

### No Aggressive Movements

Until Medical Division grants clearance:

- No sprinting.
    
- No jumping.
    
- No climbing ladders.
    
- No lifting heavy machinery.
    
- No "I'll be fine."
    

Especially no "I'll be fine."

---

### Drink Plenty of Water

Proper hydration is considered essential for maintaining Neko's accelerated recovery.

Personnel are encouraged to remind him regularly.

---

## **III. THE REMINDER TEAM**

Medical Division has unofficially delegated reminder duties to those most likely to be listened to.

In order of effectiveness:

- Miss Neko
    
- Mirage
    
- Oracle
    

Research notes that these three have achieved the highest compliance rates.

---

## **IV. MISS NEKO**

Every morning...

Miss Neko appears carrying breakfast.

Usually...

Several sandwiches.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Morning, Lil bro."

---

She places the plate in front of him.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Eat."

---

### Neko

> "...I'm not that hungry."

---

Miss Neko simply stares.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Eat."

---

Neko quietly starts eating.

No further discussion occurs.

---

## **V. MIRAGE**

Mirage specializes in preventing unnecessary movement.

Whenever Neko instinctively reaches for something just out of reach...

Mirage is already holding it.

---

### Neko

> "...When did you get over there?"

---

### Mirage

> "...Before you tried standing."

---

If Neko attempts to move too quickly...

Mirage calmly says:

> "...No."

---

It somehow works every time.

---

## **VI. ORACLE**

Oracle has become Medical Division's most persistent reminder.

She keeps her marker with her at all times.

Examples documented over the past week include:

On Neko's notebook:

> **Drink Water**

On his lunch tray:

> **Finish Sandwich**

On the whiteboard beside his bed:

> **No Running**

On his crutch:

> **Use Me**

On his cast before it was removed:

> **Heal First**

She has also been observed simply placing a bottle of water in front of him.

Then staring.

Until he drinks.

---

### Neko

> "...Okay, okay."

---

Only after he finishes does Oracle nod in approval.

---

## **VII. THE THREE-WAY AMBUSH**

One afternoon...

Neko quietly attempted to stand without telling anyone.

He made it exactly one step.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro."

---

He froze.

Mirage was already standing beside his crutches.

Holding them out.

---

### Mirage

> "...These."

---

Oracle calmly held up her marker.

Already written:

> **Bad idea.**

Neko looked from one to another.

Sighed.

Sat back down.

---

### Neko

> "...I didn't even get two steps."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Correct."

---

### Mirage

> "...New record."

---

Oracle added another line.

> **Good choice.**

---

She rewarded him with a gentle head bump.

---

## **VIII. MEDICAL OBSERVATION**

Lead Medical Researcher reports that Neko's recovery compliance has improved dramatically.

While he still occasionally attempts to do more than he should, intervention from Miss Neko, Mirage, and Oracle consistently redirects him before any setbacks occur.

Personnel have also noted that Neko no longer seems embarrassed by the constant reminders.

Instead...

He has simply accepted that the three of them have formed an unofficial "Recovery Committee."

---

### Neko

> "...Do I get a vote?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...No."

---

### Mirage

> "...Denied."

---

Oracle immediately wrote:

> **Rest.**

---

Neko looked around.

Three against one.

He sighed dramatically.

---

### Neko

> "...Fine."

---

Oracle climbed into his lap and purred.

Mission accomplished.

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Medical Division expects a complete recovery provided current precautions remain in effect.

Personnel are reminded that Neko's regeneration accelerates healing—it does not make him invulnerable. Continued adherence to dietary recommendations, hydration, and movement restrictions remains essential.

Director Lucious attached the final note to the directive:

> **"Healing isn't just about time. It's also about letting the people who care about you tell you to sit down once in a while. Fortunately for Neko, he has three very determined reminders who refuse to let him forget."**
