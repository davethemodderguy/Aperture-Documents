## **I. RECOVERY UPDATE**

One week after the onset of her illness, Medical Division confirmed that Miss Neko's condition had stabilized.

Unfortunately...

She was still far too weak to maintain her human form for more than a few minutes.

Mandatory rest was extended for another week.

This decision was met with...

---

### Tiny Cat Miss Neko

> "Mrr..."

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "No."

---

> "Mew."

---

### Researcher

> "Still no."

---

She flattened her ears dramatically.

It did not change the decision.

---

## **II. A NEW HABIT**

Since she was unable to work normally...

Miss Neko developed a routine.

Every morning...

She would quietly climb onto Neko's shoulders.

Circle once.

Twice.

Then settle herself comfortably around the back of his neck.

Like a warm scarf.

Her tail naturally draped over one shoulder.

She fit there surprisingly well.

---

### Engineer

> "...Morning, Neko."

---

### Neko

> "...Morning."

---

The engineer looked at Miss Neko.

Who was currently asleep.

Using Neko as a pillow.

---

### Engineer

> "...Is..."

---

### Neko

> "...Yep."

---

### Engineer

> "...Comfortable?"

---

### Neko

> "...Apparently."

---

Without opening her eyes...

Tiny Cat Miss Neko purred softly.

## **III. ENGINEERING**

The arrangement quickly became normal.

Neko worked.

Mirage assisted.

Miss Neko slept.

An observer walking into Engineering for the first time would usually witness the following:

- Mirage holding a heavy pipe perfectly steady.
    
- Tiny Cat Miss Neko sleeping around Neko's neck.
    

No one questioned it anymore.

Despite sleeping most of the day...

Miss Neko somehow remained aware of Neko's work.

Whenever he made a small mistake—

Misreading a measurement...

Or forgetting a bolt...

Then—

A very gentle nibble on his shoulder or the side of his neck.

---

### Neko

---

Then at the correct wrench.

---

### Neko

> "...Right."

---

### Engineer

### Neko

> "...She just corrected me."

> "Mrr."

---

Then immediately went back to sleep.

---

### Neko

> "...Years of practice."

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Medical reports indicate Miss Neko's recovery is progressing steadily, although she remains unable to safely resume regular duties in human form.

During this period, she has consistently chosen to accompany Neko throughout the workday in her cat form, typically resting around his shoulders. Personnel have observed that she occasionally responds to small mistakes with a light, non-injurious nibble, after which Neko immediately recognizes the error and corrects it. This behavior appears to function as an informal communication between the siblings rather than aggression.

Mirage continues to provide valuable assistance by handling routine tasks, carrying equipment, and anticipating simple requests, allowing Neko to balance both workloads more effectively.

A handwritten note from Engineering was attached to the weekly report:

> **"Current workflow: One engineer, one hologram, and one sleeping supervisor disguised as a scarf. Against all expectations... it's working remarkably well."**
