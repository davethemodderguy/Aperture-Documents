### Final Observation

Everyone hated what had happened.

Not because Neko was gone.

But because he wasn't.

They could still see him.

Still talk to him.

Still sit beside him.

Still watch him walk past.

And every time they did, they remembered the version of Neko who laughed, argued, invented impossible things, accidentally caused blackouts, traded scientific miracles for sandwiches, and filled rooms with energy.

The contrast hurt.

But one fact remained.

Z-X09 had taken a great deal from him.

Yet the story was still being written.

Because despite everything,

Niko still showed up.

The bird still showed up.

And Neko still got up every morning.

For now, that was enough to keep the file open.
