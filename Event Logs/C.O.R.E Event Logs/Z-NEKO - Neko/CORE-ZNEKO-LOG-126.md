# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-126**

### **WHITEFALL PROTOCOL**

### **Classification:** INTERNAL – OMEGA PRIORITY / N.O.S.T.

---

## **I. THE MISSING PAGE**

Three days into **Operation: Wake the Sleeping**, Research Division had exhausted nearly every surviving page of Snowy's journals.

Every note had been scanned.

Every equation reconstructed.

Every margin examined under ultraviolet light.

Nothing.

The missing piece still wasn't there.

Until...

An archivist noticed something unusual.

One of the journals was thicker than it should have been.

The back cover had a false lining.

Hidden inside was a folded sheet of paper.

The handwriting was unmistakably Snowy's.

The room became completely silent.

---

### Niko

> "...There's another page."

---

Miss Neko unfolded it with trembling hands.

---

## **II. SNOWY'S FINAL INSTRUCTION**

The page contained only a few lines.

No diagrams.

No formulas.

No explanations.

Just instructions.

---

> **"If the light disappears..."**

> **"Do not leave me indoors."**

> **"Take me home."**

> **"Whitefall's snow remembers."**

> **"Bury me beneath the deepest untouched snow."**

> **"Do not cover my face."**

> **"Do not disturb the snow until it chooses to release me."**

---

Nobody spoke.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...That's it?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...No science..."

---

### Niko

> "...Just instructions."

---

## **III. WHITEFALL LAKE**

Every known record concerning Whitefall Lake was immediately reviewed.

For years, it had been considered anomalous.

The snow there behaved unlike ordinary snow.

It did not melt according to normal thermodynamics.

It carried traces of unknown energy that could not be replicated anywhere else.

Several N.O.S.T. expeditions had documented the phenomenon.

None had ever explained it.

---

### Researcher

> "...We've been treating Whitefall like a location."

---

He looked toward Snowy's note.

---

### Researcher

> "...What if it's part of the process?"

---

## **IV. THE DECISION**

A meeting was held immediately.

Present:

- Director Lucious
    
- Miss Neko
    
- Niko
    
- Star
    
- Nightshade
    
- Lead Medical Researcher
    
- N.O.S.T. Division
    

The discussion lasted less than ten minutes.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Any objections?"

---

No one answered.

Not because everyone was certain.

Because there was nothing left to lose.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...He loved Whitefall."

---

### Star

> "...It's home."

---

### Niko

> "...Snowy trusted it."

---

Nightshade looked toward Neko's resting place.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Then let's trust him."

---

Director Lucious nodded.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Prepare transport."

---

## **V. THE JOURNEY HOME**

The trip to Whitefall Lake was unlike any previous mission.

No weapons were loaded.

No combat teams deployed.

No scientific chatter filled the aircraft.

Only silence.

Neko was transported with the utmost care, wrapped in warm blankets despite the destination.

Miss Neko never let go of his hand during the journey.

The aircraft landed just beyond the frozen shoreline.

Fresh snow fell gently from an overcast sky.

The lake was almost perfectly still.

---

### Star

_(softly)_

> "...We're home."

---

## **VI. THE SNOW**

Following Snowy's instructions exactly, N.O.S.T. personnel located a stretch of deep, untouched snow near the lakeside.

No machinery was used.

The snow was moved carefully by hand.

Miss Neko knelt first.

She brushed away the top layer.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Sorry, lil bro."

---

Her voice trembled.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...This is going to be cold."

---

Niko quietly smiled through tears.

---

### Niko

> "...He'd complain if it wasn't."

---

Even Nightshade let out the faintest, reluctant laugh.

---

Neko was gently laid into the natural hollow.

His face remained uncovered, exactly as Snowy's instructions specified.

Fresh Whitefall snow was carefully gathered around him until he rested completely embraced by it.

Only his peaceful face remained visible beneath the falling flakes.

No one disturbed the snow afterward.

---

## **VII. WAITING**

Hours passed.

Nothing happened.

No light.

No sound.

No measurable anomaly.

Medical monitors continued to report no change.

The only sound was the wind moving softly across Whitefall Lake.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...Did we misunderstand it?"

---

Before anyone could answer—

A N.O.S.T. sensor emitted a quiet tone.

---

### Technician

> "...Director."

---

The monitor displayed an unfamiliar energy signature.

It did not originate from Neko.

Nor from any equipment.

It originated...

From the snow itself.

---

The snow surrounding him shimmered with a faint azure glow.

Almost imperceptible.

As though countless tiny lights had awakened beneath the surface.

No one moved.

No one spoke.

---

### Star

_(barely above a whisper)_

> "...The snow..."

---

### Miss Neko

Her eyes never left her younger brother.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...It remembers."

---

## **VIII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

The phenomenon began approximately four hours after Neko was placed within the untouched snowfields of Whitefall Lake.

N.O.S.T. instruments detected previously unrecorded anomalous activity emanating exclusively from the surrounding snow.

No conclusions could yet be drawn.

No declaration of recovery could be made.

But for the first time since Neko's illness began...

The monitors showed **something had changed**.

Director Lucious ordered that no one disturb the site.

---

### Director Lucious

> "We followed Snowy's instructions."

He looked across the silent lake.

> "Now..."

> "We wait."

---

As gentle snow continued to fall over Whitefall Lake, Miss Neko remained seated beside the snow-covered resting place of her younger brother.

She smiled faintly.

Not because she knew what would happen next.

But because, after weeks of dead ends...

There was finally a reason to hope again.
