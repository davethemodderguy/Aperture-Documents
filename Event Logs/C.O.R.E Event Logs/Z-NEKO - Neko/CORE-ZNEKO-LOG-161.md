# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-161**

### **PARTY TRICKS**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL OBSERVATION

---

## **I. SUMMARY**

Following several months of increasingly relaxed interactions between key personnel, an amusing trend has emerged throughout the Blacksite.

It began with Mirage.

His habit of unexpectedly doing something completely unnecessary—but entertaining—encouraged others to reveal harmless abilities they rarely, if ever, used during normal operations.

These abilities are now unofficially referred to as:

> **"Party Tricks."**

They are not intended for combat.

They serve no practical engineering purpose.

They simply make people smile.

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

None of the documented "party tricks" have demonstrated adverse effects or posed any risk to personnel. Psychological Division notes that these harmless displays have become a welcome source of levity during otherwise demanding workdays.

Interestingly, despite Mirage originating from Neko's anomalous ability, he possesses telekinesis and short-range teleportation—abilities that Neko himself does not have. When questioned about this discrepancy, Neko simply shrugged and remarked that Mirage had apparently "developed his own style."

Engineering has since adopted an informal saying:

> **"Every anomaly has a purpose. Every engineer has a party trick."**

To which Mirage invariably adds, usually by teleporting someone's pen onto the ceiling:

> **"...And every party needs at least one harmless prank."**
