## **I. RECOVERY UPDATE**

Medical Division is pleased to report a significant milestone in Miss Neko's recovery.

For the first time since the onset of her illness, she is once again capable of assuming her human form.

The transformation, however, is temporary.

Current observations indicate she can safely maintain her human form for approximately **10–20 minutes** under light activity before fatigue forces an involuntary return to her cat form.

Medical has strictly prohibited extending this limit.

## **VIII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Medical Division considers Miss Neko's ability to temporarily resume her human form to be a major indicator of recovery. Although the duration remains limited and physical exertion rapidly accelerates exhaustion, each successful transformation provides valuable evidence that her condition is steadily improving.

Personnel are reminded that once signs of fatigue become apparent, Miss Neko is to return to her cat form without delay. Attempts to prolong her human form beyond her current limits are strictly prohibited.

Mirage continues to carry the recovery backpack whenever accompanying Neko, ensuring it is immediately available if Miss Neko becomes too tired to remain human. He has also been observed instinctively preparing the backpack moments before she reaches her limit, suggesting he has become exceptionally good at recognizing the subtle signs of her fatigue.

A handwritten note from Miss Neko was later added to the medical file:

> **"Walking again felt amazing. It only lasted a little while... but I wasn't walking alone. Lil bro was beside me, and Mirage already had my bed ready before I even asked. I think I'll get all the way back... one small walk at a time."**
