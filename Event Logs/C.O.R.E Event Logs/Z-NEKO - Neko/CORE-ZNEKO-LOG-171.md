# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-171**

### **THE TIME ORACLE WAS RIGHT**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – MEDICAL / PERSONNEL SAFETY

---

## **I. INCIDENT SUMMARY**

**Date:** Classified  
**Location:** Engineering Maintenance Sector – Pipe Distribution Level

Despite repeated intervention by Z-885 ("Oracle"), Neko proceeded with a maintenance task.

Approximately forty seconds later...

A corroded overhead support bracket failed unexpectedly.

A high-pressure steel pipe fell from its mounting.

Neko successfully pushed two nearby engineers out of the impact zone.

However...

He was unable to move himself in time.

The pipe struck his right leg.

---

## **II. INJURY REPORT**

Medical Division confirmed:

- Closed fracture of the right tibia.
    
- Minor fracture of the fibula.
    
- Severe bruising.
    
- No vascular damage.
    
- No nerve damage.
    
- No head injury.
    

Recovery is expected to be complete with proper treatment.

Neko was fitted with a reinforced medical cast.

He is temporarily prohibited from climbing, running, lifting heavy equipment, or performing high-risk maintenance.

---

## **III. PRIOR WARNING**

Security footage reviewed after the incident revealed Oracle attempting to stop Neko multiple times.

She:

- Blocked the maintenance ladder.
    
- Tugged gently at his trouser leg.
    
- Sat directly in front of the work area.
    

Finally...

She picked up her marker.

On the portable whiteboard Neko carried for her, she wrote:

> **Please wait.**

Neko smiled.

Knelt down.

Scratched behind her ears.

---

### Neko

> "...I'll only be a minute."

---

Oracle immediately wrote again.

> **Bad idea.**

---

### Neko

> "...I'll be careful."

---

He stepped past her.

Forty seconds later...

The accident occurred.

---

## **IV. AFTER THE INCIDENT**

Medical personnel reached the scene within moments.

Before anyone else arrived...

Oracle was already beside Neko.

She gently rested one paw on his uninjured leg.

Looking unusually quiet.

---

### Neko

_(Through gritted teeth.)_

> "...You were right..."

---

Oracle lowered her ears.

---

> "...Mrr."

---

Neko managed a weak smile.

---

### Neko

> "...Sorry."

---

Oracle gently bumped her forehead against his hand.

As if accepting the apology.

---

## **V. THE CAST**

Later that evening...

Medical completed treatment.

Neko was resting comfortably.

His right leg elevated in bed.

Oracle walked into the room carrying her tiny marker.

She looked at the cast.

Then at Neko.

---

### Neko

> "...You want to sign it?"

---

Oracle nodded.

She carefully wrote, in large blue letters:

> **Get Well Soon!**

Beneath it...

She added a tiny paw print.

Then, after thinking for a moment, wrote one more sentence.

> **Next time listen.**

The room burst into gentle laughter.

---

### Neko

> "...Fair."

---

He didn't erase it.

---

## **VI. EVERYONE LOOKS AFTER HIM**

Since Neko was temporarily unable to perform normal maintenance duties...

The Blacksite quietly adjusted.

Not through official orders.

Simply because people cared.

Lucy made sure he never had to carry anything.

Miss Neko insisted on helping him move between departments whenever his crutches became tiring.

Mirage handled nearly all of Neko's physical maintenance assignments, only asking technical questions when absolutely necessary.

Star frequently stopped by just to keep him company during paperwork.

Nightshade pretended she was "only checking on his recovery status," though she somehow arrived with fresh tea almost every afternoon.

Even Director Lucious occasionally looked in.

Always asking the same question.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Pain manageable?"

---

### Neko

> "...Yeah."

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Good."

---

He never stayed long.

But he always came back the next day.

---

## **VII. ORACLE'S NEW ROUTINE**

Oracle refused to leave Neko's side during the first several days.

Whenever he sat down...

She climbed into his lap.

Whenever he rested...

She curled up against his uninjured leg.

Whenever he reached for his crutches...

She watched carefully.

If he tried moving too quickly...

She immediately held up her marker.

Usually writing only one word.

> **Slowly.**

Neko listened every single time.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Funny."

---

### Neko

> "...What?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Now you listen."

---

Neko looked at Oracle.

Then at the cast on his leg.

---

### Neko

> "...I learned."

---

Oracle purred.

Satisfied.

---

## **VIII. ENGINEERING**

A week later...

An engineer noticed something unusual.

Neko's cast...

Was completely covered in signatures.

Engineers.

Researchers.

Medical staff.

Security officers.

Even cafeteria workers.

Every department had left a small message.

Near the very top...

Oracle's message remained untouched.

It was the only signature no one dared write over.

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Medical Division expects Neko to make a full recovery.

Psychological Division notes that, despite the injury, staff morale remained high due to the overwhelming support shown across the facility. Personnel voluntarily assisted with tasks, visited during breaks, and ensured Neko was never left struggling on his own.

Oracle has remained especially attentive throughout his recovery. While her earlier warnings had gone unheeded, she has shown no signs of resentment, instead staying close and gently encouraging Neko to take his time.

A final note was added to the incident report by Neko himself, written beneath Oracle's signature on the cast:

> **"She warned me. I thought I knew better. Turns out the future-seeing kitten was smarter than the engineer. Next time, if Oracle says 'Bad idea'... I'm listening."**
