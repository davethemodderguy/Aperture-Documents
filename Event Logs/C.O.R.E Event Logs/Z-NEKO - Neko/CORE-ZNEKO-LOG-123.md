# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-123**

### **THE DAY THE BLACKSITE CRIED**

### **Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL LOG

---

### **08:12 Hours**

The news spread quietly.

Nobody announced it over the intercom.

Nobody needed to.

Within minutes...

The entire Blacksite knew.

The maintenance engineer was gone.

---

The corridors felt...

Empty.

Maintenance crews stopped what they were doing.

Researchers stared blankly at unfinished reports.

Even Security patrols slowed.

The usually busy Blacksite became eerily quiet.

---

### **Medical Wing**

Neko remained asleep.

His heartbeat remained steady.

His breathing remained calm.

He hadn't moved.

He didn't know.

---

Miss Neko sat beside his bed.

One hand held Neko's.

The other covered her face.

She didn't cry loudly.

She simply...

Couldn't stop crying.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro..."

> "...You would've told her it wasn't her fault..."

---

She lowered her head onto the blanket.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Why weren't you awake..."

---

### **Engineering Division**

No one worked.

Not because they couldn't.

Because they simply...

Couldn't bring themselves to.

One engineer quietly sat beside an unfinished coolant pump.

Another stared at a welding torch that had long gone cold.

Someone placed the maintenance engineer's helmet neatly on her workbench.

Nobody touched it.

---

### Senior Engineer

> "...She saved us."

---

No one answered.

---

### Another Engineer

> "...She always skipped lunch if someone needed help."

---

Silence.

---

### Third Engineer

> "...She blamed herself..."

---

No one had the heart to continue the conversation.

---

### **Star**

Star was carrying another bowl of soup.

She stopped halfway down the hallway.

Someone gently told her.

The bowl slipped from her hands.

It shattered across the floor.

She didn't even look down.

---

### Star

> "...No..."

---

Her voice trembled.

---

### Star

> "...She was waiting for him to wake up..."

---

Tears rolled freely down her face.

---

### Star

> "...She said she wanted to apologize again..."

---

She slowly covered her mouth.

Unable to say anything else.

---

### **Nightshade**

Nightshade heard the news while walking toward Medical.

She stopped.

Closed her eyes.

Took one deep breath.

Another.

Another.

Then punched the concrete wall.

The impact cracked the surface.

She didn't seem to notice.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Damn it..."

---

She leaned her forehead against the wall.

---

### Nightshade

> "...This wasn't supposed to happen..."

---

For once...

There was no sarcasm.

No teasing.

No sharp remarks.

Just grief.

---

### **Niko**

Niko stood silently outside Neko's room.

He had already read the report.

Twice.

He folded it neatly.

Then quietly put it away.

He couldn't bring himself to enter.

Not yet.

---

### Niko

> "...How am I supposed to tell him..."

---

Nobody had an answer.

---

### **Cherry**

Cherry arrived later that afternoon.

She looked around.

Saw Maintenance unusually silent.

Saw Star crying.

Saw Nightshade sitting alone.

She immediately understood that something terrible had happened.

---

### Cherry

> "...Who's gone?"

---

No one answered.

Not because they refused.

Because nobody could get the words out.

---

When she finally learned the truth...

She quietly lowered her head.

---

### Cherry

> "...She was one of the nicest people here..."

---

### **Cheer**

Cheer simply sat beside Star.

She wrapped an arm around her shoulders.

Neither of them spoke.

There was nothing left to say.

---

### **Shu**

Shu removed his cap.

Held it against his chest.

And stood silently in front of the now-empty maintenance station.

---

### Shu

> "...Rest easy."

---

### **Director Lucious**

That evening...

Director Lucious walked through the Blacksite himself.

No escorts.

No security.

Just him.

He visited Engineering.

Maintenance.

Medical.

Research.

Every department.

He saw the same thing everywhere.

People trying to work.

Unable to focus.

Trying not to cry.

Some failing.

---

He eventually reached Neko's room.

Inside...

Everything was quiet.

Miss Neko still sat beside her younger brother.

Neko still slept peacefully.

Completely unaware.

Lucious stood beside the bed for a long moment.

Then quietly spoke.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...She believed you wouldn't forgive her."

---

He looked at Neko's peaceful face.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...If you were awake..."

---

A small sigh escaped him.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...You would've proved her wrong."

---

He gently adjusted Neko's blanket.

Then left without another word.

---

## **22:46 Hours**

The Blacksite lights dimmed.

Night shift began.

For the first time in years...

No one complained about overtime.

No one joked in the cafeteria.

No one argued over coffee.

The halls remained quiet.

Only the hum of machinery echoed through the facility.

---

Inside the shared room...

Miss Neko held Neko's hand.

Star had quietly fallen asleep in a chair.

Nightshade remained awake against the wall.

Niko stood silently by the window.

None of them wanted to leave.

---

Neko continued sleeping peacefully.

Unaware that, while he rested...

The entire Blacksite had mourned together.

And for the first time since Aperture Industries had established the Blacksite...

It wasn't an anomaly...

Or an experiment...

Or a weapon...

That had broken everyone.

It was the loss of one of their own.
