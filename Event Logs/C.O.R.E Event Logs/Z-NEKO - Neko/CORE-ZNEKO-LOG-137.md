# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-137**

### **WHEN THE LIGHT WENT OUT**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE / CONFIDENTIAL OBSERVATION

---

## **I. INCREASING ISOLATION**

Finding Neko during the workday became unexpectedly difficult.

Not because he was hiding.

Because he had changed his routine.

He started arriving a little earlier than everyone else.

He took maintenance routes with the least foot traffic.

He ate lunch at irregular hours.

If a repair could be completed alone, he volunteered for it before anyone else could.

When coworkers looked for him...

The work was already finished.

His workstation would simply have a handwritten note.

> **"Completed. Have a good day."**

No signature.

Everyone already knew whose handwriting it was.

---

## **II. A CHOICE**

Lucy eventually made a decision.

She stopped accepting new assignments for the afternoon.

Maintenance could spare one engineer.

Someone else could finish the reports.

For once...

She was going to spend time with Neko.

Not because of a protocol.

Not because Medical recommended it.

Simply because she wanted to.

She eventually found him replacing insulation on a coolant line in one of the quieter utility corridors.

---

### Lucy

> "...There you are."

---

Neko looked up.

A small, polite smile appeared.

---

### Neko

> "...Hi."

---

### Lucy

> "I'm free today."

---

He blinked.

---

### Neko

> "...Really?"

---

### Lucy

> "Really."

---

For the first time in days...

Something in his expression softened.

---

## **III. THE CONVERSATION**

They worked together in comfortable silence for a while.

Eventually, Lucy spoke.

---

### Lucy

> "...You've been hard to find lately."

---

### Neko

> "...I've just been staying out of everyone's way."

---

Lucy shook her head.

---

### Lucy

> "You don't have to do that."

---

Neko didn't answer.

He kept tightening a fitting.

Lucy searched for the right words.

She wanted to reassure him.

She wanted him to understand.

Instead...

She chose the wrong sentence.

---

### Lucy

> "...Honestly..."

> "...Everyone's been so overwhelmed lately..."

> "...It's actually easier when you don't need us all the time."

---

Silence.

---

The wrench stopped turning.

---

Neko stared at the pipe.

He didn't move.

He didn't look at Lucy.

For several long seconds...

There was no sound except the ventilation system.

---

Lucy realized what she had said.

---

### Lucy

> "...Neko..."

---

He finally spoke.

Very quietly.

---

### Neko

> "...Oh."

---

Just one word.

No anger.

No accusation.

No tears.

Only understanding.

Or what sounded like understanding.

---

### Neko

> "...I see."

---

Lucy immediately stepped toward him.

---

### Lucy

> "No—that isn't what I meant."

---

### Neko

He smiled gently.

The same careful smile he had been wearing for weeks.

---

> "...It's okay."

---

### Lucy

> "...Please let me explain."

---

### Neko

> "...You don't have to."

---

He placed the wrench into the toolbox.

Closed the lid.

---

### Neko

> "...I'll make sure I'm less trouble."

---

Then he quietly picked up the toolbox.

---

### Lucy

> "...Neko."

---

He stopped walking.

But he didn't turn around.

---

### Neko

> "...Thanks for coming."

---

He continued down the corridor.

---

Lucy didn't follow.

Not because she didn't want to.

Because she couldn't think of a single sentence that would undo the one she had already said.

---

## **IV. THE EYES**

Later that evening...

Maintenance UV lighting activated automatically.

Several engineers happened to pass Neko in the corridor.

One stopped walking.

---

### Engineer

> "...Wait..."

---

Another looked over.

---

### Engineer

> "...His eyes..."

---

They no longer glowed.

Not dimly.

Not faintly.

They simply looked...

Blue.

Perfectly ordinary blue eyes.

Beautiful.

But empty of the familiar sapphire light that had become part of Neko's identity.

---

Medical was contacted immediately.

A full examination followed.

Heart rate.

Neurology.

Metabolism.

Vision.

Everything...

Normal.

There was no medical explanation.

---

## **V. THE REPORT**

The Lead Medical Researcher reviewed the scans.

Again.

And again.

Nothing was wrong physically.

Yet every anomalous sensor produced the same conclusion.

---

> **Emotional Resonance: Undetectable**

---

The researcher quietly removed their glasses.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...The anomaly hasn't disappeared."

---

A colleague looked up.

---

### Researcher

> "...Then where did the light go?"

---

No answer came.

---

## **VI. LUCY**

Lucy found herself standing outside Neko's room that night.

She raised her hand to knock.

Stopped.

Raised it again.

Stopped once more.

Finally...

She whispered through the closed door.

---

### Lucy

> "...I'm sorry."

---

There was no reply.

She didn't know whether Neko was asleep.

Or simply chose not to answer.

She left without opening the door.

---

## **VII. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

An emergency meeting was called.

Present:

- Director Lucious
    
- Niko
    
- Miss Neko
    
- Lucy
    
- Lead Medical Researcher
    

No one spoke for several moments.

Finally—

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Medical?"

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...Healthy."

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Then why?"

---

The researcher slowly shook their head.

---

### Lead Medical Researcher

> "...We don't know."

---

Lucy lowered her head.

---

### Lucy

> "...I said something."

---

No one interrupted.

She repeated the conversation exactly as it happened.

Word for word.

When she finished...

The room remained silent.

Miss Neko closed her eyes.

Not in anger.

In sadness.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You didn't mean to hurt him."

---

Lucy nodded weakly.

---

### Lucy

> "...I know."

---

Miss Neko continued quietly.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...But intent doesn't always change how words land."

---

Lucy covered her face with both hands.

No one blamed her.

That somehow made it hurt even more.

---

## **VIII. THE COINCIDENCE**

Niko placed another stack of scheduling reports on the table.

The same pattern remained.

Interrupted lunches.

Emergency assignments.

Unexpected inspections.

Delayed meetings.

Every attempt to spend meaningful time with Neko seemed to be cut short by legitimate work.

The probability calculations were... uncomfortable.

---

### Niko

> "...I still can't prove anyone is doing this."

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Neither can I."

---

### Niko

> "...But I also can't explain it."

---

Lucious looked at the reports for a long time.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Continue reviewing."

---

He closed the folder.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...No accusations."

> "...Only facts."

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

At the close of the day, Neko completed every assigned task.

His work quality remained exceptional.

He accepted no offers of company because none reached him in time.

When spoken to, he remained polite.

When thanked, he smiled.

When asked if he was alright, he gave the same answer.

> **"I'm okay."**

Medical once again reminded all personnel that Neko's own assessment should not be considered sufficient.

His eyes, once known for telling stories without words, no longer emitted their characteristic glow.

They had become ordinary blue.

Not as a sign of failing health.

But as a sign that something important had gone quiet inside him.

The cause remained uncertain.

Whether it was the accumulation of many missed moments, one painful conversation, or a pattern no one had yet explained, no report could say.

Only one conclusion was entered into the log:

> **"Physical recovery can be measured by instruments. Emotional recovery cannot. It requires time, trust, and people who are able to be present."**
