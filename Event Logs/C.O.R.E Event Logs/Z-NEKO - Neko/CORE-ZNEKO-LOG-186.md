# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-186**

### **ANOMALOUS EMOTIONAL SUPPRESSION EVENT — WEEK 2**

**Classification:** LEVEL 4 – INTERNAL / MEDICAL / N.O.S.T. JOINT INVESTIGATION

---

## **STATUS UPDATE**

Fourteen days have elapsed since the initial suppression event affecting:

- **Neko** – Creativity suppressed.
    
- **Star** – Positivity suppressed.
    

No measurable recovery has been observed.

Despite exhaustive testing, Medical and N.O.S.T. have been unable to restore either suppressed trait.

Entity **Z-885 "Aster"** has **not** been informed that she is the suspected source of the anomaly.

Director Lucious authorized withholding this information temporarily.

His reasoning was recorded in full.

> **"There is no evidence she intended any harm. Until we understand exactly what happened and whether it can be reversed, I will not allow a child to carry responsibility for something she doesn't even know she did."**

---

# **I. NEKO**

Engineering has adapted around Neko's condition.

Repairs remain flawless.

Maintenance efficiency remains high.

Safety record unchanged.

Only one thing is missing.

Innovation.

The Engineering Division has begun noticing the absence in subtle ways.

Damaged equipment is repaired exactly to specification.

Never improved.

Never optimized.

Never redesigned.

When confronted with unusual mechanical problems...

Neko now reaches for manuals instead of a pencil.

---

### Senior Engineer

> "...He still fixes everything."

A pause.

> "...He just doesn't surprise us anymore."

---

Perhaps the most striking observation occurred when a junior engineer approached him.

---

### Junior Engineer

> "I had an idea."

---

He enthusiastically described a completely impractical machine.

Months earlier...

Neko would have laughed.

Expanded on it.

Turned it into something workable.

Instead...

He quietly replied:

---

### Neko

> "...I don't know how to improve it."

---

The engineer later admitted...

That sentence hurt far more than hearing criticism ever would have.

---

# **II. STAR**

Medical notes describe Star's condition with a single repeated phrase.

> **"Emotionally stable. Psychologically deteriorating."**

She continues working.

She continues following orders.

She remains courteous.

Professional.

Reliable.

Yet those who know her best describe her as feeling...

Absent.

---

The most noticeable change...

Is her eyes.

---

Personnel have long described Star's gaze as carrying a quiet radiance.

Even during difficult operations...

There had always been a warmth behind them.

During Week Two...

That warmth disappeared.

Her eyes appeared dull.

Almost colorless.

Not physically.

Emotionally.

Several personnel independently described the same observation.

> "...She looks like the lights are still on..."

> "...But the sunrise isn't."

---

# **III. NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade has become Star's primary companion outside operational duties.

Every evening...

She makes tea.

Places a second cup beside Star.

Sits nearby.

Sometimes they talk.

Sometimes they don't.

One entry from Nightshade's personal observation report reads:

> "I don't stay because I always know what to say."

> "I stay because she shouldn't have to sit through this alone."

---

One evening...

Nightshade quietly slid a notebook across the table.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Draw something."

---

Star looked down.

---

### Star

> "...Why?"

---

### Nightshade

> "...You always liked drawing."

---

Star held the pencil for nearly five minutes.

Then slowly placed it back.

---

> "...I don't feel like making anything."

---

Nightshade didn't press further.

She simply poured another cup of tea.

---

# **IV. MIRAGE**

Mirage has remained focused on Neko.

The two continue repairing the Blacksite together.

Outwardly...

Very little has changed.

Except during breaks.

Previously...

Neko would spend idle moments sketching strange inventions.

Now...

He simply watches Mirage work.

One afternoon...

Mirage deliberately left an unfinished mechanical assembly on the workbench.

Normally...

Neko would have immediately begun modifying it.

Instead...

He assembled it exactly according to the blueprint.

When he finished...

He quietly pushed it back across the table.

---

### Mirage

> "...Nothing else?"

---

### Neko

After a long pause.

> "...That's all I could think of."

---

Mirage made no comment.

He simply nodded.

---

# **V. ASTER**

Aster remains unaware of the connection.

She continues spending time with both affected individuals whenever possible.

She seems particularly concerned about Star.

Several times each day...

She quietly approaches Star.

Places a flower beside her.

Or writes small encouraging messages.

Examples recovered this week include:

> **Good morning.**

---

> **I hope today is nice.**

---

> **Want to play later?**

---

Each time...

Star smiles gently.

Thanks her.

But never seems able to hold onto that moment for long.

---

One afternoon...

Aster drew a picture.

It showed Star smiling brightly.

Standing beside everyone else.

When she proudly handed it over...

Star stared at it for nearly a minute.

Then carefully folded it.

Placed it inside her notebook.

---

### Star

Softly.

> "...I hope I can smile like that again."

---

Aster simply assumed Star was having a difficult week.

She rubbed against Star's arm until she began purring.

---

# **VI. RESEARCH UPDATE**

N.O.S.T. has confirmed an important discovery.

The anomaly does **not** remove emotions, memories, skills, or personality.

Instead...

It selectively suppresses specific psychological or cognitive traits that appear fundamental to the affected individual's way of coping with the world.

The suppression remains continuous.

No fluctuations have been observed.

No spontaneous recovery has occurred.

Attempts to counteract the effect using Divine Arts, Whitefall resonance, or controlled anomalous fields have produced no measurable improvement.

---

# **VII. DIRECTOR'S OBSERVATION**

Director Lucious requested private meetings with both Neko and Star.

The meetings remain classified.

However, one excerpt from his notes was released.

> **"Neko still repairs the Blacksite because it's the right thing to do."**

> **"Star still protects people because it's the right thing to do."**

> **"Whatever this anomaly has taken from them, it has not taken away their kindness."**

---

# **VIII. STATUS – END OF WEEK TWO**

**Neko**

- Creativity remains fully suppressed.
    
- Continues engineering duties without incident.
    
- No signs of recovery.
    

**Star**

- Positivity remains fully suppressed.
    
- Emotional affect continues to diminish.
    
- Personnel consistently report the loss of her characteristic warmth and "glow."
    
- Nightshade remains her primary source of day-to-day support.
    

**Aster**

- Healthy.
    
- Cooperative.
    
- Continues attempting to cheer up both individuals.
    
- Still unaware that she is the suspected source of the suppression phenomenon.
    

No treatment has demonstrated measurable success.

The investigation remains active.

As Week Two concluded, a researcher reviewing surveillance footage paused on a frame of Star walking quietly through the corridor.

Months earlier, that hallway seemed brighter whenever she passed through it.

Now, it looked exactly the same as every other corridor.

The researcher closed the report with a single sentence:

> **"The hardest part isn't that Star stopped smiling. It's that she still tries, and it no longer reaches her eyes."**
