Neko and Miss Neko looked less like alternate forms of the same person.

Not in intelligence.

But visually?

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

The contrast between Neko and Miss Neko has become one of the most recognizable sights in the Blacksite.

---

Miss Neko:

- Neat
    
- Organized
    
- Elegant
    
- Well-presented
    

---

Neko:

- Messy
    
- Relaxed
    
- Hooded
    
- Bell-equipped
    
- Eyes hidden behind bangs
    

---

Despite sharing the same mind, knowledge, and engineering expertise, the two present themselves so differently that newer personnel occasionally forget they originated from the same individual.

---

The result has produced a strange phenomenon.

---

Personnel tend to admire Miss Neko.

Personnel tend to fuss over Neko.

---

A distinction that Neko finds mildly embarrassing.

And that Miss Neko finds endlessly entertaining.
