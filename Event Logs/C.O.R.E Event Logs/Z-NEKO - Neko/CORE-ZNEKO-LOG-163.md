# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-163**

### **ORACLE'S PLAYTIME**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE

---

## **I. A QUIET AFTERNOON**

Most of the Blacksite was occupied.

Engineering was processing maintenance requests.

Research was conducting scheduled experiments.

Medical was dealing with routine examinations.

The halls felt quieter than usual.

Which left only three individuals with unexpectedly little to do.

- Neko.
    
- Mirage.
    
- Oracle.
    

Miss Neko had been called away to assist Logistics.

Star had somehow volunteered for three departments at once.

Nightshade was investigating an anomaly containment report.

So...

Oracle decided today belonged to Neko.

---

## **II. CAT MEETS CAT**

Neko looked down at the tiny kitten sitting beside his boots.

---

### Neko

> "...Want to play?"

---

Oracle looked up.

Her brilliant azure eyes seemed to sparkle.

---

> "Mrr!"

---

A cloud of familiar white smoke surrounded Neko.

A moment later...

A fluffy white cat sat where he had been.

Oracle immediately froze.

Her ears perked up.

Tail stood straight.

She carefully walked closer.

Sniffed Neko once.

Twice.

Then gently bumped her forehead against his.

---

### Tiny Neko

> "Mrr."

---

Oracle answered enthusiastically.

> "Mew!"

---

The two immediately began chasing one another around the workshop.

Not wildly.

Just...

Like ordinary cats.

---

## **III. MIRAGE THE REFEREE**

Mirage watched from a nearby workbench.

Holding a clipboard.

For absolutely no reason.

Oracle darted past him.

Neko followed.

They circled the table three times.

Mirage quietly raised the clipboard.

---

### Mirage

> "...Round One."

---

Neither cat paid attention.

Mirage nodded anyway.

---

### Mirage

> "...Good round."

---

## **IV. ORACLE'S FAVORITE GAME**

Personnel eventually discovered Oracle's favorite activity.

She liked hiding.

More specifically...

She liked hiding from Cat Neko.

Oracle squeezed herself into a cardboard box.

Only her ears were visible.

Neko walked past.

Stopped.

Looked at the box.

Looked away.

Continued walking.

Oracle slowly poked her head out.

---

> "...Mew?"

---

Neko immediately spun around.

Lightly tapped the box with one paw.

Oracle exploded out of the box in surprise.

Both cats sprinted in opposite directions.

Mirage clapped once.

---

### Mirage

> "...Successful ambush."

---

## **V. THE TAIL INCIDENT**

Oracle became fascinated by Neko's fluffy tail.

She quietly stalked it for nearly five minutes.

Neko noticed almost immediately.

Pretended not to.

His tail swayed a little more than usual.

Oracle crouched.

Prepared.

Waited...

Then—

Pounce.

She caught the tip of his tail between her tiny paws.

Neko looked over dramatically.

---

### Tiny Neko

> "...Mrr?"

---

Oracle proudly chirped.

As though she had accomplished something extraordinary.

Neko simply flopped onto his side.

Accepting defeat.

Oracle climbed onto his stomach.

Victorious.

---

## **VI. WHY NOT MIRAGE?**

Later that afternoon...

Star returned just in time to witness Oracle sleeping on Cat Neko's back.

Mirage sat nearby.

Watching peacefully.

---

### Star

> "...Mirage."

---

### Mirage

> "...Yes?"

---

### Star

> "...Why don't you become a cat too?"

---

Mirage tilted his head.

---

### Mirage

> "...I can't."

---

### Star

> "...Really?"

---

Mirage nodded.

---

### Mirage

> "...I don't have one."

---

### Star

> "...But you're basically another Neko."

---

### Mirage

> "...Apparently not."

---

He sounded neither disappointed nor confused.

Just...

Matter-of-fact.

Research later confirmed the observation.

Despite sharing many characteristics with Neko, Mirage possesses no detectable cat form.

The reason remains unknown.

---

## **VII. MISS NEKO RETURNS**

Miss Neko entered the workshop.

She immediately spotted Oracle.

Curled up asleep.

On top of Cat Neko.

Cat Neko himself was asleep too.

Both were softly purring.

Mirage sat beside them.

Reading an engineering manual.

The page hadn't changed in several minutes.

He clearly didn't want to move and wake either of them.

Miss Neko smiled.

She quietly sat beside Mirage.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...How long?"

---

### Mirage

> "...Twenty-three minutes."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You've just been sitting here?"

---

### Mirage

> "...They looked comfortable."

---

Miss Neko laughed quietly.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You could've teleported away."

---

Mirage shook his head.

---

### Mirage

> "...Then nobody would be watching them."

---

Miss Neko looked at the two sleeping cats.

Then gently leaned against Mirage's shoulder.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You're a good brother."

---

Mirage smiled.

---

### Mirage

> "...Thank you."

---

## **VIII. AN UNEXPECTED HABIT**

Over the following weeks, personnel noticed a consistent pattern.

Whenever Neko transformed into his cat form...

Oracle almost always chose to stay close.

Sometimes she would walk beside him.

Sometimes she would curl up next to him during breaks.

Sometimes she would simply rest her head against his side while he napped.

Curiously...

She almost never did the same with anyone else.

Even her other caretakers.

---

### Lead Researcher

> "...Any theories?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...She likes fluffy things."

---

### Neko

> "...I'm fluffy."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Exactly."

---

Mirage looked between Oracle and Cat Neko.

---

### Mirage

> "...I am not fluffy."

---

### Neko

> "...No."

---

### Mirage

> "...That explains it."

---

Nobody had the heart to tell him that his perfectly combed hair was actually quite fluffy too.

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Oracle continues to display a clear preference for spending recreational time with Neko while he is in his cat form. Although she remains affectionate toward all four of her designated caretakers, she appears especially relaxed in Neko's presence, frequently initiating play or choosing to nap beside him.

Mirage, despite his close bond with both Neko and Oracle, has demonstrated no ability to assume a cat form. Extensive observation has revealed no evidence that such a form exists within his anomalous makeup. The reason for this distinction remains unknown.

When asked if he was bothered by it, Mirage simply watched Oracle happily batting at Neko's tail and replied:

> **"...They look happy. That's enough for me."**

A handwritten note later appeared beneath the report, signed by Miss Neko:

> **"Lil bro gets a kitten to play with. Mirage gets to watch them being adorable. Somehow... everyone's happy."**
