# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-157**

### **SMALL MOMENTS**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – MEDICAL / PERSONNEL WELFARE

---

## **I. RECOVERY STATUS**

Miss Neko remains medically restricted.

While her condition continues to improve, her strength has not yet recovered enough to safely sustain her human form. Medical estimates a full recovery will still require time and advises against unnecessary exertion.

Fortunately...

She has become much happier.

---

## **II. THE ENGINEERING ROUTINE**

Most mornings now follow the same pattern.

Neko checks his work schedule.

Mirage picks up the recovery backpack.

Miss Neko climbs inside on her own.

Circles her pillow exactly twice.

Then looks out through the transparent shell.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Ready."

---

### Neko

> "...Comfortable?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Yep."

---

Mirage carefully lifts the backpack.

---

### Mirage

> "...Let's go."

---

And the three begin another workday together.

---

## **III. GUESS WHO**

One afternoon, Neko finished repairing a coolant pump.

As he stood up—

Two identical voices spoke behind him.

---

### Neko

> "...?"

---

### Neko

> "...Lil bro."

---

He turned around.

Both the real Miss Neko and Mirage... no, wait—

Mirage had copied Neko's posture so perfectly that for a split second, Neko instinctively looked at the wrong one.

Miss Neko burst into laughter from inside the backpack.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You got yourself!"

---

Neko covered his face.

---

### Neko

> "...I can't believe that worked."

---

Mirage smiled innocently.

---

### Mirage

> "...It was funny."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...It really was."

---

## **IV. STORY TIME**

While waiting for an elevator, Miss Neko rested her paws against the transparent wall.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Mirage."

---

### Mirage

> "...Yes?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Tell me something."

---

Mirage thought.

---

### Mirage

> "...Yesterday..."

---

He pointed toward Neko.

---

### Mirage

> "...He walked into the wrong storage room."

---

### Neko

> "...That was one time."

---

### Mirage

> "...He apologized to a mop."

---

Silence.

---

Miss Neko stared at Neko.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro..."

---

### Neko

> "...I thought someone was standing there."

---

Miss Neko laughed so hard she nearly rolled off her pillow.

Even Neko couldn't help laughing with her.

---

## **V. THE LITTLE GAME**

Miss Neko eventually invented a game for long walks.

She would quietly ask a question.

The first one to answer correctly earned one point.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Favorite sandwich?"

---

### Mirage

> "...Egg."

---

### Neko

> "...Egg."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Both of you."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Favorite season?"

---

### Mirage

> "...Winter."

---

### Neko

> "...Winter."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You're impossible."

---

She giggled.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Okay..."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Who's my favorite little brother?"

---

Without hesitation—

---

### Neko

> "...Me."

---

### Mirage

> "...Neko."

---

Miss Neko smiled warmly.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Correct."

---

Mirage smiled too.

As though earning the point wasn't important.

Getting the answer right for her was enough.

---

## **VI. BREAK TIME**

During lunch, Neko sat on a bench.

Mirage placed the backpack beside him.

Miss Neko climbed halfway out.

Still too weak to walk very far.

She settled on Neko's lap instead.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Warm."

---

Neko gently scratched behind one of her ears.

She immediately started purring.

Mirage watched quietly.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Mirage."

---

### Mirage

> "...Yes?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You too."

---

Mirage hesitated.

---

### Mirage

> "...Me?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Come here."

---

Mirage knelt beside them.

Miss Neko reached up with a tiny paw and gently tapped the side of his cheek.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Good job."

---

Mirage smiled a little wider than usual.

---

### Mirage

> "...Thank you."

---

## **VII. STAR'S DISCOVERY**

Star rounded the corner and found all three together.

Neko was reading a maintenance report.

Miss Neko was purring on his lap.

Mirage was quietly holding their drinks so Neko didn't have to interrupt the ear scratches.

Star stopped walking.

---

### Star

> "...This..."

---

She pointed dramatically.

---

### Star

> "...Is the cutest thing I've seen all week."

---

Nightshade happened to pass behind her.

She glanced over once.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Agreed."

---

Then kept walking as though she hadn't admitted anything.

---

## **VIII. THE END OF THE DAY**

When work finally ended...

Mirage carried the backpack back to the shared room.

Neko unlocked the little hatch.

Miss Neko slowly climbed out.

She looked from one brother...

To the other.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Today was fun."

---

### Neko

> "...Even though you couldn't do much?"

---

She nodded.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...I don't have to."

---

She smiled.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Watching you two is already fun."

---

Mirage tilted his head.

---

### Mirage

> "...Really?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Really."

---

Mirage looked surprisingly pleased by the answer.

As the familiar azure shimmer began surrounding him, signaling the end of the day, Miss Neko quickly called out.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Goodnight, Mirage."

---

He paused.

Turned back with his gentle smile.

---

### Mirage

> "...Goodnight, Miss Neko."

---

With that, he dissolved into soft azure particles and quietly "went to sleep."

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Although Miss Neko remains physically weakened, Medical Division has noted a significant improvement in her emotional well-being. Much of her day is now spent talking with Neko and Mirage as they move through the Blacksite, turning routine engineering work into small moments of conversation and laughter.

Personnel have commented that it has become difficult to see one without the other two. If Neko is nearby, Mirage is usually a few steps behind, carrying the recovery backpack, while Miss Neko cheerfully chats with them from inside or curls up beside Neko during breaks.

A handwritten note from Lucy was added to the observation file:

> **"Recovery isn't always measured by how long someone can stand. Sometimes it's measured by how often you hear them laugh. Lately, Miss Neko has been laughing a lot."**
