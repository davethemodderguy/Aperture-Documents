## **I. ONE WEEK LATER**

The Blacksite had expected Neko to recover within a few days.

He didn't.

A week passed.

The fever lingered.

It wasn't getting significantly worse...

But it also wasn't leaving.

Medical Division remained puzzled.

---

### Medical Officer

> "Your regeneration is working."

---

### Neko

> "...Doesn't feel like it."

---

### Medical Officer

> "You're healing."

---

### Neko

> "...Very slowly."

---

### Medical Officer

> "...Very slowly."

---

## **II. THE ICE BAG**

Someone—nobody could remember who—placed a reusable ice bag on Neko's head one morning.

It stayed there.

Mostly because it helped.

The sight quickly became familiar throughout the Blacksite.

Small Neko.

Messy bangs.

Snowy's round glasses.

Azure collar.

Oversized hoodie.

A blue ice bag resting on top of his head.

---

### Engineer

> "...Morning."

---

### Neko

> "...Morning..."

---

### Engineer

> "Still sick?"

---

### Neko

> "...Unfortunately."

---

### Engineer

> "Need anything?"

---

### Neko

> "...A nap."

## **III. ENGINEERING**

Despite still insisting on working...

Everyone had reached an agreement.

Neko was no longer allowed to carry anything heavy.

Or climb ladders.

Or stay in Engineering for more than an hour without taking a break.

---

### Maintenance Supervisor

> "Break."

---

### Neko

> "...I just sat down."

---

### Maintenance Supervisor

> "Break anyway."

---

### Neko

> "...Okay."

---

Nobody even argued with him anymore.

They simply escorted him back to Residential when he looked tired.

---

## **IV. MISS NEKO**

Miss Neko had become frighteningly efficient at looking after him.

She could tell he was about to overwork himself before he even realized it.

---

One afternoon...

---

### Neko

---

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro."

---

### Neko

> "...Yeah?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "You're burning up again."

---

### Neko

> "...Oh."

---

---

### Miss Neko

> "Bed."

### Neko

> "...Five more minutes?"

### Miss Neko

> "No."

> "...Okay."

## **VIII. NIGHTSHADE**

Late one evening...

Nightshade found Neko asleep at the desk.

The ice bag had slipped sideways.

His pencil was still in his hand.

Blueprint half-finished.

---

She quietly moved the blueprint away.

Straightened the ice bag.

Pulled the blanket over his shoulders.

---

As she turned to leave...

---

### Neko

> "...Thanks..."

---

His eyes never opened.

He had spoken in his sleep.

---

Nightshade smiled faintly.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Get some rest."

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

By the second week, everyone had accepted that Neko's recovery would simply take time.

His regeneration was functioning.

His fever was gradually easing.

It just refused to disappear overnight.

The little engineer who normally solved impossible problems had been forced to confront one he couldn't engineer his way out of.

So instead...

He accepted help.

Even if he occasionally insisted he was "fine."

---

### Recorded Exchange

Miss Neko entered the room after her shift.

Neko was sitting on the bed.

Ice bag balanced on his head.

Glasses slightly crooked.

Trying very hard to read a blueprint.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro."

---

### Neko

> "...Hm?"

---

She gently straightened his glasses.

Adjusted the ice bag.

Closed the blueprint.

---

> "...You always say that."

---

### Miss Neko

---

### Neko

> "...Okay."

---

### Miss Neko

> "That's my lil bro."

---

The only sound left in the room was the quiet **jingle** of Neko's collar bell as he settled down to rest once more.
