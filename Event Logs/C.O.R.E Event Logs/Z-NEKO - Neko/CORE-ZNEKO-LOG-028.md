### Final Observation

For forty-eight hours, Aperture Industries temporarily lost one of its greatest scientists.

In exchange, it gained a fluffy Scottish Fold cat whose greatest ambitions were naps, milk, and occupying the nearest warm lap.

Most employees secretly considered the trade acceptable.

Niko openly considered it acceptable.

Neko, upon returning to normal, was horrified by the amount of photographic evidence collected during those two days.

The archives remain preserved.
