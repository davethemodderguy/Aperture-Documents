# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-117**

### **DIMMER THAN YESTERDAY**

### **Classification:** INTERNAL – MEDICAL / HIGH PRIORITY

---

## **I. MORNING OBSERVATION**

It happened again.

Medical performed the same examination.

Same room.

Same lighting.

Same camera.

The comparison image appeared on the monitor.

Nobody said anything for several seconds.

Then...

The attending physician quietly lowered the tablet.

---

### Medical Officer

> "...Another decrease."

---

This time...

Nobody asked by how much.

The answer didn't matter.

It had become dimmer.

Again.

---

## **II. THE BLACKSITE**

Officially...

Nothing had changed.

Engineering continued working.

Maintenance continued repairing.

Security continued their patrols.

Research continued their experiments.

From the outside...

The Blacksite looked completely normal.

Unofficially...

Everyone was thinking about the same person.

---

## **III. THE ROOM**

Neko remained in bed.

His fever still lingered.

The ice bag had been replaced with a warm compress whenever his temperature allowed, though it alternated depending on his symptoms.

A steaming mug of tea rested untouched on the bedside table.

His round glasses remained on.

His shortened hair framed eyes that still shone a beautiful sapphire blue...

Just...

Not as brightly.

---

He noticed people staring.

---

### Neko

> "...Is something on my face?"

---

Miss Neko looked up immediately.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...No."

---

### Neko

> "...You keep looking."

---

She smiled.

A practiced smile.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Just checking on you, lil bro."

---

Neko looked at her for another moment.

Then nodded.

---

### Neko

> "...Okay."

---

He wasn't convinced.

But he let it go.

---

## **IV. STAR**

Star still visited every morning.

She still brought warm soup.

Still made sure the temperature was safe.

Still smiled as brightly as ever.

---

### Star

> "Today's tomato soup."

---

### Neko

> "...Thanks."

---

### Star

> "...Feeling any stronger?"

---

Neko thought for a second.

---

### Neko

> "...Maybe... a little?"

---

Star's smile widened.

---

### Star

> "That's good!"

---

She kept smiling until he looked away.

Only then did her expression falter.

Her eyes drifted toward his.

She noticed the fading.

Again.

She quietly clenched the edge of the tray.

Then smiled before he looked back.

---

## **V. NIGHTSHADE**

Nightshade wasn't good at pretending.

She entered.

Looked at Neko.

Immediately noticed.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Your eyes."

---

Neko blinked.

---

### Neko

> "...What about them?"

---

She caught herself.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Nothing."

---

A pause.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Still look nice."

---

Neko gave a tired smile.

---

### Neko

> "...Thanks."

---

She turned away almost immediately.

Outside the room...

She leaned against the wall.

Closed her eyes.

And stayed there for several minutes before composing herself.

---

## **VI. ENGINEERING**

Maintenance had started a quiet tradition.

Every day...

Someone would stop by after their shift.

Sometimes with soup.

Sometimes with tea.

Sometimes just to say hello.

Today's visitor was an older maintenance engineer.

He carried a small toolbox.

---

### Engineer

> "...Thought you'd like to see."

---

He opened it.

Inside was the valve Neko had designed several weeks earlier.

Freshly repaired.

Working perfectly.

---

### Engineer

> "Your design held."

---

Neko smiled.

A genuine one.

---

### Neko

> "...Good."

---

### Engineer

> "We'll take care of everything until you're back."

---

Neko nodded.

---

### Neko

> "...I know you will."

---

## **VII. NIKO**

Niko arrived in the evening.

He didn't bring reports.

Didn't bring paperwork.

Just dinner.

---

He sat beside the bed.

---

### Niko

> "...Hungry?"

---

### Neko

> "...A little."

---

### Niko

> "Progress."

---

He handed him the bowl.

Waited patiently while Neko ate.

---

After a while...

---

### Niko

> "...Everyone misses you."

---

Neko looked surprised.

---

### Neko

> "...Really?"

---

### Niko

> "...The entire Maintenance Division has started arguing over who's going to show you the repaired pipes first."

---

Neko laughed softly.

---

### Neko

> "...That sounds like them."

---

Director Lucious received the latest eye comparison.

Placed it beside yesterday's.

He closed the folder.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Any breakthrough?"

---

### Lead Medical Officer

> "...Not yet."

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Keep looking."

---

### Lead Medical Officer

> "...We haven't stopped."

---

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Don't."

---

Late that night...

Miss Neko adjusted Neko's blanket.

He was still awake.

Looking toward the ceiling.

---

### Neko

> "...Sis?"

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Yeah, lil bro?"

---

### Neko

> "...Am I worrying everyone?"

---

She answered immediately.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Yes."

---

He looked toward her.

---

### Neko

> "...Sorry."

---

She gently tapped his forehead.

---

### Miss Neko

> "No apologizing."

---

### Neko

> "...Okay."

---

Then quietly admitted—

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You're family."

### Miss Neko

Neko smiled faintly.

---

### Neko

> "...Guess that makes sense."

## **X. THE HALLWAY**

Outside the room...

Star.

Nightshade.

Niko.

Cherry.

Cheer.

Sky.

Aurora.

Several engineers.

They all happened to arrive within minutes of each other.

Nobody had planned it.

Nobody wanted to crowd the room.

So they simply stood together in the hallway.

---

### Cherry

> "...How is he?"

---

### Miss Neko

She stepped out quietly, closing the door behind her.

She looked at everyone.

Took a slow breath.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...He's still smiling."

---

Relief spread through the group.

Small.

But real.

---

### Cheer

> "...That's our little scientist."

---

### Sky

> "...Too stubborn to stop smiling."

---

### Aurora

> "...Even now."

---

Nightshade folded her arms.

Looking toward the closed door.

---

### Nightshade

> "...He'd probably apologize if we looked too worried."

---

Everyone quietly agreed.

---

## **XI. FINAL OBSERVATION**

The latest examinations confirmed that the glow in Neko's eyes had diminished further.

Medical Division still could not determine whether the fading was a symptom, a consequence of prolonged illness, or something else entirely. They also could not say with certainty whether it would continue, stabilize, or reverse.

What changed most wasn't the medical chart.

It was the people around him.

The jokes became gentler.

The visits became longer.

The warm drinks arrived more often.

And whenever Neko looked up through his round glasses...

Everyone made the same effort.

They smiled first.

Even when they were frightened.

Because the last thing any of them wanted...

Was for him to think he was facing his illness alone.
