# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-176**

### **"NO, YOU CANNOT HAVE PEANUTS."**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL NOTICE

---

## **I. OVERVIEW**

Despite being fully informed of his newly diagnosed **severe peanut allergy**, Neko has demonstrated repeated attempts to obtain peanut-containing snacks.

When questioned, Neko's explanation has remained consistent.

---

### Neko

> "...They're still my favorite..."

---

Medical Division would like to remind personnel that:

**Favorites do not override anaphylaxis.**

## **III. NIGHTSHADE'S SOLUTION**

Nightshade decided that reasoning clearly wasn't enough.

She began carrying emergency replacement snacks.

Whenever Neko looked longingly at peanuts...

She immediately handed him something else.

Usually:

- Almond-free crackers.
    
- Dried fruit.
    
- Sandwiches.
    
- Whitefall Snow treats approved by Medical.
    

---

## **IV. ORACLE'S ESCALATION**

Oracle quickly learned to recognize "The Peanut Look."

Research officially describes it as:

> **"A prolonged visual fixation toward peanut-containing food."**

Whenever she observed it...

She acted immediately.

Step one:

Marker.

> **No.**

If ignored...

Step two:

Gentle nib.

If still ignored...

Step three:

Marker throw.

One afternoon...

Neko attempted to accept a peanut from an unsuspecting visitor.

Before the peanut reached his hand...

Oracle launched the marker.

_Bonk._

The peanut fell harmlessly onto the table.

Oracle calmly walked over.

Picked up the peanut.

Placed it back in its container.

Then wrote:

> **Not food.**

For Neko.

---

## **V. THE ENTIRE BLACKSITE KNOWS**

Word spread quickly.

Soon...

Stopping Neko from eating peanuts became an unconscious reflex.

Examples include:

A cafeteria worker quietly replacing peanut cookies before Neko reached the tray.

A researcher casually steering him away from the nut aisle during supply inventory.

An engineer intercepting a bowl of mixed nuts before it reached the lunch table.

A security officer politely informing a visitor:

> "...Please don't offer him peanuts."

It became so routine that new employees learned about Neko's allergy during orientation.

## **VIII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

While Neko occasionally still reaches for peanut-containing foods out of habit or nostalgia, intervention from personnel consistently prevents accidental exposure. His caretakers remain especially vigilant, and Oracle has proven remarkably effective at recognizing the moment temptation strikes.

Medical Division notes that Neko's desire for peanuts is understandable, but even a small amount could trigger another life-threatening reaction.

An unofficial Engineering slogan has since appeared on the cafeteria notice board:

> **"If you see Neko reaching for peanuts... stop the engineer before Medical has to start the engineer."**
