# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-179**

### **UNSUPERVISED EXPOSURE**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – MEDICAL CRITICAL INCIDENT

---

## **I. INCIDENT SUMMARY**

**Location:** Engineering Storage Annex  
**Time:** Classified

Despite extensive precautions, Neko was briefly left alone while personnel responded to unrelated maintenance calls.

During this period, Neko located a forgotten bag of peanut snacks that had been left inside a storage locker prior to the updated cafeteria and engineering restrictions.

He consumed an unknown quantity.

No witnesses were present.

---

## **II. DISCOVERY**

Approximately six minutes later...

A passing maintenance technician noticed an open storage room door.

Inside...

Neko was on the floor.

The empty peanut bag lay nearby.

He was conscious, but only barely.

Visible symptoms included:

- Severe respiratory distress.
    
- Significant swelling of the airway.
    
- Cyanosis (blue discoloration from lack of oxygen).
    
- Weak, irregular pulse.
    

Unable to speak, Neko pointed weakly toward the empty snack bag before losing consciousness.

The technician immediately declared a medical emergency.

---

## **III. EMERGENCY RESPONSE**

Medical personnel arrived within minutes.

Immediate treatment included:

- Emergency epinephrine.
    
- Oxygen support.
    
- Intravenous medication.
    
- Advanced airway management.
    
- Continuous cardiac monitoring.
    

Unlike the previous incidents, Neko had been alone for several critical minutes before discovery.

Lead Medical Researcher later stated:

> "Another few minutes without treatment could have had a very different outcome."

After prolonged treatment, Neko stabilized.

He regained consciousness several hours later.

---

## **IV. THE ROOM**

When Neko awoke...

The room was unusually quiet.

Miss Neko sat beside the bed.

Mirage stood near the window.

Oracle was asleep against the blanket.

No one spoke immediately.

Finally...

Neko broke the silence.

---

### Neko

> "...I'm sorry."

---

Miss Neko looked at him.

There was relief in her expression.

But also exhaustion.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro..."

---

She gently held his hand.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...We almost lost you."

---

Neko lowered his eyes.

---

### Neko

> "...I know."

---

Mirage finally spoke.

His voice remained calm.

---

### Mirage

> "...Why?"

---

A long silence followed.

---

### Neko

> "...I kept thinking..."

> "...Maybe this time nothing would happen."

---

He shook his head.

---

> "...I was wrong."

---

Mirage nodded once.

---

### Mirage

> "...You were."

---

He walked over to the bedside.

---

### Mirage

> "...You don't have to fight this by yourself."

---

## **V. ORACLE**

Oracle woke up.

She looked at Neko.

Then quietly picked up her marker.

She didn't write **"Bad idea."**

She didn't write **"I warned you."**

Instead, she wrote:

> **Please stay with us.**

Neko read the words several times.

His eyes began to water.

He gently rested a hand on Oracle's head.

---

### Neko

> "...I will."

---

## **VI. MEDICAL ASSESSMENT**

Following the incident, Medical Division concluded that Neko's repeated attempts to eat peanuts were no longer being treated as simple forgetfulness or curiosity.

The pattern suggested he struggled to resist eating a food he had enjoyed for years despite knowing it could be fatal.

Medical and Psychological Services jointly recommended additional support rather than relying solely on supervision.

Recommendations include:

- Regular counseling regarding adjustment to the new allergy.
    
- Removal of peanut products from all areas Neko routinely accesses.
    
- Continued supervision by trusted companions during meals when practical.
    
- Encouraging Neko to talk openly when he feels frustrated or tempted rather than trying to handle it alone.
    

---

## **VII. UPDATED SAFETY NOTICE**

All personnel are reminded:

- Peanut products are not to be stored in Engineering or other common work areas.
    
- If you observe Neko attempting to obtain peanut-containing food, calmly intervene and notify his caretakers or Medical if needed.
    
- Do not shame or mock him for the incident. Focus on helping him stay safe.
    

---

## **VIII. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Neko has recovered physically from the incident and remains under observation.

Research notes that the greatest danger was not only the allergic reaction itself, but that it occurred while he was alone, delaying treatment until another employee happened to pass by.

A note was later found on Neko's bedside table, written in Oracle's careful handwriting. Neko had written his reply beneath it.

**Oracle:**

> _Please stay with us._

**Neko:**

> _I like peanuts. I like all of you more. Next time I'm tempted, I'll ask someone to help me instead of trying to hide it._
