# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-156**

### **CATCHING UP**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – MEDICAL / PERSONNEL WELFARE

---

## **I. RECOVERY UPDATE**

Medical Division reports encouraging progress in Miss Neko's recovery.

Although she remains unable to safely maintain her human form, her strength has improved noticeably.

She now spends much more of the day awake.

Her appetite has returned.

She is alert.

And, perhaps most importantly...

She can finally talk again.

The first person she spoke to after waking for an extended period...

Was not Neko.

It was Mirage.

---

## **II. MORNING PATROL**

Mirage was carrying the transparent recovery backpack through Engineering while following Neko between work orders.

Miss Neko was awake.

Instead of sleeping, she watched the Blacksite pass by through the transparent shell.

After several quiet minutes—

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Morning."

---

Mirage looked over.

---

### Mirage

> "...Good morning."

---

A short silence followed.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...You carry me a lot."

---

### Mirage

> "...Yes."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Thank you."

---

Mirage smiled.

---

### Mirage

> "...You're welcome."

---

Nothing more was said.

The silence wasn't awkward.

It was comfortable.

---

### Mirage

## **VIII. BEDTIME**

Back in the shared room...

Neko set the backpack beside the bed and opened the hatch.

Miss Neko slowly climbed out.

She stretched, then padded over to Mirage.

He was beginning to shimmer, his work for the day finished.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Leaving already?"

---

### Mirage

> "...I'm not needed."

---

### Miss Neko

### Miss Neko

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Miss Neko's continued recovery has allowed her to remain awake for much longer periods, resulting in regular conversations with personnel throughout the day. Because Mirage spends much of his time carrying her recovery pack while accompanying Neko, the two now interact frequently.

Staff have observed that Miss Neko often uses these quiet walks to catch up on everything that happened while she was sleeping, while Mirage patiently answers every question he can. Their conversations are simple, calm, and often filled with small everyday details rather than anomalies or engineering work.

Medical considers this increased social engagement a positive sign of recovery.

A handwritten note from Miss Neko was later attached to the daily report:

> **"Lil bro keeps everyone moving. Mirage keeps everyone together. I slept through a lot... but thanks to him, I don't feel like I missed as much anymore."**
