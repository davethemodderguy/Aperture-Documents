# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-120**

### **THE MIRROR**

### **Classification:** INTERNAL – MEDICAL / OMEGA PRIORITY

---

## **I. 02:41 HOURS**

The residential quarters were quiet.

Rain tapped softly against the windows.

Most of the Blacksite had long since settled into the night shift.

Miss Neko had finally fallen asleep in the chair beside Neko's bed after staying awake for nearly two days.

Star and Nightshade occupied the opposite side of the shared room, both asleep after another long day.

For the first time in weeks...

Neko was awake.

He stared at the ceiling.

Silent.

Then he slowly pushed the blanket aside.

---

## **II. STANDING**

His feet touched the floor.

His body immediately protested.

His legs trembled.

Every muscle felt impossibly heavy.

It was like trying to walk with blocks of concrete tied to both ankles.

He gripped the bedside cabinet.

Took one step.

Stopped.

Breathed.

Another step.

Another.

The tiny bell on his azure collar gave the faintest _jingle._

Miss Neko stirred slightly.

But didn't wake.

---

## **III. THE MIRROR**

Star's vanity mirror sat against the far wall.

Neko finally reached it.

He rested both hands on the table to keep himself standing.

His breathing had become uneven from walking only a few meters.

He slowly reached up.

Lifted the edge of his bangs.

Adjusted Snowy's round glasses.

Then...

Looked.

---

For several long seconds...

Nothing happened.

He simply stared.

The reflection stared back.

His irises...

Were no longer the brilliant sapphire everyone associated with him.

They had become almost entirely black.

Only the faintest ring of azure remained at the very edge.

Barely visible.

---

He quietly reached up.

Touched beneath one eye.

As if expecting the reflection to change.

It didn't.

---

### Neko

> "...Oh..."

---

His voice was almost inaudible.

---

## **IV. THE REALIZATION**

His mind replayed the past several days.

Everyone smiling.

Everyone lingering a little longer.

Everyone insisting he rest.

Everyone saying...

_"You look fine."_

_"You'll be okay."_

_"Don't worry about it."_

_"Just focus on getting better."_

He finally understood.

---

### Neko

> "...They knew..."

---

Another long silence.

---

### Neko

> "...Everyone knew..."

---

He wasn't angry.

He wasn't betrayed.

More than anything...

He looked heartbroken.

---

### Neko

> "...They were trying..."

---

His shoulders lowered.

---

### Neko

> "...To protect me."

---

## **V. DISCOVERED**

Behind him...

A floorboard creaked.

Miss Neko had woken up.

She immediately noticed the empty bed.

Then saw Neko standing by the mirror.

Her expression changed instantly.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro?"

---

Neko didn't turn around.

---

### Neko

> "...You lied."

---

The words weren't accusatory.

Just...

Quiet.

---

Miss Neko slowly walked over.

Careful not to startle him.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...I did."

---

He continued looking at his reflection.

---

### Neko

> "...Everyone did."

---

She nodded.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Yes."

---

A long silence settled between them.

---

### Neko

> "...Because of my eyes."

---

She gently rested a hand on his shoulder.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Because we didn't want you carrying that fear while you were already fighting so hard."

---

Neko lowered his head.

---

### Neko

> "...I thought everyone was just being nice."

---

### Miss Neko

> "...We were."

---

He finally turned to face her.

His black irises reflected faintly in the room's dim light.

---

### Neko

> "...Were you scared?"

---

For the first time since this illness began...

Miss Neko didn't hide it.

Her eyes watered.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Every single day."

---

## **VI. THE OTHERS WAKE**

The quiet conversation had awakened the rest of the room.

Star sat upright first.

Nightshade followed.

Within moments, Niko—who had been checking in late and resting nearby—looked in through the open doorway.

Nobody spoke immediately.

Neko looked at each of them.

---

### Neko

> "...You all knew."

---

Star lowered her eyes.

---

### Star

> "...Yeah."

---

Nightshade gave a small nod.

---

### Nightshade

> "...We did."

---

Niko stepped closer.

---

### Niko

> "...We wanted to give Medical time."

---

### Neko

> "...Time to find an answer."

---

### Niko

> "...Exactly."

---

## **VII. LUCIOUS**

Director Lucious arrived minutes later after being informed that Neko had left his bed.

He entered quietly.

Instead of reprimanding him, he simply looked from the mirror...

...to Neko.

---

### Director Lucious

> "You figured it out."

---

Neko nodded.

---

### Neko

> "...I did."

---

Lucious remained silent for a moment.

Then spoke plainly.

---

### Director Lucious

> "Medical doesn't have all the answers."

> "That doesn't mean they've stopped looking."

---

Neko listened.

---

### Director Lucious

> "Neither have we."

---

## **VIII. BACK TO BED**

Neko's knees suddenly buckled.

Weeks of illness had caught up with him.

Before he could fall...

Miss Neko caught him.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Easy."

---

### Neko

> "...Guess... walking was a bad idea."

---

Star managed a small laugh through misty eyes.

---

### Star

> "...Definitely."

---

Nightshade folded her arms.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Idiot."

---

There was no bite in the word.

Only relief that she'd caught him before he hit the floor.

Together, they helped him back into bed.

Miss Neko tucked the blanket around him.

Niko adjusted the pillow.

Star handed him a warm mug.

Nightshade quietly pulled the chair closer and sat down.

No one wanted to leave.

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

From that night onward, there were no more reassurances built on half-truths.

No one pretended the situation wasn't serious.

But neither did anyone speak as though hope was gone.

Medical continued searching for an explanation.

Engineering offered every resource they could.

Research worked through the night.

And the people closest to Neko made one promise.

They would face whatever came next...

Together.

As Neko closed his eyes to rest again, he managed a faint smile.

---

### Neko

> "...Next time..."

> "...Just tell me the truth."

---

Miss Neko gently squeezed his hand.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...We will, lil bro."

The room fell quiet once more—not because anyone had given up, but because everyone had chosen to stand beside him, without hiding their concern and without abandoning hope.
