# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z885-LOG-009**

### **THE BLACKSITE'S KITTEN**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – PERSONNEL WELFARE

---

## **I. AN UNUSUAL DAY**

It was a rare occurrence.

Neko was repairing a reactor coolant manifold.

Mirage was assisting him several floors below.

Miss Neko was overseeing a logistics inspection.

Star had been assigned to N.O.S.T.

Nightshade was accompanying a containment transfer.

For the first time since Z-885's arrival...

Every one of Oracle's designated caretakers was occupied.

Normally...

Oracle would simply wait.

Today...

She decided to make some new friends.

---

## **II. ENGINEERING**

Oracle's first stop was Engineering.

She wandered between workbenches, occasionally stopping to watch someone tighten bolts or weld pipe joints.

One elderly engineer noticed her sitting beside his toolbox.

---

### Engineer

> "...Supervisor's here."

---

Oracle blinked.

---

> "Mew."

---

The engineer laughed.

---

### Engineer

> "...I'll take that as approval."

---

She quietly sat nearby while he finished replacing a valve.

When he stood up...

Oracle inspected the work.

Walked around the pipe once.

Then rubbed against his boot.

---

### Engineer

> "...Passed inspection."

---

From that day onward...

The engineer jokingly referred to her as **Chief Quality Inspector**.

---

## **III. MEDICAL**

Medical staff welcomed her almost immediately.

A nurse crouched down.

---

### Nurse

> "...Hello, little one."

---

Oracle walked over.

Allowed exactly three head pats.

Then climbed onto an empty waiting-room chair.

A patient waiting for a routine examination looked visibly nervous.

Oracle hopped down.

Curled up beside him.

Rested her tiny head against his arm.

The patient smiled for the first time that morning.

---

### Patient

> "...Thanks."

---

Medical staff quietly agreed that morale had increased by an immeasurable amount.

---

## **IV. RESEARCH**

Researchers expected Oracle to ignore them.

Instead...

She wandered into a laboratory.

Sat in front of a whiteboard.

And watched a discussion about anomalous energy propagation.

Completely still.

For nearly twenty minutes.

---

### Researcher

> "...Do you understand any of this?"

---

Oracle yawned.

---

> "...Mrr."

---

The room laughed.

---

### Assistant

> "...She's a better audience than most interns."

---

## **V. SECURITY**

Security officers were initially unsure what to do.

Oracle solved the problem herself.

She walked into the guard room.

Jumped onto the desk.

Curled up beside the monitoring screens.

One officer absentmindedly scratched behind her ears while watching camera feeds.

---

### Officer

> "...Quiet shift."

---

Oracle purred.

The officer smiled.

---

### Officer

> "...Let's keep it that way."

---

She remained there for almost an hour.

Watching dozens of camera feeds with surprising interest.

---

## **VI. CAFETERIA**

Lunch time.

Oracle wandered into the cafeteria.

Within minutes...

She had somehow collected an audience.

People respectfully sat around her while eating.

No one fed her unauthorized food.

Everyone had learned that lesson.

One new intern reached into his pocket.

Produced a wrapped cheese snack.

Immediately...

Half the cafeteria shouted.

---

> "**NO!**"

---

The intern froze.

---

### Senior Engineer

> "...Put the cheese away."

---

### Intern

> "...Why?"

---

### Three People Simultaneously

> "...Trust us."

---

Oracle looked at the cheese.

Looked back at the intern.

Then deliberately turned away.

Almost as if she knew it wasn't allowed.

---

### Cafeteria Staff

> "...Good girl."

---

## **VII. LOGISTICS**

Oracle eventually wandered through Logistics.

Several workers paused just long enough to wave.

One forklift operator stopped completely.

Waiting for Oracle to finish crossing the aisle.

---

### Operator

> "...Right of way."

---

Oracle casually continued walking.

Completely unaware she had temporarily halted warehouse traffic.

---

## **VIII. RETURNING HOME**

Late that evening...

Neko finally finished his maintenance assignment.

He stepped into the central corridor.

Immediately—

A familiar tiny white blur came running toward him.

---

> "Mew!"

---

Oracle sprinted the last few meters.

Leapt.

Neko caught her effortlessly.

---

### Neko

> "...Hey."

---

She rubbed against his cheek repeatedly.

Purring louder than usual.

---

### Neko

> "...Missed me?"

---

Oracle answered by climbing onto his shoulder.

Refusing to come down.

Moments later...

Miss Neko, Mirage, Star, and Nightshade arrived one after another.

Oracle looked around.

Her entire group was finally back together.

Satisfied...

She stretched.

Yawned.

Then calmly climbed from Neko's shoulder into Miss Neko's arms for a few seconds, hopped onto Mirage's shoulder to nuzzle his cheek, and finally leapt into Star's waiting hands before ending up sitting beside Nightshade, who quietly scratched behind her ears.

Nightshade glanced around.

---

### Nightshade

> "...Looks like we all got checked in."

---

### Star

> "...She made friends today!"

---

Mirage smiled.

---

### Mirage

> "...The whole Blacksite."

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

With all four designated caretakers occupied, Z-885 independently explored the Blacksite throughout the day. She visited nearly every major department, interacting calmly with personnel and displaying the same gentle temperament she is known for.

No anomalous warnings were issued during this period.

Instead, Oracle's presence had an unexpected effect on morale. Staff from Engineering, Medical, Research, Security, Logistics, and Administration all reported that the kitten's visits briefly lightened the mood during an otherwise demanding day.

An addendum was approved by Director Lucious:

> **"Oracle belongs to Neko, Miss Neko, Star, and Nightshade on paper. In practice... she's become the Blacksite's kitten. And judging by today's reports, the Blacksite has collectively decided to adopt her back."**
