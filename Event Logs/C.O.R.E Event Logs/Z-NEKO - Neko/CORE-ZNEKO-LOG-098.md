# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Neko's status within the Blacksite has evolved considerably.

---

Several years ago:

People worried about what he would invent next.

---

Now:

People still worry about what he will invent next.

---

But they also make sure he has lunch.

---

And occasionally carry him to meetings.

---

Miss Neko no longer keeps a close eye on him.

Not because she trusts the Blacksite less.

But because she trusts it more.

---

The entire facility has somehow reached an unspoken agreement:

---

If Neko is missing.

He's probably fine.

If he's being carried somewhere.

He's definitely fine.

If he's accepting a sandwich.

Everything is normal.

---

If he says:

> "I have an idea."

Everyone should start worrying again.
