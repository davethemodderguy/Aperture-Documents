## **XI. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Neko's illness lasted several days, reminding everyone that remarkable regeneration does not make someone immune to ordinary sickness.

Throughout the entire period:

- Miss Neko remained his constant caregiver, affectionately referring to him as **"Lil bro."**
    
- Star kept the room bright with food, conversation, and little gifts.
    
- Nightshade quietly looked after the practical details, making sure Neko always had a peaceful place to rest.
    
- Engineering adjusted workloads so Neko could contribute without overexerting himself.
    
- Maintenance checked on him almost hourly.
    
- The Unchosen Ones respectfully kept their distance and quietly moved on to other duties, recognizing that there was a time for jokes and a time to simply let someone recover.
    

For perhaps the first time since shrinking himself...

Neko stopped trying to prove he could handle everything alone.

He let the people around him take care of him.

And none of them minded in the slightest.
