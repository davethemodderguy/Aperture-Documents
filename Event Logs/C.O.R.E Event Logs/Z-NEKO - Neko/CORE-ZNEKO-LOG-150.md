# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-150**

### **ANOMALOUS ABILITY – "MIRAGE"**

**Classification:** INTERNAL – ENGINEERING / PERSONNEL NOTICE

---

## **I. SUMMARY**

Following routine anomalous evaluations, Neko reported the spontaneous manifestation of a new ability designated **"Mirage."**

The ability bears superficial similarities to **Star's Mirage**, in that both produce temporary holographic entities.

The similarities end there.

Unlike Star's Mirage, Neko's Mirage is capable of independent movement, interacting with physical objects, and performing simple tasks without direct control.

Most importantly:

> **Mirage is not another person.**

> **He is not a clone.**

> **He is not sentient.**

According to Neko himself—

---

### Neko

> "He's basically... me."

---

### Researcher

> "...Another you?"

---

### Neko

> "No."

---

### Researcher

> "Then what is he?"

---

Neko thought for several seconds.

---

### Neko

> "...Imagine if my autopilot became a hologram."

---

## **II. CHARACTERISTICS**

Mirage appears visually identical to Neko.

The only reliable differences are:

- Slightly translucent around the edges under strong lighting.
    
- Emits a faint azure shimmer when first manifested.
    
- Does not cast a perfectly accurate shadow.
    
- Can disappear instantly without warning.
    

Unlike ordinary holograms...

He possesses physical presence.

He can:

- Carry objects.
    
- Operate machinery.
    
- Write.
    
- Open doors.
    
- Use tools.
    
- Wave.
    
- Pat someone's shoulder.
    

However...

He demonstrates **no self-awareness**.

He never asks questions.

He never starts conversations.

He never makes independent decisions outside simple tasks.

When no task exists...

He simply stands quietly.

Almost like...

Someone waiting.

Maintenance became the first victims.

---

---

### Neko

> "Yeah?"

---

---

### Mirage

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Engineering Division has approved Mirage for routine operational use.

Current authorized applications include:

- Assisting with repairs requiring multiple hands.
    
- Holding equipment or materials.
    
- Transporting lightweight objects.
    
- Training demonstrations.
    
- Lighthearted, non-disruptive pranks on consenting coworkers.
    

Personnel are reminded that Mirage should be treated respectfully despite his lack of self-awareness. He is capable of following instructions and interacting safely with others, but should not be mistaken for a fully independent individual.

A handwritten note from Miss Neko was attached to the report:

> **"Lil bro says he isn't alive. Maybe that's true. But every time he appears, he smiles, helps everyone, says good morning, and quietly goes to sleep when he's done. That's enough reason for me to say hello to him too."**
