## **VIII. BLACKSITE NOTICE**

The personnel handbook was updated.

---

### **NEKO – PERSONAL BOUNDARIES**

When Neko is wearing Snowy's glasses:

- His eye-contact anomaly is fully suppressed.
    
- With Neko's permission, personnel may gently lift his bangs to see his eyes.
    
- Do not pull, tug, or unnecessarily handle his hair.
    

Additionally:

- **Do not touch, remove, adjust, borrow, inspect, or wear Neko's glasses without his explicit permission.**
    

The glasses are considered both essential safety equipment and a personal keepsake.

Personnel are expected to respect this boundary.

---

## **IX. FINAL OBSERVATION**

The glasses changed more than just a safety protocol.

For the first time, Neko could share one of the most distinctive parts of himself without worrying about harming someone.

He still preferred keeping his bangs over his eyes.

Not because he had to.

Simply because it felt natural.

The glasses gave him a choice he had never truly had before.

And while he was happy to let trusted friends see his eyes now...

The round spectacles themselves remained something only he would ever adjust.
