## **I. WORKLOAD INCREASE**

Engineering experienced a significant increase in workload following several concurrent infrastructure upgrades.

Although Neko remained capable of completing his assignments, Medical observed that he was spending nearly every minute focused on repairs, inspections, and emergency maintenance.

This created an unexpected problem.

Miss Neko still required supervision during recovery.

Normally...

Neko would periodically check on her.

Now...

He barely had enough time to stop moving.

---

### Lucy

> "...You're overworking again."

### Neko

> "...A little."

---

### Lucy

> "...You've said 'a little' for six straight hours."

---

Neko looked at the pile of work orders.

---

### Neko

> "...Fair."

---

## **II. A SIMPLE SOLUTION**

Rather than asking another engineer to watch over Miss Neko...

Neko simply looked beside him.

At Mirage.

---

### Neko

> "...Can you watch over sis?"

---

Mirage nodded immediately.

---

### Mirage

> "...Okay."

---

Neko removed the transparent recovery backpack from his own shoulders.

Mirage quietly put it on.

The fit was almost perfect.

Tiny Cat Miss Neko blinked awake inside the enclosure.

She looked through the transparent wall.

At Mirage.

Then at Neko.

Then back again.

---

### Tiny Cat Miss Neko

> "...Mrr?"

---

### Neko

> "...He's helping me today."

---

She tilted her head.

Thought about it for several seconds.

Then...

Curled back around her pillow.

Apparently satisfied.

## **III. ALWAYS NEARBY**

One concern was immediately raised.

---

### Engineer

> "...What if Miss Neko needs you?"

---

Neko simply pointed toward Mirage.

Who was already following him.

Exactly two steps behind.

Just like always.

---

### Neko

> "...He never leaves me."

---

Indeed...

Mirage naturally mirrored Neko's routes throughout the Blacksite.

Where Neko went...

Mirage followed.

Maintaining a comfortable distance.

The result was somewhat amusing.

Instead of Neko carrying Miss Neko...

Mirage carried her.

While following Neko.

Creating what one intern described as:

> "...A two-person conga line."

## **IV. MISS NEKO'S PERSPECTIVE**

## **X. FINAL OBSERVATION**

Miss Neko's recovery continues at a steady pace. While her condition has improved, Medical Division has not yet cleared her to resume sustained use of her human form. She remains in her cat form for the majority of each day to conserve energy.

To reduce strain on Neko during periods of increased workload, Mirage has temporarily assumed responsibility for transporting Miss Neko in the Portable Feline Recovery Containment Pack. Observations indicate that he performs this role with consistent care, remaining close to Neko while ensuring Miss Neko remains comfortable and undisturbed.

Engineering staff have grown accustomed to the sight of the trio moving through the Blacksite together:

- Neko leading the way with a toolbox.
    
- Mirage a few steps behind, wearing the transparent backpack.
    
- Miss Neko asleep inside, occasionally waking just long enough to check that both of them are still there before curling back up.
    

A handwritten note from Miss Neko, added after a particularly restful afternoon, simply read:

> **"Lil bro built me the bed. He carries the Blacksite. Mirage carries me. I think I've got the best brothers I could ask for."**
