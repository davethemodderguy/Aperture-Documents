# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-ZNEKO-LOG-124**

### **THE FLATLINE**

### **Classification:** INTERNAL – OMEGA PRIORITY

---

### **04:17 Hours**

The Medical Wing was quiet.

Only the rhythmic beeping of monitors filled the room.

Miss Neko was asleep in the chair beside Neko's bed, still holding his hand.

Star had dozed off against the wall.

Nightshade sat with her arms folded, pretending to read, though she hadn't turned a page in nearly an hour.

Niko stood by the window.

Director Lucious had returned to his office only minutes earlier.

For the first time in weeks...

Everything was still.

---

Then...

A single alarm sounded.

**Beep.**

Another.

**Beep.**

The heart monitor began to stutter.

A nurse looked up.

---

### Nurse

> "...Doctor?"

---

The waveform weakened.

Once.

Twice.

Then...

A long, uninterrupted tone filled the room.

---

**BEEEEEEEEEEEEEEP—**

---

Everyone woke instantly.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro?"

---

She grabbed his hand.

It was still warm.

---

### Medical Officer

> "Code Blue!"

---

Doctors rushed into the room.

Defibrillator.

Emergency medication.

Manual ventilation.

Every protocol was initiated within seconds.

---

### Medical Officer

> "Charge!"

---

**THUMP.**

Neko's body jolted.

Nothing.

---

### Medical Officer

> "Again!"

---

**THUMP.**

Still nothing.

---

More medication.

More compressions.

More attempts.

Minutes passed.

No response.

---

Outside the room, staff gathered silently.

No one dared enter.

Everyone knew what the alarm meant.

---

### **Star**

Star stood frozen at the doorway.

She whispered the same words over and over.

> "...Wake up..."

> "...Please..."

---

### **Nightshade**

Nightshade clenched her fists so tightly her knuckles turned white.

She didn't say anything.

She couldn't.

---

### **Niko**

Niko watched every resuscitation attempt.

Every one.

As an engineer, he had always believed that every problem had a solution.

For the first time...

He had none.

---

### **Director Lucious**

Lucious arrived while the team was still working.

He said only one sentence.

---

### Director Lucious

> "...Continue."

---

No one stopped.

Not after ten minutes.

Not after twenty.

Not after thirty.

Medical fought far longer than protocol required.

Not because procedure demanded it.

Because none of them were willing to be the first person to give up.

---

Eventually...

The Lead Medical Officer slowly lowered his hands.

He looked at Director Lucious.

His voice barely carried across the room.

---

### Lead Medical Officer

> "...Director..."

---

Lucious understood before the words came.

He closed his eyes.

Then gave a single, reluctant nod.

---

The monitor's continuous tone echoed through the Medical Wing.

---

### **Miss Neko**

She remained beside the bed.

Still holding Neko's hand.

She waited.

Surely he would wake up.

He always did.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Lil bro..."

---

Silence.

---

She smiled weakly.

As though speaking to someone merely sleeping.

---

### Miss Neko

> "...Come on..."

> "...You've slept long enough."

---

Nothing.

---

The smile disappeared.

She lowered her forehead onto the bed.

For several long moments...

The room heard nothing except quiet sobbing.

---

### **The Blacksite**

The announcement was not made over the public address system.

Instead, department heads informed their teams personally.

Work stopped.

Maintenance set their tools down.

Research terminals were left untouched.

Security officers removed their helmets.

Even the Armory, usually alive with machinery and testing, fell silent.

No one argued.

No one spoke above a whisper.

---

### **Final Entry**

For weeks, Neko had remained suspended between life and death, refusing to let go despite every prediction.

On this day, despite every effort made by Medical Division, that fight came to an end.

The Blacksite did not lose a weapon designer.

It did not lose an anomaly.

It lost a younger brother.

A colleague.

A friend.

Someone whose first concern, even while critically ill, had always been asking how everyone else was doing.

The final line added to the incident report was written by Director Lucious himself.

> **"He gave everything he had. We will remember him for the life he lived, not the illness that took him."**
