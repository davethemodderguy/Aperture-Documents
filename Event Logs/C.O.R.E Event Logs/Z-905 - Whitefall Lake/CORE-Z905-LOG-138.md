# **I. SUMMARY**

Snowy has not been seen this week.

---

This is not unusual by itself.

---

What is unusual is that he has not appeared **at all**.

---

No sightings.

No conversations.

No disappearances.

No glitter.

No footprints.

No signs of habitation.

No evidence of movement.

---

Nothing.

---

Initially, residents assumed Snowy was simply avoiding everyone again.

---

That assumption proved incorrect.

---

Lucy AI's scans found no trace of Snowy's physical manifestation anywhere within Whitefall Lake.

---

For the first time since returning to his body:

---

Snowy has completely demanifested.
