# **I. INCIDENT SUMMARY**

While conducting routine logging operations within Whitefall’s northern frost forest—

Cheer was attacked by an adult polar bear.

---

Environmental conditions:

- severe snowfall
    
- low visibility
    
- heavy wind pressure
    
- unstable ice terrain
    

---

The attack occurred suddenly.

---

Witness drones recorded the bear emerging aggressively from dense snow-covered vegetation before pinning Cheer to the ground.

---

Cheer reportedly struggled heavily beneath the animal’s weight.

---

At the point lethal force appeared imminent—

the bear abruptly stopped.

---

Reason initially unknown.

---

Then—

the animal focused on the bandages wrapped around Cheer’s leg.

---

Previous records confirm Cheer had sustained minor injuries before leaving the cabin earlier that day.

---

The bear reportedly examined the bandages briefly—

before its behavior changed completely.

---

Instead of continuing aggression,

the animal gently grabbed Cheer’s wrist using its mouth and attempted to guide her elsewhere.

---

Cheer followed.

---

---

# **II. CAVE DISCOVERY**

---

The bear led Cheer toward a small snow-covered cave system nearby.

---

Inside—

a polar bear cub was discovered injured.

---

Medical condition:

- fractured rear left leg
    
- severe pain response
    
- inability to move properly
    

---

The adult bear reportedly displayed clear distress behavior while near the cub.

---

After rapid field treatment performed by Cheer—

the cub’s injuries were stabilized using improvised splints and bandaging.

---

Following treatment,

the cub reportedly calmed almost immediately.

---

Witness drones recorded the cub attempting to stand despite injury shortly afterward.

---

---

# **III. RETURN TO CABIN**

---

Following treatment,

the mother bear and cub voluntarily followed Cheer back to Whitefall Cabin.

---

Current arrangement:

### Mother Bear

- remains outside cabin perimeter
    
- sleeps near porch area
    
- displays protective territorial behavior toward cabin surroundings
    

### Cub

- remains primarily indoors with Cheer
    
- frequently sleeps beside fireplace
    
- displays strong attachment behavior toward Cheer
    

---

The cub reportedly often curls beside Cheer during nighttime hours,

leading Lucy AI personnel to describe it informally as:

> “a living white teddy bear”

---

---

# **IV. MOBILITY CHANGES**

---

The adult bear has developed unusually cooperative behavior toward Cheer.

---

Most notable adaptation:

the mother bear frequently allows Cheer to ride on her back during long-distance travel through Whitefall.

---

This has significantly increased:

- mobility speed
    
- storm traversal safety
    
- supply transportation efficiency
    
- emergency response range
    

---

The bear reportedly responds calmly to directional gestures and vocal commands from Cheer.

---

Additionally—

despite the cub’s young age and injured condition,

its movement speed after recovery is considered abnormally high.

---

Lucy AI observations indicate:

> the cub can traverse deep snow terrain faster than most humanoid entities inside Whitefall

---

The cub frequently circles around Cheer during patrols before returning quickly to her side.

---

---

# **V. CABIN LIFE**

---

Despite the new animal companionship—

the emotional atmosphere inside Whitefall Cabin remains unchanged.

---

Quiet.

---

Heavy.

---

Incomplete.

---

Every evening,

Cheer still prepares meals for two.

---

One bowl for herself.

One bowl placed beside Snowy’s usual seat.

---

The bowl remains untouched nightly.

---

Most notable observation:

the bears never touch Snowy’s bowl.

---

Even during periods where additional food is unavailable.

---

The cub reportedly approached it once during early residence days—

before the mother bear quietly pulled it away.

---

Neither animal has disturbed the bowl since.

---

# **VI. BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATIONS**

---

The presence of the bears has improved certain survival conditions inside Whitefall.

---

Examples include:

- increased warmth near cabin exterior
    
- improved transport efficiency
    
- reduced immediate environmental danger
    
- emotional stabilization during storms
    

---

However—

Lucy AI confirms neither animal has meaningfully reduced Cheer’s loneliness.

---

Because the absence inside the cabin was not physical.

---

It was personal.

---

The bears could sit beside her.

Protect her.

Travel with her.

Sleep nearby.

---

But they could not replace:

- tiny footsteps across wooden floors
    
- quiet apologies
    
- trembling hands holding warm bowls
    
- soft wolf ears twitching during sleep
    
- a child asking if he deserved tomorrow
    

---

And Cheer knew that.

Whitefall adapted again.
