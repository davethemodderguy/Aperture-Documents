# **I. SUMMARY**

Another week has passed.

---

Snowy has still not reappeared.

---

No manifestation.

No sightings.

No voice.

No glitter.

No footprints.

No teleportation events.

---

Nothing.

---

Despite extensive monitoring, Whitefall Lake remains completely devoid of any direct evidence of Snowy's presence.

---

Almost completely.

---

Because two items remain.

---

A beanie.

And a pair of glasses.

---

---

# **II. THE DISCOVERY**

---

The items were found near the edge of the forest.

---

Partially covered by snow.

---

Not abandoned carelessly.

---

Placed.

---

Almost deliberately.

---

The beanie was folded.

---

The glasses rested beside it.

---

Neither item showed signs of damage.

---

Neither item showed signs of having been discarded in distress.

---

They simply existed.

---

As though someone had quietly left them there.

---

Lucy AI notes:

> location contained no accompanying biological, anomalous, or environmental traces

---

---

# **III. IDENTIFICATION**

---

There was no doubt regarding ownership.

---

The glasses belonged to Snowy.

---

The same glasses he continued wearing after reclaiming his body.

---

The same glasses visible in nearly every photograph taken after Whitefall's early years.

---

Beside them sat his beanie.

---

One of the few pieces of clothing Snowy consistently wore.

---

For many residents, the sight was immediately recognizable.

---

And immediately painful.

---

---

# **IV. CHEER**

---

Cheer was the first to pick up the glasses.

---

Witnesses report she stared at them for nearly a minute.

---

Without speaking.

---

Without moving.

---

Just staring.

---

Eventually she quietly asked:

> "Why would he leave these?"

---

Nobody answered.

---

Because nobody knew.

---

The glasses were one of the few possessions Snowy genuinely cared about.

---

The beanie was equally important.

---

Leaving them behind felt wrong.

---

Like a goodbye nobody wanted to interpret.

---

---

# **V. CHERRY**

---

Cherry's reaction was immediate.

---

Fear.

---

Not panic.

Not despair.

---

Fear.

---

Because she remembered something.

---

Throughout Snowy's decline, there had always been constants.

---

The eyepatch.

The bandages.

The glasses.

The beanie.

---

Small pieces of identity.

---

Small reminders of who he was.

---

Now two of those pieces were sitting alone in the snow.

---

One statement was recorded:

> "I don't like this."

---

Pause.

---

> "I really don't like this."

---

Lucy AI notes:

> discovery appears to have increased resident concern substantially

---

---

# **VI. SKY**

---

Sky returned to the discovery site several times throughout the week.

---

Not because she expected Snowy to appear.

---

Because she kept hoping there would be something else.

---

A note.

A message.

A footprint.

Anything.

---

There wasn't.

---

Only the glasses.

Only the beanie.

---

One statement was documented:

> "It feels like finding someone's shadow."

---

No one asked her to explain.

---

Everyone understood.

---

---

# **VII. STAR'S ANALYSIS**

---

Star personally reviewed the scene.

---

Her conclusion was cautious.

---

> "If this were a farewell, he'd have left a message."

---

Pause.

---

> "Snowy always leaves messages."

---

This observation was supported by multiple historical records.

---

Even during his worst periods:

He left notes.

He left words.

He left fragments.

---

Silence was unusual.

---

The complete absence of communication was what concerned Star most.

---

---

# **VIII. THE ITEMS**

---

The glasses and beanie were eventually brought back to the cabin.

---

Not to store them.

---

Not to archive them.

---

Simply because nobody wanted them left outside.

---

Cheer placed them carefully inside Snowy's room.

---

On his desk.

---

Exactly where he would normally leave them.

---

The room itself remains otherwise untouched.

---

Nobody has moved anything.

---

Nobody has claimed the space.

---

Nobody has renamed it.

---

For the first time in months, the residents seem unwilling to alter anything connected to Snowy.

---

As though changing even a single detail might somehow make his absence permanent.

---

---

# **IX. WHITEFALL'S MOOD**

---

The atmosphere throughout Whitefall continues deteriorating.

---

Not dramatically.

---

Quietly.

---

The kind of sadness that settles into routines.

---

People still eat together.

Still work.

Still talk.

---

But there is always something missing.

---

An empty chair.

A missing voice.

A joke that never gets told.

---

The absence has become part of daily life.

---

And nobody likes how quickly they're getting used to it.

---

---

# **X. FINAL NOTE**

---

Snowy has still not returned.

---

No manifestation has occurred.

---

No communication has occurred.

---

No anomaly activity has occurred.

---

Only two objects remain.

---

A beanie.

A pair of glasses.

---

Simple things.

---

Yet somehow they have become the closest thing Whitefall has to proof that Snowy was ever here at all.

---

Cheer still checks outside every morning.

---

Sky still visits the tree.

---

Cherry still watches for glitter that never comes.

---

Aurora still looks toward the doorway during dinner.

---

Bao still prepares too much food by habit.

---

Star still reviews reports hoping to find something everyone else missed.

---

And every day ends the same way.

---

No Snowy.

---

Just the glasses.

Just the beanie.

And the growing hope that wherever he is—

he still remembers who they are.
