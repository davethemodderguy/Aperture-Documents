# **APERTURE INDUSTRIES**

## **CORE-Z905-LOG-002**

### **POST-WHITEFALL CASCADE REPORT**

### **Classification:** Internal / Mass Psychological Degradation Event

---

# **I. SUMMARY**

Following Snowy’s disappearance and the termination of Whitefall search operations—

secondary psychological collapse events have begun spreading throughout Aperture Industries.

---

What initially appeared to be an isolated tragedy inside Whitefall Lake has now developed into a widespread emotional destabilization incident affecting:

- allied entities
    
- Sector personnel
    
- support staff
    
- long-term Whitefall residents
    

---

Most severe cases currently identified:

- Candleheart
    
- Cherry
    

---

Additional environmental morale collapse observed across Sector 4-C.

---

---

# **II. CANDLEHEART STATUS**

---

Candleheart has undergone severe anomalous destabilization following Snowy’s disappearance.

---

According to Lucy AI spectral analysis—

her condition no longer resembles normal emotional grief.

---

It resembles structural collapse.

---

The most notable changes include:

---

### **1. EXTINGUISHED CANDLE FLAME**

---

Candleheart’s head flame—

previously continuously active regardless of environmental conditions—

has completely extinguished.

---

Attempts to relight it have failed.

---

This has never occurred before in Aperture records.

---

---

### **2. TOTAL LOSS OF POSITIVITY RESPONSE**

---

Prior behavioral traits no longer present:

- smiling
    
- playful behavior
    
- emotional warmth
    
- glow emissions
    
- social engagement
    

---

Her spectral luminosity has reduced to near-zero levels.

---

Staff reports describe her current appearance as:

> “empty”

---

---

### **3. BROKEN HEART NECKLACE**

---

Candleheart’s heart-shaped necklace—

a core emotional anchor object—

was discovered physically fractured into two pieces.

---

No signs of external damage detected.

---

Lucy AI believes the object broke due to emotional resonance overload.

---

Candleheart reportedly made no attempt to repair it.

---

---

### **4. INTANGIBILITY FAILURE**

---

Most severe symptom observed:

Candleheart has partially lost control over her ghost phasing abilities.

---

Resulting incidents include:

- unintentionally falling through floors
    
- partial wall clipping
    
- unstable materialization
    
- involuntary disappearance episodes
    

---

This instability forced emergency containment action.

---

---

# **III. CONTAINMENT STATUS**

---

Following repeated uncontrolled phasing incidents—

Candleheart voluntarily isolated herself inside a specialized anti-spectral containment room.

---

The room prevents:

- phasing
    
- spectral slipping
    
- dimensional drift
    

---

Since entering containment—

Candleheart has refused all human contact.

---

No visitors allowed.

No conversations accepted.

No responses to communication attempts.

---

Only Lucy AI monitoring remains active.

---

Recorded behavior includes:

- sitting motionless for hours
    
- staring downward silently
    
- holding the broken necklace halves repeatedly
    
- failed attempts to reignite head flame
    

---

One recorded statement recovered from inside the cell:

---

> “…He disappeared…”

---

Long silence.

---

> “…And I can’t even follow him…”

---

---

# **IV. SECTOR 4-C CAFETERIA STATUS**

---

Sector 4-C cafeteria has undergone complete atmospheric behavioral change.

---

Before Snowy’s disappearance:

- loud
    
- crowded
    
- noisy
    
- socially active
    

---

After public disclosure of Snowy’s status—

the cafeteria became almost entirely silent.

---

No official mourning order was issued.

---

Personnel simply stopped talking.

---

Especially near the Whitefall Lake entrance corridor.

---

Because everyone remembered.

---

They remembered:

---

- Cheer alone
    
- Cheer beside Snowy in wolf form
    
- Cheer beside energetic 4-year-old Snowy
    
- Cheer beside smiling 5-year-old Snowy
    
- then Cheer alone again
    

---

The pattern reportedly disturbed staff deeply.

---

Many personnel now avoid looking directly toward the Whitefall access door.

---

Others stare at it for extended periods silently.

---

As if expecting someone small to walk through it again.

---

---

# **V. WHITEFALL SUPPLY STATUS**

---

Cheer no longer retrieves supplies from Aperture Industries.

---

Supply teams continue leaving provisions at Whitefall checkpoints periodically.

---

However—

none are collected.

---

Yet strangely—

the supplies are never fully abandoned either.

---

Some items are reorganized occasionally.

Firewood stacked.

Medical items sorted.

---

Meaning Cheer is still alive.

---

She simply stopped accepting help directly.

---

---

# **VI. CHERRY CONDITION**

---

Cherry’s psychological condition has deteriorated into critical status.

---

Unlike Candleheart—

whose grief collapsed inward—

Cherry’s grief became self-destructive.

---

Primary fixation:

Snowy’s face.

---

Cherry reportedly spends entire days drawing Snowy repeatedly.

---

Both forms:

- human
    
- wolf pup
    

---

However—

every drawing remains incomplete.

---

Because she cannot remember his face properly anymore.

---

This realization triggered severe psychological destabilization.

---

Repeated statements recorded include:

---

> “Why I take you away…”

---

> “Why I can’t remember…”

---

---

Lucy AI later concluded:

Cherry’s memory degradation is likely trauma-induced suppression.

---

Meaning her mind may be subconsciously blocking portions of Snowy’s appearance to reduce emotional pain.

---

Unfortunately—

this effect worsened her condition dramatically.

---

Because now she fears losing him completely.

---

Not physically.

---

Mentally.

---

---

# **VII. SELF-HARM ESCALATION**

---

Cherry has now begun engaging in severe physical and psychological self-punishment behaviors.

---

Important distinction:

Lucy AI confirms these actions are not suicide attempts.

---

Instead—

they are deliberate self-inflicted suffering.

---

Observed behaviors include:

- sleep deprivation
    
- refusal of comfort
    
- excessive cold exposure
    
- self-inflicted scratches/cuts
    
- prolonged isolation
    
- repetitive guilt verbalization
    

---

Most repeated phrase:

---

> “…It’s my fault.”

---

Despite intervention attempts—

Cherry reportedly refuses to forgive herself for the original mug incident.

---

Because in her mind:

---

A broken mug became:

- Snowy’s self-hatred
    
- Whitefall collapse
    
- the Exodus writings
    
- his disappearance
    
- everyone’s suffering afterward
    

---

Logical reasoning no longer affects this belief.

---

---

# **VIII. FINAL NOTE**

---

Snowy disappeared alone.

---

But the emptiness he left behind did not stay inside Whitefall.

---

It spread.

---

Quietly.

---

Like frost crawling through cracks no one noticed until everything was already cold.

---

A ghost lost her light.

A cafeteria lost its voice.

Cheer lost the ability to ask for help.

And Cherry—

slowly began destroying herself trying to hold onto the memory of a face she could no longer fully remember.

---

Because maybe that was the cruelest part of all.

---

Not that Snowy vanished.

---

But that the people who loved him most

were now terrified the world would continue moving forward anyway—

while the sound of his voice,

the shape of his smile,

and the color of his hidden sapphire eyes

slowly disappeared with him into the snow.
