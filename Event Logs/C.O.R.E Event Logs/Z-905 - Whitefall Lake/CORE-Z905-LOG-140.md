# **I. SUMMARY**

Snowy did not appear this week.

---

No manifestations.

No sightings.

No messages.

No glitter.

No signs.

---

Nothing.

---

Search efforts have largely ceased.

Not because anyone gave up.

Because there was nowhere left to search.

---

As a result, Whitefall residents found themselves confronting something they had avoided for months.

---

Their own actions.

---

Not what Snowy did.

---

What they did.

---

---

# **II. THE CABIN**

---

The discussion began unintentionally.

---

Dinner was unusually quiet.

---

Nobody had much to say.

---

Eventually, Bao quietly spoke.

---

> "I should've checked on him more."

---

Silence followed.

---

Then Aurora lowered her head.

---

> "I told myself he just needed space."

---

Pause.

---

> "Maybe he did."

---

Another pause.

---

> "But not that much."

---

Nobody disagreed.

---

---

# **III. CHEER**

---

Cheer was perhaps the hardest on herself.

---

Not because she hated Snowy.

---

Because she loved him.

---

Which made her mistakes hurt more.

---

Her own words:

> "I was supposed to be his big sister."

---

Silence.

---

> "When he was drowning under the ice, nobody was looking."

---

> "When he left after recovering, I let him go."

---

> "When everyone ignored him, I joined in."

---

Her voice reportedly broke.

---

> "I thought I was teaching him a lesson."

---

Long pause.

---

> "I never imagined I was teaching him he wasn't loved."

---

Nobody could answer that.

---

---

# **IV. SKY**

---

Sky remained quiet for most of the discussion.

---

Until someone mentioned the tree.

---

The tree marked:

> Snowy + Sky

---

Sky stared at the table.

---

Then quietly said:

> "I forgot him."

---

Nobody interrupted.

---

> "Not completely."

---

> "But enough."

---

She continued.

---

> "He remembered promises I forgot."

---

> "He remembered birthdays I forgot."

---

> "He remembered things I didn't."

---

Long silence.

---

> "And then I said things I can never take back."

---

Nobody asked which things.

---

Everyone remembered.

---

---

# **V. CHERRY**

---

Cherry's regrets were different.

---

Because she knew exactly what it felt like to be abandoned.

---

And yet...

---

She couldn't stop what happened.

---

Her statement was brief.

---

> "I should've screamed louder."

---

Confused looks followed.

---

Cherry clarified.

---

> "When everyone was punishing him."

---

Pause.

---

> "I should've screamed louder."

---

> "I knew where that road led."

---

Nobody argued.

---

Because Cherry had walked that road herself.

---

---

# **VI. AURORA**

---

Aurora struggled throughout the discussion.

---

Eventually she admitted something that had haunted her.

---

A statement she made during the worst period.

---

One she wished she could erase.

---

> "I shouldn't have come back that day."

---

The statement had been made about Snowy.

---

During the months of anger.

---

Months of blame.

---

Months of frustration.

---

Aurora buried her face in her hands.

---

> "I didn't mean it."

---

Pause.

---

> "But I said it."

---

Nobody corrected her.

---

Because intent no longer mattered.

---

The words existed.

---

---

# **VII. BAO**

---

Bao's regret centered around smaller things.

---

The things people often overlook.

---

She remembered saving birthday cake for him.

---

Waiting for him.

---

Watching the party end without him.

---

Then eating the slice alone.

---

At the time, she thought she was sad.

---

Now she realized something else.

---

> "I never went looking."

---

Nobody responded.

---

Bao continued.

---

> "I kept waiting for him to come back."

---

> "I should've gone looking."

---

---

# **VIII. STAR**

---

Star's reflection was more analytical.

---

But no less painful.

---

She reviewed the incident as a whole.

---

Then quietly summarized it.

---

> "Everybody thought someone else would help."

---

Silence.

---

> "Everybody thought someone else would stop it."

---

More silence.

---

> "Nobody did."

---

Nobody disagreed.

---

---

# **IX. YUKI AND SHIDRA**

---

Yuki and Shidra participated as well.

---

Though their words required translation.

---

Snowy was absent.

---

Meaning another translator had to be used.

---

The process was slow.

---

Painfully slow.

---

Eventually their message was understood.

---

Yuki:

> "We thought he was sad."

---

Shidra:

> "We didn't know he was dying inside."

---

Another pause.

---

Yuki:

> "We should've stayed with him."

---

Shidra:

> "Even if he told us not to."

---

Several residents reportedly cried after hearing this.

---

---

# **X. THE REALIZATION**

---

Hours into the discussion, a realization emerged.

---

Not everyone hurt Snowy intentionally.

---

Many incidents were accidents.

Misunderstandings.

Mistakes.

Moments of anger.

Moments of carelessness.

---

But the result remained the same.

---

Snowy was hurt.

---

Again.

And again.

And again.

---

Until eventually he stopped believing people loved him.

---

Then stopped believing he deserved love.

---

Then stopped believing he deserved to exist.

---

Nobody at the table could identify exactly when that happened.

---

Only that it did.

---

---

# **XI. FINAL NOTE**

---

The discussion ended long after midnight.

---

No solutions were found.

---

No discoveries were made.

---

Snowy did not return.

---

The cabin remained quiet.

---

Before everyone went to sleep, Cheer reportedly visited Snowy's room.

---

The glasses still sat on the desk.

---

The beanie remained exactly where it had been placed.

---

Neither had moved.

---

Cheer looked at them for several moments.

---

Then quietly spoke to the empty room.

---

> "I'm sorry."

---

There was no reply.

---

No manifestation.

No glitter.

No voice.

---

Just silence.

---

For perhaps the first time since Snowy disappeared, nobody spent the week wondering where he was.

---

Instead, they spent it wondering something much harder.

---

How many chances had they been given to stop this?

---

And how many had they missed?
