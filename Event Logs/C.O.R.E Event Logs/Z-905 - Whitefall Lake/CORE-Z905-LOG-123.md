# **I. SUMMARY**

A previously unknown side effect of Yoru's permanent reintegration manifested this week.

---

One of his spectral abilities appears to have survived the transition into physical existence.

---

Confirmed:

> Snowy can now disappear voluntarily.

---

Unlike his former ghost state, this is not involuntary.

Not fear-triggered.

Not instability.

---

He simply chooses to vanish.

---

When doing so, his body dissolves into a cloud of glowing azure particles.

---

The particles remain suspended briefly before dispersing naturally.

---

Lucy AI notes:

> ability appears inherited directly from prior spectral form

---

---

# **II. AZURE PARTICLES**

---

Initial concern centered around potential anomalous contamination.

---

Testing revealed something unexpected.

---

The particles produce a measurable calming effect.

---

Observed effects include:

- reduced anxiety
    
- emotional stabilization
    
- lowered stress responses
    
- decreased panic symptoms
    
- improved mood
    

---

Most residents described the sensation as:

> "warm"

or

> "comforting"

---

Daniel's analysis classified the particles as functionally similar to an antidepressant.

---

No harmful effects detected.

---

Most unusual finding:

> Snowy is completely immune.

---

No matter how concentrated the particles become,  
Snowy experiences no emotional change whatsoever.

---

Lucy AI assessment:

> subject appears incapable of benefiting from his own restorative anomaly

---

# **IX. FINAL NOTE**

---

The irony was not lost on anyone this week.

---

Snowy spent months trying to make everyone smile.

Trying to help people.

Trying to carry burdens that weren't his.

---

Now,

---

A cloud of soft azure light.

A moment of comfort.

A little less sadness.

---

Given freely to everyone nearby.

---

Everyone except him.

---

Cherry watches the glitter fall and wonders why he never receives the same peace.

Cheer watches it and wonders how much pain he still carries alone.

And somewhere beyond the drifting particles,

---

Still wearing his glasses.

Still hiding his damaged eye beneath bandages and an eyepatch.

Still avoiding closeness.

Still convinced he's harder to love than everyone insists.

---

Leaving little traces of comfort behind him wherever he goes—

while remaining unable to share in that comfort himself.
