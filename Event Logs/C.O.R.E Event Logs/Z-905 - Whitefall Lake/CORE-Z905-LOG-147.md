# **I. SUMMARY**

Something happened.

---

Without warning.

Without explanation.

Without any observable trigger.

---

Not joy.

---

The manifestation occurred near Whitefall Lake.

---

No azure glitter.

No warning signs.

No portal.

---

---

---

Breathing heavily.

---

Trembling uncontrollably.
