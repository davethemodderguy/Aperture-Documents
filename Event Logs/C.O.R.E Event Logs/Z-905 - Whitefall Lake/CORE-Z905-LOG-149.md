# **I. SUMMARY**

This week, personnel previously uninvolved in direct observation were formally briefed on Snowy's current condition.

---

Those notified included:

- Director Lucious
    
- Bao
    
- Frost
    
- Cinder
    
- Stitch
    
- Viridis
    

---

All were granted access to domain observation reports, psychological assessments, and recovery projections.

---

The reactions varied.

---

None were positive.

---

# **II. DIRECTOR LUCIOUS**

---

Director Lucious reviewed all 148 prior logs personally.

---

The review reportedly took several days.

---

No comments were made during the process.

---

No interruptions.

---

No questions.

---

After finishing the final report, he closed the file and remained silent for nearly ten minutes.

---

His eventual statement was brief.

---

> "This should never have reached this point."

---

No one disagreed.

---

# **III. STITCH**

---

Stitch's reaction was unusually subdued.

---

Normally calm and analytical, he spent much of the briefing reviewing behavioral timelines.

---

Particularly the period before Snowy's disappearance.

---

The silence eventually broke.

---

> "There were warning signs."

---

Pause.

---

> "Lots of them."

---

More silence.

---

> "Nobody realized how serious they were."

---

# **IV. CINDER**

---

Cinder struggled significantly with the reports.

---

Especially the recordings showing Snowy's current fear response.

---

Several sessions had to be paused.

---

One observation repeatedly appeared in his notes.

---

> "He's scared before anyone even speaks."

---

Pause.

---

> "That's the part that hurts."

---

# **V. VIRIDIS**

---

Viridis focused heavily on the Domain Analysis sections.

---

Particularly the revelation that domains are manifestations of deeply rooted desires, fears, and beliefs.

---

His conclusion disturbed several personnel.

---

> "The domain wasn't trying to convince him."

---

Silence.

---

> "It was reflecting what he already believed."

---

The room reportedly became very quiet after that.

---

# **VI. FROST**

---

Frost reacted differently.

---

Rather than focusing on the domain itself, he focused on Snowy's current state.

---

The disappearances.

The fear.

The fragmented speech.

---

The fact that Snowy now treats nearly every interaction as a threat.

---

Frost's statement was recorded.

---

> "He came back."

---

Pause.

---

> "But he didn't come home."

---

No further comments were made.

---

# **VII. BAO**

---

Bao was affected the most.

---

By a significant margin.

---

During review of domain footage, she learned something that had not previously been disclosed outside senior personnel.

---

Her mirage was among the most active entities inside the domain.

---

Not because Snowy feared Bao.

---

But because he cared about her.

---

The domain exploited that attachment.

---

Repeatedly.

---

Mercilessly.

---

Every appearance of Mirage Bao carried the same message:

---

That Snowy was unwanted.

That he ruined things.

That he should leave.

---

And in some of the most severe observation cycles, Mirage Bao became one of the entities directly responsible for tormenting him.

---

Bao reportedly stopped the playback herself.

---

She could not continue.

---

When asked why, she only replied:

---

> "Because that's not me."

---

Several minutes passed.

---

Then:

---

> "But he believed it was."

---

The realization devastated her.

---

# **VIII. THE BIRTHDAY INCIDENT**

---

The destroyed birthday party remained particularly difficult for Bao.

---

Observers initially believed the scene persisted because Snowy missed the party.

---

Further analysis suggests otherwise.

---

Snowy had accepted the invitation.

---

He intended to go.

---

The memory remained because it represented something he wanted.

---

Something he lost.

---

Something he believed he no longer deserved.

---

Bao reportedly cried after reviewing the scene.

---

Especially after seeing the untouched slice of cake preserved within the domain's recreation.

---

# **IX. CURRENT CONSENSUS**

---

Following the briefing, all newly informed personnel reached roughly the same conclusion.

---

The situation is no longer a search operation.

---

Snowy has already been found.

---

The challenge now is something much harder.

---

Convincing him that reality is safer than what the domain taught him.

---

Unfortunately, current evidence suggests Snowy is nowhere near ready to believe that.

---

---

As the meeting concluded, nobody discussed containment.

Nobody discussed anomalies.

Nobody discussed procedures.

---

Instead, the room focused on a single question.

---

How much of Snowy is still there?

---

Not physically.

---

Emotionally.

---

Mentally.

---

The answer remains unknown.

---

What is known is that Snowy continues to disappear whenever people get too close.

---

He continues leaving Azure Glitter behind.

---

And somewhere beneath the fear, guilt, and damage left by the domain, there may still be a frightened boy trying desperately to avoid being hurt again.

---

Whether Whitefall can ever reach him remains uncertain.

---

But for the first time, the people outside Whitefall Lake understand the scale of what happened.

---

And none of them are comfortable with it.
