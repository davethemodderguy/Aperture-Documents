# **I. SUMMARY**

Whitefall Lake has reached an unusual consensus.

---

There are now two things considered more valuable than almost anything else within the residence.

---

Not artifacts.

---

Not relics.

---

Not money.

---

Not power.

---

Two things.

---

Snowy's smile.

---

And Snowy's voice.

---

Because neither have been seen in so long that they have begun to feel impossible.

---

# **II. RARITY**

---

Months ago, hearing Snowy talk was unavoidable.

---

He talked during breakfast.

---

He talked during lunch.

---

He talked during dinner.

---

He talked during walks.

---

He talked during work.

---

He talked when nobody asked him to.

---

Sometimes he talked specifically because nobody asked him to.

---

The cabin used to complain about it.

---

Constantly.

---

Now?

---

The silence is worse.

---

Much worse.

---

Because now people know what the alternative feels like.

---

# **III. THE SMILE**

---

The same applies to his smile.

---

Once common.

---

Once ordinary.

---

Once something nobody paid attention to.

---

Now?

---

Nobody remembers the last time they saw one.

---

The expressionless face has become normal.

---

And that realization frightens people.

---

Because they don't want it to become normal.

---

# **IV. GIFTS**

---

Residents continue bringing Snowy gifts.

---

Food.

---

Books.

---

Handmade items.

---

Small decorations.

---

Personal letters.

---

Winter accessories.

---

Things they hope he might like.

---

Things they know he likes.

---

Snowy accepts almost all of them.

---

Without resistance.

---

Without complaint.

---

Without gratitude.

---

Without rejection.

---

He simply takes them.

---

And leaves them somewhere nearby.

---

Sometimes for hours.

---

Sometimes for days.

---

Untouched.

---

# **V. THE PROBLEM**

---

Nobody is upset because the gifts go unused.

---

Nobody expected repayment.

---

Nobody expected enthusiasm.

---

The problem is that Snowy used to react.

---

Even if the reaction was ridiculous.

---

Even if it became an argument.

---

Even if it somehow escalated into a forty-minute debate over something completely irrelevant.

---

There was always something.

---

Now there is nothing.

---

# **VI. BAO**

---

Among all residents, Bao has been affected the most.

---

Noticeably.

---

Painfully.

---

Because Bao remembers exactly who Snowy used to be.

---

And because Bao herself has always been one of Whitefall's brightest personalities.

---

The cheerful water goddess.

---

The whale girl who could make almost any situation feel lighter.

---

Who smiled easily.

---

Who laughed often.

---

Who spread positivity almost effortlessly.

---

Yet over recent weeks, residents have noticed something missing.

---

Not entirely gone.

---

But diminished.

---

A little weaker.

---

A little quieter.

---

# **VII. THE CUPCAKES**

---

The cupcakes became symbolic.

---

Not because of what happened.

---

Because of what didn't happen.

---

Snowy's favorite treat sat untouched.

---

Accepted.

---

But not enjoyed.

---

Present.

---

But unreachable.

---

And Bao has not forgotten that image.

---

# **VIII. OBSERVATION**

---

Several days after the cupcake incident, Bao reportedly entered the dining area carrying another small gift.

---

A simple handmade decoration.

---

One Snowy would've normally commented on immediately.

---

He accepted it.

---

Placed it beside him.

---

And resumed staring out the window.

---

No words.

---

No expression.

---

No reaction.

---

Witnesses report Bao remained standing there for nearly a minute.

---

Waiting.

---

Nothing happened.

---

Eventually she walked away.
