### **WHITEFALL INCIDENT — PHOTO ALTERATION ANOMALY**

### **Classification:** INTERNAL / HIGH CLEARANCE

### **Related Files:** CORE-Z905-LOG-160

---

# **I. SUMMARY**

A new anomaly has manifested within Whitefall Residence.

---

Every framed group photograph containing Snowy has been altered.

---

The changes were not witnessed occurring.

---

No resident reported seeing anyone enter the cabin.

---

No surveillance footage captured a culprit.

---

Yet the results appeared overnight.

---

On every photograph:

Snowy's face had been heavily scribbled over using black ink.

---

Particular focus was placed on the eyes.

---

In many cases, the face was almost completely obscured.

---

# **II. INVESTIGATION**

---

Initial assumptions suggested vandalism.

---

However, several unusual properties were quickly discovered.

---

The ink cannot be removed.

---

Attempts included:

- Water
    
- Soap
    
- Alcohol
    
- Solvents
    
- Anomalous cleaning compounds
    

---

All failed.

---

The markings remain intact.

---

Even replacement frames did not help.

---

The ink appears attached to the photograph itself.

---

# **III. AFFECTED IMAGES**

---

All known photographs depicting Snowy have been altered.

---

Examples include:

- Group celebrations
    
- Winter activities
    
- Cabin gatherings
    
- Birthday photographs
    
- Lake photographs
    
- Holiday photographs
    

---

The degree of alteration varies.

---

However one pattern remains consistent.

---

The eyes are always targeted.

---

Always.

---

# **IV. NOTES**

---

Every altered photograph was accompanied by handwritten notes.

---

The handwriting strongly resembles Snowy's.

---

Analysis remains inconclusive.

---

The messages are consistent in theme.

---

Examples include:

> "I don't belong here."

---

> "I don't deserve to exist."

---

> "Leave me."

---

> "You already did it once."

---

> "Just do it again."

---

> "Seriously."

---

Additional notes recovered throughout the cabin include:

> "Should've stayed gone."

---

> "Nobody actually needs me."

---

> "Stop looking for me."

---

> "You'll be happier."

---

> "Forget me."

---

# **V. RESIDENT REACTIONS**

---

The discovery had an immediate emotional impact.

---

Particularly because many of the photographs represented some of Whitefall's happiest memories.

---

Memories that Snowy had once been part of.

---

Now every image carried visible evidence of his current state of mind.

---

# **VI. BAO**

---

Bao was reportedly the first to discover the alterations.

---

The photograph in question depicted a winter celebration.

---

Everyone present was smiling.

---

Except Snowy's face was obscured beneath thick black marker.

---

A note had been attached.

---

> "I don't belong here."

---

Witnesses report Bao immediately removed the note.

---

She later replaced the photograph.

---

The replacement was altered again the following morning.

---

# **VII. CHERRY**

---

Cherry reacted with visible anger.

---

Not at Snowy.

---

At the notes.

---

At what they represented.

---

One statement was entered into the record.

---

> "I hate these."

---

Pause.

---

> "Not because they're mean."

---

Long silence.

---

> "Because he actually believes them."

---

# **VIII. SKY**

---

Sky reportedly spent several hours staring at one particular photograph.

---

The image had been taken near the old _Snowy + Sky_ tree.

---

Everyone else remained untouched.

---

Only Snowy's face had been obscured.

---

The accompanying note read:

> "Leave me."

---

Witnesses observed Sky quietly placing the note face-down.

---

No further comments were recorded.

---

# **IX. CHEER**

---

Cheer responded differently.

---

Rather than removing the notes immediately, she preserved them for documentation.

---

Her reasoning was simple.

---

> "If he's leaving these..."

---

Pause.

---

> "Then somewhere he's still trying to talk."

---

The statement has since become a subject of discussion among Whitefall personnel.

---

# **X. LUCY AI ANALYSIS**

---

Current analysis suggests the notes are not intended as threats.

---

Nor are they attempts to manipulate.

---

Instead, they appear to be expressions of deeply entrenched self-rejection.

---

The recurring themes include:

- Isolation
    
- Unworthiness
    
- Exclusion
    
- Self-erasure
    
- Fear of attachment
    

---

Notably absent are expressions of anger toward others.

---

The hostility observed in previous logs appears largely absent from the notes.

---

The hostility is directed inward.

---

# **XI. FINAL NOTE**

---

The photographs remain displayed throughout the cabin.

---

No one has removed them.

---

Not because the markings don't hurt.

---

They do.

---

But because the untouched portions of each image still remain.

---

The smiles.

---

The memories.

---

The moments that happened.

---

Moments that cannot be crossed out with a marker.

---

Even if Snowy tries to erase himself from the picture.

---

For now, every altered frame remains where it was originally placed.

---

A painful reminder that someone can be surrounded by people who care about them and still feel like they don't belong.

---

And a reminder that Whitefall's greatest challenge is no longer finding Snowy.

---

It's convincing him that the person scribbling out his face is wrong.
