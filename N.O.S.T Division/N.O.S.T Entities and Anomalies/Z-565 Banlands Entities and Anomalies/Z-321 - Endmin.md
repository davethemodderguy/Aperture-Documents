---

# **Overview**

Z-321, designated **"Endmin,"** is a friendly anomalous humanoid originating from the **Banlands**, where she formerly served as an **Endministrator**—an elite executioner and marksman operating directly under the authority of the Banlands' Warden, Lucy.

Despite her young appearance and gentle demeanor, Endmin possesses extensive combat experience and exceptional precision marksmanship. She currently resides at the Blacksite under the guardianship of Aperture Industries with permission from Lucy.

Since her arrival, Endmin has integrated exceptionally well with personnel and is considered a valued member of Room N-07.

---

# **Physical Description**

- Small teenage girl.
    
- Short, neatly maintained white hair.
    
- Hair color can be changed at will, though she always returns to white.
    
- Protective eye covering ("Endmin's Mask") constructed from an unidentified alloy with glowing yellow edges.
    
- Carries a bullpup bolt-action sniper rifle on her back.
    
- Usually carries a reinforced Nekonium baseball bat.
    
- Possesses two exceptionally large wings composed of condensed darkness and stabilized Ether.
    

---

# **The Eyes**

**Medical Status:** Permanently damaged by severe Ether Corruption.

Endmin's eyes are approximately **95% biologically blind**.

Rather than relying on light, they perceive the world by detecting microscopic Ether disturbances and delicate echoes traveling through the surrounding environment.

Capabilities include:

- Ether Sense.
    
- Extreme depth perception.
    
- Long-distance spatial awareness.
    
- Night vision.
    
- Reading printed text by sensing microscopic surface variations.
    
- Reading electronic displays through emitted Ether and structural vibrations.
    
- Accurate tracking of extremely fast moving objects.
    

Following Aperture Industries' successful reverse engineering of Ether, Endmin gained the ability to perceive **colors once again** through a prototype Ether enhancement.

Her biological vision remains permanently damaged.

The enhancement does **not** restore conventional eyesight.

---

# **Protective Face Covering**

The protective device worn over Endmin's eyes has **no official classification**.

The equipment shields her damaged eyes from harmful light exposure while allowing normal day-to-day activity.

Endmin continues wearing it by personal choice.

# **Psychological Notes**

Before arriving at the Blacksite, Endmin suffered repeated bullying related to her eye covering and vision.

Unexpected contact with the eye covering can trigger severe anxiety, dizziness, motion sickness, or resurfacing traumatic memories.

Personnel are instructed **never** to touch or tamper with her face covering without explicit permission.

Room N-07 residents are exempt due to established trust.

# **Anomalous Biology**

## Wings

Endmin possesses unusually large wings formed from condensed darkness interwoven with stabilized Ether.

Characteristics:

- Considerably larger than her body.
    
- Lightweight despite their size.
    
- Cannot naturally provide sustained flight without proper technique.
    
- Function primarily as defensive shields.
    
- Capable of completely surrounding and protecting multiple individuals.
    
- Possess accelerated regenerative capabilities.
    
- Fold seamlessly into her clothing when not deployed.
    

Following training by Lucy, Endmin has successfully learned controlled powered flight.

Her preferred method of aerial movement remains **gliding**.

---

## Wing Armor

Designed by Neko.

Each wing is protected by:

- Adaptive Nekonium nanoparticles.
    
- Integrated forcefield generator.
    

The armor significantly improves resistance against:

- Fragmentation.
    
- Heat.
    
- Ballistic impacts.
    
- Blunt trauma.
    

When fully folded, interaction between the nanoparticle lattice and forcefield produces an adaptive optical cloaking effect, concealing Endmin and anyone completely enclosed within the wings from unaided visual observation.

# **Combat Capabilities**

## Primary Firearm

**Bullpup Bolt-Action Rifle**

Caliber: **7.62 mm**

Features:

- Bolt-action.
    
- 20-round detachable magazine.
    
- Vertical grip.
    
- Bipod.
    
- 24× optic.
    
- Bayonet.
    
- Constructed from the same unidentified alloy as her protective face covering.
    

The rifle is almost entirely conventional.

Its only notable modification is compatibility with Banlands ammunition utilizing stabilized Ether.

---

## Evisceration

Endmin explained that Banlands weapons employ **stabilized Ether** within specialized ammunition.

This allows them to permanently eliminate corrupted souls and Ether-based entities that conventional weaponry cannot affect.

A single specially prepared emergency round is permanently carried beside the rifle's action.

According to Endmin:

> "One shot to cut all ties with the evil."

---

## Primary Melee Weapon

**Reinforced Nekonium Baseball Bat**

Manufactured by Neko.

Features:

- Reinforced Nekonium core.
    
- Optimized balance.
    
- Excellent impact durability.
    
- Intended for defensive and non-lethal engagements.
    

Despite its simplicity, Endmin demonstrates remarkable proficiency with it.

# **Marksmanship**

Endmin is considered the finest marksman currently residing within the Blacksite.

Notable accomplishments include:

- Neutralizing reconnaissance drones several kilometers outside the facility.
    
- Winning the Blacksite Marksmanship Competition.
    
- Extreme long-range accuracy measured to near-imperceptible deviation.
    
- Consistently outperforming experienced Aperture security personnel.
    

She strongly prefers deliberate, single-shot precision over automatic fire.

---

# **Combat History**

The left side of Endmin's rifle stock contains **3,560** carefully engraved tally marks.

Each represents one confirmed kill from her time serving as an Endministrator.

The engravings serve as a personal memorial rather than a celebration.
