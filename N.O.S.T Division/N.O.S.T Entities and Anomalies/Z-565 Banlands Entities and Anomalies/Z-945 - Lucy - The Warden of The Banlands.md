**Entity ID:** Z-945  
**Codename:** The Warden of the Banlands  
**Common Name:** Lucy  
**Threat Level:** White (Allied – Extreme Authority)  
**Containment Status:** Not Contained (Friendly External Authority)  
**Affiliation:** Banlands Administration  
**Title:** Warden of the Banlands

---

# **Overview**

Z-945, commonly known as **Lucy**, is the current **Warden of the Banlands** and the biological mother of Z-321 ("Endmin").

She possesses complete administrative authority over the Banlands and serves as its highest judicial, military, and executive authority.

Despite her overwhelming power, Lucy maintains cordial diplomatic relations with Aperture Industries and is considered one of its closest external allies.

Her cooperation has proven invaluable in research involving Ether, Banlands anomalies, and the revival of deceased Aperture personnel.

---

# **Physical Description**

Lucy appears as a young adult woman dressed in a professional black office suit with white accents.

Notable features include:

- Long white hair.
    
- Crimson-red eyes.
    
- Calm, composed demeanor.
    
- A sheathed crimson katana carried at her waist.
    
- Professional appearance regardless of circumstance.
    

Unlike many high-level anomalous entities, Lucy deliberately avoids intimidating displays of power during routine interactions.

---

# **Personality**

Lucy is best described as:

- Calm.
    
- Patient.
    
- Highly intelligent.
    
- Compassionate.
    
- Diplomatic.
    
- Protective.
    
- Emotionally disciplined.
    

She rarely raises her voice.

When she does, personnel are advised to pay attention.

Despite governing one of the harshest known dimensions, Lucy treats both civilians and subordinates with notable kindness.

She is especially protective of children.

---

# **The Banlands**

Lucy has repeatedly corrected a common misconception regarding the Banlands.

> **"The Banlands are not Hell."**

According to Lucy:

The Banlands function as an enormous **interdimensional prison**—a purgatory-like realm responsible for containing dangerous, corrupted, or otherwise condemned souls.

Many inhabitants deserve imprisonment.

Many do not.

Some remain capable of redemption.

Maintaining that balance is Lucy's primary responsibility.

---

# **Authority**

As Warden, Lucy possesses administrative authority over virtually every aspect of the Banlands.

Responsibilities include:

- Containing corrupted souls.
    
- Maintaining dimensional stability.
    
- Managing Ether corruption.
    
- Judicial oversight.
    
- Military command.
    
- Protection of innocent Banlands inhabitants.
    
- Regulation of Ether-based technology.
    

Her authority extends only within the Banlands and associated Ether systems.

She does not interfere with Starlands governance or Aperture Industries unless requested.

---

# **Combat Capabilities**

Lucy is considered an extremely dangerous combatant.

Unlike many powerful anomalies, she favors precision over overwhelming displays of force.

Her preferred combat style combines swordsmanship, marksmanship, and Ether manipulation.

---

## Primary Weapon

### Banlands Resonance Katana

Lucy personally forged her own weapon.

Characteristics include:

- Bright crimson blade.
    
- Extreme vibrational resonance along the cutting edge.
    
- Highly effective against Ether-corrupted entities.
    
- Exceptional cutting performance.
    

Testing indicates the blade's destructive capability comes primarily from high-frequency resonance rather than brute force.

Despite this, the blade is **unable to cut Nekonium**, a fact that amused Neko during demonstrations.

---

## Integrated Sheath Firearm

The katana's sheath conceals an integrated firearm.

Rather than firing conventional ammunition, it launches specially prepared Banlands rounds infused with stabilized Ether.

Effects include:

- Permanent elimination of corrupted souls.
    
- Severe disruption of Ether-based entities.
    
- Effective against conventional armored and unarmored targets.
    

The transition between sword and firearm occurs almost instantly.

---

# **Ether Mastery**

Lucy is among the foremost authorities on Ether.

Capabilities include:

- Ether manipulation.
    
- Ether stabilization.
    
- Soul purification.
    
- Corruption detection.
    
- Banlands portal creation.
    
- Ether weapon manufacturing.
    

Research collaboration between Lucy and Aperture Industries ultimately allowed Engineering Division to reverse engineer stabilized Ether.

This breakthrough enabled numerous technological developments, including Endmin's ability to perceive colors once again.

---

# **Flight**

Lucy possesses large wings of darkness similar in origin to Endmin's.

Unlike Endmin upon arrival at the Blacksite, Lucy has complete mastery over aerial movement.

She later became Endmin's flight instructor.

---

# **Relationship with Endmin**

Lucy is Endmin's biological mother.

She openly acknowledges that Endmin's eye injuries resulted from mistakes made under her own leadership.

She continues to carry considerable guilt regarding the incident.

Although fiercely protective, Lucy deliberately entrusted Endmin to Aperture Industries after concluding that the Blacksite offered a far healthier environment than the Banlands.

She visits regularly.

---

# **Relationship with Aperture Industries**

Lucy first arrived through a dimensional rift and requested that Director Lucious care for Endmin while she addressed several ongoing crises.

Since then she has become a trusted ally.

Her cooperation includes:

- Ether research.
    
- Weapons research.
    
- Personnel revival.
    
- Joint training exercises.
    
- Banlands diplomatic relations.
    

Director Lucious considers Lucy one of Aperture's most reliable external partners.

---

# **Notable Interactions**

### Meeting Bao

Lucy formally met **Bao**, the Starladian Deity of Clouds and Seas and mother of Frost.

The meeting quickly evolved into an informal conversation between two mothers discussing their daughters rather than politics.

Relations between the two remain warm.

---

### Combat Demonstrations

Lucy has publicly demonstrated:

- Banlands swordsmanship.
    
- Resonance blade mechanics.
    
- Ether-based ammunition.
    
- Banlands combat doctrine.
    

These demonstrations significantly improved Aperture's understanding of Banlands weapon systems.

# **Current Status**

Lucy remains the active Warden of the Banlands.

She periodically visits Endmin, collaborates with Aperture Industries on Ether-related research, and continues overseeing the Banlands while strengthening the alliance between both realms.

Lucy once remarked that she feared Aperture Industries would attempt to "fix" Endmin against her wishes.
