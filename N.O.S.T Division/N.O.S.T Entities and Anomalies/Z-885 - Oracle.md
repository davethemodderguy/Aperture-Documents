## **N.O.S.T. DIVISION — ENTITY SUB-DOCUMENT**

### **Z-885 "ORACLE"**

**Classification:** Internal / Medium Clearance

---

# **I. IDENTIFICATION**

**Entity Classification:** Z-885  
**Common Designation:** Oracle  
**Official Nickname:** _Oracle of the Blacksite_  
**Species:** Domestic Cat (_Felis catus_) – Anomalous Juvenile  
**Estimated Biological Age:** Approximately 10–12 weeks at time of acquisition.  
**Growth Rate:** Significantly slower than that of a normal domestic cat.

---

# **II. ACQUISITION**

Z-885 entered Aperture Industries custody under highly unusual circumstances.

During a classified business trip conducted outside Blacksite jurisdiction, Director Lucious was traveling undercover while attempting to reach the United States Embassy following a security incident.

According to Director Lucious's statement:

> "I was crossing the street toward a bus stop. A tiny kitten walked directly in front of me and absolutely refused to let me pass."

The kitten repeatedly blocked his path, meowed, and even stood on his shoe whenever he attempted to walk around her.

After several seconds, Director Lucious gave up and stepped away.

Approximately fifteen seconds later...

The bus he had intended to board was struck by an anti-vehicle rocket launched by an assassination team pursuing Director Lucious.

The attack destroyed the vehicle.

Director Lucious survived solely because he had been delayed by the kitten.

Following the incident, the kitten voluntarily followed Director Lucious for the remainder of the extraction operation.

She was brought back to the Blacksite.

---

### Director Lucious

> "I have survived gods, anomalies, and wars."

> "...Today I was saved by a kitten."

---

# **III. INITIAL OBSERVATIONS**

Personnel initially believed Z-885 to be an unusually affectionate stray.

Within days...

Strange patterns emerged.

Examples include:

- Repeatedly refusing to allow engineers onto catwalks later found to have suffered structural failure.
    
- Leading researchers away from laboratories moments before unstable reactions occurred.
    
- Interrupting technicians shortly before electrical equipment catastrophically malfunctioned.
    
- Preventing personnel from opening containment doors immediately before internal pressure failures.
    

None of these incidents could initially be explained.

Every warning, however...

Was correct.

---

### Engineering Report

> "She doesn't know _what_ is wrong."

> "She only knows _something_ is about to go wrong."

---

# **IV. ANOMALOUS PROPERTIES**

Following extensive observation, Z-885 was formally designated an anomalous entity.

Current evidence strongly suggests that Z-885 possesses a limited precognitive capability.

Rather than seeing the distant future...

She appears capable of perceiving imminent catastrophic events affecting nearby individuals.

The exact mechanism remains unknown.

Observed behavior includes:

- Physically blocking someone's path.
    
- Pulling on clothing.
    
- Sitting directly in front of dangerous routes.
    
- Refusing to move until personnel change direction.
    
- Leading personnel along alternate paths.
    
- Vocalizing repeatedly when immediate danger is present.
    

Testing indicates Z-885 does **not** understand the cause of the danger.

She simply recognizes that a dangerous outcome is approaching.

# **VII. GENERAL BEHAVIOR**

Despite her anomalous abilities, Z-885 behaves almost exactly like an ordinary kitten.

Observed behaviors include:

- Chasing loose cables.
    
- Sleeping inside cardboard boxes.
    
- Sitting on important paperwork.
    
- Stealing warm seats the moment someone stands up.
    
- Climbing onto shoulders without permission.
    
- Curling up on keyboards while researchers attempt to work.
    

She displays equal affection toward all personnel.

Although she recognizes her designated caretakers, she is known to randomly choose another employee to follow for the day.

Personnel have learned to pay attention when this happens.

Experience has shown that the individual Oracle chooses is often at increased risk of encountering an imminent hazardous event.

---

# **VIII. CHEESE ADVISORY**

**IMPORTANT NOTICE**

Personnel are **strictly prohibited** from intentionally feeding cheese to Z-885.

During accidental dietary testing, Oracle consumed a small piece of cheese.

Subsequent observations revealed an unexpected behavioral anomaly.

Effects include:

- Persistent demands for additional cheese.
    
- Uncharacteristically aggressive attempts to obtain cheese.
    
- Loud and continuous vocalization.
    
- Following personnel carrying dairy products.
    
- Attempting to inspect lunches for hidden cheese.
    

Fortunately...

This behavioral effect does **not** impair her precognitive abilities.

She continues protecting personnel while simultaneously demanding cheese.

Recovery typically occurs once Oracle has been gently calmed by one of her assigned caretakers.

Should accidental cheese exposure occur:

1. Notify Neko, Miss Neko, Star, or Nightshade.
    
2. Avoid escalating her excitement.
    
3. Softly restrain and comfort her if necessary.
    
4. Remove visible cheese products from the surrounding area.
    

---

# **IX. PERSONNEL NOTICE**

Personnel are reminded that Oracle's warnings should never be ignored.

Although her actions may seem inconvenient or random...

Historical data demonstrates an exceptionally high success rate in predicting immediate catastrophic hazards.

Official standing guidance is therefore as follows:

> **If Z-885 stops you, even if you are late, follow her lead. She may save your life.**

Failure to comply with her warnings is strongly discouraged.

# **XI. CURRENT STATUS**

Z-885 currently resides within the Blacksite under supervised free-roam status.

She is not considered hostile.

She requires no standard containment cell and instead spends her days wandering between departments, napping in sunny spots, and occasionally interrupting someone's schedule moments before disaster strikes.

To the scientific staff, she is a precognitive anomaly.

To Engineering, she is a tiny safety inspector.

To Neko, Miss Neko, Star, and Nightshade...

She is simply **Oracle**, a kitten who somehow keeps saving lives without ever asking for anything in return.

...Except cheese. Which she is absolutely **not** allowed to have.
