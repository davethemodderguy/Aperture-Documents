---

Z-456-S, known as **Spiritwood Syrup**, is a critical substance used within the Abyssal Express. Without it, the system does not function.

It is described as a **semi-transparent, glowing, shimmering syrup**, harvested from an anomalous tree referred to as the **Spiritwood**. The material itself emits a soft, steady light and shows no signs of decay or contamination.

Every passenger that boards the Train of the Afterlife requires it.

No exceptions.

---

## **FUNCTION**

According to Archangel Rose, the syrup serves a very specific purpose:

> “Lift the shackles over their soul, let go of the pain in their Body, let them meet His love, One, to transcend, to go beyond, to let go of your struggles.”

Not just poetic phrasing.

It’s literal.

Z-456-S is used to:

- **Sever the connection between the soul and body**
- **Erase memory of the previous life**
- **Prepare the soul for transition and rebirth**

Once administered, the subject is no longer tied to who they were.

They are ready to move forward.

---

## **MEMORY EFFECT**

Memory wipe is not perfectly absolute.

There have been recorded cases where reincarnated individuals experience:

- Short bursts of past memories
- Fragmented recollections
- Emotional echoes without context

These are **intentional**.

No explanation has been provided for why.

---

## **FAILURE CONDITION**

The process is not guaranteed to succeed.

Rose confirmed a clear indicator of failure:

> If the subject coughs **blue smoke**, the process has failed.

Failure does not result in termination.

Instead, the subject is reassigned.

---

## **THE GREATER GOOD**

Souls that fail Spiritwood processing are redirected into a separate outcome:

> **“The Greater Good.”**

This state results in the creation of what are known as:

**Starladians**

---

## **STADLADIAN ORIGIN LINK**

Z-456-S is directly tied to the existence of Starladians.

Contrary to earlier assumptions, about the Starladians are a native species to the Starlands:

> Starladians are not native to Starlands.

They are **converted souls**.

Souls that:

- Could not move on
- Were unable to fully let go of their past life
- Or were chosen for a different role

Upon conversion:

- The original identity is completely erased
- A new identity is formed
- The being becomes something entirely new

No overlap remains.

---

## **CROSS-SPECIES REINCARNATION**

Conversion is not limited to humans.

Evidence confirms that:

- Animals can also become Starladians
- Physical traits may carry over in altered forms
- Conciousness will be rebuild to the same as humans

This explains:

- Feline traits in Z-314-S (Star), Z-314-ST (Stitch), Z-314-CI (Cinder)
- Whale traits in Z-356-B (Bao)

The system does not discriminate.

---

## **THEOLOGICAL CONTEXT**

All processes tied to Z-456-S are linked to a higher entity identified as **God**.

Rose describes the system not as judgment—

but as acceptance.

> “All His Love to you, to your body, to your soul. God embraces all in His Grace.”

Within this framework:

- Some souls move on
- Some become Starladians
- Some take on higher roles (angels, managers, deities)

Every outcome is considered part of the same system.

---

## **CONCLUSION**

Spiritwood Syrup is more than a tool.

It is the point where everything changes.

Where a life ends.  
Where something new begins.

And sometimes—

Where something refuses to let go.
