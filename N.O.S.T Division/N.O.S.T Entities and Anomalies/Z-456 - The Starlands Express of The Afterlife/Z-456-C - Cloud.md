Z-356-C, designated **“Cloud”**, is a recently manifested Starladian entity assigned directly to the **Abyssal Express**, serving as both **assistant to Z-456-T (The Conductor)** and the **primary onboard attendant** of Z-456-A (The Train).

His existence was authorized following a request made by The Conductor for additional onboard support. Shortly after a confirmed meeting with **[REDACTED — GOD]**, the entity manifested within the Express.

No prior records exist.

---

## **MANIFESTATION & MEMORY STATE**

Upon manifestation, Cloud displayed:

- **Complete memory wipe**
- No identifiable past identity
- No recollection of origin
- No sense of self identity

Only the following baseline capabilities were present:

- Speech (limited, partially unstable, actively improving)
- Walking (bipedal)
- Basic cognition
- Singing ability

Unlike other Starladians, no past-life fragments or residual memories have surfaced, even with the use of the Soul Lens. After being accepted by the Express, he was given the name Cloud.

When questioned about his creation, God responded:

> “I did not make him. I guided him.”

No further clarification provided.

---

## **APPEARANCE**

Cloud deviates significantly from standard Starladian forms.

Instead of a humanoid or hybrid structure, he appears as a:

> **Fully animal-based entity (canine / puppy)**

Approximate traits:

- Age appearance: ~5 years old
- Body structure: Humanoid stance (bipedal)
- Covered entirely in **soft, silky, shiny, lustrous, cloud-like fur**
- Possesses cute paws instead of hands
- Texture described as unusually soft and light

He wears attire similar to **The Conductor**, reinforcing his role within the Express.

---

## **ROLE**

Cloud serves as the **sole onboard attendant** of the Abyssal Express.

Responsibilities include:

- Providing food and water to passengers
- Managing onboard comfort and environment
- Acting as a point of interaction for souls in transit
- Performing entertainment functions (primarily singing)

He operates continuously during transit cycles.

No replacement or secondary attendant has been observed.

---

## **BEHAVIOR**

Despite his child-like appearance, Cloud displays:

- High responsibility
- Self-awareness of mistakes
- Willingness to correct errors immediately

Behavior is described as:

> “Strangely adult and responsible”

He maintains composure even under high passenger volume or emotional environments.

---

## **ANOMALOUS TRAITS**

### **1. Vocal Ability**

Cloud possesses an **exceptionally calming, mesmerizing and “angelic” singing voice**, used to:

- Ease distressed passengers
- Stabilize emotional states
- Maintain calm within the Train

Passengers show strong positive responses.

---

### **2. Physical Interaction**

Cloud frequently engages in physical comfort behaviors:

- Allowing passengers to pet him
- Head pats, belly rubs, general interaction, everything that they would do to a puppy

This behavior significantly reduces anxiety in newly boarded souls.

---

### **3. Speech Instability**

Due to incomplete vocal development:

- Words may break mid-sentence
- Occasional substitution with **“woof”**

Initially unintentional, this behavior has since been **intentionally retained** by Cloud as a personality trait during interactions.

---

## **RELATIONSHIP WITH Z-456-T**

The Conductor has formally **adopted Cloud as her son**.

This relationship is acknowledged but not elaborated on.

Observed effects:

- Increased engagement from The Conductor (minor)
- Slight behavioral softening when interacting with Cloud

No deviation in her operational efficiency.

---

## **PERFORMANCE**

Cloud’s efficiency is consistently rated as:

> **“Strangely efficient.”**

Despite lack of experience or memory, he performs tasks:

- Accurately
- Consistently
- Without supervision

No major errors recorded.

Z-356-C is an unusual case even within Starladian classifications:

He exists not as a remnant—

But as something guided into place.
