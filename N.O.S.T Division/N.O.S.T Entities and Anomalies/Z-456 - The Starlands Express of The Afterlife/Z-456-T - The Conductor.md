**Entity ID:** Z-456-T  
**Codename:** The Conductor  
**Affiliation:** Z-456 (Starlands Afterlife Express)  
**Origin:** Z-556 (Starlands)

---

Z-456-T, referred to as **The Conductor**, is the primary operator of the Starlands’ **Train of the Afterlife**—a system responsible for guiding souls from a state of death toward whatever lies beyond, owned by The Starlands Express of The Afterlife (refer to the respective document for more info)

She takes the form of a human female, estimated to be in her mid-to-late 30s. Her appearance is grounded and familiar, wearing attire closely resembling that of a traditional Earth train conductor: formal uniform, structured coat, and cap. The only detail that breaks this normality is the badge on her chest—crafted from **pure Starlite**, unnaturally perfect in shape and glow, marking her as something far beyond human.

Despite being tied to a system that operates on a cosmic or existential level, The Conductor herself is… simple, in presentation.

No excess movement.  
No wasted words.  
No visible emotion beyond what is required.

She speaks clearly, calmly, and only when needed. Conversations with her are brief and purposeful, often ending the moment their function is fulfilled. She does not engage in casual interaction, nor does she show interest in anything outside her role.

That role is absolute.

The Conductor exists to operate the Train of the Afterlife—nothing more, nothing less. She ensures that the process of transition runs without interruption, without delay, and without error. There is no indication that she can abandon or step away from this responsibility, nor any evidence that she would want to.

Presence reports are consistent:

If the train is there,  
she is there.

Always positioned near the front.  
Always in control.

There are no records of her being absent from her post.

While not hostile, there is a clear boundary in her behavior. She does not welcome unnecessary interference, and her presence alone carries a weight that discourages it. Personnel describe interactions with her as controlled, quiet, and final—like speaking to someone who already knows how things will end.

She does not threaten.

She does not warn.

She simply… continues her work.

Of course, due to the loneliness of her job, The Starlands Express of The Afterlife have requested Aperture Industries to perodiacally send Operators to comfort her from her loneliness, since the Express's staff can't be designated to be in close proximity to her.
