Z-456, known as the **Starlands Express of the Afterlife** (or more casually, the *Abyssal Express*), is a large-scale, system-level entity responsible for one thing:

Moving souls on.

It’s not just a train. It’s an entire **afterlife transit system**, built to guide Starladian souls from death toward rebirth or whatever comes next. The whole thing is organized, maintained, and run like an actual railway service—just operating across space, time, and whatever exists beyond both.

---

## **GENERAL OVERVIEW**

The Express has been active for over **120 Starladian years**, during which it has successfully processed and transported **over 1.5 billion souls**.

No known system in Starlands operates at this scale with this level of consistency.

It does not stop.  
It does not delay.  
It does not lose passengers.

Everything runs… on time.

The entire system is overseen by:

**Z-456-R — Archangel Rose**

She is the closest thing this system has to “authority.”

## **CORE COMPONENTS**

Z-456 is not a single entity—it’s a **collection of interlinked Z-Class entities**, each responsible for a specific part of the process:

- **Z-456-T — The Conductor**  
    The one who runs the train directly. Handles movement, boarding flow, and overall operation during transit.
- **Z-456-A — The Train**  
    The locomotive itself. Not just a vehicle, but an entity capable of traveling through non-linear space and carrying souls safely across it.
- **Z-456-R — Archangel Rose**  
    System manager. Oversees the entire Express and maintains operational stability.
- **Z-456-S — Spiritwood Syrup**  
    A critical substance used during the transition process. Required for successful passage into the afterlife.

Each component is essential. Remove one, and the system likely collapses.
