Z-456-A, simply referred to as **The Train**, is the core component of the Abyssal Express and the one part the entire system cannot function without.

If Z-456 is the system,  
this is what actually _moves_ it.

The Train is responsible for transporting souls across the **Rails of the Afterlife**—a term previously requested to be redacted by Archangel Rose, but still widely used in internal documentation due to lack of alternative terminology.

---

## **GENERAL OVERVIEW**

The Train has been in continuous operation for over **500 years**, predating the current structured management of the Express.

It is the oldest confirmed component of Z-456.

Every recorded soul that has successfully transitioned through the Express has, at some point, been carried by this entity.

No exceptions.

---

## **FUNCTION**

Its role is direct:

- Accept boarded passengers (souls)
- Transport them across non-linear transit space
- Deliver them to the point of transcendence

Once a passenger is seated, the process begins automatically.

No delays.  
No reroutes.  
No early exits.

When the Train departs—

that journey is final.

---

## **BOARDING SYSTEM**

Entry to Z-456-A requires a **boarding pass**, described as:

- A **3x3 punch card**
- Issued prior to boarding
- Used to assign seating

Without this, entry is not permitted.

Once inside:

- Passengers are seated
- Movement is restricted to assigned areas
- Departure follows shortly after full boarding

No cases of unauthorized boarding have been confirmed.

---

## **STRUCTURAL NOTES**

Despite appearing as a “train,” Z-456-A does not follow known mechanical logic.

Unknowns include:

- Power source
- Propulsion system
- Internal structure consistency
- Spatial limits (interior may not match exterior size)

All attempts to analyze or document its full capabilities have been restricted.

This is not due to failure—

but by request.

## **BEHAVIORAL NOTES**

Z-456-A does not communicate.

It does not respond.

It does not acknowledge external presence.

And yet—

It is always exactly where it needs to be.

Arrival times are precise.  
Departures are immediate.

There is no visible operator input required for movement once conditions are met.

Z-456-A is less a vehicle and more a **constant** within the afterlife system.

It doesn’t break.

the same way it has for centuries.
