### **Z-356-F — Codename: “Frost”**

**Classification:** Allied Entity  
**Threat Level:** Conditional  
**Status:** Contained / Cooperative

---

### **OVERVIEW**

Z-356-F, known as **“Frost,”** is a Starladian Deity and the confirmed daughter of **Z-356-B (Bao)**.

She is the **youngest confirmed Starladian Deity**, with classification placing her within the Z-356 class while maintaining a distinct developmental and behavioral profile.

Frost is an active, cooperative presence within **Sector 4-C**, with stable integration among personnel.

---

### **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

Z-356-F presents as a soft, minimalistic humanoid with distinctly cool-toned features.

- **Skin:** Azure, ice-like in coloration and texture
- **Hair:** White, similar to Bao, but shorter and more compact in form
- **Eyes:** Half-lidded, subdued, carrying a calm, somnolent expression
- **Halo:**
    - Black, nebula-like in structure
    - Tied with a white ribbon
    - One end remains loose, drifting naturally in air
- **Wings:**
    - Two asymmetrical wings
        - One white
        - One nebula-colored, dark with cosmic-like gradients

---

**Attire:**

Frost’s clothing is not conventional apparel.

- Thick, oversized hoodie
- Multiple layered garments beneath
- Heavy, oversized scarf

These garments are **engineered to rapidly withdraw and regulate body heat**, functioning as an external stabilization system.

---

**Thermal State:**

- Required internal body temperature: **-550°C**
- Body remains stable only under sustained extreme cold conditions

Despite this, Frost is:

- **Cold to the touch**
- Still physically safe and comfortable to hug

---

### **ABILITIES**

---

**Touch of Frost**

- Instantly crystallizes any fluid upon contact
- Affects both biological and non-biological substances

---

**Frost Aura**

- Passive environmental temperature reduction
- Frost effectively functions as a **“Walking AC Unit”**
- Environmental cooling is automatically compensated by:
    - **Lucy AI**
    - Which disables and rebalances the facility’s AC systems when Frost is nearby

---

**Aftershade**

- Frost can teleport from her current location
- Leaves behind a **statue-like construct of herself**
- The construct can be manually detonated via a snap of her fingers

---

**Detonation**

- Ice-based explosive effect
- Frozen constructs and nearby ice structures detonate with force comparable to high-yield explosives

---

### **BEHAVIORAL NOTES**

- Calm, quiet, and observant
- Maintains a subdued emotional baseline
- Socially adaptive, but not overtly expressive
- Forms stable interactions with key personnel, particularly:
    - Bao
    - Star
    - Candleheart
    - Cinder (thermally balanced interaction)

---

### **CONSTRAINTS & LIMITATIONS**

- Requires constant thermal regulation
- Prolonged exposure to improper temperatures may result in:
    - Structural instability
    - Functional degradation
- Environmental interaction must account for:
    - Passive cooling effects
    - Thermal influence on surrounding systems

---

### **NOTES**

- Frost’s presence has led to measurable environmental stabilization in controlled zones
- Integration into Sector 4-C remains stable and beneficial
- Interactions with opposing thermal entities (e.g., Cinder) are **non-hostile but require careful balance**
