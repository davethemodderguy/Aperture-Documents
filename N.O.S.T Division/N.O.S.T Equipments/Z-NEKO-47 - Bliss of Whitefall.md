# **II. OVERVIEW**

Z-NEKO-047 is an anomalous large-caliber precision rifle created by Miss Neko during a two-week leave spent primarily around Whitefall Lake.

The weapon was first documented after Miss Neko submitted a formal Weapon Demonstration Authorization request to Aperture Industries.

The request was approved largely because personnel involved recognized the applicant name and collectively decided that asking questions would probably lead to more paperwork.

This decision is now considered questionable.

# **FINAL OBSERVATION**

Most engineers build machines.

Most weapons designers build firearms.

Miss Neko built something that deleted a mountain because she was bored during vacation.

The fact that she refers to Z-NEKO-047 as:

> _"My long-range problem solver."_

has been officially classified as alarming.

Despite this, she continues carrying the weapon openly throughout Aperture facilities.

At present, the only reason Z-NEKO-047 is not considered an immediate existential threat is because its owner is generally more interested in helping Maintenance, sleeping as a cat, and spending time with family than actually firing it.

The Armory Division has unanimously agreed on one conclusion:

> **If Miss Neko ever starts smiling while loading Z-NEKO-047, immediate evacuation is recommended.**
