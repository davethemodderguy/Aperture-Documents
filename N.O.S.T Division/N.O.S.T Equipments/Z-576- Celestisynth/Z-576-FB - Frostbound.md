**Status:** Bound to Z-356-F (Frost) / Active

---

### **OVERVIEW**

**Frostbound** is a Celetisynth weapon utilized by **Z-356-F (Frost)**. It is classified as the **first Celetisynth ever created**, predating **Solaris**.

The weapon manifests as a scythe and exhibits highly lethal properties, primarily centered around extreme cryogenic manipulation and internal detonation.

---

### **PRIMARY FUNCTION**

Frostbound operates through a two-stage kill process:

1. Target is **enclosed in thick, dense ice**
2. The ice subsequently **detonates from within**, causing internal destruction

This process has been confirmed as the weapon’s primary lethality mechanism.

---

### **PASSIVES**

**Heart of Frost**

- Holding Frostbound rapidly drains the wielder’s internal temperature
- Continuous use requires extreme thermal compatibility
- **Note:** “DO NOT TOUCH” (Sticky Note: Cinder)

**Frostbound (Passive Effect)**

- Any enemy slain by the weapon is:
    - Encased in thick ice
    - Preserved within cryogenic structure prior to detonation
