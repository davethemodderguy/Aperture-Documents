## **OVERVIEW**

**Breezebreaker** is a wind-aspected Celetisynth weapon wielded by **Bao**.

Forged from the **essence of wind**, this blade is designed around **mobility, control, and defensive maneuvering** rather than raw destructive output. It enhances Bao’s already strong affinity with air, allowing her to move, reposition, and redirect combat flow with minimal effort.

While it functions independently, Breezebreaker is most effective when used alongside:

> **Z-576-A — Aquaflora**

Together, they represent Bao’s dual control over:

- **Air (Breezebreaker)**
- **Water (Aquaflora)**

---

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

- Form: Lightweight katana
- Blade: Semi-transparent, wind-infused edge
- Effects: Air distortion, pressure trails, subtle wind currents around swings

The blade is almost weightless in motion—

but carries force through velocity.

---

## **CORE MECHANIC**

Unlike most Celetisynth weapons, Breezebreaker has **no formal passive ability**.

Instead, it operates on an environmental scaling principle:

**The more exposed Bao is to open air, the stronger the weapon becomes.**

Effects include:

- Increased movement speed
- Enhanced agility
- Faster attack execution

Confined spaces reduce effectiveness.

Open environments maximize it.
