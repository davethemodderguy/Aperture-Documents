## **OVERVIEW**

**Solaris** is Cinder Dawnbringer’s signature weapon and one of the earliest successfully deployed Celetisynth constructs.

Forged from the **essence of a Starladian Sun**, Solaris is built around a **high-density thermal blade** that maintains extreme heat levels at all times. The weapon emits a constant solar-like glow, with energy signatures resembling compressed stellar plasma.

Despite its destructive output, Solaris demonstrates **selective impact behavior**, meaning:

- No environmental damage
- No self-inflicted damage to wielder
- Damage exclusively applies to intended targets

This suggests built-in **intent recognition or targeting filtration**, likely tied directly to Cinder’s control.
