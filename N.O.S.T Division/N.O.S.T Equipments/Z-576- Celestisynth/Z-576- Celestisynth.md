### **OVERVIEW**

Z-576, collectively referred to as **Celetisynth**, is a class of high-grade anomalous weapons currently utilized by Dawnbringers, Starladian Deities, and select high-authority entities (including Director Lucious and Daniel).

These weapons aren’t mass-produced tools—they’re **forged concepts**.

Each Celetisynth is created using condensed cosmic or conceptual elements (Sun, Moon, Wind, Souls, etc.), resulting in weapons that don’t just deal damage, but **rewrite the rules of the fight** they’re in.

They cover a wide range:

- Close-range melee
- Long-range weapons
- Hybrid and reality-bending constructs

All known Celetisynth weapons were commissioned by **Z-314-ST (Stitch)** and **Z-314-CI (Cinder)**, and forged by the **Starladian Blacksmith Alliance** following the liberation of Starlands.
