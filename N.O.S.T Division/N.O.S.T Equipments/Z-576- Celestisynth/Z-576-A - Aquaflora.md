## **OVERVIEW**

**Aquaflora** is Bao’s secondary Celetisynth weapon, designed as a **precision rapier** focused on speed, control, and internal disruption.

At first glance, the weapon appears decorative—formed from **Starladian flora, flowing water, and coral-like structures**. Analysis confirms this is misleading.

The structure is **far denser and more durable** than it appears, behaving like a reinforced anomalous construct rather than organic material.

Like Breezebreaker, Aquaflora has **no passive ability**.

Everything it does—

it does actively.
