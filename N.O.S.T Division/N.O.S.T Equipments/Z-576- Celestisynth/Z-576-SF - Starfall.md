## **OVERVIEW**

**Starfall** is the third forged Celetisynth and the personal weapon of **Stitch Dawnbringer**.

Despite the name, this weapon has no direct relation to Star.

It reflects Stitch himself—his obsession with **Starladian Stars**, their structure, their motion, and their beauty. Starfall takes those celestial properties and turns them into something practical:

Damage. Speed. Control.

Where other weapons dominate through force, Starfall dominates through **flow**.

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

- Form: Sleek katana
- Blade: Condensed “Star Material” with constant faint glow
- Effects: Trails of stardust and comet-like streaks during swings

The weapon feels light—

but behaves like something much heavier when it hits.

## **PASSIVE ABILITY**

---

### **Dance of The Stars**

Momentum-based scaling system.

**Effect:**

- Each successful hit increases:
    - Movement speed
    - Attack speed
    - Agility

Stacking:

- Continuous hits = continuous scaling
- No confirmed limit observed

At higher stacks:

> Stitch is capable of dodging bullets and reacting at extreme speeds with minimal effort.

This turns prolonged fights into a losing situation for the opponent.

---

## **ACTIVE SKILLS**

---

### **Call of the Stars**

Area strike ability.

**Effect:**

- Summons multiple **comets** onto a targeted location
- Impacts occur rapidly and precisely

Results:

- High burst damage
- Multi-target elimination

Notes:

- No environmental damage
- Purely target-focused destruction

---

### **Supernova Implosion**

Internal detonation strike.

**Trigger:**

- Direct hit on target

**Effect:**

- Target destabilizes internally
- Explodes into **stardust** from within

Additional effect:

- Produces intense flash and sensory disruption
- Functions similarly to an **Aperture flashbang** (hallucination/disorientation)

Not just lethal—

but disorienting for anything nearby.

---

### **Nebula’s Protection**

Defensive construct.

**Effect:**

- Generates a shield made from **pure star material**
- Can be applied to:
    - Stitch
    - Allied target

Protection level:

> **Complete (100%) damage negation during active state**

No penetration recorded.

## **COMBAT BEHAVIOR**

Starfall thrives in:

- Continuous engagements
- Multi-target fights
- High-speed combat scenarios

Typical pattern:

- Build momentum (Dance of The Stars)
- Control space (Call of the Stars)
- Eliminate targets (Supernova Implosion)
- Defend when needed (Nebula’s Protection)

Once momentum builds—

Stitch becomes extremely difficult to track, hit, or counter.
