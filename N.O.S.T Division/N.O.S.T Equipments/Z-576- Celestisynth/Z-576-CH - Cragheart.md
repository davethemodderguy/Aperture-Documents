## **OVERVIEW**

**Cragheart** is the primary weapon of **Viridis**, designed as a **support-dominant Celetisynth** focused on:

- Healing
- Crowd suppression
- Environmental advantage

Unlike most Celetisynth weapons built for direct destruction, Cragheart turns the battlefield itself into a weapon—using **flora, terrain, and life energy** as extensions of its power.

It does not rush combat.

It controls it.

---

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

- Form: Long Spear with organic, root-like extensions
- Material Composition: Starladian flora, mineralized wood, and energy-infused core
- Visual Effects: Vines, blossoms, and glowing green energy manifestations

The weapon appears semi-living, often reacting to Viridis’s intent before physical movement occurs.

---

## **PASSIVE EFFECTS**

---

### **Helio’s Blessing**

Solar amplification support.

**Effect:**

- Allies exposed to sunlight receive:  
    → Increased damage output  
    → Accelerated regeneration

Encourages open-environment combat where light is present.

---

### **Nature’s Wraith**

Environmental resilience.

**Effect:**

- While near plant life:  
    → Increased durability  
    → Reduced incoming damage

Turns natural terrain into a defensive advantage.

---

## **ACTIVE SKILLS**

---

### **Wrangler**

Target immobilization.

**Effect:**

- Summons vines to restrain one or multiple targets
- Restriction strength scales with target resistance

Used for crowd control and capture.

---

### **Parry**

Countermeasure ability.

**Effect:**

- Blocks incoming attack
- Reflects force at approximately **250× intensity**

Highly effective against heavy strikes.

---

### **Venomous**

Pain-based damage loop.

**Effect:**

- Each strike injects lethal venom
- Target experiences fatal damage, followed by forced revival
- Cycle repeats multiple times

Important note:

- Pain perception is **fully retained** throughout the process

This is considered one of the most psychologically destructive abilities in the Celetisynth arsenal.

---

### **Life’s Blessing**

Emergency recovery.

**Effect:**

- Revives one critically injured ally
- Stabilizes condition immediately

Primary support function.

---

### **Nature’s Devastation**

Area suppression.

**Effect:**

- Summons a massive vine structure from below
- Crushes enemies within target zone
- Does not affect allies

---

### **Garden of Nede**

Dimensional containment.

**Effect:**

- Traps enemies inside a temporary pocket dimension
- Internal environment collapses after several seconds
- Non-hostile entities are unaffected

Used for isolation and elimination.

---

### **Held by Hope**

Hybrid support-offense.

**Effect:**

- Stabilizes allied mental/emotional state
- Simultaneously releases explosive force toward enemies

Acts as both morale recovery and disruption tool.

---

### **Artemis’s Rage**

Summoned assistance.

**Effect:**

- Generates vine-based constructs (“vine soldiers”)
- Units assist in combat autonomously

Effective for overwhelming numbers or defensive holding.

---

## **COMBAT BEHAVIOR**

Cragheart favors:

- Mid-to-long range control
- Area denial
- Team support and sustain

Under Viridis:

> The battlefield becomes alive—  
> and it chooses who gets to stand on it.

---

## **LIMITATIONS**

- Performance heavily tied to environmental conditions (plants, sunlight)
- Lower direct burst damage compared to offensive Celetisynth weapons
- Some abilities prioritize control over immediate elimination
