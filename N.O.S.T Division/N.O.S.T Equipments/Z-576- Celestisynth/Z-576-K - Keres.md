## **OVERVIEW**

**Keres** is the **first confirmed Celetisynth weapon wielded by a Banlands entity**, and remains one of the most **restricted weapons** across all known realms.

Unlike Starladian-forged Celetisynths, Keres is not a product of balance or creation—

it is a product of **aftermath**.

Forged from **blood, collapse, and condemned souls**, Keres exists as a **weapon of mass destruction**, designed specifically for dealing with threats native to the Banlands.

Due to its unstable and catastrophic nature:

> **Keres is strictly prohibited from deployment on Earth.**

Authorization pending under Daniel’s review.

---

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

- Form: Double-edged Glaive
- Material: Unknown (appears organic-metallic, constantly shifting)
- Visual Effects: Flowing blood-like aura, whispering entities embedded within the blade

The weapon appears **alive**, reacting to death, proximity of souls, and hostile intent.

Extended observation reports:

> The blade “breathes.”

---

## **BANLANDS CONTEXT**

The **Banlands** is not a realm of life—

it is where things go when they **cannot move on**.

- Failed ascensions
- Corrupted souls
- Predators formed from regret, hatred, and unfinished purpose

Within this environment, Keres functions not just as a weapon—

but as a **harvester**.

Every kill feeds the system.

Every soul is accounted for.
