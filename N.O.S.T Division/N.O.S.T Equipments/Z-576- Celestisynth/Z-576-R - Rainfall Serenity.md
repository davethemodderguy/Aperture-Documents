## **OVERVIEW**

**Rainfall Serenity** is the first confirmed **ranged Celetisynth weapon**, designed to operate alongside **Crecentia** as a support and suppression tool.

Forged from the same **lunar essence**, the weapon converts energy into high-velocity projectiles, enabling Star to control space, apply pressure, and assist from range while maintaining mobility.

Unlike most Celetisynth weapons, Rainfall Serenity has **no passive abilities**.

It doesn’t enhance the user directly.

It enhances the **battlefield around her**.

---

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

- Form: Energy-based Longbow
- Energy Signature: Lunar (blue-toned, radiant)
- Visual Effects: Thin laser trails, sky-based portals, raining light

The bow manifests as a luminous construct rather than a physical object, firing condensed energy in place of arrows.

---

## **PASSIVE EFFECTS**

---

**None**

Rainfall Serenity operates purely through its active capabilities.

This design strongly suggests a **support-oriented role**, relying on skill usage and synergy rather than constant passive enhancement.

---

## **ACTIVE SKILLS**

---

### **Strike**

Standard precision shot.

**Effect:**

- Fires a single laser projectile
- Travels at ~2400 m/s
- High accuracy and speed

Functions as the weapon’s baseline attack.

---

### **Triple Strike**

Multi-shot pressure tool.

**Effect:**

- Fires three consecutive laser projectiles
- Increased coverage and hit probability

Not significantly stronger—

just harder to avoid.

---

### **Incinerate**

Thermal enhancement.

**Effect:**

- Projectiles ignite upon impact
- Heat output comparable to **Solaris (Z-576-S)**
- Causes immediate burn damage

Transforms precision shots into lethal finishers.

---

### **Deployment**

Autonomous support mode.

**Effect:**

- Activates when the bow is released
- Operates independently of the user
- Tracks and fires at targets automatically

Allows Star to fight on multiple fronts at once.

---

### **Rainfall**

Area suppression ability.

**Effect:**

- Fires a shot upward, opening a portal
- Portal rains down continuous laser fire
- Covers a designated area

Duration:

- Persists until an **Incinerate** shot is fired
- Incinerate acts as a manual shutdown trigger

Turns the battlefield into a controlled hazard zone.

---

## **SYNERGY — Z-576-C (CRECENTIA)**

---

### **Dragon’s Rage**

Combined burst attack.

**Effect:**

- Fires Crecentia’s **Dragon’s Eye** enhanced by Triple Strike output
- Results in a high-damage, explosive projectile

Precision meets overwhelming force.

---

### **Nuke**

Enhanced Rainfall state.

**Effect:**

- Rainfall portal converts into **Lunar Fireworks** output
- Explosions disorient, blind, and damage enemies
- Area becomes highly unstable and destructive

Not just suppression—

**denial of existence in that space**.

---

## **COMBAT BEHAVIOR**

Rainfall Serenity excels at:

- Long-range engagement

- Continuous pressure
- Supporting close-range combat (via Crecentia)

On its own, it controls space.

With Crecentia—

it controls **everything in it**.
