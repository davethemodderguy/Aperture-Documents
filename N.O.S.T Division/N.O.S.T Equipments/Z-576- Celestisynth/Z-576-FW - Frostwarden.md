## **OVERVIEW**

**Frostwarden** is the first confirmed **Celetisynth weapon adapted for human use**, specifically wielded by **Director Lucious**.

Built upon the foundation of the **RSH-15 revolver**, Frostwarden has been heavily modified to integrate **Starladian crystalline properties**, allowing it to fire rounds that alter targets at both physical and structural levels.

Unlike most Celetisynth weapons, Frostwarden emphasizes:

- Precision
- Tactical versatility
- Controlled, high-impact shots

It is not excessive.

It is exact.
