## **OVERVIEW**

**Crecentia** is the sixth confirmed Celetisynth weapon and the primary weapon of **Star Dawnbringer**.

Forged from the **essence of the Moon**, Crecentia specializes in **gravity manipulation and battlefield control**, allowing Star to rapidly reposition herself and her enemies while maintaining constant pressure.

Unlike more destructive Celetisynth weapons, Crecentia is built around:

- Control
- Momentum
- Scaling damage

It doesn’t end fights instantly.

It makes sure they **can’t escape them**.

- Visual Effects: Silver-blue trails, occasional “firework-like” bursts
