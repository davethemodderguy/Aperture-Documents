## **OVERVIEW**

**Poltergeist** is the second forged Celetisynth weapon and the primary weapon of **Charlie Dawnbringer**.

Inspired by the **Legendary Battle Axe of Knight Archangel Morth**, Poltergeist is built around three simple ideas:

- **Damage**
- **Control**
- **Denial**

It is not flashy.

It does not rely on complex mechanics.

It is described by its creators as:

> “Just enough.”

And in practice—

that’s more than enough.

---

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

- Form: Heavy Battle Axe
- Structure: Reinforced celestial alloy with dense core
- Energy Signature: Low emission, high impact
- Weight: Extremely high (user-bound usability)

Every swing carries force far beyond its physical mass.

---

## **PASSIVE ABILITY**

---

### **Purpose of The Wicked**

On kill effect.

**Effect:**

- Defeated enemies are converted into **Aberrants**
- Aberrants remain active briefly after death
- They slowly pull nearby enemies toward themselves

**Final Phase:**

- Aberrants **implode violently from within**
- Resulting explosion damages all nearby targets

Notes:

- Chain reactions possible
- Effective in crowd-heavy scenarios
- No environmental damage recorded

---

## **ACTIVE SKILLS**

---

### **Cosmic Steel Annihilation**

Primary crowd control ability.

**Effect:**

- Axe is slammed into the ground
- Releases a powerful **shockwave + EMP pulse**
- Knocks back all nearby enemies

Additional effects:

- Disrupts electronic systems
- Interrupts abilities and movement

Simple, direct, and effective at resetting the battlefield.

---

### **Barrier Call**

Defensive maneuver.

**Execution:**

- Charlie performs a backward flip
- Deploys a frontal barrier lasting approximately **5 seconds**

**Effect:**

- Incoming projectiles are **instantly severed**
- Sustained attacks are fully blocked

Barrier behavior:

- Does not reflect damage
- Purely defensive, absolute in function during duration

---

## **COMBAT BEHAVIOR**

Poltergeist favors:

- Mid to close-range engagements
- Multi-target environments
- Defensive positioning with sudden counterattacks

It excels when:

- Surrounded
- Outnumbered
- Under pressure

---

## **LIMITATIONS**

- Slower attack speed compared to other Celetisynth weapons
- Skillset is limited in variety
- Relies heavily on positioning and timing

However—

none of these significantly reduce effectiveness in real combat scenarios.
